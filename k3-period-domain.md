# K3 period domain

↑ **Parent:** [Period domain](period-domain.md)

On the [K3 intersection lattice](k3-intersection-lattice.md), this open quadric has complex dimension twenty and records the period line $H^{2,0}$. Fixing a positive class $h$ of type $(1,1)$ adds $q(h,\omega)=0$ and yields a nineteen-dimensional domain for primitive polarized cohomology of signature $(2,19)$. The distinction is necessary: the unrestricted full lattice has signature $(3,19)$ and does not satisfy polarization positivity on all of $H^{1,1}$.
