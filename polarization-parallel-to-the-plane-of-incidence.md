# Polarization parallel to the plane of incidence

↑ **Parent:** [Plane of incidence](plane-of-incidence.md)

For reflection or refraction at a flat isotropic interface, [p-polarization](polarization-parallel-to-the-plane-of-incidence.md) has its [electric field](electric-field-split.md) in the incidence plane and perpendicular to its [wavevector](wavevector.md). At a lossless nonmagnetic [dielectric](dielectric.md) interface, its reflected [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) vanishes at [Brewster's angle](brewster-s-angle.md). This does not mean that its field is generally parallel to the interface surface.
