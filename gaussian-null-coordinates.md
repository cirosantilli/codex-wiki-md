# Gaussian null coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Null hypersurface](null-hypersurface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_null_coordinates)

Gaussian null coordinates adapt one coordinate to the generators of a [null hypersurface](null-hypersurface.md), one affine coordinate to transverse null geodesics, and the remaining coordinates to a spacelike cross-section. If the generator parameter is affine, the metric can locally be written

$$
ds^2=2\,dr\,d\lambda+r^2F\,d\lambda^2+2rh_i\,d\lambda\,dy^i+h_{ij}\,dy^i\,dy^j.
$$
