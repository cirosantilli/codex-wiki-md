# Homotopy-group addition in a topological group

↑ **Parent:** [Eckmann-Hilton argument](eckmann-hilton-argument.md)

For $n\ge1$, pointwise multiplication of based cube maps into a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) induces the ordinary addition in its [homotopy group](homotopy-group.md) $\pi_n(G,e)$. Concatenation and pointwise multiplication share an identity and satisfy the interchange rule, so the [Eckmann-Hilton argument](eckmann-hilton-argument.md) identifies the two operations. In particular, the $m$-th power map induces multiplication by $m$ on every [homotopy group](homotopy-group.md), for all integers $m$.
