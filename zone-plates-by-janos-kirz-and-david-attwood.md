# Zone Plates by Janos Kirz and David Attwood

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by David Attwood](work-by-david-attwood.md), [Work by Janos Kirz](work-by-janos-kirz.md)

By [Janos Kirz](janos-kirz.md) and [David Attwood](david-attwood.md). Section 4.4 of the X-Ray Data Booklet, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory.

Official text: [https://xdb.lbl.gov/Section4/Sec_4-4.html](https://xdb.lbl.gov/Section4/Sec_4-4.html)

The section specifies half-period-zone geometry and distinguishes focal resolution from first-order [diffraction](diffraction.md) efficiency. Its ideal binary amplitude [zone plate](zone-plate.md) has first-order efficiency $1/\pi^2$.
