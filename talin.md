# Talin

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[talin](talin.md) links integrin-associated adhesions to cytoskeletal machinery. Force-induced opening of its rod domains can expose [vinculin](vinculin.md)-binding sites, providing a molecular mechanism of [mechanotransduction](mechanotransduction.md).
