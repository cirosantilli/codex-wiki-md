# RMS spread versus raw root mean square

↑ **Parent:** [Root mean square](root-mean-square.md)

The raw [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) of a random variable is $\sqrt{E[X^2]}$, whereas its r.m.s. fluctuation about its [mean](expected-value-split.md) is the [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) $\sqrt{E[(X-\mu)^2]}$. The former cannot be smaller than $|\mu|$. Experimental phrases such as r.m.s. [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) or [temperature](temperature-split.md) spread usually refer to the latter and should state the reference level.
