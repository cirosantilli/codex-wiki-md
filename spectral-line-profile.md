# Spectral line profile

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)

A normalized [spectral line profile](spectral-line-profile.md) distributes a transition's emission or [absorption](absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) over [frequency](frequency.md), with $\int\varphi(\nu)d\nu=1$ and units $\mathrm{Hz^{-1}}$. Thermal motion, finite excited-state lifetime and collisions can give different contributions to the shape. Normalization lets an [optically thin](optically-thin-medium.md) integrated line measure the relevant [column density](column-density.md) without requiring the detailed profile.
