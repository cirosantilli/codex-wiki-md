# Cherenkov escape condition at a flat dielectric interface

↑ **Parent:** [Cherenkov radiation](cherenkov-radiation.md)

A photon emitted at angle $\theta_C$ to a normally incident particle can cross a planar boundary from index $n_1$ to $n_2$ only if [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) permits a real transmitted angle. Combining $\cos\theta_C=1/(n_1\beta)$ with that condition gives $n_1^2-\beta^{-2}\le n_2^2$. Otherwise the photon undergoes [total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md). For a relativistic particle in glass of index $1.55$ followed by air, the condition fails despite emission inside the glass.
