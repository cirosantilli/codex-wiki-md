# Ketamine

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology](pharmacology-split.md)

A compound with NMDA-receptor [antagonist](antagonist.md) actions among other effects, used in some experiments to perturb [learning](learning.md) and perception. A transient pharmacological model does not reproduce an entire psychiatric disorder.
