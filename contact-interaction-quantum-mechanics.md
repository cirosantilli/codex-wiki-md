# Contact interaction (quantum mechanics)

↑ **Parent:** [Second quantization](second-quantization.md)

In a one-dimensional identical-particle model, a contact interaction has two-body potential $U(x,x')=g\delta(x-x')$, using the [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md). For [bosons](boson.md), its [second quantization](second-quantization.md) is the displayed normally ordered [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). Projection onto [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) modes gives $H_{\rm int}=(g/2)\sum_{ijkl}I_{ijkl}a_i^\dagger a_j^\dagger a_ka_l$, where $I_{ijkl}=\int\varphi_i^*\varphi_j^*\varphi_k\varphi_l\,dx$. The overlap integrals determine which mode-changing processes survive. A finite-mode model describes that projection; discarded modes can still matter to the full physical system.
