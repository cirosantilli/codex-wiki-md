# Undirected graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

An undirected [graph](graph-split.md) has edges that are unordered pairs of vertices. In a simple undirected graph there are no loops or multiple edges. The [skeleton of a directed graph](skeleton-of-a-directed-graph.md) forgets the directions of its edges and is an undirected graph.
