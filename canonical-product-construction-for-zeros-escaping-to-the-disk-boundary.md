# Canonical product construction for zeros escaping to the disk boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical product](canonical-product.md)

For nonzero prescribed zeros $a_n$ with $|a_n|\to1$, the [Weierstrass elementary factors](weierstrass-elementary-factor.md) of increasing order give the displayed [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md); $m$ handles finitely many zeros at zero. On any compact disc, eventually $|z/a_n|\le q<1$, and $|\log E_n(z/a_n)|\le q^{n+1}/[(n+1)(1-q)]$. The [infinite product convergence from logarithmic tails](infinite-product-convergence-from-logarithmic-tails.md) gives a nonvanishing tail. The finite initial factors supply exactly the prescribed zeros and [multiplicities](multiplicity-mathematics.md). No [Blaschke condition](blaschke-condition.md) is required unless boundedness is imposed.
