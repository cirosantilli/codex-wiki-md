# Primal graph

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint hypergraph](constraint-hypergraph.md)

The [graph](graph-split.md) with one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) per [variable](variable-mathematics.md) and an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) between distinct [variables](variable-mathematics.md) occurring in a common constraint scope. Each [hypergraph edge](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) of the [constraint hypergraph](constraint-hypergraph.md) becomes a [clique](clique-graph-theory.md). A [constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md) consisting only of [all-different constraints](all-different-constraint.md) on a common [constraint domain](constraint-domain.md) is exactly a [proper graph colouring](graph-coloring-split.md) problem on this [primal graph](primal-graph.md).
