# Telescoping bound for a p-series

↑ **Parent:** [P-series](p-series.md)

For $p>0$ and $n\geq2$, integrating the decreasing derivative magnitude of $x^{-p}$ gives $(n-1)^{-p}-n^{-p}=p\int_{n-1}^nx^{-p-1}dx>pn^{-p-1}$. Put $p=k-1$ and sum this [telescoping series](telescoping-series.md) to obtain the bound in the title, strict for $N>1$. The partial sums are increasing and bounded, proving convergence of the [p-series](p-series.md) when $k>1$. This gives a direct [comparison test for series](comparison-test-for-series.md) proof and a finite upper bound without evaluating the sum.
