The quotient map

$$
F_2/\gamma_4(F_2)\longrightarrow F_2/\gamma_3(F_2)
$$

is a central nonsplit extension of the [Integer Heisenberg group](integer-heisenberg-group.md) by $\gamma_3(F_2)/\gamma_4(F_2)\cong\mathbb Z^2$. It cannot split because the source has abelianization $\mathbb Z^2$, whereas a central splitting would give an abelianization containing an additional direct factor $\mathbb Z^2$.
