# Littlewood interpolation inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md)

For a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) with finite fourth moment, apply [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) with conjugate exponents $3/2$ and $3$ to $|X|^{2/3}|X|^{4/3}$. This gives $\mathbb E|X|^2\leq(\mathbb E|X|)^{2/3}(\mathbb E|X|^4)^{1/3}$. Raising to the power $3/2$ proves the displayed [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) interpolation inequality.
