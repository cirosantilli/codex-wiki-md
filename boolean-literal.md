# Boolean literal

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md)

A [Boolean literal](boolean-literal.md) is a [Boolean variable](boolean-variable.md) $u$ or its [logical negation](negation.md) $\neg u$. The two [Boolean literals](boolean-literal.md) have opposite [truth values](truth-value.md): a [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) $v$ gives $v(\neg u)=1-v(u)$ when false and true are encoded as zero and one. Thus $u=0$ makes the positive [Boolean literal](boolean-literal.md) $u$ false and the negative [Boolean literal](boolean-literal.md) $\neg u$ true. A [Boolean clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) combines [Boolean literals](boolean-literal.md), and a [Boolean-pair colouring gadget](boolean-pair-colouring-gadget.md) represents a [Boolean variable](boolean-variable.md) and its [logical negation](negation.md) by two [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) with opposite Boolean colours.
