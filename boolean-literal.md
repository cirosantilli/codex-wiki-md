# Boolean literal

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md)

A Boolean literal is a [Boolean variable](boolean-variable.md) $u$ or its negation $\neg u$. The two literals have opposite truth values. A [Boolean clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) combines literals, and a [Boolean-pair colouring gadget](boolean-pair-colouring-gadget.md) represents a variable and its negation by two vertices with opposite Boolean colours.
