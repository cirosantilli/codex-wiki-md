# Harmonic-oscillator wavefunction inflections

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

For a one-dimensional [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), let $b=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$ and $\xi=x/b$. The stationary [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) gives the displayed curvature relation. Inflections occur at [classical turning points](classical-turning-point.md) $\xi=\pm\sqrt{2n+1}$ and at simple [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) [wavefunction nodes](wavefunction-node.md), provided the curvature changes sign. Thus the first excited state has an inflection at its central [wavefunction node](wavefunction-node.md) as well as at both turning points. Restricting attention to where $V=E$ misses [wavefunction node](wavefunction-node.md) inflections.
