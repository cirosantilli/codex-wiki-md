# Source monitoring

↑ **Parent:** [Memory retrieval](memory-retrieval.md)

Attributing a remembered or experienced representation to its source, such as an external event or an internally generated action. A source-monitoring account of [voice hearing](auditory-verbal-hallucination.md) is a mechanistic hypothesis, not a definition or a uniquely established cause.
