# Haploid

↑ **Parent:** [Ploidy](ploidy.md)

A [haploid](haploid.md) [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) has one complete set of [chromosomes](chromosome.md). For the stipulated $n=3$ example, both products after [meiosis I](meiosis-i.md) and products after [meiosis II](meiosis-ii.md) are [haploid](haploid.md); their [Nuclear DNA contents](nuclear-dna-content.md) differ because the former retain associated [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md). [Haploid](haploid.md) therefore does not mean “unreplicated”.[Biology 2e by Mary Ann Clark and others; §11.1, Telophase I and Cytokinesis, final two paragraphs; Meiosis II, opening paragraph](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/the-process-of-meiosis.md)
