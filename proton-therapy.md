# Proton therapy

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation therapy](radiation-therapy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_therapy)

Treatment using proton beams with controllable finite range and a [Bragg peak](bragg-peak.md). Potential normal-tissue sparing must be assessed against [range uncertainty](range-uncertainty.md), motion and biological-effect uncertainties.
