# Newman projection

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newman_projection)

A [Newman projection](newman-projection.md) views a molecule along a chosen bond, showing substituents on the front atom and rear atom. It makes relative torsions visible without assuming that the two centres lie in the plane of a skeletal drawing.
