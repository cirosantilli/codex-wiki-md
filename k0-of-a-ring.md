# K0 of a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic K-theory](algebraic-k-theory.md)

The [Grothendieck group](grothendieck-group.md) of finitely generated projective right $A$-modules under [direct sum](direct-sum.md). An element is a formal difference $[P]-[Q]$. Equality of two positive classes means $P\oplus T\cong Q\oplus T$ for some projective $T$; adding a complement of $T$ converts this into an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) after a common finite free summand. This stabilization criterion is essential in patching arguments.
