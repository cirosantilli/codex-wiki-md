# Positive-map separability criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md)

A finite-dimensional bipartite [density operator](density-matrix.md) $\rho_{AB}$ is a [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md) exactly when $(\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\Phi)(\rho_{AB})\geq0$ for every [positive linear map](positive-linear-map.md) from operators on $B$ to operators on $A$. In combination with the [Størmer-Woronowicz decomposability theorem](stormer-woronowicz-decomposability-theorem.md), it makes the [positive partial transpose criterion](positive-partial-transpose-criterion.md) sufficient for qubit-qutrit states. The criterion is established in [the Horodeckis' separability paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9605038).
