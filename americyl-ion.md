# Americyl ion

↑ **Parent:** [Americium](americium.md)

The linear oxo species $\mathrm{AmO_2^+}$ and $\mathrm{AmO_2^{2+}}$ for Am(V) and Am(VI), respectively. The charge changes by one on a single-electron redox step.
