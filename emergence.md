# Emergence

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of physics](philosophy-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergence)

[Emergence](emergence.md) concerns novel collective patterns or explanatory structures relative to a lower-level description. Robust effective behaviour can emerge through coarse-graining or singular limits while remaining derivable from the microscopic theory. The stronger claim that genuinely new causal powers cannot be reduced to that theory requires an additional argument; nonanalyticity of a limiting function alone does not establish it.
