# Spherical degree by area pullback

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md)

For a smooth map between oriented unit [spheres](sphere.md), integrate the pullback of the target [area form](area-form.md). Localizing a normalized top-degree form near a [regular value](regular-value.md), and using [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) to discard exact-form differences, identifies this integral with the [degree as a sum of local degrees](degree-as-a-sum-of-local-degrees.md).
