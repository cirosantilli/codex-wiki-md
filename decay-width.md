# Decay width

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic quantum field](relativistic-quantum-field-split.md)

In units with $\hbar=1$, the inverse lifetime of an unstable particle. A [neutral meson mixing](neutral-meson-mixing.md) effective [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) incorporates decay through its anti-Hermitian part $-i\Gamma/2$.
