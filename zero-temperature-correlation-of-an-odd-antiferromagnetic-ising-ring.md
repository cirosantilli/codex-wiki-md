# Zero-temperature correlation of an odd antiferromagnetic Ising ring

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-ring antiferromagnetic Ising correlation](finite-ring-antiferromagnetic-ising-correlation.md)

At fixed odd $N$, the zero-[temperature](temperature-split.md) [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md) equally weights the $2N$ [ground states](ground-state.md) of an [antiferromagnetic Ising ring](antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md). The single [frustrated bond in an antiferromagnetic Ising ring](frustrated-bond-in-an-antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md) lies on a chosen $n$-bond path with [probability](probability.md) $n/N$. The [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) product is $(-1)^n$ if the path excludes that bond and $-(-1)^n$ if it includes it, proving the displayed [two-point correlation function](two-point-correlation-function.md). The loss of staggered alignment upon averaging persists at zero [temperature](temperature-split.md) and is caused by [geometric frustration](geometrical-frustration.md), rather than a positive density of thermal defects. The expression remains symmetric under $n\mapsto N-n$ for odd $N$.
