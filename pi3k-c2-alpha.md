↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[PI3K-C2α](pi3k-c2-alpha.md) is a class II [phosphoinositide](phosphoinositide.md) 3-kinase implicated in local [phosphoinositide](phosphoinositide.md) production during [endocytosis](endocytosis.md). Catalytic rescue and lipid measurements help distinguish its enzymatic role from other consequences of its depletion.
