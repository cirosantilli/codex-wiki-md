# Quantum Hall antidot

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hall edge state](quantum-hall-edge-state.md)

A depleted potential hill inside a [two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md). In a strong perpendicular [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), closed [quantum Hall edge states](quantum-hall-edge-state.md) circulate around it. Coupling these states to propagating edge channels can produce resonant backscattering; [Coulomb blockade](coulomb-blockade.md) can substantially alter the single-particle resonance pattern.
