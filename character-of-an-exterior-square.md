# Character of an exterior square

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory](representation-theory-split.md)

For any finite-dimensional complex representation,

$$
\chi_{\wedge^2V}(g)=
\frac{\chi_V(g)^2-\chi_V(g^2)}2.
$$
