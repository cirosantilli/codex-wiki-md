# Generalized Specht module

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

For a row composition $b$ and a weakly decreasing marking $a$ with $0\le a_i\le b_i$, mark the first $a_i$ cells of each row. Antisymmetrize within the marked columns and span the resulting [polytabloids](polytabloid.md) in the [Young permutation module](young-permutation-module.md) $M^b$. This gives $S^{a,b}$; $S^{0,b}=M^b$ and $S^{b,b}=S^b$ for a proper partition $b$. Its characteristic-independent [Specht filtration](specht-filtration.md) is obtained by successively marking a cell or raising an unmarked row tail, with column-cancellation maps and the good-letter counting recursion.
