# Exact quadratic normal form for a perturbed linear rotor

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical transformation](canonical-transformation.md)

For $H=\omega\cdot y+\frac12|y|^2+\epsilon V(x)$ with a [Diophantine frequency vector](diophantine-frequency-vector.md), solve $\mathcal D_\omega u=\langle V\rangle-V$ and apply [translated conjugacy of a Diophantine vector field](translated-conjugacy-of-a-diophantine-vector-field.md) to $w=\epsilon\nabla u$. Set $\eta=\epsilon\nabla u+a$ and use the [symplectic cotangent lift with a closed momentum shift](symplectic-cotangent-lift-with-a-closed-momentum-shift.md). The transformed [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is exactly $E+\omega\cdot y'+\frac12y'^T D\chi^{-1}D\chi^{-T}y'+\frac12|\eta\circ\chi|^2$, where $E=\epsilon\langle V\rangle+\omega\cdot a$. Since $a=O(\epsilon^2)$, the last term is an angular potential of order $\epsilon^2$ and the quadratic coefficient is identity plus $O(\epsilon)$. The construction is on a smaller angular strip and momentum ball.
