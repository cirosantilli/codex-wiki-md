# Accreting raindrop with quadratic drag

↑ **Parent:** [Variable-mass system](variable-mass-system.md)

For a spherical drop of constant [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, radius $a>0$ and downward [velocity](velocity.md) $v>0$, suppose its volume grows at rate $c\pi a^2v$ by collecting stationary mist and its upward [quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md) is $k\rho\pi a^2v^2$, with $c,k>0$. The drop [mass](mass.md) is $4\pi\rho a^3/3$. Its incoming [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md) and the [variable-mass system](variable-mass-system.md) momentum law give the displayed equations. The accreted matter must be accelerated from rest, producing the additional $c$ term alongside drag.
