# Mode conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave-split.md)

Transfer of [wave energy](wave-energy.md) between distinct local branches when spatial variation couples their [polarizations](polarization-waves.md) or their [dispersion relations](dispersion-relation-split.md) approach one another. Loss from one observed branch need not mean dissipation: [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) includes every outgoing branch.
