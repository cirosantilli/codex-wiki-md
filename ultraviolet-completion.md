# Ultraviolet completion

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md)

An ultraviolet completion specifies a theory valid at higher energies whose low-energy limit gives an [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md). For large-field [cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md), it may also need to control the potential over a large field range, which is distinct from the energy scale of a process.
