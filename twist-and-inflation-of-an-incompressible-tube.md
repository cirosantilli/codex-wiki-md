# Twist and inflation of an incompressible tube

↑ **Parent:** [Finite elasticity](finite-elasticity.md)

In cylindrical orthonormal bases the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) is $F=\begin{pmatrix}\rho/r&0&0\\0&r/\rho&\alpha r\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}$ and has unit [determinant](determinant.md). One [principal stretch](principal-stretch.md) is $\rho/r$; the squares of the other two are the roots of $z^2-[(r/\rho)^2+\alpha^2r^2+1]z+(r/\rho)^2=0$. The reference-volume energy per height is $E=2\pi\int\rho W\,d\rho$. [Virtual work](virtual-work.md) gives end [torque](torque.md) $M=\partial_\alpha E$ and inner pressure $p=(2\pi a)^{-1}\partial_aE$ at fixed reference radii and height.
