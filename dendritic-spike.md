# Dendritic spike

↑ **Parent:** [Synaptic integration](synaptic-integration.md)

A [dendritic spike](dendritic-spike.md) is a regenerative local voltage event in a [dendrite](dendrite.md). Its effect at the [soma](soma-biology.md) depends on propagation and attenuation; local regeneration does not by itself establish a somatic [action potential](action-potential.md). It is an active mechanism beyond the [passive dendritic cable](passive-dendritic-cable.md).
