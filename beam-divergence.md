# Beam divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Physical optics](physical-optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beam_divergence)

[Beam divergence](beam-divergence.md) describes the angular spread of a beam away from its [optical axis](optical-axis.md); specify the [radius](radius.md) convention and whether the quoted [angle](angle.md) is a half-angle or full [angle](angle.md). For the fundamental [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md) in [vacuum](vacuum.md), the far-field $1/e^2$ [light intensity](light-intensity.md) [radius](radius.md) obeys

$$
w(z)\sim\theta|z|,\qquad
\theta=\lim_{z\to\infty}\frac{w(z)}{z}=\frac{w_0}{z_R}=\frac{\lambda}{\pi w_0}.
$$

Here $\theta$ is the small-[angle](angle.md) half-angle in radians under the [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md); the full [angle](angle.md) is $2\theta$. The geometric asymptote has [angle](angle.md) $\arctan(w_0/z_R)$, approximated by $w_0/z_R$ in this regime. Other beam profiles need their own [beam divergence](beam-divergence.md) definitions and propagation laws.
