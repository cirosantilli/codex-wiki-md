# Image charge

↑ **Parent:** [Electric potential](electric-potential.md)

An image charge is a fictitious point charge placed outside the physical solution region so that its potential, combined with the real sources, enforces the required conductor boundary values. The [Uniqueness of the Dirichlet problem](uniqueness-of-the-dirichlet-problem.md) then identifies the constructed potential with the physical one. The image need not describe the actual induced surface-charge distribution.
