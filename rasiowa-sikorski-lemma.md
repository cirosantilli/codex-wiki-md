<h1 id="rasiowa-sikorski-lemma">Rasiowa–Sikorski lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Generic filter](generic-filter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rasiowa–Sikorski_lemma)

If $M$ is a [countable transitive model](countable-transitive-model.md), $\mathbb P\in M$, and $p\in\mathbb P$, enumerate the dense subsets of $\mathbb P$ belonging to $M$ and recursively choose a decreasing sequence starting below $p$ that meets each one. Its upward closure is a [generic filter](generic-filter.md) over $M$ containing $p$.
