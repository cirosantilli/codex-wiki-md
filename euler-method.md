# Euler method

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability domain](linear-stability-domain.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_method)

Explicit Euler advances $y'=f(t,y)$ by $y_{n+1}=y_n+h f(t_n,y_n)$. Its [stability function](stability-function.md) is $R(z)=1+z$.
