# Standing-and-travelling decomposition of a reflected string wave

↑ **Parent:** [Standing wave](standing-wave.md)

For real negative displacement reflection amplitude $r=-a$, with $0<a<1$, use

$$
A[\cos(kx-\omega t)-a\cos(kx+\omega t)]
=(1-a)A\cos(kx-\omega t)+2aA\sin kx\sin\omega t.
$$

This separates a travelling field and a standing field algebraically. Their individual [powers](power.md) cannot be treated as independent incident-power fractions because the two fields interfere. Reflected and transmitted [power](power.md) should instead be calculated from travelling-wave amplitudes with the appropriate [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md) factors.
