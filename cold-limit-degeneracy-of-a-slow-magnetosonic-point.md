# Cold-limit degeneracy of a slow magnetosonic point

↑ **Parent:** [Regularity at a magnetosonic point](regularity-at-a-magnetosonic-point.md)

In a strictly cold flow governed by [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md) the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) vanishes, so the [slow magnetosonic wave](slow-magnetosonic-wave.md) speed is zero. A nonzero-speed outflow therefore has no ordinary finite interior slow crossing. For the [cold radial magnetohydrodynamic wind integral](cold-radial-magnetohydrodynamic-wind-integral.md), $f_y=-\alpha(x-x^{-1})^2/(y-1)^3-\beta/(x^4y^3)<0$ on the sub-Alfvénic branch $y>1$, assuming $\beta>0$. A small retained [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) is needed to locate a finite slow point; the zero-temperature limit can move it to the launch boundary. The usual simultaneous numerator and denominator regularity conditions still apply to nondegenerate critical points.
