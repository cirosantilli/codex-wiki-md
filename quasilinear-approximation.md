# Quasilinear approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

A weak-fluctuation closure retaining the averaged quadratic fluctuation flux while linearizing the fluctuating kinetic equation about a slowly evolving mean [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md). One solves the fast linear response with the mean frozen, then inserts its covariance into the slow averaged equation. A short-memory stationary limit can yield [quasilinear diffusion](quasilinear-diffusion.md), but finite-time responses retain memory. Self-consistent collective fields enter through the [dielectric function](dielectric-function.md) in homogeneous electrostatic problems.
