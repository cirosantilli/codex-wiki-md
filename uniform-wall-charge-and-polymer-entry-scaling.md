# Uniform wall charge and polymer entry scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Electro-osmosis](electro-osmosis.md)

A homogeneous charged channel can add a constant electro-osmotic drift to the ideal electrophoretic [velocity](velocity.md) at fixed field. It changes the coefficient of a linear length-versus-entry-time law without generating a new [length](length.md) exponent. Charge-dependent adsorption, heterogeneous trapping or cancellation of drift followed by [diffusion](diffusion.md) lies outside that simple claim; wall charge cannot be excluded as an indirect cause in every real experiment.
