# Tensor-power reduction of PBR overlap

↑ **Parent:** [Pusey-Barrett-Rudolph theorem](pusey-barrett-rudolph-theorem.md)

If two pure [quantum states](quantum-state.md) overlap by $(1/\sqrt2)^{1/n}$, their $n$-copy states have overlap $1/\sqrt2$. Each block spans an effective [qubit](qubit.md), with [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $e_0=\Psi_1$ and $e_1=\sqrt2\Psi_2-\Psi_1$. Applying the two-block [PBR exclusion measurement for zero and plus](pbr-exclusion-measurement-for-zero-and-plus.md) uses $2n$ independently prepared copies. A common single-copy ontic overlap of positive mass remains positive under this finite tensor power.
