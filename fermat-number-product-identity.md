# Fermat number product identity

↑ **Parent:** [Fermat number](fermat-number.md)

For each [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$, the product of the preceding [Fermat numbers](fermat-number.md) is two less than the next:

$$
\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}F_j=2^{2^n}-1=F_n-2.
$$

For example, $3\cdot5\cdot17=255=257-2$. The identity telescopes by repeated [difference of squares](difference-of-two-squares.md): $(2^{2^j}-1)(2^{2^j}+1)=2^{2^{j+1}}-1$. Its usefulness is that a huge [integer](integer-split.md) product exposes a tiny difference, two, from the next term.
