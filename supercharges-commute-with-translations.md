# Supercharges commute with translations

↑ **Parent:** [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md)

In the minimal [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md), [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md) allows $[P^\mu,Q_\alpha]=A\sigma^\mu_{\alpha\dot\beta}\bar Q^{\dot\beta}$. The [graded Jacobi identity](graded-jacobi-identity.md) with $P^\mu,Q_\alpha,Q_\beta$ forces $A=0$. Thus each [supercharge](supersymmetry-generator.md) preserves [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) and its mass Casimir, giving [supersymmetric mass degeneracy](supersymmetric-mass-degeneracy.md) in an unbroken physical [supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md).
