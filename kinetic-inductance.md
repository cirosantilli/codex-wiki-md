# Kinetic inductance

↑ **Parent:** [Inductance](inductance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_inductance)

Inductive energy associated with the inertia of current-carrying particles. In a superconducting ring it supplies the current term in the quantized fluxoid, so geometric flux need not itself be an exact integer flux quantum when this term is appreciable.
