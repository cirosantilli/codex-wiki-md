# Dendrogram

↑ **Parent:** [Agglomerative hierarchical clustering](agglomerative-hierarchical-clustering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendrogram)

A dendrogram displays successive cluster merges, with the height of each merge equal to its intercluster dissimilarity. Cutting at a height gives a partition. [Single-linkage clustering](single-linkage-clustering.md) measures the closest cross-pair and can form chains, while [complete-linkage clustering](complete-linkage-clustering.md) measures the farthest cross-pair and favors smaller within-cluster diameters.
