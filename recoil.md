# Recoil

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md)

Recoil is the motion acquired by a body when it exchanges [momentum](momentum-split.md) with an emitted, absorbed or scattered particle. For isolated emission from a body initially at rest, [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) makes the remaining body acquire the opposite momentum. If its final [mass](mass.md) is $M$ and the recoil is nonrelativistic, its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $E_R\simeq p^2/(2M)$. A [photon](photon.md) of [energy](energy-split.md) $E_\gamma$ has momentum magnitude $E_\gamma/c$, so $E_R\simeq E_\gamma^2/(2Mc^2)$. The transition energy is shared between the photon and the recoiling body; [nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md) is an important example.
