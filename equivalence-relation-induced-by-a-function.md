# Equivalence relation induced by a function

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md)

Every [function](function-split.md) $f:X\to Y$ induces an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) on $X$ by $x\sim y$ exactly when $f(x)=f(y)$. Reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity follow from the corresponding properties of equality in $Y$; the equivalence classes are the nonempty fibers of $f$.
