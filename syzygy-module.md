# Syzygy module

↑ **Parent:** [Free resolution](free-resolution.md)

Given a [free resolution](free-resolution.md) $\cdots\to F_1\to F_0\to M\to0$, its first syzygy module is $\ker(F_0\to M)$, the [module](module-mathematics.md) of relations among the chosen generators of $M$. Higher syzygy modules are successive [kernels](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md). They depend on the choice of resolution; a [minimal graded free resolution](minimal-graded-free-resolution.md) gives canonical ranks and degrees of generators over a standard [graded ring](graded-ring.md) structure on a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md).
