# Qudit shift and phase operators

↑ **Parent:** [Qudit](qudit.md)

For an $n$-level [qudit](qudit.md) and [primitive root of unity](primitive-root-of-unity.md) $\omega$, these [unitary operators](unitary-operator.md) obey $X^n=Z^n=I$ and $ZX=\omega XZ$. The inverse of $X^sZ^{-r}$ is $Z^rX^{-s}$, with the order reversed. These operators implement the receiver corrections in [qudit teleportation](qudit-teleportation.md).
