# Inverse beta decay threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse beta decay](inverse-beta-decay.md)

The [neutron](neutron-split.md) and [Positron](positron.md) together have more rest [mass](mass.md) than the initial [proton](proton.md), so a finite incident [energy](energy-split.md) is necessary even before recoil is considered. In the [proton](proton.md)'s [rest frame](rest-frame.md), use [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) conservation and a negligible [neutrino mass](neutrino-mass.md). The initial squared center-of-mass [energy](energy-split.md) is $m_p^2c^4+2m_pc^2E_\nu$; the least final squared center-of-mass [energy](energy-split.md) is $(m_n+m_e)^2c^4$. Equating them gives the threshold above. At threshold the final particles share one center-of-mass [velocity](velocity.md); they are not both at rest in the laboratory.

Writing $\Delta=m_n-m_p$, the result separates into

$$
E_{\nu,\mathrm{thr}}=(\Delta+m_e)c^2+\frac{(\Delta+m_e)^2}{2m_p}c^2.
$$

The positive second term is the laboratory recoil correction. With rounded rest [energies](energy-split.md) $m_pc^2=938.272\,\mathrm{MeV}$, $m_nc^2=939.565\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $m_ec^2=0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$, the calculated threshold is about $1.806\,\mathrm{MeV}$. These rounded inputs follow the recommended particle [masses](mass.md).[2022 CODATA adjustment, proton, neutron and electron mass-energy equivalents in MeV](fundamental-physical-constants-complete-listing-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology-and-others.md)

As a further ideal [energy](energy-split.md) balance, suppose the [Positron](positron.md) stops and annihilates with an [electron](electron.md), and all prompt annihilation [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md) are contained. Its prompt visible [energy](energy-split.md) is $T_{e^+}+2m_ec^2$. Neglecting [neutron](neutron-split.md) recoil gives $E_{\rm vis}\simeq E_\nu-(m_n-m_p)c^2+m_ec^2\simeq E_\nu-0.782\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Escaping [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md), detector response and recoil change that measured sum. The delayed capture signal is a separate energy deposit, not an extra contribution to the incident [electron antineutrino](electron-antineutrino.md)'s prompt energy reconstruction.
