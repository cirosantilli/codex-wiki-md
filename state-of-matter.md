# State of matter

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_matter)

A state of matter is a broad category of material behavior. A [solid](solid.md) resists a sustained change of shape, a [liquid](liquid.md) flows while remaining difficult to compress, and a [gas](gas.md) expands to fill its container. These distinctions are useful first descriptions, but they do not enumerate all [thermodynamic phases](thermodynamic-phase.md). [Plasma](plasma-physics.md) adds collective behavior of charged particles, while [liquid crystals](liquid-crystal.md) show that flow and structural order need not disappear together.

Distinguish three questions: what broad state of matter does the material resemble, which [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) is present, and what are its [temperature](temperature-split.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md) and other state variables? Two samples at different [temperatures](temperature-split.md) can be in the same [liquid](liquid.md) [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md). Conversely, [crystal polymorphism](polymorphism-materials-science.md) gives different [solid](solid.md) [thermodynamic phases](thermodynamic-phase.md) of the same substance, distinguished by their [crystal structures](crystal-structure.md). A [phase transition](phase-transition.md) between them need not change the broad state-of-matter category.

For an imagined substance with crystalline forms A and B, melting A produces a [solid](solid.md)–[liquid](liquid.md) [phase transition](phase-transition.md), while converting A to B produces a [solid](solid.md)–[solid](solid.md) [phase transition](phase-transition.md). A sample containing A and B has two [thermodynamic phases](thermodynamic-phase.md) even though all its material is [solid](solid.md). Cutting one uniform crystal into two pieces does not create a second [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md): count distinct equilibrium forms, rather than pieces or grains. This distinction matters when applying the [phase rule](phase-rule.md).

A [phase diagram](phase-diagram.md) locates stable [thermodynamic phases](thermodynamic-phase.md) using [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md). Its [liquid](liquid.md)–[gas](gas.md) [phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md) ends at a [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md), around which a path connects [liquid](liquid.md) and [gas](gas.md) without a [first-order phase transition](first-order-phase-transition.md). Broad categories can therefore remain useful without a sharp boundary.[§5.1.2, printed pp. 140–141, Figures 40–41: pressure–temperature coexistence and disappearance of the liquid–gas distinction](statistical-physics-by-david-tong.md)
