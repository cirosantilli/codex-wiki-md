# Quadratic equation

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic function](quadratic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_equation)

The roots of $ax^2+bx+c=0$, where $a\ne0$, are

$$
x=\frac{-b\mathbin\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}.
$$
