# Uniformly polarized sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization density](polarization-density.md)

A sphere with constant [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) $\mathbf P=P\widehat{\mathbf z}$ has zero volume bound charge and surface bound [charge density](charge-density.md) $\sigma_b=P\cos\theta$. Integrating $\mathbf r\sigma_b\,dA$ gives the displayed [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md). The surface distribution is purely degree one in [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md), so its external static [electric potential](electric-potential.md) is exactly dipolar. Its interior field is finite and is not that of a point singularity.
