# Isotropic second moment of a ball

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic second-rank tensor](isotropic-second-rank-tensor.md)

Here $V_d$ is the [volume](volume.md) of the unit ball in $d$-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md). Rotational symmetry makes the integral an [isotropic second-rank tensor](isotropic-second-rank-tensor.md) $C\delta_{ij}$. Taking its [trace](matrix-trace.md) gives $dC=\int_{|x|\le R}|x|^2\,d^dx=dV_d\int_0^Rr^{d+1}dr$. This proves the formula. For a constant [matrix](matrix.md) $M$, contraction gives $\int x^TMx\,d^dx=V_dR^{d+2}\operatorname{tr}M/(d+2)$. In three dimensions the coefficient is $4\pi R^5/15$.
