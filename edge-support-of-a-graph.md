# Edge support of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Eulerian graph](eulerian-graph.md)

For a finite undirected [multigraph](multigraph.md) $G=(V,E)$, its [edge support of a graph](edge-support-of-a-graph.md) is the [subgraph](subgraph.md) $G_+=(V_+,E)$ with $V_+=\{v\in V:\deg(v)>0\}$. It removes exactly the [isolated vertices](isolated-vertex.md), retaining every [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md), including [self-loops](loop-graph-theory.md) and parallel [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). When $E\ne\varnothing$, connectivity of this [subgraph](subgraph.md) is the relevant condition for an [Eulerian trail](eulerian-trail.md) or [Euler circuit](euler-circuit.md).
