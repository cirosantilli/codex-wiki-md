# Polymer bridging flocculation

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

Aggregation caused when an adsorbing [polymer](polymer-split.md) chain binds more than one particle. Incomplete surface coverage can favour bridges, whereas a densely covered, well-solvated surface can instead be sterically stabilized.
