# Geselowitz formula

↑ **Parent:** [Volume conductor](volume-conductor.md)

For a homogeneous [volume conductor](volume-conductor.md) with an insulating boundary $S$, the [Geselowitz formula](geselowitz-formula.md) expresses the exterior [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) as the primary [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md) field minus a surface contribution from the induced [electric potential](electric-potential.md). For a [current dipole](current-dipole.md), $\mathbf B^p=\mu_0\mathbf Q\times(\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0)/(4\pi|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0|^3)$. On a spherical boundary, $\mathbf n'$ is parallel to $\mathbf r'$, so the surface term has zero radial component at the observation point.
