# Incentive salience

↑ **Parent:** [Motivation](motivation.md)

The motivational attractiveness assigned to a represented outcome or its cue, often described as cue-triggered wanting. It can vary without a corresponding change in experienced pleasure or explicit [belief](belief.md) about value.[Journal of Neuroscience 20, 8122–8130; separate instrumental and cue training, extinction test and taste reactivity](intra-accumbens-amphetamine-increases-the-conditioned-incentive-salience-of-sucrose-reward-by-cindy-l-wyvell-and-others.md)
