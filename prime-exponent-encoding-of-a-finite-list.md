# Prime-exponent encoding of a finite list

↑ **Parent:** [Gödel numbering](godel-numbering.md)

For a finite list $(a_0,\ldots,a_{r-1})$ of [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md), encode its length with the exponent of two and its entries with the following [prime numbers](prime-number.md): $2^r\prod_{j<r}p_{j+2}^{a_j+1}$, where $p_i$ is the $i$th [prime number](prime-number.md). [Unique prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) recovers both the length and entries. Requiring positive exponents on exactly the prescribed consecutive [primes](prime-number.md) also makes validity decidable. Nesting list descriptors therefore gives a [Gödel encoding](godel-numbering.md) of [deterministic finite automata](deterministic-finite-automaton.md) and other finite syntactic objects.
