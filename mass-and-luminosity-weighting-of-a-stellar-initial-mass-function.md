# Mass and luminosity weighting of a stellar initial mass function

↑ **Parent:** [Initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md)

If birth counts obey $dN/dm\propto m^{-s}$ and individual [luminosity](luminosity.md) scales as $m^q$, multiply by [mass](mass.md) or [luminosity](luminosity.md) and by $dm=m\,d\ln m$ to obtain the displayed logarithmic weights. With $s=2.35$ and $q=4$, birth [mass](mass.md) has weight $m^{-0.35}$ while light has weight $m^{2.65}$. Finite [mass](mass.md) cutoffs are essential; stellar lifetimes also alter the [luminosity](luminosity.md) weighting of an evolved population.
