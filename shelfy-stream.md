# Shelfy stream

↑ **Parent:** [Grounding line](grounding-line.md)

A shelfy stream is a grounded, rapidly sliding ice stream whose internal resistance is dominated by longitudinal extension, as in an [ice shelf](ice-shelf.md), but which retains a basal drag. It is described by the [shallow-shelf approximation](shallow-shelf-approximation.md).
