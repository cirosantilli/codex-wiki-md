# Zero matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_matrix)

A [matrix](matrix.md) all of whose entries are zero. It is the [additive identity](additive-identity.md) among [matrices](matrix.md) of the same dimensions and represents the zero [linear map](linear-map.md) in any choice of [bases](basis.md).
