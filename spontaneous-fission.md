# Spontaneous fission

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spontaneous_fission)

[Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md) without an incoming particle supplying the activation energy. A metastable [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) can tunnel through a [fission barrier](fission-barrier.md) along collective deformation coordinates. The rate depends exponentially on a barrier action involving its height, width and collective inertia, so positive [liquid-drop fission energy](liquid-drop-fission-energy.md) alone does not imply an observable rate.
