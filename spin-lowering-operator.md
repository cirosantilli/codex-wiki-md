# Spin lowering operator

↑ **Parent:** [Spin ladder operator](spin-ladder-operator.md)

The spin lowering operator $S_-=S_x-iS_y$ decreases the [magnetic quantum number](magnetic-quantum-number.md) by one and annihilates the minimal-weight state.
