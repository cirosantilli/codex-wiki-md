# Wall-plug efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Light-emitting diode efficiency](light-emitting-diode-efficiency.md)

The emitted optical [power](power.md) divided by electrical input [power](power.md). For the bare-device calculation in [light-emitting diode efficiency](light-emitting-diode-efficiency.md), $P_{\rm elec}=IV_f$; a complete lamp also consumes [power](power.md) in its driver. [Luminous efficacy](luminous-efficacy.md), measured in lumens per watt, additionally weights the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) by human visual sensitivity and is a different quantity.
