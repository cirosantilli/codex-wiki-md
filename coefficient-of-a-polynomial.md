# Coefficient of a polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

The [scalar](scalar.md) multiplying a specified [monomial](monomial.md) in a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). Relative to the powers $1,t,t^2,\ldots$, the [polynomial coefficients](coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md) are unique. Coefficients at powers beyond the [degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) are understood to be zero.
