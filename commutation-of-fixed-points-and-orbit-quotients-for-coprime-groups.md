# Commutation of fixed points and orbit quotients for coprime groups

↑ **Parent:** [Commutation of limits and colimits](commutation-of-limits-and-colimits.md)

For commuting actions of finite [groups](group-split.md) $G,H$ with [coprime](coprime-integers.md) orders, the canonical map $A^G/H\to(A/H)^G$ is a bijection. It is always injective. On any $G$-stable $H$-orbit, all $G$-orbits have the same size, dividing both $|G|$ and the $H$-orbit size, hence dividing $|H|$. Coprimality forces that size to be one, proving surjectivity. Thus [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) indexed by $G$ commute with [colimits](colimit.md) indexed by $H$ in the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md).
