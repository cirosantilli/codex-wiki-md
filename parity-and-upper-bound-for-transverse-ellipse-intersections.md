# Parity and upper bound for transverse ellipse intersections

↑ **Parent:** [Nondegenerate ellipse](nondegenerate-ellipse.md)

Parametrize one [ellipse](ellipse.md) as $\gamma(t)=c+A(\cos t,\sin t)$ and substitute it into the quadratic equation $Q=0$ of the other. At a [transverse intersection](transverse-intersection.md), $g(t)=Q(\gamma(t))$ has a simple zero, so its sign changes. Periodicity makes the number of zeros even. The function is a [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md) of degree at most two, so multiplication by $z^2$, with $z=e^{it}$, gives a nonzero polynomial of degree at most four. Hence there are at most four distinct intersection points. Coincident ellipses are excluded by transversality.
