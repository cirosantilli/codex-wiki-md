# Simon effect

↑ **Parent:** [Simon task](simon-task.md)

The performance difference between spatially incompatible and compatible trials in a [Simon task](simon-task.md). A group difference in overall [reaction time](reaction-time.md) is distinct from a group difference in this [conflict cost](conflict-cost.md).
