# Radial equilibrium of a thick elastic cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

An axisymmetric small radial displacement $R(r)$ gives radial [strain](strain.md) $R'$ and hoop [strain](strain.md) $R/r$. Radial force balance on an annular wedge gives $\tau_r'+(\tau_r-\tau_\phi)/r=0$. Under [plane stress](plane-stress.md), isotropic [Hooke's law](hooke-s-law.md) gives $\tau_r=E(R'+\nu R/r)/(1-\nu^2)$ and $\tau_\phi=E(R/r+\nu R')/(1-\nu^2)$. Substitution into equilibrium gives the displayed displacement equation. Its power-law exponents are $+1$ and $-1$, so $R=c_1r+c_2/r$. The hoop-stress solution is not based on treating a thick wall as a membrane.
