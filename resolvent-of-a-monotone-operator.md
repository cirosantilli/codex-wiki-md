# Resolvent of a monotone operator

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone operator](monotone-operator.md)

For an operator $A$, its resolvent with step $\tau>0$ is $J_{\tau A}=(I+\tau A)^{-1}$. A [maximal monotone operator](maximal-monotone-operator.md) has an everywhere-defined single-valued [firmly nonexpansive mapping](firmly-nonexpansive-mapping.md) as its resolvent. For $A=\partial f$, the resolvent is the [proximal map](proximal-operator.md) of $\tau f$. This monotone-operator resolvent uses a different convention from the spectral resolvent of a linear operator.
