# One-sided impulse torque on a disk

↑ **Parent:** [Satellite impulse normalization](satellite-impulse-normalization.md)

Multiply encounter mass flux $\Sigma(x)Sx\,dx$ by specific angular-momentum gain $r_0\Delta v_y$. The net disk [torque](torque.md) replaces $\Sigma(x)$ by $\Sigma(x)-\Sigma(-x)$, so a symmetric [mass density](density.md) gives zero net [torque](torque.md). For constant [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md), [Keplerian shear](keplerian-shear.md) and the full-flyby value $\chi=2$, the one-sided coefficient is $8/27$ in $q^2\Sigma r_0^4\Omega^2(r_0/H)^3$, matching [the primary coplanar impulse normalization](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/468/4/4610/3098191).
