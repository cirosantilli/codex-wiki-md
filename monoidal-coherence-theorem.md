# Monoidal coherence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Monoidal category](monoidal-category.md)

Every diagram formed solely from the canonical [associators](associator.md), [unitors](unitor.md) and their inverses commutes. Consequently structural reparenthesizations can be suppressed in calculations; this statement does not make distinct [braidings](braiding.md) equal.
