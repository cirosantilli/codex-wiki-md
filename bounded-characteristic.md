# Bounded characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Nevanlinna class](nevanlinna-class.md)

A [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) has bounded characteristic when $\sup_{r<1}\int\log^+|f(r\xi)|\,dm(\xi)<\infty$, where $\log^+t=\max(0,\log t)$. Thus it belongs to the [Nevanlinna class](nevanlinna-class.md). A general [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) requires the additional pole-counting term in its characteristic; the displayed analytic definition has no such term.
