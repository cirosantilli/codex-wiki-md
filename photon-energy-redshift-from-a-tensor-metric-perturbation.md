# Photon energy redshift from a tensor metric perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor cosmological perturbation](tensor-cosmological-perturbation.md)

For a [tensor cosmological perturbation](tensor-cosmological-perturbation.md) $h_{ij}$, the time [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) in a local [orthonormal tetrad](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) gives $d\log\epsilon/d\eta=-\dot h_{ij}e^ie^j/2$. The photon comoving energy removes the background redshift; the dot is a partial time derivative, whereas the energy derivative follows the ray.
