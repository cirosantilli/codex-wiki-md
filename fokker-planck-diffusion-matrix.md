# Fokker-Planck diffusion matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md)

The diffusion matrix is the infinitesimal second jump moment, $B_{ij}(x)=\sum_r r_ir_jW(x,r)$. It is symmetric and positive semidefinite and multiplies the second-derivative term of the [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md).
