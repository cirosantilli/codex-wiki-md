# Rao-Blackwell identity for a fixed-denominator rejection estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Importance sampling](importance-sampling.md)

For rejection indicators with conditional acceptance $f(X_i)/(Mg(X_i))$, the conditional expectation of $(M/n)\sum_iI_i\phi(X_i)$ given all proposals is the unnormalized importance estimator. The [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md) reduces variance relative to that fixed-denominator estimator. It gives no such automatic comparison with the accepted-sample mean, whose denominator is the random count.
