# Independent-spin magnetic entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-one-half paramagnetic magnetization](spin-one-half-paramagnetic-magnetization.md)

For independent moments with magnetic energies $\mp\mu B$, $Z=(2\cosh x)^N$ and $S=Nk_B[\ln(2\cosh x)-x\tanh x]$, where $x=\mu B/(k_BT)$. This counts the magnetic orientations only, excluding translational and lattice [entropy](entropy-split.md).
