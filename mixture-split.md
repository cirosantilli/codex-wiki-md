# Mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixture)

A [mixture](mixture-split.md) contains two or more [chemical substances](chemical-substance-split.md) combined in proportions that need not be fixed. A [chemical compound](chemical-compound.md) instead has a definite composition: [water](water.md) has two [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) per [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md), whereas a [sucrose](sucrose.md) and [water](water.md) [mixture](mixture-split.md) can contain different sugar proportions. A homogeneous [mixture](mixture-split.md) has uniform composition on the scale of representative samples; a heterogeneous [mixture](mixture-split.md) has distinguishable regions of different composition. A [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) is homogeneous, whereas sand dispersed in [water](water.md) is a [particle suspension](suspension-chemistry-split.md).[Chemistry 2e, §1.2, Classification of Matter: mixtures, variable composition, homogeneous and heterogeneous examples](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/1-2-phases-and-classification-of-matter.md)

The distinction concerns the sample, not merely its ingredient list. A vessel with dissolved [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) and excess undissolved [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) contains a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) and an [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md) [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md). The [liquid](liquid.md) portion can be homogeneous while the whole vessel is heterogeneous. Similarly, [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) dissolved in [water](water.md) is part of a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md); visible [gas](gas.md) bubbles introduce a separate [gas](gas.md) [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md).
