# Retinal

↑ **Parent:** [Chromophore](chromophore.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retinal)

A light-absorbing aldehyde related to vitamin A, used as a [chromophore](chromophore.md) in rhodopsin-family [proteins](protein-split.md). Its local packing and bonding connect light absorption to structural changes.
