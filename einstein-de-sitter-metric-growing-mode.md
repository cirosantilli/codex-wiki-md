# Einstein-de Sitter metric growing mode

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronous gauge in cosmology](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md)

For the scalar metric variable satisfying $\ddot h+(\dot R/R)\dot h=4\pi G\bar\rho_mR^2h$, the [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) has $\dot R/R=2/\tau$ and $4\pi G\bar\rho_mR^2=6/\tau^2$. The indicial equation is $p(p-1)+2p=6$, giving exponents $2$ and $-3$. Growth of this metric variable is not growth of every gauge-invariant potential; the growing dust density contrast is compatible with constant scalar gravitational potentials.
