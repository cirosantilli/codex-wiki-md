# Image factorization in an abelian category

↑ **Parent:** [Image factorization](image-factorization.md)

For a [morphism](morphism.md) in an [abelian category](abelian-category.md), its [coimage](coimage.md) $\operatorname{coker}(\ker f)$ is canonically isomorphic to its image $\ker(\operatorname{coker}f)$. This yields an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md) followed by a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md), unique up to unique compatible [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). Every epi-mono factorization has this same middle object.
