# Twisted Dirac operator

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac operator](dirac-operator.md)

On a [spin manifold](spin-manifold.md), tensor the [spinor bundle](spinor-bundle.md) $S$ with a [Hermitian vector bundle](hermitian-vector-bundle.md) $W$ carrying a [unitary connection](unitary-connection.md). Composing the induced tensor-product [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) with [Clifford multiplication](clifford-multiplication.md) defines the twisted Dirac operator. In even dimension it maps $S^+\otimes W$ to $S^-\otimes W$ and conversely. On a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) its chiral restriction $D_W^+:H^1(S^+\otimes W)\to L^2(S^-\otimes W)$ is a [Fredholm operator](fredholm-operator.md).
