# Abelian subgroup cannot have prime-power index in a nonabelian simple group

↑ **Parent:** [Prime-power conjugacy-class obstruction to simplicity](prime-power-conjugacy-class-obstruction-to-simplicity.md)

If an abelian [subgroup](subgroup.md) $A$ has index $p^a$, the [centralizer](centralizer.md) of any $1\ne g\in A$ contains $A$, so the class size of $g$ divides $p^a$. The [prime-power conjugacy-class obstruction to simplicity](prime-power-conjugacy-class-obstruction-to-simplicity.md) rules this out. If $A=1$, the group is a [p-group](p-group.md) and has nontrivial [center of a group](center-of-a-group.md), also ruling out nonabelian simplicity.
