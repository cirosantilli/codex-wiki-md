# Computation tree logic

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal logic](temporal-logic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computation_tree_logic)

A branching [temporal logic](temporal-logic.md) whose operators quantify over [paths in a transition system](path-in-a-transition-system.md) from a [state](state-computer-science.md). In particular, $AG\Phi$, also written $\forall\square\Phi$, holds at $s$ when every infinite [path in a transition system](path-in-a-transition-system.md) from $s$ satisfies $\Phi$ at every position, including its initial position.
