# Satisfiable first-order theory

↑ **Parent:** [First-order theory](first-order-theory.md)

A set of first-order sentences is satisfiable when it has a nonempty structure in which all its sentences are true. Truth of a sentence is independent of [assignment in a first-order structure](assignment-in-a-first-order-structure.md). The [soundness theorem for first-order logic](soundness-theorem-for-first-order-logic.md) gives consistency from satisfiability, and [Gödel's completeness theorem](godel-s-completeness-theorem.md) gives the converse for the standard classical deductive system.
