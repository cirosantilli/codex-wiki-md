# MSc in nanotechnology for medicine and health care course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-nanotechnology-for-medicine-and-health-care).

Three compulsory modules: the wider context of [nanotechnology](nanotechnology.md); the fundamental science of [nanotechnology](nanotechnology.md); fundamental characterisation for [nanotechnology](nanotechnology.md). Three compulsory Oxford modules: introduction to bionanotechnology; nanomedicine: science and applications; clinical translation and commercialisation of nanomedicine. Approximately eighteen weeks of original research, written up as a dissertation.
