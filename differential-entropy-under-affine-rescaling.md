# Differential entropy under affine rescaling

↑ **Parent:** [Differential entropy](differential-entropy.md)

For real $a\ne0$ and $b$, the [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) gives $f_{aX+b}(ax+b)=f_X(x)/|a|$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{h_e(aX+b)=h_e(X)+\ln|a|.}
$$

The absolute value also covers a decreasing transformation. If a numerical length changes from metres to centimetres, $a=100$ and the [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md) changes by $\ln100\approx4.60517$ [nats](nat-unit.md), although the physical uncertainty is the same. The reference coordinate scale must therefore accompany an absolute [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md) value.
