# Squarefree discriminant certifies an integral basis

↑ **Parent:** [Integral basis](integral-basis.md)

The [discriminant](discriminant.md) of an integral full-rank lattice is the field [discriminant](discriminant.md) multiplied by the square of its index in the ring of integers. A squarefree lattice [discriminant](discriminant.md) therefore forces index one.
