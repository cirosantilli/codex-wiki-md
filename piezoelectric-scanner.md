# Piezoelectric scanner

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

A positioner using the converse effect of [piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md) to move a probe or specimen. Its calibrated motion can form an [atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md) height image under [closed-loop control](closed-loop-control.md). [piezoelectric hysteresis](piezoelectric-hysteresis.md), [piezoelectric scanner creep](piezoelectric-scanner-creep.md), travel and control bandwidth limit faithful motion.
