# DNA bridging

↑ **Parent:** [Protein–DNA interaction](protein-dna-interaction.md)

Simultaneous connection of separated [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) segments by a [protein](protein-split.md) assembly. Intramolecular bridging produces a loop; intermolecular bridging connects distinct molecules.
