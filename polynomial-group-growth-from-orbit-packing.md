# Polynomial group growth from orbit packing

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial growth of a group](polynomial-growth-of-a-group.md)

A [properly discontinuous group action](properly-discontinuous-group-action.md) by [isometries](isometry.md) of a [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) on a complete $n$-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) of nonnegative [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md) yields the displayed group growth bound. Distinct orbit points are uniformly separated, while a word of length $m$ moves a basepoint by at most $Dm$. Pack disjoint equal-volume small balls about the orbit points into $B(p,Dm+\varepsilon)$ and apply the [Bishop-Gromov inequality](bishop-gromov-inequality.md). A finite [point stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md) contributes its order to the bound; freeness is unnecessary.
