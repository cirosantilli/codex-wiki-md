# Congruence on a category

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)

A congruence on a [category](category-split.md) $\mathcal C$ is an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) on each hom-set that is compatible with composition on both sides. The quotient category $\mathcal C/{\sim}$ has the same objects and equivalence classes of morphisms, with composition induced from $\mathcal C$.
