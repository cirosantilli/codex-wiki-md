# Landau-level broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Landau level](landau-level.md)

Disorder turns ideal sharp [Landau levels](landau-level.md) into bands of states. In the integer [quantum Hall effect](quantum-hall-effect.md), localized tails and extended states near level centres play different transport roles; a [mobility gap](mobility-gap.md) need not be an empty [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md).
