# Diuretic therapy

↑ **Parent:** [Medicine](medicine.md)

Treatment intended to increase urine output. Current guidance uses monitored [diuretic therapy](diuretic-therapy.md) for [acute heart failure](acute-heart-failure.md); it is distinct from loss of [blood](blood.md) through [venesection](venesection.md). [CG187 §§1.3.3–1.3.5](acute-heart-failure-by-nice.md)
