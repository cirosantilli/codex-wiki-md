# Cavitation

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid pressure](fluid-pressure.md)

Cavitation is the formation of gas or vapour cavities when a liquid's [pressure](pressure.md) becomes sufficiently low. A [journal bearing](journal-bearing.md) model with [cavitation](cavitation.md) must specify how a partly filled film and its boundaries behave, so periodic [pressure](pressure.md) alone cannot determine the flow there.
