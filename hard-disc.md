# Hard disc

↑ **Parent:** [Hard sphere](hard-sphere.md)

The two-dimensional counterpart of a [hard sphere](hard-sphere.md): two equal discs of radius $r$ cannot have centre separation less than $2r$. Their pair-excluded area is $4\pi r^2$, but the second-virial excluded-area parameter per particle is half that value because a pair would otherwise be counted twice.
