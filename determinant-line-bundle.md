# Determinant line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

The top [exterior power](exterior-power.md) of a rank-$r$ [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) is its [determinant line bundle](determinant-line-bundle.md). A [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) of [vector bundles](vector-bundle.md) gives a canonical isomorphism of the middle [determinant](determinant.md) with the [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of the other two. Locally, a subbundle frame followed by lifts of a quotient frame defines it; changing lifts only adds off-diagonal blocks. This is the [determinant](determinant.md) step in [adjunction for a smooth submanifold](adjunction-for-a-smooth-submanifold.md).
