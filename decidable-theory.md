# Decidable theory

↑ **Parent:** [First-order theory](first-order-theory.md)

A [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) whose consequences can be decided by an [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) that terminates on every input [first-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md). Decidability of a chosen axiom set alone does not ensure decidability of its consequences.
