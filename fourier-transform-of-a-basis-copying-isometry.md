# Fourier transform of a basis-copying isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Basis-copying isometry](basis-copying-isometry.md)

For the normalized [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md) [basis](basis.md) over $\mathbb Z_N$, products of coefficient [functions](function-split.md) satisfy $f_mf_n=N^{-1/2}f_{m+n}$. Taking adjoints of the multiplication rule for the [basis-copying isometry](basis-copying-isometry.md) gives the displayed identity, with addition modulo $N$. Its $N$ output terms are orthonormal, so the prefactor is required by [norm](norm.md) preservation. Each reduced [quantum state](quantum-state.md) is $I/N$, making the output a [maximally entangled state](maximally-entangled-state.md) for $N\geq2$. This explains concretely why copying one [basis](basis.md) is incompatible with copying its Fourier [basis](basis.md).
