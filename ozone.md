# Ozone

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozone)

A triatomic form of [oxygen](oxygen.md), produced photochemically from [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md). The [ozone layer](ozone-layer.md) absorbs ultraviolet radiation; ozone can trace atmospheric oxygen without being directly emitted by metabolism.
