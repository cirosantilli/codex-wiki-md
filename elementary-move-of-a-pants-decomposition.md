# Elementary move of a pants decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Pants decomposition](pants-decomposition.md)

An elementary move replaces one cuff inside the four-holed sphere or one-holed torus obtained by uniting its adjacent pants. The replacement is an essential curve meeting the old cuff minimally. It changes the [pants decomposition graph](pants-decomposition-graph.md) without changing the underlying surface; the identity surface map identifies the two descriptions.
