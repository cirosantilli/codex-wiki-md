# Convex lens

↑ **Parent:** [Lens (optics)](lens-optics.md)

A lens thicker near its centre than at its edge. It is converging in the usual case of glass in air, but the [refractive index](refractive-index.md) relative to the surroundings matters; shape alone is not a medium-independent power specification.
