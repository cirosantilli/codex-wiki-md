# Completed localization of an adic ring

↑ **Parent:** [Adic topology](adic-topology.md)

For a complete Noetherian [ring](ring.md) with ideal of definition $I$, completed localization is the completion of the [localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md) $A_f$ in the induced [adic topology](adic-topology.md). It gives the sections of the [formal spectrum](formal-spectrum.md) over $D(f)$. For $A=k[t][[q]]$, its completed localization at $t$ is $k[t,t^{-1}][[q]]$. Ordinary localization allows a bounded pole order in $t$ across all coefficients; the latter [ring](ring.md) also contains $\sum_{n\geq0}q^nt^{-n}$, whose pole orders are unbounded.
