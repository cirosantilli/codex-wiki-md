# Rattling mode

↑ **Parent:** [Optical phonon](optical-phonon.md)

A rattling mode is a relatively soft, often localized vibration of a guest atom within a more rigid surrounding cage. An approximately harmonic narrow-frequency contribution can be modeled as an [Einstein solid](einstein-solid.md) term with frequency $\Omega$ and degeneracy $g$, giving the heat capacity in the title. It freezes out exponentially at low temperature and approaches $gR$ per mole at high temperature, so it can bend a plot of $C/T$ versus $T^2$ away from its pure electronic-plus-acoustic straight line.

Calorimetry of [Ba4Na2Ge25](ba4na2ge25.md) shows an additional low-energy contribution fitted by an Einstein energy $\hbar\Omega/k_B\simeq32\ \mathrm K$, assigned to a barium guest site.[Page 247003-3, Figure 3](superconductivity-in-the-filled-cage-compounds-ba6ge25-and-ba4na2ge25-by-friedrich-malte-grosche-and-others.md) This is a measured-material example; the narrow-mode description remains an approximation to a crystal's full vibrational spectrum.
