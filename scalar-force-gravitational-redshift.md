# Scalar-force gravitational redshift

↑ **Parent:** [Flat-background scalar-force theory of gravity](flat-background-scalar-force-theory-of-gravity.md)

For a [photon](photon.md) in the [flat-background scalar-force theory of gravity](flat-background-scalar-force-theory-of-gravity.md), $e^\Phi p^i$ is constant along its path. In a static field, energies measured by stationary laboratories therefore satisfy the displayed ratio. At first order, $\Delta\nu/\nu=-\Delta\Phi$ in units $c=1$, matching the weak-field [Pound-Rebka experiment](pound-rebka-experiment.md). This agreement does not imply light bending: a momentum change parallel to the original null tangent changes its scale, not its direction.
