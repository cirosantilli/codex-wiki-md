# Cohomology-sheaf exact sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology sheaf](cohomology-sheaf.md)

A short exact sequence of [cochain complexes](cochain-complex.md) of [sheaves](sheaf-mathematics.md) $0\to E\to F\to G\to0$ induces $\cdots\to\mathcal H^i(E)\to\mathcal H^i(F)\to\mathcal H^i(G)\to\mathcal H^{i+1}(E)\to\cdots$. Taking stalks is exact, so the construction and exactness follow from the ordinary long exact sequence of [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of complexes at every stalk. The stalkwise connecting maps come from the lift-and-differentiate construction and form a [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) morphism.
