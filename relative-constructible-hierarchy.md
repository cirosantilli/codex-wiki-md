# Relative constructible hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Relative constructible universe](relative-constructible-universe.md)

For a [transitive set](transitive-set.md) $X$, use $L_0(X)=X$, $L_{\alpha+1}(X)=\operatorname{Def}(L_\alpha(X))$ and unions at nonzero [limit ordinals](limit-ordinal.md). The successor operation is the [definable power set](definable-power-set-split.md). Each level is transitive and the levels increase; moreover $X\in L_1(X)$. Their union is $L(X)$, an [inner model](inner-model.md) of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md). The initial-set convention differs from a hierarchy using a predicate, or one starting at $X\cup\{X\}$. [Axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md) need not hold for an arbitrary base without an internally available [well-order](well-order.md).
