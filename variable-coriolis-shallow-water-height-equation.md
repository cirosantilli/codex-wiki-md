# Variable-Coriolis shallow-water height equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized shallow water equations](linearized-shallow-water-equations.md)

On a [beta plane](beta-plane.md) with $f=f_0+\beta y$, set $\mathcal F_y=\partial_t^2+f(y)^2$. Exact elimination of the [depth-integrated shallow-water transports](depth-integrated-shallow-water-transport.md) gives

$$
\mathcal F_y\left[\nabla_h^2\eta-\frac{\mathcal F_y\eta}{c^2}\right]_t=\beta(\eta_{xtt}+2f\eta_{yt}-f^2\eta_x).
$$

The essential commutator is $\partial_y(\mathcal F_yV)=\mathcal F_yV_y+2f\beta V$. Freezing undifferentiated $f$ factors to $f_0$ is a local approximation. The slow-wave limit gives the [shallow-water Rossby-wave dispersion relation](shallow-water-rossby-wave-dispersion-relation.md) with a negative zonal phase velocity for $\beta>0$.
