# Rank-zero elliptic curve with roots zero three and four

↑ **Parent:** [Two-isogeny descent](two-isogeny-descent.md)

For this [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md), the [two-isogeny formula](two-isogeny-formula.md) gives the partner $E':Y^2=X^3+14X^2+X$. The [prime-support bound in two-isogeny descent](prime-support-bound-in-two-isogeny-descent.md) and positivity of nonzero rational $x$ restrict the first [square class](square-class.md) image to $1,2,3,6$. The identity and the [rational points](rational-point.md) $(2,2),(0,0),(6,6)$ realize these classes. The partner image is contained in $\{1,-1\}$, and the [negative unit obstruction for the fourteen-coefficient isogeny cover](negative-unit-obstruction-for-the-fourteen-coefficient-isogeny-cover.md) excludes $-1$. The [two-isogeny rank formula](square-class-index-formula-for-two-isogeny-descent.md) therefore gives $2^r=4\cdot1/4=1$.
