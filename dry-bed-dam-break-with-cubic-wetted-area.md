# Dry-bed dam break with cubic wetted area

↑ **Parent:** [Prismatic-channel shallow water equations](prismatic-channel-shallow-water-equations.md)

A channel with width $b(z)=\alpha z^2$ has wetted area $A=\alpha h^3/3$. Its inviscid [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md) have characteristic speeds $u\pm c$ and the displayed [Riemann invariants](riemann-invariant.md). Releasing a reservoir of depth $H$ at rest into a dry channel gives a left-family [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md): $c=(6c_0-x/t)/7$, $u=6(c_0+x/t)/7$, $h/H=[(6-x/(c_0t))/7]^2$ between $x=-c_0t$ and $x=6c_0t$, where $c_0=\sqrt{gH/3}$. This width law differs from a geometrically parabolic trough, whose width is proportional to $\sqrt z$.
