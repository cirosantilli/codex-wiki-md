# Phases of Venus

↑ **Parent:** [Observational astronomy](observational-astronomy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phases_of_Venus)

The changing illuminated fraction of [Venus](venus.md) seen from the [Earth](earth.md). A full sequence including gibbous phases excludes the classical near-side Ptolemaic arrangement but is compatible with both a [heliocentric model](heliocentric-model.md) and a [Tychonic system](tychonic-system.md).
