# Bianchi identities from covariant derivative commutators

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md)

The [first Bianchi identity](first-bianchi-identity.md) follows by applying cyclic curvature commutators to the gradient of an arbitrary [scalar field](scalar-field.md): its symmetric Hessian makes the third-derivative terms cancel, leaving $R_{abc}{}^d+R_{bca}{}^d+R_{cab}{}^d=0$. For the [second Bianchi identity](second-bianchi-identity.md), write the vector-bundle derivative as $D_a=\partial_a+\Gamma_a$, so $[D_b,D_c]=\mathcal R_{bc}$. The operator [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) gives the cyclic sum of $\partial_a\mathcal R_{bc}+[\Gamma_a,\mathcal R_{bc}]$ equal to zero. Restoring the connection terms on the two curvature-form indices gives precisely $\nabla_aR_{bcd}{}^e+\nabla_bR_{cad}{}^e+\nabla_cR_{abd}{}^e=0$: the additional terms cancel because the connection is torsion-free and curvature is antisymmetric in its first pair.
