# Constant-flux floating extensional gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Floating extensional viscous gravity current](floating-extensional-viscous-gravity-current.md)

With fixed inlet thickness $h_0$ and [velocity](velocity.md) $u_0$, and nose tension $4\mu h_Nu_x=\rho g'h_N^2/2$, integrating momentum gives $u_x=\rho g'h/(8\mu)$. For a slice injected at $t_0$, write $\tau=t-t_0$ and $T=8\mu/(\rho g'h_0)$. Its thickness, [velocity](velocity.md) and position are $h=h_0/(1+\tau/T)$, $u=u_0(1+\tau/T)$, and $x=u_0[\tau+\tau^2/(2T)]$. Eliminating its age gives the steady profile behind the nose, while the nose is the slice with the earliest injection time.
