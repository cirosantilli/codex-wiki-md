# Spin multiplicity

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_multiplicity)

A state with total [spin angular momentum](spin.md) quantum number $S$ has $2S+1$ magnetic projections. A [singlet state](singlet-state.md) has $S=0$, a [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) has $S=1$, and the $S=3$ [chromium](chromium.md) ground term is a septet.
