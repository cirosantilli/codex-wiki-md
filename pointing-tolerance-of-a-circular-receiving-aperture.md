# Pointing tolerance of a circular receiving aperture

↑ **Parent:** [Antenna beam pattern](antenna-beam-pattern.md)

For a uniformly illuminated circular aperture of diameter D, normalized received [power](power.md) is $[2J_1(x)/x]^2$ with $x=\pi D\sin\theta/\lambda$. The first null has $\sin\theta=1.21967\lambda/D$ and the half-power point has $\sin\theta\simeq0.5145\lambda/D$. A pointing tolerance must name the tolerated [power](power.md) loss; the first-null angle is not a good low-loss tolerance. These estimates require an ideal aperture pattern and are only approximate for a dish a few [wavelengths](wavelength.md) across.
