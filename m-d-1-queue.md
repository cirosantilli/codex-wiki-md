<h1 id="m-d-1-queue">M/D/1 queue</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [M/G/1 queue](m-g-1-queue.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M/D/1_queue)

An [M/D/1 queue](m-d-1-queue.md) has a [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) of arrivals, a deterministic service time $\tau$, and one work-conserving server. With arrival rate $\lambda$ and $\lambda\tau<1$, its equilibrium idle probability is $1-\lambda\tau$: the stationary completed work per unit time is $\lambda\tau$. Its output generally need not be a [Poisson process](poisson-process.md).
