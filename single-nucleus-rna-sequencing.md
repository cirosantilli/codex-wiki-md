# Single-nucleus RNA sequencing

↑ **Parent:** [RNA sequencing](rna-sequencing.md)

Profiling [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) from individual isolated [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) preparations. The preparation samples nuclear and often incompletely processed transcripts, so its [intron](intron.md) counts are not interchangeable with mature cytoplasmic [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) counts.
