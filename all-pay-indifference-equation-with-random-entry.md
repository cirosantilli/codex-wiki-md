# All-pay indifference equation with random entry

↑ **Parent:** [Simultaneous all-pay contests](simultaneous-all-pay-contests.md)

For a prize of value $w$, let $G(b)$ be the probability that a rival is absent or enters with effort at most $b$. When rivals act independently and have no atoms at positive efforts, effort $b>0$ wins with probability $G(b)^{n-1}$. If a player mixes over an interval of [best responses](best-response.md), its payoff on that interval is constant, so $G(b)=((b+u)/w)^{1/(n-1)}$. If the absence probability is $q$ and the active [effort support](effort-support.md) starts at zero, then $u=wq^{n-1}$.
