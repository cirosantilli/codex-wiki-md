# Outgoing pulse redshift from a radial Schwarzschild infaller

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md)

A radial infalling [geodesic](geodesic.md) in [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) has $dt/ds=E/f$ and $dr/ds=-\sqrt{E^2-f}$, where $f=1-2M/r$. An outward [radial null geodesic](radial-null-geodesic.md) has fixed retarded time $u=t-r_*$ with $dr_*/dr=1/f$. Consequently $du/ds=(E+\sqrt{E^2-f})/f$. This factor stretches a short emitted pulse's duration, and its reciprocal multiplies the received frequency at infinity. Near horizon crossing $s_H$, it becomes $du/ds\sim4M/(s_H-s)$, including both [gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md) and the emitter's [Doppler shift](doppler-effect.md). The local short-pulse approximation requires its emitted duration to be much smaller than $s_H-s$.
