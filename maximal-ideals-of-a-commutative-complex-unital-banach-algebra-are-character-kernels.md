# Maximal ideals of a commutative complex unital Banach algebra are character kernels

↑ **Parent:** [Character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md)

In a commutative complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), a [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) is closed: its closure remains proper, since an element within distance less than one of the identity is invertible by the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md). The quotient is a complex division [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), hence is the complex [field](field.md) by the [Gelfand-Mazur theorem](gelfand-mazur-theorem.md). Its quotient map is a [character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md). Conversely a nonzero multiplicative complex [linear functional](linear-functional.md) has value one at the identity, is onto the complex [field](field.md), and has maximal kernel. It is automatically continuous because its value at each element belongs to that element's spectrum. The kernel determines the [algebra character](character-of-an-algebra.md) uniquely since the quotient is one-dimensional.
