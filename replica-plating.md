# Replica plating

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replica_plating)

[Replica plating](replica-plating.md) transfers samples from corresponding [biological colonies](colony-biology.md) or patches to several growth conditions while preserving their spatial arrangement. A master plate makes it possible to relate growth or failure at one location to the same source population on another plate.

The master preserves the source [biological colonies](colony-biology.md); each replica tests a sample of their [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). A fabric carrier takes one spatial impression from the master and deposits samples onto fresh plates. Different [cell culture medium](cell-culture-medium.md) formulations can then reveal different growth [phenotypes](phenotype.md) at corresponding sites. The carrier copies locations, not a guaranteed number of [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). [Replica plating spatial correspondence](replica-plating-spatial-correspondence.md) therefore lets an investigator connect a readout to a recoverable source without treating every empty replica site as a missing master [biological colony](colony-biology.md).

The left panel separates the inverted plate, fabric pile, retaining hoop and support for clarity. The right panel shows aligned plate views after incubation; the fabric holds an imprint rather than growing the pictured [biological colonies](colony-biology.md). Arrows show sample transfer. The dotted return arrow shows using a location to recover material from the unexposed master, not transferring material back from a challenged replica. Sizes and growth outcomes are schematic.

The useful trick is that a destructive or growth-restricting test can interrogate one sample while another remains available. In [indirect selection of bacterial mutants](indirect-selection-of-bacterial-mutants.md), the replica supplies information about which source to recover. This also makes a failure-to-grow [phenotype](phenotype.md) accessible: a missing replica [biological colony](colony-biology.md) cannot be picked, but its source may still be present on the master. Matching positions is the bridge between testing and retrieval.
