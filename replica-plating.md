# Replica plating

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replica_plating)

[Replica plating](replica-plating.md) transfers samples from corresponding [biological colonies](colony-biology.md) or patches to several growth conditions while preserving their spatial arrangement. A master plate makes it possible to relate growth or failure at one location to the same source population on another plate.
