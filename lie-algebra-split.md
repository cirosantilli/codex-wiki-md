# Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie theory](lie-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_algebra)

A Lie algebra over a [field](field.md) is a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $\mathfrak g$ with a bilinear operation $[x,y]$, called the [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md), that is alternating and satisfies the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md).
