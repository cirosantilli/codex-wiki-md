# Finite-width quantum-well exciton

↑ **Parent:** [Wannier-Mott exciton](wannier-mott-exciton.md)

In the envelope approximation, a [quantum well](quantum-well.md) confines [electron](electron.md) and [hole](electron-hole.md) motion perpendicular to the plane. Averaging their screened attraction over the confinement envelopes gives the displayed effective in-plane potential. Its narrow-well limit approaches the ideal [two-dimensional exciton](two-dimensional-exciton.md); finite width softens the short-distance attraction. Dielectric mismatch, nonparabolic bands and strong [electron](electron.md)–[hole](electron-hole.md) confinement mixing require more than this single-subband model.
