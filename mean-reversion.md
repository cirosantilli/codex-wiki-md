# Mean reversion

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md)

Mean reversion is a tendency for a [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) to move back towards a reference level. For an [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md), the [conditional expected value](conditional-expectation.md) of a future deviation is the present deviation multiplied by $e^{-\theta h}$ after time $h$. Individual [sample paths](sample-path.md) can move away from that level: mean reversion describes a conditional tendency, not monotone motion or a guaranteed return by a deadline. It also does not by itself imply a [stationary process](stationary-process.md); the initial [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) matters.
