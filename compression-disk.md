# Compression disk

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible surface](incompressible-surface.md)

A compression disk for a two-sided embedded [surface](topological-surface.md) $F$ in a [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) is an embedded disk meeting $F$ exactly in its boundary, with boundary essential in $F$. Replacing an annular neighborhood of this boundary by two parallel copies of the disk reduces the surface's genus or separates it into simpler pieces.
