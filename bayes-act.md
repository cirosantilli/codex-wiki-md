# Bayes act

↑ **Parent:** [Bayes risk](bayes-risk.md)

A Bayes act minimizes $\int L(\theta,a)\Pi(d\theta)$ for a specified parameter distribution $\Pi$. Applying the same minimization to the posterior after observing data gives a [Bayes decision rule](bayes-decision-rule.md). The optimal data-based [Bayes risk](bayes-risk.md) is the expectation of the optimal posterior no-data loss.
