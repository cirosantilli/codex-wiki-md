# Breit-Wigner resonance cross section

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering resonance](scattering-resonance.md)

For an isolated [particle resonance](particle-resonance.md) of [spin](spin.md) $J_R$, the Breit-Wigner approximation relates a production-and-decay [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) to its partial [decay widths](decay-width.md). In [natural units](natural-units.md), $p_i$ is either incident particle's momentum magnitude in the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md), $g=(2J_R+1)/[(2J_a+1)(2J_b+1)]$ for unpolarized incident particles, and $\Gamma$ is the total width. The formula assumes the stated spin counting and neglects nonresonant backgrounds and their interference. Multiplication by $(\hbar c)^2$ converts an inverse-squared-energy cross section to area when momenta are expressed in energy units.
