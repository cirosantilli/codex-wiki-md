# Gaussian phase-screen correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Random phase screen](random-phase-screen.md)

Let $W(z)$ be a stationary real [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) of mean zero and [variance](variance-split.md) one, with [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md) $\rho(\zeta)$. The [random phase screen](random-phase-screen.md) $E(z)=e^{i\beta W(z)}$ has uncentered correlation $C_E(\zeta)=\mathbb E[E(z+\zeta)\overline{E(z)}]=e^{-\beta^2(1-\rho(\zeta))}$. This follows from the [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) of the Gaussian difference, whose [variance](variance-split.md) is $2(1-\rho(\zeta))$. Joint Gaussianity is essential; normal one-point [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md) alone are insufficient.
