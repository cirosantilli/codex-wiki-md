# Empirical spectral measure

↑ **Parent:** [Random matrix](random-matrix.md)

For an $N$-by-$N$ [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md), the empirical spectral measure puts mass $1/N$ at each [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md), counted with multiplicity. It is a [probability measure](probability-measure.md). Integrating a test [function](function-split.md) against it gives the average of that [function](function-split.md) evaluated at the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md).
