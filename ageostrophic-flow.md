# Ageostrophic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-geostrophic approximation](quasi-geostrophic-approximation.md)

Ageostrophic flow is the part of velocity beyond leading [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md). Its small horizontal divergence supplies vertical motion and the stretching term in the [quasi-geostrophic potential-vorticity equation](quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity-equation.md).
