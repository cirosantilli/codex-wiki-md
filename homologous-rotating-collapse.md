# Homologous rotating collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar homology](stellar-homology.md)

During a spherical [stellar homology](stellar-homology.md) contraction at fixed mass and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), a constant inertia coefficient gives $I=\alpha MR^2$ and [solid-body rotation](solid-body-rotation.md) gives $\Omega=J/I\propto R^{-2}$. A fixed fluid label $s=r/R$ retains its enclosed mass and polar angle, so centrifugal acceleration scales as $R^{-3}$ and gravity as $R^{-2}$. Their ratio increases as $R^{-1}$. This assumes spin conservation, not continuous synchronization to an external orbit.
