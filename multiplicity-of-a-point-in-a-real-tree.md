# Multiplicity of a point in a real tree

↑ **Parent:** [Real tree](real-tree.md)

The multiplicity of $a$ in a [real tree](real-tree.md) $T$ is the number of [connected components](connected-component.md) of $T\setminus\{a\}$. A point of multiplicity one is a leaf, one of multiplicity two lies in the interior of an unbranched arc, and one of multiplicity at least three is a branch point.
