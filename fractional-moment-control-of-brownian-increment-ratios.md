# Fractional-moment control of Brownian increment ratios

↑ **Parent:** [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md)

The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) give the displayed estimate, with $C=(\mathbb E|N|^{-1/2})^{1/2}<\infty$. No independence between $R_\varepsilon$ and $B_\varepsilon$ is required. If $R_\varepsilon=\int_0^\varepsilon(H_s-H_0)dB_s$ for a bounded continuous adapted process, the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) and the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) make the right side tend to zero. Time shifting proves recovery of $H_t$ in probability from the ratio of a short stochastic integral to its Brownian increment.
