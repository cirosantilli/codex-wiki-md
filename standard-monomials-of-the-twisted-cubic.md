# Standard monomials of the twisted cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Twisted cubic](twisted-cubic.md)

Modulo the three quadric relations of the [twisted cubic](twisted-cubic.md), replace $x_1^2$ by $x_0x_2$, $x_2^2$ by $x_1x_3$, and $x_1x_2$ by $x_0x_3$. Each replacement reduces the total exponent of $x_1,x_2$. The resulting monomials are $x_0^ax_3^b$, $x_0^ax_1x_3^b$, and $x_0^ax_2x_3^b$. In degree $m$ their images under $x_i\mapsto s^{3-i}t^i$ are all $3m+1$ distinct monomials of degree $3m$. They are linearly independent, so the three quadrics generate the full [homogeneous ideal](homogeneous-ideal.md), not merely its radical. The quotient is the third [Veronese subring](veronese-subring.md) of $k[s,t]$.
