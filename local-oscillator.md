# Local oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Optical homodyne detection](optical-homodyne-detection.md)

A coherent reference field that selects the measured [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md) through its relative phase. Mode matching and phase [stability](stability-of-a-numerical-method.md) determine how faithfully the desired signal [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md) is sampled.
