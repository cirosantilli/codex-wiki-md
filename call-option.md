# Call option

↑ **Parent:** [Option (finance)](option-finance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_option)

A [financial option](option-finance.md) granting the right to buy its underlying [financial asset](financial-asset.md) at a specified [strike price](strike-price.md) $K$. Its [exercise payoff](exercise-payoff.md) on one [stock](stock.md) share is the [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) $(S-K)^+$. A [European call option](european-call-option.md) permits exercise at expiry, while an [American call option](american-call-option.md) also permits earlier exercise. A [forward-start call option](forward-start-call-option.md) determines the strike at a future fixing date.
