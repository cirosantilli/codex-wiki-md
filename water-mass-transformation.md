# Water-mass transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean circulation](ocean-circulation.md)

A change in the amount of ocean water belonging to a specified [density](density.md) class, driven by surface [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md), freshwater exchange or interior mixing. [Advection](advection.md) along an [isopycnal](isopycnal.md) transports a water mass without necessarily transforming it; [diapycnal mixing](diapycnal-mixing.md) can transfer water between density classes.
