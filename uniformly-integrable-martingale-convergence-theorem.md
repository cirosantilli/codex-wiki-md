# Uniformly integrable martingale convergence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md)

A [uniformly integrable](uniform-integrability.md) [martingale](martingale-split.md) $M_n$ has [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md) and [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md) to an [integrable random variable](integrable-random-variable.md) $M_\infty$, and $M_n=\mathbb E[M_\infty\mid\mathcal F_n]$.
