# Down-and-out European call

↑ **Parent:** [European call option](european-call-option.md)

A [down-and-out European call](down-and-out-european-call.md) pays the usual [European call option](european-call-option.md) payoff only if a lower barrier has not been hit before maturity. In the zero-interest, zero-dividend [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md), for $0<B\le K<S$, its value is $C(S,K)-(S/B)C(B^2/S,K)$. This follows from reflection of the drifted logarithmic price killed at the barrier.
