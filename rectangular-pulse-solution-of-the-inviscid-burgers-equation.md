# Rectangular pulse solution of the inviscid Burgers equation

↑ **Parent:** [Inviscid Burgers equation](inviscid-burgers-equation.md)

Start the [Inviscid Burgers equation](inviscid-burgers-equation.md) with height $a>0$ on $[x_L,x_L+L]$, $L>0$, and zero elsewhere. The upward left jump gives a [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md) $u=(x-x_L)/t$, while the downward right jump gives an [entropy shock](entropy-shock.md) at $x_L+L+at/2$. They meet at $t_*=2L/a$. Afterward the entire nonzero profile is the triangular fan on $x_L<x<s(t)$, with $s(t)=x_L+\sqrt{2aLt}$ and zero outside. To prove the continuation, the [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) gives $s'=(s-x_L)/(2t)$, and matching at $t_*$ fixes the integration constant. The triangle has conserved mass $aL$, and its left shock state $(s-x_L)/t$ is twice the shock speed, certifying causality.
