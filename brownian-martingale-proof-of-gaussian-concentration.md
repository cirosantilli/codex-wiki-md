# Brownian martingale proof of Gaussian concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian concentration inequality](gaussian-concentration-inequality.md)

For a standard Gaussian vector $G$ and a smooth [Lipschitz function](lipschitz-continuity.md) with $\lVert\nabla f\rVert\le K$, represent its centered value as the terminal [martingale](martingale-split.md) $P_{1-t}f(B_t)-P_1f(0)$. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives gradient integrand $P_{1-t}\nabla f$, whose norm is at most $K$. Its terminal [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) is therefore at most $K^2$. The [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) bounds the terminal deviation by a maximum of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) over $[0,K^2]$. Symmetry, the [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md), and the [Gaussian tail bound](gaussian-tail-bound.md) give the displayed inequality. The square on the Lipschitz constant is essential by scaling.
