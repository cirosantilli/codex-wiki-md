# Two-term bounds for Mills ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Mills ratio](mills-ratio.md)

Two successive [integrations by parts](integration-by-parts.md) of the [standard normal density](standard-normal-density.md) tail give positive remainder integrals establishing these strict inequalities for every $x>0$. The lower bound can be negative at small $x$ without invalidating the inequality.
