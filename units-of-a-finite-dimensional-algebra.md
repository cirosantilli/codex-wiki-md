# Units of a finite-dimensional algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

The unit group $E^\times$ is a nonempty open subset of the affine space $E$, detected by the determinant of left multiplication. It is therefore an irreducible connected [linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md). Its map to $(E/J(E))^\times$ has kernel $1+J(E)$, a closed unipotent subgroup since the radical is nilpotent.
