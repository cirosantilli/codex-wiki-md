# Whitney sum formula for Chern classes

↑ **Parent:** [Chern class](chern-class.md)

The [Total Chern class](total-chern-class.md) of a direct sum of complex [vector bundles](vector-bundle.md) is the product of their total Chern classes. In each degree, $c_k(E\oplus F)=\sum_{i+j=k}c_i(E)\smile c_j(F)$. The degree-zero class is one, classes beyond the bundle rank vanish, and a trivial bundle has total class one.
