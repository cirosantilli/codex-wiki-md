# Frobenius alternant character formula

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius characteristic map](frobenius-characteristic-map.md)

For a [partition of an integer](partition-of-an-integer.md) $\lambda\vdash n$, padded to $m$ rows, let $\delta=(m-1,\ldots,0)$. If a permutation has $\alpha_q$ cycles of length $q$, put $p_\alpha=\prod_q(\sum_i x_i^q)^{\alpha_q}$. The [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of the [Specht module](specht-module.md) $S^\lambda$ is the displayed coefficient. Equivalently $p_\alpha A_\delta=\sum_\lambda\chi^\lambda(\alpha)A_{\lambda+\delta}$, the alternant form of the [Frobenius characteristic map](frobenius-characteristic-map.md). It gives finite coefficient computations without constructing representation matrices.
