# Translation surface

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translation_surface)

A surface with an atlas outside finitely many cone points whose changes of coordinate are [translations](translation-geometry.md). On a [compact](compact-space.md) surface it is equivalent to a nonzero [holomorphic one-form](holomorphic-one-form.md). A zero of order $m$ has [cone angle](cone-angle.md) $2\pi(m+1)$.
