# Unique hue

↑ **Parent:** [Hue](hue.md)

A [hue](hue.md) judged to contain no perceptual mixture of a specified opponent pair, such as a yellow judged neither reddish nor greenish. Its measured setting depends on observer and conditions and should not simply be identified with one retinal cardinal axis.
