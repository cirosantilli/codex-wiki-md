# Mixed panel and exact-death likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Panel-observed multi-state likelihood](panel-observed-multi-state-likelihood.md)

A clinic-observed interval ending in a recorded state contributes a [transition probability](transition-probability.md). If the next observation is exact entry into an [absorbing state](absorbing-state.md) but the preceding state is unobserved, its [statistical probability density](probability-density-function.md) is $\sum_{s\text{ living}}p_{rs}(t)q_{sD}$, not $p_{rD}(t)$. Multiplying these conditional contributions yields a [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) for mixed panel and exact-event observations.
