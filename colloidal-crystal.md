# Colloidal crystal

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

An ordered arrangement of colloidal particles. Nearly monodisperse hard-sphere-like particles can order through packing [entropy](entropy-split.md) at sufficient [concentration](concentration.md), while weak reversible attractions or templates can guide ordering. An irreversibly aggregated solid is not necessarily a [colloidal crystal](colloidal-crystal.md).
