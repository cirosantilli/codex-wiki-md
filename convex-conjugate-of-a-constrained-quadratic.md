# Convex conjugate of a constrained quadratic

↑ **Parent:** [Convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md)

Maximize $px-x^2/2$ over $|x|\leq1$. The maximizer is $x=\max(-1,\min(p,1))$, giving the displayed [Huber loss](huber-loss.md). The [indicator functional of a constraint set](indicator-functional-of-a-constraint-set.md) is zero on the interval and infinity outside; it is not the zero-one indicator used in [measure theory](measure-theory-split.md).
