# Regular heptagon

↑ **Parent:** [Regular polygon](regular-polygon.md)

A regular heptagon is a [regular polygon](regular-polygon.md) with seven sides. Its unit-circle vertices have complex coordinates $e^{2\pi ik/7}$ for $k=0,\ldots,6$. It cannot be constructed from a unit segment by a [straightedge and compass construction](straightedge-and-compass-construction-split.md): the first coordinate $\cos(2\pi/7)$ has degree three over $\mathbb Q$, since the seventh [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md) has degree six and degree two over its real subfield. Every finite constructible coordinate extension has power-of-two degree, which cannot contain a degree-three subextension by the [tower law](tower-law.md).
