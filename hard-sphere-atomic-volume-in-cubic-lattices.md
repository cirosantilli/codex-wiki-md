# Hard-sphere atomic volume in cubic lattices

↑ **Parent:** [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md)

For identical touching atoms represented by [hard spheres](hard-sphere.md) of radius $r$, a [face-centered cubic lattice](face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) has conventional side $a_F=2\sqrt2r$ and four atoms per conventional cell. A [body-centered cubic lattice](body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) has side $a_B=4r/\sqrt3$ and two atoms per conventional cell. Their [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md) volumes are respectively

$$
\Omega_F=4\sqrt2r^3,\qquad \Omega_B=\frac{32}{3\sqrt3}r^3.
$$

Each primitive cell contains one atom in the monatomic model, so its [mass density](density.md) is atomic [mass](mass.md) divided by $\Omega$. These are contact-geometry estimates; a fixed hard-sphere radius does not encode [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md) or phase-dependent electronic bonding.
