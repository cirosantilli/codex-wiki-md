# Triangular lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

The planar [graph](graph-split.md) with vertices $\{a(1,0)+b(1/2,\sqrt3/2):a,b\in\mathbb Z\}$ and [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) between vertices at Euclidean distance one. Every [vertex of a graph](vertex-graph-theory.md) has six neighbors. Its [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md) model is a particularly tractable setting for [conformal invariance of planar percolation](conformal-invariance-of-planar-percolation.md).
