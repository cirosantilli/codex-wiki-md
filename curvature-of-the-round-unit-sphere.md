# Curvature of the round unit sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md)

With outward normal $N(x)=x$, the unit sphere has $A(X,Y)=-\langle X,Y\rangle N$. The [Gauss equation](gauss-equation.md) therefore gives sectional curvature one on every tangent two-plane.
