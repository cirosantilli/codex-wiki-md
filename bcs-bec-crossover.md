# BCS-BEC crossover

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

The continuous evolution, in suitable attractive Fermi systems, from weakly paired overlapping [Cooper pairs](cooper-pair.md) to tightly bound composite [bosons](boson.md) undergoing [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md). Pair formation, phase coherence and the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) must be determined consistently across the crossover. A weak-coupling gap-temperature formula with fixed [density of states](density-of-states.md) is not uniformly valid through the molecular limit.
