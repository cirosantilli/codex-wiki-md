# Ferrers diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Integer partition](integer-partition.md)

A [Ferrers diagram](ferrers-diagram.md) of an [integer partition](integer-partition.md) $\lambda$ is a left-aligned array of dots with $\lambda_i$ dots in row $i$, using the longest row at the top here. Replacing each dot by a square cell gives a [Young diagram](young-diagram.md). Reading the column lengths gives the [conjugate partition](conjugate-partition.md).[section 1.7, Ferrers and Young diagrams](enumerative-combinatorics-volume-1-second-edition-draft-by-richard-stanley.md)
