# Degeneracy map for cusp forms

↑ **Parent:** [Old subspace of cusp forms](old-subspace-of-cusp-forms.md)

If $M\mid N$ and $D\mid N/M$, conjugating $\Gamma_0(N)$ by $\operatorname{diag}(D,1)$ gives matrices $\begin{pmatrix}a&Db\\c/D&d\end{pmatrix}$ in $\Gamma_0(M)$. This proves the weight transformation of $f(D\tau)$. At any rational [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md), factor the rational change of variable into an integral cusp representative followed by an upper-triangular rational matrix of positive slope. The original cusp expansion then stays holomorphic and has zero constant term. Thus this map takes [cusp forms](cusp-form.md) of level $M$ to those of level $N$.
