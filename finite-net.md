# Finite net

↑ **Parent:** [Metric covering number](metric-covering-number.md)

An $\varepsilon$-net of a [metric space](metric-space.md) is a set $G$ such that every point lies within distance $\varepsilon$ of a point of $G$. A Cartesian grid of coordinate spacing $\eta$ is a finite $\sqrt d\,\eta$-net of a bounded cube in the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md). A [Lipschitz bound](lipschitz-bound.md) transfers control on the grid to control over the whole cube.
