# Cold-atom realization of the Haldane model

↑ **Parent:** [Haldane model](haldane-model.md)

[Gregor Jotzu](gregor-jotzu.md) and collaborators realized an artificial [Haldane model](haldane-model.md) in 2014. They loaded spin-polarized potassium-40 [fermions](fermion.md) into a laser-made [honeycomb lattice](honeycomb-lattice.md). Piezoelectric mirrors shook the [optical lattice](optical-lattice.md) along two axes; their relative phase controlled [time-reversal symmetry](t-symmetry.md) breaking. Optical phase control adjusted the sublattice offset.[Jotzu et al., pp. 1–2, Fig. 1; Methods A–C](experimental-realisation-of-the-topological-haldane-model-by-gregor-jotzu-and-others.md)

A [magnetic field gradient](magnetic-field-gradient.md) drove [Bloch oscillations](bloch-oscillation.md). Band mapping measured interband transfer to locate valley-gap closures. Opposite-force drift measurements distinguished cancelling from reinforcing [Berry curvature](berry-curvature.md) contributions; reversing shaking handedness reversed the drift. Interband transfer and harmonic confinement affected this readout, including drift beyond ideal transition lines. This neutral-atom [Floquet Hamiltonian](floquet-hamiltonian.md) realization did not measure electrical [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md). Magnetic fields were used for preparation and forcing.[Jotzu et al., pp. 2–4, Figs. 2–4; Methods A–C; inspected v2 revision 2018, original publication 2014](experimental-realisation-of-the-topological-haldane-model-by-gregor-jotzu-and-others.md)
