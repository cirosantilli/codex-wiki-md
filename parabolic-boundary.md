# Parabolic boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)

For a forward [heat equation](heat-equation.md) or a related [reaction-diffusion equation](reaction-diffusion-system.md) on a spatial domain $\Omega$, the parabolic boundary is the union of the initial face and the lateral spatial boundary. It excludes the interior of the final-time face. This is the boundary relevant to the forward [maximum principle](maximum-principle.md): values at a later time are controlled by earlier data and the lateral boundary. Writing $\Omega\times\{0\}$ instead of $\overline\Omega\times\{0\}$ in the union gives the same set, since the lateral term includes the initial rim.
