# Lang-Nagata theorem for Ci fields

↑ **Parent:** [Ci field](ci-field.md)

A finitely generated [field extension](field-extension.md) of transcendence degree $s$ of a $C_i$ field is $C_{i+s}$. In particular a function field of a curve over a [C1 field](c1-field.md) is $C_2$. The theorem includes stability under finite algebraic extensions when $s=0$.
