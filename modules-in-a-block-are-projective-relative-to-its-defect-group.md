# Modules in a block are projective relative to its defect group

↑ **Parent:** [Defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md)

For a [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) with identity $e$ and [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) $D$, the [trace criterion for defect groups](trace-criterion-for-defect-groups.md) writes $e=\operatorname{Tr}_D^G(a)$ with $a\in(RG)^D$. On any module in the block, multiplication by $a$ is $D$-linear and its [relative trace](relative-trace.md) is the identity. The [D. Higman criterion](d-higman-criterion.md) therefore makes the module relatively $D$-projective.
