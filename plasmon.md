# Plasmon

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasmon)

A [plasmon](plasmon.md) is a quantum of a collective oscillation of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) density in a [plasma](plasma-physics.md) or conducting material. Long-range [Coulomb interaction](coulomb-interaction.md) can give its long-wavelength longitudinal mode a nonzero frequency.
