# Geophone

↑ **Parent:** [Seismometer](seismometer.md)

A sensor of ground motion, commonly giving an electrical response proportional to particle velocity over a specified frequency band. Three-component [geophones](geophone.md) record different motion directions and can help distinguish P- and S-wave responses. Ground coupling and orientation are essential for interpreting amplitudes and [seismic reflection polarity](seismic-reflection-polarity.md).
