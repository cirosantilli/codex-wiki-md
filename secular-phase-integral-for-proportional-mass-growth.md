# Secular phase integral for proportional mass growth

↑ **Parent:** [Secular forcing of a test particle](secular-forcing-of-a-test-particle.md)

If both precession and forcing in $\dot z=ig(t)(z-z_f)$ share a common mass factor while $z_f$ stays fixed, then $z=z_f+(z(0)-z_f)\exp(i\int_0^t g(t\prime)\,dt\prime)$. The [Argand diagram](complex-plane.md) circle is unchanged; only its traversal speed varies. With $g(t)=g_f t/t_p$, the phase is $g_ft^2/(2t_p)$.
