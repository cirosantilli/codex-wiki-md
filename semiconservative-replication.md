# Semiconservative replication

↑ **Parent:** [DNA replication](dna-replication.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiconservative_replication)

In [semiconservative replication](semiconservative-replication.md), each daughter duplex retains one strand of its immediate parent. Under complete copying without strand loss or exchange, one starting duplex gives $2^n$ duplexes after $n$ rounds, but still only two original strands. For $n\geq1$, the fraction of duplexes containing an original strand is $2/2^n=2^{1-n}$: all after one round, half after two, a quarter after three. The fraction of all strands that are original is instead $2^{-n}$. A strand made in round 1 is parental when round 2 begins.
