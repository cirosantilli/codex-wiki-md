# Odd symmetries of a zero-dimensional polynomial model

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-dimensional supersymmetric field theory](zero-dimensional-supersymmetric-field-theory.md)

For $P=W^{\prime}$ and $S=|P|^2-P^{\prime}\theta_1\theta_2-\overline{P^{\prime}}\bar\theta_1\bar\theta_2$, four odd [graded derivations](graded-derivation.md) preserve $S$: $\theta_1\partial_z-\bar P\partial_{\theta_2}$, $\theta_2\partial_z+\bar P\partial_{\theta_1}$ and their barred counterparts. [Left Grassmann derivatives](left-grassmann-derivative.md) fix the signs.
