# Magnetic turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics-split.md)

[Magnetic turbulence](magnetic-turbulence.md) consists of irregular, fluctuating [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md) coupled to moving [plasma](plasma-physics.md). Scattering from these fluctuations can randomize a charged particle's direction in the local fluid frame and permit repeated [shock wave](shock-wave.md) crossings in [diffusive shock acceleration](diffusive-shock-acceleration.md).
