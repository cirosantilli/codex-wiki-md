<h1 id="root-su-2-subalgebras-of-su-3">Root SU(2) subalgebras of SU(3)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(3) Lie algebra](su-3-lie-algebra.md)

Each [root](root-of-a-root-system.md) $\alpha$ of the [SU(3) Lie algebra](su-3-lie-algebra.md) defines an [SU(2) Lie algebra](su-2-lie-algebra.md) generated by $J_3=\alpha\cdot H$ and $J_\pm=\sqrt2 E_{\pm\alpha}$ in the root-length-one convention. They obey $[J_3,J_\pm]=\pm J_\pm$ and $[J_+,J_-]=2J_3$. These subalgebras explain [root](root-of-a-root-system.md) strings, [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) reflections and the integer [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) of [highest weights](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md).
