# Nuclear power

↑ **Parent:** [Energy](energy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_power)

Useful power generated from nuclear energy release, usually controlled [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md) in a reactor supplying heat to a power cycle. Operational carbon emissions, full fuel-cycle impacts, accident risks, waste and decommissioning are distinct assessment dimensions. Replacing existing output requires comparison of both annual energy and dependable capacity.
