# Integral projective curve

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md)

An [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) which is projective, [reduced](reduced-ring.md) and [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md). Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), its only [global regular functions](global-regular-function.md) are constants and its [arithmetic genus](arithmetic-genus.md) is $h^1(\mathcal O_C)\ge0$.
