# Coasting-fluid age bound

↑ **Parent:** [Age of a flat matter-coasting-fluid universe](age-of-a-flat-matter-coasting-fluid-universe.md)

For nonnegative matter and coasting-fluid densities, $0\leq\Omega_m\leq1$. Since $a\leq\Omega_m+(1-\Omega_m)a\leq1$ on the normalized integration interval, the age integrand lies between $\sqrt a$ and one. The [age of a flat matter-coasting-fluid universe](age-of-a-flat-matter-coasting-fluid-universe.md) therefore decreases monotonically with the matter fraction and stays between the pure-fluid endpoint ages.
