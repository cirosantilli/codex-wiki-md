# Equal-prior discrimination of qubit states

↑ **Parent:** [Holevo–Helstrom theorem](holevo-helstrom-theorem.md)

For equiprobable [qubit](qubit.md) states with [Bloch vectors](bloch-vector.md) $r,s$, their density-matrix difference is $(r-s)\cdot\sigma/2$, with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\pm|r-s|/2$. An effect $0\le E\le I$ declaring the first state yields success $1/2+\operatorname{Tr}[E(\rho_r-\rho_s)]/2$. Selecting its positive-eigenvalue eigenspace maximizes this [trace](matrix-trace.md) and proves the displayed result. Measure along $r-s$ and attach the corresponding state labels; if the vectors coincide, no measurement improves on guessing.
