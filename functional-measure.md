# Functional measure

↑ **Parent:** [Path integral](path-integral.md)

The functional measure is the formal integration measure over field configurations in a [path integral](path-integral.md). Its transformation under a symmetry can preserve the corresponding Ward identity or produce a [quantum anomaly](anomaly-physics.md).
