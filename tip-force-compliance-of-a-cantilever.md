# Tip-force compliance of a cantilever

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic filament](elastic-filament.md)

For a clamped [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md) of [filament bending modulus](filament-bending-modulus.md) $A$, a transverse tip force $f$ gives $h(x)=fx^2(3L-x)/(6A)$ in the small-slope model. Thus $h(L)/f=L^3/(3A)$. This differs from the effective tip stiffness under a distributed load. The zero-load Gaussian response reproduces the [thermal bending fluctuations of a clamped filament](thermal-bending-fluctuations-of-a-clamped-filament.md), $\langle h(L)^2\rangle=k_BTL^3/(3A)$ for one transverse coordinate.
