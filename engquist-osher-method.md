# Engquist-Osher method

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone conservative scheme](monotone-conservative-scheme.md)

The Engquist-Osher method is the [finite volume method](finite-volume-method.md) using the [Engquist-Osher flux](engquist-osher-flux.md). Under $(k/d)\sup|f\prime|\leq1$ on the invariant state interval, its update is monotone. It preserves the state range, is contractive for summable perturbations in $L^1$, and decreases discrete [total variation](total-variation.md).
