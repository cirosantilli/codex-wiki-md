# Spectral Lipschitz bound from Frobenius distance

↑ **Parent:** [Hoffman–Wielandt inequality](hoffman-wielandt-inequality.md)

For equally sized [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) and a real test [function](function-split.md) with [Lipschitz bound](lipschitz-bound.md) one, the difference of its averages under their [empirical spectral measures](empirical-spectral-measure.md) is at most $N^{-1/2}$ times the [Frobenius norm](frobenius-norm.md) of their difference. Pair the increasing [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), apply the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) and [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), then use the [Hoffman–Wielandt inequality](hoffman-wielandt-inequality.md).
