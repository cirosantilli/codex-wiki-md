# Contact-interaction Bose-gas field integral

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md)

For a uniform dilute repulsive gas, the periodic complex-field integral has uniform saddle $n_0=\mu/g$ for $\mu>0$. The paired Gaussian fluctuation kernel has [determinant](determinant.md) $(\hbar\omega_n)^2+\varepsilon_k(\varepsilon_k+2gn_0)$, giving the [Bogoliubov spectrum](bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md). In three dimensions the physical low-energy coupling is $g=4\pi\hbar^2a_s/m$ and the weak-depletion parameter is $na_s^3\ll1$. Contact coupling beyond mean field needs ultraviolet matching to the [scattering length](scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md).
