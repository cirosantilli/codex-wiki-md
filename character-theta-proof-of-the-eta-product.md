# Character theta proof of the eta product

↑ **Parent:** [Character theta transformation](character-theta-transformation.md)

Take the even [primitive Dirichlet character](primitive-dirichlet-character.md) $\chi_{12}$ whose values at $1,5,7,11$ modulo twelve are $1,-1,-1,1$. Its [Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character](gauss-sum-of-a-dirichlet-character.md) is $\sqrt{12}$. The displayed series $A$ has $A(-1/\tau)=\sqrt{-i\tau}A(\tau)$ and $A(\tau+1)=e^{\pi i/12}A(\tau)$. Thus $A^{24}$ is the normalized weight-twelve [modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md). It has no interior zeros by the [valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md). Its [logarithmic derivative](logarithmic-derivative.md) equals $E_2/24$, so integration gives the [Dedekind eta function](dedekind-eta-function.md) product. The odd character modulo four similarly produces a series $B$ with $B^8=A^{24}$ and leading term $q^{1/8}$; consequently $B=A^3$.
