# Cabibbo-angle extraction from semileptonic decay rates

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo angle](cabibbo-angle.md)

If two [semileptonic decays](semileptonic-decay.md) differ by $s\to u$ versus $d\to u$, an equal-hadronic-strength approximation and a fifth-power [phase space](phase-space.md) ansatz give $r_s=K\sin^2\theta_C\,\Delta_s^5$ and $r_d=K\cos^2\theta_C\,\Delta_d^5$. Their partial decay rates are $r_i=B_i/\tau_i$, where $B_i$ is the appropriate [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md) and $\tau_i$ the total [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md). Taking the ratio gives the displayed expression. Different hadronic [hadronic form factors](hadronic-form-factor.md), finite [electron](electron.md) [mass](mass.md) and recoil corrections prevent this simplified extraction from being an exact determination.
