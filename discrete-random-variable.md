# Discrete random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Random variable](random-variable-split.md)

A [random variable](random-variable-split.md) supported on a finite or countable subset of the real line. Its law is specified by a [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md). Countable support need not consist of integers: a variable constantly equal to $3/2$ is discrete. Integer-indexed [tail-sum formulas](tail-sum-formula.md) therefore require a separate integer-valued hypothesis.
