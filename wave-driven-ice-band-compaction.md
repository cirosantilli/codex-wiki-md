# Wave-driven ice-band compaction

↑ **Parent:** [Ice-edge band](ice-edge-band.md)

If forward-going [surface-gravity-wave energy](surface-gravity-wave-energy.md) decays as $a^2(x)=a_0^2e^{-\kappa x}$ and local wave force is $F=C\rho_wgR^2a^2$, its spatial decrease is $-\partial_xF=C\rho_wgR^2\kappa a^2$. In a weak-reflection independent-layer approximation, $\kappa\simeq pR^2/d$. Standard deep-water [wave radiation stress](radiation-stress.md) gives $C=1/2$, hence a compaction-force density $\rho_wgpR^4a^2/(2d)$. Different amplitude or force conventions change the coefficient; the force formula alone does not fix $\kappa$. This quantity is force per horizontal area, not automatically the three-dimensional [stress](stress.md) in the ice skeleton.
