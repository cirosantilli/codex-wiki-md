# Symplectic blowup

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)

A symplectic blowup at a point removes a small [symplectic ball](symplectic-ball.md) and collapses its Hopf-circle boundary fibers, replacing the center by an [exceptional divisor](exceptional-divisor.md) $E\cong\mathbb{CP}^{n-1}$. The resulting form agrees with the original form away from the surgery region, and its integral over a projective line in $E$ records the blowup size. In real dimension four, $E\cong\mathbb{CP}^1$ and $E^2=-1$.
