# Dual variable

↑ **Parent:** [Dual linear program](dual-linear-program.md)

A [dual variable](dual-variable.md) is a multiplier associated with a primal constraint in a [dual linear program](dual-linear-program.md). In a minimization [linear program](linear-programming-split.md) with equality constraints $Ax=b$ and nonnegative primal variables, the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) $c^Tx+y^T(b-Ax)$ yields the dual objective $b^Ty$ and inequalities $A^Ty\le c$; the equality-constraint multipliers $y$ are unrestricted in sign. For a [transportation problem](transportation-problem.md), row and column [dual variables](dual-variable.md) give potentials $u_i,v_j$ satisfying $u_i+v_j\le c_{ij}$. [Complementary slackness](complementary-slackness.md) makes occupied routes attain equality.
