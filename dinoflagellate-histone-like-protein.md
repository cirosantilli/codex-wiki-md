# Dinoflagellate histone-like protein

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatin](chromatin.md)

A [dinoflagellate histone-like protein](dinoflagellate-histone-like-protein.md) in the HU-related families is distinct from a canonical eukaryotic core histone. Its distribution is lineage dependent; the label must not be used to imply restoration of a conventional nucleosomal genome.
