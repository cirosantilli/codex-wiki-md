# Cartan subalgebra existence from a minimal Engel subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md)

For a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $L$ over an infinite [field](field.md), let $L_{0,x}=\ker(\operatorname{ad}x)^{\dim L}$. A member of minimum dimension among these generalized zero spaces is a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md), using the standard fact that each $L_{0,x}$ is a self-normalizing [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md). The key minimality argument is more general: if $A\subseteq H=L_{0,x}$ and $H$ minimizes dimension for $x\in A$, then $H\subseteq L_{0,y}$ for every $y\in A$.

Indeed $H$ is preserved by $\operatorname{ad}(x+ty)$, and the induced operator on $L/H$ has determinant a polynomial in $t$ nonzero at zero, by the [finite-dimensional Fitting decomposition](finite-dimensional-fitting-decomposition.md). For all but finitely many $t$, its generalized zero space lies in $H$ and hence equals $H$ by minimality. The restriction to $H$ is therefore nilpotent for all such $t$. Its characteristic coefficients are polynomials in $t$ vanishing infinitely often; their highest-degree terms are the corresponding coefficients of $\operatorname{ad}y|_H$. These vanish, so that restriction is nilpotent. For a global minimum set $A=H$; [Engel's theorem](engel-s-theorem.md) makes $H$ nilpotent and self-normalization gives the result.
