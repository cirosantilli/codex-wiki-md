# Planet-stationary orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Planet-synchronous orbit](planet-synchronous-orbit.md)

A prograde, equatorial [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) with [orbital period](orbital-period.md) equal to the planet's spin period. In the ideal model its satellite remains above one equatorial ground location. Nonzero [orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md) or [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) destroys exact stationarity even when the periods agree.
