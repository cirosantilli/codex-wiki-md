# Biharmonic operator

↑ **Parent:** [Laplacian](laplacian.md)

The biharmonic operator applies the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) twice. Under clamped boundary conditions, two integrations by parts give the positive quadratic form

$$
\langle\Delta^2u,u\rangle=\int_\Omega|\Delta u|^2.
$$
