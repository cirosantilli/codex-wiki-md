# Principal cube root of a Banach-algebra element

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic functional calculus](holomorphic-functional-calculus.md)

If the [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md) $x$ avoids $(-\infty,0]$, its [holomorphic functional calculus](holomorphic-functional-calculus.md) value for the [principal cube root](principal-cube-root.md) is the unique $y$ with $y^3=x$ and spectrum in $\{z\ne0:|\arg z|<\pi/3\}$. For uniqueness, any other such $v$ defines a [continuous](continuous-function.md) unital [algebra homomorphism](algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md) $f\mapsto\Theta_v(f\circ(z\mapsto z^3))$ from the slit-plane [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) to the algebra. It sends the coordinate to $x$, so [continuity and uniqueness of holomorphic functional calculus](continuity-and-uniqueness-of-holomorphic-functional-calculus.md) identify it with $\Theta_x$. Applying it to the principal cube root gives $v=y$. This argument does not assume commutativity of the ambient algebra or separation by [algebra characters](character-of-an-algebra.md).
