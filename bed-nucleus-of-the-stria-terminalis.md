# Bed nucleus of the stria terminalis

↑ **Parent:** [Brain](brain.md)

A set of nuclei participating in extended-amygdala circuits, including sustained threat responses in particular paradigms. A duration-related dissociation in one assay is not a universal division between all [fear](fear.md) and [anxiety](anxiety.md).
