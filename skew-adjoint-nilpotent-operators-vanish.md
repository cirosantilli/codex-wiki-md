# Skew-adjoint nilpotent operators vanish

↑ **Parent:** [Skew-adjoint generator](skew-adjoint-generator.md)

On a finite-dimensional positive inner-product space, a skew-adjoint [nilpotent operator](nilpotent-linear-map.md) is zero. In an orthonormal basis, $\|A\|_F^2=\operatorname{tr}(A^*A)=-\operatorname{tr}(A^2)=0$, since a [nilpotent matrix](nilpotent-matrix.md) has zero trace and so does its square. The [Frobenius norm](frobenius-norm.md) therefore vanishes. In particular, a nonzero nilpotent adjoint map rules out a [compact Lie algebra](compact-lie-algebra.md).
