# Chlorite

↑ **Parent:** [Silicate mineral](silicate-mineral.md)

A group of sheet-silicate [minerals](mineral.md); an idealized Mg-rich composition used in weathering models is $\mathrm{Mg_5Al_2Si_3O_{10}(OH)_8}$. Its alteration to [gibbsite](gibbsite.md) or [kaolinite](kaolinite.md) depends on dissolved silica and the magnesium-to-proton [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) ratio.
