# Rudolph measure rigidity theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Host equidistribution theorem](host-equidistribution-theorem.md)

A probability measure on the circle invariant under $T_2$ and $T_3$ and ergodic for their jointly generated semigroup is [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) if either transformation has positive [Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy](kolmogorov-sinai-entropy.md). Without joint ergodicity, a mixture of [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) and an atomic invariant measure shows why positive entropy alone is insufficient.
