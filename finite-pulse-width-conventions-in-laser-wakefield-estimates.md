# Finite pulse-width conventions in laser wakefield estimates

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma wakefield acceleration](plasma-wakefield-acceleration.md)

A full pulse duration of roughly half a [plasma](plasma-physics.md) period and a Gaussian rms duration of order the inverse [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md) are different conventions for a short resonant driver. Numerical estimates should state which width is meant. Maximum cold-wave fields also need not be produced by a specified driver with normalized [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) below unity.
