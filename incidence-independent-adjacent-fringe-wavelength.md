# Incidence-independent adjacent-fringe wavelength

↑ **Parent:** [Double-slit experiment](double-slit-experiment.md)

In oblique-incidence two-slit interference, the incident wave contributes a constant phase offset while changing observation angle changes the outgoing [optical path difference](optical-path-difference.md) by $a\Delta\sin\theta$. Subtracting the phase conditions of an adjacent minimum and maximum cancels the unknown incidence angle and order and gives the displayed wavelength. Nonadjacent extrema need their intervening phase-order difference; angular spacing should not be replaced by a small-angle formula unless justified.
