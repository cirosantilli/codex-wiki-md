# Pump

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A device that supplies mechanical energy to a fluid to drive a flow. A specified pump installation does not by itself determine the resulting [mass flow rate](mass-flow-rate.md); the connected system's resistance also matters.
