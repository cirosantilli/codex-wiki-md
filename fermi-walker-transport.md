# Fermi-Walker transport

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel transport](parallel-transport.md)

Transport defining a nonrotating frame along an accelerated [timelike curve](timelike-curve.md). With [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) normalization $u^\mu u_\mu=-c^2$ and [four-acceleration](four-acceleration.md) $a^\mu=Du^\mu/d\tau$, the displayed equation preserves inner products and keeps a spatial spin orthogonal to $u$. Along a [geodesic](geodesic.md), $a^\mu=0$ and it reduces to [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md).
