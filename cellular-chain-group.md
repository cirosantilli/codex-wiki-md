# Cellular chain group

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md)

For a [CW complex](cw-complex.md), the qth [cellular chain group](cellular-chain-group.md) is the relative [homology group](homology-group.md) of successive skeleta, a [free abelian group](free-abelian-group.md) with one generator for each q-cell. The attaching maps determine the [cellular boundary](cellular-boundary.md), and the resulting [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) computes [singular homology](singular-homology.md).
