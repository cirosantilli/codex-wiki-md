# First Brillouin zone

↑ **Parent:** [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md)

The [Wigner-Seitz cell](wigner-seitz-cell.md) of a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) centered at the origin. It contains wavevectors at least as close to zero as to any other [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) point. Boundary sharing conventions do not change its area or volume.
