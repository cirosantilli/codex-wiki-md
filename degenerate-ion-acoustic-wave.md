# Degenerate ion-acoustic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Ion-acoustic wave](ion-acoustic-wave.md)

For equal densities of singly charged ions and electrons, a completely degenerate electron equation of state gives $dP_e/dn_e=m_ev_{Fe}^2/3$. Coupling this restoring force to ion inertia yields the sound speed in the title. If both species are degenerate and $m_e\ll m_i$, then $v_{Fi}\ll c_s\ll v_{Fe}$. Ion resonances are absent, while the electron parallel [marginal distribution](marginal-distribution.md) has a small slope at the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md), producing relative [Landau damping](landau-damping.md) of order $\sqrt{m_e/m_i}$.
