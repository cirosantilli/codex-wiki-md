# Adjoint eigenfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md)

An adjoint [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) is a nonzero [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) of the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) on its adjoint domain. For an [inner product](inner-product.md) conjugate-linear in its first argument, $L^*g=\overline\lambda g$ implies $\langle g,(L-\lambda)f\rangle=0$ for every $f$ in the domain of $L$. Consequently a necessary [solvability condition](solvability-condition.md) for $(L-\lambda)f=h$ is $\langle g,h\rangle=0$. If $L-\lambda I$ is a [Fredholm operator](fredholm-operator.md), orthogonality to every element of its adjoint kernel is also sufficient. [Boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) and weights in a coupled system enter the adjoint domain and must be derived by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md); copying the original [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) without checking them can give a wrong projection.
