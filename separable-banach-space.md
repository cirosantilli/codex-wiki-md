# Separable Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Banach space](banach-space-split.md)

A [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) is separable when it has a countable [dense subset](dense-set.md) for its [norm topology](norm-topology.md). It then has a countable base of norm balls and a [countable norming family](countable-norming-family.md). Its [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md) need not be norm separable.
