# Posterior odds

↑ **Parent:** [Posterior probability](posterior-probability.md)

For two exhaustive mutually exclusive models, posterior odds are the ratio of their [posterior probabilities](posterior-probability.md). [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) gives

$$
\frac{\mathbb P(H_0\mid y)}{\mathbb P(H_1\mid y)}
=\frac{\mathbb P(H_0)}{\mathbb P(H_1)}\frac{p(y\mid H_0)}{p(y\mid H_1)}.
$$

The second factor is the [Bayes factor](bayes-factor.md), formed from model [prior predictive distributions](bayesian-model-evidence.md). Evidence favouring one model need not make that model's posterior probability exceed one half if its prior probability was sufficiently small.
