# Finite-dimensional simple algebra is semisimple

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple algebra](semisimple-algebra.md)

For a nonzero [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) $A$ of finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) that is a [simple ring](simple-ring.md), choose a [minimal left ideal](minimal-left-ideal.md) $I$. Right multiplication $I\to Ia$ is a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md), so $Ia$ is zero or a [simple module](irreducible-module.md). The sum $\sum_{a\in A}Ia$ is a nonzero [two-sided ideal](two-sided-ideal.md), hence $A$. Finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) reduces this to a finite sum. The [finite sum of simple submodules](finite-sum-of-simple-submodules.md) lemma shows that the [left regular module](left-regular-module.md) is a [semisimple module](semisimple-module.md).
