# Carotenoid

↑ **Parent:** [Accessory pigment](accessory-pigment.md)

A class of pigments that can contribute to light harvesting and photoprotection. Some broaden [phytoplankton](phytoplankton.md) absorption in blue to blue-green wavelengths.
