# Spinor covariant derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Spin connection](spin-connection.md)

Lift the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) to the [spinor bundle](spinor-bundle.md) using the spin representation of the Lorentz algebra. Under a [local Lorentz transformation](local-lorentz-transformation.md), its matrix $\Omega_\mu=\tfrac14\omega_{\mu AB}\gamma^{AB}$ obeys $\Omega'_\mu=S\Omega_\mu S^{-1}-(\partial_\mu S)S^{-1}$, so $\nabla'_\mu\psi'=S\nabla_\mu\psi$. The [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) identity $[\gamma^C,\gamma^{AB}]=2(\eta^{CA}\gamma^B-\eta^{CB}\gamma^A)$ fixes the coefficient. The tetrad postulate then gives $\partial_\mu\gamma^\nu+\Gamma^\nu_{\mu\rho}\gamma^\rho+[\Omega_\mu,\gamma^\nu]=0$, establishing compatibility with curved Clifford multiplication.
