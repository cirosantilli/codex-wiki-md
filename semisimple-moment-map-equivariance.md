# Semisimple moment-map equivariance

↑ **Parent:** [Moment-map equivariance obstruction](moment-map-equivariance-obstruction.md)

For a semisimple [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), its nondegenerate [Killing form](killing-form.md) turns a two-cocycle into an operator $D$ through $B(DX,Y)=\kappa(X,Y)$. The cocycle identity makes $D$ a derivation. Choose $Z$ by $B(Z,X)=\operatorname{tr}(D\operatorname{ad}X)$. Using $[D,\operatorname{ad}X]=\operatorname{ad}(DX)$ and cyclicity of trace gives $B([Z,X],Y)=B(DX,Y)$, so $D=\operatorname{ad}Z$. Consequently $\kappa(X,Y)=B(Z,[X,Y])$ is a coboundary. This proves that component Hamiltonians can be normalized to a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) homomorphism.
