# Integer multiple

↑ **Parent:** [Divisibility](divisibility-split.md)

An integer multiple of an [integer](integer-split.md) $d$ is a [product](product-of-numbers.md) $dk$ with $k$ an [integer](integer-split.md); equivalently, $d$ is a [divisor](divisor.md) of that [product](product-of-numbers.md). The [integer multiples](integer-multiple.md) of $12$ are $\ldots,-24,-12,0,12,24,\ldots$. Zero is an [integer multiple](integer-multiple.md) of every [integer](integer-split.md), and the only [integer multiple](integer-multiple.md) of zero is zero. Shared positive [integer multiples](integer-multiple.md) lead to the [least common multiple](least-common-multiple.md).
