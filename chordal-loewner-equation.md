# Chordal Loewner equation

↑ **Parent:** [Loewner differential equation](loewner-differential-equation.md)

For a capacity-parameterized locally growing hull family,

$$
\partial_tg_t(z)=\frac2{g_t(z)-U_t},
\qquad g_0(z)=z,
$$

where the continuous real function $U$ is the [Loewner driving function](loewner-driving-function.md).
