<h1 id="time-dependent-schrodinger-equation">Time-dependent Schrödinger equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md)

The time-dependent Schrodinger equation evolves a quantum state according to

$$
i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial t}=H\psi.
$$
