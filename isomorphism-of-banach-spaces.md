# Isomorphism of Banach spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md)

An isomorphism of Banach spaces is a bijective bounded [linear map](linear-map.md) whose inverse is bounded. The [bounded inverse theorem](bounded-inverse-theorem.md) makes boundedness of the inverse automatic.
