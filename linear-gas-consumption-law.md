# Linear gas-consumption law

↑ **Parent:** [Star formation rate](star-formation-rate.md)

A linear gas-consumption law assumes that the net rate of locking mass into long-lived stars is proportional to the gas mass, $\psi=\epsilon M_g$, with constant $\epsilon$. In a [leaky-box model of galactic chemical evolution](leaky-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md) with ambient-metallicity outflow $\alpha\psi$, gas mass decays as $M_g=M_{g,0}e^{-(1+\alpha)\epsilon t}$, while [gas-phase metallicity](gas-phase-metallicity.md) grows as $Z=Z_0+p\epsilon t$. The wind changes the time to consume gas but, under these assumptions, not the enrichment rate at a given time.
