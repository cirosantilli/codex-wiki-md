# Frozen-temperature approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Directional solidification](directional-solidification.md)

The frozen-temperature approximation prescribes the external thermal field during interface perturbations, rather than solving a perturbation heat equation. An interface displacement samples that prescribed field at its new location. Local [liquidus](liquidus.md) and [Gibbs-Thomson effect](gibbs-thomson-relation.md) conditions still apply, and the solute field may retain full time-dependent [diffusion](diffusion.md).
