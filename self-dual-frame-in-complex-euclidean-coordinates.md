# Self-dual frame in complex Euclidean coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Self-dual differential form](self-dual-differential-form.md)

For $w=x^1+ix^2$, $z=x^3+ix^4$ and the standard oriented [Euclidean metric](euclidean-metric.md), the real and imaginary parts of $dw\wedge dz$, together with $i(dw\wedge d\bar w+dz\wedge d\bar z)$, span the [self-dual two-forms](self-dual-differential-form.md). The printed complex four-form $dw\wedge dz\wedge d\bar w\wedge d\bar z$ is four times the metric [volume form](volume-form.md) and gives the same [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
