# M2-brane near-horizon geometry

↑ **Parent:** [M2-brane supergravity solution](m2-brane-supergravity-solution.md)

For $H\sim R^6/r^6$, changing radial coordinate to $z=R^3/(2r^2)$ gives an AdS metric of radius $R/2$ times a round sphere of radius $R$. The [Freund-Rubin compactification](freund-rubin-compactification.md) flux has magnitude $6/R$ in the AdS volume form. This is the regular positive-charge membrane throat, rather than the limit of an arbitrary singular harmonic multipole.
