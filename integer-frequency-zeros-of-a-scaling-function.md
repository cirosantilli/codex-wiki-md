# Integer-frequency zeros of a scaling function

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth-mask vanishing-moment criterion](smooth-mask-vanishing-moment-criterion.md)

Assume the [scaling function](scaling-function.md) [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) and the [MRA low-pass filter](low-pass-filter-of-a-multiresolution-analysis.md) are $C^{p-1}$, and $m^{(k)}(\pi)=0$ for $k<p$. For every nonzero [integer](integer.md) $j$, write $j=2^r\ell$ with $\ell$ odd. Iterating the [scaling refinement equation](scaling-refinement-equation.md) at $2\pi j+t$ supplies a factor $m(\pi\ell+t/2^{r+1})$. All its [derivatives](derivative.md) of order less than $p$ vanish at $t=0$, by periodicity. The [product rule](product-rule.md) gives $\widehat\varphi^{(k)}(2\pi j)=0$ for $k<p$.
