# Variational symmetry of a Lagrangian density

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory.md)

A differentiable infinitesimal transformation is a variational symmetry when its change of the [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) is a divergence, $\delta\mathcal L=\varepsilon\partial_\mu K^\mu$. Under suitable [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) the [action](action.md) is invariant, so exact pointwise invariance is unnecessary. The off-shell variation identity gives the [Noether current for a first-derivative scalar field](noether-current-for-a-first-derivative-scalar-field.md). This definition is for a constant transformation parameter; a spacetime-dependent parameter is a different, gauge-symmetry problem.
