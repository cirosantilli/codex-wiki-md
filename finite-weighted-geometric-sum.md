# Finite weighted geometric sum

↑ **Parent:** [Finite geometric series](finite-geometric-series.md)

Differentiate the [finite geometric series](finite-geometric-series.md) $\sum_{n=0}^Nr^n=(1-r^{N+1})/(1-r)$ with respect to $r$, then multiply by $r$. This proves the title identity for $r\ne1$ without any infinite-series differentiation. At $r=1$, the sum is $N(N+1)/2$. For $|r|<1$, both $Nr^N$ and $Nr^{N+1}$ tend to zero, yielding $\sum_{n=1}^\infty nr^n=r/(1-r)^2$.
