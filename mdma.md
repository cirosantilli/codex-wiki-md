# MDMA

↑ **Parent:** [Psychoactive drug](psychoactive-drug.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDMA)

MDMA is 3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine, a [psychoactive drug](psychoactive-drug.md) commonly called ecstasy. A product's street label does not establish its measured chemical contents. Epidemiological comparisons should distinguish single-substance outcomes from outcomes attributed to several drugs.
