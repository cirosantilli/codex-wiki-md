# Tartaric acid

↑ **Parent:** [Carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md)

[Tartaric acid](tartaric-acid.md) has two [hydroxy groups](hydroxy-group.md) and two [carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md) groups. Its [meso compound](meso-compound.md) form illustrates why two [stereocenters](stereocenter.md) need not make a [chiral molecule](chiral-molecule.md).
