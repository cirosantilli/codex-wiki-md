# Short exact sequence of cochain complexes

↑ **Parent:** [Cochain complex](cochain-complex.md)

A degreewise [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) of [cochain complexes](cochain-complex.md) induces a [long exact sequence from a coefficient sequence](long-exact-sequence-from-a-coefficient-sequence.md) in the coefficient case, and a long exact cohomology sequence generally. Its degree-one [connecting homomorphism](connecting-homomorphism.md) lifts a quotient cocycle, takes its coboundary, and identifies that result in the subcomplex.
