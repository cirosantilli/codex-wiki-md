# Inner derivation of a Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Derivation of a Lie algebra](derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

An inner [derivation of a Lie algebra](derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is $\operatorname{ad}_x:y\mapsto[x,y]$. The [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) makes this a [derivation of a Lie algebra](derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). For every [derivation of a Lie algebra](derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $D$, $[D,\operatorname{ad}_x]=\operatorname{ad}_{Dx}$, so the inner derivations form an [ideal of a Lie algebra](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md) in the [derivation Lie algebra](derivation-lie-algebra.md). The [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $x\mapsto\operatorname{ad}_x$ is the [center of a Lie algebra](center-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
