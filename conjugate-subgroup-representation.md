# Conjugate subgroup representation

↑ **Parent:** [Induced representation](induced-representation.md)

For an $H$-representation $V$ and $g\in G$, the conjugate representation ${}^gV$ of $gHg^{-1}$ is defined by $(ghg^{-1})v=hv$. This transports the acting subgroup; it is distinct from conjugating complex matrix entries. It occurs in the [Mackey restriction formula](mackey-restriction-formula.md).
