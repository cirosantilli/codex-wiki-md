# Discrete opfibration

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor.md)

A [functor](functor.md) with a unique lift, from each specified object $c$, of every arrow $Fc\to d$. The lifted arrow's target is part of the uniqueness requirement. This is dual to a [discrete fibration](discrete-fibration.md). Lifting a [cocone](cocone-under-a-diagram.md) of a finite [connected category](connected-category.md) gives a cocone upstairs: lifts of its legs have a common target because uniqueness identifies targets along every diagram arrow. Thus a discrete opfibration over a [weakly filtered category](weakly-filtered-category.md) has weakly filtered domain, including the possibility of an empty domain.
