# Critical slowing down

↑ **Parent:** [Bifurcation theory](bifurcation-theory-split.md)

Critical slowing down is the divergence of a relaxation time as a stable [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) approaches loss of hyperbolicity. For a scalar [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) it occurs when $f'(N^*)\uparrow0$ and the [recovery time of a stable equilibrium](recovery-time-of-a-stable-equilibrium.md) $-1/f'(N^*)$ diverges. At the threshold, nonlinear terms may replace exponential decay by algebraic decay; for example $N'=-kN^3$ has $N(t)\sim(2kt)^{-1/2}$ for positive initial data.
