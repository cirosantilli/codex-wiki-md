# Dephasing channel

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli channel](pauli-channel.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dephasing_channel)

A [dephasing channel](dephasing-channel.md) suppresses off-diagonal entries of a [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) in a preferred basis while preserving [quantum state populations](quantum-state-population.md). Complete [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) is identical to a [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) in that basis with its outcome discarded.

For the [Bell correlations versus a classical mixture](bell-correlations-versus-a-classical-mixture.md) example, completely [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) Alice's [qubit](qubit.md) in the [computational basis](computational-basis.md) maps $\rho_{\mathrm{Bell}}$ to $\rho_{\mathrm{mix}}$. It preserves their perfect $Z$ agreement and each [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md), yet destroys the $X$ correlation. This is why testing only populations can miss damage to a quantum memory: the stored [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) may have disappeared while every preferred-basis measurement still looks correct. The joint [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) rises from zero to one [bit](bit.md), although each local entropy stays fixed.
