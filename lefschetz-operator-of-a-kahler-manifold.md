<h1 id="lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold">Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md)

The Lefschetz operator of a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) is exterior multiplication by its Kähler form,

$$
L\alpha=\omega\wedge\alpha.
$$

Its formal adjoint is denoted by $\Lambda=L^*$ and lowers the bidegree of a [differential form](differential-form-split.md) by $(1,1)$.

This wedge operator participates in the [Kähler identities](kahler-identities.md); the theorem on integral $(1,1)$ classes is a different result.
