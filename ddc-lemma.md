# ddc lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Ddbar lemma](ddbar-lemma.md)

On a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), if a differential form $\alpha$ is $d$-closed and $d^c$-exact, then it is $dd^c$-exact: there is a form $\beta$ two degrees lower such that $\alpha=dd^c\beta$. This real-form statement is equivalent, after decomposing by type, to the [ddbar lemma](ddbar-lemma.md).
