# Nonpolar molecule

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical polarity](chemical-polarity.md)

A [nonpolar molecule](nonpolar-molecule.md) has no permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md). This distinguishes its charge distribution from the separated partial charges of a polar [molecule](molecule.md).
