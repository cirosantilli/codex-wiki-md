# Oscillation frequency with fluid added mass

↑ **Parent:** [Added mass](added-mass.md)

Fluid [added mass](added-mass.md) increases inertia but need not alter the same restoring [force](force-split.md). A submerged spring's constant [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) shifts equilibrium, whereas a pendulum's [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) reduces its restoring [torque](torque.md). Thus an unbounded-fluid sphere has spring [frequency](frequency.md) factor $\sqrt{\rho/(\rho+\rho_f/2)}$ and downward-pendulum factor $\sqrt{(\rho-\rho_f)/(\rho+\rho_f/2)}$. The latter stable configuration requires $\rho>\rho_f$.
