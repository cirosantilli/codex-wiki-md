# Quantifier elimination for infinite underlying vector spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Quantifier elimination](quantifier-elimination.md)

In the language naming addition, zero and every [scalar multiplication](scalar-multiplication.md) over a fixed [field](field.md), the [theory](first-order-theory.md) of infinite underlying [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) eliminates [quantifiers](quantifier.md). In a [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md) of [linear equations](linear-equation.md) and inequations, an equation with a nonzero [coefficient](scalar-multiplication.md) solves for the quantified variable. Otherwise the variable is excluded from only finitely many values, so infinitude supplies a witness. Disjunction and induction finish elimination. This works over [finite fields](finite-field.md) with infinite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) and over infinite [fields](field.md) with every nonzero [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md).
