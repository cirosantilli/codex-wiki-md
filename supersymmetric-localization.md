# Supersymmetric localization

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md)

A symmetry-preserving deformation can leave protected observables unchanged while concentrating their integral on a smaller critical locus. [Supersymmetric Ward identities](supersymmetric-ward-identity.md) justify the deformation; convergence and boundary terms must be controlled.
