# Cubic-root quantum-plane socle

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum enveloping algebra action on the quantum plane](quantum-enveloping-algebra-action-on-the-quantum-plane.md)

At a primitive cube [root of unity](root-of-unity.md), the cubic homogeneous [module](module-mathematics.md) has [basis](basis.md) $x^3,x^2y,xy^2,y^3$. Its only [simple modules](irreducible-module.md) occurring as [submodules](submodule.md) are the one-dimensional subspaces of $\langle x^3,y^3\rangle$, on which $K=1$ and $E=F=0$. Its quotient by this two-dimensional [submodule](submodule.md) is simple, but it has no simple complement: any lift of the highest $K$ [weight space](weight-space.md) contains $x^2y$, whose $E$ image is $x^3\ne0$. Thus the cubic [module](module-mathematics.md) is not a [semisimple module](semisimple-module.md).
