# Second law of black-hole mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole thermodynamics](black-hole-thermodynamics.md)

Under the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md) and the usual global assumptions excluding naked pathologies, the area of every cross-section of a future [event horizon](event-horizon.md) is nondecreasing toward the future. The [Null Raychaudhuri equation](null-raychaudhuri-equation.md) and future completeness rule out negative expansion of its generators.
