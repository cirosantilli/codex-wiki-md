# Relativistic quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

A description of [quantum states](quantum-state.md) and dynamics compatible with [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md). Relativistic one-particle equations have a useful domain, while interactions that create or destroy particles generally require [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md).
