# Pullback-volume representation of a topological current

↑ **Parent:** [Topological current](topological-current.md)

A closed target $d$-form $\omega$ pulls back under a spacetime field $\phi$ to a closed form $\alpha=\phi^*\omega$. Its dual defines an identically conserved [topological current](topological-current.md). For a compact oriented spatial $d$-manifold and an equally dimensional oriented target with $\int\omega=1$, the spatial integral is the [topological degree](topological-degree.md). Conservation follows without the field equations; changing boundary flux can change the charge.
