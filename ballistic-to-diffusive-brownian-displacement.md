# Ballistic-to-diffusive Brownian displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

With thermal initial velocities and relaxation time $\tau=m/\zeta$, the one-dimensional displacement variance is $2D[t-\tau(1-e^{-t/\tau})]$. It approaches $(k_BT/m)t^2$ at short times and $2Dt$ at long times. Initial velocity preparation changes the short-time result.
