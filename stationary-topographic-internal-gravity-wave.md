# Stationary topographic internal gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary-forced internal gravity wave](boundary-forced-internal-gravity-wave.md)

A fixed terrain obstacle forces a zero laboratory-frequency [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) through the [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) $w=U h_x$. Its [intrinsic frequency](intrinsic-frequency.md) is $-kU$. Propagation, radiation and the transient arrival of its permanent component are distinct questions.
