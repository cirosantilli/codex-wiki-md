<h1 id="fisher-s-geometric-model">Fisher's geometric model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)

A model in which a [phenotype](phenotype.md) is a point in a multidimensional trait space and [genotype fitness](genotype-fitness.md) declines with displacement from an optimum. With displacement $\mathbf z$ and mutational step $\mathbf a$, improvement in squared distance requires $2\mathbf z\mathbin{\cdot}\mathbf a+\|\mathbf a\|^2<0$.
