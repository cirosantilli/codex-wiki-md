<h1 id="moller-scattering">Møller scattering</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Møller_scattering)

Elastic [electron](electron.md)-[electron](electron.md) scattering. The electromagnetic [tree-level Feynman diagrams](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) exchange a [photon](photon.md) in the $t$ and $u$ channels, with a relative minus sign from the identical [fermions](fermion.md). In the massless limit the initial-spin-averaged squared [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) is $2e^4[(s^2+u^2)/t^2+(s^2+t^2)/u^2+2s^2/(tu)]$. The exchange interference is essential. A final-state counting factor for integrating a [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) is separate from this squared [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md).
