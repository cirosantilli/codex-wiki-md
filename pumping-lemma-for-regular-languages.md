# Pumping lemma for regular languages

↑ **Parent:** [Formal language theory](formal-language-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumping_lemma_for_regular_languages)

For a [deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md) with $N$ states, every accepted word $w$ with $|w|\geq N$ has a decomposition $w=xyz$ such that $|xy|\leq N$, $|y|\geq1$, and $xy^iz$ is accepted for every integer $i\geq0$.
