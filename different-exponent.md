# Different exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Different ideal](different-ideal.md)

The different exponent at a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak P$ is its exponent in the [prime ideal factorization](prime-ideal-factorization.md) of the [different ideal](different-ideal.md). It equals the local different exponent of the corresponding extension of completions. The [different exponent and tame ramification](different-exponent-and-tame-ramification.md) theorem detects unramified, tame, and wild behavior without assuming a [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md).
