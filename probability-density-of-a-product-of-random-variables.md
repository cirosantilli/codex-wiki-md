# Probability density of a product of random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Change of variables for probability densities](change-of-variables-for-probability-densities.md)

If $(X,Y)$ has a [joint probability density](joint-probability-density.md) $f$, the product has the displayed [probability density function](probability-density-function.md), interpreted almost everywhere. For fixed $x\ne0$, substitute $z=xy$ in the [integral](integral-split.md) defining $\mathbb P(XY\in A)$; the absolute [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $|x|$. The [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) then exchanges the nonnegative [integrals](integral-split.md). The line $x=0$ has two-dimensional [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) zero, so its omission creates no [probability mass](probability-mass.md). A density may be infinite at exceptional points without creating an atom.
