# Augmentation ideal of the infinite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md)

The [group ring](group-ring.md) of an [infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md) is the Laurent polynomial ring $R=\mathbb Z[t,t^{-1}]$, with augmentation $\varepsilon(t)=1$. Multiply any element in the kernel by a power of $t$ to obtain a polynomial vanishing at one. The [factor theorem](factor-theorem.md) makes it divisible by $t-1$. Thus the [augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md) is $(t-1)R$. Since $R$ is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), multiplication by $t-1$ is an injective module map, so this ideal is a free $R$-module of rank one, even though its additive group has infinite rank.
