# Closed fermion loop sign

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion loop](fermion-loop.md)

A closed [fermion loop](fermion-loop.md) carries an additional minus sign from the odd [Grassmann parity](grassmann-parity.md) of the fields in the [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md) contraction expansion. Equivalently, integrating out [Grassmann fields](grassmann-field.md) gives a [determinant](determinant.md), whose logarithmic interaction expansion has the fermionic sign relative to a bosonic inverse [determinant](determinant.md). This sign must be included in addition to propagator and vertex phases.
