<h1 id="bezout-s-theorem">Bézout's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a projective curve](degree-of-a-projective-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bézout's_theorem)

Two [projective plane curves](projective-plane-curve.md) of degrees $m$ and $n$ with no common irreducible component have total [intersection multiplicity](intersection-multiplicity.md) $mn$. In particular, two nonempty projective plane curves always intersect.
