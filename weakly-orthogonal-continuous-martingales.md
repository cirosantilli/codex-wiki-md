# Weakly orthogonal continuous martingales

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md)

For zero-starting [L2-bounded continuous martingales](l2-bounded-continuous-martingale.md), this condition is equivalent to $\mathbb E[M_tN_t]=0$ at equal times, by conditioning the later factor. The [covariance identity for continuous square-integrable martingales](covariance-identity-for-continuous-square-integrable-martingales.md) also makes it equivalent to $\mathbb E[M,N]_t=0$. It is weaker than the vanishing bracket condition for [orthogonal continuous local martingales](orthogonal-continuous-local-martingales.md): $B_{t\wedge1}$ and $B_{t\wedge1}^2-(t\wedge1)$ have zero equal-time covariance but nonzero bracket $2\int_0^{t\wedge1}B_sds$.
