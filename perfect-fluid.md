# Perfect fluid

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_fluid)

A perfect fluid has no viscosity or heat flux in its local rest frame; its stress is specified by energy density and isotropic pressure. Its relativistic realization is the [perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md).
