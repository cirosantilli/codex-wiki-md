# Effective pressure in subglacial drainage

↑ **Parent:** [Subglacial drainage](subglacial-drainage.md)

Effective [pressure](pressure-split.md) is [ice](ice.md)-overburden [pressure](pressure-split.md) minus [water pressure](fluid-pressure.md) beneath the [ice](ice.md). It controls creep closure of channels and cavities; smaller positive $N$ weakens that closure and favors separation of [ice](ice.md) from bed obstacles. It is therefore central to both [basal sliding](basal-sliding.md) and [subglacial drainage](subglacial-drainage.md).
