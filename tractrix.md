# Tractrix

↑ **Parent:** [Plane curve](plane-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tractrix)

A [tractrix](tractrix.md) is a [plane curve](plane-curve.md) whose tangent segment from the point of contact to a fixed axis has constant length. For segment length one and a positive acute tangent angle $\theta$, its height is $r=\sin\theta$. The slope relation $dr/dh=\tan\theta$ gives $dh/d\theta=\cos^2\theta/\sin\theta$. Integrating yields the displayed axial coordinate up to an additive constant. Conversely these coordinates have the required slope, and their tangent intercept is at distance $r/\sin\theta=1$. Rotating this profile about its axis gives a [tractoid](tractoid.md).
