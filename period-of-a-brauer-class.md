# Period of a Brauer class

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer group](brauer-group.md)

The period is the order of a class in the [Brauer group](brauer-group.md). It divides its index. Over a [local field](local-field.md) with finite [residue field](residue-field.md), the period and index are both the reduced denominator of its [local Brauer invariant](local-brauer-invariant.md).
