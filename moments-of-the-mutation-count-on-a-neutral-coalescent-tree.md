# Moments of the mutation count on a neutral coalescent tree

↑ **Parent:** [Mutation count on a neutral coalescent tree](mutation-count-on-a-neutral-coalescent-tree.md)

The [independent mutation counts in coalescent epochs](independent-mutation-counts-in-coalescent-epochs.md) have the [geometric mutation count in a coalescent epoch](geometric-mutation-count-in-a-coalescent-epoch.md) laws. Summing their [expectations](expected-value.md) and [variances](variance-split.md) gives the displayed formulas, where $H_m=\sum_{i=1}^m1/i$ and $H_m^{(2)}=\sum_{i=1}^m1/i^2$. The extra quadratic term records randomness of the ancestral tree, beyond the conditional [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) variance.
