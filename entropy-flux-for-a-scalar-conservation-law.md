# Entropy flux for a scalar conservation law

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy solution](entropy-solution.md)

A convex entropy $\eta$ is paired with any flux antiderivative satisfying $q'(z)=\eta'(z)f'(z)$ almost everywhere. Its admissibility inequality is $\partial_t\eta(u)+\partial_xq(u)\le0$ distributionally, with the matching initial entropy trace. Affine entropies $\eta(z)=\pm z$ recover the [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md), while bounded classical solutions satisfy equality for smooth entropies and then by approximation for piecewise smooth convex entropies.
