# Heyting operations on subsheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra.md)

Meets of subsheaves are pointwise intersections. Joins are J-closures of pointwise unions: a section lies in the join when its restrictions locally belong to some member, with the member allowed to vary. Implication consists of sections every restriction of which belongs to $D$ whenever it belongs to $A$; this gives $C\leq(A\Rightarrow D)$ exactly when $C\cap A\leq D$. Negation is implication into the initial [subobject](subobject.md). Empty covers mean that the initial sheaf need not be pointwise empty.
