# Homoclinic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)

A homoclinic orbit is a nonconstant trajectory that approaches the same [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) as time tends to both positive and negative infinity. Its closure includes that equilibrium. Such an orbit may form a basin boundary, although a homoclinic orbit alone does not imply a chaotic invariant set.
