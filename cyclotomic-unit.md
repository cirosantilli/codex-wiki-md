# Cyclotomic unit

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

Cyclotomic units include roots of unity and suitable ratios $(1-\zeta^a)/(1-\zeta)$ in [cyclotomic fields](cyclotomic-field.md). Compatible roots $\zeta$ make these ratios norm-compatible along cyclotomic towers. Their closed norm-limit module is a concrete submodule of global units, and its local images generate the power series in the [Iwasawa main conjecture](main-conjecture-of-iwasawa-theory.md).
