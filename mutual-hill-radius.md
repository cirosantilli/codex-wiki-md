# Mutual Hill radius

↑ **Parent:** [Hill radius](hill-radius.md)

A scale for the combined gravitational influence of two small orbiting bodies relative to their common host [star](star.md). It is often used to express the separation of neighboring [planetary embryos](planetary-embryo.md). For equal masses and nearly equal orbital radii it exceeds the individual [Hill radius](hill-radius.md) by $2^{1/3}$.
