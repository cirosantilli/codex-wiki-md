# Normal modes of a rigid beam on two end springs

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

For a uniform rigid beam of [mass](mass.md) $m$ and two identical vertical end [springs](spring.md) of stiffness $k$, small vertical end [displacements](displacement.md) have [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $m(\dot x_1^2+\dot x_1\dot x_2+\dot x_2^2)/6$ and displacement-dependent [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $k(x_1^2+x_2^2)/2$. The [mass](mass.md) and [stiffness matrices](stiffness-matrix.md) are $\mathsf M=(m/6)\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$ and $\mathsf K=k\mathsf1$. Their [generalized eigenvalue problem](generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md) gives symmetric translation $(1,1)$ and antisymmetric rocking $(1,-1)$ at the displayed [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md). The centre coordinate contains only the symmetric mode. Neglect [spring](spring.md) inertia, [damping](damping.md) and finite-angle geometric corrections.
