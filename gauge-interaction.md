# Gauge interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge theory](gauge-theory.md)

An interaction whose [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) couples to a matter current fixed by a [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md). In the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md), the [photon](photon.md), [gluons](gluon.md), [W boson](w-boson.md) and [Z boson](z-boson.md) have different flavour, charge and [chirality](chirality-physics.md) selection rules.
