# Pathline

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A [pathline](pathline.md) is a fluid particle's material trajectory through a time-dependent velocity field. Unlike a [streamline](streamline.md), which follows the velocity field at one frozen time, a [pathline](pathline.md) solves the displayed ODE. The two curves coincide in a steady flow but generally differ in an unsteady one.
