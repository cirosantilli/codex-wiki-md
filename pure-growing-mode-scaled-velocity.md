# Pure growing-mode scaled velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Growth-time form of cosmological dust equations](growth-time-form-of-cosmological-dust-equations.md)

For a pure scalar growing [density contrast](density-contrast.md) with time-independent boundary conditions, $\nabla^2\psi=\delta_*$ fixes the scaled [peculiar gravitational potential](peculiar-gravitational-potential.md) up to an irrelevant constant. The linearized continuity equation fixes the longitudinal scaled velocity to $-\nabla\psi$. The remaining homogeneous velocity is $\mathbf u_h=\mathbf C(\mathbf x)/(a^2\dot b)$; a divergence-free such field does not change the growing density at linear order. Excluding this decaying velocity component is necessary for time-independent $\mathbf u$. In an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), $b=a$ and $\mathbf u_h\propto a^{-3/2}$, furnishing a counterexample if the pure growing-velocity hypothesis is omitted.
