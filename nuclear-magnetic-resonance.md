# Nuclear magnetic resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)

[NMR spectroscopy](nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) measures resonant responses of [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus.md) to applied [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md) in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). In [residual-group monitoring of polyurethane formation](residual-group-monitoring-of-polyurethane-formation.md), distinguishable signal [integrals](integral.md) supply a relative residual-group readout; overlap or very weak signals can limit that inference.
