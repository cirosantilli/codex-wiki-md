# Alpha-spending function

↑ **Parent:** [Group sequential design](group-sequential-design.md)

An alpha-spending function assigns the cumulative [Type I error](type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) budget to successive information fractions in a [group sequential design](group-sequential-design.md). Its total at the final analysis is the overall [significance level](significance-level.md). The rejection boundaries must be calibrated using the joint distribution of the sequential [test statistics](test-statistic.md); independent fixed-level tests do not automatically spend the assigned budget correctly.
