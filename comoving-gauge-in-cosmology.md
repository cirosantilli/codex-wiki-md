# Comoving gauge in cosmology

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar cosmological perturbation](scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md)

A [comoving gauge in cosmology](comoving-gauge-in-cosmology.md) chooses hypersurfaces with zero scalar momentum density for the specified fluid or total system. For cold [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) it can be implemented by taking the matter velocity potential to vanish. The choice of component must be stated in a multi-fluid system; [density contrasts](density-contrast.md) on this slicing can differ from those in a Newtonian or spatially flat gauge.
