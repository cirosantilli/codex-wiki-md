# Long exact cohomology sequence of a pair

↑ **Parent:** [Relative cohomology](relative-cohomology.md)

For an inclusion $A\subseteq X$, the restriction [cochain map](cochain-map.md) fits into an [exact triangle](exact-triangle-in-a-derived-category.md) with the relative [cochain complex](cochain-complex.md). Its [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) gives $\cdots\to H^i(X,A)\to H^i(X)\to H^i(A)\xrightarrow{\delta}H^{i+1}(X,A)\to\cdots$. The same construction with derived sections of a [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) complex gives relative [hypercohomology](hypercohomology.md) and is natural under restrictions of pairs.
