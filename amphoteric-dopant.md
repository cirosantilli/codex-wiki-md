# Amphoteric dopant

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md)

An amphoteric dopant can act as a [donor impurity](donor-impurity.md) or an [acceptor impurity](acceptor-impurity.md) according to its incorporated lattice site or defect configuration. [Silicon](silicon.md) in [gallium arsenide](gallium-arsenide.md) is the standard example: substitution on the [gallium](gallium.md) sublattice gives a shallow donor, while substitution on the [arsenic](arsenic.md) sublattice gives a shallow acceptor. Its electrical effect therefore depends on growth conditions as well as total silicon concentration.[Abstract: neutral and ionized donor species occupy Ga sites; neutral and ionized acceptor species occupy As sites](thermal-equilibrium-concentrations-of-the-amphoteric-dopant-si-and-the-associated-carrier-concentrations-in-gaas-by-chang-ho-chen-and-others.md)
