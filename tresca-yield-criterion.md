# Tresca yield criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Plasticity (physics)](plasticity-physics.md)

The [Tresca yield criterion](tresca-yield-criterion.md) bounds the largest resolved [shear stress](shear-stress.md), expressed through the [principal stresses](principal-stress.md). In [spherical symmetry](spherical-symmetry.md), the two tangential [principal stresses](principal-stress.md) coincide, so $f=|\tau_{rr}-\tau_{\theta\theta}|/2$. Here $\tau_Y$ is a shear [yield stress](yield-stress.md); the corresponding uniaxial [yield strength](yield-strength.md) is $2\tau_Y$.
