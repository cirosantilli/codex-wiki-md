# Scirrhous hardening

↑ **Parent:** [Pathology](pathology.md)

A descriptive historical finding of hard, indurated tissue. Gross hardness by itself does not establish the modern microscopic diagnosis [scirrhous carcinoma](scirrhous-carcinoma.md).
