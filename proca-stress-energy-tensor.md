# Proca stress-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-Proca theory](einstein-proca-theory.md)

The Proca stress-energy tensor is the metric variation of the matter part of the [Proca action](proca-action.md). It consists of the [electromagnetic stress-energy tensor](electromagnetic-stress-energy-tensor.md) plus the quadratic mass contribution $m^2(A_aA_b-g_{ab}A^2/2)$.
