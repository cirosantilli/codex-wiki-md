# One-loop proper vertices of massless phi-fourth theory

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic interaction](quartic-interaction.md)

The [interaction vertex](interaction-vertex.md) $-i\lambda$ and [scalar propagator](scalar-propagator.md) $-i/(p^2-i\epsilon)$ give one four-point [bubble diagram](bubble-diagram.md) for each of three [momentum](momentum.md) channels, each with [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) $1/2$. The two-point [tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md) is zero as a scaleless integral in [dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md). If full proper vertices include the free quadratic kernel, $\widehat\tau_2^{(0)}=-p^2$; the interaction self-energy convention instead starts at zero.
