# Maximum before a lower Brownian barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian running maximum](brownian-running-maximum.md)

Stopping Brownian motion at its first crossing below $-b$, the nonnegative local martingale $(W_{t\wedge\tau}+b)/b$ starts at one and ends at zero. The [maximal identity for a continuous nonnegative local martingale tending to zero](maximal-identity-for-a-continuous-nonnegative-local-martingale-tending-to-zero.md) gives tail $b/(b+x)$ for the stopped maximum, hence density $b/(b+x)^2$ for $x>0$. There is no atom at zero.
