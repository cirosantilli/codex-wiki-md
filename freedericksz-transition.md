<h1 id="freedericksz-transition">Fréedericksz transition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fréedericksz_transition)

The [Fréedericksz transition](freedericksz-transition.md) is a field-driven orientational instability of a confined [nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md). Elasticity and surface anchoring resist the [electric field](electric-field.md) or magnetic torque; a lowest allowed distortion mode becomes unstable at a finite threshold. Positive [dielectric anisotropy of a nematic](dielectric-anisotropy-of-a-nematic.md) permits a normal electric field to tilt a planar anchored director.
