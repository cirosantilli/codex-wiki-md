# Electron self-energy

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion self-energy](fermion-self-energy.md)

The [fermion self-energy](fermion-self-energy.md) of an [electron](electron.md) in [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md). After [renormalization](renormalization.md), its contribution in a bound [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) is one source of the [Lamb shift](lamb-shift.md); other [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md) and nuclear structure also affect the observed [energy levels](energy-level.md).
