# Failure of transitivity for mixed-frame simultaneity

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski simultaneity relative to an inertial observer](minkowski-simultaneity-relative-to-an-inertial-observer.md)

In units $c=1$, consider [spacetime events](event-relativity.md) $O=(0,0)$, $C=(1,2)$ and $D=(1,0)$, with coordinates ordered $(t,x)$. A [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) of speed $1/2$ assigns $t'_O=t'_C=0$, while the original [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) assigns $t_C=t_D=1$. Nevertheless $O,D$ are [timelike separated](timelike-separation.md), so no [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) regards them as simultaneous. Hence the relation “simultaneous in at least one [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md)” is not transitive. This does not contradict transitivity of [Minkowski simultaneity relative to an inertial observer](minkowski-simultaneity-relative-to-an-inertial-observer.md) for one fixed observer.
