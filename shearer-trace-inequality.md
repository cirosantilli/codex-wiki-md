# Shearer trace inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Trace of a set family](trace-of-a-set-family.md)

If every ground element belongs to at least $t$ members of a family $\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal P([n])$, then

$$
|\mathcal A|\leq
\left(\prod_{F\in\mathcal F}|T_F(\mathcal A)|\right)^{1/t}.
$$

Apply [Shearer's inequality](shearer-s-inequality.md) to the characteristic vector of a uniformly random member of $\mathcal A$.
