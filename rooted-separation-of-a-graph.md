# Rooted separation of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex connectivity](vertex-connectivity.md)

For a specified [set](set-split.md) $S$ of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), a rooted separation is a pair $(A,B)$ with $A\cup B=V(G)$, $S\subseteq A$, and no [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) between $A\setminus B$ and $B\setminus A$. Its order is $|A\cap B|$. It avoids a branch set $C$ when $A\cap C=\varnothing$. A graph with [vertex connectivity](vertex-connectivity.md) at least $|S|$ has no rooted separation of order below $|S|$ avoiding a nonempty branch set: both sides of such a separation would be nonempty after deleting its separator.
