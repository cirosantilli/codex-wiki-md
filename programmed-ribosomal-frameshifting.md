# Programmed ribosomal frameshifting

↑ **Parent:** [Reading frame](reading-frame.md)

[Programmed ribosomal frameshifting](programmed-ribosomal-frameshifting.md) changes the reading frame during translation at a specific recoding signal. A slippery sequence and a downstream RNA structure can bias the event, allowing one transcript to produce different polypeptides in controlled proportions.
