# Repeated-root dynamic-boundary heat kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic boundary condition for the heat equation](dynamic-boundary-condition-for-the-heat-equation.md)

For the [dynamic boundary condition for the heat equation](dynamic-boundary-condition-for-the-heat-equation.md) with $\alpha=2a$, $\beta=a^2$, the boundary resolvent denominator is $(\sqrt p-a)^2$. With the [principal square root](principal-square-root-of-a-complex-number.md), its inverse kernel is

$$
J_a(x,t)=(1-ax+2a^2t)e^{-ax+a^2t}\operatorname{erfc}\!\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt t}-a\sqrt t\right)+2a\sqrt{t/\pi}\,e^{-x^2/(4t)}.
$$

Equivalently, $J_a=\int_0^\infty r e^{ar}P(x+r,t)dr$ using the [Heat Poisson kernel](heat-poisson-kernel.md). This representation fixes the repeated-root sign and remains valid for either sign of $a$.
