# Basis permutation of a quantum Hamiltonian matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian (quantum mechanics)](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md)

Reordering an orthonormal basis changes its matrix by a permutation similarity transformation. The off-diagonal block can move to different row and column indices without changing any [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md). Matrix entries must therefore be compared in the same declared basis order, not only by their visual positions.
