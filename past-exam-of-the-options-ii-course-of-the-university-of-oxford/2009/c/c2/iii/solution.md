[Gauss lemma for quadratic residues](../../../../../gauss-s-lemma-number-theory.md) states: for $p\nmid a$, take the least positive residues of $a,2a,\ldots,((p-1)/2)a$ modulo $p$, and let $s$ be the number greater than $p/2$. Then

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac ap\right)=(-1)^s}.
$$
