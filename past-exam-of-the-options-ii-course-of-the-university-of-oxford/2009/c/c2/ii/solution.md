↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Euler criterion](../../../../../euler-criterion.md) states that

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac ap\right)\equiv a^{(p-1)/2}\pmod p}.
$$

When $p\nmid a$, the power is congruent to $+1$ or $-1$, according as $a$ is or is not a [quadratic residue](../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md).
