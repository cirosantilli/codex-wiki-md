<h1 id="e/e4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The underlying set of the [quotient space](../../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) is the set of [equivalence classes](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) $X/R$. With [quotient map](../../../../../../quotient-map.md) $q(x)=[x]$, its [quotient topology](../../../../../../quotient-topology.md) is defined by declaring $U\subseteq X/R$ open exactly when $q^{-1}(U)$ is open in $X$. This makes $q$ a [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) surjection and is the finest [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) with that property.
