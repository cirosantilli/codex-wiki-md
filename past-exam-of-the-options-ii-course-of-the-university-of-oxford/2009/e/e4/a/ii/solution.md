<h1 id="e/e4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A separation of $f(X)$ would pull back under the [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) map to two disjoint nonempty [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md) covering $X$: nonemptiness follows because their points belong to the image. This contradicts [connectedness](../../../../../../connected-space.md) of $X$. Hence **a [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) image of a [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) space is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md)**.
