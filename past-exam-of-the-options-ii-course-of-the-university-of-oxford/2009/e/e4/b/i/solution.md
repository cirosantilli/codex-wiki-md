<h1 id="e/e4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $C$ be any [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md). Since $A$ is both open and closed, $C\cap A$ is both open and closed in the [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $C$. Its [connectedness](../../../../../../connected-space.md) makes $C\cap A$ either empty or all of $C$. Thus an [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) meeting $A$ lies wholly in $A$. Hence **$A$ is saturated**, a [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) of whole equivalence classes.
