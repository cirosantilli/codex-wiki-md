<h1 id="e/e4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The difference map $(z,w)\mapsto z-w$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md), and $X$ is its [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md) of the [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$. Thus $X$ is open in the product.

The class of $(z,w)$ is $C_z=\{z\}\times(\mathbb C\setminus\{z\})$. A punctured plane is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md): two points can be joined by a polygonal path avoiding the deleted point. Consequently every class is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md).

The projection $\pi$ is onto, since $(z,z+1)\in X$ for every $z$. It is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) as a restricted coordinate projection. If $U$ is open in $X$, it is open in the product because $X$ itself is open. For a point $(z,w)\in U$, the product-[topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) [basis](../../../../../basis.md) gives open sets $V,W$ with $(z,w)\in V\times W\subseteq U$. Then $V\subseteq\pi(U)$. Hence $\pi(U)$ is open.

The map is constant exactly on the equivalence classes, so it induces a bijection $\overline\pi:X/R\to\mathbb C$. It is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) by the defining property of the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md). It is also open: for quotient-open $V$, $\overline\pi(V)=\pi(q^{-1}(V))$ is open. Thus

$$
\boxed{X/R\cong\mathbb C\text{ homeomorphically}}.
$$

The plane is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md), and the classes are [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md), so part (b) now yields **$X$ is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md)**.
