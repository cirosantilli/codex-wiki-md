<h1 id="e/e3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $x\ne b$, then $d_a(b,x)=d(b,a)+d(a,x)\geq d(b,a)$, so no such $x$ lies in the strict [open ball](../../../../../../open-ball.md). Its centre always does. Hence

$$
\boxed{B_{d_a}(b,d(b,a))=\{b\}}.
$$

Thus every point other than $a$ is isolated.
