<h1 id="e/e3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The singleton calculation shows that every map out of $(X,d_a)$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at each point other than $a$: use the domain ball consisting only of that point. Thus global [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) in this [metric](../../../../../../metric.md) is equivalent to [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $a$, proving $[\alpha]\Longleftrightarrow[\beta]$.

For every $x$, including $x=a$, one has $d_a(a,x)=d(a,x)$. Therefore the [open balls](../../../../../../open-ball.md) centred at $a$ are identical for $d_a$ and $d$. The epsilon-delta definitions of [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $a$ are identical, giving $[\beta]\Longleftrightarrow[\gamma]$. Hence **all three conditions are equivalent**.
