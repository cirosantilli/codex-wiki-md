<h1 id="e/e3/b/iv/beta/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Β](../beta.md)

If $A$ is [open](../../../../../../../open-set.md) and contains $a$, it contains some $d_a$-ball about $a$, which is exactly the corresponding $d$-ball. Conversely, if $B_d(a,\varepsilon)\subseteq A$, this is a $d_a$-neighbourhood of $a$, while every other point of $A$ has its open singleton inside $A$. Hence $A$ is [open](../../../../../../../open-set.md). Thus the stated ball-containment condition is **necessary and sufficient**.
