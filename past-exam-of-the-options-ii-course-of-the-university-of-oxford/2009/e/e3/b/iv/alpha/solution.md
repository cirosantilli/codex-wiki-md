↑ **Parent:** [Α](../alpha.md)

Every point of $A$ differs from $a$, so its singleton is an [open ball](../../../../../../../open-ball.md) by part (ii). The [union](../../../../../../../set-union.md) of these singleton [open sets](../../../../../../../open-set.md) is $A$. Thus **$A$ is open** whenever $a\notin A$.
