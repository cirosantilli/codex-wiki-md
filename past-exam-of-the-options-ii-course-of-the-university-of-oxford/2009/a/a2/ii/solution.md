<h1 id="a/a2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Define $\vartheta(x)=x^p$ for every $x\in K$. In characteristic $p$, all intermediate binomial coefficients vanish, so $\vartheta(x+y)=\vartheta(x)+\vartheta(y)$; multiplicativity and preservation of one are immediate. If $x^p=y^p$, then $(x-y)^p=0$ and the [field](../../../../../field.md) property gives $x=y$. Thus this [field homomorphism](../../../../../field-homomorphism.md) is injective, and finiteness makes it surjective. It is therefore a [field automorphism](../../../../../field-automorphism.md), sending $\lambda$ to $\lambda^p$ as required.
