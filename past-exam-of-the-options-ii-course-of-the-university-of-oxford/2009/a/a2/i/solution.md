↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [simple field extension](../../../../../simple-field-extension.md), the powers $1,\lambda,\ldots,\lambda^{d-1}$ form a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) when the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) has degree $d$: reduction modulo that [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) spans the extension, and a relation of smaller degree would contradict minimality. Hence $[F(\lambda):F]=d$. The [field](../../../../../field.md) is $K$, whose dimension is $n$, giving **$d=n$**.
