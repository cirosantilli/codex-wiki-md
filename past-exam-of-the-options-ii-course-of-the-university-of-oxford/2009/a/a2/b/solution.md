<h1 id="a/a2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Regard $K$ as a [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over its [prime subfield](../../../../../prime-field.md) $\mathbb F_p$. Since $K$ is finite, a maximal [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md) list terminates and is a finite [basis](../../../../../basis.md), of some dimension $n\geq1$. Each [field](../../../../../field.md) element has a unique coordinate vector in $\mathbb F_p^n$, so

$$
\boxed{|K|=p^n}.
$$
