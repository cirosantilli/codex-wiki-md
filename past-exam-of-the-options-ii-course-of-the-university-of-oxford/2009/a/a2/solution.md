<h1 id="a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

The [characteristic of a field](../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) is the least positive [integer](../../../../integer-split.md) $r$ with $r1_K=0$, or zero if none exists. In a [finite field](../../../../finite-field.md) a positive such [integer](../../../../integer-split.md) exists because the sequence $0,1_K,2\cdot1_K,\ldots$ must repeat.

The [prime subfield](../../../../prime-field.md) is the [intersection](../../../../set-intersection.md) of all subfields of $K$, equivalently the [subfield](../../../../subfield.md) generated by $1_K$. In characteristic $p$ it is the copy of $\mathbb F_p$ formed by the multiples of $1_K$. Every [field automorphism](../../../../field-automorphism.md) preserves $1_K$ and addition, so it fixes every element of this [subfield](../../../../subfield.md).

Finally, once the order-$n$ [Frobenius automorphism](../../../../frobenius-automorphism.md) below is established, all its $n$ powers are distinct. Any automorphism fixes $F$ and is determined by its value at the generator $\lambda$. That value must be a root of the degree-$n$ [minimal polynomial](../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) of $\lambda$, so there are at most $n$ automorphisms. Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Aut}(K)=\langle\vartheta\rangle\cong C_n}.
$$
