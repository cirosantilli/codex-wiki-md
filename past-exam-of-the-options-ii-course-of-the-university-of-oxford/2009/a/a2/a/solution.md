↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If the positive [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) were composite, say $r=uv$ with $1<u,v<r$, then $(u1_K)(v1_K)=0$ while neither factor is zero, by minimality of $r$. This contradicts a [field](../../../../../field.md) having no [zero divisors](../../../../../zero-divisor.md). Thus the characteristic is a **prime** $p$.
