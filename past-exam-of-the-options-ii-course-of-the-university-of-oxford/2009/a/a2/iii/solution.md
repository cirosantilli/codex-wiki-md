<h1 id="a/a2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Here the iteration index is a positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md): $1\leq m<n$. If $\vartheta^m(\lambda)=\lambda$, then $\vartheta^m$ fixes $F$ and the generator, so it fixes all of $K$. Every element of $K$ would then solve $T^{p^m}-T=0$. A nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree $p^m$ over a [field](../../../../../field.md) has at most $p^m$ roots, contradicting $|K|=p^n>p^m$. Thus **$\vartheta^m(\lambda)\ne\lambda$**. The printed inequality alone omits positivity of $m$; at $m=0$ the claimed inequality would be false.
