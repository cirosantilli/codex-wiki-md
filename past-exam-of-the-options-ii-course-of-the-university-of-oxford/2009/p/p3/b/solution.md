<h1 id="p/p3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiplication by the displayed [Vandermonde matrix](../../../../../vandermonde-matrix.md) maps the coefficient vector of a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $n$ to its vector of values at the nodes. Part (a) proves that every vector of node values is attained uniquely, so this square linear map is bijective. Therefore **$A_n$ is invertible**. Equivalently, a [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) vector would define a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $n$ with $n+1$ distinct roots and would have to vanish.
