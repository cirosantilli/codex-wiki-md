<h1 id="p/p4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $x\in S_v$, then $v^Tx=0$ and the defining formula immediately gives $H_vx=x$. For arbitrary $x$,

$$
v^TH_vx=v^Tx-2\frac{v^Tv}{v^Tv}v^Tx=-v^Tx.
$$

Thus the [Householder reflection](../../../../../householder-transformation.md) fixes the hyperplane perpendicular to $v$ pointwise and reverses the component along its normal.
