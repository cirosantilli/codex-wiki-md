<h1 id="p/p4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $P=vv^T/(v^Tv)$, which is a nonzero symmetric [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md): $P^T=P$ and $P^2=P$. Then $H_{v,\alpha}=I-\alpha P$ is symmetric and

$$
H_{v,\alpha}^TH_{v,\alpha}=(I-\alpha P)^2=I+(\alpha^2-2\alpha)P.
$$

Since $P\ne0$, this equals the identity exactly when $\alpha(\alpha-2)=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{H_{v,\alpha}\text{ is orthogonal}\iff\alpha\in\{0,2\}}.
$$
