↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is nonnegative and measurable, so [Lebesgue integrability](../../../../../../integrability.md) is equivalent to finiteness of its integral. For $\alpha\ne-1$,

$$
\int_\varepsilon^1x^\alpha\,dx=\frac{1-\varepsilon^{\alpha+1}}{\alpha+1}.
$$

Letting $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ gives a finite limit exactly when $\alpha>-1$; at $\alpha=-1$ the integral is $-\log\varepsilon$ and diverges. By the [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) for truncations,

$$
\boxed{\alpha>-1}.
$$
