<h1 id="d/d3/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\alpha\ne-1$,

$$
\int_1^Rx^\alpha\,dx=\frac{R^{\alpha+1}-1}{\alpha+1}.
$$

The limit as $R\to\infty$ is finite exactly when $\alpha<-1$; the case $\alpha=-1$ diverges logarithmically. Positivity and the [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) identify these limits with the Lebesgue integral. Hence

$$
\boxed{\alpha<-1}.
$$
