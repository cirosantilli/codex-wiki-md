<h1 id="d/d3/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is measurable and negative on this interval. Its absolute value obeys

$$
0\leq\frac{-x\log x}{(1+x)^2}\leq-x\log x,
\qquad\int_0^1-x\log x\,dx=\frac14.
$$

The final integral follows by [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) and $x^2\log x\to0$ at zero. The [comparison test for integrals](../../../../../../comparison-test-for-integrals.md) proves **integrability**.
