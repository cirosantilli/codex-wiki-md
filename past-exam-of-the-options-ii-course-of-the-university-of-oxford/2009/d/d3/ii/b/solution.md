<h1 id="d/d3/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $x\geq1$, $(1+x)^2\leq4x^2$, so

$$
\frac{x\log x}{(1+x)^2}\geq\frac{\log x}{4x}\geq0.
$$

The integral of the lower bound from one to $R$ is $(\log R)^2/8$, which diverges. Thus the nonnegative [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) has infinite integral and is **not integrable**.
