A [group action](../../../../../group-action-split.md) assigns to each $g\in G$ and $x\in\Omega$ a point $g\cdot x$ so that $1\cdot x=x$ and $(gh)\cdot x=g\cdot(h\cdot x)$. The [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) of $x$ is $Gx=\{g\cdot x:g\in G\}$. The action is [transitive](../../../../../transitive-relation.md) if there is exactly one [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), equivalently any point can be carried to any other by some group element.
