<h1 id="m/m5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $i\notin A$, the event of ever reaching $A$ can be conditioned on the first step. Given $X_1=j$, its remaining [hitting probability](../../../../../../hitting-probability.md) is $h_j$ by the [Markov property](../../../../../../markov-property.md), including $h_j=1$ if $j\in A$. The [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) thus gives

$$
\boxed{h_i=\sum_jp_{ij}h_j}.
$$

For $i\in A$ the definition uses time zero and gives $h_i=1$, so the harmonic equation is needed only outside $A$.
