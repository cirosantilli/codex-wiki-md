<h1 id="m/m4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The interarrival times are [independent and identically distributed](../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with [exponential distribution](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) of rate $\lambda$: for any finite $k$ their joint density is $\lambda^k\exp(-\lambda\sum_{j=1}^kx_j)$ on $x_j>0$. In particular

$$
M_{X_1}(\theta)=\int_0^\infty\lambda e^{-(\lambda-\theta)x}\,dx
=\boxed{\frac\lambda{\lambda-\theta}},\qquad\theta<\lambda.
$$
