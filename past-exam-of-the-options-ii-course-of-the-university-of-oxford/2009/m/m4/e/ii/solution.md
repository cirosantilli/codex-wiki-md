<h1 id="m/m4/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each fixed $\theta$, the [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) of the exponential yields

$$
\log M_{U_t}(\theta)=\frac{\theta^2}2+O((\lambda t)^{-1/2})\ \longrightarrow\ \frac{\theta^2}2.
$$

The limiting moment-generating [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $e^{\theta^2/2}$ is that of the standard [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) and is finite around zero. The [moment-generating function continuity theorem](../../../../../../convergence-theorem-for-moment-generating-functions.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{U_t\xrightarrow{d}N(0,1)}.
$$
