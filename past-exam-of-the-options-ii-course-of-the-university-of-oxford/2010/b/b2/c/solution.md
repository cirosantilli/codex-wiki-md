<h1 id="b/b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Embed $\operatorname{GL}_n(F)$ by $A\mapsto(A,0)$. The multiplication and inverse formulas show that this is an injective [group homomorphism](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md), so its image $H$ is a [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md). The translations form $N=\{(I,c):c\in F^n\}$, with $(I,c)(I,d)=(I,c+d)$ and inverse $(I,-c)$. Hence $N$ is isomorphic to the additive [group](../../../../../group-split.md) of $F^n$.

For an arbitrary affine transformation,

$$
\boxed{(A,b)(I,c)(A,b)^{-1}=(I,Ac).}
$$

[Conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) therefore preserves $N$, proving that $N$ is normal in $G$.
