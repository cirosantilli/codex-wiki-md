<h1 id="b/b2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Every affine transformation factors as

$$
(A,b)=(I,b)(A,0),
$$

so $G=NH$. A translation fixing zero has zero translation vector, so $H\cap N=\{(I,0)\}$. Together with normality from part (c), these are the internal [semidirect product](../../../../../semidirect-product.md) conditions. Thus

$$
\boxed{G\cong(F^n,+)\rtimes\operatorname{GL}_n(F).}
$$

The reconstruction data are the additive [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $F^n$, the linear [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $\operatorname{GL}_n(F)$, and its standard action $A:c\mapsto Ac$ on translations. In pair notation $(b,A)$, the product is $(b,A)(d,C)=(b+Ad,AC)$, equivalent to the formula in part (b).
