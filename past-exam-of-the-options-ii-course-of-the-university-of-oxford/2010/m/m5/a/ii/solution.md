The [transition matrix](../../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) is $P=(P_{ij})_{i,j\in S}$, where $P_{ij}=P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i)$. Its entries are nonnegative and each row sums to one. On states absent from a particular initial law, these probabilities specify the same chain when started from that state.
