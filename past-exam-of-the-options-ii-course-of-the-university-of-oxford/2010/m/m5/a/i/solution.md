<h1 id="m/m5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A time-homogeneous [Markov chain](../../../../../../markov-chain-split.md) on a countable [state space](../../../../../../state-space.md) $S$ is a sequence of $S$-valued [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) for which, for every positive-probability finite history,

$$
P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_0=i_0,\ldots,X_n=i)
=P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i)=P_{ij}.
$$

Thus the conditional next-step law depends only on the current state, and the transition probabilities do not depend on time.
