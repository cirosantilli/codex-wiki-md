<h1 id="m/m4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Away from the null event $X=0$, the inverse transformation is $x=u$, $y=uv$. Its [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is $u$, so its absolute value is $|u|$. [Independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) therefore gives the joint density

$$
\boxed{f_{U,V}(u,v)=|u|f(u)g(uv).}
$$

A value at $u=0$ may be assigned arbitrarily without changing the distribution.
