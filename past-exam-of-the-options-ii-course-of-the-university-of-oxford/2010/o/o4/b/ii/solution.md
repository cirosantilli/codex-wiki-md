<h1 id="o/o4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A linear combination of independent normal variables is normal; the corresponding asymptotic statement follows from the joint [central limit theorem](../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) and the [continuous mapping theorem](../../../../../../continuous-mapping-theorem.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\widehat p-\widehat q\ \approx\ N\left(p-q,
\frac{p(1-p)}{2M}+\frac{q(1-q)}{2N}\right).}
$$

The two variances add because of [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md). This approximation assumes both sample sizes grow and the true probabilities are interior.
