<h1 id="e/e4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A subset $B$ of a [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) is [bounded](../../../../../../bounded-set.md) if it lies in some ball of finite radius: there are $x_0$ in the space and $R<\infty$ with $d(x,x_0)\leq R$ for every $x\in B$. For a nonempty subset, this is equivalent to finite [diameter](../../../../../../diameter.md), $\sup_{x,y\in B}d(x,y)<\infty$. The empty subset is bounded by convention.
