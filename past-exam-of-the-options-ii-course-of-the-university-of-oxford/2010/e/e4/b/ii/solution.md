↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) is [complete](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) if **every [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) in it converges to a point of that same space**. A limit in a larger ambient completion is insufficient for this definition.
