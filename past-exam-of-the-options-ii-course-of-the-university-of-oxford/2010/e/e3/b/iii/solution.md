<h1 id="e/e3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $C$ be a component of the [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $X\subseteq\mathbb R$. Being a connected subset of the real line, $C$ is an [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md). For any $x\in C$, openness of $X$ gives $\epsilon>0$ with $(x-\epsilon,x+\epsilon)\subseteq X$. This [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) is connected and meets $C$, so their union is connected. Maximality of $C$ makes the entire [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) lie in $C$. Thus every point of $C$ is interior, so **each component is an open [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md)**, allowing unbounded endpoints.
