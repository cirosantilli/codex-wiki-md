<h1 id="e/e3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let

$$
\boxed{K_a=\bigcup\{S\subseteq X:S\text{ is connected and }a\in S\}.}
$$

This family is nonempty because it contains $\{a\}$. All its members intersect at $a$, so part (a)(iii) proves that $K_a$ is connected. It contains $a$ and, by its construction, contains every connected subset containing $a$. Thus it has exactly the required largest-subset property. This is the [connected component](../../../../../../connected-component.md) containing $a$.
