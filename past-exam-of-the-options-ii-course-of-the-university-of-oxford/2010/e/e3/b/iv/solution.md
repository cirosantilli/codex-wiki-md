<h1 id="e/e3/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Every point belongs to its component, so the components cover $X$; by part (ii) they are pairwise disjoint, and by part (iii) they are nonempty open [intervals](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md). Each contains a [rational number](../../../../../../rational-number.md). Fix an enumeration of $\mathbb Q$ and choose the first rational belonging to each component. Distinct components receive distinct rationals, giving an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) from the component family into a [countable set](../../../../../../countable-set.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{X\text{ is a countable union of pairwise disjoint open intervals}.}
$$

The empty [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) is represented by the empty union.
