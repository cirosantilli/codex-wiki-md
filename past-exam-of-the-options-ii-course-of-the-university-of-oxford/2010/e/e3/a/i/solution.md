↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A subset $A$ is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) if its [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md) admits no separation into two disjoint nonempty relatively open subsets. Equivalently, every [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) $A\to\{0,1\}$, with the target discrete, is constant: a nonconstant map gives the separation by its two fibers, and a separation defines such a map.
