↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The connected subsets of the usual real line are **the empty set and all [intervals](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md)**, including singletons, bounded [intervals](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) of any endpoint type, rays, and the whole line. Equivalently, a nonempty subset is connected exactly when it contains every point between any two of its points.
