<h1 id="a/a2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The extension $K/F$ is a [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over $F$. Since $K$ is finite, a maximal linearly independent subset is finite and spans it; let its size be $d$. Relative to this [basis](../../../../../basis.md), every element of $K$ has a unique list of $d$ coefficients in $F$. There are $q$ choices for each coefficient, so

$$
\boxed{|K|=q^d,\qquad d=[K:F].}
$$

The inclusion $F\subseteq K$ makes $d\geq1$.
