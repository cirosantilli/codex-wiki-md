↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

By the allowed finite multiplicative-[subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) theorem, the [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $K^\times$ is cyclic. Let $z$ generate it. Every nonzero element of $K$ is a power of $z$, hence belongs to $F(z)$; zero belongs as well. Since the reverse inclusion is automatic,

$$
\boxed{K=F(z).}
$$

Thus a finite extension of a [finite field](../../../../../finite-field.md) is a [simple field extension](../../../../../simple-field-extension.md).
