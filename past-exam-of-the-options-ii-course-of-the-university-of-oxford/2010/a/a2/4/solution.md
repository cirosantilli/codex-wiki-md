<h1 id="a/a2/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

For $\alpha\neq0$, [Lagrange's theorem for finite groups](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) in $K^\times$, whose order is $q^m-1$, gives $\alpha^{q^m-1}=1$. The zero element satisfies the equation too, so every $\alpha\in K$ has $\alpha^{q^m}=\alpha$.

Now let $f\in F[X]$ be irreducible of degree $m$. The quotient $F[X]/(f)$ is a [field](../../../../../field.md) with $q^m$ elements, by its [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $1,\overline X,\ldots,\overline X^{m-1}$. Its element $\overline X$ therefore satisfies $\overline X^{q^m}-\overline X=0$. This means precisely that $X^{q^m}-X$ lies in the ideal $(f)$, giving

$$
\boxed{f(X)\mid X^{q^m}-X.}
$$
