<h1 id="a/a2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Write $Q=q^m$ and take a [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) $L$ of $X^Q-X$ over $F$. Its [formal derivative](../../../../../formal-derivative.md) is $-1$, since the characteristic divides $Q$, so it has $Q$ distinct [roots](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) in $L$. Let $K$ be their set. The [Frobenius endomorphism](../../../../../frobenius-endomorphism.md) identity gives

$$
(u+v)^Q=u^Q+v^Q,\qquad(uv)^Q=u^Qv^Q.
$$

Thus $K$ contains $0,1$ and is closed under addition, negatives and multiplication. If $u\neq0$ belongs to $K$, then $(u^{-1})^Q=(u^Q)^{-1}=u^{-1}$, so it is closed under inverses too. Hence $K$ is a [field](../../../../../field.md) with exactly $Q$ elements. Every $c\in F$ satisfies $c^q=c$ by [Lagrange's theorem for finite groups](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) applied to $F^\times$, together with the zero case; iterating gives $c^Q=c$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{F\subseteq K,\qquad |K|=q^m.}
$$
