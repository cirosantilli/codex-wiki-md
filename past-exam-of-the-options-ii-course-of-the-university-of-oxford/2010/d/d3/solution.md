The [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) states that for nonnegative measurable functions $f_n\uparrow f$ almost everywhere, $\int f=\lim_n\int f_n$, allowing the value $+\infty$.

The [dominated convergence theorem](../../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md) states that if measurable functions $f_n\to f$ almost everywhere and $|f_n|\leq g$ almost everywhere for some integrable $g$, then $f$ is integrable and $\int f_n\to\int f$. In fact $\int|f_n-f|\to0$ as well. Both statements apply on any [measure space](../../../../measure-space.md).
