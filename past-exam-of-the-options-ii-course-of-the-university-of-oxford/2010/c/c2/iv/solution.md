<h1 id="c/c2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Let $R$ be the sum of the nonzero [quadratic residues](../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md). Each has two square [roots](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) by part (iii), so

$$
2R\equiv\sum_{x=1}^{p-1}x^2\equiv0\pmod p,
$$

where part (ii) applies because $p>3$ makes $p-1$ not divide $2$. Since $2$ is invertible modulo $p$, $R\equiv0$.

The sum of all nonzero classes is $p(p-1)/2\equiv0\pmod p$. Subtracting $R$ shows that the sum of [quadratic nonresidues](../../../../../quadratic-nonresidue.md) is also zero. Consequently **both requested sums vanish modulo $p$**.
