<h1 id="c/c2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) equal to $1$ says that $a$ is a nonzero square modulo $p$. Choose one [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) $r\neq0$. Then $-r$ is another [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md), distinct because $p$ is odd. Any solution $x$ satisfies

$$
0=x^2-r^2=(x-r)(x+r)
$$

in the [field](../../../../../field.md) $\mathbb F_p$, so $x=r$ or $x=-r$. Thus there are

$$
\boxed{2\text{ solutions}.}
$$
