↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [primitive root modulo $p$](../../../../../primitive-root-modulo-n.md) is a [residue](../../../../../residue.md) class generating the cyclic multiplicative [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times$, so its order is $p-1$. If $g$ is one generator, all generators are $g^j$ with $\gcd(j,p-1)=1$. Thus the number is

$$
\boxed{\varphi(p-1),}
$$

where $\varphi$ is [Euler's totient function](../../../../../euler-totient-function.md). This includes $p=2$, with $\varphi(1)=1$.
