<h1 id="p/p3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expand both squared norms using the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md):

$$
\|x\pm y\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2\pm2\operatorname{Re}\langle x,y\rangle.
$$

Adding cancels the cross terms, giving the [parallelogram law](../../../../../parallelogram-law.md)

$$
\boxed{\|x-y\|^2+\|x+y\|^2=2(\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2).}
$$

The real part may be omitted in a real inner-product space.
