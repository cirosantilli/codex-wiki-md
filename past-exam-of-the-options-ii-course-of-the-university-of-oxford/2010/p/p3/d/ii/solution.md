↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expand the [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) of the orthonormal-[basis](../../../../../../basis.md) expression. All cross terms vanish and $\|e_k\|=1$, so

$$
\boxed{\|P_nx\|^2=\sum_{k=1}^n|\langle x,e_k\rangle|^2.}
$$
