<h1 id="p/p3/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The residual $x-P_nx$ is orthogonal to $P_nx\in U_n$. Thus the [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) gives $\|x\|^2=\|x-P_nx\|^2+\|P_nx\|^2$. Substitute part (ii) and rearrange:

$$
\boxed{\|x-P_nx\|^2=\|x\|^2-\sum_{k=1}^n|\langle x,e_k\rangle|^2.}
$$

Nonnegativity of the left-hand side also gives the finite form of [Bessel's inequality](../../../../../../bessel-s-inequality.md).
