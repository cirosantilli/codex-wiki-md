<h1 id="j2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set $r_0=q\mathcal E/(m\omega^2)$ and $y=r-r_0$. Completing the square in the [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) gives

$$
\frac12m\omega^2|r|^2-q\mathcal E\cdot r
=\frac12m\omega^2|y|^2-\frac{q^2|\mathcal E|^2}{2m\omega^2}.
$$

Translation leaves the [Laplacian](../../../../laplacian.md) unchanged, and the shifted three-dimensional [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) separates into three one-dimensional oscillators. Their energies add, giving

$$
\boxed{E_N=(N+\tfrac32)\hbar\omega-\frac{q^2|\mathcal E|^2}{2m\omega^2},\qquad N=n_1+n_2+n_3.}
$$

The [energy-level degeneracy](../../../../energy-level-degeneracy.md) is the dimension of the energy eigenspace, or number of linearly independent states with that energy. Counting nonnegative integer triples of total $N$ by [stars and bars](../../../../stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md) gives

$$
\boxed{d_N=\binom{N+2}{2}=\frac{(N+1)(N+2)}2.}
$$

The constant electric [field](../../../../field.md) displaces the oscillator and shifts all levels by the same amount, preserving their degeneracies.
