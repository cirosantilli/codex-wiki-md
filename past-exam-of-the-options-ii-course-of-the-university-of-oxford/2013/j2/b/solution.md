<h1 id="j2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $\gamma=m\omega/\hbar$. Substitution of $\psi=F(y)e^{-\gamma y^2/2}$ in the one-dimensional [Schrödinger equation](../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) with energy $(n+1/2)\hbar\omega$ gives

$$
F''-2\gamma yF'+2\gamma nF=0.
$$

For degrees zero, one and two, coefficient comparison yields, up to independent nonzero constants,

$$
\boxed{F_0=1,\qquad F_1=y,\qquad F_2=y^2-\frac1{2\gamma}.}
$$

For the shifted coordinates $y_i=x_i-(r_0)_i$, let $G(y)=e^{-\gamma|y|^2/2}$. A basis of [wave functions](../../../../wave-function-split.md) for the first three energy levels is:

- $N=0$: $\boxed{G}$.
- $N=1$: $\boxed{y_1G,\ y_2G,\ y_3G}$.
- $N=2$: $\boxed{(y_i^2-1/(2\gamma))G\ (i=1,2,3),\quad y_1y_2G,\ y_1y_3G,\ y_2y_3G}$.

These are products of the one-dimensional [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) solutions. Arbitrary linear combinations within each listed eigenspace are also stationary states. Their counts $1,3,6$ agree with part (a), so the degeneracies have all been included.
