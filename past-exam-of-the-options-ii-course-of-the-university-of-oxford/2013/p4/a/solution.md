<h1 id="p4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A real square [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) is [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) when $Q^TQ=I$, equivalently when its columns form an orthonormal basis. Taking [determinants](../../../../determinant.md) gives $(\det Q)^2=1$, so

$$
\boxed{\det Q\in\{1,-1\}.}
$$

In particular it is invertible with inverse $Q^T$ and preserves the Euclidean norm.
