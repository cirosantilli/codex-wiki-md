<h1 id="p4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Comparing the $j$th columns of $A=QR$ gives $a_j=Qr_j$. Thus

$$
\|a_j\|^2=r_j^TQ^TQr_j=r_j^Tr_j=\|r_j\|^2.
$$

Taking nonnegative square roots proves $\boxed{\|a_j\|=\|r_j\|}$. This is simply norm preservation by an orthogonal [matrix](../../../../matrix.md).
