<h1 id="d4/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each integer $m\geq1$, list the $m^2$ [functions](../../../../../function-split.md)

$$
f_{m,j}(x)=m\,\mathbf1_{[j/m^2,(j+1)/m^2)}(x),\qquad0\leq j<m^2,
$$

in increasing $j$, and concatenate the blocks in increasing $m$. Each [function](../../../../../function-split.md) has $L^1$ norm $1/m$, so the resulting [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) has norm tending to zero. Every $x\in(0,1)$ lies in exactly one interval of each block, where the [function](../../../../../function-split.md) takes value $m$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\|f_n\|_1\to0,\qquad\limsup_n f_n(x)=\infty\quad(0<x<1).}
$$

This [unbounded typewriter sequence with vanishing L1 norm](../../../../../unbounded-typewriter-sequence-with-vanishing-l1-norm.md) is a moving-spike construction: the support shrinks fast enough to beat the increasing height.
