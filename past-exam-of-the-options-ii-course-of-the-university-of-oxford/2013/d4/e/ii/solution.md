<h1 id="d4/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**No such [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) exists.** By [Fatou's lemma](../../../../../fatou-s-lemma.md) on $(0,1)$,

$$
\int_0^1\liminf_n|g_n(x)|\,dx
\leq\liminf_n\int_0^1|g_n(x)|\,dx
\leq\liminf_n\|g_n\|_1=0.
$$

But the proposed nonnegative measurable integrand is positive at every point of $(0,1)$, so its [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) cannot be zero. Explicitly, the sets where it is at least $1/k$ cover $(0,1)$; at least one has positive measure, forcing a positive [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md). This contradicts the displayed bound.
