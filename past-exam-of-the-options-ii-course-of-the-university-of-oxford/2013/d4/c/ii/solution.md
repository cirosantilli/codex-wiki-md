<h1 id="d4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each $n$, let $N_n$ be the [null set](../../../../../null-set.md) where $f_n\ne g_n$. Their union $N$ is null. At any $x\notin N$, the two [sequences](../../../../../sequence.md) agree term by term, so their [pointwise limits](../../../../../pointwise-limit.md) are equal: $f(x)=g(x)$. Therefore **$f\sim g$**. Taking the countable union first is essential: it produces one exceptional set outside which all equalities hold simultaneously.
