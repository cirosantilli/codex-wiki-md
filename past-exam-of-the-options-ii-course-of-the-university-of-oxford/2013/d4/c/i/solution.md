<h1 id="d4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The relation is reflexive because the exceptional set for $f=f$ is empty, and symmetric because the exceptional sets for $f=g$ and $g=f$ agree. If $f=g$ off a [null set](../../../../../null-set.md) $N_1$ and $g=h$ off a [null set](../../../../../null-set.md) $N_2$, then $f=h$ off $N_1\cup N_2$, which is null by part (b). Hence it is transitive as well, so **equality [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md) is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../equivalence-relation.md)**.
