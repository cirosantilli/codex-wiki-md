<h1 id="d4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Given $\epsilon>0$, cover $E_1$ by open intervals of total length less than $\epsilon/2$, and cover $E_2$ similarly. Combining the two countable covers gives a cover of their union of total length less than $\epsilon$. Thus **$E_1\cup E_2$ is a [null set](../../../../../null-set.md)**.
