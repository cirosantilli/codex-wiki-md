<h1 id="d4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each $n\geq1$, choose a countable interval cover of $E_n$ of total length below $\epsilon/2^n$. The union of these covers is countable and covers $\bigcup_n E_n$, with total length less than $\sum_n\epsilon/2^n=\epsilon$. As $\epsilon$ is arbitrary, **a countable union of [null sets](../../../../../null-set.md) is null**.
