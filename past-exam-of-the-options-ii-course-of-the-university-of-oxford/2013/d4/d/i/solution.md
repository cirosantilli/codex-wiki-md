↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Exactly those integrable [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) that vanish [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md):

$$
\boxed{\|f\|_1=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f=0\text{ almost everywhere}.}
$$

Thus this expression is a seminorm on actual [functions](../../../../../function-split.md), becoming a norm after the quotient in part (ii).
