<h1 id="d3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $\alpha>0$, [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) on $[0,R]$ gives

$$
\int_0^R xe^{-\alpha x}\,dx
=\frac1{\alpha^2}-e^{-\alpha R}\left(\frac R\alpha+\frac1{\alpha^2}\right).
$$

The exponential remainder tends to zero. Since the integrand is nonnegative, the [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) applied to truncations identifies the improper [integral](../../../../integral-split.md) with the Lebesgue [integral](../../../../integral-split.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_0^\infty xe^{-\alpha x}\,dx=\alpha^{-2}.}
$$

Its finite value proves integrability as well as evaluating it.
