↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**Yes, it is a [uniformly most powerful test](../../../../uniformly-most-powerful-test.md) for the one-sided alternative.** For every fixed $\sigma_1^2>\sigma_0^2$, the likelihood ratio is increasing in the same statistic $S$. The size-$\alpha$ threshold found in part (c) depends only on the null [variance](../../../../variance-split.md) and $\alpha$, not on the selected alternative [variance](../../../../variance-split.md). Thus one and the same test is most powerful against each simple member of the composite alternative, which is exactly uniform most powerfulness.
