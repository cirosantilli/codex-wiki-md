<h1 id="b2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On the set of left [cosets](../../../../coset.md) $G/H$, define $g\cdot(xH)=(gx)H$. Given $xH$ and $yH$, the element $yx^{-1}$ sends the first to the second. Thus **the [coset action](../../../../coset-action.md) is [transitive](../../../../transitive-group-action.md)**. In particular the [orbit](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) of $H$ is the entire [coset](../../../../coset.md) space, and its [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is $H$.
