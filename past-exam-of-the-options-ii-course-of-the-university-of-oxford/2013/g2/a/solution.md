<h1 id="g2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For fixed endpoint variations, the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) are

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_i}-\frac{\partial L}{\partial q_i}=0,\qquad i=1,\ldots,n.}
$$

Indeed the [first variation](../../../../first-variation.md) is $\int\sum_i(L_{q_i}\delta q_i+L_{\dot q_i}\delta\dot q_i)dt$. [Integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) removes the [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md) from the variations; their zero endpoint values remove the boundary term. The fundamental lemma then makes every remaining coefficient vanish.
