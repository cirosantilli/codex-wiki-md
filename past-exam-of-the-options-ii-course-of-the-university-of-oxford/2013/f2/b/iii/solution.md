<h1 id="f2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $n\geq1$, the unit sphere is nonempty and [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) by the [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md). The [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $k(x)=\|Ax\|^2$ is continuous and therefore attains a minimum and a maximum on it by the [extreme value theorem](../../../../../extreme-value-theorem.md). Either point supplies a nonzero [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) in part (ii), with a real multiplier. Thus **$A^TA$ always has a real [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md)**, indeed a nonnegative one. This gives existence without assuming the spectral theorem first.
