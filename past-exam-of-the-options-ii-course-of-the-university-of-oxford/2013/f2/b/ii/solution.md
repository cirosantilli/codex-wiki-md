<h1 id="f2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the constraint set, $\|x\|=1$, so $\nabla f(x)=2x\ne0$. Part (a)(iv) and the [Lagrange multiplier theorem](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) give $2A^TAx=2\lambda x$, hence

$$
\boxed{A^TAx=\lambda x.}
$$

Since $x$ is nonzero, it is an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md). Moreover $\lambda=x^TA^TAx=\|Ax\|^2\geq0$, so the associated [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is real and nonnegative.
