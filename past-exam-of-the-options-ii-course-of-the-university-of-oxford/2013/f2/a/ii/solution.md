↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The map is **[continuously differentiable](../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md)** if it is differentiable at every point of $U$ and the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) map $x\mapsto DF_x$ is continuous. In finite dimensions this is equivalent to all first partial [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) of all its components existing and being continuous on $U$. The class is denoted $C^1(U,\mathbb R^m)$.
