<h1 id="f2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $F:U\subseteq\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ is differentiable at $x$, and $G$ is defined on a neighborhood of $F(x)$ and differentiable there, the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) states

$$
\boxed{D(G\circ F)_x=DG_{F(x)}\circ DF_x.}
$$

In standard coordinates the same statement is

$$
\boxed{J_{G\circ F}(x)=J_G(F(x))J_F(x).}
$$

The evaluation point in the first factor is $F(x)$, and the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) order follows the order in which the [linear maps](../../../../../linear-map.md) act on increments.
