↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [total derivative](../../../../../total-derivative.md) of $F$ at $x$ is the [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $DF_x:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ such that

$$
\boxed{F(x+h)=F(x)+DF_x(h)+o(\|h\|).}
$$

The [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) is the $m\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $J_F(x)=(\partial F_i/\partial x_j)(x)$. When the total [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) exists, this is its [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) in standard bases: $DF_x(h)=J_F(x)h$. Existence of partial [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) alone need not imply existence of the total [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md).
