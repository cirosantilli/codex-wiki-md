↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md) is **[compact](../../../../../compact-space.md)** if every [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) of it has a finite subcover. That is, if $X=\bigcup_{i\in I}U_i$ with all $U_i$ open, finitely many of those same [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) already cover $X$.
