<h1 id="e4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose no $F_N$ is contained in $U$. Then $K_i=F_i\cap(X\setminus U)$ are nested nonempty closed subsets of the [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) space. Part (ii) gives a point in their intersection. Such a point lies both in $\bigcap_iF_i$ and outside $U$, contradicting the hypothesis. Thus some $F_N\subseteq U$, and nesting gives

$$
\boxed{F_i\subseteq U\quad\text{for every }i\geq N.}
$$

[Compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md) upgrades containment of the limiting intersection to eventual containment of the whole nested sets.
