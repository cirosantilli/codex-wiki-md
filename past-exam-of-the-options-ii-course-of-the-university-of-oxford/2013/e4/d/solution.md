<h1 id="e4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Put $K=\bigcap_iF_i$. If some $F_i$ is empty, so is $K$, which is [connected](../../../../connected-space.md). Otherwise the [compact](../../../../compact-space.md) sets are closed in the Hausdorff ambient space, and can all be considered as nested nonempty closed subsets of the [compact](../../../../compact-space.md) space $F_0$. Part (a)(ii) gives $K\ne\varnothing$, and $K$ is [compact](../../../../compact-space.md).

Suppose $K$ were disconnected. Part (c)(iii) gives disjoint ambient [open sets](../../../../open-set.md) $U,V$ covering $K$, both meeting it. Part (a)(iii), applied within $F_0$ to $U\cup V$, gives $F_N\subseteq U\cup V$ for some $N$. Both $U$ and $V$ meet $F_N$ because they meet $K\subseteq F_N$. Their intersections with $F_N$ would therefore disconnect $F_N$, a contradiction. Thus

$$
\boxed{K\text{ is connected}.}
$$
