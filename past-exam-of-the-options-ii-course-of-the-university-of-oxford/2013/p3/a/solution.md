<h1 id="p3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With the [weighted inner product](../../../../weighted-inner-product.md) $\langle p,q\rangle_w=\int_{-1}^1p(x)q(x)w(x)\,dx$, a family of [orthogonal polynomials](../../../../orthogonal-polynomial-split.md) has $\deg p_j=j$, each $p_j$ nonzero, and

$$
\boxed{\langle p_j,p_k\rangle_w=0\quad(j\ne k).}
$$

Continuity and positivity of the weight make the [integral](../../../../integral-split.md) finite and give $\langle p_j,p_j\rangle_w>0$. Unit norm or monic leading coefficient is an additional optional normalization.
