<h1 id="o5/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

There are twelve cells and one fitted parameter, so the likelihood-ratio reference is approximately $\chi_{10}^2$. The supplied value gives

$$
\boxed{p\text{-value}\approx1-F_{10}(14)=0.18.}
$$

Thus **do not reject the Poisson goodness-of-fit model at the conventional 5% level**; it would also not be rejected at 10%. The data do not provide strong evidence of a departure from this model. This is a compatibility conclusion, not proof that the Poisson model is true. A fully specified Poisson mean would instead use eleven degrees of freedom, which is not the fitted situation here.
