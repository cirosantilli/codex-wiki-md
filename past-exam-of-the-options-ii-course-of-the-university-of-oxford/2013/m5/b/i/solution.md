<h1 id="m5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Chebyshev's inequality](../../../../../chebyshev-s-inequality.md) states $\mathbb P(|Y-\mu|\geq r)\leq\sigma^2/r^2$ for $r>0$ when $Y$ has mean $\mu$ and finite [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$. Here each summand has mean zero and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) one, so independence gives $\mathbb ES_n=0$ and $\operatorname{Var}S_n=n$. Taking $r=an$ gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(|S_n|\geq an)\leq\min\left(1,\frac1{a^2n}\right).}
$$
