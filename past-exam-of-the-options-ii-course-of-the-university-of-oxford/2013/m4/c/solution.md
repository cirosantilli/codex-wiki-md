<h1 id="m4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

After a jump from one to $k$, the first return to one occurs exactly $k$ steps later, with [probability](../../../../probability.md) $\alpha_k$. Later return times are sums of such positive-support values. Thus their greatest common divisor is

$$
d=\gcd\{k:\alpha_k>0\}.
$$

In an irreducible chain this is the common [period](../../../../period-of-a-state-in-a-markov-chain.md) of every state. Hence **the chain is [aperiodic](../../../../aperiodic-markov-chain.md) exactly when $d=1$**. A self-loop at one is sufficient but not necessary; support at two and three also has greatest common divisor one.
