<h1 id="m4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The first return time $\tau_1$ has distribution $\mathbb P_1(\tau_1=k)=\alpha_k$. It is finite almost surely, so state one is recurrent. It is [positive recurrent](../../../../positive-recurrent-markov-chain.md) precisely when its mean return time is finite:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E_1\tau_1=\sum_{k\geq1}k\alpha_k<\infty.}
$$

By irreducibility the same recurrence class contains every state. Thus the condition is finiteness of the [expected value](../../../../expected-value-split.md) of the positive-integer distribution $(\alpha_k)$. If that mean is infinite, the chain is null recurrent, not transient.
