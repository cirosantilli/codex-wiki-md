<h1 id="b/b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $C_n=\langle g\rangle$ and set $N=\langle g^p\rangle$. The [order](../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) of $g^p$ is $n/\gcd(n,p)=m$, so $N\cong C_m$. Every [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) of an [abelian group](../../../../../abelian-group.md) is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md). The [quotient group](../../../../../quotient-group.md) $C_n/N$ is generated by $gN$ and has order $n/m=p$, hence is $C_p$ and is a [simple group](../../../../../simple-group.md). By the [subgroup correspondence theorem](../../../../../subgroup-correspondence-theorem.md), there is no proper [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) strictly between $N$ and $C_n$. Therefore **$N$ is a maximal [normal subgroup](../../../../../normal-subgroup.md)** with the required [quotient group](../../../../../quotient-group.md).
