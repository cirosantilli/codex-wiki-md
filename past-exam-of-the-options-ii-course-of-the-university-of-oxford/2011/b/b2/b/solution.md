In the [Klein four-group](../../../../../klein-four-group.md) $V=\{1,a,b,ab\}$, the chains

$$
1\triangleleft\langle a\rangle\triangleleft V,
\qquad
1\triangleleft\langle b\rangle\triangleleft V
$$

are distinct [composition series](../../../../../composition-series.md). All their [composition factors](../../../../../composition-factor.md) are $C_2$.

The [Jordan–Hölder theorem](../../../../../jordan-holder-theorem.md) states that any two [composition series](../../../../../composition-series.md) of a [group](../../../../../group-split.md) have the same length and the same [composition factors](../../../../../composition-factor.md) up to permutation and [group isomorphism](../../../../../group-isomorphism.md). Thus the [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) chains may differ, while the multiset of simple [quotients](../../../../../quotient-group.md) is intrinsic.
