**False.** Let

$$
k=\mathbf1_{\bigcup_{n\geq1}[n,n+2^{-n-2}]}.
$$

This is a bounded [measurable function](../../../../../../measurable-function.md), and the intervals are disjoint, so

$$
\int_0^\infty|k(x)|\,dx=\sum_{n\geq1}2^{-n-2}<\infty.
$$

Nevertheless $k(n)=1$ for every positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $n$, while $k(n+1/2)=0$. Hence it has no limit at infinity. Narrow spikes separate [Lebesgue integrability](../../../../../../integrability.md) from pointwise decay.
