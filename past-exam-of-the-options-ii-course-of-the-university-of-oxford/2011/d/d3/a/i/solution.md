↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** Take the bounded [measurable function](../../../../../../measurable-function.md)

$$
k(x)=\begin{cases}(1+x)^{-1},&x>0,\\0,&x\leq0.\end{cases}
$$

It tends to zero at positive infinity, but

$$
\int_0^R|k(x)|\,dx=\log(1+R)\longrightarrow\infty.
$$

Thus decay to zero alone does not imply [Lebesgue integrability](../../../../../../integrability.md).
