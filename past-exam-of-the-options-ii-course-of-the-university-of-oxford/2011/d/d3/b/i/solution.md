↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $H=\int_0^\pi|h(t)|\,dt<\infty$. By [periodicity](../../../../../../periodic-function.md) and [countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md) of the [Lebesgue integral](../../../../../../lebesgue-integral.md) of a nonnegative function,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^\infty e^{-\beta x}|h(x)|\,dx
&=\sum_{k=0}^\infty e^{-\beta k\pi}
\int_0^\pi e^{-\beta t}|h(t)|\,dt\\
&\leq H\sum_{k=0}^\infty e^{-\beta k\pi}
=\frac{H}{1-e^{-\beta\pi}}<\infty.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus **$f$ is Lebesgue integrable** for every $\beta>0$.
