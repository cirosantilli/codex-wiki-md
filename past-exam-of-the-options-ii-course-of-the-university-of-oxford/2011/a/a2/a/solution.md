<h1 id="a/a2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) of $f$ over $F$ is a [field extension](../../../../../field-extension.md) $K/F$ in which $f$ is a product of linear factors and which is generated over $F$ by its roots. The generation condition rules out adjoining unrelated elements.

For existence, induct on the [degree of a polynomial](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md). Choose an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) divisor $p$ of $f$. The [quotient ring](../../../../../quotient-ring.md) $F[X]/(p)$ is a [field](../../../../../field.md) because $(p)$ is a [maximal ideal](../../../../../maximal-ideal.md); the class $\alpha$ of $X$ is a [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) of $p$, hence of $f$. Over this [field extension](../../../../../field-extension.md), divide out $X-\alpha$ and apply induction to the remaining [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md). This gives an extension in which $f$ splits. Inside it, the [subfield](../../../../../subfield.md) generated by all roots is a [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md). The induction starts with a linear [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md), whose [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is already $F$. Thus **every positive-degree [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has a splitting [field](../../../../../field.md)**; being monic is unnecessary for existence.
