<h1 id="a/a2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The cubic has no [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) in $\mathbb F_2$, since its values at $0$ and $1$ are both $1$. A cubic over a [field](../../../../../field.md) is [irreducible](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) precisely when it has no [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md). Consequently

$$
K=\mathbb F_2[\alpha],\qquad \alpha^3+\alpha+1=0,
$$

is a [field extension](../../../../../field-extension.md) of [degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-field-extension.md) $3$, with eight elements. The [Frobenius endomorphism](../../../../../frobenius-endomorphism.md) $x\mapsto x^2$ takes a [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) of a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) over $\mathbb F_2$ to another [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md), so the roots are $\alpha,\alpha^2,\alpha^4$. They are distinct: equality between any two would make the [Frobenius endomorphism](../../../../../frobenius-endomorphism.md) orbit have length less than $3$, contradicting the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) of $\alpha$. They all lie in $K$, and one of them generates $K$. Hence

$$
\boxed{K\cong\mathbb F_8,\qquad [K:\mathbb F_2]=3.}
$$
