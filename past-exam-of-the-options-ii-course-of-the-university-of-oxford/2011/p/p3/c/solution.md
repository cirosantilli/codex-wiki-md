<h1 id="p/p3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Define the reflected [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $\widetilde p(x)=-p(-x)$. It belongs to $\Pi_n$. Since $u$ is odd, a change of variable and the evenness of the weight give

$$
\|u-\widetilde p\|^2
=\int_{-1}^1x^2(u(x)+p(-x))^2\,dx
=\int_{-1}^1x^2(u(x)-p(x))^2\,dx.
$$

Thus $\widetilde p$ is another [polynomial least-squares approximation](../../../../../polynomial-least-squares-approximation.md). By uniqueness, $\widetilde p=p$, so

$$
\boxed{p(-x)=-p(x).}
$$

The conclusion includes the zero [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md), which is both odd and even.
