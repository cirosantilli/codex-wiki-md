↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [polynomial least-squares approximation](../../../../../polynomial-least-squares-approximation.md) $p_n\in\Pi_n$ minimizes the weighted residual [norm](../../../../../norm.md):

$$
\boxed{\|u-p_n\|=\min_{q\in\Pi_n}\|u-q\|.}
$$

Equivalently it minimizes $\int_{-1}^1x^2(u(x)-q(x))^2\,dx$. This is a best approximation within a finite-dimensional [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) space, without an interpolation requirement.
