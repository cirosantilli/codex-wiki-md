<h1 id="p/p3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

By part (c), the best [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $2$ is odd, hence has the form $p_2(x)=cx$. The [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) condition with $q=x$ gives

$$
c=\frac{(x^{2011},x)}{(x,x)}
=\frac{\int_{-1}^1x^{2014}\,dx}{\int_{-1}^1x^4\,dx}
=\frac{2/2015}{2/5}=\frac1{403}.
$$

The residual is odd, so its weighted inner products with the even polynomials $1$ and $x^2$ vanish automatically. Thus all three [normal equations](../../../../../normal-equations-for-linear-least-squares.md) hold and

$$
\boxed{p_2(x)=\frac{x}{403}.}
$$
