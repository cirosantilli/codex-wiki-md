<h1 id="f/f2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Writing $f=(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$, the $j$th [partial derivative](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) at $x$ is the vector limit

$$
\partial_j f(x)=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(x+te_j)-f(x)}t,
$$

when it exists. The [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) is a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) $Df(x):\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ satisfying

$$
f(x+h)=f(x)+Df(x)h+o(\|h\|).
$$

The [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) has entries $J_f(x)_{ij}=\partial_j f_i(x)$. If the [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) exists, its matrix in the standard [bases](../../../../../../basis.md) is precisely $J_f(x)$, because substitution of $h=te_j$ identifies every column. Existence of the [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) alone does not in general imply existence of the [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md).
