↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a scalar-valued differentiable function, its [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is the column vector

$$
\nabla f(x)=\begin{pmatrix}\partial_1f(x)\\\vdots\\\partial_nf(x)\end{pmatrix}.
$$

It represents the [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) by the [Euclidean inner product](../../../../../../dot-product.md):

$$
\boxed{Df(x)h=\nabla f(x)\cdot h.}
$$

Thus $J_f(x)=\nabla f(x)^T$ is a row matrix; the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) and the [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) represent the same first-order information but have different types.
