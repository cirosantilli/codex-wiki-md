↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) states that $f\circ g$ is differentiable at $x$ and

$$
\boxed{D(f\circ g)(x)=Df(g(x))\circ Dg(x).}
$$

In terms of [Jacobian matrices](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md),

$$
\boxed{J_{f\circ g}(x)=J_f(g(x))J_g(x).}
$$

The factors have dimensions $k\times m$ and $m\times n$, respectively, giving the required $k\times n$ [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md).
