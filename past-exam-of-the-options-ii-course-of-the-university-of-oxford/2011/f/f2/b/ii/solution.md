At $(-1,0)$ the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is $(1/2,0)^T$. Therefore the [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) is the [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md)

$$
\boxed{Df(-1,0)(h,k)=\frac h2,}
$$

represented by the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}1/2&0\end{pmatrix}$.
