<h1 id="f/f2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) of $g$ sends a scalar increment $s$ to

$$
Dg(t)s=s\begin{pmatrix}\cos t\\-\sin t\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Since $\sin^2t+\cos^2t=1$, the [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) $f\circ g$ is constantly $1/2$, so $D(f\circ g)(t)=0$. Directly, the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) product is

$$
J_f(g(t))J_g(t)=
\begin{pmatrix}-\sin t/2&-\cos t/2\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}\cos t\\-\sin t\end{pmatrix}=0.
$$

This verifies the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) at every $t$.
