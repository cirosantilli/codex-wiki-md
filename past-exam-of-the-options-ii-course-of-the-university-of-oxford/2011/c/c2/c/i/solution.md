Here $b$ is odd and nonzero. Choose $k=4|b|-1$. This is a positive odd [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md), $k\equiv3\pmod4$, and $\gcd(k,b)=1$. By [multiplicativity of the Jacobi symbol](../../../../../../multiplicativity-of-the-jacobi-symbol.md),

$$
\left(\frac ak\right)=\left(\frac{-1}k\right)\left(\frac bk\right)^2
=(-1)^{(k-1)/2}\cdot1=\boxed{-1}.
$$

The coprimality condition ensures that the squared [Jacobi symbol](../../../../../../jacobi-symbol.md) is $1$ rather than $0$.
