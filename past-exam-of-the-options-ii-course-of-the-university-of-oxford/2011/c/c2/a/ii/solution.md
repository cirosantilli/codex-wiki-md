The [Euler criterion](../../../../../../euler-criterion.md) states that for an odd [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$ and $p\nmid a$,

$$
\boxed{a^{(p-1)/2}\equiv\left(\frac ap\right)\pmod p.}
$$

The same [modular congruence](../../../../../../modular-congruence.md) remains true when $p\mid a$, with both sides zero.
