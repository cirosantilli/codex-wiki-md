<h1 id="m/m5/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

State $0$ is an [absorbing state](../../../../../../absorbing-state.md) and a [recurrent state](../../../../../../recurrent-state.md). From every positive state, the stock decreases strictly at the next step and can never increase, so a return to that positive state is impossible. Thus **all positive states are transient**, with return [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) zero. Starting from $N$, absorption occurs within at most $N$ sticker-taking children.
