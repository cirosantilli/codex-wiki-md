<h1 id="m/m5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [state space](../../../../../../state-space.md) is $\{0,1,\ldots,N\}$, with initial state $X_1=N$. With rows indexed by the current state and columns by the next state, the [transition matrix](../../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{P_{ij}=\begin{cases}
1,&i=j=0,\\
1/i,&1\leq i\leq N,\quad0\leq j<i,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}}
$$

The state strictly decreases at each step until absorption at $0$.
