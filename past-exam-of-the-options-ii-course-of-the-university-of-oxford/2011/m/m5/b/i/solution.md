<h1 id="m/m5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [state space](../../../../../../state-space.md) $\{1,\ldots,N\}$, since an empty box is refilled immediately. The [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) that formerly led from $i$ to $0$ now leads to $N$. Thus the [transition matrix](../../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{P_{ij}=\begin{cases}
1/i,&1\leq j<i,\\
1/i,&j=N,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}}
$$

For $i=N$, the second line includes the self-loop obtained when all stickers are taken. When $N=1$, this is simply the one-state [Markov chain](../../../../../../markov-chain-split.md).
