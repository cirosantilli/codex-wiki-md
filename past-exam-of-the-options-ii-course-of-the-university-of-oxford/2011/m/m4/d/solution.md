<h1 id="m/m4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $x\geq0$, the [geometric distribution](../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) tail gives

$$
P(T_k>x)=P(S_1>kx)
=\left(1-\frac1k\right)^{\lfloor kx\rfloor}
\longrightarrow e^{-x}.
$$

For $x<0$, $P(T_k\leq x)=0$. The limiting [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is therefore $0$ for negative $x$ and $1-e^{-x}$ for nonnegative $x$, and it is continuous everywhere. This proves

$$
\boxed{T_k\xrightarrow{d}\operatorname{Exp}(1),}
$$

with rate parameter $1$ and mean $1$.
