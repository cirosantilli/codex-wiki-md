<h1 id="m/m4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiate the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md):

$$
g'(z)=\frac52(14-5z)^{-3/2},\qquad
g''(z)=\frac{75}4(14-5z)^{-5/2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\mathbb ET=g'(1)=\frac5{54},\qquad
\mathbb E[T(T-1)]=g''(1)=\frac{25}{324}.
$$

Using the relation between the second factorial moment and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md),

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(T)=g''(1)+g'(1)-g'(1)^2
=\frac{235}{1458}.}
$$
