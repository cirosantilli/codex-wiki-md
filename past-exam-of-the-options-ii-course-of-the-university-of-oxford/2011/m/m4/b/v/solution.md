<h1 id="m/m4/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Apply [Markov inequality](../../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) to the nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $2^T$. On $\{T\geq5\}$ it is at least $32$, so

$$
\boxed{P(T\geq5)\leq\frac{\mathbb E(2^T)}{32}=\frac7{192}<0.05.}
$$

This exponential bound is substantially stronger here than applying [Markov inequality](../../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) directly to $T$.
