↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [binomial series](../../../../../../binomial-series.md) gives, for $|z|<14/5$,

$$
(14-5z)^{-1/2}
=\frac1{\sqrt{14}}\sum_{n=0}^\infty
\frac{\binom{2n}n}{4^n}\left(\frac5{14}\right)^n z^n.
$$

All coefficients are nonnegative, and $g(1)=2/3+1/3=1$. Thus define

$$
P(T=0)=\frac23+\frac1{\sqrt{14}},\qquad
P(T=n)=\frac1{\sqrt{14}}\binom{2n}n\left(\frac5{56}\right)^n\quad(n\geq1).
$$

These numbers sum to $1$, so they define a [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) on the nonnegative integers with the desired [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md).
