<h1 id="m/m4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $Z$ and $u>0$, [Markov inequality](../../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) states

$$
\boxed{P(Z\geq u)\leq\frac{\mathbb EZ}{u}.}
$$

It follows by taking expectations in $Z\geq u\mathbf1_{\{Z\geq u\}}$. A finite [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) makes the bound informative.
