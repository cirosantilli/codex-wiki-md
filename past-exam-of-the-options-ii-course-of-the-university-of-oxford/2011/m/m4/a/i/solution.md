↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) is $M_X(t)=\mathbb E(e^{tX})$ at real $t$ for which this [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is finite. The [characteristic function](../../../../../../characteristic-function-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\varphi_X(t)=\mathbb E(e^{itX}),\qquad t\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Unlike the [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md), the [characteristic function](../../../../../../characteristic-function-split.md) always exists, because $|e^{itX}|=1$.
