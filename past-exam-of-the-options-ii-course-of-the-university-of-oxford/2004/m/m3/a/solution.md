↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [moment-generating function](../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) of a [random variable](../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $X$ is $M_X(t)=\mathbb E[e^{tX}]$ wherever this [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is finite. Existence on an open interval containing zero is the condition needed for the usual uniqueness and convergence theorems. For the [exponential distribution](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with rate $\lambda>0$,

$$
M_X(t)=\int_0^\infty\lambda e^{-(\lambda-t)x}\,dx
=\boxed{\frac\lambda{\lambda-t}\quad(t<\lambda)}.
$$

For $t\geq\lambda$ the integral diverges. Differentiating at zero gives mean $1/\lambda$ and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) $1/\lambda^2$.
