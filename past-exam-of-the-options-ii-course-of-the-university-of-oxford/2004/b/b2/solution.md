<h1 id="b/b2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B2](../b2.md)

Take $\boxed{K=F[x]/(f)}$ and let $\alpha$ be the [residue](../../../../residue.md) class of $x$. Division with remainder represents every class by a [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree less than $n$, so every element is a [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $\alpha$ and $f(\alpha)=0$. A nonzero class represented by $u$ has $\gcd(u,f)=1$ because $f$ is irreducible. Bézout's identity gives $au+bf=1$, making the class of $a$ its inverse. Thus $K$ is a [field](../../../../field.md), containing $F$ through its constant classes.

A [splitting field](../../../../splitting-field.md) of a nonconstant [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) is an extension in which it factors into linear factors and which is generated over the original [field](../../../../field.md) by its roots. To construct one, choose an irreducible factor and use the preceding quotient construction to adjoin a root. Divide off a linear factor in that extension and repeat on the remaining [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md). Each stage lowers the remaining degree, so finitely many stages suffice. The [field](../../../../field.md) generated by all roots in the resulting [field](../../../../field.md) is the required [splitting field](../../../../splitting-field.md). This induction works with repeated roots and does not assume separability. The two roman clauses in the source specify properties of this one construction; they are not separate questions.
