The roots are $\pm\sqrt{-5}$, so one root already supplies both. The [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has no rational root and is irreducible of degree two. Thus its [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-5})$, with $\boxed{[K:\mathbb Q]=2}$.
