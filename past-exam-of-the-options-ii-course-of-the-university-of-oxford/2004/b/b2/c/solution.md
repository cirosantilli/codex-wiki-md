<h1 id="b/b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Over $\mathbb F_5$,

$$
x^3+3=(x-3)(x^2+3x+4).
$$

The quadratic discriminant is $9-16\equiv3$, while the squares modulo five are $0,1,4$. Thus that factor is irreducible. Adjoining either quadratic root creates a degree-two extension and supplies the other root by the sum-of-roots relation. Consequently the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb F_{25}$ and $\boxed{[K:\mathbb F_5]=2}$.
