<h1 id="d/d4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under the bounded-support convention, **false in general, true if $f(0)=0$**. For the first claim, take $g=0$ and $f\equiv1$: the composition is one on all of $\mathbb R$, and cannot have bounded support. For the second, refine the finitely many defining intervals into disjoint intervals on which $g$ is constant. The composition is constant on each of them and vanishes outside their bounded union because $f(0)=0$. Hence it is another [step function](../../../../../step-function.md).

Under the unrestricted piecewise-constant convention, **true in both cases**: composition with any function preserves constancy on the same partition, including its unbounded cells. This also applies to the broader measurable-partition convention. [Continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of $f$ is not needed for this particular assertion.
