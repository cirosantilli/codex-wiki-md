↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**True in both cases.** For every open $U$, [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of $f$ makes $f^{-1}(U)$ open. Then

$$
(f\circ g)^{-1}(U)=g^{-1}(f^{-1}(U))
$$

is [Lebesgue measurable](../../../../../lebesgue-measurable-set.md) by [measurability](../../../../../measurability.md) of $g$. The value $f(0)$ has no bearing on this [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md) argument.
