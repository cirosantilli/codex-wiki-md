Lebesgue [measurability](../../../../measurability.md) of $g$ means that $g^{-1}(U)$ is a [Lebesgue measurable](../../../../lebesgue-measurable-set.md) subset of $\mathbb R$ for every open subset $U\subseteq\mathbb R$. Equivalently one can check all sets $\{x:g(x)>t\}$ with real $t$.

There are two conventions for a [step function](../../../../step-function.md) on the whole real line. Under the bounded-support integration convention it is a finite [linear combination](../../../../linear-combination.md) of indicators of bounded intervals; under the unrestricted piecewise-constant convention nonzero constant tails are allowed. The conclusions below state both conventions explicitly, since only the first assertion depends on this choice.
