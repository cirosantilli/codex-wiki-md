<h1 id="o/o4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [likelihood ratio](../../../../../likelihood-ratio.md) for the two binomial probabilities is

$$
\frac{f_{3/4}(y)}{f_{1/4}(y)}=3^{2y-5},
$$

strictly increasing in $y$. By the [Neyman-Pearson lemma](../../../../../neyman-pearson-lemma.md), reject in the highest upper tail. Under the null,

$$
\mathbb P_{1/4}(Y\geq4)=\frac{5\cdot3+1}{4^5}=\frac{16}{1024}=\frac1{64}.
$$

Hence **reject exactly when $Y\geq4$**, with no randomization required. Its [statistical power](../../../../../statistical-power.md) at the stated alternative is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P_{3/4}(Y\geq4)=\frac{5\cdot3^4+3^5}{4^5}=\frac{81}{128}}.
$$
