An [estimator](../../../../../estimator.md) $T$ of a parameter or estimand $g(\theta)$ is an [unbiased estimator](../../../../../unbiased-estimator.md) if its [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) exists and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E_\theta[T]=g(\theta)\quad\text{for every admissible }\theta}.
$$

Unbiasedness concerns the sampling mean of the [estimator](../../../../../estimator.md) over repetitions, rather than equality to the target for any particular observed sample.
