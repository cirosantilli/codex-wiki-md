↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Reading positive entries as directed edges, the [communicating class](../../../../../communicating-class.md) decomposition is

$$
\boxed{\{1,5,8,9\}\text{ closed};\qquad\{2,3\}\text{ open};\qquad\{4,6,7\}\text{ open}}.
$$

For the first class, paths $1\to5\to8\to9\to1$ establish mutual reachability, and every transition from one of these states stays in this set. States 2 and 3 communicate, but state 2 can enter the first class and never return. States 4,6,7 communicate through $4\to6\to7\to4$, but state 6 can enter state 2 with no route back. These paths also exclude any larger [communicating class](../../../../../communicating-class.md). In a finite [Markov chain](../../../../../markov-chain-split.md), the closed class is recurrent and the two open classes are transient.
