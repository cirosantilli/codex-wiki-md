<h1 id="b/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\zeta=e^{\pi i/4}$. Its odd powers are exactly the four roots, so $\mathbb Q(\zeta)$ is the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md). The [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $x^4+1$ is [irreducible](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md): after the invertible substitution $x\mapsto x+1$, it becomes $x^4+4x^3+6x^2+4x+2$, which satisfies [Eisenstein criterion](../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at two. Hence

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb Q(\zeta):\mathbb Q]=4.}
$$

Equivalently the [field](../../../../../field.md) is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i)$, since $\zeta=(1+i)/\sqrt2$, $\zeta^2=i$ and $\zeta+\zeta^{-1}=\sqrt2$.
