First address the general splitting-field request. A [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is an extension in which the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) factors into linear factors and which is generated over the base [field](../../../../../field.md) by its roots. For a degree-$m$ [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md), adjoin a root of an [irreducible](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) factor. The [quotient](../../../../../quotient.md) construction in the preceding parts gives an extension of degree at most $m$. Remove the resulting linear factor and repeat, now with a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree $m-1$ over that extension. Induction and the [tower law](../../../../../tower-law.md) give

$$
[L:F]\leq m(m-1)\cdots1=m!.
$$

The resulting [field](../../../../../field.md) is generated by the roots and is a [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md). Repeated roots or inseparability can only reduce the required degrees; separability was not used.

For the specified cubic, put $a=\sqrt[3]2$ and $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$. Its [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb Q(a,\omega)$. [Eisenstein criterion](../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at two gives $[\mathbb Q(a):\mathbb Q]=3$. This cubic [field](../../../../../field.md) is real and therefore does not contain the nonreal $\omega$, whose quadratic [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is $x^2+x+1$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb Q(a,\omega):\mathbb Q]=3\cdot2=6.}
$$
