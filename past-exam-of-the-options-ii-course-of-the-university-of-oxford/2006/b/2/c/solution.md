↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Factor

$$
x^4-x^2-2=(x^2-2)(x^2+1).
$$

The roots are $\pm\sqrt2,\pm i$, so the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i)$. The real quadratic [field](../../../../../field.md) $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$ does not contain $i$, which adds a further degree two. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i):\mathbb Q]=4.}
$$
