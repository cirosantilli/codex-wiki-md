<h1 id="b/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) has positive degree, so its [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) is proper: otherwise $1=fh$ would contradict degrees. Every nonzero class $[g]$ has a representative not divisible by $f$. Irreducibility gives $\gcd(g,f)=1$, and the [Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md) supplies [polynomials](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $u,v$ with $ug+vf=1$. Passing to the [quotient](../../../../../quotient.md) gives $[u][g]=1$. Thus the [quotient](../../../../../quotient.md) is a nonzero [commutative ring](../../../../../commutative-ring.md) in which every nonzero element has an inverse, hence **a [field](../../../../../field.md)**. Constants embed injectively because no nonzero constant lies in the positive-degree [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) $(f)$.
