<h1 id="b/2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Polynomial division](../../../../../polynomial-division.md) gives a unique remainder of degree less than $n$ in every [coset](../../../../../coset.md). Thus the classes

$$
1,[x],\ldots,[x]^{n-1}
$$

span the [quotient](../../../../../quotient.md) as an $F$-vector space. A [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) of them that vanishes would be a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree less than $n$ divisible by $f$, so it is the zero [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md). They are therefore [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md), proving

$$
\boxed{[K:F]=n.}
$$
