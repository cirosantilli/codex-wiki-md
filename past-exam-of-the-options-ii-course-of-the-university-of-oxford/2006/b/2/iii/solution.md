<h1 id="b/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take $a=[x]$. Every [quotient](../../../../../quotient.md) class is a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) expression in $a$ with coefficients from $F$, so $K=F[a]$. Substitution gives $f(a)=[f(x)]=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{K=F[a],\qquad f(a)=0.}
$$

This constructs a [field](../../../../../field.md) containing a root even when no root existed in the original [field](../../../../../field.md).
