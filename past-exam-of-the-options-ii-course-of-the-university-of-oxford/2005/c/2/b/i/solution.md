[Fermat's little theorem](../../../../../../fermat-little-theorem.md) states that for a [prime](../../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$ and an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $a$ not divisible by $p$,

$$
\boxed{a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p.}
$$

Equivalently, $a^p\equiv a\pmod p$ for every [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $a$, with [divisibility](../../../../../../divisibility-split.md) by $p$ allowed in that latter formulation.
