<h1 id="h/h2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose the $z$-axis along the [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) and the origin at its midpoint, so $x^2+y^2\leq b^2$ and $-a\leq z\leq a$. [Reflection symmetry](../../../../../reflection-symmetry.md) puts the [centroid](../../../../../centroid.md) at the origin and makes all products of inertia zero. The [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is

$$
\boxed{m=2\pi\rho ab^2.}
$$

Cylindrical integration gives $\int_A\rho x^2\,dV=\int_A\rho y^2\,dV=mb^2/4$, while the uniform height coordinate has $\int_A\rho z^2\,dV=ma^2/3$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{J_G=m\operatorname{diag}\left(\frac{b^2}4+\frac{a^2}3,\ \frac{b^2}4+\frac{a^2}3,\ \frac{b^2}2\right).}
$$

The two equal transverse entries reflect the [rotational symmetry](../../../../../rotational-symmetry.md) about the [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) axis.
