<h1 id="h/h2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $E(t)$ be the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) whose columns are the moving [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) [vectors](../../../../../vector.md). Then $EE^T=I$. Differentiating gives

$$
\dot EE^T+E\dot E^T=0,
$$

so $W=\dot EE^T$ is [skew-symmetric](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md). Every real [skew-symmetric](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) $3\times3$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) has the unique form

$$
W=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\omega_3&\omega_2\\\omega_3&0&-\omega_1\\-\omega_2&\omega_1&0\end{pmatrix},
$$

which acts on a [vector](../../../../../vector.md) as $Wv=\boldsymbol\omega\times v$. Since $\dot E=WE$, each column satisfies

$$
\boxed{\dot{\mathbf e}_i=\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf e_i.}
$$

The [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $\boldsymbol\omega$ is the instantaneous [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md). It may also be written $\boldsymbol\omega=\tfrac12\sum_i\mathbf e_i\times\dot{\mathbf e}_i$.
