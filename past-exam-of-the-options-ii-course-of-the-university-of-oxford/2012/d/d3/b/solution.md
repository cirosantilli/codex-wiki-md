<h1 id="d/d3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On finite [intervals](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) the function is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md), but

$$
\int_0^R\frac{x}{1+x^2}\,dx=\frac12\log(1+R^2)\longrightarrow\infty.
$$

Consequently its nonnegative [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) on the half-line is infinite: **it is not [integrable](../../../../../integrability.md)**. The divergence occurs at infinity, not at the origin.
