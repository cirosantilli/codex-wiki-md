↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

We prove [absolute integrability](../../../../../integrability.md). On $(0,1]$, $|\sin(1/x)|\leq1$ and the denominator is at least one, so $|f(x)|\leq1$. On $[1,\infty)$, the standard inequality $|\sin t|\leq|t|$ gives

$$
|f(x)|\leq\frac1{x(1+(\log x)^2)}.
$$

[Substitution](../../../../../substitution-algebra.md) $t=\log x$ yields

$$
\int_1^\infty\frac{dx}{x(1+(\log x)^2)}=\int_0^\infty\frac{dt}{1+t^2}=\frac\pi2.
$$

Thus $\int_0^\infty|f(x)|\,dx\leq1+\pi/2<\infty$, and **the function is [integrable](../../../../../integrability.md)** despite its oscillation near zero.
