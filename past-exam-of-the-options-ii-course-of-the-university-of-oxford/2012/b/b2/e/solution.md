↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

[Conjugacy](../../../../../conjugate-group-elements.md) is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../equivalence-relation.md): the [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md) gives reflexivity, [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) by $g^{-1}$ reverses [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) by $g$, and composing conjugations gives transitivity. Its [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) therefore partition the finite set $G$. Counting this disjoint [union](../../../../../set-union.md) gives

$$
\boxed{|G|=\sum_{i=1}^k|C_i|.}
$$

Separating singleton classes from the others gives the [class equation](../../../../../class-equation.md) $|G|=|Z(G)|+\sum_{|C_i|>1}|C_i|$.
