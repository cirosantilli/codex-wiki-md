<h1 id="b/b2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Write $|G|=p^n$, where $n\geq1$. By part (c), each conjugacy-class size divides $p^n$ and hence is a power of $p$. Every nonsingleton class consequently has size divisible by $p$. The [class equation](../../../../../class-equation.md) implies

$$
|Z(G)|\equiv|G|\equiv0\pmod p.
$$

The [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md) lies in the [centre](../../../../../center-geometry.md), so the [centre](../../../../../center-geometry.md) is nonempty. Its size is a positive multiple of $p$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{|Z(G)|\geq p\geq2.}
$$

This proves that a nontrivial finite [p-group](../../../../../p-group.md) has a nontrivial [centre](../../../../../center-geometry.md).
