↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md) satisfies $e*x=exe^{-1}=x$. For $g,h\in G$,

$$
(gh)*x=ghxh^{-1}g^{-1}=g*(h*x).
$$

Both [action](../../../../../action.md) axioms hold, so **[conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) is a [group action](../../../../../group-action-split.md) on $G$**.
