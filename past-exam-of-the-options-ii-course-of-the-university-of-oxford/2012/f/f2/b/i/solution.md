<h1 id="f/f2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The function is [smooth](../../../../../../smooth-function.md), $f(0,0)=0$, and

$$
f_x=ye^x+e^y\cos x,\qquad f_y=e^x+e^y\sin x.
$$

At the origin both [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) equal one. In particular, $f_y(0,0)=1\neq0$, so the [implicit function theorem](../../../../../../implicit-function-theorem.md) supplies a [neighbourhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) in which

$$
\boxed{C=\{(x,h(x))\},\qquad h\in C^1,\quad h(0)=0.}
$$

In fact $h$ is [smooth](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) because $f$ is [smooth](../../../../../../smooth-function.md).
