A $C^1$ [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) between [open subsets](../../../../../../open-set.md) of $\mathbb R^n$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) $f:U\to V$ for which both $f$ and $f^{-1}$ are [continuously differentiable](../../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md). More generally, a $C^k$ [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) requires that regularity for both maps; a [smooth](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) has [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) of every order. In the $C^1$ setting used here,

$$
\boxed{f\text{ bijective},\qquad f,f^{-1}\in C^1.}
$$
