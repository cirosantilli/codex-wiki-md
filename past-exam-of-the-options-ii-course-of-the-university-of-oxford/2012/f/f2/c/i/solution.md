<h1 id="f/f2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
DG(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}
f_x(x,y)&f_y(x,y)\\
f_x(x,y^2)&2y f_y(x,y^2)
\end{pmatrix},\qquad DG(0,0)=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is $-1$, so it is invertible. The map $G$ is [smooth](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) and $G(0,0)=(0,0)$. The [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) therefore yields an [open neighbourhood](../../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) $U$ of the origin for which $G(U)$ is [open](../../../../../../open-set.md), contains the origin, and

$$
\boxed{G:U\to G(U)\text{ is a diffeomorphism}.}
$$
