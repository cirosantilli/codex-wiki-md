↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The inverse [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) formula, evaluated at $G(0,0)=(0,0)$, gives

$$
\boxed{D(G^{-1})(0,0)=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Multiplying the two displayed [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) in either order gives the [identity element](../../../../../../identity-element.md), verifying the [total derivative](../../../../../../total-derivative.md) explicitly.
