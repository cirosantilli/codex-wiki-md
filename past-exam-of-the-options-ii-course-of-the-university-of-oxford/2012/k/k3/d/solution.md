<h1 id="k/k3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the [analytic continuation](../../../../../analytic-continuation.md) inside the [circle](../../../../../circle.md), only the image location $c=a^2/b$ is singular; $b$ lies outside. Squaring the velocity gives

$$
(w')^2=\frac{A^2}{(z-b)^4}-\frac{2AB}{(z-b)^2(z-c)^2}+\frac{B^2}{(z-c)^4}.
$$

The last term has no [residue](../../../../../residue.md), and the middle term has [residue](../../../../../residue.md)

$$
\operatorname{res}((w')^2;c)=\left.\frac{d}{dz}\left(-\frac{2AB}{(z-b)^2}\right)\right|_{z=c}
=\frac{4AB}{(c-b)^3}
=-\frac{4m^2a^2b}{(b^2-a^2)^3},
$$

because $AB=m^2a^2/b^2$ and $c-b=-(b^2-a^2)/b$. The [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) and part (a) therefore yield

$$
\boxed{F_x-iF_y=-\pi\rho\operatorname{res}((w')^2;c)=\frac{4\pi\rho m^2a^2b}{(b^2-a^2)^3}.}
$$

Thus $F_y=0$ and $F_x>0$. **The [force](../../../../../force-split.md) points towards the [planar potential doublet](../../../../../two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md) and is [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of its orientation**, since the factors $e^{i\alpha}$ and $e^{-i\alpha}$ cancel in $AB$.
