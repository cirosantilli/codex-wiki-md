<h1 id="k/k3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](../../../../../milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) states that if $f$ is an analytic complex potential with no [singularity](../../../../../complex-singularity.md) on or inside $|z|=a$, then

$$
w(z)=f(z)+\overline{f(a^2/\bar z)}
$$

makes $|z|=a$ a [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md) while retaining the original exterior [singularities](../../../../../complex-singularity.md) and adding reflected [singularities](../../../../../complex-singularity.md) inside the [circle](../../../../../circle.md). On the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) the two terms are conjugates, so the resulting [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) is zero there.

Put $A=me^{i\alpha}$ and $c=a^2/b$. Applying the formula to the exterior [planar potential doublet](../../../../../two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md) gives

$$
\boxed{w(z)=\frac{me^{i\alpha}}{z-b}+\frac{me^{-i\alpha}z}{a^2-bz}.}
$$

Equivalently, up to an irrelevant constant,

$$
w(z)=\frac A{z-b}-\frac B{z-c},\qquad B=\frac{ma^2}{b^2}e^{-i\alpha}.
$$

The image [planar potential doublet](../../../../../two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md) is inside the obstacle at $c<a$; the only physical [fluid](../../../../../fluid.md) [singularity](../../../../../complex-singularity.md) is the prescribed one at $b$.
