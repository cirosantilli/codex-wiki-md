<h1 id="k/k3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Orient the body [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) counterclockwise and write its [tangent](../../../../../tangent.md) displacement as $(dx,dy)$. The outward normal from the body has displacement-weighted components $(dy,-dx)$. [Pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) therefore exerts the [force](../../../../../force-split.md) components $F_x=-\oint p\,dy$ and $F_y=\oint p\,dx$, or

$$
F_x-iF_y=-i\oint_{\partial B}p\,d\bar z.
$$

For steady [irrotational](../../../../../irrotational-vector-field.md) incompressible flow at constant [density](../../../../../density.md), Bernoulli gives $p=p_0-\rho|w'(z)|^2/2$, with any constant far-field kinetic term absorbed into $p_0$. Its constant contribution integrates to zero. The [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) is a [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md), so $\operatorname{Im}w$ is constant there and $w'(z)\,dz$ is real. Thus $w'\,dz=\overline{w'}\,d\bar z$, giving $|w'|^2d\bar z=(w')^2dz$. This [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md) remains valid at a [stagnation point](../../../../../stagnation-point.md) as both sides vanish. [Substitution](../../../../../substitution-algebra.md) yields [Blasius force theorem](../../../../../blasius-force-theorem.md):

$$
\boxed{F_x-iF_y=\frac{i\rho}2\oint_{\partial B}(w'(z))^2\,dz.}
$$
