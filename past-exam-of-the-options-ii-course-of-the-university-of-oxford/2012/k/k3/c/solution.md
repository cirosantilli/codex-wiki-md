<h1 id="k/k3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [complex velocity](../../../../../complex-velocity.md) obtained from part (b) is

$$
w'(z)=-\frac A{(z-b)^2}+\frac B{(z-c)^2}.
$$

For a nonzero [planar potential doublet](../../../../../two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md), $B/A=(a^2/b^2)e^{-2i\alpha}$. Its zeros therefore satisfy

$$
\frac{z-c}{z-b}=t_\pm,\qquad t_\pm=\pm\frac ab e^{-i\alpha},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{z_\pm=\frac{c-bt_\pm}{1-t_\pm}=a\frac{a/b\mp e^{-i\alpha}}{1\mp(a/b)e^{-i\alpha}}.}
$$

The denominators are nonzero because $a/b<1$. For either sign,

$$
|a/b\mp e^{-i\alpha}|^2=|1\mp(a/b)e^{-i\alpha}|^2,
$$

so $|z_\pm|=a$. They are distinct because $t_+\neq t_-$ and $t\mapsto(c-bt)/(1-t)$ is [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md), its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) being $c-b\neq0$. Clearing the velocity denominators gives a quadratic with these two [roots](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md), so there are no further finite [stagnation points](../../../../../stagnation-point.md). Thus **both [stagnation points](../../../../../stagnation-point.md) lie on the obstacle [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md)**.
