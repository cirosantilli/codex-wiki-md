<h1 id="m/m5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Successive interarrival times $W_1=T_1$ and $W_2=T_2-T_1$ are [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) exponential variables of rate $\lambda$. Their [joint density](../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) is $\lambda^2e^{-\lambda(w_1+w_2)}$ for $w_1,w_2>0$. The transformation $t_1=w_1$, $t_2=w_1+w_2$ has [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) one, giving

$$
\boxed{f_{T_1,T_2}(t_1,t_2)=\lambda^2e^{-\lambda t_2}\,\mathbf1_{\{0<t_1<t_2\}}.}
$$

Integrating over $0<t_1<t_2<\infty$ gives one, as a [joint density](../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) should.
