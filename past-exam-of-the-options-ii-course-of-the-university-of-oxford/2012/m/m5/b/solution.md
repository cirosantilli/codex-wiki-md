<h1 id="m/m5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Stationary increments](../../../../../stationary-increments.md) give $N_5-N_3\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(2\lambda)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(N_5-N_3=4)=e^{-2\lambda}\frac{(2\lambda)^4}{4!}.}
$$

There is zero [probability](../../../../../probability.md) of an arrival at either deterministic endpoint, so endpoint inclusion does not alter the result.
