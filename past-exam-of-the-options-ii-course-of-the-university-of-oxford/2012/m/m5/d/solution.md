<h1 id="m/m5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write $N_3=N_2+(N_3-N_2)$. [Independent increments](../../../../../independent-increments.md) make $N_2$ [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the second term. Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cov}(N_2,N_3)=\operatorname{Var}(N_2)=2\lambda.}
$$

The overlapping time [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) contributes the [covariance](../../../../../covariance.md); the extra unit of time contributes none.
