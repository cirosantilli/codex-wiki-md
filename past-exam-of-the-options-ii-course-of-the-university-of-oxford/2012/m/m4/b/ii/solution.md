↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Convergence in distribution](../../../../../../convergence-in-distribution.md) to $Y$ means convergence of [cumulative distribution functions](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) at every [continuity point](../../../../../../continuity-point.md) of the [limiting distribution](../../../../../../limiting-distribution.md) function:

$$
\boxed{F_{Y_n}(x)\longrightarrow F_Y(x)\quad\text{whenever }F_Y\text{ is continuous at }x.}
$$

Convergence at a jump of $F_Y$ is not required. Only the laws are involved, so this definition does not require a particular [coupling](../../../../../../coupling.md) of the variables.
