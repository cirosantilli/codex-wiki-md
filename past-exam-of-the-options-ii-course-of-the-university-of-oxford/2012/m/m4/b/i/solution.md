[Convergence in probability](../../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md) to $Y$ means that for every $\varepsilon>0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(|Y_n-Y|>\varepsilon)\longrightarrow0.}
$$

Here the variables must be defined on a common [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) space so that their differences are meaningful.
