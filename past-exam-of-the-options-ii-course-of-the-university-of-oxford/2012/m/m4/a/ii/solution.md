<h1 id="m/m4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Replacement and mixing make the one hundred draws [independent and identically distributed](../../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md). Writing $S=\sum_{j=1}^{100}X_j$, [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) and additivity of variances of [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) variables give

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E S=100\cdot4=400,\qquad\operatorname{Var}(S)=100\cdot\frac{28}5=560.}
$$
