<h1 id="m/m4/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [central limit theorem](../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) suggests approximating $S$ by a normal variable with mean $400$ and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) $560$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(S\geq500)\approx1-\Phi\left(\frac{500-400}{\sqrt{560}}\right).}
$$

Since $S$ is integer-valued, the usual [continuity correction](../../../../../../continuity-correction.md) replaces $500$ by $499.5$:

$$
\mathbb P(S\geq500)\approx1-\Phi\left(\frac{99.5}{\sqrt{560}}\right).
$$

These are approximation formulas rather than consequences of Chebyshev's bound; the [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) is $\sqrt{560}$, not $560$.
