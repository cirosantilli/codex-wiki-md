↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md) of $A$ is the [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) of every [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) subset containing it:

$$
\boxed{\overline A=\bigcap\{F\subseteq X:F\text{ closed and }A\subseteq F\}.}
$$

The collection is nonempty because it contains $X$. Arbitrary [intersections](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) of [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) sets are [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md), so this is the smallest [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) set containing $A$.
