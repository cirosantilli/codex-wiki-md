<h1 id="e/e3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $x\notin\overline A$, some [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) superset $F$ of $A$ misses $x$. Its [complement](../../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) is an [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) containing $x$ and disjoint from $A$. Conversely, if an [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $U$ contains $x$ and misses $A$, the [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) set $X\setminus U$ contains $A$ and misses $x$, so $x\notin\overline A$.

Negating this equivalence gives the [neighbourhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) characterization

$$
\boxed{x\in\overline A\iff U\cap A\neq\varnothing\text{ for every open }U\ni x.}
$$
