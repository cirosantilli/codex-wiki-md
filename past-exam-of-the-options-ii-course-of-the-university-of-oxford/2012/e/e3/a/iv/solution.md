↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

An [accumulation point](../../../../../../limit-point.md) of $A$ is a point $x\in X$ such that every [open neighbourhood](../../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) of $x$ contains a point of $A$ different from $x$:

$$
\boxed{x\in A'\iff (U\setminus\{x\})\cap A\neq\varnothing\text{ for every open }U\ni x.}
$$

The point itself need not belong to $A$. Requiring a different point distinguishes accumulation from mere membership in the [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md).
