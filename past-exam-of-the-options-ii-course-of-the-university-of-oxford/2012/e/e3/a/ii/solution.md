<h1 id="e/e3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Every subset is contained in its [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md), giving $\overline A\subseteq\overline{\overline A}$. On the other hand, $\overline A$ is already [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) by its definition as an [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) of [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) sets. It is therefore one of the [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) supersets used when taking its own [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md), so $\overline{\overline A}\subseteq\overline A$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\overline{\overline A}=\overline A.}
$$

This is the [idempotence](../../../../../../idempotence.md) of the [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md) operation.
