<h1 id="e/e3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In a [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md),

$$
\boxed{x\in A'\iff\text{there exists a sequence }a_n\in A\setminus\{x\}\text{ with }a_n\to x.}
$$

The [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) may equivalently be required to have pairwise distinct terms. The metric [hypothesis](../../../../../../hypothesis.md) makes a countable [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) sufficient; arbitrary [topological spaces](../../../../../../topological-space.md) need not have this sequential characterization.
