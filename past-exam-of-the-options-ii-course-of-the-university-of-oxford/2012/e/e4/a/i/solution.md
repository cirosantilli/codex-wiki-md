The [quotient topology](../../../../../../quotient-topology.md) is defined by

$$
\boxed{U\subseteq X/\mathcal R\text{ open}\iff p^{-1}(U)\text{ open in }X.}
$$

Preimages commute with arbitrary [unions](../../../../../../set-union.md) and finite [intersections](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) and take the empty and whole sets to the corresponding sets, so this definition indeed gives a [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md).
