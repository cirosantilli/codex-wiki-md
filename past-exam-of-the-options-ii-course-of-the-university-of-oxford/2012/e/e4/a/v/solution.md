<h1 id="e/e4/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Define $\widetilde f:X/\mathcal F\to Y$ by $\widetilde f([x])=f(x)$. Its definition is [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the representative because the classes are precisely the fibres of $f$. It is onto since $f$ is onto, and [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) because two points with equal $f$-values lie in the same fibre.

The relation $\widetilde f\circ p=f$ and part (iii) imply [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of $\widetilde f$. Since $X$ is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) and $p$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md), $X/\mathcal F$ is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md). A [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) from a [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) space to a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{X/\mathcal F\cong Y.}
$$
