<h1 id="e/e4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every iterate $g^n(x,y)=(x+n,(-1)^n y)$ is an [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md) of the strip with its Euclidean subspace metric. Let $a,b$ lie in distinct [orbits](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). Since the first-coordinate difference in $a-g^n b$ grows without bound as $|n|\to\infty$, the positive distances $d(a,g^n b)$ have a positive minimum $\delta$. Only finitely many indices can give a [distance](../../../../../../distance.md) below any fixed bound, and none gives zero.

Choose $0<\varepsilon<\delta/3$. Saturate the relative [open balls](../../../../../../open-ball.md) by setting

$$
U=\bigcup_{n\in\mathbb Z}g^nB_S(a,\varepsilon),\qquad
V=\bigcup_{n\in\mathbb Z}g^nB_S(b,\varepsilon).
$$

These are [open](../../../../../../open-set.md) saturated [neighbourhoods](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of the two [orbits](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). Any two centres $g^n a,g^m b$ are at least $\delta$ apart, since their [distance](../../../../../../distance.md) equals $d(a,g^{m-n}b)$. Their radius-$\varepsilon$ balls therefore cannot intersect. Hence $U\cap V=\varnothing$, and part (a)(iv), an instance of [positive orbit separation implies a Hausdorff quotient](../../../../../../positive-orbit-separation-implies-a-hausdorff-quotient.md), proves

$$
\boxed{S/\mathcal R'\text{ is Hausdorff}.}
$$
