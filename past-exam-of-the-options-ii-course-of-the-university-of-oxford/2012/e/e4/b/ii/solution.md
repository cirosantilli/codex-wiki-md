<h1 id="e/e4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $D=[0,1]\times[-1,1]$ and $q=p\circ i:D\to S/\mathcal R'$. This is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). It is onto: given $(x,y)\in S$, choose an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) shift bringing $x$ into $[0,1]$, with the corresponding possible sign change in $y$; the resulting representative lies in $D$.

Two representatives in $D$ are in the same [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) only if their first coordinates differ by an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md). An interior point can therefore be equivalent only to itself. Distinct [boundary](../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) representatives have first coordinates zero and one, and the iterate $g$ identifies exactly $(0,y)$ with $(1,-y)$. Thus the fibres of $q$ are precisely the [equivalence classes](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) defining the [Möbius band](../../../../../../mobius-band.md) $M$.

The [rectangle](../../../../../../rectangle.md) $D$ is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md), and the target is [Hausdorff](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) by part (i). Applying part (a)(v) to $q$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{M=D/\mathcal R\cong S/\mathcal R'.}
$$

The [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) argument supplies [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the inverse without requiring a discontinuous choice of one representative per [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md).
