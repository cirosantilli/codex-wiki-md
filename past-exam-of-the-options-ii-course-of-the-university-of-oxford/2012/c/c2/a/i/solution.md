<h1 id="c/c2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $a$ and an [odd prime](../../../../../../odd-prime.md) $p$, the [Legendre symbol](../../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac ap\right)=\begin{cases}0,&p\mid a,\\1,&a\not\equiv0\pmod p\text{ and }a\text{ is a square modulo }p,\\-1,&a\text{ is not a square modulo }p.\end{cases}}
$$

The value one refers to a nonzero [quadratic residue](../../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md); divisibility by $p$ is recorded separately by zero.
