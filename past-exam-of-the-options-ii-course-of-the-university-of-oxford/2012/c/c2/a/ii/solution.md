<h1 id="c/c2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Euler's criterion](../../../../../../euler-s-criterion.md) states that

$$
\boxed{a^{(p-1)/2}\equiv\left(\frac ap\right)\pmod p.}
$$

For $p\nmid a$ this says that the power is $1$ for a [quadratic residue](../../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md) and $-1$ for a [quadratic nonresidue](../../../../../../quadratic-nonresidue.md). When $p\mid a$, both sides are zero modulo $p$.
