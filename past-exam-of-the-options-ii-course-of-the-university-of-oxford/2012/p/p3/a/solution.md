<h1 id="p/p3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A real square [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $Q$ is [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) when $Q^TQ=I$, equivalently $Q^{-1}=Q^T$. For $c=\cos\theta$ and $s=\sin\theta$,

$$
M(\theta)^TM(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}c&-s\\s&c\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}c&s\\-s&c\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}c^2+s^2&0\\0&c^2+s^2\end{pmatrix}=I.
$$

Thus **$M(\theta)$ is [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) for every allowed angle**, and indeed for every real angle.
