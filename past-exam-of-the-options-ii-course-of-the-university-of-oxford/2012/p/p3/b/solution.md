<h1 id="p/p3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md),

$$
\|Qx\|^2=(Qx)^TQx=x^TQ^TQx=x^Tx=\|x\|^2.
$$

Both [norms](../../../../../norm.md) are nonnegative, so taking square [roots](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) proves

$$
\boxed{\|Qx\|=\|x\|.}
$$

Thus an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) is a [Euclidean isometry](../../../../../euclidean-isometry.md) fixing the origin.
