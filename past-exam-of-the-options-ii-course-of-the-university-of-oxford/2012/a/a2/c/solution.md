<h1 id="a/a2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Induct through the finitely many construction steps. Initially all available coordinates lie in $\mathbb Q$. If coordinates obtained so far lie in a real [field](../../../../../field.md) $F$, the [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) through two of its points has [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md) in $F$. A [circle](../../../../../circle.md) with [centre](../../../../../center-geometry.md) in $F^2$ and [radius](../../../../../radius.md) a [distance](../../../../../distance.md) between two such points has equation

$$
(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2,
$$

whose [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md) lie in $F$, since the squared [distance](../../../../../distance.md) $r^2$ lies in $F$; the [radius](../../../../../radius.md) itself need not lie there.

Part (b) therefore puts every new [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) in $F$ or a real [quadratic extension](../../../../../quadratic-extension.md) of $F$. If a step produces several points, adjoin their coordinates successively, omitting extensions that add nothing. A finite construction ends in a real [field](../../../../../field.md) $K$ containing the desired coordinates, with a tower of genuine quadratic extensions

$$
\mathbb Q=F_0\subset F_1\subset\cdots\subset F_n=K.
$$

The [tower law for field extensions](../../../../../tower-law-for-field-extensions.md) yields

$$
\boxed{[K:\mathbb Q]=\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}[F_{i+1}:F_i]=2^n.}
$$

A construction involving no nontrivial extension has $n=0$ and [degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) one.
