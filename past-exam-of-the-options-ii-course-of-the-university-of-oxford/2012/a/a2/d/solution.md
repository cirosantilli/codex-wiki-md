<h1 id="a/a2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [constructible regular polygon](../../../../../constructible-regular-polygon.md) can be translated, rotated, and rescaled by constructible operations to one inscribed in the unit [circle](../../../../../circle.md) with a vertex at $(1,0)$. Thus the real and imaginary parts of $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/p}$ are constructible. Part (c) puts both in a real [field](../../../../../field.md) $K$ of [degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $2^n$ over $\mathbb Q$. Consequently $\zeta\in K(i)$, and $[K(i):\mathbb Q]=2^{n+1}$ because $K\subset\mathbb R$.

For [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$, the supplied irreducibility of $1+x+\cdots+x^{p-1}$ shows that $[\mathbb Q(\zeta):\mathbb Q]=p-1$. The intermediate-field tower law implies that $p-1$ divides $2^{n+1}$. Every positive divisor of a [power of two](../../../../../power-of-two.md) is a [power of two](../../../../../power-of-two.md), so

$$
\boxed{p=2^r+1\quad\text{for an integer }r\geq0.}
$$

This necessary condition includes $p=2$ with $r=0$. It does not assert sufficiency for arbitrary [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md) of this form.
