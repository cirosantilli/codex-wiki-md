<h1 id="a/a2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Consider an isolated [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) of the two curves. For two distinct [lines](../../../../../straight-line.md), solving their [linear equations](../../../../../linear-equation.md) by elimination gives coordinates in $F$ whenever there is an [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md).

For a [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) and a [circle](../../../../../circle.md), solve the [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) for one coordinate with a nonzero [coefficient](../../../../../coefficient.md) and substitute into the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) equation. The resulting quadratic has [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md) in $F$. A real [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) lies in $F$ if its [discriminant](../../../../../discriminant.md) is a square in $F$, and otherwise in $F(\sqrt D)$ for its positive nonsquare [discriminant](../../../../../discriminant.md) $D\in F$. The other coordinate is an $F$-linear expression in that [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md). Thus both coordinates lie in the same real [quadratic extension](../../../../../quadratic-extension.md). A [tangent](../../../../../tangent.md) [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) has [discriminant](../../../../../discriminant.md) zero and already lies in $F$.

Write two [circle](../../../../../circle.md) equations in normalized form $x^2+y^2+u_ix+v_iy+w_i=0$. Subtracting eliminates the quadratic terms. If the [circles](../../../../../circle.md) are distinct and meet, this gives a nontrivial [line](../../../../../straight-line.md), reducing the problem to the preceding case. Concentric distinct [circles](../../../../../circle.md) do not intersect. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{each isolated intersection has coordinates in }F\text{ or in }F(\sqrt D),\quad [F(\sqrt D):F]=2.}
$$

The isolated-intersection qualification is implicit in the construction operation. If coincident curves were allowed and every point on their common curve counted as an [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) point, the printed assertion would be false: a rational [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) contains points with transcendental coordinates.
