<h1 id="j/j2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) solution of [degree](../../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $n$ has $a_n\neq0$ but $a_{n+2}=0$. The [recurrence](../../../../../../recurrent-markov-chain.md) therefore forces $2n+1-\epsilon=0$. Since $\epsilon=2E/(\hbar\omega)$,

$$
\boxed{E_n=\left(n+\frac12\right)\hbar\omega,\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$

Conversely, for this value of $\epsilon$, choose only the [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) containing [degree](../../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $n$ and set the other initial [coefficient](../../../../../../coefficient.md) to zero. The [recurrence](../../../../../../recurrent-markov-chain.md) terminates at $n$, producing a nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) times the decaying [Gaussian](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md). Thus every displayed [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) occurs under the supplied normalizability criterion.
