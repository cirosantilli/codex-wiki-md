<h1 id="j/j2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiate the [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) twice and align the [coefficient](../../../../../../coefficient.md) of $\xi^k$. The equation becomes

$$
(k+2)(k+1)a_{k+2}+(\epsilon-1-2k)a_k=0\qquad(k\geq0).
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{a_{k+2}=\frac{2k+1-\epsilon}{(k+2)(k+1)}a_k.}
$$

Even [coefficients](../../../../../../coefficient.md) are generated from $a_0$, and odd [coefficients](../../../../../../coefficient.md) from $a_1$; the two [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) [sequences](../../../../../../sequence.md) are [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md).
