<h1 id="o/o5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) and normality give $\overline X\sim N(\mu,1/n)$. Consequently $Z=\sqrt n(\overline X-\mu)$ is standard normal, and

$$
\mathbb P\left(\overline X<\mu+\frac{z_\alpha}{\sqrt n}\right)=\Phi(z_\alpha)=1-\alpha.
$$

Rearranging the event gives $\mu>\overline X-z_\alpha/\sqrt n$. Thus the random [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $(\overline X-z_\alpha/\sqrt n,\infty)$ covers the fixed parameter with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $1-\alpha$. After observing the sample, its realized [confidence interval](../../../../../confidence-interval.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left(\overline x-\frac{z_\alpha}{\sqrt n},\infty\right).}
$$

The coverage refers to repeated samples, not to a [posterior probability](../../../../../posterior-probability.md) for the fixed parameter.
