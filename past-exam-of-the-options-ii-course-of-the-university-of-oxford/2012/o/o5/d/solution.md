<h1 id="o/o5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For every particular alternative mean $\mu_1>\mu_0$, the [likelihood ratio](../../../../../likelihood-ratio.md) is increasing in the same statistic $\overline x$. Its size-$\alpha$ threshold is fixed solely by the null distribution, so it is always the same $c_\alpha$ found in part (c), [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of $\mu_1$.

The single [rejection region](../../../../../rejection-region.md) is therefore most powerful against every member of the composite alternative. Hence **the test is uniformly most powerful for $H_1:\mu>\mu_0$**.
