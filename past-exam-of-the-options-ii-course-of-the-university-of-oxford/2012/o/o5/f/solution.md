↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The [standard normal distribution](../../../../../standard-normal-distribution.md) function is strictly increasing. Thus $p>\alpha$ implies

$$
1-\Phi(\sqrt n(\overline x-\mu_0))>\alpha
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sqrt n(\overline x-\mu_0)<z_\alpha.
$$

Rearranging gives

$$
\boxed{\mu_0>\overline x-\frac{z_\alpha}{\sqrt n},\quad\text{so }\mu_0\in\left(\overline x-\frac{z_\alpha}{\sqrt n},\infty\right).}
$$

This is the confidence-interval/test duality for the matching one-sided procedures. The strict inequalities agree with the printed [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md).
