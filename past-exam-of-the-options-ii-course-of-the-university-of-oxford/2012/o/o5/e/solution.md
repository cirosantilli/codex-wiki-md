↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the observed mean, the one-sided $p$-value is the null [probability](../../../../../probability.md) of a [sample mean](../../../../../sample-mean.md) at least as large:

$$
p=\mathbb P_{\mu_0}(\overline X\geq\overline x).
$$

Standardization under the null gives

$$
\boxed{p=1-\Phi\bigl(\sqrt n(\overline x-\mu_0)\bigr).}
$$
