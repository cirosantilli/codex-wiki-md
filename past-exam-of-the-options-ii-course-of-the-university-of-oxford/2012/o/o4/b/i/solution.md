<h1 id="o/o4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For nonnegative [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $j,k$, repeated [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives the [beta integral](../../../../../../beta-integral.md) $\int_0^1y^j(1-y)^k\,dy=j!k!/(j+k+1)!$. Multiplying the stated [density](../../../../../../density.md) by $y$ and using this formula yields

$$
\mathbb E Y_{(r)}=\frac{n!}{(r-1)!(n-r)!}\frac{r!(n-r)!}{(n+1)!}.
$$

Cancellation gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E Y_{(r)}=\frac r{n+1}.}
$$
