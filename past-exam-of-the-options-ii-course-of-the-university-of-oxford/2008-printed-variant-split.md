# 2008 printed variant

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the options II course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This printed variant of the 2008 Options II paper was released in [FOI 20210708 1 Data](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_papers_2/response/1850333/attach/3/FOI%2020210708%201%20Data.zip?cookie_passthrough=1). It differs from the native paper in E4(c), where the codomain is written as $Y$ rather than $X$. The unchanged solution text is reused from the corresponding native-paper draft; E4(c) supplies a counterexample to the unrestricted variant and a valid corrected theorem. The same advertised-answer exclusion for H2 is retained. The complete statement transcription is held separately.
