↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The change-of-variables formula is

$$
\boxed{\int_{T(U)}f(y)\,dy=\int_U f(T(x))|\det DT_x|\,dx}.
$$

For an integrable $f$, the transformed function with its absolute-Jacobian factor is integrable as well. The absolute value is essential because the integral does not depend on whether the coordinate change preserves [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
