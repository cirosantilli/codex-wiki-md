↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**True.** Every singleton in a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) is closed. Thus $f^{-1}(\{y\})$ is closed in $X$ by [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md). A closed subset of a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) space is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md), whether or not that [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) space is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md). Hence every fibre is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md).
