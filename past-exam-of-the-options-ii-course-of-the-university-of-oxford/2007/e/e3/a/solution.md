**True.** Pull an open cover of $Y$ back through $f$. Surjectivity makes those inverse images cover $X$. A finite subcover of the [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) space $X$ maps back to a finite subcover of $Y$. Thus a continuous image of a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) space is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md).
