**False.** Let $X$ be an indiscrete two-point space and $Y$ a one-point space. The constant map is continuous and onto; $X$ is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) because it is finite and [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) because its only nonempty [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) is the whole space. It is not [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md), since its two points have no disjoint nonempty neighbourhoods. The one-point codomain is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md).
