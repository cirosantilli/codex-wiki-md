↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**False.** Take $X=[-1,1]$, $Y=[0,1]$ and $f(x)=x^2$, with their usual topologies. The domain is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) and [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md), the codomain is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md), and the map is continuous and onto. But the fibre over one is $\{-1,1\}$, a disconnected two-point subspace.
