<h1 id="e/e4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the surjection $p$ to the set of equivalence classes, define a subset $V$ of the quotient to be open exactly when $p^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$. Inverse images preserve the empty and full sets, arbitrary unions and finite intersections. Applying the topology axioms in $X$ proves this is a topology on the quotient, and it makes $p$ continuous.

The map need not be open. On $X=\mathbb R$, identify zero with one and leave every other point in its own class. The set $U=(-1/4,1/4)$ is open, but its [saturation](../../../../../homogeneity-plus-realization-of-empty-set-types-implies-saturation.md) is

$$
p^{-1}(p(U))=U\cup\{1\},
$$

which is not open in $\mathbb R$. Thus $p(U)$ is not open in the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md). [Continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of a [quotient map](../../../../../quotient-map.md) does not imply openness.
