<h1 id="p/p4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Gershgorin's second theorem states that a union of $k$ row discs separated from the union of all remaining discs contains exactly $k$ eigenvalues, counted with [algebraic multiplicity](../../../../../algebraic-multiplicity.md). In particular a disc disjoint from every other disc contains exactly one [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). The same assertion holds for column discs by applying it to the transpose.
