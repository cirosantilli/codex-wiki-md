↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A primitive seventh root generates all seventh roots, so the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)$. Its [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is the given irreducible [cyclotomic polynomial](../../../../../cyclotomic-polynomial.md) $1+x+\cdots+x^6$. Therefore $\boxed{[K:\mathbb Q]=6}$.
