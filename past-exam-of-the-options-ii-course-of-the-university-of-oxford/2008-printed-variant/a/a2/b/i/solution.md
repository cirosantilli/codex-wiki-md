<h1 id="a/a2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each flag choose an adapted [basis](../../../../../../basis.md): its first $n_i$ vectors span $V_i$, obtained by extending a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) successively through the nested subspaces and finally through $V$. For two flags, the invertible [linear transformation](../../../../../../linear-map.md) taking one adapted [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) to the other takes each member of the first flag to the corresponding member of the second. Hence **the action is transitive**.
