↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Yes.** Suppose $g$ maps the first flag to the second and has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $\delta\ne0$. Relative to an adapted [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of the first flag, scaling its first [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) vector by $\delta^{-1}$ and fixing all others defines a transformation $h$ preserving that flag, with [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $\delta^{-1}$. Then $gh$ still maps the first flag to the second and has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) one. Thus the special linear [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) acts transitively too.
