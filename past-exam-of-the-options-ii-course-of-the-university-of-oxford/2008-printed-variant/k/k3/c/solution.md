<h1 id="k/k3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Reflection in the line $y=-x$ is $Rz=\overline{iz}=-i\bar z$. The printed expression has two conjugation bars: $w(z)+\overline{w(\overline{iz})}$. On the line $Rz=z$, this is $w(z)+\overline{w(z)}$, which is real; hence its [stream function](../../../../../stream-function.md) vanishes there. Applying this reflection to the dipole gives

$$
\overline{\frac{m}{-i\bar z-a}}=\frac{\bar m}{iz-\bar a}=-\frac{i\bar m}{z+i\bar a}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{W(z)=\frac{m}{z-a}-\frac{i\bar m}{z+i\bar a}}.
$$

The image lies at $-i\bar a$, with moment $-i\bar m$, on the opposite side of the wall.
