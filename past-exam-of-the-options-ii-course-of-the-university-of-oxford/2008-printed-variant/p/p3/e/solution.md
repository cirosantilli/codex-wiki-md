<h1 id="p/p3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The residual is [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $q\in\Pi_k$, so Pythagoras gives

$$
\|f\|^2=\|q\|^2+\|f-q\|^2\ge\|q\|^2.
$$

Taking nonnegative square roots proves $\boxed{\|q\|\le\|f\|}$.
