<h1 id="d/d3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The partial sums $S_N=\sum_{k=0}^Na_k$ are measurable, nonnegative and increasing. By the [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md),

$$
\boxed{\int\sum_{k=0}^\infty a_k=\sum_{k=0}^\infty\int a_k<\infty}.
$$

A nonnegative measurable [function](../../../../../function-split.md) with finite integral is finite almost everywhere: if it were infinite on a positive-measure set, every constant multiple of that set's indicator would be a lower bound, forcing an infinite integral. Thus the series **converges almost everywhere**.
