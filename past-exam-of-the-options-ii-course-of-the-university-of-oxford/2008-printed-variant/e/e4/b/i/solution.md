<h1 id="e/e4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every $K_n$ is closed in the [Hausdorff](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) ambient space, because it is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md). Inside the [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) space $K_1$, these sets have the [finite intersection property](../../../../../../finite-intersection-property.md): a finite [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) equals the member with largest index and is nonempty. If the total [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) were empty, their open complements would cover $K_1$; a finite [subcover](../../../../../../subcover.md) would contradict that property. Thus

$$
\boxed{\bigcap_nK_n\ne\varnothing}.
$$
