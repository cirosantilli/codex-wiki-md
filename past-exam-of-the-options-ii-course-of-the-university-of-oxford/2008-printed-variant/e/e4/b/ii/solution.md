<h1 id="e/e4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If no $K_n$ were contained in $U$, the sets $L_n=K_n\setminus U$ would be nonempty, nested and [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md), since each is closed in $K_n$. Part (i) would supply a point in $\bigcap_nL_n$, belonging to every $K_n$ but not to $U$, a contradiction. Thus some $K_{n_0}\subseteq U$, and nesting gives **the same containment for every later index**.
