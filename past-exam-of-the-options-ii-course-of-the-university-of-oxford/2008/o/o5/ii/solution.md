<h1 id="o/o5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each normal sample, its [sample mean](../../../../../sample-mean.md) is independent of its [sample variance](../../../../../sample-variance.md), and the scaled residual sum of squares is chi-squared. [Independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the samples also makes their two residual sums independent. Adding their chi-squared distributions gives

$$
\frac{(m-1)S_X^2+(n-1)S_Y^2}{\sigma^2}\sim\chi^2_{m+n-2}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{d=(m+n-2)/\sigma^2,\qquad dS^2\sim\chi^2_{m+n-2}}.
$$

The pooled [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is also independent of the difference of sample means.
