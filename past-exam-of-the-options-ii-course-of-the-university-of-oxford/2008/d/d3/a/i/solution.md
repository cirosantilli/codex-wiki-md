The [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) says that if measurable functions satisfy $0\leq f_1\leq f_2\leq\cdots$ and converge pointwise to $f$, then $f$ is measurable and

$$
\boxed{\int f=\lim_n\int f_n},
$$

allowing the common value to be $+\infty$.
