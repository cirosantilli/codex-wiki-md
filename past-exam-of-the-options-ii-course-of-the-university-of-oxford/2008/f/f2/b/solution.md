<h1 id="f/f2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The defining [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $h(x)=x_1^2/a^2+x_2^2/b^2+x_3^2/c^2-1$ is smooth and has [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) $2(x_1/a^2,x_2/b^2,x_3/c^2)$. This cannot vanish on its zero level, since the origin does not lie there. The regular level-set theorem therefore makes $M$ a two-dimensional submanifold, with [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md) $\ker Dh(x)$ and normal space

$$
\boxed{(T_xM)^\perp=\operatorname{span}\{(x_1/a^2,x_2/b^2,x_3/c^2)\}}.
$$
