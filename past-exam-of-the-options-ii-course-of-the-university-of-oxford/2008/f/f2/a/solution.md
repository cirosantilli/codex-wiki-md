<h1 id="f/f2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A two-dimensional embedded submanifold is locally flattened by a continuously differentiable coordinate diffeomorphism of an ambient [open neighbourhood](../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) onto a coordinate plane, or equivalently locally admits a regular two-parameter chart. A tangent vector at $x$ is the velocity $\gamma'(0)$ of a differentiable curve in $M$ through $x$. All such vectors form the two-dimensional [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md) $T_xM$, the image of the differential of a regular parametrization. Normal vectors form its [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) complement $(T_xM)^\perp$ in $\mathbb R^3$, which has **dimension one**.
