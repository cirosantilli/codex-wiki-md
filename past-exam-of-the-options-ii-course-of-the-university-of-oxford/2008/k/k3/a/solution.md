<h1 id="k/k3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](../../../../../milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) says that, when $f$ is a [complex potential](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) analytic inside and on $|z|=R$ and all its flow singularities are outside, the potential outside an impermeable circle is

$$
W(z)=f(z)+\overline{f(R^2/\bar z)}.
$$

The reflected expression is analytic in the exterior: conjugating a local power series turns its argument into $R^2/z$. It creates only interior image singularities. On $|z|=R$ we have $R^2/\bar z=z$, so $W=f+\bar f$ is real. The [stream function](../../../../../stream-function.md) is therefore constant on the circle, which is the [impermeability condition](../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md). At infinity the reflected term is $\overline{f(0)}+O(z^{-1})$, so it changes the velocity by $O(z^{-2})$ and leaves the imposed far flow unchanged. These observations prove the theorem, up to the irrelevant additive potential constant and with no extra [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) introduced.

With $a=z_1$, $|a|>1$, and unit radius, it gives

$$
\boxed{W(z)=\frac{m}{z-a}+\frac{\bar m z}{1-\bar a z}}.
$$

The image dipole is at $1/\bar a$ with moment $-\bar m/\bar a^2$; writing the image term this way changes only an additive constant.
