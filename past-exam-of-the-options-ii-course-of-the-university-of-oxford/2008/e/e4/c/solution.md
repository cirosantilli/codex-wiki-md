<h1 id="e/e4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The inclusion $f(\bigcap_nK_n)\subseteq\bigcap_nf(K_n)$ holds for every map. Conversely, let $y$ belong to the right side. The sets $L_n=K_n\cap f^{-1}(\{y\})$ are nonempty and nested. They are [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) because singletons are closed in a [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) space and $f$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md). By part (b)(i), some $x$ lies in every $L_n$. Then $x\in\bigcap_nK_n$ and $f(x)=y$, proving **equality of the two sets**.
