↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [stabilizer](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) of the indicated flag consists of block upper triangular matrices with diagonal block sizes $1,2,2$. The diagonal blocks can be any elements of $\operatorname{GL}(1,q)$, $\operatorname{GL}(2,q)$ and $\operatorname{GL}(2,q)$. The upper off-diagonal blocks have $2+2+4=8$ arbitrary entries. Thus the [stabilizer](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) order is $(q-1)|\operatorname{GL}(2,q)|^2q^8$. The [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{|\Omega|=\frac{|\operatorname{GL}(5,q)|}{(q-1)|\operatorname{GL}(2,q)|^2q^8}
=\frac{(q^5-1)(q^4-1)(q^3-1)}{(q-1)^2(q^2-1)}}.
$$
