↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An invertible matrix is an ordered [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $K^n$. After choosing $j$ independent columns, their span has $q^j$ elements, so the next column has $q^n-q^j$ choices. Thus

$$
\boxed{|\operatorname{GL}(n,q)|=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(q^n-q^j)}.
$$
