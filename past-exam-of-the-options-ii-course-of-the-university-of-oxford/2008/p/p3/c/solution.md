<h1 id="p/p3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $q=A+Bx$ and $\ell=\log3$. [Orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of $x^2-q$ to $1$ and $x$ gives

$$
\begin{pmatrix}\ell&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}A\\B\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}4\\26/3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The [Gram matrix](../../../../../gram-matrix.md) is positive definite, with [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) $4(\ell-1)>0$, so the solution is unique. Solving yields

$$
\boxed{q(x)=-\frac1{3(\log3-1)}+\left(\frac{13}{6}+\frac1{6(\log3-1)}\right)x}.
$$
