<h1 id="p/p4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [QR factorization](../../../../../qr-decomposition.md) is $A=QR$, where $Q^TQ=I$ and $R$ is upper triangular. If $A$ is [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md), so is $R=Q^TA$. Its inverse is upper triangular, but $R^{-1}=R^T$ is lower triangular, forcing $R$ to be diagonal. [Orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{R=\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_n),\quad\varepsilon_i\in\{1,-1\}}.
$$

With the customary positive diagonal convention, $R=I$.
