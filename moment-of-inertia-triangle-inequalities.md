# Moment-of-inertia triangle inequalities

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

For three mutually perpendicular axes through one point of a nonnegative [mass distribution](mass-distribution.md), $I_x+I_y-I_z=2\int z^2\,dm\ge0$, with cyclic counterparts. Equality holds precisely when the [mass distribution](mass-distribution.md) is supported in the plane $z=0$, giving the [perpendicular axis theorem](perpendicular-axis-theorem.md). The point need not be the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md), and the axes need not be [principal axes](principal-axis.md).
