# Three-dimensional box norm

↑ **Parent:** [Box norm](box-norm.md)

For a complex function on $X_1\times X_2\times X_3$, its eighth power is the average of the conjugated product over the eight vertices of a coordinate cube. Three successive applications of the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) show that it bounds correlation with a product of three bounded functions, each omitting one coordinate. This is the analytic estimate behind the [tetrahedron counting lemma](tetrahedron-counting-lemma.md).
