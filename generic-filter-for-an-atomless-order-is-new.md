# Generic filter for an atomless order is new

↑ **Parent:** [Atomless forcing order](atomless-forcing-order.md)

If a [generic filter](generic-filter.md) $G$ for an [atomless forcing order](atomless-forcing-order.md) belonged to its ground model, then the complement $\mathbb P\setminus G$ would be a dense ground-model [set](set-split.md). A condition outside $G$ is already there; a condition in $G$ has two incompatible strengthenings, at least one outside its directed filter. Genericity would then require meeting the complement, a contradiction.
