# Vacuum functional

↑ **Parent:** [Generating functional](generating-functional.md)

The zero-[source](source-quantum-field-theory.md) [generating functional](generating-functional.md) is the [vacuum state](vacuum-state.md) persistence amplitude, conventionally written $Z[0]=\int\mathcal D\phi\,e^{iS[\phi]}$ with a suitable [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md). Dividing by $Z[0]$ removes disconnected [Vacuum Feynman diagrams](vacuum-feynman-diagram.md) from normalized [Green functions](green-s-function-split.md). Adding a constant [vacuum energy density](vacuum-energy-density.md) to the [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) changes $Z[0]$ by a spacetime-volume phase but leaves these normalized [Green functions](green-s-function-split.md) unchanged.
