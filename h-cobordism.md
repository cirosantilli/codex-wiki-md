# H-cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Cobordism](cobordism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H-cobordism)

A compact smooth [cobordism](cobordism.md) is an [h-cobordism](h-cobordism.md) when both boundary inclusions $M_i\hookrightarrow W$ are [homotopy equivalences](homotopy-equivalence.md). The name does not itself impose simple connectivity. A [trivial cobordism](trivial-cobordism.md) is an [h-cobordism](h-cobordism.md) because a product cylinder retracts onto either end.
