# Bare coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

A [bare coupling](bare-coupling.md) is an interaction coefficient in a regulated action. It is related to a [renormalized coupling](renormalized-coupling.md) through counterterms and any required powers of the [renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md). For quartic scalar theory in $4-\epsilon$ dimensions, its [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) is $\epsilon$, and $\lambda_0=\mu^\epsilon[\lambda_R+a\lambda_R^2/\epsilon+\cdots]$. At fixed regulator, changing $\mu$ while leaving the theory unchanged keeps $\lambda_0$ fixed; the corresponding change of $\lambda_R$ defines its [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md).
