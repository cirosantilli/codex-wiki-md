# Raw moment recursion for a compound Poisson distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md)

Write $m_k=\mathbb E S^k$, $m_0=1$, and $\mu_j=\mathbb E X^j$. Apply the [Poisson size-bias identity](poisson-size-bias-identity.md) with $h(s)=s^{k-1}$, and expand $(S+X)^{k-1}$ using the [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md) and [independence](independent-random-variables.md). A finite $k$th severity [moment](moment.md) suffices. In particular, the aggregate [expected value](expected-value.md), [variance](variance-split.md), and third [central moment](central-moment.md) are $\lambda\mu_1$, $\lambda\mu_2$, and $\lambda\mu_3$, respectively; the latter two use raw severity [moments](moment.md).
