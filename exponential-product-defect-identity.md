# Exponential product defect identity

↑ **Parent:** [Lie product formula](lie-product-formula.md)

For finite [matrices](matrix.md), differentiate the ordered product to obtain $\Phi'-(A+B)\Phi=[e^{tA},B]e^{tB}$. Multiplying the error by $e^{-t(A+B)}$ and integrating gives $\Phi(t)-e^{t(A+B)}=\int_0^t e^{(t-s)(A+B)}[e^{sA},B]e^{sB}\,ds$. This exact [variation-of-constants formula](variation-of-constants-formula.md) identifies noncommutativity as the source of [Lie-Trotter splitting](lie-product-formula.md) error.
