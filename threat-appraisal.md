# Threat appraisal

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical psychology](clinical-psychology.md)

Interpretation of an event or sensation in terms of its danger, likelihood, consequences and available [coping](coping.md). An appraisal becomes a proposed maintaining process when it predicts continuing fear and changes in it alter the relevant response.
