# Magnetic entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic demagnetization](adiabatic-demagnetization.md)

[Magnetic entropy](magnetic-entropy.md) counts the uncertainty in the occupied magnetic [microstates](microstate.md). For independent localized [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) with one-site [probabilities](probability.md) $p_j$,

$$
S_{\rm mag}=-Nk_B\sum_j p_j\ln p_j,
$$

where $N$ counts sites and $k_B$ is the [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md). This is the [Gibbs entropy](gibbs-entropy.md) for the magnetic degrees of freedom. Equal occupation of two states gives $Nk_B\ln2$; certain occupation of one state gives zero. Calling this disorder is useful only if the relevant states and their [probabilities](probability.md) are specified. In an interacting material, correlations generally prevent replacing the many-body [Gibbs entropy](gibbs-entropy.md) by this sum of independent one-site contributions.

Increasing the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) at fixed positive [temperature](temperature-split.md) favors lower magnetic [energy levels](energy-level.md), reducing [magnetic entropy](magnetic-entropy.md). During ideal isolated demagnetization, both the level separation and [temperature](temperature-split.md) fall together, leaving the occupation [probabilities](probability.md) unchanged. Thus the independent-spin idealization cools without increasing spin disorder along the isolated leg. When a real crystal's vibrating lattice also cools, its lost [entropy](entropy-split.md) can instead be taken up by the magnetic degrees of freedom. The conserved quantity is the total [entropy](entropy-split.md) of the thermally isolated stage, including any attached load in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md).
