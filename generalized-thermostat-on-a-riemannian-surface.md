# Generalized thermostat on a Riemannian surface

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic flow](geodesic-flow.md)

A smooth real function $\lambda$ on the [unit tangent bundle](unit-tangent-bundle.md) determines this unit-speed [smooth flow](smooth-flow.md). Its base velocity is $v$, so its generator never vanishes. In the canonical frame its divergence in [Liouville volume of a surface geodesic flow](liouville-volume-of-a-surface-geodesic-flow.md) is $V\lambda$. The special case where $\lambda$ depends only on the base is a [magnetic flow](magnetic-flow-on-a-riemannian-surface.md); when $\lambda(x,v)=\langle E(x),iv\rangle$ it is a [Gaussian thermostat](gaussian-thermostat.md).
