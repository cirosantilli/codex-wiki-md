# Informational masking

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic masking](acoustic-masking.md)

Interference involving uncertainty, source confusion or selection beyond a simple account of overlapping acoustic energy. Demonstrating it requires suitable controls for audibility and peripheral overlap; merely finding a difficult listening task is insufficient.
