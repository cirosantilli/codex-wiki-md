# Equal-covariance Gaussian classification error

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian Bayes classifier](gaussian-bayes-classifier.md)

For two [multivariate normal](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) classes with equal priors and common [positive-definite](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md) covariance, the optimal score is $(\mu_1-\mu_2)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-(\mu_1+\mu_2)/2)$. Its distribution within either class is normal with [variance](variance-split.md) $D^2$ and mean respectively $D^2/2$ or $-D^2/2$. Both class error probabilities are therefore the displayed normal tail.
