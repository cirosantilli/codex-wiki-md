# Killing-flow four-acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md)

With [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(+---)$, normalize a timelike [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) by $U^a=K^a/N$, $N^2=K^aK_a$. The [Killing equation](killing-equation.md) gives $K^b\nabla_bK_a=-\tfrac12\nabla_aN^2$, while $K^b\nabla_bN=0$. Thus the [four-acceleration](four-acceleration.md) of its normalized flow is the displayed gradient. With [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-+++)$ the corresponding lower-index formula has the opposite sign.
