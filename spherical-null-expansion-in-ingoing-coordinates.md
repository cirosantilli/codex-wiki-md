# Spherical null expansion in ingoing coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Null expansion](null-expansion.md)

For a four-dimensional spherical metric $ds^2=-f\,dv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2d\Omega^2$, a radial [null vector](null-vector.md) normal $K$ to a round sphere has [null expansion](null-expansion.md) $\theta_K=\tfrac12q^{AB}\mathcal L_Kq_{AB}=2K(r)/r$, where $q_{AB}=r^2\gamma_{AB}$. The angular metric changes only by a common scale, so radial congruences have zero [null shear](null-shear.md); hypersurface orthogonality also gives zero [null twist](null-twist.md). The convention here is the trace, not the screen-averaged expansion.
