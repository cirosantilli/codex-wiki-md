# Taylor swimming sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Microswimmer](microswimmer.md)

The Taylor swimming sheet is an infinite two-dimensional [microswimmer](microswimmer.md) whose prescribed traveling deformation drives [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md). A transverse wave $y_s=\epsilon\sin(x-t)$ of small amplitude swims opposite to its direction of propagation, with speed $U=\epsilon^2/2+O(\epsilon^4)$ when lengths are scaled by inverse wavenumber and velocities by wave speed. Its [biharmonic stream function for planar Stokes flow](biharmonic-stream-function-for-planar-stokes-flow.md) has first-order part $\psi_1=(1+y)e^{-y}\sin(x-t)$.
