# Elastic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Elasticity (physics)](elasticity-physics-split.md)

Elastic energy is the recoverable energy stored by deforming an elastic material. In the small-deflection model of an [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md), the bending contribution is a positive quadratic functional of the curvatures, such as $\tfrac12\int(Ay''^2+Bz''^2)\,dx$. Work by applied loads must also be included when minimizing the total potential energy.
