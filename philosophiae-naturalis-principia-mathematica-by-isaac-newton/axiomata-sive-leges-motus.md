Normalized Latin transcription of Axiomata sive leges motus from the 1687 book. The Newton Project published the transcription online in November 2009; host and transcription editors are distinct from [Isaac Newton](../isaac-newton.md), the book author.

Cited passages: Isaac Newton, Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica (1687), Axiomata sive leges motus, printed p. 13, Lex III and its explanation; pp. 16–17, Corollarium III; Newton, Axiomata sive leges motus (1687), Lex II.
