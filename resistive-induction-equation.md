# Resistive induction equation

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive magnetohydrodynamics](resistive-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

For a [solenoidal vector field](solenoidal-vector-field.md) $\mathbf B$ and constant [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) $\eta$, the magnetic induction equation is

$$
\partial_t\mathbf B=\nabla\times(\mathbf u\times\mathbf B)+\eta\nabla^2\mathbf B,\qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0.
$$

It combines [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) with the moving-conductor relation for [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md), neglecting displacement current. With [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), its advective form is $\partial_t\mathbf B+(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf B=(\mathbf B\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u+\eta\nabla^2\mathbf B$.
