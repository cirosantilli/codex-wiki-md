# Minimum-degree Ramsey lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilistic combinatorics](probabilistic-combinatorics-split.md)

If a graph $H$ has [minimum degree](minimum-degree-of-a-graph.md) $d$, then its [graph Ramsey number](graph-ramsey-number.md) satisfies $R(H)\geq2^{d/2}$. The probabilistic proof chooses a red-blue edge colouring and applies the local lemma to its monochromatic copies of $H$.
