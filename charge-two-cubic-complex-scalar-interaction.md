# Charge-two cubic complex-scalar interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md)

This interaction preserves the [global U(1) symmetry](global-u-1-symmetry.md) with relative charges $q_\phi=1$, $q_\Phi=2$. The two conjugate [interaction vertices](interaction-vertex.md) have [Feynman rule](feynman-rule.md) $-ig$, with either one charge-two arrow entering and two charge-one arrows leaving, or all arrows reversed. For $\phi\phi\to\phi\phi$ only the heavy $s$-channel exchange occurs at tree level. For $\phi\bar\phi\to\phi\bar\phi$, with outgoing particle labelled 3 and outgoing antiparticle labelled 4, only the heavy $u$-channel exchange occurs. For small momenta and heavy mediator mass, the amplitudes coincide but the identical-particle total [scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md) is smaller by the [identical final-state symmetry factor](identical-particle-factor-in-a-final-state-phase-space-integral.md) $1/2$.
