# Degree of polymerization

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_of_polymerization)

The [degree of polymerization](degree-of-polymerization.md) counts [monomeric units](monomeric-unit.md) in a chain. $X_n$ is its number average over a specified collection of chains. State whether unreacted [monomers](monomer.md) are included and distinguish this count from [constitutional repeating units](repeat-unit.md) or statistical segments. See [IUPAC: degree of polymerization](https://doi.org/10.1515/pac-2023-0304).
