# Fourier transform

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourier_transform)

For $f\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)$, the angular-frequency Fourier transform is

$$
\widehat f(\xi)
=\int_{\mathbb R^n}f(x)e^{-ix\cdot\xi}\,dx.
$$
