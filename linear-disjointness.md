# Linear disjointness

↑ **Parent:** [Field compositum](field-compositum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_disjointness)

More generally, two field extensions inside a common overfield are [linearly disjoint field extensions](linear-disjointness.md) when the multiplication map from their [tensor product of fields](tensor-product-of-fields.md) into that overfield is injective. The following basis criterion is the finite-degree case.

Finite extensions $L_1/K$ and $L_2/K$ are linearly disjoint when products of basis elements form a basis of their compositum. In particular,

$$
[L_1L_2:K]=[L_1:K][L_2:K].
$$

Extensions of coprime finite degrees are linearly disjoint.
