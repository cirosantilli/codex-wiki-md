# Aldous tightness criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Tightness of a probability measure](tightness-of-a-probability-measure.md)

For càdlàg processes on a finite horizon, compact containment together with uniformly small increments over pairs of bounded [stopping times](stopping-time.md) whose separation tends to zero is a sufficient criterion for tightness in the [Skorokhod J1 topology](skorokhod-j1-topology.md). The increment condition is in probability, not merely at deterministic times. If in addition the largest jump tends to zero in probability, every limiting law is supported on continuous paths.
