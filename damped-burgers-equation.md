# Damped Burgers equation

↑ **Parent:** [Burgers' equation](burgers-equation.md)

The [Burgers' equation](burgers-equation.md) with constant linear damping has the displayed form. For zero viscosity and $\gamma>0$, [characteristics](characteristic-of-a-field.md) are $u=u_0(a)e^{-\gamma t}$ and $x=a+u_0(a)(1-e^{-\gamma t})/\gamma$. A negative initial slope can make this coordinate map cease to be invertible, producing [shock formation by characteristic intersection](shock-formation-by-characteristic-intersection.md). The transformation $u=e^{-\gamma t}v$, $\tau=(1-e^{-\gamma t})/\gamma$ converts the inviscid equation to the ordinary inviscid [Burgers' equation](burgers-equation.md). Across a thin viscous layer the damping contributes only at lower order to the leading [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md).
