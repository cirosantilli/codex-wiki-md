# Einstein radius

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing.md)

For a point lens of [mass](mass.md) $m$ at observer distance $D_l$ and source distance $D_s$ in a nearby Euclidean geometry, the [Einstein radius](einstein-radius.md) in the lens plane is $R_E=\sqrt{4GmD_l(D_s-D_l)/(c^2D_s)}$. Its angular version is $\theta_E=R_E/D_l$. This is the ring radius for perfect alignment and the characteristic transverse scale of a [gravitational microlensing](gravitational-microlensing.md) event. A crossing timescale is $t_E=R_E/v_\perp$, proportional to the square root of lens mass at fixed geometry and speed.
