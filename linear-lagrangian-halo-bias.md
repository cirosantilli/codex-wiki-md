# Linear Lagrangian halo bias

↑ **Parent:** [Peak-background split](peak-background-split.md)

Linear Lagrangian halo bias is the fractional response of halo abundance to a long-wavelength overdensity in the initial coordinates. For Press-Schechter halos, $b_L=(\nu^2-1)/\delta_c$.
