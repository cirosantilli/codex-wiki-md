# Quantum nonlocality

↑ **Parent:** [Bell theorem](bell-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_nonlocality)

Quantum correlations incompatible with [Bell local causality](bell-local-causality.md) under additional assumptions including [measurement independence](measurement-independence.md). This is an operational statement about joint probabilities, not controllable faster-than-light signaling: quantum predictions also obey [quantum no-signalling](quantum-no-signalling.md). Entanglement alone does not guarantee violation of a specified Bell inequality in a given measurement arrangement.
