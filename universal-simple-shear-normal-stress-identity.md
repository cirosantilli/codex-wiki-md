# Universal simple-shear normal-stress identity

↑ **Parent:** [Coaxiality of isotropic elastic stress](coaxiality-of-isotropic-elastic-stress.md)

For [simple shear](simple-shear.md), $B=\begin{pmatrix}1+\gamma^2&\gamma\\\gamma&1\end{pmatrix}$. The off-diagonal entry of $\sigma B-B\sigma$ is $\gamma(\sigma_{11}-\sigma_{22})-\gamma^2\sigma_{12}$. [Coaxiality of isotropic elastic stress](coaxiality-of-isotropic-elastic-stress.md) therefore proves the identity for $\gamma\ne0$. At $\gamma=0$, [material isotropy](material-isotropy.md) makes the stress isotropic, proving the same identity.
