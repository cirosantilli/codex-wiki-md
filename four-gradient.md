# Four-gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)

For coordinates $x^\mu=(ct,x,y,z)$, the [four-gradient](four-gradient.md) of a scalar field is its [covector](covector.md) of coordinate derivatives. Under a constant [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md), $\partial'_\mu=(\Lambda^{-1})^\nu{}_{\mu}\partial_\nu$. In [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(+,-,-,-)$, raising its index gives $\partial^\mu=(c^{-1}\partial_t,-\nabla)$. Contracting it with a [four-vector](four-vector.md) field produces the [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) [four-divergence](four-divergence.md). In particular $\partial_\mu J^\mu=0$ is charge continuity in every [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md).
