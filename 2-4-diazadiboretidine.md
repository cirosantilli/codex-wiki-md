<h1 id="2-4-diazadiboretidine">2,4-Diazadiboretidine</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

A [2,4-Diazadiboretidine](2-4-diazadiboretidine.md) contains an alternating four-membered B–N–B–N ring. [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) [lone pairs](lone-pair.md) provide sigma-donor sites, while the ring can bind an [electron](electron.md)-rich [metal](metal.md) through a pi interaction involving both B and N.
