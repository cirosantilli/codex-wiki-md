# Proton-precession magnetometer

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic survey](magnetic-survey.md)

An absolute scalar [magnetometer](magnetometer.md) that infers $|\mathbf B|$ from the free [Larmor precession](larmor-precession.md) frequency of polarized [protons](proton.md). It measures field strength rather than field direction.
