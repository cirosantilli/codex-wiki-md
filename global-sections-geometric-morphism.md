# Global sections geometric morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md)

Every [Grothendieck topos](grothendieck-topos.md) has a unique [geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md) to sets, up to isomorphism. Its inverse image is $\Delta S=\coprod_{s\in S}1$ and its direct image is $\Gamma A=\operatorname{Hom}(1,A)$. A geometric inverse image out of sets must have this form because it preserves [coproducts](coproduct.md) and the [terminal object](terminal-object.md). A [local topos](local-topos.md) is one for which $\Gamma$ itself is an inverse image.
