# Null-homotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Null-homotopic map](null-homotopic-map.md)

A [null-homotopy](null-homotopy.md) of a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $f:X\to Y$ is a [homotopy](homotopy-split.md) from $f$ to a [constant function](constant-function.md) $c:X\to Y$. Existence of such a [homotopy](homotopy-split.md) means precisely that $f$ is [null-homotopic](null-homotopic-map.md).
