# Serotonin

↑ **Parent:** [Neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md)

A [monoamine](monoamine.md) transmitter also called 5-hydroxytryptamine. Distinct receptors and circuits can have different effects; blood levels are not a direct reading of synaptic transmission in the [brain](brain.md).
