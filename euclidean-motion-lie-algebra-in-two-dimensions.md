# Euclidean motion Lie algebra in two dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

The real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak{iso}(2)$ is a semidirect product of planar translations with infinitesimal rotations. One orientation convention is $[J,E_1]=-E_2$, $[J,E_2]=E_1$, $[E_1,E_2]=0$. Its translation space is a two-dimensional abelian [ideal of a Lie algebra](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
