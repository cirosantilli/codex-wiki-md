# Statistical significance

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_significance)

A result is [statistically significant](statistical-significance.md) at a specified [significance level](significance-level.md) $\alpha$ when its valid [p-value](p-value.md) is at most $\alpha$ and the corresponding [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md) is rejected. Significance does not measure effect size, establish practical importance, or give the probability that the null is true. Software stars encode thresholds for $p$-values; they do not supply an additional statistic.
