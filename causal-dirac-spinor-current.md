# Causal Dirac spinor current

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac current](dirac-current.md)

For a commuting spinor in mostly-plus signature, orient its [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) to the future and write $V^i=\epsilon^\dagger\alpha^i\epsilon$, with Hermitian $\alpha^i=-\gamma^0\gamma^i$. For every spatial [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $u$, $(u\cdot\alpha)^2=1$ implies $|u\cdot V|\le\epsilon^\dagger\epsilon=V^0$. Choosing $u$ along the spatial current proves $V^2\le0$. Thus spinor bilinears supply causal [vectors](vector.md) suitable for energy-condition estimates. A Grassmann-valued spinor does not have this ordinary positivity interpretation.
