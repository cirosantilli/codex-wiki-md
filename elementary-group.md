# Elementary group

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group](finite-group.md)

In finite-group character theory, an elementary group is a group that is [p-elementary group](p-elementary-group.md) for some prime $p$. Such groups are solvable and provide the subgroup tests in [Brauer's characterization of characters](brauer-s-characterization-of-characters.md). This does not mean [elementary abelian group](elementary-abelian-group.md).
