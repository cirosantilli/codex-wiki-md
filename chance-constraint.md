# Chance constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

A constraint imposed with a specified [probability](probability.md) when data or demands are random. Distributional assumptions can turn it into a deterministic condition on decision variables. The choice of strict versus non-strict event matters when the distribution has atoms, as in the [deterministic boundary in an upper-tail chance constraint](deterministic-boundary-in-an-upper-tail-chance-constraint.md).
