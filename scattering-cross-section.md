# Scattering cross-section

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md)

A scattering cross-section divides the scattered rate per target by the incident [particle flux](particle-flux.md). Its differential form resolves that rate by outgoing direction. It has units of area for three-dimensional scattering and length for two-dimensional scattering. In two dimensions, the convention $\psi\sim e^{ikr\cos\theta}+e^{i\pi/4}f(\theta)e^{ikr}/\sqrt{kr}$ gives $d\sigma/d\theta=|f(\theta)|^2/k$ by comparing outgoing and incident [probability currents](probability-current.md).
