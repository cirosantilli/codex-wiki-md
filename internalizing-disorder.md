# Internalizing disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Mental disorder](mental-disorder.md)

A grouping of disorders involving inwardly experienced distress, including several [anxiety](anxiety.md) and depressive conditions. It is not one single diagnosis, and an intervention effect on an aggregate measure need not hold for every component.
