# Lattice gauge theory

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge theory](gauge-theory.md)

A formulation of a [gauge theory](gauge-theory.md) with discretized [spacetime](spacetime.md). Matter fields are assigned to sites and gauge-group elements to links between nearby sites. Products of link variables around closed paths provide gauge-invariant quantities. This structure preserves the local [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md) while making nonperturbative numerical calculations possible. Physical predictions require control of finite spacing, finite volume and statistical errors rather than treating one finite grid as the continuum theory.
