# Head normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Untyped lambda calculus](untyped-lambda-calculus.md)

A [lambda term](lambda-term.md) has head normal form when it beta-reduces to a term $\lambda x_1\cdots x_k.\,yM_1\cdots M_r$ with a variable $y$ at the head. The argument terms need not be in [beta-normal form](beta-normal-form.md). A term with no head normal form cannot be beta-equivalent to a [Church numeral](church-numeral.md).
