# MicroRNA tumour suppressor

↑ **Parent:** [Tumour suppressor gene](tumour-suppressor-gene.md)

A [microRNA](microrna.md) whose endogenous activity restrains [tumour](tumour.md) development or progression in a specified context. Specific loss, controlled restoration and target tests provide stronger evidence than an expression correlation.
