# Stieltjes matrix resolvent

↑ **Parent:** [Resolvent of an operator](resolvent-of-an-operator.md)

For a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) $X$ and nonreal $z$, this is the negative of the usual [resolvent of an operator](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) convention $(zI-X)^{-1}$. Its normalized trace is the [Stieltjes transform of a measure](stieltjes-transform-of-a-measure.md) of the [empirical spectral measure](empirical-spectral-measure.md) with kernel $(x-z)^{-1}$. Naming the convention prevents sign errors in self-consistency identities.
