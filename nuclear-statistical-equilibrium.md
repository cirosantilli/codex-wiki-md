# Nuclear statistical equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_statistical_equilibrium)

At sufficiently high temperature and density, forward and inverse nuclear reactions can balance so that abundances are determined by thermodynamic conditions, nuclear binding and conserved quantities. [Silicon burning](silicon-burning.md) approaches such an equilibrium among iron-group nuclei. Weak reactions can separately change the electron fraction, so the favored mixture need not be pure [iron](iron.md).
