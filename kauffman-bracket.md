# Kauffman bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Link diagram](link-diagram.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kauffman_bracket)

The Kauffman bracket is the unoriented diagram invariant determined by resolving each crossing into its two smoothings and replacing every additional disjoint circle by the loop factor $-A^2-A^{-2}$. It is invariant under the second and third [Reidemeister moves](reidemeister-move.md).
