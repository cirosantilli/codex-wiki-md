# Schizophrenia

↑ **Parent:** [Mental disorder](mental-disorder.md)

A psychiatric disorder involving characteristic disturbances of perception, belief, thought and functioning. Genetic evidence often concerns diagnostic liability rather than a single symptom mechanism or the exact timing of onset.
