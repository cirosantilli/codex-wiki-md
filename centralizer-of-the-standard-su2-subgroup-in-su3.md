# Centralizer of the standard SU2 subgroup in SU3

↑ **Parent:** [Centralizer](centralizer.md)

Embed [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) into [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) by $U\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(U,1)$. Its defining [representation](group-representation.md) decomposes as an irreducible doublet and a [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md). The [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) forces every commuting [matrix](matrix.md) to be $\operatorname{diag}(zI_2,w)$. Unitarity and unit [determinant](determinant.md) imply $|z|=1$ and $w=z^{-2}$, proving the formula. With [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md) generator $X=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2)$, the defining [representation](group-representation.md) restricts as $\mathbf2_1\oplus\mathbf1_{-2}$ and the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) as $\mathbf3_0\oplus\mathbf2_3\oplus\mathbf2_{-3}\oplus\mathbf1_0$. The [U(1) charges](u-1-charge.md) in the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) are differences of defining charges. The generated connected [subgroup](subgroup.md) is $(SU(2)\times U(1))/\mathbb Z_2$, since the two factors meet in $\operatorname{diag}(-I_2,1)$.
