# Spherical derivative of a meromorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md)

For a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) viewed as a [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) to the unit [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), this is the length density of the pulled-back spherical metric. Its square is the pulled-back area density. At a [pole](pole.md) use the coordinate $1/f$, which gives the same density. Some conventions omit the factor two and use a sphere of area $\pi$; all area normalizations must then change accordingly.
