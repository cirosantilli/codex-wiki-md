# Radial tensor moments in three dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic tensor integral](isotropic-tensor-integral.md)

For a radial weight $F$ with the required absolutely integrable moments, [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md) makes the second and fourth moments [isotropic tensors](isotropic-tensor.md). The second moment is $A\delta_{ij}$; its [trace](matrix-trace.md) gives $3A=4\pi\int_0^\infty r^4F(r)\,dr$. Complete symmetry of the fourth moment forces equal coefficients of the three [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md) pairings. Its double [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md) gives $15B=4\pi\int_0^\infty r^6F(r)\,dr$, proving the displayed expression. Odd moments vanish under $x\mapsto-x$. Choosing $F$ to be the [indicator function](indicator-function.md) of a [spherical shell](spherical-shell.md) gives all the corresponding shell moments without separate angular integrations.
