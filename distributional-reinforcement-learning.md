# Distributional reinforcement learning

↑ **Parent:** [Reinforcement learning](reinforcement-learning.md)

[Learning](learning.md) about a distribution of possible future returns rather than only its mean. Multiple prediction channels can vary in optimism and sensitivity to positive and negative errors. Such population heterogeneity can refine a [reward prediction error](reward-prediction-error.md) account without implying that every [dopamine](dopamine.md) signal performs the same computation.
