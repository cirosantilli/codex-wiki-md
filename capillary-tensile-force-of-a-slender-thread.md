# Capillary tensile force of a slender thread

↑ **Parent:** [Slender viscous thread](slender-viscous-thread.md)

For an axisymmetric [slender viscous thread](slender-viscous-thread.md), the [Young–Laplace equation](young-laplace-equation.md) and radial extensional [viscous stress](viscous-stress-tensor.md) give $p=p_e+\gamma/a-\mu w_z$. The axial liquid stress relative to ambient [pressure](pressure.md) is $3\mu w_z-\gamma/a$. Its cross-sectional [integral](integral.md) plus the circumferential [surface tension](surface-tension.md) [force](force.md) $2\pi\gamma a$ gives the displayed tensile [force](force.md). Thus the net capillary contribution is $\pi\gamma a$, and axial [force](force.md) balance with downward gravity is $T_z+\pi\rho ga^2=0$.
