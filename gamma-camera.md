# Gamma camera

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear medicine](nuclear-medicine.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma_camera)

A position-sensitive detector, usually using a [collimator](collimator.md), [scintillator](scintillator.md) and light sensors, that forms a projection image of a gamma-emitting tracer. Energy selection reduces scattered-photon contamination.
