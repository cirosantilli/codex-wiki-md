# Field-induced triplon condensation

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md)

A [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) can close the lowest gap of a dilute bosonic triplet branch. In a shifted-parabola model with actual [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) zero, $\epsilon_k=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)+\nu n-\eta(H-H_g)$ and repulsion $\nu>0$ prevent indefinite [density](density.md) growth. The normal minimum reaches zero when $\eta(H_c-H_g)=\nu n$. At that point the finite thermal excited population saturates and macroscopic ground-state occupation can develop.
