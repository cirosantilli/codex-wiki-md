# Normal-ordered free scalar four-momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum of a free real scalar field](four-momentum-of-a-free-real-scalar-field.md)

For a canonically quantized [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md), the quadratic Hamiltonian contains $\frac12\sum_{\mathbf p}E_{\mathbf p}$ in a regulated box. [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) subtracts this [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md), leaving

$$
P^\mu=\int\frac{d^3p}{(2\pi)^3}p^\mu a_{\mathbf p}^\dagger a_{\mathbf p}.
$$

The vacuum momentum is zero with an inversion-symmetric regulator. The [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) then gives $[P^\mu,a_{\mathbf p}^\dagger]=p^\mu a_{\mathbf p}^\dagger$, identifying the momentum and energy added by one particle. The un-subtracted energy differs by a constant and is not literally the same operator.
