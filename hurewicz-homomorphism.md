# Hurewicz homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md)

A sphere map $f:S^n\to X$ is sent to $f_*[S^n]\in H_n(X;\mathbb Z)$. After tensoring with $\mathbb Q$ this gives the rational [Hurewicz homomorphism](hurewicz-homomorphism.md). It is natural in $X$, sends [Whitehead products](whitehead-product.md) to zero, and is an isomorphism in the first nonzero [homotopy](homotopy.md) degree of a [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) space by the [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md).
