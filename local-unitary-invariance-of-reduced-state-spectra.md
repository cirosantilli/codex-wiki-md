# Local-unitary invariance of reduced-state spectra

↑ **Parent:** [Local unitary operation](local-unitary-operation.md)

Under a product [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) $U_A\otimes U_B$, the [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) of $A$ becomes $U_A\rho_AU_A^\dagger$. The unitary on $B$ cancels under the [partial trace](partial-trace.md). Unitary conjugation preserves [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), so the squared [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md) of a bipartite [pure state](pure-state.md) are unchanged. Conversely, two normalized bipartite pure states with the same squared Schmidt coefficients, including multiplicities and zero padding, have [Schmidt decompositions](schmidt-decomposition.md) carried into each other by local unitaries mapping their Schmidt bases. Thus reduced-state spectra completely classify bipartite pure states up to local unitary operations.
