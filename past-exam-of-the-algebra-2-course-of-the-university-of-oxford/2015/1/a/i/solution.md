<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $a=ub$ with $u$ a [unit in a ring](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md), then $a$ lies in the [principal ideal](../../../../../principal-ideal.md) generated by $b$. This gives $(a)\subseteq(b)$. Since $u$ has a [multiplicative inverse](../../../../../multiplicative-inverse.md), $b=u^{-1}a$, giving the reverse inclusion. Therefore

$$
\boxed{a=ub,\ u\in R^\times\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(a)=(b).}
$$

Thus [associate elements](../../../../../associate-element.md) always generate the same [principal ideal](../../../../../principal-ideal.md); the [associator ring](../../../../../associator-ring.md) condition asks whether equality of these [ideals](../../../../../ideal.md) also forces the generators to differ by a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md).
