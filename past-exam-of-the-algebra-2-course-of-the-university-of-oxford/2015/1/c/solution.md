<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $A=R/(p^k)$. A [principal ideal domain](../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) is a [unique factorization domain](../../../../unique-factorization-domain.md), so every nonzero element of this [quotient ring](../../../../quotient-ring.md) can be written

$$
\overline a=\overline{p}^{\,e}\overline s,
\qquad 0\leq e<k,\qquad p\nmid s.
$$

Indeed, remove the largest power of the [prime element](../../../../prime-element.md) $p$ from a representative that is not divisible by $p^k$. Since $s$ and $p^k$ have [greatest common divisor](../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) $1$ up to a [unit](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md), [Bézout's identity](../../../../bezout-s-identity.md) in the [principal ideal domain](../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) gives $xs+yp^k=1$. Thus $\overline s$ is a [unit](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) in $A$.

Consequently every nonzero [principal ideal](../../../../principal-ideal.md) in $A$ is generated by one of $1,\overline p,\ldots,\overline p^{\,k-1}$. Include the zero [ideal](../../../../ideal.md) by assigning it exponent $e=k$. These [ideals](../../../../ideal.md) are distinct: their inverse images in $R$ are $(1),(p),\ldots,(p^k)$, and equality of two different powers would, by cancellation in the [integral domain](../../../../integral-domain.md), make the nonunit $p$ a [unit](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md).

Now suppose $(\overline a)=(\overline b)$. If this is the zero [ideal](../../../../ideal.md), both generators are zero and are [associate elements](../../../../associate-element.md). Otherwise their exponents agree, so write

$$
\overline a=\overline p^{\,e}\overline s,\qquad
\overline b=\overline p^{\,e}\overline t
$$

with $\overline s,\overline t$ both [units](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md). Then

$$
\overline a=(\overline s\,\overline t^{-1})\overline b,
$$

and the multiplier is a [unit](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{R/(p^k)\text{ is an associator ring for every }k\geq1.}
$$

The exponent detects the [principal ideal](../../../../principal-ideal.md), while its remaining factor is invertible; this is why [zero divisors](../../../../zero-divisor.md) cause no obstruction here.
