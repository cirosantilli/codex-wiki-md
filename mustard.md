# Mustard

↑ **Parent:** [Plant medicinal material](plant-medicinal-material.md)

Mustard seed materials used in historical preparations. Moistening crushed black-mustard [seeds](seed.md) generates an irritant volatile product containing [allyl isothiocyanate](allyl-isothiocyanate.md); the volatile product differs from the fixed seed [oil](oil.md).
