# Magnetic-to-electric energy ratio in a driven disc capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md)

For thin parallel circular plates of radius $a$ and separation $L$, the quasistatic axial field $E_z(t)$ induces $H_\phi=\epsilon_0r\dot E_z/2$. Integrating cycle-averaged vacuum [energy](energy-split.md) [densities](density.md) for sinusoidal drive gives $\overline U_B/\overline U_E=(\omega a/c)^2/8$. The same fields have radial Poynting [flux](flux.md) $S_r=-\epsilon_0rE_z\dot E_z/2$, whose leading net inward [power](power.md) equals the time derivative of stored electric [energy](energy-split.md). The small ratio diagnoses the quasistatic regime.
