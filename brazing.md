# Brazing

↑ **Parent:** [Materials science](materials-science.md)

Joining by a liquid filler metal that wets and fills a gap while the parent pieces remain solid. A filler alloy with a suitable [eutectic temperature](eutectic-temperature.md) can provide a lower and narrow melting interval.
