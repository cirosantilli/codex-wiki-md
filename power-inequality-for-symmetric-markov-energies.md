# Power inequality for symmetric Markov energies

↑ **Parent:** [Joint energy of a symmetric Markov semigroup](joint-energy-of-a-symmetric-markov-semigroup.md)

For $a,b\geq0$ and $q>2$, [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) applied to the integral of $(q/2)s^{q/2-1}$ gives $(a^{q/2}-b^{q/2})^2\leq q^2( a^{q-1}-b^{q-1})(a-b)/(4(q-1))$. Integrating against the symmetric two-point kernel measure and taking the energy limit proves the displayed inequality.
