# Elliptic regularity

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic differential operator](elliptic-differential-operator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_regularity)

Elliptic regularity says that solutions of an [elliptic equation](elliptic-differential-operator.md) gain derivatives wherever the coefficients and forcing are regular. For the [Laplacian](laplacian.md), local estimates typically control two derivatives of $u$ by $\Delta u$ together with a lower-order norm of $u$.
