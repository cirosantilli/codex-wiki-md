<h1 id="relative-entropy-in-kac-s-model">Relative entropy in Kac's model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kac model](kac-model.md)

Use $\gamma_N=(2\pi)^{-N/2}e^{-|v|^2/2}$ as the reference probability density. The [relative entropy](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) is nonnegative by writing $F=\gamma_Nu$ and integrating $u\log u-u+1$. Its expansion is $\int F\log F+(N/2)\log(2\pi)+(1/2)\int|v|^2F$. Equality holds only at $F=\gamma_N$, whereas other radial densities may still be stationary for the [Kac master equation](kac-master-equation.md).
