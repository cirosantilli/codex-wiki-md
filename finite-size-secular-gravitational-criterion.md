# Finite-size secular gravitational criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-drag secular gravitational growth rate](weak-drag-secular-gravitational-growth-rate.md)

For a dust layer of extent $L$, its smallest admissible wavenumber is of order $1/L$. The [secular gravitational instability](secular-gravitational-instability-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md) band ends at $k_{\mathrm{crit}}=2\pi G\sigma_0/c^2=2/(QH)$, with [Toomre parameter](toomre-parameter.md) $Q=c\Omega/(\pi G\sigma_0)$ and $H=c/\Omega$. Requiring an admissible mode in the band gives $Q\lesssim L/H$. Boundary conditions fix the order-unity prefactor; a finite disk lifetime also limits practical growth.
