# Plasma-frequency form of London penetration depth

↑ **Parent:** [London penetration depth](london-penetration-depth.md)

For carrier [number density](number-density.md) $n$, charge $q$ and effective [mass](mass.md) $m$, the [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md) is $\omega_p^2=nq^2/(\epsilon_0m)$. Combining the second [London equation](london-equations.md) with the [magnetostatic Ampère law](magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) and [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) gives $\nabla^2\mathbf B=(\omega_p^2/c^2)\mathbf B$. Therefore the decaying planar solution has length $\lambda_L=c/\omega_p=\sqrt{m/(\mu_0nq^2)}$.
