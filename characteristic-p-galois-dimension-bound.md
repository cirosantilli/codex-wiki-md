# Characteristic p Galois dimension bound

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomological dimension at a prime](cohomological-dimension-at-a-prime.md)

A field of characteristic $p$ satisfies $\operatorname{cd}_p(K)\le1$. The Artin-Schreier sequence and vanishing of positive-degree cohomology of the additive separable-closure module yield the bound. It concerns discrete $p$-primary modules; it does not assert that the $p$-primary [Brauer group](brauer-group.md) vanishes.
