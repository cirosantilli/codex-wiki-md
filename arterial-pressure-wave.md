# Arterial pressure wave

↑ **Parent:** [Physiological fluid dynamics](physiological-fluid-dynamics.md)

A small [pressure](pressure.md) disturbance in an elastic artery propagates through the coupled inertia of blood and compliance of the wall. If $\mathcal A$ is the undisturbed area, $\rho$ the [mass density](density.md) and $c$ the [wave speed](wave-speed.md), the linearized equations give $c^2=\mathcal A/\rho\,dp/d\mathcal A$. A travelling [pressure](pressure.md) amplitude $p_+$ carries flow amplitude $\mathcal A p_+/(\rho c)$; the oppositely travelling wave has the opposite flow sign.
