# Thermocline

↑ **Parent:** [Physical oceanography](physical-oceanography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermocline)

A water layer across which [temperature](temperature-split.md) changes rapidly with depth. In a [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) description of the upper ocean, its displacement is represented by the interface between warm surface water and colder deep water. Deepening the eastern tropical Pacific thermocline makes [upwelling](upwelling.md) less effective at cooling the surface during [El Niño](el-nino.md).
