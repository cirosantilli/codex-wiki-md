# Negative minimal density in an arbitrage-free one-period market

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal martingale measure in a one-period market](minimal-martingale-measure-in-a-one-period-market.md)

Let a discounted gain $Y$ take values $-1,1,10$ with physical probabilities $1/10,4/5,1/10$. Then $m=17/10$ and $C=801/100$, so the [minimal martingale measure in a one-period market](minimal-martingale-measure-in-a-one-period-market.md) has signed density $Z_*(10)=-610/801$. Nevertheless $(109/200,89/200,1/100)$ is a strictly positive pricing probability with zero mean gain. Thus absence of [arbitrage](arbitrage.md) does not ensure positivity of the minimal density. The minimum-norm identity holds for signed pricing densities; interpretation as a [dominated martingale measure](dominated-martingale-measure.md) requires nonnegativity.
