# Seabed gouging by ice

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seabed_gouging_by_ice)

A moving ice keel can gouge sediment when its draft exceeds water depth. In an [exponential ridge-draft model](exponential-ridge-draft-model.md), a point crossed by ice at relative speed $|V|$ experiences candidate encounters at rate $|V|\mu\exp[-(\max(D,h_0)-h_0)/(h_m-h_0)]$. Actual erosion also depends on grounding, deformation of the keel and sediment strength.
