# Cyclic ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

A [cyclic ordering](cyclic-ordering.md) of a finite ground set records its elements around an oriented circle, identifying [permutations](permutation.md) that differ by rotation. There are $(n-1)!$ such orderings for $n\geq1$. A uniformly random [permutation](permutation.md) induces a uniformly random [cyclic ordering](cyclic-ordering.md).
