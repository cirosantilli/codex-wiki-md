# Gravity localization in a noncompact warped dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Randall–Sundrum model](randall-sundrum-model.md)

In a one-brane warped background, the proper extra dimension can be infinite while the four-dimensional [graviton](graviton.md) zero mode has finite norm. The [Einstein-Hilbert action](einstein-hilbert-action.md) weighs its four-dimensional curvature term by the warp factor integral shown above, finite for $k>0$. Thus the proper length is not the effective gravitational volume. This differs from an unwarped noncompact direction, whose volume integral diverges. It also differs from the two-brane hierarchy mechanism, where finite brane separation redshifts masses on the distant brane. The noncompact construction is established in [the original single-brane warped-gravity paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9906064).
