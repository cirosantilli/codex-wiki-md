# Gaussian shell covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum-shell renormalization group](momentum-shell-renormalization-group.md)

For a centered shell [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md) of a real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) with positive shell kernel $K(p)=\kappa p^2+r$, its [Gaussian functional integral](gaussian-functional-integral.md) gives $\langle\widetilde\phi(p)\widetilde\phi(q)\rangle=(2\pi)^D\delta^{(D)}(p+q)/K(p)$ on the shell. Inverse [Fourier transforms](fourier-transform.md) therefore yield $G_>(x-y)=\int_{\Lambda/b<|p|\leq\Lambda}d^Dp\,(2\pi)^{-D}e^{ip\cdot(x-y)}/K(p)$. The kernel must be positive throughout the shell. [Translation invariance](translation-invariance.md) makes the separation essential; a numerator involving $x$ alone is valid only with $y=0$ or if $x$ is redefined as the separation.
