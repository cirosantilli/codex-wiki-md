# Characteristic diamond for speed one minus y squared

↑ **Parent:** [Finite propagation speed](finite-propagation-speed.md)

For [Cauchy data](cauchy-data.md) prescribed on $x=0$ over $|y|<a<1$, put $S=\operatorname{artanh}a$. The maximal region determined solely by those data is

$$
\left\{(x,y):|x|+|\operatorname{artanh}y|<S\right\}.
$$

In the [travel-time coordinate for a one-dimensional variable-speed wave equation](travel-time-coordinate-for-a-one-dimensional-variable-speed-wave-equation.md) $(x,s)$ this is the ordinary characteristic diamond $|x|+|s|<S$, as follows from [finite propagation speed](finite-propagation-speed.md).
