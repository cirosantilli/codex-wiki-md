# Nuclear emulsion track experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear emulsion](nuclear-emulsion.md)

In 1947, [Cesare Lattes](cesare-mansueto-giulio-lattes.md), [Hugh Muirhead](hugh-muirhead.md), [Giuseppe Occhialini](giuseppe-paolo-stanislao-occhialini.md) and [Cecil Frank Powell](cecil-frank-powell.md) reported [charged particle](charged-particle.md) tracks in which a stopping particle was followed by a secondary particle. Their paper treated the interpretation as provisional.[Lattes, Muirhead, Occhialini and Powell, Processes Involving Charged Mesons, Nature 159 (24 May 1947), pp. 694–697, abstract and byline](processes-involving-charged-mesons-by-cesare-mansueto-giulio-lattes-and-others.md) The eventual distinction between [pions](pion.md) and [muons](muon.md) made a striking use of [photography](photography-split.md): a tiny permanent record could distinguish particles by how they slowed and transformed.

The method already had a history. In their jointly authored 1937 report, [Marietta Blau](marietta-blau.md) and [Hertha Wambacher](hertha-wambacher.md) described [photographic plates](photographic-plate.md) exposed for five months at Hafelekar, $2300\,\mathrm m$ above sea level.[Blau and Wambacher, Disintegration Processes by Cosmic Rays with the Simultaneous Emission of Several Heavy Particles, Nature 140 (1937), p. 585, opening paragraph and joint byline](disintegration-processes-by-cosmic-rays-with-the-simultaneous-emission-of-several-heavy-particles-by-marietta-blau-and-hertha-wambacher.md) Neither shared the 1950 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md), awarded to [Cecil Frank Powell](cecil-frank-powell.md).[Opening award statement: Powell's 1950 prize concerned development of the method and meson discoveries](research-profile-cecil-powell-by-luisa-bonolis.md) This was a development of photographic [particle detection](particle-detection-split.md), with earlier contributors, rather than its invention by one person.

In his own later account, [Cesare Lattes](cesare-mansueto-giulio-lattes.md) recalls calibrating emulsion shrinkage and the [particle range](particle-range.md)–[energy](energy-split.md) relation with accelerator-produced particles. [Giuseppe Occhialini](giuseppe-paolo-stanislao-occhialini.md) took borax-treated and untreated plates to Pic du Midi for about a month. The untreated plates provided a control; the treated plates retained more events because [latent image](latent-image.md) fading was reduced. Later, [Cesare Lattes](cesare-mansueto-giulio-lattes.md) exposed plates at the higher Chacaltaya station in Bolivia and obtained more candidate events.[Lattes, Meu trabalho em física de mésons com emulsões nucleares, 1984 recollection in Portuguese translation, paragraphs beginning “Foi-me dada a tarefa”, “Occhialini e eu decidimos”, “Quando Occhialini revelou” and “Assim, propus”; the recollection's altitude estimate is not adopted here](meu-trabalho-em-fisica-de-mesons-com-emulsoes-nucleares-by-cesare-mansueto-giulio-lattes.md) The Pic du Midi [cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md) exposure was roughly $3\,\mathrm{km}$ above sea level.[“The discovery of the pion”: Pic du Midi exposure altitude, given as 3000 metres](research-profile-cecil-powell-by-luisa-bonolis.md)
