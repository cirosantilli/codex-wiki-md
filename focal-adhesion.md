# Focal adhesion

↑ **Parent:** [Cell adhesion](cell-adhesion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focal_adhesion)

A [focal adhesion](focal-adhesion.md) is a multiprotein connection between [integrins](integrin.md) and the [actin](actin.md) [cytoskeleton](cytoskeleton.md). It can transmit traction and change its assembly in response to loading.
