# One-dimensional wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

The one-dimensional wave equation has left-moving and right-moving travelling-wave solutions. Their superposition is described by the [D'Alembert formula](d-alembert-s-formula.md) and remains valid distributionally for [locally integrable profiles](locally-integrable-function.md).
