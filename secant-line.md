# Secant line

↑ **Parent:** [Straight line](straight-line.md)

A [secant line](secant-line.md) is a [straight line](straight-line.md) passing through two distinct points of a [curve](curve.md). For a graph $y=f(x)$ at $x=a$ and $x=a+h$, its [slope](slope.md) is the [difference quotient](difference-quotient.md) $(f(a+h)-f(a))/h$. As the second point approaches the first, a limiting [secant line](secant-line.md) can give the [tangent line](tangent-line.md).
