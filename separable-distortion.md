# Separable distortion

↑ **Parent:** [Distortion measure (information theory)](distortion-measure-information-theory.md)

For a [block code](block-code.md), [separable distortion](separable-distortion.md) is the average of the individual symbol costs. With [Hamming distortion](hamming-distortion.md) it is the fraction of incorrectly reconstructed symbols, rather than the unnormalized number of mismatches. This normalization makes [expected distortion](expected-distortion.md) comparable across different block lengths.[§23.2, Definition 23.1, printed pp. 242–243](6-441-information-theory-spring-2016-lecture-notes-by-yury-polyanskiy-and-others/chapter-23-rate-distortion-theory.md)
