# Polymer globule

↑ **Parent:** [Poor solvent](poor-solvent.md)

A [polymer globule](polymer-globule.md) is a compact conformation favored by effective segment attractions in a [poor solvent](poor-solvent.md). If its internal segment density is independent of chain length, its volume is proportional to $N$ and a size measure scales as $N^{1/3}$. The proportionality between physical volume and the cube of a [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md) depends on shape and density profile. Compactness alone supplies scaling, not a uniquely determined numerical monomer volume.
