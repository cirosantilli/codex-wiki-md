# Velocity rotation in a time-dependent axial magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

Under the magnetic-only [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md), planar velocity has constant magnitude and an accumulated phase determined by the time integral of the axial field. For $B_z(t)=\beta t$, the planar position involves [Fresnel integrals](fresnel-integral.md). With initial planar velocity $(1,0)$ and zero initial position, its limit is $(\sqrt{\pi m/(4q\beta)},-\sqrt{\pi m/(4q\beta)})$. The model prescribes a magnetic-only force; a physical time-dependent field may also produce an induced electric field.
