# Biexciton

↑ **Parent:** [Exciton](exciton.md)

A [biexciton](biexciton.md) is a two-[electron](electron.md), two-[electron hole](electron-hole.md) excitation. If $E_X$ and $E_{XX}$ are total excitation [energies](energy-split.md) relative to the empty emitter, the cascade $XX\to X\to0$ emits photons of [energies](energy-split.md) $E_{XX}-E_X$ and $E_X$. Define its binding energy by $B_{XX}=2E_X-E_{XX}$. The first cascade photon then has [energy](energy-split.md) $E_X-B_{XX}$. Positive binding shifts that line below the single-[exciton](exciton.md) line; confinement can also produce negative binding, shifting it above. The cascade does not emit a single photon of twice the [exciton](exciton.md) energy.
