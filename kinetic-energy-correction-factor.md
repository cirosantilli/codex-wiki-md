# Kinetic energy correction factor

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md)

For unidirectional flow through area $A$ with mean speed $\overline u$, the outgoing kinetic-energy flux is $\tfrac12\rho A\alpha\overline u^3$, where $\alpha=(A\overline u^3)^{-1}\int_Au^3dA$. Cubing the mean speed generally differs from averaging the cube. A uniform profile has $\alpha=1$; circular laminar [Hagen-Poiseuille flow](hagen-poiseuille-equation.md) has $\alpha=2$.
