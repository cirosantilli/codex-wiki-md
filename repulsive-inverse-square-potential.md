# Repulsive inverse-square potential

↑ **Parent:** [Potential energy](potential-energy.md)

This [potential energy](potential-energy.md) generates a repulsive [central force](central-force.md) $F_r=-V'(r)=2c/r^3$. It is an inverse-square potential but an inverse-cube force; the powers must not be confused with the Coulomb or gravitational potential proportional to $1/r$.
