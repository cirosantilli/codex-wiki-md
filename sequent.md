# Sequent

↑ **Parent:** [Logical derivation](logical-derivation.md)

A [sequent](sequent.md) $\Gamma\vdash A$ records that the formula $A$ is derivable with undischarged assumptions drawn from $\Gamma$. In [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md), a [temporary assumption](temporary-assumption.md) contributes to $\Gamma$ only until an appropriate discharge rule closes its scope.
