# Macroautophagy

↑ **Parent:** [Autophagy](autophagy.md)

In [macroautophagy](macroautophagy.md), a portion of [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md), potentially including an [organelle](organelle.md), is enclosed in an [autophagosome](autophagosome.md) before delivery to a [lysosome](lysosome.md). Sequestration precedes digestion.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; Multiple Pathways Deliver Materials to Lysosomes, autophagy paragraph and Figure 13-35](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/transport-from-the-trans-golgi-network-to-lysosomes.md)
