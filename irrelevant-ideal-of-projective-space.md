# Irrelevant ideal of projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Projective Nullstellensatz](projective-nullstellensatz.md)

In the standard graded [homogeneous coordinate ring](homogeneous-coordinate-ring.md) $S=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ of $\mathbb P^n$, the irrelevant ideal is

$$
S_+=(x_0,\ldots,x_n).
$$

It vanishes only at the origin of the corresponding [affine cone](affine-cone.md), which does not represent a [projective point](projective-point.md).

It is the polynomial-ring instance of the [irrelevant ideal of a graded ring](irrelevant-ideal-of-a-graded-ring.md).
