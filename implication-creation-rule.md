# Implication creation rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

From a [logical derivation](logical-derivation.md) of $\psi$, infer $\phi\to\psi$ under the same surrounding assumptions. To derive the rule, temporarily assume $\phi$, retain the available $\psi$, and discharge $\phi$ by the [implication introduction rule](implication-introduction-rule.md). Its being unused is allowed.
