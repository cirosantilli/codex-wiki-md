# Dynamical upper bound on black-hole fuelling

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole accretion](black-hole-accretion.md)

For a [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md) of dispersion $\sigma$ and gas fraction $f_g$, placing all gas inside radius $R$ onto a hole in time $R/\sigma$ gives $\dot M_{\rm dyn}\sim2f_g\sigma^3/G$. This is an optimistic supply bound that omits [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) transport, [star formation](star-formation.md), and [active-galactic-nucleus feedback](active-galactic-nucleus-feedback.md). A numerical comparison with the [Eddington accretion rate](eddington-accretion-rate.md) requires a gas fraction, reference radiative efficiency, opacity, and crossing-time convention.
