# Every principal ideal domain is a unique factorization domain

↑ **Parent:** [Principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md)

The [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md) supplies existence of factorizations into [irreducible elements](irreducible-element.md). The [Bezout identity](bezout-identity.md) makes every irreducible element prime, which supplies uniqueness up to order and multiplication by units.
