# Telescoping bound for products of operators

↑ **Parent:** [Operator norm](operator-norm.md)

If $A_j$ and $B_j$ have [operator norm](operator-norm.md) at most one, then inserting and subtracting one factor at a time gives

$$
\left\|\prod_{j=1}^mA_j-\prod_{j=1}^mB_j\right\|
\leq\sum_{j=1}^m\|A_j-B_j\|.
$$
