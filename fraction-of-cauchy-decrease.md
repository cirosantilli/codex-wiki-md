# Fraction of Cauchy decrease

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy decrease bound](cauchy-decrease-bound.md)

A feasible trial step $s_k$ achieves a fixed [fraction of Cauchy decrease](fraction-of-cauchy-decrease.md) if $m_k(0)-m_k(s_k)\geq\kappa[m_k(0)-m_k(s_{C,k})]$ for one constant $\kappa\in(0,1]$ independent of $k$. The [Cauchy decrease bound](cauchy-decrease-bound.md) then supplies a quantitative model decrease without solving the [trust-region subproblem](trust-region-subproblem.md) exactly. Both the fraction and the relevant model regularity must be controlled in a convergence argument.
