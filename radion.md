# Radion

↑ **Parent:** [Compactification (physics)](compactification-physics.md)

A radion is a lower-dimensional [scalar field](scalar-field.md) describing the size or separation modulus of compact extra dimensions. In a two-brane [Randall–Sundrum model](randall-sundrum-model.md), its expectation determines the inter-brane separation and the warp-generated hierarchy. A background size alone does not supply a radion potential or a stabilization mechanism.
