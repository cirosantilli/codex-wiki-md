# Faraday constant

↑ **Parent:** [Amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faraday_constant)

The [Faraday constant](faraday-constant.md) is the [electric charge](electric-charge.md) associated with one [mole](mole-unit.md) of [elementary charges](elementary-charge.md), $F=N_{\mathrm A}e\approx96485.33212\,\mathrm{C\,mol^{-1}}$. The product of the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) and [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md) is exact under the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) definitions; the displayed decimal is rounded. For [ions](ion.md) with signed charge number $z$, the molar electrical contribution to an [electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md) is $zF\psi$, where $\psi$ is [electric potential](electric-potential.md). [NIST, CODATA 2022: Faraday constant and defining constants](https://www.physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/wall_2022.pdf).
