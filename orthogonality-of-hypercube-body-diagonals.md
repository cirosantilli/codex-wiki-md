# Orthogonality of hypercube body diagonals

↑ **Parent:** [Hypercube](hypercube.md)

Two sign-vector vertices of a centered [hypercube](hypercube.md) differing in $h$ coordinates have [dot product](dot-product.md) $n-2h$. Thus their body diagonals can be [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) only in even dimension. A collection of mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) nonzero diagonals has at most $n$ members; achieving $n$ is equivalent to the existence of a [Hadamard matrix](hadamard-matrix.md) of order $n$. For unoriented lines the angle is determined by $|n-2h|/n$, while oriented vertex vectors retain the sign of their [dot product](dot-product.md).
