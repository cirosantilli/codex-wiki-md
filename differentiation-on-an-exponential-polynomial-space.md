# Differentiation on an exponential-polynomial space

↑ **Parent:** [Differential operator](differential-operator.md)

For distinct real or complex numbers $\lambda_j$ and positive integers $m_j$, the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) spanned by $t^ke^{\lambda_jt}$, $0\leq k<m_j$, is preserved by [differentiation](differentiation.md). In each ordered [basis](basis.md) $(e^{\lambda_jt},te^{\lambda_jt},\ldots)$, its [matrix](matrix.md) has $\lambda_j$ on the diagonal and $1,2,\ldots,m_j-1$ just above it. This follows from the [product rule](product-rule.md). Rescaling to $t^ke^{\lambda_jt}/k!$ makes each block a [Jordan block](jordan-block.md). In particular, [differentiation](differentiation.md) is invertible on this [vector space](vector-space-split.md) exactly when every $\lambda_j\ne0$: the [determinant](determinant.md) is $\prod_j\lambda_j^{m_j}$.
