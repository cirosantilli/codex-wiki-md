# Nitrogen dissociation threshold from fragment images

↑ **Parent:** [Photofragment-ion imaging](photofragment-ion-imaging.md)

A [molecular nitrogen](molecular-nitrogen.md) experiment used a $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ pulsed nozzle and two skimmers. A $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ tube raised the rotational [temperature](temperature.md). Opposed vacuum-ultraviolet [laser](laser.md) pulses excited selected molecular levels and, about $10\,\mathrm{ns}$ later, selectively ionized excited [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) fragments. A [microchannel plate detector](microchannel-plate-detector.md), phosphor screen and camera recorded radii.[Lu, Jiang and Gao, §2, printed manuscript pp. 2–3: nozzle, tube, pump/probe and imaging readout](accurate-measurements-of-the-bond-dissociation-energies-of-14n2-14n15n-and-15n2-by-liya-lu-and-others.md)

The measured squared radii were fitted against molecular excitation [energies](energy.md), referenced to the parent [ground state](ground-state.md):

$$
E_j=D_{0,\rm channel}+\kappa R_j^2.
$$

The intercept at zero radius gave the excited-product threshold; the fitted slope absorbed the detector scale. A wavemeter monitored the excitation source; scanning the probe covered the recoil [Doppler shift](doppler-effect.md). Subtracting the atomic excitation [energies](energy.md) converted the intercept to ground-product $D_0$.[§1: image-radius equation; §2: wavemeter and probe scan; §3 and Figure 4: fits; §4: atomic excitation correction](accurate-measurements-of-the-bond-dissociation-energies-of-14n2-14n15n-and-15n2-by-liya-lu-and-others.md)

An apertured grounded plate and detector timing rejected faster competing channels. Selecting fragment electronic states distinguished nearby thresholds. Agreement of $D_e$ across [isotopes](isotope.md) after [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) correction tested consistency. The method still requires correctly assigned levels, adequate near-threshold signal and stable imaging; a signal drop alone can reflect another channel or a barrier.[§2: shielding plate and timing; §3: state-selected channels; §4 and Table 2: isotope consistency and limitations](accurate-measurements-of-the-bond-dissociation-energies-of-14n2-14n15n-and-15n2-by-liya-lu-and-others.md)

This measurement determines a state-resolved [ground-state dissociation energy](ground-state-dissociation-energy.md). Obtaining a finite-[temperature](temperature.md) [bond-dissociation enthalpy](bond-dissociation-enthalpy.md) requires the thermal increments above; obtaining a [bond potential-well depth](bond-potential-well-depth.md) requires the [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) correction. The three quantities describe different starting conditions for the same specified separation.
