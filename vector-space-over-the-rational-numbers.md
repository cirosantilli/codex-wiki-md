# Vector space over the rational numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)

A [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with scalar [field](field.md) $\mathbb Q$ is equivalently a [torsion-free divisible Abelian group](torsion-free-divisible-abelian-group.md). An embedding of such groups automatically respects rational scalar multiplication because solutions to $nb=ma$ are unique.
