# Square-pyramidal molecular geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md)

[Square-pyramidal molecular geometry](square-pyramidal-molecular-geometry.md) has four basal donor positions in a square and one axial donor. Removing one axial ligand from an [octahedral molecular geometry](octahedral-molecular-geometry.md) lowers orbitals with $z$ character. A common $d$-level order is $d_{x^2-y^2}>d_{z^2}>d_{xy}>d_{xz}=d_{yz}$, although the middle pair depends on axial strength and distances.
