# Orbit map

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action.md)

For a group $G$ acting on a set $X$ and a point $x\in X$, the orbit map is $G\to X$, $g\mapsto gx$. Its image is the [orbit of a group action](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) of $x$, and its fibres are cosets of the [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $x$.
