# Highest-weight complex-coordinate orbital wavefunction

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md)

For a differentiable radial factor and integer $n\geq0$, the coordinate polynomial obeys $L_3\psi=n\hbar\psi$ and $L_+\psi=0$. The identity $L^2=L_-L_++L_3^2+\hbar L_3$ gives the displayed angular [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). Lowering gives $L_-\psi=-2n\hbar z(x+iy)^{n-1}f(r)$, a nonzero [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) with the same angular [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) when $n>0$ and the original state is nonzero. At $n=0$ lowering gives the zero function, which is not an [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md). Negative powers satisfy local differential identities off the axis but are not regular angular states.
