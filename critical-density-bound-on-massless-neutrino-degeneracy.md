# Critical-density bound on massless neutrino degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino degeneracy parameter](neutrino-degeneracy-parameter.md)

Combining the [degenerate neutrino and antineutrino energy density](degenerate-neutrino-and-antineutrino-energy-density.md) with a [critical density](critical-density.md) bound constrains the [neutrino degeneracy parameter](neutrino-degeneracy-parameter.md) through the fourth root of the available energy budget. The numerical bound depends on $T_{\nu,0}/T_{\gamma,0}$ and the number of degenerate flavors. The standard ratio $(4/11)^{1/3}$ assumes [neutrino decoupling](neutrino-decoupling.md) before [electron-positron annihilation in cosmology](electron-positron-annihilation-in-cosmology.md); it should be stated as a thermal-history assumption if the degeneracy is very large. The massless formula is inappropriate for a species that is nonrelativistic today.
