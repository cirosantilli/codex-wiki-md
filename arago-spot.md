# Arago spot

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arago_spot)

The [Arago spot](arago-spot.md) is the bright axial point in the shadow of a circular obstruction under suitably coherent illumination. In ideal [scalar diffraction theory](scalar-diffraction-theory.md), secondary fields from the circular edge arrive in phase on axis. [Fresnel diffraction by an opaque disk](fresnel-diffraction-by-an-opaque-disk.md) gives an axial intensity equal to the unobstructed intensity in its paraxial model; this is not a claim that the whole geometric shadow is bright.
