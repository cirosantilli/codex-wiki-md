# Sorting record

↑ **Parent:** [Sort key](sort-key.md)

A complete entry comprising a [sort key](sort-key.md) together with any associated data. In $(2,A)$, the [sort key](sort-key.md) is $2$ and $A$ stands for the associated data or an identity label. [Sorting permutation preservation](sorting-permutation-preservation.md) concerns these complete entries, rather than only their extracted [sort keys](sort-key.md).
