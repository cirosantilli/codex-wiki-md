# Silanone

↑ **Parent:** [Organosilicon compound](organosilicon-compound.md)

A compound containing a [silicon](silicon.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md) [double bond](double-bond.md), $\mathrm{R_2Si=O}$. Ordinary small-substituent [silanones](silanone.md) are highly reactive; oligomerization to [siloxane](siloxane.md) linkages is favoured over persistence as freely dissolved monomers.
