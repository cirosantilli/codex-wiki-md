# Braided monoidal category

↑ **Parent:** [Monoidal category](monoidal-category.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braided_monoidal_category)

A [monoidal category](monoidal-category.md) with natural invertible [braidings](braiding.md) $c_{X,Y}:X\otimes Y\to Y\otimes X$ satisfying the two hexagon axioms. The equation $c_{Y,X}c_{X,Y}=1$ is an additional symmetric condition, not a braided axiom.
