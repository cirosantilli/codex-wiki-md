# Weinberg operator

↑ **Parent:** [Seesaw mechanism](seesaw-mechanism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weinberg_operator)

The Weinberg operator is the dimension-five Standard Model operator $(LH)(LH)/\Lambda$. After the [Higgs field](higgs-field.md) condenses, it gives a left-handed neutrino Majorana mass of order $v^2/\Lambda$.
