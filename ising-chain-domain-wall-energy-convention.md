# Ising-chain domain-wall energy convention

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)

For $J>0$ and $S_i^z=\pm1/2$, a parallel bond has energy $-J/2$ while an antiparallel bond has $+J/2$. A wall therefore costs J, rather than the $2J$ of a differently normalized $-J\sum s_is_{i+1}$ model. At zero field its density is $[1-\tanh(\beta J/2)]/2$, positive at every nonzero [temperature](temperature-split.md). Walls occur in pairs on a periodic ring, but that global parity restriction does not remove their nonzero density in the [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md). The low-temperature [correlation length](correlation-length.md) is approximately $ae^{\beta J}/2$.
