# Canonical genus-four curve as a quadric-cubic intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical map](canonical-map.md)

For a nonhyperelliptic [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) of [geometric genus](geometric-genus.md) four, the [canonical map](canonical-map.md) is an embedding of degree six. Dimension counts $10>h^0(2K)=9$ and $20>h^0(3K)=15$ supply a quadric and a cubic not divisible by it. The quadric is irreducible because the curve is nondegenerate. Degree six and [unmixedness of a complete intersection](unmixedness-of-a-complete-intersection.md) then identify the intersection with the curve as a scheme.
