# Group ring of a weight lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Weight lattice](weight-lattice.md)

The [group ring](group-ring.md) of a [weight lattice](weight-lattice.md) $P$ consists of finite sums $\sum_\lambda a_\lambda e(\lambda)$ with integer coefficients, where $e(\lambda)e(\mu)=e(\lambda+\mu)$. A finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) has [formal character](formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md) $\sum_\lambda(\dim V_\lambda)e(\lambda)$ in this ring. The [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) acts by $e(\lambda)\mapsto e(w\lambda)$. The ambient ring need not consist of genuine [characters](character-of-a-representation.md); the [representation ring of a semisimple Lie algebra](representation-ring-of-a-semisimple-lie-algebra.md) of finite-dimensional modules for a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) identifies with the invariant subring $\mathbb Z[P]^W$.
