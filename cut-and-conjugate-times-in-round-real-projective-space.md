# Cut and conjugate times in round real projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian cut point](riemannian-cut-point.md)

In unit-curvature [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) of dimension at least two, [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md) is $d([v],[u])=\arccos|\langle v,u\rangle|$ for unit lifts. A unit-speed [geodesic](geodesic.md) has lift $\cos t\,v+\sin t\,w$, with $w$ unit and perpendicular to $v$, and therefore ceases to minimize after $\pi/2$. Its normal [Jacobi fields](jacobi-field.md) with zero initial value are $\sin t$ times [parallel vector fields along a curve](parallel-vector-field-along-a-curve.md), giving first [conjugate point](conjugate-point.md) at time $\pi$. The two times differ in every direction.
