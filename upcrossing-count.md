# Upcrossing count

↑ **Parent:** [Upcrossing](upcrossing.md)

The [upcrossing count](upcrossing-count.md) $U_N[a,b]$ counts completed [upcrossings](upcrossing.md) by time $N$, allowing the first purchase at time zero. For a [martingale](martingale-split.md), the [Doob upcrossing inequality](doob-upcrossing-inequality.md) bounds its [expected value](expected-value.md) by $\mathbb E(M_N-a)^-/(b-a)$.
