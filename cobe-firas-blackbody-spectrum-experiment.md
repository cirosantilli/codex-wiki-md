# COBE FIRAS blackbody spectrum experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Far Infrared Absolute Spectrophotometer](far-infrared-absolute-spectrophotometer.md)

[FIRAS](far-infrared-absolute-spectrophotometer.md) compared sky radiation with an adjustable internal [blackbody](blackbody.md) using an [interferometer](interferometer.md) and cold [bolometers](bolometer.md). An external [blackbody](blackbody.md) could cover the sky entrance for [measurement calibration](measurement-calibration.md). The team adjusted the internal [temperature](temperature-split.md) to minimize the difference signal.[§III.D, pp.1337–1338; §V.A, p.1341](nobel-lecture-from-the-big-bang-to-the-nobel-prize-and-beyond-by-john-c-mather.md)

Comparisons across detectors and mirror strokes tested instrumental errors; sky mapping separated [Milky Way](milky-way.md) emission. [John C. Mather](john-c-mather.md)'s lecture reports a final root-mean-square spectral departure of 50 parts per million of peak brightness. This is neither the preliminary 1990 precision nor a relative error in every spectral bin. Calibration drift and nonideal reference emission required correction.[§V.A, p.1342: calibration checks and final spectrum](nobel-lecture-from-the-big-bang-to-the-nobel-prize-and-beyond-by-john-c-mather.md)

Functional comparison, not an optical layout. Calculated curves use stipulated [temperatures](temperature-split.md), not measured COBE data.

The advantage of a [null measurement](null-measurement.md) is that a small difference can be resolved without assigning the same absolute precision to a large detector output. Nevertheless, matching an internal reference is meaningful only after its relation to an independently calibrated [blackbody](blackbody.md) has been tested. A bound on departures from a [Planck spectrum](blackbody-spectrum.md) constrains energy redistribution after [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md); it does not require the [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) to have exactly the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) in every direction.
