# One-sided exponential approximate identity

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate identity](approximate-identity.md)

If $L>0$ and $f$ is bounded and continuous at zero, the positive kernel $ne^{-nx}$ has mass $1-e^{-nL}$ on $[0,L]$, tending to one, and mass at most $e^{-n\delta}$ outside $[0,\delta]$. Split the integral of $f(x)-f(0)$ at $\delta$: [continuity](continuous-function.md) makes the near part arbitrarily small, and boundedness times the exponentially small tail controls the far part. This proves the displayed endpoint [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md). A shrinking cutoff $L_n=n^{-1/2}$ still captures mass $1-e^{-\sqrt n}$ and therefore gives the same limit.
