# Neuronal paired-pulse recovery

↑ **Parent:** [Neuronal refractory period](neuronal-refractory-period.md)

[Neuronal paired-pulse recovery](neuronal-paired-pulse-recovery.md) probes the effect of an earlier [action potential](action-potential.md) with a later test stimulus. First establish a conditioning [action potential](action-potential.md); then vary the delay $\Delta$ to a test pulse of specified amplitude, duration and delivery site. Record whether a second [action potential](action-potential.md) occurs, its timing and, where relevant, its waveform. Repeating the test across amplitudes distinguishes recovery of responsiveness to one pulse from recovery of the minimum effective pulse. Direct [current clamp](current-clamp.md) stimulation can probe the receiving [neuron](neuron.md)'s excitability; stimulation through a [synapse](synapse.md) additionally allows [synaptic plasticity](synaptic-plasticity.md) to alter the input.

A documented whole-nerve teaching experiment keeps a bundle of [axons](axon.md) in physiological solution, with its proximal end drawn into a stimulating tube and its distal end into a recording tube. The suction [electrodes](electrode.md) use short plastic tubes and $1\,\mathrm{ml}$ syringe-plunger terminals, held by O-rings. Longitudinal [electric currents](electric-current.md) create a voltage drop in the thin solution layer between bundle and tube, read by a cathode-ray oscilloscope. Electrode fit affects amplitude. An immediate stimulation artifact remains even without the nerve, whereas the neural signal arrives after a delay.[The Nerve Impulse Seen from Outside, Topic 14, Figure 14 and suction-electrode construction](the-nerve-impulse-seen-from-outside-by-dexter-m-easton/topic-14-stimulating-and-recording-arrangements.md)[Topic 15, Figure 15: bath conduction, stimulus artifact and delayed neural response](the-nerve-impulse-seen-from-outside-by-dexter-m-easton/topic-15-stimulating-arrangements-the-stimulus-artifact.md)

The paired-stimulus exercise sets $0.1\,\mathrm{ms}$ pulses to obtain a maximal undistorted A wave of the [compound action potential](compound-action-potential.md), then increases the spacing. The described first response remains constant while the second grows with spacing. Different [axons](axon.md) recover at different times; the summed response therefore grows as more contribute. A smaller second [compound action potential](compound-action-potential.md) does not mean every individual [action potential](action-potential.md) has shrunk. The source contrasts this with a single [axon](axon.md)'s response or failure. Its response-amplitude recovery criterion is not a universal single-cell threshold criterion.[Topic 26, Conditioning-testing, stimulus duration and Figure 26: constant first response and recovery of the second response](the-nerve-impulse-seen-from-outside-by-dexter-m-easton/topic-26-conditioning-testing.md)

Useful additional controls would include single-pulse baselines interleaved with paired trials, a verified conditioning [action potential](action-potential.md), enough rest between trials, and fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), electrode geometry, pulse width and initial [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md). Confirm stimulus delivery and amplifier recovery, separate the stimulus artifact from a second neural event, and check whether waveforms overlap before measuring amplitudes. These are proposed checks, not additional results claimed for that teaching experiment. Even a clean [compound action potential](compound-action-potential.md) does not uniquely identify [sodium](sodium.md) availability: recruitment, propagation and synchronization of different [axons](axon.md) influence the summed signal. Nor does failure at a distant recording site alone distinguish failed initiation from failed propagation.
