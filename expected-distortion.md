# Expected distortion

↑ **Parent:** [Distortion measure (information theory)](distortion-measure-information-theory.md)

[Expected distortion](expected-distortion.md) is the [expectation](expected-value.md) of the reconstruction cost under the joint [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) of $X$ and $\widehat X$. It weights common source outcomes more strongly than rare ones. A bound on [expected distortion](expected-distortion.md) is therefore different from a bound on the cost for every input, or on the [probability](probability.md) of exceeding a specified cost.
