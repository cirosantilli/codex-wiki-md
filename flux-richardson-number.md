# Flux Richardson number

↑ **Parent:** [Richardson number](richardson-number.md)

The ratio of stable buoyancy destruction to shear production of turbulent kinetic energy. With down-gradient transport and [turbulent Prandtl number](turbulent-prandtl-number.md) $\mathrm{Pr}_t$, it obeys $\mathrm{Ri}_f=\mathrm{Ri}_g/\mathrm{Pr}_t$, rather than automatically equaling the [gradient Richardson number](gradient-richardson-number.md). Unstable buoyancy production gives a negative value in this convention.
