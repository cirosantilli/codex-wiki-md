# Scalar-QED photon-tadpole symmetry factor

↑ **Parent:** [Seagull vertex](seagull-vertex.md)

The two identical [photon](photon.md) fields at a [scalar electrodynamics](scalar-electrodynamics.md) [seagull](seagull-vertex.md) give a [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) multiplicity two. Joining them into one closed [photon](photon.md) line gives [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) $1/2$. Directly expanding $-e^2A^2\phi^\dagger\phi$ gives only one [photon](photon.md) [Wick contraction](wick-contraction.md), independently checking the compensation. In a transverse gauge the remaining [tensor](tensor.md) [trace](matrix-trace.md) is $d-1$; a massless [photon tadpole](tadpole-diagram.md) is a [scaleless integral](scaleless-integral.md) in [dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md).
