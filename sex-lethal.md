# Sex-lethal

↑ **Parent:** [RNA-binding protein](rna-binding-protein.md)

A Drosophila regulator whose [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) recognition supports sex-specific and autoregulatory splicing. It can act through competition at early recognition or through substrate-specific later spliceosomal interactions.
