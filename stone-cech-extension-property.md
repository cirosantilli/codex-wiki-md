<h1 id="stone-cech-extension-property">Stone-Čech extension property</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md)

Every [continuous map](continuous-map.md) from a space to a [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md) extends continuously to its [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md). Uniqueness follows from density of the original space and the [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) property of the target. Two [Hausdorff compactifications](hausdorff-compactification.md) with this property have comparison maps both ways; their composites fix the [dense](dense-set.md) original space and therefore are identity maps, proving equivalence of the compactifications.
