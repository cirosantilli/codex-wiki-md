# Lotka-Volterra food-chain parity

↑ **Parent:** [Food chain](food-chain.md)

For an unregulated exponential basal resource and nearest-neighbor [Lotka-Volterra equations](lotka-volterra-equations.md), the weighted interaction matrix is [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md). An even chain has an invertible tridiagonal interaction matrix; its positive [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), when feasible, fixes all long-term [means](expected-value.md). An odd chain has a null direction and requires a special balance between resource growth and mortality to permit bounded full coexistence. For a three-level chain, $X_1^{b_2}X_3^{a_1}=C\exp[(b_2r-a_1d_3)t]$. A positive even-chain [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) gives a conserved coercive relative-entropy-type function, proving every species remains bounded above and away from zero.
