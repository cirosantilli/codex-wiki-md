# Cross edge in depth-first search

↑ **Parent:** [DFS edge classification](dfs-edge-classification.md)

A [directed edge](directed-edge.md) whose endpoints have no ancestor-descendant relationship in the [depth-first search](depth-first-search.md) forest. Their discovery-to-finishing intervals are disjoint by the [DFS parenthesis property](depth-first-search-parenthesis-property.md). Such an edge can connect different completed branches or different search trees.
