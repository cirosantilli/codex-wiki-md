# Variational group velocity of a Taylor-Goldstein mode

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor–Goldstein equation](taylor-goldstein-equation.md)

For a regular real mode between rigid boundaries, multiply the [Taylor–Goldstein equation](taylor-goldstein-equation.md) by its vertical structure and integrate to get the stationary quotient $k^2=\int(\ell^2\phi^2-\phi_z^2)dz/\int\phi^2dz$. Along a differentiable dispersion branch, stationarity removes the variation of the [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md), giving $2k=(\partial I/\partial c)dc/dk$. Since $\partial_c\ell^2=N^2/(U-c)^3+\ell^2/(U-c)$, the displayed [group velocity](group-velocity.md) follows from $d(kc)/dk$. Critical levels and vanishing branch derivatives require a separate limiting analysis.
