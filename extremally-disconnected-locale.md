# Extremally disconnected locale

↑ **Parent:** [Locale](locale.md)

A [locale](locale.md) is extremally disconnected when the closure of every open is open. Equivalently its [regular open elements](regular-element-of-a-heyting-algebra.md) are complemented, or double negation preserves binary joins, or its [Booleanization of a locale](booleanization-of-a-locale.md) is a [flat sublocale](flat-sublocale.md).
