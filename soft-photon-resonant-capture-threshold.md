# Soft-photon resonant capture threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md)

In [energy](energy-split.md) units $m=m_{\rm in}c^2$ and $M=M_Ac^2$, capture of a [photon](photon.md) of [energy](energy-split.md) $e$ by a massive particle into one [resonance](resonance.md) satisfies $E-P\cos\theta=(M^2-m^2)/(2e)=2E_0$, with $P=pc$. Head-on incidence gives the largest [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) at fixed $E$, so the threshold solves $E+P=2E_0$. Using $E^2-P^2=m^2$ gives the title expression. It is the minimum over incident angle; above that [energy](energy-split.md) the exact angle is fixed by the invariant equation, not arbitrary. The soft-photon regime $2E_0\gg m$ excludes exactly co-propagating incidence.
