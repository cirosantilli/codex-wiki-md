# Astigmatism of an equal-strength quadrupole doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Beam optics](beam-optics.md)

For an initially parallel bundle passing through opposite equal thin-quadrupole strengths separated by $L$, the two downstream focal distances differ as shown. Approximating the doublet by a common effective [focal length](focal-length.md) $f^2/L$ discards that difference. A real common final focus requires additional incoming optical information or adjusted strengths.
