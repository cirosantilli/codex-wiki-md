# Discriminant validity

↑ **Parent:** [Construct validity](construct-validity.md)

Evidence that measures distinguish the intended construct from relevant competing constructs. It requires substantive expectations about relationships, not a universal rule that every [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) with another construct must be zero.
