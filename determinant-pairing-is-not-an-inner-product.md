# Determinant pairing is not an inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Inner product](inner-product-split.md)

On real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) of size $n\ge2$, the displayed pairing is symmetric because $\det(AC)=\det A\det C$. It is not [bilinear](bilinear-map.md): $B(I,2I)=2^n$, whereas linearity would require $2B(I,I)=2$. Its self-pairing is always nonnegative, but a nonzero [singular matrix](singular-matrix.md) has $B(A,A)=0$, so [positive definiteness](positive-definiteness.md) fails as well. For $n=1$ it reduces to the ordinary real [inner product](inner-product-split.md). The [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md) $\operatorname{tr}(A^TC)$ supplies the appropriate bilinear, positive-definite geometry in every size.
