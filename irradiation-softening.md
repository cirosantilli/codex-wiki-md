# Irradiation softening

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation damage in materials](radiation-damage-in-materials.md)

A reduction in resistance to plastic deformation associated with [irradiation](irradiation.md). Removal of pre-existing strengthening obstacles can exceed new [irradiation hardening](irradiation-hardening.md), for example when an initially hardened [microstructure](microstructure.md) undergoes recovery or precipitate dissolution. Compare an appropriate thermal control before attributing a measured change to radiation.
