# Regular part of the planar Dirichlet Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

For the canonical [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md) with logarithmic singularity, its harmonic correction satisfies $G_z(z)=-\log\operatorname{crad}_D(z)$. If $\phi$ maps the unit disc to $D$ with $\phi(0)=z$, then

$$
G_z(\phi(w))=-\log\left|\frac{\phi(w)-\phi(0)}w\right|.
$$

The quotient extends at zero to $\phi'(0)$. On unbounded domains the canonical Green function, rather than boundary values alone, fixes the harmonic correction.
