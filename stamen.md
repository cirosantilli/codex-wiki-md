# Stamen

↑ **Parent:** [Flower](flower.md)

A [stamen](stamen.md) is a [flower](flower.md)'s male reproductive organ, typically an [anther](anther.md) supported by a [stamen filament](stamen-filament.md). The [anther](anther.md) produces and releases [pollen](pollen.md).[Biology 2e, §32.1 Flower Structure and Male Gametophyte (The Pollen Grain), Figures 32.3 and 32.6](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)
