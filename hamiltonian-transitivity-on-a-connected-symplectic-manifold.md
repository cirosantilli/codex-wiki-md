# Hamiltonian transitivity on a connected symplectic manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md)

Compactly supported [Hamiltonian vector fields](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) can attain any prescribed tangent vector at a point of a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md): prescribe the derivative of a local Hamiltonian and multiply by a [bump function](bump-function.md). Composing flows for a tangent [basis](basis.md) gives an open orbit by the [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md). Every orbit is open, so on a [connected](connected-space.md) manifold the compactly supported Hamiltonian transformations act transitively.
