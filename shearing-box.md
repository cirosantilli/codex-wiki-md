# Shearing box

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md)

A [shearing box](shearing-box.md) solves the [shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md) equations in a finite domain with periodic azimuthal and vertical faces and a [shearing-periodic boundary condition](shearing-periodic-boundary-condition.md) on its radial faces. It follows a small region rotating at a reference orbital frequency. The model can sustain stress and exchange energy with its imposed differential rotation, but its identified radial faces do not represent a global disk mass sink.
