# Transient climate response

↑ **Parent:** [Climate sensitivity](climate-sensitivity.md)

Global mean surface warming near the time of carbon-dioxide doubling in an idealized experiment increasing concentration by one per cent per year. Ocean heat uptake generally keeps it below the equilibrium response.
