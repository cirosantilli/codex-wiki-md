# Bosonic squeezed-vacuum contractions

↑ **Parent:** [Two-mode squeezed vacuum](two-mode-squeezed-vacuum.md)

Let $b_k=\cosh\theta_k\,a_k+\sinh\theta_k\,a_{-k}^\dagger$ for real even $\theta_k$, and let every $b_k$ annihilate a normalized [vacuum state](vacuum-state.md). Inverting the [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) gives $a_k=\cosh\theta_k\,b_k-\sinh\theta_k\,b_{-k}^\dagger$. Only $\langle b_kb_q^\dagger\rangle=\delta_{kq}$ survives, proving the displayed [vacuum expectation values](vacuum-expectation-value.md). Taking the [Hermitian adjoint](adjoint-operator.md) gives the same real negative pair amplitude for two [creation operators](creation-operator.md). The normal [occupation number](occupation-number.md) is quadratic at small $\theta_k$, whereas the pair amplitude is linear. For one opposite-[momentum](momentum-split.md) pair the [two-mode squeezed vacuum](two-mode-squeezed-vacuum.md) is $\operatorname{sech}\theta\sum_{n\ge0}(-\tanh\theta)^n|n,n\rangle$, fixing the sign convention directly.
