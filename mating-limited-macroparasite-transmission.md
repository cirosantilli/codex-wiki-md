# Mating-limited macroparasite transmission

↑ **Parent:** [Macroparasite burden model](macroparasite-burden-model.md)

For sexually reproducing worms, an infected [host](host-biology.md) need not contain both sexes. Independent random sexes split a Poisson total burden of [mean](expected-value.md) $m$ into independent male and female [Poisson distributions](poisson-distribution.md) of [mean](expected-value.md) $m/2$, giving the displayed [probability](probability.md) of a fertile mixed-sex burden. For an arbitrary burden [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) $G$, this [probability](probability.md) is $1-2G(1/2)+G(0)$. Its low-burden behaviour can introduce an [Allee effect](allee-effect.md) in transmission, so an asexual mean-burden invasion calculation is not automatically valid.
