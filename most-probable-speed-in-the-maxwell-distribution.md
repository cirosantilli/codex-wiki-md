# Most probable speed in the Maxwell distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell speed distribution](maxwell-speed-distribution.md)

The most probable speed is the [mode](mode-statistics.md), or density maximum, of the [Maxwell speed distribution](maxwell-speed-distribution.md):

$$
v_{\rm mp}=\sqrt{\frac{2k_BT}{m}}=\sqrt2\,s.
$$

For small equal-width [speed](speed.md) bins, the bin near $v_{\rm mp}$ contains the largest expected fraction of particles. This does not mean that any exact [speed](speed.md) has positive [probability](probability.md), or that every particle has this [speed](speed.md). The three common summaries obey

$$
\frac{v_{\rm mp}}s=\sqrt2\approx1.414,
\qquad \frac{\langle v\rangle}s=\sqrt{\frac8\pi}\approx1.596,
\qquad \frac{v_{\rm rms}}s=\sqrt3\approx1.732,
$$

so $v_{\rm mp}<\langle v\rangle<v_{\rm rms}$. The first describes a density peak, the second the [mean](expected-value-split.md) [speed](speed.md), and the third the [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) associated with [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). [OpenStax, characteristic molecular speeds](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/2-4-distribution-of-molecular-speeds)

For a concrete scale $s=300\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, these are approximately $424$, $479$, and $520\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. The most probable [velocity](velocity.md) vector and each most probable component remain zero in the gas rest frame. A zero vector labels one small region of [velocity](velocity.md) space, whereas a positive [speed](speed.md) labels an entire [spherical shell](spherical-shell.md).

A short check uses the [derivative](derivative.md) of the [logarithm](logarithm.md) of the [Maxwell speed distribution](maxwell-speed-distribution.md) for $v>0$:

$$
\frac{d}{dv}\log g(v)=\frac2v-\frac{v}{s^2}.
$$

It is positive below $\sqrt2\,s$ and negative above it, locating the unique peak. The factor $v^2$ from the [spherical shell](spherical-shell.md) supplies the term $2/v$; omitting it would incorrectly move the [speed](speed.md) peak to zero.
