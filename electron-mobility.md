# Electron mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Charge carrier mobility](charge-carrier-mobility.md)

The positive [electron mobility](electron-mobility.md) relates the [drift velocity](drift-velocity.md) of conduction [electrons](electron.md) to an [electric field](electric-field-split.md): $\mathbf v_d=-\mu_e\mathbf E$. In the isotropic [Drude model](drude-model.md), $\mu_e=e\tau/m^*$, where $\tau$ is the [momentum relaxation time](momentum-relaxation-time.md) and $m^*$ the [band effective mass](band-effective-mass.md). The minus sign describes negative charge; the [electron mobility](electron-mobility.md) itself is positive.
