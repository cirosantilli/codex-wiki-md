# Characteristic-ideal bridge from local units to the main conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory](main-conjecture-of-iwasawa-theory.md)

In the real cyclotomic tower, the [class-field unit sequence](class-field-unit-sequence.md) gives $0\to E/C\to U/C\to X\to Y\to0$, where $X$ is the p-ramified [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md) and $Y$ the unramified one. The modules are torsion over each one-variable character component. Splitting the four-term sequence into two [short exact sequences](short-exact-sequence.md) proves the displayed identity by multiplicativity of [characteristic ideals](characteristic-ideal.md). The [Iwasawa theorem on local cyclotomic units](iwasawa-theorem-on-local-cyclotomic-units.md) computes $\operatorname{char}(U/C)$ analytically. Thus the main conjecture for $X$ is equivalent to $\operatorname{char}(E/C)=\operatorname{char}(Y)$; local interpolation alone does not prove this remaining global equality.
