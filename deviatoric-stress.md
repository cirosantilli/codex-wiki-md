# Deviatoric stress

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)

The deviatoric part of a three-dimensional [stress](stress.md) [tensor](tensor.md) is $\boldsymbol\sigma_{\rm dev}=\boldsymbol\sigma-\operatorname{tr}(\boldsymbol\sigma)\mathbf I/3$. It is traceless. Adding an isotropic pressure does not change a [normal-stress difference](normal-stress-difference.md).
