# Eigenfrequency

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

An eigenfrequency is the natural [frequency](frequency.md) associated with a free [normal mode](normal-mode.md). Always specify whether it means the [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$ or the [frequency](frequency.md) $f=\omega/(2\pi)$ in hertz. For a uniform fixed-end [wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md), $\omega_n=n\pi\sqrt{\tau/\mu}/L$ and $f_n=n\sqrt{\tau/\mu}/(2L)$; the [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) does not change either within the linear model. In a [generalized eigenvalue problem for small oscillations](generalized-eigenvalue-problem-for-small-oscillations.md), the [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $\omega^2$, rather than $\omega$ itself.

For a first-order system $\dot z=iAz$, a real [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$ of $A$ instead gives a signed eigenfrequency in $e^{i\lambda t}$. A positive imaginary part of $\lambda$ gives exponential decay with this sign convention. Thus a complex damped eigenfrequency encodes both [oscillation](oscillation.md) and decay; it is not just the number of cycles per second.
