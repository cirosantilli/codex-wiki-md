# Eigenfrequency

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

The temporal frequency of a [normal mode](normal-mode.md). In $\dot z=iAz$, a real [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of $A$ is a signed eigenfrequency; its positive imaginary part after damping gives exponential decay in $e^{i\lambda t}$.
