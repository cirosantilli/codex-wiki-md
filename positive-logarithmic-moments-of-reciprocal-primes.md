# Positive logarithmic moments of reciprocal primes

↑ **Parent:** [Mertens first theorem](mertens-first-theorem.md)

For fixed $\lambda>1$, integrate $(\log t)^{\lambda-1}$ against $A(t)=\sum_{p\le t}(\log p)/p$. The [Mertens first theorem](mertens-first-theorem.md) gives $A(t)=\log t+O(1)$. [Partial summation](abel-s-summation-formula.md) makes the main integral $(\log x)^\lambda/\lambda$; the bounded error in $A$ contributes at most a constant times $(\log x)^{\lambda-1}$. The restriction $\lambda>1$ ensures that integrating the derivative $(\lambda-1)(\log t)^{\lambda-2}/t$ has this bound.
