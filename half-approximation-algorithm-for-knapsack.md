# Half-approximation algorithm for knapsack

↑ **Parent:** [0-1 knapsack problem](0-1-knapsack-problem.md)

After removing overweight items and handling free items, compare the feasible density-sorted prefix with the best feasible singleton. The [fractional knapsack problem](fractional-knapsack-problem.md) optimum is at most the sum of their profits, so the better solution has [approximation ratio](approximation-ratio.md) $1/2$.
