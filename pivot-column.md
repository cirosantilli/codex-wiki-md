# Pivot column

↑ **Parent:** [Column rank](column-rank.md)

A [pivot column](pivot-column.md) of a [matrix](matrix.md) is a column whose position contains a leading nonzero [matrix entry](matrix-entry.md) in its [row echelon form](row-echelon-form.md). The corresponding columns of the original [matrix](matrix.md) form a [basis](basis.md) of its [column space](column-space.md). The column positions survive [row operations](elementary-row-operation.md), although the column [vectors](vector.md) themselves generally change.[Interactive Linear Algebra, §1.2.3, definitions of pivot position and pivot column](interactive-linear-algebra-by-dan-margalit-and-others/row-reduction.md)
