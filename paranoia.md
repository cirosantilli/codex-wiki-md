# Paranoia

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological assessment](psychological-assessment.md)

Suspicious or persecutory thinking, varying in severity and conviction. A dimensional [paranoia](paranoia.md) score in a nonclinical sample should not be equated with a [diagnosis](clinical-diagnosis.md) or with a persistent [persecutory delusion](persecutory-delusion.md).
