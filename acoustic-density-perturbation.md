# Acoustic density perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)

An acoustic density perturbation is the deviation of fluid [mass density](density.md) from its quiescent reference value. In a homogeneous isentropic linear acoustic field, its corresponding [pressure](pressure.md) perturbation is $p'=c_0^2\rho'$. Nonlinear and entropy-dependent departures from this relation enter the [Lighthill stress tensor](lighthill-stress-tensor.md).
