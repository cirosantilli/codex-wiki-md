# Hydrolytic deamination

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md)

[Hydrolytic deamination](hydrolytic-deamination.md) replaces an amino group using [water](water.md), releasing [ammonia](ammonia.md) or [ammonium](ammonium.md) according to pH. [Adenosine deaminase](adenosine-deaminase.md) introduces solvent [oxygen](oxygen.md) at the C6 position of [adenosine](adenosine.md).
