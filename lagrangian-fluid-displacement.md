# Lagrangian fluid displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement field (mechanics)](displacement-field-mechanics.md)

The Lagrangian fluid displacement $\boldsymbol\xi$ compares neighbouring fluid trajectories. Linear [velocity](velocity.md) [perturbations](perturbation.md) about a steady flow $\mathbf U$ obey $\delta\mathbf u=(\partial_t+\mathbf U\cdot\nabla)\boldsymbol\xi-(\boldsymbol\xi\cdot\nabla)\mathbf U$. For a background scalar $f_0$, its material [perturbation](perturbation.md) is $\Delta f=\delta f+\boldsymbol\xi\cdot\nabla f_0$. In an axisymmetric [normal mode](normal-mode.md) about cylindrical rotation, the radial and vertical [velocities](velocity.md) are $-i\omega\xi_r$ and $-i\omega\xi_z$. The displacement of a [free surface](free-surface.md) is needed to impose the [Lagrangian pressure perturbation](lagrangian-pressure-perturbation.md) condition.
