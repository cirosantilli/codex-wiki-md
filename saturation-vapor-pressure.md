# Saturation vapor pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md)

The equilibrium partial [pressure](pressure-split.md) of a vapour in contact with its condensed phase at a specified [temperature](temperature-split.md). With constant specific [latent heat](latent-heat.md) and negligible condensed-phase volume, $d\ln e_s/dT=L/(R_vT^2)$. The condensed phase and any metastability convention must be specified.
