# Collision-coalescence

↑ **Parent:** [Cloud droplet](cloud-droplet.md)

Growth of liquid [cloud droplets](cloud-droplet.md) by collisions followed by merging. Differences in droplet fall speeds and collection efficiency affect the process. Increasing droplet number at fixed liquid-water content can reduce typical sizes and delay drizzle, but [cloud](cloud.md) mixing and water-content adjustments prevent a universal precipitation response.
