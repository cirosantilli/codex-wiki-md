# Aronszajn tree

↑ **Parent:** [Set-theoretic tree](set-theoretic-tree.md)

A [set-theoretic tree](set-theoretic-tree.md) of height $\omega_1$ with countable levels and no [cofinal branch](cofinal-branch.md). More generally, a $\kappa$-Aronszajn tree has height a regular uncountable $\kappa$, levels of size less than $\kappa$, and no [cofinal branch](cofinal-branch.md).
