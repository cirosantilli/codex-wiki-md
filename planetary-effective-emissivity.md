# Planetary effective emissivity

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary equilibrium temperature](planetary-equilibrium-temperature.md)

The ratio of top-of-atmosphere outgoing thermal flux to the blackbody flux at a chosen representative surface temperature. In global equilibrium with a uniform surface, $\epsilon_{\rm eff}=(T_{\rm eq}/T_s)^4$. It parametrizes the relation between surface and radiating temperatures and need not equal the material [emissivity](emissivity.md) of the surface or an atmospheric layer. An effective value below one is compatible with a black surface when colder atmospheric layers contribute to radiation escaping to space.
