# F4 Dynkin diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md)

The finite [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) $F_4$ is a four-vertex chain whose central bond is double. The two consecutive long roots lie on one side, the two consecutive short roots on the other, and the arrow points toward the short pair. The remaining two bonds are single.
