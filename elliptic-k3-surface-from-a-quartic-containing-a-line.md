# Elliptic K3 surface from a quartic containing a line

↑ **Parent:** [K3 surface](k3-surface.md)

A smooth quartic surface $X\subseteq\mathbb P^3$ containing a line $L$ is a [K3 surface](k3-surface.md). The pencil of planes through $L$ cuts out $L$ plus a residual plane cubic; the divisor class $H-L$ has square zero and its basepoint-free pencil defines an [elliptic fibration](elliptic-surface.md) $X\to\mathbb P^1$.
