# Occurrence-position representation of a sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Data structure](data-structure-split.md)

Represent a [sequence](sequence.md) by a [function](function-split.md) mapping each value to its increasing [list](list-abstract-data-type.md) of occurrence positions. On finite inputs, querying a value produces every matching index and a terminating tail. On infinite inputs, absence of further occurrences may be uncomputable by a generic scan, as expressed by the [absence-detection obstruction for infinite lists](absence-detection-obstruction-for-infinite-lists.md). Parity filtering and index rescaling represent alternate-element subsequences, but may fail to preserve [productivity](stream-productivity.md).
