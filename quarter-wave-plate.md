# Quarter-wave plate

↑ **Parent:** [Waveplate](waveplate.md)

A [quarter-wave plate](quarter-wave-plate.md) gives a quarter-cycle [phase retardation](phase-retardation.md). Equal transmitted [wave amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) initially in [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) at $45^\circ$ become [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md); unequal [wave amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) generally give [elliptical polarization](elliptical-polarization.md). Reversing the sign reverses the traversal of the same circle.
