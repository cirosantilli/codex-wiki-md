# Capacity preservation under uniform limits

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic capacity](symplectic-capacity.md)

Suppose diffeomorphisms preserving capacities of [solid ellipsoids](solid-ellipsoid.md) converge uniformly on compact sets to a diffeomorphism $\phi$. For $0<a<1$, uniform closeness and [degree of a continuous mapping](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) give $\phi_j(aE)\subseteq\phi(E)\subseteq\phi_j(a^{-1}E)$. The capacity axioms squeeze $c(\phi(E))$ between $a^2c(E)$ and $a^{-2}c(E)$. Letting $a$ tend to one proves equality. Rescaling a smooth $\phi$ near a point and repeating the argument also proves capacity preservation by its derivative.
