# Strategy support

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md)

The support of a finite [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) consists of the [pure strategies](pure-strategy.md) assigned positive probability. At a [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md), every action in the support is a [best response](best-response.md). In a game satisfying [nondegeneracy of a bimatrix game](nondegeneracy-of-a-bimatrix-game.md), the two equilibrium supports have equal size.
