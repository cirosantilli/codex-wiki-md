# Pearson chi-squared statistic for contingency tables

↑ **Parent:** [Pearson chi-squared test of homogeneity](pearson-chi-squared-test-of-homogeneity.md)

For observed counts $O_{ij}$ and positive expected counts $E_{ij}$ in a [contingency table](contingency-table.md), the statistic is $X^2=\sum_{ij}(O_{ij}-E_{ij})^2/E_{ij}$. Under common category probabilities or statistical independence, estimate $E_{ij}$ by the product of the row and column totals divided by the grand total. With large expected counts, this gives the test statistic for the [Pearson chi-squared test of homogeneity](pearson-chi-squared-test-of-homogeneity.md) or the [Pearson chi-squared test of independence](pearson-chi-squared-test-of-independence.md), with asymptotic [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) on $(r-1)(c-1)$ [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md).
