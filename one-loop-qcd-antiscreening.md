# One-loop QCD antiscreening

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md)

The $+11$ contribution to $\beta_0$ is the net [Non-Abelian gauge theory](yang-mills-theory.md) contribution; the $-2n_f/3$ term comes from [quark](quark.md) loops. Thus [quarks](quark.md) screen [color charge](color-charge.md), opposing [asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md). It is the net [gluon](gluon.md) contribution that produces antiscreening: the inferred [QCD coupling](strong-coupling-constant.md) decreases toward shorter distances. Unlike the electrically neutral [photon](photon.md), a [gluon](gluon.md) carries [color charge](color-charge.md) and has self-interactions. Describing this as [vacuum polarization](vacuum-polarization.md) must not imply that all vacuum contributions have the screening sign of [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md).[2005 reprint, pp. 843–844, magnetic-response explanation; Eqs. 1–3](the-discovery-of-asymptotic-freedom-and-the-emergence-of-qcd-by-david-j-gross.md)

Individual gauge-dependent [Feynman diagrams](feynman-diagram.md) do not separately define observable screening clouds. A proof of the coefficient sums the [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md), [Faddeev-Popov ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) and matter contributions and extracts the [charge renormalization](charge-renormalization.md); the final one-[loop order](loop-order.md) coefficient is independent of the gauge choice. The [beta function](beta-function-physics.md) is negative near zero for integer $0\leq n_f\leq16$. This is a statement about [weak coupling](weak-coupling.md), not a determination of the low-[energy](energy-split.md) [particle](particle-physics.md) spectrum.[1973, pp. 1347–1348, Eqs. 3–7; warning below Eq. 7](reliable-perturbative-results-for-strong-interactions-by-hugh-david-politzer.md)
