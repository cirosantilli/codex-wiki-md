# Antisymmetrized covector derivative is tensorial

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md)

For a smooth [one-form](one-form.md) $A$, the components $\partial_aA_b-\partial_bA_a$ transform as a covariant rank-two [tensor](tensor.md). The non-tensorial second derivatives in the [ordinary covector derivative under nonlinear coordinate changes](ordinary-covector-derivative-under-nonlinear-coordinate-changes.md) are symmetric in $a,b$ and cancel. For a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md) the same components are $\nabla_aA_b-\nabla_bA_a$. This is the coordinate expression of $dA$, without choosing a metric.
