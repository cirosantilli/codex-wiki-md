# C2h molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The [molecular point group](molecular-point-group.md) containing identity, one twofold rotation, an [inversion centre](inversion-centre.md) and a mirror plane perpendicular to the twofold axis. Its four operations are $E,C_2,i,\sigma_h$. An extended anti conformation of [hexa-1,5-diyne](hexa-1-5-diyne.md) provides an example; changing the conformation can change the [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md).
