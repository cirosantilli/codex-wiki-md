# Phosphatidylethanolamine

↑ **Parent:** [Glycerophospholipid](glycerophospholipid.md)

[phosphatidylethanolamine](phosphatidylethanolamine.md) is a [glycerophospholipid](glycerophospholipid.md) with an ethanolamine headgroup. Its relatively small headgroup can favor negative monolayer [spontaneous curvature](spontaneous-curvature.md), depending on chain composition and conditions.
