# Dyck path

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice path](lattice-path.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyck_path)

A [Dyck path](dyck-path.md) starts at $(0,0)$, ends at $(2n,0)$, uses steps $U=(1,1)$ and $D=(1,-1)$, and never passes below the horizontal axis. Its [semilength of a Dyck path](semilength-of-a-dyck-path.md) is $n$: there are $n$ up steps and $n$ down steps. The nonnegative height records a constraint throughout the journey, not just the endpoint. The [Catalan number](catalan-number.md) $C_n$ counts the [Dyck paths](dyck-path.md) of this size.
