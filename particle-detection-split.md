# Particle detection

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)

[Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md) infers a [particle](particle-physics.md)'s passage from a physical change in matter. A [cloud chamber](cloud-chamber.md) makes the spatial pattern of [ionization](ionization.md) visible; an [ionization chamber](ionization-chamber.md) instead collects [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) from [ionization](ionization.md). Choosing the readout determines what can be inferred from a single event.

The [Richter-Ting independent J/psi discovery experiments](richter-ting-independent-j-psi-discovery-experiments.md) show how different readouts can establish the same [particle resonance](particle-resonance.md): reconstruct daughter [invariant masses](invariant-mass.md) after a beam hits a stationary target, or tune the initial [center-of-mass energy](center-of-mass-energy.md) of two colliding beams. Both require an ensemble of events and calibrated [particle detectors](particle-detector.md).
