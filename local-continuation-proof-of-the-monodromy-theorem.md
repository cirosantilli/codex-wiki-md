# Local continuation proof of the monodromy theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Monodromy theorem](monodromy-theorem.md)

Along one [path](continuous-path.md), [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) is a chain of [function elements](function-element.md) on overlapping [open discs](open-disc.md), each agreeing with the preceding [germ of a holomorphic function](germ-of-a-holomorphic-function.md) near the crossing point. The [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md) makes this chain unique along that fixed [path](continuous-path.md). Equality at a crossing means equality on a [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md), not just equality of a single value.

For the deformation step, fix one intermediate [path](continuous-path.md) $t\mapsto H(s_0,t)$ and its continuation. By [compactness](compact-space.md) of $[0,1]$, choose a finite chain of representative [open discs](open-disc.md) and subdivide the parameter so that each subarc fits in its assigned [open disc](open-disc.md), with consecutive junctions in overlap [neighbourhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) where the representatives agree. Joint [continuity](continuous-function.md) of $H$ ensures that every sufficiently nearby intermediate [path](continuous-path.md) uses that same chain and those same overlaps. The endpoints stay fixed, so the terminal [germ of a holomorphic function](germ-of-a-holomorphic-function.md) stays unchanged. Existence along every intermediate [path](continuous-path.md) supplies this argument at each $s_0$; local constancy and [connectedness](connected-space.md) of $[0,1]$ give equality at $s=0$ and $s=1$. This proves the [monodromy theorem](monodromy-theorem.md) without assuming that every [analytic germ projection](analytic-germ-projection.md) is a [covering map](covering-space.md).
