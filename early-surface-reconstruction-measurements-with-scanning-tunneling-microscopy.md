# Early surface-reconstruction measurements with scanning tunneling microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Scanning tunneling microscopy](scanning-tunneling-microscopy.md)

For [gold](gold.md) (110), the 1982 paper compared an untreated surface with one prepared by [argon](argon.md) [sputtering](sputtering.md) and [annealing](annealing-materials-science.md). Repeated cleaning reproduced the corrugation; the gap-modulation signal checked [work function](work-function.md) variation. This supported a structural interpretation while exposing the danger of confusing [work function](work-function.md) contrast with geometric relief.[p. 59, Au preparation, repeated cleaning and modulated signal](surface-studies-by-scanning-tunneling-microscopy-by-gerd-binnig-and-others.md)

The authored lecture describes resolving the $1\times2$ [surface reconstruction](surface-reconstruction.md) on [gold](gold.md) (110), alongside ribbons of other periodicities. It also reports [gold](gold.md) transfer to the tip on contact and fluctuating resolution, attributed tentatively to migrating tip [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). The probe could change during observation: an image was evidence requiring controls, not an unambiguous map of atomic positions.[pp. 398–399, Au(110) reconstruction, tip coating and changing resolution](scanning-tunneling-microscopy-from-birth-to-adolescence-by-gerd-binnig-and-others.md)
