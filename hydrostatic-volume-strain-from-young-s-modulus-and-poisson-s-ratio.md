<h1 id="hydrostatic-volume-strain-from-young-s-modulus-and-poisson-s-ratio">Hydrostatic volume strain from Young's modulus and Poisson's ratio</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md)

For an isotropic linearly elastic solid, equal compressive principal [stresses](stress.md) $-p$ produce three equal normal [strains](strain.md) $-(1-2\nu)p/E$. Adding them gives the displayed [volume](volume.md) [strain](strain.md), or [bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md) $K=E/[3(1-2\nu)]$. It is a small-strain relation using the [pressure](pressure-split.md) change, not an arbitrary absolute [pressure](pressure-split.md) reference.
