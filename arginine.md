# Arginine

↑ **Parent:** [Proteinogenic amino acid](proteinogenic-amino-acid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arginine)

A [proteinogenic amino acid](proteinogenic-amino-acid.md) represented by R or Arg in [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md). Its [amino acid side chain](amino-acid-side-chain.md) carries predominantly positive [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) near neutral [pH](ph.md).[Organic Chemistry by John McMurry; Table 26.1, lysine and arginine rows; side-chain protonation at pH 7.3](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/26-1-structures-of-amino-acids.md)

[Trypsin](trypsin.md) preferentially cleaves after its [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md).
