# Spatially forced convection amplitude equation

↑ **Parent:** [Landau amplitude equation](landau-amplitude-equation.md)

A steady spatial forcing at twice the critical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) permits coupling of the negative critical [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) to the positive critical mode. A [weakly nonlinear expansion](weakly-nonlinear-expansion.md) then permits a term $\beta\overline A$ in the [Landau amplitude equation](landau-amplitude-equation.md). Its coefficient is found by projecting the resonant forcing-advection terms onto the [adjoint eigenfunction](adjoint-eigenfunction.md). Reflection-symmetric forcing permits real coefficients. The [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) permission does not prove a nonzero coefficient: it can vanish for a particular model or mode structure.
