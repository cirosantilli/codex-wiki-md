# Internal-wave envelope radiation condition

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation condition](radiation-condition.md)

For a slowly switched-on [plane internal gravity wave](plane-internal-gravity-wave.md) in the half-space $z>0$, the [method of multiple scales](method-of-multiple-scales.md) gives transport of its amplitude at the vertical [group velocity](group-velocity.md). For $\omega^2=N^2k^2/(k^2+m^2)$, $c_{gz}=-\omega m/(k^2+m^2)$. Initially undisturbed fluid and forcing at the bottom require $c_{gz}>0$. The outgoing [radiation condition](radiation-condition.md) therefore selects $m<0$ when $\omega>0$, although the vertical [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) is downward.
