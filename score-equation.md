# Score equation

↑ **Parent:** [Informant function](informant-function.md)

A score equation sets the [score function](informant-function.md) equal to zero. Its solutions are stationary points of the [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md), and a negative-definite likelihood Hessian identifies an interior local maximum.
