# Morse oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Morse potential](morse-potential.md)

The quantum Hamiltonian with kinetic energy $p^2/(2\mu)$ and [Morse potential](morse-potential.md) has bound energies of the displayed form, where $A=\hbar\omega_e$, $B=A^2/(4D_e)>0$ and $\omega_e=a\sqrt{2D_e/\mu}$. Adjacent energy differences are $A-2B(n+1)$, distinct and positive over the physical bound-level range. A finite truncation with nonzero adjacent dipole couplings therefore satisfies [connected nondegenerate transition chain generates special unitary control](connected-nondegenerate-transition-chain-generates-special-unitary-control.md). Controllability of such a truncation does not by itself prove controllability on the full space including dissociation states.
