# Arnoldi decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Arnoldi iteration](arnoldi-iteration.md)

The factorization produced by the [Arnoldi iteration](arnoldi-iteration.md), with [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) $Q_{k+1}$ and an [upper Hessenberg matrix](upper-hessenberg-matrix.md) $\widetilde H_k$. Multiplying by $Q_k^T$ identifies its leading square block as $H_k=Q_k^TAQ_k$.
