# Penguin diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor-changing neutral current](flavor-changing-neutral-current.md)

A [penguin](penguin-diagram.md) diagram is a [loop](loop-feynman-diagram.md) topology in which a neutral gauge boson is emitted during a flavour-changing transition. A representative electroweak top-quark diagram contains a charged vector and a [down-type quark](down-quark.md) [loop](loop-feynman-diagram.md). Individual attachments need not be gauge invariant; the physical [amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) includes the required companion graphs and [flavour](flavor-eigenstate.md) sums.
