# Passive sign convention

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)

The [passive sign convention](passive-sign-convention.md) defines terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V=\phi(A)-\phi(B)$ and positive [electric current](electric-current.md) entering at $A$. Then $VI$ is absorbed electrical [power](power.md): positive means the component receives [energy](energy-split.md), negative means it supplies [energy](energy-split.md). Reversing both reference directions leaves the product unchanged. For a positive [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) obeying [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md), both $V$ and $I$ reverse together and [Joule heating](joule-heating.md) remains $I^2R\geq0$.
