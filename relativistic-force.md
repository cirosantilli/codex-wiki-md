# Relativistic force

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum](four-momentum.md)

For three-momentum $p=\gamma mv$, force and acceleration satisfy

$$
F=m\gamma\left(a+\frac{\gamma^2}{c^2}(v\mathbin{\cdot}a)v\right),
\qquad
a=\frac1{m\gamma}\left(F-\frac{F\mathbin{\cdot}v}{c^2}v\right).
$$

The associated [four-force](four-force.md) uses [proper time](proper-time.md) instead of coordinate time and is a different, four-dimensional object.
