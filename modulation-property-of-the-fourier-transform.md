# Modulation property of the Fourier transform

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md)

Multiplication by a unit-modulus linear phase shifts the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md). Substituting $e^{ia\cdot x}$ into the defining integral gives the displayed identity. For a surface measure this permits independent translations of cap transforms without changing their input [norms](norm.md) or angular supports.
