# Equal-time canonical anticommutator of a Dirac field

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md)

For mode normalization $1/\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf p}}$, particle and antiparticle operators satisfy $\{a_{\mathbf p}^s,a_{\mathbf q}^{r\dagger}\}=\{b_{\mathbf p}^s,b_{\mathbf q}^{r\dagger}\}=(2\pi)^3\delta^{sr}\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$. The [Dirac spinor](dirac-spinor.md) completeness relations imply $\sum_s[u_s(\mathbf p)u_s^\dagger(\mathbf p)+v_s(-\mathbf p)v_s^\dagger(-\mathbf p)]=2E_{\mathbf p}I$. Inserting this into the [mode expansion of a Dirac field](mode-expansion-of-a-dirac-field.md) gives the displayed local anticommutator. The reversed momentum in the antiparticle completeness term is essential.
