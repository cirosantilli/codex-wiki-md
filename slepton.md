# Slepton

↑ **Parent:** [Sparticle](sparticle.md)

A [slepton](slepton.md) is a scalar [sparticle](sparticle.md) partner of a [lepton](lepton.md). In the [MSSM](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md), the [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md) $L$ and $E^c$ package the corresponding [Standard Model fermions](standard-model-fermion.md) together with their [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) partners. Their [gauge group representations](gauge-group-representation.md) and [hypercharges](hypercharge.md) follow those of the left-handed fermion convention, including charge-conjugated singlets.
