# RecA

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[RecA](reca.md) is a bacterial [recombinase](recombinase.md) that assembles with [single-stranded DNA](single-stranded-dna.md) and [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) into a [presynaptic filament](presynaptic-filament.md). It promotes donor capture, complementarity testing and [DNA strand exchange](dna-strand-exchange.md).
