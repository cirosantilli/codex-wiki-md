# Cyclic subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic group](cyclic-group.md)

The cyclic subgroup generated by an element $g$ is $\langle g\rangle=\{g^n:n\in\mathbb Z\}$. Its size is the [order](order-of-a-group-element.md) of $g$.
