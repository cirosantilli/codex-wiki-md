# Low-energy effective action

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md)

A low-energy effective action retains the light degrees of freedom and describes the effects of heavier ones through local interactions and a [derivative expansion](derivative-expansion.md). It can be obtained by matching an [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md) or by a low-energy [compactification](compactification-physics.md). A [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md) implements integration of high-energy modes with a scale cutoff. The [quantum effective action](effective-action.md) that generates [one-particle-irreducible vertices](one-particle-irreducible-vertex.md) is a different construction; the two need not coincide.
