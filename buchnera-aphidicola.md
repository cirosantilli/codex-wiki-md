# Buchnera aphidicola

↑ **Parent:** [Bacteria](bacteria.md)

[Buchnera aphidicola](buchnera-aphidicola.md) is an intracellular aphid symbiont associated with nutritional complementation. Its reduced [genome](genome.md) retains functions relevant to amino-acid provision, illustrating a host-dependent mutualism rather than simply a pathogenic strategy.
