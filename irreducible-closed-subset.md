# Irreducible closed subset

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible topological space](irreducible-topological-space.md)

A nonempty [closed subset](closed-set.md) which, with its induced [topology](topology-split.md), is an [irreducible topological space](irreducible-topological-space.md). In an [affine algebraic set](affine-algebraic-set.md) these subsets correspond, with reversed inclusion, to [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md) of the [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md).
