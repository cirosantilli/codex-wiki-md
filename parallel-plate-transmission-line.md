# Parallel-plate transmission line

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission line](transmission-line.md)

Two perfectly conducting wide strips of width $a$ and separation $d\ll a$, filled with a homogeneous nonmagnetic [dielectric](dielectric.md) of absolute [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\varepsilon$, support a [transverse electromagnetic mode](transverse-electromagnetic-mode.md). Ignoring edge fields, their distributed [capacitance](capacitance.md) is $C'=\varepsilon a/d$ and distributed external [inductance](inductance.md) is $L'=\mu_0d/a$. The magnetic-energy integral $\mu_0(I/a)^2ad/2=L'I^2/2$ gives the latter. Their [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md) is $Z_0=(d/a)\sqrt{\mu_0/\varepsilon}$ and propagation speed is $1/\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon}$. These parameters are per unit length, distinct from a lumped capacitor and inductor.
