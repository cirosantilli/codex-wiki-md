# Focus (optics)

↑ **Parent:** [Lens (optics)](lens-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_(optics))

An [optical focus](focus-optics.md) is a point where a specified bundle of [light rays](light-ray.md) converges, or where its backward extensions meet. For a converging [thin lens](thin-lens.md) receiving [light rays](light-ray.md) parallel to the [optical axis](optical-axis.md), the rear [optical focus](focus-optics.md) is $(f,0)$ when the [thin lens](thin-lens.md) is at $x=0$; its front [optical focus](focus-optics.md) is $(-f,0)$. An object's image is generally elsewhere: the [optical focus](focus-optics.md) describes the parallel incoming bundle, while the [thin lens](thin-lens.md) equation locates the image for a finite object distance.
