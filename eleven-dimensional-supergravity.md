# Eleven-dimensional supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

Eleven-dimensional supergravity contains a metric, a three-form potential and a gravitino. Its bosonic on-shell counts are $11(11-3)/2=44$ for the metric and $\binom93=84$ for the three-form, totaling $128$. Flat toroidal zero-mode reduction gives [type IIA supergravity](type-iia-supergravity.md) in ten dimensions and [four-dimensional N=8 supergravity](four-dimensional-n-8-supergravity.md) in four dimensions without losing these bosonic polarizations.
