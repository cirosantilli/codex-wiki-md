# Powder X-ray diffraction

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md)

[Powder X-ray diffraction](powder-x-ray-diffraction.md) measures [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md) from many crystal grains with different orientations. For each allowed set of lattice spacings, suitable grains satisfy [Bragg's law](bragg-s-law.md) and produce a cone of diffracted directions, seen as a ring on an area detector or a peak in an angular scan. Several peak positions and systematic absences can identify a crystal structure even when a sample no longer retains one single-crystal orientation. Preferred orientation, strain and phase coexistence can complicate interpretation.
