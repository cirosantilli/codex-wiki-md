# Thin-film mass flux

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film equation](thin-film-equation.md)

The thin-film mass flux is the layer thickness times its depth-averaged velocity. In a quasi-steady transition region, mass conservation makes this flux approximately constant along the layer.
