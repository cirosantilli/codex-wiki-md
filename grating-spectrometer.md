# Grating spectrometer

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md)

A [spectrometer](spectrometer.md) that resolves [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) using the [angular dispersion](angular-dispersion.md) of a [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md). A narrow slit at a [collimator](collimator.md) focal plane supplies nearly parallel illumination; a telescope or focusing lens forms separate images for different outgoing angles. At normal incidence, the [grating equation](grating-equation.md) is $d\sin\theta=m\lambda$. The slit width, illuminated groove count, aberrations and detector sampling jointly determine whether a calculated [angular separation](angular-separation.md) is actually resolved.
