# Four-force

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum](four-momentum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-force)

The [four-force](four-force.md) is the [proper time](proper-time.md) derivative of [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). For constant rest mass and coordinate three-force $\mathbf F=d\mathbf p/dt$, its components are $K^\mu=\gamma(\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v/c,\mathbf F)$ in the convention $p^\mu=(E/c,\mathbf p)$. Thus it packages the power transfer and three-force into one Lorentz-covariant vector.
