# Statistical Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

A statistical Hamiltonian assigns an energy to a microscopic configuration. Its [Boltzmann weight](boltzmann-factor.md) defines the [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md), and summing or integrating that weight gives a [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md). A reduced Hamiltonian absorbs inverse temperature into its coefficients, so the weight is $e^{-H_{\rm reduced}}$. An [Ising model](ising-model.md) uses discrete [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) configurations, while [Landau-Ginzburg theory](landau-ginzburg-theory.md) uses a coarse [scalar field](scalar-field.md).
