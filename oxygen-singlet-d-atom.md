# Oxygen singlet D atom

↑ **Parent:** [Oxygen](oxygen.md)

An electronically excited atomic oxygen state produced in part by [ozone](ozone.md) [photodissociation](photodissociation.md). Reaction with [water](water.md) can yield two [hydroxyl radicals](hydroxyl-radical.md), while collisional quenching returns the atom to its triplet ground term without the same [radical](radical-chemistry.md) production. Its short-lived steady balance is distinct from the lifetime of [ozone](ozone.md) or [water](water.md).
