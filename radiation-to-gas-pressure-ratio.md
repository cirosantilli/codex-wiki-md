# Radiation-to-gas pressure ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical structure of an astrophysical disk](vertical-structure-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md)

The radiation-to-gas pressure ratio compares [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) with [gas pressure](gas-pressure.md). It is distinct from [plasma beta](plasma-beta.md), which compares gas pressure with [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md). Both are sometimes denoted by $\beta$, so the convention must be stated.
