# Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md)

For particles of mass $m$ in a classical gas at temperature $T$, the normalized velocity density is

$$
f(\mathbf v)=
\left(\frac{m}{2\pi k_BT}\right)^{3/2}
\exp\left(-\frac{m|\mathbf v|^2}{2k_BT}\right).
$$

Its Cartesian components are independent centered [normal random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md) of variance $k_BT/m$.
