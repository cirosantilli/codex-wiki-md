# T7 trisilanol cage

↑ **Parent:** [Silsesquioxane](silsesquioxane.md)

A corner-deficient cage $\mathrm{R_7Si_7O_9(OH)_3}$. Removing one [silicon](silicon.md) corner from a T8 cage leaves three silanol-bearing [silicon](silicon.md) sites, three further sites adjacent to those, and a unique opposite site: symmetry classes 3:3:1.
