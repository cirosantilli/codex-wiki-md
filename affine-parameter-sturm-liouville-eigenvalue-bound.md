# Affine-parameter Sturm-Liouville eigenvalue bound

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Ritz method](rayleigh-ritz-method.md)

For a self-adjoint [Sturm-Liouville operator](sturm-liouville-operator.md) $L_\epsilon=L_0+\epsilon L_1$ with common boundary conditions and an $L^2$-normalized lowest [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) $U_0$ of $L_0$, evaluating the [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) on $U_0$ gives the displayed upper bound. More generally the lowest eigenvalue is the infimum of affine functions of $\epsilon$, so it is concave in that parameter. The trial state must satisfy the boundary requirements of the variational domain; changing the operator without changing the trial function's normalization can still be legitimate when the weight is fixed.
