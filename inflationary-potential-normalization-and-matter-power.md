# Inflationary potential normalization and matter power

↑ **Parent:** [Slow-roll curvature power spectrum](slow-roll-curvature-power-spectrum.md)

At a fixed horizon-exit e-fold count and with the later cosmology fixed, rescaling a canonical single-field inflationary potential by a positive constant leaves its potential slow-roll parameters and field solution unchanged. The [slow-roll curvature power spectrum](slow-roll-curvature-power-spectrum.md) is proportional to $V/\epsilon_V$, so the [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md) scales by the same factor while its leading tilt and transfer-function shape remain unchanged. A changed specified reheating history can also shift the horizon-exit count assigned to a fixed physical wavenumber.
