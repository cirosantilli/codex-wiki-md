# Optical microscope

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument.md)

An [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) uses [optical lens](lens-optics.md) elements to obtain a magnified image of a small object or an intermediate image. A calibrated eyepiece can measure image dimensions; its own [diffraction](diffraction.md) and [optical aberrations](optical-aberration.md) limit the readout.
