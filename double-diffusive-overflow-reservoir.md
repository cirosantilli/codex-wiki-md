# Double-diffusive overflow reservoir

↑ **Parent:** [Thermosolutal convection](thermosolutal-convection.md)

A steady well-mixed hot salty reservoir receives volume flux $Q$ and loses the same flux over a weir. Heat and salt balances are $Q(T_i-T)=AF_T$ and $Q(S_i-S)=AF_S$. If $\beta F_S/(\alpha F_T)=R_F$ and $\alpha F_T=b(\alpha\Delta T)^{4/3}/R_\rho^2$, define $R_0=\beta(S_i-S_\infty)/[\alpha(T_i-T_\infty)]$. Then $\eta_s=(R_F/R_0)\theta_s$ and $C=Ab[\alpha(T_i-T_\infty)]^{1/3}/(Q R_0^2)$. For $R_0>1$ and $0\leq R_F<1$ the interface is statically stable while preferential heat transfer can drive [double-diffusive convection](double-diffusive-convection.md).
