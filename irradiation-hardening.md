# Irradiation hardening

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation damage in materials](radiation-damage-in-materials.md)

An increase in resistance to plastic flow caused by irradiation-produced obstacles to [dislocation glide](dislocation-glide.md), including loops, clusters and precipitates. Increased strength can coexist with reduced ductility.
