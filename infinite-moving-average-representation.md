# Infinite moving-average representation

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-average model](moving-average-model.md)

A causal square-integrable linear [time series](time-series-split.md) can be written $X_t=\mu+\sum_{j\geq0}\psi_j\varepsilon_{t-j}$. Square summability of the coefficients ensures mean-square convergence for [white noise](white-noise.md). An innovation representation additionally identifies its driving noise as the [innovation process](innovation-process.md).
