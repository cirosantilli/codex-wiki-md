# Derived series

↑ **Parent:** [Solvable group](solvable-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derived_series)

The derived series is defined by

$$
G^{(0)}=G,\qquad G^{(n+1)}=[G^{(n)},G^{(n)}].
$$

A group is [solvable](solvable-group.md) exactly when this series reaches the trivial group after finitely many steps.
