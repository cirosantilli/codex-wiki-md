# Nozzle area-velocity relation

↑ **Parent:** [Isentropic nozzle flow](isentropic-nozzle-flow.md)

Steady [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md), inviscid momentum balance and $dp=c_s^2d\rho$ give $d\ln A=(M^2-1)d\ln u$, where $M=u/c_s$. A smooth sonic crossing needs an area extremum; a minimum-area throat supports an accelerating subsonic-to-supersonic branch. A throat is a necessary geometric condition, not a sufficient specification of operating [pressures](pressure-split.md).
