# S-wave bound-state threshold in a spherical square well

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical square well](spherical-square-well.md)

For [binding energy](binding-energy.md) $\varepsilon>0$, match the inner reduced radial wave $\sin(Kr)$ to an outer decaying exponential. The resulting equation is $K\cot(Ka)=-\chi$, with $K^2=2m(V_0-\varepsilon)/\hbar^2$ and $\chi^2=2m\varepsilon/\hbar^2$. A bound level reaches zero [energy](energy-split.md) when $\kappa a=(n+1/2)\pi$. These are divergences of the [spherical square-well scattering length](spherical-square-well-scattering-length.md). The exact zero-energy threshold wave is not [square-integrable](square-integrable-function.md); a [normalizable](normalizable-wavefunction.md) [bound state](bound-state.md) appears on the more attractive side.
