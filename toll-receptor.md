# Toll receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Cell-surface receptor](cell-surface-receptor.md)

A receptor involved in embryonic patterning and immunity. In the early [Drosophila melanogaster](drosophila-melanogaster.md) embryo, spatially restricted activation by [Spätzle](spatzle.md) promotes nuclear [Dorsal protein](dorsal-protein.md).
