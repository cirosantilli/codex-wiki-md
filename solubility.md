# Solubility

↑ **Parent:** [Physical properties of salts](physical-properties-of-salts.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solubility)

[Solubility](solubility.md) specifies the amount that can remain dissolved at [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) under stated conditions; it is different from how quickly a sample dissolves. Separating [ions](ion.md) from an [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md) costs [energy](energy.md), but [solvation](solvation.md) establishes new interactions with the [solvent](solvent.md). In [water](water.md), [hydration of ions](hydration-of-ions.md) couples charged [ions](ion.md) to polar [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule.md). The net [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) of dissolution can have either sign; [entropy](entropy.md) and [concentration](concentration.md) also enter the [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) balance. Consequently, being a [salt](salt-chemistry.md) does not guarantee high [solubility](solubility.md) in [water](water.md) or any other [solvent](solvent.md).[§11.1, “The Formation of Solutions”, Figure 11.4; §11.3, definition of solubility; §16.4, free energy](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

Low [solubility](solubility.md) must also be distinguished from weak [electrolyte](electrolyte.md) behavior. A small amount of an [ionic compound](salt-chemistry.md) can dissolve essentially completely as [ions](ion.md), giving few charge carriers because little material dissolved. Calling that a [weak electrolyte](weak-electrolyte.md) solely because its [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) conducts poorly would conflate two different questions.[§11.2, “Ionic Electrolytes”: sparingly soluble compounds can be strong electrolytes](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)
