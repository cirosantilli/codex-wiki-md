# Electric and magnetic dimensions of a form-coupled brane

↑ **Parent:** [Wess-Zumino brane coupling](wess-zumino-brane-coupling.md)

A $(p+1)$-form [gauge potential](gauge-field.md) couples electrically through its pullback to a $(p+1)$-dimensional [worldvolume](worldvolume.md), whose object has $p$ spatial dimensions. Its field strength has degree $p+2$ and its magnetic dual potential has degree $d-p-3$, hence magnetic spatial dimension $d-p-4$. Electric and magnetic fluxes are integrated over linking spheres of dimensions $d-p-2$ and $p+2$, respectively. This dimensional dictionary applies when the indicated objects and dual descriptions exist; top forms need separate treatment and carry no ordinary propagating polarization.
