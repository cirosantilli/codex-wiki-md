# Virtually free group

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtually_free_group)

A group is virtually free when it has a [free group](free-group.md) as a finite-index subgroup. Every finitely generated virtually free group is quasi-isometric to a tree.
