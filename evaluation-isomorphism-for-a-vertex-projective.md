# Evaluation isomorphism for a vertex projective

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex projective module of a path algebra](vertex-projective-module-of-a-path-algebra.md)

Evaluation at $e_i$ identifies a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) $h:kQe_i\to X$ with $h(e_i)\in e_iX$. Given $x\in e_iX$, the inverse sends $p$ to $px$. The isomorphism is natural and shows that [Hom functor](hom-functor.md) evaluation on a vertex projective is exact.
