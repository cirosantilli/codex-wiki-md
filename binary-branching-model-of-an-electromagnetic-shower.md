# Binary branching model of an electromagnetic shower

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic shower](electromagnetic-shower.md)

If every particle branches into two equal-energy daughters after one [radiation length](radiation-length.md), the multiplicity is $2^n$ and each [energy](energy-split.md) is $E_0/2^n$. Multiplication stops at the [critical energy of an electromagnetic shower](critical-energy-of-an-electromagnetic-shower.md). This predicts a branching depth, not a realistic containment quantile: [stochastic](statistical-fluctuation.md) interaction distances, unequal radiation spectra and the residual [stopping range](particle-range.md) produce a longitudinal tail.
