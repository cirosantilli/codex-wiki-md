# Counterterm Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Counterterm](counterterm.md)

The local [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) whose coefficients absorb the regulated [ultraviolet divergences](ultraviolet-divergence.md) and implement chosen [renormalization conditions](renormalization-condition.md). In a renormalizable four-dimensional theory, [power counting](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) permits operators of [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) at most four, subject to the actual symmetries and up to [total derivatives](total-derivative.md). Operators absent from the initial displayed interaction can still be required for [counterterm closure](counterterm-closure.md).
