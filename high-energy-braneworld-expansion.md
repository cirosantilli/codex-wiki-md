# High-energy braneworld expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Randall–Sundrum model](randall-sundrum-model.md)

For a spatially flat, tuned one-brane background without energy transfer to the bulk, the [Israel junction conditions](israel-junction-conditions.md) produce the displayed modified [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md). Positive [brane tension](brane-tension.md) $\lambda$ gives ordinary expansion when $\rho\ll\lambda$, while the quadratic density correction enhances expansion at high energy. [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md), early acoustic evolution and primordial spectra can constrain this change. Spatial curvature, a residual cosmological constant and bulk energy exchange require their additional terms rather than this special formula.
