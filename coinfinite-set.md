# Coinfinite set

↑ **Parent:** [Set](set-split.md)

A [subset](subset.md) $A$ of a specified ambient [set](set-split.md) $X$ is coinfinite if its [complement of a set](complement-of-a-set.md) $X\setminus A$ is infinite. In $\mathbb N$, the even numbers are both infinite and coinfinite, whereas a [cofinite set](cofinite-set.md) is not coinfinite.
