# Cosmological density parameter flow

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological density parameter](cosmological-density-parameter.md)

For an expanding [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) with positive [energy density](energy-density.md), put $\Omega=8\pi G\rho/(3H^2)$ and $w=P/\rho$, in units $c=1$. The [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md), [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) and [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) give

$$
\frac{\dot H}{H^2}=-1-\frac12(1+3w)\Omega,\qquad
\frac{d\Omega}{d\log a}=(1+3w)\Omega(\Omega-1).
$$

For constant $w$, linearizing at $\Omega=1$ gives $\Omega-1\propto a^{1+3w}$. For variable $w$, replace the power by $\exp\int(1+3w(a))\,d\log a$. This describes the [flatness problem](flatness-problem.md) for decelerating expansion, away from epochs at which $H=0$.
