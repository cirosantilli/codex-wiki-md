# Electronic noise

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_noise)

Unwanted stochastic fluctuations of [voltage](voltage.md) or [electric current](electric-current.md). Contributions include [thermal noise](thermal-noise.md), [shot noise](shot-noise.md), and resistance fluctuations under bias; their physical origins and dependence on [frequency](frequency.md) need not be identical. A [low-pass filter](low-pass-filter.md) attenuates the high-[frequency](frequency.md) part of such fluctuations along with any high-[frequency](frequency.md) signal.
