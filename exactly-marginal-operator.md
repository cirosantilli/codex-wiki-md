# Exactly marginal operator

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling dimension](scaling-dimension.md)

An exactly marginal operator has scaling dimension equal to the spacetime dimension and a deformation coupling whose beta function vanishes to all orders. Varying that coupling moves along a family of conformal field theories without introducing a scale.
