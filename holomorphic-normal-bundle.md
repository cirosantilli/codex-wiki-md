# Holomorphic normal bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Complex submanifold](complex-submanifold.md)

For a [complex submanifold](complex-submanifold.md) $Y\subseteq X$, the holomorphic normal bundle is the quotient [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

$$
N_{Y/X}=TX|_Y/TY.
$$
