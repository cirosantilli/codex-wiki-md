# Skew Young diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Young diagram](young-diagram.md)

For diagram inclusion $\mu\subseteq\lambda$, the skew Young diagram $\lambda/\mu$ consists of the cells of $\lambda$ outside $\mu$. Diagram inclusion is not the [dominance order on partitions](dominance-order-on-partitions.md). Edge adjacency defines its connected components, and a connected skew diagram without a $2\times2$ square is a [rim hook](rim-hook.md).
