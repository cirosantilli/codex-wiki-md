# Stationary deviation of a primary rainbow ray

↑ **Parent:** [Rainbow](rainbow.md)

For the one-internal-reflection branch, [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) and $\delta=\pi+2\alpha-4\beta$ give $\delta'=2-4\cos\alpha/(n\cos\beta)$. At an interior [stationary point](stationary-point.md), $\cos\alpha=(n/2)\cos\beta$, hence $\sin^2\beta=(4-n^2)/(3n^2)$. Another [differentiation](differentiation.md) gives $\delta''=3\tan\beta>0$ there. The interior minimum exists for $1<n<2$. The internal [specular reflection](specular-reflection.md) can be partial; [total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md) is not required.
