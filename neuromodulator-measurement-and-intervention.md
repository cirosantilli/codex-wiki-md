# Neuromodulator measurement and intervention

↑ **Parent:** [Neuromodulation](neuromodulation.md)

Observations establish what signals naturally covary with defined events; [interventions](treatment.md) estimate consequences of changing a specified target. Convergence requires matching cell population, projection, timescale and behavioural endpoint, with direct [target engagement](target-engagement.md) measurements and controls for [off-target drug effects](off-target-drug-effect.md).
