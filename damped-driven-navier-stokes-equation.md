# Damped-driven Navier-Stokes equation

↑ **Parent:** [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md)

A damped-driven Navier-Stokes equation adds linear damping and external forcing. In two-dimensional steady vorticity form it reads

$$
-\nu\Delta\omega+\gamma\omega+u\mathbin\cdot\nabla\omega=g,
\qquad \nabla\mathbin\cdot u=0.
$$
