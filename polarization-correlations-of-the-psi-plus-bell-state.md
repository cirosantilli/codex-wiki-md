# Polarization correlations of the Psi-plus Bell state

↑ **Parent:** [Bell state](bell-state-split.md)

For $|\Psi^+\rangle=(|01\rangle+|10\rangle)/\sqrt2$, the [Pauli operator](pauli-operator.md) correlations are $\langle XX\rangle=1$, $\langle ZZ\rangle=-1$, and $\langle XZ\rangle=\langle ZX\rangle=0$. Substituting the [measurement of linear photon polarization](measurement-of-linear-photon-polarization.md) into $\langle M(\alpha)\otimes M(\beta)\rangle$ gives $\sin2\alpha\sin2\beta-\cos2\alpha\cos2\beta=-\cos2(\alpha+\beta)$. In particular, opposite physical angles give perfect anticorrelation. The sum of angles here depends on this choice of [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) and basis conventions.
