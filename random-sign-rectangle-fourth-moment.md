# Random sign rectangle fourth moment

↑ **Parent:** [Box norm](box-norm.md)

Give every entry of an $m$ by $n$ matrix an independent uniformly random sign. A four-entry rectangle product has expectation zero unless its two row indices coincide or its two column indices coincide; in those cases it is one. Counting the union of these diagonal cases gives $mn^2+m^2n-mn$ before normalization. Thus some sign matrix has [box norm](box-norm.md) fourth power at most the displayed expectation, and its [cut norm](cut-norm.md) tends to zero when both dimensions tend to infinity.
