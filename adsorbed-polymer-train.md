# Adsorbed polymer train

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer adsorption](polymer-adsorption.md)

A contiguous sequence of segments making contacts with an adsorbing surface. The [adsorption](adsorption.md) [energy](energy-split.md) gained per contact competes with the loss of chain configurations. A chain can have several trains separated by loops rather than lying wholly flat.
