# Quadrupolar point-mass tidal potential

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md)

Subtracting the constant and uniform-acceleration terms from the point-mass [relative potential](relative-potential.md) gives $\psi_{\mathrm{tid}}=Gm_p[3(\mathbf r\cdot\widehat{\mathbf r}_p)^2-r^2]/(2r_p^3)$, with force convention $\nabla\psi$. Its [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md) is $Gm_p(3\widehat{\mathbf r}_p\widehat{\mathbf r}_p^T-I)/r_p^3$. This is the leading term for target size small compared with separation.
