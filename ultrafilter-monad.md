# Ultrafilter monad

↑ **Parent:** [Monad structure on a terminal endofunctor](monad-structure-on-a-terminal-endofunctor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrafilter_monad)

The [ultrafilter functor](ultrafilter-functor.md) carries the monad structure obtained from its terminality among finite-coproduct-preserving endofunctors of sets. Its unit sends a point to its [principal ultrafilter](principal-ultrafilter.md); its multiplication sends an ultrafilter of ultrafilters to the ultrafilter of subsets whose corresponding basic set of ultrafilters is large.
