# Wave crest

↑ **Parent:** [Crest and trough](crest-and-trough.md)

A wave crest is a [local maximum](local-maximum.md) of the wave's displacement. In a monochromatic travelling [wave](wave.md) it lies at a fixed phase and propagates at the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md).
