# Massless scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md)

A free real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) with zero [mass](mass.md) has the displayed wave equation in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md). In curved spacetime its dynamics also depend on curvature coupling: a [minimally coupled massless scalar field](minimally-coupled-massless-scalar-field.md) and a [conformally coupled scalar field](conformally-coupled-scalar-field.md) need not have the same cosmological modes.
