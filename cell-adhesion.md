# Cell adhesion

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_adhesion)

[Cell adhesion](cell-adhesion.md) is attachment of a [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) to another cell or to its surroundings through molecular interactions. Adhesion strength and turnover help determine how cells transmit forces and rearrange.
