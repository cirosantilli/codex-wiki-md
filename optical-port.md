# Optical port

↑ **Parent:** [Beam splitter](beam-splitter.md)

An [optical port](optical-port.md) specifies where and in which direction a beam enters or leaves an [optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md). To attach one [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) to it, also specify the beam's transverse field profile, [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md), [frequency](frequency.md) and reference plane for [wave phase](phase-waves.md). Reversing propagation changes the input/output assignment. Four geometrical connections do not imply four independent input [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md) in the chosen two-input configuration.
