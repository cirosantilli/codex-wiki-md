<h1 id="lambert-s-cosine-law">Lambert's cosine law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambert's_cosine_law)

For an ideal diffuse emitting or reflecting planar surface, the radiant intensity in a direction at angle $\theta$ to its normal is proportional to $\cos\theta$. The factor comes from projected area. [Lambertian reflectance](lambertian-reflectance.md) instead describes the corresponding direction-independent radiance; intensity and radiance are distinct quantities.
