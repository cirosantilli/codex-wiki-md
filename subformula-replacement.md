# Subformula replacement

↑ **Parent:** [Subformula occurrence](subformula-occurrence.md)

[Subformula replacement](subformula-replacement.md) is the operation of replacing one specified [subformula occurrence](subformula-occurrence.md) by another [logical formula](logical-formula.md). Its preservation of well-formedness is the [subformula replacement theorem](subformula-replacement-theorem.md).
