# Population principal component

↑ **Parent:** [Principal component analysis](principal-component-analysis.md)

The [population principal components](population-principal-component.md) are centered linear compounds along an orthonormal eigenbasis of the [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md), ordered by decreasing [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). They are uncorrelated and have [variances](variance-split.md) $\lambda_j$. Each successive direction maximizes [variance](variance-split.md) subject to unit length and orthogonality to earlier directions. Uncorrelatedness does not imply [independence](independent-random-variables.md) unless additional distributional assumptions hold.
