# Organocuprate

↑ **Parent:** [Organometallic compound](organometallic-compound.md)

An organocopper reagent capable of carbon-group transfer. Suitable [organocuprates](organocuprate.md) can favour [conjugate addition](conjugate-addition.md) to an enone over direct addition to its [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md).
