# Fibers of compactification comparison maps

↑ **Parent:** [Hausdorff compactification](hausdorff-compactification.md)

If $\pi:\beta X\to K$ is the canonical map from the [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md) onto a [Hausdorff compactification](hausdorff-compactification.md), its fibers over the original points are singletons. If $\pi(z)=x\in X$ and $z\ne x$, choose an open neighborhood $U$ of $x$ in $\beta X$ whose closure excludes $z$. Choose open $V\subseteq K$ with $x\in V$ and $V\cap X\subseteq U$. The open neighborhood $\pi^{-1}V$ of $z$ has dense trace $V\cap X$, placing $z$ in $\overline U$, a contradiction. This controls when images of closed remainder sets stay in the remainder.
