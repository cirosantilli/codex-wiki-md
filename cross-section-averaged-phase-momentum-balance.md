# Cross-section averaged phase momentum balance

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

For a phase occupying area $\alpha A$ of a constant-area pipe, define $\bar\rho=(\alpha A)^{-1}\int\rho\,dA$, $\bar v=\int\rho v\,dA/(\alpha A\bar\rho)$ and $\overline{v^2}=\int\rho v^2\,dA/(\alpha A\bar\rho)$. The [momentum](momentum-split.md) per length is $A\alpha\bar\rho\bar v$, the advected [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) is $A\alpha\bar\rho\overline{v^2}$, and end pressures contribute $-A\partial_x(\alpha\bar p)$. Applying [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) to a slice proves the displayed balance, with $M$ all other phase forces per length. Interphase pressure terms can cancel the derivative of the area fraction. Equality of the second moment and squared mean requires a separate closure.
