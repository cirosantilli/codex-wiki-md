# Glass alteration gel

↑ **Parent:** [Waste-glass corrosion](waste-glass-corrosion.md)

A hydrated, compositionally modified interfacial layer formed during [glass](glass.md) [corrosion](corrosion.md). Its [porosity](porosity.md), transport properties and continuing reactions can affect the residual alteration rate.
