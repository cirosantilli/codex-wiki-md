# Inclination projection of an axisymmetric stellar velocity tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar pressure-energy tensor](stellar-pressure-energy-tensor.md)

For an axisymmetric random [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) tensor with $\Pi_{11}=\Pi_{22}=M\sigma_0^2$ and $\Pi_{33}=M\sigma_0^2(1-\delta)$, contracting with the line-of-sight unit vector $(\sin i,0,\cos i)$ gives the displayed mass-weighted [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md). A major-axis rotation amplitude projects as $V_r=V\sin i$. By contrast, an azimuthally averaged line-of-sight streaming root-mean-square is $V\sin i/\sqrt2$ when $V^2$ is the full in-plane streaming mean square. Aperture weights and the definition of the measured rotation statistic must match the intrinsic statistic.
