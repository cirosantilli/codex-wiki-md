# Suspended sediment

↑ **Parent:** [Sediment transport](sediment-transport.md)

[Suspended sediment](suspended-sediment.md) is particulate material carried within a fluid rather than solely along the bed. Its [concentration](concentration.md) balance includes [advection](advection.md), erosion into suspension and deposition from suspension. For [water](water.md) depth $h$ and suspended [mass](mass.md) [concentration](concentration.md) $c$, the conservative balance $(hc)_t+(huc)_x=\rho_s(v_E-v_D)$ reduces using [water](water.md) continuity to $h(c_t+uc_x)=\rho_s(v_E-v_D)$.
