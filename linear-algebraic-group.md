# Linear algebraic group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie theory](lie-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_algebraic_group)

An affine algebraic group is an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) $G$ whose multiplication and inversion are [regular maps](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md). Equivalently, its [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) $k[G]$ is a commutative [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md), with comultiplication induced by multiplication in $G$.
