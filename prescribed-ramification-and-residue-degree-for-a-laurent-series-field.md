# Prescribed ramification and residue degree for a Laurent series field

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent series field](laurent-series-field.md)

For arbitrary positive integers $e,f$, the displayed extension has degree $ef$, [ramification index](ramification-index.md) $e$, and [residue degree](residue-degree.md) $f$. If $|t|=p^{-1}$, its unique extended [absolute value on a field](absolute-value-algebra.md) is $|\sum a_nu^n|=p^{-\min\{n:a_n\ne0\}/e}$. A basis consists of $\gamma_i u^j$, where the $\gamma_i$ form an [basis](basis.md) of $\mathbb F_{p^f}$ and $0\le j<e$. Regrouping a Laurent series by its exponent modulo $e$ proves the basis assertion. When $p$ divides $e$ the extension can be inseparable; the valuation calculation still applies.
