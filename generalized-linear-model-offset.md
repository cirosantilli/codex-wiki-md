# Generalized linear model offset

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized linear model](generalized-linear-model.md)

An [offset](generalized-linear-model-offset.md) is a known term in a [generalized linear model](generalized-linear-model.md)'s [linear predictor](linear-predictor.md), with coefficient fixed to one. With a [log link](logarithmic-link-function.md) and exposure $n_i$, choosing $o_i=\log n_i$ gives a mean count $n_i\exp(x_i^T\beta)$. Its role differs from that of a fitted explanatory-variable coefficient.
