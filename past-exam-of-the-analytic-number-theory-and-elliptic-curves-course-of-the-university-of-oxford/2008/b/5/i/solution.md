<h1 id="b/5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $p=3$, a [root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) would have [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) zero because five is a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md). It would therefore reduce to a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) cube modulo nine. The only cube [residue classes](../../../../../residue-class.md) modulo nine are $0,1,-1$, none equal to five. Thus **no [root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) exists in $\mathbb Q_3$**.

For $p=5$, the [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) equation would be $3v_5(x)=1$, impossible because the [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) of a nonzero element of $\mathbb Q_5$ is an [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md). Thus **no [root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) exists in $\mathbb Q_5$**.

For $p=13$, $7^3\equiv5\pmod{13}$ and the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $3\cdot7^2\equiv4\not\equiv0\pmod{13}$. [Hensel's lemma](../../../../../hensel-s-lemma.md) lifts this simple [root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) to a [root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) in $\mathbb Z_{13}\subseteq\mathbb Q_{13}$. Thus **a [root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) exists in $\mathbb Q_{13}$**.
