<h1 id="b/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a point with $u\ne0$, consider the [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) $v_\ell$ at any rational [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md). If $v_\ell(u)<0$, the term $u^3$ has strictly lower [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) than $a_1u^2$ and $b_1u$, since the [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md) are integral. Thus $2v_\ell(v)=3v_\ell(u)$, forcing $v_\ell(u)$ even. If $v_\ell(u)>0$ and $\ell\nmid b_1$, the factor $u^2+a_1u+b_1$ is a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md), so $2v_\ell(v)=v_\ell(u)$, again even. At [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) zero there is no odd exponent to remove.

Therefore every odd [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) in the [square class](../../../../../square-class.md) of $u$ lies at a [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) dividing $b_1$, and all negative valuations are even. Removing rational squares gives a signed [squarefree integer](../../../../../square-free-integer.md) $r$ with $r\mid b_1$. For $(0,0)$ the assigned class is that of $b_1$, whose [squarefree](../../../../../square-free-integer.md) representative also divides $b_1$; the identity has representative one. Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{im}q\subseteq\{[r]:r\text{ is a signed squarefree divisor of }b_1\}.}
$$

The brackets stress that these [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md) represent [square classes](../../../../../square-class.md), which is the target of $q$.
