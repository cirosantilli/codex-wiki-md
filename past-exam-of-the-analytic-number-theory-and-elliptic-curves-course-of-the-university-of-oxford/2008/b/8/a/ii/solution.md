<h1 id="b/8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The preceding bound at $m=2$ gives $|h_x(2Q)-4h_x(Q)|\le C$ for every point $Q$. For $a_n=4^{-n}h_x(2^nP)$, take $Q=2^nP$ to obtain

$$
|a_{n+1}-a_n|\le C4^{-n-1}.
$$

For $m>n$, summing the geometric tail gives

$$
\boxed{|a_m-a_n|\le\sum_{j=n}^\infty C4^{-j-1}=\frac C{3\cdot4^n}\longrightarrow0.}
$$

Thus the sequence is [Cauchy](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) and converges by [completeness of the real numbers](../../../../../../completeness-of-the-real-numbers.md). The estimate also gives a quantitative bound on its distance from the limit.
