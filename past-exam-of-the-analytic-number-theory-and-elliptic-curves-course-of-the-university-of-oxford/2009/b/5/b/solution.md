<h1 id="b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The discriminant of $y^2=x^3-9$ is $-16\cdot27\cdot81=-2^4 3^7$, so five and seven are [primes](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) of [good reduction of an elliptic curve](../../../../../good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). Count solutions over their residue [fields](../../../../../field.md).

Modulo five the values of $x^3-9$ for $x=0,1,2,3,4$ are $1,2,4,3,0$. They yield $2,0,2,0,1$ affine points, respectively, and the point at infinity gives $\#E(\mathbb F_5)=6$.

Modulo seven the values for $x=0,\ldots,6$ are $5,6,6,4,6,4,4$. Only the three values four are nonzero squares, giving six affine points and $\#E(\mathbb F_7)=7$.

For a rational [elliptic curve](../../../../../elliptic-curve.md) at an odd [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) of [good reduction of an elliptic curve](../../../../../good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md), the kernel of reduction on $\mathbb Q_p$ has no [torsion element](../../../../../torsion-element.md): it is the [formal group](../../../../../formal-group-law.md) on $p\mathbb Z_p$, whose [formal logarithm](../../../../../formal-logarithm.md) embeds it in the additive [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $p\mathbb Z_p$. Thus the entire [torsion subgroup](../../../../../torsion-subgroup.md) injects into each of the two finite reduction [groups](../../../../../group-split.md). Its order divides both six and seven, hence is one:

$$
\boxed{E(\mathbb Q)_{\mathrm{tors}}=\{o\}.}
$$

Equivalently one may use prime-to-residue-characteristic injection at both [primes](../../../../../prime-number-split.md); it gives the same [coprime](../../../../../coprime-integers.md) bound. This is a [two-prime bound on rational elliptic torsion](../../../../../two-prime-bound-on-rational-elliptic-torsion.md).
