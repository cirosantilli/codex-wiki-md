<h1 id="b/5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The strict inequality means $v_3(x^2+2)>2$. Taking $x=5$ gives $x^2+2=27$, so

$$
\boxed{|5^2+2|_3=3^{-3}<3^{-2}.}
$$

For the other expression, if $3\nmid x$ then $x^2+3\not\equiv0\pmod3$ and its [P-adic valuation](../../../../../p-adic-valuation.md) is zero. If $3\mid x$, then $x^2+3=3(3(x/3)^2+1)$ has [P-adic valuation](../../../../../p-adic-valuation.md) exactly one. It can never have valuation greater than two. Thus **no such [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) exists** for the second strict inequality.
