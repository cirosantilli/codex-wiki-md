<h1 id="b/5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the [P-adic numbers](../../../../../p-adic-number.md), $\sum_{j\ge0}p^{2j}$ converges to $1/(1-p^2)$. Consequently

$$
\frac{1+2p}{p-p^3}=(p^{-1}+2)\sum_{j\ge0}p^{2j}
=\boxed{p^{-1}+2+p+2p^2+p^3+2p^4+\cdots.}
$$

Since $p\ne2$, the digits one and two are both between zero and $p-1$, so no carrying is required. These are its [p-adic digits](../../../../../p-adic-digit.md), with the unique negative-power digit at exponent minus one.
