<h1 id="b/8/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $p\equiv1\pmod4$, choose $i\in\mathbb F_p$ with $i^2=-1$. The cubic has the three distinct roots $0,im,-im$, so all four two-torsion points are rational over $\mathbb F_p$. They form a subgroup of order four, hence $4\mid\#E(\mathbb F_p)$ by [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md).

If $p\equiv3\pmod4$, let $\chi$ be the [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) with $\chi(0)=0$. Since $f(x)=x(x^2+m^2)$ is odd and $\chi(-1)=-1$, the terms for $x$ and $-x$ cancel in $\sum_x\chi(f(x))$. Counting the possible second coordinates gives

$$
\#E(\mathbb F_p)=1+\sum_x(1+\chi(f(x)))=p+1\equiv0\pmod4.
$$

Thus in both cases

$$
\boxed{4\mid\#E(\mathbb F_p).}
$$

The assumptions $p\ne2$ and $m\ne0$ ensure nonsingularity and keep the displayed two-torsion roots distinct when they exist.
