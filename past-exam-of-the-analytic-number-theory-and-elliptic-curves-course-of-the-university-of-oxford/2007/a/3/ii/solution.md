<h1 id="a/3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Complete multiplicativity and $f(p)=\pm1$ give, for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$,

$$
\boxed{F(s)=\prod_p(1-f(p)p^{-s})^{-1}.}
$$

The product is nonzero there; its absolutely convergent [logarithm](../../../../../logarithm.md) has terms $f(p)^mp^{-ms}/m$. Thus

$$
3\log\zeta(\sigma)+4\log|F(\sigma+it)|+\log|\zeta(\sigma+2it)|
=\sum_{p,m\ge1}\frac{3+4f(p)^m\cos(mt\log p)+\cos(2mt\log p)}{mp^{m\sigma}}.
$$

Each numerator is one of $3\pm4\cos\theta+\cos2\theta$, so it is nonnegative. Exponentiation gives the required lower bound one.

If $F(1+it)=0$ with $t\ne0$, its assumed holomorphy makes $|F(\sigma+it)|^4=O((\sigma-1)^4)$, or smaller for a higher-order zero. The zeta factor at real $\sigma$ grows at most as $(\sigma-1)^{-3}$, while the zeta factor near $1+2it$ stays bounded. The product tends to zero, a contradiction. Hence

$$
\boxed{F(1+it)\ne0\qquad(t\ne0).}
$$
