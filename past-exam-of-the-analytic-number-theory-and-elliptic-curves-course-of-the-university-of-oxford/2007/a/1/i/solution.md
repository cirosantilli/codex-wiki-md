<h1 id="a/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Dirichlet convolution](../../../../../dirichlet-convolution.md) is $(f_1*f_2)(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}f_1(d)f_2(n/d)$. Fix $s$ with $\sigma=\operatorname{Re}s>1$. [Absolute convergence](../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) of the two [Dirichlet series](../../../../../dirichlet-series.md) gives

$$
\sum_{a,b\ge1}|f_1(a)f_2(b)(ab)^{-s}|
=\left(\sum_a|f_1(a)|a^{-\sigma}\right)
\left(\sum_b|f_2(b)|b^{-\sigma}\right)<\infty.
$$

Thus the double [series](../../../../../series-mathematics.md) may be regrouped by $n=ab$, proving

$$
\boxed{F_1(s)F_2(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}(f_1*f_2)(n)n^{-s}.}
$$

The same estimate proves [absolute convergence](../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) of the resulting [Dirichlet series](../../../../../dirichlet-series.md).
