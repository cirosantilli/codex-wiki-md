<h1 id="a/1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Möbius function](../../../../../mobius-function.md) satisfies $\mu(p^e)^2=1$ for $e=0,1$ and is zero for $e\ge2$. Therefore

$$
H_1(s)=\prod_p(1+p^{-s})=\frac{\zeta(s)}{\zeta(2s)}.
$$

The inverse local factor is $(1+p^{-s})^{-1}=\sum_{e\ge0}(-1)^ep^{-es}$. Consequently the inverse under [Dirichlet convolution](../../../../../dirichlet-convolution.md) is the [Liouville function](../../../../../liouville-function.md)

$$
\boxed{h_2(n)=(-1)^{\Omega(n)},}
$$

where $\Omega(n)$ counts prime factors with multiplicity. Both functions are [multiplicative functions](../../../../../multiplicative-function.md). At $p^e$ with $e\ge1$, their convolution is $(-1)^e+(-1)^{e-1}=0$, while its value at one is one. Multiplicativity then proves $h_1*h_2=I$ on every positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md). Its [Dirichlet series](../../../../../dirichlet-series.md) is $H_2(s)=\zeta(2s)/\zeta(s)$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>1$.
