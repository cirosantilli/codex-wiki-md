<h1 id="b/4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) of $f'$ about $a_i$ has integral coefficients, so

$$
|f'(a_i+h_i)-f'(a_i)|\le|h_i|=dq_i<d.
$$

In a [Non-Archimedean absolute value](../../../../../non-archimedean-absolute-value.md), adding an error of norm strictly smaller than $d$ cannot change a quantity of norm $d$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{|f'(a_i)|=d\quad\text{for every }i.}
$$

This simultaneously verifies the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) assumption used at the next step of part (i).
