<h1 id="b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Nonsingularity with $b=a^2$ forces $a\ne0$. Take

$$
\boxed{P=(0,a).}
$$

It lies on the curve. Its [tangent](../../../../../tangent.md) slope is $a/(2a)=1/2$, so the doubling formula gives $x(2P)=1/4$. If $P$ were torsion, $2P$ would be torsion as well, contradicting the integrality just proved. Therefore $P$ has infinite order.
