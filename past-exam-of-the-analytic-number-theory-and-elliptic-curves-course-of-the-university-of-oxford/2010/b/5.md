↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

We first prove all three general torsion assertions before treating the lettered cases. If a finite point has $|x|_p>1$, the cubic term dominates the integral lower terms, so $v_p(x)=-2m$, $v_p(y)=-3m$ for an [integer](../../../integer-split.md) $m\ge1$. Its parameter $z=-x/y$ then lies in $p\mathbb Z_p$, with norm at most $p^{-1}$. The integral Weierstrass model defines its [formal group](../../../formal-group-law.md) at infinity over $\mathbb Z_p$: using $w=-1/y$ gives $w=z^3+Azw^2+Bw^3$, an integral power series starting in degree three.

The supplied torsion estimate excludes a nonzero torsion parameter for $p\ge3$, since $p^{-1}<p^{-1/(p-1)}$, and the lower bound increases for higher $p$-powers. For $p=2$ it excludes orders $2^n$ with $n\ge2$, because their bound is strictly above $1/2$. The remaining possibility is order two, which would imply the finite point equals its negative and hence $y=0$. A nonintegral $x$ cannot solve the monic cubic with integral coefficients, so this is impossible too. Thus every finite torsion point has integral $x$, and the equation then forces integral $y$.

For [good reduction](../../../good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) the reduction map is a [group homomorphism](../../../group-homomorphism.md) whose [kernel](../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is this [formal group](../../../formal-group-law.md) at infinity. Its torsion-free [kernel](../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) proves injectivity on torsion, giving a [subgroup](../../../subgroup.md) of the finite reduced group. Applying local integrality to a rational torsion point at every prime gives $x,y\in\mathbb Z$.

If $y\ne0$, the double of a rational torsion point is finite and integral. The doubling formula

$$
x(2P)=\frac{(3x^2+a)^2}{4y^2}-2x
$$

therefore implies $y^2\mid(3x^2+a)^2$. Also $y^2=x^3+ax+b$. Evaluating the supplied [polynomial](../../../polynomial-split.md) identity at the integral $x$ now gives

$$
\boxed{y=0\quad\text{or}\quad y^2\mid4a^3+27b^2.}
$$

This establishes the needed [Nagell–Lutz theorem](../../../nagell-lutz-theorem.md) conclusions without assuming them in the lettered applications.
