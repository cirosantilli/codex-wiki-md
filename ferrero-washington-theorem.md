# Ferrero-Washington theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa invariants](iwasawa-invariants.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrero–Washington_theorem)

For a finite abelian extension of $\mathbb Q$, the [unramified Iwasawa module](unramified-iwasawa-module.md) of its [cyclotomic Zp-extension](cyclotomic-zp-extension.md) has $\mu=0$. This removes $p$-power elementary factors from its characteristic ideal. The theorem does not assert $\mu=0$ for arbitrary noncyclotomic [Zp-extensions](zp-extension.md).
