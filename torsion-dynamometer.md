# Torsion dynamometer

↑ **Parent:** [Current-loop torque experiment](current-loop-torque-experiment.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Torsion balance](torsion-balance.md)

A [torsion dynamometer](torsion-dynamometer.md) compares an applied [torque](torque.md) with the restoring [torque](torque.md) of a twisted elastic element. Returning the lever to its zero position allows a [current-loop torque experiment](current-loop-torque-experiment.md) to hold the test orientation fixed while reading the compensating [torque](torque.md).
