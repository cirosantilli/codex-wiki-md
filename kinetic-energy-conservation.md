# Kinetic energy conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation of mechanical energy](conservation-of-mechanical-energy.md)

Total [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is unchanged between the incoming and outgoing states of an [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md). This does not require each body's [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) to be unchanged, nor that [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) remain constant during temporary elastic deformation. In general [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) includes [potential energy](potential-energy.md), so conservation of [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) alone does not imply constant [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md).
