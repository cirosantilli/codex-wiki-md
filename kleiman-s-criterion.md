<h1 id="kleiman-s-criterion">Kleiman's criterion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ample Cartier divisor](ample-cartier-divisor.md)

For a [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md), a divisor class is in the [ample cone](ample-cone.md) exactly when it is strictly positive on every nonzero element of the [closed cone of curves](closed-cone-of-curves.md). Equivalently, the ample cone is the interior of the [nef cone](nef-cone.md). The projectivity assumption matters: the same characterization is not asserted here for arbitrary proper schemes.
