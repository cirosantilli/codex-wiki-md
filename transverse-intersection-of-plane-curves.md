# Transverse intersection of plane curves

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection multiplicity](intersection-multiplicity.md)

Two [projective plane curves](projective-plane-curve.md) with no common [irreducible component](irreducible-component.md) meet transversely at $p$ if both are nonsingular there and their [projective tangent lines](tangent-line-to-a-projective-plane-curve.md) differ. This holds exactly when their [intersection multiplicity](intersection-multiplicity.md) is one. Indeed, in the local ring of the ambient smooth surface, independent linear terms of the two equations generate the maximal ideal and leave a quotient of length one. Conversely, length one forces those linear terms to span the two-dimensional cotangent space, proving both nonsingularity and distinctness of the tangents.
