# Nitroxide-mediated polymerization

↑ **Parent:** [Reversible-deactivation radical polymerization](reversible-deactivation-radical-polymerization.md)

Controlled [radical polymerization](radical-polymerization.md) based on reversible trapping of growing radicals by a nitroxide, forming dormant alkoxyamines. Initiation and end-group retention must be controlled to obtain predictable chain length.
