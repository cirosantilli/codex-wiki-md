# Spinor tensor square for so5

↑ **Parent:** [Spin representation](spin-representation.md)

For the [B2 root system](b2-root-system.md) with long [simple root](simple-root.md) first, the four-dimensional [spinor representation](spin-representation.md) has [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $(0,1)$. Its [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) has [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $(0,2)$ and [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) ten, so it is the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). The [exterior square](exterior-square.md) has the four nonzero defining [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) and two zero [weights](weight-representation-theory.md); it splits into the five-dimensional [vector representation](vector-representation.md) of [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $(1,0)$ and a [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md). Thus the [tensor square](tensor-square.md) decomposes as displayed. The invariant alternating [bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md) reflects the [pseudoreal representation](pseudoreal-representation.md) structure. The [spinor representation](spin-representation.md) is a [group representation](group-representation.md) of the [Spin group](spin-group.md) $\operatorname{Spin}(5)$, while its [tensor square](tensor-square.md) descends to $SO(5)$.
