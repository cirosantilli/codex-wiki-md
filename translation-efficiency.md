# Translation efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Translational regulation](translational-regulation.md)

An operational measure often estimated from normalized [ribosome](ribosome.md) footprints relative to [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) abundance. It reflects occupancy per transcript and is not automatically a direct rate of completed [protein](protein-split.md) production.
