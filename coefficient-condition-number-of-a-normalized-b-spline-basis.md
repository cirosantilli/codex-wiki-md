# Coefficient condition number of a normalized B-spline basis

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform-norm stability of a B-spline basis](uniform-norm-stability-of-a-b-spline-basis.md)

For a nonnegative partition-normalized [B-spline](b-spline.md) [basis](basis.md), the synthesis [linear map](linear-map.md) $Tc=\sum_jc_jN_j$ has [operator norm](operator-norm.md) one. Its [condition number](condition-number.md) is consequently $\|T^{-1}\|$, with the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) on the [spline](spline-mathematics.md) space and the maximum [norm](norm.md) on its [coefficients](coefficient.md). The [uniform-norm stability of a B-spline basis](uniform-norm-stability-of-a-b-spline-basis.md) bounds this number independently of the [spline knot sequence](spline-knot-sequence.md) when the order is fixed. This is different from the [condition number](condition-number.md) of a particular sampled [B-spline collocation matrix](b-spline-collocation-matrix.md).
