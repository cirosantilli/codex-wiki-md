# Dynamical phase

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum phase](quantum-phase.md)

For an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) with instantaneous [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) $E(t)$, the [dynamical phase](dynamical-phase.md) is

$$
\delta(T)=-\frac{1}{\hbar}\int_0^T E(t)\,dt.
$$

It comes from the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) and depends on the time spent evolving. In an ideal cycle under the [adiabatic theorem](adiabatic-theorem.md), a periodic choice of instantaneous [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) gives $|\Psi(T)\rangle=e^{i(\delta+\gamma)}|\Psi(0)\rangle$, with [Berry phase](berry-phase.md) $\gamma$.
