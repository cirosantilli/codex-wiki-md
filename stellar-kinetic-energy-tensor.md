# Stellar kinetic-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md)

This tensor records the ordered [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) of the [streaming velocity](streaming-velocity.md) $u_i=\langle v_i\rangle$. It excludes random motion; its vanishing is compatible with a nonzero [stellar pressure-energy tensor](stellar-pressure-energy-tensor.md).
