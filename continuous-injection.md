# Continuous injection

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous map](continuous-map.md)

A [continuous map](continuous-map.md) that is also an [injective function](injective-function.md). A [continuous injection](continuous-injection.md) need not be a [topological embedding](topological-embedding.md): its inverse on its [image](image-of-a-function.md) need not be [continuous](continuous-function.md). If its source is a [compact space](compact-space.md) and its target is a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md), however, the [compact-to-Hausdorff continuous bijection theorem](compact-to-hausdorff-continuous-bijection-theorem.md) makes that inverse [continuous](continuous-function.md).
