# Rod containment and simultaneity

↑ **Parent:** [Relativity of simultaneity](relativity-of-simultaneity.md)

A moving rod can have both ends inside a shorter shed on a single constant-time slice of the shed frame. [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) sends the two simultaneous endpoint events to different times in the rod frame; it does not imply simultaneous containment there. Entrance and first collision can be spacelike separated and occur in reversed time order. After impact, length changes must propagate through the rod causally; a perfectly rigid instantaneous stopping model is not relativistically admissible.
