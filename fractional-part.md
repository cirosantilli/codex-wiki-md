# Fractional part

↑ **Parent:** [Floor function](floor-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_part)

The fractional part of a [real number](real-number.md) is

$$
\{x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor\in[0,1).
$$

It is the amount left after removing the lower enclosing [integer](integer-split.md), even for negative inputs. Thus $\{-2.7\}=-2.7-(-3)=0.3$. It is not the signed string of digits obtained by [truncation toward zero](truncation-toward-zero.md).

Translation by an [integer](integer-split.md) leaves the [fractional part](fractional-part.md) unchanged: $\{x+m\}=\{x\}$. It is therefore a [periodic function](periodic-function.md) with period one, increasing from zero toward one on each unit [interval](interval-mathematics.md), then returning to zero at the next [integer](integer-split.md). In [Euclidean division](euclidean-division.md) $N=kq+r$ with $0\leq r<k$, $\{N/k\}=r/k$; for $102/20$, this is $2/20=0.1$.

The notation is useful for following repeated motion modulo an [integer](integer-split.md). In [normality and equidistribution under integer multiplication](normality-and-equidistribution-under-integer-multiplication.md), the values $\{K^n x\}$ shift the digits of a [positional notation](positional-notation.md); the [fractional part](fractional-part.md) discards the leading [integer](integer-split.md) portion without losing the remaining digits. Its braces must be distinguished by context from the braces denoting a [set](set-split.md).
