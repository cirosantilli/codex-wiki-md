# Kernel of the cube face-axis action

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational symmetry group of a cube](rotational-symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md)

Permuting the three pairs of opposite faces gives a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from the [rotational symmetry group of a cube](rotational-symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md) to $S_3$. Its kernel consists of the identity and the half-turns about the three coordinate axes: these are precisely the diagonal sign matrices with [determinant](determinant.md) one. Thus it is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of order four, a [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md).
