# Rational-phase Type II estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Type II sum](type-ii-sum.md)

Suppose $|a_u|\le\tau(u)$, $|b_d|\le\log(2N)$, $u,d>X\ge1$, $ud\le N$, and $|\theta-a/q|\le q^{-2}$ with $(a,q)=1$. A [dyadic decomposition](dyadic-decomposition.md) reduces $S=\sum a_ub_de(\theta ud)$ to blocks $u\asymp U,d\asymp D$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), [divisor-square summatory bound](divisor-square-summatory-bound.md) and the [reciprocal fractional-part sum near a rational](reciprocal-fractional-part-sum-near-a-rational.md) give

$$
|S_{U,D}|^2\ll UD\left(U+\frac{UD}{q}+q+D\right)\log^6(2N).
$$

The condition $ud\le N$ makes the allowed $u$ values for each pair $d_1,d_2$ an interval, so the [exponential geometric sum bound](exponential-geometric-sum-bound.md) applies despite the hyperbolic cutoff. Since $UD\le N$ and $U,D\gg X$, take square roots and sum the $O(\log^2(2N))$ blocks to get the displayed estimate.
