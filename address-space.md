# Address space

↑ **Parent:** [Virtual memory](virtual-memory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Address_space)

The collection of addresses interpreted in a particular context. An [operating-system process](process-computing.md)'s [address space](address-space.md) includes addresses that may be unmapped or inaccessible; belonging to its numerical range does not imply permission to access them.
