# Line with two origins

↑ **Parent:** [Non-Hausdorff manifold](non-hausdorff-manifold.md)

Take $\mathbb R\times\{0,1\}$ with its [Euclidean topology](euclidean-topology.md) and identify $(t,0)$ with $(t,1)$ for every $t\neq0$, retaining two distinct origins. This [quotient space](quotient-topological-space.md) is locally [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to the [real line](real-line.md) but is not [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md). Any [open](open-set.md) [neighbourhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of its origins have [preimages](preimage.md) containing $(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\times\{0\}$ and $(-\delta,\delta)\times\{1\}$, so a nonzero $|t|<\min(\varepsilon,\delta)$ gives a shared image point. All [equivalence classes](equivalence-class.md) in the original [metric space](metric-space.md) are finite and [closed](closed-set.md). This shows that [closed](closed-set.md) [equivalence classes](equivalence-class.md) do not suffice for a [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) [quotient space](quotient-topological-space.md).
