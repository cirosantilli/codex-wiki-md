<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) for the flux $f(u)=u^2/2$ gives, for distinct states,

$$
\boxed{\dot s=\frac{f(u_-)-f(u_+)}{u_--u_+}=\frac{u_-+u_+}{2}}.
$$

The [characteristic speed](../../../../characteristic-speed.md) is $f'(u)=u$. A causal [entropy shock](../../../../entropy-shock.md) has characteristics entering from both sides, so $u_->\dot s>u_+$, equivalently

$$
\boxed{u_->u_+}.
$$

An upward jump instead resolves into a [rarefaction wave](../../../../rarefaction-wave.md); the jump condition alone does not select the physical weak solution.
