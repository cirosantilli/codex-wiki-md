# Finite-horizon alternating-offer bargaining

↑ **Parent:** [Extensive-form game](extensive-form-game.md)

Two players alternate proposals for dividing a pie, with a fixed final round. Agreement ends the [game](game.md); rejection shrinks the next available pie by a factor $0<\alpha<1$. Final rejection gives both players zero. Offers range continuously over the entire available pie. [Backward induction in games](backward-induction-in-games.md) determines immediate agreement and the proposer's share through the [continuous-offer bargaining acceptance lemma](continuous-offer-bargaining-acceptance-lemma.md) and the [finite-horizon bargaining parity effect](finite-horizon-bargaining-parity-effect.md). Discrete offer grids can change both the boundary behavior and the exact shares.
