# Bolometric correction

↑ **Parent:** [Luminosity](luminosity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolometric_correction)

The adjustment from an astronomical magnitude in a particular observational band to the bolometric magnitude, accounting for energy outside that band. It depends on the [stellar spectrum](stellar-spectrum.md), hence on [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md), composition and surface gravity. A ratio of observed single-band [luminosities](luminosity.md) should not be inserted into the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) as a bolometric ratio without allowing for different corrections.
