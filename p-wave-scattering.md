# P-wave scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Partial-wave scattering](partial-wave-scattering.md)

The [partial-wave scattering](partial-wave-scattering.md) channel with [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) quantum number one. Its angular dependence is built from degree-one [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md). For a finite-range potential away from a threshold pole, the [finite-range partial-wave threshold law](finite-range-partial-wave-threshold-law.md) gives phase shift $\delta_1=O(k^3)$ and a partial elastic [scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md) proportional to $k^4$ as the relative [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ tends to zero.
