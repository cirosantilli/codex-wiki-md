# Presheaf (category theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Functor category](functor-category.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presheaf_(category_theory))

A presheaf on a [category](category-split.md) $\mathcal C$ is a [functor](functor.md) $X:\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set}$. A [morphism](morphism.md) $f:C\to D$ induces the restriction map $X(f):X(D)\to X(C)$, reversing direction. The indexing [category](category-split.md) need not be small. For the [category](category-split.md) of open subsets of a [topological space](topological-space.md), this specializes to a [presheaf of sets on a topological space](presheaf-of-sets-on-a-topological-space.md).
