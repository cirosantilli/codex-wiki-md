# Anxiety management

↑ **Parent:** [Youth anxiety treatment](youth-anxiety-treatment.md)

Strategies intended to recognise, tolerate or regulate [anxiety](anxiety.md), including [coping](coping.md) routines, relaxation and [breathing exercises](breathing-exercise.md). These should be distinguished from targeted tests of threat predictions. Whether a technique supports approach or becomes a perceived safety requirement depends on its use, and efficacy of a [CBT](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md) package does not isolate the technique's effect.[Clinical Child and Family Psychology Review 23, 102–121; 75 studies, 111 CBT conditions and component meta-regressions](a-meta-analysis-to-guide-the-enhancement-of-cbt-for-childhood-anxiety-exposure-over-anxiety-management-by-stephen-p-h-whiteside-and-others.md)
