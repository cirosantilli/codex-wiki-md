# Neutron diffraction

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron scattering](neutron-scattering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutron_diffraction)

In [neutron diffraction](neutron-diffraction.md), [coherent elastic scattering](coherent-elastic-scattering.md) adds [scattering amplitudes](scattering-amplitude.md) from regularly positioned scatterers. Since $E_f=E_i$, the [neutron scattering kinematics](neutron-scattering-kinematics.md) gives $k_f=k_i=2\pi/\lambda$ and

$$
Q=\frac{4\pi}{\lambda}\sin\theta.
$$

For [lattice planes](lattice-plane.md) of [interplanar spacing](interplanar-spacing.md) $d_{\mathrm p}$, constructive addition requires $Q=2\pi n/d_{\mathrm p}$ along the plane normal. Equating these expressions proves [Bragg's law](bragg-s-law.md):

$$
2d_{\mathrm p}\sin\theta=n\lambda.
$$

For example, stipulate $d_{\mathrm p}=2$ [ångströms](angstrom.md) and $\lambda=2$ [ångströms](angstrom.md). The first [diffraction order](diffraction-order.md) has $\theta=30^\circ$ and detector deflection $60^\circ$. A peak position constrains a period; its [intensity](intensity-physics.md) also depends on the [crystal basis structure factor](crystal-basis-structure-factor.md).

[Clifford Glenwood Shull](clifford-glenwood-shull.md) describes powdered [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md), a [crystal](crystal.md) [monochromator](monochromator.md), and a shielded counter recording angular counts. Inserting [cadmium](cadmium.md) measured background; substantial diffuse scattering complicated peak interpretation.[1994 lecture, pp. 100–103, Figs. 3–4](early-development-of-neutron-scattering-by-clifford-glenwood-shull.md)

Powdering supplies many [crystal](crystal.md) orientations. Comparing the angular peaks with [Bragg's law](bragg-s-law.md) constrains [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md); a background estimate helps distinguish specimen peaks from unwanted counts. A two-axis diffraction instrument has no separate outgoing [energy](energy-split.md) analysis, so an angular pattern alone does not separate every inelastic contribution. The richer magnetic example, including a warm/cold comparison and its limits, is the [manganese monoxide neutron diffraction experiment](manganese-monoxide-neutron-diffraction-experiment.md). Its extra peaks reveal [magnetic order](magnetic-order.md) beyond the chemical periodicity; the associated [magnetic neutron diffraction](magnetic-neutron-diffraction.md) model is developed there.
