# Residual estimate of Gaussian noise variance

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md)

For a correctly specified full-rank fixed-design Gaussian [linear regression](linear-regression-split.md) with $p<n$, dividing the [residual sum of squares](residual-sum-of-squares.md) by the [residual degrees of freedom](residual-degrees-of-freedom.md) $n-p$ gives an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of the noise [variance](variance-split.md). The projection removes the whole deterministic mean, so the numerator's expectation is $(n-p)\sigma^2$. A misspecified subset model need not have this property.
