# Hyperplane class

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperplane line bundle](hyperplane-line-bundle.md)

The [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md) of the [hyperplane line bundle](hyperplane-line-bundle.md). On [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md) it generates degree-two [integral cohomology](integral-cohomology.md), and on $\mathbb{CP}^n$ its top power evaluates as one on the complex-oriented [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md).
