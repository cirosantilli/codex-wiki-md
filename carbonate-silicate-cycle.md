# Carbonate-silicate cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Solid Earth](solid-earth.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbonate–silicate_cycle)

A long-term carbon cycle coupling silicate weathering, carbonate formation and interior recycling with volcanic carbon dioxide supply. Its temperature- and water-dependent weathering can provide a climate feedback. Active [plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md) can support recycling, but the effectiveness of the feedback also depends on rock exposure, water and reaction rates.
