# One-dimensional second-Wasserstein convergence criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Second Wasserstein distance](second-wasserstein-distance.md)

For probability measures $\mu_n,\mu$ on the real line with finite second moments, $W_2(\mu_n,\mu)\to0$ exactly when $\mu_n$ converges [weakly](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) to $\mu$ and their second moments converge. The common-quantile coupling is optimal and satisfies

$$
W_2(\mu_n,\mu)^2
=\int_0^1|F_{\mu_n}^{-1}(u)-F_\mu^{-1}(u)|^2\,du.
$$
