# BV translation estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

For a smooth function, integrate its directional [derivative](derivative.md) along each segment of length $|h|$ and then integrate in space. This gives the displayed bound with $\int|\nabla w|$. Smooth BV approximation and [sequential lower semicontinuity](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md) give the same inequality for general functions in the [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md). Averaging it over a [mollifier](mollifier.md) proves the [BV mollification error estimate](bv-mollification-error-estimate.md).
