# Double of a manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold](topological-manifold.md)

Glue two copies of a compact [manifold](topological-manifold.md) along their boundaries to form its double. A collar gives the double a smooth structure when $W$ is smooth. Folding the copies onto $W$ gives a [retraction](retraction.md); in particular, inclusion of either copy is injective on [homology](homology-split.md). For an oriented [manifold](topological-manifold.md), use the opposite [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) on the second copy.
