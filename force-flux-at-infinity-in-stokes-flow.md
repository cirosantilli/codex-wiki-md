# Force flux at infinity in Stokes flow

↑ **Parent:** [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md)

In a three-dimensional exterior [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) with no [body force](body-force.md), $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma=0$. The [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) on the fluid between a rigid body and a large sphere $S_R$ gives the displayed force on the body, where $\mathbf n$ points outward from the sphere. The body's outward normal points oppositely to the inner normal of this fluid domain. A nonzero resultant [force](force-split.md) produces a leading [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md), so [velocity](velocity.md) is $O(R^{-1})$, gauge-fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md) is $O(R^{-2})$ and [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md) is $O(R^{-2})$. The finite force is therefore compatible with pointwise decay of the [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md). Force-free bodies may have faster decay.
