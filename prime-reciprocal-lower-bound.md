# Prime reciprocal lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Euler proof that the sum of reciprocals of primes diverges](euler-proof-that-the-sum-of-reciprocals-of-primes-diverges.md)

For $x\geq2$, the [harmonic number](harmonic-number.md) $H_{\lfloor x\rfloor}$ exceeds $\log x$. The finite [Euler product](euler-product.md) over the [primes](prime-number.md) at most $x$ contains every summand of this [harmonic number](harmonic-number.md). Taking [logarithms](logarithm.md) and applying the [geometric-series bound for the logarithmic remainder](geometric-series-bound-for-the-logarithmic-remainder.md) bounds the total remainder by $\frac12\sum_{n=2}^\infty[n(n-1)]^{-1}=1/2$, proving the displayed bound.
