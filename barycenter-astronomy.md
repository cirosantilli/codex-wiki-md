# Barycenter (astronomy)

↑ **Parent:** [Center of mass](center-of-mass.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barycenter_(astronomy))

The [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) of an astronomical system. In an isolated [two-body problem](two-body-problem.md), both bodies follow [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) around their shared astronomical barycenter; the [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) of their relative displacement has a [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) equal to the sum of their individual [semi-major axes](semi-major-axis.md).
