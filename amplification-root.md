# Amplification root

↑ **Parent:** [Amplification polynomial of a multistep method](amplification-polynomial-of-a-multistep-method.md)

An amplification root is a [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) governing a geometric mode $y_n=\xi^n$ of a numerical recurrence on the [Dahlquist test equation](dahlquist-test-equation.md). Its [modulus](modulus.md) measures damping or growth per step. A repeated root produces additional modes such as $n\xi^n$; therefore a [unit-circle root](unit-circle-root.md) can be permitted by a bounded-root condition only when it is simple. The principal root approximates $e^z$ near zero, while additional roots can describe [parasitic amplification roots](parasitic-amplification-root.md).
