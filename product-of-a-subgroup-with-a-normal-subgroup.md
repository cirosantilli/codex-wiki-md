# Product of a subgroup with a normal subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)

If $H$ is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) and $K$ a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of a [group](group-split.md) $G$, their product set $HK=\{hk:h\in H,k\in K\}$ is a [subgroup](subgroup.md). Normality gives $(h_1k_1)(h_2k_2)=h_1h_2(h_2^{-1}k_1h_2)k_2\in HK$ and $(hk)^{-1}=h^{-1}(hk^{-1}h^{-1})\in HK$. Together with the [identity element](identity-element.md) this proves the [subgroup test](subgroup-test.md). For finite [subgroups](subgroup.md), $|HK|=|H||K|/|H\cap K|$: the pairs representing one product differ exactly by multiplying the first factor by an element of $H\cap K$ and its second factor by its inverse.
