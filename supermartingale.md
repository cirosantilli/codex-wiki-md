# Supermartingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermartingale)

An integrable adapted process $(V_n)$ is a supermartingale when $\mathbb E[V_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\leq V_n$.
