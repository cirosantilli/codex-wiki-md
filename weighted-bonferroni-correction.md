# Weighted Bonferroni correction

↑ **Parent:** [Bonferroni correction](bonferroni-correction.md)

For positive deterministic weights $w_i$, the weighted Bonferroni correction rejects $H_i$ when

$$
p_i\leq\frac{\alpha w_i}{\sum_jw_j}.
$$

The [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) over the true nulls controls the [familywise error rate](familywise-error-rate.md) without an independence assumption.
