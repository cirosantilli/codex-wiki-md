# Magnus effect

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnus_effect)

The [Magnus effect](magnus-effect.md) is a transverse [force](force-split.md) on a spinning body in relative motion through a [fluid](fluid.md). Rotation changes the surrounding [circulation](circulation-physics.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md) distribution and wake. In a two-dimensional cylinder model, the [Kutta–Joukowski theorem](kutta-joukowski-theorem.md) gives [force](force-split.md) per unit span $\mathbf F'=\rho\mathbf U\times(\Gamma\hat{\mathbf z})$, with relative [fluid](fluid.md) [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf U$ and [circulation](circulation-physics.md) $\Gamma$ positive anticlockwise viewed from $+z$. Real [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) and boundary-layer behavior help determine the [circulation](circulation-physics.md); impermeability alone does not determine it from spin rate.
