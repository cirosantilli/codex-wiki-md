# Uniform absolute convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md)

A [series](series-mathematics.md) of [functions](function-split.md) is uniformly absolutely convergent on a [set](set-split.md) if the series of their [absolute values](absolute-value.md) has [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) there. A sufficient condition is the [Weierstrass M-test](weierstrass-m-test.md): if $|f_n(x)|\leq M_n$ for every $x$ and $\sum_n M_n<\infty$, then the tail is bounded uniformly by $\sum_{n\geq N}M_n\to0$. This also proves [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) of the original [series](series-mathematics.md) by the [Uniform Cauchy criterion](uniform-cauchy-criterion.md).
