# Rectangular contour for an exponential beta integral

↑ **Parent:** [Residue theorem](residue-theorem.md)

For $0<a<1$, integrate $e^{az}/(1+e^z)$ around a rectangle of imaginary height $2\pi$. Its single pole at $\pi i$ has residue $-e^{\pi ia}$, the top-edge integral is $-e^{2\pi ia}$ times the bottom-edge integral, and both vertical sides vanish at large real width. The [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md) gives $\int_{\mathbb R}e^{ax}/(1+e^x)\,dx=\pi/\sin(\pi a)$. Substitution $t=e^x$ gives the equivalent integral $\int_0^\infty t^{a-1}/(1+t)\,dt$.
