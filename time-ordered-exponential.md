# Time-ordered exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Time ordering](time-ordering.md)

The time-ordered exponential solves $i\hbar\partial_tU=H(t)U$ with initial condition $U(t_0,t_0)=I$. Its expansion is the [Dyson series](dyson-series.md). For bounded continuous [Hamiltonian operators](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) the series converges, as in [differentiation of the Dyson time-ordered exponential](differentiation-of-the-dyson-time-ordered-exponential.md). If the generators commute at all times, time ordering is unnecessary and an ordinary operator exponential suffices.
