# Lubrication gravity-current flux

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

For a thin viscous [gravity current](gravity-current.md) with [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) $p=\rho g(h-z)$, a [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md) at its base and a [stress-free boundary condition](stress-free-boundary-condition.md) at its top, horizontal momentum balance gives $u=(g/\nu)h_x(z^2/2-hz)$. Integrating across the depth gives the [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $q=-(g/3\nu)h^3h_x$. Horizontal spreading follows $h_t+q_x=0$ if the substrate is impermeable.
