# Eckmann-Hilton argument

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental group of a topological group](fundamental-group-of-a-topological-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eckmann–Hilton_argument)

If two unital operations on one set share their identity and satisfy $(a*b)\cdot(c*d)=(a\cdot c)*(b\cdot d)$, then the operations coincide and are commutative.
