# Similarity exponents of a draining gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Draining gravity current](draining-gravity-current.md)

If a [draining gravity current](draining-gravity-current.md) is supplied with [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) proportional to $t^\alpha$, a first-kind [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) has

$$
H=t^aF(x/t^b),
\qquad
a=\frac{2\alpha+1}{5},
\qquad
b=\frac{3\alpha+4}{5},
$$

provided

$$
\beta=\frac1a=\frac5{2\alpha+1}.
$$

These relations follow by balancing the time derivative, horizontal [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) flux, vertical drainage, and imposed inlet flux.
