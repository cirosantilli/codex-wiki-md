# Multiplicity of a zero

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md)

A [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) that is not identically zero has a zero of multiplicity $m$ at $z_0$ if $f(z)=(z-z_0)^m h(z)$ for a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $h$ with $h(z_0)\ne0$. Its [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) identifies $m$ as the first nonzero coefficient index. Logarithmic differentiation gives $f'/f=m/(z-z_0)+h'/h$, so the [residue](residue.md) of $f'/f$ is $m$. Counting zeros with [zero multiplicity](multiplicity-of-a-zero.md) repeats that zero $m$ times.
