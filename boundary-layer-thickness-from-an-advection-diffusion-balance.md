# Boundary-layer thickness from an advection-diffusion balance

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous boundary layer](viscous-boundary-layer.md)

For streamwise length $L$, outer speed $U$, and layer thickness $\delta$, balancing $u u_x\sim U^2/L$ with $\nu u_{yy}\sim\nu U/\delta^2$ gives

$$
\frac\delta L\sim\operatorname{Re}^{-1/2},
\qquad
\operatorname{Re}=\frac{UL}{\nu}.
$$
