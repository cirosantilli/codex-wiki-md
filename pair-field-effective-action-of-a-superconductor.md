# Pair-field effective action of a superconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Microscopic derivation of Ginzburg-Landau theory](microscopic-derivation-of-ginzburg-landau-theory.md)

A pairing-channel [Hubbard–Stratonovich transformation](hubbard-stratonovich-transformation.md) replaces an attractive fermion interaction by a complex [Cooper pair](cooper-pair.md) field coupled to fermion pairs. A [Grassmann Gaussian integral](grassmann-gaussian-integral.md) then gives a trace-log effective [action](action.md), with its trace convention fixed to avoid doubling independent spin-pair blocks. The uniform stationary equation is the [BCS gap equation](bcs-gap-equation.md). A small-field, slow-gradient expansion near the transition yields [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md); the normalization of the phenomenological field need not equal that of the microscopic gap.
