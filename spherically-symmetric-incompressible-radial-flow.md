# Spherically symmetric incompressible radial flow

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

For radial flow outside a sphere, [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) makes

$$
Q(t)=4\pi r^2u_r(r,t)
$$

independent of radius. Its velocity potential is $\phi=-Q/(4\pi r)$.
