# Entropy continuity from Jordan decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md)

For [density operators](density-matrix.md) in dimension $d$, let $\delta=\tfrac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1$. The positive and negative parts of their difference have the same trace $\delta$. Normalize them as $\tau_+,\tau_-$ and write $(\rho+\delta\tau_-)/(1+\delta)=(\sigma+\delta\tau_+)/(1+\delta)$. Applying the two [entropy bounds for a quantum mixture](entropy-bounds-for-a-quantum-mixture.md) to this common state proves the displayed bound. The function $g(\delta)=(1+\delta)h_2(\delta/(1+\delta))$ is increasing and concave, with $g(0)=0$, which also controls average entropy errors in pure-source compression.
