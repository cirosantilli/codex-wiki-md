# Disconnected spectrum yields a nontrivial invariant subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Riesz projection](riesz-projection.md)

For a bounded operator on a complex [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), partition a disconnected spectrum into two nonempty compact pieces. The [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) equal to one near the first piece and zero near the second defines a [Riesz projection](riesz-projection.md). The [holomorphic spectral mapping theorem](holomorphic-spectral-mapping-theorem.md) makes it neither zero nor identity. Its closed range is a proper nonzero invariant [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md). This need not be an orthogonal projection, and the argument does not require a normal operator.
