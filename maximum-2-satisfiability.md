# Maximum 2-satisfiability

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md)

Maximum 2-satisfiability asks for an assignment satisfying as many [clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) occurrences as possible when every nonempty [clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) has at most two [literals](boolean-literal.md). Occurrences are counted even when a [clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) is repeated. Its decision problem is [NP-complete](np-completeness.md) by the [seven-clause gadget for MAX-2SAT](seven-clause-gadget-for-max-2sat.md), while the [method of conditional probabilities](method-of-conditional-probabilities.md) gives a polynomial-time half approximation.
