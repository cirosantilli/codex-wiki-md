# Flux homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic isotopy](symplectic-isotopy.md)

For a symplectic isotopy generated by $X_t$, its flux is

$$
\operatorname{Flux}(\phi_t)=\left[\int_0^1\iota_{X_t}\omega\,dt\right]\in H^1(M;\mathbb R).
$$

It vanishes for a [Hamiltonian isotopy](hamiltonian-isotopy.md).
