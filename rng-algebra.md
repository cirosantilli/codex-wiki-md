# Rng (algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rng_(algebra))

A rng has an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) under [addition](addition.md), an [associative](associative-property.md) [multiplication](multiplication.md), and the [distributive property](distributive-property.md); a multiplicative [identity element](identity-element.md) is not required. Every [ring](ring.md) under this wiki's unital convention is a rng. A [nonunital ring](rng-algebra.md) may happen to have an identity: the terminology removes an axiom rather than imposing the absence of an identity. For example, the [even integers](even-number.md) with their usual operations have no multiplicative identity.
