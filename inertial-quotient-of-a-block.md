# Inertial quotient of a block

↑ **Parent:** [Defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md)

For a maximal [Brauer pair](brauer-pair.md) $(D,b_D)$ of a [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md), the inertial quotient is the displayed quotient of its pair stabilizer by $DC_G(D)$. It has order coprime to $p$, and its order is the [inertial index of a block](inertial-index-of-a-block.md). For abelian $D$, conjugation embeds it in $\operatorname{Aut}(D)$.
