# Alpha-particle assault frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha decay](alpha-decay.md)

In a semiclassical escape model, an [alpha-particle assault frequency](alpha-particle-assault-frequency.md) is the interior rate scale multiplying the barrier [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md). Confinement alone gives $p\sim\hbar/L$ and hence $\nu\sim\hbar/(m_\alpha L^2)$ up to geometric factors. Counting encounters with either wall differs by a factor of two from returns to one wall. A stationary bound [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) does not itself follow a classical collisional trajectory.
