# Bucharest Early Intervention Project

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological research methods](psychological-research-methods.md)

A randomized study of a supported foster-care intervention for young children already living in institutions. The randomized offer supplies evidence about changing care; subsequent care changes, adherence and attrition affect the estimand and analysis. Mediator claims about attachment need assumptions beyond randomization of the whole package.[2012, design and attachment analysis](attachment-security-as-a-mechanism-linking-foster-care-placement-to-improved-mental-health-outcomes-in-previously-institutionalized-children-by-katie-a-mclaughlin-and-others.md)
