# Reverse yielding

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic unloading](elastic-unloading.md)

Renewed plastic deformation during unloading or reversed loading when the [stress](stress.md) reaches the opposite part of the [yield surface](yield-surface.md). For the [spherical elastoplastic cavity](spherical-elastoplastic-cavity.md) with maximum [pressure](pressure-split.md) $P_m>4\tau_Y/3$, [elastic unloading](elastic-unloading.md) first reaches the opposite [Tresca yield criterion](tresca-yield-criterion.md) at the cavity wall after a [pressure](pressure-split.md) decrease $8\tau_Y/3$. Purely elastic return to zero [pressure](pressure-split.md) is possible when $P_m\leq8\tau_Y/3$, with equality reaching the [yield surface](yield-surface.md) exactly at the endpoint.
