# Perron family of subharmonic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Perron method](perron-method.md)

A nonempty collection of real continuous [subharmonic functions](subharmonic-function.md) on a connected [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md) is a Perron family if it is closed under pairwise maxima and [harmonic lifting of a continuous subharmonic function](harmonic-lifting-of-a-continuous-subharmonic-function.md) on every relatively compact coordinate disk. Its pointwise supremum is either everywhere infinite or a [harmonic function on a Riemann surface](harmonic-function-on-a-riemann-surface.md). At a point where the supremum is finite, take increasing disk lifts approaching that value. The [Harnack inequality for harmonic functions](harnack-inequality-for-harmonic-functions.md) gives a finite harmonic limit on the disk. Lifting their maxima with any other member gives a larger harmonic limit with the same value at the chosen point, hence the same limit by the [strong maximum principle for harmonic functions](strong-maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md). The supremum is therefore harmonic on that disk; connectedness propagates finiteness to the whole surface.
