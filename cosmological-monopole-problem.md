# Cosmological monopole problem

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

The [cosmological monopole problem](cosmological-monopole-problem.md) is the excessive relic density of stable [magnetic monopoles](magnetic-monopole.md) produced in some early-universe symmetry-breaking scenarios. [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md) dilutes their [number density](number-density.md) as $a^{-3}$. Successful later [reheating](reheating.md) must also avoid recreating an excessive abundance.
