# Weighted voting game

↑ **Parent:** [Transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md)

A weighted voting game assigns player weights and a threshold, and declares [coalitions](coalition-game-theory.md) winning when their total weight passes that threshold. The strict $>t$ convention is equivalent to quota $t+1$ for integer weights; another common convention writes a weak inequality directly. Nontrivial examples are [simple cooperative games](simple-cooperative-game.md). Having nonnegative weights does not guarantee a [convex cooperative game](convex-cooperative-game.md): two-of-three majority supplies a counterexample.
