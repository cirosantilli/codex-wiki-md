# Pell equation

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine approximation](diophantine-approximation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pell_equation)

For a nonsquare positive integer $D$, the Pell equation is

$$
x^2-Dy^2=1.
$$

Periodic [continued fractions](continued-fraction.md) of $\sqrt D$ produce infinitely many integer solutions.
