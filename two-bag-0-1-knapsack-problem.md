# Two-bag 0-1 knapsack problem

↑ **Parent:** [0-1 knapsack problem](0-1-knapsack-problem.md)

Assign each item to neither bag or exactly one of two bags, respecting both capacities and maximizing total value. With nonnegative integer capacities $W_1,W_2$, a [dynamic programming state](dynamic-programming-state.md) indexed by the item prefix and both capacities gives $O(n(W_1+1)(W_2+1))$ [time complexity](time-complexity.md). Every transition uses the preceding item layer to prevent reuse of an item.
