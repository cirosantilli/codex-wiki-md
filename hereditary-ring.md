# Hereditary ring

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

A ring is left hereditary if every submodule of a left [projective module](projective-module.md) is projective. Equivalently, every left module has a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) of length at most one. The [standard projective resolution of a quiver representation](standard-projective-resolution-of-a-quiver-representation.md), used also for arbitrary infinite-dimensional modules, proves that [path algebras](path-algebra.md) are left hereditary.
