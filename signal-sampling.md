# Signal sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Signal processing](signal-processing.md)

[Signal sampling](signal-sampling.md) records a continuous signal at discrete times. Uniform sampling creates shifted frequency spectra separated by the sample rate; avoiding overlap requires suitable bandwidth limits and an [anti-aliasing filter](anti-aliasing-filter.md). The [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md) describes exact reconstruction for an ideal band-limited signal.
