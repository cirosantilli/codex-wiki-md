# Presheaf of sets on a topological space

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)

A presheaf of sets on a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$ is a contravariant functor from the inclusion-ordered category $\mathcal O(X)$ of open subsets to $\mathbf{Set}$. Its maps $F(U)\to F(V)$ for $V\subseteq U$ are restriction maps.
