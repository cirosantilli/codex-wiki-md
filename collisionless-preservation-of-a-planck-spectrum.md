# Collisionless preservation of a Planck spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md)

Let the scale factor grow by $f$ from an initially thermal [photon](photon.md) distribution. [geodesic](geodesic.md) redshift gives $\nu_i=f\nu$, and conserving [photons](photon.md) in corresponding comoving [frequency](frequency.md) bins gives $n_\nu(t)=f^{-2}n_{f\nu}(t_i)$. Inserting [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md) leaves the same spectral form at [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T_i/f$. Total photon-number conservation alone would not prove that the entire [radiation spectrum](radiation-spectrum.md) stays Planckian; [frequency](frequency.md) redshift and collisionless propagation are also required. Absorption, injection or spectral mixing can invalidate this one-temperature argument.
