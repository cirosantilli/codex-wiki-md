# Entropy gain from impact and thermal equilibration

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy production](entropy-production.md)

A body of mass $m$, [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) $c$, [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T_i$ and speed $v$ that stops in a large reservoir at $T_L$ produces [entropy](entropy-split.md) $mc[\log(T_L/T_i)+T_i/T_L-1]+mv^2/(2T_L)$, assuming constant [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) and no phase changes. The first term follows by adding the body's logarithmic [entropy](entropy-split.md) change to the reservoir's [heat](heat-split.md) gain; the second is kinetic-energy dissipation. A numerical answer needs the reservoir [temperature](temperature-split.md).
