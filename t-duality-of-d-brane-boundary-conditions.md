# T-duality of D-brane boundary conditions

↑ **Parent:** [T-duality](t-duality.md)

For a compact [string embedding map](string-embedding-map.md) coordinate, [T-duality](t-duality.md) preserves its left-moving part and reverses its right-moving part. Consequently $\partial_\tau\widetilde X=\partial_\sigma X$ and $\partial_\sigma\widetilde X=\partial_\tau X$. A [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) becomes a [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) and conversely. A [D-brane](d-brane.md) wrapping the circle loses one spatial worldvolume dimension; a [D-brane](d-brane.md) transverse to it gains one in the dual description.
