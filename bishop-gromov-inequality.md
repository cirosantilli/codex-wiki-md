# Bishop-Gromov inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop–Gromov_inequality)

For a complete connected $n$-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) with nonnegative [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md), the displayed ratio is nonincreasing, tends to one at zero, and is at most one. In particular $\operatorname{Vol}B(p,r)\leq\omega_nr^n$. In [geodesic polar coordinates](geodesic-polar-coordinates.md), the radial [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) determinant is at most the Euclidean density $r^{n-1}$ until the cut time. The trace of the [radial Riccati equation for distance spheres](radial-riccati-equation-for-distance-spheres.md), with [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), proves this comparison. Equality for every radius forces the Euclidean polar metric and absence of a [cut locus](cut-locus.md).
