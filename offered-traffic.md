# Offered traffic

↑ **Parent:** [Loss network](loss-network.md)

For calls arriving at rate $\nu_r$ with mean holding time $1/\mu_r$, offered traffic is the dimensionless load $\alpha_r=\nu_r/\mu_r$, often measured in Erlangs. The [Erlang loss formula](erlang-loss-formula.md) distinguishes this offered traffic from the mean traffic actually carried.
