# Sum-frequency generation

↑ **Parent:** [Three-wave mixing](three-wave-mixing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sum-frequency_generation)

A [nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md) process producing a field at the sum of two input [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md). The driven [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) contains a term proportional to the product of the input [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md). With undepleted plane-wave inputs, integration along a crystal gives an output amplitude proportional to $L e^{i\Delta kL/2}\operatorname{sinc}(\Delta kL/2)$; the [sinc function](sinc-function.md) therefore determines the ideal length and [phase mismatch](phase-mismatch.md) dependence. A nonzero initial output field also interferes with this generated field.
