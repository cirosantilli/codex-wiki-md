# Reductive alpha-alkylation of an enone

↑ **Parent:** [Enolate alkylation](enolate-alkylation.md)

Conjugate reduction followed by carbon-electrophile trapping of the resulting [enolate](enolate.md). The original enone alpha position determines the alkylation site before any later equilibration.
