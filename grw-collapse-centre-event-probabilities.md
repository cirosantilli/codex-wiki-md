# GRW collapse-centre event probabilities

↑ **Parent:** [GRW localization operator](grw-localization-operator.md)

For equal particle rates in the [GRW model](ghirardi-rimini-weber-theory.md), the next centre falls in $B$ with probability $N^{-1}\sum_i\int_B\|L_i(c)\Psi\|^2dc$. With zero [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), two successive centres in $B$ have joint probability $N^{-2}\sum_{ij}\int_Bdc\int_Bdd\|L_j(d)L_i(c)\Psi\|^2$. Dividing by the first-event probability gives the conditional second event, including repeated particle labels. Spatial probabilities depend on actual packet mass, not solely the nominal packet centres.
