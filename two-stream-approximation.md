# Two-stream approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md)

A two-stream approximation replaces the angular distribution of radiation by upward and downward representative intensities or hemispheric fluxes, with a specified angular closure. Angular path factors can be absorbed into an effective [optical depth](optical-depth.md), producing exponential attenuation in that coordinate. Different closures change numerical emission depths and diffusivity factors. Fluxes and directional radiances must remain distinguished, and a prescribed effective optical depth need not equal literal vertical absorption optical depth.
