# Solid-body rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

In solid-body rotation the [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) is independent of distance from the rotation axis, so tangential speed is proportional to cylindrical radius. A galactic region with this [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md) has [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) $\kappa=2\Omega$.
