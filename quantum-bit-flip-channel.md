# Quantum bit-flip channel

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli channel](pauli-channel.md)

A bit-flip channel applies the [Pauli X gate](pauli-x-gate.md) with [probability](probability.md) $p$ and does nothing otherwise. If the classical error outcome is not recorded, its output is the convex mixture $\mathcal E_p(\rho)=(1-p)\rho+pX\rho X$, not a coherent superposition of the two alternatives.
