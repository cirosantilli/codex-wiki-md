# Kronecker symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kronecker_symbol)

The Kronecker symbol extends the [Jacobi symbol](jacobi-symbol.md) to all integer denominators. For a fundamental discriminant $D$, the function $\chi_D(n)=\left(\frac Dn\right)$ is the primitive quadratic [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) associated with $\mathbb Q(\sqrt D)$.
