# Electroweak triple gauge coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md)

An interaction of three [electroweak gauge bosons](electroweak-gauge-boson.md), especially $W^+W^-\gamma$ and $W^+W^-Z$. Their [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) coefficients and momentum dependence come from the [cubic and quartic Yang-Mills self-interactions](cubic-and-quartic-yang-mills-self-interactions.md) after neutral-field mixing. W-pair production tests their relative strengths through interference with neutrino exchange; effective anomalous couplings require a stated convention and consistency constraints.[2004 abstract: measured charged triple-gauge parameters](measurement-of-triple-gauge-boson-couplings-of-the-w-boson-at-lep-by-l3-collaboration.md)
