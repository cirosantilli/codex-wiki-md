# Hypergeometric cosine identity

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary specialization of a hypergeometric solution](elementary-specialization-of-a-hypergeometric-solution.md)

For compatible local branches near $x=0$,

$$
F\left(\frac{k}{2},-\frac{k}{2};\frac12;\sin^2x\right)=\cos(kx).
$$

It follows by transforming the [harmonic oscillator equation](simple-harmonic-motion.md) $y''+k^2y=0$ into the [Gauss hypergeometric equation](gauss-hypergeometric-equation.md) and selecting the solution with value one and derivative zero at the origin.
