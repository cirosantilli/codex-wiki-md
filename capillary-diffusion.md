# Capillary diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Buckley-Leverett equation](buckley-leverett-equation.md)

Eliminating phase pressures with $p_c=p_n-p_w$ gives $\phi s_t+QF(s)_x=(D_cs_x)_x$. For decreasing [capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md), $D_c\geq0$. This nonlinear diffusion broadens a saturation shock; it can degenerate where a [phase mobility](phase-mobility.md) vanishes. Endpoint conservation retains the limiting [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md).
