# Quasi-norm

↑ **Parent:** [Norm](norm.md)

A quasi-[norm](norm.md) on a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is nonnegative, vanishes only at zero, is absolutely homogeneous, and satisfies a [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) with one constant factor $C\geq1$. For $0<p<1$, $(\int|f|^p)^{1/p}$ on an [Lp space](lp-space.md) is a quasi-[norm](norm.md): $\|f+g\|_p^p\leq\|f\|_p^p+\|g\|_p^p$ gives a quasi-[triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md). Measure-preserving composition preserves this quantity even though the [Minkowski inequality](minkowski-inequality.md) is unavailable below exponent one.
