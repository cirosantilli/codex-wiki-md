# Substrate-depletion pattern mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md)

An autocatalytic species promotes its own growth while consuming a substrate that promotes that growth. Its reaction Jacobian has the displayed signs after choosing the [activator](activator-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md) first. This differs from the [activator](activator-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md)-[inhibitor](inhibitor-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md) sign pattern $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}+&-\\+&-\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, where the [activator](activator-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md) stimulates its [inhibitor](inhibitor-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md). Appropriate magnitudes, diffusivities and admissible spatial modes are needed for a [Turing instability](turing-instability.md); signs alone are insufficient.
