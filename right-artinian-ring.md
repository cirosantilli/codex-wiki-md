# Right Artinian ring

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative algebra](noncommutative-algebra-split.md)

A [ring](ring.md) is right Artinian if its [right ideals](right-ideal.md) satisfy the [descending chain condition](descending-chain-condition.md), equivalently its right regular [module](module-mathematics.md) is an [Artinian module](artinian-module.md). Left and right chain conditions should be distinguished for a general [ring](ring.md).
