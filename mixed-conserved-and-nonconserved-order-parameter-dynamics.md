# Mixed conserved and nonconserved order-parameter dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Order parameter](order-parameter.md)

With constant positive [kinetic coefficient](kinetic-coefficient.md) $\Gamma$ and [mobility](order-parameter-mobility.md) $M$, two independent relaxation channels give

$$
\dot p_j=-\partial_iW_{ij}-\Gamma\mu_j+f_j,
\qquad W_{ij}=-M\partial_i\mu_j+N_{ij},
\qquad\mu_j=\frac{\delta F}{\delta p_j}.
$$

The deterministic mobility operator is $\Gamma-M\nabla^2$. Each channel separately satisfies [microscopic reversibility](microscopic-reversibility.md) when its noise obeys the corresponding [Model A fluctuation-dissipation relation](model-a-fluctuation-dissipation-relation.md) or [fluctuation-dissipation relation for a conserved flux](fluctuation-dissipation-relation-for-a-conserved-flux.md).
