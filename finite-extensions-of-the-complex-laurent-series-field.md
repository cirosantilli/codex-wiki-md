# Finite extensions of the complex Laurent series field

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent series field](laurent-series-field.md)

A finite extension of the complete discretely valued field $\mathbb C((t))$ has residue field $\mathbb C$, hence is totally ramified. If $\pi$ is its uniformizer, write $t=u\pi^n$. A root of the residue of $u$ lifts by [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) to an $n$th root $v$ of $u$, since the residue characteristic is zero. Then $s=v\pi$ satisfies $s^n=t$. Distinct valuations modulo $n$ make $1,s,\ldots,s^{n-1}$ linearly independent over the base, so $L=\mathbb C((t))(s)$. Expanding in $s$ with constant coefficients identifies it with $\mathbb C((s))$. Its automorphisms are $s\mapsto\zeta_ns$, giving a cyclic Galois group.
