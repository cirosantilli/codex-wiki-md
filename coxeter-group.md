# Coxeter group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie theory](lie-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_group)

A Coxeter group is a [group](group-split.md) with a presentation

$$
W=\langle x_i\ (i\in I): (x_ix_j)^{m_{ij}}=1\rangle,
$$

where $m_{ii}=1$ and $m_{ij}=m_{ji}\in\{2,3,\ldots,\infty\}$ for $i\ne j$. The pair consisting of $W$ and its distinguished generating reflections is a [Coxeter system](coxeter-system.md).
