# Buoyancy perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy](buoyancy.md)

A buoyancy perturbation is the departure $b'$ from a prescribed basic buoyancy field. In a fluid with constant [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) $N$, vertical velocity $w'$ changes it according to $(\partial_t+\mathbf U\cdot\nabla)b'=-N^2w'$.
