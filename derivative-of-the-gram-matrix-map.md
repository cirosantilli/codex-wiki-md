# Derivative of the Gram matrix map

↑ **Parent:** [Transpose](transpose.md)

For real rectangular [matrices](matrix.md), expanding $(A+H)^t(A+H)$ leaves the quadratic remainder $H^tH$, whose [matrix norm](matrix-norm.md) is $O(\|H\|^2)$. Thus the displayed [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md) is a [linear map](linear-map.md) in the increment $H$. At an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md), its kernel consists of increments $H=A\Omega$ with $\Omega$ [skew-symmetric](skew-symmetric-matrix.md). This is the tangent-space condition obtained by differentiating $A^tA=I$.
