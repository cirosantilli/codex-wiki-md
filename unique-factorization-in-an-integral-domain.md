# Unique factorization in an integral domain

↑ **Parent:** [Unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md)

Every nonzero nonunit in a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md) is a finite product of [irreducible elements](irreducible-element.md). Such a factorization is unique up to permutation and multiplication of the factors by units. Irreducibles are [prime elements](prime-element.md), so divisibility can be checked by comparing their multiplicities. This applies to a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) over a [field](field.md) and is useful for intersections of localizations inside its [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md).
