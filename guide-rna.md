# Guide RNA

↑ **Parent:** [CRISPR genome editing](crispr-genome-editing.md)

An [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) molecule whose complementary sequence directs a targeting complex to a nucleic-acid sequence. In [CRISPR genome editing](crispr-genome-editing.md), guide complementarity and a compatible [protospacer adjacent motif](protospacer-adjacent-motif.md) jointly constrain targeting.
