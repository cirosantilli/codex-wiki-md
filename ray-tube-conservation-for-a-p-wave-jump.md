# Ray-tube conservation for a P-wave jump

↑ **Parent:** [P-wavefront discontinuity transport](p-wavefront-discontinuity-transport.md)

A tube of neighboring [elastic rays](elastic-ray.md) follows the [wavefront](wavefront.md) normal. If $J$ is its infinitesimal transverse area, its expansion is $d\log J/ds=\nabla\cdot\mathbf n$ along arclength. The [P-wavefront discontinuity transport](p-wavefront-discontinuity-transport.md) law therefore gives the displayed conserved flux. Geometrical spreading, [mass density](density.md) and [wave speed](wave-speed.md) determine the leading jump [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) together; the rule is valid before the ray tube reaches a [caustic](wave-caustic.md).
