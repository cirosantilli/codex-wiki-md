# Pointwise directed supremum

↑ **Parent:** [Function space of complete partial orders](function-space-of-complete-partial-orders.md)

For a [directed set](directed-set.md) of [Scott continuous maps](scott-continuous-map.md) $f_i:D\to E$, its [least upper bound](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) is $f(x)=\sup_i f_i(x)$. The two directed suprema over $i$ and over inputs commute, proving that $f$ remains Scott continuous. Thus the [function space of complete partial orders](function-space-of-complete-partial-orders.md) is a [complete partial order](complete-partial-order.md).
