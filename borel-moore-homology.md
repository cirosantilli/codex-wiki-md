# Borel-Moore homology

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)

For a locally compact space, replace finite [singular chains](singular-chain.md) by chains for which every compact subset meets the images of only finitely many simplices with nonzero coefficients. The [boundary operator](boundary-operator.md) preserves this local finiteness, giving the Borel-Moore chain complex and its [homology](homology-split.md). On a compact space it reduces to ordinary singular homology. A noncompact manifold has a locally finite [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md), with its [orientation local system](orientation-local-system.md) when necessary. The chain groups and homology are zero in negative degrees.
