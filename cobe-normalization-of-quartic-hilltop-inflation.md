# COBE normalization of quartic hilltop inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic hilltop inflation](quartic-hilltop-inflation.md)

For a negligible tensor contribution, the historical COBE horizon amplitude $\delta_H\sim2\times10^{-5}$ corresponds to curvature power $\mathcal P_{\mathcal R}\sim25\delta_H^2/4$. In [quartic hilltop inflation](quartic-hilltop-inflation.md), $\alpha=\pi^2\mathcal P_{\mathcal R}/(8N^3)$ is of order $10^{-14}$ at $N=50$, independent of the small symmetry-breaking scale at this order. The exact inferred amplitude depends on the late cosmology and tilt used in the COBE transfer calculation.
