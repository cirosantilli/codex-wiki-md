# Electric field at a conducting plane from axial point charges

↑ **Parent:** [Image charge](image-charge.md)

For point [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) at heights $h_j>0$ on one normal above an infinite grounded [conducting plane](conducting-plane.md), the [method of images](method-of-images.md) places opposite [image charges](image-charge.md) at $-h_j$. At the point directly below them, tangential [electric fields](electric-field-split.md) cancel and normal components add, giving the displayed one-sided field. The [electric field](electric-field-split.md) inside the metal is zero instead. The boundary jump is supported by induced [surface charge](surface-charge.md).
