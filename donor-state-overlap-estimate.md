# Donor-state overlap estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Donor impurity](donor-impurity.md)

For shallow [hydrogenic donor states](hydrogenic-donor-state.md) of radius $a_B^*$ and random donor [number density](number-density.md) $N_D$, the mean spacing is of order $N_D^{-1/3}$. Taking this spacing comparable to $a_B^*$ gives $N_D\sim(a_B^*)^{-3}$; a geometric diameter criterion instead gives $N_D\sim(2a_B^*)^{-3}$. The orbitals have exponentially decaying tails rather than hard boundaries, so there is no exact geometric onset. Appreciable overlap also limits the independent-donor ionisation model; a genuine transport transition needs additional electronic physics.
