# Smooth non-displaceability from self-intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian displacement (symplectic geometry)](lagrangian-displacement-symplectic-geometry.md)

For a compact orientable Lagrangian $L$, the identification $NL\cong T^*L$ gives

$$
[L]\cdot[L]=\pm\langle e(TL),[L]\rangle=\pm\chi(L).
$$

If $\chi(L)\ne0$, no smoothly isotopic copy of $L$ can be disjoint from it.
