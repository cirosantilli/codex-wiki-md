# Carbon monosulfide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

A heteronuclear [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md) consisting of [carbon](carbon.md) and [sulfur](sulfur.md). Its permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) permits an electric-dipole [rotational spectrum](rotational-spectrum.md). The [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) model relates its [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md) to its [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) and bond length.
