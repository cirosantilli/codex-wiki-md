# Vacuum photodiode

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

A [photodetector](photodetector.md) in which [light](light.md) releases [electrons](electron.md) from a [cathode](cathode.md) into a [vacuum](vacuum.md), and an [anode](anode.md) collects them. A positive collecting-anode bias relative to the emitting [cathode](cathode.md) drives collection across the [vacuum](vacuum.md) gap.
