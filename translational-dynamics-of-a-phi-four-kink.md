# Translational dynamics of a phi-four kink

↑ **Parent:** [Phi-four kink](phi-four-kink.md)

For $\phi_K(x-X)=c\tanh(c(x-X))$ in the unit kinetic normalization, $\int(\phi_K')^2dx=M=4c^3/3$. Substitution of $X(t)$ therefore gives the free-particle [collective-coordinate effective Lagrangian](collective-coordinate-effective-lagrangian-for-a-soliton.md) $-M+M\dot X^2/2$. Its quantization has [momentum](momentum.md) $p$ and energy $M+p^2/(2M)$ to this order. The exact uniformly moving classical kink is a [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) of the static one, with energy $M/\sqrt{1-v^2}$.
