# Hydraulic flow in an inverted channel

↑ **Parent:** [Hydraulic control](hydraulic-control.md)

A light shallow layer flows below a roof of height $H(x)$ and width $W(x)$ through a deeper approximately motionless ambient. [Hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) and inviscid steady [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) give the displayed specific [energy](energy.md) for constant [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $Q$ and [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $g'$. At fixed position, $E_h=g'(1-\mathrm{Fr}^2)$ and $h_c=[Q^2/(g'W^2)]^{1/3}$. A roof depression raises the minimum admissible energy, just as a bed elevation does for a heavy lower layer.
