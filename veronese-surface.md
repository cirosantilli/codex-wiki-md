# Veronese surface

↑ **Parent:** [Projective variety](projective-variety.md)

The complete system of quadratic forms maps $\mathbb P^2$ to a smooth surface of degree four in $\mathbb P^5$. The six coordinates are $x_0^2,x_0x_1,x_0x_2,x_1^2,x_1x_2,x_2^2$. It occurs as a degree-two image of a [K3 surface](k3-surface.md) and, after taking a projective cone, in the plane-quintic exception to the [K3 linear system dichotomies](k3-linear-system-dichotomies.md).
