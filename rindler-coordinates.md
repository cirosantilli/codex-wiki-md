# Rindler coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rindler_coordinates)

For $\rho>0$, these coordinates cover a wedge of [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) and give $ds^2=-\kappa^2\rho^2dt^2+d\rho^2$ in two dimensions. A constant-$\rho$ observer has proper acceleration $1/\rho$ and the [Unruh effect](unruh-effect.md) temperature $1/(2\pi\rho)$ in natural units. The apparent degeneration at $\rho=0$ is a coordinate horizon.
