# Standard polytabloid

↑ **Parent:** [Polytabloid](polytabloid.md)

A standard polytabloid is the [polytabloid](polytabloid.md) associated with a [standard Young tableau](standard-young-tableau.md). The standard polytabloids of one shape form a basis of the corresponding [Specht module](specht-module.md). For linear independence, sort each row of a [tabloid](tabloid.md) increasingly and compare row-reading words lexicographically. In the column antisymmetrization of a standard tableau, the identity term is its unique least row word: in the first row affected by a nontrivial column permutation, each moved-in entry is larger than the entry it replaces. Hence its coefficient is one, and all other terms are larger. Distinct standard tableaux have distinct least tabloids, so a linear relation is impossible by taking its least nonzero term. Spanning is the straightening part of the standard polytabloid basis theorem.
