# Genome reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Genome](genome.md)

[genome reduction](genome-reduction.md) is evolutionary loss of genomic material and functions. Host complementation can relax selection on some pathways, while transmission bottlenecks and [genetic drift](genetic-drift.md) can also permit deleterious losses; reduction is not necessarily wholly adaptive.
