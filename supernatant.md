# Supernatant

↑ **Parent:** [Centrifugation](centrifugation.md)

A [supernatant](supernatant.md) is the liquid remaining above settled or pelleted material. It can contain dissolved [molecules](molecule.md) and particles that have not reached the [centrifugation pellet](centrifugation-pellet.md); a visually clear [supernatant](supernatant.md) need not be chemically empty.
