# Zeeman spectrum for orbital angular momentum one

↑ **Parent:** [Paschen-Back effect](paschen-back-effect.md)

For [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $\ell=1$ and [spin](spin.md) $s=1/2$, consider

$$
H=\frac{a}{\hbar}(L_z+2S_z)+\frac{2K}{\hbar^2}\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S,\qquad a=\mu_B B.
$$

The [quantum number](quantum-number.md) $m_j=m_\ell+m_s$ is conserved. The stretched states with $m_j=\pm3/2$ have energies $K\pm2a$. The remaining [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) follow from two $2\times2$ blocks with off-diagonal entries $\sqrt2K$:

$$
E_{+}^{(\pm)}=\frac{a-K}{2}\pm\frac12\sqrt{(a+K)^2+8K^2},\qquad
E_{-}^{(\pm)}=\frac{-a-K}{2}\pm\frac12\sqrt{(a-K)^2+8K^2}.
$$

The subscripts denote $m_j=\pm1/2$. For $K>0$, the zero-field multiplets are $j=3/2$ at $K$ and $j=1/2$ at $-2K$. Their [weak-field Zeeman splitting](weak-field-zeeman-splitting.md) is $g_jam_j$, with [Landé g-factors](lande-g-factor.md) $4/3$ and $2/3$, respectively. When $a\gg|K|$, the [Paschen-Back effect](paschen-back-effect.md) gives approximate product states $(m_\ell,m_s)$ and energies $a(m_\ell+2m_s)+2Km_\ell m_s$. Mixing within a fixed $m_j$ causes an [avoided crossing](avoided-crossing.md); different $m_j$ sectors can cross because the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) does not couple them.
