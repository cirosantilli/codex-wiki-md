# Steady gradient Ricci soliton scalar identity

↑ **Parent:** [Ricci soliton](ricci-soliton.md)

For $\operatorname{Ric}+\nabla^2 f=0$, the [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md) and the commutation identity $\Delta\nabla f=\nabla\Delta f+\operatorname{Ric}(\nabla f)$ imply $\nabla R=2\operatorname{Ric}(\nabla f)$. Meanwhile $\nabla|\nabla f|^2=-2\operatorname{Ric}(\nabla f)$, so their sum is constant on every [connected component](connected-component.md).
