# Graph total variation denoising

↑ **Parent:** [Graph total variation](graph-total-variation.md)

Graph total variation denoising is a [penalized least-squares estimator](penalized-least-squares-estimator.md) for noisy vertex signals on a [graph](graph-split.md). The squared loss is strictly [convex](convex-function.md), so the fitted signal is unique. Its [basic inequality for a penalized least-squares estimator](basic-inequality-for-a-penalized-least-squares-estimator.md) separates a noise inner product from the change in penalty. The [incidence pseudoinverse decomposition](incidence-pseudoinverse-decomposition.md) controls the nonconstant noise through the geometry of the [graph](graph-split.md).
