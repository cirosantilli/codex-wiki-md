# Point at infinity of the complex projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Projective line](projective-line.md)

In the affine coordinate $z\mapsto[z:1]$, the complex [projective line](projective-line.md) consists of the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) together with the single extra [projective point](projective-point.md) $[1:0]$, called its point at infinity. It is the only point whose second [homogeneous coordinate](homogeneous-coordinate.md) vanishes. This coordinate choice distinguishes the point; it is not intrinsically special under [projective transformations](projective-linear-transformation.md). The map $[u:v]\mapsto[v:u]$ exchanges zero and infinity. The resulting one-point compactification is the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md).
