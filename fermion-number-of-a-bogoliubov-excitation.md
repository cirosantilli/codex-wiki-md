# Fermion number of a Bogoliubov excitation

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov quasiparticle](bogoliubov-quasiparticle.md)

For a normalized spinless paired [ground state](ground-state.md) $u|0\rangle+i v c_p^\dagger c_{-p}^\dagger|0\rangle$, its mean [fermion number](fermion-number.md) is $2v^2$. The canonical [Bogoliubov quasiparticle](bogoliubov-quasiparticle.md) [creation operator](creation-operator.md) produces $c_p^\dagger|0\rangle$, containing exactly one fermion. With [BCS coherence factors](bcs-coherence-factors.md) $v^2=(1-\epsilon/E)/2$, the change in mean [fermion number](fermion-number.md) is $1-2v^2=\epsilon/E$. This need not be an integer because the pairing [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) preserves [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) while failing to conserve the original [fermion number operator](fermion-number-operator.md). Every measurement of that [number operator](number-operator.md) still has integer outcomes.
