# Dilogarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Polylogarithm](polylogarithm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilogarithm)

The dilogarithm is the order-two [polylogarithm](polylogarithm.md), defined for $|z|<1$ by $\operatorname{Li}_2(z)=\sum_{n\geq1}z^n/n^2$. Its derivative is $-\operatorname{Log}(1-z)/z$, with a removable value at zero; a chosen [complex logarithm](complex-logarithm.md) branch gives its [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md).
