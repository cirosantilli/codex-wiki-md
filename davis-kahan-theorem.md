# Davis-Kahan theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral gap](spectral-gap.md)

The Davis-Kahan theorem bounds the change of an invariant [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) in terms of a matrix perturbation and an appropriate [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md). The [rank-one eigenprojector perturbation bound](rank-one-eigenprojector-perturbation-bound.md) below depends only on the unperturbed gap. [Yu, Wang and Samworth's formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.0680) gives population-gap bounds in greater generality.
