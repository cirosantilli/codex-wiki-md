# Hartree-Fock wavefunction

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

An antisymmetric single [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md) whose occupied orbitals make its energy stationary under occupied-virtual orbital rotations. A stationary solution need not be a stable or global minimum.
