# Viscous attenuation of an internal-wave beam

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

When [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) and [mass diffusivity](mass-diffusivity.md) are equal to $\epsilon$, a monochromatic internal-wave beam with [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ and ray angle $\theta$ has leading stream-function amplitude

$$
A(\zeta)=A(0)\exp\left(-\frac{\epsilon k^3\zeta}{N\cos\theta}\right)
=A(0)e^{-k\zeta/\operatorname{Re}},
\qquad
\operatorname{Re}=\frac{N\cos\theta}{\epsilon k^2}.
$$

The cubic dependence on $k$ makes slope-focused, short internal waves dissipate especially rapidly.
