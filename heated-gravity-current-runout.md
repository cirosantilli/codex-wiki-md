# Heated gravity-current runout

↑ **Parent:** [Heated particle-laden gravity current](heated-particle-laden-gravity-current.md)

In a constant-volume [gravity-current box model](gravity-current-box-model.md) without settling, $\phi=\phi_0$ and $g'=g\phi_0(\gamma-\beta t)$. A constant [Froude number](froude-number.md) then gives finite [runout length of a gravity current](runout-length-of-a-gravity-current.md) at the neutral-buoyancy time $\gamma/\beta$. Further motion requires a different physical model once the [stable density stratification](stable-density-stratification.md) is lost.
