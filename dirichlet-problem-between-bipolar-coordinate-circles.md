# Dirichlet problem between bipolar coordinate circles

↑ **Parent:** [Bipolar coordinates](bipolar-coordinates.md)

For a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) between $\tau=a$ and $\tau=b$, $0<a<b$, in [bipolar coordinates](bipolar-coordinates.md), suppose $\Phi(\sigma,a)=0$ and $\Phi(\sigma,b)=F(\sigma)$. Write the periodic boundary values as a [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

$$
F(\sigma)=F_0+\sum_{n\geq1}[A_n\cos(n\sigma)+B_n\sin(n\sigma)].
$$

[Separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) gives

$$
\Phi(\sigma,\tau)=F_0\frac{\tau-a}{b-a}+\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{\sinh[n(\tau-a)]}{\sinh[n(b-a)]}[A_n\cos(n\sigma)+B_n\sin(n\sigma)].
$$

Each radial factor solves $M''-n^2M=0$ and has endpoint values zero and one; the mean mode solves $M''=0$. Sufficiently regular boundary data give the required [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) solution. [Uniqueness](uniqueness.md) follows from the [maximum principle](maximum-principle.md) for a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) on both circles. The formula follows the coordinate values; $\tau=b$ is the geometrically smaller circle.
