# Transformed Eulerian mean

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformed_Eulerian_mean)

The transformed Eulerian mean combines the Eulerian-mean meridional circulation with an eddy-induced circulation. This moves eddy buoyancy transport out of the mean thermodynamic equation and gathers wave forcing into the divergence of an [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md).
