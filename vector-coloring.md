# Vector coloring

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring.md)

A vector $k$-coloring, for $k>1$, assigns a [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $v_i$ to each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) of a [graph](graph-split.md), such that $\langle v_i,v_j\rangle\leq-1/(k-1)$ on every [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). A [graph colouring](graph-coloring.md) with $k$ colors gives a vector $k$-coloring by placing the colors at the vertices of a [regular simplex](regular-simplex.md). The [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) of these [vectors](vector.md) allows [semidefinite programming](semidefinite-programming.md) to search for such a representation.
