# Reversible heat engine

↑ **Parent:** [Heat engine](heat-engine.md)

An ideal cyclic [heat engine](heat-engine.md) producing no [entropy](entropy-split.md) overall. Between two equilibrium reservoirs its efficiency reaches the Carnot bound; finite reservoir [temperatures](temperature-split.md) can be treated as varying through successive reversible cycles.
