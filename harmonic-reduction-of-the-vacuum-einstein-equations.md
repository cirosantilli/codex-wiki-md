# Harmonic reduction of the vacuum Einstein equations

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic coordinate](harmonic-coordinate.md)

With the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) convention $R^\mu{}_{\nu\rho\sigma}=\partial_\rho\Gamma^\mu{}_{\nu\sigma}-\partial_\sigma\Gamma^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}+\cdots$, [harmonic coordinates](harmonic-coordinate.md) remove the mixed second derivatives from the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md). The remainder $Q_{ab}$ contains at most first derivatives of the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). The vacuum [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) become a system of [quasilinear partial differential equations](quasilinear-partial-differential-equation.md) with a Lorentzian [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) principal part. Harmonic coordinate constraints must be maintained when using the reduced system.
