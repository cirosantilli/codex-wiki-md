# Alidade

↑ **Parent:** [Astrolabe](astrolabe.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alidade)

A sighting rule used to measure a direction or angle. On a conventional [astrolabe](astrolabe.md), it rotates over the back's angular scale; it is distinct from the front rule used to read the projected sky geometry.
