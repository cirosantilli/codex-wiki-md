# Lie derivative of a vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md)

For [vector fields](vector-field.md) $X,Y$, the Lie derivative is the [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) $\mathcal L_XY=[X,Y]$. Pulling $Y$ back by the [local flow](local-flow.md) of $X$ and differentiating gives $dY(X)-dX(Y)$. No [affine connection](affine-connection.md) is needed.
