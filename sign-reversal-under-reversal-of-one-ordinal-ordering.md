# Sign reversal under reversal of one ordinal ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal categorical variable](ordinal-categorical-variable.md)

Reverse the order of exactly one of two [ordinal categorical variables](ordinal-categorical-variable.md). Every [concordant pair](concordant-pair.md) becomes a [discordant pair](discordant-pair.md) and conversely, while pairs tied on either variable remain tied. Therefore the numerator $C-D$ changes sign and the denominator $C+D$ does not: [Goodman and Kruskal's gamma](goodman-and-kruskal-s-gamma.md) changes sign whenever defined. The same argument applies to [Somers' D](somers-d.md), [Kendall's tau-b](kendall-s-tau-b.md) and [Stuart's tau-c](stuart-s-tau-c.md), whose denominators depend on tie counts or table dimensions rather than the orientation. Reversing both orders preserves the sign. This explains why an association's numerical sign must be interpreted using the actual coding directions; it does not change the substantive paired observations. Measures of nominal association such as [Cramér's V](cramer-s-v.md) are invariant under either reversal.
