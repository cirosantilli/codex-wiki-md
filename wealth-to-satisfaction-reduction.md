# Wealth-to-satisfaction reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Consumption satisfaction stock](consumption-satisfaction-stock.md)

For [CRRA utility](constant-relative-risk-aversion-utility.md) of a [consumption satisfaction stock](consumption-satisfaction-stock.md), jointly scaling initial [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md), [consumption satisfaction](consumption-satisfaction-stock.md) and controls gives [homogeneity](homogeneity.md) of degree $1-R$. The [value function](value-function.md) therefore depends on one dimensionless ratio after factoring out $\xi^{1-R}$. The effective [consumption](consumption.md) coefficient becomes $\xi^{-R}[(1-R)v-(x+1)v']$.
