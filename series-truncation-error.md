# Series truncation error

↑ **Parent:** [Truncation error](truncation-error.md)

For a [convergent series](convergent-series.md) with [sum](sum.md) $S$, the error from keeping only the first $N$ terms is the remaining [sum](sum.md) $R_N=\sum_{n>N}a_n$. This [truncation error](truncation-error.md) exists even with exact arithmetic. The [zeta series truncation error](zeta-series-truncation-error.md) gives explicit bounds for positive reciprocal-power terms.
