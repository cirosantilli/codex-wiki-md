# Total duration of a circular planetary transit

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md)

The first-to-fourth-contact duration of an [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md) by a spherical opaque [planet](planet.md) of [radius](radius.md) ratio $k=R_p/R_\star$ on a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md). The [transit impact parameter](transit-impact-parameter.md) is $b=(a/R_\star)\cos i$. The formula includes finite planet size and orbital curvature; it reduces to the [transit duration of a small planet](transit-duration-of-a-small-planet.md) when $k\to0$ and $R_\star/a\ll1$. It describes contact geometry, not a uniquely specified light-curve threshold in the presence of [limb darkening](limb-darkening.md).
