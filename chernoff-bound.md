# Chernoff bound

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration inequality](concentration-inequality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernoff_bound)

For every $\lambda>0$, [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) applied to $e^{\lambda X}$ gives

$$
\mathbb P(X\geq t)\leq e^{-\lambda t}\mathbb E[e^{\lambda X}].
$$

Optimizing over $\lambda$ gives the Chernoff bound in terms of the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) of $X$.
