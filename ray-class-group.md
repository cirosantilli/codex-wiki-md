# Ray class group

↑ **Parent:** [Modulus of a number field](modulus-of-a-number-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_class_group)

The ray class group modulo $\mathfrak m$ is $I_K(\mathfrak m)/P_K(\mathfrak m)$, where the numerator consists of fractional ideals prime to $\mathfrak m_0$ and the denominator consists of $(\alpha)$ with $\alpha\equiv1$ at the finite modulus and positive at every real place in $\mathfrak m_\infty$.
