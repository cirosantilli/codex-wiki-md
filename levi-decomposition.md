# Levi decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Lie theory](lie-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_decomposition)

In characteristic zero, a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is a semidirect sum of its solvable radical and a semisimple [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md). The group analogue splits appropriate groups as a semidirect product with a [Levi subgroup](levi-subgroup.md).
