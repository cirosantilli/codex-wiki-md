# Lagrangian graph

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md)

The graph of a [one-form](one-form.md) $\alpha$ on a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) $Q$ is a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) of its [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md) exactly when $d\alpha=0$. Indeed the graph pulls the tautological one-form back to $\alpha$, and therefore pulls the canonical [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) back to $-d\alpha$. Locally the [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) writes $\alpha=dS$, giving $p_i=\partial_iS$. Globally a closed one-form may have nonzero periods and no single generating function.
