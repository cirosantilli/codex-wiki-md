# Sea-ice shear ridge

↑ **Parent:** [Sea-ice pressure ridge](sea-ice-pressure-ridge.md)

Relative tangential motion across a fracture in [sea ice](sea-ice.md) can break and pile blocks along the fracture. Local convergence at rough contacts makes ridging possible even when the large-scale deformation is mainly shear.
