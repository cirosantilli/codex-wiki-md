# Helium-3 superfluidity

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluidity](superfluidity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superfluid_helium-3)

[Helium-3 superfluidity](helium-3-superfluidity.md) involves [Cooper pairs](cooper-pair.md) of [fermions](fermion.md); [helium-4](helium-4.md) atoms are [bosons](boson.md). Individual [helium-3](helium-3.md) atoms do not undergo [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md). [Lee, 1996 lecture; 1997 reprint pp.645–647](the-extraordinary-phases-of-liquid-3he-by-david-morris-lee.md)

The [helium-3 superfluid discovery experiment](helium-3-superfluid-discovery-experiment.md) connects an unexpected [pressure](pressure-split.md) feature to a change in the state of a [liquid](liquid.md). Its lesson is unusually instructive: finding a reproducible anomaly and identifying the material that changed are separate experimental achievements. [Pomeranchuk cooling](pomeranchuk-cooling.md) explains how the experiment reached its operating regime; an independent [nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) channel distinguishes the phases.
