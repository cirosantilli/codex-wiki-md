# Squarefree divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor](divisor.md)

A squarefree divisor of an [integer](integer.md) $n$ is a [divisor](divisor.md) $d\mid n$ that is a [squarefree integer](squarefree-integer.md). It is specified by choosing a subset of the distinct [prime factors](prime-factor.md) of $n$.
