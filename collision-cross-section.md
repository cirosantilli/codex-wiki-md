# Collision cross-section

↑ **Parent:** [Collision mechanics](collision-mechanics.md)

A collision cross-section is the effective area that converts a target [number density](number-density.md) and incident particle speed into a [collision](collision.md) rate. It can depend on particle species, relative speed, and long-range forces, and need not equal a purely [geometric collision cross-section](geometric-collision-cross-section.md).
