# Processor clock period

↑ **Parent:** [Clock signal](clock-signal.md)

The interval between successive designated clock edges of a processor's [clock signal](clock-signal.md). In a synchronous sequential circuit it must be long enough for the required register-to-register propagation and setup timing.
