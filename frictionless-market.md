# Frictionless market

↑ **Parent:** [Financial market](financial-market.md)

A [financial market](financial-market.md) model that omits trading frictions such as transaction costs and a difference between buying and selling prices. The exact idealization must still specify permitted holdings, [short selling](short-finance.md), and borrowing and lending terms; the worked [binomial market](discrete-time-binomial-market.md) allows divisible holdings and unrestricted borrowing at the bank growth factor.
