# Financial volatility

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volatility_(finance))

Financial [volatility](financial-volatility.md) quantifies variability of asset returns. In the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md), $\sigma$ is the coefficient of the Brownian increment in the stock return $dS_t/S_t=\mu dt+\sigma dW_t$, so its instantaneous log-return [variance](variance-split.md) rate is $\sigma^2$. For deterministic time-varying [volatility](financial-volatility.md), the [variance](variance-split.md) over an interval is the integral of that squared coefficient.
