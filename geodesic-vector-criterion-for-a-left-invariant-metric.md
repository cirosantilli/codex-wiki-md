# Geodesic-vector criterion for a left-invariant metric

↑ **Parent:** [Left-invariant metric](left-invariant-metric.md)

For a [left-invariant metric](left-invariant-metric.md) on a [Lie group](lie-group.md), the one-parameter subgroup $\gamma_\xi(t)=\exp(t\xi)$ is a [geodesic](geodesic.md) exactly when

$$
\langle\xi,[\xi,\eta]\rangle=0
$$

for every $\eta$ in the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). The [Koszul formula](koszul-formula.md) gives $\langle\nabla_\xi\xi,\eta\rangle=-\langle\xi,[\xi,\eta]\rangle$.
