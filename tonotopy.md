# Tonotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Hearing](hearing.md)

An orderly relation between preferred acoustic frequency and anatomical position in a sensory structure. Cochlear mechanical tonotopy originates in graded mechanics, while later tonotopic neural organization involves additional processing. A frequency map is not itself a complete sound-object representation.
