# Exponential growth of the ternary affine Baumslag-Solitar group

↑ **Parent:** [Baumslag-Solitar group](baumslag-solitar-group.md)

Represent $y$ by translation $t\mapsto t+1$ and $x$ by $t\mapsto t/3$; the [relator](relator.md) holds and $y$ has infinite order. Base-three digits $d_j\in\{0,1,2\}$ give words recursively by $W_0=1$, $W_{j+1}=x^{-1}W_jxy^{d_j}$. These have length at most $4k$ and represent all $y^m$, $0\le m<3^k$. Thus the [growth function of a finitely generated group](growth-function-of-a-finitely-generated-group.md) obeys $\beta(4k)\ge3^k$. Counting freely reduced words in four letters gives $\beta(n)\le2\cdot3^n-1$. The growth is therefore exponential up to coarse equivalence.
