# Scaled centered risk under concave utility

↑ **Parent:** [Expected utility maximization](expected-utility-maximization.md)

If $U$ is concave and $\mathbb E Z=0$, then

$$
G(s)=\mathbb E[U(m+sZ)]
$$

is concave in $s$. By [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md), $G(s)\leq G(0)$; concavity then makes $G$ nonincreasing on $[0,\infty)$.
