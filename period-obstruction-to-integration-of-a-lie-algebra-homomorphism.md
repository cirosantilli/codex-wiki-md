# Period obstruction to integration of a Lie algebra homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Integration of a Lie algebra homomorphism](integration-of-a-lie-algebra-homomorphism.md)

For a connected source, first integrate on its [universal covering Lie group](universal-covering-lie-group.md). The resulting map descends to the original [group](group-split.md) exactly when it kills the discrete kernel of the [covering map](covering-space.md). For the [circle group](circle-group.md), a real-linear map $t\mapsto ct$ integrates to a circle [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) exactly when $c\in\mathbb Z$, since $2\pi\mathbb Z$ must map into itself. This is a global period condition invisible to the zero bracket of the circle's [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md).
