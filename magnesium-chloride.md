# Magnesium chloride

↑ **Parent:** [Salt (chemistry)](salt-chemistry.md)

[Magnesium chloride](magnesium-chloride.md) is an [ionic compound](salt-chemistry.md) with two [chloride](chloride.md) [anions](anion.md) per [magnesium](magnesium.md) [cation](cation.md). Complete dissolution supplies one divalent magnesium ion and two monovalent chloride ions per formula unit. At analytical salt concentration $C$, these species contribute molar [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md) $I=\tfrac12(4C+2C)=3C$, provided additional dissolved ions are absent.
