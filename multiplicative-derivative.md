# Multiplicative derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Gowers uniformity norm](gowers-uniformity-norm.md)

A product that cancels constant phases and lowers the degree of a polynomial phase. For $f(x)=e(\alpha x^2)$, it is $e(2\alpha hx+\alpha h^2)$. Some conventions conjugate the entire expression and reverse the frequency signs. The [Gowers uniformity norm](gowers-uniformity-norm.md) derivative identity relates a higher norm to the average of lower norms of these derivatives.
