# Tidal torque theory

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological structure formation](cosmological-structure-formation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_torque_theory)

Tidal torque theory explains the early [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) of a protohalo through the misalignment of its [mass second-moment tensor](mass-second-moment-tensor.md) $I_{ij}$ and the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) $T_{ij}=\partial_i\partial_j\Phi$ of the surrounding [peculiar gravitational potential](peculiar-gravitational-potential.md). At leading order,

$$
J_i\propto a^2\dot D\,\epsilon_{ijk}T_{jl}I_{lk}.
$$
