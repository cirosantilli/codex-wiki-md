# Assumption discharge

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

[Assumption discharge](assumption-discharge.md) closes a designated [temporary assumption](temporary-assumption.md) together with its dependent subproof. [Implication introduction rule](implication-introduction-rule.md) turns a [logical derivation](logical-derivation.md) of $B$ under $A$ into $A\to B$ outside that temporary scope. [Negation introduction rule](negation-introduction-rule.md) turns a [logical derivation](logical-derivation.md) of [logical falsity](logical-falsity.md) under $A$ into $\neg A$. [Disjunction elimination rule](disjunction-elimination-rule.md) discharges its separate case assumptions while retaining any surrounding premises.
