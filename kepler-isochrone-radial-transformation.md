<h1 id="kepler-isochrone-radial-transformation">Kepler–isochrone radial transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Radial orbit time transformation](radial-orbit-time-transformation.md)

With $a=(1-\lambda)GM$, $h=\lambda E>0$ and $\bar E>0$, set $\bar E q^2=hr^2+ar$. If $a\geq0$, its positive-radius root is $r=\sqrt{\bar E/h}(\sqrt{q^2+b^2}-b)$, with $b=a/(2\sqrt{h\bar E})$. The transformed [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) is an additive constant plus $-G\bar M/(b+\sqrt{q^2+b^2})$, the [spherical isochrone model](spherical-isochrone-model.md). The [mass](mass.md) sign and branch conditions must be checked.
