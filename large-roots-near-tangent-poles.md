# Large roots near tangent poles

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

For fixed real $\mu\ne0$, roots of $\tan x=\mu x$ tending to positive infinity approach tangent [poles](pole.md) $a_n=(n+1/2)\pi$. Expanding $-\cot(x-a_n)$ gives

$$
x_n=a_n-\frac1{\mu a_n}+\frac{1/(3\mu^3)-1/\mu^2}{a_n^3}+O(a_n^{-5}).
$$

The expansion works for either sign of $\mu$ when $|\mu|a_n\gg1$, but is nonuniform at $\mu=0$, where the roots lie at $n\pi$ instead. The [pole](pole.md) labeling need not equal the ordered root index at small $n$.
