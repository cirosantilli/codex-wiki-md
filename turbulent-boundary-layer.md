# Turbulent boundary layer

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary layer](boundary-layer.md)

A [boundary layer](boundary-layer.md) in which turbulent velocity fluctuations transport momentum and scalars. A near-wall constant-stress region supports the [law of the wall](law-of-the-wall.md); buoyancy modifies it through [Monin-Obukhov similarity theory](monin-obukhov-similarity-theory.md). A viscous or roughness sublayer and an outer-layer scale bound the range of either approximation.
