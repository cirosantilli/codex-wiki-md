# D5-brane

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

A type-IIB [D-brane](d-brane.md) with five spatial dimensions. Its RR charge is magnetic with respect to the two-form potential $C_2$, or electric with respect to the dual six-form potential. It is distinct from the [NS5-brane](ns5-brane.md), whose charge is magnetic under the NS-NS two-form and whose string-frame tension has a different power of the coupling.
