# Boltzmann approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md)

When $E-\mu\gg kT$, the unit term in the denominator of the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) is negligible. The resulting exponentially small occupation is the [Boltzmann approximation](boltzmann-approximation.md). It justifies the usual nondegenerate [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) carrier formulas; it fails near strongly occupied band states.
