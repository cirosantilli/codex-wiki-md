# Euclidean semigroup growth bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean logarithmic norm](euclidean-logarithmic-norm.md)

The [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md) of the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) gives the lower bound through the [spectral abscissa](spectral-abscissa.md) $s(A)$. Differentiating the squared [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) along $q'=Aq$ and bounding its Hermitian quadratic form gives the upper bound through the [Euclidean logarithmic norm](euclidean-logarithmic-norm.md) $\mu_2(A)$. A [non-normal matrix](non-normal-matrix.md) can have $\mu_2(A)>0$ despite $s(A)<0$, allowing [transient growth](transient-growth.md) before its ultimate decay.
