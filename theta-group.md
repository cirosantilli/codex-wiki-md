# Theta group

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi theta function](jacobi-theta-function.md)

The theta group is $\Gamma_\theta=\Gamma(2)\cup\Gamma(2)S$. It is generated by $T^2$ and $S$ and has index three in the [modular group](modular-group.md).
