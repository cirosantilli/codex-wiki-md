# Flat-band hybridization Fermi-velocity suppression

↑ **Parent:** [Band hybridization](band-hybridization.md)

Mix a parabolic [energy band](energy-band.md) $\epsilon_k=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$ with a perfectly [flat band](flat-band.md) at $E_d>0$ through momentum-independent $V$. At zero coupling suppose the two orbital sectors have equal populations, with the dispersive sector filled to $k_0=\sqrt{2mE_d}/\hbar$. At fixed total density and sufficiently low temperature, nonzero coupling fills only the monotonic lower hybridized branch, whose [Fermi wavevector](fermi-wavevector.md) is $k_F=2^{1/3}k_0$, provided the available momentum-space states accommodate it. Its small slope at that radius gives the displayed ratio to $v_F^{(1)}=\hbar k_0/m$ for $|V|\ll E_d$. The result uses the singular perfectly flat limit and an empty upper band; it is not implied by [Fermi-volume conservation in two hybridized electron bands](fermi-volume-conservation-in-two-hybridized-electron-bands.md) for arbitrary pairs of dispersive bands. The zero-temperature limit must resolve the small hybridization energy scale.
