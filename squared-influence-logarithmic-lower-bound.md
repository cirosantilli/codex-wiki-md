# Squared-influence logarithmic lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Kahn-Kalai-Linial theorem](kahn-kalai-linial-theorem.md)

For a zero-one [Boolean function](boolean-function.md) of mean $t$, the displayed bound holds for sufficiently large $n$, uniformly in $t$. If it failed, put $v=t(1-t)$, $\lambda=v\log n$ and split its nonconstant [Fourier weight](fourier-weight.md) at level $b=(\log n)/3$. The [total influence](total-influence.md) bound puts at most $3v/4$ above that level. The [hypercontractive weighted influence bound](hypercontractive-weighted-influence-bound.md) with $\delta=1-3\log\log n/\log n$ puts only $o(v)$ below it: the conversion factor $\max_{1\leq s\leq b}[s\delta^{s-1}]^{-1}$ stays bounded, while $\lambda^{2/(1+\delta)}n^{1-2/(1+\delta)}=vO((\log n)^{-1/2+o(1)})$. This contradicts total nonconstant weight $v$.
