# Radar cross section

↑ **Parent:** [Radar](radar.md)

The monostatic [radar cross section](radar-cross-section.md) is $4\pi$ times the backward [differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md). Equivalently, $\sigma_{\rm back}=\lim_{r\to\infty}4\pi r^2S_{\rm back}/S_{\rm inc}$. It is a directional scattering measure with dimensions of area, not generally the geometric area or the total [scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md). The definition may additionally specify the transmitted and received polarizations.
