# Dihedral subgroups of every divisor order

↑ **Parent:** [Dihedral group](dihedral-group.md)

Write the [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) as $D_{2n}=\langle r,s:r^n=s^2=e,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle$. If $k\mid n$, the [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md) $\langle r^{n/k}\rangle$ has order $k$. For an even divisor $k=2m$ of $2n$, $m\mid n$ and $\langle r^{n/m},s\rangle$ has $m$ rotations and $m$ reflections, hence order $k$. An odd divisor of $2n$ always divides $n$. This proves that the necessary divisibility condition from [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md) is also sufficient in a [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md), although it is not sufficient in arbitrary [finite groups](finite-group.md).
