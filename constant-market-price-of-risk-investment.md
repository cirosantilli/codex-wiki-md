# Constant market price of risk investment

↑ **Parent:** [Investment-consumption problem](investment-consumption-problem.md)

When the [market price of risk](market-price-of-risk.md) is constant and nonzero [spot volatility](spot-volatility.md) gives access to the driving [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), changes in volatility only rescale the dollar holding needed for fixed [Brownian portfolio exposures](brownian-portfolio-exposures.md). With unrestricted holdings and constant interest and discount rates, the [CRRA utility](constant-relative-risk-aversion-utility.md) value is consequently independent of the factor state. Zero volatility requires separate treatment.
