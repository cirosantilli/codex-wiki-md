# Benign subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md)

A subgroup $H$ of a [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) $G$ is benign if some [finitely presented group](finitely-presented-group.md) $P$ contains $G$ and some [finitely generated subgroup](finitely-generated-subgroup.md) $L\le P$ satisfies $H=G\cap L$. The subgroup $H$ itself need not be finitely generated. A centralizing [HNN extension](hnn-extension.md) of $P$ along $L$ is finitely presented even when the corresponding intersection subgroup $H$ is infinitely generated.
