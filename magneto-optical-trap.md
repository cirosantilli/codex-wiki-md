# Magneto-optical trap

↑ **Parent:** [Laser cooling](laser-cooling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magneto-optical_trap)

A magneto-optical trap combines [laser cooling](laser-cooling.md) with a position-dependent [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and suitable beam [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) to produce a [restoring force](restoring-force.md). Its spatial confinement is additional to the [velocity](velocity.md) [damping](damping.md) of uniform [optical molasses](optical-molasses.md).
