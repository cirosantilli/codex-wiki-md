# Coherent-state resolution of identity

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent state](coherent-state.md)

For an [unnormalized bosonic coherent state](unnormalized-bosonic-coherent-state.md), the measure is $d^2\psi/\pi$ over the complex plane. Gaussian moments $\int d^2\psi\,e^{-|\psi|^2}\psi^n\bar\psi^m/\pi=n!\delta_{nm}$ recover the identity in the [Fock state](fock-state.md) basis. For a [fermionic coherent state](fermionic-coherent-state.md), use independent [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md) and an ordered [Berezin integral](berezin-integral.md) with $\int d\bar\psi\,d\psi\,\bar\psi\psi=-1$. The coefficient of $-\bar\psi\psi$ in the weighted projector is the identity. Products of these one-mode measures give the finite-mode result.
