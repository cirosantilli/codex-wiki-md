# Divisor-to-Picard map

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor sheaf on a complex manifold](divisor-sheaf-on-a-complex-manifold.md)

The [connecting homomorphism](connecting-homomorphism.md) of

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X^*\longrightarrow\mathcal K_X^*\longrightarrow\mathcal K_X^*/\mathcal O_X^*\longrightarrow0
$$

sends a [divisor on a complex manifold](divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md) $D$ to its [holomorphic line bundle associated to a divisor](holomorphic-line-bundle-associated-to-a-divisor.md) $\mathcal O(D)$ in the [Picard group](picard-group.md). Its kernel consists exactly of the [principal divisors](principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) of global nonzero [meromorphic functions](meromorphic-function.md).
