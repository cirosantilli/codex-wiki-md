# Subgroup correspondence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient group](quotient-group.md)

For a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) $N$ of $G$, projection to the [quotient group](quotient-group.md) gives a bijection between [subgroups](subgroup.md) of $G$ containing $N$ and [subgroups](subgroup.md) of $G/N$. The maps are $H\mapsto H/N$ and inverse image under projection. Their two composites are identities because each relevant subgroup is a union of cosets of $N$. They preserve inclusions and normality; consequently a [composition series](composition-series.md) in a quotient lifts to a subnormal chain above $N$ with the same [composition factors](composition-factor.md).
