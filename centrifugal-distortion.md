# Centrifugal distortion

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md)

Real [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) can stretch as [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md) increases. The resulting larger [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) lowers the [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) relative to a [rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md). Within one vibrational [quantum state](quantum-state.md), a leading effective correction is

$$
\frac{E_J}{h}=B_vJ(J+1)-D_v[J(J+1)]^2+\cdots,\qquad
\nu_{J\to J+1}=2B_v(J+1)-4D_v(J+1)^3+\cdots.
$$

Both $B_v$ and $D_v$ are in [frequency](frequency.md) units here; $v$ labels the vibrational [quantum state](quantum-state.md). For positive $D_v$, the adjacent-line spacing becomes

$$
\nu_{J+1\to J+2}-\nu_{J\to J+1}=2B_v-4D_v(3J^2+9J+7),
$$

so the departures grow with $J$. [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md) also changes the effective [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md): even the lowest vibrational [quantum state](quantum-state.md) does not have motionless [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md).[§2a, equations (7)–(8): distortion and vibration-dependent constants](diatomic-spectral-database-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology/1-sigma-ground-state-molecules.md)

The quartic correction is a limited expansion, not a model to extrapolate to arbitrary $J$. It is small relative to the rigid contribution only while $(D_v/B_v)J(J+1)\ll1$; eventually higher terms, changed internal motion, or [chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md) breaking matter. In the synthetic plot, the largest upper-state label is $J=5$, giving a stipulated correction ratio of $0.03$. No universal maximum valid $J$ follows without a particular [molecule](molecule-split.md) and precision target.

The [quantum numbers](quantum-number.md) also have limits of meaning. Here $J$ describes spin-free rotation. In a [molecule](molecule-split.md) with electronic [spin](spin.md), rotation alone is commonly labelled $N$, while $J$ includes electronic [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md); including nuclear [spin](spin.md) introduces additional total-[angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) labels and resolved splittings. An applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md) can mix different field-free $J$ states through the [Stark effect](stark-effect.md). For identical [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md), the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) can restrict combinations of rotational and nuclear-[spin](spin.md) states. The simple $2J+1$ degeneracy and every-integer-$J$ ladder therefore describe the stated idealization, not all observed sublevels.[§2a: hyperfine discussion; §2b: distinct definitions of J, N and F](diatomic-spectral-database-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology/1-sigma-ground-state-molecules.md)
