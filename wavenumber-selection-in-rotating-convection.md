# Wavenumber selection in rotating convection

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rotating-rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

For the free-slip plane layer, onset selects the smallest [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md) across all admissible stationary and oscillatory [normal modes](normal-mode.md). The first vertical mode wins because increasing vertical index raises viscous cost and reduces oscillatory admissibility. Oscillatory minimization replaces the right-hand side of the stationary wave-number equation by $P^2\mathrm{Ta}\pi^2/(1+P)^2$. For the oscillatory minimum also require positive [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) squared. Where both selected curves are admissible, at rapid rotation the ratio of the minimized thresholds is $R_{o,c}/R_{s,c}\sim2P^{4/3}/(1+P)^{1/3}$. Equality gives $8P^4-P-1=0$, with positive root about $0.67660$; below it sufficiently rapid rotation can select oscillatory primary onset.
