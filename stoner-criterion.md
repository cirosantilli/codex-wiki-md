# Stoner criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Itinerant ferromagnetism](itinerant-ferromagnetism.md)

In the homogeneous spin-vector mean-field scheme with field cost $M^2/(2U)$ and [spin-summed density of states](spin-summed-density-of-states.md) per site, the paramagnetic curvature is proportional to $U^{-1}-\int\nu(\epsilon)[-n_F'(\epsilon)]d\epsilon$. It changes sign at $U\chi_0=1$. For a smooth low-temperature [density of states](density-of-states.md), $\chi_0\simeq\nu(\mu)$, giving the familiar Fermi-level criterion. Interaction and spin-counting conventions must be specified before comparing coefficients.
