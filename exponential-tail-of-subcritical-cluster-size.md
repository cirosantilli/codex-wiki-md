# Exponential tail of subcritical cluster size

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential decay of subcritical percolation](exponential-decay-of-subcritical-percolation.md)

For independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on $\mathbb Z^d$ below the [percolation critical probability](percolation-critical-probability.md), there are $K,c>0$ such that $\mathbb P_p(|C(x)|\geq n)\leq Ke^{-cn}$, uniformly in $x$. This concerns the number of vertices in a [percolation cluster](percolation-cluster.md), a stronger conclusion than decay of its radius alone.
