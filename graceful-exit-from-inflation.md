# Graceful exit from inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)

A [graceful exit from inflation](graceful-exit-from-inflation.md) ends sustained [accelerating expansion](accelerating-expansion-of-the-universe.md) and connects it to a hot [radiation-dominated universe](radiation-dominated-universe.md). Crossing $\epsilon_H=1$ ends acceleration in a homogeneous expanding model; [reheating](reheating.md) requires an additional mechanism to transfer [inflaton](inflaton.md) energy into other species. Pure [exponential-potential power-law inflation](exponential-potential-power-law-inflation.md) keeps $\epsilon_H$ constant and its [inflaton potential](inflaton-potential.md) has no finite minimum, so neither an automatic endpoint nor oscillatory [reheating](reheating.md) follows from that potential alone.
