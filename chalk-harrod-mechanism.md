# Chalk-Harrod mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrosilylation](hydrosilylation.md)

A [hydrosilylation](hydrosilylation.md) cycle involving Si–H [oxidative addition](oxidative-addition.md), [alkene](alkene.md) insertion into the [metal](metal.md)–[hydride](hydride.md) bond, and C–Si [reductive elimination](reductive-elimination.md). This defines the conventional version; insertion into a [metal](metal.md)–silyl bond describes a modified alternative.
