# Reductive group

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductive_group)

A reductive algebraic group is a smooth connected affine algebraic group whose largest connected normal [unipotent algebraic group](unipotent-algebraic-group.md) is trivial.
