# Covariant representables preserve limits

↑ **Parent:** [Representable functor](representable-functor.md)

A [representable functor](representable-functor.md) $\mathcal C(R,-)$ preserves every small [categorical limit](categorical-limit.md) that exists. A map from $R$ into a limit is exactly a compatible family of maps from $R$ to the diagram objects, and such a family is exactly an element of the corresponding limit of sets. The comparison bijection is the one induced by the limit projections.
