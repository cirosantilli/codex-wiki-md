# Annihilator of a subgroup of a finite abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Character group of a finite abelian group](character-group-of-a-finite-abelian-group.md)

For a [subgroup](subgroup.md) $K$ of a [finite abelian group](finite-abelian-group.md) $G$, its annihilator is

$$
K^\perp=\{\chi\in\widehat G:\chi(k)=1\text{ for every }k\in K\}.
$$

These are exactly the [linear characters](linear-character.md) that descend to the [quotient group](quotient-group.md) $G/K$. The [character group of a finite abelian group](character-group-of-a-finite-abelian-group.md) has the same size as the group, so $|K^\perp|=|G/K|=|G|/|K|$. The annihilator is what [abelian hidden-subgroup Fourier sampling](abelian-hidden-subgroup-fourier-sampling.md) measures.
