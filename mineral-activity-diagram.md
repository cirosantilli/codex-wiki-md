# Mineral activity diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Phase diagram](phase-diagram.md)

A diagram of stable [mineral](mineral.md) assemblages as functions of specified aqueous [thermodynamic activities](thermodynamic-activity.md), at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md). The coordinates often use logarithmic activity ratios. Mass-action equations give pairwise coexistence lines; a line segment is stable only when no omitted mineral has a lower relevant thermodynamic potential under the same imposed chemical potentials.
