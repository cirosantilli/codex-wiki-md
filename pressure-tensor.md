# Pressure tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure.md)

In the local rest frame of a gas, the [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md) is the directional [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) $P_{ij}=\int p_iv_jf(\mathbf p)\,d^3p$, where $f$ is the [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) per unit [momentum](momentum.md) volume and $\mathbf v$ is particle velocity. For an isotropic distribution, $P_{ij}=P\delta_{ij}$, recovering the [kinetic pressure of an isotropic gas](kinetic-pressure-of-an-isotropic-gas.md). An anisotropic distribution can exert different normal [pressures](pressure.md) along different directions.
