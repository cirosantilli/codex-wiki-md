# Moreau decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Proximal operator](proximal-operator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moreau_decomposition)

For $t>0$, the generalized Moreau decomposition is $\operatorname{prox}_{tf}(y)=y-t\operatorname{prox}_{t^{-1}f^*}(y/t)$, where $f^*$ is the [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md).
