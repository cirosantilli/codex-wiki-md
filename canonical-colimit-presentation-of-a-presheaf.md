# Canonical colimit presentation of a presheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Presheaf category](presheaf-category.md)

For a [categorical presheaf](presheaf-category-theory.md) $P$ on a [small category](small-category.md), its [category of elements](category-of-elements.md) has objects $(c,x)$ with $x\in P(c)$ and arrows $u:(c,x)\to(d,y)$ satisfying $P(u)y=x$. The canonical [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) $H_c\to P$ sends $v:a\to c$ to $P(v)x$. These maps exhibit $P$ as the displayed [colimit](colimit.md). At $a$, a representative $(c,x,v)$ maps to $P(v)x$, with inverse $z\mapsto(a,z,1_a)$. The arrow $v:(a,P(v)x)\to(c,x)$ identifies every representative with its canonical inverse image, proving the bijection and the colimit universal property.
