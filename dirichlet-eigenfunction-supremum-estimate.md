# Dirichlet eigenfunction supremum estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Laplacian eigenfunction](dirichlet-laplacian-eigenfunction.md)

For $n\geq3$ and a bounded smooth domain, a [Dirichlet Laplacian eigenfunction](dirichlet-laplacian-eigenfunction.md) with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$ satisfies the displayed estimate. A [signed power test for a Laplacian eigenfunction](signed-power-test-for-a-laplacian-eigenfunction.md) and the zero-boundary [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md) give a [Moser iteration](moser-iteration.md) with exponents $p_j=2[n/(n-2)]^j$. The [geometric series](geometric-series.md) $\sum_j1/p_j=n/4$ gives the exact power of $\lambda$, while $\sum_j(\log p_j)/p_j<\infty$ controls the remaining constant. The constant depends only on dimension, because the zero-boundary [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md) follows by extension by zero to the whole space.
