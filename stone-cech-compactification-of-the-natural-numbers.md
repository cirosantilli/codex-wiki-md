<h1 id="stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers">Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md)

The Stone-Cech compactification $\beta\mathbb N$ can be identified with the space of ultrafilters on $\mathbb N$. Its basic clopen sets are $\bar A=\{\mathcal U:A\in\mathcal U\}$.

This is the natural-number-space case of [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md).
