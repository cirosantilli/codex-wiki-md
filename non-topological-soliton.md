# Non-topological soliton

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field-theory soliton](classical-field-theory-soliton-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-topological_soliton)

A [non-topological soliton](non-topological-soliton.md) is a localized finite-energy field configuration whose existence or stability is not protected by a nontrivial [topological charge](topological-charge.md). A conserved ordinary [Noether charge](noether-charge.md) can instead obstruct its decay. A [Q-ball](q-ball.md) is a standard complex-scalar example: its profile is localized while its phase rotates in time.
