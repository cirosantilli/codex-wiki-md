# Crystal motif

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)

The arrangement of atoms or other constituents attached identically to every [lattice point](lattice-point.md) of a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md). The lattice specifies translations; the motif specifies the contents repeated by those translations. A multi-atom motif does not make its atoms separate lattice points.
