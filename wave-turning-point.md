# Wave turning point

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md)

A zero of the local squared [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) in $E''+k^2(x)E=0$. At a simple zero, $(k^2)'(x_T)\ne0$, the [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) fails in a layer with scale $|(k^2)'(x_T)|^{-1/3}$ and the [Airy ordinary differential equation](airy-ordinary-differential-equation.md) supplies the local replacement. Two nearby [wave turning points](wave-turning-point.md) require a uniform treatment rather than independent [Airy turning-point connection formulas](airy-turning-point-connection-formula.md).
