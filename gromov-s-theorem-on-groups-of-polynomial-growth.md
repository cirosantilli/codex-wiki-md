<h1 id="gromov-s-theorem-on-groups-of-polynomial-growth">Gromov's theorem on groups of polynomial growth</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial growth of a group](polynomial-growth-of-a-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gromov's_theorem_on_groups_of_polynomial_growth)

Every finitely generated [group](group-split.md) of [polynomial growth of a group](polynomial-growth-of-a-group.md) has a [virtually nilpotent group](virtually-nilpotent-group.md) structure. Applying the [one-scale virtual nilpotence theorem](one-scale-virtual-nilpotence-theorem.md) gives a finite normal subgroup with nilpotent quotient; centralizing that finite subgroup produces a nilpotent subgroup of finite index.
