# PeakForce quantitative nanomechanical mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

A force-curve mapping mode that controls the peak repulsive normal [force](force-split.md) during repeated low-frequency tip-surface contacts. Calibrated [force](force-split.md) curves provide topography and spatial maps of [adhesion](adhesion.md), deformation, modulus and dissipated energy. Mechanical-property accuracy depends on tip geometry, [cantilever](cantilever.md) [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) and the contact model.
