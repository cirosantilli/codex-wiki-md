# Comet energy diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary scattering](planetary-scattering.md)

Model successive uncorrelated planetary kicks as a [random walk](random-walk.md) in inverse [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) $x=1/a$, proportional to negative [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md). If the [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) kick per periapsis passage is $s_x$ and the initial value is $x_0$, the characteristic number of passages to reach the escape scale is $N\sim(x_0/s_x)^2$. For $s_x=10M_p/(a_pM_\star)$ and passage interval $P(a_0)$,

$$
t_{\rm ej}\sim\frac{P(a_0)}{100}\left(\frac{a_p}{a_0}\right)^2
\left(\frac{M_\star}{M_p}\right)^2.
$$

The quantity $s_x$ has inverse-length units, not the units of a variance-per-time [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md). With $\langle(\Delta x)^2\rangle=2D_xt$, that coefficient is $D_x=s_x^2/(2P)$. This estimate assumes many independent encounters; changing [orbital periods](orbital-period.md) and resonant correlations can invalidate the clock. It is not an exact mean [first-passage time](first-passage-time.md).
