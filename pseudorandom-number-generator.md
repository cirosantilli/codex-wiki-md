# Pseudorandom number generator

↑ **Parent:** [Monte Carlo method](monte-carlo-method.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudorandom_number_generator)

A [pseudorandom number generator](pseudorandom-number-generator.md) is a deterministic state-update algorithm producing numbers intended to imitate an independent sequence from a prescribed [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md). For [Monte Carlo integration](monte-carlo-integration.md), desirable features include good one-dimensional and multidimensional uniformity, a period far longer than the calculation, negligible detectable dependence, reproducible seeds, sufficient numerical precision and fast generation. Uniform marginal distributions alone do not provide independent draws.
