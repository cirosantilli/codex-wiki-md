# Odds ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odds_ratio)

The odds ratio is the ratio of two event odds. For rare events it is close to the corresponding [risk ratio](risk-ratio.md), because $p/(1-p)\simeq p$ when $p$ is small.
