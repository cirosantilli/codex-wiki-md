# Two-dimensional Killing algebra with a periodic generator is abelian

↑ **Parent:** [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md)

Suppose the full [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of [Killing fields](killing-vector-field.md) has basis $T,\Phi$ and the flow of $\Phi$ has a nonzero period $P$. Write $[T,\Phi]=aT+b\Phi$ with constant coefficients. The [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of the periodic flow satisfies $e^{P\operatorname{ad}\Phi}=1$. Its real eigenvalues are $-a$ and zero, so $a=0$. The remaining adjoint operator is nilpotent and its exponential is $1+P\operatorname{ad}\Phi$, forcing $b=0$. This proves commutation from genuine periodic axial symmetry, without differentiating mere asymptotic component limits.
