# Sedimenting rectangular-channel characteristic compatibility

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md)

With [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $b$ and [settling velocity](settling-velocity.md) $V_s$, a mixed rectangular-channel current obeys $D h=-hu_x$, $D u=-bh_x-hb_x/2$, $D b=-V_sb/h$, where $D=\partial_t+u\partial_x$. The [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) are $u,u\pm c$, with $c^2=bh$. Along the latter curves,

$$
du\pm\frac ch\,dh\pm\frac h{2c}\,db=\mp\frac{V_sc}{2h}\,dt.
$$

The density differential matters: $u\pm2c$ are [Riemann invariants](riemann-invariant.md) only when the [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) is constant without deposition.
