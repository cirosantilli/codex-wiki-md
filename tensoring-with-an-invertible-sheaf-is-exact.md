# Tensoring with an invertible sheaf is exact

↑ **Parent:** [Line bundle](line-bundle.md)

For an [invertible sheaf](line-bundle.md) $L$, tensoring [sheaves of modules](sheaf-of-modules-split.md) with $L$ is an exact functor: on an [open cover](open-cover.md) trivializing $L$, it is naturally the identity functor. Equivalently, its [stalks](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) are free modules of rank one and hence [flat modules](flat-module.md). Its inverse functor is tensoring with the [dual of a sheaf](dual-of-a-sheaf.md) $L^\vee$, because $L\otimes L^\vee\cong\mathcal O_X$. This justifies untwisting a surjection onto a positively twisted [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md).
