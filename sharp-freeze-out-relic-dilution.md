# Sharp-freeze-out relic dilution

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological particle freeze-out](cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md)

In a stable-particle approximation with negligible later [annihilation](annihilation.md), comoving particle number is conserved and physical [number density](number-density.md) falls as $a^{-3}$. Replacing scale-factor ratios by photon-temperature ratios additionally assumes no change in the [entropy degrees of freedom](entropy-degrees-of-freedom.md).
