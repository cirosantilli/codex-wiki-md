# Simultaneous returns of independent simple random walks

↑ **Parent:** [Simple random walk on the integer line](simple-random-walk-on-the-integer-line.md)

For $d$ [independent](independent-random-variables.md) symmetric walks on $\mathbb Z$, the probability that all are at the origin at time $2n$ is

$$
\left(\frac{\binom{2n}{n}}{4^n}\right)^d\asymp n^{-d/2}.
$$

The [Borel-Cantelli lemmas](borel-cantelli-lemmas.md) therefore show that simultaneous returns occur only finitely often almost surely when $d\geq3$.
