# Ramp-state expansion in a symmetric infinite well

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md)

In a well $-a<x<a$, the normalized state proportional to $x$ on $0<x<a$ and zero elsewhere has stationary-state coefficients

$$
c_n=-\frac{2\sqrt3(-1)^n}{n\pi}-\frac{4\sqrt3\sin(n\pi/2)}{n^2\pi^2}.
$$

They follow from the [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) inner products. Their squared magnitudes give energy-measurement probabilities by the [Born rule](born-rule.md); splitting even and odd indices in the [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md) gives convergent integer-coefficient series identities. The expansion is interpreted in the square-integrable state norm.
