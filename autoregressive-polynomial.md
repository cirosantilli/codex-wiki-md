# Autoregressive polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md)

The autoregressive polynomial is $\Phi(z)=1-\sum_{j=1}^p\phi_jz^j$. Substituting the [backshift operator](backshift-operator.md) gives the autoregressive filter. Zeros outside the unit disk give the [causality root criterion for an autoregressive model](causality-root-criterion-for-an-autoregressive-model.md); zeros on the unit circle obstruct a nondegenerate stationary innovation-driven solution.
