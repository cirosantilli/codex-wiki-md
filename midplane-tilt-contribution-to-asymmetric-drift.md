# Midplane tilt contribution to asymmetric drift

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar asymmetric drift](stellar-asymmetric-drift.md)

Reflection symmetry makes $\overline{v_Rv_z}$ odd in height, so its value at the midplane vanishes while its derivative need not vanish. The radial [Jeans equation](jeans-equation.md) includes this derivative in the [stellar asymmetric drift](stellar-asymmetric-drift.md). For a [velocity ellipsoid](velocity-ellipsoid.md) locally aligned with spherical coordinates, $\overline{v_Rv_z}\simeq(\sigma_R^2-\sigma_z^2)z/R$, and its midplane contribution is $-(\sigma_R^2-\sigma_z^2)$. Dropping a mixed moment at the symmetry plane does not justify dropping its derivative.
