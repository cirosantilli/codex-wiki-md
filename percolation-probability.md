# Percolation probability

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation theory](percolation-theory-split.md)

For independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on an infinite [graph](graph-split.md) with a specified root $o$, let $C_o$ be its [percolation cluster](percolation-cluster.md). The percolation probability is

$$
\theta(p)=\mathbb P_p(|C_o|=\infty).
$$

It concerns the root, rather than the existence of an [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md) somewhere else. Even above the [percolation critical probability](percolation-critical-probability.md) of the [square lattice](square-lattice.md), an isolated root has [probability](probability.md) $(1-p)^4$, because its four incident [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) can all be closed. Thus $\theta(p)\leq1-(1-p)^4<1$ when $p<1$. Positive percolation probability does not mean every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) is connected to infinity.

On the [square lattice](square-lattice.md), the [events](event.md) that $o$ is joined by an [open path](open-path-in-bond-percolation.md) to the boundary of $[-n,n]^2$ decrease as $n$ increases. Their [probabilities](probability.md) converge to $\theta(p)$ by [continuity from above of a measure](continuity-from-above-of-a-measure.md). This relates an infinite question to finite observations, without equating any one finite boundary connection with survival forever. The [monotone coupling of Bernoulli percolation](monotone-coupling-of-bernoulli-percolation.md) shows that $\theta$ is a [monotone function](monotonic-function.md) of $p$.
