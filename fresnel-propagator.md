# Fresnel propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Free-space diffraction](free-space-diffraction.md)

In two transverse dimensions, the [Fresnel propagator](fresnel-propagator.md) has kernel $k_0(2\pi i x)^{-1}\exp(ik_0|\mathbf z-\mathbf z'|^2/(2x))$ for $x>0$. It evolves the homogeneous [parabolic wave equation](parabolic-wave-equation.md).
