# Excitatory postsynaptic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excitatory_postsynaptic_potential)

An [excitatory postsynaptic potential](excitatory-postsynaptic-potential.md) is a [synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md) associated with increased likelihood of a postsynaptic [action potential](action-potential.md). Its size and effect depend on the receiving [neuron](neuron.md)'s state; the definition concerns firing, not a fixed [neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md) identity. See [synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md) for the mechanism and source.
