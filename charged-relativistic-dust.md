# Charged relativistic dust

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic dust](relativistic-dust.md)

For identical particles of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $m$ and [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $q$, the [four-current](four-current.md) is $j^\mu=(q/m)\rho u^\mu$. The matter [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md) obeys $\nabla_\mu T_{\rm m}^{\mu\nu}=F^{\nu\alpha}j_\alpha$, with the opposite force density in the electromagnetic [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md). Antisymmetry of the [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) makes its contraction with two [four-velocities](four-velocity.md) vanish, so mass continuity still holds. Removing that term leaves the displayed covariant [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) law. The matter tensor alone is not divergence-free when an electromagnetic force acts.
