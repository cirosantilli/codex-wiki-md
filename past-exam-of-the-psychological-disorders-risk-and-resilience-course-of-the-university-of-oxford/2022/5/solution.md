↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**Inherited variation contributes to psychiatric vulnerability and to some measured differences in adaptation after [psychological stress](../../../psychological-stress.md), but its importance is [phenotype](../../../phenotype.md)-, population- and environment-dependent.** Genetic influence is not equivalent to inevitability, a single large-effect [gene](../../../gene.md) or a wholly separate set of protective [genes](../../../gene.md). A satisfactory answer must distinguish genetic effects on [exposure](../../../exposure.md), on symptoms and on responses conditional on [exposure](../../../exposure.md).

Family resemblance provides an initial clue, but families transmit environments and social circumstances as well as DNA. Twin comparisons exploit differences in relatedness: monozygotic pairs share essentially all inherited sequence variation, whereas dizygotic pairs share about half their segregating additive variation on average. Under an [ACE model](../../../ace-model.md), greater resemblance of monozygotic pairs can support a genetic component if the relevant environmental and ascertainment assumptions hold. Equal environments means comparable effects of relevant environmental similarity, not literally identical treatment of every twin.

Disorder-level evidence is substantial. [Patrick F. Sullivan](../../../patrick-f-sullivan.md) and colleagues' ascertainment-aware synthesis of [schizophrenia](../../../schizophrenia.md) [twin studies](../../../twin-study.md) estimated liability [heritability](../../../heritability.md) at roughly $81\%$, with uncertainty. [Paul Lichtenstein](../../../paul-lichtenstein.md) and colleagues' Swedish family-register study supported partially shared inherited liability to [schizophrenia](../../../schizophrenia.md) and [bipolar disorder](../../../bipolar-disorder.md) using biological, adoptive and sibling relationships. The convergence supports inherited vulnerability while showing that diagnostic categories do not necessarily partition genetic causes into entirely independent sets.[2003, twin-liability synthesis](../../../schizophrenia-as-a-complex-trait-by-patrick-f-sullivan-and-others.md)[2009, family/adoption comparisons and cross-disorder liability](../../../common-genetic-determinants-of-schizophrenia-and-bipolar-disorder-in-swedish-families-by-paul-lichtenstein-and-others.md)

Molecular evidence provides a different test. The [Psychiatric Genomics Consortium](../../../psychiatric-genomics-consortium.md)'s 2014 [schizophrenia](../../../schizophrenia.md) study identified associations spanning 108 loci in a large multi-stage genome-wide analysis. Many common variants contribute small effects; an associated locus is not automatically one causal [gene](../../../gene.md) or a known mechanism. [Population stratification](../../../population-stratification.md), [linkage disequilibrium](../../../linkage-disequilibrium.md), case ascertainment and sample overlap still require control. A [genetic risk score](../../../genetic-risk-score.md) can predict risk differences in independent data while remaining far from a deterministic diagnosis or a transportable measure of one person's [psychological resilience](../../../psychological-resilience.md).[2014, multi-stage associations and polygenic interpretation](../../../biological-insights-from-108-schizophrenia-associated-genetic-loci-by-psychiatric-genomics-consortium.md)

[Psychological resilience](../../../psychological-resilience.md) needs additional definition because favorable outcomes without [adversity](../../../adversity.md) do not establish [psychological stress](../../../psychological-stress.md) resistance. [Ananda B. Amstadter](../../../ananda-b-amstadter.md) and colleagues studied 7,500 adult twins at two assessments, modeling internalizing symptoms relative to cumulative stressful events. Their [regression residual](../../../regression-residual.md)-based [phenotype](../../../phenotype.md) had estimated [heritability](../../../heritability.md) around $31\%$ at each wave; longitudinal modeling that accounted for [measurement error](../../../measurement-error.md) and occasion-specific influences gave a larger latent estimate, around $50\%$. This supports both inherited and environmental contributions to that operationalization, not a universal percentage for every meaning of [psychological resilience](../../../psychological-resilience.md).[2014, residual phenotype, wave-specific and longitudinal estimates](../../../psychiatric-resilience-longitudinal-twin-study-by-ananda-b-amstadter-and-others.md)

The shift in estimates is conceptually informative. Independent [measurement error](../../../measurement-error.md) adds to observed [variance](../../../variance-split.md) without adding the corresponding genetic signal. In a simple measurement model, if $V_G$ is genetic [variance](../../../variance-split.md), $V_T$ true-score [variance](../../../variance-split.md) and $V_\epsilon$ independent error [variance](../../../variance-split.md), then

$$
h^2_{\mathrm{observed}}=\frac{V_G}{V_T+V_\epsilon}=\frac{V_T}{V_T+V_\epsilon}h^2_{\mathrm{true}}.
$$

Removing noise can therefore raise estimated [heritability](../../../heritability.md) without changing anyone's [genes](../../../gene.md). This illustration is not a reconstruction of the study's full model: occasion-specific effects and longitudinal assumptions also matter, so $31/50$ should not be reported as an independently established [measurement reliability](../../../measurement-reliability.md) coefficient.

A [regression residual](../../../regression-residual.md) [phenotype](../../../phenotype.md) also inherits assumptions from its construction. If [adversity](../../../adversity.md) is undermeasured or a prediction model is misspecified, the [regression residual](../../../regression-residual.md) includes more than protective capacity. Genetic effects on [depression](../../../depression.md) or [anxiety](../../../anxiety.md) can appear as genetic effects on low symptom burden after [psychological stress](../../../psychological-stress.md), even without a separate buffering mechanism. Demonstrating a [psychological resilience](../../../psychological-resilience.md)-specific effect would require stronger evidence that inherited variation changes the response to comparable [exposure](../../../exposure.md), with adequate [exposure](../../../exposure.md) measurement and explicit competing models.

[Gene](../../../gene.md)–environment processes further complicate simple partitioning. [Gene-environment correlation](../../../gene-environment-correlation.md) occurs when inherited characteristics influence environments encountered or elicited, or when parental characteristics affect both inherited liability and the rearing environment. [Genotype-environment interaction](../../../genotype-environment-interaction.md) instead means that inherited differences have different consequences across environments. Neither can be inferred merely because both [genes](../../../gene.md) and [psychological stress](../../../psychological-stress.md) predict the outcome. Restricting analysis to people with a particular [exposure](../../../exposure.md) can also create [selection bias](../../../selection-bias.md) if [exposure](../../../exposure.md) is influenced by several causes relevant to symptoms.

Historical candidate-[gene](../../../gene.md) findings illustrate the need for stringent evidence. [Richard Border](../../../richard-border.md) and colleagues' large, preregistered analyses did not support widely proposed [depression](../../../depression.md) candidate-[gene](../../../gene.md) or candidate-[gene](../../../gene.md)-by-environment effects. This challenges those specific claims and the power of small discovery studies; it does not demonstrate that all interactions are absent. [Replication](../../../replication-in-experimental-design.md), correction for multiple testing, ancestry-aware analyses and precise [phenotype](../../../phenotype.md) definitions are essential before a plausible biological story becomes a reliable finding.[2019, candidate-variant, gene and interaction tests](../../../no-support-for-historic-candidate-gene-or-candidate-gene-by-interaction-hypotheses-for-major-depression-across-multiple-large-samples-by-richard-border-and-others.md)

Finally, high [heritability](../../../heritability.md) does not mean an outcome cannot be changed. It describes sources of variation under a particular range of environments, not the response to an environment absent from the data. Genetic effects can act through modifiable developmental systems, and external support can benefit people across inherited-risk levels. Predictive accuracy and equity can also deteriorate when scores are transferred to populations unlike the discovery sample.

**[Genes](../../../gene.md) contribute meaningfully to liability and to particular measured adaptation [phenotypes](../../../phenotype.md), while evidence for specific protective mechanisms is narrower than evidence for [heritability](../../../heritability.md).** The strongest account combines family and molecular results with explicit [exposure](../../../exposure.md) and developmental models, rather than assigning a fixed genetic percentage to an individual or replacing environmental prevention with genetic classification.
