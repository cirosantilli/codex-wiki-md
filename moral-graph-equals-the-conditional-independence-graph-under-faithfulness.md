# Moral graph equals the conditional independence graph under faithfulness

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional independence graph](conditional-independence-graph.md)

If [D-separation](d-separation.md) and [conditional independence](conditional-independence.md) are equivalent for a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md), its [moral graph](moral-graph.md) is its [conditional independence graph](conditional-independence-graph.md). After conditioning on all other vertices, an active path can only be a direct edge or a two-edge collider through a common child: two adjacent interior vertices cannot both be colliders. These are exactly the moral edges.
