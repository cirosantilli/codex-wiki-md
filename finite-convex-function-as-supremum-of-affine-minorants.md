# Finite convex function as supremum of affine minorants

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

For a finite real [convex function](convex-function.md) on an entire real [vector space](vector-space-split.md), apply the [convex domination form of the Hahn-Banach theorem](convex-domination-form-of-the-hahn-banach-theorem.md) to $\phi(y+z)-\phi(y)$ and the zero functional on $\{0\}$. It supplies a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $\ell_y$ dominated by this translated function. The [affine function](affine-function.md) $a_y(x)=\phi(y)+\ell_y(x-y)$ lies below $\phi$ everywhere and equals it at $y$. Taking the supremum proves the formula. These are algebraic affine minorants; continuity requires additional topological hypotheses.
