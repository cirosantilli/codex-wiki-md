# Mutual-information loss as conditional mutual information

↑ **Parent:** [Data processing for quantum mutual information](data-processing-for-quantum-mutual-information.md)

For a channel on $B$, use a [Stinespring dilation](stinespring-dilation.md) $B\to B'E$. Isometry invariance gives $I(A:B)=I(A:B'E)$, so the loss on discarding $E$ is $I(A:B)-I(A:B')=I(A:E\mid B')$. This [quantum conditional mutual information](quantum-conditional-mutual-information.md) is nonnegative by [Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md). The identity holds for arbitrary mixed inputs and identifies the correlations lost to the discarded environment.
