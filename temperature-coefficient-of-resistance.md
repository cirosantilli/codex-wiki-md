# Temperature coefficient of resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md)

The fractional change in [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) per unit temperature change, with geometry and other conditions specified. For a small interval around $T_0$, $R(T)\simeq R(T_0)[1+\alpha_R(T-T_0)]$. Low $|\alpha_R|$ is useful in stable [resistors](resistor.md).
