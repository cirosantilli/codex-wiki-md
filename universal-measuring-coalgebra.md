# Universal measuring coalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Measuring coalgebra](measuring-coalgebra.md)

A terminal [measuring coalgebra](measuring-coalgebra.md): every measuring map from $C$ factors uniquely through its universal measuring map by a [coalgebra](coalgebra.md) homomorphism $C\to P(A,B)$. Composition of universal measurings makes $P(A,A)$ a [bialgebra](bialgebra.md), with the identity map providing its unit.
