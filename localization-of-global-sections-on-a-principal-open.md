# Localization of global sections on a principal open

↑ **Parent:** [Global section functor](global-section-functor.md)

For a quasi-compact [separated scheme](separated-scheme.md) and a global [regular function](regular-function.md) $f$, [global sections](global-section.md) on $X_f$ are the [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md) of [global sections](global-section.md) by $f$. Choose a finite affine [open cover](open-cover.md). Its intersections are affine, so the [sheaf gluing axiom](sheaf-gluing-axiom.md) describes [global sections](global-section.md) as the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of the difference map between two finite products of affine [coordinate rings](coordinate-ring.md). [Exactness of localization](exactness-of-localization.md) preserves this [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md), and [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md) commutes with those finite products. Localizing the same cover gives the claimed [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). This applies before knowing whether $X$ itself is affine.
