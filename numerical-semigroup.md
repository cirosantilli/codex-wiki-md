# Numerical semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Semigroup](semigroup.md)

A numerical semigroup is an additive subset of the [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md) that contains zero and has finite complement. Positive generators with [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) one generate such a semigroup; the missing nonnegative integers are its gaps.
