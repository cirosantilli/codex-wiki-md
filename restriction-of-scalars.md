# Restriction of scalars

↑ **Parent:** [Change of rings](change-of-rings.md)

For a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $\theta:R\to A$, an $A$-[module](module-mathematics.md) becomes an $R$-[module](module-mathematics.md) by the displayed action. Its underlying [abelian group](abelian-group.md) stays the same.
