<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the [Beth number](../../../../beth-number.md)

$$
\boxed{\kappa=\beth_\omega=\sup_{n<\omega}\beth_n,\qquad\beth_0=\aleph_0,\quad\beth_{n+1}=2^{\beth_n}.}
$$

[Cantor's theorem](../../../../cantor-s-theorem.md) makes this sequence strictly increasing. It is a countable cofinal sequence in $\kappa$, while no finite set is cofinal in the [limit ordinal](../../../../limit-ordinal.md) $\kappa$. Hence $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)=\aleph_0$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{2^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}=2^{\aleph_0}=\beth_1<\beth_\omega=\kappa.}
$$

Thus [Beth omega is a singular strong limit above the continuum](../../../../beth-omega-is-a-singular-strong-limit-above-the-continuum.md). No [Continuum hypothesis](../../../../continuum-hypothesis.md) is needed for the example.
