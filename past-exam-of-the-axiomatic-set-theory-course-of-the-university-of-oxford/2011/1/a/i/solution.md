The [cumulative hierarchy](../../../../../cumulative-hierarchy.md) is the [transfinite recursion](../../../../../transfinite-recursion.md)

$$
\boxed{V_0=\varnothing,\qquad V_{\alpha+1}=\mathcal P(V_\alpha),\qquad V_\lambda=\bigcup_{\alpha<\lambda}V_\alpha\quad(\lambda\text{ a nonzero limit ordinal}).}
$$

Here $\mathcal P$ is the [power set](../../../../../power-set.md). The class $V=\bigcup_{\alpha\in\mathrm{Ord}}V_\alpha$ consists of all sets in [ZF](../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md), by the [Axiom of foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) and [Axiom schema of replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md).
