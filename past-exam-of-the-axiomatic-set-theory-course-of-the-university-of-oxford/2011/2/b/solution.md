<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Begin with [bounded formulas in set theory](../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md). Atomic membership and equality have the same interpretation on shared elements of $U_1\subseteq U_2$, each a [transitive class](../../../../transitive-class.md). Boolean connectives preserve agreement. For a bounded quantifier with bound $a\in U_1$, every member of $a$ lies in $U_1$ by transitivity. Its quantifier range is therefore exactly the same in both structures. [Structural induction](../../../../structural-induction.md) proves [set-theoretic absoluteness](../../../../set-theoretic-absoluteness.md) for every [bounded formula in set theory](../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md).

A [Sigma-one formula in set theory](../../../../sigma-one-formula-in-set-theory.md) is upward absolute: witnesses in $U_1$ are still witnesses in $U_2$, and the bounded matrix agrees. A [Pi-one formula in set theory](../../../../pi-one-formula-in-set-theory.md) is downward absolute, by negation or by restricting its universal quantifiers.

For the given [Delta-one formula modulo ZF](../../../../delta-one-formula-modulo-zf.md) $\varphi$, select a $\Sigma_1$ equivalent $\sigma$ and a $\Pi_1$ equivalent $\pi$. Both structures satisfy [ZF](../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md), so both equivalences hold in both structures. Upward absoluteness of $\sigma$ proves the forward implication, and downward absoluteness of $\pi$ proves the reverse implication. Thus [Delta-one absoluteness](../../../../delta-one-absoluteness.md) gives

$$
\boxed{U_1\models\varphi(\mathbf a)\iff U_2\models\varphi(\mathbf a)\qquad(\mathbf a\in U_1^n).}
$$
