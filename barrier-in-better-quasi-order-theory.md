# Barrier in better-quasi-order theory

↑ **Parent:** [Better-quasi-ordering](better-quasi-ordering.md)

A barrier on an infinite $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ is a family of nonempty finite increasing sequences, viewed as finite subsets, no one contained properly in another, such that every infinite subset of $A$ has a member of the family as an initial segment. Write $s\triangleleft t$ if there is a finite increasing sequence $u$ of which $s$ is an initial segment and of which $t$ is an initial segment after its first term is deleted. This shift relation underlies the good-array definition of [better-quasi-ordering](better-quasi-ordering.md).
