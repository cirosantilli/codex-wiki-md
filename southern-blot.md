# Southern blot

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

Detection of selected [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) fragments after separation and transfer to a [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md), using a complementary probe. A virus-specific probe distinguishes viral from abundant host [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) in a mixed sample.
