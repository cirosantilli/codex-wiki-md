# Orthogonal projection matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal projection onto a finite-dimensional subspace](orthogonal-projection-onto-a-finite-dimensional-subspace.md)

A complex matrix $P$ represents an [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) exactly when

$$
P^2=P=P^\dagger.
$$

Its image and kernel are orthogonal complements.
