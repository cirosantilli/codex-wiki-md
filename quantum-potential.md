# Quantum potential

↑ **Parent:** [de Broglie-Bohm theory](de-broglie-bohm-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_potential)

The amplitude-dependent extra potential in the [Madelung equations](madelung-equations.md). Taking the [gradient](gradient.md) of their phase equation and using the [guidance equation](guidance-equation.md) yields $mD\mathbf v/Dt=-\nabla(V+Q)$, where $D/Dt$ is the [material derivative](material-derivative.md). At [wavefunction](wave-function.md) nodes this local expression may be singular.
