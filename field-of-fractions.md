# Field of fractions

↑ **Parent:** [Integral domain](integral-domain.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_of_fractions)

The fraction field of an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $R$ consists of fractions $a/b$ with $a,b\in R$ and $b\ne0$, modulo the usual equivalence relation.
