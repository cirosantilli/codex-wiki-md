# Planar anchoring

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md)

A surface preference for the [nematic director](nematic-director.md) to lie tangent to the local boundary. Degenerate planar anchoring leaves the in-plane direction unselected; a directionally treated surface can instead select an azimuth. Uniform axial alignment satisfies degenerate planar anchoring on every side point of a cylindrical channel.
