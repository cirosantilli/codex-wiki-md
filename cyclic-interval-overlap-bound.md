# Cyclic interval overlap bound

↑ **Parent:** [Scan statistic](scan-statistic.md)

For independent uniformly placed cyclic intervals of length $k<d$, an overlap is possible at at most $2k-1$ starting-point offsets, or at all $d$ offsets if that number exceeds $d$. Thus the overlap [probability](probability.md) is at most $\min\{1,(2k-1)/d\}$. If $R=|S\cap T|$, then $0\leq R\leq k$ and $\mathbb E(e^{aR/k}-1)\leq(2k/d)(e^a-1)$ for $a\geq0$. The overlap law is not the [hypergeometric distribution](hypergeometric-distribution.md) for unrestricted random subsets.
