# Bergman space

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md)

For a planar domain $D$, the Bergman space $A^2(D)$ consists of [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function-split.md) with finite area [L2 norm](l2-norm.md), using the inherited [inner product](inner-product-split.md). It is a closed subspace of $L^2(D)$. Indeed the disk mean-value estimate $|f(z)|\leq(\pi r^2)^{-1/2}\|f\|_{L^2(B(z,r))}$ bounds values locally by the [L2 norm](l2-norm.md). An $L^2$-Cauchy sequence of holomorphic functions consequently converges uniformly on compact subsets to a holomorphic function, agreeing almost everywhere with its $L^2$ limit.

On the unit disk, the normalized monomials $\sqrt{(n+1)/\pi}\,z^n$, $n\geq0$, form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). Angular integration makes them orthogonal and gives $\|z^n\|_2^2=\pi/(n+1)$. Applying the same integration to a Taylor expansion gives $\|\sum_na_nz^n\|_2^2=\pi\sum_n|a_n|^2/(n+1)$, proving completeness of this basis.
