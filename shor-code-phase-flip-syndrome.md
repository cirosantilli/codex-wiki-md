# Shor-code phase-flip syndrome

↑ **Parent:** [Shor code](shor-code.md)

Group the nine physical [qubits](qubit.md) into three blocks and write $B_r$ for the product of three [Pauli X gates](pauli-x-gate.md) in block $r$. The commuting [stabilizer generators](stabilizer-generator.md) $B_1B_2$ and $B_2B_3$ have eigenvalue $+1$ on both logical codewords. A [Pauli Z gate](pauli-z-gate.md) in blocks one, two or three gives syndromes $(-,+)$, $(-,-)$ or $(+,-)$ respectively. Apply a Z gate to any one qubit of the indicated block to recover the logical state. Z errors at distinct locations within a block act identically on the code, so this correction is degenerate.
