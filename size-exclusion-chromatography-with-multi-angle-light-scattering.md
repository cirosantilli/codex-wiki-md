# Size-exclusion chromatography with multi-angle light scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md)

[Size-exclusion chromatography with multi-angle light scattering](size-exclusion-chromatography-with-multi-angle-light-scattering.md) combines separation by hydrodynamic behaviour with light-scattering and concentration measurements. With appropriate optical parameters it estimates molar mass across a peak, helping distinguish assembly from shape-dependent elution.
