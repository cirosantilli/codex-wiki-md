# Natural bijection

↑ **Parent:** [Natural isomorphism](natural-isomorphism.md)

A natural bijection between set-valued [functors](functor.md) is a [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) whose component functions are [bijections](bijection.md). Their inverse functions are automatically natural, so it is a [natural isomorphism](natural-isomorphism.md).
