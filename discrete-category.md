# Discrete category

↑ **Parent:** [Category of small categories](category-of-small-categories.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_category)

The discrete category on a [set](set-split.md) has that set as its objects and only identity morphisms. Functors from it are exactly assignments of objects in the target category.
