# Bending wave of a Keplerian disk

↑ **Parent:** [Hermite vertical mode of an isothermal disk](hermite-vertical-mode-of-an-isothermal-disk.md)

For the $n=1$, $m=1$ [Hermite vertical mode of an isothermal disk](hermite-vertical-mode-of-an-isothermal-disk.md), the local dispersion relation is $[(\omega-\Omega_K)^2-\Omega_K^2]^2=k^2c_s^2(\omega-\Omega_K)^2$. Its low inertial-frame frequency branches satisfy $\omega\simeq\pm kc_s/2$. These [waves](wave.md) transport a changing tilt of successive annuli, or a warp, with [group velocity](group-velocity.md) $\pm c_s/2$. The vertical velocity is approximately independent of height. A rigid tilt is the zero-wavenumber, zero-pattern-frequency limit; the gas still executes vertical orbital motion at frequency $\Omega_K$ in its rotating frame.
