# Horizon angular velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md)

In an axisymmetric stationary spacetime, the [horizon angular velocity](horizon-angular-velocity.md) is the constant coefficient in the null horizon generator $\chi=k+\Omega_Hm$, where $k$ is the stationary Killing vector normalized at infinity and $m$ generates axial orbits with period $2\pi$. It measures rotation relative to the stationary frame at infinity. For a [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md), $\Omega_H=a/(r_+^2+a^2)$. It enters the rotating physical-process first law through the horizon flux of $E-\Omega_HJ$.
