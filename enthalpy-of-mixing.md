# Enthalpy of mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md)

The mixture enthalpy minus the sum of the unmixed component enthalpies at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) and pressure. Its sign does not alone determine miscibility, which depends on the full [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md).
