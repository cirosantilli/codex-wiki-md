<h1 id="sine-gordon-backlund-transformation">Sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon theory](sine-gordon-theory.md)

For the angular field $u=\beta\phi$ and coordinates $\xi=m(x+t)/2$, $\eta=m(x-t)/2$, let $w=(v+u)/2$ and $d=(v-u)/2$. The displayed relations imply $u_{\xi\eta}=\sin u$ and $v_{\xi\eta}=\sin v$. They define a [Bäcklund transformation](backlund-transformation.md) with the reciprocal parameter convention used in the [Bianchi permutability for sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformations](bianchi-permutability-for-sine-gordon-backlund-transformations.md). Physical-field trigonometric arguments include the coupling $\beta$.
