# Perpendicular lines

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)

Two intersecting [straight lines](straight-line.md) are perpendicular if their angle is $90^\circ$. Their [direction vectors](direction-vector.md) have zero [dot product](dot-product.md).
