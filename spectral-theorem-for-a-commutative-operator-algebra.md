# Spectral theorem for a commutative operator algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Projection-valued measure](projection-valued-measure.md)

If $A$ is a commutative [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) of bounded operators on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) containing its identity, there is a unique regular [projection-valued measure](projection-valued-measure.md) $E$ on the [character space](character-space-of-an-algebra.md) $\Phi_A$ such that $E(\Phi_A)=I$ and $a=\int_{\Phi_A}\widehat a\,dE$ for every $a\in A$, where $\widehat a$ is the [Gelfand transform](gelfand-representation.md).
