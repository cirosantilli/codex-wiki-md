# Expected seller revenue

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanism design](mechanism-design.md)

The expectation of the seller's total receipts over the buyers' valuation types and any randomization in the sales mechanism. In an [auction](auction.md) it is the sum of the bidders' expected payments; with a [posted price](posted-price.md) it is price times sale probability. It does not subtract selling costs or the seller's opportunity cost of retaining the item, so it is revenue rather than profit.
