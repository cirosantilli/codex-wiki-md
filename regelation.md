# Regelation

↑ **Parent:** [Basal sliding](basal-sliding.md)

[Pressure](pressure-split.md) changes the melting temperature of [ice](ice.md) according to the [Clapeyron equation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md). [Ice](ice.md) pushed against an obstacle can melt on its high-[pressure](pressure-split.md) side and refreeze downstream where [pressure](pressure-split.md) is lower. Conduction of the released [latent heat](latent-heat.md) back towards the melting region permits motion around the obstacle. Small obstacles favor this heat-transfer mechanism; larger ones can be bypassed mainly by [ice](ice.md) deformation.
