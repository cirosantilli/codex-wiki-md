# Radiation reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md)

[Radiation reaction](radiation-reaction.md) is the influence on a source's motion of its own electromagnetic field, including the balance of radiated [energy](energy-split.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) with changes of the near field. For sufficiently weak radiation loss, an averaged [power](power.md) balance can give the leading damping law without solving the full self-field dynamics. Replacing a constant-frequency radiation formula by its instantaneous slowly varying value requires an additional time-scale assumption; it is not an exact treatment of arbitrarily rapid motion changes.
