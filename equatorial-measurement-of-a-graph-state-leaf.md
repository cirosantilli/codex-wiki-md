# Equatorial measurement of a graph-state leaf

↑ **Parent:** [Graph state](graph-state.md)

Let $1$ be a leaf with neighbour $2$ and let the graph on the other [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) be generated by the [Controlled-Z gate](controlled-z-gate.md) product $E_{\mathrm{rest}}$. If the leaf has [Pauli Z gate](pauli-z-gate.md) frame $Z_1^r$, measuring it at angle $\alpha$ with result $s$ applies [one-bit teleportation](one-bit-teleportation.md) and leaves

$$
E_{\mathrm{rest}}\left(X_2^sJ(\alpha)_2Z_2^r|+\rangle_2\otimes|+\rangle_{\mathrm{other}}\right).
$$

Any known [Pauli Z gate](pauli-z-gate.md) frames on other [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) remain as additional factors before those [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md)' input states. The identity $J(\alpha)Z^r=X^rJ(\alpha)$ combines the leaf byproduct with the measurement result.
