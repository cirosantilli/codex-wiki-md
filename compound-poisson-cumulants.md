# Compound Poisson cumulants

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md)

Conditioning on the [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) count in a random sum gives $\mathbb Ee^{tS}=\exp(\lambda(M_X(t)-1))$ wherever finite. Differentiation at zero gives the displayed [cumulants](cumulant.md) when the relevant derivatives exist. The aggregate [variance](variance-split.md) is the count rate times the raw second claim [moment](moment.md), not just the claim [variance](variance-split.md). Positive [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) arguments need an exponential [moment](moment.md); finite first and second claim [moments](moment.md) suffice for the aggregate [mean](expected-value.md) and [variance](variance-split.md) by conditioning or right derivatives at zero of the [Laplace transform of a nonnegative random variable](laplace-transform-of-a-nonnegative-random-variable.md).
