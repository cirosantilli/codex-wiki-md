# Incompressible inextensible-fibre plane strain

↑ **Parent:** [Inextensible fibre constraint](inextensible-fibre-constraint.md)

Let $e=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$ be the current fibre direction and $n=(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta)$. [Inextensibility](inextensible-filament.md) fixes the first column of the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) to $e$, and [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) fixes the second to $\gamma e+n$. Commuting mixed derivatives gives $\gamma_{,1}=\theta_{,1}$ and $\theta_{,2}=\gamma\theta_{,1}$. Thus $\gamma=\theta+f(X_2)$, and curves tangent to $(-\gamma,1)$ map to straight transverse curves with constant $\theta$.
