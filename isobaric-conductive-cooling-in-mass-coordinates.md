# Isobaric conductive cooling in mass coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md)

With fixed [pressure](pressure.md) $p$, let $A=\mu p/\mathcal R$, so $\rho=A/T$. A material mass coordinate has $\partial_x=\rho\partial_m$ and the material time derivative becomes $\partial_t|_m$. The energy and continuity equations then give the displayed equation with $\tau=Ct$ and $C=(\gamma-1)(\mu/\mathcal R)^2p/\gamma$. A mass integral from a fixed origin is material only when the [mass flux](mass-flux.md) there vanishes; otherwise subtract its time-integrated flux to label parcels. Uniform [pressure](pressure.md) is a low-Mach approximation when sound crossing is faster than thermal evolution.
