# Dense immersed cylinder in a three-dimensional torus

↑ **Parent:** [Integral manifold](integral-manifold.md)

The map descends to an injective [immersion](immersion.md) from $\mathbb R\times(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)$ into the [three-dimensional torus](three-dimensional-torus.md). Injectivity follows because an integer difference in $s$ with an integer difference in $\alpha s$ must be zero. Its image is dense by an [irrational rotation of the circle](irrational-rotation.md), and is tangent to the commuting independent fields $\partial_x+\alpha\partial_z$ and $\partial_y$. It is a [leaf of a regular foliation](leaf-of-a-regular-foliation.md), but cannot be an [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md): in an embedding chart the coordinate plane would be locally closed yet contain a dense ambient subset.
