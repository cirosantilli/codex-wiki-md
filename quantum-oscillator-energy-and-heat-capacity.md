# Quantum-oscillator energy and heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal partition function of a quantum harmonic oscillator](thermal-partition-function-of-a-quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For one [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) of [frequency](frequency.md) $\Omega$, $Z=e^{-x/2}/(1-e^{-x})$ with $x=\hbar\Omega/(k_BT)$. Differentiating $\ln Z$ gives $U=\hbar\Omega[1/2+1/(e^x-1)]$ and $C_V=k_Bx^2e^x/(e^x-1)^2$. [Zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) leaves $U\to\hbar\Omega/2$ as $T\to0$ but contributes no [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). Excitation freeze-out makes $C_V$ exponentially small, while high [temperature](temperature-split.md) gives $U\sim k_BT$ and $C_V\to k_B$.
