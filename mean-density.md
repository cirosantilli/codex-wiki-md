# Mean density

↑ **Parent:** [Density](density.md)

The mean [mass density](density.md) of a body is its total [mass](mass.md) divided by its [volume](volume.md). A nonuniform density distribution can have the same mean as a uniform one; orbital information that determines only $M/V$ does not determine the internal density profile.
