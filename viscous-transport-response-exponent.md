# Viscous transport response exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous instability of an accretion disk](viscous-instability-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

The response to a small [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) perturbation is $\delta(\bar\nu\Sigma)=q\bar\nu\delta\Sigma$. For an instantaneous law $\bar\nu\propto r^a\Sigma^b$, $q=b+1$. This exponent is not the orbital shear exponent or a satellite-to-star mass ratio, which also often use the letter $q$.
