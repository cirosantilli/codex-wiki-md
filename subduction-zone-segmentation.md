# Subduction zone segmentation

↑ **Parent:** [Subduction zone](subduction-zone.md)

Along-strike changes in the structure, deformation, coupling or thermal state of a [subduction zone](subduction-zone.md). Segments inferred from [megathrust locking](megathrust-locking.md), [episodic tremor and slip](episodic-tremor-and-slip.md), past [earthquake](earthquake.md) ruptures, [slab dip](slab-dip.md) and [seismic tomography](seismic-tomography.md) need not have identical boundaries. A common spatial pattern can motivate a physical hypothesis, but does not alone prove a persistent rupture barrier or identify a unique cause.
