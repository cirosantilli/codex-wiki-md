# Power maps of a reciprocal-coordinate hyperbola

↑ **Parent:** [Complex analysis](complex-analysis-split.md)

Let $a>0$, $t>0$ and $z=t+ia/t$. The real and imaginary coordinates obey $XY=a$, a [hyperbola](hyperbola.md). Put $s=t^2-a^2/t^2$, which is strictly increasing. Then $z^2=s+2ia$ traces a horizontal line, and $z^4=s^2-4a^2+4ias$ traces a [parabola](parabola.md). Eliminating $s$ gives the displayed quadratic relation between [real part](real-part.md) and [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md). The value of $t$ fixes an orientation and determines which segment is traversed; the full real parabola is obtained as $t$ ranges over $(0,\infty)$.
