# Adjoint triplet Higgs spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [SO(3) vector Higgs model](so-3-vector-higgs-model.md)

For a gauged [real scalar triplet](real-scalar-triplet.md) with vacuum $ve_3$ and generators $(t^a)_{bc}=-i\epsilon^{abc}$, the covariant [kinetic term](kinetic-term.md) yields gauge-boson squared masses $g^2v^2\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$. Two [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md) become longitudinal vector modes through the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md). One radial scalar, two massive three-polarization vector fields and one massless two-polarization field remain. The physical count $3+3\cdot2=1+2\cdot3+2=9$ is unchanged. In [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md), the scalar [kinetic term](kinetic-term.md) includes $\frac12g^2(v+h)^2[(B^1)^2+(B^2)^2]$.
