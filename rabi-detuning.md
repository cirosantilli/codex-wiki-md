# Rabi detuning

↑ **Parent:** [Driven two-level Rabi model](driven-two-level-rabi-model.md)

The [Rabi detuning](rabi-detuning.md) is the unperturbed transition [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) minus the drive [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) in this convention. Its sign controls the longitudinal rotation of the [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md); the single-pulse population formula is even in $\delta$. Reversing the definition of [Rabi detuning](rabi-detuning.md) requires reversing its sign in the rotating-frame [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) as well.
