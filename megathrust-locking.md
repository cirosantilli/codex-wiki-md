# Megathrust locking

↑ **Parent:** [Megathrust earthquake](megathrust-earthquake.md)

Suppression of relative slip on part of a [megathrust](megathrust.md) during an interval of plate convergence, producing a slip deficit and elastic deformation. [GNSS](gnss.md), leveling and offshore geodesy constrain it through a fault-geometry and deformation model. A locking fraction is therefore an inferred kinematic quantity; it is not a direct measurement of friction or the magnitude of the next [earthquake](earthquake.md).
