# Gauge coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field](gauge-field.md)

A gauge coupling is the constant multiplying the gauge potential in the [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md). Its normalization depends on that of the Lie-algebra generators; each simple or Abelian factor of a gauge algebra may have an independent coupling.
