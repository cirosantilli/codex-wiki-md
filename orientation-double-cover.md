# Orientation double cover

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

An oriented coordinate neighborhood of a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) gives two sheets distinguished by the sign of its [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md). On an overlap, the sheets agree where the two local orientation forms differ by a positive function. These open agreement sets define a two-sheeted smooth [covering space](covering-space.md), whose projection is locally a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md).
