# Constant superpotential in global supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Superpotential](superpotential.md)

Adding a constant to a global [superpotential](superpotential.md) does not change the component action because the chiral [superspace](superspace.md) integral of that constant vanishes. Neither the [F-term scalar potential](f-term-scalar-potential.md) nor the [fermion](fermion.md) mass matrix depends on it. This statement does not extend to [supergravity](supergravity.md), where the [superpotential](superpotential.md) itself enters the gravitational potential.
