# Smoothing spline

↑ **Parent:** [Nonparametric regression](nonparametric-regression.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smoothing_spline)

A penalized function estimate balancing a residual loss against an integrated [derivative](derivative.md) penalty. For [squared-error loss](squared-error-loss.md) and the [second derivative roughness penalty](second-derivative-roughness-penalty.md), the estimate is a [cubic smoothing spline](cubic-smoothing-spline.md). Higher [derivative](derivative.md) orders give other spline degrees.
