# Magnetic rigidity

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_rigidity)

[Magnetic rigidity](magnetic-rigidity.md) measures the resistance of a [charged particle](charged-particle.md)'s [momentum](momentum-split.md) to bending by a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The [relativistic cyclotron orbit](relativistic-cyclotron-orbit.md) gives $p_\perp=|q|Br$, where $r$ is the transverse [gyroradius](gyroradius.md). For motion perpendicular to a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), $p_\perp=p$ is the whole [momentum](momentum-split.md). A measured [magnetic rigidity](magnetic-rigidity.md) fixes [momentum](momentum-split.md) per magnitude of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md); it does not by itself fix [mass](mass.md), [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), or the sign of the [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). This distinction makes the [Anderson positron track experiment](anderson-positron-track-experiment.md) an especially revealing application of the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md).
