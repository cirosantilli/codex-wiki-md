# Weak coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Perturbation series](perturbation-series.md)

[Weak coupling](weak-coupling.md) means a suitably dimensionless interaction strength is small. In [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md), $\nu_sV\ll1$ gives a gap exponentially small compared with the pairing-shell energy cutoff; the expansion parameter is not the dimensional quantity $V$ alone.
