# Subformula replacement theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Subformula](subformula.md)

Replacing one designated [subformula occurrence](subformula.md) in a [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) by another [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) preserves well-formedness. Induct on the context surrounding the occurrence: at the root the result is the [replacement](subformula-replacement-theorem.md) [formula](logical-formula.md); under a [connective](logical-connective.md) apply the induction hypothesis to the affected child and rebuild the parent using its [formation rule](formal-grammar.md).
