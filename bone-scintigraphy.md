# Bone scintigraphy

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear medicine](nuclear-medicine.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bone_scintigraphy)

Functional imaging of bone-seeking tracer uptake with a [gamma camera](gamma-camera.md) or [single-photon emission computed tomography](single-photon-emission-computed-tomography.md). Uptake patterns can reveal abnormal bone turnover.
