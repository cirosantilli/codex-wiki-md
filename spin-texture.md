# Spin texture

↑ **Parent:** [Spin polarization](spin-polarization.md)

A spin texture describes how a [spin expectation value](spin-expectation-value.md) or [spin polarization](spin-polarization.md) varies with [position](position.md) or [wavevector](wavevector.md). It specifies a [vector field](vector-field.md), including its magnitude when that matters; a direction field alone is undefined wherever that magnitude vanishes. A [Rashba spin texture](rashba-spin-texture.md) is an example in which [spin expectation values](spin-expectation-value.md) wind around circles in the plane of [wavevectors](wavevector.md).
