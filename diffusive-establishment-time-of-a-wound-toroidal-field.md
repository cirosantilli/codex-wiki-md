# Diffusive establishment time of a wound toroidal field

↑ **Parent:** [Steady toroidal induction by spherical differential rotation](steady-toroidal-induction-by-spherical-differential-rotation.md)

With a fixed axial seed field and prescribed localized [differential rotation](differential-rotation.md), the winding source is time independent. Early induction gives $B_\phi\sim B_0\Omega t$, while the steady balance has $B_\phi\sim B_0\Omega\ell^2/\eta$. Their comparison gives the [magnetic diffusion](magnetic-diffusion.md) time, not merely the rotation period. It diverges as conductivity becomes infinite. In an unbounded domain, this is a local equilibration estimate on scale $\ell$, not finite-time exact convergence everywhere. [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) feedback may invalidate the prescribed-flow approximation sooner.
