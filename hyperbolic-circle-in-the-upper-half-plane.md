# Hyperbolic circle in the upper half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic circle](hyperbolic-circle.md)

In the [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md), a [hyperbolic circle](hyperbolic-circle.md) of radius $R$ centred at $u+iv$ is the Euclidean circle $(x-u)^2+(y-v\cosh R)^2=v^2\sinh^2R$. Its Euclidean centre is shifted upward from its hyperbolic centre, and its Euclidean radius is $v\sinh R$. The lower height $ve^{-R}$ is positive. The formula follows by mapping the centre to zero in the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md) and using radial distance $2\operatorname{artanh}\rho$.
