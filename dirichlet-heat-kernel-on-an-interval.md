# Dirichlet heat kernel on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

For the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) on $(0,L)$,

$$
H_L(x,y,t)=\frac2L\sum_{n\ge1}e^{-(n\pi/L)^2t}\sin(n\pi x/L)\sin(n\pi y/L).
$$

It is also obtained by the [method of images](method-of-images.md), taking the difference between Gaussian sources at $y+2jL$ and $-y+2jL$. The [eigenfunction expansion](eigenfunction-expansion.md) is useful at long times; the image expansion is useful at short times.
