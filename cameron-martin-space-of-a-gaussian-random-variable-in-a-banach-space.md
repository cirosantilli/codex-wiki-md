# Cameron-Martin space of a Gaussian random variable in a Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Cameron-Martin space of a Gaussian measure](cameron-martin-space-of-a-gaussian-measure.md)

Let $\mathcal G$ be the [first Gaussian chaos](first-gaussian-chaos.md) of a centered [Gaussian random variable in a Banach space](gaussian-random-variable-in-a-banach-space.md). By [Fernique's theorem](fernique-s-theorem.md), $Sg=\mathbb E[Xg]$ is a [Bochner integral](bochner-integral.md) in $B$. The map $S$ is injective, because $Sg=0$ implies $\mathbb E[g\ell(X)]=0$ for every [continuous linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) $\ell$, hence $g=0$. Its range becomes a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) under $\langle Sg,Sv\rangle_H=\mathbb E[gv]$. The embedding in $B$ is continuous by [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Its [reproducing property](reproducing-property.md) is $\langle h,S\ell(X)\rangle_H=\ell(h)$. Thus this is the [Reproducing kernel Hilbert space](reproducing-kernel-hilbert-space.md) of the random variable, including degenerate laws. For $h=Sg$, the scalar coordinate $\widehat h(X)=g$ has [variance](variance-split.md) $\|h\|_H^2$; it is generally a measurable linear coordinate rather than a continuous functional on $B$.
