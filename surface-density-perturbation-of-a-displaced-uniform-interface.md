# Surface density perturbation of a displaced uniform interface

↑ **Parent:** [Eulerian and Lagrangian fluid perturbations](eulerian-and-lagrangian-fluid-perturbations.md)

Moving a uniform-density boundary produces a delta-function sheet in the Eulerian [mass density](density.md) perturbation. For a slab displaced vertically by $\eta_\pm$, $\rho^{\prime}=\rho_0\eta_+\delta(z-H)-\rho_0\eta_-\delta(z+H)$. [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) then gives a continuous gravitational potential with derivative jumps fixed by those sheets.
