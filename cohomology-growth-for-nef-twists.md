# Cohomology growth for nef twists

↑ **Parent:** [Fujita vanishing](fujita-vanishing.md)

Let $N$ be a [nef Cartier divisor](nef-line-bundle.md) and let the [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$ have support dimension $d$. Then $h^i(X,\mathcal F(mN))=O(m^{d-i})$ for $1\leq i\leq d$. This does not require reducedness or smoothness.

Choose a very ample $A$ and fix $a$ sufficiently large for [Fujita vanishing](fujita-vanishing.md). Choose a section of $aA$ avoiding the [associated points](associated-point-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md) of $\mathcal F$. Its multiplication gives $0\to\mathcal F\to\mathcal F(aA)\to\mathcal Q\to0$, where $\mathcal Q$ has support dimension at most $d-1$. After tensoring with $\mathcal O_X(mN)$, the middle term has zero higher [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md), uniformly in $m$. Hence $h^i(\mathcal F(mN))\leq h^{i-1}(\mathcal Q(mN))$. Induct on support dimension: for $i=1$ use the [polynomial bound for sections of a fixed divisor](polynomial-bound-for-sections-of-a-fixed-divisor.md), and for $i>1$ use the induction hypothesis. Degree greater than $d$ vanishes by [Grothendieck vanishing](grothendieck-vanishing.md).
