# Isentropic temperature-volume derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell relations](maxwell-relations.md)

For a [simple compressible system](simple-compressible-system.md) at fixed particle number, assume the [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) has continuous second [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) and $T$ is a locally invertible function of [entropy](entropy-split.md) at fixed [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). Its [exact differential](exact-differential.md) $dU=T\,dS-p\,dV$ gives the [Maxwell relation](maxwell-relations.md) $T_V|_S=-p_S|_V$. Meanwhile the [constant-volume heat capacity](heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) is $C_V=T S_T|_V$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) therefore gives

$$
\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial V}\right)_S=-\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_V\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial S}\right)_V=-\frac{T}{C_V}\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_V.
$$

For one mole of an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) with constant molar $C_V>0$, this becomes $T_V|_S=-RT/(C_VV)$, whose [integral curves](integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) satisfy $TV^{R/C_V}=\text{constant}$. It expresses cooling on reversible [adiabatic expansion](adiabatic-expansion.md) in terms of measurable thermal and mechanical derivatives.
