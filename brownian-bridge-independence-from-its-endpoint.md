# Brownian bridge independence from its endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md)

For standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), the [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md) $U_t=B_t-tB_1$, $0\leq t\leq1$, is independent of $B_1$. Every finite vector of bridge coordinates is jointly [multivariate normal](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) with $B_1$, and its cross-[covariances](covariance.md) with $B_1$ vanish. The [independence of uncorrelated jointly normal variables](independence-of-uncorrelated-jointly-normal-variables.md) and [independence extended from generating pi-systems](independence-extended-from-generating-pi-systems.md) prove independence of the whole [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md).
