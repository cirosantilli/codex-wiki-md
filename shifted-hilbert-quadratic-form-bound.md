# Shifted Hilbert quadratic-form bound

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert matrix](hilbert-matrix.md)

For real $a_r$, put $h(z)=\sum_{r=1}^na_rz^r$. The quadratic form equals $\int_0^1h(x)^2dx$, proving its nonnegativity. Apply the [centred logarithmic keyhole identity](centred-logarithmic-keyhole-identity.md) bound to the [entire function](entire-function.md) $h^2$. Since $|h(e^{i\theta})^2|=|h(e^{i\theta})|^2$, orthogonality of the finite Fourier sum gives $\int_0^{2\pi}|h(e^{i\theta})|^2d\theta=2\pi\sum_ra_r^2$. Multiplication by the contour estimate's factor $1/2$ proves the upper bound. The matrix $(1/(r+s+1))$ is the principal submatrix of a [Hilbert matrix](hilbert-matrix.md) obtained by deleting its first row and column.
