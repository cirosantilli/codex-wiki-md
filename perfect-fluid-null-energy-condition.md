# Perfect-fluid null energy condition

↑ **Parent:** [Null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md)

For $T_{ab}=(\rho+p)u_au_b+pg_{ab}$ and nonzero null $k$, contraction gives $T_{ab}k^ak^b=(\rho+p)(u\cdot k)^2$. A timelike fluid velocity has nonzero scalar product with every nonzero null vector, so the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md) is exactly the displayed inequality. Individual nonnegativity of pressure or density is unnecessary for null focusing. A [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) term has zero null contraction.
