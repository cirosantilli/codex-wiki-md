# Transport mean free path

↑ **Parent:** [Mean free path](mean-free-path.md)

The length associated with relaxation of carrier momentum. In a parabolic band, [electron mobility](electron-mobility.md) measures the [momentum relaxation time](momentum-relaxation-time.md), giving the displayed expression. Frequent weak small-angle scattering can make the single-particle scattering time shorter than the transport time, and [electron phase coherence](electron-phase-coherence.md) has its own lifetime.
