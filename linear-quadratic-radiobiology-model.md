# Linear-quadratic radiobiology model

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation therapy](radiation-therapy.md)

A model for surviving fraction $S$ after radiation dose $d$. Independent fully repaired fractions add their logarithmic effects. For $N$ equal fractions and no repopulation, dividing the total log kill by $\alpha$ gives the [biologically effective dose](biologically-effective-dose.md) $Nd(1+d/(\alpha/\beta))$. Comparing tissue types requires their own parameters and the same stated repair assumptions.
