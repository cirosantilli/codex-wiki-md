# Cup-product construction of local reciprocity

↑ **Parent:** [Local Artin map](local-artin-map.md)

For $G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, [Tate cohomology of a finite group](tate-cohomology-of-a-finite-group.md) identifies the source as $\widehat H^{-2}(G,\mathbb Z)$ and the target as $\widehat H^0(G,L^\times)$. Cup product with the [local fundamental class](local-fundamental-class.md) is an isomorphism; its inverse is the finite [Local Artin map](local-artin-map.md). The algebraic cup-product isomorphism applies because every subgroup has first cohomology zero by [Hilbert theorem 90](hilbert-s-theorem-90.md), and second cohomology cyclic of order equal to its size by the [local Brauer invariant](local-brauer-invariant.md). Compatibility with restriction and corestriction yields the tower and norm compatibility of [Local Artin reciprocity](local-artin-reciprocity.md).
