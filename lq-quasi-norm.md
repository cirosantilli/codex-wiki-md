# Lq quasi-norm

↑ **Parent:** [Lp norm](lp-norm.md)

The displayed quantity is homogeneous and separates zero, but generally violates the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md). Its $q$th power is subadditive because $(a+b)^q\le a^q+b^q$ for nonnegative $a,b$. Minimizing it is equivalent to minimizing its $q$th power. On finite [vectors](vector.md) it is a standard nonconvex sparsity penalty.
