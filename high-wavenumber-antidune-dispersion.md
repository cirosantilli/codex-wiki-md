# High-wavenumber antidune dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Antidune](antidune.md)

In the fast-[water](water.md) limit of an erodible-bed [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md) model, let $e=E'(1)>0$, $q_1=q'(1)>0$ and $\gamma>0$ be the suspended-load relaxation coefficient. [Linearization](linearization.md) and elimination give the [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $\lambda=-k^2[q_1+\beta e/(\gamma+ik)]/[3\delta+ik(F^2-1)]$. Its real part tends to the finite constant in the title, and phase [velocity](velocity.md) tends to $-q_1/(F^2-1)$. Thus the unstable upstream branch requires [supercritical flow](supercritical-flow.md) and enough sediment feedback to overcome [friction](friction.md); subcritical flow has the opposite propagation sign and decay at leading order. The near-critical limit needs a separate balance.
