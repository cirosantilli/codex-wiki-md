# Turbulent magnetic pumping

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md)

[Turbulent magnetic pumping](turbulent-magnetic-pumping.md) is an effective transport of the mean [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) produced by correlations in inhomogeneous or anisotropic turbulence. Its contribution to the [mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md) is $\boldsymbol{\mathcal E}_{\rm pump}=\boldsymbol\gamma\times\overline{\mathbf B}$. In the mean induction equation this enters as $\nabla\times(\boldsymbol\gamma\times\overline{\mathbf B})$, the same algebraic form as [advection](advection.md) by a [velocity](velocity.md) $\boldsymbol\gamma$. Thus it supplements the mean material [velocity](velocity.md) without requiring an equal mean mass flow. Downward pumping can help retain or couple fields in a [solar dynamo](solar-dynamo.md).
