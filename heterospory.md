# Heterospory

↑ **Parent:** [Spore](spore.md)

Production of distinct [microspores](microspore.md) and [megaspores](megaspore.md) that give rise to male and female [gametophytes](gametophyte.md). Retention of developmental stages within spore walls changes protection and provisioning without itself removing the need for water-dependent sperm movement in heterosporous ferns.
