# ASCA: MCG-6-30-15 by NASA HEASARC

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by NASA HEASARC](work-by-nasa-heasarc.md)

The mission result description reports a broad asymmetric iron K-alpha profile with a low-energy wing relative to the approximately $6.4\ \mathrm{keV}$ rest [energy](energy-split.md). It discusses [gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md) and the [relativistic Doppler effect](relativistic-doppler-effect.md) in a disk model. The page presents the original observational result and its interpretation, rather than establishing that every possible [absorption](absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) or illumination model is excluded.
