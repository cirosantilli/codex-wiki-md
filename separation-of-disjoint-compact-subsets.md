# Separation of disjoint compact subsets

↑ **Parent:** [Separation of a point and a compact set](separation-of-a-point-and-a-compact-set.md)

Two [disjoint](disjoint-sets.md) [compact subsets](compact-space.md) of a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) have [disjoint](disjoint-sets.md) [open neighbourhoods](open-neighbourhood.md). For every $p\in P$, [separation of a point and a compact set](separation-of-a-point-and-a-compact-set.md) gives [disjoint](disjoint-sets.md) [open sets](open-set.md) $G_p\ni p$ and $H_p\supseteq Q$. Choose finitely many $G_p$ covering $P$, then take their [union](set-union.md) for $U$ and the matching finite [intersection](set-intersection.md) of the $H_p$ for $V$. The [compactness](compact-space.md) of both [subsets](subset.md) is used, once in the point-versus-[compact set](compact-space.md) result and once in the outer finite selection. The ambient [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) need not be [compact](compact-space.md) or a [normal space](normal-space.md).
