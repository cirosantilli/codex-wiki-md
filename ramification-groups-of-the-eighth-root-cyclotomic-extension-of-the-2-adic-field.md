# Ramification groups of the eighth-root cyclotomic extension of the 2-adic field

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups](uniformizer-criterion-for-lower-ramification-groups.md)

For $\mathbb Q_2(\zeta_8)/\mathbb Q_2$, the shifted polynomial $(1+T)^4+1$ is [Eisenstein](eisenstein-criterion.md) and $\zeta_8-1$ is a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md). The three nonidentity automorphisms have displacement [valuations](valuation.md) two, four and two. Thus the lower breaks are one and three, the upper breaks one and two, and the [different exponent from ramification groups](different-exponent-from-ramification-groups.md) is eight.
