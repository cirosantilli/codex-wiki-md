# Two-photon spontaneous emission

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md)

An excited state can decay by emitting two [photons](photon.md) whose energies sum to the transition energy. In [hydrogen](hydrogen.md), this permits the metastable $2s$ state to reach the [ground state](ground-state.md) during [cosmological recombination](recombination-cosmology.md).
