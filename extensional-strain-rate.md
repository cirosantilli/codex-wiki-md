# Extensional strain rate

↑ **Parent:** [Extensional flow](extensional-flow.md)

The [extensional strain rate](extensional-strain-rate.md) is the rate of relative stretching along a specified material direction. If a short material line of [length](length.md) $\ell$ follows a principal stretching direction, $\dot\epsilon=d\log\ell/dt$ is the corresponding [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of the [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md). For the planar model in [extensional flow](extensional-flow.md), $\dot\epsilon=\partial u_x/\partial x$; the two transverse rates are $-\dot\epsilon$ and zero. For [uniaxial extensional flow](uniaxial-extensional-flow.md), each transverse rate is $-\dot\epsilon/2$.
