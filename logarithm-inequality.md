# Logarithm inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithm](logarithm.md)

For $x>0$, [concavity](concave-function.md) of the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) gives

$$
\ln x\leq x-1.
$$

Equivalently, $\log_bx\leq(\log_be)(x-1)$ for $b>1$.
