# Perpetual motion

↑ **Parent:** [First law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perpetual_motion)

A proposed cyclic machine that continuously delivers useful [work](work.md) without the corresponding permitted [energy](energy-split.md) input. A first-kind machine violates [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md); a second-kind machine would turn heat from a single reservoir completely into work in a cycle, contrary to the [Kelvin-Planck statement](kelvin-planck-statement.md). Continuing ideal motion without withdrawing work is a different claim.
