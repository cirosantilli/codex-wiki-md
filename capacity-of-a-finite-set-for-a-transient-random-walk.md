# Capacity of a finite set for a transient random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Equilibrium measure of a finite set](equilibrium-measure-of-a-finite-set.md)

The capacity of a finite set is the total mass of its [equilibrium measure of a finite set](equilibrium-measure-of-a-finite-set.md):

$$
\operatorname{cap}(A)=\sum_xe_A(x)=\mathcal E(h_A,h_A).
$$

Capacity is monotone under inclusion, though strict inclusion need not give strict inequality.
