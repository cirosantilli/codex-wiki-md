# Rotated null contours for half-range Fourier inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Half-range Fourier transform](half-range-fourier-transform.md)

For $Q(k)=\int_0^\infty e^{-ikx}q(x)\,dx$, the [Half-range Fourier transform](half-range-fourier-transform.md) is [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) in the lower half-plane. Let $\alpha=e^{2\pi i/3}$, $E=\{0<\arg k<\pi/3\}$ and $D=\{2\pi/3<\arg k<\pi\}$, with their boundaries oriented counterclockwise. The rotated arguments $\alpha^2k$ on $E$ and $\alpha k$ on $D$ remain in the lower half-plane. For $x>0$ and transform-admissible smooth decay, the [Cauchy integral theorem](cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) and [Jordan lemma](jordan-s-lemma.md) give the displayed null integrals. Arbitrary multiples can therefore be added to [Fourier inversion](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) without changing the recovered function on the positive half-line.
