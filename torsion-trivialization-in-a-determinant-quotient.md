# Torsion trivialization in a determinant quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Evaluation determinant on a product of curves](evaluation-determinant-on-a-product-of-curves.md)

Evaluation [determinants](determinant.md) for $L\otimes P$ and $L$ have a quotient which is a rational section of $\bigotimes_i\operatorname{pr}_i^*P$, not intrinsically a scalar. If $P^N\cong\mathcal O_C$, its $N$th power becomes a [rational function](rational-function.md) after choosing that trivialization. In [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) notation, write $P=\mathcal O_C(p)$ and $\operatorname{div}(h)=Np$. With raw meromorphic evaluations the scalar expression is $r^N\prod_i h(z_i)$. The extra product accounts for the line-bundle frames; it is suppressed when evaluation already uses compatible torsion trivializations.
