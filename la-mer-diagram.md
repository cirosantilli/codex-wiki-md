# La Mer diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleation](nucleation.md)

A schematic time history of growth-species [concentration](concentration.md) showing buildup, a short [nucleation](nucleation.md) interval above an effective [nucleation](nucleation.md) threshold, then growth above the equilibrium [solubility](solubility.md) but below substantial new [nucleation](nucleation.md). The [concentration](concentration.md) history suggests how to separate particle birth from growth; it does not establish that every particle synthesis follows that mechanism.
