# Gas clumping factor

↑ **Parent:** [Density](density.md)

The [gas clumping factor](gas-clumping-factor.md) measures unresolved inhomogeneity in electron [number density](number-density.md) within a chosen averaging region. Nonnegative [variance](variance-split.md) gives $C_e\ge1$. At a uniform [temperature](temperature-split.md), [thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) scales with $\langle n_e^2\rangle$, whereas the [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) scales with $\langle n_e\rangle$. A homogeneous [cluster distance from Sunyaev-Zeldovich and X-ray signals](cluster-distance-from-sunyaev-zeldovich-and-x-ray-signals.md) inference then gives $d_{A,\mathrm{inferred}}=d_{A,\mathrm{true}}/C_e$, for the same geometry and temperature. Correlated temperature variations require additional weighted averages.
