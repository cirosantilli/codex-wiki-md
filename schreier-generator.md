# Schreier generator

↑ **Parent:** [Reidemeister–Schreier theorem](reidemeister-schreier-theorem.md)

For a subgroup $H$ of a group generated by a set $S$, choose representatives $t$ of right cosets $Ht$. If $\overline{tg}$ represents $Htg$, then $tg\,\overline{tg}^{-1}$ lies in $H$. These [Schreier generators](schreier-generator.md) generate $H$: inserting successive coset representatives along a word telescopes the word into such factors. Lifted relators can be rewritten in the same factors.
