# Dilaton Euler-characteristic weighting

↑ **Parent:** [String genus expansion](string-genus-expansion.md)

A constant [dilaton](dilaton.md) contributes $\Phi_0\chi(\Sigma)$ to the Euclidean [worldsheet](worldsheet.md) action by the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md). With [string coupling](string-coupling.md) $g_s=e^{\Phi_0}$ and [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) $\chi=2-2g$, the path-integral weight is $g_s^{2g-2}$. Canonically normalized external closed-string vertices add one power of $g_s$ each.
