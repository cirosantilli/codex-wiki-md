# Photon continuity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Photon Boltzmann hierarchy](photon-boltzmann-hierarchy.md)

In [Newtonian gauge in cosmology](newtonian-gauge.md), the [Free-streaming photon Boltzmann equation](free-streaming-photon-boltzmann-equation.md) is $\dot\Theta+\mathbf e\cdot\nabla\Theta=\dot\phi-\mathbf e\cdot\nabla\psi$. Angular averaging and the [photon angular temperature moments](photon-angular-temperature-moments.md) give the displayed [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md). Its dipole yields the [photon Euler equation](photon-euler-equation.md), with the sign of the [anisotropic stress](anisotropic-stress.md) term determined by the stress convention.
