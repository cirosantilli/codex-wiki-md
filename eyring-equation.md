# Eyring equation

↑ **Parent:** [Transition state theory](transition-state-theory.md)

A [transition state theory](transition-state-theory.md) expression relating a [rate constant](rate-constant.md) to the standard free energy of activation. For a bimolecular reaction expressed in concentration units, $k_2=(k_{\mathrm B}T/h)(c^\circ)^{-1}\exp[-\Delta G^{\ddagger\circ}/(RT)]$ in the unit-transmission approximation.
