# Distribution of a critical linear birth-death population

↑ **Parent:** [Critical branching process](critical-branching-process.md)

For equal individual birth and death rates $\lambda$ and one initial individual, the [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) is $F(z,t)=1-(1-z)/[1+\lambda t(1-z)]$. It solves $F_t=\lambda(z-1)^2F_z$ with $F(z,0)=z$, and its geometric expansion gives the displayed [probabilities](probability.md). The [mean](expected-value-split.md) remains one and the [variance](variance-split.md) is $2\lambda t$, although extinction occurs with [probability](probability.md) tending to one. The surviving fraction $1/(1+\lambda t)$ has conditional [mean](expected-value-split.md) population $1+\lambda t$.
