# Terminality of the Eilenberg-Moore adjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor](eilenberg-moore-comparison-functor.md)

The free-forgetful [adjunction](adjoint-functors.md) for the [Eilenberg-Moore category](eilenberg-moore-category.md) is terminal in the [category of adjunctions inducing a fixed monad](category-of-adjunctions-inducing-a-fixed-monad.md). The unique [morphism](morphism.md) into it is the [Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor](eilenberg-moore-comparison-functor.md) $B\mapsto(GB,G\varepsilon_B)$. Its underlying objects and arrows are forced by the forgetful [functor](functor.md), and counit compatibility forces its algebra actions.
