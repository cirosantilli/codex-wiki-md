# Pauli Y eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli eigenstate](pauli-eigenstate.md)

The two [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) rays of the [Pauli Y gate](pauli-y-gate.md) have the displayed normalized representatives and respective [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $+1$ and $-1$. They form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). Multiplication by $Y$ changes only the [global phase](global-phase.md) of each of these states; it does not exchange their [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) labels.
