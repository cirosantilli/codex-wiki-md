# Circular orbit in a quadratic central potential

↑ **Parent:** [Circular orbit](circular-orbit.md)

For $V(r)=kmr^2$, fixed nonzero [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $h$ gives the [effective potential](effective-potential.md) $V_{\rm eff}=h^2/(2mr^2)+kmr^2$. A positive-radius circular orbit exists when $k>0$, at the displayed radius, and $V_{\rm eff}''(r_0)=8km>0$ proves radial stability by the [effective potential stability criterion](effective-potential-stability-criterion.md). Its angular speed has magnitude $\sqrt{2k}$ and its small radial oscillation frequency is $\sqrt{8k}$. If $k\le0$, no such orbit with nonzero angular momentum exists; for $h=0,k>0$, the sole minimum is at the origin, not a positive-radius circular orbit.
