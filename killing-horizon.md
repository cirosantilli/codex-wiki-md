# Killing horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Null hypersurface](null-hypersurface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_horizon)

A Killing horizon is a [null hypersurface](null-hypersurface.md) whose null generators are orbits of a [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) $K$. On the horizon, $K^b\nabla_bK^a=\kappa K^a$ defines its [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md).
