# Affine three-space

↑ **Parent:** [Affine scheme](affine-scheme.md)

Affine three-space is $\operatorname{Spec}k[x_1,x_2,x_3]$.

The three-dimensional scheme has an [affine space](affine-space.md) of rational points over its base field; its scheme structure also records non-rational points and local rings.
