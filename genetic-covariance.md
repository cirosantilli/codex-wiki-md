# Genetic covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

Shared variation in inherited contributions to different [phenotypic traits](phenotypic-trait.md). It can arise from [pleiotropy](pleiotropy.md) or associations between separate causal [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md); measuring it does not distinguish these mechanisms by itself.
