# Core (group theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Core_(group_theory))

The core of a [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H\leq G$ is

$$
\operatorname{Core}_G(H)=\bigcap_{g\in G}gHg^{-1}.
$$

It is the largest [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of $G$ contained in $H$. If $H$ has finite index $k$, the action on $G/H$ gives $[G:\operatorname{Core}_G(H)]\leq k!$.
