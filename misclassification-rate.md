# Misclassification rate

↑ **Parent:** [Classification in statistical learning](classification-in-statistical-learning.md)

The misclassification rate is the expected [zero-one loss](misclassification-loss.md) of a [classification in statistical learning](classification-in-statistical-learning.md) rule. Its empirical version is the fraction of incorrectly labeled observations. Evaluation on the fitting sample gives a [training error](training-error.md); evaluation on an independent [test set](test-set.md) estimates [prediction error](prediction-error.md). For a majority-class leaf of a [classification tree](classification-tree.md), the training error count is $n_t-\max_kn_{tk}$, unlike [classification-tree deviance](classification-tree-deviance.md), which also uses the fitted class [probabilities](probability.md).
