# Optical molasses

↑ **Parent:** [Laser cooling](laser-cooling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_molasses)

Optical molasses surrounds slow [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) with opposing red-detuned [laser](laser-split.md) beams, usually three pairs along perpendicular axes. In the elementary [Doppler cooling](doppler-cooling.md) picture, each pair damps one component of [velocity](velocity.md). The result resembles viscous drag, even though the atoms are moving through a [vacuum](vacuum.md) rather than a liquid.

Uniform balanced [optical molasses](optical-molasses.md) supplies [velocity](velocity.md) [damping](damping.md), without a [restoring force](restoring-force.md) towards a chosen position. An atom at rest has zero mean [force](force-split.md) wherever it sits within the uniform illuminated region. Without noise or external [forces](force-split.md), $m\dot v=-\alpha v$ gives

$$
v(t)=v_0e^{-\alpha t/m},\qquad
x(t)=x_0+\frac{mv_0}{\alpha}\bigl(1-e^{-\alpha t/m}\bigr).
$$

The atom approaches a position determined by its initial condition, not the beam intersection. Recoil-driven [diffusion](diffusion.md) and [gravity](gravity.md) eventually remove atoms from finite beams. A long residence time in [optical molasses](optical-molasses.md) is therefore compatible with the absence of position trapping. An [optical trap](optical-tweezers.md) provides spatial confinement by a different [force](force-split.md) mechanism; a [magneto-optical trap](magneto-optical-trap.md) adds a position-dependent [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and suitable beam [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) to create a [restoring force](restoring-force.md).
