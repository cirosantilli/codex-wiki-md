# Stochastic matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_matrix)

The transition matrix of a finite [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) has entries

$$
P_{ij}=\mathbb P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i).
$$
