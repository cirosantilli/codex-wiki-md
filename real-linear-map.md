# Real-linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Linear map](linear-map.md)

A [real-linear map](real-linear-map.md) is a [linear map](linear-map.md) over the [real numbers](real-number.md). For a map from the [real line](real-line.md) to itself, real linearity means [multiplication](multiplication.md) by a fixed [real number](real-number.md), its value at one. [Rational-linear maps](rational-linear-map.md) need not be real-linear without an additional regularity condition.
