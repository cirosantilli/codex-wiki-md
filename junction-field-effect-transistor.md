# Junction field-effect transistor

↑ **Parent:** [Field-effect transistor](field-effect-transistor.md)

A [junction field-effect transistor](junction-field-effect-transistor.md) controls a conducting [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) channel using reverse-biased [p-n junctions](p-n-junction.md) at its gate. Changing gate voltage changes the [depletion region](depletion-region.md) and therefore the cross-section available to mobile [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md). A depletion-mode n-channel device normally conducts at zero gate-source voltage and is depleted by a more negative gate. The small reverse gate [electric current](electric-current.md) gives high input resistance, while a source-drain supply provides output power.
