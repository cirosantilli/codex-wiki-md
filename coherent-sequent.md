# Coherent sequent

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent logic](coherent-logic.md)

A coherent sequent is an implication $\phi\vdash_{\vec x}\psi$ between [coherent formulas](coherent-formula.md) in a common context. Its semantics is inclusion of the two interpreted subobjects of that context. The context is universally quantified outside the formulas.
