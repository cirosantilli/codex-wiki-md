# Connected triple supports generate an alternating group

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating group](alternating-group.md)

A connected finite hypergraph of three-element supports, with a [three-cycle](three-cycle.md) on each support, generates the full alternating group. To merge a new triple meeting an old alternating support in two letters, conjugate over unordered pairs of old letters. For one-letter overlap compare $(a\,b\,c)$ and $(a'\,b\,c)$: their quotient is $(a\,b\,a')$, reducing to two two-letter-overlap steps.
