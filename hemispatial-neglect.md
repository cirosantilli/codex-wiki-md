# Hemispatial neglect

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive neurology](cognitive-neurology.md)

A disproportionate failure to attend or respond to events on one side of space, often contralesional to [brain](brain.md) injury. It may affect external space, the body or internally generated scenes and can occur without an equivalent primary visual-field loss.[1978, imagined viewpoints and spatial omissions](unilateral-neglect-of-representational-space-by-edoardo-bisiach-and-others.md)
