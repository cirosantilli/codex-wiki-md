# Proteomics

↑ **Parent:** [Biochemistry](biochemistry-split.md)

[proteomics](proteomics.md) studies the identities, amounts, modifications and associations of [proteins](protein-split.md) in biological material. Measurements depend on extraction, detectability and reference condition; absence of a detected [peptide](peptide.md) is not proof that its [protein](protein-split.md) is absent.
