# Distress

↑ **Parent:** [Emotion](emotion.md)

An aversive subjective experience that can accompany [anxiety](anxiety.md), [intrusive memories](intrusive-memory.md) or other difficulties. Its intensity is distinct from the conviction of a [belief](belief.md), a physiological response or [functional impairment](functional-impairment.md).
