# Area bound on equal-mass Schwarzschild merger radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Hawking's area theorem](hawking-s-area-theorem.md)

Two initially well-separated [Schwarzschild black holes](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) of mass $m$ each have total horizon area $32\pi G^2m^2/c^4$. If they merge into a [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md), [Hawking's area theorem](hawking-s-area-theorem.md) gives final mass $M\ge\sqrt2m$. Conserved total energy then bounds escaped radiation by the displayed expression, or $1-1/\sqrt2$ of the initial rest energy. This is an upper bound under classical horizon and energy assumptions, not a proof that an actual merger can saturate it.
