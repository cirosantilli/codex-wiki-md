# Smooth proper family of compact complex manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

A proper holomorphic submersion has compact smooth fibres, and the [Ehresmann fibration theorem](ehresmann-fibration-theorem.md) gives differentiable local trivializations. Their integral cohomology, modulo torsion, forms a [local system](local-system.md). If the fibres are [Kähler manifolds](kahler-manifold.md), their Hodge filtrations form holomorphic subbundles and give a [variation of Hodge structure](variation-of-hodge-structure.md). Properness is needed for this usual conclusion.
