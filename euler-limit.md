# Euler limit

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)

The Euler limit sends the [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) to zero while observing the flow at a fixed positive distance from a wall. If the velocity and its derivatives remain regular there, the viscous term disappears and the outer flow obeys the [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md). No penetration remains the wall condition for the outer problem; tangential no slip is enforced in a separate [viscous boundary layer](viscous-boundary-layer.md) and cannot generally be imposed on the inviscid outer flow.
