# Wurtzite

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)

A hexagonal tetrahedrally coordinated structure with ABAB stacking of the relevant bilayers, contrasted with ABCABC stacking in [zinc blende](sphalerite.md). It can occur as a nanowire polytype even when bulk conditions favour another phase.
