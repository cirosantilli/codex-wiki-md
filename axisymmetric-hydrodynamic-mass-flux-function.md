# Axisymmetric hydrodynamic mass flux function

↑ **Parent:** [Stream function](stream-function.md)

For a steady axisymmetric compressible [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md), [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) permits this representation. The mass flow through an annulus between two stream surfaces is $2\pi\Delta\psi$. In a steady adiabatic wind without magnetic forces, the [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md), [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) and [Bernoulli function](bernoulli-function.md) are locally functions of $\psi$ on connected regular stream surfaces.
