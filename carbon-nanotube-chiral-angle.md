# Carbon nanotube chiral angle

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon nanotube chiral vector](carbon-nanotube-chiral-vector.md)

The angle of the wrapping vector from the zigzag direction of [graphene](graphene.md). For chiral indices $0\le m\le n$, $\theta=\tan^{-1}[\sqrt3m/(2n+m)]$, ranging from zero for zigzag tubes to 30 degrees for armchair tubes. Together with diameter, it determines the discrete wrapping geometry, but does not alone determine the observed colour of a nanotube suspension.
