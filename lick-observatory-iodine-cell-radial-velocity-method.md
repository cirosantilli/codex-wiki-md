# Lick Observatory iodine-cell radial-velocity method

↑ **Parent:** [Iodine-cell Doppler calibration](iodine-cell-doppler-calibration.md)

At [Lick Observatory](lick-observatory.md), the Hamilton [spectrograph](spectrograph.md) records starlight with an imprinted [iodine](iodine.md) reference. The reference tracks instrumental [wavelength](wavelength.md) and profile changes during the science exposure. Barycentric correction and a time series of relative stellar shifts then support a dynamical companion model.
