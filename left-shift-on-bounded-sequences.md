# Left shift on bounded sequences

↑ **Parent:** [L-infinity sequence space](l-infinity-sequence-space.md)

The left shift is a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) of [operator norm](operator-norm.md) one on the [l-infinity sequence space](l-infinity-sequence-space.md). For $y=x-Sx$, telescoping gives $N^{-1}\sum_{n=1}^Ny_n=(x_1-x_{N+1})/N\to0$. This identity is useful in constructing a [Banach limit](banach-limit.md).
