# Squill

↑ **Parent:** [Plant medicinal material](plant-medicinal-material.md)

The sliced and dried [bulb](bulb.md) specified as Scilla in the [British Pharmacopoeia by the General Medical Council (1867)](british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md), botanically attributed there to Urginea scilla, with Scilla maritima as another name. The medicinal organ is a [bulb](bulb.md), not a [root](root-botany.md).
