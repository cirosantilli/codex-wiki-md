# Laboratory phase of a resonant spin pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Driven two-level Rabi model](driven-two-level-rabi-model.md)

With $H_0=-\hbar\omega_0\sigma_z/2$ and a co-rotating transverse magnetic drive of [phase](phase-waves.md) $\varphi$, an initial spin-up state becomes, up to common [phase](phase-waves.md), $\cos(\Omega\tau/2)|\uparrow\rangle+i e^{-i(\varphi+\omega_0\tau)}\sin(\Omega\tau/2)|\downarrow\rangle$. A quarter-turn pulse therefore prepares laboratory $+x$ when $\varphi_{\rm end}=\pi/2$ and $+y$ when $\varphi_{\rm end}=0$. Dropping the free-precession [phase](phase-waves.md) instead specifies targets in rotating axes. Transverse laboratory targets subsequently precess if the static field is retained.
