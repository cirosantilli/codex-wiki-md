# Two-step family with a third-order member

↑ **Parent:** [Linear multistep method](linear-multistep-method.md)

The [exponential-symbol order criterion for a multistep method](exponential-symbol-order-criterion-for-a-multistep-method.md) gives leading defect $-(1+5a)z^3/12$, so the family has order two except at $a=-1/5$, where it has order three. The [root condition for a multistep method](root-condition-for-a-multistep-method.md) holds exactly for $-1\leq a<1$, and the convergent [A-stable](a-stability.md) members are exactly $0\leq a<1$. At $a=0$ it reduces to the [trapezoidal rule](trapezoidal-rule.md) after removal of a zero root. At $a=1$ a repeated root one destroys [convergence of a numerical method](convergence-of-a-numerical-method.md) of the original recurrence despite a common [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) factor and a formally vanishing second-order residual.
