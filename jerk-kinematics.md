# Jerk (kinematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Acceleration](acceleration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerk_(physics))

The kinematic jerk is the time derivative of [acceleration](acceleration.md). For a [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) with relative position $\mathbf r$, relative velocity $\mathbf v$, $r=|\mathbf r|$, $\dot r=\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf v/r$ and total [mass](mass.md) $M$, differentiation of [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation.md) gives $\mathbf j=-GM(\mathbf v-3\dot r\,\mathbf r/r)/r^3$.
