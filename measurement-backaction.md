# Measurement backaction

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md)

A [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) changes the conditional [density operator](density-matrix.md), rather than merely revealing a pre-existing classical state. For an instrument with one operator $K_m$ per outcome, the displayed update occurs with [probability](probability.md) $\operatorname{Tr}(K_m\rho K_m^\dagger)$. Forgetting the outcome gives the corresponding [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md). [Measurement-based quantum feedback](measurement-based-quantum-feedback.md) must include this disturbance and the statistical information in the record.
