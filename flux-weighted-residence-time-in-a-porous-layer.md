# Flux-weighted residence time in a porous layer

↑ **Parent:** [Porous-media flow](porous-media-flow-split.md)

For steady incompressible flow with no stagnant volume, partition the layer into streamtubes of [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $dQ$. The pore volume in a streamtube is $\tau\,dQ$, so $\int\tau\,dQ=V_{\rm pore}$. This gives the mean residence time $V_{\rm pore}/Q$ even when individual path times vary widely. [Streamfunction](stream-function.md) labels measure flux in two-dimensional flow, so they supply the correct weighting.
