# On the resultant of a large number of vibrations of the same pitch and of arbitrary phase by John William Strutt, 3rd Baron Rayleigh

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by John William Strutt, 3rd Baron Rayleigh](work-by-john-william-strutt-3rd-baron-rayleigh.md)

By [John William Strutt, 3rd Baron Rayleigh](john-william-strutt-3rd-baron-rayleigh.md), credited as Lord Rayleigh. Philosophical Magazine, Series 5, 10(60), 73–78 (August 1880). The manuscript ends June 1880; 28 April 2009 is the publisher online date.

DOI: [https://doi.org/10.1080/14786448008626893](https://doi.org/10.1080/14786448008626893)

Inspected transcription: [https://www.scribd.com/document/472852365/Rayleigh1880](https://www.scribd.com/document/472852365/Rayleigh1880)

Cited passages: pp. 73–74, expected intensity; pp. 75–77, planar construction and polar integration; pp. 77–78, unequal component amplitudes. The argument concerns large numbers of components.
