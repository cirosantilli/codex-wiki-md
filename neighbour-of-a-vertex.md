# Neighbour of a vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Graph neighbourhood](graph-neighbourhood.md)

A neighbour of a [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) $v$ in a [graph](graph-split.md) is a [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) joined to $v$ by an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). These [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) form the [graph neighbourhood](graph-neighbourhood.md) $N(v)$.
