# Compact dual of a period domain

↑ **Parent:** [Period domain](period-domain.md)

The compact dual is the complex projective flag variety imposing prescribed filtration dimensions and $Q(F^p,F^{w-p+1})=0$, but neither real opposition nor positivity. In weight one it is the complex [Lagrangian Grassmannian](lagrangian-grassmannian.md). In polarized K3 weight two it is an isotropic-line quadric.
