# Bloch nuclear induction experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

In their 1946 letter, [Felix Bloch](felix-bloch.md), [William Webster Hansen](william-webster-hansen.md), and [Martin Packard](martin-packard.md) reported [water](water.md) at room [temperature](temperature-split.md). A steady [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) along $z$ and an oscillating field along $x$ produced a [signal](signal.md) in a pickup coil along $y$, displayed on an [oscilloscope](oscilloscope.md). Some samples included paramagnetic additives to accelerate [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md). Resonance fields at two [frequencies](frequency.md) gave the same field-to-frequency ratio, consistent with the [proton](proton.md) value.[p. 127, Nuclear Induction, entire letter; geometry, water, additives, readout and two-frequency check](nuclear-induction-by-felix-bloch-and-others.md)

The detailed paper describes glass [water](water.md) bulbs inside tuned receiving coils. Transmitter coils, shields and a conducting paddle controlled leakage. Field modulation at 60 Hz drove the horizontal [oscilloscope](oscilloscope.md) sweep. A [diode](diode.md) and audio amplification supplied the vertical trace. Vibration changed coupling: their largest difficulty. Coil tuning capacitors formed tank circuits. The actual receiver omitted the conceptual diagram's radiofrequency [amplifier](amplifier.md).[pp. 475–480, Figs. 1–4, APPARATUS and opening RESULTS; actual diode receiver versus conceptual amplifier](the-nuclear-induction-experiment-by-felix-bloch-and-others.md)

The orthogonal coil axes suppress direct transmitter pickup, but do not eliminate it. A field-dependent [signal](signal.md) at the nuclear resonance must be distinguished from leakage and [noise](noise-signal-processing.md); merely detecting a radiofrequency voltage is insufficient. This is why the field sweep and the independently checked field-to-frequency ratio matter.
