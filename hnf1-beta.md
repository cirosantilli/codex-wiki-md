<h1 id="hnf1-beta">HNF1β</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

Hepatocyte nuclear factor 1 beta, a [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)-binding transcriptional regulator. The supplied exam model uses it as an isoform-dependent corepressor; that experimental role should not be generalized to every tissue or target.
