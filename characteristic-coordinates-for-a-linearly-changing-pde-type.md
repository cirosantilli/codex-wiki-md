# Characteristic coordinates for a linearly changing PDE type

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md)

This [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) has [discriminant](discriminant.md) $y$, so it is a [hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) above $y=0$ and an [elliptic partial differential equation](elliptic-partial-differential-equation.md) below it. In its hyperbolic region the displayed [characteristic coordinates](characteristic-coordinate.md) have [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $18\sqrt y$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives

$$
yu_{xx}-u_{yy}=36y\,u_{\phi\psi}+\frac3{2\sqrt y}(u_\phi-u_\psi),\qquad u_{\phi\psi}=\frac{u_\psi-u_\phi}{6(\psi-\phi)}.
$$

The first derivative terms arise from differentiating the coordinate coefficients and cannot be discarded. The coordinate change degenerates at the line where the two characteristic directions merge.
