# MSc in statistical science course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-statistical-science).

Five core modules: applied [statistics](statistics.md); foundations of [statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md); statistical programming; computational [statistics](statistics.md); statistical [machine learning](machine-learning.md). Four option units are also required. The central page labels its option examples as 2025–26, so they are not presented here as a guaranteed current list. Compulsory dissertation, with the main work taking place from June to September.
