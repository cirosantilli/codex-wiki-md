# Hydropentalenyl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Pentalene](pentalene.md)

The monoanionic $\mathrm{C_8H_7^-}$ [coordination ligand](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) obtained by removing one proton from [dihydropentalene](dihydropentalene.md). An aromatic five-membered portion can bind a [metal](metal.md) in an eta-five mode while the fused ring retains an uncoordinated [alkene](alkene.md).
