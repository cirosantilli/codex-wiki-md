# Response surface methodology

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Response_surface_methodology)

[Response surface methodology](response-surface-methodology.md) fits local [regression](regression-analysis.md) approximations to a quantitative response as experimental settings change. A first-order model estimates a local gradient; augmenting the design permits [quadratic regression](quadratic-regression.md), curvature assessment and local optimization. Extrapolating a fitted surface outside the experimentally supported region requires caution.
