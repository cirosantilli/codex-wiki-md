# Semisimplicity of a classical quotient from regular elements in essential ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Goldie's theorem](goldie-s-theorem.md)

Suppose $R$ is a [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md) and every [essential right ideal](essential-right-ideal.md) contains a [regular element of a ring](regular-element-of-a-ring.md). Preimages of essential regular principal right ideals under left multiplication are essential, so they contain regular elements and prove the [right Ore condition](right-ore-condition.md). In the [classical right ring of quotients](classical-right-ring-of-quotients.md) $Q$, every [right ideal](right-ideal.md) is generated by its contraction to $R$, making $Q$ right [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md). An [essential right ideal](essential-right-ideal.md) of $Q$ contracts to an essential right ideal of $R$, hence contains a regular element and is all of $Q$. A maximal disjoint complement to any [right ideal](right-ideal.md) has essential sum with it, so every [right ideal](right-ideal.md) is complemented. The right regular [module](module-mathematics.md) is therefore a finite [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of [simple modules](irreducible-module.md), making $Q$ semisimple Artinian.
