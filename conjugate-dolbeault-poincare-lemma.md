<h1 id="conjugate-dolbeault-poincare-lemma">Conjugate Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma](dolbeault-poincare-lemma.md)

On a [polydisc](polydisc.md), a $\partial$-closed [differential form of type (p, q)](differential-form-of-type-p-q.md) with $p>0$ is $\partial$-exact. Apply the [Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma](dolbeault-poincare-lemma.md) to its complex conjugate, whose antiholomorphic degree is $p$.
