# Routhian

↑ **Parent:** [Routhian mechanics](routhian-mechanics.md)

Use the convention $R=L-\sum_i p_i\dot q_i$ for the eliminated [cyclic coordinates](cyclic-coordinate.md), expressing their velocities in terms of their fixed [conjugate momenta](canonical-momentum.md). Terms involving derivatives of those eliminated velocities cancel because $\partial L/\partial\dot q_i=p_i$. Thus the remaining coordinates obey the usual [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) for R. For a particle of fixed [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $\ell$ in a central potential, this gives $R=m\dot r^2/2-V(r)-\ell^2/(2mr^2)$, rather than the expression obtained by direct substitution into L alone. An opposite overall sign convention is also used, with correspondingly signed mixed Hamiltonian equations.
