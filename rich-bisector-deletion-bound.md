# Rich-bisector deletion bound

↑ **Parent:** [Circular-arc graph for distinct distances](circular-arc-graph-for-distinct-distances.md)

Every supporting centre of an arc between fixed endpoints lies on their [perpendicular bisector](perpendicular-bisector.md). Multiplicity at least $k$ therefore makes that [line](straight-line.md) contain at least $k$ of the original points. For each such [line](straight-line.md) containing $s$ points, its $s$ centres each support at most $t$ [circles](circle.md); on one [circle](circle.md) at most two consecutive-point arcs have that symmetry axis. The [rich line bound](rich-line-bound.md) summed over dyadic occupancies gives the displayed bound.
