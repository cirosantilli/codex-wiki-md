# Kelvin stellar mode

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible stellar surface mode](incompressible-stellar-surface-mode.md)

The [Kelvin stellar modes](kelvin-stellar-mode.md) are the potential [incompressible stellar surface modes](incompressible-stellar-surface-mode.md) of a nonrotating self-gravitating uniform-density sphere with [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md). For a regular displacement potential $U=A r^lY_l^m$, the free-surface [Lagrangian pressure perturbation](lagrangian-pressure-perturbation.md) condition and the [surface gravity perturbation of a uniform-density sphere](surface-gravity-perturbation-of-a-uniform-density-sphere.md) give

$$
\omega_l^2=\frac{2l(l-1)}{2l+1}\frac{GM}{R^3},\qquad l\geq1.
$$

In detail $\delta p/\rho=\omega^2U-\delta\Phi$ and $\delta p/\rho=(GM/R^2)\xi_r$ at $r=R$, so $\omega_l^2=lGM/R^3-4\pi G\rho l/(2l+1)$. The $l=1$ branch is rigid translation with zero restoring force. This potential family does not exhaust vortical zero-frequency displacements: $\boldsymbol\xi=\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf r$ is tangent to the sphere and has zero restoring force but nonzero [curl](curl.md).
