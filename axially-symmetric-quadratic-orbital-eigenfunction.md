# Axially symmetric quadratic orbital eigenfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator.md)

Each [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) operator annihilates a radial factor and $r^2$. Direct differentiation gives $L^2x_3^2=\hbar^2(6x_3^2-2r^2)$ and $L_3x_3^2=0$. Hence the displayed trace-free quadratic polynomial, times any admissible radial factor, has orbital labels $l=2,m=0$. Its angular dependence is proportional to the [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) $Y_{20}$; subtracting $r^2/3$ removes the rotational scalar part.
