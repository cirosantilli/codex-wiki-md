# Maximal bound for a nonnegative martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Doob maximal inequality for a nonnegative submartingale](doob-maximal-inequality-for-a-nonnegative-submartingale.md)

For a nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md), $T_R=\inf\{n:M_n\geq R\}$ satisfies $\mathbb P(T_R<\infty)\leq\mathbb E M_0/R$. Stop at $T_R\wedge n$ and use nonnegativity before passing to the increasing event. A limit of thresholds from below also gives the bound for $\{\sup_nM_n\geq R\}$ even when the supremum is not attained.
