# Rigid coverage

↑ **Parent:** [Coverage on a category](coverage-on-a-category.md)

A [coverage on a category](coverage-on-a-category.md) is rigid when each object is covered by the sieve generated by arrows from [J-irreducible objects of a site](j-irreducible-object-of-a-site.md). Restriction to their full subcategory identifies the [sheaves on a site](sheaf-on-a-site.md) with its [presheaf category](presheaf-category.md). A presheaf on that subcategory extends by the right Kan extension $C\mapsto\operatorname{Nat}(yC|_{\mathcal D},F)$: every covering sieve restricts to the full representable on $\mathcal D$, which proves the sheaf condition directly.
