# G-delta criterion for topological completeness

↑ **Parent:** [Topological completeness](topological-completeness.md)

A subspace of a complete [metric space](metric-space.md) admits a compatible complete [metric](metric.md) if and only if it is a [G-delta set](g-delta-set.md). For the reverse direction, adjoining the coordinates $1/d(x,X\setminus U_n)$ to the ambient [metric](metric.md) prevents Cauchy sequences from approaching any excluded closed boundary. For the forward direction, small-diameter relative open covers in a compatible complete [metric](metric.md) produce the ambient open sets.
