# Ultra-peripheral collision

↑ **Parent:** [Collision](collision.md)

An encounter of charged [particle beams](particle-beam.md) at an [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) large enough to suppress direct overlap of the [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md), while their [electromagnetic fields](electromagnetic-field.md) still interact.
