# Expression vector

↑ **Parent:** [Plasmid](plasmid.md)

An [expression vector](expression-vector.md) supplies regulatory and maintenance sequences for producing a chosen [protein](protein-split.md) or RNA in a host. The coding insert must have a compatible [promoter](promoter-genetics.md), initiation signals and reading frame; successful plasmid propagation does not establish successful expression.
