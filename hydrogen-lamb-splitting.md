# Hydrogen Lamb splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Lamb shift](lamb-shift.md)

The zero-field [energy levels](energy-level.md) here are averaged over [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md); $h$ is the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md). [Electron self-energy](electron-self-energy.md) and [vacuum polarization](vacuum-polarization.md) produce [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md). Self-energy describes the bound [electron](electron.md)'s radiative interaction with its own [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md). Precision predictions also include [nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md) and [proton charge radius](proton-charge-radius.md) effects; assigning recoil terms to the reference or Lamb shift is convention-dependent. A finite [proton charge radius](proton-charge-radius.md) changes binding independently of radiative effects.[§II, Eqs. (1)–(6); §III A, Eq. (7); §§IV–V](theory-of-the-lamb-shift-in-hydrogen-and-light-hydrogen-like-ions-by-vladimir-yerokhin-and-others.md)

A vacuum-[electric field](electric-field-split.md) “jiggling” picture motivates one contribution, not every [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) [energy](energy-split.md) correction.
