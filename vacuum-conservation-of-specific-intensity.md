# Vacuum conservation of specific intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Specific intensity](specific-intensity.md)

In a transparent, stationary Euclidean vacuum, the [radiative transfer equation](radiative-transfer-equation.md) has zero absorption and emission, so $dI_\nu/ds=0$. Equivalently, two surface elements exchange beam power $I_\nu\cos\theta_1\cos\theta_2\,dA_1dA_2d\nu/s^2$; reversing the description of the same beam cancels its geometric factors. The observed [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) of an unresolved source still falls as its apparent [solid angle](solid-angle.md), proportional to $s^{-2}$. Across gravitational or cosmological frequency shifts the invariant is $I_\nu/\nu^3$, not generally $I_\nu$.
