# Existential sentence

↑ **Parent:** [First-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md)

An existential sentence has a block of existential quantifiers followed by a [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md). Empty blocks are allowed. These sentences are preserved when a [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) embeds into another structure.
