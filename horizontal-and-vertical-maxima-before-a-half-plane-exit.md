# Horizontal and vertical maxima before a half-plane exit

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian running maximum](brownian-running-maximum.md)

Start [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) at $i$ and stop at its first hit of the real axis. The vertical maximum has the displayed tail by [optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) in a strip, so its $p$th moment is $1/(1-p)$ for $p<1$ and infinite for $p\geq1$. Reflection of the horizontal coordinate at a first level hit gives $\mathbb P(\sup X>\lambda)=2\pi^{-1}\int_\lambda^\infty(1+x^2)^{-1}dx$. The absolute horizontal maximum has upper and lower bounds of this same inverse-level order, hence the same positive-moment threshold. Its small-value [probability](probability.md) is exponentially small in the reciprocal level, so all negative moments also exist.
