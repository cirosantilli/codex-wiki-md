# Major-axis relaxation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

At fixed [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), rotational [energy](energy-split.md) is minimized by [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) about the largest principal [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md). Indeed, $T=\sum_iL_i^2/(2I_i)\geq\sum_iL_i^2/(2I_{\max})$. Weak internal dissipation can therefore relax a nearly rigid isolated body towards that axis. A perfectly rigid torque-free body conserves [energy](energy-split.md) and does not settle; both extreme-inertia axes are stable in its ideal dynamics. The maximum-inertia axis need not be the geometrically longest axis.
