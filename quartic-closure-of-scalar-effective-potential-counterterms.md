# Quartic closure of scalar effective-potential counterterms

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop scalar effective potential](one-loop-scalar-effective-potential.md)

For $d=4-\epsilon$, the scalar one-loop determinant has the displayed local divergent potential. If $V$ is a quartic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), then $V''$ has degree at most two and $(V'')^2$ has degree at most four. Its subtraction therefore uses the original polynomial couplings and a vacuum-energy term. A polynomial potential of degree $r>4$ instead produces degree $2r-4>r$, requiring new couplings. This is a direct potential-level illustration of [power counting in quantum field theory](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) and [renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md).
