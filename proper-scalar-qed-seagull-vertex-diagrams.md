# Proper scalar-QED seagull-vertex diagrams

↑ **Parent:** [Seagull vertex](seagull-vertex.md)

With zero quartic coupling, a proper one-loop vertex with two scalar and two labelled [photon](photon.md) legs has two boxes, five triangles and two bubbles. A directed scalar chain connects the charged external ends; the internal [photon](photon.md) joins its endpoints. Cubic vertices have one [photon](photon.md) slot, seagulls two. All nine graphs have [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) one with external labels fixed. At zero external [momentum](momentum-split.md) a transverse internal [photon](photon.md) kills any graph with a cubic endpoint, leaving only the two seagull bubbles.
