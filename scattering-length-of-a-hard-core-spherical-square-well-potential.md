# Scattering length of a hard-core spherical square-well potential

↑ **Parent:** [Hard-core spherical square-well potential](hard-core-spherical-square-well-potential.md)

For outer radius $b=2a$ and zero-energy well wavenumber $\kappa_0=\sqrt{2mV_0}/\hbar$, matching the [S-wave scattering](s-wave-quantum-scattering.md) logarithmic derivative gives

$$
a_s=2a-\frac{\tan(\kappa_0a)}{\kappa_0}.
$$

It diverges when $\kappa_0a=(n+\tfrac12)\pi$, where the well has a [zero-energy scattering resonance](zero-energy-scattering-resonance.md).
