# Uniform gravity-driven Newtonian film

↑ **Parent:** [Falling film flow](falling-film-flow.md)

A uniform steady film of thickness $d$ on a plane inclined by $\theta$ above horizontal has downslope coordinate $s$ and outward normal coordinate $x$. Neglecting air shear, the pressure is $p_{\rm atm}+\rho g\cos\theta(d-x)$ and its downslope gradient vanishes. The [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) momentum equation is $\eta v''+\rho g\sin\theta=0$. The [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md) $v(0)=0$ and shear-free [free surface](free-surface.md) $v'(d)=0$ give the displayed parabolic profile. Integrating over thickness gives volume flux per unit width $q$. The wall shear $\rho gd\sin\theta$ balances the film's downslope weight, and the vertical-wall case is $\theta=\pi/2$.
