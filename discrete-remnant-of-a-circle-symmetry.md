# Discrete remnant of a circle symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md)

Suppose fields with integer [U(1) charges](u-1-charge.md) $Q_i$ acquire nonzero [vacuum expectation values](vacuum-expectation-value.md), with at least one $Q_i\ne0$. The [stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md) in the [circle group](circle-group.md) is exactly $\mathbb Z_d$, where $d=\gcd(|Q_1|,\ldots,|Q_r|)$. Indeed, $e^{i\theta}$ preserves every condensate if and only if $e^{iQ_i\theta}=1$ for every $i$. The [Bezout identity](bezout-s-identity.md) implies $e^{id\theta}=1$, and conversely $d\mid Q_i$ proves every $d$th [root of unity](root-of-unity.md) preserves every condensate. Neutral fields impose no constraint; if all condensing fields are neutral, the whole [circle group](circle-group.md) survives.
