# Rayleigh distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous probability distribution](continuous-probability-distribution-split.md)

At unit scale the [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is $r\exp(-r^2/2)$ for $r>0$. Its square has an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of rate $1/2$. Thus $\sqrt{-2\log U}$ has this distribution for a [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) variable $U$ on $(0,1)$.
