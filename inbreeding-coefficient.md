# Inbreeding coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Inbreeding](inbreeding.md)

The inbreeding coefficient is the [probability](probability.md) that the two [allele](allele.md) copies at a locus within an individual are [identical by descent](identity-by-descent.md). It equals the [kinship coefficient](kinship-coefficient.md) of the individual's parents. It concerns ancestral identity, rather than merely [homozygosity](homozygosity.md) of observed allelic states.
