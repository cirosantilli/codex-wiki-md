# Adjoint eigenvector identity for a normal matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Normal matrix](normal-matrix.md)

If a [normal matrix](normal-matrix.md) satisfies $Ax=\lambda x$, then $A^\dagger x=\overline\lambda x$. Indeed, $M=A-\lambda I$ is normal and $\|Mx\|^2=x^\dagger M^\dagger Mx=x^\dagger MM^\dagger x=\|M^\dagger x\|^2$. Since $Mx=0$, also $M^\dagger x=0$. This identity proves orthogonality of distinct [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md) and gives spectral restrictions for [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md), [skew-Hermitian matrices](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) and [unitary matrices](unitary-matrix.md).
