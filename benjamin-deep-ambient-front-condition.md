# Benjamin deep-ambient front condition

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-current front condition](gravity-current-front-condition.md)

In an ideal [Boussinesq](boussinesq-approximation.md) [gravity current](gravity-current.md) advancing beneath a much deeper ambient, the local head [Froude number](froude-number.md) is $u_f/\sqrt{g'h_f}=\sqrt2$. The ambient must be displaced around the finite-depth head. This differs from the [Saint-Venant dry-front condition](saint-venant-dry-front-condition.md), which neglects ambient inertia. The coefficient belongs to the ideal deep-ambient head model; confinement, mixing and drag can change it.
