# Van der Waals equation

↑ **Parent:** [Equation of state](equation-of-state.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_der_Waals_equation)

The [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md) is a simple [real gas](real-gas.md) [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) that can also describe a [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md). With particle count $N$ fixed, write the [volume per particle](volume-per-particle.md) as $v=V/N$:

$$
p=\frac{k_BT}{v-b}-\frac{a}{v^2},\qquad v>b,\quad a>0,\quad b>0,
$$

or equivalently $(p+aN^2/V^2)(V-Nb)=Nk_BT$. Here $p$ is [pressure](pressure.md), $T$ is absolute [temperature](temperature.md), and $k_B$ is the [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md). In [SI](international-system-of-units.md), $v$ and $b$ have units $\mathrm{m^3}$ per particle, $a$ has units $\mathrm{Pa\,m^6}=\mathrm{J\,m^3}$, and $k_B$ has units $\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$; the particle count is dimensionless. The pole at $v=b$ excludes smaller [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) values from this model.

The $b$ correction represents [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md); the attraction term lowers [pressure](pressure.md) at fixed [temperature](temperature.md) and particle [number density](number-density.md) $1/v$. This is a [mean-field theory](mean-field-theory.md), replacing detailed intermolecular structure by two parameters. At $v\gg b$ with $a/v^2\ll k_BT/v$, it approaches the [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md) $pv=k_BT$. It supplies a useful qualitative route from a dilute [gas](gas.md) to [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md), without a claim of quantitative accuracy for every [fluid](fluid.md).[opening explanation and equation 6.156: excluded volume and attractive pressure correction; Fitzpatrick uses molar volume](thermodynamics-and-statistical-mechanics-by-richard-fitzpatrick/van-der-waals-gas.md)

Do not mix this particle convention with the [molar volume](molar-volume.md) convention elsewhere in this article. With [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) $n_{\rm mol}=N/N_A$, [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) $N_A$, and [molar gas constant](molar-gas-constant.md) $R=N_Ak_B$, one has $v_m=N_Av$, $b_m=N_Ab$, and $a_m=N_A^2a$, so $p=RT/(v_m-b_m)-a_m/v_m^2$. The molar parameters have units $\mathrm{m^3\,mol^{-1}}$ and $\mathrm{Pa\,m^6\,mol^{-2}}$, respectively.

[Johannes Diderik van der Waals](johannes-diderik-van-der-waals.md) introduced this [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) in 1873, connecting molecular attraction with departures from the dilute [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md). Its historical significance is the unified treatment of [gases](gas.md) and [liquids](liquid.md), rather than a universally exact fit.[Nobel Prize Outreach, Johannes Diderik van der Waals, Work: equation introduced in 1873 and molecular attraction](johannes-diderik-van-der-waals-facts-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
