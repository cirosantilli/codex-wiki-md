# Bayes quantile under asymmetric absolute-error loss

↑ **Parent:** [Asymmetric absolute-error loss](asymmetric-absolute-error-loss.md)

For a [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) with finite first moment, the [posterior](bayesian-posterior.md) risk [derivative](derivative.md) at continuity points is $(\gamma+\delta)F(a)-\gamma$. [Convexity](convex-function.md) therefore makes a [posterior](bayesian-posterior.md) $\gamma/(\gamma+\delta)$-quantile a [Bayes estimator](bayes-estimator.md). With atoms, the condition is $F(a^-)\leq\gamma/(\gamma+\delta)\leq F(a)$. Increasing the cost of underestimation moves the optimal estimate upward.
