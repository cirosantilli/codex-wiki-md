# Linearization of a Riccati equation

↑ **Parent:** [Riccati equation](riccati-equation.md)

For constant nonzero $c$, putting $y=x'/(cx)$ in $y'+cy^2+a(t)y+b(t)=0$ cancels the quadratic derivative terms and gives $x''+a(t)x'+cb(t)x=0$. The [logarithmic derivative](logarithmic-derivative.md) is defined on intervals where $x\ne0$, and multiplying $x$ by a nonzero scalar does not change $y$. For variable $c(t)\ne0$, the derivative coefficient becomes $a-c'/c$.
