# Polynomial ring over a field is Euclidean

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean domain](euclidean-domain.md)

For a [field](field.md) $F$, [polynomial division](polynomial-division.md) makes the one-variable [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) $F[X]$ a [Euclidean domain](euclidean-domain.md) with [Euclidean function](euclidean-function.md) equal to the [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md). For a divisor $g\ne0$, subtract a suitable scalar multiple of $X^{\deg f-\deg g}g$ to cancel the [leading coefficient](leading-coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md) of the current remainder. Its degree strictly decreases. Iteration terminates at $f=qg+r$, where $r=0$ or $\deg r<\deg g$. Division by the leading coefficient is valid because it is nonzero in a [field](field.md).
