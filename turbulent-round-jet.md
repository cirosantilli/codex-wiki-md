# Turbulent round jet

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)

A circular [turbulent round jet](turbulent-round-jet.md) supplied with kinematic [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) $m_0$ but negligible [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) conserves $m=m_0$. A [top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md) and constant [Batchelor entrainment](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md) give $dq/ds=2\alpha\sqrt{\pi m_0}$. From a point source with zero [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md), $q=2\alpha\sqrt{\pi m_0}s$, radius $b=2\alpha s$, and axial [velocity](velocity.md) $V=m_0/q\propto s^{-1}$.
