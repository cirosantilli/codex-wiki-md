# Epicyclic azimuthal advance

↑ **Parent:** [Epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md)

The azimuthal advance over one complete small radial oscillation is $\Delta\psi=2\pi\Omega/\kappa$. The oscillatory azimuthal correction averages to zero at first order. This angle compares successive radial turning points of the same type and describes [apsidal precession](apsidal-precession.md) when it differs from a full revolution.
