# Standard Gaussian random vector

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian random vector](gaussian-random-vector.md)

A [Gaussian random vector](gaussian-random-vector.md) with independent [normal](normal-distribution.md) coordinates of [expectation](expected-value.md) zero and [variance](variance-split.md) one. Its distribution is invariant under [orthogonal transformations](orthogonal-transformation.md). In particular, its direction in a fixed two-dimensional plane is uniform, which underlies the [Gaussian sign-correlation identity](gaussian-sign-correlation-identity.md).
