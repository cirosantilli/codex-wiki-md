# Binomial probability

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md)

The [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) with parameters $n$ and $p$ assigns the displayed [probability](probability.md) to $k$ successes, for integers $0\le k\le n$. Each specified sequence of independent trials has probability $p^k(1-p)^{n-k}$, and the [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient-split.md) counts the sequences with exactly $k$ successes. Outside this range the probability is zero.
