# Artin fixed-field theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)

If a finite group $G$ acts faithfully by automorphisms on a field $L$, then $L/L^G$ is Galois, has degree $|G|$, and has Galois group $G$.

This theorem constructs a [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) from a finite group of field automorphisms; it is not an alternative name for the extension itself.
