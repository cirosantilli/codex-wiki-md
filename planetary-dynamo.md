# Planetary dynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary magnetic field](planetary-magnetic-field.md)

A planetary dynamo maintains an intrinsic [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) by motion of an electrically conducting fluid. The [resistive induction equation](resistive-induction-equation.md) balances stretching and transport against [magnetic diffusion](magnetic-diffusion.md). A sufficiently large [magnetic Reynolds number](magnetic-reynolds-number.md) is necessary but does not ensure that the flow regenerates every required field component. Thermal or compositional [convection](convection.md) supplies mechanical power; rotation organizes the flow through the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md), and the [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) eventually modifies the flow and limits growth. A fluid core can fail to operate a dynamo if it is stably stratified or inadequately driven.
