# Western blot membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Western blot transfer](western-blot-transfer.md)

A [Western blot membrane](western-blot-membrane.md) is a thin [protein](protein.md)-binding support that receives the separated pattern. It is a laboratory support, distinct from a [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md). Retaining [proteins](protein.md) on its surface makes them accessible for [Western blot antibody detection](western-blot-antibody-detection.md).
