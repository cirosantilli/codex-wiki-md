# Chromatic adaptation

↑ **Parent:** [Neural adaptation](neural-adaptation.md)

Adjustment of visual responses to the spectral statistics of prevailing stimulation. Changes in gain or reference levels can partially stabilize [color](color.md) judgments despite different illumination or receptor signals. Adaptation is an explanatory mechanism to test; similarity on a [color](color.md) test does not uniquely identify its neural implementation.
