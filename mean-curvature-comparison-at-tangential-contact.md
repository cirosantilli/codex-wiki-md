# Mean-curvature comparison at tangential contact

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental forms of a graph surface](fundamental-forms-of-a-graph-surface.md)

Suppose two graph surfaces $z=f(x,y)$ and $z=g(x,y)$ are tangent at the origin, are oriented upward, and $g\geq f$ nearby. Then $g-f$ has a local minimum, so its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is [positive semidefinite](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). At the common horizontal tangent plane,

$$
H_g-H_f=\frac12\operatorname{tr}\operatorname{Hess}(g-f)\geq0.
$$
