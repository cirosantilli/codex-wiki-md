# Upper ramification numbering

↑ **Parent:** [Herbrand function](herbrand-function.md)

The upper numbering is obtained by applying the inverse [Herbrand function](herbrand-function.md) to the index in the [lower ramification numbering](lower-ramification-numbering.md). It is compatible with quotient Galois groups, whereas lower numbering is compatible with subgroups.
