# Scalar QED wave-function pole

↑ **Parent:** [Wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md)

In [scalar electrodynamics](scalar-electrodynamics.md) with [photon propagator](photon-propagator.md) $-i[g_{\mu\nu}-(1-\xi)l_\mu l_\nu/l^2]/(l^2+i0)$ and [dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md) $d=4-2\epsilon$, the [minimal subtraction scheme](minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) gives $Z_\phi=1+e^2(3-\xi)/(16\pi^2\epsilon)+O(e^4)$. For an external [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) $p$, expand the exchange numerator as $4p^2-4\xi p\cdot l+\xi l^2-4(1-\xi)(p\cdot l)^2/l^2$. The logarithmic [ultraviolet poles](ultraviolet-pole.md) of the scalar, vector and tensor integrals give the $p^2$ coefficient $4-2\xi-(1-\xi)=3-\xi$. A massless [photon](photon.md) [tadpole integral](tadpole-integral.md) does not change it. The [scalar QED Ward identity](scalar-quantum-electrodynamics-ward-identity.md) gives the same vertex [renormalization constant](renormalization-constant.md).
