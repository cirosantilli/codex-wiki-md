# Binomial funnel control limits

↑ **Parent:** [Hospital mortality funnel plot](hospital-mortality-funnel-plot.md)

The displayed pointwise [control limits](control-limits.md) follow from the [normal approximation](normal-approximation.md) to an observed [binomial proportion](binomial-proportion.md). Clip plotted limits to the probability range. Their nominal coverage is approximate and can be poor for small expected counts; exact binomial tails give a discrete alternative. Pointwise coverage does not provide simultaneous coverage of all hospitals; use [Bonferroni correction](bonferroni-correction.md) or another [familywise error rate](familywise-error-rate.md) procedure when that is the required guarantee.
