# Symplectic contraction of an exterior square

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior square](exterior-square.md)

For a [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) $(V,\omega)$, contraction is the map $c:\Lambda^2V\to\mathbb C$ defined by $c(v\wedge w)=\omega(v,w)$. It is equivariant for the [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md). In dimension four its five-dimensional [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is irreducible of [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\omega_2$, while the invariant inverse-form bivector spans a complementary [trivial Lie algebra representation](trivial-lie-algebra-representation.md).
