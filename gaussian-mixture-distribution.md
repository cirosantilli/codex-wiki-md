# Gaussian mixture distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Mixture distribution](mixture-distribution.md)

A [Gaussian mixture distribution](gaussian-mixture-distribution.md) is a weighted sum of [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) densities, with nonnegative weights summing to one. It need not itself be a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md): components with different means and [variances](variance-split.md) can produce [skewness](skewness.md) or several modes. Its raw [moments](moment.md) are weighted sums of the component [moments](moment.md).
