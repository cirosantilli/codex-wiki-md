# Purine

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide base](nucleotide-base.md)

[Purine](purine.md) is the fused six- and five-membered [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) heterocyclic framework underlying [adenine](adenine.md) and [hypoxanthine](hypoxanthine.md). Its numbered ring nitrogens distinguish [protonation](protonation.md) and hydrogen-bonding sites.
