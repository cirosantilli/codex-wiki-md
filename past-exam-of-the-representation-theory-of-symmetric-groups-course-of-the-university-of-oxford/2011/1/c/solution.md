<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Young subgroup](../../../../young-subgroup.md) for $(1^n)$ is trivial. Hence $M^{(1^n)}_{\mathbb C}$ is the [regular representation](../../../../regular-representation.md) of $S_n$. The subgroup-average formula reduces to

$$
\boxed{m_{(1^n),\mu}=\chi_\mu(1)=\dim S^\mu_{\mathbb C}=\frac{n!}{\prod_{u\in[\mu]}h(u)}.}
$$

This is the usual [multiplicity](../../../../multiplicity-mathematics.md) of an [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) in the [regular representation](../../../../regular-representation.md).
