<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [permutation module](../../../../permutation-module.md) $M_F^\mu$ is the $F$-vector space with the $\mu$-tabloids as [basis](../../../../basis.md), with $S_n$ acting by permuting their entries. For a tableau $t$ with column [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) $C_t$, its [polytabloid](../../../../polytabloid.md) is

$$
e_t=\sum_{g\in C_t}\operatorname{sgn}(g)\{gt\}.
$$

The [Specht module](../../../../specht-module.md) $S_F^\mu$ is the [submodule](../../../../submodule.md) of $M_F^\mu$ spanned by these [polytabloids](../../../../polytabloid.md), equivalently generated by any one $e_t$ under the [group action](../../../../group-action-split.md).

The [standard basis](../../../../standard-basis.md) theorem states that

$$
\boxed{\{e_t:t\text{ is a standard }\mu\text{-tableau}\}}
$$

is an $F$-basis of $S_F^\mu$ in every [characteristic](../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md). Its [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is the number of [standard tableaux](../../../../standard-young-tableau.md) of shape $\mu$; in the [hook](../../../../hook-of-a-young-diagram.md) shape used below the [basis](../../../../basis.md) is exactly the displayed $e_{1ij}$ with $2\leq i<j\leq n$.
