# Solenoid fringe field from an axisymmetric vector potential

↑ **Parent:** [Solenoid](solenoid.md)

Near the axis of a [solenoid](solenoid.md), the [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) $A_\theta=\rho B(z)/2$ gives

$$
B_z=B(z),\qquad B_\rho=-\frac\rho2 B'(z),\qquad B_\theta=0.
$$

The radial fringe component enforces the [solenoidal magnetic-field constraint](solenoidal-magnetic-field-constraint.md). A sharp axial step in $B$ must retain this fringe as a distributional field; a purely axial field that jumps to zero violates [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md). The hard-edge model is a convenient limit for near-axis trajectories, not a literal monopole sheet.
