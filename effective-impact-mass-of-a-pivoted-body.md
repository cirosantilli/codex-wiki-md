# Effective impact mass of a pivoted body

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid-body collision](rigid-body-collision.md)

A short transverse [impulse](impulse.md) $J$ at lever arm $b$ gives a pivoted [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\Omega=Jb/I$ and contact-point [velocity](velocity.md) $b\Omega=J/m_{\rm eff}$, where $I$ is its pivot [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md). Thus $m_{\rm eff}=I/b^2$ plays the role of a translational [mass](mass.md) in the impact direction. A point projectile elastically striking an initially stationary body stops immediately after impact precisely when its [mass](mass.md) equals this effective value. The pivot can exchange [linear momentum](momentum-split.md) while doing no [work](work.md).
