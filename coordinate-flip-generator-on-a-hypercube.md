# Coordinate-flip generator on a hypercube

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier-Walsh transform](fourier-walsh-transform.md)

For independent coordinate flips at rate $1/2$, a [Walsh character](walsh-character.md) $w_A$ is an eigenfunction with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $-|A|$. The generator is self-adjoint and negative semidefinite. Its [Dirichlet form of a Markov chain](dirichlet-form-of-a-markov-chain.md) is $-\mathbb E[fLf]=\frac14\sum_i\mathbb E(f(\omega^{(i)})-f(\omega))^2$.
