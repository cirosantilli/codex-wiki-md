# Bobylev identity

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell molecule collision operator](maxwell-molecule-collision-operator.md)

For the [Maxwell molecule collision operator](maxwell-molecule-collision-operator.md) and the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) with exponent $-i\xi\cdot v$, put $\xi^\pm=(\xi\pm|\xi|\sigma)/2$. The [angular exchange for elastic collisions](angular-exchange-for-elastic-collisions.md), rotation invariance of spherical surface area and factorization of the two velocity [integrals](integral.md) give the displayed formula. This identity converts the collision [integral](integral.md) into a spherical average of products at two related frequencies.
