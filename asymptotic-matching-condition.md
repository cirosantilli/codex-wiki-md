# Asymptotic matching condition

↑ **Parent:** [Matched asymptotic expansion](matched-asymptotic-expansion.md)

An asymptotic matching condition requires the [inner expansion](inner-expansion.md) and [outer expansion](outer-expansion.md), re-expressed in a common overlap region, to agree to the retained order. Both function values and slopes may constrain the free constants. In the [square-root reaction problem with nested boundary layers](square-root-reaction-problem-with-nested-boundary-layers.md), the shared small plateau sets the [first integral of a square-root reaction layer](first-integral-of-a-square-root-reaction-layer.md), while its large-field fourth-power tail fixes the neighboring broad layer.
