# Fully convective star

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar convective stability](stellar-convective-stability.md)

A fully convective star has [convection](convection.md) throughout nearly its entire interior. Very low-mass [red dwarfs](red-dwarf.md) are fully convective; the transition to this structure is used in the [disrupted magnetic braking model](disrupted-magnetic-braking-model.md).
