# Pushout in a category

↑ **Parent:** [Colimit](colimit.md)

A pushout of $B\leftarrow A\to C$ is a [colimit](colimit.md) with morphisms $B\to P$ and $C\to P$ making the square commute and universal among such commuting pairs. In the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md), form the disjoint union of $B,C$ and identify the two images of each element of $A$.
