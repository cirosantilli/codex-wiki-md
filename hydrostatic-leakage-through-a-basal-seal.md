# Hydrostatic leakage through a basal seal

↑ **Parent:** [Leaky porous gravity current](leaky-porous-gravity-current.md)

A dense [porous gravity current](porous-gravity-current.md) of height $h$ places excess [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) $\Delta\rho gh$ on a basal seal. For seal [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) $\lambda$ and thickness $b$, [Darcy law](darcy-law.md) gives downward flux $\lambda\Delta\rho gh/(\mu b)$. Dividing the stored height-volume balance by [porosity](porosity.md) $\phi$ gives a linear drainage term $-\Omega h$. The coefficient has inverse-time units and does not contain an extra factor of the aquifer permeability.
