# Static potential across a compact circle

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein mode](kaluza-klein-mode.md)

On three noncompact spatial dimensions times a circle of circumference $L$, the [method of images](method-of-images.md) gives $\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}[r^2+j^2L^2]^{-1}=\pi\coth(\pi r/L)/(Lr)$ at equal compact coordinates. This interpolates from $1/r^2$ at short distances to $\pi/(Lr)$ at long distances. A gravitational circle-radius scalar can change the long-range coefficient if it remains unstabilized.
