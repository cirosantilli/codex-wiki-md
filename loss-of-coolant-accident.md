# Loss-of-coolant accident

↑ **Parent:** [Reactor thermal hydraulics](reactor-thermal-hydraulics.md)

An accident involving loss of reactor coolant through a breach of its pressure boundary. Depressurization, loss of inventory and changes in heat removal require a transient assessment; stopping the fission chain reaction does not remove [decay heat](decay-heat.md).
