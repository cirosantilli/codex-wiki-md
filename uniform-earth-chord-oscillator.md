# Uniform-Earth chord oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md)

Inside a homogeneous spherical [mass](mass.md), [gravity](gravity.md) is proportional to position. Projection onto a fixed straight chord gives $\ddot y=-\omega_0^2y$, independently of its perpendicular offset from the [sphere](sphere.md) centre. Release from one surface endpoint at rest reaches the opposite endpoint after $\pi/\omega_0$. The transverse [force](force-split.md) is supplied by the tunnel constraint. This idealization neglects removed tunnel [mass](mass.md), rotation, density variation and dissipative [forces](force-split.md).
