# Bidentate ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [bidentate ligand](bidentate-ligand.md) binds a [metal](metal.md) through two donor atoms. Simultaneous attachment can produce a [chelate](chelate.md) ring; the two donor atoms need not be the same element.
