# Torsion-free cancellative commutative monoid

↑ **Parent:** [Cancellative commutative monoid](cancellative-commutative-monoid.md)

A [cancellative commutative monoid](cancellative-commutative-monoid.md) is torsion-free in this sense when $na=nb$ implies $a=b$ for every positive integer $n$. Its [Grothendieck group](grothendieck-group.md) is then a [torsion-free abelian group](torsion-free-abelian-group.md), because $n(a-b)=0$ forces $na=nb$. This is the condition inherited by a submonoid of a [torsion-free abelian group](torsion-free-abelian-group.md).
