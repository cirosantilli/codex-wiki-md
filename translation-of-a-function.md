# Translation of a function

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)

Translation shifts the argument without changing the shape of a [function](function-split.md). On the [real line](real-line.md), the displayed convention shifts a graph to the right by $h$. [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) invariance preserves every finite [Lp norm](lp-norm.md); its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) is multiplied by $e^{-ih\xi}$.
