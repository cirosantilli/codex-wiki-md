# Graph structure lifted through a covering map

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

A [covering map](covering-space.md) over a [topological graph](graph-topology.md) lifts its [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) to all their preimages and each open [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) to disjoint open [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). Their endpoints are supplied by the [path lifting theorem](path-lifting-theorem.md) applied to each parametrizing [path](continuous-path.md) of a closed [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). The [covering sheets](covering-sheet.md) over small vertex stars verify that the resulting [graph](graph-split.md) topology agrees with the original [topology](topology-split.md) upstairs. A constant finite [degree of a covering map](degree-of-a-covering-map.md) $d$ therefore multiplies both vertex and edge counts by $d$, proving the [Euler characteristic under a finite covering](euler-characteristic-under-a-finite-covering.md) formula for finite [topological graphs](graph-topology.md).
