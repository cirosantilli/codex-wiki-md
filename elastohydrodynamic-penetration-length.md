# Elastohydrodynamic penetration length

↑ **Parent:** [Sperm number](sperm-number.md)

The elastohydrodynamic penetration length balances the transverse viscous force $c_\perp\omega y$ against the bending force $By/\ell_\omega^4$ in a periodically driven filament. The [sperm number](sperm-number.md) $L/\ell_\omega$ distinguishes nearly rigid, weakly deformed filaments from long filaments whose response is localized near the actuator.
