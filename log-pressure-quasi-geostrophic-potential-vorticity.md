# Log-pressure quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity

↑ **Parent:** [Three-dimensional quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md)

In a [log-pressure coordinate](log-pressure-coordinate.md), [quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) uses density-weighted vertical stretching. For stratification $N^2=RS/H$ and [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md) $\mathbf u_g=(-\psi_y,\psi_x,0)$, the inviscid equation is $D_gq/Dt=0$. Combining horizontal [vorticity](vorticity.md) balance with the thermal equation eliminates [ageostrophic flow](ageostrophic-flow.md) and gives this conservation law.
