# Eccentric circular-hole disk inertia

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

A uniform [disk](disk-mathematics.md) of radius $R$ and area [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ has a circular hole of radius $r_o$, whose centre H is displaced by $r_o$ from the original centre C. Set $D=\pi\rho R^2$, $h=\pi\rho r_o^2$ and $W=D-h$. The [parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md) gives $I_H=DR^2/2+Dr_o^2-hr_o^2/2$. Removing the material shifts the remaining [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) G away from H by $CG=hr_o/W$, so $HG=Dr_o/W$ and $I_G=I_H-D^2r_o^2/W$. Thus the rotational component of free rigid-body [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is smaller than fixed-axis [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) about H at the same [angular speed](angular-speed.md), although total rolling [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) also includes translation.
