# Sunada theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md)

Suppose a finite [group](group-split.md) $G$ acts by [Riemannian isometries](riemannian-isometry.md) on a compact [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) $M$, and [Gassmann equivalent](gassmann-equivalence.md) subgroups $H_1,H_2$ act freely. The quotient manifolds $H_i\backslash M$ have equal [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) spectra. For an [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) space $E$, the quotient multiplicity is $|H_i|^{-1}\sum_{h\in H_i}\operatorname{tr}(h|E)$, and the equality follows by summing its [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) over [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md). Nonconjugacy inside $G$ alone does not guarantee that the quotients are nonisometric.
