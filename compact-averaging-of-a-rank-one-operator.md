# Compact averaging of a rank-one operator

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md)

For a strongly continuous [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md) of a [compact group](compact-group.md), this [Bochner integral](bochner-integral.md) exists in the [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md) and is a positive [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md) commuting with the representation. It is nonzero if $\xi\ne0$, because $\langle K_\xi\xi,\xi\rangle=\int_G|\langle U_g\xi,\xi\rangle|^2\,dg>0$. A nonzero [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) is finite-dimensional and invariant. Applying this on [orthogonal complements](orthogonal-complement.md) yields an [orthogonal direct sum](orthogonal-direct-sum.md) of finite-dimensional [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md), with no separability assumption on the represented [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md).
