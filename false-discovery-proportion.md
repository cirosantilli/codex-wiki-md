# False discovery proportion

↑ **Parent:** [False discovery rate](false-discovery-rate.md)

In [multiple hypothesis testing](multiple-hypothesis-testing.md), let $R$ count all rejections and $V$ count rejected true [null hypotheses](null-hypothesis.md). The false discovery proportion is the random variable $V/(R\vee1)$, defined to be zero when $R=0$. Its expectation is the [false discovery rate](false-discovery-rate.md). Distinguishing the realized proportion from its expectation matters: controlling the [false discovery rate](false-discovery-rate.md) does not by itself give a high-probability bound on the realized proportion.
