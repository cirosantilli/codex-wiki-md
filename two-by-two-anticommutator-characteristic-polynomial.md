# Two-by-two anticommutator characteristic polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Anticommutator](anticommutator.md)

If $A$ is diagonalizable, the [anticommutator](anticommutator.md) map $B\mapsto AB+BA$ has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $2\lambda_1$, $\lambda_1+\lambda_2$ twice, and $2\lambda_2$. Their product gives the displayed [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md). The [density of diagonalizable complex matrices](density-of-diagonalizable-complex-matrices.md) and continuity of polynomial coefficients prove the formula also for defective matrices.
