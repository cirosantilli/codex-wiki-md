# Hermiticity of a pseudoscalar Dirac bilinear

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction](pseudoscalar-yukawa-interaction.md)

With the usual [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md), Hermitian $\gamma^5$ and $\{\gamma^0,\gamma^5\}=0$, taking the adjoint gives $\psi^\dagger\gamma^5\gamma^0\psi=-\psi^\dagger\gamma^0\gamma^5\psi$. Consequently a real pseudoscalar with coupling $-g\phi\bar\psi\gamma^5\psi$ has a Hermitian interaction only if $g^*=-g$. Writing $g=i g_P$ with real $g_P$ gives the familiar interaction $-i g_P\phi\bar\psi\gamma^5\psi$. Formal [Euler-Lagrange field equations](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) obtained with independent spinor and adjoint variables can still be computed for the original symbolic coupling; it must not silently be identified with a real physical coupling.
