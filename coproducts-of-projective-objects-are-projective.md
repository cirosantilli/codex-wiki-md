# Coproducts of projective objects are projective

↑ **Parent:** [Projective object](projective-object.md)

A set-indexed [coproduct in a category](coproduct.md) of [projective objects in a category](projective-object.md) is projective. Restrict a map out of the coproduct to each summand, lift each restriction through the given [epimorphism](epimorphism.md), and assemble the lifts by the coproduct universal property. Choosing an arbitrary family of lifts uses the usual [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md). The empty coproduct, an [initial object](initial-object.md), is projective as well.
