# Contrast (statistics)

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments.md)

A contrast is a [linear combination](linear-combination.md) $\sum_i c_i\mu_i$ of group means with $\sum_i c_i=0$. It compares responses without depending on a common additive level. [Treatment contrasts](treatment-contrast.md) and [factorial contrasts](factorial-contrast.md) specify comparisons in a [design of experiments](design-of-experiments.md).
