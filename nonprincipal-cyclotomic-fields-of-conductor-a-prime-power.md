# Nonprincipal cyclotomic fields of conductor a prime power

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

Every field $\mathbb Q(\zeta_{59^r})$ contains $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-59})$ and is totally ramified over it at the prime above $59$. The [class number divisibility under total ramification](class-number-divisibility-under-total-ramification.md) therefore shows that its [class number](class-number.md) is divisible by three. Its ring of integers is not a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md). The degrees $58\cdot59^{r-1}$ strictly increase, giving infinitely many distinct examples.
