# Acid-base chemistry

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)

[Acid-base chemistry](acid-base-chemistry.md) studies how [chemical acids](acid-chemistry.md) and [chemical bases](base-chemistry.md) exchange [protons](proton.md) or [electron pairs](electron-pair.md). Here the focus is transfer of [protons](proton.md) in [water](water.md): a [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md) donates a [proton](proton.md), and a [chemical base](base-chemistry.md) accepts it. The resulting [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) determines [pH](ph.md), while a [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md) can resist changes in [pH](ph.md). This connects microscopic [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction.md) with a compact, measurable description of a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md).

The important distinction is between how much [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md) is present and how readily it transfers a [proton](proton.md). A [weak acid](weak-acid.md) and a [strong acid](strong-acid.md) at the same total [concentration](concentration.md) need not have the same [pH](ph.md). Conversely, two [buffer solutions](buffer-solution.md) can have the same [pH](ph.md) while containing very different total quantities of their [chemical acids](acid-chemistry.md) and [chemical bases](base-chemistry.md).
