# Mean temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature measurement](temperature-measurement.md)

An average of a specified [temperature](temperature-split.md) record over a stated interval or ensemble. For a time mean, observations must represent elapsed durations: irregularly spaced readings generally require time weighting. The half-sum of a daily maximum and minimum is a conventional daily statistic and need not equal the continuous time mean.
