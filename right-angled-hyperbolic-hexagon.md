# Right-angled hyperbolic hexagon

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic hexagon](hyperbolic-hexagon.md)

A geodesic hexagon in the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) with all six interior angles $\pi/2$. Any three positive alternating side lengths determine it up to [isometry](isometry.md). If those sides are $\ell_i/2$, the opposite seam satisfies $\cosh s_i=(\cosh(\ell_i/2)+\cosh(\ell_j/2)\cosh(\ell_k/2))/(\sinh(\ell_j/2)\sinh(\ell_k/2))$. Two congruent such hexagons glued along the other three sides form a [pair of pants](pair-of-pants-mathematics.md) with boundary lengths $\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3$.
