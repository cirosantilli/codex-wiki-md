# Speckle contrast of a sinusoidal wavefront error

↑ **Parent:** [Speckle pattern](speckle-pattern.md)

For a small sinusoidal [optical path length](optical-path-length.md) error $h_0\cos(2\pi x/\Lambda)$, expand its phase factor to first order. The two exponential harmonics each have field amplitude $i\pi h_0/\lambda$ relative to the unperturbed field, so each speckle has contrast $C=(\pi h_0/\lambda)^2$. Its angular displacement is $\lambda/\Lambda$. Here $h_0$ is optical path error, not physical mirror displacement; a reflecting mirror introduces twice its surface displacement in the near-normal limit.
