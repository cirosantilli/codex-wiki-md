# Electric-field splitting of a Landau level in a rectangle

↑ **Parent:** [Landau levels in crossed electric and magnetic fields](landau-levels-in-crossed-electric-and-magnetic-fields.md)

In [Landau gauge](landau-gauge-for-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md) with periodic boundary conditions of length $L_y$, adjacent guiding centres differ by $\Delta k_y=2\pi/L_y$. A perpendicular electric field tilts the energy by $-(E/B)\hbar k_y$, so adjacent states formerly in one degenerate [Landau level](landau-level.md) are separated by $2\pi\hbar|E/B|/L_y$.
