# Polysomnography

↑ **Parent:** [Sleep](sleep.md)

Combined recording used to classify sleep and waking, commonly including [electroencephalography](electroencephalography.md), eye-movement signals and muscle activity. Classification of a sleep stage is not itself a measure of the content of a memory.
