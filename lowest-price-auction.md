# Lowest-price auction

↑ **Parent:** [Auction](auction.md)

The highest bidder wins a single item but pays the lowest submitted bid, while losers pay nothing. This differs from a [lowest unique bid auction](least-unique-bid-auction.md). With at least two bidders using increasing strategies, the winning bidder pays the smallest opponent bid.
