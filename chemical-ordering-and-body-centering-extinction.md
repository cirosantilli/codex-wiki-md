# Chemical ordering and body-centering extinction

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal basis structure factor](crystal-basis-structure-factor.md)

Sites at 000 and one half of each cubic coordinate give $F=f_A+(-1)^{h+k+l}f_B$. Identical sites produce a [body-centered cubic lattice](body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) with absent odd-parity reflections. Distinct chemical species generally preserve only the [simple cubic lattice](primitive-cubic-lattice.md) translations, so those same odd reflections need not vanish. A geometric body center is not by itself a symmetry translation of the decorated crystal.
