# Quadratic-inflation endpoint estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic-potential slow-roll solution](quadratic-potential-slow-roll-solution.md)

For $V=m^2\phi^2/2$ and unreduced [Planck mass](planck-mass.md) $m_P=G^{-1/2}$, the [potential slow-roll parameter](potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) is $\epsilon_V=m_P^2(V'/V)^2/(16\pi)=m_P^2/(4\pi\phi^2)$. Its conventional value one gives the displayed endpoint estimate. Exact accelerated expansion ends at $\epsilon_H=1$, or $\dot\phi^2=V$ for a canonical scalar alone. Substitution of slow-roll formulas into this exact condition gives a different order-one coefficient because slow roll is failing there; a precise endpoint requires integrating the full [inflaton equation of motion](inflaton-equation-of-motion.md) and [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md). The large-field e-fold estimate is insensitive to this order-one endpoint uncertainty.
