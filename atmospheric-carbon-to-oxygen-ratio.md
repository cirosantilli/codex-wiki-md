# Atmospheric carbon-to-oxygen ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric metallicity of a giant planet](atmospheric-metallicity-of-a-giant-planet.md)

The atmospheric carbon-to-oxygen ratio compares the elemental abundances of carbon and oxygen in the atmosphere. It influences molecular composition under [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md), but condensation, [disequilibrium chemistry in an exoplanet atmosphere](disequilibrium-chemistry-in-an-exoplanet-atmosphere.md), and incomplete spectral coverage complicate its inference from molecular bands.
