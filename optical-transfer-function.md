# Optical transfer function

↑ **Parent:** [Point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_transfer_function)

The [optical transfer function](optical-transfer-function.md) is the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) of the normalized intensity [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md) for [incoherent imaging](incoherent-imaging.md) with [translation invariance](translation-invariance.md), using [spatial frequency](spatial-frequency.md) in cycles per unit length. It has $H(\mathbf0)=1$. For an ideal circular pupil it is the normalized pupil autocorrelation, with cutoff $2\mathrm{NA}/\lambda_0$ in object coordinates: no overlap remains when two pupil copies are shifted apart by more than one pupil [diameter](diameter.md).[p. 8, §E(b): OTF as Fourier transform of intensity PSF and pupil autocorrelation](mit-2-71-and-2-710-optics-spring-2014-by-nicholas-x-fang/review-for-optics.md)
