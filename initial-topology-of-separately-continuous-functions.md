# Initial topology of separately continuous functions

↑ **Parent:** [Initial topology](initial-topology.md)

On the real plane, take the [initial topology](initial-topology.md) of all [separately continuous](separate-continuity.md) real-valued [functions](function-split.md). Its coordinate lines have their usual induced [topology](topology-split.md), and the nonzero rational grid is [dense](dense-set.md), but its diagonal is [uncountable](uncountable-set.md) and discrete. Thus the space is [separable](separable-topological-space.md) and nonmetrizable. Rectangular bump [functions](function-split.md) show that no [sequence](sequence.md) from the nonzero rational grid converges to the origin, so the space is not [first countable](first-countable-space.md) either.
