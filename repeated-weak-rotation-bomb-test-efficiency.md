# Repeated weak-rotation bomb-test efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Elitzur-Vaidman bomb tester](elitzur-vaidman-bomb-tester.md)

For an [Elitzur-Vaidman bomb tester](elitzur-vaidman-bomb-tester.md) using a rotation and its inverse, let $p$ be the probability of entering the absorbing path and $q=1-p$. Conditional on a live absorber, one trial has explosion probability $p$, successful safe identification probability $pq$, and inconclusive probability $q^2$. The inconclusive [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) resets the input, so summing a [geometric series](geometric-series.md) gives the displayed success probability after at most $N$ trials. Its limit is $q/(1+q)$, approaching one half as $p$ tends to zero through positive values. Exactly zero coupling produces no information.
