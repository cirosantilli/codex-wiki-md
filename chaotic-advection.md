# Chaotic advection

↑ **Parent:** [Advection](advection.md)

Chaotic advection is complicated, sensitive material motion generated by a smooth [velocity field](velocity-field.md), often even when the Eulerian flow is periodic and simple. Transverse intersections of [stable manifolds](stable-manifold.md) and [unstable manifolds](unstable-manifold.md) create stretching, folding and lobe transport across former [separatrices](separatrix.md). [Incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) preserves parcel area and advected scalar values in the absence of [diffusion](diffusion.md), so chaotic motion can create fine filaments without independently producing molecular homogenization.
