# Compact embedding of a confining-potential energy space

↑ **Parent:** [Confining-potential energy space](confining-potential-energy-space.md)

The embedding $\Sigma_V\hookrightarrow L^2(\mathbb R^d)$ is compact. The [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) gives compactness on each fixed ball, while

$$
\int_{|x|>R}|u|^2
\leq\left(\inf_{|x|>R}V(x)\right)^{-1}
\int_{\mathbb R^d}V|u|^2
$$

makes the tails uniformly small.
