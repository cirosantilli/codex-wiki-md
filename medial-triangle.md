# Medial triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

The medial [triangle](triangle.md) joins the three [midpoints](midpoint.md) of a [triangle](triangle.md)'s sides. Each side is parallel to the corresponding original side and has half its length: $((\mathbf x+\mathbf z)/2)-((\mathbf x+\mathbf y)/2)=(\mathbf z-\mathbf y)/2$. Each [altitude of a triangle](altitude-of-a-triangle.md) in the medial [triangle](triangle.md) is a [perpendicular bisector](perpendicular-bisector.md) of an original side, so its [orthocenter](orthocenter.md) is the original [triangle](triangle.md)'s [circumcenter](circumcenter.md).
