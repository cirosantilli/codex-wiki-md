# Adiabatic shielding of tidal encounters

↑ **Parent:** [Impulse approximation](impulse-approximation.md)

Adiabatic shielding suppresses the heating of an orbit when an external [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md) varies slowly compared with its orbital frequency. In a nonresonant slow encounter, [adiabatic invariance of an orbital action](adiabatic-invariance-of-an-orbital-action.md) lets the orbit adjust approximately reversibly, so a frozen-position [impulse approximation](impulse-approximation.md) overestimates the energy transfer. An [orbital resonance](orbital-resonance.md) can invalidate the simple slow-variation argument.
