<h1 id="vaughan-identity-for-the-mobius-function">Vaughan identity for the Möbius function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vaughan's identity](vaughan-s-identity.md)

The displayed identity is exact for all positive integers and all positive cutoffs. Expand $(\mu-\mu_{\leq U})*(\mu-\mu_{\leq V})*\mathbf1$ and use $\mu*\mathbf1=\varepsilon$. The two truncated terms are short sums; the negative term is a [type I sum](type-i-sum.md) over [divisors](divisor.md) at most $UV$; the last term is a [type II sum](type-ii-sum.md) with both factor variables larger than a cutoff. This form of the [Vaughan identity](vaughan-s-identity.md) is specific to Möbius weights.
