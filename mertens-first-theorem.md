# Mertens first theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Mertens' theorems](mertens-theorems.md)

The [prime sum](prime-sum.md) $\sum_{p\leq y}(\log p)/p=\log y+O(1)$ is the first Mertens theorem. It controls the mean logarithmic size in a [truncated Euler-product lower bound](truncated-euler-product-lower-bound.md).
