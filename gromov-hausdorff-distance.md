# Gromov-Hausdorff distance

↑ **Parent:** [Hausdorff distance](hausdorff-distance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gromov-Hausdorff_distance)

For [compact metric spaces](compact-metric-space.md) $X$ and $Y$, the Gromov-Hausdorff distance is

$$
d_{GH}(X,Y)=\inf_{Z,\varphi,\psi}d_H^Z(\varphi(X),\psi(Y)),
$$

where the [infimum](infimum.md) runs over all [metric spaces](metric-space.md) $Z$ and all [isometric embeddings](isometric-embedding.md) $\varphi:X\to Z$ and $\psi:Y\to Z$. It measures how closely the two spaces can be placed inside one ambient [metric space](metric-space.md).
