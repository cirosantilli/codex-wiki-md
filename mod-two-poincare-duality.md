# Mod-two Poincare duality

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md)

For a closed $n$-dimensional [manifold](topological-manifold.md), [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) holds over $\mathbb F_2$ without an [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) assumption. The mod-two [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) exists because orientation signs disappear. The resulting complementary-degree [intersection pairing](intersection-pairing.md) is a [perfect pairing](perfect-pairing.md).
