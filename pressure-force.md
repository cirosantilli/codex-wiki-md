# Pressure force

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure-split.md)

The resultant of distributed surface [pressure](pressure-split.md); in a fluid its [force](force-split.md) per unit volume is $-\nabla P$. A spherical shell in [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) balances this against its gravitational weight.
