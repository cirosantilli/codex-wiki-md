# Sea ice

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary ice shell](planetary-ice-shell.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_ice)

Sea ice is frozen ocean water. A local thermodynamic description uses [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md), a surface energy balance and the [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md); salinity, mechanical transport and fractional ice coverage add further physics.
