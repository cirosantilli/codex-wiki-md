# Hand-over-hand motion of a two-headed motor

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular motor](molecular-motor.md)

In hand-over-hand motion, two attached heads exchange their leading and trailing roles. For a centre-of-mass advance $d$, one head can move about $2d$ while the other remains fixed. The motion of a fluorescent label also depends on its position on the motor.[Alternating light-chain label displacements support head exchange](myosin-v-walks-hand-over-hand-single-fluorophore-imaging-with-1-5-nm-localization-by-ahmet-yildiz-and-others.md)
