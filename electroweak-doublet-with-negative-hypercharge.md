# Electroweak doublet with negative hypercharge

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md)

An electroweak doublet with $Y=-1/2$ has component charges $(0,-1)$ under $Q=T^3+Y$. Its upper-component vacuum therefore preserves [electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md). The canonical covariant-derivative term gives $m_W=g_2v/2$, $m_Z=v\sqrt{g_1^2+g_2^2}/2$ and a massless [photon](photon.md). Neutral [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) are proportional to $(g_2,-g_1)$ for $Z$ and $(g_1,g_2)$ for the [photon](photon.md). This is the conjugate-doublet convention to the usual positive-hypercharge Higgs doublet and gives the same [mass](mass.md) ratio and electric coupling.
