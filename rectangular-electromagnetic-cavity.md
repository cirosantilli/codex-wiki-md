# Rectangular electromagnetic cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md)

A closed rectangular [perfect conductor](perfect-conductor.md) cavity has discrete [normal modes](normal-mode.md) selected by zero tangential [electric field](electric-field-split.md) at the walls. Each [frequency](frequency.md) has the displayed form. With all three indices positive there are two independent [polarizations of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md); modes with exactly one zero index have one, and those with two zero indices are absent. For example the $n=0$ family can have $E_z\propto\sin(\pi lx/L_x)\sin(\pi my/L_y)$, independent of $z$. It satisfies the wall conditions because $E_z$ is normal to the $z$ faces and tangential to the side walls. Omitting such zero-index families is harmless for leading bulk [density of states](density-of-states.md), but changes the low-[temperature](temperature-split.md) physics of a thin cavity.
