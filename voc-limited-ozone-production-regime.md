# VOC-limited ozone-production regime

↑ **Parent:** [Photochemical ozone production](photochemical-ozone-production.md)

A regime with abundant [nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md) in which volatile-organic oxidation and radical supply limit net [tropospheric ozone](tropospheric-ozone.md) production. Reducing [volatile organic compounds](volatile-organic-compound.md) lowers production. A small NOx reduction can initially increase [ozone](ozone.md) by weakening OH termination and NO titration; sufficiently large reductions can cross into an [NOx-limited ozone-production regime](nox-limited-ozone-production-regime.md). Local [concentration](concentration.md) and regional emission sensitivities need not coincide.
