# Antisymmetry of the second-order monochromatic alpha tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Monochromatic small-Reynolds-number mean electromotive force](monochromatic-small-reynolds-number-mean-electromotive-force.md)

For $\mathbf C=\mathbf u\times(\mathbf B\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u$, the [cross product](cross-product.md) identity $(\mathbf u\times\nabla\times\mathbf C)_i=u_j\partial_iC_j-u_j\partial_jC_i$ and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) give $\mathcal E^{(2)}_i=-\langle\partial_i u_jC_j\rangle$. Its [alpha tensor](alpha-tensor.md) is

$$
\alpha^{(2)}_{ij}=-\langle\partial_i\mathbf u\cdot(\mathbf u\times\partial_j\mathbf u)\rangle
=\langle\mathbf u\cdot(\partial_i\mathbf u\times\partial_j\mathbf u)\rangle.
$$

Interchanging $i,j$ reverses the [cross product](cross-product.md), showing that the coefficient is an [antisymmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md). Such a coefficient represents a [turbulent magnetic pumping](turbulent-magnetic-pumping.md) contribution rather than the symmetric part of the [alpha effect](alpha-effect.md).
