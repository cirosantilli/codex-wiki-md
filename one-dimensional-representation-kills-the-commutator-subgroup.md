# One-dimensional representation kills the commutator subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Complex representation](complex-representation.md)

The image of a one-dimensional representation lies in the abelian group $\mathbb C^\times$, so its kernel contains the [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) of the represented group.
