<h1 id="cubic-b-spline-bezier-extraction">Cubic B-spline Bézier extraction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal cubic B-spline](cardinal-cubic-b-spline.md)

For the unit-spaced cubic cardinal [B-spline](b-spline.md) on $[0,4]$, the four scalar cubic [Bernstein basis](bernstein-basis.md) coefficient rows are $(0,0,0,1/6)$, $(1/6,1/3,2/3,2/3)$, $(2/3,2/3,1/3,1/6)$ and $(1/6,0,0,0)$. Each row applies on its interval with local parameter in $[0,1]$. They result from the compactly supported $C^2$ truncated-cube combination $\tfrac16\sum_{j=0}^4(-1)^j\binom4j(x-j)_+^3$. Each graph piece is a cubic [Bézier curve](bezier-curve.md) with abscissa controls $j,j+1/3,j+2/3,j+1$.
