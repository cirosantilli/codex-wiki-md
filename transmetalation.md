# Transmetalation

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

Transfer of an organic group from one element to a metal centre. A rhodium [hydroxide](hydroxide.md) and an arylboronic acid can exchange Rh-OH and B-Ar connectivity to form Rh-Ar and boric acid.
