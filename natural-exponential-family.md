# Natural exponential family

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential family](exponential-family-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_exponential_family)

A natural [exponential family](exponential-family-split.md) uses its observation vector itself as the canonical [sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md). Its [cumulant function](cumulant-function-of-an-exponential-family.md) is $\kappa(\theta)=\log\int h(y)e^{\theta^Ty}\,d\nu(y)$ and its [natural parameter space](natural-parameter-space.md) consists of parameters for which this integral is finite. On the interior, differentiating gives [mean parameter of an exponential family](mean-parameter-of-an-exponential-family.md) $\nabla\kappa$ and [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $\nabla^2\kappa$.
