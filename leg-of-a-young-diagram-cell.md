# Leg of a Young-diagram cell

↑ **Parent:** [Hook of a Young diagram](hook-of-a-young-diagram.md)

The leg consists of the cells strictly below $(i,j)$ in its column of the [Young diagram](young-diagram.md); its length is $l_\lambda(i,j)=\lambda'_j-i$, where $\lambda'$ is the [conjugate partition](conjugate-partition.md). It excludes the anchor cell. At $(1,2)$ in $(3,2)$ the leg contains only $(2,2)$, so its length is one. Transposing the [Young diagram](young-diagram.md) exchanges the leg and the [arm of a Young-diagram cell](arm-of-a-young-diagram-cell.md).
