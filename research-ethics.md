# Research ethics

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological research methods](psychological-research-methods.md)

Principles and practices for responsible research, including respect for participants, scientific integrity, proportionate risk assessment and independent review. [Informed consent](informed-consent.md), privacy and honest reporting are part of the research design rather than substitutes for its scientific quality.[2021, Sections 2–4 and 13](code-of-human-research-ethics-by-british-psychological-society.md)
