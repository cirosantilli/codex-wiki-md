# Indecomposable modules for a cyclic p-group

↑ **Parent:** [Modular representation theory](modular-representation-theory.md)

For $P$ cyclic of order $q=p^n$ in characteristic $p$, its [group algebra](group-algebra.md) is $k[t]/(t^q)$. The nilpotent [Jordan normal form](jordan-normal-form.md) classifies its indecomposable finite-dimensional modules as $V_i$, $1\leq i\leq q$. A module homomorphism $V_i\to V_j$ is determined by an element annihilated by $t^i$, so its dimension is $\min(i,j)$.
