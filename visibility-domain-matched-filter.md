# Visibility-domain matched filter

↑ **Parent:** [Matched filter](matched-filter.md)

For data $d=aT+n$, known complex source template $T$, real amplitude $a$, and known noise covariance $C$, generalized least squares gives the displayed amplitude. This convention takes $C$ in the quadratic objective $(d-aT)^\dagger C^{-1}(d-aT)$; factors of two in the amplitude [variance](variance-split.md) depend on whether complex-component or real-component covariance is used. Source position, shape and contaminants must be fitted or marginalized when uncertain. An extended-source [complex visibility](complex-visibility.md) template accounts for resolved-out flux rather than assuming the source is unresolved.
