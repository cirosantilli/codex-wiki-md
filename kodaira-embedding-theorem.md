# Kodaira embedding theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Positive holomorphic line bundle](positive-holomorphic-line-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodaira_embedding_theorem)

The Kodaira embedding theorem says that a compact [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) carrying a [positive holomorphic line bundle](positive-holomorphic-line-bundle.md) is projective: sufficiently large [tensor powers](tensor-power.md) of the bundle have global holomorphic sections defining a [holomorphic embedding](holomorphic-embedding.md) into [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md). Conversely, the restriction of $\mathcal O(1)$ gives a positive line bundle on every projective complex manifold.
