# Hiemenz flow

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous boundary layer](viscous-boundary-layer.md)

The steady planar [stagnation point](stagnation-point.md) [boundary layer](boundary-layer.md) with outer [velocity](velocity.md) $(Ax,-Ay)$ has the [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) $u=Ax f'(\eta)$, $v=-\sqrt{A\nu}f(\eta)$, $\eta=y\sqrt{A/\nu}$. The [Prandtl boundary-layer equation](prandtl-boundary-layer-equation.md) becomes $f'''+ff''-(f')^2+1=0$, with $f(0)=f'(0)=0$ and $f'(\infty)=1$. Its thickness is of order $\sqrt{\nu/A}$, independent of horizontal distance. Normal dynamic [pressure](pressure-split.md) corrections are of smaller order than the tangential driving [pressure](pressure-split.md).
