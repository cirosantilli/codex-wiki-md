# Koszul self-Ext algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Ext groups between polynomial-ring residue modules](ext-groups-between-polynomial-ring-residue-modules.md)

For an ideal generated by a finite [regular sequence](regular-sequence.md) in a commutative ring, the self-[Ext functor](ext-functor.md) algebra is exterior on the degree-one dual generators. Contraction chain maps on the [Koszul resolution](koszul-resolution.md) represent those generators, square to zero, and anticommute. The exterior square-zero relations also apply in characteristic two.
