# Johnson graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson_graph)

The Johnson graph has the $k$-element subsets of an $n$-element set as vertices, with two vertices adjacent exactly when their intersection has $k-1$ elements. Relabelling the ground set gives graph automorphisms. The [maximal cliques of a Johnson graph](maximal-cliques-of-a-johnson-graph.md) allow reconstruction of those ground-set labels when the two clique sizes are different.
