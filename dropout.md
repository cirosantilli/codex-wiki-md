# Dropout

↑ **Parent:** [Neural network](neural-network.md)

A training regularization method that randomly removes selected activations or connections. In inverted dropout with keep probability $p$, multiply each retained activation by $1/p$, so its conditional [expectation](expected-value-split.md) equals the original activation; inference then uses the unmasked activations. The random masks discourage dependence on a particular small collection of activations.
