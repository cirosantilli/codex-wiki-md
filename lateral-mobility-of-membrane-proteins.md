# Lateral mobility of membrane proteins

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane protein](membrane-protein.md)

[Lateral mobility of membrane proteins](lateral-mobility-of-membrane-proteins.md) is movement along a membrane surface. It differs from [membrane transport](membrane-transport.md) through the boundary and from reversing [membrane topology](membrane-topology.md). Attachment, crowding and barriers can restrict [diffusion](diffusion.md); the [fluid mosaic model](fluid-mosaic-model.md) does not require every [membrane protein](membrane-protein.md) to wander freely.[Cooper, The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition, Structure of the Plasma Membrane, Mobility of Membrane Proteins: lateral movement and restrictions](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/structure-of-the-plasma-membrane.md)

An original local model treats a small, approximately flat patch as an unbounded plane with uniform [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$, no drift and no trapping. Represent the two displacement coordinates by independent [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md), each with zero [expected value](expected-value-split.md) and [variance](variance-split.md) $2Dt$. Since $r^2=x^2+y^2$, addition of the two coordinate expectations gives

$$
\langle r^2\rangle=4Dt,\qquad r_{\mathrm{rms}}=\sqrt{4Dt}.
$$

This is a root-mean-square displacement, not distance traveled along a jagged trajectory or the displacement of every [protein](protein-split.md). For a stipulated $D=0.08\,\mu\mathrm{m}^2\mathrm{s}^{-1}$ and $t=5\,\mathrm{s}$, $\langle r^2\rangle=1.6\,\mu\mathrm{m}^2$ and $r_{\mathrm{rms}}\approx1.265\,\mu\mathrm{m}$. Reaching a stipulated root-mean-square scale $L=2\,\mu\mathrm{m}$ gives $t=L^2/(4D)=12.5\,\mathrm{s}$. These model values are not measured [diffusion coefficients](diffusion-coefficient.md) or mixing times from the historical experiment.

Multiplying the target displacement scale by two multiplies the model time by four. This quadratic scaling is a useful way to see why local encounters and redistribution over an entire [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) need not happen on the same timescale. A curved, bounded [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md), restricted paths and different mobile populations require more than this flat-patch model; disappearance of an initial pattern alone does not determine $D$.
