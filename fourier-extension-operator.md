# Fourier extension operator

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier restriction theory](fourier-restriction-theory.md)

A [Fourier extension operator](fourier-extension-operator.md) forms an oscillatory integral from a density on a frequency surface with measure $\sigma$. The opposite sign in the phase merely reflects the output variable, so it gives identical [Lp norms](lp-norm.md). In the usual dual formulation it is the adjoint of restricting the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) to that surface.
