# Total variation distance

↑ **Parent:** [Probability and statistics](probability-and-statistics-split.md)

The total variation distance between probability distributions is

$$
\lVert\mu-\nu\rVert_{\mathrm{TV}}
=\sup_A|\mu(A)-\nu(A)|.
$$
