# Cylindrical basis

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical coordinate system](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md)

The local [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) associated with [cylindrical coordinates](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md): $\mathbf e_r=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0)$, $\mathbf e_\theta=(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta,0)$, and $\mathbf e_z=(0,0,1)$. The first two [basis vectors](basis-vector.md) depend on the angular coordinate and are undefined on the axis without a choice of angle.
