# Evenly covered neighbourhood

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

An [open](open-set.md) [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $U$ of a point in the base of a [covering map](covering-space.md) $p$ is an [evenly covered neighbourhood](evenly-covered-neighbourhood.md) if $p^{-1}(U)$ is a disjoint union of [open subsets](open-set.md), each mapped onto all of $U$ by a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) obtained by restricting $p$.
