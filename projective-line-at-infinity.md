# Projective line at infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane](projective-plane.md)

After choosing the affine chart $z\ne0$ in the [projective plane](projective-plane.md), its complement $z=0$ is the projective line at infinity. Affine parallel lines meet at the same [projective point](projective-point.md) on this line, corresponding to their common direction. The choice of this line is part of the affine chart, not an intrinsic distinction preserved by every [projective transformation](projective-linear-transformation.md).
