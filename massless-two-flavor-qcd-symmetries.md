# Massless two-flavor QCD symmetries

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)

The [gauge-covariant kinetic term](gauge-covariant-kinetic-term.md) for two massless [quarks](quark.md) separates into left- and right-handed parts. Independent constant unitary [quark flavor](quark-flavor.md) rotations preserve these terms, giving classical $U(2)_L\times U(2)_R$ internal [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) in addition to local color $SU(3)$. The singlet axial $U(1)_A$ has a [chiral anomaly](chiral-anomaly.md), while the nonsinglet [chiral symmetry](chiral-symmetry.md) and vector [baryon number](baryon-number.md) remain exact quantum symmetries, up to their shared discrete identifications. Equal nonzero [quark masses](quark-mass.md) reduce the continuous [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) to $SU(2)_V\times U(1)_V$.
