# Strong bisimulation

↑ **Parent:** [Bisimulation](bisimulation.md)

A relation on states of [labelled transition systems](labelled-transition-system.md) matching every transition with a transition of the same label in either direction, with successor states again related. In particular, $\tau$ transitions are matched individually, rather than silently skipped. For [CSP processes](csp-process.md) this preserves [CSP traces](csp-trace.md), [CSP stable failures](csp-stable-failure.md) and [CSP divergences](csp-divergence.md).
