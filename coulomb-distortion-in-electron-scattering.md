# Coulomb distortion in electron scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md)

The change in incident and outgoing [electron](electron.md) [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md) caused by the target [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md). It can invalidate a [plane wave](plane-wave.md) treatment in the [Born approximation](born-approximation.md), especially for a large target [atomic number](atomic-number.md).
