# Totient divisibility along divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md)

Write $n=\prod p^{a_p}$ and $d=\prod p^{b_p}$, with $0\le b_p\le a_p$. The prime-power formula for the [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md) gives an integer contribution to $\varphi(n)/\varphi(d)$ from each prime: it is $p^{a_p-b_p}$ when $b_p>0$, and $p^{a_p-1}(p-1)$ when $b_p=0<a_p$. Multiplying proves the [divisibility](divisibility.md) assertion, including $d=1$ with $\varphi(1)=1$.
