# Membrane bending rigidity

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal membrane undulation](thermal-membrane-undulation.md)

This coefficient multiplies the squared mean-curvature term $\frac\kappa2\int(2H-C_0)^2dA$ in the [Helfrich membrane energy](thermal-membrane-undulation.md). It has units of [energy](energy-split.md), unlike a beam's flexural rigidity. For small height modes of a nearly flat membrane of area $A$, with Fourier normalization $h_q=A^{-1}\int h(\mathbf x)e^{-i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf x}d^2x$, quadratic bending and [tension](tension.md) energies give $\langle|h_q|^2\rangle=k_BT/[A(\kappa q^4+\sigma q^2)]$ by the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md). Thermal-flicker measurements can therefore separate short-wavelength bending from longer-wavelength tension effects.
