# Arithmetical transfinite recursion theory

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order arithmetic](second-order-arithmetic.md)

[Arithmetical transfinite recursion theory](arithmetical-transfinite-recursion-theory.md) permits [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) recursive definitions along coded countable well-orders, together with the usual [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) comprehension background. [Friedman's finite form of Kruskal's theorem](friedman-s-finite-form-of-kruskal-s-theorem.md) is not provable in this theory.
