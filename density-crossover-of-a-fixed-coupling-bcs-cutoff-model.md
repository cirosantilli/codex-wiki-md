# Density crossover of a fixed-coupling BCS cutoff model

↑ **Parent:** [Asymmetric-cutoff BCS transition temperature](asymmetric-cutoff-bcs-transition-temperature.md)

Set the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) equal to the degenerate three-dimensional spin-degenerate [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md), proportional to $n^{2/3}$, and keep the effective coupling and upper cutoff fixed. The [asymmetric-cutoff BCS transition temperature](asymmetric-cutoff-bcs-transition-temperature.md) scales as $n^{1/3}$ below $n_\theta$ and saturates above it. At low density the predicted $k_BT_c/\mu$ instead grows as $n^{-1/3}$, eventually invalidating the degenerate and logarithmic approximations. This is a prescribed cutoff model, not a self-consistent treatment of the [BCS-BEC crossover](bcs-bec-crossover.md).
