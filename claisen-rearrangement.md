# Claisen rearrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Organic chemistry](organic-chemistry.md)

A concerted six-electron \[3,3\]-sigmatropic rearrangement of an allyl vinyl ether into an unsaturated [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) compound. Suprafacial bond reorganization correlates stereochemistry across the allyl fragment.
