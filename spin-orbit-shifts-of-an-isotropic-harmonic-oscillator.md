# Spin-orbit shifts of an isotropic harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-orbit energy shift in a central potential](spin-orbit-energy-shift-in-a-central-potential.md)

For an isotropic three-dimensional [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), $V(r)=m\omega^2r^2/2$ makes $r^{-1}V'(r)=m\omega^2$. The [spin-orbit energy shift in a central potential](spin-orbit-energy-shift-in-a-central-potential.md) is therefore independent of the radial excitation. For [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md), the $l=0,j=1/2$ shift is zero, while the two $l=1$ shifts are $-\hbar^2\omega^2/(2mc^2)$ for $j=1/2$ and $+\hbar^2\omega^2/(4mc^2)$ for $j=3/2$.

These shifts are quadratic in trap frequency. Their degeneracy-weighted sum in the $l=1$ manifold is zero, since the multiplicities are two and four. They are perturbative corrections rather than full oscillator energies; a nonrelativistic treatment requires them to be small compared with the orbital spacings.
