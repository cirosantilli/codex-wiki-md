# Ferromagnetic ground-state multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg ferromagnet](heisenberg-ferromagnet.md)

A connected isotropic spin-$S$ ferromagnet with positive exchanges has a fully polarized [ground state](ground-state.md) and a maximal-total-spin multiplet. For $N$ sites the total spin is $NS$, with $2NS+1$ magnetic states generated by total-spin lowering. Global rotations span the same degenerate space. Slowly varying reorientations produce gapless [ferromagnetic magnons](ferromagnetic-magnon.md); finite-volume degeneracy must be distinguished from a thermodynamic statement about spontaneous order.
