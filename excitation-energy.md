# Excitation energy

↑ **Parent:** [Energy level](energy-level.md)

[Excitation energy](excitation-energy.md) is an [energy level](energy-level.md)'s energy above a specified [ground state](ground-state.md). Emitted [photon](photon.md) [energies](energy-split.md) measure differences between levels, with a small [recoil](recoil.md) correction when the emitter has finite [mass](mass.md).
