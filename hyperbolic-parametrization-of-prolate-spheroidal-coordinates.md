# Hyperbolic parametrization of prolate spheroidal coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Prolate spheroidal coordinates](prolate-spheroidal-coordinates.md)

For focal distance $a>0$, $\xi\geq0$ and $0\leq\theta\leq\pi$, the displayed [prolate spheroidal coordinates](prolate-spheroidal-coordinates.md) are [orthogonal coordinates](orthogonal-coordinates.md). The three coordinate [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md) have lengths $h_\xi=h_\theta=a\sqrt{\sinh^2\xi+\sin^2\theta}$ and $h_\phi=a\sinh\xi\sin\theta$. Their product gives the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $a^3(\sinh^2\xi+\sin^2\theta)\sinh\xi\sin\theta$. A positive constant $\xi$ gives a [prolate spheroid](prolate-spheroid.md) with equatorial semiaxis $a\sinh\xi$, polar semiaxis $a\cosh\xi$, and foci $(0,0,\pm a)$. The surface degenerates to the focal segment at $\xi=0$; this coordinate degeneracy is distinct from a singularity of the surrounding [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md).
