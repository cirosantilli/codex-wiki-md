# Spin structure

↑ **Parent:** [Spin manifold](spin-manifold.md)

A spin structure is a consistent choice of lifting oriented orthonormal frame changes to their spin double cover, permitting globally defined [spinor fields](spinor-field.md). For a spatial circle in a [worldsheet](worldsheet.md), the two choices give periodic and antiperiodic fermions, hence the [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md) and [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md). Local field equations do not choose the global spin structure.
