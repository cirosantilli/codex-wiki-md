# Gradient-like vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Morse function](morse-function.md)

A descending gradient-like field decreases $f$ away from its [critical points](critical-point.md) and agrees with a negative gradient in chosen [Morse lemma](morse-lemma.md) coordinates near those points. It transports [attaching spheres](attaching-sphere.md) and [belt spheres](belt-sphere.md) across regular levels. Perturbations away from the critical points can arrange the transverse intersections used by the [Morse-Smale complex](morse-smale-complex.md), while preserving the prescribed local models.
