# Charge relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Ohmic conductor](ohmic-conductor.md)

In a homogeneous [Ohmic conductor](ohmic-conductor.md), the [charge continuity equation](charge-continuity-equation.md) and [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) imply exponential decay of the bulk [charge density](charge-density.md), $\rho(t)=\rho(0)e^{-\sigma t/\epsilon}$. For an isolated finite body, bulk charge moves to its boundary; the accumulating [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) preserves total [electric charge](electric-charge.md). The formula uses a constant permittivity $\epsilon$ and the corresponding free-charge convention, or $\epsilon_0$ for total charge in the vacuum form of [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md).
