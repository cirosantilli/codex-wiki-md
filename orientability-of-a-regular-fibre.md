# Orientability of a regular fibre

↑ **Parent:** [Normal bundle of a regular fibre is trivial](normal-bundle-of-a-regular-fibre-is-trivial.md)

Choose an [orientation of a vector space](orientation-of-a-vector-space.md) on $T_qN$. The quotient identification for a [regular value](regular-value.md) gives a smoothly oriented normal bundle along $F=f^{-1}(q)$. An oriented normal frame followed by a frame of $TF$ defines the latter's orientation through the ambient orientation. Changing normal lifts adds only tangent components, so the [determinant](determinant.md) sign is unchanged. No orientation on all of $N$ is required.
