# Volterra series for the linear Boltzmann equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Boltzmann equation](linear-boltzmann-equation.md)

The [factorial bound for a Volterra iterate](factorial-bound-for-a-volterra-iterate.md) gives convergence in the space of bounded strongly measurable $L^2$-valued functions on $[0,T]$. The sum solves $(I-\tau)f=F$, where $F=U_a(t,0)f_0$, and satisfies $\sup_{t\leq T}\|f(t)\|_2\leq e^{CT}\|f_0\|_2$. Applying the same iterate bound to a difference $g=\tau g$ proves uniqueness on every finite interval.
