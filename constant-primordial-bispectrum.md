# Constant primordial bispectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md)

A constant [primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md) means a constant [bispectrum shape function](primordial-bispectrum-shape-function.md), not a bispectrum independent of wavenumber. With $P(k)=2\pi^2\Delta_\zeta^2/k^3$ and scale-independent $\Delta_\zeta^2$, one normalization is $B=f_{\rm NL}[P(k_1)P(k_2)P(k_3)]^{2/3}=4\pi^4f_{\rm NL}\Delta_\zeta^4/(k_1k_2k_3)^2$.
