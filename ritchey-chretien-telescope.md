<h1 id="ritchey-chretien-telescope">Ritchey–Chrétien telescope</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cassegrain reflector](cassegrain-reflector.md)

A Ritchey–Chrétien telescope has hyperbolic primary and secondary mirrors chosen to cancel third-order [spherical aberration](spherical-aberration.md) and [coma](coma-optics.md). It retains [astigmatism](astigmatism-optical-systems.md) and [field curvature](petzval-field-curvature.md); wide-field instruments may require additional correction.
