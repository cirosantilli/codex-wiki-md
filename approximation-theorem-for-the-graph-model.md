# Approximation theorem for the graph model

↑ **Parent:** [Graph model of lambda calculus](graph-model-of-lambda-calculus.md)

The interpretation of a [lambda term](lambda-term.md) in the [graph model of lambda calculus](graph-model-of-lambda-calculus.md) is the union of the interpretations of all its [finite Böhm approximants](finite-bohm-approximant.md). Every finite indexed interpretation reduces monotonically to an indexed normal approximant. Taking the union over all occurrence indices gives one inclusion; replacing subterms by bottom and using beta soundness gives the other. This identifies denotational information with finite observable parts of a [Böhm tree](bohm-tree.md).
