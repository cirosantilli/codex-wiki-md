# Coded relative constructible stage

↑ **Parent:** [Relative constructible hierarchy](relative-constructible-hierarchy.md)

This [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) asserts the existence of a history function on $\xi+1$ starting at $X$, applying [definable power set](definable-power-set-split.md) at successors and unions at nonzero [limit ordinals](limit-ordinal.md), and ending at $B$. In a [transitive set](transitive-set.md) satisfying [finite relation closure for set-theoretic coding](finite-relation-closure-for-set-theoretic-coding.md), each history is correct by [transfinite induction](transfinite-induction.md), so $B=L_\xi(X)$. Relation operations produce restrictions to shorter histories. Since [stage histories appear below every limit constructible level](stage-histories-appear-below-every-limit-constructible-level.md), every history of length $\xi+1$ for $\xi<\alpha$ belongs to $L_\alpha(X)$ when $\alpha$ is a nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md). This is the coding step behind [relative constructible level recognition](relative-constructible-level-recognition.md).
