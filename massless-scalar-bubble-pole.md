# Massless scalar bubble pole

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar bubble integral](scalar-bubble-integral.md)

With a mostly-plus [metric signature](metric-signature.md), define $f_d=(1/i)\int d^dk/[(2\pi)^d(k^2-i\epsilon)((P-k)^2-i\epsilon)]$. [Wick rotation](wick-rotation.md) and a [Feynman parameter](feynman-parameter.md) give $f_d=\Gamma(2-d/2)(4\pi)^{-d/2}\int_0^1[x(1-x)P^2]^{d/2-2}dx$. At nonzero Euclidean $P^2$, the [Gamma function](gamma-function.md) supplies the pole. Exceptional massless [momentum](momentum.md) can introduce infrared ambiguity.
