# Complex multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_multiplication)

An [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md) of dimension $g$ has complex multiplication when its rational [endomorphism ring](endomorphism-ring.md) contains a commutative semisimple $\mathbb Q$-algebra of dimension $2g$. For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) in characteristic zero, this means its geometric [endomorphism ring of an elliptic curve](endomorphism-ring-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) is an order in an [imaginary quadratic field](imaginary-quadratic-field.md). The resulting rank-one [Tate module](tate-module.md) representations connect torsion points to [class field theory](class-field-theory.md).
