# Density bound on a relativistically decoupled massive relic

↑ **Parent:** [Conserved cosmological relic abundance](conserved-cosmological-relic-abundance.md)

A species freezing out while relativistic retains its thermal comoving number. If it is nonrelativistic today, $\rho_x\simeq m_xn_x$. Comparing with [photons](photon.md) gives $\Omega_x/\Omega_\gamma=m_x(n_x/n_\gamma)/\langle E_\gamma\rangle$. An upper density fraction therefore bounds its mass inversely with its relic number density. The relation does not apply unchanged to a species whose abundance is depleted by nonrelativistic annihilation.
