# Geodesic quadrilateral in a hyperbolic metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic metric space](hyperbolic-metric-space.md)

Every side of a geodesic quadrilateral in a $\delta$-hyperbolic metric space lies in the closed $2\delta$-neighbourhood of the other three sides. Indeed, a diagonal divides the quadrilateral into two $\delta$-thin triangles.
