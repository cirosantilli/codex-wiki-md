# Lorentz algebra in three spacetime dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra of the Lorentz group](lie-algebra-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

With signature $(-,+,+)$, two [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) generators and one spatial rotation obey $[J,K_1]=K_2$, $[J,K_2]=-K_1$ and $[K_1,K_2]=-J$. Replacing the boosts by $iK_1,iK_2$ gives the compact cyclic brackets, proving the complexified isomorphism with the [SU(2) Lie algebra](su-2-lie-algebra.md). The real algebra has dimension three and rank one. Its finite-dimensional complex [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) have dimensions $m+1$ for nonnegative integer [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $m$.
