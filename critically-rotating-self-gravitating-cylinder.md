# Critically rotating self-gravitating cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical fluid dynamics](astrophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)

A uniform-[mass density](density.md) self-gravitating cylinder has $\Phi_R=2\pi G\rho_0R$. Radial balance with the displayed rotation law gives $p_R=\rho_0(k^2R^3-2\pi G\rho_0R)$. At central [pressure](pressure.md) $\pi^2G^2\rho_0^3/k^2$, [pressure](pressure.md) becomes $\rho_0k^2(R^2-R_0^2)^2/4$ and the [free surface](free-surface.md) has zero effective gravity. The positive radius uses $|k|$ if either rotation direction is allowed.
