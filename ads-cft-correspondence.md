<h1 id="ads-cft-correspondence">AdS/CFT correspondence</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-gravity duality](gauge-gravity-duality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdS/CFT_correspondence)

The AdS/CFT correspondence identifies a conformal field theory with quantum gravity on an asymptotically anti-de Sitter spacetime. Boundary sources couple to CFT operators and equal boundary values of bulk fields.
