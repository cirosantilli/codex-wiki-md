# Conformal map of a symmetric circular lens

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map of a circular lens](conformal-map-of-a-circular-lens.md)

The intersection of the unit disks centered at $1/2\pm i\sqrt3/2$ has endpoints zero and one. The [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $w=z/(1-z)$ maps it onto the sector $|\arg w|<\pi/6$: the two disk conditions become $\operatorname{Re}(e^{\pm i\pi/3}w)>0$. Cubing gives the right half-plane, and the [Cayley transform between the half-plane and disk](cayley-transform-between-the-half-plane-and-disk.md) gives the displayed holomorphic bijection to the unit disk. The sector width ensures that cubing is injective.
