# Edge density of a bipartite graph

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilistic combinatorics](probabilistic-combinatorics-split.md)

For disjoint nonempty vertex sets $A,B$, their edge density is

$$
d(A,B)=\frac{e(A,B)}{|A||B|}.
$$
