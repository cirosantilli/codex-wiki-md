# Air shower

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_shower)

An [air shower](air-shower.md) is a cascade of secondary particles produced by an energetic particle interacting in the atmosphere. Both a [charged particle](charged-particle.md) and a [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) can start one.[A new world of particles, secondary showers; Cosmic accelerators, gamma-ray showers](cosmic-rays-particles-from-outer-space-by-cern.md)
