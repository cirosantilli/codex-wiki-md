# Bessel power local martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel process](bessel-process.md)

For a [Bessel process](bessel-process.md) of dimension $\delta$, started at $x>0$, the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives

$$
d(X_t^{2-\delta})=(2-\delta)X_t^{1-\delta}\,dW_t
$$

on its stochastic interval before hitting zero. Thus the power is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md) after localization away from zero. For $\delta\geq2$ this interval is the whole lifetime; at $\delta=2$ the power is constant. The unqualified assertion fails for a reflecting Bessel process of dimension one, which acquires [local time of a semimartingale](local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md) at zero.
