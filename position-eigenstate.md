# Position eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Position operator](position-operator.md)

A generalized eigenstate of the [position operator](position-operator.md) is labeled by its position eigenvalue. In a continuous [position](position.md) coordinate the normalization is distributional: $\langle q|q'\rangle=\delta(q-q')$ and $\int dq\,|q\rangle\langle q|=1$. Individual position eigenstates are not normalizable Hilbert-space vectors. The [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md) normalization makes $\langle q_f|U|q_i\rangle$ the integral kernel of a [time-evolution operator](time-evolution-operator.md), such as the [free-particle propagator](free-particle-propagator.md).
