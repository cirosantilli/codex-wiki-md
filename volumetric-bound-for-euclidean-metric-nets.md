# Volumetric bound for Euclidean metric nets

↑ **Parent:** [Metric net](metric-net.md)

A subset of the radius-$R$ [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md) admits an $\varepsilon$-[metric net](metric-net.md) of size at most $(1+2R/\varepsilon)^n$. Take a maximal separated set: its disjoint radius-$\varepsilon/2$ [Euclidean balls](euclidean-ball.md) lie in the radius-$(R+\varepsilon/2)$ ball. Comparing [Lebesgue measures](lebesgue-measure.md) gives the estimate. In particular, the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md) admits a net of radius one with at most $3^n$ points.
