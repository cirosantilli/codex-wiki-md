# Lactate

↑ **Parent:** [Lactic acid](lactic-acid.md)

[Lactate](lactate.md) is the [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) of [lactic acid](lactic-acid.md). [Lactate dehydrogenase](lactate-dehydrogenase.md) couples its interconversion with [pyruvate](pyruvate.md) to the [NAD+](nad-plus.md)/[NADH](nadh.md) [redox](redox.md) pair.
