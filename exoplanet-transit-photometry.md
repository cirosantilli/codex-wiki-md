# Exoplanet transit photometry

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmospheric observatory](exoplanet-atmospheric-observatory.md)

Exoplanet transit photometry measures the stellar flux decrement as an [exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md) crosses the visible stellar disc. After accounting for stellar intensity variation and transit geometry, its depth is approximately $(R_p/R_\star)^2$, determining a radius ratio rather than an absolute planetary radius.
