# Tisserand periapsis bound

↑ **Parent:** [Tisserand parameter](tisserand-parameter.md)

For bound [planet](planet.md)-crossing [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md), $T=2/(q+Q)+2\sqrt{2qQ/(q+Q)}\cos I$, with the [planet](planet.md) radius taken as one. At fixed $q<1$, $T$ is maximized by $I=0$ and $Q=1$. For $2<T<3$ this gives $q\ge(1-\sqrt{3-T})^2/[1+2\sqrt{3-T}-(3-T)]$. This form remains regular at $T=\sqrt8$. It is an invariant-based accessibility bound, not a guarantee that a scattering history attains it; for $0<T\le2$ it gives no positive universal [pericentre](periapsis.md) floor.
