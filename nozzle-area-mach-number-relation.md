# Nozzle area-Mach number relation

↑ **Parent:** [Isentropic nozzle flow](isentropic-nozzle-flow.md)

For a calorically [perfect gas](ideal-gas-split.md) on a steady isentropic [nozzle](nozzle.md) branch,

$$
\frac A{A_*}=\frac1M\left[\frac2{\gamma+1}\left(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right)\right]^{(\gamma+1)/(2(\gamma-1))}.
$$

Here $A_*$ is the sonic area. An area ratio greater than one generally admits a subsonic and a supersonic root; [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) choose the physical branch. Numerical inversion must retain that branch information.
