# Affine plane with doubled origin

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

The affine plane with doubled origin is formed by gluing two copies of $\mathbb A_k^2$ by the identity away from the origin. The overlap is the [punctured affine plane](punctured-affine-plane.md), so this scheme is nonseparated and the two-open affine cover is not acyclic for the structure sheaf.

The gluing resembles a [non-Hausdorff manifold](non-hausdorff-manifold.md) example, but this node concerns an algebraic scheme and its nonseparatedness, not a real manifold.
