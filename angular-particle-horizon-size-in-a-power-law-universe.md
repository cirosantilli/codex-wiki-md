# Angular particle-horizon size in a power-law universe

↑ **Parent:** [Particle horizon in a power-law universe](particle-horizon-in-a-power-law-universe.md)

Divide the [particle horizon in a power-law universe](particle-horizon-in-a-power-law-universe.md) at emission by the [angular diameter distance in a flat power-law universe](angular-diameter-distance-in-a-flat-power-law-universe.md). The result is the small angular extent of one radial horizon [length](length.md). Matter-only and radiation-only histories have powers $1/3$ and $1/2$, respectively. These are idealized single-era histories; a real [recombination](recombination-cosmology.md) horizon and distance depend on different portions of the expansion history.
