# Stark effect

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical physics](mathematical-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stark_effect)

An [electric field](electric-field.md) shifts and can split the [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue.md) of an [atom](atom-physics.md). This is the Stark effect; its [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) ground-state case is quadratic in a weak field.
