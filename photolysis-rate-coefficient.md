# Photolysis rate coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Photodissociation](photodissociation.md)

The first-order rate coefficient for a specified [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) channel. It integrates spectral [actinic flux](actinic-flux.md), absorption [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) and channel [quantum yield](quantum-yield.md). Absorption without dissociation does not contribute to that channel's rate. Its units are inverse time.
