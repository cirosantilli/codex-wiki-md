# Dispersive wave

↑ **Parent:** [Dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md)

A wave whose phase speed depends on frequency. In a one-dimensional branch, phase velocity is $\omega/k$ and the narrow-packet [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $d\omega/dk$. Loss, mode conversion and broad spectral bandwidth require care before identifying group speed with energy or signal transport. Confinement can create dispersion even when the underlying unbounded material has a constant wave speed.
