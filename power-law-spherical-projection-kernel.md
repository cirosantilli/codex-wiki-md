# Power-law spherical projection kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical Abel deprojection](spherical-abel-deprojection.md)

For $A,b>0$ and $\alpha>1/2$, the projected profile $F(R)=A(R^2+b^2)^{-\alpha}$ has the displayed spherical [Abel transform](abel-transform.md) inverse. Indeed its composed transform is $A(r^2+b^2)^{1/2-\alpha}I_\alpha$, with $I_\alpha=\int_1^\infty s^{-\alpha}(s-1)^{-1/2}\,ds=B(1/2,\alpha-1/2)$. Differentiate in $r$, divide by $-2\pi r$, and use the [Gamma function recurrence](gamma-function-recurrence.md) to obtain the coefficient. The same kernel reconstructs tracer [number density](number-density.md) or isotropic random pressure from their respective projected profiles.
