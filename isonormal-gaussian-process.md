# Isonormal Gaussian process

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isonormal_Gaussian_process)

An isonormal Gaussian process over a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $H$ is a centered [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) $(W(h))_{h\in H}$ satisfying

$$
\mathbb E[W(f)W(g)]=\langle f,g\rangle_H.
$$

It turns the geometry of $H$ into the [covariance](covariance.md) structure of a Gaussian family.
