# Dust birth ring

↑ **Parent:** [Debris disk](debris-disk.md)

A dust birth ring is a belt of parent [planetesimals](planetesimal.md) whose collisions supply dust. [Radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) gives the fragments different orbits even when their release velocities match those of their parents.
