# Rosser separation by a bounded stage comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Rosser sentence](rosser-sentence.md)

For disjoint [sets](set-split.md) numeralwise enumerated by $P,R$, finite-initial-segment and total-comparison [axioms](axiom.md) let $H$ separate the first [set](set-split.md) from the second in the [theory](first-order-theory.md). For a member of the first [set](set-split.md) use a positive $P$ stage and finitely many refutations of $R$. For a member of the second [set](set-split.md) use its positive $R$ stage: earlier proposed $P$ stages are all refuted by a finite bounded proof, while later stages lose to that known $R$ witness. This proves a separator without inferring arbitrary negative facts from the mere [Sigma-1](sigma-1-formula.md) form of the enumerators.
