# Flux of a linear field through a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Surface integral](surface-integral.md)

On an origin-centered radius-$a$ sphere, $\mathbf n=\mathbf x/a$. Spherical symmetry gives $\int_{S_a}x_i\,dS=0$ and $\int_{S_a}x_ix_j\,dS=(4\pi a^4/3)\delta_{ij}$: off-diagonal moments vanish by reflection, diagonal moments are equal, and their sum is $a^2$ times the surface area. Contracting these identities with $M_{ij}/a$ proves the displayed [flux](flux.md) directly, including a zero contribution from a constant field $\mathbf b$. It agrees with the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md), since $\nabla\cdot(M\mathbf x+\mathbf b)=\operatorname{tr}M$.
