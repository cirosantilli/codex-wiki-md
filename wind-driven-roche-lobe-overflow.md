# Wind-driven Roche-lobe overflow

↑ **Parent:** [Donor-wind Roche-lobe response](donor-wind-roche-lobe-response.md)

A fast escaping [Jeans-mode mass loss](jeans-mode-mass-loss.md) wind can drive a giant into [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md) because its radius changes as it loses mass, even though the wind itself is not substantially captured. This differs from slow [Wind Roche-lobe overflow](wind-roche-lobe-overflow.md). For $R_d\propto M_d^{-n}$ and $R_L\propto a(M_d/M)^{1/3}$, wind alone gives $d\log(R_d/R_L)/dt=\dot M[3(1-n)q-(1+3n)]/[3M_d(1+q)]$, where $q=M_d/M_a$ and $\dot M<0$ is the escaping wind rate. The displayed inequality is exactly the condition that overfill increases.
