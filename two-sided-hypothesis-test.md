# Two-sided hypothesis test

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test.md)

A [two-sided test](two-sided-hypothesis-test.md) detects departures in either direction from the [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md), for example $\theta\ne\theta_0$. With a continuous symmetric null statistic centred at zero, its [p-value](p-value.md) is $P_0(|T|\ge|t_{\mathrm{obs}}|)=2P_0(T\ge|t_{\mathrm{obs}}|)$. This is the usual coefficient test printed by Gaussian regression summaries, unlike a directional one-sided comparison.
