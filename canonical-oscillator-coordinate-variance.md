# Canonical oscillator coordinate variance

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

A canonical [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) has geometric number probabilities and mean occupation $(e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1)^{-1}$. The position operator then gives the displayed variance. It interpolates between zero-point fluctuations $\hbar/(2m\omega)$ and the classical variance $k_BT/(m\omega^2)$. The kinetic and potential [energies](energy-split.md) are equal at every [temperature](temperature-split.md), although classical equipartition describes their values only at high [temperature](temperature-split.md).
