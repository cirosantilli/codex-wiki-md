# Orientation of a contour

↑ **Parent:** [Complex contour](complex-contour.md)

The orientation of a [contour](complex-integration-contour.md) specifies the direction in which its parametrization is traversed. Reversing that direction negates its [contour integral](contour-integral.md). For a [simple closed curve](simple-closed-curve.md) in the [complex plane](complex-plane.md), [positive orientation of a contour](positive-orientation-of-a-contour.md) is the direction keeping its bounded interior on the left.
