# Direct piezoelectric effect

↑ **Parent:** [Piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md)

Linear electrical-displacement response to mechanical [stress](stress.md) in a suitable piezoelectric material. It is distinct from the converse effect, in which an applied electric field causes [strain](strain.md). The measured [voltage](voltage.md) also depends on electrical loading and mechanical constraints.
