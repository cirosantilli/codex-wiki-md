# Magnetic field from a Zeeman diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Sodium D-line Zeeman pattern](sodium-d-line-zeeman-pattern.md)

For weak-field sodium D lines neglecting [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md), the smallest adjacent spectral spacing in the full electronic pattern is $s_E=2\mu_BB/3$. A drawing without a labelled [energy](energy-split.md) axis can still estimate $B$ if it includes both unsplit D lines with known [wavelengths](wavelength.md): their separation calibrates $hc(1/\lambda_2-1/\lambda_1)$. Divide the drawn split spacing by the drawn unsplit separation before using that [energy](energy-split.md) scale. Graphical line thickness, unequal drawn spacings and uncalibrated rendering limit precision; they do not justify exact numerical field values.
