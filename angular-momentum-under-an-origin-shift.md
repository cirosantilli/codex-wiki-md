# Angular momentum under an origin shift

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md)

Move the fixed origin from $O$ to $O'=O+\mathbf a$, keeping the axes parallel and the [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md)'s [velocity](velocity.md) measurements unchanged. The new [positions](position.md) are $\mathbf r'_i=\mathbf r_i-\mathbf a$, so distributivity of the [cross product](cross-product.md) gives

$$
\mathbf L_{O'}=\sum_i(\mathbf r_i-\mathbf a)\times\mathbf p_i
=\mathbf L_O-\mathbf a\times\mathbf P,\qquad
\mathbf P=\sum_i\mathbf p_i.
$$

Here $\mathbf P$ is total [linear momentum](momentum.md). For the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) example with $\mathbf a=(1,0,0)\,\mathrm m$, the new [position](position.md) is $(1,0,0)\,\mathrm m$ and $L_{O',z}=6-3=3\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2/s}$. If total [momentum](momentum.md) is zero, [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is independent of this origin shift. A moving origin additionally contributes $-\dot{\mathbf a}\times\mathbf P$ when this expression is differentiated while retaining the original [momenta](momentum.md); see [angular momentum about the centre of mass](angular-momentum-about-the-centre-of-mass.md) for an important case where that term vanishes.
