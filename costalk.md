# Costalk

↑ **Parent:** [Stalk of a sheaf](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md)

The derived [costalk](costalk.md) at $x$ is $i_x^!E$, where $i_x:\{x\}\hookrightarrow X$. It computes local [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) supported at $x$. On an oriented $N$-manifold the constant [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) has [costalk](costalk.md) $\mathbb Q[-N]$, whose [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) is in degree $N$.
