# Modulus of a string compactification

↑ **Parent:** [Compactification in string theory](compactification-in-string-theory.md)

A parameter of an internal geometry, gauge background or [dilaton](dilaton.md) configuration that becomes a scalar field in the lower-dimensional theory. A continuously undetermined parameter gives a flat direction and often a massless field. Fluxes and other effects can generate a potential, leading to [moduli stabilization](moduli-stabilization.md). Shape, size and coupling moduli affect low-energy observables.
