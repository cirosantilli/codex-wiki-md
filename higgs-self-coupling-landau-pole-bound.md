# Higgs self-coupling Landau-pole bound

↑ **Parent:** [Landau pole](landau-pole.md)

In the self-coupling-dominated approximation $d\alpha_H/d\ln\mu^2=b\alpha_H^2$, $b>0$, the [running coupling](running-coupling.md) reaches a perturbative [Landau pole](landau-pole.md) at the displayed scale. With the conventional [Higgs potential](higgs-field-potential.md), $m_H^2=2\lambda_Hv^2$ and one can normalize $\alpha_H=\lambda_H/(4\pi)$. A larger [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) mass therefore decreases the logarithmic interval before the self-coupling becomes strong. Requiring perturbative validity up to a fixed high cutoff gives an upper Higgs-mass bound in this approximation. The full [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) beta function also contains gauge and [Yukawa couplings](yukawa-interaction.md); its sign is not universally positive. A perturbative pole is not a theorem excluding every nonperturbative ultraviolet completion.
