# Cauchy coupling proves continuity of the half-plane Poisson extension

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson integral](poisson-integral.md)

For bounded continuous boundary data $f$, the half-plane [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel.md) is the law of $x+yC$. On one common [probability space](probability-space-split.md), $x+yC\to x_0$ almost surely as $(x,y)\to(x_0,0)$ with $y>0$. Boundedness of $f$ and [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) imply $\phi(x+iy)\to f(x_0)$. This proves [continuity](continuous-function.md) at every finite boundary point even for tangential approach. No moment of the [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) is needed.
