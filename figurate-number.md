# Figurate number

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figurate_number)

A [figurate number](figurate-number.md) is an [integer](integer-split.md) illustrated by a regular geometric arrangement of points. [Triangular numbers](triangular-number.md) and [square numbers](square-number.md) are basic examples: growing a prescribed shape turns a counting problem into a [sequence](sequence.md) of [integers](integer-split.md).
