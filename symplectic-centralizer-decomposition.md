# Symplectic centralizer decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md)

Let $\mathfrak a$ be a [toral subalgebra](toral-subalgebra.md) and an [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md) in the [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md), over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) of [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md). Its [defining representation](defining-representation-of-a-matrix-lie-algebra.md) has a [weight decomposition](weight-decomposition.md). The [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md) identity gives $(\lambda(a)+\mu(a))\omega(v,w)=0$ for $v\in V_\lambda$, $w\in V_\mu$, so $\omega(V_\lambda,V_\mu)=0$ unless $\mu=-\lambda$. Since $\omega$ is a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md), its restrictions give a [perfect pairing](perfect-pairing.md) between $V_\lambda$ and $V_{-\lambda}$, and a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) on $V_0$.

An element of the [centralizer of a Lie subalgebra](centralizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md) preserves each [weight space](weight-space.md). On $V_0$ it is any element of $\mathfrak{sp}(V_0)$. On each nonzero pair, its action $A$ on $V_\lambda$ is arbitrary, and the [perfect pairing](perfect-pairing.md) uniquely forces the action on $V_{-\lambda}$ to be the negative [dual map](transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) $-A^*$. These independent choices preserve [Lie brackets](lie-bracket.md), proving the displayed [direct sum of Lie algebras](direct-sum-of-lie-algebras.md); the sum runs over one representative per nonzero pair.

This [centralizer of a Lie subalgebra](centralizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md) is an [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md) precisely when $V_0=0$ and all the nonzero [weight spaces](weight-space.md) are one-dimensional. Indeed, $\mathfrak{gl}_m$ is nonabelian for $m\geq2$ by $[E_{11},E_{12}]=E_{12}$, while every nonzero [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) contains a two-dimensional [symplectic subspace](symplectic-subspace.md) on which $[\operatorname{diag}(1,-1),E_{12}]=2E_{12}$. In the stated case the surviving summands are copies of $\mathfrak{gl}_1$ and commute. Repeated [weights of a representation](weight-of-a-representation.md), including a zero one, are therefore exactly what obstructs this commutativity.
