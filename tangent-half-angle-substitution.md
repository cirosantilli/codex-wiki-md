# Tangent half-angle substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangent_half-angle_substitution)

The [tangent half-angle substitution](tangent-half-angle-substitution.md) $t=\tan(x/2)$ converts rational trigonometric expressions into rational functions: $\sin x=2t/(1+t^2)$, $\cos x=(1-t^2)/(1+t^2)$ and $dx=2\,dt/(1+t^2)$.
