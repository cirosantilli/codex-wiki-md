# Height-correlation dependence of coherent reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent reflection from a stationary rough surface](coherent-reflection-from-a-stationary-rough-surface.md)

The quadratic coherent correction weights the [power spectrum of surface height](power-spectrum-of-surface-height.md) by $\beta(q)$, so equal [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) heights can give different mean fields. Only an additional long-spatial-scale approximation, with spectrum concentrated at $|q|\ll k$, reduces it to $2k^2\sigma^2$. Second-order surface scattering therefore contains information absent from first-order coherent reflection.
