# Spreadsheet formula

↑ **Parent:** [Spreadsheet cell](spreadsheet-cell.md)

A [mathematical expression](expression-mathematics.md) stored in a [spreadsheet cell](spreadsheet-cell.md), with references to other [spreadsheet cells](spreadsheet-cell.md). In this example `=A1+A2` asks for [addition](addition.md), `=2*A1` for [multiplication](multiplication.md), and `=A2^2` for [exponentiation](exponentiation.md). The evaluator below assumes these [spreadsheet formulas](spreadsheet-formula.md) have already been represented by their ordered lists of referenced [spreadsheet cells](spreadsheet-cell.md) and operations on the resulting numbers; it does not execute arbitrary formula text.
