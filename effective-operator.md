# Effective operator

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md)

An effective operator is a local interaction built from the retained fields in an [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md). Its [coefficient](coefficient.md) records effects of heavier degrees of freedom, and allowed operators respect the relevant [symmetries](symmetry-physics.md). In four dimensions an operator of [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) $\Delta_i$ has the displayed coefficient for dimensionless $C_i$ and a heavy [mass](mass.md) scale $M$. For example a dimension-six [proton-decay operator](proton-decay-operator.md) has a coefficient proportional to $M^{-2}$.
