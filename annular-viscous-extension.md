# Annular viscous extension

↑ **Parent:** [Slender viscous thread](slender-viscous-thread.md)

Uniform [axial strain rate](axial-strain-rate.md) $E$ in an annular [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) gives radial [velocity](velocity.md) $u_r=-Er/2+C/r$. For radii $R_i,R_e$, area parameter $A=R_e^2-R_i^2$, and constant gas [pressures](pressure.md),

$$
C=\frac{R_i^2R_e^2}{2\mu A}\left[p_i-p_e-\gamma\left(\frac1{R_i}+\frac1{R_e}\right)\right],\qquad
p=p_e-\frac{(p_i-p_e)R_i^2}{A}+\frac{\gamma(R_e+R_i)}A-\mu E.
$$

The opposite signs of inner and outer curvature in the [Young–Laplace equation](young-laplace-equation.md) give these expressions. The [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) implies $\dot A=-EA$ and

$$
\frac d{dt}(R_e-R_i)=-\frac E2(R_e-R_i)+\frac\gamma{2\mu}-\frac{(p_i-p_e)R_iR_e}{2\mu(R_i+R_e)}.
$$
