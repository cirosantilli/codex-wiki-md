# Cupping

↑ **Parent:** [History of medicine](history-of-medicine.md)

Application of a [cupping vessel](cupping-vessel.md) to produce local suction. [Dry cupping](dry-cupping.md) uses intact [skin](skin.md); [wet cupping](wet-cupping.md) includes incisions and removal of [blood](blood.md). This procedural distinction does not itself establish therapeutic efficacy. [II.11.1–6](on-medicine-by-aulus-cornelius-celsus.md)
