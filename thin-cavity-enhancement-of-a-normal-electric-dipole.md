# Thin-cavity enhancement of a normal electric dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization-selective spontaneous emission between conducting plates](polarization-selective-spontaneous-emission-between-conducting-plates.md)

For a normal transition dipole of magnitude $D$, the [Fermi's golden rule](fermi-s-golden-rule.md), one-polarization mode density $\omega/(2\pi c^2d)$ and single-photon squared field $\hbar\omega/(2\epsilon_0V)$ give $A_\perp=D^2\omega_0^2/(2\epsilon_0\hbar c^2d)$. Dividing by the [free-space electric-dipole spontaneous emission rate](free-space-electric-dipole-spontaneous-emission-rate.md) gives the title. The factor three relative to the scalar density-of-states ratio comes from the isotropic free-space polarization average. This ideal [weak coupling](weak-coupling.md) formula assumes wide plates and unperturbed atomic structure, with atomic size much smaller than $d$.
