# Exergy

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic potential](thermodynamic-potential-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exergy)

For a [closed system](closed-system.md) with fixed composition in an environment at [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T_R>0$ and [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p_R$, define $A=U+p_RV-T_RS$, where $U$ is [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md), $V$ is [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) and $S$ is [entropy](entropy-split.md). The [exergy](exergy.md) is this [availability](exergy.md) relative to the equilibrium reference state, $A-A_{\mathrm{eq}}$. When the combined system and environment are isolated except for the specified exchanges, $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=-\Delta A/T_R$, so the [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) makes $A$ decrease toward its constrained minimum. Away from equilibrium, the environmental $T_R,p_R$ must not be replaced by the system's instantaneous values.
