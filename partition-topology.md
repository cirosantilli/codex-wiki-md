# Partition topology

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

Given a partition of a [set](set-split.md), declare a subset open precisely when it is a union of partition blocks. Arbitrary unions and finite intersections of such unions are again unions of blocks, giving a [topology](topology-split.md). The quotient map to the block set with its [discrete topology](discrete-space.md) pulls that topology back to the original set. For the partition of $\mathbb Z$ into its two parity classes, the only open sets are the empty set, those two classes, and all of $\mathbb Z$.
