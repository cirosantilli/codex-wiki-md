# Right ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

A right ideal $I$ in a unital [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) $A$ is a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) with $IA\subseteq I$, equivalently a [submodule](submodule.md) of the right regular [module](module-mathematics.md). It need not be a left [ideal](ideal.md). Its finite generation refers to right multiplication by elements of $A$.
