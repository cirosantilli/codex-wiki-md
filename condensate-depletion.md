# Condensate depletion

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensate](bose-einstein-condensate.md)

Occupation outside the macroscopically occupied condensate mode, caused by interactions or thermal excitations. A weak homogeneous three-dimensional Bose gas has zero-temperature depletion fraction $8\sqrt{na_s^3}/(3\sqrt\pi)$ in the leading dilute-gas approximation. Depletion measures single-particle mode occupation, not directly the [superfluid fraction](superfluid-fraction.md).
