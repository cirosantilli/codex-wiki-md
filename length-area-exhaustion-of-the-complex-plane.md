# Length-area exhaustion of the complex plane

↑ **Parent:** [Ahlfors covering surface theory](ahlfors-covering-surface-theory.md)

For a nonconstant [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) on the plane, let $S(R)$ be its normalized pulled-back spherical area on $|z|<R$, and $L(R)$ the spherical length of its boundary image counted with [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md). The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $L(R)^2\le8\pi^2R S'(R)$. If $L/S$ stayed above a positive constant for all large $R$, integration of $(1/S)'\le-c/R$ would make $1/S$ negative. Thus there are arbitrarily large radii with $L/S$ arbitrarily small, even when the total area is finite.
