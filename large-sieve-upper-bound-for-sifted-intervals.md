# Large sieve upper bound for sifted intervals

↑ **Parent:** [Large sieve](large-sieve.md)

For an interval set avoiding one residue at each sieving [prime](prime-number.md), the [Ramanujan sum](ramanujan-sum.md) at the forbidden [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) residue gives a linear combination of Fourier samples equal to $\mu(d)|S|$. [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives sample energy at least $|S|^2/\varphi(d)$ for each [squarefree](squarefree-integer.md) modulus. Summing these energies and applying the [analytic large sieve inequality](exponential-sum-large-sieve.md) gives the bound. If [primes](prime-number.md) dividing $q$ are excluded, restrict the denominator to $(d,q)=1$; it is uniformly at least a constant times $(\varphi(q)/q)\log(2Q)$.
