# Fourier representation of a Kronecker delta

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform.md)

Orthogonality of integer-frequency [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) gives this identity: the integral is one for $n=m$ and zero otherwise. It converts integer step-count constraints into products of generating functions, as in the [dilute-hopping lattice propagator](dilute-hopping-lattice-propagator.md).
