# Hofer metric from the spatial supremum norm

↑ **Parent:** [Hofer metric](hofer-metric.md)

Use compactly supported Hamiltonians on open manifolds and zero-mean Hamiltonians on closed manifolds. Because their values straddle zero, $\|H_t\|_\infty\leq\max H_t-\min H_t\leq2\|H_t\|_\infty$. Thus $\rho_\infty\leq d_H\leq2\rho_\infty$. These are two equivalent, but not identically normalized, conventions for [Hofer's metric](hofer-metric.md). A statement about zero distance or zero displacement energy is independent of this choice.
