# Bound current

↑ **Parent:** [Bound charge and bound current](bound-charge-and-bound-current.md)

The current associated with [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) and [magnetization](magnetization.md) in matter. If the magnetic response is neglected, it is $\mathbf J_b=\partial_t\mathbf P$. It is distinguished from a free conduction current in the [Maxwell equations in matter](maxwell-equations-in-matter.md).
