# Continuous-time Gaussian white noise

↑ **Parent:** [White noise](white-noise.md)

Continuous-time [Gaussian white noise](gaussian-white-noise.md) is a random distribution rather than an ordinary finite-variance function at every time. Integrating it against deterministic kernels gives [Gaussian random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md) with covariance $q\int f(s)g(s)ds$. For independent isotropic components, a three-dimensional dot covariance has amplitude $3q$. An ordinary rapidly decaying force covariance approximates this model only above its correlation time.
