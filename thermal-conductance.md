# Thermal conductance

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal resistance](thermal-resistance.md)

[Thermal conductance](thermal-conductance.md) is the [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) rate divided by the driving [temperature](temperature-split.md) difference in a linear contact model. It is the reciprocal of [thermal resistance](thermal-resistance.md), with units $\mathrm{W\,K^{-1}}$; thus $\dot Q=G(T_1-T_2)$ for [heat](heat-split.md) transferred from body 1 to body 2.
