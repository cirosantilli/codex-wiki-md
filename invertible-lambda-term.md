# Invertible lambda term

↑ **Parent:** [Composition of lambda terms](composition-of-lambda-terms.md)

A [lambda term](lambda-term.md) $p$ is invertible under [composition of lambda terms](composition-of-lambda-terms.md) if another [lambda term](lambda-term.md) $q$ satisfies both displayed equations in [beta-eta equivalence](beta-eta-equivalence.md), where $I$ is the [I combinator](i-combinator.md). An inverse on just one side does not by itself justify using the inverse equation on the other side.
