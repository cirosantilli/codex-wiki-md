# Normal dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Dispersion (optics)](dispersion-optics.md)

In [normal dispersion](normal-dispersion.md), the [refractive index](refractive-index.md) increases with frequency, $dn/d\omega>0$, equivalently $dn/d\lambda<0$ for vacuum wavelength $\lambda$. Transparent regions away from isolated absorption resonances often have this behaviour. Near a resonance the slope can reverse, producing [anomalous dispersion](anomalous-dispersion.md) accompanied by absorption.
