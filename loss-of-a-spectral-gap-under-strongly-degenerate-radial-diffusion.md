# Loss of a spectral gap under strongly degenerate radial diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md)

Choose a nonzero smooth bounded profile $\phi(s)$ supported in $0\leq s<1$, constant near zero, and set $R_\varepsilon(r)=\phi(r/\varepsilon)$. Its [radial diffusion Rayleigh quotient](radial-diffusion-rayleigh-quotient.md) is

$$
\mathcal R[R_\varepsilon]
=\varepsilon^{n-2}\frac{\int_0^1s^{n+1}\phi'(s)^2\,ds}{\int_0^1s\phi(s)^2\,ds}.
$$

For $n>2$ this tends to zero, proving that there is no positive [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md). Zero is not an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) with the outer [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md): zero energy would force $R'=0$ on $0<r<1$, hence $R=0$. Thus the bottom is not attained, and a lowest exponentially decaying [fundamental diffusion mode](fundamental-diffusion-mode.md) must not be assumed merely because the spatial region is bounded.
