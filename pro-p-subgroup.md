# Pro-p subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)

A closed [subgroup](subgroup.md) of a [profinite group](profinite-group.md) that is a [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) with the induced topology. Its images in finite continuous quotients are [p-groups](p-group.md).
