# Dedekind-infinite characterization

↑ **Parent:** [Dedekind-finite set](dedekind-finite-set.md)

In [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md), an injection $h:\omega\to X$ yields an injective nonsurjective self-map by shifting $h(n)$ to $h(n+1)$ and fixing the complement. Conversely, if an injection $g:X\to X$ omits $a$, the [natural-number recursion theorem](natural-number-recursion-theorem.md) defines $h(n)=g^n(a)$. Any repetition would, after cancellation, put $a$ in the image of $g$. Thus $h$ is injective. The argument uses no [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md), even when some infinite sets are [Dedekind-finite sets](dedekind-finite-set.md).
