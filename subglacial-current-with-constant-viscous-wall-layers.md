# Subglacial current with constant viscous wall layers

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity current](gravity-current.md)

In a water layer confined between horizontal rock and a movable constant-pressure ice roof, [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) and two wall stresses $\mu u/\delta_v$ give $u=-Dh h_x$, $q=-Dh^2h_x$ and $h_t=D(h^2h_x)_x$, with $D=\rho_wg\delta_v/(2\mu)>0$. This closure uses a constant [viscous boundary layer](viscous-boundary-layer.md) thickness, not a generic quadratic turbulent drag law.
