# Zariski coverage on finitely presented rings

↑ **Parent:** [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md)

On the opposite of the category of finitely presented unital [commutative rings](commutative-ring.md), a basic covering family is $A\to A[f_i^{-1}]$, where finitely many $f_i$ generate the [unit ideal](unit-ideal.md). Include the empty cover of the zero ring. Localization commutes with [base change](base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md), so these families define a [coverage on a category](coverage-on-a-category.md). Their [sheaf topos](grothendieck-topos.md) classifies the [coherent theory of local rings](coherent-theory-of-local-rings.md). This coverage is subcanonical: representable functors obey the usual localization gluing equations.
