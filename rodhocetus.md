# Rodhocetus

↑ **Parent:** [Protocetid](protocetid.md)

An [Eocene](eocene.md) whale whose ankle anatomy supplies evidence of artiodactyl relationships. A reconstructed tail fluke is not a directly preserved observation in its known skeletons.
