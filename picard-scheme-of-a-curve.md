# Picard scheme of a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Picard functor of a curve](picard-functor-of-a-curve.md)

For a smooth projective [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) of genus $g$, this smooth proper $g$-dimensional [scheme](scheme.md) represents degree-$d$ [line bundles](line-bundle.md) in families. Charts come from nonspecial degree-$g$ bundles: their first [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) vanishes, so the [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives exactly one independent [global section](global-section.md), whose zero [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) varies algebraically. Twisting by fixed [line bundles](line-bundle.md) gives charts covering every degree-$d$ class. For degree zero the [tensor product](tensor-product.md) gives the [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md); other components are its torsors.
