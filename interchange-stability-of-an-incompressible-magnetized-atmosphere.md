# Interchange stability of an incompressible magnetized atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible magnetic displacement energy identity](incompressible-magnetic-displacement-energy-identity.md)

For a horizontal field $B(z)e_x$ in constant downward gravity, equilibrium obeys $(p+B^2/(2\mu_0))_z=-\rho g$. The displacement energy reduces to the displayed [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md). Perturbations independent of the field direction do not bend [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md) and therefore have no [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) penalty: an increasing [mass density](density.md) with height permits instability. A nonincreasing [mass density](density.md) gives nonnegative energy for all admitted incompressible perturbations. Along-field variation adds [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) proportional to [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) squared, stabilizing sufficiently short waves when buoyancy is unfavorable.
