# Screened sine-Gordon energy

↑ **Parent:** [Direct method in the calculus of variations](direct-method-in-the-calculus-of-variations.md)

For real $f\in L^2(\mathbb R^3)$, this [energy functional](energy-functional.md) on $H^1(\mathbb R^3)$ is coercive and attains its minimum by the [direct method in the calculus of variations](direct-method-in-the-calculus-of-variations.md). The nonnegative potential term is weakly lower semicontinuous by [local Sobolev compactness gives lower semicontinuity of a nonnegative integral](local-sobolev-compactness-gives-lower-semicontinuity-of-a-nonnegative-integral.md). Its [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) is $-\Delta u+u+\sin u=f$ in the [weak solution](weak-solution.md) sense. The extra linear restoring term screens the [Sine-Gordon equation](sine-gordon-equation.md) nonlinearity. The scalar potential $V(s)=s^2/2+1-\cos s$ is convex because $V''(s)=1+\cos s\geq0$; hence any weak critical point is a minimizer. The [elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md) estimate for $-\Delta+1$, together with $|\sin u|\leq|u|$, gives $u\in H^2$. The [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md) then puts $u$ in $W^{1,6}$, so [Morrey's inequality](morrey-s-inequality.md) and [uniformly continuous integrable functions vanish at infinity](uniformly-continuous-integrable-functions-vanish-at-infinity.md) give a continuous representative tending to zero.
