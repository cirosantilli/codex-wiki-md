# Gauge-coupling sign in Ginzburg-Landau superconductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity.md)

For [energy](energy.md) with kinetic term $\hbar^2|D_\eta\psi|^2/(2m)$, $\eta=\pm1$, variation gives $-\hbar^2D_\eta^2\psi/(2m)+(\alpha+\beta|\psi|^2)\psi=0$ and current $j=\eta q\hbar\,\operatorname{Im}(\psi^*\nabla\psi)/m-q^2|\psi|^2A/m$. Thus changing the sign of the covariant [derivative](derivative.md) changes the phase-gradient term of the current. The displayed [energy](energy.md), field equation and current must use the same convention.
