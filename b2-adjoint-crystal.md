# B2 adjoint crystal

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal basis](crystal-basis.md)

With long-first [simple roots](simple-root.md) $\alpha_1=\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2$, $\alpha_2=\varepsilon_2$, the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) has [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2=2\Lambda_2$. Its [crystal basis](crystal-basis.md) has one vertex $x_\beta$ for every root and two distinct zero-weight vertices $z_1,z_2$. The color-one strings are $x_{\varepsilon_1}\to x_{\varepsilon_2}$, $x_{\alpha_1}\to z_1\to x_{-\alpha_1}$, and $x_{-\varepsilon_2}\to x_{-\varepsilon_1}$. The color-two strings are $x_{\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2}\to x_{\varepsilon_1}\to x_{\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2}$, $x_{\varepsilon_2}\to z_2\to x_{-\varepsilon_2}$, and $x_{-\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2}\to x_{-\varepsilon_1}\to x_{-\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2}$. All other edges vanish. One construction takes the component generated by $1\otimes2$ in the tensor square of the [crystal of the defining odd-orthogonal representation](crystal-of-the-defining-odd-orthogonal-representation.md); the [crystal tensor-product rule](crystal-tensor-product-rule.md) produces exactly these ten vertices. The equivariant isomorphism $\Lambda^2\mathbb C^5\cong\mathfrak{so}_5$ identifies its highest weight and representation.
