# Dimerized tight-binding chain

↑ **Parent:** [Peierls distortion](peierls-distortion.md)

For spin-degenerate [electrons](electron.md) at atomic spacing $a$, alternate the hopping amplitudes $-(t+\delta)$ and $-(t-\delta)$, with $t>0$ and $|\delta|<t$. The doubled [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md) gives an off-diagonal [Bloch Hamiltonian](bloch-hamiltonian.md) entry $-(t+\delta)-(t-\delta)e^{-2ika}$. Its two [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are the displayed [energy bands](energy-band.md), for $|k|\leq\pi/(2a)$. The [band gap](band-gap.md) at the edge of the [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md) is $4|\delta|$. At half filling there are two [electrons](electron.md) per doubled [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md), so the lower band is full and the upper empty. This explicit model realizes the period-doubling form of a [Peierls distortion](peierls-distortion.md).
