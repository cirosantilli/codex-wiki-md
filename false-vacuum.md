# False vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-field vacuum](scalar-field-vacuum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_vacuum)

A local minimum of the [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) that has higher energy than a global minimum. It can be stable against infinitesimal classical fluctuations but metastable to a transition to a lower-energy vacuum, including [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md). The [stability of a cubic-quartic scalar potential](stability-of-a-cubic-quartic-scalar-potential.md) gives an elementary example distinguishing local curvature from global stability.
