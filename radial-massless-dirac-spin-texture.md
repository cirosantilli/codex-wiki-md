# Radial massless Dirac spin texture

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-momentum locking](spin-momentum-locking.md)

For $H=c(p_x\sigma_x+p_y\sigma_y)$ with $c>0$ and physical [spin](spin.md) [matrices](matrix.md), $\langle\mathbf S\rangle_s=s(\hbar/2)\widehat{\mathbf p}$. The positive-energy branch is parallel and the negative-energy branch antiparallel to [momentum](momentum-split.md). Both are collinear; no preferred [spin](spin.md) direction is defined at zero [momentum](momentum-split.md).
