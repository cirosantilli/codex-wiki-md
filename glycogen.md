# Glycogen

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolism](metabolism-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glycogen)

[Glycogen](glycogen.md) is a highly branched [polysaccharide](polysaccharide.md) of [glucose](glucose.md) units, serving as a storage form of [glucose](glucose.md) in animals. Liver and muscle provide familiar examples. The classical [second messenger](second-messenger.md) experiments measured regulation of an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) involved in [glycogen](glycogen.md) breakdown, [glycogen phosphorylase](glycogen-phosphorylase.md). [Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e, 3.2: glycogen](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-2-carbohydrates)
