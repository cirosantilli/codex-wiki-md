# Comodule tensor transformation formula

↑ **Parent:** [Comodule](comodule.md)

A natural underlying transformation of tensor bifunctors on right [comodules](comodule.md) over a [field](field.md) has components $\varphi_{M,N}(u\otimes v)=\sum u_{(0)}\otimes v_{(0)}\gamma(u_{(1)},v_{(1)})$. Recover $\gamma=(\varepsilon\otimes\varepsilon)\varphi_{H,H}$; colinearity and monoidal axioms impose further equations.
