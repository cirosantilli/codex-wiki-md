# Tag SNP

↑ **Parent:** [Single-nucleotide polymorphism](single-nucleotide-polymorphism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tag_SNP)

A tag SNP represents other nearby [single-nucleotide polymorphisms](single-nucleotide-polymorphism.md) whose [alleles](allele.md) are strongly correlated through [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md). Tagging reduces the number of markers needed for [association mapping](association-mapping.md); it does not establish that the tag itself causes the phenotype.
