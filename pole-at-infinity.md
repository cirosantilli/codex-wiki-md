# Pole at infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Pole](pole.md)

A [function](function-split.md) [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) outside a [disk](disk-mathematics.md) has a [pole](pole.md) of positive integer order $N$ at infinity when its reciprocal-coordinate representative $f(1/w)$ has a [pole](pole.md) of that order at $w=0$. Equivalently $f(z)=z^Ng(1/z)$ with $g$ [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) and nonzero at zero. The [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) about infinity is interpreted in this coordinate: finitely many positive powers of $z$ form its principal part. For example, $z^Ne^{1/z}=z^N+z^{N-1}+\cdots$ has a pole of order $N$ at infinity despite its [essential singularity](essential-singularity.md) at zero.
