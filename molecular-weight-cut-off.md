# Molecular weight cut-off

↑ **Parent:** [Dialysis (biochemistry)](dialysis-biochemistry.md)

The [molecular weight cut-off](molecular-weight-cut-off.md) rates retention of reference [molecules](molecule-split.md). One manufacturer's definition uses the [mass](mass.md) at which approximately $90\%$ remains after seventeen-hour [dialysis](dialysis-biochemistry.md), tested with globular [proteins](protein-split.md). A $10\,\mathrm{kDa}$ rating uses [dalton](dalton-unit.md) units: a [mass](mass.md), not a length or an absolute passage threshold.[Tech Tip 20, p. 1, Factors Affecting Dialysis Rate: MWCO definition and globular reference molecules](dialysis-an-overview-pierce-biotechnology-technical-note.md)

A [semipermeable membrane](semipermeable-membrane.md) has a distribution of passage sizes, and a [molecule](molecule-split.md)'s shape affects passage as well as its [mass](mass.md). Near the nominal [MWCO](molecular-weight-cut-off.md), slow passage can make substantial retention compatible with eventual leakage. The [protein](protein-split.md)-retention experiment below makes retention an observable quantity rather than a universal yes-or-no property inferred from the label.
