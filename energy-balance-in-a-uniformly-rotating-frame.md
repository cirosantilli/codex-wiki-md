# Energy balance in a uniformly rotating frame

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)

For uniform [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega$, potential $\phi$, and additional [forces](force.md) doing no work in the rotating frame,

$$
E=\frac m2|v'|^2+\phi-\frac m2|\omega\times r|^2,
\qquad \dot E=(\partial_t\phi)_{r'}.
$$

The [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) does no work; the [centrifugal potential](centrifugal-potential.md) accounts for the remaining apparent [force](force.md). This energy is conserved precisely when the potential has no explicit time dependence in the rotating coordinates. An inertial time-independent [conservative force](conservative-force.md) need not meet that condition unless its potential is also invariant under the frame's rotation. The balance prevents applying a rotating-frame conservation law to a nonaxisymmetric stationary inertial potential.
