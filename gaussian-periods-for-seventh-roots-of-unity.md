# Gaussian periods for seventh roots of unity

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian period](gaussian-period.md)

With $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/7}$, put $\alpha=\zeta+\zeta^2+\zeta^4$ and $\beta=\zeta^3+\zeta^5+\zeta^6$. These [Gaussian periods](gaussian-period.md) obey $\beta=\overline\alpha$, $\alpha+\beta=-1$, and $\alpha^2=\alpha+2\beta$. Consequently $\alpha^2+\alpha+2=0$. The positive imaginary part of $\alpha$ fixes $\alpha=(-1+i\sqrt7)/2$ and $\beta=(-1-i\sqrt7)/2$. They are the nonidentity kernel values of the two degree-three [irreducible characters](irreducible-character.md) of the [nonabelian group of order 21](nonabelian-group-of-order-21.md).
