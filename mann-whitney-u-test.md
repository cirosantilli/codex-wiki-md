<h1 id="mann-whitney-u-test">Mann–Whitney U test</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nonparametric statistics](nonparametric-statistics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mann–Whitney_U_test)

For two independent samples of sizes $m,n$, rank their pooled observations and let $W_X$ be the sum of the ranks of the first sample. With continuous observations, the [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md) of a common distribution makes every allocation of $m$ ranks to that sample equally probable. A one-sided alternative that the first sample is stochastically smaller rejects for small $W_X$, equivalently small $U_X$. In a common-shape location model this tests a negative location difference. Without the common-shape assumption it is not simply a test of [medians](median.md). The shifted statistic $U_X$ counts cross-sample pairs for which the first observation exceeds the second. Ties use average ranks and a conditional [permutation test](permutation-test.md) or a tie-adjusted [normal approximation](normal-approximation.md).
