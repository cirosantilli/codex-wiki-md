# Hyperbolic motion from an initial event and rapidity

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic motion](hyperbolic-motion.md)

In units $c=1$, constant signed [proper acceleration](proper-acceleration.md) $a$ gives

$$
t-t_0=\frac{\sinh(\eta_0+a\tau)-\sinh\eta_0}{a},\qquad
x-x_0=\frac{\cosh(\eta_0+a\tau)-\cosh\eta_0}{a}.
$$

Position data determine $t_0,x_0$ but leave the initial [rapidity](rapidity.md) $\eta_0$ free. Proper-acceleration magnitude alone also leaves its orientation unspecified. The familiar centred [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) $x^2-t^2=a^{-2}$ follows for the additional initial data $t_0=0$, $x_0=1/a$, $\eta_0=0$ and [acceleration](acceleration.md) toward increasing $x$.
