# Formal pseudodifferential residue

↑ **Parent:** [Formal pseudodifferential operator](formal-pseudodifferential-operator.md)

The formal residue is the [coefficient](coefficient.md) of $\partial^{-1}$ after [coefficients](coefficient.md) have been moved to the left by the [formal pseudodifferential composition rule](formal-pseudodifferential-composition-rule.md). It is not an analytic [residue](residue.md) at a point. For scalar [coefficients](coefficient.md), the residue of a [commutator](commutator.md) is a total [derivative](derivative.md); integrating it with vanishing boundary terms produces the cyclic [Adler trace](adler-trace.md).
