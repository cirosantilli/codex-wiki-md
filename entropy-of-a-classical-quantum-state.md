# Entropy of a classical-quantum state

↑ **Parent:** [Classical-quantum state](classical-quantum-state.md)

The [classical-quantum state](classical-quantum-state.md) is block diagonal. If $\lambda_{xj}$ are the eigenvalues of $\rho_x$, its eigenvalues are $p_x\lambda_{xj}$. Inserting these into the definition of [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) gives the displayed sum of classical [Shannon entropy](information-entropy.md) and mean conditional quantum entropy. Its [quantum mutual information](quantum-mutual-information.md) is therefore the [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md) $S(\sum_xp_x\rho_x)-\sum_xp_xS(\rho_x)$.
