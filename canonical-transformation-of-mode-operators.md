# Canonical transformation of mode operators

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)

A linear change of [creation operators](creation-operator.md) and [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md) is canonical when it preserves their defining brackets. For finitely many bosonic modes, the [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) require $UU^\dagger-VV^\dagger=I$ and $UV^T-VU^T=0$. For fermions, the [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) instead require $UU^\dagger+VV^\dagger=I$ and $UV^T+VU^T=0$. A [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) uses such mixing to diagonalize a quadratic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) or relate different vacuum descriptions. Infinite-mode [unitary](unitary-connection.md) implementation has further conditions, as in [bosonic mode mixing implementability](bosonic-mode-mixing-implementability.md).
