# Gravitational lens

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_lens)

A gravitational lens is a foreground distribution of matter whose gravitational field deflects light from a background source. Its [projected surface mass density](projected-surface-mass-density.md) determines the mapping in the thin-lens approximation. The lens is the deflecting object; [gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing.md) is the resulting optical process.
