# Queens completion problem

↑ **Parent:** [N-queens problem](n-queens-problem.md)

Complete a prescribed subset of occupied cells to a solution of the [N-queens problem](n-queens-problem.md) while retaining every prescribed queen. In a row-variable [constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md), a clue $(r,c)$ gives the singleton [constraint domain](constraint-domain.md) $D_r=\{c\}$. Conflicting clues prove impossibility immediately, but nonattacking clues need not extend to a full solution. [Backtracking](backtracking.md) decides the finite problem; [hill climbing](hill-climbing.md) can search large candidate boards without an exhaustive guarantee.
