# BUGS

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian statistics](bayesian-statistics.md)

[BUGS](bugs.md) specifies a probabilistic model through stochastic nodes, deterministic nodes and observed data, then uses [Markov chain Monte Carlo](markov-chain-monte-carlo.md) to simulate its [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md). Its normal sampling notation uses a [precision parameter](precision-parameter.md) rather than a [variance](variance-split.md), and its gamma notation uses shape and rate.
