# Ronchi ruling

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument.md)

A [Ronchi ruling](ronchi-ruling.md) is a grating of alternating transparent and opaque parallel bands. A known band spacing can calibrate the scale of an [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) image.
