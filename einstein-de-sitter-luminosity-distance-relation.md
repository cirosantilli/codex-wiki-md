# Einstein-de Sitter luminosity-distance relation

↑ **Parent:** [Luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md)

In an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) normalized by $a_0=1$, the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $H(z)=H_0(1+z)^{3/2}$. Integrating the radial [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) gives $r(z)=c\int_0^z dz'/H(z')=2(c/H_0)[1-(1+z)^{-1/2}]$. The [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md) is $(1+z)r(z)$, yielding the displayed expression. Near the observer it is $(c/H_0)[z+z^2/4+O(z^3)]$; the limiting radial [comoving distance](comoving-radial-distance.md) is finite even though the [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md) diverges.
