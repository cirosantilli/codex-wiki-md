# Natural frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md)

A system's [natural frequency](natural-frequency.md) describes a free oscillation in the absence of external driving. Distinguish the undamped [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega_0$ from a damped oscillation frequency and from the driving frequency that maximizes a particular measured response.
