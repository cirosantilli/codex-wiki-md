# Constant-burn rocket with ground friction

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

For fuel mass rate $s>0$ and backward relative exhaust speed $u$, a rocket sliding on a horizontal plane has remaining mass $q(t)$ and satisfies the displayed [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) with [kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md). Integration over a sliding interval gives $v(t)-v(t_0)=u\log[q(t_0)/q(t)]-\mu g(t-t_0)$. From initial rest, immediate forward sliding additionally requires thrust to overcome [static friction](static-friction.md). If both friction coefficients equal $\mu$, motion starts only when $su>\mu gq(t)$. An algebraically negative sliding speed is not the physical solution at rest.
