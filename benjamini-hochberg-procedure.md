# Benjamini-Hochberg procedure

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple hypothesis testing](multiple-hypothesis-testing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamini-Hochberg_procedure)

Order $m$ p-values as $p_{(1)}\leq\cdots\leq p_{(m)}$ and take the largest $k$ with $p_{(k)}\leq\alpha k/m$. The Benjamini-Hochberg procedure rejects the hypotheses corresponding to $p_{(1)},\ldots,p_{(k)}$ and controls the [false discovery rate](false-discovery-rate.md) under independence and several forms of positive dependence.
