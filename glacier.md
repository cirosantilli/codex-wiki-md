# Glacier

↑ **Parent:** [Glaciology](glaciology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glacier)

A glacier is a persistent mass of land-based [ice](ice.md) that deforms and flows under gravity. [Ice accumulation](ice-accumulation.md), [ice ablation](ice-ablation.md), internal deformation and [basal sliding](basal-sliding.md) determine its geometry and motion.
