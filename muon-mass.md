# Muon mass

↑ **Parent:** [Muon](muon.md)

The rest [mass](mass.md) of the [muon](muon.md), approximately $105.66\,\mathrm{MeV}$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). Keeping it in [phase space](phase-space.md) is necessary in precision [lepton universality](lepton-universality.md) tests.
