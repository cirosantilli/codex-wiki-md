# Markov chain invariant measure

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain.md)

A nonnegative row vector $x$, not necessarily of total mass one, is an invariant measure for a [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) if $x_j=\sum_i x_ip_{ij}$. On an [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md), every nonzero invariant measure has positive coordinates: iterate stationarity and use a positive transition probability along a path.
