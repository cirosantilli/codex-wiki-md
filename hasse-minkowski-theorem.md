# Hasse-Minkowski theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic quadratic form](isotropic-quadratic-form.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse–Minkowski_theorem)

A nondegenerate [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) over a [number field](number-field.md) is isotropic if and only if it is isotropic over every completion. Over $\mathbb Q$, these are the real field and all [P-adic numbers](p-adic-number.md). It turns a rational existence problem into local tests; denominators can then be cleared for an integral homogeneous equation.
