# Xanthone

↑ **Parent:** [Ketone](ketone.md)

A dibenzo-gamma-[pyrone](pyrone.md) framework with two outer [benzene](benzene.md) rings, an endocyclic [ether](ether.md) [oxygen](oxygen.md) and an exocyclic [ketone](ketone.md) [oxygen](oxygen.md). The outer rings are strongly aromatic; the central ring has a more polarized, partial aromatic description.
