# Electron-positron annihilation into two photons

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)

The leading [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) is the sum of two [tree-level Feynman diagrams](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md), with the outgoing photons attached in either order to the fermion line. The [Dirac propagator](dirac-propagator.md) carries momentum $p-k_1$ or $p-k_2$ for incoming electron momentum $p$. Their sum obeys the [two-photon fermion Ward identity](two-photon-fermion-ward-identity.md), although individual diagrams need not. [Crossing symmetry](crossing-symmetry.md) relates this amplitude to [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md).
