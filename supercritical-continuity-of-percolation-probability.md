# Supercritical continuity of percolation probability

↑ **Parent:** [Right continuity of percolation probability](right-continuity-of-percolation-probability.md)

Independent [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md) and [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on the [cubic lattice](cubic-lattice.md) have continuous [percolation probability](percolation-probability.md) on $(p_c,1]$. Fix $p>p_c$ and an intermediate $q\in(p_c,p)$. By [uniqueness of the infinite percolation cluster](uniqueness-of-the-infinite-percolation-cluster.md), every origin in the infinite $p$-cluster has a finite $p$-open [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) to the infinite $q$-cluster. In the uniform-label [monotone coupling of Bernoulli percolation](monotone-coupling-of-bernoulli-percolation.md), the finitely many labels on this [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) are strictly below fixed $p$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), so it persists at some parameter below $p$. The [coupled onset parameter for origin percolation](coupled-onset-parameter-for-origin-percolation.md) has no attained atom there, giving left continuity, including at $1$. Combine this with [right continuity of percolation probability](right-continuity-of-percolation-probability.md).
