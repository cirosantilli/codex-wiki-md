# Mordukhovich criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Aubin property](aubin-property.md)

In finite dimensions, if the graph is locally closed at $(\bar u,\bar v)$, the [Aubin property](aubin-property.md) holds exactly when $D^*S(\bar u\mid\bar v)(0)=\{0\}$. Thus no nonzero horizontal vector in the [limiting normal cone](limiting-normal-cone.md) may occur. The exact local Lipschitz modulus is the outer norm of this [coderivative](limiting-coderivative.md).
