# Affine subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_subspace)

An affine subspace of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is a translate $v+W$ of a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $W$. Its dimension is the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of $W$.
