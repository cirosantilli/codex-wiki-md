# Hodge integration by parts in arbitrary degree

↑ **Parent:** [Codifferential](codifferential.md)

On a closed oriented [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), let $\alpha$ have degree $r-1$ and $\beta$ degree $r$. The [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) and [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) give $\int d\alpha\wedge*\beta=(-1)^r\int\alpha\wedge d*\beta$. The [codifferential](codifferential.md) convention $\delta\beta=(-1)^{n(r+1)+1}*d*\beta$, combined with the square of the [Hodge star](hodge-star-operator.md), gives $*\delta\beta=(-1)^rd*\beta$. This proves that the [codifferential](codifferential.md) is the [formal adjoint](formal-adjoint.md) of the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md). A boundary requires the corresponding boundary term or specified boundary conditions.
