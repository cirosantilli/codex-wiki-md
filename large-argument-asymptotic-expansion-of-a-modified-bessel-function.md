# Large-argument asymptotic expansion of a modified Bessel function

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Bessel function](modified-bessel-function.md)

For fixed real $\nu$ as $z\to+\infty$, the [Modified Bessel function of the first kind](modified-bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md) and [Modified Bessel function of the second kind](modified-bessel-function-of-the-second-kind.md) have [asymptotic expansions](asymptotic-expansion.md)

$$
I_\nu(z)\sim\frac{e^z}{\sqrt{2\pi z}}\sum_{j=0}^\infty\frac{(-1)^ja_j(\nu)}{z^j},\qquad
K_\nu(z)\sim\sqrt{\frac\pi{2z}}e^{-z}\sum_{j=0}^\infty\frac{a_j(\nu)}{z^j},\qquad
a_0=1,\quad a_{j+1}=\frac{4\nu^2-(2j+1)^2}{8(j+1)}a_j.
$$

The expansion and its error bounds establish actual growing and decaying solutions, beyond formal matching of a [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md). These formulas are documented in [NIST DLMF, equations 10.40.1–2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.40).
