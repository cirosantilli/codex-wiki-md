# Nuclear level reconstruction from energy differences

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma-cascade intensity balance](gamma-cascade-intensity-balance.md)

Reconstructing a nuclear gamma scheme amounts to assigning measured [photon](photon.md) energies to differences between ordered level energies. Cascade [energy](energy-split.md) sums constrain the graph, while population-flow balances constrain its orientation and feeding. Experimental central energies need not close exactly; a weighted least-squares energy-network fit uses their uncertainties consistently. Multiple graphs can satisfy the same spectrum when coincidence or lifetime information is absent.
