# Weather forecasting

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)

Weather forecasting estimates future meteorological conditions from observations and physical models. Numerical models use an approximate [initial condition](initial-condition.md) and equations of [fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md) to simulate future conditions; forecasters also compare model outputs and apply experience and research.[National Weather Service, The forecast process](https://www.weather.gov/rah/virtualtourforecast) The [forecast horizon](forecast-horizon.md) describes how far ahead a prediction remains useful at a specified accuracy; [sensitive dependence on initial conditions](butterfly-effect.md) can limit detailed predictions under [deterministic evolution](deterministic-evolution.md).
