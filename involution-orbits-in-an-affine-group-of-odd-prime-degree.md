# Involution orbits in an affine group of odd prime degree

↑ **Parent:** [Affine group over a finite field](affine-group-over-a-finite-field.md)

A nonidentity [involution](involution.md) in the [affine group over a finite field](affine-group-over-a-finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_\ell$, for odd prime $\ell$, has linear coefficient $-1$: a translation has order $\ell$, while $u^2=1$ forces $u=\pm1$. Its unique fixed point is $b/2$, and its remaining orbits have size two. This orbit count computes [primes in an intermediate field as double cosets](primes-in-an-intermediate-field-as-double-cosets.md) for an order-two [decomposition group](decomposition-group.md) acting on the radical roots.
