# Spectroscopic origin of the Pauli exclusion principle

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md)

In 1925, [Wolfgang Pauli](wolfgang-pauli.md) connected two puzzles: [alkali metal](alkali-metal.md) doublets and their [anomalous Zeeman effect](anomalous-zeeman-effect.md), and the capacities of closed [electron shells](electron-shell.md). His paper assigned the extra two-valued property to the optically active [electron](electron.md), rather than an angular momentum of the closed core. Its [Paschen-Back effect](paschen-back-effect.md) classification used four [quantum numbers](quantum-number.md); these historical labels should not be identified literally with modern $n,\ell,m_\ell,m_s$. The exclusion rule forbade matching all four. This connected resolved one-[electron](electron.md) states with closed-group populations. An additional test was the missing triplet counterpart of the equivalent two-$s$-[electron](electron.md) [ground state](ground-state.md) in [alkaline earth metals](alkaline-earth-metal.md). Pauli explicitly claimed counts of allowed states, not their [energies](energy-split.md) or ordering.[Pauli 1925, §§1–2: four labels, closed groups, exclusion statement and absent triplet s-term; English translation](https://www.chemteam.info/Chem-History/Pauli-1925/Pauli-1925.html)

The modern question is especially concrete: if a [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) has a lowest [one-particle state](one-particle-state.md), why cannot every [electron](electron.md) occupy it? Minimizing [energy](energy-split.md) answers which of the allowed [quantum states](quantum-state.md) is preferred; the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) determines which [quantum states](quantum-state.md) are allowed in the first place. [Spectral lines](spectral-line.md) then test [energy](energy-split.md) differences between allowed [multiparticle quantum states](multiparticle-quantum-state.md). A line count is not itself a direct count of occupied [spin orbitals](spin-orbital.md): transitions also depend on [selection rules](selection-rule.md) and the prepared [quantum state populations](quantum-state-population.md). The observed [anomalous Zeeman effect](anomalous-zeeman-effect.md) supplied constraints for the historical classification; the [two-particle antisymmetrization](two-particle-antisymmetrization.md) below supplies the modern mathematical explanation of exclusion.
