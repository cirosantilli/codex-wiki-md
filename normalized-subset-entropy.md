# Normalized subset entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Han's entropy inequality](han-s-entropy-inequality.md)

For a discrete [random vector](random-vector.md) with $n$ coordinates, this is the average [information entropy](information-entropy.md) per coordinate over all subsets of size $k$. Independent coordinates give the same average for every $k$, even when their individual entropies differ. If all coordinates are copies of one random variable $Z$, the average is $h(Z)/k$.
