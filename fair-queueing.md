# Fair queueing

↑ **Parent:** [Congestion control](congestion-control.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_queueing)

[Fair queueing](fair-queueing.md) separates traffic into queues and schedules their service to control sharing, reducing the ability of an unresponsive sender to dominate unrelated traffic merely by filling one common queue.
