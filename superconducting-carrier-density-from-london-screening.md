# Superconducting carrier density from London screening

↑ **Parent:** [London penetration depth](london-penetration-depth.md)

In a local [London equation](london-equations.md) description, the measured [London penetration depth](london-penetration-depth.md) satisfies $\lambda_L^2=m/(\mu_0n_se^2)$ when $n_s$ counts superconducting [electrons](electron.md) and $m$ is their [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md). Thus screening measures $n_s/m$. If instead one counts [Cooper pairs](cooper-pair.md), use pair [mass](mass.md) $2m$ and pair [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $-2e$, giving pair density $n_s/2$ for the same screening length. Identifying this superconducting carrier density with the total conduction-electron density requires the additional assumption that the relevant carriers all participate in screening at the measurement [temperature](temperature-split.md).
