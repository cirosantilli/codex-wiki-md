# Scale-invariant prior

↑ **Parent:** [Improper prior](improper-prior.md)

The measure $d\sigma/\sigma$ is invariant under multiplication by every positive constant. In the logarithmic coordinate it is [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). It cannot be normalized over $(0,\infty)$, although a bounded truncation is a [log-uniform distribution](log-uniform-distribution.md).
