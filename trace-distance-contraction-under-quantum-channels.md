# Trace-distance contraction under quantum channels

↑ **Parent:** [Trace distance](trace-distance.md)

By the [variational characterization of trace distance](variational-characterization-of-trace-distance.md), maximize $\operatorname{Tr}[P\mathcal E(\rho-\sigma)]$ over $0\leq P\leq I$. The adjoint of a [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) is positive and unital, so $0\leq\mathcal E^*(P)\leq I$. Moving the channel onto $P$ therefore restricts, rather than enlarges, the effects available in the input variational problem. This proves the displayed bound for every pair of [density operators](density-matrix.md).
