# Average monotonicity of pure-state entanglement entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement entropy](entanglement-entropy.md)

For a bipartite [pure state](pure-state.md), [entanglement entropy](entanglement-entropy.md) is the [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) of either [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md). When Alice makes a local measurement with pure conditional branches, Bob's reductions average to his original reduction. [Concavity of Von Neumann entropy](concavity-of-von-neumann-entropy.md) therefore gives $\sum_r p_rS(\rho_{B,r})\leq S(\rho_B)$. When Bob measures, apply concavity to Alice's reductions. In each pure branch the two reduced entropies coincide by the [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md). Refining measurement outcomes to individual [Kraus operators](kraus-operator.md) and iterating proves the inequality for arbitrary finite-round [LOCC](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md). Discarded local systems can be measured and their outcomes refined as well, so discarding does not evade the bound.
