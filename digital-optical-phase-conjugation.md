# Digital optical phase conjugation

↑ **Parent:** [Phase conjugation](phase-conjugation.md)

[Digital optical phase conjugation](digital-optical-phase-conjugation.md) uses a [wavefront sensor](wavefront-sensor.md) and a [spatial light modulator](spatial-light-modulator.md) to measure an incident field and launch a beam with the opposite spatial [wave phase](phase-waves.md). A phase-only [spatial light modulator](spatial-light-modulator.md) illuminated uniformly generally does not reproduce the incident [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) as well; exact $\gamma U^*$ requires the appropriate [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) too. The experimentally useful question is how much of the original optical mode can be recovered with the measured and controlled field components.

In 2010, [Meng Cui](meng-cui.md) and [Changhuei Yang](changhuei-yang.md) demonstrated [digital optical phase conjugation](digital-optical-phase-conjugation.md) through a prepared [multiple scattering](multiple-scattering.md) sample. They dried mixed-size [polystyrene](polystyrene.md) microspheres on cover glass, added immersion oil, and sealed them with another cover glass. A 532 nm [laser](laser-split.md) supplied signal and reference beams. The signal crossed the sample between two objectives; the reference interfered with it on a [charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md). Phase-stepped [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) recovered the spatial [wave phase](phase-waves.md); a calibrated [spatial light modulator](spatial-light-modulator.md) imposed its negative on the reference-derived return beam. The reference supplied playback power, without nonlinear mixing pumps. An [optical camera](optical-camera.md) read out the recovered signal. With the same sample and a zero-phase [spatial light modulator](spatial-light-modulator.md) control, the return instead produced a [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md) pattern. This comparison supports mode recovery by spatial [wave phase](phase-waves.md) control, not merely increased illumination. A separate ordinary-mirror control tested source displacements. Finite sampling and collection limited recovery; the system controlled [wave phase](phase-waves.md) rather than the complete vector field. These restrictions distinguish demonstrated reconstruction from exact reversal of every scattered component.[Cui and Yang, 2010, §§2–3, Figs. 1–4; §4.2, Fig. 6(e–f); §§5.1–5.2, Fig. 7(a–c), printed pp. 3446–3453](implementation-of-a-digital-optical-phase-conjugation-system-and-its-application-to-study-the-robustness-of-turbidity-suppression-by-phase-conjugation-by-meng-cui-and-changhuei-yang.md)
