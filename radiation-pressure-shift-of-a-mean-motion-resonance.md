# Radiation-pressure shift of a mean-motion resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior mean-motion resonance](exterior-mean-motion-resonance.md)

A grain with [radiation-pressure coefficient](radiation-pressure-coefficient.md) $\beta<1$ feels effective central [gravitational parameter](standard-gravitational-parameter.md) $GM_\star(1-\beta)$. Combining its [mean motion](mean-motion.md) with the planet's [mean motion](mean-motion.md) at an exterior-type $(j+1):j$ frequency commensurability gives the displayed [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md). The commensurability lies geometrically exterior only for $j<\sqrt{1-\beta}/(1-\sqrt{1-\beta})$. Radiation changes the grain's attraction, so the same frequency ratio need not imply the usual radial ordering.
