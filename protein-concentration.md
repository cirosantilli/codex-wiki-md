# Protein concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration](concentration.md)

[Protein concentration](protein-concentration.md) specifies protein amount per sample volume, commonly as mass concentration or molar concentration. A molar value must state the counted entity, such as chains or oligomeric particles, and quantification must account for contaminants and optical properties.
