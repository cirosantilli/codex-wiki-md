# Reinsurance retention

↑ **Parent:** [Reinsurance](reinsurance.md)

A threshold limiting the insurer's retained payment under a [reinsurance](reinsurance.md) contract. Under [excess of loss reinsurance](excess-of-loss-reinsurance.md) it applies to each [claim size](claim-size.md); under [aggregate stop loss reinsurance](aggregate-stop-loss-reinsurance.md) it applies to the total loss over the stated period. These contracts therefore cap different quantities even when their thresholds have the same numerical value.
