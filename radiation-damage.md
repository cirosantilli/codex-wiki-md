# Radiation damage

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement-split.md)

[radiation damage](radiation-damage.md) is a radiation-induced change in a specimen. In biological [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md), accumulated exposure can alter chemical groups and remove high-resolution information, so recording more electrons has competing signal and damage effects.
