# TT coordinate separation and measured separation

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse-traceless gauge](transverse-traceless-gauge.md)

In [transverse-traceless gauge](transverse-traceless-gauge.md), particles initially at rest have fixed spatial coordinates because $\Gamma^\mu{}_{00}=0$. Their separation measured in a [parallel-propagated orthonormal frame](parallel-propagated-orthonormal-frame.md) changes: to first order $S^{\hat A}=S^A+h_{AB}S^B/2$. Hence the physical [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md) is $\ddot\xi^{\hat A}=\ddot h_{AB}L^B/2$, whereas the coordinate displacement obeys $\ddot\xi^A_{\rm coord}=0$. The extra $\partial_0\Gamma^A{}_{0B}L^B$ term in the coordinate expansion of the second [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) reconciles the two results. This applies to infinitesimal separations and linear [metric perturbations](linearized-gravity.md).
