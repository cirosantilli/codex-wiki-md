# Transverse electrostatic field inside a dielectric cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric](dielectric.md)

For an infinitely long circular [dielectric](dielectric.md) cylinder in a uniform transverse field in vacuum, the inside [electrostatic potential](electric-potential.md) is linear in the transverse coordinate. Matching the tangential [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and normal [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) to the outside dipolar solution gives the displayed uniform internal field. The result assumes no free interface charge and neglects end effects; an exterior [dielectric](dielectric.md) changes the [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) ratio.
