# Effective proton-proton reaction network

↑ **Parent:** [Proton-proton chain branches](proton-proton-chain-branches.md)

An effective [Proton–proton chain](proton-proton-chain.md) network can track [protons](proton.md), [deuterium](deuterium.md), [helium-3](helium-3.md), [helium-4](helium-4.md) and lithium-7 after eliminating a rapidly adjusting beryllium intermediate. Identical-reactant event rates include a factor one half. Stoichiometric changes must count both [helium-4](helium-4.md) products of lithium-7 [proton](proton.md) capture. The weighted baryon [mass density](density.md) $n_1+2n_2+3n_3+4n_4+7n_7$ is conserved by the nuclear reactions at fixed volume.
