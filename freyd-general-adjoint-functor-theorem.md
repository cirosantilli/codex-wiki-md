# Freyd general adjoint functor theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint functors](adjoint-functors.md)

For a [functor](functor.md) $U:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ between [locally small categories](locally-small-category.md), with $\mathcal C$ a [complete category](complete-category.md), $U$ has a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) if and only if it preserves small [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) and satisfies the [solution-set condition](solution-set-condition.md). The dual statement uses cocompleteness, small-colimit preservation and weakly terminal solution sets to characterize existence of a [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md).
