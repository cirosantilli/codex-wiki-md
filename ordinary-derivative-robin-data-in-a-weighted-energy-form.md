# Ordinary-derivative Robin data in a weighted energy form

↑ **Parent:** [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md)

For $-(pu')'=f$, $u(b)=0$ and $u'(c)+\kappa u(c)=0$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives the displayed endpoint coefficient. Ordinary derivative data and conormal data $p(c)u'(c)+\kappa u(c)=0$ are different boundary conditions. If $p$ is bounded above and below by positive constants and $\kappa\geq0$, the [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) on functions vanishing at $b$ proves continuity and coercivity. The symmetric form defines an [energy norm](energy-norm.md), and [Galerkin orthogonality](galerkin-orthogonality.md) gives exact best approximation in that norm.
