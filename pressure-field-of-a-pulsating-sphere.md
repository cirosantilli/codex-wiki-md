# Pressure field of a pulsating sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Spherically symmetric incompressible radial flow](spherically-symmetric-incompressible-radial-flow.md)

In an unbounded [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) with [spherical symmetry](spherical-symmetry.md), the [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) on a sphere of radius $R(t)$ gives $u_r=R^2\dot R/r^2$ and [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) $\phi=G(t)-R^2\dot R/r$. The [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md) at infinity has $F(t)=\dot G+p_\infty/\rho$. Subtracting it from the finite-radius equation gives the displayed [pressure](pressure-split.md), independently of $G$. Time differentiation is at fixed $r$. Evaluating at $r=R(t)$ yields the [Rayleigh equation for an inviscid spherical bubble](rayleigh-equation-for-an-inviscid-spherical-bubble.md) when the boundary [pressure](pressure-split.md) is prescribed.
