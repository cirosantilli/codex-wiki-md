# Linear drag

↑ **Parent:** [Drag (physics)](drag-physics.md)

Linear drag opposes the [velocity](velocity.md) relative to the surrounding medium with magnitude proportional to its [speed](speed.md), where $\alpha>0$. Its [power](power.md) is $\mathbf F_d\cdot\mathbf v=-\alpha|\mathbf v|^2$, so it dissipates [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). Under a constant external [force](force-split.md), the resulting [linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) relaxes exponentially on the [time](time-in-physics.md) scale $m/\alpha$.
