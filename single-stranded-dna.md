# Single-stranded DNA

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

[single-stranded DNA](single-stranded-dna.md) is [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) present as one polymer strand rather than an ordinary paired duplex. It can form secondary structures or associate with [proteins](protein-split.md); single-stranded does not imply an entirely straight, unstructured chain.
