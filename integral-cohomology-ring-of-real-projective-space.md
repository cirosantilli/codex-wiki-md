# Integral cohomology ring of real projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md)

For $m\geq0$, the [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md) is

$$
H^*(\mathbb{RP}^{2m};\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z[a]/(2a,a^{m+1}),\qquad |a|=2,
$$

and

$$
H^*(\mathbb{RP}^{2m+1};\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z[a,b]/(2a,a^{m+1},ab,b^2),\qquad |a|=2,\quad |b|=2m+1.
$$

The generator $a$ reduces to the square of the degree-one generator in the [mod-two cohomology ring of real projective space](mod-two-cohomology-ring-of-real-projective-space.md); $b$ is the integral [orientation class](fundamental-class.md) in odd top dimension.
