# Iterated collinear boosts with equal rapidity

↑ **Parent:** [Rapidity](rapidity.md)

The [relativistic velocity-addition formula](velocity-addition-formula.md) becomes addition of [rapidities](rapidity.md) for collinear [Lorentz boosts](lorentz-boost.md). Starting from rest, applying the same positive rest-frame [velocity](velocity.md) increment $c\tanh\alpha$ repeatedly gives $u_n=c\tanh(n\alpha)$. For increment $c/2$, $\alpha=\tfrac12\log3$ and $u_n=c(3^n-1)/(3^n+1)$. Every finite sequence remains below the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md).
