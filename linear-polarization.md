# Linear polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md)

A wave is linearly polarized when its oscillating [electric field](electric-field-split.md) always lies along one fixed line. For a transverse [plane wave](plane-wave.md), that line is perpendicular to the [wavevector](wavevector.md). Unlike [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md), the field oscillates along the line rather than rotating through directions.
