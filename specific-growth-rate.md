# Specific growth rate

↑ **Parent:** [Monod equation](monod-equation.md)

The [specific growth rate](specific-growth-rate.md) is biomass production per unit existing biomass per unit time. For [biomass concentration](biomass-concentration.md) $x>0$ with growth alone, $\dot x=\mu x$ and $\mu=\mathrm{d}(\ln x)/\mathrm{d}t$. A constant positive [specific growth rate](specific-growth-rate.md) produces [exponential growth](exponential-growth.md), $x(t)=x(0)e^{\mu t}$, with doubling time $\ln2/\mu$. In a [chemostat](chemostat.md), removal makes the net rate $\dot x/x=\mu(s)-D$, rather than $\mu(s)$ itself.
