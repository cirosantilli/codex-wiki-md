# Holomorphic cotangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic tangent bundle](holomorphic-tangent-bundle.md)

The dual of the [holomorphic tangent bundle](holomorphic-tangent-bundle.md) is a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) with local frames $dz^1,\ldots,dz^n$. Coordinate changes have holomorphic invertible Jacobian matrices, so these frames have holomorphic transition functions. Its [holomorphic sections](holomorphic-section.md) are exactly the [holomorphic one-forms](holomorphic-one-form.md).
