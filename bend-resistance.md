# Bend resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Ballistic electron transport](ballistic-electron-transport.md)

A four-terminal [bend resistance](bend-resistance.md) uses adjacent current contacts and the other adjacent pair as [voltage](voltage.md) probes. It can be negative when a ballistic injector preferentially populates the opposite [voltage](voltage.md) probe. Its sign is a nonlocal routing property, not the sign of the total electrical dissipation.
