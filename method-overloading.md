# Method overloading

↑ **Parent:** [Object-oriented programming](object-oriented-programming.md)

Several callable methods share one name but differ in parameter signatures. In ordinary [Scala](scala.md) calls, the [compiler](compiler.md) chooses among applicable alternatives using the receiver and arguments' [static types](static-type.md), preferring a uniquely most-specific applicable alternative. Overloading is therefore a different step from [method overriding](method-overriding.md) and receiver-based [dynamic dispatch](dynamic-dispatch.md).
