# Thermal-continuum fitting of an accretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion-disk tests of strong-field gravity](accretion-disk-tests-of-strong-field-gravity.md)

A geometrically thin, optically thick disk has a radially varying effective temperature. Relativistic transport and an atmosphere model convert its thermal spectrum into an inferred emitting area and inner radius. Black-hole [mass](mass.md), distance, inclination and spectral hardening are important external constraints. Selecting thermal-state observations with moderate [luminosity](luminosity.md) limits contamination and the breakdown of the thin-disk approximation. Relating the fitted inner radius to the [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md) remains a physical modeling assumption.
