# Equivariant dynamical system

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

An equivariant dynamical system has a [vector field](vector-field.md) commuting with the action of a symmetry group. Its [flow map](flow-map.md) preserves every subgroup's fixed-point subspace, because equivariance and uniqueness keep initial states fixed by that subgroup. Polynomial [normal forms](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md) are constrained by the weights and parity of the [group representation](group-representation.md).
