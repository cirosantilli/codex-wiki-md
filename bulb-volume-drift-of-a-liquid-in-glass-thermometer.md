# Bulb-volume drift of a liquid-in-glass thermometer

↑ **Parent:** [Liquid-in-glass thermometer](liquid-in-glass-thermometer.md)

A bulb-volume change shifts the indicated [temperature](temperature-split.md). Contraction raises the liquid column at a fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md); expansion lowers it. Permanent and temporary changes require distinction when interpreting an ice-point check.[NIST SP 1088, p. 4 and §§7.4–7.5](maintenance-and-validation-of-liquid-in-glass-thermometers-by-christina-d-cross-and-others.md)
