# Coordinate-integral primitive on a polydisc

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic Poincaré lemma](holomorphic-poincare-lemma.md)

For a closed [holomorphic one-form](holomorphic-one-form.md) $\sum b_kdz_k$ on a polydisc, the displayed coordinate-path integral is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md). Differentiating in $z_j$ gives the $k=j$ endpoint term; for $k>j$ use $\partial_jb_k=\partial_kb_j$ and integrate that derivative. The resulting sum telescopes to $b_j(z)$. Thus $\partial F=\sum b_kdz_k$, giving an explicit degree-one proof of the [holomorphic Poincaré lemma](holomorphic-poincare-lemma.md).
