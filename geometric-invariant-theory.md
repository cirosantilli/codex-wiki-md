# Geometric invariant theory

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant theory](invariant-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_invariant_theory)

[Geometric invariant theory](geometric-invariant-theory.md) constructs algebraic quotients of [group actions](group-action.md) by a [reductive algebraic group](reductive-group.md) using invariant sections and a chosen [linearization of a line bundle](linearization-of-a-line-bundle.md). An affine quotient is the spectrum of the [polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md); a projective quotient uses homogeneous invariants and excludes the points where all positive-degree invariants vanish. Stability separates the orbits which give well-behaved [geometric quotient](geometric-quotient.md) points.
