# Nonaccreting similarity disk supplied by an inner torque

↑ **Parent:** [Compact self-similar disk with density-linear viscosity](compact-self-similar-disk-with-density-linear-viscosity.md)

The $a=5/4$ [compact self-similar disk with density-linear viscosity](compact-self-similar-disk-with-density-linear-viscosity.md) has outward drift $r/(2t)$ but zero limiting mass flux at the origin. Its [viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) has a positive finite inner limit for $t>0$, equal to $dJ/dt$. Thus disk mass conservation coexists with growth of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md); an external central torque supplies that growth.
