# Hull-White model

↑ **Parent:** [Short rate](short-rate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hull-White_model)

The [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) [Hull-White model](hull-white-model.md) has pricing dynamics $dr_t=(\beta(t)-ar_t)dt+\eta dW_t^Q$. Its deterministic drift function can fit the initial [yield curve](yield-curve.md) exactly, while its [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md) part controls stochastic variation. Like the [Vasicek model](vasicek-model.md), it permits negative rates.
