# Star-subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Star-algebra](star-algebra.md)

A linear [subalgebra](subalgebra.md) closed under the involution. In [bounded operators](continuous-linear-operator.md), this means [closure](closure-topology.md) under the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md); neither [norm](norm.md) [closure](closure-topology.md) nor operator-topology [closure](closure-topology.md) is part of the definition. These distinctions matter in the [Von Neumann double commutant theorem](von-neumann-double-commutant-theorem.md) and the [Kaplansky density theorem](kaplansky-density-theorem.md).
