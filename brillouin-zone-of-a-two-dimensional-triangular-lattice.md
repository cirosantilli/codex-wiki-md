# Brillouin zone of a two-dimensional triangular lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md)

The reciprocal lattice of a two-dimensional triangular [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) is triangular, so its [Wigner-Seitz cell](wigner-seitz-cell.md) is a regular hexagon. Opposite boundary points are identified modulo reciprocal-lattice translations, and the six corners fall into two inequivalent classes of three corners each, conventionally denoted $K$ and $K'$.
