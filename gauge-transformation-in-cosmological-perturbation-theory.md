# Gauge transformation in cosmological perturbation theory

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological perturbation theory](linear-cosmological-perturbation-theory-split.md)

A cosmological gauge transformation changes the identification of a perturbed spacetime with its homogeneous background. Individual field perturbations can change without changing physical observables. At first order the metric change is the [linear metric gauge-transformation law](linear-metric-gauge-transformation-law.md); matter scalars obey the corresponding background-shift rule.
