# Peculiar gravitational potential

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological density perturbation](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md)

The peculiar gravitational potential is the Newtonian potential after subtracting the potential of the homogeneous expanding background. In comoving coordinates it obeys a Poisson equation sourced by the [density contrast](density-contrast.md), so its gradient accelerates matter relative to the [Hubble flow](hubble-flow.md).
