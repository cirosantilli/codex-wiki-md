# Salt bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemistry](electrochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_bridge)

A [salt bridge](salt-bridge.md) is an ionic connection between [electrolyte](electrolyte.md) compartments that limits bulk mixing while permitting [electric charge](electric-charge.md) transport. Its mobile [ions](ion.md) compensate changes caused by the [electrode](electrode.md) [half-reactions](half-reaction.md), keeping the bulk [chemical solutions](solution-chemistry.md) approximately electrically neutral during sustained [electric current](electric-current.md). It does not conduct [Electrons](electron.md) like the external wire, and its [ions](ion.md) need not be consumed at either [electrode](electrode.md). The ionic return path and compensating ion flows are explained in [Purdue University, Electrochemical Reactions: salt bridge and charge balance](https://chemed.chem.purdue.edu/genchem/topicreview/bp/ch20/electro.php).

In a discharging [Daniell cell](daniell-cell.md), [anions](anion.md) moving toward the [anode](anode.md) compartment and [cations](cation.md) moving toward the [cathode](cathode.md) compartment both contribute to the internal [conventional current](conventional-current.md) from [anode](anode.md) toward [cathode](cathode.md). This charge-balance role is compatible with a negative [anode](anode.md): terminal signs do not imply that the whole [anode](anode.md) [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) is negatively charged. The simple compensating-inventory example in [Daniell cell](daniell-cell.md) does not fix all individual [ion](ion.md) fluxes; [diffusion](diffusion.md) and the cell's other mobile [ions](ion.md) can contribute. A real bridge also introduces [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) and may introduce a junction [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md), so its presence does not guarantee an exact thermodynamic cell reading. See [IUPAC Gold Book: salt bridge and liquid-junction potential](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/09080).
