# Finite-Green-energy measure pairing with a Gaussian free field

↑ **Parent:** [Green-kernel expansion in the Dirichlet space](green-kernel-expansion-in-the-dirichlet-space.md)

If a measure $\rho$ has finite [Green energy](green-energy.md), then

$$
(h,\rho)=\sum_{n\geq1}\xi_n\int_De_n\,d\rho
$$

converges in $L^2$ for any [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $(e_n)$ of $H_0^1(D)$ and independent standard normal coefficients $\xi_n$. The limit is centered Gaussian with variance equal to the Green energy of $\rho$ and does not depend on the chosen basis.
