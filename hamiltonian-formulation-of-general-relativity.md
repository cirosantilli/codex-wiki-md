# Hamiltonian formulation of general relativity

↑ **Parent:** [3+1 decomposition of spacetime](3-plus-1-decomposition-of-spacetime.md)

Using the [lapse function](lapse-function.md) $N$, [shift vector](shift-vector.md) $N^i$ and spatial metric $h_{ij}$, the [Legendre transform in mechanics](legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) of the [Einstein-Hilbert action](einstein-hilbert-action.md) gives, in units $16\pi G=1$,

$$
H_{\rm bulk}=\int d^3x\sqrt h\,(N\mathcal H+N^i\mathcal H_i),\qquad\mathcal H=\frac{\pi^{ij}\pi_{ij}-\pi^2/2}{h}-{}^{(3)}R,\qquad\mathcal H_i=-2D_j\left(\frac{\pi^j{}_i}{\sqrt h}\right).
$$

Here $\pi=h_{ij}\pi^{ij}$ and $\pi^{ij}$ is the [canonical momentum of the spatial metric](canonical-momentum-of-the-spatial-metric.md). Varying lapse and shift imposes the [Hamiltonian constraint](hamiltonian-constraint.md) and [momentum constraint](momentum-constraint.md). An asymptotic time translation requires an [Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy](arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md) boundary term. The bulk constraints generate gauge evolution; the nonzero asymptotic energy comes from the boundary.
