# Extremal

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus of variations](calculus-of-variations-split.md)

A sufficiently regular admissible [function](function-split.md) at which the [first variation](first-variation.md) of a [functional](functional.md) vanishes for every permitted [variation](variation.md). An [extremal](extremal.md) is a stationary candidate; it need not be a local maximum or minimum. For a first-order integral [functional](functional.md) with fixed endpoint values, it satisfies the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) under the usual regularity assumptions.
