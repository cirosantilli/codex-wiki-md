# Scale-free Kuzmin superposition

↑ **Parent:** [Kuzmin reflection method](kuzmin-reflection-method.md)

For $B>0$ and $0<\beta<1$, the unreferenced [relative potential](relative-potential.md) of the [Kuzmin reflection method](kuzmin-reflection-method.md) diverges at large $b$. Referencing each component to the origin before integrating gives the finite potential difference

$$
\psi(R,z)=GB\int_0^\infty b^\beta\left[\frac1{\sqrt{R^2+(|z|+b)^2}}-\frac1b\right]db.
$$

At small $b$ the subtraction is integrable because $\beta>0$; at large $b$ the difference is $O(b^{-2})$, integrable because $\beta<1$. Its plane force is $\partial_R\psi=-CR^{\beta-1}$, where $C=(GB/2)B((1+\beta)/2,1-\beta/2)$. A [circular speed](circular-speed.md) $V=KR^{\beta/2}$ fixes $C=K^2$, giving

$$
\Sigma(R)=\frac{K^2R^{\beta-1}}{2\pi G}\frac{\Gamma(1+\beta/2)\Gamma((1-\beta)/2)}{\Gamma((1+\beta)/2)\Gamma(1-\beta/2)}.
$$

The [beta function](beta-function.md) substitutions for the force and [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) have different exponents. Its central mass is locally finite, but its total mass diverges at large radius; a potential zero at infinity cannot be imposed. The force and density approach those of the [Mestel disk](mestel-disk.md) as $\beta\downarrow0$.
