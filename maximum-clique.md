# Maximum clique

↑ **Parent:** [Clique (graph theory)](clique-graph-theory.md)

A [maximum clique](maximum-clique.md) is a [clique](clique-graph-theory.md) with the greatest possible number of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in the [graph](graph-split.md). Its size is the [clique number](clique-number.md). Every [maximum clique](maximum-clique.md) is a [maximal clique](maximal-clique.md), since adding a [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) would produce a larger [clique](clique-graph-theory.md). A [graph](graph-split.md) can have several [maximum cliques](maximum-clique.md); the word maximum specifies their common size, rather than uniqueness.
