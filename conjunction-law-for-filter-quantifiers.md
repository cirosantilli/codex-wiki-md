# Conjunction law for filter quantifiers

↑ **Parent:** [Filter quantifier](filter-quantifier.md)

The [filter quantifier](filter-quantifier.md) satisfies $\forall_{\mathcal F}(p\wedge q)$ if and only if both $\forall_{\mathcal F}p$ and $\forall_{\mathcal F}q$. Finite-intersection closure gives one direction and upward closure gives the other. Properness ensures contradictory truth sets cannot both be filter members.
