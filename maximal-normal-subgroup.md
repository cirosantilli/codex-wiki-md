# Maximal normal subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)

A proper [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) maximal among proper normal subgroups. Its quotient is a nontrivial [simple group](simple-group.md). Maximality among normal subgroups does not imply maximality among all subgroups.
