# Centralizer of a subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

For a subalgebra $B$ of an [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) $A$, its centralizer is

$$
C_A(B)=\{a\in A:ab=ba\text{ for every }b\in B\}.
$$

It is a subalgebra of $A$. When $A$ is a [group algebra](group-algebra.md) and $B$ is the group algebra of a [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$, its elements are exactly the linear combinations with coefficients constant on the conjugation orbits of $H$.
