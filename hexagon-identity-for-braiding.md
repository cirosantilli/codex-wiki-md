# Hexagon identity for braiding

↑ **Parent:** [Braiding](braiding.md)

The compatibility equations between [braiding](braiding.md) and the [associator](associator.md) in a [braided monoidal category](braided-monoidal-category.md). Omitting the [associators](associator.md) from the notation, they read $c_{a,b\otimes c}=(1_b\otimes c_{a,c})(c_{a,b}\otimes1_c)$ and $c_{a\otimes b,c}=(c_{a,c}\otimes1_b)(1_a\otimes c_{b,c})$. Thus exchanging a composite with another object is the sequence of exchanges of its factors.
