# Procellarum KREEP Terrane

↑ **Parent:** [Lunar crustal dichotomy](lunar-crustal-dichotomy.md)

A nearside lunar geochemical region enriched in [KREEP](kreep.md)-associated material and heat-producing elements. Its distribution offers a constraint on the [Moon](moon.md)'s chemical and thermal evolution. Enrichment can help sustain melting but does not, by itself, identify the process that first made the hemispheric crustal contrast.
