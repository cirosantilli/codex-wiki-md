# Nonexponential band transmission

↑ **Parent:** [Beer-Lambert law](beer-lambert-law.md)

A finite-band [transmittance](transmittance.md) $\overline{\mathfrak T}=\langle e^{-m\kappa_\nu}\rangle$ averages different monochromatic attenuation rates. Its effective attenuation $a_{\rm eff}=-d\log\overline{\mathfrak T}/dm$ obeys $a_{\rm eff}'=-\operatorname{Var}_m(\kappa)\le0$, where the weighting is proportional to the transmitted spectrum. Strongly absorbing parts saturate first, so surviving radiation samples weaker absorption. [Jensen's inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) gives $\overline{\mathfrak T}\ge e^{-m\langle\kappa\rangle}$. Equality throughout requires a spectrally constant coefficient.
