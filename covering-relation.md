# Covering relation

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covering_relation)

In a [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md), $y$ covers $x$ when $x<y$ and there is no $z$ with $x<z<y$. Thus a [cover relation](covering-relation.md) records an immediate increase, rather than every comparable pair. For a finite [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md), every strict comparison can be obtained by chaining [cover relations](covering-relation.md).
