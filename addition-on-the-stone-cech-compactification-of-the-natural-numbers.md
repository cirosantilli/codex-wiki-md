<h1 id="addition-on-the-stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers">Addition on the Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers](stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers.md)

For ultrafilters $\mathcal U,\mathcal V\in\beta\mathbb N$, define $\mathcal U+\mathcal V$ by

$$
A\in\mathcal U+\mathcal V
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\{x:\{y:x+y\in A\}\in\mathcal V\}\in\mathcal U.
$$

This operation is [associative](associative-operation.md), and each right translation $\mathcal U\mapsto\mathcal U+\mathcal V$ is continuous, so $\beta\mathbb N$ is a compact Hausdorff left-topological [semigroup](semigroup.md).
