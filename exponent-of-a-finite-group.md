# Exponent of a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Group theory](group-theory-split.md)

The exponent of a finite group is the least common multiple of the orders of all its elements.

Every [finite group](finite-group.md) is a [torsion group](torsion-group.md); its exponent is a common multiple of the [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) for every element.
