# Positive orbit separation implies a Hausdorff quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient topology](quotient-topology.md)

Suppose a [group](group-split.md) acts by [isometries](isometry.md) on a [metric space](metric-space.md) $X$, and for points $a,b$ in different [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) one has $\inf_g d(a,gb)>0$. Then the [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) space with its [quotient topology](quotient-topology.md) is [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md). Indeed, choose $\varepsilon$ less than one third of this positive infimum and saturate the [open](open-set.md) radius-$\varepsilon$ balls about $a$ and $b$ by the [action](action.md). These saturated sets are [open](open-set.md) because every acting [isometry](isometry.md) is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md). Their translates cannot meet: their centres stay at least the given positive [distance](distance.md) apart. Their disjoint [open](open-set.md) images in the quotient separate the two [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) points, since the preimage of each image is its saturated set. This applies to the [glide reflection](glide-reflection.md) $(x,y)\mapsto(x+1,-y)$ on a [closed](closed-set.md) strip: the [distance](distance.md) from one point to the translates of another tends to infinity as the [integer](integer-split.md) translation grows, so two distinct [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) have a positive minimum [distance](distance.md).
