# Multilayer perceptron

↑ **Parent:** [Feedforward neural network](feedforward-neural-network.md)

A layered [neural network](neural-network.md) with [affine maps](affine-map.md) and [activation functions](activation-function.md), usually with dense connections between successive layers. A dense layer with $n$ inputs and $m$ outputs has $m(n+1)$ parameters including biases.
