# Optical cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_cavity)

An [optical cavity](optical-cavity.md) supports optical fields whose phases and spatial profiles reproduce themselves after a round trip through its reflecting or guiding elements. Its resonance conditions include propagation and reflection phases. A cavity bounded by two plane mirrors is a simple example; [Fabry–Pérot resonance spacing](fabry-perot-resonance-spacing.md) describes the frequency intervals of an ideal two-mirror arrangement.
