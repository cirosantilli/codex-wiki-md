# Curvature radius of a constant-curvature space

↑ **Parent:** [Sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md)

A space with nonzero constant [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) $K$ has [curvature radius of a constant-curvature space](curvature-radius-of-a-constant-curvature-space.md) $R_c=1/\sqrt{|K|}$. Scaling a [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) by $a^2$ divides its [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) by $a^2$. In an [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) whose spatial normalization is $k=\pm1$, the [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) is $K=k/a^2$, so its [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) equals $R_c$. For $k=0$ there is no finite intrinsic curvature length.
