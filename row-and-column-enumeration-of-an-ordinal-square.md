# Row-and-column enumeration of an ordinal square

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite cardinal arithmetic](infinite-cardinal-arithmetic.md)

A compatible transfinite family of [bijections](bijection.md) can enumerate the square of an [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) by adjoining a new row and then the remaining new column. Starting with $g_0=\varnothing$, extend at a successor by $g_{\alpha+1}(\alpha,\beta)=\gamma_\alpha+\beta$ for $\beta\leq\alpha$ and $g_{\alpha+1}(\beta,\alpha)=\gamma_\alpha+(\alpha+1)+\beta$ for $\beta<\alpha$. Thus $\gamma_{\alpha+1}=\gamma_\alpha+(\alpha+1)+\alpha$. At limits take compatible unions. The two new domain pieces are disjoint; the column is empty at the initial step $\alpha=0$. Each step enumerates consecutive [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) intervals, proving bijectivity by [transfinite induction](transfinite-induction.md). Finite stages have $\gamma_n=n^2$. Successor and limit [cardinality](cardinality.md) arguments then give the square equality for well-orderable infinite cardinals without using that equality to justify the construction.
