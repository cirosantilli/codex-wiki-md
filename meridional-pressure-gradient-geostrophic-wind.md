# Meridional-pressure-gradient geostrophic wind

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic wind](geostrophic-wind.md)

With $x$ eastward and $y$ northward, a purely meridional [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) gives the velocity in the title. For $f>0$, pressure increasing northward gives westward wind, leaving lower pressure to the wind's left. Reversing the pressure gradient reverses the velocity. At a fixed gradient and density the speed scales as $1/|\sin\phi|$; the formal equatorial divergence signals breakdown of [geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md), rather than a real infinite wind.
