# Absolute convergence of the real xi logarithmic derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Real logarithmic derivative of Riemann xi](real-logarithmic-derivative-of-riemann-xi.md)

The [Jensen zero-count bound](jensen-zero-count-bound.md) gives $O(T\log T)$ zeros up to ordinate $T$. Since their real parts lie in $(0,1)$, a fixed-point real [logarithmic derivative](logarithmic-derivative.md) summand is $O(|\Im\rho|^{-2})$. Dyadic ordinate bands then contribute $O(j2^{-j})$. This proves [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md) of the real sum, while the unpaired complex sum of reciprocals need not converge absolutely.
