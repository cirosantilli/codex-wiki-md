# Height bound for a polynomial evaluation

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute multiplicative Weil height](absolute-multiplicative-weil-height.md)

Let $P,Q\in\mathbb Z[X_1,\ldots,X_k]$ have degree at most $n_j$ in $X_j$. Then

$$
H\left(\frac{P(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_k)}
{Q(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_k)}\right)
\leq
\max\{\mathcal L(P),\mathcal L(Q)\}
\prod_{j=1}^kH(\alpha_j)^{n_j}
$$

whenever the quotient is defined. At each non-Archimedean place, the integral coefficients contribute at most one; at the Archimedean places, the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) contributes the [polynomial length](polynomial-length.md). Multiplying these local estimates and using the [product formula](product-formula.md) gives the claim.
