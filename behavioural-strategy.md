# Behavioural strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

An assignment of an action [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) to every [information set](information-set.md) of a player in an [extensive-form game](extensive-form-game.md). Randomization is fresh at each encounter. It differs from a [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md), which chooses a complete deterministic plan once before play. [Perfect recall](perfect-recall.md) is central to equivalence of their induced outcome distributions.
