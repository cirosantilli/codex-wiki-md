# Core-collapse shock revival

↑ **Parent:** [Core-collapse supernova](core-collapse-supernova.md)

The process by which a stalled outgoing [shock](shock-wave.md) in a collapsing stellar core regains sufficient energy to expel outer material. [Neutrino](neutrino.md) heating and multidimensional flow can contribute, while dissociation and [radiation](radiation.md) drain energy. Collapse does not guarantee a successful ordinary [supernova](supernova.md).
