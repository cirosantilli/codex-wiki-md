<h1 id="newton-cartan-theory">Newton–Cartan theory</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton–Cartan_theory)

Newton–Cartan theory describes [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics.md) geometrically using absolute time, a degenerate spatial inverse metric, and a compatible torsion-free [affine connection](affine-connection.md). It is not a theory with a nondegenerate Lorentzian metric. Gravitationally accelerated particles are geodesics of that connection, whose curvature measures tidal gravity.
