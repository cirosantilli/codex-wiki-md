# Frustration freeness

↑ **Parent:** [Frustration-free quantum Hamiltonian](frustration-free-quantum-hamiltonian.md)

A decomposition $H=\sum_Zh_Z$ has [frustration freeness](frustration-freeness.md) when a global [ground state](ground-state.md) minimizes every term simultaneously. For finite-dimensional Hermitian terms this implies $E_0(H)=\sum_ZE_0(h_Z)$. Shifting each term by its minimum produces [positive operators](positive-operator.md) with a common null vector. The property depends on the decomposition, not only the total operator.
