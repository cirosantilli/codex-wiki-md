# Sequential two-site enzyme reaction network

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme kinetics](enzyme-kinetics.md)

An [enzyme](enzyme.md) may bind one molecule of a [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) to form $C_1$, then a second to form $C_2$, with product-release reactions $C_1\to E+P$ and $C_2\to C_1+P$. The [law of mass action](law-of-mass-action.md) gives conservation laws $e+c_1+c_2=e_0$ and $s+c_1+2c_2+p=s_0$. Scaling substrate by $s_0$, complexes by $e_0$ and time by $(k_1e_0)^{-1}$ exposes the small ratio $\epsilon=e_0/s_0$: the substrate evolves on the slow scale while the complex equations have the form $\epsilon v_i'=g_i$. This is a starting point for [quasi-steady-state approximation](quasi-steady-state-approximation.md) when $\epsilon\ll1$.
