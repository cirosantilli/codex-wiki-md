<h1 id="russell-s-paradox">Russell's paradox</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell's_paradox)

Unrestricted formation of a [set](set-split.md) $R=\{x:x\notin x\}$ contradicts its own membership condition: substituting $R$ for $x$ gives the displayed equivalence. Thus a predicate cannot always define a [set](set-split.md) of all objects satisfying it. Restricted [set comprehension](restricted-set-comprehension.md) forms a [subset](subset.md) of a previously given [set](set-split.md), avoiding this argument. The contradiction does not assert that a [set](set-split.md) whose elements happen not to contain themselves is impossible; it rules out the universal collection with exactly the specified membership condition.
