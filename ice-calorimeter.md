# Ice calorimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Calorimeter](calorimeter.md)

A [calorimeter](calorimeter.md) that infers transferred [heat](heat-split.md) from ice melting at a specified equilibrium [temperature](temperature-split.md). The [latent heat](latent-heat.md) sets the proportionality after correcting for other thermal changes and extraneous melting.
