# Bockstein linking invariant of a three-dimensional lens space

↑ **Parent:** [Bockstein isomorphism for a three-dimensional lens space](bockstein-isomorphism-for-a-three-dimensional-lens-space.md)

For a generator $a\in H^1(L(p);\mathbb F_p)$, [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) and the [Bockstein isomorphism for a three-dimensional lens space](bockstein-isomorphism-for-a-three-dimensional-lens-space.md) make $t(a)$ nonzero. Replacing $a$ by $na$ multiplies $t(a)$ by $n^2$. An orientation-reversing homotopy equivalence multiplies the evaluation by $-1$, so its existence forces $n^2=-1$ in $\mathbb F_p$ for some $n$.
