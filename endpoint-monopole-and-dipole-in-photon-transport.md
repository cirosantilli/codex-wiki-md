# Endpoint monopole and dipole in photon transport

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial-source photon transport solution](polynomial-source-photon-transport-solution.md)

A direction-independent term at a fixed observer contributes an angular monopole; a term linear in [photon](photon.md) direction contributes a dipole. They do not directly generate higher angular multipoles. In a gravitational line-of-sight solution, separating such endpoint terms from a transported recombination term can clarify which contribution appears in a sky anisotropy map. The observable must retain all emitter/observer terms consistently with the metric gauge and the chosen brightness normalization.
