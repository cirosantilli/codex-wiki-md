# Lattice gas

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

A [lattice gas](lattice-gas.md) describes [particle](particle-physics.md) positions by occupations of fixed cells. In the single-occupancy model, $n_\alpha\in\{0,1\}$ and $N=\sum_\alpha n_\alpha$. Its [grand canonical partition function](grand-canonical-partition-function.md) sums configurations with weight $\exp[-\beta(U-\widetilde\mu N)]$. Discretizing a classical position [integral](integral-split.md) into cells of [volume](volume.md) $b$ gives $\widetilde\mu=\mu+k_BT\ln(b/\lambda^3)$, with [thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) $\lambda$.

For [energy](energy-split.md) $U=-4J\sum_{\langle\alpha\alpha'\rangle}n_\alpha n_{\alpha'}$ on a regular lattice of coordination $q$, the substitution $n_\alpha=(1+\sigma_\alpha)/2$ maps the model to the [Ising model](ising-model.md) with coupling $J$ and effective field $h=qJ+\widetilde\mu/2$. The mean Ising spin is a density imbalance, $\langle\sigma\rangle=2bn-1$. For $J>0$ below the critical [temperature](temperature-split.md), the field-driven Ising transition becomes gas-liquid [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md).
