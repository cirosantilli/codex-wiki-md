# Superconducting critical current density

↑ **Parent:** [Superconducting critical current](superconducting-critical-current.md)

A limiting transport current per conductor cross-sectional area for specified [temperature](temperature-split.md), [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and dissipation criterion. Local current distribution, flux motion and weak links can make it distinct from an intrinsic depairing limit. Its variation with the operating conditions is summarized by a [superconducting critical surface](superconducting-critical-surface.md).
