# Even extension

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier cosine series](fourier-cosine-series.md)

The even extension of a [function](function-split.md) $f:[0,L]\to\mathbb R$ is $F_e(x)=f(|x|)$ for $-L\leq x\leq L$. It is an [even function](even-function.md). Its $2L$-[periodic extension](periodic-extension.md) is the function expanded by the [half-range Fourier cosine series](half-range-fourier-cosine-series.md).
