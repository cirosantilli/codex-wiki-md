<h1 id="ferraro-s-law-of-isorotation">Ferraro's law of isorotation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric magnetic winding](axisymmetric-magnetic-winding.md)

A stationary axisymmetric [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) in ideal purely rotational flow requires constant angular velocity along the [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md) of its [poloidal magnetic field](poloidal-magnetic-field.md). Otherwise [axisymmetric magnetic winding](axisymmetric-magnetic-winding.md) generates a time-dependent toroidal component.
