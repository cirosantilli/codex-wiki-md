# Finite-size condensate chemical potential

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensate](bose-einstein-condensate.md)

For a noninteracting [ground state](ground-state.md) of [energy](energy-split.md) zero, $N_0=(e^{-\beta\mu}-1)^{-1}$ gives $\mu=-k_BT\ln(1+1/N_0)$. Thus a finite condensate has a slightly negative [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md); exact zero pinning is a macroscopic limiting statement.
