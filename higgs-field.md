# Higgs field

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higgs_field)

The Standard Model Higgs field is a complex $SU(2)_L$ doublet of hypercharge $1/2$. Its nonzero vacuum expectation value triggers the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md).
