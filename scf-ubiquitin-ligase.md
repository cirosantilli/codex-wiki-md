# SCF ubiquitin ligase

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)

An SKP1–cullin–F-box [ubiquitin ligase](ubiquitin-ligase.md) complex directing substrate-selective ubiquitination. Recognition of degradation signals couples substrate destruction to cellular state.
