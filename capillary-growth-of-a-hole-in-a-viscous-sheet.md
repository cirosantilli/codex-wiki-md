# Capillary growth of a hole in a viscous sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric viscous-sheet stretching equations](axisymmetric-viscous-sheet-stretching-equations.md)

For a uniform annular sheet attached to a fixed outer rim $R_0$, two [surface tension](surface-tension.md) pulls give $h\sigma_{rr}=-2\gamma$ at the hole radius $R$. The zero-pressure radial equation gives $u=Ar+B/r$, with $B=-AR_0^2$ and $A=-\gamma R^2/[\mu h(R_0^2+3R^2)]$. The thickness remains uniform by [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md). With negligible initial hole volume, $h=h_0/(1-x^2)$ and

$$
\dot x=\frac\gamma{\mu h_0}\frac{x(1-x^2)^2}{1+3x^2},\qquad x=R/R_0.
$$

A positive seed hole grows initially exponentially. An exactly zero initial radius remains zero within this continuum evolution law.
