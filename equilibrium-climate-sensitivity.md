# Equilibrium climate sensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Climate sensitivity](climate-sensitivity.md)

The long-term global surface warming after atmospheric carbon dioxide is doubled and the modeled system reaches equilibrium, with stated feedback conventions. A response to another forcing amplitude must be rescaled before being called the conventional ECS.
