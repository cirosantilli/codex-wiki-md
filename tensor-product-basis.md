# Tensor-product basis

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product](tensor-product.md)

If $\{e_i\}$ and $\{f_j\}$ are [bases](basis.md) of finite-dimensional [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) $V,W$, then $\{e_i\otimes f_j\}$ is a basis of $V\otimes W$. Bilinearity gives spanning, and applying products of dual coordinate functionals gives independence. Thus $\dim(V\otimes W)=\dim V\dim W$.
