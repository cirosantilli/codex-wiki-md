# Holevo capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum channel](quantum-channel.md)

The Holevo capacity of a [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) is the maximum one-use output [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md),

$$
\chi^*(\Lambda)=\max_{\{p_x,\rho_x\}}
\left[S\left(\sum_xp_x\Lambda(\rho_x)\right)-\sum_xp_xS(\Lambda(\rho_x))\right].
$$

It is the unassisted classical communication limit for [product state](product-state.md) encodings with collective output measurements. Arbitrary entangled inputs require the regularized expression in the [Holevo-Schumacher-Westmoreland theorem](holevo-schumacher-westmoreland-theorem.md).
