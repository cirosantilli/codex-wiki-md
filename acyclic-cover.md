# Acyclic cover

↑ **Parent:** [Open cover](open-cover.md)

An open cover is acyclic for a specified [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) if its restriction to every nonempty finite intersection has zero higher [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md). The [acyclic cover theorem](leray-s-theorem.md) then identifies the cover's [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md) with sheaf cohomology. On a [separated scheme](separated-scheme.md), an affine open cover is acyclic for every [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md).
