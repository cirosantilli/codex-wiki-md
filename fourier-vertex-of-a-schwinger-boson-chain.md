# Fourier vertex of a Schwinger boson chain

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum XY chain](quantum-xy-chain.md)

With the [Schwinger boson representation](schwinger-boson-representation.md) and $a_n=L^{-1/2}\sum_qe^{-iqn}A_q$, the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) of a periodic [quantum XY chain](quantum-xy-chain.md) takes the displayed form. The four [inverse discrete Fourier transforms](inverse-discrete-fourier-transform.md) and site sum give $1/L$ and lattice [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md). The forward and reverse nearest-neighbour exchange phases are $e^{i(q-t)}$ and $e^{-i(q-t)}$, giving the [cosine](cosine.md). All [momenta](momentum-split.md) are defined modulo $2\pi$; neighbouring sites must be distinct for the stated [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md).
