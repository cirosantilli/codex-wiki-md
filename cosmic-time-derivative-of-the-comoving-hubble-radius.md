# Cosmic-time derivative of the comoving Hubble radius

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving Hubble radius](comoving-hubble-radius.md)

With $\mathcal H=aH=\dot a$, the [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives

$$
\frac d{dt}\frac1{\mathcal H}=-\frac{\ddot a}{\dot a^2}=\frac\Omega{2a}(1+3w).
$$

The coefficient is positive when $a>0$, $\rho>0$ and $H\ne0$. Thus growth of the [comoving Hubble radius](comoving-hubble-radius.md) and linear growth of departures from flatness have the same sign criterion in an expanding universe. This local criterion is distinct from a global [particle horizon](particle-horizon.md), which depends on the whole past history.
