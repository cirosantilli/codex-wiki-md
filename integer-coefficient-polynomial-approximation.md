# Integer-coefficient polynomial approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)

The [uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md) closure of the integer-coefficient [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) on $[0,1]$ consists precisely of the [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) taking [integer](integer.md) values at both endpoints. Necessity follows because evaluations at zero and one are [integers](integer.md) and convergent [integer](integer.md) sequences are eventually constant. For sufficiency, the [rounded Bernstein polynomial](rounded-bernstein-polynomial.md) has [integer](integer.md) monomial [coefficients](coefficient.md) and differs uniformly by a vanishing amount from the [Bernstein polynomial](bernstein-polynomial.md), which converges uniformly to the target.
