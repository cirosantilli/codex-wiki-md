# Partition interpretation of Gaussian binomial coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian binomial coefficient](gaussian-binomial-coefficient.md)

The [Young diagrams](young-diagram.md) of [partitions in a rectangle](partition-in-a-rectangle.md) with at most $k$ rows and at most $n-k$ cells in each row correspond to the [lattice paths](lattice-path.md) in the [lattice-path interpretation of Gaussian binomial coefficients](lattice-path-interpretation-of-gaussian-binomial-coefficients.md). Read the shaded row lengths from top to bottom; the result is a nonincreasing list of [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md). This [bijection](bijection.md) preserves the number $|\lambda|$ of cells.

In a two-by-two [rectangle](rectangle.md), the possibilities are the empty [integer partition](integer-partition.md), $(1)$, $(2)$, $(1,1)$, $(2,1)$, and $(2,2)$. Their sizes are $0,1,2,2,3,4$, giving $G_{4,2}(t)$. In particular, **there are two allowed diagrams of size two**, explaining the middle [coefficient](coefficient.md).[§1.7, Proposition 1.7.3, pp. 64–66](enumerative-combinatorics-volume-1-second-edition-draft-by-richard-stanley.md)
