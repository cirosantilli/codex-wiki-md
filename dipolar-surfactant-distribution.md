# Dipolar surfactant distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Translating surfactant-coated bubble](translating-surfactant-coated-bubble.md)

Linear steady transport on a translating sphere gives $B=2AC_0a/(ka^2+2D_s)$. The components of its unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) are degree-one [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md). The condition $2|A|Ua/(ka^2+2D_s)\ll1$ ensures the concentration perturbation is small relative to $C_0$.
