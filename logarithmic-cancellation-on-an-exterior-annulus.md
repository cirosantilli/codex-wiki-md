# Logarithmic cancellation on an exterior annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Global harmonic conjugate criterion by vanishing periods](global-harmonic-conjugate-criterion-by-vanishing-periods.md)

For $|z|>1$, both logarithms in the display can use their [principal logarithms](principal-complex-logarithm.md). The [real part](real-part.md) is $\log(|z+1|^2/|z-1|^2)$ and the [derivative](derivative.md) is $2/(z+1)-2/(z-1)$. The two [residues](residue.md) cancel on a loop surrounding both points. Equivalently $f=4\sum_{m\ge0}z^{-(2m+1)}/(2m+1)$ is globally single-valued outside the unit [circle](circle.md), although individual logarithms of $z+1$ and $z-1$ are not.
