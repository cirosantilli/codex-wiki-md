# Malate

↑ **Parent:** [Malic acid](malic-acid.md)

[Malate](malate.md) is the [anion](anion.md) obtained by removing both carboxyl [protons](proton.md) from [malic acid](malic-acid.md). Its [protonation](protonation.md) state distinguishes it from the acid.
