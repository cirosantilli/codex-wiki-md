<h1 id="su-3-casimir-in-chevalley-generators">SU(3) Casimir in Chevalley generators</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic Casimir operator](quadratic-casimir-operator.md)

For $e_3=[e_1,e_2]$, $f_3=-[f_1,f_2]$, the operator $C=h_1^2+h_2^2+h_1h_2+3(h_1+h_2)+3\sum_{i=1}^3f_ie_i$ commutes with all generators. In its commutator with $e_1$, the Cartan polynomial contributes $3h_1e_1$, while the last sum contributes $-3h_1e_1$; the two mixed terms involving $f_2e_3$ cancel. On highest weight $(p,q)$ its eigenvalue is $p^2+q^2+pq+3p+3q$. This normalization is three times the conventional [SU(3) quadratic Casimir eigenvalue](su-3-quadratic-casimir-eigenvalue.md).
