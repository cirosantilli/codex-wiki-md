# Upper-half-plane harmonic measure of the positive half-axis

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md)

For planar [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at $(x,y)$ with $y>0$, the probability that its first hit of the real axis lies in $(0,\infty)$ is

$$
\frac12+\frac1\pi\arctan\frac{x}{y}
=1-\frac1\pi\arg(x+iy).
$$

This is the bounded [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) in the upper half-plane with boundary values zero on the negative half-axis and one on the positive half-axis.
