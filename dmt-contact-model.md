# DMT contact model

↑ **Parent:** [Contact mechanics](contact-mechanics.md)

An adhesive sphere-on-flat contact approximation combining [Hertzian contact](hertzian-contact.md) deformation with an attractive [force](force-split.md) outside the contact area. It is most appropriate for sufficiently stiff, small-radius contacts with a small [Tabor parameter](tabor-parameter.md); a fitted reduced modulus is not automatically the sample [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md).
