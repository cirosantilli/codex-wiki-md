# Direct cosmological communication and a shared causal past

↑ **Parent:** [Particle horizon](particle-horizon.md)

In a spatially flat [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) with initial [conformal time](conformal-time.md) zero, two comoving worldlines at separation $d$ can exchange a one-way light signal by time $\eta$ only if $d\le\eta$. Their past light cones overlap if $d\le2\eta$: a common event halfway between them can influence both. These are different statements. For opposite emission events observed at conformal time $\eta_0$, their separation is $2(\eta_0-\eta_e)$, so the respective thresholds are $\eta_e\ge2\eta_0/3$ and $\eta_e\ge\eta_0/2$.
