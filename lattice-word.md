# Lattice word

↑ **Parent:** [Semistandard Young tableau](semistandard-young-tableau.md)

A word in positive integers is a [lattice word](lattice-word.md) if every initial segment has at least as many $i$'s as $(i+1)$'s for every $i$. This is the reading-word condition in the [Littlewood–Richardson rule](littlewood-richardson-rule.md). Under [good-letter matching in a tableau word](good-letter-matching-in-a-tableau-word.md), every letter of a [lattice word](lattice-word.md) is good.
