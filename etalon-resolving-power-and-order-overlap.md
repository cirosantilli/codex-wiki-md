# Etalon resolving power and order overlap

↑ **Parent:** [Finesse](finesse.md)

For an ideal air-gap [Fabry–Pérot etalon](fabry-perot-etalon.md) at [normal incidence](normal-incidence.md), the [free spectral range](free-spectral-range.md) is $\nu_{\rm FSR}=c/(2d)$ and the peak [full width at half maximum](full-width-at-half-maximum.md) is $\delta\nu=\nu_{\rm FSR}/\mathcal F$. To separate a pair of lines spaced by $\Delta\nu$ by at least $M$ peak widths, require $d\mathcal F\ge Mc/(2\Delta\nu)$. Putting both lines inside one unambiguous [free spectral range](free-spectral-range.md) additionally requires $d<c/(2\Delta\nu)$, so $\mathcal F>M$. In terms of their [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavelengths](wavelength.md), $c/\Delta\nu=\lambda_1\lambda_2/|\lambda_2-\lambda_1|$.

The strict order condition needs a margin if tails from adjacent periodic peaks are to be negligible. If every neighbouring peak in a scanned two-line periodic spectrum must be separated by $M$ widths, the stronger condition is $\min(\Delta\nu,\nu_{\rm FSR}-\Delta\nu)\ge M\nu_{\rm FSR}/\mathcal F$. Ordinary resolving power alone does not prevent ambiguous repeated orders. A fixed etalon's transmission as a function of [wavelength](wavelength.md) is a periodic-in-frequency filter; it is not automatically a plot of the incident source's spectral lines.
