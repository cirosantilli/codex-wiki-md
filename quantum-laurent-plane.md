# Quantum Laurent plane

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum plane](quantum-plane.md)

For $q\ne0$, define a vector space with basis $u^iv^j$, $i\in\mathbb Z$, $j\ge0$, and product $(u^iv^j)(u^kv^\ell)=q^{-jk}u^{i+k}v^{j+\ell}$. Bilinearity of the exponent verifies associativity. This constructs the algebra and proves the basis independently of its presentation. It localizes the [quantum plane](quantum-plane.md) at powers of $u$ and gives a quotient witness for noncommutativity and noncocommutativity of the [coordinate Hopf algebra of quantum SL2](coordinate-hopf-algebra-of-quantum-sl2.md).
