# Posterior equality for proportional likelihoods

↑ **Parent:** [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md)

If every model's [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) is multiplied by the same positive factor independent of its parameter or model index, [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) cancels that factor from the normalized posterior. Fixed-count Bernoulli sampling and sampling stopped at a specified positive head count give such proportional [likelihoods](likelihood-function.md) for the same total heads and tails. This conclusion uses their actual sampling [likelihoods](likelihood-function.md); it is not a claim that every selection or stopping rule can be ignored.
