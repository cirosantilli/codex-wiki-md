# Full subcategory

↑ **Parent:** [Subcategory](subcategory.md)

A full subcategory contains all ambient [morphisms](morphism.md) between its chosen objects. Its inclusion is a [fully faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md) [functor](functor.md).
