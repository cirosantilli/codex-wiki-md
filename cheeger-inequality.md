# Cheeger inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Cheeger constant](cheeger-constant.md)

For a reversible chain with spectral gap $\gamma$ and Cheeger constant $\Phi$,

$$
\frac{\Phi^2}{2}\leq\gamma\leq2\Phi.
$$
