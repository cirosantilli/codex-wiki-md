# Scarp diffusion model

↑ **Parent:** [Fault scarp](fault-scarp.md)

A simple model of hillslope smoothing in which sediment flux is proportional to negative slope. For constant transport coefficient $\kappa$, an ideal initial step broadens on scale $\sqrt{\kappa t}$. Real free-face collapse, nonlinear transport, changing climate and deposition can invalidate the linear [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) approximation.
