# Continuous slowing-down approximation range

↑ **Parent:** [Electron stopping power](electron-stopping-power.md)

An approximation to mean travelled path length, obtained by integrating reciprocal total [electron stopping power](electron-stopping-power.md) and neglecting energy-loss fluctuations. It differs from mean forward penetration depth when [electrons](electron.md) change direction.
