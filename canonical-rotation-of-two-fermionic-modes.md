# Canonical rotation of two fermionic modes

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md)

A real two-mode [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) preserving the full [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md). The self [anticommutators](anticommutator.md) become $\{a,a^\dagger\}=\{b,b^\dagger\}=u^2+v^2$, while $\{a,b\}=-uv+uv=0$ and every other cross [anticommutator](anticommutator.md) vanishes. Thus its [matrix](matrix.md) is an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md). For the [two-orbital fermionic pairing Hamiltonian](two-orbital-fermionic-pairing-hamiltonian.md), let $E=\sqrt{\varepsilon^2+\Delta^2}$, take $u=\sqrt{(E+\varepsilon)/(2E)}$ and $v=\sqrt{(E-\varepsilon)/(2E)}$, and obtain $H=E(c^\dagger c+d^\dagger d)+\varepsilon-E$. Inverting gives $c=ua-vb^\dagger$, $d=va^\dagger+ub$.
