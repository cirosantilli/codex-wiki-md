# Generalized coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_coordinates)

A generalized coordinate is one of a set of independent parameters specifying the [configuration](mechanical-configuration.md) of a mechanical system subject to its constraints. An [angle](angle.md) locates a bead constrained to a fixed circular hoop. Expressing [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) in these coordinates gives the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) and its [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md).
