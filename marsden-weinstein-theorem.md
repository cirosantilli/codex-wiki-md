# Marsden-Weinstein theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic reduction](symplectic-reduction.md)

For a [Hamiltonian group action](hamiltonian-group-action.md), let $c$ be a [regular value](regular-value.md) of the [moment map](moment-map.md) fixed by the [coadjoint action](coadjoint-representation.md), and assume the action on $\mu^{-1}(c)$ is free and proper. The quotient is a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) of dimension $\dim M-2\dim G$. Its unique reduced [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) is characterized by $\pi^*\omega_c=\iota^*\omega$, where $\iota$ includes the level set and $\pi$ is the quotient projection. The kernel of the restricted two-form consists precisely of the orbit directions. More general noncentral levels use their stabilizer group; the stated central-level version suffices for [circle group](circle-group.md) reduction.
