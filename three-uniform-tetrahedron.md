# Three-uniform tetrahedron

↑ **Parent:** [Complete uniform hypergraph](complete-uniform-hypergraph.md)

The three-uniform tetrahedron is the [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) on four vertices with all four possible triple hyperedges. It is the [complete uniform hypergraph](complete-uniform-hypergraph.md) $K_4^3$, and differs from its geometric realization as a [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md). Its edge pattern encodes four-term [arithmetic progressions](arithmetic-progression.md).
