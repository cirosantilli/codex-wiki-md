# Genus of a complete-intersection space curve

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic genus](arithmetic-genus.md)

For a [projective complete intersection](projective-complete-intersection.md) curve cut out by a homogeneous [regular sequence](regular-sequence.md) of positive degrees $a,b$ in $\mathbb P^3_k$, the [Koszul resolution](koszul-resolution.md) gives [Hilbert polynomial](hilbert-polynomial.md) $ab\,m+ab(4-a-b)/2$. Hence its [arithmetic genus](arithmetic-genus.md) is $1+ab(a+b-4)/2$. The same resolution shows $H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=k$, and the [dimension of a scheme](dimension-of-a-scheme.md) is one, so this genus equals $\dim_kH^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$. Smoothness and irreducibility of the curve are not required. For degrees five and seven, the value is $141$.
