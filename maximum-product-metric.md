# Maximum product metric

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)

The displayed metric on a product of two [metric spaces](metric-space.md) satisfies its [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) because $\max\{a+c,b+d\}\le\max\{a,b\}+\max\{c,d\}$ for nonnegative coordinates. Its open balls are products of equal-radius balls, so it induces the [product topology](product-topology.md).
