# Integral basis of the cubic field of discriminant minus 140

↑ **Parent:** [Integral basis](integral-basis.md)

The [power basis](power-basis.md) $1,\theta,\theta^2$ is an [integral basis](integral-basis.md). The defining [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is irreducible by [Eisenstein's criterion](eisenstein-criterion.md) at two and its [discriminant](discriminant.md) is $-140$. The [discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice](discriminant-index-formula-for-an-integral-lattice.md) restricts its index in the [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) to one or two. An index of two would supply an [algebraic integer](algebraic-integer.md) $\eta=(a+b\theta+c\theta^2)/2$ with $a,b,c\in\{0,1\}$ not all zero. Its [field trace](field-trace.md) $(3a-4c)/2$ forces $a=0$. If $b=0$ this gives $\theta^2/2$ directly; if $b=1$, the identity $\theta\eta=\theta^2/2-c\theta-c$ gives the same integrality. But $\theta^2/2=-1-\theta^{-1}$, while $N(\theta^{-1})=-1/2$ excludes integrality by the [field norm](field-norm.md). Thus the index is one, proving the claim.
