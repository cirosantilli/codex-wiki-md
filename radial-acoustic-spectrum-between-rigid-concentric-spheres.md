# Radial acoustic spectrum between rigid concentric spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)

A [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) obeying the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) with constant [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md) $c_0$ has spherical normal modes $\phi=e^{-i\omega t}[A\cos kr+B\sin kr]/r$, $k=\omega/c_0$. Zero radial velocity at radii $a,b$ gives Neumann conditions on the potential, not on its numerator. The inner condition is built into $F(r)=C[\cos k(r-a)+(ka)^{-1}\sin k(r-a)]/r$. The outer condition gives $k(b-a)\cos k(b-a)-(1+k^2ab)\sin k(b-a)=0$, equivalent to the displayed equation for nonzero oscillatory modes. The formal zero root is a constant potential gauge, rather than a nonzero-frequency acoustic wave. Applying a one-dimensional sine condition to the numerator would miss the spherical geometric factors.
