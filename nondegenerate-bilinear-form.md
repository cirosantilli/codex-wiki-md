# Nondegenerate bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear form](bilinear-form.md)

A [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) $B$ on a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is nondegenerate when

$$
B(v,w)=0\text{ for every }v\quad\Longrightarrow\quad w=0,
$$

equivalently when $w\mapsto B(-,w)$ is an isomorphism from $V$ to its [dual space](dual-space.md).
