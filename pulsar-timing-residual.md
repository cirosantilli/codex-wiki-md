# Pulsar timing residual

↑ **Parent:** [Pulsar timing](pulsar-timing.md)

The difference between an observed arrival time and its model prediction. Positive [pulsar timing residuals](pulsar-timing-residual.md) mean late arrivals in this convention. A periodic residual can expose an omitted [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md); a smooth long-term residual can also come from a wrong spin model or clock correction. Fitting and checking alternative causes are part of the inference, rather than interpreting every residual as [gravitational radiation](gravitational-wave-split.md).
