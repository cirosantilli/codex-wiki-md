# Cerium dioxide electrodeposition

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemistry](electrochemistry-split.md)

Electrochemical growth of cerium dioxide from dissolved cerium ions. [Oxidation](oxidation.md) of cerium(III) to the solid oxide transfers one [electron](electron.md) per cerium; [proton](proton.md) activity affects the potential strongly. A poorly conducting film can slow further growth.
