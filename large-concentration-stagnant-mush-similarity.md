# Large-concentration stagnant-mush similarity

↑ **Parent:** [Stagnant mushy-layer model](stagnant-mushy-layer-model.md)

For $\mathcal C=C_0/\Delta C\gg1$ and [inverse Stefan number](latent-to-sensible-heat-ratio.md) $\mathcal S=O(\mathcal C)$, a [stagnant mushy-layer model](stagnant-mushy-layer-model.md) has nearly constant effective [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), giving $\Omega^2\theta_t=\kappa\theta_{zz}$ with $\Omega^2=1+\mathcal S/\mathcal C$. With negligible liquid [solutal diffusivity](solutal-diffusivity.md), roof value $\theta=-1$, positive liquid superheat $\theta_\infty$, and edge $h=2\lambda\sqrt{\kappa t}$, the mush profile is $\theta=-1+\operatorname{erf}(\Omega\eta)/\operatorname{erf}(\Omega\lambda)$. Matching [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) requires $\Omega e^{\lambda^2}\operatorname{erfc}\lambda=\theta_\infty e^{\Omega^2\lambda^2}\operatorname{erf}(\Omega\lambda)$. The square on $\Omega$ in the exponential is essential. The [porosity](porosity.md) $\mathcal C/(\mathcal C-\theta)$ records the leading composition balance; the linear thermal profile is asymptotic, not exact at finite $\mathcal C$.
