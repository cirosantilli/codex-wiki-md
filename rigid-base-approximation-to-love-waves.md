# Rigid-base approximation to Love waves

↑ **Parent:** [Love wave](love-wave.md)

A free top and rigid base impose transverse [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md) $(n+1/2)\pi/h$ on [guided SH modes between rigid and free planes](guided-sh-modes-between-rigid-and-free-planes.md). Their [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) and [group velocity](group-velocity.md) are $c=\beta'/\sqrt{1-\omega_n^2/\omega^2}$ and $U=\beta'\sqrt{1-\omega_n^2/\omega^2}$ for $\omega>\omega_n$. A real [Love wave](love-wave.md) replaces the rigid base by an evanescent elastic load; the approximation becomes reliable sufficiently above the corresponding strong-dispersion transition, not at the actual mode cutoff.
