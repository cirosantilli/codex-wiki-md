# GTP hydrolysis

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md)

[GTP hydrolysis](gtp-hydrolysis.md) converts [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md) with [water](water.md) to [GDP](guanosine-diphosphate.md) and inorganic [phosphate](phosphate.md). In many signaling [G proteins](g-protein.md), it favors a switch toward an inactive effector-binding state.
