# Spherical Bessel function

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel function](bessel-function-split.md)

The spherical Bessel functions solve the radial wave equation in three dimensions. In particular,

$$
j_2(x)=\left(\frac3{x^3}-\frac1x\right)\sin x-\frac3{x^2}\cos x,
\qquad j_2(x)=\frac{x^2}{15}+O(x^4).
$$

The apparent singularity at the origin is removable, as follows from the power series of the [Bessel function of the first kind](bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md).
