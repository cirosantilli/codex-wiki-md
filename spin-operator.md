# Spin operator

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

A [spin operator](spin-operator.md) describes intrinsic [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). Its components obey angular-momentum [commutators](commutator.md); for spin one half, $\hat S_j=\hbar\sigma_j/2$ in terms of the [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md).
