# Chord diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)

A cyclically ordered collection of endpoints paired into chords of a circle. The crossing relation defines a [circle graph](circle-graph.md); two chords with distinct endpoints cross exactly when their endpoints alternate.
