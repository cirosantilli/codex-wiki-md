# Fermionic operator

↑ **Parent:** [Fermionic Fock space](fermionic-fock-space.md)

A [fermionic operator](fermionic-operator.md) is odd under [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md): if $P=(-1)^N$ is the parity operator, then $PAP=-A$. A [fermionic creation operator](fermionic-creation-operator.md) or [fermionic annihilation operator](fermionic-annihilation-operator.md) is an example. Interchanging odd insertions in a [time-ordered product](time-ordered-product.md) contributes the corresponding [fermionic sign](fermionic-sign.md). Odd parity alone does not imply that every pair of such operators has zero [anticommutator](anticommutator.md).
