# Alternating binomial moment

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md)

For nonnegative integers $r,n$, the alternating binomial moment satisfies $S_r(n)=0$ when $r<n$ and $S_n(n)=(-1)^nn!$. Expand the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $X^r$ in [falling factorials](falling-factorial.md) and differentiate the [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md) to prove these identities. Equivalently, alternating [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md) implement a finite difference that annihilates polynomials of degree less than $n$.
