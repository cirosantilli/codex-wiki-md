<h1 id="jordan-s-totient-function">Jordan's totient function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan's_totient_function)

The Jordan totient function counts ordered $k$-tuples modulo $n$ that together with $n$ have [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) one. The [inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) gives

$$
J_k(n)=n^k\prod_{p\mid n}(1-p^{-k}).
$$
