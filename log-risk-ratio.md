# Log risk ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Risk ratio](risk-ratio.md)

The logarithm of a [risk ratio](risk-ratio.md) is zero at equal risks, negative when the first risk is smaller, and positive when it is larger. For independent binomial event counts $d_1,d_0$ from samples $n_1,n_0$, its usual delta-method [variance](variance-split.md) estimate is $1/d_1-1/n_1+1/d_0-1/n_0$. This approximation requires nonzero counts; zero-event arms need another procedure. Log estimates are convenient for inverse-[variance](variance-split.md) [meta-analysis](meta-analysis.md), and exponentiation returns to the risk-ratio scale.
