# Protein

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)

A [protein](protein.md) is a chain of amino-acid residues whose sequence and conformation determine its biological functions. Some proteins assemble into filaments whose mechanical properties can be described by [biopolymer mechanics](biopolymer-mechanics.md).
