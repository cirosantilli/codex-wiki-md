# SATPlan

↑ **Parent:** [Classical planning](classical-planning.md)

An approach to [automated planning](automated-planning.md) that constructs a [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md) for a bounded [planning horizon](planning-horizon.md), solves it and decodes a satisfying assignment into a [plan](plan.md). A typical encoding has time-indexed action occurrences and [planning fluents](planning-fluent.md), initial and goal clauses, [action precondition axioms](action-precondition-axiom.md), state-transition constraints and [action exclusion axioms](action-exclusion-axiom.md). Sequential and parallel encodings have different notions of a step.

[Henry Kautz](henry-kautz.md) and [Bart Selman](bart-selman.md) introduced [planning](planning.md) as satisfiability in their 1992 paper.[ECAI 1992, pp. 359–363; publication listed in the author's Cornell bibliography](planning-as-satisfiability-by-henry-kautz-and-bart-selman.md)
