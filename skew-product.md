# Skew product

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)

A skew product evolves a base coordinate by a [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md) $R$ and a fibre coordinate by a map $S_x$ depending on the current base coordinate: $F(x,y)=(R(x),S_x(y))$. The base evolution is independent of the fibre, while the fibre may depend on the base. Continuity or measurability is imposed according to the setting. An [irrational skew shift](irrational-skew-shift.md) is a useful affine example on a [torus](torus.md).
