# Pressure equation for inertial oscillations of a barotropic star

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly rotating barotropic star](uniformly-rotating-barotropic-star.md)

In the [Cowling approximation](cowling-approximation.md), a mode with phase $e^{i\omega t+im\phi}$ has rotating-frame frequency $\sigma=\omega+m\Omega$. The horizontal momentum matrix has determinant $4\Omega^2-\sigma^2$. Eliminating [velocities](velocity.md) yields a pressure-potential equation with vertical coefficient $1-4\Omega^2/\sigma^2$ and density-gradient term $2m\Omega\rho_RW/(R\sigma)$. Frequencies zero and plus or minus $2\Omega$ need the original system rather than this inversion.
