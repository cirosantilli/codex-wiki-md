# Photon reception by a uniformly accelerated observer

↑ **Parent:** [Rindler horizon](rindler-horizon.md)

For the standard right-wedge [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) and a stationary source at $x=L$ emitting rightmoving [photons](photon.md), the retarded [null coordinate](null-coordinate.md) is $p=t-x=-e^{-a\tau}/a$. Equating it to the emission value $t_e-L$ gives the displayed reception relation. Differentiating yields the Doppler [frequency](frequency.md) ratio $\nu/\nu_0=dt_e/d\tau=e^{-a\tau}$. The limiting emission time $L$ is a supremum: emission exactly at that time is never received at finite [proper time](proper-time.md). These expressions use $c=1$ and [acceleration](acceleration.md) continued indefinitely.
