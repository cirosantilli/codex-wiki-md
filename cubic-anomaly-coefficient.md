# Cubic anomaly coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Dynkin index](dynkin-index.md)

The cubic anomaly coefficient is defined by the symmetrized trace of three representation matrices. A conjugate representation has the opposite coefficient, while a real or pseudoreal representation has coefficient zero.
