# Markov chain state

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md)

A possible value of a [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md) at one time. The collection of such values is its [state space](state-space.md). A pair of molecule counts is one [Markov chain state](markov-chain-state.md) when two [chemical species](chemical-species.md) are tracked together.
