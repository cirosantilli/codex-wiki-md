# Triangle-free graphs with arbitrarily large chromatic number

↑ **Parent:** [Mycielskian](mycielskian.md)

Define $M_2=K_2$ and $M_{k+1}=\mu(M_k)$ for $k\geq2$. By the [Mycielskian chromatic increase](mycielskian-chromatic-increase.md) and [Mycielskian triangle preservation](mycielskian-triangle-preservation.md), each $M_k$ is a finite [triangle-free graph](triangle-free-graph.md) with [chromatic number](chromatic-number.md) $k$. The first steps are $K_2,C_5$, the [Grötzsch graph](grotzsch-graph.md), and then a 23-[vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) [graph](graph-split.md) needing five colours. The [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) recurrence $n_{k+1}=2n_k+1$ gives $n_k=3\cdot2^{k-2}-1$. These explicit examples show how arbitrarily strong [graph coloring](graph-coloring-split.md) constraints can accumulate without any three-[vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) [clique](clique-graph-theory.md).
