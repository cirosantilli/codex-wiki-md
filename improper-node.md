# Improper node

↑ **Parent:** [Node (dynamical systems)](node-dynamical-systems.md)

A planar [node](node-dynamical-systems.md) whose [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has a repeated nonzero real [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) and only one independent [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) is an improper node. In suitable real coordinates its [linearization of a dynamical system](linearization-of-a-dynamical-system.md) has the displayed form, with solutions

$$
u(t)=Ae^{\lambda t},\qquad v(t)=(B+cAt)e^{\lambda t}.
$$

It is a [stable node](stable-node.md) for $\lambda<0$ and an [unstable node](unstable-node.md) for $\lambda>0$. The factor $t$ makes generic trajectories tangent to the unique eigendirection as they approach the equilibrium in the attracting time direction. For a nonlinear [autonomous system](autonomous-system-mathematics.md) with this [hyperbolic equilibrium](hyperbolic-equilibrium-point.md), the sign of $\lambda$ still determines local stability; the local [phase portrait](phase-portrait.md) has the same topological type as its linearization.
