# Bialgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra over a commutative ring](algebra-over-a-commutative-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bialgebra)

An [algebra over a commutative ring](algebra-over-a-commutative-ring.md) that is also a [coalgebra](coalgebra.md), whose [comultiplication](comultiplication.md) and [counit](counit.md) preserve the multiplication and unit. Equivalently it is a [bimonoid](bimonoid.md) in $k$-modules.
