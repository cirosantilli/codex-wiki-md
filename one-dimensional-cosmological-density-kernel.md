# One-dimensional cosmological density kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Standard perturbation theory density kernel](standard-perturbation-theory-density-kernel.md)

For plane-parallel pressureless matter in an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), before [shell crossing](shell-crossing.md), the symmetrized growing-mode [standard perturbation theory density kernel](standard-perturbation-theory-density-kernel.md) is

$$
F_{n,s}(q_1,\ldots,q_n)=\frac{(q_1+\cdots+q_n)^n}{n!q_1\cdots q_n}.
$$

In particular $F_{2,s}=(q_1+q_2)^2/(2q_1q_2)$. An unsymmetrized representation of the one-dimensional [beta mode-coupling kernel](beta-mode-coupling-kernel.md) is $(q_1+q_2)/q_1$; its symmetric part is the expression above.
