# Auto-activation deficit

↑ **Parent:** [Abulia](abulia.md)

A severe reduction of spontaneous mental and behavioral activity that improves with external stimulation or instruction. Clinical descriptions after bilateral [basal ganglia](basal-ganglia.md) injury motivated experiments separating self-activation, incentive translation and motor capacity.[1984, externally elicited versus spontaneous behavior](pure-psychic-akinesia-with-bilateral-lesions-of-basal-ganglia-by-dominique-laplane-and-others.md)
