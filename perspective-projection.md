# Perspective projection

↑ **Parent:** [Computer graphics](computer-graphics-split.md)

Projection from a fixed centre onto an image plane. With centre at the origin and plane $z=f$, a [point](point-geometry.md) $(x,y,z)$ with $z\ne0$ maps to $(fx/z,fy/z,f)$. The image-plane convention may put the plane in front of the centre, giving a virtual upright image, or behind it, giving an inverted physical image.
