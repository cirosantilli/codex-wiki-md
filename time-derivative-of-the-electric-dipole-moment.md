# Time derivative of the electric dipole moment

↑ **Parent:** [Electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md)

For a localized charge and current distribution satisfying [charge conservation from Maxwell equations](charge-conservation-from-maxwell-equations.md),

$$
\dot{\mathbf p}=\int\mathbf J(x,t)\,d^3x.
$$
