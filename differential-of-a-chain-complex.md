# Differential of a chain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)

The [chain differential](boundary-operator.md) of a homological [chain complex](chain-complex.md) is a family of group or module homomorphisms $\partial_n:C_n\to C_{n-1}$ satisfying $\partial_{n-1}\partial_n=0$. The identity implies $\operatorname{im}\partial_{n+1}\subset\ker\partial_n$, so the quotient $H_n=\ker\partial_n/\operatorname{im}\partial_{n+1}$ is defined. In a graded complex over $\mathbb Z[U]$, the [chain differential](boundary-operator.md) can have degree $-1$ while $U$ has degree $-2$, with $\partial(Ux)=U\partial x$.
