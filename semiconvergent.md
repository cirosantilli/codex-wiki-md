# Semiconvergent

↑ **Parent:** [Continued fraction](continued-fraction.md)

Given consecutive [continued fraction convergents](continued-fraction-convergent.md) $p_{k-1}/q_{k-1}$ and $p_k/q_k$, the intermediate fractions $(tp_k+p_{k-1})/(tq_k+q_{k-1})$ for $1\le t\le a_{k+1}$ are semiconvergents, where $a_{k+1}$ is the next partial quotient. The endpoint is the next convergent. These fractions matter in [best rational approximation](best-rational-approximation.md) when a [denominator](denominator.md) bound cuts between consecutive convergents.
