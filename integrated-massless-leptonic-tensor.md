# Integrated massless leptonic tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Leptonic tensor](leptonic-tensor.md)

For the current with $(1-\gamma^5)$ rather than $P_L$, the spin-summed symmetric [leptonic tensor](leptonic-tensor.md) is $8(q_{1\mu}q_{2\nu}+q_{1\nu}q_{2\mu}-g_{\mu\nu}q_1\cdot q_2)$. Integrating the standard [two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space](two-body-lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) of two massless particles gives

$$
\int d\Phi_2(q;q_1,q_2)\,L_{\mu\nu}=\frac1{3\pi}(q_\mu q_\nu-g_{\mu\nu}q^2).
$$

Its antisymmetric [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md) term integrates to zero. The normalization changes by a factor of four if the current uses $P_L$ instead. The result separates the leptonic integration from hadronic [pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar form factors](pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar-form-factor.md) in a three-body [decay rate](decay-width.md).
