# Regular zero locus

↑ **Parent:** [Section of a vector bundle](section-of-a-vector-bundle.md)

The zero locus of a holomorphic [section of a vector bundle](section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) of a rank-$r$ bundle is regular when its derivative has rank $r$ along the locus. Its kernel is then the tangent bundle of the smooth zero set. Smoothness of the underlying set alone is insufficient: the square of a reduced defining function has the same set of zeros but zero derivative there.
