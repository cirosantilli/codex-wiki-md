# Open and closed maps

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_and_closed_maps)

An [open map](open-map.md) sends each [open set](open-set.md) to an [open set](open-set.md); a closed map sends each [closed set](closed-set.md) to a [closed set](closed-set.md). These properties concern images, whereas [continuity](continuous-function.md) concerns inverse images of open sets. Neither openness nor closedness alone implies continuity.
