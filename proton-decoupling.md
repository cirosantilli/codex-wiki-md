# Proton decoupling

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

Irradiation that removes resolved [scalar spin-spin coupling](scalar-spin-spin-coupling.md) to [protons](proton.md) from another nucleus’s spectrum. Other couplings, such as carbon–fluorine coupling in a proton-decoupled [carbon-13 NMR](carbon-13-nmr.md) spectrum, can remain.
