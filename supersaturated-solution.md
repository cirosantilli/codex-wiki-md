# Supersaturated solution

↑ **Parent:** [Saturation criterion for precipitation](saturation-criterion-for-precipitation.md)

A [supersaturated solution](supersaturated-solution.md) has an [ion product](ion-product.md) above the relevant [solubility product](solubility-product.md). It can persist through [metastability](metastability.md): [nucleation](nucleation.md) must first create a sufficiently large new [solid](solid.md) region. Added surfaces, impurities and mixing history can affect that barrier. Therefore a clear sample during a finite observation does not prove undersaturation, and visible cloudiness does not measure the [ion product](ion-product.md).
