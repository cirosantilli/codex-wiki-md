# Molar mass from sedimentation and diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Analytical ultracentrifugation](analytical-ultracentrifugation.md)

Let $M$ be molar mass in $\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}$, $\bar v$ partial specific volume, $\rho_s$ solvent density, and $N_A$ the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md). The buoyant molecular mass is $m_b=M(1-\bar v\rho_s)/N_A$. Combining [sedimentation coefficient](sedimentation-coefficient.md) $s=m_b/f_h$ and the thermal diffusion-friction relation $D=k_BT/f_h$ eliminates the unknown friction and proves the displayed formula, with $R_{\rm gas}=N_Ak_B$. Once friction is known, $R_h=f_h/(6\pi\eta)$ defines a [hydrodynamic radius](hydrodynamic-radius.md) using [Stokes's law](stokes-s-law.md). Mass and radius are different inferences: an extended molecule need not occupy the volume of that friction-equivalent sphere.
