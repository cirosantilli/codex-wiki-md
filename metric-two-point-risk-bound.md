# Metric two-point risk bound

↑ **Parent:** [Le Cam two-point lemma](le-cam-two-point-lemma.md)

If two parameters are separated by at least $2r$ in a [metric](metric.md), any [estimator](estimator.md) gives a test by deciding whether its distance from the first parameter is below $r$. For $0<\eta<1$, the event $|Z-1|\le\eta$ and [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) give the displayed [minimax risk](minimax-risk.md) bound, where $Z$ is the product [likelihood ratio](likelihood-ratio.md). The unrestricted claim for every positive $\eta$ is false when both factors on the right are negative.
