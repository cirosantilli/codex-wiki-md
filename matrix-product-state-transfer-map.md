# Matrix product state transfer map

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix product state](matrix-product-state.md)

For an MPS tensor $A^i$, the transfer map is the [completely positive map](completely-positive-map.md) $\mathcal E(X)=\sum_iA^iX(A^i)^\dagger$. Its fixed points control normalization and local expectation values, while its subleading eigenvalues control correlation lengths.
