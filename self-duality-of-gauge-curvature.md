# Self-duality of gauge curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Self-dual Yang-Mills equations](self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md)

In oriented Euclidean four-space, gauge curvature is self-dual when $F=*F$. The Hodge star squares to one on two-forms, so this picks its positive eigenspace; the negative eigenspace is [anti-self-duality of gauge curvature](anti-self-duality-of-gauge-curvature.md). The [Bianchi identity](bianchi-identity.md) then implies the [Yang-Mills equations](yang-mills-equations.md), and the action saturates the positive-charge [Yang-Mills instanton Bogomolny bound](yang-mills-instanton-bogomolny-bound.md).
