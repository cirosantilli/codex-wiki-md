# Irreducible uniform cover

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform cover](uniform-cover.md)

A uniform cover is irreducible when it is not the disjoint union, as a multiset, of two nonempty uniform covers. There are only finitely many irreducible uniform covers of a fixed finite set: their multiplicity vectors form an [antichain](antichain.md) in $\mathbb N^{2^n}$, and [Dickson lemma](dickson-s-lemma.md) forbids an infinite antichain there.
