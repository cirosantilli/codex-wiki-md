# Invertible tensor powers span the Schur algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Schur algebra](schur-algebra.md)

The commutant of the [permutation](permutation.md) action on $V^{\otimes n}$ identifies with the [symmetric tensors](symmetric-tensor.md) in $\operatorname{End}(V)^{\otimes n}$. [Polarization spanning of symmetric tensors](polarization-spanning-of-symmetric-tensors.md) spans it by $a^{\otimes n}$. Interpolating $(a+tI)^{\otimes n}$ at $n+1$ values where $a+tI$ is invertible expresses each such power using invertible ones. Hence the [Schur algebra](schur-algebra.md) is the linear span of the diagonal general linear action.
