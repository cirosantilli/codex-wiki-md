# Nanoparticle ink

↑ **Parent:** [Nanoparticle](nanoparticle.md)

A processable dispersion of [nanoparticles](nanoparticle.md) with a [solvent](solvent.md) and any stabilizers or binders. For a metallic printed conductor, drying, [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) removal and consolidation must turn the deposited particles into a connected low-resistance structure without damaging the substrate.
