# Sine trial bound for a linear potential on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md)

On $(0,L)$ with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), the normalized trial $\sqrt{2/L}\sin(\pi x/L)$ has kinetic [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) $\pi^2/L^2$ and mean position $L/2$ by reflection symmetry. The [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md) therefore gives the displayed upper bound for a real linear potential. For $\epsilon\geq0$ the lower bound $\lambda_1\geq\pi^2/L^2$ follows from the [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md). For $\epsilon\ne0$ the trial is not an exact eigenfunction, so the upper bound is strict.
