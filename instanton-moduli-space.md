# Instanton moduli space

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills instanton](yang-mills-instanton.md)

For a fixed [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md), orientation and instanton sign, quotient the [Yang-Mills instanton](yang-mills-instanton.md) solutions by [gauge equivalence of principal connections](gauge-equivalence-of-principal-connections.md). Boundary or framing conditions determine the gauge group $\mathcal G$ on a noncompact base. At an anti-self-dual solution, infinitesimal deformations obey $P_+D_Aa=0$ modulo $a\mapsto a+D_A\varepsilon$. Combining this with $D_A^*a=0$ gives an elliptic deformation operator. This description does not assert that every quotient is a smooth manifold: reducible connections can have nontrivial stabilizers.
