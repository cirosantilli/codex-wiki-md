# Sound pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_pressure)

[Sound pressure](sound-pressure.md) is the deviation of [pressure](pressure-split.md) from the undisturbed background. In [linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md), it is small compared with the background [pressure](pressure-split.md); a [microphone](microphone.md) measures a response to this varying [pressure](pressure-split.md), rather than directly recording fluid [displacement](displacement.md).
