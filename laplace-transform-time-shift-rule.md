# Laplace transform time-shift rule

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform shift theorem](laplace-transform-shift-theorem.md)

The delayed unit step has

$$
\mathcal L\{H(t-t_0)\}(s)=\frac{e^{-st_0}}s,
\qquad \operatorname{Re}s>0.
$$
