# Left-invariant differential form

↑ **Parent:** [Left and right translation on a Lie group](left-and-right-translation-on-a-lie-group.md)

A differential form $\alpha$ on a [Lie group](lie-group.md) is left-invariant when $L_g^*\alpha=\alpha$ for every $g$. It is determined by its value at the identity.
