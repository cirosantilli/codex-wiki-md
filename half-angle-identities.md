# Half-angle identities

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)

These identities supply the formulas used in the [tangent half-angle substitution](tangent-half-angle-substitution.md). The half-angle identities include

$$
\tan\frac\theta2=\frac{1-\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}
=\frac{\sin\theta}{1+\cos\theta}.
$$
