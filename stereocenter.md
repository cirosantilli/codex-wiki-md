# Stereocenter

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereocenter)

At a [stereocenter](stereocenter.md), exchanging two attached groups produces a different spatial configuration. The open-chain [ribose](ribose.md) and [glucose](glucose.md) examples have [carbon](carbon.md) [stereocenters](stereocenter.md) bearing four different groups; C2 of [deoxyribose](deoxyribose.md) has two identical [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) substituents and is not a [stereocenter](stereocenter.md). See [Moss, Basic Terminology of Stereochemistry, IUPAC Recommendations 1996: stereogenic units](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/stereo/RS.html#stereogenic).
