# Solid stress

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum mechanics](continuum-mechanics-split.md)

[solid stress](solid-stress.md) is stress carried by solid constituents of a tissue or material. Compressive solid stress, interstitial fluid pressure and elastic stiffness are distinct quantities that can coexist in a tumour.
