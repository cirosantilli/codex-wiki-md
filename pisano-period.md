# Pisano period

↑ **Parent:** [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md)

The Pisano period $\pi(m)$ is the period of the [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md) sequence in [modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic-split.md) modulo $m$, with $F_0=0$ and $F_1=1$. Consecutive residues evolve by the invertible update $(a,b)\mapsto(b,a+b)$ on a [finite set](finite-set.md), so the pair sequence returns to $(0,1)$ and is periodic from the start.
