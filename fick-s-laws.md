<h1 id="fick-s-laws">Fick's laws</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Flux](flux.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fick's_laws)

Fick's laws describe transport down a concentration [gradient](gradient.md) and the resulting change of concentration with time. Combining [Fick's first law](fick-s-first-law.md) with a [conservation law](conservation-law.md) gives the [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md).
