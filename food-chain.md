# Food chain

↑ **Parent:** [Food web](food-web.md)

A [food chain](food-chain.md) orders species so that each consumer feeds on the level below and may be eaten by the level above. Basal resource growth, consumer mortality, conversion efficiency and density regulation determine whether all levels can persist.
