# Spectrum of the round sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md)

For the positive [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) on the unit [sphere](sphere.md) $S^d$, with $d\geq1$, the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $n(n+d-1)$ for integers $n\geq0$. Their [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md) consist of restrictions of degree-$n$ [harmonic polynomials](harmonic-polynomial.md) and have dimensions $\binom{n+d}{d}-\binom{n+d-2}{d}$, with the second term zero for $n<2$. The [Laplacian in polar coordinates](laplacian-in-polar-coordinates.md) gives the eigenvalue; [Fischer decomposition](harmonic-decomposition-of-homogeneous-polynomials.md) and the [Stone-Weierstrass theorem](stone-weierstrass-theorem.md) prove completeness.
