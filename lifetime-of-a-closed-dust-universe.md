# Lifetime of a closed dust universe

↑ **Parent:** [Closed matter-dominated Friedmann solution](closed-matter-dominated-friedmann-solution.md)

The cycloidal [closed matter-dominated Friedmann solution](closed-matter-dominated-friedmann-solution.md) reaches its maximum [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) at $u=\pi$ and the [Big Crunch](big-crunch.md) at $u=2\pi$. Thus its total lifetime is twice its time of maximum expansion. The displayed formula uses a presently expanding normalization with $H_0>0$ and $a_0=1$; the remaining time is this lifetime minus the [age of a closed dust universe](age-of-a-closed-dust-universe.md).
