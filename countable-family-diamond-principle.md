# Countable-family diamond principle

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary diamond at a regular cardinal](stationary-diamond-at-a-regular-cardinal.md)

At each $\alpha\in S$, a countable family $E_\alpha\subseteq\mathcal P(\alpha)$ is supplied. Every $X\subseteq\lambda$ has $X\cap\alpha\in E_\alpha$ on a stationary [subset](subset.md) of $S$. The [countable-family diamond equivalence](countable-family-diamond-equivalence.md) shows that this apparently weaker prediction is equivalent to a single diamond guess at each index.
