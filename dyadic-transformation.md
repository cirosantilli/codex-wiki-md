# Dyadic transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Devaney chaos](devaney-chaos.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyadic_transformation)

The doubling map on the unit circle is

$$
F(x)=2x\pmod1.
$$

It is chaotic in Devaney's sense.
