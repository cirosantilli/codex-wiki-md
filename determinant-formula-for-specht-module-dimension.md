# Determinant formula for Specht module dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md)

For a partition $\lambda\vdash n$ with $k$ positive parts, the number of [standard Young tableaux](standard-young-tableau.md), and hence the dimension of its complex [Specht module](specht-module.md), is $n!\det(1/(\lambda_i-i+j)!)$. Negative factorial arguments give zero entries. Setting $L_i=\lambda_i+k-i$ turns the determinant into $\prod_{i<j}(L_i-L_j)/\prod_iL_i!$ by the [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md), matching the [hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md).
