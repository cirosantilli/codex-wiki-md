# SDS-PAGE

↑ **Parent:** [Gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SDS-PAGE)

An analytical form of [gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md) using an anionic [surfactant](surfactant.md) and a [polymer](polymer.md) gel to separate [proteins](protein.md) predominantly by chain size under denaturing conditions. [Western blot separation](western-blot-separation.md) often uses [SDS-PAGE](sds-page.md); the resulting apparent [mass](mass.md) and subsequent [antibody](antibody.md) binding provide complementary, incomplete evidence about identity.
