# Duals of a subspace and its quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Transpose of a linear map](transpose-of-a-linear-map.md)

For a finite-dimensional vector space and $U\leq V$, dualizing the quotient and inclusion maps gives

$$
(V/U)^*\cong U^\circ,
\qquad
U^*\cong V^*/U^\circ.
$$
