# Membrane fission

↑ **Parent:** [Cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md)

[Membrane fission](membrane-fission.md) separates a connected membrane into distinct components, for example by severing a vesicle neck. Bending a membrane into a bud without severing that neck is not yet fission.
