# Dissipative Cayley-transform contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Dissipative operator](dissipative-operator.md)

If $\operatorname{Re}\langle Lv,v\rangle\leq0$ in a finite-dimensional Hilbert space, $I-hL/2$ is invertible for every positive $h$. The relation $v_+-v_-=(h/2)L(v_++v_-)$ gives $\|v_+\|^2-\|v_-\|^2=(h/2)\operatorname{Re}\langle L(v_++v_-),v_++v_-\rangle\leq0$. Thus the [Crank-Nicolson method](crank-nicolson-method.md) contracts for such a linear system even when $L$ is a [non-normal matrix](non-normal-matrix.md). This is a direct energy result, not an unqualified inequality by a scalar rational function evaluated at a logarithmic norm.
