# Christoffel symbols of a spatially flat FLRW metric

↑ **Parent:** [Spatially flat FLRW metric](spatially-flat-flrw-metric.md)

For coordinates $(t,x,y,z)$ and line element $ds^2=c^2dt^2-a^2(t)d\mathbf x^2$, these are the nonzero [Christoffel symbols](christoffel-symbol.md). The prime means differentiation with respect to $t$, and coordinate zero is $t$, not $ct$. They follow either from the metric-derivative formula or the [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) for the affine [geodesic Lagrangian](geodesic-lagrangian.md). Both mixed lower-index orders must be counted to obtain the factor of two in the spatial [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md).
