# Pointwise classification of a second-order partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order partial differential equation](second-order-partial-differential-equation.md)

For $Au_{xx}+2Bu_{xy}+Cu_{yy}+\cdots=0$, the sign of $\Delta=B^2-AC$ distinguishes a [hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) when $\Delta>0$, an [elliptic partial differential equation](elliptic-partial-differential-equation.md) when $\Delta<0$, and a [parabolic partial differential equation](parabolic-partial-differential-equation.md) when $\Delta=0$ and the [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) has rank one. If $A=B=C=0$, the second-order [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) has rank zero and the differential order drops; this fully degenerate case must be checked separately for compatibility with the lower-order terms and forcing.
