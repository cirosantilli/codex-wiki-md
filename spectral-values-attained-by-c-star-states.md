# Spectral values attained by C-star states

↑ **Parent:** [State on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md)

On the linear span of $1,x$, define $g(a1+bx)=a+b\lambda$. Affine spectral mapping gives $|a+b\lambda|\leq\|a1+bx\|$, making this well-defined with norm one and $g(1)=1$. A norm-preserving [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) extension to the whole [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) remains unital; the norm characterization of a [positive functional on a C-star algebra](positive-functional-on-a-c-star-algebra.md) makes it a [state on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md) attaining the spectral value.
