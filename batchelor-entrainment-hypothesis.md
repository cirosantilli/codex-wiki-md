# Batchelor entrainment hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent plume](turbulent-plume-split.md)

The [Batchelor entrainment hypothesis](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md) takes the inward mean edge velocity of a [turbulent plume](turbulent-plume-split.md) to be $v_e=\alpha w$ in the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md). Its density-corrected non-Boussinesq form is $v_e=\alpha w\sqrt{\rho/\rho_0}$, where $\rho$ and $\rho_0$ are plume and ambient [mass densities](density.md) and $\alpha$ is the [entrainment coefficient](entrainment-coefficient.md).
