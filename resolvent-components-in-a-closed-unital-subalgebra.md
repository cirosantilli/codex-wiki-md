# Resolvent components in a closed unital subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md)

For a [closed](closed-set.md) [unital](unital-algebra.md) [subalgebra](subalgebra.md) $B$ of a [unital](unital-algebra.md) [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) $A$ and $x\in B$, the smaller-algebra [algebra resolvent set](resolvent-set-of-a-banach-algebra-element.md) is relatively [open](open-set.md) and [closed](closed-set.md) in the larger one. Openness follows from the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md); closedness follows from [continuity](continuous-function.md) of inversion in $A$ and [norm](norm.md) closedness of $B$. Every larger-algebra resolvent component is therefore either retained whole or absorbed into the smaller-algebra [Banach algebra spectrum](spectrum-of-an-element.md). The unbounded component is always retained, since inverses at large modulus are norm-convergent series in $x$.
