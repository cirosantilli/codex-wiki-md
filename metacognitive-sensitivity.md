# Metacognitive sensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Subjective confidence](subjective-confidence.md)

The ability of confidence to distinguish a person's correct from incorrect responses, or to rank items by later success. It is distinct from [metacognitive calibration](metacognitive-calibration.md). A [type 2 receiver operating characteristic](type-2-receiver-operating-characteristic.md) measures discrimination in a binary-correctness task; other metamemory designs may need other measures.
