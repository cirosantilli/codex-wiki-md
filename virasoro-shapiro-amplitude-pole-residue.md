<h1 id="virasoro-shapiro-amplitude-pole-residue">Virasoro–Shapiro amplitude pole residue</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro–Shapiro amplitude](virasoro-shapiro-amplitude.md)

For $a=-1-\alpha's/4$, $b=-1-\alpha't/4$ and $a+b+c=1$, the amplitude has generic [simple poles](simple-pole.md) at $a=-n$. The [gamma function](gamma-function.md) [residue](residue.md) and recurrence give

$$
\operatorname*{Res}_{s=s_n}A^{(4)}=-\frac{8\pi g_s^2}{\alpha'(n!)^2}\prod_{j=1}^n(b-j)^2.
$$

The [residue](residue.md) polynomial has degree $2n$, consistent with closed-string exchange up to spin $2n$. When $a=-n+\delta_a$ and $b=-m+\delta_b$, the reciprocal factor $1/\Gamma(a+b)$ removes a putative double pole: the leading ratio is $\binom{n+m}{n}^2(1/\delta_a+1/\delta_b)$. Thus channel singularities must be counted using the kinematic constraint, not by multiplying independent numerator poles.
