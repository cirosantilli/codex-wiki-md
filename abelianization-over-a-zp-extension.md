# Abelianization over a Zp-extension

↑ **Parent:** [Coinvariant module](coinvariant-module.md)

Suppose $1\to X\to\mathcal G\to\Gamma\to1$ is an exact sequence of [pro-p groups](pro-p-group.md), with $X$ abelian and $\Gamma\cong\mathbb Z_p$. Conjugation gives $X$ a [compact Galois module](compact-galois-module.md) structure. The closed [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is $(\gamma-1)X$: its image is closed by compactness, and after quotienting by it, a lift of $\gamma$ centralizes $X$ and topologically generates the remaining quotient. Hence $0\to X_\Gamma\to\mathcal G^{\mathrm{ab}}\to\Gamma\to0$ is exact.
