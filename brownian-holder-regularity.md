<h1 id="brownian-holder-regularity">Brownian Hölder regularity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

On every compact time interval, a Brownian path is almost surely a [Hölder continuous function](holder-condition.md) of every exponent $\alpha<1/2$, and of no exponent $\alpha\geq1/2$. In particular, $B_t=o(t^\gamma)$ as $t\downarrow0$ for every $\gamma<1/2$ after choosing a Hölder exponent strictly between $\gamma$ and $1/2$.
