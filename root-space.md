# Root space

↑ **Parent:** [Root-space decomposition](root-space-decomposition.md)

For a root $\alpha$, its root space is the simultaneous eigenspace

$$
\mathfrak g_\alpha=\{x:[h,x]=\alpha(h)x\text{ for every }h\in\mathfrak t\}
$$

of the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of the [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md).
