# Endomorphism-induced tensor derivation

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor derivation](tensor-derivation.md)

A smooth [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) $A$ of the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) defines the [tensor derivation](tensor-derivation.md) $D_A$ by $D_Af=0$ and $D_AY=A(Y)$. On a [differential one-form](one-form.md), $D_A\omega=-\omega\circ A$. On a general [tensor field](tensor-field.md), it acts by $A$ in each vector factor and by the negative dual action in each covector factor. The two actions cancel in each contracted pairing, proving compatibility with [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md).
