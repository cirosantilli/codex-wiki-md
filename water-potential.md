# Water potential

↑ **Parent:** [Osmosis](osmosis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_potential)

[Water potential](water-potential.md) expresses the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) of [water](water.md) relative to a stated pure-[water](water.md) reference in units of [pressure](pressure-split.md). For a nearly incompressible liquid at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), use a constant reference [molar volume](molar-volume.md) $\bar V_w$ and write

$$
\Psi=\frac{\mu_w-\mu_w^{\mathrm{ref}}}{\bar V_w}.
$$

The reference is pure [water](water.md) at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) and a chosen [pressure](pressure-split.md) $P_0$. Both sides must use the same reference. Neglecting height differences and interactions with solid surfaces, the approximation is

$$
\mu_w\simeq\mu_w^{\mathrm{ref}}+RT\ln a_w+\bar V_w(P-P_0),\qquad
\Psi\simeq(P-P_0)+\frac{RT}{\bar V_w}\ln a_w=\Psi_p+\Psi_s.
$$

Here $a_w$ is the dimensionless [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) of [water](water.md), $R$ the [molar gas constant](molar-gas-constant.md), and $T$ the absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md). The [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) is therefore applied to a dimensionless quantity. The [pressure](pressure-split.md) contribution $\Psi_p=P-P_0$ increases with [pressure](pressure-split.md); the [solute potential](solute-potential.md) $\Psi_s$ is negative when $a_w<1$. Across an available passive water route, net [water](water.md) transfer favors higher to lower [water potential](water-potential.md). The familiar [plant](plant.md) description adds effects of height and interactions with solid surfaces when they matter; they are omitted in the two liquid compartments here.[Biology 2e, §30.5, Water Potential, Solute Potential, Pressure Potential and Figure 30.32: component balance and direction of transfer](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/30-5-transport-of-water-and-solutes-in-plants.md)

Adding [solute](solute.md) and raising [pressure](pressure-split.md) can thus cancel in [water potential](water-potential.md) while each contribution remains nonzero. Equality of [water potential](water-potential.md) does not imply equality of [pressure](pressure-split.md) or of every [solute](solute.md) [concentration](concentration.md). In a supported [plant](plant.md) cell, the [cell wall](cell-wall.md) permits a positive [turgor pressure](turgor-pressure.md) to balance a negative [solute potential](solute-potential.md); [plasmolysis](plasmolysis.md) illustrates the opposite loss of support after [water](water.md) leaves.
