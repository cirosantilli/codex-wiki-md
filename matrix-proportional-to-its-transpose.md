# Matrix proportional to its transpose

↑ **Parent:** [Transpose](transpose.md)

For a [matrix](matrix.md) over the [real numbers](real-number.md) or [complex numbers](complex-number.md), applying the [transpose](transpose.md) twice to $A=\lambda A^T$ gives $A=\lambda^2 A$. A nonzero matrix is therefore possible only for $\lambda=1$ or $\lambda=-1$. These cases are precisely the [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) and [skew-symmetric matrices](skew-symmetric-matrix.md), respectively. For every other scalar, the only solution is the zero matrix.
