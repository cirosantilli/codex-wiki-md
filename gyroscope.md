# Gyroscope

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyroscope)

A rotating instrument whose nearly symmetric rotor provides a direction reference through its [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). In an inertial frame the spin changes according to the applied [torque](torque.md), so negligible torque approximately preserves its direction. An ideal freely falling test gyroscope in [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) has its spin undergo [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) along its [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md). Comparing that direction with distant reference axes measures [Lense-Thirring precession](lense-thirring-precession.md); the direction need not remain fixed in arbitrary rotating coordinates.
