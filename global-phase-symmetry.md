# Global phase symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Global symmetry in field theory](global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md)

An internal [circle group](circle-group.md) action with spacetime-independent parameter $\alpha$. For canonical [complex scalar fields](complex-scalar-field.md), [Noether's theorem](noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) gives $j^\mu=i\sum_jq_j\varphi_j^\dagger\overleftrightarrow{\partial^\mu}\varphi_j$. The [Noether charge](noether-charge.md) of the [particle](particle-physics.md) annihilated by $\varphi_j$ is $q_j$, and its [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) has charge $-q_j$.
