# Hubble friction

↑ **Parent:** [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md)

Hubble friction is the damping term proportional to the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) in a field or fluid equation in an expanding universe. A homogeneous scalar field, for example, has the term $3H\dot\phi$ in its equation of motion.
