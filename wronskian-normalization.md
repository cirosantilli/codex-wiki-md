# Wronskian normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Mode expansion of a free field](mode-expansion-of-a-free-field.md)

The [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) fixes the antisymmetric product of a mode function $f$ and its canonical-momentum mode $g$. In the convention $[a,a^\dagger]=1$, one commonly has $fg^*-f^*g=i$.
