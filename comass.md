# Comass

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form](differential-form-split.md)

The comass is the operator norm of a differential form restricted to simple unit tangent multivectors. Reversing orientation gives the equivalent two-sided bound. It differs from the Euclidean norm of all the form's components. The [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) bound uses comass because tangent planes are represented by simple multivectors.
