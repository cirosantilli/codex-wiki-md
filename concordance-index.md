# Concordance index

↑ **Parent:** [Survival analysis](survival-analysis-split.md)

A survival [concordance index](concordance-index.md) measures agreement between predicted risk ordering and observed event ordering over comparable subject pairs, with half credit for prediction ties. A pair is ordinarily comparable when the earlier observed follow-up ends in an event. Censoring-adjusted versions can reduce dependence on follow-up patterns. Good discrimination does not imply good [survival prediction calibration](survival-prediction-calibration.md) or correct proportional hazards.
