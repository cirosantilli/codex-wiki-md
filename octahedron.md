# Octahedron

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octahedron)

An [octahedron](octahedron.md) is a [polyhedron](polyhedron.md) with eight faces. The regular [octahedron](octahedron.md) has six vertices, twelve equal edges and eight equilateral triangular faces. One realization has vertices $\pm\mathbf e_1,\pm\mathbf e_2,\pm\mathbf e_3$; two vertices are adjacent exactly when they are not an opposite pair. Its edge graph is the [octahedral graph](octahedral-graph.md).
