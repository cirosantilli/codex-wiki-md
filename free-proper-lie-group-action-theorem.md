# Free proper Lie-group action theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Principal bundle](principal-bundle.md)

If a [Lie group](lie-group.md) acts smoothly, freely, and properly on a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) $P$, then the quotient $P/G$ has a unique smooth structure for which $P\to P/G$ is a [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md). An action by a compact Lie group is automatically proper.
