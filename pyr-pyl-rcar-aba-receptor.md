# PYR-PYL-RCAR ABA receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Receptor (biochemistry)](receptor-biochemistry.md)

A family of soluble [abscisic acid](abscisic-acid.md) receptors. ABA-bound receptor complexes inhibit selected clade-A [protein phosphatase 2C](protein-phosphatase-2c.md) enzymes, allowing ABA-responsive kinases to remain active.
