# Pressure similarity in a spherical blast wave

↑ **Parent:** [Self-similar blast wave](self-similar-blast-wave.md)

For a spherical [self-similar blast wave](self-similar-blast-wave.md), put $\ell=(Et^2/\rho_0)^{1/5}$, $\xi=r/\ell$, $\rho=\rho_0f_\rho(\xi)$ and $u=(r/t)f_u(\xi)$. The radial [Euler equation](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) then requires $p=\rho_0(\ell/t)^2f_p(\xi)$, where

$$
f_p'=-\xi f_\rho\left[f_u^2-f_u+\xi\left(f_u-\frac25\right)f_u'\right].
$$

This follows from $\partial_t\xi=-2\xi/(5t)$ and $\partial_r\xi=1/\ell$. The momentum equation alone leaves a spatially uniform additive [pressure](pressure-split.md) undetermined. The scale-free strong-shock boundary data fix that freedom consistently with the displayed similarity scaling; an independently prescribed ambient pressure introduces another scale.
