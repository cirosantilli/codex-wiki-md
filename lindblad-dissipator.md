# Lindblad dissipator

↑ **Parent:** [Lindblad equation](lindblad-equation.md)

A [Lindblad dissipator](lindblad-dissipator.md) is the non-Hamiltonian generator associated with a [Lindblad operator](lindblad-operator.md) $V$. It preserves the [trace](matrix-trace.md) because cyclicity gives $\operatorname{Tr}(V\rho V^\dagger)=\operatorname{Tr}(V^\dagger V\rho)$, cancelling the two [anticommutator](anticommutator.md) terms. It preserves Hermiticity and satisfies $\mathcal D[V](I)=[V,V^\dagger]$; it need not preserve the identity or the [purity of a density operator](purity-of-a-density-operator.md).
