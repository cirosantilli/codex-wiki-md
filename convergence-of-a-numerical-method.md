# Convergence of a numerical method

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

For an [initial value problem](initial-value-problem.md), convergence means that the largest [global error](global-discretization-error.md) on each fixed finite time interval tends to zero as the step size tends to zero and the starting errors vanish. For [linear multistep methods](linear-multistep-method.md), the [Dahlquist equivalence theorem](dahlquist-equivalence-theorem.md) separates this requirement into [consistency of a numerical method](consistency-of-a-numerical-method.md) and [zero-stability](zero-stability.md).
