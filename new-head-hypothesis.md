# New Head hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Developmental biology](developmental-biology.md)

The hypothesis that integration of [neural crest](neural-crest.md), [ectodermal placodes](ectodermal-placode.md) and other cranial tissues helped enable vertebrate head innovation and more active feeding. Developmental necessity, morphological diversification and ancestral ecological reconstruction are different evidential claims.
