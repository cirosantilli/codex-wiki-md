# Cancellation of positive-integer gamma singularities on a Hankel contour

↑ **Parent:** [Residue extraction by a Hankel contour](residue-extraction-by-a-hankel-contour.md)

Keep the circular portion of a [Hankel contour](hankel-contour.md) at fixed nonzero radius. Its numerator $I(z)=\int_Ht^{z-1}e^t\,dt$ is an [entire function](entire-function.md) of $z$, because the contour avoids zero and its tails decay exponentially, uniformly on compact parameter sets. At a positive integer $n$, the integrand is single-valued and entire in $t$, so the bank integrals cancel and the circular integral is zero. Thus $I(n)=0$, canceling the simple zero of $2i\sin(\pi z)$ in the Hankel representation of the [gamma function](gamma-function.md). At a nonpositive integer $-n$, the circular integral instead gives $2\pi i/n!$, yielding residue $(-1)^n/n!$.
