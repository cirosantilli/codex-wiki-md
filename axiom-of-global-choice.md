# Axiom of global choice

↑ **Parent:** [Axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiom_of_global_choice)

A single class [function](function-split.md) chooses one member of every nonempty [set](set-split.md). In the usual class theory with [replacement](axiom-schema-of-replacement.md), it is equivalent to a set-like global [well-ordering](well-order.md) of the universe. Ordinary [choice](axiom-of-choice.md) asserts choice only for each set-sized family separately.
