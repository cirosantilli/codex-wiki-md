# Resonance hybrid

↑ **Parent:** [Resonance (chemistry)](resonance-chemistry.md)

A [resonance hybrid](resonance-hybrid.md) is the combined electronic description expressed using several [resonance structures](resonance-structure.md). The word does not describe a mixture of separate [molecules](molecule-split.md) or [ions](ion.md): a [nitrate ion](nitrate.md) has one [electron density](electron-density.md), rather than three species in [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md). Equal weighting is appropriate for the equivalent [nitrate](nitrate.md) contributors in the elementary model; the unequal [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) contributors illustrate why it is not a general rule. See [Chemistry 2e, 7.4: resonance hybrids do not fluctuate between forms](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-4-formal-charges-and-resonance).
