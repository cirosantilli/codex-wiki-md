# Hydrogen passivation of magnesium acceptors in gallium nitride

↑ **Parent:** [Acceptor impurity](acceptor-impurity.md)

In [gallium nitride](gallium-nitride.md), incorporated [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) can neutralize [magnesium](magnesium.md) [acceptor impurities](acceptor-impurity.md). Activation treatments restore electrical activity; adding [magnesium](magnesium.md) alone does not establish a mobile [electron hole](electron-hole.md) population.
