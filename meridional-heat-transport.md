# Meridional heat transport

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transport](heat-transport.md)

The component of [heat transport](heat-transport.md) crossing a latitude. A poleward transport is northward in the Northern Hemisphere and southward in the Southern Hemisphere. Equal and opposite volume transports can carry net [heat](heat-split.md) when the poleward branch is warmer. For [eddies](eddy.md), the corresponding transport is determined by the correlation of meridional [velocity](velocity.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md) anomalies, closely related to [eddy buoyancy flux](eddy-buoyancy-flux.md) in a thermal [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md).
