# Israel junction conditions

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

Across a thin timelike matter layer, the induced metric is continuous while the jump in its [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md) is determined by the surface [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md). Choose one common spacelike normal and $K_{ab}=\tfrac12\partial_yh_{ab}$ in Gaussian normal coordinates. The singular tangential [Einstein tensor](einstein-tensor.md) is $-([K_{ab}]-h_{ab}[K])\delta(y)$; equating it to $\kappa_D^2S_{ab}\delta(y)$ gives the displayed conditions. Reversing the normal or the sign convention for [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md) changes the corresponding jump signs consistently. These conditions determine the cosmological expansion law of a thin gravitating [brane](brane.md).
