# Jahn-Teller stabilization energy

↑ **Parent:** [Jahn-Teller effect](jahn-teller-effect.md)

The positive energy reduction from a symmetry-lowering [Jahn-Teller effect](jahn-teller-effect.md), measured from the undistorted reference configuration. In the harmonic linear-coupling model $E(Q)=-\alpha Q+\beta Q^2/2$, with $\alpha,\beta>0$, completing the square gives $Q_0=\alpha/\beta$ and $E(Q_0)=-\alpha^2/(2\beta)$. Relating this local energy directly to a cooperative transition temperature is an extra approximation.
