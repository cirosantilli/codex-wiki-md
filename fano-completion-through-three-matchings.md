# Fano completion through three matchings

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph link graph](hypergraph-link-graph.md)

If $xyz$ is a [hyperedge](hyperedge.md) and the links of $x,y,z$ on four other vertices contain the three perfect matchings of a $K_4$, then those seven vertices contain a [Fano plane](fano-plane.md). There is a second completion: if the link of $v$ contains three disjoint pairs, and the other six vertices contain all transversal triples through those pairs, take four triples of even parity and the three triples containing $v$. This again gives a Fano plane. These finite templates convert forbidden Fano copies into missing cross-part triples.
