# Chang theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Large spectrum](large-spectrum.md)

Chang's theorem says that $\operatorname{Spec}_\rho(1_A)$ is contained in the span of a [dissociated set](dissociated-set.md) of size $O(\rho^{-2}\log(\alpha^{-1}))$.
