# Anamorphic magnification of a grating

↑ **Parent:** [Grating equation](grating-equation.md)

At fixed [wavelength](wavelength.md), the [grating equation](grating-equation.md) gives $d\beta/d\alpha=-\cos\alpha/\cos\beta$. Thus an incident angular width is magnified by $\cos\alpha/\cos\beta$ in the dispersion direction. Including the ratio of camera and [collimator](collimator.md) [focal lengths](focal-length.md) gives the projected slit width. The [ESO B&C operating manual, Appendix A](https://www.eso.org/sci/libraries/historicaldocuments/Operating_Manuals/Operating_Manual_No.9_A1b.pdf) explicitly includes this anamorphic factor.
