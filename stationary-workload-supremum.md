# Stationary workload supremum

↑ **Parent:** [Workload of a queue](workload-of-a-queue.md)

For [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) arrivals with finite mean below service $c$, the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) makes the net cumulative input tend to minus infinity, so the supremum is finite [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). Applying the same formula to the past input gives a stationary solution of the [Lindley recursion](lindley-recursion.md). This representation turns [queue workload](workload-of-a-queue.md) overflow into an event involving some lookback horizon.
