# Adsorbed polymer loop

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer adsorption](polymer-adsorption.md)

A nonadsorbed chain section whose two ends connect to surface-bound portions of the same adsorbed [polymer](polymer-split.md). Loops extend into the [solvent](solvent.md) and contribute to the thickness, [steric repulsion](steric-repulsion.md) and [conformational entropy](conformational-entropy.md) of an adsorbed layer.
