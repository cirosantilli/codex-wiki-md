# Legendre prime-counting formula

↑ **Parent:** [Prime-counting function](prime-counting-function.md)

For $a=\pi(\sqrt x)$, let $p_1,\ldots,p_a$ be the [primes](prime-number.md) at most $\sqrt x$. The [inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) counts unsieved positive [integers](integer.md) by $\phi(x,a)=\sum_S(-1)^{|S|}\lfloor x/\prod_{i\in S}p_i\rfloor$. These are $1$ and the remaining [primes](prime-number.md), since every [composite number](composite-number.md) has a [prime factor](prime-factor.md) at most its square root. Hence $\pi(x)=\phi(x,a)+a-1$ for $x\geq2$. This is a prime-counting identity, distinct from the [Legendre formula](legendre-s-formula.md) for factorial valuations.
