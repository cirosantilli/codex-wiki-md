# Nevanlinna theory

↑ **Parent:** [Meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevanlinna_theory)

Nevanlinna theory studies the growth and value distribution of [meromorphic functions](meromorphic-function.md) through their integrated pole counts and logarithmic proximity to target values. The [Nevanlinna characteristic](nevanlinna-characteristic.md) combines both quantities, while the [Nevanlinna first main theorem](nevanlinna-first-main-theorem.md) compares it with the corresponding counts and proximity for any fixed target.
