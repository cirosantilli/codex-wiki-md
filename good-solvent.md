# Good solvent

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer solution](polymer-solution.md)

A [good solvent](good-solvent.md) favors contact with [polymer](polymer-split.md) segments strongly enough that the effective [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md) is positive. A long dilute flexible chain swells relative to an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md); the three-dimensional size exponent is approximately $0.588$, often replaced by $3/5$ in a scaling estimate. This designation depends on the polymer, solvent and [temperature](temperature-split.md), rather than on the solvent alone.
