The distinction illuminates both a route to the [Lorentz transformation](../../../lorentz-transformation.md) and the explanatory scope of [special relativity](../../../special-relativity-split.md). In a [constructive theory](../../../constructive-theory.md), one models constituent systems and their interactions to recover observable behavior. A [principle theory](../../../principle-theory.md) instead imposes general empirical constraints on whatever microscopic models may be admissible. [Albert Einstein](../../../albert-einstein.md) explicitly contrasted these strategies in 1919, using molecular [kinetic theory](../../../kinetic-theory-split.md), [thermodynamics](../../../thermodynamics-split.md) and relativity as examples.[discussion of constructive theories, principle-theories and the classification of relativity](../../../what-is-the-theory-of-relativity-by-albert-einstein.md)

The [postulates of special relativity](../../../postulates-of-special-relativity.md) constrain coordinates calibrated by rods, clocks and [Einstein synchronisation](../../../einstein-synchronisation.md). For a boost along $x$, spacetime homogeneity makes the transformation affine, while the world line $x=vt$ of the moving origin gives $x'=A(v)(x-vt)$ after choosing coincident origins. Preservation of the two [light](../../../light.md) rays $x=\pm ct$ then requires

$$
t'=A(v)\left(t-\frac{vx}{c^2}\right).
$$

Reciprocity together with spatial [isotropy](../../../isotropy.md) gives $A(-v)=A(v)$ and

$$
A(v)^2(1-v^2/c^2)=1.
$$

Continuity and the usual orientation convention select $A(v)=\gamma>0$. Thus a pair of empirical principles, supplemented by homogeneity, [isotropy](../../../isotropy.md) and coordinate conventions, yields the [Lorentz transformation](../../../lorentz-transformation.md) without a microscopic calculation of a particular clock. [Einstein's principle of relativity](../../../einstein-s-principle-of-relativity.md) alone is insufficient: the [Galilean transformation](../../../galilean-transformation.md) also realizes a relativity principle, with no finite invariant [speed](../../../speed.md).[sections 2–3: the two principles and the transformation of coordinates](../../../on-the-electrodynamics-of-moving-bodies-by-albert-einstein.md)

The distinction also tells us what has not been derived. A [principle theory](../../../principle-theory.md) constrains all acceptable microscopic laws and the measurements they permit. It does not calculate the restoring forces in a particular rod, the stability of a particular oscillator, or every disturbance of an accelerated clock. Those are questions for detailed dynamics. Conversely, a [constructive theory](../../../constructive-theory.md) that successfully treats one electromagnetic clock has not yet established the universal behavior of clocks using other interactions.

The content of [special relativity](../../../special-relativity-split.md) consequently includes more than a coordinate formula: it identifies the common causal and metrical structure and constrains the possible dynamics of physical systems. Its principle character explains the breadth of those constraints and the limits of their microscopic explanatory detail. A constructive realization can complement them. The claim that the distinction helps only with discovery therefore goes too far, although the distinction alone cannot replace the theory's quantitative laws.
