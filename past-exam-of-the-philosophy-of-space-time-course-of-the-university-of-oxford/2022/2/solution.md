<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The crucial distinction is between an observer-independent binary relation of absolute simultaneity and a relation defined relative to an [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md). The [relativity of simultaneity](../../../relativity-of-simultaneity.md) removes the former from the usual structure of [special relativity](../../../special-relativity-split.md); it does not make the latter meaningless or subjective.

For two [spacetime events](../../../event-relativity.md) separated by $(\Delta t,\Delta x)$, a [Lorentz boost](../../../lorentz-boost.md) of [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $v$ gives

$$
\Delta t'=\gamma\left(\Delta t-\frac{v\Delta x}{c^2}\right).
$$

If $\Delta t=0$ and $\Delta x\ne0$, then $\Delta t'\ne0$ for a nonzero boost along their separation. More generally, [spacelike separated](../../../spacelike-separation.md) events can be simultaneous in some [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md), and their time order can reverse between [inertial frames](../../../inertial-frame.md). Distinct [timelike separated](../../../timelike-separation.md) or [null separated](../../../null-separation.md) events cannot be simultaneous in any [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md); their causal order is preserved by time-orientation-preserving [Lorentz transformations](../../../lorentz-transformation.md). These are precise geometric facts, independent of anyone's beliefs.

In [Minkowski simultaneity relative to an inertial observer](../../../minkowski-simultaneity-relative-to-an-inertial-observer.md), the observer is represented by a constant future-directed [four-velocity](../../../four-velocity.md) $u$, normalized by $\eta(u,u)=-c^2$ in [metric signature](../../../metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$. Then

$$
\Delta t_u=-\frac{\eta(u,Q-P)}{c^2},\qquad P\sim_u Q\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \eta(u,Q-P)=0.
$$

For a fixed $u$, this is a perfectly well-defined [equivalence relation](../../../equivalence-relation.md). It can be specified without coordinates. What the [Minkowski metric](../../../minkowski-metric.md) does not supply is a uniquely preferred $u$ and hence a uniquely preferred simultaneity [foliation](../../../foliation.md). This is the strongest defensible interpretation of the claim by [Tim Maudlin](../../../tim-maudlin.md): there is no additional fundamental, frame-independent simultaneity relation in the bare theory.

Calling it merely “relative to an observer” can mislead in two ways. First, distant simultaneity does not depend on when an image is received: travel time of the [light](../../../light.md) signal must be accounted for. Second, two observers at the same [spacetime event](../../../event-relativity.md) but with different [four-velocities](../../../four-velocity.md) assign different simultaneity [hyperplanes](../../../hyperplane.md); the dependence is on state of motion, rather than consciousness. [Einstein synchronisation](../../../einstein-synchronisation.md) operationalizes the standard frame assignment by setting a reflected pulse's remote reading to the midpoint of its sending and returning readings.[section 1: separated clocks and the reflected-light prescription](../../../on-the-electrodynamics-of-moving-bodies-by-albert-einstein.md)

A material system can pick out a useful rest frame, for example a gas in thermal equilibrium. That adds physical information beyond the [Minkowski metric](../../../minkowski-metric.md); it does not provide a universal absolute present. Accordingly, Maudlin's denial is justified if it denies a primitive observer-independent relation, but is too strong if read as denying every objective relation $P\sim_u Q$. The indexed relation and its measurable consequences remain part of the physics.
