<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

[General relativity](../../../general-relativity-split.md) is not required for the ideal [twin paradox](../../../twin-paradox.md) in [Minkowski spacetime](../../../minkowski-spacetime.md). The relevant observable is the difference in [proper times](../../../proper-time.md) recorded by clocks that meet at departure and reunion. For a [world line](../../../world-line.md) $x(t)$ in one [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md), the [clock hypothesis](../../../clock-hypothesis.md) gives

$$
\tau=\int_0^T\sqrt{1-\frac{\dot x(t)^2}{c^2}}\,dt.
$$

The home clock with $x=0$ records $T$. A traveler coasting outward at $v$ for $T/2$ and inward at $-v$ for $T/2$ records $T/\gamma$, in the ideal short-turnaround limit. For $T=10$ years and $v=0.6c$, these readings are ten and eight years. The complete paths, rather than conflicting applications of reciprocal [time dilation](../../../time-dilation.md) to one pair of [inertial frames](../../../inertial-frame.md), determine the invariant difference.

An exactly instantaneous reversal is an idealization, not an essential premise. Replace it by a smooth timelike turnaround. The [proper time](../../../proper-time.md) accumulated in a reversal interval of home duration $\delta t$ lies between zero and $\delta t$, so its contribution vanishes as the interval shrinks. The finite clock difference persists. Repeatedly lengthening the coasting legs while keeping the same reversal maneuver also changes the clock difference; the difference is not a universal function only of the peak [proper acceleration](../../../proper-acceleration.md).

The usual apparent symmetry fails because no single traveler [inertial frame](../../../inertial-frame.md) covers both legs. In the instantaneous rest frame at a traveler event $(t,x(t))$, the home event assigned as simultaneous has time

$$
t_H=t-\frac{v(t)x(t)}{c^2}.
$$

Hence a finite turnaround changes the home simultaneity assignment continuously. In the sharp-turnaround limit at $x=vT/2$, changing $v$ to $-v$ changes that assigned home time by $v^2T/c^2$. This is a change in a distant [relativity of simultaneity](../../../relativity-of-simultaneity.md) assignment, not a sudden physical jump in the home clock's reading. The full [twin paradox simultaneity change](../../../twin-paradox-simultaneity-change.md) reconciles the two inertial coasting descriptions.

An accelerated-coordinate calculation is also available within [special relativity](../../../special-relativity-split.md). Nonzero connection coefficients in that chart do not establish nonzero [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md). Thus neither using accelerated coordinates nor making the traveler accelerate requires the [Einstein field equations](../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md). The [clock hypothesis](../../../clock-hypothesis.md) is a physical assumption about a sufficiently ideal clock, not a guarantee that every real apparatus survives arbitrary acceleration without disturbances. Experiments such as the [muon storage-ring time-dilation experiments](../../../muon-storage-ring-time-dilation-experiment.md) test clock behavior in specified regimes.

Real flights around [Earth](../../../earth.md) also compare different [gravitational potentials](../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) and require gravitational corrections, as in the [twin paradox clock experiments](../../../twin-paradox-clock-experiments.md). Those refinements involve [general relativity](../../../general-relativity-split.md). They do not make the flat-spacetime reunion calculation incomplete on its stated assumptions. Even two mirrored, accelerating travelers can record equal [proper times](../../../proper-time.md); the decisive quantity is the [proper time](../../../proper-time.md) of each entire path between the common events.
