↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The phrase [equivalence principle](../../../equivalence-principle.md) covers several related claims. The [weak equivalence principle](../../../weak-equivalence-principle.md) concerns the universality of [free fall](../../../free-fall.md) for [test particles](../../../test-particle.md) of negligible self-gravity, with the same initial conditions and no relevant nongravitational [forces](../../../force-split.md). In a Newtonian notation,

$$
m_I\mathbf a=m_G\mathbf g
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathbf a=\frac{m_G}{m_I}\mathbf g.
$$

Composition-independent motion requires the ratio of passive [gravitational mass](../../../gravitational-mass.md) to [inertial mass](../../../inertial-mass.md) to be universal. Its common value can then be absorbed into the normalization of $\mathbf g$; the empirical assertion is universality, not the numerical convention used to express the ratio.

The [Einstein equivalence principle](../../../einstein-equivalence-principle.md) includes that universality and extends the claim to local nongravitational experiments through [local Lorentz invariance](../../../local-lorentz-invariance.md) and [local position invariance](../../../local-position-invariance.md). A sufficiently small freely falling laboratory should recover the nongravitational laws of [special relativity](../../../special-relativity-split.md). The [strong equivalence principle](../../../strong-equivalence-principle.md) extends the scope to local gravitational experiments and bodies with appreciable gravitational self-energy. An experiment establishing one part of the weak statement does not automatically establish these stronger claims.

The motivating elevator comparison makes both the power and the limitation clear. Release a small object in a freely falling cabin. The cabin and object share the local [free fall](../../../free-fall.md), so the object initially floats relative to the cabin. In a cabin supported against [gravity](../../../gravity.md), a released object instead accelerates relative to the floor, as it does in a suitably accelerated cabin in flat [spacetime](../../../spacetime.md). This suggested replacing a universal gravitational [force](../../../force-split.md) by geometric [inertial motion](../../../inertial-motion.md). It does not identify a finite planetary [gravitational field](../../../gravitational-field.md) with an accelerated frame everywhere: separated falling objects can have a measurable relative [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md).

In [general relativity](../../../general-relativity-split.md), the precise local geometric statement is that one can choose [normal coordinates](../../../normal-coordinates.md) at an event $p$ with

$$
g_{\mu\nu}(p)=\eta_{\mu\nu},\qquad
\partial_\rho g_{\mu\nu}(p)=0,\qquad
\Gamma^\mu{}_{\alpha\beta}(p)=0.
$$

This eliminates the connection contribution to the [geodesic equation](../../../geodesic-equation.md) at that point. It does not generally set the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md) to zero. In normal coordinates the first metric corrections away from $p$ are quadratic in the coordinate separation, and [geodesic deviation](../../../geodesic-deviation.md) measures the associated [tidal forces](../../../tidal-force.md). Equivalence is a local approximation for finite experiments whose extent and duration make these corrections negligible; it is not an assertion that curvature has been transformed away.[sections 3.3.2 and 3.3.4: normal coordinates and geodesic deviation](../../../general-relativity-by-david-tong.md)

There are direct experimental consequences beyond the elevator thought experiment. An [Eötvös experiment](../../../eotvos-experiment.md) compares bodies of different composition on a [torsion balance](../../../torsion-balance.md), using a differential signal to test the universality of gravitational [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md). [MICROSCOPE](../../../microscope-satellite.md) performed an analogous test in orbit using electrostatic control of titanium-alloy and platinum-alloy test masses. Its first published result in 2017 constrained their differential [free fall](../../../free-fall.md); this was already available before the examination. It tested a specific weak-equivalence prediction, not every possible gravitational law.[2017 abstract and experimental description: composition comparison and differential control forces](../../../the-microscope-mission-first-results-of-a-space-test-of-the-equivalence-principle-by-pierre-touboul-and-others.md) Comparisons of different [atomic clocks](../../../atomic-clock.md) can instead test [local position invariance](../../../local-position-invariance.md), including the expected [gravitational redshift](../../../gravitational-redshift.md). These measurements constrain related but distinct parts of the framework.

The principle remains important as a restriction on how matter couples to gravity and as an organizing guide for experimental tests. It does not, on its own, derive the [Einstein field equations](../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md). Universally coupling matter to a [metric tensor](../../../metric-tensor.md) still leaves choices for that metric's [action](../../../action.md); the [equivalence principle does not uniquely fix field equations](../../../equivalence-principle-does-not-uniquely-fix-field-equations.md). For example, a metric gravitational action containing a [scalar curvature](../../../scalar-curvature.md) squared term can retain the same minimal matter coupling while changing the gravitational dynamics. Selecting the [Einstein-Hilbert action](../../../einstein-hilbert-action.md) requires further dynamical assumptions and empirical success. The present role of the principle is consequently precise and limited: it supports local relativistic physics and universal matter coupling, while curvature, gravitational field equations and their quantitative predictions remain additional content.
