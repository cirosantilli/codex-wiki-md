# Neutron capture

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutron_capture)

Capture of a neutron increases mass number without immediately changing atomic number. It avoids the charged-particle [Coulomb barrier](coulomb-barrier.md). Subsequent [beta-minus decay](beta-minus-decay.md) can increase atomic number. Whether capture is slow or fast compared with relevant beta decays distinguishes the [slow neutron-capture process](s-process.md) from the [rapid neutron-capture process](r-process.md).
