# Reduction of compound lotteries

↑ **Parent:** [Lottery](lottery.md)

A compound [lottery](lottery.md) first selects branch $k$ with [probability](probability.md) $p_k$ and then outcome $\omega_j$ with conditional [probability](probability.md) $q_{kj}$. Its reduced [lottery](lottery.md) assigns $\omega_j$ the total [probability](probability.md) $\sum_kp_kq_{kj}$. The [law of total expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md) gives the same [expected utility](expected-utility.md) in either description:

$$
\sum_kp_k\sum_jq_{kj}u(\omega_j)=\sum_j\left(\sum_kp_kq_{kj}\right)u(\omega_j).
$$

This rule assumes that only the final outcome matters. A preference for a particular randomization procedure requires including that procedure in the outcome description; it cannot be captured by changing the arithmetic of [expected utility](expected-utility.md) on a fixed outcome set.
