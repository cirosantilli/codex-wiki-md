# Proportional frailty model

↑ **Parent:** [Frailty model](frailty-model.md)

A proportional frailty model has conditional hazard $h(t\mid U=u)=u h_0(t)$. Its conditional survival function is $e^{-uH_0(t)}$, so its population survival function is the [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) of the frailty distribution evaluated at $H_0(t)$.
