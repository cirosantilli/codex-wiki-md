# Hitting time of the bridge vertex between two cliques

↑ **Parent:** [Expected hitting time](expected-hitting-time.md)

For [simple random walk](simple-random-walk.md) on two disjoint [complete graphs](complete-graph.md) of size $n\geq2$ joined through one new vertex $w$, let $h_1$ be the [expected hitting time](expected-hitting-time.md) of $w$ from either vertex attached to $w$, and $h_o$ the [expected hitting time](expected-hitting-time.md) from any other clique vertex. [First-step analysis](first-step-analysis.md) gives $h_1=1+(n-1)h_o/n$ and $h_o=h_1+n-1$, so $h_1=n^2-n+1$ and $h_o=n^2$. The [expected hitting time](expected-hitting-time.md) from $w$ is zero. In discrete time the [random walk](random-walk.md) is an [aperiodic Markov chain](aperiodic-markov-chain.md) only for $n\geq3$.
