# Rigid inner pair in hierarchical secular inclination dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Lagrange inclination matrix](laplace-lagrange-inclination-matrix.md)

If two nearby inner planets couple much more strongly to each other than to a distant third planet, their slow [secular eigenmode](secular-eigenmode.md) has a common [complex inclination](complex-inclination.md). With $L_{12}=L_1+L_2$, the effective inner-to-outer coupling is

$$
c=\frac{L_1B_{13}+L_2B_{23}}{L_{12}}.
$$

[Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) balance makes the reverse coupling $cL_{12}/L_3$. The reduced matrix has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $0$ and $-c(1+L_{12}/L_3)$. If $L_3\gg L_{12}$, the nonzero frequency is approximately $-c$, while the fast inner differential mode has frequency $-(B_{12}+B_{21})$.
