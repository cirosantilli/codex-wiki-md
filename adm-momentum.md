# ADM momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy](arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md)

For an asymptotically flat spatial metric $h$ and $k_{ij}=-g(\nabla_i n,e_j)$, the displayed surface integral gives ADM spatial momentum. Together with [ADM energy](arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md) it forms the asymptotic energy-momentum four-vector. Reversing the sign convention for extrinsic curvature reverses the corresponding surface-integral sign.
