# Coordinate covalent bond

↑ **Parent:** [Covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md)

A [coordinate covalent bond](coordinate-covalent-bond.md) is described as forming by donation of a [lone pair](lone-pair.md) from one partner into an acceptor orbital of the other. This formation description does not imply that the final [electrons](electron.md) remain distinguishable by origin.
