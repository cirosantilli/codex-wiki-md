# Cross-ratio normalization of four projective points

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md)

For four distinct [projective points](projective-point.md) on the complex [projective line](projective-line.md), use the convention

$$
(P_0P_1:P_2P_3)=\frac{[p_0,p_2][p_1,p_3]}{[p_0,p_3][p_1,p_2]},
$$

where $[p_i,p_j]$ is the [determinant](determinant.md) of chosen representative vectors. Rescaling any representative cancels, and applying an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md) multiplies every bracket by its determinant, which also cancels. Thus the expression is a projective invariant. The [projective general linear group action on the projective line](projective-general-linear-group-action-on-the-projective-line.md) has a unique element $T$ taking the first three points to $\infty,0,1$. In those coordinates the displayed expression is exactly $T(P_3)$. Consequently the [cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md) completely determines the [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) of the ordered quadruple under the [projective linear group](projective-linear-group.md). Its value lies in $\mathbb C\setminus\{0,1\}$.
