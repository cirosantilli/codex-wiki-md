# Sample mean

↑ **Parent:** [Sample mean and covariance](sample-mean-and-covariance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sample_mean)

For observations $X_1,\ldots,X_n$, the sample mean is $\overline X=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$.
