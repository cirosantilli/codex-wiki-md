# Small baryon abundance delays hydrogen recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Recombination temperature](recombination-temperature.md)

For [hydrogen ionization fraction](hydrogen-ionization-fraction.md) $X_e$ of order one, the [hydrogen-only Saha equation](hydrogen-only-saha-equation.md) gives $1\sim n_b(2\pi/(m_eT))^{3/2}e^{B/T}$. The [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) and [photon number density](photon-number-density.md) imply $n_b\sim\eta T^3$. Therefore $1\sim\eta(T/m_e)^{3/2}e^{B/T}$ and the displayed logarithmic balance follows. A small $\eta$ supplies many ionizing [photons](photon.md) per [baryon](baryon.md), so neutral [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) forms at a [temperature](temperature.md) much smaller than its [Hydrogen binding energy](hydrogen-binding-energy.md). The numerical temperature depends on the ionization-fraction convention and on whether equilibrium remains valid.
