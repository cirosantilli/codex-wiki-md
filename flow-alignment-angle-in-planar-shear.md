# Flow-alignment angle in planar shear

↑ **Parent:** [Flow alignment of a polar order parameter](flow-alignment-of-a-polar-order-parameter.md)

For a uniform [polar order parameter](polar-order-parameter.md) in [simple shear flow](simple-shear-flow.md) $\mathbf v=(gy,0)$, the angular equation is $\dot\theta=g(\xi\cos2\theta-1)/2$. A fixed orientation for nonzero shear therefore requires $|\xi|\ge1$. For $|\xi|>1$, its angular stability condition is $g\xi\sin2\theta>0$; equality at $|\xi|=1$ requires nonlinear analysis. The ordered magnitude must separately satisfy the radial stationarity equation and be nonzero.
