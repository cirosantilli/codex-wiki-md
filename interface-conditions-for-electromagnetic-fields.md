# Interface conditions for electromagnetic fields

↑ **Parent:** [Plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interface_conditions_for_electromagnetic_fields)

Across an interface with unit normal $\mathbf n$ from medium one to medium two, [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations.md) imply $\mathbf n\times(\mathbf E_2-\mathbf E_1)=0$, $\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf B_2-\mathbf B_1)=0$, $\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf D_2-\mathbf D_1)=\sigma_f$, and $\mathbf n\times(\mathbf H_2-\mathbf H_1)=\mathbf K_f$. Here $\sigma_f$ and $\mathbf K_f$ are the free surface [electric charge](electric-charge.md) and [electric current](electric-current.md) densities.
