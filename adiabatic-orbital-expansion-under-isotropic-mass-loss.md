# Adiabatic orbital expansion under isotropic mass loss

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic stellar mass loss](isotropic-stellar-mass-loss.md)

Time averaging over a [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) gives $\langle\cos f\rangle=-e$, as follows from $dt=(1-e\cos E)dE/n$ and $\cos f=(\cos E-e)/(1-e\cos E)$. Consequently slow [isotropic stellar mass loss](isotropic-stellar-mass-loss.md) preserves the secular [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md). Conserved [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) then gives $\mu a=\mathrm{constant}$, so [pericentre](periapsis.md), [apocentre](apoapsis.md) and [semimajor axis](semi-major-axis.md) expand inversely with the remaining gravitational parameter.
