# Bhattacharyya distance

↑ **Parent:** [Bhattacharyya coefficient](bhattacharyya-coefficient.md)

The [Bhattacharyya distance](bhattacharyya-distance.md) is the negative [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) of the [Bhattacharyya coefficient](bhattacharyya-coefficient.md), with $-\ln0=\infty$. It is zero for identical [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md) and infinite for disjoint [probability supports](support-of-a-probability-distribution.md). Despite the name, it need not satisfy the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) required of a [metric](metric.md).
