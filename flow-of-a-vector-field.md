# Flow of a vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field](vector-field.md)

The local flow of a smooth [vector field](vector-field.md) is the family of solutions of its [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md), with $\Phi_0(p)=p$. Uniqueness gives $\Phi_{s+t}=\Phi_s\circ\Phi_t$ wherever defined. A compactly supported smooth [vector field](vector-field.md) on a manifold without boundary is complete, so its flow exists for all real times.
