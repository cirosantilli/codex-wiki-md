# Tensile testing

↑ **Parent:** [Materials science](materials-science.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensile_testing)

[Tensile testing](tensile-testing.md) measures the response of a specimen to an axial pulling [force](force-split.md). A [tensile modulus experiment](tensile-modulus-experiment.md) uses the initial proportional response to infer [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md).
