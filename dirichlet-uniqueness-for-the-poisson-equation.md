# Dirichlet uniqueness for the Poisson equation

↑ **Parent:** [Uniqueness of Poisson equation](uniqueness-of-poisson-equation.md)

The difference of two classical [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) solutions with the same [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) vanishing on the boundary. Under sufficient boundary smoothness, [Green's first identity](green-s-first-identity.md) gives $\int_V|\nabla(\phi_1-\phi_2)|^2=0$. More generally the [maximum principle for harmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md) proves the same uniqueness for functions continuous up to the boundary and twice continuously differentiable in the bounded interior.
