# Nonlinear Klein-Gordon equation

↑ **Parent:** [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md)

A local scalar nonlinearity preserves [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md) of the wave operator when the wave [speed](speed.md) is one. If $f=F'$, the equation follows from $\mathcal L=(\partial_t\phi)^2/2-(\partial_x\phi)^2/2+F(\phi)$. The physical potential is $-F$, so its sign must be distinguished from the sign written in the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md).
