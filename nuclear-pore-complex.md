# Nuclear pore complex

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear envelope](nuclear-envelope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_pore_complex)

A [nuclear pore complex](nuclear-pore-complex.md) is an assembly of [proteins](protein-split.md) spanning the [nuclear envelope](nuclear-envelope.md) and regulating passage between the [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) and [cytosol](cytosol.md). It is a selective gateway, not simply an unregulated hole: [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) export and import of nuclear [proteins](protein-split.md) require appropriate transport machinery. See [Alberts et al.: nuclear pore complexes](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26932/).

The [nuclear pore complex](nuclear-pore-complex.md) occupies a junction of both [nuclear envelope](nuclear-envelope.md) membranes. Its [protein](protein-split.md) scaffold surrounds an aqueous passage connecting [cytosol](cytosol.md) to the [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), rather than the intermembrane space. [Diffusion](diffusion.md) permits small soluble cargo to pass; selective receptors transport other cargo. [Molecular mass](molecular-mass.md) alone is not a universal cutoff.[Nuclear Pore Complexes Perforate the Nuclear Envelope, Figures 12-10–12-11; Nuclear Localization Signals Direct Nuclear Proteins to the Nucleus](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)
