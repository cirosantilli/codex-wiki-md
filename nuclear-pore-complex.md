# Nuclear pore complex

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear envelope](nuclear-envelope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_pore_complex)

A [nuclear pore complex](nuclear-pore-complex.md) is an assembly of [proteins](protein.md) spanning the [nuclear envelope](nuclear-envelope.md) and regulating passage between the [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) and [cytosol](cytosol.md). It is a selective gateway, not simply an unregulated hole: [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) export and import of nuclear [proteins](protein.md) require appropriate transport machinery. See [Alberts et al.: nuclear pore complexes](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26932/).
