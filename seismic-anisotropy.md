# Seismic anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

[Seismic anisotropy](seismic-anisotropy.md) is direction dependence of elastic-wave propagation. Aligned crystals or layering can produce it; using it to infer deformation requires a model connecting fabric to flow.
