# Astatic Galvanometer, Nobili Pattern by Museo Galileo

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

[https://catalogue.museogalileo.it/object/AstaticGalvanometerNobiliPattern.html](https://catalogue.museogalileo.it/object/AstaticGalvanometerNobiliPattern.html)

Museum inventory 1417, dated 1840, identifies a many-turn fine-wire instrument with [Carlo Matteucci](carlo-matteucci.md)'s muscle-current research. Its inscription reads “Galvanometro del Matteucci / 1840 / Corrente Muscolare”.
