# Fiber metric

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

A fiber metric on a real smooth [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) is a positive-definite [inner product](inner-product.md) on each fiber that varies smoothly in [vector bundle trivializations](vector-bundle-trivialization.md). A smooth [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) subordinate to trivializing charts averages their Euclidean inner products. Nonnegative weights preserve positivity and local finiteness ensures smoothness. Hence every real vector bundle over a Hausdorff second-countable [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) admits a fiber metric. Applied to the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md), this is existence of a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md).
