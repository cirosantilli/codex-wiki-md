# Minor arc

↑ **Parent:** [Hardy-Littlewood circle method](hardy-littlewood-circle-method.md)

Minor arcs are the complement of the specified [major arcs](major-arc.md). They contribute an error only when a suitable pointwise or mean-value cancellation estimate is proved; their name does not imply that their measure is small.
