# Meaning postulate

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic-synthetic distinction](analytic-synthetic-distinction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meaning_postulate)

A rule constraining the admissible [interpretations](interpretation-logic.md) of predicates in a stated formal language. A sentence can then count as analytic relative to the chosen rules if it holds in every admitted interpretation. This precise relative notion does not itself establish an unrevisable division of all natural-language statements.[sections 1–3](meaning-postulates-by-rudolf-carnap.md)
