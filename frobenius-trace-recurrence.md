# Frobenius trace recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic-curve point count over a finite field](elliptic-curve-point-count-over-a-finite-field.md)

For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) over $\mathbb F_q$, the [Frobenius isogeny](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) has characteristic polynomial $T^2-tT+q$. Taking traces of its powers gives this recurrence. If $\alpha,\beta$ are the roots, then $t_n=\alpha^n+\beta^n$ and $\#E(\mathbb F_{q^n})=q^n+1-t_n$. The [Hasse bound](hasse-s-theorem-on-elliptic-curves.md) implies $|\alpha|=|\beta|=\sqrt q$, including the repeated-root case.
