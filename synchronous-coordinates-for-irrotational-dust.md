# Synchronous coordinates for irrotational dust

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronous coordinates](synchronous-coordinates.md)

For [relativistic dust](relativistic-dust.md) with nowhere-zero [density](density.md), [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) implies zero [four-acceleration](four-acceleration.md). If its normalized [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) $u$ has [hypersurface orthogonality](hypersurface-orthogonality.md), then $u^\flat\wedge du^\flat=0$ and $\iota_u du^\flat=0$. The [interior product](interior-product.md) of the first identity gives $du^\flat=0$. The [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) supplies $u^\flat=-dt$ locally; spatial [coordinate functions](coordinate-function.md) constant along the [smooth flow](smooth-flow.md) of $u$ give $u=\partial_t$, $g_{tt}=-1$ and $g_{ti}=0$. The resulting [synchronous coordinates](synchronous-coordinates.md) describe the dust until the local coordinate construction fails.
