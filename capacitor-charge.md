# Capacitor charge

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor](capacitor.md)

For an ideal linear [capacitor](capacitor.md), the [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) on the plate at positive [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) is $Q=CV$, where $C$ is its [capacitance](capacitance.md). The opposite plate carries $-Q$. An initial charge supplies the integration constant in an [op amp integrator](op-amp-integrator.md).
