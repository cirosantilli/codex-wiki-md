# Multipole radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation zone](radiation-zone.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multipole_radiation)

[Multipole radiation](multipole-radiation.md) expands radiation from localized time-dependent sources into electric and magnetic multipoles. [Electric dipole radiation](electric-dipole-radiation.md) is the leading electric term, while [magnetic dipole radiation](magnetic-dipole-radiation.md) and higher electric multipoles contribute when the leading term vanishes or higher precision is needed.
