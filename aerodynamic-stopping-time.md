# Aerodynamic stopping time

↑ **Parent:** [Drag (physics)](drag-physics.md)

The aerodynamic stopping time is the characteristic time on which [drag force](drag-physics.md) damps a solid particle's velocity relative to the surrounding fluid. Linear drag gives relative acceleration $-\mathbf v_{\rm rel}/\tau$.
