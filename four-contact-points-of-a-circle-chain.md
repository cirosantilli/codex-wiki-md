# Four contact points of a circle chain

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent circles](tangent-circles.md)

Four positive-[radius](radius.md) [circles](circle.md) with pairwise disjoint interiors, successive [external tangencies](external-tangency.md), and four distinct contact points have those contact points on a [circle](circle.md). Apply the reciprocal [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $w=1/(z-T)$ at one contact $T$. The two [circles](circle.md) through $T$ become disjoint [parallel lines](parallel-lines.md); the other two [disks](disk-mathematics.md) lie between them and avoid zero. [Collinearity for tangent circles between parallel lines](collinearity-for-tangent-circles-between-parallel-lines.md) places the three finite transformed contacts on a [straight line](straight-line.md). That [straight line](straight-line.md) misses zero: otherwise its segment across the strip would pass through zero, while that segment is contained in the two transformed closed [disks](disk-mathematics.md). Its inverse is therefore a genuine [circle](circle.md) through $T$, containing all four original contacts. Allowing [internal tangency](internal-tangency.md) without the pictured disjoint-interior hypotheses admits collinear degeneracies instead.
