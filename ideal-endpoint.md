# Ideal endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic line](hyperbolic-line.md)

An ideal endpoint of a [hyperbolic line](hyperbolic-line.md) is one of its two limiting points on the boundary at infinity. In the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md) these lie on the unit [circle](circle.md); in the [upper half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md) they lie on $\mathbb R\cup\{\infty\}$. These are boundary points, not points of the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md). Any two distinct [ideal endpoints](ideal-endpoint.md) determine a unique [hyperbolic line](hyperbolic-line.md).
