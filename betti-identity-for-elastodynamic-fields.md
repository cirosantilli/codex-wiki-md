# Betti identity for elastodynamic fields

↑ **Parent:** [Elasticity (physics)](elasticity-physics.md)

For two Fourier [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md) fields with the same real [elastic stiffness tensor](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md), its major symmetry cancels the cross-strain terms in the [divergence](divergence.md) displayed above. The force-balance equations supply the remaining volume terms, and the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) gives their boundary integral of reciprocal [tractions](traction.md). Taking one field to be a [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) gives energy-work identities and weighted modal [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md). The bilinear identity uses no [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) until that field is chosen explicitly.
