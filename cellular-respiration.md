# Cellular respiration

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolism](metabolism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellular_respiration)

[Cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md) transfers [energy](energy.md) from chemical fuels into usable forms including [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md). [Plants](plant.md) carry out [cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md) as well as [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md); in its aerobic form, [cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md) consumes [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) and releases [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 8.3: energy flow](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/8-3-using-light-energy-to-make-organic-molecules).
