# Cellular respiration

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolism](metabolism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellular_respiration)

[Cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md) transfers [energy](energy-split.md) from chemical fuels into usable forms including [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md). [Oxidation](oxidation.md) of the fuel is coupled to [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) of an [electron acceptor](electron-acceptor.md); the [energy](energy-split.md) transfer can support [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) synthesis. [Plants](plant.md) carry out [cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md) as well as [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md). Their [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) need usable [energy](energy-split.md) in darkness too, and a [seed](seed.md) can draw on stored fuel during [germination](germination.md).[Biology 2e, §7.1, Electrons and Energy and ATP in Living Systems: redox and energy carriers](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)[Biology 2e, §8.3, The Energy Flow, Figures 8.21–8.22: plant respiration and energy transfer](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)

Follow three different accounts: [carbon](carbon.md) moving from fuel to products, [electrons](electron.md) moving to an [electron acceptor](electron-acceptor.md), and [energy](energy-split.md) becoming available for cellular work. They are connected, but an [oxygen](oxygen.md) uptake measurement alone does not identify the fuel or count the [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) made. The [pea seed respirometry](pea-seed-respirometry.md) experiment makes this distinction visible with simple apparatus.
