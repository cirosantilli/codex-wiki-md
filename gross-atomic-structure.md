# Gross atomic structure

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)

[Gross atomic structure](gross-atomic-structure.md) denotes the leading nonrelativistic Coulomb binding levels, before fine and hyperfine splittings. For a hydrogenic two-body system, the [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) controls the binding [energy](energy-split.md) and characteristic separation. [Fine structure](fine-structure.md) is smaller by roughly $\alpha^2$; nuclear magnetic hyperfine effects have an additional electron-to-nuclear [mass](mass.md) suppression.
