# Sliding-bead gravitational wave detector

↑ **Parent:** [TT coordinate separation and measured separation](tt-coordinate-separation-and-measured-separation.md)

A short rigid nonrotating guide constrains two freely sliding beads to one axis $n$. In its [parallel-propagated orthonormal frame](parallel-propagated-orthonormal-frame.md), the axial [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md) is $\ddot L=-R_{n0n0}L$. For a transverse [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave.md), $R_{n0n0}=-\epsilon\ddot h_{nn}/2$, so a wave incident after the beads are at rest changes the measured separation by $\epsilon h_{nn}L_0/2$ to first order. Transverse reaction forces do not supply an axial restoring force. Alignment with the propagation direction gives no first-order length change.
