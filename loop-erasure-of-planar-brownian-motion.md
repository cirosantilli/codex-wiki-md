# Loop-erasure of planar Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Loop-erased random walk](loop-erased-random-walk.md)

The time reversal of a [Brownian motion conditioned to exit at a boundary point](brownian-motion-conditioned-to-exit-at-a-boundary-point.md) can be coupled to radial $\operatorname{SLE}_2$ so that its loop-erasure is the radial curve. The coupling requires the Brownian path's first hit of each initial radial segment to occur at its tip. It is an existence theorem for a coupling and does not assert that the Brownian trace equals the simple radial curve, or that an elementary deterministic finite-loop algorithm is defined for all continuum paths.
