# Massive particle little group

↑ **Parent:** [Little group](little-group.md)

The stabilizer of a timelike [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) in $D$-dimensional [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) has connected group $SO(D-1)$. Massive particle polarizations form representations of this group.
