# Directional solidification

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directional_solidification)

Directional solidification advances a freezing region preferentially along an imposed temperature gradient. Pulling material through fixed heat exchangers can make the [mushy layer](mushy-layer.md) stationary in the apparatus frame even while solid and liquid pass through it.
