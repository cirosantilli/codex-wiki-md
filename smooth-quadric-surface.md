# Smooth quadric surface

↑ **Parent:** [Product of projective varieties](product-of-projective-varieties.md)

A [smooth quadric surface](smooth-quadric-surface.md) is a smooth two-dimensional [quadric hypersurface](quadric-algebraic-geometry.md). Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) other than two, every smooth quadric surface in $\mathbb P^3$ becomes

$$
V(x_0x_3-x_1x_2),
$$

after a [projective linear transformation](projective-linear-transformation.md). The [Segre embedding](segre-embedding.md) identifies it with $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$.
