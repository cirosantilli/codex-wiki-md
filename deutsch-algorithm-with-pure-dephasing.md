# Deutsch algorithm with pure dephasing

↑ **Parent:** [Deutsch algorithm](deutsch-algorithm.md)

If the input [qubit](qubit.md) of the [Deutsch algorithm](deutsch-algorithm.md) acquires conditional environment states with overlap $\langle e_0|e_1\rangle=ve^{i\alpha}$ between its two [Hadamard gates](hadamard-gate.md), its fixed zero-for-constant, one-for-balanced rule succeeds with probability $(1+v\cos\alpha)/2$. A known phase can be compensated before the last [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md), raising the success probability to $(1+v)/2$. An orthogonal pair of environment states gives $v=0$ and destroys all information in the input [qubit](qubit.md) about the answer; unit overlap modulus preserves coherence up to a known phase.
