# Complete discrete valuation ring

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md)

A complete discrete [valuation](valuation.md) ring is a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) complete for its maximal-ideal-adic topology. Equivalently, the canonical map to the [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) of the residue rings is an isomorphism: compatible residue classes determine a Cauchy sequence, and completeness gives its unique limit. Standard examples are $\mathbb Z_p$ and $k[[t]]$. This completeness is the hypothesis that makes [Newton iteration over a valued field](newton-iteration-over-a-valued-field.md) converge in the ring in the usual [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md).
