# Iterate

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete dynamical system](discrete-dynamical-system-split.md)

An [iterate](iterate.md) is the result of repeatedly applying a self-map to an initial state. The $n$th [iterate](iterate.md) of $x$ is $F^n(x)$, where the superscript denotes composition.
