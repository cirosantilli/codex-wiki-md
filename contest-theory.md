# Contest theory

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

Contest theory studies how [prize allocation rules](prize-allocation-rule.md) and effort costs influence strategic effort investment, participation, and the resulting [Nash equilibria](nash-equilibrium.md) or [Bayesian Nash equilibria](bayesian-nash-equilibrium.md). Unlike a sale in which only the winner pays, an [all-pay auction](all-pay-auction.md) charges every participant for its effort.
