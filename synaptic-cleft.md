# Synaptic cleft

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical synapse](chemical-synapse.md)

The synaptic cleft is the extracellular gap between the sending and receiving [cell membranes](cell-membrane-split.md) of a [chemical synapse](chemical-synapse.md). Released [neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md) crosses this gap to reach a [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md).
