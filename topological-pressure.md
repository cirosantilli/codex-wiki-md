# Topological pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic formalism](thermodynamic-formalism.md)

For a finite [full shift](full-shift.md) and a potential with summable variations, the displayed symbolic pressure exists and does not depend on the reference tail $\eta$. The [almost-additive partition-function limit](almost-additive-partition-function-limit.md) proves existence. The function $\gamma\mapsto P(\gamma U)$ is [convex](convex-function.md); finite-volume second derivatives are energy variances divided by block length. If $-B\leq U\leq-b<0$, its increments for $\delta>0$ lie between $-B\delta$ and $-b\delta$, so it is Lipschitz continuous, strictly decreasing, and has one positive zero.
