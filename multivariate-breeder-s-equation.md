<h1 id="multivariate-breeder-s-equation">Multivariate breeder's equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

The local response approximation $\Delta\overline{\mathbf z}=\mathbf G\boldsymbol\beta$, where $\mathbf G$ is the [additive genetic covariance matrix](additive-genetic-covariance-matrix.md) and $\boldsymbol\beta$ is a [phenotypic selection gradient](phenotypic-selection-gradient.md). Correlated inheritance can facilitate one direction of response and inhibit another.[Evolution 33, pp. 402–416](quantitative-genetic-analysis-of-multivariate-evolution-applied-to-brain-body-size-allometry-by-russell-lande.md)
