# Normal and Weyl symbols of a harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md)

For the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) $H=\hbar\omega(a^\dagger a+1/2)$, its normal symbol is $H_N=\hbar\omega(\bar\psi\psi+1/2)$. The [Weyl ordering](weyl-ordering.md) symbol of $a^\dagger a$ is $\bar\psi\psi-1/2$, so $H_W=\hbar\omega\bar\psi\psi=p^2/(2m)+m\omega^2q^2/2$. Switching from adjacent-label [coherent-state time slicing](coherent-state-time-slicing.md) to a midpoint [phase-space path integral](phase-space-path-integral.md) must retain this ordering correction. Both correctly normalized constructions give the [thermal partition function of a quantum harmonic oscillator](thermal-partition-function-of-a-quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) $Z=[2\sinh(\beta\hbar\omega/2)]^{-1}$; retaining the normal-symbol constant after switching prescriptions would count the zero-point shift twice.
