# Background gauge field

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field](gauge-field.md)

A [background gauge field](background-gauge-field.md) is a nondynamical source for a [conserved current](conserved-current.md), rather than a field integrated over in the [path integral](path-integral.md). For a [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) with source term $+B_\mu j^\mu$, the combined transformations $\psi\mapsto e^{i\alpha}\psi$ and $B_\mu\mapsto B_\mu+\partial_\mu\alpha$ preserve the [action](action.md). Whether this also preserves the [generating functional](generating-functional.md) depends on the absence of the corresponding [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md).
