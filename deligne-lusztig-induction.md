# Deligne-Lusztig induction

↑ **Parent:** [Deligne-Lusztig theory](deligne-lusztig-theory.md)

For a [rational maximal torus](rational-maximal-torus.md) $T$, choose a [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md) containing it with [unipotent radical](unipotent-radical.md) $U$. The alternating compactly supported [étale cohomology](etale-cohomology.md) of $\mathcal L_F^{-1}(U)$, in the appropriate $T^F$-isotypic component, defines the [virtual character](virtual-character.md) of $G^F$ $R_T^G(\theta)$. It is independent of the chosen Borel. When the [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md) is rational, this is ordinary induction from its rational points after [inflation of a group representation](inflation-of-a-group-representation.md) from $T^F$.
