# Metric epsilon-net

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)

An [epsilon-net](metric-epsilon-net.md) in a [metric space](metric-space.md) is a set within distance $\varepsilon$ of every point. A maximal $\varepsilon$-separated set is an [epsilon-net](metric-epsilon-net.md): any uncovered point could be added. On the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md) of $\mathbb R^k$, disjoint [Euclidean balls](euclidean-ball.md) of radius $\varepsilon/2$ around separated points lie in the ball of radius $1+\varepsilon/2$. Comparing [Lebesgue measures](lebesgue-measure.md) gives a net of [cardinality](cardinality.md) at most $(1+2/\varepsilon)^k$.
