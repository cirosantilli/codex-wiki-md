# Log-gamma prior for a Poisson log-intercept

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-gamma conjugacy](poisson-gamma-conjugacy.md)

If the positive baseline rate has a [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) of shape $a$ and rate $b$, its [logarithm](logarithm.md) has this [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) with respect to $d\mu$. The extra factor $e^\mu$ is the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) of the transformation. After a [Gibbs sampler](gibbs-sampler.md) draws the conditional baseline rate, taking its [logarithm](logarithm.md) is an exact update of the intercept. Omitting the Jacobian changes the prior.
