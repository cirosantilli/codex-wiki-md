# QR algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QR_algorithm)

The [QR algorithm](qr-algorithm.md) computes [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) through repeated [QR decompositions](qr-decomposition.md) and similarity updates. Shifted and unshifted forms have different convergence rates; the [unshifted QR algorithm](unshifted-qr-algorithm.md) uses a zero shift at every step.
