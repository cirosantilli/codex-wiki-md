<h1 id="lake-coupled-rothlisberger-drainage-instability">Lake-coupled Röthlisberger drainage instability</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Röthlisberger channel drainage](rothlisberger-channel-drainage.md)

A lake supplies a melt-opened subglacial channel while its changing hydrostatic head changes [effective pressure](effective-pressure-in-subglacial-drainage.md) and [material creep](creep-deformation.md) closure. In the displayed scaled model the positive [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is $S=\mu^{3/4}$, $N=\mu^{1/12}$. Its [Jacobian](jacobian-matrix.md) has [trace](matrix-trace.md) $\mu^{1/4}/3>0$ and [determinant](determinant.md) $4\mu^{7/6}$. The instability becomes oscillatory when $\mu>1/1728$. Growing linear [oscillations](oscillation.md) signal departure from steady drainage; they do not prove unbounded physical lake discharge after nonlinear effects and lake depletion become important.
