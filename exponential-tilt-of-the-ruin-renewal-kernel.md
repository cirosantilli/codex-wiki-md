# Exponential tilt of the ruin renewal kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Defective renewal equation](defective-renewal-equation.md)

The [ultimate ruin probability](ultimate-ruin-probability.md) satisfies a tail-forced [defective renewal equation](defective-renewal-equation.md). Multiplying it by $e^{Ru}$ at an [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) turns its kernel into the probability density $k_R$. Normalization is exactly $\lambda(M(R)-1)=cR$. The density is absolutely continuous even when the original claim law has atoms; thus it has a [nonarithmetic distribution](nonarithmetic-distribution.md).
