# Linear independence of adjacency powers up to the diameter

↑ **Parent:** [Graph diameter](graph-diameter.md)

If a connected graph has adjacency matrix $A$ and diameter $d$, then

$$
I,A,\ldots,A^d
$$

are linearly independent. For the largest index $k$ with a nonzero coefficient, choose vertices at distance $k$; the corresponding entries of all lower powers vanish, while $(A^k)_{uv}>0$ by the [walk count from powers of an adjacency matrix](walk-count-from-powers-of-an-adjacency-matrix.md).
