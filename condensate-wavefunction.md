# Condensate wavefunction

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensate](bose-einstein-condensate.md)

A [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) describing a coherent condensate in [mean-field theory](mean-field-theory.md). In the convention $\int|\psi|^2d^3r=N$, its squared modulus is the [condensate density](condensate-density.md). Dividing by $\sqrt N$ gives a normalized orbital; formulas for interaction strengths and nonlinear terms must use the same normalization convention.
