# Renormalization-group mass suppression criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Running mass](running-mass.md)

The effective dimensionless mass is $e^{-t}m(t)/\mu$. It vanishes precisely when this integrated exponent tends to minus infinity, for positive initial mass. An eventual bound $\delta\le1-\eta$ is sufficient. Replacing the massive correlator by its [massless limit](massless-limit.md) additionally requires regularity uniform along the coupling trajectory.
