# Muonic atom

↑ **Parent:** [Atom (Physics)](atom-physics.md)

A [muonic atom](muonic-atom.md) has a negative [muon](muon.md) bound in the [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) of an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md). Its much larger [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) than in an electronic [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) greatly reduces the orbital scale, increasing sensitivity of transition [energies](energy-split.md) to the [nuclear charge radius](nuclear-charge-radius.md) and [nuclear charge distribution](nuclear-charge-distribution.md). Measuring muonic X-ray transitions therefore complements [electron scattering](electron-scattering.md). Strong relativistic and nuclear-size effects in heavy systems require a treatment beyond a point-nucleus nonrelativistic Coulomb spectrum.
