# Matter

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matter)

Material constituents such as [particles](particle-physics.md) and their composites. In [cosmology](cosmology-split.md), the narrower phrase [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) usually refers to nonrelativistic components whose [rest energy](rest-energy.md) dominates their random [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), giving negligible [pressure](pressure-split.md) and density proportional to the inverse cube of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md). This usage distinguishes the matter contribution from relativistic [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md) and [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md).
