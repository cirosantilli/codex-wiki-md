# Countability of negative curves

↑ **Parent:** [Negative curve on a projective surface](negative-curve-on-a-projective-surface.md)

Distinct [negative curves](negative-curve-on-a-projective-surface.md) cannot have the same integral cohomology class: their mutual intersection would equal a negative self-intersection, contradicting nonnegative [intersection multiplicities](intersection-multiplicity.md) for distinct curves. A smooth complex projective surface has finitely generated integral [cohomology groups](cohomology-group.md), so its negative curves form an at most countable set. Zero-self-intersection curves need not obey this restriction: one ruling of $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$ gives an uncountable family.
