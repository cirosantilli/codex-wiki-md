# Spin-reversal cross-ratio for beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Polarized beta-decay angular distribution](polarized-beta-decay-angular-distribution.md)

Consider a teaching extension with two opposite detectors, labelled $+$ and $-$ by fixed laboratory directions, and two preparation states $s=+1,-1$ with equal and opposite [nuclear polarization](nuclear-polarization.md). This is an original two-detector model; the historical [Wu experiment](wu-experiment.md) used a fixed internal [electron](electron.md) counter with reversal.[Apparatus explanation and December 27 notebook: fixed beta counter, reversed solenoid current, and warming](the-reversal-of-parity-law-in-nuclear-physics-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md) Let $C_s>0$ describe the exposure in each state and $\eta_d>0$ the stable [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md) of detector $d$.

For matched opposite acceptances, write their common mean absolute cosine as $k$. The four expected positive counts are

$$
N_+^{\,+}=C_+\eta_+(1+\epsilon),\qquad
N_-^{\,+}=C_+\eta_-(1-\epsilon),\qquad
N_+^{\,-}=C_-\eta_+(1-\epsilon),\qquad
N_-^{\,-}=C_-\eta_-(1+\epsilon),\qquad
\epsilon=ak,\quad |\epsilon|<1.
$$

The same-state ratios each retain the unequal [detection efficiencies](detection-efficiency.md):

$$
\frac{N_+^{\,+}}{N_-^{\,+}}=\frac{\eta_+}{\eta_-}\frac{1+\epsilon}{1-\epsilon},\qquad
\frac{N_+^{\,-}}{N_-^{\,-}}=\frac{\eta_+}{\eta_-}\frac{1-\epsilon}{1+\epsilon}.
$$

Dividing these ratios gives the [spin-reversal cross-ratio for beta decay](spin-reversal-cross-ratio-for-beta-decay.md):

$$
R=\frac{N_+^{\,+}N_-^{\,-}}{N_-^{\,+}N_+^{\,-}}
=\left(\frac{1+\epsilon}{1-\epsilon}\right)^2,\qquad
\epsilon=\frac{\sqrt R-1}{\sqrt R+1}.
$$

Both the exposure ratio $C_+/C_-$ and the [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md) ratio $\eta_+/\eta_-$ cancel. The positive square root is essential: positive expected counts and $|\epsilon|<1$ make $(1+\epsilon)/(1-\epsilon)$ positive.

For the chosen $a=-0.3$, opposite hemispheres give $k=1/2$, hence $\epsilon=-0.15$. Choose $C_+=10000$, $C_-=8000$, $\eta_+=0.8$, and $\eta_-=0.5$, with their common acceptance factor absorbed into $C_s$. The four expected counts in the displayed order are $6800$, $5750$, $7360$, and $3400$. Thus

$$
\frac{N_+^{\,+}}{N_-^{\,+}}=\frac{136}{115},\qquad
\frac{N_+^{\,-}}{N_-^{\,-}}=\frac{184}{85},\qquad
R=\frac{289}{529},\qquad \sqrt R=\frac{17}{23},\qquad
\epsilon=-\frac3{20}.
$$

The first raw detector ratio exceeds one despite negative $\epsilon$: detector imbalance can overwhelm the physical directional preference. Reversal separates the stable imbalance from the term that changes sign.
