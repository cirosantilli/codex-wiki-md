# Haplotype block

↑ **Parent:** [Haplotype](haplotype.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haplotype_block)

A haplotype block is a chromosomal region with limited historical [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md), strong local [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md) and comparatively few common [haplotypes](haplotype.md). Its definition is operational and population-dependent; it does not mean that recombination never occurs within it.
