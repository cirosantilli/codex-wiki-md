# Subatomic particle

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subatomic_particle)

A subatomic particle is a constituent of matter or radiation smaller than an atom. It may be elementary or composite.
