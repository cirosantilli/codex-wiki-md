# Geodesic segment

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)

A geodesic segment is the restriction of a [geodesic](geodesic.md) to a compact parameter interval. In the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md), two points determine a unique geodesic segment, which minimizes the [hyperbolic distance](hyperbolic-distance.md) between them.
