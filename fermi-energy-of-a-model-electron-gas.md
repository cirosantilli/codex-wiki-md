# Fermi energy of a model electron gas

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md)

Choose $n=10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$ for a uniform noninteracting [electron](electron.md) model with two equally populated [spin](spin.md) states. This is an original worked example, not a measured [number density](number-density.md) assigned to an actual material. Using $m_e\simeq9.10938\times10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $\hbar\simeq1.05457\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$, and $k_B=1.380649\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$ gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
k_F&=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}\simeq6.6651\times10^9\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\\
p_F&=\hbar k_F\simeq7.0288\times10^{-25}\,\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-1}},\\
E_F&=\frac{p_F^2}{2m_e}\simeq2.7117\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm J\simeq1.6925\,\mathrm{eV},\\
T_F&=\frac{E_F}{k_B}\simeq1.9641\times10^4\,\mathrm K.
\end{aligned}
$$

The conversion uses $1\,\mathrm{eV}=1.602176634\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$: an [electronvolt](electronvolt.md) is an [energy](energy-split.md) unit, while a [joule](joule.md) is its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) counterpart. The dimensions provide a quick check: $n^{1/3}$ is inverse [length](length.md), $\hbar n^{1/3}$ has units of [momentum](momentum-split.md), $p_F^2/m$ has units of [energy](energy-split.md), and $E_F/k_B$ has units of [temperature](temperature-split.md). The ratio $p_F/(m_ec)\simeq0.002574$ verifies the [nonrelativistic limit](nonrelativistic-limit.md) for this model, where $c$ is the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md).

Increasing this chosen [number density](number-density.md) eightfold doubles the [Fermi momentum](fermi-momentum.md) and quadruples the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md), giving approximately $6.7701\,\mathrm{eV}$. The exponent $2/3$ has a geometric origin: the number of occupied states grows as the cube of a [momentum](momentum-split.md) radius, but [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) of [nonrelativistic particles](nonrelativistic-particle.md) grows as its square. This makes [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) a useful bridge between a count of particles and a mechanical [energy](energy-split.md) scale.
