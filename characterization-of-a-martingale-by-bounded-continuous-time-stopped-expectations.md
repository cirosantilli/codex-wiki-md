# Characterization of a martingale by bounded continuous-time stopped expectations

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

An integrable [adapted process](adapted-process.md) is a [martingale](martingale-split.md) if all its values at [bounded stopping times](bounded-stopping-time.md) are integrable with the same [expectation](expected-value.md) as $X_0$. For the converse, compare deterministic $t$ with $U=s\mathbf1_A+t\mathbf1_{A^c}$, where $s<t$ and $A\in\mathcal F_s$. The resulting identity $\mathbb E[(X_t-X_s)\mathbf1_A]=0$ is precisely the defining test for [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md). In the forward direction, use the [optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) for a [càdlàg martingale](cadlag-martingale.md) under the [usual conditions for a filtration](usual-conditions-for-a-filtration.md).
