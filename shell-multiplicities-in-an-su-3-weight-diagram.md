<h1 id="shell-multiplicities-in-an-su-3-weight-diagram">Shell multiplicities in an SU(3) weight diagram</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Weight diagram](weight-diagram.md)

Let $P_{a,b}$ be the convex hull of the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) orbit of the [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $a\omega_1+b\omega_2$ for the [SU(3) Lie algebra](su-3-lie-algebra.md). Restrict the displayed formula to the [weight lattice](weight-lattice.md) coset $p\omega_1+q\omega_2+$ root lattice. It describes successive inward shells: the outer boundary has [weight multiplicity](weight-multiplicity.md) one, each inward shell increases it by one, and the final triangular region has the constant value $\min(p,q)+1$. When $p=q$ that region is a single point. A triangular diagram with $p=0$ or $q=0$ has multiplicity one throughout. This is a geometric form of the [dominant weight multiplicity formula for sl3](dominant-weight-multiplicity-formula-for-sl3.md), transported by Weyl reflections.
