# Smoothness of an algebraic variety

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth algebraic variety](smooth-algebraic-variety.md)

Over a [perfect field](perfect-field.md), a point of an [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) is smooth exactly when its [local ring](local-ring.md) is a [regular local ring](regular-local-ring.md). The [smooth locus of a variety](smooth-locus-of-a-variety.md) is open; its closed complement is the [singular locus](singular-locus.md).
