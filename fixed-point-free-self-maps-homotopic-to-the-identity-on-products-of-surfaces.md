# Fixed-point-free self-maps homotopic to the identity on products of surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md)

Such a self-map of $\Sigma_{g_1}\times\Sigma_{g_2}$ exists exactly when one factor is a torus. Necessity follows because its [Lefschetz number](lefschetz-number.md) equals the [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) $4(1-g_1)(1-g_2)$ and must vanish. Sufficiency is translation by a nonzero point in the torus factor, times the identity on the other factor. The condition does not determine the product $g_1g_2$, since the other genus can be arbitrary.
