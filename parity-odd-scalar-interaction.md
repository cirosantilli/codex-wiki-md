# Parity-odd scalar interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md)

A parity-odd scalar interaction is rotationally invariant but changes sign under [spatial reflection](spatial-reflection.md). It must contain a [pseudoscalar](pseudoscalar.md) contraction, such as a [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md) contracted with three spatial gradients.
