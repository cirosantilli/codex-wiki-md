# Decimal trailing-zero pairing function

↑ **Parent:** [Pairing function](pairing-function.md)

The bracket enumerates the positive [integers](integer.md) not divisible by $10$. Every positive [integer](integer.md) has exactly one decomposition into a power of $10$ times such an integer, so this formula is a [bijection](bijection.md) from $\mathbb N^2$ to $\mathbb N$. Subtracting one in the codomain includes zero without requiring a trailing-zero convention for its decimal representation. For the inverse, write $N+1=10^i q$ with $10\nmid q$, then $j=q-1-\lfloor q/10\rfloor$.
