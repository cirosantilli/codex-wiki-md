# Flocculation

↑ **Parent:** [Particle aggregation](particle-aggregation.md)

Association of dispersed particles into larger clusters, often after repulsive stabilization is weakened. Salt screening, reduced surface charge, [polymer](polymer-split.md) bridging or [depletion force](depletion-force.md) can promote it. Clusters may sediment rapidly; preventing aggregation and preventing gravitational separation are distinct stability requirements.
