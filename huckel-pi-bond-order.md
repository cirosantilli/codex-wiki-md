<h1 id="huckel-pi-bond-order">Hückel pi bond order</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hückel method](huckel-method.md)

For real [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) site functions, $p_{ij}=\sum_k f_k c_{ik}c_{jk}$, where $f_k$ is orbital occupation. It measures the off-diagonal pi density [matrix](matrix.md); a complete C–C bond also contains a sigma contribution.
