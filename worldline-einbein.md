# Worldline einbein

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic particle phase-space action](relativistic-particle-phase-space-action.md)

An einbein specifies a one-dimensional [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) along a [worldline](world-line.md). In the [relativistic particle phase-space action](relativistic-particle-phase-space-action.md) it is the [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) for the [mass-shell condition](string-mass-shell-condition.md) and transforms as $\delta e=\dot\epsilon$ under the corresponding canonical [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md). After eliminating the [momentum](momentum.md), its geometric interpretation depends on the normalization of the particle [action](action.md).
