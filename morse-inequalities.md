# Morse inequalities

↑ **Parent:** [Morse chain complex](morse-chain-complex.md)

For a closed [manifold](topological-manifold.md) and a [Morse function](morse-function.md), the displayed strong inequalities hold for every $j$, with equality at the top dimension. Indeed the [Morse chain complex](morse-chain-complex.md) rank formula makes the alternating sum equal to $r_{j+1}\ge0$. Adding the inequalities at $j$ and $j-1$ gives $c_j\ge b_j$. The weak inequalities, even together with equality of [Euler characteristics](euler-characteristic.md), are less restrictive: $b=(1,0,0,1)$ and $c=(2,0,0,2)$ satisfy those numerical conditions but violate the strong inequality at $j=1$.
