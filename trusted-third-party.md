# Trusted third party

↑ **Parent:** [Computer security](computer-security.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trusted_third_party)

A participant whose correct behaviour and protection of secret state are assumptions of a security argument. It can mediate [authentication](authentication.md) and [session key transport](session-key-transport.md), but the [protocol](communication-protocol.md) still needs to authenticate its responses.
