# Synaptic normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Synaptic competition](synaptic-competition.md)

A learning rule can constrain the sum or norm of incoming synaptic weights to prevent unbounded [Hebbian learning](hebbian-learning.md). For $\sum_iw_{ai}=S$, subtracting the mean unconstrained update from each update preserves the sum. Bounds and renormalization may also be needed to keep excitatory weights nonnegative. Normalization of learning weights is distinct from divisive gain control in instantaneous neural responses.
