# Cycloid

↑ **Parent:** [Parametric curve](parametric-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cycloid)

A cycloid is the [plane curve](plane-curve.md) traced by a point on a circle rolling without slipping along a straight line. A parametrization with rolling radius $R$ is $x=R(\theta-\sin\theta)$, $y=R(1-\cos\theta)$; reversing the sign of $y$ reflects the curve. Its cusps occur at integer multiples of $2\pi$. A descending cycloidal arc solves the classical [brachistochrone problem](brachistochrone-problem.md).
