# Latin square

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_square)

A Latin square of order $q$ is a $q\times q$ array of $q$ symbols, each occurring once in each row and column. Used as a [row-column design](row-column-design.md), its symbols label [treatments](treatment.md). Centered [treatment](treatment.md) indicators are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) to both row and column indicators, because every row-treatment and column-treatment pair occurs once. This balance separates additive effects from two nuisance directions.
