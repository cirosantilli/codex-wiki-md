# Globular-cluster age constraint on cosmology

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar age](stellar-age.md)

A cosmological age must exceed the ages of its oldest stellar populations plus their formation interval. [globular cluster](globular-cluster.md) ages use the [main-sequence turnoff](main-sequence-turnoff.md) with modeled distances, composition, nuclear rates and stellar transport. Their errors are correlated with these inputs, so a lower age bound must propagate the stellar and distance uncertainties rather than treating an isochrone as an exact clock.[Abstract and age/formation-time analysis](new-globular-cluster-age-estimates-and-constraints-on-the-cosmic-equation-of-state-and-the-matter-density-of-the-universe-by-lawrence-m-krauss-and-brian-chaboyer.md)
