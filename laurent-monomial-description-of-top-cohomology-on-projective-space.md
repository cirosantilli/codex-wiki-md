# Laurent-monomial description of top cohomology on projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Twisting sheaf on projective space](twisting-sheaf-on-projective-space.md)

For $n\ge1$, the top [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md) of the standard affine cover of [projective space](projective-space-split.md) is the degree-$m$ quotient of the Laurent ring by the sum of rings in which at least one variable has not been inverted. Its basis consists of [Laurent monomials](laurent-monomial.md) $X_0^{a_0}\cdots X_n^{a_n}$ with every $a_i<0$ and $\sum_i a_i=m$. Hence it vanishes for $m\ge-n$ and has dimension $\binom{-m-1}{n}$ for $m\le-n-1$.
