# Finite extensions preserve finite pro-p presentations

↑ **Parent:** [Finite pro-p presentation](finite-pro-p-presentation.md)

Suppose $K$ is an open [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of a [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) $G$ and has a [finite pro-p presentation](finite-pro-p-presentation.md). Choose lifts of generators of the finite quotient $G/K$. Add finitely many relators giving their conjugation and inverse conjugation on the generators of $K$, and replace each quotient relator by its value in $K$. The resulting finite [finite pro-p presentation](finite-pro-p-presentation.md) maps onto $G$. Its closed subgroup generated by the kernel generators is isomorphic to $K$, and its quotient by that subgroup is exactly $G/K$, so the map is also injective.
