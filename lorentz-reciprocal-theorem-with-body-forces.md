# Lorentz reciprocal theorem with body forces

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz reciprocal theorem for Stokes flow](lorentz-reciprocal-theorem-for-stokes-flow.md)

For two incompressible [Stokes flows](stokes-flow-split.md) with the same [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md), $\nabla\cdot\sigma^{(a)}+\mathbf f^{(a)}=0$. The divergence of $u_i^{(2)}\sigma_{ij}^{(1)}-u_i^{(1)}\sigma_{ij}^{(2)}$ equals $-\mathbf u^{(2)}\cdot\mathbf f^{(1)}+\mathbf u^{(1)}\cdot\mathbf f^{(2)}$. The cross-[strain-rate tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md) products cancel by reciprocity. Integrating gives equality of the two surface cross-work terms after adding the corresponding volume body-force work. The outward domain normal must be used consistently, especially at an exterior domain's inner surface.
