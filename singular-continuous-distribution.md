# Singular continuous distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous probability distribution](continuous-probability-distribution-split.md)

A [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) is singular continuous if it has no point masses and assigns probability one to a [set](set-split.md) of zero [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). Its [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is continuous, but the distribution has no [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) with respect to [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). This distinguishes continuity of the [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) from an [absolutely continuous probability distribution](absolutely-continuous-probability-distribution.md), whose probabilities are [integrals](integral-split.md) of a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md).
