# Venous reservoir

↑ **Parent:** [Vein](vein.md)

A lumped [venous reservoir](venous-reservoir.md) stores [blood](blood.md) at a [pressure](pressure-split.md) determined by its volume and [vascular compliance](vascular-compliance.md). If its inflow and outflow are $Q_{\mathrm{in}}$, $Q_{\mathrm{out}}$, the constant-compliance approximation gives $C_v\dot p_v=Q_{\mathrm{in}}-Q_{\mathrm{out}}$. It forms the filling side of a [three-compartment ventricular circulation model](three-compartment-ventricular-circulation-model.md).
