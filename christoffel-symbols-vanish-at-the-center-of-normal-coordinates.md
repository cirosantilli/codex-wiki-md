# Christoffel symbols vanish at the center of normal coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic normal coordinates](geodesic-normal-coordinates.md)

In [geodesic normal coordinates](geodesic-normal-coordinates.md) at $p$, radial [geodesics](geodesic.md) have coordinates $x(t)=tv$. The [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) at $t=0$ gives $\Gamma^i_{jk}(p)v^jv^k=0$ for every $v$. The [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) is torsion free, making these coefficients symmetric in $j,k$; the [polarization identity](polarization-identity.md) then gives $\Gamma^i_{jk}(p)=0$. This does not imply vanishing curvature, which depends on derivatives of the coefficients.
