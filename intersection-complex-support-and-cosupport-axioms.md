# Intersection-complex support and cosupport axioms

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection complex](intersection-complex.md)

For an isolated singular point of an $N$-dimensional oriented pseudomanifold, the unshifted middle [intersection complex](intersection-complex.md) restricts to $\mathbb Q$ on the regular stratum and has no negative [cohomology](cohomology-split.md). Its support condition is $H^j(i_x^*IC_{\bar p})=0$ for $j>\bar p(N)$; its cosupport condition is $H^j(i_x^!IC_{\bar p})=0$ for $j\le\bar p(N)+1$. These conditions, together with constructibility and normalization, characterize it. On a [Witt space](witt-space.md) they are exchanged by $IC\cong D(IC)[-N]$.
