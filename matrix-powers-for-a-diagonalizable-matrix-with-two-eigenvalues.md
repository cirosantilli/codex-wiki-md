# Matrix powers for a diagonalizable matrix with two eigenvalues

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix power](matrix-power.md)

If a [diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md) has distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $r,s$, its [spectral projections of a diagonalizable matrix](spectral-projection-of-a-diagonalizable-matrix.md) are $(A-sI)/(r-s)$ and $(rI-A)/(r-s)$. Raising the diagonal form to a nonnegative integer power gives the displayed formula. Equivalently, interpolate the scalar function $t^n$ by a degree-one polynomial at $r,s$, then apply it to $A$. The [diagonalizability](diagonalizable-matrix.md) assumption matters: knowledge of two eigenvalues alone does not exclude nontrivial [Jordan blocks](jordan-block.md).
