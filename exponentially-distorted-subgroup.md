# Exponentially distorted subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Subgroup distortion](subgroup-distortion.md)

A subgroup is exponentially distorted when elements with intrinsic length exponential in $n$ can have ambient length linear in $n$. In $\operatorname{BS}(1,2)$, the identity $b^nab^{-n}=a^{2^n}$ makes the cyclic subgroup generated by $a$ exponentially distorted.
