# Lagrange planetary equations

↑ **Parent:** [Disturbing function](disturbing-function.md)

The Lagrange planetary equations express changes of osculating [orbital elements](orbital-element.md) in terms of derivatives of a [disturbing function](disturbing-function.md). Their small-eccentricity secular limit becomes a linear equation for [complex eccentricity](complex-eccentricity.md).
