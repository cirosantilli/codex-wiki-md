# Quantum gate fidelity

↑ **Parent:** [Operation fidelity](operation-fidelity.md)

An [operation fidelity](operation-fidelity.md) used to compare a physically implemented quantum gate with its intended operation. A quantitative value requires a declared input-state average or another explicit channel-comparison convention; different conventions need not give the same number.
