# Lattice-ordered group homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md)

A [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) preserving both [join](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) and [meet](greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). A bijective such map is an isomorphism of [lattice-ordered groups](lattice-ordered-group.md); an injective one is an embedding.
