# Whitney sum of vector bundles

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

The fiberwise direct sum of two [vector bundles](vector-bundle.md) over the same base. It is formed from the fiber product of their total spaces, with the direct-sum linear structure in each fiber. Transition matrices are block diagonal.
