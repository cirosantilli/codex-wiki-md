# Baryon asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Baryogenesis](baryogenesis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baryon_asymmetry)

The [baryon asymmetry](baryon-asymmetry.md) is an excess of [baryons](baryon.md) over [antibaryons](antibaryon.md). Here $n_B-n_{\bar B}$ counts net [baryon number](baryon-number.md) per unit volume, including the baryons bound in [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus.md). It is dynamically generated by [baryogenesis](baryogenesis.md). Once the [antibaryon](antibaryon.md) abundance is negligible, its photon-normalized value agrees with the usual [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md).
