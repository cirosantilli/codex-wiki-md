# Q-linear convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Linear convergence](linear-convergence.md)

A sequence with errors $e_k=x_k-x^*$ converges Q-linearly in a specified norm if there is a constant $q<1$ with $\|e_{k+1}\|\leq q\|e_k\|$ eventually. The limsup of consecutive nonzero error ratios is its asymptotic factor. This is stronger than merely having a bound $\|e_k\|\leq Cq^k$, which is called R-linear convergence. The norm and whether an iteration is one coordinate step or an entire sweep must be specified when quoting a factor.
