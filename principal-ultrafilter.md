# Principal ultrafilter

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md)

The principal ultrafilter at $x\in X$ is $\{A\subseteq X:x\in A\}$. An ultraproduct by a principal ultrafilter is isomorphic to the factor indexed by $x$.
