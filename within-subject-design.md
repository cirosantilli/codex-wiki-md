# Within-subject design

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments-split.md)

A design in which the same participant supplies measurements under several levels of a factor. The observations are dependent, so the analysis must preserve the pairing or repeated-measure structure. Order, practice and [carryover effects](carryover-effect.md) can require design controls.
