# Image-kernel orthogonality for an adjoint

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md)

For a [linear map](linear-map.md) $T:V\to W$ between finite-dimensional [inner product spaces](inner-product-space.md),

$$
(\operatorname{im}T)^\perp=\ker T^*,
\qquad
\operatorname{im}T=(\ker T^*)^\perp.
$$

The first equality follows directly from the defining identity for the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md); the second follows by taking [orthogonal complements](orthogonal-complement.md). In an infinite-dimensional [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), the second equality generally requires closure of the image.
