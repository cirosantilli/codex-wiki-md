# Arithmetic-geometric mean iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric mean](geometric-mean.md)

Starting from $0<a_1\leq b_1$, replace the lower endpoint by the [geometric mean](geometric-mean.md) and the upper endpoint by the [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md). The [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) gives $a_n\leq a_{n+1}\leq b_{n+1}\leq b_n$. The [bounded monotone sequence theorem](bounded-monotone-sequence-theorem.md) gives limits, and the arithmetic recurrence forces them to coincide. The limiting value is the arithmetic-geometric mean of the initial pair.
