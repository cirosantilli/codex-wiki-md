# Annual modulation in dark-matter direct detection

↑ **Parent:** [Dark-matter direct detection](dark-matter-direct-detection.md)

The Earth's changing velocity relative to a halo changes the recoil-rate integral above a detector threshold. Its phase and [energy](energy-split.md) dependence depend on the velocity distribution and interaction, so a sinusoidal fit is a model approximation. Seasonal instrumental or environmental backgrounds can also vary annually; [energy](energy-split.md) [radiation spectrum](radiation-spectrum.md), multiple-hit samples, time stability and independent targets help test a proposed dark-matter interpretation.
