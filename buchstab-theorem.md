# Buchstab theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Buchstab function](buchstab-function.md)

For each fixed $u>1$, the number $\Phi(z^u,z)$ of [z-sieved numbers](rough-number.md) at most $z^u$ satisfies $\Phi(z^u,z)\sim z^u w(u)/\log z$ as $z\to\infty$. The fixed-$u$ asymptotic does not extend to $u=1$.
