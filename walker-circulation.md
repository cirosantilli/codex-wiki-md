# Walker circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walker_circulation)

A tropical zonal overturning circulation in the [terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md), coupled to east–west ocean [temperature](temperature-split.md) contrasts. Its mean Pacific branch has rising air over the warmer western Pacific and sinking air farther east. The circulation is named after [Gilbert Walker](gilbert-walker.md).
