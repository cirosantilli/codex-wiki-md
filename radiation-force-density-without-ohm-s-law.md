<h1 id="radiation-force-density-without-ohm-s-law">Radiation-force density without Ohm's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md)

For a monochromatic transverse field depending on $x,t$, with vacuum permittivity and permeability and arbitrary induced transverse current, Ampère's law gives $J_y=-\partial_xH_z-\epsilon_0\partial_tE_y$. Multiplying by $\mu_0H_z$ and averaging [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives the displayed gradient. No constitutive assumption $J=\sigma E$ is required. A steady average removes the field-momentum time derivative.
