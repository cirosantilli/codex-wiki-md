<h1 id="integral-kunneth-torsion-polynomial">Integral Künneth torsion polynomial</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md)

For spaces whose integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) has only free and $\mathbb Z/2$ summands, let $F$ encode free ranks and $T$ count order-two summands by degree. An additively split integral [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) gives $F_{A\times B}=F_AF_B$ and the displayed rule for $T$. The $t^{-1}$ term is the cohomological degree shift of the [Tor functor](tor-functor.md) contribution. The formula requires this torsion type and the usual finite-type hypotheses.
