# Omega-measurable cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Measurable cardinal](measurable-cardinal.md)

A cardinal is omega-measurable if it carries a [countably complete ultrafilter](countably-complete-ultrafilter.md) that is a [nonprincipal ultrafilter](nonprincipal-ultrafilter.md). Countable completeness rules out countable underlying sets. Such a measure is weaker than the cardinal's own full completeness requirement, but the [least omega-measurable cardinal is measurable](least-omega-measurable-cardinal-is-measurable.md).
