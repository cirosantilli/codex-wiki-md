# Projective bundle relation in complex cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Chern class in complex cobordism](chern-class-in-complex-cobordism.md)

The relation uses $x=c_1(S)$ for the tautological line on the projectivization of $E$. It follows from the rank of the complementary bundle and Whitney multiplication. The alternating signs are ring subtraction; they do not assert that the [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md) of a dual line is the additive negative in [complex cobordism](complex-cobordism.md), where duality uses the formal-group inverse.
