# Multiplicity of a conjugate point

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate point](conjugate-point.md)

Along a nonconstant affinely parametrized [geodesic](geodesic.md), the multiplicity of a [conjugate point](conjugate-point.md) at positive time $t_0$ is the dimension of the space of [Jacobi fields](jacobi-field.md) vanishing at both endpoints. The tangential component of such a [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) is affine and vanishes at both endpoints, hence is zero. Uniqueness for the [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) equation embeds this space into the $(n-1)$-dimensional normal space by $J\mapsto D_tJ(0)$. Thus its dimension is at most $n-1$.
