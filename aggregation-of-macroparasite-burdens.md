# Aggregation of macroparasite burdens

↑ **Parent:** [Macroparasite burden model](macroparasite-burden-model.md)

If host-specific [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) [mean](expected-value.md) burden has a [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md), conditional [Poisson distributions](poisson-distribution.md) produce a [Poisson-gamma mixture](poisson-gamma-mixture.md), hence a [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md). With [mean](expected-value.md) $m$ and shape $k$, its zero-burden [probability](probability.md) is $(1+m/k)^{-k}$. Decreasing $k$ increases aggregation: a smaller infected-host [prevalence](prevalence.md) can accompany the same [mean](expected-value.md) burden and more heavily infected individuals.
