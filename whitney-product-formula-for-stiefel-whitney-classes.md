<h1 id="whitney-product-formula-for-stiefel-whitney-classes">Whitney product formula for Stiefel–Whitney classes</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Splitting principle for real vector bundles](splitting-principle-for-real-vector-bundles.md)

For real vector bundles $E$ and $F$,

$$
w(E\oplus F)=w(E)w(F),
\qquad
w_k(E\oplus F)=\sum_{i+j=k}w_i(E)w_j(F).
$$

After applying the [splitting principle for real vector bundles](splitting-principle-for-real-vector-bundles.md), both sides are the product of $1+w_1(L)$ over all line summands.
