# Schwarz-Pick theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz lemma](schwarz-lemma.md)

A holomorphic self-map of the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) contracts its pseudohyperbolic distance. Conjugating by [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) that send one chosen source point and its image to zero reduces the statement to the [Schwarz lemma](schwarz-lemma.md). In the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) this gives

$$
\left|\frac{f(z)-f(w)}{f(z)-\overline{f(w)}}\right|\leq\left|\frac{z-w}{z-\overline w}\right|.
$$

If $f(iY)=iY+o(Y)$ at infinity, multiply the equivalent inequality between one minus the squared distances by $Y$ and let $Y\to\infty$. It yields $\operatorname{Im}f(z)\geq\operatorname{Im}z$. Applied to the inverse [mapping-out function](mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md) of a [compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md), this proves the nonnegativity of its [half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md).
