# Molecule bookkeeping

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)

Four [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) contain eight [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and four [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), twelve constituent [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) altogether. Counting the constituents does not mean they have been separated by breaking [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md). The same sample has different counts because the specified entities differ.

**[Figure 2](#image-molecule-atom-and-amount-bookkeeping). One water sample: molecule, constituent chemical atom, and amount of substance bookkeeping**. An original four-[molecule](molecule-split.md) inventory cartoon, not a measurement or a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md). Dashed boundaries identify whole [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md); each contains two [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and one [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). All three counts describe the same bound sample. The [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) uses the exact [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md); the displayed decimal is rounded.

For $N$ [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md), their [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) is $n=N/N_{\mathrm A}$, while the constituent [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and [oxygen](oxygen.md) inventories correspond to $2n$ and $n$ respectively. The exact [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) is $N_{\mathrm A}=6.02214076\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}$. The [mole](mole-unit.md) applies to specified entities, including [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), [molecules](molecule-split.md), and [ions](ion.md).[SI base unit: mole, definition and the requirement to specify the elementary entity](si-base-unit-mole-by-bureau-international-des-poids-et-mesures.md) Thus four [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) correspond to approximately $6.6422\times10^{-24}\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [water](water.md), but twice that [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of constituent [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). A macroscopic conversion through measured [mass](mass.md) additionally needs the relevant [molar mass](molar-mass.md), as in [water mass to molecular count](water-mass-to-molecular-count.md).
