# Reciprocal-predictor regression

↑ **Parent:** [Linear regression](linear-regression-split.md)

A reciprocal-predictor [linear regression](linear-regression-split.md) uses $\mathbb E(Y\mid v)=\alpha+\beta/v$ for nonzero predictor $v$. It remains linear in its coefficients. Its flattening shape can suit a saturating response over a positive predictor range; residual checks and [cross-validation](cross-validation.md) must assess adequacy, and extrapolation toward zero is hazardous.
