# Conformal invariance of extremal length

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal length](extremal-length.md)

For a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) $f$ and a [conformal metric](conformal-metric.md) $\rho$ on its image, the pullback density is $\rho(f(z))|f'(z)|$. The [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) formula preserves its metric [area](surface-area.md), and the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) preserves the length of each corresponding curve. Applying this to $f$ and its inverse proves the title equality. This allows [extremal length](extremal-length.md) computations on rectangles, discs and annuli to be transported to other domains.
