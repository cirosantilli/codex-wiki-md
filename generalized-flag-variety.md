# Generalized flag variety

↑ **Parent:** [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_flag_variety)

For a connected reductive algebraic group $G$ and a [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md) $B$, the quotient $G/B$ is its complete flag variety. Quotients $G/P$ by [parabolic subgroups](parabolic-subgroup.md) are partial flag varieties.
