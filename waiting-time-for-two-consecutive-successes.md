# Waiting time for two consecutive successes

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping time](stopping-time.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) trials of success [probability](probability.md) $p\in(0,1]$, let $T$ be the first index completing two consecutive successes. If $e_0,e_1$ are the mean remaining times with no trailing success and with one trailing success, conditioning gives $e_0=1+(1-p)e_0+pe_1$ and $e_1=1+(1-p)e_0$. Solving yields $e_0=(1+p)/p^2$, so fair trials have mean waiting time six. Disjoint pairs give a geometric tail bound and justify finiteness before solving the equations. Overlapping pairs are not [independent](independent-random-variables.md) trials.
