# Legendre necessary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Second variation](second-variation.md)

A nonnegative [second variation](second-variation.md) requires its highest-[derivative](derivative.md) coefficient $P=f_{vv}$ to be nonnegative. If it were negative on an interval, a smooth compactly supported factor times $\sin(nx)$ would have negative [derivative](derivative.md) energy of order $n^2$, while its zero-order energy stays bounded. Large $n$ would contradict nonnegativity. This is a local necessary condition; it does not rule out negative directions caused by the lower-order terms.
