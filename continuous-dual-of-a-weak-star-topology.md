# Continuous dual of a weak-star topology

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md)

A [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on $X^*$ is continuous for the [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md) exactly when it is evaluation at an element of $X$. Continuity makes it vanish on the common kernel of finitely many evaluations, so it is a linear combination of those evaluations. The [canonical embedding into the bidual](canonical-embedding-into-the-bidual.md) identifies this dual with $X$.
