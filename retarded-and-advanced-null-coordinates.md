# Retarded and advanced null coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Null coordinates in two-dimensional Minkowski spacetime](null-coordinates-in-two-dimensional-minkowski-spacetime.md)

In the $(t,z)$ plane, $u=t-z$ is retarded time and $v=t+z$ is advanced time. The radial part of the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) is $-dt^2+dz^2=-du\,dv$.
