# Winged edge

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary representation](boundary-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winged_edge)

An edge record stores endpoint [vertices of a polyhedron](vertex-of-a-polyhedron.md), its two incident faces, and predecessor and successor edges for each face. These four boundary-neighbour references are the wings. Face and [vertex of a polyhedron](vertex-of-a-polyhedron.md) records also carry entry references for traversal. Local edits must preserve all reciprocal incidence references and orientations.
