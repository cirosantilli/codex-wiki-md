# Hochschild cup product

↑ **Parent:** [Hochschild cochain complex](hochschild-cochain-complex.md)

For $f\in C^p(A,A)$ and $g\in C^q(A,A)$, set $(f\smile g)(a_1,\ldots,a_{p+q})=f(a_1,\ldots,a_p)g(a_{p+1},\ldots,a_{p+q})$. This associative cochain multiplication descends to [Hochschild cohomology](hochschild-cohomology.md), where it gives a [graded commutative algebra](graded-commutative-algebra.md). It is one operation in the [Gerstenhaber algebra](gerstenhaber-algebra.md) structure.
