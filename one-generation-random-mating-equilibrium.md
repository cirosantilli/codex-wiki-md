# One-generation random-mating equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Hardy-Weinberg principle](hardy-weinberg-principle.md)

If parental [genotype](genotype.md) frequencies are $(u,v,w)$ and the two parental gametes are independently sampled from the same [allele](allele.md) pool, [Mendelian segregation](mendelian-segregation.md) gives allele frequency $p=u+v/2$. Their independent union has the displayed [Hardy-Weinberg proportions](hardy-weinberg-principle.md), irrespective of parental genotype proportions. Equal allele frequencies in the two sexes and no transmission or viability distortion are part of this conclusion.
