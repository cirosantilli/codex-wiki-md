<h1 id="s02/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $m(r)$ for enclosed mass. The [stellar hydrostatic equation](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure-support-equation.md) is $dP/dr=-Gm\rho/r^2$. If [radiation pressure](../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) dominates, compare it directly with the [radiative diffusion](../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md) result for $dP_r/dr$:

$$
\frac{\kappa\rho L}{4\pi r^2c}\simeq\frac{Gm\rho}{r^2},\qquad
\boxed{L\simeq\frac{4\pi Gc}{\kappa_{\rm th}}M\propto M.}
$$

This is the limiting [Eddington luminosity](../../../../../eddington-luminosity.md) scaling, assuming fixed composition and electron-scattering [opacity](../../../../../opacity.md).

If gas pressure dominates, use [stellar homology](../../../../../stellar-homology.md): characteristic density $\rho\sim M/R_*^3$ and pressure $P\sim GM^2/R_*^4$. The [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) then gives

$$
T\sim\frac{\mu m_H}{k_B}\frac{GM}{R_*}.
$$

Estimating $|dT/dr|\sim T/R_*$ in [radiative diffusion](../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md) gives

$$
L\sim\frac{ac}{\kappa}\frac{R_*T^4}{\rho}
\propto\frac{\mu^4M^3}{\kappa}.
$$

Thus at fixed $\mu$ and constant electron-scattering [opacity](../../../../../opacity.md), $\boxed{L\propto M^3}$. These are [stellar homology](../../../../../stellar-homology.md) estimates for radiative [stars](../../../../../star.md), not exact relations valid for arbitrary composition and internal transport.
