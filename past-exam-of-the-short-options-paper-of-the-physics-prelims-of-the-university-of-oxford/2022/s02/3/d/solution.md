<h1 id="s02/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The angle estimates the largest transverse region at decoupling that could have established causal contact after the big bang in the assumed decelerating model. The [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) has nearly the same [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) in directions separated by much larger angles. This is the [horizon problem](../../../../../horizon-problem.md): those regions have no shared causal history in that simple model, yet their thermodynamic conditions are strikingly alike. The [particle horizon](../../../../../particle-horizon.md) should be distinguished from the smaller [sound horizon](../../../../../sound-horizon.md), which controls acoustic propagation in the primordial fluid.

An early epoch dominated by a sufficiently large positive effective [cosmological constant](../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) gives $\ddot R>0$ and approximately $R\propto e^{Ht}$. During this [cosmic inflation](../../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md), the comoving Hubble length $c/(RH)$ shrinks. A patch that was initially in causal contact can expand to contain the whole presently observable [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) surface, while expansion suppresses spatial curvature. A finite period of [cosmic inflation](../../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) followed by reheating and the usual hot expansion therefore explains the uniformity without superluminal local signals. An eternally large unchanged [cosmological constant](../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) would prevent the later matter-dominated history; an exit from [cosmic inflation](../../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) and reheating are required. Sufficient duration and a suitable pre-inflationary causal patch are part of this explanation.
