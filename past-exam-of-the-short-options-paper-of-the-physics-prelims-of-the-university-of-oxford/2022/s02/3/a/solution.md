<h1 id="s02/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $\rho_r,\rho_m$ as energy per volume, as specified in the prose, and let $R$ have dimensions of length so that the positive curvature magnitude $k$ is dimensionless. The [Friedmann equation](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) for spatial curvature $-k$ is

$$
\boxed{H^2=\left(\frac{\dot R}{R}\right)^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}(\rho_r+\rho_m)+\frac{kc^2}{R^2}+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}.}
$$

For an energy density the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) is $\dot\rho+3H(\rho+P)=0$. Nonrelativistic matter has negligible pressure and [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) has $P_r=\rho_r/3$. Thus the four contributions to $H^2$ scale respectively as

$$
\boxed{R^{-4},\quad R^{-3},\quad R^{-2},\quad R^0.}
$$

For nonzero positive densities and $\Lambda>0$, the usual possible sequence is [radiation domination](../../../../../radiation-domination.md), [matter domination](../../../../../matter-domination.md), curvature domination, then [cosmological-constant domination](../../../../../cosmological-constant-domination.md). A distinct era for each is not guaranteed: coefficients may cause curvature to be overtaken by the [cosmological constant](../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) before it ever dominates. If $\Lambda=0$, curvature dominates asymptotically; negative $\Lambda$ does not give a late positive vacuum-dominated expansion.

The printed auxiliary relation $P_r=\rho_rc^2/3$ uses mass-density notation, in conflict with the stated energy-density definition. It is consistent if $\rho_r$ there is understood as energy density divided by $c^2$; in that convention the density term in the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) is $8\pi G\rho/3$. The two conventions must not be mixed.
