↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

- A [main-sequence star](../../../../../main-sequence-star.md) is supported by [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) and powered predominantly by hydrogen [nuclear fusion](../../../../../nuclear-fusion.md) in its core. More massive [main-sequence stars](../../../../../main-sequence-star.md) are generally hotter and more luminous and consume their fuel faster.
- On the [red giant branch](../../../../../red-giant-branch.md), core hydrogen is exhausted. Hydrogen-shell [nuclear fusion](../../../../../nuclear-fusion.md) surrounds an inert helium core; the envelope expands and cools while the [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) rises. In low-mass [stars](../../../../../star.md) the contracting helium core becomes supported by [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) before helium ignition.
- A [horizontal-branch star](../../../../../horizontal-branch.md) has core helium [nuclear fusion](../../../../../nuclear-fusion.md) and usually hydrogen-shell [nuclear fusion](../../../../../nuclear-fusion.md). Its [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) varies comparatively little across the branch, while envelope mass produces a wide range of [effective temperatures](../../../../../effective-temperature.md). It follows helium ignition in a low-mass [red giant](../../../../../red-giant.md).
- A [white dwarf](../../../../../white-dwarf.md) is a compact exposed remnant, typically with a carbon-oxygen interior after ordinary low- or intermediate-mass [stellar evolution](../../../../../stellar-evolution.md). [Electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) supports it; sustained core [nuclear fusion](../../../../../nuclear-fusion.md) has ended. It can be hot yet faint because its radius is small, and it moves down its cooling sequence as it loses stored thermal energy.
