<h1 id="s03/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [double-slit experiment](../../../../../double-slit-experiment.md), each [photon](../../../../../photon.md) is detected as a localized event carrying energy $h\nu$, while repeated events build the [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) pattern of coherent probability amplitudes from the two slits. Acquiring information that distinguishes the paths suppresses the [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md); splitting the classical intensity into two incoherent alternatives would not reproduce the fringes.

Put one plate before each slit at normal incidence, with the same known [refractive index](../../../../../refractive-index.md) $n$ and illumination of vacuum [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda$. Relative to the plate-free reference, their thickness difference $\Delta d=d_1-d_2$ produces an [optical path difference](../../../../../optical-path-difference.md) $(n-1)\Delta d$ and a phase shift

$$
\Delta\phi=\frac{2\pi(n-1)\Delta d}{\lambda}.
$$

Observe the displacement of the full [interference fringe](../../../../../interference-fringe.md) pattern, or scan a controllable reference phase and estimate $\Delta\phi$. This compares the plates directly; a detectable phase displacement establishes unequal thickness under the common-material assumption. For fringe spacing $\lambda D/b$, the displacement is $-D(n-1)\Delta d/b$, where $b$ is slit separation and $D$ the screen distance, with sign set by the slit labeling.

A differential phase measurement can resolve a small difference sensitively and avoid determining two large absolute thicknesses. It does not guarantee a universally faster or more photon-efficient test: noise, coherence, alignment, and required accuracy matter. Moreover a null phase shift at one [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) only implies

$$
\boxed{\Delta d=m\lambda/(n-1),\quad m\in\mathbb Z,}
$$

so equality requires a prior bound, a broadband or multiple-[wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) measurement, or continuously counting [interference fringes](../../../../../interference-fringe.md) as the plates are introduced. If the [refractive indices](../../../../../refractive-index.md) differ, this apparatus measures optical thickness difference rather than geometrical thickness difference.
