A pure bipartite [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md) is [entangled](../../../../../entangled-state.md) if it cannot be written as a [tensor product](../../../../../tensor-product.md) $|a\rangle\otimes|b\rangle$. More generally a mixed [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md) is [entangled](../../../../../entangled-state.md) if its [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) cannot be expressed as a convex mixture of product [density matrices](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md). Two applications are [quantum teleportation](../../../../../quantum-teleportation.md), which transfers an unknown [qubit](../../../../../qubit.md) using a shared entangled pair plus classical communication, and [superdense coding](../../../../../superdense-coding.md), which uses a shared entangled pair and transmission of one [qubit](../../../../../qubit.md) to communicate two classical bits. Neither permits faster-than-light communication.
