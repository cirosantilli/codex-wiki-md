<h1 id="s03/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Send a beam through a vertical [Stern-Gerlach spin measurement](../../../../../stern-gerlach-spin-measurement.md) apparatus, spatially resolve its two [spin](../../../../../spin.md) components, and block the negative-$z$ beam. The transmitted particles are prepared in $\boxed{|\uparrow\rangle}$. For an initially unpolarized beam, half the intensity survives ideally. This is conditional [quantum state preparation](../../../../../quantum-state-preparation.md) by a [projective measurement](../../../../../projective-measurement.md) and filtering.
