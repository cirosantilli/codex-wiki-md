↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A horizontal [Stern-Gerlach spin measurement](../../../../../../stern-gerlach-spin-measurement.md) measures $S_x$. The inputs are its orthogonal [eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md), so

$$
\boxed{p(x+|\psi_+)=1,\quad p(x+|\psi_-)=0.}
$$

The output beam identifies the input with certainty in a single ideal measurement.
