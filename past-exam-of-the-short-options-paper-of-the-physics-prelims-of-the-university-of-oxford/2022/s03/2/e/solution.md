<h1 id="s03/2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The first [Stern-Gerlach spin measurement](../../../../../stern-gerlach-spin-measurement.md) transmits $x+$ with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $|\langle x+|\uparrow\rangle|^2=1/2$. The selected [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md) $|x+\rangle$ then yields $z-$ with conditional [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $|\langle\downarrow|x+\rangle|^2=1/2$. Relative to the original beam,

$$
\boxed{p(x+\text{ then }z-)=\frac12\cdot\frac12=\frac14.}
$$

Among particles reaching the second apparatus, the negative-$z$ fraction is $1/2$; the requested fraction uses the original intensity and is $1/4$.
