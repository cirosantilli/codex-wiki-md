<h1 id="s03/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Stern-Gerlach experiment](../../../../../stern-gerlach-experiment.md) uses a nonuniform [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) to exert force $F_z\simeq\mu_z\,\partial_zB$ on a magnetic dipole. The two resolved beams reveal discrete projections of [spin](../../../../../spin.md), rather than a continuum of classical dipole orientations. For the ground-state [silver](../../../../../silver.md) [chemical atom](../../../../../atom-physics.md), filled shells contribute no net [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and the outer $5s$ [electron](../../../../../electron.md) has orbital [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) zero, leaving an effective [spin](../../../../../spin.md)-$1/2$ magnetic moment. This provides evidence for the electron's two-valued intrinsic [spin](../../../../../spin.md).

Neutral [silver](../../../../../silver.md) [chemical atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) can pass through the apparatus without the large charge-dependent [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) that would deflect and broaden a free-[electron](../../../../../electron.md) beam. Neutrality isolates the small magnetic-gradient force far more cleanly. The later parts use an ideal [spin](../../../../../spin.md) filter model; a literal free-[electron](../../../../../electron.md) apparatus requires additional control of charge forces.
