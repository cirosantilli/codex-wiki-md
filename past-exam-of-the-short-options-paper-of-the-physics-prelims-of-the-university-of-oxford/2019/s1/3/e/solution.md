<h1 id="s1/3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Shrink the positive outer [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) to the [branch cut](../../../../../branch-cut.md) $[-1,1]$. On its upper bank the chosen [branch of a multivalued function](../../../../../branch-of-a-multivalued-function.md) has $\sqrt{x^2-1}=i\sqrt{1-x^2}$ and on the lower bank it has the negative of this value. The upper bank runs from $1$ to $-1$, and the lower bank runs from $-1$ to $1$. The endpoint circles contribute $O(\sqrt\epsilon)$ and vanish. Hence

$$
\frac{3\pi i}{4}=2i\int_{-1}^1\frac{x^4}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,dx,\qquad
\boxed{\int_{-1}^1\frac{x^4}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,dx=\frac{3\pi}{8}.}
$$

The [change of variables](../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $x=\sin t$ independently reduces the result to the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) of $\sin^4t$ over $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$.
