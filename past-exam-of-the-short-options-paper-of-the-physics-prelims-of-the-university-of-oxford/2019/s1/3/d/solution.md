<h1 id="s1/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the [branch cut](../../../../../branch-cut.md) $[-1,1]$ and the [branch of a multivalued function](../../../../../branch-of-a-multivalued-function.md) normalized by $\sqrt{z^2-1}\sim z$. The [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) from the preceding solution has $z^{-1}$ coefficient $3/8$. Consequently every sufficiently large positively oriented circle gives

$$
\boxed{\oint_{C_\infty}f(z)\,dz=\frac{3\pi i}{4}.}
$$

Although $f$ grows cubically, its other [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) terms have zero [contour integral](../../../../../contour-integral.md). Choosing the opposite [branch of a multivalued function](../../../../../branch-of-a-multivalued-function.md) reverses the sign; neither the [branch of a multivalued function](../../../../../branch-of-a-multivalued-function.md) nor the [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) can be omitted from the answer.
