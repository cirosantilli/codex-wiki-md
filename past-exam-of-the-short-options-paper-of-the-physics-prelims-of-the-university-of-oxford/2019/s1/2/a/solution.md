<h1 id="s1/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $n\ge1$, $f=z^ne^{n/z}$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) at every finite nonzero point. At zero its [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) has infinitely many negative powers, so zero is an [essential singularity](../../../../../essential-singularity.md). If the [Riemann sphere](../../../../../riemann-sphere.md) is included, $f(1/w)=w^{-n}e^{nw}$ has a [pole](../../../../../pole.md) of order $n$ at $w=0$, so infinity is a [pole](../../../../../pole.md) of order $n$.
