<h1 id="s1/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

If $f(z)=\sum a_jz^j$, then $f(-z)=\sum(-1)^ja_jz^j$ and its [residue](../../../../../residue.md) at zero is $-a_{-1}$. The circle $|z-1|=2$ encloses zero and no other finite [singularity](../../../../../complex-singularity.md). Thus the [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\oint_{|z-1|=2}[f(z)-f(-z)]\,dz=4\pi i\frac{n^{n+1}}{(n+1)!}}
$$

for positive [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
