<h1 id="s1/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [complex derivative](../../../../../complex-derivative.md) computed along the real and imaginary directions gives $f'=u_x+iv_x=v_y-iu_y$. Equality yields the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md)

$$
\boxed{u_x=v_y,\qquad u_y=-v_x.}
$$

The [gradients](../../../../../gradient.md) satisfy $\nabla u\cdot\nabla v=u_xv_x+u_yv_y=0$. Since a [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is normal to a regular [level set](../../../../../level-set.md), the two families meet at right angles by [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) wherever $f'\ne0$. At a [critical point](../../../../../critical-point.md) the regular-curve argument fails: for $f=z^2$ both [gradients](../../../../../gradient.md) vanish at zero, so the assertion requires this qualification.
