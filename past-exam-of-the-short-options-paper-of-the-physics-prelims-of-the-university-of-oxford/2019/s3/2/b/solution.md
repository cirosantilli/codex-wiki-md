<h1 id="s3/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take a stationary initial electron of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $m_e$, a photon incident along $x$, and a nonrelativistic recoil [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) of magnitude $v$. Choose $\theta$ above $x$ for the scattered photon and $\phi$ below $x$ for the electron. [Conservation of momentum](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{h\nu}{c}=\frac{h\nu'}{c}\cos\theta+m_ev\cos\phi,\qquad
0=\frac{h\nu'}c\sin\theta-m_ev\sin\phi.}
$$

The electron's unchanged rest [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) cancels in [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md), leaving

$$
\boxed{h\nu=h\nu'+\frac12m_ev^2.}
$$

Equivalently use $h\nu/c=h/\lambda$ and $h\nu=hc/\lambda$. The [nonrelativistic limit](../../../../../nonrelativistic-limit.md) requires $v/c\ll1$, and treating the electron as free requires its binding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to be negligible on the scale of the collision.
