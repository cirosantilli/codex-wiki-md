<h1 id="s3/1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Using the stipulated $570\,\mathrm{nm}$ as a wavelength-density peak, [Wien's displacement law](../../../../../wien-s-displacement-law.md) gives

$$
\boxed{T_{\rm inferred}=\frac{0.002897}{570\times10^{-9}}\simeq5.08\times10^3\,\mathrm K.}
$$

This is a rough estimate from the supplied characteristic [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md), not an accurate measurement of the Sun's [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md). The Sun emits a broad, approximately thermal visible [spectrum](../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) and appears white outside the atmosphere; its familiar yellow appearance does not identify a monochromatic thermal peak. A photospheric [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) near $5770\,\mathrm K$ instead corresponds to a wavelength-density maximum near $502\,\mathrm{nm}$.[Statement that the Sun emits all visible colours and appears white](../../../../../sun-colouring-page-by-nasa-space-place.md)[Surface temperature approximately 5770 K](../../../../../solar-physics-facts-by-nasa-marshall.md)

The core must be much hotter to provide the [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) required for [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) and to sustain [nuclear fusion](../../../../../nuclear-fusion.md). Outward [energy transport](../../../../../energy-transport.md) through an opaque interior proceeds down a [temperature gradient](../../../../../temperature-gradient.md), by [radiative diffusion](../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md) and then [convection](../../../../../convection-split.md) through much of the outer envelope. The observable [photosphere](../../../../../photosphere.md) radiates to space at the temperature needed to carry the solar [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md), $L_\odot=4\pi R_\odot^2\sigma T_{\rm eff}^4$; it is not an unobstructed view of the hot core. The printed core estimate above $20$ million degrees is also too high for the present Sun: the standard central [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is about $1.56\times10^7\,\mathrm K$. This correction leaves the large core-to-surface contrast and its explanation intact.[Central temperature approximately 15,600,000 K](../../../../../solar-physics-facts-by-nasa-marshall.md)
