<h1 id="s3/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume the given [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is zero outside $[0,L]$. [Wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) requires

$$
1=\int_0^L|Nx|^2dx=|N|^2\frac{L^3}{3},\qquad
\boxed{|N|=\sqrt{\frac3{L^3}}.}
$$

An arbitrary overall [quantum phase](../../../../../quantum-phase.md) is unobservable, so choose the positive real representative $N=\sqrt3/L^{3/2}$.

The [linear ramp state in an infinite square well](../../../../../linear-ramp-state-in-an-infinite-square-well.md) has a boundary subtlety: the limiting value at $L$ is $\sqrt{3/L}$, rather than zero. The function is still a normalized element of the well's [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) $L^2(0,L)$, so [position measurement](../../../../../position-measurement.md) and energy-projection probabilities are meaningful. It is not in the domain of the Dirichlet [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md), nor is it a finite-energy box state. Altering the value at the single endpoint does not cure its nonzero boundary trace; a physical finite-energy preparation must smooth the function to zero near the wall. The following probabilities refer to the supplied idealized state.
