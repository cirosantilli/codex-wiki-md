<h1 id="s3/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply the position version of the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) to the normalized [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md):

$$
\boxed{\Pr(0\le x\le L/3)=\int_0^{L/3}|\Psi(x)|^2dx=\frac3{L^3}\left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^{L/3}=\frac1{27}\simeq0.0370.}
$$

The result is independent of $L$ because the state and the measurement interval scale together. Most [position probability](../../../../../position-probability.md) lies towards the right wall, since the [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) grows as $x^2$.
