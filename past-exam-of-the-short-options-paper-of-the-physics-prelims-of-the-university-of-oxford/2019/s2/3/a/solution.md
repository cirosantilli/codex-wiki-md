↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Milky Way](../../../../../milky-way.md) has a thin stellar [galactic disk](../../../../../galactic-disk.md), radius roughly $15$–$20\,\mathrm{kpc}$ and scale height about $0.3\,\mathrm{kpc}$, containing gas, dust, young and old stars, [spiral arms](../../../../../spiral-arm.md) and most current [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md). Its stellar mass is a few $10^{10}M_\odot$. A thicker, older [galactic disk](../../../../../galactic-disk.md) has scale height around $1\,\mathrm{kpc}$ and stellar mass of order $10^{10}M_\odot$, with little cold gas.

The central [galactic bulge](../../../../../galactic-bulge.md) and bar extend a few thousand [parsecs](../../../../../parsec.md) and contain about $10^{10}M_\odot$, largely older stars. The central [supermassive black hole](../../../../../supermassive-black-hole.md) has mass about $4\times10^6M_\odot$ and is negligible in the total galactic budget. A diffuse old metal-poor [stellar halo](../../../../../stellar-halo.md), extending tens to over $100\,\mathrm{kpc}$, contains [globular clusters](../../../../../globular-cluster.md) and roughly $10^9M_\odot$ in stars. An extended [dark matter halo](../../../../../dark-matter-halo.md) reaches roughly $200\,\mathrm{kpc}$ and dominates the total mass, of order $10^{12}M_\odot$. These are approximate component scales; the gaseous [galactic halo](../../../../../galactic-halo.md) also extends beyond the stellar [galactic disk](../../../../../galactic-disk.md).
