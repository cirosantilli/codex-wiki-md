<h1 id="s2/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Three physically distinct contributions are unseen [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md), hot diffuse [intracluster gas](../../../../../intracluster-medium.md) omitted from the summed stellar [mass](../../../../../mass.md), and faint stars or [stellar remnants](../../../../../stellar-remnant.md) missed by transferring the [Milky Way](../../../../../milky-way.md) [mass-to-light ratio](../../../../../mass-to-light-ratio.md) to the cluster. The first two are actual additional components; the third changes the inferred stellar budget. Departures from [virial equilibrium](../../../../../virial-equilibrium.md), [velocity anisotropy](../../../../../velocity-anisotropy.md) or extra nonthermal [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) can also bias the dynamical or [X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) estimates, although they need not remove the discrepancy.
