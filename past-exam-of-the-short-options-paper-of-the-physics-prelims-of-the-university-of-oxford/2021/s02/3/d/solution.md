<h1 id="s02/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [gravitational lensing](../../../../../gravitational-lensing-split.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md) includes [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md), hot intracluster gas, stars, and any other material in the projected aperture. Most is not stellar: in the literal convention above the stellar fraction is only $2.22\times10^{12}/(5.89\times10^{14})\simeq0.00376$.

Assume the cluster shares the cosmic [cosmic baryon fraction](../../../../../cosmic-baryon-fraction.md), $f_b=\Omega_b/\Omega_m=1/6$, and compare the same aperture for all components. Then

$$
M_b\simeq\frac16M_E=9.82\times10^{13}M_\odot,\qquad
\boxed{M_{\rm gas}\simeq\frac16M_E-M_*\simeq9.60\times10^{13}M_\odot.}
$$

Neglecting stars entirely would give $9.82\times10^{13}M_\odot$. Thus the assumed hot gas dominates the [baryonic matter](../../../../../baryonic-matter.md), and the remainder $M_{\rm DM}\simeq(5/6)M_E=4.91\times10^{14}M_\odot$ is [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md). The budget is consistent: stars are about $2.26\%$ of the inferred [baryonic matter](../../../../../baryonic-matter.md), and all components sum to $M_E$.

For a thermal scale, take gas extent $R_g\simeq2R_E=465\,\mathrm{kpc}$ and mean particle [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $\mu m_p$ with $\mu\simeq0.61$ for ionized gas. A simple spherical [virial theorem](../../../../../virial-theorem.md) estimate with gas binding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $W_g\sim-GM_EM_g/R_g$ and thermal [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $K_g=3M_gk_BT/(2\mu m_p)$ gives

$$
\boxed{T\sim\frac{\mu m_pGM_E}{3k_BR_g}\simeq1.34\times10^8\,\mathrm K,\qquad k_BT\sim12\,\mathrm{keV}.}
$$

This is the scale of a very hot, massive X-ray emitting [galaxy cluster](../../../../../galaxy-cluster.md). The numerical prefactor depends on the radial [mass density](../../../../../density.md) profile, gas distribution and boundary [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md); for example an isothermal-sphere estimate $k_BT\sim\mu m_pGM/(2R_g)$ gives about $2.0\times10^8\,\mathrm K$. Moreover $M_E$ is a projected aperture [mass](../../../../../mass.md) at $R_E$, whereas the estimate needs a spherical enclosed [mass](../../../../../mass.md) at $2R_E$; the given data do not determine that conversion. The quoted [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is therefore an order-of-magnitude estimate, not a uniquely measured value. Nor do the data determine the total gas [mass](../../../../../mass.md) outside the [Einstein ring](../../../../../einstein-ring.md) without a profile. The gas budget above pertains to the matched aperture under the cosmic-fraction assumption.
