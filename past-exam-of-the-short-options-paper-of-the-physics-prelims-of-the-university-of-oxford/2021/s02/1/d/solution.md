**TODO: the plotted location of Procyon B is missing from the damaged source diagram.** The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) would determine the [stellar radius](../../../../../stellar-radius.md) from its [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) and [bolometric luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{R_B}{R_\odot}=\left(\frac{L_B}{L_\odot}\right)^{1/2}\left(\frac{T_\odot}{T_B}\right)^2.}
$$

A visual [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) alone must first be converted with the appropriate [bolometric correction](../../../../../bolometric-correction.md). [Procyon B](../../../../../procyon-b.md) is a [white dwarf](../../../../../white-dwarf.md), so its small [stellar radius](../../../../../stellar-radius.md) and hot surface place it below the [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md), rather than among [giant stars](../../../../../giant-star.md) or [supergiants](../../../../../supergiant.md).
