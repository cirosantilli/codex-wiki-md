**TODO: numerical endpoints require the unreadable source diagram.** Let $\ell_{V,\max}$ and $\ell_{V,\min}$ be the maximum and minimum [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md) visual [luminosities](../../../../../luminosity.md), in units of the [Sun](../../../../../sun.md)'s visual [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md). At a distance $d$, the [inverse-square law](../../../../../inverse-square-law.md) and the [magnitude difference](../../../../../magnitude-difference.md) relation give

$$
m_V-m_{V,\odot}=-2.5\log_{10}\left[\ell_V\left(\frac{1\,\mathrm{AU}}d\right)^2\right].
$$

Thus, using $m_{V,\odot}=-26.77$,

$$
\boxed{d(\mathrm{pc})=\frac{10^{(m_V+26.77)/5}}{206264.806}\sqrt{\ell_V}.}
$$

At the naked-eye detection threshold $m_V=6$, the greatest distances for the intrinsically brightest and faintest [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md) stars would respectively be

$$
\boxed{d_{\max}=17.36\sqrt{\ell_{V,\max}}\,\mathrm{pc},\qquad d_{\min}=17.36\sqrt{\ell_{V,\min}}\,\mathrm{pc}.}
$$

The historical $m_V=1$ endpoint is a brightness reference, not the visibility threshold: if that endpoint were intended instead, the corresponding coefficient would be $1.736$ rather than $17.36$.

The source [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](../../../../../hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) has lost the stellar loci and [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) scale except for a surviving top tick; neither endpoint can be read reliably. If its scale was [bolometric luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) rather than visual [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md), [bolometric corrections](../../../../../bolometric-correction.md) would also be needed, especially for very hot and very cool stars. Equating the two [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) ratios would then be an explicitly approximate assumption. These missing data are not replaced by invented endpoint values. [Interstellar extinction](../../../../../interstellar-extinction.md) is neglected in the displayed distance relation.
