↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [astronomical magnitude](../../../../../astronomical-magnitude.md) convention is $m_1-m_2=-2.5\log_{10}(F_1/F_2)$, with both [fluxes](../../../../../flux.md) measured through the same [photometric passband](../../../../../photometric-passband.md). A five-unit [magnitude difference](../../../../../magnitude-difference.md) therefore corresponds to

$$
\boxed{\frac{F_b}{F_f}=10^{(6-1)/2.5}=100.}
$$

The brighter endpoint has one hundred times the visual [flux](../../../../../flux.md) of the fainter endpoint. Absolute [fluxes](../../../../../flux.md) require a [magnitude zero point](../../../../../magnitude-zero-point.md), which is not given.
