Here $h$ is the [Planck constant](../../../../../planck-constant.md), $\nu$ is the radiation [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), $c$ is the [speed of light](../../../../../speed-of-light.md) in vacuum, $k_B$ is the [Boltzmann constant](../../../../../boltzmann-constant.md), and $T$ is the absolute [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) in [kelvin](../../../../../kelvin.md). The quantity $U_\nu$ is spectral [energy density](../../../../../energy-density.md) per unit [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), so $U_\nu\,d\nu$ is [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) per unit [volume](../../../../../volume.md) in the interval $[\nu,\nu+d\nu]$, and $h\nu/(k_BT)$ is dimensionless.
