<h1 id="s03/1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Convert the skin [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) to $T_s=308.15\,\mathrm K$. To locate the peak per unit [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) in [Planck's law](../../../../../planck-s-law.md), write $x=hc/(\lambda k_BT)$ and maximize $x^5/(e^x-1)$. Its positive stationary point obeys

$$
5(1-e^{-x})=x,\qquad x=4.965114\ldots.
$$

This gives [Wien's displacement law](../../../../../wien-s-displacement-law.md), $\lambda_{\max}T=2.89777\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m\,K}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\lambda_{\max}=9.40\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm m\simeq9.4\,\mathrm{\mu m},}
$$

in the thermal [infrared](../../../../../infrared.md). A peak per unit [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) would occur at a different spectral position; this answer uses the conventional wavelength peak.
