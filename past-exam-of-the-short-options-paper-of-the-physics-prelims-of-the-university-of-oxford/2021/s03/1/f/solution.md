<h1 id="s03/1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

If the [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md), exposed [area](../../../../../surface-area.md), and surroundings remain fixed, the net radiated [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) over one day is

$$
\boxed{E=P_{\rm net}(86400\,\mathrm s)\simeq1.60\times10^7\,\mathrm J.}
$$

Using the supplied conversion gives

$$
\boxed{E\simeq3.82\times10^6\,\mathrm{cal}=3.82\times10^3\,\mathrm{kcal}.}
$$

A dietary Calorie is one [kilocalorie](../../../../../kilocalorie.md), so the result is about 3820 dietary Calories, rather than 3.82 million dietary Calories.
