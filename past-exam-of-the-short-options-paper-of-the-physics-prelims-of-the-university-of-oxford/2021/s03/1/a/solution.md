<h1 id="s03/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [blackbody](../../../../../blackbody.md) absorbs all incident [electromagnetic radiation](../../../../../electromagnetic-radiation-split.md), at every [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), direction and polarization: its absorptivity is one. In [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md), [Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation](../../../../../kirchhoff-s-law-of-thermal-radiation.md) makes its [emissivity](../../../../../emissivity.md) one as well. Its emitted [spectrum](../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) depends only on its absolute [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), through [Planck's law](../../../../../planck-s-law.md), rather than on the material of its surface.

A small aperture in a large isothermal cavity approximates a [blackbody](../../../../../blackbody.md) because radiation entering it undergoes many reflections and is very unlikely to escape before absorption. The equilibrium [radiation field](../../../../../radiation-field.md) is isotropic and unpolarized. The total emitted [radiant exitance](../../../../../radiant-exitance.md) is $\sigma T^4$ by the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md); the peak of the wavelength [spectrum](../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) shifts to shorter [wavelengths](../../../../../wavelength.md) as [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) increases according to [Wien's displacement law](../../../../../wien-s-displacement-law.md). An ideal absorber can therefore glow brightly when hot: “black” describes absorption, not the absence of thermal emission.
