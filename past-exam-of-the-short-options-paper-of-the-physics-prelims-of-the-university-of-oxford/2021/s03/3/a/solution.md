<h1 id="s03/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [particle on a circle](../../../../../particle-in-a-ring.md), the tangential speed is $v=r\dot\phi$, the [moment of inertia](../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) is $I=mr^2$, and the [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $L=I\dot\phi=mrv$. Hence its [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is

$$
\boxed{K=\frac12mv^2=\frac12I\dot\phi^2=\frac{L^2}{2I}=\frac{L^2}{2mr^2}.}
$$

There is no [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) variation along the circle in the stated model.
