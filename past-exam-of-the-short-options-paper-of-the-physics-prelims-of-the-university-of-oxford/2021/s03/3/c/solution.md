<h1 id="s03/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Applying the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) to $\psi_n$ gives $\psi_n''=-n^2\psi_n$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{E_n=\frac{\hbar^2n^2}{2I}=\frac{\hbar^2n^2}{2mr^2},\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) $n=0$ has zero [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). Every positive [energy level](../../../../../energy-level.md) is of [quantum degeneracy](../../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md) two, because $n$ and $-n$ correspond to opposite [angular momenta](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) with equal squared magnitude.

The confinement scale suggests a tangential [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) scale $\hbar/r$ and thus an excitation [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) scale $\hbar^2/(mr^2)$, consistent with the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md). More precisely the [de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../de-broglie-wavelength.md) must fit an integer number of times around the circumference: $2\pi r=|n|\lambda$, so $|p|=h/\lambda=|n|\hbar/r$. This explains both the $r^{-2}$ dependence and the $n^2$ sequence.

One must not infer a nonzero [ground-state energy](../../../../../ground-state-energy.md) by applying $\Delta\phi\Delta L\ge\hbar/2$ naively. Angle is periodic and is not a globally canonical position operator on the circle. The uniform $n=0$ [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md), with $\Delta L=0$, is a concrete counterexample to that naive inequality. The ordinary Cartesian [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) is not violated by the zero-[angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) state.
