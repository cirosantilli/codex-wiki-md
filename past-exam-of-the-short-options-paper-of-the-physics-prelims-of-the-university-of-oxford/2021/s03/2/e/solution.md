<h1 id="s03/2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Work in the initial rest frame of the free [electron](../../../../../electron.md). If it absorbed a [photon](../../../../../photon.md) of positive [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E_\gamma$, [conservation of momentum](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) would give the final electron [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) magnitude $p_f=E_\gamma/c$, while [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) would give $E_f=m_ec^2+E_\gamma$. But the final [electron](../../../../../electron.md) must obey the relativistic [energy–momentum relation](../../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md),

$$
E_f^2=m_e^2c^4+p_f^2c^2=m_e^2c^4+E_\gamma^2.
$$

Combining these statements gives

$$
(m_ec^2+E_\gamma)^2=m_e^2c^4+E_\gamma^2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2m_ec^2E_\gamma=0,
$$

which contradicts $E_\gamma>0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{An isolated free electron cannot absorb a single real photon.}}
$$

The initial rest frame always exists for an [electron](../../../../../electron.md), so this excludes the process in every inertial frame. [Compton scattering](../../../../../compton-scattering.md) is allowed because an outgoing [photon](../../../../../photon.md) carries additional [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md). Absorption by an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) bound in matter is also possible because the rest of the system can share recoil.
