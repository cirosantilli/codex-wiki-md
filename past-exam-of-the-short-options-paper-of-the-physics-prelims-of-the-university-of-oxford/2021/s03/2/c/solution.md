<h1 id="s03/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The incident [photon](../../../../../photon.md) rate is $\dot N_\gamma=P/(hc/\lambda)\simeq2.30\times10^{15}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. The stated [quantum efficiency](../../../../../quantum-efficiency.md) is $\eta=0.005$, so the emitted [electron](../../../../../electron.md) rate is $\eta\dot N_\gamma\simeq1.15\times10^{13}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. If all emitted [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) are collected, the [photocurrent](../../../../../photocurrent.md) magnitude is

$$
\boxed{I=e\eta\frac{P\lambda}{hc}\simeq1.84\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm A=1.84\,\mathrm{\mu A}.}
$$

The light [power](../../../../../power.md) controls the number of events; the [work function](../../../../../work-function.md) already determined the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) per emitted [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and should not be subtracted from the [photon energy](../../../../../photon-energy.md) when counting incident [photons](../../../../../photon.md). Incomplete collection would reduce the measured [photocurrent](../../../../../photocurrent.md) below this value.
