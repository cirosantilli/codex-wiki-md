<h1 id="s01/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $f$ be [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on a neighbourhood of a positively oriented, piecewise smooth [simple closed curve](../../../../../simple-closed-curve.md) and its interior except at finitely many [isolated singularities](../../../../../isolated-singularity-split.md) $a_j$ strictly inside. The [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\oint_\Gamma f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_j\operatorname{Res}(f,a_j).}
$$

The [residue](../../../../../residue.md) is the coefficient of $(z-a_j)^{-1}$ in the local [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md). No [singularity](../../../../../complex-singularity.md) may lie on the [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md). For more general closed [contours](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md), each [residue](../../../../../residue.md) is multiplied by the corresponding [winding number](../../../../../winding-number.md).
