<h1 id="s01/1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Parametrize the positively oriented [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) by $z=e^{i\theta}$. Then $dz=iz\,d\theta$ and

$$
\frac{z^4-10z^2+1}{z^2}=2\cos(2\theta)-10=-4(2+\sin^2\theta).
$$

Consequently the [contour integral](../../../../../contour-integral.md) already evaluated satisfies

$$
I=-\frac i4\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2+\sin^2\theta},\qquad
\boxed{\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{2+\sin^2\theta}=4iI=\frac{2\pi}{\sqrt6}.}
$$
