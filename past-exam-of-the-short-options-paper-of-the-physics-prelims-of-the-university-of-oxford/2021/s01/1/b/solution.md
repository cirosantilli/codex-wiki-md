<h1 id="s01/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md) for $(2+z)^{-1}$ converges for $|z|<2$, while the cancelled [sine](../../../../../sine.md) terms have an everywhere convergent [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md). Hence the requested [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) has no negative powers:

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{(-1)^n z^n}{2^{n+1}}+\sum_{k=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^k z^{2k-1}}{(2k+1)!},\qquad 0<|z|<2.}
$$

For example,

$$
f(z)=\frac12-\frac{5z}{12}+\frac{z^2}{8}-\frac{13z^3}{240}+\frac{z^4}{32}+O(z^5).
$$

The extension through zero also makes this a [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md). The [pole](../../../../../pole.md) at $-2$ fixes its [radius of convergence](../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md) at two.
