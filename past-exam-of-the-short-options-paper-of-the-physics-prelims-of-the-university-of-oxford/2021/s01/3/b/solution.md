<h1 id="s01/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Excise a small disc about zero and a thin slit along the negative real axis, using the orientations shown in the source: $C_1$ upwards, $C_3$ towards zero above the slit, $C_4$ clockwise around zero, and $C_5$ away from zero below the slit. On the resulting region $F$ is a single-valued [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md); zero lies in the excluded disc and there are no [poles](../../../../../pole.md) in the region. [Cauchy's integral theorem](../../../../../cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int_{C_1}F\,dz+\int_{C_2}F\,dz+\int_{C_3}F\,dz+\int_{C_4}F\,dz+\int_{C_5}F\,dz+\int_{C_6}F\,dz=0.}
$$

Here the paths on the cut mean limiting paths from its two sides. Apply [Cauchy's integral theorem](../../../../../cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) before taking those limits, when the entire [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) lies inside the slit domain.
