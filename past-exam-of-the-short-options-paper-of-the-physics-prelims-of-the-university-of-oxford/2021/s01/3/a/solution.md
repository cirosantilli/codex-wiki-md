<h1 id="s01/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the principal [complex square root](../../../../../complex-square-root.md), $\sqrt z=\sqrt r\,e^{i\theta/2}$ with $-\pi<\theta<\pi$. For $F(z)=e^{z-\sqrt z}/z$, the local expansion on this [holomorphic branch](../../../../../holomorphic-branch.md) is

$$
F(z)=\frac1z-\frac1{\sqrt z}+\frac32+O(\sqrt z).
$$

The fractional power prevents a single-valued [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) in a punctured disc. In particular, zero is a [branch point](../../../../../branch-point.md), not an [isolated singularity](../../../../../isolated-singularity-split.md) classified as a [pole](../../../../../pole.md). Merely observing that $zF(z)\to1$ on the slit plane would not establish a [simple pole](../../../../../simple-pole.md): $zF(z)$ does not extend as a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) through a whole disc around zero.

The negative real half-line is a chosen [branch cut](../../../../../branch-cut.md). Its upper and lower boundary values differ because $\sqrt{-x+i0}=i\sqrt x$ and $\sqrt{-x-i0}=-i\sqrt x$ for $x>0$. Continuation once around zero changes the sign of the [complex square root](../../../../../complex-square-root.md), and hence changes $F$ in general. Other [branch cuts](../../../../../branch-cut.md) are possible; this one defines the principal [holomorphic branch](../../../../../holomorphic-branch.md).
