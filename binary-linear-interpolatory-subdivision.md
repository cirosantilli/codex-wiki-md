# Binary linear interpolatory subdivision

↑ **Parent:** [Subdivision curve](subdivision-curve.md)

The centered [subdivision mask](subdivision-mask.md) $[1,2,1]/2$ retains old [control points](control-point.md) and inserts edge midpoints. Its [control polygon](control-polygon.md) is unchanged as a geometric curve by refinement. Its basic limit is the hat $\phi(t)=\max(1-|t|,0)$, with support $[-1,1]$. This [linear spline](linear-spline.md) representation reproduces constants and linear functions, and is $C^0$ but generally not $C^1$ at its [spline knots](spline-knot.md).
