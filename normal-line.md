# Normal line

↑ **Parent:** [Normal vector](normal-vector.md)

At a [point](point-geometry.md) $\mathbf p$ of a smooth [surface](topological-surface-split.md) with nonzero [normal vector](normal-vector.md) $\mathbf n$, its normal line is $\{\mathbf p+t\mathbf n:t\in\mathbb R\}$. For a [level set](level-set.md) $F=c$ with $\nabla F(\mathbf p)\ne0$, one can use $\mathbf n=\nabla F(\mathbf p)$.
