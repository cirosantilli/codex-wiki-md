# Icosian game

↑ **Parent:** [Hamilton cycle](hamilton-cycle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icosian_game)

The [Icosian game](icosian-game.md) made the [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md)-visit puzzle tangible. The report of its 1857 presentation describes a plane projection of a regular [dodecahedron](dodecahedron.md), with holes at the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) for ivory pins. The Trinity College Dublin archive connects this game with the search for closed routes visiting each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) exactly once. This puzzle supplies a historical illustration of the [Hamilton cycle](hamilton-cycle.md) requirement.[The Icosian Calculus, Hamilton papers archive: opening historical introduction and quoted report of the 1857 British Association presentation: dodecahedron projection and ivory pins](the-icosian-calculus-hamilton-papers-archive.md)
