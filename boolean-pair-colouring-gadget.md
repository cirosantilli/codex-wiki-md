# Boolean-pair colouring gadget

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring.md)

Two [graph triangles](triangle-in-a-graph.md) sharing a vertex $B$ force their two opposite edges to use the same two colours in a [graph colouring](graph-coloring.md) with three colours. A palette [graph triangle](triangle-in-a-graph.md) $T,F,B$ and another [graph triangle](triangle-in-a-graph.md) $u,\bar u,B$ therefore force the literal pair to have opposite Boolean colours. This gadget is used in a reduction from [3-SAT](3-sat.md) to the [three-colourability problem](three-colourability-problem.md).
