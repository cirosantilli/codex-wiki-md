<h1 id="erdos-ko-rado-theorem-from-shadows">Erdős-Ko-Rado theorem from shadows</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Erdős-Ko-Rado theorem](erdos-ko-rado-theorem.md)

For an [intersecting family](intersecting-family.md) of $r$-sets with $n\geq2r$, the rank-$r$ [lower shadow](lower-shadow.md) of its complement family is disjoint from the original family. Iterating the [Kruskal-Katona theorem](kruskal-katona-theorem.md) shows that exceeding $\binom{n-1}{r-1}$ original members would force this shadow to have more than $\binom{n-1}r$ members, contradicting the total number $\binom nr$ of $r$-sets.
