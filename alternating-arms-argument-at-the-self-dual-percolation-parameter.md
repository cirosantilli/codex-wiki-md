# Alternating arms argument at the self-dual percolation parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Harris-Kesten theorem](harris-kesten-theorem.md)

At parameter $1/2$, primal and [dual bond percolation](dual-bond-percolation.md) on the [square lattice](square-lattice.md) have the same [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md). Suppose an [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md) exists. Large boxes meet it with [probability](probability.md) tending to one. The [square-root trick for positively associated events](square-root-trick-for-positively-associated-events.md) and quarter-turn symmetry imply that each side has an infinite exterior open arm with [probability](probability.md) tending to one. The same holds for dual arms. A [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) then gives positive [probability](probability.md) of primal top and bottom arms together with dual left and right arms.

Use a primal box $[-r,r]^2$ and a dual box with sides at $\pm(r+1/2)$. Open only primal [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) whose endpoints both lie in the primal box, preserving all outward and exterior arm [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). The two primal arms are joined inside. Planarity prevents the two alternating dual arms from joining: a proposed dual connection together with the inner boundary arc separates one primal infinite arm from infinity. Thus the modified configuration has two infinite dual clusters. Its positive [probability](probability.md) by [finite modification of Bernoulli percolation](finite-modification-of-bernoulli-percolation.md) contradicts [uniqueness of the infinite percolation cluster](uniqueness-of-the-infinite-percolation-cluster.md). Consequently the [percolation probability](percolation-probability.md) at $1/2$ is zero. This is often called Zhang's argument.
