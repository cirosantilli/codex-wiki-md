# Sloping-boundary Eady edge wave

↑ **Parent:** [Eady edge wave](eady-edge-wave.md)

For a semi-infinite stratified fluid with [thermal wind](thermal-wind.md) $U=\Lambda z$ above a gently sloping boundary $z=\alpha y$, a zero-interior-[potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) perturbation decays as $e^{-\mu z}$, where $\mu=N|k|/|f_0|$. The boundary [buoyancy perturbation](buoyancy-perturbation.md) obeys a single oscillatory equation with [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) $c=(\Lambda-\alpha N^2/f_0)/\mu$. The topographic and thermal-wind contributions cancel when the boundary follows a background [isopycnal](isopycnal.md).
