# Papperitz symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann's differential equation](riemann-s-differential-equation.md)

A Papperitz or P-symbol lists the three regular singular points of a second-order equation and the two characteristic exponents at each.

This symbol records the local exponents of [Riemann's differential equation](riemann-s-differential-equation.md).
