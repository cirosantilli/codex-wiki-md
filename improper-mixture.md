# Improper mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix-split.md)

The reduced [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) obtained by taking the [partial trace](partial-trace.md) of a larger [entangled state](entangled-state.md). It predicts the local measurement statistics, but its mixed form does not imply that the total state has collapsed into one of the displayed components. Different ensembles can represent the same [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md).
