# Operator monotonicity of logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithm](logarithm.md)

For positive-definite [Hermitian operators](hermitian-operator.md),

$$
0<A\leq B\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\log A\leq\log B.
$$

The same statement on supports follows by regularizing with a positive multiple of the identity and taking a [limit](limit-of-a-function.md).
