# Smoothness

↑ **Parent:** [Differentiable function](differentiable-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smoothness)

Smoothness describes the degree of [differentiability](differentiability.md) of a [function](function-split.md). The class $C^k$ has continuous [derivatives](derivative.md) through order $k$; a [smooth function](smooth-function.md) is of class $C^\infty$.
