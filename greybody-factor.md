# Greybody factor

↑ **Parent:** [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md)

For a [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md), a [greybody factor](greybody-factor.md) is a mode's transmission probability through the exterior scattering potential. The spectrum of [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md) at [future null infinity](future-null-infinity.md) is the thermal horizon occupation multiplied by this factor, with a sum over angular modes.
