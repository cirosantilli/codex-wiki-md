# Weak subsolution of a divergence-form elliptic equation

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence-form elliptic operator](divergence-form-elliptic-operator.md)

For the sign convention $Lu=D_i(a^{ij}D_j u)$, a [weak subsolution](weak-subsolution-of-a-divergence-form-elliptic-equation.md) satisfies $Lu\geq0$ as a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md), equivalently $\int a^{ij}D_j uD_i\phi\leq0$ for every nonnegative compactly supported [test function](test-function.md) $\phi$. A [weak supersolution](weak-supersolution-of-a-divergence-form-elliptic-equation.md) has the reversed inequality.
