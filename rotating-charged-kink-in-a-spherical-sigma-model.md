# Rotating charged kink in a spherical sigma model

↑ **Parent:** [Mass-deformed spherical sigma model](mass-deformed-spherical-sigma-model.md)

For $\alpha=\omega t+\alpha_0$ and $|\omega|<m$, the [kink](scalar-field-kink.md) is $\phi=2\arctan e^{\kappa(x-x_0)}$ with $\kappa=\sqrt{m^2-\omega^2}$. Its rest [energy](energy.md) and mechanical internal charge are $M=rm^2/\kappa$ and $Q_0=r\omega/\kappa$. Eliminating the frequency gives the displayed [mass](mass.md) relation. The orientation rotates while the [energy](energy.md) profile remains stationary.
