# Torque-free rotation of a spherical squirmer

↑ **Parent:** [Squirmer](squirmer.md)

For a sphere of radius $a$ with prescribed surface slip $u'$ and zero external [torque](torque.md), the [Lorentz reciprocal theorem for Stokes flow](lorentz-reciprocal-theorem-for-stokes-flow.md) gives

$$
\boldsymbol\Omega=-\frac{3}{8\pi a^3}\int_S n\times u'\,dS,
$$

where $n$ points from the sphere into the fluid. Use an auxiliary [rotating sphere in Stokes flow](rotating-sphere-in-stokes-flow.md) with arbitrary angular velocity $\widehat\Omega$ and traction $-3\mu\widehat\Omega\times n$. The actual torque-free cross-work vanishes. Substituting the boundary velocity $\Omega\times an+u'$ into the other cross-work leaves $-8\pi\mu a^3\widehat\Omega\cdot\Omega-3\mu\widehat\Omega\cdot\int n\times u'=0$. A possible translation contributes zero because the auxiliary net force is zero.
