# Potential theory

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potential_theory)

[Potential theory](potential-theory.md) studies [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md), [subharmonic functions](subharmonic-function.md), and the [Laplace operator](laplace-operator.md), including [harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md), [regular boundary points](regular-boundary-point.md), and the [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md). The [Brownian representation of the Dirichlet problem](brownian-representation-of-the-dirichlet-problem.md) connects these analytic objects with exit [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md).
