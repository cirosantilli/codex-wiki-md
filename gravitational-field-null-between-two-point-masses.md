# Gravitational field null between two point masses

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

Two positive [point masses](point-mass.md) $M_1,M_2$ separated by $D$ have a unique intervening [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) zero. At distance $x$ from $M_1$, [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md) gives $M_1/x^2=M_2/(D-x)^2$, hence the displayed value. The signed offset towards $M_2$ from their [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) is $x-DM_2/(M_1+M_2)$. This instantaneous cancellation is distinct from a [Lagrange point](lagrange-point.md), which includes the [centrifugal force](centrifugal-force.md) of a co-rotating description. Along the joining line the zero is unstable: the derivative of the signed [acceleration](acceleration.md) is $2GM_1/x^3+2GM_2/(D-x)^3>0$.
