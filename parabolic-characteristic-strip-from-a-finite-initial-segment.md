# Parabolic characteristic strip from a finite initial segment

↑ **Parent:** [Method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md)

The equations of the [method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md) give $(x,y,u)=(s+t^2/2,t,se^t)$. Their parameter map to the plane has [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) one, so it is globally invertible and $u=(x-y^2/2)e^y$ is uniquely determined exactly where $1\leq x-y^2/2\leq2$. Both positive and negative $y$ occur. Outside this closed parabolic strip, $u=e^yF(x-y^2/2)$ allows arbitrary smooth changes of $F$ outside the initial parameter interval. The boundary [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) carry the data from its endpoints.
