# Flux convergence in turbulent buoyancy mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy-gradient mixing-length closure](buoyancy-gradient-mixing-length-closure.md)

In the area normalization $d^2$, the [buoyancy-gradient mixing-length closure](buoyancy-gradient-mixing-length-closure.md) transports dense-fluid buoyancy downward with magnitude $\mathcal F=d^2KG_z=d^4(G_z)^{3/2}$. Its convergence in an upward coordinate is $\mathcal F_z$. Transport has units $L^4T^{-3}$ and convergence units $L^3T^{-3}$. Constant positive $G_z$ has nonzero transport and zero convergence; the two quantities cannot be identified.
