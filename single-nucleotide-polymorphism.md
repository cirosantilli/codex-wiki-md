# Single-nucleotide polymorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic variant](genetic-variant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-nucleotide_polymorphism)

A single-nucleotide polymorphism is variation at one nucleotide position in sampled genomes. Under an [infinite sites mutation model](infinite-sites-mutation-model.md), the number of such variable positions in a sample equals the number of mutations on the genealogy below its most recent common ancestor.
