# Neuronal refractory period

↑ **Parent:** [Action potential](action-potential.md)

After an [action potential](action-potential.md), channel inactivation and recovery temporarily suppress subsequent firing. An absolute refractory interval forbids another spike; a relative interval requires stronger excitation. A memoryless Poisson spike model omits this dependence on recent spikes.
