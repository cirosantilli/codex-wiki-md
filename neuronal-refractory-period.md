# Neuronal refractory period

↑ **Parent:** [Action potential](action-potential.md)

A [neuronal refractory period](neuronal-refractory-period.md) is the change in excitability following an [action potential](action-potential.md): recent firing changes whether, and how readily, a [neuron](neuron.md) can fire again. The [absolute neuronal refractory period](absolute-neuronal-refractory-period.md) prevents a second regenerative [action potential](action-potential.md); during the [relative neuronal refractory period](relative-neuronal-refractory-period.md), some stimuli that work after full recovery still fail. These are useful distinctions, but their durations are not universal constants. The preparation, [temperature](temperature-split.md), starting [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md), stimulation protocol and criterion for detecting an [action potential](action-potential.md) must accompany a measured interval.

The mechanism continues the opposing [sodium](sodium.md) and [potassium](potassium.md) [electric currents](electric-current.md) described under [action potential](action-potential.md) and the [Hodgkin-Huxley model](hodgkin-huxley-model.md). After a spike, [ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md) leaves fewer [sodium](sodium.md)-selective [voltage-gated ion channels](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md) available for regenerative [depolarization](depolarization.md), while persisting [potassium](potassium.md) [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) opposes that [depolarization](depolarization.md). In the [Hodgkin-Huxley model](hodgkin-huxley-model.md), recovery of $h$ restores [sodium](sodium.md) availability and relaxation of $n$ reduces [potassium](potassium.md) [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md). Closed but available channels differ from inactivated channels: opening and recovery from [ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md) are different processes.[§2.2.1, Activation and De-inactivation; §2.2.3, Figure 2.6 and Ion channel dynamics during spike generation](neuronal-dynamics-from-single-neurons-to-networks-and-models-of-cognition-by-wulfram-gerstner-and-others/2-2-hodgkin-huxley-model.md)

This short memory matters for [spike trains](spike-train.md): a stimulus-only [Poisson process](poisson-process-split.md) can predict very short [interspike intervals](interspike-interval.md) that a refractory [neuron](neuron.md) cannot produce. [Neuronal paired-pulse recovery](neuronal-paired-pulse-recovery.md) makes that memory visible. The [phenomenological paired-pulse recovery](phenomenological-paired-pulse-recovery.md) example below separates a chosen recovery rule, pulse strength and event readout; [refractory limits on neuronal firing](refractory-limits-on-neuronal-firing.md) then shows why an absolute bound is not a prediction of sustained firing rate.
