# Exact triangle in a derived category

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded below derived category of sheaves](bounded-below-derived-category-of-sheaves.md)

An exact, or distinguished, triangle is one isomorphic to $E\xrightarrow{f}F\to\operatorname{Cone}(f)\to E[1]$. For a degreewise short exact sequence $0\to E\to F\to G\to0$, the map $\operatorname{Cone}(f)\to G$ taking $(v,e)$ to the image of $v$ is a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md): its kernel is the contractible cone of the identity on $E$. Consequently the short exact sequence defines such a triangle.
