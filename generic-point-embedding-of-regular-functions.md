# Generic-point embedding of regular functions

↑ **Parent:** [Integral scheme](integral-scheme.md)

On an [integral scheme](integral-scheme.md) $X$, every nonempty open $U$ contains its [generic point](generic-point.md) $\eta$. Taking a [germ](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) gives an injective [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) from its [regular functions](regular-function.md) to the [function field](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) $\mathcal O_{X,\eta}$. Injectivity follows on each nonempty [affine open subscheme](affine-open-subscheme.md) from the injection of an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) into its [field of fractions](field-of-fractions.md), and then from the [sheaf gluing axiom](sheaf-gluing-axiom.md). This lets regular functions on different open subsets be compared inside one field.
