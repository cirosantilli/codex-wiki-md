# Extremal Euclidean throat

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal black hole](extremal-black-hole.md)

At a degenerate static horizon the Euclidean proper radial distance diverges logarithmically, so the horizon is an infinite end rather than a finite polar origin. Its time circle is not contracted at a conical tip, and local cone regularity imposes no finite period. The vanishing limiting [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) determines zero Hawking temperature; the simple-horizon periodicity argument must not be applied unchanged at the degenerate root.
