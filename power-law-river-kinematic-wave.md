# Power-law river kinematic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic wave](kinematic-wave.md)

With constant lateral supply $M$, a discharge law of the displayed form gives [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) [area](surface-area.md) $[(m+1)(Q_{\rm in}+Ms)/c]^{1/(m+1)}$ and [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) [speed](speed.md) $cA^m$. Under a [Chezy law](chezy-friction-law.md), a wetted-perimeter relation $P_b\propto A^q$ gives $m=(1-q)/2$. Small [area](surface-area.md) perturbations are transported downstream with [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) inversely proportional to this [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) [speed](speed.md); finite compressive disturbances can still form shocks because the [flux](flux.md) is [convex](convex-function.md).
