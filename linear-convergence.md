# Linear convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Order of convergence](order-of-convergence.md)

An [iteration of a map](iterated-function.md) has linear convergence to its [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) when its nonzero errors $e_k=x_k-\alpha$ satisfy $|e_{k+1}|/|e_k|\to\rho$ for some $0<\rho<1$. The error eventually shrinks by approximately the same factor at each step. This is exact [order of convergence](order-of-convergence.md) one, in contrast to [quadratic convergence](quadratic-convergence.md).
