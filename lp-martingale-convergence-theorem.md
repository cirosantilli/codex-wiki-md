# Lp martingale convergence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md)

A [martingale](martingale-split.md) satisfying $\sup_n\mathbb E|M_n|^p<\infty$, for $1<p<\infty$, has [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md) and [convergence in Lp](convergence-in-lp.md) to a limit $M_\infty\in L^p$. Also $M_n=\mathbb E[M_\infty\mid\mathcal F_n]$. The [Doob Lp maximal inequality](doob-lp-maximal-inequality.md) makes $\sup_n|M_n|$ integrable to the power $p$; the [martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md) gives the almost-sure limit, and the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) gives convergence in the [Lp norm](lp-norm.md). Boundedness in $L^1$ alone does not imply [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md).
