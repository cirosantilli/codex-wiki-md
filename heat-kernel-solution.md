# Heat-kernel solution

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

For initial data $u_0$ on the real line, Fourier transformation or convolution with the fundamental solution gives

$$
u(x,t)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{e^{-(x-\xi)^2/(4Dt)}}{\sqrt{4\pi Dt}}
u_0(\xi)\,d\xi.
$$

For a [bounded function](bounded-function.md) $u_0$, the [convolution](convolution.md) is well defined at every positive time because the [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) has integral one. The [error-function diffusion front](error-function-diffusion-front.md) uses a [Heaviside step function](heaviside-step-function.md) [initial condition](initial-condition.md) and gives one smoothed interface. The [heat equation with interval-indicator initial data](heat-equation-with-interval-indicator-initial-data.md) instead has two interfaces; subtracting their two [error functions](error-function.md) produces the finite-interval profile.
