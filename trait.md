# Trait

↑ **Parent:** [Object-oriented programming](object-oriented-programming.md)

A reusable type and collection of members that can be composed into a [class in object-oriented programming](class-programming.md). In [Scala](scala.md), a [trait](trait.md) can declare required members and provide concrete implementations. Several [traits](trait.md) can contribute [mixin](mixin.md) behavior to one class, subject to linearization and member-conflict rules.
