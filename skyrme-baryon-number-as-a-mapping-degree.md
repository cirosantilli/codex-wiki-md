# Skyrme baryon number as a mapping degree

↑ **Parent:** [Topological baryon number in the Skyrme model](topological-baryon-number-in-the-skyrme-model.md)

Under [SU(2) as the three-sphere](su-2-as-the-three-sphere.md), a [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md) field with $U\to I$ at infinity defines $S^3\to S^3$. Choose $T_i=-i\tau_i$ and positive left [volume form](volume-form.md) $\theta^1\wedge\theta^2\wedge\theta^3$. The normalized form is $-(24\pi^2)^{-1}\operatorname{tr}(U^{-1}dU)^3$, so the [topological baryon number in the Skyrme model](topological-baryon-number-in-the-skyrme-model.md) equals $\deg U$. Generator and orientation conventions fix the sign.
