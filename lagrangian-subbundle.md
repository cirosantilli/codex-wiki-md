# Lagrangian subbundle

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic vector bundle](symplectic-vector-bundle.md)

A rank-$n$ [vector subbundle](vector-subbundle.md) of a rank-$2n$ [symplectic vector bundle](symplectic-vector-bundle.md) is Lagrangian when each fibre is a [Lagrangian subspace](lagrangian-subspace.md). If it exists, a [compatible almost complex structure](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md) supplies a complementary [Lagrangian subbundle](lagrangian-subbundle.md) by applying $J$. Existence is a global condition: the area-oriented [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) of the two-sphere has no real line subbundle, by triviality of real [line bundles](line-bundle.md) over the sphere and the [Hairy ball theorem](hairy-ball-theorem.md).
