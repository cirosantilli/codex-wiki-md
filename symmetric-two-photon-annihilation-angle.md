# Symmetric two-photon annihilation angle

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-positron annihilation](electron-positron-annihilation.md)

An electron with [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $\alpha m_ec^2$ annihilating with a stationary positron into two equal-angle [photons](photon.md) has $\tan\phi=\sqrt{2/\alpha}$ for $\alpha>0$. Equal [photon](photon.md) energies follow from the specified symmetric laboratory geometry.
