# Direct-adjoint looping

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal control](optimal-control.md)

[Direct-adjoint looping](direct-adjoint-looping.md) repeatedly integrates a constrained state equation forward, integrates its [costate](costate.md) or [formal adjoint](formal-adjoint.md) backward using terminal data, extracts the control [gradient](gradient.md), and updates the [control input](control-input.md) within its constraints. It gives a practical route to local optimization but does not by itself prove a global minimum.
