# Weight-average molar mass

↑ **Parent:** [Molar mass](molar-mass.md)

The [weight-average molar mass](weight-average-molar-mass.md) weights each population by its contribution to total [mass](mass.md):

$$
M_w=\frac{\sum_i n_iM_i^2}{\sum_i n_iM_i}.
$$

Here $n_i$ counts [molecules](molecule-split.md) with [molar mass](molar-mass.md) $M_i$. [SEC light-scattering detection](sec-light-scattering-detection.md) can infer this average for an eluting slice under its optical and [concentration](concentration.md) assumptions; overlapping species need not have the same individual [molar mass](molar-mass.md).
