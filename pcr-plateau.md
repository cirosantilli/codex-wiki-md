# PCR plateau

↑ **Parent:** [PCR efficiency](pcr-efficiency.md)

A [PCR plateau](pcr-plateau.md) is the late phase in which further cycles add little product. Depletion of substrates and accumulation of reaction by-products can reduce [PCR efficiency](pcr-efficiency.md). Consequently, similar final [amplicon](amplicon.md) amounts need not mean similar initial template amounts.
