# Existential theory of a structure

↑ **Parent:** [First-order theory](first-order-theory.md)

The [existential theory of a structure](existential-theory-of-a-structure.md) is the [set](set-split.md) of [existential sentences](existential-sentence.md) with quantifier-free matrices true in it. Its witnesses persist in extensions because quantifier-free truth is preserved by [structure embeddings](structure-embedding.md). It differs from an existential diagram, which additionally uses names for [Elements](euclid-s-elements.md).
