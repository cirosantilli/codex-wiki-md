# Information matrix of an experimental design

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate experimental design](approximate-experimental-design.md)

The normalized information [matrix](matrix.md) for a homoscedastic [linear regression](linear-regression-split.md) model is the average outer product of its regressor vector. It is [positive-definite](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md) precisely when the supported regressors span the parameter space. The full [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md) under [normal](normal-distribution.md) errors is $NM(\xi)/\sigma^2$.
