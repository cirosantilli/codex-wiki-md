# Apparent horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Trapped surface](trapped-surface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apparent_horizon)

An [apparent horizon](apparent-horizon.md) is the outer boundary of the future-trapped region on a chosen spatial slice, under the usual regularity conditions. Its location depends on the slice; it is not the globally defined [event horizon](event-horizon.md). At a regular boundary the outward [null expansion](null-expansion.md) vanishes while the inward [null expansion](null-expansion.md) is negative. Its area cannot be substituted into every version of the [Penrose inequality](penrose-inequality.md) without the appropriate area and initial-data hypotheses.
