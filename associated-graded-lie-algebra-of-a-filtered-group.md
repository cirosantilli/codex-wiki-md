# Associated graded Lie algebra of a filtered group

↑ **Parent:** [Filtration on a group](filtration-on-a-group.md)

For $G_\lambda=\{g:\omega(g)\geq\lambda\}$ and $G_{\lambda+}=\{g:\omega(g)>\lambda\}$, the associated graded group

$$
\operatorname{gr}G=\bigoplus_\lambda G_\lambda/G_{\lambda+}
$$

is a graded [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) with bracket induced by the [group commutator](group-commutator.md). For a p-valuation, $t\operatorname{gr}_\lambda(g)=\operatorname{gr}_{\lambda+1}(g^p)$ makes it a graded $\mathbb F_p[t]$-Lie algebra.
