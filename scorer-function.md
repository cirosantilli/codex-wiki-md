# Scorer function

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scorer_function)

The Scorer functions are particular solutions of inhomogeneous [Airy equations](airy-equation.md): $\operatorname{Hi}''(x)-x\operatorname{Hi}(x)=1/\pi$ and $\operatorname{Gi}''(x)-x\operatorname{Gi}(x)=-1/\pi$. They occur when an endpoint participates in an Airy-type transition. The [NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions](https://dlmf.nist.gov/9.12) gives their standard normalizations.
