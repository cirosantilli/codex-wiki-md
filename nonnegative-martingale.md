# Nonnegative martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

A nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md) takes nonnegative values [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) at each time. In discrete time its [expectations](expected-value.md) are constant, so it is bounded in $L^1$ and the [martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md) gives a finite integrable almost sure limit. Its [expectations](expected-value.md) need not converge to the [expectation](expected-value.md) of that limit; [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md) is needed for [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md).
