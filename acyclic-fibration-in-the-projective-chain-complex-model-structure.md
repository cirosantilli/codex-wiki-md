# Acyclic fibration in the projective chain-complex model structure

↑ **Parent:** [Projective model structure on nonnegative chain complexes](projective-model-structure-on-nonnegative-chain-complexes.md)

A map that is both a [model fibration](model-fibration.md) and a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md). The homology isomorphism together with degree-one surjectivity forces surjectivity in degree zero. Equivalently it has the [right lifting property](right-lifting-property.md) against the [generating cofibrations for nonnegative chain complexes](generating-cofibrations-for-nonnegative-chain-complexes.md).
