# Occupational diffuse scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal scattering](crystal-scattering.md)

Static fluctuations in which [chemical element](chemical-element.md) occupies a site produce scattering beyond coherent [Bragg diffraction](bragg-diffraction.md) from the mean [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md). For independent binary occupations with [probabilities](probability.md) $p$ and $1-p$, the [variance](variance-split.md) of the site's [atomic scattering factor](atomic-scattering-factor.md) is $p(1-p)|f_A-f_B|^2$. Writing each site amplitude as its mean plus a zero-mean fluctuation shows that the mean squared total amplitude is the coherent contribution plus a term proportional to the number of sites and this [variance](variance-split.md). Consequently a forbidden reflection of the average [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) can still sit on a diffuse background. Correlated occupations add nontrivial [scattering vector](scattering-vector.md) dependence.
