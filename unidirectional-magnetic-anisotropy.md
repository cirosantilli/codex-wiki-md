# Unidirectional magnetic anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic anisotropy](magnetic-anisotropy.md)

A directional magnetic preference that distinguishes a [Magnetization](magnetization.md) direction from its reverse, unlike a purely uniaxial easy-axis energy. A pinned interface can supply such a preference and produce [exchange bias](exchange-bias.md). In a simple model its interfacial energy term is proportional to $-J_{\rm int}\cos\theta$.
