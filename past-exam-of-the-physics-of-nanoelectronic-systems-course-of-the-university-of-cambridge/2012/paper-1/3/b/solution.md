For a [double quantum dot](../../../../../double-quantum-dot.md), write $(n_L,n_R)$ for occupation. Tune a bias window allowing the cycle $(0,1)\to(1,1)\to(0,2)\to(0,1)$. Two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) in the same lowest orbital must form a [singlet state](../../../../../singlet-state.md) by the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). The $(0,2)$ triplet requires an excited orbital, with an [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) cost $\Delta_{ST}$ above the accessible window. Approximately spin-conserving tunnelling transfers $S(1,1)$ into $S(0,2)$, but cannot transfer $T(1,1)$ into that singlet. Once a triplet loads, transport stops until [spin relaxation](../../../../../spin-relaxation.md) or mixing occurs: this is [Pauli spin blockade](../../../../../pauli-spin-blockade.md). The reverse bias can avoid trapping in this sequence, producing rectification. Coulomb charging sets the allowed occupancies, but the extra blockade is a spin-selection restriction, not merely an energetically forbidden charge state.

[magnetically driven spin-blockade lifting](../../../../../magnetically-driven-spin-blockade-lifting.md) uses [electron paramagnetic resonance](../../../../../electron-paramagnetic-resonance.md). A transverse oscillating [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) drives [spin](../../../../../spin.md) transitions at

$$
\boxed{hf=|g^*|\mu_B|B_0|.}
$$

Flipping one [spin](../../../../../spin.md) in $|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle$ gives $|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle=(|T_0\rangle-|S\rangle)/\sqrt2$, which has a singlet component and can resume charge transfer. A perfectly uniform simultaneous rotation of two identical [spins](../../../../../spin.md) preserves their triplet character; effective relative rotation needs unequal local [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md) conditions or another singlet–triplet mixing mechanism. In a real GaAs double dot, inhomogeneous nuclear fields can supply that difference. Hyperfine mixing, spin-orbit effects, [cotunnelling](../../../../../cotunnelling.md) and ordinary relaxation also produce leakage and must be distinguished from driven [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md).

A demonstrated device used surface Ti/Au gates to define two dots in a GaAs/AlGaAs [electron](../../../../../electron.md) gas $90\,\mathrm{nm}$ below the surface. An insulated on-chip gold coplanar stripline supplied the oscillating [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). With the dots biased in [spin](../../../../../spin.md) blockade, RF excitation produced current peaks at the two field polarities satisfying [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md). Peak positions varied linearly with [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), giving $|g^*|=0.35\pm0.01$. RF applied to a side gate instead gave negligible response, testing magnetic rather than electric excitation. Gate pulses and RF bursts further produced coherent spin-rotation oscillations in the charge readout.[author preprint, pp.3–5 and Figs.1–4: device, magnetic-control comparison, resonance slope and pulsed rotations](../../../../../driven-coherent-oscillations-of-a-single-electron-spin-in-a-quantum-dot-by-f-h-l-koppens-and-others.md)

An ideal isolated resonant [spin](../../../../../spin.md) has [Rabi oscillation](../../../../../rabi-cycle.md) [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $P_{\rm flip}(t)=\sin^2(\Omega_Rt/2)$. For a linearly oscillating field of peak [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $B_{\rm ac}$, the rotating-wave convention is $\Omega_R=|g^*|\mu_BB_{\rm ac}/(2\hbar)$. Repeated initialization, isolated manipulation and blockade-sensitive readout can therefore map a damped oscillatory singlet yield into an average current. Nuclear fluctuations, readout rates and [dephasing](../../../../../dephasing-channel.md) change the envelope and contrast; an ideal sine squared is not a reconstruction of experimental data. Compare RF on/off, [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md) tracking with [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), drive-amplitude dependence and detuning controls. Current enhancement alone does not prove coherent rotations; the pulse-duration oscillations provide the further time-domain test.
