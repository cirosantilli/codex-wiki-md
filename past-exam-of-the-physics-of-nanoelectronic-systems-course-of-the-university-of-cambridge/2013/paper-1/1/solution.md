For the [spin separation at a smooth quantum Hall edge](../../../../spin-separation-at-a-smooth-quantum-hall-edge.md), linearize the [confinement](../../../../confinement.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) at a crossing. Compensating a [spin](../../../../spin.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) difference by a potential-energy change gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta y\simeq\frac{E_Z}{|V'|}
=\frac{\hbar\omega_c}{3|V'|}.}
$$

Here $V'$ is interpreted as the specified slope of [electron](../../../../electron.md) [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md). If it denotes electrostatic [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) per [length](../../../../length.md) instead, the denominator is $e|V'|$. The two edges of a neck and the two sides of an [antidot](../../../../quantum-hall-antidot.md) have oppositely directed slopes; the formula is a positive local separation on any one edge. For local filling two, only the $n=0$ [spin](../../../../spin.md) pair is occupied at the neck minimum: $E_{0,+}+U_{\min}<E_F<E_{1,-}+U_{\min}$. Raising both branches with the [confinement](../../../../confinement.md) produces two crossings on each side, as shown in the original neck sketch. This fixed-slope estimate precedes self-consistent [electrostatic screening](../../../../electrostatic-screening.md).

An [antidot](../../../../quantum-hall-antidot.md) is a depleted potential hill. [guiding centres](../../../../guiding-center.md) circulate around its closed equipotentials, with chirality opposite to the outer sample edge. A phase-coherent round trip contributes magnetic phase $2\pi BS/\Phi_0$, with $\Phi_0=h/e$. A localized orbit resonant with the incident [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) can transfer an [electron](../../../../electron.md) between extended channels. If the outgoing channel returns toward the injecting reservoir, the process is resonant reflection and reduces [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md). If coupling instead connects an otherwise blocked incident channel to the far reservoir, it is resonant transmission and adds [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md). Transmission peaks require that forward connectivity; a closed orbit by itself does not guarantee them. Interchannel scattering can change which orbit participates.[section I and section III.C, especially Fig. 7: localized states, interchannel scattering and resonant transmission](../../../../electron-electron-interactions-in-antidot-based-aharonov-bohm-interferometers-by-s-ihnatsenka-and-others.md)

For the [antidot resonance conductance bounds](../../../../antidot-resonance-conductance-bounds.md) with one active spin-resolved resonance, write

$$
\mathcal L(E)=\frac{\Gamma_L\Gamma_R}{(E-\epsilon_d)^2+(\Gamma_L+\Gamma_R)^2/4},\qquad
0\leq\mathcal L\leq1.
$$

Here $\Gamma_L,\Gamma_R$ are energy broadenings; the corresponding escape rates are $\Gamma_L/\hbar,\Gamma_R/\hbar$. In a reflection-dominated configuration with two otherwise transmitted channels, $G=(e^2/h)(2-\mathcal L)$: a $2e^2/h$ background can dip to $e^2/h$. In a transmission-dominated configuration with one spectator channel and the second otherwise blocked, $G=(e^2/h)(1+\mathcal L)$: peaks rise from $e^2/h$ toward $2e^2/h$. These are the two ideal channel-count examples plotted below. More generally, $G=(e^2/h)(N_{\rm open}-R)$ or $(e^2/h)(N_{\rm dir}+T)$; a different spectator count changes the integer offset. The constriction filling alone does not specify every tunnel coupling or resonance height. Equal couplings permit unit resonant transfer in the isolated-level model; asymmetry, [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) and [dephasing](../../../../dephasing-channel.md) reduce the contrast. Holding the effective orbit [area](../../../../surface-area.md) approximately fixed gives $\Delta B\simeq h/(eS)$ for one branch, not a universal charging-corrected [period](../../../../period-of-a-function.md).

In [quantum-dot versus antidot resonance topology](../../../../quantum-dot-versus-antidot-resonance-topology.md), a [quantum dot](../../../../quantum-dot.md) is a potential well holding a finite [electron](../../../../electron.md) island. Its closed guiding-centre circulation is reversed relative to an [antidot](../../../../quantum-hall-antidot.md) at the same field. With two barriers in series, current follows source $\to$ dot $\to$ drain; off resonance there need not be a direct conducting path. A gate shifts a level or an addition electrochemical potential into alignment with the reservoirs and produces a [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md) peak. For an isolated coherent spin-resolved level, the zero-temperature peak is at most $e^2/h$ and reaches it only for equal couplings; unresolved noninteracting [spin degeneracy](../../../../spin-degeneracy.md) doubles this bound. In weak [sequential tunnelling](../../../../sequential-tunnelling.md), peak height depends on rates and [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) rather than being quantized. Charging produces [Coulomb blockade](../../../../coulomb-blockade.md) peaks with spacing set by [addition energy](../../../../addition-energy.md) and gate lever arm. An [antidot](../../../../quantum-hall-antidot.md) also supports discrete orbit/charging resonances, but can be side-coupled to already open channels and produce dips. Gate [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) changes both orbit [area](../../../../surface-area.md) and [confinement](../../../../confinement.md), whereas a magnetic sweep directly changes enclosed [flux](../../../../flux.md).

[Coulomb interactions](../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) require a self-consistent [confinement](../../../../confinement.md) potential. A gradual decrease in [electron](../../../../electron.md) [density](../../../../density.md) cannot leave arbitrary uncompensated donor charge at no cost. Partly occupied [Landau levels](../../../../landau-level.md) have a large charge response: [electrostatic screening](../../../../electrostatic-screening.md) flattens their effective [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) near the [Fermi energy](../../../../fermi-energy.md) over [compressible quantum Hall edge strips](../../../../compressible-quantum-hall-edge-strip.md). Between them, integer-filled [incompressible quantum Hall edge strips](../../../../incompressible-quantum-hall-edge-strip.md) have a gap, weak [density](../../../../density.md) response and most of the potential variation. In an idealized cross-section the effective branch thus alternates between flat, partially occupied portions and rising portions crossing a gap; its [carrier density](../../../../carrier-density.md) varies oppositely. The bare levels still have [Landau level](../../../../landau-level.md) and [spin](../../../../spin.md) splittings locally, but the common electrostatic potential is no longer an imposed straight slope. [exchange interaction](../../../../exchange-interaction.md) can additionally renormalize [spin](../../../../spin.md) gaps. Widths must follow electrostatics and the [density](../../../../density.md) gradient, so the unscreened $E_Z/|V'|$ separation is not a quantitative screened-strip width.[abstract: steplike self-consistent potential and alternating compressible and incompressible strips](../../../../electrostatics-of-edge-channels-by-d-b-chklovskii-and-others.md)
