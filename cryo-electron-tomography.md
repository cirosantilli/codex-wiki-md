# Cryo-electron tomography

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md)

[cryo-electron tomography](cryo-electron-tomography.md) reconstructs three-dimensional specimen information from tilted projections at cryogenic temperature. Radiation exposure and incomplete angular coverage limit resolution and interpretation.
