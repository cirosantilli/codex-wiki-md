# Exponential-sum large sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Large sieve](large-sieve.md)

If $\theta_r$ have [circular spacing](circular-spacing.md) at least $\delta$, then

$$
\sum_r\left|\sum_{M<n\leq M+N}a_ne(n\theta_r)\right|^2\leq(\delta^{-1}+2\pi N)\sum_{M<n\leq M+N}|a_n|^2.
$$

Multiply the [exponential sum](exponential-sum.md) by $e(-Mt)$, apply the [Sobolev–Gallagher inequality](sobolev-gallagher-inequality.md) on disjoint arcs of length $\delta$, and sum. The [finite-interval Parseval identities](finite-interval-parseval-identities.md) and [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) bound the derivative contribution by $2\pi N\sum|a_n|^2$.
