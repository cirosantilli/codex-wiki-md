# Cuprate superconductor

↑ **Parent:** [High-temperature superconductivity](high-temperature-superconductivity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuprate_superconductor)

A [cuprate superconductor](cuprate-superconductor.md) is a [copper](copper.md)-[oxide](oxide.md) material exhibiting [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md), with layered [copper](copper.md)-[oxygen](oxygen.md) units central to its [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md). Chemical composition and [oxygen](oxygen.md) content must accompany a quoted [superconducting transition temperature](superconducting-transition-temperature.md); the nominal recipe of a mixed-phase pellet is not the composition of its superconducting constituent. The [formal hole count in a cuprate](formal-hole-count-in-a-cuprate.md) illustrates the distinction between composition and electronic interpretation.
