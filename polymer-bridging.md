# Polymer bridging

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

[Polymer bridging](polymer-bridging.md) occurs when one [polymer](polymer-split.md) chain attaches to more than one particle, producing an effective attraction or network connection. Surface affinity and incomplete coverage are essential to this mechanism. It should be distinguished from both [steric stabilization](steric-stabilization.md) by densely coated particles and the [depletion force](depletion-force.md) produced by nonadsorbing chains.
