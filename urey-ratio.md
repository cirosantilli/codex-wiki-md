# Urey ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Mantle thermal budget](mantle-thermal-budget.md)

The ratio of [radiogenic heating](radiogenic-heating.md) to heat loss for a stated planetary reservoir, named for [Harold Urey](harold-urey.md). A convective mantle [Urey ratio](urey-ratio.md) uses mantle radioactive production and heat leaving the mantle; a bulk-silicate or surface ratio uses a different reservoir and heat budget. It must be labeled accordingly. A ratio below one is consistent with drawing on stored heat, but core heat or other inputs must also be included before inferring the sign of the mantle's [temperature](temperature-split.md) change.
