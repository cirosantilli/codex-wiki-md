# Hard Lewis acid

↑ **Parent:** [Hard and soft acids and bases](hard-and-soft-acids-and-bases.md)

A [Lewis acid](lewis-acid.md) whose acceptor interaction favours small, weakly polarizable donor atoms. High charge density favours hard character; Eu(III) typically favours [oxygen](oxygen.md) and [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) donors over a softer sulfur donor.
