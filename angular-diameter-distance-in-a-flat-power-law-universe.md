# Angular diameter distance in a flat power-law universe

↑ **Parent:** [Angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md)

For $R=At^p$, $0<p<1$, integrate the radial [photon](photon.md) path from emission to reception and multiply by the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) at emission. This gives the displayed [angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md). The arbitrary normalization $A$ cancels, and the distance is not obtained by multiplying light speed by elapsed [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md).
