# Superoperator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum information theory](quantum-information-theory-split.md)

A [linear map](linear-map.md) between spaces of [linear operators](linear-operator.md). A physical [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) is a superoperator that is trace preserving and [completely positive](completely-positive-map.md); a general superoperator need not have either property.
