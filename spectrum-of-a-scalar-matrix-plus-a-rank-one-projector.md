# Spectrum of a scalar matrix plus a rank-one projector

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md)

Let $v$ be a real [unit vector](unit-vector.md) in $d$-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md). Then $P=vv^{\mathsf T}$ is a [rank-one orthogonal projection](rank-one-orthogonal-projection.md): $Pv=v$, while $Pw=0$ for $w$ [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) to $v$. Consequently the [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $A=\alpha I+\beta P$ has [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\alpha+\beta$ on the line spanned by $v$ and [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\alpha$ on its [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md). Any [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of that complement completes $v$ to an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). Thus

$$
\operatorname{tr}A=d\alpha+\beta,\qquad\det A=\alpha^{d-1}(\alpha+\beta).
$$

The [trace](matrix-trace.md) and [determinant](determinant.md) formulas remain valid when one of these [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) is zero. When $\beta=0$, all nonzero [vectors](vector.md) have the same [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md), and the chosen splitting is optional.
