# Subshift of finite type

↑ **Parent:** [Symbolic dynamics](symbolic-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subshift_of_finite_type)

A subshift of finite type is a [closed subset](closed-set.md) of a finite-alphabet [full shift](full-shift.md) defined by finitely many forbidden finite words, with the restricted [left shift](left-shift.md). After replacing symbols by sufficiently long blocks, its constraints can be described by a zero-one [symbolic transition matrix](transition-matrix-for-a-subshift.md). In this presentation, membership means that every consecutive pair follows an allowed transition. The resulting [sequence](sequence.md) space is a [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md) and the restricted [left shift](left-shift.md) is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md).
