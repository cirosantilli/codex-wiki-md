# IP multicast

↑ **Parent:** [Internet Protocol](internet-protocol.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IP_multicast)

[IP multicast](ip-multicast.md) sends an [Internet Protocol](internet-protocol.md) datagram to a group identified by a multicast [IP address](ip-address.md). Delivery retains the underlying best-effort nature of [Internet Protocol](internet-protocol.md).
