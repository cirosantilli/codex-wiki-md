# Seebeck effect

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoelectric effect](thermoelectric-effect.md)

The [Seebeck effect](seebeck-effect.md) is the generation of a thermoelectric voltage by a temperature difference. Two materials with different thermopowers form a circuit whose electromotive force is the temperature integral of their Seebeck-coefficient difference, with sign set by circuit orientation. In a nondegenerate [semiconductor](semiconductor.md), [Electron](electron.md) and hole transport usually give opposite thermopower signs.
