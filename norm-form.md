# Norm form

↑ **Parent:** [Field norm](field-norm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norm_form)

For a finite [number field](number-field.md) $L/\mathbb Q$ with a rational basis $\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_n$, the norm form is the homogeneous polynomial

$$
F(X_1,\ldots,X_n)
=N_{L/\mathbb Q}(X_1\omega_1+\cdots+X_n\omega_n).
$$
