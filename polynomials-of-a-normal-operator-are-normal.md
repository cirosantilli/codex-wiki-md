# Polynomials of a normal operator are normal

↑ **Parent:** [Normal operator](normal-operator.md)

For a [normal operator](normal-operator.md) $T$ on a real or complex [inner product space](inner-product-space.md) and a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $f(x)=\sum_j a_jx^j$, the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) identity gives

$$
f(T)^*=\sum_j\bar a_j(T^*)^j.
$$

Every power of $T$ commutes with every power of $T^*$, since $TT^*=T^*T$. Expanding finite sums consequently proves that $f(T)$ commutes with $f(T)^*$, so it is a [normal operator](normal-operator.md). The bar on $f$ means coefficientwise [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md), and is essential for complex coefficients.
