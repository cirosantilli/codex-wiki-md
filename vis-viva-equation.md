# Vis-viva equation

↑ **Parent:** [Specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vis-viva_equation)

For a [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) with [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) $a$, the [vis-viva equation](vis-viva-equation.md) is

$$
v^2=\mu\left(\frac2r-\frac1a\right).
$$

For the convention that a [hyperbolic Kepler orbit](hyperbolic-kepler-orbit.md) has positive semi-major-axis magnitude $a$, its final term instead has a plus sign.
