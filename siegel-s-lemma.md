<h1 id="siegel-s-lemma">Siegel's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine approximation](diophantine-approximation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegel's_lemma)

Let $K$ be a [number field](number-field.md) of degree $D$, and let $L_1,\ldots,L_M$ be [linear forms](linear-functional.md) in $N>DM$ variables with coefficients in $K$ and [projective height](projective-height.md) at most $\mathcal H\geq1$. There is a nonzero $\mathbf x\in\mathbb Z^N$ satisfying every $L_j(\mathbf x)=0$ and

$$
\lVert\mathbf x\rVert_\infty
\leq(N\mathcal H)^{DM/(N-DM)}.
$$

For $K=\mathbb Q$, this follows by applying the [pigeonhole principle](pigeonhole-principle.md) to the images of the integer box $\{0,\ldots,Y\}^N$ under $(L_1,\ldots,L_M)$. Expanding coefficients in a rational basis of $K$ gives the factor $D$ in the general count.
