# CP eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md)

In a convention where the [CP](cp-symmetry.md) operation squares to one on the relevant bosonic state space, a [CP eigenstate](cp-eigenstate.md) has [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $+1$ or $-1$. If the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) commutes with [CP](cp-symmetry.md), its nondegenerate eigenstates can also be [CP eigenstates](cp-eigenstate.md). In the neutral-[kaon](kaon.md) convention $CP|K^0\rangle=-|\bar K^0\rangle$, the antisymmetric flavor combination is [CP](cp-symmetry.md) even and the symmetric one [CP](cp-symmetry.md) odd. Rephasing flavor states changes the written combinations, not the invariant content.
