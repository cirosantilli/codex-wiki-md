# Mean molecular weight per ion

↑ **Parent:** [Mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md)

The [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md) per ion counts nuclei rather than nuclei plus free electrons: $\rho/(\mu_i m_u)$ is the ion number density. For species with mass fractions $X_j$ and mass numbers $A_j$, $\mu_i^{-1}=\sum_jX_j/A_j$. It is useful when ions provide a thermal [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) pressure but [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) supplies a separate contribution.
