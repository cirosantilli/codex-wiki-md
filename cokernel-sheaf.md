# Cokernel sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of sheaves](morphism-of-sheaves.md)

For a [morphism of sheaves](morphism-of-sheaves.md) $\phi:\mathcal F\to\mathcal G$ of abelian groups or modules, the cokernel sheaf is the [sheafification](sheafification.md) of the [presheaf](presheaf-of-sets-on-a-topological-space.md) $U\mapsto\mathcal G(U)/\phi(\mathcal F(U))$. Its [stalk](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) at $x$ is $\operatorname{coker}(\mathcal F_x\to\mathcal G_x)$. The presheaf quotient itself need not satisfy the [sheaf gluing axiom](sheaf-gluing-axiom.md), which explains why a surjective [morphism of sheaves](morphism-of-sheaves.md) need not be surjective on [global sections](global-section.md).
