# Nonuniform two-intersecting family bound

↑ **Parent:** [Two-intersecting family](two-intersecting-family.md)

On an even ground set $n=2h$, a [two-intersecting family](two-intersecting-family.md) has size at most $\sum_{j=h+1}^n\binom nj$. On an odd ground set $n=2h+1$, the bound is $\binom{2h}{h+1}+\sum_{j=h+2}^n\binom nj$. Pair lower levels with complements of upper levels using the [intersecting shadow lemma](intersecting-shadow-lemma.md); apply the [Erdős-Ko-Rado theorem](erdos-ko-rado-theorem.md) to the remaining middle level in the odd case.
