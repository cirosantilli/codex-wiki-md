<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using the [probability mass function](../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) of the [Poisson distribution](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) and the power series of the [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md),

$$
M_X(t)=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}e^{tj}e^{-\mu}\frac{\mu^j}{j!}
=e^{-\mu}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\mu e^t)^j}{j!}
=\boxed{\exp\{\mu(e^t-1)\}.}
$$

This [moment-generating function of a Poisson distribution](../../../../../moment-generating-function-of-a-poisson-distribution.md) is finite for every real $t$. It also covers $\mu=0$, when $X=0$ with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) one and the [moment-generating function](../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) is identically one.
