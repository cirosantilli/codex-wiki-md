↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [strong law of large numbers](../../../../strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) says that, for real [independent and identically distributed random variables](../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $Z_1,Z_2,\ldots$ with $\mathbb E|Z_1|<\infty$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P\!\left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n Z_i\longrightarrow\mathbb E Z_1\right)=1.}
$$

The assertion is [almost sure convergence](../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md) of the entire sequence of [sample means](../../../../sample-mean.md). Finite first absolute [moment](../../../../moment.md) suffices; a finite [variance](../../../../variance-split.md) is not required.
