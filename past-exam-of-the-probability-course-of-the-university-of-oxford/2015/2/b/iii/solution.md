<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For fixed $v>0$, the support inequalities become $v/2<u<v$. The [marginal density](../../../../../marginal-density.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{f_V(v)=\int_{v/2}^{v}e^{-u}\,du
=\begin{cases}e^{-v/2}-e^{-v},&v>0,\\0,&v\leq0.\end{cases}}
$$

It is nonnegative and integrates to $2-1=1$. Another interpretation is that $V=X+2Y$ sums [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) [exponential random variables](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) of rates $1$ and $1/2$, so it has a [hypoexponential distribution](../../../../../hypoexponential-distribution.md). The [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is $1+2=3$, which agrees with $\int_0^\infty v(e^{-v/2}-e^{-v})\,dv=4-1=3$.
