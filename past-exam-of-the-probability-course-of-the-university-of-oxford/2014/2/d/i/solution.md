↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Poisson interarrival times](../../../../../poisson-interarrival-times.md) of a homogeneous [Poisson process](../../../../../poisson-process-split.md) are [independent and identically distributed](../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with the [exponential distribution](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) of rate $\lambda$. Consequently $Y_1,Y_2$ are [independent random variables](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) and their [joint probability density](../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) is

$$
\boxed{f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)=\lambda^2e^{-\lambda(y_1+y_2)}\mathbf1_{\{y_1>0,\ y_2>0\}}.}
$$

Here $Y_2$ is the second interarrival duration, rather than the time of the second arrival.
