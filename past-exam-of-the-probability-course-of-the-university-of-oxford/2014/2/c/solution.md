↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [exponential distribution](../../../../exponential-distribution.md) of rate $\lambda$ is a [Gamma distribution](../../../../gamma-distribution.md) with shape one. Repeated [additivity of independent gamma distributions with a common rate](../../../../additivity-of-independent-gamma-distributions-with-a-common-rate.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{E_1+\cdots+E_n\sim\operatorname{Gamma}(n,\lambda).}
$$

With integer shape this is also called an [Erlang distribution](../../../../erlang-distribution.md).
