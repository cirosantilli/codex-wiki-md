<h1 id="3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This is the [synchronous product of simple symmetric random walks](../../../../../synchronous-product-of-simple-symmetric-random-walks.md), rather than a walk that changes only one coordinate at a time. Independence of the three coordinate processes gives

$$
\mathbb P\bigl((X_{2m},Y_{2m},Z_{2m})=(0,0,0)\bigr)
=\left[\binom{2m}{m}2^{-2m}\right]^3
\sim\frac1{(\pi m)^{3/2}}.
$$

Odd-time return probabilities vanish. The [p-series](../../../../../p-series.md) with exponent $3/2$ converges, so the sum of all return probabilities is finite. By the [recurrence criterion by return probabilities](../../../../../recurrence-criterion-by-return-probabilities.md), the origin is a [transient state](../../../../../transient-state.md): **the three-dimensional process is transient**. From the origin its [communicating class](../../../../../communicating-class.md) consists of integer triples whose coordinates have the same parity; translation gives the same classification for every other class.
