<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the symmetric [simple random walk on the integer line](../../../../../simple-random-walk-on-the-integer-line.md), odd return times are impossible. A return after $2m$ steps requires exactly $m$ positive increments, so

$$
p_{00}^{(2m)}=\binom{2m}{m}2^{-2m}\sim\frac1{\sqrt{\pi m}}
$$

by [Stirling's approximation](../../../../../stirling-formula.md). The resulting sum diverges by the [p-series](../../../../../p-series.md) criterion. The [recurrence criterion by return probabilities](../../../../../recurrence-criterion-by-return-probabilities.md) therefore makes zero a [recurrent state](../../../../../recurrent-state.md). The [simple random walk](../../../../../simple-random-walk.md) is an [irreducible Markov chain](../../../../../irreducible-markov-chain.md) on $\mathbb Z$, so **the symmetric chain is recurrent**.
