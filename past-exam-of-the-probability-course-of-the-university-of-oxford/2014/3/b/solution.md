<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Start the [Markov chain](../../../../markov-chain-split.md) at a state $i$ and define the strictly positive [first return time](../../../../first-return-time.md) $T_i^+=\inf\{n\geq1:X_n=i\}$. A [recurrent state](../../../../recurrent-state.md) satisfies $\mathbb P_i(T_i^+<\infty)=1$; a [transient state](../../../../transient-state.md) has this [probability](../../../../probability.md) strictly below one. Equivalently a [recurrent state](../../../../recurrent-state.md) is visited infinitely often [almost surely](../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md) when started there, whereas a [transient state](../../../../transient-state.md) receives finitely many visits [almost surely](../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md). The [recurrence criterion by return probabilities](../../../../recurrence-criterion-by-return-probabilities.md) gives the equivalent test

$$
i\text{ recurrent}\iff\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p_{ii}^{(n)}=\infty.
$$

States in a [communicating class](../../../../communicating-class.md) share this classification. In an [irreducible Markov chain](../../../../irreducible-markov-chain.md) all states communicate, so the chain is called a [recurrent Markov chain](../../../../recurrent-markov-chain.md) if all its states are recurrent and a [transient Markov chain](../../../../transient-markov-chain.md) if all are transient.
