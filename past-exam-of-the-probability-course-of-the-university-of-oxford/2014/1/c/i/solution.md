<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $S_{n+1}=S_n+X_{n+1}$. The new [random variable](../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $X_{n+1}$ is [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the entire history generated by $X_1,\ldots,X_n$, hence of $(S_1,\ldots,S_n)$. This is a [Markov chain obtained from independent innovations](../../../../../markov-chain-obtained-from-independent-innovations.md): conditional on the past, only the current sum enters the next state. Its time-homogeneous [transition probabilities](../../../../../transition-probability.md) are

$$
\boxed{P_{st}=\begin{cases}\dfrac1{(t-s)(t-s+1)},&t>s,\\0,&t\leq s,\end{cases}\qquad s,t\in\{1,2,\ldots\}.}
$$

Every jump is a positive integer, and each row sums to one by the [probability mass function](../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) from part (b).
