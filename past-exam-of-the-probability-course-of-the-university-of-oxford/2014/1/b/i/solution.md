<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Subtract adjacent values of the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md). Its value at zero is zero, so the same calculation applies also to $k=1$:

$$
\boxed{p_k=\left(1-\frac1{k+1}\right)-\left(1-\frac1k\right)=\frac1{k(k+1)},\qquad k\geq1.}
$$

The [probability mass function](../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) sums to one by a [telescoping series](../../../../../telescoping-series.md).
