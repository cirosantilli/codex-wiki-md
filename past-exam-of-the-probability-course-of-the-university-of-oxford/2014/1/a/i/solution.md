[Convergence in distribution](../../../../../convergence-in-distribution.md) means that the [cumulative distribution functions](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) satisfy

$$
\mathbb P(X_n\leq x)\longrightarrow\mathbb P(X\leq x)
$$

at every $x$ where the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) of $X$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md). Values at its discontinuities need not converge. This definition uses only the [probability distributions](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md), so the [random variables](../../../../../random-variable-split.md) need not be defined on the same [probability space](../../../../../probability-space-split.md).
