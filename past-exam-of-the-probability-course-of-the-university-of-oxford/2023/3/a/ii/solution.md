<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The assumed identities are the [detailed balance equations](../../../../../detailed-balance.md). Summing over $i$ and using the row sums of the [transition matrix](../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) gives, for every $j$,

$$
(\pi P)_j=\sum_i\pi_ip_{i,j}=\sum_i\pi_jp_{j,i}=\pi_j\sum_ip_{j,i}=\pi_j.
$$

All summands are nonnegative, so these countable sums are justified. Thus

$$
\boxed{\pi P=\pi,}
$$

and $\pi$ is an [invariant distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md).
