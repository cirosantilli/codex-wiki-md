A [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) on the countable [state space](../../../../../state-space.md) $\mathcal S$ is precisely a row vector satisfying

$$
\boxed{\pi_i\geq0\quad(i\in\mathcal S),\qquad\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}\pi_i=1.}
$$

A [transition matrix](../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) is a [stochastic matrix](../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md), meaning

$$
\boxed{p_{i,j}\geq0\quad(i,j\in\mathcal S),\qquad\sum_{j\in\mathcal S}p_{i,j}=1\quad(i\in\mathcal S).}
$$

Every row therefore gives the conditional [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) of the next state given the present state. These conditions do not require $\pi$ to be an [invariant distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md) of $P$.
