<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fix $0<c<1$ and choose $R_c$, independently of $X$, with the [normal distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{R_c\sim\mathcal N\bigl((1-c)\mu,(1-c^2)\sigma^2\bigr).}
$$

The [linear combination of independent normal random variables](../../../../../linear-combination-of-independent-normal-random-variables.md) $cX+R_c$ is normal. Its [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is $c\mu+(1-c)\mu=\mu$ and its [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is $c^2\sigma^2+(1-c^2)\sigma^2=\sigma^2$. Hence $cX+R_c\overset d=X$ for every $c$, proving that every specified [normal distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) is a [self-decomposable distribution](../../../../../self-decomposable-distribution.md).
