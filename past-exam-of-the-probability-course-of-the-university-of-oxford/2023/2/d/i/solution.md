<h1 id="2/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [independent and identically distributed random variables](../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $X_j\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)$, the [linear combination of independent normal random variables](../../../../../linear-combination-of-independent-normal-random-variables.md) $n^{-1/2}\sum_{j=1}^nX_j$ has [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) zero and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) $n^{-1}n\sigma^2=\sigma^2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{n^{-1/2}(X_1+\cdots+X_n)\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)\quad(n\geq1).}
$$

This is exactly the requested $2$-stability. The paper's definition is a [strictly stable distribution](../../../../../strictly-stable-distribution.md), since it allows no centering term.
