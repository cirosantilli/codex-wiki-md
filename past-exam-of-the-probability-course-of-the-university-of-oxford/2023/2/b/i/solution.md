↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) has [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $1/2$ on $(-1,1)$. Therefore, for $t\ne0$,

$$
M_U(t)=\frac12\int_{-1}^1e^{tu}\,du=\frac{e^t-e^{-t}}{2t},\qquad \boxed{M_U(t)=\begin{cases}\sinh(t)/t,&t\ne0,\\1,&t=0.\end{cases}}
$$

The value at zero follows either directly from the definition of the [moment-generating function](../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) or by continuity. This answer needs no assumption about $B$.
