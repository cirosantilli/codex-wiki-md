<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $0<b\leq1$, then $0<U_j\leq1$ [almost surely](../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md). Intersecting these events over all $j$ still gives an event of [probability](../../../../../probability.md) one. On it, $0\leq W_{n+1}\leq W_n$, so the products have a finite nonnegative limit $W_\infty$.

For every $\varepsilon>0$, the inclusion $\{W_\infty>\varepsilon\}\subseteq\{W_n>\varepsilon\}$ and part (c)(i) imply $\mathbb P(W_\infty>\varepsilon)=0$. Taking $\varepsilon=1/m$ for positive integers $m$ proves $W_\infty=0$ [almost surely](../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{W_n\longrightarrow0\quad\text{almost surely for }0<b\leq1.}
$$

The monotonicity is the extra ingredient that upgrades [convergence in probability](../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md) to [almost sure convergence](../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md) here.
