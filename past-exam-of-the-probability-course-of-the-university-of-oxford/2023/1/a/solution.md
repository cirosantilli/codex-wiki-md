The [strong law of large numbers](../../../../strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) states that, for [independent and identically distributed random variables](../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ with $\mathbb E|X_1|<\infty$,

$$
\boxed{\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^nX_j\longrightarrow\mathbb E X_1\quad\text{almost surely}.}
$$

Thus, outside an event of [probability](../../../../probability.md) zero, the [sample mean](../../../../sample-mean.md) converges to the common [expected value](../../../../expected-value-split.md).
