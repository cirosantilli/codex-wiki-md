<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiating the [moment-generating function](../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) at zero gives

$$
\boxed{M_X'(0)=\mathbb E[X],\qquad M_X''(0)=\mathbb E[X^2].}
$$

The second derivative is the second raw [moment](../../../../../moment.md), so the [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is $M_X''(0)-(M_X'(0))^2$. The assumed exponential integrability justifies these derivatives.
