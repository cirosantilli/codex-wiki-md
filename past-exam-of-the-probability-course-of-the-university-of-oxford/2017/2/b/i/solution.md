<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The given [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) imply $M_X(0)=1$, $M_X'(0)=0$ and $M_X''(0)=1$. The [Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) therefore gives

$$
M_X(t)=1+\frac{t^2}{2}+o(t^2).
$$

Since $\log(1+u)=u+o(u)$ as $u\to0$, it follows that

$$
\boxed{\log M_X(t)=\frac{t^2}{2}+o(t^2),\qquad\alpha=\frac12.}
$$

This is the [quadratic expansion of a cumulant-generating function](../../../../../quadratic-expansion-of-a-cumulant-generating-function.md) for a centered variable of variance one.
