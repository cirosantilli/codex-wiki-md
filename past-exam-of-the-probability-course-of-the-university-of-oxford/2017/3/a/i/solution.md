↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [transition matrix](../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) $P$ defines an [irreducible Markov chain](../../../../../irreducible-markov-chain.md) if every state is accessible from every other state:

$$
\boxed{\text{for every }i,j\in S,\ \text{there is }n\geq0\text{ with }(P^n)_{ij}>0.}
$$

Equivalently, all states belong to one [communicating class](../../../../../communicating-class.md). When $i\ne j$, a positive number of steps is necessarily used.
