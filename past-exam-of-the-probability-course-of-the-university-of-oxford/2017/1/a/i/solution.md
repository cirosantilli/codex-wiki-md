↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By [independence of random variables](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), the [joint probability density](../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) is the product of the two [probability density functions](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) of the rate-one [exponential distributions](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md):

$$
\boxed{f_{X,Y}(x,y)=e^{-(x+y)}\mathbf1_{\{x>0,\ y>0\}}.}
$$

Its values on the boundary are immaterial, because the boundary has zero area.
