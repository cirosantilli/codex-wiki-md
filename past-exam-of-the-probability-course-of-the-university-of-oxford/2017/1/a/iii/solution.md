<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $W=X+Y$. The independent pair $(U,W)$ has the same [joint distribution](../../../../../joint-probability-distribution.md) as $(V,W)$ from part (ii), because both first coordinates are uniform and independent of the same sum law. Hence

$$
U(X+Y)\ \stackrel d=\ VW=X.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{U(X+Y)\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1).}
$$

The rate is one. This multiplication identity is the mechanism behind the [exponential invariant law of a random affine recursion](../../../../../exponential-invariant-law-of-a-random-affine-recursion.md).
