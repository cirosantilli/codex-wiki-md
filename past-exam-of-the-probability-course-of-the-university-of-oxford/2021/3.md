# 3

↑ **Parent:** [2021](../2021-split.md)

Put $q=1-p=a/(a+b)$. The [random walk](../../random-walk-split.md) has [independent and identically distributed](../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) increments $Y_n$ equal to $a$ with [probability](../../probability.md) $p=b/(a+b)$ and $-b$ with [probability](../../probability.md) $q$. Thus $S_0=0$, $S_n=\sum_{j=1}^nY_j$, and $\mathbb EY_n=0$.
