<h1 id="2/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take a [discrete probability distribution](../../../../../discrete-probability-distribution-split.md) on three points:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(Y=-2)=\frac25,\qquad\mathbb P(Y=1)=\frac12,\qquad\mathbb P(Y=3)=\frac1{10}.}
$$

The [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) are positive and sum to one. Direct calculation gives

$$
\mathbb EY=-\frac45+\frac12+\frac3{10}=0,\qquad
\mathbb EY^3=-\frac{16}5+\frac12+\frac{27}{10}=0,
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb EY^4=\frac{32}5+\frac12+\frac{81}{10}=15<\infty.}
$$

Nevertheless it is not a [symmetric distribution](../../../../../symmetric-distribution.md), since $\mathbb P(Y=3)=1/10$ while $\mathbb P(Y=-3)=0$. Vanishing of just the first and third [moments](../../../../../moment.md) does not force all odd [moments](../../../../../moment.md) to vanish.
