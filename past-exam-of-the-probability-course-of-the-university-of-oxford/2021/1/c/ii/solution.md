<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The unit-time increments $D_j=N_j-N_{j-1}$ are [independent and identically distributed random variables](../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md), by [independent increments](../../../../../independent-increments.md) and [stationary increments](../../../../../stationary-increments.md) of the [Poisson process](../../../../../poisson-process-split.md). Each has the [Poisson distribution](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) of parameter one, hence [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) one and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) one. The [central limit theorem](../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{N_n-n}{\sqrt n}=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{j=1}^n(D_j-1)\ \xrightarrow{\mathrm d}\ N(0,1).}
$$
