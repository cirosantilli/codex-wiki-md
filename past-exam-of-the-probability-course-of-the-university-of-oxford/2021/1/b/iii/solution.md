<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use $X_n\sim\operatorname{Unif}[0,1/n]$ from part (a). We already proved [convergence in distribution](../../../../../convergence-in-distribution.md) to zero. For any prescribed $p\in[0,1]$, put $c_n=p/n$. Then $c_n\to0$, and the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{F_n(c_n)=F_n(p/n)=p\quad\text{for every }n.}
$$

This includes $p=0$ and $p=1$, since the [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) assigns zero [probability](../../../../../probability.md) to each endpoint. The limit [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is discontinuous at zero, explaining why the conclusion of (ii) does not apply there.
