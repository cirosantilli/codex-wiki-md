# Coreflective subcategory

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)

A full subcategory is coreflective when its inclusion $I$ has a right adjoint $q$. The counit $IqX\to X$ is universal for maps to $X$ from objects of the subcategory. The [comonad](comonad.md) $Iq$ is idempotent. For a finite-limit-closed coreflective subcategory of a [topos](elementary-topos.md), its coalgebra construction can prove that the subcategory is itself a topos.
