# Homotopy-group effect of attaching higher cells

↑ **Parent:** [Cell attachment](cell-attachment.md)

Attaching cells of dimension $i+1$ to a connected [CW complex](cw-complex.md), for $i\geq2$, preserves all [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) below degree $i$. In degree $i$ it takes the quotient by the subgroup generated by the attaching maps and their translates under the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) action. This follows from the relative [homotopy](homotopy.md) sequence and the generation of the first nonzero relative group by the new cells. In particular, a single attaching map representing a generator of $\pi_i\cong\mathbb Z$ kills that group even when the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is nontrivial. Higher attachments can create higher [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md), which may then be killed at later stages.
