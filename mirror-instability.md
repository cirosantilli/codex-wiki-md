# Mirror instability

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure anisotropy](pressure-anisotropy.md)

A compressive kinetic [plasma](plasma-physics.md) instability generally driven by sufficiently large [perpendicular plasma pressure](perpendicular-plasma-pressure.md) relative to [parallel plasma pressure](parallel-plasma-pressure.md). Perturbed field-strength minima trap particles and alter perpendicular [pressure-balanced plasma perturbation](pressure-balanced-plasma-perturbation.md). Its threshold depends on species distributions and [plasma beta](plasma-beta.md); isotropic equilibrium alone does not imply this instability.
