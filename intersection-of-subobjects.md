# Intersection of subobjects

↑ **Parent:** [Subobject](subobject.md)

In a [complete category](complete-category.md), the intersection of a set-indexed family of [subobjects](subobject.md) of $C$ is their wide [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md) over $C$. The resulting morphism into $C$ is a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md), and it factors through every member. In a [well-powered category](well-powered-category.md), even the intersection of all subobjects satisfying a specified property is small.
