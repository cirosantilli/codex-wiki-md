# Borel remainder for a cubic exponential integral

↑ **Parent:** [Cubic exponential integral Stokes sectors](cubic-exponential-integral-stokes-sectors.md)

Near $\arg z=\pi/3$, set $\lambda=-z^3$ on the branch with argument near zero and truncate the endpoint expansion at $N$ terms. Its remaining factorial series, indexed by $r=N+p$, is $\lambda^{-1/3}/[3\Gamma(2/3)]$ times the [Borel summation](borel-summation.md) of $\sum_{p\geq0}\Gamma(N+2/3+p)e^\lambda/\lambda^{N+2/3+p}$. The upper-pole contour fixes the stated remainder and its continuation from the sector below the switching line. With $N+2/3\sim|z|^3$, the [Gaussian pole transition for a large gamma parameter](gaussian-pole-transition-for-a-large-gamma-parameter.md) smooths the algebraic coefficient from $C$ to $Ce^{2\pi i/3}$.
