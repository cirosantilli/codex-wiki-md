# Kerr metric

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerr_metric)

The Kerr metric is the stationary axisymmetric vacuum metric of a rotating mass. Parameters $M$ and $a$ determine whether its geometry has the horizons of a [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md); the metric also admits over-extremal parameters with no horizon.
