# Homology of the complement of a smooth conic

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth plane conic](smooth-plane-conic.md)

The complement of a closed [tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md) of a [smooth plane conic](smooth-plane-conic.md) has the displayed integral [homology](homology-split.md). The [Excision theorem](excision-theorem.md) and [Thom isomorphism theorem](thom-isomorphism-theorem.md) reduce the calculation to the relative long exact sequence: the ambient degree-four [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) restricts with coefficient one, while the degree-two map is intersection with the conic and has coefficient two. A [collar neighborhood](collar-neighbourhood.md) identifies the open and compact exterior [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md) types. The boundary instead has first [homology](homology-split.md) $\mathbb Z/4$, from the [Gysin sequence](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) and the normal [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md) four.
