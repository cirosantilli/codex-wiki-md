# Colluvial wedge

↑ **Parent:** [Colluvium](colluvium.md)

A wedge-shaped slope deposit, commonly thickest beside a scarp and thinning away from it. When combined with faulting, burial and cross-cutting relations it can record a past surface-rupturing [earthquake](earthquake.md); a wedge alone does not prove a separate event.
