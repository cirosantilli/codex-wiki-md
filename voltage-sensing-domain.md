# Voltage-sensing domain

↑ **Parent:** [Voltage-gated ion channel](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md)

A [voltage-sensing domain](voltage-sensing-domain.md) changes conformation in response to membrane voltage. Charged residues, often in an S4 helix, redistribute in the electric field and couple to other functional parts of the protein.
