# Progressive measurability

↑ **Parent:** [Adapted process](adapted-process.md)

A process $H$ is progressively measurable when, for every $T$, its restriction to $[0,T]\times\Omega$ is measurable for $\mathcal B([0,T])\otimes\mathcal F_T$. Continuous [adapted processes](adapted-process.md) are progressively measurable, and a Borel function of such a process remains so. A progressively measurable square-integrable Brownian integrand has an equivalent predictable integrand for the [Itô integral](ito-integral.md), up to equality for $dt\,d\mathbb P$.
