# Integral generator signs constrain Steenrod comparisons

↑ **Parent:** [Stable cohomology operation](stable-cohomology-operation.md)

Suppose two integral cohomology groups of a space are free of rank one. An integral isomorphism can send each chosen generator only to itself or its negative. After reduction modulo $p$, naturality of a [Steenrod reduced power](steenrod-reduced-power.md) between those groups therefore permits its coefficient to change only by a sign, even though an abstract vector-space change of basis could rescale by any nonzero field element. At $p=5$, coefficients one and two cannot be related by signs, so they obstruct an integral [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md). This constraint is stronger than comparing the isolated mod-five Steenrod modules with arbitrary bases.
