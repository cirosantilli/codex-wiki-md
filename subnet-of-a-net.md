# Subnet of a net

↑ **Parent:** [Net (mathematics)](net-mathematics.md)

A subnet is obtained from a [net](net-mathematics.md) by an order-preserving cofinal map from another directed index set. It preserves any limit of the original net, and eventual properties still hold along it. Every net in a [compact set](compact-space.md) has a convergent subnet. This supplies compactness arguments when sequential compactness is unavailable.
