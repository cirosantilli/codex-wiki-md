# Reciprocal-Jacobian quadratic hyperelastic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)

On positive-determinant [deformation gradients](deformation-gradient.md), the first Piola-Kirchhoff [stress](stress.md) is $\mu(F-J^{-1}F^{-T})$. Its zero set is $SO(3)$: multiplication by $F^T$ gives $FF^T=J^{-1}I$, and [determinants](determinant.md) give $J^5=1$. These are also the global [minimizers](global-minimizer.md), since $|F|^2\geq3J^{2/3}$ and $3J^{2/3}/2+J^{-1}$ has its unique [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) $5/2$ at $J=1$. The [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) energy is $5\mu/2$ rather than zero; an additive constant changes no [stress](stress.md).
