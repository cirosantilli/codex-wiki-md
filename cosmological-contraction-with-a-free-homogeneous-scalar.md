# Cosmological contraction with a free homogeneous scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous free scalar as a stiff fluid](homogeneous-free-scalar-as-a-stiff-fluid.md)

In units $8\pi G=1$, a free homogeneous scalar has $\rho=P=\dot\phi_0^2/2$ and therefore a stiff equation of state. Its equation gives $a^2\phi_0'=\text{constant}$, while the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $3\mathcal H^2=\phi_0'^2/2$. The displayed contracting branch satisfies both equations for negative [conformal time](conformal-time.md). Constant normalizations and an additive scalar shift can be restored.
