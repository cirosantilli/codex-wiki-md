# Wilcoxon signed-rank statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Wilcoxon signed-rank test](wilcoxon-signed-rank-test.md)

For a continuous sample, the statistic also equals $\sum_i\mathbf1_{\{X_i>0\}}+\sum_{i<j}\mathbf1_{\{X_i+X_j>0\}}$. In each pair the sign of the sum is the sign of the observation with larger absolute value, proving the identity by counting its smaller-rank partners. Under a continuous symmetric null, the pair kernel has projection $F(x)-1/2$ and projection [variance](variance-split.md) $1/12$. The [U-statistic central limit theorem](u-statistic-central-limit-theorem.md) yields [asymptotic normality](asymptotic-normality.md) of $W_+$, with [variance](variance-split.md) asymptotic to $n^3/12$.
