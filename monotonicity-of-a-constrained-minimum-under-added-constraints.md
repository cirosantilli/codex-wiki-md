# Monotonicity of a constrained minimum under added constraints

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

Adding an [optimization constraint](optimization-constraint.md) shrinks the admissible set, so it cannot lower the infimum of the same [objective function](objective-function.md). Every value attained on the smaller set was already available on the larger set. If both minima exist and the larger problem has a unique minimizer absent from the smaller set, the inequality is strict. This compares beam energies for free, pinned, and clamped endpoints without evaluating the energy integrals.
