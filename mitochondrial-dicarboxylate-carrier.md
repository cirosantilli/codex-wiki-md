# Mitochondrial dicarboxylate carrier

↑ **Parent:** [Inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md)

A transport [protein](protein-split.md) exchanging suitable dicarboxylates, including [succinate](succinate.md) and [malate](malate.md), with counter-substrates such as [phosphate](phosphate.md). Its relevance to respiration depends on the fuel and preparation.
