# Information projection

↑ **Parent:** [Method of types](method-of-types.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_projection)

An information projection of $Q$ onto a set $\mathcal C$ of [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md) minimizes $D(P\Vert Q)$ over $P\in\mathcal C$. For a closed [convex set](convex-set.md) and suitable $Q$, it obeys the Pythagorean inequality

$$
D(P\Vert Q)\geq D(P\Vert P^*)+D(P^*\Vert Q).
$$
