# Opmonoidal functor

↑ **Parent:** [Monoidal category](monoidal-category.md)

A [functor](functor.md) with natural maps $F_2:F(X\otimes Y)\to FX\otimes FY$ and $F_0:FI\to I$ satisfying the reversed [monoidal functor](monoidal-functor.md) associativity and unit diagrams. No invertibility is required.
