# Singlet oxygen

↑ **Parent:** [Oxygen](oxygen.md)

[Singlet oxygen](singlet-oxygen.md) is an electronically excited form of molecular oxygen with reactivity distinct from ground-state oxygen. Energy transfer from triplet chlorophyll can generate it during photosynthetic excitation stress.
