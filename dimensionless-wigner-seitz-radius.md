# Dimensionless Wigner-Seitz radius

↑ **Parent:** [Wigner-Seitz radius](wigner-seitz-radius.md)

Write $(3/(4\pi n))^{1/3}=r_sa_B^*$, where $a_B^*=4\pi\varepsilon\hbar^2/(m^*e^2)$. Here $r_s$ is dimensionless, unlike a [Wigner-Seitz radius](wigner-seitz-radius.md) measured as a length. Electron-gas [energy](energy-split.md) formulas quoted in effective atomic units commonly use this convention.
