# Classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations

↑ **Parent:** [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md)

For every $n\geq0$ there is one irreducible $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)$-module $V(n)$ of dimension $n+1$, with weights $n,n-2,\ldots,-n$, each of multiplicity one. Every finite-dimensional representation is a direct sum of these modules.
