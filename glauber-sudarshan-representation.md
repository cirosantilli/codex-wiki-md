# Glauber-Sudarshan representation

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mixture of coherent states](classical-mixture-of-coherent-states.md)

The diagonal [coherent state](coherent-state.md) representation writes a single-mode [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) in the form shown. Its weight may be a generalized distribution rather than an ordinary nonnegative probability density. A [nonclassical state of light](nonclassical-state-of-light.md) has no nonnegative measure of this form. This use of $P$ differs from the [Wigner distribution](wigner-distribution.md); a nonnegative Wigner function does not by itself certify classicality.
