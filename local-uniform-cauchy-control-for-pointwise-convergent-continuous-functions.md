# Local uniform Cauchy control for pointwise convergent continuous functions

↑ **Parent:** [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md)

Suppose [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) $f_n:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ converge [pointwise](pointwise-convergence.md) to $f$. For each $\epsilon>0$, some nonempty [open interval](open-interval.md) $I$ and integer $N$ satisfy $|f_n(x)-f(x)|\leq\epsilon$ for every $x\in I$ and $n\geq N$. Indeed the [closed sets](closed-set.md) $Q_N=\bigcap_{n,m\geq N}\{|f_n-f_m|\leq\epsilon\}$ cover $\mathbb R$. The [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md) gives an interval inside one $Q_N$, and passing $m\to\infty$ proves the assertion. The interval may depend on $\epsilon$; this does not assert [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) on one fixed interval.
