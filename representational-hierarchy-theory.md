# Representational hierarchy theory

↑ **Parent:** [Neural coding](neural-coding.md)

An account of regional specialization in terms of the complexity and conjunctions represented, rather than a strict division between [perception](perception.md) and memory. The [feature-conjunction account of perirhinal cortex](feature-conjunction-account-of-perirhinal-cortex.md) proposes object-level combinations that resolve shared-feature ambiguity; higher-order relations can require further interactions.[2002, model predictions and controlled feature-ambiguity contrast](perirhinal-cortex-resolves-feature-ambiguity-in-complex-visual-discriminations-by-timothy-j-bussey-and-others.md)
