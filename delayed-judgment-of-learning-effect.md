# Delayed judgment-of-learning effect

↑ **Parent:** [Judgment of learning](judgment-of-learning.md)

Improved itemwise predictive accuracy of [judgments of learning](judgment-of-learning.md) made after a delay under suitable conditions. The available cue matters: delaying a cue-alone judgment can be useful even when delaying a cue–target judgment gives little benefit.[1992, abstract](importance-of-the-kind-of-cue-for-judgments-of-learning-and-the-delayed-jol-effect-by-john-dunlosky-and-thomas-o-nelson.md)
