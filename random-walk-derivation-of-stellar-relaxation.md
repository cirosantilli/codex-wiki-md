# Random-walk derivation of stellar relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar relaxation time](stellar-relaxation-time.md)

[Small-angle gravitational encounters](small-angle-gravitational-encounter.md) occur at rate $2\pi b\,db\,nu$ and supply kicks $2Gm/(bu)$. Independent kick variances add to $d\langle\Delta v^2\rangle/dt=8\pi G^2m^2n\ln\Lambda/u$. Setting the accumulated variance to $u^2$ and using virial scaling gives a [stellar relaxation time](stellar-relaxation-time.md) of order $0.1N/\ln N$ [stellar crossing times](stellar-crossing-time.md), with the coefficient dependent on profile and crossing convention.
