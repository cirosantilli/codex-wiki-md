# Longitudinal polarization vector

↑ **Parent:** [Particle polarization](particle-polarization.md)

For an on-shell massive vector with $k^\mu=(E,|\mathbf k|\widehat{\mathbf k})$ in [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) signature $+---$, the displayed vector satisfies $k\cdot\epsilon_L=0$ and $\epsilon_L^2=-1$. It specifies the longitudinal one of the three physical polarization states. It is not a replacement for a final polarization sum when other states are unobserved.
