# Flux-driven convective layer cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Mantle convection](mantle-convection.md)

For [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) per area $H=kd/\kappa$, imposed basal flux $F$, fixed top [temperature](temperature-split.md), and [marginal-stability thermal boundary-layer scaling](marginal-stability-thermal-boundary-layer-scaling.md), the well-mixed interior has $H\dot\theta=F-C\theta^{4/3}$. Its steady excess [temperature](temperature-split.md) is $\theta_*=(F/C)^{3/4}$. The near-equilibrium [relaxation time](relaxation-time.md) is $\tau=3H\theta_*/(4F)=3d\delta_*/(4\kappa)$, with $\delta_*=k\theta_*/F$. A full large-amplitude cooling history is nonlinear rather than a single exponential.
