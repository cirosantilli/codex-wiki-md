# Coherent continuum phase matching

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh scattering](rayleigh-scattering.md)

In a homogeneous medium driven by a [plane wave](plane-wave.md), coherent scattering amplitudes carry phases $e^{i(\mathbf k_i-\mathbf k_s)\cdot\mathbf r}$. An infinite uniform volume cancels arbitrary nonforward components. Finite shapes, boundaries and fluctuations determine any side signal; summing independent-particle intensities is not the continuous-medium limit.
