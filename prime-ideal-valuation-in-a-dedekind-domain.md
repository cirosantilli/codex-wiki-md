# Prime-ideal valuation in a Dedekind domain

↑ **Parent:** [Unique factorization of ideals in a Dedekind domain](unique-factorization-of-ideals-in-a-dedekind-domain.md)

For a nonzero [ideal](ideal.md) $I$ in a [Dedekind domain](dedekind-domain.md), $v_P(I)$ is the exponent of the nonzero [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $P$ in the [unique factorization of ideals in a Dedekind domain](unique-factorization-of-ideals-in-a-dedekind-domain.md). Then $I\subseteq J$ if and only if $v_P(I)\geq v_P(J)$ for every $P$, and $v_P(I+J)=\min(v_P(I),v_P(J))$.
