<h1 id="b/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) coordinate change preserves the [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and takes the [particle exchange operator](../../../../particle-exchange-operator.md) to $(x_+,x_-)\mapsto(x_+,-x_-)$. Thus the spatial [wave function](../../../../wave-function-split.md) is even in $x_-$ for [bosons](../../../../boson.md) and odd for [fermions](../../../../fermion.md).

The total [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) and [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) separate as

$$
V=\frac{m\omega^2}{2}x_+^2+\frac{m\omega^2(1+2\kappa)}2x_-^2,\qquad
H=H_+(\omega)+H_-(\Omega),\quad\Omega=\omega\sqrt{1+2\kappa}.
$$

Assume $1+2\kappa>0$ so the relative [oscillator](../../../../oscillator.md) is confining. Its spectrum is $E_{n_+,n_-}=\hbar\omega(n_++1/2)+\hbar\Omega(n_-+1/2)$, and its [parity](../../../../parity.md) in the [two-particle relative coordinate](../../../../two-particle-relative-coordinate.md) is $(-1)^{n_-}$.

The lowest [boson](../../../../boson.md) state uses $n_+=n_-=0$, whereas the lowest [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) state uses $n_+=0$, $n_-=1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{E_B=\tfrac12\hbar\omega[1+\sqrt{1+2\kappa}],\qquad
E_F=\tfrac12\hbar\omega[1+3\sqrt{1+2\kappa}].}
$$

For an [oscillator](../../../../oscillator.md) of frequency $\Omega$, $\langle x_-^2\rangle_n=(n+1/2)\hbar/(m\Omega)$. Since $(x_1-x_2)^2=2x_-^2$,

$$
\boxed{\langle(x_1-x_2)^2\rangle_B=\frac\hbar{m\Omega},\qquad
\langle(x_1-x_2)^2\rangle_F=\frac{3\hbar}{m\Omega}.}
$$

The factor three is the extra relative-coordinate excitation required by antisymmetry. The supplied [oscillator](../../../../oscillator.md) [wave functions](../../../../wave-function-split.md) correspond to [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $P^2/(2m)$; the extra factor $1/2$ printed before that term in the hint is inconsistent with those functions and the stated energies. The calculation uses the conventional [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) dictated by particle [mass](../../../../mass.md) $m$.
