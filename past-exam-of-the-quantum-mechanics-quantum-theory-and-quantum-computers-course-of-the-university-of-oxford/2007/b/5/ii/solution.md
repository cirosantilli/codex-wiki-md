<h1 id="b/5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For identical [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) the full [wave function](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is antisymmetric:

$$
\boxed{\psi(x_2,x_1)=-\psi(x_1,x_2).}
$$

In particular it vanishes when the two complete one-particle arguments coincide. Applying this to the position-only model assumes that no independent [spin](../../../../../spin.md) symmetry changes the required spatial [parity](../../../../../parity.md).
