<h1 id="b/5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For identical [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) the full [wave function](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is symmetric under the [particle exchange operator](../../../../../particle-exchange-operator.md):

$$
\boxed{\psi(x_2,x_1)=\psi(x_1,x_2).}
$$

In the given position-only model this is the spatial parity under the [particle exchange operator](../../../../../particle-exchange-operator.md). If additional [spin](../../../../../spin.md) variables are present, the symmetry applies to the complete state including them.
