# Weak Lq space

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

For $1<q<\infty$, the weak Lq space consists of measurable functions for which $A_q(f)=\sup_{t>0}t|\{|f|>t\}|^{1/q}$ is finite. This distribution-function expression is a [quasi-norm](quasi-norm.md). The equivalent norm $N_q(f)=\sup_{0<|E|<\infty}|E|^{-1/q'}\int_E|f|$ satisfies $A_q\leq N_q\leq q'A_q$, with $q'=q/(q-1)$. The [layer cake representation](layer-cake-representation.md) bounds the integral by $\int_0^\infty\min(|E|,(A_q/t)^q)dt=q'A_q|E|^{1/q'}$. The lower bound uses finite-measure subsets of a superlevel set.
