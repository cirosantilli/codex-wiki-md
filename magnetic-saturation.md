# Magnetic saturation

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetization](magnetization.md)

[Magnetic saturation](magnetic-saturation.md) is the regime in which increasing the applied [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md) produces little further increase in [magnetization](magnetization.md). The total [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) can still increase through its vacuum contribution, $\mathbf B=\mu_0(\mathbf H+\mathbf M)$. Consequently a constant large [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) cannot describe a core throughout this regime; ideal [magnetic circuit](magnetic-circuit.md) predictions based on that assumption overestimate field concentration.
