# Chief series

↑ **Parent:** [Normal series of a group](normal-series-of-a-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_series)

A chief series is a strict [normal series of a group](normal-series-of-a-group.md) with no proper normal-series refinement. Its factors are [minimal normal subgroups](minimal-normal-subgroup.md) of the appropriate quotient groups. A chief factor of a finite soluble group is elementary abelian, whereas a composition factor of a finite soluble group is cyclic of prime order. A chief factor can therefore have order larger than a prime.
