# Best linear prediction from a finite past

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

The best mean-square [linear predictor](linear-predictor.md) is [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto the span of the available centered observations, plus the known mean. Orthogonal innovations give a convenient basis of that span. Prediction at a future horizon must use only actually observed variables; it need not be a one-step predictor.
