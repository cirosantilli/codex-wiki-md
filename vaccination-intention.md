# Vaccination intention

↑ **Parent:** [Vaccination](vaccination.md)

A person's reported intention or willingness to receive a vaccine under the conditions of a question. It is distinct from actual [vaccination status](vaccination-status.md). The response depends on the question, time and circumstances; an intention score does not directly measure later receipt or a medical effect.
