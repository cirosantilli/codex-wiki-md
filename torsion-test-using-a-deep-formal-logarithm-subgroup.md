# Torsion test using a deep formal logarithm subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Deep logarithm subgroup of a formal group](deep-logarithm-subgroup-of-a-formal-group.md)

Let $E/\mathbb Q_p$ have [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md), and choose an integer $m>1/(p-1)$. Its [filtration of elliptic-curve points over a local field](filtration-of-elliptic-curve-points-over-a-local-field.md) has a [torsion-free group](torsion-free-group.md) $E_m$ identified by the [formal logarithm](formal-logarithm.md) with $p^m\mathbb Z_p$. The [quotient group](quotient-group.md) $G=E(\mathbb Q_p)/E_m$ is finite. For $g\in G$ of [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) $N$, choose a lift $P$. There is a [torsion point of an elliptic curve](torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) in this coset exactly when the displayed condition holds. Indeed, it is equivalent to the existence of $Q\in E_m$ with $[N]Q=[N]P$. Then $P-Q$ is killed by $N$; conversely every torsion representative has order $N$, since $E_m$ is torsion-free. The logarithmic division and the [formal group exponential](formal-group-exponential.md) give the unique such $Q$. Changing the lift changes the logarithm by an element of $Np^m\mathbb Z_p$, so the criterion is well-defined. This finite-coset test includes residue-characteristic torsion; only the [prime-to-p torsion lifts uniquely at good reduction](prime-to-p-torsion-lifts-uniquely-at-good-reduction.md) are automatic.
