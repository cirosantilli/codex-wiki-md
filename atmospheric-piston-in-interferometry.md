# Atmospheric piston in interferometry

↑ **Parent:** [Optical interferometry](optical-interferometry.md)

A nearly uniform wavefront phase over one collector is called its piston. Differential atmospheric piston changes the pairwise optical phase without requiring a change in the source. Delay tracking and [closure phase](closure-phase.md) address different consequences of this phase error; [intensity](intensity-physics.md) correlations are unchanged by an ideal pure phase multiplication at either detector.
