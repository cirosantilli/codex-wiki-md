# Edge colourings of a tetrahedron under rotations

↑ **Parent:** [Burnside's lemma](burnside-s-lemma.md)

The twelve rotational symmetries of a [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md) act on its six edges. Their edge cycle patterns are six fixed edges for the identity, two three-cycles for each of eight [rotational symmetries](rotational-symmetry.md) of order three, and two fixed edges plus two transpositions for each of three half-turns. [Burnside's lemma](burnside-s-lemma.md) therefore counts [edge colourings](edge-coloring.md) with $q$ labelled colours by $(q^6+8q^2+3q^4)/12$.
