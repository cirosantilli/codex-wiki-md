# Modified Bessel function of the first kind

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Bessel function](modified-bessel-function.md)

The solution of the [Modified Bessel differential equation](modified-bessel-differential-equation.md) with [power series](power-series.md) $I_\nu(z)=\sum_{j\geq0}(z/2)^{2j+\nu}/[j!\Gamma(j+\nu+1)]$, interpreted through the reciprocal [gamma function](gamma-function.md) at its zeros. For fixed real order it grows exponentially along the positive real axis.
