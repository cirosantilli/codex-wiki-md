# Calibration for semicircular hyperbolic geodesics

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic in the Poincare half-plane model](geodesic-in-the-poincare-half-plane-model.md)

In the upper half-plane $y>0$, the [hyperbolic length](hyperbolic-length-in-the-poincare-disc.md) element is $ds/y$. The function $\Phi=\operatorname{arsinh}(x/y)$ has [gradient](gradient.md) $(1,-x/y)/\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ and norm $1/y$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\int ds/y\geq\Phi(B)-\Phi(A)$ for any oriented path from $A$ to $B$. Equality holds along a semicircle $x^2+y^2=R^2$ traversed with increasing $x$, because its tangent is parallel to that gradient. This is a [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) proof of global minimality, beyond the stationary-path conclusion of the [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md). Translating $x$ gives the analogous proof for semicircles with other boundary centers.
