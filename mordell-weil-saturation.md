# Mordell-Weil saturation

↑ **Parent:** [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md)

The saturation of a subgroup $H$ of the [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md) consists of all points with a positive multiple in $H$. Saturation includes the full [torsion subgroup](torsion-subgroup.md). For a full-rank $H$ containing that torsion, it equals $E(K)$, but the index of $H$ may exceed one. Checking that $\ell P\in H$ forces $P\in H$ for every [prime](prime-number.md) divisor of a certified index bound proves that $H$ is already the whole group. A [canonical height pairing](canonical-height-pairing.md) proves independence of found points; saturation answers the separate question of whether a full-rank subgroup is the full group.
