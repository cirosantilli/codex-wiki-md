# Non-Abelian gauge transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

For the convention $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+gA_\mu$, a [gauge group](gauge-group.md) transformation $\Phi\mapsto R\Phi$ acts on the [gauge potential](gauge-field.md) by $A_\mu\mapsto RA_\mu R^{-1}-g^{-1}(\partial_\mu R)R^{-1}$. The [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) transforms by conjugation. This makes $D_\mu\Phi$ transform in the same [gauge group representation](gauge-group-representation.md) as $\Phi$.
