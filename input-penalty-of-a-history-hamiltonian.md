# Input penalty of a history Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman-Kitaev Hamiltonian](feynman-kitaev-hamiltonian.md)

The [input penalty of a history Hamiltonian](input-penalty-of-a-history-hamiltonian.md) tests the prescribed [ancilla qubits](ancilla-qubit.md) at clock time zero and leaves the [quantum witness](quantum-witness.md) unrestricted. Zero [ancilla qubits](ancilla-qubit.md) are tested by $|1\rangle\langle1|$, and plus [ancilla qubits](ancilla-qubit.md) by $|-\rangle\langle-|$. Their sum $Q$ is positive and has positive integer [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). Together with propagation, its kernel selects histories of valid initial data.
