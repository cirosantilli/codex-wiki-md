# Simplicial cone

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)

Realization of the simplicial construction produces the corresponding [topological cone](cone-topology.md).

The simplicial cone $v*K$ adjoins a new vertex $v$ to every simplex of $K$ and is contractible.
