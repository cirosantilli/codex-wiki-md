# Drag dissipation of a bead-spring pair

↑ **Parent:** [Hookean dumbbell model](hookean-dumbbell-model.md)

When the midpoint follows a [linear velocity field](linear-velocity-field.md), the two beads' slip [velocities](velocity.md) are $\pm(\dot{\mathbf R}-K\mathbf R)/2$. Summing their individual drag powers $\zeta|\mathbf v_{\rm slip}|^2$ proves the displayed expression. Using a [probability current velocity](probability-current-velocity.md) in place of the instantaneous relative velocity defines the corresponding overdamped dissipative functional; adding independent thermal velocities is a different stochastic description.
