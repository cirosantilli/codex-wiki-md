# Red clump

↑ **Parent:** [Horizontal branch](horizontal-branch.md)

The concentration of core-helium-burning [stars](star.md) on the cool part of the [horizontal branch](horizontal-branch.md). It must be distinguished from the ascending hydrogen-shell-burning [red giant branch](red-giant-branch.md).
