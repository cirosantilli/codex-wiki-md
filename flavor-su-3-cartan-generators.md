<h1 id="flavor-su-3-cartan-generators">Flavor SU(3) Cartan generators</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md)

For [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) acting on $(u,d,s)$, trace-orthonormal Hermitian diagonal generators are $h_1=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)/\sqrt2$ and $h_2=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2)/\sqrt6$. The [isospin](isospin.md) and [flavor hypercharge](flavor-hypercharge.md) operators are $I_3=h_1/\sqrt2$ and $Y=\sqrt{2/3}\,h_2$. Their multiples by $i$ lie in the compact [SU(3) Lie algebra](su-3-lie-algebra.md).
