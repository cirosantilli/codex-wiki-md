# Moving mechanical constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

A [constraint](constraint-mechanics.md) that depends explicitly on time can exert a [normal force](normal-force.md) that does actual [work](work.md), even when its [force](force-split.md) does no [work](work.md) on an allowed instantaneous virtual [displacement](displacement.md). The surface motion gives the constrained particle a normal component of actual [velocity](velocity.md).
