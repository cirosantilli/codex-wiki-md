# Boundary-irreducible three-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

A compact [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) is boundary-irreducible when there is no embedded disk with interior in the manifold and boundary an essential curve in its boundary. Thus its boundary has no compressing disk.
