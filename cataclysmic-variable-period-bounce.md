# Cataclysmic-variable period bounce

↑ **Parent:** [Cataclysmic-variable period minimum](cataclysmic-variable-period-minimum.md)

The reversal from decreasing to increasing [orbital period](orbital-period.md) during continuing donor mass loss. The [Roche-lobe-filling period-density relation](roche-lobe-filling-period-density-relation.md) gives $d\log P/d\log M_d=(3\zeta-1)/2$, where $\zeta$ is the effective [stellar radius response exponent](stellar-radius-response-exponent.md) along the mass-losing sequence. A crossing from $\zeta>1/3$ to $\zeta<1/3$ gives a local [cataclysmic-variable period minimum](cataclysmic-variable-period-minimum.md).
