# Trefoil knot

↑ **Parent:** [Torus knot](torus-knot.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trefoil_knot)

The trefoil is the torus knot $T_{2,3}$. It has [Seifert genus](seifert-genus.md) one, [crossing number of a knot](crossing-number-of-a-knot.md) three, and [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md) $t-1+t^{-1}$ up to a unit.
