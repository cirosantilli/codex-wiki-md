# Trefoil knot

↑ **Parent:** [Torus knot](torus-knot.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trefoil_knot)

The trefoil is the [torus knot](torus-knot.md) $T_{2,3}$. It is the simplest nontrivial [knot](knot.md) by [crossing number of a knot](crossing-number-of-a-knot.md): $c(T_{2,3})=3$. A standard three-crossing [alternating knot diagram](alternating-knot-diagram.md) is reduced, so the [Tait crossing-number theorem](tait-crossing-number-theorem.md) establishes minimality. The four-crossing [knot diagram](knot-diagram.md) shown below also represents the [trefoil knot](trefoil-knot.md), illustrating that a particular projection need not achieve that minimum. Its [Seifert genus](seifert-genus.md) is one.

Here is a small algebraic certificate that a [trefoil knot](trefoil-knot.md) cannot be deformed into an [unknot](unknot.md). One [Seifert matrix](seifert-matrix.md) for a [trefoil knot](trefoil-knot.md) is

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad tA-A^T=\begin{pmatrix}t-1&1\\-t&t-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Taking the [determinant](determinant.md) gives the [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md)

$$
\det(tA-A^T)=(t-1)^2+t=t^2-t+1\doteq t-1+t^{-1},
$$

where $\doteq$ allows multiplication by a [Laurent unit](unit-of-a-laurent-polynomial-ring.md) $\pm t^n$ in the [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md). No such multiplication turns this three-term [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md) into the [unknot](unknot.md)'s value $1$. Since the [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md) is a [knot invariant](knot-invariant.md), this computation rules out every possible [ambient isotopy](ambient-isotopy.md) between the two [knots](knot.md); it does not merely rule out the deformations we happened to try.

The [knot group](knot-group.md) supplies another certificate. The presentation $\langle a,b\mid a^2=b^3\rangle$ maps onto the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_3$ by $a\mapsto(12)$ and $b\mapsto(123)$, since both sides of the relation map to the identity. These images generate a [non-abelian group](non-abelian-group.md), so the [knot group](knot-group.md) cannot be the [unknot](unknot.md)'s [infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md). However, a [trefoil knot](trefoil-knot.md) and its [mirror of a link](mirror-of-a-link.md) have isomorphic [knot groups](knot-group.md): the abstract [knot group](knot-group.md) alone does not distinguish all [knots](knot.md).
