# Lobatto quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

An $s$-node Lobatto quadrature rule includes both endpoints and is exact for [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $2s-3$. On $[-1,1]$, its interior nodes are the zeros of the derivative of the degree-$(s-1)$ [Legendre polynomial](legendre-polynomial.md). The three-node rule on $[0,1]$ has nodes $0,1/2,1$ and weights $1/6,2/3,1/6$. Integrating the interpolation basis from zero to each node gives the three-stage [Lobatto IIIA method](lobatto-iiia-method.md).
