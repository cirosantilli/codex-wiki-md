# Normal-normal credibility

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian credibility](bayesian-credibility.md)

Conditionally independent [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) observations of mean $\theta$ and known variance $\sigma_1^2$, with normal prior mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma_2^2$, give [normal-normal conjugacy with known observation variance](normal-normal-conjugacy-with-known-observation-variance.md). Their [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) has the displayed exact [credibility estimate](credibility-estimate.md) form. It minimizes posterior [squared-error loss](squared-error-loss.md) and predicts the conditional mean of the next observation.
