# Kelvin-Voigt step-stress creep

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin-Voigt model](kelvin-voigt-model.md)

For $E\varepsilon+\eta\dot\varepsilon=\sigma_0\Theta(t)$ with initially zero [strain](strain.md), $\varepsilon=(\sigma_0/E)(1-e^{-t/\tau})$ and $\tau=\eta/E$. [Strain](strain.md) is continuous at load application, approaches a bounded value and recovers exponentially after unloading.
