# Hole mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Charge carrier mobility](charge-carrier-mobility.md)

The positive hole mobility is the magnitude of an [electron hole](electron-hole.md)'s weak-field [drift velocity](drift-velocity.md) per unit [electric field](electric-field-split.md). In an isotropic relaxation-time model, $\mu_h=e\tau_h/m_h^*$, and its contribution to [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) is $ep\mu_h$. The sign is opposite to an electron's drift because the [electron hole](electron-hole.md) has positive charge.
