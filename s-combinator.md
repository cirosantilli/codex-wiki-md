# S combinator

↑ **Parent:** [Combinator](combinator.md)

In [lambda calculus](lambda-calculus.md), $S\equiv\lambda xyz.xz(yz)$ distributes its third argument to its first two arguments. In [combinatory logic](combinatory-logic.md), its primitive constant satisfies $SABC=AC(BC)$ and implements the application clause of [bracket abstraction](bracket-abstraction.md).
