# Cannibal phase of a decoupled sector

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal decoupling in cosmology](thermal-decoupling-in-cosmology.md)

A massive decoupled sector enters a cannibal phase when its particles are nonrelativistic but rapid number-changing reactions maintain [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) and [zero chemical potential from number-changing reactions](zero-chemical-potential-from-number-changing-reactions.md). Rest-mass energy released by $3\to2$ reactions heats the remaining particles, so the sector cools much more slowly than an isolated number-conserving nonrelativistic gas. The temperature laws follow directly from [cosmological entropy conservation](cosmological-entropy-conservation.md). An example is discussed in [Cannibal Dark Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.04219).
