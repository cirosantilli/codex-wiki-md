# Ringed space

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ringed_space)

A ringed space $(X,\mathcal O_X)$ is a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$ equipped with a [sheaf of rings](sheaf-of-rings.md) $\mathcal O_X$. A morphism consists of a continuous map $f:X\to Y$ and a compatible morphism $f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y\to\mathcal O_X$.
