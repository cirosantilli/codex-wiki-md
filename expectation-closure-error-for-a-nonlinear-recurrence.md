# Expectation closure error for a nonlinear recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Law of total expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md)

A bound $\mathbb E[B_{j+1}\mid B_j]\leq f(B_j)$ implies $\mathbb E B_{j+1}\leq\mathbb E f(B_j)$. It does not by itself imply $\mathbb E B_{j+1}\leq f(\mathbb E B_j)$. For $f(b)=b^2/n$, [Jensen's inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) goes in the opposite direction. The missing contribution is the [variance](variance-split.md): $\mathbb E[B_j^2]=(\mathbb E B_j)^2+\operatorname{Var}(B_j)$.
