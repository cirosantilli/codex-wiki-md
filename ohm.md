# Ohm

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohm)

The [ohm](ohm.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md), one [volt](volt.md) per [ampere](ampere.md). A component of [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $2\,\Omega$ needs an [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) of $2\,\mathrm V$ to carry [electric current](electric-current.md) $1\,\mathrm A$ under [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md). [Ling, Sanny and Moebs, §9.3: the ohm](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/9-3-resistivity-and-resistance).
