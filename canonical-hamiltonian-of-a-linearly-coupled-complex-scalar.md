# Canonical Hamiltonian of a linearly coupled complex scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Linear-in-potential charged-scalar Lagrangian](linear-in-potential-charged-scalar-lagrangian.md)

For the [linear-in-potential charged-scalar Lagrangian](linear-in-potential-charged-scalar-lagrangian.md), $\pi=\dot\phi^*-ieA_0\phi^*$ and $\pi^*=\dot\phi+ieA_0\phi$. With $A_\mu=(A_0,-\mathbf A)$, the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) density is

$$
\mathcal H=(\pi+ieA_0\phi^*)(\pi^*-ieA_0\phi)
+|\boldsymbol\nabla\phi|^2+m^2|\phi|^2
-ie\mathbf A\cdot(\phi\boldsymbol\nabla\phi^*-\phi^*\boldsymbol\nabla\phi).
$$

The first product includes $+e^2A_0^2|\phi|^2$ after the [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md); that term can arise in [canonical variables](canonical-variables.md) even though the original [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) has no interaction quadratic in $A$.
