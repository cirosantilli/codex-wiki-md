# Variance of a treatment contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Treatment contrast](treatment-contrast.md)

For a vector of estimated treatment means $\widehat\mu$, the [variance of a treatment contrast](variance-of-a-treatment-contrast.md) with coefficient vector $c$ is $c^T\operatorname{Cov}(\widehat\mu)c$. Averaging subsamples from a common [experimental unit](experimental-unit.md) does not make their shared [variance component](variance-component.md) disappear.
