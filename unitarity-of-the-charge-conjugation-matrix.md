# Unitarity of the charge-conjugation matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation of a Dirac field](charge-conjugation-of-a-dirac-field.md)

For $\psi^C=\eta_C C\bar\psi^T$ and $\gamma^{0T}=\gamma^0=-C\gamma^0C^{-1}$, the [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md) transforms as $\bar\psi^C=-\eta_C^*\psi^TC^\dagger$. Anticommuting the components of the [fermion bilinear](fermion-bilinear.md) gives $(\bar\psi\psi)^C=\bar\psi(C^\dagger C)^T\psi$. Invariance of the scalar bilinear therefore fixes $C^\dagger C=I$.
