# Scott isomorphism theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Countable infinitary logic](countable-infinitary-logic.md)

Every countable structure in a countable [first-order language](first-order-language.md) has a [Scott sentence](scott-sentence.md). Hence countable structures satisfying the same sentences of [countable infinitary logic](countable-infinitary-logic.md) are [isomorphic](isomorphism.md). The proof uses [Scott formulas](scott-formula.md), stabilization on the countable set of finite [tuples](finite-tuple.md), and the [back-and-forth method](back-and-forth-method.md).
