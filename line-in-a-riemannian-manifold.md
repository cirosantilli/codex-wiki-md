# Line in a Riemannian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)

A line is a unit-speed geodesic $\gamma:\mathbb R\to M$ that minimizes between every two of its points: $d_g(\gamma(s),\gamma(t))=|s-t|$.

This is a globally [minimizing geodesic](minimizing-geodesic.md), a Riemannian specialization of the general geometric line notion.
