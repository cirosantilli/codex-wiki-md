# Nonreal complex number

↑ **Parent:** [Complex number](complex-number.md)

A [nonreal complex number](nonreal-complex-number.md) is a [complex number](complex-number.md) with nonzero imaginary part. Being nonreal is a property of one number; forming a [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) pair additionally specifies the relation between two numbers.
