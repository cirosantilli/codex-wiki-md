# Flattened bispectrum configuration

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md)

A flattened [primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md) configuration lies at the collinear boundary of the allowed momentum-triangle domain. For a [cubic time-derivative curvature bispectrum](cubic-time-derivative-curvature-bispectrum.md), it is finite rather than singular. Its equal-short-side value $k_1=k_2=k_3/2$ gives $k_1k_2k_3/(k_1+k_2+k_3)^3=1/32$, compared with $1/27$ at an [equilateral bispectrum configuration](equilateral-bispectrum-configuration.md).
