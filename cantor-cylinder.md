# Cantor cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Cantor set](cantor-set.md)

A Cantor cylinder fixes finitely many of the zero-or-two ternary digits in the standard [Cantor set](cantor-set.md). These sets form finite [clopen](clopen-set.md) partitions of diameter at most $3^{-n}$ at level $n$, and a base for the relative topology. They permit uniform approximation of continuous functions by locally constant functions.
