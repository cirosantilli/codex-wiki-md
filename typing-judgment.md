# Typing judgment

↑ **Parent:** [Type system](type-system.md)

A [typing judgment](typing-judgment.md) asserts that expression $e$ has type $\tau$ under a [typing context](typing-context.md) $\Gamma$. A rule displays prerequisite [typing judgments](typing-judgment.md) above a line and their conclusion below it. Repeatedly applying rules gives a derivation; the addition example under [type system](type-system.md) uses two assumptions and one addition rule.
