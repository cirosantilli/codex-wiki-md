# Full faithfulness from counit coequalizers

↑ **Parent:** [Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor](eilenberg-moore-comparison-functor.md)

If each standard counit presentation is a [coequalizer](coequalizer.md), the [Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor](eilenberg-moore-comparison-functor.md) is [full and faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md). An [monad algebra morphism](morphism-of-algebras-for-a-monad.md) $\alpha:GB\to GC$ makes $\varepsilon_CF\alpha$ equalize $FG\varepsilon_B$ and $\varepsilon_{FGB}$; its unique descent is the required [morphism](morphism.md) $B\to C$. The [triangle identities for an adjunction](triangle-identities-for-an-adjunction.md) make $G\varepsilon_B$ a [split epimorphism](split-epimorphism.md), so descent has underlying arrow exactly $\alpha$. Epimorphic cancellation at $\varepsilon_B$ proves faithfulness.
