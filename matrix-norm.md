# Matrix norm

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_norm)

A matrix norm is a [norm](norm.md) on a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of [matrices](matrix.md); common choices include induced [operator norms](operator-norm.md) and the [Frobenius norm](frobenius-norm.md). A submultiplicative matrix norm additionally satisfies $\|AB\|\leq\|A\|\|B\|$. The [matrix 2-norm](matrix-2-norm.md) is the norm induced by [Euclidean norms](euclidean-norm.md) and equals the largest [singular value](singular-value.md).
