# Stellar composition profile

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

A stellar composition profile gives chemical mass fractions against radius or [enclosed mass](enclosed-mass.md). Flat portions can indicate mixing in a [convective core](convective-core.md) or [convective envelope](convective-envelope.md), while burning shells and moving convective boundaries leave gradients and discontinuities.
