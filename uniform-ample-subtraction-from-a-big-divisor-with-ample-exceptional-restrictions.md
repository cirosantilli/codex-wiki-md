# Uniform ample subtraction from a big divisor with ample exceptional restrictions

↑ **Parent:** [Negative curves of a big real divisor lie in finitely many divisors](negative-curves-of-a-big-real-divisor-lie-in-finitely-many-divisors.md)

If a big class $D=B+E$ has [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) restriction to every component $E_i$ of the effective support, then for any [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) $A$ a sufficiently small $\epsilon>0$ makes $D-\epsilon A$ [nef](nef-line-bundle.md). Use openness of the [ample cone](ample-cone.md) to keep $B-\epsilon A$ and all $(D-\epsilon A)|_{E_i}$ [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) with one finite minimum of bounds. Curves inside the support use these restrictions; curves outside use effectivity of $E$. Thus one does not need a uniform choice over infinitely many [subvarieties](closed-subvariety.md).
