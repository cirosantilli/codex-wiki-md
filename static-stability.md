# Static stability

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

Resistance of a stratified fluid to a small vertical parcel displacement. For dry homogeneous air, $N^2=(g/\theta)d\theta/dz$ in terms of [potential temperature](potential-temperature.md); $N^2>0$ gives a restoring [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) force. Moisture and composition gradients modify the parcel comparison. Across an ideal sharp interface, a denser lower fluid is stable to an infinitesimal interchange.
