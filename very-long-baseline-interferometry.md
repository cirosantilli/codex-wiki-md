# Very-long-baseline interferometry

↑ **Parent:** [Radio interferometry](radio-interferometry.md)

[very-long-baseline interferometry](very-long-baseline-interferometry.md) correlates accurately time-referenced radio signals recorded at widely separated collectors, giving [angular resolution](angular-resolution.md) of order [wavelength](wavelength.md) divided by projected baseline. Geometric delays, [frequency](frequency.md) standards, atmospheric propagation and source structure enter the [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md). Compact [maser](maser-split.md) positions and motions are one application.
