# Reducing subspace of a Hilbert-space operator

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md)

A [closed](closed-set.md) subspace of a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) reduces a [bounded operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) when both the operator and its adjoint leave the subspace invariant. Equivalently, the [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) projection onto it commutes with the operator: the adjoint invariance makes its [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) invariant too. For a star-closed algebra of operators, the [closed](closed-set.md) span of the orbit of any [vector](vector.md) is reducing for the whole algebra. This enables an [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) direct-sum decomposition into cyclic actions when constructing [Borel functional calculus for a normal operator](borel-functional-calculus-for-a-normal-operator.md).
