# Smoluchowski association rate

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion and capture](diffusion-and-capture.md)

For an absorbing spherical target of radius $a$ in an infinite uniform reservoir, steady [diffusion](diffusion.md) gives $c(r)=c_\infty(1-a/r)$. Integrating the inward flux yields $k=4\pi D_{\rm rel}a$, where $D_{\rm rel}=D_A+D_B$ if both partners diffuse. Here $k$ multiplies [number densities](number-density.md); its units are volume per time. Molecular orientation, finite-domain transients and imperfect capture require corrections.
