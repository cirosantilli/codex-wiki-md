# Constrained Gibbs minimization for chemical equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Thermochemical equilibrium](thermochemical-equilibrium.md)

At fixed [temperature](temperature.md) and [pressure](pressure.md), a closed reacting system reaches [thermochemical equilibrium](thermochemical-equilibrium.md) by minimizing [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) over nonnegative species amounts subject to conserved elemental abundances. For an allowed [stoichiometric vector](stoichiometric-vector.md) $\nu$, an interior minimum has $\sum_i\nu_i\mu_i=0$. More generally the [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) vector lies in the row span of the elemental conservation matrix at positive concentrations, with the appropriate one-sided inequalities for absent species. The [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) gives the thermodynamic differential, while the [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics.md) supplies the minimization direction.
