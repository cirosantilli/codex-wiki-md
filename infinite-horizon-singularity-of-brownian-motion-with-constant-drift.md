# Infinite-horizon singularity of Brownian motion with constant drift

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion with drift](brownian-motion-with-drift.md)

The laws on continuous paths of a standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) and a [Brownian motion with drift](brownian-motion-with-drift.md) $\mu\ne0$ are [mutually singular measures](mutually-singular-measures.md) on the whole half-line. The event $\lim_{t\to\infty}\omega(t)/t=0$ has full measure for the former and zero measure for the latter by the [strong law for Brownian motion](strong-law-for-brownian-motion.md). On every fixed finite horizon, their laws are equivalent by the [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md). The finite-horizon densities $e^{\mu B_t-\mu^2t/2}$ tend to zero [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) while retaining [expectation](expected-value.md) one, so they lack [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md) over all time.
