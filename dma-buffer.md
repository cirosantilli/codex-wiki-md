# DMA buffer

↑ **Parent:** [Direct memory access](direct-memory-access.md)

A region of [computer memory](computer-memory.md) prepared for [DMA](direct-memory-access.md). In the four-[byte](byte.md) receive example it is the payload destination, and must remain available until the transfer and required [DMA buffer synchronization](dma-buffer-synchronization.md) are complete.
