# Stable bundle of an Anosov flow

↑ **Parent:** [Anosov flow](anosov-flow.md)

The stable bundle consists of the transverse directions exponentially contracted in positive time. The unstable bundle has the corresponding negative-time property. A [positive time change of a smooth flow](positive-time-change-of-a-smooth-flow.md) can tilt these bundles in the flow direction, even though it preserves the underlying orbits.
