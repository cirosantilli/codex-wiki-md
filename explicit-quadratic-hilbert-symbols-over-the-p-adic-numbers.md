# Explicit quadratic Hilbert symbols over the p-adic numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic Hilbert symbol](quadratic-hilbert-symbol.md)

For odd $p$ and [p-adic units](p-adic-unit.md) $u,v$, the [quadratic Hilbert symbol](quadratic-hilbert-symbol.md) is

$$
(p^\alpha u,p^\beta v)_p=(-1)^{\alpha\beta(p-1)/2}\left(\frac up\right)^\beta\left(\frac vp\right)^\alpha.
$$

The [Legendre symbols](legendre-symbol.md) record the two unit [square classes](square-class.md). For $p=2$, put $\epsilon(u)=(u-1)/2\bmod2$ and $\omega(u)=(u^2-1)/8\bmod2$. Then

$$
(2^\alpha u,2^\beta v)_2=(-1)^{\epsilon(u)\epsilon(v)+\alpha\omega(v)+\beta\omega(u)}.
$$

The basis $-1,2,5$ of the [square-class group of the 2-adic numbers](square-class-group-of-the-2-adic-numbers.md) gives this formula: its only negative basis pairings are $(-1,-1)_2$ and $(2,5)_2=(5,2)_2$. The first is obstructed by sums of squares modulo four, the second by $x^2-2y^2$ modulo eight; the remaining pairings have explicit [field norm](field-norm.md) representatives.
