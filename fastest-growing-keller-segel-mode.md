<h1 id="fastest-growing-keller-segel-mode">Fastest-growing Keller--Segel mode</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Keller--Segel aggregation threshold](keller-segel-aggregation-threshold.md)

For the two-field [Keller--Segel model](keller-segel-model.md), put $a=D_2$, $b=D_\rho$, $C=D_1f_0\geq0$, $c=\kappa-a_0f'_0$. The upper growth rate is $s_+(z)=-[c+(a+b)z]/2+\sqrt{[c+(b-a)z]^2+4Cz}/2$. When $C>\max(ac,-bc)$ its unique maximum occurs at positive $z_*$ satisfying $s_+'(z_*)=0$. For equal diffusion coefficients $a=b$, this gives $z_*=(C^2/a^2-c^2)/(4C)$. For $c<0$ and $C\leq-bc$, the fastest growth is homogeneous, with infinite [wavelength](wavelength.md). The finite-domain answer must maximize the growth rate over the permitted [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md). Eliminating chemical dynamics instantaneously generally changes this selected scale.
