# Genome

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genome)

A [genome](genome.md) is the complete set of genetic information under discussion. For a cellular [organism](organism.md) it is encoded in [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), including [genes](gene.md) and other sequences. Specify whether a comparison concerns a nuclear [genome](genome.md), a [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md)'s [genome](genome.md) or all cellular genetic material: the [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) does not contain every [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence in a typical [eukaryote](eukaryote.md). See [NHGRI: genome](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Genome).
