# Linear congruence from a discrete-logarithm Fourier sample

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete-logarithm Fourier sampling](discrete-logarithm-fourier-sampling.md)

Let $d=\gcd(c_1,M)$. A supported sample guarantees $d\mid c_2$. Dividing its [linear congruence](linear-congruence.md) by $d$ gives an invertible coefficient modulo $M/d$ and a unique residue $y_0$ there. The $d$ compatible [discrete logarithms](discrete-logarithm-problem.md) modulo $M$ are $y_0+jM/d$ for $0\leq j<d$. Only $d=1$ determines the [discrete logarithm](discrete-logarithm-problem.md) from the sample alone; $(c_1,c_2)=(0,0)$ conveys no information for $M>1$.
