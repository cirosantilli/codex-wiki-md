# Muon antineutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Antineutrino](antineutrino.md)

The [antineutrino](antineutrino.md) associated with the [muon](muon.md) flavour of the [weak charged current](charged-current.md) has zero [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and [lepton number](lepton-number.md) $-1$ in the usual production-and-detection description. A negative [muon](muon.md) decays to an [electron](electron.md), an [electron antineutrino](electron-antineutrino.md) and a [muon neutrino](muon-neutrino.md); the charge-conjugate process instead produces the muon antineutrino.
