# Rational model of a collapsed complex projective subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md)

The quotient $\mathbb{CP}^{5}/\mathbb{CP}^{2}$ is five-connected and has [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) only in degrees $0,6,8,10$, with positive products zero. Its [minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md) therefore starts with closed $a_6,b_8,c_{10}$; no decomposable differential can occur below degree twelve. Sending these three generators to the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) [basis](basis.md) and all later generators to zero is a [chain map](chain-map.md) and a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md). Thus its full [minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md) is the same as that of $S^6\vee S^8\vee S^{10}$. It starts with generators $u_{11},v_{13},w_{15},t_{15}$ satisfying $du=a^2,dv=ab,dw=ac,dt=b^2$, followed by generators killing remaining products and syzygies.
