# Core disk of a handle

↑ **Parent:** [Handle attachment](handle-attachment.md)

The core disk of a $k$-[handle attachment](handle-attachment.md) $D^k\times D^{n-k}$ is $D^k\times\{0\}$. Together with its cap in the preceding stage, it often represents a [homology class](homology-class.md); for a [surgery trace](surgery-trace.md), capped two-handle cores give the basis of its [surgery linking matrix](surgery-linking-matrix.md).
