# Weil pairing for an abelian variety

↑ **Parent:** [Dual abelian variety](dual-abelian-variety.md)

For an [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md) and an integer $N$ invertible in the base [field](field.md), a torsion [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $M\in\widehat A[N]$ has trivial pullback by $[N]$. Choose a trivialization. The descent action of the kernel $A[N]$ on that trivial [line bundle](line-bundle.md) acts through a character $\chi_M:A[N]\to\mu_N$. Define $e_N(P,M)=\chi_M(P)$. This is independent of rescaling the trivialization and bilinear under translation and [tensor product](tensor-product.md). A trivial character descends the trivial bundle, so $M$ is trivial. Since both groups have order $N^{2\dim A}$, the character map is an isomorphism and the [Weil pairing for an abelian variety](weil-pairing-for-an-abelian-variety.md) is perfect. The [theta autoduality of a Jacobian](theta-autoduality-of-a-jacobian.md) turns it into a pairing on two copies of the [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md)'s torsion group.
