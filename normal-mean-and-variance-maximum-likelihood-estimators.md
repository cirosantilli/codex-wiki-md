# Normal mean and variance maximum-likelihood estimators

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md)

For [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $X_1,\ldots,X_n\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$, the [maximum-likelihood estimators](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) are

$$
\widehat\mu=\overline X,
\qquad
\widehat{\sigma}^2=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\overline X)^2.
$$

The first is [unbiased](unbiased-estimator.md), while $\mathbb E\widehat{\sigma}^2=(n-1)\sigma^2/n$.
