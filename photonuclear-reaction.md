# Photonuclear reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md)

A nuclear reaction initiated by absorption or scattering of a [photon](photon.md). Such channels may contribute to attenuation at photon energies above their relevant nuclear thresholds; they are distinct from atomic [photoelectric absorption](photoelectric-absorption.md) and electron–positron [pair production](pair-production.md).
