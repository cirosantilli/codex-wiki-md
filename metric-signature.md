# Metric signature

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentzian manifold](lorentzian-manifold.md)

The [metric signature](metric-signature.md) records the signs of the diagonalized nondegenerate [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md), equivalently its numbers of negative and positive directions. For a four-dimensional [Lorentzian manifold](lorentzian-manifold.md), both $(-,+,+,+)$ and its overall negative convention are common. The numerical signs of contractions and component [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) identities must be consistent with that choice and the [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
