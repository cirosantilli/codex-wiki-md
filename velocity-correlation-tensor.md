# Velocity correlation tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md)

The [velocity correlation tensor](velocity-correlation-tensor.md) is $Q_{ij}(\mathbf r)=\langle u_i(\mathbf x)u_j(\mathbf x+\mathbf r)\rangle$. For [isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md) it splits into longitudinal and transverse parts. Incompressibility constrains them, while the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) gives the [turbulent energy spectrum](turbulent-energy-spectrum.md). Its trace is the scalar velocity-dot-product correlation.
