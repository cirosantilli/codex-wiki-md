# Symplectic reduction of an isotropic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic reduction](symplectic-reduction.md)

For $H=\sum_j(q_j^2+p_j^2)/2$ and $E>0$, the oscillator flow is a free circle action on the sphere of radius $\sqrt{2E}$. Its quotient is [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md). For two modes, the reduced form in $w=z_2/z_1$ is $iE\,dw\wedge d\bar w/(1+|w|^2)^2$, of total area $2\pi E$. Reduction at zero energy is singular instead of a smooth quotient of the asserted dimension.
