# Extended Hales-Jewett theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorial subspace](combinatorial-subspace.md)

For every alphabet size $m$, number of colors $k$, and dimension $d$, some $n$ has the property that every $k$-coloring of $[m]^n$ contains a monochromatic $d$-dimensional [combinatorial subspace](combinatorial-subspace.md). It follows from the [Hales-Jewett theorem](hales-jewett-theorem.md) by identifying $[m]^{dN}$ with the word space over the alphabet $[m]^d$.
