# Carpel

↑ **Parent:** [Flower](flower.md)

A [flower](flower.md) may have several [carpels](carpel.md), including fused ones.[Biology 2e, §32.1 Flower Structure: individual carpel and one or multiple carpels](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)
