# Cut lemma for minimum spanning trees

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md)

Let an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) set $F$ be contained in some [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md), and let a [cut of a graph](cut-graph-theory.md) have no crossing [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) in $F$. Any least-weight [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) crossing that [cut of a graph](cut-graph-theory.md) can be added to $F$ while preserving its containment in some [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md). Insert the chosen [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) into an optimum containing $F$, then remove another crossing [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) from the resulting [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md); it is no lighter and cannot belong to $F$. Ties imply existence of an optimum containing the chosen [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md), rather than membership in every optimum.
