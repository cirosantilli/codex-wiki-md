# One-body density matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix-split.md)

The [one-body density matrix](one-body-density-matrix.md) contains first-order spatial coherence and mode occupations of a many-particle state. Its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is the particle number in the convention displayed. A macroscopic [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) identifies [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md); in a homogeneous condensate the long-distance [matrix](matrix.md) approaches a nonzero constant. A finite density or local coherence alone does not imply this long-distance limit.
