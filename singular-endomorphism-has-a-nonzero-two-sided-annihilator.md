# Singular endomorphism has a nonzero two-sided annihilator

↑ **Parent:** [Endomorphism](endomorphism.md)

Let $T$ be a singular [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) of a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md). Choose $0\ne u\in\ker T$ and a nonzero [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $\lambda$ vanishing on $\operatorname{im}T$, possible because that [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) is proper. Then $S(x)=\lambda(x)u$ is a nonzero rank-one [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) with $TS=ST=0$.
