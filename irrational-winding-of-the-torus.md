# Irrational winding of the torus

↑ **Parent:** [Immersed submanifold](immersed-submanifold.md)

For irrational $\alpha$, the map

$$
\mathbb R\longrightarrow S^1\times S^1,
\qquad t\longmapsto(e^{it},e^{i\alpha t})
$$

is an injective [immersion](immersion.md) with dense image. It is not a [smooth embedding](smooth-embedding.md), since points with arbitrarily large parameter return arbitrarily close to its initial point in the subspace topology.
