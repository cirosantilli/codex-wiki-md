# Cornish-Fisher expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Edgeworth series](edgeworth-series.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornish-Fisher_expansion)

The Cornish-Fisher expansion inverts an [Edgeworth expansion](edgeworth-series.md) to approximate [quantiles](quantile-function.md). For a standardized iid sum, let $\gamma_1$ be the [skewness](skewness.md) of one summand and $z_\alpha$ the [standard normal quantile](standard-normal-quantile.md). The first [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) correction is $-\gamma_1(z^2-1)\phi(z)/(6\sqrt n)$. Substituting $y_\alpha=z_\alpha+c/\sqrt n$ and expanding $\Phi$ gives $c=\gamma_1(z_\alpha^2-1)/6$. This is a fixed-quantile asymptotic, with $\alpha$ fixed in $(0,1)$.
