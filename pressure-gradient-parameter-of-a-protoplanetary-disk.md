# Pressure-gradient parameter of a protoplanetary disk

↑ **Parent:** [Sub-Keplerian rotation](sub-keplerian-rotation.md)

A convention for the small fractional reduction of azimuthal gas speed due to radial [pressure](pressure-split.md) support. With $c_s^2=P_g/\rho_g$, radial balance gives $v_{g\phi}^2=v_K^2(1-2\eta)$ and hence $v_{g\phi}\simeq v_K(1-\eta)$. Other authors absorb the factor of two into the definition; the convention must accompany a drift formula.
