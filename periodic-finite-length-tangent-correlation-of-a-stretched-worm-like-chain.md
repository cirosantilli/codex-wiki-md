# Periodic finite-length tangent correlation of a stretched worm-like chain

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse tangent correlation of a stretched worm-like chain](transverse-tangent-correlation-of-a-stretched-worm-like-chain.md)

Periodizing the bulk [transverse tangent correlation of a stretched worm-like chain](transverse-tangent-correlation-of-a-stretched-worm-like-chain.md) gives $C_L(r)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}C(r+jL)$, where $d_L(r)$ is the distance to zero on the periodic interval. Summing the geometric series yields the displayed result. At zero separation its finite-length factor is $\coth(L/(2\xi))$; for $L\gg\xi$ the bulk result is recovered. Periodic tangent fluctuations are a mathematical boundary convention, not a claim that a pulled physical chain forms a closed loop.
