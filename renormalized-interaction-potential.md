# Renormalized interaction potential

↑ **Parent:** [Interaction potential](interaction-potential.md)

For a twice continuously differentiable [interaction potential](interaction-potential.md) with $\|D^2W\|\leq L$ and a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) with finite first [absolute moment](absolute-moment.md), this subtraction produces a finite potential even if $W*\mu$ is infinite. Taylor's formula and linear growth of the [gradient](gradient.md) give

$$
|W(x-z)-W(-z)|\leq\bigl(|\nabla W(0)|+L|z|\bigr)|x|+\frac L2|x|^2.
$$

Dominated [differentiation under the integral sign](differentiation-under-the-integral-sign.md) gives $\nabla V_{\mathrm{ren}}=(\nabla W)*\mu$ and $D^2V_{\mathrm{ren}}=(D^2W)*\mu$. If the unrenormalized [convolution](convolution.md) is finite, the two potentials differ only by a constant, so they generate the same [velocity field](velocity-field.md) when that field depends on their [gradient](gradient.md).
