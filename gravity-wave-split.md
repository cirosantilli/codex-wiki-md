# Gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_wave)

A gravity wave is a fluid disturbance restored by gravity or [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). A [surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md) displaces a [free surface](free-surface.md), while an [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) displaces density surfaces within a stratified fluid. Rotation can additionally contribute to restoration, giving an [inertia-gravity wave](inertia-gravity-wave.md).
