# Type-II phase matching

↑ **Parent:** [Phase matching](phase-matching.md)

The daughter [photons](photon.md) occupy orthogonal ordinary and extraordinary polarization classes. Alternative assignments to two collected paths can interfere if timing, spectrum and spatial mode do not distinguish them. [spatial walk-off](spatial-walk-off.md) and unequal group delays often require compensation.
