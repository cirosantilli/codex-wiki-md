# Hochschild-Kostant-Rosenberg theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hochschild cohomology](hochschild-cohomology.md)

For the [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) $A=k[X_1,\ldots,X_r]$ in [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) zero, antisymmetrization identifies $HH^*(A,A)$ with $\bigwedge_A^*\operatorname{Der}_k(A)$. The map on a wedge of $p$ [derivations](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) is $\frac1{p!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_p}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\prod_jD_{\sigma(j)}(a_j)$. The [Hochschild cup product](hochschild-cup-product.md) becomes the [exterior product](exterior-product.md), and the left [Gerstenhaber bracket](gerstenhaber-bracket.md) becomes the left [Schouten-Nijenhuis bracket](schouten-nijenhuis-bracket.md). This assertion requires a smoothness hypothesis when generalized beyond [polynomial rings](polynomial-ring.md).
