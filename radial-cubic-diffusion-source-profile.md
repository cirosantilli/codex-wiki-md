# Radial cubic-diffusion source profile

↑ **Parent:** [Barenblatt solution](barenblatt-solution.md)

For the planar [porous medium equation](porous-medium-equation.md) $n_t=[D_0/(3n_0^2)]\Delta(n^3)$, the mass-$Q$ point-source [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) is $n=n_0\lambda^{-2}\sqrt{[1-r^2/(r_0^2\lambda^2)]_+}$, with $\lambda=(6D_0t/r_0^2)^{1/6}$ and $r_0^2=3Q/(2\pi n_0)$. Its front grows as $t^{1/6}$ and its central density falls as $t^{-1/3}$. The [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) integral uses the planar area element $2\pi r\,dr$. Although the density slope diverges at the front, its diffusion flux vanishes there, giving a compactly supported [weak solution](weak-solution.md) with initial measure $Q\delta^{(2)}$.
