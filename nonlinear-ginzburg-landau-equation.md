# Nonlinear Ginzburg-Landau equation

↑ **Parent:** [Complex Ginzburg–Landau equation](complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md)

This nondispersive cubic [complex Ginzburg–Landau equation](complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) balances linear growth against nonlinear damping. Its real-wavenumber [Ginzburg-Landau plane wave](ginzburg-landau-plane-wave.md) has amplitude $\sqrt{\mu-k^2}$ when $\mu>k^2$. Relaxation within that single-mode amplitude family does not by itself establish stability against general perturbations.
