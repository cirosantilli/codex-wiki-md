# Ion pair

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic bonding](ionic-bonding.md)

An [ion pair](ion-pair.md) is an associated [cation](cation.md) and [anion](anion.md). An isolated [ion pair](ion-pair.md) lacks the extended neighbourhood of an [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md). In a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), [ion pairs](ion-pair.md) can coexist with more widely separated [ions](ion.md); their abundance depends on conditions. A neutral gas-phase [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) [molecule](molecule.md) can be treated as a discrete entity, with its own [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md) and dissociation products. That use of [molecule](molecule.md) does not describe the constituents of ordinary solid [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md).[§8–3, distinction between vaporized NaCl pairs and separated gaseous ions](the-feynman-lectures-on-physics-volume-ii-by-richard-feynman-and-others.md)[Conductivity of Solutions by Harvard Natural Sciences Lecture Demonstrations](conductivity-of-solutions-by-harvard-natural-sciences-lecture-demonstrations.md)
