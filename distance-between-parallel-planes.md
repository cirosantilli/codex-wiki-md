# Distance between parallel planes

↑ **Parent:** [Plane](plane.md)

Planes with common unit normal $n$ have distance $|(a_1-a_2)\cdot n|$. Every joining displacement has this normal component, giving a lower bound by its absolute value; a displacement parallel to the normal attains the bound.

For a common nonzero [normal vector](normal-vector.md) $\mathbf n$, not necessarily of unit [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md), the [planes](plane.md) $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf x=d_1$ and $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf x=d_2$ have distance $|d_2-d_1|/\|\mathbf n\|$. Thus $2x-y+2z=3$ and $2x-y+2z=9$ are distance $6/3=2$ apart. First put both [plane equations](cartesian-equation-of-a-plane.md) into the same normal-vector scale: subtracting offsets from independently rescaled equations need not give a geometric distance.
