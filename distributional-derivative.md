# Distributional derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution theory](distribution-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributional_derivative)

For a distribution $u$ and a [multi-index](multi-index-notation.md) $\alpha$, its distributional derivative is defined by

$$
\langle D^\alpha u,\varphi\rangle=(-1)^{|\alpha|}\langle u,D^\alpha\varphi\rangle.
$$
