# Fourier decay from a derivative jump

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md)

For a piecewise smooth decaying function, a jump in its $j$th derivative at $a$ contributes the displayed algebraic term to its high-frequency [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md). Distributionally, $D^{j+1}f$ contains $[f^{(j)}]_a\delta(x-a)$, and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) or the derivative-transform identity supplies the factor $(ik)^{-j-1}$. Adequate integrability of the subsequent derivatives controls the remainder. Smooth localized polynomial terms give no algebraic cusp contribution.
