# Lorentz reality condition on chiral generators

↑ **Parent:** [Chiral decomposition of the complex Lorentz algebra](chiral-decomposition-of-the-complex-lorentz-algebra.md)

For Hermitian rotations $J_i$ and boosts $K_i$, the complex combinations $A_i=(J_i+iK_i)/2$ and $B_i=(J_i-iK_i)/2$ are adjoints of one another. Although their complex brackets split into two commuting $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ factors, they are not two independent Hermitian compact rotation algebras. The real Lorentz [spin](spin.md) group is $SL(2,\mathbb C)$; $SU(2)\times SU(2)$ is a different compact real form.
