# Transmission disequilibrium test

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic association](genetic-association.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transmission_disequilibrium_test)

For affected offspring and heterozygous parents at a diallelic locus, let $b$ count transmissions of allele 1 with allele 2 untransmitted and $c$ count the reverse. Under the fair-transmission null, conditional on $b+c$, $b$ is binomial with probability $1/2$. The asymptotic statistic is $(b-c)^2/(b+c)$, with a one-degree-of-freedom chi-squared null distribution. Homozygous-parent transmissions give no contrast. Conditioning within families protects this test from ordinary [population stratification](population-stratification.md).
