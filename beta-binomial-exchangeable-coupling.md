# Beta-binomial exchangeable coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Beta-binomial conjugacy](beta-binomial-conjugacy.md)

Draw $P\sim\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\beta)$, $X\mid P\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,P)$ and $Q\mid X\sim\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha+X,\beta+n-X)$, with $\alpha,\beta>0$ and integer $n\ge0$. The joint density of $(P,X,Q)$ is symmetric in $P,Q$, making them [exchangeable random variables](exchangeable-random-variables.md) with identical [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md). The posterior beta [normalization constant](normalizing-constant.md) depends on $X$ and must be retained. The [correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) is $n/(n+\alpha+\beta)$: this constructs tunable dependence without altering either marginal.
