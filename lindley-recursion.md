# Lindley recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Workload of a queue](workload-of-a-queue.md)

For arrivals added before the slot's offered service, $Z_k$ is the arriving work minus that service. With independent identically distributed increments of negative mean, the stationary [queue workload](workload-of-a-queue.md) has the law $\sup_{m\geq0}\sum_{j=1}^mZ_j$. Iterating the recursion from the past gives this [supremum](supremum.md); the strong law makes it finite. A different arrival/service ordering changes slot-boundary workload but requires its convention to be stated.
