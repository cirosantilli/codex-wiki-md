# Gravity-driven spreading of a planar viscous drop

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

A wide, shallow drop with cross-sectional area $A$ has the gravity-driven [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $q=-gh^3h_x/(3\nu)$ when substrate no-slip and zero surface shear are imposed. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) yields a [porous medium equation](porous-medium-equation.md), $h_t=[g/(3\nu)](h^3h_x)_x$. Its symmetric source-type [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) has half-width $\ell$ in the display and height $h=[9\nu(\ell^2-x^2)/(10gt)]_+^{1/3}$, where $I=\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^{5/3}\theta\,d\theta$. Integrating the profile recovers $A$. The contact-edge slope diverges, so the bulk [lubrication approximation](lubrication-theory.md) requires an inner edge regularization if contact-line microphysics or [surface tension](surface-tension.md) is retained.
