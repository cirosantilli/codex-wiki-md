# Rock physics

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

The study of how rock composition, structure, pressure, [temperature](temperature-split.md), fluids and deformation determine measurable physical properties such as [density](density.md), elastic wave speeds, electrical conductivity and attenuation. These relations are needed to interpret [seismic tomography](seismic-tomography.md) and [gravity anomalies](gravity-anomaly.md); one measured property rarely determines a unique material state.
