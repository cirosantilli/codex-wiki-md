# Good-solvent size calibration for polystyrene

↑ **Parent:** [Polystyrene](polystyrene.md)

The [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md) of a dilute linear [polystyrene](polystyrene.md) coil in a [good solvent](good-solvent.md) depends on its [molar mass](molar-mass.md) as $R_g=KM^\nu$, with a material- and solvent-dependent amplitude. A manufacturer-supplied light-scattering calibration for [polystyrene](polystyrene.md) in [benzene](benzene.md) or toluene near room temperature is the displayed relation, quoted over $M\simeq2\times10^5$ to $5\times10^7\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. The exponent is close to the [Flory approximation](flory-approximation.md) $3/5$. Values outside the quoted mass range are extrapolations, not extra measured data. Using $c^*=M/[N_A(4\pi/3)R_g^3]$ then gives the [polymer overlap concentration](polymer-overlap-concentration.md) scaling $c^*\propto M^{1-3\nu}$. [Brookhaven Instruments, How to Use ParSEC, Appendix C, PDF page 47: polystyrene good-solvent radius-of-gyration calibration and its stated molar-mass range](https://www.brookhaveninstruments.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/ParSEC-Manual.pdf)
