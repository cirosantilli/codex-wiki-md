# Sunspot

↑ **Parent:** [Solar magnetic field](solar-magnetic-field.md)

A sunspot is a relatively cool, dark photospheric region associated with concentrated magnetic flux. The strong [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) inhibits convective heat transport, reducing the emergent local intensity. Spots commonly emerge in bipolar active regions, whose polarity and systematic tilt follow [Hale's polarity law](hale-s-polarity-law.md) and [Joy's law](joy-s-law.md). Their number and latitude distribution are visible tracers of the [solar cycle](solar-cycle.md).
