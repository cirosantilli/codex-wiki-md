# Protein binding site

↑ **Parent:** [Protein tertiary structure](protein-tertiary-structure.md)

A [protein binding site](protein-binding-site.md) is a region of a [protein structure](protein-structure.md) that interacts with a [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md). Its arrangement of [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) helps determine recognition; connecting loops supported by a [beta sheet](beta-sheet.md) can contribute to it. An [enzyme](enzyme.md)'s [active site](active-site.md) binds a [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) and accelerates a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md). See [Alberts et al.: protein binding sites and active sites](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26911/).
