# Rao-Blackwellization

↑ **Parent:** [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md)

Replace an estimator by its [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) given selected information. The mean is unchanged and the variance cannot increase, by the [law of total variance](law-of-total-variance.md). In simulation this produces [conditional Monte Carlo](conditional-monte-carlo.md) estimators. Sufficiency is needed for the classical inferential strengthening, but not for the elementary variance reduction identity.
