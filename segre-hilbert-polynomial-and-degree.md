# Segre Hilbert polynomial and degree

↑ **Parent:** [Segre embedding](segre-embedding.md)

For the [Segre embedding](segre-embedding.md) of $\mathbb P^n\times\mathbb P^m$, the [homogeneous coordinate ring](homogeneous-coordinate-ring.md) is the algebra generated by $x_i y_j$, assigning degree one to every generator. Its degree-$t$ piece consists of all [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) bihomogeneous of bidegree $(t,t)$: pair the $t$ factors of an $x$-monomial with the $t$ factors of a $y$-monomial to express any such [monomial](monomial.md) as a [product](product-of-numbers.md) of generators. Counting the independent [monomials](monomial.md) gives the displayed [Hilbert polynomial](hilbert-polynomial.md), agreeing with the [Hilbert function](hilbert-function.md) for every integer $t\geq0$. Its [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) degree is $n+m$, giving the [variety](algebraic-variety-split.md)'s [dimension](dimension-of-an-algebraic-set.md). Its [leading coefficient](leading-coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md) is $1/(n!m!)$, giving embedded projective degree $(n+m)!/(n!m!)=\binom{n+m}{n}$. The two uses of degree therefore describe different quantities.
