# Area expansion under uniaxial compression

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

For a thin sheet of initial face [area](surface-area.md) $A$, compressed normally by [force](force-split.md) $F$ between frictionless plates, each lateral [strain](strain.md) is $\nu F/(EA)$. To first order the [area](surface-area.md) of either broad face increases by $2\nu F/E$; total two-face [area](surface-area.md) changes twice as much.
