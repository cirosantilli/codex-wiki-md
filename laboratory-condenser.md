# Laboratory condenser

↑ **Parent:** [Distillation](distillation.md)

A [laboratory condenser](laboratory-condenser.md) removes [heat](heat-split.md) from [vapor](vapor.md) so that it becomes [liquid](liquid.md). In [distillation](distillation.md), the downstream [laboratory condenser](laboratory-condenser.md) makes collectable distillate. Complete condensation, without loss or reaction, preserves the entering [vapor](vapor.md)'s overall component [mole fractions](mole-fraction.md); cooling alone does not give another enrichment step. Partial condensation can instead leave two streams of different composition, requiring both [vapor-liquid equilibrium](vapor-liquid-equilibrium.md) and a [distillation material balance](distillation-material-balance.md).
