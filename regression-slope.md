# Regression slope

↑ **Parent:** [Regression coefficient](regression-coefficient.md)

In a [simple linear regression](simple-linear-regression.md) with [conditional mean](conditional-expectation.md) $\mathbb E[Y\mid X=x]=\alpha+\beta x$, the slope $\beta$ is the change in that mean per unit increase in the predictor. The [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md) estimate is $\widehat\beta=S_{xy}/S_{xx}$ when $S_{xx}>0$. It describes a [statistical association](statistical-association.md); a causal interpretation requires further assumptions.
