# Special Lagrangian submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md)

A Lagrangian submanifold in a Calabi–Yau geometry is special when the phase of its holomorphic volume form is constant. With orientation chosen so that the real part is positive, it is a [calibrated submanifold](calibrated-submanifold.md) for $\operatorname{Re}(e^{-i\theta}\Omega)$. In $\mathbb C^3$, its spatial three-fold can describe a supersymmetric curved [D3-brane](d3-brane.md).
