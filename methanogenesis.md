# Methanogenesis

↑ **Parent:** [Methane](methane.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methanogenesis)

Methanogenesis is biological production of [methane](methane.md) by anaerobic microorganisms. Waterlogged soil and lake sediments can favour it. Warming or thaw changes substrate availability, but production is distinct from atmospheric emission because oxidation and transport intervene.
