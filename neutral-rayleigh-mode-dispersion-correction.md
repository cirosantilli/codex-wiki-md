# Neutral Rayleigh-mode dispersion correction

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral-mode critical-layer matching](neutral-mode-critical-layer-matching.md)

Let $I=\int\phi_0^2dy$, $J=\operatorname{PV}\int U''\phi_0^2/(U-c_0)^2dy$, and $M=U'''(0)\phi_0(0)^2/U'(0)^2$. The [Wronskian](wronskian.md) identity for the first-order [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md), combined with [neutral-mode critical-layer matching](neutral-mode-critical-layer-matching.md), gives $2k_0k_1I=-c_1(J+i\pi M)$. For real $k_1$, its imaginary part is $2k_0k_1I\pi M/(J^2+\pi^2M^2)$. The [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) is necessary when the outer forcing has a simple real pole.
