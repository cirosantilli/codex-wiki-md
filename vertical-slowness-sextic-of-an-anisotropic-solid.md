# Vertical slowness sextic of an anisotropic solid

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic slowness surface](elastic-slowness-surface.md)

Fix horizontal [slowness](slowness.md) $p_\alpha$ and define $T_{ip}=c_{i3p3}$, $R_{ip}=c_{i3p\alpha}p_\alpha$ and $Q_{ip}=c_{i\alpha p\beta}p_\alpha p_\beta$. Under [strong ellipticity in elasticity](strong-ellipticity-in-elasticity.md), $T$ is positive definite, so the vertical-slowness determinant has degree six with real coefficients. Real roots are intersections of a vertical line with the [elastic slowness surface](elastic-slowness-surface.md); complex-conjugate pairs give [evanescent waves](evanescent-wave.md).
