# Parabolic coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal coordinates](orthogonal-coordinates.md)

The displayed three-dimensional parabolic coordinates use $u,v\geq0$ and an azimuthal angle $\phi$. Their coordinate [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md) have pairwise zero [dot products](dot-product.md), with [scale factors of orthogonal coordinates](scale-factors-of-orthogonal-coordinates.md) $h_u=h_v=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}$ and $h_\phi=uv$. The [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $uv(u^2+v^2)$. Constant $u$ or $v$ gives a family of confocal circular [elliptic paraboloids](elliptic-paraboloid.md); the coordinate system degenerates on the axis where $uv=0$. For example, $0\leq u\leq v\leq1$ fills the cap $(x^2+y^2-1)/2\leq z\leq0$, whose rim is the unit [circle](circle.md) and whose vertex is $(0,0,-1/2)$.
