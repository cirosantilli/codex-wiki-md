# Generalized KdV solitary wave

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Korteweg–De Vries equation](generalized-korteweg-de-vries-equation.md)

If $Q''-Q+Q^p=0$, then

$$
Q_c(x)=c^{1/(p-1)}Q(\sqrt c\,x)
$$

satisfies $Q_c''-cQ_c+Q_c^p=0$, and $u(t,x)=Q_c(x-ct)$ is a traveling-wave solution of the [Generalized Korteweg–De Vries equation](generalized-korteweg-de-vries-equation.md).
