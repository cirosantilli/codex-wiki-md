# Pollen

↑ **Parent:** [Pollination](pollination.md)

An [angiosperm](angiosperm.md) [pollen](pollen.md) grain is a small male [gametophyte](gametophyte.md), not itself a [seed](seed.md) or a single [gamete](gamete.md). Its tube-forming [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) supports delivery of [sperm](sperm.md).[Teaching notes: pollen grain and tube as male gametophyte](observing-the-growth-of-pollen-tubes-practical-biology.md)
