# Doob Lp maximal inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

For $p>1$ and a martingale $(M_k)_{k\leq n}$,

$$
\left\|\max_{k\leq n}|M_k|\right\|_p
\leq\frac p{p-1}\|M_n\|_p.
$$

This strong $L^p$ bound is one form of [Doob's martingale inequality](doob-s-martingale-inequality.md).
