# Bloch impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating-impedance transmission line](alternating-impedance-transmission-line.md)

The voltage-to-current ratio of a selected Bloch eigenmode at a specified position within a periodic cell. It depends on the chosen cell boundary, unlike the cell's propagation [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). In a pass band a physical semi-infinite mode carries positive real power; in an ideal stop band its [input impedance](input-impedance.md) is reactive and the mode decays into the line.
