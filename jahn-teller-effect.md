# Jahn-Teller effect

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

The [Jahn-Teller effect](jahn-teller-effect.md) is an instability of a nonlinear molecular arrangement with an orbitally degenerate electronic ground state toward a symmetry-lowering distortion. An octahedral high-[spin](spin.md) $d^4$ ion has unequal occupation of its $e_g$ orbitals and often undergoes tetragonal elongation.
