# Gravitino

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitino)

The gravitino is the spin-$3/2$ gauge field of local [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) and the partner of the [graviton](graviton.md). A massless four-dimensional gravitino has two physical [helicities](helicity.md), $\pm3/2$; a massive one has four, adding $\pm1/2$. The [super-Higgs mechanism](super-higgs-mechanism.md) supplies these extra states from the [goldstino](goldstino.md).
