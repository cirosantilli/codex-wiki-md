# Covariant divergence formula

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md)

The [Christoffel trace identity](christoffel-trace-identity.md) converts the [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) of a [vector field](vector-field.md) into a coordinate divergence weighted by the metric volume density. This is valid for the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md). Applied to the raised gradient of a [scalar field](scalar-field.md), it gives $\Box\phi=|g|^{-1/2}\partial_\mu(\sqrt{|g|}g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\nu\phi)$ in Lorentzian signature, the curved [d'Alembert operator](d-alembert-operator.md).
