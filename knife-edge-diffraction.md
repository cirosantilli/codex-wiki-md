# Knife-edge diffraction

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md)

For an opaque half-plane whose edge lies height $h$ above the direct source–receiver ray, the paraxial parameter is $v=h\sqrt{2(d_1+d_2)/(\lambda d_1d_2)}$. The unobstructed half-aperture supplies the [Cornu spiral](euler-spiral.md) chord from $v$ to infinity. Division by the full-aperture chord gives the displayed [intensity](intensity-physics.md) transmission. It is $1/4$ at grazing incidence, approaches one well into the illuminated region and tends to zero deep in shadow.[Annex 1, Sections 2.7 and 4.1](propagation-by-diffraction-itu-r-p-526-13-by-international-telecommunication-union.md)
