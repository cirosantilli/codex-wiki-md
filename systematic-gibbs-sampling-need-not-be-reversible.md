# Systematic Gibbs sampling need not be reversible

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs sampler](gibbs-sampler.md)

Every full-conditional coordinate update satisfies [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) with the target distribution. Their systematic ordered composition preserves the target but need not satisfy [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md), since compositions of reversible kernels need not be reversible. A fixed-probability random-scan mixture remains reversible.
