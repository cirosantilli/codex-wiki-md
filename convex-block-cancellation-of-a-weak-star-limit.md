# Convex-block cancellation of a weak-star limit

↑ **Parent:** [Convex block](convex-block.md)

If a bounded sequence $(y_n)$ in $X$ converges weak-star to $\phi\in X^{**}$ under the [canonical embedding into the bidual](canonical-embedding-into-the-bidual.md), then any [convex blocks](convex-block.md) $(u_n)$ have the same scalar limit on every $f\in X^*$. Thus $y_n-u_n$ is a [weakly null sequence](weakly-null-sequence.md), even when $\phi$ does not belong to the embedded space $X$.
