# Free-fall timescale

↑ **Parent:** [Free fall](free-fall.md)

The gravitational collapse scale $t_{\rm ff}\sim\sqrt{R^3/(GM)}$. Radial fall from rest at $R$ to a fixed central [point mass](point-mass.md) has the exact time $\pi\sqrt{R^3/(GM)}/(2\sqrt2)$.
