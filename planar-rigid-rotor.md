# Planar rigid rotor

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md)

A [rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md) restricted to one angular coordinate $\phi$. Single-valued spatial states $e^{im\phi}$ have $m\in\mathbb Z$ and [energy](energy-split.md) $E_m=\hbar^2m^2/(2I)$, with [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$. The zero-angular-momentum level has [state degeneracy](state-degeneracy.md) one; every nonzero $|m|$ has two directions. For labelled molecular ends its high-temperature [rotational partition function](rotational-partition-function.md) is $\sqrt{\pi T/\theta_{\rm rot}}$, where $\theta_{\rm rot}=\hbar^2/(2Ik_{\rm B})$. Equivalent orientations and [nuclear spin statistics](nuclear-spin-statistics.md) require a further molecular counting convention. One quadratic [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) gives rotational [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) $k_{\rm B}/2$ per molecule, rather than $k_{\rm B}$ for an unrestricted [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md).
