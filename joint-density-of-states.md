# Joint density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Density of states](density-of-states.md)

For filled valence and empty conduction [energy bands](energy-band.md), the joint [density of states](density-of-states.md) counts pairs of states at a prescribed interband energy difference. In a direct optical transition the photon momentum is usually negligible compared with a [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md) dimension, so initial and final states have nearly the same [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md). The absorption spectrum weights this count by transition [matrix elements](matrix-element.md); its onset and structure can constrain the [band gap](band-gap.md) and [electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md). Indirect transitions additionally involve [phonons](phonon.md) to supply crystal momentum.
