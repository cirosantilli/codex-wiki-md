# Rotation-clamped reflection between two spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Stresslet reflection between two force-free and forced spheres](stresslet-reflection-between-two-force-free-and-forced-spheres.md)

If the otherwise [force-free](force-free.md) second [sphere](sphere.md) is held against rotation, its [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) relative to the incident fluid is $-3a(\mathbf U_0\times\mathbf n)/(4R^2)$. The [rotlet](rotlet.md) reflected back to the first [sphere](sphere.md) adds

$$
\Delta\mathbf U_{\mathrm{rot}}=-\frac34\left(\frac aR\right)^4\left[\mathbf U_0-(\mathbf U_0\cdot\mathbf n)\mathbf n\right].
$$

This follows by evaluating the [rotating sphere in Stokes flow](rotating-sphere-in-stokes-flow.md) at displacement $-R\mathbf n$ and using the vector triple-product identity. The holding couple does no work because the held [sphere](sphere.md)'s [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) is zero.
