# Parity-check matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Linear code](linear-code.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parity-check_matrix)

A parity-check matrix $H$ for a [linear code](linear-code.md) $C$ satisfies

$$
C=\ker H=\{c:Hc^T=0\}.
$$
