# Radiation-enhanced diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation damage in materials](radiation-damage-in-materials.md)

Transport accelerated by irradiation-maintained populations of mobile [point defects](point-defect.md). It can change precipitation, segregation and recovery. Its consequences depend on defect production, recombination and [defect sinks](defect-sink.md), rather than simply on the nominal [displacement dose](displacements-per-atom.md).
