# Effect size

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effect_size)

An [effect size](effect-size.md) measures the magnitude of an observed or modeled relationship. A [standardized mean difference](standardized-mean-difference.md) expresses a mean contrast in standard-deviation units; other [effect sizes](effect-size.md) include a [correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) or [odds ratio](odds-ratio.md). Magnitude is distinct from the evidence against a [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md) supplied by a [p-value](p-value.md): a small [effect size](effect-size.md) can have a small [p-value](p-value.md) in a sufficiently large sample.
