# Extremal black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremal_black_hole)

An extremal black hole lies at the parameter bound where its inner and outer horizons coincide. Its [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) and hence its semiclassical [Hawking temperature](hawking-temperature.md) vanish.
