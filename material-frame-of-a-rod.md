# Material frame of a rod

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic rod](elastic-rod.md)

A right-handed [orthonormal frame](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) attached to the cross-sections of an [elastic rod](elastic-rod.md). For an unshearable [elastic rod](elastic-rod.md), $\mathbf d_3$ is the unit [tangent vector](tangent-vector-split.md) to its centreline, while $\mathbf d_1$ and $\mathbf d_2$ follow the orientation of its material cross-section. Unlike a frame constructed only from the centreline [curvature](curvature.md), the material frame records the twist of the material about the tangent.
