# Gibbsite

↑ **Parent:** [Mineral](mineral.md)

An aluminium hydroxide [mineral](mineral.md) with composition $\mathrm{Al(OH)_3}$. In a simplified [mineral activity diagram](mineral-activity-diagram.md), it is favored over [kaolinite](kaolinite.md) at sufficiently low dissolved silica [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md).
