# Projective special linear group over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear group over a finite field](special-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md)

The scalar center has order $\gcd(n,q-1)$. For $n>1$ the quotient is simple except at $(n,q)=(2,2),(2,3)$. Its faithful primitive projective-point action, abelian fixed-center transvections, elementary-matrix generation and perfectness give the [Iwasawa simplicity lemma](iwasawa-simplicity-lemma.md) proof.
