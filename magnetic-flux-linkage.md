# Magnetic flux linkage

↑ **Parent:** [Inductance](inductance.md)

[Magnetic flux linkage](magnetic-flux-linkage.md) adds the signed [magnetic fluxes](magnetic-flux.md) through the turns of a coil:

$$
\Lambda=\sum_{k=1}^N\Phi_k,\qquad
\Phi_k=\int_{S_k}\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf S.
$$

Choose each [oriented surface](oriented-surface.md) $S_k$ consistently with positive [electric current](electric-current.md) around its turn using the [right-hand rule](right-hand-rule.md). If all $N$ turns link approximately the same [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) $\Phi$, then $\Lambda\approx N\Phi$; equality holds when every turn links exactly the same [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md). Uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) perpendicular to identical flat turns of [area](surface-area.md) $A$ gives $\Phi=BA$. For a stipulated $100$-turn coil with [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) $0.015\,\mathrm{Wb}$ per turn, [magnetic flux linkage](magnetic-flux-linkage.md) is $1.5\,\mathrm{Wb}$, not $0.015\,\mathrm{Wb}$. The turn count is dimensionless. [OpenStax University Physics 2, §14.2: flux through the turns of a coil](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/14-2-self-inductance-and-inductors).
