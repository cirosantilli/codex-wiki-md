# Carbon nanotube forest

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon nanotube](carbon-nanotube.md)

A dense population of approximately aligned [carbon nanotubes](carbon-nanotube.md) growing from a support. Collective crowding can favor alignment even when the support does not epitaxially select every tube orientation.
