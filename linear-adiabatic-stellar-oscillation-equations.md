# Linear adiabatic stellar oscillation equations

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar oscillation](stellar-oscillation.md)

For a static spherical star, a [fluid displacement](lagrangian-displacement-fluid-mechanics.md) $\boldsymbol\xi e^{-i\omega t}$ gives $\rho_1=-\nabla\cdot(\rho\boldsymbol\xi)$, $-\omega^2\rho\boldsymbol\xi=-\nabla p_1-\rho_1\nabla\Phi-\rho\nabla\phi_1$ and $\nabla^2\phi_1=4\pi G\rho_1$. The adiabatic relation is $\Delta P=-\Gamma_1P\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi$. Centre regularity and surface mechanical/gravitational boundary conditions select discrete [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) in a conservative model.
