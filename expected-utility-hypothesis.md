# Expected utility hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Utility function](utility-function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expected_utility_hypothesis)

The [expected utility hypothesis](expected-utility-hypothesis.md) ranks uncertain outcomes by the [expected value](expected-value.md) of a [utility function](utility-function-split.md). When these preference assumptions hold, [expected utility maximization](expected-utility-maximization.md) selects an admissible outcome with greatest expected utility.
