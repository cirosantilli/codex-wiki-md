# Localization away from one plus an ideal lies in the Jacobson radical

↑ **Parent:** [Universal property of localization](universal-property-of-localization.md)

For a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$ and [ideal](ideal.md) $I$, the set $S=1+I$ is a [multiplicative subset](multiplicatively-closed-set.md). For $i\in I$ and $s,t\in S$, the numerator $st-ri$ of $1-(r/t)(i/s)$ again lies in $S$. Thus this difference is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) in the [localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md). The [unit criterion for the Jacobson radical](unit-criterion-for-the-jacobson-radical.md) proves $S^{-1}I\subseteq J(S^{-1}R)$. This requires no [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md) hypothesis.
