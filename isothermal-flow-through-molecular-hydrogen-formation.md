# Isothermal flow through molecular hydrogen formation

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

Across a thin steady transition with negligible external impulse, [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) and [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) give the displayed relations. If the gas stays isothermal while neutral atomic [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) becomes [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md), the mean particle [mass](mass.md) doubles and $a_2^2=a_1^2/2$ for its [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md). Let $J=u_1+a_1^2/u_1$. The downstream subsonic branch has $u_2=[J-\sqrt{J^2-2a_1^2}]/2$, $\rho_2/\rho_1=u_1/u_2$, and $p_2-p_1=\rho_1u_1(u_1-u_2)>0$.

For $u_1\ll a_1$, the leading behavior is $u_2\simeq u_1/2$, $\rho_2\simeq2\rho_1$ and $p_2\simeq p_1$, at unchanged [temperature](temperature-split.md). Radiation of the molecular binding energy prevents that released energy from necessarily heating the gas; it does not, by itself, establish an isothermal thermal closure. If temperature evolution is allowed, an energy balance and a cooling model are required to determine all four changes uniquely.
