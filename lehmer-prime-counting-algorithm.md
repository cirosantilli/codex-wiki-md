# Lehmer prime-counting algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Prime-counting function](prime-counting-function.md)

A [prime counting](prime-counting.md) [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) that evaluates $\pi(x)$ by recursively counting [integers](integer-split.md) not divisible by the first few [primes](prime-number-split.md) and correcting for products of larger [primes](prime-number-split.md). A small [number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md) and cached recursive values allow it to handle inputs much larger than the precomputed table.
