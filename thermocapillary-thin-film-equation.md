# Thermocapillary thin-film equation

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film equation](thin-film-equation.md)

For a liquid film heated below and cooled above, the [quasistatic temperature of a conductively cooled film](quasistatic-temperature-of-a-conductively-cooled-film.md) makes a thicker region colder at the surface. If [surface tension](surface-tension.md) decreases with temperature, [Marangoni stress](marangoni-effect.md) draws fluid towards thicker regions, competing with smoothing by [capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md). Writing $\gamma_s=\gamma_0+bh$ gives $a_c=\gamma_0/(3\mu)$, $a_m=b/(2\mu)$. A balanced long-wave scaling gives $H_\tau+(H^2H_X)_X+(H^3H_{XXX})_X=0$. The [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) $s=k^2-k^4$ has unstable band $0<|k|<1$ and fastest [normal mode](normal-mode.md) at $|k|=1/\sqrt2$. This thermal mechanism does not require [surfactant](surfactant.md).
