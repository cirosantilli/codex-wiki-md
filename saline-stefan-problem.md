# Saline Stefan problem

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

A saline Stefan problem couples a [heat equation](heat-equation.md), a solute [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md), a [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md), solute conservation, and a concentration-dependent [liquidus](liquidus.md) at a moving solid-liquid boundary. Different thermal and solutal diffusivities produce nested boundary layers.
