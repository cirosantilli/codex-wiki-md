# Coupled torsional pendulums

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion pendulum](torsion-pendulum.md)

Two identical torsional loads of [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$ hang in series from massless fibres of lengths $\ell_1,\ell_2$ and common [filament torsional stiffness](filament-torsional-stiffness.md) $K_t$. Put $R=\ell_2/\ell_1$ and $\omega_0^2=K_t/(I\ell_1)$. The elastic [energy](energy-split.md) is $K_t\phi_1^2/(2\ell_1)+K_t(\phi_2-\phi_1)^2/(2\ell_2)$ and the kinetic [energy](energy-split.md) is $I(\dot\phi_1^2+\dot\phi_2^2)/2$. Their [normal modes](normal-mode.md) have the displayed squared [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md). Writing $\lambda=\omega^2/\omega_0^2$, their amplitude ratio is $A_2/A_1=1/(1-R\lambda)$. The lower mode twists in phase and approaches a combined inertia $2I$ as $R\to0$, giving $\omega_-^2\to\omega_0^2/2$. The upper mode approaches the original single-load oscillation as $R\to\infty$, giving $\omega_+^2\to\omega_0^2$.
