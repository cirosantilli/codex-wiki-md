# Survival probability

↑ **Parent:** [First-passage time](first-passage-time.md)

The survival probability is $Q(x,t)=\mathbb P_x(T_A>t)$. When absorption eventually occurs,

$$
\mathbb E_xT_A=\int_0^\infty Q(x,t)\,dt.
$$
