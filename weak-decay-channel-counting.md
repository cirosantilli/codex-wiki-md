# Weak-decay channel counting

↑ **Parent:** [Inclusive spectator model](inclusive-spectator-model.md)

In a massless [Fermi interaction](fermi-interaction.md) approximation, a charged-current decay channel contributes a common mass-to-the-fifth width multiplied by its mixing and final-state multiplicity factors. A final quark-antiquark pair has three color choices, while a [lepton](lepton.md) pair has one. An initial [quark](quark.md) does not acquire a factor of three in its lifetime merely from its possible initial colors.
