# Balance wheel

↑ **Parent:** [Clock](clock.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balance_wheel)

A wheel that oscillates about a spindle and regulates a watch through an [escapement](escapement.md). In the ideal balance-spring model, a [torsion spring](torsion-spring.md) supplies torque $-\kappa\phi$ and the wheel has [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$, giving $I\ddot\phi+\kappa\phi=0$. The period follows from [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md). Real spring, bearing and escapement effects disturb this ideal.
