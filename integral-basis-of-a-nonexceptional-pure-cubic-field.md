# Integral basis of a nonexceptional pure cubic field

↑ **Parent:** [Integral basis](integral-basis.md)

If $m>1$ is a [square-free integer](square-free-integer.md), $3\nmid m$, and $m\not\equiv\pm1\pmod9$, then $1,\sqrt[3]{m},\sqrt[3]{m^2}$ is an [integral basis](integral-basis.md) of the [pure cubic number field](pure-cubic-number-field.md), with [field discriminant](field-discriminant.md) $-27m^2$. The [discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice](discriminant-index-formula-for-an-integral-lattice.md) restricts a possible index to primes dividing $3m$. At primes dividing $m$ the [different exponent](different-exponent.md) is $2$ by [tame ramification](tamely-ramified-extension.md). A [shifted Eisenstein polynomial](shifted-eisenstein-polynomial.md) gives total wild ramification at $3$, so its [different exponent](different-exponent.md) is at least $3$. These exponents exhaust the polynomial [discriminant](discriminant.md) and force index one.
