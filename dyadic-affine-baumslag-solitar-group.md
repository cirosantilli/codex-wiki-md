# Dyadic affine Baumslag-Solitar group

↑ **Parent:** [Baumslag-Solitar group](baumslag-solitar-group.md)

In the [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md) $\langle a,t\mid tat^{-1}=a^2\rangle$, let $a$ act by $x\mapsto x+1$ and $t$ by $x\mapsto2x$. The conjugates $a_i=t^{-i}at^i$ satisfy $a_{i+1}^2=a_i$, so their increasing cyclic subgroups give the additive group $\mathbb Z[1/2]$. Every element has the form $a_i^m t^k$, and the affine action proves that this form can be the identity only if $k=m=0$. Thus the displayed [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md) identification holds. This is a finitely presented group with a series having torsion-free abelian factors, but it is not a [poly-infinite cyclic group](poly-infinite-cyclic-group.md), since $\mathbb Z[1/2]$ is not finitely generated.
