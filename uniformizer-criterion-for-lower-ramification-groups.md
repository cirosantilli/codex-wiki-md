# Uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification group](ramification-group.md)

If $L/K$ is a finite totally ramified Galois extension and $\pi_L$ is a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md), then

$$
G_i=\{\sigma:v_L(\sigma(\pi_L)-\pi_L)\geq i+1\}
$$

for every $i\geq0$.
