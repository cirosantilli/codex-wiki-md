# Stinespring representation of a completely positive map

↑ **Parent:** [Completely positive map](completely-positive-map.md)

In finite dimensions, a [completely positive map](completely-positive-map.md) has the form $\mathcal N(X)=\operatorname{Tr}_E(VXV^\dagger)$ for $V:\mathcal H_Q\to\mathcal H_{Q\prime}\otimes\mathcal H_E$. From a [Kraus representation](kraus-representation.md) take $V=\sum_jK_j\otimes|j\rangle$. The observable-picture adjoint is $\mathcal N^\dagger(Y)=V^\dagger(Y\otimes I_E)V$. A general completely positive map need not have isometric $V$; it is a [linear isometry of Hilbert spaces](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md) precisely when the map is trace preserving.
