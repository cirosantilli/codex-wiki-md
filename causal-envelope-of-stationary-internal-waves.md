# Causal envelope of stationary internal waves

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave ray tracing](internal-wave-ray-tracing.md)

In uniform positive flow $U$, upward stationary [internal gravity waves](internal-wave.md) have laboratory [group velocity](group-velocity.md) $U(\cos^2\theta,\sin\theta\cos\theta)$. Packets emitted by a localized source at startup lie on $(x-Ut/2)^2+z^2=(Ut/2)^2$, with later emissions inside. This envelope belongs to the permanent stationary component, not every possible startup transient.
