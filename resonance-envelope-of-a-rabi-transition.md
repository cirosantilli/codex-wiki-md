# Resonance envelope of a Rabi transition

↑ **Parent:** [Rabi cycle](rabi-cycle.md)

In the [Driven two-level Rabi model](driven-two-level-rabi-model.md), the transition [probability](probability.md) is $P(t)=\Omega^2\sin^2(\sqrt{\Omega^2+\delta^2}\,t/2)/(\Omega^2+\delta^2)$. Maximizing over time at each detuning gives a [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md), with first maximizing time $\pi/\sqrt{\Omega^2+\delta^2}$ and [full width at half maximum](full-width-at-half-maximum.md) $2|\Omega|$ in angular frequency. The amplitude parameter $\Omega$ must use the specified [Rabi angular frequency](rabi-angular-frequency.md) convention.

This envelope differs from a spectrum obtained with a fixed pulse duration: optimizing the time separately removes the sine-squared factor, while fixing time retains it. A finite linewidth here describes detuning-limited transfer under coherent driving, without requiring dissipative decay.
