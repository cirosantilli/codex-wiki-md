# Kepler velocity hodograph

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

In axes directed along the [periapsis](periapsis.md) and transverse to it, a [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) with gravitational parameter $\mu_g$ and [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $h$ has the displayed [velocity](velocity.md). The [velocity](velocity.md) tip traces a circle of radius $\mu_g/h$ centred at $(0,e\mu_g/h)$. To derive it, differentiate $r=h^2/[\mu_g(1+e\cos f)]$, use $r^2\dot f=h$, and resolve $\dot r\widehat{\boldsymbol r}+r\dot f\widehat{\boldsymbol\theta}$. A rotation of the spatial axes rotates the entire hodograph by the same angle.
