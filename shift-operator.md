# Shift operator

↑ **Parent:** [Operator theory](linear-operator-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shift_operator)

A shift operator translates the components of a function or sequence by a fixed displacement. Bilateral shifts act on doubly infinite sequences; the [unilateral shift operator](unilateral-shift-operator.md) acts on a one-sided sequence space and has distinct forward and backward versions.
