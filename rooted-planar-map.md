# Rooted planar map

↑ **Parent:** [Planar map](planar-map.md)

A rooted planar map is a [planar map](planar-map.md) with a distinguished oriented edge called its root edge. Its tail is the root vertex, and the face on a chosen side of the edge may be designated as the root face.
