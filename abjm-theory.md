# ABJM theory

↑ **Parent:** [AdS/CFT correspondence](ads-cft-correspondence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABJM_theory)

ABJM theory is a three-dimensional supersymmetric Chern–Simons matter theory with a large-$N$ holographic dual. In its M-theory regime the bulk Newton constant scales as $G_N\sim N^{-3/2}$ in AdS units.
