# Electronic bandwidth

↑ **Parent:** [Energy band](energy-band.md)

The [electronic bandwidth](electronic-bandwidth.md) is the difference between the maximum and minimum of one [energy band](energy-band.md) over the [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md). It has units of energy, unlike a frequency bandwidth in signal processing. A nearest-neighbour [tight-binding model](tight-binding.md) on a [primitive cubic lattice](primitive-cubic-lattice.md) with equal real [hopping integrals](hopping-integral.md) $t$ has $E(\mathbf k)=E_0+2t\sum_{i=1}^3\cos(k_i a)$ and $W=12|t|$.
