# Hindered-settling flux inflection

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic sedimentation](kinematic-sedimentation.md)

For [hindered settling](hindered-settling.md) with normalized [particle volume fraction](particle-volume-fraction.md) $s$, upward coordinate and downward isolated [settling velocity](settling-velocity.md) magnitude $V_0$, the [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md) has [conservation law flux](conservation-law-flux.md) $f(s)=-V_0s(1-s)^\alpha$. For $\alpha>0$, its [convexity](convex-function.md) changes at $s=2/(\alpha+1)$ when this lies in the physical interval. This change distinguishes spreading [rarefaction waves](rarefaction-wave.md) from compressing [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md); a flux with an inflection can produce both a [shock wave](shock-wave.md) and a [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md) in one transition. At $\alpha=0$ the flux is linear and the profile simply translates.
