# Cohomological pushforward between closed oriented manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md)

For a map between closed oriented manifolds of the same dimension, [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) turns homological pushforward into a degree-preserving map on [cohomology](cohomology-split.md). It is adjoint to pullback under the duality pairing: $\langle f^!a\smile b,[N]\rangle=\langle a\smile f^*b,[M]\rangle$. If $f$ has degree $d$, nondegeneracy gives $f^!f^*=d\,\mathrm{id}$. Maps of different dimensions require the corresponding degree shift.
