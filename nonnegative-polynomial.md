# Nonnegative polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

A real [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is globally nonnegative if its value is nonnegative at every point of its real domain. A [sum of squares polynomial](polynomial-sos.md) is always nonnegative, but the [Horn copositive matrix](horn-copositive-matrix.md) gives a nonnegative quartic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) that is not a [sum of squares polynomial](polynomial-sos.md).
