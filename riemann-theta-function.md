# Riemann theta function

↑ **Parent:** [Theta function](theta-function.md)

For a symmetric matrix $B$ with positive definite imaginary part, this [theta function](theta-function.md) converges normally on [compact](compact-space.md) subsets of $\mathbb C^g$. It obeys $\theta(z+m+Bn,B)=e^{-\pi i n^tBn-2\pi i n^tz}\theta(z,B)$, so its zeros define a [divisor](divisor.md) on the associated [complex torus](complex-torus.md). If $B$ is a curve's normalized period matrix, its zero [divisor](divisor.md) is a translate of the effective degree-$g-1$ locus, by the [Riemann vanishing theorem](riemann-vanishing-theorem.md). A general symmetric positive period matrix need not come from a curve.
