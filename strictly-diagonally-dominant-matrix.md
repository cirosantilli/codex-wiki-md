# Strictly diagonally dominant matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Diagonally dominant matrix](diagonally-dominant-matrix.md)

A matrix is strictly diagonally dominant when every diagonal-dominance inequality is strict. A real symmetric strictly diagonally dominant matrix with positive diagonal is [positive definite](positive-definite-matrix.md) by the [Gershgorin circle theorem](gershgorin-circle-theorem.md).
