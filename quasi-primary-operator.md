# Quasi-primary operator

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional conformal field theory](two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md)

A [quasi-primary operator](quasi-primary-operator.md) transforms with weights $(h,\widetilde h)$ under global conformal, or Möbius, transformations. A [primary operator](primary-field.md) has this covariance under every locally invertible holomorphic and antiholomorphic coordinate change. In [radial quantization](radial-quantization.md), quasi-primary states are annihilated by $L_1,\widetilde L_1$, whereas [primary states](primary-state.md) are annihilated by every positive Virasoro mode. The stress tensor at nonzero [central charge](central-charge.md) is quasi-primary but not primary because its transformation under a general coordinate change includes a Schwarzian term.
