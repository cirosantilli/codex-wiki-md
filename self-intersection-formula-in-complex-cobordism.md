# Self-intersection formula in complex cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Gysin map in complex cobordism](gysin-map-in-complex-cobordism.md)

Restricting the tubular-neighborhood collapse of an embedding back to its source is the normal bundle's zero section. The [Thom isomorphism in complex cobordism](thom-isomorphism-in-complex-cobordism.md) therefore makes pullback after the Gysin map multiplication by the [Euler class in a generalized cohomology theory](euler-class-in-a-generalized-cohomology-theory.md). For an actual complex normal bundle this is its top [Chern class in complex cobordism](chern-class-in-complex-cobordism.md).
