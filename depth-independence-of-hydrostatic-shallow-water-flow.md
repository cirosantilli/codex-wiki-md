# Depth independence of hydrostatic shallow-water flow

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized shallow water equations](linearized-shallow-water-equations.md)

In a homogeneous layer under the [hydrostatic approximation](hydrostatic-approximation.md), the horizontal [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) is independent of depth. The depth derivatives of velocity obey $(u_z)_t-fv_z=0$, $(v_z)_t+fu_z=0$. If the motion starts without shear, these derivatives remain zero. Hydrostatic pressure alone does not eliminate a pre-existing freely oscillating shear.
