# Failure of the source-size bound for the screened sine-Gordon equation

↑ **Parent:** [Screened sine-Gordon energy](screened-sine-gordon-energy.md)

Take $a=3\pi/2$, $L=10$ and $u(x)=a\exp(-|x|^2/L^2)$ on $\mathbb R^3$. Set $f=-\Delta u+u+\sin u$. Both functions are smooth and belong to [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md), and $u\in H^1$. The strictly increasing reaction $h(s)=s+\sin s$ has $0\leq h(u)\leq h(a)=a-1$, while

$$
|\Delta u|=\frac{a}{L^2}|4q-6|e^{-q}\leq\frac{6a}{L^2},\qquad q=|x|^2/L^2.
$$

Consequently $\|f\|_\infty\leq a-1+6a/L^2<a=\|u\|_\infty$. This $u$ is a minimizer of the [screened sine-Gordon energy](screened-sine-gordon-energy.md): it solves the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) and that energy is convex. Thus even the minimizing and smooth hypotheses do not justify the stronger source-size bound. The appropriate general estimate is [maximum bound for a monotone reaction term](maximum-bound-for-a-monotone-reaction-term.md).
