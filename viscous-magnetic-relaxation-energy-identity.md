# Viscous magnetic-relaxation energy identity

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic relaxation](magnetic-relaxation.md)

For constant [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu>0$, ideal induction, zero boundary [velocity](velocity.md) and tangent [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), put $E=\int_D[\rho|\mathbf v|^2/2+|\mathbf B|^2/(2\mu_0)]dV$. Multiplying the momentum equation by $\mathbf v$ and integrating cancels pressure work and advective flux. The magnetic-energy derivative is minus the integral of [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) dotted with [velocity](velocity.md), canceling the same work in the kinetic-energy equation. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) then gives the displayed identity. For [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) with zero boundary velocity, $\int|\nabla\mathbf v|^2=\int|\nabla\times\mathbf v|^2$.
