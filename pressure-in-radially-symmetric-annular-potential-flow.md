# Pressure in radially symmetric annular potential flow

↑ **Parent:** [Radially symmetric incompressible flow in a planar annulus](radially-symmetric-incompressible-flow-in-a-planar-annulus.md)

If the pressure at $r=b(t)$ is $p_\infty$, the [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md) gives

$$
p(a,t)-p_\infty
=\rho\left[\frac d{dt}(a\dot a)\log\frac ba
-\frac{(a\dot a)^2}{2}\left(\frac1{a^2}-\frac1{b^2}\right)\right].
$$
