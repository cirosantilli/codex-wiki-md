# Integrality of the generic fibre of an affine dominant morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme-theoretic fibre](scheme-theoretic-fibre.md)

For an injective [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) between nonzero [integral domains](integral-domain.md) $A\to B$, the [scheme-theoretic fibre](scheme-theoretic-fibre.md) over the [generic point](generic-point.md) of $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is $\operatorname{Spec}(S^{-1}B)$ with $S=A\setminus\{0\}$. Localization at these nonzero elements is a nonzero integral domain, so the generic fibre is a nonempty [integral scheme](integral-scheme.md). A surjective morphism between these affine schemes necessarily gives an injective ring map: a prime above $(0)$ contains its kernel.
