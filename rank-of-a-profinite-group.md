# Rank of a profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Profinite group](profinite-group.md)

The rank of a [profinite group](profinite-group.md) is $\operatorname{rk}(G)=\sup_{K\le_cG}d(K)$, where $K$ runs through closed [subgroups](subgroup.md) and $d(K)$ is the least number of topological generators. If an [open subgroup](open-subgroup.md) $H$ has rank $r$, its [normal core](core-group-theory.md) $C$ is open and has rank at most $r$. Every closed $K\le G$ has a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) $K\cap C$ generated by at most $r$ elements, while $K/(K\cap C)$ has order at most $[G:C]$. A [finite group](finite-group.md) of order at most $M$ is generated by at most $\lfloor\log_2M\rfloor$ elements, since each necessary new generator at least doubles the generated [subgroup](subgroup.md). Lifting generators yields $d(K)\le r+\lfloor\log_2[G:C]\rfloor$. Thus finite rank is preserved in both directions under passage to an [open subgroup](open-subgroup.md).
