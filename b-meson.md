# B meson

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B_meson)

A [meson](meson.md) containing a [bottom quark](bottom-quark.md) or its [antiquark](antiquark.md) partner. The neutral pair $B^0=d\bar b$ and $\bar B^0=\bar d b$ undergoes [neutral meson mixing](neutral-meson-mixing.md).
