# Homoscedasticity and heteroscedasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homoscedasticity_and_heteroscedasticity)

These describe constancy or variation of conditional error [variance](variance-split.md) across observations. A homoscedastic [regression model](regression-model.md) has $\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_i\mid X)=\sigma^2$; a heteroscedastic model allows this variance to depend on the observation or its covariates.
