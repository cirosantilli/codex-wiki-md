# Unconfined aquifer

↑ **Parent:** [Aquifer](aquifer.md)

An unconfined aquifer has a free groundwater table. In a shallow aquifer of thickness $h(x,t)$, hydrostatic pressure and [Darcy's law](darcy-law.md) give horizontal flux proportional to $-hh_x$.
