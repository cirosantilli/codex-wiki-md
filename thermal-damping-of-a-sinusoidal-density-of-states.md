# Thermal damping of a sinusoidal density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi window](fermi-window.md)

A [density of states](density-of-states.md) $g(\epsilon)=g_0[1+\alpha\cos(2\pi\epsilon/\Delta)]$ has zero-temperature oscillatory [grand potential](grand-potential.md) $g_0\alpha\Delta^2\cos(2\pi\mu/\Delta)/(4\pi^2)$. Its Fermi-window [convolution](convolution.md) multiplies that [oscillation](oscillation.md) by $R_T$ when the lower band edge is far below the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md). The [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) of the even window removes its [sine](sine.md) component and gives the title factor. At high $X$, $R_T\sim2Xe^{-X}$; at small $X$, $R_T=1-X^2/6+O(X^4)$. The corresponding thermal change relative to zero [temperature](temperature-split.md) has [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) proportional to $1-R_T$, rather than $R_T$.
