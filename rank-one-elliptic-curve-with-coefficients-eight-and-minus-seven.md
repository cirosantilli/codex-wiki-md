# Rank-one elliptic curve with coefficients eight and minus seven

↑ **Parent:** [Two-isogeny descent](two-isogeny-descent.md)

The [two-isogeny formula](two-isogeny-formula.md) gives the partner $E':y^2=x^3-16x^2+92x$. The [two-torsion square-class homomorphism](two-torsion-square-class-homomorphism.md) image for $E$ is $\{1,-7\}$: [prime](prime-number.md) support leaves only $\{1,-1,7,-7\}$, the [modulo-sixteen obstruction for the negative unit descent class](modulo-sixteen-obstruction-for-the-negative-unit-descent-class.md) excludes $-1$, and the subgroup property excludes $7$. For $E'$, $(x-8)^2+28>0$ forces nonnegative $x$, so the image is contained in $\{1,2,23,46\}$. The two-torsion point realizes $23$ and the [rational point](rational-point.md) $(18,48)$ realizes $2$, giving all four classes. The [two-isogeny rank formula](square-class-index-formula-for-two-isogeny-descent.md) yields $2^r=2\cdot4/4=2$, hence rank one. The [dual isogeny](dual-isogeny.md) sends $(18,48)$ to $(16/9,116/27)$; this statement does not assert that the resulting point is a generator of the full free part.
