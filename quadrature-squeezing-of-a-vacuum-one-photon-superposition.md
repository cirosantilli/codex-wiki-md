<h1 id="quadrature-squeezing-of-a-vacuum-one-photon-superposition">Quadrature squeezing of a vacuum–one-photon superposition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md)

For real $\epsilon$ with $|\epsilon|\leq1$, let $|\psi\rangle=\sqrt{1-\epsilon^2}|0\rangle+\epsilon|1\rangle$. The [Fock state](fock-state.md) matrix elements give $\langle a\rangle=\epsilon\sqrt{1-\epsilon^2}$, $\langle a^2\rangle=0$ and $\langle a^\dagger a\rangle=\epsilon^2$. Therefore

$$
\operatorname{Var}X_1=\frac14-\frac12\epsilon^2+\epsilon^4,\qquad \operatorname{Var}X_2=\frac14+\frac12\epsilon^2.
$$

Only the first of these [optical quadratures](optical-quadrature.md) is squeezed for this real superposition, with minimum variance $3/16$ at $\epsilon^2=1/4$. The second is never below vacuum noise. This non-Gaussian superposition is distinct from an [optical squeezed vacuum](optical-squeezed-vacuum.md).
