# Spectral theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Normal operator](normal-operator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_theorem)

The spectral theorem represents a [normal operator](normal-operator.md) by multiplication by its spectral parameter. In a finite-dimensional [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), this is diagonalization in an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md); for a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), it uses a [projection-valued measure](projection-valued-measure.md). Unbounded operators additionally require their natural square-integrability domain.
