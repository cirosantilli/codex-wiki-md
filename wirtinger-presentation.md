# Wirtinger presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Knot group](knot-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wirtinger_presentation)

A Wirtinger presentation has one meridional generator for every arc of a [knot diagram](knot-diagram.md) and one conjugacy relator at every crossing. For a connected diagram one crossing relator is redundant, giving a deficiency-one presentation of the [knot group](knot-group.md).
