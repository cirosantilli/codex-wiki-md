<h1 id="richter-ting-independent-j-psi-discovery-experiments">Richter-Ting independent J/psi discovery experiments</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

In 1974, [Samuel Chao Chung Ting](samuel-chao-chung-ting.md)'s Brookhaven team and [Burton Richter](burton-richter.md)'s SPEAR team independently found the narrow state now called the [J/psi meson](j-psi-meson.md). Each discovery paper notes learning of the other result during manuscript preparation.[Aubert and others, p. 1406, note 5: independent SPEAR result](experimental-observation-of-a-heavy-particle-j-by-j-j-aubert.md)[Augustin and others, p. 1408, note 3: independent Brookhaven result](discovery-of-a-narrow-resonance-in-e-plus-e-annihilation-by-j-e-augustin.md)

The common question was whether the observed excess represented a new [particle resonance](particle-resonance.md), rather than a peculiarity of [particle identification](particle-identification.md), acceptance or calibration. The important difference was the horizontal coordinate of the search: **Brookhaven reconstructed a final-state [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md); SPEAR scanned an initial-state [center-of-mass energy](center-of-mass-energy.md).** The [invariant-mass selection of bubble-chamber events](invariant-mass-selection-of-bubble-chamber-events.md) uses the first kind of reasoning, despite a very different recording medium.
