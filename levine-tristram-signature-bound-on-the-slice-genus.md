# Levine-Tristram signature bound on the slice genus

↑ **Parent:** [Slice genus](slice-genus.md)

For every unit complex number $\omega$ at which the signature form is nonsingular,

$$
2g_4(K)\geq|\sigma_\omega(K)|.
$$
