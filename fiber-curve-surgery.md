# Fiber-curve surgery

↑ **Parent:** [Dehn filling](dehn-filling.md)

Let a [knot](knot.md) lie in a fiber of a [mapping torus](mapping-torus.md) of an oriented [topological surface](topological-surface.md). Surgery with coefficient $-1$ relative to its [surface framing](surface-framing.md) replaces the monodromy by its composition with a [right-handed Dehn twist](right-handed-dehn-twist.md). Coefficient $+1$ gives the inverse twist. The composition side depends on the choice of seam and time orientation; the existence of the new [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md) does not.
