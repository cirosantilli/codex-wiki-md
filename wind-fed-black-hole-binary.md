# Wind-fed black-hole binary

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray binary](x-ray-binary.md)

A binary in which a [black hole](black-hole-split.md) captures part of its companion's [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md). The captured [gas](gas.md) can dissipate [energy](energy-split.md) and, with sufficient [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md), form an [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md). This differs from [gas](gas.md) overflowing a [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md) through the inner [Lagrange point](lagrange-point.md). The fraction captured, its [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and changes in the wind affect the [accretion](accretion.md) flow and emitted [X-rays](x-ray.md).
