# Quadratic Hilbert symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert norm residue symbol](hilbert-norm-residue-symbol.md)

The symbol is one exactly when $b$ is a norm from $K_v(\sqrt a)$, and is always one if $a$ is square. Equivalently the conic $z^2=ax^2+by^2$ is isotropic. This proves symmetry. The [cyclic local norm index](cyclic-local-norm-index.md) makes the norm subgroup have index two for nonsquare $a$, proving bilinearity and nondegeneracy on local square classes. Also $(a,-a)_v=1$ and $(a,a)_v=(a,-1)_v$.
