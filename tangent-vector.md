# Tangent vector

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangent_vector)

A tangent vector to a smooth curve is the [derivative](derivative.md) of a parametrization of that curve. Tangent vectors to a surface form its tangent plane.
