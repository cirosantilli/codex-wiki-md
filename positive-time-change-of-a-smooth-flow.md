# Positive time change of a smooth flow

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth flow](smooth-flow.md)

Write $\rho=1/f$ and $t=\int_0^s\rho(\phi_r x)\,dr$. For an [Anosov stable bundle](stable-bundle-of-an-anosov-flow.md), the new stable directions are $v+\lambda_s(x,v)F(x)$, where $\lambda_s=\rho(x)^{-1}\int_0^\infty d\rho_{\phi_r x}(D\phi_rv)\,dr$. Exponential contraction makes this integral uniformly convergent. Differentiating the clock equation shows that its flow-direction coefficient after time $t$ is $\rho(\phi_sx)^{-1}\int_s^\infty d\rho(D\phi_rv)\,dr$, which decays exponentially and has the same graph form at the new point. Negative-time integrals give the unstable graph. Compactness and positivity of $f$ compare old and new times uniformly, preserving the [Anosov flow](anosov-flow.md) property.
