# Parton on-shell delta identity

↑ **Parent:** [Massless collinear parton approximation](massless-collinear-parton-approximation.md)

In the leading [massless collinear parton approximation](massless-collinear-parton-approximation.md), $P^2=0$ and $q^2=-Q^2$ imply $(\xi P+q)^2=2\xi P\cdot q-Q^2$. With [Bjorken x](bjorken-x.md) $x=Q^2/(2P\cdot q)$ and $P\cdot q>0$, the [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md) change-of-variable formula yields the displayed identity. The positive-energy branch of the final [parton](parton.md) also gives $\delta(E_q+\xi E_P-E_k)/E_k=2\delta((E_q+\xi E_P)^2-E_k^2)$ locally on physical support. Keeping target mass instead introduces $\xi^2M^2$ and changes the root and Jacobian.
