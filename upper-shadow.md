# Upper shadow

↑ **Parent:** [Set family shadow](set-family-shadow.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_shadow)

The upper shadow of $\mathcal A\subseteq[n]^{(r)}$ is

$$
\nabla\mathcal A=\{B\in[n]^{(r+1)}:A\subset B\text{ for some }A\in\mathcal A\}.
$$

Among families of fixed size, an initial [lexicographic](lexicographic-order.md) segment minimizes its upper shadow.
