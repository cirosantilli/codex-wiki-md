# Absorption (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Physical chemistry](physical-chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absorption_(chemistry))

[Absorption](absorption-chemistry.md) takes a [chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md) into a material's bulk, as when [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) dissolves in [water](water.md). [Adsorption](adsorption.md) instead concerns accumulation at a boundary. Bulk uptake and interfacial accumulation can coexist; total uptake alone does not separate their contributions. This use of absorption concerns material uptake, whereas [absorbance](absorbance.md) describes attenuation of [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md).
