# Moving-target interception rate

↑ **Parent:** [Collision mechanics](collision-mechanics.md)

An ideal one-dimensional stream is emitted at a constant rate $\lambda$, with every projectile moving at the same ground-frame speed v. A target at x(t), moving in the same direction with speed $U<v$, intercepts projectiles emitted at retarded time $s=t-x(t)/v$. Differentiating the count $N=\lambda s$ gives the displayed rate, after the stream's leading edge has arrived. Thus constant emission rate does not imply constant impact rate. Combining this flux with the [momentum](momentum-split.md) transferred in each impact gives the mean [force](force-split.md) on the target.
