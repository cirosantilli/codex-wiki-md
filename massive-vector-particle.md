# Massive vector particle

↑ **Parent:** [Massive particle little group](massive-particle-little-group.md)

A massive spin-one particle transforms as a vector under its [massive particle little group](massive-particle-little-group.md) $SO(D-1)$. Its $D-1$ polarizations are orthogonal to its timelike [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). Under transverse $SO(D-2)$ rotations they split into a vector and a scalar.
