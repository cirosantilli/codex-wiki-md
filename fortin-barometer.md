# Fortin barometer

↑ **Parent:** [Cistern barometer](cistern-barometer.md)

Restoring the cistern surface to a fixed index makes the lower mercury level reproducible before the upper level is read.[§5.1, Fortin barometers](mercury-barometers-and-manometers-by-w-g-brombacher-and-others.md)
