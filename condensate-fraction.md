# Condensate fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Bose condensation in a three-dimensional harmonic trap](bose-condensation-in-a-three-dimensional-harmonic-trap.md)

The [condensate fraction](condensate-fraction.md) is the population $N_0$ of a macroscopically occupied [one-particle state](one-particle-state.md) divided by the total particle number $N$. For an interacting system, use a macroscopic [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of the one-particle [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md), rather than assume that the relevant mode is an undeformed single-particle trap [ground state](ground-state.md). The [condensate fraction](condensate-fraction.md) is not generally the fraction measured as [superfluid](superfluid.md) by a flow or rotation response.
