# Ray tracing (computer graphics)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer graphics](computer-graphics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_tracing_(computer_graphics))

Rendering by tracing geometric [rays](ray-geometry.md) to surfaces, selecting visible intersections and evaluating illumination. Primary [rays](ray-geometry.md) determine camera visibility, while [shadow rays](shadow-ray.md) test visibility of light sources; recursive variants trace reflected and transmitted continuations.
