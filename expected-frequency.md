# Expected frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Frequency table](frequency-table.md)

The [expected value](expected-value-split.md) of the number of observations in a category under a specified [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md). For $n$ observations with common category [probability](probability.md) $p_j$, [linearity of expectation](linearity-of-expectation.md) gives $E_j=np_j$; the calculation does not itself require [independence](independent-random-variables.md). In a [contingency table](contingency-table.md), fitting [independence](independent-random-variables.md) gives $E_{ij}=n_{i+}n_{+j}/n$. These are model-based counts, which may be fractional, rather than an additional set of observed responses. [Pearson's chi-squared test](pearson-s-chi-squared-test.md) compares observed counts with their corresponding [expected frequencies](expected-frequency.md).
