# Atmospheric internal gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

An atmospheric internal gravity wave is an [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) propagating through a stably stratified atmosphere. Flow across mountains can generate nearly stationary waves whose vertical propagation is controlled by the wind and buoyancy-frequency profiles.
