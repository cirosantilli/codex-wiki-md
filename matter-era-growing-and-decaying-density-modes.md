# Matter-era growing and decaying density modes

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological density perturbation](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md)

During [matter domination](matter-domination.md) in a flat [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), the [cosmological continuity equation](cosmological-continuity-equation.md) gives $\rho\propto a^{-3}$, and the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $a\propto\tau^2$ and $8\pi G\rho a^2=12/\tau^2$. The [CDM density equation in a matter-radiation universe](cdm-density-equation-in-a-matter-radiation-universe.md), with radiation neglected, is therefore $\delta''+2\delta'/\tau-6\delta/\tau^2=0$. Its [Euler-Cauchy equation](euler-cauchy-equation.md) powers satisfy $(p-2)(p+3)=0$, proving the displayed general solution. The growing [density contrast](density-contrast.md) is proportional to the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md), while the decaying mode is proportional to $a^{-3/2}$. This all-scale form uses the CDM-comoving [synchronous gauge](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md); other density slices require their corresponding gauge transformation.
