# Conjugate point to a spacelike surface

↑ **Parent:** [Null focusing theorem](null-focusing-theorem.md)

A [conjugate point to a spacelike surface](conjugate-point-to-a-spacelike-surface.md) occurs where normal [geodesics](geodesic.md) develop a focal degeneracy: the transverse [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) map from initial points on the surface ceases to be invertible. Collapse of the transverse area makes the [null expansion](null-expansion.md) diverge. Beyond a first such point, the normal [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) cannot continue as a generator of the [achronal boundary](achronal-boundary.md) of the surface's future.
