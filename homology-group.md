# Homology group

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)

A homology group is one of the [abelian groups](abelian-group.md) produced by a [homology](homology-split.md) theory. Its elements are [homology classes](homology-class.md), represented in singular homology by cycles modulo boundaries.
