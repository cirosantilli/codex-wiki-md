# Quotient metric

↑ **Parent:** [Category of metric spaces and non-expansive maps](category-of-metric-spaces-and-non-expansive-maps.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotient_metric)

The quotient metric is the largest metric on a quotient set for which the quotient map is [non-expansive](non-expansive-map.md). It is obtained by taking infima of lengths of chains that may jump freely within equivalence classes.
