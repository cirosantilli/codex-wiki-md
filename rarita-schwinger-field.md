# Rarita-Schwinger field

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

A vector-spinor describes a spin-three-halves field after its lower-spin components are removed by constraints and, in the massless theory, gauge equivalence. The massless equation is invariant under $\psi_\mu\mapsto\psi_\mu+\partial_\mu\eta$. Gamma-trace gauge and the field equation give $\gamma\cdot\psi=\partial\cdot\psi=\not\partial\psi_\mu=0$. Only helicities $\pm3/2$ remain. A four-dimensional Majorana field has two physical polarizations; a complex field also has independent antiparticle modes. The [gravitino](gravitino.md) is the [supergravity](supergravity.md) realization of this field.
