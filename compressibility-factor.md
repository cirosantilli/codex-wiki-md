# Compressibility factor

↑ **Parent:** [Equation of state](equation-of-state.md)

The [compressibility factor](compressibility-factor.md) is the ratio $p/(nk_BT)$, where $n$ is [number density](number-density.md). A classical [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) has $Z=1$. The [virial expansion](virial-expansion.md) writes $Z=1+B_2n+B_3n^2+\cdots$ for a dilute [gas](gas.md); this dimensionless ratio is distinct from [isothermal compressibility](isothermal-compressibility.md), which is a response [derivative](derivative.md) with units of inverse [pressure](pressure-split.md).
