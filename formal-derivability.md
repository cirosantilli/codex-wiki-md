# Formal derivability

↑ **Parent:** [Formal proof](formal-proof.md)

A conclusion is formally derivable from premises when a permitted [formal proof](formal-proof.md) exists with those premises as its undischarged assumptions. This is a syntactic relation, distinct from [semantic entailment](semantic-entailment.md).
