# Interpolatory quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md)

An [interpolatory quadrature](interpolatory-quadrature.md) approximates a [definite integral](definite-integral.md) by exactly integrating the [interpolation polynomial](interpolation-polynomial.md) through prescribed sampled values. For distinct [quadrature nodes](quadrature-node.md) $x_0,\ldots,x_n$, its [quadrature weights](quadrature-weight.md) are $W_j=\int_a^b\ell_j(x)\,dx$, where $\ell_j$ are the [Lagrange cardinal polynomials](lagrange-cardinal-polynomial.md). It is exact for all [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $n$, because [polynomial interpolation](polynomial-interpolation.md) reproduces them. [Newton-Cotes quadrature](newton-cotes-quadrature.md) adds equal spacing of the [quadrature nodes](quadrature-node.md).
