# Drum-frequency dimensional scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md)

A geometrically similar taut membrane of [radius](radius.md) $r$, uniform isotropic [tension](tension.md) $T$ per unit [length](length.md) and areal [mass density](density.md) $\sigma$ has [normal mode](normal-mode.md) [frequencies](frequency.md) $f=C r^{-1}\sqrt{T/\sigma}$. Matching [mass](mass.md), [length](length.md) and time dimensions fixes these exponents; the dimensionless constant depends on the [normal mode](normal-mode.md) and boundary shape. The result does not apply unchanged when bending stiffness adds an independent material parameter.
