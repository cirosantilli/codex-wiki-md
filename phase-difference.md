# Phase difference

↑ **Parent:** [Phase (waves)](phase-waves.md)

The difference of two [wave phases](phase-waves.md) at the same place and time, considered modulo $2\pi$. It determines whether two same-frequency [oscillations](oscillation.md) reinforce, cancel, or are in [phase quadrature](phase-quadrature.md).
