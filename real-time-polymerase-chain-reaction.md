# Real-time polymerase chain reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real-time_polymerase_chain_reaction)

[Real-time polymerase chain reaction](real-time-polymerase-chain-reaction.md) monitors [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) during cycling. With validated specificity, calibration and [PCR efficiency](pcr-efficiency.md), that record supports estimating initial template amount; this quantitative use is called [qPCR](real-time-polymerase-chain-reaction.md). Ordinary endpoint [PCR amplification](polymerase-chain-reaction.md) examines accumulated product afterwards. [qPCR](real-time-polymerase-chain-reaction.md) need not start from [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md), and [RT-PCR](reverse-transcription-polymerase-chain-reaction.md) need not monitor [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md). Combining both operations gives [RT-qPCR](rt-qpcr.md).[Real-Time PCR Basics: amplification monitoring and quantification](real-time-pcr-qpcr-basics-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)
