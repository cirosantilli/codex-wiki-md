# BBGKY hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

The hierarchy obtained by integrating the $N$-particle [Hamiltonian Liouville equation](hamiltonian-liouville-equation.md) over successively fewer particle coordinates. Each marginal equation involves the next marginal, so an autonomous one-particle equation requires a closure. For a [pair potential](pair-potential.md), its first equation couples the [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) to the [two-particle distribution function](two-particle-distribution-function.md). Number-normalized factorial marginals and unit-normalized probability marginals have different explicit combinatorial factors.
