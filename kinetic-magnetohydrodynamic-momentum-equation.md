# Kinetic magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic magnetohydrodynamics](kinetic-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

In [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md), the equation in the title uses the gyrotropic [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md) $\mathbf P=p_\perp\mathbf I+(p_\parallel-p_\perp)\mathbf b\mathbf b$. This allows [pressure anisotropy](pressure-anisotropy.md). Its magnetic force contains both [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) and [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md).
