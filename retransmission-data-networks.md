# Retransmission (data networks)

↑ **Parent:** [Transport protocol](transport-protocol.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retransmission_(data_networks))

[Data retransmission](retransmission-data-networks.md) sends data again to recover from loss or detected corruption. [Transport sequence numbers](transport-sequence-number.md) let a receiver recognize a duplicate if the original transmission arrived but its [acknowledgement](acknowledgement.md) was lost.
