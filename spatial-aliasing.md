# Spatial aliasing

↑ **Parent:** [Aliasing](aliasing.md)

Ambiguity between spatial oscillations sampled on a grid. At positions $x_j=j\Delta x$, the factors $e^{ikx_j}$ and $e^{i(k+2\pi m/\Delta x)x_j}$ agree for every [integer](integer-split.md) $m$. Spatial wavenumbers outside $|k|<\pi/\Delta x$ therefore cannot be distinguished without additional assumptions. For a traveling event of temporal [frequency](frequency.md) $f$ and apparent speed $v_{\mathrm{app}}$, the associated criterion is $\Delta x<v_{\mathrm{app}}/(2f)$, with a margin for filtering. Temporal oversampling does not remove this spatial ambiguity.
