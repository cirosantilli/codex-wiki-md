# Gas slip correction to Stokes drag

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes's law](stokes-s-law.md)

The [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) factor $C$ compares a corrected [linear drag](linear-drag.md) resistance with the continuum [Stokes's law](stokes-s-law.md): dividing by $C>1$ reduces the resistance. The [oil drop charge readout and calibration](oil-drop-charge-readout-and-calibration.md) article explains why this matters for [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) inference.
