# Liquid water

↑ **Parent:** [Water](water.md)

The [liquid](liquid.md) phase of [water](water.md). Its [density](density.md) is much greater than that of dilute [water vapor](water-vapor.md), allowing the [liquid](liquid.md)'s [specific volume](specific-volume.md) to be neglected in the usual integrated [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md).
