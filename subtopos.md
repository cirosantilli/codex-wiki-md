# Subtopos

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric embedding](geometric-embedding.md)

A [subtopos](subtopos.md) is a [Grothendieck topos](grothendieck-topos.md) embedded by a [geometric embedding](geometric-embedding.md), considered up to equivalence over the ambient topos. On a [site](site-category-theory.md) $(\mathcal C,J)$, [subtoposes](subtopos.md) correspond to Grothendieck topologies $J'\supseteq J$. On a [classifying topos](classifying-topos.md) this is the [duality between geometric quotients and subtoposes](duality-between-geometric-quotients-and-subtoposes.md).
