# Most powerful test

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical test](statistical-test.md)

At level $\alpha$, a most powerful test against a specified simple alternative maximises [statistical power](statistical-power.md) among [statistical tests](statistical-test.md) whose [size of a statistical test](size-of-a-statistical-test.md) is at most $\alpha$. The [Neyman-Pearson lemma](neyman-pearson-lemma.md) constructs it by a [likelihood ratio](likelihood-ratio.md) threshold. A [uniformly most powerful test](uniformly-most-powerful-test.md) is most powerful simultaneously against every member of the specified alternative family; maximising power at one alternative alone need not give uniform optimality.
