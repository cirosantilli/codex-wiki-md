# Simple module of a finite-dimensional commutative complex algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Simple module over a commutative ring](simple-module-over-a-commutative-ring.md)

For a finite-dimensional commutative complex [algebra](algebra-split.md), a [simple module](irreducible-module.md) is a cyclic quotient of the algebra and hence finite-dimensional. Every action map is a module endomorphism, since the algebra is commutative. The complex [Schur's lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) makes each action scalar, so every vector subspace is a submodule; simplicity forces dimension one. The finite-dimensional hypothesis cannot simply be deleted: $\mathbb C(t)$ acting on itself is a simple module of infinite complex dimension.
