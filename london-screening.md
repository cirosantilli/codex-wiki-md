# London screening

↑ **Parent:** [London equations](london-equations.md)

Combining the static [London equations](london-equations.md) with the [magnetostatic Ampère law](magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) and $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0$ gives the displayed bulk equation for a uniform superconducting carrier density. A planar boundary gives exponentially decaying field on the [London penetration depth](london-penetration-depth.md) scale; boundary geometry and vortices require their corresponding field solutions.
