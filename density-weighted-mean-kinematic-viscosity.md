# Density-weighted mean kinematic viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md)

For a vertically stratified [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) with [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $\Sigma=\int\rho\,dz$, the density-weighted mean [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) is $\bar\nu=\Sigma^{-1}\int\rho\nu\,dz$. In an [alpha disk](alpha-disk.md) with $\nu=\alpha p/(\rho\Omega)$, this becomes $\bar\nu=\alpha(\Sigma\Omega)^{-1}\int p\,dz$.
