# Cost of complexity

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

A proposed reduction in the rate of adaptation when [mutations](mutation.md) affect more independently selected dimensions. In [Fisher's geometric model](fisher-s-geometric-model.md), a large random step can improve one component while damaging others. The prediction depends on the scaling of mutational effects and need not follow from a simple count of organismal traits.
