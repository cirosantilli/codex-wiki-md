<h1 id="higman-s-embedding-theorem">Higman's embedding theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Recursive presentation of a group](recursive-presentation-of-a-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higman's_embedding_theorem)

A [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) embeds in a [finitely presented group](finitely-presented-group.md) if and only if it has a [recursive presentation of a group](recursive-presentation-of-a-group.md). The necessity follows by enumerating proofs that substituted generator words are trivial in the larger [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md). The sufficiency is the embedding theorem; it also applies to effectively countably generated recursive presentations after an effective two-generator embedding. This is the theorem of [Subgroups of finitely presented groups](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1961.0132).
