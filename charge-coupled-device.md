# Charge-coupled device

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charge-coupled_device)

A charge-coupled device stores photoelectrons in potential wells and transfers the charge through clocked stages to an output amplifier. The transfer differs from independently addressed pixels in a [hybrid infrared detector](hybrid-infrared-detector.md).

In an imaging [charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md), absorbed [photons](photon.md) produce mobile [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md) in a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md); collection stores an [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) packet associated with each [optical detector pixel](optical-detector-pixel.md). Closely spaced [MOS capacitors](mos-capacitor.md) allow applied [electric potential differences](electric-potential-difference.md) to move the packet from site to site. [Willard Sterling Boyle](willard-sterling-boyle.md) and [George Elwood Smith](george-elwood-smith.md) described storage, transfer and detection as distinct operations, including imaging as one application.[Charge Coupled Semiconductor Devices, April 1970, p. 587, abstract and opening device-concept paragraphs; scanned original hosted by VTDA](charge-coupled-semiconductor-devices-by-willard-sterling-boyle-and-george-elwood-smith.md)

The useful architectural trick is that many [optical detector pixels](optical-detector-pixel.md) can send their [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) to a shared output [electronic amplifier](electronic-amplifier.md). This makes repeatable conversion possible but makes fidelity depend on every transfer along the route. An [active-pixel sensor](active-pixel-sensor.md) made with [CMOS](cmos.md) technology instead uses circuitry at pixels to convert or buffer their signal and select it for readout; independent pixel addressing does not require transporting each packet through the whole image. This compares readout architectures, not whether either technology can use a [photodiode](photodiode.md).[NASA Spinoff, 2017, NASA Technology, pixel-to-pixel transfer and amplification at individual pixels](cmos-sensors-enable-phone-cameras-hd-video-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)
