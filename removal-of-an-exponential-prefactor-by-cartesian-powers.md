# Removal of an exponential prefactor by Cartesian powers

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian powers of cap sets](cartesian-powers-of-cap-sets.md)

Suppose every [cap set](cap-set.md) in $\mathbb F_3^n$ has [cardinality](cardinality.md) at most $K\theta^n$, with $K$ independent of $n$. Applying this to the $k$-fold [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) of a fixed [cap set](cap-set.md) $A$ gives $|A|^k\leq K\theta^{nk}$. Taking $k$th roots and letting $k$ tend to infinity proves $|A|\leq\theta^n$. More generally this argument applies to any class of finite objects closed under products with multiplicative size and additive dimension.
