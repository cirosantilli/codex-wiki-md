<h1 id="berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-transition">Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lower critical dimension](lower-critical-dimension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless_transition)

The Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition in the two-dimensional [XY model](xy-model.md) separates a low-temperature phase with algebraically decaying correlations from a high-temperature phase with unbound vortices and exponentially decaying correlations. It does not produce conventional long-range order.

How can something resist a twist when its orientation never settles down? The distinction is between [long-range order](long-range-order.md), a nonzero limiting [correlation function](correlation-function.md), and [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md), a [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) cost for a slow spatial twist. In a uniform two-dimensional [XY model](xy-model.md) at positive [temperature](temperature-split.md), smooth [spin waves](spin-wave.md) can erase the first while leaving the second finite. [Vortex-antivortex binding](vortex-antivortex-binding.md) then decides whether that resistance survives on arbitrarily large scales.

[Vadim Lvovich Berezinskii](vadim-lvovich-berezinskii.md) developed an earlier description of two-dimensional order. [John Michael Kosterlitz](john-michael-kosterlitz.md) and [D. J. Thouless](david-james-thouless.md) independently developed the defect mechanism; Kosterlitz's 2016 lecture explicitly acknowledges their initial ignorance of Berezinskii's work. The central step was to include rare [vortices](phase-vortex.md) alongside smooth [spin waves](spin-wave.md).[2016 Nobel lecture, pp. 89–94, sections 2–3; independent Berezinskii work, winding sectors and vortex renormalization](topological-defects-and-phase-transitions-by-john-michael-kosterlitz.md)

The [Mermin-Wagner theorem](mermin-wagner-theorem.md) concerns [continuous symmetry](continuous-symmetry.md) and sufficiently short-range interactions in the [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md). It does not say that every two-dimensional system is disordered in every sense, nor that every [continuous symmetry](continuous-symmetry.md) gives a [Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition](berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-transition.md). Here the [order parameter](order-parameter.md) has a circular phase, permitting integer [winding numbers](winding-number.md). [Finite-scale vortex renormalization](finite-scale-vortex-renormalization.md) explains why a finite sample can look coherent even when its infinite counterpart lacks [long-range order](long-range-order.md).
