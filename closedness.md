# Closedness

↑ **Parent:** [Closed set](closed-set.md)

The property of being a [closed subset](closed-set.md) in a specified ambient [topological space](topological-space.md). In a [metric space](metric-space.md), a subset has this property exactly when it contains the limits of all its convergent sequences whose limits lie in the ambient space.
