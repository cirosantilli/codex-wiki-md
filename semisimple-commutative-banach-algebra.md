# Semisimple commutative Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

A commutative [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) is semisimple when its [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) is zero. For a complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) this is equivalent to injectivity of the [Gelfand transform](gelfand-representation.md). This condition concerns the [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md); it does not assert a finite product decomposition as in finite-dimensional semisimple algebra theory.
