# Constant-equation-of-state conformal Riccati equation

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

For a [perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) with constant [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w$, use [conformal time](conformal-time.md) $dt=a\,d\tau$ and the [conformal Hubble parameter](conformal-hubble-parameter.md) $\mathcal H=a'/a=aH$. The [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives $\mathcal H'=a\ddot a=-4\pi Ga^2(1+3w)\rho/3$. The [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) eliminates $\rho$ and gives this [Riccati equation](riccati-equation.md). For positive [energy density](energy-density.md), $\alpha\ne0$ and appropriately shifted [conformal time](conformal-time.md), its usual branches are $\cot(\alpha\tau)$ for $k=1$, $1/(\alpha\tau)$ for $k=0$, and $\coth(\alpha\tau)$ for $k=-1$. Their [scale factors](scale-factor-cosmology.md) are proportional to $\sin^{1/\alpha}(\alpha\tau)$, $(\alpha\tau)^{1/\alpha}$ and $\sinh^{1/\alpha}(\alpha\tau)$ respectively on intervals where the bases are positive. At $\alpha=0$ the [conformal Hubble parameter](conformal-hubble-parameter.md) is constant and the separate solution is $a=Ae^{h\tau}$.
