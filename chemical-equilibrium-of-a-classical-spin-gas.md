# Chemical equilibrium of a classical spin gas

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md)

For a nondegenerate spin-one-half ideal gas with energies $\mp\mu_BB(\mathbf r)$, the two component [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) are $k_BT\ln(n_\pm/n_Q)\mp\mu_BB$. Diffusive and spin-conversion equilibrium require one spatially constant common chemical potential, provided spin conversion is allowed. Hence the displayed densities give total [number density](number-density.md) $n=2n_Qe^{\beta\mu}\cosh(\beta\mu_BB)$ and [magnetization](magnetization.md) $M=2\mu_Bn_Qe^{\beta\mu}\sinh(\beta\mu_BB)=n\mu_B\tanh(\beta\mu_BB)$. The high-temperature fixed-density limit is $M\simeq n\mu_B^2B/(k_BT)$, a [Curie law](curie-s-law.md). At fixed chemical potential the density itself varies with temperature, so the unnormalized magnetization need not have a pure $1/T$ dependence.
