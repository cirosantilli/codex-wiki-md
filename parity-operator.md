# Parity operator

↑ **Parent:** [Parity](parity.md)

The parity operator acts on a spatial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) by $(P\psi)(x)=\psi(-x)$ and satisfies $P^2=I$. For a [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) with an even potential and parity-invariant domain, $HP=PH$. A [nondegenerate energy eigenvalue](nondegenerate-energy-eigenvalue.md) has a one-dimensional [eigenspace](eigenspace.md), so its eigenfunction is an eigenvector of $P$ with eigenvalue $+1$ or $-1$.
