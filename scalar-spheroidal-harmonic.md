# Scalar spheroidal harmonic

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar wave separation in Kerr spacetime](scalar-wave-separation-in-kerr-spacetime.md)

A [scalar spheroidal harmonic](scalar-spheroidal-harmonic.md) is a regular angular solution of $(\sin\theta\,S')'/\sin\theta+[c^2\cos^2\theta-m^2/\sin^2\theta+\Lambda]S=0$; with $e^{im\phi}$ it is smooth at both axes. For real $c$, regularity gives discrete eigenvalues $\Lambda_{\ell m}(c)$. At $c=0$ it reduces to the angular part of a [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md), with $\Lambda=\ell(\ell+1)$. The sign of the $c^2$ term distinguishes the convention used here.
