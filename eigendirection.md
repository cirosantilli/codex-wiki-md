# Eigendirection

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenspace](eigenspace.md)

An [eigendirection](eigendirection.md) is the one-dimensional [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) spanned by an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md). Multiplying the [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) by a nonzero [scalar](scalar.md) leaves this direction unchanged. In a planar linear [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md), a negative real [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) gives a direction of exponential approach to the [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), while a positive one gives exponential departure. For a hyperbolic [saddle equilibrium](saddle-equilibrium.md), the nonlinear [stable manifold](stable-manifold.md) and [unstable manifold](unstable-manifold.md) are tangent to the corresponding [eigendirections](eigendirection.md).
