# Torsion-free abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian group](abelian-group.md)

An abelian group in which no nonzero element has finite order. [Free abelian groups](free-abelian-group.md) are torsion-free, but torsion-free groups need not be free, as the additive rationals show. This algebraic meaning differs from the torsion-free connection in differential geometry.
