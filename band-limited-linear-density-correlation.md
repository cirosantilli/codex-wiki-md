# Band-limited linear density correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md)

For an isotropic [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md) $P(k)=Ak$ on $0\leq k\leq k_{\max}$ and zero elsewhere, put $K=k_{\max}r$. Angular integration of the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) gives $\xi(r)=A(2\pi^2r)^{-1}\int_0^{k_{\max}}k^2\sin(kr)\,dk$, and two integrations by parts yield the displayed answer. Its apparent origin singularity is removable, with $\xi(0)=Ak_{\max}^4/(8\pi^2)$. The sharp cutoff creates oscillatory, including negative, correlations without violating positivity of the spectrum.
