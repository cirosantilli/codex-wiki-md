# Synchronous perfect-fluid density equation

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronous gauge in cosmology](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md)

For constant $P=w\rho$ and [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) $v_i=ik_i\theta$, [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) gives the displayed density equation in [synchronous gauge in cosmology](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md). Here $h$ is the trace of the spatial metric perturbation, and primes denote [conformal time](conformal-time.md). In particular cold baryons obey $\delta_b'=k^2\theta_b-h'/2$. A positive sign in front of $k^2\theta_b$ on the left contradicts this potential convention.
