# Burst nucleation

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleation](nucleation.md)

Formation of most nuclei within an interval short compared with subsequent particle growth. A common birth period can help narrow a [particle size distribution](particle-size-distribution.md) if mixing and later growth are sufficiently uniform and secondary [nucleation](nucleation.md) is suppressed.
