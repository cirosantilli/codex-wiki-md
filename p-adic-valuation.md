# P-adic valuation

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_valuation)

For a prime $p$ and a nonzero integer $N$, the $p$-adic valuation is

$$
v_p(N)=\max\{a\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}:p^a\mid N\}.
$$

It extends to nonzero rational numbers by $v_p(a/b)=v_p(a)-v_p(b)$.
