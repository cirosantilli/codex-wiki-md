# Euclidean rigidity of maximal asymptotic volume ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Bishop-Gromov inequality](bishop-gromov-inequality.md)

The [Bishop-Gromov inequality](bishop-gromov-inequality.md) forces every volume ratio to equal one if the asymptotic ratio is one. Equality in the radial Jacobian comparison forces the radial operator $A(X)=\nabla_X\partial_r$ of each distance sphere to be $r^{-1}I$ (it is the negative of the outward [shape operator](shape-operator.md) in the convention $S=-\nabla\nu$), by the traced [radial Riccati equation for distance spheres](radial-riccati-equation-for-distance-spheres.md) and equality in [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Any finite cut time would remove a positive cone of radial volume at a later radius, contradicting equality. Thus the [Riemannian exponential map](exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md) is global and one-to-one, and the polar metric is $dr^2+r^2g_{S^{n-1}}$, the Euclidean metric.
