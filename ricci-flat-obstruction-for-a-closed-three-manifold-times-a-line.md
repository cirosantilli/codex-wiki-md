# Ricci-flat obstruction for a closed three-manifold times a line

↑ **Parent:** [Cheeger-Gromoll splitting theorem](cheeger-gromoll-splitting-theorem.md)

If a connected [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) $M^3$ has a noncontractible [universal cover](universal-cover.md), then $M\times\mathbb R$ has no complete metric making it a [Ricci-flat Riemannian manifold](ricci-flat-riemannian-manifold.md). The product has two ends and hence contains a [line in a Riemannian manifold](line-in-a-riemannian-manifold.md) for any complete metric. The [Cheeger-Gromoll splitting theorem](cheeger-gromoll-splitting-theorem.md) would split it isometrically as $N^3\times\mathbb R$. Its three-dimensional factor is Ricci-flat and therefore flat by [three-dimensional curvature from the Ricci tensor](three-dimensional-curvature-from-the-ricci-tensor.md). The complete simply connected cover of the product would be Euclidean four-space. But that cover is $\widetilde M\times\mathbb R$, which retracts onto $\widetilde M$, contradicting noncontractibility.
