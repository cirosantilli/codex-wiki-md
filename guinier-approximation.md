# Guinier approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md)

The low-wave-number approximation relating the scattering form factor of an isotropic dilute population to its [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md). Fitting its range requires $qR_g$ sufficiently small and negligible or corrected interparticle scattering.
