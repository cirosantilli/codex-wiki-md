# Hydraulic jump

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulic_jump)

A hydraulic jump connects shallow [supercritical flow](supercritical-flow.md) to deeper [subcritical flow](subcritical-flow.md) while conserving discharge and momentum and dissipating mechanical energy. Its [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) uses the channel's actual area and integrated [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md), which depend on cross-sectional geometry.
