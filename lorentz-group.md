# Lorentz group

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorentz_group)

The Lorentz group consists of real matrices preserving the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md): $\Lambda^T\eta\Lambda=\eta$.
