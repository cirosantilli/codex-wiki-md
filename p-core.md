# p-core

↑ **Parent:** [Fitting subgroup](fitting-subgroup.md)

The largest normal [p-subgroup](p-subgroup.md) of a [finite group](finite-group.md). Products of normal [p-subgroups](p-subgroup.md) are again normal [p-subgroups](p-subgroup.md), since $|AB|=|A||B|/|A\cap B|$. Thus the subgroup generated by all such subgroups is itself a [p-subgroup](p-subgroup.md). It is a [characteristic subgroup](characteristic-subgroup.md).
