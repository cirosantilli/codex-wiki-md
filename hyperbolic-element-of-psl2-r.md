<h1 id="hyperbolic-element-of-psl2-r">Hyperbolic element of PSL2(R)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [PSL2(R)](psl2-r.md)

A real [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) represented by $A\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ with $|\operatorname{tr}A|>2$. It has two fixed ideal boundary points and translates along the [geodesic](geodesic.md) joining them; its [hyperbolic translation length](hyperbolic-translation-length.md) is $2\operatorname{arcosh}(|\operatorname{tr}A|/2)$. Conjugating by a real [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) puts it in the form $z\mapsto e^\ell z$ with $\ell>0$; its axis is then the imaginary axis.
