# Peebles primordial-plasma predictions

↑ **Parent:** [Physical cosmology](physical-cosmology.md)

[P. J. E. Peebles](p-james-peebles.md) made the connection between microscopic processes and cosmological observables quantitative. His 1968 paper treated [cosmological recombination](recombination-cosmology.md) as a kinetic problem. Capturing an [electron](electron.md) directly into the [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [ground state](ground-state.md) releases a [photon](photon.md) that can ionize another atom. Net neutralization depends on escape of resonance radiation through [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) and on [two-photon spontaneous emission](two-photon-spontaneous-emission.md) from the $2s$ state. An equilibrium [Saha equation](saha-ionization-equation.md) alone does not give that history.[Peebles, Recombination of the Primeval Plasma (1968), §§IIb–IId, pp.3–6](recombination-of-the-primeval-plasma-by-p-j-e-peebles.md)

This matters for an acoustic prediction because the [hydrogen ionization fraction](hydrogen-ionization-fraction.md) fixes the supply of free [electrons](electron.md) for [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md). In physical units the scattering rate per [photon](photon.md) is $\Gamma_T=n_e\sigma_Tc$. The [cosmological visibility function](cosmological-visibility-function.md) describes the distribution of last-scattering times. Thus [atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md) helps determine when the [photon-baryon acoustic oscillator](photon-baryon-acoustic-oscillator.md) is read out, as well as how rapidly [tight coupling](tight-coupling-approximation.md) fails.

In 1970 [P. J. E. Peebles](p-james-peebles.md) and [J. T. Yu](j-t-yu.md) followed [adiabatic cosmological perturbations](adiabatic-initial-conditions.md) by numerically integrating the [photon](photon.md) collision equation through [cosmological recombination](recombination-cosmology.md). Their calculation connected acoustic evolution and dissipation to residual matter structure and microwave brightness fluctuations. It did not assume that a perfect [photon-baryon fluid](photon-baryon-fluid.md) remained valid throughout last scattering. Their illustrative cosmological models and normalization were historical choices, rather than the present [Lambda-CDM model](lambda-cdm-model.md); their predicted brightness variance versus angular resolution should not be relabelled as a modern measured $C_\ell$ spectrum.[Peebles and Yu, Primeval Adiabatic Perturbation in an Expanding Universe (1970), pp.815–816; §VI, pp.829–833, Figs.4–9](primeval-adiabatic-perturbation-in-an-expanding-universe-by-p-j-e-peebles-and-j-t-yu.md)

The continuing idea is a forward prediction: specify [initial conditions](initial-condition.md), evolve the coupled matter and radiation, project onto the sky, and compare the result with an instrument's readout. [Coherent initial phases are needed for acoustic variance peaks](coherent-initial-phases-are-needed-for-acoustic-variance-peaks.md) explains why a collection of oscillating modes does not automatically produce a peaked [power spectrum](power-spectrum.md). The [BOOMERANG acoustic-peak measurement](boomerang-acoustic-peak-measurement.md) provides a later observational test of this physical programme.
