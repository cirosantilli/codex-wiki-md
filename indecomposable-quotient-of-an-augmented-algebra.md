# Indecomposable quotient of an augmented algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Graded algebra](graded-algebra.md)

For an augmented graded algebra $A$, its positive augmentation ideal $A^+$ has indecomposable quotient $Q(A)=A^+/(A^+)^2$. For a [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md) $(\Lambda V,d)$, this quotient is $V$ with zero differential because every $dv$ is decomposable. The map $H^+(\Lambda V,d)\to V$ induced by projection is dual to the rational [Hurewicz homomorphism](hurewicz-homomorphism.md) under the finite-type model identification.
