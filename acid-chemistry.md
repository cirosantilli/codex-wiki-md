# Acid (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Acid-base chemistry](acid-base-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acid)

A [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md) can donate a [proton](proton.md), or accept an [electron pair](electron-pair.md) to form a [covalent bond](covalent-bond.md). The [pH](ph.md) and [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md) examples below use the former meaning. See [IUPAC Gold Book, acid](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/A00071).
