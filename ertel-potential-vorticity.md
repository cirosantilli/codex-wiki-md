# Ertel potential vorticity

↑ **Parent:** [Potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md)

Ertel potential vorticity is the scalar product of [absolute vorticity](absolute-vorticity.md) with the gradient of a materially conserved stratifying scalar. In [Boussinesq flow](boussinesq-approximation.md) with buoyancy $b$,

$$
Q=\boldsymbol\omega_a\mathbin{\cdot}\nabla b.
$$

It is materially conserved by inviscid, adiabatic motion.
