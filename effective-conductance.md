# Effective conductance

↑ **Parent:** [Effective resistance](effective-resistance.md)

The effective conductance between two terminals is the reciprocal of their [effective resistance](effective-resistance.md). Under a unit voltage difference it equals the total current flowing from one terminal to the other.
