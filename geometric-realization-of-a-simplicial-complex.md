# Geometric realization of a simplicial complex

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md)

Take one copy of the standard geometric [simplex](simplex.md) $\Delta^\sigma$ for every face $\sigma$ of an [abstract simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) $K$, label its vertices by $\sigma$, and identify equally labelled common faces. Give the result the [quotient topology](quotient-topology.md) from the disjoint union of all these simplexes. Equivalently, a point is a finitely supported family of [barycentric coordinates](barycentric-coordinate-system.md) $\lambda_v\geq0$ with $\sum_v\lambda_v=1$ and $\{v:\lambda_v>0\}\in\Sigma$.

The [weak topology of a simplicial realization](weak-topology-of-a-simplicial-realization.md) makes $U$ open precisely when $U\cap|\sigma|$ is open in every closed simplex. This definition determines the topology even for infinite complexes, when a [metric realization of a simplicial complex](metric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) may give a different topology.[Definitions I.17–I.18, printed pp. 8–9: labelled simplexes and gluing of their faces](topology-and-groups-by-marc-lackenby.md)
