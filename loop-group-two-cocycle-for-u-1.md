<h1 id="loop-group-two-cocycle-for-u-1">Loop group two-cocycle for U(1)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Positive energy representation of the loop group of U(1)](positive-energy-representation-of-the-loop-group-of-u-1.md)

Write a loop as $z^w e^{ih}$ with $h$ smooth real periodic and $h_0$ its mean. For the level-one representation with modes filled below zero and $\omega(h,k)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}h'k$, choose implementers $U(w,h)=S^w e^{iA(h)}$, where $S$ increases charge by one, $SQS^*=Q-I$, and $[A(h),A(k)]=i\omega(h,k)I$. Their product differs from $U(w+v,h+k)$ by the displayed [two-cocycle](two-cocycle.md). Its phase is bilinear, which verifies the cocycle identity; integer $v$ makes it independent of the $2\pi$ ambiguity in $h_0$. Rephasing implementers changes it by a [group coboundary](group-coboundary.md).
