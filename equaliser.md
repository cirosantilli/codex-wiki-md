# Equaliser

↑ **Parent:** [Finite limit](finite-limit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equaliser_(mathematics))

The equalizer of parallel arrows $f,g:A\rightrightarrows B$ is a universal arrow $e:E\to A$ satisfying $fe=ge$.
