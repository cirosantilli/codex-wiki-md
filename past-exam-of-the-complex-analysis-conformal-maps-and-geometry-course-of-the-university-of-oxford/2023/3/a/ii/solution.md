<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [concentric normalization of an eccentric annulus](../../../../../concentric-normalization-of-an-eccentric-annulus.md) here uses

$$
T(z)=\frac{z-1/2}{1-z/2}.
$$

This [Möbius transformation](../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) preserves the unit circle and sends the inner circle's real endpoints $0,4/5$ to $-1/2,1/2$. Its image circle is symmetric about the real axis and hence is $|w|=1/2$. Equivalently, expanding $|T(z)|^2=1/4$ gives $|z|^2=(4/5)\operatorname{Re}z$, precisely the inner-circle equation. Thus $T$ sends the domain to $1/2<|w|<1$. [Conformal invariance of harmonic measure](../../../../../conformal-invariance-of-harmonic-measure.md) and [i](../i.md) give

$$
\boxed{\omega_\Omega(z,\mathbb T)=\frac{\log(2|T(z)|)}{\log2}=1+\frac{\log\left|\frac{z-1/2}{1-z/2}\right|}{\log2}}.
$$

The zero of $T$ lies in the removed inner disk, so this logarithm is harmonic throughout $\Omega$.
