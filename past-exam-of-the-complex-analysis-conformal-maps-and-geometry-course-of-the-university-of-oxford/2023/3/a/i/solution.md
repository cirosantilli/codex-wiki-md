<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The radial [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md) with value zero at $|z|=r$ and one at $|z|=1$ is

$$
\boxed{\omega_{A(r,1)}(z,\mathbb T)=\frac{\log(|z|/r)}{\log(1/r)}}.
$$

Indeed $\log|z|$ is harmonic away from zero, and the displayed expression has the required boundary values. Uniqueness of the [Dirichlet problem](../../../../../dirichlet-problem.md) identifies it as the [harmonic measure of an annulus](../../../../../harmonic-measure-of-an-annulus.md).
