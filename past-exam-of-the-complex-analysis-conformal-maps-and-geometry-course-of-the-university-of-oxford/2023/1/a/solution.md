<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $a\in\mathbb D$, the [automorphism of the unit disk](../../../../automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md) $T_a(z)=(z-a)/(1-\bar a z)$ sends $a$ to zero. The [holomorphic function](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) $F=T_{f(a)}\circ f\circ T_a^{-1}$ maps the [unit disc](../../../../unit-disc.md) into itself and satisfies $F(0)=0$. The [Schwarz lemma](../../../../schwarz-lemma.md) therefore gives $|F(w)|\leq|w|$. Evaluate at $w=T_a(b)$ to obtain the [Schwarz-Pick theorem](../../../../schwarz-pick-theorem.md):

$$
\boxed{\left|\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{1-f(b)\overline{f(a)}}\right|\leq\left|\frac{b-a}{1-b\bar a}\right|}.
$$

The denominators do not vanish because both their products have [modulus](../../../../modulus.md) less than one.
