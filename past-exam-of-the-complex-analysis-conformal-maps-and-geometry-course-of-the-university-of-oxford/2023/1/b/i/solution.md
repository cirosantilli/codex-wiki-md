↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Koebe growth theorem](../../../../../koebe-growth-theorem.md) states that every [normalized univalent function](../../../../../normalized-univalent-function.md) $\phi\in\mathcal S$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{\frac{|z|}{(1+|z|)^2}\leq|\phi(z)|\leq\frac{|z|}{(1-|z|)^2}\qquad(z\in\mathbb D)}.
$$

Both bounds are sharp. Rotations of the [Koebe function](../../../../../koebe-function.md), $\phi_\theta(z)=z/(1-e^{i\theta}z)^2$, attain them along suitable radial directions.
