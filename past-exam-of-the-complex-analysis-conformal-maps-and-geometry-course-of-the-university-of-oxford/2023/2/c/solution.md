<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Regard the upper half-disc, with cyclic boundary vertices $-1,0,x,1$, as a [conformal quadrilateral](../../../../conformal-quadrilateral.md). Map it to a rectangle of width $w$ and height $h$, so the boundary arcs $[-1,0]$ and $[x,1]$ become its vertical sides. The rectangle formulas for the [conformal modulus of a quadrilateral](../../../../conformal-modulus-of-a-quadrilateral.md) give

$$
d=\frac wh,\qquad d_*=\frac hw,\qquad\boxed{d\,d_*=1}.
$$

The remaining two boundary arcs, $(0,x)$ and the semicircle, become the horizontal sides. Thus these are precisely the dual joining families in the same quadrilateral.
