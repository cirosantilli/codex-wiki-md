↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the complex analysis: conformal maps and geometry course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source: [released examination PDF, pages 349–352](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=349). Solutions follow the numbering of the 6 June 2023 paper. In 2(d), the depicted domain is interpreted as a simply connected Jordan domain and the separating curves are boundary-to-boundary crosscuts. These topological details matter. The square-root construction in 1(b) is the odd transform of a general normalized univalent map, rather than one fixed extremal Koebe function.
