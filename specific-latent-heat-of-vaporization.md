# Specific latent heat of vaporization

↑ **Parent:** [Latent heat](latent-heat.md)

The [energy](energy-split.md) per unit [mass](mass.md) required to change a saturated [liquid](liquid.md) to vapour at a stated [pressure](pressure-split.md) and coexistence [temperature](temperature-split.md). In a steam-condensation [calorimetry](calorimetry-split.md) experiment, sensible cooling of the condensate must be subtracted from the measured receiver heat gain.
