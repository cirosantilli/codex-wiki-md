# Density-weighted mean

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted arithmetic mean](weighted-arithmetic-mean.md)

For positive mean [density](density.md), this average weights a [velocity](velocity.md) by local mass rather than volume. It satisfies the exact identity $\langle\rho u\rangle=\langle\rho\rangle\widetilde u$, so it naturally represents [mass flux](mass-flux.md) in averaged [conservation laws](conservation-law.md). The ordinary volume mean generally fails that identity when [density](density.md) and [velocity](velocity.md) are correlated. Applied inside a phase, the same construction uses [density](density.md) times that phase's indicator as its weight.
