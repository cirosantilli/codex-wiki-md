# QCD scaling violation

↑ **Parent:** [Bjorken scaling](bjorken-scaling.md)

[Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md) makes [parton distribution functions](parton-distribution-function.md) depend on resolution scale through [gluon](gluon.md) radiation and parton splitting. Thus [Bjorken scaling](bjorken-scaling.md) and the linear-[energy](energy-split.md) [neutrino](neutrino.md) [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) are approximate leading-order patterns rather than exact laws.
