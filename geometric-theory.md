# Geometric theory

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric logic](geometric-logic.md)

A set of sequents between [geometric formulas](geometric-formula.md) over a many-sorted signature. Its [geometric syntactic category](geometric-syntactic-category.md) and [geometric syntactic topology](geometric-syntactic-topology.md) yield its [classifying topos](classifying-topos.md). A model satisfies a sequent when the interpreted antecedent [subobject](subobject.md) is contained in the consequent in the indicated context.
