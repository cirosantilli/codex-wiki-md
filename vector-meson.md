# Vector meson

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_meson)

A [meson](meson.md) with total angular momentum one and negative [parity](parity.md). In the ordinary [quark model](quark-model.md), a [quark](quark.md)-[antiquark](antiquark.md) pair with [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) zero and total [spin](spin.md) one has these quantum numbers; its neutral self-conjugate states have [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) $C=-1$. The light-flavour tensor product gives an octet and a singlet, forming a vector nonet, just as spin-zero negative-parity pairs form a [pseudoscalar meson nonet](pseudoscalar-meson-nonet.md). Nonzero orbital excitations can also produce vector states.
