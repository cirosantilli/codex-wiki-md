# Modulus of a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Class field theory](class-field-theory.md)

A modulus is a formal product $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak m_0\mathfrak m_\infty$, where $\mathfrak m_0$ is a nonzero integral ideal and $\mathfrak m_\infty$ is a product of distinct real places.
