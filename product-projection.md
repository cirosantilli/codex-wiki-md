# Product projection

↑ **Parent:** [Product topology](product-topology.md)

A product projection takes a tuple to its coordinate with the chosen index. In the [product topology](product-topology.md) every such map is continuous, and [inverse images](preimage.md) of open subsets under all these maps form a [subbasis of a topology](subbasis-of-a-topology.md). A map into the product is continuous exactly when its compositions with all product projections are continuous.
