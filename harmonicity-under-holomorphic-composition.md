# Harmonicity under holomorphic composition

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)

If $f:U\to V$ is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) between planar [open sets](open-set.md) and $\psi:V\to\mathbb R$ is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md), then $\psi\circ f$ is [harmonic](harmonic-function.md). In fact the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) and the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md), with $f=u+iv$, give

$$
\Delta(\psi\circ f)=\psi_{uu}|\nabla u|^2+2\psi_{uv}\nabla u\cdot\nabla v+\psi_{vv}|\nabla v|^2+\psi_u\Delta u+\psi_v\Delta v=|f'|^2(\Delta\psi)\circ f.
$$

Here the two [gradients](gradient.md) are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) and have equal length, while $u,v$ are [harmonic](harmonic-function.md). The formula holds even where $f'=0$. A [biholomorphism](biholomorphism.md) therefore induces inverse correspondences between [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) by [function composition](function-composition.md) with it and its inverse.
