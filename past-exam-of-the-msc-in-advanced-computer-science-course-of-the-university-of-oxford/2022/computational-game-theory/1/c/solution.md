<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

First evaluate the inner [lottery](../../../../../lottery.md): $U(\ell_1)=12p+3(1-p)=3+9p$. By [reduction of compound lotteries](../../../../../reduction-of-compound-lotteries.md), the outer [expected utility](../../../../../expected-utility.md) is

$$
U(\ell_2)=2p+(1-p)(3+9p)=3+8p-9p^2
=\frac{43}{9}-9\left(p-\frac49\right)^2.
$$

Since $4/9$ lies in the permitted [real interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md), the square is minimized there. The unique maximizer and maximum [expected utility](../../../../../expected-utility.md) are **$p=4/9$ and $U(\ell_2)=43/9$**. Directly flattening the [lottery](../../../../../lottery.md) gives [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) $p(1-p)$, $(1-p)^2$ and $p$ for the three outcomes; these sum to one and give the same expression.
