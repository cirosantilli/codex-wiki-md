↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Yes.** Give players $1,2,3$ weights $1,1,2$, respectively, and let the quota be three. This [weighted voting game](../../../../../../weighted-voting-game.md) is a [simple cooperative game](../../../../../../simple-cooperative-game.md) whose minimal winning [coalitions](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) are $\{1,3\}$ and $\{2,3\}$. In orders $123,132,213,231$, player 3 supplies the winning [marginal contribution](../../../../../../marginal-contribution.md). In order $312$ player 1 does, and in order $321$ player 2 does. Averaging the six [marginal contribution vectors](../../../../../../marginal-contribution-vector.md) gives exactly **$(1/6,1/6,4/6)$** as the vector of [Shapley values](../../../../../../shapley-value.md).
