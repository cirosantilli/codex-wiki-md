<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Yes.** Use the [simple cooperative game](../../../../../../simple-cooperative-game.md) in which a [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) wins exactly when it contains at least two players. In every [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md), precisely the second arrival changes a losing [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) into a winning one. Each player is second in two of the six [permutations](../../../../../../permutation.md), so each [Shapley value](../../../../../../shapley-value.md) is $2/6=1/3$. This constructs the required vector while verifying that singleton [coalitions](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) lose and the grand [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) wins.
