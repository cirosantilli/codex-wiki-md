↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**No.** In a [simple cooperative game](../../../../../../simple-cooperative-game.md), a player's [marginal contribution](../../../../../../marginal-contribution.md) along any [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) is zero or one. With three players there are six equally likely [permutations](../../../../../../permutation.md), so every [Shapley value](../../../../../../shapley-value.md) is an integer multiple of $1/6$, by the [Shapley value denominator in a simple game](../../../../../../shapley-value-denominator-in-a-simple-game.md). But $6(5/12)=5/2$ and $6(3/12)=3/2$ are not integers. The vector sums to one, but fails this necessary denominator condition.
