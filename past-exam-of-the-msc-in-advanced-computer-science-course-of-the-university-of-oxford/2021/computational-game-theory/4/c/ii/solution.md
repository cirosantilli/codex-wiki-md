<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The [core](../../../../../../core-game-theory.md) is empty.** Efficiency would require $x_1+x_2+x_3=v(N)=1$. Each two-player [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) has value $1$, so [core](../../../../../../core-game-theory.md) membership would also require

$$
x_1+x_2\ge1,\qquad x_1+x_3\ge1,\qquad x_2+x_3\ge1.
$$

Adding gives $2(x_1+x_2+x_3)\ge3$, contradicting efficiency, which makes the left side $2$. Thus no allocation can meet all [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) demands. The obstruction uses only pair [coalitions](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) and the grand [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md); imposing individual rationality cannot remove it. The equal allocation $(1/3,1/3,1/3)$ is efficient but gives each pair only $2/3<1$, so symmetry does not supply a [core](../../../../../../core-game-theory.md) allocation.
