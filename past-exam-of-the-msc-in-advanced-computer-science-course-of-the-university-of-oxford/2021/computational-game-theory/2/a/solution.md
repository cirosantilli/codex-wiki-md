<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $p=\Pr(T)$ and $q=\Pr(L)$, so the complementary [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) are $1-p$ and $1-q$. All four expressions are the row player's [expected payoff](../../../../../expected-payoff.md), including those indexed by a column action. Direct [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) calculations give

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\pi_1(p,L)&=2p-(1-p)=3p-1,\\
\pi_1(p,R)&=-3p+(1-p)=1-4p,\\
\pi_1(T,q)&=2q-3(1-q)=5q-3,\\
\pi_1(B,q)&=-q+(1-q)=1-2q.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The column player's [expected payoff](../../../../../expected-payoff.md) is the negative of the row player's because this is a [zero-sum game](../../../../../zero-sum-game.md).
