# Implicit differentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Implicit_differentiation)

Implicit differentiation differentiates an identity $F(x,y(x))=0$ along a [differentiable](differentiable-function.md) branch and uses the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) to solve for its [derivative](derivative.md):

$$
F_x+F_y\,y'=0,\qquad y'=-\frac{F_x}{F_y}\quad(F_y\ne0).
$$

The [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) $F_x,F_y$ treat $x,y$ as independent coordinates before evaluating on the branch. The [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md) justifies the branch's local existence and regularity under its hypotheses; differentiating alone does not establish either. For the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) at $(3/5,4/5)$, this calculation yields the [tangent line](tangent-line.md) $y-4/5=-(3/4)(x-3/5)$.
