# Skew representation of a symmetric group

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory of the symmetric group](representation-theory-of-the-symmetric-group-split.md)

For $|\lambda|=n$, $|\mu|=m$, and $k=n-m$, define

$$
V^{\lambda/\mu}=\operatorname{Hom}_{S_m}(V^\mu,\operatorname{Res}^{S_n}_{S_m}V^\lambda).
$$

The copy of $S_k$ acting on the last $k$ letters commutes with $S_m$ and acts on this multiplicity space. Iterated [restriction branching rule for a symmetric group](restriction-branching-rule-for-a-symmetric-group.md) supplies a basis indexed by [standard skew Young tableaux](standard-skew-young-tableau.md). Every such basis vector is a [cyclic vector for a group representation](cyclic-vector-for-a-group-representation.md): admissible swaps connect all tableaux, and their off-diagonal coefficients in the [Young orthogonal form](young-orthogonal-form.md) are nonzero.
