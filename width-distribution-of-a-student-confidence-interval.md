# Width distribution of a Student confidence interval

↑ **Parent:** [Small normal sample mean confidence interval](small-normal-sample-mean-confidence-interval.md)

For a [normal sample](normal-sample.md) of size $n\geq2$, the central [Student t confidence interval](student-t-confidence-interval.md) has width $W=2t_{n-1,1-\alpha/2}S/\sqrt n$. Its squared width is therefore a fixed multiple of the [sample variance](sample-variance.md). The exact sampling identity $(n-1)S^2/\sigma^2\sim\chi^2_{n-1}$ gives the displayed law. It quantifies the random variability in interval precision even when the [sample size](sample-size.md) and coverage level are held fixed.
