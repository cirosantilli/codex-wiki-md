# Fluorophore

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence](fluorescence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluorophore)

A [fluorophore](fluorophore.md) is a fluorescent [molecule](molecule.md) or fluorescent part of a [molecule](molecule.md). Its usefulness as a label depends on how efficiently it absorbs the chosen excitation [light](light.md), its [fluorescence quantum yield](fluorescence-quantum-yield.md), and its resistance to [photobleaching](photobleaching.md). A large [fluorescence quantum yield](fluorescence-quantum-yield.md) alone does not guarantee a bright image if few excitation [photons](photon.md) are absorbed or collected.
