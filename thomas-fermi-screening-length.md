# Thomas-Fermi screening length

↑ **Parent:** [Thomas-Fermi screening](thomas-fermi-screening.md)

The [Thomas-Fermi screening length](thomas-fermi-screening-length.md) is the exponential decay scale of a statically screened potential in the local degenerate-electron approximation. For a three-dimensional parabolic [free electron model](free-electron-model.md), $g(E_F)=3n/(2E_F)$, so $\lambda_{\rm TF}^2=2\varepsilon_b E_F/(3ne^2)$. At fixed mass and background permittivity it scales as $n^{-1/6}$, rather than the classical Debye density power at fixed temperature.
