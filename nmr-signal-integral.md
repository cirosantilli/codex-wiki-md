# NMR signal integral

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

The area of a resonance or multiplet, proportional to the contributing nuclear population under suitable excitation, relaxation and acquisition conditions. Relative proton integrals can count chemically distinct [protons](proton.md).
