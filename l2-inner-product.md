# L2 inner product

↑ **Parent:** [L2 space is a Hilbert space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md)

The L2 inner product is the [inner product](inner-product.md) $\langle f,g\rangle=\int f\overline g\,d\mu$ on [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md). Its induced [norm](norm.md) is the [L2 norm](l2-norm.md), and the [mass matrix](mass-matrix.md) represents it in a [finite element](finite-element.md) basis.
