# Time-orientation-preserving Lorentz group in one spatial dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz group](lorentz-group.md)

For $J=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$, the [matrices](matrix.md) satisfying $A^TJA=J$ and $A_{11}>0$ form the time-orientation-preserving [Lorentz group](lorentz-group.md) in one spatial dimension. They are exactly $A=H(t)D_\varepsilon$, where $H(t)=\begin{pmatrix}\cosh t&\sinh t\\\sinh t&\cosh t\end{pmatrix}$, $D_\varepsilon=\operatorname{diag}(1,\varepsilon)$ and $\varepsilon=\pm1$. Preservation of the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) makes the first column a future-pointing unit timelike [vector](vector.md); [Minkowski inner product](minkowski-inner-product.md) orthogonality determines the second up to sign. The identity $D_\varepsilon H(s)=H(\varepsilon s)D_\varepsilon$ gives the multiplication law $(t,\varepsilon)(s,\eta)=(t+\varepsilon s,\varepsilon\eta)$ and inverse $(-\varepsilon t,\varepsilon)$.
