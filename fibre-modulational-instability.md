# Fibre modulational instability

↑ **Parent:** [Modulational instability](modulational-instability.md)

Linearizing a uniform Kerr-fibre background couples opposite [frequency](frequency.md) sidebands. With $iA_z-\beta_2A_{TT}/2+\gamma|A|^2A=0$, the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) permits exponential growth precisely when $\beta_2\gamma<0$ and $0<\Omega^2<-4\gamma P/\beta_2$. For positive $\gamma$, this is anomalous [group velocity dispersion](group-velocity-dispersion.md). [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) requires the paired complex-mode ansatz on the unstable branch. [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and [power](power.md) growth exponents differ by a factor of two.
