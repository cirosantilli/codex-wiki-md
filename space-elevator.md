# Space elevator

↑ **Parent:** [Aerospace engineering](aerospace-engineering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_elevator)

A proposed transport system using a cable extending from a planetary surface beyond a synchronous orbit. In a co-rotating model, the outward effective [force](force-split.md) on the outer portion supports the inner portion. The endpoint loads, distribution of [mass](mass.md), and material strength determine a feasible [tension](tension.md) profile; the [uniform unloaded space-elevator cable](uniform-unloaded-space-elevator-cable.md) is an ideal limit.
