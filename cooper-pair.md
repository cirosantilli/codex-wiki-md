# Cooper pair

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_pair)

A Cooper pair is a correlated pair of [fermions](fermion.md) associated with [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md). The conventional [BCS ground state](bcs-ground-state.md) uses opposite-momentum, opposite-spin pair creation operators $c_{k\uparrow}^\dagger c_{-k\downarrow}^\dagger$. Their total momentum is zero for every relative momentum $k$; zero total momentum does not restrict $k$ to zero.
