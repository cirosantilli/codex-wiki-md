# Lognormal random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Log-normal distribution](log-normal-distribution.md)

A positive [random variable](random-variable-split.md) whose [logarithm](logarithm.md) has a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). Equivalently it can be represented as $e^{m+\sqrt v Z}$, with $Z$ having a [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) and $v\geq0$, allowing the deterministic limiting case $v=0$.
