# Covolume of a fractional ideal lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski embedding of a number field](minkowski-embedding-of-a-number-field.md)

Let $j(\mathfrak b)$ be the [Minkowski embedding of a number field](minkowski-embedding-of-a-number-field.md) applied to a nonzero [fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md). Under the metric $|x|^2=\sum_{v\text{ real}}x_v^2+2\sum_{v\text{ complex}}|x_v|^2$, its [covolume](covolume.md) is $\sqrt{|D_K|}N(\mathfrak b)$. For ordinary coordinate [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) $\prod dx_v\prod d\operatorname{Re}z_v\,d\operatorname{Im}z_v$, its [covolume](covolume.md) is instead $2^{-r_2}\sqrt{|D_K|}N(\mathfrak b)$. Here $D_K$ is the [field discriminant](field-discriminant.md), and the absolute [norm of a fractional ideal](norm-of-a-fractional-ideal.md) is positive and multiplicative. The formula follows by taking the embedding determinant of an [integral basis](integral-basis.md), then using the index of an [integral ideal](integral-ideal.md) and scaling to handle a [fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md).
