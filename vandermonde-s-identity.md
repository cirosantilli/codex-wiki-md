<h1 id="vandermonde-s-identity">Vandermonde's identity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandermonde's_identity)

Choose a $k$-element [subset](subset.md) of a disjoint union of sets of sizes $m$ and $n$. Partition the choices by the number $i$ of selected elements from the first set. The resulting count is the displayed convolution of [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md), where coefficients outside their admissible ranges are zero. This also proves the formula when $k>m+n$.
