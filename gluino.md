# Gluino

↑ **Parent:** [Gaugino](gaugino.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gluino)

The [gluino](gluino.md) is the spin-one-half [sparticle](sparticle.md) paired with a [gluon](gluon.md). In the [MSSM](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md), the eight adjoint color components form a [Majorana spinor](majorana-spinor.md) in $(\mathbf8,\mathbf1,0)$ under $SU(3)_c\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$. Its gauge representation is real, so the opposite-helicity antiparticle states are already included in the adjoint multiplet.
