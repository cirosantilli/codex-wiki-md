# Polyphyly

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)

Assembly of separate lineages without including their relevant connecting ancestry, often using independently acquired similar features. Selected tips alone do not always distinguish this from [paraphyly](paraphyly.md); the ancestry included and the interpretation of characters matter.
