# Holder inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Functional analysis](functional-analysis-split.md)

For conjugate exponents $p,q$,

$$
\int|fg|\leq\|f\|_p\|g\|_q,
$$

with the analogous inequality $\sum_n|x_ny_n|\leq\lVert x\rVert_p\lVert y\rVert_q$ for sequences.
