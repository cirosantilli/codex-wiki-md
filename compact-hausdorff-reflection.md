# Compact Hausdorff reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Reflective subcategory](reflective-subcategory.md)

The inclusion of [compact Hausdorff spaces](compact-hausdorff-space.md) in [topological spaces](topological-space.md) has a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md). Its unit $X\to RX$ is universal among [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) from $X$ to compact Hausdorff spaces and has [dense](dense-set.md) image. Completeness follows from [products in a category](product-category-theory.md) and closed [equalizers](equaliser.md). For any map $X\to K$, its closed image has a dense subset of cardinality at most $|X|$ and hence cardinality at most $2^{2^{|X|}}$. Representatives of all such compact Hausdorff spaces and maps establish the [solution-set condition](solution-set-condition.md), so the [general adjoint functor theorem](freyd-general-adjoint-functor-theorem.md) gives the reflection. The unit need not be injective or a topological embedding for an arbitrary $X$.
