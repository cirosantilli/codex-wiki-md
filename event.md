# Event

↑ **Parent:** [Probability space](probability-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Event_(probability_theory))

An event is a [measurable set](measurable-set.md) in a [probability space](probability-space.md): a question with a yes-or-no answer for each outcome. On $\Omega=\{1,\ldots,6\}^2$, the [event](event.md) that the sum is at least nine consists of ten ordered pairs. Its [probability](probability.md) is therefore $10/36=5/18$ under independent uniform dice. Saying that an [event](event.md) occurred means the actual outcome belongs to that [set](set-split.md).
