# Normal subgroups and quotients of the symmetric group on three letters

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric group](symmetric-group.md)

The [conjugacy classes of the symmetric group on three letters](conjugacy-classes-of-the-symmetric-group-on-three-letters.md) have sizes $1,3,2$: the identity, the three transpositions and the two 3-cycles. A [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) is a union of whole [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) and contains the identity. Its candidate orders are therefore $1,4,3,6$. [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md) excludes four, leaving precisely $\{e\}$, $A_3$ and $S_3$. The order-three subgroup is the cyclic group of the two 3-cycles and the identity; it is also the [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) of the sign map.

The [first isomorphism theorem](first-isomorphism-theorem.md) gives the corresponding quotients $S_3$, $C_2$ and the trivial group. Thus these are exactly the possible images of surjective [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) from $S_3$. Each occurs, by the identity map, the sign map and the constant identity map, respectively.
