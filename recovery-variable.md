# Recovery variable

↑ **Parent:** [FitzHugh-Nagumo model](fitzhugh-nagumo-model.md)

A [recovery variable](recovery-variable.md) represents feedback that reduces activation after an excitation in a [FitzHugh-Nagumo model](fitzhugh-nagumo-model.md). Its equation must be specified separately from the activation equation. Holding it externally constant gives a scalar reduction; requiring it to remain constant while keeping its original evolution equation generally imposes an additional constraint and need not permit the same solutions.
