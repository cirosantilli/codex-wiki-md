# Symmetry of the second fundamental form

↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)

The [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) of an [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md) of a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) is symmetric. Its ambient [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) is a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md), so

$$
II(X,Y)-II(Y,X)=\bigl(\overline\nabla_XY-\overline\nabla_YX-[X,Y]\bigr)^\perp=0.
$$

The [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) tangent to the submanifold is tangent and therefore has zero normal component. This proof applies to a curved ambient manifold as well as Euclidean space.
