# Simplicial vertex (graph theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect elimination ordering](perfect-elimination-ordering.md)

A [simplicial vertex](simplicial-vertex-graph-theory.md) is a [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) whose [graph neighbourhood](graph-neighbourhood.md) is a [clique](clique-graph-theory.md). Every isolated [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) and every [leaf of a graph](leaf-of-a-graph.md) qualifies. In the three-triangle [chordal graph](chordal-graph.md), initially $a,e$ qualify, but $b$ only becomes a [simplicial vertex](simplicial-vertex-graph-theory.md) after deleting $a$. This is a property of adjacency in a [graph](graph-split.md); a [vertex of a simplicial complex](vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) is an element of a different kind of structure.
