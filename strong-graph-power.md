# Strong graph power

↑ **Parent:** [Strong product of graphs](strong-product-of-graphs.md)

An iterated [strong graph product](strong-product-of-graphs.md) has vertices that are words of length $m$ in the graph's vertex set. Distinct words are nonadjacent exactly when some coordinate contains distinct nonadjacent entries. This differs from the distance-based [graph power](graph-power.md).
