# Hall bar

↑ **Parent:** [von Klitzing-Dorda-Pepper quantum Hall experiment](von-klitzing-dorda-pepper-quantum-hall-experiment.md)

A [Hall bar](hall-bar.md) is a strip with current contacts at its ends and additional voltage contacts along its sides. Opposite-side contacts measure a transverse [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md); same-side contacts at different longitudinal positions measure a longitudinal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). Separate voltage leads reduce the influence of current-contact [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) when their input [electric currents](electric-current.md) are negligible.
