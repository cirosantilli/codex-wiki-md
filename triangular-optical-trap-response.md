# Triangular optical-trap response

↑ **Parent:** [Repeated kicks from a circular optical trap](repeated-kicks-from-a-circular-optical-trap.md)

A triangular localized [potential energy](potential-energy.md) has forces $+F$ behind and $-F$ ahead of the trap centre. With $v_c=F/\zeta=Lf_c$, $\alpha=2X_0/L$ and $\beta=f_T/f_c$, its circular response is $f_p/f_c=\alpha\beta/(\beta^2-1+\alpha)$ for $\beta>1$. A trapped particle has $f_p/f_c=\beta$ for $0\le\beta\le1$, interpreting the discontinuous [force](force.md) at the cusp by a sticking or rounded-well limit.
