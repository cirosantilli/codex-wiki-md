# Closed subalgebra of a Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

A norm-closed [subalgebra](subalgebra.md) of a [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) is itself a Banach algebra with the inherited norm: completeness follows from closedness, and the submultiplicative norm bound restricts to it. A unital inclusion uses the same identity as the ambient algebra. Inverse membership may still differ between the two algebras; [spectrum in a closed unital subalgebra](spectrum-in-a-closed-unital-subalgebra.md) describes that difference.
