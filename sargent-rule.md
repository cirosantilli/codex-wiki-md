# Sargent rule

↑ **Parent:** [Allowed beta decay](allowed-beta-decay.md)

The approximate fifth-power scaling of an allowed [beta decay](beta-decay.md) rate with available [energy](energy-split.md) when [electron](electron.md) [mass](mass.md) and Coulomb distortion are negligible and the nuclear [matrix element](matrix-element.md) is held fixed. It follows from $\int_0^Qp^2(Q-p)^2dp=Q^5/30$. Different nuclear [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) or nonrelativistic [electron](electron.md) kinematics spoil an exact universal law.
