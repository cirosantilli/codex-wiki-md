# Invertible module

↑ **Parent:** [Projective module](projective-module.md)

An invertible module over a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $A$ is a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) that is a [projective module](projective-module.md) and whose [localization at a prime ideal](localization-at-a-prime-ideal.md) is free of rank one at every prime. Its [dual module](dual-module.md) $L^*=\operatorname{Hom}_A(L,A)$ is also finite projective. Evaluation $L\otimes_A L^*\to A$ is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) after every prime localization, hence is an isomorphism globally by [localization detects zero elements](localization-detects-zero-elements.md). The [Picard group of a ring](picard-group-of-a-ring.md) consists of the isomorphism classes of these modules under the [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md).
