# Box-counting dimension of separated digit sets

↑ **Parent:** [Box-counting dimension](box-counting-dimension.md)

Consider base-$b$ expansions using $m$ allowed digits with positive gaps between the corresponding first-level intervals. At depth $k$ there are $m^k$ cylinders of diameter comparable to $b^{-k}$, and separated cylinders cannot all be covered by fewer than a constant multiple of $m^k$ balls of that scale. Thus $N_{b^{-k}}(E)\asymp m^k$. Monotonicity between successive scales gives equal upper and lower [Minkowski dimensions](box-counting-dimension.md), both $\log m/\log b$.
