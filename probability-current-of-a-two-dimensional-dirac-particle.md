# Probability current of a two-dimensional Dirac particle

↑ **Parent:** [Probability current](probability-current.md)

For a Hermitian local mass coefficient and $H=c\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf p+V\sigma_z$, the density and current obey $\partial_t(\Psi^\dagger\Psi)+\nabla\cdot(c\Psi^\dagger\boldsymbol\sigma\Psi)=0$. A normalized $\sigma_x=-1$ interface [spinor](spinor.md) carries $j_x=-c\rho$ and $j_y=0$.
