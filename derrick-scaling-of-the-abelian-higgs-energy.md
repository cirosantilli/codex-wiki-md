# Derrick scaling of the Abelian Higgs energy

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian Higgs model](abelian-higgs-model.md)

In two dimensions, scale $\Phi_\lambda(x)=\Phi(\lambda x)$ and $A_{j,\lambda}(x)=\lambda A_j(\lambda x)$. The [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) has scaling weight two, the [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md) of the [Higgs field](higgs-field.md) has weight one, and the potential has weight zero. Their integrated energies therefore have the powers shown. Stationarity gives $E_B=E_P$, a [Derrick virial identity](derrick-virial-identity.md). This balance allows a planar [Abelian Higgs vortex](nielsen-olesen-vortex.md) to have finite size even though a scalar-gradient-plus-nonnegative-potential model has no comparable magnetic term.
