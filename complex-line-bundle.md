# Complex line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

A complex line bundle is a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) whose fibers are one-dimensional complex vector spaces and whose transition maps are complex linear. Its underlying real bundle has rank two and a canonical orientation, and its Euler class equals its [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md).
