# Fractionating column

↑ **Parent:** [Fractional distillation](fractional-distillation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractionating_column)

A [fractionating column](fractionating-column.md) provides contact between rising [vapor](vapor.md) and returning [liquid](liquid.md) during [fractional distillation](fractional-distillation.md). Packing or trays provide contact area; the resulting separation depends on both [vapor-liquid equilibrium](vapor-liquid-equilibrium.md) and the actual flow and exchange between the [thermodynamic phases](thermodynamic-phase.md).[Introduction, paragraphs 3–4](distillation-by-hong-kong-university-of-science-and-technology.md)
