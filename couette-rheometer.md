# Couette rheometer

↑ **Parent:** [Rheometer](rheometer.md)

Concentric cylinders impose shear while [torque](torque.md) and rotation measure a material's response. The displayed calibration holds for Newtonian laminar flow, inner-cylinder rotation and a stationary outer cylinder. In a narrow gap, the rate is approximately $\Omega R_i/(R_o-R_i)$ and inner-wall stress is $\mathcal T/(2\pi LR_i^2)$. End effects, [wall slip](wall-slip.md) and inertial secondary flow require control.
