# Electron pocket

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md)

An [electron pocket](electron-pocket.md) surrounds occupied states near a conduction-band minimum. In two dimensions its [carrier density](carrier-density.md) is $gA/(2\pi)^2$, where $A$ is its total reciprocal-space area and $g$ the unresolved degeneracy. Portions drawn at opposite zone faces can belong to the same pocket after periodic identification.
