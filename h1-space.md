# H1 space

↑ **Parent:** [First-order Sobolev space](first-order-sobolev-space.md)

The H1 space is the [first-order Sobolev space](first-order-sobolev-space.md) whose elements and first [weak derivatives](weak-derivative.md) belong to [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md). It is a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) with inner product given by the sum of the [L2 inner products](l2-inner-product.md) of the functions and of their first [weak derivatives](weak-derivative.md).
