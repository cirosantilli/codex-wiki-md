# Episodic tremor and slip

↑ **Parent:** [Subduction zone](subduction-zone.md)

A coupled phenomenon in which transient slow fault slip occurs with low-frequency seismic tremor, often near the downdip transition from locked to steadily creeping parts of a [megathrust](megathrust.md). Geodetic displacement and seismic signals provide complementary observations. Along-strike differences in recurrence, migration and cumulative slip help characterize [subduction zone segmentation](subduction-zone-segmentation.md); tremor counts alone do not uniquely give fault slip or coupling.
