# Euler-Bernoulli beam equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

The Euler-Bernoulli beam equation models transverse linear bending waves in a slender beam. The normalized equation has $c_b=1$ and may be reduced by the [Schrodinger factorization of the elastic beam equation](schrodinger-factorization-of-the-elastic-beam-equation.md). Prescribing displacement and curvature at an endpoint supplies two boundary traces for its fourth spatial derivative.
