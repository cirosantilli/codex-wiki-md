# Six-dimensional cubic scalar counterterms

↑ **Parent:** [Counterterm Lagrangian](counterterm-lagrangian.md)

For a [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) in six spacetime dimensions with interaction $-g\phi^3/6$, the [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) of $g$ is zero. With Euclidean sharp [cutoff regularization](cutoff-regularization.md), $C=1/(128\pi^3)$ and $L=\log(\Lambda^2/m^2)$, choose the Minkowski [counterterm Lagrangian](counterterm-lagrangian.md) $\delta_Z(\partial\phi)^2/2-\delta_m\phi^2/2-\delta_g\phi^3/6-\delta_h\phi$. Its one-loop divergent coefficients are

$$
\delta_Z=-\frac{Cg^2}{6}L,\qquad
\delta_m=\frac{Cg^2}{2}(\Lambda^2-2m^2L),\qquad
\delta_g=-Cg^3L,\qquad
\delta_h=-\frac{Cg}{2}(\Lambda^4/2-m^2\Lambda^2+m^4L).
$$

The proof uses the [Euclidean effective action](euclidean-effective-action.md) $\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}\log(-\partial_E^2+m^2+g\phi)$. Its first three background derivatives have divergent parts $gI_1/2$, $-g^2B(P)/2$, $g^3I_3$. Radial integration gives $I_1=C(\Lambda^4/2-m^2\Lambda^2+m^4L)$, $I_3=CL$, and $B(P)=C(\Lambda^2-2m^2L-P^2L/3)$ modulo finite terms. Subtraction proves the coefficients. The [tadpole counterterm](tadpole-counterterm.md) is essential despite the absence of a linear term in the original [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md). A separate constant handles the [vacuum energy density](vacuum-energy-density.md).
