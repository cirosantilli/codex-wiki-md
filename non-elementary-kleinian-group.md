# Non-elementary Kleinian group

↑ **Parent:** [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md)

A [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md) whose [Kleinian limit set](limit-set-of-a-kleinian-group.md) contains at least three points. A discrete group fixing a common boundary point has at most two limit points and is therefore elementary.
