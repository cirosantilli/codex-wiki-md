# Intersection graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersection_graph)

An [intersection graph](intersection-graph.md) has one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) for each member of a family of [sets](set-split.md). Two distinct [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) have an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) exactly when their associated [sets](set-split.md) have a nonempty [intersection](set-intersection.md). A [clique](clique-graph-theory.md) therefore describes pairwise intersections; it need not give a single element common to all those [sets](set-split.md).
