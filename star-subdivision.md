# Star subdivision

↑ **Parent:** [Fan subdivision](fan-subdivision.md)

This operation is a [fan subdivision](fan-subdivision.md). A star subdivision inserts a ray through a lattice point in a cone and subdivides every cone containing that point. It induces a proper birational [toric morphism](toric-morphism.md).
