# Ferricyanide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[ferricyanide](ferricyanide.md) is a redox-active iron-cyanide complex commonly used as an external electron acceptor in biological assays. Reduction shows electron accessibility under assay conditions, not necessarily a native physiological export pathway.
