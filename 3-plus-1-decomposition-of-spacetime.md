<h1 id="3-plus-1-decomposition-of-spacetime">3+1 decomposition of spacetime</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical relativity](numerical-relativity-split.md)

A 3+1 decomposition writes $ds^2=-\alpha^2dt^2+\gamma_{ij}(dx^i+\beta^idt)(dx^j+\beta^jdt)$ in terms of lapse, shift, and spatial metric.

A spacetime foliation separates a spatial metric, lapse and shift. The [ADM formalism](adm-formalism.md) uses this decomposition to express Einstein evolution and constraint equations in Hamiltonian form.
