# Equal-degeneracy two-level energy and heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Two-level system](two-level-system.md)

For N [independent](independent-random-variables.md) units with nondegenerate levels 0 and $\Delta>0$, the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $(1+e^{-\beta\Delta})^N$. Its [energy](energy-split.md) is $N\Delta/(1+e^{\beta\Delta})$ and [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) is $Nk_Bx^2e^x/(1+e^x)^2$, with $x=\Delta/(k_BT)$. Both are activated at low [temperature](temperature-split.md). At high [temperature](temperature-split.md) the [energy](energy-split.md) saturates at $N\Delta/2$ and [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) decays as $T^{-2}$. The equal-degeneracy Schottky peak is at $x\simeq2.39936$ and height $0.43923Nk_B$. A common [energy](energy-split.md) shift leaves [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) unchanged; unequal level degeneracies require a different [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md).
