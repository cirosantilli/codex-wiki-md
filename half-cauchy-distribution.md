# Half-Cauchy distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md)

The absolute value of a centered [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) variable with scale $\gamma>0$ has this [probability density function](probability-density-function.md). Its positive support and doubled density distinguish it from the two-sided distribution. Its unit-scale case arises as the ratio of two independent, equally scaled [half-normal distribution](half-normal-distribution.md) variables.
