# Polarization (waves)

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polarization_(waves))

[Polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) describes the direction and time-dependent geometry of the [oscillations](oscillation.md) of a [wave](wave.md). For a transverse [wave](wave.md), [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) oscillates along one fixed line; [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md) rotates along a circle, while [elliptical polarization](elliptical-polarization.md) traces an [ellipse](ellipse.md). [Polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) describes these possibilities for the [electric field](electric-field.md) vector.
