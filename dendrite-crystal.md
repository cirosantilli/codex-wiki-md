# Dendrite (crystal)

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendrite_(crystal))

A crystal dendrite is a branched crystal-growth morphology. Diffusive access to undercooling or supersaturation favours protruding tips, while interfacial capillarity regularizes fine scales and [crystal interface anisotropy](crystal-interface-anisotropy.md) selects directions. [Dendritic growth of a snowflake](dendritic-growth-of-a-snowflake.md) is a vapour-growth example.
