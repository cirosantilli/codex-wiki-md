# Chern connection component adjoint

↑ **Parent:** [Chern connection](chern-connection.md)

For the $(1,0)$ component of a [Chern connection](chern-connection.md), $(D'_E)^*=-*_{E^*}D'_{E^*}*_E$, where the middle operator is the corresponding component of the dual [Chern connection](chern-connection.md). This is the holomorphic-degree counterpart of the [bundle-valued Dolbeault adjoint](bundle-valued-dolbeault-adjoint.md).
