# Constant pulse-axis condition for removing time ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Time-ordered exponential](time-ordered-exponential.md)

For $H(t)=\Omega_1(t)A+\Omega_2(t)B$ with fixed noncommuting [Hermitian operators](hermitian-operator.md), $[H(t),H(s)]=[\Omega_1(t)\Omega_2(s)-\Omega_2(t)\Omega_1(s)][A,B]$. A constant ratio of the envelopes is sufficient to remove [time ordering](time-ordering.md). Independently changing that ratio generally produces [commutator](commutator.md) corrections in the [Magnus expansion](magnus-expansion.md), and a product of successive pulses need not equal the exponential of their summed areas.
