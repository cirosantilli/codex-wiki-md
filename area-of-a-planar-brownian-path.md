# Area of a planar Brownian path

↑ **Parent:** [Planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md)

A [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) range has zero two-dimensional [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) almost surely. Assuming finite expected area, [Brownian scaling](brownian-scaling.md) gives half that expected area for each half-time range. Inclusion-exclusion then makes their expected overlap zero. Translate their ranges by the midpoint position; the [independent Brownian arms at a deterministic time](independent-brownian-arms-at-a-deterministic-time.md) make the expected overlap the integral of the squared point-hitting [probabilities](probability.md) for a half-time range. Those [probabilities](probability.md) vanish almost everywhere, so the half-time expected area, and hence the full-time expected area, vanish. This argument does not assert zero hitting [probability](probability.md) for every individual point solely from an almost-everywhere integral conclusion.
