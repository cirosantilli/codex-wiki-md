# Orthogonal eigenvectors of a compact operator

↑ **Parent:** [Compact operator](compact-operator-split.md)

If $Tx_j=\lambda_jx_j$ for an infinite orthonormal sequence $(x_j)$ and a compact operator $T$ on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), then $\lambda_j\to0$. Otherwise a subsequence of the vectors $Tx_j$ stays pairwise separated, contradicting compactness.
