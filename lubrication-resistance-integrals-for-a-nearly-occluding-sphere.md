# Lubrication resistance integrals for a nearly occluding sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Parabolic lubrication gap](parabolic-lubrication-gap.md)

A [sphere](sphere.md) almost filling a tube has local [parabolic lubrication gap](parabolic-lubrication-gap.md) $h\simeq a[\epsilon+(z/a)^2]/2$. The resistance integrals are $I_1=2\pi\epsilon^{-1/2}$, $I_2=(2\pi/a)\epsilon^{-3/2}$ and $I_3=(3\pi/a^2)\epsilon^{-5/2}$, obtained with $z=a\sqrt\epsilon\,\xi$ and $\int(1+\xi^2)^{-n}d\xi$. [Moving-boundary lubrication flux](moving-boundary-lubrication-flux.md) weights shear-driven and pressure-driven transport by different powers of the gap, explaining the three distinct divergence rates.
