# Coulomb blockade

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coulomb_blockade)

Suppression of tunnelling because adding or removing a discrete island charge costs more energy than the accessible transport and thermal energy window. Weak junction coupling and small island [capacitance](capacitance.md) make it observable.
