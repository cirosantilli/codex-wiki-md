# Upper central series of a Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent Lie algebra](nilpotent-lie-algebra.md)

Each term is the preimage of the [center of a Lie algebra](center-of-a-lie-algebra.md) after quotienting by the preceding term. Equivalently, $Z_{i+1}=\{x:[\mathfrak g,x]\subseteq Z_i\}$. A [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is nilpotent exactly when this series reaches the whole algebra after finitely many steps. For the [Heisenberg Lie algebra](heisenberg-lie-algebra.md), the series is $0\subset\mathbb Cc\subset\mathfrak g$.
