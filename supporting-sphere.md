# Supporting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md)

A supporting sphere for an embedded surface at $p$ is a sphere tangent at $p$ such that the surface lies locally on one side of the sphere. Comparing second fundamental forms at the tangency controls the surface's [normal curvature](normal-curvature.md).
