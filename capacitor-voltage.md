# Capacitor voltage

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor](capacitor.md)

The [voltage](voltage.md) across an ideal linear [capacitor](capacitor.md) of constant [capacitance](capacitance.md) C is its signed plate charge divided by C. With the passive current convention, $I=C\dot V_C$.
