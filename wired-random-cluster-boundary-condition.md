# Wired random-cluster boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Random-cluster boundary condition](random-cluster-boundary-condition.md)

All boundary vertices are identified into one vertex for component counting. This is the most wired [random-cluster boundary condition](random-cluster-boundary-condition.md) on a fixed boundary, and maximizes increasing-event [probabilities](probability.md) when $q\geq1$ by [boundary monotonicity of the random-cluster measure](boundary-monotonicity-of-the-random-cluster-measure.md).
