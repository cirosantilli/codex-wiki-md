# Prevertex of a Schwarz-Christoffel map

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz-Christoffel mapping](schwarz-christoffel-mapping.md)

A boundary point of the source half-plane that maps to a [polygon](polygon.md) vertex. If that corner has angle $\pi\alpha$, the [Schwarz-Christoffel mapping](schwarz-christoffel-mapping.md) has local behavior $F(z)-F(a)\sim C(z-a)^\alpha$ at its [prevertex](prevertex-of-a-schwarz-christoffel-map.md), so the [derivative](derivative.md) has exponent $\alpha-1$. Infinity may also be a [prevertex](prevertex-of-a-schwarz-christoffel-map.md), expressed in the local coordinate $1/z$.
