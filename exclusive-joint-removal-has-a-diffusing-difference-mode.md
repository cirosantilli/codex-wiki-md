# Exclusive joint removal has a diffusing difference mode

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field joint removal of two chemical species](mean-field-joint-removal-of-two-chemical-species.md)

If individual removal is absent, simultaneous removal of one molecule from each species leaves $X_1-X_2$ unchanged. Independent constant-rate births change it by plus or minus one, each at rate $\lambda$. The difference is therefore a symmetric [continuous-time random walk](continuous-time-random-walk.md) on the integers with variance growing as displayed. It has no normalizable stationary law. This explains why a deterministic attracting line of equilibria does not provide a stationary stochastic distribution.
