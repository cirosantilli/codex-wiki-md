# Almost-exponential locality of filtered Hamiltonian terms

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral filtering of Hamiltonian terms](spectral-filtering-of-hamiltonian-terms.md)

In a [telescoping local-shell decomposition](telescoping-local-shell-decomposition.md), split the [spectral filter](spectral-filter.md) integral at a time proportional to the shell distance. A [Lieb-Robinson bound](lieb-robinson-bound.md) controls short times, while the two-sided almost-exponential [spectral filter](spectral-filter.md) tail controls long times. Polynomial shell-weight growth then gives the displayed decay. The estimate is asymptotic at large distance; small shells retain the elementary operator-norm bound. The [evenization of a nonnegative bandlimited filter](evenization-of-a-nonnegative-bandlimited-filter.md) can supply the two-sided tail from a one-sided existence statement.
