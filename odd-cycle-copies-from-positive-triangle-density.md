# Odd-cycle copies from positive triangle density

↑ **Parent:** [Graph embedding lemma for regular pairs](graph-embedding-lemma-for-regular-pairs.md)

For any fixed odd length $\ell\ge3$, positive [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) density forces positive density of $\ell$-cycles. A [Szemerédi regularity lemma](szemeredi-regularity-lemma.md) partition retains a [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) of regular dense pairs after the sparse and exceptional pairs are removed. Count embeddings of a proper three-colouring of $C_\ell$ into those three clusters. The constants depend on $\beta$ and $\ell$, not on [graph](graph-split.md) order.
