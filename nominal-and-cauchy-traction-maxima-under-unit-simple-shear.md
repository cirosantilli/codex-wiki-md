# Nominal and Cauchy traction maxima under unit simple shear

↑ **Parent:** [Simple shear](simple-shear.md)

For the reciprocal-Jacobian quadratic energy under $F=I+e_1\otimes e_2$, $T_R/\mu$ has off-diagonal entries $(1,2)$ and $(2,1)$ equal to one and all others zero. Every unit reference [normal](normal-distribution.md) in the first two coordinate directions therefore maximizes nominal [traction](traction.md). The [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) has upper-left block $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its largest absolute [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$, with spatial [normal](normal-distribution.md) proportional to $(\varphi,1,0)$. Pulling this back by $F^T$ gives reference [normal](normal-distribution.md) proportional to $(1,\varphi,0)$ and [plane](plane.md) $x_1+\varphi x_2=0$.
