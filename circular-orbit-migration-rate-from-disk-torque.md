# Circular-orbit migration rate from disk torque

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary migration](planetary-migration.md)

For a fixed mass on a Keplerian [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md), $L_s=M_s\sqrt{GM_*r_0}$ has derivative $M_sr_0\Omega/2$. The [torque](torque.md) on the satellite is minus the [torque](torque.md) $\Gamma$ on the disk. This gives the displayed drift law. In the [density-slope satellite torque](density-slope-satellite-torque.md) model, $\dot r_0=-(8\chi/9)\beta q(\Sigma_0r_0^2/M_*)r_0\Omega(H/r_0)^{-2}$.
