# Physical rapidity strip

↑ **Parent:** [S-matrix](s-matrix.md)

For relativistic two-body scattering in one spatial dimension, this strip is the standard domain containing bound-state and crossed-channel [poles](pole.md). A stable-particle interpretation requires appropriate [pole](pole.md) kinematics and residues; not every singularity automatically represents a new particle. Crossing relates [rapidity](rapidity.md) $\vartheta$ to $i\pi-\vartheta$.
