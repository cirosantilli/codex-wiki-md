# Forward scattering amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md)

The [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) at zero deflection angle. Its real part changes the phase of a coherent beam in matter; its imaginary part is related by the [optical theorem](optical-theorem.md) to removal from that beam. Different forward amplitudes for a [kaon](kaon.md) and a [neutral antikaon](neutral-antikaon.md) allow [neutral kaon regeneration](neutral-kaon-regeneration.md) without fundamental [CP violation](cp-violation.md).
