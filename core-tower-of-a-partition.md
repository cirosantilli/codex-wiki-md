# Core tower of a partition

↑ **Parent:** [Abacus of a partition](abacus-of-a-partition.md)

The $p$-core tower places at level $r$ the $p$-cores of all partitions at level $r$ of the [quotient tower of a partition](quotient-tower-of-a-partition.md). If $q_r$ is the sum of the sizes at quotient level $r$ and $c_r$ the sum at core level $r$, then

$$
q_r=c_r+p q_{r+1}.
$$
