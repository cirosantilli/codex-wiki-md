# Muscle force

↑ **Parent:** [Muscle contraction](muscle-contraction.md)

A [force](force-split.md) exerted by an active or passively stretched [muscle](muscle.md). Its contribution to rotation about an [anatomical joint](anatomical-joint.md) depends on the [muscle moment arm](muscle-moment-arm.md), rather than its magnitude alone.
