# Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum ideal-gas statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell–Boltzmann_distribution)

When $E-\mu\gg k_BT$, either quantum occupation law reduces to the classical factor

$$
e^{-(E-\mu)/(k_BT)}.
$$

For a classical ideal gas the associated velocity distribution is $f(\mathbf v)=(m/(2\pi k_BT))^{3/2}\exp[-m|\mathbf v|^2/(2k_BT)]$. The occupation factor is the corresponding [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) for a one-particle energy.
