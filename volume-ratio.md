# Volume ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md)

The [volume ratio](volume-ratio.md) of a $d$-dimensional [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) is $\operatorname{vr}(E)=(\operatorname{vol}(B_E)/\operatorname{vol}(\mathcal E))^{1/d}$, where $\mathcal E$ is its [John ellipsoid](john-ellipsoid.md). It is unchanged by invertible linear changes of coordinates. In [John position](john-position.md), polar integration gives $\operatorname{vr}(E)^d=\int_{S^{d-1}}\|u\|_E^{-d}\,d\sigma(u)$, with normalized spherical measure. Consequently $\sigma\{\|u\|_E\leq t\}\leq(\operatorname{vr}(E)t)^d$.
