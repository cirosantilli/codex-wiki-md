# Multinomial central limit theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md)

The vector of category counts is the sum of independent categorical indicator vectors. Their means are $p$ and covariance is $\operatorname{diag}p-pp^T$, so the [multivariate central limit theorem](multivariate-central-limit-theorem.md) gives the displayed limit. At uniform probabilities on $k$ categories, Pearson-standardized deviations have covariance equal to the orthogonal projection onto the $(k-1)$-dimensional subspace of zero-sum vectors. Their squared norm has an asymptotic [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $k-1$ degrees of freedom.
