# Power law

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_law)

A power law is a relation of the form $f(x)=Cx^p$ for constants $C$ and $p$. Under rescaling of its argument it satisfies $f(\lambda x)=\lambda^pf(x)$.
