# Radiative flux

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation-field moment](radiation-field-moment.md)

Radiative flux is the first angular moment $F_\nu=\int I_\nu\cos\theta\,d\Omega$ of [specific intensity](spectral-radiance.md) and measures net radiant [power](power.md) crossing unit area. Integrating over [frequency](frequency.md) gives bolometric [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md). The receiving surface's orientation matters through $\cos\theta$; [observed radiative flux](observed-radiative-flux.md) from a compact distant source conventionally refers to an area perpendicular to its [line of sight](line-of-sight.md).
