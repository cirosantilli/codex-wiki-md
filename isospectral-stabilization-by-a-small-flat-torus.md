# Isospectral stabilization by a small flat torus

↑ **Parent:** [Product Laplacian eigenspace decomposition](product-laplacian-eigenspace-decomposition.md)

Two [isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md) remain isospectral after taking a [Riemannian product](riemannian-product.md) with the same [closed](closed-set.md) factor: [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) add, with [multiplicities](multiplicity-mathematics.md) given by the [product Laplacian eigenspace decomposition](product-laplacian-eigenspace-decomposition.md). For nonisometric [closed](closed-set.md) connected factors $M_1,M_2$, choose $\varepsilon<\delta<\min_i\operatorname{inj}(M_i)$. Every [closed](closed-set.md) product [geodesic](geodesic.md) of length less than $\delta$ has constant projection to $M_i$, since a nonconstant [closed geodesic](closed-geodesic.md) shorter than the [injectivity radius](injectivity-radius.md) is impossible. The short coordinate [geodesics](geodesic.md) of the small [flat torus](flat-torus.md) span exactly its tangent factor at every point. Any product [Riemannian isometry](riemannian-isometry.md) must preserve that intrinsically defined [smooth distribution](distribution-differential-geometry.md) and its [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md), hence restrict to an [isometry](isometry.md) of the original factors. Thus these products are still nonisometric. Taking $M_i$ to be four-dimensional [tori](torus.md) proves the higher-dimensional stabilization statement without assuming a cancellation theorem for arbitrary products.
