# Alpha-squared dynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha effect](alpha-effect.md)

An alpha-squared dynamo generates a mean [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) using an [alpha effect](alpha-effect.md) without requiring an additional large-scale shear coupling. For $\boldsymbol\alpha=\alpha_0\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$ and a transverse mean field varying as $e^{iKz}$, the growth rates are $-\eta K^2\pm\alpha_0K$. A nonzero $\alpha_0$ therefore allows growth for sufficiently long wavelengths, provided the domain and scale separation permit them.
