# Real rotation orbits on a complex unit quadric

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit (group theory)](orbit-group-theory.md)

For $z=x+iy\in\mathbb C^3$ satisfying $z^Tz=1$, the real and imaginary parts obey $|x|^2-|y|^2=1$ and $x\cdot y=0$. Simultaneous real rotations preserve $r=|y|$. At $r=0$ the [group orbit](orbit-group-theory.md) is the unit sphere, with [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) $SO(2)$. At each $r>0$, the normalized pair $(x,y)$ is an oriented two-frame, giving a free transitive [SO(3) group](so-3-group.md) action. The [group orbit](orbit-group-theory.md) space is $[0,\infty)$, and the whole quadric is diffeomorphic to the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) of $S^2$. Its varying Hermitian norm prevents it from being a single [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) [group orbit](orbit-group-theory.md).
