# Brownian first-passage Laplace transform

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian first-passage time](brownian-first-passage-time.md)

For standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at zero, $H_a=\inf\{t\geq0:B_t\geq a\}$ is finite [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) for $a\geq0$ and

$$
\mathbb E e^{-\lambda H_a}=e^{-a\sqrt{2\lambda}},\qquad\lambda\geq0.
$$

The [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) proves finiteness. For $u\geq0$, the [Exponential martingale for Brownian motion](exponential-martingale-for-brownian-motion.md) stopped at $H_a\wedge t$ is bounded by $e^{ua}$. The [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) gives $\mathbb E e^{ua-u^2H_a/2}=1$, and taking $u=\sqrt{2\lambda}$ gives the transform.
