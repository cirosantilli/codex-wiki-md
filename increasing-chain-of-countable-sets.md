# Increasing chain of countable sets

↑ **Parent:** [Countable set](countable-set.md)

A family of countable [sets](set-split.md) indexed by a [linear order](linear-order.md) is increasing if $i\leq j$ implies $X_i\subseteq X_j$. Its union has [cardinality](cardinality.md) at most $\aleph_1$, by the [cardinal bound for an increasing chain of countable sets](cardinal-bound-for-an-increasing-chain-of-countable-sets.md). The index order need not be a [well-order](well-order.md).
