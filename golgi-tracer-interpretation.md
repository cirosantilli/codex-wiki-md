# Golgi tracer interpretation

↑ **Parent:** [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md)

The [pancreatic secretory-protein pulse-chase experiment](pancreatic-secretory-protein-pulse-chase-experiment.md) of [James D. Jamieson](james-d-jamieson.md) and [George E. Palade](george-e-palade.md) used guinea pig [pancreas](pancreas.md) slices and a short pulse of tritium-labeled [leucine](leucine.md) followed by a chase. Thin sections on coated grids received Ilford L-4 emulsion; developed grains were read against structures by [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md). Their Table II localized label to peripheral [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md) vesicles and condensing vacuoles, without significant labeling of the piled flattened [Golgi cisternae](golgi-cisterna.md).[p. 598 Methods; p. 609 Table II and its footnote](intracellular-transport-of-secretory-proteins-in-the-pancreatic-exocrine-cell-ii-transport-to-condensing-vacuoles-and-zymogen-granules-by-james-d-jamieson-and-george-e-palade.md)

An important comparison checked the isolated granule fraction by [autoradiography](autoradiography.md) and [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md): early labeling came substantially from contaminating condensing vacuoles, rather than mature [secretory granules](secretory-granule.md). Thus a fraction's name did not uniquely identify the labeled structure. This supported transport into the [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md)'s condensing region and onward storage, while fixed samples and finite grain localization limited interpretation. It did not establish a resolved cis-to-trans itinerary across every [Golgi cisterna](golgi-cisterna.md). The preparation's labeling also did not mark secretory cargo exclusively.[p. 609 Figure 14; p. 613, Transport of Labeled Proteins to Condensing Vacuoles, Zymogen Granules, and the Acinar Lumen: comparison of fraction labeling with microscopy; p. 611, General Premises](intracellular-transport-of-secretory-proteins-in-the-pancreatic-exocrine-cell-ii-transport-to-condensing-vacuoles-and-zymogen-granules-by-james-d-jamieson-and-george-e-palade.md)

**[Figure 12](#image-golgi-tracer-interpretation-cargo-position-versus-compartment-identity). Golgi tracer interpretation: cargo position versus compartment identity**. Original interpretation diagram motivated by [pulse-chase analysis](pulse-chase-analysis.md). The upper panel explains the granule-fraction comparison in the inspected experiment; it gives no counts or time course. The lower panels are constructed alternatives: the same cargo can encounter early and late identities by transfer between stable [Golgi cisternae](golgi-cisterna.md) or by [cisternal maturation](cisternal-maturation.md). The colored identities and cargo symbols are illustrative, not observed fluorescence or reconstructed micrographs.

For a minimal worked inference, record only two outcomes for one labeled cargo cohort: early identity first, late identity later. Model V transfers cargo from container $C_1$ to container $C_2$; model M keeps it in $C_1$ while $C_1$ changes identity. Both produce the same recorded outcomes, so those outcomes alone cannot distinguish V from M. The proof is constructive: exhibit both histories and discard container identity from each record. More informative tracking would retain both cargo and resident-compartment markers, while resolving whether the same compartment persists. This is why [pulse-chase analysis](pulse-chase-analysis.md) and compartment tracking answer complementary questions about the [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md).
