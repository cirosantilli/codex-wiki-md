# Nonrotating layerwise-two-dimensional vortex dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex dynamics](vortex-dynamics.md)

In the limiting model of infinitely strong stable [density stratification](density-stratification.md), small [Froude number](froude-number.md) and fixed vertical scales suppress vertical displacement. Each horizontal level evolves by [two-dimensional vortex dynamics](two-dimensional-vortex-dynamics.md); $z$ labels independent copies of the horizontal [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md). The leading normalized [Ertel potential vorticity](ertel-potential-vorticity.md) is $Q/N^2=\zeta$. This is a constrained asymptotic model: finite [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) restores vertical motion and coupling, and arbitrarily short vertical scales invalidate the independent-layer limit. [McIntyre's discussion of potential-vorticity inversion](https://pordlabs.ucsd.edu/wryoung/theorySeminar/pdf14/McIntyrePV.pdf) explains the limiting independent-layer picture.
