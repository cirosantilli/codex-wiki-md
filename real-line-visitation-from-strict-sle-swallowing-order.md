# Real-line visitation from strict SLE swallowing order

↑ **Parent:** [Strict ordering threshold for SLE boundary swallowing](strict-ordering-threshold-for-sle-boundary-swallowing.md)

A continuous capacity-parameterized [Loewner trace](trace-of-a-loewner-chain.md) with finite, strictly ordered positive swallowing times visits every positive real point. An unvisited swallowed point has an interval disjoint from the compact trace up to its swallowing time; that interval is swallowed simultaneously, contradicting strict order. Reflection yields the negative-axis conclusion for chordal [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) at $\kappa\geq8$.
