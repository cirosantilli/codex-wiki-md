<h1 id="faxen-s-rotational-law">Faxén's rotational law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

For a sphere of radius $a$ in a slowly varying ambient [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) of [vorticity](vorticity.md) $\boldsymbol\omega_\infty$, its angular velocity and applied torque obey

$$
\boldsymbol\Omega=\frac{\mathbf G}{8\pi\mu a^3}+\frac12\boldsymbol\omega_\infty
$$

at the sphere centre.
