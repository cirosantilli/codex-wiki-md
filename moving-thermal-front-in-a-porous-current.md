# Moving thermal front in a porous current

↑ **Parent:** [Slope-driven porous gravity current](slope-driven-porous-gravity-current.md)

A localized temperature adjustment separates regions with different fluid density, viscosity and Darcy speed. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) in the moving frame gives the displayed plateau-depth relation. A speed stated as $V_T=\Gamma u_c$ with $u_c$ a [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) has effective retardation parameter $\phi\Gamma$. Setting both laboratory-frame mass fluxes equal neglects storage associated with passage of the temperature front.
