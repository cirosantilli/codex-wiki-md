# Thermopile absorber

↑ **Parent:** [Thermopile](thermopile.md)

A [thermopile absorber](thermopile-absorber.md) converts incident [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) into [heat](heat-split.md) near the [thermocouple measuring junctions](thermocouple-measuring-junction.md). Electrically separate junctions can share its [temperature](temperature-split.md) through thermal contact. The reference support provides a path for [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) and a comparison [temperature](temperature-split.md); “cold” denotes this reference role, even if it is warmer than the absorber.

Distinguish incident [power](power.md) from absorbed [power](power.md). A window, the illuminated [area](surface-area.md), and the absorption of the coating determine their relation. A detector's measured voltage sensitivity can depend on where a small illuminated spot lies.[p. 3, §3-1 Sensitivity, Eq. (3-1) and following paragraph: uniform illumination versus a localized spot](thermopile-detectors-by-hamamatsu-photonics-k-k.md) The model in [thermopile thermal response](thermopile-thermal-response.md) takes absorbed [power](power.md) as its input, so an optical calibration is a separate step.
