# Photon number flux density

↑ **Parent:** [Photon](photon.md)

The [photon number flux density](photon-number-flux-density.md) is the number of [photons](photon.md) crossing unit normal [area](surface-area.md) per unit time. A monochromatic beam of [light intensity](light-intensity.md) $I$ and [frequency](frequency.md) $f$ has $\Phi_N=I/(hf)$ because each [photon](photon.md) carries [energy](energy-split.md) $hf$. Its units are $\mathrm{m^{-2}s^{-1}}$. For a mixed spectrum, integrate the spectral [light intensity](light-intensity.md) divided by the corresponding [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md).
