# Transverse tidal equation of a Brinkmann plane wave

↑ **Parent:** [Plane-fronted gravitational wave](plane-fronted-gravitational-wave.md)

For the [plane-fronted gravitational wave](plane-fronted-gravitational-wave.md) $ds^2=2du\,dv+d\mathbf x^2+\mathbf x^TM(u)\mathbf x\,du^2$, synchronized neighboring [geodesics](geodesic.md) parametrized by $u$ have transverse separation $\boldsymbol\xi$ satisfying the displayed equation. Along the central [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) $(u,v,\mathbf x)=(\tau,-\tau/2,0)$ the [coordinate basis](coordinate-basis.md) is [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md), so this is also the transverse [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) equation in a transported frame. Vacuum requires $\operatorname{tr}M=0$, producing equal and opposite instantaneous transverse [tidal acceleration](tidal-acceleration.md) eigenvalues. The ordinary coordinate equation $\ddot\xi^v=0$ holds on that central reference, but generally fails on off-axis reference curves.
