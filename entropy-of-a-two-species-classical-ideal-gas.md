# Entropy of a two-species classical ideal gas

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy of mixing](entropy-of-mixing.md)

Two physically distinguishable noninteracting species with fixed particle numbers and the same single-particle [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) have the displayed [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md). If $Z_1\propto VT^{3/2}$, the leading large-particle-number [entropy](entropy-split.md) is $S=k_B\sum_{i=A,B}N_i[\ln(Z_1/N_i)+5/2]$. With $N=N_A+N_B$ and $x_i=N_i/N$, this equals the one-species expression at total number $N$ plus $-Nk_B\sum_i x_i\ln x_i$. Merely attaching labels to otherwise identical indistinguishable atoms does not justify this mixing term.
