# Two-mode string recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Standing wave](standing-wave.md)

For two distinct [sine](sine.md) [normal modes](normal-mode.md) with nonzero [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) and initial [cosine](cosine.md) [phases](phase-waves.md) zero, [linear independence](linear-independence.md) of their spatial profiles means the initial shape returns exactly only when both time factors equal one. A rational [frequency](frequency.md) ratio $\omega_2/\omega_1=p/q$ in lowest terms gives first positive recurrence $2\pi q/\omega_1$. An irrational ratio gives no exact positive recurrence, although arbitrarily close two-mode returns exist. These statements refer to the complete spatial profile, not a displacement at one observation point.
