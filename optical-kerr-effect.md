# Optical Kerr effect

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr effect](kerr-effect.md)

An intensity-dependent [refractive index](refractive-index.md) produced by the optical field itself. In a simple instantaneous isotropic model, $n=n_0+n_2I$. A transversely nonuniform [intensity](intensity-physics.md) produces [self-focusing](self-focusing.md) for positive $n_2$ and [self-defocusing](self-defocusing.md) for negative $n_2$. Temporal intensity variation produces [self-phase modulation](self-phase-modulation.md). These are consequences of a third-order [nonlinear optical susceptibility](nonlinear-optical-susceptibility.md), distinct from a static applied-field [electro-optic Kerr effect](electro-optic-kerr-effect.md).
