# Block-copolymer kinetic trapping

↑ **Parent:** [Diblock copolymer](diblock-copolymer.md)

A domain structure can persist because chain relaxation is too slow to reach the [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) morphology. A glassy block, rapid [solvent](solvent.md) loss or strong segregation can impede defect correction. Controlled thermal or [solvent](solvent.md) [annealing](annealing-materials-science.md) and time-dependent structural measurements distinguish arrested patterns from equilibrated phase stability.
