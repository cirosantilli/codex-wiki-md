# Inner Lagrange point

↑ **Parent:** [Collinear Lagrange point](collinear-lagrange-point.md)

The [Lagrange point](lagrange-point.md) lying between the components of a [circular restricted three-body problem](circular-restricted-three-body-problem.md). The critical effective potential through this saddle defines their [Roche lobes](roche-lobe.md); gas passing through it can initiate [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md).
