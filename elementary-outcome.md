# Elementary outcome

↑ **Parent:** [Sample space](sample-space.md)

An [elementary outcome](elementary-outcome.md) is one member of the [sample space](sample-space.md). For the ordered two-toss model, $HT$ is one outcome; it contains both toss results. By contrast, “at least one head” is an [event](event.md) containing three outcomes. The outcome $HT$ and the [singleton set](singleton-mathematics.md) $\{HT\}$ are different objects: when that [singleton](singleton-mathematics.md) is measurable, the notation $\mathbb P(\{HT\})$ gives its [probability](probability.md). In the finite model below, every [subset](subset.md) is measurable.

The word elementary refers to the chosen level of detail. An outcome can be an [ordered pair](ordered-pair.md), a sequence, or an entire realization of a [point process](point-process.md). A [random variable](random-variable-split.md) then extracts a numerical feature of that outcome; the [window count of a point process](window-count-of-a-point-process.md) is one example.
