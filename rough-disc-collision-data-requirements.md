# Rough-disc collision data requirements

↑ **Parent:** [Collision mechanics](collision-mechanics.md)

A rough contact may transmit both normal and tangential [impulses](impulse.md). Conservation of linear and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) fixes totals but does not specify their division between translation and rotation. Predicting outgoing states requires constitutive data such as normal restitution and a [friction](friction.md) or tangential restitution law. Rough contact is not synonymous with adhesive contact.
