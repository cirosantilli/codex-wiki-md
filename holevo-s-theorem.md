<h1 id="holevo-s-theorem">Holevo's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holevo's_theorem)

The classical mutual information obtained by any measurement of a quantum ensemble is at most its [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md). This follows from the [data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy](data-processing-inequality-for-quantum-relative-entropy.md) applied to the measurement channel.
