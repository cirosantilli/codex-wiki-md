# Renormalization of the tent map near its fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Tent map](tent-map.md)

For $1<\mu\le\sqrt2$, the interval $J=[1/(\mu+1),\mu/(\mu+1)]$ is invariant under the second iterate of the [tent map](tent-map.md). The affine coordinate $h(x)=(\mu/(\mu+1)-x)/((\mu-1)/(\mu+1))$ conjugates that restriction to the tent map with parameter $\mu^2$. Its two linear pieces give the identity directly. This turns the two-iterate horseshoe threshold into a four-iterate threshold and explains why chaotic invariant sets need not have periodic points near the original nonzero fixed point.
