# Energy Levels of Neutral Strontium by NIST

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

[https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/strontiumtable5.htm](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/strontiumtable5.htm)

Tabulated [strontium](strontium.md) [electron configurations](electron-configuration.md), [atomic terms](atomic-term.md), total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and [energy levels](energy-level.md) in $\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. Multiplication by $100$ converts to $\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The entries for $5s5p$ and $5s6s$ independently test assignments inferred from rounded optical [wavelengths](wavelength.md).
