# Cartan form of a regular mechanical Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics.md)

A [regular Lagrangian](regular-lagrangian.md) $L:TQ\to\mathbb R$ defines a fibre derivative $\mathbb FL:TQ\to T^*Q$ and the Cartan one-form $\theta_L=(\mathbb FL)^*\theta$. Its two-form $\omega_L=-d\theta_L$ is nondegenerate when the velocity Hessian is invertible. The energy is $E_L=v^iL_{v^i}-L$. The equation $\iota_{\Gamma_L}\omega_L=dE_L$ determines the Euler–Lagrange vector field. A [hyperregular Lagrangian](hyperregular-lagrangian.md) makes the fibre derivative a global diffeomorphism and transports this dynamics to Hamiltonian phase space.
