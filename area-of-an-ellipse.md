# Area of an ellipse

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipse](ellipse.md)

The region $x^2/a^2+y^2/b^2\leq1$, $a,b>0$, has [area](surface-area.md) $\pi ab$. The [linear map](linear-map.md) $(u,v)\mapsto(au,bv)$ sends the unit disk onto that region and multiplies [area](surface-area.md) by its [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $ab$.
