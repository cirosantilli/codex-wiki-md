# Regular gauge slice

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md)

A gauge condition $F(x)=0$ is regular at a representative $x_0$ if its derivative along the [gauge orbit](gauge-orbit.md) is an invertible [matrix](matrix.md). The [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) then makes the gauge-condition values valid local coordinates along that orbit. A unique zero without this regularity is insufficient: the translation action on the line with $F(x)=x^3$ has a unique zero but a zero derivative there. Multiple regular intersections give a [Gribov ambiguity](gribov-ambiguity.md) rather than a globally unique slice.
