# Nitrate

↑ **Parent:** [Anion](anion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitrate)

The [nitrate ion](nitrate.md), $\mathrm{NO}_3^-$, is an [anion](anion.md) with one [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) connected to three [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) and net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $-1$ in units of [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). Its 24 [valence electrons](valence-electron.md) admit three equivalent octet [resonance structures](resonance-structure.md); see [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md) for their construction and [chemical resonance](resonance-chemistry.md) for their interpretation. The N–O connections are [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md) within the [ion](ion.md). See [7.2 Covalent Bonding](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/7-2-covalent-bonding.md).
