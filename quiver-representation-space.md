# Quiver representation space

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a quiver](representation-of-a-quiver.md)

At fixed dimension vector, $\operatorname{Rep}_Q(\mathbf n)=\prod_{\rho:i\to j}\operatorname{Hom}_k(k^{n_i},k^{n_j})$ is an irreducible [affine space](affine-space.md) of dimension $\sum_\rho n_{s(\rho)}n_{t(\rho)}$. Its points are arrow-matrix collections, and its base-change orbits are isomorphism classes.
