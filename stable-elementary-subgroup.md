# Stable elementary subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Stable general linear group](stable-general-linear-group.md)

The subgroup generated by elementary [transvections](transvection.md) in finite [matrix](matrix.md) sizes, with identity stabilization. A third distinct index expresses each [transvection](transvection.md) as a commutator; stable block identities show that this subgroup is normal and that its quotient in the [stable general linear group](stable-general-linear-group.md) is abelian. Thus it is precisely the [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md).
