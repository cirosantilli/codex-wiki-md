<h1 id="normalization-proof-of-young-s-inequality">Normalization proof of Young's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Young's inequality for products](young-s-inequality-for-products.md)

The [conjugate-power constrained product maximum](conjugate-power-constrained-product-maximum.md) proves [Young's inequality for products](young-s-inequality-for-products.md) by rescaling. For nonnegative $x,y$ and [conjugate exponents](conjugate-exponents.md) $p,q>1$, let $A=x^p/p+y^q/q$. If $A=0$, both variables vanish. Otherwise the displayed normalized variables satisfy $X^p/p+Y^q/q=1$, hence $XY\leq1$. Since $1/p+1/q=1$, this means $xy/A\leq1$, proving $xy\leq x^p/p+y^q/q$. Equality in the constrained maximum requires $X=Y=1$, equivalent to $x^p=y^q$; the zero case gives the same equality criterion. This approach simultaneously gives the sharp constant and every equality case.
