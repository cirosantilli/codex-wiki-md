# Two-state ensembles of a maximally mixed qubit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state ensemble](quantum-state-ensemble.md)

For normalized [pure states](pure-state.md) $|u\rangle,|v\rangle$, a two-member [quantum state ensemble](quantum-state-ensemble.md) gives the [maximally mixed state](maximally-mixed-state.md) of a [qubit](qubit.md) exactly when its members are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) and equally probable. To prove necessity, take [expectation values](expectation-value.md) of its [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) in the two members: $1/2=p+(1-p)|\langle u|v\rangle|^2\geq p$ and $1/2=1-p+p|\langle u|v\rangle|^2\geq1-p$. Hence $p=1/2$ and the overlap vanishes. Sufficiency is the [resolution of the identity](resolution-of-the-identity.md) in an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md).
