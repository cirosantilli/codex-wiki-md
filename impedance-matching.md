# Impedance matching

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impedance_matching)

For a source with internal [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md) $Z_s$, conjugate matching $Z_L=Z_s^*$ maximizes delivered mean [power](power.md) when the source is fixed. For a purely resistive source and equal load [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R$, the load sees half the open-circuit [voltage](voltage.md). A [Johnson-Nyquist noise](thermal-electrical-noise.md) source with open-circuit mean-square voltage $4k_BTR\,d\nu$ therefore delivers $k_BT\,d\nu$ to the matched load, rather than $4k_BT\,d\nu$.
