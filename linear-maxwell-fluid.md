# Linear Maxwell fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)

A linear Maxwell fluid has [constitutive equation](constitutive-equation.md) $\boldsymbol\sigma+\lambda\partial_t\boldsymbol\sigma=\mu\dot{\boldsymbol\gamma}$, with $\dot{\boldsymbol\gamma}$ twice the [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md). Here $\mu$ is the zero-frequency [shear viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) and $\lambda>0$ is the [viscoelastic relaxation time](viscoelastic-relaxation-time.md). Stress at fixed strain decays as $e^{-t/\lambda}$; the corresponding series spring and dashpot have shear modulus $G=\mu/\lambda$. In small-amplitude simple shear from an isotropic relaxed state, the first-order forcing is off-diagonal, so both [normal-stress differences](normal-stress-difference.md) vanish at linear order. This is not a statement about the nonlinear objective model at second order in shear rate.
