# Exosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric escape](atmospheric-escape.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exosphere)

The exosphere is the dilute outer atmospheric region in which a particle can travel a substantial distance without a [collision](collision.md). Its lower boundary, the [exobase](exobase.md), is near equality of the [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) and the local [atmospheric scale height](atmospheric-scale-height.md). Escaping and gravitationally bound trajectories can coexist.
