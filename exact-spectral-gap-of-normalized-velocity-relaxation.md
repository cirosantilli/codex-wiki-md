# Exact spectral gap of normalized velocity relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted Hilbert structure of velocity-reset relaxation](weighted-hilbert-structure-of-velocity-reset-relaxation.md)

For the [normalized velocity-reset collision operator](normalized-velocity-reset-collision-operator.md) on the weighted [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), $L=\Pi-I$ vanishes on the equilibrium direction and equals $-I$ on its [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md). The [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) is exactly one. Conservation of the projected component and the displayed energy identity give exact exponential decay of the remaining component; the full solution need not decay to zero.
