# Scalar graph power-counting identity

↑ **Parent:** [Superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md)

For a connected [scalar field theory](scalar-field-theory-split.md) graph without derivative vertices, each independent [loop momentum](loop-momentum.md) supplies $d$ powers and each internal [scalar propagator](scalar-propagator.md) removes two. Thus $D=dL-2I$. The graph identities $L=I-\sum_nV_n+1$ and $2I+E=\sum_n nV_n$ give the displayed formula. The [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) of the field is $(d-2)/2$ and that of $g_n$ is $n-d(n/2-1)$. Negative-dimensional couplings allow the superficial degree to grow with vertex count. This is overall [power counting in quantum field theory](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md); subgraphs can diverge even when the whole graph has negative degree.
