# GHZ contradiction for N congruent to 3 modulo 4

↑ **Parent:** [GHZ theorem](ghz-theorem.md)

For $|G_N^-\rangle=(|0\rangle^{\otimes N}-|1\rangle^{\otimes N})/\sqrt2$, let $O_j$ have one [Pauli X gate](pauli-x-gate.md) at site $j$ and [Pauli Y gates](pauli-y-gate.md) elsewhere, and let $X_N=X^{\otimes N}$. The relations $Y|0\rangle=i|1\rangle$, $Y|1\rangle=-i|0\rangle$ show that $O_j|G_N^-\rangle$ is proportional to $|G_N^-\rangle$ exactly when $N$ is odd. In that case its [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $\lambda_N=(-1)^{(N+1)/2}$, while $X_N$ always has [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $-1$.

Under the [EPR criterion of reality](epr-criterion-of-reality.md) and locality, each site's $X$ and $Y$ outcomes have predetermined values $x_j,y_j\in\{\pm1\}$. Multiplying the $N$ certain equations $x_j\prod_{k\ne j}y_k=\lambda_N$ gives $\prod_jx_j=\lambda_N$ when $N$ is odd, since every $y_k$ appears an even number of times. This contradicts the certain quantum value $-1$ precisely when $N\equiv3\pmod4$. Thus the displayed [GHZ state](greenberger-horne-zeilinger-state.md) gives an infinite family of perfect-correlation contradictions with [local hidden-variable theory](local-hidden-variable-theory.md), without an inequality.
