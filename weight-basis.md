# Weight basis

↑ **Parent:** [Weight-space decomposition](weight-space-decomposition.md)

A weight basis of a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) is a [basis](basis.md) consisting of [weight vectors](weight-vector.md) for a chosen [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md). When the Cartan action is diagonalizable, choose a basis separately in every [weight space](weight-space.md) and combine them. This makes [weight multiplicities](weight-multiplicity.md) directly visible and gives diagonal matrices for Cartan elements.
