# Euclidean norm duality

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md)

The Euclidean norm is self-dual:

$$
\sup_{\lVert v\rVert_2\leq1}|x^Tv|=\lVert x\rVert_2.
$$

The upper bound is [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), and equality holds at $v=x/\lVert x\rVert_2$ when $x\ne0$.
