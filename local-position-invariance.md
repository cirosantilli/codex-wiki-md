# Local position invariance

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein equivalence principle](einstein-equivalence-principle.md)

Local position invariance requires identical local experiments to have the same outcome independently of where and when they are performed. For nongravitational experiments it is part of the [Einstein equivalence principle](einstein-equivalence-principle.md). Comparisons of clocks through [gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md) test this principle once the gravitational potential difference is independently specified; they do not by themselves test the whole [strong equivalence principle](strong-equivalence-principle.md).
