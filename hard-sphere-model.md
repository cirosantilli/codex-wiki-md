# Hard-sphere model

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere packing](sphere-packing.md)

A model assigning nonoverlapping [spheres](sphere.md) to particles; contacts express geometric constraints. A crystallographic sublattice need not be physically close-packed even when its [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) is face-centred cubic.
