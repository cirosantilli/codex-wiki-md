# Dyadic filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Filtration (probability theory)](filtration-probability-theory.md)

On $(0,1]^d$, the dyadic [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) is generated by the partitions into half-open cubes of side $2^{-n}$. The [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) of an integrable function is its average on each atom. The increasing partitions generate the Borel sigma-algebra. Step functions on these partitions are dense in $L^1$; approximation and the [Doob maximal inequality](doob-maximal-inequality-for-a-nonnegative-submartingale.md) show that their conditional averages converge in $L^1$ and [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) to the original function.
