# Shear stress

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shear_stress)

Shear stress is the tangential component of traction on a surface. For leading unidirectional flow $u(y)$ in a Newtonian fluid, $\tau=\mu\,du/dy$.
