<h1 id="picard-lindelof-theorem">Picard-Lindelöf theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picard–Lindelöf_theorem)

A vector field that is continuous in time and locally [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) in the state variable gives a unique local solution to its initial-value problem. A solution extends while it remains in a compact subset of the vector field's domain.
