# Formal adjoint of the worldsheet conformal Killing operator

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet diffeomorphism ghost operator](worldsheet-diffeomorphism-ghost-operator.md)

Use the half-normalized operator $(P_1v)_{ab}=(\nabla_av_b+\nabla_bv_a-h_{ab}\nabla_cv^c)/2$. On a closed [worldsheet](worldsheet.md), a symmetric trace-free tensor obeys

$$
\int\sqrt h\,t^{ab}(P_1v)_{ab}=-\int\sqrt h\,(\nabla_at^{ab})v_b.
$$

This [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) identifies the displayed formal [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md). A convention with twice $P_1$ has twice this adjoint, with identical kernels. The [worldsheet conformal Killing group](worldsheet-conformal-killing-group.md) corresponds to the kernel of $P_1$, while [infinitesimal worldsheet moduli](infinitesimal-worldsheet-modulus.md) correspond to the trace-free kernel of its adjoint.
