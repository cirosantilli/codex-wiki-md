# Inversion of the gauge-fixed Maxwell kinetic operator

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant gauge](covariant-gauge.md)

[Gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) removes the noninvertible longitudinal direction of the Maxwell quadratic action. In a gauge with parameter $\xi$, the Fourier kernel is $-k^2\eta^{\mu\nu}+(1-\xi^{-1})k^\mu k^\nu$. Multiplying its inverse by $i$ gives the [photon propagator](photon-propagator.md), with the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md). At $\xi=1$ the answer is $-i\eta_{\mu\nu}/(k^2+i0)$.
