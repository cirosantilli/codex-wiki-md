# Heterotrimeric G protein

↑ **Parent:** [G protein](g-protein.md)

A [heterotrimeric G protein](heterotrimeric-g-protein.md) is a signaling assembly with $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ subunits. Receptor-promoted nucleotide exchange on $\alpha$ regulates interactions with $\beta\gamma$ and effectors; both signaling components can contribute downstream.
