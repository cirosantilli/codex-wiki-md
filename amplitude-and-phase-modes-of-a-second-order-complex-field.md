# Amplitude and phase modes of a second-order complex field

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mode](higgs-mode.md)

For $-\gamma\ddot\psi=-\psi''+\beta(|\psi|^2-1)\psi$ with positive $\beta,\gamma$, expand about $\psi=1$ as $1+\rho/2+i\theta$. The real and imaginary equations become $\gamma\ddot\rho=\rho''-2\beta\rho$ and $\gamma\ddot\theta=\theta''$. Plane waves give a gapped radial amplitude branch and a gapless tangential phase branch. This second-order model has two independent real mode coordinates, unlike [density-phase conjugacy](density-phase-conjugacy.md) in first-order dynamics.
