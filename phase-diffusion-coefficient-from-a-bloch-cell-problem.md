# Phase-diffusion coefficient from a Bloch cell problem

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch stability operator](bloch-stability-operator.md)

Suppose a [Bloch stability operator](bloch-stability-operator.md) has expansion $L(q)=L_0+iqL_1-q^2L_2+\cdots$, with a simple neutral [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) $v_0$ and an adjoint neutral function $w$ normalized by $\langle w,v_0\rangle=1$. Expand $v(q)=v_0+iqv_1+\cdots$ and $\lambda(q)=icq-Dq^2+\cdots$. The [Fredholm alternative](fredholm-alternative.md) gives $c=\langle w,L_1v_0\rangle$ and the cell problem $L_0v_1=cv_0-L_1v_0$, $\langle w,v_1\rangle=0$. Applying the adjoint solvability condition at second order gives the displayed phase-diffusion [coefficient](coefficient.md). Multiple translation [phases](phase-waves.md) or a conserved mean require a coupled matrix problem rather than this scalar formula.
