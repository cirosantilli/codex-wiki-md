# Bogomolny vortex equation

↑ **Parent:** [Nielsen-Olesen vortex](nielsen-olesen-vortex.md)

At critical coupling on a surface with conformal metric $g=\Omega dzd\bar z$, the vortex equations are

$$
D_{\bar z}\phi=0,
\qquad
B=\frac\Omega2(1-|\phi|^2).
$$
