# Faddeev-Zamolodchikov associativity constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Faddeev-Zamolodchikov algebra](faddeev-zamolodchikov-algebra.md)

Starting with three ordered [rapidities](rapidity.md), the adjacent-exchange sequences $121$ and $212$ must give identical coefficients for every final species word in the [Faddeev-Zamolodchikov algebra](faddeev-zamolodchikov-algebra.md). The exchange parameters are $\theta$, $\theta+\theta'$ and $\theta'$ because rapidity differences add. These coefficient identities express the spectral [Yang-Baxter equation](yang-baxter-equation.md). One scalar identity is necessary but generally does not exhaust all the constraints.
