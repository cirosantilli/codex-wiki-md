# Ontological model of a quantum system

↑ **Parent:** [Foundations of quantum mechanics](foundations-of-quantum-mechanics.md)

An ontological model associates each [quantum state preparation](quantum-state-preparation.md) with a [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) over underlying physical states $\lambda$. A [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md)'s response probabilities depend on its specification and $\lambda$, sum to one, and reproduce the [Born rule](born-rule.md) when averaged over the preparation distribution. The model need not assign deterministic outcomes. Overlap or disjointness of preparation measures defines the [psi-epistemic model](psi-epistemic-model.md) versus [psi-ontic model](psi-ontic-model.md) distinction.
