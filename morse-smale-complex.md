# Morse-Smale complex

↑ **Parent:** [Morse chain complex](morse-chain-complex.md)

Here $n(p,q)$ is the signed number of unparametrized trajectories of a [Morse-Smale gradient flow](morse-smale-gradient-flow.md) from $p$ to $q$. Compactifying one-dimensional trajectory moduli spaces gives $\partial^2=0$. The generators coincide with the cells furnished by the [unstable manifolds](unstable-manifold.md), and the trajectory count is their cellular incidence number, so the complex computes integral [homology](homology-split.md).
