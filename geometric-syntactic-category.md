# Geometric syntactic category

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric theory](geometric-theory.md)

Objects are [geometric formulas](geometric-formula.md) in context modulo provable equivalence. Morphisms are provably total, single-valued geometric relations; composition is existential conjunction. Conjunction and equality give [finite limits](finite-limit.md). Interpreting formulas in a model gives a finite-limit-preserving [functor](functor.md), with the additional continuity condition supplied by the [geometric syntactic topology](geometric-syntactic-topology.md).
