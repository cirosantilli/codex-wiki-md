# Moving-defect locking of a cubic-quintic front

↑ **Parent:** [Translation-mode solvability for a bistable front](translation-mode-solvability-for-a-bistable-front.md)

At the [Maxwell balance for a scalar reaction-diffusion front](maxwell-balance-for-a-scalar-reaction-diffusion-front.md), let $q=3\alpha/4$, $\beta=\sqrt3\alpha/2$, and perturb the linear growth rate by $\varepsilon K\delta(x+cT)$, $T=\varepsilon t$. Projection onto the front's translation mode gives $X_T=-K\operatorname{sech}^2(\beta Z/2)$, where $Z=X+cT+\beta^{-1}\log q$. Hence $Z_T=c-K\operatorname{sech}^2(\beta Z/2)$. Finite locked positions exist for $0<c/K\le1$. For a strict inequality, the root with $KZ<0$ is stable and the other unstable; they meet in a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md) at $c=K$. This is a leading-order [solvability condition](solvability-condition.md), not an exact finite-perturbation solution.
