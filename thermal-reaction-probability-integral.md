# Thermal reaction probability integral

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md)

For a one-dimensional channel, $k(T)Q_r=(2\pi\hbar)^{-1}\int_0^\infty e^{-\beta E}P(E)\,dE$. The reactant [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) and any length normalization must be specified.
