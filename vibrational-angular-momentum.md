# Vibrational angular momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)

The [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) associated with motion in a degenerate bending [normal mode](normal-mode.md) of a linear [molecule](molecule-split.md). A one-quantum bend has $l=\pm1$, and the total rotational [quantum number](quantum-number.md) obeys $J\geq|l|$.
