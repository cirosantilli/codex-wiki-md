# Gaussian hot spot under diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

On $\mathbb R^d$, the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) with initial [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) $u_0(x)=e^{-|x-x_0|^2/a}$, $a>0$, has the displayed solution. Convolving the initial profile with the [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) adds the Gaussian width parameters; conservation of its spatial integral fixes the prefactor. Direct differentiation provides another proof. The width increases while the peak decreases, with no loss of the total integral.
