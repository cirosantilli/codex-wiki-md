# Fluid mosaic model

↑ **Parent:** [Cell membrane](cell-membrane.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_mosaic_model)

The [fluid mosaic model](fluid-mosaic-model.md) describes a [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md) as a fluid [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md) with embedded [membrane proteins](membrane-protein.md). It connects a continuous barrier to a mosaic of functional components: [diffusion](diffusion.md) within the membrane can coexist with selective passage across it. S. J. Singer and G. L. Nicolson presented the [fluid mosaic model](fluid-mosaic-model.md) in Science in 1972; see [Singer and Nicolson, The fluid mosaic model of the structure of cell membranes](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4333397/). The useful picture does not require every [protein](protein.md) to move freely: attachments and interactions can restrict movement, as discussed in [Cooper, The Cell: structure of the plasma membrane](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9898/).
