# Seed plant

↑ **Parent:** [Vascular plant](vascular-plant.md)

A plant lineage reproducing through [seeds](seed.md), including [gymnosperms](gymnosperm.md) and [angiosperms](angiosperm.md). Reduction and retention of gametophytes and male delivery through [pollen](pollen.md) distinguish their reproductive ecology from a typical free-living fern [gametophyte](gametophyte.md).
