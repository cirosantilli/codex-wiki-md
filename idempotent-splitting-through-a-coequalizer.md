# Idempotent splitting through a coequalizer

↑ **Parent:** [Splitting of an idempotent morphism](splitting-of-an-idempotent-morphism.md)

If $e=ir$ and $ri=1$, then $r$ is the [coequalizer](coequalizer.md) of $(e,1)$: an arrow $h$ with $he=h$ factors uniquely as $(hi)r$. Conversely a [coequalizer](coequalizer.md) $q$ makes $e$ factor as $iq=e$; epimorphic cancellation gives $qi=1$. Thus the [splitting of an idempotent morphism](splitting-of-an-idempotent-morphism.md) is equivalent to this particular [coequalizer](coequalizer.md), without assuming arbitrary [coequalizers](coequalizer.md) exist.
