# Laminar Ekman boundary stress

↑ **Parent:** [Ekman layer](ekman-layer.md)

For $f>0$, a stationary lower [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md) below uniform exterior [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md) $W_g$ produces $W_E=-W_g e^{-(1+i)z/\delta_E}$. The upward shear component at the bottom is $T_b=\rho\nu W_E^{\prime}(0)=\rho\sqrt{f\nu/2}(1+i)W_g$; the bottom traction on the fluid is $-T_b$. This law cannot be assigned to an arbitrary prescribed surface [wind stress](wind-stress.md) without an additional boundary condition.
