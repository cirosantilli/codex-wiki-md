# Cdc25

↑ **Parent:** [Cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cdc25)

[Cdc25](cdc25.md) is a [protein phosphatase](protein-phosphatase.md): it removes inhibitory [phosphate](phosphate.md) groups from [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md), relieving inhibition of an appropriately prepared [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md)–[CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) assembly. Its reaction is removal, whereas [Wee1](wee1.md) is a [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) adding inhibitory modification. Removing the inhibitory sites is different from removing the activating [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) supplied by [CAK](cdk-activating-kinase.md).[Components of the Cell-Cycle Control System: inhibitory phosphorylation and Figure 17-18, Cdc25-mediated removal of inhibitory phosphates](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/components-of-the-cell-cycle-control-system.md)

[Cdc25](cdc25.md) action on [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) must also be distinguished from [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) of [Cdc25](cdc25.md) itself. In [cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md), [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md)-dependent regulation promotes [Cdc25](cdc25.md) activity, which can then activate additional inhibited [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md). The complete regulatory path can involve intermediates. A [histone H1 kinase assay](histone-h1-kinase-assay.md) of a mixture reports downstream [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) action; assigning its change to [Cdc25](cdc25.md) requires a perturbation or an independent [protein phosphatase](protein-phosphatase.md) assay, rather than interpreting [histone H1](histone-h1.md) labeling as phosphate removal.
