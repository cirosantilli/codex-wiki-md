# Patched conic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Celestial mechanics](celestial-mechanics-split.md)

The [patched conic approximation](patched-conic-approximation.md) models different portions of a spacecraft trajectory by separate two-body [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md), matching [position](position.md) and [velocity](velocity.md) where one attracting body gives way to another. For a surface departure from a nonrotating planet, [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) gives $u^2=v_e^2+w^2$, where $u$ is surface launch [speed](speed.md), $v_e$ is the planet's surface [escape speed](escape-velocity.md), and $w$ is the planet-relative [hyperbolic excess speed](hyperbolic-excess-speed.md). Adding that excess [velocity](velocity.md) vector to the planet's orbital [velocity](velocity.md) supplies the heliocentric initial condition. It neglects the simultaneous full gravitational action of both bodies near the matching region.
