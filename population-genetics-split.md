# Population genetics

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_genetics)

Population genetics studies the frequencies of [alleles](allele.md) and [genotypes](genotype.md) in populations, and their changes through inheritance, mutation, selection, migration and random sampling. [Kingman's coalescent](kingman-s-coalescent.md) describes a neutral sample's ancestry backward in time.
