# Two-field scalar Wick identity

↑ **Parent:** [Wick's theorem](wick-s-theorem.md)

For a free real [scalar field](scalar-field.md), split $\phi=\phi^{(+)}+\phi^{(-)}$ into annihilation and creation parts. Moving the creation operators left gives $\phi(x)\phi(y)=:\phi(x)\phi(y):+[\phi^{(+)}(x),\phi^{(-)}(y)]$. The [commutator](commutator.md) is the [Wightman function](wightman-function.md). Choosing either time order gives the displayed identity with the [Feynman propagator](feynman-propagator.md). It is an identity of [operator-valued distributions](operator-valued-distribution.md); coincident-point products need a suitable prescription.
