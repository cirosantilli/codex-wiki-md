# Picard iterate

↑ **Parent:** [Picard iteration](picard-iteration.md)

A [Picard iterate](picard-iterate.md) is one of the successive approximate [solutions of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) obtained by repeatedly applying its integral solution operator. If $y'=f(t,y)$ and $y(0)=b$, the usual operator is $(Ty)(t)=b+\int_0^t f(s,y(s))\,ds$. An [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) can produce a different, equivalent operator with better estimates, as in [Global Picard iteration with a decaying kernel](global-picard-iteration-with-a-decaying-kernel.md). The word iterate denotes an approximate function; [Picard iteration](picard-iteration.md) denotes the procedure.
