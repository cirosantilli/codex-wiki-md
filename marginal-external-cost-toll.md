# Marginal external cost toll

↑ **Parent:** [Wardrop equilibrium](wardrop-equilibrium.md)

For a differentiable link delay $D_j(y)$, the toll $T_j(y)=yD_j'(y)$ charges a user for the additional delay imposed on the other users. Its perceived link cost is the derivative of $yD_j(y)$. The resulting [Beckmann potential](beckmann-potential.md) equals total travel delay. If total delay is [convex](convex-function.md), its [Wardrop equilibria](wardrop-equilibrium.md) minimize that delay globally. Strict increase of $D_j$ alone does not imply this convexity; [optimal-flow calibrated congestion tolling](optimal-flow-calibrated-congestion-tolling.md) avoids that extra requirement.
