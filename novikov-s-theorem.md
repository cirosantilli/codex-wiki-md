<h1 id="novikov-s-theorem">Novikov's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian random field](gaussian-random-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novikov's_theorem)

For a centered [Gaussian random field](gaussian-random-field.md) $W$ with covariance $C$ and a sufficiently regular functional $F[W]$, the Furutsu--Novikov formula is the functional [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) identity

$$
\mathbb E[W(x)F[W]]
=\int C(x,y)\mathbb E\left[\frac{\delta F}{\delta W(y)}\right]dy.
$$

It closes moment equations for systems driven multiplicatively by Gaussian fluctuations.
