# Product substitution for a first-order equation

↑ **Parent:** [Separable differential equation](separable-differential-equation.md)

The [product rule](product-rule.md) gives $v'=xy'+y$ for $v=xy$, reducing the displayed equation to the autonomous [separable differential equation](separable-differential-equation.md) $v'=F(v)$. On an interval where $F(v)\ne0$, integrate $\int dv/F(v)=x+C$ and recover $y=v/x$ for $x\ne0$. Zeros of $F$ give additional constant solutions for $v$ and must be considered before division. A solution for $y$ extends through zero only if the recovered quotient has the necessary finite derivatives and satisfies the undivided original equation there.
