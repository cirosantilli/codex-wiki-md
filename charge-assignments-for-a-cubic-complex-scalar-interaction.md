# Charge assignments for a cubic complex-scalar interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Global phase symmetry](global-phase-symmetry.md)

The interaction $-\tfrac12(h\phi^2\Phi+h^*\phi^{\dagger2}\Phi^\dagger)$ is invariant under a [global phase symmetry](global-phase-symmetry.md) precisely when the displayed charge relation holds. With $q_\phi=1$, a heavy [particle](particle-physics.md) annihilated by $\Phi$ has charge $-2$, so its allowed [decay channel](decay-channel.md) is $\Phi\to\bar\phi\bar\phi$. The reverse heavy [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) decays into $\phi\phi$. Crossing only one heavy external state changes the exchange channel of mixed-species [quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md).
