# Stefan-Boltzmann estimate of Schwarzschild evaporation time

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole evaporation](black-hole-evaporation.md)

Approximate an isolated [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) as emitting photon [blackbody radiation](black-body-radiation.md) from an area $16\pi G^2M^2/c^4$ at [Hawking temperature](hawking-temperature.md) $\hbar c^3/(8\pi Gk_BM)$. The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives $P=\hbar c^6/(15360\pi G^2M^2)$. Integrating $c^2dM/dt=-P$ gives the displayed lifetime, about $2\times10^{67}$ years for a solar mass. [Greybody factors](greybody-factor.md) and additional emitted species modify the coefficient; the robust large-mass scaling is $M_0^3$, and the semiclassical calculation does not determine the final quantum-gravity endpoint.
