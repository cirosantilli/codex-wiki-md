# Stationary Gaussian density for a linear Fokker-Planck equation

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate Fokker-Planck probability current](multivariate-fokker-planck-probability-current.md)

For linear drift $A(x)=ax$ and constant symmetric positive-definite diffusion matrix $b$, a zero-current stationary Gaussian $P(x)\propto\exp(-x^TDx/2)$ obeys

$$
a+\frac12bD=0.
$$

This reversible solution exists when $D=-2b^{-1}a$ is symmetric positive definite. Its covariance $C=D^{-1}$ satisfies the [Continuous Lyapunov equation](continuous-lyapunov-equation.md)

$$
aC+Ca^T+b=0.
$$
