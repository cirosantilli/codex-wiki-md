# Alpha preformation probability

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha decay](alpha-decay.md)

The [alpha preformation probability](alpha-preformation-probability.md) is a model-dependent nuclear-structure factor multiplying an interior assault [frequency](frequency.md) and barrier [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md) in an alpha-decay rate estimate. It accounts for the availability of an alpha-like cluster at the matching surface. It is not fixed by the exterior Coulomb tunnelling exponent alone.
