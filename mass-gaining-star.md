# Mass-gaining star

↑ **Parent:** [Binary star](binary-star.md)

A mass-gaining star accretes material from a companion. Its changing mass and composition can alter its [main sequence](main-sequence.md) location and cause [stellar rejuvenation](stellar-rejuvenation.md).
