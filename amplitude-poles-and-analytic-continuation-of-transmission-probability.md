# Amplitude poles and analytic continuation of transmission probability

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md)

For a real one-dimensional potential, a real-axis [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md) is a modulus square. Its algebraic complex continuation need not have only physical [bound-state poles](bound-state-pole-of-the-scattering-amplitude.md). In a square well, $t=e^{-ika}/D(k)$ while the continued [probability](probability.md) is $1/[D(k)D(-k)]$. Zeros of the second factor can encode a different [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md). In units $\hbar^2/(2m)=1$, a well of width two and depth $1+\cot^2 1$ has a continued-probability [pole](pole.md) at $k=i\cot1$ which satisfies neither decaying even nor decaying odd matching. The physical bound-state criterion uses the outgoing [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) on its decaying sheet.
