# Decision problem

↑ **Parent:** [Computational problem](computational-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_problem)

A decision problem asks for one of two answers, conventionally encoded as zero and one. Equivalently, it asks whether an input belongs to a [formal language](formal-language.md).
