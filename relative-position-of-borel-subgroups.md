# Relative position of Borel subgroups

↑ **Parent:** [Bruhat decomposition](bruhat-decomposition.md)

The [relative position of Borel subgroups](relative-position-of-borel-subgroups.md) $B_1,B_2$ is the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) index of their simultaneous conjugacy orbit. After moving the first subgroup to a fixed $B$, the second has the form $gBg^{-1}$ and its index is the double coset $BgB$. This identifies the diagonal action on pairs of flags with the [Bruhat decomposition](bruhat-decomposition.md).
