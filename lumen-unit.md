# Lumen (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [Luminous flux](luminous-flux.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumen_(unit))

The [lumen](lumen-unit.md) is the [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md): $1\,\mathrm{lm}=1\,\mathrm{cd}\,\mathrm{sr}$. A source with constant [luminous intensity](luminous-intensity.md) $1\,\mathrm{cd}$ within a cone of [solid angle](solid-angle.md) $1\,\mathrm{sr}$ supplies $1\,\mathrm{lm}$ into that cone. A source with the same [luminous intensity](luminous-intensity.md) in every direction supplies $4\pi\,\mathrm{lm}$ over a full [sphere](sphere.md). [SI base unit: candela (cd)](si-base-unit-candela-cd.md)
