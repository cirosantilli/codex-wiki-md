# Particle identification

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

[Particle identification](particle-identification.md) assigns a species hypothesis to a detector signal or reconstructed track. Curvature in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) supplies [magnetic rigidity](magnetic-rigidity.md); additional responses, such as those of a [Cherenkov detector](cherenkov-detector.md) or a [particle shower](particle-shower.md), help distinguish an [electron](electron.md) from a [hadron](hadron.md). The identity hypothesis enters the [energy–momentum relation](energy-momentum-relation.md) and therefore the reconstructed [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md). An apparatus can observe a peak before it can identify every decay channel, as the [SPEAR psi energy-scan experiment](spear-psi-energy-scan-experiment.md) illustrates.
