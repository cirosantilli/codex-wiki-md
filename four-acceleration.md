# Four-acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-acceleration)

The [four-acceleration](four-acceleration.md) is the [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) of [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) along a massive observer's [worldline](world-line.md): $A^\mu=dU^\mu/d\tau+\Gamma^\mu_{\nu\rho}U^\nu U^\rho$. In Cartesian [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) coordinates the [Christoffel symbols](christoffel-symbol.md) vanish, leaving the ordinary [proper time](proper-time.md) derivative. Differentiating the constant velocity norm gives $U\cdot A=0$; in the instantaneous [rest frame](rest-frame.md), $A=(0,\mathbf a)$, so $A\cdot A=-a^2$ with $+---$ [metric signature](metric-signature.md). The magnitude $a$ is the [proper acceleration](proper-acceleration.md).
