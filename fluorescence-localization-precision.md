# Fluorescence localization precision

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md)

The precision of a fitted single-emitter position is distinct from its diffraction-broadened [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md) width. An ideal [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) image of width $s$ with $N$ independent detected [photons](photon.md) gives $\sigma_{\rm loc}\simeq s/\sqrt N$. Pixel size, background, model error, sample motion and drift limit practical performance; precision alone does not guarantee accuracy.[Localization uncertainty with finite photon number, pixelation and background](precise-nanometer-localization-analysis-for-individual-fluorescent-probes-by-russell-e-thompson-and-others.md)
