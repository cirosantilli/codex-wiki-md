# Universal property of a tensor product

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product](tensor-product.md)

For [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) $V_1,\ldots,V_r$, every [multilinear map](multilinear-map.md) $F:V_1\times\cdots\times V_r\to W$ factors uniquely through a [linear map](linear-map.md) $\widetilde F:V_1\otimes\cdots\otimes V_r\to W$ sending a decomposable tensor to $F$ of its factors. Thus multilinear formulas define [linear maps](linear-map.md) on [tensor products](tensor-product.md) without choosing a basis. [Tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md) is an example, using the evaluation pairing between a vector and a [covector](covector.md).
