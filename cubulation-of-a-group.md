# Cubulation of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Dual cube complex of a wallspace](dual-cube-complex-of-a-wallspace.md)

A cubulation of a group is a metrically proper action by cubical automorphisms on a [CAT(0) cube complex](cat-0-cube-complex.md). It is cocompact when the action is also a [cocompact group action](cocompact-group-action.md).
