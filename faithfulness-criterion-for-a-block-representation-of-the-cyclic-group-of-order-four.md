# Faithfulness criterion for a block representation of the cyclic group of order four

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic group of order four](cyclic-group-of-order-four.md)

For real $b,c$, this matrix defines a [group representation](group-representation.md) of $C_4=\langle a:a^4=e\rangle$ exactly when $bc=\pm1$. Indeed its lower block $B$ satisfies $B^2=bcI_2$, so $T(a)^4=I_3$ exactly when $(bc)^2=1$. If $bc=-1$, $T(a)^2=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1)\ne I_3$, so its order is four and the representation is [faithful](faithful-group-action.md). If $bc=1$, its order is exactly two and the [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is $\{e,a^2\}$, giving an unfaithful representation. Other values do not give representations at all, including $bc=0$, when the proposed matrix is singular.
