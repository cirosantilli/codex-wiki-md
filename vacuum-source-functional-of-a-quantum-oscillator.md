# Vacuum source functional of a quantum oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized vacuum generating functional](normalized-vacuum-generating-functional.md)

[Completing the square](completing-the-square.md) in $S[q]=\tfrac12qKq+Jq$ with $K=-\partial_t^2-m^2+i\epsilon$ gives the displayed source factor, where $G=K^{-1}$. In the frequency convention $e^{i\omega t}$, $\widetilde G=(\omega^2-m^2+i\epsilon)^{-1}$ and $G(t)=-ie^{-im|t|}/(2m)$ for $m>0$. The physical time-ordered oscillator [quantum field theory propagator](propagator.md) is $iG$. Sources are first taken as [test functions](test-function.md) so the pairings are defined.
