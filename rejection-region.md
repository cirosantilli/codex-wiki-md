# Rejection region

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test.md)

A rejection region is the set of sample outcomes on which a [hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test.md) rejects its [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md). Its probability under the null determines the [Type I error](type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) rate.
