# Nanowire junction resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Metal nanowire percolation](metal-nanowire-percolation.md)

[Electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) associated with transfer between intersecting [nanowires](nanowire.md). Contact geometry, adsorbed [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) and oxide layers, [welding](welding.md) and pressure affect it. Reducing [nanowire junction resistance](nanowire-junction-resistance.md) can improve a transparent network's [sheet resistance](sheet-resistance.md) without increasing its metal coverage.
