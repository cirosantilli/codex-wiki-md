# Developmental dyscalculia

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical difficulty](mathematical-difficulty.md)

Persistent difficulty with mathematical [learning](learning.md) in an appropriate developmental and educational assessment context. The label does not establish one identical cognitive deficit in every child or justify diagnosis from a single low test score.
