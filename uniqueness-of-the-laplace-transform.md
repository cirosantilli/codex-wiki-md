# Uniqueness of the Laplace transform

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md)

If two piecewise continuous [functions](function-split.md) of [exponential order](exponential-order.md) on $[0,\infty)$ have the same unilateral [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) for every sufficiently large positive real parameter, they agree at every common point of continuity and agree almost everywhere. For continuous [functions](function-split.md), the equality holds everywhere. Values at an isolated discontinuity cannot be recovered from an ordinary [integral transform](integral-transform-split.md).
