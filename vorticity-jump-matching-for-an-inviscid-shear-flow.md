# Vorticity-jump matching for an inviscid shear flow

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md)

Across a jump of $U'$ in a constant-density parallel flow with continuous $U$, the [normal mode](normal-mode.md) vertical velocity and pressure are continuous. The linearized momentum equation gives $\widehat p=\rho[(U-c)\widehat w'-U'\widehat w]/(ik)$, yielding the displayed conditions. Equivalently, away from $U=c$, $(U-c)[\widehat w']=[U']\widehat w$. Derivative continuity is generally incorrect when the background [vorticity](vorticity.md) jumps. This is the constant-density, continuous-velocity specialization of [jump conditions for stratified inviscid shear flow](jump-conditions-for-stratified-inviscid-shear-flow.md).
