# Uniform convergence preserves integrals on a compact interval

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md)

For [Riemann integrable](riemann-integrable-function.md) functions on a fixed compact interval, [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) implies convergence of their [integrals](integral.md) by the displayed estimate. It need not imply convergence of [derivatives](derivative.md): $f_n(x)=\sin(n^2x)/n$ converges uniformly to zero on $[-1,1]$, while $f_n'(0)=n$.
