# Bounding volume hierarchy traversal

↑ **Parent:** [Bounding volume hierarchy](bounding-volume-hierarchy.md)

A [bounding volume hierarchy traversal](bounding-volume-hierarchy-traversal.md) visits only nodes whose ancestors passed a [conservative enclosure test](conservative-enclosure-test.md). It can use [depth-first search](depth-first-search.md); its [time complexity](time-complexity.md) depends on how many nodes pass, not just the [height of a binary tree](height-of-a-binary-tree.md).
