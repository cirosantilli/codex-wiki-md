# Homothetic convex bodies

↑ **Parent:** [Convex body](convex-body.md)

Two [convex bodies](convex-body.md) are [homothetic convex bodies](homothetic-convex-bodies.md) if one is a [translation](translation-geometry.md) of a positive [uniform dilation](uniform-dilation.md) of the other. Their [lengths](length.md) scale by $s$ and their [volumes](volume.md) by $s^n$. Closed [disks](disk-mathematics.md) of positive [radii](radius.md) are [homothetic convex bodies](homothetic-convex-bodies.md); axis-aligned positive-sided [rectangles](rectangle.md) are [homothetic convex bodies](homothetic-convex-bodies.md) exactly when their aspect ratios agree. The [Brunn-Minkowski theorem](brunn-minkowski-theorem.md) characterizes this relation by equality of root-volumes in [Minkowski addition](minkowski-addition.md).
