# Discrete Poisson barrier on a square

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete maximum principle](discrete-maximum-principle.md)

For the five-point discretization $L_h$ of $-\Delta$ on the unit square, the displayed quadratic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) has $L_hB=1$ exactly, nonnegative boundary values and $0\leq B\leq1/8$. If $L_he=\tau$ and $e$ has zero boundary values, the [discrete maximum principle](discrete-maximum-principle.md) applied to $\pm e-\|\tau\|_\infty B$ gives $|e|\leq\|\tau\|_\infty B\leq\|\tau\|_\infty/8$. The same estimate holds for $L_h+I$, since $(L_h+I)B=1+B\geq1$. This turns a uniform [local truncation error](local-truncation-error.md) bound into a pointwise [global error](global-discretization-error.md) bound without inverting the full matrix.
