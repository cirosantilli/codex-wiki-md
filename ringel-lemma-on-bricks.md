# Ringel lemma on bricks

↑ **Parent:** [Brick module](brick-module.md)

An indecomposable finite-dimensional [quiver representation](representation-of-a-quiver.md) that is not a [brick module](brick-module.md) contains a brick with nonzero self-extensions. The [Ringel form](ringel-form.md) then gives $q_Q(\dim B)=1-\dim\operatorname{Ext}^1_Q(B,B)\leq0$, impossible for a positive definite [Tits form of a quiver](tits-form-of-a-quiver.md).
