# Salt-dependent membrane-protein unfolding

↑ **Parent:** [Single-molecule force spectroscopy](single-molecule-force-spectroscopy.md)

Force peaks in membrane-protein extraction can reflect steric barriers, membrane anchoring and [electrostatic interactions](electrostatic-interaction.md). Changing [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md) changes screening and possibly specific ion binding; it need not weaken every barrier in the same direction. The rising branch of an exposed chain and rupture of a remaining structural barrier are different physical processes.
