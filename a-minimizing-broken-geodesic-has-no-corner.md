# A minimizing broken geodesic has no corner

↑ **Parent:** [Minimizing geodesic](minimizing-geodesic.md)

At an interior junction of two unit-speed [geodesic](geodesic.md) segments, let $u$ be the incoming and $v$ the outgoing tangent. Choose points close to the junction in a common [convex normal neighbourhood](convex-normal-neighbourhood.md). Moving the junction with velocity $z$ changes the sum of the two segment lengths by $\langle u-v,z\rangle$, using the [first variation of geodesic energy](first-variation-of-geodesic-energy.md) or the [Gauss lemma](gauss-s-lemma-riemannian-geometry.md). If $u\ne v$, choose $z=v-u$ to reduce length. Hence a length-minimizing broken [geodesic](geodesic.md) has matching tangents and is one smooth [geodesic](geodesic.md).
