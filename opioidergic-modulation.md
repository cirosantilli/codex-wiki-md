# Opioidergic modulation

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscience](neuroscience-split.md)

Regulation through opioid-receptor signaling. Blocking such signaling can test its causal contribution to a task response, but a system-level pharmacological effect does not identify the same [neurons](neuron-split.md) as the representation of two different experiences.
