# Stability diagram of the linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

For the [linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation](linear-complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) with real $U,c_d,\mu$ and positive real diffusion coefficient scaled to one, the maximum [temporal growth rate](growth-rate.md) is $\mu$ and the [absolute growth rate](absolute-growth-rate.md) is $\mu-U^2/[4(1+c_d^2)]$. For $U>0$, $\mu<0$ is stable, $0<\mu<\mu_a$ has [convective hydrodynamic instability](convective-hydrodynamic-instability.md), and $\mu>\mu_a$ has [absolute hydrodynamic instability](absolute-hydrodynamic-instability.md). Both equality boundaries are marginal in exponential rate.
