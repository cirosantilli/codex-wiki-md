# Seasonality

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

Seasonality is systematic repetition at a calendar period. It can appear in a deterministic mean, in a periodic variance, or in dependence across seasons. A [seasonal difference operator](seasonal-difference-operator.md) removes a fixed periodic mean but [seasonal differencing does not remove periodic variance](seasonal-differencing-does-not-remove-periodic-variance.md). A periodic-looking path alone does not prove a [nonstationary process](nonstationary-process.md).
