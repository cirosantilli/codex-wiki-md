# Conservative pre-giant period-ratio minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Pre-giant Roche-lobe-filling period limit](pre-giant-roche-lobe-filling-period-limit.md)

For fixed total solar-unit mass $m$ and conserved orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), [conservative binary mass transfer](conservative-binary-mass-transfer.md) preserves $K=P[m_d(m-m_d)]^3$. Hence $P/m_d^2=K/[m_d^5(m-m_d)^3]$. Its logarithmic derivative is $-5/m_d+3/(m-m_d)$, vanishing at $5m/8$ with positive second derivative. The ratio diverges at both endpoints, so this is its global minimum. Comparing this minimum to a pre-giant bound $P_0$ tests whether any such conservative antecedent can overflow before the giant branch; satisfying it is necessary, not a proof of an entire evolutionary history.
