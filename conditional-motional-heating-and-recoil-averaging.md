# Conditional motional heating and recoil averaging

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic-oscillator recoil matrix element](harmonic-oscillator-recoil-matrix-element.md)

A transition between oscillator [energy](energy-split.md) [eigenstates](eigenstate.md) that adds one quantum changes kinetic and potential expectation energies by $\hbar\omega/2$ each. A sudden unobserved [momentum](momentum-split.md) kick instead initially adds its [energy](energy-split.md) only to kinetic motion and leaves the position distribution unchanged. Coherent superpositions distinguish these descriptions. Averaging over motional outcomes gives a recoil-scale total [energy](energy-split.md); time averaging or [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) gives equal kinetic and potential shares. Broad-line transition rates need not be exactly equal to bare overlap probabilities if finite spectral weights matter.
