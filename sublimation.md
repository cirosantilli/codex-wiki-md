# Sublimation

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)

A direct transition from a solid phase to a gaseous phase. Heated [interplanetary dust](interplanetary-dust.md) or a [comet](comet.md) can lose material through this process, changing its size and [radiation-pressure coefficient](radiation-pressure-coefficient.md) or destroying it before gravitational or drag-driven evolution is complete.
