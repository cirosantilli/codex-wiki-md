# Rado solution inside an m-p-c set

↑ **Parent:** [M-p-c set](m-p-c-set.md)

Suppose the columns $a_i$ of a [matrix](matrix.md) over the [rational numbers](rational-number.md) satisfy the [columns condition](columns-property.md) with blocks $B_1,\ldots,B_s$. Choose rational coefficients $\lambda_{ij}$ such that

$$
\sum_{i\in B_j}a_i+\sum_{i\in B_1\cup\cdots\cup B_{j-1}}\lambda_{ij}a_i=0.
$$

Choose a positive integer $c$ clearing all denominators and $p\geq\max|c\lambda_{ij}|$. For generators of any full positive [M-p-c set](m-p-c-set.md), the coordinates

$$
x_i=cz_h+\sum_{j>h}c\lambda_{ij}z_j\qquad(i\in B_h)
$$

lie in that set and satisfy $Ax=0$. Thus the [monochromatic m-p-c set theorem](monochromatic-m-p-c-set-theorem.md) supplies the sufficiency direction of [Rado's theorem](rado-s-theorem.md).
