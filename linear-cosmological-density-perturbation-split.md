# Linear cosmological density perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

For pressureless subhorizon matter perturbations, a Fourier mode in conformal time obeys

$$
\delta''+\mathcal H\delta'-\frac32\Omega_M\mathcal H^2\delta=0,
$$

where $\mathcal H=a'/a$.

These equations describe the small-amplitude phase of [structure formation](structure-formation.md); nonlinear growth requires a more general treatment.
