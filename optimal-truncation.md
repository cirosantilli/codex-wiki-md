# Optimal truncation

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

A divergent [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) should usually be stopped near its smallest term rather than summed indefinitely. For factorial terms $n!z^{-n}$ the cutoff has $n=|z|+O(1)$. This exposes exponentially small contributions and the [Stokes phenomenon](stokes-phenomenon.md). The smallest term alone does not always bound the remainder, especially when a saddle meets a pole.
