# Wald statistics with a shared control

↑ **Parent:** [Wald test](wald-test.md)

Independent normal arm means with variances $v_0,v_1,v_2$ give treatment-contrast [Wald test](wald-test.md) statistics whose [correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) is $v_0/\sqrt{(v_0+v_1)(v_0+v_2)}$. The common control mean creates positive dependence between comparisons.
