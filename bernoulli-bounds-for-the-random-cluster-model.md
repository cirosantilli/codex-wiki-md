# Bernoulli bounds for the random-cluster model

↑ **Parent:** [Random-cluster model](random-cluster-model.md)

For $q\geq1$, the [random-cluster measure](random-cluster-model.md) lies between the two [Bernoulli](bernoulli-distribution.md) product measures displayed above. The upper [Holley condition](holley-condition.md) ratio is $q^{k(\omega\wedge\eta)-k(\eta)}\geq1$. The lower ratio is $q^{k(\omega\vee\eta)-k(\omega)+|\eta\setminus\omega|}\geq1$, because adding an edge reduces the component count by at most one. The comparisons persist under wired limits and give $p_c(1)\leq p_c(q)\leq qp_c(1)/(1+(q-1)p_c(1))$.
