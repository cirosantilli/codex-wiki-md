# Power method

↑ **Parent:** [Operator theory](linear-operator-theory-split.md)

Iterate $q^{(k+1)}=Aq^{(k)}/\|Aq^{(k)}\|$. With a unique [dominant eigenvalue](dominant-eigenvalue.md) separated by a [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) and a starting [vector](vector.md) that is [nonorthogonal](nonorthogonal-vectors.md) to its [eigenvector](eigenvector.md), the directions converge at the spectral-ratio rate.

The power method is [power iteration](power-iteration.md); its convergence depends on the dominant spectral component of the starting vector.
