# Phonon scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon](phonon.md)

[Phonon scattering](phonon-scattering.md) changes the occupation or propagation of lattice-vibration [normal modes](normal-mode.md) through interactions with boundaries, defects or other [phonons](phonon.md). Anharmonic terms permit phonon-phonon scattering. Different processes need not relax heat flow equally: normal processes conserve total [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) within the first [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md), whereas [Umklapp scattering](umklapp-scattering.md) changes its representative by a nonzero [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector.
