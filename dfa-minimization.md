# DFA minimization

↑ **Parent:** [Deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DFA_minimization)

[DFA minimization](dfa-minimization.md) merges states with identical acceptance behavior for every suffix and removes unreachable states. It produces a [minimal deterministic finite automaton](minimal-deterministic-finite-automaton.md), unique up to renaming states.
