# Anaphase

↑ **Parent:** [Mitosis](mitosis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaphase)

In mitotic [anaphase](anaphase.md), [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) separate into daughter [chromosomes](chromosome.md) moving toward opposite poles.

Release of [cohesin](cohesin.md)-mediated association permits the sister [centromere](centromere.md) regions to separate. Each former [chromatid](chromatid.md) is then counted as an independent daughter [chromosome](chromosome.md). This changes the counting convention without making additional [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) copies. For the $2n=4$ example, the whole undivided [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) transiently contains eight daughter [chromosomes](chromosome.md), four moving toward each pole. Movement toward poles and separation of the poles both contribute to partition in the illustrated [animal](animal.md) setting.[Biology 2e by Mary Ann Clark and others; §10.2, Karyokinesis, Figure 10.6 and Anaphase: release of cohesion, daughter chromosomes and spindle elongation](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/the-cell-cycle.md)
