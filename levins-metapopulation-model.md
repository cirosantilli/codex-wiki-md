# Levins metapopulation model

↑ **Parent:** [Metapopulation](metapopulation.md)

Colonization of empty habitat at rate $c$ and extinction of occupied habitat at rate $m$ give the displayed patch-occupancy equation. The positive [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is $1-m/c$ when $c>m$. Competition can be incorporated through replacement fluxes between patches occupied by different species.
