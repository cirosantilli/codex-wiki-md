# Poisson limit for strictly balanced subgraph counts

Here $v,e,a$ are the numbers of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and [graph automorphisms](graph-automorphism.md) of $H$. Vertex-disjoint ordered $r$-tuples contribute $(n)_{rv}p^{re}/a^r\to(c^e/a)^r$ to the [factorial moment](factorial-moment.md). Adding a copy overlapping the previous union in $f$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and $g$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) changes the power of $n$ by $-f+(v/e)g\le0$, strictly when the intersection is a proper nonempty [subgraph](subgraph.md). The first overlap is strict because $H$ is connected and the previous copies are disjoint. Finitely many overlap types therefore contribute $o(1)$. The [factorial-moment criterion for Poisson convergence](factorial-moment-criterion-for-poisson-convergence.md) gives the result.
