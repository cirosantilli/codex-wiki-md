# Thermal-diffusion scaling of a convection layer

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

Using layer depth $d$ and [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md) $\kappa$ gives time scale $d^2/\kappa$, [velocity](velocity.md) scale $\kappa/d$, and perturbation [pressure](pressure.md) scale $\rho_0\kappa^2/d^2$. With temperature scale $\Delta T$, the [Linearized Boussinesq equations](linearized-boussinesq-equations.md) have viscous coefficient [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) $\mathrm{Pr}=\nu/\kappa$ and buoyancy coefficient $\mathrm{Ra}\,\mathrm{Pr}$, where [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md) $\mathrm{Ra}=g\alpha_T\Delta T d^3/(\nu\kappa)$.
