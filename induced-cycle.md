# Induced cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Cycle in a graph](cycle-in-a-graph.md)

An [induced cycle](induced-cycle.md) is a [cycle in a graph](cycle-in-a-graph.md) whose [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) induce exactly its cycle [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md), with no [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) between nonconsecutive [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). A triangle is an [induced cycle](induced-cycle.md) of length three. A [chordal graph](chordal-graph.md) forbids [induced cycles](induced-cycle.md) of length at least four.
