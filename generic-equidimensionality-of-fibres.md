# Generic equidimensionality of fibres

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber dimension theorem](fiber-dimension-theorem.md)

For a dominant [morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) between irreducible varieties, there is a nonempty [Zariski-open subset](zariski-open-set.md) of the target over which every fibre is nonempty and every irreducible component has the displayed dimension. Apply [Noether normalization](noether-normalization.md) to affine source charts over the target [function field](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) and clear denominators on the target to get a generic upper bound. Combine it with the closed-fibre lower bound in the [fiber dimension theorem](fiber-dimension-theorem.md).
