# Vandermonde matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vandermonde_matrix)

A Vandermonde matrix has entries $V_{ij}=\lambda_i^{j-1}$. Distinct nodes give determinant $\prod_{i<j}(\lambda_j-\lambda_i)\ne0$, explaining independence of polynomial evaluations and distinct-eigenvalue [Krylov subspaces](krylov-subspace.md).
