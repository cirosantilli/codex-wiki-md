# Time-origin alignment of clinical endpoints

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical trial](clinical-trial.md)

A [clinical trial](clinical-trial.md) comparison must define a common time origin and observation window for both assigned treatments. A probability measured from allocation cannot be replaced by one measured from a later treatment-dependent release or discharge. The latter may describe a different time interval and a selected population. A numerical [sample size](sample-size.md) cannot be obtained by silently treating those probabilities as interchangeable.
