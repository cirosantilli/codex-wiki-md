# Vertex-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md)

For the [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md), the [initial segment](initial-segment.md) of the [simplicial order on the discrete cube](simplicial-order-on-the-discrete-cube.md) minimizes the size of the closed [vertex neighbourhood](vertex-neighbourhood.md) among families of a given size. Equivalently, it minimizes the [external vertex boundary](external-vertex-boundary.md). This is a vertex assertion, distinct from the [edge-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube](edge-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md).
