# Photoionization heating

↑ **Parent:** [Photoionization](photoionization.md)

Absorption of an ionizing photon leaves energy in a photoelectron above the binding energy. Thermalization supplies heat to the gas. [Photoionization heating](photoionization-heating.md) competes with the [astrophysical cooling function](astrophysical-cooling-function.md), can establish thermal equilibrium, and can raise the pressure enough to inhibit baryon accretion into shallow [dark-matter haloes](dark-matter-halo.md).
