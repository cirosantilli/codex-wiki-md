<h1 id="endpoint-poincare-inequality">Endpoint Poincaré inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md)

For the [one-dimensional Sobolev representative](one-dimensional-sobolev-representative.md) with $v(c)=0$, the [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) gives $v(x)=-\int_x^cv'(t)\,dt$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) then gives $|v(x)|^2\leq(c-x)\|v'\|_2^2$. Integrating proves the displayed, generally nonoptimal [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md). In particular $\|v\|_{H^1}^2\leq[1+(c-b)^2/2]\|v'\|_2^2$. Only one endpoint must be fixed, which is useful for mixed [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) and for controlling a negative potential.
