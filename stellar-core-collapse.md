# Stellar core collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

Rapid contraction of a stellar core when its pressure support becomes inadequate. An evolved iron-group core can lose support through electron capture and photodisintegration and collapse towards a [neutron star](neutron-star.md) or [black hole](black-hole-split.md). A successful outgoing explosion produces a [core-collapse supernova](core-collapse-supernova.md); core collapse need not always give a bright successful supernova.
