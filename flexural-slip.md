# Flexural slip

↑ **Parent:** [Folding](folding.md)

Relative movement parallel to bedding during [folding](folding.md), allowing layers at different radii of curvature to accommodate different arc lengths. For two layer surfaces separated by thickness $h$ through bend angle $\theta$, their arc-length difference is approximately $h\theta$. Bedding-parallel slip can accommodate that mismatch without requiring each layer to stretch by the same amount. Shallow [flexural slip](flexural-slip.md) can produce surface breaks above a [blind reverse fault](blind-reverse-fault.md).
