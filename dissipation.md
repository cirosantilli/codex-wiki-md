# Dissipation

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)

Conversion of organized [work](work.md) into internal modes with macroscopic [entropy](entropy-split.md) production. Total [energy](energy-split.md) remains conserved. For a passive linear material, the loss coefficient must give nonnegative supplied [work](work.md) over a completed cycle.
