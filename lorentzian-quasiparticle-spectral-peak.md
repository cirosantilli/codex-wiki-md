# Lorentzian quasiparticle spectral peak

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral function](spectral-function.md)

A normalized retarded amplitude $G^R(\mathbf k,t)=-i\Theta(t)e^{-i\Omega_{\mathbf k}t-\Gamma_{\mathbf k}t}$, with constant $\Gamma_{\mathbf k}>0$, has [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) $G^R(\mathbf k,\omega)=(\omega-\Omega_{\mathbf k}+i\Gamma_{\mathbf k})^{-1}$. Consequently $A=-\operatorname{Im}G^R/\pi$ is the displayed positive [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md), of unit frequency integral. Its maximum is at $\Omega_{\mathbf k}$ and its half width at half maximum is $\Gamma_{\mathbf k}$. The energy half-width is $\hbar\Gamma_{\mathbf k}$. Amplitude decays on time $1/\Gamma_{\mathbf k}$ whereas probability decays on time $1/(2\Gamma_{\mathbf k})$. A general interacting [electron](electron.md) has [quasiparticle residue](quasiparticle-residue.md) $Z$ and incoherent spectral weight in addition to this normalized local pole model; frequency-dependent width or [self-energy](self-energy.md) can invalidate a global Lorentzian.
