# Local system

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_system)

A local system is a locally constant [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) of modules, for example the integral cohomology of fibres of a [smooth proper family of compact complex manifolds](smooth-proper-family-of-compact-complex-manifolds.md). Parallel continuation around loops gives its [monodromy](monodromy.md) representation.
