# Isolation mass

↑ **Parent:** [Feeding zone of a planetary embryo](feeding-zone-of-a-planetary-embryo.md)

The [mass](mass.md) reached after consuming an idealized local annulus of solids. With full annular width $\Delta a=B a[M_{\rm iso}/(3M_\star)]^{1/3}$ and solid [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $\Sigma_s$, the balance $M_{\rm iso}=2\pi a\Delta a\Sigma_s$ gives the displayed value. Radial replenishment, migration and embryo mergers can overcome local isolation; defining the width with a mutual rather than single-body [Hill radius](hill-radius.md) changes the numerical coefficient.
