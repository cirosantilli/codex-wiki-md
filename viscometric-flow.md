# Viscometric flow

↑ **Parent:** [Simple fluid](simple-fluid.md)

A viscometric flow has the local relative deformation history of steady [simple shear flow](simple-shear-flow.md), up to rigid changes of frame. For an isotropic [simple fluid](simple-fluid.md), symmetry leaves one shear stress and two independent [normal-stress differences](normal-stress-difference.md). The corresponding [viscometric functions](viscometric-functions.md) depend on the magnitude of the [shear rate](shear-rate.md), while [pressure](pressure.md) supplies the isotropic part.
