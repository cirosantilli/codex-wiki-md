# Symmetric subset of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate group](approximate-group.md)

A subset $A$ of a multiplicatively written [group](group-split.md) is symmetric when it contains the [identity element](identity-element.md) and $A^{-1}=A$. Consequently $\langle A\rangle=\bigcup_{m\geq1}A^m$.
