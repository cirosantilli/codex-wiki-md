# First-order substitution

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

Replacing every free occurrence of variable $z$ in a [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) by a [first-order term](first-order-term.md) $\tau$. Bound occurrences of $z$ are left alone. The operation requires either that $\tau$ be [free for substitution](free-for-substitution.md) at those occurrences or that conflicting [bound variables](bound-variable.md) first be renamed, as in [capture-avoiding first-order substitution](capture-avoiding-first-order-substitution.md).
