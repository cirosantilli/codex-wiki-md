# Abrupt line-current wavefront singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Switched infinite-wire retarded potential](switched-infinite-wire-retarded-potential.md)

The ideal fields after instantaneous switching behave as $E_z,H_\phi\propto(t-r/c)^{-1/2}$ near arrival, and the radial [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) as $(t-r/c)^{-1}$. A finite switching rise time smooths the ideal front; the singular solution does not represent a finite-energy real pulse.
