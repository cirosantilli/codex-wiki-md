# Galerkin method

↑ **Parent:** [Weak solution](weak-solution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galerkin_method)

The Galerkin method seeks an approximate solution in a finite-dimensional subspace and requires the equation's residual to be orthogonal to that subspace. Uniform energy estimates and compactness can then produce a [weak solution](weak-solution.md) as the subspaces become dense.
