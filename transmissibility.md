# Transmissibility

↑ **Parent:** [Forced oscillator](forced-oscillator.md)

A ratio of response [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) to imposed excitation [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) in a vibration problem. One must specify the input and output: displacement transmissibility and force transmissibility are different measurements. For a [base-excited oscillator](base-excited-oscillator.md) whose [spring](spring.md) and [viscous damper](dashpot.md) both act relative to its support, complex displacement amplitudes obey $X/Y=(k+i\gamma\omega)/(k-m\omega^2+i\gamma\omega)$. Its magnitude is the displacement transmissibility. A damper fixed to the laboratory rather than the support has a different numerator, so the attachment geometry matters.
