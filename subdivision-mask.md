# Subdivision mask

↑ **Parent:** [Subdivision surface](subdivision-surface.md)

A stationary binary [subdivision mask](subdivision-mask.md) $a$ specifies a refinement operator $q_j=\sum_i a_{j-2i}p_i$. Indices may be vectors for a surface. Coefficients restricted to a parity class give the stencil for one type of refined vertex.
