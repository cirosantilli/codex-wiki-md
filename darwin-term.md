# Darwin term

↑ **Parent:** [Fine structure](fine-structure.md)

The spin-one-half low-energy expansion of the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) includes this local correction in a static potential energy V. For $V=-Ze^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0r)$, $\nabla^2V=Ze^2\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf r)/\epsilon_0$, so the correction is positive and probes the wavefunction at the origin. In point-Coulomb [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) states it contributes only to S waves at leading nonrelativistic order. It is part of [fine structure](fine-structure.md), distinct from the radiative [Lamb shift](lamb-shift.md). [§3.1.4, printedpp.76–78](topics-in-quantum-mechanics-by-david-tong.md)
