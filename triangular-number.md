# Triangular number

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_number)

A triangular number counts objects arranged in rows of lengths $1,2,\ldots,n$. Pairing that sum with its reverse gives $T_n=n(n+1)/2$. The identity $T_{n+1}^2-T_n^2=(n+1)^3$ then telescopes to $\sum_{r=1}^n r^3=T_n^2$.
