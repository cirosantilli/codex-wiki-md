<h1 id="point-particle-faddeev-popov-ghost-action">Point-particle Faddeev–Popov ghost action</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [BRST quantization](brst-quantization.md)

For a relativistic particle, the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) gauge parameter changes the [worldline einbein](worldline-einbein.md) by its time derivative. Fixing the einbein to a constant therefore gives a [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md) of $\partial_t$, represented by anticommuting [FP ghosts](faddeev-popov-ghost.md). Gauge zero modes and the [proper-time modulus](proper-time-modulus.md) are treated separately. The displayed phase convention gives the odd canonical bracket $\{b,c\}=-i$.
