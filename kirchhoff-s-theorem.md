<h1 id="kirchhoff-s-theorem">Kirchhoff's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Spanning tree](spanning-tree.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirchhoff's_theorem)

The matrix-tree theorem expresses the weighted number of [spanning trees](spanning-tree.md) of a finite [graph](graph-split.md) as a cofactor of its [Graph Laplacian](laplacian-matrix.md). For a directed graph with out-Laplacian $L$, deleting the row and column indexed by a root $r$ gives

$$
\det L^{(r)}=\sum_T\prod_{e\in T}w_e,
$$

where the sum runs over [directed spanning trees](directed-spanning-tree.md) rooted towards $r$.
