# Optical pinhole

↑ **Parent:** [Aperture](aperture.md)

An [optical pinhole](optical-pinhole.md) is a small [aperture](aperture.md) used to select a narrow part of a beam or approximate an [optical point source](optical-point-source.md). Its finite [diameter](diameter.md) and [diffraction](diffraction.md) limit that approximation; an illuminated [optical pinhole](optical-pinhole.md) at a [collimator](collimator.md)'s [optical focus](focus-optics.md) supplies a narrow bundle of nearly parallel [light rays](light-ray.md).
