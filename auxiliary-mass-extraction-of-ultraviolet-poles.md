# Auxiliary-mass extraction of ultraviolet poles

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md)

For an overall logarithmically divergent one-loop [Feynman integral](feynman-integral.md), its leading large-[momentum](momentum-split.md) term fixes its [ultraviolet pole](ultraviolet-pole.md). Giving its denominators a positive auxiliary [mass](mass.md) $M$ regulates the artificial small-momentum singularity produced by setting external scales to zero. The difference from the original leading expansion must be [ultraviolet](ultraviolet.md) convergent. This justifies extracting its [ultraviolet pole](ultraviolet-pole.md) without mistaking a [scaleless integral](scaleless-integral.md) for an ultraviolet-finite one; multiloop subdivergences require additional treatment.
