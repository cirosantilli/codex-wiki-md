# Cold-plasma right-handed wave

↑ **Parent:** [Cold magnetized plasma](cold-magnetized-plasma.md)

For propagation along $+\mathbf B_0$, use $e^{ikz-i\omega t}$ and $\epsilon_{xy}=ig$. This transverse [electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) has $E_y=iE_x$ and [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n^2=S-g$. Its real [electric field](electric-field-split.md) rotates in the sense of [electron](electron.md) gyration. With immobile [ions](ion.md) and positive [electron](electron.md) [cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md) magnitude $\Omega_e$, $R=1-\omega_{pe}^2/[\omega(\omega-\Omega_e)]$.
