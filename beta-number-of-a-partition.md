# Beta number of a partition

↑ **Parent:** [Beta set of a partition](beta-set-of-a-partition.md)

For a partition padded to $l$ rows, its [beta numbers](beta-number-of-a-partition.md) are the distinct nonnegative integers $\lambda_i+l-i$. They are its occupied [abacus of a partition](abacus-of-a-partition.md) positions. With exactly the positive row count, they equal the first-column [hook lengths](hook-length.md); extra zero rows change the [representation](group-representation.md) of the [beta set](beta-set-of-a-partition.md) while preserving the partition. Individual [beta numbers](beta-number-of-a-partition.md) describe bead positions, whereas the [beta set](beta-set-of-a-partition.md) is the collection of all these numbers.
