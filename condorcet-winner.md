# Condorcet winner

↑ **Parent:** [Social choice theory](social-choice-theory.md)

A [Condorcet winner](condorcet-winner.md) defeats every other alternative by a strict majority in pairwise voting. It is unique when it exists. For an odd electorate with [single-peaked preferences](single-peaked-preferences.md), the median peak is a [Condorcet winner](condorcet-winner.md): alternatives on either side lose to the majority whose peaks lie at or beyond the median in the other direction. With an even electorate ties may prevent a strict winner.
