# Anorthosite

↑ **Parent:** [Igneous rock](igneous-rock.md)

An [igneous rock](igneous-rock.md) composed predominantly of [plagioclase](plagioclase.md). Lunar anorthositic rocks are central to the flotation interpretation of the [lunar magma ocean](lunar-magma-ocean.md). Their mineral and chemical composition does not alone date the formation of the complete crustal layer; sample context and [radiometric dating](radiometric-dating.md) are needed.
