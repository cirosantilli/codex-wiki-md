# Allele frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Allele](allele.md)

The allele frequency is the proportion of gene copies at a specified [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) carrying an [allele](allele.md). Two unrelated copies can have the same observed allele without being [identical by descent](identity-by-descent.md); under independent sampling their matching probability is the sum of squared allele frequencies. Pedigree [autozygosity](autozygosity.md) instead traces copies to the same ancestral copy.
