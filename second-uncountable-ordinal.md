# Second uncountable ordinal

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal](ordinal.md)

The second uncountable ordinal $\omega_2$ is the least ordinal whose [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) is strictly larger than that of the [first uncountable ordinal](first-uncountable-ordinal.md) $\omega_1$. Equivalently, it is the initial ordinal of the successor cardinal $\aleph_2$.
