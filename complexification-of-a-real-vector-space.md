# Complexification of a real vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)

The complexification of a real [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $V$ allows complex scalar coefficients while preserving its real linear relations. It can be constructed as the [tensor product](tensor-product.md) $V\otimes_\mathbb R\mathbb C$, or as formal sums $u+iv$ with $u,v\in V$. A real basis of $V$ becomes a complex basis of $V_\mathbb C$, so its complex dimension equals the original real dimension. Every real [linear map](linear-map.md) extends uniquely by complex linearity. In particular a [real representation](real-representation.md) extends by viewing its real matrices as complex matrices. Complexifying the [realification of a complex representation](realification-of-a-complex-representation.md) produces the original complex representation together with its complex conjugate; the coordinate change $(u,v)\mapsto(u+iv,u-iv)$ exhibits the two blocks.
