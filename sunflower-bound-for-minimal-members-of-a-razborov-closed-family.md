# Sunflower bound for minimal members of a Razborov-closed family

↑ **Parent:** [Razborov closure](razborov-closure.md)

An inclusion-minimal $s$-set in an upward-closed family cannot belong to an $r$-member [delta-system](delta-system.md) of such minimal sets: its proper core would be adjoined by [Razborov closure](razborov-closure.md), violating minimality. The [Erdős–Rado sunflower lemma](erdos-rado-sunflower-lemma.md) therefore bounds the number $a_s$ of minimal $s$-sets. This weaker factorial bound is sufficient for the [superpolynomial monotone clique lower bound](superpolynomial-monotone-clique-lower-bound.md).
