# Magnetic buoyancy instability

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

A [magnetic buoyancy instability](magnetic-buoyancy-instability.md) is a gravitational instability of a magnetized stratified fluid in which displacements of the gas and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) can release [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md). [Magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) supports part of the weight. Both interchange displacements and undular displacements can enter this broad category; [Parker instability](parker-instability.md) is the undular mechanism involving drainage along bent [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md).
