# Bubble clearing in a cylindrical vessel

↑ **Parent:** [Void fraction](void-fraction.md)

For initially uniform bubbles with upward speed $V_s(1-\phi)$, no new gas production and free escape at the top, gas conservation gives flux $V_s\phi(1-\phi)$. A clearing [shock](shock-wave.md) rises from the impermeable bottom with speed $V_s(1-\phi_0)$ by the [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md). The displayed mean [void fraction](void-fraction.md) averages the remaining uniform region over the whole liquid depth $H$. This assumes the dilute regime and no retained foam layer.
