# Derivative of a body-fixed basis vector

↑ **Parent:** [Principal body frame](principal-body-frame.md)

If $e_i$ is fixed in a rigid body with [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega$, then its derivative in the fixed space frame is

$$
\dot e_i=\omega\times e_i.
$$
