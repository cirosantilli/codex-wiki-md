# Dual isomorphisms induced by a nondegenerate pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md)

A [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) $b:U\times V\to K$ with zero left and right radicals induces injective [linear maps](linear-map.md) $U\to V^*$ and $V\to U^*$. Finite dimensions force $\dim U=\dim V$, so both maps are isomorphisms. For any other bilinear form $c$, composing its maps to the [dual spaces](dual-space.md) with these inverse isomorphisms gives unique endomorphisms $S,T$ with $c(x,y)=b(Sx,y)=b(x,Ty)$. In bases with invertible pairing matrix $B$ and coefficient matrix $C$, $S=B^{-T}C^T$ and $T=B^{-1}C$.
