# Spacelike submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentzian manifold](lorentzian-manifold.md)

In [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,\ldots,+)$, a [spacelike submanifold](spacelike-submanifold.md) of a [Lorentzian manifold](lorentzian-manifold.md) has an induced [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) defining a [positive-definite quadratic form](positive-definite-quadratic-form.md). With the overall reversed [metric signature](metric-signature.md), its induced [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) instead has only negative [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). The definition applies to any dimension: enclosing charge-integration surfaces in four dimensions are two-dimensional examples, whereas a spacelike [Cauchy hypersurface](cauchy-surface.md) in four dimensions is three-dimensional.
