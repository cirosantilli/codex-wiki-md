# Viscous mountain spreading over an underthrust plate

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

For a Newtonian layer with density $\rho$, [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) $\mu$, thickness $h$ and a plate moving at $U$, a stress-free roof and no-slip base give $q=Uh-\rho gh^3h_x/(3\mu)$. Zero net mountain-material [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) produces $h^3=9\mu Ux/(\rho g)$ when the contact height vanishes. Very near its idealized contact line the slope violates the [lubrication approximation](lubrication-theory.md).
