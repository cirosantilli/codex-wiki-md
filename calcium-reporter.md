# Calcium reporter

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium signaling](calcium-signaling.md)

A probe whose measurable output depends on [calcium ion](calcium-ion.md) binding. Its affinity, binding kinetics, localization and buffering influence what aspect of a [calcium signature](calcium-signature.md) can be observed.
