# Displacive transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)

A cooperative change of atomic positions and lattice shape without the long-range compositional [diffusion](diffusion.md) required by a diffusional transformation. Tetragonal-to-monoclinic transformation in [zirconia](zirconia.md) is a nonmetallic example.
