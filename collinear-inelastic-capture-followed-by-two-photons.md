# Collinear inelastic capture followed by two photons

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md)

In an isolated collinear completely inelastic capture, add incoming total energies and signed momenta. Their invariant fixes the composite [rest mass](invariant-mass.md), including internal excitation. If it later converts all [energy](energy-split.md) to two opposite axial [photons](photon.md), the two conservation equations immediately give the displayed [photon energies](photon-energy.md). The laboratory [frequency](frequency.md) ratio is $(E+Pc)/(E-Pc)$, not generally one; equality holds in the composite [rest frame](rest-frame.md).
