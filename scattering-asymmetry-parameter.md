# Scattering asymmetry parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering phase function](scattering-phase-function.md)

The [scattering asymmetry parameter](scattering-asymmetry-parameter.md) is the mean scattering-angle cosine, $g=(4\pi)^{-1}\int P\cos\Theta\,d\Omega$ in the $4\pi$ normalization. Forward/backward symmetry gives $g=0$; positive $g$ indicates a net forward preference. In a two-stream transport approximation, the reduced scattering [optical depth](optical-depth.md) is $(1-g)\tau$.
