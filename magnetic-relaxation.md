# Magnetic relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

Magnetic relaxation converts [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) into material motion through the [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md), and dissipates the resulting [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) through [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md). In a [perfect conductor](perfect-conductor.md), [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md) preserves field-line connectivity throughout every smooth stage. A regular limiting state has zero [velocity](velocity.md) and is a [magnetostatic equilibrium](magnetostatic-equilibrium.md), but its [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) can contain [current sheets](current-sheet.md). Conservation of one global [magnetic helicity](magnetic-helicity.md) is weaker than preservation of the complete frozen field-line structure.
