# Prime-set decomposition of elementary groups

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary group](elementary-group.md)

Write an [elementary group](elementary-group.md) as $P\times C$ with $P$ a p-group and $C$ cyclic of order prime to $p$. Split $C$ into its cyclic pi-part and pi-complementary part. Attach $P$ to whichever part contains $p$, producing a [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) of a [pi-group](pi-group.md) and a complementary-prime group.
