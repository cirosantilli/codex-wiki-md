# Coulomb head-on closest approach with target recoil

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb orbit](coulomb-orbit.md)

A projectile of [mass](mass.md) $m_1$, [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $q_1$, and nonrelativistic laboratory [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $K_{\rm lab}$ approaches an initially stationary free target of [mass](mass.md) $m_2$ and like-sign [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $q_2$. The relative-motion [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $K_*=m_2K_{\rm lab}/(m_1+m_2)$. At a head-on turning point it is converted to the repulsive [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) $k_eq_1q_2/r_{\min}$, proving the displayed separation. The remaining laboratory [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is the translation of the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md); it cannot be converted into the pair's separation energy. An externally fixed target is the infinite-target-mass limit.
