# Direct comparison test

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence and series](sequence-and-series-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_comparison_test)

If $0\le a_n\le b_n$ eventually and $\sum b_n$ converges, then $\sum a_n$ converges. The nondecreasing partial sums of the nonnegative $a_n$ are bounded above by a finite initial contribution plus the sum of $b_n$; [bounded monotone sequence theorem](bounded-monotone-sequence-theorem.md) proves the claim. Consequently $|c_n|\le b_n$ with summable $b_n$ proves [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md) of $\sum c_n$. Finite changes to initial terms do not affect convergence.
