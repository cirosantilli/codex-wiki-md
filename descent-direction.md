# Descent direction

↑ **Parent:** [Line search](line-search.md)

A [vector](vector.md) $s$ is a [descent direction](descent-direction.md) for a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) $f$ at $x$ if its [directional derivative](directional-derivative.md) $\nabla f(x)^Ts$ is negative. The first-order expansion $f(x+\alpha s)=f(x)+\alpha\nabla f(x)^Ts+o(\alpha)$ then gives $f(x+\alpha s)<f(x)$ for sufficiently small positive $\alpha$. The negative [gradient](gradient.md) is a [descent direction](descent-direction.md) whenever the [gradient](gradient.md) is nonzero.
