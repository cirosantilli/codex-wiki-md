# Coincidence circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Trigger (particle physics)](trigger-particle-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coincidence_circuit)

A [coincidence circuit](coincidence-circuit.md) responds when detector signals occur within a specified time window. Two counters placed on opposite sides of a chamber can preferentially select a [charged particle](charged-particle.md) that crosses both. Simultaneity within the electronics' window alone does not establish that two signals have the same physical cause: unrelated detections produce [accidental coincidences](accidental-coincidence.md).
