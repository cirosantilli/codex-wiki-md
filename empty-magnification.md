# Empty magnification

↑ **Parent:** [Optical microscope](optical-microscope.md)

[Empty magnification](empty-magnification.md) enlarges an image without revealing additional specimen detail. Adequate [angular magnification](angular-magnification.md) can make existing detail visible to the eye, but it cannot undo the objective's [diffraction](diffraction.md) blur. For equally bright, mutually incoherent [optical point sources](optical-point-source.md) and an ideal circular [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md), a commonly used lateral [Rayleigh criterion](rayleigh-criterion.md) in object space is

$$
d_{\rm R}\simeq\frac{0.61\lambda}{\mathrm{NA}},\qquad \mathrm{NA}=n\sin\alpha,
$$

where $\lambda$ is the vacuum [wavelength](wavelength.md), $n$ is the specimen-medium [refractive index](refractive-index.md), and $\alpha$ is the admitted half-[angle](angle.md). For this model, $\lambda=550\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $\mathrm{NA}=0.25$ give $d_{\rm R}\simeq1.342\,\mathrm{\mu m}$; changing only [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md) to $0.50$ gives $0.671\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. Changing only the eyepiece leaves this object-space scale unchanged. Illumination, [optical coherence](optical-coherence.md), [optical aberrations](optical-aberration.md) and specimen visibility matter in real instruments; this criterion is not a universal threshold.[Formula 2, discussion after Formulas 1–3 and final resolving-power paragraph](resolution-by-michael-w-davidson.md)

The geometric reason is especially simple. If a fixed recorded image is enlarged by a factor $q>0$, a feature separation $s$ and a blur width $b$ become $qs$ and $qb$. Their ratio remains

$$
\frac{qs}{qb}=\frac{s}{b}.
$$

This is the lower-panel construction in the diagram. A change to the [microscope objective](microscope-objective.md) can change [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md) and hence the specimen detail captured; a display zoom changes how large that captured detail looks. [Microscope scale calibration](microscope-scale-calibration.md), [diffraction-limited resolution](diffraction-limited-resolution.md) and [angular magnification](angular-magnification.md) are three complementary properties to report.
