# S-duality

↑ **Parent:** [String duality](string-duality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S-duality)

An [S-duality](s-duality.md) relates descriptions at different coupling strengths. In [type IIB superstring theory](type-iib-superstring-theory.md), $SL(2,\mathbb Z)$ acts on the [axion-dilaton](axion-dilaton.md) and string charge doublets.
