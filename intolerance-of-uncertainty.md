# Intolerance of uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Threat appraisal](threat-appraisal.md)

A difficulty accepting unresolved possibilities, often accompanied by demands for certainty. It can contribute to checking or [reassurance seeking](reassurance-seeking.md), but the function of those responses depends on context and objectively relevant risks.
