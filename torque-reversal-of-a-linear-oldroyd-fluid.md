# Torque reversal of a linear Oldroyd fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Oldroyd-B model](oldroyd-b-model.md)

In an inertialess [cone-and-plate rheometer](cone-and-plate-rheometer.md), reverse a steady torque from T to -T at time zero. The polymer shear stress remains continuous while the positive solvent viscosity allows the rate to jump. The linear stress equation gives $\lambda_2=\mu_0\tau/(\mu_0+G_0\tau)$ and the displayed velocity for t\>0, where $\Omega_+=3\alpha T/[2\pi a^3(\mu_0+G_0\tau)]$. The transient backward speed exceeds the final backward speed because stored polymer stress initially retains its former sign.
