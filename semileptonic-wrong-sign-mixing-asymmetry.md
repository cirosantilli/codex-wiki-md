# Semileptonic wrong-sign mixing asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [CP violation in neutral-meson mixing](cp-violation-in-neutral-meson-mixing.md)

If [semileptonic decay](semileptonic-decay.md) tags the decaying [flavour](flavor-eigenstate.md), wrong-sign decays require [neutral meson mixing](neutral-meson-mixing.md). With no direct [CP violation](cp-violation.md) and no wrong-sign direct [decay amplitudes](decay-amplitude.md), their rate asymmetry is $(|p/q|^2-|q/p|^2)/(|p/q|^2+|q/p|^2)=(1-|q/p|^4)/(1+|q/p|^4)$. The common time dependence cancels; contaminating direct amplitudes invalidate that isolation.
