# DNA polymerase proofreading

↑ **Parent:** [DNA replication](dna-replication.md)

Removal of a misincorporated terminal [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) by a suitable proofreading [exonuclease](exonuclease.md), commonly acting in the 3′→5′ direction. It is distinct from later [DNA mismatch repair](dna-mismatch-repair.md).
