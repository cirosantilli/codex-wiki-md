# Complex analytic representation of biharmonic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Biharmonic equation](biharmonic-equation.md)

If $H,G$ are [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function-split.md), both real and imaginary parts of $\bar zH(z)+G(z)$ solve the [biharmonic equation](biharmonic-equation.md). In [Wirtinger derivatives](wirtinger-derivatives.md), $\Delta=4\partial_z\partial_{\bar z}$, so $\Delta(\bar zH+G)=4H'$ is [harmonic](harmonic-function.md) and its next [Laplacian](laplacian.md) is zero. This construction gives local representations of planar solutions of the [biharmonic equation](biharmonic-equation.md); it is useful for the [biharmonic stream function for planar Stokes flow](biharmonic-stream-function-for-planar-stokes-flow.md).
