# Goldschmidt packing principle

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic bonding](ionic-bonding-split.md)

A geometric heuristic favouring the largest [coordination number](coordination-number.md) compatible with neighbouring opposite-charge [ions](ion.md) touching the central [ion](ion.md) without overlap. A limiting [radius ratio rule](radius-ratio-rule.md) comes from additionally making the surrounding [ions](ion.md) just touch. It is a [hard-sphere model](hard-sphere-model.md), not an exact stability criterion.
