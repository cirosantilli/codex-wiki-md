# High-spin complex

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

A [high-spin complex](high-spin-complex.md) retains more [unpaired electrons](unpaired-electron.md) when the energy required for pairing exceeds the relevant orbital splitting. An octahedral high-[spin](spin.md) $d^6$ center has $t_{2g}^4e_g^2$ and $S=2$.
