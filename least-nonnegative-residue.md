# Least nonnegative residue

↑ **Parent:** [Modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic.md)

The least nonnegative residue of an [integer](integer.md) $a$ modulo a positive [integer modulus](modulus-in-modular-arithmetic.md) $m$ is the unique representative $r$ of its [congruence class](congruence-class.md) in $\{0,\ldots,m-1\}$. [Euclidean division](euclidean-division.md) gives $a=qm+r$. For example, $-3=(-1)\cdot5+2$, so $-3\bmod5=2$; the [integer congruence](integer-congruence.md) $-3\equiv2\pmod5$ does not assert equality of those two [integers](integer.md).
