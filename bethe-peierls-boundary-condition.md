# Bethe-Peierls boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering length from a partial-wave S-matrix](scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md)

This zero-range [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) replaces a short-range interaction by its [S-wave scattering](s-wave-quantum-scattering.md) length. If $\psi=A/r+B+O(r)$, it requires $B=-A/a$. An outgoing spherical wave also has a constant $ik$ term; retaining that term gives the unitary scattering-length amplitude $f_0=-a/(1+ika)$. The model neglects effective-range corrections and is applied outside the microscopic range, not literally inside a finite potential.
