# Photodetachment

↑ **Parent:** [Electron affinity](electron-affinity.md)

[Photodetachment](photodetachment.md) removes an [electron](electron.md) from an [anion](anion.md) using a [photon](photon.md). For [chlorine](chlorine.md), the reaction is $\mathrm{Cl}^-(g)+\gamma\longrightarrow\mathrm{Cl}(g)+e^-$. Measuring the minimum [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) for the specified final [quantum state](quantum-state.md) gives a [photodetachment threshold](photodetachment-threshold.md) and hence an [electron affinity](electron-affinity.md).
