# Active constraint set

↑ **Parent:** [Feasible region](feasible-region.md)

For inequalities $c_j(x)\geq0$, the active constraints at a [feasible point](feasible-point.md) are those holding with equality. Inactive constraints remain strictly satisfied in a sufficiently small neighborhood and do not restrict first-order feasible velocities there. The active gradients determine the [linearized tangent cone](linearized-tangent-cone.md) and appear in the [linear independence constraint qualification](linear-independence-constraint-qualification.md). At a [KKT point](kkt-point.md), inactive constraints have zero [Lagrange multipliers](lagrange-multiplier.md) by [complementary slackness](complementary-slackness.md).
