# Credibility factor

↑ **Parent:** [Bühlmann model](buhlmann-model.md)

The credibility factor in the [Bühlmann model](buhlmann-model.md) is the weight $Z=na/(na+v)$ placed on the [sample mean](sample-mean.md) after $n$ observations. The remaining weight is placed on the population [expected value](expected-value.md). More experience or larger [variance of hypothetical means](variance-of-hypothetical-means.md) increases $Z$; larger [expected process variance](expected-process-variance.md) decreases it.
