# Relativistic cyclotron orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

In a constant [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) $B\widehat z$ with no [electric field](electric-field.md), a charged relativistic particle moves in a circle in the perpendicular plane. Its angular frequency with respect to [proper time](proper-time.md) is

$$
\omega_\tau=\frac{qB}{m},
$$

whereas its angular frequency in an inertial coordinate time is $\omega_t=qB/(\gamma m)$. The orbit radius at speed $v$ is

$$
R=\frac{\gamma mv}{|q|B}.
$$
