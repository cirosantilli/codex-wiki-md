# Moist-layer mass constraint in a light atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric column mass](atmospheric-column-mass.md)

For a dry background inventory of column weight $P_d$, a mixed layer of fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md) thickness $\Delta p$, and saturated surface vapor [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md) $E$, put $x=E/P$ and let $\epsilon$ be the vapor-to-background [molar mass](molar-mass.md) ratio. The vapor [mass fraction](mass-fraction.md) is $\epsilon x/[1+(\epsilon-1)x]$, so [hydrostatic balance](hydrostatic-balance.md) and [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) give the displayed [quadratic equation](quadratic-equation.md). Physical solutions require $x\leq1$ and a nonnegative upper-layer [pressure](pressure-split.md). At $x=1$, $P=E=P_d+\Delta p$. A real positive algebraic [root](root-of-a-root-system.md) beyond that value need not be physically admissible.
