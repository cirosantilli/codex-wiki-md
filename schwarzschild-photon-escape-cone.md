# Schwarzschild photon escape cone

↑ **Parent:** [Photon sphere](photon-sphere.md)

At $2\mu<r\le3\mu$, measure the emission [angle](angle.md) $\alpha$ from the outward radial direction in a static observer's [orthonormal tetrad](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md). Local [photon](photon.md) energy is $E_{\rm loc}=E_\infty/\sqrt{1-2\mu/r}$, and its tangential [momentum](momentum-split.md) is $E_{\rm loc}\sin\alpha/c$. Thus its [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) is $b=r\sin\alpha/\sqrt{1-2\mu/r}$. Combining this with the [null effective potential in Schwarzschild spacetime](null-effective-potential-in-schwarzschild-spacetime.md) gives escape precisely for outward directions $0\le\alpha<\alpha_{\rm crit}$; the limiting ray approaches the [photon sphere](photon-sphere.md) and does not escape.

As $r\downarrow2\mu$ the cone shrinks to zero opening. A static observer does not exist on the horizon itself, and no future-directed ray starting on the [Schwarzschild event horizon](schwarzschild-event-horizon.md) reaches exterior infinity. At $r=3\mu$, the critical half-angle is $\pi/2$; the exactly tangential critical ray stays on the unstable circular orbit. Escape cones for a moving observer are changed by [relativistic aberration](relativistic-aberration.md).
