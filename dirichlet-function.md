# Dirichlet function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann integrability criterion](riemann-integrability-criterion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_function)

The indicator of the [rational numbers](rational-number.md) is not [Riemann integrable](riemann-integrable-function.md) on a nondegenerate interval: every subinterval contains both rational and irrational points, so its lower sum is zero and its upper sum is the interval length.
