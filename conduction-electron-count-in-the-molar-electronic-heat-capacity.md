# Conduction-electron count in the molar electronic heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Free electron model](free-electron-model.md)

The low-temperature [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) of a three-dimensional [free electron gas](free-electron-model.md) is $(\pi^2/2)N_ekT/T_F$. Expressed per mole of metal atoms with $z$ model conduction electrons each, this becomes the displayed result. Expressed per mole of conduction electrons, set $z=1$. A thermal-window counting argument predicts the $T/T_F$ scaling but gives only an order-one coefficient; it must still retain the number of electrons when changing the molar basis.
