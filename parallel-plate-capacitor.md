# Parallel-plate capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor](capacitor.md)

Two parallel conducting plates of area $A$ separated by a small gap $d$, filled by material of [permittivity](permittivity.md) $\epsilon$, have [capacitance](capacitance.md) $C\simeq\epsilon A/d$ when fringing fields are negligible. Indeed, the interior [electric field](electric-field.md) is approximately $Q/(\epsilon A)$, and the [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) is approximately $Qd/(\epsilon A)$. A narrow curved capacitor gap has the same leading local approximation when its width is much less than the curvature radius.
