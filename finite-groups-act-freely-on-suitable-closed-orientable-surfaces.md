# Finite groups act freely on suitable closed orientable surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Deck transformation group](deck-transformation-group.md)

If a finite [group](group-split.md) $G$ is generated by $h$ elements, send the $a$-generators of the genus-$h$ [surface group](fundamental-group-of-a-surface.md) to those generators and its $b$-generators to the identity. The surface relation is satisfied, so this is a surjective [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md). Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) defines a connected regular [covering space](covering-space.md), on which the [deck transformation group](deck-transformation-group.md) $G$ acts freely. The lifted [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) and multiplicativity of the [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) give genus $g=1+|G|(h-1)$.
