# Corticostriatal circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Neural circuit](neural-circuit.md)

A circuit involving [cerebral cortex](cerebral-cortex.md) and [striatum](striatum.md), often understood within larger loops through [basal ganglia](basal-ganglia.md) and [thalamus](thalamus.md). Different loops can contribute to [learning](learning.md) and selecting actions; involvement of one component does not imply exclusivity of the whole function.
