<h1 id="ordered-cech-cochain-complex">Ordered Čech cochain complex</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Čech cochain complex](cech-cochain-complex.md)

For a [well-order](well-order.md) on the indices of an [open cover](open-cover.md), the ordered [Čech cochain complex](cech-cochain-complex.md) uses only strictly increasing tuples, with the alternating restriction [Čech differential](cech-differential.md). It computes the same [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md) as the complex using all tuples. If every finite intersection is nonempty and the coefficient [constant sheaf](constant-sheaf.md) has the same [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $A$ on each intersection, inserting the least index $\alpha$ gives a [cochain homotopy](cochain-homotopy.md): set $(hs)_I=s_{\alpha,I}$ when $\alpha$ is not in $I$, and $(hs)_I=0$ otherwise. Expanding the alternating sums gives $\delta h+h\delta=\operatorname{id}$ in every positive degree, since the terms deleting an index of $I$ cancel and the term deleting $\alpha$ is $s_I$. Hence positive [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md) vanishes in this situation; degree zero remains $A$.
