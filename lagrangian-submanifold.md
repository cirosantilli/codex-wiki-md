# Lagrangian submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrangian_submanifold)

A Lagrangian submanifold of a $2n$-dimensional symplectic manifold is an $n$-dimensional submanifold $L$ satisfying $\omega|_L=0$. The symplectic form identifies its [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) with its [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md).
