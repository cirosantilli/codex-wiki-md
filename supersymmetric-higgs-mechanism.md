# Supersymmetric Higgs mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric gauge theory](supersymmetric-gauge-theory.md)

At a vacuum obeying [F-flatness](f-flatness.md) and [D-flatness](d-flatness.md), charged [scalar field](scalar-field.md) expectation values can break an internal [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md) while preserving [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md). For each broken generator, a massless [vector multiplet](supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) combines with a [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md) into a [massive N=1 vector multiplet](massive-n-1-vector-multiplet.md). The vector absorbs one real [Goldstone boson](goldstone-boson.md); a real scalar remains, and the [gaugino](gaugino.md) mixes with a [higgsino](higgsino.md). All four bosonic and four fermionic on-shell states have a common mass.
