# Uniform integrability from bounded second moments

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md)

If $\sup_n\mathbb E[X_n^2]<\infty$, then $(X_n)$ is [uniformly integrable](uniform-integrability.md). Indeed, the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) give

$$
\mathbb E[|X_n|\mathbf1_{\{|X_n|>K\}}]
\leq\frac{\mathbb E[X_n^2]}{K}.
$$
