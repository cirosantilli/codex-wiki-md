# Spectral gap of the adjacent transposition shuffle

↑ **Parent:** [Random adjacent transposition shuffle](random-adjacent-transposition-shuffle.md)

For the [random adjacent transposition shuffle](random-adjacent-transposition-shuffle.md) with $n\geq2$, the generator $P-I$ is the [interchange process](interchange-process.md) on a [path graph](path-graph.md) with each edge rate $1/n$. The [Aldous spectral gap theorem](aldous-spectral-gap-theorem.md) reduces its [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) to that of the single-label [random walk](random-walk.md). The [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) $k\mapsto\cos(\ell\pi(k-1/2)/n)$, for $0\leq\ell<n$, have generator [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $-2(1-\cos(\ell\pi/n))/n$; direct substitution verifies both the interior and endpoint equations. Consequently $\gamma\sim\pi^2/n^3$.
