# Lie algebra of a normal Lie subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Lie subgroup](lie-subgroup.md)

Conjugation by every element of $G$ preserves a normal [Lie subgroup](lie-subgroup.md) $H$. Its differential therefore preserves $\mathfrak h=T_eH$. Differentiate that invariance along $\exp(tX)$ to obtain $\operatorname{ad}(X)\mathfrak h\subseteq\mathfrak h$, proving that $\mathfrak h$ is an [ideal of a Lie algebra](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Closed subgroups have a canonical Lie-subgroup structure; an arbitrary abstract subgroup need not.
