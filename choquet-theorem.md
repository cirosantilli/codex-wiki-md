# Choquet theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Compact metrizable convex set](compact-metrizable-convex-set.md)

Every point of a compact metrizable convex subset of a Hausdorff locally convex space is the [barycenter](barycenter.md) of a [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md) concentrated on its [extreme points](extreme-point.md). The theorem is an existence assertion; uniqueness requires additional hypotheses such as a simplex structure.
