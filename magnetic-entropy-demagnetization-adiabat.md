# Magnetic-entropy demagnetization adiabat

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic demagnetization](adiabatic-demagnetization.md)

When the [entropy](entropy-split.md) of independent magnetic moments dominates, a reversible isolated change fixes $B/T$. Lowering the field therefore lowers [temperature](temperature-split.md) as $T_f/T_i=B_f/B_i$. Other [heat capacities](heat-capacity.md), internal interactions and residual fields limit this ideal relation.
