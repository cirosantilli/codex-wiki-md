# Kerr-Newman metric

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerr–Newman_metric)

The stationary axisymmetric Einstein–Maxwell family of [asymptotically flat spacetimes](asymptotically-flat-spacetime.md) extends the [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md) by including [electric charge](electric-charge.md). Its horizon polynomial is the displayed $\Delta$; for $M^2>Q^2+a^2$ the roots are $r_\pm=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-Q^2-a^2}$. The outer [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md) has area $4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)$, angular velocity $a/(r_+^2+a^2)$ and electric potential $Qr_+/(r_+^2+a^2)$.
