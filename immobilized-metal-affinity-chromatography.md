# Immobilized metal affinity chromatography

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatography](chromatography-split.md)

[Immobilized metal affinity chromatography](immobilized-metal-affinity-chromatography.md) retains suitable ligands, including polyhistidine-tagged proteins, on a stationary phase bearing coordinated metal ions. Contaminants can also bind, so affinity recovery alone does not establish purity or identity.
