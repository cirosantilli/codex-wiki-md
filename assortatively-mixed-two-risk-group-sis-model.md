# Assortatively mixed two-risk-group SIS model

↑ **Parent:** [SIS model](sis-model.md)

For high-risk group fraction $p$, fixed total contact rates and reciprocal cross-group contacts, let $C=Bp/(1-p)$. The displayed [SIS model](sis-model.md) describes within-group infectious fractions. In fractions the transmission [matrix](matrix.md) is $M=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\C&D-C\end{pmatrix}$; in infected-person counts it is $\operatorname{diag}(p,1-p)M\operatorname{diag}(p,1-p)^{-1}$. A common [recovery rate](recovery-rate.md) gives [basic reproduction number](basic-reproduction-number.md) $\rho(M)/\gamma$. The [mean](expected-value.md) population susceptible fraction does not generally equal its reciprocal.
