# Composition of harmonic conjugate pairs

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic conjugate](harmonic-conjugate.md)

If $u+iv$ and $p+iq$ are [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md), with the image of the first inside the domain of the second, then $p(u,v)+iq(u,v)$ is holomorphic by the [chain rule](chain-rule.md). Directly, their [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) imply $[p(u,v)]_x=[q(u,v)]_y$ and $[p(u,v)]_y=-[q(u,v)]_x$. Differentiating these relations proves harmonicity of both parts. No nonvanishing derivative hypothesis is required.
