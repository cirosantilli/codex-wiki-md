# Intrinsic time-reversal phase

↑ **Parent:** [Time reversal of a complex scalar field](time-reversal-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md)

In the scalar transformation $\hat Ta(p)\hat T^{-1}=\eta_Ta(p_P)$, preservation of canonical commutators or the involution condition gives $|\eta_T|=1$. [Antiunitarity](antiunitary-operator.md) conjugates the phase on a second application, so the condition does not restrict it to plus or minus one. Redefine $a'=e^{i\chi}a$; then $\hat Ta'\hat T^{-1}=e^{-2i\chi}\eta_Ta'(p_P)$. Choosing $\chi=\arg(\eta_T)/2$ makes the intrinsic phase one. Oppositely rephasing the [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) annihilator implements the same change as a phase convention for the whole scalar field.
