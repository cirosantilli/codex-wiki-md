# Dimensionless concentration equilibrium constant

↑ **Parent:** [Equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md)

For ideal [concentration](concentration.md) activities and signed stoichiometric coefficients $\nu_i$, $K_c=\prod_i(c_i/c^\circ)^{\nu_i}$ at equilibrium. Its numerical value depends on the stated [standard concentration](standard-concentration.md) when $\sum_i\nu_i\ne0$. Number concentrations require a number-concentration reference; molar concentrations require the corresponding molar reference.
