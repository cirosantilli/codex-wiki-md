# Climate-methane chemical-lifetime feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

Changes in [temperature](temperature-split.md), [water vapor](water-vapor.md) and [radical](radical-chemistry.md) chemistry alter [methane](methane.md) removal. Warming can increase its [hydroxyl radical](hydroxyl-radical.md) reaction coefficient and the [hydroxyl radical](hydroxyl-radical.md) source from [oxygen singlet D atom](oxygen-singlet-d-atom.md) plus [water](water.md), tending to reduce chemical lifetime at fixed precursor conditions. Changed emissions and competing [radical](radical-chemistry.md) sinks can modify or reverse that conditional response.
