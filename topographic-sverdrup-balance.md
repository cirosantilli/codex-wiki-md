# Topographic Sverdrup balance

↑ **Parent:** [Sverdrup balance](sverdrup-balance.md)

When depth varies but the [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md) is constant, forcing can balance advection of $f/H$ rather than advection of planetary vorticity. This topographic potential-vorticity gradient plays the role normally played by the beta effect.
