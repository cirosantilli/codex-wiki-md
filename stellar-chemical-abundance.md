# Stellar chemical abundance

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar atmosphere](stellar-atmosphere.md)

A stellar abundance can be expressed as an elemental or isotopic mass fraction, or as a number ratio to [hydrogen](hydrogen.md). Inferring it from a [spectral line](spectral-line.md) requires a model for level populations, [opacity](opacity.md), radiation and velocity fields. Agreement between suitable ionization stages and weak photospheric lines tests whether the adopted [stellar atmosphere](stellar-atmosphere.md) is adequate.
