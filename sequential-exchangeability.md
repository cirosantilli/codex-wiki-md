# Sequential exchangeability

↑ **Parent:** [G-computation](g-computation.md)

For treatment history $\bar A_t$, observed history $H_t$, and final [potential outcome](potential-outcome.md) $Y(\bar a)$, sequential exchangeability requires

$$
Y(\bar a)\mathrel\perp A_t\mid H_t
$$

at every treatment time. Together with [consistency of potential outcomes](consistency-in-causal-inference.md) and [positivity in causal inference](positivity-assumption.md), it identifies longitudinal effects by [G-computation](g-computation.md).
