# Critical photon orbits of Schwarzschild spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial null orbit in Schwarzschild spacetime](equatorial-null-orbit-in-schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

At the critical [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $b=3\sqrt3M$, the null orbit equation factors as $(u')^2=(u-1/3)^2(2u+1/3)$ for $u=M/r$. One oriented family has the displayed solution. As $\phi\to-\infty$, positive $A$ approaches the [photon sphere](photon-sphere.md) from larger radii and negative $A$ from smaller radii; $A=0$ is the circular orbit. The physical domain must retain $r>0$ and the positive square root in its implicit parametrization.
