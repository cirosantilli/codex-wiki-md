# Kaon CP mixing parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral kaon mixing](neutral-kaon-mixing.md)

In the flavor convention $K_1=(K^0-\bar K^0)/\sqrt2$ and $K_2=(K^0+\bar K^0)/\sqrt2$, the parameter $\epsilon=(a-b)/(a+b)$ writes normalized [mass](mass.md) states as $(K_1+\epsilon K_2)/\sqrt{1+|\epsilon|^2}$ and $(K_2+\epsilon K_1)/\sqrt{1+|\epsilon|^2}$. In the Hermitian [mass](mass.md)-only approximation with correlated roots, $\epsilon=i\tan(\varphi/2)$ and the states are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md). For the full non-Hermitian decay matrix the real part need not vanish. The parameter depends on the flavor-state phase convention; observable [CP violation](cp-violation.md) involves convention-invariant combinations with decay amplitudes.
