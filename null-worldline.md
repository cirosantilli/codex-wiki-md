# Null worldline

↑ **Parent:** [World line](world-line.md)

A [worldline](world-line.md) whose tangent is a [null vector](null-vector.md). In flat [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), a freely propagating light signal follows a straight [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md). Its coordinate speed is $c$ in an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md), even when another observer moves relative to that frame.
