# Geiger-Marsden angular-scattering experiment of 1913

↑ **Parent:** [Geiger-Marsden experiment](geiger-marsden-experiment.md)

The later angular test used an evacuated chamber with a [radon](radon.md) source and scattering foil fixed in place. A diaphragm served as a [collimator](collimator.md); a [zinc sulfide](zinc-sulfide.md) screen and attached [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) rotated about the foil on a graduated platform. [Particle scintillation](scintillation-physics.md) counts sampled successive [angles](angle.md). Foil-out counts supplied [background subtraction](background-subtraction.md), with corrections for source [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) and finite beam width. Shielding reduced interfering radiation, but screen glow limited large [angles](angle.md). This tested the [Rutherford differential cross-section](rutherford-differential-cross-section.md).[The Laws of Deflexion of α Particles through Large Angles, transcription PDF pp. 3–7, Variation of Scattering with Angle, Fig. 1 and Tables I–II](the-laws-of-deflexion-of-alpha-particles-through-large-angles-by-hans-geiger-and-ernest-marsden.md)
