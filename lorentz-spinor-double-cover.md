# Lorentz spinor double cover

↑ **Parent:** [Proper orthochronous Lorentz group](proper-orthochronous-lorentz-group.md)

Identify Minkowski vectors with Hermitian matrices $X=x^0I+x^a\sigma_a$. The action $X\mapsto SXS^\dagger$ preserves $\det X=(x^0)^2-|\mathbf x|^2$. It gives a surjective homomorphism from $SL(2,\mathbb C)$ to the [Proper orthochronous Lorentz group](proper-orthochronous-lorentz-group.md) with kernel $\{I,-I\}$. Polar decomposition retracts the covering group onto [SU(2)](su-2-group.md), making it simply connected. This cover permits half-integer spin [group representations](group-representation.md) that do not descend to the Lorentz group itself.
