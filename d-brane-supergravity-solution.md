# D-brane supergravity solution

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

For the asymptotically flat extremal D$p$ solution with $p<7$, $H=1+Q/r^{7-p}$ is harmonic away from the source and $e^\Phi=g_sH^{(3-p)/4}$. The electric potential can be chosen as $C_{0\cdots p}=g_s^{-1}(H^{-1}-1)$; magnetic descriptions use the dual flux, and a [D3-brane](d3-brane.md) has self-dual five-form flux. An equally oriented probe has DBI density $-\mu_p g_s^{-1}H^{-1}$ and Wess-Zumino density $+\mu_p g_s^{-1}(H^{-1}-1)$, whose sum is position independent. This proves the corresponding [parallel D-brane no-force identity](parallel-d-brane-no-force-identity.md) in the classical background. The supergravity approximation requires controlled curvature and coupling; it does not resolve every brane core.
