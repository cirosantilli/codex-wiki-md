# Geodynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary dynamo](planetary-dynamo.md)

The geodynamo is the maintenance of Earth's [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) by electrically conducting fluid motion in its liquid outer core. Rotation and buoyant [convection](convection.md) organize helical flow and permit mean-field poloidal–toroidal regeneration. A scalar homogeneous alpha model explains a mechanism and a threshold but omits spherical boundaries, [anisotropy](anisotropy.md), spatial variations and [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) feedback. Reversals and saturation require dynamical or time-dependent transport information beyond a growing constant-coefficient kinematic mode.
