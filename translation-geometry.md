# Translation (geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Affine space](affine-space.md)

On an [affine space](affine-space.md) modeled on a [vector space](vector-space-split.md), the map $T_v(x)=x+v$ for a fixed vector $v$. Its [derivative](derivative.md) is the identity, and $T_vT_w=T_{v+w}$. Changes between [flat coordinates](natural-coordinate-of-a-holomorphic-differential.md) on a [translation surface](translation-surface.md) have this form.
