# Longest increasing subsequence

↑ **Parent:** [Increasing subsequence](increasing-subsequence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longest_increasing_subsequence)

A longest increasing subsequence is an [increasing subsequence](increasing-subsequence.md) of maximum length. Changing or deleting one term changes its length by at most one.
