# Starburst galaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy evolution](galaxy-evolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starburst_galaxy)

A starburst galaxy undergoes an episode of rapid [star formation](star-formation.md). An instantaneous-burst idealization places all newly formed [stars](star.md) at one [stellar age](stellar-age.md), so their present evolutionary-state fractions follow from the [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) at that fixed age rather than from an average over formation times.
