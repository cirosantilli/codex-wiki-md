# Matrix exponential when the square is minus the identity

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md)

If a real square matrix satisfies $M^2=-I$, split the absolutely convergent [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) into even and odd powers to obtain $e^{tM}=I\cos t+M\sin t$. It is the fundamental matrix for $\mathbf x'=M\mathbf x$. Consequently every solution has period $2\pi$, and the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) over the complex numbers belong to $\{i,-i\}$.
