# Radical of the augmentation Specht module

↑ **Parent:** [Modular Specht module](modular-specht-module.md)

The [Specht module](specht-module.md) $S^{(n-1,1)}$ is the augmentation subspace of the natural permutation module $F^n$, with the standard [tabloid bilinear form](tabloid-bilinear-form.md). Its orthogonal complement in $F^n$ is the constant-vector line. That line lies in the augmentation subspace exactly when the [field](field.md) characteristic divides $n$, giving the displayed radical. Equivalently, the [basis](basis.md) $v_i-v_n$ has [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) $I_{n-1}+J_{n-1}$. The [James submodule theorem](james-submodule-theorem.md) makes every nonzero radical quotient simple, with [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $n-2$ when $p\mid n$ and $n-1$ otherwise. The case $n=2,p=2$ gives the zero quotient rather than a nonzero [simple module](irreducible-module.md).
