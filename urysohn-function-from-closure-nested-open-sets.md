# Urysohn function from closure-nested open sets

↑ **Parent:** [Urysohn's lemma](urysohn-s-lemma.md)

Let [open sets](open-set.md) $U_t$ be indexed by a [dense subset](dense-set.md) of $[0,1]$, with $\overline{U_s}\subseteq U_t$ for $s<t$. Taking the infimum of membership indices, with value one outside their [union](set-union.md), gives a [continuous function](continuous-function.md). The strict sublevel [sets](set-split.md) are [unions](set-union.md) of $U_t$; strict superlevel [sets](set-split.md) are [unions](set-union.md) of [complements](complement-of-a-set.md) of $\overline{U_t}$. These identities prove [continuity](continuous-function.md).
