# Homogeneous mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Compartmental models (epidemiology)](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md)

[Homogeneous mixing](homogeneous-mixing.md) assumes that contacts sample the whole modelled population without differences between groups or locations. In the [SIR model](sir-model.md), the chance that a sampled contact belongs to the [infectious compartment](infectious-compartment.md) is $i=I/N$. This assumption makes a [frequency-dependent transmission](frequency-dependent-transmission.md) rule depend on the overall [infectious compartment](infectious-compartment.md) rather than a [graph](graph-split.md) of individual contacts. Structured contacts require a different [mathematical model](mathematical-model.md), such as a [discrete SIR epidemic on a graph](discrete-sir-epidemic-on-a-graph.md).
