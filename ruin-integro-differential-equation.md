# Ruin integro-differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ultimate ruin probability](ultimate-ruin-probability.md)

In the [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md), condition on the first arrival of the [Poisson process](poisson-process.md). If available capital just before it is $v$, ruin has probability $\overline F_X(v)+\int_0^v f_X(x)\psi(v-x)\,dx$. Integrate this expression at $v=u+ct$ against $\lambda e^{-\lambda t}$ and change variables from $t$ to $v$. Differentiating the lower limit gives the displayed equation. The claim-density [convolution](convolution.md) is continuous for bounded $\psi$, so the first-arrival representation also supplies the differentiability required by the equation.
