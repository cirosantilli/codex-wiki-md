# Stationary Rossby-wave vertical propagation

↑ **Parent:** [Baroclinic Rossby wave](baroclinic-rossby-wave.md)

For constant [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) and uniform [zonal flow](zonal-flow.md), the [three-dimensional quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) equation gives $\omega=Uk-\beta k/[k^2+l^2+(f_0^2/N^2)m^2]$. Set $\omega=0$ with $k\ne0$ to obtain the displayed formula. For $\beta>0$, oscillatory vertical structure requires $0<U<\beta/(k^2+l^2)$. Differentiation gives $c_{g,z}=2\beta k(f_0^2/N^2)m/[k^2+l^2+(f_0^2/N^2)m^2]^2$, so an upward outgoing [radiation condition](radiation-condition.md) selects $km>0$. The [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md) enters squared: the criterion does not reverse between hemispheres. A density-scale-height correction belongs to a different, compressible [log-pressure quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](log-pressure-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) model.
