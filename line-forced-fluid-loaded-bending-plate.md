# Line-forced fluid-loaded bending plate

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic plate](elastic-plate.md)

An [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) coupled to one compressible fluid half-space has an outgoing [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) kernel $D$, where $\gamma^2=k^2-\omega^2/c_0^2$. A harmonic line [force](force.md) $F_0$ produces displacement transform $F_0\gamma/D$. Propagating acoustic components have $\gamma=-i\sqrt{\omega^2/c_0^2-k^2}$, whereas guided components decay normally to the plate. The outgoing prescription separates [acoustic energy flux](acoustic-energy-flux.md) from guided-wave energy transport.
