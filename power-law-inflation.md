# Power-law inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)

[Power-law inflation](power-law-inflation.md) is [cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md) with a [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) proportional to $t^p$ for constant $p>1$. Its [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) is $H=p/t$ and its [first Hubble slow-roll parameter](first-hubble-slow-roll-parameter.md) is $\epsilon_H=1/p$. The [comoving Hubble radius](comoving-hubble-radius.md) decreases as $t^{1-p}$. A canonical [inflaton](inflaton.md) with an exponential [inflaton potential](inflaton-potential.md) admits an exact example. Because $\epsilon_H$ is constant, an indefinitely maintained power law has no [graceful exit from inflation](graceful-exit-from-inflation.md).
