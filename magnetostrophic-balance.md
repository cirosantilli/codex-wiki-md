# Magnetostrophic balance

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

The leading force balance in a rapidly rotating conducting fluid when inertial and viscous forces are small, while [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md), [pressure](pressure.md), [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) and [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) remain significant. The [Rossby number](rossby-number.md) and [Ekman number](ekman-number.md) measure the omitted inertia and bulk viscosity. Rotation does not balance an arbitrary magnetic torque: impenetrable axisymmetric boundaries impose the [Taylor constraint](taylor-constraint.md). Magnetic diffusion can remain in the induction equation even when viscosity is neglected in momentum balance.
