# Thermal expansion tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md)

The tensor relating a small unconstrained thermal [strain](strain.md) to a [temperature](temperature-split.md) change: $\boldsymbol\varepsilon^{\rm th}=\boldsymbol\alpha\Delta T$. A crystal orientation rotates this tensor into laboratory coordinates.
