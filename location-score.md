# Location score

↑ **Parent:** [Location family](location-family.md)

The location score is the [score function](informant-function.md) for translating a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $f$. The minus sign comes from differentiating $f(y-\theta)$ in $\theta$. Under regular tail conditions, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $E_f\rho=0$ and $E_f(\varepsilon\rho)=1$. These identities determine projections of the [score function](informant-function.md) onto the span of the centered error.
