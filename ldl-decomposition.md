# LDL decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical linear algebra](numerical-linear-algebra.md)

An LDL decomposition of a real symmetric matrix is

$$
A=LDL^T,
$$

where $L$ is unit lower triangular and $D$ is diagonal. Symmetric elimination computes it one [Schur complement](schur-complement.md) at a time. The matrix is positive definite exactly when every diagonal pivot in $D$ is positive.
