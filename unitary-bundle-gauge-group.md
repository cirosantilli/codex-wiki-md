# Unitary bundle gauge group

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary bundle gauge transformation](unitary-bundle-gauge-transformation.md)

The group of [unitary bundle gauge transformations](unitary-bundle-gauge-transformation.md) under composition. It acts on connections and their solution spaces. The stabilizer of a connection is the centralizer of its [holonomy](holonomy.md) acting by parallel automorphisms, so a quotient can have singularities when this stabilizer increases.
