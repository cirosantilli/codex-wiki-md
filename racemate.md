# Racemate

↑ **Parent:** [Enantiomer](enantiomer.md)

A [racemate](racemate.md) contains the same [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of each member of an [enantiomer](enantiomer.md) pair. Their equal and opposite contributions cancel in ordinary solution [polarimetry](polarimetry.md) under identical conditions. Zero net [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md) therefore does not establish [achirality](achirality.md) of the individual [molecules](molecule-split.md).[McMurry, Organic Chemistry, §5.8: equal enantiomer populations and cancelling optical rotations](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/5-8-racemic-mixtures-and-the-resolution-of-enantiomers.md)
