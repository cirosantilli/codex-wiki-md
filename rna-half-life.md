# RNA half-life

↑ **Parent:** [Post-transcriptional regulation](post-transcriptional-regulation.md)

The time for half of an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) population to disappear under specified conditions. For a homogeneous first-order decay process it equals $\ln 2/k_{\mathrm d}$; ongoing synthesis and changing [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) states complicate abundance measurements.
