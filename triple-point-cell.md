# Triple-point cell

↑ **Parent:** [Triple point](triple-point.md)

A triple-point cell turns [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) into a reproducible reference for [thermometer calibration](thermometer-calibration.md). The NIST water-cell demonstration uses a sealed glass vessel containing [water](water.md) of specified [isotopic composition](isotopic-composition.md) and an open central thermometer well. Cooling this well with [solid](solid.md) [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) and alcohol freezes an [ice](ice.md) mantle while leaving [liquid](liquid.md) [water](water.md) and its vapor. The coolant is outside the sealed sample; the sample's vapor space does not communicate with room air.[Triple-Point Cell, preparation description, uploaded 21 December 2016](triple-point-cell-nist-demonstration.md)

In the actual NIST cell-comparison experiment, newly frozen mantles were compared with an aged reference mantle in a controlled maintenance bath. A warm aluminium rod melted a thin inner layer around the well, allowing the mantle to rotate freely and establishing an [ice](ice.md)–[water](water.md) interface close to the sensor. A [standard platinum resistance thermometer](standard-platinum-resistance-thermometer.md) and a [resistance bridge](resistance-bridge.md) measured the [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) ratio to a calibrated, temperature-controlled reference resistor. Readings at two currents permitted extrapolation to zero electrical [power](power.md), correcting self-heating; measured immersion depths supplied [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) corrections. Comparing preparations over time tested stabilization rather than assuming freshly frozen [ice](ice.md) was ready.[Furukawa, Mangum and Strouse, Effects of different methods of preparation of ice mantles of triple point of water cells on the temporal behaviour of the triple-point temperatures (1997), Abstract, §1 and §§3.2–3.5: aged reference, inner melt, bridge readout, zero-power and hydrostatic corrections](effects-of-different-methods-of-preparation-of-ice-mantles-of-triple-point-of-water-cells-on-the-temporal-behaviour-of-the-triple-point-temperatures-by-g-t-furukawa-and-others.md)

The inference is a reproducible fixed-point response for the [thermometer](thermometer.md), not a direct reading of three [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md). The reference cell anchors the comparison; a water point alone does not determine a [thermometer](thermometer.md)'s response at every other [temperature](temperature-split.md). The ideal $F=0$ argument excludes impurity and [isotope](isotope.md) variation, finite interface effects and unintended [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md); real cells also require adequate immersion and stable electrical references. Repeatability is evidence that these effects are controlled, not a reason to omit their uncertainty.

Since 20 May 2019 the [kelvin](kelvin.md) has been defined through a fixed numerical value of the [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md). The thermodynamic [temperature](temperature-split.md) of the [water](water.md) triple point is consequently a measured quantity with uncertainty, rather than an exact value imposed by the [kelvin](kelvin.md) definition. Its continued use as an assigned reference on the [International Temperature Scale of 1990](international-temperature-scale-of-1990.md) is a separate convention.[Resolution 1 of the 26th CGPM (2018), effective date; Appendix 2, third bullet; Appendix 3, kelvin definition](on-the-revision-of-the-international-system-of-units-si-26th-cgpm-resolution-1-2018.md)[§2.3.1: practical temperature scales and ITS-90](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md)
