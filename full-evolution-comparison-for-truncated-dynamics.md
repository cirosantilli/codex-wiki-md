# Full-evolution comparison for truncated dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Lieb-Robinson bound](lieb-robinson-bound.md)

A Duhamel comparison can bound truncation error using [commutators](commutator.md) evolved under the full Hamiltonian. This avoids assuming that a black-box [Lieb-Robinson bound](lieb-robinson-bound.md) for $H$ also holds for every truncation. Polynomial shell weights and exponential distance suppression make the sum over omitted shells convergent. Comparing two truncations through the full evolution bounds their difference.
