# Inclination to the invariable plane of two orbital planes

↑ **Parent:** [Invariable plane](invariable-plane.md)

For two groups carrying [angular momenta](angular-momentum.md) $L_a,L_b$ with [mutual inclination](mutual-inclination.md) $\Delta I$, resolving total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) parallel and perpendicular to the second group's normal gives its [orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md) relative to the [invariable plane](invariable-plane.md):

$$
I_b=\operatorname{atan2}(L_a\sin\Delta I,L_b+L_a\cos\Delta I).
$$

For a positive denominator, $\tan I_b=\sin\Delta I/(L_b/L_a+\cos\Delta I)$. The larger-angular-momentum group lies closer to the [invariable plane](invariable-plane.md).
