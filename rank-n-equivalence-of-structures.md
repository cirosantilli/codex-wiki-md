# Rank-n equivalence of structures

↑ **Parent:** [Quantifier rank](quantifier-rank.md)

Two [first-order structures](first-order-structure.md) are rank-n equivalent if they agree on all sentences of [quantifier rank](quantifier-rank.md) at most $n$. The tuple version compares formulas at fixed tuples. For finite relational vocabulary this is equivalent to a Duplicator winning strategy in the $n$-round [Ehrenfeucht-Fraïssé game](ehrenfeucht-fraisse-game.md).
