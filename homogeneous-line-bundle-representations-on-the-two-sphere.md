# Homogeneous line-bundle representations on the two-sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Line bundle](line-bundle.md)

On $S^2=SU(2)/U(1)$ a degree-$q$ equivariant line bundle corresponds to isotropy character $\operatorname{diag}(e^{it},e^{-it})\mapsto e^{-iqt}$. The [Peter-Weyl theorem](peter-weyl-theorem.md) computes its section multiplicities by the occurrence of weight $-q$ in the [SU(2) representation](representation-theory-of-su-2.md) $\operatorname{Sym}^n\mathbb C^2$. Its weights $n,n-2,\ldots,-n$ give the displayed multiplicity-one decomposition. The spinor lines have degrees $-1,+1$ and thus contain all odd $n$, once each.
