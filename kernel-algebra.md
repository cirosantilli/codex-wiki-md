# Kernel (algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Homomorphism](homomorphism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kernel_(algebra))

A [kernel](kernel-algebra.md) records the information lost by a [homomorphism](homomorphism.md). For a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) it is the [preimage](preimage.md) of the [identity element](identity-element.md), giving the [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md); for a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) or [linear map](linear-map.md) it is the preimage of zero, giving the [kernel of a ring homomorphism](kernel-of-a-ring-homomorphism.md) or [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). More generally, the relation $a\sim b\iff f(a)=f(b)$ records which inputs the homomorphism identifies. In the group case, its [equivalence classes](equivalence-class.md) are [cosets](coset.md) of the [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md), as explained by [fibres of a group homomorphism are cosets of its kernel](fibres-of-a-group-homomorphism-are-cosets-of-its-kernel.md).
