# Galactic tide

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy dynamics](galaxy-dynamics.md)

The differential [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md) of a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) across a smaller bound system. Its [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md) can slowly change the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) of distant [comets](comet.md), changing their [periapses](periapsis.md) and their access to planetary encounters.
