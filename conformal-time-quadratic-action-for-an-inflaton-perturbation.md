# Conformal-time quadratic action for an inflaton perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Canonically rescaled de Sitter scalar mode](canonically-rescaled-de-sitter-scalar-mode.md)

Neglecting metric perturbations, the rescaling $f=a\delta\phi$ removes the expansion friction from the scalar perturbation action after a boundary term is discarded. Its canonical momentum is $f'$, and each real Fourier oscillator has time-dependent squared frequency $k^2+a^2V''-a''/a$. The oscillator can become inverted on [superhorizon scales](superhorizon-scale.md); the canonical commutation relations still apply.
