# Trimethylaluminium

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

The [aluminium](aluminium.md) methyl compound commonly forming $\mathrm{Al_2Me_6}$ with four terminal [methyl groups](methyl-group.md) and two electron-deficient methyl bridges. Variable-temperature NMR distinguishes slow and fast site exchange.
