# Poisson formula for a holomorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson integral](poisson-integral.md)

For $f$ holomorphic on a neighbourhood of the radius-$R$ [closed disk](closed-disc.md) and $|z|<R$, the displayed representation holds even though $f$ may be complex-valued. For $z\ne0$, put $w=R^2/\bar z$. On $|\zeta|=R$, the [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel.md) equals $\zeta/(\zeta-z)-\zeta/(\zeta-w)$. Replacing $d\theta$ by $d\zeta/(i\zeta)$ reduces the formula to the [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md) minus a zero contour integral whose pole lies outside. The case $z=0$ is the [mean value property for holomorphic functions](mean-value-property-for-holomorphic-functions.md).
