<h1 id="levy-measure">Lévy measure</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy process](levy-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lévy_measure)

The Lévy measure $\nu$ of a [Lévy process](levy-process.md) records the intensity of its jumps: for every measurable set $A$ bounded away from zero, $\nu(A)$ is the expected number per unit time of jumps whose sizes lie in $A$. It satisfies

$$
\nu(\{0\})=0,
\qquad
\int_{\mathbb R^d}(1\wedge |x|^2)\,\nu(dx)<\infty.
$$
