# Pure cubic number field

↑ **Parent:** [Number field](number-field.md)

A pure cubic number field is a degree-three [number field](number-field.md) generated by a root of $X^3-m$ for a rational number $m$ that is not a rational cube. For a positive [square-free integer](square-free-integer.md) $m>1$, this polynomial is an [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md) at any prime dividing $m$. The [ring of integers of a number field](ring-of-integers.md) depends on congruences at $3$, as expressed by the [integral basis of a nonexceptional pure cubic field](integral-basis-of-a-nonexceptional-pure-cubic-field.md).
