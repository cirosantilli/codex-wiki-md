# Consistency of a uniquely bracketed zero

↑ **Parent:** [Z-estimator](z-estimator.md)

Suppose random continuous real functions have unique zeros. If pointwise [convergence in probability](convergence-in-probability.md) gives opposite strict limiting signs at the two ends of every sufficiently small interval about the target, the [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) traps their zeros in those intervals with [probability](probability.md) tending to one. The limiting function need not be continuous and the random functions need not be monotone.
