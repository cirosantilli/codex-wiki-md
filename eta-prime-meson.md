# Eta prime meson

↑ **Parent:** [Eta and eta prime mesons](eta-and-eta-prime-mesons.md)

The heavier physical isoscalar member of the light [pseudoscalar meson nonet](pseudoscalar-meson-nonet.md). It mixes the octet and singlet flavor directions, and is distinct from the lighter [eta meson](eta-meson.md); both occupy the central flavor weight.
