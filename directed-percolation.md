# Directed percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation theory](percolation-theory.md)

In [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on a [directed graph](directed-graph.md), independently open each [directed edge](directed-edge.md) with [probability](probability.md) $p$. The out-cluster of $x$ consists of vertices reachable by open [directed paths](directed-path.md). In [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md), independently open vertices and require every vertex of the [directed path](directed-path.md), including $x$, to be open. The same definitions allow a [multigraph](multigraph.md). The orientation matters: an infinite underlying [connected graph](connected-graph.md) need not contain an infinite out-cluster from a specified root.
