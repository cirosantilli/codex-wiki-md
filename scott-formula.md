# Scott formula

↑ **Parent:** [Countable infinitary logic](countable-infinitary-logic.md)

A Scott formula describes a finite [tuple](finite-tuple.md) at a transfinite stage of the [back-and-forth method](back-and-forth-method.md). Stage zero specifies the complete atomic description; successor stages specify matching one-element extensions in both directions; limit stages conjoin the earlier descriptions. For a countable structure the equivalences on its finite [tuples](finite-tuple.md) stabilize at a countable [ordinal](ordinal.md).
