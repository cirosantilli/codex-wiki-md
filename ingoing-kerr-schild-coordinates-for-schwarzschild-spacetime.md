# Ingoing Kerr-Schild coordinates for Schwarzschild spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md)

With $f=1-2\mu/r$, the time coordinate $t'=t+(2\mu/c)\log|r/(2\mu)-1|$ gives $ds^2=fc^2dt'^2-(4\mu c/r)dt'\,dr-(1+2\mu/r)dr^2-r^2d\Omega^2$. Its radial [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) block has [determinant](determinant.md) $-c^2$, so it extends regularly across the future [Schwarzschild event horizon](schwarzschild-event-horizon.md). The advanced null time of [ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md) is $v=t'+r/c$, not $t'$ itself. Radial null directions satisfy $dr/(c\,dt')=-1$ or $(1-2\mu/r)/(1+2\mu/r)$.
