# Isentropic Euler equations

↑ **Parent:** [Isentropic flow](isentropic-flow.md)

In one spatial dimension, inviscid isentropic mass and momentum conservation are

$$
\rho_t+(\rho u)_x=0,
\qquad
u_t+uu_x+\rho^{-1}p_x=0,
\qquad p=p(\rho).
$$
