# Sugar puckering

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)

[Sugar puckering](sugar-puckering.md) describes the nonplanar conformation of a sugar ring. In nucleic acids, C2′-endo and C3′-endo conformations alter backbone geometry and help distinguish [B-DNA](b-dna.md) from [A-DNA](a-dna.md).
