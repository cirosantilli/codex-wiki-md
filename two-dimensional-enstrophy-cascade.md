# Two-dimensional enstrophy cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Energy cascade](energy-cascade.md)

A two-dimensional forward [two-dimensional enstrophy cascade](two-dimensional-enstrophy-cascade.md) has the idealized spectrum $E(k)\propto k^{-3}$. The energy integral above a wavenumber scales as its inverse square, whereas the cumulative [enstrophy](enstrophy.md) integral $\int k^2E(k)\,dk$ grows logarithmically. The spectrum used here omits possible further logarithmic corrections.
