# Index-one plane-parallel polytrope

↑ **Parent:** [Polytrope](polytrope.md)

For a symmetric self-gravitating slab with [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md) $p=K\rho^2$ and constant $K>0$, [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $2K\rho'=-\psi'$. The plane-parallel [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) then yields $\rho''+(2\pi G/K)\rho=0$. Reflection symmetry at the midplane fixes the title profile. For a vacuum surface, retain the nonnegative profile only on $|z|\le z_s=\pi/(2k)$ and put $\rho=0$ outside. The half-thickness is independent of central density in this ideal index-one model, while the surface mass density is $\Sigma=2\rho_c/k$.
