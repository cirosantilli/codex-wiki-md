# Semiclassical soliton mass

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field-theory soliton](classical-field-theory-soliton-split.md)

A weak-coupling expansion of a [vacuum-subtracted soliton mass](vacuum-subtracted-soliton-mass.md) begins with the classical energy and adds the [Gaussian fluctuation approximation](gaussian-fluctuation-approximation.md). For [Sine-Gordon theory](sine-gordon-theory.md), $M=8m/\beta^2+\Delta M_{1\mathrm{loop}}+O(m\beta^2)$. The term of order $m$ includes both the vacuum-subtracted fluctuation frequencies and the appropriate [mass counterterm](mass-counterterm.md).
