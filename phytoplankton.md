# Phytoplankton

↑ **Parent:** [Plankton](plankton.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phytoplankton)

[Phytoplankton](phytoplankton.md) are drifting aquatic [organisms](organism-split.md) that carry out [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md), including many [algae](algae.md). They can supply food to [zooplankton](zooplankton.md) in an [ecosystem](ecosystem.md).
