# Aspect ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensionless quantity](dimensionless-quantity.md)

A ratio of characteristic dimensions of a shape, with the dimension pair stated. For a [nanowire](nanowire.md) it usually means length divided by diameter. A high [aspect ratio](aspect-ratio.md) can promote spanning connectivity at low projected metal coverage.
