# Isolobal analogy

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital theory](molecular-orbital-theory.md)

A correspondence between molecular fragments based on the number, approximate symmetry, shape, energy and occupation of frontier orbitals. Equal total [electron](electron.md) count alone is insufficient; the comparison predicts transferable bonding possibilities rather than identical chemistry.
