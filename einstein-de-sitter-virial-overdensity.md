# Einstein-de Sitter virial overdensity

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical-collapse model](spherical-collapse-model.md)

For ideal isolated [spherical collapse](spherical-collapse-model.md), energy conservation and the [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) give $R_{\rm vir}=R_{\rm ta}/2$ and $\rho_{\rm vir}=8\rho_{\rm ta}$ if the density-profile shape is unchanged. The cycloid gives $t_{\rm coll}=2t_{\rm ta}$ and $\rho_{\rm ta}/\bar\rho(t_{\rm ta})=9\pi^2/16$. Since the [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) has $\bar\rho\propto t^{-2}$, the overdensity at the nominal collapse time is $32(9\pi^2/16)=18\pi^2$. Hence $\rho_{\rm vir}=18\pi^2\rho_0(1+z_{\rm coll})^3$. The collapse time is that of the formal cold solution; actual virialization replaces its point collapse.
