# Nuclear mass defect

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic mass](atomic-mass.md)

The [mass](mass.md) deficit of a bound [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus.md) relative to its separated [protons](proton.md) and [neutrons](neutron.md). If their [rest masses](invariant-mass.md) are $m_p$ and $m_n$, the [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus.md) has [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $M_{\rm nuc}$, and the positive [nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md) is $B$, then

$$
\Delta m=Zm_p+Nm_n-M_{\rm nuc}=\frac{B}{c^2},
$$

with $c$ the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). Releasing [energy](energy.md) when the [nucleons](nucleon.md) bind reduces the [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) of the bound system without changing the exact count $A=Z+N$. A neutral [atomic mass](atomic-mass.md) also includes $Z$ [Electron](electron.md) [rest masses](invariant-mass.md), reduced by the [energy](energy.md) required to separate those bound [Electrons](electron.md). Consequently $A-m_{\rm a}/\mathrm u$ is not the definition of the [nuclear mass defect](nuclear-mass-defect.md): the reference unit and constituent [rest masses](invariant-mass.md) must also be accounted for.
