# Diagonal of a polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Polygon](polygon.md)

A [diagonal of a polygon](diagonal-of-a-polygon.md) joins two [vertices of a polygon](vertex-of-a-polygon.md) that are not endpoints of the same [side of a polygon](side-of-a-polygon.md). A [square](square.md) has two [diagonals of a polygon](diagonal-of-a-polygon.md), forming a two-element [group orbit](orbit-group-theory.md) under its [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of symmetries.
