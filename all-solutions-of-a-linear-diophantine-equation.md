# All solutions of a linear Diophantine equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Diophantine equation](linear-diophantine-equation.md)

If $a,b$ are nonzero [integers](integer.md), $d=\gcd(a,b)$, and $(x_0,y_0)$ is one solution of the [linear Diophantine equation](linear-diophantine-equation.md) $ax+by=c$, then all solutions are

$$
x=x_0+\frac bd\,t,\qquad y=y_0-\frac ad\,t,\qquad t\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Subtract two solutions and use the fact that $a/d$ and $b/d$ are [coprime integers](coprime-integers.md). The [linear Diophantine equation solvability criterion](linear-diophantine-equation-solvability-criterion.md) says that a particular solution exists exactly when $d$ divides $c$; the [extended Euclidean algorithm](extended-euclidean-algorithm.md) constructs one. This parametrization separates existence from the complete family of answers.
