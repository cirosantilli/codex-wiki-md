# Conservative Thomson scattering does not thermalize radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md)

Coherent [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) by cold [electrons](electron.md) changes a [photon](photon.md)'s direction without changing its [frequency](frequency.md) in the electron rest frame. A monochromatic incident radiation field therefore remains monochromatic after arbitrarily many such scatterings. Large scattering [optical depth](optical-depth.md) can isotropize radiation but does not imply a [Planck photon distribution](planck-photon-distribution.md). True absorption and emission, or suitable additional inelastic processes and photon production, are needed to establish a [blackbody](blackbody.md) spectrum independently of the initial radiation field.
