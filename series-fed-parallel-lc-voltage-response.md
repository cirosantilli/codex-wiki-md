# Series-fed parallel LC voltage response

↑ **Parent:** [LC circuit](lc-circuit.md)

An ideal [voltage](voltage.md) source drives a series [resistor](resistor.md) feeding a parallel [inductor](inductor.md) and [capacitor](capacitor.md). In the $e^{i\omega t}$ convention, the branch [voltage](voltage.md) ratio is the title expression. It is a band-pass response with unit peak at $1/\sqrt{LC}$, phase from $+\pi/2$ through zero to $-\pi/2$, and half-power bandwidth $1/(RC)$. At [resonance](resonance.md) the input current vanishes while equal and opposite [inductor](inductor.md)/[capacitor](capacitor.md) currents remain finite. Reactive branch-current magnitudes must be combined as [phasors](phasor.md), not added as positive scalars.
