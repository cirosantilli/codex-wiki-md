# Young subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory of the symmetric group](representation-theory-of-the-symmetric-group-split.md)

For a composition $\lambda$ of $n$, a Young subgroup permutes independently the blocks of a set partition of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ with sizes $\lambda_i$, and is isomorphic to $S_{\lambda_1}\times\cdots\times S_{\lambda_r}$. Reordering the block sizes gives a conjugate subgroup. Inducing its [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) gives a [Young permutation module](young-permutation-module.md).
