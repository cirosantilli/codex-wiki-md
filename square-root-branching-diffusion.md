# Square-root branching diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md)

A nonnegative diffusion with zero absorbing, often called the critical Feller branching diffusion; other conventions multiply the coefficient by a fixed positive constant. Its diffusion coefficient is not Lipschitz at zero. Independent copies add according to the [addition law for square-root branching diffusions](addition-law-for-square-root-branching-diffusions.md), and a [cutoff construction of an absorbed square-root diffusion](cutoff-construction-of-an-absorbed-square-root-diffusion.md) gives strong existence without applying the global Lipschitz theorem to the original coefficient.
