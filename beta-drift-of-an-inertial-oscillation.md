# Beta drift of an inertial oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial oscillation](inertial-oscillation.md)

On a [beta plane](beta-plane.md), the turning rate of an [inertial oscillation](inertial-oscillation.md) is larger on the poleward side of its orbit than on the equatorward side. For initial speed $V$ and $\beta V/f_0^2\ll1$, this asymmetry produces westward mean drift of magnitude $\beta V^2/(2f_0^2)$.
