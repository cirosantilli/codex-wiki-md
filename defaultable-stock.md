# Defaultable stock

↑ **Parent:** [Stock](stock.md)

A [stock](stock.md) model whose price can be killed or reduced at a [default time](default-time.md). The [zero-recovery default model](zero-recovery-default-model.md) combines continuous price dynamics before [default](credit-default.md) with a jump to zero.
