# Disconnected Feynman diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) with more than one connected component. Products of component contributions exponentiate; taking the logarithm of a Gaussian perturbation expansion removes disconnected products and keeps [connected Feynman diagrams](connected-feynman-diagram.md).
