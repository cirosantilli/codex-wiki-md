# Chaperone (protein)

↑ **Parent:** [Protein folding](protein-folding.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaperone_(protein))

A [molecular chaperone](chaperone-protein.md) is a [protein](protein-split.md) that assists other [proteins](protein-split.md) in reaching or maintaining useful arrangements, for example by limiting unwanted association during [protein folding](protein-folding.md). Assistance does not rewrite the client's [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md). In a crowded [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md), reducing [protein aggregation](protein-aggregation.md) can make successful [protein folding](protein-folding.md) more reliable. See [Cooper: molecular chaperones and folding assistance](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9843/).
