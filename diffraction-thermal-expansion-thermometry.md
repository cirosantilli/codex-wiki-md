# Diffraction thermal-expansion thermometry

↑ **Parent:** [Powder X-ray diffraction](powder-x-ray-diffraction.md)

At fixed radiation [wavelength](wavelength.md), differentiating [Bragg's law](bragg-s-law.md) for an isotropically expanding cubic specimen gives the displayed angular sensitivity, with angles in radians. Accurate temperature inference requires a calibrated lattice-parameter versus temperature curve, stable wavelength and angular zero, and separation of thermal expansion from stress or composition shifts. Counting statistics can permit centroid precision finer than the observed diffraction-line width.
