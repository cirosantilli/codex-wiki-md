# Gumbel distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Extreme value theory](extreme-value-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gumbel_distribution)

The standard [Gumbel distribution](gumbel-distribution.md) for maxima has [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $\Lambda(x)=e^{-e^{-x}}$ for every real $x$. The [maximum domain of attraction](maximum-domain-of-attraction.md) is characterized by an exponential tail ratio on the scale of a [Gumbel auxiliary function](gumbel-auxiliary-function.md).
