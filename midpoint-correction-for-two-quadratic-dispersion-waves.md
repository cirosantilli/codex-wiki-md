# Midpoint correction for two quadratic-dispersion waves

↑ **Parent:** [Dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md)

For the [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $m\omega=k^2$, take two [plane waves](plane-wave.md) with [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md) $\bar k\pm\delta k$ and corresponding [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) $\bar\omega\pm\delta\omega$. Adding their two relations proves the title identity, while subtracting gives $\delta\omega/\delta k=2\bar k/m$. Thus the exact two-tone envelope speed differs from twice the midpoint carrier [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) by a second-order bandwidth correction. In the narrow-band limit the derivative [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is exactly twice the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) on the central branch.
