# Angular-momentum estimate of a self-gravitating galactic disk

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md)

Let a disk contain fraction $f_d$ of its host mass and let its edge [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) be $f_jR_hv_h$. With halo relation $v_h^2=GM_h/R_h$ and disk geometry coefficient $\kappa=v_d^2R_d/(GM_d)$, combining centrifugal balance and the edge [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) gives the displayed estimates. The coefficient records the radial mass profile and thickness: [enclosed mass does not determine a disc rotation curve](enclosed-mass-does-not-determine-a-disc-rotation-curve.md). Treating $\kappa=1$ as a monopole estimate must not be mistaken for an exact thin-disk theorem.
