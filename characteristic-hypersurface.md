# Characteristic hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Principal symbol of a partial differential equation](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md)

A [hypersurface](hypersurface.md) $\Sigma=\{\phi=0\}$ is characteristic for a differential equation at $x\in\Sigma$ when its [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) vanishes on the conormal $d\phi(x)$:

$$
p(x,d\phi(x))=0.
$$

It is non-characteristic where this quantity is nonzero.
