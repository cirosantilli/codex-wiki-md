# Effective field theory of large-scale structure

↑ **Parent:** [Large-scale structure of the universe](large-scale-structure-of-the-universe-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effective_field_theory_of_large-scale_structure)

The effective field theory of large-scale structure represents unresolved short-scale dynamics by an effective stress tensor. Its leading deterministic correction is proportional to $k^2\delta^{(1)}$ and renormalizes ultraviolet-sensitive loop contributions.
