# Manin symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Modular symbol](modular-symbol.md)

For a finite-index modular subgroup, a Manin symbol records a coset and its associated oriented unimodular edge. Over the rationals the presentation has the relations $[g]+[gS]=0$ and $[g]+[gST]+[g(ST)^2]=0$, for inversion $S$ and translation $T$. They express reversing an edge and the boundary of a triangle. Integral computation also removes torsion caused by elliptic stabilizers. The boundary map sends the symbol to its terminal cusp minus its initial cusp; its kernel is absolute [homology](homology-split.md).
