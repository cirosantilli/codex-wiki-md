# Convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convolution)

The convolution of integrable functions on the real line is

$$
(f*g)(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(y)g(x-y)\,dy.
$$
