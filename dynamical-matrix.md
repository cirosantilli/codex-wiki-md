# Dynamical matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

The mass-weighted force-constant [matrix](matrix.md) $D=M^{-1/2}KM^{-1/2}$ for [small oscillations](small-oscillation.md). Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are squared [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md); its normalized [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) specify mass-weighted normal-mode displacements.
