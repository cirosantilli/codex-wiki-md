# Pezosiren portelli

↑ **Parent:** [Sirenia](sirenia.md)

An [Eocene](eocene.md) Jamaican sirenian retaining four substantial limbs and a strong sacroiliac connection alongside aquatic specializations. It informs the terrestrial-to-aquatic transition without being demonstrated to be a direct ancestor. [Nature 413, pp. 625–627; Pezosiren locomotor and aquatic character mosaic](the-earliest-known-fully-quadrupedal-sirenian-by-daryl-p-domning.md)
