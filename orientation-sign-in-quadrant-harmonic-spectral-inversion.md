# Orientation sign in quadrant harmonic spectral inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md)

The clockwise definitions in [quarter-plane oblique boundary spectral functions](quarter-plane-oblique-boundary-spectral-functions.md) require the minus sign in this reconstruction. Indeed integrating first in $k$ gives $i/(z-w)$, and clockwise boundary traversal has [Cauchy integral](cauchy-transform.md) $-2\pi i\,q_z(z)$. Reversing both boundary spectra or both reconstruction-ray orientations reverses this sign; mixing the two conventions gives the negative of the desired [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md).
