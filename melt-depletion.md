# Melt depletion

↑ **Parent:** [Partial melting](partial-melting.md)

Chemical modification of residual [rock](rock-geology.md) by extraction of [melt](geological-melt.md). In [mantle](planetary-mantle.md) [peridotite](peridotite.md), depletion commonly changes mineral proportions and trace-element contents, and can accompany volatile loss. Compositional buoyancy and [water](water.md) content must be assessed separately from thermal [density](density.md) changes when explaining a long-lived [craton](craton.md).
