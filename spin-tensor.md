# Spin tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Strain-rate tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_tensor)

The spin tensor is the antisymmetric part of the velocity gradient, $\boldsymbol\Omega=(\nabla\mathbf u-(\nabla\mathbf u)^T)/2$. It describes local rigid-body rotation.
