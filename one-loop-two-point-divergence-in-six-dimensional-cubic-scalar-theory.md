# One-loop two-point divergence in six-dimensional cubic scalar theory

↑ **Parent:** [Phi cubed theory](phi-cubed-theory.md)

The two-vertex cubic bubble in $d=6-\epsilon$ dimensions has the displayed amputated pole. A [Feynman parameter](feynman-parameter.md) gives mass $m^2+x(1-x)k^2$, and $\Gamma(-1+\epsilon/2)$ supplies the pole. The full propagator receives $D_0^2 I$, while its inverse receives $-I$. This sign distinguishes a two-point insertion from an inverse-propagator correction.
