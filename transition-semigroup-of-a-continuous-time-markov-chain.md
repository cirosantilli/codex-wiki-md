# Transition semigroup of a continuous-time Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

For a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md), $P_{ij}(t)=\mathbb P_i(X_t=j)$ and the matrices satisfy $P(s+t)=P(s)P(t)$. On a finite state space with [Q-matrix](transition-rate-matrix.md) $Q$, $P(t)=e^{tQ}$.
