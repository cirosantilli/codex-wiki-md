# Confinement energy

↑ **Parent:** [Confinement potential](confinement-potential.md)

The average of a [confinement potential](confinement-potential.md) against a [probability measure](probability-measure.md). A potential bounded below with at most quadratic growth has finite energy on [probability measures](probability-measure.md) with finite second [absolute moments](absolute-moment.md). A lower [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) bound $D^2V\geq\lambda I$ gives $\lambda$-[displacement convexity](displacement-convexity.md): apply [convexity](convex-function.md) of $V-\lambda|\cdot|^2/2$ along each particle segment in an optimal [transport plan](transport-plan.md), then integrate. Quadratic growth of $V$ alone does not justify two differentiations under the energy integral.
