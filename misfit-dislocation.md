# Misfit dislocation

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

A [dislocation](dislocation.md) that accommodates some [lattice mismatch](lattice-mismatch.md) at an interface. An initially coherent epitaxial layer or island can instead store elastic deformation; introducing a misfit dislocation trades some strain energy for the defect's formation energy. A projected image must be indexed before a particular spacing discrepancy is assigned to misfit strain.
