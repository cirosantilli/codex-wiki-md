# Stationary phase endpoint cancellation in a half-strip contour

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md)

For fixed $\nu$, the vertical sides of the half-strip integral of $e^{-ix\sin z+i\nu z}$ contribute $-\sin(\pi\nu)/(\pi x)+O(x^{-2})$. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) on the nonstationary horizontal pieces contributes the opposite endpoint term. After cancellation, the two stationary points give $\sqrt{2/(\pi x)}\cos(x-\nu\pi/2-\pi/4)+O(x^{-3/2})$.
