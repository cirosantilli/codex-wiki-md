# Surface gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Surface covariant derivative](surface-covariant-derivative.md)

For an embedded surface with unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) $\mathbf n$, the surface gradient is $\nabla_sf=(\mathbf I-\mathbf n\mathbf n^T)\nabla f$. It projects the ambient [gradient](gradient.md) onto the [tangent space](tangent-space.md) and is independent of how $f$ is extended normally off the surface.
