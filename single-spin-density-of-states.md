# Single-spin density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Density of states](density-of-states.md)

The [single-spin density of states](single-spin-density-of-states.md) counts momentum states for one spin species. For degenerate up and down species, the spin-summed [density of states](density-of-states.md) is twice this value. Specify separately whether the density is per unit volume or extensive. In the [BCS gap equation](bcs-gap-equation.md), the momentum sum labels opposite-spin pairs once and therefore uses the single-spin density per volume when the interaction has normalization $V/\mathcal V$.
