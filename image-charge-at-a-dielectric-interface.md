# Image charge at a dielectric interface

↑ **Parent:** [Image charge](image-charge.md)

For a point [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $q$ in a homogeneous medium of [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\epsilon_1$ beside a plane interface with [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\epsilon_2$, its potential on the source side is reproduced by a mirrored [image charge](image-charge.md) $q'=q(\epsilon_1-\epsilon_2)/(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)$. The interface has no free surface charge; continuity of tangential [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and normal [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) fixes this coefficient.
