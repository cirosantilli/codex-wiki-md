# Effective aperture in time reversal

↑ **Parent:** [Time reversal acoustics](time-reversal-acoustics.md)

[Multiple scattering](multiple-scattering.md) can enlarge the angular diversity reaching a receiver array. Reversing the paths then makes the array act like a larger [aperture](aperture.md), improving the focus in appropriate regimes of [wave propagation in a random medium](wave-propagation-in-a-random-medium.md).
