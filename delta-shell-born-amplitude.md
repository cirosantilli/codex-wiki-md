# Delta-shell Born amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Born approximation](born-approximation.md)

For a real shell potential $V(r)=V_0\delta(r-R)$, define $g=2mV_0R/\hbar^2$ and $\operatorname{sinc}u=\sin u/u$, with value one at zero. Angular integration of its three-dimensional [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) gives $4\pi V_0R^2\operatorname{sinc}(qR)$, where the [momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md) has wave-number magnitude $q=2k\sin(\theta/2)$. Substitution in the [first Born approximation](born-approximation.md) proves the displayed [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md). It is real. Its squared [modulus](modulus.md) is a nonzero order-$V_0^2$ [differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md), whose [optical theorem](optical-theorem.md) counterpart requires the order-$V_0^2$ imaginary part of the forward amplitude from the next Born term. Thus squaring the first-order amplitude does not supply a consistently unitary second-order amplitude.
