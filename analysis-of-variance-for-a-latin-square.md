# Analysis of variance for a Latin square

↑ **Parent:** [Latin square](latin-square.md)

For one response per cell in an order-$q$ [Latin square](latin-square.md), fit an [intercept](regression-intercept.md) and additive row, column and [treatment](treatment.md) effects with sum-to-zero constraints. Each factor contributes $q-1$ [statistical degrees of freedom](statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md); the residual has $q^2-1-3(q-1)=(q-1)(q-2)$. The centered factor spaces are mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md), so sequential sums of squares do not depend on fitting order. With an additional [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) symbol factor from a [Graeco-Latin square](graeco-latin-square.md), residual [statistical degrees of freedom](statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md) become $(q-1)(q-3)$. Exact [F-tests](f-test.md) require independent equal-variance [normal](normal-distribution.md) errors; unmodeled [interactions](interaction-statistics.md) can invalidate the error term.
