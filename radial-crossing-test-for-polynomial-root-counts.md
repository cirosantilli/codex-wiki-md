# Radial crossing test for polynomial root counts

↑ **Parent:** [Argument principle](argument-principle.md)

If $p(z(t))=t$ and $p'(z)\ne0$, implicit differentiation gives $z'(t)=1/p'(z)$. At a crossing of the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), the displayed radial derivative determines whether the root enters or leaves the [disk](disk-mathematics.md) as the real parameter increases. Root counts are constant between such boundary crossings, including multiplicities. At the crossing parameter itself, boundary roots are excluded from an open-disk count. This complements the [winding number](winding-number.md) form of the [argument principle](argument-principle.md) and handles a self-intersecting [image](image-of-a-function.md) [contour](complex-integration-contour.md) without guessing its enclosed regions.
