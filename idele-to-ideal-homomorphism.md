# Idele-to-ideal homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Idele group](idele-group.md)

The map from an [idele](idele.md) to its finite-place [fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md) is surjective, has kernel the product of the infinite multiplicative groups and finite-place unit groups, and sends diagonal elements to [principal fractional ideals](principal-fractional-ideal.md). It realizes the [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md) as the corresponding quotient of the [idele group](idele-group.md).
