# Global unit of a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md)

A [global unit](global-unit-of-a-number-field.md) and its inverse are algebraic integers in the number [field](field.md). Equivalently its valuation is zero at every nonarchimedean prime. [Global units](global-unit-of-a-number-field.md) embed in [local unit](local-unit-of-a-local-field.md) groups; their p-adic closures can be smaller than the full local groups. This discrepancy is measured by the [class-field unit sequence](class-field-unit-sequence.md).
