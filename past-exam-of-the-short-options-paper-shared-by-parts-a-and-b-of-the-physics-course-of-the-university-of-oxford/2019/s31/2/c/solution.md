<h1 id="s31/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At a steady state in pseudo-time, $\partial_\tau\Phi=0$ and the evolution becomes $\Phi_{xx}=4\pi G\rho$, the desired [Poisson equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md). Deviations from a steady solution obey a [diffusion equation](../../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md), so nonconstant error modes decay. This turns an elliptic boundary-value solve into repeated relaxation steps using familiar time-evolution methods. Boundary data and source compatibility still matter; pseudo-time does not remove a nonexistent steady solution.
