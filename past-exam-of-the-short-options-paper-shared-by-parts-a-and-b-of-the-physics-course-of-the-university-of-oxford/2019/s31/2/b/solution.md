↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a second-order scalar [partial differential equation](../../../../../partial-differential-equation-split.md), classification refers to its principal second-derivative form. An [elliptic partial differential equation](../../../../../elliptic-partial-differential-equation.md) has a definite principal form, exemplified by [Poisson's equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md); it typically determines a spatial field from boundary data. A [parabolic partial differential equation](../../../../../parabolic-partial-differential-equation.md) has a distinguished first-order evolution direction and diffusive second spatial derivatives, exemplified by $u_t=D u_{xx}$. Its initial data relax or spread with time. Thus the pseudo-time equation is parabolic, while its steady-state spatial equation is elliptic.
