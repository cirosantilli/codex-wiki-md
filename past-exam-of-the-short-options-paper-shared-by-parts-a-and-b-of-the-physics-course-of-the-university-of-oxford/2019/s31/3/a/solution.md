<h1 id="s31/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $y^{(0)}=y$ and $y^{(1)}=\dot y$. Then

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dt}\begin{pmatrix}y^{(0)}\\y^{(1)}\end{pmatrix}
=\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-k^2&-2\gamma\end{pmatrix}}_{M}
\begin{pmatrix}y^{(0)}\\y^{(1)}\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The second row is the original [damped harmonic oscillator](../../../../../damped-harmonic-oscillator.md) equation and the first row defines the auxiliary [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md). This converts a second-order [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) into two first-order equations without changing the required two initial values.
