↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A numerical evolution has [numerical stability](../../../../../stability-of-a-numerical-method.md) if small perturbations in initial data, forcing or arithmetic are not amplified without control over the time interval of interest. For this constant-coefficient decaying [linear system](../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md), an absolute-stability test requires the homogeneous numerical amplification factors to have magnitude at most one, with no growing Jordan-block contribution on the unit circle. Stability is distinct from accuracy: a strongly overdamped numerical solution can be stable while representing the physical [oscillation](../../../../../oscillation.md) poorly.
