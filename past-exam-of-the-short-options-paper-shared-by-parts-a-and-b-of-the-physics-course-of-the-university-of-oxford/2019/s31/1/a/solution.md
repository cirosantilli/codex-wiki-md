↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Round-off error](../../../../../round-off-error.md) arises from representing numbers and carrying out arithmetic at finite precision: storing $1/3$ or subtracting nearly equal numbers can lose information even when the mathematical algorithm is exact. [Truncation error](../../../../../truncation-error.md) arises from replacing a continuous or infinite mathematical operation by a finite approximation, such as discarding higher [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) terms in a [finite difference](../../../../../finite-difference-split.md). Refining a grid usually reduces [truncation error](../../../../../truncation-error.md) but may increase accumulated [round-off error](../../../../../round-off-error.md); the two have different origins.
