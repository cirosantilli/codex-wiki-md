<h1 id="s25/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

$F$ is the imposed [radiative forcing](../../../../../radiative-forcing.md); $-\lambda T_s$ is the restoring change in outgoing radiation; and $-\gamma(T_s-T_d)$ transfers [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) from the surface layer to the deep ocean, whose equal and opposite gain appears in its own [energy balance](../../../../../energy-balance.md). The additional $-\lambda'(T_s-T_d)$ is a radiative adjustment correlated with the surface–deep-ocean temperature contrast, representing a pattern-dependent effective feedback rather than another conserved interlayer [heat flux](../../../../../heat-flux-density.md).

For constant forcing, final [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) has $T_s=T_d=F/\lambda$. During transient warming $T_d<T_s$, so the radiative response inferred from a recent global [energy budget](../../../../../energy-budget.md) is $\lambda_{\rm eff}=\lambda+\lambda'(1-T_d/T_s)>\lambda$. If that response were mistaken for the final feedback, the inferred [equilibrium climate sensitivity](../../../../../equilibrium-climate-sensitivity.md) would be too small. Summing the two equations makes the distinction explicit: $d(C_sT_s+C_dT_d)/dt=F-\lambda T_s-\lambda'(T_s-T_d)$.
