<h1 id="s25/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Downward-from-space [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) at wavelength $\lambda$ is

$$
\boxed{\tau_\lambda(z)=\int_z^\infty\kappa_\lambda(z')\rho(z')\,dz',}
$$

where $\kappa_\lambda$ is the mass [absorption coefficient](../../../../../absorption-coefficient.md). A nearly isothermal, [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), compositionally well-mixed atmosphere with height-independent $\kappa_\lambda$ has $\rho\propto e^{-z/H}$ and $\tau\propto e^{-z/H_\tau}$ with $H_\tau=H=R_aT/g$. Thus [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), mean molecular [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) set the basic [scale height](../../../../../scale-height.md). Changes in the absorber mixing ratio, [pressure broadening](../../../../../pressure-broadening.md), temperature dependence of line strengths, collision-induced absorption and departures from [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) modify it. For example if local absorption scales as $\rho^2$ in an isothermal atmosphere, $H_\tau=H/2$.
