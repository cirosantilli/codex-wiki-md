<h1 id="s10/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Define the positive mass [collision stopping power](../../../../../collision-stopping-power.md) as $S_{\rm col}/\rho=-(1/\rho)dT/dx$. For a projectile of charge $ze$, mass $M\gg m_e$, speed $v=\beta c$ and [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\Gamma=(1-\beta^2)^{-1/2}$, the [Bethe stopping formula](../../../../../bethe-stopping-formula.md) can be written

$$
\boxed{\frac{S_{\rm col}}\rho=4\pi N_A r_e^2m_ec^2\frac{Z}{A}\frac{z^2}{\beta^2}\left[\frac12\ln\!\left(\frac{2m_ec^2\beta^2\Gamma^2T_{\max}}{I^2}\right)-\beta^2-\frac\delta2-\frac CZ\right].}
$$

Here $Z$ is the absorber [atomic number](../../../../../atomic-number.md), $A$ its molar [mass](../../../../../mass.md), $I$ its mean excitation [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), $r_e=e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0m_ec^2)$, and $N_A$ the [Avogadro constant](../../../../../avogadro-constant.md). Also

$$
T_{\max}=\frac{2m_ec^2\beta^2\Gamma^2}{1+2\Gamma m_e/M+(m_e/M)^2}.
$$

The optional $\delta$ and $C/Z$ represent density and shell corrections. For a very heavy projectile $T_{\max}\simeq2m_ec^2\beta^2\Gamma^2$, and the bracket reduces to $\ln(2m_ec^2\beta^2\Gamma^2/I)-\beta^2$ when those corrections are neglected. If $T$ denotes remaining projectile [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), the printed $dT/(\rho dx)$ is negative; the conventional positive [stopping power](../../../../../stopping-power.md) has the minus sign above.[Section 3.5, use of Bethe theory at high energies and measured low-energy stopping powers](../../../../../pstar-and-astar-database-methods-by-nist.md)
