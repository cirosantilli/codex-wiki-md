<h1 id="s10/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Start with a fast massive projectile following an approximately straight path. Its time-dependent [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) perturbs bound absorber [electrons](../../../../../electron.md). At large impact parameter, treating the perturbation to first order and summing excitation and [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) transition probabilities gives an [energy transfer](../../../../../energy-transfer.md) weighted by the absorber's oscillator strengths. The oscillator-strength sum rule reduces the leading coefficient to the number of target [electrons](../../../../../electron.md), while their logarithmically weighted excitation energies define $I$.

The classical impulse picture explains the logarithm: a transverse impulse scales as $1/(bv)$, so transferred [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) scales as $1/(b^2v^2)$; multiplying by the number of encountered [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) in $2\pi b\,db\,dx$ gives $db/b$. Quantum [scattering](../../../../../scattering.md) replaces ad hoc short- and long-distance cutoffs by recoil kinematics and bound-state excitation. Relativistic projectile kinematics supplies $T_{\max}$ and the $-\beta^2$ term. Summation over many independent encounters gives the mean [stopping power](../../../../../stopping-power.md), rather than the [energy-loss straggling](../../../../../energy-loss-straggling.md) of individual tracks.

The leading approximation assumes $M\gg m_e$, a sufficiently fast projectile for perturbation theory, a largely fixed projectile charge, and weak correlations between collisions. At low [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), electron binding, charge exchange and [nuclear stopping](../../../../../nuclear-stopping.md) become important; at high [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), polarization screens distant interactions and gives the density correction. The projectile's [bremsstrahlung](../../../../../bremsstrahlung.md) is suppressed by its large [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and is excluded from the [collision stopping power](../../../../../collision-stopping-power.md).
