<h1 id="s10/1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

After thermalization, approximate the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) as initially at rest in their [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). Their total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $2m_ec^2$ and total [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) is zero. Two [photons](../../../../../photon.md) must therefore have equal [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) and opposite [momenta](../../../../../momentum-split.md), since $|\mathbf p_\gamma|=E_\gamma/c$. [Conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
2E_\gamma=2m_ec^2,\qquad \boxed{E_\gamma=m_ec^2=511\,\mathrm{keV}.}
$$

Residual pair [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) and electron binding cause small departures from exact collinearity and exact photon [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md); the standard [PET](../../../../../photoinduced-electron-transfer.md) calculation neglects them.
