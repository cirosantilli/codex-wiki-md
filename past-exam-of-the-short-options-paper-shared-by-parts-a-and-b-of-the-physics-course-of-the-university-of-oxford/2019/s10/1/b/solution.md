A diagnostic example is [technetium-99m methylene diphosphonate](../../../../../technetium-99m-methylene-diphosphonate.md), which accumulates at sites of bone turnover for [bone scintigraphy](../../../../../bone-scintigraphy.md). A therapeutic example is [sodium iodide iodine-131](../../../../../sodium-iodide-iodine-131.md), taken up by thyroid tissue and used for selected thyroid disease including differentiated thyroid cancer.

For diagnosis, an escaping [gamma ray](../../../../../gamma-ray.md) or a [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) followed by annihilation [photons](../../../../../photon.md) allows external detection, with an appropriate short [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md) and limited deposited [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). For molecular [radiotherapy](../../../../../radiation-therapy.md), short-range charged emissions such as [beta particles](../../../../../beta-particle.md), [alpha particles](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) or [Auger electrons](../../../../../auger-electron.md) deposit substantial local [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) in the targeted tissue. [Iodine-131](../../../../../iodine-131.md) emits therapeutic [beta particles](../../../../../beta-particle.md) as well as imageable [gamma rays](../../../../../gamma-ray.md), so these categories are functional rather than mutually exclusive isotope classes.
