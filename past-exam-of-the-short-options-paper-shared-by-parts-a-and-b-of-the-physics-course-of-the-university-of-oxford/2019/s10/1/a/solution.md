<h1 id="s10/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

After injection, the [radiopharmaceutical](../../../../../radiopharmaceutical.md) circulates and accumulates according to its biological targeting and clearance. [Technetium-99m](../../../../../technetium-99m.md) undergoes an isomeric [nuclear transition](../../../../../nuclear-transition.md), producing approximately $140\,\mathrm{keV}$ [gamma rays](../../../../../gamma-ray.md); its roughly six-hour [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md) permits useful counting with limited persistence. Some [photons](../../../../../photon.md) undergo absorption or [Compton scattering](../../../../../compton-scattering.md) in the patient. A lead [collimator](../../../../../collimator.md) in the [gamma camera](../../../../../gamma-camera.md) accepts a narrow range of directions so the accepted event retains positional information.

An accepted [gamma ray](../../../../../gamma-ray.md) deposits energy, commonly in a NaI(Tl) [scintillator](../../../../../scintillator.md), by [photoelectric effect](../../../../../photoelectric-effect.md) or [Compton scattering](../../../../../compton-scattering.md). The resulting visible [particle scintillation](../../../../../scintillation-physics.md) light is collected by [photomultipliers](../../../../../photomultiplier.md). Their relative pulse sizes determine an event position and their summed pulse estimates its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). A [pulse-height analyser](../../../../../pulse-height-analyser.md) accepts an energy window around the [photopeak](../../../../../photopeak.md) to reject many scattered events. Position and uniformity corrections are applied; accepted events increment a two-dimensional pixel histogram. After acquisition the computer displays this count map as a planar projection of tracer activity. Finite counting statistics, [attenuation](../../../../../attenuation.md), [scattering](../../../../../scattering.md) and the [collimator](../../../../../collimator.md) limit the image's quantitative accuracy and [spatial resolution](../../../../../spatial-resolution.md).[Gamma-camera detection, technetium-99m photon energy and half-life](../../../../../bio-medical-applications-of-jefferson-lab-s-nuclear-physics-detector-technology-by-drew-weisenberger.md)
