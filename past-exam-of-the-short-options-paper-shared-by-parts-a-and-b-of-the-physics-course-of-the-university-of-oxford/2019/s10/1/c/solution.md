[Single-photon emission computed tomography](../../../../../single-photon-emission-computed-tomography.md) detects individual [gamma rays](../../../../../gamma-ray.md) from a tracer. Mechanically collimated [gamma cameras](../../../../../gamma-camera.md) acquire projections from multiple angles for [tomography](../../../../../tomography.md). The [collimator](../../../../../collimator.md) discards most emitted [photons](../../../../../photon.md), creating a strong sensitivity–[spatial resolution](../../../../../spatial-resolution.md) trade-off.

[Positron emission tomography](../../../../../positron-emission-tomography.md) uses a [Positron](../../../../../positron.md)-emitting tracer. The emitted [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) travels a short distance before [electron-positron annihilation](../../../../../electron-positron-annihilation.md) produces an approximately back-to-back photon pair. Coincident detection in a detector ring defines a [line of response](../../../../../line-of-response.md), giving electronic collimation and greater detection efficiency; [time-of-flight PET](../../../../../time-of-flight-pet.md) further constrains position along that line. The [positron range](../../../../../positron-range.md), non-collinearity, detector size, scatter and accidental coincidences limit [spatial resolution](../../../../../spatial-resolution.md) and quantification. In both hybrids, [computed tomography](../../../../../computed-tomography.md) supplies anatomy and an [attenuation correction](../../../../../attenuation-correction.md) map, with attention to registration and respiratory motion.[Sections describing SPECT, PET and hybrid CT imaging](../../../../../nuclear-medicine-by-nibib.md)
