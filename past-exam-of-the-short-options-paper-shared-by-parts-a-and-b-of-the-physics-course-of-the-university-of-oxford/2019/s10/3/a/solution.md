↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Photon radiotherapy](../../../../../photon-radiotherapy.md) uses indirectly ionizing [photons](../../../../../photon.md). Their [Compton scattering](../../../../../compton-scattering.md), [photoelectric effect](../../../../../photoelectric-effect.md) and, at higher [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), [pair production](../../../../../pair-production.md) create charged particles that deposit [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). A megavoltage beam has a dose-build-up region followed by a gradual decline and substantial exit dose. Multiple shaped beams or [intensity-modulated radiotherapy](../../../../../intensity-modulated-radiotherapy.md) distribute this dose around a target.

[Proton therapy](../../../../../proton-therapy.md) uses directly ionizing charged particles with a finite [particle range](../../../../../particle-range.md) and a [Bragg peak](../../../../../bragg-peak.md). Tuning beam [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) controls depth and scanning controls lateral delivery; multiple energies cover a finite target thickness. Reduced distal and integral normal-tissue dose is a potential advantage. Limitations include [range uncertainty](../../../../../range-uncertainty.md), [Multiple Coulomb scattering](../../../../../multiple-coulomb-scattering.md), nuclear secondary production, motion-dependent delivery, and a variable [relative biological effectiveness](../../../../../relative-biological-effectiveness.md), particularly near the distal edge. A physical dose advantage must be assessed against target coverage and clinically relevant toxicity; it does not by itself establish superior tumour control.
