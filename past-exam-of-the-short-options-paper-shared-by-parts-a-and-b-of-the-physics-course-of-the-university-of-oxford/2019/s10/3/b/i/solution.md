↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A localized paediatric intracranial tumour is a representative indication where [proton therapy](../../../../../../proton-therapy.md) can reduce unnecessary [absorbed dose](../../../../../../absorbed-dose.md) to the developing brain and other normal tissue. The finite [particle range](../../../../../../particle-range.md) can reduce exit and integral dose compared with many [photon radiotherapy](../../../../../../photon-radiotherapy.md) plans, which may reduce late effects and secondary cancer risk over a long expected survival. It remains necessary to maintain target coverage, account for changing anatomy and keep uncertain distal-edge [relative biological effectiveness](../../../../../../relative-biological-effectiveness.md) away from sensitive structures. Travel, cost and daily positioning are practical disadvantages. The relevant comparison is a robust individual [treatment plan](../../../../../../treatment-plan.md) and clinical endpoint, rather than the label “proton” alone.[Paediatric treatment considerations and sparing of developing normal tissues](../../../../../../acr-ars-practice-parameter-for-proton-beam-therapy.md)
