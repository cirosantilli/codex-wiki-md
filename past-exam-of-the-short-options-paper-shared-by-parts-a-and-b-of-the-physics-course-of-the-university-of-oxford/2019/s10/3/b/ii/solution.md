<h1 id="s10/3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A strongly moving lung target is a relative contraindication to a particular [proton therapy](../../../../../../proton-therapy.md) plan if respiratory motion and changing air–tissue paths make robust target coverage and normal-tissue constraints unattainable. A changing water-equivalent path changes the [particle range](../../../../../../particle-range.md); pencil-beam scanning can also interact with respiratory motion. A [photon radiotherapy](../../../../../../photon-radiotherapy.md) plan may then offer more reliable coverage despite its greater exit dose. This is a conditional planning contraindication, not a blanket claim that lung cancer cannot be treated with [protons](../../../../../../proton.md): gating, breath-hold, rescanning and adaptive planning can change the decision. If a robust comparative plan shows no meaningful benefit, the additional cost and access burden also weigh against the proton plan.[Report 290, dose uncertainty from respiratory motion and need for site-specific planning studies](../../../../../../respiratory-motion-management-for-particle-therapy-by-aapm.md)
