<h1 id="s7/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a regular [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md), form the [canonical momenta](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) $p_i=L_{\dot q_i}$, invert for $\dot q_i(\mathbf q,\mathbf p,t)$, and take the [Legendre transform](../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) $H=\sum_ip_i\dot q_i-L$. On the upper cone let $s>0$ be distance along a generator from the apex and $\phi$ the [azimuth](../../../../../azimuth.md). Then the horizontal radius is $s\sin\theta$ and height is $s\cos\theta$, so

$$
L=\frac m2(\dot s^2+s^2\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi^2)-mgs\cos\theta,
\quad p_s=m\dot s,\quad p_\phi=ms^2\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{H=\frac{p_s^2}{2m}+\frac{p_\phi^2}{2ms^2\sin^2\theta}+mgs\cos\theta.}
$$
