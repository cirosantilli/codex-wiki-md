<h1 id="s7/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

To match the printed order $df/dt=\{H,f\}$, define the [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) with the sign convention

$$
\boxed{\{F,G\}_{\rm paper}=\sum_i\left(F_{p_i}G_{q_i}-F_{q_i}G_{p_i}\right).}
$$

[Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) imply $\dot f=\sum_i(f_{q_i}H_{p_i}-f_{p_i}H_{q_i})=\{H,f\}_{\rm paper}$. With the other common [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) convention the correct statement is instead $\dot f=\{f,H\}_{\rm standard}$. For example $H=p^2/(2m)$, $f=q$ gives $\{H,q\}_{\rm standard}=-p/m$, contradicting the printed order unless its sign convention is adopted. Since $H_t=0$ and $\{H,H\}=0$, the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is conserved. Also $\dot p_\phi=-H_\phi=0$: this [canonical momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is the axial [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md).
