<h1 id="s7/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppress primes on the angular [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) while retaining rotating components. The [canonical momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is $\partial L'/\partial\dot{\mathbf r}'=m(\dot{\mathbf r}'+\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf r')$. Varying $L'$ with respect to position and applying the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{m\ddot{\mathbf r}'=-\nabla' U'-2m\boldsymbol\Omega\times\dot{\mathbf r}'-m\boldsymbol\Omega\times(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf r')-m\dot{\boldsymbol\Omega}\times\mathbf r'.}
$$

The terms are the physical [force](../../../../../force-split.md), the velocity-dependent [Coriolis force](../../../../../coriolis-force.md), the outward [centrifugal force](../../../../../centrifugal-force.md), and the [Euler force](../../../../../euler-force.md) caused by changing angular [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md). The common origin is inertial; an accelerating origin would introduce another translational apparent [force](../../../../../force-split.md).
