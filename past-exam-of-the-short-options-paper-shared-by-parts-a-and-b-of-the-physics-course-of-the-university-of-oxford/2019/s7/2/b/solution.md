<h1 id="s7/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The inertial [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is $L=m|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2/2-U(\mathbf r,t)$. Put $\mathbf r=R(t)\mathbf r'$ and define $U'(\mathbf r',t)=U(R(t)\mathbf r',t)$. The transport identity and invariance of the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) under a [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) give

$$
\boxed{L'=\frac m2|\dot{\mathbf r}'+\boldsymbol\Omega'\times\mathbf r'|^2-U'(\mathbf r',t).}
$$

Its expansion contains the ordinary [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), $m\dot{\mathbf r}'\cdot(\boldsymbol\Omega'\times\mathbf r')$, and $m|\boldsymbol\Omega'\times\mathbf r'|^2/2$. These velocity and position terms generate the apparent forces in the rotating frame.
