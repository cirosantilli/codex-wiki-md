<h1 id="s7/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At [latitude](../../../../../geographical-latitude.md) $\ell$, the Earth's angular [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) in east–north–up coordinates is $\boldsymbol\Omega=(0,\Omega\cos\ell,\Omega\sin\ell)$. Assume a small horizontal patch, constant $\Omega$, negligible vertical [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), and a vertical constraint balancing effective [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md). Absorb the steady [centrifugal force](../../../../../centrifugal-force.md) into that effective [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md). With $f=2\Omega\sin\ell$ and physical horizontal [force](../../../../../force-split.md) per unit mass $F_x',F_y'$, the [Coriolis force](../../../../../coriolis-force.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\ddot x'=f\dot y'+F_x',\qquad\ddot y'=-f\dot x'+F_y',\quad A=D=0,\ B=f,\ C=-f.}
$$

Large-scale atmospheric and oceanic flows deflect to their right in the northern hemisphere and left in the southern hemisphere; their balance with a horizontal [pressure gradient](../../../../../pressure-gradient.md) produces [geostrophic flow](../../../../../geostrophic-flow.md). A [Foucault pendulum](../../../../../foucault-pendulum.md) has an oscillation plane that precesses relative to the ground at angular rate $-\Omega\sin\ell$. Both effects vanish at the equator within this horizontal approximation.
