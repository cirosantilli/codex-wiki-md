<h1 id="s7/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The appropriate invariance is invariance of the [action](../../../../../action.md) element $L\,dt$, including the change of the time measure. Under $\delta t=\lambda\phi$ and $\delta q_i=\lambda\psi_i$, [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) of $q_i'(t')$ gives $\delta\dot q_i=\lambda(D_t\psi_i-\dot q_iD_t\phi)$. Therefore

$$
\frac{\delta(L\,dt)}{\lambda\,dt}=L_t\phi+\sum_iL_{q_i}\psi_i+\sum_i p_i(D_t\psi_i-\dot q_iD_t\phi)+L D_t\phi.
$$

Using the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) and $D_tE=-L_t$, this becomes

$$
D_t\left[\sum_i p_i\psi_i-E\phi\right].
$$

An [action symmetry](../../../../../action-symmetry.md) therefore yields the [Noether charge](../../../../../noether-charge.md)

$$
\boxed{Q=\sum_i p_i\psi_i+\left(L-\sum_i p_i\dot q_i\right)\phi,\qquad D_tQ=0.}
$$

For $\phi=0$ this reduces to the usual spatial form of [Noether's theorem](../../../../../noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md). The same on-shell calculation permits velocity-dependent generators when they define a legitimate variational [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md). Merely preserving the numerical value of $L$ is insufficient for transformations of time: the [Jacobian matrix and determinant](../../../../../jacobian-matrix-and-determinant.md) $dt'/dt$ matters. If the [action](../../../../../action.md) changes by a boundary term $\lambda D_tB$, the conserved quantity is $Q-B$.
