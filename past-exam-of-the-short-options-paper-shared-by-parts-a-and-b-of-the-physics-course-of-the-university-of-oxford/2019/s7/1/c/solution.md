<h1 id="s7/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [central potential](../../../../../central-potential.md) use $L=m|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2/2-V(|\mathbf r|)$. An infinitesimal [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) about any constant unit [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf n$ has $\phi=0$ and $\boldsymbol\psi=\mathbf n\times\mathbf r$. [Noether's theorem](../../../../../noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) gives $Q=m\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot(\mathbf n\times\mathbf r)=\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf L$, where $\mathbf L=\mathbf r\times m\dot{\mathbf r}$. Since $\mathbf n$ is arbitrary, every component of [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is conserved. A [time-translation symmetry](../../../../../time-translation-symmetry.md) has $\phi=1$, $\boldsymbol\psi=0$, giving $Q=-E$ and

$$
\boxed{\mathbf L=\mathbf r\times m\dot{\mathbf r},\qquad E=\frac12m|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2+V(r)}
$$

as conserved quantities.
