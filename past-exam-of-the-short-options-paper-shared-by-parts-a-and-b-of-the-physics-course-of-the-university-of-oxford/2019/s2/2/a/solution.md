<h1 id="s2/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A thin spherical shell has outward [pressure force](../../../../../pressure-force.md) $-4\pi r^2(dP/dr)dr$ and inward [gravitational force](../../../../../gravitational-force.md) $GM(r)\,4\pi r^2\rho(r)dr/r^2$. [Hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) requires

$$
\boxed{\frac{dP}{dr}=-\frac{GM(r)\rho(r)}{r^2},\qquad \frac{dM}{dr}=4\pi r^2\rho(r).}
$$

A uniform-[density](../../../../../density.md) [solar model](../../../../../solar-model.md) gives $M(r)=M_\odot(r/R_\odot)^3$, $\bar\rho=3M_\odot/(4\pi R_\odot^3)$, and after [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) from $R_\odot$ to zero,

$$
P_c=\frac{3GM_\odot^2}{8\pi R_\odot^4}=1.34\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{Pa},\qquad
T_c=\frac{P_c\mu m_p}{\bar\rho k_B}=6.9\times10^6\,\mathrm K\quad(\mu=0.6).
$$

Thus the appropriate orders of magnitude are $10^{14}$–$10^{16}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and $10^7\,\mathrm K$. The uniform-[density](../../../../../density.md) approximation underestimates the actual [central pressure](../../../../../central-pressure.md) because the Sun is centrally concentrated. [Radiation pressure](../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) is neglected in applying the [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md).
