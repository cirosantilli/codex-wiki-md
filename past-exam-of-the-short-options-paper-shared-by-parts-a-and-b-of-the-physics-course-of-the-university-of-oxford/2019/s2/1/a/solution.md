↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Kepler's third law](../../../../../kepler-s-third-law.md) for two [point masses](../../../../../point-mass.md) in a [Newtonian gravitational field](../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-field.md) is

$$
\boxed{P^2=\frac{4\pi^2a^3}{G(M_1+M_2)}.}
$$

Here $a$ is the [semimajor axis](../../../../../semi-major-axis.md) of their relative [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), $P$ the [orbital period](../../../../../orbital-period.md) and $G$ the [gravitational constant](../../../../../gravitational-constant.md). Equivalently $(P/\mathrm{yr})^2=(a/\mathrm{AU})^3/[(M_1+M_2)/M_\odot]$, to the accuracy with which these astronomical units normalize the [solar mass](../../../../../solar-mass.md).
