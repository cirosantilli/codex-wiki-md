<h1 id="s2/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Adopt the approximate [mass-luminosity relation](../../../../../mass-luminosity-relation.md) $L/L_\odot\simeq(M/M_\odot)^3$ and equal available fuel fractions. The [main-sequence lifetime](../../../../../main-sequence-lifetime.md) then scales as $M/L$, giving

$$
\boxed{t_1\simeq1.54\times10^8\,\mathrm{yr},\qquad t_2\simeq6.16\times10^8\,\mathrm{yr}.}
$$

Using an exponent $3.5$ instead gives about $5.4\times10^7$ and $3.1\times10^8$ years; these are model-dependent estimates. At $r=3r_s=6GM/c^2$ the [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md) released from infinity is $GMm/r=mc^2/6$. Half remains as [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) in a circular [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), so the radiatable [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) is $GMm/(2r)$:

$$
\boxed{\eta_{\rm Newtonian}=\frac1{12}\simeq8.3\%.}
$$

Using the whole [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) loss would give $1/6$, which does not subtract the final [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). A fully relativistic calculation for a nonrotating [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) gives $1-\sqrt{8/9}\simeq5.7\%$. When the surviving star exhausts [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md), it expands off the [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md) and can fill its [Roche lobe](../../../../../roche-lobe.md). [Binary mass transfer](../../../../../binary-mass-transfer.md) to the [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) can form an [accretion disk](../../../../../accretion-disk.md) and an [X-ray binary](../../../../../x-ray-binary.md); unstable [binary mass transfer](../../../../../binary-mass-transfer.md) may instead produce a [common envelope](../../../../../common-envelope.md). This is conditional on the [binary star](../../../../../binary-star.md) remaining bound after the first remnant forms. The supplied [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) endpoint is an assumption, not a universal outcome for an isolated $8M_\odot$ star.
