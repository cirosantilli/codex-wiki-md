<h1 id="s1/1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose a continuous [branch of a multivalued function](../../../../../branch-of-a-multivalued-function.md) of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) on a simply connected region avoiding zero. Then

$$
g=e^{-\theta}e^{i\ln r}=e^{i\operatorname{Log}z},\qquad
\boxed{g'(z)=\frac{i}{z}g(z).}
$$

Alternatively $u_r=-e^{-\theta}\sin(\ln r)/r=v_\theta/r$ and $v_r=e^{-\theta}\cos(\ln r)/r=-u_\theta/r$, verifying the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md). The [holomorphy](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) is local to a chosen [branch of a multivalued function](../../../../../branch-of-a-multivalued-function.md): increasing $\theta$ by $2\pi$ multiplies $g$ by $e^{-2\pi}$, so this is not a single-valued [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the whole punctured plane.
