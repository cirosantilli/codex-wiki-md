<h1 id="s1/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) in [polar coordinates](../../../../../polar-coordinates.md) gives $u_r=u_x\cos\theta+u_y\sin\theta$ and $u_\theta=r(-u_x\sin\theta+u_y\cos\theta)$, with analogous expressions for $v$. Substituting the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) gives

$$
\boxed{ru_r=v_\theta,\qquad rv_r=-u_\theta,\quad r>0.}
$$

The restriction excludes the coordinate singularity at the origin.
