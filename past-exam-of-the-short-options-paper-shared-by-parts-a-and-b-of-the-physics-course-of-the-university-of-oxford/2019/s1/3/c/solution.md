<h1 id="s1/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The square root produces [branch points](../../../../../branch-point.md) at $z=\pm1$, with local behaviour proportional to $(z\mp1)^{-1/2}$. One [branch cut](../../../../../branch-cut.md) can join them along $[-1,1]$. Another choice uses the two rays $(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty)$. Each choice prevents a loop from enclosing just one [branch point](../../../../../branch-point.md) within a single-valued domain. For the finite-segment choice fix $\sqrt{z^2-1}\sim z$ at infinity. Infinity is then a [pole](../../../../../pole.md) of order three, not a [branch point](../../../../../branch-point.md), since

$$
f(z)=z^3+\frac12z+\frac{3}{8z}+O(z^{-3}).
$$
