Expanding the [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md) produces the [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md)

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{n^k}{k!}z^{n-k},\qquad 0<|z|<\infty.}
$$

In particular its [residue](../../../../../residue.md) is $a_{-1}=n^{n+1}/(n+1)!$.
