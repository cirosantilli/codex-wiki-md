<h1 id="s1/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a positively oriented circle of any radius $R>0$, the [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\oint_{|z|=R}f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\frac{n^{n+1}}{(n+1)!}.}
$$

Only the $z^{-1}$ coefficient in the [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) contributes. Reversing the [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) changes the sign.
