<h1 id="s14/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Doubt was reasonable if it concerned the physical cause or unlimited extrapolation of [Newton's law of universal gravitation](../../../../newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md); wholesale dismissal of the quantitative evidence was harder to justify. Early eighteenth-century investigators had to distinguish the success of an inverse-square [force law](../../../../force-law.md) in explaining observations from the claim that every material body directly attracts every other one without a mediating mechanism.

The strongest case for [Isaac Newton](../../../../isaac-newton.md)'s framework was its unification of phenomena previously treated separately. [Kepler's third law](../../../../kepler-s-third-law.md) and nearly [elliptic orbits](../../../../elliptic-orbit.md) could be connected to an inverse-square central [force](../../../../force-split.md). Comparing terrestrial [gravity](../../../../gravity.md) with the [centripetal acceleration](../../../../centripetal-acceleration.md) of the Moon supported continuity between falling bodies and celestial [motion](../../../../motion.md). The [Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica by Isaac Newton](../../../../philosophiae-naturalis-principia-mathematica-by-isaac-newton.md) also developed treatments of interacting bodies, [tides](../../../../tide.md) and the Earth's figure. These were substantive tests and organized a research programme capable of producing further quantitative comparisons.[Book III, propositions on planetary forces, lunar gravity and universal attraction](../../../../philosophiae-naturalis-principia-mathematica-by-isaac-newton.md)

There were nevertheless rational reservations. A law proportional to $m_1m_2/r^2$ inferred from accessible bodies and distances did not logically establish unrestricted universality. Individual celestial predictions involved uncertain observations and approximations to difficult many-body [classical mechanics](../../../../classical-mechanics-split.md). The Moon's motion and the Earth's shape were demanding tests whose successful treatment was not complete at the beginning of the century. A dissenter could question auxiliary assumptions, numerical accuracy or the inference from special cases without denying every success.

A deeper objection concerned explanation. In traditions requiring contact mechanisms, unexplained attraction across empty space appeared to reintroduce an occult quality. [René Descartes](../../../../rene-descartes.md)'s vortices supplied a mechanical alternative, though they faced their own serious dynamical difficulties. [Newton](../../../../newton-unit.md) himself separated a mathematically established pattern from a claimed cause of [gravity](../../../../gravity.md); the 1713 [General Scholium by Isaac Newton](../../../../general-scholium-by-isaac-newton.md) declined to invent an unsupported physical mechanism. Treating every criticism of action at a distance as a failure to understand evidence would therefore misrepresent the available positions.[1713 discussion of gravitational cause and the evidential status of hypotheses](../../../../general-scholium-by-isaac-newton.md)

Later confirmations cannot be counted as evidence already possessed by an early-eighteenth-century critic. Subsequent geodetic measurements, improved lunar theory and successful comet predictions helped shift the balance, but their timing matters. Nor did retaining a mechanical alternative remain equally reasonable after every failed prediction: responsible doubt required comparative assessment and willingness to revise it. The balanced conclusion is that selective, explicit scepticism about universality or cause was defensible, while the breadth of [Newtonian mechanics](../../../../newtonian-mechanics-split.md)' quantitative achievements already imposed a substantial explanatory burden on rival accounts.
