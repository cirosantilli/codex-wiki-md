<h1 id="s16/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

One mole of original atoms contains $(1-\zeta)$ moles of neutral atoms, $\zeta$ moles of [protons](../../../../../proton.md) and $\zeta$ moles of [electrons](../../../../../electron.md). All are monatomic translational species, so their combined [thermal energy](../../../../../thermal-energy.md) is $\frac32RT(1+\zeta)$. Taking the neutral ground state as zero [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md), [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) stores $\zeta N_AE_I$. Thus

$$
\boxed{U=\frac32RT(1+\zeta)+\zeta N_AE_I=\frac32RT(1+\zeta)+\zeta\frac{E_I}{k_BT}RT.}
$$

Radiation, excited states and nonideal interactions have been excluded consistently with the elementary [Saha ionization equation](../../../../../saha-ionization-equation.md).
