<h1 id="s16/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At fixed [volume](../../../../../volume.md), logarithmic [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) of the [Saha ionization equation](../../../../../saha-ionization-equation.md) gives

$$
\left(\frac2\zeta+\frac1{1-\zeta}\right)\zeta'=\frac1T\left(\frac32+\frac{E_I}{k_BT}\right),\quad
T\zeta'=\frac{\zeta(1-\zeta)}{2-\zeta}\left(\frac32+\frac{E_I}{k_BT}\right).
$$

[Differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) the [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) then yields

$$
\boxed{C_V=\frac32R(1+\zeta)+R\frac{\zeta(1-\zeta)}{2-\zeta}\left(\frac32+\frac{E_I}{k_BT}\right)^2.}
$$

At low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $\zeta\to0$ and $C_V\to3R/2$. At high nonrelativistic [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) the gas is fully ionized, $\zeta\to1$, and $C_V\to3R$. The large intermediate excess reflects [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) spent increasing [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md), as well as heating and creating additional translational particles. “Very high” here remains below regimes where relativistic, radiative or pair-creation contributions invalidate the model.
