<h1 id="s16/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Population can move between the levels by electron-impact excitation and de-excitation, spontaneous emission, radiative absorption and stimulated emission. Other collision partners and radiative cascades can matter in a fuller atom. In the elementary [coronal approximation](../../../../../coronal-approximation.md), the plasma is dilute, optically thin, steady, and weakly illuminated at the transition frequency. Electron impacts predominantly excite the lower level, while spontaneous emission predominantly empties the upper level; collisional de-excitation and photon trapping are negligible. The population balance is

$$
n_eC_{12}N_1=A_{21}N_2,\qquad\boxed{\frac{N_2}{N_1}=\frac{n_eC_{12}}{A_{21}}.}
$$

A key condition is $n_eC_{21}\ll A_{21}$, together with insignificant external pumping and competing channels. A Maxwellian electron [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md) lets the tabulated rate coefficient be expressed using one [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).
