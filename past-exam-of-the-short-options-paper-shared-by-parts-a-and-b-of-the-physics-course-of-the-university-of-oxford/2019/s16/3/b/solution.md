<h1 id="s16/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [quasineutrality](../../../../../quasineutrality.md), $n_e=Zn_i$. Multiplying the supplied collision time by the electron [plasma frequency](../../../../../plasma-frequency.md) and using the preceding definition gives

$$
\boxed{\omega_p\tau_{ei}=\frac{9.6}{Z\ln\Lambda}N_D.}
$$

Thus $\tau_{ei}\gg2\pi/\omega_p$ requires $N_D\gg(2\pi/9.6)Z\ln\Lambda$; for ordinary $Z$ and $\ln\Lambda\sim1$–$30$, this is a large [plasma parameter](../../../../../plasma-parameter.md). Many particles then collectively screen a charge, fluctuations in a [Debye sphere](../../../../../debye-sphere.md) are relatively small, and mean-field [electrostatics](../../../../../electrostatics-split.md) descriptions become meaningful. A large [plasma parameter](../../../../../plasma-parameter.md) is also associated with weak [Coulomb coupling](../../../../../coulomb-coupling.md), well-separated screening and close-collision scales, and collective [plasma oscillations](../../../../../plasma-oscillation.md) surviving many oscillations before collisionally relaxing. Roughly, relative counting fluctuations scale as $N_D^{-1/2}$ and the ratio of interaction to thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) decreases as $N_D$ increases. Small $N_D$ signals that strong correlations can invalidate the simple weakly coupled [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) description.
