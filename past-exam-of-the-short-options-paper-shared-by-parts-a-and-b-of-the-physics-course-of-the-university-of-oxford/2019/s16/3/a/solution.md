<h1 id="s16/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Linearize the electron [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md), $n_e(\phi)\simeq n_{e0}[1+e\phi/(k_BT)]$, about a uniform fixed-ion background. [Poisson's equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md) gives $\nabla^2\phi=\phi/\lambda_D^2$, where the electron [Debye length](../../../../../debye-length.md) is

$$
\lambda_D=\sqrt{\frac{\epsilon_0k_BT}{n_ee^2}}.
$$

Define the [plasma parameter](../../../../../plasma-parameter.md) as the number of [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) in a [Debye sphere](../../../../../debye-sphere.md):

$$
\boxed{N_D=\frac{4\pi}{3}n_e\lambda_D^3=\frac{4\pi}{3}\frac{\epsilon_0^{3/2}(k_BT)^{3/2}}{e^3n_e^{1/2}}.}
$$

Some authors omit $4\pi/3$ in this definition. Mobile ions alter the total screening length by contributing their own susceptibility; the electron-only convention is used consistently here.
