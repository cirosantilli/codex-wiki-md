<h1 id="s30/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Neglect disk self-[gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) and take $|z|\ll r$ around a central [point mass](../../../../../point-mass.md) $M_\star$. Its vertical gravitational [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) is

$$
g_z=-\frac{GM_\star z}{(r^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\simeq-\frac{GM_\star}{r^3}z=-\Omega^2z.
$$

Vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) is $dP/dz=\rho g_z$. For a vertically isothermal [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), $P=\rho c_s^2$ and $c_s$ is independent of $z$, giving

$$
c_s^2\frac{d\rho}{dz}=-\Omega^2\rho z,\qquad
\boxed{\rho(r,z)=\rho_0(r)e^{-z^2/(2h^2)},\quad h=\frac{c_s}{\Omega},\quad\frac hr=\frac{c_s}{v_K}.}
$$

Here $v_K=r\Omega=\sqrt{GM_\star/r}$, $c_s^2=k_BT/(\mu m_p)$ is the isothermal [sound speed](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) squared, and $h$ is the Gaussian [scale height](../../../../../scale-height.md). A different convention writing $e^{-z^2/h^2}$ changes the quoted height by $\sqrt2$ but not the physics.
