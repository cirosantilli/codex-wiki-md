<h1 id="s30/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At full phase, reflected-light [contrast](../../../../../contrast-statistics.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{F_{p,\lambda}^{\rm refl}}{F_{\star,\lambda}}=A_{g,\lambda}\left(\frac{R_p}{d}\right)^2,}
$$

where $A_g$ is the [geometric albedo](../../../../../geometric-albedo.md), and other phases multiply this by a [phase function](../../../../../phase-function.md) normalized to one at full phase. Thermal [contrast](../../../../../contrast-statistics.md) for uniform blackbody emitters is

$$
\boxed{\frac{F_{p,\lambda}^{\rm th}}{F_{\star,\lambda}}=\left(\frac{R_p}{R_\star}\right)^2\frac{B_\lambda(T_p)}{B_\lambda(T_{\rm eff})}.}
$$

The corresponding bolometric thermal ratio is $(R_p/R_\star)^2(T_p/T_{\rm eff})^4$. If zero internal heating and equilibrium are assumed, energy balance also gives a bolometric reflected ratio $\alpha(R_p/d)^2/4=(\alpha\epsilon/(1-\alpha))(R_p/R_\star)^2(T_p/T_{\rm eff})^4$ for the luminosity ratio; it is not a directional full-phase [contrast](../../../../../contrast-statistics.md).

Earth's thermal emission peaks around $10\,\mu\mathrm m$, and solar emission around $0.5\,\mu\mathrm m$. At $0.5\,\mu\mathrm m$ the thermal [contrast](../../../../../contrast-statistics.md) for $T_p=288\,\mathrm K$ is negligible, about $5\times10^{-46}$; reflected light dominates. Taking illustrative visible $A_g\simeq0.3$ gives $\boxed{C_{0.5}\sim5\times10^{-10}}$. A [Bond albedo](../../../../../bond-albedo.md) does not uniquely determine a spectral [geometric albedo](../../../../../geometric-albedo.md); a Lambert sphere with Bond albedo $0.3$ would instead have $A_g=0.2$, giving $3.6\times10^{-10}$. At $20\,\mu\mathrm m$, the [Planck function](../../../../../planck-function.md) gives $\boxed{C_{20}\simeq1.0\times10^{-6}}$ for $288\,\mathrm K$; using $255\,\mathrm K$ gives about $7\times10^{-7}$. These are order estimates, since the terrestrial [spectrum](../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) is not a uniform blackbody.
