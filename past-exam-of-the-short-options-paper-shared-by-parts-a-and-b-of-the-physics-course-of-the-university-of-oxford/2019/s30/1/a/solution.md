<h1 id="s30/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [exoplanet transit](../../../../../exoplanet-transit.md) occurs when a planet crosses the projected stellar disk and blocks some stellar [flux](../../../../../flux.md). For a circular [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) with isotropically distributed angular-momentum directions, $\cos i$ is uniform on $[0,1]$. A transit requires $a|\cos i|<R_\star+R_p$, so

$$
\boxed{p_{\rm tr}=\frac{R_\star+R_p}{a}\simeq\frac{R_\star}{a}.}
$$

For a dark planet, a uniformly bright stellar disk and negligible dilution, the transit [exoplanet transit depth](../../../../../exoplanet-transit-depth.md) is $\boxed{\delta\simeq(R_p/R_\star)^2}$. For $R_\star/a\ll1$, a small planet and [impact parameter](../../../../../impact-parameter.md) $b=a\cos i/R_\star$,

$$
\boxed{t_{\rm tr}\simeq\frac{P}{\pi}\frac{R_\star}{a}\sqrt{1-b^2}.}
$$

The central-transit result sets $b=0$. A first-to-fourth-contact duration retaining planet size replaces $\sqrt{1-b^2}$ by $\sqrt{(1+R_p/R_\star)^2-b^2}$ and includes the exact projected-speed geometry when needed. [Eccentricity](../../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md), [limb darkening](../../../../../limb-darkening.md) and grazing geometry modify these expressions.
