<h1 id="s30/1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Reducing [orbital inclination](../../../../../../orbital-inclination.md) from exactly edge-on increases the [impact parameter](../../../../../../impact-parameter.md) and shortens the chord, giving $t_{\rm tr}\propto\sqrt{1-b^2}$ in the small-planet, circular approximation. For a uniformly bright disk, a fully internal transit retains approximately the same [exoplanet transit depth](../../../../../../exoplanet-transit-depth.md) $(R_p/R_\star)^2$. Once grazing, the occulted area and [exoplanet transit depth](../../../../../../exoplanet-transit-depth.md) decrease; for $b>1+R_p/R_\star$ the [exoplanet transit](../../../../../../exoplanet-transit.md) disappears. With [limb darkening](../../../../../../limb-darkening.md), a higher-$b$ path is also typically shallower before grazing.
