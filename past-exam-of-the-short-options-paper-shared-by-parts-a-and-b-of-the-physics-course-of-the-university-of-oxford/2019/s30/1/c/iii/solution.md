<h1 id="s30/1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

As the star expands onto the [red giant branch](../../../../../../red-giant-branch.md), a planet of fixed [radius](../../../../../../radius.md) produces a shallower [exoplanet transit](../../../../../../exoplanet-transit.md), $\delta\propto R_\star^{-2}$. For a fixed circular [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) and an approximately central chord, duration grows approximately as $R_\star$, and transit probability also rises. At fixed [orbital inclination](../../../../../../orbital-inclination.md) the [impact parameter](../../../../../../impact-parameter.md) in stellar-radius units decreases, further changing the chord. These statements neglect orbital evolution, stellar [mass loss](../../../../../../mass-loss-astrophysics.md), tidal effects and eventual engulfment, all of which can matter for a very close planet.
