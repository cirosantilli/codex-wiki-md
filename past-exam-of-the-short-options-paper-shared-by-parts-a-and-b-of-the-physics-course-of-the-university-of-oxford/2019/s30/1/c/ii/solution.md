<h1 id="s30/1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Limb darkening](../../../../../../limb-darkening.md) makes the central stellar surface brighter than the disk mean. A small planet at projected position $\mathbf x$ blocks fractional [flux](../../../../../../flux.md) approximately $\delta(\mathbf x)=(R_p/R_\star)^2I(\mathbf x)/\langle I\rangle$. A central transit is therefore deepest near mid-transit and rounded rather than flat-bottomed; a near-limb chord can be shallower. The geometrical contact times do not change for fixed radii and [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), although a threshold-based measured duration can change because ingress and egress are fainter.
