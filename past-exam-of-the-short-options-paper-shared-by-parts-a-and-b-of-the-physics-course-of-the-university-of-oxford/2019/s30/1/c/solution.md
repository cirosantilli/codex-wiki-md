<h1 id="s30/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

To distinguish the companion, measure the host's [radial velocity](../../../../../radial-velocity.md) reflex motion to infer a [mass](../../../../../mass.md); a low-mass [star](../../../../../star.md) generally produces a much larger signal than a planet. Also test for a detectable secondary eclipse, colour-dependent dilution, doubled spectral lines and out-of-eclipse ellipsoidal variation. High angular-resolution imaging and shifts of the image [centroid](../../../../../centroid.md) can identify a blended background [eclipsing binary](../../../../../eclipsing-binary.md). A Jupiter-sized [radius](../../../../../radius.md) alone is not decisive because low-mass stars and brown dwarfs can have comparable radii. The three labelled effects on the transit are treated individually below.
