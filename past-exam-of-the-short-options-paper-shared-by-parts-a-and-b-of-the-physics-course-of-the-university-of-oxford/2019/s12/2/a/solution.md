<h1 id="s12/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A high-[numerical aperture](../../../../../numerical-aperture.md) objective focuses a laser to a steep intensity gradient. For a transparent particle with higher [refractive index](../../../../../refractive-index.md) than its surroundings, refraction transfers photon [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) to the particle, giving a [optical gradient force](../../../../../optical-gradient-force.md) towards the intensity maximum. A forward scattering [force](../../../../../force-split.md) pushes along the beam; a stable single-beam [optical trap](../../../../../optical-tweezers.md) requires the restoring axial [optical gradient force](../../../../../optical-gradient-force.md) to exceed that push. In the small-particle limit, the same result follows from the induced-dipole [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md), proportional to minus the local squared electric field. Close to the trap centre,

$$
F_x\simeq-kx,\qquad U(x)\simeq\frac12kx^2.
$$

An [optical tweezers](../../../../../optical-tweezers.md) experiment can attach a bead to one end of a [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) molecule, hold the other end fixed, and obtain its [force-extension relation](../../../../../force-extension-relation.md) from the bead displacement and calibrated trap stiffness. The measurement must distinguish the restoring [force](../../../../../force-split.md) from [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) and account for heating or optical damage.
