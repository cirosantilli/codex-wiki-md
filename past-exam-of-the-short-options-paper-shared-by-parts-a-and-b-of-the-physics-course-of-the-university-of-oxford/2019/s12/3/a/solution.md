<h1 id="s12/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[ATP synthase](../../../../../atp-synthase.md) has a membrane-embedded $F_o$ sector, a catalytic $F_1$ head and mechanical connections between them. The $F_o$ sector contains an ion-conducting subunit $a$ adjoining a rotating ring of $c$ subunits. A central shaft including the $\gamma$ subunit connects that ring to $F_1$. A peripheral stator holds the head fixed relative to the membrane. The $F_1$ head has three catalytic $\beta$ subunits alternating with three $\alpha$ subunits, surrounding the asymmetric central shaft; the middle panel of [solution](../../2/c/solution.md) labels these relationships.

A transmembrane [proton-motive force](../../../../../proton-motive-force.md) drives proton movement through offset half-channels and protonation changes around the ring, producing [torque](../../../../../torque.md). Shaft rotation changes the conformations of the three catalytic sites, cycling their affinities for [ADP](../../../../../adenosine-diphosphate.md), inorganic phosphate and [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md). In the [binding-change mechanism](../../../../../binding-change-mechanism.md), mechanical work particularly promotes release of tightly bound [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md); the threefold head couples a full revolution to three catalytic turnovers. The number of protons per revolution depends on the ring stoichiometry. Reversing the available [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) can reverse the motor: isolated $F_1$ hydrolyses [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) and rotates, and the coupled enzyme can pump protons. This structural separation of rotor, stator and catalytic head converts electrochemical [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) into chemical [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md).[Nobel lecture, asymmetric catalytic sites and coupling of rotation to nucleotide binding changes](../../../../../energy-life-and-atp-by-paul-d-boyer.md)
