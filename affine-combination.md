# Affine combination

↑ **Parent:** [Affine space](affine-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_combination)

An [affine combination](affine-combination.md) of points $P_i$ is $\sum_i a_iP_i$ with $\sum_i a_i=1$, allowing negative coefficients. It is independent of the choice of origin: translating every point by $T$ translates the result by $T$. It also commutes with every [affine map](affine-map.md). When all coefficients are nonnegative, it is a [convex combination](convex-combination.md), placing the result inside the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of the points.
