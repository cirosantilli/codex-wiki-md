# Spherical spatial slices of a closed FLRW universe

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md)

A round [three-sphere](three-sphere.md) of radius $R$ embedded by $|\mathbf x|^2+u^2=R^2$ has metric $R^2\{dr^2/(1-r^2)+r^2d\Omega^2\}$ for $r=|\mathbf x|/R$. Multiplying by $b(t)^2$ gives a [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) $a=Rb$ and normalized positive [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) $K=1$. This radial chart covers a hemisphere; $r=\sin\chi$ gives the global polar description.
