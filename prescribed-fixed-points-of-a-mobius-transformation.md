<h1 id="prescribed-fixed-points-of-a-mobius-transformation">Prescribed fixed points of a Möbius transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed point of a Möbius transformation](fixed-point-of-a-mobius-transformation.md)

For $f(z)=(az+b)/(z+1)$, the finite fixed points are the roots of

$$
z^2+(1-a)z-b=0.
$$

Thus a repeated fixed point or two prescribed fixed points determine $a$ and $b$ by the usual sum and product of roots.
