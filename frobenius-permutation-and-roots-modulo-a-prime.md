# Frobenius permutation and roots modulo a prime

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius conjugacy class](frobenius-conjugacy-class.md)

Let a monic integer [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) have distinct roots in its [splitting field](splitting-field.md). Outside the finitely many primes dividing its [polynomial discriminant](polynomial-discriminant.md) or ramifying in that field, its roots reduce to distinct residue roots. The [Frobenius automorphism](frobenius-automorphism.md) permutes them by $a\mapsto a^p$; a reduced root belongs to $\mathbb F_p$ exactly when it is fixed. Distinctness of reduction identifies this with a fixed root of the Galois permutation itself. Combining this observation with a [derangement in a transitive group action](derangement-in-a-transitive-group-action.md) and the [Chebotarev density theorem](chebotarev-density-theorem.md) proves that an irreducible polynomial of degree at least two has rootless reduction at a positive [Dirichlet density](dirichlet-density.md) of primes. The degree bound is essential: every monic linear integer polynomial has a root modulo every prime.
