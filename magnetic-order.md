# Magnetic order

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetism](magnetism.md)

[Magnetic order](magnetic-order.md) is a reproducible spatial organization of microscopic [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). A net [magnetization](magnetization.md) describes their sum; a [staggered magnetization](staggered-magnetization.md) describes an alternating pattern. These are different [order parameters](order-parameter.md). For example, an alternating row of equal up and down arrows has zero sum but a definite pattern, whereas randomly oriented arrows can have a small sum without that pattern.
