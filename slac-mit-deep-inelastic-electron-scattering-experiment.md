# SLAC-MIT deep inelastic electron scattering experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md)

Starting in 1967, a collaboration at the Stanford Linear Accelerator Center and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology directed an [electron](electron.md) beam at [liquid](liquid.md) [hydrogen](hydrogen.md), providing [proton](proton.md) targets. [Jerome Isaac Friedman](jerome-isaac-friedman.md), [Henry Way Kendall](henry-way-kendall.md) and [Richard Edward Taylor](richard-edward-taylor.md) participated in this experimental program.[1990 Nobel lecture, section I.A, opening account of the program](deep-inelastic-scattering-experiments-on-the-proton-and-the-observation-of-scaling-by-henry-way-kendall.md)

The 1969 paper Observed Behavior of Highly Inelastic Electron-Proton Scattering reports inclusive [electron](electron.md) spectra at $6^\circ$ and $10^\circ$ with incident [energies](energy-split.md) from $7$ to $17\,\mathrm{GeV}$. Its scaling comparison estimated $\nu_{\rm lab}W_2$ under a transverse-[virtual photon](virtual-photon.md) assumption, finding approximate agreement as a function of $\omega=1/x$ for $Q^2>1\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$ and $\omega>4$. These are the paper's conditions, not a claim of exact [Bjorken scaling](bjorken-scaling.md) at every [momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md).[1969, printed p. 935, abstract, opening paragraphs and Fig. 1; the paper writes its positive transfer variable as q squared](observed-behavior-of-highly-inelastic-electron-proton-scattering-by-m-breidenbach-and-others.md)
