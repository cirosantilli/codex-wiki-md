# Prime convex lattice subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Convex lattice subgroup](convex-lattice-subgroup.md)

A proper [convex lattice subgroup](convex-lattice-subgroup.md) $P$ is prime when $a,b\ge1$ and $a\wedge b=1$ imply $a\in P$ or $b\in P$. Equivalently the ordered right cosets of $P$ form a [totally ordered set](totally-ordered-set.md). Prime refers to this order property, and does not require a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) or prime index.
