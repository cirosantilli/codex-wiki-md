# Velocity-space divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence](divergence.md)

The [divergence](divergence.md) with respect to [velocity](velocity.md) coordinates, with position held fixed. In $d$ [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md), $\mathbf A(\mathbf x,\mathbf v)=\mathbf B(\mathbf x)-a(\mathbf x)\mathbf v$ has $\nabla_{\mathbf v}\cdot\mathbf A=-da$. A kinetic conservation law must retain this term for velocity-dependent forces.
