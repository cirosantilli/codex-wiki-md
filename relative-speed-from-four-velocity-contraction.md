# Relative speed from four-velocity contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity-addition formula](velocity-addition-formula.md)

For future-directed unit timelike [four-velocities](four-velocity.md) in [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(+---)$, $U_1\cdot U_2/c^2$ is the [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) of either observer measured in the [rest frame](rest-frame.md) of the other. In a common [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) it equals $\gamma_1\gamma_2(1-\boldsymbol\beta_1\cdot\boldsymbol\beta_2)$. Therefore

$$
\beta_{\rm rel}^2=1-\frac{(1-\beta_1^2)(1-\beta_2^2)}{(1-\boldsymbol\beta_1\cdot\boldsymbol\beta_2)^2}.
$$

Expanding the numerator converts this to the dot-and-cross-product form of relativistic velocity addition. The invariant construction explains why the relative speed is symmetric even when the spatial velocity components belong to different [rest frames](rest-frame.md).
