# Magnetic dipole inclination versus latitude

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md)

For a centred [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) pointing toward geographic south, the field at northern latitude $\lambda$ has northward component $B_N=B_{\rm eq}\cos\lambda$ and downward component $B_D=2B_{\rm eq}\sin\lambda$. These follow by resolving the [magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md) into local radial and meridional directions. The downward inclination is therefore $\tan\iota=B_D/B_N=2\tan\lambda$, while the magnitude is $B_{\rm eq}\sqrt{1+3\sin^2\lambda}$. This ideal aligned-dipole model has no eastward component and does not capture actual geographic departures of the geomagnetic field.
