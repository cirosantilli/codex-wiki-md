# Smallest non-linear ordinary degree of an alternating group

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating-group restriction of Specht modules](alternating-group-restriction-of-specht-modules.md)

For $A_n$, the least non-linear ordinary degree is $3$ at $n=4,5$ and $n-1$ for $n\ge6$. For $n\le3$ no non-linear [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md) exists. The degree-$n-1$ [representation](group-representation.md) is unique for $n\ge2$, except $n=3$ where it does not exist and $n=6$ where two distinct degree-five [irreducibles](irreducible-representation.md) occur. The proof uses tableau branching to bound the degrees of nonstandard shapes and of the half-degree constituents of self-conjugate shapes, with the small cases checked by the [hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md).
