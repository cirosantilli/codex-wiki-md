# Coordinate ring and singularity of the semicubical parabola

↑ **Parent:** [Semicubical parabola](semicubical-parabola.md)

The homomorphism

$$
k[x,y]\to k[t],
\qquad x\mapsto t^2,\quad y\mapsto t^3
$$

has kernel $(y^2-x^3)$ and image $k[t^2,t^3]$. The quotient is therefore an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), so the curve is irreducible. In characteristic zero its gradient $(-3x^2,2y)$ vanishes at the origin, making the origin its unique [singular point of an algebraic variety](singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md).
