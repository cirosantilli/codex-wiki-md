# Protoplanetary dust coagulation

↑ **Parent:** [Planet formation](planet-formation.md)

Growth of solid aggregates through collisions and sticking in a [protoplanetary disk](protoplanetary-disk.md). Relative motion can arise from [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), settling, turbulence and differential radial drift. Bouncing and fragmentation can interrupt growth; collision frequency alone does not determine whether an encounter increases aggregate [mass](mass.md).
