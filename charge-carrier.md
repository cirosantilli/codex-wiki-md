# Charge carrier

↑ **Parent:** [Current density](current-density.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charge_carrier)

A [charge carrier](charge-carrier.md) transports [electric charge](electric-charge.md) by moving: [Electrons](electron.md) are one example, and [ions](ion.md) are another. For species $s$ with [number density](number-density.md) $n_s$, [electric charge](electric-charge.md) $q_s$ and mean [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf v_s$, the transport contributions add as $\mathbf J=\sum_s n_sq_s\mathbf v_s$. A stationary charged background contributes to [charge density](charge-density.md) but not to this [current density](current-density.md). [Ling, Sanny and Moebs, §9.1: positive and negative carriers](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/9-1-electrical-current).
