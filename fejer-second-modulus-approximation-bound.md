<h1 id="fejer-second-modulus-approximation-bound">Fejér second-modulus approximation bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fejér sum](fejer-sum.md)

The even positive [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md) of total mass one gives $\sigma_nf-f=\tfrac12\int F_n(t)[f(\cdot+t)-2f+f(\cdot-t)]dt$. The scaling property of the [second modulus of smoothness](second-modulus-of-smoothness.md) bounds this by $\omega_2(f,\delta)(1+\delta^{-2}\int t^2F_n(t)dt)$. On $[-\pi,\pi]$, $\sin(|t|/2)\ge |t|/\pi$ gives $\int t^2F_n(t)dt\le\pi^2/n$. Choose $\delta=n^{-1/2}$. For twice continuously differentiable functions, the [second-difference integral formula](second-difference-integral-formula.md) makes the error $O(n^{-1})$.
