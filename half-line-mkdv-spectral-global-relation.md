# Half-line mKdV spectral global relation

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Korteweg-De Vries equation](modified-korteweg-de-vries-equation.md)

For the three corner-normalized [Lax pair](lax-pair.md) eigenfunctions, let $s=\mu_3(0,0,k)$ and $S=e^{4ik^3T\widehat\sigma_3}\mu_2(0,T,k)^{-1}$. Their constant connection matrices give the displayed [global relation](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md). If their second columns are $(b,a)^T$ and $(B,A)^T$, its upper-right entry is $Ab-Ba=e^{8ik^3T}\mu_{3,12}(0,T,k)$. The right side is analytic in the lower half-plane. In the linear limit this is the [backward-sign Airy half-line global relation](backward-sign-airy-half-line-global-relation.md) at Fourier parameter $2k$, coupling all three boundary traces rather than allowing three independent boundary conditions.
