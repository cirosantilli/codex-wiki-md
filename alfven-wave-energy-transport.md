<h1 id="alfven-wave-energy-transport">Alfvén wave energy transport</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md)

Dot the two [Linear Alfvén wave equations](linear-alfven-wave-equations.md) with $\mathbf u_\perp$ and $\mathbf b_\perp/\mu_0$, respectively, and add. The result is [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) with

$$
\mathcal E=\frac{\rho|\mathbf u_\perp|^2}{2}+\frac{|\mathbf b_\perp|^2}{2\mu_0},
\qquad F_z=-\frac{B_0}{\mu_0}\mathbf u_\perp\cdot\mathbf b_\perp.
$$

A single traveling [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) has equal [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and perturbation [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md), and $F_z=\pm v_A\mathcal E$. This equality need not hold instantaneously at each point of a superposed [standing wave](standing-wave.md).

As an original ideal-model example, stipulate $B_0=1.00\,\mathrm T$, $\rho=10^4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and a uniform path $L=0.20\,\mathrm m$. Using $\mu_0\simeq4\pi\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H\,m^{-1}}$, the [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md) is $8.92\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and the transit time $L/v_A$ is $22.4\,\mathrm{ms}$. Doubling the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) halves that time. A sinusoidal [velocity](velocity.md) with peak [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $0.010\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ has magnetic peak [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $1.12\,\mathrm{mT}$ and mean [energy](energy-split.md) flux $\langle F_z\rangle=\rho v_AU^2/2=4.46\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$ in the positive direction. These are stipulated ideal parameters, not historical measurements or a prediction including vessel walls.
