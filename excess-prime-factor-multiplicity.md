# Excess prime-factor multiplicity

↑ **Parent:** [Total number of prime factors](total-number-of-prime-factors.md)

The nonnegative [arithmetic function](arithmetic-function.md) $\Omega(n)-\omega(n)$ counts the repeated occurrences beyond the first of each [prime factor](prime-factor.md). Its [expectation](expected-value.md) under uniform sampling from $[N]$ is bounded by $\sum_p1/(p(p-1))\le1$, independently of $N$.
