# Stellar reflex motion

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md)

The motion of a [star](star.md) around the [astronomical barycenter](barycenter-astronomy.md) caused by an orbiting companion. In an isolated [two-body problem](two-body-problem.md) with relative [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) $a$, the stellar [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) is $a_\star=M_pa/(M_\star+M_p)$. [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md) measures its line-of-sight [velocity](velocity.md) and [astrometry](astrometry.md) measures its projected position on the sky.
