# Transient response

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

The part of a response that dies away after an [initial condition](initial-condition.md) or an input change. In a stable [linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation.md), the decaying [homogeneous solution](homogeneous-solution.md) gives a transient response relative to a chosen [particular solution](particular-solution.md). A [homogeneous solution](homogeneous-solution.md) that grows or persists is not a decaying transient.
