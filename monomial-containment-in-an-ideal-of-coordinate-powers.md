# Monomial containment in an ideal of coordinate powers

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md)

For integers $d_i\ge0$, every [monomial](monomial.md) of total degree $\sum_i d_i$ is divisible by some $X_i^{d_i}$. If any $d_i=0$ the ideal is the whole ring. Otherwise a [monomial](monomial.md) avoiding all these divisibilities would have total degree at most $\sum_i(d_i-1)$, a contradiction. The sharper least degree containing every [monomial](monomial.md) is $\sum_i(d_i-1)+1$ when all $d_i>0$.
