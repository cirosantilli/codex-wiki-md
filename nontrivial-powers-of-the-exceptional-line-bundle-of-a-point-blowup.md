# Nontrivial powers of the exceptional line bundle of a point blowup

↑ **Parent:** [Exceptional divisor](exceptional-divisor.md)

For the [blowup of a complex manifold at a point](blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md) in dimension $n\ge2$, the [exceptional divisor](exceptional-divisor.md) is $\mathbb{CP}^{n-1}$ and $[E]|_E=\mathcal O_E(-1)$. The local blowup is the total space of the tautological [line bundle](line-bundle.md), so its zero section has that [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md). Restricting a power to a [projective line](projective-line.md) inside $E$ gives degree $-r$; a trivial bundle has degree zero. This proves that no nonzero power of $[E]$ is trivial, without assuming global projectivity of the original manifold.
