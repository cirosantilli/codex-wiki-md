# Weight decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Weight space](weight-space.md)

For a representation on which a commuting torus or Cartan action is semisimple, the weight decomposition is the direct sum of simultaneous [weight spaces](weight-space.md). A finite-dimensional representation of a [compact](compact-space.md) [torus](torus.md) can be made unitary by [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) averaging; its commuting unitary matrices are simultaneously diagonalizable. Each diagonal character is an integral [weight of a representation](weight-of-a-representation.md). The decomposition makes tensor and exterior-power weights additive. For nonsemisimple actions, simultaneous generalized eigenspaces give a generalized weight decomposition instead.
