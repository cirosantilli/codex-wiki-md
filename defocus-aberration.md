# Defocus aberration

↑ **Parent:** [Point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defocus_aberration)

[Defocus](defocus-aberration.md) is the mismatch between the receiving plane and the intended [optical focus](focus-optics.md). Changing the plane changes the [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md) even when the [optical lens](lens-optics.md) is otherwise ideal.
