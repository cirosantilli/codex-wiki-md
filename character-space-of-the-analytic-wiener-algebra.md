# Character space of the analytic Wiener algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic Wiener algebra](analytic-wiener-algebra.md)

Every [algebra character](character-of-an-algebra.md) of the [analytic Wiener algebra](analytic-wiener-algebra.md) is evaluation at a unique point $z$ of the closed [unit disk](unit-disk.md). Indeed, for $u(w)=w$, continuity and multiplicativity give $z=\phi(u)$, $|z|\leq\|u\|=1$ and $\phi(f)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nz^n$. Conversely, this formula defines a continuous [algebra character](character-of-an-algebra.md) for every such $z$. The evaluation correspondence is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) for the [Gelfand topology](gelfand-topology.md), since the [power series](power-series.md) are uniformly convergent on the closed [unit disk](unit-disk.md).
