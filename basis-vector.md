# Basis vector

↑ **Parent:** [Basis](basis.md)

A [basis vector](basis-vector.md) is one of the [vectors](vector.md) in a specified [basis](basis.md) of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md). Its coordinate column in that [basis](basis.md) has one entry equal to one and all other entries zero. Columns of a [matrix of a linear map](matrix-representation-of-a-linear-map.md) are the images of domain [basis vectors](basis-vector.md), expressed in the chosen codomain [basis](basis.md).
