# Stopped-martingale uniform integrability criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Stopped martingale in discrete time](stopped-martingale-in-discrete-time.md)

For an [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) finite [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $T$, if $\mathbb E|M_T|<\infty$ and $\mathbb E[|M_n|\mathbf1_{\{T>n\}}]\to0$, then $M_{n\wedge T}\to M_T$ with [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md). Thus [L1 convergence implies uniform integrability](l1-convergence-implies-uniform-integrability.md) makes the [stopped martingale](stopped-martingale.md) [uniformly integrable](uniform-integrability.md).
