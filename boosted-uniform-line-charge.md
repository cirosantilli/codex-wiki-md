# Boosted uniform line charge

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic electromagnetism](relativistic-electromagnetism-split.md)

An infinite uniform line with proper charge per unit [length](length.md) $\lambda_0$ moving axially at [speed](speed.md) $u$ has Lorentz-contracted [density](density.md) $\lambda=\gamma\lambda_0$ and current $I=\lambda u$. Transverse distance $r$ is unchanged by the boost. The rest [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $\lambda_0\widehat{\mathbf r}/(2\pi\epsilon_0r)$ transforms into $\mathbf E=\lambda\widehat{\mathbf r}/(2\pi\epsilon_0r)$ and $\mathbf B=\mathbf u\times\mathbf E/c^2$. This equals the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) $\mu_0I\widehat{\boldsymbol\varphi}/(2\pi r)$ from [Ampère's law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md). The formulas concern a uniform stationary current pattern, not an accelerating finite filament's radiation.
