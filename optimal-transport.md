# Optimal transport

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_transport)

Optimal transport minimizes the cost of moving one [probability measure](probability-measure.md) to another. The [Monge optimal transport problem](monge-optimal-transport-problem.md) uses a [transport map](transport-map.md); the [Kantorovich optimal transport problem](kantorovich-optimal-transport-problem.md) allows a [transport plan](transport-plan.md) that can split mass.
