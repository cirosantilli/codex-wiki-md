# Grassmann derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Grassmann algebra](grassmann-algebra.md)

A [Grassmann derivative](grassmann-derivative.md) is an odd differentiation operation on a [Grassmann algebra](grassmann-algebra.md). A [left Grassmann derivative](left-grassmann-derivative.md) obeys $\partial_\theta^L(ab)=(\partial_\theta^La)b+(-1)^{|a|}a(\partial_\theta^Lb)$ for homogeneous $a$. Right derivatives use the corresponding right-sided product rule. Their placement must be fixed when differentiating fermionic sources in a [generating functional](generating-functional.md), since commuting ordinary derivatives would lose [fermionic signs](fermionic-sign.md).
