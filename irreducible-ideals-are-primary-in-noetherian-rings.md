# Irreducible ideals are primary in Noetherian rings

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible ideal](irreducible-ideal.md)

In a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), an [irreducible ideal](irreducible-ideal.md) is a [primary ideal](primary-ideal.md). In its quotient, if $xy=0$ and $x\ne0$, choose $r$ after the [annihilators](annihilator-ring-theory.md) of powers of $y$ stabilize. Then $(x)\cap(y^r)=0$, so irreducibility forces $y^r=0$. Combined with finite decomposition into irreducible ideals, this proves the [Lasker–Noether theorem](lasker-noether-theorem.md).
