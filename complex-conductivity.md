# Complex conductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md)

[Complex conductivity](complex-conductivity.md) relates the [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md) of [current density](current-density.md) and [electric field](electric-field-split.md) at an [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$ in sinusoidal response. Its [modulus](modulus.md) gives the amplitude ratio, while its [argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) encodes their relative [phase](phase-waves.md). Both the exponential time convention and whether amplitudes are peak or [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) values must be stated when interpreting [phase](phase-waves.md) or average [power](power.md).
