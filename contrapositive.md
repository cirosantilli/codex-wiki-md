# Contrapositive

↑ **Parent:** [Logical implication](logical-implication.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contrapositive)

The contrapositive of $A\to B$ is $\neg B\to\neg A$. In [classical propositional logic](classical-propositional-logic.md) an implication and its contrapositive are logically equivalent, so either can be proved in place of the other.
