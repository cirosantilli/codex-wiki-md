# Contrapositive

↑ **Parent:** [Logical implication](logical-implication.md)

The contrapositive of a [logical implication](logical-implication.md) $A\to B$ reverses the direction and applies [logical negation](negation.md) to both sides: $\neg B\to\neg A$. In [classical propositional logic](classical-propositional-logic.md) they are equivalent, so either can be proved in place of the other. The [contrapositive](contrapositive.md) of “[divisibility](divisibility-split.md) by four implies being an [even number](even-number.md)” says that a number that is not an [even number](even-number.md) cannot be divisible by four.

In [Intuitionistic propositional logic](intuitionistic-propositional-logic.md), $A\to B$ still implies its [contrapositive](contrapositive.md): assumptions $A$ and $\neg B$ would yield $B$ and hence a [contradiction](contradiction.md). The reverse direction is not generally valid. From $\neg B\to\neg A$ and $A$, one obtains only $\neg\neg B$; obtaining $B$ additionally requires [double-negation elimination](double-negation-elimination.md).
