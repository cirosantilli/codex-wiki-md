# VC dimension of a vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical learning theory](statistical-learning-theory.md)

The sign class of a $d$-dimensional real [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of functions has VC dimension at most $d$. If $n>d$ points were shattered, the [evaluation map](linear-map.md) from the function space to $\mathbb R^n$ would have a nonzero vector orthogonal to its image. Choosing labels according to the signs of that vector contradicts orthogonality.
