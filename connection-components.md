# Connection components

↑ **Parent:** [Affine connection](affine-connection.md)

For a local [basis](basis.md) $e_a$ of the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md), an [affine connection](affine-connection.md) has components defined by $\nabla_{e_c}e_b=\Gamma^a{}_{bc}e_a$. If $V=V^be_b$, then $(\nabla_{e_c}V)^a=e_c(V^a)+\Gamma^a{}_{bc}V^b$. The [connection components](connection-components.md) depend on the chosen [basis](basis.md) and have an inhomogeneous transformation law; they are not themselves a [tensor field](tensor-field.md). In a [coordinate basis](coordinate-basis.md), a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md) has symmetry in the two lower indices.
