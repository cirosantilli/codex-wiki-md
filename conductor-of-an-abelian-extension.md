# Conductor of an abelian extension

↑ **Parent:** [Class field theory](class-field-theory.md)

The conductor $\mathfrak f_{L/K}$ is the smallest modulus through which the [Artin reciprocity map](artin-reciprocity-law.md) for a finite abelian extension $L/K$ factors. Its finite prime divisors are exactly the ramified primes, with exponents measuring the depth of ramification.
