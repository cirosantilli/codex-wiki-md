# Residual plot

↑ **Parent:** [Regression residual](regression-residual.md)

A residual plot displays [regression residuals](regression-residual.md) against fitted responses, predictor values or observation order. Curvature can suggest an incorrect [regression function](regression-function.md); changing spread can suggest unequal error [variances](variance-split.md); ordered runs can suggest dependence. A plot is a diagnostic, not a proof of [independence](independent-random-variables.md). Dependence confined to higher [moments](moment.md), as for [adjacent products of independent Gaussian variables](adjacent-products-of-independent-gaussian-variables.md), may call for lagged squared [regression residuals](regression-residual.md) rather than a basic scatter plot. Fitting also induces dependence among [regression residuals](regression-residual.md) even under independent original errors.
