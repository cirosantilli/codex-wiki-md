# Satisfying assignment

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md)

A [satisfying assignment](satisfying-assignment.md) is a complete [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) $v$ for which a [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md) $F$ has [truth value](truth-value.md) one, written $v\models F$. The notation describes a relation between the [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) and the [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md). For the example in [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md), $(0,1,0)$ is a [model of a Boolean formula](satisfying-assignment.md) $F$, whereas $(0,0,0)$ is not. [SAT](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md) asks for existence of at least one [satisfying assignment](satisfying-assignment.md), rather than for every [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) to work; the latter condition makes $F$ a [tautology](tautology.md).
