# Pointwise limit

↑ **Parent:** [Pointwise convergence](pointwise-convergence.md)

A [pointwise limit](pointwise-limit.md) assigns to each input the [limit of a sequence](limit-of-a-sequence.md) of [function](function-split.md) values there, on the specified [domain of a function](domain-of-a-function.md). It is unique wherever it exists, by uniqueness of the numerical [limit of a sequence](limit-of-a-sequence.md). Its existence supplies no common approximation rate over the [domain of a function](domain-of-a-function.md). For $f_n(x)=x^n$ on $[0,1]$, it equals zero below one and one at one; restricting to $[0,a]$ with $a<1$ gives the zero [function](function-split.md).
