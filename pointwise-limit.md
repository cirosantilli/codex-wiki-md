# Pointwise limit

↑ **Parent:** [Pointwise convergence](pointwise-convergence.md)

A pointwise limit assigns to each point the limit of the sequence of function values there. The convergence rate may depend on that point. [Uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) additionally bounds the error simultaneously over the whole domain.
