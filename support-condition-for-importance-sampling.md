# Support condition for importance sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Importance sampling](importance-sampling.md)

An [importance sampling](importance-sampling.md) proposal $q$ must be positive wherever the target-weighted integrand can contribute. The usual sufficient condition is that the target probability is absolutely continuous with respect to $q$. Otherwise multiplying observed draws by $f/q$ cannot recover a missing part of the integral. A strict rejection envelope supplies this condition for the [rejected-proposal distribution](rejected-proposal-distribution.md).
