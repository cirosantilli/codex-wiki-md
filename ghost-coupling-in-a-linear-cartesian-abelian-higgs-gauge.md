# Ghost coupling in a linear Cartesian Abelian Higgs gauge

↑ **Parent:** [R-xi gauge](r-xi-gauge.md)

For $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-igA_\mu$, transformations $\delta A_\mu=-\partial_\mu\theta$, $\delta\phi=-ig\theta\phi$, and Cartesian shift $\phi=(v+h+ib)/\sqrt2$, the [gauge function](gauge-fixing.md) $G=\partial\cdot A-\xi gvb$ varies by $[-\Box+\xi g^2v(v+h)]\theta$. Exponentiating the [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md) with the convention $-\bar c(\delta G/\delta\theta)c$ gives [ghost mass](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) squared $\xi g^2v^2$ and one [Higgs-ghost](ghost-coupling-in-a-linear-cartesian-abelian-higgs-gauge.md) [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) $-i\xi g^2v$. A scalar-dependent [Abelian](abelian-group.md) gauge therefore has interacting [ghosts](faddeev-popov-ghost.md); a pure linear [Lorenz gauge](lorenz-gauge-condition.md) has a field-independent [determinant](determinant.md).
