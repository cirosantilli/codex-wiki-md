# Alternating bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear form](bilinear-form.md)

A [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) is alternating if $B(v,v)=0$ for every [vector](vector.md) $v$. Expanding $B(v+w,v+w)$ implies $B(v,w)=-B(w,v)$. The converse requires [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two. In that characteristic a skew-symmetric [matrix](matrix.md) of [odd integer](odd-integer.md) size has zero [determinant](determinant.md), since $\det A=\det(-A^T)=(-1)^n\det A$. Thus an alternating form on a finite odd-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is degenerate. A [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) alternating form defines a [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md).
