# Vertical velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity field](velocity-field.md)

The vertical velocity is the component of a fluid [velocity field](velocity-field.md) along the selected upward vertical direction. In [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), impermeable horizontal boundaries impose $w=0$. Eliminating [pressure](pressure.md) often yields a closed [hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md) equation for this component.
