# Ultrasound

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrasound)

[Ultrasound](ultrasound.md) is sound above the conventional upper limit of human hearing, approximately $20\,\mathrm{kHz}$. In a [crystal](crystal.md), ultrasonic travel-time measurements determine long-wavelength elastic [wave speeds](wave-speed.md), which are the small-[wavevector](wavevector.md) slopes of the corresponding [acoustic phonon branches](acoustic-phonon-branch.md).
