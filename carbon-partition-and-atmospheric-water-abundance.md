# Carbon partition and atmospheric water abundance

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric carbon-to-oxygen ratio](atmospheric-carbon-to-oxygen-ratio.md)

In hot hydrogen-rich [thermochemical equilibrium](thermochemical-equilibrium.md), much carbon can be bound in [carbon monoxide](carbon-monoxide.md), consuming oxygen that is unavailable for [water](water.md). At a C/O ratio near or above one, water can be strongly depleted. Cooler [methane](methane.md)-dominated chemistry can release more oxygen for water at the same elemental inventory. Pressure, quenching, dissociation and [atmospheric photochemistry](atmospheric-photochemistry.md) can modify this temperature-dependent partition.
