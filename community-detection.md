# Community detection

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

[Community detection](community-detection.md) partitions a network into groups according to a specified structural criterion, often excess internal connectivity. The output depends on the objective, so a partition inferred from topology need not by itself establish distinct biological functions.
