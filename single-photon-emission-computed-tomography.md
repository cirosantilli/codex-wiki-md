# Single-photon emission computed tomography

↑ **Parent:** [Tomography](tomography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-photon_emission_computed_tomography)

A form of [tomography](tomography.md) that reconstructs an internal radioactive source from directional counts of individually emitted [photons](photon.md). Known spatial [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) gives the exponential path weight in an [attenuated Radon transform](attenuated-radon-transform.md). Full directed data and a suitable reconstruction formula recover the source intensity rather than a transmitted exterior-beam attenuation map.
