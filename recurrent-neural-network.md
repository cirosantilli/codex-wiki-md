# Recurrent neural network

↑ **Parent:** [Neural network](neural-network.md)

A [neural network](neural-network.md) whose hidden state follows a recurrence $h_t=F(h_{t-1},x_t)$ with shared parameters. It represents dependence across sequence positions through its hidden state. The standard recurrence is sequential across positions, and differentiation through many steps multiplies step [Jacobian matrices](jacobian-matrix.md), potentially causing a [vanishing gradient](vanishing-gradient-problem.md) or an [exploding gradient](exploding-gradient-problem.md).
