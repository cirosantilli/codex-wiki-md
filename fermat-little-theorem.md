# Fermat little theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_little_theorem)

For a prime p, every integer a satisfies a^p congruent to a modulo p.
