# Fichtenholz-Kantorovich independent family

↑ **Parent:** [Independent family of sets](independent-family-of-sets.md)

For every infinite [set](set-split.md) $X$ there is an [independent family of sets](independent-family-of-sets.md) consisting of $2^{|X|}$ subsets of $X$. One construction uses a set $D$ of pairs $(s,H)$, where $s\subseteq X$ is finite and $H\subseteq\mathcal P(s)$. For each $A\subseteq X$, let $I_A=\{(s,H):A\cap s\in H\}$. Given distinct $A_1,\ldots,A_m$, choose a finite $s$ distinguishing all their traces and choose $H$ to contain precisely the traces prescribed to have value one. This realizes every finite Boolean choice. With the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md), $|D|=|X|$, so a [bijection](bijection.md) transports this family to $X$. Its independent assignments give $2^{2^{|X|}}$ distinct [ultrafilters](ultrafilter.md).
