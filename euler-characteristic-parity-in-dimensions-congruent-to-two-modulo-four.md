# Euler characteristic parity in dimensions congruent to two modulo four

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md)

For a closed oriented [manifold](topological-manifold.md) of dimension $4r+2$, [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) pairs all rational [Betti numbers](betti-number.md) except the middle one, making their total contribution to the [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) even. The middle cohomology degree is odd, so its nondegenerate [Poincare duality pairing](poincare-duality-pairing.md) is an [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md) and has even dimension. Hence the Euler characteristic is even. Nonorientable manifolds can have odd Euler characteristic, as [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) of even dimension demonstrates.
