# Hartree product state

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree equations](hartree-equations.md)

A many-particle trial state built from one-particle factors, without the additional correlations of a general many-body [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md). For identical [bosons](boson.md) occupying one normalized orbital $\varphi$, the symmetric fixed-number form is $(a_\varphi^\dagger)^N|0\rangle/\sqrt{N!}$. Its field expectation is zero because annihilation changes particle number, while its one-body [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) has the macroscopic eigenvalue $N$. An antisymmetrized multi-orbital fermionic trial state instead belongs to the Hartree-Fock construction.
