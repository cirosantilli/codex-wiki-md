# Weakly dominant strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Dominant strategy](dominant-strategy.md)

A [strategy](strategy-in-an-infinite-game.md) giving at least as large a [payoff](payoff.md) as every alternative against every opponent [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md). Under the convention distinguishing weak dominance from complete payoff equivalence, require a strict advantage somewhere. In a two-action [finite game](finite-game.md), it suffices to check the opponent's [pure strategies](pure-strategy.md): [expected payoff](expected-payoff.md) differences against an opponent's [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) are weighted averages of those pure-action comparisons.
