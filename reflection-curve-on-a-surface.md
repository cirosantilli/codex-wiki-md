# Reflection curve on a surface

↑ **Parent:** [Specular reflection](specular-reflection.md)

For a [parametric surface](parametric-surface.md) $P(u,v)$, source [parametric curve](parametric-curve.md) $Q(t)$ and eye $E$, put $a=(Q-P)/\lVert Q-P\rVert$ and $b=(E-P)/\lVert E-P\rVert$. The visible reflection locus satisfies $(a+b)\cdot P_u=(a+b)\cdot P_v=0$, with the source and eye on the reflecting side. Rank two of the resulting [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) gives a locally one-dimensional locus in $(u,v,t)$ by the [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md).
