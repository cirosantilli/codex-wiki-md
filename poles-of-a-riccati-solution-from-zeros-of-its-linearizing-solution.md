# Poles of a Riccati solution from zeros of its linearizing solution

↑ **Parent:** [Linearization of a Riccati equation](linearization-of-a-riccati-equation.md)

At a regular point of a second-order [linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation.md), a nontrivial solution cannot have both $x$ and $x'$ zero, by initial-value uniqueness. Its zeros are therefore simple. Under the [linearization of a Riccati equation](linearization-of-a-riccati-equation.md), such a zero $t_*$ produces $y=x'/(cx)\sim1/[c(t-t_*)]$. Zeros separate the intervals on which the [Riccati equation](riccati-equation.md) solution is finite.
