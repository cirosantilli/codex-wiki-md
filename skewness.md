# Skewness

↑ **Parent:** [Central moment](central-moment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skewness)

Skewness is the standardized third central [moment](moment.md) of a [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md). A symmetric distribution with finite third [moment](moment.md) has zero skewness. Cosmology also uses the reduced skewness $S_3=\langle\delta^3\rangle/\sigma^4$, which differs from the standardized skewness by a factor of $\sigma$.
