# Prandtl number

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prandtl_number)

The [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) is the ratio of [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) to [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md). It compares the rates at which momentum and [temperature](temperature.md) variations diffuse. In thermal-diffusion units the [Boussinesq equations](boussinesq-equations.md) have momentum inertia multiplied by $\operatorname{Pr}^{-1}$; large, rather than small, [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) supplies the usual instantaneous [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) limit.
