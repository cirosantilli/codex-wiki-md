# Morrey-Campanato space

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morrey–Campanato_space)

[Morrey-Campanato spaces](morrey-campanato-space.md) control local integrals on balls by a power of their radius. The Morrey form uses $\sup_{x,r}r^{-\lambda}\int_{B_r(x)}|u|^p$; the [Campanato space](campanato-space.md) uses $|u-u_{B_r(x)}|^p$ instead. Mean subtraction measures oscillation and relates this family to [Hölder spaces](holder-space.md).
