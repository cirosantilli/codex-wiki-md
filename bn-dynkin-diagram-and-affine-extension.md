# Bn Dynkin diagram and affine extension

↑ **Parent:** [Bn root system](bn-root-system.md)

For $n\ge2$, the [Bn root system](bn-root-system.md) has [simple roots](simple-root.md) $\alpha_i=\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_{i+1}$ for $i<n$ and $\alpha_n=\varepsilon_n$. Its [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) is a chain with a double terminal bond pointing toward short $\alpha_n$. The [highest root](highest-root.md) is $\theta=\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2$. In the [Extended Dynkin diagram](extended-dynkin-diagram.md), $\alpha_0=-\theta$ attaches by a single bond to $\alpha_2$ for $n\ge3$. For $n=2$, both long nodes $0,1$ attach by double bonds to short node $2$. Rank one is the [rank-one root system](rank-one-root-system.md); its affine diagram has two equal-length nodes joined by a double bond without a short-root arrow.
