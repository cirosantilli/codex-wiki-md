# Homogeneous sum of squares representation

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial SOS](polynomial-sos.md)

If a degree-$2d$ [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) is a [sum of squares polynomial](polynomial-sos.md), it has a representation as squares of degree-$d$ [homogeneous polynomials](homogeneous-polynomial.md). In any sum of squares, the highest-degree terms cannot cancel, since their homogeneous part is itself a sum of squares. Likewise the lowest nonzero degree cannot cancel. Thus every nonzero summand has only degree $d$.
