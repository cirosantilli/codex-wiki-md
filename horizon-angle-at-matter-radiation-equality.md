# Horizon angle at matter-radiation equality

↑ **Parent:** [Particle horizon](particle-horizon.md)

A horizon-length patch on the [matter-radiation equality](matter-radiation-equality.md) surface has physical size $a_{\rm eq}\tau_{\rm eq}$ in a flat universe with [conformal time](conformal-time.md) measured from the [Big Bang](big-bang.md). Dividing by its [angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md) $a_{\rm eq}(\tau_0-\tau_{\rm eq})$ gives its small angular size. For the [radiation-matter scale factor in conformal time](radiation-matter-scale-factor-in-conformal-time.md), this is $(\sqrt2-1)\sqrt{\Omega_{r,0}}/[1-\sqrt{2\Omega_{r,0}}]$. A patch diameter is twice the angle associated with one horizon radius. The [particle horizon](particle-horizon.md) is an integral over past evolution and must not be silently replaced by the instantaneous [Hubble radius](hubble-radius.md).
