# Sublocale

↑ **Parent:** [Locale](locale.md)

A sublocale is determined by a [nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md) $j$ on $\mathcal O(X)$. Its [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) consists of the fixed elements of $j$, with inherited meets and regularized joins. Increasing the nucleus makes the sublocale smaller.
