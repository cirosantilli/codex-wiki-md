# Germline mutation

↑ **Parent:** [Mutation](mutation.md)

A [germline mutation](germline-mutation.md) occurs in the lineage that makes [gametes](gamete.md) and can therefore be inherited by an offspring [organism](organism-split.md). Transmission depends on whether the affected lineage actually contributes a [gamete](gamete.md) to that offspring. Compare [somatic mutation](somatic-mutation.md).
