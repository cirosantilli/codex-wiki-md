# Capped claim moments

↑ **Parent:** [Excess of loss reinsurance](excess-of-loss-reinsurance.md)

For a positive claim and $r>0$, the [tail integral formula for moments](tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md) gives the displayed expression. In particular the first moment is $\int_0^M\overline F(x)\,dx$ and the raw second moment is $2\int_0^M x\overline F(x)\,dx$. If the claim law has a density, capping adds an [atom of a measure](atom-measure-theory.md) at $M$ with mass $\mathbb P(X\ge M)$.
