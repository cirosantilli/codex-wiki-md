# Secondary metabolic byproduct

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolism](metabolism.md)

A compound released through specialized functions such as signaling, defense or stress adaptation rather than the core reactions needed for energy and biomass. [Dimethyl sulfide](dimethyl-sulfide.md) and [chloromethane](chloromethane.md) are examples discussed as atmospheric biosignatures. Their specialized origins may improve specificity while lowering the global emission flux.
