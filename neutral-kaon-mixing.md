# Neutral kaon mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Kaon](kaon.md)

The neutral flavour states $d\bar s$ and $s\bar d$ can mix through second-order weak interactions. Box diagrams with [W bosons](w-boson.md) and internal up-type quarks induce a strangeness-changing-by-two four-quark operator. The [GIM mechanism](gim-mechanism.md) removes its flavour-independent part.
