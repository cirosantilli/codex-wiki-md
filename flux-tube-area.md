# Flux-tube area

↑ **Parent:** [Flux tube](flux-tube.md)

The cross-sectional area of a narrow [flux tube](flux-tube.md), measured normal to its [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). Constancy of the [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) gives $A|\mathbf B|=\mathrm{constant}$ along the tube. This area controls the mass transport when flow follows the field.
