# Spin-orbit energy shift in a central potential

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md)

For a central-potential [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) with the [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md) $H_{\rm so}=[r^{-1}V'(r)/(2m^2c^2)]\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$, use $\mathbf J=\mathbf L+\mathbf S$ and

$$
\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S=\tfrac12(J^2-L^2-S^2).
$$

A coupled [angular momentum eigenstate](angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) makes this angular factor a number. The radial operator commutes with all angular factors, giving the energy shift in the title. It is independent of the magnetic projection $m_j$, so a fixed-$j$ multiplet retains its $2j+1$ degeneracy.

For [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md), the angular bracket is $l$ when $j=l+1/2$ and $-(l+1)$ when $j=l-1/2$. Only the former exists at $l=0$, where it is zero. For the [Coulomb potential energy](coulomb-potential-energy.md), $r^{-1}V'(r)=e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^3)$; the radial normalization convention must be respected when computing its [expectation value](expectation-value.md).
