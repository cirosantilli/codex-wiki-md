# Tangent sheaf of a scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of relative Kähler differentials](sheaf-of-relative-kahler-differentials.md)

The relative tangent sheaf is the [dual of a sheaf](dual-of-a-sheaf.md) of [Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md). Its sections are derivations of the [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) that kill base scalars. For a [smooth morphism](smooth-morphism.md) it is [locally free](locally-free-sheaf.md) and describes first-order deformations of sections into the scheme.
