# Entire function algebra admits no complete algebra norm

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra norm](algebra-norm.md)

The displayed formula is a submultiplicative norm on the algebra of [entire functions](entire-function.md): definiteness follows from the [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md). No submultiplicative norm on the whole algebra can be complete. The coordinate function $Z(z)=z$ has every complex number in its algebraic [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md), because $Z-\lambda$ vanishes somewhere and has no entire inverse. This contradicts compactness of spectra in a [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md).
