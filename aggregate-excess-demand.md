# Aggregate excess demand

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange economy](exchange-economy.md)

[Aggregate excess demand](aggregate-excess-demand.md) subtracts total endowment from the sum of consumers' [demand correspondences](demand-correspondence.md). A market-clearing price satisfies $z(p)=0$. Under [Walras law](walras-s-law.md), one equation is redundant after price normalization.
