# Subadditive cost game

↑ **Parent:** [Cooperative cost game](cooperative-cost-game.md)

A [cooperative cost game](cooperative-cost-game.md) in which disjoint [coalitions](coalition-game-theory.md) can merge without increasing their combined cost. Negating its cost function gives a [superadditive coalitional game](superadditive-coalitional-game.md). Subadditivity by itself does not ensure a nonempty cost [core](core-game-theory.md).
