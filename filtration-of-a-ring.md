# Filtration of a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Filtered ring](filtered-ring.md)

A filtration of a [ring](ring.md) is an exhaustive increasing family of additive subgroups $(F_iR)_{i\in\mathbb Z}$, with $1\in F_0R$ and the displayed multiplicativity condition. Its [associated graded ring](associated-graded-ring.md) is $\bigoplus_i F_iR/F_{i-1}R$. It differs from a probabilistic [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md), which is a nested family of sigma-algebras.
