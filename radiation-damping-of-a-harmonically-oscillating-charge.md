# Radiation damping of a harmonically oscillating charge

↑ **Parent:** [Electric dipole radiation](electric-dipole-radiation.md)

A charge $q$ undergoing [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md) of [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$ and slowly varying amplitude $A$ radiates the averaged power

$$
\langle P\rangle=\frac{\mu_0q^2A^2\omega^4}{12\pi c}.
$$

Its mechanical energy $E=mA^2\omega^2/2$ therefore has [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) rate $\gamma=\mu_0q^2\omega^2/(6\pi mc)$ and [half-life](half-life.md)

$$
t_{1/2}=\frac{6\pi mc\log2}{\mu_0q^2\omega^2}.
$$
