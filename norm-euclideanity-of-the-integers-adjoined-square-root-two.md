# Norm-Euclideanity of the integers adjoined square root two

↑ **Parent:** [Ring of integers of Q of square root two](ring-of-integers-of-q-of-square-root-two.md)

The ring $\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]$ is a [Euclidean domain](euclidean-domain.md) for the absolute [field norm](field-norm.md). Approximate both rational coefficients of a quotient by nearest integers. If the errors are $u,w\in[-1/2,1/2]$, then $|u^2-2w^2|\leq1/2<1$, giving a remainder of strictly smaller absolute norm. In particular this ring is a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md) and has trivial [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md).
