# Fuchsian group

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuchsian_group)

A Fuchsian group is a discrete subgroup of $\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb R)$, acting by orientation-preserving isometries of the hyperbolic plane. It is non-elementary when it has no finite orbit in the hyperbolic plane or its ideal boundary.
