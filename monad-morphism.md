# Monad morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Monad](monad.md)

A [morphism](morphism.md) of [monads](monad.md) on one [category](category-split.md) is a [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) $\alpha:S\Rightarrow T$ with $\alpha\eta^S=\eta^T$ and $\alpha\mu^S=\mu^T(T\alpha)(\alpha S)$. These equations preserve the unit and multiplication. A terminal [endofunctor](endofunctor.md) in a full composition-closed subcategory containing the identity receives a unique such [morphism](morphism.md) from every [monad](monad.md) whose [functor](functor.md) belongs to that subcategory.
