# Computer Graphics examination paper by the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The six-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) Finals [computer graphics](computer-graphics-split.md) paper dated Monday 8 June 2015. The released [examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains it as `2015-graphics.pdf`. Questions occupy printed pp. 2–6. Question 2(b)(iii)'s two printed rotation products require an action-order qualification: their intended comparison works when the rotations are applied in written sequence, while ordinary column-vector products apply the rightmost factor first.
