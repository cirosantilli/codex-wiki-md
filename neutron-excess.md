# Neutron excess

↑ **Parent:** [Electron fraction in stellar matter](electron-fraction-in-stellar-matter.md)

The [neutron excess](neutron-excess.md) is the number of [neutrons](neutron.md) minus [protons](proton.md) per baryon. A mixture of carbon-12 and oxygen-16 has zero [neutron excess](neutron-excess.md), while neon-22 contributes $2/22$ per baryon in that isotope. Thus a neon-22 [mass](mass.md) fraction $X_{22}$ gives $\eta=X_{22}/11$. A nonzero excess shifts iron-group ashes from symmetric nickel-56 toward neutron-rich isotopes, even before additional weak reactions occur.
