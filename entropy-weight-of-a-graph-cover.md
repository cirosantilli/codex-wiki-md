# Entropy weight of a graph cover

↑ **Parent:** [Coloring entropy weight](coloring-entropy-weight.md)

If graphs $G_i$ cover the edges of an $n$-vertex graph with [independence number](independence-number.md) at most $\alpha$, their coloring entropy weights satisfy the displayed bound. A random vertex's color is recorded in every covering graph containing it; independent dummy colors with matching marginal laws fill the other positions. Conditional on all recorded colors, compatible vertices form an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md), leaving conditional entropy at most $\log_2\alpha$. The [mutual information](mutual-information.md) carried by the colors is at most $n^{-1}\sum_iw(G_i)$, proving the bound.
