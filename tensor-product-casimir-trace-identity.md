# Tensor-product Casimir trace identity

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic Casimir operator](quadratic-casimir-operator.md)

For traceless generators, the [tensor product of Lie algebra representations](tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md) has generators $t_a\otimes I+I\otimes s_a$. The [trace](matrix-trace.md) of their summed squares is the sum of the two factor Casimir [traces](matrix-trace.md); the cross term vanishes because $\operatorname{tr}t_a=\operatorname{tr}s_a=0$. Decomposition into [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md), with multiplicities counted, gives the displayed identity. Without tracelessness an extra term $2\sum_a\operatorname{tr}t_a\operatorname{tr}s_a$ is present. A one-dimensional abelian representation of charge one tensored with itself has Casimir four, not the sum two; semisimple [Lie algebras](lie-algebra-split.md) avoid this problem because every generator is a sum of [commutators](commutator.md).
