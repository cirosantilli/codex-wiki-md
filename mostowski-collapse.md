# Mostowski collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Mostowski collapse theorem](mostowski-collapse-theorem.md)

The recursively defined [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) from a [well-founded](well-founded-relation.md) extensional set-like relation to membership on a [transitive set](transitive-set.md) or class. The [Mostowski collapse theorem](mostowski-collapse-theorem.md) asserts its existence and uniqueness; the class form requires each predecessor extension to be a set.
