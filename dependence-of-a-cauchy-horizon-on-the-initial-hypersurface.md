# Dependence of a Cauchy horizon on the initial hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md)

A complete [Cauchy hypersurface](cauchy-surface.md) in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), such as $t=0$, has full [domain of dependence](domain-of-dependence.md) and empty [Cauchy horizons](cauchy-horizon.md). This does not forbid horizons for a non-Cauchy initial surface in the same spacetime. The smooth edgeless spacelike hyperboloid $t=\sqrt{a^2+r^2}$, $a>0$, has domain of dependence $t>r$ and a past [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md) on the future light cone of the origin. Every causal curve starting inside that cone crosses the hyperboloid in the direction needed for dependence: $t^2-r^2$ passes through $a^2$, while a null generator of the cone never meets it. The time-reversed hyperboloid gives the analogous future horizon.
