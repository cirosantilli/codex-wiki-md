# Propositional formula

↑ **Parent:** [Propositional logic](propositional-logic.md)

A [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) is built from atomic propositions using [logical connectives](logical-connective.md). Unlike a [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md), it has no quantifiers over individuals. A [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) interprets its atoms as truth values and evaluates its connectives recursively.
