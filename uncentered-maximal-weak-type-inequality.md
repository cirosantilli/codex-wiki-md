# Uncentered maximal weak-type inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Uncentered maximal function of a finite measure](uncentered-maximal-function-of-a-finite-measure.md)

For a finite positive measure on Euclidean space, cover a compact subset of the maximal superlevel set by finitely many high-density balls. [Wiener covering lemma](wiener-covering-lemma.md) selects disjoint balls whose triples cover it. Their total measure mass is at most the mass of the original measure. [Inner regularity of Lebesgue measure](inner-regularity-of-lebesgue-measure.md) yields the displayed bound. Applied to approximation errors, it proves the [Lebesgue differentiation theorem](lebesgue-differentiation-theorem.md).
