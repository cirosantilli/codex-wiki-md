# Elliptical polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization (waves)](polarization-waves.md)

For an [elliptically polarized wave](elliptical-polarization.md), the displacement vector at a fixed point traces an [ellipse](ellipse.md) during each [oscillation](oscillation.md) period. [Linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) and [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md) are limiting cases.
