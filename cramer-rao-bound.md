<h1 id="cramer-rao-bound">Cramér-Rao bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cramér–Rao_bound)

For a regular scalar model with [score function](informant-function.md) $S_\theta$ and [Fisher information](fisher-information-matrix.md) $I(\theta)=\mathbb E_\theta S_\theta^2$, an estimator $T$ with mean $\psi(\theta)$ satisfies

$$
\operatorname{Var}_\theta(T)
\ge\frac{\psi'(\theta)^2}{I(\theta)}.
$$

Indeed, differentiating $\mathbb E_\theta T=\psi(\theta)$ gives $\operatorname{Cov}_\theta(T,S_\theta)=\psi'(\theta)$, and the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives the bound.
