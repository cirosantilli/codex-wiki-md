# Strip automorphism with a prescribed image of its midpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

On $S_h=\{z:0<\operatorname{Im}z<h\}$, let $\beta\in S_h$ and write $e^{\pi\beta/h}=A+iB$ with $B>0$. The [exponential function](exponential-function.md) $E(z)=e^{\pi z/h}$ is a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) from the strip bijectively onto the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), sending $ih/2$ to $i$. The affine map $w\mapsto A+Bw$ is an automorphism of the half-plane sending $i$ to $E(\beta)$. Conjugate it by $E$, using the [principal complex logarithm](principal-complex-logarithm.md) as the inverse. The displayed formula is a conformal automorphism of the strip and satisfies $g_\beta(ih/2)=\beta$. The branch range $0<\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Log}w<\pi$ is what prevents an unwanted vertical period in this inverse.
