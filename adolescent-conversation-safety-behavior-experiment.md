# Adolescent conversation safety-behavior experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Clark–Wells model of social anxiety disorder](clark-wells-model-of-social-anxiety-disorder.md)

Leigh and colleagues compared counterbalanced conversations under combined internal-focus/[safety behavior](safety-behavior.md) instructions versus external-focus/safety-reduction instructions. Self, partner and independent ratings in a symptom-selected sample provided experimental evidence for the combined manipulation. Because two processes changed together, this experiment does not by itself identify their separate [causal effects](causal-effect.md) or establish treatment efficacy in diagnosed patients.[PLOS ONE 16, e0247703; counterbalanced conversations, 57 participants, partner and independent ratings](self-focused-attention-and-safety-behaviours-maintain-social-anxiety-in-adolescents-an-experimental-study-by-eleanor-leigh-and-others.md)
