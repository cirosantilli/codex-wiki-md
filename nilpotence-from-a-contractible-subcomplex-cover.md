# Nilpotence from a contractible subcomplex cover

↑ **Parent:** [Relative cup product](relative-cup-product.md)

If a pointed CW complex is covered by $l$ contractible subcomplexes containing the basepoint, every reduced class lifts to a relative class for each appropriate subcomplex. Their [relative cup product](relative-cup-product.md) lies in $h^*(X,\bigcup_jA_j)=h^*(X,X)=0$, so every product of $l$ reduced classes vanishes. The conclusion also holds if $X$ is path connected. Without either condition a two-point space covered by its two points is a counterexample in ordinary degree-zero cohomology.
