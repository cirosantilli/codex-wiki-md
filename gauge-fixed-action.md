# Gauge-fixed action

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md)

A [gauge-fixed action](gauge-fixed-action.md) supplements a gauge-invariant action with a term imposing the gauge condition and with [Faddeev-Popov ghost fields](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) representing the [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md). A local gauge condition gives local differential operators and a local action.
