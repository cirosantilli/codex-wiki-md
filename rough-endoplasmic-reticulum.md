# Rough endoplasmic reticulum

↑ **Parent:** [Endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

[Rough endoplasmic reticulum](rough-endoplasmic-reticulum.md) bears attached [ribosomes](ribosome.md) on its [cytosol](cytosol.md) surface. In a common mammalian route, [protein targeting](protein-targeting.md) delivers a [nascent chain](nascent-chain.md) into the [ER lumen](er-lumen.md) during [biological translation](translation-biology.md). Other [proteins](protein-split.md) enter the membrane. Free and attached [ribosomes](ribosome.md) share the same basic machinery; targeting depends on their cargo.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; Membrane-bound Ribosomes Define the Rough ER, Figures 12-36 and 12-37; Translocation Across the ER Membrane Does Not Always Require Ongoing Polypeptide Chain Elongation](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/the-endoplasmic-reticulum.md)
