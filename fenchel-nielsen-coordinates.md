<h1 id="fenchel-nielsen-coordinates">Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Pants decomposition](pants-decomposition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenchel–Nielsen_coordinates)

For a fixed [pants decomposition](pants-decomposition.md), the positive cuff lengths and real twist parameters give coordinates on [Teichmüller space](teichmuller-space.md). With twists measured in length units, a full [Dehn twist](dehn-twist.md) changes $\tau_i$ by $\ell_i$; with angle units it changes the twist by $2\pi$.
