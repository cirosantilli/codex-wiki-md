# Product of scalar superfields

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar superfield transformation](scalar-superfield-transformation.md)

A product of scalar [superfields](superfield.md) is a scalar [superfield](superfield.md): the pullback of a product is the product of the pullbacks at the same transformed [superspace](superspace.md) point. Infinitesimally, the even supersymmetry variation obeys $\delta(ST)=(\delta S)T+S(\delta T)$. This follows from the [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) for the odd [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md), after including their odd parameters.
