# Coordinate support in a free module

↑ **Parent:** [Basis of a module](basis-of-a-module.md)

For an element $m=\sum_{e\in E}r_ee$ of a [free module](free-module.md) with specified [basis of a module](basis-of-a-module.md) $E$, its coordinate support is the finite set of [basis of a module](basis-of-a-module.md) elements having nonzero coefficients. This is well-defined by uniqueness of the expression. The coordinate support of a sum is contained in the union of the two coordinate supports, and scalar multiplication cannot enlarge it. It is distinct from the prime-ideal-valued [support of a module](support-of-a-module.md).
