# Wax

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

A class of organic materials that are solid or plastic at ordinary temperatures and melt on heating. Candle waxes can include [hydrocarbons](hydrocarbon.md), esters and mixtures; [paraffin wax](paraffin-wax.md) is one example.
