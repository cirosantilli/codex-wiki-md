# Closed ball

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)

In a [metric space](metric-space.md) $(M,d)$, the closed ball centred at $x$ of radius $r\geq0$ is $\{y\in M:d(x,y)\leq r\}$. It is a [closed set](closed-set.md), since the distance to $x$ is a [continuous](continuous-function.md) [function](function-split.md). Its boundary need not be the whole corresponding distance sphere in an arbitrary [metric space](metric-space.md).
