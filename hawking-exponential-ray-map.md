# Hawking exponential ray map

↑ **Parent:** [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md)

The late outgoing rays from a nonextremal collapsing [black hole](black-hole.md) have an exponential relation between a regular early affine null coordinate $U$ and late retarded time $u$. Logarithmic divergence of the [tortoise coordinate](tortoise-coordinate.md) at the horizon gives $U_H-U=Ae^{-\kappa u}$ up to subleading terms. Backward propagation turns $e^{-i\omega u}$ into $(U_H-U)^{i\omega/\kappa}$; its positive- and negative-frequency Fourier parts give a thermal [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md).
