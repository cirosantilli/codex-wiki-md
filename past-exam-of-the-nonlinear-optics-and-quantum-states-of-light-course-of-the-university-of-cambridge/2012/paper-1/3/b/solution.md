<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a second-order response, $P_i^{(2)}(\omega_3)=\epsilon_0\sum_{jk}\chi_{ijk}^{(2)}(\omega_3;\omega_1,\omega_2)E_j(\omega_1)E_k(\omega_2)$, with the appropriate [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) conservation and negative-frequency convention for difference-frequency processes. Interchanging the two input field-frequency pairs gives [intrinsic permutation symmetry of nonlinear susceptibility](../../../../../intrinsic-permutation-symmetry-of-nonlinear-susceptibility.md):

$$
\chi_{ijk}^{(2)}(\omega_3;\omega_1,\omega_2)
=\chi_{ikj}^{(2)}(\omega_3;\omega_2,\omega_1).
$$

For [second-harmonic generation](../../../../../second-harmonic-generation.md) the input frequencies agree, so the last two Cartesian indices are symmetric and can be contracted into six input-pair labels. This reduces the unrestricted 27 Cartesian entries to at most 18 before crystal symmetries are imposed.

Crystal point-group transformations must leave the material tensor invariant and can [force](../../../../../force-split.md) entries to vanish or become related. Under spatial inversion both [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) and [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) reverse, whereas the field product is even. A centrosymmetric bulk therefore has zero electric-dipole $\chi^{(2)}$. Surfaces and interfaces can break [inversion symmetry](../../../../../inversion-symmetry.md), and higher-multipole processes are outside that simple prohibition.

[Kleinman symmetry](../../../../../kleinman-symmetry.md) is a stronger approximate permutation symmetry used sufficiently far from resonances when dispersion can be neglected over the participating frequencies. It is not justified merely because two input frequencies are equal; resonant or appreciably dispersive media need the full frequency-dependent relations. These distinctions determine which [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) and propagation geometries have nonzero effective coupling, in addition to whether they satisfy [phase matching](../../../../../phase-matching.md).[1962, symmetry of the nonlinear dielectric response](../../../../../nonlinear-dielectric-polarization-in-optical-media-by-d-a-kleinman.md)
