# Separated solution

↑ **Parent:** [Separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md)

A [solution of a partial differential equation](solution-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) expressed as a product of [functions](function-split.md) of separate groups of variables. Substitution reduces many [linear partial differential equations](linear-partial-differential-equation.md) to [ordinary differential equations](ordinary-differential-equation.md); [superposition](superposition-principle.md) then combines the resulting [normal modes](normal-mode.md).
