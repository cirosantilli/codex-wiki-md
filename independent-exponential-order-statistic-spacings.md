# Independent exponential order-statistic spacings

↑ **Parent:** [Order statistic](order-statistic.md)

For $n$ [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) rate-$\lambda$ [exponential random variables](exponential-distribution.md), let $M_0=0$ and $M_1<\cdots<M_n$ be their [order statistics](order-statistic.md). The gaps $D_i=M_i-M_{i-1}$ are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) and have rates $(n-i+1)\lambda$. After the first clock rings, the [memoryless property](memorylessness-of-the-exponential-distribution.md) makes the surviving clocks fresh independent exponentials, independently of the elapsed minimum and the winning label. Induction gives the result. Equivalently, the ordered [joint probability density](joint-probability-density.md) is $n!\lambda^n\exp[-\lambda\sum_i m_i]$ on $0<m_1<\cdots<m_n$; changing to gaps gives a product of exponential densities because $\sum_i m_i=\sum_i(n-i+1)D_i$.
