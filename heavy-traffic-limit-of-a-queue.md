# Heavy-traffic limit of a queue

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling limit of a queue](scaling-limit-of-a-queue.md)

For fixed [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) arrivals of mean $\mu$ and positive finite [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, put service at $\mu+\varepsilon d$. Rescaling net input by space $\varepsilon$ and time $\varepsilon^{-2}$ gives [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) with drift $-d$ by the [functional central limit theorem](donsker-s-theorem.md). The continuous [Skorokhod reflection map on the half-line](skorokhod-reflection-map-on-the-half-line.md) gives the displayed transient limit, with a correspondingly scaled initial workload. A stationary approximation additionally requires justification of limit interchange. When valid, the [stationary law of negatively drifted reflected Brownian motion](stationary-law-of-negatively-drifted-reflected-brownian-motion.md) predicts $\mathbb P(Q_\varepsilon>b/\varepsilon)\approx e^{-2db/\sigma^2}$.
