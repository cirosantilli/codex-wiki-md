# Bloch ball

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md)

The qubit [density operators](density-matrix.md) $\rho=(I+\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)/2$ correspond exactly to the closed unit ball: their [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $(1\pm|\mathbf s|)/2$, so positivity is equivalent to $|\mathbf s|\leq1$. The boundary is the [Bloch sphere](bloch-sphere.md) of pure states, and the centre is $I/2$. A [depolarizing channel](quantum-depolarizing-channel.md) scales this ball isotropically, possibly with a sign reversal in the larger completely positive parameter interval.
