# Ridge-ridge-transform triple junction

↑ **Parent:** [Triple junction](triple-junction.md)

For pure normal symmetric spreading, the two ridge constraints are [perpendicular bisectors](perpendicular-bisector.md) of two edges of the [plate velocity triangle](plate-velocity-triangle.md). The transform constraint lies along the third edge. For a nondegenerate triangle their common intersection exists only when the angle opposite that third edge is a right angle; the junction [velocity](velocity.md) is then the midpoint of the transform edge.
