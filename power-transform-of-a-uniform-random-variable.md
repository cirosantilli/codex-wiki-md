# Power transform of a uniform random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md)

The [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) of $U^a$ on $[0,1]$ is $z^{1/a}$. Differentiating gives density $z^{1/a-1}/a$ on $(0,1)$, which is the [beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) with parameters $(1/a,1)$. This also gives a simple instance of [inverse transform sampling](inverse-transform-sampling.md): to sample a density $\alpha z^{\alpha-1}$ on $(0,1)$, raise a uniform draw to the power $1/\alpha$.
