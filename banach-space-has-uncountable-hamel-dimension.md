# Banach space has uncountable Hamel dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md)

No infinite-dimensional [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) has a countable [Hamel basis](basis.md). If $(e_n)$ were such a basis, the space would be the countable union of the finite-dimensional subspaces $\operatorname{span}(e_1,\ldots,e_n)$. Each is closed and has empty interior, contradicting the [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md).
