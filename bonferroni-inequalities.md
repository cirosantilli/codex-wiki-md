# Bonferroni inequalities

↑ **Parent:** [Inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonferroni_inequalities)

Truncating the [inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) after an odd number of terms gives an upper bound, and truncating after an even number gives a lower bound. In particular,

$$
\mathbb P\left(\bigcup_iA_i\right)
\geq\sum_i\mathbb P(A_i)-\sum_{i<j}\mathbb P(A_i\cap A_j).
$$
