# Sedimentary facies

↑ **Parent:** [Sedimentology](sedimentology.md)

A distinguishable set of properties of a [sedimentary deposit](sedimentary-deposit.md), including composition, grain size, structures and biological contents, interpreted in relation to its depositional environment. Spatial and vertical changes among [sedimentary facies](sedimentary-facies.md) help reconstruct a [paleoenvironment](paleoenvironment.md); no single property invariably identifies a unique setting.
