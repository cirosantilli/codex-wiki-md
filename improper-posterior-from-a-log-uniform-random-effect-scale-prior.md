# Improper posterior from a log-uniform random-effect scale prior

↑ **Parent:** [Improper prior](improper-prior.md)

A random-effect model with positive marginal likelihood at scale zero cannot yield a proper [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) under a prior proportional to the reciprocal scale near zero. On a compact set of the other parameters, continuity and positivity give a positive likelihood lower bound, while $\int_0^\varepsilon d\lambda/\lambda=\infty$. Individual full [conditional distributions](conditional-distribution.md) can still be proper, so their existence alone does not justify a [Gibbs sampler](gibbs-sampler.md) with a probability distribution as its joint target.
