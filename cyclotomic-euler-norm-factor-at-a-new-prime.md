# Cyclotomic Euler norm factor at a new prime

↑ **Parent:** [Root-of-unity Euler system](root-of-unity-euler-system.md)

Let $q\nmid m$ be outside the excluded primes, let $\zeta$ have order $m$, and let $\rho$ be a primitive qth [root of unity](root-of-unity.md). Galois equivariance identifies the conjugates with $\Phi(\rho^i\zeta)$ for $1\leq i\leq q-1$. The distribution axiom includes the missing $i=0$ factor, so the [field norm](field-norm.md) is $\Phi(\zeta^q)/\Phi(\zeta)$. Arithmetic [Frobenius](frobenius-automorphism.md) sends $\zeta$ to $\zeta^q$, giving the displayed factor. At higher powers of an already ramified prime all q translates occur instead. These are different norm relations; norm compatibility in just a p-power tower does not supply the auxiliary-prime relation by itself.
