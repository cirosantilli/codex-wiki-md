# Oral candidiasis

↑ **Parent:** [Candidiasis](candidiasis.md)

An opportunistic fungal [infection](infection.md) of the oral mucosa, historically called [Muguet](oral-candidiasis.md). White plaques are distinct from an [aphthous ulcer](aphthous-ulcer.md).
