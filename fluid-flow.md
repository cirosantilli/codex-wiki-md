# Fluid flow

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md) is motion described by a [velocity field](velocity-field.md) $\mathbf u(\mathbf x,t)$. The trajectories of [fluid elements](fluid-element.md) satisfy $d\mathbf x/dt=\mathbf u(\mathbf x,t)$; the [mass density](density.md) and other fields obey their [conservation laws](conservation-law.md) and [constitutive equations](constitutive-equation.md). This physical motion differs from a [flow](flow.md) on a [flow network](flow-network-split.md).
