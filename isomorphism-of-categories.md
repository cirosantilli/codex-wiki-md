# Isomorphism of categories

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence of categories](equivalence-of-categories.md)

An isomorphism of [categories](category-split.md) is a [functor](functor.md) with a strictly inverse [functor](functor.md). It is equivalently bijective on objects and on each hom-set. An [equivalence of categories](equivalence-of-categories.md) only requires inverse composites up to invertible [natural transformations](natural-transformation.md). For example, the [category of partial functions](category-of-partial-functions.md) and the [category of pointed sets](category-of-pointed-sets.md) are equivalent but their actual object collections prevent an isomorphism: the former has one [zero object](zero-object.md), the empty [set](set-split.md), while the latter has distinct singleton [pointed set](pointed-set.md) objects that are all zero objects.
