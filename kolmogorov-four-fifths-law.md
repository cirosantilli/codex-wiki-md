# Kolmogorov four-fifths law

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov equation for structure functions](kolmogorov-equation-for-structure-functions.md)

The signed third-order [longitudinal structure function](longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md) has this leading [inertial range](inertial-range.md) form under homogeneity, isotropy and the usual local-equilibrium scale separation. Its coefficient follows from the [Kármán-Howarth equation](karman-howarth-equation.md) and the mean [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) budget, without assuming the [Kolmogorov two-thirds law](kolmogorov-two-thirds-law.md). Its negative sign encodes a forward [energy cascade](energy-cascade.md); it does not establish every higher-order similarity exponent.
