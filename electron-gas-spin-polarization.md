# Electron-gas spin polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock electron gas](hartree-fock-electron-gas.md)

For number densities $n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow$, $\zeta=(n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow)/(n_\uparrow+n_\downarrow)$. Uniform [spin](spin.md) [polarization](polarization-waves.md) raises kinetic [energy](energy-split.md) by concentrating occupation in one Fermi sphere, while lowering exchange [energy](energy-split.md).
