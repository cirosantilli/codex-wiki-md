# Geostrophic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-geostrophic approximation](quasi-geostrophic-approximation.md)

A geostrophic flow is a horizontal flow in leading [geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md). Its velocity follows pressure contours rather than pointing directly down the pressure gradient.
