# Canonical residual automaton

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal deterministic finite automaton](minimal-deterministic-finite-automaton.md)

The canonical residual automaton of a [regular language](regular-language.md) has its distinct [left quotients of a formal language](left-quotient-of-a-formal-language.md) as states, initial state $L$, accepting states those containing the [empty word](empty-word.md), and letter transition $R\mapsto a^{-1}R$. It is a complete [minimal deterministic finite automaton](minimal-deterministic-finite-automaton.md); every other complete [minimal deterministic finite automaton](minimal-deterministic-finite-automaton.md) for that language is [isomorphic](isomorphism.md) to it.
