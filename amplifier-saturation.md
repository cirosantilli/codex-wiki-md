# Amplifier saturation

↑ **Parent:** [Operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md)

In [amplifier saturation](amplifier-saturation.md), the requested output lies outside the amplifier's linear operating range, such as when supply rails limit output voltage. The [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md) [virtual short](virtual-short.md) approximation then need not hold. Output swing, current drive and recovery behaviour depend on the device and load; finite [slew rate](slew-rate.md) is a separate dynamic limit even when the output has not reached a rail.
