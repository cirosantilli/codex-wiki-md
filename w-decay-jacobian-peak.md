# W-decay Jacobian peak

↑ **Parent:** [Polarized massless leptonic W decay](polarized-massless-leptonic-w-decay.md)

For an on-shell W with zero transverse recoil and [massless](massless-particle.md) [leptons](lepton.md), $p_T=(M_W/2)\sin\theta$. Mapping both angular roots to one transverse [momentum](momentum-split.md) produces an integrable inverse-square-root edge. For a normalized polar distribution $3(1-\cos\theta)^2/8$, the density in $x=2p_T/M_W$ is $3x(2-x^2)/(4\sqrt{1-x^2})$. Finite width, transverse recoil and [detector resolution](particle-detector.md) smear the edge.
