# Response variable

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)

A [response variable](response-variable.md) is the outcome whose variation is described by a statistical model or whose change is compared across [treatments](treatment.md). Its unit, measurement procedure and observation time must be specified. An outcome consisting of a feature count is one response per sketch, not one independent experimental response per feature.
