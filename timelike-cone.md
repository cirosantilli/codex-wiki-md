# Timelike cone

↑ **Parent:** [Timelike vector](timelike-vector.md)

The [timelike cone](timelike-cone.md) is the open set of nonzero [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md) with timelike squared norm. It has future and past components once a time [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) is chosen. Homogeneity extends an identity for unit [timelike vectors](timelike-vector.md) to this whole open set. In particular, if a symmetric [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) vanishes on every [timelike vector](timelike-vector.md), its polynomial vanishes on an open set and therefore vanishes identically. This observation proves the converse from [geodesic](geodesic.md) first integrals to the Killing equation.
