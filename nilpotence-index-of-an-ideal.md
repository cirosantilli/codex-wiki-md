# Nilpotence index of an ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent ideal](nilpotent-ideal.md)

For a [nilpotent ideal](nilpotent-ideal.md) $I$, its [nilpotence index of an ideal](nilpotence-index-of-an-ideal.md) is the least positive integer $n$ for which $I^n=0$. In $k[X]/(g^e)$, with $g$ an [irreducible polynomial](irreducible-polynomial.md), the unique [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) $(g)$ has index $e$. In a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) with $p=u\pi^e$, the [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) of its reduction modulo $p$ has the same index. Comparing these indices under a [quotient ring](quotient-ring.md) isomorphism recovers the ramification exponent in the [Kummer-Dedekind theorem](kummer-dedekind-theorem.md).
