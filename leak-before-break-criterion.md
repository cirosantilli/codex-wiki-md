# Leak-before-break criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Fracture toughness](fracture-toughness.md)

A requirement that a crack penetrate a pressure boundary and produce a detectable leak before unstable fracture. A simple screening comparison uses critical crack size versus wall thickness, but the result depends on crack geometry and on whether [stress](stress.md) or pressure is held fixed.
