# Integration limit

↑ **Parent:** [Definite integral](definite-integral.md)

The lower and upper integration limits $a,b$ specify the ordered endpoints of a [definite integral](definite-integral.md) $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$. Interchanging the [integration limits](integration-limit.md) reverses its sign: $\int_b^a f(x)\,dx=-\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$.
