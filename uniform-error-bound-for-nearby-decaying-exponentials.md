# Uniform error bound for nearby decaying exponentials

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

For positive rates $a,b$, the [mean value theorem](mean-value-theorem.md) in the rate gives a bound by $|a-b|x e^{-\min(a,b)x}$. Its maximum is $|a-b|/[e\min(a,b)]$. Combining this estimate with coefficient errors bounds a finite spectral sum uniformly on a half-line, including slow scales much longer than one. Positivity of both rates is essential: unstable exponentials need a different time-window or relative-error analysis.
