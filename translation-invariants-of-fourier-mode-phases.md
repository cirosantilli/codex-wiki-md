# Translation invariants of Fourier-mode phases

↑ **Parent:** [Equivariant dynamical system](equivariant-dynamical-system.md)

If a [translation symmetry](translational-symmetry.md) acts on complex [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) by $A_j\mapsto e^{iq_j\cdot s}A_j$, a linear combination of their [phases](phase-waves.md) is invariant precisely when its [coefficient](coefficient.md) vector lies in the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of the matrix of [wavevectors](wavevector.md). For four [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) whose [wavevectors](wavevector.md) span a plane, this leaves two independent [phase](phase-waves.md) variables after quotienting translations. At zero [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md), polar [phases](phase-waves.md) are undefined, so smooth [centre manifold](center-manifold.md) equations should first be written in Cartesian [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md).
