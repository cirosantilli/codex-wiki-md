# Radiation-pressure limit of a dusty starburst

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar feedback](stellar-feedback.md)

For [flux](flux.md) $F$ and effective dust [opacity](opacity.md) $\kappa$, radiative acceleration is $\kappa F/c$. Comparing it with the gravitational acceleration gives an [Eddington luminosity](eddington-luminosity.md) condition appropriate to dusty gas. In an optically thick [starburst galaxy](starburst-galaxy.md), feedback can limit the [star formation rate](star-formation-rate.md) per area and drive a [galactic outflow](galactic-outflow.md), while the gas supply limits the duration. A surface-density limit is not a universal ceiling on total [star formation rate](star-formation-rate.md): the star-forming area matters. [Radiation-supported starburst disk calculations](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0503027) give characteristic optically thick infrared fluxes near $10^{13}L_\odot\,\mathrm{kpc}^{-2}$.
