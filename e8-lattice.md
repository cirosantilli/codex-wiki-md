# E8 lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Even unimodular lattice](even-unimodular-lattice.md)

The positive-definite rank-eight [even unimodular lattice](even-unimodular-lattice.md) can be realized as vectors in $\mathbb Z^8$ or $(\mathbb Z+1/2)^8$ whose coordinate sum is even, with the ordinary Euclidean inner product. Negating this form gives $E_8(-1)$ in the [K3 intersection lattice](k3-intersection-lattice.md).
