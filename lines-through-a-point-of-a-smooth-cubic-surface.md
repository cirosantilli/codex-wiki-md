# Lines through a point of a smooth cubic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth cubic surface](smooth-cubic-surface.md)

Every [projective line](projective-line.md) through $P$ on a [smooth cubic surface](smooth-cubic-surface.md) lies in its [projective tangent plane](projective-tangent-plane.md). Each is a distinct linear factor of the cubic [plane section](plane-section.md). There can therefore be at most three. Equality occurs when the tangent section consists of three concurrent lines.
