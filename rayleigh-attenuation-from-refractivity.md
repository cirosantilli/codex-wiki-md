# Rayleigh attenuation from refractivity

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh scattering](rayleigh-scattering.md)

In a dilute nonmagnetic medium of isotropic independent polarizable particles, $n-1\simeq N\alpha/(2\epsilon_0)$ and the [Rayleigh scattering](rayleigh-scattering.md) cross-section is $8\pi^3\alpha^2/(3\epsilon_0^2\lambda^4)$. Eliminating [polarizability](electric-polarizability.md) and setting direct-beam attenuation coefficient to $N\sigma$ gives the title relation. It describes scattering out of a narrow beam, not absorption of all radiant [energy](energy-split.md). It assumes fixed [number density](number-density.md), negligible [correlations](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md)/local-field corrections and a scalar [polarizability](electric-polarizability.md); molecular anisotropy, aerosols and stratification require additional information.
