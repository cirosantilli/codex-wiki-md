# Western blot transfer control

↑ **Parent:** [Western blot controls](western-blot-controls.md)

A [Western blot transfer control](western-blot-transfer-control.md) tests retention after [Western blot transfer](western-blot-transfer.md). Total-[protein](protein-split.md) staining of the [Western blot membrane](western-blot-membrane.md) can reveal uneven lanes or blank patches; staining the gel after transfer tests what remained behind. Transferred reference bands check selected positions, not recovery of every target. A pretransfer loading image alone cannot establish the amount retained on the receiving sheet.
