<h1 id="radial-schrodinger-equation-for-a-central-potential">Radial Schrödinger equation for a central potential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md)

For a spherically symmetric [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $V(r)$, [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) with $\psi=R(r)Y_{lm}(\theta,\phi)$ reduces the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) to the displayed radial equation. Writing $u=rR$ instead removes the first [derivative](derivative.md):

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}u''+
\left[V(r)+\frac{\hbar^2l(l+1)}{2mr^2}\right]u=Eu.
$$

The angular contribution acts as a centrifugal barrier. Boundary behavior and [wavefunction normalization](wavefunction-normalization.md) must still be imposed; an arbitrary radial function is not an [energy eigenfunction](energy-eigenfunction.md).
