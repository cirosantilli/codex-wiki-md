# Q value

↑ **Parent:** [Energy](energy-split.md)

The [Q value](q-value.md) of a reaction or [particle decay](particle-decay.md) is the initial total rest [energy](energy-split.md) minus the final total rest [energy](energy-split.md). Positive $Q$ releases [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md); a negative $Q$ requires energy supplied by incident motion or another source. Atomic and bare nuclear [mass](mass.md) conventions must be distinguished when counting [electrons](electron.md) in [beta decay](beta-decay.md).
