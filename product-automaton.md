# Product automaton

↑ **Parent:** [Deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md)

A [deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md) whose [automaton state](automaton-state.md) is a pair of [automaton states](automaton-state.md) of two given [deterministic finite automata](deterministic-finite-automaton.md), updated componentwise by each input symbol. Choosing accepting pairs by a [Boolean function](boolean-function.md) on component acceptance realizes [intersection](set-intersection.md), [union](set-union.md), or [set difference](set-difference.md) of the recognized [regular languages](regular-language.md).
