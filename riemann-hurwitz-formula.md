# Riemann-Hurwitz formula

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann–Hurwitz_formula)

For a degree-$d$ holomorphic map, $2g_X-2=d(2g_Y-2)+\sum(e_p-1)$.

Let $f:X\to Y$ be a nonconstant [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) between [compact Riemann surfaces](compact-riemann-surface.md) that are [connected spaces](connected-space.md), without boundary. Write $g_X,g_Y$ for their [genera](genus-of-a-surface.md) and $d\geq1$ for the [degree of a holomorphic map](degree-of-a-holomorphic-map.md). With [analytic ramification index](ramification-index-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) $e_p$ at each $p\in X$, the formula is

$$
2g_X-2=d(2g_Y-2)+\sum_{p\in X}(e_p-1),\qquad
\chi(X)=d\chi(Y)-\sum_{p\in X}(e_p-1).
$$

Here $\chi=2-2g$ is the [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md). Only finitely many [ramification points](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) contribute. The [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) is automatically [surjective](surjective-function.md); the [valency theorem](valency-theorem.md) gives $\sum_{p\in f^{-1}(y)}e_p=d$ even at a [branch value of a holomorphic map](branch-value-of-a-holomorphic-map.md). Constant maps are excluded.[Lecture 5, Theorems 5.1 and 5.3; Definition 5.16 and Theorem 5.17, printed pp. 16-19](riemann-surfaces-by-constantin-teleman-lent-term-2003.md)

The useful interpretation is that [total ramification of a holomorphic map](total-ramification-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) measures how much the source [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) falls short of $d$ copies of the target. The [ramification of a power map of the Riemann sphere](ramification-of-a-power-map-of-the-riemann-sphere.md) spends that entire correction at two points; the [double cover of the sphere with four branch values](double-cover-of-the-sphere-with-four-branch-values.md) spends it at four points and produces a [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-one surface.
