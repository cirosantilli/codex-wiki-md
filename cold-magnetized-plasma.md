# Cold magnetized plasma

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

A [plasma](plasma-physics.md) whose linear [wave](wave-split.md) response retains the equilibrium [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and particle inertia but neglects [pressure](pressure-split.md) forces, thermal dispersion and [collisions](collision.md). Its applicability requires $kv_{\mathrm{th},s}\ll|\omega|$ and separate care near [cyclotron resonances](cyclotron-resonance.md).
