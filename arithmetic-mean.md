# Arithmetic mean

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted arithmetic mean](weighted-arithmetic-mean.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_mean)

The arithmetic mean of $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ is

$$
\bar x=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n x_i.
$$

It is the [weighted mean](weighted-arithmetic-mean.md) with equal weights.
