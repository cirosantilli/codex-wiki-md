# Constraint dependence of longitudinal and transverse radiation comparisons

↑ **Parent:** [Liénard radiated-power formula](lienard-radiated-power-formula.md)

For one particle at a fixed [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md), the [Liénard radiated-power formula](lienard-radiated-power-formula.md) gives $P_\parallel=C_q\gamma^6a_\parallel^2$ and $P_\perp=C_q\gamma^4a_\perp^2$, where $C_q=q^2/(6\pi\epsilon_0c^3)$. Consequently equal coordinate [acceleration](acceleration.md) magnitudes make transverse motion radiate less by $\gamma^2$. Equal applied laboratory [force](force-split.md) magnitudes instead give $a_\parallel=F/(m\gamma^3)$ and $a_\perp=F/(m\gamma)$, so $P_\perp/P_\parallel=\gamma^2$. These opposite orderings concern distinct constraints and are consistent. An [energy](energy-split.md) budget without a time, length, [force](force-split.md), or [acceleration](acceleration.md) constraint cannot by itself select a unique minimum-loss accelerator design.
