# Geodynamic modelling

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

Quantitative simulation of evolving planetary deformation, flow and thermal or compositional structure under specified physical laws and constitutive assumptions. Feasibility of a regime does not supply its historical onset date.
