# Binomial exponential-utility terminal wealth

↑ **Parent:** [Constant absolute risk aversion utility](constant-absolute-risk-aversion-utility.md)

For unrestricted final [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md) in an $n$-period [binomial market](discrete-time-binomial-market.md), write $R$ for the bank growth factor, $a>0$ for absolute risk aversion, and $Z=dQ/dP$ for the [binomial-market probability density](binomial-market-probability-density.md). The displayed optimizer has the [state-price budget constraint](state-price-budget-constraint.md) $\mathbb E_QH^*=R^nw_0$. Its [exponential utility](constant-absolute-risk-aversion-utility.md) derivative satisfies $e^{-aH^*}=\lambda Z$ for a constant $\lambda>0$. The [concave supporting-tangent inequality](concave-supporting-tangent-inequality.md) bounds any competing [expected utility](expected-utility.md) by this value, because the expected linear error is $\lambda\mathbb E_Q(H-H^*)=0$. Strict [concavity](concave-function.md) proves uniqueness, and [market completeness](complete-market.md) supplies replication. The formula is altered by a constraint requiring nonnegative final wealth. With physical up probability $p$ and [risk-neutral probability](risk-neutral-probability.md) $q$, $\mathbb E_Q\log Z=n[q\log(q/p)+(1-q)\log((1-q)/(1-p))]$.
