# Antibody affinity

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)

[Antibody affinity](antibody-affinity.md) concerns one [paratope](paratope.md) binding one [epitope](epitope.md). A [Fab fragment](fragment-antigen-binding.md) supplies a single-site comparison. [Immunobiology, 5th edition, Glossary: affinity, single-site binding](immunobiology-the-immune-system-in-health-and-disease-5th-edition-by-charles-a-janeway-jr-and-others/glossary.md) In the dilute [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) model below, smaller [dissociation constant](dissociation-constant.md) $K_d$ means larger [antibody equilibrium occupancy](antibody-equilibrium-occupancy.md) at the same free [antigen](antigen.md) [concentration](concentration.md). State the [antigen](antigen.md), [temperature](temperature.md) and [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md): a [dissociation constant](dissociation-constant.md) is not a context-free score. It does not alone specify how fast attachment or detachment occurs.
