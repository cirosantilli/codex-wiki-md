# Primary planetary atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmosphere](exoplanet-atmosphere.md)

A primary planetary atmosphere is a retained gas envelope acquired from the protoplanetary nebula, commonly rich in [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) and [helium](helium.md). The solar-system giant planets retain such envelopes, while escape and subsequent processing can substantially change their composition.
