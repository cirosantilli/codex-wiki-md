# Maxwell Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md)

The source-free electromagnetic [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) is quadratic in the [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md). Its [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is $\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=0$. Antisymmetry of $F$ makes the density invariant under [gauge transformations](gauge-transformation.md), and contraction of its indices makes it a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md).
