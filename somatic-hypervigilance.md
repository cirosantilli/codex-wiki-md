# Somatic hypervigilance

↑ **Parent:** [Self-focused attention](self-focused-attention.md)

Sustained monitoring of bodily sensations for signs of danger. Monitoring can change which sensations are noticed and how they are interpreted; its proposed maintaining role must be tested rather than used to dismiss the original sensation.
