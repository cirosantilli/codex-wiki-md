The [Thomson charge-to-mass experiment](thomson-charge-to-mass-experiment.md) used electric and magnetic deflections of [cathode rays](cathode-ray.md) to investigate their charged constituents. It determines a [charge-to-mass ratio](charge-to-mass-ratio.md); separating [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) from [mass](mass.md) requires additional information.
