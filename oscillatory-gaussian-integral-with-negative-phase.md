# Oscillatory Gaussian integral with negative phase

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel integral](fresnel-integral.md)

For $t>0$, contour rotation of the [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) gives

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-itu^2}du
=e^{-i\pi/4}\sqrt{\frac\pi t}.
$$
