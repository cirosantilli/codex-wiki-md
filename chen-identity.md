# Chen identity

↑ **Parent:** [Signature of a bounded variation path](signature-of-a-bounded-variation-path.md)

Partition each ordered integration simplex according to the number of times before the concatenation point. The integral over each resulting region factors into an initial iterated integral and a final iterated integral. Summing these factorizations proves the identity in the [truncated tensor algebra](truncated-tensor-algebra.md).
