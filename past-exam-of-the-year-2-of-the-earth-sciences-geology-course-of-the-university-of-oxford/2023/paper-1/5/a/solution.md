<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [gravity anomaly](../../../../../gravity-anomaly.md) is measured [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md), reduced to a stated reference surface and compared with a reference [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) field. A local geological interpretation further removes or models the regional background. An absolute negative [Bouguer gravity anomaly](../../../../../bouguer-gravity-anomaly.md) is therefore not itself a local negative [geophysical density contrast](../../../../../geophysical-density-contrast.md).

Measure relative [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) with a calibrated [gravimeter](../../../../../gravimeter.md) at stations across the profile, tie it to a base station, repeat base readings to estimate instrumental drift, and measure station coordinates and accurate elevations, for example by [GNSS](../../../../../gnss.md) and levelling. Record observation times for tide corrections. Subtract the latitude-dependent reference [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md), correct instrument drift and Earth tides, add the [free-air correction](../../../../../free-air-correction.md) for station height, and subtract the attraction of the reference-density [Bouguer slab](../../../../../bouguer-slab.md). A terrain correction accounts for relief missing from or exceeding that slab. Use a consistent [density](../../../../../density.md) such as $2670\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ for the topographic correction.[Gravity anomaly reduction: free-air, simple-Bouguer and complete-Bouguer corrections](../../../../../geophysical-data-grids-for-the-conterminous-united-states-by-united-states-geological-survey.md)

The question's reference to the anomaly in panel b is a panel-label slip: the upper plot contains [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md), while the lower plot supplies geometry and elevations. A geological slab thickness is the vertical thickness of the anomalous body, not simply its height above sea level.
