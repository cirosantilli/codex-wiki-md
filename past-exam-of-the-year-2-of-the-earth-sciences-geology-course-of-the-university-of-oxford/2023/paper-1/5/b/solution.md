<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a laterally extensive [Bouguer slab](../../../../../bouguer-slab.md) of thickness $h$ and [geophysical density contrast](../../../../../geophysical-density-contrast.md) $\Delta\rho$,

$$
\Delta g=2\pi G\Delta\rho h,\qquad \rho_{\rm gs}=2670+\frac{\Delta g}{2\pi Gh}.
$$

The [greenstone belt](../../../../../greenstone-belt.md) reaches from roughly $+1.0$ to $-0.5\ \mathrm{km}$ near its deepest central part, so use $h\simeq1500\ \mathrm m$. The [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) maximum is approximately $-100\ \mathrm{mGal}$; the exposed host-rock background in the south is approximately $-115\ \mathrm{mGal}$. Taking this as a constant regional reference gives a local excess $\Delta g\simeq15\ \mathrm{mGal}=1.5\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\rho_{\rm gs}\simeq2670+\frac{1.5\times10^{-4}}{2\pi(6.6743\times10^{-11})(1500)}\simeq2.91\times10^3\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}}.
$$

This is an approximate [density](../../../../../density.md) under a constant-background, infinite-slab model. The finite width, variable thickness and neighboring [pluton](../../../../../pluton.md) require a fuller [gravity inversion](../../../../../gravity-inversion.md) for a precise value. In particular, the absolute value $-100\ \mathrm{mGal}$ must not be inserted as the local excess.
