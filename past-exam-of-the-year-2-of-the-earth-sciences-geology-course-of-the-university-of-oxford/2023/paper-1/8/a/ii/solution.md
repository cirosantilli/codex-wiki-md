↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [chlorite](../../../../../../chlorite.md)–[kaolinite](../../../../../../kaolinite.md) reaction, take water and both solids to have unit [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md). Its [reaction quotient](../../../../../../reaction-quotient.md) is $Q=a_{\mathrm{Mg}^{2+}}^5a_{\mathrm{H_4SiO_4}}/a_{\mathrm H^+}^{10}$. Therefore

$$
5y+x=61.14,\qquad \boxed{y=12.228-0.20x}.
$$

This line has a shallower negative slope than the [chlorite](../../../../../../chlorite.md)–[gibbsite](../../../../../../gibbsite.md) line, with points $(0,12.228)$ and $(-5,13.228)$. Plot both against predominantly negative $x$, because the dissolved silica [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) is generally below one.
