<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Trace the [strike-slip fault](../../../../../../strike-slip-fault.md) along the southwest–northeast valley, approximately through $(u,v)=(0.12,0.83),(0.28,0.75),(0.44,0.68),(0.53,0.62),(0.65,0.42),(0.83,0.20),(0.97,0.04)$. The long oblique strand is interpreted as **[left-lateral strike-slip faulting](../../../../../../left-lateral-strike-slip-faulting.md)**: draw the southeastern side moving northeast and the northwestern side moving southwest, parallel to the trace.

This sense is consistent with horizontal north–south maximum compression: for an approximately northeast-striking [fault](../../../../../../geological-fault.md), the resolved [shear stress](../../../../../../shear-stress.md) moves the southeastern side northeastward relative to the northwestern side. The deflected central drainage supports that interpretation. The regional [stress](../../../../../../stress.md) direction supplies a consistency check rather than a uniquely precise trace location; follow the geomorphic break rather than drawing a single straight line through every coordinate.
