<h1 id="3/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Put the [reverse fault](../../../../../../reverse-fault.md) along the mountain-front trace identified in part (ii), with **the mountainous southern/southwestern block as [hanging wall](../../../../../../hanging-wall.md) and the central basin to its north/northeast as [footwall](../../../../../../footwall.md)**. Draw teeth on the southern [hanging wall](../../../../../../hanging-wall.md) side of the line. In the simplest cross-section the [fault](../../../../../../geological-fault.md) dips south and the southern block moves northward and up over the basin edge. The image constrains the surface trace and relative uplift more securely than its subsurface [fault dip](../../../../../../fault-dip.md); the precise dip cannot be measured from this map alone.
