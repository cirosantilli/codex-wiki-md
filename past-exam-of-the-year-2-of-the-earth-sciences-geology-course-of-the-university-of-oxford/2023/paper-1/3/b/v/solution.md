↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The network partitions deformation into oblique horizontal displacement on the [strike-slip fault](../../../../../../strike-slip-fault.md) and more directly north–south [crustal shortening](../../../../../../crustal-shortening.md) on the approximately east–west [reverse fault](../../../../../../reverse-fault.md). On the oblique strand, northeastward motion of the southeastern side relative to the northwestern side has a northward component, reducing separation in the direction of $\sigma_1$. On the [reverse fault](../../../../../../reverse-fault.md), northward and upward motion of the southern [hanging wall](../../../../../../hanging-wall.md) shortens and thickens the crust.

Sketch north–south opposing compression arrows around the plan view, left-lateral arrows along the oblique trace, and teeth on the southern side of the [reverse fault](../../../../../../reverse-fault.md) trace. A section normal to the latter shows horizontal shortening converted into [tectonic uplift](../../../../../../tectonic-uplift.md). Where strands meet, [folding](../../../../../../folding.md), distributed [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) or subsidiary [faulting](../../../../../../faulting.md) can maintain displacement compatibility; a [strike-slip fault](../../../../../../strike-slip-fault.md) terminating abruptly in undeforming rock would not. This is an interpretation involving [transpression](../../../../../../transpression.md), and the map does not by itself give a unique partition of slip rates between the strands.
