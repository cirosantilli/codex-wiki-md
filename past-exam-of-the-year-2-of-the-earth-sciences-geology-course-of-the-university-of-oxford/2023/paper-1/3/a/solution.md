<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use calibrated [multispectral imaging](../../../../../multispectral-imaging.md) of reflected sunlight, with visible, near-infrared and short-wave-infrared bands. A satellite records [radiance](../../../../../radiance.md) in several passbands for each ground pixel. Perform geometric registration and [atmospheric correction](../../../../../atmospheric-correction.md) to estimate [spectral reflectance](../../../../../spectral-reflectance.md), then account for illumination differences on slopes and mask cloud, water and dense vegetation. A [false-colour composite](../../../../../false-colour-composite.md), diagnostic band ratios or a trained [statistical classification](../../../../../statistical-classification.md) can separate surface [lithologies](../../../../../lithology.md); field observations and representative spectra validate any mapped contact.

Different [minerals](../../../../../mineral.md) have different absorption features: iron-bearing materials affect visible/near-infrared [spectral reflectance](../../../../../spectral-reflectance.md), while hydroxyl-bearing [clay minerals](../../../../../clay-mineral.md) and carbonate-bearing rocks can differ in short-wave-infrared bands. Adjacent patches with reproducibly different spectra and coherent geological boundaries are candidates for [lithological contacts](../../../../../lithological-contact.md). This is particularly useful in dry, exposed terrain. Vegetation, soil cover, similar rock compositions, weathering and topographic shadow can hide or mimic a contact; a colour boundary alone is insufficient.

Choose [spatial resolution](../../../../../spatial-resolution.md) appreciably finer than the narrowest unit being mapped, with several pixels across that unit. Approximately $10$–$30\ \mathrm m$ is suitable for regional contacts separated by hundreds of metres; metre-scale units require finer imagery or field work. [Sentinel-2](../../../../../sentinel-2.md) provides $10$–$20\ \mathrm m$ bands useful for this purpose, while the reflected-light multispectral bands of [Landsat](../../../../../landsat.md) are typically $30\ \mathrm m$. Broad multispectral passbands distinguish many rock assemblages but cannot resolve every narrow [mineral](../../../../../mineral.md) absorption feature; [hyperspectral imaging](../../../../../hyperspectral-imaging.md) is preferable when those features are essential.
