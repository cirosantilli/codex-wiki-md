<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a heterogeneous [fault slip](../../../../../fault-slip.md) distribution, [seismic moment](../../../../../seismic-moment.md) is

$$
M_0=G\int_A D\,dA=GA\overline D,
$$

where $G=30\times10^9\ \mathrm{Pa}$ is the [shear modulus](../../../../../shear-modulus.md), $A$ the [fault plane](../../../../../fault-plane.md) area, and $\overline D$ the area-averaged [fault slip](../../../../../fault-slip.md). The peak colour cannot be used as $\overline D$: most of the illustrated [fault plane](../../../../../fault-plane.md) has much smaller [fault slip](../../../../../fault-slip.md).

One reproducible graphical estimate divides the approximately $190\ \mathrm{km}$ long and $40\ \mathrm{km}$ down-dip panel into seven $25\ \mathrm{km}$ strips and a final $15\ \mathrm{km}$ strip. Reading area averages from the colour scale, from southwest to northeast, gives roughly $1.7,2.7,3.1,1.8,1.9,1.7,1.0,0.3\ \mathrm m$. This includes the pale, nearly unslipped lower part of each strip. Hence

$$
\int_A D\,dA\simeq40\{25(1.7+2.7+3.1+1.8+1.9+1.7+1.0)+15(0.3)\}\ \mathrm{km^2\,m}\simeq1.4\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{m^3},
$$

and **$M_0\simeq4.2\times10^{20}\ \mathrm{N\,m}$**. A reasonable graphical range is approximately $3$–$6\times10^{20}\ \mathrm{N\,m}$, dominated by colour averaging and the down-dip extent of small [fault slip](../../../../../fault-slip.md). The corresponding [moment magnitude](../../../../../moment-magnitude.md) is about $M_w=7.7$, using $M_w=\frac23(\log_{10}(M_0/\mathrm{N\,m})-9.1)$. This is an estimate from the supplied distribution, rather than a substitution of a catalogued [earthquake](../../../../../earthquake.md) magnitude.
