↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The star is near the southwestern end of the main [fault slip](../../../../../fault-slip.md) patch, so a much longer part of the [finite-source rupture](../../../../../finite-source-rupture.md) propagates northeastward. For comparable distances, ground conditions and buildings, **the northeastern site receives the stronger, shorter direct pulse; the southwestern site receives the weaker, longer direct shaking**. This is [rupture directivity](../../../../../rupture-directivity.md).[Explanation of focusing by successive rupture elements](../../../../../what-is-directivity-by-united-states-geological-survey.md)

For a segment of length $\ell$, [rupture velocity](../../../../../rupture-velocity.md) $v_r$ and [seismic wave](../../../../../seismic-wave.md) speed $c>v_r$, successive arrival times span approximately

$$
\Delta t_{\rm forward}=\ell(1/v_r-1/c),\qquad \Delta t_{\rm backward}=\ell(1/v_r+1/c).
$$

Forward arrivals overlap and concentrate [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) into a shorter pulse. Backward arrivals spread out. The supplied [finite-source rupture](../../../../../finite-source-rupture.md) is not perfectly unilateral, so the prediction concerns the dominant contribution. [Seismic radiation patterns](../../../../../seismic-radiation-pattern.md), [seismic attenuation](../../../../../seismic-attenuation.md), sediment amplification and building response can alter the ranking of actual damage or total coda duration.
