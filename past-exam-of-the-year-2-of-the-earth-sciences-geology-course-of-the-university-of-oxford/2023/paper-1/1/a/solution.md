↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**The [fault dips](../../../../../fault-dip.md) are approximately $90^\circ$.** The mapped senses of [fault slip](../../../../../fault-slip.md) are predominantly [strike-slip faulting](../../../../../strike-slip-faulting.md). In an [Andersonian faulting regime](../../../../../andersonian-faulting-regime.md), the intermediate [principal stress](../../../../../principal-stress.md) $\sigma_2$ is vertical and the maximum and minimum [principal stresses](../../../../../principal-stress.md) $\sigma_1,\sigma_3$ are horizontal, so the preferred [fault planes](../../../../../fault-plane.md) are vertical. Local bends can introduce [oblique-slip faulting](../../../../../oblique-slip-faulting.md) without changing this first-order interpretation.
