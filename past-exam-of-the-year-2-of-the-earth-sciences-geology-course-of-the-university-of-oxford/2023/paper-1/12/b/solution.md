<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [mass extinction](../../../../../mass-extinction.md) is driven by changes that overwhelm organisms' physiological tolerances or destroy their food and habitat systems. Distinguish the initiating forcing from the linked environmental mechanisms: one event can generate several contributors.

- **Large extraterrestrial impact.** At the [Cretaceous–Palaeogene boundary](../../../../../cretaceous-palaeogene-boundary.md), the [Chicxulub impact](../../../../../chicxulub-impact.md) injected dust, aerosols and soot that reduced incoming sunlight and [photosynthesis](../../../../../photosynthesis.md), disrupting [food webs](../../../../../food-web.md); rapid heating, fires and subsequent cooling added stresses. The impact is the trigger, while darkness and loss of primary production are proximate mechanisms.
- **Large-scale [volcanism](../../../../../volcanism.md).** Eruption and intrusion of [large igneous provinces](../../../../../large-igneous-province.md), notably the [Siberian Traps](../../../../../siberian-traps.md) around the end-[Permian](../../../../../permian.md) crisis, release or mobilize gases and can produce rapid [climate change](../../../../../climate-change.md). Carbon dioxide favors warming; sulfur-bearing emissions can cause shorter cooling and acid deposition. The outcome depends on rate, duration and interacting reservoirs, rather than eruption volume alone.
- **Ocean [anoxia](../../../../../anoxia.md) and [euxinia](../../../../../euxinia.md).** Warming lowers oxygen solubility and can strengthen stratification; altered nutrient supply increases oxygen demand. Oxygen loss removes habitable marine volume, while sulfide in anoxic waters can be toxic. These processes contributed to the end-[Permian](../../../../../permian.md) marine crisis and can reinforce warming-driven mortality.
- **Ocean [ocean acidification](../../../../../ocean-acidification.md) and elevated carbon dioxide.** Rapid addition of carbon dioxide changes seawater carbonate chemistry and can impair [biomineralization](../../../../../biomineralization.md), while high carbon dioxide also challenges physiological acid–base regulation. Susceptibility varies with physiology; this is a linked mechanism rather than proof that every calcifying group responds identically.
- **Rapid cooling and [eustatic sea level change](../../../../../eustatic-sea-level-change.md).** Late [Ordovician](../../../../../ordovician.md) glaciation and regression reduced shallow-marine habitat and changed temperatures and circulation; subsequent warming and oxygen loss introduced a different set of stresses. Habitat loss and climate change can therefore contribute in successive phases of one crisis.

These examples supply more than four drivers or contributors. The historical effects of [volcanism](../../../../../volcanism.md), warming, oxygen loss and elevated carbon dioxide can act together rather than compete as mutually exclusive explanations.[Abstract and introduction: linked physiological effects of warming, anoxia, sulfide and hypercapnia](../../../../../paleophysiology-and-end-permian-mass-extinction-by-andrew-h-knoll-and-others.md)
