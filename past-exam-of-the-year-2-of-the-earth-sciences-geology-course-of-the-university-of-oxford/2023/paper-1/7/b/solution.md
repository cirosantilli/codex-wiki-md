<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [pressure correction to a mineral reaction](../../../../../pressure-correction-to-a-mineral-reaction.md) for the solids, and the gas [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) $f/P_0$ for water. These are separate contributions: do not also add a gas-volume pressure correction after including $RT\ln(f/P_0)$. For this reaction,

$$
\Delta_rG=\Delta H^\circ-T\Delta S^\circ+\Delta V_{\rm solids}(P-P_0)+RT\ln\left(\frac{a_{\rm san}a_{\rm cor}}{a_{\rm mus}}\frac f{P_0}\right).
$$

At $7000\ \mathrm{bar}$ with pure solids and an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), $f=P$ and the solid-activity ratio is one. Therefore

$$
0=98157-164T-0.61(7000-1)+8.3145T\ln7000,
$$

and

$$
\boxed{T_{\rm eq}=\frac{98157-0.61(6999)}{164-8.3145\ln7000}=1038.74\ \mathrm K=765.59\ ^\circ\mathrm C}.
$$

The pressure unit in the solid term is [bar](../../../../../bar.md), matching the supplied $\mathrm{J\,bar^{-1}}$. The gas logarithm is dimensionless because $P/P_0=7000$. High water [chemical fugacity](../../../../../chemical-fugacity.md) opposes dehydration and raises the equilibrium [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) substantially.
