↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [fugacity coefficient](../../../../../fugacity-coefficient.md) $\phi=f/P=0.7$ gives $f=3500\ \mathrm{bar}$. Keep the same solid [thermodynamic activities](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) and [pressure correction to a mineral reaction](../../../../../pressure-correction-to-a-mineral-reaction.md) as in part (c), changing only the water [chemical fugacity](../../../../../chemical-fugacity.md):

$$
\boxed{T_{\rm eq}=\frac{98157-0.61(4999)}{164-8.3145\ln(0.7\times5000\times0.6237816764)}=950.38\ \mathrm K=677.23\ ^\circ\mathrm C}.
$$

The lower water [chemical fugacity](../../../../../chemical-fugacity.md) makes the dehydrated assemblage more favorable, so the equilibrium [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) decreases by about $29.0\ \mathrm K$. This direction is a useful independent check on the logarithm's sign.
