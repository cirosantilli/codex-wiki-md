<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For pure phases at the reference [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) $P_0=1\ \mathrm{bar}$, the [Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) is $\Delta_rG=\Delta H^\circ-T\Delta S^\circ$. [Chemical equilibrium](../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) requires $\Delta_rG=0$, so

$$
\boxed{T_{\rm eq}=\frac{98157}{164.0}=598.52\ \mathrm K=325.37\ ^\circ\mathrm C}.
$$

The reaction is [muscovite](../../../../../muscovite.md) dehydration to [sanidine](../../../../../sanidine.md), [corundum](../../../../../corundum.md) and water; its positive [standard entropy of reaction](../../../../../standard-entropy-of-reaction.md) makes the product assemblage favored at high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) under this constant-property approximation.
