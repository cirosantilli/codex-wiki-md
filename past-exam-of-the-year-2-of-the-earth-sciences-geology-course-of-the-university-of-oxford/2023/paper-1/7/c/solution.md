<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Under ideal independent [mixing on crystallographic sites](../../../../../mixing-on-crystallographic-sites.md), a [mineral endmember](../../../../../mineral-endmember.md)'s [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) is the product of its required site fractions, one factor per mixed site. [Muscovite](../../../../../muscovite.md) has one K and three Al per formula unit, [sanidine](../../../../../sanidine.md) has one K and one Al, and [corundum](../../../../../corundum.md) has two Al. Interpreting the given Al fractions as the fractions on every relevant Al/Fe site gives

$$
a_{\rm mus}=0.95(0.9)^3=0.69255,\quad a_{\rm san}=0.75(0.9)=0.675,\quad a_{\rm cor}=(0.8)^2=0.64.
$$

Thus $A_s=a_{\rm san}a_{\rm cor}/a_{\rm mus}=0.6237816764$. The cubic power for [muscovite](../../../../../muscovite.md) includes its tetrahedral Al site and two octahedral Al sites; if site-specific fractions differed, bulk Al/(Al+Fe) alone would not determine this activity. The calculation uses the uniform-site ideal-mixing interpretation intended by the given data.

At $P=5000\ \mathrm{bar}$ with ideal water, solve

$$
T=\frac{98157-0.61(5000-1)}{164-8.3145\ln(5000A_s)},
$$

giving **$T_{\rm eq}=979.40\ \mathrm K=706.25\ ^\circ\mathrm C$**. Using the bulk [mole fraction](../../../../../mole-fraction.md) only once for a multisite [mineral endmember](../../../../../mineral-endmember.md) would omit part of its ideal mixing [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md).
