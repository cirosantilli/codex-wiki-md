↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [arithmetic mean](../../../../../arithmetic-mean.md) is $\bar x=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n x_i$, the total measured concentration divided by the number of observations. The [median](../../../../../median.md) is the central value of the sorted observations, or an interpolation between the two central values under the chosen even-sample convention. It is the 50th [percentile](../../../../../percentile.md).

The table's “75th quartile” means the 75th [percentile](../../../../../percentile.md), or upper [quartile](../../../../../quartile.md) $Q_3$, with approximately three quarters of observations at or below it. Its “25th quartile” means the 25th [percentile](../../../../../percentile.md), or lower [quartile](../../../../../quartile.md) $Q_1$. There are four quarters of a distribution, rather than 75 different quartiles. [Quantile](../../../../../quantile-function.md) interpolation rules can differ for a finite sample; their intended population interpretation is unchanged.
