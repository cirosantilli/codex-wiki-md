↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Squaring each seasonal [standard deviation](../../../../../standard-deviation.md) gives the seasonal [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) in squared concentration units:

- Winter: **$3.29^2=10.8241$**.
- Spring: **$3.22^2=10.3684$**.
- Summer: **$2.06^2=4.2436$**.
- Fall: **$3.21^2=10.3041$**.

No unit is printed in the held table, so a numerical concentration unit should not be invented. The markedly smaller summer [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is also a warning when assessing the common-variance assumption of ordinary [ANOVA](../../../../../analysis-of-variance.md).
