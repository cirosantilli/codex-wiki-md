The [sandstone](../../../../../sandstone.md) records deposition of transported sand, followed by burial and cementation. For the Bunter setting, river channels, floodplains with channel sands and arid continental dune systems are plausible environments; a [sandstone](../../../../../sandstone.md) name alone does not uniquely distinguish them. Well-sorted or weakly cemented sand can retain the connected [porosity](../../../../../porosity.md) and [porous permeability](../../../../../permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) needed for a [saline aquifer](../../../../../saline-aquifer.md).

The Zechstein [evaporite](../../../../../evaporite.md) suggests restricted marine basins or salinas in an arid climate, where evaporation concentrates seawater until dissolved salts precipitate. Repeated restriction and replenishment can build thick [evaporite](../../../../../evaporite.md) sequences, including rock salt. The Triassic [limestone](../../../../../limestone.md) indicates carbonate accumulation, plausibly in warm, shallow marine settings with limited siliciclastic supply, followed by cementation. Some [limestones](../../../../../limestone.md) can also form in lakes; the marine interpretation here uses the stated regional sequence.
