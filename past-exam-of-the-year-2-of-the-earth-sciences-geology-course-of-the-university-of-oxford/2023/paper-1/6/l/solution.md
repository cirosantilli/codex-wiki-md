<h1 id="6/l/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [L](../l.md)

P-wave [seismic impedance](../../../../../seismic-impedance.md) is $Z_P=\rho V_P$. In layer order limestone, sandstone, salt,

$$
\boxed{(Z_{P1},Z_{P2},Z_{P3})=(5.50,4.00,4.00)\times10^6\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-2}\,s^{-1}}}.
$$

The salt's larger P-wave [seismic velocity](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md) is exactly offset by its smaller printed [density](../../../../../density.md), so the second interface has no P-wave [seismic impedance](../../../../../seismic-impedance.md) contrast in this stipulated model.
