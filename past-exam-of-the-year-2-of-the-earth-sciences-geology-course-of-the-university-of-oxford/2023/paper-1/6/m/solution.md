<h1 id="6/m/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [M](../m.md)

S-wave [seismic impedance](../../../../../seismic-impedance.md) is $Z_S=\rho V_S$. With $V_{S3}=3000\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ as documented in part (i),

$$
\boxed{(Z_{S1},Z_{S2},Z_{S3})=(3.30,2.40,3.00)\times10^6\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-2}\,s^{-1}}}.
$$

Unlike the P-wave case, the reservoir base has a nonzero S-wave [seismic impedance](../../../../../seismic-impedance.md) contrast.
