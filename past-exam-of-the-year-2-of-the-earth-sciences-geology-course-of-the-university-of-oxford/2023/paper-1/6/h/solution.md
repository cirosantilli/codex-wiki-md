<h1 id="6/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

For transmission into salt, $V_{P3}=4000\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, so

$$
\sin t_P=\frac{4000}{2000}\sin20^\circ,\qquad \boxed{t_P=43.16^\circ}.
$$

The transmitted P wave bends away from vertical because it enters a faster medium. The incident angle remains below the $30^\circ$ P-wave [seismic critical angle](../../../../../seismic-critical-angle.md) at this interface.
