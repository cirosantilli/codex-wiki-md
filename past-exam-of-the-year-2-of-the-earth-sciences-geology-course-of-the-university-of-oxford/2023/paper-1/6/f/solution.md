<h1 id="6/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For the transmitted S wave in [sandstone](../../../../../sandstone.md), [Snell law for elastic waves](../../../../../snell-law-for-elastic-waves.md) gives

$$
\sin t_S=\frac{1200}{2500}\sin20^\circ,\qquad \boxed{t_S=9.45^\circ}.
$$

The different transmitted P- and S-wave angles result from their different [seismic velocities](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md), despite their common horizontal [seismic ray parameter](../../../../../seismic-ray-parameter-in-horizontal-layering.md).
