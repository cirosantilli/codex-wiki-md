<h1 id="6/n/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [N](../n.md)

Specify the amplitude convention. For incident stress/pressure amplitude one, the normal-incidence [seismic reflection coefficient](../../../../../seismic-reflection-coefficient.md) and stress [seismic transmission coefficient](../../../../../seismic-transmission-coefficient.md) are

$$
R=\frac{Z_b-Z_a}{Z_b+Z_a},\qquad T_\sigma=1+R=\frac{2Z_b}{Z_a+Z_b}.
$$

At limestone–sandstone, **$R_P=-3/19=-0.1579$ and $T_{\sigma P}=16/19=0.8421$**. At sandstone–salt, **$R_P=0$ and $T_{\sigma P}=1$**. The first return reverses the chosen stress-pulse polarity; the second interface produces no normal-incidence P return.

For a particle-velocity amplitude convention, $R_v=-R$ and $T_v=1-R$, giving $(R_v,T_v)=(0.1579,1.1579)$ and $(0,1)$ respectively. These are amplitude coefficients, not fractions of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). The lossless reflected and transmitted energy fractions are $\mathcal R=R^2$ and $\mathcal T=4Z_aZ_b/(Z_a+Z_b)^2=1-R^2$, which sum to one.
