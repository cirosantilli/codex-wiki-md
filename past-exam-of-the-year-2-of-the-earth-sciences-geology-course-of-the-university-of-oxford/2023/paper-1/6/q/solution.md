<h1 id="6/q/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Q](../q.md)

Quality is degraded by vessel and flow noise, poor receiver coupling or position uncertainty, insufficient acquisition aperture and fold, coarse sampling and [aliasing](../../../../../aliasing.md), and inaccurate source timing. Finite source bandwidth limits [vertical seismic resolution](../../../../../vertical-seismic-resolution.md); shallow-water reverberations, bubble pulses, source/receiver ghosts and [seismic multiples](../../../../../seismic-multiple.md) overlap primary reflections. In $50\ \mathrm m$ of water with sound speed about $1500\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, the water-layer round-trip time is only about $0.067\ \mathrm s$.

[Seismic attenuation](../../../../../seismic-attenuation.md) removes high-frequency energy, geometric spreading weakens distant returns, and heterogeneous [lithology](../../../../../lithology.md), scattering and [seismic anisotropy](../../../../../seismic-anisotropy.md) complicate travel paths and amplitudes. Incorrect [seismic velocity](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md) models misplace reflectors during [seismic migration](../../../../../seismic-migration.md); dipping structure and out-of-plane arrivals particularly challenge two-dimensional processing. Thin layers can cause wavelet interference and tuning. Finally, even noiseless data cannot recover a normal-incidence P reflection from the stipulated zero-contrast sandstone–salt boundary: that is absent signal rather than poor recording.
