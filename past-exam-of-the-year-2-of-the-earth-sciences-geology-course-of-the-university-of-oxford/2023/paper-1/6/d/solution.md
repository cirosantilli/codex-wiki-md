<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a horizontal interface, [Snell law for elastic waves](../../../../../snell-law-for-elastic-waves.md) conserves horizontal [seismic ray parameter](../../../../../seismic-ray-parameter-in-horizontal-layering.md) $p=\sin i/V$. At the limestone–sandstone interface the incident P wave has $p=\sin20^\circ/2500$. The reflected S wave remains in the [limestone](../../../../../limestone.md), so

$$
\sin r_S=1500p=0.6\sin20^\circ,\qquad \boxed{r_S=11.84^\circ}
$$

from vertical. All angles below use this normal-to-interface convention.
