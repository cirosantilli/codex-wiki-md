<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The transmitted P wave travels in the [sandstone](../../../../../sandstone.md). Conservation of the [seismic ray parameter](../../../../../seismic-ray-parameter-in-horizontal-layering.md) gives

$$
\sin t_P=\frac{2000}{2500}\sin20^\circ,\qquad \boxed{t_P=15.88^\circ}.
$$

It bends toward the vertical because its [seismic velocity](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md) is smaller.
