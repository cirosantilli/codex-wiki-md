<h1 id="10/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By [Fourier's law](../../../../../../fourier-s-law.md), $q_x=-kT_x$. With nonzero [thermal conductivity](../../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md) $k$, zero outward [heat flux](../../../../../../heat-flux-density.md) at both endpoints gives homogeneous [Neumann boundary conditions](../../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md):

$$
\boxed{T_x(0,t)=0,\qquad T_x(L,t)=0}.
$$

The outward normal has opposite signs at the two ends, but a zero-flux condition gives zero derivative at each.
