<h1 id="10/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $t>0$, fixed zero endpoint [temperatures](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) are homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md):

$$
\boxed{T(0,t)=0,\qquad T(L,t)=0}.
$$

An [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) is also needed to determine a particular solution of the [heat equation](../../../../../../heat-equation.md).
