<h1 id="10/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write $U(x,\tau)=T(x,t_f+\tau)$ after removal, with $\tau\geq0$. Neglecting heat loss to air means thermal insulation, rather than an end to internal [thermal conduction](../../../../../thermal-conduction.md). The evolution and conditions are

$$
\boxed{U_\tau=\kappa U_{xx},\quad0<x<L,\quad U_x(0,\tau)=U_x(L,\tau)=0,\quad U(x,0)=T(x,t_f)}.
$$

Here $T(x,t_f)$ is the nonuniform bath-cooled profile derived in part (c). At the switching instant it need not have zero endpoint derivatives: the sudden replacement of [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) by [Neumann boundary conditions](../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md) can produce another initial-boundary corner incompatibility. The insulated [heat equation](../../../../../heat-equation.md) nevertheless recovers the given profile and satisfies its new boundary conditions for $\tau>0$.
