<h1 id="10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $k_n=n\pi/L$ and $\lambda_n=\kappa k_n^2$. Direct [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) of $T_n=A\sin(k_nx)e^{-\lambda_nt}$ gives

$$
\partial_tT_n=-\lambda_nT_n,\qquad \partial_x^2T_n=-k_n^2T_n,
$$

so $\partial_tT_n=\kappa\partial_x^2T_n$, the [heat equation](../../../../../../heat-equation.md). At $x=0$, the sine factor vanishes; at $x=L$, it is $\sin(n\pi)=0$ for integer $n$. Thus the homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) hold. The nonzero, nonredundant spatial modes use $n=1,2,\ldots$; $n=0$ is the zero function and negative $n$ only change the amplitude sign.
