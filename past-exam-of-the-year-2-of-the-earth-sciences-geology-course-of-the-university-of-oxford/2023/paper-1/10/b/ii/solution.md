<h1 id="10/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $T_n=A\cos(k_nx)e^{-\kappa k_n^2t}$, direct [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) again gives $\partial_tT_n=-\kappa k_n^2T_n$ and $\partial_x^2T_n=-k_n^2T_n$. Thus it satisfies the [heat equation](../../../../../../heat-equation.md). Moreover,

$$
\partial_xT_n=-Ak_n\sin(k_nx)e^{-\kappa k_n^2t}
$$

vanishes at both endpoints for integer $n$, proving the homogeneous [Neumann boundary conditions](../../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md). Now $n=0$ gives the nonzero constant mode $T=A$, which does not decay. It represents conserved mean [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) in an insulated plate.
