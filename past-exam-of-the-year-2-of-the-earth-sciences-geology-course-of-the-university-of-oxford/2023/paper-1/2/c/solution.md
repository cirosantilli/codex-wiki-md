<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use compression-positive [effective stress](../../../../../effective-stress.md) normal to the plane $\sigma'_n$ on the horizontal axis and [shear stress](../../../../../shear-stress.md) $\tau$ on the vertical axis. For any chosen pair of [principal stresses](../../../../../principal-stress.md), [Mohr's circle](../../../../../mohr-s-circle.md) has centre $C=(\sigma'_1+\sigma'_3)/2$ and radius $R=(\sigma'_1-\sigma'_3)/2$.

For the [reverse fault](../../../../../reverse-fault.md) example, $C=60\ \mathrm{MPa}$ and $R=30\ \mathrm{MPa}$. Draw the two [Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion](../../../../../mohr-coulomb-failure-criterion.md) lines $\tau=\pm\sigma'_n/\sqrt3$. The tangencies are

$$
(\sigma'_n,\tau)=(45,\pm15\sqrt3)\ \mathrm{MPa}.
$$

These are [shear failure](../../../../../shear-failure.md) points with nonzero [shear stress](../../../../../shear-stress.md). The two smaller three-dimensional [Mohr circles](../../../../../mohr-s-circle.md) use $(\sigma'_1,\sigma'_2)$ and $(\sigma'_2,\sigma'_3)$; they lie inside the outer circle and do not touch the envelope in this example.

For the opening [geological joints](../../../../../geological-joint.md), use the [tensile failure criterion](../../../../../tensile-failure-criterion.md) $\sigma'_n=-T_0$. The example gives $C=3.5\ \mathrm{MPa}$, $R=6.5\ \mathrm{MPa}$ and $T_0=3\ \mathrm{MPa}$. The circle first touches this vertical cutoff at **$(\sigma'_n,\tau)=(-3,0)\ \mathrm{MPa}$**. Thus the [geological joints](../../../../../geological-joint.md) form on planes normal to $\sigma_3$ and have zero resolved [shear stress](../../../../../shear-stress.md). A complete brittle envelope can combine this tensile cutoff with compressive [Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion](../../../../../mohr-coulomb-failure-criterion.md), using sufficient [cohesion](../../../../../cohesion.md) that shear failure does not occur first. The failure-mechanics figure displays these two distinct constructions.
