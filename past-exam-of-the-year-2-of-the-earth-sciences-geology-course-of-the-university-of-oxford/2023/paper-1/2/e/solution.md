<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Within a shallow [fault zone](../../../../../fault-zone.md) undergoing [brittle fracture](../../../../../brittle-fracture.md), repeated sliding crushes and grinds grains. Expect [fault gouge](../../../../../fault-gouge.md), potentially rich in fine [clay minerals](../../../../../clay-mineral.md), and coarser angular [fault breccia](../../../../../fault-breccia.md); a cohesive, cemented crushed rock is a [cataclasite](../../../../../cataclasite.md). [Slickensides](../../../../../slickenside.md) or displaced grains can record the sliding direction.

An opening [geological joint](../../../../../geological-joint.md) need not contain any [fault rock](../../../../../fault-rock.md): it may be an open crack. If fluids circulated, it can contain a [mineral vein](../../../../../mineral-vein.md), for example [calcite](../../../../../calcite.md) or [quartz](../../../../../quartz.md), grown from the fracture walls. Thus **comminuted [fault rock](../../../../../fault-rock.md) supports shear, while open space or a crack-filling [mineral vein](../../../../../mineral-vein.md) supports opening**. Mineral filling is conditional on fluid chemistry and timing; its precise composition cannot be inferred from the photograph.
