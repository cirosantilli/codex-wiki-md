↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The offset layered sequence indicates **reverse slip on the west-dipping [fault](../../../../../geological-fault.md)**: its western [hanging wall](../../../../../hanging-wall.md) moved up relative to the eastern [footwall](../../../../../footwall.md). With the [fault strike](../../../../../fault-strike.md) perpendicular to the east–west image plane, an [Andersonian faulting regime](../../../../../andersonian-faulting-regime.md) has $\sigma_1$ horizontal east–west, $\sigma_2$ horizontal north–south, and $\sigma_3$ vertical. Draw opposing compression arrows east–west and a smaller opposing pair vertically; the intermediate pair points into and out of the page.

For example, in east, north, up coordinates, a compression-positive [effective stress](../../../../../effective-stress.md) tensor consistent with a $30^\circ$ [reverse fault](../../../../../reverse-fault.md) and zero [cohesion](../../../../../cohesion.md) is

$$
\boldsymbol\sigma'=\begin{pmatrix}90&0&0\\0&60&0\\0&0&30\end{pmatrix}\ \mathrm{MPa}.
$$

These magnitudes illustrate one possible [stress tensor](../../../../../cauchy-stress-tensor.md), rather than a measurement from the photograph. With $\mu=1/\sqrt3$, the $\sigma_1$–$\sigma_3$ [Mohr circle](../../../../../mohr-s-circle.md) is tangent to the [Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion](../../../../../mohr-coulomb-failure-criterion.md) at a plane $30^\circ$ from horizontal. The original failure-mechanics figure above sketches these [principal stress](../../../../../principal-stress.md) directions.
