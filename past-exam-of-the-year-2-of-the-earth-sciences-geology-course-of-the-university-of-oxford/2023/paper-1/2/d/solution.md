<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [reverse fault](../../../../../reverse-fault.md) requires strong east–west compression; the vertical opening [geological joints](../../../../../geological-joint.md) require the least [principal stress](../../../../../principal-stress.md) in the [effective stress](../../../../../effective-stress.md) tensor east–west. **They need not have formed at the same time or under the same local [stress](../../../../../stress.md) field.** Compression can form the [reverse fault](../../../../../reverse-fault.md) during burial and [crustal shortening](../../../../../crustal-shortening.md); subsequent [tectonic exhumation](../../../../../tectonic-exhumation.md), unloading, relaxation, cooling or another tectonic episode can rotate the [principal stress](../../../../../principal-stress.md) ordering and permit opening [geological joints](../../../../../geological-joint.md).

Furthermore, local [stress](../../../../../stress.md) near [faults](../../../../../geological-fault.md), layer interfaces and topography can differ from the regional [stress](../../../../../stress.md). A high [pore pressure](../../../../../pore-pressure.md) shifts every [Mohr circle](../../../../../mohr-s-circle.md) left by $p$, helping [tensile failure](../../../../../tensile-failure-criterion.md), but isotropic [pore pressure](../../../../../pore-pressure.md) alone cannot exchange the east–west and vertical [principal stress](../../../../../principal-stress.md) ordering. Determine relative timing using cross-cutting relations, offsets of [geological joints](../../../../../geological-joint.md), and any generations of fracture-filling [minerals](../../../../../mineral.md). Spatial coexistence is therefore evidence of a deformation history, rather than a requirement that the two failure diagrams describe one simultaneous homogeneous [stress](../../../../../stress.md) state.
