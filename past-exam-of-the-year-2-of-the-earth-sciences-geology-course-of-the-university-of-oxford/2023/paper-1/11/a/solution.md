↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Gregor Mendel](../../../../../gregor-mendel.md)'s first law is **[Mendelian segregation](../../../../../mendelian-segregation.md)**: the two [alleles](../../../../../allele.md) at a [genetic locus](../../../../../genetic-locus.md) in a [diploid](../../../../../diploid.md) individual separate into different [gametes](../../../../../gamete.md), so each gamete receives one. In ordinary [meiosis](../../../../../meiosis.md), separation of [homologous chromosomes](../../../../../homologous-chromosome.md) at [meiosis I](../../../../../meiosis-i.md) provides the chromosomal mechanism; their sister chromatids separate at [meiosis II](../../../../../meiosis-ii.md).

The second law is **[independent assortment](../../../../../independent-assortment.md)**: segregation at one [genetic locus](../../../../../genetic-locus.md) is independent of segregation at another when the loci are unlinked. Random orientation of different pairs of [homologous chromosomes](../../../../../homologous-chromosome.md) at [meiosis I](../../../../../meiosis-i.md) supplies the mechanism for genes on different chromosomes. [Genetic linkage](../../../../../genetic-linkage.md) is an essential qualification: nearby loci on one [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) do not generally assort independently, although sufficiently frequent [genetic recombination](../../../../../genetic-recombination.md) can make their observed combinations approach independence. [Genetic dominance](../../../../../dominance-genetics.md) concerns the [phenotype](../../../../../phenotype.md) of a [heterozygote](../../../../../heterozygote.md) and is not one of these two segregation laws.
