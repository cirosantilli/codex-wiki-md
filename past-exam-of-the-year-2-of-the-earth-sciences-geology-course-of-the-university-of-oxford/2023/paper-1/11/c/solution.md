**[Allopatric speciation](../../../../../allopatric-speciation.md)** begins when a geographic barrier divides a population and reduces [gene flow](../../../../../gene-flow.md). Independent [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md), [genetic drift](../../../../../genetic-drift.md) and divergent [natural selection](../../../../../natural-selection.md) can then build [reproductive isolation](../../../../../reproductive-isolation.md); if populations later meet, they remain distinct when isolation is sufficiently strong. Sister lineages of snapping shrimp in the genus [Alpheus](../../../../../alpheus.md) on opposite sides of the Isthmus of Panama illustrate the role of separated marine populations. Their divergences are not all synchronous with the final closure: genetic data indicate several separation histories, so one barrier date should not be assigned indiscriminately to every pair.[Abstract: comparisons of 15 trans-isthmian pairs and heterogeneous divergence](../../../../../new-dates-and-new-rates-for-divergence-across-the-isthmus-of-panama-by-nancy-knowlton-and-others.md)

**[Sympatric speciation](../../../../../sympatric-speciation.md)** develops without a geographic barrier separating the initial population. Divergent ecological selection combined with [assortative mating](../../../../../assortative-mating.md), habitat-associated mating, different reproductive times or a chromosomal change can reduce [gene flow](../../../../../gene-flow.md) within a shared area. The palms [Howea forsteriana](../../../../../howea-forsteriana.md) and [Howea belmoreana](../../../../../howea-belmoreana.md) on Lord Howe Island provide a documented candidate: different soil associations and substantially different flowering times contribute to [reproductive isolation](../../../../../reproductive-isolation.md), and their sister relationship and timing support divergence on the island.[Abstract and Figures 2–3: flowering-time and soil associations](../../../../../sympatric-speciation-in-palms-on-an-oceanic-island-by-vincent-savolainen-and-others.md)

The distinction concerns the geography of divergence, not whether present-day ranges overlap. Present coexistence alone cannot distinguish [sympatric speciation](../../../../../sympatric-speciation.md) from earlier [allopatric speciation](../../../../../allopatric-speciation.md) followed by range expansion. [Parapatric speciation](../../../../../parapatric-speciation.md) describes divergence between adjacent populations with limited exchange and provides an intermediate geographical category; the two main modes requested are the allopatric and sympatric endpoints.
