The **[biological species concept](../../../../../biological-species-concept.md)** groups natural populations that actually or potentially interbreed and are reproductively isolated from other such groups. Its central criterion is [reproductive isolation](../../../../../reproductive-isolation.md) and [gene flow](../../../../../gene-flow.md), rather than visible similarity. It is hard to apply directly to [fossils](../../../../../fossil.md), organisms reproducing strictly by [asexual reproduction](../../../../../asexual-reproduction.md), and geographically separated populations whose potential interbreeding cannot be tested.

The **[phenetic species concept](../../../../../phenetic-species-concept.md)** groups organisms by overall similarity in measured [phenotypic traits](../../../../../phenotypic-trait.md). A practical [morphological species concept](../../../../../morphological-species-concept.md) uses anatomical form as its main evidence; strict phenetic classification can combine many kinds of measured traits. This makes such approaches usable for preserved specimens and much of the [fossil record](../../../../../fossil-record.md), but the decision depends on which traits are measured and how similarities are weighted.

At least four difficulties arise. [Cryptic species](../../../../../cryptic-species.md) may be reproductively isolated while looking nearly identical, so similarity can lump separate lineages. Conversely, [sexual dimorphism](../../../../../sexual-dimorphism.md), juvenile/adult differences and [phenotypic plasticity](../../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md) can make one interbreeding [species](../../../../../species.md) look like several, causing over-splitting. [Convergent evolution](../../../../../convergent-evolution.md) can make distantly related organisms resemble one another in ecologically selected traits. Finally, continuous geographic or temporal variation supplies no unique gap at which to place a boundary: character choice, sample size, preservation and similarity thresholds can alter the inferred [species delimitation](../../../../../species-delimitation.md). These are distinct from the biological concept's practical difficulty of testing actual interbreeding.
