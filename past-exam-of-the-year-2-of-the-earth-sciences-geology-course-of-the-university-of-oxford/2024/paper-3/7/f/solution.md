<h1 id="7/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Use [Parseval identity for Fourier series](../../../../../parseval-identity-for-fourier-series.md) with the coefficient normalization above. Only [odd](../../../../../odd-function.md) nonzero indices contribute; summing the positive and negative indices gives

$$
1=\sum_{r\in\mathbb Z}\frac{|u_r|^2}{\mathrm{V^2}}
=\frac8{\pi^2}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac1{(2j+1)^2}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac1{(2j+1)^2}=\frac{\pi^2}{8}.}
$$

The factor of two from the negative [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md) is essential. The discontinuities do not obstruct [Parseval's identity](../../../../../parseval-identity.md), which is a statement about [L2 space](../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md).
