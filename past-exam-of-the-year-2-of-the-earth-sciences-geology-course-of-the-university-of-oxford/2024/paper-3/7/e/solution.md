<h1 id="7/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The squared signal equals $1\,\mathrm{V^2}$ except at irrelevant isolated jump times. Its [mean-square signal power](../../../../../signal-power.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{P=\frac1{4\,\mathrm s}\int_0^{4\,\mathrm s}|g(t)|^2\,dt=1\,\mathrm{V^2}.}
$$

Here “power” has the signal-processing definition specified in the question. To convert this voltage mean square into electrical [power](../../../../../power.md) in watts, a load [electrical resistance](../../../../../electrical-resistance.md) would also be required: $P_{\rm electrical}=\langle g^2\rangle/R$.
