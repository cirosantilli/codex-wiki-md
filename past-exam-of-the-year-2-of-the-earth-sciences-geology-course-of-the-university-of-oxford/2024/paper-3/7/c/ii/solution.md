↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The waveform repeats after one positive and one negative interval, so its fundamental [period](../../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) is **$T=4\,\mathrm s$**. A two-second shift reverses its sign and is not a period.
