<h1 id="10/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Subtract the nepheline–corundum reaction from the albite–corundum reaction and divide by two. In the albite-to-nepheline direction the resulting net reaction is

$$
\mathrm{NaAlSi_3O_8}+4\mathrm{H_2O}
\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{NaAlSiO_4}+2\mathrm{H_4SiO_4}.
$$

Additivity of the [standard Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../standard-gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) makes the logarithms of the [equilibrium constants](../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) combine with the same coefficients:

$$
\boxed{\log_{10}K=\frac{-15.18-6.88}{2}=-11.03.}
$$

This normalization is per one [albite](../../../../../albite.md) formula unit. The doubled reaction has $\log_{10}K=-22.06$, and reversing the reaction changes its sign.
