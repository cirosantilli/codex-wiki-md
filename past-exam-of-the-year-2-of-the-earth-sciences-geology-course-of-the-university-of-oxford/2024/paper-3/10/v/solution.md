↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The three stable fields are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\text{nepheline: }&x<-5.515,\quad y>3.44-x,\\
\text{albite: }&x>-5.515,\quad y>-7.59-3x,\\
\text{corundum: }&y<\min(3.44-x,-7.59-3x).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The bounding equalities give the stable two-mineral coexistences. In particular, the stable [corundum](../../../../../corundum.md) region lies below the lower of the two oblique lines; treating every line extension as stable would mislabel parts of the [mineral activity diagram](../../../../../mineral-activity-diagram.md).
