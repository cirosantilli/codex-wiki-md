<h1 id="10/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use base-ten [logarithms](../../../../../logarithm.md) and dimensionless [thermodynamic activities](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) at the stated temperature and pressure. Define $x=\log_{10}a_{\mathrm{H_4SiO_4}}$ and $y=\log_{10}(a_{\mathrm{Na^+}}/a_{\mathrm{H^+}})$. The concentration notation is an ideal dilute-solution approximation with a consistent concentration reference.

With pure solids and unit water activity, the two [reaction quotients](../../../../../reaction-quotient.md) are $10^{2y+2x}$ and $10^{2y+6x}$. Equating them to the corresponding [equilibrium constants](../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) gives

$$
\boxed{y=3.44-x,\qquad y=-7.59-3x.}
$$

The slopes on the [mineral activity diagram](../../../../../mineral-activity-diagram.md) are $-1$ and $-3$. The lines meet at $(x_*,y_*)=(-5.515,8.955)$, within the negative silica-logarithm range.
