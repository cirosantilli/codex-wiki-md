<h1 id="8/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At $x=10\delta_D$, the daily component is multiplied by $e^{-10}\simeq4.54\times10^{-5}$ and has a lag of ten radians. The annual component instead has $x/\delta_Y=10/\sqrt{365}\simeq0.5234$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Theta-\Theta_0={}&A e^{-10/\sqrt{365}}\cos(\omega_Yt-10/\sqrt{365})\\
&+B e^{-10}\cos(\omega_Dt-10).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The sketch is nearly a smooth annual sinusoid, with annual [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) **about $0.5925A$** and annual [phase lag](../../../../../../phase-lag.md) **about $0.5234$ radians**, corresponding to about $30.4$ days on the chosen year scale. The tiny daily component is retained in the formula rather than declared identically zero.
