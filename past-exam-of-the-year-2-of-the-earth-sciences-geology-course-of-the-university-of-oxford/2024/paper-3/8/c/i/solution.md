↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At the surface both attenuation factors are one and both lags vanish:

$$
\boxed{\Theta(0,t)=\Theta_0+A\cos(\omega_Yt)+B\cos(\omega_Dt).}
$$

The sketch is a rapid daily oscillation on a slowly varying annual background. The [amplitudes](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) cannot be inferred uniquely from the sampling intervals alone.
