<h1 id="8/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The true daily component has $f_D=1\,\mathrm{day^{-1}}$, above machine 3's [Nyquist frequency](../../../../../../nyquist-frequency.md). At sampling times $t_n=2n/3$ days,

$$
\cos(2\pi t_n)=\cos(4\pi n/3)=\cos(2\pi n/3).
$$

The last sequence is also produced by a continuous cosine with frequency $0.5\,\mathrm{day^{-1}}$ sampled at those same times. Hence the observed peak is the [sampled sinusoid alias](../../../../../../sampled-sinusoid-alias.md)

$$
\boxed{f_{\rm alias}=|f_D-f_s|=0.5\,\mathrm{day^{-1}}.}
$$

It is not evidence for an additional two-day physical temperature cycle.
