↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The conventional [Nyquist frequency](../../../../../../nyquist-frequency.md) is

$$
\boxed{f_N=f_s/2=0.75\,\mathrm{day^{-1}},\qquad T_N=1/f_N=32\,\mathrm h.}
$$

Frequencies above this cutoff are indistinguishable from lower-frequency [sampled sinusoid aliases](../../../../../../sampled-sinusoid-alias.md). The [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](../../../../../../nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md) requires a band limit strictly below the cutoff for general phase information: at exactly the cutoff, some phases can give zero samples. Thus $32$ hours is the conventional limiting period, with unambiguous reconstruction requiring a longer period under those sampling assumptions.
