<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At the one-bar [standard state](../../../../../standard-state.md), pure phases have unit [thermodynamic activities](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md). The [Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) balance is $\Delta_rH^\circ-T\Delta_rS^\circ=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{141120}{170.7}=826.714\,\mathrm K\simeq553.564\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.}
$$

This uses the stipulated temperature-independent reaction [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) and [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) differences, taken per mole of the written reaction.
