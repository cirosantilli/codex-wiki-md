<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an approximately [isotropic](../../../../../isotropy.md) [rock](../../../../../rock-geology.md), the [P wave](../../../../../p-wave.md) and [S wave](../../../../../s-wave.md) speeds are

$$
V_p=\sqrt{\frac{K+4\mu/3}{\rho}},\qquad V_s=\sqrt{\frac{\mu}{\rho}},
$$

where $K$ is [bulk modulus](../../../../../bulk-modulus.md), $\mu$ is [shear modulus](../../../../../shear-modulus.md), and $\rho$ is [density](../../../../../density.md). Five important controls are:

- **Mineralogy and cementation.** Stiff, well-cemented [mineral](../../../../../mineral.md) frameworks usually have higher [elastic modulus](../../../../../elastic-modulus.md) values and faster [seismic velocities](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md) than poorly consolidated [sediments](../../../../../sediment.md). [Density](../../../../../density.md) alone is insufficient: both stiffness and [density](../../../../../density.md) enter the equations.
- **Porosity and cracks.** Larger [porosity](../../../../../porosity.md), especially compliant cracks, usually lowers effective [elastic modulus](../../../../../elastic-modulus.md) values and [seismic velocities](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md). Crack orientation can make the [seismic velocities](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md) direction-dependent through [seismic anisotropy](../../../../../seismic-anisotropy.md).
- **Pore-fluid composition and saturation.** Replacing liquid [water](../../../../../water.md) by [gas](../../../../../gas.md) generally lowers the effective [bulk modulus](../../../../../bulk-modulus.md) sharply and reduces $V_p$. [Pore fluids](../../../../../pore-fluid.md) have negligible static [shear modulus](../../../../../shear-modulus.md); $V_s$ responds chiefly through the solid framework and the changed bulk [density](../../../../../density.md), rather than through a shear restoring force in the fluid.
- **Effective confining pressure.** Increasing [effective stress](../../../../../effective-stress.md) closes cracks and improves grain contacts, usually increasing both [seismic velocities](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md). Increasing [pore fluid pressure](../../../../../pore-fluid-pressure.md) at fixed total [stress](../../../../../stress.md) reduces [effective stress](../../../../../effective-stress.md) and can reverse this trend.
- **Temperature.** At otherwise comparable conditions, increasing [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) generally softens [minerals](../../../../../mineral.md) and lowers [seismic velocities](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md); thermal cracking or partial [melting](../../../../../melting.md) can lower them further. Increasing burial commonly increases both [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), so their separate effects need not have the same sign.

The measured core [seismic velocities](../../../../../seismic-velocity.md) should therefore be checked against in-situ saturation, [effective stress](../../../../../effective-stress.md), and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md); laboratory values need not transfer unchanged offshore.
