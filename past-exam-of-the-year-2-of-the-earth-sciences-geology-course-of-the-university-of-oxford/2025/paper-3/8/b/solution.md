↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [wave equation](../../../../../wave-equation-split.md) is second order in time. Even one [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) obeys a second-order [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md), so its initial displacement and initial velocity are independent data. Physically they specify stored elastic deformation and the initial motion.

The [heat equation](../../../../../heat-equation.md) is first order in time; its initial [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) already fixes the instantaneous time derivative through the spatial [Laplacian](../../../../../laplacian.md). It needs one initial field. In either case spatial [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) remain necessary and are not substitutes for initial data. Classical fixed-end string data must satisfy the appropriate endpoint compatibility, including $f(0)=f(L)=0$ and, when endpoint velocities exist, $g(0)=g(L)=0$.
