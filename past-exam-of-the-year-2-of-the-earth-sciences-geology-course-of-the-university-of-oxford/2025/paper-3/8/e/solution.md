<h1 id="8/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For each spatial [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md), the temporal equation is $T_n''+\omega_n^2T_n=0$, with [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega_n=ck_n=cn\pi/L$. Hence

$$
\boxed{T_n(t)=A_n\cos(\omega_nt)+B_n\sin(\omega_nt).}
$$

The ordinary [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) is $\omega_n/(2\pi)=cn/(2L)$; it should not be confused with the angular frequency used in the sine and cosine arguments.
