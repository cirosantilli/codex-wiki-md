<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since [tension](../../../../../tension.md) is a [force](../../../../../force-split.md) and $\lambda_0$ is a [linear mass density](../../../../../linear-mass-density.md),

$$
[c]^2=\frac{\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-2}}}{\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}}}
=\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-2}}.
$$

Thus **$c$ has units $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$**. It is the transverse [wave speed](../../../../../wave-speed.md) of the ideal uniform string. The [wave equation on a string](../../../../../wave-equation-on-a-string.md) assumes small transverse slopes, constant positive [tension](../../../../../tension.md), positive uniform [linear mass density](../../../../../linear-mass-density.md), and negligible [damping](../../../../../damping.md) and bending stiffness.
