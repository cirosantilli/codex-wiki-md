A [blind reverse fault](../../../../../blind-reverse-fault.md) can transfer displacement into [folding](../../../../../folding.md) above its buried tip. Look for an asymmetric growing [anticline](../../../../../anticline.md), often with a steep forelimb and gentler backlimb, aligned with the inferred [fault strike](../../../../../fault-strike.md). A [fault-propagation fold](../../../../../fault-propagation-fold.md) is one possible geometry; the fold alone does not uniquely establish the fault's depth or shape. Exposed older [geological strata](../../../../../geological-stratum.md) may be more steeply tilted than younger deposits, while [growth strata](../../../../../growth-strata.md) thin and onlap toward an uplifting crest or fan in dip as deformation accumulates. Progressive [unconformities](../../../../../unconformity.md) and warped young deposits help distinguish active folding from an inherited fold.

In the landscape, seek an elongate uplifted ridge, warped or tilted [river terraces](../../../../../river-terrace.md) and [alluvial fans](../../../../../alluvial-fan.md), localized [fold scarps](../../../../../fold-scarp.md), incision across the rising structure, and drainage deflection or abandoned channels. Compare landforms of known relative ages: increasing deformation with age records repeated motion. Opposite fold limbs may show contrasting uplift and tilting, while active axial surfaces can concentrate deformation into narrow belts even without the main [fault](../../../../../geological-fault.md) reaching the surface. Combine geological mapping and dating with [seismic reflection](../../../../../seismic-reflection.md) or evidence from [geodesy](../../../../../geodesy.md) where available; an erosional ridge alone is not proof of a buried active reverse fault.[Sections “Active fold scarp” and “Syncline axis corresponding to the base of a fold forelimb”: surface folding and scarps can locate deformation associated with buried thrust tips.](../../../../../blind-thrust-mapping-report-by-united-states-geological-survey.md)
