<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the [Fourier transform of a derivative](../../../../../fourier-transform-of-a-derivative.md) twice in each spatial coordinate, and commute the time derivative with the spatial [Fourier transform](../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md). For the [Laplacian](../../../../../laplacian.md),

$$
\mathcal F[\Delta u]=-(k_x^2+k_y^2)\widehat u.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\partial_t\widehat u=-\alpha(k_x^2+k_y^2)\widehat u.}
$$

The minus sign is essential: positive [thermal diffusivity](../../../../../thermal-diffusivity.md) damps short spatial scales rather than amplifying them. The [zero Fourier mode](../../../../../zero-fourier-mode.md) is unchanged in the absence of a source.
