<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The uniform source adds a background without changing the spatial gradients relative to the unforced solution:

$$
\boxed{u_{\rm forced}(x,y,t)=u_{\rm unforced}(x,y,t)+1-e^{-t/10}.}
$$

At $10\,\mathrm s$ the offset is **$1-e^{-1}\simeq0.6321$**. On the corrected grid the central peak is therefore **about $0.8321$**, and regions far from the hot spot are near $0.6321$. The hot spot's broadening is unchanged; the source is spatially uniform. The [spatial mean of a periodic heat equation](../../../../../spatial-mean-of-a-periodic-heat-equation.md) rises by the same amount, tending to a total increment of one temperature unit as $t\to\infty$.
