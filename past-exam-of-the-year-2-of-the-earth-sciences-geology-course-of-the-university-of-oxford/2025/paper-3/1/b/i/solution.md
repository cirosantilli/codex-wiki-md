↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the fully water-filled reservoir, the approximate [densities](../../../../../../density.md) and [wave speeds](../../../../../../wave-speed.md) give

$$
Z_{p2}\simeq3.06\times10^6,\quad Z_{s2}\simeq1.05\times10^6\quad\mathrm{kg\,m^{-2}\,s^{-1}},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{R_p\simeq\frac{3.0625-2.25}{3.0625+2.25}\simeq+0.15,\qquad R_s\simeq\frac{1.05-0.900}{1.05+0.900}\simeq+0.08.}
$$

Both are positive: the reservoir has larger [P wave](../../../../../../p-wave.md) and [shear-wave impedances](../../../../../../shear-wave-impedance.md) than the cap rock, and neither reflection reverses the chosen stress-wave [seismic reflection polarity](../../../../../../seismic-reflection-polarity.md).
