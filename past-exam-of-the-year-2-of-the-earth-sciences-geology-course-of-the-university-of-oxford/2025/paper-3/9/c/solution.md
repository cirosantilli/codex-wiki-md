<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The compositions alone do not specify a unique [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) model. Adopt the usual ideal, independent [mixing on crystallographic sites](../../../../../mixing-on-crystallographic-sites.md) approximation: three Mg–Fe positions per formula unit, with the other sites fixed at their stated endmember compositions. Then the [mineral endmember](../../../../../mineral-endmember.md) activities are

$$
a_{\rm phlogopite}=0.85^3,\qquad a_{\rm pyrope}=0.23^3.
$$

The exponent counts the three mixing cations; treating each whole mineral formula as a single mixing site would be a different model. In particular, the magnesian [biotite](../../../../../biotite.md) and [garnet](../../../../../garnet.md) components here are [phlogopite](../../../../../phlogopite.md) and [pyrope](../../../../../pyrope.md).

For the written reaction, the [reaction quotient](../../../../../reaction-quotient.md) is

$$
Q=\frac{a_{\rm pyrope}}{a_{\rm phlogopite}}\frac{P}{P_0},
$$

with the other solid activities equal to one. The [Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) balance therefore gives

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{130862.28}
{170.7-8.3145\ln\!\left[4500(0.23/0.85)^3\right]}
=981.235\,\mathrm K\simeq708.085\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.}
$$

The smaller product-endmember activity lowers the reaction [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) and lowers the equilibrium temperature in this model. Nonideal [activity coefficients](../../../../../activity-coefficient.md) or unequal occupancies of crystallographic sites would require further information rather than a revised universal numerical answer.[Online teaching draft, chapter 3, Example 3.4: pyrope activity as a product of site fractions, $a_{\rm pyrope}=X_{\rm Mg}^3X_{\rm Al}^2$. Here the aluminium sites are fixed at their endmember occupancy.](../../../../../geochemistry-by-william-m-white.md)
