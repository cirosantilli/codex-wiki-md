<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At the one-bar [standard state](../../../../../standard-state.md), pure [mineral endmembers](../../../../../mineral-endmember.md) and [water vapour](../../../../../water-vapor.md) treated as an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) all have unit [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md). The [Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) is $\Delta_rG^\circ=\Delta_rH^\circ-T\Delta_rS^\circ$. Setting it to zero gives

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{141120}{170.7}=826.714\,\mathrm K\simeq553.564\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.}
$$

The quantities are taken per mole of the reaction as written. The simplified result uses the stipulated temperature-independent [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) and [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) differences.
