Strong geological [magnetic anomalies](../../../../../magnetic-anomaly.md) generally occur in [rocks](../../../../../rock-geology.md) containing abundant strongly magnetic iron oxides, especially [magnetite](../../../../../magnetite.md) and [titanomagnetite](../../../../../titanomagnetite.md). Many [mafic rocks](../../../../../mafic-rock.md), including [basalt](../../../../../basalt.md) and [gabbro](../../../../../gabbro.md), and some [ultramafic rocks](../../../../../ultramafic-rock.md) have large signatures; magnetite-rich iron formations can be exceptionally strong, while [serpentinization](../../../../../serpentinization.md) can create additional [magnetite](../../../../../magnetite.md). Actual [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) and [remanent magnetization](../../../../../remanent-magnetization.md), rather than the rock name alone, determine the response. Sedimentary and felsic [rocks](../../../../../rock-geology.md) are often weaker but have important exceptions.

[Magnetite](../../../../../magnetite.md), [titanomagnetite](../../../../../titanomagnetite.md) and [maghemite](../../../../../maghemite.md) exhibit strong [ferrimagnetism](../../../../../ferrimagnetism.md); some [pyrrhotite](../../../../../pyrrhotite.md) is also strongly magnetic. [Hematite](../../../../../hematite.md) is generally much weaker in induced [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md), although it can carry stable [remanent magnetization](../../../../../remanent-magnetization.md). [Quartz](../../../../../quartz.md) and [calcite](../../../../../calcite.md) have very small, usually negative, susceptibilities, and many ordinary silicates have only weak [paramagnetism](../../../../../paramagnetism.md). A steel car exhibits strong [ferromagnetism](../../../../../ferromagnetism.md) and can therefore stand out against a weakly magnetic host, but its shape and pre-existing [remanent magnetization](../../../../../remanent-magnetization.md) complicate the signal.[Rock-susceptibility discussion and the section “Remanent or Permanent Magnetization”.](../../../../../applications-manual-for-portable-magnetometers-by-geometrics.md)
