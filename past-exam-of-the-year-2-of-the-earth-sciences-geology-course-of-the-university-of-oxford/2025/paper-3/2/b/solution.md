<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Lay out a surveyed grid extending beyond the possible burial area into unaffected ground. Use closely spaced parallel traverses with measurements along each line; spacing should resolve a car-scale [magnetic anomaly](../../../../../magnetic-anomaly.md), with a finer grid over any candidate. For a shallow car, a pilot survey with roughly metre-scale spacing is a sensible starting point, refined according to anomaly width rather than treated as a universal prescription. Record position, time, sensor height, repeat readings and any nearby interference. Cross-lines and repeat stations check reproducibility. Maintain a constant sensor height on a nonmagnetic staff and remove magnetic items from the operator; map fences, gates, pipes, buildings and vehicles before interpreting an anomaly.

Operate a synchronized stationary [base-station magnetometer](../../../../../base-station-magnetometer.md) in quiet ground while the rover surveys, or make frequent base returns if a second instrument is unavailable. A [total-field magnetic anomaly](../../../../../total-field-magnetic-anomaly.md) is the corrected field magnitude minus a specified background:

$$
\Delta T(\mathbf x)=F_{\mathrm{corrected}}(\mathbf x)-F_{\mathrm{background}}(\mathbf x).
$$

For a field-sized target, a local background estimated away from the car is often sufficient. A larger regional survey may subtract the [International Geomagnetic Reference Field](../../../../../international-geomagnetic-reference-field.md) and an independently justified regional trend. Do not remove the car's broad anomaly by overfitting that trend.

Correct rover observations for time-varying [geomagnetic field](../../../../../geomagnetic-field.md) using the base record, for example $F_c(\mathbf x,t)=F_r(\mathbf x,t)-[F_b(t)-F_b(t_0)]$. Check instrument offsets and tie-line differences, reject disturbed or poor-quality readings, and allow for differing sensor heights. Preserve localized target signals when interpolating a contour map or comparing with a [magnetic dipole](../../../../../magnetic-dipole.md) model. There are no compulsory gravity-style free-air or [Bouguer gravity anomaly](../../../../../bouguer-gravity-anomaly.md) reductions for this magnetic search.

Postpone surveying during a [geomagnetic storm](../../../../../geomagnetic-storm.md) or rapid irregular magnetic variation, when base correction may be inadequate. Avoid periods of moving farm machinery or electrical interference, and recognize that strong permanent cultural interference or highly magnetic host [rock](../../../../../rock-geology.md) may make this particular method unsuitable. Ordinary cloud cover or darkness does not prevent a [magnetic survey](../../../../../magnetic-survey.md).[“Magnetic Environment” and survey/base-station operation: magnetic cleanliness, spatial gradients, and time variation.](../../../../../g-857-memory-mag-operation-manual-by-geometrics.md)
