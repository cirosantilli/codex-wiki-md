<h1 id="2/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Near the **south magnetic pole**, the inducing [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and induced [magnetic moment](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) are vertically upward. Directly over the car its dipole field is also upward, again reinforcing the main field. The [total-field magnetic anomaly](../../../../../../total-field-magnetic-anomaly.md) is therefore the **same positive central high and weak negative side lobes as at the north magnetic pole** under the same induced-dipole assumptions. A fixed signed vertical field component would reverse, but the scalar field strength measured by the [proton-precession magnetometer](../../../../../../proton-precession-magnetometer.md) does not. If the car's [remanent magnetization](../../../../../../remanent-magnetization.md) dominates, neither this symmetry nor these predicted signs is assured.
