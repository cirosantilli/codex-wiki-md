<h1 id="2/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At the **magnetic equator**, the inducing [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and induced [magnetic moment](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) are horizontal. Let $x$ point along this field, approximately magnetic north, and $y$ across it. Directly over the car, the dipole field opposes the main field, producing a negative central [total-field magnetic anomaly](../../../../../../total-field-magnetic-anomaly.md). A north–south traverse through the car gives

$$
\Delta T(x)\propto\frac{2x^2-h^2}{(x^2+h^2)^{5/2}},
$$

a symmetric central low with positive shoulders. An east–west traverse through the car instead has $\Delta T(y)\propto-(y^2+h^2)^{-3/2}$, a single negative trough. The equatorial anomaly therefore needs a stated traverse orientation; there is no universal single equatorial line profile.
