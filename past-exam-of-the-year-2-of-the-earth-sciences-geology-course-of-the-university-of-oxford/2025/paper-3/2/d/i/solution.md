<h1 id="2/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Near the **north magnetic pole**, take the inducing [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and induced [magnetic moment](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) as vertically downward. Directly above the car, its anomalous field reinforces the main field: a positive central [total-field magnetic anomaly](../../../../../../total-field-magnetic-anomaly.md) results. Along a horizontal traverse, the vertical-dipole approximation gives a symmetric central high flanked by weaker negative side lobes:

$$
\Delta T(x)\propto\frac{2h^2-x^2}{(x^2+h^2)^{5/2}},
$$

where $h$ is the depth from the sensor to the effective dipole centre. The anomaly width scales with $h$; no absolute amplitude can be calculated without the car's [magnetic moment](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md).
