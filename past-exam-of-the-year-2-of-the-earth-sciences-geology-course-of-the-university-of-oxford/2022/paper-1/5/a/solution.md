<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A **[gravity anomaly](../../../../../gravity-anomaly.md)** is measured gravity minus a specified reference gravity after the stated reductions. A [Bouguer gravity anomaly](../../../../../bouguer-gravity-anomaly.md) aims to remove the predictable effects of latitude, observation elevation and surrounding topographic mass, so residual variations can be related to subsurface [geophysical density contrasts](../../../../../geophysical-density-contrast.md).

Measure relative gravity with a calibrated [gravimeter](../../../../../gravimeter.md) at stations across and beyond the crater, and survey station positions and elevations accurately, preferably with [GNSS](../../../../../gnss.md) tied to a reference datum. Repeat a base station through the day to estimate instrument drift and tie the traverse to a gravity datum. Record station times, because tidal and drift corrections are time dependent.

Apply instrument scale and drift corrections, solid-Earth and relevant ocean-loading tidal corrections, latitude/reference-ellipsoid gravity subtraction, the [free-air correction](../../../../../free-air-correction.md), the [Bouguer slab](../../../../../bouguer-slab.md) correction for material between station and datum, and a [terrain correction](../../../../../terrain-correction.md) for departures from a flat slab. The crater’s strong relief makes the last correction important. A local regional-field/background estimate is also needed if the target is the crater’s residual anomaly. State the reduction density; it is not the inferred breccia density.[Gravity-anomaly discussion: latitude, free-air, simple Bouguer and terrain reductions](../../../../../geophysical-data-grids-for-the-conterminous-united-states-by-united-states-geological-survey.md) For a fixed land traverse there is no moving-platform [Eötvös correction](../../../../../eotvos-correction.md) to add.
