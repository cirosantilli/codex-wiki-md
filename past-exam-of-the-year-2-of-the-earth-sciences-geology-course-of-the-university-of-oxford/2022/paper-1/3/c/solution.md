<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use **low-altitude [structure-from-motion photogrammetry](../../../../../structure-from-motion-photogrammetry.md)**, with strongly overlapping aerial images and well-distributed surveyed [ground control points](../../../../../ground-control-point.md). Bundle adjustment estimates camera geometry and a dense surface reconstruction; independent check points test its accuracy. A small unmanned aircraft or carefully controlled close-range aerial platform can cover the $0.05\,\mathrm{km^2}$ site with centimetre ground sampling. Surveyed targets and stable camera calibration are essential: centimetre pixels alone do not establish centimetre vertical accuracy.

For exposed trench walls or especially demanding local geometry, terrestrial [lidar](../../../../../lidar.md) is an alternative or complement. Remove vegetation where permissible, retain the trench’s sharp topographic breaks and state whether the output is a visible surface model or a bare-ground [digital elevation model](../../../../../digital-elevation-model.md).
