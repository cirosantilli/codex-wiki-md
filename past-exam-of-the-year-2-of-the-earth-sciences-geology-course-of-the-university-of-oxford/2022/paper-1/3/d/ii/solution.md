<h1 id="3/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

After removing orbital, topographic and atmospheric contributions, an earthquake [InSAR](../../../../../../interferometric-synthetic-aperture-radar.md) [radar interferogram](../../../../../../radar-interferogram.md) would show elongated displacement lobes and generally closed or nested fringes associated with uplift and horizontal shortening of the [hanging wall](../../../../../../hanging-wall.md). Each full phase cycle represents $\lambda_r/2$ of [line-of-sight displacement](../../../../../../line-of-sight-displacement.md), where $\lambda_r$ is radar [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md); equivalently $\Delta\phi=\pm4\pi d_{\mathrm{LOS}}/\lambda_r$, with sign fixed by processing convention.[Phase differencing, nuisance contributions and half-wavelength displacement per fringe](../../../../../../interferometry-by-nasa.md)

If primary slip reaches the surface, expect a displacement discontinuity across the surface trace: fringes can terminate or change abruptly there, and large near-fault gradients or disturbed ground may decorrelate. For a [blind reverse fault](../../../../../../blind-reverse-fault.md), the elastic displacement field is continuous at the ground surface and spread over a broader zone, giving smoother gradients and fringes over the uplifted [fold](../../../../../../fold.md). A blind rupture can still create sharp local gradients or secondary faulting.

**The exact colour order, number of fringes and sign of the displacement lobes cannot be fixed from this shaded relief alone.** They depend on slip, depth and radar look direction; uplift is only one contribution to [line-of-sight displacement](../../../../../../line-of-sight-displacement.md).
