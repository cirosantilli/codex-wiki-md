<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the same conditional [optimal normal-fault dip relation](../../../../../optimal-normal-fault-dip-relation.md) as in question 1, with $\delta=55^\circ$:

$$
\varphi=2(55^\circ)-90^\circ=20^\circ,\qquad
\boxed{\mu_f=\tan20^\circ=0.364.}
$$

The second panel of the original [Mohr circle](../../../../../mohr-s-circle.md) figure has $\sigma_3'=1$ and $\sigma_1'=2.03961$; its tangent from the origin has slope $0.364$. Resolving the stresses onto a $55^\circ$ plane places its stress point at that tangency. The diagram assumes negligible [cohesion](../../../../../cohesion.md), a vertical maximum [principal stress](../../../../../principal-stress.md) and an optimally oriented new or equivalently oriented sliding plane. Without those assumptions, a dip and a statement that sliding began do not uniquely determine the [coefficient of friction](../../../../../coefficient-of-friction.md).
