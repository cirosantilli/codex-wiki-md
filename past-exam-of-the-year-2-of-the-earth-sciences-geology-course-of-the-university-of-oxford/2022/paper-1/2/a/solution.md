For an [Andersonian faulting regime](../../../../../andersonian-faulting-regime.md) appropriate to the stated [normal fault](../../../../../normal-fault.md), take **$\sigma_1$ vertical, $\sigma_2$ north–south along strike, and $\sigma_3$ east–west**. Thus the minimum horizontal compression is normal to the fault’s north–south strike. In an east–up cross-section, draw $\sigma_1$ vertically and $\sigma_3$ horizontally, with $\sigma_2$ perpendicular to the page. These are plausible regional [principal stress](../../../../../principal-stress.md) directions, rather than measured directions uniquely recoverable from one photograph.
