↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A **[normal fault](../../../../../normal-fault.md)** is the plausible interpretation. The photograph shows a steep, exposed planar rock face with lower ground on its open side; this geometry is consistent with an exhumed [footwall](../../../../../footwall.md) and a down-dropped [hanging wall](../../../../../hanging-wall.md). A steep dip alone is not a unique indicator of [shear sense](../../../../../shear-sense.md), and the photograph does not supply a displaced marker proving the sense independently. Under an [Andersonian faulting regime](../../../../../andersonian-faulting-regime.md), a plausible maximum compression $\sigma_1$ is approximately vertical, with $\sigma_3$ horizontal in the extension direction and $\sigma_2$ approximately along strike.
