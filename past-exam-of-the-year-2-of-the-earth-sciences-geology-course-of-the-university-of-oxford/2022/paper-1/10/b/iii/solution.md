<h1 id="10/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [diffusion equation](../../../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md) is linear, so [superposition](../../../../../../superposition-principle.md) gives

$$
\boxed{T(t,y)=A_1e^{-\kappa(2\pi/\lambda_1)^2t}\sin\frac{2\pi y}{\lambda_1}
+A_2e^{-\kappa(2\pi/\lambda_2)^2t}\sin\frac{2\pi y}{\lambda_2}.}
$$

Each term separately satisfies the equation, and their sum recovers the stated initial data at $t=0$. Its e-folding times are $\tau_i=\lambda_i^2/(4\pi^2\kappa)$, so **$\lambda_1<\lambda_2$ implies $\tau_1<\tau_2$**. The shorter-wavelength oscillation loses a given fraction of its [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) first. It approaches zero exponentially rather than disappearing at a finite instant. This preferential damping explains smoothing of fine-scale [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) structure.
