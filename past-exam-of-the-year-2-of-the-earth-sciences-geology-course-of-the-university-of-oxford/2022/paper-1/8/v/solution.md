<h1 id="8/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The stable fields are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{ll}
\text{corundum:}&y<\min(3.44-x,-7.59-3x),\\
\text{nepheline:}&x<-5.515,\quad y>3.44-x,\\
\text{albite:}&x>-5.515,\quad y>-7.59-3x.
\end{array}}
$$

Thus [corundum](../../../../../corundum.md) occupies the low-$y$ field, [nepheline](../../../../../nepheline.md) the upper-left field and [albite](../../../../../albite.md) the upper-right field. Pairwise coexistence applies on the indicated stable boundaries, and all three coexist at $(-5.515,8.955)$. These are stability statements within the three-mineral chemical system, not a claim that other possible minerals have been excluded in nature.
