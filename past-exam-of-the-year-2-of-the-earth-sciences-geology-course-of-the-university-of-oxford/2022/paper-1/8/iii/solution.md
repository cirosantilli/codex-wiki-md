↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the reaction direction in part ii, pure solids and unit water activity give $K_c=1/a_{\mathrm{H_4SiO_4^0}}^2$. Therefore

$$
11.03=-2x,\qquad\boxed{x=-5.515.}
$$

Plot a **vertical line** through the common intersection, independent of $y$. Increasing dissolved silica favors [albite](../../../../../albite.md), consistently with its larger Si/Al ratio. The vertical line’s stable and metastable portions are selected by comparison with [corundum](../../../../../corundum.md), not by its own mass-action equation alone.
