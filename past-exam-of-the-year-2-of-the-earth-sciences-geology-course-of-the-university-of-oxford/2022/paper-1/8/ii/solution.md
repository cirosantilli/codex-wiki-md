<h1 id="8/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Subtract reaction (b) from reaction (a) and divide by two. In the direction chosen here, the resulting balanced conversion is

$$
\mathrm{NaAlSiO_4}+2\mathrm{H_4SiO_4^0}
=\mathrm{NaAlSi_3O_8}+4\mathrm{H_2O}.
$$

Thus [nepheline](../../../../../nepheline.md) plus dissolved silica converts to [albite](../../../../../albite.md). Additivity of the [Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md), equivalently [Hess's law](../../../../../hess-s-law.md), gives

$$
\boxed{\log_{10}K_c=\tfrac12(\log K_a-\log K_b)
=\tfrac12(6.88+15.18)=11.03.}
$$

For the reverse reaction the answer is $-11.03$; the reaction direction must accompany the [equilibrium constant](../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md).
