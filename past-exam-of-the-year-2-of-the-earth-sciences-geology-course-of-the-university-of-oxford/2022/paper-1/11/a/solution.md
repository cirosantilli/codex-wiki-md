**[Extinction selectivity](../../../../../extinction-selectivity.md) is systematic variation in the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) or rate of [extinction](../../../../../extinction.md) among lineages with different traits**, rather than trait-independent loss. Traits may be biological, ecological or geographical: for example, survival may differ between narrow-ranging and widespread taxa within the same [extinction](../../../../../extinction.md) interval.

A test needs an explicit denominator of taxa at risk, a defined taxonomic level and time window, and a specified trait measurement. Regional disappearance is not automatically global [extinction](../../../../../extinction.md). Nor is an association necessarily a causal mechanism: traits covary with habitat, abundance and preservation, and their effects can change between background intervals and [mass extinctions](../../../../../mass-extinction.md).
