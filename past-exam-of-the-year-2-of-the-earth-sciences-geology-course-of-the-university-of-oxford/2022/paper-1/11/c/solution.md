One fossil-record study is **[Selectivity of end-Cretaceous marine bivalve extinctions by David Jablonski and others](../../../../../selectivity-of-end-cretaceous-marine-bivalve-extinctions-by-david-jablonski-and-others.md)**, which compared survival of marine [bivalve](../../../../../bivalve.md) genera across the end-[Cretaceous](../../../../../cretaceous.md) [mass extinction](../../../../../mass-extinction.md) against their pre-event attributes. Geographically widespread genera had lower [extinction](../../../../../extinction.md) intensity than narrowly distributed ones. In the same analysis, [body size](../../../../../body-size.md), burrowing versus exposed life position, and shelf bathymetric position were not significant selectors; the apparent feeding-mode contrast was concentrated in particular groups rather than establishing a universal feeding rule.[Science 268, pp. 389–391, abstract: tested traits, range result and qualifications to the feeding-mode result](../../../../../selectivity-of-end-cretaceous-marine-bivalve-extinctions-by-david-jablonski-and-others.md)

The logic of the test is to define the pre-event genera at risk, classify their geographic ranges using pre-event occurrences, then compare the fractions becoming extinct. Using pre-event rather than surviving ranges avoids measuring the predictor after the outcome. A two-by-two [contingency table](../../../../../contingency-table.md) gives an elementary broad/narrow-range comparison; a [logistic regression](../../../../../logistic-regression.md) can instead retain a continuous range measure and include correlated covariates. These are explanations of the inferential design, not a claim that this paper used that exact regression specification.

A persuasive interpretation must address unequal rock exposure, collecting intensity, taxonomic revision, incomplete occurrence records and the difference between local loss and global [extinction](../../../../../extinction.md). The important result is selective survival in a real [fossil](../../../../../fossil.md) dataset and the failure of several plausible alternative predictors in that setting. It is observational evidence: geographic range can correlate with unmeasured ecological properties, so the association alone does not prove a unique kill mechanism.
