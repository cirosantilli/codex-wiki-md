↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For pure phases at the $1$ bar reference [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), the equilibrium condition is $\Delta_rG^\circ=\Delta_rH^\circ-T\Delta_rS^\circ=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{141120}{170.7}=826.71\ \mathrm K=553.56^\circ\mathrm C.}
$$

The calculation uses the stipulated constant reaction [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) and [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md), with the fluid at its reference [chemical fugacity](../../../../../chemical-fugacity.md).
