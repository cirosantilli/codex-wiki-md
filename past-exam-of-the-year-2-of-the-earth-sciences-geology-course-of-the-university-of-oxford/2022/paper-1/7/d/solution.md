<h1 id="7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With water [fugacity coefficient](../../../../../fugacity-coefficient.md) $\phi_f=0.8$, use $f=\phi_fP=4800$ bar. Retain the same ideal mineral-site activities, giving

$$
Q=\frac{4800}{64}=75.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{127442.28}{170.7-8.3145\ln75}=945.40\ \mathrm K=672.25^\circ\mathrm C.}
$$

The lower fluid [chemical fugacity](../../../../../chemical-fugacity.md) stabilizes the water-bearing products of the forward dehydration relative to the ideal-gas calculation and lowers the required [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) by about $13.19$ K. Here the stated [fugacity coefficient](../../../../../fugacity-coefficient.md) is treated as a given constant for evaluating the equilibrium.
