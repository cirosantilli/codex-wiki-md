↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](../../../../../../nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md) gives a **Nyquist rate of $200$ samples per second** for a $100$ Hz upper [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md). In practice choose **$f_s>200$ Hz**, for example $500$ Hz with $\Delta t=2$ ms, and use an [anti-aliasing filter](../../../../../../anti-aliasing-filter.md) whose passband retains the required signal below the [Nyquist frequency](../../../../../../nyquist-frequency.md). Exactly $200$ Hz leaves no filter transition band and can entirely miss a $100$ Hz sinusoid at an unfortunate phase; it is a limiting rate, not a robust acquisition choice.
