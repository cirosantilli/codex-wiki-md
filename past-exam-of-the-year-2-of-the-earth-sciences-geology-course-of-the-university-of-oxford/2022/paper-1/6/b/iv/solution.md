<h1 id="6/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The transmitted converted [S wave](../../../../../../s-wave.md) obeys

$$
\sin i_{S2}=\frac{2000}{1500}\sin30^\circ=\frac23.
$$

Hence **$i_{S2}=41.81^\circ$ from vertical**. A propagating transmitted SV mode can exist even though the transmitted P mode is already supercritical.
