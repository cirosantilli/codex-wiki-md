<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The required transmitted [P wave](../../../../../../p-wave.md) angle would satisfy

$$
\sin i_{P2}=\frac{3500}{1500}\sin30^\circ=\frac76>1.
$$

Therefore **there is no real transmitted P-ray angle**. The incidence exceeds the P critical angle $\arcsin(1500/3500)=25.38^\circ$; the transmitted P contribution is [evanescent](../../../../../../evanescent-wave.md) away from the interface rather than a propagating body ray. Reporting $90^\circ$ would describe critical incidence, not the stated $30^\circ$ incidence.
