<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The reflected [P wave](../../../../../../p-wave.md) remains in the upper layer. Conservation of horizontal [seismic ray parameter](../../../../../../seismic-ray-parameter-in-horizontal-layering.md) gives $\sin i_r/V_{P1}=\sin30^\circ/V_{P1}$, so the **reflected P angle is $30^\circ$ from vertical**. This is specular reflection at the horizontal interface.
