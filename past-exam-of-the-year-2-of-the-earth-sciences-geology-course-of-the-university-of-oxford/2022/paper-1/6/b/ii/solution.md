<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the converted reflected [S wave](../../../../../../s-wave.md), [Snell law for elastic waves](../../../../../../snell-law-for-elastic-waves.md) gives

$$
\frac{\sin i_{S1}}{900}=\frac{\sin30^\circ}{1500},\qquad
\sin i_{S1}=0.300.
$$

Thus **$i_{S1}=17.46^\circ$ from vertical**, in the upper layer. The converted in-plane mode is SV; an [isotropic](../../../../../../isotropy.md) planar P incidence does not generate an independent SH mode.
