↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The normal-incidence [acoustic impedance](../../../../../../acoustic-impedance.md) is $Z=\rho V_P$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{Z_1=1500(1500)=2.25\times10^6\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-2}\,s^{-1}},}
$$



$$
\boxed{Z_2=2000(3500)=7.00\times10^6\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-2}\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

These are $2.25$ and $7.00$ MRayl. They combine the material’s density and wave speed; using $V_S$ would give shear rather than acoustic P impedance.
