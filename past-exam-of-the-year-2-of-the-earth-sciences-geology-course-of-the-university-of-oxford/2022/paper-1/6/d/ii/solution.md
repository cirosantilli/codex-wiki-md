<h1 id="6/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Specify the [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) convention. For normal-stress/[pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) [amplitudes](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md), the [seismic reflection coefficient](../../../../../../seismic-reflection-coefficient.md) and [seismic transmission coefficient](../../../../../../seismic-transmission-coefficient.md) are

$$
\boxed{R_\sigma=\frac{Z_2-Z_1}{Z_2+Z_1}=\frac{19}{37}=0.51351,\qquad
T_\sigma=\frac{2Z_2}{Z_1+Z_2}=\frac{56}{37}=1.51351.}
$$

For particle displacement or velocity resolved in one fixed normal coordinate, the reflected [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) changes sign and the transmitted ratio is instead

$$
R_v=-\frac{19}{37},\qquad T_v=\frac{2Z_1}{Z_1+Z_2}=\frac{18}{37}=0.48649.
$$

These are alternative conventions, not conflicting energy predictions. The reflected and transmitted [energy flux](../../../../../../energy-flux.md) fractions are

$$
\mathcal R=\frac{361}{1369}=0.26370,\qquad
\mathcal T=\frac{1008}{1369}=0.73630,
$$

which sum to one for the ideal lossless interface. Thus an [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) transmission coefficient greater than one does not violate [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md).
