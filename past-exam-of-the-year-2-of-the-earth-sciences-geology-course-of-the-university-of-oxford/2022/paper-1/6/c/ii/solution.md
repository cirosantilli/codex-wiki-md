<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a converted lower-layer [S wave](../../../../../../s-wave.md) head wave, set the transmitted S angle to $90^\circ$:

$$
\frac{\sin i_{c,S}}{V_{P1}}=\frac1{V_{S2}},\qquad
\boxed{i_{c,S}=\arcsin\frac{1500}{2000}=48.59^\circ.}
$$

This is possible because $V_{S2}>V_{P1}$. It is the incident P angle needed for the converted SV branch to become tangential, not an S incident angle in layer 1.
