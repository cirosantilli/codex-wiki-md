<h1 id="6/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At P critical incidence the transmitted [P wave](../../../../../../p-wave.md) travels along the interface. The common horizontal [seismic ray parameter](../../../../../../seismic-ray-parameter-in-horizontal-layering.md) is $1/V_{P2}$, giving

$$
\boxed{i_{c,P}=\arcsin\frac{1500}{3500}=25.38^\circ.}
$$

The resulting [seismic head wave](../../../../../../seismic-head-wave.md) radiates back into the slower upper layer.
