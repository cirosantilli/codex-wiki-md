# Steady precession quadratic for an orientation-dependent potential

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

For an axially symmetric rotational [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) with potential $Mgh\cos\theta$, fixed tilt and conservation of the spin and azimuthal momenta give constant precession. The tilt equation becomes $A\Omega^2\cos\alpha-Cn\Omega+Mgh=0$. A real precession rate requires the [quadratic discriminant](quadratic-discriminant.md) to be nonnegative. The inertia and height parameters must correspond consistently to the chosen [fixed point](fixed-point.md).
