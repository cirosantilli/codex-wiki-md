# Symmetric eigenvalue residual bound

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenpair residual](eigenpair-residual.md)

For a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $M$, real $\mu$ and nonzero [vector](vector.md) $u$, some [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of $M$ lies within the stated ratio of $\mu$. In an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md), write $u=\sum_jc_jq_j$ and $Mq_j=\theta_jq_j$. Then $\|Mu-\mu u\|_2^2=\sum_j(\theta_j-\mu)^2c_j^2\geq\min_j|\theta_j-\mu|^2\|u\|_2^2$, proving the bound.
