# Hall-Witt identity

↑ **Parent:** [Group commutator](group-commutator.md)

The Hall-Witt identity is a three-variable identity among iterated [group commutators](group-commutator.md). Passing to the leading terms of a filtered group turns it into the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) for the associated graded Lie algebra.
