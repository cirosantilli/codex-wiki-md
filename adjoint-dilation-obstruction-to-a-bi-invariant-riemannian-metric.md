# Adjoint dilation obstruction to a bi-invariant Riemannian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Bi-invariant Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-riemannian-metric.md)

If a [Lie group](lie-group.md) has an [Adjoint representation of a Lie group](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md) element with a real eigenvector of eigenvalue whose modulus differs from one, it has no [bi-invariant Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-riemannian-metric.md). Conjugation is a composition of left and right translations, hence would preserve the positive identity inner product. Applying it to that eigenvector gives $\|v\|=|\lambda|\|v\|$, a contradiction. The [orientation-preserving affine group of the real line](orientation-preserving-affine-group-of-the-real-line.md) supplies a connected example: conjugation by dilation of factor two multiplies the infinitesimal translation by two.
