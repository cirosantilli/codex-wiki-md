# Coxeter complex

↑ **Parent:** [Dual geometric representation of a Coxeter group](dual-geometric-representation-of-a-coxeter-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_complex)

The Coxeter complex has faces represented by cosets $wW_J$ of [standard parabolic subgroups](standard-parabolic-subgroup.md), ordered by reverse inclusion. Geometrically, its maximal simplices are the chambers of the Coxeter arrangement intersected with a sphere.
