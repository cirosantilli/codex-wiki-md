# 2022

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the multisensory perception: attention, integration, and development course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all six essay options in the [multisensory perception: attention, integration, and development course of the University of Oxford](../multisensory-perception-attention-integration-and-development-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) examination held on 24 May 2022. The examination required three options; all six are covered here, with each answer below 1,800 words. The scientific evidence used was published before the examination. Source release: [released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/experimental_psychology_past_exa/response/2757831/attach/3/New%20Compressed%20zipped%20Folder.zip?cookie_passthrough=1). The held PDF prints A16860W1; the archive contains two byte-identical members, including a16860w1.pdf. Original figures below illustrate stated models, not measured participant results.
