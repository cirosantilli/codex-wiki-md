# Skeleton of a directed graph

↑ **Parent:** [Directed graph](directed-graph.md)

The skeleton of a [directed graph](directed-graph.md) is the [undirected graph](undirected-graph.md) obtained by replacing every directed edge with an undirected edge and forgetting its orientation. In causal structure learning, the [PC algorithm](pc-algorithm.md) first recovers this adjacency structure before learning orientations.
