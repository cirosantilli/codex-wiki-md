# Lieb-Robinson localization by Haar twirling

↑ **Parent:** [Lieb-Robinson bound](lieb-robinson-bound.md)

Apply [Haar twirling conditional expectation](haar-twirling-conditional-expectation.md) to the complement of a neighbourhood around the initial operator support. A [Lieb-Robinson bound](lieb-robinson-bound.md) on commutators with arbitrary complement-supported unitaries then bounds the approximation error without summing over sites. For a bound proportional to $e^{-\mu d}(e^{2kst}-1)$, choosing $v=4ks/\mu$ gives an error at most $\mu vt\,|X|\|A_X\|e^{-\mu l/2}$ outside radius $vt+l$.
