# Organ of Corti

↑ **Parent:** [Cochlea](cochlea.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organ_of_Corti)

The sensory apparatus supported by the [basilar membrane](basilar-membrane.md) and containing cochlear [hair cells](hair-cell.md). Their [mechanotransduction](mechanotransduction.md) helps convert vibration into auditory signals.
