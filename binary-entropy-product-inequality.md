# Binary entropy product inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Union-closed sets conjecture](union-closed-sets-conjecture.md)

For $x,y\in[0,1]$, the [binary entropy](binary-entropy.md) and the [golden ratio](golden-ratio.md) $\varphi$ satisfy

$$
h_2(xy)\geq\frac{\varphi}{2}\bigl(xh_2(y)+yh_2(x)\bigr).
$$
