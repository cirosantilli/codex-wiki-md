# Randomized play-the-winner rule

↑ **Parent:** [Response-adaptive randomization](response-adaptive-randomization.md)

In a two-treatment urn design, begin with $a$ balls of each type, draw and replace a ball to allocate treatment, then add $b$ balls of the same type after success or of the opposite type after failure. This [response-adaptive randomization](response-adaptive-randomization.md) favours treatments with more observed successes. For RPW$(1,1)$, type-0 probability after counts $(s_0,f_0,s_1,f_1)$ is $(1+s_0+f_1)/(2+s_0+f_0+s_1+f_1)$.
