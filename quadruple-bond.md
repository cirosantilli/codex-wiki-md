# Quadruple bond

↑ **Parent:** [Covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md)

A formal fourfold bond. In the familiar d-metal example, one [sigma bond](sigma-bond.md), two [pi bonds](pi-bond.md) and one [delta bond](delta-bond.md) are occupied, giving the configuration $\sigma^2\pi^4\delta^2$ and formal [bond order](bond-order.md) four.
