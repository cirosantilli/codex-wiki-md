# Finite-past innovation

↑ **Parent:** [Best linear prediction from a finite past](best-linear-prediction-from-a-finite-past.md)

Starting observations at a finite time, subtract the best linear predictor based on the available earlier observations. The resulting residual is orthogonal to their linear span. The unit-triangular relation between observations and residuals makes the residuals an [orthogonal basis](orthogonal-basis.md) of the same finite observation space. These differ from infinite-past innovations until the initialization effect disappears.
