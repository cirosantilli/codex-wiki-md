# Coil-globule transition

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

The change of an isolated flexible [polymer](polymer-split.md) from an expanded coil toward a dense [polymer globule](polymer-globule.md) as its [solvent](solvent.md) becomes less favorable. In the [Flory approximation](flory-approximation.md), $F/(k_BT)\sim R^2/(Na^2)+vN^2/R^3+uN^3/R^6$, where the positive three-segment coefficient $u$ prevents collapse to zero size. Positive $v$ gives swollen-coil scaling, $v=0$ gives the [theta temperature](theta-temperature.md) condition, and negative $v$ favors a dense state with $R^3\sim uN/|v|$. Finite chain [length](length.md) rounds the change; [polymer aggregation](polymer-aggregation.md) is a distinct multi-chain process and must be controlled in an experiment.
