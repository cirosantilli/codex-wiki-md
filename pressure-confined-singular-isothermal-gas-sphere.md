# Pressure-confined singular isothermal gas sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md)

Truncating the singular isothermal gas profile at radius $r_0$ gives $M=2c_s^2r_0/G$ and boundary [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p_0=c_s^4/(2\pi Gr_0^2)$. The [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) of a [monatomic gas](monatomic-gas.md) is $U=3Mc_s^2/2$, while shell integration gives gravitational self-energy $\Omega=-GM^2/r_0=-2Mc_s^2$. Thus $2U+\Omega=3p_0\mathcal V$, with $\mathcal V=4\pi r_0^3/3$. The confining [pressure](pressure-split.md) supplies the virial surface term. This singular-central-density model differs from a regular pressure-confined isothermal sphere.
