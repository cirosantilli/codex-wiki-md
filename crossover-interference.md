# Crossover interference

↑ **Parent:** [Chromosomal crossover](chromosomal-crossover.md)

Crossover interference means that an exchange affects the occurrence of other nearby [chromosomal crossovers](chromosomal-crossover.md). The [Haldane mapping function](haldane-mapping-function.md) assumes this dependence is absent. See [Meiosis](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/meiosis.md).
