# Symmorphic space group

↑ **Parent:** [Space group](space-group.md)

A [space group](space-group.md) for which an origin can be chosen so that representatives of all point-group operations have zero translation part. It is a [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md) of its [lattice translations](lattice-translation.md) and [point group](point-group.md). Presence of centring translations in a conventional cell does not alone make a group nonsymmorphic.
