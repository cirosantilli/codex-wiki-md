# Mirror neuron

↑ **Parent:** [Neuron](neuron-split.md)

A [neuron](neuron-split.md) whose recorded activity has related selectivity during action execution and observation. This response property does not by itself identify the [neuron](neuron-split.md)’s necessary behavioural function or its developmental origin.
