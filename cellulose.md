# Cellulose

↑ **Parent:** [Polysaccharide](polysaccharide.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellulose)

[Cellulose](cellulose.md) is a [polysaccharide](polysaccharide.md) of [glucose](glucose.md) units whose chains form strong assemblies in plant [cell walls](cell-wall.md). It illustrates a structural role for [carbohydrates](carbohydrate.md). See [Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell: the plant cell wall](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26928/).
