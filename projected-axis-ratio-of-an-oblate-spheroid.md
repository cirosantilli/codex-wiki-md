# Projected axis ratio of an oblate spheroid

↑ **Parent:** [Oblate spheroid](oblate-spheroid.md)

If the symmetry axis of an [oblate spheroid](oblate-spheroid.md) makes angle $i$ with the line of sight, its projected axis ratio satisfies

$$
Q^2=\cos^2i+q^2\sin^2i.
$$

Thus a face-on spheroid appears round and an edge-on spheroid reveals its intrinsic axis ratio.
