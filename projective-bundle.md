# Projective bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

The projective bundle of a real or complex [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E\to X$ has fiber the space of one-dimensional subspaces of $E_x$. It carries a [tautological bundle](tautological-bundle.md) $\lambda\subset p^*E$; in the real case this is the [projectivization of a real vector bundle](projectivization-of-a-real-vector-bundle.md).

The same construction applies to an algebraic [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) over an [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md): local trivializations glue products with [projective space](projective-space-split.md). In the lines convention its universal [tautological bundle](tautological-bundle.md) is a [line bundle](line-bundle.md); the alternative [projectivization by quotients](projectivization-by-quotients.md) convention parametrizes one-dimensional quotient spaces. Both conventions give dimension $\dim X+\operatorname{rank}(E)-1$ and a [proper morphism](proper-morphism.md) to the base.
