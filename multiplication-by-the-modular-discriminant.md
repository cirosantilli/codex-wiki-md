# Multiplication by the modular discriminant

↑ **Parent:** [Modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md)

The [modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md) has a simple zero at infinity and no zero in the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), by the [valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md). Multiplication by it identifies weight-$k$ [modular forms](modular-form.md) with weight-$(k+12)$ [cusp forms](cusp-form.md). Conversely dividing a cusp form by it preserves interior holomorphy and removes one order of vanishing at infinity.
