# Ellipsoidal cyclotron mass

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotron effective mass](cyclotron-effective-mass.md)

For a positive definite parabolic [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) tensor $M$, the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) equation is $M\dot{\mathbf v}=e\mathbf B\times\mathbf v$. Its nonzero eigenfrequencies are $\pm eB/m_c$, giving the displayed mass. With principal values $(m_t,m_t,m_l)$, a field along the long valley axis gives $m_c=m_t$; a perpendicular field gives $\sqrt{m_tm_l}$. For equally oriented cubic valleys viewed along $[111]$, all valleys give $m_c=\sqrt{3m_t^2m_l/(2m_t+m_l)}$.
