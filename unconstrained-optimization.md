# Unconstrained optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

[Optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md) with no additional [optimization constraints](optimization-constraint.md) on the chosen variable domain. At an interior local [extremum](maximum-and-minimum.md) of a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md), the [first-order optimality condition](first-order-optimality-condition.md) is $\nabla f=0$.
