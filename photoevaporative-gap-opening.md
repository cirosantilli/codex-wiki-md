# Photoevaporative gap opening

↑ **Parent:** [Photoevaporation](photoevaporation.md)

When a [protoplanetary disk](protoplanetary-disk.md)'s inward viscous supply approaches its wind-loss rate, [photoevaporation](photoevaporation.md) can prevent replenishment of the inner disk. That region drains on a [viscous timescale](viscous-timescale.md), leaving a gap or hole. A negative density in a formal [steady state](steady-state.md) calculation signals the breakdown of that approximation, not a physical continuation. An exposed outer edge can subsequently disperse rapidly.
