# Godement resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Flasque resolution](flasque-resolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godement_resolution)

Embed a [sheaf of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md) $\mathcal F$ into the sheaf $C(\mathcal F)(U)=\prod_{P\in U}\mathcal F_P$ by all its [germs](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md). Restrictions are projections, so $C(\mathcal F)$ is [flasque](flasque-sheaf.md). Repeat on the successive sheaf [cokernels](cokernel.md) to obtain a canonical [flasque resolution](flasque-resolution.md). Its complex of [global sections](global-section.md) computes [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md). When $\mathcal F$ is itself [flasque](flasque-sheaf.md), the flasque-kernel section-lifting lemma makes every successive quotient flasque and makes the global-section complex exact in positive degrees, proving $H^q(X,\mathcal F)=0$ for $q>0$ without assuming that vanishing in the construction.
