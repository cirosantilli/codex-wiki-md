# Intensive quantity

↑ **Parent:** [Intensive and extensive thermodynamic quantities](intensive-and-extensive-thermodynamic-quantities.md)

An [intensive quantity](intensive-quantity.md) is unchanged under replication at the same [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md). [Temperature](temperature-split.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md), [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) and the [mass density](density.md) of a uniform substance are examples. Independence from sample size does not mean independence from state: a material's [mass density](density.md) can change with [temperature](temperature-split.md) or [pressure](pressure-split.md).

The relation to [extensive quantities](extensive-quantity.md) is particularly elegant for a differentiable bulk fundamental relation. Put $\mathbf x=(S,V,n)$ and write

$$
U(\lambda\mathbf x)=\lambda U(\mathbf x).
$$

Taking a [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md) with respect to $x_i$ and cancelling $\lambda$ gives

$$
\frac{\partial U}{\partial x_i}(\lambda\mathbf x)
=\frac{\partial U}{\partial x_i}(\mathbf x).
$$

The equilibrium differential $dU=T\,dS-p\,dV+\mu\,dn$ identifies these derivatives with [temperature](temperature-split.md), minus [pressure](pressure-split.md) and molar [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md). They are degree-zero [homogeneous functions](homogeneous-function.md). The same degree-one assumption yields the [Euler relation in thermodynamics](euler-relation-in-thermodynamics.md), but a [surface energy](surface-energy.md) contribution requires extra care.

An [intensive quantity](intensive-quantity.md) generally has no meaningful sum across samples: two equal [temperatures](temperature-split.md) do not add to a doubled [temperature](temperature-split.md). Neither is the final [temperature](temperature-split.md) after thermal contact generally an unweighted average; [heat capacities](heat-capacity.md) and the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) determine the balance. Ratios of additive inventories lead to the useful weighted rules in [normalizing extensive quantities](normalizing-extensive-quantities.md).
