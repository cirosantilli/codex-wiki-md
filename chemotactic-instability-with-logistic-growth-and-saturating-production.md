# Chemotactic instability with logistic growth and saturating production

↑ **Parent:** [Chemotactic pattern-forming instability](chemotactic-pattern-forming-instability.md)

For $b_t=d_bb_{xx}-\chi_b\partial_x[b(1+n)^{-2}n_x]+b(1-b)$ and $n_t=d_nn_{xx}+\lambda b^2/(b_0^2+b^2)-\mu bn$, the positive homogeneous [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is $(1,n_*)$, where $n_*=\lambda/[\mu(1+b_0^2)]$. Set $\kappa=\chi_b/(1+n_*)^2$ and $\eta=\lambda(b_0^2-1)/(1+b_0^2)^2$. The [linear stability](linear-stability.md) [matrix](matrix.md) at squared [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $q$ is $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-1-d_bq&\kappa q\\\eta&-\mu-d_nq\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is negative and its [determinant](determinant.md) is $d_bd_nq^2+(d_n+\mu d_b-\kappa\eta)q+\mu$. Minimizing this [quadratic polynomial](quadratic-polynomial.md) over $q>0$ proves the displayed continuous-[wavenumber](wavenumber.md) growth criterion. It requires $b_0>1$. On a bounded domain an actual permitted nonzero [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) must lie strictly inside the negative-determinant interval. This is a criterion for growth of infinitesimal perturbations, not a general exclusion of finite-amplitude patterns.
