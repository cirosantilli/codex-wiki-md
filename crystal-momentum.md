# Crystal momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch state](bloch-state.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_momentum)

Crystal momentum is the translation quantum number $\hbar k$ of a [Bloch state](bloch-state.md). Since $k$ and $k+G$ give the same translation character whenever $G$ belongs to the [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md), crystal momentum is defined modulo reciprocal-lattice vectors.
