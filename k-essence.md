# K-essence

↑ **Parent:** [Field theory in cosmology](field-theory-in-cosmology.md)

[K-essence](k-essence.md) is a cosmological scalar-field mechanism in which noncanonical kinetic dynamics supply negative pressure or accelerated expansion. Its action is a particular use of a [P(X, phi) scalar field theory](p-x-phi-scalar-field-theory.md); that general action also includes theories without kinetic dark energy.
