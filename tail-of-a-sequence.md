# Tail of a sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

A tail of a [sequence](sequence.md) consists of its terms from some index $N$ onward, retaining their order and repetitions. Discarding finitely many initial terms does not change its [limit of a sequence](limit-of-a-sequence.md) or whether it is a [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md). A tail can also be considered as the [set](set-split.md) of values $\{x_n:n\geq N\}$ when forming an [infimum](infimum.md), [supremum](supremum.md), or [tail diameter of a sequence](tail-diameter-of-a-sequence.md); that [set](set-split.md) alone forgets the original order and repetitions.
