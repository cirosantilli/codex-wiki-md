# Cauchy convolution semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md)

The normalized [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) densities $K_a$, $a>0$, form a [convolution](convolution.md) semigroup: convolution adds their scale parameters. This follows from the [convolution theorem](convolution-theorem.md), since their [Fourier transforms](fourier-transform-split.md) are proportional to $e^{-a|k|}$. Consequently an integral equation with a Cauchy kernel can be solved by subtracting scale parameters when the resulting scale is positive.
