# Ideal of definition

↑ **Parent:** [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md)

An ideal $I$ of a [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md) $(A,\mathfrak m)$ is an ideal of definition when $A/I$ has [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) zero. Equivalently, $I$ is [$\mathfrak m$-primary](primary-ideal.md), so $\sqrt I=\mathfrak m$ and $\mathfrak m^r\subseteq I$ for some [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $r$.
