# Error-prone PCR

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md)

[Error-prone PCR](error-prone-pcr.md) deliberately introduces sequence diversity during amplification by conditions or enzymes with reduced fidelity. The mutation burden and library composition affect how much useful sequence space can be explored.
