# Effective-potential geometry of Lagrange points

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange point](lagrange-point.md)

The [rotating-frame effective potential](rotating-frame-effective-potential.md) of the [circular restricted three-body problem](circular-restricted-three-body-problem.md) has negative singular wells at both primaries. Its three [Collinear Lagrange points](collinear-lagrange-point.md) are planar saddles, and its two [Triangular Lagrange points](triangular-lagrange-point.md) are planar local maxima for nonzero primary masses. These geometric labels do not replace a dynamical stability analysis including the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md).
