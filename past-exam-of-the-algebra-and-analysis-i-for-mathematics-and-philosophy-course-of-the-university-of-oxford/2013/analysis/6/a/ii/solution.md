↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [removable singularity](../../../../../../removable-singularity.md) is an [isolated singularity](../../../../../../isolated-singularity-split.md) for which the function on the punctured disk extends to a [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the full disk, by assigning or correcting its value at $a$. In [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) terms,

$$
\boxed{c_j=0\text{ for every }j<0.}
$$

The remaining power series defines the extension with value $c_0$ at $a$. Conversely, a holomorphic extension has a [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) there, so uniqueness of the [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) forces all negative coefficients to vanish.
