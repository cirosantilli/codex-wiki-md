<h1 id="analysis/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the reciprocal [Möbius transformation](../../../../../mobius-transformation.md)

$$
\boxed{F(z)=\frac1z.}
$$

It is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on $U_1$ by part (b). For $z\neq0$,

$$
|F(z)+i|=\frac{|1+iz|}{|z|}=\frac{|z-i|}{|z|}.
$$

Part (c) now gives $z\in U_1$ if and only if $|F(z)+i|>1$, so $F(U_1)\subseteq U_2$. Conversely, $0\notin U_2$ because $|0+i|=1$. For any $w\in U_2$, put $z=1/w$; the same identity gives $|z-i|>|z|$, so $z\in U_1$ and $F(z)=w$. Finally, reciprocation is injective on nonzero complex numbers. This proves bijectivity, with inverse

$$
\boxed{F^{-1}(w)=\frac1w.}
$$

The inverse is also holomorphic, so this is a [biholomorphism](../../../../../biholomorphism.md).
