↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Euclidean domain](../../../../../euclidean-domain.md) is an [integral domain](../../../../../integral-domain.md) $R$ admitting a [Euclidean function](../../../../../euclidean-function.md) $\delta:R\setminus\{0\}\to\mathbb Z_{\geq0}$ such that division with a smaller remainder is possible: for every $a\in R$ and nonzero $b\in R$, there are $q,r\in R$ with

$$
\boxed{a=qb+r,\qquad r=0\ \text{or}\ \delta(r)<\delta(b).}
$$

Thus the [Euclidean function](../../../../../euclidean-function.md) measures progress in repeated division. Definitions which additionally normalize it to be nondecreasing under multiplication give the same class of [Euclidean domains](../../../../../euclidean-domain.md); the division property is the one needed below.
