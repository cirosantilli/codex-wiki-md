The [dual space](../../../../../dual-space-split.md) is the [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of all [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md) on $V$:

$$
\boxed{V'=\operatorname{Hom}_F(V,F).}
$$

Addition and scalar multiplication are defined pointwise. The [annihilator of a vector subspace](../../../../../annihilator-of-a-vector-subspace.md) is

$$
\boxed{U^0=\{f\in V':f(u)=0\text{ for every }u\in U\}.}
$$

It is a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $V'$: linear combinations of functionals vanishing on $U$ also vanish on $U$.
