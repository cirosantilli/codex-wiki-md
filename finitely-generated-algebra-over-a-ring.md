# Finitely generated algebra over a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra over a commutative ring](algebra-over-a-commutative-ring.md)

An [algebra over a commutative ring](algebra-over-a-commutative-ring.md) $A$ is finitely generated if it is a [quotient ring](quotient-ring.md) of a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) $A[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$ in finitely many variables. Equivalently, finitely many elements generate it under addition, multiplication, and the action of $A$. The induced map of [affine schemes](affine-scheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is a [morphism of finite type](morphism-of-finite-type.md) exactly when this holds. Finite generation as an algebra does not require finite generation as an $A$-[module](module-mathematics.md).
