# Riemannian parallel-spinor Ricci-flatness

↑ **Parent:** [Compact harmonic-spinor rigidity under nonnegative scalar curvature](compact-harmonic-spinor-rigidity-under-nonnegative-scalar-curvature.md)

A parallel spinor has zero [spin](spin.md) [curvature](curvature.md). Contracting the spinorial [Ricci identity](curvature-commutator-on-a-covariant-tensor.md) with a [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) gives $R_{\mu\nu}\gamma^\nu\psi=0$. For each fixed $\mu$, squaring [Clifford multiplication](clifford-multiplication.md) by that Ricci row gives $|R_{\mu\cdot}|^2\psi=0$. Riemannian positivity and a nonzero spinor force every row to vanish. This implication is local and does not itself require compactness; the compact hypothesis is used to obtain parallelism from harmonicity.
