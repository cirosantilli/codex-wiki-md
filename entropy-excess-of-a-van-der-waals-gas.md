# Entropy excess of a van der Waals gas

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md)

For one mole with temperature-independent parameters $a,b$, the [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) is $P=RT/(V-b)-a/V^2$. A [Maxwell relation](maxwell-relations.md) gives $(\partial S/\partial V)_T=R/(V-b)$, so the volume dependence is $R\ln(V-b)$. Matching the [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) entropy at infinite volume fixes the excess at the same $V,T$ to $R\ln(1-b/V)$. The attraction contributes $-a/V$ to the [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) but no excess entropy when $a$ is independent of temperature. A temperature-dependent attraction parameter would change this conclusion.
