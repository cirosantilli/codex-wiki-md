# Interpretation (logic)

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpretation_(logic))

A choice of a domain and meanings for the symbols of a [formal language](formal-language.md), used to evaluate its sentences. The same sentence can have different [truth values](truth-value.md) under different interpretations, unless it is valid under every admitted interpretation.
