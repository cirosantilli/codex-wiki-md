# Extracellular single-unit recording

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscience](neuroscience-split.md)

[Extracellular single-unit recording](extracellular-single-unit-recording.md) uses an [electrode](electrode.md) outside a [neuron](neuron-split.md) to detect electrical events attributed to that unit's [action potentials](action-potential.md). It records a [spike train](spike-train.md), not the intracellular [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) or every input [synapse](synapse.md). [Hubel and Wiesel (1959), p. 575: extracellular recording methods](https://xbio-live.s3.amazonaws.com/narratives/57358/Hubel%20and%20Wiesel%201959_Guided%20paper.pdf)
