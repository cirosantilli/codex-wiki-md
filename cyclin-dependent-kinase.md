# Cyclin-dependent kinase

↑ **Parent:** [Protein kinase](protein-kinase.md)

A kinase activated and regulated through association with a [cyclin](cyclin.md). Changing activity helps impose the order of replication, [mitosis](mitosis.md) and mitotic exit.
