# Injective entire functions are affine

↑ **Parent:** [Entire function](entire-function.md)

An injective [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) cannot have an [essential singularity](essential-singularity.md): [Casorati-Weierstrass theorem](casorati-weierstrass-theorem.md) would give values from a small punctured neighbourhood inside the open image of a disjoint neighbourhood, contradicting injectivity. Apply this at infinity to an injective [entire function](entire-function.md). A removable singularity there would make the function constant by [Liouville's theorem](liouville-theorem.md). A pole makes it a polynomial by the [Cauchy coefficient formula](cauchy-coefficient-formula.md); its derivative has no zero, so the [fundamental theorem of algebra](fundamental-theorem-of-algebra.md) forces degree one.
