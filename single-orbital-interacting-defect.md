# Single-orbital interacting defect

↑ **Parent:** [On-site Coulomb repulsion](on-site-coulomb-repulsion.md)

An isolated spin-degenerate orbital coupled to a particle reservoir has [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H=\epsilon(n_\uparrow+n_\downarrow)+Un_\uparrow n_\downarrow$. The four occupation [microstates](microstate.md) have weights $1,z,z,qz^2$, where $z=e^{\beta(\mu-\epsilon)}$ and $q=e^{-\beta U}$. Thus $\Xi=1+2z+qz^2$ and $\bar n=(2z+2qz^2)/\Xi$. The factor two counts distinct [spin](spin.md) states. At $U=0$ the grand [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) factorizes into two [independent](independent-random-variables.md) fermionic orbitals; positive U separates the two electron-addition thresholds.
