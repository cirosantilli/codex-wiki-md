# Integrating-factor equation for a planar vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Conservative vector field](conservative-vector-field.md)

If $\psi(A_1,A_2)$ is a [conservative vector field](conservative-vector-field.md), equality of the mixed derivatives of its potential gives $\partial_y(\psi A_1)=\partial_x(\psi A_2)$. Expanding proves the displayed necessary equation. For continuously differentiable data on the whole plane, the vanishing planar curl is also sufficient for a potential. A useful [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) is nonzero on the region under consideration.
