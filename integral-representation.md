# Integral representation

↑ **Parent:** [Integral](integral.md)

An integral representation expresses a [function](function-split.md) through an [integral](integral.md) of specified data and an [integral kernel](integral-kernel.md). [Green-function representations](green-function-representation.md) solve [differential equations](differential-equation-split.md) this way, while the [Bromwich inversion formula](bromwich-inversion-formula.md) reconstructs a function from its [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md).
