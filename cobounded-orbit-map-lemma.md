# Cobounded orbit map lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Milnor–Švarc lemma](milnor-svarc-lemma.md)

A [cobounded group action](cobounded-group-action.md) by isometries on a nonempty [geodesic metric space](geodesic-metric-space.md) admits an [orbit map](orbit-map.md) that is a [quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md) for a possibly infinite [generating set of a group](generating-set-of-a-group.md). If $Gx$ is $R$-dense, take $S=\{g\ne1:d(x,gx)\leq2R+1\}$. Subdivide a [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) into segments of length at most one and approximate subdivision vertices by orbit points, keeping the endpoints exact. The resulting group increments generate and give

$$
|g|_S\leq d(x,gx)+1,\qquad d(x,gx)\leq(2R+1)|g|_S.
$$

The first estimate includes zero displacement: a nontrivial stabilizer element has length one. No finiteness assertion about $S$ survives without properness.
