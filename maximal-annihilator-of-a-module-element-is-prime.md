# Maximal annihilator of a module element is prime

↑ **Parent:** [Annihilator of a module](annihilator-of-a-module.md)

Over a commutative unital [ring](ring.md), an ideal maximal among annihilators of nonzero elements of a [module](module-mathematics.md) is a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md). Each such ideal is the [annihilator of a module](annihilator-of-a-module.md) given by the cyclic submodule generated by that element. If $P=\operatorname{Ann}(m)$ and $ab\in P$ with $b\notin P$, then $bm\ne0$ and $P\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}(bm)$. Maximality makes these annihilators equal, so $a\in P$. A [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) ensures that a nonzero module has a maximal element annihilator by the [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md) on ideals.
