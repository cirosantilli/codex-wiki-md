# Sawtooth Ising chain

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)

An [Ising model](ising-model.md) on a chain of corner-sharing triangles, with one base [spin](spin.md) and one apex [spin](spin.md) per cell. If the apex interacts only with the two base corners, summing each apex independently gives a two-by-two [transfer matrix](transfer-matrix.md) on base [spins](spin.md). For symmetric [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) and [energy](energy-split.md) $Jss'+\bar J\bar s(s+s')$, its entries are $2e^{-\beta Jss'}\cosh[\beta\bar J(s+s')]$.
