<h1 id="mobius-calculation-of-circular-brownian-exit">Möbius calculation of circular Brownian exit</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md)

A [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) beginning at $a\in(0,1)$ in the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) has circular exit law obtained by pulling back uniform angular measure through $\psi_a(w)=(w-a)/(1-aw)$. This [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) sends the starting point to zero. The right semicircle maps to an arc with endpoints at angles $\pm(\pi/2+2\arctan a)$, giving positive-half-plane exit probability $1/2+(2/\pi)\arctan a$.
