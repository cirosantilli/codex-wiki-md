# Schouten-Nijenhuis bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial polyvector field](polynomial-polyvector-field.md)

The left Schouten-Nijenhuis bracket on [polynomial polyvector fields](polynomial-polyvector-field.md) is the degree-minus-one [Gerstenhaber bracket](gerstenhaber-bracket.md) extending the [commutator](commutator.md) of [derivations](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) and $[D,f]=D(f)$ by graded antisymmetry and the left [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md). For $\Pi=\partial_X\wedge\partial_Y$, this convention gives $[h\Pi,f]=h(f_Y\partial_X-f_X\partial_Y)$ and $[F\partial_X+G\partial_Y,h\Pi]=(D(h)-h(F_X+G_Y))\Pi$. A right insertion convention reverses the degree-two-with-function formula.
