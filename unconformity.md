# Unconformity

↑ **Parent:** [Stratigraphy](stratigraphy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unconformity)

A surface separating deposits across a gap in the geological record. In an angular [unconformity](unconformity.md), differently oriented strata can record deposition, deformation, removal of material and renewed deposition.[volume I, chapter VI, section II, Jedburgh observations](theory-of-the-earth-by-james-hutton-1795.md)
