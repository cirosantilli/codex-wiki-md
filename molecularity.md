# Molecularity

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary reaction](elementary-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecularity)

[Molecularity](molecularity.md) counts the reacting entities in one [elementary reaction](elementary-reaction.md): one for a unimolecular step, two for a bimolecular step. An entity can be a [molecule](molecule-split.md), [ion](ion.md), or [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). [Solvent](solvent.md) entities involved only through [solvation](solvation.md) are excluded. [Molecularity](molecularity.md) describes a microscopic event; [order of reaction](order-of-reaction.md) describes the [concentration](concentration.md) dependence of a [rate law](rate-law.md). Assigning [molecularity](molecularity.md) to a net multi-step [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md) is therefore inappropriate.[Glossary of Terms Used in Physical Organic Chemistry, IUPAC Recommendations 1994, entry “molecularity”: elementary event and exclusion of solvation medium](glossary-of-terms-used-in-physical-organic-chemistry-iupac-recommendations-1994-m-entries-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry.md)
