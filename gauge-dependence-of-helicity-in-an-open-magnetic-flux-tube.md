# Gauge dependence of helicity in an open magnetic flux tube

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic helicity](magnetic-helicity.md)

An open [flux tube](flux-tube.md) carries [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) through its end caps. Its ordinary [magnetic helicity](magnetic-helicity.md) can therefore change under a single-valued [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md). For $\mathbf B=aR\mathbf e_\phi+b\mathbf e_z$ inside $R<R_0$ and zero outside, a regular family of [magnetic vector potentials](magnetic-vector-potential.md) is $A_\phi=bR/2$, $A_z=C-aR^2/2$ inside and $A_\phi=bR_0^2/(2R)$, $A_z=C-aR_0^2/2$ outside. Their tangential components agree at $R_0$. Inside, $\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf B=bC$, so the helicity per unit length is $\pi bCR_0^2$. Changing $C$ is the gauge transformation $\Lambda=\Delta C\,z$, and changes this result whenever $b\ne0$. The convention $A_z=0$ outside gives $C=aR_0^2/2$ and helicity per unit length $\pi abR_0^4/2$, while $C=0$ gives zero. Without an end-cap or gauge convention the ordinary helicity is not unique.
