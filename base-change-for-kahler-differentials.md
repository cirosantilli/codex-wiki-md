<h1 id="base-change-for-kahler-differentials">Base change for Kähler differentials</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler differential](kahler-differential.md)

The [Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md) commute with extension of the base ring. A derivation of the base-changed algebra that kills $k'$ is determined by its restriction to $A\otimes1$, and the [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) provides its unique extension. No flatness hypothesis is required for this isomorphism.
