# B-Tribromoborazine

↑ **Parent:** [Borazine](borazine.md)

[B-Tribromoborazine](b-tribromoborazine.md) is $\mathrm{B_3Br_3N_3H_3}$, retaining all three N–H groups. Ring-retaining bromination replaces B–H groups, whereas simple hydrogen-halide addition also changes ring saturation.
