# Strict dominance

↑ **Parent:** [Pure strategy](pure-strategy.md)

A strategy is strictly dominated when another strategy gives a strictly larger payoff against every opposing strategy. A strictly dominated strategy cannot receive positive probability in a [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md), because moving that probability to its dominator increases expected payoff.
