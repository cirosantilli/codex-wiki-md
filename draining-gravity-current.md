# Draining gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity current](gravity-current.md)

A draining gravity current loses fluid through its substrate while spreading horizontally. For a two-dimensional viscous current of thickness $H(x,t)$ that loses fluid according to [pressure-dependent Darcy drainage](pressure-dependent-darcy-drainage.md),

$$
H_t=A(H^3H_x)_x-DH^{1-\beta},
\qquad A=\frac{\rho g}{3\mu},
$$

when the [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) is $p=\rho gH$ and $k/h\propto p^{-\beta}$.
