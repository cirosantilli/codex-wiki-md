# Non-atomic measure

↑ **Parent:** [Atom (measure theory)](atom-measure-theory.md)

A non-atomic [measure](measure.md) contains no [atom of a measure](atom-measure-theory.md). For a finite [Borel measure](borel-measure.md) on a [Polish space](polish-space.md), this is equivalent to giving every singleton mass zero. For a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) on $\mathbb R$, it is also equivalent to continuity of its [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md).
