# Rademacher function

↑ **Parent:** [Rademacher distribution](rademacher-distribution.md)

On $[0,1)$ with Lebesgue measure, the functions $r_n(t)=(-1)^{\lfloor2^nt\rfloor}$, $n\ge1$, are independent symmetric [Rademacher random variables](rademacher-distribution.md). Every prescribed pattern for the first $n$ functions occupies a dyadic interval of measure $2^{-n}$. Their closed span in $L^1$ is isomorphic to the [l2 sequence space](l2-sequence-space.md) by the [Khintchine inequality](khintchine-inequality.md).
