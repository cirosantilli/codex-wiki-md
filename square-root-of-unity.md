# Square root of unity

↑ **Parent:** [Root of unity](root-of-unity.md)

A [square root of unity](square-root-of-unity.md) is an element $x$ of a [ring](ring.md) with identity satisfying $x^2=1$. In a [field](field.md) of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two, the only possibilities are $1$ and $-1$: the [difference of squares](difference-of-two-squares.md) identity $(x-1)(x+1)=0$ has no other roots because a [field](field.md) has no [zero divisors](zero-divisor.md). In a general [ring](ring.md) more solutions may occur; modulo eight the four residue classes $1,3,5,7$ all have square one.
