# Delayed carbon-dioxide ventilation feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Pulmonary ventilation](pulmonary-ventilation.md)

A one-compartment [carbon-dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) balance couples metabolic production with pressure-dependent ventilatory removal. A [positive-part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) response models a nonnegative threshold-linear respiratory drive, and a transport/response delay can destabilize [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md). With $T=t/\tau$, $P=P_0+[M/(GP_0)]p$, the scaled law is $p'=\alpha[1-(1+\mu p)[p(T-1)]_+]$, where $\alpha=\tau GP_0/K$ and $\mu=M/(GP_0^2)$. Its positive [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is $2/[1+\sqrt{1+4\mu}]$; $1/(1+\mu)$ is only its first-order small-$\mu$ approximation.
