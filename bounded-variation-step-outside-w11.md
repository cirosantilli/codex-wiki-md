# Bounded-variation step outside W11

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

The [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) of this [indicator function](indicator-function.md) is $e_1\mathcal H^1$ on the vertical segment $x_1=1/2$, so its [total variation seminorm on a domain](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md) is one. This is singular with respect to planar [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md), whereas [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) weak [derivatives](derivative.md) in $W^{1,1}$ have integrable densities. Its [bounded-variation space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) [norm](norm.md) is $1/2+1=3/2$.
