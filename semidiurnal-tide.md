# Semidiurnal tide

↑ **Parent:** [Tide](tide.md)

A [semidiurnal tide](semidiurnal-tide.md) has two principal oscillations per day. The dominant lunar constituent has a period of approximately 12 hours 25 minutes; the solar semidiurnal constituent has a period of 12 hours.[Examples M2 and S2](about-harmonic-constituents-by-national-oceanic-and-atmospheric-administration.md)
