# Kinetic coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Nonconserved order-parameter dynamics](nonconserved-order-parameter-dynamics.md)

For [nonconserved order-parameter dynamics](nonconserved-order-parameter-dynamics.md), the positive kinetic coefficient $\Gamma$ converts the [functional derivative](functional-derivative.md) of the [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) into a relaxation rate through $\dot p=-\Gamma\,\delta F/\delta p$. [Microscopic reversibility](microscopic-reversibility.md) fixes the accompanying [Gaussian white noise](gaussian-white-noise.md) variance by the [Model A fluctuation-dissipation relation](model-a-fluctuation-dissipation-relation.md).
