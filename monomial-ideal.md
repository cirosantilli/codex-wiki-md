# Monomial ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monomial_ideal)

An [ideal](ideal.md) in a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) generated by [monomials](monomial.md). Its exponent vectors form an upward-closed subset of $\mathbb N^r$ under the componentwise order. The [Dickson lemma](dickson-s-lemma.md) makes the minimal exponent set finite. This description permits [Maclagan's theorem](reverse-inclusion-well-quasi-ordering-of-monomial-ideals.md) to compare ideals by reverse inclusion independently of the coefficient field.
