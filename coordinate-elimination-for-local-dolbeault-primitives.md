# Coordinate elimination for local Dolbeault primitives

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma](dolbeault-poincare-lemma.md)

For a $\bar\partial$-closed positive-antiholomorphic-degree [differential form of type (p, q)](differential-form-of-type-p-q.md) on a [polydisc](polydisc.md), write $\varphi=d\bar z_j\wedge\alpha+\beta$ with no $d\bar z_j$ in $\alpha,\beta$. Apply the [Cauchy-Green operator](cauchy-green-operator.md) in coordinate $j$ to the coefficients of $\alpha$. Subtracting its [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md) derivative removes every $d\bar z_j$ term. Closedness then makes the remaining coefficients holomorphic in $z_j$. Eliminating successive coordinates preserves holomorphicity in earlier coordinates and produces a local primitive after finitely many steps.
