# Coarse-grained energy dissipation

↑ **Parent:** [Internal intermittency](internal-intermittency.md)

[Coarse-grained energy dissipation](coarse-grained-energy-dissipation.md) is a spatial average of the local rate $2\nu S_{ij}S_{ij}$ over a region of size $r$. Its random value $\epsilon_r$ has mean $\epsilon$ in [homogeneous turbulence](homogeneous-turbulence.md). [Refined similarity](kolmogorov-refined-similarity-hypothesis.md) uses this local averaged [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) to scale velocity increments.
