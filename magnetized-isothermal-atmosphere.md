# Magnetized isothermal atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostatic equilibrium](magnetostatic-equilibrium.md)

A plane-parallel [magnetostatic equilibrium](magnetostatic-equilibrium.md) with constant downward [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $g$, horizontal [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), constant [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md) $c_s$, and constant [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md) $v_a$ has

$$
H=\frac{c_s^2+v_a^2/2}{g},\qquad \rho=\rho_0e^{-z/H},\qquad p=c_s^2\rho,\qquad B=B_0e^{-z/(2H)}.
$$

Indeed the [magnetohydrodynamic total pressure](magnetohydrodynamic-total-pressure.md) is $(c_s^2+v_a^2/2)\rho$, and its vertical derivative is $-g\rho$. The [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) therefore increases the [scale height](scale-height.md) above that of an unmagnetized atmosphere.
