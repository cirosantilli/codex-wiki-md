# Resistive magnetic-helicity balance

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic helicity](magnetic-helicity.md)

For a [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) $\mathbf A$, a [solenoidal](solenoidal-vector-field.md) [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) $\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A$ and constant [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) $\eta_m$, write $\partial_t\mathbf A=\mathbf u\times\mathbf B-\eta_m\nabla\times\mathbf B-\nabla\phi$. In a fixed closed volume with zero helicity surface flux, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) makes the two differentiated factors in $H=\int_V\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf B\,dV$ equal; the scalar-potential boundary term vanishes when $\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf n=0$. One sufficient boundary gauge is $\mathbf n\times\mathbf A=0$; another standard setting is integration over all space with sufficient decay. Orthogonality of $\mathbf u\times\mathbf B$ to $\mathbf B$ gives the displayed identity. Unlike the resistive [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) loss, $\dot H$ is not sign definite. The zero normal [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) also makes $H$ [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) under single-valued smooth gauge transformations.
