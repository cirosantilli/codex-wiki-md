<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [canonical momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) of the [harmonic oscillator](../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) is $p=\partial L_0/\partial\dot q=m\dot q$. Substituting $\dot q=p/m$ into the [Legendre transform in mechanics](../../../../../legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) gives

$$
\boxed{p=m\dot q,\qquad
H_0(q,p)=p\dot q-L_0=\frac{p^2}{2m}+\frac{m\omega^2q^2}{2}.}
$$
