<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Work on $I>0$, with a local choice of the periodic [angle](../../../../../angle.md) $\theta$. The inverse formulas are

$$
I=\frac12\left(\alpha q^2+\frac{p^2}{\alpha}\right),\qquad
\theta=\operatorname{atan2}(\alpha q,p)\pmod{2\pi}.
$$

In particular, $\theta_q=p/(2I)$, $\theta_p=-q/(2I)$, $I_q=\alpha q$ and $I_p=p/\alpha$. Their [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) is

$$
\boxed{\{\theta,I\}=\frac{p^2}{2I\alpha}+\frac{\alpha q^2}{2I}=1.}
$$

The other fundamental [Poisson brackets](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) vanish by antisymmetry. Thus this is a [canonical transformation](../../../../../canonical-transformation.md) for every $\alpha>0$, on local charts away from the origin. At $I=0$ the [angle](../../../../../angle.md) is undefined.

In the new coordinates the [harmonic oscillator](../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is

$$
H_0(\theta,I)=I\left(\frac{\alpha}{m}\cos^2\theta
+\frac{m\omega^2}{\alpha}\sin^2\theta\right).
$$

For $I$ to be a [conserved quantity](../../../../../conserved-quantity.md) throughout [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md), [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) require $\dot I=-\partial_\theta H_0=0$ identically. The two coefficients must coincide. For the usual $m>0$ and $\omega>0$, positivity of $\alpha$ selects

$$
\boxed{\alpha=m\omega,\qquad H_0=\omega I,\qquad
I(t)=I(0),\qquad\theta(t)=\theta(0)+\omega t\pmod{2\pi}.}
$$

These are [harmonic oscillator action-angle variables](../../../../../harmonic-oscillator-action-angle-variables.md); the [action variable](../../../../../action-variable.md) is $I=H_0/\omega$.
