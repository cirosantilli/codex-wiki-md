↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [type-two generating function for a canonical transformation](../../../../../type-two-generating-function-for-a-canonical-transformation.md) uses the mixed variables $(q,P)$ and defines

$$
\boxed{p=\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial q},\qquad
Q=\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial P}.}
$$

For a local invertible [canonical transformation](../../../../../canonical-transformation.md), require $F_{2,qP}\ne0$ so that $p=F_{2,q}(q,P)$ can be solved for $P$. The differential identity $dF_2=p\,dq+Q\,dP$ gives $d(F_2-PQ)=p\,dq-P\,dQ$, expressing preservation of the canonical [symplectic form](../../../../../symplectic-form.md).
