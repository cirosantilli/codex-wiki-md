<h1 id="3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a time-dependent [type-two generating function for a canonical transformation](../../../../../type-two-generating-function-for-a-canonical-transformation.md), the differential identity

$$
p\,dq-H\,dt=P\,dQ-K\,dt+d(F_2-PQ)
$$

gives $K=H+\partial_tF_2$, with the [partial derivative](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) taken at fixed $(q,P)$. Here $F_2=\cos q\sin P$ has no explicit time dependence, so the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) correction is

$$
\boxed{\Lambda(Q,P,t)=0,\qquad
K(Q,P,t)=H\left(\arccos\frac{Q}{\cos P},
-\sin P\sqrt{1-Q^2\sec^2P},t\right).}
$$

As always, adding a function of time alone to the new [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) leaves [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) unchanged. Choosing a time-independent [type-two generating function for a canonical transformation](../../../../../type-two-generating-function-for-a-canonical-transformation.md) fixes that irrelevant freedom and gives the displayed $\Lambda=0$.
