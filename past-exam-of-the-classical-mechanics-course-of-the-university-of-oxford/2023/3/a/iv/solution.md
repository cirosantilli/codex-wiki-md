<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Set $D=\partial_t+\{\,\cdot\,,H\}$. The hypotheses mean $Df=Dg=0$ as identities on [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md). By the [Jacobi identity for the Poisson bracket](../../../../../jacobi-identity-for-the-poisson-bracket.md),

$$
\{\{f,g\},H\}=\{\{f,H\},g\}+\{f,\{g,H\}\}.
$$

Also, the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) applied to the defining coordinate formula gives

$$
\partial_t\{f,g\}=\{f_t,g\}+\{f,g_t\}.
$$

Adding these identities proves

$$
D\{f,g\}=\{Df,g\}+\{f,Dg\}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dt}\{f,g\}=0.}
$$

This proves the [Poisson bracket of time-dependent conserved quantities](../../../../../poisson-bracket-of-time-dependent-conserved-quantities.md) theorem, including explicit time dependence of $f$ and $g$.
