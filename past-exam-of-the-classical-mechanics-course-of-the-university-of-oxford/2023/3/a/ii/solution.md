<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In [canonical coordinates](../../../../../canonical-variables.md) on [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md), the [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) is

$$
\boxed{\{f,g\}=\sum_{i=1}^n\left(
\frac{\partial f}{\partial q_i}\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_i}
-\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_i}\frac{\partial g}{\partial q_i}\right).}
$$

It treats $t$ as a parameter. Along a solution of [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md), the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{df}{dt}=\sum_i\left(f_{q_i}\dot q_i+f_{p_i}\dot p_i\right)+f_t
=\sum_i\left(f_{q_i}H_{p_i}-f_{p_i}H_{q_i}\right)+f_t.
$$

Therefore the [Hamiltonian conservation law](../../../../../hamiltonian-conservation-law.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{df}{dt}=\{f,H\}+\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}.}
$$

The total [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) follows the trajectory; the explicit [partial derivative](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) holds $(\mathbf q,\mathbf p)$ fixed.
