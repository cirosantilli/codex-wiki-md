<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) is antisymmetric, so $\{H,H\}=0$. The [Hamiltonian energy balance](../../../../../hamiltonian-energy-balance.md) consequently gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{dH}{dt}=\frac{\partial H}{\partial t}=0.}
$$

For a [regular Lagrangian](../../../../../regular-lagrangian.md) the [Legendre transform in mechanics](../../../../../legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) gives $H_t=-L_t$, evaluated at the corresponding velocity. In particular, a [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) with no explicit time dependence has [time-translation symmetry](../../../../../time-translation-symmetry.md). [Noether theorem](../../../../../noether-theorem.md) associates this continuous [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of the [action](../../../../../action.md) with the [conserved quantity](../../../../../conserved-quantity.md) $\sum_i\dot q_i\partial L/\partial\dot q_i-L=H$. For an ordinary natural mechanical [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) $L=T-V$, with $T$ quadratic in the velocities, this quantity is the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $T+V$.
