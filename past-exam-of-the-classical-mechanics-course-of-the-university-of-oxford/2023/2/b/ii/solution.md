<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [symmetric top](../../../../../symmetric-top.md), set $I_1=I_2=J$. The third of [Euler's equations](../../../../../euler-s-equations-rigid-body-dynamics.md) reduces to $I_3\dot\omega_3=\tau_3=0$. Thus the axial [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) component is a [conserved quantity](../../../../../conserved-quantity.md):

$$
\boxed{\omega_3(t)=\omega_3(0).}
$$

The transverse [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) components are generally variable. A [torque](../../../../../torque.md) constant in the [principal body frame](../../../../../principal-body-frame.md) generally rotates in the [inertial frame](../../../../../inertial-frame.md); constancy in the two frames is different.
