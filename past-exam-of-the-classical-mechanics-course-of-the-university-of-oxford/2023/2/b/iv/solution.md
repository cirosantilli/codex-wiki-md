<h1 id="2/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Now take zero [torque](../../../../../torque.md) and $\boldsymbol\omega(0)=(1,0,1)$. The axial [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) is $\omega_3=1$ and the complex transverse component obeys $\dot z=i\nu z$ with $z(0)=1$ and $\nu=(I_3-I_1)/I_1$. Hence the [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) components in the [principal body frame](../../../../../principal-body-frame.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf L_S(t)=\left(I_1\cos\nu t,\ I_1\sin\nu t,\ I_3\right),\qquad
\nu=\frac{I_3-I_1}{I_1}.}
$$

The transverse projection therefore traces a [circle](../../../../../circle.md) with signed [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\nu$. Its magnitude is constant, $L=\sqrt{I_1^2+I_3^2}$.

In the [inertial frame](../../../../../inertial-frame.md), zero [torque](../../../../../torque.md) means that $\mathbf L$ is fixed. Using

$$
\mathbf L=I_1\boldsymbol\omega+(I_3-I_1)\omega_3\mathbf e_3
$$

and the [derivative of a body-fixed basis vector](../../../../../derivative-of-a-body-fixed-basis-vector.md), the symmetry axis satisfies

$$
\dot{\mathbf e}_3=\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf e_3
=\frac{\mathbf L}{I_1}\times\mathbf e_3.
$$

Thus there is uniform [torque-free precession of a symmetric top](../../../../../torque-free-precession-of-a-symmetric-top.md) about the fixed [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md), at a constant cone [angle](../../../../../angle.md) $\beta$ and space [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md)

$$
\boxed{\cos\beta=\frac{I_3}{\sqrt{I_1^2+I_3^2}},\qquad
\Omega_{\mathrm{space}}=\frac{\sqrt{I_1^2+I_3^2}}{I_1}.}
$$

The cone [angle](../../../../../angle.md) stays constant, with no [nutation](../../../../../nutation.md). The [body-frame and space-frame frequencies of free symmetric-top precession](../../../../../body-frame-and-space-frame-frequencies-of-free-symmetric-top-precession.md) differ: $\nu$ describes rotating components, whereas $\Omega_{\mathrm{space}}$ describes the symmetry axis in space. The formulas also cover $I_3=I_1$, where the [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) components in the [principal body frame](../../../../../principal-body-frame.md) are constant.
