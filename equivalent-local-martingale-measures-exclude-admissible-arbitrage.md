# Equivalent local martingale measures exclude admissible arbitrage

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalent local martingale measure](equivalent-local-martingale-measure.md)

Suppose a finite-horizon market has a strictly positive [numéraire](numeraire.md) $B$ and an equivalent measure under which all prices $S^i/B$ are [local martingales](local-martingale.md). The discounted wealth of a [self-financing strategy](self-financing-portfolio.md) is a [stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md) against those prices. If it is bounded below by a deterministic constant, that wealth is a [supermartingale](supermartingale.md): add the lower-bound constant to obtain a [nonnegative local martingale](nonnegative-local-martingale.md), then apply conditional Fatou to a localizing sequence. A zero-cost admissible [portfolio](investment-portfolio.md) therefore has expected discounted terminal wealth at most zero. Nonnegative terminal wealth with positive probability of a strict gain would have strictly positive expectation under the equivalent measure, a contradiction. Thus there is no admissible [arbitrage](arbitrage.md). True [martingales](martingale-split.md) are not needed for this implication.
