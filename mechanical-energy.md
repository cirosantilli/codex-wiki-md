# Mechanical energy

↑ **Parent:** [Energy](energy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_energy)

Mechanical energy is the sum of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](potential-energy.md). It is conserved when all forces are conservative and the potential has no explicit time dependence.
