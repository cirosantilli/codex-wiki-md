# Transition intensity matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

For a finite-state [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md), a transition intensity matrix has nonnegative off-diagonal rates $q_{rs}$ and $q_{rr}=-\sum_{s\ne r}q_{rs}$. Every row sums to zero. In the time-homogeneous case its [transition probability matrix](transition-semigroup-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) $P(t)=e^{tQ}$. An [absorbing state](absorbing-state.md) has a zero row.
