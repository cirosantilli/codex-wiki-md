# Separately conserved cosmological fluids

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md)

A noninteracting [perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) has $\nabla_\mu T_i^{\mu\nu}=0$, so $\dot\rho_i+3H(\rho_i+P_i)=0$. If energy is transferred between species, source terms $Q_i$ appear and only their sum vanishes. The [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) still involves the sum of all component stress tensors because all components share one spacetime metric.
