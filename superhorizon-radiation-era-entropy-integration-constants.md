# Superhorizon radiation-era entropy integration constants

↑ **Parent:** [Superhorizon adiabatic CDM mode in radiation domination](superhorizon-adiabatic-cdm-mode-in-radiation-domination.md)

In the leading [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md) limit, neglect $k^2\delta_r$ and the matter contribution to gravity. The radiation equation gives $S''=0$. Substitution into $\delta_c''+\delta_c'/\tau=3\delta_r/\tau^2$ gives

$$
\delta_c=C_g\tau^2+C_d\tau^{-2}-\frac34S_0-S_1\tau,\qquad
\delta_r=\frac43C_g\tau^2+\frac43C_d\tau^{-2}-\frac13S_1\tau.
$$

Thus [adiabatic initial conditions](adiabatic-initial-conditions.md) select both $S_0=0$ and $S_1=0$, leaving the growing and decaying power-law modes. The four constants describe the leading supplied coupled system; regularity and additional initial [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) constraints can further restrict their physical interpretation.
