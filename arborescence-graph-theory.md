# Arborescence (graph theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Oriented tree](oriented-tree.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arborescence_(graph_theory))

An arborescence is a rooted [oriented tree](oriented-tree.md) whose edges all point away from its root. Reversing every edge gives an in-arborescence. An $n$-vertex arborescence embeds in every [tournament](tournament-graph-theory.md) on $2n-2$ vertices: repeatedly extend an outward leaf in a [half-sparse median-order embedding](half-sparse-median-order-embedding.md). Reversal gives the analogous existence statement for in-arborescences.
