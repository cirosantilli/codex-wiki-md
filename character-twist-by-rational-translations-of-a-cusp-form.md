# Character twist by rational translations of a cusp form

↑ **Parent:** [Cusp form](cusp-form.md)

For a level-one [cusp form](cusp-form.md) and any [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) modulo $N>1$, this translation sum is a [cusp form](cusp-form.md) on $\Gamma_1(N)\cap\Gamma_0(N^2)$. Conjugating that subgroup by $\begin{pmatrix}1&j/N\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ yields integral determinant-one [matrices](matrix.md); [cusp holomorphy under rational slash operators](cusp-holomorphy-under-rational-slash-operators.md) supplies all cusp conditions. Its exact [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) at positive indices are $a_n(f)\sum_{j\in(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times}\chi(j)^{-1}e^{2\pi inj/N}$. For a [primitive Dirichlet character](primitive-dirichlet-character.md) they equal $g(\overline\chi)\chi(n)a_n(f)$, by the [finite Fourier transform of a primitive Dirichlet character](finite-fourier-transform-of-a-primitive-dirichlet-character.md). For imprimitive characters the sum can be nonzero at nonunits, so the simplified twist formula need not hold.
