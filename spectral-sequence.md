# Spectral sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Homological algebra](homological-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_sequence)

A succession of bigraded pages with $E_{r+1}=H(E_r,d_r)$. In the cohomological convention, the differential has bidegree $(r,1-r)$. A filtered complex produces such successive approximations to its [cohomology](cohomology-split.md).
