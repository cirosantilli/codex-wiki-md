[https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/cassini.pdf](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/cassini.pdf)

The background discussion of Saturn states helium-to-molecular-hydrogen ratios and relates upper-atmosphere [helium](helium.md) depletion to settling [helium](helium.md) and additional [heat](heat-split.md). It is evidence for keeping He/H distinct from its reciprocal, not a current precision composition measurement.
