# Generalized Bernoulli number

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli number](bernoulli-number.md)

For a [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) $\chi$ of conductor $f$, define the generalized Bernoulli numbers by

$$
\sum_{a=1}^{f}\chi(a)\frac{z e^{az}}{e^{fz}-1}
=\sum_{k\geq0}B_{k,\chi}\frac{z^k}{k!}.
$$

They express special values of [Dirichlet L-functions](dirichlet-l-function.md) at nonpositive integers and occur in the interpolation formula for [Kubota-Leopoldt p-adic L-functions](kubota-leopoldt-p-adic-l-function.md).
