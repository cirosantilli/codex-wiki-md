# Unstable asymmetric cycles of a cubic swap map

↑ **Parent:** [Cubic swap-map two-cycle factorization](cubic-swap-map-two-cycle-factorization.md)

The nonzero-sum [two-cycles](period-two-orbit.md) of the cubic swap map exist for $a>3$. Their two-step [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) has [trace](matrix-trace.md) $T$ and [determinant](determinant.md) $D$ as displayed. Since $1-T+D=-2(a-3)(a+1)<0$, one [periodic-orbit multiplier](multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) exceeds one, proving instability throughout. The other crosses minus one at $a=(1+\sqrt{29})/2$; the cycles are saddles before that threshold and repelling afterwards. At $a=3$ they meet the fixed branches in an unstable subcritical-flip branch.
