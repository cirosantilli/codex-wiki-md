# Ice floe

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)

An ice floe is a connected floating piece of [sea ice](sea-ice.md). Its diameter relative to [wavelength](wavelength.md) and its thickness affect the reflection, transmission and dissipation of [surface gravity waves](surface-gravity-wave.md).
