# Presynaptic filament

↑ **Parent:** [DNA repair](dna-repair.md)

A [presynaptic filament](presynaptic-filament.md) in [homologous recombination](homologous-recombination.md) is the [recombinase](recombinase.md)–[single-stranded DNA](single-stranded-dna.md) assembly formed before productive donor pairing. Distinguish its formation from later donor capture and [DNA strand exchange](dna-strand-exchange.md).
