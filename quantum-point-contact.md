# Quantum point contact

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md)

A short electrostatically defined constriction connecting two [electron](electron.md) reservoirs. Transverse confinement produces transport modes, and sufficiently narrow contacts can collimate carriers for electron-focusing experiments. Its quantum conductance depends on mode transmission; an opening described only as narrower than a micrometre is not automatically in a single-mode limit.
