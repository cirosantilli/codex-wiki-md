# Matrix ring

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_ring)

The [matrix ring](matrix-ring.md) consists of all $n\times n$ [matrices](matrix.md) over a [ring](ring.md) $R$, using entrywise [addition](addition.md) and [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md). Its [identity element](identity-element.md) is the [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md). Even when $R$ is a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md), this [ring](ring.md) need not have [commutative](commutativity.md) [multiplication](multiplication.md).
