# Horizon frequency comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity](surface-gravity.md)

Let $K=\partial_v$ generate a [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md) with constant [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) $\kappa$, so $\nabla_KK=\kappa K$. In [geometric optics](geometrical-optics-split.md), an affinely transported [light](light.md) [wavevector](wavevector.md) on the same generator is $k=q(v)K$. The [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) gives $q'+\kappa q=0$. Observers related by the [isometry](isometry.md) generated by $K$ have the same $-K\cdot W$ at their crossings, where $W$ is their [four-velocity](four-velocity.md), so their measured [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) $\omega=-k\cdot W$ obey the displayed relation. A [degenerate Killing horizon](degenerate-killing-horizon.md) gives ratio one for equally prepared observers; changing their crossing velocities can produce a separate [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md).
