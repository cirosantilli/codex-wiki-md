# Homotopy invariance of cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Induced map on cohomology](induced-map-on-cohomology.md)

Homotopic continuous maps induce the same map on [cohomology](cohomology-split.md). In particular, a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md) induces an isomorphism of [cohomology rings](cohomology-ring.md).
