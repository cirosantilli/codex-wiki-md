# Lee-Yang proposal for a parity test

↑ **Parent:** [Parity violation](parity-violation.md)

In 1956, [Tsung-Dao Lee](tsung-dao-lee.md) and [Chen Ning Yang](chen-ning-yang.md) examined the evidence for [parity conservation](parity-conservation.md) in the [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md). Their paper, Question of Parity Conservation in Weak Interactions, distinguished an untested assumption from an established experimental result and proposed comparing [electron](electron.md) emission along opposite directions relative to oriented nuclear [spins](spin.md), explicitly considering [cobalt-60](cobalt-60.md). It proposed a test; it did not report the later [Wu experiment](wu-experiment.md).[pp. 254–255; evidence review and “Possible Experimental Tests of Parity Conservation in beta Decays,” first proposal](question-of-parity-conservation-in-weak-interactions-by-tsung-dao-lee-and-chen-ning-yang.md)

The conceptual improvement is the choice of observable. An angle between two [polar vectors](polar-vector.md) is preserved by [spatial reflection](spatial-reflection.md); its distribution can conceal [parity violation](parity-violation.md). An angle between an [axial vector](pseudovector.md) and a [polar vector](polar-vector.md) changes to its supplement. Thus a [spin](spin.md)-resolved directional measurement asks a different question from an unpolarized total [decay rate](decay-width.md).
