# Axiom of extensionality

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiom_of_extensionality)

Two [sets](set-split.md) with exactly the same members are equal. In a [transitive model](transitive-model.md), all actual members of its sets are available internally, so ambient extensionality gives the internal axiom as well.
