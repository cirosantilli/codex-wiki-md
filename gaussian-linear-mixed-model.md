# Gaussian linear mixed model

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized linear mixed model](generalized-linear-mixed-model.md)

A Gaussian linear mixed model combines [fixed effects](fixed-effect.md), [random effects](random-effect.md) with a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md), and independent [Gaussian noise](gaussian-noise.md). For $b\sim N(0,D)$ and $\varepsilon\sim N(0,R)$ independently, its marginal distribution is $Y\sim N(X\beta,V)$ with $V=ZDZ^T+R$. The [random effects](random-effect.md) induce dependence among observations sharing their design columns. A [random-intercept linear mixed model](random-intercept-linear-mixed-model.md) and a [random-slope linear mixed model](random-slope-linear-mixed-model.md) are special cases.
