# Exterior-power Lie algebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

A [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) on $V$ induces one on each [exterior power](exterior-power.md) by acting on every factor and summing. The [tensor product of Lie algebra representations](tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md) preserves the defining alternating relations, so this descends to the quotient in every [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md). In [characteristic two](characteristic-two.md) define the [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md) by the relations $v\wedge v=0$, rather than by dividing a tensor antisymmetrizer by $2$.
