# Self-avoiding walk generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md)

The [generating function](generating-function.md) weights each [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md) by $x$ to the power of its length, including the zero-length walk. Its [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) is the reciprocal of the upper exponential growth rate of the rooted counts, by the [Cauchy-Hadamard theorem](cauchy-hadamard-theorem.md).
