# Full local reflection in a one-sentence extension is inconsistent

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md)

Assume the [Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions](hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md) and the [diagonal lemma](diagonal-lemma.md). If $T+X$ proves $\operatorname{Pr}_T(\ulcorner Y\urcorner)\to Y$ for every sentence $Y$, then $T\vdash\neg X$. Take the single instance $Y=\neg X$ and discharge the assumption: $T\vdash X\to(\Box\neg X\to\neg X)$. This propositionally implies $T\vdash\Box\neg X\to\neg X$, and [Löb's theorem](lob-s-theorem.md) gives $T\vdash\neg X$. A diagonal proof is also possible using the [Gödel sentence](godel-sentence.md) of the extension and formalized proof transfer, without appealing to Löb's theorem.
