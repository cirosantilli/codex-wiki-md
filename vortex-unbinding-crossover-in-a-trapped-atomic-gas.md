# Vortex-unbinding crossover in a trapped atomic gas

↑ **Parent:** [Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition](berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-transition.md)

In 2006, [Zoran Hadzibabic](zoran-hadzibabic.md), [Peter Krüger](peter-kruger.md), [Marc Cheneau](marc-cheneau.md), [Baptiste Battelier](baptiste-battelier.md) and [Jean Dalibard](jean-dalibard.md) split a cooled [rubidium](rubidium.md)-87 cloud into two independent planar gases using an [optical lattice](optical-lattice.md). After release, the expanding sheets overlapped; [absorption imaging](absorption-imaging.md) recorded their [matter-wave interference](matter-wave-interference.md). Smooth fringe waviness revealed [thermal phase fluctuations](thermal-phase-fluctuation.md), while sharp dislocations indicated isolated [vortices](phase-vortex.md). The initial evaporative-cooling endpoint controlled the preparation; central fringe contrast served as a degeneracy proxy because direct thermometry in the lattice was difficult.[Pages 1–2, preparation and readout; Fig. 1; temperature control and Fig. 2](berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-crossover-in-a-trapped-atomic-gas-by-zoran-hadzibabic-and-others.md)

Loss of extended coherence coincided with increased vortex signatures. The [optical trap](optical-tweezers.md) and finite cloud produced a crossover; tightly bound pairs were unresolved, and short-wavelength modes could contaminate high-temperature counts. These were observations of the [vortex](phase-vortex.md)-unbinding mechanism, not a direct measurement of [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md) or a sharp homogeneous [universal stiffness jump](universal-stiffness-jump.md). The authors left the sample's [superfluidity](superfluidity.md) open.[Pages 3–4, Figs. 3–4; geometry, invisible pairs, detection limits and final paragraph on unmeasured superfluidity](berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-crossover-in-a-trapped-atomic-gas-by-zoran-hadzibabic-and-others.md)
