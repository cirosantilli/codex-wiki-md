# Joint injectivity of a pair of functions

↑ **Parent:** [Universal property of Cartesian products](universal-property-of-cartesian-products.md)

The [pairing map to a Cartesian product](universal-property-of-cartesian-products.md) is [injective](injective-function.md) exactly when its coordinate [functions](function-split.md) are jointly injective in the displayed sense. Either coordinate being [injective](injective-function.md) is sufficient, but neither need be [injective](injective-function.md) individually. For example, pairing three points with $(0,0),(0,1),(1,0)$ distinguishes all points even though each coordinate separately identifies two of them.
