# Transverse nuclear magnetization decay

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch equations](bloch-equations.md)

[Transverse relaxation](transverse-relaxation.md) describes loss of ensemble transverse [magnetization](magnetization.md). It must not automatically be identified with pure [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md). In a simple completely positive two-level relaxation model,

$$
\frac1{T_2}=\frac1{2T_1}+\frac1{T_\phi},
$$

where $T_\phi$ describes additional pure [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md). Thus population recovery itself contributes to transverse decay. A spread of static [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md) can further cancel ensemble phases without the same microscopic relaxation; an observed free signal may have an effective $T_2^*$ rather than homogeneous $T_2$. The simple exponential examples below assume a uniform field and a single homogeneous $T_2$.
