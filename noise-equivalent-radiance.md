# Noise-equivalent radiance

↑ **Parent:** [Noise-equivalent power](noise-equivalent-power.md)

[Noise-equivalent radiance](noise-equivalent-radiance.md) is the incident [radiance](radiance.md) change producing signal-to-noise ratio one for a specified spectral and electrical bandwidth. A linear [radiometer](radiometer.md) with voltage response $G=dV/dB$ and RMS voltage noise $\sigma_V$ has $\mathrm{NER}=\sigma_V/|G|$. Its convention must distinguish per-sample noise from a noise amplitude per square-root bandwidth.
