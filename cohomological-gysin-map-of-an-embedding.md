# Cohomological Gysin map of an embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Gysin sequence of an embedding](gysin-sequence-of-an-embedding.md)

For a closed [smooth embedding](smooth-embedding.md) $i:N\hookrightarrow M$ of codimension $r$ whose [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) is oriented over a coefficient ring $R$, a [tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md), [excision](excision-theorem.md) and the [Thom isomorphism theorem](thom-isomorphism-theorem.md) identify

$$
H^{q-r}(N;R)\cong H^q(M,M\setminus N;R).
$$

The relative-to-absolute map defines $i_!:H^j(N;R)\to H^{j+r}(M;R)$. For closed oriented $M,N$, it is characterized by

$$
\langle i_!b\smile c,[M]\rangle=\langle b\smile i^*c,[N]\rangle
$$

with compatible orientations. It obeys $i_!(b\smile i^*c)=i_!b\smile c$. If $j:M\setminus N\hookrightarrow M$ and $\delta:H^q(M\setminus N;R)\to H^{q+1-r}(N;R)$ is the connecting map followed by the inverse Thom isomorphism, then, over $\mathbb F_2$,

$$
\delta(j^*c\smile u)=i^*c\smile\delta u.
$$

These identities follow from naturality of the relative [cup product](cup-product.md) and show how the exact sequence determines multiplication in complements. Over $\mathbb F_2$ every real [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) has the required orientation.
