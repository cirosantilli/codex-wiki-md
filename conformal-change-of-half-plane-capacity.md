# Conformal change of half-plane capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md)

Suppose a capacity-parameterized [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md) lies in a domain on which $\psi$ is a [conformal map](conformal-map.md). For its image chain set $\psi_t=\widetilde g_t\circ\psi\circ g_t^{-1}$. Then the image [Loewner driving function](loewner-driving-function.md) is $\widetilde U_t=\psi_t(U_t)$ and

$$
d\operatorname{hcap}(\widetilde A_t)=2\psi_t'(U_t)^2\,dt.
$$

The derivative is real by the [Schwarz reflection principle](schwarz-reflection-principle.md). Cancellation of the pole in the differentiated conjugacy identity proves the squared-derivative rule.
