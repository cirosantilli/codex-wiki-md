# Canonical transverse photon field

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical quantization of the electromagnetic field](canonical-quantization-of-the-electromagnetic-field.md)

The free transverse [photon](photon.md) field is a Hermitian sum of two transverse-polarization plane-wave modes with creation and [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md). Their standard bosonic [commutator](commutator.md) gives the [transverse equal-time commutator](transverse-equal-time-commutator.md). The normal-ordered [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is a sum of positive oscillator energies.
