# Camera lucida

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camera_lucida)

A camera lucida superposes the apparent image of an object on a drawing surface, allowing [microscope](microscope.md) positions to be marked by hand.
