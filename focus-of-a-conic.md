# Focus of a conic

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)

In the focus-directrix description of a noncircular [conic section](conic-section.md), the focus is the fixed point $F$ for which every point $X$ of the curve satisfies $|X-F|=e\,\operatorname{dist}(X,L)$, where $L$ is a [directrix of a conic](directrix-of-a-conic.md) and $e$ is the [eccentricity of a conic](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md). For $y^2=4ax$ with $a>0$, the [focus of a conic](focus-of-a-conic.md) is $(a,0)$, the [directrix of a conic](directrix-of-a-conic.md) is $x=-a$, and $e=1$.
