# Tabloid bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Modular Specht module](modular-specht-module.md)

The tabloid bilinear form makes the tabloid basis orthonormal and restricts to an invariant [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) on each [Specht module](specht-module.md). In positive [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md), its radical controls the corresponding simple quotient.
