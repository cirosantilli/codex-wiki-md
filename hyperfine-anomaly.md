# Hyperfine anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperfine interaction](hyperfine-interaction.md)

A departure of ratios of magnetic-dipole [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) constants from ratios of nuclear [gyromagnetic ratios](gyromagnetic-ratio.md). Finite spatial distributions of nuclear charge and magnetization alter electronic contact fields, so even the same nominal electronic [energy level](energy-level.md) does not provide an exactly isotope-independent proportionality constant.
