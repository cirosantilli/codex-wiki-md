# Thermal cutoff of Lyman-alpha line widths

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal Doppler broadening](thermal-doppler-broadening.md)

A purely thermal hydrogen [absorption line](absorption-line.md) has velocity-space optical depth proportional to $\exp[-(v-v_0)^2/b^2]$ with the displayed width. Additional independent Gaussian broadening contributes positive terms to $b^2$. Fitting the lower envelope of width against neutral-hydrogen [column density](column-density.md), with numerical calibration for density and pressure smoothing, estimates the [temperature-density relation of photoionized intergalactic gas](temperature-density-relation-of-photoionized-intergalactic-gas.md). A single broad line only bounds the temperature from above when its nonthermal width is unknown.
