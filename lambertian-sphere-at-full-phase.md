# Lambertian sphere at full phase

↑ **Parent:** [Lambertian reflectance](lambertian-reflectance.md)

A spherical body of [radius](radius.md) R observed from distance $d\gg R$ is illuminated and viewed from the same direction, with incident [irradiance](irradiance.md) $F_\star$. For uniform [Lambertian reflectance](lambertian-reflectance.md) A, a patch at angle $\theta$ has outgoing [radiance](radiance.md) $AF_\star\cos\theta/\pi$. The observer receives this radiance times projected area divided by $d^2$. Since $\int_{\mathrm{hemisphere}}\cos^2\theta\,dS=2\pi R^2/3$, integration proves the displayed full-phase [irradiance](irradiance.md). Its [geometric albedo](geometric-albedo.md) is $2A/3$. Total reflected power alone does not specify the brightness in one direction for a non-Lambertian surface.
