# Square-root tail bound for martingale absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Absorption-time bound from conditional variance and overshoot](absorption-time-bound-from-conditional-variance-and-overshoot.md)

Under the conditional-[variance](variance-split.md) and overshoot assumptions of the [absorption-time bound from conditional variance and overshoot](absorption-time-bound-from-conditional-variance-and-overshoot.md), $\mathbb P(T>n)\leq2\tau/(\sigma\sqrt n)$. Bound the probability by $\mathbb P(T_R<\infty)+\mathbb E(T\wedge T_R)/n$ and optimize $1/R+\tau^2R/(\sigma^2n)$. If the resulting bound is below one, its minimizing threshold is greater than one; otherwise the probability bound is trivial.
