# Hyperbolic area

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md)

The metric area of a [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md). In the [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md) the density is $dx\,dy/y^2$. A finite-area torsion-free quotient of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$ with $r$ cusps has area $2\pi(2g-2+r)$ by the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md).
