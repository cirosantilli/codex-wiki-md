# Veneziano amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Open-string scattering amplitude](open-string-scattering-amplitude.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veneziano_amplitude)

The ordered tree-level four-tachyon [open string](open-string.md) amplitude is proportional to $\Gamma(-1-\alpha's)\Gamma(-1-\alpha't)/\Gamma(-2-\alpha'(s+t))$. Its [Mandelstam variables](mandelstam-variables.md) locate the mass levels, and pole residues reveal spin content.
