<h1 id="a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Independent increments](../../../../../independent-increments.md) give $B_s\sim N(0,s)$ independent of $B_t-B_s\sim N(0,t-s)$. Their [joint probability density function](../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) at $(x,v)$ is the product

$$
\boxed{\frac1{2\pi\sqrt{s(t-s)}}\exp\left(-\frac{x^2}{2s}-\frac{v^2}{2(t-s)}\right).}
$$

The [change of variables](../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $(x,v)\mapsto(x,y)=(x,x+v)$ has [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) one. Therefore the [joint probability density function](../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) of $(B_s,B_t)$ is

$$
\boxed{\frac1{2\pi\sqrt{s(t-s)}}\exp\left(-\frac{x^2}{2s}-\frac{(y-x)^2}{2(t-s)}\right).}
$$

In particular these coordinates are correlated, with [covariance](../../../../../covariance.md) $s$, although the first pair has independent coordinates.
