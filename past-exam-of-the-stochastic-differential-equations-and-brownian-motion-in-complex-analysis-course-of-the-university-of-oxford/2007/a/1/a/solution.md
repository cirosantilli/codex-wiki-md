The raw [natural filtration](../../../../../natural-filtration.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal F_t^B=\sigma(B_u:0\leq u\leq t).}
$$

Equivalently it is the smallest [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) making every observation $B_u$, $u\leq t$, [measurable](../../../../../measurability.md). As $s\leq t$ enlarges the set of observations, $\mathcal F_s^B\subseteq\mathcal F_t^B$. It is the smallest [filtration](../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) to which the process is [adapted](../../../../../adapted-process.md). If the [usual conditions for a filtration](../../../../../usual-conditions-for-a-filtration.md) are imposed, take the completion by the probability-zero sets and its right-continuous augmentation. Brownian [independent increments](../../../../../independent-increments.md) remain valid for this usual augmentation. Adding arbitrary future information is not part of the generated [filtration](../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md).
