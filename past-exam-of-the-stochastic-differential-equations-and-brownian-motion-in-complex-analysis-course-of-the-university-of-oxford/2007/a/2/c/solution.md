<h1 id="a/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](../../../../../levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md) says that a [continuous local martingale](../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md) $M$ with $M_0=0$ is standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) with respect to its [filtration](../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) if its [quadratic variation](../../../../../quadratic-variation.md) is $\langle M\rangle_t=t$. Equivalently one may require $M_t^2-t$ to be a [local martingale](../../../../../local-martingale.md).

Any [weak solution](../../../../../weak-solution.md) has the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) form $Z_t=\int_0^t\operatorname{sgn}(Z_s-2)\,dW_s$. Its bounded [integrand](../../../../../integrand.md) makes it a [continuous local martingale](../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md) starting at zero, and

$$
\langle Z\rangle_t=\int_0^t\operatorname{sgn}(Z_s-2)^2ds=t.
$$

The specified value of the sign at zero has square one as well. [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](../../../../../levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md) therefore proves that **every such [weak solution](../../../../../weak-solution.md) is [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md)**. This is a statement about its law and [filtration](../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md); it does not assert $Z=W$ or provide a strong solution as a function of a specified driver.
