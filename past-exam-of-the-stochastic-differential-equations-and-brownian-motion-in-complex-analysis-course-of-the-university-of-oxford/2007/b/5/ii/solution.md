<h1 id="b/5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $z\neq w$, apply translation, rotation and [Brownian scaling](../../../../../brownian-scaling.md) to

$$
\widetilde Z_t=1+\frac{Z_{|z-w|^2t}-z}{z-w}
=\frac{Z_{|z-w|^2t}-w}{z-w}.
$$

This is standard [planar Brownian motion](../../../../../planar-brownian-motion.md) started at one. Its almost-sure visits to arbitrarily small discs about zero translate to visits of $Z$ to discs about $w$, with radii multiplied by $|z-w|$. Therefore **the same neighborhood recurrence holds for every pair $z,w$**. If $z=w$, every such [disc](../../../../../topological-disc.md) is already hit at time zero. If positive hitting times are requested, [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) gives positive times within every fixed [disc](../../../../../topological-disc.md) as well. A countable sequence of shrinking radii suffices to obtain one probability-one event for this assertion.
