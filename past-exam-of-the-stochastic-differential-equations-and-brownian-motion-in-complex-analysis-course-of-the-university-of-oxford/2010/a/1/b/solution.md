<h1 id="a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use $s$ for the dummy integration variable in the printed integral. The [Itô isometry](../../../../../ito-isometry.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E|z_t^{(n+1)}|^2=C\int_0^t\mathbb E|z_s^{(n)}|^2\,ds
\leq\frac{C^{n+1}}{n!}\int_0^t s^n\,ds
=\boxed{\frac{C^{n+1}t^{n+1}}{(n+1)!}.}
$$

The assumed second-moment bound also proves the integrability needed to apply the [Itô isometry](../../../../../ito-isometry.md). The extra time integration is the source of the factorial improvement.
