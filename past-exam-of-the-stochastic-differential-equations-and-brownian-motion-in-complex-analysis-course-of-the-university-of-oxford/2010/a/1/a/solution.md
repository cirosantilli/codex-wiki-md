<h1 id="a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the [stochastic differential equation](../../../../../stochastic-differential-equation.md) in integral form and replace the unknown integrand by the preceding [Picard iterate](../../../../../picard-iterate.md). With the deterministic initial vector $a$, the recursion is

$$
\boxed{y_t^{(0)}=a,\qquad y_t^{(m+1)}=a+\sum_{i=1}^d\int_0^t A^iy_s^{(m)}\,dB_s^i\quad(m\geq0).}
$$

Each iterate is a continuous adapted process. The [Itô isometry](../../../../../ito-isometry.md) inductively gives its square integrability on every bounded time interval, so all these [Itô integrals](../../../../../ito-integral.md) are well defined.
