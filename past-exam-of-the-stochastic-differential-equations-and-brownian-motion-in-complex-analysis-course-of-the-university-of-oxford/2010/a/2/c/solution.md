<h1 id="a/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Itô formula](../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md) for $f(x)=x^4$ gives

$$
d(B_t^4)=4B_t^3dB_t+6B_t^2dt.
$$

The stochastic integral has expectation zero: its square integrability on a finite horizon follows from the finite sixth moments of a [normal distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md). Since $\mathbb EB_s^2=s$, integration and expectation yield

$$
\boxed{\mathbb EB_t^4=6\int_0^t s\,ds=3t^2.}
$$
