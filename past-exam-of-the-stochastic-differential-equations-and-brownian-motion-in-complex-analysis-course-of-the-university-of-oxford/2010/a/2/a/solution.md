↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) is centered, so $m_t=0$. If $s\leq t$, write $B_t=B_s+(B_t-B_s)$; the final increment is independent of $B_s$ and has mean zero. Thus

$$
\boxed{m_t=0,\qquad\phi_{st}=\mathbb E(B_sB_t)=\min(s,t).}
$$

This is the [Brownian covariance kernel](../../../../../brownian-covariance-kernel.md). Together with joint Gaussianity and continuous paths, it gives the [Gaussian-process characterization of Brownian motion](../../../../../gaussian-process-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md).
