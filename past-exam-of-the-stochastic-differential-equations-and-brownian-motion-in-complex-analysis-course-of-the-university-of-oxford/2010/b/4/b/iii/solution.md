<h1 id="b/4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\alpha=\pi q/a\in(0,\pi)$. With $q$ and $a$ fixed,

$$
\frac{1+A}{B}\longrightarrow\frac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}=\cot\alpha.
$$

The principal [arctangent](../../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) satisfies $\arctan(\cot\alpha)=\pi/2-\alpha$ throughout this interval. Substitution gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_{p\to\infty}\mathbb P(\operatorname{Im}Z_{T_S}=a)=q/a.}
$$

Far from the vertical boundary, the limit agrees with the one-dimensional [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) probability of reaching height $a$ before zero.
