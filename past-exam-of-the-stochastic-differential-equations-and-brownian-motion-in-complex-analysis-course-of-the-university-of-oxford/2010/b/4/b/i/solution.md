<h1 id="b/4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $f:D\to D'$ is a [conformal map](../../../../../../conformal-map.md), the image of planar [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) in $D$ is Brownian motion in $D'$ after the increasing clock change

$$
A_t=\int_0^t|f'(Z_s)|^2ds.
$$

More precisely, $f(Z_{A^{-1}(u)})$ is planar Brownian motion, starting at $f(Z_0)$ and run up to the relevant exit time. The [Itô formula](../../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md) and the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give image-coordinate local martingales with common bracket $A$ and zero cross bracket; the [Lévy characterization of multidimensional Brownian motion](../../../../../../levy-characterization-of-multidimensional-brownian-motion.md) identifies the time-changed process. In particular, [conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion](../../../../../../conformal-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md) transports exit locations and [harmonic measure](../../../../../../harmonic-measure.md), while changing traversal speed.
