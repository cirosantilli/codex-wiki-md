<h1 id="b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The disk exit time is finite almost surely; for example, either coordinate of the planar [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) eventually leaves $(-1,1)$. By path continuity, the exit lies on the unit circle. Starting at the origin, rotational invariance of [planar Brownian motion](../../../../../planar-brownian-motion.md) makes the exit law invariant under every circle rotation. The unique rotation-invariant probability measure is normalized [arc length](../../../../../arc-length.md). Consequently **$Z_{T_D}$ is uniform on the unit circle**, or equivalently its angle has density $1/(2\pi)$ on $[0,2\pi)$.
