<h1 id="b/5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By [Brownian scaling](../../../../../../brownian-scaling.md), $r^{-1}Z_{r^2t}$ is planar Brownian motion started at $w_0/r$. Its unit-disk exit angle is exactly the original radius-$r$ exit angle. Substitute $z_0=w_0/r$ into the supplied [Poisson kernel for the unit disk](../../../../../../poisson-kernel-for-the-unit-disk.md):

$$
\boxed{P^{w_0}(\theta_{T_{D_r}}\in d\theta)
=\frac{r^2-|w_0|^2}{2\pi|w_0-r e^{i\theta}|^2}\,d\theta.}
$$

This density is with respect to angle $d\theta$. Its density with respect to [arc length](../../../../../../arc-length.md) would be smaller by a factor $r$.
