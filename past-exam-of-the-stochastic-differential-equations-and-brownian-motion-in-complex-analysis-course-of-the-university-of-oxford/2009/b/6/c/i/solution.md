<h1 id="b/6/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $Z_t=X_t+iY_t$ and $u=\operatorname{Re}g$. Since $g$ is entire, $u$ is a [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md), so $u_{xx}+u_{yy}=0$. The two-dimensional [Itô formula](../../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md) gives

$$
du(Z_t)=u_x(Z_t)\,dX_t+u_y(Z_t)\,dY_t
+\tfrac12(u_{xx}+u_{yy})(Z_t)\,dt.
$$

The drift vanishes. Stop on successively larger disks to bound the derivatives and define the stochastic integrals without global growth assumptions. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}g(Z_t)\text{ is a local martingale}.}
$$

This is the [harmonic functions of Brownian motion](../../../../../../harmonic-functions-of-brownian-motion.md) principle.
