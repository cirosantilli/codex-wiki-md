↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An analytic function is an [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md) when it is **holomorphic on the whole complex plane**. Thus every finite complex point has a neighborhood on which the function is complex differentiable; there is no finite singularity. [polynomials](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) and the exponential are entire, whereas $1/z$ is not.
