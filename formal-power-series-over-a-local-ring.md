# Formal power series over a local ring

↑ **Parent:** [Formal power series](formal-power-series.md)

If $(A,\mathfrak m)$ is a [local ring](local-ring.md), then $B=A[[x]]$ is local with [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) $I=\mathfrak mB+xB$ and [residue field](residue-field.md) $A/\mathfrak m$. A series $f=\sum a_nx^n$ is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) if and only if $a_0$ is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md). Necessity follows by taking constant coefficients of an inverse; sufficiency follows from the recursion $b_0=a_0^{-1}$ and $b_n=-a_0^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^na_ib_{n-i}$ for its inverse. Thus the nonunits are precisely series with $a_0\in\mathfrak m$, which is exactly $I$, since $f=a_0+xg$. The constant-coefficient map followed by reduction modulo $\mathfrak m$ has [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) $I$ and image $A/\mathfrak m$.
