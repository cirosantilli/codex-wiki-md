# Angular reconstruction of a radial orbit transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Radial orbit time transformation](radial-orbit-time-transformation.md)

A transformed radial equation containing $L^2/(2q^2)$ becomes a full planar central-force orbit by choosing $d\bar\theta/d\tau=L/q^2$. For $d\tau/dt=(q/r)dq/dr$ and old [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $L/r^2$, this gives $d\bar\theta/d\theta=(r/q)dq/dr$. Keeping the old angle generally fails to preserve the stated transformed [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
