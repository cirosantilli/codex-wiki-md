# Absorption at zero of a nonnegative martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

Once a nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md) hits zero, it stays zero almost surely. [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) on $\{M_n=0\}$ gives $\mathbb E[M_{n+1}\mathbf1_{\{M_n=0\}}]=0$; nonnegativity forces the next value to vanish there. A countable intersection makes these assertions simultaneous for all times, proving absorption at the first zero.
