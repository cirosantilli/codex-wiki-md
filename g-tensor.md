# g tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic anisotropy](magnetic-anisotropy.md)

The tensor connecting [spin](spin.md) to magnetic moment in an effective [spin](spin.md) Hamiltonian. Its principal values specify direction-dependent Zeeman coupling.
