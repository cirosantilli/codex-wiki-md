<h1 id="coates-wiles-homomorphism">Coates–Wiles homomorphism</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman power series for a CM formal group](coleman-power-series-for-a-cm-formal-group.md)

The $k$th logarithmic derivative in the additive formal logarithm coordinate defines the Coates–Wiles homomorphism. Products of local units become sums under this map. For [elliptic units](elliptic-unit.md), differentiating the underlying complex elliptic function expresses the result in terms of critical [Hecke L-function](hecke-l-function.md) values divided by a [CM period](cm-period.md).
