<h1 id="closed-graph-theorem-for-frechet-spaces">Closed graph theorem for Fréchet spaces</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Closed graph theorem](closed-graph-theorem.md)

A [linear map](linear-map.md) $T:E\to F$ between [Fréchet spaces](frechet-space.md) is continuous if its [graph of a linear operator](graph-of-a-linear-operator.md) is closed in $E\times F$. The closed graph is itself a [Fréchet space](frechet-space.md). Its first-coordinate projection is a continuous linear bijection onto $E$, so the [open mapping theorem for Fréchet spaces](open-mapping-theorem-for-frechet-spaces.md) makes the inverse continuous. Compose that inverse with the second-coordinate projection to obtain $T$. Completeness of both original spaces is part of the theorem.
