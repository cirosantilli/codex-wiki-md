# Naive polynomial height

↑ **Parent:** [Projective height](projective-height.md)

The naive height of a polynomial is the largest absolute value of its coefficients. For a primitive polynomial in $\mathbb Z[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$, this equals the [projective height](projective-height.md) of its coefficient vector.
