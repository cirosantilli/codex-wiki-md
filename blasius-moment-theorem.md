# Blasius moment theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Blasius force theorem](blasius-force-theorem.md)

For steady [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) and [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) of constant [mass density](density.md), with no [body force](body-force.md), this formula gives the [hydrodynamic torque](hydrodynamic-torque.md) about the origin per unit span on an impermeable body. Orient its boundary counterclockwise, and let positive [torque](torque.md) be counterclockwise. The proof uses $M=\operatorname{Re}\oint_Cpz\,d\bar z$, the [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) $p=P_*-\rho|w'|^2/2$, and the tangency identity $|w'|^2d\bar z=(w')^2dz$. The real part of the constant-pressure term vanishes because $\operatorname{Re}(z\,d\bar z)=d|z|^2/2$. The full complex integral of $z\,d\bar z$ need not vanish.
