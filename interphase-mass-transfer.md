# Interphase mass transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Multiphase flow](multiphase-flow.md)

The source $\Gamma_i$ is the mass gained by phase $i$ per mixture volume and time, through processes such as [evaporation](evaporation.md) or condensation. Internal exchange conserves total mass, so its phase sources sum to zero. In an immiscible material-interface model without phase change, every $\Gamma_i$ is zero. The interface may still move: absence of transfer means that mass does not cross it relative to its own motion.
