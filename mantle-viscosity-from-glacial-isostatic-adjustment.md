# Mantle viscosity from glacial isostatic adjustment

↑ **Parent:** [Glacial isostatic adjustment](glacial-isostatic-adjustment.md)

Infer mantle relaxation from dated relative sea levels and shorelines, geodetic [displacements](displacement.md), [gravity](gravity.md) changes and a reconstructed ice/water load history. The inferred [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) trades off with ice history, lithospheric thickness, radial structure and transient [rheology](rheology-split.md), so independent constraints and uncertainty propagation are essential.
