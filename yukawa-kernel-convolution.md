# Yukawa kernel convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Helmholtz equation](modified-helmholtz-equation.md)

For $m,\beta>0$, set $K_m(r)=e^{-mr}/r$. Distributionally, $(\Delta-m^2)K_m=-4\pi\delta$, so the [modified Helmholtz equation](modified-helmholtz-equation.md) with unit delta source has [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) $-K_m/(4\pi)$. The difference $[K_\beta-K_m]/(\beta^2-m^2)$ solves the equation with source $K_\beta$: the two delta terms cancel. It has no added point source at zero and decays at infinity. The [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) representation proves the displayed convolution with its positive sign.

At coincident rates, the continuous limit is $e^{-mr}/(2m)$; at $r=0$, it is $1/(m+\beta)$. The latter also follows by directly integrating $e^{-(m+\beta)r'}dr'$ after the spherical factors cancel. These limits prevent the formula's apparent singular denominators from being mistaken for physical divergences.
