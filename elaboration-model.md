# Elaboration model

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical association](statistical-association.md)

The [elaboration model](elaboration-model.md) compares a pooled [statistical association](statistical-association.md) with the associations conditional on a third variable. **Replication** describes a similar pattern within the strata. **Specification** describes a relationship whose size or direction varies between strata. Attenuation can suggest **explanation** by an antecedent [confounder](confounder.md), or **interpretation** through an intervening [causal mediator](causal-mediator.md). These causal readings require justified temporal and causal assumptions; conditional tables alone do not identify the third variable's role.[Chapter 15, printed p. 448, Table 15-5: relationship patterns and antecedent/intervening test variables](the-practice-of-social-research-13th-edition-by-earl-babbie.md)

Assess the patterns through conditional [probabilities](probability.md) or comparable [effect sizes](effect-size.md). Different [p-values](p-value.md) also reflect different sample sizes and [standard errors](standard-error.md), so [significant and nonsignificant results need not differ significantly](significant-and-nonsignificant-results-need-not-differ-significantly.md).
