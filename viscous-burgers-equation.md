# Viscous Burgers equation

↑ **Parent:** [Burgers' equation](burgers-equation.md)

The viscous Burgers equation adds diffusion with viscosity $\nu>0$ to nonlinear transport. Smooth initial data remain smooth, and the diffusion regularizes the shocks of the [Inviscid Burgers equation](inviscid-burgers-equation.md).
