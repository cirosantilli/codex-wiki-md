# Whitney product formula for Euler classes

↑ **Parent:** [Euler class of a vector bundle](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md)

For oriented real vector bundles $E$ and $F$, the ordered direct-sum orientation satisfies

$$
e(E\oplus F)=e(E)\smile e(F).
$$

The formula follows because the [Thom class](thom-class.md) of the direct sum is the fiberwise exterior product of the two Thom classes.
