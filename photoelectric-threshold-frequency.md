# Photoelectric threshold frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein photoelectric equation](einstein-photoelectric-equation.md)

The [photoelectric threshold frequency](photoelectric-threshold-frequency.md) is the lower [frequency](frequency.md) boundary allowed by the ideal single-[photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) balance. With [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda=c/\nu$ and [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) $c$, its corresponding longest [wavelength](wavelength.md) is $\lambda_0=hc/\Phi$. The chosen $2.30\,\mathrm{eV}$ [work function](work-function.md) gives $\nu_0\simeq5.561375\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $\lambda_0\simeq539.062\,\mathrm{nm}$. These are model parameters, not a measured threshold for a named metal. Detectable [photocurrent](photocurrent.md) need not begin abruptly at the ideal boundary: collection, finite spectral width, thermal broadening and surface inhomogeneity matter.
