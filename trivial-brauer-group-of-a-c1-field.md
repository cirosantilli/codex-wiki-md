# Trivial Brauer group of a C1 field

↑ **Parent:** [C1 field](c1-field.md)

A [central division algebra](central-division-algebra.md) of degree $d>1$ has an anisotropic [reduced norm](reduced-norm.md) of degree $d$ in $d^2>d$ variables. That contradicts the [C1 field](c1-field.md) property. Thus only degree one is possible, and every [central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md) is split.
