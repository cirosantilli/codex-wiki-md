# Anticipated discrimination

↑ **Parent:** [Mental illness stigma](mental-illness-stigma.md)

An expectation of unfair treatment linked to an attributed characteristic. It can influence participation or concealment before a particular rejection occurs; absence of reported prior [discrimination](discrimination.md) does not prove that the expectation is unfounded.
