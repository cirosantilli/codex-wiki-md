# Formal kernel of a minimal Weierstrass equation

↑ **Parent:** [Formal group of an elliptic curve](formal-group-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) over a [local field](local-field.md) with integral [Minimal Weierstrass equation](minimal-weierstrass-equation.md), let $E_0(K)$ consist of points with nonsingular reduction and let $E_1(K)$ be the kernel of $E_0(K)\to\widetilde E_{\mathrm{ns}}(k)$. This definition also applies at a prime of [bad reduction of an elliptic curve](bad-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). The point at infinity is always smooth. With $t=-x/y$ and $w=-1/y$, the [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) becomes

$$
w=t^3+a_1tw+a_2t^2w+a_3w^2+a_4tw^2+a_6w^3.
$$

The unit linear coefficient in $w$ gives a unique integral [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) $w(t)=t^3+O(t^4)$. Then $x=t/w$ and $y=-1/w$, so the nonidentity points of $E_1(K)$ have $v(x)=-2r$ and $v(y)=-3r$ for $r=v(t)>0$. The [local parameter](local-parameter-on-a-smooth-algebraic-curve.md) $t$ bijects $E_1(K)$ with $\mathfrak m_K$ and turns its addition into the [formal group of an elliptic curve](formal-group-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). At [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) this agrees with the [kernel of reduction of an elliptic curve](kernel-of-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md).
