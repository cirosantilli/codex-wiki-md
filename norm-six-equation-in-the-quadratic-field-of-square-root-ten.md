# Norm-six equation in the quadratic field of square root ten

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic field](quadratic-field.md)

The ring of integers is $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{10}]$, its class number is two, and its units are $\pm(3+\sqrt{10})^n$ for $n\in\mathbb Z$. Factoring a norm-six principal ideal above 2 and 3 gives all solutions

$$
x+\sqrt{10}y=\pm\epsilon^n(\epsilon+(-1)^n),\qquad \epsilon=3+\sqrt{10},\quad n\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Restriction to nonnegative $n$ gives only the positive-coordinate branch up to simultaneous sign. For example, $4-\sqrt{10}$ is a norm-six element requiring $n=-1$; adding conjugates repairs a nonnegative-index presentation.
