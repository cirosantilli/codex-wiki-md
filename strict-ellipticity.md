# Strict ellipticity

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic differential operator](elliptic-differential-operator.md)

For a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) field $A$, strict ellipticity means that $A(x)$ is a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) at every point. Continuity makes its smallest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) positive uniformly on each [compact subset](compact-space.md) of the domain. A uniform positive lower bound on the entire domain is the stronger [uniform ellipticity](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) condition. Some conventions include a finite uniform upper bound in the latter term, while others state coefficient boundedness separately.
