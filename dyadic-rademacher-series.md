# Dyadic Rademacher series

↑ **Parent:** [Rademacher sum](rademacher-sum.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [Rademacher random variables](rademacher-distribution.md), this series converges absolutely on every sign sequence and has the [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $[-1,1]$. Its first $n$ terms are uniform on the $2^n$ midpoints of equal subintervals of $[-1,1]$, giving [weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) by [Riemann sums](riemann-sum.md). The remaining tail has absolute value at most $2^{-n}$, and its mean square is $4^{-n}/3$ by [independence](independent-random-variables.md). Thus convergence is also [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) and in $L^2$.
