# Round-robin arbitration

↑ **Parent:** [Digital electronics](digital-electronics.md)

An arbiter selects at most one asserted request, searching cyclically after the most recent successful recipient. After a grant it advances that remembered position. This implements circular priority among [bits](bit.md) representing competing requests; it is distinct from assigning a fixed priority forever.
