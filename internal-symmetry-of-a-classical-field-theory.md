# Internal symmetry of a classical field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory-split.md)

An internal symmetry changes field values while leaving spacetime coordinates fixed, preserving the [action](action.md). A constant phase rotation of a [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) is an example. This distinguishes internal charges from the spacetime translation charges in the [canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md). Localizing the parameter generally requires gauge fields; a global internal symmetry is not automatically a gauge symmetry.
