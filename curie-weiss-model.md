<h1 id="curie-weiss-model">Curie–Weiss model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)

The zero-field ferromagnetic Curie–Weiss model is an [Ising model](ising-model.md) on $n$ spins with an equal interaction between every pair. For $s_i\in\{-1,1\}$ and $\beta>0$, its normalized [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) is $Z_{n,\beta}^{-1}\exp[\beta(\sum_is_i)^2/(2n)]$. The [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) $Z_{n,\beta}$ sums these weights over all configurations. The square includes constant diagonal terms; these affect only normalization. [Spin inversion symmetry](spin-inversion-symmetry.md) makes every spin's [expected value](expected-value.md) zero, while grouping configurations by their [spin magnetization](spin-magnetization.md) gives

$$
\mathbb P(M=m)=Z_{n,\beta}^{-1}\binom{n}{n(1+m)/2}e^{\beta nm^2/2}.
$$

Here $m=-1+2k/n$, $0\leq k\leq n$.
