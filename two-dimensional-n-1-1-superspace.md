<h1 id="two-dimensional-n-1-1-superspace">Two-dimensional N=(1,1) superspace</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Superspace](superspace.md)

In [light-cone coordinates](light-cone-coordinates.md) $x^\pm$ on two-dimensional [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), add two real odd [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md) $\theta^\pm$. With [left Grassmann derivatives](left-grassmann-derivative.md), the [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) and [supersymmetric covariant derivatives](supersymmetric-covariant-derivative.md) can be represented as

$$
Q_\pm=\partial_{\theta^\pm}+i\theta^\pm\partial_\pm,\qquad D_\pm=\partial_{\theta^\pm}-i\theta^\pm\partial_\pm.
$$

The identity $\{\partial_\theta,\theta\}=1$ gives $Q_\pm^2=i\partial_\pm$, $D_\pm^2=-i\partial_\pm$, $\{Q_+,Q_-\}=\{D_+,D_-\}=0$, and $\{Q_s,D_t\}=0$ for every $s,t$. A [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) acts as $x^\pm\mapsto e^{\pm\omega}x^\pm$, $\theta^\pm\mapsto e^{\pm\omega/2}\theta^\pm$. Thus $D_\pm$ have boost weights $\mp1/2$, and $D_-D_+$ is a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md). Since it commutes with both [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md), an equation $iD_-D_+\Phi=W(\Phi)$ for an even scalar [superfield](superfield.md) is [Lorentz invariant](lorentz-invariance.md) and [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) covariant.
