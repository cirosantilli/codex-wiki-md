# Free surface

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_surface)

A free surface is a fluid boundary whose position is part of the solution and whose traction is prescribed by the adjoining medium. Its motion obeys a [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md), while its pressure or stress supplies a dynamic boundary condition.
