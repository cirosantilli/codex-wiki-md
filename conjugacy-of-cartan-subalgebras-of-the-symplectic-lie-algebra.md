# Conjugacy of Cartan subalgebras of the symplectic Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md)

Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) of [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md), any two [Cartan subalgebras](cartan-subalgebra.md) of the [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md) are conjugate by the [symplectic group](symplectic-group.md). A [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak h$ is an [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md) and equals its [centralizer of a Lie subalgebra](centralizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md). The [symplectic centralizer decomposition](symplectic-centralizer-decomposition.md) therefore forces all its [weight spaces](weight-space.md) in $V$ to be one-dimensional, nonzero, and paired by opposite [weights of a representation](weight-of-a-representation.md). Choose a [basis vector](basis-vector.md) in each space, normalizing the [perfect pairing](perfect-pairing.md) with its opposite to one. These vectors form a [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md) $\mathcal B$. In this [basis](basis.md), $\mathfrak h$ is contained in the [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) of [diagonal matrices](diagonal-matrix.md) with entries $(t_1,\ldots,t_n,-t_1,\ldots,-t_n)$. All those [diagonal matrices](diagonal-matrix.md) commute with $\mathfrak h$, so equality with its [centralizer of a Lie subalgebra](centralizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md) forces equality with the entire diagonal [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md). Repeating for $\mathfrak h'$ gives a second [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md). The [linear map](linear-map.md) sending one [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md) to the other belongs to the [symplectic group](symplectic-group.md) and conjugates the two diagonal [Lie subalgebras](lie-subalgebra.md), proving the claim.
