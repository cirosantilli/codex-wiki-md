# Parker dynamo wave

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field dynamo](mean-field-dynamo.md)

A Parker dynamo wave is a traveling or oscillatory mode of a mean-field dynamo coupling poloidal and toroidal magnetic-field components through an [alpha effect](alpha-effect.md) and shear. A reduced complex-amplitude model can represent fluctuating alpha by a time-dependent coupling. Its periodically switched growth must be calculated from the [monodromy matrix of a periodic linear system](monodromy-matrix-of-a-periodic-linear-system.md), rather than by averaging noncommuting generators.
