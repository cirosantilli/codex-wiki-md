# Classification of compact surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Topological surface](topological-surface.md)

A [connected](connected-space.md) [compact](compact-space.md) [surface](topological-surface.md) is determined by its [orientability](orientability.md), number $b$ of [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) circles and [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md). An [orientable](orientable-surface.md) [surface](topological-surface.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$ has [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) $2-2g-b$; a [surface](topological-surface.md) which is not [orientable](orientable-surface.md) with $r$ crosscaps has [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) $2-r-b$. In particular, a closed [connected](connected-space.md) [surface](topological-surface.md) with vanishing first mod-two [homology](homology-split.md) is a [sphere](sphere.md), and a [connected](connected-space.md) closed [orientable](orientable-surface.md) [surface](topological-surface.md) of [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) zero is a [torus](torus.md).
