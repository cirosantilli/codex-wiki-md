# Subgroup generator bound for finitely generated Abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian group](abelian-group.md)

Every [subgroup](subgroup.md) of an [Abelian group](abelian-group.md) generated by $n$ elements has at most $n$ generators. Lift it under a surjection $\mathbb Z^n\to A$. A [subgroup](subgroup.md) $L\leq\mathbb Z^n$ has at most $n$ generators by induction: its first-coordinate [image](image-of-a-function.md) is either zero or $d\mathbb Z$. In the latter case choose a lift of $d$, and subtract its multiples to reduce to the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) in $\mathbb Z^{n-1}$. The images of these generators generate the original [subgroup](subgroup.md). The Abelian hypothesis matters: $S_{2n+2}$ is generated by a transposition and a full cycle but contains the [elementary abelian group](elementary-abelian-group.md) generated by $n+1$ disjoint transpositions, requiring $n+1$ generators by its vector-space [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) over $\mathbb F_2$.
