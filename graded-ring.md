# Graded ring

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graded_ring)

A graded ring is a direct sum $R=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}R_n$ with $R_mR_n\subseteq R_{m+n}$.
