# Decimal expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Positional notation](positional-notation.md)

A nonnegative integer's [decimal expansion](decimal-expansion.md) uses digits $0\leq a_i\leq9$. Testing divisibility reduces this polynomial in ten modulo the divisor: for example $10\equiv-1\pmod{11}$ makes divisibility by eleven equivalent to vanishing of the alternating digit sum.
