# Cylinder-function density in Cantor space

↑ **Parent:** [Cantor space](cantor-space.md)

A [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on [Cantor space](cantor-space.md) is uniformly continuous. Replacing it by one constant on each length-$n$ prefix [cylinder set](cylinder-set.md) changes it by at most its oscillation on sets of diameter $2^{-n}$. This tends uniformly to zero. Each approximant is a finite linear combination of continuous [indicator functions](indicator-function.md), proving density in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md).
