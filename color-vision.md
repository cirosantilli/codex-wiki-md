# Color vision

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_vision)

[Color vision](color-vision.md) distinguishes distributions of visible [light](light.md) across [wavelengths](wavelength.md) through the different spectral sensitivities of visual receptors. A perceived colour need not correspond to one [wavelength](wavelength.md): different [optical spectra](optical-spectrum.md) can produce the same receptor responses. Consequently the broad spectral filtering of [thin-film interference](thin-film-interference.md) need not produce pure monochromatic colours.
