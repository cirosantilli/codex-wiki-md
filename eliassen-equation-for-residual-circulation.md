# Eliassen equation for residual circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Residual mean circulation](residual-mean-circulation.md)

For constant Coriolis parameter $f_0$ and [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) $N$, thermal-wind balance and transformed mean-flow equations give

$$
f_0^2\chi_{zz}^*+N^2\chi_{yy}^*
=-f_0(\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf F)_z,
$$

where $\chi^*$ is the residual-circulation streamfunction and $\mathbf F$ is the [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md).
