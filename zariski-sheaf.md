# Zariski sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf on a site](sheaf-on-a-site.md)

A contravariant set-valued [functor](functor-split.md) on [schemes](scheme.md) is a Zariski sheaf if compatible local elements on any [Zariski cover](zariski-cover.md) glue uniquely. Explicitly, $F(T)$ is the [equalizer](equaliser.md) of $\prod_iF(U_i)\rightrightarrows\prod_{i,j}F(U_i\times_TU_j)$. The [functor of points](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) of every [scheme](scheme.md) is a Zariski sheaf because [morphisms of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) glue over [open covers](open-cover.md).
