# Recession cone

↑ **Parent:** [Convex set](convex-set.md)

The recession cone of a nonempty [convex set](convex-set.md) $C$ consists of directions $d$ for which $x+td\in C$ for every $x\in C$ and $t\ge0$. For a [linear polyhedron](linear-polyhedron.md) $P=\{x:Ax\le b\}$ it is exactly $\{d:Ad\le0\}$. A nonzero recession direction gives an unbounded ray, so a bounded nonempty polyhedron has recession cone $\{0\}$. This excludes spurious zero-scale feasible points in the [Charnes-Cooper transformation](charnes-cooper-transformation.md).
