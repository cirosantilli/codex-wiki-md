# Perfectly conducting thermal boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md)

In a fixed-temperature [convection](convection.md) problem, the boundary [temperature](temperature.md) is prescribed, and its perturbation satisfies a thermal [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). A homogeneous condition means the boundary follows the prescribed conductive background [temperature](temperature.md). This does not necessarily mean a vertical wall has a spatially constant [temperature](temperature.md); that additional assumption can conflict with a background vertical [gradient](gradient.md).
