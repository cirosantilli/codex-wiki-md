# Freiling axiom of symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum hypothesis](continuum-hypothesis.md)

Every function $f:\mathbb R\to[\mathbb R]^{<\omega_1}$ has reals $x,y$ with $x\notin f(y)$ and $y\notin f(x)$. The values are countable [subsets](subset.md). Requiring distinct witnesses is equivalent, by first adjoining $x$ to $f(x)$. The [Freiling theorem](freiling-theorem.md) identifies this axiom with the negation of the [Continuum hypothesis](continuum-hypothesis.md) in [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md).
