# Conformal rescaling of a parallel spinor to a twistor spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor spinor](twistor-spinor.md)

In the conformal frame for $g=\Omega^2\eta$, identify the spinor fibres using the chosen [vierbein](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md). If $\epsilon_0$ is a flat constant [spinor field](spinor-field.md), the [spin connection of a conformally flat metric](spin-connection-of-a-conformally-flat-metric.md) gives $\nabla_a\epsilon=\widehat\gamma_a\widehat\gamma^c(\partial_c\log\Omega)\epsilon/2$. Contracting with curved $\gamma^a=\Omega^{-1}\widehat\gamma^a$ yields $\gamma^a\nabla_a\epsilon=n\Omega^{-1}\widehat\gamma^c(\partial_c\log\Omega)\epsilon/2$. Thus $\nabla_a\epsilon=\gamma_a\gamma^b\nabla_b\epsilon/n$: the rescaled parallel spinor is a [twistor spinor](twistor-spinor.md).
