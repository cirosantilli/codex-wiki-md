# Natural rubber

↑ **Parent:** [Elastomer](elastomer.md)

A rubbery material based mainly on cis-rich [polyisoprene](polyisoprene.md). A lightly crosslinked network provides recoverable deformation through [rubber elasticity](rubber-elasticity.md).
