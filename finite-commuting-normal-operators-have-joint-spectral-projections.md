# Finite commuting normal operators have joint spectral projections

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral theorem for normal operators](spectral-theorem-for-normal-operators.md)

A finite family of pairwise commuting [normal operators](normal-operator.md) on a finite-dimensional complex [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), commuting also with each other's adjoints, generates a commutative unital [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md). Its finite [maximal ideal space](maximal-ideal-space-of-a-commutative-banach-algebra.md) identifies the algebra with functions on finitely many points. The characteristic functions of those points pull back under the [Gelfand transform](gelfand-representation.md) to mutually orthogonal self-adjoint [linear projections](projection-linear-algebra.md) summing to the identity. Their ranges are the simultaneous eigenspaces, giving simultaneous diagonalization without choosing separate incompatible eigenbases.
