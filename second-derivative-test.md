# Second-derivative test

↑ **Parent:** [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second-derivative_test)

At an interior local maximum of a twice differentiable function, its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is negative semidefinite; at a local minimum it is positive semidefinite. For a scalar function on Euclidean space, the Laplacian is therefore nonpositive at a local maximum and nonnegative at a local minimum.
