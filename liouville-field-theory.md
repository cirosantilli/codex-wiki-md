# Liouville field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional conformal field theory](two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liouville_field_theory)

[Liouville field theory](liouville-field-theory.md) is a two-dimensional scalar theory with a curvature background charge and an exponential interaction. In the usual spacelike normalization, quantum conformal invariance gives $Q=b+b^{-1}$ and [central charge](central-charge.md) $c_L=1+6Q^2$. When a noncritical [string worldsheet](worldsheet.md) conformal factor is retained as a dynamical field, its effective action has this form and its [central charge](central-charge.md) combines with the matter and [worldsheet ghost field](worldsheet-ghost-field.md) charges. It is closely related to a [background-charge scalar field](background-charge-scalar-field.md), but its exponential potential supplies an additional interaction.
