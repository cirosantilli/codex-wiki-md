# Symmetry-axis equation of a heavy symmetric top

↑ **Parent:** [Heavy symmetric top](heavy-symmetric-top.md)

Writing the unit symmetry axis as $n$ and axial spin momentum as $s=I_3\omega_3$, [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is $L=I_1n\times\dot n+sn$. Taking its derivative and applying gravitational [torque](torque.md) gives $I_1n\times\ddot n+s\dot n=-mg\ell n\times\widehat z$.
