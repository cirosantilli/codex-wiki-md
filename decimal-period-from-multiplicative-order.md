# Decimal period from multiplicative order

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicative order](multiplicative-order.md)

For $q>1$ coprime to $10$, long division of $1/q$ has remainders $r_n\equiv10^n\pmod q$ in $1,\ldots,q-1$. Its digits satisfy $10r_{n-1}=qa_n+r_n$. A congruence $10^m\equiv1\pmod q$ therefore makes the remainders and digits periodic with period dividing $m$. The least period is the [multiplicative order](multiplicative-order.md) of $10$ modulo $q$.
