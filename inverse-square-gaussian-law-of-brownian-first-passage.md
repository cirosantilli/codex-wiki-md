# Inverse-square Gaussian law of Brownian first passage

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian first-passage time](brownian-first-passage-time.md)

The [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) gives $\mathbb P(\tau_x\leq t)=\mathbb P(|N|\geq x/\sqrt t)$ for a standard normal random variable $N$. This is the distribution function of $x^2/N^2$. An independent [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) observed at this hitting time has the [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) with scale $x$.
