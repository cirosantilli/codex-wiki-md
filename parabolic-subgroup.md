# Parabolic subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parabolic_subgroup)

A parabolic subgroup of a connected reductive group is a closed subgroup containing a [Borel subgroup](borel-subgroup.md). Its quotient in the group is projective.
