# Fastest discrete mode of a stratified magnetorotational instability

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded vertical modes of a sech-squared magnetized disk](bounded-vertical-modes-of-a-sech-squared-magnetized-disk.md)

The growing root of the [ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation](ideal-magnetorotational-dispersion-relation.md) is maximized continuously at $a=v_A^2k^2/\Omega^2=15/16$, with growth $3\Omega/4$. Discrete vertical modes instead have $a_n=2n(n+1)/\beta$. The fastest one is found by comparing admissible integers on either side of the continuous maximum, rather than rounding a vertical wavenumber blindly. At $\beta=24$, $n=3$ gives $s=\Omega\sqrt{(\sqrt{17}-3)/2}$.
