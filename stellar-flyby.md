# Stellar flyby

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary system dynamics](planetary-system-dynamics.md)

A passing [star](star.md) perturbs a planetary or cometary system through its differential [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md). A distant encounter is predominantly tidal; a sufficiently close one can substantially change [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), eject a [comet](comet.md), or shift its [periapsis](periapsis.md) into or out of the planetary region.
