# Age comparison for an initial vacuum-dominated phase

↑ **Parent:** [Age of the universe](age-of-the-universe.md)

Match flat [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md) histories at [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) $R_1$ and [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) $H_1$, and give them identical subsequent evolution. Below $R_1$, constant [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md) gives $H=H_1$, while a [radiation-dominated universe](radiation-dominated-universe.md) gives $H=H_1(R_1/R)^2$. From $\epsilon R_1$ to $R_1$ the former takes longer by $H_1^{-1}\int_\epsilon^1(1/x-x)\,dx>0$. Its duration diverges as $\epsilon\to0$; the radiation duration tends to $1/(2H_1)$. A finite period of [cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md) requires a finite initial state and a specified matching before such age comparisons are meaningful.
