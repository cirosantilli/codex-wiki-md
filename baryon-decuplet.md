# Baryon decuplet

↑ **Parent:** [Eightfold way](eightfold-way.md)

The completely symmetric three-quark representation of [flavour symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) has dimension ten. Its [weight diagram](weight-diagram.md) has rows $(Y,I)=(1,3/2),(0,1),(-1,1/2),(-2,0)$. It includes $uuu$ at the $\Delta^{++}$ weight and $sss$ at the $\Omega^-$ weight. A spatially symmetric three-quark colour singlet realizes this multiplet with spin $3/2$.
