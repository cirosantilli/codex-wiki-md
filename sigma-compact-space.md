# Sigma-compact space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

A space is sigma-compact if it is a countable union of compact subsets. A locally compact, sigma-compact [metrizable space](metrizable-space.md) is [second countable](second-countable-space.md): each compact metrizable subset has a countable dense set, their union is countable and dense, and a separable metric space has a countable basis.
