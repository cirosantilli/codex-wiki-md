# Quantum reflection amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum reflection probability](quantum-reflection-probability.md)

A [quantum reflection amplitude](quantum-reflection-amplitude.md) is the complex ratio of the outgoing reflected wave to the incident wave in a chosen scattering convention. In one reflected channel with the same exterior speed, the reflection [probability](probability.md) is $|r|^2$. Its phase affects [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) even when its modulus is one; shifting the reference plane changes that phase.
