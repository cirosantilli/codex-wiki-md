# Bullet Cluster dark-matter separation

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing-split.md)

The merger separates X-ray-emitting collisional gas from galaxies and lensing [mass](mass.md) concentrations. Comparing those observables provides evidence for a dominant less-collisional unseen gravitating component, without identifying its particle species.
