# Spin rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

A [spin rotation](spin-rotation.md) acts on a [quantum state](quantum-state.md) through the [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md) generated by its [angular momentum operators](angular-momentum-operator-split.md). With $\hbar=1$, [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) by angle $\theta$ about axis $\hat{\mathbf n}$ is $e^{-i\theta\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\mathbf S}$. Simultaneously rotating every site is a global [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) of an isotropic [Heisenberg model](heisenberg-model.md).
