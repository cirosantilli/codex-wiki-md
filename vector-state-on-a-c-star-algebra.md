# Vector state on a C-star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [State on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md)

For a unital represented [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) $A\subseteq\mathcal B(H)$, the displayed functional is a state. It is linear under the first-argument-linear inner-product convention, has $x_\eta(1)=1$, and is positive because $x_\eta(a^*a)=\|a\eta\|^2\geq0$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $|x_\eta(a)|\leq\|a\|$, while evaluation at the unit gives equality of its [norm](norm.md) with one. Its GNS null [left ideal](left-ideal.md) consists exactly of the operators annihilating $\eta$.
