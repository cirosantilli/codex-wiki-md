# Coupled onset parameter for origin percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone coupling of Bernoulli percolation](monotone-coupling-of-bernoulli-percolation.md)

In the uniform-label [monotone coupling of Bernoulli percolation](monotone-coupling-of-bernoulli-percolation.md), let $I_p$ mean that the origin belongs to an [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md), and let $M=\inf\{p:I_p\}$. The occurrence parameters form an upper interval. Therefore $\{M<p\}=\bigcup_{r<p,\ r\in\mathbb Q}I_r$ and $\theta(p-)=\mathbb P(M<p)$. Since $\{M<p\}\subseteq I_p\subseteq\{M\leq p\}$, the jump is $\theta(p)-\theta(p-)=\mathbb P(I_p\cap\{M=p\})$. Occurrence at the infimum is not presumed.
