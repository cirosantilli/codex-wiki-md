# Scalar Feynman propagator pole prescription

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman propagator](feynman-propagator.md)

With $\Delta_F=\langle0|T\{\phi(x)\phi(y)\}|0\rangle$, for a mode of positive [energy](energy.md) $E>0$ the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) puts poles $+E-i0$ and $-E+i0$ in opposite half-planes. The [energy](energy.md) [contour integral](contour-integral.md) closes clockwise below for positive time separation and counterclockwise above for negative separation. The [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md) gives $e^{-iE|t|}/(2E)$ in either case. Thus the four-dimensional Fourier integral has numerator $i$ and denominator $p^2-m^2+i0$. The integral is understood as a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) limit, not an ordinary absolutely convergent integral; changing the definition to $i\Delta_F$ would change the displayed normalization.
