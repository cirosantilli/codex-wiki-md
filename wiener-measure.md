# Wiener measure

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiener_measure)

Wiener measure is the [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) on a continuous-path space, usually $C_0([0,T])$ or $C_0([0,\infty))$.
