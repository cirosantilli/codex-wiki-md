# LU decomposition of an upper Hessenberg matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Hessenberg matrix](hessenberg-matrix.md)

Without [row interchanges](row-interchange.md), an [upper Hessenberg matrix](upper-hessenberg-matrix.md) needs only one elimination per [pivot column](pivot-column.md). Its lower factor is unit lower bidiagonal, while its upper factor is [upper triangular](upper-triangular-matrix.md). If the original [matrix](matrix.md) is symmetric, it is tridiagonal and the upper factor is also bidiagonal.
