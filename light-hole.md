# Light hole

↑ **Parent:** [Electron hole](electron-hole.md)

A hole in the $j=3/2$, $m_j=\pm1/2$ valence branch near a specified axis. Its curvature and [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) differ from those of the heavy-hole branch; confinement and strain can split branches that are degenerate at the bulk zone centre.
