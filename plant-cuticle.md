# Plant cuticle

↑ **Parent:** [Plant tissue](plant-tissue.md)

A surface barrier that limits water exchange with the environment. Its development helps regulate drying, but varies between [gametophytes](gametophyte.md), [sporophytes](sporophyte.md) and plant organs.
