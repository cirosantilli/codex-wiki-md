# Transverse equal-time commutator

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical transverse photon field](canonical-transverse-photon-field.md)

The reduced [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) for a transverse field uses a transverse delta function instead of an unconstrained componentwise delta. Its Fourier transform is the spatial transverse projector. It can be derived from the two-polarization oscillator expansion or from the [Dirac bracket](dirac-bracket.md) after [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md).
