# Quadratic-Hill mutual repression switch

↑ **Parent:** [Mutual repression](mutual-repression.md)

For $\dot Y=-Y+\gamma/(1+Z^2)$ and $\dot Z=-Z+\gamma/(1+Y^2)$, subtraction of the [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) conditions gives $(Y-Z)(1-YZ)=0$. The symmetric state solves $x^3+x=\gamma$. Two asymmetric positive states exist precisely for $\gamma>2$: $Y_\pm=(\gamma\pm\sqrt{\gamma^2-4})/2$, $Z_\pm=Y_\mp$. Their [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $-1\pm2/\gamma$, both negative; the symmetric state is then a [saddle point](saddle-point.md). For $0<\gamma<2$ only the attracting symmetric state remains.
