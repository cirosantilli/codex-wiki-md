# Symbol grounding

↑ **Parent:** [Artificial intelligence](artificial-intelligence-split.md)

The problem of connecting internal symbols to what they designate in perception, action or other meaningful relations. Supplying an [intelligent agent](intelligent-agent.md) with perfectly identified objects and relations can conceal this problem rather than solve it.
