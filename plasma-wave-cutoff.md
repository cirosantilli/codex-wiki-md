# Plasma wave cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Cold magnetized plasma](cold-magnetized-plasma.md)

A point on a [plasma](plasma-physics.md) [wave](wave-split.md) branch where the local [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) tends to zero at finite [frequency](frequency.md). The mode may change between propagation and evanescence. For a finite [magnetized cold-plasma dielectric tensor](magnetized-cold-plasma-dielectric-tensor.md), candidate cutoffs satisfy $PRL=0$; special propagation angles require checking which factor belongs to the chosen branch.
