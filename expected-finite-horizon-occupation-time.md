# Expected finite-horizon occupation time

↑ **Parent:** [Occupation time of a continuous-time Markov chain](occupation-time-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

For a finite-state homogeneous [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) with [generator matrix](generator-matrix.md) $Q$, integrate the indicator of occupying state $j$ over $[0,T]$. Interchanging the bounded time integral and [expected value](expected-value.md) gives the displayed integral of [transition probabilities](transition-probability.md). It counts all visits, not only the first [holding time](holding-time.md). Summing over states gives $T$. A zero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of $Q$ does not cause a problem for this integral, although it prevents casually writing it with an [inverse matrix](matrix-inverse.md) $Q^{-1}$.
