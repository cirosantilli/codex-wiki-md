# Time-dependent lapse can disguise flat spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md)

For positive lapse $N$, this time reparametrization gives a local [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md). If the new time coordinate ranges over the entire real line, it is a global isometry to Minkowski space. In particular $N=\cosh t$ gives $T=\sinh t$ and complete nonperiodic timelike geodesics. A lapse depending on the spatial coordinate instead can describe a different curved geometry; exchanging the arguments is not a harmless relabeling of the same metric.
