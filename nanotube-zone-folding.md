# Nanotube zone folding

↑ **Parent:** [Single-walled carbon nanotube](single-walled-carbon-nanotube.md)

An approximation that samples the [graphene](graphene.md) bands on wave vectors satisfying $\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf C_h=2\pi\ell$, where $\mathbf C_h$ is the [carbon nanotube chiral vector](carbon-nanotube-chiral-vector.md). It produces one-dimensional subbands and the ideal metallicity criterion $n-m\equiv0\pmod3$; curvature and interactions can modify its predictions.
