# Omega baryon

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omega_baryon)

The light-quark $sss$ member of the spin-three-halves [baryon decuplet](baryon-decuplet.md) has [strangeness](strangeness.md) $S=-3$, [baryon number](baryon-number.md) $B=1$, [flavor hypercharge](flavor-hypercharge.md) $Y=-2$, and [isospin](isospin.md) zero. The [Gell-Mann--Nishijima formula](gell-mann-nishijima-formula.md) gives electric charge minus one. Its position at the bottom of the decuplet's triangular [weight diagram](weight-diagram.md) follows by replacing all three up or down quarks with strange quarks.
