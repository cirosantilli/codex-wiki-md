# Fourier amplification symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier stability analysis](fourier-stability-analysis.md)

A constant-coefficient translation-invariant grid update is diagonalized spatially by [discrete Fourier modes](discrete-fourier-mode.md). Its scalar or matrix multiplier is the displayed symbol. In a semidiscrete evolution, the analogous symbol is a continuous-time generator and its exponential propagates each mode. [Discrete Parseval identity](discrete-parseval-identity.md) converts uniform modal bounds to discrete [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) bounds.
