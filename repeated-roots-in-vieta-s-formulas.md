<h1 id="repeated-roots-in-vieta-s-formulas">Repeated roots in Vieta's formulas</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vieta's formulas](vieta-s-formulas.md)

For the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $(x-2)^2(x+1)=x^3-3x^2+4$, the root list is $2,2,-1$, not $2,-1$. Its [elementary symmetric polynomials](elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md) are

$$
e_1=2+2-1=3,\qquad e_2=(2)(2)+(2)(-1)+(2)(-1)=0,\qquad e_3=(2)(2)(-1)=-4.
$$

The zero [coefficient](coefficient.md) of $x$ is accounted for by the cancellation in $e_2$. Omitting one occurrence of the double [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) loses both the [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) and the correct [coefficients](coefficient.md). [Multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md) is part of the root data.
