# Atmospheric supersaturation

↑ **Parent:** [Supersaturated vapor](supersaturated-vapor.md)

The fractional excess of water-vapor [pressure](pressure-split.md) above equilibrium saturation over a flat pure-water surface at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md): $s=p_v/p_{\rm sat}(T)-1$. Positive $s$ can drive droplet growth, but activation of a particular [aerosol](aerosol.md) particle requires exceeding its [critical supersaturation](critical-supersaturation.md). Cooling, condensation and mixing jointly determine parcel supersaturation.
