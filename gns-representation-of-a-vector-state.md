# GNS representation of a vector state

↑ **Parent:** [Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction](gelfand-naimark-segal-construction.md)

For a [vector state on a C-star algebra](vector-state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md), $N=\{a:a\eta=0\}$ and $\langle[a],[b]\rangle=\langle a\eta,b\eta\rangle$. Thus $U_0[a]=a\eta$ is a well-defined [linear isometry](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md) from the quotient pre-Hilbert space onto $A\eta$. It extends to an [isometry](isometry.md) from the completed GNS space onto $K=\overline{A\eta}$: its range is complete and therefore closed, and contains the dense subspace $A\eta$. The space $K$ is invariant under both $A$ and its adjoints. On quotient [vectors](vector.md), $U\pi(a)[b]=ab\eta=aU[b]$, proving the displayed unitary equivalence after [continuity](continuous-function.md). The cyclic [vector](vector.md) maps to $\eta$.

If $A=\mathcal B(H)$, then $A\eta=H$, since the rank-one map $v\mapsto\langle v,\eta\rangle\zeta$ sends $\eta$ to any chosen $\zeta$. In this case, for the rank-one [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) $p_\eta$, $a\eta=0$ is equivalent to $ap_\eta=0$, by evaluating at $\eta$ and using $(ap_\eta)v=\langle v,\eta\rangle a\eta$.
