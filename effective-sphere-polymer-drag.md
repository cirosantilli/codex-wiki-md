# Effective-sphere polymer drag

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic radius](hydrodynamic-radius.md)

Approximating a dilute [polymer](polymer-split.md) coil as a hydrodynamic object of size $R$ gives $D\sim k_BT/(\eta R)$. If a stipulated model uses the theta-chain end-to-end distance $R=2a\sqrt N$ as a literal solid-sphere radius, [Stokes law](stokes-s-law.md) gives $D=k_BT/(12\pi\eta a\sqrt N)$. The scaling matches theta-solvent hydrodynamic coupling, but a permeable coil's [hydrodynamic radius](hydrodynamic-radius.md) is not generally equal to its end-to-end distance.
