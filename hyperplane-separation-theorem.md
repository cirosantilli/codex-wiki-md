# Hyperplane separation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Convex set](convex-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperplane_separation_theorem)

Two nonempty disjoint [convex sets](convex-set.md) in a finite-dimensional real [vector space](vector-space-split.md) admit a separating nonzero [linear functional](linear-functional.md): for an appropriate $h\ne0$ and $a$, $h\cdot x\leq a\leq h\cdot y$ on the respective sets. Strict separation requires additional assumptions, such as one set being compact and the other closed. For an open [convex cone](convex-cone.md) $C$ excluding zero, separation from $\{0\}$ gives $h\cdot c\geq0$ for all $c\in C$; scaling cone elements forces the separating level to be zero. This finite-dimensional result is related to the [supporting hyperplane theorem](supporting-hyperplane-theorem.md).
