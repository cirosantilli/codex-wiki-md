# Mott insulator

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mott_insulator)

A [Mott insulator](mott-insulator.md) is insulating because of [electron](electron.md) interactions even where a noninteracting [electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md) would predict conduction. This mechanism differs from disorder-driven [Anderson localization](anderson-localization.md).
