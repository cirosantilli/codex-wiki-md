# Collider effective mass

↑ **Parent:** [Missing transverse momentum](missing-transverse-momentum.md)

A scalar sum of [missing transverse momentum](missing-transverse-momentum.md) magnitude and selected high-energy transverse momenta, used as a hard-event-scale diagnostic in some [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) searches. Which jets and [leptons](lepton.md) enter must be defined. It is not an invariant [mass](mass.md) or the solid-state effective [mass](mass.md), and no universal equality with a produced particle [mass](mass.md) holds.
