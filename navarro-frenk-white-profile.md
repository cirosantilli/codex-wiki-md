<h1 id="navarro-frenk-white-profile">Navarro--Frenk--White profile</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navarro--Frenk--White_profile)

The Navarro--Frenk--White profile is

$$
\rho(r)=\frac{\rho_s}{(r/r_s)(1+r/r_s)^2}.
$$

It has an inner $r^{-1}$ density cusp and an outer $r^{-3}$ tail.
