# Dispersion relation of a floating elastic plate

↑ **Parent:** [Flexural-gravity wave](flexural-gravity-wave.md)

For [bending stiffness](bending-stiffness.md) $D$ and mass per area $m$, a [plane wave](plane-wave.md) of surface displacement $\eta$ satisfies $m\eta_{tt}+D\nabla_H^4\eta=p$. An inviscid deep-water [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) has vertical dependence $e^{kz}$; the [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) and linear [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) give $p=(\rho_w\omega^2/k-\rho_wg)\eta$. Eliminating $p$ gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). With water depth $d$, replace it by $\omega^2=(gk+Dk^5/\rho_w)\tanh(kd)/[1+(m/\rho_w)k\tanh(kd)]$.
