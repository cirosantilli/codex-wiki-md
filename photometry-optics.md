# Photometry (optics)

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photometry_(optics))

[Photometry](photometry-optics.md) weights visible [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) by standardized visual response functions; [radiometry](radiometry-split.md) measures its [energy](energy-split.md) and [power](power.md) without visual weighting.[Radiometry and Photometry: Review for Vision Optics](osa-handbook-of-optics-volume-iii-visual-optics-and-vision/radiometry-and-photometry-review-for-vision-optics.md)

The question is how much [light](light.md) a source supplies under a stated visual weighting. A beam containing $1\,\mathrm{mW}$ at the defining [frequency](frequency.md) $540\,\mathrm{THz}$ has [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md) $0.683\,\mathrm{lm}$. Moving the same [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) to a region of smaller [spectral luminous efficacy](spectral-luminous-efficacy.md) reduces its [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md). Changing the [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) changes this conversion even when its total [power](power.md) stays fixed. This makes [photometry](photometry-optics.md) useful for comparing lighting, while [radiometry](radiometry-split.md) remains necessary for an [energy](energy-split.md) budget. The [lumen](lumen-unit.md) counts [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md); the [candela](candela.md) additionally specifies its distribution over [solid angle](solid-angle.md).

Two measurement routes implement the weighting: measure an [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) and evaluate a weighted [integral](integral-split.md), or use a [photometer](photometer.md) whose [spectral responsivity](spectral-responsivity.md) approximates the prescribed weighting. Both require a specified collection geometry. Neither is a complete prediction of an individual's perception of a scene.[Radiometry and Photometry: Review for Vision Optics](osa-handbook-of-optics-volume-iii-visual-optics-and-vision/radiometry-and-photometry-review-for-vision-optics.md)
