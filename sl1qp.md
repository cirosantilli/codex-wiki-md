# Sl1QP

↑ **Parent:** [Sequential quadratic programming](sequential-quadratic-programming.md)

[Roger Fletcher](roger-fletcher-mathematician.md)'s method uses an [L1 exact penalty](l1-exact-penalty.md) on linearized constraints and a bounded [trust region](trust-region.md). It therefore admits a model minimizer even when hard linearized constraints are inconsistent or the quadratic model is indefinite.[§3.3, equations (3.25)–(3.26)](sequential-quadratic-programming-methods-by-philip-e-gill-and-elizabeth-wong.md)
