# Product decay in a cyclic imitation chain

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic imitation chain](cyclic-imitation-chain.md)

For the [cyclic imitation chain](cyclic-imitation-chain.md), its [infinitesimal generator](infinitesimal-generator-stochastic-processes.md) satisfies $\mathcal L_N(x_1x_2x_3)=-3x_1x_2x_3/N$. Therefore $\mathbb E[X_1^N(t)X_2^N(t)X_3^N(t)]=e^{-3t/N}\mathbb E[X_1^N(0)X_2^N(0)X_3^N(0)]$. While all three types remain, the [product of numbers](product-of-numbers.md) is at least $(N-2)/N^3$ for $N\geq3$, so this identity proves that one type is lost almost surely. The remaining pair has a fixed winner, which eventually occupies the whole population. The conserved product in the deterministic [fluid limit](fluid-limit.md) therefore describes fixed finite horizons, not the chain's infinite-time behavior.
