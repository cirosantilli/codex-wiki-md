# Mod-three Galois representation of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Division field of an elliptic curve](division-field-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

The absolute Galois group acts on $E[3]\simeq\mathbb F_3^2$, giving the mod-three Galois representation. Its invariant vectors are precisely the rational points of order dividing three.
