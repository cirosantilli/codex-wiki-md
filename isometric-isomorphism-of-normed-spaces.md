# Isometric isomorphism of normed spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md)

An isometric isomorphism between normed spaces is a bijective linear map $T$ satisfying $\|Tx\|=\|x\|$ for every vector $x$.
