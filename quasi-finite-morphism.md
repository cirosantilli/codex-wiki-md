# Quasi-finite morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of finite type](morphism-of-finite-type.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-finite_morphism)

A finite-type [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) is quasi-finite if its fibres are finite discrete schemes. A proper quasi-finite morphism is a [finite morphism](finite-morphism.md). In particular, a proper finite-type morphism of [projective varieties](projective-variety.md) with no positive-dimensional fibres is finite; a positive-dimensional projective fibre would contain an integral curve.
