# Actomyosin ring

↑ **Parent:** [Cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actomyosin_ring)

An [actomyosin ring](actomyosin-ring.md) is a contractile assembly containing [actin](actin.md) filaments and [myosin II](myosin-ii.md). In typical [animal](animal.md) [cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md), it forms in the [cell cortex](cell-cortex.md) beneath the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md). Its contraction draws the boundary inward to produce a [cleavage furrow](cleavage-furrow.md); an inward groove is not yet complete separation of the daughters.[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition, Chapter 14, The Events of M Phase, Cytokinesis and Figure 14.30: actin–myosin II ring beneath the plasma membrane](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/the-events-of-m-phase.md)
