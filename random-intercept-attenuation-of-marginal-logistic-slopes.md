# Random-intercept attenuation of marginal logistic slopes

↑ **Parent:** [Logistic random-intercept model for repeated binary outcomes](logistic-random-intercept-model-for-repeated-binary-outcomes.md)

Put $p_B=\operatorname{logit}^{-1}(\eta+B)$ and $M(\eta)=\mathbb E(p_B)$. Differentiation gives $M'=\mathbb E[p_B(1-p_B)]=M(1-M)-\operatorname{Var}(p_B)$. Hence the displayed derivative lies between zero and one, strictly below one for a nondegenerate [random intercept](random-intercept.md). A marginal [logit](logit.md) is therefore generally nonlinear and its slope differs from the conditional slope even without [confounding](confounding.md). This is a precise instance of [noncollapsibility of the odds ratio](noncollapsibility-of-the-odds-ratio.md).
