# Dimension of a bounded-total-degree polynomial space

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate polynomial](multivariate-polynomial.md)

The [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of formal [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) over a [field](field.md) $k$ in $n$ variables with [total degree of a polynomial](total-degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $d$ has the [monomial](monomial.md) basis $x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_n^{a_n}$ for nonnegative exponents with $\sum_i a_i\leq d$. Introducing a slack exponent and using [stars and bars](stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md) counts $\binom{n+d}{n}$ basis elements. These are formal [polynomials](polynomial-split.md); over a [finite field](finite-field.md), distinct formal [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) need not define distinct [polynomial functions](polynomial-function.md) unless suitable degree restrictions hold.
