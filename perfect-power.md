# Perfect power

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_power)

An integer is a perfect power when it has the form $x^k$ for integers $x$ and $k>1$.
