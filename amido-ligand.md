# Amido ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

An [amido ligand](amido-ligand.md) contains an anionic [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) donor, such as $\mathrm{NR_2^-}$. A neutral secondary [amine](amine.md) retaining its N–H bond is a different donor type; confusing them changes formal metal [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md).
