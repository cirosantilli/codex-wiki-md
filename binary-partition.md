# Binary partition

↑ **Parent:** [Integer partition](integer-partition.md)

An [integer partition](integer-partition.md) in which every part is a [power of two](power-of-two.md). Its [generating function](generating-function.md) is $\prod_{j\ge0}(1-x^{2^j})^{-1}$, because the factor indexed by $j$ chooses the multiplicity of the part $2^j$.
