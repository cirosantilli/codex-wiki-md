# Unitary product telescoping

↑ **Parent:** [Telescoping bound for products of operators](telescoping-bound-for-products-of-operators.md)

Insert one factor difference at a time: the $j$th term is $U_m\cdots U_{j+1}(U_j-V_j)V_{j-1}\cdots V_1$. Multiplication by the surrounding [unitary operators](unitary-operator.md) preserves the [spectral norm](matrix-2-norm.md), so the triangle inequality gives the displayed dimension-independent bound. No commutation assumption is required.
