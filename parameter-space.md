# Parameter space

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical model](statistical-model-split.md)

The [parameter space](parameter-space.md) of a [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md) is the set of allowed [statistical parameter](statistical-parameter.md) values indexing its [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md). For an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) parametrized by its [mean](expected-value-split.md), $\Theta=(0,\infty)$; for a [Bernoulli distribution](bernoulli-distribution.md), $\Theta=[0,1]$. Intersecting a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) with the known [parameter space](parameter-space.md) cannot remove the true [statistical parameter](statistical-parameter.md) and therefore preserves its [coverage probability](coverage-probability.md).
