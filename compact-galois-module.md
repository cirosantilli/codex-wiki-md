# Compact Galois module

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)

A compact Galois module is a compact [profinite abelian group](profinite-abelian-group.md) equipped with a continuous [Galois group](galois-group.md) action. An abelian [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) is naturally a compact $\mathbb Z_p$-[module](module-mathematics.md). A continuous action of the [Galois group](galois-group.md) of a [Zp-extension](zp-extension.md) extends to its [Iwasawa algebra of a Zp-extension](iwasawa-algebra-of-a-zp-extension.md). This compact topology differs from the discrete topology usually used in [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md).
