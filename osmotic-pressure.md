# Osmotic pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Osmosis](osmosis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmotic_pressure)

[Osmotic pressure](osmotic-pressure.md) is the excess [pressure](pressure-split.md) needed on a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) to maintain osmotic [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) with pure [solvent](solvent.md) across a [semipermeable membrane](semipermeable-membrane.md). For a nearly incompressible [solvent](solvent.md), equality of [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) gives $\Pi\simeq-(RT/\bar V_w)\ln a_w$. It is an equilibrium property; an observed initial swelling rate is a different measurement.[Gold Book, osmotic pressure: equilibrium definition, solvent activity formula and dilute ideal limit](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/osmotic-pressure.md)

For a dilute ideal [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) of retained particles,

$$
\Pi=\frac{nRT}{V}=RTc_{\mathrm{osm}}.
$$

Here $n$ is the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of independently dissolved [solute](solute.md) particles, measured in moles; $V$ is the final [volume](volume.md) of [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), not the starting [volume](volume.md) of pure [solvent](solvent.md). Count the resulting [ions](ion.md) separately if a [solute](solute.md) dissociates; do not multiply twice if $n$ already counts them. With $c_{\mathrm{osm}}$ in $\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}}$ and $R$ in $\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$, $RTc_{\mathrm{osm}}$ has units $\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}=\mathrm{Pa}$. This resembles the [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md), although the particles here are dissolved in a liquid.

The assumptions are uniform [temperature](temperature-split.md), a dilute ideal [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), a nearly incompressible [solvent](solvent.md), and a [semipermeable membrane](semipermeable-membrane.md) that retains the counted [solutes](solute.md) while passing [solvent](solvent.md). Association, incomplete dissociation and interactions change the relation between added material and independent particles; nonideal [thermodynamic activities](thermodynamic-activity.md) invalidate simple particle counting. The formula does not describe a concentrated brine merely because the apparatus contains a membrane.

A short derivation explains why particles are counted. In an ideal mixture, $a_w=x_w=n_w/(n_w+n)$, with $n_w$ the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of [water](water.md). If $n\ll n_w$, then $-\ln x_w=\ln(1+n/n_w)\simeq n/n_w$ and $V\simeq n_w\bar V_w$. Substitution gives $\Pi\simeq RTn/V$. This is a dilute-limit approximation, not a claim that [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) stop moving or that dissolved particles are a gas.
