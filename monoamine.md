# Monoamine

↑ **Parent:** [Amine](amine.md)

In neuroscience, a class of transmitters including [serotonin](serotonin.md) and the catecholamines [norepinephrine](norepinephrine.md) and [dopamine](dopamine.md). Their effects depend on receptor, pathway and temporal context as well as amount.
