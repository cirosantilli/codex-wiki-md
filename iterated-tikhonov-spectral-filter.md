# Iterated Tikhonov spectral filter

↑ **Parent:** [Iterated Tikhonov regularization](iterated-tikhonov-regularization.md)

The implicit iteration $(I+\tau K^*K)u_{k+1}=u_k+\tau K^*f$, starting from zero, multiplies each data [singular vector](singular-vector.md) by $[1-(1+\tau\sigma^2)^{-k}]/\sigma$. Its [operator norm](operator-norm.md) is at most $\sqrt{k\tau}$.
