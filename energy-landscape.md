# Energy landscape

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

An [energy landscape](energy-landscape.md) assigns an [energy](energy-split.md) to each possible state and describes its variation between neighbouring states. Its [local minima](local-minimum.md) and intervening [energy barriers](energy-barrier.md) help distinguish favourable states from states that a specified dynamics can reach quickly. In a [Hopfield network](hopfield-network.md), the assigned [energy](energy-split.md) is an objective function and need not be a conserved physical quantity.
