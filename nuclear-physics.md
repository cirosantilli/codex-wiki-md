# Nuclear physics

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_physics)

The study of [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md), their structure, reactions and interactions. [Nuclear forces](nuclear-force-split.md) between [nucleons](nucleon.md) and [nuclear binding energies](nuclear-binding-energy.md) connect it to the low-energy consequences of [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md).
