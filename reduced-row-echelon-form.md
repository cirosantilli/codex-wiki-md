# Reduced row echelon form

↑ **Parent:** [Row echelon form](row-echelon-form.md)

A [row echelon form](row-echelon-form.md) whose pivots are one and whose pivot columns have zeros in every other row. Over a [field](field.md), each [row space](row-space.md) has a unique such [matrix](matrix.md) after zero rows are removed. It describes a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) independently of which initial [basis](basis.md) was used; [Gaussian binomial coefficients](gaussian-binomial-coefficient.md) exploit this uniqueness to count [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) over a [finite field](finite-field.md).
