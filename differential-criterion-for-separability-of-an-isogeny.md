# Differential criterion for separability of an isogeny

↑ **Parent:** [Separable isogeny](separable-isogeny.md)

A nonzero [isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md) is separable exactly when its pullback of an [invariant differential on an elliptic curve](invariant-differential-on-an-elliptic-curve.md) is nonzero. For a map of smooth curves in characteristic $p$, a zero differential means the function-field extension has a nontrivial inseparable part. The criterion turns sums of elliptic endomorphisms into computations of their tangent scalars.
