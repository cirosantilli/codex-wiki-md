# Denominator ideal proof for global regular functions

↑ **Parent:** [Global regular function](global-regular-function.md)

A [regular function](regular-function.md) $f$ on an entire [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) is a [polynomial function](polynomial-function.md), even if the [variety](algebraic-variety-split.md) is reducible. Put $J=\{g\in A(X):gf\in A(X)\}$, an [ideal](ideal.md) of its [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md). Near a [point](point-geometry.md) $p$, write $f=a/b$ with $b$ nonzero, and choose a [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(h)$ containing $p$ inside that [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md). Then $(hb)f=ha$ on all of $X$: on $D(h)$ this is the fraction identity, and off $D(h)$ both sides vanish. Hence $hb\in J$ and $(hb)(p)\neq0$. No [point](point-geometry.md) is a common zero of $J$, so the weak [Hilbert Nullstellensatz](hilbert-nullstellensatz.md) implies $J=A(X)$. Thus $1\in J$ and $f\in A(X)$. This proof does not require a [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) for a reducible [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md).
