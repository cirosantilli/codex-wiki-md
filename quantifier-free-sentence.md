# Quantifier-free sentence

↑ **Parent:** [Quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md)

A quantifier-free sentence is a [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md) with no free variables. In a language whose only closed group terms denote zero, all quantifier-free sentences have the same truth value in every group, even when existential sentences distinguish them.
