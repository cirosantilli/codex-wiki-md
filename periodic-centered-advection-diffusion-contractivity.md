# Periodic centered advection-diffusion contractivity

↑ **Parent:** [Energy method](energy-method.md)

The semidiscrete system $U'=D_{xx}U+\alpha D_0U$, for real fixed $\alpha$, is contractive in the periodic discrete [L2 norm](l2-norm.md). [Discrete summation by parts](discrete-summation-by-parts.md) makes the drift term's real energy contribution zero and the diffusion term nonpositive. Equivalently its [Fourier amplification symbol](fourier-amplification-symbol.md) generator has real part $-4h^{-2}\sin^2(\theta/2)$. This continuous-time estimate does not prove stability of an arbitrarily chosen time integrator.
