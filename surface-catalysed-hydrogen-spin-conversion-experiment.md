# Surface-catalysed hydrogen spin conversion experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Spin isomers of hydrogen](spin-isomers-of-hydrogen.md)

The 1935 primary paper [The Spin-Isomerization of Hydrogen at Paramagnetic and Diamagnetic Surfaces](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja01310a023), pp. 1251–1252, describes evacuated [catalyst](catalyst.md) samples cooled before admitting [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md). Gas samples were analyzed in a [glass](glass.md) cell in [liquid air](liquid-air.md): a heated [platinum](platinum.md) wire and [Wheatstone bridge](wheatstone-bridge.md) converted changes in [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) into [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) readings, calibrated against [normal hydrogen](normal-hydrogen.md).[p. 1251, Experimental Details: sample handling and heated-wire thermal-conductivity analysis](the-spin-isomerization-of-hydrogen-at-paramagnetic-and-diamagnetic-surfaces-by-hugh-stott-taylor-and-h-diamond.md)

Its comparison of [oxide](oxide.md) surfaces supplied a control: specimens exhibiting strong [paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md) converted the gas much faster than the comparison exhibiting [diamagnetism](diamagnetism.md) despite all adsorbing [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md). Contact time, [pressure](pressure-split.md), and [temperature](temperature-split.md) were recorded. This supports a role for surface magnetic properties beyond [adsorption](adsorption.md) alone; it does not identify every microscopic surface site or directly measure nuclear-spin orientations.[p. 1252, Table I description and Discussion of Results: rare-earth oxide comparison and adsorption qualification](the-spin-isomerization-of-hydrogen-at-paramagnetic-and-diamagnetic-surfaces-by-hugh-stott-taylor-and-h-diamond.md)

The inference chain is preparation, contact with a specified surface, then a calibrated bulk transport readout of composition. It is distinct from a direct [calorimetry](calorimetry-split.md) measurement of [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md): transport and [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) can both depend on the spin populations, but they are different measured quantities. Changing the catalyst's own surface structure, introducing magnetic impurities, or changing the detector's gas [pressure](pressure-split.md) can confound an uncalibrated comparison. The free-rotor [partition functions](canonical-partition-function.md) predict equilibrium populations and stored [energy](energy-split.md), not the conversion rate on a particular surface.
