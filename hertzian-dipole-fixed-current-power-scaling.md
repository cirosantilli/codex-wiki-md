# Hertzian dipole fixed-current power scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Hertzian dipole](hertzian-dipole.md)

A uniform electrically short [current element](current-element.md) of [length](length.md) $\ell$ with sinusoidal peak current $I_0$ has [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $p_0=I_0\ell/\omega$. The mean [electric dipole radiation](electric-dipole-radiation.md) [power](power.md) is $\omega^4p_0^2/(12\pi\epsilon_0c^3)$, so fixing current makes it proportional to $\ell^2\omega^2$, rather than $\ell^2\omega^4$. Current, charge excursion and dipole moment are different quantities to hold fixed when comparing antennas. The approximation requires $\ell\ll\lambda$ and does not impose the nonuniform standing-current profile of a long antenna.
