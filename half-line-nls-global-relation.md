# Half-line NLS global relation

↑ **Parent:** [Half-line NLS spectral functions](half-line-nls-spectral-functions.md)

Evaluating the [Lax pair](lax-pair.md) connection formula at $(0,T)$ gives $S^{-1}s=e^{2ik^2T\widehat\sigma_3}\mu_3(0,T,k)$. The upper off-diagonal entry is the displayed relation, where $c_T=[\mu_3(0,T)]_{12}$ is analytic in the upper half-plane and is $O(1/k)$. A finite-time relation must retain this final-state term.
