# Projective modules are flat

↑ **Parent:** [Projective module](projective-module.md)

A [projective module](projective-module.md) is a [direct summand](direct-summand.md) of a [free module](free-module.md). [Free modules](free-module.md) are flat because tensoring with a [free module](free-module.md) produces a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of copies of the original module and map. If $F=P\oplus Q$ is flat, tensoring an [injection](injective-function.md) with $F$ is the [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of its tensor maps with $P$ and $Q$, so each component is [injective](injective-function.md). Applying this to a [free module](free-module.md) containing $P$ proves that $P$ is [flat](flat-module.md).
