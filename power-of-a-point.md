# Power of a point

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

The power of a point $P$ relative to a [circle](circle.md) with center $O$ and [radius](radius.md) $r$ is $OP^2-r^2$. If a line through $P$ meets the [circle](circle.md) at $A$ and $B$, the product of the directed distances $PA$ and $PB$ equals this quantity.
