<h1 id="higman-s-lemma">Higman's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higman's_lemma)

If $Q$ is a [well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md), its finite [words](string.md) form a [well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md) under subsequence embedding with coordinatewise increase of letters. A [minimal bad sequence](minimal-bad-sequence.md) proof removes the last letter of selected [words](string.md) whose last letters form a nondecreasing [subsequence](subsequence.md), then contradicts minimality. The result implies [finite-subset lifting of a well-quasi-order](finite-subset-lifting-of-a-well-quasi-order.md) under [Hoare domination preorder](hoare-domination-preorder.md).
