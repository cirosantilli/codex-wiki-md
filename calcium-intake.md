# Calcium intake

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium in biology](calcium-in-biology.md)

Calcium intake is the amount of calcium consumed, including food and supplements. It is an [exposure](exposure.md) that can be changed by an intervention, and should not be identified automatically with [serum calcium](serum-calcium.md) in a [causal inference](causal-inference-split.md) analysis.
