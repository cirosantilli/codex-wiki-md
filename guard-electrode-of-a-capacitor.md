# Guard electrode of a capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Fringing field of a capacitor](fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md)

A [guard electrode of a capacitor](guard-electrode-of-a-capacitor.md) surrounds an electrically isolated sensing [electrode](electrode.md) and is held at the same [electric potential](electric-potential.md). The small insulating slot separates their readout paths. The guard extends the nearly planar [equipotential](equipotential.md), moving the main outer edge away from the sensing region; it does not abolish the [fringing field of a capacitor](fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) everywhere. In a capacitance fixture, fringe [electric current](electric-current.md) is routed through the guard instead of the sensing readout. Keysight describes this three-[electrode](electrode.md) method in [Materials Measurement: Dielectric Materials](materials-measurement-dielectric-materials.md) (Solution, 16451B fixture).[Solution: guard absorbs current through fringe capacitance](materials-measurement-dielectric-materials.md)

Measuring the sensing and guard [electric currents](electric-current.md) together would include the diverted response again. Grounding an arbitrary surrounding [electrode](electrode.md) also does not reproduce a guard unless its [electric potential](electric-potential.md) matches the sensing [electrode](electrode.md). Finite guard width, the insulating slot, plate misalignment and specimen air gaps limit the approximation. Use [capacitance measurement calibration](capacitance-measurement-calibration.md) and the geometry appropriate to the sensing region when inferring [permittivity](permittivity.md). The source also warns that guarding changes the [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md) unfavourably at high [frequency](frequency.md), so the low-[frequency](frequency.md) electrostatic picture has a restricted range.[Solution: air gaps, high-frequency guard limitations, fixture compensation](materials-measurement-dielectric-materials.md)
