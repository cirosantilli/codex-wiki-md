# Ligand-gated ion channel

↑ **Parent:** [Ion channel](ion-channel.md)

A [ligand-gated ion channel](ligand-gated-ion-channel.md) couples ligand occupancy to the probability of pore opening. The ligand-binding site and ion pathway can be distinct, and sustained occupancy can lead to a desensitized nonconducting state.
