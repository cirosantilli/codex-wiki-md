<h1 id="truncated-mobius-inverse-identity-for-the-riemann-zeta-function">Truncated Möbius inverse identity for the Riemann zeta function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

For $T/2\le\Im s\le T$ and $0<\Re s=\sigma\le1$, the [Hardy-Littlewood approximation to the Riemann zeta function](hardy-littlewood-approximation-to-the-riemann-zeta-function.md) truncates zeta at $T$ with error $O(T^{-\sigma})$. Multiply the finite sums and use $a_n=\sum_{m\mid n,\ m\le M,\ n/m\le T}\mu(m)$. All coefficients with $1<n\le\min(M,T)$ vanish by the [Möbius divisor-sum identity](mobius-divisor-sum-identity.md). The total error is bounded using $\sum_{m\le M}m^{-\sigma}\ll M^{1-\sigma}\log M$.
