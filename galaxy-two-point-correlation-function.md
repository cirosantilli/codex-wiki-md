# Galaxy two-point correlation function

↑ **Parent:** [Correlation function (astronomy)](correlation-function-astronomy.md)

For a homogeneous isotropic [point process](point-process.md) of [galaxies](galaxy-split.md) with mean number density $\bar n$, the [galaxy two-point correlation function](galaxy-two-point-correlation-function.md) gives the excess pair probability at distinct positions. Dividing the factorial pair density by the probability of a central [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) gives conditional neighbouring-galaxy density $\bar n[1+\xi(r)]$. Choosing a central location uniformly in volume does not perform this conditioning.
