# Translational symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry (physics)](symmetry-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translational_symmetry)

Invariance of an action, equation or configuration under a [translation](translation-geometry.md). For a continuous spatial translation symmetry, [Noether's theorem](noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) supplies the conserved [momentum](momentum-split.md); spacetime translation gives the [canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md).
