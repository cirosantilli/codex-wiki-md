# Renormalization of spontaneously broken gauge theories

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

[Renormalization of spontaneously broken gauge theories](renormalization-of-spontaneously-broken-gauge-theories.md) means that the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md) can generate [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) [masses](mass.md) while a fixed finite set of [renormalized couplings](renormalized-coupling.md), [masses](mass.md) and field normalizations still suffices to absorb [ultraviolet divergences](ultraviolet-divergence.md). The symmetry constrains the [counterterms](counterterm.md) even when the chosen [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) hides it. This makes [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md) to the [electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md) calculable after inputs have been fixed by [renormalization conditions](renormalization-condition.md).

A useful distinction is between choosing variables that display the physical [particles](particle-physics.md) and choosing variables that display good [power counting](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md). [Unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md) removes the would-be [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md) from the explicit fields. [R-xi gauge](r-xi-gauge.md) keeps them and the required [Faddeev-Popov ghosts](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) in intermediate [Feynman diagrams](feynman-diagram.md), exposing simpler large-[momentum](momentum-split.md) behavior. The physical [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) must agree after all contributions and [counterterms](counterterm.md) have been included consistently.

In Renormalizable Lagrangians for massive Yang-Mills fields (1971), [Gerard 't Hooft](gerardus-t-hooft.md) constructed spontaneously broken [gauge theories](gauge-theory.md) with massive [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md). His worked [SU(2) gauge theory with an adjoint Higgs field](su-2-gauge-theory-with-an-adjoint-higgs-field.md) leaves a massless [photon](photon.md); it is a demonstration model, not the full chiral [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md). The [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md), [Faddeev-Popov ghosts](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) and [Ward identities](ward-identity.md) connect [renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md) with physical [unitarity](unitary-operator.md).[§§3–5, pp. 171–177; Eqs. (3.8)–(3.14)](renormalizable-lagrangians-for-massive-yang-mills-fields-by-gerardus-t-hooft.md)

Regularization and renormalization of gauge fields (1972), by [Gerard 't Hooft](gerardus-t-hooft.md) and [Martinus J. G. Veltman](martinus-justinus-godefridus-veltman.md), developed [dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md) with recursive subtraction of [ultraviolet subdivergences](ultraviolet-subdivergence.md). It continued [Feynman integrals](feynman-integral.md) in the dimension, keeping the relevant [Ward identities](ward-identity.md) in non-anomalous cases; its final section examined [quantum anomalies](anomaly-physics.md). The procedure does not make an inconsistent chiral [gauge theory](gauge-theory.md) consistent: [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md) cancellation remains essential.[§1, pp. 189–191; §4, pp. 198–205; §6, pp. 206–208](regularization-and-renormalization-of-gauge-fields-by-gerardus-t-hooft-and-martinus-justinus-godefridus-veltman.md)

The organizing proof outline is to use good large-[momentum](momentum-split.md) [propagators](propagator.md), subtract every divergent subgraph, and constrain the remaining local divergences by the [Ward identities](ward-identity.md). A finite family of allowed [counterterms](counterterm.md) must close under this operation at every [loop order](loop-order.md). The same identities must remove unphysical intermediate contributions from physical amplitudes. [Power counting](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) alone establishes neither closure nor these cancellations. Modern [BRST symmetry](brst-symmetry.md) packages these restrictions; the scalar subtraction below illustrates only locality.
