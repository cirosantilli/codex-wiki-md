↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Constantin Carathéodory](work-by-constantin-caratheodory.md)

By [Constantin Carathéodory](constantin-caratheodory.md). Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo, volume 32, pp. 193–217 (1911).

Publisher metadata: [https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF03014795](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF03014795)

English translation: [https://nchlswllms.github.io/translations/cc1911.pdf](https://nchlswllms.github.io/translations/cc1911.pdf)

The translation is titled On the Domain of Variability of Fourier Coefficients of Positive Harmonic Functions and credits [Nicholas J. Williams](nicholas-j-williams.md) as translator. It records the original announcement on 26 March 1911 and printing on 16 August 1911; neither dates the translation. The original German scan was not accessible.

Cited passages: Introduction §4, original printed p. 195; §I opening.
