# Causal structure of the magnetic dilaton black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetically charged dilaton black hole](magnetically-charged-dilaton-black-hole.md)

For $r_-<r_+$, an ingoing chart with $v=t+r_*$ has metric $-Vdv^2+2dv\,dr+R^2d\Omega_2^2$. In its future interior, $\nabla r=\partial_v+V\partial_r$ is future timelike, so every future causal curve decreases $r$ and cannot reach [future null infinity](future-null-infinity.md). The singularity $r=r_-$ is spacelike. A [Kruskal–Szekeres coordinates](kruskal-szekeres-coordinates.md) extension has the same qualitative [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md) as [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md), with two exterior regions, a future black-hole interior and a past [white hole](white-hole.md) interior. The past interior has the same radial interval but is not a [black hole](black-hole.md) region; specifying the radial interval alone does not identify the future component. For $r_->r_+$, the physical exterior $r>r_-$ has $V>0$ everywhere and outgoing null rays escape from arbitrarily near a timelike [naked singularity](naked-singularity.md).
