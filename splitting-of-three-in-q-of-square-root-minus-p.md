# Splitting of three in Q of square root minus p

↑ **Parent:** [Splitting of rational primes in a quadratic field](splitting-of-rational-primes-in-a-quadratic-field.md)

If $p\equiv11\pmod{12}$ and $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-p})$, then $\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[(1+\sqrt{-p})/2]$. The generator's minimal polynomial reduces modulo $3$ to $T(T-1)$, so $(3)$ splits into two distinct prime ideals of norm three.
