<h1 id="de-la-vallee-poussin-criterion">De la Vallée-Poussin criterion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md)

On a finite measure space, a family is [uniformly integrable](uniform-integrability.md) exactly when it admits the displayed bound for some nonnegative increasing convex superlinear [function](function-split.md) $\Psi$. By the [Dunford-Pettis theorem](dunford-pettis-theorem.md) this ensures relative weak [compactness](compact-space.md) in $L^1$. For the sufficient direction, choose $R_A$ so $\Psi(s)\geq As$ for $s>R_A$; then $\int_E|v_j|\leq R_A\mu(E)+A^{-1}\sup_j\int\Psi(|v_j|)$ gives [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md). Conversely choose increasing thresholds $R_m\to\infty$ so $\sup_j\int_{|v_j|>R_m}|v_j|\leq2^{-m}$. The [convex function](convex-function.md) $\Psi(s)=\sum_{m\geq1}(s-R_m)_+$ is locally a finite sum, has uniformly bounded integrals by summing these tail estimates, and is superlinear because for $s\geq2R_N$ its first $N$ terms sum to at least $Ns/2$. This proves the criterion in both directions.
