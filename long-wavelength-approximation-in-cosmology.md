# Long-wavelength approximation in cosmology

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear cosmological perturbation theory](nonlinear-cosmological-perturbation-theory.md)

When physical wavelengths greatly exceed the [Hubble radius](hubble-radius.md), spatial derivatives are small compared with temporal expansion scales. A cosmological gradient expansion then approximates neighbouring regions as locally homogeneous universes, while retaining finite perturbation amplitudes. This does not alone discard frozen [tensor cosmological perturbations](tensor-cosmological-perturbation.md).
