# Mersenne prime

↑ **Parent:** [Mersenne number](mersenne-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mersenne_prime)

A [Mersenne prime](mersenne-prime.md) is a [Mersenne number](mersenne-number.md) that is a [prime number](prime-number-split.md). It is the prime factor required by the [Euclid-Euler theorem](euclid-euler-theorem.md); $3,7,31,127$ generate $6,28,496,8128$. The family is named after [Marin Mersenne](marin-mersenne.md).
