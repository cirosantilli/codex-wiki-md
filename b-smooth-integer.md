# B-smooth integer

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth number](smooth-number.md)

For a specified finite prime [factor base](factor-base.md) $B$, a nonzero integer is $B$-smooth if every prime dividing its absolute value belongs to $B$. Negative residues are handled by including the sign $-1$ in their exponent vectors. This notation refers to a finite prime set, rather than the alternative convention in which a numerical smoothness bound is called $B$.
