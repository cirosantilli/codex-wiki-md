# Sharp area-cost bound for a barrier with fixed endpoints

↑ **Parent:** [Isoperimetric constraint](isoperimetric-constraint.md)

For $y(0)=y(1)=0$, write $A=\int_0^1y\,dx$ and $w=x(1-x)$. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $2A=\int_0^1y'w'\,dx$, while $\int_0^1w'^2dx=1/3$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) therefore gives the displayed sharp bound, with equality exactly when $y=Kw$. A prescribed positive budget $B=\int_0^1(y+y'^2)dx$ consequently gives

$$
A\leq\frac{\sqrt{1+48B}-1}{24},\qquad y_{\mathrm{opt}}(x)=\frac{\sqrt{1+48B}-1}{4}x(1-x).
$$

This proves the unique global optimum, rather than only stationarity from a [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md).
