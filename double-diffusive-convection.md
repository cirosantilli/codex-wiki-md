# Double-diffusive convection

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal convection](thermal-convection.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-diffusive_convection)

Double-diffusive convection occurs when two properties, such as [temperature](temperature.md) and composition, affect [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) but diffuse at different rates. A stabilizing composition gradient can oppose a destabilizing thermal gradient and change the form of heat transport.
