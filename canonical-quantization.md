# Canonical quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canonical_quantization)

Canonical quantization promotes [canonical variables](canonical-variables.md) to operators and their [Poisson brackets](poisson-bracket.md) to [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md). Composite observables require an ordering prescription, and [quantum anomalies](anomaly-physics.md) can modify their algebra.
