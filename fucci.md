# Fucci

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)

A fluorescent ubiquitination-based cell-cycle indicator using phase-dependent stability of reporter fusions. The original [Cdt1](cdt1.md) and [geminin](geminin.md) reporters distinguish much of [G1 phase](g1-phase.md) from [S phase](s-phase.md)/[G2 phase](g2-phase.md)/[M phase](m-phase.md), rather than reporting all phase boundaries independently.[Abstract and reporter design](visualizing-spatiotemporal-dynamics-of-multicellular-cell-cycle-progression-by-asako-sakaue-sawano-and-others.md)
