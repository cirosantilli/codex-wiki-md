# Spot variance

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted realized variance](weighted-realized-variance.md)

The spot variance is the instantaneous squared noise coefficient $\sigma(t)^2$ of an [Itô integral](ito-integral.md). It can be estimated by averaging the [weighted realized variance](weighted-realized-variance.md) over a shrinking time window. A [Hölder continuity](holder-condition.md) assumption bounds the smoothing [bias](bias-of-an-estimator.md).
