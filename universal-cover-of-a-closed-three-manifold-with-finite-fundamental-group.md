# Universal cover of a closed three-manifold with finite fundamental group

↑ **Parent:** [Universal cover](universal-cover.md)

The [universal cover](universal-cover.md) of a connected [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) of dimension three with finite [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is a closed simply connected three-manifold. It is orientable, since its orientation character has trivial domain. Its first integral [homology](homology-split.md) vanishes, and the [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) then gives $H^1=0$. Integral [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) gives $H_2=H^1=0$, while $H_0$ and $H_3$ are $\mathbb Z$. Thus it is an [integral homology sphere](homology-sphere.md). This proof does not require classifying simply connected three-manifolds.
