# Adiabatic scaling of a Fermi gas with power-law density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md)

For [density of states](density-of-states.md) $AV\epsilon^a$, $a>-1$, the [grand canonical ensemble](grand-canonical-ensemble.md) has $\log\mathcal Z=VT^{a+1}f(\mu/T)$. [Strict monotonicity of Fermi gas entropy per particle](strict-monotonicity-of-fermi-gas-entropy-per-particle.md) makes $\mu/T$ constant at fixed entropy and particle number. Therefore $VT^{a+1}$ and $PV^{(a+2)/(a+1)}$ are constant along a reversible [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md).
