<h1 id="rankin-selberg-unfolding-identity-for-a-holomorphic-eisenstein-series">Rankin–Selberg unfolding identity for a holomorphic Eisenstein series</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rankin–Selberg convolution](rankin-selberg-convolution.md)

If $f$ and $g$ have respective weights $k$ and $l$, and $r=k-l>2$, unfolding the weight-$r$ Eisenstein series gives

$$
\langle f,gG_r\rangle
=\frac{2\zeta(r)\Gamma(k-1)}{(4\pi)^{k-1}}L(f,g,k-1).
$$
