# Fermi-surface mean free path from sheet resistivity

↑ **Parent:** [Sheet resistivity](sheet-resistivity.md)

For a spin-degenerate parabolic two-dimensional band, the [Drude model](drude-model.md) gives $\tau=m^*/(ne^2\rho_\square)$ and $v_F=\hbar k_F/m^*$. Their product is the displayed mass-independent [mean free path](mean-free-path.md). A Fermi-surface transport interpretation requires a sufficiently [degenerate electron gas](degenerate-electron-gas.md); a quantum lifetime need not equal the transport relaxation time.
