# Twistor theory

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twistor_theory)

[Twistor theory](twistor-theory.md) encodes the [conformal geometry](conformal-geometry.md) of four-dimensional spacetime using incidence geometry in a complex vector space. In the flat correspondence, a spacetime point is a two-plane in a four-dimensional [twistor space](twistor-space.md), and hence a projective line in [projective twistor space](projective-twistor-space.md). Null separation becomes intersection of these lines. Choosing a spacetime signature is an additional reality condition on the complex geometry.
