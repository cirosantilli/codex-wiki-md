# Accretion trajectories in a compact viscous similarity disk

↑ **Parent:** [Compact self-similar disk with density-linear viscosity](compact-self-similar-disk-with-density-linear-viscosity.md)

For the $a=1$ [compact self-similar disk with density-linear viscosity](compact-self-similar-disk-with-density-linear-viscosity.md), radial drift is $\dot r=r/(2t)-R/(10t)$. An integrating factor gives the displayed trajectories. From $0<r_i<R(t_i)$, the arrival time at the origin is $t_i[1-r_i/R(t_i)]^{-10}$, finite for every interior point. The outer-edge trajectory has zero [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) and carries no mass.
