# Quantum vortex

↑ **Parent:** [Phase vortex](phase-vortex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_vortex)

A [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md) has integer [winding number](winding-number.md) of a condensate's [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md). For atomic mass $m$, its [superfluid velocity](superfluid-velocity.md) gives [circulation](circulation-physics.md) $2\pi\hbar q/m$ for integer $q$. A straight vortex has a density-depleted core and, outside it, tangential [velocity](velocity.md) $q\hbar/(mr)$. Its long-range flow [energy](energy.md) is proportional to $q^2\log(R/a)$, while its [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) has the sign of $q$.
