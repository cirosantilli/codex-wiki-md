# Semisimple algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_algebra)

A finite-dimensional [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) is semisimple when its left regular module is a direct sum of simple modules. Equivalently, every [left ideal](left-ideal.md) is a direct summand. In particular, a semisimple algebra has no nonzero square-zero left ideal: if $L^2=0$ and a module projection $A\to L$ exists, its value $e$ at $1$ satisfies $e\in L$ and $e^2=e$, forcing $e=0$.
