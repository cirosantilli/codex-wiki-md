# Internal Heyting algebra of truth values

↑ **Parent:** [Subobject classifier](subobject-classifier.md)

The subobject classifier $\Omega$ of a topos carries internal truth, falsity, meet, join and implication. Its generalized elements over $X$ are subobjects of $X$; implication satisfies $R\leq(P\Rightarrow Q)$ exactly when $R\cap P\leq Q$. Pullback-compatible operations make this an internal [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra.md), not merely a structure on global truth values.
