# Huygens-Fresnel principle

↑ **Parent:** [Physical optics](physical-optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huygens–Fresnel_principle)

The [Huygens-Fresnel principle](huygens-fresnel-principle.md) describes propagation by coherent addition of secondary-wave contributions from a wavefront or aperture. Their [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md), including relative [wave phases](phase-waves.md), must be summed before calculating [light intensity](light-intensity.md). The [Fresnel propagator](fresnel-propagator.md) follows by retaining quadratic propagation [wave phase](phase-waves.md) in the [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md).
