# Adult hippocampal neurogenesis

↑ **Parent:** [Hippocampus](hippocampus.md)

Generation of new neurons in the adult hippocampal dentate region. Whether it mediates a particular drug effect depends on species, age, task and intervention; a rodent dependency does not prove a universal human clinical mechanism.
