# Weight-transfer proof of irreducibility of symmetric powers

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric power](symmetric-power.md)

In the [symmetric power](symmetric-power.md) $\operatorname{Sym}^m\mathbb C^n$ of the defining [unitary group](unitary-group.md) representation, diagonal [torus](torus.md) weights are indexed by nonnegative integer tuples summing to $m$, with one monomial per [weight space](weight-space.md). Torus averaging extracts a monomial from any nonzero invariant complex subspace. The complexified [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) acts by $E_{ij}=x_i\partial_{x_j}$ on the symmetric algebra. Whenever $a_j>0$, the displayed action moves one unit of degree from $j$ to $i$ with nonzero coefficient. Finite transfers connect every degree-$m$ monomial, so the subspace is the entire representation. The case $m=0$ is the one-dimensional trivial representation. This proof needs neither the [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md) nor the [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md).
