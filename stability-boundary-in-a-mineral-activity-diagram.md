# Stability boundary in a mineral activity diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Mineral activity diagram](mineral-activity-diagram.md)

A pairwise equilibrium line restricted to conditions where both minerals are stable against the other allowed phases. Compute the [Gibbs energy of reaction](gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) for independent conversions to compare them. A pairwise line can continue into a region where a third [mineral](mineral.md) is favoured; this continuation is a metastable equilibrium relation, rather than a boundary of stable fields.
