# Hyperbolic area bounds an inscribed disc

↑ **Parent:** [Area of a hyperbolic disc](area-of-a-hyperbolic-disc.md)

If a domain of finite [hyperbolic area](hyperbolic-area.md) $A$ contains a hyperbolic disc of radius $r$ in curvature $-1$, then $2\pi(\cosh r-1)\leq A$, hence $r\leq\operatorname{arcosh}(1+A/(2\pi))$. For an interior point of a [hyperbolic triangle](hyperbolic-triangle.md), the open disc with radius equal to its distance from the boundary lies inside the triangle. Its area is at most $\pi$ by [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md), giving the elementary bound $r\leq\operatorname{arcosh}(3/2)$; this need not be the sharp triangle inradius bound.
