<h1 id="plunnecke-inequality">Plünnecke inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sumset](sumset.md)

For nonempty finite subsets $A,B$ of an [abelian group](abelian-group.md), $|A+B|\leq K|A|$ implies that some nonempty $X\subseteq A$ satisfies this bound simultaneously for every integer $m\geq0$, with $0B=\{0\}$. The [Petridis minimal-growth lemma](petridis-minimal-growth-lemma.md) proves this by choosing $X$ to minimize $|X+B|/|X|$. The [Ruzsa triangle inequality](ruzsa-triangle-inequality.md) then gives the [Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality](plunnecke-ruzsa-inequality.md) $|kB-\ell B|\leq K^{k+\ell}|A|$.
