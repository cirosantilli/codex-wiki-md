# John ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md)

The [John ellipsoid](john-ellipsoid.md) of a convex body is its inscribed [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) of largest volume. For the [unit ball](unit-ball.md) $K$ of a $d$-dimensional [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md), change coordinates so that this [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) is the [Euclidean unit ball](euclidean-unit-ball.md). The resulting coordinates are called [John position](john-position.md). In these coordinates the [John contact decomposition](john-contact-decomposition.md) implies $B_2^d\subseteq K\subseteq\sqrt d B_2^d$, or $\|x\|_E\leq |x|\leq\sqrt d\|x\|_E$.
