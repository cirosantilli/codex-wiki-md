# t-intersecting family

↑ **Parent:** [Intersecting family](intersecting-family.md)

A [set family](set-family.md) is t-intersecting if every pair of its members has at least $t$ common elements. For $r$-sets on an $n$-point ground set, the whole level is t-intersecting when $n\leq2r-t$. Ordinary [intersecting families](intersecting-family.md) are the case $t=1$.
