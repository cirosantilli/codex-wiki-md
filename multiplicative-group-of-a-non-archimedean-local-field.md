# Multiplicative group of a non-Archimedean local field

↑ **Parent:** [Local field](local-field.md)

If a non-Archimedean [local field](local-field.md) $K$ has [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\pi$, [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) $\mathcal O_K$, maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_K$ and residue field $\mathbb F_q$, then

$$
K^\times\cong\pi^{\mathbb Z}\times\mu_{q-1}\times(1+\mathfrak m_K).
$$

The first factor records the [discrete valuation](discrete-valuation.md), the [Teichmuller representatives](teichmuller-representative.md) split reduction $\mathcal O_K^\times\to\mathbb F_q^\times$, and $1+\mathfrak m_K$ is the group of [principal units](principal-unit.md).
