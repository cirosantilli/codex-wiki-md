# Factorial

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factorial)

For a nonnegative [integer](integer.md) $n$, the factorial is

$$
n!=1\cdot2\cdots n,
$$

with $0!=1$.
