# Jeans length and gravitational time conventions

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans length](jeans-length.md)

The physical [Jeans length](jeans-length.md) is $\ell_J=c_s\sqrt{\pi/(G\rho_0)}$. A pressureless uniform sphere of initial density $\rho_0$ has [free-fall time of a uniform sphere](free-fall-time-of-a-uniform-sphere.md) $t_{\rm ff}=\sqrt{3\pi/(32G\rho_0)}$, giving the title relation. Thus a full-wavelength [sound-crossing time](sound-crossing-time.md) and this exact free-fall convention agree in scaling, not numerical value. Using the scale $k_J^{-1}$ instead gives $1/(c_sk_J)=1/\sqrt{4\pi G\rho_0}$, the inverse long-wave [static Jeans growth-rate spectrum](static-jeans-growth-rate-spectrum.md) limit. At the threshold itself pressure balances gravity and the net growth rate vanishes; the pressureless free-fall time must not be confused with that marginal mode's infinite growth time.
