# Invariance of a bilinear form on a Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra](invariant-bilinear-form-on-a-lie-algebra.md)

For a symmetric [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) on a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), invariance is the displayed identity. It equivalently says that each [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) endomorphism is skew-adjoint with respect to $B$. The [Killing form](killing-form.md) has this property by cyclic invariance of the [trace](matrix-trace.md).
