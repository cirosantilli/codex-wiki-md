# Hilbertian field

↑ **Parent:** [Field](field.md)

A [field](field.md) $K$ is Hilbertian if each finite family of [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) irreducible over $K(T)$ and separable in its root variable remains irreducible of the same degree at infinitely many $T=a\in K$, after any finitely many prescribed bad parameter values are excluded. Equivalently, every finite separable extension of $K(T)$ has infinitely many unramified $K$-rational places whose residue extension retains its full degree.
