# Metric realization of a simplicial complex

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric realization of a simplicial complex](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md)

Represent a point by finitely supported [barycentric coordinates](barycentric-coordinate-system.md) $\lambda_v\geq0$ with $\sum_v\lambda_v=1$ and support a [simplex](simplex.md). Restricting the displayed [Euclidean metric](euclidean-metric.md) on these coordinates gives a metric realization. The coordinate-sum is finite because both points have finite support. For a finite [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md), this agrees with the [weak topology of a simplicial realization](weak-topology-of-a-simplicial-realization.md): the coordinate map from the finite quotient realization is a continuous [bijection](bijection.md) from a [compact space](compact-space.md) to a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md), hence a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md). For an infinite complex the two topologies can differ, as the countable-star examples demonstrate.
