# Trinomial tree

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)

A trinomial tree allows three next-step states. For the [explicit log-price scheme for local-volatility pricing](explicit-log-price-scheme-for-local-volatility-pricing.md) these are $x-h,x,x+h$, with the displayed weights. They sum to one; if nonnegative they are transition probabilities and make backward pricing a conditional average. Their first two increment moments are $bk$ and $2ak$, matching the diffusion to first order in the time step. Coefficients can depend on the node, so the tree need not have constant transition probabilities.
