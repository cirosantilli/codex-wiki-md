# Molar solubility

↑ **Parent:** [Solubility product](solubility-product.md)

The [molar solubility](molar-solubility.md) $s$ is the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of a specified [solid](solid.md) dissolved per [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) of saturated [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), under stated conditions. It counts dissolved formula units, not the sum of all dissolved [ion](ion.md) [concentrations](concentration.md). For AB with no initial A or B and no competing [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md), one-to-one [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) gives $c_{\mathrm A}=c_{\mathrm B}=s$. For an AB$_2$ [solid](solid.md), the corresponding [concentrations](concentration.md) are $s$ and $2s$.[Chemistry 2e by Paul Flowers and others; §15.1, Ksp and Solubility: dissolution coefficients relate molar solubility to individual ion concentrations](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/15-1-precipitation-and-dissolution.md)
