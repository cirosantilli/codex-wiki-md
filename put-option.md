# Put option

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent claim](contingent-claim.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Put_option)

A put option gives its holder the right to sell an underlying asset at a specified strike price. A [European put option](european-put-option.md) may be exercised only at maturity, whereas an [American put option](american-put-option.md) permits earlier exercise.
