# Scalar potential

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field](scalar-field.md)

A scalar potential is the derivative-free energy density $V(\phi)$ of a [scalar field](scalar-field.md). Its derivative supplies the force term in the field equation, and its local minima describe candidate vacuum field values.
