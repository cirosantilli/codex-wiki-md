# Semisimplicity from the regular module

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple algebra](semisimple-algebra.md)

If the [left regular module](left-regular-module.md) of a unital [ring](ring.md) is semisimple, then every left module is semisimple. Every module is a quotient of a direct sum of copies of the regular module, hence a quotient of a sum of [simple modules](irreducible-module.md). Their simple or zero images span the quotient. A maximal independent direct sum of simple submodules must span: otherwise one of these simple images has zero intersection with it and can be added. Thus the quotient is a direct sum of simples. For an infinite sum the maximality argument uses [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md).
