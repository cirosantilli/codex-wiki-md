# Landau-Yang theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Photon](photon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau–Yang_theorem)

A massive [spin](spin.md)-one state cannot decay into two identical on-shell [photons](photon.md). In their [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md), opposite [photon helicities](photon-helicity.md) have helicity difference two and require $J\geq2$. Equal helicities have difference zero; exchanging the two identical [bosons](boson.md) multiplies their total-[angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) wave by $(-1)^J$, so [Bose-Einstein statistics](bose-einstein-statistics.md) requires even $J$. Neither case permits $J=1$. The on-shell and identical-particle assumptions are essential; the theorem does not exclude a decay with one virtual [photon](photon.md).
