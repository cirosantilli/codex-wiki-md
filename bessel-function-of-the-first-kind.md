# Bessel function of the first kind

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel function](bessel-function.md)

The Bessel function of the first kind $J_\nu$ is the solution of the [Bessel differential equation](bessel-differential-equation.md) selected by its regular power-series behavior at the origin. For $\operatorname{Re}\nu>-1/2$, it has the Poisson integral representation

$$
J_\nu(z)=\frac{(z/2)^\nu}{\Gamma(\nu+1/2)\sqrt\pi}
\int_{-1}^{1}e^{izt}(1-t^2)^{\nu-1/2}\,dt.
$$
