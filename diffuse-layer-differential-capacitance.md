# Diffuse-layer differential capacitance

↑ **Parent:** [Grahame equation](grahame-equation.md)

Differentiate the [Grahame equation](grahame-equation.md) for a planar symmetric monovalent electrolyte with respect to surface potential at fixed reservoir [concentration](concentration.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md). The result is the displayed [capacitance](capacitance.md) per unit [area](surface-area.md). It approaches $\varepsilon\kappa_D$ in the [Debye–Hückel approximation](debye-huckel-approximation.md). A compact surface layer can contribute a separate series [capacitance](capacitance.md); point-ion packing assumptions also fail at sufficiently high near-surface [concentration](concentration.md).[section VI, equations (43)–(48), printed p. 474](the-electrical-double-layer-and-the-theory-of-electrocapillarity-by-david-c-grahame.md)
