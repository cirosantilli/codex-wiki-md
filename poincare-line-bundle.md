<h1 id="poincare-line-bundle">Poincaré line bundle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dual abelian variety](dual-abelian-variety.md)

The normalized universal [line bundle](line-bundle.md) restricts to the class $\xi$ on $A\times\{\xi\}$ and is trivialized on both zero sections. For a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $L$ on an [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md), its [homomorphism associated to a line bundle on an abelian variety](homomorphism-associated-to-a-line-bundle-on-an-abelian-variety.md) satisfies

$$
(\operatorname{id},\phi_L)^*P_A\cong m^*L\otimes p_1^*L^{-1}\otimes p_2^*L^{-1}
$$

as [Picard group](picard-group.md) classes. A choice of trivialization of $L_0$ normalizes the right side; uniqueness then follows from the [Seesaw theorem](seesaw-theorem.md).
