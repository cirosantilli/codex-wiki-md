# Input-output

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science](computer-science-split.md)

Transfers of data between a computer and its devices or external environment. An [operating-system process](process-computing.md) can need to wait for an [input-output](input-output.md) operation to complete, leaving the [CPU](central-processing-unit.md) available for other runnable work. [Polled input-output](polled-input-output.md), [interrupt-driven input-output](interrupt-driven-input-output.md) and [DMA](direct-memory-access.md) describe different aspects of arranging and observing such transfers.
