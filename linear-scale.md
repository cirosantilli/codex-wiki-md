# Linear scale

↑ **Parent:** [Number line](number-line.md)

A [linear scale](linear-scale.md) places a value $x$ at a position $u=ax+c$, where $a>0$ and $c$ are [constants](constant-mathematics.md). Equal differences in $x$ occupy equal distances because $u_2-u_1=a(x_2-x_1)$. This contrasts with a [logarithmic scale](logarithmic-scale.md), which assigns equal distances to equal [ratios](ratio.md).
