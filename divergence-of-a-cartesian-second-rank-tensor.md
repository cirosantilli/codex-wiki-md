# Divergence of a Cartesian second-rank tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian second-rank tensor](cartesian-second-rank-tensor.md)

For a constant orthogonal Cartesian coordinate change $x'=Rx$, a [Cartesian second-rank tensor](cartesian-second-rank-tensor.md) obeys $T'_{ij}=R_{ik}R_{jl}T_{kl}$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $\partial'_j=R_{jm}\partial_m$. Consequently $\partial'_jT'_{ij}=R_{ik}R_{jl}R_{jm}\partial_mT_{kl}=R_{ik}\partial_lT_{kl}$, the transformation rule for a [vector](vector.md). Under non-Cartesian position-dependent coordinate changes, partial derivatives must be replaced by the appropriate [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md).
