# Discrete summation by parts

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference method](finite-difference-method.md)

On a periodic uniform grid, shifting an index in the discrete [inner product](inner-product.md) gives the displayed skew-adjoint identity for the centered first difference. It also gives $\langle U,D_{xx}V\rangle_h=-\langle D_+U,D_+V\rangle_h$. With finite boundaries there are additional boundary terms unless the prescribed endpoint traces eliminate them. These identities are discrete counterparts of [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) and prove energy estimates for [finite difference methods](finite-difference-method.md).
