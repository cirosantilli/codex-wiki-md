# Muon

↑ **Parent:** [Lepton](lepton.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muon)

The muon is the negatively charged second-generation [lepton](lepton.md), with [electric charge](electric-charge.md) $-1$ and [lepton number](lepton-number.md) $+1$. Its [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) is the [antimuon](antimuon.md).
