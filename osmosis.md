# Osmosis

↑ **Parent:** [Passive transport](passive-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmosis)

[Osmosis](osmosis.md) is net movement of [water](water.md) across a boundary whose permeability differs for [water](water.md) and relevant [solutes](solute.md). The direction depends on the [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) of [water](water.md), including both dissolved material and [pressure](pressure.md). A [cell wall](cell-wall.md) can let [water](water.md) reach the [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md), yet oppose expansion through [turgor pressure](turgor-pressure.md). Zero net movement does not mean individual [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule.md) have stopped crossing.
