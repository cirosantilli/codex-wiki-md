# Improper rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md)

An orientation-reversing [orthogonal transformation](orthogonal-transformation.md). In three dimensions it is a rotation about an axis followed by a [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) perpendicular to that axis. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $-1,e^{i\theta},e^{-i\theta}$, giving $\operatorname{tr}R=-1+2\cos\theta$. A plane [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) is the case $\theta=0$, while inversion through the origin is the case $\theta=\pi$.
