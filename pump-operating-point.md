# Pump operating point

↑ **Parent:** [Centrifugal pump](centrifugal-pump.md)

The flow and head at which a pump's characteristic curve intersects the hydraulic system's required-head curve. Increasing system resistance at unchanged speed normally reduces flow on a conventional falling pump curve.[Module HT-03, pp.51–53, System Operating Point](thermodynamics-heat-transfer-and-fluid-flow-volume-3-by-the-united-states-department-of-energy.md)
