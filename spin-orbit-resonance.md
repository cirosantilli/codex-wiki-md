# Spin-orbit resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal locking](tidal-locking.md)

A prograde [spin-orbit resonance](spin-orbit-resonance.md) relates mean spin [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) to [mean motion](mean-motion.md) by a ratio of small positive [integers](integer.md), $\langle\omega\rangle/n=p/q$: the body makes $p$ turns in $q$ [orbital periods](orbital-period.md). [Synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md) is $1:1$; [Mercury](mercury-planet.md)'s $3:2$ [spin-orbit resonance](spin-orbit-resonance.md) is nonsynchronous. The relevant combination of spin and orbital angles must stay bounded around a preferred orientation, not merely have a near-integer frequency ratio. For an elongated body on an eccentric orbit, the companion's gravitational [torque](torque.md) on the permanent shape can sustain the resonance while [tidal dissipation](tidal-dissipation.md) permits capture. [Rodríguez et al. (2012): spin-orbit coupling](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1209.1580).
