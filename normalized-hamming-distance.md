# Normalized Hamming distance

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md)

On binary words of length $n$, [normalized Hamming distance](normalized-hamming-distance.md) is the fraction of differing coordinates. Unlike unscaled [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md), it makes the [Hamming cubes](hamming-cube.md) a [Lévy family](levy-family-of-graphs.md) under uniform [probability measures](probability-measure.md).
