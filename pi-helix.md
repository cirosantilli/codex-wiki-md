# Pi helix

↑ **Parent:** [Protein secondary structure](protein-secondary-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi_helix)

A [pi helix](pi-helix.md) contains carbonyl-i to NH-(i+5) hydrogen bonds, sixteen-membered hydrogen-bonded rings, and approximately 4.4 residues per turn.
