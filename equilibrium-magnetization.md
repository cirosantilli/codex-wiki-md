# Equilibrium magnetization

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)

The equilibrium magnetization is a value of the [magnetization](magnetization.md) that minimizes the [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md). A stable equilibrium has nonnegative second derivative with respect to the magnetization; degenerate nonzero minima describe symmetry-related magnetized phases.
