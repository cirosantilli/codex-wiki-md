# Species

↑ **Parent:** [Taxonomy (biology)](taxonomy-biology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Species)

A [species](species.md) is a named unit of biological classification. The useful criteria vary among groups: reproductive compatibility can inform classification in many [animals](animal.md), while microbial comparisons also use [genomes](genome.md) and other characters. [Escherichia coli](escherichia-coli.md) is a [species](species.md); K-12 MG1655 identifies a particular strain within it. See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 18.2: species concepts](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/18-2-formation-of-new-species).
