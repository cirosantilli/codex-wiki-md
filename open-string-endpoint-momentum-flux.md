# Open-string endpoint momentum flux

↑ **Parent:** [Open string](open-string.md)

For the [Nambu-Goto phase-space action](nambu-goto-phase-space-action.md), $\dot P_m=\partial_\sigma J_m$ with $J_m=eT^2X'_m+uP_m$. Thus $\dot{\mathcal P}_m=J_m(\pi)-J_m(0)$. Free endpoint variations force $J_m=0$ individually. A [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) allows an external support or [D-brane](d-brane.md) to absorb momentum in the fixed direction.
