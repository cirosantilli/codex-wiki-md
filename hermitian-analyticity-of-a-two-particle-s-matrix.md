# Hermitian analyticity of a two-particle S-matrix

↑ **Parent:** [S-matrix](s-matrix.md)

In a real species basis, the two-particle [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) satisfies $S(-\theta^*)=S(\theta)^\dagger$ under the usual scattering analyticity assumptions. On the real [rapidity](rapidity.md) axis this relates inverse-rapidity amplitudes to [complex conjugates](complex-conjugate.md). Combined with the algebraic inverse relation $S(\theta)S(-\theta)=I$, it gives physical [unitarity](unitary-operator.md).
