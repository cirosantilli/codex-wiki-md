# Residue characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Residue field](residue-field.md)

The residue characteristic of a local ring is the [characteristic of a ring](characteristic-of-a-ring.md) of its [residue field](residue-field.md). A finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$ has residue characteristic $p$.
