# Two-generator Frobenius theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius number](frobenius-number.md)

For [coprime](coprime-integers.md) [integers](integer-split.md) $a,b>1$, the [Frobenius number](frobenius-number.md) is $ab-a-b$. Every larger [integer](integer-split.md) is a nonnegative [integer](integer-split.md) [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of $a,b$.
