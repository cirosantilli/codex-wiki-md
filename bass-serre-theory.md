# Bass-Serre theory

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass–Serre_theory)

Bass-Serre theory describes group splittings as actions on trees and reconstructs a graph of groups from the stabilizers of such an action.
