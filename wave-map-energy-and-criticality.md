# Wave map energy and criticality

↑ **Parent:** [Wave map energy](wave-map-energy.md)

Under $\phi_\lambda(t,x)=\phi(t/\lambda,x/\lambda)$, the conserved [wave map energy](wave-map-energy.md) scales as $\lambda^{n-2}E$. Energy is subcritical for $n=1$, critical for $n=2$, and supercritical for $n\geq3$. The critical derivative index of the [homogeneous Sobolev space](homogeneous-sobolev-space.md) is $s_c=n/2$. Small energy alone is not an appropriate general small-data regularity hypothesis in supercritical dimensions.
