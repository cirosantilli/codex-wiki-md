# Dimension vector of a quiver representation

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a quiver](representation-of-a-quiver.md)

The dimension vector of a finite-dimensional [quiver representation](representation-of-a-quiver.md) is $(\dim_kX_i)_{i\in Q_0}$. [Direct sums](direct-sum.md) add dimension vectors. Fixing the vector gives an [affine space](affine-space.md) of possible arrow matrices, the [quiver representation space](quiver-representation-space.md).
