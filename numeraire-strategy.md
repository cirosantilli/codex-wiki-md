<h1 id="numeraire-strategy">Numéraire strategy</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Numéraire portfolio](numeraire-portfolio.md)

A numéraire strategy is a [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) with zero consumption whose wealth is strictly positive at every date. Its wealth process can carry consumption received at an earlier date forward to a later date.
