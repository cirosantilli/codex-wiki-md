# Thermal fluctuation

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

A thermal fluctuation is the randomness of an observable in an equilibrium [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md). If the energy contains $-hA$, differentiation of its [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) gives $\partial_h\langle A\rangle=\beta(\langle A^2\rangle-\langle A\rangle^2)$. Thus the fluctuation variance is related to the response to a conjugate source. In a field theory, the two-point version is the [connected correlation function](connected-correlation-function.md).
