<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The wall position is $x_w(t)=\operatorname{Re}(ce^{-i\omega t})$, so its [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is $\operatorname{Re}(-i\omega ce^{-i\omega t})$. The [kinematic boundary condition](../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition.md) equates the normal fluid [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) to the normal wall [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md). Linearizing the evaluation point to the mean wall position gives

$$
\boxed{\phi_x(0,z,t)=\operatorname{Re}(-i\omega ce^{-i\omega t}),\qquad f_x(0,z)=-i\omega c}.
$$

The other wall and bottom retain $f_x(L,z)=0$ and $f_z(x,-h)=0$, while the free surface gives $f_z(x,0)=(\omega^2/g)f(x,0)$.
