<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply [separation of variables](../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) to [Laplace's equation](../../../../../laplace-equation.md). The side-wall [Neumann boundary conditions](../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md) give horizontal factors $\cos(k_nx)$ with $k_n=n\pi/L$. The bottom condition fixes the vertical factor to $\cosh(k_n(z+h))$. Substitution into the surface condition in [i](../i.md) gives the [normal modes of surface gravity waves in a rectangular tank](../../../../../normal-modes-of-surface-gravity-waves-in-a-rectangular-tank.md):

$$
\boxed{\phi_n=\cos(k_nx)\cosh(k_n(z+h))\,[A_n\cos(\omega_nt)+B_n\sin(\omega_nt)],\qquad\omega_n^2=gk_n\tanh(k_nh),\quad n\geq1}.
$$

The corresponding [surface elevation](../../../../../surface-elevation.md) follows from $\eta_n=-\partial_t\phi_n(x,0,t)/g$. The spatially uniform $n=0$ mode has zero frequency; its constant elevation changes total fluid volume, so it is absent when that volume is fixed. An additive constant in $\phi$ changes no fluid [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md).
