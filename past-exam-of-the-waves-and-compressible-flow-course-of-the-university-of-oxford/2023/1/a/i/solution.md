<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) gives horizontal and vertical [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) components $(\phi_x,\phi_z)$. The [kinematic boundary condition](../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition.md) at each fixed impermeable wall is zero normal fluid velocity, hence

$$
\boxed{\phi_x=0\text{ at }x=0,L,\qquad\phi_z=0\text{ at }z=-h}.
$$

Let $\eta(x,t)$ be the [surface elevation](../../../../../surface-elevation.md). To first order in its [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md), the [kinematic boundary condition for a free-surface graph](../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition-for-a-free-surface-graph.md) gives $\eta_t=\phi_z$ at $z=0$. The [dynamic boundary condition for an inviscid interface](../../../../../dynamic-boundary-condition-for-an-inviscid-interface.md) fixes the surface [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) to atmospheric pressure. The unsteady [Bernoulli equation](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md), after choosing the time-dependent gauge of $\phi$, then gives $\phi_t+g\eta=0$ at $z=0$: the quadratic velocity term is second order in [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md). Differentiate this last equation in time and eliminate $\eta_t$ to obtain the [linearized free-surface boundary conditions](../../../../../linearized-free-surface-boundary-conditions.md) in combined form,

$$
\boxed{\phi_{tt}+g\phi_z=0\qquad(z=0)}.
$$
