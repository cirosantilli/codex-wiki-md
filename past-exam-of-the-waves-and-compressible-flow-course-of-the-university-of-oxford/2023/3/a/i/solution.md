<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take a unit-width [control volume](../../../../../control-volume.md) spanning the stationary [hydraulic jump](../../../../../hydraulic-jump.md). [Mass conservation](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) makes the discharge $q=hu$ constant. The horizontal [momentum flux](../../../../../momentum-flux.md) is $\rho h u^2$, and the integrated [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) force on a vertical cross-section is $\int_0^h\rho g(h-z)dz=\rho gh^2/2$. [Momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{[hu]_-^+=[hu^2+gh^2/2]_-^+=0}.
$$

These are the [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) for the [shallow water equations](../../../../../shallow-water-equations.md) in the stationary [shock frame](../../../../../shock-frame.md). With depths $h_1\ne h_2$ on the two sides, eliminating the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) gives

$$
q^2=\frac g2h_1h_2(h_1+h_2).
$$
