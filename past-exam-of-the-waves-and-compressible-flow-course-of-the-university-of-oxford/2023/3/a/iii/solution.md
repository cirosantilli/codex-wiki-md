<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $R=h_2/h_1>1$. The squared [Froude numbers](../../../../../froude-number.md) follow directly from the discharge relation:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Fr}_1^2=\frac{u_1^2}{gh_1}=\frac{R(1+R)}2>1,\qquad \mathrm{Fr}_2^2=\frac{u_2^2}{gh_2}=\frac{1+R}{2R^2}<1}.
$$

Thus the jump connects [supercritical flow](../../../../../supercritical-flow.md) to [subcritical flow](../../../../../subcritical-flow.md). Long [surface gravity waves](../../../../../surface-gravity-wave.md) have speed $\sqrt{gh}$ relative to the fluid, so the [characteristic speeds](../../../../../characteristic-speed.md) in the stationary frame are $u\pm\sqrt{gh}$. Upstream both point with the flow; downstream one points against it. The jump changes whether long-wave information can propagate upstream through the local flow.
