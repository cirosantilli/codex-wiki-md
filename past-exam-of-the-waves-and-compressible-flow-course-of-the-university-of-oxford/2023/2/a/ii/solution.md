<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $w=e^{\alpha z/2}W$, direct [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) gives

$$
w_{zz}-\alpha w_z=e^{\alpha z/2}\left(W_{zz}-\frac{\alpha^2}{4}W\right),\qquad w_{xx}=e^{\alpha z/2}W_{xx}.
$$

Thus the transformed [partial differential equation](../../../../../partial-differential-equation-split.md) and its impermeable-wall [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) are

$$
\boxed{\partial_t^2\left(\nabla^2W-\frac{\alpha^2}{4}W\right)+\alpha g W_{xx}=0,\qquad W(x,0,t)=W(x,h,t)=0}.
$$

The positive exponential factor does not change where the vertical [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) vanishes.
