<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write the cosine in [i](../i.md) as two complex exponentials. On a ray $x=vt$, the oscillatory phases are $kv\pm\omega(k)$. The [stationary phase method](../../../../../stationary-phase-method.md) requires $v=\mp\omega'(k)$. By [ii](../../b/ii.md), the largest absolute horizontal [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) for the occupied $n=1$ mode is

$$
\boxed{\frac{|x|}{t}\leq\frac{N}{b_1}=\frac{2h\sqrt{\alpha g}}{\sqrt{4\pi^2+\alpha^2h^2}}}.
$$

Inside this region there are stationary wavenumbers supporting appreciable [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) contributions. Outside it no stationary wavenumber exists; oscillatory cancellation suppresses the large-time response. This explains "mainly confined": the initial Lorentzian profile already has nonzero algebraic tails, so the displayed region is not the exact support of the solution. Near its boundary the stationary points meet $k=0$ and the usual nondegenerate stationary-phase formula needs a transition approximation.
