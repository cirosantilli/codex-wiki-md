<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose the rightgoing signed [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega(k)=Nk/\sqrt{k^2+b_n^2}$. The horizontal [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) and [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) are

$$
\boxed{c_p=\frac\omega k=\frac{N}{\sqrt{k^2+b_n^2}},\qquad c_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk}=\frac{Nb_n^2}{(k^2+b_n^2)^{3/2}}}.
$$

The leftgoing branch has their negatives. For a convention using positive frequency at both signs of $k$, multiply each displayed speed by $\operatorname{sgn}k$. For every nonzero $k$, $0<c_g<c_p$ on the rightgoing branch; the [horizontal group-speed bound for a confined internal wave](../../../../../horizontal-group-speed-bound-for-a-confined-internal-wave.md) is $c_g\leq N/b_n$, approached as $k\to0$.
