# Entropy mode

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic wave](magnetohydrodynamic-wave.md)

An [entropy mode](entropy-mode.md) is a perturbation of [entropy](entropy.md) and [density](density.md) with no leading [pressure](pressure.md) or [velocity](velocity.md) perturbation in a uniform ideal fluid. For an [adiabatic equation of state](adiabatic-equation-of-state.md) define $s=\delta p/p_0-\gamma\delta\rho/\rho_0$. An [entropy](entropy.md) perturbation can have $\delta p=0$ and $\delta\rho/\rho_0=-s/\gamma$, so it has zero [frequency](frequency.md) in the local fluid frame. In anisotropic [reduced magnetohydrodynamics](reduced-magnetohydrodynamics.md), $Ds=0$ with $D=\partial_t+\mathbf u_\perp\cdot\nabla_\perp$, and [turbulence](turbulence-split.md) mixes its [variance](variance-split.md) as a [passive scalar](passive-scalar.md).
