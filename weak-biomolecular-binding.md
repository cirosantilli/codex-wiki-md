# Weak biomolecular binding

↑ **Parent:** [Protein-protein interaction](protein-protein-interaction.md)

Reversible biomolecular association with a relatively high [dissociation constant](dissociation-constant.md) under specified conditions. The same equilibrium constant can result from different association and dissociation rates, so weak binding does not alone establish a short lifetime.
