# Fast-slow matching for a calcium pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium wave](calcium-wave.md)

In a two-variable calcium-wave model, the fast front scale can reduce to the displayed [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md). Its equilibria are intersections of the kinetic [nullcline](nullcline.md) $V=g(U)$ and a line of slope $-1/\gamma$. At a simple intersection with $f_V<0$, the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $f_V(1+\gamma g')$; a negative determinant identifies a [saddle point](saddle-point.md). Matching a rising front requires a [heteroclinic orbit](heteroclinic-orbit.md) from one saddle to another, selected by a [shooting method](shooting-method.md) in the speed $s$. A falling connection must use the same speed. Between fronts the slow equation is $s(1+\gamma g')U'=\mu-U$. Recovery on the two outer rising branches requires the resting value $\mu$ to lie between the low landing value and the high takeoff value. The nullcline shape supplies this geometry but does not by itself prove existence of both connections.
