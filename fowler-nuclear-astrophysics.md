# Fowler nuclear astrophysics

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md)

[William Alfred Fowler](william-alfred-fowler.md)'s 1983 lecture connects laboratory [nuclear reaction cross-sections](nuclear-reaction-cross-section.md) to [stellar nucleosynthesis](stellar-nucleosynthesis.md) and credits [Fred Hoyle](fred-hoyle.md)'s stellar synthesis framework.[II, pp. 179–180; III, pp. 181–183](experimental-and-theoretical-nuclear-astrophysics-the-quest-for-the-origin-of-the-elements-by-william-alfred-fowler.md) This complements [Bethe stellar energy-production theory](bethe-stellar-energy-production-theory.md): a fuel reservoir alone cannot predict surviving [stellar chemical abundances](stellar-chemical-abundance.md).

Transient [beryllium-8](beryllium-8.md) must capture an [alpha particle](alpha-particle.md) before dissociation; subsequent electromagnetic decay of the [Hoyle state](hoyle-state.md) can stabilize [carbon-12](carbon-12.md) despite dominant breakup.
