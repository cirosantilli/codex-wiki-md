# Conduction band

↑ **Parent:** [Electron hole](electron-hole.md)

The initially empty [energy band](energy-band.md) above the [band gap](band-gap.md) in the simple [semiconductor](semiconductor.md) description. Excited [Electrons](electron.md) in it can carry [electric current](electric-current.md); they can also bind to [valence band](valence-band.md) [electron holes](electron-hole.md) as [excitons](exciton.md).
