# Osgood uniqueness criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

If a continuous nondecreasing modulus $\mu$ satisfies this divergence condition, an estimate $|b(t,x)-b(t,y)|\leq A(t)\mu(|x-y|)$ with locally integrable $A$ gives uniqueness of [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md). Apply a regularized integral comparison to their separation. For the [log-Lipschitz modulus](log-lipschitz-modulus.md), $\delta'(t)\leq A(t)\delta(1-\log\delta)$ gives $\delta(t)\leq\exp(1-(1-\log\delta(0))\exp(-\int_0^tA))$ while $\delta\leq1$. Zero initial separation stays zero. Unlike the usual linear [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md), this criterion allows non-Lipschitz vector fields.
