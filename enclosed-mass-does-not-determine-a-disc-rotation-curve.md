# Enclosed mass does not determine a disc rotation curve

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md)

The [spherical shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md) yields $v_c^2=GM(<r)/r$ only in spherical geometry. A razor-thin axisymmetric disc instead requires $v_c^2=R\partial_R\Phi(R,0)$, including forces from exterior annuli. The [interior gravitational force of an exterior thin ring](interior-gravitational-force-of-an-exterior-thin-ring.md) gives a concrete counterexample with identical enclosed mass but different circular speeds.
