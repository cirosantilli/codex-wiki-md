# Linear coefficient bound for normalized univalent functions

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized univalent function](normalized-univalent-function.md)

For $f(z)=z+\sum_{n\geq2}a_nz^n$ in the [normalized univalent function](normalized-univalent-function.md) class, the [integral mean bound for an odd square-root transform](integral-mean-bound-for-an-odd-square-root-transform.md) gives $(2\pi)^{-1}\int|f(re^{i\theta})|d\theta\leq r/(1-r)$. The [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md) for [Taylor coefficients](taylor-coefficient.md) therefore yields $|a_n|\leq r^{1-n}/(1-r)$. Choosing $r=(n-1)/n$ and applying the [logarithm tangent bound](logarithm-tangent-bound.md) gives the displayed bound for every $n\geq2$.
