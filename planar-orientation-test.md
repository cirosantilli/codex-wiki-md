# Planar orientation test

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional cross product](two-dimensional-cross-product.md)

For planar points $A,B,P$, the sign of $(B-A)\times(P-A)$, using the [two-dimensional cross product](two-dimensional-cross-product.md), determines which side of the directed line from $A$ to $B$ contains $P$. Zero means collinearity. A point is strictly inside a nondegenerate [triangle](triangle.md) exactly when these signs for its three cyclically ordered sides are all strictly positive or all strictly negative.
