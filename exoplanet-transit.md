# Exoplanet transit

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit photometry](exoplanet-transit-photometry.md)

An [exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md) passes in front of its host [star](star.md) as seen by an observer and blocks part of the stellar flux. [Exoplanet transit photometry](exoplanet-transit-photometry.md) infers geometry and radii from the resulting light curve. A true alignment involves the [true anomaly](true-anomaly.md), not generally the [mean anomaly](mean-anomaly.md).
