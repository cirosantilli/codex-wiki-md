# Heuristic optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

[Heuristic optimization](heuristic-optimization.md) seeks useful feasible solutions without necessarily providing a universal [approximation ratio](approximation-ratio.md) or a polynomial worst-case guarantee. Examples are [local search](local-search.md), [simulated annealing](simulated-annealing.md) and [tabu search](tabu-search.md). A claimed [approximation algorithm](approximation-algorithm.md) needs a separate proved performance guarantee.
