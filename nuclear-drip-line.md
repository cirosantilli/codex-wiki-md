# Nuclear drip line

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear separation energy](nuclear-separation-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_drip_line)

A [nuclear drip line](nuclear-drip-line.md) marks a boundary beyond which one- or few-nucleon emission becomes energetically allowed. One- and two-particle [nuclear separation energies](nuclear-separation-energy.md) can give distinct boundaries because of [nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md).
