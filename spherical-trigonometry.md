# Spherical trigonometry

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical geometry](spherical-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_trigonometry)

[Spherical trigonometry](spherical-trigonometry.md) relates side lengths and angles of [spherical triangles](spherical-triangle.md). Side lengths are measured as central angles, or equivalently arc lengths on the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md).
