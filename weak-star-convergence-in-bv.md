# Weak-star convergence in BV

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

Convergence in the displayed sense combines strong function convergence with weak-star convergence of [derivative](derivative.md) [vector measures](vector-measure.md) against $C_0$ tests. Strong $L^1$ convergence and a uniform variation bound imply the [derivative](derivative.md) convergence by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) and uniform test-function approximation. Conversely the [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md) bounds the [derivative](derivative.md) [measures](measure.md) of a convergent sequence.
