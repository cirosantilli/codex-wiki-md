# Complex amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md)

A [complex number](complex-number.md) can encode both the [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and phase of a harmonic disturbance: $a(x,t)=\operatorname{Re}\{\hat a e^{i(kx-\omega t)}\}$. Its [modulus](modulus.md) is the real oscillation amplitude and its [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) fixes the [wave phase](phase-waves.md).
