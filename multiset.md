# Multiset

↑ **Parent:** [Set](set-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiset)

A multiset records a nonnegative integer multiplicity for each element of a [set](set-split.md). A finite multiset has finite support and finite total multiplicity. Its [cardinality](cardinality.md) is the sum of its multiplicities; its disjoint union with another multiset adds multiplicities. The [hook lengths](hook-length.md) of a [Young diagram](young-diagram.md) form a multiset, since different cells can have equal lengths.
