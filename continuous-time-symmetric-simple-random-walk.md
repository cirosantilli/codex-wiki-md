# Continuous-time symmetric simple random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md)

At unit jump rate, this is $X_t=\sum_{k=1}^{N_t}\xi_k$, where $N$ is a rate-one [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) and the independent marks are uniform signs. Equivalently it is the difference of two independent rate-$1/2$ Poisson processes. It is a [Lévy process](levy-process.md) with characteristic function $\exp\{t(\cos\theta-1)\}$, mean zero, [variance](variance-split.md) $t$, and [Lévy characteristic exponent](characteristic-exponent-of-a-levy-process.md) $1-\cos\theta$ in the negative-exponent convention. A general total jump rate rescales time.
