# Strong-shock polar for a perfect gas

↑ **Parent:** [Shock polar](shock-polar.md)

The normal [velocity](velocity.md) is reduced by $q$ in the strong-shock limit while tangential [velocity](velocity.md) is unchanged. If the upstream speed is $U$ and the shock normal makes angle $\beta$ with its direction, then $u_X=U[1-(1-q)\cos^2\beta]$ and $u_Y=\pm U(1-q)\cos\beta\sin\beta$. Eliminating the angle gives the displayed circle, centered at $\gamma U/(\gamma+1)$ with radius $U/(\gamma+1)$. It is the limiting [shock polar](shock-polar.md) in downstream [velocity](velocity.md) space.
