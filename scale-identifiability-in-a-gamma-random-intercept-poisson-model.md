# Scale identifiability in a gamma random-intercept Poisson model

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma random-intercept Poisson model](gamma-random-intercept-poisson-model.md)

When $a_{ij}=\exp(\beta_0+\beta^{\mathsf T}x_{ij})$, replacing $U_i$ by $cU_i$ and the fixed intercept by $\beta_0-\log c$ leaves every conditional count mean and the integrated data law unchanged. Thus without fixing the random multiplier's mean, the [gamma random-intercept Poisson model](gamma-random-intercept-poisson-model.md) identifies the marginal intercept $\beta_0+\log\tau$ and relative heterogeneity $\theta/\tau^2$, rather than these three parameters separately. Normalizing $\tau=1$ removes this scale ambiguity.
