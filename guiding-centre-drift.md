# Guiding-centre drift

↑ **Parent:** [Gyromotion](gyromotion.md)

For a constant perpendicular force in a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), write the particle velocity as $\mathbf v=\mathbf v_d+\mathbf u$. Choosing $q\mathbf v_d\times\mathbf B=-\mathbf F$ leaves the ordinary [gyromotion](gyromotion.md) equation for $\mathbf u$. The gyroperiod-averaged transverse velocity is therefore the displayed drift. An electric force gives the same drift for both charge signs; a charge-independent applied force gives opposite drifts.
