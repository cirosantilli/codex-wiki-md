# Surface micromachining

↑ **Parent:** [Nanotechnology](nanotechnology.md)

Fabrication of small structures from deposited, patterned surface films. A structural layer can be built on a [sacrificial layer](sacrificial-layer.md) and released by selective removal of that support while retaining anchors. Film thickness sets a vertical dimension; residual stress, access to the sacrificial material and release-induced [stiction](stiction.md) require separate control.
