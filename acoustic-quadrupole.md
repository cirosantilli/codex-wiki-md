# Acoustic quadrupole

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic multipole source](acoustic-multipole-source.md)

An acoustic quadrupole has forcing $\partial_i\partial_jT_{ij}$. The [acoustic compact-source approximation](acoustic-compact-source-approximation.md) reduces its leading far-field density to $n_in_j\ddot S_{ij}/(4\pi c_0^4r)$ in three dimensions, with $S_{ij}=\int T_{ij}d^3y$ evaluated at retarded time.
