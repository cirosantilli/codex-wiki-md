# Selective sweep

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)

A change in linked variation accompanying the spread of a favorable [allele](allele.md). Its genomic signature depends on ancestry, [recombination rate](recombination-rate.md) and whether selection acts on a new or pre-existing variant; a [population bottleneck](population-bottleneck.md) can mimic some features.
