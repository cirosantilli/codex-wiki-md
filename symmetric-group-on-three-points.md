# Symmetric group on three points

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric group](symmetric-group.md)

The [permutation group](permutation-group.md) of a three-element set has six elements: the identity, three [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md), and two three-cycles. A three-cycle and any transposition generate it, with relations $x^3=h^2=e$, $hxh^{-1}=x^{-1}$. Its [alternating group](alternating-group.md) is the normal cyclic subgroup of order three. It is the unique nonabelian isomorphism type of a [group of order six](group-of-order-six.md).
