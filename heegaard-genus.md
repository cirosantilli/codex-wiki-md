# Heegaard genus

↑ **Parent:** [Heegaard splitting](heegaard-splitting.md)

The least genus of a [Heegaard surface](heegaard-surface.md) among all [Heegaard splittings](heegaard-splitting.md) of a closed orientable [three-manifold](3-manifold.md). Having a splitting of genus $g$ only proves $g_H(M)\le g$; stabilization supplies larger-genus splittings of the same manifold.
