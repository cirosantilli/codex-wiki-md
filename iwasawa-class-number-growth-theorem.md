# Iwasawa class-number growth theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa invariants](iwasawa-invariants.md)

For the finite layers of a [Zp-extension](zp-extension.md) of a [number field](number-field.md), the p-primary [ideal class groups](ideal-class-group.md) eventually satisfy the displayed formula with integer invariants $\mu,\lambda\geq0$ and $\nu\in\mathbb Z$. The first two are the [Iwasawa invariants](iwasawa-invariants.md) of the [unramified Iwasawa module](unramified-iwasawa-module.md). The [Iwasawa module structure theorem](iwasawa-module-structure-theorem.md) and control of the finite-layer norm and ramification terms give the result. For a cyclotomic extension of an abelian field, the [Ferrero-Washington theorem](ferrero-washington-theorem.md) gives $\mu=0$. This growth theorem records two invariants; it is weaker than identifying the full [characteristic ideal](characteristic-ideal.md) in the [Iwasawa main conjecture](main-conjecture-of-iwasawa-theory.md).
