# Luminosity-distance ordering of flat dust-vacuum universes

↑ **Parent:** [Luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md)

At fixed positive [redshift](redshift.md), intrinsic [luminosity](luminosity.md) and present [Hubble constant](hubble-constant.md), a flat dust-vacuum model with $0<\Omega_{\Lambda0}<1$ has [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md) between those of an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) and pure positive vacuum. The [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) of the dust-only model is largest at every positive integration [redshift](redshift.md); that of pure vacuum is smallest. Therefore $c(1+z)\int_0^z dz'/H(z')$ is smallest for dust alone and largest for pure vacuum. Received [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) has the reverse ordering. This compares physical distances rather than arbitrarily normalized [comoving coordinates](comoving-coordinate.md).
