# Large-deviation rate of a minimum of independent copies

↑ **Parent:** [Contraction principle for large deviations](contraction-principle-for-large-deviations.md)

For fixed $k$ [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) whose families have the same [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) with [good rate function](good-rate-function.md) $I$, the [product large-deviation principle](product-large-deviation-principle.md) gives the sum of coordinate costs. Under the minimum map, one coordinate must equal $m$ and the others must be at least $m$, yielding the displayed [rate function](rate-function.md). If $I$ is [convex](convex-function.md) with unique zero $\mu$, then $J(m)=I(m)$ for $m\le\mu$ and $J(m)=kI(m)$ for $m\ge\mu$.
