# Baryon octet

↑ **Parent:** [Eightfold way](eightfold-way.md)

The spin-one-half octet of [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) contains the [nucleons](nucleon.md), [Sigma baryons](sigma-baryon.md), [Lambda baryon](lambda-baryon.md), and [Xi baryons](xi-baryon.md). Its [weight diagram](weight-diagram.md) has six peripheral states and two states at $(I_3,Y)=(0,0)$: $\Sigma^0$ and $\Lambda^0$, with different total [isospin](isospin.md).
