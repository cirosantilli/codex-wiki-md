# Inter-multiplet Zeeman coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-field Zeeman splitting](weak-field-zeeman-splitting.md)

The [Zeeman effect](zeeman-splitting.md) need not be diagonal between different $J$ multiplets. The familiar [Landé g-factor](lande-g-factor.md) gives the interaction projected into one isolated multiplet. Inter-multiplet [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) cause higher-order weak-field shifts and become important in the [Paschen-Back effect](paschen-back-effect.md).
