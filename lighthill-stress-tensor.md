# Lighthill stress tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Lighthill acoustic analogy](lighthill-acoustic-analogy.md)

This tensor contains convective momentum flux, departure from the reference linear pressure-density relation, and viscous stress. Its double divergence is an [acoustic quadrupole](acoustic-quadrupole.md) distribution in [Lighthill acoustic analogy](lighthill-acoustic-analogy.md).
