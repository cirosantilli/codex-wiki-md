# Density of states of a monatomic harmonic chain

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon density of states](phonon-density-of-states.md)

For the nearest-neighbour [monatomic-chain dispersion relation](monatomic-chain-dispersion-relation.md), $\omega_m=2\sqrt{\alpha/M}$ and $d\omega/dq=(a/2)\sqrt{\omega_m^2-\omega^2}$ on the positive half-zone. The folded wave-number count gives the displayed [density of states](density-of-states.md), including both propagation directions. Its upper-edge [Van Hove singularity](van-hove-singularity.md) is integrable: $\int_0^{\omega_m}g(\omega)d\omega=N$. A finite chain has discrete modes; the continuous density describes its large-size limit.
