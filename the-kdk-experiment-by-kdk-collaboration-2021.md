# The KDK experiment by KDK collaboration (2021)

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

TIPP 2021 presentation: [https://indico.cern.ch/event/981823/contributions/4295324/attachments/2250469/3818551/KDK_TIPP_2021_pub.pdf](https://indico.cern.ch/event/981823/contributions/4295324/attachments/2250469/3818551/KDK_TIPP_2021_pub.pdf)

Its [potassium-40](potassium-40.md) decay scheme gives the approximate atomic mass-energy differences of $1.311$ MeV to [calcium-40](calcium-40.md) and $1.505$ MeV to [argon-40](argon-40.md), and distinguishes capture to an excited argon level from the rare positron branch. The positron kinetic-energy endpoint additionally subtracts $2m_e$ from the atomic mass difference.
