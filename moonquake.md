# Moonquake

↑ **Parent:** [Apollo lunar seismology](apollo-lunar-seismology.md)

A seismic event originating within the [Moon](moon.md). Its arrival times and waveforms constrain lunar structure when recorded by a suitable [seismic network](seismic-network.md). Lunar impacts are another useful seismic source but have a different origin; their known locations can help calibrate an otherwise sparse network.
