# Fourier nonexpansion for Maxwell molecules

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier distance of order two](fourier-distance-of-order-two.md)

Unit-mass solutions of the isotropic [Maxwell molecule collision operator](maxwell-molecule-collision-operator.md) with matching first moments obey this comparison. The [Bobylev identity](bobylev-identity.md), the Fourier bound by one, and $|\xi^+|^2+|\xi^-|^2=|\xi|^2$ give a scalar damped differential inequality. Its [integral](integral.md) form and the [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) yield nonexpansion in the [Fourier distance of order two](fourier-distance-of-order-two.md). This does not alone establish strict contraction or equilibrium convergence.
