# Dependence of Lefschetz maps on the Dolbeault class

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md)

Wedge multiplication by a closed $(1,1)$ form induces maps on [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md). If $\omega'-\omega=\bar\partial\theta$, then $(\omega')^k-\omega^k=\bar\partial(\theta\wedge\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}(\omega')^j\wedge\omega^{k-1-j})$. Wedging with a closed representative shows that the maps agree. For a change of [Kähler metric potential](kahler-potential-complex-geometry.md), take $\theta=-i\partial f$.
