# 22G RNA

↑ **Parent:** [Small interfering RNA](small-interfering-rna.md)

A class of [Caenorhabditis elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md) secondary small [RNAs](ribonucleic-acid.md) with a characteristic 5′ guanine and approximately 22-nucleotide length, generated by [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md)-dependent [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) polymerases.
