# Joukowski circle-to-ellipse mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Joukowski map](joukowski-map.md)

For $\rho>0$, substitution in the [Joukowski map](joukowski-map.md) gives the title parametrization. If $\rho\ne1$, its image is an [ellipse](ellipse.md) with semiaxes $\rho+\rho^{-1}$ and $|\rho-\rho^{-1}|$ and [foci](focus-dynamical-systems.md) $\pm2$. Reciprocal radii give the same [ellipse](ellipse.md) in opposite orientations. At $\rho=1$ the image degenerates to $[-2,2]$, each interior point covered once by each unit [semicircle](semicircle.md).
