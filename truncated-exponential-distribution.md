# Truncated exponential distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Truncated distribution](truncated-distribution.md)

Condition an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of rate $d>0$ on falling below $t>0$. Normalizing its [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) gives the displayed density. Integration gives [expectation](expected-value.md) $1/d-t/(e^{dt}-1)$. As $d\downarrow0$, the density tends to the [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(0,t)$ and the [expectation](expected-value.md) tends to $t/2$.
