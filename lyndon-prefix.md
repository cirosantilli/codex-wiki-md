# Lyndon prefix

↑ **Parent:** [Lyndon word](lyndon-word.md)

A prefix of a word that is itself a [Lyndon word](lyndon-word.md). The longest Lyndon prefix is useful when ranking necklaces and substrings of a [De Bruijn sequence](de-bruijn-sequence.md).
