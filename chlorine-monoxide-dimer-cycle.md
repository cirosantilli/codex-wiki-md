# Chlorine monoxide dimer cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

A low-temperature catalytic [ozone](ozone.md) destruction pathway involving ClO association to ClOOCl, [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) of the dimer and regeneration of [chlorine](chlorine.md) atoms. Combined with two Cl + O3 reactions its net effect is $2\mathrm O_3\to3\mathrm O_2$. Low [temperature](temperature-split.md) suppresses thermal dimer dissociation, while sunlight is needed for the photolytic step. [Polar stratospheric clouds](polar-stratospheric-cloud.md) can activate reservoir [chlorine](chlorine.md) before the sunlit cycle becomes rapid.
