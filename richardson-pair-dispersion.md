# Richardson pair dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent mixing](turbulent-mixing.md)

Richardson pair dispersion concerns relative separation of two marked [fluid elements](fluid-element.md), rather than their centre-of-mass motion. In an [inertial range](inertial-range.md), scale-local relative diffusivity is of order $\epsilon^{1/3}r^{4/3}$. A self-similar closure gives $d\langle r^2\rangle/dt=A\epsilon^{1/3}\langle r^2\rangle^{2/3}$ and hence a $t^3$ regime after loss of memory of initial separation. Stationarity and inertial-range separation alone do not imply this law at arbitrarily short times.
