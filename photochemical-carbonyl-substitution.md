# Photochemical carbonyl substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand substitution](ligand-substitution.md)

Photoexcitation can dissociate a [carbonyl ligand](carbonyl-ligand.md), creating a coordinatively unsaturated [metal](metal.md) fragment. Capture by solvent or a tethered donor competes with CO recombination; net substitution therefore depends on both the photochemical step and subsequent trapping.
