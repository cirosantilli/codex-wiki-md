# PSPACE

↑ **Parent:** [Deterministic space complexity class](deterministic-space-complexity-class.md)

PSPACE consists of decision problems solvable using a polynomial amount of work space. Running time may be exponential. Enumerating all polynomial-length relation encodings and reusing working storage preserves this bound, as does complementation. In particular fixed-sentence [second-order logic](second-order-logic.md) model checking lies in PSPACE.
