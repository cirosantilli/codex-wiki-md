<h1 id="ramanujan-s-function">Ramanujan's function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md)

For $t>0$, this [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) converges at zero because $dx/[x\log^2x]$ is integrable, and at infinity because of exponential damping. Its large-$t$ behavior is governed by a logarithmic zero-endpoint singularity and begins with $1/\log t$, rather than a power of $t$.
