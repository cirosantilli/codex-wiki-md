# Blue light-emitting diode

↑ **Parent:** [Light-emitting diode](light-emitting-diode.md)

Blue transitions motivate [wide-bandgap semiconductors](wide-bandgap-semiconductor.md), but a large [band gap](band-gap.md) alone cannot guarantee efficient emission. [Gallium nitride](gallium-nitride.md) has a [direct band gap](direct-band-gap.md); [indium gallium nitride](indium-gallium-nitride.md) composition tunes the transition [energy](energy-split.md).[§2, printed pp. 8–10: direct/indirect gaps and nitride composition range](https://www.kapitza.ras.ru/~glazkov/students/Nobel-akasaki-lecture.pdf)

For an emitted [photon](photon.md), with [frequency](frequency.md) $\nu$ and vacuum [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda$,

$$
E_\gamma=h\nu=\frac{hc}{\lambda},\qquad
\lambda=\frac{hc}{E_\gamma},
$$

where $h$ is the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) and $c$ the vacuum [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). Multiplying [energy](energy-split.md) by [wavelength](wavelength.md) gives $hc=1239.841984\,\mathrm{eV\,nm}$. Thus chosen [wavelengths](wavelength.md) $450\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $630\,\mathrm{nm}$ correspond to $2.75520\,\mathrm{eV}$ and $1.96800\,\mathrm{eV}$: blue requires more [energy](energy-split.md) per [photon](photon.md) than red. A stipulated transition [energy](energy-split.md) $3.40\,\mathrm{eV}$ instead gives $364.659\,\mathrm{nm}$, in the [ultraviolet](ultraviolet.md). These are conversions, not measured emission lines.

For near-edge emission, $E_\gamma\approx E_g$ estimates the [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) from the emitting material's [band gap](band-gap.md). [Quantum well](quantum-well.md) confinement or impurity transitions can change it. The 1994 [blue light-emitting diode](blue-light-emitting-diode.md) used [zinc](zinc.md)-doped [indium gallium nitride](indium-gallium-nitride.md), emitting below its near-edge transition.[p. 1688, Fig. 2 discussion: Zn-related emission](candela-class-high-brightness-ingan%E2%81%84algan-double-heterostructure-blue-light-emitting-diodes-by-shuji-nakamura-and-others.md)

The measured forward terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V_f$ is also not fixed by $E_g/q$, where $q$ is the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). It depends on [electric current](electric-current.md), [temperature](temperature-split.md), contacts and series [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). Even the simple [Shockley diode equation](shockley-diode-equation.md) with series [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) has $V_f=V_j+IR_s$, whereas an optical transition does not acquire an extra [energy](energy-split.md) $qIR_s$ merely because the contact drop increases. Consequently, extracting a [band gap](band-gap.md) from one terminal-voltage reading is not justified.
