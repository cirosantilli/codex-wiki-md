# Standard parabolic subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Coxeter system](coxeter-system.md)

For $J\subseteq I$, the standard parabolic subgroup is $W_J=\langle s_j:j\in J\rangle$. Its cosets index faces of a fixed type in the [Coxeter complex](coxeter-complex.md).
