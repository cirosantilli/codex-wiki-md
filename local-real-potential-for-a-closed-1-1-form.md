<h1 id="local-real-potential-for-a-closed-1-1-form">Local real potential for a closed (1,1)-form</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler form](kahler-form.md)

For a closed real $(1,1)$ form, the [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) gives a real one-form $\eta$ with $d\eta=\omega$. Its $(0,1)$ part is $\bar\partial$-closed, so the [Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma](dolbeault-poincare-lemma.md) gives $\eta^{0,1}=\bar\partial\psi$. Reality gives $\eta^{1,0}=\partial\bar\psi$, hence $\omega=i\partial\bar\partial(2\operatorname{Im}\psi)$. Positivity is a separate requirement for this form to define a metric.
