# Trigonometric form of the beta function

↑ **Parent:** [Beta function](beta-function.md)

For positive real $p,q$, substitute $x=\sin^2\theta$ in the defining [Euler beta function](beta-function.md) integral. The increasing substitution maps $[0,\pi/2]$ onto $[0,1]$ and has $dx=2\sin\theta\cos\theta\,d\theta$. Combining this factor with $x^{p-1}(1-x)^{q-1}$ gives the displayed identity. Positivity of both parameters ensures convergence at the two endpoints, even when the integrand is unbounded there.
