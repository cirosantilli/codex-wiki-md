# pH indicator

↑ **Parent:** [Titration endpoint](titration-endpoint.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/pH_indicator)

A [pH indicator](ph-indicator.md) changes color as its [protonation](protonation.md) distribution changes. In a simple [weak acid](weak-acid.md)/[conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) model, its two forms have different colors and their ratio depends on [pH](ph.md) through the [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md). The visible transition spans a range of [pH](ph.md); it is not a direct signal that the sample has reached an [equivalence point](equivalence-point.md). An indicator is useful when its transition lies in a steep part of the [titration curve](titration-curve.md), so a modest range of [pH](ph.md) corresponds to a small added [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md).[Chemistry 2e, §14.7, Acid-Base Indicators, Figures 14.19–14.21: indicator equilibrium, transition ranges, and selection](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/14-7-acid-base-titrations.md)
