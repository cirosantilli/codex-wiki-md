# Countable saturation of a nonprincipal ultraproduct over omega

↑ **Parent:** [Saturated model](saturated-model.md)

In a [countable](countable-set.md) language, an [ultraproduct](ultraproduct.md) over a [nonprincipal ultrafilter](nonprincipal-ultrafilter.md) on $\omega$ realizes every [countable](countable-set.md) finitely satisfiable type, including types over countably many parameters. Enumerate the type $\varphi_1,\varphi_2,\ldots$, and let $A_n$ be the ultrafilter-large [set](set-split.md) of coordinates where the first $n$ [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) have a simultaneous witness. Put $B_n=\{i:i\geq n\}\cap\bigcap_{j\leq n}A_j$. At coordinate $i$, choose a witness for the largest $n\leq i$ with $i\in B_n$, or an arbitrary element when there is none. For each fixed $n$, the resulting [function](function-split.md) satisfies the first $n$ [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) on $B_n$. The [Łoś theorem](los-theorem.md) then realizes the entire type. Countability of the language and parameter [set](set-split.md) makes the enumeration possible.
