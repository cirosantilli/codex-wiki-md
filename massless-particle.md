# Massless particle

↑ **Parent:** [Particle (physics)](particle-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massless_particle)

A [particle](particle-physics.md) with zero invariant rest [mass](mass.md). A positive-energy [massless](massless-particle.md) state has $E=|\mathbf p|$ in natural units and has no rest frame. Its finite-helicity representations differ from massive spin representations.
