# Second-order product formula

↑ **Parent:** [Product-formula Hamiltonian simulation](product-formula-hamiltonian-simulation.md)

The symmetric second-order formula, also called Strang splitting, is

$$
e^{-i\delta A/2}e^{-i\delta B}e^{-i\delta A/2}
=e^{-i\delta(A+B)}+O(\delta^3)
$$

for bounded operators, with the constant controlled by nested [commutators](commutator.md).
