# Topology of membrane compartments

↑ **Parent:** [Endomembrane system](endomembrane-system.md)

Ordinary vesicle budding and fusion preserve which side of a membrane a protein domain faces: a cytoplasmic domain remains cytoplasmic, and a luminal domain remains luminal. Invagination can enclose previously external space without transporting its contents through a bilayer. The [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) lumen is continuous with the space between the two [nuclear envelope](nuclear-envelope.md) membranes; it is not the chromosome-containing nucleoplasm. Any proposed evolutionary remodelling must account for these distinct spaces and the membrane connections between them.
