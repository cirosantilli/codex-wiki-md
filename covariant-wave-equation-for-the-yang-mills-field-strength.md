# Covariant wave equation for the Yang-Mills field strength

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills equations](yang-mills-equations.md)

Apply $D^\rho$ to the [gauge-theory Bianchi identity](gauge-theory-bianchi-identity.md) and commute it through the other derivatives using $[D_\mu,D_\nu]X=[F_{\mu\nu},X]$. The [Yang-Mills equations](yang-mills-equations.md) remove the differentiated divergences. The two remaining [commutator](commutator.md) terms both equal $[F_\mu{}^\rho,F_{\nu\rho}]$, proving the displayed nonlinear [covariant wave equation for the Yang-Mills field strength](covariant-wave-equation-for-the-yang-mills-field-strength.md) on flat spacetime.
