# Divisor sum

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicative function](multiplicative-function.md)

The divisor sum of an arithmetic function $f$ is

$$
F(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}f(d).
$$

If $f$ is multiplicative, then $F$ is multiplicative as well.
