# Nonvanishing of Dirichlet L-functions on the line one

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md)

For a nonprincipal [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md), the line is zero-free. [Euler product positivity for L-function nonvanishing](euler-product-positivity-for-l-function-nonvanishing.md) proves this for nonreal characters at every height, and for all characters at nonzero height, where a possibly principal squared-character factor has no [pole](pole.md). The point of height zero is the [nonvanishing of nonprincipal Dirichlet L-functions at one](nonvanishing-of-a-nonprincipal-dirichlet-l-function-at-one.md). A principal L-function has a [pole](pole.md) at one, and no zeros at the other points of this line.
