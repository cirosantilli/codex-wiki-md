# Divisorial section ring of a well-formed weighted projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Class-group generator of a well-formed weighted projective space](class-group-generator-of-a-well-formed-weighted-projective-space.md)

With $D=\sum a_iF_i$ and $\sum q_ia_i=1$, the dual [toric lattice](lattice-in-toric-geometry.md) is $M=\{m\in\mathbb Z^{n+1}:\sum q_im_i=0\}$. A [torus character](characters-of-a-real-torus.md) $\chi^m$ is a section of $rD$ exactly when $b_i=m_i+ra_i\geq0$. The vector $b$ has weighted degree $\sum q_ib_i=r$, and the correspondence $\chi^m\mapsto X^b$ is bijective on bases in every degree and respects multiplication. This gives the displayed graded-ring isomorphism, even when $D$ is a non-Cartier [Weil divisor](weil-divisor.md).
