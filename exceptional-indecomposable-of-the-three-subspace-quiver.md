# Exceptional indecomposable of the three-subspace quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Three-subspace decomposition](three-subspace-decomposition.md)

Take sources $k,k,k$, sink $k^2$, and arrows sending $1$ to $e_1,e_2,e_1+e_2$. An endomorphism preserving these three lines must be scalar, so the representation has [endomorphism ring](endomorphism-ring.md) $k$ and is a [brick module](brick-module.md). It is the only indecomposable of the [three-subspace quiver](three-subspace-quiver.md) with a two-dimensional sink.
