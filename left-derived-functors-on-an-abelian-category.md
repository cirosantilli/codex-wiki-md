# Left derived functors on an abelian category

↑ **Parent:** [Left derived functor](left-derived-functor.md)

For a [right-exact additive functor](right-exact-additive-functor.md) out of an [abelian category with enough projectives](abelian-category-with-enough-projectives.md), apply the [functor](functor.md) to a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) and take [homology objects](homology-object.md). The zeroth derived [functor](functor.md) is $F$, positive derived [functors](functor.md) vanish on projective objects, and the degreewise split [horseshoe lemma](horseshoe-lemma.md) produces a long exact sequence from every [short exact sequence in an abelian category](short-exact-sequence-in-an-abelian-category.md). The target can be any abelian [category](category-split.md), including an [opposite category](opposite-category.md) of one.
