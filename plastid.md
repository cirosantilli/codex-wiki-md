# Plastid

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle](organelle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastid)

A [plastid](plastid.md) is an [organelle](organelle.md) of the lineage that includes [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md). A [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) is a photosynthetic [plastid](plastid.md); the terms are not interchangeable because some [plastids](plastid.md) are nonphotosynthetic. [Endosymbiotic theory](endosymbiotic-theory.md) connects [plastid](plastid.md) origins to [cyanobacteria](cyanobacteria.md), while [endosymbiotic gene transfer](endosymbiotic-gene-transfer.md) and [organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md) explain genetic and functional integration with the host.
