# Phase plane

↑ **Parent:** [Autonomous planar system](autonomous-planar-system.md)

The phase plane is the two-dimensional state space of an [autonomous planar system](autonomous-planar-system.md). Its oriented trajectories, equilibria, nullclines, and invariant sets display the system's qualitative dynamics.
