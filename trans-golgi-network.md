# trans-Golgi network

↑ **Parent:** [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md)

The [trans-Golgi network](trans-golgi-network.md) is the sorting and export region at the trans side of the [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md). Onward traffic can supply the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md), [secretory granules](secretory-granule.md), or the [lysosome](lysosome.md) route.[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Protein Sorting and Export from the Golgi Apparatus, Figure 9.27 and lysosomal targeting discussion](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/the-golgi-apparatus.md)

Processing and sorting answer different questions. In a constructed example, two cargo [proteins](protein-split.md) could receive the same [carbohydrate](carbohydrate.md) modification yet carry different targeting information and leave in different [transport vesicles](transport-vesicle.md). Conversely, two chemically different cargo [proteins](protein-split.md) could share a destination. A processing readout alone therefore cannot establish the final address; an experiment must also identify cargo location or delivery. Return traffic and retention likewise matter: the [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md) is a working compartment, not merely a one-way queue that empties all of its [enzymes](enzyme-split.md) into outgoing carriers.
