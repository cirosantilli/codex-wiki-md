# Quota share reinsurance

↑ **Parent:** [Reinsurance](reinsurance.md)

Quota share reinsurance retains a fixed proportion $\alpha\in[0,1]$ of each claim. The annual retained aggregate is $\alpha S$, whose [expected value](expected-value.md) and [variance](variance-split.md) are $\alpha\mathbb ES$ and $\alpha^2\operatorname{Var}(S)$.
