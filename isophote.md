# Isophote

↑ **Parent:** [Observational astronomy](observational-astronomy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isophote)

An [isophote](isophote.md) is a contour of equal surface brightness in an astronomical image. Deviations from fitted ellipses encode additional information about a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md)'s projected stellar structure.
