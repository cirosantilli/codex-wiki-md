# Binary planetesimal

↑ **Parent:** [Planetesimal](planetesimal.md)

A binary planetesimal is a gravitationally bound pair of [planetesimals](planetesimal.md). Its internal [two-body problem](two-body-problem.md) and its [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) orbit respond differently to external forces, including [gas drag](gas-drag.md).
