# Positive-energy spectrum condition

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)

The joint energy-momentum spectrum lies in the closed forward [light cone](light-cone.md). In axiomatic [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md), this condition permits analytic continuation of [Wightman functions](wightman-function.md) in suitable complex spacetime domains. Together with [Lorentz invariance](lorentz-invariance.md), [microcausality](microcausality.md) and the positivity of the physical state space, it is one of the hypotheses behind the [CPT theorem](cpt-theorem.md).
