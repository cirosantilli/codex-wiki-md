# Corotating point-mass tidal field

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md)

Use local axes radial, tangential in the orbital plane, and perpendicular to that plane around a [circular Kepler orbit](circular-kepler-orbit.md) with $\Omega^2=GM/R^3$. The gravitational [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md) is $\Omega^2\operatorname{diag}(2,-1,-1)$. A uniformly corotating frame adds the gradient of [centrifugal acceleration](centrifugal-acceleration.md), $\Omega^2\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$, giving the displayed effective tensor for stationary offsets. Thus the radial differential acceleration is $3GMa/R^3$. These are accelerations, or forces per unit test mass; an actual differential force includes that mass. Moving offsets also experience [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md).
