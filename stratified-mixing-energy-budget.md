# Stratified mixing energy budget

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent mixing](turbulent-mixing.md)

With boundary fluxes suppressed, let $B$ transfer [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) to [potential energy](potential-energy.md), $M$ transfer [available potential energy](available-potential-energy.md) irreversibly to [background potential energy](background-potential-energy.md), and $W$ transfer [internal energy](internal-energy.md) to potential energy. Then $\dot P=B+W$, $\dot P_b=M+W$, $\dot A=B-M$, and $\dot K=F-B-\epsilon$. The internal reservoir has $\dot I=\epsilon-W$, conserving total energy apart from forcing $F$. The distinction between reversible buoyancy exchange and irreversible mixing is essential outside a quasi-steady negligible-$A$ regime.
