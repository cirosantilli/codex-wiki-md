# Closed immersion criterion for affine schemes

↑ **Parent:** [Closed immersion](closed-immersion.md)

A surjective [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $A\to B=A/I$ identifies the [spectrum of a ring](spectrum-of-a-commutative-ring.md) $B$ with the closed subset $V(I)$ and induces a surjective map of structure sheaves. Conversely, the [direct image sheaf](direct-image-sheaf.md) of $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}B}$ under an affine morphism is the sheaf associated with the $A$-module $B$: its sections on $D(a)$ are $B_{\phi(a)}$. The cokernel of the structure-sheaf map is therefore associated with the module cokernel of $A\to B$. If the sheaf map is surjective, all localizations of that cokernel vanish and the module cokernel is zero. This proves the converse without assuming global sections preserve arbitrary sheaf surjections.
