# Reduction of cyclotomic units at the ramified prime

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic unit](cyclotomic-unit.md)

For $1\le k<p$, choose $l$ with $kl\equiv1\pmod p$. The geometric sum $1+\zeta_p^k+\cdots+\zeta_p^{k(l-1)}$ is the inverse of $u_k$. Hence these are units, and their residues cover $\mathbb F_p^\times$. In the [cyclotomic Milnor square](cyclotomic-milnor-square.md), this makes the $K_1$ map onto the common [field](field.md) surjective and eliminates its connecting obstruction to $K_0$.
