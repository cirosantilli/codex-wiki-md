# Caccioppoli partition

↑ **Parent:** [Set of finite perimeter](set-of-finite-perimeter.md)

A [Caccioppoli partition](caccioppoli-partition.md) is a countable measurable partition, up to null sets, into [sets of finite perimeter](set-of-finite-perimeter.md) with finite sum of relative perimeters. Its interior interfaces are counted twice by that sum. It represents regions of a piecewise constant [image signal](image-signal.md) in the [piecewise-constant Mumford–Shah problem](piecewise-constant-mumford-shah-problem.md).
