# Hermitian element of a star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Star-algebra](star-algebra.md)

A [Hermitian algebra element](hermitian-element-of-a-star-algebra.md) of a [star-algebra](star-algebra.md) is an element fixed by its [algebra involution](algebra-involution.md). The identity and zero are [Hermitian algebra elements](hermitian-element-of-a-star-algebra.md). For every $x$, both $xx^*$ and $x^*x$ are [Hermitian algebra elements](hermitian-element-of-a-star-algebra.md). A real spectrum for all such elements is an extra condition in a general [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), rather than a consequence of the involution identities alone.
