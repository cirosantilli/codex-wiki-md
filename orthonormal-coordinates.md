# Orthonormal coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md)

Coordinates relative to an origin $\mathbf o$ and an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $\mathbf e_1,\ldots,\mathbf e_n$ preserve [Euclidean distances](euclidean-distance.md): if $\mathbf x-\mathbf y=\sum_i d_i\mathbf e_i$, then $\|\mathbf x-\mathbf y\|^2=\sum_i d_i^2$. This matters when finding the [eccentricity](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) of a [conic section](conic-section.md) in an oblique [plane](plane.md): arbitrary projected [coordinates](coordinate-linear-algebra.md) need not preserve the ratio of its [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) to its [semi-minor axis](semi-minor-axis.md).
