# Linear similarity profiles for surfactant spreading

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-mass Marangoni spreading on a liquid film](finite-mass-marangoni-spreading-on-a-liquid-film.md)

For symmetric [finite-mass Marangoni spreading on a liquid film](finite-mass-marangoni-spreading-on-a-liquid-film.md), the leading outer profiles are $h=2h_0|x|/x_N$ and $C=(M/x_N)(1-|x|/x_N)$ inside $|x|<x_N$, where $x_N=(6AMh_0t/\mu)^{1/3}$. Outside, $h=h_0$ and $C=0$. The [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) follows from $H\Gamma^{\prime}=-\eta/3$, $\eta H^{\prime}=H$, $2\int\Gamma\,d\eta=1$ and $\int(H-1)\,d\eta=0$. The film reaches height $2h_0$ immediately behind the front, leaving an outer height jump that gravity or [surface tension](surface-tension.md) must smooth. These are ideal leading outer profiles, not a description of the microscopic central dry region or the smoothed edge.
