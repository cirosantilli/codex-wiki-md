# Kernel of an isogeny

↑ **Parent:** [Isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md)

For an isogeny $\phi:E\to E'$, its kernel is denoted $E[\phi]$. In characteristic zero its cardinality equals $\deg\phi$.
