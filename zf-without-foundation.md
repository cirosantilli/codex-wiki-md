# ZF without foundation

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)

[ZF without foundation](zf-without-foundation.md) consists of the axioms of Zermelo–Fraenkel [set](set-split.md) theory with the [Axiom of foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md) omitted. Ordinal-indexed [transfinite recursion](transfinite-recursion.md) still defines the [cumulative hierarchy](cumulative-hierarchy.md). Its [union](set-union.md) is the well-founded part and need not include all ambient [sets](set-split.md).
