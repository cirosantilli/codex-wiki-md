# Reflection in an affine hyperplane

↑ **Parent:** [Reflection (mathematics)](reflection-mathematics.md)

For a nonzero real normal [vector](vector.md) $n$, orthogonal reflection in $n^Tx=d$ has the displayed [affine map](affine-map.md). It is $x\mapsto Hx+t$ with $H=I-2nn^T/(n^Tn)$ and $t=2dn/(n^Tn)$. In [homogeneous coordinates](homogeneous-coordinate.md) its matrix is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}H&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Every point on the hyperplane is fixed, and the signed normal displacement changes sign. Choosing any point $p$ on the hyperplane gives $t=p-Hp$, so translating by $-p$, applying the [reflection matrix](reflection-matrix.md) $H$ and translating back gives the same transformation.
