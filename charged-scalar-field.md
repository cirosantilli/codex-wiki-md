# Charged scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field](scalar-field.md)

A charged scalar field is a complex [scalar field](scalar-field.md) transforming nontrivially under a gauge symmetry. For an electromagnetic potential $A_a$, its gauge-covariant derivative is $\mathcal D_a=\nabla_a-iqA_a$.
