# p orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic orbital](atomic-orbital.md)

An [atomic orbital](atomic-orbital.md) with [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) quantum number $l=1$. Real $p_x,p_y,p_z$ combinations have opposite [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md) in their two lobes and a nodal plane through the [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md). An orbital phase is not an [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md).
