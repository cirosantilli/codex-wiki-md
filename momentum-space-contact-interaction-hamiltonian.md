# Momentum-space contact-interaction Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Contact interaction (quantum mechanics)](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md)

For a periodic box of [volume](volume.md) $V$, expanding $\psi(\mathbf r)=V^{-1/2}\sum_{\mathbf k}e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}a_{\mathbf k}$ in $H_{\rm int}=\tfrac u2\int d^3r\,\psi^\dagger\psi^\dagger\psi\psi$ gives the displayed [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md). The spatial [integral](integral-split.md) is $V\delta_{\mathbf k_1+\mathbf k_2,\mathbf k_3+\mathbf k_4}$ by [Fourier orthogonality](fourier-orthogonality.md), enforcing [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) and leaving the factor $V^{-1}$. The coupling $u$ in three dimensions has units of [energy](energy-split.md) times [volume](volume.md).
