# Isometry group

↑ **Parent:** [Isometry](isometry.md)

The [isometry group](isometry-group.md) of a [metric space](metric-space.md) consists of its bijective [isometries](isometry.md), with composition as the group operation. For a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), these are the [diffeomorphisms](diffeomorphism.md) preserving the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). Right translations of a [right-invariant Riemannian metric](right-invariant-riemannian-metric.md) supply a faithful subgroup isomorphic to the underlying [Lie group](lie-group.md).
