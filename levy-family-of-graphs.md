<h1 id="levy-family-of-graphs">Lévy family of graphs</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration of measure](concentration-of-measure.md)

A [Lévy family of graphs](levy-family-of-graphs.md) consists of finite connected [graphs](graph-split.md) with their uniform [probability measures](probability-measure.md) and [graph distances](distance-graph-theory.md) divided by their diameters, whose [concentration of measure](concentration-of-measure.md) functions vanish at every fixed positive normalized radius. Equivalently, every family occupying at least half the vertices has a radius-$\lfloor\varepsilon D_n\rfloor$ neighbourhood occupying a proportion tending to one, uniformly over such families. [Harper theorem](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md) and [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) tail bounds prove this for the [hypercube graphs](hypercube-graph.md).
