# Sine-Gordon breather

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon equation](sine-gordon-equation.md)

A sine-Gordon breather is a spatially localized solution that is periodic in time and can be interpreted through a complex-conjugate pair of discrete scattering eigenvalues.

This is a [breather](breather.md) of the [Sine-Gordon equation](sine-gordon-equation.md); the general breather concept also occurs in other nonlinear systems.
