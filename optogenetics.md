# Optogenetics

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic engineering](genetic-engineering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optogenetics)

[Optogenetics](optogenetics.md) uses genetically introduced light-responsive components to perturb or monitor biological processes with spatial and temporal control. Light-only, construct-only and expression-matched controls help distinguish the intended response from phototoxicity.
