# Displacement damage

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation damage in materials](radiation-damage-in-materials.md)

Damage initiated when energetic particles transfer enough momentum to displace lattice atoms. A recoil can generate a [displacement cascade](displacement-cascade.md), while many displaced atoms recombine during the ensuing relaxation.
