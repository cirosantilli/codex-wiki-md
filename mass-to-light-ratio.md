# Mass-to-light ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy stellar mass](galaxy-stellar-mass.md)

The [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md) compares the mass assigned to an astronomical system with its luminosity in a specified band, often in solar units. A stellar value uses only stellar mass and depends on the stellar population, age, composition and band. A dynamical value can include gas and [dark matter](dark-matter.md), so it need not equal the stellar value. In a halo-to-galaxy mapping, $L=M_*/\Upsilon_*$ requires the stellar ratio and not the total [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md) ratio.
