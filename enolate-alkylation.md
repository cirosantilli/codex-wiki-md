# Enolate alkylation

↑ **Parent:** [Enolate](enolate.md)

[Enolate alkylation](enolate-alkylation.md) forms a [carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) bond by attack of an [enolate](enolate.md) [carbon](carbon.md) on an alkyl [electrophile](electrophile.md), commonly through an [SN2 reaction](sn2-reaction.md). Solvent, [counterion](counterion.md), aggregation and [electrophile](electrophile.md) identity affect competition with [oxygen](oxygen.md) alkylation.
