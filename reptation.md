# Reptation

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

[Reptation](reptation.md) is the snake-like motion of a long [polymer](polymer-split.md) through the constraints imposed by neighboring chains or a fixed porous matrix. A tube model restricts large transverse motion while allowing curvilinear motion along the chain contour. For a flexible chain with additive segment friction, contour diffusion scales as $N^{-1}$ and the time to renew a contour of length proportional to $N$ scales as $N^3$. With coil size squared proportional to $N$, this gives a centre-of-mass [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) proportional to $N^{-2}$. Applying this tube picture to weak-field [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md) gives an inverse-length mobility over its appropriate regime; field-induced orientation and trapping can invalidate that simple law.
