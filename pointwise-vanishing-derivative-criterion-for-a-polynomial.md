# Pointwise vanishing derivative criterion for a polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Entire function](entire-function.md)

Suppose that for every $z\in\mathbb C$, at least one derivative $f^{(n)}(z)$ of an [entire function](entire-function.md) vanishes. The closed sets $E_n=\{z:f^{(n)}(z)=0\}$ cover $\mathbb C$. By the [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md), some $E_N$ has nonempty interior. The [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md) gives $f^{(N)}=0$ everywhere, so $f$ is a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree below $N$.
