# Spectral Tikhonov differentiation for the Volterra operator

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed-boundary singular system of the Volterra operator](mixed-boundary-singular-system-of-the-volterra-operator.md)

The [Tikhonov regularization](tikhonov-regularization.md) of the [Volterra integration operator](volterra-operator.md) replaces the unbounded differentiation gain $a_n$ by $a_n/(1+\alpha a_n^2)$. The series converges in $L^2$ for every datum and $\alpha>0$. On the [range of the Volterra integration operator](range-of-the-volterra-integration-operator.md), it converges to the [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md) of the datum as $\alpha\downarrow0$. No pointwise differentiation of arbitrary noisy [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) data is required.
