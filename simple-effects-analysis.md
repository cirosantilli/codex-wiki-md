# Simple-effects analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Interaction (statistics)](interaction-statistics.md)

Analysis of one factor's effect at a specified level of another factor, for example a cue-congruence difference separately at each delay. The [interaction](interaction-statistics.md) compares those effects directly. One significant simple-effect test and one nonsignificant simple-effect test do not themselves establish an [interaction](interaction-statistics.md); compare the estimates with the appropriate uncertainty and [multiple testing](multiple-hypothesis-testing.md) treatment.
