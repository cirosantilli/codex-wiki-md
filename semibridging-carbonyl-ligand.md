# Semibridging carbonyl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Carbonyl ligand](carbonyl-ligand.md)

A [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) attached primarily to one metal but with a secondary interaction with another. Formula alone does not determine whether terminal, bridging or semibridging CO is present.
