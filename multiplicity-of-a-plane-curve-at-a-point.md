# Multiplicity of a plane curve at a point

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md)

Translate the point to the origin of an affine chart and expand a local defining [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) as $\sum a_{ij}x^iy^j$. Its lowest nonzero total degree is the multiplicity. A [smooth point of a variety](smooth-point-of-a-variety.md) has multiplicity one; a double point has multiplicity two. On a point [blowup of a smooth algebraic surface](blowup-of-a-smooth-algebraic-surface.md), the total transform is the [strict transform of an algebraic subvariety](strict-transform-of-an-algebraic-subvariety.md) plus this multiplicity times the [algebraic exceptional divisor](algebraic-exceptional-divisor.md).
