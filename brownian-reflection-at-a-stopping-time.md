# Brownian reflection at a stopping time

↑ **Parent:** [Reflection principle (Wiener process)](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md)

Reflecting a [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) $B$ after a [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $T$ by $\widehat B_t=2B_T-B_t$ for $t>T$, and leaving it unchanged for $t\leq T$, preserves its path [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md). For finite $T$, the [Strong Markov property](strong-markov-property.md) and symmetry of the future [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) prove this. When $T=\infty$, the path is left unchanged.
