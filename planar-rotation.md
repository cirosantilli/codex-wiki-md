# Planar rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md)

A planar rotation about the origin is the [linear map](linear-map.md) represented by the [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) $R_\theta=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. It preserves [inner products](inner-product.md), lengths, oriented angles and area, with positive angles measured anticlockwise. Its inverse is $R_{-\theta}$ and its [determinant](determinant.md) is one.
