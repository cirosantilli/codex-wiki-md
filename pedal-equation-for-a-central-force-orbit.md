# Pedal equation for a central-force orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Central force](central-force.md)

Let $p$ be the perpendicular distance from the force centre to the tangent of a regular planar [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md). Conserved physical [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) has magnitude $|h|=mpv$, while conserved [energy](energy.md) is $E=mv^2/2+V(r)$. Eliminating speed gives the displayed equation when $h\ne0$. For a differentiable polar curve, $p=r^2/\sqrt{r^2+(dr/d\theta)^2}$, connecting the geometric tangent distance to the radial energy equation.
