# Predictive variance

↑ **Parent:** [Predictive distribution](predictive-distribution.md)

The [variance](variance-split.md) of a [predictive distribution](predictive-distribution.md) quantifies spread of a future quantity conditional on the observed data and model assumptions. For a noisy future observation $Y_*=f_*+\epsilon_*$ with fresh independent noise of [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, it is $\operatorname{Var}(f_*\mid\mathcal D)+\sigma^2$. It should therefore be distinguished from the latent-function [posterior variance](posterior-variance.md).
