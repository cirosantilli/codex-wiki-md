# Internal friction angle

↑ **Parent:** [Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion](mohr-coulomb-failure-criterion.md)

The angle whose tangent gives the slope of a linear frictional [failure envelope](failure-envelope.md). It controls both the confinement dependence of strength and the optimum new shear-fracture orientation.
