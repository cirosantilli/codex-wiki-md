# Upward skip-free random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Random walk](random-walk.md)

An upward skip-free [random walk](random-walk.md) has integer-valued increments bounded above by one. Arbitrarily large downward jumps are permitted, but a first crossing of a nonnegative integer level $k$ from below must land exactly at $k$. This removes the overshoot from the [stopped likelihood ratio under exponential tilting](stopped-likelihood-ratio-under-exponential-tilting.md) and permits the exact [geometric maximum of an upward skip-free random walk](geometric-maximum-of-an-upward-skip-free-random-walk.md). For a real threshold $k$, the first crossing is the crossing of $\lceil k\rceil$, so the overshoot-free formula must use that integer level.
