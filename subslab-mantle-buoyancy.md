# Subslab mantle buoyancy

↑ **Parent:** [Mantle convection](mantle-convection.md)

The [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) of the [mantle](planetary-mantle.md) beneath a descending [slab](slab.md). Thermal, compositional or melt-related [density](density.md) anomalies can modify pressure and traction on the slab, potentially changing its contact with the [overriding plate](overriding-plate.md). It differs from the slab's own [slab buoyancy](slab-buoyancy.md). Inferring it from low seismic velocities requires independent constraints because temperature, fluids, melt and composition can produce overlapping signatures.
