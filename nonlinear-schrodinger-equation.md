<h1 id="nonlinear-schrodinger-equation">Nonlinear Schrödinger equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear partial differential equation](nonlinear-partial-differential-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_Schrödinger_equation)

The nonlinear Schrödinger equation adds an amplitude-dependent interaction to Schrödinger-type wave evolution. Its [Laplacian](laplacian.md) term disperses the complex wave amplitude, while the sign and form of the nonlinear term determine focusing or defocusing behavior. A one-dimensional cubic specialization is the [focusing nonlinear Schrodinger equation](focusing-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md); members of this broader family need not be [integrable systems](integrable-systems-split.md).
