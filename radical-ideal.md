# Radical ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_ideal)

An ideal $I$ is radical when $f^m\in I$ for some positive integer $m$ implies $f\in I$. Over an algebraically closed field, the [Hilbert Nullstellensatz](hilbert-nullstellensatz.md) identifies the vanishing ideal of $V(I)$ with the radical $\sqrt I$.
