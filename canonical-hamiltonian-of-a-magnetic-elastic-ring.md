# Canonical Hamiltonian of a magnetic elastic ring

↑ **Parent:** [Charged circular elastic ring in a uniform magnetic field](charged-circular-elastic-ring-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md)

Although the energy in velocities is $m(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2)/2+2\pi^2k(r-r_0)^2$, the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) in [canonical variables](canonical-variables.md) is

$$
H=\frac{p_r^2}{2m}+\frac{(p_\phi-qBr^2/2)^2}{2mr^2}+2\pi^2k(r-r_0)^2.
$$

The field therefore enters [Hamilton's equations](hamilton-s-equations.md) through the relation between canonical and [mechanical momentum](mechanical-momentum.md), even though a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) does no work.
