# G2 adjoint branching to a short-root sl2 subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md)

For a [short root](short-root.md) $\alpha$ in the [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md), restriction of the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) to the [sl2 subalgebra associated with a root](sl2-subalgebra-associated-with-a-root.md) gives

$$
\mathfrak g\cong V(3)^{\oplus2}\oplus V(2)\oplus V(0)^{\oplus3}.
$$

Here $V(n)$ is the irreducible [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md) representation of [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $n$ and dimension $n+1$. Every nonzero [root vector](root-vector.md) $x\in\mathfrak g_\alpha$ has $\dim Z_{\mathfrak g}(x)=6$: the [raising operator](raising-operator.md) has a one-dimensional [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) in each of these six irreducible summands.
