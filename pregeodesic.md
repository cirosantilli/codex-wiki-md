# Pregeodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)

A [pregeodesic](pregeodesic.md) is a curve whose tangent obeys $\nabla_UU=\kappa U$ for some scalar $\kappa$. A change of parameter makes it a [geodesic](geodesic.md) with an [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md). A varying speed along a geodesic changes its parameter, not its image.
