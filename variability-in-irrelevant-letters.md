# Variability in irrelevant letters

↑ **Parent:** [Orthographic learning](orthographic-learning.md)

Variation in surrounding letters while a target mapping remains stable. A first-grade training experiment found transfer benefits from varied consonants around vowel mappings, motivating tests of how example distributions support abstraction.[Developmental Psychology 49, 1348–1365; 224 first graders, vowel mappings with varied/similar consonants and transfer tasks](statistical-learning-in-reading-variability-in-irrelevant-letters-helps-children-learn-phonics-skills-by-keith-s-apfelbaum-and-others.md)
