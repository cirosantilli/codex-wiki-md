# Critical step response of a galvanometer

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-coil galvanometer](moving-coil-galvanometer.md)

For $\ddot\theta+2\omega_0\dot\theta+\omega_0^2\theta=F$ switched on from rest, $\theta(t)=(F/\omega_0^2)[1-(1+\omega_0t)e^{-\omega_0t}]$. [critical damping](critical-damping.md) is the fastest nonoscillatory [step response](step-response.md) at fixed [natural frequency](natural-frequency.md) within the ordinary second-order model.
