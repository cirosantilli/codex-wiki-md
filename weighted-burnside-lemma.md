# Weighted Burnside lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Burnside's lemma](burnside-s-lemma.md)

For an orbit-invariant weight $w$ on a finite [group action](group-action.md) $G\curvearrowright X$, the sum of one weight per orbit is $|G|^{-1}\sum_g\sum_{x:gx=x}w(x)$. Double counting gives $\sum_x|G_x|w(x)$ before dividing by $|G|$; each orbit contributes $|G|$ copies of its common weight. Thus the usual [Burnside's lemma](burnside-s-lemma.md) extends to weights in any commutative coefficient ring.
