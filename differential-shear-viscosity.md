# Differential shear viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Newtonian fluid](generalized-newtonian-fluid.md)

Differential shear viscosity measures the incremental shear-stress response around a given positive base [shear rate](shear-rate.md). It differs from the secant [shear viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) mu. A regular locally stable stress-to-rate inversion needs positive differential viscosity. Zero differential viscosity makes a first-order stress perturbation singular, and negative differential viscosity identifies a decreasing constitutive branch.
