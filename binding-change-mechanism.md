# Binding-change mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md)

The mechanism in which rotary changes of the catalytic head alter nucleotide affinities and release tightly bound [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md). It couples mechanical and chemical [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) through an asymmetric central shaft.
