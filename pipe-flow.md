# Pipe flow

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipe_flow)

Pipe flow is fluid motion through a closed cylindrical conduit. In fully developed axisymmetric flow, the axial velocity depends only on radius and the axial pressure gradient sets a shear stress linear in radius.
