# Adversity

↑ **Parent:** [Developmental psychopathology](developmental-psychopathology.md)

Experiences or conditions threatening relevant development, safety or functioning. [Exposure](exposure.md) should be characterized by nature, severity, duration, timing and context rather than assumed identical across people with the same event count. Objective circumstances and their subjective appraisal provide different information.
