# Torsion-free connections are determined by their parametrized geodesics

↑ **Parent:** [Parametrized geodesics determine the symmetric part of an affine connection](parametrized-geodesics-determine-the-symmetric-part-of-an-affine-connection.md)

The difference of two [torsion-free connections](torsion-free-connection.md) is symmetric, because subtracting their zero-torsion identities gives $\Delta(X,Y)=\Delta(Y,X)$. Agreement of parametrized [geodesics](geodesic.md) makes this difference skew-symmetric as well, by [parametrized geodesics determine the symmetric part of an affine connection](parametrized-geodesics-determine-the-symmetric-part-of-an-affine-connection.md). Over the real numbers it must therefore vanish. Without the torsion-free condition, an arbitrary skew-symmetric difference changes torsion without changing the parametrized [geodesics](geodesic.md).
