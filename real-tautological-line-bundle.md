# Real tautological line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Tautological bundle](tautological-bundle.md)

Over [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) $\mathbb{RP}^n$, the real tautological line bundle is

$$
\gamma_n=\{(\ell,v):v\in\ell\}\subseteq\mathbb{RP}^n\times\mathbb R^{n+1}.
$$

On the standard chart $U_i=\{[x]:x_i\ne0\}$, let $s_i([x])=x/x_i$. A [local trivialization](local-trivialization.md) sends $([x],t)$ to $([x],t s_i([x]))$, with inverse $([x],v)\mapsto([x],v_i)$. These maps prove that this is a [real line bundle](real-line-bundle.md). Its unit [sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md) is the [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) quotient $S^n\to\mathbb{RP}^n$. Its [mod-two Euler class of a real line bundle](mod-two-euler-class-of-a-real-line-bundle.md) is the degree-one generator of the [mod-two cohomology ring of real projective space](mod-two-cohomology-ring-of-real-projective-space.md).
