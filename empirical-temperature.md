# Empirical temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Zeroth law of thermodynamics](zeroth-law-of-thermodynamics.md)

An empirical temperature labels classes of [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) using a reproducible [thermometer](thermometer.md) response. Equal labels express the [zeroth law of thermodynamics](zeroth-law-of-thermodynamics.md) comparison. A suitable continuous response and calibration let ordinary laboratory states be represented on a numerical scale; the abstract [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) alone does not guarantee a useful real-valued coordinate. An [injective function](injective-function.md) of the label preserves equality, so the [zeroth law of thermodynamics](zeroth-law-of-thermodynamics.md) alone fixes neither unit nor spacing. [Thermometer calibration](thermometer-calibration.md) supplies a chosen response relation; [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md) supplies the additional absolute-scale structure.
