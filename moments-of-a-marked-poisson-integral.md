# Moments of a marked Poisson integral

↑ **Parent:** [Independent marking theorem for Poisson point processes](independent-marking-theorem-for-poisson-point-processes.md)

Attach conditionally [independent](independent-random-variables.md) nonnegative marks to a [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md), with a [measurable](measurability.md) mark law depending only on location. If a mark at $z$ has [mean](expected-value.md) $m(z)$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $v(z)$, and the displayed [integrals](integral.md) are finite, their total $W$ is finite [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) and has the displayed moments. The [Campbell first-moment formula](campbell-first-moment-formula.md) gives the first moment and finiteness. The [Poisson factorial moment measure](poisson-factorial-moment-measure.md) formula gives the square of that first moment for distinct pairs; the diagonal contributes $\int(v+m^2)\,d\mu$. Their difference is the [variance](variance-split.md). Signed marks require absolute first-moment integrability.
