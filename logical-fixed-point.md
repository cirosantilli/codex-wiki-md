# Logical fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)

A logical fixed point is a formula or sentence equivalent to the result of applying a specified formula operation to itself. In [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md), self-reference is mediated by a [Gödel number](godel-number.md) and the [diagonal lemma](diagonal-lemma.md), yielding a [first-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md) $G$ with a provable equivalence $G\leftrightarrow H(\ulcorner G\urcorner)$. In [modal logic](modal-logic.md), a [modal formula](modal-formula.md) $A$ satisfies $A\leftrightarrow F(A)$ modulo the chosen logic. A merely true semantic equivalence is weaker than a specified provable or canonical construction, so its hypotheses must be kept distinct.
