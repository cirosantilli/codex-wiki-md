# Down-and-out digital call

↑ **Parent:** [Down-and-out claim](down-and-out-claim.md)

A [digital call option](digital-call-option.md) that pays its terminal indicator only if a lower barrier was avoided. When its strike equals the lower barrier, survival already implies the terminal indicator is one. It is then a [no-touch option](no-touch-option.md), with alive price $C_d(s,t;B)-(s/B)^{1-2r/\sigma^2}C_d(B^2/s,t;B)$ in the dividend-free [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md).
