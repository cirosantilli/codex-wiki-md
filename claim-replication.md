# Claim replication

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent claim](contingent-claim.md)

A [contingent claim](contingent-claim.md) is replicated when an admissible [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) has exactly its terminal payoff. In the absence of [arbitrage](arbitrage.md), two [replicating strategies](replicating-strategy.md) for the same payoff have the same initial cost.
