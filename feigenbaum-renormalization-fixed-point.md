# Feigenbaum renormalization fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Period-doubling renormalization operator](period-doubling-renormalization-operator.md)

A Feigenbaum [fixed point](fixed-point.md) is an even analytic [unimodal interval map](unimodal-interval-map.md) $g$ with $g(0)=1$, a critical maximum of even order $d$, $a=g(1)\in(-1,0)$ and

$$
g(x)=a^{-1}g(g(ax)).
$$

In the quadratic class $g''(0)<0$. Its spatial scaling uses the [Feigenbaum constants](feigenbaum-constants.md) convention $\alpha=-1/a$; the values depend on the critical order. The central restrictive intervals are successively reduced by $|a|$ under renormalization. This [fixed point](fixed-point.md) describes the limiting shape of repeatedly renormalized maps at a period-doubling accumulation.
