# Gauge-boson mass matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge boson](gauge-boson.md)

With canonically normalized [gauge field](gauge-field.md) [kinetic terms](kinetic-term.md), a gauge-boson mass matrix is the real symmetric matrix of coefficients in the displayed quadratic mass term. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are squared physical [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) masses. For a complex [scalar field](scalar-field.md) vacuum $\Phi_0$ in the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md), $(M^2)_{ab}=2\operatorname{Re}[(g_aT_a\Phi_0)^\dagger(g_bT_b\Phi_0)]$. Thus for real $x_a$, $x^TM^2x=2\|\sum_a x_ag_aT_a\Phi_0\|^2\geq0$: it is a [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) and a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). An unbroken generator annihilates the [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) and lies in its null space. Diagonalizing the matrix identifies the massive and massless field combinations.
