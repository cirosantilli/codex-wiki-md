# Boundary-point swallowing time for a Loewner chain

↑ **Parent:** [Chordal Loewner equation](chordal-loewner-equation.md)

For a real point $b$ away from the initial driver, this is the maximal lifetime of the real solution $\partial_tg_t(b)=2/(g_t(b)-\xi_t)$. At finite lifetime its centred image collides with the [Loewner driving function](loewner-driving-function.md). A point may be visited or separated from infinity by the hull, so swallowing is not the same as an individual trace visit.
