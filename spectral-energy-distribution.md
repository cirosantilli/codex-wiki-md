# Spectral energy distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Observed radiative flux](observed-radiative-flux.md)

The distribution of received [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) or emitted [luminosity](luminosity.md) across [frequency](frequency.md) or [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md). Plotting $\nu F_\nu$ against $\log\nu$ makes the area equal to the integrated [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md), since $F_\nu\,d\nu=\nu F_\nu\,d\log\nu$. A convention such as $F_\nu$, $\nu F_\nu$ or $F_\lambda$ must be specified before comparing spectral slopes.
