# Dominated martingale measure

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)

For discounted one-period asset gains $Y$, a dominated martingale measure is a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) $Q$ [absolutely continuous with respect to](absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) the physical [probability measure](probability-measure.md) $P$, under which the gains have zero [expectation](expected-value.md). Its [Radon-Nikodym derivative](radon-nikodym-derivative.md) $Z$ satisfies $Z\geq0$, $\mathbb E_PZ=1$ and $\mathbb E_P[ZY]=0$. An [equivalent martingale measure](risk-neutral-measure.md) additionally has $Z>0$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). Absolute continuity permits some positive-probability physical states to receive zero pricing probability; equivalence does not.
