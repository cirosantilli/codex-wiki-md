# Right-linear grammar

↑ **Parent:** [Context-free grammar](context-free-grammar.md)

A right-linear grammar has productions of the form $A\to wB$ or $A\to w$, where $A,B$ are nonterminals and $w$ is a terminal word, possibly empty. Each right-hand side has at most one nonterminal, at its right end. Such grammars generate exactly [regular languages](regular-language.md): regard nonterminals as states and expand each terminal word into a finite path to another state or an accepting endpoint. Conversely the transition graph of a [deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md) gives these productions directly.
