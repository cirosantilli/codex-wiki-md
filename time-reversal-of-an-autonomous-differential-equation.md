# Time reversal of an autonomous differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Autonomous system (mathematics)](autonomous-system-mathematics.md)

If $z(t)$ solves an [autonomous differential equation](autonomous-system-mathematics.md), $w(t)=z(-t)$ solves the equation with negated [vector field](vector-field.md). The trajectories and [equilibrium points](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) stay in place but their orientations reverse; stable and unstable directions interchange. A [saddle equilibrium](saddle-equilibrium.md) remains a saddle, and a clockwise [centre equilibrium](center-equilibrium.md) becomes counterclockwise.
