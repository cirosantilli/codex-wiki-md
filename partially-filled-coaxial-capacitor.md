# Partially filled coaxial capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Coaxial cylindrical capacitor](coaxial-cylindrical-capacitor.md)

A long [coaxial cylindrical capacitor](coaxial-cylindrical-capacitor.md) of length $L$ and radii $a,b$ has a dielectric-filled overlap of length $x$ and [relative permittivity](relative-permittivity.md) $\epsilon_r$. Neglecting end and interface fringing, its filled and vacuum portions are in parallel because both share the same two conductors. The [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V$ produces $E_r=V/[r\ln(b/a)]$ in either portion; the [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) and electrode charge differ by the local permittivity. Adding their capacitances gives the title formula. The dielectric boundary is axial while the interior [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is radial and tangential to that interface, consistent with the [electrostatic interface boundary conditions](electrostatic-interface-boundary-conditions.md).
