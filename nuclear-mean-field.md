# Nuclear mean field

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md)

An effective one-[nucleon](nucleon.md) [potential energy](potential-energy.md) representing the average influence of the other [nucleons](nucleon.md). The [nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md) treats motion in this field first, then includes residual interactions. The field experienced by a [proton](proton.md) also includes the repulsive [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md); [proton](proton.md) and [neutron](neutron-split.md) [energy levels](energy-level.md) need not have identical spacings.
