# Poisson pencil

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson structure](poisson-structure.md)

Two [Poisson structures](poisson-structure.md) are compatible when every linear combination $\Pi_1+\lambda\Pi_2$ is a [Poisson structure](poisson-structure.md). This family is a Poisson pencil. In terms of the [Schouten-Nijenhuis bracket](schouten-nijenhuis-bracket.md), compatibility means $[\Pi_1,\Pi_2]=0$, in addition to the two individual [Jacobi identities](jacobi-identity.md). Constant [Poisson bivectors](poisson-bivector.md) are compatible. Over a real [manifold](topological-manifold.md), complex parameter values refer to the complexification of its [function](function-split.md) algebra.
