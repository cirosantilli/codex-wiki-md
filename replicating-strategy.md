# Replicating strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent claim](contingent-claim.md)

A replicating strategy is a [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) whose terminal wealth equals the prescribed payoff of a [contingent claim](contingent-claim.md). Admissibility specifies the permitted wealth bounds and integrability of its holdings.
