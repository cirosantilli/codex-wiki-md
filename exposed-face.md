# Exposed face

↑ **Parent:** [Face of a convex set](face-of-a-convex-set.md)

An exposed face of a [convex set](convex-set-split.md) $K$ is the nonempty set on which a supporting real [linear functional](linear-functional.md) attains its maximum. It is a [face of a convex set](face-of-a-convex-set.md): if $x=ty+(1-t)z$ with $0<t<1$ attains the maximum and $y,z\in K$, each of $f(y),f(z)$ is at most that maximum, so both equal it. On a complex [vector space](vector-space-split.md), take the real part of a complex [linear functional](linear-functional.md). An [exposed point](exposed-point.md) is an exposed face consisting of one point.
