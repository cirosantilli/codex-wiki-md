<h1 id="cadlag-process">Càdlàg process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md)

A càdlàg process has sample paths that are [càdlàg functions](cadlag.md): they are right-continuous and have a finite left limit at every positive time.
