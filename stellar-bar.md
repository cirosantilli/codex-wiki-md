# Stellar bar

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy dynamics](galaxy-dynamics.md)

An elongated nonaxisymmetric stellar structure in a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md), often approximated as a rotating perturbation of an axisymmetric or spherical [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md). Its pattern [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) need not equal an individual star's orbital [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md). The [Galactic bar](galactic-bar.md) is the particular example in the [Milky Way](milky-way.md).
