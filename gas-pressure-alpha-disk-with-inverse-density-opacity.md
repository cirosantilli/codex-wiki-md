# Gas-pressure alpha disk with inverse-density opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha disk](alpha-disk.md)

For an optically thick [alpha disk](alpha-disk.md) dominated by [gas pressure](gas-pressure.md), vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $H\propto T^{1/2}/\Omega$ and $\rho\propto\Sigma\Omega T^{-1/2}$. The specified [opacity](opacity.md) therefore scales as $\kappa\propto\Sigma^{-3/2}\Omega^{-3/2}T^2$. Viscous heating scales as $Q^+\propto\alpha\Sigma T\Omega$, while [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) gives $Q^-\propto T^4/(\kappa\Sigma)\propto\Sigma^{1/2}\Omega^{3/2}T^2$. Their balance gives $T\propto\alpha\Sigma^{1/2}\Omega^{-1/2}$, hence $\nu\propto\alpha^2\Sigma^{1/2}\Omega^{-3/2}$. Fixed $\alpha$ and [Keplerian rotation](keplerian-disk.md) yield the displayed law.

The local [thermal stability of an accretion disk](thermal-stability-of-an-accretion-disk.md) follows because, at fixed surface density and radius, cooling has logarithmic temperature slope two while heating has slope one. The [viscous stability of an accretion disk](viscous-stability-of-an-accretion-disk.md) follows from $\partial_\Sigma(\nu\Sigma)>0$ on the thermal-equilibrium branch. Both conclusions apply within this pressure, opacity and transport closure.
