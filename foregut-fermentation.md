# Foregut fermentation

↑ **Parent:** [Fermentation](fermentation.md)

Microbial digestion in an enlarged compartment before the principal acid and enzymatic digestive regions. Independent specializations occur in [ruminants](ruminant.md), [colobine monkeys](colobine-monkey.md) and [macropods](macropod.md).
