# Superconducting gap function

↑ **Parent:** [Superconducting gap](superconducting-gap.md)

A complex momentum-dependent pairing amplitude. Its crystal-symmetry transformation, nodes and relative signs distinguish candidate superconducting states. Magnitude-sensitive spectra alone generally do not establish all relative signs; [phase-sensitive superconducting gap experiments](phase-sensitive-superconducting-gap-experiment.md) test them through interference.
