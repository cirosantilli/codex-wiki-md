# Hydrostatic temperature of an inverse-square halo

↑ **Parent:** [Virial temperature](virial-temperature.md)

Suppose an isothermal [gas](gas.md) with $\rho_g\propto r^{-2}$ is supported in a spherical halo with $M(r)=Mr/R$. Then [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) and $P=\rho_gk_BT/\bar m$ give $-2\rho_gk_BT/(\bar m r)=-\rho_gGM/(Rr)$, hence $k_BT=\bar mGM/(2R)$. Here $\bar m$ is the dimensionful [mean particle mass](mean-particle-mass.md). The factor two follows from the [density](density.md) slope and isothermal [pressure](pressure-split.md) response; it is not the uniform-sphere virial coefficient. The singular profile and finite boundary are idealizations.
