# Order isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Total order](total-order.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_isomorphism)

An [order isomorphism](order-isomorphism.md) is a [bijection](bijection.md) preserving and reflecting the order relation. It transfers [well-foundedness](well-founded-relation.md) and [order type](order-type.md) between a coded order and its [ordinal](ordinal.md) interpretation.
