# Visible unit

↑ **Parent:** [Positive and negative phases of Boltzmann learning](positive-and-negative-phases-of-boltzmann-learning.md)

A [visible unit](visible-unit.md) represents an observed coordinate of the data. Clamping it means holding that coordinate fixed while other units update. In a [Boltzmann machine](boltzmann-machine.md), [hidden units](hidden-unit.md) are unobserved coordinates summed out to obtain the visible [marginal distribution](marginal-distribution.md).
