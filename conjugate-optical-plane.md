# Conjugate optical plane

↑ **Parent:** [Image formation](image-formation.md)

Two [conjugate optical planes](conjugate-optical-plane.md) are object and image planes related by an imaging system: points in one are imaged onto corresponding points in the other in the [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md). For a converging [thin lens](thin-lens.md) forming a [real image](real-image.md) in air, their distances $u,v>0$ obey $1/u+1/v=1/f$. Exchanging $u$ and $v$ preserves this relation; this symmetry gives the two settings of the [Bessel lens displacement method](bessel-lens-displacement-method.md).
