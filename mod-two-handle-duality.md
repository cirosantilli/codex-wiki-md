# Mod-two handle duality

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz duality](lefschetz-duality.md)

Reverse a [handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md) of a compact $n$-dimensional [manifold](topological-manifold.md). Absolute $k$-handles become relative $(n-k)$-handles based on the boundary. The absolute boundary matrices count [attaching sphere](attaching-sphere.md)-[belt sphere](belt-sphere.md) intersections; the corresponding relative matrices are their transposes over $\mathbb F_2$. The absolute [chain complex](chain-complex.md) is thus the complementary-degree dual of the relative [chain complex](chain-complex.md). The [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) proves [Poincare-Lefschetz duality](lefschetz-duality.md) without an [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) hypothesis.
