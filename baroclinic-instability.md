# Baroclinic instability

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroclinic_instability)

[Baroclinic instability](baroclinic-instability.md) converts [potential energy](potential-energy.md) of a stratified sheared basic flow into growing disturbances. In the [Eady model](eady-model.md), coupled [Boundary Rossby waves](boundary-rossby-wave.md) provide a useful mechanism: their induced velocities displace boundary [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) anomalies, and suitable wavelengths permit phase locking. A single neutral boundary wave does not by itself imply exponential instability.
