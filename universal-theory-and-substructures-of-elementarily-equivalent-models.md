# Universal theory and substructures of elementarily equivalent models

↑ **Parent:** [Existential-theory embedding criterion](existential-theory-embedding-criterion.md)

If $B$ satisfies the universal [theory](first-order-theory.md) of $A$, every [existential sentence](existential-sentence.md) true in $B$ is true in $A$, since its falsehood in $A$ would supply a universal [negation](negation.md). Apply the [existential-theory embedding criterion](existential-theory-embedding-criterion.md) to $B$ and the [complete theory](complete-theory.md) of $A$ to obtain $A^*$. Conversely universal truth descends to [substructures](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md). The [elementarily equivalent](elementary-equivalence.md) [model](model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) need not be the original structure itself.
