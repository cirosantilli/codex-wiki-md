# Transform fault

↑ **Parent:** [Plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md)

A plate boundary at which relative motion is tangential to the boundary. It neither creates nor consumes [lithosphere](lithosphere.md) in the ideal kinematic model. The admissible junction [velocities](velocity.md) form a line parallel to the fault through either adjacent plate [velocity](velocity.md) in [velocity](velocity.md) space.
