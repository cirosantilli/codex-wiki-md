# Velocity alignment

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

An interaction making particle [velocities](velocity.md) approach those of their neighbors. Symmetric nonnegative weights $K_{ij}$ conserve total [momentum](momentum-split.md) for equal particle [masses](mass.md) and dissipate relative [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). Connectivity and quantitative lower bounds on the interaction matter for eventual consensus; nonnegativity alone permits noninteracting groups.
