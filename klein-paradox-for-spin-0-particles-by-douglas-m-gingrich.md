# Klein Paradox for Spin-0 Particles by Douglas M. Gingrich

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Douglas M. Gingrich](work-by-douglas-m-gingrich.md)

The institutional lecture derives the outgoing branch, boundary amplitudes and signed charge-current balance for a scalar particle at an electrostatic step. Equations 4.142–4.155 distinguish ordinary transmission, evanescence and the negative-kinetic-energy regime; the following discussion interprets the latter through pair creation.
