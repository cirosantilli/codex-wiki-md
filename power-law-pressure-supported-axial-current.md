# Power-law pressure-supported axial current

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance](cylindrical-magnetostatic-pressure-balance.md)

For constant axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) and $p=p_0(1+R^2/a^2)^{-k}$ with $p_0>0$ and $k>0$, [cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance](cylindrical-magnetostatic-pressure-balance.md) gives, with $x=R^2/a^2$,

$$
I^2(R)=2\pi c^2p_0a^2
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle\frac{1-(1+kx)(1+x)^{-k}}{k-1},&k\ne1,\\
\log(1+x)+(1+x)^{-1}-1,&k=1.
\end{cases}
$$

The nontrivial [electric current](electric-current.md) is finite at infinity exactly when $k>1$, in which case $I^2(\infty)=2\pi c^2p_0a^2/(k-1)$. Either sign of $I$ is possible because the [pressure](pressure.md) balance fixes its square.
