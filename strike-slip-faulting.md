# Strike-slip faulting

↑ **Parent:** [Geological fault](geological-fault.md)

Predominantly horizontal relative motion along a fault. A pure strike-slip double couple has two vertical nodal planes; a [focal mechanism](focal-mechanism.md) gives both possible planes, and independent evidence selects the actual one.
