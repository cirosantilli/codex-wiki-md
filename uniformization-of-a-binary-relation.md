# Uniformization of a binary relation

↑ **Parent:** [Descriptive set theory](descriptive-set-theory-split.md)

For $A\subseteq X\times Y$, a uniformization is a [function](function-split.md) $f$ on the projection of $A$ to $X$ such that $(x,f(x))\in A$ for every $x$ in that projection. It chooses one witness from every nonempty vertical section of $A$.
