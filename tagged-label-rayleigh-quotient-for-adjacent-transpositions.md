# Tagged-label Rayleigh quotient for adjacent transpositions

↑ **Parent:** [Random adjacent transposition shuffle](random-adjacent-transposition-shuffle.md)

For the [random adjacent transposition shuffle](random-adjacent-transposition-shuffle.md) on $S_n$ with $n\geq2$, the position $f(\sigma)=\sigma^{-1}(i)$ of one label is uniform on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ in equilibrium, so $\operatorname{Var}_\pi(f)=(n^2-1)/12$. Each adjacent [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) moves the label with probability $2/n$, giving $\mathcal E(f,f)=(n-1)/n^2$. The [Poincare inequality for a reversible Markov chain](poincare-inequality-for-a-reversible-markov-chain.md) variational formula therefore gives $\gamma\leq12/[n^2(n+1)]$. In particular the continuous [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(0,1)$ has [variance](variance-split.md) $1/12$, not $1/6$.
