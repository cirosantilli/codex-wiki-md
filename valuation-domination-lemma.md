# Valuation domination lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Valuation ring](valuation-ring.md)

Every local subring $A$ of a [field](field.md) $K$ is dominated by a [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) with [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) $K$. Order local overrings by inclusion and contraction of their maximal [ideals](ideal.md); chain unions are local, so [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) gives a maximal one. For each $z\in K^\times$, at least one of the extensions of its [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) to $V[z]$ and $V[z^{-1}]$ is proper: otherwise two least-degree relations for $1$ with maximal-ideal coefficients reduce one another to a smaller-degree relation. Localizing the proper extension gives a dominating overring, so maximality forces either $z$ or $z^{-1}$ into $V$.
