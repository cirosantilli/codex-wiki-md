# Tortoise coordinate of a five-dimensional charged black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Five-dimensional Reissner--Nordstrom black hole](five-dimensional-reissner-nordstrom-black-hole.md)

For distinct horizon radii $a>b>0$, integrating $dr_\star/dr=f^{-1}$ gives $r_\star=r+\frac{a^3}{2(a^2-b^2)}\log|(r-a)/(r+a)|-\frac{b^3}{2(a^2-b^2)}\log|(r-b)/(r+b)|+C$. The [tortoise coordinate](tortoise-coordinate.md) defines constant retarded or advanced coordinates on radial [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md).
