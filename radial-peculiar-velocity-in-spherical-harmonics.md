# Radial peculiar velocity in spherical harmonics

↑ **Parent:** [Peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md)

With $\delta(\mathbf x)=\int\delta_k e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}d^3k/(2\pi)^3$ and logarithmic growing-mode rate $f=d\log D_+/d\log a$, the [linearized cosmological continuity equation](linearized-cosmological-continuity-equation.md) gives $\mathbf v_k=-iaHf\delta_k\mathbf k/k^2$. Apply the [Rayleigh plane-wave expansion](rayleigh-plane-wave-expansion.md) to its velocity potential and differentiate radially to obtain the displayed expression. The factor $aH$ supplies the velocity dimensions. At the present epoch $U/H_0$ has the same formula with $aH$ removed; the commonly used $f\simeq\Omega_m^{0.6}$ is an approximation, not an exact general growth law.
