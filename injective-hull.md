# Injective hull

↑ **Parent:** [Injective module](injective-module.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injective_hull)

The injective hull of a module $M$ is a minimal injective extension of $M$ in which $M$ is an essential submodule.
