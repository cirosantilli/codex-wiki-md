# Age of a dust universe without a cosmological constant

↑ **Parent:** [Age of an FLRW universe](age-of-an-flrw-universe.md)

For an expanding [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) universe with zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), normalize $x=a/a_0$. [Dust relation between deceleration and density](dust-relation-between-deceleration-and-density.md) and mass conservation give $\dot x^2=H_0^2(1-2q_0+2q_0/x)$. Integrating from the [Big Bang](big-bang.md) yields the age integral above.

For $q_0>1/2$, set $x=q_0(1-\cos\theta)/(2q_0-1)$ to obtain $H_0t_0=q_0(\theta_* -\sin\theta_*)/(2q_0-1)^{3/2}$, with $\cos\theta_*=(1-q_0)/q_0$ on the expanding branch. For $0<q_0<1/2$, the corresponding hyperbolic expression is $q_0(\sinh\eta_* -\eta_*)/(1-2q_0)^{3/2}$ with $\cosh\eta_*=(1-q_0)/q_0$. The flat-dust and empty [Milne universe](milne-model.md) limits are respectively $2/3$ and $1$.
