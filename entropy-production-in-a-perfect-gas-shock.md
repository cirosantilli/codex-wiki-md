# Entropy production in a perfect-gas shock

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure-density Hugoniot relation for a perfect gas](pressure-density-hugoniot-relation-for-a-perfect-gas.md)

For the [pressure-density Hugoniot relation for a perfect gas](pressure-density-hugoniot-relation-for-a-perfect-gas.md), the change in [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md) is $[s]/c_v=\log P-\gamma\log D(P)$, with $c_v$ the [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md) at constant volume. Its derivative is

$$
\frac{d([s]/c_v)}{dP}
=\frac{(\gamma^2-1)(P-1)^2}{P[(\gamma+1)P+\gamma-1][(\gamma-1)P+\gamma+1]}.
$$

It is positive for a compressive [normal shock wave](normal-shock-wave.md) with $P>1$. For a [weak shock](weak-shock.md) with $P=1+\delta$, integrating the leading term gives

$$
\frac{[s]}{c_v}=\frac{\gamma^2-1}{12\gamma^2}\delta^3+O(\delta^4).
$$

Thus the [entropy production](entropy-production.md) is cubic in the small [pressure](pressure.md) jump, even though the [mass density](density.md) and [temperature](temperature.md) changes already appear at first order.
