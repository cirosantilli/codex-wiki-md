# Perforated patch recording

↑ **Parent:** [Whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md)

[Perforated patch recording](perforated-patch-recording.md) obtains intracellular electrical access through pores in the patch under the [patch pipette](patch-pipette.md), with less open exchange of large cytoplasmic components than conventional [whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md). Pore selectivity matters: retaining some components does not guarantee unchanged [ion](ion.md) [concentrations](concentration.md). Monitor [patch-clamp series resistance](patch-clamp-series-resistance.md) as access develops and distinguish a perforated patch from accidental full rupture.
