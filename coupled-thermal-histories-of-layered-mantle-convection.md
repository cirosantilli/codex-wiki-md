# Coupled thermal histories of layered mantle convection

↑ **Parent:** [Mantle convection](mantle-convection.md)

Two separately mixed layers with an interfacial thermal bottleneck require separate [energy](energy-split.md) budgets. For lower input $F$, interlayer transfer $J$, surface loss $E$, and per-area [heat capacities](heat-capacity.md) $H_l,H_u$, these are $H_l\dot T_l=F-J$ and $H_u\dot T_u=J-E$. At equilibrium all three fluxes agree, but a finite interface resistance retains a [temperature](temperature-split.md) difference. Thus layer count alone does not specify the cooling rate.
