<h1 id="perfect-map-characterization-by-stone-cech-remainders">Perfect-map characterization by Stone-Čech remainders</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect map](perfect-map.md)

For a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $f:X\to Y$ between [Tychonoff spaces](completely-regular-hausdorff-space.md), extend it to $F:\beta X\to\beta Y$ by the [Stone-Čech extension property](stone-cech-extension-property.md). If $f$ is perfect, [compactness](compact-space.md) of a [fiber](fiber-of-a-function.md) and closedness of $f$ show that no remainder point can map to a point of $Y$: separate such a point from the [fiber](fiber-of-a-function.md) by a neighborhood in $\beta X$, and use closedness to obtain a target neighborhood whose preimage stays inside it, contradicting density. Conversely, if $F^{-1}(Y)=X$, each [fiber](fiber-of-a-function.md) is [compact](compact-space.md), and for closed $A\subseteq X$ the equality $f(A)=F(\overline A^{\beta X})\cap Y$ proves closedness. Equivalently $F(\beta X\setminus X)\subseteq\beta Y\setminus Y$.
