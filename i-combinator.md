# I combinator

↑ **Parent:** [Combinator](combinator.md)

The [identity function](identity-function.md) represented as a closed [lambda term](lambda-term.md). In [combinatory logic](combinatory-logic.md), take $I$ as the abbreviation $SKK$, where $S$ is the [S combinator](s-combinator.md) and $K$ is the [K combinator](k-combinator.md); the basic equality axioms give $IA=A$.
