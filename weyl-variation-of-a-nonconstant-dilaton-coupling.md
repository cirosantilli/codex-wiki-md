# Weyl variation of a nonconstant dilaton coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Dilaton](dilaton.md)

In two worldsheet dimensions, $\delta_\omega(\sqrt gR)=-2\sqrt g\Delta_g\omega$. Thus the standard [dilaton](dilaton.md) curvature coupling does not have classical [Weyl invariance](weyl-transformation.md) for a general spacetime-varying dilaton. A constant dilaton gives the topological [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) instead. In the quantum sigma model, this classical variation combines with the loop anomaly; vanishing improved [sigma-model beta functions](sigma-model-beta-function.md) is the full background conformal condition.
