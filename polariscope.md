# Polariscope

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)

An instrument with a [polarizer](polarizer.md) and an [optical analyzer](optical-analyzer.md), often with a specimen between them, used to reveal changes of polarization through transmitted intensity. Crossed ideal elements give extinction without a polarization-changing sample.
