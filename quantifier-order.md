# Quantifier order

↑ **Parent:** [First-order formula](first-order-formula.md)

In general, exchanging a universal and an existential quantifier changes a formula's meaning. A [uniform witness](uniform-witness.md) satisfies a condition for every input, while a [pointwise witness](pointwise-witness.md) may vary with the input. For the equality relation on a two-element set, $\forall x\exists y\,(x=y)$ holds but $\exists y\forall x\,(x=y)$ fails.
