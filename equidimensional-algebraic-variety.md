# Equidimensional algebraic variety

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of an algebraic set](dimension-of-an-algebraic-set.md)

An [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) whose [irreducible components](irreducible-component.md) all have the same [dimension of an algebraic set](dimension-of-an-algebraic-set.md). The components of a generic fibre in the [fiber dimension theorem](fiber-dimension-theorem.md) have this property even when that fibre is reducible.
