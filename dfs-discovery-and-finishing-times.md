# DFS discovery and finishing times

↑ **Parent:** [Depth-first search](depth-first-search.md)

A traversal clock incremented on each vertex discovery and finish records two times per vertex. The active [recursion](recursion-computer-science.md) interval of an ancestor contains that of each descendant. A finished vertex has no unexamined outgoing edge. These facts constrain possible finishing orders even when traversal order is unspecified.
