# Commutant of a semisimple representation

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple representation](semisimple-representation.md)

For a finite-dimensional complex [semisimple representation](semisimple-representation.md) $V=\bigoplus_\lambda(\mathbb C^{m_\lambda}\otimes V_\lambda)$ with pairwise inequivalent [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) $V_\lambda$, its commuting [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) are exactly $\bigoplus_\lambda(A_\lambda\otimes I_{V_\lambda})$. To prove it, write each block as an [intertwiner](intertwiner.md) and apply [Schur's lemma](schur-s-lemma.md): blocks between inequivalent constituents vanish, and blocks between fixed copies of the same constituent are scalar. The dimension is $\sum_\lambda m_\lambda^2$.
