# Philosophy of physics

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_physics)

[Philosophy of physics](philosophy-of-physics.md) studies the interpretation and explanatory structure of physical theories, including the relation between mathematical models, observable phenomena and claims about what exists. Its questions include [theoretical reduction](theoretical-reduction.md), [emergence](emergence.md) and competing formulations of physical locality.
