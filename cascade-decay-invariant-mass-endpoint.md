# Cascade-decay invariant-mass endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant mass](invariant-mass.md)

For sequential on-shell two-body decays $C\to B+t$ and $B\to A+s$, with massless $s,t$, evaluate their energies in the B [rest frame](rest-frame.md). Their pair [mass](mass.md) is $2E_sE_t(1-\cos\theta)$, so its squared [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) ranges from zero to the displayed endpoint. The [mass](mass.md) ordering is $m_C>m_B>m_A>0$. [spin](spin.md) correlations can alter the [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) inside these limits, but cannot move the kinematic endpoints while the same on-shell decay chain applies.
