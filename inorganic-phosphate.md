# Inorganic phosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Phosphate](phosphate.md)

[Inorganic phosphate](inorganic-phosphate.md), abbreviated $\mathrm P_i$, denotes the free [phosphate](phosphate.md) pool in biochemical [chemical equations](chemical-equation.md), including [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md). Its constituent [chemical species](chemical-species.md) depend on [pH](ph.md) and [protonation](protonation.md); $\mathrm P_i$ does not specify a single [electric charge](electric-charge.md) or proton count. See the [IUBMB recommendations on biochemical reactant pools](https://iubmb.qmul.ac.uk/thermod2/).
