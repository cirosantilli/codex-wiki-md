# Effective Hamiltonian for neutral-meson mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral meson mixing](neutral-meson-mixing.md)

A decaying particle-antiparticle pair is described by a two-dimensional [effective Hamiltonian for neutral-meson mixing](effective-hamiltonian-for-neutral-meson-mixing.md) with dispersive matrix $M$ and decay matrix $\Gamma$, both [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md). The [effective Hamiltonian for neutral-meson mixing](effective-hamiltonian-for-neutral-meson-mixing.md) itself is generally not a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md). [CPT](cpt-symmetry.md) symmetry equates its diagonal entries, by separately equating diagonal [masses](mass.md) and [decay widths](decay-width.md). For $H_{\rm eff}=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&a\end{pmatrix}$ the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $a\pm\sqrt{bc}$ and generic [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) are $(\sqrt b,\pm\sqrt c)^T$, with correlated square-root branches. Complex [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) give [masses](mass.md) and [decay widths](decay-width.md) through $\mu=m-i\Gamma/2$.
