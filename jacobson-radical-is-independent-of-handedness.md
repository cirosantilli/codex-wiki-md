# Jacobson radical is independent of handedness

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md)

The intersection of [maximal left ideals](maximal-left-ideal.md) is the intersection of annihilators of [simple modules](irreducible-module.md) on the left, hence is a two-sided [ideal](ideal.md). If $j$ belongs to it, $1-rj$ has a left inverse for every $r$. Its left inverse also differs from $1$ by a radical element and has a left inverse; this makes the original inverse two-sided. The identity $(1-ba)^{-1}=1+b(1-ab)^{-1}a$ then shows that $1-jr$ is invertible too. If $j$ were outside a [maximal right ideal](maximal-right-ideal.md) $I$, one would have $1-jr\in I$ for some $r$, a contradiction. Applying the same argument to the [opposite ring](opposite-ring.md) proves equality.
