<h1 id="positive-extension-from-a-unital-subspace-of-c-k">Positive extension from a unital subspace of C(K)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md)

A real [positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md) on a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) of $C(K)$ containing $1$ extends positively to all of $C(K)$. Its norm is $\phi(1)$ by order bounds. If this is positive, apply the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) to obtain an extension of the same norm, normalize it to value one at $1$, and use the [unital contraction positivity criterion](unital-contraction-positivity-criterion.md). If $\phi(1)=0$, the functional is zero and its zero extension suffices.
