# Complexation

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

Formation of a [coordination complex](coordination-complex.md) through association of a central [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) or [ion](ion.md) with donor groups. Different [oxidation states](oxidation-state.md) can have different complex stabilities, coupling [complexation](complexation.md) to [redox equilibrium](redox-equilibrium.md).
