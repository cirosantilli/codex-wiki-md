# Bell swapping visibility under random labels

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement swapping](entanglement-swapping.md)

Suppose independent resource pairs have [density matrices](density-matrix-split.md) $v_j|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|+(1-v_j)I_4/4$. An ideal complete [Bell-basis measurement](bell-basis-measurement.md), followed by outcome-dependent [Pauli gates](pauli-gate.md), produces a chosen [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) with white-noise visibility $v_1v_2$: either white-noise input makes the remote pair maximally mixed. If the reported label is reliable with probability $q$ and otherwise uniformly random, averaging the erroneous corrections makes that branch maximally mixed too, so $v_{\rm out}=v_1v_2q$. A second independent detector of the same quality during [quantum teleportation](quantum-teleportation.md) adds another factor $q$ to the teleported [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md).
