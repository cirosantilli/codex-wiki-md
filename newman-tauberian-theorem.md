# Newman Tauberian theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md)

For bounded locally integrable $f$ on the nonnegative real line, suppose its [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) $F$ extends holomorphically to an open neighborhood of the closed right half-plane. Then the ordinary improper integral converges to $F(0)$. [Contour damping for bounded Laplace transforms](contour-damping-for-bounded-laplace-transforms.md) bounds the difference from the finite-interval transform by $2\|f\|_\infty/R$ in the limit for every radius $R$. Extension merely to the open right half-plane is insufficient, as the constant function and transform $1/z$ show.
