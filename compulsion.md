# Compulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Obsessive-compulsive disorder](obsessive-compulsive-disorder.md)

A repetitive behavior or mental act performed according to rules or in response to an [obsession](obsession.md), commonly to reduce distress or prevent a feared outcome. Covert neutralization can serve the same maintaining function as a visible ritual.
