# Energy-dependent Schwarzschild material capture threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild marginally bound capture](schwarzschild-marginally-bound-capture.md)

For an incoming timelike Schwarzschild [geodesic](geodesic.md) with asymptotic [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) $k\ge1$, the critical unstable circular orbit obeys

$$
k^2=\frac{(r_c-2\mu)^2}{r_c(r_c-3\mu)},\qquad
h_c^2=\frac{\mu c^2r_c^2}{r_c-3\mu}.
$$

At $k=1$, $r_c=4\mu$ and $|h_c|=4\mu c$. For $k>1$, the critical radius lies between $3\mu$ and $4\mu$ and $|h_c|>4\mu c$. The marginally bound angular-momentum bound remains sufficient at larger energies but is no longer the exact threshold. Origin at infinity and an inward initial branch are essential; an orbit already inside a barrier is a different [initial value problem](initial-value-problem.md).
