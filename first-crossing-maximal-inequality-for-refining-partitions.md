# First-crossing maximal inequality for refining partitions

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md)

Let a [probability space](probability-space-split.md) have countable measurable partitions of positive-[measure](measure-split.md) cells, each partition refining its predecessor. An exceeding point has a first level where its cell average of $|f|$ exceeds $t$. The cells at those first crossings are disjoint: intersecting cells at different levels are nested, and an earlier exceeding cell prevents any of its descendants from being a first crossing. They cover exactly the maximal superlevel set. Summing their strict average inequalities gives the displayed [weak type (1,1)](weak-type-1-1.md) estimate with constant one. The same proof works on a general [measure](measure-split.md) space when every relevant cell has positive finite [measure](measure-split.md).
