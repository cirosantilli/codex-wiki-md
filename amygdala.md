# Amygdala

↑ **Parent:** [Brain](brain.md)

A collection of nuclei involved in [learning](learning.md), valuation and defensive regulation among other functions. Different nuclei and projections can have different or opposing effects; the anatomical name is not a unitary [fear](fear.md) centre.
