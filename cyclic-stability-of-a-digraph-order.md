# Cyclic stability of a digraph order

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent cyclic order of a digraph](coherent-cyclic-order-of-a-digraph.md)

The cyclic stability is the largest size of an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) appearing consecutively in a representative of a [coherent cyclic order of a digraph](coherent-cyclic-order-of-a-digraph.md). It is at most the ordinary [independence number](independence-number.md). A set with no forward path between its distinct vertices can be moved into a consecutive block by swapping nonadjacent vertices. Choose a representative minimizing the span of the set; a vertex in a gap can be moved left or right past the block, contradicting minimality unless there are no gaps.
