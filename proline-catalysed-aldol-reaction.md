# Proline-catalysed aldol reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Aldol reaction](aldol-reaction.md)

An [enamine](enamine.md) formed reversibly between [proline](proline.md) and an enolizable [carbonyl compound](carbonyl-compound.md) attacks an electrophilic [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md). The carboxylic acid group can orient and activate the electrophile; [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) releases the aldol product and regenerates [proline](proline.md).
