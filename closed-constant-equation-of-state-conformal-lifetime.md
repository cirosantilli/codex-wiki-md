# Closed constant-equation-of-state conformal lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal time](conformal-time.md)

For positive curvature and zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), the single-fluid Friedmann solution has a bang-to-crunch [conformal time](conformal-time.md) interval $2\pi/(1+3w)$ for $w\geq0$. A [radial null geodesic in FLRW spacetime](radial-null-geodesic-in-flrw-spacetime.md) travels that same great-circle comoving distance. Relative to a three-sphere circumference $2\pi$, it is $1/(1+3w)$ circuits: one for dust and one half for radiation, with the final point reached only in the singular-endpoint limit.
