# Latent heat of a temperature-dependent cubic-quartic Landau transition

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature-dependent cubic-quartic Landau free energy](temperature-dependent-cubic-quartic-landau-free-energy.md)

At an equilibrium minimum, $dF_{\min}/dT=\partial_TF$ because $\partial_\phi F=0$. For $0<\gamma<1$, the [entropy](entropy-split.md) decrease at coexistence is $S_0-S_+=c(1-\gamma)T^*(\phi^*)^2$. Multiplying by $T^*$ gives the positive [latent heat](latent-heat.md) released on cooling. The units are total heat or heat density according to the normalization of $F$.
