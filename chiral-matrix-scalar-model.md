# Chiral matrix scalar model

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field theory](scalar-field-theory-split.md)

A theory of an $N\times N$ matrix of [complex scalar fields](complex-scalar-field.md) with kinetic term $\operatorname{tr}(\partial\Phi^\dagger\partial\Phi)$ and potential $V=\alpha\operatorname{tr}[(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)^2]/2+\beta[\operatorname{tr}(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)]^2/2+m^2\operatorname{tr}(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)$. It is invariant under global $SU(N)_L\times SU(N)_R\times U(1)$ transformations. For $\alpha,\beta>0$ and $m^2<0$, minimizing the nonnegative eigenvalues of $\Phi^\dagger\Phi$ gives a [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) $C\mathbf1$ with $C^2=-m^2/(\alpha+N\beta)$, up to a global symmetry transformation. The connected stabilizer is diagonal $SU(N)$, with $N^2$ broken generators. Expanding $\Phi=C\mathbf1+(H+iP)/\sqrt2$ with [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) gives $N^2$ massless [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md) in $P$ and squared [masses](mass.md) $2C^2(\alpha+N\beta)$ for the singlet in $H$ and $2\alpha C^2$ for its traceless [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). The faithful unbroken conjugation action factors through $SU(N)/\mathbb Z_N$.
