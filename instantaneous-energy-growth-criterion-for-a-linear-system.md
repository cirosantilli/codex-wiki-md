# Instantaneous energy-growth criterion for a linear system

↑ **Parent:** [Transient growth](transient-growth.md)

For $x'=Lx$ and $E=\|x\|_2^2$, the [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) $H=(L+L^*)/2$ gives $\dot E=2x^*Hx$. Thus some initial condition grows immediately iff the largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of $H$ is positive. All trajectories have nonincreasing energy iff $H$ is a [negative semidefinite matrix](negative-semidefinite-matrix.md). This differs from requiring negative real parts for the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $L$: a [non-normal matrix](non-normal-matrix.md) can satisfy modal decay while allowing [transient growth](transient-growth.md).
