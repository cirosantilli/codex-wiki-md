# Moment map

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian group action](hamiltonian-group-action.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_map)

A moment map assigns to each element of a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) a [Hamiltonian function](hamiltonian-function.md) for its [fundamental vector field](fundamental-vector-field.md), and is equivariant for the [coadjoint action](coadjoint-representation.md). For an abelian [Lie group](lie-group.md), constant shifts are allowed; the numerical scale also depends on the chosen [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) generators and the normalization of the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md).
