# Picard group of a smooth affine quadric surface

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth quadric surface](smooth-quadric-surface.md)

Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), remove a smooth hyperplane conic $C$ from a [smooth quadric surface](smooth-quadric-surface.md) $Q$. The [rulings of a smooth quadric surface](rulings-of-a-smooth-quadric-surface.md) generate $\operatorname{Pic}(Q)=\mathbb Z^2$, and $C$ has class $(1,1)$. [Picard-group localization on a smooth variety](picard-group-localization-on-a-smooth-variety.md) gives the quotient by $\mathbb Z(1,1)$. The restricted ruling bundle $\mathcal O_Q(1,0)$ is a generator, exhibiting a nontrivial [line bundle](line-bundle.md) on an affine surface.
