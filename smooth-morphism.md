# Smooth morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_morphism)

A morphism of [schemes](scheme.md) is smooth when it is locally of finite presentation, flat and has geometrically regular fibres. Smooth fibres alone do not imply that a morphism is smooth: flatness and the dimension behaviour matter as well. The [Abel map of an algebraic curve](abel-map-of-an-algebraic-curve.md) has smooth [projective space](projective-space-split.md) fibres whose dimensions can jump.
