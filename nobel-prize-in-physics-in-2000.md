# Nobel Prize in Physics in 2000

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

For foundational work in information and communication technology, [Zhores I. Alferov](zhores-ivanovich-alferov.md) and [Herbert Kroemer](herbert-kroemer.md) received one quarter each for developing [semiconductor heterostructures](semiconductor-heterostructure.md) used in high-speed electronics and [optoelectronics](optoelectronics.md). [Jack S. Kilby](jack-st-clair-kilby.md) received one half for his part in inventing the [integrated circuit](integrated-circuit.md).[Individual official 2000 summary: Alferov and Kroemer one quarter each for semiconductor heterostructures; Kilby one half for integrated circuits.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2000-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
