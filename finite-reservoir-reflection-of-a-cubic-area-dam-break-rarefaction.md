# Finite-reservoir reflection of a cubic-area dam-break rarefaction

↑ **Parent:** [Dry-bed dam break with cubic wetted area](dry-bed-dam-break-with-cubic-wetted-area.md)

An impermeable wall at $x=-\ell$ first receives the upstream-moving head at $t_1$. The leading downstream [characteristic curve](characteristic-curve.md) of the reflected adjustment obeys $\dot x=u+c=(12c_0+5x/t)/7$ in the unmodified [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md), giving the displayed curve. It stays behind the inviscid dry tip $x_f=6c_0t$ at every finite time. The finite reservoir drains and its upstream depth decreases, while only the region behind $x_r$ has yet received the wall signal. Bottom drag or other tip physics can change the front law.
