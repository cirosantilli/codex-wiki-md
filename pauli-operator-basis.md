# Pauli operator basis

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md)

The identity and the three [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) form an [orthogonal basis](orthogonal-basis.md) for the complex [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of two-by-two [matrices](matrix.md), with [Hilbert-Schmidt inner product](hilbert-schmidt-inner-product.md) $\operatorname{Tr}(P^\dagger Q)=2\delta_{PQ}$. Thus every operator has the expansion $A=\frac12\sum_{P\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}}\operatorname{Tr}(PA)P$. For a [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md), the identity coefficient is $1/2$ and the remaining real coefficients are half the components of its [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md). This converts [unitary conjugation](unitary-conjugation.md) into transformations of three real components.
