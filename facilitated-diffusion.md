# Facilitated diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Passive transport](passive-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facilitated_diffusion)

[Facilitated diffusion](facilitated-diffusion.md) is [passive transport](passive-transport.md) through a [membrane transport protein](membrane-transport-protein.md), such as an [ion channel](ion-channel.md) or [carrier protein](carrier-protein.md), down the relevant [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md). The [protein](protein-split.md) supplies a route; it does not by itself provide [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) for uphill transfer.
