# Read-copy-update

↑ **Parent:** [Memory reclamation](memory-reclamation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Read-copy-update)

A family of concurrent access techniques that publishes replacement versions and postpones [memory reclamation](memory-reclamation.md) until a suitable [grace period](grace-period.md) has passed. Correct reader tracking, publication ordering and lifetime rules are essential.
