# Air pump

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pump)

A device that transfers air into or out of a vessel. Historical vacuum experiments used a receiver, valves and a mechanical pump; leakage limited the attainable [pressure](pressure-split.md).
