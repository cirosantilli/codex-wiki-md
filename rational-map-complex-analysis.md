# Rational map (complex analysis)

↑ **Parent:** [Meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md)

A rational map of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) is a quotient of two complex polynomials, extended to infinity. A nonconstant map has a well-defined positive degree and is a branched covering of the sphere.
