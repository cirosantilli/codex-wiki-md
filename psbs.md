# PsbS

↑ **Parent:** [Photosystem II](photosystem-ii.md)

[PsbS](psbs.md) participates in rapid proton-dependent regulation of photosynthetic antenna quenching in plants. Loss of PsbS-dependent quenching can be separated experimentally from loss of light-harvesting capacity.
