# Constant-power rotational acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

A [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) with [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$, constant driving [power](power.md) $P$ and linear resisting [torque](torque.md) $-\gamma\omega$ has an exponential approach of $\omega^2$ to $P/\gamma$. The ideal drive requires [torque](torque.md) $P/\omega$, so starting from rest is a limiting [energy](energy-split.md) model with singular initial [torque](torque.md).
