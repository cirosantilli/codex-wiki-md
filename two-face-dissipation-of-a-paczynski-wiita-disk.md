<h1 id="two-face-dissipation-of-a-paczynski-wiita-disk">Two-face dissipation of a Paczyński-Wiita disk</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Paczyński-Wiita circular orbit](paczynski-wiita-circular-orbit.md)

For a [steady accretion disk with arbitrary rotation law](steady-accretion-disk-with-arbitrary-rotation-law.md) and a [zero-torque inner boundary condition](zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) at $R=3R_S$, the outward [viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) is $\mathcal G=\dot M(j-j_{\mathrm{in}})$. The dissipation per unit planar area summed over both disk faces is $D=-\mathcal G\Omega'/(2\pi R)=\nu\Sigma(R\Omega')^2$. Integrating $2\pi R D$ gives $L=\dot M GM/(8R_S)=\dot M c^2/16$ in the [Paczyński-Wiita potential](paczynski-wiita-potential.md). The radiative flux from one face is $D/2$, preventing a factor-of-two error in the luminosity.
