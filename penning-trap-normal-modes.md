# Penning trap normal modes

↑ **Parent:** [Penning trap](penning-trap.md)

Define positive [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) magnitudes $\omega_c=|Q|B/m$ and $\omega_z>0$, with uniform $\mathbf B=B\hat{\mathbf z}$, $B>0$. Choose the static electrode polarity through the [potential energy](potential-energy.md)

$$
Q\Phi=\frac{m\omega_z^2}{2}\left(z^2-\frac{x^2+y^2}{2}\right).
$$

Its axial [force](force-split.md) gives $\ddot z=-\omega_z^2z$, while its transverse [electric force](electric-force.md) points outwards. For $Q>0$, let $w=x+iy$. The [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) gives

$$
\ddot w+i\omega_c\dot w-\frac{\omega_z^2}{2}w=0.
$$

Trying $w\propto e^{-i\omega t}$ gives the two radial [normal mode](normal-mode.md) magnitudes $\omega_\pm$ displayed above: the fast modified [cyclotron](cyclotron.md) motion and slow [magnetron motion](magnetron-motion.md). For $Q<0$ the rotation directions reverse. Bounded confinement in this ideal model requires $\omega_c^2>2\omega_z^2>0$; the repeated-root boundary needs exclusion because it allows a linearly growing solution. At large $\omega_c/\omega_z$, $\omega_-\simeq\omega_z^2/(2\omega_c)$ and $\omega_+\simeq\omega_c-\omega_-$.

The ideal identities

$$
\omega_++\omega_-=\omega_c,\qquad
\omega_+\omega_-=\frac{\omega_z^2}{2},\qquad
\omega_+^2+\omega_-^2+\omega_z^2=\omega_c^2
$$

follow directly from that quadratic. They show why the measured modified [cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md) must not simply be substituted for the free [cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md). These are ideal-model deductions; the response to real electrode and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) imperfections needs a separate correction analysis.
