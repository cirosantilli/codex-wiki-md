# Relative topological K-theory

↑ **Parent:** [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md)

For a nonempty subcomplex $A\subset X$, relative [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md) is $K^i(X,A)=\widetilde K^i(X/A)$, where the collapsed subcomplex is the basepoint. It fits into the [K-theory six-term exact sequence](k-theory-six-term-exact-sequence.md).
