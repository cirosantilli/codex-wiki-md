# Superadditivity of quantum relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md)

For a bipartite [density operator](density-matrix.md) $\rho_{AB}$ and a product reference state,

$$
D(\rho_{AB}\|\sigma_A\otimes\sigma_B)
\geq D(\rho_A\|\sigma_A)+D(\rho_B\|\sigma_B).
$$

The difference between the two sides is the nonnegative [quantum mutual information](quantum-mutual-information.md) of $\rho_{AB}$.
