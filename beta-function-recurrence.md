# Beta-function recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Beta function](beta-function.md)

The [Beta function](beta-function.md) obeys

$$
B(x+1,y)=\frac{x}{x+y}B(x,y),
\qquad
B(x,y+1)=\frac{y}{x+y}B(x,y).
$$

These identities follow immediately from the [beta--gamma identity](beta-gamma-identity.md) and the [Gamma function recurrence](gamma-function-recurrence.md).
