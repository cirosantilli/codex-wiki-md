# Marsh test

↑ **Parent:** [Arsenic detection](arsenic-detection.md)

A historical arsenic-separation and recognition procedure associated with [James Marsh](james-marsh.md). Volatile [arsine](arsine.md) carries arsenic away from a sample matrix and yields a deposit; [stibine](stibine.md) can interfere.
