# Singular homology

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_homology)

Singular homology forms a [chain complex](chain-complex.md) from formal sums of continuous maps from standard simplices into a [topological space](topological-space.md). Its homology groups are invariant under [homotopy](homotopy.md).
