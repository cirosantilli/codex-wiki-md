# Euler theorem for homogeneous functions

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous function](homogeneous-function.md)

Let $f$ be a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) on an open domain stable under positive rescaling, with $f(\lambda x)=\lambda^kf(x)$ for $\lambda>0$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) differentiates this [homogeneous function](homogeneous-function.md) identity at $\lambda=1$ to give

$$
\sum_ix_i\partial_i f(x)=k f(x).
$$

If $f$ is twice differentiable, differentiating once more gives $\sum_i x_i\partial_i\partial_jf=(k-1)\partial_j f$. In degree one its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) therefore annihilates the radial vector $x$. This is the identity used in the [no-scale identity from degree-one homogeneity](no-scale-identity-from-degree-one-homogeneity.md). For an [extensive quantity](extensive-quantity.md), the degree-one formula also supplies the thermodynamic identity behind the [Gibbs-Duhem equation](gibbs-duhem-equation.md).
