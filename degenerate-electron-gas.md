# Degenerate electron gas

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md)

An electron [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md) whose occupation is governed mainly by the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md), with thermal energy small compared with its kinetic [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md). At zero temperature its momentum states are filled up to $p_F$. In the ultrarelativistic limit $E/V=3ncp_F/4\propto n^{4/3}$, with $n=4\pi g_sp_F^3/(3h^3)$. This supplies the pressure supporting a [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) until the relativistic mass limit is approached.
