# Bipartite quantum system

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

A [quantum system](quantum-system-split.md) with two specified subsystems has a [tensor product](tensor-product.md) [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). A joint [density operator](density-matrix-split.md) determines subsystem $A$'s measurement statistics through its [partial trace](partial-trace.md) $\rho_A=\operatorname{Tr}_B\rho_{AB}$. A joint [pure state](pure-state.md) need not give a pure reduced state: the [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) $(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$ gives $\rho_A=I/2$.
