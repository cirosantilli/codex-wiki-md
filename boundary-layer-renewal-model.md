# Boundary-layer renewal model

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal boundary layer](thermal-boundary-layer.md)

In a renewal model, a diffusive [thermal boundary layer](thermal-boundary-layer.md) grows until a convective instability overturns it, after which growth restarts. A layer reaching thickness $\delta\sim\sqrt{\kappa\tau}$ has mean [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) of order $k\Delta T/\delta$. A diffusive $t^{-1/2}$ flux averaged over one cycle differs only by an order-one coefficient. Distinct thermal and material layers need not share a renewal time.
