# Permanent collisions in constant-probability slotted ALOHA

↑ **Parent:** [Slotted ALOHA](slotted-aloha.md)

With independent Poisson arrivals of any positive rate and a fixed retry probability $0<f\leq1$, the infinite-population [slotted ALOHA](slotted-aloha.md) model eventually has collisions in every slot almost surely. For $f<1$, an [exponential-supermartingale escape bound](exponential-supermartingale-escape-bound.md) proves transience of the backlog chain. Bounded martingale increments then give backlog growth at the arrival rate, and the [Conditional Borel-Cantelli lemma](conditional-borel-cantelli-lemma.md) makes the exponentially unlikely noncollision slots finite in number. At $f=1$, two old packets suffice for permanent collisions. At $f=0$ the assertion is false.
