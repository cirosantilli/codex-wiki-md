# Higher cohomology vanishing from an ample hyperplane restriction

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform Serre vanishing for two ample twists](uniform-serre-vanishing-for-two-ample-twists.md)

Let $H$ be a [very ample](very-ample-line-bundle.md) [effective Cartier divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md) on a [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md) $X$, and suppose $L|_H$ is [ample](ample-line-bundle.md). Then $H^i(X,L^m)=0$ for $i\ge2$ and sufficiently large $m$. By [uniform Serre vanishing for two ample twists](uniform-serre-vanishing-for-two-ample-twists.md), cohomology of $L^m(tH)|_H$ vanishes uniformly for $t\ge0$. The restriction sequence identifies the higher groups of $L^m(tH)$ and $L^m((t+1)H)$ for $i\ge2$. For fixed $m$, large $t$ kills them by [Serre vanishing](serre-vanishing.md) for $H$; descend to $t=0$. No $H^1$ vanishing is claimed.
