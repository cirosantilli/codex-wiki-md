# Respiratory control

↑ **Parent:** [Electron transport chain](electron-transport-chain.md)

[Respiratory control](respiratory-control.md) links mitochondrial [oxygen](oxygen.md) consumption to demand for [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md). When [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) permits faster [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) turnover, [proton](proton.md) return lowers the opposing [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md), permitting faster [electron](electron.md) transport.[Chapter 14, Electron-Transport Chains and Their Proton Pumps, Respiratory Control Normally Restrains Electron Flow Through the Chain](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

The [mitochondrial oxygen electrode assay](mitochondrial-oxygen-electrode-assay.md) tests this connection by changing [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) availability while observing [oxygen](oxygen.md). A slowed [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md) alone cannot locate the cause: depleted [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md), scarce [oxygen](oxygen.md), impaired [enzymes](enzyme-split.md) and a strong opposing [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) can all restrict turnover. A renewed response to [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) helps distinguish these possibilities.
