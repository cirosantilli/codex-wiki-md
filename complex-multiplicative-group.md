# Complex multiplicative group

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian group](abelian-group.md)

The nonzero [complex numbers](complex-number.md) form an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) under multiplication, with identity one and inverse $z^{-1}$. As a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) it is isomorphic to $\mathbb R\times S^1$ by logarithmic modulus and phase. It appears naturally as a [Möbius pointwise stabilizer of two points](mobius-pointwise-stabilizer-of-two-points.md).
