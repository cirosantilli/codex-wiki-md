# Type IIB supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_IIB_supergravity)

Type IIB supergravity is the chiral ten-dimensional maximal [supergravity](supergravity.md) with metric, [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md), [dilaton](dilaton.md), Ramond-Ramond zero-form, two-form and four-form potentials, and a self-dual five-form field strength. Two same-chirality Majorana-Weyl [gravitini](gravitino.md) and two opposite-to-gravitino-chirality Majorana-Weyl [dilatini](dilatino.md) complete its $128+128$ massless spectrum.
