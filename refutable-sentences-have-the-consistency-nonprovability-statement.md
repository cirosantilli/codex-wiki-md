# Refutable sentences have the consistency nonprovability statement

↑ **Parent:** [Gödel second incompleteness theorem](godel-second-incompleteness-theorem.md)

Let $T$ be a [first-order consistent](first-order-consistency.md) [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) with the [Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions](hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md), and suppose $T\vdash\neg A$. Provability of $A$ and provability of falsity are equivalent inside $T$, because either refutable [first-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md) implies the other. Thus $\neg\operatorname{Pr}_T(A)$ is equivalent inside $T$ to its [formal consistency statement](formal-consistency-statement.md). The second incompleteness theorem gives $T\nvdash\neg\operatorname{Pr}_T(A)$.
