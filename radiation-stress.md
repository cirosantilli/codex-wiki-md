# Radiation stress

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiation_stress)

The excess depth-integrated, time-averaged [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) of a [surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md) is its radiation stress. In deep water $c_g/c_p=1/2$, so $S_{xx}=\mathcal E/2=\rho_wga^2/4$. The force per transverse width on a reflecting object is the incident plus reflected minus transmitted radiation stress. Reflection increases force because the reflected wave carries momentum in the opposite direction.
