# Facial isomer

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric isomer](geometric-isomer.md)

In an octahedral [facial isomer](facial-isomer.md), three selected like donors occupy one triangular face and are pairwise cis. Unsymmetrical bidentate ligands can give facial and [meridional isomers](meridional-isomer.md), each with distinct optical configurations.
