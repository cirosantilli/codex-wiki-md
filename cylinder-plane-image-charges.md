# Cylinder-plane image charges

↑ **Parent:** [Method of images](method-of-images.md)

An infinite conducting cylinder of radius $a$ at centre height $d>a$ above a grounded plane has the same exterior [electric potential](electric-potential.md) as opposite [line charges](line-charge.md) $\pm\lambda$ at heights $\pm c$. If $R_+$ and $R_-$ are distances from the positive and negative lines, $V=\lambda\ln(R_-/R_+)/(2\pi\varepsilon_0)$. The plane has $R_-=R_+$; the cylinder has $R_-/R_+=(d+c)/a$. Its voltage is therefore $\lambda\operatorname{arcosh}(d/a)/(2\pi\varepsilon_0)$, giving the displayed [capacitance per unit length](capacitance-per-unit-length.md). The [Uniqueness of the Dirichlet problem](uniqueness-of-the-dirichlet-problem.md) fixes the exterior field. A Gaussian contour surrounding the cylinder encloses the positive image line, so $\lambda$ is also its physical charge per unit length.
