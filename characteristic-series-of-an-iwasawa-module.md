# Characteristic series of an Iwasawa module

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic ideal](characteristic-ideal.md)

For a finitely generated torsion [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md), this is a generator of its [characteristic ideal](characteristic-ideal.md), unique up to a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) of the [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) ring. It may be chosen as a power of $p$ times a product of distinguished polynomials. The series itself and the ideal it generates are different objects; changing a generator by a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) preserves the ideal. When the finite [Euler characteristic of a one-variable Iwasawa module](euler-characteristic-of-a-one-variable-iwasawa-module.md) is defined, the inverse p-adic absolute value of its constant term gives that invariant.
