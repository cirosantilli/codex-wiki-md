# Time-stepping method

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

A time-stepping method approximates the evolution of an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) by successive updates at discrete times. It can be applied after a spatial [finite element method](finite-element-method-split.md) has converted a time-dependent [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) into an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) system. The [implicit time-stepping method](implicit-time-stepping-method.md) is one class of such methods.
