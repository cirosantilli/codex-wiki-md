# Equivariant map of monoid sets

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid action](monoid-action.md)

An equivariant map $h:A\to B$ between left [M-sets](m-set.md) obeys $h(m\cdot a)=m\cdot h(a)$. These are the natural transformations of the corresponding functors from the one-object monoid category.
