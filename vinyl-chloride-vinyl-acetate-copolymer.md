# Vinyl chloride-vinyl acetate copolymer

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical copolymer](statistical-copolymer.md)

A [copolymer](copolymer.md) containing $-\mathrm{CH_2-CHCl}-$ and $-\mathrm{CH_2-CH(OAc)}-$ units. Hydrolysis changes the acetate side groups without requiring cleavage of the carbon backbone, provided competing degradation is controlled.
