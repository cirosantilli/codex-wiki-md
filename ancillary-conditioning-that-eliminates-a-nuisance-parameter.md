# Ancillary conditioning that eliminates a nuisance parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Partial ancillary statistic](partial-ancillary-statistic.md)

For a [sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md) $(C,S)$ and parameter $(\psi,\lambda)$, a useful strong reduction requires the law of $S$ to be free of $\psi$ and the [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md) of $C$ given $S$ to be free of the [nuisance parameter](nuisance-parameter.md) $\lambda$. In the discrete case it gives

$$
f(x;\psi,\lambda)=\phi_0(x)\phi_C(C(x),S(x);\psi)\phi_S(S(x);\lambda),
$$

where $\phi_0$ sums to one on each $(C,S)$ fiber, $\phi_S$ sums to one over $s$, and $\phi_C$ sums to one over $c$ separately for every fixed $s$. Ordinary [ancillarity](ancillary-statistic.md), or just the law of $S$ being free of $\psi$, does not by itself guarantee that conditioning removes $\lambda$.
