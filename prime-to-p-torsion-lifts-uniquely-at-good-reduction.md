# Prime-to-p torsion lifts uniquely at good reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Reduction of torsion points on an elliptic curve](reduction-of-torsion-points-on-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For [good reduction of an elliptic curve](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) over a complete [local field](local-field.md) of residue characteristic $p$, smoothness and the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) make reduction surjective. Its kernel is a [formal group](formal-group-law.md) on the maximal ideal, where multiplication by $q^m$, $q\ne p$, is bijective by [prime-to-residue-characteristic multiplication on a formal group](prime-to-residue-characteristic-multiplication-on-a-formal-group.md). Thus there is no nonzero $q$-power torsion in the kernel. Given a lift $P$ of a reduced point killed by $q^m$, choose the unique kernel point $R$ with $q^mR=q^mP$. Then $P-R$ is a torsion lift. This proves both surjectivity and uniqueness of torsion lifting. For finite residue field these groups are finite.
