<h1 id="juan-fernandez-ridge">Juan Fernández Ridge</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nazca Plate](nazca-plate.md)

A volcanic ridge system entering the South American [subduction zone](subduction-zone.md) near central Chile, associated with the southern transition of the Pampean flat-slab region. Its crustal structure, hydration and past geometry constrain explanations of the [slab dip](slab-dip.md); present-day bathymetry alone does not give the buoyancy of its already subducted continuation.
