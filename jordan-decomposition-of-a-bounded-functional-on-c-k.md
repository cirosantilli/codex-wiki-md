<h1 id="jordan-decomposition-of-a-bounded-functional-on-c-k">Jordan decomposition of a bounded functional on C(K)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md)

For a bounded real [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on the [space of continuous functions on a compact space](space-of-continuous-functions-on-a-compact-space.md), define $\phi^+(f)=\sup_{0\leq g\leq f}\phi(g)$ for $f\geq0$. The decomposition $g=\min(g,f)+(g-f)^+$ for $0\leq g\leq f+h$ proves additivity on the positive cone; positive homogeneity is immediate. Extend by differences, and put $\phi^-=\phi^+-\phi$. Both are [positive linear functionals](positive-linear-functional.md). Their norms sum to $2\phi^+(1)-\phi(1)=\sup_{\|g\|_\infty\leq1}\phi(g)=\|\phi\|$, using the substitution $g=2h-1$.
