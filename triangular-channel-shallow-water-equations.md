# Triangular-channel shallow water equations

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md)

In a channel with width $2\lambda xz$ at height $z$, a well-mixed [particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md) of depth $h$ occupies area $\lambda xh^2$. Its [volume conservation](volume-conservation.md), momentum and [particle deposition flux](particle-deposition-flux.md) balances give

$$
h_t+uh_x+(h/2)u_x=-uh/(2x),\quad
u_t+uu_x+d\phi h_x+(dh/3)\phi_x=0,\quad
\phi_t+u\phi_x=-2W_s\phi/h,
$$

where the [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) is $g'=d\phi$. The $h/3$ coefficient is the cross-section-weighted mean of $h-z$ in the [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) force. The [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) have speeds $u$ and $u\pm\sqrt{g'h/2}$.
