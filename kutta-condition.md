# Kutta condition

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

The [Kutta condition](kutta-condition.md) selects the circulation of an idealized [potential flow](potential-flow.md) past a body with a sharp trailing edge by requiring finite limiting velocity at that edge. For the [Joukowski map](joukowski-map.md) $z=\zeta+a^2/\zeta$, the trailing edge $z=2a$ corresponds to a simple zero of $dz/d\zeta$ at $\zeta=a$. If $W$ is the [complex potential](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) in the circle plane, then $dw/dz=W'(\zeta)/(1-a^2/\zeta^2)$ is finite there only if $W'(a)=0$. For a [uniform flow](uniform-flow.md) of speed $U$ at angle $\alpha$ and a [point vortex](line-vortex.md) of circulation $\Gamma$, this gives $\Gamma=-4\pi Ua\sin\alpha$, and cancellation of the simple zeros gives the tangential trailing-edge velocity $U\cos\alpha$. The opposite endpoint can retain a leading-edge singularity.
