# Derangement in a transitive group action

↑ **Parent:** [Transitive group action](transitive-group-action.md)

A derangement is an element fixing none of the points of a [group action](group-action.md). Every finite transitive action on at least two points has a derangement. Count pairs $(g,x)$ with $gx=x$: transitivity gives $\sum_g|\operatorname{Fix}(g)|=\sum_x|G_x|=|G|$. If every element fixed a point, the identity's extra fixed points would make this sum strictly larger than $|G|$. This is the [Burnside lemma](burnside-s-lemma.md) counting argument specialized to existence of a derangement.
