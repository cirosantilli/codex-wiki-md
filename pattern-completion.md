# Pattern completion

↑ **Parent:** [Associative memory in neural networks](associative-memory-in-neural-networks.md)

Reinstatement of a stored representation from an incomplete or degraded cue. Recurrent [autoassociative memory](autoassociative-memory.md) provides one mechanism; its success depends on the cue entering the correct [basin of attraction](basin-of-attraction.md). Partial-cue deficits after CA3-targeted [NMDA receptor](nmda-receptor.md) deletion support a [hippocampal](hippocampus.md) role without identifying a unique weight-update rule.[Abstract; pp. 211–218, adult CA3-restricted deletion and full-cue versus partial-cue recall](requirement-for-hippocampal-ca3-nmda-receptors-in-associative-memory-recall-by-kazu-nakazawa-and-others.md)
