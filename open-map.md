# Open map

↑ **Parent:** [Local diffeomorphism](local-diffeomorphism.md)

An open map sends every [open subset](open-set.md) of its source to an [open subset](open-set.md) of its target. This is an image property; [continuity](continuous-function.md) instead concerns [preimages](preimage.md) of [open sets](open-set.md). A [local diffeomorphism](local-diffeomorphism.md) has both properties, but openness alone does not promise unique recovery of an input. In the periodic coordinate map under the [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md), open patches give open output regions while multiple angle values still give the same output.

Together, [open and closed maps](open-and-closed-maps.md) describe the two image-set preservation properties.
