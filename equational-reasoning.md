# Equational reasoning

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical proof](mathematical-proof.md)

Prove equality of expressions through a chain of substitutions justified by definitions, previously established equalities, or algebraic laws. Each step preserves the expression's value. In a [programming language](programming-language-split.md) with [lazy evaluation](lazy-evaluation.md), an asserted program law must also preserve termination and the demanded amount of a partial value, not just agree on finite defined inputs.
