# Continuous poset

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_poset)

A [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) in which, for every $x$, the elements [way-below](way-below-relation.md) $x$ form a [directed set](directed-set.md) with [least upper bound](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) $x$. Directed [least upper bounds](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) other than these approximating ones need not exist.
