# Orthonormal representation of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Lovász number](lovasz-number.md)

Assign a real unit vector $u_v$ to each graph vertex, with $u_v\perp u_w$ whenever distinct vertices are nonadjacent. A handle is a further unit vector $h$. In the handle formulation of the [Lovász number](lovasz-number.md), one minimizes $\max_v|h\cdot u_v|^{-2}$ over representations and handles. Orthonormal here does not mean every pair of assigned vectors is orthogonal.
