# Mass mixing ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Mass fraction](mass-fraction.md)

The mass mixing ratio used in a dilute [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md) model is absorber [mass density](density.md) divided by total atmospheric [mass density](density.md). A hydrostatic pressure interval contributes absorber [mass path](mass-path.md) $q\,dp/g$. Some meteorological conventions divide by dry-air mass instead; the convention must be stated. A volume or mole mixing ratio instead contributes absorber moles $x\,dp/(gM)$, with background [molar mass](molar-mass.md) $M$.
