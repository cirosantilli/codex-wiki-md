# Projective joins and meets by cross products

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane](projective-plane.md)

In the [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md), represent a [projective point](projective-point.md) by a nonzero vector and a [projective line](projective-line.md) by its nonzero coefficient covector, identified with a vector in chosen coordinates. The join of distinct [projective points](projective-point.md) represented by $p,q$ has coefficient vector $p\times q$, using the [cross product](cross-product.md). It is nonzero because the representatives are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md), and its [dot product](dot-product.md) with each representative vanishes. Dually, two distinct [projective lines](projective-line.md) with coefficients $\ell,m$ meet in the [projective point](projective-point.md) represented by $\ell\times m$. Scaling an input scales the output and leaves the projective object unchanged. This makes joins, intersections, concurrency, and [collinearity](collinearity.md) exact calculations with [homogeneous coordinates](homogeneous-coordinate.md).
