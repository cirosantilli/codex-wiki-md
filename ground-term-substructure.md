# Ground-term substructure

↑ **Parent:** [Substructure of a first-order structure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md)

The values of all [closed terms](closed-term.md) in a [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) form a [substructure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) when the [first-order language](first-order-language.md) has at least one constant. The constant ensures nonemptiness, and applying a [function symbol](function-symbol.md) to closed-term values again gives a closed-term value. Relations are restricted from the ambient [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md). Every [universal sentence](universal-sentence.md) true in the ambient [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) remains true in this [substructure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md).
