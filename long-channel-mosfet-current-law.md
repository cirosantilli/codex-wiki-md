# Long-channel MOSFET current law

↑ **Parent:** [MOSFET](mosfet.md)

For an n-channel [MOSFET](mosfet.md), assume a gradual channel, constant [charge carrier mobility](charge-carrier-mobility.md) $\mu$, gate capacitance per area $C_{\rm ox}$, width $W$ and length $L$. With overdrive $V_{\rm ov}=V_{GS}-V_T>0$, the inversion-sheet charge magnitude is $C_{\rm ox}(V_{\rm ov}-V(x))$. The drift-current relation $I_D=\mu WC_{\rm ox}(V_{\rm ov}-V)V'$ is constant along the channel. Integrating from $V=0$ to $V_{DS}$ over length $L$ proves the displayed expression for $0\leq V_{DS}\leq V_{\rm ov}$. At the drain pinch-off condition $V_{DS}=V_{\rm ov}$, it yields $I_{D,\rm sat}=\mu C_{\rm ox}WV_{\rm ov}^2/(2L)$. This ideal saturation neglects channel-length modulation and short-channel velocity saturation.
