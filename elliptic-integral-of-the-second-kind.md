# Elliptic integral of the second kind

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic integral](elliptic-integral.md)

For real modulus $0\leq k\leq1$, the displayed [integral](integral-split.md) is the incomplete elliptic integral of the second kind, with amplitude $\phi$. The modulus convention matters: some sources instead use the parameter $m=k^2$. The [arc length](arc-length.md) of an [ellipse](ellipse.md) reduces to this [integral](integral-split.md), because a trigonometric parametrization gives a [square root](square-root.md) of $1-k^2\sin^2\theta$. At $k=0$ it reduces to $\phi$; at $k=1$ the real integrand is $|\cos\theta|$.
