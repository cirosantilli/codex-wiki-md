# Tensor index symmetrization

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor](tensor.md)

Symmetrization over $r$ selected [tensor](tensor.md) indices is the average over all $r!$ permutations, with every sign positive. Parentheses denote this operation, for example $T_{(ab)}=(T_{ab}+T_{ba})/2$. It is a [projection](projection-linear-algebra.md) onto the symmetric part and commutes with a [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) acting on indices outside the parentheses.
