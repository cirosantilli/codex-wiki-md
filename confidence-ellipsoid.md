# Confidence ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Confidence region](confidence-region.md)

An ellipsoidal [confidence region](confidence-region.md) defined by a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) $A$ and a calibrated threshold $c$. For a known-covariance [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) sample, $A=n\Sigma^{-1}$ and the $1-\alpha$ quantile of the [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $d$ [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md) give exact coverage for the $d$-dimensional mean.
