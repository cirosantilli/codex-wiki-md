# Geostrophic adjustment of a surface-height jump

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic adjustment](geostrophic-adjustment.md)

A constant-depth layer initially at rest with a step in its [free surface](free-surface.md) adjusts by radiating [inertia-gravity waves](inertia-gravity-wave.md). Conservation of linearized [shallow-water potential vorticity](shallow-water-potential-vorticity.md) leaves the bounded balanced profile $\eta_s=\eta_0\operatorname{sgn}(x)(1-e^{-|x|/R_D})$, where $R_D=\sqrt{gH}/|f|$ is the [Rossby deformation radius](rossby-deformation-radius.md). The released [potential energy](potential-energy.md) per unit transverse length is $3\rho g\eta_0^2R_D/2$; the final [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $\rho g\eta_0^2R_D/2$, leaving $\rho g\eta_0^2R_D$ in radiated waves.
