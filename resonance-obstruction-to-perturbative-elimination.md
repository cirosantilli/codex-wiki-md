# Resonance obstruction to perturbative elimination

↑ **Parent:** [Schrieffer-Wolff transformation](schrieffer-wolff-transformation.md)

A perturbative generator dividing by an unperturbed energy difference cannot eliminate a nonzero coupling between exactly degenerate states. Those states must remain in the retained degenerate block. Small denominators also invalidate an expansion when the actual hopping [matrix](matrix.md) element, including occupation enhancement, is not small in comparison.
