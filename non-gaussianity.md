# Non-Gaussianity

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Gaussianity)

[Non-Gaussianity](non-gaussianity.md) means a departure from Gaussian statistics of a physical quantity or random field. Higher connected correlation functions describe such departures. [Primordial non-Gaussianity](primordial-non-gaussianity.md) concerns the initial cosmological perturbations.
