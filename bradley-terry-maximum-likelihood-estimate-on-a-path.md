# Bradley-Terry maximum-likelihood estimate on a path

↑ **Parent:** [Bradley-Terry maximum-likelihood estimate on a comparison tree](bradley-terry-maximum-likelihood-estimate-on-a-comparison-tree.md)

For observed neighbor win fractions $p_0,\ldots,p_{n-1}\in(0,1)$ on the [path graph](path-graph.md) $0,1,\ldots,n$, the normalized [Bradley-Terry model](bradley-terry-model.md) estimate with $\widehat\theta_n=1$ is $\widehat\theta_i=\prod_{k=i}^{n-1}p_k/(1-p_k)$. This is backward propagation of empirical odds along the path. Every edge proportion is fitted exactly, and strict concavity in edge log-ratios proves that this is the unique global maximum.
