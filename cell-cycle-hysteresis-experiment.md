# Cell-cycle hysteresis experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md)

A 2003 study split a [Xenopus laevis egg extract](xenopus-laevis-egg-extract.md) into portions with different histories. One received [calcium](calcium.md) to destroy endogenous [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) before receiving nondegradable [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md); the other received nondegradable [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) first, then [calcium](calcium.md). The same sustained [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) dose was approached from low or high [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) activity. A [phosphorimager](phosphorimager.md) quantified [histone H1](histone-h1.md) [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md), sampling every $15$–$30$ minutes until an apparent stationary response. At $45$–$60\,\mathrm{nM}$, the dose maintained high activity but failed to induce it from the low state.[pp. 3–4, Figure 3a–c: history protocols and H1 readout; p. 6, Methods: phosphorimaging](building-a-cell-cycle-oscillator-hysteresis-and-bistability-in-the-activation-of-cdc2-by-joseph-r-pomerening-and-others.md)

Controls tracked labeled endogenous [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) disappearance after [calcium](calcium.md) treatment and persistence of nondegradable [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md), addressing unequal remaining input. The comparison supports [hysteresis](hysteresis.md) under extract conditions; finite observations cannot establish indefinite persistence. It does not isolate each reinforcing path or establish thresholds in intact [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md).[p. 3, control discussion; p. 4, Figure 3h; reported 60–90 minute approach to apparent steady state](building-a-cell-cycle-oscillator-hysteresis-and-bistability-in-the-activation-of-cdc2-by-joseph-r-pomerening-and-others.md)
