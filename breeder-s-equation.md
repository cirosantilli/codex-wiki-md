<h1 id="breeder-s-equation">Breeder's equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

A one-trait short-term response model $R=h^2S$, where $R$ is the inherited change in mean, $S$ the phenotypic selection differential and $h^2$ narrow-sense [heritability](heritability.md). It assumes an appropriate additive inheritance model and does not turn environmental phenotype change into inherited response.
