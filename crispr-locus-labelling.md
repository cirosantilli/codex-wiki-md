# CRISPR locus labelling

↑ **Parent:** [CRISPR genome editing](crispr-genome-editing.md)

Sequence-targeted fluorescent localization of chromosomal sites using a nuclease-inactive targeting system. Colocalization with a [protein](protein-split.md) is evidence of shared location, not necessarily direct molecular contact.
