# Kaplansky density theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann double commutant theorem](von-neumann-double-commutant-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaplansky_density_theorem)

For a [unital](unital-algebra.md) [star-subalgebra](star-subalgebra.md) $A\subseteq B(H)$, its [unit ball](unit-ball.md) is dense in the [unit ball](unit-ball.md) of $A''$ in the [strong operator topology](strong-operator-topology.md). The same holds for [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) unit balls and for positive unit balls, and the full unit balls may be approximated strongly together with their adjoints. Passing first to the [norm](norm.md) [closure](closure-topology.md) allows [continuous functional calculus](continuous-functional-calculus.md); [resolvent](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) identities prove strong [continuity](continuous-function.md) of continuous functions vanishing at infinity, even for unbounded approximating nets of [self-adjoint operators](self-adjoint-operator.md). A cutoff function supplies the [norm](norm.md) bound, and a two-by-two [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) [matrix](matrix.md) removes the [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) restriction.
