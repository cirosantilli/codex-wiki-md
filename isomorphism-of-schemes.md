# Isomorphism of schemes

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md)

An isomorphism of schemes is a [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) admitting a two-sided inverse morphism. It is simultaneously a homeomorphism of the underlying spaces and an isomorphism of their structure sheaves.
