# Dolev-Yao model

↑ **Parent:** [Cryptography](cryptography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolev–Yao_model)

A symbolic adversarial model of [Danny Dolev](danny-dolev.md) and [Andrew Yao](andrew-yao.md) granting control of the network while treating cryptographic operations as ideal constructors and destructors. An adversary may split unprotected tuples, replay messages and encrypt with known [public keys](public-key.md), but cannot decrypt without the required key. Separate encrypted components are not automatically bound to adjacent clear fields.
