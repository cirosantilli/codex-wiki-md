# Complex special linear group in dimension two

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear group](special-linear-group.md)

Complex two-by-two [matrices](matrix.md) of [determinant](determinant.md) one form a complex three-dimensional [Lie group](lie-group.md), or a real six-dimensional [Lie group](lie-group.md). Their congruence action on Hermitian two-by-two [matrices](matrix.md) gives the [Lorentz spinor double cover](lorentz-spinor-double-cover.md) with kernel $\{I,-I\}$. The two [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) representations are $A$ and $(A^\dagger)^{-1}$. Treating the group as real is essential for the second, antiholomorphic representation.
