# Vanishing-discount limit of an elimination all-pay contest

↑ **Parent:** [Backward-induction threshold in an elimination all-pay contest](backward-induction-threshold-in-an-elimination-all-pay-contest.md)

As $\delta\uparrow1$, both active players' effective prizes in every nonfinal subgame tend to the marginal valuation $v_{r+1}$. The [two-player complete-information all-pay equilibrium](two-player-complete-information-all-pay-equilibrium.md) then gives each a winning probability $1/2$. In the final stage, actual valuations determine the asymmetric winning probabilities. Taking this limit from discounted equilibria specifies the continuation selection instead of independently choosing an undiscounted game equilibrium.
