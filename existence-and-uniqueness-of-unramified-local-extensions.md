# Existence and uniqueness of unramified local extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Unramified extension](unramified-extension.md)

For a non-Archimedean [local field](local-field.md) $K$, every finite extension $\ell/k_K$ of its [residue field](residue-field.md) is realized by an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of $K$, unique up to $K$-isomorphism. Lift the irreducible polynomial of a residue generator to a monic integral polynomial and adjoin a root. Its residue degree equals its field degree, forcing ramification index one. In any other such extension the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) lifts the same simple residue root, giving an isomorphism.
