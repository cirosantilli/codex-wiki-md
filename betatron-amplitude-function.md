# Betatron amplitude function

↑ **Parent:** [Betatron oscillation](betatron-oscillation.md)

For rms geometric [beam emittance](beam-emittance.md) $\epsilon$, the transverse beam variance is $\sigma^2(s)=\epsilon\beta(s)$. At a beam waist, the angular variance is $\epsilon/\beta$. Reducing both transverse amplitude functions by a factor $a$ at fixed emittances increases the ideal head-on [collider luminosity](collider-luminosity.md) by $a$; practical finite-bunch-length and crossing-angle effects can limit this gain.
