# Cross multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Fraction](fraction.md)

For nonzero denominators, $a/b=c/d$ exactly when $ad=bc$. For positive denominators, $a/b<c/d$ exactly when $ad<bc$. This compares [fractions](fraction.md) with exact [integer arithmetic](integer-arithmetic.md), avoiding rounding from [floating-point arithmetic](floating-point-arithmetic-split.md).
