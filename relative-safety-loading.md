# Relative safety loading

↑ **Parent:** [Classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md)

If the expected claim outflow per unit time is $\lambda\mu$, the relative safety loading is $\rho=c/(\lambda\mu)-1$. Positive loading means expected premium income exceeds expected claim outflow. This is the net profit condition in the [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md).
