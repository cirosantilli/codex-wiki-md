# Differential entropy under an increasing transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Differential entropy](differential-entropy.md)

Let $g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ have finite, strictly positive [derivative](derivative.md) everywhere. Its inverse is defined on its image, and [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) gives $p_{g(X)}(g(x))=p_X(x)/g'(x)$. If $h(X)$ and $\mathbb E\log_2 g'(X)$ are finite, taking the expectation of the logarithm proves the displayed [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md) identity.
