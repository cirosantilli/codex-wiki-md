# Differential entropy under an increasing transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Differential entropy](differential-entropy.md)

Let $X$ have a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) on an open interval, and let $g$ be continuously [differentiable](differentiable-function.md), strictly increasing there, with $g'(x)>0$. The [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) gives

$$
f_{g(X)}(g(x))=\frac{f_X(x)}{g'(x)}.
$$

If the [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md) and the expected absolute [logarithm](logarithm.md) of $g'(X)$ are finite, taking the [expectation](expected-value-split.md) of the negative [logarithm](logarithm.md) proves the displayed formula, for a fixed base $b>1$. Local stretching reduces [probability density](probability-density.md); its contribution to [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md) is the expected logarithmic stretch. The map is invertible, so this change does not mean that the transformation destroyed information about $X$.
