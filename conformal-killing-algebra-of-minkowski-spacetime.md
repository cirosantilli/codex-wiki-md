# Conformal Killing algebra of Minkowski spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal Killing vector field](conformal-killing-vector-field.md)

In [Cartesian coordinates](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) on four-dimensional [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), all [Conformal Killing vector fields](conformal-killing-vector-field.md) have the form

$$
X^\mu=a^\mu+\omega^\mu{}_{\nu}x^\nu+cx^\mu+2(b\cdot x)x^\mu-x^2b^\mu,
\qquad \omega_{\mu\nu}=-\omega_{\nu\mu},\qquad x^2=\eta_{\mu\nu}x^\mu x^\nu.
$$

Their [conformal Killing factor](conformal-killing-factor.md) is $c+2b\cdot x$. There are four [translation](translation-geometry.md) generators, six [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) generators, one [dilation](uniform-dilation.md) generator, and four [special conformal transformation](special-conformal-transformation.md) generators. These form a fifteen-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) under the [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md).

For completeness, differentiate the [Conformal Killing equation](conformal-killing-equation.md) in three index orders to obtain

$$
\partial_\mu\partial_\nu X_\rho
=\eta_{\nu\rho}\partial_\mu\lambda+\eta_{\mu\rho}\partial_\nu\lambda-\eta_{\mu\nu}\partial_\rho\lambda.
$$

Its [divergence](divergence.md) gives $2\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\lambda=-\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box\lambda$; its [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md) gives $\Box\lambda=0$. Hence $\lambda$ is an [affine function](affine-function.md). Subtracting the displayed quadratic and [dilation](uniform-dilation.md) terms leaves a [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field-split.md) whose second [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) vanish, proving completeness of the list. The [vector fields](vector-field.md) are globally smooth, but some [special conformal transformations](special-conformal-transformation.md) have incomplete flows on affine [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md); the [dilation](uniform-dilation.md) flow $x\mapsto e^s x$ is global.
