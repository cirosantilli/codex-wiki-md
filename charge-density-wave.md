# Charge density wave

↑ **Parent:** [Condensed matter physics](condensed-matter-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charge_density_wave)

A periodic modulation of electronic [charge density](charge-density.md), often coupled to a lattice distortion. In a one-dimensional metal, a [Peierls distortion](peierls-distortion.md) with $Q=2k_F$ can open a gap at opposite [Fermi points](fermi-point.md), lowering the occupied electron [energy](energy-split.md) at an elastic energy cost. A charge modulation and a [spin density wave](spin-density-wave.md) are distinct: the latter can modulate spin while leaving the total charge nearly uniform.
