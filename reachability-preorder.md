# Reachability preorder

↑ **Parent:** [Preorder](preorder.md)

Reachability in a [directed graph](directed-graph.md), allowing length-zero paths, is a [preorder](preorder.md): zero steps give reflexivity and concatenation gives transitivity. Distinct mutually reachable vertices violate [antisymmetry](antisymmetry.md). Its equivalence classes under mutual reachability are the [strongly connected components](strongly-connected-component.md); the induced reachability relation on those classes is a [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md). Equivalently, contracting the classes gives the acyclic [condensation graph](condensation-graph.md).
