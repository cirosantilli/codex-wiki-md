# Alpha tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha effect](alpha-effect.md)

The alpha tensor is the linear map from a uniform test [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) to the corresponding [mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md). Its components satisfy $\mathcal E_i=\alpha_{ij}B_{0j}$. Anisotropic helical flows can produce a real symmetric rank-two response, such as $\alpha_0\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$.
