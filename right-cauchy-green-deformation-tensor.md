# Right Cauchy-Green deformation tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md)

The right Cauchy-Green tensor measures stretch on the [reference configuration](reference-configuration.md). Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are the same squared [principal stretches](principal-stretch.md) as those of the [left Cauchy-Green deformation tensor](left-cauchy-green-deformation-tensor.md), but its [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) are reference directions.
