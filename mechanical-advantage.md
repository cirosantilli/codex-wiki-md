# Mechanical advantage

↑ **Parent:** [Simple machine](simple-machine.md)

The ratio of the useful load [force](force-split.md) to the applied effort [force](force-split.md). For an ideal [simple machine](simple-machine.md) in slow motion, the ratio of effort displacement to load displacement gives the same number by conservation of [work](work.md).
