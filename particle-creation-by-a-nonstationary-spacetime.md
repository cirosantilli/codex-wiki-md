# Particle creation by a nonstationary spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory in curved spacetime](quantum-field-theory-in-curved-spacetime.md)

Positive-frequency modes in stationary asymptotic past and future regions can differ by a [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) mixing positive and negative frequency solutions. The conserved [Klein-Gordon inner product](klein-gordon-inner-product.md) gives its canonical normalization. The out annihilation operator then contains in creation operators, yielding the displayed number expectation in the initial vacuum. A nonzero mixing coefficient changes the particle content relative to final observers without violating deterministic field evolution.
