# Advection

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advection)

Advection transports a quantity with a [velocity field](velocity-field.md). For a scalar field $f$, its local advective rate is $\mathbf u\mathbin\cdot\nabla f$, the spatial contribution to the [material derivative](material-derivative.md). The same term transports the components of a vector field.
