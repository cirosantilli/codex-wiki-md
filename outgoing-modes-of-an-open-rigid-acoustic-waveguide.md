# Outgoing modes of an open rigid acoustic waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic waveguide](acoustic-waveguide.md)

For an even field between rigid plates at $y=\pm b$, the transverse modes are $\cos(n\pi y/b)$, with longitudinal wave numbers $k_n=\sqrt{k_0^2-(n\pi/b)^2}$. With time dependence $e^{i\omega t}$, outgoing modes into $x>0$ use $e^{-ik_nx}$: choose positive real $k_n$ for cut-on modes and negative imaginary $k_n$ for cut-off modes. A lower-half-plane [Fourier inversion](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) extracts these modes by [residues](residue.md). Their decaying components are [evanescent waves](evanescent-wave.md), not backward-propagating waves. At an exact cutoff one takes the radiation continuation before its limit.
