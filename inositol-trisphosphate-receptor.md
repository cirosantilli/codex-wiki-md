# Inositol trisphosphate receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Inositol trisphosphate](inositol-trisphosphate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inositol_trisphosphate_receptor)

An [IP3 receptor](inositol-trisphosphate-receptor.md) is an intracellular [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md)-gated [ion channel](ion-channel.md), rather than the extracellular signal's [cell-surface receptor](cell-surface-receptor.md). Its opening is regulated by both [IP3](inositol-trisphosphate.md) and cytosolic [calcium](calcium.md): [IP3](inositol-trisphosphate.md) binding is not a command that fixes one constant release rate. Mammalian [IP3 receptors](inositol-trisphosphate-receptor.md) have three subtypes, with possible mixed assemblies and splice variants; these need not have identical regulation or cellular roles. The 2010 authored account distinguishes established channel behavior from then-unresolved structural explanations.[Taylor and Tovey, IP3 Receptors: Toward Understanding Their Activation, Abstract; regulation by cytosolic Ca2+ and Structural Determinants of IP3R Activation: three subtypes and tetrameric assemblies](ip3-receptors-toward-understanding-their-activation-by-colin-w-taylor-and-stephen-c-tovey.md)
