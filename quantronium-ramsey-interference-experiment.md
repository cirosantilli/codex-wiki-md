# Quantronium Ramsey interference experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey interferometry](ramsey-interferometry.md)

The 2002 experiment described in [Manipulating the Quantum State of an Electrical Circuit](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0205343) demonstrates [Ramsey interferometry](ramsey-interferometry.md) in a circuit using [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md). An aluminum island between two small [Josephson junctions](josephson-junction.md) and a larger readout [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md) formed the loop; a [dilution refrigerator](dilution-refrigerator.md) cooled it. Manipulation used the charge/flux bias point least sensitive to first-order fluctuations. [Microwave](microwave.md) pulse amplitude and duration were calibrated using [Rabi oscillations](rabi-cycle.md). Two phase-coherent $\pi/2$ pulses then enclosed a variable free interval. A current pulse switched the large [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md) to finite [voltage](voltage.md) with state-dependent [probability](probability.md); a preamplifier and threshold discriminator counted repeated trials.[Manipulating the Quantum State of an Electrical Circuit, PDF pp. 1–4, Fig. 1 and Fig. 3; pulse calibration immediately following Fig. 3](manipulating-the-quantum-state-of-an-electrical-circuit-by-denis-vion-and-others.md)

As a readout control, switching was tested against pulse height at [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md). The [Ramsey interferometry](ramsey-interferometry.md) oscillation period agreed with inverse [frequency](frequency.md) detuning, supporting relative [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) accumulation against the external oscillator. The decaying envelope indicated finite [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md), while reduced contrast was attributed provisionally to population relaxation during readout. This supports controlled coherent evolution over a finite interval, without establishing perfect isolation or identifying one exclusive noise mechanism.[Manipulating the Quantum State of an Electrical Circuit, PDF p. 2 readout test; Fig. 3B and PDF p. 4 Ramsey fringes and contrast discussion](manipulating-the-quantum-state-of-an-electrical-circuit-by-denis-vion-and-others.md)
