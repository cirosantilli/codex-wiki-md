# Bridging hydrogen

↑ **Parent:** [Multicentre bonding](multicentre-bonding.md)

A [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) associated with more than one skeletal centre. Removal as a [proton](proton.md) must be distinguished from [hydride](hydride.md) transfer; the cluster charge and [electron](electron.md) count change differently in the two operations.
