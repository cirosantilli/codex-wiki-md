# Transit-timing variation

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit photometry](exoplanet-transit-photometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transit-timing_variation)

A departure of transit arrival times from a constant-period ephemeris can reveal gravitational interactions with other bodies. Dynamical fits may constrain an [exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md) mass independently of transit depth, providing a mass to combine with radius for [exoplanet interior](exoplanet-interior.md) models.
