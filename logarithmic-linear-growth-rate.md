# Logarithmic linear growth rate

↑ **Parent:** [Linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md)

For scale-independent linear growth $\delta(\mathbf k,a)=D(a)\delta_{\rm ini}(\mathbf k)$, the [logarithmic linear growth rate](logarithmic-linear-growth-rate.md) measures fractional amplification per logarithmic change of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md). In an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), $D\propto a$ and $f=1$. The [cosmological Poisson equation](cosmological-poisson-equation.md) for conserved [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) gives $\psi\propto D/a$ at fixed [comoving coordinate](comoving-coordinate.md), and therefore $\psi'=\mathcal H(f-1)\psi$. This relates suppression of matter growth to the [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md).
