# Real matrices have real minimal polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md)

The [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) of a real [matrix](matrix.md), initially computed over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), has real coefficients. Its coefficientwise [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) is also a [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) of the same degree annihilating the [matrix](matrix.md). Uniqueness of the [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) forces the two [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) to agree.
