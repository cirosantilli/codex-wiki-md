# Gravitational potential producing a circular hanging chain

↑ **Parent:** [Isoperimetric constraint](isoperimetric-constraint.md)

For uniform [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\mu$, a chain minimizing $\mu\int\Phi(z)\,ds$ at fixed length has [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md) $(\mu\Phi-\lambda)/\sqrt{1+z'^2}=C$. In the domain $z<h$, the displayed potential has downward [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $-\Phi'(z)=-\kappa/(h-z)^2$. A circular arc $z=h-\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$ satisfies the identity because $\sqrt{1+z'^2}=a/(h-z)$.

This arc is a global minimum among admissible competitors of the same length with the same endpoints in $z<h$. Set $w=h-z>0$. The variable part of the energy is $\mu\kappa\int ds/w$, a [hyperbolic length](hyperbolic-length-in-the-poincare-disc.md) in the [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md). The [calibration for semicircular hyperbolic geodesics](calibration-for-semicircular-hyperbolic-geodesics.md) $S=\operatorname{arsinh}(x/w)$ has $|\nabla S|=1/w$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\int ds/w\geq S(B)-S(A)$, with equality along the circle traversed with increasing $x$. The constant-potential part is unchanged by the fixed-length constraint.
