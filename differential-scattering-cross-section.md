# Differential scattering cross-section

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering.md)

The differential scattering cross-section is the effective incident area scattered per unit [solid angle](solid-angle.md). It is defined by the ratio of the outgoing particle rate through $d\Omega$ to the incident flux.
