# Vascular plant

↑ **Parent:** [Plant](plant.md)

A land plant with the specialized conducting tissues [xylem](xylem.md) and [phloem](phloem.md). An independent, usually conspicuous [sporophyte](sporophyte.md) supports transport and growth; the ecological requirements of the [gametophyte](gametophyte.md) still matter for completing reproduction.
