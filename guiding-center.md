# Guiding center

↑ **Parent:** [Charged particle in a uniform magnetic field](charged-particle-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guiding_center)

The guiding center is the center of a charged particle's circular cyclotron orbit. In a nonuniform field it drifts slowly compared with the rapid [cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md).
