# First strain invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Left Cauchy-Green deformation tensor](left-cauchy-green-deformation-tensor.md)

The first principal invariant of the [left Cauchy-Green deformation tensor](left-cauchy-green-deformation-tensor.md) is the sum of the squares of the [principal stretches](principal-stretch.md). Since $B=FF^T$, it also equals $\sum_{i,I}F_{iI}^2$. Its derivative with respect to the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) is $2F$. For a [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) $W=w(I_1)$ with [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md), the [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) is $T=2w'(I_1)B-pI$.
