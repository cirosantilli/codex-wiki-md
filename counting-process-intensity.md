# Counting-process intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Counting process](counting-process.md)

A predictable intensity $\lambda(t)$ is the derivative of an absolutely continuous [compensator of a counting process](compensator-of-a-counting-process.md): $N(t)-\int_0^t\lambda(u)\,du$ is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). It represents the conditional event rate given the immediately preceding history. In [survival analysis](survival-analysis-split.md) the individual intensity is an at-risk indicator multiplied by its [hazard function](hazard-function.md).
