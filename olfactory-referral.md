# Olfactory referral

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor perception](flavor-perception.md)

Attribution or localization of an odor contribution to an object or event, especially food in the mouth. Mouth localization does not entail gustatory receptor transduction. Controlled route, [attention](attention.md) and rating manipulations are needed to distinguish localization, quality and verbal labeling.
