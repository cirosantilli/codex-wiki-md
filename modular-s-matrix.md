# Modular S-matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Modular tensor category](modular-tensor-category.md)

The [matrix](matrix.md) of [Hopf link](hopf-link.md) evaluations divided by the [total quantum dimension](total-quantum-dimension.md) $\mathcal D$. In a unitary [modular tensor category](modular-tensor-category.md) it is a symmetric [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md), with $S_{1a}=d_a/\mathcal D$ and $S_{\bar a b}=S_{ab}^*$. Its columns diagonalize the [anyon fusion matrices](anyon-fusion-matrix.md) through the [Verlinde formula](verlinde-formula.md).
