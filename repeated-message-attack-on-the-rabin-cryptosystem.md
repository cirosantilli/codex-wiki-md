# Repeated-message attack on the Rabin cryptosystem

↑ **Parent:** [Rabin cryptosystem](rabin-cryptosystem.md)

For coprime moduli $N,N'$ and $0<m<\min(N,N')$, the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) recovers the integer $m^2$ uniquely in $[0,NN')$ from the two ciphertexts. An ordinary integer square root then recovers the message. If distinct semiprime moduli share a factor, their greatest common divisor instead reveals factors of both moduli. Learning one message in the coprime case does not by itself give a general factoring algorithm or a decryption key for other ciphertexts.
