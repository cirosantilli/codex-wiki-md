# Near-field diffraction laboratory experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md)

A documented experiment uses curved illumination rather than the uniform [Fresnel slit propagation](fresnel-slit-propagation.md) model. [Laboratory Exercise No. 4: Diffraction, MIT 6.237, Fall 2024](laboratory-exercise-no-4-diffraction-mit-6-237-fall-2024.md), printed pp. 5–6, gives a helium–neon [laser](laser-split.md) at $632.8\,\mathrm{nm}$, a microscope objective [optical lens](lens-optics.md), a circular [aperture](aperture.md) and a screen 5–40 cm from the object. Preparation includes comparing central brightness in [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md) and [Fraunhofer diffraction](fraunhofer-diffraction.md). A 40× objective produces a nonplanar incident [wavefront](wavefront.md); the opening is placed less than 1 cm from it. With objective-to-screen distance fixed, students move the opening, sketch patterns, and record positions of consecutive central nulls to estimate aperture size. The [Fraunhofer diffraction](fraunhofer-diffraction.md) pattern supplies a regime comparison. A separate spherical-obstacle task observes a bright centre inside the shadow. The handout supplies procedures, rather than measured distances or profiles.[p. 1 introduction; p. 2 PL4.1(d); p. 5 §B and Fig. 3; p. 6 §§4.9–4.10: wavelength, apparatus, fixed-distance comparison and readout](laboratory-exercise-no-4-diffraction-mit-6-237-fall-2024.md)

The circular-opening test probes cancellation of [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md) at the centre while the source [wavefront](wavefront.md) curvature changes. To interpret it, include that curvature in the aperture field $U_0$ and use a circular [aperture](aperture.md), rather than substituting the plane-illuminated [Fresnel slit propagation](fresnel-slit-propagation.md) profile. Useful additional controls are to retain the same [laser](laser-split.md) and opening, centre the illumination, repeat position readings and check background with the beam blocked. If a camera replaces the visual screen readout, avoid saturation and calibrate its position scale. Alignment, imperfect aperture edges, illumination variation and limited contrast near a null constrain the inferred size. A bright central obstacle spot illustrates why the sharp shadows of [geometrical optics](geometrical-optics-split.md) can fail; it does not imply that every [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md) pattern has a bright centre.
