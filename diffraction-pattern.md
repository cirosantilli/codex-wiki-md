# Diffraction pattern

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction](diffraction.md)

The spatial distribution of [intensity](intensity-physics.md) produced by interference of a wave passing an aperture or obstacle. A circular aperture's far-field point-source pattern is an [Airy disk](airy-disk.md); a [Rayleigh criterion](rayleigh-criterion.md) comparison superposes the intensities of two mutually incoherent sources.
