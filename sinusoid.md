# Sinusoid

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)

A sinusoid is a function $A\cos(\omega t+\phi)$, where $A$ is its amplitude, $\omega$ its angular frequency and $\phi$ its phase. For $\omega\ne0$ its period is $2\pi/|\omega|$. A uniformly randomized phase can produce a [strictly stationary process](strictly-stationary-process.md) even though individual paths look periodic.
