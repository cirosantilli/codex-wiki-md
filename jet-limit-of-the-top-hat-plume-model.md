# Jet limit of the top-hat plume model

↑ **Parent:** [Top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md)

With zero source [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) and zero [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) but positive source [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md), the axisymmetric [top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md) reduces to a turbulent round jet. For full-cross-section kinematic fluxes, put $c=2\alpha_e\sqrt\pi$. [Entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md) gives $Q'=c\sqrt M$, while momentum conservation gives $M=M_0$. Hence $Q=c\sqrt{M_0}z$, speed $w=\sqrt{M_0}/(cz)$ and radius $r=cz/\sqrt\pi$. A finite source flux replaces $z$ by $z+Q_0/(c\sqrt{M_0})$. The ideal zero-flux source has singular speed and is a far-field model, not a finite nozzle.
