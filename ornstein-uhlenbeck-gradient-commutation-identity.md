# Ornstein-Uhlenbeck gradient commutation identity

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup](ornstein-uhlenbeck-semigroup.md)

Differentiating the [Mehler formula for the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup](mehler-formula-for-the-ornstein-uhlenbeck-semigroup.md) proves the identity for continuously differentiable functions with bounded [derivative](derivative.md). Density extends it to the Gaussian Sobolev form domain. Together with a weighted [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), it yields the [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck entropy dissipation identity](ornstein-uhlenbeck-entropy-dissipation-identity.md) proof of the [Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality](gaussian-logarithmic-sobolev-inequality.md).
