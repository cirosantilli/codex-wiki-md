# Type IIA superstring theory

↑ **Parent:** [Type II string theory](type-ii-string-theory.md)

A closed [superstring theory](superstring-theory.md) whose left and right [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md) projections have opposite ten-dimensional Majorana-Weyl chiralities. It has 32 real spacetime supercharges and is nonchiral. Its massless fields form [type IIA supergravity](type-iia-supergravity.md); the RR gauge potentials have odd degrees, including a one-form and a three-form.
