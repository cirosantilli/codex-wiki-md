# Convection zone

↑ **Parent:** [Convection](convection.md)

A stellar [convection zone](convection-zone.md) is a region where buoyant fluid motion transports a significant part of the stellar heat flux. Rising and sinking parcels carry enthalpy, so heat transport is not entirely radiative. The [Sun](sun.md)'s outer [convection zone](convection-zone.md) extends from near $0.7R_\odot$ to the surface and overlies its radiative interior. The [tachocline](tachocline.md) lies near this transition, and the [convection](convection.md) helps power the [solar dynamo](solar-dynamo.md).
