# 4-thiouridine

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)

A sulfur-containing [uridine](uridine.md) analogue used in [metabolic RNA labelling](metabolic-rna-labelling.md). Its incorporation and chemical conversion allow labelled molecules to be distinguished from older [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md).
