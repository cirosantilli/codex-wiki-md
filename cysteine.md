# Cysteine

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid composition](amino-acid-composition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cysteine)

[Cysteine](cysteine.md) is an [amino acid](amino-acid.md) whose [amino acid side chain](amino-acid-side-chain.md) contains [sulfur](sulfur.md), represented by Cys or C for an incorporated [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md). Two [cysteine](cysteine.md) positions can be joined through a [protein disulfide bond](protein-disulfide-bond.md). See [IUPAC Blue Book, P-103.1: cysteine's structure and symbols](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/P10.html) and [Cooper: cysteine crosslinks in proteins](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9879/). The C in a sequence means [cysteine](cysteine.md), whereas the C-end label in the toy diagram means [C-terminus](c-terminus.md).
