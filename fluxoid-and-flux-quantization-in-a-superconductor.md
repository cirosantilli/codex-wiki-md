# Fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic flux quantum](magnetic-flux-quantum.md)

For a nonvanishing superconducting [order parameter](order-parameter.md) $\psi=\sqrt{n_s}e^{i\theta}$, $j=(qn_s/m)(\hbar\nabla\theta-qA)$. Single-valuedness gives phase winding $2\pi N$ around a closed curve, hence the [fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor](fluxoid-and-flux-quantization-in-a-superconductor.md) identity above. Magnetic flux alone is $Nh/q$ when the current integral vanishes. If the entire spanning surface is in a region where $D\psi=0$, the field vanishes there and the winding is zero; nonzero quantized flux requires a hole or a vortex core where that condition does not hold.
