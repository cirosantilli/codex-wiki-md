# Thermodynamic activity

↑ **Parent:** [Standard state](standard-state.md)

The thermodynamic activity is the dimensionless quantity defined by $\mu_i=\mu_i^\circ+RT\ln a_i$, where $R$ is the [molar gas constant](molar-gas-constant.md). For an ideal dilute solution with a specified [concentration](concentration.md) reference, $a_i=c_i/c^\circ$; departures from ideality are represented by an [activity coefficient](activity-coefficient.md) multiplying this ratio.
