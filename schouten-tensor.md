# Schouten tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md)

For dimension $n\geq3$, the Schouten tensor packages the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) and [scalar curvature](scalar-curvature.md) in the trace part of the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md). In dimension three it is $S_{ij}=R_{ij}-Rg_{ij}/4$, and the full [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) is reconstructed from it.
