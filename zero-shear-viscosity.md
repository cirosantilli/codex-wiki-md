# Zero-shear viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md)

For an integrable [relaxation modulus](relaxation-modulus.md), a small constant shear rate sustained from the remote past gives stress equal to that rate times the displayed integral, including any instantaneous solvent mass. For the linear [Oldroyd-B model](oldroyd-b-model.md) it is $\mu_0+G_0\tau$. A nondecaying elastic modulus would make this integral infinite rather than describe a fluid with finite steady viscosity.
