# Carrier compensation and even band filling in a square lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Band filling](band-filling.md)

With [spin degeneracy](spin-degeneracy.md) two, one full square-zone band has density $Q^2/(2\pi^2)$. Thus $n_e-n_h=n-Q^2/(2\pi^2)$. If $Q=\alpha\sqrt{2\pi n}$, compensation requires $\alpha=\sqrt\pi$ and the real-space cell contains two electrons. A global gap is also necessary for an insulator.
