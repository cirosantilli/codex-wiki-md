# Crank-Nicolson method

↑ **Parent:** [Implicit Runge-Kutta method](implicit-runge-kutta-method.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crank–Nicolson_method)

The Crank--Nicolson method applies the [trapezoidal rule](trapezoidal-rule.md) to a semidiscrete evolution equation. For a linear system $u'=Lu$ its update is

$$
\left(I-\frac h2L\right)u^{n+1}=\left(I+\frac h2L\right)u^n.
$$
