# Fourth normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Database normalization](database-normalization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_normal_form)

A [relational schema](relational-schema.md) is in [fourth normal form](fourth-normal-form.md) if the determinant of every non-trivial [multivalued dependency](multivalued-dependency.md) is a [superkey](superkey.md). This implies [Boyce-Codd normal form](boyce-codd-normal-form.md) because every [functional dependency](functional-dependency.md) implies a [multivalued dependency](multivalued-dependency.md).
