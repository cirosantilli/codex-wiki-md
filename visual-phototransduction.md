# Visual phototransduction

↑ **Parent:** [Photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md)

Conversion of absorbed [light](light.md) into a [photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md) response. In vertebrate rods and [cone cells](cone-cell.md), activation of visual pigment changes a signaling cascade and light-sensitive membrane current, typically hyperpolarizing the cell and changing transmitter release. A receptor voltage, ganglion-cell spikes and a reported [color](color.md) are distinct measurement levels.
