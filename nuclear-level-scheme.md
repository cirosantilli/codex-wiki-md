# Nuclear level scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear structure](nuclear-structure.md)

A [nuclear level scheme](nuclear-level-scheme.md) represents nuclear [energy levels](energy-level.md) and transitions among them. [Gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) [energies](energy-split.md) constrain pairwise differences; emission counts and coincidence measurements help resolve different schemes having the same difference multiset.
