# Zinc finger

↑ **Parent:** [Protein domain](protein-domain.md)

A [protein](protein-split.md) structural motif stabilized by zinc coordination. Different zinc-[finger](finger.md) families can bind [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) or [proteins](protein-split.md); the motif alone does not establish a transcriptional role.
