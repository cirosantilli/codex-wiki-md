# Polymer-brush volume fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer brush](polymer-brush.md)

Conservation of material in a uniform [polymer brush](polymer-brush.md) gives its mean [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) as $Nv_0s/H$, with material [volume](volume.md) $v_0$ per counted segment, chain [length](length.md) $N$, [grafting density](grafting-density.md) $s$ and height $H$. Identifying $v_0$ with $a^3$ is an extra coarse-graining assumption. The [Alexander-de Gennes brush model](alexander-de-gennes-brush-model.md) then gives $\phi\sim(sa^2)^{(3\nu-1)/(2\nu)}$, including powers $2/3$ in a [good solvent](good-solvent.md) and $1/2$ in a [theta solvent](theta-solvent.md).
