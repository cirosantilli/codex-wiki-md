# Wiener-Hopf solution for an open parallel-plate waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic waveguide](acoustic-waveguide.md)

For rigid plates $x>0$, $y=\pm b$, an even incident plane wave travelling toward the opening can be scattered using [Wiener-Hopf factorization](wiener-hopf-factorization.md). With time dependence $e^{i\omega t}$ and transform $\Phi(k)=\int\phi(x)e^{ikx}dx$, take $\gamma=\sqrt{k^2-k_0^2}$ on the outgoing sheet. The common normal derivative on $y=b$ is supported on $x<0$; its transform is $D^-=iL^+(-k_0)L^-(k)/(k+k_0)$. The interior transform is $D^-\cosh(\gamma y)/(\gamma\sinh(\gamma b))$, while the exterior transform is $-D^-e^{-\gamma(|y|-b)}/\gamma$. These follow from continuity of normal velocity and the half-line pressure jump, followed by [pole subtraction in a Wiener-Hopf equation](pole-subtraction-in-a-wiener-hopf-equation.md) and a finite-energy edge condition.
