# Accidental massless radial modes beyond Goldstone modes

↑ **Parent:** [Goldstone directions in the scalar mass matrix](goldstone-directions-in-the-scalar-mass-matrix.md)

The [Goldstone theorem](goldstone-theorem.md) guarantees zero directions tangent to the vacuum orbit, not the absence of other zero eigenvalues. For $V(\varphi)=\kappa(\varphi^T\varphi-v^2)^4$, $\kappa>0$, the minima form the same sphere as in ordinary $O(n)$ breaking, but the entire [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) vanishes there. Thus all n scalar quadratic masses vanish, although only n-1 are symmetry-protected [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md). Exact Goldstone counting requires nonzero curvature transverse to the orbit.
