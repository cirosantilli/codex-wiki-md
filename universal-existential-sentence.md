# Universal-existential sentence

↑ **Parent:** [Universal sentence](universal-sentence.md)

A universal-existential sentence has a universal block followed by an existential block and a [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md). Such axioms are preserved under unions of chains of structures: the universally chosen tuple occurs in one stage, whose existential witnesses remain in the union. An [inductive first-order theory](inductive-first-order-theory.md) has an axiomatization by these sentences.
