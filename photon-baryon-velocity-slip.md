# Photon-baryon velocity slip

↑ **Parent:** [Tight-coupling approximation](tight-coupling-approximation.md)

The velocity slip is the difference between the bulk velocities of photons and baryons. [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) suppresses it at a rate $\Gamma(1+R^{-1})$, where $R=3\rho_b/(4\rho_\gamma)$ and $\Gamma=-\dot\tau>0$. Its first correction in the [tight-coupling approximation](tight-coupling-approximation.md) produces heat-conduction damping of acoustic waves.
