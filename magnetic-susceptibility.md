# Magnetic susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli paramagnetism](pauli-paramagnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_susceptibility)

Magnetic susceptibility measures the linear change of magnetization or magnetic moment with an applied magnetic field. With the relevant convention stated explicitly, $\chi=\partial M/\partial B$.
