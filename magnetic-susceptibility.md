# Magnetic susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_susceptibility)

[Magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md) describes how readily a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) changes a material's [magnetization](magnetization.md). The field variable and normalization matter: [volume magnetic susceptibility](volume-magnetic-susceptibility.md) in the [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md) is dimensionless, whereas a [derivative](derivative.md) of total [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) with respect to $B$ has units of $\mathrm{J\,T^{-2}}$. A slope measured against the applied [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md) also depends on the sample's [demagnetizing field](demagnetizing-field.md).

For an isotropic, reversible linear response through the origin, write

$$
\mathbf M=\chi_v\mathbf H_{\rm int},\qquad
\mathbf B_{\rm int}=\mu_0(\mathbf H_{\rm int}+\mathbf M)
=\mu_0(1+\chi_v)\mathbf H_{\rm int}.
$$

Here $\mathbf M$ is [magnetization](magnetization.md), or [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) per [volume](volume.md); $\mathbf H_{\rm int}$ is the internal [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md); $\mathbf B_{\rm int}$ is the internal [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md); and $\mu_0$ is the [vacuum permeability](vacuum-permeability.md). Both $M$ and $H$ have units $\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$. The [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) is $\mu_0(1+\chi_v)$. [Richard Fitzpatrick, Magnetic susceptibility and permeability, equations (866)–(868): isotropic linear response and permeability](classical-electromagnetism-an-intermediate-level-course-by-richard-fitzpatrick/magnetic-susceptibility-and-permeability.md)

The [independent-moment magnetic response](independent-moment-magnetic-response.md) below shows why one number cannot describe every field: [magnetization](magnetization.md) approaches a [saturation magnetization](saturation-magnetization.md), while its [differential magnetic susceptibility](differential-magnetic-susceptibility.md) approaches zero. [Anisotropy](anisotropy.md) requires a [magnetic susceptibility tensor](magnetic-susceptibility-tensor.md); [hysteresis](hysteresis.md) requires specifying the preparation and sweep branch as well as the bias [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), [temperature](temperature-split.md), and timescale. The [Gouy balance](gouy-balance.md) turns a small response into a measurable [force](force-split.md) without presuming that its readout is already the intrinsic [volume magnetic susceptibility](volume-magnetic-susceptibility.md).
