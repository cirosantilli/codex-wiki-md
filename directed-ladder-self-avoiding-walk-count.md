# Directed ladder self-avoiding walk count

↑ **Parent:** [Self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md)

On the [doubly infinite ladder graph](doubly-infinite-ladder-graph.md), [self-avoiding walks](self-avoiding-walk.md) from a fixed [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) using only rightward or vertical steps are in [bijection](bijection.md) with words in $R,V$ containing no consecutive $V$ symbols. Their count is $\sigma_n=F_{n+2}$, since $\sigma_0=1$, $\sigma_1=2$, and $\sigma_n=\sigma_{n-1}+\sigma_{n-2}$. Its exponential growth rate is the [golden ratio](golden-ratio.md).
