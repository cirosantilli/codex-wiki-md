# Free parallel RLC voltage decay

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel RLC circuit](parallel-rlc-circuit.md)

With no external current, [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) is $C\dot V+V/R+I_L=0$, and $L\dot I_L=V$. Differentiating gives the title equation. For $4R^2>L/C$, its real solutions are $Ae^{-t/(2RC)}\cos(\omega_dt+\phi)$ with $\omega_d^2=1/(LC)-1/(4R^2C^2)$. The original current balance fixes the compatible initial [inductor](inductor.md) current. A single [complex exponential](complex-exponential-function.md) represents a physical [voltage](voltage.md) through its [real part](real-part.md); general complex solutions require both conjugate characteristic roots.
