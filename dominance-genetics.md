# Dominance (genetics)

↑ **Parent:** [Phenotype](phenotype.md)

The relationship between the [phenotype](phenotype.md) of a [heterozygote](heterozygote.md) and those of the two corresponding [homozygotes](homozygote.md) at a [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md). Under [complete dominance](complete-dominance.md), one [allele](allele.md) gives the same [phenotype](phenotype.md) in the [heterozygote](heterozygote.md) as in its [homozygote](homozygote.md); the term describes expression rather than the frequency or evolutionary advantage of the [allele](allele.md).
