# Integral homology

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)

Integral homology is [homology](homology-split.md) with coefficients in $\mathbb Z$. It retains both the free and [torsion](torsion-subgroup.md) parts of each homology group.
