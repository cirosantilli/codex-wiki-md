# Mobility edge

↑ **Parent:** [Anderson localization](anderson-localization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobility_edge)

A [mobility edge](mobility-edge.md) separates localized from extended [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) on the [energy](energy-split.md) axis. Three-dimensional disordered models can have both. In the infinite one-dimensional [tight-binding model](tight-binding.md) with independent continuous random site [energies](energy-split.md), all states are localized for any nonzero disorder, with probability one. This statement excludes specially correlated disorder.[Local Moments and Localized States, pp.395–396, mobility edge and one-dimensional localization; institutional mirror as above](local-moments-and-localized-states-by-philip-warren-anderson.md)

A [mobility edge](mobility-edge.md) is not a [band gap](band-gap.md): states can exist on both sides of $E_c$, but differ in spatial extent. For [electrons](electron.md), the location of the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) relative to a [mobility edge](mobility-edge.md) matters for low-[temperature](temperature-split.md) [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md). A three-dimensional sample need not have an extended interval at every disorder strength. In particular, the finite one-dimensional calculations below neither locate a three-dimensional [mobility edge](mobility-edge.md) nor establish a nonzero universal critical disorder.
