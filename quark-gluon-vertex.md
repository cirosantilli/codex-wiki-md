# Quark-gluon vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)

Expanding $\bar q i\gamma^\mu D_\mu q$ gives a vertex joining a [quark](quark.md), its conjugate line and a [gluon](gluon.md). In the convention $\mathcal L_{\rm int}=-g_s\bar q\gamma^\mu T^aqA_\mu^a$, its rule is the displayed matrix. The [gluon](gluon.md) changes the [color charge](color-charge.md) state through the generator $T^a$ but preserves the [quark flavour](quark-flavor.md). With distinct flavours in $q\bar q\to q'\bar q'$, this permits the tree-level annihilation graph but not a flavour-changing exchange along one [quark](quark.md) line.
