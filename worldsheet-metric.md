# Worldsheet metric

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet](worldsheet.md)

The intrinsic Lorentzian metric in the [Polyakov action](polyakov-action.md) is independent of the [string embedding map](string-embedding-map.md). Its equation relates it conformally to the [induced worldsheet metric](induced-worldsheet-metric.md). A [Weyl transformation](weyl-transformation.md) changes the intrinsic metric without changing the classical action.
