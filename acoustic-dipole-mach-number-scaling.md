# Acoustic dipole Mach-number scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic dipole](acoustic-dipole.md)

For a small body of size $l$ in flow of speed $u$, force of order $\rho_0u^2l^2$ varying on time $l/u$ produces an [acoustic dipole](acoustic-dipole.md) density amplitude of the displayed order, where $M=u/c_0$ is the [Mach number](mach-number.md). Its sound power scales as $\rho_0u^6l^2/c_0^3$. The corresponding [compact acoustic quadrupole Mach-number scaling](compact-acoustic-quadrupole-mach-number-scaling.md) has density amplitude of order $(l/r)M^4$ and power $\rho_0u^8l^2/c_0^5$.
