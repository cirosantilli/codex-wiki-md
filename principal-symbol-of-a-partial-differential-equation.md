# Principal symbol of a partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Quasilinear partial differential equation](quasilinear-partial-differential-equation.md)

For a scalar differential operator of order $k$, the principal symbol replaces each order-$k$ derivative $\partial^\alpha$ by $\xi^\alpha$ and discards lower-order terms. For a [quasilinear partial differential equation](quasilinear-partial-differential-equation.md), the coefficients are evaluated at the prescribed lower-order data.
