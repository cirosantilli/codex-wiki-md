# Four-subspace quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Subspace quiver](subspace-quiver.md)

The [four-subspace quiver](four-subspace-quiver.md) has four sources with arrows into one sink. Four one-dimensional sources mapped to the lines $ke_1$, $ke_2$, $k(e_1+e_2)$ and $k(\lambda e_1+e_2)$ in $k^2$ give a [brick module](brick-module.md) for every $\lambda$. Preserving the first three lines forces scalar endomorphisms. Its [Tits form of a quiver](tits-form-of-a-quiver.md) is zero, so the [Ringel form](ringel-form.md) gives one-dimensional self-extensions.
