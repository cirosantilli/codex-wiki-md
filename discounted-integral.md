# Discounted integral

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)

An integral of future flows weighted by a specified [discount factor](discount-factor.md), such as $\int_0^\infty f(t+s)e^{-rs}ds$. For an exponentially growing flow, convergence requires the discount rate to exceed its growth rate.
