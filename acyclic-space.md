# Acyclic space

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)

A nonempty space is acyclic with the chosen coefficients if its reduced homology vanishes in every degree. Equivalently it has the [homology](homology-split.md) of a point. It need not be contractible.
