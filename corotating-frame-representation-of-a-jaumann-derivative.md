# Corotating-frame representation of a Jaumann derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Jaumann derivative](jaumann-derivative.md)

If an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) $Q$ satisfies $\dot Q=WQ$ for the [spin tensor](spin-tensor.md) $W^T=-W$, the [Jaumann derivative](jaumann-derivative.md) becomes an ordinary derivative in that frame:

$$
\frac{d}{dt}(Q^TTQ)=Q^T(\dot T-WT+TW)Q.
$$

This follows by differentiating all three factors and using $\dot Q^T=-Q^TW$. It converts a [tensor](tensor.md) relaxation law using the [Jaumann derivative](jaumann-derivative.md) into a componentwise ordinary differential equation.
