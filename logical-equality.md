# Logical equality

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

In [first-order logic](first-order-logic.md) with [logical equality](logical-equality.md), $s=t$ asserts that the terms denote the same domain element. [Logical equality](logical-equality.md) is interpreted as identity rather than as an arbitrary binary predicate. Its [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) rules include reflexivity and substitution: equal terms can replace one another in a [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md), subject to [capture-avoiding substitution](capture-avoiding-substitution.md). Double-negating [logical equality](logical-equality.md) atoms in a [Gödel-Gentzen negative translation](godel-gentzen-negative-translation.md) preserves these rules when the transported translated [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) is stable.
