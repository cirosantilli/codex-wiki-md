# Principal direction

↑ **Parent:** [Principal curvature](principal-curvature.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_direction)

A principal direction is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of the [shape operator](shape-operator.md). The [normal curvature](normal-curvature.md) in a unit principal direction is the corresponding [principal curvature](principal-curvature.md).
