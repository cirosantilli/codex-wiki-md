# Recombination fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md)

The recombination fraction is the [probability](probability.md) that a transmitted [gamete](gamete.md) carries a recombinant [haplotype](haplotype.md) between two specified [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md), relative to the parent's known [haplotype](haplotype.md) phase. Write it as $r$ here; the symbol $\theta$ is also common in [genetic linkage](genetic-linkage.md) analysis. In the ordinary symmetric two-locus model, $0\le r\le1/2$, with $r=0$ for [complete genetic linkage](complete-genetic-linkage.md) and $r=1/2$ for [independent assortment](independent-assortment.md).

For [phase-known genotype](phase-known-genotype.md) $AB/ab$, fair [Mendelian segregation](mendelian-segregation.md) and equal reciprocal [gamete](gamete.md) [probabilities](probability.md) give

$$
P(AB)=P(ab)=\frac{1-r}{2},\qquad P(Ab)=P(aB)=\frac r2.
$$

For example, 1,000 independent informative transmissions with observed counts $400,400,100,100$ in this order have empirical [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md) $(100+100)/1000=0.2$. Those illustrative counts match the [expected values](expected-value-split.md) in the toy model, but actual counts fluctuate. The [genetic linkage](genetic-linkage.md) diagram shows both the parental and recombinant classes, so their separate [probabilities](probability.md) are not confused with the total $r$.

The [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md) measures the [probability](probability.md) of an odd number of origin switches along the transmitted [chromatid](chromatid.md). It is neither the [expected value](expected-value-split.md) of the exchange count nor the [probability](probability.md) of at least one exchange: even positive counts can be invisible at the two endpoints. Converting an observed [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md) to a [genetic map](genetic-map.md) distance therefore needs an explicit model such as the [Haldane mapping function](haldane-mapping-function.md). The distinction between transmitted-[chromatid](chromatid.md) counts and four-[chromatid](chromatid.md) exchange counts is discussed in [Weeks, Tang and Kwon (2009), Casares' map function: No need for a 'corrected' Haldane's map function, Genetica 135, 305–307](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2704907/).
