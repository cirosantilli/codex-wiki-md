# Inductive impulse outside a switched solenoid

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic induction](electromagnetic-induction.md)

Changing the [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) through a long [solenoid](solenoid.md) produces an exterior azimuthal [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $E_\phi(r,t)=-\dot\Phi(t)/(2\pi r)$ by [Faraday's law of induction](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md). A charge $q$ whose displacement is negligible during the switching receives mechanical [momentum](momentum-split.md)

$$
\Delta\mathbf p=-\frac{q\,\Delta\Phi}{2\pi r}\hat{\boldsymbol\phi}.
$$

The result assumes the usual quasistatic field approximation and neglects the particle's displacement while the pulse acts. It describes an [electromagnetic induction](electromagnetic-induction.md) effect, distinct from the static [Aharonov-Bohm effect](aharonov-bohm-effect.md).
