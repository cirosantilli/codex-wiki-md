# Polar vector

↑ **Parent:** [Vector](vector.md)

A polar [vector](vector.md) transforms as $\mathbf v'=Q\mathbf v$ under an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) $Q$, including an orientation-reversing transformation. Displacement, [velocity](velocity.md) and [force](force-split.md) are polar [vectors](vector.md). An [axial vector](pseudovector.md) instead transforms as $\mathbf a'=(\det Q)Q\mathbf a$.
