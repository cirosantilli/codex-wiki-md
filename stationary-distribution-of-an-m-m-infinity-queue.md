# Stationary distribution of an M-M-infinity queue

↑ **Parent:** [M/M/∞ queue](m-m-%E2%88%9E-queue.md)

For every $\lambda,\mu>0$, an $M/M/\infty$ queue is positive recurrent and has the [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) with mean $\rho=\lambda/\mu$ as its unique stationary distribution:

$$
\pi_n=e^{-\rho}\frac{\rho^n}{n!}.
$$
