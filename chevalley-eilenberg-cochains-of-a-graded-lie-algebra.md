<h1 id="chevalley-eilenberg-cochains-of-a-graded-lie-algebra">Chevalley–Eilenberg cochains of a graded Lie algebra</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Graded Lie algebra](graded-lie-algebra.md)

For a positively [graded Lie algebra](graded-lie-algebra.md) $L$ over $\mathbb Q$, finite-dimensional in each degree, its cochain algebra is free graded commutative on the graded dual of $sL$, with differential dual to the Lie bracket and its Koszul signs. The Jacobi identity gives $d^2=0$. A Lie element of degree $r$ contributes a cochain generator of degree $r+1$. Its [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) computes $\operatorname{Ext}_{U(L)}(\mathbb Q,\mathbb Q)$ with total grading; the standard free enveloping-algebra resolution gives this identification. For a [free graded Lie algebra](free-graded-lie-algebra.md), the tensor-algebra augmentation has a length-one free resolution, explaining the square-zero [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of a rational [wedge sum](wedge-sum.md) of spheres.
