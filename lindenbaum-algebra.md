# Lindenbaum algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Propositional logic](propositional-logic.md)

Identify classical propositional formulae when their equivalence is provable from a fixed theory. Their equivalence classes form the [Lindenbaum algebra](lindenbaum-algebra.md): meet and join are induced by conjunction and disjunction, complementation by negation, and the order by provable implication. Provable equivalence is preserved by each connective, so these operations are well-defined; the familiar classical propositional identities make this a [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md). A [homomorphism of lattices](homomorphism-of-lattices.md) to $\{0,1\}$ preserving zero and one also preserves complements and therefore specifies a truth valuation of the atomic propositions. All axioms of the fixed theory represent one. Separating the class of an unprovable formula from one consequently gives a countervaluation, proving the [completeness theorem for propositional logic](completeness-theorem-for-propositional-logic.md).
