# Space-time harmonic functions along Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Backward heat equation](backward-heat-equation.md)

For $f\in C^2(\mathbb R^2)$ and standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) $B$, the drift in [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) for $f(B_t,t)$ vanishes exactly when the displayed equation holds for every spatial point and every $t\geq0$. Necessity follows because a zero finite-variation integral has continuous integrand $f_t(B_t,t)+\tfrac12f_{xx}(B_t,t)$ equal to zero, and at every positive time the [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) of $B_t$ has full support. Continuity extends the equation to time zero. There is no condition at negative times except compatibility with the global smoothness of $f$.
