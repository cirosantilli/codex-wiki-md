# Power coefficient of reactivity

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature coefficient of reactivity](temperature-coefficient-of-reactivity.md)

The change of [reactor reactivity](reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md) with thermal [power](power.md) along a specified family of states. In a [quasistatic process](quasistatic-process.md), the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) weights each [temperature coefficient of reactivity](temperature-coefficient-of-reactivity.md) by that component's temperature response to [power](power.md).
