# Bad-prime eigenvalue of a trivial-character newform

↑ **Parent:** [Newform](newform.md)

For a [newform](newform.md) of weight $k\ge2$, level $M$ and trivial [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md), the bad-prime [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md) $U_p$ has the displayed possibilities. The local trace relation on the [new subspace of cusp forms](new-subspace-of-cusp-forms.md) gives $U_p=-p^{k/2-1}W_p$ when $p\parallel M$, where the normalized local Atkin-Lehner operator squares to one; if $p^2\mid M$, the new-vector relation gives $U_p=0$. In particular $|a_p|<p^{k/2}$, a strict inequality useful for proving irreducibility of old blocks.
