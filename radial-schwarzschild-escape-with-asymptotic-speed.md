# Radial Schwarzschild escape with asymptotic speed

↑ **Parent:** [Local radial speed of a Schwarzschild geodesic](local-radial-speed-of-a-schwarzschild-geodesic.md)

For asymptotic [speed](speed.md) $0\le u<c$, $k=(1-u^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$ and the local outward [speed](speed.md) obeys $v_{\rm loc}^2=u^2+(2GM/r)(1-u^2/c^2)$. It tends to $c$ as the horizon is approached from outside, though a static observer cannot actually remain on the horizon.
