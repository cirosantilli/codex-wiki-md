# Twisted Lagrangian graph criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Twisted cotangent symplectic form](twisted-cotangent-symplectic-form.md)

With $\omega=-d\alpha$ and $\omega_\sigma=\omega+\pi^*\sigma$, the [one-form graph](graph-of-a-differential-one-form.md) of $\theta$ is a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) exactly when $d\theta=\sigma$. Indeed its pulled-back [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) is $\sigma-d\theta$ and its dimension is half that of the total space. This extends [graph of a closed one-form is Lagrangian](graph-of-a-closed-one-form-is-lagrangian.md).
