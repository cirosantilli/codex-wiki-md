# Bayesian network

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilistic graphical model](probabilistic-graphical-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayesian_network)

A Bayesian network consists of a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md) $G$ whose nodes are [random variables](random-variable-split.md), together with [conditional distributions](conditional-distribution.md) satisfying $p(x)=\prod_jp(x_j\mid x_{\operatorname{pa}_G(j)})$. Its [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md) encodes [conditional independence](conditional-independence.md) constraints. For unrestricted binary variables it has $\sum_j2^{|\operatorname{pa}_G(j)|}$ free [conditional probabilities](conditional-probability.md). A [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md) used this way need not have a causal interpretation.
