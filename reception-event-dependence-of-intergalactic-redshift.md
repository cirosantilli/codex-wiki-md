# Reception-event dependence of intergalactic redshift

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md)

A [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) relates a particular emission event to a particular reception event along a [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md). Two sources' redshifts on Earth's past light cone do not specify the light exchanged between those sources. If a photon from B passes through A before reaching Earth, and A's comparison emission lies at that passage event, then $1+z_{BA}=(1+z_B)/(1+z_A)$. Without this causal and temporal specification the ratio need not give the redshift measured in A.
