# Positive intersection of J-holomorphic curves

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic submanifold](symplectic-submanifold.md)

Distinct simple $J$-holomorphic curves in an almost complex four-manifold have positive local intersection multiplicities. Consequently their homological intersection number is nonnegative whenever they intersect in finitely many points.
