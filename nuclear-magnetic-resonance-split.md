# Nuclear magnetic resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_magnetic_resonance)

[Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) detects the response of [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) with a [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) to a [radio frequency](radio-frequency.md) [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) in a steady [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). Its striking possibility is to measure a nuclear property through an ordinary electrical [signal](signal.md) from a bulk sample: an enormous number of tiny [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) can produce a measurable change in a receiving circuit.

For a nucleus with [spin angular momentum](spin.md) $\mathbf J$, define its [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md) by $\boldsymbol\mu=\gamma\mathbf J$. Throughout this article $\gamma$ is angular, measured in radians per second per tesla; $\gamma/(2\pi)$ is the corresponding value in cycles per second per tesla. With $\mathbf B_0=B_0\hat{\mathbf z}$, the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is $H=-\gamma B_0J_z$. Adjacent [energy levels](energy-level.md) differ by

$$
\Delta E=\hbar|\gamma|B_0,\qquad \omega_0=|\gamma|B_0,\qquad f_0=\frac{|\gamma|B_0}{2\pi}.
$$

Thus a measured resonance [frequency](frequency.md) and a calibrated [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) determine $|\gamma|$. Converting that into a [nuclear magnetic moment](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) also requires the [spin](spin.md) quantum number: the maximum projection has magnitude $|\gamma|\hbar I$. This simple experiment does not by itself determine the sign of $\gamma$.

The [Rabi molecular beam magnetic resonance experiment](rabi-molecular-beam-magnetic-resonance-experiment.md) detects transitions through changes in a selected [molecular beam](molecular-beam.md). The bulk experiments below instead detect electrical consequences of nuclear [magnetization](magnetization.md) in matter at ordinary density. A small [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) population imbalance is multiplied by a very large number of nuclei. For noninteracting [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) nuclei of number density $n$ and positive $\gamma$, their equilibrium [magnetization](magnetization.md) is

$$
M_0=n\frac{\gamma\hbar}{2}\tanh\!\left(\frac{\gamma\hbar B_0}{2k_{\rm B}T}\right).
$$

This follows by weighting the two projections $\pm\gamma\hbar/2$ with their [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) probabilities. It explains both the smallness of the bulk polarization at ordinary [temperature](temperature-split.md) and the value of many nuclei contributing to one receiving circuit.

[NMR spectroscopy](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) measures resonant responses of [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) to applied [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). In [residual-group monitoring of polyurethane formation](residual-group-monitoring-of-polyurethane-formation.md), distinguishable signal [integrals](integral-split.md) supply a relative residual-group readout; overlap or very weak signals can limit that inference.
