# Schwarzschild tidal force

↑ **Parent:** [Radial Weyl curvature of Schwarzschild spacetime](radial-weyl-curvature-of-schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

The orthonormal-frame components of Schwarzschild curvature scale as $M/r^3$. The [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md) equation therefore gives radial stretching and transverse compression whose magnitude diverges as $r\to0$.
