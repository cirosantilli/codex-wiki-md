# Incremental validity

↑ **Parent:** [Criterion validity](criterion-validity.md)

Predictive contribution beyond specified existing measures. A social-[intelligence](intelligence.md) measure should be tested against relevant general-ability or [personality](personality.md) measures rather than credited for any unadjusted [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) with social outcomes.
