# Superheated liquid

↑ **Parent:** [Superheating](superheating.md)

A [liquid](liquid.md) maintained above its equilibrium [boiling](boiling.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md) at the imposed [pressure](pressure-split.md). A barrier to [nucleation](nucleation.md) can delay its conversion into [vapor](vapor.md), making this a [metastable](metastability.md) state.
