# Kac master equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kac model](kac-model.md)

The jump process with total collision rate $N$ and normalized pair-angle operator $Q$ evolves by $\partial_tF_N=N(Q-I)F_N$. It preserves mass and the total squared velocity. Its one-coordinate distribution satisfies the [Kac marginal evolution equation](kac-marginal-evolution-equation.md), and its Gaussian [relative entropy in Kac's model](relative-entropy-in-kac-s-model.md) decreases at the rate given by [Kac entropy production](kac-entropy-production.md).
