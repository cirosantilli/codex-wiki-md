# Scaled length of an Archimedean spiral

↑ **Parent:** [Archimedean spiral](archimedean-spiral.md)

Let an [Archimedean spiral](archimedean-spiral.md) have [polar radius](polar-radius.md) $r=at$ and [angle](angle.md) $t$ in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md), with $0\leq t\leq R/a$, where $R>0$ is fixed. Parameterizing by $r$ gives the [arc length](arc-length.md)

$$
aL_a=\int_0^R\sqrt{a^2+r^2}\,dr.
$$

The bounds $r\leq\sqrt{a^2+r^2}\leq r+a$ yield

$$
\frac{R^2}{2}\leq aL_a\leq\frac{R^2}{2}+aR.
$$

The [squeeze theorem](squeeze-theorem.md) proves the displayed [limit](limit-of-a-function.md). Thus the [arc length](arc-length.md) grows like $R^2/(2a)$ as the turns become closely spaced, without requiring an explicit antiderivative.
