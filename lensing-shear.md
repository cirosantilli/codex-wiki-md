# Lensing shear

↑ **Parent:** [Weak gravitational lensing](weak-gravitational-lensing.md)

The trace-free part of the angular lensing-potential Hessian stretches an image anisotropically. The source-image [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has eigenvalues $1-\kappa\pm|\gamma|$. Thus a circular source becomes an ellipse in the local linear approximation; the orientation follows the shear axes.
