Sixteen-page scanned official teaching notes: title on PDF p. 1, repeated questions 5–8 on PDF pp. 2–5, handwritten answer pages 1–11 on PDF pp. 6–16. The repeated questions retain code 2775 and printed exam pages 7–10. These notes concern Section II of the combined B1 paper, not Section I. Some requested derivations are replaced by textbook or tutorial pointers.
