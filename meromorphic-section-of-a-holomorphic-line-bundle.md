# Meromorphic section of a holomorphic line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md)

In a [holomorphic local frame](holomorphic-local-trivialization.md), a meromorphic section has a meromorphic scalar coefficient; the coefficients transform by the bundle's [transition functions of a vector bundle](transition-function-of-a-vector-bundle.md). A nonzero meromorphic section has a [divisor on a complex manifold](divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md) recording its zero and pole orders.
