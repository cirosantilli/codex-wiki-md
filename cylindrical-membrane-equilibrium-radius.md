# Cylindrical membrane equilibrium radius

↑ **Parent:** [Helfrich energy](helfrich-energy.md)

Minimizing the [Helfrich energy](helfrich-energy.md) per length of an unconstrained cylindrical membrane gives $r_0=\sqrt{k_c/(2\sigma)}$, balancing the area cost against bending curvature.
