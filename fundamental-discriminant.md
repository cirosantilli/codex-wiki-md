# Fundamental discriminant

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic field](quadratic-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_discriminant)

A fundamental discriminant is an integer that occurs as the [field discriminant](field-discriminant.md) of a [quadratic number field](quadratic-field.md). For square-free $d$, it is $d$ when $d\equiv1\pmod4$ and $4d$ otherwise.
