# Nucleotide sugar

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide](nucleotide.md)

A [nucleotide sugar](nucleotide-sugar.md) carries an activated sugar linked to a nucleotide-containing group. It supports selective glycosyl transfer; donor identity alone does not specify the linkage or final glycan structure.
