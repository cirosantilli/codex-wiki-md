# Acidic permanganate-iron balance

↑ **Parent:** [Oxidation state](oxidation-state.md)

An increase in [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) records [oxidation](oxidation.md); a decrease records [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md). Thus $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\to\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ raises the [iron](iron.md) [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) by one, while $\mathrm{MnO}_4^-\to\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}$ lowers the [manganese](manganese.md) [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) from $+7$ to $+2$. Five [iron](iron.md) [ions](ion.md) supply the five-[electron](electron.md) inventory required per [permanganate](permanganate.md) [ion](ion.md).

In an acidic [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), the complete [half-reactions](half-reaction.md) are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}&\longrightarrow\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}+e^-,\\
\mathrm{MnO}_4^-+8\mathrm H^++5e^-&\longrightarrow\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}+4\mathrm H_2\mathrm O.
\end{aligned}
$$

First balance [oxygen](oxygen.md) with four [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md), then [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) with eight [protons](proton.md), and finally [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) with five [electrons](electron.md). Multiply the [iron](iron.md) [half-reaction](half-reaction.md) by five and add; the exchanged [electrons](electron.md) cancel:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{MnO}_4^-+8\mathrm H^++5\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\longrightarrow\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}+4\mathrm H_2\mathrm O+5\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}.}
$$

Both sides contain one [manganese](manganese.md), four [oxygen](oxygen.md), eight [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and five [iron](iron.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), with net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $+17e$. The $\mathrm H^+$ notation represents the acidic aqueous [proton](proton.md) inventory; it does not imply bare [protons](proton.md) moving independently of [water](water.md). These [half-reactions](half-reaction.md) and their sum are the acidic balance used in the documented [permanganate-iron titration](permanganate-iron-titration.md).[Iron Tablet Determination Analytical Competition, “Setting the Scene”: both half-reactions and the overall equation](iron-tablet-determination-analytical-competition-royal-society-of-chemistry-resource.md)

Original exact [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) diagram. Matching five formal [electron](electron.md) slots is a bookkeeping operation, not a picture of free [electrons](electron.md) in the [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). The lower table verifies the net [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md); its $+17$ is the net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) number of the written reacting species, not the bulk [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md).

The coefficients predict amounts consumed, not a collision of five [ions](ion.md) or an [elementary reaction](elementary-reaction.md). Bulk [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) also contains compensating [counterions](counterion.md), which are omitted from this net [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md). The [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and [oxygen](oxygen.md) [oxidation states](oxidation-state.md) are unchanged; balancing their inventories remains essential even though they do not account for the exchanged [electrons](electron.md).
