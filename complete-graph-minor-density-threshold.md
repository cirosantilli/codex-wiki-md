# Complete graph minor density threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Graph minor](graph-minor.md)

The complete graph minor density threshold is

$$
c(t)=\inf\{c:\text{every nonempty finite graph }G\text{ with }e(G)\geq c|G|\text{ has a }K_t\text{ minor}\}.
$$

The density here is [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) count divided by order, half the average [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md). For large $t$, this threshold has order $t\sqrt{\log t}$, with the logarithm taken to base $e$.
