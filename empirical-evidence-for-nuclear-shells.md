# Empirical evidence for nuclear shells

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md)

[Isotope](isotope.md) abundance, [nuclear separation energies](nuclear-separation-energy.md), [spin](spin.md) and [nuclear magnetic moments](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) supply complementary tests: abundance concerns which [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) survive and accumulate, removal [energy](energy-split.md) concerns binding, and magnetic properties test the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) assignments. Agreement across these observables is more informative than fitting one list of numbers.

In her 1963 lecture, [Maria Goeppert Mayer](maria-goeppert-mayer.md) recalled excess abundances at $N=50$ and $82$. She contrasted the strong binding of the last [neutron](neutron-split.md) in [oxygen](oxygen.md) [isotope](isotope.md) ${}^{16}\mathrm O$ with the weaker extra [neutron](neutron-split.md) in [oxygen](oxygen.md) [isotope](isotope.md) ${}^{17}\mathrm O$, and displayed [beta decay](beta-decay.md) [energies](energy-split.md) near $N=20$ and $50$. These are compiled empirical patterns, rather than readings from an apparatus she built.[§2, p. 21; Figs. 2–3, p. 23](the-shell-model-by-maria-goeppert-mayer.md)

Her 1949 letter compared observed [spins](spin.md) and [nuclear magnetic moments](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) with orbital assignments. For example, [lead](lead.md) [isotope](isotope.md) ${}^{207}\mathrm{Pb}$ was assigned a $p_{1/2}$ neutron hole near $N=126$, whereas [bismuth](bismuth.md)'s odd [proton](proton.md) began the next shell as $h_{9/2}$. The moments tested orbital character as well as [spin](spin.md).[p. 1970, discussion and Table II](on-closed-shells-in-nuclei-ii-by-maria-goeppert-mayer.md)

A useful comparison holds $Z$ fixed when comparing neighboring [isotopes](isotope.md), and examines odd-even staggering separately from a shell discontinuity. Abundance additionally depends on production and decay history. Neither the abundance pattern nor a successful [spin](spin.md) assignment directly images orbiting particles; neither fixes a unique [nuclear mean field](nuclear-mean-field.md). The experimental techniques themselves include [mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md) and [nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md), whose preparation and readout differ. The [Hofstadter nuclear electron scattering experiment](hofstadter-nuclear-electron-scattering-experiment.md) separately constrains nuclear size and [nuclear charge distributions](nuclear-charge-distribution.md), rather than measuring these occupancies.
