# Fourth derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Third derivative](third-derivative.md)

The [fourth derivative](fourth-derivative.md) is the [derivative](derivative.md) of the [third derivative](third-derivative.md). It vanishes for a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most three. For a [cubic spline](cubic-spline.md), this statement applies inside each knot [interval](interval-mathematics.md); a jump of the [third derivative](third-derivative.md) can prevent a classical [fourth derivative](fourth-derivative.md) from existing at a [spline knot](spline-knot.md).
