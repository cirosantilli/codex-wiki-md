<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [normed space](../../../../../normed-vector-space-split.md) $X$, let $X^*$ be its [continuous dual space](../../../../../continuous-dual-space-split.md) and $X^{**}=(X^*)^*$ its [bidual space](../../../../../bidual-of-a-normed-space.md). Its [canonical embedding into the bidual](../../../../../canonical-embedding-into-the-bidual.md) is

$$
J_X:X\to X^{**},\qquad(J_Xx)(\ell)=\ell(x).
$$

The definition of a [reflexive space](../../../../../reflexive-space.md) in this setting is

$$
\boxed{J_X(X)=X^{**}.}
$$

The [Hahn-Banach theorem](../../../../../hahn-banach-theorem.md) makes $J_X$ a [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) that is an [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md). For any [normed space](../../../../../normed-vector-space-split.md) $Z$, its [continuous dual space](../../../../../continuous-dual-space-split.md) is a [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md): a [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) $(\ell_n)$ in the [operator norm](../../../../../operator-norm.md) has scalar limits $\ell(z)=\lim_n\ell_n(z)$, and the uniform Cauchy bound proves both boundedness of $\ell$ and $\|\ell_n-\ell\|\to0$. Apply this with $Z=X^*$ to see that $X^{**}$ is [complete](../../../../../completeness.md). If $J_X$ is onto, the [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md) transfers this [completeness](../../../../../completeness.md) to $X$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{X\text{ reflexive}\ \Longrightarrow\ X\text{ complete}.}
$$
