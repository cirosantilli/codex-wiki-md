<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Because the [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md) $\ell_0,\ell_1$ agree with endpoint [point evaluations](../../../../../point-evaluation-functional.md) on [continuous functions](../../../../../continuous-function.md),

$$
\ell_i(e_j)=\delta_{ij}\qquad(i,j\in\{0,1\}).
$$

The endpoint values also make $e_0,e_1$ [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md). By its definition, the [image of a linear map](../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) $T$ lies in $E=\langle e_0,e_1\rangle$. Conversely $Te_j=e_j$, so $T$ acts as the [identity operator](../../../../../identity-operator.md) on $E$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}T=\langle e_0,e_1\rangle,\qquad\operatorname{rank}T=2.}
$$

For every $f$, $Tf\in E$, whence $T^2f=Tf$. It is a [linear projection](../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md), and an annihilating [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{p(z)=z(z-1),\qquad p(T)=T^2-T=0.}
$$
