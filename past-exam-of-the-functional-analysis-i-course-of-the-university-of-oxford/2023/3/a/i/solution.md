<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Writing $\mathcal B(X)$ for the [Banach space of bounded linear operators](../../../../../banach-space-of-bounded-linear-operators.md) on $X$, the [spectrum](../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sigma(T)=\{\lambda\in\mathbb C:\lambda I-T\text{ has no bounded two-sided inverse in }\mathcal B(X)\}.}
$$

Here $I$ is the [identity operator](../../../../../identity-operator.md). Since $X$ is a [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md), the [bounded inverse theorem](../../../../../bounded-inverse-theorem.md) makes this equivalent to failure of bijectivity of $\lambda I-T$.

The [spectral radius](../../../../../spectral-radius.md) is

$$
\boxed{r(T)=\sup\{|\lambda|:\lambda\in\sigma(T)\}.}
$$

For a nonzero complex [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md), the [spectrum](../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) of a [bounded linear operator](../../../../../continuous-linear-operator.md) is nonempty and [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md), so the [supremum](../../../../../supremum.md) is a maximum. If the zero space is allowed, its empty [spectrum](../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) is assigned [spectral radius](../../../../../spectral-radius.md) zero.
