<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [continuous function](../../../../../continuous-function.md) $g$ on $[0,1]$, the [L-infinity norm](../../../../../l-infinity-norm.md) of its restriction to $(0,1)$ equals its [supremum norm](../../../../../supremum-norm.md) on $[0,1]$. Indeed, values arbitrarily close to a maximum occur on an interval of positive [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md), even if the maximum lies at an endpoint. Also, [continuous functions](../../../../../continuous-function.md) equal [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md) on $(0,1)$ are equal everywhere on $[0,1]$. Thus restriction embeds $C([0,1])$ isometrically as a [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $X$.

The [point evaluation functional](../../../../../point-evaluation-functional.md) $E_{x_0}(g)=g(x_0)$ is complex-linear and satisfies $|E_{x_0}(g)|\leq\|g\|_\infty$. The constant [function](../../../../../function-split.md) one attains equality, so $\|E_{x_0}\|=1$. The [complex Hahn-Banach theorem](../../../../../complex-hahn-banach-theorem.md) extends this [bounded linear functional](../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) from that [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) to $X$, preserving its [operator norm](../../../../../operator-norm.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\ell_{x_0}\in X^*,\qquad\|\ell_{x_0}\|=1,\qquad\ell_{x_0}(g)=g(x_0)\quad(g\in C([0,1])).}
$$
