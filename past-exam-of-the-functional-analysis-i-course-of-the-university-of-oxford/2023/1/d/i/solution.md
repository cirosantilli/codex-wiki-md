<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By the [mean value theorem](../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md), a [continuously differentiable function](../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md) with zero [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) on $[-1,1]$ is constant. Thus the [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of the [differentiation operator](../../../../../differentiation-operator.md) is

$$
\boxed{W=\{x\mapsto a:a\in\mathbb R\}=\langle1\rangle.}
$$

It is a [closed set](../../../../../closed-set.md) by the [finite-dimensional subspace is closed](../../../../../finite-dimensional-subspace-is-closed.md) theorem, applied in the [normed space](../../../../../normed-vector-space-split.md) $(C^1([-1,1]),\|\cdot\|_\infty)$. It is proper because the [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $x\mapsto x$ has nonzero [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md).

For $n\geq1$, take $u_n(x)=\sin(nx)$. Its [supremum norm](../../../../../supremum-norm.md) is at most one, whereas $u_n'(0)=n$ and $|u_n'(x)|\leq n$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\|Tu_n\|_\infty=n,\quad\|u_n\|_\infty\leq1;\qquad T\text{ is unbounded}.}
$$
