<h1 id="1/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) $W=\langle1\rangle$ is [complete](../../../../../completeness.md): the map $a\mapsto a1$ is an [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md) from $\mathbb R$ with its absolute-value [norm](../../../../../norm.md) onto $W$ with the [supremum norm](../../../../../supremum-norm.md).

Define a [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $S:Y\to X$ by

$$
(Sg)(x)=\int_0^x g(t)\,dt.
$$

The [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) gives $Sg\in C^1([-1,1])$ and $TSg=g$. Also,

$$
|(Sg)(x)|\leq|x|\|g\|_\infty\leq\|g\|_\infty,
\qquad\|Sg\|_X\leq\|g\|_Y.
$$

Thus $S$ is a bounded [right inverse](../../../../../right-inverse.md), and $Y=C([-1,1])$ is a [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md). Applying part (c),

$$
\boxed{\bigl(C^1([-1,1]),\|f\|_\infty+\|f'\|_\infty\bigr)\text{ is complete}.}
$$
