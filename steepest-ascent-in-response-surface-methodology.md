# Steepest ascent in response surface methodology

↑ **Parent:** [Response surface methodology](response-surface-methodology.md)

For a fitted local first-order response $\widehat m(x)=\widehat\beta_0+\widehat b^Tx$, the unit direction maximizing its directional derivative in coded Euclidean distance is $d=\widehat b/\|\widehat b\|$, by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Run sequential feasible settings $x_0+hd$, increase $h$ while measured response improves, and refit near promising settings. Physical increments are $h_jhd_j$, so coded and unscaled physical gradients need not give the same experimental path. A near-zero gradient calls for further modeling rather than division by zero.
