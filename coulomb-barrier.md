# Coulomb barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fusion](nuclear-fusion.md)

Repulsion between positively charged nuclei creates this electrostatic energy barrier outside the short-range nuclear interaction. [Quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) allows reaction at thermal energies below its classical height and contributes the penetration factor in the [Gamow peak](gamow-peak.md).
