<h1 id="pressure-compatibility-of-nonlinear-torsional-alfven-profiles">Pressure compatibility of nonlinear torsional Alfvén profiles</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Torsional Alfvén wave](torsional-alfven-wave.md)

For uniform-density [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) with $u_\phi=f(R,z+vt)+g(R,z-vt)$, $B_\phi/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}=f-g$ and $B_z/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}=v(R)$, the azimuthal [magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation](magnetohydrodynamic-momentum-equation.md) and [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md) hold automatically. Let $\Pi=p/\rho+\Phi+[(f-g)^2+v^2]/2$. The remaining equations require $\Pi_z=0$ and $\Pi_R=4fg/R$, so $\partial_z(fg)=0$ is necessary and locally sufficient. A single traveling profile is unrestricted; two nonzero profiles on an unrestricted axial domain must be opposite exponentials or independent of their traveling arguments. Bounded profiles of both signs therefore reduce to steady solutions.
