# Handle-cut formula for anyon ground-state degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Modular tensor category](modular-tensor-category.md)

Cutting one nonseparating circle on each handle of a closed [orientable surface](orientable-surface.md) gives a [sphere](sphere.md) with $g$ pairs of oppositely oriented boundary circles. The [topological quantum field theory](topological-quantum-field-theory.md) gluing rule sums over the paired [anyon](anyon.md) labels $a,\bar a$. The resulting [anyon fusion matrix](anyon-fusion-matrix.md) formula becomes $\dim V(\Sigma_g)=\mathcal D^{2g-2}\sum_a d_a^{2-2g}$ by the [Verlinde formula](verlinde-formula.md).
