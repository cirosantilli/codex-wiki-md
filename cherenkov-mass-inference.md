# Cherenkov mass inference

↑ **Parent:** [Cherenkov radiation](cherenkov-radiation.md)

The cone relation $\cos\theta=1/(n\beta)$ determines speed. Together with a measured [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $T$, the rest [mass](mass.md) is $m=T/[(\gamma-1)c^2]$. The inference requires the radiation to be identified as [Cherenkov radiation](cherenkov-radiation.md), and the frequency-dependent [phase refractive index](phase-refractive-index.md) must be known.
