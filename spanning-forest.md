# Spanning forest

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

An acyclic spanning subgraph containing a [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md) of each [connected component](connected-component.md) of the original graph. For a graph with $n$ vertices and $c$ components, a spanning forest has $n-c$ edges.
