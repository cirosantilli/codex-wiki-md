# Mixing on crystallographic sites

↑ **Parent:** [Solid solution](solid-solution.md)

An activity model in which distinct atomic positions in a [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) are occupied independently by specified species. For ideal random mixing, a pure [mineral endmember](mineral-endmember.md) requiring $\nu_s$ specified atoms on site type $s$ has [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) $a=\prod_sX_s^{\nu_s}$ relative to that pure endmember, with other occupations held fixed. Its mixing contribution to the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is $RT\ln a$. The powers count occupied positions per formula unit; they are not always the mole fraction of whole endmember formulae.
