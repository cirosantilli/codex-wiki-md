# Snow line

↑ **Parent:** [Protoplanetary disk](protoplanetary-disk.md)

A transition beyond which a specified volatile can predominantly condense into solid grains under local [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md). Without qualification it often means the water-ice transition. Its location evolves with disk heating, cooling and composition; it is not a universal fixed orbital radius.
