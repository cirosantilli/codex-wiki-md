# Product probability space

↑ **Parent:** [Probability space](probability-space.md)

For finitely many [probability spaces](probability-space.md) $(X_i,\mathcal F_i,\mu_i)$, their product [probability](probability.md) space has underlying [set](set-split.md) $\prod_iX_i$, the [product sigma-algebra](product-sigma-algebra.md) and [product measure](product-measure.md) $\bigotimes_i\mu_i$. The coordinate projections are jointly independent: for measurable $B_i\subseteq X_i$, the [probability](probability.md) of all coordinate conditions is $\prod_i\mu_i(B_i)$. Conversely a family of independent coordinates has this product joint distribution. Product structure permits coordinate exposure and independent resampling in concentration arguments.
