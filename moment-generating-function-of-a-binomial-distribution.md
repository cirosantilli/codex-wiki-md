# Moment-generating function of a binomial distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md)

For a [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) with parameters $n$ and $p$, the [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md) gives the displayed [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md), finite for every real $t$. Products of these [moment-generating functions](moment-generating-function.md) show that the sum of [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [binomial random variables](binomial-random-variable.md) with the same success [probability](probability.md) again has a [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md), with trial counts added.
