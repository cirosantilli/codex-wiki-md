# Solar magnetic network

↑ **Parent:** [Solar magnetic field](solar-magnetic-field.md)

A spatially extended pattern of magnetic concentrations near boundaries of [supergranulation](supergranulation.md). Surface transport and convergence redistribute magnetic elements into this network; flux emergence, cancellation and diffusion also continually modify it. It is distinct from the much smaller-scale field pattern in individual [intergranular lanes](intergranular-lane.md) and from the organized strong fields of [sunspots](sunspot.md).
