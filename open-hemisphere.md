# Open hemisphere

↑ **Parent:** [Hemisphere](hemisphere.md)

For a nonzero vector $u$, the open hemisphere of $S^d$ with direction $u$ is $\{x\in S^d:u\cdot x>0\}$. Its boundary is excluded, so opposite [open hemispheres](open-hemisphere.md) are disjoint.
