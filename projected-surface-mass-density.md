# Projected surface mass density

↑ **Parent:** [Density](density.md)

Projected surface mass density is mass per unit projected area, obtained by integrating [mass density](density.md) along the line of sight. It determines the enclosed cylindrical mass used by a [thin gravitational lens equation](thin-gravitational-lens-equation.md). It need not equal a physical disk's density, and its aperture mass differs from a spherical three-dimensional enclosed mass.
