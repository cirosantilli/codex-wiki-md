# Seifert surface

↑ **Parent:** [Knot](knot.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seifert_surface)

A Seifert surface for an oriented knot $K$ is a compact connected oriented surface $F\subset S^3$ with oriented boundary $\partial F=K$.
