# Decreasing Hubble rate does not alone fix scalar tilt

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar spectral index in Hubble slow-roll parameters](scalar-spectral-index-in-hubble-slow-roll-parameters.md)

The tensor spectrum is proportional to $H^2$, but the single-field [slow-roll](slow-roll-approximation.md) curvature spectrum is proportional to $H^2/\epsilon_H$. Thus $n_s-1\simeq-2\epsilon_H-d\ln\epsilon_H/dN$. A decreasing $H$ gives a red leading tensor tilt, while the evolving scalar conversion factor can change the sign of the scalar tilt.
