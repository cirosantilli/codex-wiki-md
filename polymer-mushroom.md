# Polymer mushroom

↑ **Parent:** [Grafted polymer chain](grafted-polymer-chain.md)

A dilute layer of end-tethered [polymer](polymer-split.md) coils whose lateral separation greatly exceeds the isolated coil size. Each coil retains approximately its isolated-solvent conformation, modified by the impenetrable tethering surface. Increasing the grafting density drives overlap and the crossover to a [polymer brush](polymer-brush.md).
