# Hertzsprung gap

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertzsprung_gap)

The Hertzsprung gap is a sparsely populated region of the [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) traversed rapidly by many stars evolving away from the [main sequence](main-sequence.md) toward the [red giant](red-giant.md) region.
