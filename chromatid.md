# Chromatid

↑ **Parent:** [Chromosome](chromosome.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatid)

A chromatid is one of the two copies of a replicated [chromosome](chromosome.md). Each [homologous chromosome](homologous-chromosome.md) contributes two sister chromatids before separation in [meiosis](meiosis.md); [chromosomal crossovers](chromosomal-crossover.md) can exchange segments between nonsister chromatids.
