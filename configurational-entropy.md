# Configurational entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann's entropy formula](boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md)

The part of [entropy](entropy-split.md) associated with the number of distinct spatial or occupancy arrangements consistent with chosen macroscopic constraints. For equally weighted configurations, the [Boltzmann entropy](boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md) formula gives the displayed expression. Unequal probabilities require $-k_B\sum_i p_i\ln p_i$ over configurations. For $n$ indistinguishable empty sites among $M$ otherwise occupied sites, $\Omega_{\rm conf}=\binom{M}{n}$. Vibrational or internal degrees of freedom can contribute additional entropy even at fixed site arrangement.
