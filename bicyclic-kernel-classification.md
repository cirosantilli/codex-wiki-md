# Bicyclic kernel classification

↑ **Parent:** [Bicyclic graph](bicyclic-graph.md)

The [graph kernel](graph-kernel.md) of a [bicyclic graph](bicyclic-graph.md) satisfies $\sum_v(\deg v-2)=2$, with all degrees at least three. It therefore has either one degree-four vertex carrying two loops, a [bouquet graph](bouquet-graph.md), or two degree-three vertices. In the latter case connectivity and degree counting give either three parallel edges, the three-edge [Theta graph](theta-graph.md), or one edge between the vertices and one loop at each, a [dumbbell graph](dumbbell-graph.md). All bicyclic cores are subdivisions of these three kernels, subject to simplicity restrictions on loop lengths and parallel paths.
