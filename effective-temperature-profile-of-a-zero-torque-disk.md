# Effective-temperature profile of a zero-torque disk

↑ **Parent:** [Standard thin-disk dissipation flux](standard-thin-disk-dissipation-flux.md)

A [Keplerian accretion disk](keplerian-accretion-disk.md) with a [zero-torque inner boundary condition](zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) and local [blackbody](blackbody.md) cooling has $T_{\rm eff}^4=3GM\dot M(1-\sqrt{r_{\rm in}/r})/(8\pi\sigma r^3)$. The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) uses the flux through one face; the [standard thin-disk dissipation flux](standard-thin-disk-dissipation-flux.md) includes both faces. The scale $T_{\rm in}=[3GM\dot M/(8\pi\sigma r_{\rm in}^3)]^{1/4}$ is a normalization, not the actual temperature at the stress-free inner edge.
