# Nusselt number

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nusselt_number)

The [Nusselt number](nusselt-number.md) is the ratio of total [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) to conductive [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) across the same length scale: $\mathrm{Nu}=qh/(k_T\Delta T)$, where $q$ is heat flux and $k_T$ is [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md).
