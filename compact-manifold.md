# Compact manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md)

A compact manifold is a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) whose underlying [topological space](topological-space.md) is [compact](compact-space.md). Every smooth [vector field](vector-field.md) on a compact manifold without boundary has a flow defined for all real time. On a manifold with boundary, tangency to the boundary or an appropriate inward-flow condition must also be imposed.
