# Thermal speed

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

A characteristic random particle [speed](speed.md). Here [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T_s$ is measured in [energy](energy-split.md) units, and the factor-two convention makes a [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md) proportional to $e^{-v^2/v_{\mathrm{th},s}^2}$. Other conventions, such as the one-dimensional standard deviation $\sqrt{T_s/m_s}$, differ by fixed factors.
