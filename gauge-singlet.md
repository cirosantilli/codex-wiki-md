# Gauge singlet

↑ **Parent:** [Trivial representation](trivial-representation.md)

A field is a [gauge singlet](gauge-singlet.md) if it transforms in the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) of the entire [gauge group](gauge-group.md): every gauge transformation leaves it unchanged. A singlet of only one factor need not be a gauge singlet of the product group. For example a [right-handed neutrino](right-handed-neutrino.md) with representation $(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0$ is a [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) gauge singlet, whereas a color-singlet charged [lepton](lepton.md) is still acted on by the electroweak group. A singlet can have ordinary derivatives in its [kinetic term](kinetic-term.md) and gauge-invariant masses, subject to [Lorentz invariance](lorentz-invariance.md) and any additional global symmetries.
