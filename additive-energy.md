# Additive energy

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Additive_energy)

The additive energy of a finite set $A$ is the number of [additive quadruples](additive-quadruple.md) in $A^4$, often normalized by $|A|^3$.
