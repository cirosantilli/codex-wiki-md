# Reactive strategy (game theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Iterated prisoner's dilemma](iterated-prisoner-s-dilemma.md)

A [reactive strategy](reactive-strategy-game-theory.md) cooperates with [probability](probability.md) $p$ after the opponent cooperated and [probability](probability.md) $q$ after the opponent defected. It ignores its own previous action. [Independent](independent-random-variables.md) behavioural randomization gives a four-state joint-action [Markov chain](markov-chain.md). The reduced marginal recurrences depend on the prescribed initial action distribution.
