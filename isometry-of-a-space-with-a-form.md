# Isometry of a space with a form

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear form](bilinear-form.md)

A linear bijection between spaces carrying compatible [bilinear forms](bilinear-form.md), [Hermitian forms](hermitian-form.md) or [quadratic forms](quadratic-form.md) is an [isometry of a space with a form](isometry-of-a-space-with-a-form.md) when it preserves the specified form. Preserving the polar form alone need not preserve a [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) in characteristic two. Subspaces may have degenerate restricted forms even when the ambient form is nonsingular.
