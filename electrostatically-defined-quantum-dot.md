# Electrostatically defined quantum dot

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum dot](quantum-dot.md)

A small electron island confined laterally by depletion gates in an already vertically confined [two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md). Tunnel barriers couple it to reservoirs, and a plunger gate tunes charge occupation. [Coulomb blockade](coulomb-blockade.md) and orbital confinement resolve individual electron additions.
