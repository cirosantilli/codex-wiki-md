# Nicol prism

↑ **Parent:** [Polarizer](polarizer.md)

A prism polarizer associated with [William Nicol](william-nicol-geologist.md). Its birefringent crystal arrangement selects one transmitted polarization; two such prisms can serve as the polarizer and analyser in a [polariscope](polariscope.md).
