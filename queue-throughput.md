# Queue throughput

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)

The long-run rate at which admitted customers complete service. In a stable queue without abandonment it equals the admitted arrival rate, which may be less than the offered rate because of blocking. [Little's law](little-s-law.md) uses this actual rate for the chosen subsystem.
