# Hydrodynamic transfield equation for an axisymmetric wind

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar wind](stellar-wind.md)

Using an [axisymmetric hydrodynamic mass flux function](axisymmetric-hydrodynamic-mass-flux-function.md), set $s=s(\psi)$, $\ell=Ru_\phi=\ell(\psi)$ and $\varepsilon=\varepsilon(\psi)$. The azimuthal [vorticity](vorticity.md) is $\omega_\phi=-R\nabla\cdot(\nabla\psi/(\rho R^2))$. The meridional projection of the [Crocco form of the unsteady Euler equation](crocco-form-of-the-unsteady-euler-equation.md) gives the displayed equation. The [mass density](density.md) also satisfies the Bernoulli relation $\varepsilon=|\nabla\psi|^2/(2\rho^2R^2)+\ell^2/(2R^2)+w+\Phi$, so an [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) is part of closing the system.
