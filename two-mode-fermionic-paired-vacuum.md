# Two-mode fermionic paired vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical rotation of two fermionic modes](canonical-rotation-of-two-fermionic-modes.md)

The normalized [vacuum state](vacuum-state.md) annihilated by $c=ua-vb^\dagger$ and $d=va^\dagger+ub$, for positive $u,v$ with $u^2+v^2=1$. Since $(a^\dagger b^\dagger)^2=0$, it can also be written $u\exp[(v/u)a^\dagger b^\dagger]|0\rangle$. Acting with either [annihilation operator](annihilation-operator.md) makes the two terms cancel. Its original [occupation numbers](occupation-number.md) each have [expected value](expected-value-split.md) $v^2$ and its pair amplitude is $\langle ab\rangle=-uv$. It has even [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) although the original total [number operator](number-operator.md) is not sharp. For the [two-orbital fermionic pairing Hamiltonian](two-orbital-fermionic-pairing-hamiltonian.md), it is the unique [ground state](ground-state.md) of [energy](energy-split.md) $\varepsilon-\sqrt{\varepsilon^2+\Delta^2}$.
