# Risk-neutral pricing

↑ **Parent:** [Risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md)

Under an [equivalent martingale measure](risk-neutral-measure.md) $Q$, the no-arbitrage value of a replicable payoff is its discounted conditional expectation under $Q$.
