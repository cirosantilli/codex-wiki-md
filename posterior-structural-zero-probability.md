# Posterior structural-zero probability

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-inflated Poisson regression](zero-inflated-poisson-regression.md)

In a [zero-inflated Poisson regression](zero-inflated-poisson-regression.md), [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) separates structural zeros from susceptible zero counts. A positive count rules out the structural-zero class; a zero raises its posterior probability according to the displayed formula, but usually does not determine class membership with certainty. This same probability supplies the E-step of the [EM algorithm for zero-inflated Poisson regression](em-algorithm-for-zero-inflated-poisson-regression.md).
