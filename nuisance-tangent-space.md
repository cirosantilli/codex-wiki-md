# Nuisance tangent space

↑ **Parent:** [Semiparametric statistics](semiparametric-statistics.md)

The nuisance tangent space is the closed linear span of [score functions](informant-function.md) from [statistical paths](statistical-path.md) that vary the [nuisance parameter](nuisance-parameter.md) while fixing the target [statistical parameter](statistical-parameter.md). It is a [closed subspace of a Hilbert space](closed-subspace-of-a-hilbert-space.md) inside $L^2_0(P)$. Removing its component from a parametric [score function](informant-function.md) leaves the information that nuisance variation cannot imitate.
