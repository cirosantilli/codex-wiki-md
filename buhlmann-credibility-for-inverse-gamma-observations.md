<h1 id="buhlmann-credibility-for-inverse-gamma-observations">Bühlmann credibility for inverse-gamma observations</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bühlmann credibility premium](buhlmann-credibility-premium.md)

In the [Gamma prior for an inverse-gamma scale](gamma-prior-for-an-inverse-gamma-scale.md) model, assume $k>2$. The [variance of hypothetical means](variance-of-hypothetical-means.md) is $a=\alpha/[\lambda^2(k-1)^2]$, and the [expected process variance](expected-process-variance.md) is $v=\alpha(\alpha+1)/[\lambda^2(k-1)^2(k-2)]$. The [Bühlmann credibility factor](credibility-factor.md) is $Z=n/(n+v/a)$, giving the displayed expression. The optimal affine [credibility estimate](credibility-estimate.md) is $(1-Z)\alpha/[\lambda(k-1)]+Z\overline X$. Its fixed-weight arithmetic-mean form differs from the reciprocal-based [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md).
