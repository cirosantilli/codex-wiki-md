<h1 id="student-s-t-test">Student's t-test</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student's_t-test)

Student's t-test divides a normally distributed estimated effect by its estimated standard error. Under its null hypothesis the statistic has a [Student's t-distribution](student-s-t-distribution.md), with degrees of freedom determined by the variance estimate.
