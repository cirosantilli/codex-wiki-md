# Zeldovich pancake thickness after shell crossing

↑ **Parent:** [Shell crossing](shell-crossing.md)

For the [Zeldovich approximation](zeldovich-approximation.md) $\mathbf x=\mathbf q-b\nabla_q\psi$, let $\lambda_i$ be the eigenvalues of the potential Hessian, with this sign convention. Before [shell crossing](shell-crossing.md), [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives the displayed density. The first positive eigenvalue reaches $b\lambda_i=1$ and collapses one direction, creating a sheetlike [cosmological caustic](cosmological-caustic.md). Extrapolating straight trajectories after crossing lets particles pass through without self-consistent gravitational trapping, so the predicted multistream sheet spreads excessively. Collisionless gravitational evolution keeps matter more tightly bound; the linear displacement extrapolation cannot determine its late thickness reliably.
