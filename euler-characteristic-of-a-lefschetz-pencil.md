# Euler characteristic of a Lefschetz pencil

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz pencil](lefschetz-pencil.md)

If a Lefschetz pencil on a closed four-manifold $X$ has smooth fiber of genus $g$, $b$ base points and $m$ critical points, then

$$
\chi(X)=4-4g+m-b.
$$

Indeed, blowing up the base points gives $\chi(\widetilde X)=\chi(X)+b$, while a genus-$g$ fibration over $S^2$ contributes $2(2-2g)$ and each Lefschetz critical point contributes one.
