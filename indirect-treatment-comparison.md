# Indirect treatment comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Network meta-analysis](network-meta-analysis.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) studies comparing treatments $A$ and $B$ to common control $C$, a consistent [log odds ratio](log-odds-ratio.md) comparison uses $\widehat\delta_{AB}=\widehat\delta_{AC}-\widehat\delta_{BC}$, with [variance](variance-split.md) $v_{AC}+v_{BC}$. This follows from subtracting two estimates of additive effects relative to the same reference. With overlapping evidence, subtract twice their [covariance](covariance.md). Causal comparability across the studies is supplied by [transitivity in network meta-analysis](transitivity-in-network-meta-analysis.md), not by the [variance](variance-split.md) calculation.
