# Papez circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Neural circuit](neural-circuit.md)

A historically influential arrangement connecting hippocampal, mammillary, anterior thalamic and cingulate regions. [James Papez](james-papez.md) proposed it in relation to emotion in 1937. Contemporary memory accounts require additional inputs and parallel connections; the diagram of a loop is not a proof that every connection has identical necessity.
