# Thin-film equations with insoluble surfactant

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film equation](thin-film-equation.md)

For a no-slip planar liquid film with an [insoluble surfactant](insoluble-surfactant.md), [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) gives $q=-h^3p_x/(3\mu)+h^2\gamma_x/(2\mu)$ and surface [velocity](velocity.md) $u_s=-h^2p_x/(2\mu)+h\gamma_x/\mu$. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) and [conservation of insoluble surfactant on a moving interface](conservation-of-insoluble-surfactant-on-a-moving-interface.md) give $h_t+q_x=0$ and $C_t+(Cu_s)_x=0$. With $\gamma=\gamma_0-AC$ and small slopes, $p_x=\rho gh_x-(\gamma h_{xx})_x$. The different coefficients in $q$ and $u_s$ are essential: [Marangoni stress](marangoni-effect.md) and hydrostatic return flow transport fluid and [surfactant](surfactant.md) differently.
