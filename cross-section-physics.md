# Cross section (physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_section_(physics))

An effective [area](surface-area.md) describing the strength of a specified interaction. With an incident [particle flux](particle-flux.md) $j$ and $N_T$ independent target particles, the ideal event rate for that channel is $jN_T\sigma$.
