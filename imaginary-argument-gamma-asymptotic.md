# Imaginary-argument gamma asymptotic

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma function](gamma-function.md)

Rotating the integral defining the [Gamma function](gamma-function.md) toward the positive imaginary axis introduces the factor $ie^{-\pi n/2}$. After scaling by $n$, the remaining phase is $\log s-s$, whose unique stationary point is $s=1$ with second derivative $-1$. The [stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md) gives $e^{-in-i\pi/4}\sqrt{2\pi/n}$ for this scaled integral, producing the displayed asymptotic. The boundary contour integral must be Abel-regularized; its off-saddle tails are smaller by integration by parts.
