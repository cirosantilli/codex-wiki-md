# Jacobian matrix and determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariable calculus](multivariable-calculus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobian_matrix_and_determinant)

The [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) collects all first [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) of a [differentiable map](differentiable-map.md). When it is square, its [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) gives the signed local volume factor.
