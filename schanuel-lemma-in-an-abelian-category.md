# Schanuel lemma in an abelian category

↑ **Parent:** [Short exact sequence in an abelian category](short-exact-sequence-in-an-abelian-category.md)

Two projective presentations of the same object in an [abelian category](abelian-category.md), with kernels $K,K'$ and middle objects $P,P'$, satisfy $K\oplus P'\cong K'\oplus P$. Form their [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md). Its two induced [short exact sequences in an abelian category](short-exact-sequence-in-an-abelian-category.md) split because each quotient is a [projective object in a category](projective-object.md). The two resulting [biproduct](biproduct.md) descriptions of the pullback give the isomorphism.
