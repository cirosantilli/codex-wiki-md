# Dihedral splitting when the half-rotation order is odd

↑ **Parent:** [Dihedral group](dihedral-group.md)

Let $r$ have order $2m$ with $m$ odd, and let $s^2=1$, $srs=r^{-1}$. The [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H=\langle r^2,s\rangle$ is a dihedral group of order $2m$, while $K=\langle r^m\rangle$ is a central [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) of order two. Since $m$ is odd, $H\cap K=\{1\}$ and $r$ lies in $HK$ by [Bézout's identity](bezout-identity.md). Thus the original group of order $4m$ is the [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) $H\times K$. This statement specifies group orders and avoids the two competing conventions for dihedral notation.
