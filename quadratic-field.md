# Quadratic field

↑ **Parent:** [Number field](number-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_field)

A quadratic number field is a [number field](number-field.md) of [degree of a field extension](degree-of-a-field-extension.md) two, necessarily of the form $\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$ for a [square-free integer](square-free-integer.md) $d\ne0,1$. Its two [field embeddings](field-embedding.md) send $a+b\sqrt d$ to $a\pm b\sqrt d$. If $d>0$, both images lie in the [real numbers](real-number.md), giving a [totally real number field](totally-real-number-field.md); if $d<0$, they form a pair related by [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md), giving an [imaginary quadratic field](imaginary-quadratic-field.md). The [ring of integers of a quadratic field](ring-of-integers-of-a-quadratic-field.md) depends on $d$ modulo four.[Keith Conrad, Factoring in Quadratic Fields, §§1–3](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/gradnumthy/quadraticgrad.pdf)
