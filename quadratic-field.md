# Quadratic field

↑ **Parent:** [Number field](number-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_field)

A quadratic number field is a degree-two extension of $\mathbb Q$, necessarily of the form $\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$ for a square-free integer $d\ne0,1$.
