# Interferometric coherence

↑ **Parent:** [Interferometric synthetic-aperture radar](interferometric-synthetic-aperture-radar.md)

A normalized measure of consistency of complex radar returns over a neighborhood. Low coherence makes interferometric [phase](phase-waves.md) unreliable and can arise from vegetation, surface changes, viewing differences or extreme displacement gradients.
