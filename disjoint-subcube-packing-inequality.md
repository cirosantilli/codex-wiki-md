# Disjoint subcube packing inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Separating family of disjoint set pairs](separating-family-of-disjoint-set-pairs.md)

Pairwise disjoint subcubes of $\{0,1\}^m$ fixing $d_1,\ldots,d_n$ coordinates satisfy $\sum_{x=1}^n2^{-d_x}\leq1$. By [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md), their average codimension is at least $\log_2n$. Consequently a [separating family of disjoint set pairs](separating-family-of-disjoint-set-pairs.md) of total incidence at most $\lambda mn$, with $\lambda>0$, has $m\geq\lceil\log_2n/\lambda\rceil$.
