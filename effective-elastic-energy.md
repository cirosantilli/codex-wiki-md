# Effective elastic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Composite material](composite-material.md)

The [effective elastic energy](effective-elastic-energy.md) is the minimum averaged [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) under prescribed affine boundary [displacement](displacement.md). For differentiable stable elastic response without [body force](body-force.md), varying the prescribed mean [strain](strain.md) gives $DW^{\mathrm{eff}}(E)=\langle\sigma\rangle$. The result follows by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md), because the interior variation with zero boundary data does no work against an equilibrated [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md).
