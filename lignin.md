# Lignin

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

[lignin](lignin.md) is an aromatic plant-wall polymer that contributes to mechanical and chemical resistance. It can obstruct access to [cellulose](cellulose.md) and adsorb enzymes nonproductively.
