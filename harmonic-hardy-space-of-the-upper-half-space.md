# Harmonic Hardy space of the upper half-space

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)

For the upper half-space $H^{d+1}=\mathbb R^d\times(0,\infty)$, this is the space of [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) with finite [norm](norm.md) $\|u\|_{h^1}=\sup_{t>0}\int_{\mathbb R^d}|u(x,t)|\,dx$. It differs from an analytic [Hardy space](hardy-space.md): no [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) condition is imposed, and its boundary object can be a singular finite [Borel measure](borel-measure.md).
