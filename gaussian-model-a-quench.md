# Gaussian Model A quench

↑ **Parent:** [Nonconserved order-parameter dynamics](nonconserved-order-parameter-dynamics.md)

After a sudden change from quadratic kernel $K_I(q)$ to $K_F(q)$, each Fourier mode is an [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md) with decay rate $r(q)=\Gamma K_F(q)$. Its equal-time covariance interpolates exponentially between $k_BT/K_I(q)$ and $k_BT/K_F(q)$.
