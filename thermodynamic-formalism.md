# Thermodynamic formalism

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)

Thermodynamic formalism studies weighted orbit sums, [invariant measures](invariant-measure.md) and geometric scaling through analogues of [partition functions](canonical-partition-function.md). A symbolic potential assigns a weight to each orbit segment; its asymptotic logarithmic normalization is [topological pressure](topological-pressure.md). The resulting [Gibbs measure](gibbs-measure.md) depends on the potential, so choosing an arbitrary Gibbs law does not automatically select geometric volume.
