# Double commutant theorem for star-algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Commutant of an operator algebra](commutant-of-an-operator-algebra.md)

A unital [subalgebra](subalgebra.md) $A\subseteq\operatorname{End}(V)$ closed under the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md), on a finite-dimensional real or complex [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), equals its double [commutant of an operator algebra](commutant-of-an-operator-algebra.md). Here is a proof that does not require a classification of [algebras](algebra-split.md). For $N=\dim V$, choose a [basis](basis.md) $(v_1,\ldots,v_N)$ and let $W=\{(av_1,\ldots,av_N):a\in A\}\subseteq V^N$. The diagonal action of $A$ preserves $W$ and $W^\perp$, so the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto $W$ commutes with that action. Each matrix entry of this projection belongs to $A'$. Hence the diagonal action of $b\in A''$ commutes with the projection and preserves $W$. Since $1\in A$, the tuple $(v_1,\ldots,v_N)$ belongs to $W$, and therefore $(bv_1,\ldots,bv_N)=(av_1,\ldots,av_N)$ for one $a\in A$. This implies $b=a$. Without the unital hypothesis the conclusion is $A''=A+\mathbb F1$.
