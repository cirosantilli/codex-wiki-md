# A conformal Gauss map does not imply a minimal surface

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss map](gauss-map.md)

For a [minimal surface](minimal-surface.md), the [shape operator](shape-operator.md) has eigenvalues $k,-k$, so $\langle dN(v),dN(w)\rangle=k^2\langle v,w\rangle$. Conversely this metric identity only implies that the two principal curvatures have equal squares. A sphere has equal nonzero principal curvatures and satisfies the identity while having nonzero [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md). The distinction is between opposite and equal signs, not equality of magnitudes.
