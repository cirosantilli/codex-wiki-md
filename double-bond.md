# Double bond

↑ **Parent:** [Covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md)

A [double bond](double-bond.md) is a covalent bond of bond order two. Restricted rotation and substituent geometry at a carbon–carbon double bond can affect acyl-chain packing in a [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md).
