# Bohr radius

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohr_radius)

For reduced mass $\mu$, the Coulomb length scale is

$$
a=\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar^2}{\mu e^2}.
$$
