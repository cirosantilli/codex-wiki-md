<h1 id="baillon-haddad-theorem">Baillon–Haddad theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cocoercivity](cocoercivity.md)

On the whole Euclidean space, an $L$-[Lipschitz gradient](lipschitz-gradient.md) of a [convex function](convex-function.md) is $1/L$-cocoercive. Subtract the supporting affine function at one point, apply the [descent lemma](descent-lemma.md) to a gradient step at the other point, then interchange the points and add. The conclusion needs convexity but no second derivative.
