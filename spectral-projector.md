# Spectral projector

↑ **Parent:** [Projection-valued measure](projection-valued-measure.md)

A spectral projector is the value $E(B)$ of the [projection-valued measure](projection-valued-measure.md) of a normal operator on a measurable part $B$ of its spectrum. In finite dimension, the spectral projector onto an eigenspace extracts the corresponding eigenvector component.
