# Conical pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Simple pendulum](simple-pendulum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conical_pendulum)

A conical pendulum moves at constant latitude around a vertical axis. For length $\ell$ and angle $\theta$ from the downward vertical, the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives $\dot\phi^2\cos\theta=g/\ell$. Its horizontal speed satisfies $v^2=g\ell\sin^2\theta/\cos\theta$. It is a useful example of a relative equilibrium: the position changes while its shape relative to the symmetry axis is fixed.
