# Kinetic Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect

↑ **Parent:** [Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md)

Bulk motion of a scattering electron cloud relative to the [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background.md) gives a first-order Doppler [temperature](temperature.md) shift. With positive $v_{\parallel}$ directed away from the observer, the displayed sign applies, and $\tau$ is the [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) [optical depth](optical-depth.md). In the nonrelativistic, single-scattering limit it has the spectrum of a small [blackbody](blackbody.md) temperature change, unlike the [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md).
