# Essential surjectivity

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence of categories](equivalence-of-categories.md)

A [functor](functor.md) $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ is essentially surjective when every object of $\mathcal D$ is isomorphic to $FA$ for some $A\in\mathcal C$. Together with [full and faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md), this characterizes an [equivalence of categories](equivalence-of-categories.md) under the appropriate [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md) convention. For large categories, choosing a quasi-inverse on all objects requires a universe or a class-choice convention.
