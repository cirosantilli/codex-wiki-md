# Modification site occupancy

↑ **Parent:** [Post-translational modification](post-translational-modification.md)

The fraction of molecules modified at a specified site under specified conditions. Occupancies at separate sites do not alone establish the joint distribution of modifications across intact [proteoforms](proteoform.md).
