# Total ramification criterion for local cyclotomic extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic extension of a p-adic field](cyclotomic-extension-of-a-p-adic-field.md)

Here $a\geq0$ and $p\nmid m$. The prime-to-$p$ [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) generate an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of degree equal to the multiplicative order of $p$ modulo $m$; for $m=1$ this degree is one. Indeed, their distinct residues lift uniquely by [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md), and the [Frobenius automorphism](frobenius-automorphism.md) acts by raising them to $p$. The $p$-power [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) generate a [totally ramified extension](totally-ramified-extension.md), because the translated [cyclotomic polynomial](cyclotomic-polynomial.md) $\Phi_{p^a}(1+T)$ is Eisenstein. The two fields have trivial intersection and their compositum has the same residue degree as the prime-to-$p$ field, by [unramified base change preserves total ramification](unramified-base-change-preserves-total-ramification.md). Thus total ramification is equivalent to $p\equiv1\pmod m$.
