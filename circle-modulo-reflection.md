# Circle modulo reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient topology](quotient-topology.md)

Identify points of the [circle](circle.md) having the same real coordinate, equivalently points related by [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md). The [continuous](continuous-function.md) [surjection](surjective-function.md) $h(z)=(1+\operatorname{Re}z)/2$ has exactly these [equivalence classes](equivalence-class.md) as its fibers. It induces a [continuous bijection](continuous-bijection.md) from the [quotient space](quotient-topological-space.md) to the [closed interval](closed-real-interval.md). The [quotient space](quotient-topological-space.md) is [compact](compact-space.md) as a [continuous image of a compact space](continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md), while the [closed interval](closed-real-interval.md) is [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md), so the [compact-to-Hausdorff continuous bijection theorem](compact-to-hausdorff-continuous-bijection-theorem.md) makes the induced map a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md). The two fixed points of the reflection become the two interval endpoints.
