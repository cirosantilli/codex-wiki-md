# Slow and entropy fluctuations in reduced magnetohydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced magnetohydrodynamics](reduced-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

In ideal [reduced magnetohydrodynamics](reduced-magnetohydrodynamics.md) with constant background sound and [Alfvén speeds](alfven-speed.md), perpendicular total-pressure balance gives $\delta p+\rho_0v_A^2\delta B_\parallel/B_0=0$. Put $s=\delta p/p_0-\gamma\delta\rho/\rho_0$, $r=\delta\rho/\rho_0+s/\gamma$, $c_T=v_Ac_s/(v_A^2+c_s^2)^{1/2}$ and $h=c_s^2r/c_T$. Then the parallel momentum, continuity and induction equations yield

$$
Du_\parallel=-c_T\nabla_\parallel h,\qquad Dh=-c_T\nabla_\parallel u_\parallel,\qquad Ds=0.
$$

Thus $f^\pm=u_\parallel\pm h$ satisfy $Df^\pm=\mp c_T\nabla_\parallel f^\pm$. The two slow-wave populations propagate along bent [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md) and are mixed by the perpendicular [Alfvénic turbulence](alfvenic-turbulence.md), while the [entropy](entropy.md) fluctuation is simply advected. They do not force the perpendicular Alfvénic system at this leading order.
