# Parallel frame along a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel transport](parallel-transport.md)

A parallel frame along a curve consists of a basis of [vector fields](vector-field.md) $E_a$ satisfying $D_tE_a=0$ for its [covariant derivative along a curve](covariant-derivative-along-a-curve.md). Transporting a basis at the initial point constructs the frame. If the connection preserves the metric, an initially orthonormal basis stays orthonormal. In this frame the [Jacobi equation](jacobi-equation.md) becomes a linear matrix [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md).
