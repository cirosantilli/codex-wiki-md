# Planck 2015 inflation constraints by Planck Collaboration

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Planck Collaboration](work-by-planck-collaboration.md)

Primary analysis of the 2015 [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) data and inflationary implications. The original February2015 version reports a red scalar tilt and upper limits on tensors; it already disfavors a simple quadratic potential relative to lower-tensor alternatives. Original version: [https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.02114v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.02114v1) . Full title: Planck2015results.XX.Constraints on inflation.
