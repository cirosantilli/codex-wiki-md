# Bounding volume hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Bounding volume](bounding-volume.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bounding_volume_hierarchy)

A [bounding volume hierarchy](bounding-volume-hierarchy.md) stores enclosing volumes in a tree, with each internal volume enclosing all descendants. Collision detection descends only through pairs of overlapping volumes.
