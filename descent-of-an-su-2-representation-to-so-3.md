<h1 id="descent-of-an-su-2-representation-to-so-3">Descent of an SU(2) representation to SO(3)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint double cover from SU(2) to SO(3)](adjoint-double-cover-from-su-2-to-so-3.md)

A [SU(2) representation](representation-theory-of-su-2.md) descends to the [SO(3) group](so-3-group.md) exactly when $-I$ acts trivially. In $\operatorname{Sym}^n(\mathbb C^2)$ it acts by $(-1)^n$, so descent holds precisely for even $n$, or integer spin $j=n/2$. This is the [inflation of a group representation](inflation-of-a-group-representation.md) criterion for the kernel $\{\pm I\}$. Half-integer-spin representations instead remain representations of the covering group.
