# Martingale product identity

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md)

For continuous local martingales $M$ and $N$, the [Itô product rule](ito-product-rule.md) says that

$$
M_tN_t-M_0N_0-[M,N]_t
$$

is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). If the martingales are square-integrable and converge in $L^2$, then

$$
\mathbb E[M_\infty N_\infty]=\mathbb E[M_0N_0]+\mathbb E[M,N]_\infty.
$$
