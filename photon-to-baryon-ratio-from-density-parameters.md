# Photon-to-baryon ratio from density parameters

↑ **Parent:** [Photon-to-baryon ratio](photon-to-baryon-ratio.md)

With $n_\gamma=0.24Q^3$, $u_\gamma=0.66\hbar cQ^4$, $Q=k_BT/(\hbar c)$ and $n_B=\Omega_B\rho_c/m_p$, eliminate $Q$ using $u_\gamma=\Omega_\gamma\rho_cc^2$. The prefactor is $0.24/0.66^{3/4}=0.328$. Here $\rho_c$ is the [mass-equivalent cosmological critical density](mass-equivalent-cosmological-critical-density.md); inserting an energy-density critical value without its $c^2$ conversion would give incorrect dimensions. A numerical estimate needs photon and baryon density parameters as additional inputs.
