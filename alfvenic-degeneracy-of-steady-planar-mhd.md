<h1 id="alfvenic-degeneracy-of-steady-planar-mhd">Alfvénic degeneracy of steady planar MHD</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Steady planar ideal-MHD field-line invariants](steady-planar-ideal-mhd-field-line-invariants.md)

The two axial invariants in [steady planar ideal-MHD field-line invariants](steady-planar-ideal-mhd-field-line-invariants.md) give the displayed identity. When $V-U\ne0$, a finite smooth flow cannot reach horizontal [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md). When $V=U$, an everywhere Alfvénic branch is possible even with $B_z\ne0$: constant $\rho$ and $\mathbf u=\mathbf B/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$ give an example. The nondegenerate exclusion must not be applied to this branch.
