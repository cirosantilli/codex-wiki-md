# Derivation Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Derivation of a Lie algebra](derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

The [derivations of a Lie algebra](derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) form a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) of $\operatorname{End}(\mathfrak g)$ with the [commutator](commutator.md) bracket. The identity $[D,\operatorname{ad}x]=\operatorname{ad}(Dx)$ makes the inner derivations an [ideal of a Lie algebra](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md). For a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), the [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md) and the vanishing [center of a Lie algebra](center-of-a-lie-algebra.md) imply $\operatorname{Der}(\mathfrak g)=\operatorname{ad}\mathfrak g$.
