Here $[a]$ is the trivial [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of $S_a$, $[0]$ is the induction-product unit, $[a]=0$ for $a<0$, and multiplication is induction of external [tensor products](tensor-product.md). The [Frobenius characteristic map](frobenius-characteristic-map.md) turns the expression into the [Jacobi–Trudi identity](jacobi-trudi-identity.md). A sign-reversing involution on intersecting lattice-path systems leaves precisely the nonintersecting systems corresponding to [Semistandard Young tableaux](semistandard-young-tableau.md), proving the [determinant](determinant.md) identity.
