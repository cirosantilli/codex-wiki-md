# Change of dependent variable

↑ **Parent:** [Change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md)

A change of dependent variable expresses the unknown [function](function-split.md) of a [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) in terms of a new unknown, while keeping the independent variable fixed. For example, substituting $y=e^{-ax/2}v$ in $y''+ay'+by=f$ removes the first [derivative](derivative.md) and gives $v''+(b-a^2/4)v=e^{ax/2}f$ when $a,b$ are constant. Transforming the [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) as well is essential, particularly for a [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md).
