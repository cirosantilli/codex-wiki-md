# Single slit

↑ **Parent:** [Single-slit diffraction](single-slit-diffraction.md)

A single transmitting aperture whose finite width produces [diffraction](diffraction.md). For a uniformly illuminated rectangular opening, its far-field amplitude is the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) of a constant over that opening, giving the [sinc function](sinc-function.md) pattern.
