# Nondegeneracy of the trace form of a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Trace form of a number field](trace-form-of-a-number-field.md)

For nonzero $x\in K$, choose $y=x^{-1}$. Then

$$
B(x,y)=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(1)=[K:\mathbb Q]\ne0,
$$

so the trace form is [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md).
