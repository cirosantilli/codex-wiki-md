# Picard operator on a closed ball

↑ **Parent:** [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](picard-lindelof-theorem.md)

Suppose $f$ is continuous and Lipschitz in its state variable on a closed cylinder $|t-a|\leq h$, $\|y-b\|\leq k$, with bounds $\|f\|\leq M$ and Lipschitz constant $L_f$. On a smaller interval of radius $\eta$, the displayed [Picard operator](picard-operator-on-a-closed-ball.md) maps continuous curves in that [closed ball](closed-ball.md) into itself when $\eta M\leq k$, and it is a [contraction mapping](contraction-mapping.md) in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) when $\eta L_f<1$. This function space is complete because it is a closed subset of a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) of continuous vector-valued [functions](function-split.md). The [contraction mapping theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md) gives a unique [fixed point](fixed-point.md), and the [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) makes it a solution of the [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md). Choosing $\eta M<k$ also prevents any solution with the same initial data from first exiting the cylinder during that interval.
