↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)

[Chelex extraction](chelex-extraction.md) releases [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) from heated [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) in the presence of a resin that binds metal ions. The [PV92 teaching experiment](pv92-teaching-experiment.md) transfers the [supernatant](supernatant.md), leaving resin and debris behind.
