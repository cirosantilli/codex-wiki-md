# First odd bound-state threshold of a square well

↑ **Parent:** [Finite square well](finite-square-well.md)

For a symmetric attractive well of width $a$ and depth $V_0$, the first odd [bound state](bound-state.md) obeys $-k\cot(ka/2)=q>0$, where $q$ is the inverse tail [length](length.md). Its lowest branch has $\pi<ka<2\pi$. Since $k^2<2mV_0/\hbar^2$, existence requires $V_0>V_{\rm odd}$; it is also sufficient by continuity of the matching equation on that branch. At equality the exterior solution has zero decay rate and is not square-integrable. Thus a well at or below this threshold has only its even [ground state](ground-state.md).
