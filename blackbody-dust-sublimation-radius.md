# Blackbody dust sublimation radius

↑ **Parent:** [Dust sublimation](dust-sublimation.md)

A spherical black grain of [radius](radius.md) $a$ absorbs luminosity $L$ through projected area $\pi a^2$ and emits over $4\pi a^2$. Radiative balance at sublimation [temperature](temperature-split.md) gives $r_{\rm sub}=[L/(16\pi\sigma_{\rm SB}T_{\rm sub}^4)]^{1/2}$. Grain size cancels only under the stated unit-efficiency approximation; shielding and wavelength-dependent emissivity change the result.
