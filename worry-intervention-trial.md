# Worry Intervention Trial

↑ **Parent:** [Contributory-causal treatment development](contributory-causal-treatment-development.md)

A [randomised](randomization.md) trial of a brief worry-focused intervention added to standard care for persistent [persecutory delusions](persecutory-delusion.md). The standard-care comparator permits inference about the added package, while unequal therapeutic contact limits a more ingredient-specific claim.[Lancet Psychiatry 2, 305–313; 150 patients, six worry sessions plus usual care versus usual care, eight-/24-week outcomes](effects-of-cognitive-behaviour-therapy-for-worry-on-persecutory-delusions-in-patients-with-psychosis-wit-a-parallel-single-blind-randomised-controlled-trial-with-a-mediation-analysis-by-daniel-freeman-and-others.md)
