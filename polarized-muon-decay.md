# Polarized muon decay

↑ **Parent:** [Muon decay](muon-decay.md)

For a negative [muon](muon.md) with rest-frame [spin](spin.md) vector $\mathbf s$, the leading massless-electron distribution is $d\Gamma/(dx\,d\Omega)=G_F^2m_\mu^5x^2[(3-2x)+(1-2x)\widehat{\mathbf k}\cdot\mathbf s]/(384\pi^4)$, where $x=2E_e/m_\mu\in[0,1]$. Its final-spin sum is $64G_F^2(k\cdot q')(q\cdot(p-m_\mu s))$. The [spin](spin.md) vector is axial whereas the electron direction is polar, so their scalar product reverses under [parity](parity.md). Its occurrence demonstrates parity violation by the left-chiral [weak charged current](charged-current.md).
