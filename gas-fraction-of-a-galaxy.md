# Gas fraction of a galaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic chemical evolution](galactic-chemical-evolution.md)

The gas fraction of a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) is commonly $\mu=M_g/(M_g+M_s)$, using the gas and stellar masses remaining in the system. In a [leaky-box model of galactic chemical evolution](leaky-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md), it differs from the fraction $g=M_g/M_{g,0}$ of the initial gas reservoir. For an initially star-free box with outflow rate $\alpha\dot M_s$, $\mu=(1+\alpha)g/(1+\alpha g)$. These different fractions give different-looking metallicity formulae and [effective yields](effective-yield.md).
