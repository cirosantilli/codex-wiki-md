# Finite prime-power Teichmuller representative

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmuller representative](teichmuller-representative.md)

For a prime $p$ and $n\geq1$, the unique residue $y$ modulo $p^n$ satisfying $y\equiv x\pmod p$ and $y^p\equiv y\pmod{p^n}$ is the displayed residue. Existence follows from [Fermat's little theorem](fermat-little-theorem.md) and [prime-power contraction under pth powers](prime-power-contraction-under-pth-powers.md). Uniqueness follows by raising any other such residue repeatedly to the $p$th power and applying the same contraction to its congruence with $x$. This is the finite-modulus version of a [Teichmuller representative](teichmuller-representative.md), including the zero class.
