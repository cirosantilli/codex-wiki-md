# Mixture distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

A mixture distribution first chooses a component $j$ with nonnegative [mixture weights](mixture-weight.md) $w_j$ summing to one, then samples from its law $P_j$. Consequently $P(A)=\sum_jw_jP_j(A)$, and any finite [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is the same weighted sum of the component transforms. A component may be a [Dirac measure](dirac-measure.md), so a mixture may have both discrete and continuous parts.
