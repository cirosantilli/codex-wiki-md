# Stress glut tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md)

A [stress glut tensor](stress-glut-tensor.md) describes a seismic source through an equivalent body-force [density](density.md) $\mathbf f=-\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\Gamma$. For a symmetric [stress glut tensor](stress-glut-tensor.md) supported away from the boundary, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) makes its coupling to a [normal mode](normal-mode.md) equal to $\int\boldsymbol\Gamma:\mathbf e^*\,dV$, where $\mathbf e$ is that mode's [strain](strain.md).
