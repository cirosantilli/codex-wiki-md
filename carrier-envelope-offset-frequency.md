# Carrier-envelope offset frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Optical frequency comb](optical-frequency-comb.md)

The [carrier-envelope offset frequency](carrier-envelope-offset-frequency.md) expresses the carrier's pulse-to-pulse [phase](phase-waves.md) slip relative to its envelope. Fixing the repetition [frequency](frequency.md) leaves this slip undetermined.[Defining and Measuring Optical Frequencies: The Optical Clock Opportunity – and More, p.131: offset](defining-and-measuring-optical-frequencies-the-optical-clock-opportunity-and-more-by-john-l-hall.md) With the positive-exponential convention derived above, $f_0=f_r\Delta\phi/(2\pi)$ modulo $f_r$. Changing $f_0$ translates every tooth equally; differentiating $f_n=n f_r+f_0$ at fixed $n$ gives

$$
\delta f_n=n\,\delta f_r+\delta f_0.
$$

Thus at $n=2\times10^6$, a chosen $1\,\mathrm{Hz}$ change in spacing moves that tooth by $2\,\mathrm{MHz}$ if $f_0$ stays fixed. The large multiplier is why a stable-looking pulse train is insufficient for accurate optical [metrology](metrology.md).
