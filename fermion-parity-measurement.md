# Fermion-parity measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion parity](fermion-parity.md)

A fermion-parity measurement projects onto an eigenspace of the parity of a chosen collection of fermionic modes. For two [Majorana fermion operators](majorana-fermion-operator.md) $\gamma_a$ and $\gamma_b$, the projectors are $(1\pm i\gamma_a\gamma_b)/2$.
