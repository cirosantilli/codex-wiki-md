# Superlattice

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superlattice)

A [superlattice](superlattice.md) is a spatial periodic structure with a repeat length larger than an underlying atomic lattice period. Its additional [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vectors fold electronic bands into a smaller [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md), and allowed couplings can open small gaps at the folded crossings. The gap sizes depend on potential Fourier components and symmetry, not on the geometric period alone.
