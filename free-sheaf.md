# Free sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md)

A free sheaf of finite rank on a [scheme](scheme.md) is a [sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md) globally isomorphic to $\mathcal O_X^{\oplus r}$. A [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md) only requires such an isomorphism on an open cover; its local frames need not glue to one global frame.
