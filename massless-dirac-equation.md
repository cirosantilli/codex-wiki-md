# Massless Dirac equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md)

The massless [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) is $\gamma^m\partial_m\Psi=0$. The [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) makes its square the massless wave equation. Quantization of a worldline spin variable as $\widehat\psi^m=\gamma^m/\sqrt2$ turns its fermionic constraint into this equation.
