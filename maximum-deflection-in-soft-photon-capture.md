# Maximum deflection in soft-photon capture

↑ **Parent:** [Soft-photon resonant capture threshold](soft-photon-resonant-capture-threshold.md)

When $e\ll m\ll E_0$, the incoming massive-particle [energy](energy-split.md) is $E\simeq2E_0/(1-\cos\theta)$. [Momentum](momentum-split.md) addition then gives $\tan\phi\simeq[e/(2E_0)]\sin\theta(1-\cos\theta)$. Differentiating the angular factor gives its maximum at $\cos\theta=-1/2$. This is an asymptotic result for both the energy-angle constraint and final deflection; corrections are controlled by $m^2/E_0^2$ and $e/E_0$.
