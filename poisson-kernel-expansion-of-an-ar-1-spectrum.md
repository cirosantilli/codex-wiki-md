<h1 id="poisson-kernel-expansion-of-an-ar-1-spectrum">Poisson-kernel expansion of an AR(1) spectrum</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive process of order one](autoregressive-process-of-order-one.md)

For $|\phi|<1$, the bilateral sum $\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}\phi^{|k|}z^k$ equals $(1-\phi^2)/((1-\phi z)(1-\phi z^{-1}))$ on the unit circle. Multiplying by $\sigma^2/(\pi(1-\phi^2))$ gives the one-sided autoregressive spectrum and reads off its [covariance](covariance.md) coefficients.
