# Normal approximation to a compound Poisson aggregate

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md)

For a [compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md) of count parameter $\lambda$ and severity [moments](moment.md) $m_1,m_2$, the [expected value](expected-value.md) is $\lambda m_1$ and the [variance](variance-split.md) is $\lambda m_2$. As $\lambda\to\infty$ with the severity law fixed and $m_2<\infty$, the standardized aggregate converges in distribution to the standard [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). Indeed, the logarithm of its [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) is $\lambda[\varphi_X(t/\sqrt{\lambda m_2})-1]-it\lambda m_1/\sqrt{\lambda m_2}$; the second-order expansion of $\varphi_X$ makes this tend to $-t^2/2$. The approximation loses the zero atom and assigns positive [probability](probability.md) to negative losses.
