# Lambert series

↑ **Parent:** [Series (mathematics)](series-mathematics.md)

A Lambert series has the form $\sum_{n\ge1}a_nq^n/(1-q^n)$. Expanding each denominator as a [geometric series](geometric-series.md) shows that its coefficient of $q^m$ is $\sum_{d\mid m}a_d$.
