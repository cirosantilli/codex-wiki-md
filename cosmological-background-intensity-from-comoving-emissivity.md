# Cosmological background intensity from comoving emissivity

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological spectral background](cosmological-spectral-background.md)

Let $\epsilon_{\nu_e}(z)$ be the [comoving spectral emissivity](comoving-spectral-emissivity.md), namely total emitted [spectral luminosity](spectral-luminosity.md) per [comoving volume](comoving-volume.md). In a homogeneous transparent [FRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), a radial shell contributes its [comoving volume](comoving-volume.md) times the [cosmological spectral flux-density relation](cosmological-spectral-flux-density-relation.md). Its transverse-area factors cancel, leaving the displayed integral. In a flat model this cancellation is explicit: $dV_c=r^2(c/H)dz\,d\Omega$, whereas $F_{\nu_0}=(1+z)L_{\nu_e}/[4\pi(1+z)^2r^2]$. Absorption inserts a transmission factor, and evolving sources are represented by the redshift dependence of $\epsilon$.
