# Grothendieck space

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grothendieck_space)

A Banach space $X$ is a Grothendieck space when every weak-star convergent sequence in $X^*$ is weakly convergent. Every [reflexive Banach space](reflexive-banach-space.md) has this property, and every separable Grothendieck space is reflexive.
