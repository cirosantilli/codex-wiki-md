# Sensory preconditioning

↑ **Parent:** [Classical conditioning](classical-conditioning.md)

Training a relation between initially neutral cues before giving one cue biological significance. Responding to the other cue can then depend on inference through the learned structure rather than direct cue–outcome reinforcement.
