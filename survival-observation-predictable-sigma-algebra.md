# Survival-observation predictable sigma-algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md)

On $\Omega=(0,\infty)$, observe only $X_t(\omega)=\mathbf1_{\{t\leq\omega\}}$. Before and at the observed lifetime, the [natural filtration](natural-filtration.md) cannot distinguish any two surviving outcomes. The [predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md) on positive times therefore consists exactly of sets whose trace on $D=\{\omega\geq t\}$ is $D\cap(\Omega\times T)$ for a [Borel set](borel-set.md) $T$, and whose trace on $D^c$ is arbitrary product-Borel. To prove sufficiency, $D$ is predictable because $X$ is left-continuous and adapted; on $D^c$, insert a rational $s$ with $\omega<s<t$ and use the predictable rectangles $(E\cap(0,s))\times(s,\infty)$.
