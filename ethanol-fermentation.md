# Ethanol fermentation

↑ **Parent:** [Fermentation](fermentation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethanol_fermentation)

[Alcoholic fermentation](ethanol-fermentation.md) produces [ethanol](ethanol.md) and [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) from [sugars](sugar.md) through an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md)-catalyzed pathway. See [Saccharomyces cerevisiae](saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md) for its ideal [glucose](glucose.md) balance and [yeast](yeast.md) for the distinction between living cells and active extracts.
