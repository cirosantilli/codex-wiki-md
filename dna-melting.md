# DNA melting

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

[DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) melting is local or extensive separation of the two strands when stabilizing [base pairing](base-pairing.md) and stacking no longer maintain a duplex. [Temperature](temperature-split.md), chemical environment or [proteins](protein-split.md) can favour opening; a [transcription initiation](transcription-initiation.md) bubble is a localized example.
