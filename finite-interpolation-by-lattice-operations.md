# Finite interpolation by lattice operations

↑ **Parent:** [Order n-transitivity](order-n-transitivity.md)

Suppose $h_{ij}$ sends the $i$th and $j$th nodes of an increasing input tuple to the corresponding output nodes. At the $i$th input, every $h_{ij}$ agrees, so their pointwise [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) takes the required value. At every other input it is bounded above by the required value, using the corresponding $j$ term. The pointwise [maximum](maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) over $i$ therefore interpolates the entire tuple. The construction uses closure under finite [join](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) and [meet](greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md), without assuming arbitrary restrictions of group elements belong to the group.
