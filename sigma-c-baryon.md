# Sigma c baryon

↑ **Parent:** [Charmed baryon](charmed-baryon.md)

The symmetric light pairs $uu,\{ud\},dd$ with one [charm quark](charm-quark.md) give [isospin](isospin.md) one and charges $2,1,0$. Their light spin is one; coupling the charm spin gives the $J=1/2$ [Sigma c baryon](sigma-c-baryon.md) triplet and $J=3/2$ excited-spin triplet conventionally marked by a star.
