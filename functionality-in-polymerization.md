# Functionality in polymerization

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerization](polymerization.md)

The [functionality in polymerization](functionality-in-polymerization.md) is the number of reactive [functional groups](functional-group.md) on a [molecule](molecule-split.md) for the specified joining chemistry. The [linear step-growth molecule count](linear-step-growth-molecule-count.md) assumes $f=2$ for every feed [molecule](molecule-split.md). A [diol](diol.md) supplies two [hydroxy groups](hydroxy-group.md) in the [polyurethane](polyurethane.md) example; an impurity supplying only one suitable group can cap a chain. A group count must reflect the selected [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md), not simply every chemically recognizable group in the [molecule](molecule-split.md).
