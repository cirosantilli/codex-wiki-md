# Conjugation preserves element order

↑ **Parent:** [Order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md)

For every positive integer $n$, cancellation gives $(hah^{-1})^n=ha^nh^{-1}$. Thus the power on one side is the [identity element](identity-element.md) exactly when $a^n=e$, proving preservation of the [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md), including infinite order. In particular, $ab$ and $ba$ are conjugate because $a^{-1}(ab)a=ba$, and hence have the same order.
