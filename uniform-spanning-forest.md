# Uniform spanning forest

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform spanning tree](uniform-spanning-tree.md)

A uniform spanning forest is an infinite-volume limit of [uniform spanning trees](uniform-spanning-tree.md) on finite graph approximations. Free and wired boundary identifications give the [free uniform spanning forest](free-uniform-spanning-forest.md) and [wired uniform spanning forest](wired-uniform-spanning-forest.md), respectively.
