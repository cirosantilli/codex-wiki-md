# Spatial light modulator

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatial_light_modulator)

A [spatial light modulator](spatial-light-modulator.md) applies an electronically controlled spatially varying optical phase, amplitude, or polarization change. In a [4f pulse shaper](4f-pulse-shaper.md), frequency maps to transverse position, allowing the pixels to implement a calibrated spectral mask. A phase-only modulator generally needs additional polarization optics or another modulation stage for independent amplitude control.
