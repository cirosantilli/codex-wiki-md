# Beam optics

↑ **Parent:** [Particle accelerator](particle-accelerator-split.md)

The transverse transport of a charged-particle beam relative to a reference trajectory. Linear focusing gives [betatron oscillations](betatron-oscillation.md), described by [Hill's equation](hill-s-equation.md) and [Courant-Snyder parameters](courant-snyder-parameters.md); the beam envelope depends on its [geometric emittance](geometric-emittance.md).
