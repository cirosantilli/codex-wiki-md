# Kuramoto model

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuramoto_model)

The Kuramoto model couples phase oscillators by sine differences. With all-to-all coupling, $\dot\theta_i=\omega_i+Kr\sin(\psi-\theta_i)$, where $re^{i\psi}$ is the [Kuramoto order parameter](kuramoto-order-parameter.md). Natural-frequency diversity competes with coupling-induced phase coherence. In the symmetric stationary continuum branch, oscillators with $|\omega|\leq Kr$ lock to the mean phase and the remainder drift.
