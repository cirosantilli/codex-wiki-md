# Essential spectrum of a bounded self-adjoint operator

↑ **Parent:** [Essential spectrum of a closed operator](essential-spectrum-of-a-closed-operator.md)

For a bounded [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) $L$ on a complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), define

$$
\Sigma_{\mathrm e}(L)=\{\lambda\in\mathbb R:\ker(L-\lambda I)\text{ is infinite-dimensional or }\operatorname{ran}(L-\lambda I)\text{ is not closed}\}.
$$

This equals the [spectrum of a bounded operator](spectrum-of-a-bounded-operator.md) after removing its isolated [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of finite multiplicity. To see isolation when the kernel is finite and the shifted range is closed, restrict the shifted operator to its kernel complement: it is invertible there by [image-kernel orthogonality for an adjoint](image-kernel-orthogonality-for-an-adjoint.md). A [Neumann-series perturbation](neumann-series-perturbation.md) leaves nearby nonzero shifts invertible on that complement and on the finite kernel. The closed range must concern the shifted operator, not the original one.
