# Circular spacing

↑ **Parent:** [Large sieve](large-sieve.md)

Points $\theta_r$ have circular spacing at least $\delta$ when $\|\theta_r-\theta_s\|\geq\delta$ for distinct $r,s$, where $\|t\|=\min_{k\in\mathbb Z}|t-k|$. The distance is measured on the [circle group](circle-group.md) $\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$, so points near zero and one can be close.
