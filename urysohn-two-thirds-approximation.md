# Urysohn two-thirds approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Tietze extension theorem](tietze-extension-theorem.md)

Let $C$ be closed in a [normal topological space](normal-space.md) and $g:C\to[-M,M]$ continuous. Separate its closed level sets $g\leq-M/3$ and $g\geq M/3$ by [Urysohn's lemma](urysohn-s-lemma.md) to obtain $h:X\to[-M/3,M/3]$ taking the corresponding endpoint values. Checking the outer and middle ranges gives the displayed bounds. Iteration on residuals gives a uniformly convergent series with total [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) at most $M$. The [uniform limit theorem](uniform-limit-theorem.md) gives the bounded [Tietze extension theorem](tietze-extension-theorem.md).
