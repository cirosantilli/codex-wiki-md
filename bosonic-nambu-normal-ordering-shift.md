# Bosonic Nambu normal-ordering shift

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu spinor](nambu-spinor.md)

In a bosonic [Nambu spinor](nambu-spinor.md) expression, the second diagonal entry commonly contains $a_{-k}a_{-k}^\dagger$. The [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) add one constant per mode when this is normal ordered. For an antiferromagnetic chain coefficient $JS$, rewriting the quadratic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) as a full Nambu sum therefore changes the exterior constant from $-NJS^2$ to $-NJS(S+1)$. Omitting the shift corrupts the [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md) even when the dispersion is unchanged.
