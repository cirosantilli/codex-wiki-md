# Compactness of the future horismos of a trapped surface

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose singularity theorem](penrose-singularity-theorem.md)

In a future null-complete [globally hyperbolic spacetime](globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md) obeying the [null convergence condition](null-convergence-condition.md), the [future horismos](future-horismos.md) of a [compact](compact-space.md) [trapped surface](trapped-surface.md) is [compact](compact-space.md). Normalize its future null normals continuously using a timelike field. [Compactness](compact-space.md) gives a uniform negative bound $\theta\leq-c<0$ on both initial [null expansions](null-expansion.md). The [null focusing theorem](null-focusing-theorem.md) bounds all boundary generators by affine length $2/c$. Their endpoints lie in the image under a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) of the [compact](compact-space.md) bundle of normalized null normals times $[0,2/c]$. The [future horismos](future-horismos.md) is [closed](closed-set.md), so is a [compact](compact-space.md) subset of that image.
