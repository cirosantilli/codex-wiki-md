# Long-time resonance kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md)

As $t\to+\infty$, the displayed limit holds in [distribution theory](distribution-theory-split.md). Indeed $\sin(tx)/x=\tfrac12\int_{-t}^{t}e^{iux}\,du$ and the [Fourier representation of the Dirac delta function](fourier-representation-of-the-dirac-delta-function.md) gives the limit on smooth rapidly decaying [test functions](test-function.md). For a regular detuning $\Delta(\mathbf J)$, the [coarea formula](coarea-formula.md) reduces its use under an integral over [action variables](action-variable.md) to this one-dimensional identity. The kernel grows like $t$ at exact [resonance](resonance.md), so this is not pointwise convergence.
