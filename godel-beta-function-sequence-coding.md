<h1 id="godel-beta-function-sequence-coding">Gödel beta-function sequence coding</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Existential bounded representation of a primitive recursive function](existential-bounded-representation-of-a-primitive-recursive-function.md)

The Gödel beta function codes finite sequences by remainders:

$$
\beta(b,c,i)=r
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
r<1+(i+1)c\ \land\ \exists q\leq b\,[b=q(1+(i+1)c)+r].
$$

Choosing $c$ sufficiently large and divisible by the relevant small integers makes the moduli pairwise coprime, so the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) codes any prescribed finite sequence.
