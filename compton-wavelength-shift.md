# Compton wavelength shift

↑ **Parent:** [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md)

For a photon scattered through angle $\theta$ by an initially stationary free [electron](electron.md), exact relativistic [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) and [conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md) give $\lambda\prime-\lambda=(h/m_ec)(1-\cos\theta)$. Backscatter gives the maximum shift, $2h/(m_ec)$, independent of the initial photon wavelength.
