# Ultraviolet fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization group](renormalization-group.md)

A zero of the [beta function](beta-function.md) approached by the [running coupling](running-coupling.md) as the energy scale increases. A simple one-coupling fixed point with $\beta'(g_*)<0$ attracts nearby flows. Such a point can control [two-point scaling at an ultraviolet fixed point](two-point-scaling-at-an-ultraviolet-fixed-point.md).
