# Lyapunov exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyapunov_exponent)

A Lyapunov exponent measures the asymptotic exponential rate of an infinitesimal tangent displacement under a [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md), when the displayed [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) exists for a nonzero tangent vector $v$. A positive exponent represents exponential separation along that direction. In a smooth [velocity field](velocity-field.md), the tangent displacement obeys $\dot{\boldsymbol l}=(\nabla\mathbf u)\boldsymbol l$. Finite-separation growth eventually leaves this linear regime; moments of random separation need not grow at the same rate as typical separation.
