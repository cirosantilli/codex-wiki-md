# Even Dirichlet character

↑ **Parent:** [Character parity](character-parity.md)

A [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) with even parity satisfies $\chi(-n)=\chi(n)$, so its extension to [integer](integer.md) arguments is an [even function](even-function.md).
