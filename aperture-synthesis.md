# Aperture synthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Radio astronomy](radio-astronomy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aperture_synthesis)

Aperture synthesis reconstructs the sky's brightness from [complex visibilities](complex-visibility.md) measured at different [interferometric baselines](interferometric-baseline.md). Each measurement constrains a [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) component of the brightness, rather than supplying a little photograph. Combining many such components can distinguish sources that each individual [antenna](antenna-radio.md) leaves unresolved. The remarkable economy is that changing separations and the rotation of [Earth](earth.md) can provide many measurements with only a few physical [antennas](antenna-radio.md).

[Martin Ryle](martin-ryle.md)'s Cambridge group made [aperture synthesis](aperture-synthesis.md) a practical method of astronomical imaging at the Mullard Radio Astronomy Observatory. Cambridge's institutional history connects that work with the [One-Mile Telescope](one-mile-telescope.md) and its larger successors.[Cavendish Laboratory, Mullard Radio Astronomy Observatory at Lord’s Bridge, sections Foundation and early development and A centre for radio interferometry](mullard-radio-astronomy-observatory-at-lord%E2%80%99s-bridge-by-cavendish-laboratory-university-of-cambridge.md)

In his 1974 lecture Radio Telescopes of Large Resolving Power, [Martin Ryle](martin-ryle.md) recalled the problem of distinguishing weak extragalactic sources and resolving their structure. He explained sequential wavefront sampling using one simultaneously observed element as a [wave phase](phase-waves.md) reference, and the saving obtained by letting [Earth](earth.md) rotate the apparent baseline.[Ryle (1974), Radio Telescopes of Large Resolving Power, pp. 187–192, Figures 1 and 4](radio-telescopes-of-large-resolving-power-by-martin-ryle.md)

The 1974 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) distinguished [Martin Ryle](martin-ryle.md)'s observations and inventions, especially [aperture synthesis](aperture-synthesis.md), from [Antony Hewish](antony-hewish.md)'s role in discovering [pulsars](pulsar.md). The latter is a separate astronomical discovery, with its own observational history.[Nobel Prize in Physics 1974, award explanation and equal prize shares](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1974-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
