# Kirchhoff stress tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)

The Kirchhoff tensor is the [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) multiplied by the local volume ratio. Its [stress](stress.md) power is per reference volume. Pushing forward the [second Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor](second-piola-kirchhoff-stress-tensor.md) gives the displayed formula.
