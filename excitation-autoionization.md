# Excitation-autoionization

↑ **Parent:** [Collisional ionization](collisional-ionization.md)

Electron impact can excite an ion into a state above an electron-emission threshold, which then ejects a bound electron by [autoionization](autoionization.md). This two-stage excitation-autoionization contributes to [collisional ionization](collisional-ionization.md) in addition to direct impact removal.
