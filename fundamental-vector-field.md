# Fundamental vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group action](lie-group-action.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_vector_field)

For a [Lie group action](lie-group-action.md) and $X$ in its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), the fundamental [vector field](vector-field.md) is $X_M(p)=\left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_{t=0}\exp(tX)\cdot p$.
