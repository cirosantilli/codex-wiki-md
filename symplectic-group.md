# Symplectic group

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic geometry](symplectic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_group)

The real symplectic group consists of matrices $A$ satisfying $A^TJA=J$. It is an embedded matrix Lie group of dimension $2n^2+n$ in size $2n$.
