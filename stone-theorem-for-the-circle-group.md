# Stone theorem for the circle group

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary representation](unitary-representation.md)

A strongly continuous [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md) of the [circle group](circle-group.md) splits as an orthogonal sum of character subspaces. The [projections](projection-linear-algebra.md) are $P_n=\int_{\mathbb T}z^{-n}U_z\,dz$. Character [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) makes them mutually orthogonal, and [Fejér sum](fejer-sum.md) shows their ranges span the represented [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). The [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) generator has integer [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), with domain given by $\sum_n n^2\|P_n\xi\|^2<\infty$.
