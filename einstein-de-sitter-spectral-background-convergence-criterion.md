# Einstein-de Sitter spectral-background convergence criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological background intensity from comoving emissivity](cosmological-background-intensity-from-comoving-emissivity.md)

For nonevolving [comoving number density](comoving-number-density.md) $n_0$, identical spectra $L_\nu\propto\nu^\alpha$, and no attenuation, the [cosmological background intensity from comoving emissivity](cosmological-background-intensity-from-comoving-emissivity.md) in an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) is proportional to $\int_0^\infty(1+z)^{\alpha-5/2}dz$. The [improper integral](improper-integral.md) is finite exactly for $\alpha<3/2$, with value $1/(3/2-\alpha)$. Equality gives logarithmic divergence; larger $\alpha$ gives a power-law divergence. A formation epoch or a spectral break can regularize this idealized model.
