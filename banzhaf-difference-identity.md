# Banzhaf difference identity

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilistic Banzhaf index](probabilistic-banzhaf-index.md)

For distinct players $i,j$, expand each [probabilistic Banzhaf index](probabilistic-banzhaf-index.md) by whether the sampled [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) contains the other player. The terms $v(T)$ and $v(T\cup\{i,j\})$ cancel on subtraction, leaving the displayed identity. In a [weighted voting game](weighted-voting-game.md) with $w_i\leq w_j$, every summand is nonnegative, proving that the index is monotone in the player's weight. Equal weights give equal indices, although different weights can also have equal indices.
