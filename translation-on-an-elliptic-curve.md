# Translation on an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Chord-and-tangent group law](chord-and-tangent-group-law.md)

For a fixed point $Q$ on an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md), translation is the automorphism $P\mapsto P+Q$. Its inverse is translation by $-Q$. Translations let local computations of an [invariant differential on an elliptic curve](invariant-differential-on-an-elliptic-curve.md) control every point of the curve.
