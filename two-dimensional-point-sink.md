# Two-dimensional point sink

↑ **Parent:** [Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md)

A [two-dimensional point sink](two-dimensional-point-sink.md) of strength $Q>0$ removes [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $Q$ per unit depth at $z_0$. Its [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) is the displayed expression, so $u-iv=-Q/[2\pi(z-z_0)]$. The radial [velocity](velocity.md) is $u_r=-Q/(2\pi r)$: integrating over a circle gives outward flux $2\pi r u_r=-Q$. This is the planar cross-section of a [line sink](line-sink.md). A passive particle satisfies $d(r^2)/dt=-Q/\pi$ and reaches the ideal point singularity in finite time. This is a flow singularity, distinct from a stable [sink equilibrium](sink-equilibrium.md) of a regular dynamical system.
