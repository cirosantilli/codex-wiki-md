# Hopf criticality for an asymmetric Lienard center

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md)

For $\ddot q=-\omega^2q+g_2q^2+g_3q^3+(\tau+f_1q+f_2q^2)\dot q+\cdots$, the [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md) amplitude equation has cubic coefficient $(f_2+f_1g_2/\omega^2)/8$ in the leading position amplitude. The quadratic restoring term produces mean shift $g_2r^2/(2\omega^2)$ and second harmonic $-g_2r^2\cos(2\omega t)/(6\omega^2)$. Consequently $\langle q\dot q^2\rangle=g_2r^4/8$ and $\langle q^2\dot q^2\rangle=\omega^2r^4/8$, giving the coefficient by [energy](energy.md) averaging. A negative value gives a supercritical stable [limit cycle](limit-cycle.md); a zero value requires higher-order analysis.
