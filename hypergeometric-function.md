# Hypergeometric function

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss hypergeometric equation](gauss-hypergeometric-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypergeometric_function)

The Gauss hypergeometric function is the normalized analytic solution at zero,

$$
{}_2F_1(a,b;c;z)
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a)_n(b)_n}{(c)_n}\frac{z^n}{n!},
$$

initially for $|z|<1$ and $c\notin\{0,-1,-2,\ldots\}$, followed by [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) where possible.

The ordinary [hypergeometric function](hypergeometric-function.md) is ${}_2F_1(a,b;c;z)=\sum_{n\geq0}(a)_n(b)_nz^n/((c)_n n!)$ near zero for admissible $c$. It solves the [Gauss hypergeometric equation](gauss-hypergeometric-equation.md) and extends by [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md).
