# Underreplicated DNA

↑ **Parent:** [DNA replication](dna-replication.md)

[DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) whose copying has not completed at a relevant cell-cycle transition. It can contribute to abnormal mitotic structures or breakage, but requires evidence distinct from a general DNA-damage signal.
