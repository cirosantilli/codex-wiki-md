# Chief factor

↑ **Parent:** [Chief series](chief-series.md)

A chief factor of $G$ is a quotient $N/M$ with $M,N$ normal in $G$ and no $G$-normal subgroup strictly between them. Equivalently, $N/M$ is a [minimal normal subgroup](minimal-normal-subgroup.md) of $G/M$. Such factors are the successive factors of a [chief series](chief-series.md), and are direct products of isomorphic simple groups when $G$ is finite.
