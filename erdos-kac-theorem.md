<h1 id="erdos-kac-theorem">Erdős-Kac theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilistic number theory](probabilistic-number-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős-Kac_theorem)

For a real [strongly additive arithmetic function](strongly-additive-arithmetic-function.md) with uniformly bounded values on [primes](prime-number.md), put $A(x)=\sum_{p\le x}f(p)/p$ and $B(x)^2=\sum_{p\le x}f(p)^2/p$. If $B(x)\to\infty$, then $(f(n)-A(N))/B(N)$ under uniform sampling from $[N]$ converges to the [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md). For the [prime omega function](prime-omega-function.md), the mean and variance scales are $\log\log N$.
