# Transverse and longitudinal momentum projectors

↑ **Parent:** [Photon propagator](photon-propagator.md)

At non-null [four-momentum](four-momentum.md), these complementary [projectors](projection-linear-algebra.md) satisfy $P_L^2=P_L$, $P_T^2=P_T$ and $P_LP_T=0$. The [gauge-fixed Maxwell kinetic operator](gauge-fixed-maxwell-kinetic-operator.md) is $-k^2(P_T+\xi^{-1}P_L)$; its inverse gives propagator $-i(P_T+\xi P_L)/k^2$. Transversality here removes one four-dimensional longitudinal direction, leaving rank three; it does not by itself project onto the two physical photon polarizations. The pole requires the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md).
