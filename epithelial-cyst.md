# Epithelial cyst

↑ **Parent:** [Lumen formation](lumen-formation.md)

An epithelial enclosure around a lumen, used experimentally to study coordinated polarity, division and surface traffic. Multiple lumina do not necessarily imply loss of polarity in every cell.
