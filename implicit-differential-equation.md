# Implicit differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

An implicit [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) relates the [derivative](derivative.md) to the independent and dependent [variables](variable-mathematics.md) without requiring it to be explicitly isolated. For example $y y'=1$ is meaningful at every pair $(x,y)$ as an equation, but no differentiable [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) can pass through $y=0$: its left side would vanish there. Dividing by $y$ gives $y'=1/y$ only on the domain $y\ne0$. Conversely multiplying an explicit equation by a vanishing denominator can introduce points where the original equation was undefined. A solution of the multiplied equation at such a point need not solve the original [initial value problem](initial-value-problem.md).
