# Fox calculus

↑ **Parent:** [Free presentation](free-presentation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_calculus)

Fox calculus assigns noncommutative derivatives $\partial/\partial x_i$ to words in a free group. The product rule and the identity

$$
r-1=\sum_i\frac{\partial r}{\partial x_i}(x_i-1)
$$

turn relators into module maps and give the presentation relation sequence.
