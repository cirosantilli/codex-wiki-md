# KdV mass and energy conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Korteweg–De Vries equation](generalized-korteweg-de-vries-equation.md)

Sufficiently regular decaying solutions conserve

$$
M(u)=\int_{\mathbb R}u^2,
\qquad
E(u)=\frac12\int_{\mathbb R}u_x^2-
\frac1{p+1}\int_{\mathbb R}u^{p+1}.
$$

Both identities follow by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) from the divergence form of the equation.
