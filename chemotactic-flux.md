# Chemotactic flux

↑ **Parent:** [Chemotaxis](chemotaxis.md)

A [chemotactic flux](chemotactic-flux.md) is the transport flux caused by directed cell movement in response to a chemical gradient. For a cell density $q$ and attractant concentration $c$, a common flux is $\chi q\nabla c$ with $\chi>0$.
