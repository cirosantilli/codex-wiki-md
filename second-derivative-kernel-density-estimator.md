# Second derivative kernel density estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative kernel density estimator](derivative-kernel-density-estimator.md)

Differentiating a twice differentiable [kernel density estimator](kernel-density-estimation.md) twice estimates $f^{\prime\prime}$. Its [integrated variance of a kernel density estimator](integrated-variance-of-a-kernel-density-estimator.md) analogue is at most $\|L^{\prime\prime}\|_2^2/(nb^5)$. This explains why [derivative](derivative.md) estimation requires more smoothing than ordinary [density estimation](density-estimation.md).
