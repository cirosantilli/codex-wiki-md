# Seismic multiple

↑ **Parent:** [Layer reverberation](layer-reverberation.md)

A [seismic phase](seismic-phase.md) reflected more than once before recording. Water-column and seabed multiples can obscure primary [seismic reflections](seismic-reflection.md) and must be distinguished using their timing, moveout and spatial behaviour.
