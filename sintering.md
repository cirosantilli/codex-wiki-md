# Sintering

↑ **Parent:** [Materials science](materials-science.md)

[Sintering](sintering.md) joins particles into a coherent [solid](solid.md) by heating, sometimes with applied [pressure](pressure-split.md), without completely [melting](melting.md) the material. It is used to consolidate powders of [ceramics](ceramic.md) and [metals](metal.md).
