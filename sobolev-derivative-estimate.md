# Sobolev derivative estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

For every multi-index $\alpha$ and real $s$,

$$
\|D^\alpha u\|_{H^{s-|\alpha|}}
\leq\|u\|_{H^s}.
$$

This follows from the Fourier multiplier $(i\xi)^\alpha$.
