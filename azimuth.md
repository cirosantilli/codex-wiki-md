# Azimuth

↑ **Parent:** [Horizontal coordinate system](horizontal-coordinate-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azimuth)

[Azimuth](azimuth.md) is the [angle](angle.md) about the local vertical measured in the [astronomical horizon](astronomical-horizon.md). A common convention increases eastward from north; some astronomical formulae use westward from north. The convention changes the sign in the [hour angle](hour-angle.md) transformation.
