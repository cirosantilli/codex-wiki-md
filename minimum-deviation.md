# Minimum deviation

↑ **Parent:** [Prism (optics)](prism-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_deviation)

For a homogeneous [optical prism](prism-optics.md) in air, with apex angle $\phi$ and [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n>1$, minimum deviation occurs at the symmetric ray path. Then both internal angles are $\phi/2$ and both external angles are $(\gamma_{\min}+\phi)/2$, so [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) gives

$$
n=\frac{\sin[(\gamma_{\min}+\phi)/2]}{\sin(\phi/2)}.
$$

Measuring the deviation therefore determines the refractive index at the chosen [wavelength](wavelength.md).
