# Dimethyl sulfoxide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Dimethyl sulfoxide](dimethyl-sulfoxide.md) is a polar aprotic solvent. It supports reactions of anions with many organic [electrophiles](electrophile.md) without providing an O–H [proton](proton.md).
