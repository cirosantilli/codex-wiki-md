# Norm of an integral element over a normal domain

↑ **Parent:** [Field norm](field-norm.md)

Let $A$ be an [integrally closed domain](integrally-closed-domain.md) with fraction field $K$, and let $L/K$ be finite. If $b\in L$ is an [integral element](integral-element.md) over $A$, its monic [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) lies in $A[T]$: its roots are integral, so its coefficients are integral elements of $K$, hence belong to $A$. If that polynomial has degree $r$ and constant coefficient $a_0$, then $N_{L/K}(b)=((-1)^ra_0)^{[L:K(b)]}\in A$. This remains true for inseparable extensions. An infinite algebraic extension has no usual determinant field norm.
