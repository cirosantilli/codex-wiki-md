# Phonon mean free path

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon scattering](phonon-scattering.md)

The [phonon mean free path](phonon-mean-free-path.md) is the typical distance traveled by a [phonon](phonon.md) between the relevant scattering events. For heat transport, the relevant relaxation time must describe relaxation of heat current, not merely count all collisions. An isotropic kinetic estimate is $\kappa\simeq c_Vv_s\ell/3$, where $c_V$ is [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) per unit [volume](volume.md). Boundary-limited $\ell$ and the low-temperature [Debye model](debye-model.md) give $\kappa\propto T^3$.
