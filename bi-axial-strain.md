# Bi-axial strain

↑ **Parent:** [Principal strain rates](principal-strain-rates.md)

Bi-axial extension has two positive [principal strain rates](principal-strain-rates.md) and one negative rate in [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md). It flattens a material volume toward a sheet while extending its two in-plane directions. A negative mean product of the three rates indicates amplitude-weighted preference for this geometry, but alone does not establish an unweighted event-frequency majority.
