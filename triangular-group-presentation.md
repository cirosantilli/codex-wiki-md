# Triangular group presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Group presentation](group-presentation.md)

A triangular [group presentation](group-presentation.md) has relators of word length at most three. Every finitely presented group admits one. For a relator $s_1\cdots s_\ell$ of length $\ell\ge4$, introduce generators $y_2,\ldots,y_{\ell-2}$ and replace it by

$$
s_1s_2y_2^{-1},\qquad y_{j-1}s_jy_j^{-1}\ (3\le j\le\ell-2),\qquad y_{\ell-2}s_{\ell-1}s_\ell.
$$

The defining relations say $y_j=s_1\cdots s_j$; eliminating those generators by [Tietze transformations](tietze-transformations.md) recovers the original relation. Every new relator has length three. Geometrically these operations triangulate each relator disk in its [presentation complex](presentation-complex.md) by inserting diagonals, so they preserve the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md).
