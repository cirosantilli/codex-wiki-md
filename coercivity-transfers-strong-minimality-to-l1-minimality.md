# Coercivity transfers strong minimality to L1 minimality

↑ **Parent:** [Strong local minimizer of a variational integral](strong-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md)

With fixed endpoint values on a finite interval, the displayed [derivative](derivative.md) [coercivity](coercive-function.md) makes a finite-energy [strong local minimizer of a variational integral](strong-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md) also a local minimizer in $L^1$. Otherwise choose better competitors converging in $L^1$. The upper energy bound controls their derivatives in $L^p$, and a common endpoint controls their function norms. Compact [Sobolev embedding](failure-of-first-order-sobolev-embedding-into-linfinity-in-two-dimensions.md) gives a uniformly convergent subsequence; its limit is the $L^1$ limit. This contradicts strong local minimality. [Convexity](convex-function.md) of the integrand is unnecessary for this neighborhood-transfer argument.
