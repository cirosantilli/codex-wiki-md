# Canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md)

For the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md) projection $\pi:T^*X\to X$, the canonical [differential one-form](one-form.md) is $\lambda_{(x,\xi)}(v)=\xi(d\pi(v))$. In coordinates it is $\sum_j\xi_jdx_j$. The canonical [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) is $-d\lambda$ in the position-first convention $\sum_jdx_j\wedge d\xi_j$.
