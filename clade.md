# Clade

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clade)

A [clade](clade.md) comprises an ancestor and all its descendants. Membership is about [common descent](common-descent.md), not a taxonomic rank or a degree of cellular complexity. In a [two-domain system](two-domain-system.md), the nuclear lineage of [eukaryotes](eukaryote.md) nests within [archaea](archaea.md); a group containing only the [prokaryotic](prokaryote.md) members excludes descendants and is therefore not that complete [clade](clade.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 20.2: clades and shared ancestry](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/20-2-determining-evolutionary-relationships).
