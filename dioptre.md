# Dioptre

↑ **Parent:** [Optical power](optical-power.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dioptre)

A [dioptre](dioptre.md), also spelled [diopter](dioptre.md), is the unit of [optical power](optical-power.md) equal to one reciprocal metre. In $\Phi=1/f$, convert [focal length](focal-length.md) to metres before taking the reciprocal: $f=25\,\mathrm{cm}=0.25\,\mathrm m$ gives $\Phi=4\,\mathrm D$, not $0.04\,\mathrm D$. A [dioptre](dioptre.md) measures focusing strength, distinct from the [transverse magnification](transverse-magnification.md) of an image.
