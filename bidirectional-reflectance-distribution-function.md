# Bidirectional reflectance distribution function

↑ **Parent:** [Reflectance](reflectance.md)

A function relating reflected [radiance](radiance.md) in one direction to incident irradiance from another direction at a surface location. A local nonemissive, nonfluorescent [optical reflection](optical-reflection.md) model integrates $f_rL_i\cos\theta_i$ over incident [solid angle](solid-angle.md) to obtain outgoing [radiance](radiance.md). Transmission, [wavelength](wavelength.md) conversion and nonlocal transport need additional models.
