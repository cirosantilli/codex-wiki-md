# Synapse

↑ **Parent:** [Neuron](neuron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synapse)

A signaling junction between a neuron and another cell. Chemical synapses release [neurotransmitters](neurotransmitter.md), which alter the receiving cell's membrane currents; electrical synapses couple cells more directly. Synaptic inputs can favor or oppose subsequent spike generation.
