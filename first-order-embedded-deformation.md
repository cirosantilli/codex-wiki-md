# First-order embedded deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert functor](hilbert-functor.md)

A first-order embedded deformation of a [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md) $Z\subseteq X$ is a closed subscheme $Z_\varepsilon\subseteq X\times_k\operatorname{Spec}(k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2))$ flat over the [dual numbers](dual-number.md) and with special fibre equal to the given embedded $Z$. It is a tangent vector of the [Hilbert scheme](hilbert-scheme.md), not a deformation modulo arbitrary automorphisms of the ambient scheme. These deformations are naturally the [global sections](global-section.md) of the [normal sheaf](normal-sheaf.md).
