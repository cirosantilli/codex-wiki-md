# Finite model theory

↑ **Parent:** [Model theory](model-theory.md)

Finite model theory studies logical definability and equivalence on finite [first-order structures](first-order-structure.md). Unlike unrestricted model theory, first-order compactness is unavailable when models are required to be finite. Locality, bounded-variable games and descriptive characterizations of [complexity classes](complexity-class.md) are central tools.
