# Missing energy

↑ **Parent:** [Missing four-momentum in electron-positron collisions](missing-four-momentum-in-electron-positron-collisions.md)

The [energy](energy-split.md) component of the difference between the known initial [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) and the measured visible final [four-momenta](four-momentum.md). In equal-beam [electron](electron.md)-[Positron](positron.md) collisions it is $\sqrt{s}-\sum_iE_i$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). It can arise from escaping [neutrinos](neutrino.md), but also from detector losses; it is distinct from the [missing invariant mass](missing-invariant-mass.md) and [missing transverse momentum](missing-transverse-momentum.md).
