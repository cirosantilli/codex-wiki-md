# Seafloor spreading

↑ **Parent:** [Spreading ridge](spreading-ridge.md)

[Seafloor spreading](seafloor-spreading.md) creates oceanic [crust](planetary-crust.md) at a [spreading ridge](spreading-ridge.md) and carries it away with the adjoining plates. With symmetric steady spreading, an age interval maps to distance by the [half-spreading rate](half-spreading-rate.md), not the full relative opening [speed](speed.md). A time sequence of field polarities can therefore become paired [marine magnetic stripes](marine-magnetic-stripe.md).
