# Degree charge of an O3 sigma-model lump

↑ **Parent:** [Sigma-model lump](sigma-model-lump.md)

A unit-vector field $\mathbf n:\mathbb R^2\to S^2$ approaching a fixed value at infinity extends to $S^2\to S^2$. Its [topological charge](topological-charge.md) is the [topological degree](topological-degree.md) $Q=(4\pi)^{-1}\int\mathbf n\cdot(\partial_1\mathbf n\times\partial_2\mathbf n)\,d^2x$. This normalized target area pullback counts the number of oriented coverings.
