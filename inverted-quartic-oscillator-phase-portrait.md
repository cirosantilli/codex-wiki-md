# Inverted quartic oscillator phase portrait

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian system](hamiltonian-system.md)

The [autonomous planar system](autonomous-planar-system.md) $\dot x=y-2y^3$, $\dot y=-x$ preserves $K$. Its origin is a [center equilibrium](center-equilibrium.md) and $(0,\pm1/\sqrt2)$ are [saddle equilibria](saddle-equilibrium.md). The level $K=1/4$ is $x=\pm(y^2-1/2)$: its two inner arcs are [heteroclinic orbits](heteroclinic-orbit.md) enclosing periodic motion, while its outer branches connect saddles to escaping trajectories. Since $|\dot y|\sim y^2$ on escaping levels, infinity is reached in finite time. Adding $\alpha x/2$ to $\dot x$ gives $\dot K=\alpha x^2$, so positive $\alpha$ produces outward crossing of central levels and breaks the equal-energy heteroclinic connections.
