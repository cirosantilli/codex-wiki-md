# Feedback polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Linear-feedback shift register](linear-feedback-shift-register.md)

The feedback polynomial records the coefficients of an [LFSR](linear-feedback-shift-register.md) recurrence. With the convention

$$
s_{n+d}=a_{d-1}s_{n+d-1}+\cdots+a_0s_n,
$$

it is $x^d+a_{d-1}x^{d-1}+\cdots+a_0$.
