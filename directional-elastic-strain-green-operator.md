# Directional elastic strain Green operator

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic strain Green operator](elastic-strain-green-operator.md)

Let $B_na=\operatorname{sym}(a\otimes n)$; its adjoint on symmetric [stress tensors](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) is $B_n^T\tau=\tau n$. Then $B_n^TCB_n$ is the [acoustic tensor](acoustic-tensor.md). The [directional elastic strain Green operator](directional-elastic-strain-green-operator.md) satisfies the resolvent identity $\widetilde\Gamma_r(C^r-C^s)\widetilde\Gamma_s=\widetilde\Gamma_s-\widetilde\Gamma_r$, by the elementary inverse identity $K_r^{-1}(K_r-K_s)K_s^{-1}=K_s^{-1}-K_r^{-1}$.
