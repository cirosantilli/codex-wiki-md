# Fatty acid desaturase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A [fatty acid desaturase](fatty-acid-desaturase.md) introduces a double bond into an acyl substrate. Chain length, double-bond position, carrier preference and electron-supply compatibility matter when transferring the activity between organisms.
