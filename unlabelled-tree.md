# Unlabelled tree

↑ **Parent:** [Tree (graph theory)](tree-graph-theory-split.md)

A [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md) considered up to [graph isomorphism](graph-isomorphism.md), with [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) names ignored. For example, all four [labelled trees](labelled-tree.md) that are [star graphs](star-graph-theory.md) on $\{1,2,3,4\}$ represent one [unlabelled tree](unlabelled-tree.md). Dividing the total in [Cayley's formula](cayley-s-formula.md) by $n!$ does not count [unlabelled trees](unlabelled-tree.md): different shapes can have different numbers of distinct labellings.
