# Balanced parentheses in propositional syntax

↑ **Parent:** [Propositional formula](propositional-formula.md)

Every [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) generated by a parenthesized grammar has equally many opening and closing parentheses. By [structural induction](structural-induction.md), atomic formulas begin with equal zero counts; [negation](negation.md) preserves them; and a parenthesized binary [logical connective](logical-connective.md) adds its two subformula counts and one parenthesis of each kind. An optional paired-parenthesis construction also adds one to both counts. This necessary syntactic invariant is useful for checking generated expressions, though equal counts alone do not guarantee correctly nested syntax.
