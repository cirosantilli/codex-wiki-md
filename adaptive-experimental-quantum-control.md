# Adaptive experimental quantum control

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optimal control](quantum-optimal-control.md)

Parametrize a reproducible pulse by a finite vector $a$ and evaluate its objective experimentally on freshly prepared systems. Repeatedly update the pulse from measured objective values or finite-difference derivatives, including penalties for resources. This is [closed-loop control](closed-loop-control.md) across experimental runs, even when each pulse is [open-loop control](open-loop-control.md) within a run. It reduces reliance on an inaccurate dynamical model but still requires preparation reproducibility, sufficient measurement statistics and stability of the experimental conditions.
