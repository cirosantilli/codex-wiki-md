# Marginal-utility verification of optimal consumption

↑ **Parent:** [Utility duality with martingale deflators](utility-duality-with-martingale-deflators.md)

For [concave](concave-function.md) increasing utility, the [concave supporting-tangent inequality](concave-supporting-tangent-inequality.md) gives $e^{-bt}[U(\hat c_t)-U(c_t)]\leq Y_t(\hat c_t-c_t)$. If the proposed [consumption](consumption.md) exhausts the state-price budget and every competitor has no larger budget, integration proves optimality. The objective must be well defined; a positive discount rate and finite utility bounds are a convenient sufficient condition.
