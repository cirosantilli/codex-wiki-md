# Redox mediator

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemistry](electrochemistry-split.md)

A [redox mediator](redox-mediator.md) transfers reducing equivalents between components through changes in oxidation state. A current-enhancing additive is not necessarily a mediator unless its redox participation and regeneration are established.
