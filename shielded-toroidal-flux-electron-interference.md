# Shielded toroidal-flux electron interference

↑ **Parent:** [Aharonov-Bohm effect](aharonov-bohm-effect.md)

In 1986, [Akira Tonomura](akira-tonomura.md) and collaborators coated a [ferromagnet](ferromagnet.md) shaped as a [torus](torus.md) with superconducting [niobium](niobium.md) and [copper](copper.md). A cooled stage held it in a 150 kV field-emission [electron microscope](electron-microscope.md). An [electron biprism](electron-biprism.md) overlapped object and reference waves; film [holograms](hologram.md) were reconstructed optically. Room-temperature leakage screening selected samples; varying [copper](copper.md) thickness tested electron penetration.[pp. 792–794, Figs. 1–4](evidence-for-aharonov-bohm-effect-with-magnetic-field-completely-shielded-from-electron-wave-by-akira-tonomura-and-others.md)

Below the superconducting transition, through-hole and outside [electron](electron.md) waves differed in [wave phase](phase-waves.md) by zero or $\pi$ modulo $2\pi$. Warming through it changed the [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) reversibly. Leakage and coherent-penetration bounds do not prove zero [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).[p. 794, Figs. 6–7: experimental limits](evidence-for-aharonov-bohm-effect-with-magnetic-field-completely-shielded-from-electron-wave-by-akira-tonomura-and-others.md)

Why these two phases? The shield's [superconducting flux quantum](superconducting-flux-quantum.md) is $h/(2e)$, whereas the probing [electron](electron.md) has [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $-e$. Substituting $\Phi=Nh/(2e)$ gives

$$
e^{i\delta}=e^{-iN\pi}=(-1)^N.
$$

An odd $N$ therefore moves a carrier fringe by half its spacing; an even $N$ leaves it unchanged modulo a full spacing. The phase quantization provides an internal check on the superconducting enclosure. It does not mean that zero [wave phase](phase-waves.md) proves zero trapped [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md).[2006, p. 49, Confirmation experiments on the AB effect; Fig. 2 on p. 48](the-aharonov-bohm-effect-and-its-applications-to-electron-phase-microscopy-by-akira-tonomura.md)

The readout is [electron holography](electron-holography.md) in the [off-axis holography](off-axis-holography.md) geometry: a known tilted reference converts an unknown relative [wave phase](phase-waves.md) into a spatial carrier displacement. Reconstructing a magnetic [wave phase](phase-waves.md) assumes stable reference phase, adequate coherence and a calibrated carrier; electrostatic charging and material traversal can also shift [wave phase](phase-waves.md). Optical [wave phase](phase-waves.md) amplification rescales the displayed fringe displacement. The experiment's shielding and comparison tests support the magnetic inference; an interferogram by itself measures the total relative [wave phase](phase-waves.md), rather than separately certifying every contribution. The ideal field-free model describes the limiting geometry, while finite leakage and penetration remain questions of experimental bounds.
