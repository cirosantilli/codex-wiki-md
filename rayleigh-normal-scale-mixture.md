# Rayleigh-normal scale mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian scale mixture](gaussian-scale-mixture.md)

An independent unit-scale [Rayleigh distribution](rayleigh-distribution.md) variable $R$ and [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) variable $Z$ give $RZ$ with [Laplace distribution](laplace-distribution.md) of density $e^{-|x|}/2$. Indeed its [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) is $\mathbb E e^{-t^2R^2/2}=1/(1+t^2)$.
