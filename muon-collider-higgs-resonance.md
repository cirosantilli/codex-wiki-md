# Muon-collider Higgs resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md)

A [muon](muon.md) and [antimuon](antimuon.md) can form a [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) directly in the annihilation channel. Their Higgs [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md) is proportional to the muon [mass](mass.md), making the corresponding partial width much larger than for an electron pair. The observable resonant cross section also depends on the collider's energy distribution: a beam spread broader than the resonance smears its ideal peak.
