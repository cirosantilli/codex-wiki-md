# Piezoelectric stress sensor

↑ **Parent:** [Piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md)

A transducer using stress-induced electrical charge. A uniformly loaded slab of thickness $t$ has ideal open-circuit voltage sensitivity $dt/(\epsilon_0\kappa)$; leakage and input [capacitance](capacitance.md) change its measured response.
