# Alternative quantization of an AdS scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [AdS scalar-field boundary asymptotics](ads-scalar-field-boundary-asymptotics.md)

When both scalar falloffs in anti-de Sitter spacetime are normalizable, alternative quantization exchanges which near-boundary coefficient is treated as the source and which as the expectation value. In AdS4, a field with $m^2=-2$ may be dual to a scalar operator of dimension one.
