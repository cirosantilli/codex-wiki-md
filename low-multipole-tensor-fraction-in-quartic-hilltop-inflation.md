# Low-multipole tensor fraction in quartic hilltop inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic hilltop inflation](quartic-hilltop-inflation.md)

Using the approximate large-angle CMB relation $T/S\simeq-7n_T$ gives $T/S\simeq7\sigma^4/(96M^4N^3)$ in [quartic hilltop inflation](quartic-hilltop-inflation.md). It is suppressed by the fourth power of the small-field scale. This large-angle temperature ratio is not the primordial [tensor-to-scalar ratio](tensor-to-scalar-ratio.md) $r=16\epsilon_V$; their transfer functions and normalization differ.
