# Concentration-blob size in a theta solvent

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration blob](concentration-blob.md)

For an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md) inside a blob, $\nu=1/2$ and $\xi\sim b\sqrt g$. Together with $\phi\sim gv_0/\xi^3$, this gives the displayed concentration-blob size. Under the site-volume approximation $v_0\sim b^3$, $\xi\sim b/\phi$ and $g\sim\phi^{-2}$. The estimate describes local overlap rather than the full chain size and requires $1\ll g\ll N$ for a well-developed semidilute regime.
