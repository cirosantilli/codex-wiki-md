# Atom (measure theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atom_(measure_theory))

An atom of a [measure](measure.md) $\mu$ is a measurable set $A$ of positive measure for which every measurable $B\subseteq A$ has either $\mu(B)=0$ or $\mu(B)=\mu(A)$. A singleton of positive mass is an example. A [transport map](transport-map.md) cannot split the mass of a singleton among several destinations.
