# Tachyon vertex operator

↑ **Parent:** [String vertex operator](string-vertex-operator.md)

The bosonic closed-string tachyon vertex is the normal-ordered exponential ${}:e^{ip\cdot X}:$ integrated over its worldsheet position. Pairwise contractions of such exponentials generate the [Koba-Nielsen factor](koba-nielsen-factor.md).
