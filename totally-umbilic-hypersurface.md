# Totally umbilic hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)

At every point its second fundamental form is a scalar multiple of its induced metric. Equivalently its shape operator is scalar, so all principal normal curvatures agree. For a three-dimensional hypersurface, $K=3\lambda$ and $K_{ab}K^{ab}=3\lambda^2$. The sign of its intrinsic scalar in a vacuum Lorentzian ambient space then follows from the [Gauss equation for a nonnull hypersurface](gauss-equation-for-a-nonnull-hypersurface.md).
