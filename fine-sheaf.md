# Fine sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fine_sheaf)

A sheaf of modules over the sheaf of [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) is fine when every locally finite [open cover](open-cover.md) admits a subordinate family of sheaf endomorphisms summing to the identity. Fine sheaves on a [paracompact space](paracompact-space.md) are acyclic, so their positive-degree [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) vanishes.
