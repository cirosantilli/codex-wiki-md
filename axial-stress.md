# Axial stress

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)

The longitudinal normal [stress](stress.md) in an elongated body. For uniform loading it is the axial resultant [force](force-split.md) divided by the relevant [cross-sectional area](cross-sectional-area.md); [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) components use current area. Positive values conventionally indicate tension and negative values compression.
