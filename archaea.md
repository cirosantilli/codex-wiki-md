# Archaea

↑ **Parent:** [Prokaryote](prokaryote.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaea)

[Archaea](archaea.md) are a major evolutionary lineage distinct from [bacteria](bacteria.md). The cellular comparison here concerns the [prokaryotic](prokaryote.md) members of [archaea](archaea.md); see [two-domain system](two-domain-system.md) for broader [clade](clade.md) usage. Their [biological cells](biological-cell.md) have a [nucleoid](nucleoid.md) rather than a membrane-bounded [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), yet aspects of [DNA replication](dna-replication.md), [transcription](transcription-biology.md) and [biological translation](translation-biology.md) resemble those of [eukaryotes](eukaryote.md). Their characteristic [lipids](lipid.md) differ chemically from the usual bacterial membrane [lipids](lipid.md), and their [cell walls](cell-wall.md), when present, lack [peptidoglycan](peptidoglycan.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 22.2: archaeal distinctions](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/22-2-structure-of-prokaryotes-bacteria-and-archaea).

Some [archaea](archaea.md) perform [methanogenesis](methanogenesis.md), producing [methane](methane.md) as part of their [metabolism](metabolism.md). [Archaea](archaea.md) also inhabit ordinary soils and oceans: extreme habitats do not define the lineage. See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 22.1: moderate and extreme environments](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/22-1-prokaryotic-diversity). Woese and Fox's 1977 analysis of [ribosomal RNA](ribosomal-rna.md) distinguished the methanogenic group they called archaebacteria from typical [bacteria](bacteria.md). Their proposed three primary lines of descent were a historical interpretation, rather than the final resolution of deep [phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md). See [Woese and Fox, 1977: Phylogenetic structure of the prokaryotic domain](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.74.11.5088).
