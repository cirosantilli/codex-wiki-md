# Minimum size of a sufficient set for an IID source

↑ **Parent:** [Epsilon-sufficient set](epsilon-sufficient-set.md)

For finite-alphabet [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md), fixed $0\leq\epsilon<1$ and $\delta>0$, every [epsilon-sufficient set](epsilon-sufficient-set.md) $S_n$ of length-$n$ words satisfies $|S_n|\geq(1-\epsilon)2^{n(H-\delta)}/2$ for sufficiently large $n$. Intersect $S_n$ with a [typical set](typical-set.md) whose excluded mass is at most $(1-\epsilon)/2$. The intersection retains that much probability and each word has probability at most $2^{-n(H-\delta)}$.
