# Permanent magnet

↑ **Parent:** [Magnet](magnet.md)

A [permanent magnet](permanent-magnet.md) retains [magnetization](magnetization.md) after the applied magnetizing [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) is removed. Its working [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) persists without a driving [electric current](electric-current.md) in a winding.
