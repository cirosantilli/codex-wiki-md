# Failure of ordinary sheaf-dual Serre duality for a skyscraper sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Serre duality](serre-duality.md)

On $\mathbb P_k^n$ with $n>0$, let $\mathcal F=i_*k$ at a rational point. It has one-dimensional [global sections](global-section.md), but its [dual of a sheaf](dual-of-a-sheaf.md) is zero: a local map from the residue field into the local polynomial ring must have image annihilated by a nonzero parameter, which is impossible in an [integral domain](integral-domain.md). Thus $H^n(\mathcal F^\vee(-n-1))=0$. [Serre duality](serre-duality.md) for arbitrary coherent sheaves instead uses an [Ext functor](ext-functor.md); replacing this by the ordinary dual can lose torsion information.
