# Comparator composition

↑ **Parent:** [Digital comparator](digital-comparator.md)

To combine more and less significant [digital comparators](digital-comparator.md), compute $E_H=\neg(L_H\lor G_H)$ and use the displayed formulas. Unequal upper blocks decide the order, because their difference outweighs every possible lower-block difference; equal upper blocks delegate the decision to the lower block. This is an [associative](associative-property.md) operation on the three comparison outcomes. A [balanced binary tree](balanced-binary-tree.md) of these combinations has $O(n)$ [circuit size](circuit-size.md) and $O(\log n)$ [circuit depth](depth-of-a-boolean-circuit.md) for $n$-[bit](bit.md) inputs.
