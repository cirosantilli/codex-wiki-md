# Action

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_(physics))

The action of a path is the time integral of its [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md). The [principle of stationary action](principle-of-stationary-action.md) requires its first variation to vanish for every admissible fixed-endpoint variation.
