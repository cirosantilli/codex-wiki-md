# Meson octet

↑ **Parent:** [Eightfold way](eightfold-way.md)

The pseudoscalar octet of [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) contains three [pions](pion.md), four [kaons](kaon.md) and the [Eta octet state](eta-octet-state.md). In its [weight diagram](weight-diagram.md), the neutral [pion](pion.md) and $\eta_8$ are separate states at the origin.
