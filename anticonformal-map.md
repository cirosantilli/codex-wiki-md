# Anticonformal map

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

An orientation-reversing local [conformal map](conformal-map.md) of planar domains. Equivalently, its [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) with nonzero [derivative](derivative.md). [Complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) $z\mapsto\bar z$ is the basic example. The metric pullback uses the modulus of its local scale factor, just as for an orientation-preserving [conformal map](conformal-map.md).
