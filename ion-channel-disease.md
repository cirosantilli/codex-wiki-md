# Ion-channel disease

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysics](biophysics.md)

A disease in which altered [ion channel](ion-channel.md) number, transport or gating contributes to abnormal cell function. A causal account connects measured channel behavior to cellular activity, rather than inferring mechanism from a sequence variant alone.
