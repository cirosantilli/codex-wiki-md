# PV92 teaching experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md)

The DNA Learning Center's [Detection of an Alu Insertion Polymorphism by PCR by DNA Learning Center, Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory](detection-of-an-alu-insertion-polymorphism-by-pcr-by-dna-learning-center-cold-spring-harbor-laboratory.md) supplies a concrete length-based example. It collects mouthwash [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md), concentrates them with a [microcentrifuge](microcentrifuge.md), heats them by [Chelex extraction](chelex-extraction.md) and transfers the [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)-containing [supernatant](supernatant.md). Its $25\,\mathrm{\mu L}$ reaction combines $2.5\,\mathrm{\mu L}$ of that preparation with primer mix and a reagent bead in a [thermal cycler](thermal-cycler.md). Thirty cycles use $94$, $68$ and $72^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$, each for thirty seconds. An electrophoresis chamber separates products by [agarose gel electrophoresis](agarose-gel-electrophoresis.md) in a 2 percent gel; a [UV transilluminator](uv-transilluminator.md) reveals stained [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md).[pp. 2–3, Procedures I–III: extraction, 25 microlitre reaction, cycle profile, gel and UV readout](detection-of-an-alu-insertion-polymorphism-by-pcr-by-dna-learning-center-cold-spring-harbor-laboratory.md)

Its [PCR primers](pcr-primer.md) flank an insertion site at the [PV92 locus](pv92-locus.md). The student protocol predicts a 715-[base pair](base-pair.md) product with the [Alu element](alu-element.md) and a 415-[base pair](base-pair.md) product without it. Two bands support a [heterozygous](heterozygosity.md) [genotype](genotype.md); one corresponding band supports a [homozygous](homozygosity.md) [genotype](genotype.md) only if amplification and detection of both [alleles](allele.md) were adequate. These are the protocol's expected readouts, not newly collected observations.[p. 1, Introduction: primer-flanked insertion and stated 715/415 bp genotype patterns](detection-of-an-alu-insertion-polymorphism-by-pcr-by-dna-learning-center-cold-spring-harbor-laboratory.md)

The instructor manual describes extra bands from nonspecific priming and a small diffuse [primer dimer](primer-dimer.md) band. A missing target band therefore cannot on its own distinguish failed extraction, inhibited copying, poor binding or lack of the target. Nor does detection of the [Alu element](alu-element.md) at this one [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) establish a complete [genome](genome.md), a unique personal identity or a clinical conclusion. The [PCR controls](pcr-controls.md) help separate these possibilities; [DNA sequencing](dna-sequencing.md) can add sequence evidence to the size comparison.[printed pp. 18 and 29, Results and Discussion and Amplify DNA by PCR: primer dimer and nonspecific priming](using-an-alu-insertion-polymorphism-to-study-human-populations-by-dolan-dna-learning-center-cold-spring-harbor-laboratory.md)
