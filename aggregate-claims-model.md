# Aggregate claims model

↑ **Parent:** [Actuarial statistics](actuarial-statistics-split.md)

An aggregate claims model separates the number $N$ of claims from their sizes $X_j$, with total $S=\sum_{j=1}^N X_j$ and the empty sum equal to zero. Under [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) for claim sizes, independent of $N$, [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) gives tractable moments and transforms.
