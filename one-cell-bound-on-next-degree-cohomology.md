# One-cell bound on next-degree cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular cohomology](cellular-cohomology.md)

If an $n$-dimensional [CW complex](cw-complex.md) is extended by exactly one cell in dimension $n+1$, then its cellular [cochain](singular-cochain.md) group in that degree is the coefficient [field](field.md) $k$. Regardless of how many higher cells are attached, the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) is a quotient of a subspace of that one-dimensional group. This gives the displayed bound. Finiteness of the higher cell sets is unnecessary for this particular conclusion.
