# Transverse eigenvalue crossing

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md)

A [transverse eigenvalue crossing](transverse-eigenvalue-crossing.md) occurs when the real part of an eigenvalue passes through zero with nonzero parameter derivative. This spectral condition is separate from the nonlinear nondegeneracy conditions in the Hopf theorem.
