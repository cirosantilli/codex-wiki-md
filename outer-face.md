# Outer face

↑ **Parent:** [Face of a plane graph](face-of-a-plane-graph.md)

The [outer face](outer-face.md) is the unique unbounded [face of a plane graph](face-of-a-plane-graph.md) for a finite [plane graph](plane-graph.md). It counts once even though it surrounds the whole [graph drawing](graph-drawing.md). A [tree](tree-graph-theory.md) has only this [face of a plane graph](face-of-a-plane-graph.md).
