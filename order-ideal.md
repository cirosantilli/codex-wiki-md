# Order ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)

A subset $I$ of a [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) such that $x\in I$ and $y\le x$ imply $y\in I$. Thus membership forces all smaller elements to belong as well. This definition concerns an order, rather than an ideal of a ring.
