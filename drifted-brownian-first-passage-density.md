# Drifted Brownian first-passage density

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian first-passage time](brownian-first-passage-time.md)

For a positive level $a$, this density describes the [first-passage time](first-passage-time.md) of $B_t+ct$. The [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md) multiplies the zero-drift density by the stopped likelihood $e^{ca-c^2t/2}$. Its total mass is one for nonnegative drift and $e^{2ac}$ for negative drift; the latter has remaining mass at infinite hitting time.
