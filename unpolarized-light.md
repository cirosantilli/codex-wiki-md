# Unpolarized light

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization (waves)](polarization-waves.md)

[Unpolarized light](unpolarized-light.md) has no preferred transverse polarization direction in its time-averaged statistics. An ideal linear [polarizer](polarizer.md) transmits half its incident [light intensity](light-intensity.md): equal transverse components give the averaged projection factor $\langle\cos^2\theta\rangle=1/2$. This statistical description differs from the fixed deterministic phase relation of a [monochromatic wave](monochromatic-wave.md) in a pure [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) state.
