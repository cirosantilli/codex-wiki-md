# Center of an ellipse

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipse](ellipse.md)

The [center of an ellipse](center-of-an-ellipse.md) is its unique [center of symmetry](center-of-symmetry.md). In the equation $(\mathbf x-\mathbf c)^TM(\mathbf x-\mathbf c)=R$, with $M$ a [symmetric positive-definite matrix](symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md) and $R>0$, reflection $\mathbf x\mapsto2\mathbf c-\mathbf x$ preserves the [ellipse](ellipse.md). For $\mathbf x^TM\mathbf x+\mathbf b^T\mathbf x+d=0$, [completing the square](completing-the-square.md) gives $\mathbf c=-M^{-1}\mathbf b/2$.
