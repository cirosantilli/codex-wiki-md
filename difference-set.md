# Difference set

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)

For [finite sets](finite-set.md) $A,B\subseteq G$ in an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $G$, their [difference set](difference-set.md) is

$$
A-B=\{a-b:a\in A,\ b\in B\}=A+(-B),\qquad -B=\{-b:b\in B\}.
$$

Here $-b$ is the [additive inverse](additive-inverse.md) in $G$. The [difference set](difference-set.md) records possible displacements from a member of $B$ to a member of $A$; it is distinct from the [set difference](set-difference.md) $A\setminus B$.

For the [integer](integer-split.md) [sets](set-split.md) $A=\{0,1,2,3\}$ and $B=\{0,1,4,10\}$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
A-A&=\{-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3\},& |A-A|&=7,\\
B-B&=\{-10,-9,-6,-4,-3,-1,0,1,3,4,6,9,10\},& |B-B|&=13.
\end{aligned}
$$

The positive differences for $B$ are $1,3,4,6,9,10$, each obtained once; reversing each [ordered pair](ordered-pair.md) supplies the corresponding negative difference. Every nonempty [finite set](finite-set.md) $S$ has $0\in S-S$, with $|S|$ representations $(s,s)$. Swapping the members of an [ordered pair](ordered-pair.md) also shows $S-S=-(S-S)$. In the [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z$, the [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H=\{0,2,4\}$ satisfies $H-H=H$ as well as $H+H=H$.
