# Conical singularity of negative-mass BTZ geometry

↑ **Parent:** [BTZ black hole](btz-black-hole.md)

For the nonrotating [BTZ black hole](btz-black-hole.md) family with $M<0$ and angular period $2\pi$, the circumference of a small circle divided by its proper radius tends to $2\pi\sqrt{-M}$. A smooth axis requires this ratio to be $2\pi$, so only $M=-1$ gives regular [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md). Other values have a [conical singularity](conical-singularity.md), with deficit angle $\delta$ or an angular excess when $\delta<0$. The lapse $r^2/\ell^2-M$ is strictly positive, so outward [radial null geodesics](radial-null-geodesic.md) escape from arbitrarily near the axis: the defect is a [naked singularity](naked-singularity.md), despite finite local curvature away from it.
