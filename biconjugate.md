# Biconjugate

↑ **Parent:** [Convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md)

The [biconjugate](biconjugate.md) of $f$ is the [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md) of its [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md):

$$
f^{**}(x)=\sup_p\{\langle p,x\rangle-f^*(p)\}.
$$

It is the supremum of all [affine minorants](affine-minorant.md). For a proper function with an affine minorant, the [Fenchel-Moreau theorem](fenchel-moreau-theorem.md) identifies it with the closed convex envelope; a proper [lower semicontinuous](lower-semicontinuity.md) [convex function](convex-function.md) equals its biconjugate.
