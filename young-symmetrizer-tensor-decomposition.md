# Young-symmetrizer tensor decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Schur–Weyl duality](schur-weyl-duality.md)

The standard-tableau right-ideal decomposition of $\mathbb CS_n$, tensored over that algebra with $V^{\otimes n}$, gives this direct sum of [general linear group](general-linear-group.md) [modules](module-mathematics.md). With $e_t=h_t/H_\lambda$, the map $e_t\mathbb CS_n\otimes T\to e_tT$ is an isomorphism. Individual summands generally need not be [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) [submodules](submodule.md); instead each is a [Schur module](schur-module.md) for the commuting action.
