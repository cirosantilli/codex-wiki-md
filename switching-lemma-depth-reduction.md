# Switching-lemma depth reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Håstad switching lemma](hastad-switching-lemma.md)

Switching-lemma depth reduction applies the [Håstad switching lemma](hastad-switching-lemma.md) to every bottom-layer gate, replaces each restricted gate by a shallow decision tree, and merges adjacent layers of the same gate type. Repetition reduces a constant-depth circuit to a bounded-depth decision tree while retaining many live variables.
