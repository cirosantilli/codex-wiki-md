# Phase quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Phase (waves)](phase-waves.md)

Two sinusoidal signals are in phase quadrature when their [phase difference](phase-difference.md) is a quarter cycle, for example $\cos\phi$ and $\sin\phi$. Their time averages have zero [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) over a cycle. In a [plane wave](plane-wave.md), this separates fields such as [displacement](displacement.md) and its time [derivative](derivative.md), [velocity](velocity.md).
