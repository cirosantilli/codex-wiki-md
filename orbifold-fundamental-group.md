# Orbifold fundamental group

↑ **Parent:** [Orbifold](orbifold.md)

The [orbifold fundamental group](orbifold-fundamental-group.md) records loops together with their local isotropy relations, so a loop around a cone point of order $m$ has its $m$th power trivial rather than being trivial itself. For an oriented closed surface of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$ with cone orders $m_1,\ldots,m_r$, its presentation is $\langle a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_g,b_g,c_1,\ldots,c_r\mid c_i^{m_i}=1,\ \prod_j[a_j,b_j]c_1\cdots c_r=1\rangle$. A torsion-free [finite-index subgroup](finite-index-subgroup.md) of a [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md) [orbifold](orbifold.md) gives a smooth locally isometric surface cover. The absence of torsion means that the [subgroup](subgroup.md) meets no nonidentity local cone stabilizer.
