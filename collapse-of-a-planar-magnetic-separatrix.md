# Collapse of a planar magnetic separatrix

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic relaxation](magnetic-relaxation.md)

For a planar [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) $\mathbf B=(\psi_y,-\psi_x,0)$, ideal induction advects the [Cartesian magnetic flux function](cartesian-magnetic-flux-function.md): $D_t\psi=0$. [Incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) also preserves the area inside each material contour. During relaxation, the arms of a magnetic [separatrix](separatrix.md) can approach each other while their field-line connectivity remains fixed. A transition of width $\epsilon$ with a finite change of tangential field has [electric current density](current-density.md) of order $\epsilon^{-1}$ and can approach a [current sheet](current-sheet.md). This is a possible limiting mechanism, not a claim that every planar equilibrium is singular.
