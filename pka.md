# pKa

↑ **Parent:** [Acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md)

The [pKa](pka.md) is the negative base-ten [logarithm](logarithm.md) of the [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md). A lower [pKa](pka.md) means a larger [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md) and a stronger tendency to donate a [proton](proton.md) under the same [standard state](standard-state.md), [solvent](solvent.md), and [temperature](temperature-split.md) conventions. The [Henderson-Hasselbalch equation](henderson-hasselbalch-equation.md) places equal [weak acid](weak-acid.md) and [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) [thermodynamic activities](thermodynamic-activity.md) at [pH](ph.md) equal to [pKa](pka.md). See [Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/14-6-buffers).
