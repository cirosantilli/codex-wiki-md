# Asymptotic mean squared error

↑ **Parent:** [Mean squared error](mean-squared-error.md)

The [asymptotic mean squared error](asymptotic-mean-squared-error.md) is the leading large-sample approximation to the sum of [variance](variance-split.md) and squared [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md). Minimizing it balances stochastic variation against systematic error; the resulting choice must still satisfy the assumptions used in the approximation.
