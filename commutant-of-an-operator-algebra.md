# Commutant of an operator algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Centralizer of a subalgebra](centralizer-of-a-subalgebra.md)

The commutant of $A\subseteq\operatorname{End}(T)$ is $A\prime=\{b:ba=ab\text{ for all }a\in A\}$. This centralizer is an operator algebra. The [double-centralizer theorem for semisimple operator algebras](double-centralizer-theorem-for-semisimple-operator-algebras.md) and [Schur–Weyl duality](schur-weyl-duality.md) use this meaning, which is distinct from the centralizer of a single element of a group.
