# External force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

An [external force](external-force.md) is a [force](force-split.md) exerted on a member inside the chosen system boundary by a body outside it. Changing that boundary can change which [forces](force-split.md) are external. Their sum changes the system's total [momentum](momentum-split.md) when its [internal forces](internal-force.md) cancel.
