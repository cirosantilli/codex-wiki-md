# Adjoint branching from G2 to long-root A2

↑ **Parent:** [Long-root A2 subsystem of G2](long-root-a2-subsystem-of-g2.md)

The [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of [G2](g2-mathematics.md) has one [weight](weight-representation-theory.md) at each of its twelve [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md) and two at zero. Its six long [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md) form the [A2 root system](a2-root-system.md); together with the two zero [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) they give the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of [SU(3)](su-3-group.md). The remaining six short [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md) form the [weight systems](weight-system.md) of its defining [representation](group-representation.md) and [dual representation](dual-representation.md). Hence restriction to the long-root [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) gives $\mathbf{14}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}$. This is a [branching of a representation](branching-of-a-representation.md); the complement is invariant under the [subgroup](subgroup.md), not under the full [G2](g2-mathematics.md) action.
