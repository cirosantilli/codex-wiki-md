# Connected regular graph with two adjacency eigenvalues

↑ **Parent:** [Regular graph](regular-graph.md)

A connected regular graph has exactly two distinct adjacency eigenvalues if and only if it is a [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $K_n$ with $n\geq2$. Its eigenvalues are $n-1$ on the constant vectors and $-1$ on their orthogonal complement.
