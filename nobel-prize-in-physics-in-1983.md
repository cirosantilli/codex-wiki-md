# Nobel Prize in Physics in 1983

One half went to [Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar](subrahmanyan-chandrasekhar.md) for theoretical studies of processes governing stellar structure and evolution. The other half went to [William Alfred Fowler](william-alfred-fowler.md) for theoretical and experimental studies of [nuclear reactions](nuclear-reaction.md) involved in forming the [chemical elements](chemical-element.md) in the universe.[Individual official 1983 summary: equal halves; stellar structure/evolution and nuclear reactions forming chemical elements are distinct motivations.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1983-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
