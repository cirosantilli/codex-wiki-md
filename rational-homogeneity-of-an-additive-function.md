# Rational homogeneity of an additive function

↑ **Parent:** [Additive function](additive-function.md)

For every [additive function](additive-function.md) $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$, every [real number](real-number.md) $x$, and every [rational number](rational-number.md) $q$, one has $f(qx)=qf(x)$. This is the [scalar multiplication](scalar-multiplication.md) part of being a [linear map](linear-map.md) over the [rational numbers](rational-number.md).

First $f(0)=f(0)+f(0)$ gives $f(0)=0$, and $f(x)+f(-x)=f(0)$ gives $f(-x)=-f(x)$. Repeated [addition](addition.md) gives $f(nx)=nf(x)$ for each positive [integer](integer-split.md) $n$, and the negative case follows from the sign identity. If $q=m/n$ with $m\in\mathbb Z$ and $n>0$, then

$$
nf\left(\frac mn x\right)=f(mx)=mf(x),
\qquad
f\left(\frac mn x\right)=\frac mn f(x).
$$

No [continuity](continuous-function.md) was used. In particular $f(1)=3$ fixes $f(-2/3)=-2$, $f(5/6)=5/2$, and every other rational value, even for a [discontinuous additive function](discontinuous-additive-function.md).
