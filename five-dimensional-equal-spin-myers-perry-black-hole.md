# Five-dimensional equal-spin Myers-Perry black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Myers-Perry black hole](myers-perry-black-hole.md)

A five-dimensional [Myers-Perry black hole](myers-perry-black-hole.md) with equal rotational parameters has a metric depending on a single radial coordinate. In a fibered coordinate system, its radial functions can be written $f=1-r_0^2/r^2+\alpha^2r_0^2/r^4$, $h=1+\alpha^2r_0^2/r^4$, and $\Omega=\alpha r_0^2/(r^4h)$. For $0<\alpha<r_0/2$ there are outer and inner [Killing horizons](killing-horizon.md); at equality they form a [degenerate Killing horizon](degenerate-killing-horizon.md). With the standard sphere identifications and $G_5=1$, its [Komar mass](komar-mass.md) is $3\pi r_0^2/8$.
