# Total comoving visibility radius

↑ **Parent:** [Particle horizon](particle-horizon.md)

In a spatially flat [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), the supremum of the [comoving radial distance](comoving-radial-distance.md) from which a signal emitted at the initial time can eventually reach a fixed comoving observer is the total [conformal time](conformal-time.md) interval. At an intermediate time $t$, this equals its [comoving particle horizon](comoving-particle-horizon.md) plus the comoving radius of its [cosmological event horizon](cosmological-event-horizon.md), when both integrals converge. This is a visibility bound for emission arbitrarily close to the initial boundary; it does not assert that a transparent universe or a source existed there.
