# Complemented subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Banach space](banach-space-split.md)

A closed [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $Y$ of a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $X$ is complemented if a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $P:X\to X$ satisfies $P^2=P$ and $P(X)=Y$. Equivalently, $X$ is the topological [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of $Y$ and a closed subspace: a projection gives $X=Y\oplus\ker P$, and the coordinate projection of such a decomposition is bounded.
