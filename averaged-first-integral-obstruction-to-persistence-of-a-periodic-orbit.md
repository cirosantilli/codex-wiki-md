# Averaged first-integral obstruction to persistence of a periodic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Energy balance method](energy-balance-method.md)

Suppose $V$ is a [first integral](first-integral.md) of an unperturbed planar system and $\dot V=\varepsilon G+O(\varepsilon^2)$ after perturbation. A periodic orbit converging to an unperturbed orbit $\gamma$ can persist only if

$$
\int_\gamma G\,dt=0.
$$

Indeed, the net change of $V$ over every period is zero. A nonzero averaged drift therefore obstructs persistence.
