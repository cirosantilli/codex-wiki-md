# Spherical potential-vorticity anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Stretched coordinates for quasi-geostrophic inversion](stretched-coordinates-for-quasi-geostrophic-inversion.md)

A uniform positive [quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) anomaly in a sphere of stretched radius $R$ has the displayed regular, decaying [streamfunction](stream-function.md). Both it and its radial derivative match at the sphere. In its equatorial plane the horizontal [relative vorticity](relative-vorticity.md) is $2Q_0/3$ inside and $-Q_0R^3/(3r^3)$ outside. The exterior potential-vorticity anomaly remains zero because its vertical stretching contribution compensates that [relative vorticity](relative-vorticity.md). A stretched sphere corresponds to a flattened ellipsoid in physical coordinates when $N\gg|f|$.
