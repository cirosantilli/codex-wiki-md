# Benzodiazepine

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology](pharmacology-split.md)

A drug class including positive allosteric modulators at susceptible [GABA-A receptors](gaba-a-receptor.md). A behavioural effect can involve anxiolysis, sedation, altered [memory](memory-in-animals.md) or movement, which must be distinguished.
