# Hom into the regular algebra as a right module

↑ **Parent:** [Module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md)

For a left [module](module-mathematics.md) $M$ over a unital [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) $A$, right multiplication in $A$ gives a [right module](right-module.md) structure on $\operatorname{Hom}_A(M,A)$ by $(f\cdot a)(m)=f(m)a$. This remains a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) because $f(bm)a=bf(m)a$; associativity and the unit verify the action axioms. When $A=M_d(\mathbb C)$ and $M=\mathbb C^d$ is its [simple module](irreducible-module.md) of columns, every such map is $m\mapsto mr$ for a unique row $r$, making the Hom space the simple row [right module](right-module.md). This illustrates how left and right simple [modules](module-mathematics.md) correspond for a [semisimple algebra](semisimple-algebra.md).
