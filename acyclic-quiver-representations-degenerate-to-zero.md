# Acyclic quiver representations degenerate to zero

↑ **Parent:** [Base change action on quiver representations](base-change-action-on-quiver-representations.md)

Choose integer vertex weights increasing along every arrow of a finite acyclic [quiver](quiver.md). Acting at vertex $i$ by $t^{w_i}I$ multiplies each arrow by a positive power of $t$. At $t=0$ all arrows vanish. Thus every orbit closure contains the origin of the [quiver representation space](quiver-representation-space.md).
