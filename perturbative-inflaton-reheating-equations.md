# Perturbative inflaton reheating equations

↑ **Parent:** [Reheating](reheating.md)

A coarse-grained description in which quadratic [inflaton](inflaton.md) [oscillations](oscillation.md) have zero mean [pressure](pressure-split.md) and lose [energy](energy-split.md) at constant effective decay rate $\Gamma$ to a relativistic component. Adding the equations preserves total [energy](energy-split.md) accounting with the appropriate expansion work. Efficient transfer occurs around $H\sim\Gamma$, but producing particles and achieving [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) are different steps. The potential alone does not specify the decay rate or thermalization interactions.
