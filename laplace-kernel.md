# Laplace kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Positive-semidefinite kernel](positive-semidefinite-kernel.md)

The Laplace kernel on $\mathbb R^d$ is a translation-invariant [positive-semidefinite kernel](positive-semidefinite-kernel.md). Independent standard [Cauchy random variables](cauchy-random-variable.md) give its [Random Fourier feature](random-fourier-features.md) representation through their characteristic function $e^{-|t|}$.
