# Graded Dirac bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac bracket](dirac-bracket.md)

A [Dirac bracket](dirac-bracket.md) on a graded phase space eliminates second-class constraints while respecting the [graded Poisson bracket](graded-poisson-bracket.md). The fermionic kinetic term $i\psi^m\dot\psi_m/2$ gives $\{\psi^m,\psi^n\}_D=-i\eta^{mn}$. Quantization then gives $\{\widehat\psi^m,\widehat\psi^n\}_+=\eta^{mn}$.
