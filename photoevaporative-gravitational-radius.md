# Photoevaporative gravitational radius

↑ **Parent:** [Photoevaporation](photoevaporation.md)

The thermal binding scale defined by equating heated gas [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) to circular [Keplerian orbit](kepler-orbit.md) speed. It is $r_g=GM/c_s^2$. The circular-orbit binding energy is $GM/(2r)$ per unit mass, while the gas [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) is of order $c_s^2$. A heated disk surface at radii comparable to or larger than $r_g$ can therefore drive [photoevaporation](photoevaporation.md). The actual launching radius can differ by a factor of order unity; $c_s$ is not the ballistic escape speed.
