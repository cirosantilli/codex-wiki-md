# Slow-roll approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow-roll_approximation)

For a canonical scalar field with potential $V(\phi)$, slow roll reduces the field and Friedmann equations to

$$
H^2\simeq\frac{V}{3M_{\rm Pl}^2},
\qquad
3H\dot\phi\simeq-V'.
$$
