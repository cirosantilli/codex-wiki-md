# Positive distance between a unit sphere and a disjoint finite-dimensional subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Closed vector subspace](closed-vector-subspace.md)

If $E$ is a nonzero [closed linear subspace](closed-vector-subspace.md) of a normed ambient space and $F$ is finite-dimensional with $E\cap F=\{0\}$, the unit sphere of $E$ has positive distance from $F$. Otherwise a sequence of nearly coincident points yields a bounded sequence in $F$, hence a convergent subsequence; closedness of $E$ puts its norm-one limit in both [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md). In applications to a [bidual space](bidual-of-a-normed-space.md), completeness of the [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) makes its canonical image closed.
