# Semicircular disk

↑ **Parent:** [Disk (mathematics)](disk-mathematics.md)

A semicircular disk is one half of a [disk](disk-mathematics.md) cut along a diameter, including its interior. It is a [circular sector](circular-sector.md) of angle $\pi$, with [area](surface-area.md) $\pi a^2/2$. Source [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) use $0\leq r\leq a$, $0\leq\theta\leq\pi$, and [area element](area-element-of-a-surface.md) $r\,dr\,d\theta$. Its boundary consists of a [semicircle](semicircle.md) and a diameter; the [semicircle](semicircle.md) alone is a [curve](curve.md) and has no planar interior.
