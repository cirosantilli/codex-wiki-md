# Single-particle analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md)

[single-particle analysis](single-particle-analysis.md) reconstructs a three-dimensional structure from many two-dimensional images of individual particles in different orientations. Classification, alignment and assumptions about symmetry or structural sameness determine which information can be combined.
