<h1 id="alternating-integers-have-no-first-order-cesaro-sum">Alternating integers have no first-order Cesàro sum</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md)

For $a_n=(-1)^n(n+1)$, direct finite [summation](summation.md) gives $s_{2m}=m+1$ and $s_{2m+1}=-(m+1)$. Consequently

$$
\sigma_{2m}=\frac{m+1}{2m+1}\longrightarrow\frac12,\qquad
\sigma_{2m+1}=0.
$$

The distinct even and odd [subsequential limits](subsequential-limit.md) prevent first-order [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md). More averaging is a different method; a value assigned by another method is not a first-order [Cesàro sum](cesaro-sum.md).
