# Forward problem

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)

A forward problem computes observable data $f=Ku$ from a known state or parameter $u$ and a specified model $K$. Its associated [inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md) reconstructs $u$ from $f$ and the model. Inverse problems can fail existence, uniqueness or continuous dependence on the data.
