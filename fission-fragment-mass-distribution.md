# Fission-fragment mass distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md)

The yield of fragments as a function of fragment [mass number](mass-number.md) or mass fraction. Low-excitation actinide [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md) commonly gives two peaks, corresponding to complementary lighter and heavier fragments. Smooth [liquid-drop fission energy](liquid-drop-fission-energy.md) by itself does not predict this yield: nuclear-shell structure and the dynamics of the descent toward scission matter. Increasing excitation can weaken shell effects and change the distribution. A two-peaked illustrative curve is therefore not a universal measured spectrum.
