# Hypotenuse

↑ **Parent:** [Right triangle](right-triangle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypotenuse)

The [hypotenuse](hypotenuse.md) is the side opposite the [right angle](right-angle.md) in a [right triangle](right-triangle.md). Its [length](length.md) $c$ is determined by the [Pythagorean theorem](pythagorean-theorem.md).
