# Comparative half-life

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay transition](beta-decay-transition.md)

The product of a dimensionless beta-decay statistical phase-space factor $f$ and the partial [half-life](half-life.md) $t$ for the transition. The tabulated $\log ft$ normally means $\log_{10}[ft/(1\,\mathrm s)]$. Factoring out this approximate [phase space](phase-space.md) dependence allows comparisons of nuclear weak matrix elements. Strong superallowed [Fermi transitions](fermi-transition.md) have small $\log ft$; [forbidden beta decays](forbidden-beta-decay.md) generally have larger values, with nuclear-structure exceptions and overlaps between categories.
