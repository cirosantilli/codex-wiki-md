# Particle polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Little group](little-group.md)

A particle polarization specifies a physical spin state at fixed [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). Massive spin states transform under the rotation [little group](little-group.md) $SO(D-1)$; finite-spin massless states transform under its transverse rotation part $SO(D-2)$. A [massive two-form field](massive-two-form-field.md) has $\binom{D-1}{2}$ such states, while a [two-form gauge field](two-form-gauge-field.md) has $\binom{D-2}{2}$. Polarization components must be counted after field equations and any gauge equivalence are imposed.
