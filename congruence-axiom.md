# Congruence axiom

↑ **Parent:** [Indirect revealed preference](indirect-revealed-preference.md)

The [congruence axiom](congruence-axiom.md) says that an affordable alternative indirectly revealed at least as desirable as a chosen alternative must also be chosen. For a chosen bundle $x$ and an affordable unchosen bundle $y$, the axiom rules out $yRx$; because $xRy$, every strict-part-preserving completion ranks $x$ strictly above $y$.
