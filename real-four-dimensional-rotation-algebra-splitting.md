# Real four-dimensional rotation algebra splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md)

For antisymmetric Maurer–Cartan forms with $d\lambda_{ij}=\lambda_{ik}\wedge\lambda_{kj}$, let $\alpha^\pm=(\lambda_{12}\pm\lambda_{43},\lambda_{23}\pm\lambda_{41},\lambda_{31}\pm\lambda_{42})$. Direct expansion gives $d\alpha_1^\pm=-\alpha_2^\pm\wedge\alpha_3^\pm$ and cyclic analogues, with no mixed terms. The dual triples form two commuting ideals, each with the [SO(3) Lie algebra](so-3-lie-algebra.md) brackets. This is a real compact splitting, distinct from the splitting of the complexified algebra into two copies of $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)$.
