# Endosymbiont

↑ **Parent:** [Endosymbiosis](endosymbiosis.md)

An [endosymbiont](endosymbiont.md) is the internal partner in an [endosymbiosis](endosymbiosis.md). Calling an [organism](organism.md) an [endosymbiont](endosymbiont.md) does not by itself classify it as an [organelle](organelle.md) or imply an irreversible evolutionary transition.
