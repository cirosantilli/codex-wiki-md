# Crystallographic defect

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallographic_defect)

A [crystallographic defect](crystallographic-defect.md) is a departure from ideal repeated structure. In an [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md), a missing [ion](ion.md) can provide an available site for another [ion](ion.md) to move into; movement still requires overcoming an [activation energy](activation-energy.md). This is why an ideal immobile-site model is a limit rather than a universal law for [solid](solid.md) [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md).[§10.5, “Crystal Defects”: vacancies and other imperfections](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)
