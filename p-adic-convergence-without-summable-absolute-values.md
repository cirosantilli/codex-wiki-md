# p-adic convergence without summable absolute values

↑ **Parent:** [P-adic number](p-adic-number.md)

In a complete [Non-Archimedean absolute value](non-archimedean-absolute-value.md) [field](field.md), a series converges if and only if its terms tend to zero: the [ultrametric inequality](ultrametric-inequality.md) bounds every finite tail by its largest term. The displayed [sequence](sequence.md) tends to zero in $\mathbb Q_p$. But each block $p^k\leq n<p^{k+1}$ contributes $p-1$ to the real sum of its [p-adic absolute values](p-adic-absolute-value.md), so that real sum diverges.
