# Steady flow

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid flow](fluid-flow.md)

In steady flow, the velocity and other relevant flow fields are independent of time in the chosen reference frame. [Streamlines](streamline.md) then coincide with the paths of moving particles, while [convective acceleration](convective-acceleration.md) can still be nonzero.
