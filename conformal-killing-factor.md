# Conformal Killing factor

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal Killing vector field](conformal-killing-vector-field.md)

For a [Conformal Killing vector field](conformal-killing-vector-field.md) $X$ on an $n$-dimensional [pseudo-Riemannian manifold](pseudo-riemannian-manifold.md), the [conformal Killing factor](conformal-killing-factor.md) is the [scalar field](scalar-field.md) $\lambda$ in $\mathcal L_Xg=2\lambda g$. For the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), taking the [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md) of the [Conformal Killing equation](conformal-killing-equation.md) gives $\lambda=\nabla_\mu X^\mu/n$. A [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field-split.md) has $\lambda=0$, while the [dilation](uniform-dilation.md) generator $x^\mu\partial_\mu$ on [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) has $\lambda=1$.
