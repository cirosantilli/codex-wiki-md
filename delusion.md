# Delusion

↑ **Parent:** [Psychosis](psychosis.md)

A conviction assessed in relation to the evidence, its responsiveness to alternatives and the person's cultural and clinical context. Disagreement, an unfamiliar cultural explanation or unusual content alone is not a sufficient assessment.
