# Photoionization-recombination thermal balance

↑ **Parent:** [Photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md)

When the number of [photoionizations](photoionization.md) equals the number of recombinations, and only those processes exchange [thermal energy](thermal-energy.md), [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) requires $\langle h\nu-h\nu_0\rangle_{\rm abs}=\langle E_{\rm rec}(T)\rangle$. [Photon](photon.md) absorption and [electron](electron.md) capture have their own spectral weights. The density cancels from this event balance, but the actual [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) temperature requires the two energy averages.[Photoionization heating and Recombination radiation sections](heating-and-cooling-of-hii-regions-by-ian-czekala.md)
