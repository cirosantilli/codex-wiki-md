# Seesaw mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Right-handed neutrino](right-handed-neutrino.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seesaw_mechanism)

The type-I seesaw mechanism combines a Dirac neutrino mass $m_D$ with a much larger right-handed-neutrino [Majorana mass term](majorana-mass-term.md) $M$. Diagonalizing the mass matrix gives a light eigenvalue of order $m_D^2/M$ and a heavy eigenvalue of order $M$.
