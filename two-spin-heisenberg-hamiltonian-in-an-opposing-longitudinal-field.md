# Two-spin Heisenberg Hamiltonian in an opposing longitudinal field

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg model](heisenberg-model.md)

For two spin-one-half particles,

$$
H=\alpha\mathbf S^{(1)}\mathbin\cdot\mathbf S^{(2)}
+B(S_z^{(1)}-S_z^{(2)})
$$

has two eigenvalues $\alpha\hbar^2/4$ and two further eigenvalues

$$
-\frac{\alpha\hbar^2}{4}
\pm\sqrt{B^2\hbar^2+\frac{\alpha^2\hbar^4}{4}}.
$$

For positive $\alpha$, the zero-field limit has a unique [spin-one-half singlet state](spin-one-half-singlet-state.md) ground state and a triply degenerate [spin-one-half triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) excited level.
