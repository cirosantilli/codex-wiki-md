# TLC retardation factor

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-layer chromatography](thin-layer-chromatography.md)

For [thin-layer chromatography](thin-layer-chromatography.md), define the [TLC retardation factor](tlc-retardation-factor.md) using distances along the development direction:

$$
R_f=\frac{d_{\rm spot}}{d_{\rm front}}
=\frac{y_{\rm spot}-y_0}{y_{\rm front}-y_0}.
$$

The origin coordinate is $y_0$; use the centre of a resolved spot and a front marked at the end of development. Both distances begin at the origin, never at the plate bottom. The ratio is dimensionless. An immobile spot has $R_f=0$; a spot at the front has $R_f=1$, where separation from other fast components may be poor. This distance ratio is distinct from the [chromatographic partition coefficient](chromatographic-partition-coefficient.md) and from a column retention time.

For an original stylized plate, choose absolute vertical coordinates $y_0=1\,\mathrm{cm}$, $y_{\rm front}=11\,\mathrm{cm}$, $y_A=4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $y_B=8\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the plate bottom. The [solvent](solvent.md) distance is therefore $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, while the spot distances are $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $7\,\mathrm{cm}$:

$$
R_{f,A}=\frac{4-1}{11-1}=0.3,\qquad
R_{f,B}=\frac{8-1}{11-1}=0.7.
$$

Using $4/11$ or $8/11$ would wrongly include the unused centimetre below the origin. These chosen positions do not represent measured [pigments](pigment.md) or the protocol's plate dimensions.

Original stylized [thin-layer chromatography](thin-layer-chromatography.md) plate with deliberately chosen coordinates, not experimental data. The pencil origin at $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ lies below both spots and the front at $11\,\mathrm{cm}$. All three measurement arrows start at the origin. The two colours label anonymous components and do not identify particular [pigments](pigment.md).

The [TLC retardation factor](tlc-retardation-factor.md) depends on the sample, [chromatographic stationary phase](chromatographic-stationary-phase.md), [eluent](eluent.md) composition, and [temperature](temperature-split.md); plate handling and development conditions must also match when comparing runs. The moving wet front and possible surface [adsorption](adsorption.md) require their own transport assumptions. The equilibrium mobile fraction $f_m$ in the [chromatographic partition coefficient](chromatographic-partition-coefficient.md) model is not unconditionally equal to $R_f$; even that model depends on the phase [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) ratio. Coincident [TLC retardation factors](tlc-retardation-factor.md) alone never prove chemical identity.
