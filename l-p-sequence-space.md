# l-p sequence space

↑ **Parent:** [Banach space](banach-space-split.md)

For $1\le p<\infty$, the space $\ell^p$ consists of scalar sequences $x=(x_n)$ satisfying

$$
\|x\|_p=\left(\sum_{n=1}^\infty|x_n|^p\right)^{1/p}<\infty.
$$

It is a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), and $\ell^q\subset\ell^p$ when $1\le q<p$.

As a [Lebesgue space](lp-space.md) it uses counting measure; as a space of sequences it is a [sequence space](sequence-space.md).
