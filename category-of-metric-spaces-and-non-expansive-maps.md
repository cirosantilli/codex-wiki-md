# Category of metric spaces and non-expansive maps

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)

The category $\mathbf{Met}$ has [metric spaces](metric-space.md) as objects and maps $f$ satisfying $d(fx,fy)\leq d(x,y)$ as morphisms. Its binary product uses the maximum metric.
