# Photon emission rate from monochromatic power

↑ **Parent:** [Photon energy](photon-energy.md)

A monochromatic beam of [frequency](frequency.md) $f$ and mean radiated [power](power.md) $P$ carries mean [photon](photon.md) rate $\dot N=P/(hf)$. It follows by dividing [energy](energy-split.md) per unit time by [energy](energy-split.md) per [photon](photon.md). A broadband source instead requires integrating spectral [power](power.md) divided by the frequency-dependent [photon energy](photon-energy.md); electrical input [power](power.md) must first be converted to radiated [power](power.md).
