# Spherical blast wave in a power-law ambient density

↑ **Parent:** [Self-similar blast wave](self-similar-blast-wave.md)

For an [energy](energy.md)-conserving strong spherical [shock wave](shock-wave.md) expanding into [mass density](density.md) $Cr^{-\beta}$, $0\leq\beta<3$, [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) gives the displayed radius law. Neglect ambient [pressure](pressure.md), radiation losses and gravity in this blast-dominated approximation. Write $u=\dot R U(\xi)$, $\rho=CR^{-\beta}D(\xi)$ and $p=CR^{-\beta}\dot R^2P(\xi)$, with $\xi=r/R$. The radial [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md), momentum equation and adiabatic [pressure](pressure.md) equation reduce to

$$
(U-\xi)D'+D(U'+2U/\xi-\beta)=0,\qquad
(U-\xi)U'+\frac{\beta-3}{2}U=-P'/D,
$$



$$
(U-\xi)P'+[\gamma(U'+2U/\xi)-3]P=0.
$$

The [Strong-shock Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](strong-shock-rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) give $U(1)=P(1)=2/(\gamma+1)$ and $D(1)=(\gamma+1)/(\gamma-1)$. The explosion [energy](energy.md) fixes the radius coefficient through $E=4\pi CR^{3-\beta}\dot R^2\int_0^1[DU^2/2+P/(\gamma-1)]\xi^2d\xi$.
