# Spectroscopic test of the Twin Quasar lens

↑ **Parent:** [Strong gravitational lensing](strong-gravitational-lensing.md)

The two resolved [quasar](quasar.md) images of 0957+561 provide an observational question: are they separate sources, or different light paths from one source? In May 1979, [spectrophotometry](spectrophotometry.md) used the McDonald Observatory 2.7 m [reflecting telescope](reflecting-telescope.md), the UVITS [spectrograph](spectrograph.md), and an intensified dissector scanner at the f/9 focus of the [Cassegrain reflector](cassegrain-reflector.md). A 600-groove-per-millimetre [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md) produced 4100-7100 [ångström](angstrom.md) scans sampled every 2 [ångströms](angstrom.md), with about 7 [ångström](angstrom.md) resolution over most of that range. Two [spectrograph slits](optical-slit.md), about 52 arcseconds apart, recorded object and [sky background](sky-brightness.md) simultaneously for subtraction. Interleaving observations of the two images reduced instrumental and atmospheric gain changes.[Section II, printed p. 2: instrument, grating, sampling, resolution, two apertures, sky subtraction and interleaving](spectrophotometry-of-the-double-qso-0957-plus-561-by-beverley-j-wills-and-d-wills.md)

The readout was received light versus [wavelength](wavelength.md) for each image. Closely matching [absorption line](absorption-line.md) [redshifts](redshift.md) supported a common source and absorber; differences in continua and line strengths motivated differential [interstellar extinction](interstellar-extinction.md). Spectral agreement supplied evidence for [gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing-split.md), rather than proving the [point-mass gravitational lens](point-mass-gravitational-lens.md) model. The authors discussed a physical double and discrepant optical and radio light ratios. Their note added in proof attributed the redder spectrum to foreground [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) contamination and emphasized an extended [mass](mass.md) distribution.[Abstract, printed p. 1; section III, pp. 4-5; section IV, pp. 6-7; note added in proof, p. 7, and Figure 3, p. 8: spectral agreement, model limitations and updated galaxy-contamination interpretation](spectrophotometry-of-the-double-qso-0957-plus-561-by-beverley-j-wills-and-d-wills.md)
