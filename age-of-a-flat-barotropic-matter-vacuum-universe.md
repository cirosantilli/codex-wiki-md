# Age of a flat barotropic matter-vacuum universe

↑ **Parent:** [Flat barotropic matter-vacuum solution](flat-barotropic-matter-vacuum-solution.md)

The [flat barotropic matter-vacuum solution](flat-barotropic-matter-vacuum-solution.md) has $H_\Lambda/H_0=\sqrt{\Omega_{\Lambda0}}$ and therefore the displayed age, measured from its [Big Bang](big-bang.md). At fixed $H_0$ and [density](density.md) fractions it is $1/(1+w)$ times the dust-plus-vacuum age. Its $w=0$ case is the [age of a flat matter-Lambda universe](age-of-a-flat-matter-lambda-universe.md).
