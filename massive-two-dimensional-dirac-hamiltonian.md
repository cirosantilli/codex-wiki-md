# Massive two-dimensional Dirac Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac Hamiltonian](dirac-hamiltonian.md)

The two-component model $H=c(p_x\sigma_x+p_y\sigma_y)+V\sigma_z$ has energies $E_s=s\sqrt{c^2p^2+V^2}$. The sign of $V$ changes [eigenstates](eigenstate.md) and [spin](spin.md) [polarization](polarization-waves.md) but not the uniform energy dispersion at fixed $|V|$. The [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) may represent actual [spin](spin.md) or an effective pseudospin, depending on the physical realization.
