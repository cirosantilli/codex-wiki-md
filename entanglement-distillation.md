# Entanglement distillation

↑ **Parent:** [Entangled state](entangled-state.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entanglement_distillation)

Entanglement distillation uses [local operations and classical communication](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md) on many noisy [entangled states](entangled-state.md) to produce fewer high-fidelity [Bell pairs](bell-pair.md). It is also called entanglement purification. The protocol may fail or abort; naming a purification step does not guarantee successful output for arbitrary inputs. If the output is an exact [pure state](pure-state.md) of [Bell pairs](bell-pair.md), every [purification of a density operator](purification-of-a-density-operator.md) factors between those pairs and the environment, so measuring each pair in the [computational basis](computational-basis.md) gives a shared uniform bit independent of an eavesdropper. Approximate privacy follows when the entire retained output is sufficiently close to that pure target. A [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md) cannot be distilled into a [Bell pair](bell-pair.md) by [LOCC](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md), because each local branch preserves separability.
