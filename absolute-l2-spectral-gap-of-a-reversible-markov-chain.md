# Absolute L2 spectral gap of a reversible Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric ergodicity](geometric-ergodicity.md)

For a [reversible Markov chain](reversible-markov-chain.md) with invariant law $\mu$, its [Markov kernel](markov-kernel.md) acts as a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) $P$ on $L^2(\mu)$. An absolute [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) means that $\|P\|$ on the mean-zero functions is at most $r<1$. [Geometric ergodicity](geometric-ergodicity.md) implies this gap for a reversible chain. Indeed, for bounded mean-zero $g$ supported where the geometric prefactor is bounded, the total-variation estimate gives $\langle g,P^{2n}g\rangle\leq C_g r^{2n}$. The [spectral theorem for normal operators](spectral-theorem-for-normal-operators.md) puts its spectral measure in $[-r,r]$. Such test functions are dense in the mean-zero subspace.
