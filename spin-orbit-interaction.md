# Spin-orbit interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Fine structure](fine-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin–orbit_interaction)

The spin-orbit interaction couples [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) to [spin](spin.md). In [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) it helps determine [fine structure](fine-structure.md) and the coupling scale that separates [weak-field Zeeman splitting](weak-field-zeeman-splitting.md) from the [Paschen-Back effect](paschen-back-effect.md).
