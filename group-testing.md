# Group testing

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)

A testing strategy that pools samples to determine which individuals satisfy a binary condition. A negative pool rules out every member, whereas a positive pool requires more information to identify the affected individuals. [Conditional probabilities](conditional-probability.md) and [dynamic programming](dynamic-programming.md) can determine an efficient adaptive strategy under a specified model.
