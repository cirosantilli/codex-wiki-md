# Attributional correction

↑ **Parent:** [Attribution theory](attribution-theory.md)

Revising an initial impression to incorporate additional information about why conduct occurred. A cognitive-load manipulation can test the processing requirements of correction, but a final judgment alone does not establish a mandatory sequence of mental stages.
