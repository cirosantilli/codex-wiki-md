# Boltzmann H functional

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md)

The [integral](integral.md) uses $0\log0=0$ when it is defined. For the [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md), the [Boltzmann H theorem](h-theorem.md) makes it nonincreasing under suitable regularity and integrability. This functional is distinct from the microstate-counting [Boltzmann entropy](boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md); the physical kinetic [entropy](entropy.md) has the opposite sign.
