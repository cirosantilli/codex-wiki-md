# Grahame equation

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)

For a planar surface contacting a symmetric monovalent ideal electrolyte on one side, the [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md) is $\psi''=(2en_\infty/\varepsilon)\sinh(e\psi/(k_BT))$. Multiplication by $\psi'$ and integration using the zero field and zero potential in the bulk gives

$$
(\psi')^2=\frac{8n_\infty k_BT}{\varepsilon}\sinh^2\left(\frac{e\psi}{2k_BT}\right).
$$

The solution decays towards the bulk, and the boundary condition $\sigma=-\varepsilon\psi'(0^+)$ therefore yields the displayed charge-potential relation, with $\kappa^2=2e^2n_\infty/(\varepsilon k_BT)$. Its small-potential limit is the [planar Debye-Hückel double layer](planar-debye-huckel-double-layer.md). It is nonlinear but still a mean-field model with point-like [ions](ion.md) and constant [permittivity](permittivity-split.md); it does not include a compact interfacial layer or finite-ion-size saturation.
