# Continuous extension

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous function](continuous-function.md)

A continuous extension of a [function](function-split.md) $f:D\to Y$ to a larger [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) $E\supseteq D$ is a [continuous function](continuous-function.md) $F:E\to Y$ satisfying $F(x)=f(x)$ for $x\in D$. The [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) and [codomain](codomain.md) carry specified [topologies](topology-split.md). Prescribing values on a dense [subset](subset.md) does not by itself prove the existence of a continuous extension.
