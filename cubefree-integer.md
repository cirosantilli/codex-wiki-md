# Cubefree integer

↑ **Parent:** [Integer](integer-split.md)

A cubefree integer is a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) divisible by no cube of a [prime](prime-number-split.md). Equivalently, every [prime exponent](p-adic-valuation.md) in its [prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) is at most two.
