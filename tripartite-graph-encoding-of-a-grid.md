# Tripartite graph encoding of a grid

↑ **Parent:** [Tripartite graph](tripartite-graph.md)

For $A\subseteq[n]^2$, use three disjoint labelled [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) classes $X=[n]$, $Y=[n]$, $Z=[2n]$. Each $(x,y)\in A$ inserts the three [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) $xy,x(x+y),y(x+y)$, producing pairwise [edge-disjoint triangles](edge-disjoint-triangles.md). Every [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) with labels $(x,y,z)$ corresponds to the three points $(x,y),(x,z-x),(z-y,y)$ of $A$. It is canonical when $z=x+y$; otherwise it gives a [corner in an integer grid](corner-in-an-integer-grid.md) with nonzero displacement $z-x-y$.
