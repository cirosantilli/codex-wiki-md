# Energy eigenfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md)

A nonzero position-space [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) representing an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md). For a time-independent [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), its time evolution is a [global phase](global-phase.md), so its position [probability density](probability-density.md) is stationary.
