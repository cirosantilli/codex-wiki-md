<h1 id="marechal-approximation">Maréchal approximation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Strehl ratio](strehl-ratio.md)

For small residual phase errors, $S\simeq e^{-\sigma_\phi^2}$, where $\sigma_\phi=2\pi h_{\rm rms}/\lambda$. The variance is taken after removing irrelevant constant phase. This approximation to the [Strehl ratio](strehl-ratio.md) is most useful near the [diffraction limit of a telescope](diffraction-limit-of-a-telescope.md).
