# Principal stress plane

↑ **Parent:** [Principal stress](principal-stress.md)

A plane whose [unit normal vector](unit-normal.md) is an eigenvector of the [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md). Its [traction](traction.md) has no tangential component, so the resolved [shear stress](shear-stress.md) vanishes. This is distinct from a principal plane in [geometrical optics](geometrical-optics-split.md).
