# U-tube liquid oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md)

In a uniform U-tube containing incompressible liquid of [density](density.md) $\rho$, cross section $A$ and total moving length $l$, let one surface fall by $y$ while the other rises by $y$. The height difference is $2y$, and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) relative to equilibrium is $U=\rho gAy^2$. The whole moving [mass](mass.md) is $\rho Al$, so $K=\rho Al\dot y^2/2$. [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) or [energy](energy-split.md) conservation gives $\ddot y+(2g/l)y=0$ and $\omega_0=\sqrt{2g/l}$. This assumes small excursions that keep the free surfaces in the uniform vertical limbs.
