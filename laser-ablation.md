# Laser ablation

↑ **Parent:** [Laser](laser-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_ablation)

In [laser ablation](laser-ablation.md), concentrated optical energy removes or disrupts material locally. Cutting a loaded cellular structure and observing recoil can probe its mechanics, but the recoil also depends on dissipation and surrounding constraints.
