# Top-hat wavefunction

↑ **Parent:** [Wave function](wave-function-split.md)

A [top-hat wavefunction](top-hat-wavefunction.md) is constant on an interval and zero elsewhere. It has a uniform [position](position.md) [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) and a [sinc function](sinc-function.md) [momentum](momentum-split.md) [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md). Its discontinuous edges place it outside the finite-kinetic-energy domain of the free [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). Smooth-edged localized packets can approximate its [position](position.md) profile while having different [momentum](momentum-split.md) tails.
