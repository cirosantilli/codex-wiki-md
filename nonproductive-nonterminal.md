# Nonproductive nonterminal

↑ **Parent:** [Variable-based grammar](variable-based-grammar.md)

A nonterminal is nonproductive when no word derived from it consists entirely of terminal symbols. Every derivation containing such a symbol therefore fails to produce a word in the generated [language](formal-language-theory.md).
