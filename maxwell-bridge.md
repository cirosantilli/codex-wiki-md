# Maxwell bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_bridge)

A null [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) for measuring a lossy [inductor](inductor.md) against a standard [capacitor](capacitor.md). If opposite arms are $R_x+i\omega L_x$ and $(1/R_1+i\omega C)^{-1}$, and the remaining arms are $R_2,R_3$, balance requires their products to agree. Consequently $R_x=R_2R_3/R_1$ and $L_x=CR_2R_3$. Two independent real adjustments are necessary; changing the source [frequency](frequency.md) does not change the ideal balance conditions.
