# CRIRES

↑ **Parent:** [Very Large Telescope](very-large-telescope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRIRES)

CRIRES is a high-resolution infrared spectrograph. Its upgraded form supports molecular spectroscopy of [exoplanet atmospheres](exoplanet-atmosphere.md) and measurements based on the [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md). See [ESO's instrument overview](https://www.eso.org/public/teles-instr/paranal-observatory/vlt/vlt-instr/crires%2B/).
