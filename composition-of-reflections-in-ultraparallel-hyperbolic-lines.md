# Composition of reflections in ultraparallel hyperbolic lines

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic reflection](hyperbolic-reflection.md)

The composition of [hyperbolic reflections](hyperbolic-reflection.md) in two [ultraparallel hyperbolic lines](ultraparallel-hyperbolic-lines.md) has infinite order. Map their common perpendicular to the [imaginary axis](imaginary-axis.md) in the [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md). The two reflecting [hyperbolic lines](hyperbolic-line.md) become semicircles centred at zero, with distinct radii $r_1,r_2$. Reflection in the radius-$r$ semicircle is $z\mapsto r^2/\overline z$, so their composition is $z\mapsto(r_2/r_1)^2z$. Every positive power remains nonidentity, because $(r_2/r_1)^{2k}\ne1$ for $k\ge1$.
