# Semiconducting carbon nanotube

↑ **Parent:** [Single-walled carbon nanotube](single-walled-carbon-nanotube.md)

In ideal [nanotube zone folding](nanotube-zone-folding.md), a [single-walled carbon nanotube](single-walled-carbon-nanotube.md) with indices $(n,m)$ satisfying $n-m\not\equiv0\pmod3$. Circumferential quantization misses the [Dirac valleys in graphene](dirac-valleys-in-graphene.md), opening a band gap.
