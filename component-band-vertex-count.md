# Component-band vertex count

↑ **Parent:** [Achlioptas process](achlioptas-process.md)

This is the total number of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in [graph components](component-graph-theory.md) with orders in the specified half-open interval. It differs from the number of those [graph components](component-graph-theory.md). Write $N_{\geq k}$ for the total above the lower endpoint. Then $N_{[k,\ell)}=N_{\geq k}-N_{\geq\ell}$. Disjoint size bands count disjoint sets of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) at a common time, even though their mass may move between bands as an [Achlioptas process](achlioptas-process.md) adds [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md).
