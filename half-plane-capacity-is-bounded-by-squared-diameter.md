# Half-plane capacity is bounded by squared diameter

↑ **Parent:** [Half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md)

There is a universal constant $C$ such that every compact H-hull satisfies

$$
\operatorname{hcap}(A)\leq C\operatorname{diam}(A)^2.
$$

Translate and scale so the hull lies in a unit half-disc, then use the [Brownian representation of half-plane capacity](brownian-representation-of-half-plane-capacity.md) and the $O(y^{-1})$ [harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md) of that half-disc as viewed from $iy$.
