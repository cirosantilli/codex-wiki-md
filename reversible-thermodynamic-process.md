# Reversible thermodynamic process

↑ **Parent:** [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md)

A thermodynamically reversible process can be reversed without a net change of the system and surroundings. Its entropy change equals reversible heat transfer divided by temperature. A reversible [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) conserves [entropy](entropy-split.md); adiabaticity alone does not forbid internal entropy production in an irreversible evolution.
