# Neural decoding

↑ **Parent:** [Neural coding](neural-coding.md)

Inferring a stimulus, task state or behavior from neural measurements. Successful decoding demonstrates information accessible to the chosen decoder; it does not by itself establish the neural site's causal necessity, exclusive representation or downstream use.
