<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume $A\ne0$, as is implicit in the claimed conclusion. Suppose that a [surjective](../../../../surjective-function.md) [ring homomorphism](../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) $f:R\to M_2(A)$ exists. Choose $r_1,r_2\in R$ mapping to the [matrix units](../../../../matrix-unit.md) $E_{12}$ and $E_{21}$. Applying $f$ to the assumed relation gives integers $c_1,\ldots,c_n$ such that

$$
E_{22}=E_{21}E_{12}
=\sum_{k=1}^n c_kE_{12}^{\,k}E_{21}.
$$

But $E_{12}^2=0$ and $E_{12}E_{21}=E_{11}$, so the right side is $c_1E_{11}$. Its $(2,2)$ entry is zero, whereas the $(2,2)$ entry of $E_{22}$ is $1_A\ne0$. This contradiction proves that no such [surjective](../../../../surjective-function.md) [ring homomorphism](../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) exists.

If the convention for [rings](../../../../ring.md) allows $A$ to be the [zero ring](../../../../zero-ring.md), that case must be excluded from the assertion: $M_2(0)=0$, and the zero [ring homomorphism](../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) is [surjective](../../../../surjective-function.md). This is a convention issue in the printed question.
