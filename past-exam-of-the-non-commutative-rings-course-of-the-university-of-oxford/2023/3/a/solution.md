<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [Weyl algebra](../../../../weyl-algebra.md) convention $[y_i,x_j]=\delta_{ij}$, with $[x_i,x_j]=[y_i,y_j]=0$, where the brackets are [commutators](../../../../commutator.md). Its [order filtration of a Weyl algebra](../../../../order-filtration-of-a-weyl-algebra.md) is

$$
F_jR=\sum_{|\beta|\leq j}\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]y^\beta\quad(j\geq0),\qquad F_jR=0\quad(j<0).
$$

Thus $x_i$ has degree zero and $y_i$ degree one. By the [ordered monomial basis of a Weyl algebra](../../../../ordered-monomial-basis-of-a-weyl-algebra.md), the [associated graded ring](../../../../associated-graded-ring.md) is

$$
A=\operatorname{gr}R\cong\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n,\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n],\qquad \deg x_i=0,\quad\deg\xi_i=1,
$$

where $\xi_i$ is the symbol of $y_i$.

The [Rees ring of a filtered algebra](../../../../rees-ring-of-a-filtered-algebra.md) is the [graded ring](../../../../graded-ring.md)

$$
\boxed{\widetilde R=\bigoplus_{j\geq0}(F_jR)t^j\subseteq R[t],}
$$

where $t$ is a central indeterminate. Here it is generated by $x_i$, $Y_i=y_it$, and $t$, with degrees $0,1,1$, respectively, and relations

$$
[Y_i,x_j]=\delta_{ij}t,\qquad [x_i,x_j]=[Y_i,Y_j]=[t,x_i]=[t,Y_i]=0.
$$

It interpolates between the [associated graded ring](../../../../associated-graded-ring.md) and the original [filtered ring](../../../../filtered-ring.md):

$$
\widetilde R/(t)\cong\operatorname{gr}R,\qquad \widetilde R/(t-1)\cong R.
$$

A [good filtration for the order filtration](../../../../good-filtration-for-the-order-filtration.md) on $M$ is an increasing exhaustive family of complex [vector subspaces](../../../../vector-subspace.md) $(M_j)_{j\in\mathbb Z}$, zero for sufficiently negative $j$, with $F_iR\,M_j\subseteq M_{i+j}$, such that the [associated graded module](../../../../associated-graded-module.md)

$$
\operatorname{gr}M=\bigoplus_{j\in\mathbb Z}M_j/M_{j-1}
$$

is a [finitely generated module](../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) over $A$. Equivalently, there are finitely many $m_1,\ldots,m_s$ and integer shifts $d_1,\ldots,d_s$ such that $M_j=\sum_{\ell=1}^s(F_{j-d_\ell}R)m_\ell$. In particular, choose any finite generating family $m_1,\ldots,m_s$ of $M$ and put

$$
\boxed{M_j=\sum_{\ell=1}^s(F_jR)m_\ell.}
$$

This is a [good filtration for the order filtration](../../../../good-filtration-for-the-order-filtration.md).

For $M\ne0$, its [dimension of a Weyl algebra module for the order filtration](../../../../dimension-of-a-weyl-algebra-module-for-the-order-filtration.md) is

$$
\boxed{d(M)=\dim\operatorname{Supp}_A(\operatorname{gr}M)
=\dim_{\mathrm{Krull}}\bigl(A/\operatorname{Ann}_A(\operatorname{gr}M)\bigr).}
$$

Here [support of a module](../../../../support-of-a-module.md), [annihilator of a module](../../../../annihilator-of-a-module.md), and [Krull dimension](../../../../krull-dimension.md) are taken over the commutative [polynomial ring](../../../../polynomial-ring.md) $A$. The corresponding [algebraic set](../../../../algebraic-set.md) is the [order characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module](../../../../order-characteristic-variety-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md). This dimension is independent of the chosen [good filtration for the order filtration](../../../../good-filtration-for-the-order-filtration.md); one may set $d(0)=-\infty$.

The pieces $F_jR$ generally have infinite complex [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md), since they contain all polynomials in the $x_i$. Thus a polynomial-growth definition based directly on $\dim_{\mathbb C}M_j$ would be inappropriate for this [order filtration of a Weyl algebra](../../../../order-filtration-of-a-weyl-algebra.md).
