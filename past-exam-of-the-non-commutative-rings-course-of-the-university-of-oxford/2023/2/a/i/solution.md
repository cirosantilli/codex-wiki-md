<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [multiplicative subset](../../../../../multiplicatively-closed-set.md) $S$ is a [left Ore set](../../../../../left-ore-set.md) if for every $s\in S$ and $r\in R$ there are $s'\in S$ and $r'\in R$ with

$$
\boxed{s'r=r's.}
$$

Equivalently, the subsets $Sr$ and $Rs$ intersect for each such pair. This is the common-left-multiple condition needed for [Ore localization](../../../../../ore-localization.md) with denominators on the left. In a [noncommutative domain](../../../../../noncommutative-domain.md), no extra condition concerning annihilation by denominators is needed, since all members of $S$ are [regular elements of a ring](../../../../../regular-element-of-a-ring.md).
