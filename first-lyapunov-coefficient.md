# First Lyapunov coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md)

The [first Lyapunov coefficient](first-lyapunov-coefficient.md) is the [cubic](cubic-polynomial.md) radial coefficient in the center-manifold normal form of a smooth [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md), after an appropriate normalization. In $\dot r=\beta r+l_1r^3+\cdots$, its nonzero sign distinguishes [stable](lyapunov-stability.md) and [unstable](lyapunov-stability.md) bifurcating small [cycles](periodic-orbit.md) relative to the eigenvalue-crossing direction. If $l_1=0$, higher-order terms are required and the generic [cubic](cubic-polynomial.md) [Hopf theorem](hopf-bifurcation.md) does not apply.
