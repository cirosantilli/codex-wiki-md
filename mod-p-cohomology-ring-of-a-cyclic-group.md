# Mod-p cohomology ring of a cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

For a prime $p$, a classifying [Eilenberg–MacLane space](eilenberg-maclane-space.md) for $C_p$ is the infinite lens space $S^\infty/C_p$, using scalar multiplication by a primitive $p$th root on the complex-coordinate sphere. Its contractible [universal cover](universal-cover.md) is $S^\infty$. The standard two-periodic free resolution has differentials $g-1$ and $1+g+\cdots+g^{p-1}$, both zero after applying [cochains](singular-cochain.md) with trivial $\mathbb F_p$ coefficients. Thus every [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) degree has dimension one. If $p=2$, the ring is $\mathbb F_2[t]$, $|t|=1$. If $p$ is odd, it is $\Lambda(a)\otimes\mathbb F_p[b]$, with $|a|=1$, $|b|=2$: graded commutativity gives $a^2=0$ and the periodicity class $b$ induces the degree-two isomorphisms. The [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) gives two copies of these generators for $C_p\times C_p$; the dimension in degree $d$ is $d+1$ for every $p$.
