<h1 id="yule-walker-estimator-for-an-autoregressive-process-of-order-one">Yule–Walker estimator for an autoregressive process of order one</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive process of order one](autoregressive-process-of-order-one.md)

For $X_t-\mu=\phi(X_{t-1}-\mu)+\varepsilon_t$, the [autocovariance](autocovariance.md) equation is $\gamma(1)=\phi\gamma(0)$. Replacing both autocovariances by sample sums divided by the same $n$ yields $\widehat\phi_{YW}=\widehat\gamma(1)/\widehat\gamma(0)$, the lag-one [sample autocorrelation function](sample-autocorrelation-function.md).
