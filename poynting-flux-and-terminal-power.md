# Poynting flux and terminal power

↑ **Parent:** [Static Poynting flow](static-poynting-flow.md)

For static [electric fields](electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md), [Ampère's law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) and $\mathbf E=-\nabla\phi$ give $\boldsymbol{\mathcal P}=\mathbf E\times\mathbf H=\phi\mathbf J_f-\nabla\times(\phi\mathbf H)$. [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) on an oriented separating surface then gives

$$
\int_\Sigma\boldsymbol{\mathcal P}\cdot\mathbf n\,dS
=\sum_k\phi_k I_k-\oint_{\partial\Sigma}\phi\mathbf H\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell,
$$

where each [perfect conductor](perfect-conductor.md) crossing it has constant [electric potential](electric-potential.md) $\phi_k$ and signed [electric current](electric-current.md) $I_k$. The line term is absent for a closed surface and must vanish in the limiting sense at infinity for a noncompact surface. Under that condition the incoming [Poynting flux](poynting-flux.md) equals terminal power. If the signed [electric currents](electric-current.md) sum to zero, the result is independent of the reference for [electric potential](electric-potential.md).
