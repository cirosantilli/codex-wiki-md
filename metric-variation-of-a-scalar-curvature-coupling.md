# Metric variation of a scalar-curvature coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-tensor theory](scalar-tensor-theory.md)

With the [curvature sign convention](curvature-sign-convention.md) in which $\delta R_{bc}=\nabla_c\delta\Gamma^a{}_{ba}-\nabla_a\delta\Gamma^a{}_{bc}$, the inverse-metric [variation](variation.md) of $\int\sqrt{-g}\,\Phi R$ is $\int\sqrt{-g}\,E_{ab}\delta g^{ab}$ plus a [boundary term](boundary-term.md), for a [scalar](scalar.md) multiplier held fixed in that variation. The [Palatini identity](palatini-identity.md) expresses the [curvature](curvature.md) variation as divergences of $\delta\Gamma$. Two [integrations by parts](integration-by-parts.md) move the [derivatives](derivative.md) onto $\Phi$, yielding the displayed terms. For constant $\Phi$ they vanish. The opposite [curvature](curvature.md) convention instead has $E_{ab}=\Phi G_{ab}+g_{ab}\Box\Phi-\nabla_a\nabla_b\Phi$.
