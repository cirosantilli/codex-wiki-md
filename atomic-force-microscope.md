# Atomic force microscope

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

An atomic force microscope measures interactions through the deflection or dynamics of a calibrated compliant cantilever. In a static force curve, calibrated deflection $d$ and spring constant $k_c$ give $F=k_cd$; converting stage position to a surface gap also requires the bending correction and a contact reference. The [colloidal probe technique](colloidal-probe-technique.md) replaces a sharp probe with a sphere of controlled radius.
