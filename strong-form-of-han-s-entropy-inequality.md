<h1 id="strong-form-of-han-s-entropy-inequality">Strong form of Han's entropy inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Han's inequality for relative entropy](han-s-inequality-for-relative-entropy.md)

The strong form

$$
(n-1)\sum_iH(X^{(i)})
\geq n(n-2)H(X_{1:n})+\sum_iH(X_i)
$$

follows by iterating [entropy submodularity](entropy-submodularity.md).
