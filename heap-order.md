# Heap order

↑ **Parent:** [Binary heap](binary-heap.md)

The minimum convention for [heap order](heap-order.md) requires every [parent in a rooted tree](parent-in-a-rooted-tree.md) to have priority no greater than each of its [children in a rooted tree](child-in-a-rooted-tree.md). The displayed formula uses the [array](array-data-structure.md) representation of a [binary heap](binary-heap.md). It permits equal priorities and does not require a [sorted array](sorted-array.md); the maximum convention reverses the inequality.
