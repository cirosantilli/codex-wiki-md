# Quenched atmospheric mixing ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical quench level](chemical-quench-level.md)

Above a [chemical quench level](chemical-quench-level.md), rapid eddy mixing can keep a species' number mixing ratio $f_A$ approximately fixed at its deeper value, provided condensation, photochemical loss, and molecular diffusion remain negligible. The species' [number density](number-density.md) is then $n_A=f_Ap/(k_BT)$; constant mixing ratio does not imply constant [number density](number-density.md). In an isothermal atmosphere, $n_A$ decreases exponentially with altitude on the [atmospheric scale height](atmospheric-scale-height.md).
