# Percentage

↑ **Parent:** [Ratio](ratio.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percentage)

A [percentage](percentage.md) expresses a [ratio](ratio.md) in hundredths: $p$ as a fraction is $100p\%$. For example, $18/60=0.3=30\%$. Comparing two [percentages](percentage.md) requires specifying whether the change is an absolute difference in [percentage points](percentage-point.md) or a relative change divided by the initial value.
