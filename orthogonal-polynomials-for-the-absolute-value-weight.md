# Orthogonal polynomials for the absolute-value weight

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md)

For the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) obtained by integrating with weight $|x|$ on $[-1,1]$, odd moments vanish and the moment of $x^{2m}$ is $1/(m+1)$. The first [monic orthogonal polynomials](monic-orthogonal-polynomial.md) are $1$, $x$ and $x^2-1/2$. [Positive definiteness](positive-definiteness.md) follows because the weight is positive [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) and a nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) cannot vanish [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md).
