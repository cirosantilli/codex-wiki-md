# Finite canonical partition function at the bosonic Hagedorn threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Hagedorn temperature](hagedorn-temperature.md)

For a critical [closed bosonic string](closed-string.md) in 25 noncompact spatial dimensions, the smoothed [string oscillator](string-oscillator.md) rest-mass density behaves as $\rho(M)\sim C M^{-26}e^{\beta_H M}$, with $\beta_H=4\pi\sqrt{\alpha'}$. At large $M$, the centre-of-mass [momentum](momentum.md) integral of $e^{-\beta\sqrt{M^2+p^2}}$ is asymptotic to $(2\pi M/\beta)^{25/2}e^{-\beta M}$, up to the convention for phase-space measure. Thus the high-mass tail of the one-string [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is proportional to $\int_{M_0}^\infty M^{-27/2}e^{-(\beta-\beta_H)M}\,dM$. This converges at $\beta=\beta_H$ but fails for $\beta<\beta_H$. Expanding about the endpoint gives twelve finite derivatives and a nonanalytic term proportional to $(\beta-\beta_H)^{25/2}$; the thirteenth derivative diverges. Consequently exponential state growth does not imply divergence of the [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) at the threshold itself. The discussion assumes the separate infrared instability of the bosonic [tachyon](tachyon.md) has been regulated and does not claim a stable bosonic thermal vacuum.
