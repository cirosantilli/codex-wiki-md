<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The vectors are [linearly independent](../../../../linear-independence.md) over $F$ if $\sum_{j=1}^n c_j\mathbf v_j=0$ with $c_j\in F$ implies $c_j=0$ for every $j$. Otherwise there is a nontrivial [linear dependence](../../../../linear-dependence.md). Forming a matrix with the displayed vectors as columns gives

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&2&-1\\3&4&0\\2&0&4\end{pmatrix}=16\alpha-16.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\alpha=1}
$$

is the unique dependent case. At that value the explicit relation $2\mathbf v_1-\tfrac32\mathbf v_2-\mathbf v_3=0$ verifies the dependence.
