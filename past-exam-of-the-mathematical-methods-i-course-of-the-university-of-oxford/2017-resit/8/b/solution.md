<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The plane has [normal vector](../../../../normal-vector.md) $\mathbf n=(2,3,6)$ of length seven. Projecting the displacement from any point on the plane onto the [unit normal](../../../../unit-normal.md) gives the [distance from a point to a plane](../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md)

$$
\boxed{d=\frac{|2x_0+3y_0+6z_0-30|}{7}.}
$$

For example the perpendicular foot is $\mathbf r_0-\mathbf n(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf r_0-30)/49$. Substitution places it on the plane, and its displacement from $\mathbf r_0$ is parallel to $\mathbf n$, verifying that this is the minimum distance.
