<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Hooke's law](../../../../hooke-s-law.md) and [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) give $m\ddot x=-kx$, so $\omega_0=\sqrt{k/m}$. The initial conditions select the [simple harmonic motion](../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md)

$$
\boxed{x(t)=x_0\cos\omega_0t,\qquad
K(t)=\frac{kx_0^2}{2}\sin^2\omega_0t,\qquad
U(t)=\frac{kx_0^2}{2}\cos^2\omega_0t.}
$$

The [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [elastic potential energy](../../../../elastic-energy.md) exchange periodically while their sum $E_0=kx_0^2/2$ is constant.
