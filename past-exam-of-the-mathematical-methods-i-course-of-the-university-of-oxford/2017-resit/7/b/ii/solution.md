<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At [critical damping](../../../../../critical-damping.md), the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) has the repeated root $r=-\omega_0$. Its independent solutions are $e^{-\omega_0t}$ and $te^{-\omega_0t}$, giving

$$
\boxed{x(t)=(A+Bt)e^{-\omega_0t}.}
$$

The factor $t$ supplies the second independent solution that would be lost by using just one exponential. There is no oscillatory factor at this boundary between the underdamped and overdamped regimes.
