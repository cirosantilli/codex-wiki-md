<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [damped harmonic oscillator](../../../../../damped-harmonic-oscillator.md), the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $r^2+2\gamma r+\omega_0^2$. When $0\le\gamma<\omega_0$, its roots are $-\gamma\pm i\Omega$, with $\Omega=\sqrt{\omega_0^2-\gamma^2}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{x(t)=e^{-\gamma t}\bigl(A\cos\Omega t+B\sin\Omega t\bigr).}
$$

Here $A$ and $B$ are arbitrary real constants. The frequency is $\Omega$, and the amplitude envelope decays with time constant $1/\gamma$ when $\gamma>0$; the zero-damping limit recovers [simple harmonic motion](../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md).
