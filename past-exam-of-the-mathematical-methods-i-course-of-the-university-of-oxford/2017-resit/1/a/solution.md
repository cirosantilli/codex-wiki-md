<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

By [De Moivre's theorem](../../../../de-moivre-s-theorem.md), $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^3=\cos3\theta+i\sin3\theta$. Expanding the cube and equating its [real part](../../../../real-part.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\cos3\theta=\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta\sin^2\theta=4\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta.}
$$

The last form uses $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$; comparing the [imaginary parts](../../../../imaginary-part.md) similarly gives the corresponding sine identity.
