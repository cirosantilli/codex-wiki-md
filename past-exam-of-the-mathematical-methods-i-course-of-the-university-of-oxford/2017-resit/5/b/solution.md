<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With $\alpha=\beta=6$, the eliminated equation gives $4y=3$. Back substitution yields

$$
\boxed{(x,y,z)=\left(\frac12,\frac34,0\right).}
$$

The three left-hand sides are respectively $4$, $2$ and $6$, verifying the solution in the original [linear system](../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md). Its [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is $-8\ne0$, so this solution is unique.
