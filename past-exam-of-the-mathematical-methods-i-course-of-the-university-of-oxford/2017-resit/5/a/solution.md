<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Gaussian elimination](../../../../gaussian-elimination.md) of the first two equations gives $x=2y-1$ and $z=3-4y$. The third then reduces to $(\alpha-2)y=\beta-3$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{ll}
\alpha=2,\ \beta\ne3:&\text{no solution},\\
\alpha=2,\ \beta=3:&\text{infinitely many solutions},\\
\alpha\ne2:&\text{one solution}.
\end{array}}
$$

In the infinite family $y=t$, $x=2t-1$ and $z=3-4t$, for $t\in\mathbb R$. This makes the distinction between an inconsistent [linear system](../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) and a free parameter explicit; its [coefficient matrix](../../../../coefficient-matrix.md) has [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $4-2\alpha$.
