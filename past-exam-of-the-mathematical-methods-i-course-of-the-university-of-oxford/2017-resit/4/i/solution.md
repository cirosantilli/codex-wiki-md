<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [orthogonal matrix](../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) satisfies $A^TA=I$. Taking [determinants](../../../../determinant.md) and using $\det A^T=\det A$ gives $(\det A)^2=1$, so

$$
\boxed{\det A=+1\quad\text{or}\quad\det A=-1.}
$$

This calculation remains valid over the [complex numbers](../../../../complex-number.md): orthogonality uses the [transpose](../../../../transpose.md), whereas a [unitary matrix](../../../../unitary-matrix.md) uses the [conjugate transpose](../../../../conjugate-transpose.md).
