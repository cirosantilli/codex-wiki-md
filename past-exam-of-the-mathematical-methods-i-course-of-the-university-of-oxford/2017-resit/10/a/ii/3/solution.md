↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

A [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md) has $A^*A=I$. For example,

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad A^*A=I.}
$$

This example is invertible, has nonreal off-diagonal entries, and preserves the standard [Hermitian inner product](../../../../../../hermitian-inner-product.md). Although it is also symmetric, that additional property is not required for [unitarity](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md).
