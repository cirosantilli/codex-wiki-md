A [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) has $A^T=A$, without conjugation. One invertible example with nonreal off-diagonal entries is

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}1&i\\i&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad\det A=3.}
$$

It is symmetric but not [Hermitian](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), illustrating the importance of the distinction over $\mathbb C$.
