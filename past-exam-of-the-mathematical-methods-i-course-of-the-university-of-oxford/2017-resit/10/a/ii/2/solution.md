<h1 id="10/a/ii/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

A [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) has $A^*=A$. Choose

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}1&i\\-i&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad\det A=1.}
$$

Its off-diagonal entries are [complex conjugates](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) and its diagonal entries are real, so the defining condition holds. It is also invertible.
