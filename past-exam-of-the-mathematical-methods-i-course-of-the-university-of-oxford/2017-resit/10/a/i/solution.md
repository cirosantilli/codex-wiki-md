<h1 id="10/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) is associative, so

$$
(AB)(B^{-1}A^{-1})=A(BB^{-1})A^{-1}=I,\qquad
(B^{-1}A^{-1})(AB)=B^{-1}(A^{-1}A)B=I.
$$

The uniqueness of the [matrix inverse](../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}.}
$$

The order is reversed; $A^{-1}B^{-1}$ does not generally work because [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) need not be commutative.
