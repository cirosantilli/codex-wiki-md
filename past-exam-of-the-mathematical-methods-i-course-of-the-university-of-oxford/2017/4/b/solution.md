<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\lambda=1$ and $\mu=1/2$, the third equation gives $x=z-1$. Combining it with $2x-z=-4$ gives $z=-2$ and $x=-3$, after which the second equation gives $y=12$. Hence

$$
\boxed{(x,y,z)=(-3,12,-2).}
$$

The [coefficient matrix](../../../../coefficient-matrix.md) has [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $\lambda-2=-1\ne0$, proving uniqueness. Direct substitution gives the required right-hand sides $1$, $5$ and $-1$.
