<h1 id="8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md), the two equal off-diagonal contributions together supply the mixed term. Comparing coefficients gives

$$
\boxed{M=I_0\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

For a nontrivial physical [moment of inertia](../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) scale take $I_0>0$; then this [inertia tensor](../../../../../inertia-tensor.md) is positive definite.
