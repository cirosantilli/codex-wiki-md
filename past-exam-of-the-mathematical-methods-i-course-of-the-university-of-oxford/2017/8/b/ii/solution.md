<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The normalized vectors $u_1=(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$ and $u_2=(-1,1)^T/\sqrt2$ give [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $I_0$ and $3I_0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{U=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
D=\begin{pmatrix}I_0&0\\0&3I_0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The columns are orthonormal, so $U^{-1}=U^T=U^*$ and direct multiplication verifies $U^{-1}MU=D$. Signs of individual [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) are free; the displayed signs also make $\det U=+1$.
