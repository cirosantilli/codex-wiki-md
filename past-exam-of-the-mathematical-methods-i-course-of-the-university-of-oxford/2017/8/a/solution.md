<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let the columns of the given [unitary matrix](../../../../unitary-matrix.md) be $u_j$, with $Mu_j=\lambda_ju_j$. Columnwise, $MU=UD$ for $D=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$. Multiplication by $U^*$ and [unitarity](../../../../unitary-operator.md) give

$$
\boxed{U^*MU=D.}
$$

The premise includes an [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). It is not true that an arbitrary matrix admits such a basis; over $\mathbb C$ it exists precisely for a [normal matrix](../../../../normal-matrix.md). The real symmetric inertia matrix in part (b) does satisfy the required condition.
