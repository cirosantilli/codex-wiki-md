<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $(1+i)^2=2i$, the two [square roots](../../../../square-root.md) are $1+i$ and $-1-i$. The [principal square root](../../../../principal-square-root-of-a-complex-number.md) is $1+i$, whose [real part](../../../../real-part.md) is

$$
\boxed{1.}
$$

If the notation is meant to include both square-root values, their real parts are $+1$ and $-1$. Stating the principal-branch convention removes that ambiguity.
