<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A map between [vector spaces](../../../../vector-space-split.md) over the same [field](../../../../field.md) is linear when $L(av+bw)=aL(v)+bL(w)$ for every $v,w$ and scalars $a,b$. Its [kernel](../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $\ker L=\{v\in V:L(v)=0\}$. It contains zero because $L(0)=0$. If $v,w\in\ker L$, then

$$
L(av+bw)=aL(v)+bL(w)=0.
$$

Thus it is closed under linear combinations and is a [vector subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $V$. No injectivity or equality of the dimensions of $V,W$ is needed.
