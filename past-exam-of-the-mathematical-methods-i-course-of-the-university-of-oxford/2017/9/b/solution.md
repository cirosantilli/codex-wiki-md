<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write both [cross products](../../../../cross-product.md) with the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md). Its contraction identity gives

$$
[a\times(b\times c)]_i=\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{klm}a_jb_lc_m
=(\delta_{il}\delta_{jm}-\delta_{im}\delta_{jl})a_jb_lc_m
=b_i(a\cdot c)-c_i(a\cdot b).
$$

Therefore the [vector triple product](../../../../vector-triple-product.md) identity is

$$
\boxed{a\times(b\times c)=(a\cdot c)b-(a\cdot b)c.}
$$

The order matters: the [cross product](../../../../cross-product.md) is not associative, and exchanging the two nested arrangements changes the expression.
