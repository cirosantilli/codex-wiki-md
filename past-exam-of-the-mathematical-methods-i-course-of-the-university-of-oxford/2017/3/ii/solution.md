<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [unitary matrix](../../../../unitary-matrix.md) preserves the [Hermitian inner product](../../../../hermitian-inner-product.md) and hence the [norm](../../../../norm.md). If $Uv=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$, then

$$
\|v\|^2=\|Uv\|^2=|\lambda|^2\|v\|^2.
$$

Dividing by the positive squared norm gives

$$
\boxed{|\lambda|=1.}
$$

Unlike the Hermitian case, this constrains [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) to the [unit circle](../../../../complex-unit-circle.md), not necessarily the real axis.
