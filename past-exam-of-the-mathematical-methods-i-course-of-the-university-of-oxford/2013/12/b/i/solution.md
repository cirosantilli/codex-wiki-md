<h1 id="12/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On an interval with $x\ne-1$, an [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $(x+1)^{-2}$. Multiplication gives

$$
\frac d{dx}\left(\frac{y}{(x+1)^2}\right)=x+1.
$$

Integration therefore yields

$$
\boxed{y=\frac12(x+1)^4+C(x+1)^2.}
$$

The polynomial formula has a smooth continuation at $x=-1$, but the original coefficient $2/(x+1)$ is undefined there. Solution intervals for the stated equation must avoid that point, and the constants on its two sides need not agree.
