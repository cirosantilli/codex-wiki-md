<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an ordinary real [quadratic form](../../../../quadratic-form-split.md), split the matrix into its [symmetric part](../../../../symmetric-part-of-a-matrix.md) and [skew-symmetric part](../../../../skew-symmetric-part-of-a-matrix.md):

$$
A=M+K,\qquad M=\frac{A+A^T}{2},\quad K=\frac{A-A^T}{2}.
$$

The scalar $\mathbf x^TK\mathbf x$ equals its transpose $\mathbf x^TK^T\mathbf x=-\mathbf x^TK\mathbf x$, so it is zero. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x^TA\mathbf x=\mathbf x^TM\mathbf x,\qquad
M=\frac{A+A^T}{2}=M^T.}
$$

This uses transpose because the source form is bilinear. A complex Hermitian form $\mathbf x^\dagger A\mathbf x$ would instead require the conjugate-transpose decomposition.
