<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The cyclic products obtained by direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) are $\sigma_x\sigma_y=i\sigma_z$, $\sigma_y\sigma_z=i\sigma_x$, and $\sigma_z\sigma_x=i\sigma_y$. Reversing any two distinct indices changes the sign; equal-index products are $I$. Therefore every pair is covered by

$$
\boxed{\{\sigma_i,\sigma_j\}=2\delta_{ij}I,\qquad
[\sigma_i,\sigma_j]=2i\sum_k\epsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k.}
$$

Here $\{A,B\}$ is the [anticommutator](../../../../anticommutator.md) and $[A,B]$ the [commutator](../../../../commutator.md). Explicitly the three diagonal [anticommutators](../../../../anticommutator.md) are $2I$, all six off-diagonal ones are zero, and all three diagonal [commutators](../../../../commutator.md) vanish. The cyclic [commutators](../../../../commutator.md) are $2i\sigma_z,2i\sigma_x,2i\sigma_y$, respectively, with their reversed partners negative. The convention is $\epsilon_{xyz}=1$.
