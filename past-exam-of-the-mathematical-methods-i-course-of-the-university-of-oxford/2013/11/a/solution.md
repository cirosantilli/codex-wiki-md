<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Each [Pauli matrix](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) has zero diagonal sum and [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $-1$. Direct multiplication gives $\sigma_x^2=\sigma_y^2=\sigma_z^2=I$. Thus all requested quantities, for each $j=x,y,z$, are

$$
\boxed{\det\sigma_j=-1,\qquad \operatorname{tr}\sigma_j=0,\qquad
\sigma_j^2=I,\qquad\sigma_j^{-1}=\sigma_j.}
$$

For example, $\sigma_y^2=\operatorname{diag}((-i)i,i(-i))=I$, checking that its imaginary entries do not introduce a minus sign in the square.
