<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For any value of the [complex logarithm](../../../../complex-logarithm.md), $\log z=\ln|z|+i\arg z$ up to adding an integer multiple of $2\pi i$. Its [real part](../../../../real-part.md) therefore satisfies

$$
\operatorname{Re}\log z=2\iff\ln|z|=2\iff\boxed{|z|=e^2.}
$$

The [locus](../../../../locus-mathematics.md) is **the circle centred at the origin with radius $e^2$**. Different logarithm values change only the imaginary part. If a particular analytic [branch of the complex logarithm](../../../../branch-of-the-complex-logarithm.md) with a cut is imposed, restrict the circle to that branch's domain; the modulus equation itself is branch independent.
