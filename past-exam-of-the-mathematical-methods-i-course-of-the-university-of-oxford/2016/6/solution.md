<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Use direction $\mathbf u=(-3,-2,-1)$ and displacement $\mathbf w=(1,2,3)-(6,5,4)=(-5,-3,-1)$. The [point-to-line distance](../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-line.md) is the height of the [parallelogram](../../../parallelogram.md) spanned by these vectors:

$$
\boxed{D=\frac{|\mathbf w\times\mathbf u|}{|\mathbf u|}=\frac{|(1,-2,1)|}{\sqrt{14}}=\frac{\sqrt{21}}7.}
$$

Equivalently the closest line parameter is $t=(\mathbf w\cdot\mathbf u)/|\mathbf u|^2=11/7$, giving the foot $(9/7,13/7,17/7)$. Its separation from the point is [orthogonal](../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $\mathbf u$ and has the same length.
