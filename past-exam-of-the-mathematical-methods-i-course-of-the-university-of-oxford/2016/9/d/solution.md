<h1 id="9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the [Cauchy-Euler equation](../../../../cauchy-euler-equation.md) $x^2y''-xy'+y=0$, the power trial $y=x^r$ on $x>0$ gives $(r-1)^2=0$. The repeated root supplies the logarithmic second solution. On either half-line,

$$
\boxed{y=x\bigl(C_1+C_2\ln|x|\bigr),\qquad x\ne0.}
$$

One may also obtain this by setting $y=xu$, which reduces the equation to $(xu')'=0$. Constants on opposite sides of the singular point need not agree.
