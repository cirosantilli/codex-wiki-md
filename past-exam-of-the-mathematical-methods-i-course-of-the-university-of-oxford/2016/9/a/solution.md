<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $x\ne0$, the [integrating factor](../../../../integrating-factor.md) for $y'-y/x=3$ can be taken as $1/x$ on either half-line. Thus $(y/x)'=3/x$, giving

$$
\boxed{y=x\bigl(C+3\ln|x|\bigr).}
$$

The arbitrary constant may differ on the disconnected domains $x>0$ and $x<0$.
