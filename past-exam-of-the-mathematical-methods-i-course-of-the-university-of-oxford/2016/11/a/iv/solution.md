<h1 id="11/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If $A$ and $B$ are [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), then $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger=BA$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{AB\text{ is Hermitian}\iff AB=BA\iff[A,B]=0.}
$$

The required condition is that the matrices [commute](../../../../../commuting-elements.md).
