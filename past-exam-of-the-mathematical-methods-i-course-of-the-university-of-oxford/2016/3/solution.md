<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The scalar product $\mathbf r\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}=d$ fixes the component of the [position vector](../../../position-vector.md) along the given [unit normal vector](../../../unit-normal.md). Thus the [locus](../../../locus-mathematics.md) is **a plane normal to $\hat{\mathbf n}$**, through $d\hat{\mathbf n}$. Its signed distance from the origin along that normal is $d$, and its perpendicular [distance](../../../distance.md) from the origin is $|d|$. Any [displacement](../../../displacement.md) lying within the plane is [orthogonal](../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $\hat{\mathbf n}$.
