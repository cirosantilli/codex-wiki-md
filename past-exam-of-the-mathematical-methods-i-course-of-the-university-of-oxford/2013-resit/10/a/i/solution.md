<h1 id="10/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use active [rotations](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) on column vectors and positive angles counter-clockwise when viewed from the positive axis toward the origin. Rotation about $z$ takes $\mathbf e_x$ to $(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha,0)$, $\mathbf e_y$ to $(-\sin\alpha,\cos\alpha,0)$, and leaves $\mathbf e_z$ fixed. These images are the columns of the [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md):

$$
\boxed{R_z(\alpha)=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\alpha&-\sin\alpha&0\\
\sin\alpha&\cos\alpha&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

It satisfies $R_z^TR_z=I$ and $\det R_z=1$. Stating the viewing convention avoids confusing this active [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) with a passive change of axes.
