<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Put $z=x+iy$. The expression whose [complex argument](../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) is constrained becomes

$$
z^2+1=(x^2-y^2+1)+2ixy.
$$

Its [principal argument](../../../principal-argument.md) is $\pi/2$ exactly when its [real part](../../../real-part.md) is zero and its [imaginary part](../../../imaginary-part.md) is **strictly positive**. Hence

$$
\boxed{y^2-x^2=1,\qquad xy>0.}
$$

The [locus](../../../locus-mathematics.md) consists of the upper-right half of the upper [hyperbola](../../../hyperbola.md) branch, $y=\sqrt{x^2+1}$ with $x>0$, and the lower-left half of the lower branch, $y=-\sqrt{x^2+1}$ with $x<0$. The vertices $z=\pm i$ are excluded because $z^2+1=0$ there and zero has no [complex argument](../../../argument-complex-analysis.md). The remaining halves have negative [imaginary part](../../../imaginary-part.md) and give argument $-\pi/2$, not $\pi/2$. The right panel of the original illustration marks the excluded vertices with open circles.
