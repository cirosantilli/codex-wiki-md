<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Dot the reciprocal-basis expansion with each member of the original [basis](../../../../basis.md). All mismatched dot products vanish and the matching one equals one. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf a,\qquad
\beta=\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf b,\qquad
\gamma=\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf c.}
$$

These are coefficients in the [reciprocal basis](../../../../reciprocal-basis.md). They would instead involve dot products with the reciprocal vectors if $\mathbf r$ were being expanded in the original [basis](../../../../basis.md). The two coefficient conventions should not be interchanged for a nonorthonormal [basis](../../../../basis.md).
