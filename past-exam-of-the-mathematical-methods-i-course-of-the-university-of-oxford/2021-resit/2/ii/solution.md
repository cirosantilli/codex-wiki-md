<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [polar form](../../../../polar-form-of-a-complex-number.md) is $1+i\sqrt3=2e^{i\pi/3}$. By [De Moivre's theorem](../../../../de-moivre-s-theorem.md),

$$
(1+i\sqrt3)^3=8e^{i\pi}=-8.
$$

Therefore the [real part](../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../imaginary-part.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}=-8,\qquad\operatorname{Im}=0.}
$$
