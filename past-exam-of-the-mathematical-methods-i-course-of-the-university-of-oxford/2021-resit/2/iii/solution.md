<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Using [Euler's formula](../../../../euler-s-formula.md), the difference of the two [complex exponentials](../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) is $2i\sin(\pi/4)=i\sqrt2$. Hence

$$
(i\sqrt2)^4=4i^4=4,
\qquad\boxed{\operatorname{Re}=4,\qquad\operatorname{Im}=0.}
$$

These are the [real part](../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../imaginary-part.md), respectively.
