<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply numerator and denominator by the denominator's [complex conjugate](../../../../complex-conjugate.md):

$$
\frac{3+2i}{2-3i}=\frac{(3+2i)(2+3i)}{2^2+3^2}
=\frac{6+13i-6}{13}=i.
$$

Thus the [real part](../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../imaginary-part.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}=0,\qquad\operatorname{Im}=1.}
$$
