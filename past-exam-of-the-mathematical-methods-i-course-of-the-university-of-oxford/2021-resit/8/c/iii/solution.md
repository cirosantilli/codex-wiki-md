Yes. The old [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is triangular, with [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) zero and [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) $0+0+1+1=2$. The upper-left two-by-two block of the new [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) has [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) zero, so the whole [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) remains zero. Its [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is

$$
-\frac12+\frac12+\frac32+\frac12=2.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\det[F]_{\mathcal B}=\det[F]_{\mathcal C}=0,\qquad
\operatorname{tr}[F]_{\mathcal B}=\operatorname{tr}[F]_{\mathcal C}=2.}
$$

This verifies both [change of basis](../../../../../change-of-basis.md) invariants from part (b).
