<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [normal vectors](../../../../normal-vector.md) are $\mathbf n_A=(1,2,-4)$ and $\mathbf n_B=(2,-1,3)$. Their [dot product](../../../../dot-product.md) is $-12$, and their [norms](../../../../norm.md) are $\sqrt{21}$ and $\sqrt{14}$. The usual [angle between two planes](../../../../angle-between-two-planes.md) is the smaller, unoriented angle, so

$$
\boxed{\theta=\arccos\frac{12}{\sqrt{21}\sqrt{14}}
=\arccos\frac{2\sqrt6}{7}\approx45.6^\circ.}
$$

For the particular signed [normal vectors](../../../../normal-vector.md) printed above, the angle is the supplement $\pi-\theta\approx134.4^\circ$. Reversing either [normal vector](../../../../normal-vector.md) exchanges these two angles without changing either [plane](../../../../plane.md).
