<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A parallel [plane](../../../../plane.md) has the same [normal vector](../../../../normal-vector.md), so write $C:x+2y-4z=d$. Substitution of the given point gives $d=4+4-4=4$. Thus

$$
\boxed{C:x+2y-4z=4.}
$$

A displacement between the two [planes](../../../../plane.md) has a component of magnitude $|5-4|/\|\mathbf n_A\|$ along their common [unit vector](../../../../unit-vector.md) normal. The [distance between parallel planes](../../../../distance-between-parallel-planes.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{dist}(A,C)=\frac1{\sqrt{21}}.}
$$
