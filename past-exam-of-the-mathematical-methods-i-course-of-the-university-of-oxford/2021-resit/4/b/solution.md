<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiplying a three-dimensional [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) by a real $\lambda$ multiplies its [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) by $\lambda^3$, whereas multiplicativity of the [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) gives $\det(A^5)=(\det A)^5$. Therefore equality of the two [parallelepiped](../../../../parallelepiped.md) volumes means

$$
23|\lambda|^3=23^5,\qquad |\lambda|^3=23^4.
$$

Both real signs are allowed:

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\pm23^{4/3}.}
$$

A negative value reverses the [oriented volume](../../../../oriented-volume.md) while preserving the ordinary volume. If a positive scale factor is intended, select the positive sign.
