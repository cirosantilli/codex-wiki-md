<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [parallelepiped](../../../../parallelepiped.md) volume is the absolute value of the [scalar triple product](../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) of its edge [vectors](../../../../vector.md), equivalently the absolute [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) of the [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) with those columns. Expansion along the first row gives

$$
\det A=1(1-2)-2(-2-3)+2(4+3)=23.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{V=|\det A|=23.}
$$
