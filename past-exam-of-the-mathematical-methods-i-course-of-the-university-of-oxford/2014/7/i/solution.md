↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The finite-dimensional [spectral theorem](../../../../spectral-theorem.md) diagonalizes the [Hermitian matrix](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) in its [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). The [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is the product of the [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md):

$$
\boxed{\det H=\lambda_1\lambda_2.}
$$
