<h1 id="7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Every [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) of a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) is real: if $He=\lambda e$ with $e^\dagger e=1$, then $\lambda=e^\dagger He=\overline{e^\dagger He}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}(\lambda_1\lambda_2)=0.}
$$
