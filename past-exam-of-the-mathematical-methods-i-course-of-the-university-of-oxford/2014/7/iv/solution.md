<h1 id="7/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) of a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) have [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md), since $\lambda_2e_1^\dagger e_2=e_1^\dagger He_2=\lambda_1e_1^\dagger e_2$. The given normalization then gives

$$
\boxed{e_1^\dagger(e_1+e_2)=1+0=1.}
$$
