<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The elementary [row operation](../../../elementary-row-operation.md) $R_2\leftarrow R_2+3R_1$ gives

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&4&2\\0&a+12&0\\0&0&b\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The nonzero rows here have distinct [pivots](../../../pivotal-quantity.md). Therefore the [matrix rank](../../../matrix-rank.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}A=\begin{cases}
3,&a\ne-12,\ b\ne0,\\
2,&\text{exactly one of }a=-12,\ b=0\text{ holds},\\
1,&a=-12,\ b=0.
\end{cases}}
$$

There is always at least one nonzero row, so rank zero is impossible.
