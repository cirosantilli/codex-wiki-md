↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Here $M^2=-I$, so its even powers alternate between $\pm I$ and its odd powers between $\pm M$. Collecting even and odd terms of the [matrix exponential](../../../matrix-exponential.md) yields

$$
R(\phi)=I\cos\phi+M\sin\phi
=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}\cos\phi&-\sin\phi\\\sin\phi&\cos\phi\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Thus $R^TR=I$, $\det R=1$, and $R(\phi)(1,0)^T=(\cos\phi,\sin\phi)^T$. It is the [rotation matrix](../../../rotation-matrix.md) for a counterclockwise [rotation](../../../rotation-mathematics.md) through angle $\phi$, preserving [lengths](../../../length.md) and [orientation](../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
