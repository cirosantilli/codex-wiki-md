<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Let $\omega=e^{i\pi/7}$, a primitive fourteenth [root of unity](../../../root-of-unity.md). The [geometric series](../../../geometric-series.md) gives $\sum_{k=1}^{14}\omega^k=0$. Taking the [real part](../../../real-part.md) and adding the remaining fifteenth term therefore yields

$$
\boxed{S=\cos\frac{15\pi}{7}=\cos\frac\pi7.}
$$

The upper summation limit matters: the first fourteen terms complete one full cycle, and the fifteenth supplies one additional term.
