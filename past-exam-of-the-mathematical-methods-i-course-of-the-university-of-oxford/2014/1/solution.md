<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Multiply numerator and denominator by the denominator's [complex conjugate](../../../complex-conjugate.md):

$$
\frac{3+2i}{5-4i}=\frac{(3+2i)(5+4i)}{25+16}=\frac{7+22i}{41}.
$$

Thus the [real part](../../../real-part.md) is $\boxed{7/41}$ and the [imaginary part](../../../imaginary-part.md) is $\boxed{22/41}$.
