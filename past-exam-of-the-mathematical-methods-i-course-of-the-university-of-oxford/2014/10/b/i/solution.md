<h1 id="10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $r=0$, the [norm](../../../../../norm.md) equation requires $x=c$. Thus the [locus](../../../../../locus-mathematics.md) is

$$
\boxed{\{c\}\text{ if }a\cdot(c-b)=0;\qquad\varnothing\text{ otherwise}.}
$$

This includes $a=0$, when the first condition imposes no restriction.
