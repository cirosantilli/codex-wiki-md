<h1 id="10/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) of the origin onto the [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) occurs at $t_*=-p\cdot u/|u|^2=-1/5$, giving closest point $(2,4/5,-2/5)$. Hence the [distance from a point to a line in three dimensions](../../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-line-in-three-dimensions.md) is

$$
\boxed{d=\sqrt{4+\frac{16+4}{25}}=\sqrt{\frac{24}{5}}=\frac{2\sqrt{30}}5.}
$$
