<h1 id="10/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Eliminating $2y-z$ from the two [plane](../../../../../plane.md) equations gives $x=2$, followed by $2y-z=2$. Taking $y=1$ gives the point $\boxed{p=(2,1,0)}$. The entire intersection [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) is $\mathbf r=p+tu$, where $u=(0,1,2)$.
