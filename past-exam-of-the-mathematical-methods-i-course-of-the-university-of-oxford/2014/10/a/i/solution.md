<h1 id="10/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A direction of the intersection [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) must be [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to both [normal vectors](../../../../../normal-vector.md). Their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is

$$
(2,6,-3)\times(5,2,-1)=(0,-13,-26).
$$

Thus a convenient parallel [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is $\boxed{u=(0,1,2)}$.
