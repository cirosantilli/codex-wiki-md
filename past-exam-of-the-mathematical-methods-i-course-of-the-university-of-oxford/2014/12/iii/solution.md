<h1 id="12/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) gives $\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)$, which is C1. The [linearity](../../../../linearity.md) of [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) and [matrix multiplication](../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives C2 and C3. Finally symmetry of $A$ gives

$$
\langle A,A\rangle=\operatorname{tr}(A^2)=\sum_{i,j}A_{ij}A_{ji}=\sum_{i,j}A_{ij}^2\ge0,
$$

with equality exactly when every entry is zero. Thus C4 also holds. This is the [Frobenius inner product](../../../../frobenius-inner-product.md) restricted to the real [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) of [symmetric matrices](../../../../symmetric-matrix.md).
