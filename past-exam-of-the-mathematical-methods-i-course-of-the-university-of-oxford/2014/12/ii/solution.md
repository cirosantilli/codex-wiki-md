<h1 id="12/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An explicit [basis](../../../../basis.md) is

$$
\boxed{E_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E_3=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Indeed every real [symmetric matrix](../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$ has the unique [linear combination](../../../../linear-combination.md) $aE_1+cE_2+bE_3$.
