<h1 id="12/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Taking the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) of the proposed [commutator](../../../../commutator.md) identity would give

$$
0=\operatorname{tr}(AB)-\operatorname{tr}(BA)=\operatorname{tr}I=n,
$$

a contradiction for positive integer $n$ over the real numbers. **No such finite real matrices exist.** This is the [trace of a commutator](../../../../trace-of-a-commutator.md) obstruction; it also applies to complex finite matrices.
