<h1 id="11/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The remaining [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) is $\boxed{\lambda_3=0}$. At a [steady state](../../../../steady-state.md), the three cyclic fluxes agree: $\epsilon A=\eta B=\delta C$. A corresponding nonzero [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{v_0=\begin{pmatrix}1/\epsilon\\1/\eta\\1/\delta\end{pmatrix},}
$$

or its scalar multiple $(\delta\eta,\epsilon\delta,\epsilon\eta)^T$. Its positive components express a possible equilibrium concentration ratio.
