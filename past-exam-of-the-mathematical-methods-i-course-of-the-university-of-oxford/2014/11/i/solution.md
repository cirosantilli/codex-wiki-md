<h1 id="11/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Reading the coefficients in the ordered concentration [vector](../../../../vector.md) gives the [linear system of ordinary differential equations](../../../../linear-system-of-differential-equations.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot x=Mx,\qquad M=\begin{pmatrix}-\epsilon&0&\delta\\\epsilon&-\eta&0\\0&\eta&-\delta\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its columns sum to zero, so $d(A+B+C)/dt=0$. This [three-state cyclic conversion model](../../../../three-state-cyclic-conversion-model.md) conserves total concentration.
