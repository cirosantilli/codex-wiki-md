<h1 id="11/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $p=\epsilon+\delta+\eta$ and $q=\epsilon\delta+\delta\eta+\eta\epsilon$. Direct expansion gives the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md)

$$
\det(\lambda I-M)=(\lambda+\epsilon)(\lambda+\eta)(\lambda+\delta)-\epsilon\eta\delta
=\lambda(\lambda^2+p\lambda+q).
$$

The quadratic formula therefore gives

$$
\boxed{K=-\frac{\epsilon+\delta+\eta}{2},\qquad W=\epsilon\delta+\delta\eta+\eta\epsilon,\qquad\lambda_\pm=K\pm\sqrt{K^2-W}.}
$$

Since $p,q>0$, both nonzero [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) have negative [real part](../../../../real-part.md). They need not be real; equal rates give a complex-conjugate pair.
