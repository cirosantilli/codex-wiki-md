<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [determinant](../../../determinant.md) is $a^2-i(-i)=a^2-1$. The [adjugate matrix](../../../adjugate-matrix.md) formula gives

$$
\boxed{A^{-1}=\frac1{a^2-1}\begin{pmatrix}a&-i\\i&a\end{pmatrix},\qquad a\ne\pm1.}
$$

When $a=1$ or $a=-1$ we have a [singular matrix](../../../singular-matrix.md), so no [inverse matrix](../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) exists. This criterion holds for real or complex $a$.
