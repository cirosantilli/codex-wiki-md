<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Rearrange as $x''-4x'+3x=10t$. The [characteristic polynomial](../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) factors as $(r-1)(r-3)$, giving the [homogeneous solutions](../../../homogeneous-solution.md) $e^t,e^{3t}$. A particular [polynomial](../../../polynomial-split.md) solution $At+B$ obeys $3A=10$ and $3B-4A=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{x(t)=C_1e^t+C_2e^{3t}+\frac{10}{3}t+\frac{40}{9}.}
$$

This is the full two-constant solution of the [second-order linear differential equation](../../../second-order-linear-differential-equation.md).
