<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [substitution](../../../../substitution-algebra.md) $u=x+y$ turns the equation into $u'=1+u^2$. Separating and integrating gives $\arctan u=x+C$, hence

$$
\boxed{y=\tan(x+C)-x.}
$$

Each real solution is defined on an [interval](../../../../interval-mathematics.md) containing no pole of the [tangent](../../../../tangent.md). There is no real constant-$u$ solution discarded by division, since $1+u^2>0$.
