<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [first-order linear differential equation](../../../../first-order-linear-differential-equation.md) has [integrating factor](../../../../integrating-factor.md) $e^{\sin x}$. Multiplying through gives

$$
(ye^{\sin x})'=2\sin x\cos x\,e^{\sin x}.
$$

With $u=\sin x$, the right side integrates to $2e^u(u-1)$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{y=2\sin x-2+Ce^{-\sin x}.}
$$

All coefficients are smooth, so these real solutions extend over the whole real axis.
