<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

**One scalar initial condition is needed in each case**, because each equation is first order and each regular solution has one integration constant. The usual [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](../../../picard-lindelof-theorem.md) applies locally at a regular initial point. For part (c), this requires $x_0\ne0$ and $y_0\ne0$; a condition at an excluded singular endpoint cannot specify a classical solution there. Arbitrary distant boundary data also need not be compatible with one common [interval](../../../interval-mathematics.md) of existence.
