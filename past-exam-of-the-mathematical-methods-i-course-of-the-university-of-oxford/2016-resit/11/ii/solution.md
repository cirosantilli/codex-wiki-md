<h1 id="11/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The weak-damping [mechanical energy](../../../../mechanical-energy.md) is proportional to the squared oscillation amplitude, so it decays as $e^{-\gamma t}$. Over one period $P=2\pi/\omega_d$, the fractional loss is $1-e^{-\gamma P}\simeq\gamma P$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{E}{\Delta E}\simeq\frac1{\gamma P}
\simeq\frac{\omega_0}{2\pi\gamma}.}
$$

For the release-from-rest solution, comparison at successive corresponding turning points gives the more precise ratio $E/\Delta E=(1-e^{-\gamma P})^{-1}$. The [quality factor](../../../../q-factor.md) is $Q\simeq2\pi E/\Delta E=\omega_0/\gamma$; the requested energy-to-loss ratio itself has the extra factor $1/(2\pi)$.
