<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume $k>0$, $m>0$ and $\gamma\ge0$. The [spring constant](../../../../spring-constant.md) $k$ measures restoring [force](../../../../force-split.md) per displacement from the equilibrium position $x_0$. The term $-\gamma m v$ is [linear drag](../../../../linear-drag.md), with damping rate $\gamma$ in inverse-time units. Writing $\omega_0=\sqrt{k/m}$, the equation is

$$
\ddot x+\gamma\dot x+\omega_0^2(x-x_0)=0.
$$

For small positive damping it gives [underdamped](../../../../underdamping.md) motion: oscillations about $x_0$ with a decaying amplitude. If $\gamma=0$, the motion is undamped [simple harmonic motion](../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md).
