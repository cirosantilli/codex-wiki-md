<h1 id="9/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Both expressions are the same scalar:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf u^{\mathsf T}\mathbf v=\sum_i u_iv_i
=\sum_i v_iu_i=\mathbf v^{\mathsf T}\mathbf u.}
$$

This identity uses the ordinary [transpose](../../../../transpose.md) and remains true for complex entries. A [Hermitian inner product](../../../../hermitian-inner-product.md) uses a conjugate transpose instead and has conjugate symmetry, not this bilinear symmetry.
