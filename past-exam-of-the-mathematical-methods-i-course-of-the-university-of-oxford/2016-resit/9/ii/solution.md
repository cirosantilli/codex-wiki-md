<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The $y$ coordinate is an independent eigen-direction, while the $xz$ block is $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$. Thus

$$
\det(A-\lambda I)=(1-\lambda)^2(3-\lambda),
$$

which gives $\det(A-I)=0$. The [eigenspaces](../../../../eigenspace.md) are

$$
\boxed{\lambda=1:\ \operatorname{span}\{(1,0,-1),(0,1,0)\};\qquad
\lambda=3:\ \operatorname{span}\{(1,0,1)\}.}
$$

Divide the two diagonal $xz$ vectors by $\sqrt2$ to obtain an [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). The eigenvalue one has multiplicity two; giving only the $y$ axis would omit an independent eigenvector.
