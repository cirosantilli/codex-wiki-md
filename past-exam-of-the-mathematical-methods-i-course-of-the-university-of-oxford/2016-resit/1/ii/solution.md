<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $z=x+iy$ and $z^*=x-iy$. The first two squared terms are [complex conjugates](../../../../complex-conjugate.md), so their sum is $2[(x-2)^2-(y+1)^2]$. Also $zz^*=x^2+y^2$. The equation therefore reduces to

$$
2[(x-2)^2-(y+1)^2]+2(x^2+y^2)+6
=4(x^2-2x-y+3)=0.
$$

Thus the [locus](../../../../locus-mathematics.md) is the upward-opening [parabola](../../../../parabola.md)

$$
\boxed{y=(x-1)^2+2,}
$$

with vertex $1+2i$ and axis $x=1$ in the [Argand diagram](../../../../complex-plane.md).
