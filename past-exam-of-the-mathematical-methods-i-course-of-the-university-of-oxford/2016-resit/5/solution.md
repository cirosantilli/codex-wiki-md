<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Rearrange as $y'+(x-1)y=x-1$ and put $w=y-1$. The resulting [linear differential equation](../../../linear-differential-equation.md) is $w'+(x-1)w=0$, so

$$
\boxed{y=1+C\exp\left[-\frac12(x-1)^2\right].}
$$

The initial condition gives $C=1$. Its graph is a [Gaussian function](../../../gaussian-function.md) above the horizontal asymptote $y=1$, symmetric about $x=1$, with its maximum $y=2$ there. It rises for $x<1$ and falls for $x>1$, since $y'=-(x-1)e^{-(x-1)^2/2}$.
