<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Expanding the [determinant](../../../determinant.md) along its first row gives

$$
\det A=a(a^2-bc)-b(ac-b^2)+c(c^2-ab)
=a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc.
$$

The required factorization is

$$
\boxed{(a+b+c)(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca).}
$$

Each factor is a [symmetric polynomial](../../../symmetric-polynomial.md). The second is $\tfrac12[(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2]$, making its permutation symmetry especially transparent. This factorization holds over both real and complex coefficients.
