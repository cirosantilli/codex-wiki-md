<h1 id="13/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

With $z=(D-\beta)y$ and $\gamma=\alpha$, the first equation is $z'-\alpha z=ke^{\alpha x}$. Multiplication by its [integrating factor](../../../../integrating-factor.md) gives $(e^{-\alpha x}z)'=k$, hence

$$
\boxed{z=e^{\alpha x}(C+kx).}
$$

This holds whether or not $\beta=\alpha$. If $\beta\ne\alpha$, solving $y'-\beta y=z$ by a second [integrating factor](../../../../integrating-factor.md) gives

$$
y=C_1e^{\alpha x}+C_2e^{\beta x}+\frac{kx e^{\alpha x}}{\alpha-\beta}.
$$

The extra factor $x$ is the response to the [resonance condition](../../../../resonance-condition.md) at the forcing exponent; the coincident-root case requires the next part.
