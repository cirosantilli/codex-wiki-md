<h1 id="13/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the [linear differential operator](../../../../linear-differential-operator.md) $L=(D-\alpha)(D-\beta)$, the [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) is an [eigenfunction](../../../../eigenfunction.md): $Le^{\epsilon x}=(\epsilon-\alpha)(\epsilon-\beta)e^{\epsilon x}$. Distinct $\alpha,\beta$ give the two [homogeneous solutions](../../../../homogeneous-solution.md), while the [method of undetermined coefficients](../../../../method-of-undetermined-coefficients.md) supplies a particular solution at the distinct forcing exponent $\gamma$:

$$
\boxed{y=C_1e^{\alpha x}+C_2e^{\beta x}+\frac{k e^{\gamma x}}{(\gamma-\alpha)(\gamma-\beta)}.}
$$
