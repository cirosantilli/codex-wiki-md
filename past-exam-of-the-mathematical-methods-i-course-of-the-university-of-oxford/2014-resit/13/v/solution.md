<h1 id="13/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

When all three exponents equal $\alpha$, write $y=e^{\alpha x}v$. The [exponential shift rule for differential operators](../../../../exponential-shift-rule-for-differential-operators.md) gives $(D-\alpha)y=e^{\alpha x}v'$ and $(D-\alpha)^2y=e^{\alpha x}v''$. Thus $v''=k$. Two integrations give

$$
\boxed{y=e^{\alpha x}\left(C_1+C_2x+\frac{kx^2}{2}\right).}
$$

The repeated [characteristic root](../../../../characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) supplies $e^{\alpha x}$ and $xe^{\alpha x}$ as [homogeneous solutions](../../../../homogeneous-solution.md); forcing at that same exponent needs the further $x^2$ factor.
