<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Every finite sum of the [sine](../../../../sine.md) modes is [odd](../../../../odd-function.md), and so is a limit in the [L2 norm](../../../../l2-norm.md) of such sums. In contrast, $x^2-1$ is a nonzero [even function](../../../../even-function.md). Its [inner product](../../../../inner-product-split.md) with every [odd](../../../../odd-function.md) [sine](../../../../sine.md) mode vanishes, so all its proposed [Fourier sine series](../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) coefficients would be zero. **It cannot be represented by this basis on the symmetric interval.** An [even](../../../../even-function.md) [Fourier cosine series](../../../../fourier-cosine-series.md) is the appropriate alternative there.
