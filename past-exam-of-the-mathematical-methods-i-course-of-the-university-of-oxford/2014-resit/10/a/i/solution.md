<h1 id="10/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $n\ge1$, use $\sin^2(nx)=[1-\cos(2nx)]/2$. The [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle f_n,f_n\rangle=\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\sin^2(nx)\,dx=\pi\quad(n\ge1).}
$$

The stated nonnegative index range also includes $n=0$. Since $f_0$ is the [zero function](../../../../../zero-function.md), $\langle f_0,f_0\rangle=0$, so it must be removed from any proposed [basis](../../../../../basis.md).
