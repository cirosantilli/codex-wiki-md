<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

Normalize the [normal vector](../../../normal-vector.md) $(2,4,3)$, whose [Euclidean norm](../../../euclidean-norm.md) is $\sqrt{29}$. The [plane](../../../plane.md) has the form

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf n=d,\qquad\mathbf n=\frac{(2,4,3)}{\sqrt{29}},\quad d=\frac3{\sqrt{29}}.}
$$

The scalar $d$ is the signed perpendicular [distance](../../../distance.md) from the origin in the chosen [unit normal](../../../unit-normal.md) direction; the unsigned [distance](../../../distance.md) is $|d|$. The nearest point is $d\mathbf n$.
