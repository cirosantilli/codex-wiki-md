<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [polar form](../../../../polar-form-of-a-complex-number.md) $1+i=\sqrt2e^{i\pi/4}$ gives $(1+i)^6=-8i$. Multiplication therefore scales the [modulus](../../../../modulus.md) by $8$ and rotates the [complex argument](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) through $-\pi/2$:

$$
\boxed{|(1+i)^6z|=8\sqrt{a^2+b^2},\qquad\arg((1+i)^6z)=\arg(a+ib)-\frac\pi2\pmod{2\pi}.}
$$

Equivalently the result is $8b-8ia$, whose [complex argument](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) is $\operatorname{atan2}(-a,b)$. If $a=b=0$, its [modulus](../../../../modulus.md) is zero and its [complex argument](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) is undefined.
