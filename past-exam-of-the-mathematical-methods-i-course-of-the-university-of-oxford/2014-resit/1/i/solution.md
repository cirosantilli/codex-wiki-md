↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [complex exponential](../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) factors as $e^{(3+i)(a+ib)}=e^{3a-b}e^{i(a+3b)}$. Its positive real prefactor is its [modulus](../../../../modulus.md), while its phase gives its [complex argument](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md):

$$
\boxed{|e^{(3+i)z}|=e^{3a-b},\qquad\arg(e^{(3+i)z})=a+3b\pmod{2\pi}.}
$$

For the principal [complex argument](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md), reduce $a+3b$ to the chosen principal interval.
