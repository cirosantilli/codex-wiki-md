<h1 id="11/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Conjugating the first [vector](../../../../vector.md) gives

$$
\langle a,b\rangle=ie^{-ix}+(1-i)e^{inx},\qquad
\operatorname{Re}\langle a,b\rangle=\sin x+\cos(nx)+\sin(nx).
$$

Their squared [norms](../../../../norm.md) are $3$ and $2$, independently of $x,n$. Thus the specified [real angle in a complex inner product space](../../../../real-angle-in-a-complex-inner-product-space.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{\cos\theta=\frac{\sin x+\cos(nx)+\sin(nx)}{\sqrt6}.}
$$

The complex [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) ensures that this real quotient lies in $[-1,1]$.
