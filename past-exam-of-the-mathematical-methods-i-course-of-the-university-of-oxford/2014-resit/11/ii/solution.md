<h1 id="11/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expanding the squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../euclidean-norm.md) and applying the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives

$$
|a+b|^2=|a|^2+2a\cdot b+|b|^2
\le|a|^2+2|a|\,|b|+|b|^2=(|a|+|b|)^2.
$$

Both sides are nonnegative, so taking [square roots](../../../../square-root.md) proves the [triangle inequality](../../../../triangle-inequality.md), $\boxed{|a+b|\le|a|+|b|}$. For nonzero [vectors](../../../../vector.md), equality requires a positive real proportionality factor.
