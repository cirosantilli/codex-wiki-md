<h1 id="11/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Using the [Hermitian inner product](../../../../hermitian-inner-product.md), the scalar product is $1-i+2i^2=-1-i$. The two squared [norms](../../../../norm.md) are $6$ and $4$. The specified [real angle in a complex inner product space](../../../../real-angle-in-a-complex-inner-product-space.md) therefore has

$$
\boxed{\cos\theta=\frac{\operatorname{Re}(-1-i)}{\sqrt6\sqrt4}=-\frac1{2\sqrt6}.}
$$

The real part is essential: the complex scalar product itself need not be real.
