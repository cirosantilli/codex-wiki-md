<h1 id="11/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

With conjugation in its first slot, a complex [inner product](../../../../inner-product-split.md) has [conjugate symmetry](../../../../conjugate-symmetry.md), [linearity](../../../../linearity.md) in its second slot, and [positive definiteness](../../../../positive-definiteness.md). For the proposed [Hermitian inner product](../../../../hermitian-inner-product.md), direct expansion gives

$$
\langle z,y\rangle=\overline{\langle y,z\rangle},\qquad
\langle z,\alpha y+\beta w\rangle=\alpha\langle z,y\rangle+\beta\langle z,w\rangle,\qquad
\langle z,z\rangle=\sum_j|z_j|^2\ge0.
$$

The last expression vanishes exactly for the [zero vector](../../../../zero-vector.md). [Conjugate symmetry](../../../../conjugate-symmetry.md) and [linearity](../../../../linearity.md) also give [antilinearity](../../../../antilinear-map.md) in the first slot: $\langle\alpha z+\beta w,y\rangle=\bar\alpha\langle z,y\rangle+\bar\beta\langle w,y\rangle$. These verify all three axioms and the associated first-slot consequence.
