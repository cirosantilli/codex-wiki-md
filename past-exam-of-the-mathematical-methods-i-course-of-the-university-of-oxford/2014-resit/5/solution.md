<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of the [Hermitian matrix](../../../hermitian-operator.md) is

$$
\det(A-\lambda I)=\left(\frac32-\lambda\right)^2-\frac{25}{4}=(\lambda-4)(\lambda+1).
$$

Solving the two [eigenvector](../../../eigenvector.md) equations and using the [Hermitian inner product](../../../hermitian-inner-product.md) to normalize gives

$$
\boxed{\lambda_1=4,\quad v_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}\binom{i}{1};\qquad
\lambda_2=-1,\quad v_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}\binom{-i}{1}.}
$$

Indeed $v_1^\dagger v_2=0$ and both [norms](../../../norm.md) equal one. Multiplication of either [eigenvector](../../../eigenvector.md) by any unit [phase factor](../../../phase-factor.md) gives an equally valid normalized choice.
