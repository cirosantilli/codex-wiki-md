<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is an [alternating multilinear map](../../../../alternating-multilinear-map.md) of its rows, so exchanging two rows gives **the negative of the original determinant**: $\det A\mapsto-\det A$. This also holds when the [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is zero.
