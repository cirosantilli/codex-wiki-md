**The determinant is unchanged by transposition**: $\boxed{\det(A^T)=\det A}$. In the [Leibniz formula for determinants](../../../../leibniz-formula-for-determinants.md), replacing each [permutation](../../../../permutation.md) by its inverse interchanges the row and column roles without changing its [sign of a permutation](../../../../sign-of-a-permutation.md).
