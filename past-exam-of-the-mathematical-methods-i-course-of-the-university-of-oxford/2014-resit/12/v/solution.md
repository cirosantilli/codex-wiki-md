<h1 id="12/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

If all $n$ indices are distinct and each lies in $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, their list is a [permutation](../../../../permutation.md) of $(1,\ldots,n)$. Sorting it takes some number $N$ of transpositions. The [n-dimensional Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../n-dimensional-levi-civita-symbol.md) equals $(-1)^N$ because its sorted component is $1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_{i_1\cdots i_n}=\begin{cases}
0,&\text{some indices repeat},\\
+1,&(i_1,\ldots,i_n)\text{ is an even permutation},\\
-1,&(i_1,\ldots,i_n)\text{ is an odd permutation}.
\end{cases}}
$$

The [parity of a permutation](../../../../parity-of-a-permutation.md) is independent of the chosen sequence of transpositions.
