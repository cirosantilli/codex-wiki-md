<h1 id="12/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Three [vectors](../../../../vector.md) form a [basis](../../../../basis.md) of $\mathbb R^3$ exactly when they are [linearly independent](../../../../linear-independence.md). The [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) with those [vectors](../../../../vector.md) as columns is their [scalar triple product](../../../../scalar-triple-product.md), so the condition is

$$
\boxed{A\cdot(B\times C)\ne0.}
$$

Geometrically their [parallelepiped](../../../../parallelepiped.md) must have nonzero [volume](../../../../volume.md).
