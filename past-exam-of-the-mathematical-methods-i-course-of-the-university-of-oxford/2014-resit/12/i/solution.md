<h1 id="12/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In components the [scalar triple product](../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) is $A_i\epsilon_{ijk}B_jC_k$. A cyclic [permutation](../../../../permutation.md) has positive [sign of a permutation](../../../../sign-of-a-permutation.md), so $\epsilon_{ijk}=\epsilon_{kij}$. Relabelling the summed indices therefore gives

$$
A_i\epsilon_{ijk}B_jC_k=\epsilon_{kij}A_iB_jC_k=(A\times B)_kC_k,
$$

which proves $A\cdot(B\times C)=(A\times B)\cdot C$. Equivalently both expressions are the same three-column [determinant](../../../../determinant.md).
