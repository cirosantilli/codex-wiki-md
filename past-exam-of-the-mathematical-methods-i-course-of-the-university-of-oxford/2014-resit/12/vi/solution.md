<h1 id="12/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Reversal has one [inversion of a permutation](../../../../inversion-of-a-permutation.md) for every pair of indices, hence $\binom n2=n(n-1)/2$ [inversions of a permutation](../../../../inversion-of-a-permutation.md). The [sign of a permutation](../../../../sign-of-a-permutation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_{n,n-1,\ldots,1}=(-1)^{n(n-1)/2}.}
$$

It is $+1$ for $n\equiv0,1\pmod4$ and $-1$ for $n\equiv2,3\pmod4$.
