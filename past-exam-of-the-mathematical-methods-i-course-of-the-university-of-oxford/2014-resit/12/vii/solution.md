<h1 id="12/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

Every nonzero component of the [n-dimensional Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../n-dimensional-levi-civita-symbol.md) is $\pm1$, so its square is one. There are exactly $n!$ [permutations](../../../../permutation.md) of the $n$ distinct indices; all remaining index lists have a repeat and contribute zero. Hence

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i_1,\ldots,i_n=1}^n\epsilon_{i_1\cdots i_n}^{\,2}=n!.}
$$
