<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

The common real [variable](../../../variable-mathematics.md) gives the [parametric equation](../../../parametric-equation.md) of the [line](../../../straight-line.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r=(2,-1,4)+t(1,3,2),\qquad t\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Write $p=(2,-1,4)$ and $u=(1,3,2)$. The point closest to the origin is [orthogonal](../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $u$, so $t_*=-p\cdot u/|u|^2=-7/14=-1/2$. Its position is $(3/2,-5/2,3)$. The [distance from a point to a line in three dimensions](../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-line-in-three-dimensions.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{d=\sqrt{|p|^2-\frac{(p\cdot u)^2}{|u|^2}}=\sqrt{\frac{35}{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{70}}2.}
$$
