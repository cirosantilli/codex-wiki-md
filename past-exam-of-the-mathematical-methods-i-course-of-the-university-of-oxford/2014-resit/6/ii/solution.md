<h1 id="6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $Av=\lambda v$ for a nonzero [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md), [idempotence](../../../../idempotence.md) gives $\lambda^2v=A^2v=Av=\lambda v$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\lambda\in\{0,1\}.}
$$

These are the only possible [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md); either may be absent. In fact $v=Av+(I-A)v$ decomposes every [vector](../../../../vector.md) into the [image](../../../../image-of-a-function.md) and [kernel](../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of $A$, so every [idempotent matrix](../../../../idempotent-matrix.md) is [diagonalizable](../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) even when it is not an [orthogonal projection](../../../../orthogonal-projection.md).
