<h1 id="6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [idempotent matrix](../../../../idempotent-matrix.md), $A^k=A$ for every integer $k\ge1$. Thus any scalar [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) reduces to

$$
\boxed{p(A)=p(0)I+[p(1)-p(0)]A,}
$$

or equivalently $aI+bA$. This expression describes all [polynomials](../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $A$, although its two coefficients need not be unique if $A=0$ or $A=I$.
