<h1 id="12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The function $x^2-1$ is an [even function](../../../../even-function.md), while every sine basis function is an [odd function](../../../../odd-function.md). Their product is odd, so every sine coefficient is zero:

$$
\langle x^2-1,\sin(nx)\rangle=0.
$$

The function itself is not zero, and therefore **it cannot be represented by this sine basis**. The basis spans the odd subspace, whereas the requested nonzero even function lies in its [orthogonal complement](../../../../orthogonal-complement.md). Equivalently, any pointwise limit of odd partial sums remains odd wherever both reflected points have limits.
