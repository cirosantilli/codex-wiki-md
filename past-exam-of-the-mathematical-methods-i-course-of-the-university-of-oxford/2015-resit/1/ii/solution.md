<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [modulus](../../../../modulus.md) obeys $|z|^4=4$, giving $|z|=\sqrt2$. The [complex argument](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) obeys $4\arg z=\pi/2$ modulo $2\pi$. The four distinct [complex roots](../../../../complex-root.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{z_k=\sqrt2\exp\left[i\left(\frac\pi8+\frac{k\pi}2\right)\right],\qquad k=0,1,2,3.}
$$

They form a square centred at the origin, on the circle of radius $\sqrt2$, with the first vertex at argument $\pi/8$.
