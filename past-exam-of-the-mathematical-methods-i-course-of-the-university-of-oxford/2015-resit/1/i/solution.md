<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $z=x+iy$, with real $x,y$. Its [complex conjugate](../../../../complex-conjugate.md) is $z^*=x-iy$, so equating real and imaginary parts gives $4x=1$, $2y=1$. Thus the [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md) locus is the single point

$$
\boxed{z=\frac14+\frac i2.}
$$
