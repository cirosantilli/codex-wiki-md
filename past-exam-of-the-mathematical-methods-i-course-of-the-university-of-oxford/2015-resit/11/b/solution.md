<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A power of a [diagonal matrix](../../../../diagonal-matrix.md) acts entrywise. Thus

$$
\boxed{D^n=\operatorname{diag}((-1)^n,2^n,1).}
$$

From $A=BDB^{-1}$, adjacent $B^{-1}B$ factors cancel in repeated multiplication, giving

$$
\boxed{A^n=BD^nB^{-1}.}
$$

This is the [matrix power](../../../../matrix-power.md) rule for a [similarity transformation](../../../../similarity-transformation.md), proved directly rather than by taking powers of separate noncommuting factors.
