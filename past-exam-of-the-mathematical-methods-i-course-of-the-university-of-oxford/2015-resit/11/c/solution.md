<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

All three [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are nonzero, so $D^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1/2,1)$. The [inverse matrix](../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{A^{-1}=BD^{-1}B^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}4&3&-5\\1/2&1/2&-1/2\\3&3&-4\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Multiplying this matrix by $A$ gives $I$, independently checking both the sign and the fractions.
