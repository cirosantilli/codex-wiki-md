<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is multiplicative and unchanged by a [similarity transformation](../../../../similarity-transformation.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{\det(A^n)=\det(B)\det(D^n)\det(B^{-1})=[(-1)\,2\,1]^n=(-2)^n.}
$$

For $n=1$, expansion of the original determinant gives

$$
\det A=(-2-6)-6(1)+(-2)(-6)=-8-6+12=-2,
$$

in agreement with the product of its [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md).
