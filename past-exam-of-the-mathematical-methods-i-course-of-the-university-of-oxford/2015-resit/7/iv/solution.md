↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**Yes, over $\mathbb C$.** The column matrix is $\operatorname{diag}(1,1+i)$, with nonzero [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $1+i$. Every $(z_1,z_2)\in\mathbb C^2$ has the unique expansion

$$
(z_1,z_2)=z_1(1,0)+\frac{z_2}{1+i}(0,1+i).
$$

The scalar field matters: the same space regarded as a real [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) has dimension four.
