**Yes.** The two columns form the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$, whose [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is $-4\ne0$. They are therefore [linearly independent](../../../../linear-independence.md) and span the two-dimensional real [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md), so they form a [basis](../../../../basis.md).
