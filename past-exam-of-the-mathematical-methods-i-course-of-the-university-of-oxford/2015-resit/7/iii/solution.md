**No.** Calling the three vectors $v_1,v_2,v_3$ in order, direct addition gives $v_3=v_1+v_2$. This nontrivial [linear dependence](../../../../linear-dependence.md) prevents them from forming a [basis](../../../../basis.md) of $\mathbb R^3$. The first two are not proportional, so the span has dimension two.
