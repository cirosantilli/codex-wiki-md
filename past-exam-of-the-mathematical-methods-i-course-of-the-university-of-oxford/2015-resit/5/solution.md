<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The [scalar triple product](../../../scalar-triple-product.md) is the [determinant](../../../determinant.md) with the three vectors as columns. A cyclic permutation of three columns is even, while exchanging two columns reverses the determinant's sign. Consequently

$$
(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf c=\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)=-\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf c\times\mathbf b).
$$

Equivalently, expand the first expression as $\epsilon_{ijk}a_i b_j c_k$ and interchange the last two indices, using the antisymmetry of the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../levi-civita-symbol.md). This argument includes linearly dependent vectors, for which both sides vanish.
