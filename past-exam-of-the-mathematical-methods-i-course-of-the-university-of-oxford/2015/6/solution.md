<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $(5-\lambda)(3-\lambda)-3=(\lambda-6)(\lambda-2)$. Corresponding normalized [eigenvectors](../../../eigenvector.md) are

$$
\lambda=6:\quad e_6=\frac12(\sqrt3,1),\qquad
\lambda=2:\quad e_2=\frac12(-1,\sqrt3).
$$

These form an [orthonormal basis](../../../orthonormal-basis.md). Write $x=u e_6+v e_2$; the [quadratic form](../../../quadratic-form-split.md) becomes

$$
6u^2+2v^2=4,\qquad\boxed{\frac{u^2}{2/3}+\frac{v^2}{2}=1.}
$$

The locus is an [ellipse](../../../ellipse.md) centred at the origin. Its minor semiaxis has length $\sqrt{2/3}$ along the direction $30^\circ$ from the positive horizontal axis; its major semiaxis has length $\sqrt2$ along $120^\circ$. The right panel of the figure in part 1 shows these principal directions.
