<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

An [integrating factor](../../../integrating-factor.md) for the [first-order linear differential equation](../../../first-order-linear-differential-equation.md) is $\mu(x)=e^{\sin x}$. Multiplication gives

$$
(ye^{\sin x})'=2\sin x\cos x\,e^{\sin x}.
$$

Substitute $u=\sin x$ in the [antiderivative](../../../antiderivative.md): $\int2u e^u\,du=2(u-1)e^u$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=2\sin x-2+Ce^{-\sin x},\qquad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Differentiating verifies the equation everywhere on the real line; no division by $\cos x$ restricts the solution.
