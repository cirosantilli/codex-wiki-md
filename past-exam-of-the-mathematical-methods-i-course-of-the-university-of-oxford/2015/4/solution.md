<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The addition formulas for the [hyperbolic functions](../../../hyperbolic-function.md) imply

$$
B(u)B(v)=B(u+v),\qquad B(u)=\begin{pmatrix}\cosh u&\sinh u\\\sinh u&\cosh u\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Since $M=B(y)/2$, its fourth [matrix power](../../../matrix-power.md) is

$$
\boxed{M^4=\frac1{16}\begin{pmatrix}\cosh4y&\sinh4y\\\sinh4y&\cosh4y\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Alternatively, the orthonormal [eigenvectors](../../../eigenvector.md) $(1,\pm1)/\sqrt2$ have [eigenvalues](../../../eigenvalue.md) $e^{\pm y}/2$. Raising these [eigenvalues](../../../eigenvalue.md) to the fourth power gives the same result by [spectral decomposition](../../../spectral-decomposition.md).
