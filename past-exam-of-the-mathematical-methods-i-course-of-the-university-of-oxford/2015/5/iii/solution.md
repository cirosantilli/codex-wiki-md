<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The diagonal entries of a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) satisfy $A_{jj}=\overline{A_{jj}}$, so each is real. Consequently their sum, the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md), is real. **No additional condition is required.** The same conclusion follows by summing the real [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md).
