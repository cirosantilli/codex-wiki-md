<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Complex conjugation changes $i$ to $-i$, and transposition reverses the factors. Hence

$$
[i(AB-BA)]^\dagger=-i(BA-AB)=i(AB-BA).
$$

Thus the expression is a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) **without any additional condition**. Equivalently, the [commutator](../../../../commutator.md) of two [Hermitian matrices](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) is skew-Hermitian.
