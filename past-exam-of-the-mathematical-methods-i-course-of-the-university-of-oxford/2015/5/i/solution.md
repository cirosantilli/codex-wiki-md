<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Taking the [conjugate transpose](../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) reverses the product order: $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger=BA$. Thus

$$
\boxed{AB\text{ is Hermitian if and only if }AB=BA.}
$$

The necessary and sufficient additional condition is that the two [Hermitian matrices](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) [commute](../../../../commuting-elements.md).
