<h1 id="12/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Direct multiplication of the [Pauli matrices](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) gives $\sigma_j^2=I$, $\sigma_1\sigma_2=i\sigma_3$ and its cyclic permutations; reversing unequal factors changes the sign. Hence, using the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md),

$$
\sigma_j\sigma_k=\delta_{jk}I+i\epsilon_{jk\ell}\sigma_\ell,\qquad
\boxed{[\sigma_j,\sigma_k]=2i\epsilon_{jk\ell}\sigma_\ell.}
$$

Repeated $\ell$ is summed, while the [Kronecker delta](../../../../kronecker-delta.md) handles the equal-index case.
