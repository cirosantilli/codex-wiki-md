<h1 id="12/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The anticommuting unequal [Pauli matrices](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) cancel the cross terms, leaving

$$
T^2=(\alpha_1^2+\alpha_2^2+\alpha_3^2)I=|\alpha|^2I.
$$

Also $\operatorname{tr}T=0$, so its [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $\pm|\alpha|$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\lambda_\pm(U)=e^{\pm i|\alpha|/2}.}
$$

For $|\alpha|>0$, the [matrix exponential for a scalar square](../../../../matrix-exponential-for-a-scalar-square.md) also gives $U=I\cos(|\alpha|/2)+iT\sin(|\alpha|/2)/|\alpha|$. At $\alpha=0$, its continuous limit is $U=I$. The two exponential [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) coincide when $|\alpha|=2\pi k$, making $U=(-1)^kI$.
