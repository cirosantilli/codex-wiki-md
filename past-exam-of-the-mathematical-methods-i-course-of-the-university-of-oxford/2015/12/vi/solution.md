<h1 id="12/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

With only $\alpha_3$ nonzero, $T=\operatorname{diag}(\alpha_3,-\alpha_3)$ and

$$
U=\operatorname{diag}(e^{i\alpha_3/2},e^{-i\alpha_3/2}).
$$

For $\alpha_3\notin2\pi\mathbb Z$, the [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md) are **the nonzero multiples of $(1,0)^T$ and $(0,1)^T$**. When $\alpha_3=2\pi k$, $U=(-1)^kI$ and **every nonzero vector is an eigenvector**.
