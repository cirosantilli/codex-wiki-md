<h1 id="12/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

The transformed components are $a'=e^{i\alpha_3/2}a$, $b'=e^{-i\alpha_3/2}b$. Hence $(a')^*b'=e^{-i\alpha_3}a^*b$, while the two [moduli](../../../../modulus.md) are unchanged. Taking real and imaginary parts in the expression from part (ii) gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}x'_1\\x'_2\\x'_3\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\alpha_3&\sin\alpha_3&0\\-\sin\alpha_3&\cos\alpha_3&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\x_2\\x_3\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

This is a right-handed active [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) through **$-\alpha_3$ about the third axis**. The minus sign follows from the positive sign in $U=e^{+iT/2}$; changing that convention reverses the rotation. A $2\pi$ spatial [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) changes the [spinor](../../../../spinor.md) sign while leaving the three-vector unchanged.
