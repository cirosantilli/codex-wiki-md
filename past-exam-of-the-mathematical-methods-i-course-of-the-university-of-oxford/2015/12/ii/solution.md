<h1 id="12/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $v=(a,b)^T$, multiplying each [Pauli matrix](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) between $v^\dagger$ and $v$ gives

$$
\boxed{x=\left(\operatorname{Re}(a^*b),\operatorname{Im}(a^*b),\frac{|a|^2-|b|^2}{2}\right).}
$$

All three components are real. Moreover $|x|=(|a|^2+|b|^2)/2=\|v\|^2/2$. Thus normalized two-component [spinors](../../../../spinor.md) map to the radius-$1/2$ version of the [Bloch sphere](../../../../bloch-sphere.md); the usual unit-radius convention multiplies these coordinates by two.
