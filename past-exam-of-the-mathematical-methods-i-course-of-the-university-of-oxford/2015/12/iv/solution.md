<h1 id="12/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If $Tv=tv$ with $v\ne0$, induction gives $T^kv=t^kv$ for every nonnegative integer $k$. Substituting into the [matrix exponential](../../../../matrix-exponential.md) series gives

$$
Uv=\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac{(i/2)^kT^kv}{k!}=e^{it/2}v.
$$

Thus **each eigenvector of $T$ is an eigenvector of $U$**, with [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $e^{it/2}$. Different [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $T$ can produce the same exponential [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md).
