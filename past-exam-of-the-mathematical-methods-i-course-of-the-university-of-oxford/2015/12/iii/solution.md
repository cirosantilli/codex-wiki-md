<h1 id="12/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Each [Pauli matrix](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) is Hermitian and the coefficients $\alpha_j$ are real, so $T^\dagger=T$. Taking the [conjugate transpose](../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) of the absolutely convergent [matrix exponential](../../../../matrix-exponential.md) series yields

$$
U^\dagger=e^{-iT/2}.
$$

The exponents $-iT/2$ and $iT/2$ [commute](../../../../commuting-elements.md). Therefore $U^\dagger U=e^{-iT/2+iT/2}=I$, proving that **$U$ is unitary**.
