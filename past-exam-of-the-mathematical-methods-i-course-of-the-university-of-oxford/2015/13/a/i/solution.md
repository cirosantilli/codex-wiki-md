<h1 id="13/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) obeys $(I-M)X=c$. Thus the necessary and sufficient existence condition is

$$
\boxed{c\in\operatorname{range}(I-M).}
$$

If $1$ is not an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $M$, then $I-M$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../invertible-matrix.md) and the unique constant solution is $\boxed{X=(I-M)^{-1}c}$. If $I-M$ is singular but the equation is consistent, all constant solutions are $X_0+\ker(I-M)$ for any particular solution $X_0$. For an existing [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md), $x_{k+1}-X=M(x_k-X)$; the successive difference in the question likewise obeys $\delta x_{k+1}=M\delta x_k$.
