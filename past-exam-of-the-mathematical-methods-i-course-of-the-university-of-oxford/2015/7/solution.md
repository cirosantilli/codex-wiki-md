<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Subtract the first vertex from the remaining three to obtain edge vectors $a=(1,2,-2)$, $b=(0,1,3)$, $c=(-1,-1,0)$. Their [scalar triple product](../../../scalar-triple-product.md) is

$$
a\cdot(b\times c)=(1,2,-2)\cdot(3,-3,1)=-5.
$$

A [tetrahedron](../../../tetrahedron.md) occupies one sixth of the corresponding [parallelepiped](../../../parallelepiped.md), so its [volume](../../../volume.md) is

$$
\boxed{V=\frac16|a\cdot(b\times c)|=\frac56.}
$$

The absolute value removes the orientation sign of the [determinant](../../../determinant.md).
