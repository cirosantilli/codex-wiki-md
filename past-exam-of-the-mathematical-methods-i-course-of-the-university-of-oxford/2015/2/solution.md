↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

All [complex logarithms](../../../complex-logarithm.md) of $i$ are $i(\pi/2+2\pi k)$ for integer $k$. By [complex exponentiation](../../../complex-exponentiation.md),

$$
\boxed{i^i=\exp\!\left[-\frac\pi2-2\pi k\right],\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Every value is positive and real. If the power is defined using the [principal logarithm](../../../principal-complex-logarithm.md), the single value is $\boxed{e^{-\pi/2}}$. Keeping the logarithm branch explicit distinguishes this value from the full multivalued set.
