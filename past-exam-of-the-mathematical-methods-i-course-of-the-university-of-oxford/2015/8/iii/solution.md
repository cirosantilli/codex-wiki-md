<h1 id="8/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Multiplying $A$ by $c$ multiplies each of its $n$ columns by $c$. The [multilinearity](../../../../multilinearity.md) of the [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\det(cA)=c^n\det A.}
$$

This includes $c=0$.
