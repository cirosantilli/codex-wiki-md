↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Leibniz formula for determinants](../../../../leibniz-formula-for-determinants.md) is unchanged by interchanging rows and columns, or equivalently replacing each [permutation](../../../../permutation.md) by its inverse. Thus

$$
\boxed{\det(A^T)=\det A.}
$$
