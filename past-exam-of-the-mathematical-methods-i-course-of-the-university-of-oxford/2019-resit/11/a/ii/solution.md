↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) is [anti-Hermitian](../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md): $[A,B]^\dagger=-[A,B]$. It is also [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) precisely when it is zero, so **it is not [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) in general; it is [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) if and only if $A$ and $B$ commute**. For example, $[\sigma_x,\sigma_y]=2i\sigma_z\ne0$ for the [Pauli matrices](../../../../../pauli-matrices.md).
