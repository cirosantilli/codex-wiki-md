<h1 id="11/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Taking the [Hermitian adjoint](../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) reverses the products and leaves their sum unchanged:

$$
\left(\frac{AB+BA}{2}\right)^\dagger=\frac{BA+AB}{2}.
$$

Thus **the symmetrized product is [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md)**, whether or not $A$ and $B$ commute. This is the [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) [anticommutator](../../../../../anticommutator.md) divided by two.
