<h1 id="11/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

One has $(C^\dagger C)^\dagger=C^\dagger C$, with no requirement that $C$ itself be [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). Moreover,

$$
\langle v,C^\dagger Cv\rangle=\|Cv\|^2\geq0.
$$

Thus **$C^\dagger C$ is [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and [positive semidefinite](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md)**. The [positive semidefinite](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) property is stronger than the [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) conclusion alone.
