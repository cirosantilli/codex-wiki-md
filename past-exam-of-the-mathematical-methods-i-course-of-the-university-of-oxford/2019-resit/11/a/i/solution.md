<h1 id="11/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For finite-dimensional operators, use $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger$ and the [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) assumptions. The [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) satisfies $[A,B]^\dagger=-[A,B]$, so

$$
(i[A,B])^\dagger=-i(-[A,B])=i[A,B].
$$

Thus **$i[A,B]$ is [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md)**. These adjoint identities are the intended matrix-level setting; for unbounded operators, domains and self-adjointness require additional analysis.
