<h1 id="11/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each displayed [Pauli matrix](../../../../../pauli-matrices.md) has zero [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) and [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) $-1$. Direct multiplication gives $\sigma_i^2=I$, hence

$$
\boxed{\det\sigma_i=-1,\quad\operatorname{tr}\sigma_i=0,\quad\sigma_i^2=I,\quad\sigma_i^{-1}=\sigma_i\qquad(i=x,y,z).}
$$

Their two [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are therefore $+1$ and $-1$.
