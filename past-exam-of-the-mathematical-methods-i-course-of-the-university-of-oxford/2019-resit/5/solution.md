<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The common ratio supplies a [line](../../../straight-line.md) parameter $s$:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r=\mathbf p+s\mathbf v,\qquad\mathbf p=(1,1,1),\quad\mathbf v=(2,3,4).}
$$

The nearest point is the [orthogonal projection](../../../orthogonal-projection.md) of the origin onto the [line](../../../straight-line.md). Minimize $|\mathbf p+s\mathbf v|^2$: its [derivative](../../../derivative.md) is $2\mathbf v\cdot(\mathbf p+s\mathbf v)$, so $s_*=-9/29$ and the foot is $(11,2,-7)/29$. Thus

$$
\boxed{d=\sqrt{\frac6{29}}.}
$$

The [cross product](../../../cross-product.md) formula $d=|\mathbf p\times\mathbf v|/|\mathbf v|$ agrees, since $\mathbf p\times\mathbf v=(1,-2,1)$.
