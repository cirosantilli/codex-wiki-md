<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [integrating factor](../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $e^{2x}$, giving $(e^{2x}y)'=1+e^{2x}(x^2+2x)$. A quadratic particular solution for the [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) forcing is $x^2/2+x/2-1/4$, while the resonant exponential forcing contributes $xe^{-2x}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{y=(x+C)e^{-2x}+\frac{x^2+x}{2}-\frac14.}
$$

Substitution gives $y'+2y=e^{-2x}+x^2+2x$, and the free multiple of $e^{-2x}$ is the [homogeneous solution](../../../../homogeneous-solution.md).
