<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Eliminate $y$ using $y=1-x'$ to get $x''+x=-1$. The initial data give $x(0)=1$ and $x'(0)=0$, so the [simple harmonic motion](../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) about the equilibrium $x=-1$ is

$$
\boxed{x(t)=-1+2\cos t,\qquad y(t)=1+2\sin t.}
$$

Indeed, $x'+y=1$ and $y'-x=1$ identically, and both initial coordinates equal one. This also describes a [circle](../../../circle.md) of [radius](../../../radius.md) two around $(-1,1)$ in the [phase plane](../../../phase-plane.md).
