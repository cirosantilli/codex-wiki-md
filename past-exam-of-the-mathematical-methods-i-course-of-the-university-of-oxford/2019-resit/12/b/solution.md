<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the [Wronskian](../../../../wronskian.md) and cancel the mixed first-derivative terms:

$$
W'=u_1u_2''-u_2u_1''=u_1(-au_2'-bu_2)-u_2(-au_1'-bu_1)=-aW.
$$

This is the constant-coefficient form of [Abel's identity](../../../../abel-s-identity.md). Hence $W(x)=Ce^{-ax}$, or $W(x)=W(x_0)e^{-a(x-x_0)}$. Since the [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) is never zero, $W$ is either identically zero or nowhere zero on a connected interval of the homogeneous equation.
