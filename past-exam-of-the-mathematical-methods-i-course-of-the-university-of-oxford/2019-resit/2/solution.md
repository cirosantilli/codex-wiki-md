↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Here $i^3=-i$, so the [argument](../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) condition concerns $z+i$. Writing $z=x+iy$, the [complex number](../../../complex-number.md) $x+i(y+1)$ must point along the direction $1+i$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{z=-i+s(1+i),\quad s>0,\qquad\text{equivalently }y=x-1,\ x>0.}
$$

This is an [open ray](../../../open-ray.md) in the [Argand plane](../../../complex-plane.md), rather than the whole straight [line](../../../straight-line.md). Its endpoint $z=-i$ is excluded because the [argument](../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) of zero is undefined; the opposite half-line has a different [argument](../../../argument-complex-analysis.md).
