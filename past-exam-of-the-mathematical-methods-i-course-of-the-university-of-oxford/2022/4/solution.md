<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Set $v=\dot z$. The second-order position equation becomes the first-order [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) $\dot v+\gamma v=-g$. For $\gamma>0$, multiply by the [integrating factor](../../../integrating-factor.md) $e^{\gamma t}$ and impose $v(0)=v_0$:

$$
\boxed{v(t)=\left(v_0+\frac g\gamma\right)e^{-\gamma t}-\frac g\gamma.}
$$

The limiting [terminal velocity](../../../terminal-velocity.md) is $-g/\gamma$, consistent with upward-positive position and downward [gravity](../../../gravity.md). The continuous zero-drag limit is $v_0-gt$; one must take that limit of the whole expression rather than set $\gamma=0$ in its two singular-looking terms separately.
