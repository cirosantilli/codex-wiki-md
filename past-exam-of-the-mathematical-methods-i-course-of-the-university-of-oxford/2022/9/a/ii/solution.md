<h1 id="9/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The base is $2e^{i\pi/4}$. Its [complex logarithms](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) are $\ln2+i(\pi/4+2\pi k)$, $k\in\mathbb Z$. Exponentiating $i$ times each logarithm gives all values of the multivalued power:

$$
\boxed{z_k=e^{-\pi/4-2\pi k}\exp(i\ln2),\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The values form a discrete [geometric sequence](../../../../../geometric-progression.md) along the Argand ray of angle $\ln2$. Consecutive radii differ by $e^{-2\pi}$. The origin is an accumulation point but is not itself a value. If the power is instead defined using the [principal logarithm](../../../../../principal-complex-logarithm.md), only $k=0$ is retained. The phrase “all values” requires this branch distinction.
