<h1 id="9/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $z\ne-1$, multiply numerator and denominator by $\overline z+1$. The real part is

$$
\operatorname{Re}\frac{z-1}{z+1}=\frac{|z|^2-1}{|z+1|^2}.
$$

Thus its zero locus is

$$
\boxed{z=e^{i\theta},\qquad \theta\not\equiv\pi\pmod{2\pi}.}
$$

It is the [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) with the point $-1$ removed. That point cannot be restored by the zero numerator obtained after clearing a denominator: the original expression is undefined there.
