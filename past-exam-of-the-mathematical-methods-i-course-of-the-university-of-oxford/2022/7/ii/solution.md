<h1 id="7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [translated vector-space structure](../../../../translated-vector-space-structure.md) follows by translating the new origin to the ordinary one using the [bijection](../../../../bijection.md) $T(\mathbf x)=\mathbf x-\mathbf a$. Transporting ordinary [scalar multiplication](../../../../scalar-multiplication.md) through $T$ gives

$$
\boxed{k\odot\mathbf x=\mathbf a+k(\mathbf x-\mathbf a).}
$$

Then $T(\mathbf x\oplus\mathbf y)=T(\mathbf x)+T(\mathbf y)$ and $T(k\odot\mathbf x)=kT(\mathbf x)$, so the transported operations satisfy all [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) axioms. In particular, an explicit distributive check is

$$
k\odot(\mathbf x\oplus\mathbf y)=\mathbf a+k(\mathbf x+\mathbf y-2\mathbf a)
=(k\odot\mathbf x)\oplus(k\odot\mathbf y).
$$

The other distributive law follows in the same [coordinates](../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md), or directly by expanding $(k+l)\odot\mathbf x$.
