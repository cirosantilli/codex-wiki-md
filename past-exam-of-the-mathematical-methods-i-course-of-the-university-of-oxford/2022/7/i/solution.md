<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [additive identity](../../../../additive-identity.md) for the shifted operation must satisfy $\mathbf x+\mathbf0_{\oplus}-\mathbf a=\mathbf x$, giving $\mathbf0_{\oplus}=\mathbf a$. The [additive inverse](../../../../additive-inverse.md) must satisfy $\mathbf x+\mathbf x'-\mathbf a=\mathbf a$, giving

$$
\boxed{\mathbf0_{\oplus}=\mathbf a,\qquad\mathbf x'=2\mathbf a-\mathbf x.}
$$

The notation distinguishes the new [additive identity](../../../../additive-identity.md) from the ordinary zero [vector](../../../../vector.md). This is a translated [abelian group](../../../../abelian-group.md) structure on the same underlying set.
