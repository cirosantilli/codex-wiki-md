<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiplication by $A$ rotates the $(x,y)$ components by $\theta$ counterclockwise when viewed from positive $z$ toward the origin, leaving $z$ unchanged. It is a [proper rotation](../../../../proper-rotation.md) about the $z$ axis, with [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $+1$. Multiplication by $B$ sends $(x,y,z)$ to $(x,y,-z)$: it is a [reflection](../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) in the $xy$ plane, with [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $-1$. Neither operation changes a [vector](../../../../vector.md)'s Euclidean [norm](../../../../norm.md).
