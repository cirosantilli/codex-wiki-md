<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Directly multiplying the columns' [scalar products](../../../../dot-product.md) gives $C^TC=I$, and direct evaluation gives $\det C=1$. Thus **the operation is a [proper rotation](../../../../proper-rotation.md)**. Its [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) is zero. A three-dimensional [proper rotation](../../../../proper-rotation.md) has [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1,e^{i\varphi},e^{-i\varphi}$, hence $\operatorname{tr}C=1+2\cos\varphi$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\varphi=\frac{2\pi}{3}=120^\circ.}
$$

This determines the usual unsigned angle in $[0,\pi]$ without changing [coordinates](../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md). The axis can be checked, if desired, from $C(0,1,-1)^T=(0,1,-1)^T$. The [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) test must precede the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) formula: an [improper rotation](../../../../improper-rotation.md) instead has [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) $-1+2\cos\varphi$.
