<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [unitary matrices](../../../../unitary-matrix.md), $U^\dagger U=I$ and $V^\dagger V=I$. The [adjoint matrix](../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) reverses the order of multiplication, so

$$
(UV)^\dagger(UV)=V^\dagger U^\dagger UV=V^\dagger V=I.
$$

Thus **the product is unitary**. Likewise $(UV)(UV)^\dagger=I$, or this follows from invertibility in finite dimension.
