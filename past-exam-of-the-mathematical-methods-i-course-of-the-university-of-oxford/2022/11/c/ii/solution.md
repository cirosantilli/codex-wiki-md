<h1 id="11/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\beta^2=1$, the reduced equation is $u''+u=1+\cos x$. The constant forcing has particular solution one, while the resonant cosine requires $u_p=(x/2)\sin x$. Thus

$$
\boxed{y=e^x\left[A\cos x+B\sin x+1+\frac x2\sin x\right],\qquad\beta=\pm1.}
$$

The secular factor $x$ is the signature of [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md). It must replace the divergent nonresonant coefficient $1/(\beta^2-1)$; substituting $\beta^2=1$ directly into that coefficient is invalid.
