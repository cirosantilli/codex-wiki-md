<h1 id="8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $c>0$, this is the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) centered at $\mathbf a$ with radius $c$. If $c=0$, its locus is the singleton $\{\mathbf a\}$; if $c<0$, it is empty, because a Euclidean [norm](../../../../../norm.md) cannot be negative. The equation describes the surface, not the enclosed ball.
