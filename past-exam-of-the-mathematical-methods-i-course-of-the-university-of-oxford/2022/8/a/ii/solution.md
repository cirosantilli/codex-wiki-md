<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume $\mathbf a\ne0$ and allow all $\mathbf R\ne0$. Put $\widehat{\mathbf a}=\mathbf a/|\mathbf a|$ and $\mathbf u=\mathbf R/|\mathbf R|$. Every resulting [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is perpendicular to $\mathbf a$ and has $|\mathbf r|=|c|\sin\theta\le|c|$. Conversely, for $c\ne0$ and any $\mathbf q=\mathbf r/c$ perpendicular to $\widehat{\mathbf a}$ with $|\mathbf q|\le1$, choose

$$
\mathbf u=\widehat{\mathbf a}\times\mathbf q
+\sqrt{1-|\mathbf q|^2}\,\widehat{\mathbf a}.
$$

The [vector triple product](../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) gives $\mathbf u\times\widehat{\mathbf a}=\mathbf q$, and $|\mathbf u|=1$. Thus every point of the **closed disk centered at the origin, perpendicular to $\mathbf a$, of radius $|c|$** is attained. The boundary circle alone would omit allowed directions of $\mathbf R$. For $c=0$ the locus is the origin; for $\mathbf a=0$ the original expression is undefined.
