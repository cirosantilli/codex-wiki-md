<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Apply the [vector triple product identity](../../../vector-triple-product.md) $p\times(c\times d)=c(p\cdot d)-d(p\cdot c)$ with $p=a\times b$. This gives

$$
(a\times b)\times(c\times d)
=c\big((a\times b)\cdot d\big)-d\big((a\times b)\cdot c\big).
$$

For column [vectors](../../../vector.md) $a,b,d$, the [scalar triple product](../../../scalar-triple-product.md) is $(a\times b)\cdot d=\det(a,b,d)$; replacing $d$ by $c$ gives the other coefficient. Therefore

$$
\boxed{(a\times b)\times(c\times d)=\det(a,b,d)c-\det(a,b,c)d.}
$$

The signs follow the stated column convention and the cyclic invariance of the [scalar triple product](../../../scalar-triple-product.md). No independence assumptions on the four [vectors](../../../vector.md) are needed.
