<h1 id="11/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Take [determinants](../../../../../determinant.md) of the idempotence equation and use multiplicativity of the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md):

$$
(\det M)^2=\det(M^2)=\det M.
$$

Thus $d=\det M$ satisfies $d(d-1)=0$, proving

$$
\boxed{\det M\in\{0,1\}.}
$$

If the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is one, $M$ is invertible and [solution](../ii/solution.md) gives $M=I$. Every other [idempotent matrix](../../../../../idempotent-matrix.md) has zero [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md).
