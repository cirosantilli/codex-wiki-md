<h1 id="11/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $M$ is an $r\times s$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md), the product $M^2=MM$ is defined in ordinary [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) only when $s=r$. Hence the defining equation of an [idempotent matrix](../../../../../idempotent-matrix.md) already requires equal row and column counts:

$$
\boxed{M\text{ is square}.}
$$

Equivalently, an [idempotent linear map](../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) is an [endomorphism](../../../../../endomorphism.md), so the same [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) is both its domain and codomain.
