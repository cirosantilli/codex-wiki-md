<h1 id="9/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By [Vieta's formulas](../../../../../vieta-s-formulas.md), the sum of all five [polynomial roots](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md), counted with [multiplicity of a root](../../../../../multiplicity-of-a-root.md), is minus the coefficient of $z^4$ divided by the leading coefficient. Hence

$$
\boxed{\sum_{j=1}^5z_j=5.}
$$
