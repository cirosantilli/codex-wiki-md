<h1 id="9/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The coefficients are real, so the supplied [polynomial root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) $2+2i$ has its [complex conjugate](../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) $2-2i$ as another [polynomial root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md). Their monic [polynomial factor](../../../../../polynomial-factor.md) of degree two is

$$
(z-2-2i)(z-2+2i)=(z-2)^2+4=z^2-4z+8.
$$

Polynomial division then gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
z^5-5z^4+11z^3-3z^2-12z+8
&=(z^2-4z+8)(z^3-z^2-z+1)\\
&=(z^2-4z+8)(z-1)^2(z+1).
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus all five [polynomial roots](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md), with [multiplicity of a root](../../../../../multiplicity-of-a-root.md), are

$$
\boxed{2+2i,\qquad2-2i,\qquad1,\qquad1,\qquad-1.}
$$

Their sum is $5$ and their product is $8\cdot1\cdot(-1)=-8$, agreeing with [solution](../i/solution.md) and [solution](../ii/solution.md).
