<h1 id="9/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a monic [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree five, [Vieta's formulas](../../../../../vieta-s-formulas.md) give the product of its [polynomial roots](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) as $(-1)^5$ times its constant coefficient. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\prod_{j=1}^5z_j=-8.}
$$
