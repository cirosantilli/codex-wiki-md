<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Squaring the [modulus](../../../../modulus.md) and doubling the [complex argument](../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) gives

$$
(2e^{i\pi/4})^2=4e^{i\pi/2}=\boxed{0+4i}.
$$

This uses [Euler's formula](../../../../euler-s-formula.md) $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$.
