<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiply numerator and denominator by the [complex conjugate](../../../../complex-conjugate.md) of the denominator:

$$
\frac{1+i}{1-i}=\frac{(1+i)^2}{(1-i)(1+i)}=\frac{2i}{2}=\boxed{0+i}.
$$

The denominator is the squared [modulus](../../../../modulus.md) $|1-i|^2=2$.
