<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the [matrix trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) in [Einstein summation convention](../../../../einstein-notation.md):

$$
\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\sum_{i,j}A_{ij}B_{ji}=\sum_{j,i}B_{ji}A_{ij}=\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(BA)}.
$$

The entries are [scalars](../../../../scalar.md), so their multiplication commutes, and the two summed indices may be relabelled. The argument works for square [matrices](../../../../matrix.md) of any finite size, and also for rectangular $A,B$ of compatible opposite shapes.
