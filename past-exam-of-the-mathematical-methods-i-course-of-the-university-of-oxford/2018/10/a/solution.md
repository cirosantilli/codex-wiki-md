<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Adopt the convention that an [inner product](../../../../inner-product-split.md) is linear in its second slot. Over $\mathbb R$, it is a [symmetric bilinear form](../../../../symmetric-bilinear-form.md) with $\langle v,v\rangle\geq0$, equality only for $v=0$. Over $\mathbb C$, a [Hermitian inner product](../../../../hermitian-inner-product.md) obeys

$$
\langle u,av+bw\rangle=a\langle u,v\rangle+b\langle u,w\rangle,\qquad
\langle u,v\rangle=\overline{\langle v,u\rangle},\qquad
\langle v,v\rangle>0\quad(v\ne0).
$$

These imply conjugate linearity in the first slot: $\langle au+bv,w\rangle=\overline a\langle u,w\rangle+\overline b\langle v,w\rangle$. The strict positivity condition distinguishes an [inner product](../../../../inner-product-split.md) from a general [Hermitian form](../../../../hermitian-form.md).
