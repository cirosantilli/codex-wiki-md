<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two [reciprocal bases](../../../../reciprocal-basis.md) satisfy $\langle u_i,w_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}$. Expanding each [vector](../../../../vector.md) in its specified [basis](../../../../basis.md) and using real bilinearity gives

$$
\boxed{\langle a,b\rangle=\left\langle\sum_i a_iu_i,\sum_jb_jw_j\right\rangle=\sum_i a_ib_i.}
$$

The simple sum-of-products formula holds because the two [coordinate vectors](../../../../coordinate-vector.md) use paired [reciprocal bases](../../../../reciprocal-basis.md). It does not require either [basis](../../../../basis.md) to be [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md), and would generally fail if both sets of components were taken in the same nonorthogonal [basis](../../../../basis.md).
