<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expand the [vector](../../../../vector.md) in the [orthonormal basis](../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), $v=\sum_jv_je_j$. Using linearity in the second slot and the [Kronecker delta](../../../../kronecker-delta.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\langle e_i,v\rangle=\sum_jv_j\langle e_i,e_j\rangle=v_i.}
$$

Expanding both [vectors](../../../../vector.md) and using conjugate linearity in the first slot therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\langle u,v\rangle=\sum_{i,j}\overline{u_i}v_j\langle e_i,e_j\rangle=\sum_i\overline{u_i}v_i.}
$$

An [orthonormal set](../../../../orthonormal-set.md) that is not a [basis](../../../../basis.md) would recover only the coordinates of the [orthogonal projection](../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto its [linear span](../../../../linear-span.md); here the $n$ [vectors](../../../../vector.md) span $\mathbb C^n$.
