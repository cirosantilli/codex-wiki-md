<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $w=\operatorname{artanh}z$. The [hyperbolic tangent](../../../../hyperbolic-tangent.md) identity $z=(e^{2w}-1)/(e^{2w}+1)$ gives $e^{2w}=(1+z)/(1-z)$. Taking a compatible branch of the [complex logarithm](../../../../complex-logarithm.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{artanh}z=\frac12\log\frac{1+z}{1-z}.}
$$

As an identity for a single-valued [inverse hyperbolic tangent](../../../../inverse-hyperbolic-tangent.md), this requires consistent [branch cuts](../../../../branch-cut.md); one cannot choose unrelated logarithm branches on the two sides. Near $z=0$ the branch with value zero is fixed by $\operatorname{artanh}0=0$. The full inverse relation also allows $w\mapsto w+i\pi k$, $k\in\mathbb Z$, and $z=\pm1$ are singular points.
