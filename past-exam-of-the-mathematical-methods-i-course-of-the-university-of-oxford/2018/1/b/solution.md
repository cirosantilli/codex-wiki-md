<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expand the [sine](../../../../sine.md) of the [complex number](../../../../complex-number.md) using [hyperbolic functions](../../../../hyperbolic-function.md):

$$
\sin(x+iy)=\sin x\cosh y+i\cos x\sinh y.
$$

Its squared [complex modulus](../../../../complex-modulus.md) is

$$
|\sin(x+iy)|^2=\sin^2x\cosh^2y+\cos^2x\sinh^2y=\sin^2x+\sinh^2y\geq\sin^2x.
$$

Taking nonnegative [square roots](../../../../square-root.md) proves $\boxed{|\sin z|\geq|\sin x|}$. Equality holds exactly when $y=0$.
