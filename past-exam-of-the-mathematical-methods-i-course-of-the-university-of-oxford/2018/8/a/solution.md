<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) $r^2+5r+4=(r+1)(r+4)=0$ gives the [complementary function](../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) $C_1e^{-x}+C_2e^{-4x}$. Write the forcing as $30e^x+30e^{-x}$. The [method of undetermined coefficients](../../../../method-of-undetermined-coefficients.md) gives $3e^x$ for the first term. Because $e^{-x}$ already belongs to the [homogeneous solution](../../../../homogeneous-solution.md), [resonance in a differential equation](../../../../resonance-in-a-differential-equation.md) requires a trial $Kxe^{-x}$ for the second; substitution gives $3K=30$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y=C_1e^{-x}+C_2e^{-4x}+3e^x+10xe^{-x}.}
$$
