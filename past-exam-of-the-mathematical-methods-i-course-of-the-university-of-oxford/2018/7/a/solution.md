<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a purely imaginary candidate $z=it$, $t\in\mathbb R$, the imaginary part of the [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) is $2t(t^2-4)$. The candidate $t=0$ is not a [root](../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md), while $t=\pm2$ both make the real part zero. The resulting [factorization](../../../../factorization.md) into [quadratic polynomials](../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) gives

$$
f(z)=(z^2+4)(z^2-2z+4),\qquad
\boxed{z=2i,-2i,1+i\sqrt3,1-i\sqrt3.}
$$

The [roots of a polynomial](../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) sum to $0+2=2$ and have product $4\cdot4=16$, as required by [Vieta's formulas](../../../../vieta-s-formulas.md) for a monic quartic with cubic coefficient $-2$ and constant coefficient $16$.
