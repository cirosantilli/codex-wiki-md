<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) and [Einstein summation convention](../../../../einstein-notation.md). The [tensor contraction](../../../../tensor-contraction.md) $\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{klm}=\delta_{il}\delta_{jm}-\delta_{im}\delta_{jl}$, expressed through the [Kronecker delta](../../../../kronecker-delta.md), gives

$$
\bigl(a\times(b\times c)\bigr)_i=\epsilon_{ijk}a_j\epsilon_{klm}b_lc_m
=b_i a_jc_j-c_i a_jb_j.
$$

The last two contractions are [dot products](../../../../dot-product.md), proving the [vector triple product](../../../../vector-triple-product.md) identity $\boxed{a\times(b\times c)=(a\cdot c)b-(a\cdot b)c}$.
