<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose the [linear relation](../../../../linear-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{c=Md,\qquad c_i=M_{ij}d_j.}
$$

Then the displayed [tensor contraction](../../../../tensor-contraction.md) is $(a_ic_i)b_k-(a_ib_i)c_k=(a\cdot c)b_k-(a\cdot b)c_k$, precisely the $k$th [vector component](../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md) of $a\times(b\times c)$ by the [vector triple product](../../../../vector-triple-product.md) identity. The [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) $M$ need not be a [symmetric matrix](../../../../symmetric-matrix.md).
