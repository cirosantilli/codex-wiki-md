<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Using the [hyperbolic functions](../../../hyperbolic-function.md) identities $\sin(ix)=i\sinh x$ and $\cos(ix)=\cosh x$, divide by $i$ and put $u=e^x\ne0$. The equation becomes $-u/2-5/(2u)=3$, hence

$$
u^2+6u+5=(u+1)(u+5)=0.
$$

Taking all branches of the [complex logarithm](../../../complex-logarithm.md) gives

$$
\boxed{x=(2k+1)\pi i\quad\text{or}\quad x=\ln5+(2k+1)\pi i,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

If $x$ is required to be real, there are **no real solutions**, since $e^x$ cannot be negative. The complex branches are essential to the unrestricted answer.
