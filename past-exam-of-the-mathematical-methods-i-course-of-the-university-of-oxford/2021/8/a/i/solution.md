↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) is $\{\lambda\mathbf a+\mu\mathbf b:\lambda,\mu\in\mathbb R\}$, a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $\mathbb R^3$. Its [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is **zero** if both vectors vanish, **one** if at least one is nonzero and they are [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md), and **two** if they are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md). The corresponding geometric sets are the origin, a line through the origin, and a plane through the origin.
