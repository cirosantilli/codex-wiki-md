<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Contract the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) identity $\varepsilon_{ijk}\varepsilon_{i\ell m}=\delta_{j\ell}\delta_{km}-\delta_{jm}\delta_{k\ell}$. Using [Einstein summation convention](../../../../../einstein-notation.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|^2
&=\varepsilon_{ijk}a_jb_k\varepsilon_{i\ell m}a_\ell b_m\\
&=(\delta_{j\ell}\delta_{km}-\delta_{jm}\delta_{k\ell})a_jb_ka_\ell b_m\\
&=|\mathbf a|^2|\mathbf b|^2-(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b)^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

This [Lagrange identity for the cross product](../../../../../lagrange-identity-for-the-cross-product.md) includes zero and parallel vectors without a separate division by their norms.
