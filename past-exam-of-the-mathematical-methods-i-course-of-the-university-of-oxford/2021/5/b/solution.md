<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\mathbf u=(2,1)/\sqrt5$ be a unit [vector](../../../../vector.md) along the fixed line. The [orthogonal projection](../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) parallel to the line is $\mathbf u\mathbf u^T$; a [reflection](../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) keeps that component and reverses its orthogonal complement. Therefore

$$
\boxed{A=2\mathbf u\mathbf u^T-I=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}3&4\\4&-3\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det A=-1.}
$$

Its [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $+1$ along $(2,1)$ and $-1$ along $(-1,2)$. It preserves lengths and unsigned areas but reverses orientation. For example, $(1,0)$ maps to $(3/5,4/5)$, with their midpoint on the reflection line and their separation perpendicular to it; the right panel of the figure in [1](../../1.md) illustrates this.
