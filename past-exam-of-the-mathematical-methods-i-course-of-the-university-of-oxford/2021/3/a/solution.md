<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [matrix product](../../../../matrix-product.md), $(AB)_{ij}=\sum_{k=1}^n a_{ik}b_{kj}$. A nonzero summand in a product of [upper triangular matrices](../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md) requires both $i\le k$ and $k\le j$. If $i>j$, no such index exists, so the product is upper triangular. If $i=j$, only $k=i$ survives, giving

$$
\boxed{(AB)_{ii}=a_{ii}b_{ii}.}
$$

This also shows inductively why the diagonal of an integer [matrix power](../../../../matrix-power.md) consists of the corresponding powers of the diagonal entries.
