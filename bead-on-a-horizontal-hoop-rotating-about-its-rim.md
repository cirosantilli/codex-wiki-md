# Bead on a horizontal hoop rotating about its rim

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)

For a smooth horizontal circular hoop of radius $a$, rotated at constant [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega$ about a vertical axis through a rim point, measure $\theta$ at that pivot from the diameter toward the bead. In rotating [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) coordinates, $\mathbf r=a(1+\cos2\theta,\sin2\theta,0)$. The tangential component of the [centrifugal acceleration](centrifugal-acceleration.md) gives the displayed equation; the [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) is normal to the hoop. The farthest point $\theta=0$ is stable for $\omega\ne0$, with [small oscillation](small-oscillation.md) angular frequency $|\omega|$. The pivot itself is unstable. This geometry differs from a vertical rotating hoop and has no tangential gravitational term.
