# Solute conservation in a steadily pulled mush

↑ **Parent:** [Density-change flow in a pulled mushy layer](density-change-flow-in-a-pulled-mushy-layer.md)

With pure solid crystals, negligible solute diffusion, and the [density-change flow in a pulled mushy layer](density-change-flow-in-a-pulled-mushy-layer.md), steady solute balance gives $(1-\varphi)C=C_0$. If $\theta=(T-T_0)/\Delta T$, $\mathcal C=mC_0/\Delta T$, and the [liquidus](liquidus.md) is $T-T_0=-m(C-C_0)$, then

$$
\varphi=-\frac{\theta}{\mathcal C-\theta}.
$$
