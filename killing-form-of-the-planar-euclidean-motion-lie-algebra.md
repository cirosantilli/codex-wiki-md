# Killing form of the planar Euclidean motion Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean motion Lie algebra in two dimensions](euclidean-motion-lie-algebra-in-two-dimensions.md)

In the ordered basis $(J,E_1,E_2)$ of the [Euclidean motion Lie algebra in two dimensions](euclidean-motion-lie-algebra-in-two-dimensions.md), the [Killing form](killing-form.md) has matrix $\operatorname{diag}(-2,0,0)$. Its radical is the translation ideal, and the algebra is solvable rather than semisimple.
