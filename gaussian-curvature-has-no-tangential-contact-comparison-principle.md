# Gaussian curvature has no tangential-contact comparison principle

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-curvature comparison at tangential contact](mean-curvature-comparison-at-tangential-contact.md)

The same contact does not order [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md), because determinant is not monotone under addition of a positive-semidefinite matrix when the original Hessian is indefinite or negative definite. For example,

$$
f=-x^2-y^2,
\qquad
g=-\frac{x^2+y^2}{2}
$$

satisfy $g\geq f$ and are tangent at zero, but $K_g(0)=1<4=K_f(0)$.
