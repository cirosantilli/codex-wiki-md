# Probability generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_generating_function)

For a nonnegative integer-valued [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$, its probability generating function is

$$
G_X(s)=\mathbb E[s^X]=\sum_{n\geq0}\mathbb P(X=n)s^n.
$$

The generating function of a sum of [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) is the product of their generating functions.
