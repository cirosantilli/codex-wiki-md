# Leftist heap

↑ **Parent:** [Meldable heap](meldable-heap.md)

A binary [meldable heap](meldable-heap.md) satisfying [heap order](heap-order.md) and the leftist condition: at each node, the shortest path to a missing child is no longer in the right subtree than in the left. Melding recurses down right spines, swaps children when needed, and takes logarithmic time.
