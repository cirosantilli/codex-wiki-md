# Lepton mixing matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md)

The matrix relating weak-interaction [neutrino](neutrino.md) flavour fields to [mass](mass.md) eigenfields, $\nu_{\alpha L}=\sum_iU_{\alpha i}\nu_{iL}$. In the standard three-active-neutrino description it is unitary. Oscillation probabilities involve products of entries and mass-squared differences; lepton-number-violating amplitudes can depend on additional Majorana phases through products such as $U_{ei}^2m_i$.
