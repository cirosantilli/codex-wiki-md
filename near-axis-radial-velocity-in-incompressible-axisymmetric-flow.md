# Near-axis radial velocity in incompressible axisymmetric flow

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric flow](axisymmetric-flow.md)

For a smooth [axisymmetric flow](axisymmetric-flow.md), write $u_z(r,z)=w(z)+O(r^2)$ near the axis. The [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) condition is $r^{-1}\partial_r(ru_r)+\partial_zu_z=0$. Integrating radially and excluding a singular line source at the axis gives $u_r=-rw'(z)/2+O(r^3)$. The signed axial velocity is essential: specifying only its magnitude does not fix the sign of the radial velocity.
