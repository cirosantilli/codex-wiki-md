<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Let $u,v\in\mathcal D'(\mathbb R^n)$, with at least one a [compactly supported distribution](../../../../../compactly-supported-distribution.md), so their [convolution](../../../../../convolution.md) is defined. The [support and singular support rules for convolution](../../../../../support-and-singular-support-rules-for-convolution.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{supp}(u*v)\subseteq\operatorname{supp}u+\operatorname{supp}v,\qquad
\operatorname{sing\,supp}(u*v)\subseteq
\operatorname{sing\,supp}u+\operatorname{sing\,supp}v.}
$$

Here $A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,\ b\in B\}$ is the [Minkowski sum](../../../../../minkowski-addition.md). The compact-factor hypothesis also ensures that these sums of closed sets are closed. More generally, the same rules hold when addition is a [proper map](../../../../../proper-map.md) on $\operatorname{supp}u\times\operatorname{supp}v$, which is another sufficient condition for defining the [convolution](../../../../../convolution.md). The second inclusion explains why a compactly supported [smooth function](../../../../../smooth-function.md) smooths every [distribution](../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) under [convolution](../../../../../convolution.md).
