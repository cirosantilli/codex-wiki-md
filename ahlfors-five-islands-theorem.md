# Ahlfors five islands theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Ahlfors second fundamental theorem](ahlfors-second-fundamental-theorem.md)

Every nonconstant [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) on the complex plane has a [simple island](simple-island.md) over at least one of any five [Jordan domains](jordan-domain.md) on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) with disjoint closures. The [Ahlfors second fundamental theorem](ahlfors-second-fundamental-theorem.md), area comparison over target domains, and a [length-area exhaustion of the complex plane](length-area-exhaustion-of-the-complex-plane.md) prove this: without a simple island every counted island has degree at least two, forcing $3S\le(5/2)S+O(L)$.
