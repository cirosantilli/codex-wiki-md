# Agglomerative clustering of nonmetric dissimilarities

↑ **Parent:** [Agglomerative hierarchical clustering](agglomerative-hierarchical-clustering.md)

The min and max cross-pair definitions of [single-linkage clustering](single-linkage-clustering.md) and [complete-linkage clustering](complete-linkage-clustering.md) remain meaningful for a symmetric nonnegative [dissimilarity matrix](dissimilarity-matrix.md) that violates the triangle inequality. Such a calculation gives a valid algorithmic dendrogram, but does not turn the original matrix into Euclidean distances. A triangle-inequality counterexample identifies that source defect without requiring invented replacement data.
