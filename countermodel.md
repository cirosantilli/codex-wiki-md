# Countermodel

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

An interpretation, together with an assignment to any [free variables](free-variable.md), in which all premises of a proposed inference hold and its conclusion fails. By soundness of the [inference rules](inference-rule.md), a [countermodel](countermodel.md) proves that the inference cannot be derived. For example, on $\{0,1\}$ with $P=Q=\{0\}$ and $x=0$, the formula $\forall y(P(y)\to Q(y))$ is true but $P(x)\to\forall y Q(y)$ is false. A pointwise assumption about $x$ cannot justify unrestricted [universal quantification](universal-quantification.md).
