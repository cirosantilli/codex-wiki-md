<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The product rule for the [Lie derivative](../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) and the [Killing equation](../../../../../killing-equation.md) give

$$
\mathcal L_K\widetilde g
=\mathcal L_K(\Lambda^2g)
=K(\Lambda^2)g+\Lambda^2\mathcal L_Kg
=2\frac{K(\Lambda)}{\Lambda}\widetilde g.
$$

Consequently $K$ is a [Conformal Killing vector field](../../../../../conformal-killing-vector-field.md) for the rescaled [metric tensor](../../../../../metric-tensor.md), with smooth [conformal Killing factor](../../../../../conformal-killing-factor.md)

$$
\boxed{\sigma=K(\log|\Lambda|)=\frac{K^a\partial_a\Lambda}{\Lambda}.}
$$

It remains an actual [Killing vector field](../../../../../killing-vector-field-split.md) precisely when $K(\Lambda)=0$: the [conformal factor](../../../../../conformal-factor.md) must be constant along its [integral curves of a vector field](../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md).
