<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since the real [conformal factor](../../../../../conformal-factor.md) $\Lambda$ is nowhere zero, $\Lambda^2>0$ at every point. For any [tangent vector](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md),

$$
\widetilde g(V,V)=\Lambda^2g(V,V).
$$

The sign is unchanged. With [metric signature](../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, negative, zero and positive values therefore describe the same [timelike vectors](../../../../../timelike-vector.md), [null vectors](../../../../../null-vector.md) and [spacelike vectors](../../../../../spacelike-vector.md) for both [metric tensors](../../../../../metric-tensor.md). In particular the two [null cones](../../../../../light-cone.md) coincide.

Choose the same [time orientation](../../../../../time-orientation.md) for the two [Lorentzian metrics](../../../../../lorentzian-metric.md). A future-directed [causal curve](../../../../../causal-curve.md) or [timelike curve](../../../../../timelike-curve.md) for one has the same character for the other. Hence the [causal future](../../../../../causal-future.md), [causal past](../../../../../causal-past.md) and [chronological future](../../../../../chronological-future.md) of every point agree:

$$
\boxed{
J^\pm_{\widetilde g}(p)=J^\pm_g(p),\qquad
I^\pm_{\widetilde g}(p)=I^\pm_g(p).
}
$$

This is the preservation of [causal structure](../../../../../causal-structure.md) by a smooth, nonvanishing [conformal transformation](../../../../../conformal-map.md). It does not assert preservation of [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) or of [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md) lengths.
