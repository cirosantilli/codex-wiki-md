<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A nondegenerate [Lorentzian metric](../../../../../lorentzian-metric.md) requires $M\ne0$; take $M>0$ as the radius convention. The original coordinates describe $\lambda>0$, with $\lambda=0$ a candidate boundary rather than an ordinary point of that chart. The change of coordinate gives

$$
dt=dv+\frac{M}{\lambda^2}d\lambda.
$$

Substituting and cancelling the two terms proportional to $d\lambda^2/\lambda^2$ produces

$$
\boxed{
ds^2=-\lambda^2dv^2-2M\,dv\,d\lambda+M^2d\Omega^2.
}
$$

The $(v,\lambda)$ block of this [metric tensor](../../../../../metric-tensor.md) has [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) $-M^2$ and [inverse metric](../../../../../inverse-metric.md)

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
g^{vv}&g^{v\lambda}\\
g^{\lambda v}&g^{\lambda\lambda}
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-1/M\\
-1/M&\lambda^2/M^2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

All coefficients are smooth through $\lambda=0$, and the block has one negative and one positive [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). Together with the positive round [two-sphere](../../../../../two-sphere.md) factor this is a regular [Lorentzian metric](../../../../../lorentzian-metric.md). It extends to a neighborhood with either sign of $\lambda$. The usual polar-coordinate problem at the poles of the [two-sphere](../../../../../two-sphere.md) is removed by separate angular charts. Therefore $\lambda=0$ is only a [coordinate singularity](../../../../../coordinate-singularity.md) of the original chart. This is the degenerate-horizon patch of [Bertotti-Robinson spacetime](../../../../../bertotti-robinson-spacetime.md).
