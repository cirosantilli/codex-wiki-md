<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [coordinate basis](../../../../coordinate-basis.md) vector $K=\partial_v$ has constant components $K^a=\delta^a_v$. Its [norm](../../../../norm.md) is

$$
g_{ab}K^aK^b=g_{vv}=0,
$$

so it is a nonzero [null vector](../../../../null-vector.md) everywhere. The [Lie derivative](../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) of the [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) is

$$
(\mathcal L_Kg)_{ab}
=K^c\partial_cg_{ab}+g_{cb}\partial_aK^c+g_{ac}\partial_bK^c
=\partial_vg_{ab}=0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{K=\partial_v\text{ is a null Killing vector field}.}$ In fact it is a [parallel vector field](../../../../parallel-vector-field.md): $K_b=g_{bv}=\delta_b^u$ and the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) found below give $\nabla_aK_b=-\Gamma^u{}_{ab}=0$. This stronger property is characteristic of this [plane-fronted gravitational wave](../../../../plane-fronted-gravitational-wave.md).
