<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) implements the [cosmological principle](../../../../cosmological-principle.md): on the scales being modelled, the spatial slices have [spatial homogeneity](../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md) and [cosmological isotropy](../../../../cosmological-isotropy.md). There is a family of [comoving observers](../../../../comoving-observer.md) whose orthogonal slices share one [cosmic time](../../../../cosmic-time.md) and one [scale factor](../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md). Each spatial slice is a [locally maximally symmetric space](../../../../locally-maximally-symmetric-space.md), with time-independent normalized $k$.

The matter is represented by a [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md), at rest relative to the [comoving observers](../../../../comoving-observer.md), with [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) $\varrho(t)$ and [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) $p(t)$ depending only on [cosmic time](../../../../cosmic-time.md). Its [stress-energy tensor](../../../../stress-energy-tensor-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{T_{\mu\nu}=(\varrho+p)u_\mu u_\nu+p g_{\mu\nu},\qquad u=\partial_t.}
$$

There is no preferred spatial direction, so there is no [anisotropic stress](../../../../anisotropic-stress.md) or spatial [heat flux](../../../../heat-flux-density.md) in that rest frame. Gravity obeys the [Einstein field equations](../../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md); the equations in this paper contain no separate [cosmological constant](../../../../cosmological-constant.md), although a component with $p=-\varrho$ can represent one. An [equation of state](../../../../equation-of-state.md) relating $p$ and $\varrho$ closes the evolution equations. [Spatial homogeneity](../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md) and [cosmological isotropy](../../../../cosmological-isotropy.md) are idealizations of the cosmological background; they do not assert that individual local structures are uniform.
