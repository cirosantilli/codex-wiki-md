<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [scale factor](../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) $a(t)>0$ specifies the common time dependence of [proper distance in cosmology](../../../../proper-distance-in-cosmology.md) between [comoving observers](../../../../comoving-observer.md). Their [comoving coordinates](../../../../comoving-coordinate.md) remain fixed, while their separation is multiplied by $a(t)$. Thus $\dot a>0$ means expansion and $\dot a<0$ means contraction. The coordinate $t$ is [cosmic time](../../../../cosmic-time.md), measured by the [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md) of a [comoving observer](../../../../comoving-observer.md).

The parameter $k$ fixes the sign of the [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](../../../../spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md). More precisely, the spatial [sectional curvature](../../../../sectional-curvature.md) and three-dimensional [scalar curvature](../../../../scalar-curvature.md) are

$$
\boxed{K_{\mathrm{spatial}}=\frac{k}{a^2},\qquad {}^{(3)}R=\frac{6k}{a^2}.}
$$

The cases $k=1,0,-1$ have locally [spherical geometry](../../../../spherical-geometry.md), [Euclidean geometry](../../../../euclidean-geometry-split.md), and [hyperbolic geometry](../../../../hyperbolic-geometry-split.md), respectively. For $k=\pm1$, $a$ is the [curvature radius of a constant-curvature space](../../../../curvature-radius-of-a-constant-curvature-space.md) in the displayed normalization. Local [curvature](../../../../curvature.md) alone does not fix the global [topology](../../../../topology-split.md).
