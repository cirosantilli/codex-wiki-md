<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Set $H=\dot a/a$, the [Hubble parameter](../../../../hubble-parameter.md). The first supplied component of the [Einstein field equations](../../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md) gives the [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md)

$$
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}(\varrho+3p).
$$

Substitution into the second supplied equation gives

$$
2\left(H^2+\frac{k}{a^2}\right)
=4\pi G(\varrho-p)+\frac{4\pi G}{3}(\varrho+3p)
=\frac{16\pi G}{3}\varrho.
$$

Therefore the first [Friedmann equation](../../../../friedmann-equations.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dot a^2+k=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\varrho a^2.}
$$

Differentiate this equation with respect to [cosmic time](../../../../cosmic-time.md):

$$
2\dot a\ddot a=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\left(a^2\dot\varrho+2a\varrho\dot a\right).
$$

Insert the [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) on the left and cancel $8\pi Ga/3$, which is nonzero for $a>0$. This gives

$$
-\dot a(\varrho+3p)=a\dot\varrho+2\varrho\dot a,
\qquad
\boxed{a\dot\varrho+3(\varrho+p)\dot a=0.}
$$

This is the [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md), expressing [stress-energy conservation](../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) for the [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md). No division by $\dot a$ was made, so the derivation remains valid at a [cosmological bounce](../../../../cosmological-bounce.md) or an instant of maximum expansion.
