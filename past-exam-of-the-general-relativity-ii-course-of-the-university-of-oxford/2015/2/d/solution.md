<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the extremal case, the squared norm of the [Killing vector field](../../../../killing-vector-field-split.md) is

$$
g(K,K)=-\left(1-\frac Mr\right)^2.
$$

For $M=|Q|>0$, it is negative for every regular $r\ne M$ and vanishes precisely at $r=M$ in the extended [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{K\text{ is null exactly on }r=M.}
$$

The static chart cannot itself test the geometry at its horizon. In the regular [Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md), the normal to $h=r-M=0$ is $n=dr$, and

$$
g^{-1}(n,n)=g^{rr}=F,\qquad n^\sharp=\partial_v+F\partial_r.
$$

At $r=M$ this gives $n^\sharp=\partial_v=K$, a nonzero [null vector](../../../../null-vector.md) tangent to the surface. Therefore **$r=M$ is a [null hypersurface](../../../../null-hypersurface.md)**, even though $F$ has the same sign on its two sides.
