<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $M^2=Q^2$ with $M>0$, then

$$
\boxed{F=(1-M/r)^2,\qquad r_+=r_-=M.}
$$

The [curvature singularity](../../../../../curvature-singularity.md) remains at $r=0$. At $r=M$ the static chart has a [coordinate singularity](../../../../../coordinate-singularity.md), removed by the [Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../../eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md) above. The [Kretschmann scalar](../../../../../kretschmann-scalar.md) there equals $8/M^4$, which is finite. The coincident horizons form a [degenerate Killing horizon](../../../../../degenerate-killing-horizon.md), and its future outer branch is the [event horizon](../../../../../event-horizon.md) of the [extremal black hole](../../../../../extremal-black-hole.md). A double zero of $F$ does not remove the horizon.
