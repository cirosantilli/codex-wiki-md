<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $0<Q^2<M^2$ there are two distinct positive roots:

$$
\boxed{r_\pm=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-Q^2},\qquad F=\frac{(r-r_+)(r-r_-)}{r^2}.}
$$

Both roots are [coordinate singularities](../../../../../coordinate-singularity.md) of the static chart. In the standard extension, $r_+$ is the outer [event horizon](../../../../../event-horizon.md) and $r_-$ is an inner [Cauchy horizon](../../../../../cauchy-horizon.md). The [curvature singularity](../../../../../curvature-singularity.md) is $r=0$. Between the horizons $F<0$, so the radial direction is timelike; for $0<r<r_-$, $F>0$ again and the central [curvature singularity](../../../../../curvature-singularity.md) is timelike. These are the three radial regions of the [global structure of a charged spherical black hole](../../../../../global-structure-of-a-charged-spherical-black-hole.md).[Sections 6.2.2–6.2.5, printed pages 260–268](../../../../../general-relativity-by-david-tong.md)

For completeness, if $Q=0$ and $M>0$, the [Schwarzschild spacetime](../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md) limit has just the positive horizon $r=2M$ and a spacelike [curvature singularity](../../../../../curvature-singularity.md) at $r=0$; the formal root $r_-=0$ of $r^2F$ is not a regular inner horizon. If $M=Q=0$, the [metric tensor](../../../../../metric-tensor.md) is flat and the radial origin is regular in [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md).
