<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $M^2<Q^2$, then

$$
r^2F=(r-M)^2+(Q^2-M^2)>0.
$$

There is no positive root and no horizon hiding $r=0$ in the extended [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](../../../../../reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md). Its [Kretschmann scalar](../../../../../kretschmann-scalar.md) diverges there, so **$r=0$ is a [naked singularity](../../../../../naked-singularity.md)**. It is a timelike [curvature singularity](../../../../../curvature-singularity.md), as the normal to nearby $r=\text{constant}$ [hypersurfaces](../../../../../hypersurface.md) has positive squared norm $g^{rr}=F$.
