<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A regular [hypersurface](../../../../hypersurface.md) $h=0$, with $dh\ne0$, is a [null hypersurface](../../../../null-hypersurface.md) if its normal is null:

$$
\boxed{g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu h\,\partial_\nu h=0\quad\text{on }h=0.}
$$

The raised normal is then also tangent to the [hypersurface](../../../../hypersurface.md), and its [induced metric](../../../../induced-metric.md) is degenerate. This is a local geometric property.

In an [asymptotically flat spacetime](../../../../asymptotically-flat-spacetime.md), choose its [future null infinity](../../../../future-null-infinity.md) $\mathcal I^+$. The [black hole](../../../../black-hole-split.md) region and future [event horizon](../../../../event-horizon.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathcal B=\mathcal M\setminus J^-(\mathcal I^+),\qquad \mathcal H^+=\partial J^-(\mathcal I^+).}
$$

Here $J^-(\mathcal I^+)$ is the [causal past](../../../../causal-past.md) of [future null infinity](../../../../future-null-infinity.md): the region from which a future-directed [causal curve](../../../../causal-curve.md) can escape to arbitrarily distant observers. An [event horizon](../../../../event-horizon.md) therefore depends on the global future [causal structure](../../../../causal-structure.md), and an arbitrary [null hypersurface](../../../../null-hypersurface.md) need not be an [event horizon](../../../../event-horizon.md).
