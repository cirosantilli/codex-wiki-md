<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A nonzero [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) has [hypersurface orthogonality](../../../../hypersurface-orthogonality.md) when it is locally normal to a family of [hypersurfaces](../../../../hypersurface.md). In terms of its associated [differential one-form](../../../../one-form.md) $K^\flat=g_{\mu\nu}K^\nu dx^\mu$, the [Frobenius theorem](../../../../frobenius-theorem.md) gives the equivalent conditions

$$
K^\flat=f\,dh,\qquad K^\flat\wedge dK^\flat=0,
\qquad K_{[\mu}\nabla_\nu K_{\rho]}=0,
$$

locally with nonzero $f$ and $dh$; square brackets denote [tensor index antisymmetrization](../../../../tensor-index-antisymmetrization.md).

All coefficients of the [Reissner-Nordstrom metric](../../../../reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md) are independent of $t$, and $K=\partial_t$ has constant coordinate components. Thus the [Lie derivative](../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) obeys $\mathcal L_Kg=\partial_tg=0$: $K$ is a [Killing vector field](../../../../killing-vector-field-split.md). Moreover,

$$
K^\flat=-F(r)\,dt,\qquad dK^\flat=-F'(r)\,dr\wedge dt,
\qquad \boxed{K^\flat\wedge dK^\flat=0.}
$$

Thus $K$ has [hypersurface orthogonality](../../../../hypersurface-orthogonality.md), with the $t=\text{constant}$ [hypersurfaces](../../../../hypersurface.md) providing the orthogonal family wherever this chart is regular. It is timelike where $F>0$, since $g(K,K)=-F$. Its [hypersurface orthogonality](../../../../hypersurface-orthogonality.md) also holds in a regular extension across a horizon; singular static [coordinates](../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md) do not invalidate the [Frobenius theorem](../../../../frobenius-theorem.md) test.
