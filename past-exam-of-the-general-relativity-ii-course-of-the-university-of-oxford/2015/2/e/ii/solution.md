<h1 id="2/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With $\tau$ the [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) of the massive [test particle](../../../../../test-particle.md), the [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) has squared norm $-1$:

$$
-1=-F\dot t^2+F^{-1}\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2.
$$

The [conserved quantities](../../../../../conserved-quantity.md) in part (i) give

$$
F\dot t=\frac1m\left(E-\frac{eQ}{r}\right),\qquad \dot\phi=\frac l{r^2}.
$$

Multiplying the normalization by $F$ and substituting gives the [charged-particle radial equation in Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](../../../../../charged-particle-radial-equation-in-reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md):

$$
\boxed{\dot r^2=\frac1{m^2}\left(E-\frac{eQ}{r}\right)^2-F(r)\left(1+\frac{l^2}{r^2}\right).}
$$

At a radial [turning point](../../../../../turning-point.md) $r_*>0$, the left side is zero, so

$$
F(r_*)\left(1+\frac{l^2}{r_*^2}\right)
=\frac1{m^2}\left(E-\frac{eQ}{r_*}\right)^2\ge0.
$$

The parenthesis on the left is strictly positive. Hence $F(r_*)\ge0$. The factorization in part (b) now yields

$$
\boxed{r_*\ge r_+\quad\text{or}\quad 0<r_*\le r_-.}
$$

**There is no radial [turning point](../../../../../turning-point.md) between the horizons.** Indeed, for $r_-<r<r_+$, the right-hand side of the radial equation is strictly positive, independently of the [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $e$ of the [test particle](../../../../../test-particle.md). This is the [charged-particle turning-point exclusion in Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](../../../../../charged-particle-turning-point-exclusion-in-reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md).

The inequalities are necessary, not a claim that a radial [turning point](../../../../../turning-point.md) exists for every $E,l,e$. At a regular horizon crossing a [timelike vector](../../../../../timelike-vector.md) cannot be tangent to a [null hypersurface](../../../../../null-hypersurface.md), so a massive [test particle](../../../../../test-particle.md) actually has $\dot r\ne0$ there. Algebraic equality at $r_\pm$ would require $E=eQ/r_\pm$ and does not describe a regular finite-[proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) radial [turning point](../../../../../turning-point.md) on the horizon. In the [Schwarzschild spacetime](../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md) limit $Q=0$, the positive-radius inner alternative disappears.
