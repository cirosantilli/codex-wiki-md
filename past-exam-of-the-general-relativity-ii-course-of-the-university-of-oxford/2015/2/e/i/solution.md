<h1 id="2/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the equatorial path, the [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is

$$
L=\frac12\left(-F\dot t^2+F^{-1}\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2\right)-\frac{eQ}{mr}\dot t.
$$

Both $t$ and $\phi$ are [cyclic coordinates](../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md). The [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) therefore conserve their [canonical momenta](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md):

$$
p_t=\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot t}=-F\dot t-\frac{eQ}{mr},\qquad
p_\phi=\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot\phi}=r^2\dot\phi.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{E=-mp_t=mF\dot t+\frac{eQ}{r},\qquad l=p_\phi=r^2\dot\phi,\qquad \dot E=\dot l=0.}
$$

Here $E$ is the total conserved [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) in the specified gauge of the [electromagnetic four-potential](../../../../../electromagnetic-four-potential.md). The paper's $l$ is the [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md); the physical [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $ml$. The equatorial choice is consistent with the $\theta$ [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md), since its angular force term contains $\sin\theta\cos\theta=0$ at $\theta=\pi/2$ and the [electromagnetic four-potential](../../../../../electromagnetic-four-potential.md) has no angular component.
