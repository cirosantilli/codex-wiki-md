For the three cases, distinguish a [curvature singularity](../../../curvature-singularity.md) from a [coordinate singularity](../../../coordinate-singularity.md). The [Reissner-Nordstrom Kretschmann scalar](../../../reissner-nordstrom-kretschmann-scalar.md) is

$$
\mathcal K=R_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}R^{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}
=\frac{48M^2}{r^6}-\frac{96MQ^2}{r^7}+\frac{56Q^4}{r^8}.
$$

It diverges at $r=0$ when either $M$ or $Q$ is nonzero. Any positive root of $F$ instead admits [Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md): set $v=t+\int dr/F$ to obtain

$$
ds^2=-F\,dv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2(d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2).
$$

The [determinant](../../../determinant.md) of its $(v,r)$ block is $-1$, including at $F=0$. Hence positive roots of $F$ are removable [coordinate singularities](../../../coordinate-singularity.md) of the static chart. The apparent degeneracy at the poles of the angular chart is also a [coordinate singularity](../../../coordinate-singularity.md).
