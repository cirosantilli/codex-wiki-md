↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [Conformal Killing vector field](../../../../../conformal-killing-vector-field.md), the same [metric compatibility](../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) calculation gives

$$
\mathcal L_Xg_{\mu\nu}=\nabla_\mu X_\nu+\nabla_\nu X_\mu=2\lambda g_{\mu\nu}.
$$

Thus its [Conformal Killing equation](../../../../../conformal-killing-equation.md) is $\nabla_{(\mu}X_{\nu)}=\lambda g_{\mu\nu}$. Taking the [tensor contraction](../../../../../tensor-contraction.md) with the [inverse metric](../../../../../inverse-metric.md), and using $g^{\mu\nu}g_{\mu\nu}=4$, determines the [conformal Killing factor](../../../../../conformal-killing-factor.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla_{(\mu}X_{\nu)}=\lambda g_{\mu\nu},\qquad \lambda=\frac14\nabla_\mu X^\mu.}
$$

Parentheses include the factor $1/2$ in [tensor index symmetrization](../../../../../tensor-index-symmetrization.md).
