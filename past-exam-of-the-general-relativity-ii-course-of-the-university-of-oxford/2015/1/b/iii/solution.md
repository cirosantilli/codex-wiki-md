<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) on [Minkowski spacetime](../../../../../minkowski-spacetime.md), the [Conformal Killing equation](../../../../../conformal-killing-equation.md) becomes

$$
\partial_\mu X_\nu+\partial_\nu X_\mu=2\lambda\eta_{\mu\nu}.
$$

Differentiate the equations with index pairs $(\nu,\rho)$, $(\mu,\rho)$ and $(\mu,\nu)$ by $\partial_\mu$, $\partial_\nu$ and $\partial_\rho$, respectively. Adding the first two and subtracting the third, [Clairaut's theorem](../../../../../symmetry-of-second-derivatives.md) yields the [flat conformal Killing integrability](../../../../../flat-conformal-killing-integrability.md) formula

$$
\partial_\mu\partial_\nu X_\rho
=\eta_{\nu\rho}\partial_\mu\lambda
+\eta_{\mu\rho}\partial_\nu\lambda
-\eta_{\mu\nu}\partial_\rho\lambda.
$$

Apply $\partial^\rho$ and use $\partial^\rho X_\rho=4\lambda$. With the [d'Alembert operator](../../../../../d-alembert-operator.md) $\Box=\eta^{\alpha\beta}\partial_\alpha\partial_\beta$, this gives

$$
4\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\lambda
=2\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\lambda-\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box\lambda,
\qquad
2\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\lambda=-\eta_{\mu\nu}\Box\lambda.
$$

A [tensor contraction](../../../../../tensor-contraction.md) with $\eta^{\mu\nu}$ gives $2\Box\lambda=-4\Box\lambda$, so $\Box\lambda=0$. Returning to the preceding equation proves the stronger statement

$$
\boxed{\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\lambda=0.}
$$

Thus the [conformal Killing factor](../../../../../conformal-killing-factor.md) is an [affine function](../../../../../affine-function.md), not an arbitrary solution of the [wave equation](../../../../../wave-equation-split.md).
