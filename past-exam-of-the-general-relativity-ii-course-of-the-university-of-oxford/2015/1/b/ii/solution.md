<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use an [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md) $s$ on the [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md), so its [tangent vector](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) $U$ obeys $\nabla_UU=0$. The [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md), [metric compatibility](../../../../../metric-compatibility.md), and the [Conformal Killing equation](../../../../../conformal-killing-equation.md) give

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{d}{ds}(X_\mu U^\mu)
&=U^\nu U^\mu\nabla_\nu X_\mu+X_\mu(\nabla_UU)^\mu\\
&=U^\nu U^\mu\nabla_{(\nu}X_{\mu)}
=\lambda g_{\mu\nu}U^\mu U^\nu.
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{ds}(X\cdot U)=\lambda\,g(U,U).}
$$

**It is conserved on every [null geodesic](../../../../../null-geodesic.md) with an [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md), but generally not on other [geodesics](../../../../../geodesic.md).** On a [timelike geodesic](../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) parametrized by [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md), $g(U,U)=-1$, so the derivative is $-\lambda$. A [Killing vector field](../../../../../killing-vector-field-split.md), or a [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md) on which $\lambda$ vanishes, supplies the exceptional conservation. This is the [conformal Killing geodesic identity](../../../../../conformal-killing-geodesic-identity.md).

For an arbitrary parameter with $\nabla_UU=fU$, the right-hand side acquires $f(X\cdot U)$. Thus even for a [null geodesic](../../../../../null-geodesic.md), the simple conserved expression presupposes an [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md).
