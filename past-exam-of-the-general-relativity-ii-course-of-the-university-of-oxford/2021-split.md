# 2021

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the general relativity II course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Complete solutions to the three questions of the Part C [General relativity II course of the University of Oxford](../general-relativity-ii-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) paper, sat on 1 June 2021. [Original released PDF, bundle pages 63–65; paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/oxford_physics_past_papers_2/response/2340696/attach/9/Physics%20Papers%202020.pdf?cookie_passthrough=1#page=63). The bundle filename says 2020, but the paper's cover explicitly says Trinity Term 2021. The [metric signature](../metric-signature.md) is $(-,+,+,+)$ and the [speed of light](../speed-of-light.md) is set to one. All questions and both Roman-numbered parts of question 1(d) are covered.
