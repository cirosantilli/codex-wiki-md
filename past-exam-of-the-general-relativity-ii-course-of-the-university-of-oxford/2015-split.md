# 2015

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the general relativity II course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source: [released examination PDF, bundle pages 92–95](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/oxford_physics_past_papers_2/response/2340696/attach/3/Physics%20Papers%202014.pdf?cookie_passthrough=1#page=92).

We use [metric signature](../metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$ and set the [speed of light](../speed-of-light.md) to one. In question 2, $M$ and $Q$ have the geometric normalization of the [Reissner-Nordstrom metric](../reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md), with $M>0$ and $r>0$. Every numbered part is treated, including all three cases in question 2(b).
