<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [plane gravitational wave in linearized gravity](../../../../plane-gravitational-wave-in-linearized-gravity.md), the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\partial_\kappa\tilde h_{\mu\nu}=n_\kappa\tilde h'_{\mu\nu}$. The [Lorenz gauge in linearized gravity](../../../../lorenz-gauge-in-linearized-gravity.md), called wave gauge in the paper, imposes zero [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) of the [trace-reversed metric perturbation](../../../../trace-reversed-metric-perturbation.md). Hence

$$
0=\partial^\mu\overline{\tilde h}_{\mu\nu}
=n^\mu\tilde h'_{\mu\nu}-\frac12n_\nu\tilde h',
\qquad
\boxed{n^\mu\tilde h'_{\mu\nu}=\frac12\tilde h'n_\nu.}
$$

The [wave equation](../../../../wave-equation-split.md) itself holds for any smooth profile of $u$, because $\Box\tilde h_{\mu\nu}=(n^\kappa n_\kappa)\tilde h''_{\mu\nu}=0$ for the constant [null vector](../../../../null-vector.md) $n$.
