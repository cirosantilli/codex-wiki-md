<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $D=u^a\nabla_a$, $\theta=\nabla_a u^a$ for the [expansion scalar](../../../../expansion-scalar.md), $a_b=u^a\nabla_a u_b$ for the [four-acceleration](../../../../four-acceleration.md), and $P_b{}^c=\delta_b{}^c+u_bu^c$ for the [spatial projector of a timelike congruence](../../../../spatial-projector-of-a-timelike-congruence.md). The normalization of the [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) gives $u^ba_b=0$ and $u^b\nabla_a u_b=0$. The [product rule](../../../../product-rule.md) and [metric compatibility](../../../../metric-compatibility.md) give the [covariant derivative](../../../../covariant-derivative.md) of the [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) [stress-energy tensor](../../../../stress-energy-tensor-split.md):

$$
F_b:=\nabla^aT_{ab}
=u_b\bigl[D(\rho+p)+(\rho+p)\theta\bigr]+(\rho+p)a_b+\nabla_b p.
$$

Its [tensor contraction](../../../../tensor-contraction.md) with the [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) is

$$
u^bF_b=-D\rho-(\rho+p)\theta,
$$

and its projection with the [spatial projector of a timelike congruence](../../../../spatial-projector-of-a-timelike-congruence.md) is

$$
P_b{}^cF_c=(\rho+p)a_b+P_b{}^c\nabla_c p.
$$

Thus [stress-energy conservation](../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) implies the [relativistic perfect-fluid energy equation](../../../../relativistic-perfect-fluid-energy-equation.md) and the [relativistic Euler equation](../../../../relativistic-euler-equation.md):

$$
\boxed{u^a\nabla_a\rho+(\rho+p)\nabla_a u^a=0,\qquad
(\rho+p)u^a\nabla_a u_b+(g_{ab}+u_au_b)\nabla^a p=0.}
$$

Conversely, the two displayed equations set both projections of $F_b$ to zero. Since $F_b=P_b{}^cF_c-u_bu^cF_c$, they imply $F_b=0$. This establishes equivalence, including points where $\rho+p=0$, without dividing by that quantity.
