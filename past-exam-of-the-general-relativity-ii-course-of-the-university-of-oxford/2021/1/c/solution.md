<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The sign of $T_{ab}X^aX^b$ is unchanged by a nonzero rescaling of the [timelike vector](../../../../timelike-vector.md) $X$. Normalize it to a [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) $W$ and, after reversing its direction if necessary, write $W=\gamma(u+w)$, where $u\cdot w=0$, $w\cdot w=s\in[0,1)$ and $\gamma=(1-s)^{-1/2}$ is the [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md). For the [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md),

$$
T_{ab}W^aW^b=(\rho+p)\gamma^2-p
=\rho+(\rho+p)(\gamma^2-1).
$$

Taking $W=u$ requires $\rho\ge0$. Allowing the [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md) to become arbitrarily large requires $\rho+p\ge0$; otherwise sufficiently fast observers measure a negative value. Conversely, these two inequalities make both terms in the last expression nonnegative. Therefore the [weak energy condition for a perfect fluid](../../../../weak-energy-condition-for-a-perfect-fluid.md) is exactly

$$
\boxed{\rho\ge0,\qquad \rho+p\ge0.}
$$

Property (1) is the [weak energy condition](../../../../weak-energy-condition.md): **every physical observer measures nonnegative local [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md)**, namely $T_{ab}W^aW^b$ for their normalized [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) $W$. It allows negative [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) down to $p=-\rho$.
