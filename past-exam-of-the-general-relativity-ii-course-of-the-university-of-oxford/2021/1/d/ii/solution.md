<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the prescribed [synchronous coordinates](../../../../../synchronous-coordinates.md), the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) satisfy

$$
\Gamma^a{}_{tt}=\frac12g^{ab}(2\partial_tg_{bt}-\partial_b g_{tt})=0.
$$

Consequently the [four-acceleration](../../../../../four-acceleration.md) of $u=\partial_t$ vanishes. For a spatial index $i$, $u_i=0$, so the spatial component of the [relativistic Euler equation](../../../../../relativistic-euler-equation.md) becomes

$$
0=(\rho+p)a_i+(\delta_i{}^a+u_i u^a)\partial_a p=\partial_i p.
$$

On a connected coordinate neighbourhood,

$$
\boxed{p=p(t).}
$$

A nonzero [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) is allowed, but its spatial [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) must vanish if the fluid's [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) follows these freely falling coordinate lines. No division by $p$ or $\rho+p$ is needed.
