<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [stress-energy conservation](../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) and symmetry of the [stress-energy tensor](../../../../stress-energy-tensor-split.md). The [Killing equation](../../../../killing-equation.md) for the [Killing vector field](../../../../killing-vector-field-split.md) $V$ then gives the [stress-energy current from a Killing vector](../../../../stress-energy-current-from-a-killing-vector.md):

$$
\nabla^aJ_a=(\nabla^aT_{ab})V^b+T_{ab}\nabla^aV^b
=\frac12T^{ab}(\nabla_aV_b+\nabla_bV_a)=0.
$$

Hence $\boxed{\nabla^aJ_a=0}$. The assumption of [stress-energy conservation](../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md), supplied by the equations of motion in part (a), is essential; the [Killing equation](../../../../killing-equation.md) alone only eliminates the second term.
