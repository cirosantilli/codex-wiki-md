<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $d\Omega_2^2=d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\varphi^2$, the standard [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) on the [unit sphere](../../../../unit-sphere.md). From $t=v+m/\lambda$ one has $dt=dv-m\lambda^{-2}d\lambda$. The apparently divergent terms cancel:

$$
\boxed{g=-\lambda^2dv^2+2m\,dv\,d\lambda+m^2d\Omega_2^2.}
$$

The $(v,\lambda)$ [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) block has [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $-m^2\ne0$, independently of $\lambda$. Its coefficients are [real analytic](../../../../real-analytic-function.md), so this formula defines a regular [Lorentzian metric](../../../../lorentzian-metric.md) on

$$
\boxed{M_1=\mathbb R_v\times\mathbb R_\lambda\times S^2.}
$$

The polar-coordinate degeneracies at the poles are handled by ordinary [two-sphere](../../../../two-sphere.md) charts. Thus $\lambda=0$ is a [coordinate singularity](../../../../coordinate-singularity.md) of the original chart and is crossed by this [spacetime extension](../../../../spacetime-extension.md).
