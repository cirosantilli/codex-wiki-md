<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The inverse of the $(v,\lambda)$ [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) block is

$$
(g^{AB})=\begin{pmatrix}0&1/m\\1/m&\lambda^2/m^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The normal [covector](../../../../covector.md) $d\lambda$ has squared norm $\lambda^2/m^2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\{\lambda=\lambda_0\}\text{ is timelike for }\lambda_0\ne0,
\quad\{\lambda=0\}\text{ is null}.}
$$

More explicitly, the induced [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) is $-\lambda_0^2dv^2+m^2d\Omega_2^2$; at zero its sole zero direction is $\partial_v$. The coefficients are independent of $v$, so $K=\partial_v$ is a [Killing vector field](../../../../killing-vector-field-split.md). On $\lambda=0$,

$$
K^2=-\lambda^2=0,\qquad
K^\flat=-\lambda^2dv+m\,d\lambda=m\,d\lambda.
$$

Hence $K$ is both tangent and normal to this [null hypersurface](../../../../null-hypersurface.md), proving it is a [Killing horizon](../../../../killing-horizon.md). The definition of [surface gravity](../../../../surface-gravity.md) is $\nabla_KK=\kappa K$ on the [Killing horizon](../../../../killing-horizon.md), or equivalently $d(K^2)=-2\kappa K^\flat$. Since $d(K^2)=-2\lambda\,d\lambda$ vanishes there while $K^\flat\ne0$,

$$
\boxed{\kappa=0.}
$$

Thus this is a [degenerate Killing horizon](../../../../degenerate-killing-horizon.md), and $v$ is an [affine parameter](../../../../affine-parameter.md) on each of its [null geodesics](../../../../null-geodesic.md).

For a concrete [horizon frequency comparison](../../../../horizon-frequency-comparison.md), fix the same angular position for a narrow high-[frequency](../../../../frequency.md) [light](../../../../light.md) beam and two freely falling radial observers. Work in [geometric optics](../../../../geometrical-optics-split.md), with the beam's affinely transported [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) tangent to the [Killing horizon](../../../../killing-horizon.md), $k=qK$. Here $q>0$ is constant because $\nabla_KK=0$. Choose the observers as translates of one [timelike geodesic](../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) by the [isometry](../../../../isometry.md) generated by $K$, crossing at $v_1$ and $v_2$. Give them the same positive [Killing energy](../../../../killing-energy.md) per unit [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md), $E=-K\cdot W$. At their crossings their normalized [four-velocities](../../../../four-velocity.md) have

$$
W^\lambda=-\frac Em,\qquad W^v=\frac1{2E},\qquad
W^\theta=W^\varphi=0,
$$

which satisfy $g(W,W)=2mW^vW^\lambda=-1$. They can be realized by local [geodesics](../../../../geodesic.md) with precisely these initial data. The measured [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) is

$$
\omega=-k\cdot W=qE,\qquad
\boxed{\frac{\omega_2}{\omega_1}=1.}
$$

To connect this measurement to [surface gravity](../../../../surface-gravity.md), if $\nabla_KK=\kappa K$ with constant $\kappa$, affine transport of $k=q(v)K$ would give $dq/dv=-\kappa q$. For observers related by the same [Killing vector field](../../../../killing-vector-field-split.md) translation, $-K\cdot W$ agrees at the crossings, giving $\omega_2/\omega_1=e^{-\kappa(v_2-v_1)}$. Here there is **no accumulated [redshift](../../../../redshift.md) along the [Killing horizon](../../../../killing-horizon.md)** for these equally prepared observers. Arbitrary observers with different [four-velocities](../../../../four-velocity.md) can still measure different [frequencies](../../../../frequency.md) through their different [Killing energies](../../../../killing-energy.md).
