# Controlled-transfer gate

↑ **Parent:** [Liquid-state NMR quantum computing](liquid-state-nmr-quantum-computing.md)

A controlled [rotation gate](rotation-gate.md) followed by computational-basis [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) mixes the two target populations in the control-one subspace. If they are $c,d$, their sum is preserved and their difference is multiplied by $\cos\theta$. The operation is not unitary after dephasing. On a two-qubit diagonal state, the population of $|00\rangle$ is unchanged when either qubit is the target, because the other control bit is zero.
