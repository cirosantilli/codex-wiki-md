# Sandage-Loeb test

↑ **Parent:** [Redshift drift](redshift-drift.md)

The [Sandage-Loeb test](sandage-loeb-test.md) seeks the [spectroscopic velocity drift](spectroscopic-velocity-drift.md) of stable cosmological absorption features over an observer-time baseline. Many [Lyman-alpha forest](lyman-alpha-forest.md) lines in bright distant [quasars](quasar.md) can be combined, but wavelength calibration, instrumental stability and peculiar accelerations must be controlled below the tiny cosmological signal. The absorption-template proposal is developed in [the original 1998 redshift-drift study](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9802122).
