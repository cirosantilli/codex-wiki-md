# Differentiability does not imply continuous derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Differentiability](differentiability.md)

The function $f(x)=x^2\sin(1/x^2)$ for $x\ne0$, extended by $f(0)=0$, is [differentiable](differentiable-function.md) everywhere. At zero its [derivative](derivative.md) is zero by the difference quotient, while away from zero $f'(x)=2x\sin(1/x^2)-2x^{-1}\cos(1/x^2)$ is unbounded near zero. Differentiability of a function is therefore weaker than continuity of its derivative.
