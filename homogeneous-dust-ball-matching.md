# Homogeneous dust-ball matching

↑ **Parent:** [Oppenheimer-Snyder model](oppenheimer-snyder-model.md)

At the boundary of an [Oppenheimer-Snyder cloud](oppenheimer-snyder-cloud.md), its [areal radius](areal-radius.md) is $R=a\sin\chi_0$. Matching to a [Schwarzschild metric](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) gives $M=4\pi\rho R^3/3=a_{\max}\sin^3\chi_0/2$. Thus $\sin^2\chi_0=2M/R_0$ and $a_{\max}=\sqrt{R_0^3/(2M)}$. The boundary is a radial [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) with specific [Killing energy](killing-energy.md) $\sqrt{1-2M/R_0}=\cos\chi_0$. Its radial equation is $\dot R^2=2M/R-2M/R_0$. The induced metric and the angular [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md) agree on both sides, so no surface layer is required.
