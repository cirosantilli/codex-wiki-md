# Combinatory logic

↑ **Parent:** [Untyped lambda calculus](untyped-lambda-calculus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatory_logic)

Combinatory logic describes computation by application and fixed combinator constants, eliminating bound variables through [bracket abstraction](bracket-abstraction.md). Its intensional weak reduction does not identify every pair of terms that becomes beta-equivalent after translation into [lambda calculus](lambda-calculus.md).
