# Geometrization conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrizable three-manifold](geometrizable-three-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometrization_conjecture)

Every closed orientable [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) admits a prime and [torus](torus.md) decomposition into pieces modeled on the eight [Thurston geometries](thurston-geometry.md). In particular, a closed irreducible orientable [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) with [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) $\mathbb Z^3$ carries a flat metric. The [Bieberbach theorem](bieberbach-theorem.md) then identifies it with the three-torus because an abelian crystallographic group has trivial linear holonomy. [Simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) closed [three-manifolds](3-manifold.md) are three-spheres by the [Poincaré conjecture](poincare-conjecture.md).
