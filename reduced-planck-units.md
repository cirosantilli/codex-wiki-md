# Reduced Planck units

↑ **Parent:** [Planck units](planck-units.md)

Here the [reduced Planck mass](reduced-planck-mass.md) $M_{\rm Pl}=(8\pi G)^{-1/2}$ is one, so the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) for an energy density $\rho$ is $3H^2=\rho$. This differs by a factor of $\sqrt{8\pi}$ from setting the ordinary [Planck mass](planck-mass.md) to one; the convention must be stated in inflationary amplitudes.
