# Solution of a linear system

↑ **Parent:** [System of linear equations](system-of-linear-equations.md)

A [solution of a linear system](solution-of-a-linear-system.md) $Ax=b$ is a [vector](vector.md) $x$ whose image under the coefficient [matrix](matrix.md) $A$ is $b$. Its [solution set](solution-set.md) can be empty or have one or many elements; an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md) gives the unique solution $x=A^{-1}b$.
