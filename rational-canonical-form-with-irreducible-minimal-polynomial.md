# Rational canonical form with irreducible minimal polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Rational canonical form](rational-canonical-form.md)

Let $A$ act on a nonzero [finite-dimensional vector space](finite-dimensional-vector-space.md) over a [field](field.md) $F$, with [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) an [irreducible polynomial](irreducible-polynomial.md) $f$ of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $d$. The nonconstant [invariant factors of a linear operator](invariant-factors-of-a-linear-operator.md) all divide $f$, so each equals $f$. Their degrees sum to the [dimension of a vector space](dimension-vector-space.md), giving $d\mid n$ and [rational canonical form](rational-canonical-form.md) consisting of $n/d$ copies of the [companion matrix](companion-matrix.md) $C_f$. Equivalently, make the vector space a [module](module-mathematics.md) over the [field](field.md) $F[X]/(f)$ by letting $X$ act as $A$; a [basis](basis.md) over this [field](field.md) gives the same block decomposition.
