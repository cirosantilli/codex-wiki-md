# Momentum-space propagator in a uniform force

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum-mechanical propagator in a constant force](quantum-mechanical-propagator-in-a-constant-force.md)

For potential $V(x)=\alpha x$, [momentum](momentum-split.md) follows a characteristic with $\dot p=-\alpha$. The retarded [quantum-mechanical propagator](quantum-mechanical-propagator.md) translates its [momentum](momentum-split.md) argument and accumulates the kinetic [action](action.md) along that characteristic. Its apparent division by $\alpha$ is removable on the delta-function support; at zero [force](force-split.md) it becomes the free diagonal [momentum](momentum-split.md) kernel. [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) gives the usual constant-force position kernel with a cubic-time [action](action.md) term.
