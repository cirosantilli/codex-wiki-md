# Redox titration

↑ **Parent:** [Redox](redox.md)

A [redox titration](redox-titration.md) adds a measured [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of one [reactant](reactant.md) to determine another through a known [redox](redox.md) [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md). The [burette](burette.md) reading becomes an amount through [concentration](concentration.md); the balanced [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md) supplies the amount ratio. A colour endpoint additionally needs a chemical explanation, as in the [permanganate-iron titration](permanganate-iron-titration.md).

The useful bridge is an [electron](electron.md) inventory. If each sample entity supplies $z_A$ [electrons](electron.md) and each [titrant](titrant.md) entity accepts $z_T$, matching the formal exchanged [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of [electrons](electron.md) gives

$$
z_A n_A=z_T n_T,\qquad n_A=\frac{z_T}{z_A}c_TV_{\mathrm{eq}}.
$$

Here $c_T$ is the [titrant](titrant.md) [molar concentration](molar-concentration.md) and $V_{\mathrm{eq}}$ the [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) attributable to the sample at the [equivalence point](equivalence-point.md). These are net [half-reaction](half-reaction.md) counts, not a claim that free [electrons](electron.md) accumulate in the flask. The [redox](redox.md) conversion must be sufficiently complete and selective for the stated [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) to identify $n_A$.

This is why a small visible signal can reveal a much larger invisible inventory: the signal locates a stopping point, while [titrant standardization](titrant-standardization.md) and [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) turn delivery into [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md). The [titration endpoint](titration-endpoint.md) estimates the [equivalence point](equivalence-point.md); repeatable colour alone establishes neither correct [titrant](titrant.md) [concentration](concentration.md) nor chemical selectivity. The [permanganate-iron endpoint and blank model](permanganate-iron-endpoint-and-blank-model.md) separates these assumptions.
