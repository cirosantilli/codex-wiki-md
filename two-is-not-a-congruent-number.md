# Two is not a congruent number

↑ **Parent:** [Congruent number](congruent-number.md)

A primitive [Pythagorean triple](pythagorean-triple.md) of area twice a square would satisfy $mn(m-n)(m+n)=2s^2$. Pairwise coprimality makes the odd factors squares and the even factor twice a square. The possibility $m$ even is excluded modulo eight. Thus $m=a^2,n=2b^2,m+n=c^2,m-n=d^2$. The coprime integers $u=(c+d)/2,v=(c-d)/2$ have $uv=b^2$, hence are themselves squares, and $a^2=u^2+v^2$ contradicts [fourth-power sum cannot be a square](fourth-power-sum-cannot-be-a-square.md). Clearing denominators gives the assertion for rational triangles.
