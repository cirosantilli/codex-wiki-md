# Fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A [fluid](fluid.md) is a material that flows under sustained [shear stress](shear-stress.md) and cannot maintain a static [shear stress](shear-stress.md) in its fluid state. [Liquids](liquid.md) and [gases](gas.md) are the usual examples. A [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) has [deviatoric stress](deviatoric-stress.md) proportional to its [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md), whereas more general fluid [constitutive equations](constitutive-equation.md) can be nonlinear or have memory. In a [porous medium](porous-medium.md), the [fluid](fluid.md) occupies connected voids within a load-bearing [solid](solid.md) framework. The important distinction is the response to a continuing tangential [force](force.md): an ordinary elastic [solid](solid.md) can settle into a fixed [deformation](deformation-mechanics.md), while an ordinary [fluid](fluid.md) continues to deform. A [fluid](fluid.md) can nevertheless be at rest when no unbalanced [shear stress](shear-stress.md) drives it. [MIT, Fluid Dynamics of the Atmosphere and Ocean, lecture 3, pp. 4–5](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-800-fluid-dynamics-of-the-atmosphere-and-ocean-fall-2004/7bb9e945405534b36776cbe404a8f839_lecture3.pdf)

For a worked example, place a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) between parallel plates separated by $h=1\,\mathrm{mm}$, keep the lower plate fixed and move the upper one at $U=0.10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. With [no-slip boundary conditions](no-slip-boundary-condition.md), [steady flow](steady-flow.md), no imposed [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) and negligible edge effects, [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) has $u(y)=Uy/h$. For [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) $\mu=0.001\,\mathrm{Pa\,s}$, the [shear stress](shear-stress.md) magnitude is

$$
\tau=\mu\frac{du}{dy}=\mu\frac{U}{h}=0.10\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$

The [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md) describes neighbouring layers sliding at different speeds. Doubling $U$ doubles the required [shear stress](shear-stress.md); maintaining the same [velocity](velocity.md) keeps producing [deformation](deformation-mechanics.md), rather than fixing a final shape. This gives [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) a concrete meaning: resistance to a rate of motion, not a prohibition on motion.
