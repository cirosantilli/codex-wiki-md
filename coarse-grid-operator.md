# Coarse-grid operator

↑ **Parent:** [Multigrid method](multigrid-method-split.md)

A [coarse-grid operator](coarse-grid-operator.md) represents the equation solved for an error correction on the coarser level of a [multigrid method](multigrid-method-split.md). It may be obtained by rediscretizing the differential equation at the coarse spacing, or through the [Galerkin coarse-grid operator](galerkin-coarse-grid-operator.md) formula displayed above. Here $R$ transfers residuals to the coarse grid and $P$ transfers coarse corrections to the fine grid. The right-side and operator scalings must be consistent with the chosen transfer convention.
