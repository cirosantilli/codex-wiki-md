# Cylindrical micelle geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Micelle](micelle.md)

For a long cylindrical [micelle](micelle.md) with negligible end caps, incompressible tail [volume](volume.md) $v$ and head-group [area](surface-area.md) $a_0$ give $v/a_0=R/2$. The geometric requirement $R\leq\ell_c$ bounds the [amphiphile packing parameter](amphiphile-packing-parameter.md) by $1/2$. The conventional cylindrical range is approximately $1/3\lesssim v/(a_0\ell_c)\lesssim1/2$; aggregate transitions also depend on interactions and chain conformations.
