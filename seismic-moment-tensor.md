# Seismic moment tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave](elastic-wave.md)

A localized zero-resultant [seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md) represents the leading force-dipole part of an [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md) source through the displayed equivalent [body force](body-force.md). For a prescribed displacement jump $b_i$ across a surface with normal $n_j$, its moment density is $c_{ijkl}b_kn_l$, where $c$ is the [elastic stiffness tensor](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md). Spatial integration gives the point-source moment when the source is small compared with the wavelengths.
