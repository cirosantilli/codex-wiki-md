# Action precondition axiom

↑ **Parent:** [SATPlan](satplan.md)

A clause or formula ensuring a selected action occurrence has all of its [preconditions](precondition.md) satisfied at the current time. It is one part of a [plan](plan.md) encoding; action effects, persistence, initial conditions and goals require additional constraints.
