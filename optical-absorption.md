# Optical absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Light](light.md)

Transfer of [energy](energy-split.md) from an optical electromagnetic wave into matter. Electronic absorption depends on transition [matrix elements](matrix-element.md), occupied and empty states, and the [joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md). Bound [excitons](exciton.md) can create resonances below an independent-particle continuum edge.
