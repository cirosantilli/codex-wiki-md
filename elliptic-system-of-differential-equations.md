# Elliptic system of differential equations

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic differential operator](elliptic-differential-operator.md)

A square system whose entries have common maximal order $N$ is elliptic when its matrix [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) is invertible at every nonzero real frequency. Unlike scalar operators, a system can be first-order elliptic in three dimensions: the [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) give $A(\xi)=\sum_j\sigma_j\xi_j$ with $A(\xi)^2=|\xi|^2\mathbf1$.
