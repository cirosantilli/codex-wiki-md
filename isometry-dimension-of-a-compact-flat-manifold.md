# Isometry dimension of a compact flat manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Flat manifold](flat-manifold.md)

On a compact [flat Riemannian manifold](flat-manifold.md), the integrated [Bochner identity for Killing vector fields](bochner-identity-for-killing-vector-fields.md) makes every [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) parallel. Parallel vector fields are exactly the fixed vectors of the linear holonomy. Thus the six orientable closed flat three-manifold types have identity-component isometry dimensions three for the torus, one for each cyclic nontrivial holonomy, and zero for the [Hantzsche-Wendt manifold](hantzsche-wendt-manifold.md).
