# Gauge-invariant Maxwell stress-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical stress-energy tensor of the Maxwell field](canonical-stress-energy-tensor-of-the-maxwell-field.md)

In signature $+---$, the [Maxwell Lagrangian](maxwell-lagrangian.md) yields

$$
\Theta^\mu{}_{\nu}=-F^{\mu\rho}F_{\nu\rho}+\tfrac14\delta^\mu{}_{\nu}F_{\rho\sigma}F^{\rho\sigma}.
$$

It is symmetric after raising its second index, [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md), and conserved on the source-free [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations.md). Its energy density is $\frac12(\mathbf E^2+\mathbf B^2)$ and its energy flux is the [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md). Its difference from the [canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md) is an antisymmetric superpotential divergence plus an equation-of-motion term, so integrated charges coincide when the constraints and vanishing boundary terms are imposed.
