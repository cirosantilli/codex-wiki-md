# Rotating half-wave plate frequency shift

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational Doppler effect](rotational-doppler-effect.md)

A rotating ideal [half-wave plate](half-wave-plate.md) flips circular [polarization](polarization-waves.md). The spin change has magnitude $2\hbar$ per [photon](photon.md), so its mechanical [energy](energy-split.md) exchange produces a [frequency](frequency.md) shift of magnitude $2|\Omega|$. For Jones convention $H(\theta)=R(\theta)\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)R(-\theta)$ and $Q=\operatorname{diag}(1,i)$, the combination $QH(\theta)Q=e^{i2\theta\sigma_x}$. Acting on $(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$ gives $e^{i2\theta}$; with time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$ and $\theta=\Omega t$, the output [frequency](frequency.md) is $\omega-2\Omega$. The opposite input eigenpolarization reverses the shift.
