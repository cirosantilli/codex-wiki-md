# Fish

↑ **Parent:** [Animal](animal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish)

[Fish](fish.md) are aquatic [animals](animal.md); their feeding roles depend on their diet and life stage. Small and large [fish](fish.md) in the illustrative [food web](food-web.md) are generic groups chosen to distinguish two feeding positions, rather than a measured relationship between named [species](species.md).
