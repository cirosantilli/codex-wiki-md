# Equipartition for a homogeneous potential

↑ **Parent:** [Equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md)

For a classical canonical coordinate with confining [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $U=A|x|^s$, $A>0$ and $s>0$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) in the [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) gives $\langle xU'(x)\rangle=k_BT$. Homogeneity then gives $\langle U\rangle=k_BT/s$. The usual quadratic contribution $k_BT/2$ is the special case $s=2$.
