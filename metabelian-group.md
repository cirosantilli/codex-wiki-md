# Metabelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Solvable group](solvable-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metabelian_group)

A group is metabelian when its [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is [abelian](abelian-group.md), equivalently when its derived length is at most two.
