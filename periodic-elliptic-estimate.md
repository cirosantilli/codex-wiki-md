# Periodic elliptic estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev derivative estimate](sobolev-derivative-estimate.md)

If a periodic function $u$ has zero mean, its nonzero Fourier frequencies give

$$
\|u\|_{H^{s+2}}\leq C\|-\Delta u\|_{H^s}.
$$

Thus inversion of the Laplacian on mean-zero periodic functions gains two [Sobolev derivatives](sobolev-space-split.md).
