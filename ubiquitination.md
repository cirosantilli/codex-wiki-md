# Ubiquitination

↑ **Parent:** [Ubiquitin](ubiquitin.md)

[Ubiquitination](ubiquitination.md) covalently attaches [ubiquitin](ubiquitin.md) to a substrate, usually through successive action of a [ubiquitin-activating enzyme](ubiquitin-activating-enzyme.md), [ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme](ubiquitin-conjugating-enzyme.md) and [ubiquitin ligase](ubiquitin-ligase.md). The modification can change degradation, localization or signalling; attachment alone does not specify which outcome occurs.
