<h1 id="prekopa-leindler-inequality">Prékopa–Leindler inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue integration](lebesgue-integration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prékopa–Leindler_inequality)

If $0<\theta<1$ and nonnegative [measurable functions](measurable-function.md) satisfy $h((1-\theta)x+\theta y)\geq f(x)^{1-\theta}g(y)^\theta$ for all $x,y\in\mathbb R^n$, then their [Lebesgue integrals](lebesgue-integral.md) obey

$$
\int h\geq\left(\int f\right)^{1-\theta}\left(\int g\right)^\theta.
$$

The [quantile derivative identity](quantile-derivative-identity.md) and [monotone substitution inequality](monotone-substitution-inequality.md) prove the one-dimensional case; [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) extends it by induction. It implies the [Brunn–Minkowski inequality](brunn-minkowski-theorem.md).
