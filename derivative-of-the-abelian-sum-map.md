# Derivative of the Abelian sum map

↑ **Parent:** [Abel map of an algebraic curve](abel-map-of-an-algebraic-curve.md)

For an effective [divisor on an algebraic curve](divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) $D$ on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md), the [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md) to its [symmetric product of a curve](symmetric-product-of-a-curve.md) is $H^0(\mathcal O_D(D))$. The derivative of the [Abelian sum map](abel-map-of-an-algebraic-curve.md) is the [connecting homomorphism](connecting-homomorphism.md) of $0\to\mathcal O_X\to\mathcal O_X(D)\to\mathcal O_D(D)\to0$. Its dual under [Serre duality](serre-duality.md) is restriction of [holomorphic differential forms](holomorphic-differential-form.md) to $D$, including all jets at repeated points. Hence its rank is $\deg D+1-h^0(\mathcal O_X(D))$, and its image annihilates $H^0(K_X(-D))$.
