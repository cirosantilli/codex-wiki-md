# Sorgenfrey plane

↑ **Parent:** [Lower limit topology](lower-limit-topology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorgenfrey_plane)

The Sorgenfrey plane is the [product topology](product-topology.md) on two copies of the [Sorgenfrey line](lower-limit-topology.md). It is separable because $\mathbb Q^2$ meets every nonempty basic rectangle $[a,b)\times[c,d)$. Its antidiagonal $\{(x,-x):x\in\mathbb R\}$ is an uncountable discrete subspace: $[x,x+\epsilon)\times[-x,-x+\epsilon)$ meets it only at $(x,-x)$. In a discrete space every dense subset is the whole space, so this subspace is not separable. Thus separability need not pass to subspaces of nonmetrizable spaces.
