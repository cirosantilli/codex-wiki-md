# Finite-time extinction in a sublinear Bernoulli equation

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli differential equation](bernoulli-differential-equation.md)

For $y'=y-Ay^p$, $A>0$ and $0<p<1$, put $u=y^{1-p}$ while $y>0$. Then $u'=(1-p)(u-A)$. If $0<y_0<A^{1/(1-p)}$, $u$ decreases to zero at the displayed finite time. The positive branch joins the zero solution with zero first derivative, giving a nonnegative forward solution. The equilibrium at zero is one-sided stable even though the vector field is not locally [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) there; the positive equilibrium is unstable.
