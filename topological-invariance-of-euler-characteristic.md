# Topological invariance of Euler characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md)

A [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) preserves every [homology group](homology-group.md), hence the [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) wherever the finite rational [Betti number](betti-number.md) sum is defined. In fact, [homotopy invariance of homology](homotopy-invariance-of-homology.md) makes [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) invariant under [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md). Thus different finite [CW complex](cw-complex.md) structures on the same space have equal alternating cell counts.[Algebraic Topology, §2.2, p. 146, homotopy-type independence immediately before Theorem 2.44](algebraic-topology-by-allen-hatcher.md)
