# One-loop sextic cancellation in quartic Yukawa theory

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic Yukawa interaction in three spacetime dimensions](quartic-yukawa-interaction-in-three-spacetime-dimensions.md)

With zero scalar sextic coupling, the only potentially logarithmically divergent one-loop six-scalar vertex has three quartic Yukawa vertices on a [fermion loop](fermion-loop.md). Its leading numerator is $(\not k)^3=k^2\not k$, whose [trace](matrix-trace.md) vanishes. A mass insertion or external [momentum](momentum-split.md) lowers the degree and makes the integral convergent. This is not vanishing of every odd [gamma-matrix trace](gamma-matrix-trace.md): for $\gamma^0=\sigma_3$, $\gamma^1=i\sigma_1$, $\gamma^2=i\sigma_2$, one has $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2)=-2i$. No one-loop sextic [counterterm](counterterm.md) is needed, although a finite six-point vertex may remain.
