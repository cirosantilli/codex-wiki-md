# Action exclusion axiom

↑ **Parent:** [SATPlan](satplan.md)

A condition forbidding a conflicting pair of action occurrences in one layer. A serial [SATPlan](satplan.md) encoding excludes every distinct pair; parallel encodings exclude the pairs disallowed by their concurrency semantics. Excluding two simultaneous pickups can enforce a one-hand resource restriction even if both individual [precondition](precondition.md) sets hold.
