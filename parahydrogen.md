# Parahydrogen

↑ **Parent:** [Spin isomers of hydrogen](spin-isomers-of-hydrogen.md)

[Parahydrogen](parahydrogen.md) uses the antisymmetric [spin-one-half singlet state](spin-one-half-singlet-state.md),

$$
\chi_{00}=\frac{|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle-|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Here $I_{\rm n}$ labels total [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md); it is not the [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$ in the [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) formula. There is one nuclear-spin state for every rotational [quantum state](quantum-state.md). The lowest allowed rotational level is $J=0$, and the next is $J=2$. The description “opposite spins” alone is insufficient: the relative minus sign distinguishes this [singlet state](singlet-state.md) from the zero-projection member of the [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md).
