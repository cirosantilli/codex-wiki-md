# Pi-acceptor ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [pi-acceptor ligand](pi-acceptor-ligand.md) has a suitable unoccupied orbital for [pi backbonding](pi-backbonding.md). Stabilization of metal $t_{2g}$-derived levels increases the usual [octahedral splitting](octahedral-splitting.md).
