# Kolmogorov refined similarity hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Internal intermittency](internal-intermittency.md)

[Refined similarity](kolmogorov-refined-similarity-hypothesis.md) writes $\Delta v=(r\epsilon_r)^{1/3}V$ with universal normalized conditional statistics at high local [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md). Then $S_p=C_pr^{p/3}\langle\epsilon_r^{p/3}\rangle$. The distribution of [coarse-grained energy dissipation](coarse-grained-energy-dissipation.md) need not be universal. [Refined similarity](kolmogorov-refined-similarity-hypothesis.md) itself does not prescribe that distribution.
