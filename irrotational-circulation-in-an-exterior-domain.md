# Irrotational circulation in an exterior domain

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin's circulation theorem](kelvin-s-circulation-theorem.md)

In smooth barotropic inviscid flow with conservative [body force](body-force.md), [Kelvin's circulation theorem](kelvin-s-circulation-theorem.md) preserves circulation of [material curves](material-curve.md). Initially zero [vorticity](vorticity.md) therefore gives zero circulation on contractible loops for all times. A loop winding around an excluded obstacle can have a nonzero constant circulation: the planar field $\mathbf u=\Gamma\mathbf e_\theta/(2\pi r)$ is [irrotational](irrotational-vector-field.md) outside the origin but has circulation $\Gamma$ on a positively oriented enclosing loop.
