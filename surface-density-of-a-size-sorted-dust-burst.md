# Surface density of a size-sorted dust burst

↑ **Parent:** [Burst transport under inverse-size radial drag](burst-transport-under-inverse-size-radial-drag.md)

For initial dust mass $m_d$ in a ring at $r_0$ with a fixed-diameter [power-law size distribution](power-law-size-distribution.md) of index $\alpha<4$, [burst transport under inverse-size radial drag](burst-transport-under-inverse-size-radial-drag.md) maps radius to diameter $D_r=2\gamma t/(r_0^2-r^2)$. Set $T_r=t_r(D_{\max})$ and $\eta=D_{\min}/D_{\max}$. The [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) is the azimuthal average: $2\pi r\Sigma\,dr=(\pi\rho K/6)D_r^{3-\alpha}dD_r$. Since $dD_r/dr=2rD_r/(r_0^2-r^2)$, [mass normalization of a power-law size distribution](mass-normalization-of-a-power-law-size-distribution.md) gives the displayed expression. It is supported where $\eta\le t/T_r\le1$. It applies at $t>0$ before removal at the radius in question; the initial state is a delta-function ring. A local angular density needs additional phase information.
