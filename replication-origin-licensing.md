# Replication origin licensing

↑ **Parent:** [DNA replication](dna-replication.md)

Loading of an inactive replication-helicase precursor that makes an origin competent to fire later. In conventional [eukaryotic](eukaryote.md) cycles, low kinase activity permits licensing and subsequent high kinase activity promotes firing while preventing renewed licensing.
