# Orthogonal transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_transformation)

An orthogonal transformation of a real inner-product space is a linear map $Q$ satisfying $Q^TQ=I$. It preserves inner products, lengths, angles, and therefore [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) when applied to an embedded surface in Euclidean space.
