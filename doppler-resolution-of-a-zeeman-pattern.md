# Doppler resolution of a Zeeman pattern

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler broadening](doppler-broadening.md)

A Maxwellian emitter has [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) Doppler [frequency](frequency.md) FWHM $\Delta\nu_D/\nu=\sqrt{8k_BT\ln2/(mc^2)}$. Equating this width to the minimum adjacent weak-field sodium component separation $2\mu_BB/(3h)$ gives the title resolution scale. A [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) blend has no unique resolution threshold: disappearance of individual components and disappearance of the whole broadened multiplet are different criteria. Instrumental resolution, pressure broadening, selected [polarization](polarization-waves.md) and line strengths must be specified for a sharper prediction.
