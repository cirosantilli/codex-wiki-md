# Lectures on Landau Theory of Phase Transitions by Peter D. Olmsted

[https://site.physics.georgetown.edu/~pdo7/ps_files/landau.pdf](https://site.physics.georgetown.edu/~pdo7/ps_files/landau.pdf)

Authored lecture notes, dated 9 July 2015 on the title page. Section 4.2 covers the [vector](vector.md) [ferromagnet](ferromagnet.md); §4.3, printed pp.10–13, develops the nematic tensor, rotational invariants and discontinuous transition. The tensor and coefficient normalization differs from $S=Q(3\mathbf n\mathbf n^{\mathsf T}-I)$ and must be translated explicitly.
