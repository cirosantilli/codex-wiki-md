# Coordinates relative to a basis

↑ **Parent:** [Basis](basis.md)

The unique scalar [coefficients](coefficient.md) in the finite [linear combination](linear-combination.md) expressing a [vector](vector.md) in a chosen [basis](basis.md). Spanning gives existence, while [linear independence](linear-independence.md) makes the difference of two proposed representations have all zero coefficients. Ordering a finite basis turns these coefficients into a [coordinate vector](coordinate-vector.md).
