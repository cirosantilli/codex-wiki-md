# Frenkel exciton

↑ **Parent:** [Exciton](exciton.md)

A [Frenkel exciton](frenkel-exciton.md) is an [exciton](exciton.md) whose internal excitation is concentrated on the scale of one molecule or [unit cell](unit-cell.md). The local excitation can transfer between sites; localized internal structure does not require an immobile [center of mass](center-of-mass.md). The large-radius [Wannier-Mott exciton](wannier-mott-exciton.md) approximation cannot simply be extrapolated to this limit.[§3, printed p. 6: small-radius Frenkel contrast](excitons-in-semiconductor-quantum-dots-by-matjaz-humar.md)
