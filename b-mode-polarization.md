# B-mode polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background polarization](cosmic-microwave-background-polarization.md)

The parity-odd part of linear sky polarization is represented by the pseudoscalar potential $P_B$. At linear order an axisymmetric [scalar cosmological perturbation](scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md) gives no physical B mode. Tensor or vector sources and lensing conversion of [E-mode polarization](e-mode-polarization.md) are outside that scalar-source statement.
