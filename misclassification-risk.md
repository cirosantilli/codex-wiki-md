# Misclassification risk

↑ **Parent:** [Misclassification loss](misclassification-loss.md)

The misclassification risk of a [classifier](classifier.md) is its [expected](expected-value.md) [misclassification loss](misclassification-loss.md),

$$
R(h)=\mathbb P(h(X)\ne Y)=\mathbb E\ell_{0-1}(h,(X,Y)).
$$
