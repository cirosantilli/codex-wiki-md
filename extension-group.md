# Extension group

↑ **Parent:** [Ext functor](ext-functor.md)

An [extension group](extension-group.md) is a value of an [Ext functor](ext-functor.md). In degree one it classifies [short exact sequences](short-exact-sequence.md) $0\to N\to E\to M\to0$ up to isomorphisms fixing the endpoints. The zero class is the split extension; its addition is the [Baer sum](baer-sum.md). Higher degrees can be represented by longer exact extensions, or computed from a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md).
