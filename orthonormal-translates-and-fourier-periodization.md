# Orthonormal translates and Fourier periodization

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling function](scaling-function.md)

With $\widehat\phi(\xi)=\int\phi(x)e^{-ix\xi}\,dx$ in the [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md) sense, the integer translates of $\phi\in L^2(\mathbb R)$ are orthonormal exactly when

$$
\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}|\widehat\phi(t+2\pi k)|^2=1\quad\text{almost everywhere}.
$$

The [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) of the periodized energy are the translate inner products. [Uniqueness of Fourier coefficients in L1](uniqueness-of-fourier-coefficients-in-l1.md) therefore proves both directions. No absolute integrability assumption on $\phi$ is required.
