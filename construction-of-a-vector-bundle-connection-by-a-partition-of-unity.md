# Construction of a vector bundle connection by a partition of unity

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

A local trivialization of a smooth [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) gives a local connection by differentiating its component functions. Weight these connections by a subordinate locally finite [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) and extend the weighted terms by zero. The weights sum to one, so the connection Leibniz rule survives. This proves existence on a paracompact smooth manifold. Averaging connections is valid with weights summing to one because the collection is the [affine space of vector-bundle connections](affine-space-of-vector-bundle-connections.md).
