# Gallai theorem for an integer lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey theory](ramsey-theory-split.md)

For every finite $S\subseteq\mathbb N^d$, every [finite coloring](finite-coloring.md) of $\mathbb N^d$ contains a monochromatic homothetic copy $a+rS$. The theorem follows from the [Hales-Jewett theorem](hales-jewett-theorem.md) by coloring a word according to the sum of the points indexed by its letters.
