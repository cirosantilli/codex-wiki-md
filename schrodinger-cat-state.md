<h1 id="schrodinger-cat-state">Schrödinger cat state</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md)

A coherent [superposition](superposition-principle.md) of distinct optical coherent amplitudes, for example $N(|\alpha\rangle+e^{i\phi}|-\alpha\rangle)$. It differs from the corresponding incoherent mixture through cross terms in observables and its phase-space interference. For large $|\alpha|$, branch distinguishability is large while ordinary [quadrature squeezing](quadrature-squeezing.md) can become exponentially small.
