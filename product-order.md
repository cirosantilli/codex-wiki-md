# Product order

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_order)

The product order on a Cartesian product of [partially ordered sets](partially-ordered-set.md) compares every coordinate separately. Points that improve one coordinate and worsen another are incomparable. It is generally a [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md), not a [total order](total-order.md).
