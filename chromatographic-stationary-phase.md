# Chromatographic stationary phase

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatography](chromatography-split.md)

The [chromatographic stationary phase](chromatographic-stationary-phase.md) remains in place during separation. A porous packing in [size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md) supplies internal liquid regions with size-dependent accessibility; ideal separation requires no [adsorption](adsorption.md) of the [solute](solute.md) to the packing.

A [chromatographic stationary phase](chromatographic-stationary-phase.md) may instead expose a [solid](solid.md) surface for [adsorption](adsorption.md), or retain an immobilized liquid into which [solutes](solute.md) partition. In ordinary [silica gel](silica-gel.md) [thin-layer chromatography](thin-layer-chromatography.md), interactions with the surface delay migration. This mechanism differs from ideal [SEC pore access](sec-pore-access.md).
