# Mass-shedding limit

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron star](neutron-star.md)

The [mass-shedding limit](mass-shedding-limit.md) is the [spin](spin.md) at which equatorial surface matter can no longer remain attached in equilibrium. Newtonian gravity gives a Keplerian [frequency](frequency.md) scale at the surface. Rotation-induced deformation, [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) and the equation of state change the numerical bound. A magnetic-equilibrium formula is not valid indefinitely as the exterior magnetosphere shrinks inside the star.
