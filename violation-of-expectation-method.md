# Violation-of-expectation method

↑ **Parent:** [Looking-time method](looking-time-method.md)

Presenting outcomes predicted to be congruent or incongruent with a proposed expectation and comparing looking responses. In [false belief](false-belief.md) research, manipulating an agent’s visual access while controlling actual object location helps distinguish belief-based predictions from direct reality tracking. Longer looking is an indirect measure requiring alternative explanations to be tested.[Science 308, 255–258; green/yellow boxes, true- and false-belief induction and test reaches](do-15-month-old-infants-understand-false-beliefs-by-kristine-h-onishi-and-renee-baillargeon.md)
