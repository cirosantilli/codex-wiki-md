# Relativistic radio-cavity enthalpy

↑ **Parent:** [Active-galactic-nucleus feedback](active-galactic-nucleus-feedback.md)

For an approximately pressure-balanced cavity containing relativistic fluid with $\gamma=4/3$, internal [energy](energy-split.md) plus displacement work is $4pV$. Dividing by an estimated inflation or buoyancy age gives a mechanical-power diagnostic. Projection, pressure [gradients](gradient.md), the equation of state, age estimates and additional [shock](shock-wave.md) [energy](energy-split.md) affect the inference; cavity power is not identical to locally dissipated heat.
