# Contact-mode atomic force microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

A force-microscopy mode keeping the probe in contact while monitoring [cantilever](cantilever.md) deflection. Constant-deflection feedback records the vertical actuator motion needed to maintain an approximate normal-force setpoint; finite probe geometry and specimen deformation still affect the image.
