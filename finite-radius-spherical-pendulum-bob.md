# Finite-radius spherical pendulum bob

↑ **Parent:** [Physical pendulum](physical-pendulum.md)

A homogeneous spherical bob rigidly fixed to a massless support has centre-of-mass distance $L$ from its pivot and intrinsic [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $2Ma^2/5$. The [parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md) and linear gravitational restoring [torque](torque.md) give the title [period](period-of-a-function.md) for small [oscillations](oscillation.md). Relative to a [point mass](point-mass.md) at the same centre, the leading fractional [period](period-of-a-function.md) increase is $a^2/(5L^2)$. If $L$ is instead measured to the [sphere](sphere.md)'s surface, the centre distance must first be changed to $L+a$.
