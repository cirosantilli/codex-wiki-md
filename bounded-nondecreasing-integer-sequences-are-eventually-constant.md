# Bounded nondecreasing integer sequences are eventually constant

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone sequence](monotone-sequence.md)

If an integer-valued [monotone sequence](monotone-sequence.md) $(a_n)$ is nondecreasing and bounded above by $M$, it can increase strictly at most $\lfloor M\rfloor-a_1$ times. Thus its values eventually remain constant. Such sequences are determined by a finite initial list followed by a constant tail, so their collection is a [countable set](countable-set.md).
