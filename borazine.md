# Borazine

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Borazine](borazine.md) is the alternating six-membered $\mathrm{B_3N_3H_6}$ ring. Planar boron and [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) centers permit N-to-B pi donation, but unequal orbital energies strongly polarize the bonding. It undergoes addition and [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) much more readily than [benzene](benzene.md); the structural analogy does not give [benzene](benzene.md)-like chemical inertness.
