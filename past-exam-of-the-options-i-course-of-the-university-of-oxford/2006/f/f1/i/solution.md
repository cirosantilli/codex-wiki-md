<h1 id="f/f1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The rows of the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) $Dg(a)$ are $\nabla g_1(a)^T,\ldots,\nabla g_k(a)^T$. Rank $k$ means precisely that these $k$ [gradients](../../../../../gradient.md) are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md). The [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) condition is that real numbers $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_k$ exist with

$$
\boxed{\nabla f(a)=\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_j\nabla g_j(a)}.
$$

Geometrically, $\nabla f(a)$ is perpendicular to the [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md) of the constraint level set, whose tangent vectors form $\ker Dg(a)$. The full-rank hypothesis ensures this level set is a smooth local constraint [manifold](../../../../../topological-manifold-split.md).
