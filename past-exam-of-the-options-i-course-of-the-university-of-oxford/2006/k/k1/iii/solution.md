<h1 id="k/k1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [stream function](../../../../../stream-function.md) gives $u=2\alpha xy$ and $v=-\alpha y^2$. Its scalar [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md) is

$$
v_x-u_y=-2\alpha x.
$$

For $\alpha\ne0$ this does not vanish on any nonempty open region of the plane. A [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) would produce an [irrotational flow](../../../../../irrotational-flow.md) there. Equivalently, its imaginary part would be a [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md), whereas $\Delta\psi=2\alpha x$. Thus **no complex potential exists on any nonempty open region for this flow**; vanishing [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md) only along the vertical line does not provide a two-dimensional domain.
