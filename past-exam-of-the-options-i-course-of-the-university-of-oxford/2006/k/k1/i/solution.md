<h1 id="k/k1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a twice continuously differentiable [stream function](../../../../../stream-function.md), equality of mixed derivatives gives

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=u_x+v_y=\psi_{yx}-\psi_{xy}=0.
$$

This is the [incompressible flow](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) condition in two dimensions. A [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md) is a curve whose tangent at each regular point is parallel to the instantaneous velocity field. Parameterize it by $\gamma'(s)=c(s)(u,v)$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) yields

$$
\frac d{ds}\psi(\gamma(s))=c(s)(\psi_x\psi_y-\psi_y\psi_x)=0.
$$

Therefore **the [stream function](../../../../../stream-function.md) is constant on each [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md)**. This proof includes arbitrary regular parametrizations; at a stagnation point the velocity vanishes and the usual tangent direction is not determined.
