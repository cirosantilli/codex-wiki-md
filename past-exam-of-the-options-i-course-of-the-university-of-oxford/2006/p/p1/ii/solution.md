<h1 id="p/p1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $f(x)=x^4-6x^3+11x^2-5x$. The node [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is

$$
\omega(x)=x(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)=x^4-6x^3+11x^2-6x.
$$

It vanishes at all four nodes, so subtracting it leaves the data unchanged. Since $f(x)-\omega(x)=x$, uniqueness of [polynomial interpolation](../../../../../polynomial-interpolation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{p(x)=x}.
$$
