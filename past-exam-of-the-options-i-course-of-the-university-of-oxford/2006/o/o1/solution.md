↑ **Parent:** [O1](../o1.md)

After pooling, there are eight categories: $0,1,\ldots,6,\geq7$. Their frequencies have one constraint because they sum to $N$, and one model parameter was fitted. Thus the reference [chi-squared distribution](../../../../chi-squared-distribution.md) has

$$
\nu=8-1-1=6
$$

[degrees of freedom](../../../../degree-of-freedom.md). The reported statistic satisfies $25.914>22.46$, the stated upper $0.001$ cutoff for six [degrees of freedom](../../../../degree-of-freedom.md). Therefore **reject the Poisson model at the 0.1% significance level** under the [independence](../../../../independent-random-variables.md) and common-mean assumptions. The discrepancy is especially pronounced in the zero-count class, but the test assesses the whole fitted distribution. The conclusion uses the approximate [Pearson chi-squared goodness-of-fit test](../../../../pearson-chi-squared-goodness-of-fit-test.md), not an exact finite-sample tail probability.
