↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [field extension](../../../../../field-extension.md) $K/F$ is finite when $K$, regarded as a [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over $F$, has finite [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md). Its [degree of a field extension](../../../../../degree-of-a-field-extension.md) is

$$
\boxed{[K:F]=\dim_F K}.
$$

Thus a finite [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $u_1,\ldots,u_m$ means that every $k\in K$ is uniquely expressible as $\sum_i c_i u_i$ with coefficients $c_i\in F$.
