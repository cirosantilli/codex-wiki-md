↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A sequence converges to $x\in X$ when

$$
\boxed{\forall\varepsilon>0\;\exists N\;\forall n\geq N:\ d(x_n,x)<\varepsilon}.
$$

The phrase [convergent sequence](../../../../../convergent-sequence.md) requires the limit to belong to the stated [metric space](../../../../../metric-space.md); convergence in a larger ambient space alone does not suffice.
