A [topological space](../../../../topological-space.md) is [connected](../../../../connected-space.md) when it cannot be written as the union of two disjoint nonempty [open sets](../../../../open-set.md). Equivalently, the only subsets that are simultaneously open and closed are the empty set and the whole space. For a subset, [connectedness](../../../../connected-space.md) refers to its [subspace topology](../../../../subspace-topology.md).
