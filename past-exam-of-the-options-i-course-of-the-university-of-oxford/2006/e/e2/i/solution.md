<h1 id="e/e2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** Suppose $A\cup B=U\sqcup V$ were a separation into nonempty relatively [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md). Intersecting with $A$ gives a separation unless all of the [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) set $A$ lies on one side; similarly all of $B$ lies on one side. A point of $A\cap B$ forces both to lie on the same side. The other side would then be empty, a contradiction. Hence $A\cup B$ is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md).
