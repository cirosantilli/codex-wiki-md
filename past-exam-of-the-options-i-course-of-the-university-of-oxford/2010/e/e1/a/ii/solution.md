↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $Y$ is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T_Y=\{Y\cap U:U\in\mathcal T\}.}
$$

If $Y$ is open in $X$, every such intersection is open in $X$. Conversely, every ambient [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) contained in $Y$ equals its intersection with $Y$. Thus the relatively open sets are exactly the ambient open subsets contained in $Y$.
