<h1 id="e/e2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $p:X\to X/R$ send a point to its [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md). The [quotient topology](../../../../../../quotient-topology.md) is

$$
\boxed{U\subseteq X/R\text{ is open exactly when }p^{-1}(U)\text{ is open in }X.}
$$

It is the finest [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) on the quotient making $p$ continuous.
