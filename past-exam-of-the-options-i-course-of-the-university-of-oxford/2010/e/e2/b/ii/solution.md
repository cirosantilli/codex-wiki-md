<h1 id="e/e2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $x\in A=g^{-1}(B)$ and $yRx$, then $g(y)=g(x)\in B$, so $y\in A$. Thus $A$ contains every [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) it meets: it is a [saturated subset](../../../../../../saturation-of-a-subset.md). Always $A\subseteq p^{-1}(p(A))$; conversely, a point of the latter set is equivalent to some $x\in A$ and hence belongs to $A$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p^{-1}(p(A))=A.}
$$
