<h1 id="o/o2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Full column rank gives the least-squares estimator

$$
\boxed{\widehat\theta=(X^TX)^{-1}X^TY.}
$$

Write $A=(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$. Since $AX=I$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(\epsilon)=\sigma^2I$, we have $\widehat\theta=\theta+A\epsilon$ and

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cov}(\widehat\theta)=\sigma^2AA^T
=\sigma^2(X^TX)^{-1}.}
$$

This calculation needs the stated uncorrelated equal-[variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) errors, but not normality.
