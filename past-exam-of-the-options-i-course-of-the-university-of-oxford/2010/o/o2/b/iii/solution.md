<h1 id="o/o2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The expected activity is $E_t=\alpha+\beta(e^{-t}-c)$. Under normal errors, least squares is maximum likelihood, and invariance of maximum-likelihood estimation gives

$$
\widehat E_t=\widehat\alpha+\widehat\beta(e^{-t}-c).
$$

The covariance term vanishes by part (ii), so

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(\widehat E_t)=\sigma^2\left[
\frac1n+\frac{(e^{-t}-c)^2}{\sum_i(e^{-t_i}-c)^2}\right].}
$$

This is uncertainty in the estimated mean activity; the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) of a future individual observation would include its additional measurement-error [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md).
