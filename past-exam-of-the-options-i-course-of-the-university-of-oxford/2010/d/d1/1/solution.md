<h1 id="d/d1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For every real $b$,

$$
\boxed{f^{-1}((b,\infty))=\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty f^{-1}([b+1/n,\infty)).}
$$

Each set on the right is measurable by hypothesis. [Closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) of a [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) under countable unions proves the assertion.
