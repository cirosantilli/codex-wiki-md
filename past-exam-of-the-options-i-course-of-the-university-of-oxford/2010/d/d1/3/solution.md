<h1 id="d/d1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

If $f$ is measurable, then for a threshold $a\leq0$, $\{f^+\geq a\}=E$, while for $a>0$, $\{f^+\geq a\}=\{f\geq a\}$. The half-line criterion makes $f^+$ measurable. By part (2), $-f$ is measurable, and $f^-=(-f)^+$ is therefore measurable as well.

Conversely, if $f^+$ and $f^-$ are measurable, part (2) and the permitted [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) under sums show that $f=f^+-f^-$ is measurable. Thus **measurability is equivalent to measurability of both parts**.
