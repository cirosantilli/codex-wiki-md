<h1 id="d/d1/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Since $|f|=f^++f^-$, part (3) and [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) under sums prove that $|f|$ is measurable whenever $f$ is.

**The converse is false.** Let $A\subseteq[0,1]$ be a non-Lebesgue-measurable set and define $f=1$ on $A$ and $f=-1$ on its complement in $[0,1]$. Then $|f|=1$ is measurable, but $f^{-1}([0,\infty))=A$ is not. Taking absolute values can conceal a nonmeasurable sign choice.
