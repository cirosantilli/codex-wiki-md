<h1 id="d/d2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For $x\geq1$, $|\sin(1/\sqrt x)|\leq1/\sqrt x$, so

$$
|g(x)|\leq x^{-3/2},\qquad\int_1^\infty x^{-3/2}dx=2.
$$

Thus **$g$ is integrable** on this [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md). But for $x>1$, $\sqrt{x^2-1}<x$, hence $h(x)>1/x$. The integral of $1/x$ diverges at infinity, so **$h$ is not integrable**. Defining $h(1)=0$ changes only a [null set](../../../../../null-set.md) and has no effect on this conclusion.
