<h1 id="d/d2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**Both functions are integrable on $(0,1)$.** The oscillatory function is measurable and bounded by $1$ in absolute value, on an [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) of finite measure. For the other function,

$$
\int_0^1h(x)\,dx=\int_0^1\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\frac\pi2<\infty.
$$

In particular its singularity at the right endpoint is of integrable inverse-square-[root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) type.
