↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take $Y=X_1+X_2$. [Independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) factors the [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(e^{tY})=\mathbb E(e^{tX_1})\mathbb E(e^{tX_2})=M_1(t)M_2(t).}
$$
