<h1 id="m/m1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Take $Y=UX_1$, where $U$ is uniform on $(0,1)$ and independent of $X_1$. Conditional [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(e^{tUX_1})=\int_0^1\mathbb E(e^{utX_1})du
=\int_0^1M_1(ut)du.}
$$

For $t$ in a sufficiently small neighborhood of zero, finiteness follows from finiteness of the original [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) in such a neighborhood.

This uses the [moment-generating function of an independent product](../../../../../../moment-generating-function-of-an-independent-product.md).
