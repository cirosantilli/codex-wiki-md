↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $Y=aX_1+b$. Directly,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(e^{tY})=e^{bt}\mathbb E(e^{atX_1})=e^{bt}M_1(at).}
$$

The formula holds wherever the [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) on the right is finite.
