<h1 id="m/m1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $X_1'$ be an independent copy of $X_1$ and take $Y=X_1+X_1'$. Then

$$
\boxed{M_Y(t)=M_1(t)^2.}
$$

[Independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the copy is essential. The variable $2X_1$ would instead have [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) $M_1(2t)$.
