<h1 id="m/m2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The chain alternates at every step between the two classes $\{1,2\}$ and $\{3,4\}$. It is irreducible and has period two: returns require an even number of steps, and a two-step return has positive probability.

Writing the off-diagonal block as $B=\begin{pmatrix}1/3&2/3\\2/3&1/3\end{pmatrix}$, its square is $B^2=\begin{pmatrix}5/9&4/9\\4/9&5/9\end{pmatrix}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{P^2=\begin{pmatrix}
5/9&4/9&0&0\\4/9&5/9&0&0\\
0&0&5/9&4/9\\0&0&4/9&5/9
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Every two steps remain within the initial class, where the sampled chain is irreducible and aperiodic.
