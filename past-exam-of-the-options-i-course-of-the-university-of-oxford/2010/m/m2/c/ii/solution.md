<h1 id="m/m2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

State $2$ can be reached from state $1$ only at an even time. In the two-step sampled chain, each step while still at state $1$ reaches state $2$ with probability $4/9$, and otherwise stays at state $1$ with probability $5/9$. Thus, if $T$ is the original hitting time,

$$
\boxed{P(T=2r)=\left(\frac59\right)^{r-1}\frac49,\quad r\geq1,
\qquad P(T\text{ odd})=0.}
$$

Equivalently $T=2G$ where $G$ has the [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) on positive integers with parameter $4/9$.
