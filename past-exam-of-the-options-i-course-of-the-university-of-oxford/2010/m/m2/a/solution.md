<h1 id="m/m2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the stationary probabilities as $(\pi_1,\pi_2)$. The equation $\pi P=\pi$ reduces to $a\pi_1=b\pi_2$. Together with $\pi_1+\pi_2=1$, this gives

$$
\boxed{\pi=\left(\frac b{a+b},\frac a{a+b}\right).}
$$

The positive transition probabilities make the two states communicate, so the [stationary distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md) is unique.
