The [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac ap\right)=\begin{cases}
0,&p\mid a,\\
1,&a\text{ is a nonzero square modulo }p,\\
-1,&a\text{ is not a square modulo }p.
\end{cases}}
$$
