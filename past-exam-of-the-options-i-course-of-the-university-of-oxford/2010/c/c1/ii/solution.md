↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Euler criterion](../../../../../euler-criterion.md) states that for $p\nmid a$,

$$
\boxed{a^{(p-1)/2}\equiv\left(\frac ap\right)\pmod p.}
$$

The same [modular congruence](../../../../../modular-congruence.md) holds for $p\mid a$, with both sides zero.
