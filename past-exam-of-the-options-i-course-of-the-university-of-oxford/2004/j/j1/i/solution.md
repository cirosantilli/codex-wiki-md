The [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial t}=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\Delta\psi+V\psi.}
$$

A normalized [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md) has $\int_{\mathbb R^3}|\psi|^2\,d^3x=1$; the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) acts on [wave functions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) satisfying the appropriate domain and boundary conditions.
