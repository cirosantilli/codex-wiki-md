<h1 id="o/o2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Independence of the slope estimate and residual variance, together with their normal and chi-square laws, gives the Student [pivotal quantity](../../../../../pivotal-quantity.md)

$$
\frac{\widehat\beta-\beta}{s/\sqrt{S_{xx}}}\sim t_{n-2}.
$$

Thus an exact Gaussian-model two-sided 95% [confidence interval](../../../../../confidence-interval.md) is

$$
\boxed{\widehat\beta\ \pm\ t_{n-2,0.975}\frac{s}{\sqrt{S_{xx}}}.}
$$

Using a normal quantile without estimating the variance uncertainty would not give this exact finite-sample coverage.
