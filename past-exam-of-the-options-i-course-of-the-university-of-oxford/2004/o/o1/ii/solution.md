<h1 id="o/o1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Separate high-count classes have very small expected frequencies, making the chi-square approximation unreliable. Pooling the upper tail gives a class with expected count about $20.9$, comfortably above the customary small-cell threshold. **The pooling protects the asymptotic test approximation**, while retaining the tail count as part of the goodness-of-fit comparison.
