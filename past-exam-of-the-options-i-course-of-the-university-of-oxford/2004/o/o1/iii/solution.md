<h1 id="o/o1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

There are six pooled classes, one normalization constraint, and one fitted [Poisson distribution](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) parameter. Thus the reference [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) are $\boxed{6-1-1=4}$. Under the null model the Pearson statistic has approximately the [Chi-squared distribution](../../../../../chi-squared-distribution.md) with four [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md). Its supplied 5% upper critical value is $9.49$.

Because $\boxed{2.07<9.49}$, **do not reject the Poisson count distribution at the 5% level**. This is consistency with that distribution, not proof that it is exactly true. A test of the marginal count frequencies also does not by itself verify independence of successive time windows.
