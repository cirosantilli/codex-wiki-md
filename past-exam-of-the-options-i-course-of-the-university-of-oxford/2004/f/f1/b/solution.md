<h1 id="f/f1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The requested base point is the [identity matrix](../../../../../identity-matrix.md) $I$, not the zero [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md). Since $I$ commutes with every $H$,

$$
\exp(I+H)=\exp(I)\exp(H)=e[I+H+O(\|H\|^2)].
$$

The [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) [power series](../../../../../power-series.md) justifies the quadratic remainder near zero. Consequently

$$
\boxed{D\exp_I(H)=eH.}
$$

At zero the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) would instead be $H$; replacing the printed base point would lose the scalar factor.
