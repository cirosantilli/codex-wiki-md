Here $\exp(I)=eI$. Applying part (a) at that [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) gives $Ds_{eI}(K)=e(K^t+K)$, while part (b) gives $D\exp_I(H)=eH$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{D(s\circ\exp)_I(H)=Ds_{eI}(eH)=e^2(H^t+H).}
$$

Both the outer evaluation point and the inner [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) contribute a factor of $e$.
