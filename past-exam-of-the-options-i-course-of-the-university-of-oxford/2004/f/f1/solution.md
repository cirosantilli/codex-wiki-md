<h1 id="f/f1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F1](../f1.md)

The [total derivative](../../../../total-derivative.md) is the unique [linear map](../../../../linear-map.md) $Df_a:\mathbb R^m\to\mathbb R^n$ satisfying

$$
\boxed{f(a+h)=f(a)+Df_a(h)+o(\|h\|)\quad\text{as }h\to0.}
$$

This describes the best first-order linear approximation without choosing coordinates. The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) is

$$
\boxed{D(g\circ f)_a=Dg_{f(a)}\circ Df_a.}
$$

The evaluation point $f(a)$ in the outer [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) matters, particularly when composing a quadratic [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) map with the exponential.
