<h1 id="c/c1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $a=\prod_pp^{r_p}$ and $b=\prod_pp^{s_p}$. Divisibility is exactly comparison of [prime exponents](../../../../../../p-adic-valuation.md). Thus the [greatest common divisor](../../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) uses $\min(r_p,s_p)$ and the [least common multiple](../../../../../../least-common-multiple.md) uses $\max(r_p,s_p)$. Since their sum is $r_p+s_p$ for each prime,

$$
\boxed{\gcd(a,b)\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=\prod_pp^{r_p+s_p}=ab.}
$$
