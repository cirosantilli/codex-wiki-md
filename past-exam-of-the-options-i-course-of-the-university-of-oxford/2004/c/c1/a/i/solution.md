<h1 id="c/c1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By the [fundamental theorem of arithmetic](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md), every [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) greater than one has a [prime factorization](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md), and that factorization is unique up to reordering the factors. Equivalently, it has a unique finite prime-exponent representation $\boxed{n=\prod_pp^{v_p(n)}}$, with nonnegative [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) exponents. The unit [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) has the empty product.
