↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The inclusion $F\subseteq K$ makes $K$ a [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over $F$ by its own addition and multiplication by elements of $F$. The extension is finite when this [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) has a finite [basis](../../../../../basis.md). Its [degree of a field extension](../../../../../degree-of-a-field-extension.md) is

$$
\boxed{[K:F]=\dim_FK.}
$$

Being a [finite field extension](../../../../../finite-field-extension.md) concerns vector-space dimension, not the cardinality of the underlying field.
