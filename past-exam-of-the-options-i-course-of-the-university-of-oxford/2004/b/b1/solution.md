<h1 id="b/b1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B1](../b1.md)

The [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) $x^2-2$ is an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) over $\mathbb Q$, so $[\mathbb Q(\sqrt2):\mathbb Q]=2$. This intermediate field is real, so it does not contain $i$. Hence $x^2+1$ has no root in it and adjoining $i$ has degree two. The [tower law](../../../../tower-law.md) gives

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i):\mathbb Q]=4.}
$$

A [basis](../../../../basis.md) is $1,\sqrt2,i,i\sqrt2$. Square-bracket adjoining notation in this algebraic example gives the same field as round-bracket adjoining notation.
