<h1 id="k/k1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the curl. A [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) has zero curl, and the supplied identity, with $\nabla\cdot u=0$ and $\nabla\cdot\omega=\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times u)=0$, gives

$$
\partial_t\omega+(u\cdot\nabla)\omega-(\omega\cdot\nabla)u=0.
$$

Therefore $D\omega/Dt=(\omega\cdot\nabla)u$. In planar flow $\omega=(0,0,\partial_xv-\partial_yu)$ and $\partial_z u=0$, so the stretching term vanishes. Consequently

$$
\boxed{D\omega/Dt=0}.
$$

[Vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md) is carried unchanged with each fluid particle. It need not be spatially uniform or independent of time at a fixed spatial point.
