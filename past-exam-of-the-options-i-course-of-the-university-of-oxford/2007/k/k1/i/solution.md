<h1 id="k/k1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For steady flow the given equation gives $\nabla H=-\rho\,\omega\times u$. Dotting with $u$ yields $u\cdot\nabla H=0$, so **$H$ is constant along each [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md)**. It can have different values on different streamlines. Dotting with $\omega$ also makes it constant along vortex lines where they are defined.

If the flow is additionally irrotational, $\omega=0$ and hence $\nabla H=0$. Thus **$H$ is one constant on each [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) fluid region**, the stronger global Bernoulli conclusion.
