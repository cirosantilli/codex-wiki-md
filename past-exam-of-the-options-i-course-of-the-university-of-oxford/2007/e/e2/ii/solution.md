<h1 id="e/e2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Every subspace-[open set](../../../../../open-set.md) has the form $A\cap U=i^{-1}(U)$ for some $U\in\mathcal T$. [Continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of $i:(A,\mathcal T')\to(X,\mathcal T)$ makes every such inverse image open in $\mathcal T'$. Hence $\boxed{\mathcal T_A\subseteq\mathcal T'}$.
