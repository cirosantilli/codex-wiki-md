<h1 id="e/e2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Choose $Z=(A,\mathcal T_A)$ and let $f$ be the identity function on $A$. The composite $i\circ f$ is continuous by part (i). The stipulated universal implication therefore makes $f:(A,\mathcal T_A)\to(A,\mathcal T')$ continuous. Its inverse image of any $\mathcal T'$-[open set](../../../../../open-set.md) is that set itself, so it must lie in $\mathcal T_A$. Thus $\boxed{\mathcal T'\subseteq\mathcal T_A}$.
