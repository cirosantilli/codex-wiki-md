<h1 id="e/e2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $A\cap U$ is subspace-open, then

$$
f^{-1}(A\cap U)=(i\circ f)^{-1}(U).
$$

The right side is open in $Z$ by the assumed [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the composition. This holds for every subspace-[open set](../../../../../open-set.md), proving **$f$ is continuous into the [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md)**.
