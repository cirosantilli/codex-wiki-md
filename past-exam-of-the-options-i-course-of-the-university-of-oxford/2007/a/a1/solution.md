<h1 id="a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

[Burnside's lemma](../../../../burnside-s-lemma.md) counts the orbits of a finite [group action](../../../../group-action-split.md):

$$
|\Omega/G|=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}|\operatorname{Fix}(g)|.
$$

Identify each face with its opposite vertex. Every symmetry induces the same [permutation](../../../../permutation.md) on these two four-element sets, so a [permutation](../../../../permutation.md) with $c$ cycles fixes $3^c2^c=6^c$ colorings. The rotational symmetry [group](../../../../group-split.md) is $A_4$, with one identity, eight three-cycles, and three products of two disjoint transpositions. Their cycle counts are four, two and two. Thus the [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) count is

$$
\boxed{\frac{6^4+8\cdot6^2+3\cdot6^2}{12}=141}.
$$

Allowing reflections gives the full tetrahedral [group](../../../../group-split.md) $S_4$. In addition there are six transpositions with three cycles and six four-cycles with one cycle. The count becomes

$$
\boxed{\frac{6^4+6\cdot6^3+3\cdot6^2+8\cdot6^2+6\cdot6}{24}=126}.
$$

Equivalently, 30 of the 141 rotational classes form 15 mirror pairs, while 111 rotational classes are their own mirror class.

The product of fixed-coloring counts is an instance of the [orbit count for simultaneous colorings](../../../../orbit-count-for-simultaneous-colorings.md).
