The [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) states that if measurable nonnegative functions satisfy $f_n\uparrow f$ almost everywhere on a measure space, then

$$
\int f=\lim_{n\to\infty}\int f_n,
$$

allowing either side to be $+\infty$.
