<h1 id="d/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $\log x\leq0$ on $(0,1]$, substitute $x=e^{-s}$ to obtain

$$
\int_0^1|x^{t-1}\log x|\,dx=\int_0^\infty se^{-ts}\,ds=\frac1{t^2}<\infty\qquad(t>0).
$$

The [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) are measurable, so this proves [Lebesgue integrability](../../../../../integrability.md). Also $0<e^{-x}\leq1$ gives $|h_t(x)|\leq|g_t(x)|$. Hence **$h_t$ is integrable on $(0,1]$ as well**.
