<h1 id="d/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $E_n$ be null. Given $\varepsilon>0$, cover $E_n$ by intervals of total length less than $\varepsilon2^{-n}$, for $n\geq1$. The union of all these interval families is [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md), covers $\bigcup_nE_n$, and has total length less than $\sum_n\varepsilon2^{-n}=\varepsilon$. Thus **the [countable union](../../../../../countable-union.md) is null**.
