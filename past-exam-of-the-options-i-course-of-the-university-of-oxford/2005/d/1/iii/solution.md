<h1 id="d/1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take the middle-thirds [Cantor set](../../../../../cantor-set.md) $C$. Its $n$th stage is the union of $2^n$ closed intervals of length $3^{-n}$, and contains $C$. Their total length is $(2/3)^n\to0$; slightly enlarging them to open intervals gives covers of arbitrarily small total length. Therefore **$C$ is null**.

For uncountability map every [binary sequence](../../../../../bitstream.md) $(\varepsilon_j)_{j\geq1}$ to $\sum_{j\geq1}2\varepsilon_j3^{-j}\in C$. If two sequences first differ at index $k$, their image difference in absolute value is at least $2\cdot3^{-k}-\sum_{j>k}2\cdot3^{-j}=3^{-k}>0$. Thus the map is injective. The set of [binary sequences](../../../../../bitstream.md) is [uncountable](../../../../../uncountable-set.md) by [Cantor's diagonal argument](../../../../../cantor-s-diagonal-argument.md), so **$C$ is [uncountable](../../../../../uncountable-set.md)**.
