↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

There are six [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md), so the supplied 1% [critical value](../../../../../critical-value.md) is $q_6=16.81$. Since $42.25>16.81$, **reject [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) at the 1% [significance level](../../../../../significance-level.md)**. The data give strong evidence of an association between the two consumption classifications in this group of participants.
