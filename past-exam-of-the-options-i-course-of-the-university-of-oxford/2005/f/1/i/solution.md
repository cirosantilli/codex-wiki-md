↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [total derivative](../../../../../total-derivative.md) is a [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $Df(a):\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$ such that

$$
\boxed{f(a+h)=f(a)+Df(a)h+r(h),\qquad\frac{\|r(h)\|}{\|h\|}\longrightarrow0\quad(h\to0).}
$$

It is unique: the difference of two such [linear maps](../../../../../linear-map.md), evaluated on $h=tv$, must vanish on every fixed [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $v$ after division by $t$ and passage to the limit.
