↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [directional derivative](../../../../../directional-derivative.md) in the specified [vector](../../../../../vector.md) direction is

$$
\boxed{D_{\mathbf u}f(a)=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(a+t\mathbf u)-f(a)}t=Df(a)\mathbf u.}
$$

The equality follows from the total-derivative remainder estimate; for the zero direction the result is zero. The convention keeps the supplied [vector](../../../../../vector.md) and does not replace it by a unit [vector](../../../../../vector.md).
