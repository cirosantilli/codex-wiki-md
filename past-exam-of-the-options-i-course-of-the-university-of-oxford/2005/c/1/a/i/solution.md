The [Legendre symbol](../../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac ap\right)=\begin{cases}0,&p\mid a,\\1,&a\text{ is a nonzero square modulo }p,\\-1,&a\text{ is a nonsquare modulo }p.\end{cases}}
$$

The definition depends only on the residue class of $a$ modulo the odd [prime](../../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$.
