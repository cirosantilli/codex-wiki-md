<h1 id="c/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Euler's criterion](../../../../../euler-s-criterion.md) implies multiplicativity of the symbol on nonzero residues and gives $(-1/p)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}$. Apply [quadratic reciprocity](../../../../../quadratic-reciprocity.md) with the odd [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) 3:

$$
\left(\frac{-3}p\right)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}\left(\frac3p\right)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}(-1)^{(p-1)/2}\left(\frac p3\right)=\left(\frac p3\right).
$$

The only nonzero square modulo three is one. Since $p>3$,

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{-3}p\right)=\begin{cases}1,&p\equiv1\pmod3,\\-1,&p\equiv2\pmod3.\end{cases}}
$$
