<h1 id="h/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

[Polar coordinates](../../../../polar-coordinates.md) give speed squared $\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2$, so unit [mass](../../../../mass.md) has [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $T=\frac12(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2)$. The radial [force](../../../../force-split.md) is $-1/r^2=-V'(r)$, giving $V=-1/r$ after choosing the additive constant zero. Therefore $L=T-V$ is the stated expression.

The two [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) are

$$
\boxed{\ddot r-r\dot\theta^2=-\frac1{r^2},\qquad\frac d{dt}(r^2\dot\theta)=0.}
$$

The second gives [conservation of angular momentum](../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) $\ell=r^2\dot\theta$. The [mechanical energy](../../../../mechanical-energy.md) is

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2)-\frac1r.}
$$

Direct differentiation expresses its derivative as

$$
\dot E=\dot r\left(\ddot r-r\dot\theta^2+\frac1{r^2}\right)+\dot\theta\frac d{dt}(r^2\dot\theta)=0.
$$

Thus [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is conserved for the motion wherever $r>0$.
