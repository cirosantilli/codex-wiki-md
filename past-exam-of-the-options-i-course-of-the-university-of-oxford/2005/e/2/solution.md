<h1 id="e/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

[Continuity](../../../../continuous-function.md) means that the [inverse image](../../../../preimage.md) of every open subset of $Y$ is open in $X$. If $x\in\overline A$ and $V$ is an [open neighborhood](../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) of $f(x)$, then $f^{-1}(V)$ is an [open neighborhood](../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) of $x$, so it meets $A$. Thus $V$ meets $f(A)$. The [closure](../../../../closure-topology.md) characterization gives

$$
\boxed{f(\overline A)\subseteq\overline{f(A)}.}
$$

If $B$ is empty the assertion is immediate. Otherwise, if $B$ is [bounded](../../../../boundedness.md), choose a centre $p$ and $R<\infty$ with $d(p,b)\leq R$ for all $b\in B$. The [closed ball](../../../../closed-ball.md) $\{y:d(p,y)\leq R\}$ is closed because $|d(p,y)-d(p,z)|\leq d(y,z)$. It therefore contains $\overline B$. Hence **the [closure](../../../../closure-topology.md) of a [bounded set](../../../../bounded-set.md) is [bounded](../../../../boundedness.md)**. Empty sets cause no exception. If $f(A)$ is [bounded](../../../../boundedness.md), its [closure](../../../../closure-topology.md) is [bounded](../../../../boundedness.md) by this result, and the displayed inclusion makes **$f(\overline A)$ [bounded](../../../../boundedness.md)** too.
