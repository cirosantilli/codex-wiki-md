<h1 id="b/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Divide $f$ by $m$ in the [polynomial ring](../../../../../polynomial-ring.md) over the field: $f=qm+r$, where $r=0$ or $\deg r<\deg m$. If $f(a)=0$, then $r(a)=0$, and minimality forces $r=0$. Conversely a multiple of $m$ vanishes at $a$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(a)=0\iff m\mid f.}
$$

This includes the zero [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md).
