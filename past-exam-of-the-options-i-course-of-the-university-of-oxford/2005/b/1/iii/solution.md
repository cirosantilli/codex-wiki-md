<h1 id="b/1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $m=uv$ with both factors of positive [degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md), evaluation gives $u(a)v(a)=0$. Since $K$ is a field, one factor vanishes at $a$, contrary to the minimal [degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) of $m$. Thus **$m$ is an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) over $F$**.

For the final unnumbered request, $\zeta=e^{\pi i/3}=\frac12+i\frac{\sqrt3}2$ satisfies $\zeta^2-\zeta+1=0$. The monic quadratic $x^2-x+1$ has no rational root, since its discriminant is $-3$. It is therefore an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) over $\mathbb Q$, and

$$
\boxed{m_\zeta(x)=x^2-x+1.}
$$
