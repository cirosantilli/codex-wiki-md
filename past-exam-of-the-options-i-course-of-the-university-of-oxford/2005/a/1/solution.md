Use the right-action convention printed in the paper: $\omega1=\omega$ and $(\omega g)h=\omega(gh)$. The [orbit of a group action](../../../../orbit-group-theory.md) and [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) are

$$
\operatorname{Orb}(\omega)=\{\omega g:g\in G\},\qquad H=\operatorname{Stab}(\omega)=\{g\in G:\omega g=\omega\}.
$$

The identity belongs to $H$, products of elements of $H$ fix $\omega$, and $\omega g=\omega$ implies $\omega g^{-1}=\omega$. Thus $H$ is a [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md). For $k\in G$,

$$
k\in\operatorname{Stab}(\omega g)\iff\omega gkg^{-1}=\omega\iff gkg^{-1}\in H,
$$

proving **$\operatorname{Stab}(\omega g)=g^{-1}Hg$**.

The rotational action on the eight vertices of a [cube](../../../../cube.md) is **[transitive](../../../../transitive-relation.md)**. Model the vertices as $(\pm1,\pm1,\pm1)$. For any desired sign triple, choose a permutation whose parity makes the corresponding [signed permutation matrix](../../../../signed-permutation-matrix.md) have [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) one; it maps $(1,1,1)$ to that vertex and preserves the [cube](../../../../cube.md). A [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) fixing a vertex fixes the axis through that vertex, the centre and the opposite vertex. It cyclically permutes the three incident edges, giving precisely [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) by $0,2\pi/3,4\pi/3$. Thus **the [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is cyclic of order three**.
