<h1 id="a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [field characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$, the [binomial theorem](../../../../../binomial-theorem.md) gives $(X-b)^p=X^p-b^p$, since the intermediate [binomial coefficients](../../../../../binomial-coefficient-split.md) are divisible by the [prime number](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$. If $b\in F$ is a [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) of $X^p-a$, then $b^p=a$, and hence

$$
\boxed{X^p-a=(X-b)^p\quad\text{in }F[X].}
$$
