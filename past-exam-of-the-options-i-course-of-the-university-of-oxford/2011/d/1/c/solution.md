<h1 id="d/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $A\subseteq E$ and $E$ is a [null set](../../../../../null-set.md), every interval cover of $E$ also covers $A$. Thus [monotonicity of Lebesgue outer measure](../../../../../monotonicity-of-lebesgue-outer-measure.md) gives $0\leq m^*(A)\leq m^*(E)=0$. Hence **every subset of a null set is null**, and in particular is [Lebesgue measurable](../../../../../lebesgue-measurable-set.md).
