<h1 id="d/1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Let $d=h-g$. It vanishes outside the [null set](../../../../../null-set.md) $E$, so part (e) proves $d$ is a [measurable function](../../../../../measurable-function.md), regardless of its values on $E$. Since the sum of two [measurable functions](../../../../../measurable-function.md) is measurable, $h=g+d$ is measurable. This is the completeness of [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md) under changes on [null sets](../../../../../null-set.md).
