The [fundamental theorem of arithmetic](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) says that every integer $n>1$ is a product of [prime numbers](../../../../../../prime-number-split.md), uniquely up to reordering. Equivalently, $n=\prod_p p^{a_p}$ for a unique family of nonnegative integer exponents, only finitely many of which are nonzero.
