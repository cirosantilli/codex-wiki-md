<h1 id="c/1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If the complete list of [prime numbers](../../../../../../prime-number-split.md) were $p_1,\ldots,p_k$, then $N=p_1\cdots p_k+1>1$ would have a prime divisor by the [fundamental theorem of arithmetic](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md). No $p_i$ divides it, since $N\equiv1\pmod{p_i}$. This contradicts completeness of the list. Hence **there are infinitely many prime numbers**.
