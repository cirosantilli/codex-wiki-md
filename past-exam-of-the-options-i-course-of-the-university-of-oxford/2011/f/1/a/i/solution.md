↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A $k$-dimensional embedded [submanifold](../../../../../../submanifold.md) of $\mathbb R^n$ is a subset $M$ such that near each $p\in M$ there is a smooth [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) of ambient open neighbourhoods that sends $M$ to the coordinate plane $\mathbb R^k\times\{0\}^{n-k}$. More precisely, for some open $U\ni p$ and diffeomorphism $\Phi:U\to V\subseteq\mathbb R^n$,

$$
\Phi(M\cap U)=V\cap(\mathbb R^k\times\{0\}^{n-k}).
$$

Thus locally the subset has $k$ freely varying smooth coordinates.
