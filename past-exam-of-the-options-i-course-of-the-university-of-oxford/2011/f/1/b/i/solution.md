<h1 id="f/1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $g(x,y,z)=x^2+y^2-z^2$. The upper cone away from the apex is the part of $g^{-1}(0)$ with $z>0$. There

$$
\nabla g=(2x,2y,-2z)\ne0.
$$

The [regular level set theorem](../../../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md) says that a level set of a smooth scalar function with nonzero [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is an embedded [submanifold](../../../../../../submanifold.md) of codimension one. Intersecting it with the open half-space $z>0$ preserves this local property. Thus **$M$ is a two-dimensional submanifold of $\mathbb R^3$**.
