<h1 id="p/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $A+\gamma B$ with $\gamma\geq0$, the three [Gershgorin discs](../../../../../gershgorin-disc.md) have positive real centres $a+\gamma$, $2c+2\gamma$, $6b+6\gamma$, and radii $b+c$, $a+b+\gamma$, $a+c+\gamma$. Centre minus radius is respectively

$$
a-b-c+\gamma>0,\qquad2c-a-b+\gamma>0,\qquad6b-a-c+5\gamma>0.
$$

Thus none of the discs contains zero. By the [Gershgorin circle theorem](../../../../../gershgorin-circle-theorem.md), zero is not an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), so $A+\gamma B$ is nonsingular for every $\gamma\geq0$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\Gamma=\varnothing.}
$$
