↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [stationary distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md) is a probability row vector $\pi$ satisfying $\pi P=\pi$. Its coordinates obey $\pi_-(1-p)=\pi_+q$ and $\pi_-+\pi_+=1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\pi_-=\frac q{1-p+q},\qquad\pi_+=\frac{1-p}{1-p+q}.}
$$

Because both transitions between distinct states have positive probability, the finite [Markov chain](../../../../../markov-chain-split.md) is irreducible; its positive self-transition probabilities also make it aperiodic.
