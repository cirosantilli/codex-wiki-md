<h1 id="m/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

By the addition rule for independent [Poisson random variables](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md), $X_n$ has the same distribution as a sum of $n$ independent variables $Z_j\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda)$. Each has [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) $\lambda$. The [central limit theorem](../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{X_n-n\lambda}{\sqrt n}\ \xrightarrow{d}\ N(0,\lambda).}
$$

For $\lambda=0$, every variable is zero, and the conclusion is the degenerate normal law with zero variance.
