↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A rate-two [Poisson process](../../../../../../poisson-process-split.md) starts at $N_0=0$, has independent increments and stationary increments, and has $N_{t+h}-N_t\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(2h)$ for $h\geq0$ when time is measured in minutes. Equivalently it is a counting process with independent stationary increments satisfying, uniformly in the start time,

$$
\mathbb P(\Delta N=1)=2h+o(h),\qquad \mathbb P(\Delta N\geq2)=o(h)
$$

as $h\downarrow0$. Thus an average rate alone is insufficient; the independence and increment assumptions are part of the model.
