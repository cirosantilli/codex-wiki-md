↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Condition (1b) is the [kinematic free-surface boundary condition](../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition-for-a-free-surface-graph.md): fluid particles on the surface remain there, since the material derivative of $y-\eta(x,t)$ vanishes. Condition (1c) is the [dynamic free-surface boundary condition](../../../../../dynamic-boundary-condition-for-an-inviscid-interface.md), obtained from the unsteady Bernoulli equation with constant atmospheric pressure and a choice of potential gauge. Condition (1d) is [impermeability](../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md): no normal flow through the horizontal bed.
