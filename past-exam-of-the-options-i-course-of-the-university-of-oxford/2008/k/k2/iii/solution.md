<h1 id="k/k2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For positive wavenumber $k$, the [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) is $c_p=\omega/k$, the speed of a fixed phase. The [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) is $c_g=d\omega/dk$, the leading speed of a narrow wave packet. Differentiating the supplied [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{c_p=\sqrt{\frac gk\tanh(kH)}},
$$



$$
\boxed{c_g=\frac{g[\tanh(kH)+kH\operatorname{sech}^2(kH)]}{2\omega}
=\frac{c_p}2\left(1+\frac{2kH}{\sinh(2kH)}\right)}.
$$

For fixed $k>0$, the shallow-depth limit uses $\tanh s/s\to1$:

$$
\boxed{\lim_{H\to0}\frac{c_p^2}H=g}.
$$
