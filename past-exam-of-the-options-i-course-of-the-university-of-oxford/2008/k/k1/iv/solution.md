<h1 id="k/k1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The convective acceleration is

$$
u u_x+v u_y=\sin x\cos x,\qquad
u v_x+v v_y=-\sin y\cos y.
$$

Steady [Euler equations](../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) therefore give $p_x=-\rho\sin x\cos x$ and $p_y=\rho\sin y\cos y$. Integrating these compatible equations yields

$$
\boxed{p(x,y)=p_0-\frac\rho2\sin^2x+\frac\rho2\sin^2y},
$$

with arbitrary additive constant $p_0$. As a check, $p/\rho+(u^2+v^2)/2=p_0/\rho+1/2-\psi^2$, indeed constant on each [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md). This is a [Steady Euler flow with vorticity proportional to its stream function](../../../../../steady-euler-flow-with-vorticity-proportional-to-its-stream-function.md).
