<h1 id="k/k1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [stream function](../../../../../stream-function.md) convention $u=\psi_y$, $v=-\psi_x$. It gives $u=-\sin x\sin y$, $v=-\cos x\cos y$. Then

$$
u_x+v_y=-\cos x\sin y+\cos x\sin y=0,
$$

so **the flow is incompressible**.
