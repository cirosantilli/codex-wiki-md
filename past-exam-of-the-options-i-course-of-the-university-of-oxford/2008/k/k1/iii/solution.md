<h1 id="k/k1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For this steady, inviscid, constant-density flow without a body force, [Bernoulli's theorem](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) says that

$$
\boxed{\frac p\rho+\frac12(u^2+v^2)\text{ is constant along each streamline}}.
$$

Its constant may vary from one [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md) to another because the flow has [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md).
