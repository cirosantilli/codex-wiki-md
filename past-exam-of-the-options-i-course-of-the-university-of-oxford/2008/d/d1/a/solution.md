<h1 id="d/d1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [null set](../../../../../null-set.md) has [Lebesgue outer measure](../../../../../lebesgue-outer-measure.md) zero: for every $\varepsilon>0$ it can be covered by countably many open intervals with total length below $\varepsilon$. If $E_j$ are null, cover $E_j$ by intervals of total length below $\varepsilon2^{-j}$, for $j\geq1$. The [union](../../../../../set-union.md) of all these covers is countable and covers $\bigcup_jE_j$ with total length below $\varepsilon$. As $\varepsilon$ is arbitrary, **the countable [union](../../../../../set-union.md) is null**.
