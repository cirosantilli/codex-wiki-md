↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A coordinate absent from the [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is [cyclic](../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md). Its [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives

$$
\frac d{dt}\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_1}=\frac{\partial L}{\partial q_1}=0.
$$

Thus the corresponding [generalized momentum](../../../../../generalized-momentum.md) $\boxed{p_1=\partial L/\partial\dot q_1}$ is conserved.
