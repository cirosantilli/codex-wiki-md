<h1 id="h/h1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [energy function](../../../../../energy-function.md) is $E=\sum_a\dot q_a\,\partial L/\partial\dot q_a-L$. Along a solution,

$$
\frac{dE}{dt}=\sum_a\dot q_a\left(\frac d{dt}\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_a}-\frac{\partial L}{\partial q_a}\right)-\frac{\partial L}{\partial t}
=-\frac{\partial L}{\partial t}.
$$

Therefore time [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) gives the conserved quantity

$$
\boxed{E=\sum_a\dot q_a\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_a}-L}.
$$
