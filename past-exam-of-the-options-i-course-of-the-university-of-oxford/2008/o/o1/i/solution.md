↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a two-way [contingency table](../../../../../contingency-table.md), Pearson's statistic is

$$
\boxed{X^2=\sum_{i,j}\frac{(O_{ij}-E_{ij})^2}{E_{ij}},\qquad E_{ij}=\frac{O_{i+}O_{+j}}{N}}.
$$

Here $O_{i+},O_{+j}$ are the observed margins and $N$ their grand total. Under the [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) null, its usual large-sample reference distribution has $(r-1)(c-1)$ [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) for an $r$-by-$c$ table.
