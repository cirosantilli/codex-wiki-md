<h1 id="o/o1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

With both column totals fixed, each column is a three-category multinomial sample. Under the null the two columns share one probability vector, which has **two free parameters** because its three probabilities sum to one. Under the alternative each has its own vector, giving **four free parameters**. Their difference is two, the reference [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) for the association test.
