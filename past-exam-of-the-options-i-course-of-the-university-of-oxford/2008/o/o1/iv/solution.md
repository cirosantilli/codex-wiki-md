<h1 id="o/o1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use two [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) from part (iii). Since $8.42>5.99$, the statistic exceeds the supplied five-percent threshold. Thus **reject no association at the five-percent level**: the observed [genotype](../../../../../genotype.md) frequencies provide evidence of a difference between cases and controls. The approximate p-value is $e^{-8.42/2}\approx0.0148$ under the $\chi^2_2$ reference law.
