A subset is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) when it has no separation into two disjoint nonempty relatively open sets. It is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) when every pair of its points can be joined by a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) path from $[0,1]$ lying in the subset.

Path-[connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md) implies [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md); the reverse fails in general, for example for the [topologist's sine curve](../../../../../topologist-s-sine-curve.md). They are equivalent for open subsets of every [Euclidean space](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $\mathbb R^n$. More generally this holds for open subsets of [locally path-connected spaces](../../../../../locally-path-connected-space.md).
