↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The circle is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md), using circular arcs, and hence [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md). Its [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) image is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) by part (ii). A [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) subset of the [real line](../../../../../real-line.md) is an interval: if it contained values on opposite sides of a missing value $c$, [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) with $(-\infty,c)$ and $(c,\infty)$ would separate it. Thus **$f(C)$ is an interval**. It is also [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md), so it is a closed bounded interval, possibly reduced to one point.
