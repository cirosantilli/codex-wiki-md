<h1 id="e/e2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $f(A)$ were separated by two disjoint nonempty relatively open sets, their preimages under the restriction $f|_A$ would be disjoint nonempty relatively open sets covering $A$. [Continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) makes them open in $A$, contradicting its [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md). Therefore **$f(A)$ is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md)**.
