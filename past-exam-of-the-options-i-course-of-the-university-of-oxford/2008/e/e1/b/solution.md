↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [basis for a topology](../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) is a family of open sets such that every [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) is a [union](../../../../../set-union.md) of members of the family. Equivalently, for every point in an [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) there is a basis member containing that point and contained in the [open set](../../../../../open-set.md).

For a basis $\mathcal B$ on $Y$, [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) is equivalent to **$f^{-1}(B)$ being open for every $B\in\mathcal B$**. Necessity follows because basis sets are open. Conversely, taking preimages of a [union](../../../../../set-union.md) of basis sets gives a [union](../../../../../set-union.md) of open preimages, so every [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) has an open [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md).
