↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A map of [topological spaces](../../../../../../topological-space.md) is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) when the [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md) $f^{-1}(U)$ is open in $X$ for every [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $U$ of $Y$. Equivalently, the [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md) of every [closed subset](../../../../../../closed-set.md) of $Y$ is closed in $X$.
