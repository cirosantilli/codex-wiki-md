<h1 id="e/e1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For every $\varepsilon>0$ there must be a $\delta>0$ such that

$$
\operatorname{dist}_X(x,x_0)<\delta\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{dist}_Y(f(x),f(x_0))<\varepsilon.
$$

The choice of $\delta$ may depend on $x_0$ and $\varepsilon$. This is the [epsilon-delta definition of continuity](../../../../../../epsilon-delta-definition-of-continuity.md).
