<h1 id="e/e1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each interval $(a,b)$ is open in the usual metric: if $x\in(a,b)$, the ball with radius $\min(x-a,b-x)/2$ is contained in it. Conversely, if $U$ is open and $x\in U$, the metric definition supplies $r>0$ with $(x-r,x+r)\subseteq U$. Thus every [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) is a [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) of open intervals, which proves **they form a basis**.
