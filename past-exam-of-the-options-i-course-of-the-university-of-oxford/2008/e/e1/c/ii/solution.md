<h1 id="e/e1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $f$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md), the sets in question are preimages of the open rays $(-\infty,\lambda)$ and $(\lambda,\infty)$, so they are open. Conversely, suppose all those sets are open. For every $a<b$,

$$
f^{-1}((a,b))=\{f>a\}\cap\{f<b\}
$$

is open as the [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) of two open sets. Part (i) and the basis criterion of part (b) then prove **$f$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md)**.
