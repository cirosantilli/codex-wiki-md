<h1 id="m/m2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

State one is left after one step and never revisited. The eventual probability mass in the closed class $\{4,5\}$ is

$$
w=(1-p)\lambda_1+\lambda_4+\lambda_5.
$$

Within this class the [stationary distribution](../../../../../../stationary-distribution.md) is $(b/(a+b),a/(a+b))$, because the balance equation is $a\pi_4=b\pi_5$. The two-state recurrence shows convergence from either starting state, since $|1-a-b|<1$. The other closed class never reaches state five. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb P(X_n=5)
=\frac a{a+b}\bigl((1-p)\lambda_1+\lambda_4+\lambda_5\bigr)}.
$$
