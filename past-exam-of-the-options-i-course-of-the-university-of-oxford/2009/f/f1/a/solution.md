<h1 id="f/f1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

There are real [Lagrange multipliers](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) $\lambda_1,\lambda_2$ such that

$$
\boxed{\nabla g(x_0)=\lambda_1\nabla f_1(x_0)+\lambda_2\nabla f_2(x_0)}.
$$

Equivalently $Dg(x_0)=\lambda^T Df(x_0)$. The rank assumption says the two constraint gradients are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md); it rules out an abnormal constraint point.
