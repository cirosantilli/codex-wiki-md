<h1 id="a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

The [splitting field](../../../../splitting-field.md) is the smallest [subfield](../../../../subfield.md) of $\mathbb C$ containing $\mathbb Q$ and all the [roots of a polynomial](../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) under consideration. Equivalently it is generated over $\mathbb Q$ by those roots, and the [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) splits there into linear factors.

Put $\zeta_6=e^{\pi i/3}$. All sixth [roots of unity](../../../../root-of-unity.md) are powers of $\zeta_6$, so the required [splitting field](../../../../splitting-field.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb Q(\zeta_6)=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})}.
$$

Indeed $\zeta_6=(1+\sqrt{-3})/2$, and conversely $\sqrt{-3}=2\zeta_6-1$. Its [field extension degree](../../../../degree-of-a-field-extension.md) is two.
