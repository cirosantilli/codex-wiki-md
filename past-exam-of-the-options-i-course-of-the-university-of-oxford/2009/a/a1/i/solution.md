↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [polynomial identity](../../../../../polynomial-identity.md)

$$
x^9-1=(x^3-1)(x^6+x^3+1)
$$

shows that the roots are the ninth [roots of unity](../../../../../root-of-unity.md) which are not third roots. These are precisely the primitive ninth [roots of unity](../../../../../root-of-unity.md), all powers of $\zeta_9=e^{2\pi i/9}$. Thus the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb Q(\zeta_9)$. The supplied [irreducibility](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) statement makes the displayed degree-six [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) of $\zeta_9$, giving

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb Q(\zeta_9):\mathbb Q]=6}.
$$
