<h1 id="a/a1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $g(x)=x^6+x^3+1$. The second [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is $g(-x)$. Negating the [roots of a polynomial](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) does not change the [field](../../../../../field.md) generated by them: each original [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) is minus a new [root](../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md), and conversely. Hence this [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) is again $\mathbb Q(\zeta_9)$ and its [field extension degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-field-extension.md) is **six**. Equivalently, it is the [cyclotomic field](../../../../../cyclotomic-field.md) $\mathbb Q(\zeta_{18})=\mathbb Q(\zeta_9)$, since $\zeta_{18}=-\zeta_9^5$.
