<h1 id="e/e2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $(U_i)_{i\in I}$ be an [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) of $f(X)$, with the sets open in $Y$. The [preimages](../../../../../../preimage.md) $f^{-1}(U_i)$ form an [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) of $X$, because $f$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). By [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md), finitely many of these preimages cover $X$. Their corresponding sets $U_i$ cover $f(X)$, providing a finite [subcover](../../../../../../subcover.md). Hence **the [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) image is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md)**.
