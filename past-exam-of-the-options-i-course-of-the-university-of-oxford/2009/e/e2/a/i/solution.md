↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A subset $A$ is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) when every [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) of $A$ has a finite [subcover](../../../../../../subcover.md). This may be stated using sets open in the ambient space or using the [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $A$; the definitions agree because every subspace-[open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) is the [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) of $A$ with an ambient-[open set](../../../../../../open-set.md).
