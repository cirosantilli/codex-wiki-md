<h1 id="e/e2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The family contains the empty set and the entire [real line](../../../../../../real-line.md). The [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) of two proper nonempty members is

$$
(a,\infty)\cap(b,\infty)=(\max(a,b),\infty),
$$

so it is closed under finite [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md). For an arbitrary nonempty family of proper rays, put $c=\inf_i a_i$. If the thresholds are unbounded below, the [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) is $\mathbb R$; otherwise the [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) is $(c,\infty)$. In the latter case $c$ itself is in no ray, and every point greater than $c$ lies above some threshold, proving the equality. Empty unions and unions involving $\mathbb R$ cause no difficulty. Thus arbitrary [unions](../../../../../../set-union.md) also belong to the family, establishing the [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) axioms.
