<h1 id="e/e1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Set $S=\{x_n:n\in\mathbb N,\ x_n\ne\ell\}$. This is a countable [set](../../../../../../set-split.md), hence a [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md) in the stated [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md), and $\ell\notin S$. Consequently $X\setminus S$ is an [open neighbourhood](../../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) of $\ell$. By [convergence of a sequence](../../../../../../convergence-of-a-sequence-in-a-topological-space.md), all sufficiently late terms lie outside $S$. A sequence term outside $S$ must equal $\ell$. Thus the sequence is **eventually constant**, and only finitely many indices have $x_n\ne\ell$.
