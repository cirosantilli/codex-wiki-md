<h1 id="e/e1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $S=\{x:f(x)\ne f(a)\}$, which is countable. For any [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md) $C\subseteq\mathbb R$, consider its [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md). If $f(a)\in C$, then $a\in f^{-1}(C)$ and that [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md) is closed by definition. If $f(a)\notin C$, then $f^{-1}(C)\subseteq S$, so it is countable and again closed. Therefore every [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md) has a closed [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md), proving **[continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md)**.
