↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The family $\mathcal F$ of [closed sets](../../../../../../closed-set.md) contains $\varnothing$ and $X$, is closed under arbitrary [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md), and is closed under finite [union](../../../../../../set-union.md). Conversely these axioms define a [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) by declaring the complements of the members of $\mathcal F$ to be the [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md).
