[Convergence of a sequence](../../../../../../convergence-of-a-sequence-in-a-topological-space.md) to $\ell$ means that every [open neighbourhood](../../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) $U$ of $\ell$ contains all sufficiently late terms: there is $N$ such that $x_n\in U$ for all $n\geq N$. The threshold may depend on $U$.
