<h1 id="o/o2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [O2](../o2.md)

With immediate release and ideal independent uniform sampling of fish, each catch is a [Bernoulli trial](../../../../bernoulli-trial.md) with tagged-success probability $p=k/N$. Stopping at the $r$th success makes the count of untagged catches a [negative binomial random variable](../../../../negative-binomial-distribution.md). A total of $x$ failures requires $r-1$ successes among the first $x+r-1$ trials and a success on the last trial, so the probability is $\binom{x+r-1}{r-1}p^r(1-p)^x$. Separate days use disjoint independent sequences of trials, hence their counts form an [independent and identically distributed](../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) sample. Equal catch probabilities alone do not mathematically imply [independence](../../../../independent-random-variables.md); this is the usual repeated independent sampling assumption in the stated model.
