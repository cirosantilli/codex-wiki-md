<h1 id="h/h1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The third particle is force-free, so its [torque](../../../../../torque.md) about the origin vanishes:

$$
\frac d{dt}\left(m_3\mathbf q_3\times\dot{\mathbf q}_3\right)
=\mathbf q_3\times m_3\ddot{\mathbf q}_3=0.
$$

Thus **this [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is conserved**.
