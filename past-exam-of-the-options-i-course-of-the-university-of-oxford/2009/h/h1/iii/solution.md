<h1 id="h/h1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\mathbf F$ be the force on particle one, so the force on particle two is $-\mathbf F$, with $\mathbf F$ parallel to $\mathbf q_1-\mathbf q_2$. The combined [torque](../../../../../torque.md) is

$$
\mathbf q_1\times\mathbf F+\mathbf q_2\times(-\mathbf F)
=(\mathbf q_1-\mathbf q_2)\times\mathbf F=0.
$$

Hence **the combined [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) of particles one and two is conserved**. Centrality of their interaction, in addition to equal and opposite forces, is what makes this [torque](../../../../../torque.md) sum zero.
