[Wilson's theorem](../../../../../wilson-s-theorem.md) states that an [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $p>1$ is a [prime number](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) if and only if

$$
\boxed{(p-1)!\equiv-1\pmod p}.
$$

In particular this holds for every prime, including $p=2$.
