<h1 id="c/c1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply [Fermat's little theorem](../../../../../fermat-little-theorem.md) and [Wilson's theorem](../../../../../wilson-s-theorem.md) in the [ring of integers modulo n](../../../../../modular-arithmetic-split.md) with modulus $p$. They give

$$
a^p+(p-1)!a\equiv a-a=0,\qquad
(p-1)!a^p+a\equiv-a+a=0\pmod p.
$$

A vanishing [modular congruence](../../../../../modular-congruence.md) modulo $p$ is precisely divisibility by $p$, so **both divisibilities hold**, without requiring $p\nmid a$.
