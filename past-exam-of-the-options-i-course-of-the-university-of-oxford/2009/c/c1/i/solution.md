[Fermat's little theorem](../../../../../fermat-little-theorem.md) says that for a [prime number](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$ and any [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $a$,

$$
\boxed{a^p\equiv a\pmod p}.
$$

If $p\nmid a$, cancellation gives the equivalent statement $a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p$.
