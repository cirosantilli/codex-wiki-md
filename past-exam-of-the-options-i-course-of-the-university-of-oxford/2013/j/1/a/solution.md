↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{i\hbar\partial_t\Psi=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\partial_x^2\Psi+V(x)\Psi.}
$$

A [stationary state](../../../../../stationary-state.md) of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E$ has a [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) $\Psi(x,t)=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$, where

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\psi''+V\psi=E\psi.
$$

Its time dependence is only a [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md), and its [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) is independent of time.
