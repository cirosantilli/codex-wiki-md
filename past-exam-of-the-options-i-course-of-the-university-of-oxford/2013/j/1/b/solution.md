<h1 id="j/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $s=1+it$. Logarithmic [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) gives

$$
\frac{\partial_t\Psi}{\Psi}=-\frac{i}{2s}+\frac{imx^2}{2\hbar s^2},\qquad \frac{\partial_x^2\Psi}{\Psi}=-\frac{m}{\hbar s}+\frac{m^2x^2}{\hbar^2s^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
i\hbar\partial_t\Psi=\left(\frac{\hbar}{2s}-\frac{mx^2}{2s^2}\right)\Psi=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\partial_x^2\Psi,
$$

so the appropriate [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) is $\boxed{V(x)=0}$. The [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) never vanishes, so no other time-independent potential gives the same equation for this precise phase choice.

The squared modulus is

$$
\boxed{|\Psi(x,t)|^2=\frac1{\sqrt{1+t^2}}\exp\left(-\frac{mx^2}{\hbar(1+t^2)}\right).}
$$

It changes width and height with time, so **this is not a [stationary state](../../../../../stationary-state.md)**. Its [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is the constant $\sqrt{\pi\hbar/m}$; multiplying the [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) by $(m/(\pi\hbar))^{1/4}$ normalizes it. The normalized [Gaussian wave packet](../../../../../gaussian-wave-packet.md) stays centred at zero but spreads, with [position](../../../../../position.md) [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) $\hbar(1+t^2)/(2m)$. It represents a localized [free particle](../../../../../free-particle.md) with a spread of momenta, rather than an [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md). [Free Gaussian wave packet evolution](../../../../../free-gaussian-wave-packet-evolution.md) broadens the packet even with zero mean [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md).
