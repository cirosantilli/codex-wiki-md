A $100(1-\alpha)\%$ [credible set](../../../../../credible-set.md) is a measurable subset $C(x)\subseteq(0,1)$ with posterior [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $1-\alpha$:

$$
\boxed{\int_{C(x)}\pi(\theta\mid x)\,d\theta=1-\alpha.}
$$

In ordinary language, **given the observed data and the chosen prior model, the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) that the parameter lies in this set is $1-\alpha$**. Such a set need not be an interval or be unique; its defining requirement is posterior mass.
