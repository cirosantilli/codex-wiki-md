<h1 id="o/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $F_{A,B}$ denote the distribution function of the posterior [beta distribution](../../../../../beta-distribution.md), with $A=a+s$ and $B=b+n-s$. This distribution is continuous with positive density on $(0,1)$. Its [equal-tailed credible interval](../../../../../equal-tailed-credible-interval.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left[F_{A,B}^{-1}(\alpha/2),\quad F_{A,B}^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)\right].}
$$

Compute the two quantiles by solving $\int_0^{\theta_L}\pi(\theta\mid x)d\theta=\alpha/2$ and $\int_{\theta_U}^1\pi(\theta\mid x)d\theta=\alpha/2$. Each excluded tail has mass $\alpha/2$, leaving posterior mass $1-\alpha$ between the endpoints.
