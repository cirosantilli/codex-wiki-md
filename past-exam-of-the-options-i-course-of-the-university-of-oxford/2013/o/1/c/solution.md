<h1 id="o/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $z=z_{1-\alpha/2}=\Phi^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)$, where $0<\alpha<1$. By the preceding [asymptotic normality](../../../../../asymptotic-normality.md) and [Slutsky's theorem](../../../../../slutsky-theorem.md), $\sqrt n(\widehat\theta-\theta)/\widehat\theta$ is approximately standard normal. Inverting $|\sqrt n(\widehat\theta-\theta)/\widehat\theta|\leq z$ gives the [Wald confidence interval](../../../../../wald-confidence-interval.md)

$$
\boxed{\left[\widehat\theta-z\frac{\widehat\theta}{\sqrt n},\quad\widehat\theta+z\frac{\widehat\theta}{\sqrt n}\right]\cap(0,\infty).}
$$

Its coverage approaches $1-\alpha$ as $n\to\infty$. The interpretation of the [confidence interval](../../../../../confidence-interval.md) is repeated-sample coverage of the fixed parameter; it does not assign a posterior [probability](../../../../../probability.md) to $\theta$.
