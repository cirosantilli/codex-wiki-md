<h1 id="m/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $0\leq z\leq1$, [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) and the [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) give

$$
\mathbb P(M_n\leq z)=\prod_{j=1}^n\mathbb P(X_j\leq z)=z^n.
$$

Since $M_n\leq1$, for $0<\varepsilon<1$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(|M_n-1|>\varepsilon)=(1-\varepsilon)^n\longrightarrow0.}
$$

For $\varepsilon\geq1$ the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is zero. This is exactly [convergence in probability](../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md) of $M_n$ to one.
