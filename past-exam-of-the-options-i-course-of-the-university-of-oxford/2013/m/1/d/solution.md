<h1 id="m/1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Independent marking with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $5/6$ is [Poisson thinning](../../../../../poisson-thinning.md) and gives a [Poisson process](../../../../../poisson-process-split.md) of chip-buying arrivals, of rate $12(5/6)=10$ per hour. Its first arrival time therefore satisfies

$$
\boxed{T_{\rm chips}\sim\operatorname{Exp}(10\text{ per hour}),\qquad\mathbb E T_{\rm chips}=\frac1{10}\text{ hour}=6\text{ minutes}.}
$$

For example, its survival [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is $\mathbb E[(1/6)^{N(t)}]=\exp(12t(1/6-1))=e^{-10t}$, which also derives the thinning conclusion directly.
