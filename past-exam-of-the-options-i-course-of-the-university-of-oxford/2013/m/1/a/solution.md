↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Poisson process](../../../../../poisson-process-split.md) has a [Poisson distribution](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) for the count in an interval of length $t$, with mean equal to rate times length. Fifteen minutes is $1/4$ hour, so

$$
\boxed{N(5{:}15)-N(5{:}00)\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(3),\qquad\mathbb P(N=k)=e^{-3}\frac{3^k}{k!},\quad k\geq0.}
$$
