<h1 id="m/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first arrival time $T_1$, measured in hours after opening, has [exponential distribution](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) of rate twelve. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(1/4<T_1<1/2)=e^{-12/4}-e^{-12/2}=e^{-3}-e^{-6}.}
$$

Equivalently, there must be no arrivals in the first quarter hour and at least one in the next; [independent increments](../../../../../independent-increments.md) give $e^{-3}(1-e^{-3})$.
