<h1 id="p/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The elimination multipliers are $m_{21}=2$, $m_{31}=4$, and $m_{32}=3$. The unit [lower triangular matrix](../../../../../lower-triangular-matrix.md) in the [LU factorization](../../../../../lu-decomposition.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{L=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\2&1&0\\4&3&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Direct multiplication checks

$$
LU=\begin{pmatrix}2&1&1\\4&3&3\\8&7&9\end{pmatrix}=A.
$$

The positive signs in $L$ undo the row subtractions used during [Gaussian elimination](../../../../../gaussian-elimination.md).
