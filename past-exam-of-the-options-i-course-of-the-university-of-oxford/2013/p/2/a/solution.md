<h1 id="p/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first pivot is two. [Gaussian elimination](../../../../../gaussian-elimination.md) without [partial pivoting](../../../../../columnwise-partial-pivoting.md) uses $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ and $R_3\leftarrow R_3-4R_1$, giving

$$
\begin{pmatrix}2&1&1\\0&1&1\\0&3&5\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The second pivot is one; $R_3\leftarrow R_3-3R_2$ gives the [upper triangular matrix](../../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md)

$$
\boxed{U=\begin{pmatrix}2&1&1\\0&1&1\\0&0&2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Both pivots used for division are nonzero, so no row exchange is needed.
