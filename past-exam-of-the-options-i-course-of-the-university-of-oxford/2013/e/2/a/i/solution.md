<h1 id="e/2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $q:X\to X/\mathcal R$ send each point to its [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md). The [quotient topology](../../../../../../quotient-topology.md) is

$$
\boxed{U\subseteq X/\mathcal R\text{ is open}\iff q^{-1}(U)\text{ is open in }X.}
$$

It is the finest [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) on the quotient set making the canonical surjection $q$ continuous. The preimage test, rather than a choice of representatives, defines its [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md).
