↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Yes: at least one factor [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) must be indiscrete.** Suppose instead that both have a nonempty proper [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md), say $\varnothing\ne U\subsetneq X$ and $\varnothing\ne V\subsetneq Y$. Then

$$
W=(U\times Y)\cup(X\times V)
$$

is open in the [product topology](../../../../../../product-topology.md). Both coordinate projections of $W$ are the full factors, because $U,V$ are nonempty. If $W$ were a rectangle $A\times B$, this would force $A=X$, $B=Y$, hence $W=X\times Y$. But choosing $x\notin U$ and $y\notin V$ gives $(x,y)\notin W$, a contradiction. Thus both factors cannot have nontrivial [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md). This is [rectangularity of every product-open set](../../../../../../rectangularity-of-every-product-open-set.md).
