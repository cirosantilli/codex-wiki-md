<h1 id="f/1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) states: let $F:U\subseteq\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$ be $C^1$, with $U$ open, and suppose $DF(x_0)$ is invertible. Then there exist open neighbourhoods $U_0\subseteq U$ of $x_0$ and $V_0$ of $F(x_0)$ such that $F:U_0\to V_0$ is a $C^1$ [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md). Its inverse derivative satisfies

$$
\boxed{D(F^{-1})(F(x))=(DF(x))^{-1},\qquad x\in U_0.}
$$

The conclusion is local; invertibility of one [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) does not assert global injectivity.
