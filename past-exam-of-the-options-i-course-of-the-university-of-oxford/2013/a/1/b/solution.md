<h1 id="a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $[L:F]=n<\infty$. The intermediate [field](../../../../../field.md) $K$ is an $F$-linear [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $L$, so the finite-dimensional [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) theorem gives $[K:F]\leq n$. To handle the other [field extension](../../../../../field-extension.md), take an $F$-[basis](../../../../../basis.md) $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ of $L$. Every $\ell\in L$ is a sum $\sum f_iv_i$ with $f_i\in F\subseteq K$, so the same list spans $L$ as a $K$-[vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md). It need not remain linearly independent, but spanning suffices to give $[L:K]\leq n$. Hence **both intermediate extensions are finite**.
