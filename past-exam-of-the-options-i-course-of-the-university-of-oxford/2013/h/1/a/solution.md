<h1 id="h/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $s$ be distance along the rod from its fixed end. The position of that material point is $s\,n(t)$, where $n=(\sin\theta\cos\phi,\sin\theta\sin\phi,-\cos\theta)$ is a [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md). Its [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is $s\dot n$, and direct differentiation gives

$$
|\dot n|^2=\dot\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi^2.
$$

The uniform linear [mass density](../../../../../density.md) is $M/a$. Integrating the elemental [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) therefore proves, rather than assumes, the rod formula:

$$
T=\frac12\int_0^a s^2|\dot n|^2\frac Ma\,ds=\boxed{\frac{Ma^2}{6}(\dot\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi^2).}
$$

Equivalently, its [moment of inertia](../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) about a perpendicular axis through the pivot is $Ma^2/3$.
