<h1 id="h/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) is at height $-a\cos\theta/2$. With gravitational [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) zero at the pivot level,

$$
V=-\frac{Mga}{2}\cos\theta.
$$

Hence the [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is

$$
\boxed{L=T-V=\frac{Ma^2}{6}(\dot\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi^2)+\frac{Mga}{2}\cos\theta.}
$$

Adding a constant to the potential would change the numerical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) reference but would not change the motion.
