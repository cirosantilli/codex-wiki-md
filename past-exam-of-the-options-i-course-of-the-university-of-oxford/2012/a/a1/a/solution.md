The [degree of a field extension](../../../../../degree-of-a-field-extension.md) is the dimension of the larger [field](../../../../../field.md), regarded as a [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over the smaller field:

$$
\boxed{[K:F]=\dim_F K.}
$$

The [scalar multiplication](../../../../../scalar-multiplication.md) is the multiplication of $K$ restricted to scalars in $F$. A finite extension has finite dimension; otherwise the degree is infinite.
