<h1 id="a/a1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose $\alpha\in K\setminus F$. Since $[K:F]=2$, the linearly independent elements $1,\alpha$ form a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $K$ over $F$. Thus there are $a,b\in F$ with $\alpha^2=a+b\alpha$. The assumption on the [characteristic of a field](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) allows division by two. Set

$$
\delta=\alpha-\frac b2.
$$

Completing the square gives

$$
\boxed{\delta^2=a+\frac{b^2}{4}\in F.}
$$

Also $\delta\notin F$, since otherwise $\alpha=\delta+b/2$ would lie in $F$. Because $\alpha\in F(\delta)$ and $\delta\in F(\alpha)$, these generated [subfields](../../../../../subfield.md) agree. The basis representation shows $F(\alpha)=K$, hence

$$
\boxed{K=F(\delta),\qquad\delta^2\in F.}
$$

The square-root generator is obtained by translating an arbitrary generator to remove the linear term of its quadratic relation.
