<h1 id="m/m2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The variables are not independent.** Their joint support excludes $X>1/2$ and $Y>1/2$ simultaneously, since every point with positive joint density satisfies $X+Y\leq1$. Yet each event separately has positive [probability](../../../../../../probability.md): the density is strictly positive on open triangular subregions where $X>1/2$, and similarly where $Y>1/2$. Thus

$$
\Pr(X>1/2,Y>1/2)=0<\Pr(X>1/2)\Pr(Y>1/2).
$$

This contradicts the event factorization required for [independence of random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md). The product-shaped formula $24xy$ on its support does not undo the dependence created by the triangular support.
