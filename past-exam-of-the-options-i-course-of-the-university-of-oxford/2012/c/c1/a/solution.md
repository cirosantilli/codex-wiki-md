<h1 id="c/c1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $n$, [Euler's totient function](../../../../../euler-totient-function.md) counts the integers in a complete residue system that are [coprime](../../../../../coprime-integers.md) to $n$:

$$
\boxed{\varphi(n)=\#\{k:1\leq k\leq n,\ \gcd(k,n)=1\}.}
$$

For $n>1$ this is equally the count with $0<k<n$. The convention gives $\varphi(1)=1$.
