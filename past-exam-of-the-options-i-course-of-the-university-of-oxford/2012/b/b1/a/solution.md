A left [group action](../../../../../group-action-split.md) of $G$ on a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $S$ is a map $G\times S\to S$, written $(g,s)\mapsto g\cdot s$, satisfying

$$
\boxed{e\cdot s=s,\qquad (gh)\cdot s=g\cdot(h\cdot s)}
$$

for every $s\in S$ and $g,h\in G$, where $e$ is the [identity element](../../../../../identity-element.md) of $G$. In particular each $g$ acts by a bijection, whose inverse is the action of $g^{-1}$.
