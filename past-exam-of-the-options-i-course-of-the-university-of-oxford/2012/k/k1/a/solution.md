<h1 id="k/k1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a steady flow the local time [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) in the [Euler equations](../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) vanishes. The vector identity

$$
(u\cdot\nabla)u=\nabla\frac{|u|^2}{2}-u\times(\nabla\times u)
$$

reduces to a gradient when the [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md) $\nabla\times u$ is zero. With constant density and gravity $-gk$, the momentum equation becomes

$$
\nabla\left(\frac p\rho+\frac{|u|^2}{2}+gz\right)=0.
$$

Thus on each connected flow region,

$$
\boxed{p+\frac12\rho|u|^2+\rho gz=\text{constant}.}
$$

This is [Bernoulli's theorem](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) for a steady irrotational flow: irrotationality makes the same constant apply throughout a connected region, rather than only along an individual streamline.
