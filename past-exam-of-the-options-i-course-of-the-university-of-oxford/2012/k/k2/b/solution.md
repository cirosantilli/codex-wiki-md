<h1 id="k/k2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [point vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) of circulation $\Gamma$ at $z_0$ contributes the [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) $-i\Gamma\log(z-z_0)/(2\pi)$ with the stated convention. The [method of images](../../../../../method-of-images.md) places a vortex of the opposite circulation at $\overline z_0$ below the wall, outside the fluid domain. Superposition with the stagnation flow gives

$$
\boxed{w(z)=\frac12z^2-\frac{i\Gamma}{2\pi}\log(z-z_0)
+\frac{i\Gamma}{2\pi}\log(z-\overline z_0).}
$$

For real $z=x$, the distances $|x-z_0|$ and $|x-\overline z_0|$ agree. The two vortex contributions to the [stream function](../../../../../stream-function.md) are $-\Gamma\log|x-z_0|/(2\pi)$ and $+\Gamma\log|x-\overline z_0|/(2\pi)$, which cancel. The background [stream function](../../../../../stream-function.md) is also zero there, so $y=0$ remains an impermeable streamline. The image changes the boundary condition without introducing another singularity in the upper fluid region.
