↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An embedded $k$-dimensional [submanifold](../../../../../submanifold.md) $M\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is locally a coordinate $k$-plane: for every $p\in M$, there are open neighbourhoods $U\ni p$, $V\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ and a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) $\Phi:U\to V$ with

$$
\Phi(M\cap U)=V\cap(\mathbb R^k\times\{0\}^{n-k}).
$$

For the present $C^1$ setting, $\Phi$ and its inverse are continuously differentiable; in the smooth convention both are smooth.

A [tangent vector](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) to $M$ at $p$ is a [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $\gamma'(0)$ of a continuously differentiable curve $\gamma:(-\epsilon,\epsilon)\to M$ with $\gamma(0)=p$. The [tangent space](../../../../../tangent-space.md) is the collection of all these vectors:

$$
\boxed{T_pM=\{\gamma'(0):\gamma(0)=p,\ \gamma\text{ is a }C^1\text{ curve in }M\}.}
$$

In the local coordinates above, it is $D\Phi(p)^{-1}(\mathbb R^k\times\{0\}^{n-k})$, so it is a $k$-dimensional linear subspace of $\mathbb R^n$.
