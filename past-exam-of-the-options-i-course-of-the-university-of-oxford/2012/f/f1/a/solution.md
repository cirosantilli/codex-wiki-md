↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The function is [continuously differentiable](../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md) when all its first [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) exist on $U$ and are continuous there. Equivalently, $h$ has a [Fréchet derivative](../../../../../frechet-derivative.md) at every point and the map $x\mapsto Dh(x)$ is continuous. For a scalar-valued function,

$$
Dh(x)v=\sum_{j=1}^n\partial_jh(x)v_j.
$$

On the open domain, continuous first [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) ensure that this linear expression is the total [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md).
