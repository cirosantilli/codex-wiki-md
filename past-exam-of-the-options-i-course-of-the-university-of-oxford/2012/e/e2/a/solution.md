↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [basis of a topology](../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) $\mathcal B$ is a family of open subsets such that every open set is a union of members of $\mathcal B$. Equivalently, for every $U\in\mathcal T$ and $x\in U$, some $B\in\mathcal B$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{x\in B\subseteq U.}
$$

The empty open set is represented by the empty union.
