↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The map is $K$-[Lipschitz continuous](../../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) when

$$
\boxed{d_Y(f(x),f(x'))\leq Kd_X(x,x')\quad\text{for every }x,x'\in X.}
$$

The same finite [Lipschitz constant](../../../../../../lipschitz-constant.md) $K$ must work for all pairs of points.
