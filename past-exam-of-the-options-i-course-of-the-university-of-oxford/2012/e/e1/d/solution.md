↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For fixed $x\in X$, the function $a\mapsto d(x,a)$ on $A$ is continuous, indeed $1$-[Lipschitz continuous](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) by the [reverse triangle inequality](../../../../../reverse-triangle-inequality.md). A continuous real-valued function on a nonempty [compact space](../../../../../compact-space.md) attains its minimum by the [extreme value theorem](../../../../../extreme-value-theorem.md). Hence some $a_0\in A$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{d(x,a_0)=\min_{a\in A}d(x,a)=D_A(x).}
$$

The metric is symmetric, so this is also $d(a_0,x)$. Compactness supplies attainment of the infimum.
