<h1 id="e/e1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Given $\epsilon>0$, choose $\delta=\epsilon/K>0$. If $d_X(x,x')<\delta$, the [Lipschitz condition](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) yields

$$
d_Y(f(x),f(x'))\leq Kd_X(x,x')<K\delta=\epsilon.
$$

This single $\delta$ works for every pair, proving the map is [uniformly continuous](../../../../../uniform-continuity.md):

$$
\boxed{\delta(\epsilon)=\epsilon/K.}
$$
