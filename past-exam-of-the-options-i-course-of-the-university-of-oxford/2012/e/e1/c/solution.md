<h1 id="e/e1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [distance to a set](../../../../../distance-to-a-set.md) is finite and nonnegative because $A$ is nonempty. For $x,x'\in X$ and every $a\in A$, the [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) gives

$$
d(x,a)\leq d(x,x')+d(x',a).
$$

Taking the infimum over $a$ yields $D_A(x)\leq d(x,x')+D_A(x')$. Interchanging $x$ and $x'$ gives the opposite inequality. Together these imply

$$
\boxed{|D_A(x)-D_A(x')|\leq d(x,x').}
$$

Thus the [distance to a set](../../../../../distance-to-a-set.md) is $1$-[Lipschitz continuous](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md). No closedness, compactness or attainment of the infimum was used.
