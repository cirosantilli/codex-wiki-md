↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [Bernoulli distribution](../../../../../../bernoulli-distribution.md), with $S=\sum_iX_i$,

$$
\ell_n(\theta)=S\log\theta+(n-S)\log(1-\theta),
\qquad
-\ell_n''(\theta)=\frac S{\theta^2}+\frac{n-S}{(1-\theta)^2}.
$$

Using $\mathbb ES=n\theta$, the expected [Fisher information](../../../../../../fisher-information-matrix.md) is

$$
I_n(\theta)=\frac n\theta+\frac n{1-\theta}
=\boxed{\frac n{\theta(1-\theta)}}.
$$
