<h1 id="o/o2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The future [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) response is $E_*=\alpha+\beta x_*+\gamma z_*$. By invariance of [maximum likelihood estimation](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimation-split.md), its estimator is the fitted [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md)

$$
\boxed{\widehat E_*=\widehat\alpha+x_*\widehat\beta+z_*\widehat\gamma.}
$$

Its sampling [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is the quadratic form of the coefficient [covariance matrix](../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) with $c=(1,x_*,z_*)^T$:

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(\widehat E_*)=c^T\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\theta)c
=\sigma^2\left(\frac1n+\frac{x_*^2}{S_{xx}}+\frac{z_*^2}{S_{zz}}\right).}
$$

This estimator is unbiased because each fitted coefficient is unbiased. The future error $\epsilon_*$ does not enter the estimator of the [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md), so its independent [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is not part of this expression.
