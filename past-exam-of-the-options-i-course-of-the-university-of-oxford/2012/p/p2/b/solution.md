<h1 id="p/p2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For one interval, the [trapezoidal rule](../../../../../trapezoidal-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{I_1=\frac12\{0^4+1^4\}=\frac12.}
$$

For two intervals of width $h=1/2$, the composite [trapezoidal rule](../../../../../trapezoidal-rule.md) gives half weights at the endpoints and full weight at the internal node:

$$
\boxed{I_{1/2}=\frac12\left\{\frac12\,0^4+\left(\frac12\right)^4+\frac12\,1^4\right\}
=\frac9{32}.}
$$
