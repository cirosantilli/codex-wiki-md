<h1 id="p/p2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply the first [Richardson extrapolation](../../../../../richardson-extrapolation.md) step to the two computed approximations:

$$
\boxed{R_1(1)=\frac{4(9/32)-1/2}{3}=\frac5{24}.}
$$

The exact integral is $I=\int_0^1x^4\,dx=1/5$, so the actual absolute error is

$$
\boxed{\left|\frac5{24}-\frac15\right|=\frac1{120}\approx0.00833<10^{-2}.}
$$

Thus the requested strict error bound is verified directly. This first extrapolation of trapezoidal approximations is also the one-panel [Simpson's rule](../../../../../simpson-s-rule.md) value on the three nodes $0,1/2,1$.
