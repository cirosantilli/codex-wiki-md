<h1 id="p/p1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A polynomial $p_n\in\Pi_n$ is a best [polynomial least-squares approximation](../../../../../polynomial-least-squares-approximation.md) when it minimizes the weighted error [norm](../../../../../norm.md) over all polynomials of degree at most $n$:

$$
\boxed{\|f-p_n\|\leq\|f-q\|\quad\text{for every }q\in\Pi_n.}
$$

Equivalently it minimizes $\int_0^1x^{-1/2}(f(x)-q(x))^2\,dx$. The weight is integrable, so the expression is finite for continuous functions; it defines a genuine [norm](../../../../../norm.md) because a continuous function with zero weighted squared integral must be identically zero.
