# Photon-number half-cone under relativistic aberration

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic aberration](relativistic-aberration.md)

For isotropic planar photon emission in a rest frame, [aberration of light](relativistic-aberration.md) maps its forward half of the angular directions into a lab cone of semi-angle $\alpha$ satisfying the displayed equation. This follows from $\cos\theta=(\cos\theta'+\beta)/(1+\beta\cos\theta')$ at $\theta'=\pm\pi/2$. For a fast positive [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md), $\alpha\sim1/\gamma$. The half fraction concerns [photon](photon.md) count or directions: the [relativistic Doppler effect](relativistic-doppler-effect.md) changes the angular energy weights, so the cone need not contain half the radiant energy.
