# Rolling disk with a rim point mass

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

A uniform disk of [mass](mass.md) $2M$ and radius $R$ carries an extra [point mass](point-mass.md) $M$ on its rim. For [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md), let $\theta=0$ place that [mass](mass.md) at the contact. Its position is $[R(\theta-\sin\theta),R(1-\cos\theta)]$. Combining disk translation, disk rotation and point motion gives the displayed [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md). Its small-amplitude [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is $\sqrt{g/(3R)}$, whereas the same object pivoted at its centre has [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\sqrt{g/(2R)}$.
