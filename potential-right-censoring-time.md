# Potential right-censoring time

↑ **Parent:** [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md)

A grid of potential [right censoring](right-censoring.md) times includes every observed censoring time. Intervals between consecutive grid points have no internal censoring, so their [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md) factors telescope to a single ratio of event-free survivors to individuals observed across the interval. This gives the same estimator when endpoint [risk sets](risk-set.md) are counted consistently.
