# Spacetime supercharge from an RNS spin field

↑ **Parent:** [RNS spin field](rns-spin-field.md)

In the critical [RNS string](spinning-string.md), a spacetime [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) generator is $Q_A=\oint e^{-\phi_{\rm gh}/2}S_A$. The [RNS spin field](rns-spin-field.md) has weight $5/8$ and the superghost exponential has weight $3/8$, making a weight-one current. The [GSO projection](gso-projection.md) ensures the locality needed to act on retained vertex operators. Its operator products generate translations and relate [NS sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md) and [R sector](ramond-sector.md) states.
