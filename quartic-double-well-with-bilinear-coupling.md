# Quartic double well with bilinear coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Critical point](critical-point.md)

The [stationary points](stationary-point.md) of this [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) are the origin and $(a,a)$, $(-a,-a)$. Its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) at the origin has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\pm4a^2$, so the origin is a [saddle point](saddle-point.md). At each of the other points the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $8a^2$ and $16a^2$, giving strict [local minima](local-minimum.md). The identity

$$
g=(x^2-y^2)^2+2(xy-a^2)^2-2a^4
$$

shows that they are also the only [global minima](global-minimum.md), with value $-2a^4$.

For $-2a^4<c<0$, the [level set](level-set.md) $g=c$ has two closed components, one surrounding each minimum. The zero [level set](level-set.md) has two lobes meeting at the origin with the coordinate axes as tangent directions. For $c>0$, there is one closed outer component. These statements also follow in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) from the quadratic equation in $r^2$, together with the absence of other [stationary points](stationary-point.md).
