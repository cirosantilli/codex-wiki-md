# Spherical blast-wave similarity energy integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Self-similar blast wave](self-similar-blast-wave.md)

Use the fields of [pressure similarity in a spherical blast wave](pressure-similarity-in-a-spherical-blast-wave.md), with front radius $R=\xi_0\ell$. Since $\rho_0\ell^5/t^2=E$, changing the radial integration variable to $\xi$ gives

$$
E_{\rm kin}=2\pi E\int_0^{\xi_0}\xi^4f_\rho f_u^2\,d\xi,\qquad
E_{\rm int}=\frac{4\pi E}{\gamma-1}\int_0^{\xi_0}\xi^2f_p\,d\xi.
$$

Both are time independent for fixed integrable [similarity profiles](similarity-profile.md) and constant front coordinate $\xi_0$. With explosion energy $E$ and negligible external energy input or losses, their sum equals $E$. This normalization, together with shock matching and regularity, fixes the dimensionless blast coefficient rather than [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) alone.
