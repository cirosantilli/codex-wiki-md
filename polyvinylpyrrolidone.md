# Polyvinylpyrrolidone

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

A [polymer](polymer-split.md) containing pyrrolidone groups, widely used as a soluble stabilizer or capping agent. In [silver](silver.md) nanostructure synthesis its [adsorption](adsorption.md) can affect [facet](facet.md) growth and aggregation. Residual [polymer](polymer-split.md) can also create electrically resistive [nanowire](nanowire.md) junctions.
