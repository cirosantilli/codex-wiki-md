# Double Compton scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md)

[Double Compton scattering](double-compton-scattering.md) is $e^-+\gamma\to e^-+\gamma+\gamma$, together with its inverse. The forward reaction has one incoming and two outgoing [photons](photon.md), so it changes their number and can source the [divergence](divergence.md) of the [photon number current](photon-number-current.md). Ordinary [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md) has one incoming and one outgoing [photon](photon.md) and conserves [photon](photon.md) number. Forward and inverse rates can balance without either reaction individually conserving it.
