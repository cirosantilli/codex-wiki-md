# Magnetic sublevel

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic quantum number](magnetic-quantum-number.md)

One of the [angular momentum eigenstates](angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) in a multiplet, distinguished by the [magnetic quantum number](magnetic-quantum-number.md) along a chosen axis. An applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) can split their [energy levels](energy-level.md) through [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md).
