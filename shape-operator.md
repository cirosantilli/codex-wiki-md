# Shape operator

↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)

The shape operator is an extrinsic-curvature construction in the [differential geometry of surfaces](differential-geometry-of-surfaces.md).

For an [oriented surface](orientation-of-a-surface.md) with [Gauss map](gauss-map.md) $N$, the shape operator at $p$ is the self-adjoint linear map

$$
S_p=-dN_p:T_pS\to T_pS.
$$
