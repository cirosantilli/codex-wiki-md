# Sentence (mathematical logic)

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentence_(mathematical_logic))

A [sentence](sentence-mathematical-logic.md) is a closed formula, with no free variable occurrences. Its truth does not depend on a variable assignment. A [first-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md) uses a first-order formal language.
