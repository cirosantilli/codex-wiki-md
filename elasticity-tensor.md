# Elasticity tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

The [elasticity tensor](elasticity-tensor.md) relates [stress](stress.md) to infinitesimal [strain](strain.md) by $\sigma_{ij}=C_{ijkl}\epsilon_{kl}$. In conservative [linear](linearity.md) elasticity, the minor symmetries reflect symmetric [stress](stress.md) and [strain](strain.md), while the major symmetry $C_{ijkl}=C_{klij}$ expresses the existence of an elastic energy [density](density.md).
