# Residual resistivity

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md)

The [residual resistivity](residual-resistivity.md) is the low-[temperature](temperature-split.md) [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md) remaining in a normal [metal](metal.md) when thermally excited scattering becomes negligible. Static disorder, impurities and boundaries can relax carrier [momentum](momentum-split.md) without requiring a thermal population of [phonons](phonon.md). Its value depends on sample purity and structure; a superconducting transition is a different phenomenon.
