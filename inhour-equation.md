# Inhour equation

↑ **Parent:** [Point kinetics model](point-kinetics-model.md)

The characteristic relation between imposed [reactor reactivity](reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md) and exponential growth or decay rates in a [point kinetics model](point-kinetics-model.md). Several kinetic modes can coexist; the late-time behaviour is determined by the mode with greatest real growth rate that is actually excited.
