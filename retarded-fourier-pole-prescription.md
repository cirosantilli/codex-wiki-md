# Retarded Fourier pole prescription

↑ **Parent:** [Retarded fundamental solution](retarded-fundamental-solution.md)

For inverse time [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) $\int\widehat G(\omega)e^{-i\omega t}d\omega/(2\pi)$, a causal stable oscillator has its poles below the real frequency axis. Undamped real poles are therefore displaced by the prescription $\omega\mapsto\omega+i0$ in its frequency polynomial. For $t>0$, closing the contour below encloses poles with clockwise orientation; for $t<0$, closing above gives zero. Changing the transform's sign reverses the corresponding prescription. For a positive oscillator operator $\partial_t^2+\omega_0^2$, the inverse is $[\omega_0^2-(\omega+i0)^2]^{-1}$ and its time response is $\Theta(t)\sin(\omega_0t)/\omega_0$.
