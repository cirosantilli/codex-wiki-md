# Seifert algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md)

Apply the [oriented smoothing](oriented-smoothing.md) to every crossing of an oriented [knot diagram](knot-diagram.md), fill the resulting [Seifert circles](seifert-circle.md) by disjoint disks at suitable heights, and reconnect them by twisted bands at the original crossings. The boundary is the original [knot](knot.md). If the connected surface has $s$ disks and $c$ bands, its [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) is $s-c$ and its [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) is $(1-s+c)/2$.
