# Velocity-space measure

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity space](velocity-space.md)

For a gyrophase-independent [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md), $d^3v=2\pi v_\perp\,dv_\perp\,dv_\parallel$. With [first adiabatic invariant](first-adiabatic-invariant.md) $\mu=v_\perp^2/(2B)$, this gives the measure in the title. When the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) changes, both the measure and any explicit factor $\mu B$ in a [moment](moment.md) must be perturbed.
