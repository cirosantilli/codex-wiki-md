# Suppressed spin from a force-free distant sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Self-mobility correction from a distant force-free sphere](self-mobility-correction-from-a-distant-force-free-sphere.md)

For a forced sphere and a distant equal [force-free](force-free.md), [torque-free](torque-free.md) sphere, the leading incident [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) strain is proportional to $(\mathbf U\cdot\mathbf n)(I-3\mathbf n\mathbf n)$. Its reflected [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md) is axisymmetric about the line of centers and has zero [vorticity](vorticity.md) on that line. The nominal fifth-order self-spin coefficient therefore vanishes. Finite-size incident-strain corrections and higher force-free scattering multipoles first supply a generic seventh-order [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md). Purely longitudinal forcing has zero spin exactly by axial symmetry. The leading translation correction is fourth order for a generic orientation, but sixth order for purely transverse forcing. These cancellations matter when using the [method of reflections for Stokes flow](method-of-reflections-for-stokes-flow.md) to infer orders from far-field decay alone.
