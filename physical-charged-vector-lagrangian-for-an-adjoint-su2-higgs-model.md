# Physical charged-vector Lagrangian for an adjoint SU2 Higgs model

↑ **Parent:** [SU(2) gauge theory with an adjoint Higgs field](su-2-gauge-theory-with-an-adjoint-higgs-field.md)

For a real triplet potential $\lambda(|\phi|^2-v^2)^2/8$ and canonical gauge coupling $g_c$, [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md) gives one neutral [Higgs mode](higgs-mode.md), two oppositely charged massive vectors and one massless Abelian vector. The scalar kinetic contribution is $\tfrac12(\partial\eta)^2+g_c^2(v+\eta)^2W^+W^-$, and its potential is $\lambda v^2\eta^2/2+\lambda v\eta^3/2+\lambda\eta^4/8$. This potential convention is distinct from using $\lambda(|\phi|^2-v^2)^2/4$.
