# BGK model

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation-split.md)

Replace the [nonlinear Boltzmann collision operator](nonlinear-boltzmann-collision-operator.md) by relaxation to the [local Maxwellian](local-maxwellian.md) $\mathcal M[f]$ with the same [mass density](density.md), [mean velocity](mean-velocity-of-a-kinetic-distribution.md) and random [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) as $f$. Matching those [velocity moments](velocity-moment.md) ensures vanishing collision contributions to [mass](mass.md), [momentum](momentum-split.md) and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) balance. The [collision relaxation time](collision-relaxation-time.md) $\tau$ is often treated as a constant, although microscopic collision rates generally depend on the state and particle [velocity](velocity.md).
