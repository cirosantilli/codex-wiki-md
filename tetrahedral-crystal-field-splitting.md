# Tetrahedral crystal field splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal field theory](crystal-field-theory.md)

In the ideal tetrahedral point-charge model, the five metal [d orbitals](d-orbital.md) split into a lower twofold $e$ set and an upper threefold $t_2$ set. Relative to their weighted barycentre their energies are $-3\Delta_t/5$ and $+2\Delta_t/5$, where $\Delta_t>0$ is the separation. For occupancies $e^{n_e}t_2^{n_t}$, the [crystal field stabilization energy](crystal-field-stabilization-energy.md) is $(-0.6n_e+0.4n_t)\Delta_t$. Under weak-field high-spin filling it is $-0.6\Delta_t$, $-1.2\Delta_t$, $-0.8\Delta_t$ and zero for $d^6,d^7,d^8,d^{10}$ respectively. Shorter metal–ligand distances generally increase the splitting, so orbital filling can superimpose nonmonotonic bond-length changes on an ionic-size trend. The model alone does not determine an absolute [bond length](bond-length.md).
