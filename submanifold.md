# Submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submanifold)

A submanifold is a [manifold](topological-manifold.md) sitting inside another manifold through an immersion or embedding. An [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md) has the subspace topology and local coordinates in which it is a coordinate plane. Smooth [transverse intersections](transverse-intersection.md) are governed by the tangent spaces of the participating submanifolds.
