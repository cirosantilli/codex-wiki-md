<h1 id="section-b/24/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $S_{12}=\langle\phi_1|\phi_2\rangle$ be real, with each atomic orbital normalized. Then

$$
\boxed{\langle\psi|\psi\rangle=c_1^2+c_2^2+2c_1c_2S_{12}=1.}
$$

Together with orthonormal spin functions, this also normalizes the [Slater determinant](../../../../../../../slater-determinant.md): its norm squared is $\langle\psi|\psi\rangle^2$. Under the subsequent zero-overlap assumption,

$$
\boxed{c_1=\sqrt{1-c_2^2},\qquad -1\le c_2\le1,}
$$

using the specified nonnegative branch for $c_1$.
