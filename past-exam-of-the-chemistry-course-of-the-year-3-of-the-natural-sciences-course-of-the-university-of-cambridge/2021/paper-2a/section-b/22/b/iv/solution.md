<h1 id="section-b/22/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Without quencher, $\phi_{P,0}=k_P/(k_P+k_D)$. Taking the ratio gives the [Stern-Volmer equation](../../../../../../../stern-volmer-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{\phi_{P,0}}{\phi_P}=1+\frac{k_Q}{k_P+k_D}[Q]
=1+k_Q\tau_0[Q],\qquad\tau_0=(k_P+k_D)^{-1}.}
$$

Thus the plot has **unit intercept and slope $k_Q\tau_0$**. A triplet formation yield less than one would cancel from this ratio if it remained independent of quencher concentration. The result assumes dynamic triplet quenching and no additional quencher-induced absorption or singlet loss.[Definition and dynamic-quenching relation between quantum-yield ratios, quencher concentration and unquenched lifetime](../../../../../../../stern-volmer-kinetic-relationships-in-the-iupac-gold-book.md)
