<h1 id="section-b/22/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Vibrational and solvent relaxation remove energy before emission, producing a Stokes shift.** [Absorption](../../../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) initially excites a vertical transition from the populated ground-state vibrational levels to excited-state vibrational levels. [Vibrational relaxation](../../../../../../../vibrational-relaxation.md) and solvent rearrangement then bring the molecule near the relaxed lowest excited singlet level before [fluorescence](../../../../../../../fluorescence-split.md). Emission ends in vibrationally excited levels of the electronic ground state, so the emitted photon generally has less energy and a longer wavelength. This explanation does not require a change of electronic multiplicity.
