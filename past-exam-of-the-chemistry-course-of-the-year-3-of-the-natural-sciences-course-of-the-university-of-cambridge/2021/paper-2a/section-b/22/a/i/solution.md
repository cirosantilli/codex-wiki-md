↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [absorption spectrum](../../../../../../../absorption-spectrum.md), pass a scanned monochromatic beam through the solution and a matched solvent reference. Record the incident and transmitted intensities and calculate the [absorbance](../../../../../../../absorbance.md) $A(\lambda)=\log_{10}[I_0(\lambda)/I(\lambda)]$, using an appropriate blank and concentration to avoid saturation.

For the [fluorescence emission spectrum](../../../../../../../fluorescence-emission-spectrum.md), excite at a fixed wavelength within an absorption band, collect emission away from the incident beam, commonly at $90^\circ$, and scan an emission monochromator. Correct the detected signal for wavelength-dependent detector sensitivity, subtract solvent/background light and exclude scattered excitation light. The two plotted vertical scales have been independently normalized; their equal peak heights do not imply equal absorption and emission rates or unit [fluorescence quantum yield](../../../../../../../fluorescence-quantum-yield.md).
