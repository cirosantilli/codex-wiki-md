<h1 id="section-b/21/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The nine normal coordinates transform as $A_{1g}+B_{1g}+B_{2g}+A_{2u}+B_{2u}+2E_u$.** Place the molecule in the $xy$ plane, with its four outer atoms on the $x$ and $y$ axes, as specified for the vertical reflection planes. In the class order

$$
E,\ 2C_4,\ C_2,\ 2C'_2,\ 2C''_2,\ i,\ 2S_4,\ \sigma_h,\ 2\sigma_v,\ 2\sigma_d,
$$

the fifteen Cartesian displacements have [characters of a representation](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md)

$$
\chi_{3N}=(15,1,-1,-3,-1,-3,-1,5,3,1).
$$

For each operation this character is the number of fixed atoms times the trace of its polar-vector matrix. Subtract [translations](../../../../../../../translation-geometry.md) $A_{2u}+E_u$ and [rotations](../../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) $A_{2g}+E_g$, giving

$$
\chi_{\rm vib}=(9,-1,1,-1,1,-3,-1,5,3,1).
$$

[Molecular character reduction](../../../../../../../molecular-character-reduction.md) in the [D4h molecular point group](../../../../../../../d4h-molecular-point-group.md) yields the stated decomposition. Its dimensions sum to $1+1+1+1+1+2(2)=9=3(5)-6$, providing an independent degree-of-freedom check.
