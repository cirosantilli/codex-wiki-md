↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An [infrared](../../../../../../../infrared.md) fundamental must transform like a component of the [electric dipole moment](../../../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md); a [Raman spectroscopy](../../../../../../../raman-spectroscopy.md) fundamental must transform like a component of the symmetric [polarizability](../../../../../../../electric-polarizability.md) tensor. For this centrosymmetric [D4h molecular point group](../../../../../../../d4h-molecular-point-group.md), the resulting classification is:

| Symmetry | Number of modes of this symmetry | Infrared | Raman | $A_{1g}$ | 1 | No | Yes: $x^2+y^2,z^2$ | $B_{1g}$ | 1 | No | Yes: $x^2-y^2$ | $B_{2g}$ | 1 | No | Yes: $xy$ | $A_{2u}$ | 1 | **Parallel**, $z$ | No | $B_{2u}$ | 1 | No | No | $E_u$ | 2, each doubly degenerate | **Perpendicular**, $(x,y)$ | No |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Here parallel and perpendicular refer to the principal fourfold axis. The table obeys [infrared and Raman mutual exclusion](../../../../../../../infrared-and-raman-mutual-exclusion.md).
