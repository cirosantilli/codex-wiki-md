<h1 id="section-b/21/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

With integer fluorine mass $m_B=19m_{\rm u}$ and $R=1.5\ \mathring{\mathrm A}$,

$$
\cos\theta=1-\frac{146.588}{4(19)(1.5)^2}=0.14276,
\qquad\boxed{\theta\simeq81.8^\circ.}
$$

This angle is below the planar limit $90^\circ$, consistent with the proposed pyramid. The sulfur mass does not enter this axial [moment of inertia](../../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md).
