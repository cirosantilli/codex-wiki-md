↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**All the listed modes are Raman active; only $A_1$ and $E$ are infrared active.** For the [C4v molecular point group](../../../../../../../c4v-molecular-point-group.md), with vertical planes through the outer atoms:

| Symmetry | Number of modes of this symmetry | Infrared | Raman | $A_1$ | 2 | **Parallel**, $z$ | Yes: $x^2+y^2,z^2$ | $B_1$ | 2 | No | Yes: $x^2-y^2$ | $B_2$ | 1 | No | Yes: $xy$ | $E$ | 2, each doubly degenerate | **Perpendicular**, $(x,y)$ | Yes: $(xz,yz)$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

There is no inversion centre, so [infrared and Raman mutual exclusion](../../../../../../../infrared-and-raman-mutual-exclusion.md) does not apply.
