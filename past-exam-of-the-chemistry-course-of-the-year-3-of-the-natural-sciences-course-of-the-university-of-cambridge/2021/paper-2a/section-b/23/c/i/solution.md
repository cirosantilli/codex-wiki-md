<h1 id="section-b/23/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The nontotally symmetric longitudinal modes are the two $\Sigma_u^+$ coordinates $t$ and $a$. Both have $q_3-q_2=0$, so the middle spring is not extended. Hence its [spring constant](../../../../../../../spring-constant.md) cannot alter their energies:

$$
Dt=0,\qquad Da=\frac{2k}{m}a,
\qquad\boxed{\omega_t=0,\quad\omega_a=\sqrt{2k/m}.}
$$

These results are exact for every positive $\gamma$, not just perturbative. The zero mode expresses overall [translation invariance](../../../../../../../translation-invariance.md).
