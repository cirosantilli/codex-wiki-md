↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**$\beta=-2.4\ \mathrm{eV}$ and $\alpha=-6.8\ \mathrm{eV}$.** The [Hückel method](../../../../../../huckel-method.md) gives benzene's occupied frontier level as $\epsilon_H=\alpha+\beta$ and its unoccupied frontier level as $\epsilon_L=\alpha-\beta$. Therefore

$$
4.8=\epsilon_L-\epsilon_H=-2\beta.
$$

Using the [Koopmans' theorem](../../../../../../koopmans-theorem.md) approximation for the [ionization energy](../../../../../../ionization-energy.md), $I\simeq-\epsilon_H=9.2$, gives $\alpha+\beta=-9.2$ and the stated parameters. This calibration neglects orbital relaxation, electron correlation and any distinction between a measured optical excitation and an independent-particle orbital gap.
