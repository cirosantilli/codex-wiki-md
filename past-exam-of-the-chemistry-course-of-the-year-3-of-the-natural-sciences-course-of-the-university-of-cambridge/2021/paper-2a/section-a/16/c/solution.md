<h1 id="section-a/16/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**The descending line is $^3T_{1g}(F)$; the low rising line is $^3T_{2g}$; the steep line is $^3A_{2g}$; and the branch starting at $15B$ is $^3T_{1g}(P)$.** Their high-field slopes, in the same order, are

$$
\boxed{-\frac45,\quad\frac15,\quad\frac65,\quad\frac15.}
$$

These are slopes of the supplied absolute term energies relative to their chosen reference, not slopes of transition energies measured upward from the ground state. The two $T_{1g}$ states share a [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) and mix, whereas the crossing of $A_{2g}$ and the upper $T_{1g}$ branch is allowed because they have different [irreducible representations](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md).
