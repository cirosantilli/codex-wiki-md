<h1 id="9/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a neutral [borane](../../../../../../borane.md) $\mathrm{B_nH_m}$ with one terminal B–H bond assigned to each vertex, the skeletal [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) number is $3n+m-2n=n+m$; an overall charge $q-$ adds $q$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). Suitable twelve-electron correspondences are

$$
\boxed{\mathrm C=B_4H_8,\qquad D=[B_3H_8]^- .}
$$

For C, four BH contributions plus four additional [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) give $8+4=12$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). For D, three BH contributions plus five additional [hydrogens](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) and the negative charge give $6+5+1=12$. They map to the tetrahedral and triangular low-vertex skeletons respectively. The neutral formal alternative for D is $\mathrm{B_3H_9}$, also with twelve skeletal [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md).

The [electron counting](../../../../../../electron-counting.md) correspondence alone must not be read as evidence that the neutral [boranes](../../../../../../borane.md) are isolable stable bulk materials. Low-vertex hydrogen-rich [boranes](../../../../../../borane.md) can be transient; **the triangular octahydrotriborate anion is the established practical three-boron example**, whereas neutral $\mathrm{B_3H_9}$ is a formal analogy rather than an equally established preparative product. The requested skeletal correspondence should be distinguished from a claim about comparable stability or isolation conditions.[1980, pp.196–200, reactions of the preparatively accessible octahydrotriborate anion](../../../../../../reactions-of-the-octahydrotriborate-1-anion-b3h8-with-some-complexes-of-cobalt-i-cobalt-ii-rhodium-i-and-iridium-i-and-the-characterization-of-the-borallyl-complex-iriii-eta3-b3h7-co-h-pph3-2-by-norman-n-greenwood-and-others.md)
