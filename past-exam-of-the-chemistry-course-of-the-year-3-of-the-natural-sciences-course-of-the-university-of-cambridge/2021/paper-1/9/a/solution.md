<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the CO and NCO cases, the added fragment acts as a [Lewis base](../../../../../lewis-base.md) at [boron](../../../../../boron.md), rather than becoming a new skeletal vertex. **Both have a triangular $\mathrm{B_3}$ framework**. A useful localized drawing has three $\mathrm{BH_2}$ groups, one B–H–B bridge between two of them, and the donor attached to the third [boron](../../../../../boron.md). The [boron](../../../../../boron.md)–[boron](../../../../../boron.md) framework includes [three-centre two-electron bonds](../../../../../three-centre-two-electron-bond.md); its edges should not all be read as ordinary independent two-electron single bonds.

For the neutral [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) adduct, attach CO through [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), giving $\mathrm{B{\leftarrow}C{\equiv}O}$ at the donor-bearing $\mathrm{BH_2}$ site. For the anionic isocyanate adduct, attach $\mathrm{NCO^-}$ through [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), giving a B–N=C=O connectivity and the overall $-1$ charge. In both drawings the [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) count is $3(2)+1=7$. Weak or asymmetric additional B–H contacts can distort this idealized drawing; a hydrogen-rich [borane](../../../../../borane.md) is not fully characterized by a polygon alone. The [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) triborane structure was established in the original [borane](../../../../../borane.md) study.[1973, pp.2175–2178, structural assignment of the [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) adduct](../../../../../boranes-xxxvii-reactions-of-triborane-7-and-the-structure-of-triborane-7-carbonyl-by-james-d-glore-and-others.md)

The rhodium fragment is instead a skeletal building block. Its neutral [electron](../../../../../electron.md) count is $9+2(2)+1=14$, so it contributes two skeletal [electrons](../../../../../electron.md), like a BH vertex. Combining it with $\mathrm{B_3H_7}$ gives $3(2)+4+2=12$ skeletal [electrons](../../../../../electron.md), or six pairs, for four vertices. A consistent low-vertex structure is **a tetrahedral $\mathrm{RhB_3}$ skeleton**, with the $\mathrm{RhH(PPh_3)_2}$ fragment capping the [boron](../../../../../boron.md) [triangle](../../../../../triangle.md). Keep the terminal Rh–H ligand distinct from the seven [hydrogens](../../../../../hydrogen.md) on the [borane](../../../../../borane.md) portion. The latter can again be represented by three terminal $\mathrm{BH_2}$ groups and a B–H–B bridge. The total formula then contains eight [hydrogens](../../../../../hydrogen.md) outside the phenyl groups. Treating Rh as a pendant one-centre [Lewis base](../../../../../lewis-base.md) would miss its skeletal role.
