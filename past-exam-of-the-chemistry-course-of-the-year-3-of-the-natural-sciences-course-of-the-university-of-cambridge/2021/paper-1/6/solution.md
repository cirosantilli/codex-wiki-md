<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Open the [lactone](../../../../lactone.md) formally at its acyl–[oxygen](../../../../oxygen.md) bond, then replace the resulting secondary [alcohol](../../../../alcohol.md) by its [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) precursor. With $R=\mathrm{CH(Me)CH(Me)_2}$, the useful sequence of retrosynthetic intermediates is

$$
\text{lactone}\ \Longleftarrow\
\mathrm{HO_2CCH(Me)CH_2CH_2CH(OH)R}
\ \Longleftarrow\
\mathrm{EtO_2CCH(Me)CH_2CH_2COR}.
$$

Disconnect the last compound as a [Michael addition](../../../../michael-addition.md) product of the methyl-side [enolate](../../../../enolate.md) of $\mathrm{RCOCH_3}$ and [ethyl methacrylate](../../../../ethyl-methacrylate.md):

$$
\mathrm{RCOCH_3+CH_2{=}C(Me)CO_2Et}
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{RCOCH_2CH_2CH(Me)CO_2Et}.
$$

The substituted [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) of the methacrylate becomes the methyl-bearing [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) alpha to the [lactone](../../../../lactone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../carbonyl-group.md). The [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) becomes the oxygen-bearing ring [carbon](../../../../carbon.md). This checks the six-membered ring connectivity.

The required branched methyl [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) itself comes from [butan-2-one](../../../../butan-2-one.md) by ethyl-side [aldol addition](../../../../aldol-addition.md) to [acetone](../../../../acetone.md), followed by [dehydration](../../../../dehydration.md) and selective [hydrogenation](../../../../hydrogenation.md):

$$
\mathrm{MeCOCH_2Me}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{MeCOCH(Me)C(OH)Me_2}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{MeCOC(Me){=}CMe_2}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{MeCOCH(Me)CHMe_2}.
$$

Choose and isolate the aldol regioisomer shown. For the subsequent Michael step, use a bulky base at low temperature to favour its less hindered methyl-side [kinetic enolate](../../../../kinetic-enolate.md). Reduce the resulting [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) selectively with $\mathrm{NaBH_4}$ while retaining the [ester](../../../../ester.md), hydrolyse the [ester](../../../../ester.md), then acidify and promote intramolecular [esterification](../../../../esterification.md) with water removal. **The hydroxy acid closes to the required six-membered [lactone](../../../../lactone.md).** There is no specified stereochemical relationship to enforce, but a stereochemically pure target would require an additional selectivity or resolution step.
