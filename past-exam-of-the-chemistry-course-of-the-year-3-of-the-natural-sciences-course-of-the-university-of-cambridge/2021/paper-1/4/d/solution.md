<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A better controlled route is **selective [solvent extraction](../../../../../solvent-extraction.md) in acid, followed by selective stripping**, rather than precipitating everything near overlapping [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) thresholds. Oxidize Ce(III) to Ce(IV) in a controlled acidic nitrate medium, extract its nitrate complexes into an organic donor phase, and separate the phases. Then reduce the extracted Ce(IV) to Ce(III) and back-extract it into an aqueous phase. Trivalent rare earths are less strongly extracted under suitable conditions.

This still needs a [thorium](../../../../../thorium.md) separation: [tributyl phosphate](../../../../../tributyl-phosphate.md) can coextract Th(IV). One demonstrated strategy deliberately coextracts Ce(IV) and Th(IV), then uses reducing [peroxide](../../../../../peroxide.md) solution to strip Ce as Ce(III), followed by a separate [thorium](../../../../../thorium.md) strip. The appropriate controls are distribution measurements for each ion at the chosen acid concentration, a cerium oxidation-state check, and a complete mass balance over extraction and stripping. **Selectivity comes from combining [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) and [complexation](../../../../../complexation.md), not from assuming that one extraction removes every contaminant.**[2017, abstract: coextraction from nitric acid and successive [peroxide](../../../../../peroxide.md)/water stripping](../../../../../coextraction-and-successive-selective-stripping-of-cerium-iv-and-thorium-from-monazite-leach-solutions-with-tributyl-phosphate-tbp-by-a-h-ali.md)
