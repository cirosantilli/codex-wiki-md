↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Not as a general aqueous separation reaction for the other [lanthanides](../../../../../lanthanide.md).** Most strongly favour the trivalent state in ordinary water chemistry. Ce(IV) is unusually accessible because it has the empty $4f$ shell and is strongly stabilized by oxide formation. Some other [lanthanides](../../../../../lanthanide.md) have accessible alternative [oxidation states](../../../../../oxidation-state.md), such as Eu(II), and higher oxides of Pr or Tb can exist under suitable conditions; this does not imply that they undergo the same Ce(III)/Ce(IV) pH-controlled aqueous oxidation.
