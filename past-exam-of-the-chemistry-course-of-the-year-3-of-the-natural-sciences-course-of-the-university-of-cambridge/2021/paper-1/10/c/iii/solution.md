<h1 id="10/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Two bidentate [bipyridine](../../../../../../bipyridine.md) ligands provide four N donors and three terminal NCS ligands provide three more. Adding one water gives **$n=1$ and [coordination number](../../../../../../coordination-number.md) eight**:

$$
\mathrm{Eu(NCS)_3(bpy)_2(H_2O)}.
$$

Eight-coordinate arrangements, distorted between familiar ideal polyhedra, are plausible for a sterically crowded Eu(III) centre. A second water would give [coordination number](../../../../../../coordination-number.md) nine, which is not forbidden for a [lanthanide](../../../../../../lanthanide.md); the requested value is a chemically motivated prediction, not something fixed by charge balance. Diffraction or a direct hydration-number measurement is needed to establish it uniquely.
