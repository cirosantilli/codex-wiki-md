<h1 id="10/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Water** is the preferred solvent ligand in the qualitative prediction. Its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is a hard donor, it is less sterically demanding than ethanol, and it can occupy a crowded Eu(III) coordination sphere. Actual solvent occupancy also depends on activities and crystallization conditions, so a formula containing only the word solvent is not itself a crystallographic identification.
