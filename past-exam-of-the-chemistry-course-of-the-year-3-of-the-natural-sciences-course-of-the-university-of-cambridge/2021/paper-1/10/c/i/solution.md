↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Nitrogen-bound $\mathrm{Eu{-}NCS}$** is favoured. Eu(III) is a small, highly charged [hard Lewis acid](../../../../../../hard-lewis-acid.md), whereas the [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) end of [thiocyanate](../../../../../../thiocyanate.md) is harder than its more polarizable sulfur end. The [hard and soft acids and bases](../../../../../../hard-and-soft-acids-and-bases.md) argument therefore predicts N coordination, together with coordination through both [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) atoms of each [bipyridine](../../../../../../bipyridine.md).
