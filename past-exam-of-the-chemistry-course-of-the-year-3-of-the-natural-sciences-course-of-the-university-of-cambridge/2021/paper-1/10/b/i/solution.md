<h1 id="10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the stated local axes: $z$ is radial along O–Ru, $x$ is tangential within the Ru–O plane, and $y$ is perpendicular to that plane. The [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md)'s three $sp^2$ hybrids make the in-plane sigma framework. Its remaining **$p_y$ orbital** is perpendicular to the plane and can overlap with **one $d_{yz}$ orbital on each Ru**.

Choose the local orbital phases so their O-facing lobes agree. Of the three metal orbitals, only

$$
D=\frac{d_{yz}^{(1)}+d_{yz}^{(2)}+d_{yz}^{(3)}}{\sqrt3}
$$

has the appropriate combination to interact with [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) $p_y$. The other two independent combinations have coefficients summing to zero and are nonbonding with respect to the central [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). Mixing $D$ with $p_y$ gives one lower pi-bonding orbital and one upper pi-antibonding orbital. Each Ru also retains its $d_{xy}$ and $d_{xz}$ orbitals as nonbonding with respect to this central-oxo pi interaction. Thus the complete low-energy diagram contains **one bonding level, eight metal-nonbonding levels, and one antibonding level**. The eight consist of two $d_{yz}$ combinations, three $d_{xy}$ and three $d_{xz}$ orbitals. They need not be exactly degenerate once other ligands are considered.

For the neutral formula as printed, oxide contributes $-2$ and six acetates contribute $-6$. The Ru oxidation numbers therefore sum to $+8$, giving sixteen metal d [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) overall, formally two Ru(III) and one Ru(II), possibly delocalized. Including the [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) pi pair gives eighteen [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md), filling the [bonding orbital](../../../../../../bonding-orbital.md) and all eight [nonbonding orbitals](../../../../../../nonbonding-orbital.md), leaving pi\* empty. If the often encountered singly charged cation were intended instead, there would be seventeen [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) and one singly occupied nonbonding level. This charge distinction changes occupancy, not the orbital connectivity.
