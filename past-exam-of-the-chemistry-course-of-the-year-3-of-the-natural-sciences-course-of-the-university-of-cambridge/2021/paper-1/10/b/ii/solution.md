<h1 id="10/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The three $d_{xz}$ orbitals are in the Ru–O plane and can overlap directly along the Ru–Ru edges. Their locally rotated lobes have nonzero edge-directed overlap, producing metal–metal bonding and antibonding combinations; a simple [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md) model resolves the three orbitals into a singlet and a doublet. The phases and ordering depend on the chosen local basis, so the important feature is the **complete set of three combinations**.

In the occupancy obtained in (i), this $d_{xz}$ set contains six [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). All three combinations are filled: the stabilization of the bonding combinations is offset by occupation of their antibonding partners. Equivalently, the sum of the three eigenvalues of a pure off-diagonal interaction matrix is zero. Hence **there is no net Ru–Ru [bond order](../../../../../../bond-order.md) from this filled set in the stated model**. Overlap alone is not evidence of a net metal–metal bond. This conclusion neglects higher-order mixing and the interactions explicitly excluded from the qualitative diagram.
