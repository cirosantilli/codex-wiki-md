<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Disconnect the internal **Z [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) by a non-stabilized [Wittig reaction](../../../../../wittig-reaction.md)**:

$$
\mathrm{MeO_2C(CH_2)_5CH{=}PPh_3}
\quad+\quad
\mathrm{OHCCH(nPr)CH_2CH{=}CH_2}.
$$

The [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) becomes the right-hand internal [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md); the ylide [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) becomes the left-hand one. Both the methyl [ester](../../../../../ester.md) and the remote terminal [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) survive the olefination.

Make the [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) from pentanal by [Stork enamine reaction](../../../../../stork-enamine-reaction.md): form its [pyrrolidine](../../../../../pyrrolidine.md) [enamine](../../../../../enamine.md), react at [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) with allyl bromide, then hydrolyse to $\mathrm{OHCCH(nPr)CH_2CH{=}CH_2}$. The original alpha [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) of pentanal now bears allyl and n-propyl substituents. Control the alkylation to avoid multiple substitutions.

The other branch needs a three-carbon extension of the supplied four-carbon bromo [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md). Protect the OH of $\mathrm{HO(CH_2)_4Br}$ as a [tetrahydropyranyl ether](../../../../../tetrahydropyranyl-ether.md). Convert the bromide into an [organocuprate](../../../../../organocuprate.md) and add it conjugatively to [methyl acrylate](../../../../../methyl-acrylate.md):

$$
\mathrm{THPO(CH_2)_4{-}Cu}
+\mathrm{CH_2{=}CHCO_2Me}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{THPO(CH_2)_6CO_2Me}.
$$

Here the organocopper formula is shorthand for a suitable cuprate preparation, not a claim that an isolated monomer is necessary. The acrylate contributes two methylenes and the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). Remove the THP group, replace the resulting terminal OH by Br, and react with $\mathrm{PPh_3}$ to form $\mathrm{[Ph_3PCH_2(CH_2)_5CO_2Me]^+Br^-}$. Deprotonate adjacent to [phosphorus](../../../../../phosphorus.md) to generate the non-stabilized ylide. This proton is much more acidic than the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) alpha protons, but controlled base stoichiometry and low temperature remain important.

Add the allylated [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) under conditions favouring the usual kinetic **[Z-selective Wittig olefination](../../../../../z-selective-wittig-olefination.md)**, and separate any E isomer. The result is

$$
\boxed{(Z)\text{-}\mathrm{MeO_2C(CH_2)_5CH{=}CHCH(nPr)CH_2CH{=}CH_2}.}
$$

The four-carbon starting [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) plus the three-carbon acrylate backbone supplies the seven-carbon acid-derived chain; the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) Me gives an eighth [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). Pentanal plus allyl gives the other eight. The total is sixteen [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md), including the terminal [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md). A two-carbon extension would produce the wrong chain length.
