<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Disconnect the [amide](../../../../../amide.md) into **2-propylhexanoic acid and N-methyl-2-(4-hexylphenyl)ethylamine**. This keeps the [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) substituents distinct: one is Me and the other is the two-carbon aryl side chain. The acid's alpha [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) must bear both n-butyl and n-propyl groups.

For the acid fragment, esterify the supplied [hexanoic acid](../../../../../hexanoic-acid.md), form its [ester enolate](../../../../../ester-enolate.md) with LDA, and alkylate with 1-iodopropane:

$$
\mathrm{EtO_2CCH_2(nBu)}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{EtO_2CCH(nPr)(nBu)}.
$$

[hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) gives 2-propylhexanoic acid; conversion with $\mathrm{SOCl_2}$ gives the [acyl chloride](../../../../../acyl-chloride.md) for the final coupling. Limit the alkylating reagent and isolate the monoalkylated [ester](../../../../../ester.md) to avoid overalkylation.

For the aryl fragment, use another portion of [hexanoic acid](../../../../../hexanoic-acid.md) to make hexanoyl chloride. A [Friedel-Crafts acylation](../../../../../friedel-crafts-acylation.md) of [bromobenzene](../../../../../bromobenzene.md) with this reagent and $\mathrm{AlCl_3}$ gives ortho and para products; isolate the para bromoaryl [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md). A [Wolff-Kishner reduction](../../../../../wolff-kishner-reduction.md) converts $\mathrm{ArCO(CH_2)_4Me}$ into $\mathrm{ArCH_2(CH_2)_4Me}$ without deliberately hydrogenolyzing the aryl bromide. Acylation followed by [carbonyl reduction](../../../../../carbonyl-reduction.md) installs an unrearranged n-hexyl group; direct Friedel–Crafts alkylation is a less reliable way to do that.

Convert the retained aryl bromide into its [grignard reagent](../../../../../grignard-reagent.md), open [ethylene oxide](../../../../../ethylene-oxide.md), and work up to obtain $\mathrm{p\!-\!HexC_6H_4CH_2CH_2OH}$. Oxidize the primary [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) to $\mathrm{p\!-\!HexC_6H_4CH_2CHO}$ under conditions that stop at the [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md), then perform [reductive amination](../../../../../reductive-amination.md) with [methylamine](../../../../../methylamine.md) and [sodium triacetoxyborohydride](../../../../../sodium-triacetoxyborohydride.md). Excess [methylamine](../../../../../methylamine.md) helps favour the desired secondary amine over further alkylation. Finally acylate that secondary amine with the isolated [acyl chloride](../../../../../acyl-chloride.md), using a separate base to capture HCl.

**The final [amide](../../../../../amide.md) has an N-methyl group, the para n-hexyl phenethyl group, and the required n-butyl/n-propyl acyl branching.** The [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) check is $9$ in the acyl fragment plus $1+2+6+6=15$ in the amine fragment, giving $24$ overall. No absolute [stereochemistry](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) is specified at the acid alpha [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md).
