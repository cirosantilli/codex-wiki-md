<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

An [acetal](../../../../acetal.md) [retrosynthesis](../../../../retrosynthetic-analysis.md) separates the target into **trans-cyclohexane-1,2-diol and cyclohexyl methyl [ketone](../../../../ketone.md)**. Both fragments can be prepared using separate portions of [cyclohexene](../../../../cyclohexene.md).

For the diol, [epoxidation](../../../../epoxidation.md) with a [peroxy acid](../../../../peroxy-acid.md) such as mCPBA gives [cyclohexene](../../../../cyclohexene.md) oxide. Acid-catalysed opening with water occurs by backside attack on the protonated [epoxide](../../../../epoxide.md), giving the **anti, hence trans, diol**. A syn dihydroxylation would give the wrong relative configuration.

For the [ketone](../../../../ketone.md), add HBr to another portion of [cyclohexene](../../../../cyclohexene.md) to obtain bromocyclohexane, form its [grignard reagent](../../../../grignard-reagent.md) with Mg in dry ether, and add [acetonitrile](../../../../acetonitrile.md). Hydrolyse the resulting [magnesium](../../../../magnesium.md) imine salt to $\mathrm{C_6H_{11}COMe}$. A [nitrile](../../../../nitrile.md) is useful here because addition followed by [hydrolysis](../../../../hydrolysis.md) stops at the [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) instead of giving the double addition associated with an [ester](../../../../ester.md).

Finally combine the isolated trans diol and [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) with catalytic acid and remove water to form the cyclic [ketal](../../../../ketal.md). The [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) becomes the [acetal](../../../../acetal.md) [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) bearing Me and cyclohexyl; the two original diol C–O bonds retain their relative configuration. **Anti [epoxide](../../../../epoxide.md) opening supplies the required trans relationship before [ketal](../../../../ketal.md) formation.** [ketal](../../../../ketal.md) formation is an equilibrium and the trans-fused product may be less favourable than an unstrained [acetal](../../../../acetal.md), so effective water removal and isolation are necessary; substituting a cis diol is not an acceptable shortcut.
