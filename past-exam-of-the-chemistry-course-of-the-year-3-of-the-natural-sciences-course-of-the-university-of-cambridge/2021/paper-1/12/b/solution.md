<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The key stereochemical disconnection is the **cis-1,3-disubstituted cyclopentane**, not a direct functionalization of two arbitrary cyclopentane positions. A [Diels-Alder reaction](../../../../../diels-alder-reaction.md) of [cyclopentadiene](../../../../../cyclopentadiene.md) with [nitroethylene](../../../../../nitroethylene.md) fixes both ends of the new two-carbon bridge on the same face of the original five-membered ring.

Isolate the useful nitronorbornene adduct, convert its secondary nitro group to a [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) by a [Nef reaction](../../../../../nef-reaction.md), and then hydrogenate the remaining ring [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) to norcamphor. Performing the nitro-to-ketone conversion before catalytic [hydrogenation](../../../../../hydrogenation.md) avoids simply reducing the nitro group to an amine.

Use a [Baeyer-Villiger oxidation](../../../../../baeyer-villiger-oxidation.md) to insert [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) between the [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and its adjacent bridgehead [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). Bridgehead migration is favoured over migration of the competing methylene; isolate the required [lactone](../../../../../lactone.md) regioisomer if necessary. This opens the way to a hydroxy cyclopentyl side chain without breaking the original five-carbon ring. In the resulting bridged [lactone](../../../../../lactone.md), one original [cyclopentadiene](../../../../../cyclopentadiene.md) terminus is bonded to O and the other bears the $\mathrm{CH_2CO}$ segment.

Reduce the [lactone](../../../../../lactone.md) selectively with DIBAL to its [lactol](../../../../../lactol.md). Ring opening of that [lactol](../../../../../lactol.md) gives the [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) equivalent **cis-3-hydroxycyclopentyl-$\mathrm{CH_2CHO}$**. The ring OH and the two-carbon [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) side chain are cis because their original attachments were the two ends of the Diels–Alder bridge; neither the migration nor [lactol](../../../../../lactol.md) opening inverts those cyclopentane [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md).

Perform a [Horner-Wadsworth-Emmons reaction](../../../../../horner-wadsworth-emmons-reaction.md) on the [lactol](../../../../../lactol.md)/[aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) equilibrium using $\mathrm{(EtO)_2P(O)CH_2CO_2R}$, where $R=\mathrm{CH(Me)CH_2CH(Me)_2}$. Preform the phosphonate anion and allow for consumption of base by the [lactol](../../../../../lactol.md) OH. Standard stabilized-phosphonate conditions favour the required **E-$\mathrm{CH{=}CHCO_2R}$** unit. No further [hydrogenation](../../../../../hydrogenation.md) should follow it.

The required [ester](../../../../../ester.md) substituent can be prepared by reducing 4-methylpentan-2-one to 4-methylpentan-2-ol, esterifying with bromoacetic acid, and performing an [Arbuzov reaction](../../../../../arbuzov-reaction.md) with triethyl phosphite to make the phosphonoacetate. No absolute configuration is indicated at that alcohol-derived centre.

**Bridge-derived cis ring [stereochemistry](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) plus E-selective phosphonate olefination gives both specified relationships.** Achiral cycloaddition and subsequent reagents give a racemic pair with this relative configuration; isolation of one absolute [enantiomer](../../../../../enantiomer.md) would require resolution or an asymmetric variant. The acid fragment contains nine [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md): five from [cyclopentadiene](../../../../../cyclopentadiene.md), two from [nitroethylene](../../../../../nitroethylene.md) and two from the phosphonoacetate. The [ester](../../../../../ester.md) alkyl group adds six, giving fifteen overall. The atom tracing in the original schematic in question 8 shows why [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) insertion retains the five-membered ring rather than producing a six-membered carbocycle.
