<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Disconnect the N–cyclohexyl bond as a late [reductive amination](../../../../../reductive-amination.md) of a primary amine with [cyclohexanone](../../../../../cyclohexanone.md). Disconnect the exocyclic methylene as a [Wittig methylenation](../../../../../wittig-methylenation.md) of a substituted [cyclohexanone](../../../../../cyclohexanone.md). The useful ring precursor is **3-methyl-5-substituted cyclohex-2-en-1-one**, accessible by a [Robinson annulation](../../../../../robinson-annulation.md). The pendant substituent throughout ring construction should be $(\mathrm{CH_2})_4\mathrm{NHBoc}$.

Start with [nucleophilic substitution](../../../../../nucleophilic-substitution.md) of the supplied bromo [ester](../../../../../ester.md) by cyanide:

$$
\mathrm{Br(CH_2)_3CO_2Me}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{NC(CH_2)_3CO_2Me}.
$$

Reduce the [nitrile](../../../../../nitrile.md) selectively to the primary amine while retaining the [ester](../../../../../ester.md), for example by controlled catalytic [hydrogenation](../../../../../hydrogenation.md) with [Raney nickel](../../../../../raney-nickel.md) in the presence of ammonia, and trap the amine promptly as its Boc [carbamate](../../../../../carbamate.md). Avoid heating or concentrating the free amino [ester](../../../../../ester.md), which can cyclize to a [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md). [lithium aluminium hydride](../../../../../lithium-aluminium-hydride.md) would be unsuitable at this stage because it also reduces the [ester](../../../../../ester.md).

Partial reduction of the protected [ester](../../../../../ester.md) with DIBAL at low temperature gives $\mathrm{BocNH(CH_2)_4CHO}$. The [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) of the original [nitrile](../../../../../nitrile.md) is now the methylene nearest [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md); the original [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) is the [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). Condense this [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) with [acetone](../../../../../acetone.md) and dehydrate to $\mathrm{BocNH(CH_2)_4CH{=}CHCOMe}$.

Now add the [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) of a second [acetone](../../../../../acetone.md) molecule in a [Michael addition](../../../../../michael-addition.md). With $R=(\mathrm{CH_2})_4\mathrm{NHBoc}$, the resulting chain is

$$
\mathrm{MeCOCH_2CH(R)CH_2COMe}.
$$

Intramolecular [aldol condensation](../../../../../aldol-condensation.md) gives **3-Me-5-R-cyclohex-2-en-1-one**. The beta methyl derives from the [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) end attacked in the ring closure; the pendant aminoalkyl chain remains at C5. Hydrogenate the ring C=C selectively, retaining the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md), then methylenate the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) with preformed $\mathrm{Ph_3P{=}CH_2}$. Do the [hydrogenation](../../../../../hydrogenation.md) before installing the exocyclic methylene so that it is not hydrogenated away.

Remove Boc under brief mild conditions, checking for acid-induced exocyclic [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) isomerization. [reductive amination](../../../../../reductive-amination.md) of the resulting primary amine with [cyclohexanone](../../../../../cyclohexanone.md), followed by selective imine reduction, installs the secondary N-cyclohexyl group. Use controlled stoichiometry to suppress tertiary amine formation. **The methyl and four-carbon aminoalkyl substituents occupy the required 1,3-related ring positions, with the exocyclic methylene at the former [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md).** No ring [stereochemistry](../../../../../stereochemistry-split.md) is specified. The target contains eighteen [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md): six ring [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md), one ring methyl, one methylene, four in the linker, and six in the N-cyclohexyl group.
