<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Disconnect the tertiary [alcohol](../../../../alcohol.md) at its bond to the alkenyl group. The required [carbonyl](../../../../carbonyl-group.md) partner is $\mathrm{MeCOCH(Me)Et}$; the other partner is an **E-configured $\mathrm{PhCH_2CH{=}CH}$ organometallic reagent**. This disconnection fixes the [alkene](../../../../alkene.md) geometry before the [alcohol](../../../../alcohol.md) is made.

First form the ethyl-side [enolate](../../../../enolate.md) of [butan-2-one](../../../../butan-2-one.md) under equilibrating conditions, then trap it with ethyl iodide and isolate the required monoalkylated [ketone](../../../../ketone.md):

$$
\mathrm{MeCOCH_2Me}\longrightarrow\mathrm{MeCOCH(Me)Et}.
$$

The incoming group is Et: methylation would give a [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) skeleton one [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) too short. Because an unsymmetrical [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) can form two [enolates](../../../../enolate.md) and undergo repeated alkylation, regioisomer separation and controlled conversion are part of this step.

Independently perform a halo-[Wittig reaction](../../../../wittig-reaction.md) on phenylacetaldehyde using $\mathrm{Ph_3P{=}CHBr}$ and isolate **E-$\mathrm{PhCH_2CH{=}CHBr}$** from any geometrical mixture. [magnesium](../../../../magnesium.md) insertion in dry ether gives the corresponding [vinylmagnesium halide](../../../../vinylmagnesium-halide.md); add the isolated [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) at low temperature and protonate the resulting [alkoxide](../../../../alkoxide.md). Addition to C=O preserves the pre-existing C=C geometry:

$$
(E)\text{-}\mathrm{PhCH_2CH{=}CHMgBr}
+\mathrm{MeCOCH(Me)Et}
\longrightarrow
(E)\text{-}\mathrm{PhCH_2CH{=}CHC(OH)(Me)CH(Me)Et}.
$$

**The E [alkene](../../../../alkene.md) and the complete fifteen-carbon skeleton are obtained.** No absolute configuration is specified at the newly formed [alcohol](../../../../alcohol.md) centre, so an unselected stereoisomeric mixture there is acceptable. An alternative [carbon](../../../../carbon.md)–[carbon](../../../../carbon.md) disconnection is [acetylide](../../../../acetylide.md) addition to the same [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) followed by a controlled trans-selective [alkyne](../../../../alkyne.md) reduction; the vinyl-reagent route avoids relying on reduction of a tertiary propargylic [alcohol](../../../../alcohol.md).
