<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

The five-membered enone-like [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) framework can be obtained by an intramolecular [aldol condensation](../../../../aldol-condensation.md) of

$$
\mathrm{MeCOCH_2CH(CN)CH(CN)CH_2COMe}.
$$

Enolization at an internal methylene next to one [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) followed by attack on the opposite [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) closes a five-membered ring. [dehydration](../../../../dehydration.md) leaves one acetyl group attached to one [alkene](../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) and one methyl group on the other. The two nitrile-bearing [carbons](../../../../carbon.md) remain adjacent and their original cis relationship is retained if [epimerization](../../../../epimerization.md) is avoided.

Now reverse a reductive [ozonolysis](../../../../ozonolysis.md): the diketone arises by opening a six-membered [cyclohexene](../../../../cyclohexene.md) whose [alkene](../../../../alkene.md) [carbons](../../../../carbon.md) each carry Me. That [cyclohexene](../../../../cyclohexene.md) is the [Diels-Alder reaction](../../../../diels-alder-reaction.md) product of **2,3-dimethylbuta-1,3-diene and [maleonitrile](../../../../maleonitrile.md)**. The latter is the supplied cis dinitrile. The [stereospecificity](../../../../stereospecificity.md) of the cycloaddition puts the two CN groups on the same face.

Thus the forward plan is **Diels–Alder addition, reductive [ozonolysis](../../../../ozonolysis.md), then five-membered aldol ring closure and [dehydration](../../../../dehydration.md)**. Use a reductive ozone work-up that stops at the diketone. During the final step, avoid prolonged strongly basic heating because the [carbons](../../../../carbon.md) bearing CN can be deprotonated and epimerized. Achiral reagents give the racemic pair with the required cis relationship; resolution would be necessary if only the depicted [enantiomer](../../../../enantiomer.md) were required. The diene supplies six [carbons](../../../../carbon.md) and [maleonitrile](../../../../maleonitrile.md) supplies four, accounting for all ten [carbons](../../../../carbon.md) without an unexplained [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) deletion.
