<h1 id="57/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Without significant anomalous scattering, the [electron density](../../../../../electron-density.md) is effectively real and [Friedel's law](../../../../../friedel-s-law.md) gives $F_0(-\mathbf h)=F_0(\mathbf h)^*$, hence $I(\overline{222})=13130$.

With anomalous scattering the atomic imaginary term retains its sign when the reflection index reverses. Here $\Delta F(-222)=19.2i$, so

$$
I(-222)=|F_0^*+19.2i|^2
=13130+19.2^2-2(19.2)|F_0|\sin\phi
=11567.3\ {\rm electrons}^2.
$$

Thus the second expected intensity is approximately **$1.157\times10^4\ {\rm electrons}^2$**. Conjugating the complete anomalous [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md) would incorrectly impose Friedel equality on the second experiment.
