↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The electric potential is $\varepsilon(x-a/2)$, so inside the box

$$
H=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2}{dx^2}
+q\varepsilon(x-a/2),\quad
H^{(0)}=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2}{dx^2},\quad
H^{(1)}=x-a/2,\quad \lambda=q\varepsilon.
$$

The infinite walls impose zero [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) at $0,a$. The normalized [particle in a box](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) states are $\psi_n(x)=\sqrt{2/a}\sin(n\pi x/a)$ and $E_n^{(0)}=n^2h^2/(8ma^2)$ for positive integers $n$.
