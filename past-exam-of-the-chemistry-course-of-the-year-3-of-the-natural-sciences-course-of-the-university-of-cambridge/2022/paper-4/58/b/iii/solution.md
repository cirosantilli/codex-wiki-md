↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The third term is $2F(11)\cos[2\pi(x+y)]$. To reinforce a maximum at $(x_0,y_0)$ from the preceding table, its sign should be

$$
S(11)=(-1)^{2x_0+2y_0}=S(10)S(01).
$$

Hence the [direct-methods triplet relationship](../../../../../../direct-methods-triplet-relationship.md) is $S(10)S(01)S(11)\simeq+1$ for the strong reflections under consideration. This maximizes constructive density at shared atomic positions. For weak or competing reflections the relation is probabilistic rather than an unconditional algebraic theorem.
