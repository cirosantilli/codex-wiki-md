<h1 id="58/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Reality of the [electron density](../../../../../../electron-density.md) implies $F(-h)=F(h)^*$. Writing $F(h)=|F(h)|e^{i\phi_h}$ and pairing positive and negative indices gives

$$
\rho(x)=F(0)+2\sum_{h=1}^\infty |F(h)|
\cos(2\pi hx-\phi_h).
$$

The sign in the cosine follows from the supplied negative-sign Fourier-synthesis convention. $F(0)$ is the mean [electron density](../../../../../../electron-density.md) in the fractional-coordinate normalization.
