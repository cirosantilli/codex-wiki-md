<h1 id="58/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With an origin at an inversion center, the [structure factors](../../../../../../structure-factor.md) are real, so their phases are either $0$ or $\pi$. Defining $S(h)=\operatorname{sgn}F(h)$ gives

$$
\rho(x)=F(0)+2\sum_{h=1}^\infty S(h)|F(h)|\cos(2\pi hx).
$$

Centrosymmetry converts the phase problem into a sign problem; it does not imply that every [structure factor](../../../../../../structure-factor.md) is positive.
