<h1 id="55/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With an N-trityl substituent, the [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md) attacks a nearby trityl phenyl ring. Addition across an arene bond gives a norcaradiene-like cyclopropane, followed by electrocyclic opening to the seven-membered cycloheptatriene: this is a [Buchner ring expansion](../../../../../../buchner-ring-expansion.md). Intramolecular trapping explains the fused product rather than an intermolecular [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md) reaction.

With N-tert-butyl, there is no pendant arene. Instead the [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md) inserts into a C-H bond of one tert-butyl methyl group. The new bond between the [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md) carbon and that methyl carbon closes the five-membered N-containing ring; H is transferred to the [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md) carbon in the same insertion. The remaining two methyl groups stay on the carbon attached to N.
