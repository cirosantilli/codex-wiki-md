<h1 id="55/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The alcohol first attacks CDI, forming an activated O-carbonylimidazole derivative. Its departure gives the resonance-stabilized propargyl/allenyl cation

$$
\mathrm{Ar-C^+(CF_3)-C{\equiv}C-Ph
\leftrightarrow Ar-C(CF_3)=C=C^+(Ph)}.
$$

The ortho amino nitrogen attacks the **central carbon of the allenyl system**, closing the five-membered indole ring. Electron redistribution and loss of the N-H proton establish the indole aromatic system while leaving a [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md) at the original distal, phenyl-bearing alkyne carbon.

For atom tracking, the original carbinol carbon becomes indole C3, the proximal alkyne carbon becomes C2, and the distal $\mathrm{C(Ph)}$ becomes the exocyclic [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md). Attack at the distal alkyne carbon would make a six-membered ring and cannot give the displayed skeleton. This carbene-based rearrangement has a primary experimental precedent.[Rapid assembly of substituted dihydrocycloheptapyrroloindoles by Lisa F. Frey and colleagues](../../../../../../rapid-assembly-of-substituted-dihydrocycloheptapyrroloindoles-by-lisa-f-frey-and-colleagues.md)
