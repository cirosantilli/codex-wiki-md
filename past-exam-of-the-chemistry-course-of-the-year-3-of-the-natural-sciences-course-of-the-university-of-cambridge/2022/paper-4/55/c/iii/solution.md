<h1 id="55/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

No stereochemical information need survive from the starting carbinol. Ionization converts that tetrahedral [stereocentre](../../../../../../stereocentre.md) into a planar, delocalized propargyl/allenyl cation before cyclization. In achiral conditions the two enantiomeric pathways to the new [stereocentre](../../../../../../stereocentre.md) in the tert-butyl-derived product are equivalent, so an enantiopure alcohol is expected to give racemic product. A claim of retained enantiopurity would require evidence for unusually effective conformational memory or ion-pair control.
