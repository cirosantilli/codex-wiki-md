<h1 id="55/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Propionic acid is a weak, relatively high-boiling acid: its normal boiling point is about $141^\circ\mathrm C$, compared with about $118^\circ\mathrm C$ for acetic acid. It provides mild proton catalysis while remaining in the hot orthoester mixture more effectively. This is useful because ketene-acetal formation and the thermal [Johnson-Claisen rearrangement](../../../../../../johnson-claisen-rearrangement.md) require heating, whereas strong acid would threaten the pyran [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md).

This is a practical catalyst choice, not an absolute mechanistic prohibition on acetic acid; acetic-acid-catalysed orthoester rearrangements are also possible.
