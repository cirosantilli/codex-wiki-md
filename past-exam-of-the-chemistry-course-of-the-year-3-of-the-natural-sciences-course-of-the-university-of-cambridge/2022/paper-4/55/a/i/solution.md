<h1 id="55/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first transformation is an [Achmatowicz rearrangement](../../../../../../achmatowicz-rearrangement.md), followed by protection and selective reduction; the last is a [Johnson-Claisen rearrangement](../../../../../../johnson-claisen-rearrangement.md).

- Butyllithium removes the furan 2-proton, giving a 2-lithiofuran. Its carbon attacks the [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md); [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) of the resulting alkoxide gives the furfuryl alcohol. Carbon-carbon bond formation occurs at the lithiated position.
- Electrophilic oxygen transfer from mCPBA to the electron-rich furan gives an oxidized, ring-opened intermediate. Opening the oxygenated furan gives a hydroxy enedicarbonyl: the original furfuryl hydroxyl then attacks the remote [aldehyde](../../../../../../aldehyde.md) intramolecularly. This expands the five-membered furan into a six-membered [lactol](../../../../../../lactol.md) containing an enone [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). The original furfuryl oxygen becomes the pyran ring oxygen; oxidation of the furan supplies the new [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and [lactol](../../../../../../lactol.md) functionality.
- Ag$_2$O/MeI converts the [lactol](../../../../../../lactol.md) OH into its methyl [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md): oxygen attacks methyl iodide by [SN2 reaction](../../../../../../sn2-reaction.md), with iodide removal and proton transfer. This protects the anomeric position.
- LiAlH$_4$ delivers hydride to the remaining [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md), followed by [protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md). The methyl [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md) survives these basic reducing conditions, leaving the depicted allylic alcohol.
- Under weak-acid catalysis, that allylic OH replaces an ethoxy group of triethyl orthoacetate. Loss of another ethanol molecule, with removal of a methyl proton, gives the mixed [ketene acetal](../../../../../../ketene-acetal.md) $\mathrm{CH_2=C(OEt)-O-(allyl)}$. A six-electron, chair-like [Claisen rearrangement](../../../../../../claisen-rearrangement.md) breaks the allyl-O bond, forms a bond between the terminal allyl carbon and the ketene-acetal CH$_2$, and moves the ring double bond one position. [Carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) formation gives the $\mathrm{-CH_2CO_2Et}$ substituent.

The [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md) protects the other oxygenated position during hydride reduction. The orthoester transfers an acetate-derived two-carbon unit through a [ketene acetal](../../../../../../ketene-acetal.md); it does not directly substitute the allylic OH with an intact acetate ion.
