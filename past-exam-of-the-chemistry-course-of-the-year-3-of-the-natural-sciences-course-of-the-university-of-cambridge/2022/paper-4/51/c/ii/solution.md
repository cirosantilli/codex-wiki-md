<h1 id="51/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Event a probes [electron transfer](../../../../../../electron-transfer.md) between the semiquinone and dianion. Benzoquinone's smaller framework localizes charge and undergoes a greater change in bonding and solvation on this reduction; a larger effective reorganization barrier can make its heterogeneous transfer insufficiently fast for Nernst equilibrium on the $50\,\mathrm{mV\,s^{-1}}$ time scale. Anthraquinone's extended framework distributes the added charge and can support the more reversible response seen here.

This is a kinetic interpretation of quasi-reversibility, not a claim that benzoquinone cannot be reduced. Follow-up chemistry or [adsorption](../../../../../../adsorption.md) can also distort peak separation and return-current ratios; scan-rate-dependent measurements would distinguish them from slow [electron transfer](../../../../../../electron-transfer.md). A single trace does not uniquely identify the cause.
