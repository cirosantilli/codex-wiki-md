<h1 id="51/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Make heterogeneous [electron transfer](../../../../../../electron-transfer.md) fast relative to delivery of reactant, usually by applying sufficient [overpotential](../../../../../../overpotential.md) so that its surface concentration approaches zero. Use excess [supporting electrolyte](../../../../../../supporting-electrolyte.md) to suppress migration. For purely diffusional transient transport, suppress uncontrolled [convection](../../../../../../convection-split.md); for a [rotating disk electrode](../../../../../../rotating-disk-electrode.md), controlled [convection](../../../../../../convection-split.md) supplies the bulk while [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) across the thin interfacial boundary layer limits the current. A limiting current that no longer increases with driving potential is the characteristic test; a large current alone is not sufficient.
