<h1 id="51/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [midpoint potential](../../../../../../midpoint-potential.md) $E_m$ characterizes the redox couple under specified solution conditions: it is the potential at which oxidized and reduced forms have equal activities, or equal concentrations in the corresponding formal-potential convention. For a reversible cyclic voltammogram it is estimated by $(E_{pa}+E_{pc})/2$. It approximates the formal redox potential when the usual reversibility and [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) assumptions apply; it is not the potential halfway along a scan chosen arbitrarily.
