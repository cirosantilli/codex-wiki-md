<h1 id="61/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Wigner-Seitz radius](../../../../../../wigner-seitz-radius.md) $r_s$ is the radius of a sphere containing one electron at the local uniform density:

$$
\frac43\pi r_s^3\rho=1,\qquad
r_s=\left(\frac3{4\pi\rho}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

In [atomic units](../../../../../../atomic-units.md), the unpolarized homogeneous electron gas has exchange energy per electron $-0.4582/r_s$. The [local density approximation](../../../../../../local-density-approximation.md) applies that uniform-gas result at each position using the local density, then integrates the result weighted by $\rho$. It assumes local uniform-gas behavior rather than resolving nonlocal exchange explicitly.
