↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

$x_\sigma=|\nabla\rho_\sigma|/\rho_\sigma^{4/3}$ is a dimensionless reduced density gradient. It measures inhomogeneity relative to the local density scale, vanishes for a uniform gas, and allows a [generalized gradient approximation](../../../../../../generalized-gradient-approximation.md) correction to distinguish rapidly varying density from slowly varying density with the same local value.

The parameter $\beta$ controls the correction strength in the [Becke 1988 exchange functional](../../../../../../becke-1988-exchange-functional.md); it was fitted to atomic exchange energies. The large-gradient denominator simultaneously produces the intended asymptotic behavior, so its role is more constrained than an arbitrary multiplicative adjustment.[Reduced-gradient form, fitted parameter and asymptotic construction](../../../../../../density-functional-exchange-energy-approximation-with-correct-asymptotic-behavior-by-axel-d-becke.md)
