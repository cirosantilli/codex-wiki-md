<h1 id="56/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The hydroxylactam is a cyclic [hemiaminal](../../../../../hemiaminal.md). Its alkoxide can open to the corresponding [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md)/amidate, $\mathrm{MeNHCO-CH(OBn)-CH_2-CHO}$ in its protonated representation. This exposes an [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) without reducing or replacing the lactam [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md).

The ketophosphonate anion attacks that [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md). Formation and collapse of the phosphorus-containing intermediate give the predominantly E enone $\mathrm{MeNHCO-CH(OBn)-CH_2-CH=CH-COCH_3}$ and phosphate by [Horner-Wadsworth-Emmons reaction](../../../../../horner-wadsworth-emmons-reaction.md). The amide nitrogen then closes by intramolecular [aza-Michael addition](../../../../../aza-michael-addition.md) at the enone beta carbon. [Protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) of the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) enolate gives the observed five-membered lactam with a $\mathrm{-CH_2COCH_3}$ substituent.

A folded, relatively unencumbered closure places OBn and the developing side chain in outward-facing positions; the expected major product has **OBn and the C5 acetonyl substituent cis**. With the OBn wedge retained, show the new C5–CH$_2$COCH$_3$ bond as a solid wedge. The closure is diastereoselective, while the original wavy [hemiaminal](../../../../../hemiaminal.md) centre is erased by [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) formation. This facial prediction rests on the preferred folded conformer rather than retention of either starting [hemiaminal](../../../../../hemiaminal.md) epimer.
