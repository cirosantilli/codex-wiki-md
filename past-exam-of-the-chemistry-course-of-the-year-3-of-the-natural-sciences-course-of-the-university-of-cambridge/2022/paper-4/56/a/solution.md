<h1 id="56/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Osmium tetroxide](../../../../../osmium-tetroxide.md) adds to the alkene through a concerted cyclic addition, giving a five-membered osmate [ester](../../../../../ester.md) with both C-O bonds on the same face. Hydrolysis releases the vicinal diol; NMO reoxidizes reduced osmium so that OsO$_4$ can be catalytic.

The two new hydroxyl groups are therefore **syn**. For the drawn substrate, approach opposite the two existing solid-wedge substituents is less crowded: draw **both new C-OH bonds dashed**, while retaining the solid-wedge CO$_2$Bn and NHBoc bonds. The major diol is cis at its two new centres and anti to the pre-existing substituted face.

Syn delivery is the [stereospecificity](../../../../../stereospecificity.md) of cyclic osmylation; preference for one of the two diastereotopic alkene faces is substrate-controlled [diastereoselectivity](../../../../../diastereoselectivity.md). Attack from the other face would still be syn but would give the minor diastereomer.
