<h1 id="56/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

First the tertiary amine attacks the primary allylic bromide carbon by [SN2 reaction](../../../../../sn2-reaction.md), giving the quaternary ammonium salt. The less hindered nitrogen face is favored by the pre-existing fused-ring stereochemistry.

DBU removes the proton at the carbon alpha to both the ammonium nitrogen and the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). The resulting ammonium [ylide](../../../../../ylide.md) undergoes a concerted [2,3-sigmatropic rearrangement](../../../../../2-3-sigmatropic-rearrangement.md): the N–CH$_2$(allyl) bond breaks, a bond forms between the [ylide](../../../../../ylide.md) carbon and the **aryl-bearing terminal allyl carbon**, and the double bond moves to give $\mathrm{-CH(Ar)-C(Me)=CH_2}$. Nitrogen becomes neutral again. An ordinary alkyl substitution would attach the wrong end of the allyl unit.

An envelope-like five-membered transition structure keeps Ar outside the crowded bicyclic framework. With the pre-existing OMe dash and ring-junction geometry fixed, place the newly installed side chain on the solid-wedge face at the former [ylide](../../../../../ylide.md) carbon and choose the benzylic configuration that places Ar outward, as in the displayed intermediate. This is diastereoselective, suprafacial allyl transfer.

Finally methanesulfonic acid protonates the methyl [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md). Methanol leaves to give an oxocarbenium/iminium-stabilized electrophilic centre, which is attacked intramolecularly by the electron-rich arene at its available ortho position. [Deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) restores aromaticity and closes the bridge. The terminal isopropenyl group survives; it is **not** the bond consumed in this final [Intramolecular Friedel-Crafts reaction](../../../../../intramolecular-friedel-crafts-reaction.md). The constrained tether selects the displayed bridged diastereomer.
