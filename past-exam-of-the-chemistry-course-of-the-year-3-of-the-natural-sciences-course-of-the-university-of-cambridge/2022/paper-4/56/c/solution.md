<h1 id="56/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

This is a thermal [Claisen rearrangement](../../../../../claisen-rearrangement.md) followed by intramolecular [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) exchange. Identify the six-atom array as the cyclopentenone enol-ether C=C–O–CH$_2$–C=C unit. In its concerted $[3,3]$ rearrangement:

- The O–CH$_2$ bond breaks.
- A new C-C bond joins the other end of the cyclopentenone enol double bond to the cyclohexene carbon bearing CH(OEt)$_2$.
- The former ether-bearing cyclopentene carbon becomes a [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md), and the cyclohexene double bond becomes the exocyclic methylene.

The intermediate is thus a substituted cyclopentane-1,3-dione, with the [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) tether now correctly placed for six-membered-ring closure. Its enol oxygen attacks the [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) carbon, replacing OEt and releasing ethanol. Enolization gives the conjugated cyclic enol ether drawn in the product; the second ethoxy group remains.

A chair-like [Claisen rearrangement](../../../../../claisen-rearrangement.md) transition structure with the phenyl-bearing part of the cyclohexane directed outward avoids severe axial crowding. Suprafacial bond formation and the pre-existing configuration give the two indicated ring-junction hydrogens on opposite faces: the left H is a wedge and the right H a dash in the supplied orientation. Closure favors the [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) epimer with outward OEt, drawn as a wedge, while the original Ph remains dashed. This is substrate-controlled diastereoselection followed by conformationally selective [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) closure, rather than independent arbitrary assignment of three centres.
