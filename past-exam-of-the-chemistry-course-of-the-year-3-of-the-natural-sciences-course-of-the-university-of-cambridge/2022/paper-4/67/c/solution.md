<h1 id="67/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The natural mechanism is ionizing solvolysis: C-OTs cleavage gives a cyclopentyl cation/ion pair, capture by acetic acid gives the protonated acetate [ester](../../../../../ester.md), and [deprotonation](../../../../../deprotonation.md) gives product.

The **$1.15$** effect from H/D at the leaving-group carbon is normal alpha-secondary: that carbon develops trigonal [carbocation](../../../../../carbocation.md) character. The **$2.06$** effect from four neighboring labels is a combined beta-secondary effect. Adjacent C-H sigma bonds stabilize the developing empty p orbital by [hyperconjugation](../../../../../hyperconjugation.md); replacing H by D changes the vibrational stabilization and slows ionization. If the four contributions were equal and independent, each would be $2.06^{1/4}=1.198$.

None of those labeled C-H bonds is broken. The isotope effects support substantial [carbocation](../../../../../carbocation.md) character, although distinguishing a free cation from an intimate ion pair needs additional evidence.
