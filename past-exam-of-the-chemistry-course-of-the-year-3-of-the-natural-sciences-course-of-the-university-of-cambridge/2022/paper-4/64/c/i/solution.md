The [Langmuir adsorption model](../../../../../../langmuir-adsorption-model.md) assumes equivalent independent sites, a maximum of one adsorbate per site, no lateral adsorbate interactions, reversible adsorption-desorption equilibrium and non-dissociative [adsorption](../../../../../../adsorption.md) here. Surface coverage obeys $\theta=bC/(1+bC)$ for the solution analogue.

[BET adsorption](../../../../../../bet-adsorption.md) allows multiple layers: occupation of one layer creates sites for further [adsorption](../../../../../../adsorption.md) above it, and higher-layer binding is treated differently from first-layer binding, commonly using a condensation-like energy. Langmuir saturation is monolayer saturation; the BET model is not simply Langmuir with a different numerical equilibrium constant.
