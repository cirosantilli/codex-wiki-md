<h1 id="64/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For cubes of side $\ell$, total area divided by total mass is $S=6/(\rho\ell)$, independently of how many cubes share the $100\,\mathrm g$ sample. With $S=5000\,\mathrm{m^2\,kg^{-1}}$ and $\rho=2330\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$,

$$
\boxed{\ell=\frac6{\rho S}=5.15\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm m.}
$$

This assumes accessible external faces, negligible contact-area losses and equally sized nonporous cubes; [BET adsorption](../../../../../bet-adsorption.md) can also count accessible internal surface in a porous powder.
