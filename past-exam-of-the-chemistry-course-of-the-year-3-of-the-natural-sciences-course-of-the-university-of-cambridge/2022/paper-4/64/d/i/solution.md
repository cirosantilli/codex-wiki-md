<h1 id="64/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For competing reactant and product species on shared equivalent sites, add each $b_jC_j$ term to the denominator:

$$
\theta_i=\frac{b_iC_i}{1+\sum_jb_jC_j}.
$$

Rapid product [desorption](../../../../../../desorption.md) makes product coverages small, reducing competitive site blocking and simplifying the rate to reactant coverage alone. Without that assumption, product [adsorption](../../../../../../adsorption.md) can inhibit conversion and make the apparent rate evolve as products accumulate.
