<h1 id="66/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The neutral para-OH [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) B lies **above** the ordinary Hammett line, so an additional accelerating pathway is needed; ordinary resonance donation by OH alone predicts the wrong direction.

The phenoxide B$^-$ lies far **below** the line: its strong resonance donation reduces acyl electrophilicity, and an anionic [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) is less favorable for approach by OH$^-$. A single neutral-substituent $\sigma$ parameter is also a poor description of this charged, strongly conjugated substrate. These deviations should not be forced onto the same straight line.
