↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Hammett equation](../../../../../../hammett-equation.md) is $\log(k_X/k_H)=\rho\sigma_X$. The positive slope, $\rho=+1.97$, means electron-withdrawing para substituents accelerate hydrolysis. They increase [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) electrophilicity and stabilize the additional negative charge associated with the addition transition state/[tetrahedral intermediate](../../../../../../tetrahedral-intermediate.md). Strong electron donation has the opposite effect. [Hydroxide](../../../../../../hydroxide.md) addition to the acyl carbon, followed by tetrahedral-intermediate collapse and aryloxide loss, is consistent with both this trend and the bimolecular rate law.
