<h1 id="66/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the ordinary [esters](../../../../../../ester.md), [hydroxide](../../../../../../hydroxide.md) adds to the acyl carbon, producing a tetrahedral oxyanion; collapse expels 2,4-dinitrophenoxide, followed by acid-base equilibration to the benzoate. This is base-promoted acyl-oxygen cleavage, a [BAC2 mechanism](../../../../../../bac2-mechanism.md). Rate-limiting addition naturally gives the observed bimolecular dependence on [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) and OH$^-$.
