<h1 id="66/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Hydroxide](../../../../../../hydroxide.md) attack at the aromatic carbon bearing the leaving-group oxygen, followed by [SNAr reaction](../../../../../../nucleophilic-aromatic-substitution.md), would put solvent-derived $^{18}$O into the liberated dinitrophenol. Its absence rules out that aryl C-O cleavage route, conventionally called **BAr2**, as the significant pathway.

Acyl C-O cleavage instead releases the original aryloxy oxygen with the leaving group, so unlabeled dinitrophenol is expected for both tetrahedral acyl substitution and the elimination route below.
