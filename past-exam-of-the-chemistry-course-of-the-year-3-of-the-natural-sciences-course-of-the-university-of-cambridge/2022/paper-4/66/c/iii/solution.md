<h1 id="66/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The positive $\Delta S^\ddagger=+40\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}\,mol^{-1}}$ is compatible with fragmentation/desolvation in the elimination route. A simple rate-limiting association of [hydroxide](../../../../../../hydroxide.md) with a neutral [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) would usually have a less favorable [activation entropy](../../../../../../activation-entropy.md).

The measured activation parameters belong to the overall second-order rate constant: the acid-base pre-equilibrium and solvent reorganization contribute as well. Consequently positive [activation entropy](../../../../../../activation-entropy.md) supports the fragmentation interpretation together with the leaving-group slope, but does not by itself prove a particular elementary transition structure.
