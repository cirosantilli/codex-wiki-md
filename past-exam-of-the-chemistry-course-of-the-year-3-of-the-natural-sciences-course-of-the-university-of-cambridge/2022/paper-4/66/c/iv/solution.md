↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For B, $\Delta V^\ddagger=+12\,\mathrm{cm^3\,mol^{-1}}$ is consistent with bond fission and release/reorganization of electrostricted solvent on reaching a fragmentation transition state. The ordinary methoxy [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) has $\Delta V^\ddagger=-13\,\mathrm{cm^3\,mol^{-1}}$, consistent with associative [hydroxide](../../../../../../hydroxide.md) addition and the compact, strongly solvated developing tetrahedral species.

The opposite signs corroborate distinct mechanisms. Since both values include solvation and any pre-equilibria, they should not be interpreted merely as geometric volumes of isolated molecules.
