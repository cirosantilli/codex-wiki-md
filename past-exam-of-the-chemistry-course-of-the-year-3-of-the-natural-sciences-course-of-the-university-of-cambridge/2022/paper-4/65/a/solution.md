<h1 id="65/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [face-centered cubic](../../../../../face-centered-cubic.md) cell has atoms at its corners and face centers. The $(111)$ plane intercepts the three positive cubic axes at $(a_0,0,0)$, $(0,a_0,0)$ and $(0,0,a_0)$; it is a close-packed triangular layer. Close-packed directions are the $\langle110\rangle$ family, with the in-plane members satisfying $u+v+w=0$.

Nearest neighbors touch along a face diagonal: $4r=\sqrt2a_0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{a_0}{2\sqrt2}=1.389\,\mathrm{\mathring A}}.
$$

The body diagonal is not the touching direction for this crystal structure.
