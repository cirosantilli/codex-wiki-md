<h1 id="60/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The real symmetric quadratic form is positive for every nonzero vector of occupied-virtual rotation parameters precisely when its matrix $X$ is positive definite. Then the Hartree-Fock determinant is a strict local minimum within the considered real orbital-rotation space. A negative [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) gives an unstable lowering direction; a zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) requires higher-order analysis. Stability in this space does not by itself prove global minimality or stability under more general spin/complex-orbital variations.
