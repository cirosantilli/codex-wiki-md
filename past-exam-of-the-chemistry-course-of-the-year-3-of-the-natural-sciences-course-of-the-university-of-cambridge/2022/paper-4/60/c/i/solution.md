<h1 id="60/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The stationary determinant should be stable against orbital rotations: its orbital-energy Hessian must have no negative direction. A suitable local minimum, preferably the lowest accessible determinant of the appropriate symmetry and spin, can approximate the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md); a stationary excited determinant or saddle generally cannot. Stability is still not a guarantee of small electron-correlation error.
