<h1 id="60/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [Hartree-Fock wavefunction](../../../../../../hartree-fock-wavefunction.md) is stationary in energy with respect to all infinitesimal occupied-virtual orbital rotations, subject to orbital orthonormality. The determinant then satisfies the self-consistent Hartree-Fock equations. Stationarity alone does not guarantee that it is the lowest-energy or even a locally stable determinant.
