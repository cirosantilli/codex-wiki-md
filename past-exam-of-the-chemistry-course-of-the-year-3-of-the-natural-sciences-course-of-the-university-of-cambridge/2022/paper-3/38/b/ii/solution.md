<h1 id="38/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

CO first dissociates from the 18-electron Fischer [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md). Pyridyl nitrogen of C then coordinates at the vacant chromium site, positioning an ortho C-D bond of its [phenyl group](../../../../../../phenyl-group.md) beside the [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md) unit.

The appropriate pathway is **[oxidative hydrogen migration](../../../../../../oxidative-hydrogen-migration.md)**: chromium assists C-D cleavage while D transfers to the electrophilic [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). The resulting cyclometalated intermediate has both an aryl-Cr bond and an alkyl-Cr bond, the latter to $\mathrm{C(D)(OEt)(o\!-\!tolyl)}$. Formally Cr(0) becomes Cr(II). [Reductive elimination](../../../../../../reductive-elimination.md) between those two [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) ligands forms the new aryl–$\mathrm{C(D)(OEt)(o\!-\!tolyl)}$ bond and returns chromium to low [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md).

This accounts for all three controls. Extra CO suppresses the coordinative vacancy. Biphenyl E lacks the pyridyl-N directing group and cannot organize the necessary C-H activation geometry. The ortho deuterium moves onto the former [carbene](../../../../../../carbene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) rather than being lost to solvent: four D remain on the original phenyl ring and the fifth becomes the benzylic C-D bond.

This is not adequately represented as electrophilic aromatic substitution followed by proton loss, which would discard the diagnostic D. The primary mechanistic study distinguishes [oxidative hydrogen migration](../../../../../../oxidative-hydrogen-migration.md)/[reductive elimination](../../../../../../reductive-elimination.md) from that classical alternative.[Azacycle-directed formal aromatic C-H insertion with a chromium Fischer carbene by Xing-Qi Yao and colleagues](../../../../../../azacycle-directed-formal-aromatic-c-h-insertion-with-a-chromium-fischer-carbene-by-xing-qi-yao-and-colleagues.md)
