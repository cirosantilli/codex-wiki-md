<h1 id="42/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With $[\mathrm{H_2}]=5\times10^{-6}[\mathrm M]$, a reaction of an [electronically excited oxygen atom](../../../../../electronically-excited-oxygen-atom.md) with [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) gives one OH and, after rapid H conversion, one HO$_2$. The radical source becomes

$$
S'=\frac{2J_1[\mathrm O_3]\left(k_3[\mathrm{H_2O}]+k_{10}[\mathrm{H_2}]\right)}
{k_2[\mathrm M]+k_3[\mathrm{H_2O}]+k_{10}[\mathrm{H_2}]}
\simeq2.85\times10^4\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}s^{-1}}.
$$

This is about 4.75 times the original source. Holding [water](../../../../../water.md), [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), NO and radiation fixed keeps the fast radical partition approximately unchanged, while bimolecular termination makes total radical abundance scale as $\sqrt S$. Thus

$$
[\mathrm{HO_2}]'\simeq7.67\times10^8\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}},\qquad
\boxed{L'_{\rm HO_x}\simeq2.76\times10^6\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}s^{-1}}},
$$

**about 2.18 times the original catalytic loss**. This predicts a stronger ozone-loss tendency within the specified stationary box model, not a numerically determined percentage change in the [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) layer. The latter requires coupled odd-oxygen production, changing [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) [concentrations](../../../../../concentration.md), transport and the longer-term [water](../../../../../water.md) budget; those have been held fixed here.
