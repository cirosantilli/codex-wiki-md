<h1 id="42/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The dominant OH-to-HO$_2$ pseudo-first-order rate is $k_5[\mathrm O_3]=90\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, corresponding to **$0.0111\,\mathrm s$**. Recycling HO$_2$ through atomic [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and NO has rate $k_6[\mathrm O]+k_8[\mathrm{NO}]=0.0218\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, corresponding to **$45.9\,\mathrm s$** for an individual HO$_2$ radical. A fixed-total two-state exchange relaxes at the sum of forward and reverse rates, giving about $0.0111\,\mathrm s$ for the partition perturbation. Its fast partition is therefore

$$
\frac{[\mathrm{HO_2}]}{[\mathrm{OH}]}
\simeq\frac{k_5[\mathrm O_3]}{k_6[\mathrm O]+k_8[\mathrm{NO}]}
=4.13\times10^3.
$$

This supports a [steady-state approximation in chemical kinetics](../../../../../../steady-state-approximation-in-chemical-kinetics.md) for rapidly recycled intermediates if the parent [concentrations](../../../../../../concentration.md) vary slowly. It does not by itself establish stationarity of the entire [HOx family](../../../../../../hox-family.md). The later source–termination estimate gives a family turnover time of about 16 hours, far longer than partition relaxation; a stationary absolute abundance additionally assumes sufficiently persistent radiation and background conditions. This is the distinction between [fast family partition and slow family balance](../../../../../../fast-family-partition-and-slow-family-balance.md).
