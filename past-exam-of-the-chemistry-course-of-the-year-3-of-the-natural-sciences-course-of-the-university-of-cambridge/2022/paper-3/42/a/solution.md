<h1 id="42/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A warmer [troposphere](../../../../../troposphere.md) does not imply a warmer [stratosphere](../../../../../stratosphere.md): increased [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) can cool the middle and upper stratosphere by enhancing infrared emission. One direct kinetic effect is slower temperature-dependent destruction in the [Chapman cycle](../../../../../chapman-cycle.md), tending to increase upper-stratospheric [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md). Two indirect chemical effects can have the opposite sign. If the entry [atmospheric cold trap](../../../../../atmospheric-cold-trap.md) warms, it can admit more [water](../../../../../water.md); reaction with an [electronically excited oxygen atom](../../../../../electronically-excited-oxygen-atom.md) then supplies more of the [HOx family](../../../../../hox-family.md), enhancing catalytic [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) loss. In cold polar air, changes in [water](../../../../../water.md) and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) can favour [polar stratospheric clouds](../../../../../polar-stratospheric-cloud.md), whose surfaces activate halogen reservoirs and can enhance [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md) destruction when sunlight returns. These are conditional pathways, not a universal sign for total [ozone](../../../../../ozone.md): cold-trap [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and circulation, halogen abundance and altitude all matter. [Methane](../../../../../methane.md) oxidation can also supply stratospheric [water](../../../../../water.md).[Executive summary: Stratospheric Temperature, water-vapour influences, and temperature-dependent polar ozone loss](../../../../../scientific-assessment-of-ozone-depletion-2022-executive-summary.md)
