<h1 id="48/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The surface [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) follows by differentiating the logarithm of the [canonical partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) with respect to container area:

$$
\Pi=k_BT(\partial_a\ln Q)_{N,T}
=\frac{Nk_BT}{a-2Na_d}
=\frac{k_BT}{a_d}\frac{\phi}{1-2\phi}.
$$

Its formal divergence is therefore **$\phi=1/2$**. This lies outside the low-density regime used to derive the expression. It is a model pole, not an actual geometrical close-packing fraction.
