<h1 id="48/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Insert the stipulated near-close-packing relation for $R$ into the [hard-disc cage approximation](../../../../../hard-disc-cage-approximation.md):

$$
\mathcal A=4a_d\left(\sqrt{\frac{\phi_{\rm cp}}\phi}-1\right)^2
=4a_d\left(\sqrt{\frac{\phi_{\rm cp}a}{Na_d}}-1\right)^2.
$$

Writing $y=\sqrt{\phi_{\rm cp}a/(Na_d)}$, we have $\partial_a y=y/(2a)$ and therefore $\partial_a\ln\mathcal A=y/[a(y-1)]$. From the supplied crystalline [canonical partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md),

$$
\boxed{\Pi_{\rm xtl}=Nk_BT\partial_a\ln\mathcal A
=\frac{k_BT}{a_d}\frac{\phi}{1-\sqrt{\phi/\phi_{\rm cp}}}}.
$$

Its close-packing scale is set by the [triangular lattice packing of discs](../../../../../triangular-lattice-packing-of-discs.md), with $\phi_{\rm cp}=\pi/(2\sqrt3)$ in the ideal geometry.
