<h1 id="48/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With wall effects neglected, **$a_1=a$**. A second [hard disc](../../../../../hard-disc.md) cannot have its centre within distance $2r$ of the first centre. Its [excluded volume](../../../../../excluded-volume.md) in this two-dimensional setting is an excluded area $\pi(2r)^2=4a_d$, giving

$$
\boxed{a_2=a-4a_d}.
$$

This is exclusion of the second centre, rather than simply subtraction of the first disc's own area.
