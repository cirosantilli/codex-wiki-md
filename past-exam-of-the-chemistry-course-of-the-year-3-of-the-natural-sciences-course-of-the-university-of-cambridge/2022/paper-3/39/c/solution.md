**[Figure 7](#39/c/image-preferred-cyclohexene-half-chair-with-the-bulky-tertiary-alcohol-side-chain-pseudoequatorial). Preferred cyclohexene half-chair with the bulky tertiary-alcohol side chain pseudoequatorial**.

Use a **[cyclohexene half-chair](../../../../../cyclohexene-half-chair.md)**, not a fully puckered saturated-cyclohexane chair. The [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) carbons and their directly attached bonds form the approximately planar end; the saturated part puckers. Put the $\mathrm{C(OH)(CH_3)_2}$ substituent pseudoequatorial at the opposite saturated methine to avoid a crowded pseudoaxial arrangement. Its methine proton is correspondingly pseudoaxial. This is a preferred [conformation](../../../../../conformation.md); the achiral spectra do not assign an absolute configuration.
