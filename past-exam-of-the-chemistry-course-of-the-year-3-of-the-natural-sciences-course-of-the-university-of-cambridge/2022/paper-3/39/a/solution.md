**[Figure 5](#39/a/image-the-cinnamoyloxymethyl-and-tertiary-alcohol-side-chains-with-their-spectral-assignments). The cinnamoyloxymethyl and tertiary-alcohol side chains, with their spectral assignments**.

The two substituents are **$\mathrm{-CH_2-O-C(O)-CH=CH-Ph}$**, with the [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) E, and **$\mathrm{-C(OH)(CH_3)_2}$**. The free bond at the left of each drawing attaches to the [cyclohexene](../../../../../cyclohexene.md).

The A-D [COSY](../../../../../cosy.md) correlation and $16.2\,\mathrm{Hz}$ coupling identify the trans cinnamate double bond. Five aromatic protons and four phenyl-carbon environments identify its monosubstituted phenyl ring. F is a two-proton OCH$_2$ signal; its [HMBC](../../../../../hmbc.md) correlation to [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) 1 joins that methylene through [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) to the [ester](../../../../../ester.md). The two three-proton singlets N/O, their correlations to oxygenated quaternary [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) 10, and the missing OH resonance give the tertiary-alcohol substituent.
