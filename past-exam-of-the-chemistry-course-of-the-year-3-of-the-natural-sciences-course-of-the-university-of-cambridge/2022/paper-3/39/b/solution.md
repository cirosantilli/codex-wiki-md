On the ester-containing side chain, read outward from the [cyclohexene](../../../../../cyclohexene.md):

$$
\mathrm{ring{-}\underset{11;\ F,F}{CH_2}{-}O{-}
\underset{1}{C}(=O){-}
\underset{9;\ D}{CH}{=}
\underset{2;\ A}{CH}{-}
\underset{3\ (ipso)}{Ph}}.
$$

A is the downfield beta-vinylic proton and D the alpha-vinylic proton next to the [ester](../../../../../ester.md).

The phenyl ortho pair is **B on [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) 7**; the meta pair is **C on [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) 6**; its para proton is the remaining **C on [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) 5**. Thus the C envelope integrates to three protons, while B integrates to two. [Carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) 3 is the unprotonated ipso site.

In the other substituent, **10** is the quaternary C-OH [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). The methyl assignments are **N on 13** and **O on 16**, reading the very closely spaced [HSQC](../../../../../hsqc.md) cross peaks together with the positive methyl responses in [DEPT-135](../../../../../dept-135.md). Their mutual long-range correlations and correlations to 10 support the gem-dimethyl group. The two methyl carbons are distinguishable in this chiral molecular environment; their exact stereotopic names are not determined here.
