<h1 id="43/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Peroxyacetyl nitrate](../../../../../peroxyacetyl-nitrate.md) forms reversibly from a peroxyacetyl radical and NO$_2$. It stores reactive nitrogen while intact, then thermal dissociation can release it again. It can therefore transport nitrogen through colder free-tropospheric air and recycle it more rapidly in a warmer [planetary boundary layer](../../../../../planetary-boundary-layer.md). Deposition or further chemistry can compete, but reversible storage is its reservoir role.

[Dinitrogen pentoxide](../../../../../dinitrogen-pentoxide.md) is likewise a reservoir because $\mathrm{NO_2+NO_3+M\rightleftharpoons N_2O_5+M}$ can return reactive species. Conversion to nitric acid makes it a sink from the active [nitrogen oxides](../../../../../nitrogen-oxides.md) family, especially through heterogeneous uptake on wet [aerosol](../../../../../aerosol.md) or cloud particles, followed by deposition or washout. [Water](../../../../../water.md) vapour alone and wet-particle uptake are different mechanisms; they should not be assigned the same [rate constant](../../../../../rate-constant.md). [Temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), particle surface area and removal are often very different between the boundary layer and the free troposphere, so a reservoir can be transported in one environment and chemically removed in another.
