<h1 id="43/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the stated total number density, multiply the number [atmospheric mixing ratio](../../../../../../atmospheric-mixing-ratio.md) by $[\mathrm M]$. With NO$_x$ defined as NO plus NO$_2$,

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
[\mathrm{NO_x}]&=2.46\times10^{11},&[\mathrm{NO}]&=7.38\times10^{10},\\
[\mathrm{NO_2}]&=1.722\times10^{11},&[\mathrm O_3]&=9.84\times10^{11},\\
[\mathrm{H_2O}]&=7.38\times10^{17}
\end{aligned}\quad\mathrm{molecules\,cm^{-3}}}.
$$

The [water](../../../../../../water.md) partial [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) is $30/1000=0.030$ of the total [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md). It is a 3% number fraction, not 30 ppm.
