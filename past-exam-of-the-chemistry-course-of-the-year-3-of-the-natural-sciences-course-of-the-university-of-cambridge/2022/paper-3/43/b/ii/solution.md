<h1 id="43/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The fast family is **NO$_3$ plus N$_2$O$_5$**. Put $x=[\mathrm{NO_3}]$, $y=[\mathrm{N_2O_5}]$, and freeze the supplied parent [concentrations](../../../../../../concentration.md) while defining

$$
S=k_2[\mathrm{NO_2}][\mathrm O_3],\quad a=k_6[\mathrm{NO_2}][\mathrm M],
\quad b=k_7,\quad c=k_8[\mathrm{H_2O}],\quad
 d=k_4[\mathrm{NO}]+k_5[\mathrm{NO_2}].
$$

The two equations are $\dot x=S-(a+d)x+by$ and $\dot y=ax-(b+c)y$. Their stationary solution is

$$
\boxed{x=\frac{S}{d+ac/(b+c)}=3.09\times10^6\,\mathrm{molecules\,cm^{-3}}},
\qquad y=\frac{ax}{b+c}=7.02\times10^8\,\mathrm{molecules\,cm^{-3}}.
$$

Here $a=15.67$, $b=0.0690$, $c=3.84\times10^{-7}$ and $d=1.919$ in inverse seconds; $S=5.93\times10^6\,\mathrm{molecules\,cm^{-3}s^{-1}}$. Reversible storage must not be counted as permanent family loss: only the $ac/(b+c)$ term represents the association route followed by [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md).

Closed-family interconversion has $\tau_{\rm eq}=1/(a+b)=0.0635\,\mathrm s$. Including the supplied losses, the two linear relaxation times are approximately **$0.0566\,\mathrm s$ and $133\,\mathrm s$**, from the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $\begin{pmatrix}a+d&-b\\-a&b+c\end{pmatrix}$. Fast exchange is therefore well separated from the slower total-family adjustment. However, NO is consumed by [ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) on $1/(k_1[\mathrm O_3])\simeq59.8\,\mathrm s$, comparable to and shorter than the family relaxation. Thus the quoted stationary [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) is justified for a maintained background or a sufficiently slowly varying quasi-stationary environment; it is not automatically reached in an isolated parcel initialized with the supplied composition. NO auto-oxidation is negligible at this [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md). Additional organic losses of NO$_3$, if present, would need to be added to the model.
