<h1 id="44/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The six pi [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) of B reorganize in a concerted [electrocyclic reaction](../../../../../../electrocyclic-reaction.md): the two terminal p orbitals form the new sigma bond, and the other two pi bonds shift to give the conjugated cyclohexadiene. Thermal six-electron closure is [disrotatory](../../../../../../disrotatory-motion.md), so the termini rotate in opposite senses. Applied to the terminal configurations drawn for B, this gives the trans methyl relationship. There is no need to invoke a radical chain or a sigmatropic transfer of a methyl group.
