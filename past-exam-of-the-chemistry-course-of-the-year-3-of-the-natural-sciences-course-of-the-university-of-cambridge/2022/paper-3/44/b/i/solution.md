**C has the original cyclohexadiene connectivity but trans, rather than cis, adjacent methyl groups.** Draw one methyl wedge and the other dash, keeping both phenyl groups at the diene termini. One [enantiomer](../../../../../../enantiomer.md) is shown below; reversing both stereobonds gives its mirror image. For the symmetric achiral starting framework under achiral conditions, the two trans [enantiomers](../../../../../../enantiomer.md) are not distinguished by this orbital-symmetry argument.
