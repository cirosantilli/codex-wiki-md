↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**A to B is a photochemical six-electron electrocyclic ring opening; B to C is a thermal six-electron electrocyclic ring closure.** The broken or formed sigma bond is between the two methyl-bearing saturated carbons of the cyclohexadiene. Both transformations conserve the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) skeleton. The light-induced and thermal processes differ in their orbital occupation and therefore in their stereochemical selection rule.
