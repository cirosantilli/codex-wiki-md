<h1 id="44/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Base first gives [phenoxide](../../../../../../phenoxide.md). Use its para-carbanion/quinoid resonance contributor: the para [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bearing the tether attacks the terminal chloromethylene [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) in an intramolecular substitution, ejecting [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md). This forms a five-membered ring at that [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and produces a **para-spirocyclohexadienone**, with the [phenoxide](../../../../../../phenoxide.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) becoming a [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). The first stage is a [dearomatizing spirocyclization of a phenoxide](../../../../../../dearomatizing-spirocyclization-of-a-phenoxide.md).

On acidification, [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) protonation makes the dienone electrophilic. A tether C–C bond attached to the spiro centre migrates to the adjacent dienone [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), with the accompanying pi-bond shifts; loss of a proton restores aromaticity. This [dienone-phenol rearrangement](../../../../../../dienone-phenol-rearrangement.md) converts the spiro five-membered ring into the fused six-membered ring while regenerating the phenolic OH. The apparent bond formation meta to OH is thus explained by **para spirocyclization followed by skeletal migration**, rather than requiring the final fused connectivity to form directly in the base step.
