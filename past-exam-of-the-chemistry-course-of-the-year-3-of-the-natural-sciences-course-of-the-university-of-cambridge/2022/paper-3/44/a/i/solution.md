<h1 id="44/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Activate the [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) hydroxyl [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) with thionyl [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) to give a good N–O leaving group. In the [Beckmann rearrangement](../../../../../../beckmann-rearrangement.md) branch, the C–C bond anti to that leaving group migrates onto nitrogen as the N–O bond breaks. Here the migrating [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is the spiro centre. Inserting N between that centre and the former [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) expands the five-membered [oxime](../../../../../../oxime.md) ring to a six-membered [nitrilium ion](../../../../../../nitrilium-ion.md) ring. [Water](../../../../../../water.md) attacks the electrophilic nitrilium [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), followed by proton transfers and tautomerization to the observed spiro [lactam](../../../../../../lactam.md). The [lactam](../../../../../../lactam.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is introduced in the aqueous work-up.

The competing [Beckmann fragmentation](../../../../../../beckmann-fragmentation.md) cleaves the same anti C–C bond instead of completing migration onto N. Formation of the C≡N bond gives an open-chain [nitrile](../../../../../../nitrile.md) and a tertiary [carbocation](../../../../../../carbocation.md) at the former spiro centre. Loss of a proton from the adjacent chain [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) then gives the exocyclic alkene attached to the retained cyclopentane. The key distinction is **N insertion and ring expansion for the [lactam](../../../../../../lactam.md), versus C–C cleavage followed by elimination for the unsaturated [nitrile](../../../../../../nitrile.md)**. Stabilization of the resulting tertiary [carbocation](../../../../../../carbocation.md) makes fragmentation plausible.
