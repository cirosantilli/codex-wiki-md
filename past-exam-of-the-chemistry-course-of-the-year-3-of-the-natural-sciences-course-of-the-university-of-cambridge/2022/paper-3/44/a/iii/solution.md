<h1 id="44/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Amide base removes a proton alpha to the [nitrile](../../../../../../nitrile.md), giving a resonance-stabilized [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md). Removal of the available aryl proton ortho to [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md), together with [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) elimination, generates a [benzyne](../../../../../../benzyne.md) between the chlorine-bearing [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and its unsubstituted ortho neighbour. The side-chain [nitrile](../../../../../../nitrile.md) carbanion attacks the former chlorine-bearing aryne [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) intramolecularly; one aryne pi bond shifts to the neighbouring aryl [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). Protonation of that aryl anion by ammonia restores the aromatic ring. The newly formed C–C bond closes the **four-membered ring of the cyanobenzocyclobutane product**. Two deprotonation processes are required to generate both the [nitrile](../../../../../../nitrile.md) [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md) and the aryne. The [nitrile](../../../../../../nitrile.md) group is retained, and the product is not formed by simple replacement of aryl [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) with an external amide [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md).
