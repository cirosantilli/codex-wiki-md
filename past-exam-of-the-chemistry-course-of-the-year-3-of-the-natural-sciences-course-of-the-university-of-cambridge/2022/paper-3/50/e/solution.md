<h1 id="50/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

[Saponification](../../../../../saponification.md) cleaves the acetate ester side groups while the intended [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) backbone remains intact:

$$
\mathrm{-CH_2-CH(OCOCH_3)-+OH^-\longrightarrow-CH_2-CH(OH)-+CH_3CO_2^-}.
$$

The ideal target C is therefore **a [vinyl chloride-vinyl alcohol copolymer](../../../../../vinyl-chloride-vinyl-alcohol-copolymer.md)**, containing $-\mathrm{CH_2-CH(Cl)}-$ and $-\mathrm{CH_2-CH(OH)}-$ units in the original sequence positions. The [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)–[oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) bond to the backbone remains; [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) removes the acetyl group from [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). Incomplete [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) would leave a three-component population of [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md), acetate and alcohol units. New hydroxyl groups can strengthen adhesion and coating interactions through [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md).
