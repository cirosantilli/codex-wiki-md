<h1 id="50/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Addition consumes the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)–[carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) double bond of [vinyl chloride](../../../../../vinyl-chloride.md) and retains [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) as a side substituent. The interior repeat of A is therefore

$$
\boxed{[-\mathrm{CH_2-CH(Cl)}-]_n},
$$

namely [polyvinyl chloride](../../../../../polyvinyl-chloride.md). [Benzoyl peroxide](../../../../../benzoyl-peroxide.md) supplies initiating [free radicals](../../../../../radical-chemistry.md); head-to-tail addition gives the usual backbone connectivity. End groups and [tacticity](../../../../../tacticity.md) are not uniquely specified by this repeat formula.
