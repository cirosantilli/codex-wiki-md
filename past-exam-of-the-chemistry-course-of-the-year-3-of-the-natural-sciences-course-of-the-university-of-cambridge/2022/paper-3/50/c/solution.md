↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

B contains both $-\mathrm{CH_2-CH(Cl)}-$ and $-\mathrm{CH_2-CH(OCOCH_3)}-$ monomeric units distributed along the same [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) backbone: **a [vinyl chloride-vinyl acetate copolymer](../../../../../vinyl-chloride-vinyl-acetate-copolymer.md)**. One possible local segment is

$$
\mathrm{\cdots-CH_2-CH(Cl)-CH_2-CH(OAc)-CH_2-CH(Cl)-\cdots},
\qquad \mathrm{OAc=OC(O)CH_3}.
$$

The ester is attached through [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) to the backbone [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). A [random copolymer](../../../../../random-copolymer.md) should not be drawn as two long homopolymer blocks. The stated $75:25$ [monomer](../../../../../monomer.md) feed is not enough to prove an incorporated $75:25$ unit ratio: [monomer](../../../../../monomer.md) reactivity ratios and conversion affect composition and sequence statistics. Under an explicitly ideal equal-incorporation assumption that ratio could be used as a schematic average, not a periodic repeat.
