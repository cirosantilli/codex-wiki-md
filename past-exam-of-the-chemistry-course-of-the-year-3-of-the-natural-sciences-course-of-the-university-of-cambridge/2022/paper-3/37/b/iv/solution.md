<h1 id="37/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Low-spin Co(III) is substitution-inert. In acid, coordinated ammine [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) is suppressed, so the strongly donating amido [conjugate base](../../../../../../conjugate-base.md) that assists ligand loss is not generated appreciably. Direct aquation/linkage change consequently remains very slow.

The persistence of the starting S-bound complex therefore supports a **base-activated kinetic pathway**. It is consistent with the rapid rearrangement in base and does not [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) that the S-bound form must be the thermodynamically preferred linkage isomer under every condition.
