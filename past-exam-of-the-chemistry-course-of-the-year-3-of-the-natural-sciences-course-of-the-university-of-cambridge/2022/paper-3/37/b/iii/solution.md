<h1 id="37/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The near absence of incoming $^{14}$C label means the coordinated thiocyanate usually retains its **molecular identity** during linkage change. A mechanism that releases it into a fully mixed solution pool and then captures an arbitrary external thiocyanate would predict much more exchange.

Rapid geminate return within a [solvent cage](../../../../../../solvent-cage.md) explains the result: the original ligand turns and binds through N before an external labeled ligand can intercept the cobalt centre. A continuously bound intramolecular rearrangement is also compatible with low exchange. Therefore the label result supports an intramolecular/cage-local process but does not alone prove that the Co-S bond stays unbroken throughout.
