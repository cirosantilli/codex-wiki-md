<h1 id="49/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose mass-weighted coordinates $Q_A$, $\mathbf Q_E=(Q_{E1},Q_{E2})$, and two triplets $\mathbf Q_3,\mathbf Q_4$ for the two $T_2$ copies. About a stationary equilibrium geometry, the allowed harmonic potential is

$$
V^{(2)}=\frac12k_AQ_A^2+\frac12k_E|\mathbf Q_E|^2
+\frac12k_{33}|\mathbf Q_3|^2+k_{34}\mathbf Q_3\cdot\mathbf Q_4
+\frac12k_{44}|\mathbf Q_4|^2.
$$

The [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is one-half the sum of the squared conjugate momenta. Cross terms between inequivalent [irreducible representations](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) are forbidden, but the two equivalent $T_2$ triplets permit the displayed scalar-product term. This is [equivalent-mode harmonic coupling](../../../../../../equivalent-mode-harmonic-coupling.md). An orthogonal rotation between the two triplets diagonalizes their $2\times2$ force-constant matrix. In [molecular normal coordinates](../../../../../../molecular-normal-coordinate.md) the complete [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{H=E_0+\frac12(P_A^2+\omega_1^2Q_A^2)
+\frac12\sum_{j=1}^2(P_{Ej}^2+\omega_2^2Q_{Ej}^2)
+\frac12\sum_{j=1}^3(P_{3j}^2+\omega_3^2Q_{3j}^2+P_{4j}^2+\omega_4^2Q_{4j}^2)}.
$$

Symmetry enforces degeneracies within each multiplet; it does not require equal frequencies for the two distinct $T_2$ [normal modes](../../../../../../normal-mode.md).
