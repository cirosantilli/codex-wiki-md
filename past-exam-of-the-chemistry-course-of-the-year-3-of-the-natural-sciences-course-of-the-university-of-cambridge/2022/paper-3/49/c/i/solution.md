<h1 id="49/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the class order $(E,8C_3,3C_2,6S_4,6\sigma_d)$, the 15 Cartesian nuclear displacements of [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) have [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $(15,0,-1,-1,3)$. Only nuclei left fixed by an operation contribute to that operation's [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md). Subtract translations $T_2$ and rotations $T_1$ to obtain the [methane normal-mode representation](../../../../../../methane-normal-mode-representation.md)

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{\rm vib}=A_1+E+2T_2}.
$$

Its dimension is $1+2+3+3=9=3(5)-6$. There are four frequency families: a nondegenerate symmetric stretch, a doubly degenerate bend, and triply degenerate stretch and bend modes.
