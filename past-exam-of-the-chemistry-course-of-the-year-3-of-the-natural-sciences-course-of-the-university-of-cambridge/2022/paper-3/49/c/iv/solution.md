<h1 id="49/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The relevant decomposition is unchanged:

$$
\operatorname{Sym}^2T_2=A_1+E+T_2.
$$

Thus **the same $E$ and $T_2$ vibrational symmetries are linearly Jahn-Teller active**. Their coupling constants and the resulting [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) surface can differ from those of a $T_1$ state. The ground-geometry [methane normal-mode representation](../../../../../../methane-normal-mode-representation.md) and the symmetry-allowed harmonic invariants are also unchanged; actual force constants in the excited state need not have the same values.
