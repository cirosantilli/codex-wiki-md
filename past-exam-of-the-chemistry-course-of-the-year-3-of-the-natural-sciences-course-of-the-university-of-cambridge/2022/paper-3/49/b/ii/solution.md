<h1 id="49/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a real-character irreducible molecular representation with [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $\chi$, the multiplicity of the [fully symmetric representation](../../../../../../fully-symmetric-representation.md) in its tensor square is

$$
m_{\rm sym}=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}\chi(g)^2
=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}|\chi(g)|^2
=\boxed{1},
$$

by [character orthogonality](../../../../../../character-orthogonality.md). The identity uses the real-character assumption appropriate to the usual real [irreducible representations](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) intended here. For a general complex [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md), the [trivial representation in a tensor square](../../../../../../trivial-representation-in-a-tensor-square.md) has multiplicity one precisely when it is self-dual, and zero otherwise, by [Schur's lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md). The universally valid tensor product is $V\otimes V^*$.
