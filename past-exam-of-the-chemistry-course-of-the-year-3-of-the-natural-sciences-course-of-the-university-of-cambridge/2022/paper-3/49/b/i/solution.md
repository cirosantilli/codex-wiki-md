↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For representation spaces $V,W$, the [tensor product of group representations](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) acts on product basis vectors through

$$
D_{V\otimes W}(g)=D_V(g)\otimes D_W(g).
$$

Its [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md). Summing diagonal elements explicitly,

$$
\chi_{V\otimes W}(g)=\sum_{i,j}[D_V(g)]_{ii}[D_W(g)]_{jj}
=\boxed{\chi_V(g)\chi_W(g)}.
$$

No step assumes one-dimensional matrices, so the result applies to both degenerate and nondegenerate representations.
