↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An ordinary molecular [point group](../../../../../../point-group.md) consists of the identity, proper rotations $C_n$, and, when present, reflections $\sigma$, inversion $i$, and improper rotations $S_n$. Each operation leaves the nuclear geometry and chemical labels equivalent, so only identical nuclei can be interchanged. Translations are not elements of the [point group](../../../../../../point-group.md) of a finite isolated [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md). The [molecular symmetry operations](../../../../../../molecular-symmetry-operation.md) determine how electronic and vibrational coordinates transform.
