<h1 id="47/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At fixed [particle number](../../../../../particle-number.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), the joint [isothermal-isobaric ensemble](../../../../../isothermal-isobaric-ensemble.md) weight for a [microstate](../../../../../microstate.md) of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E$ and [volume](../../../../../volume.md) $V$ is $e^{-\beta(E+PV)}$. Write its [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) as $H=E+PV$. Differentiating the [isothermal-isobaric partition function](../../../../../isothermal-isobaric-partition-function.md) with a temperature-independent [volume](../../../../../volume.md) measure gives

$$
\partial_\beta Q_P=-\int_0^\infty dV\sum_s H_s(V)e^{-\beta H_s(V)}.
$$

After division by $Q_P$ this is the normalized [ensemble average](../../../../../expected-value-split.md), so

$$
\boxed{\langle H\rangle_P=-\partial_\beta\ln Q_P}.
$$

The differentiation assumes that the microscopic [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) and the integration measure have no additional explicit [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence. An extra temperature-dependent normalization factor must be differentiated separately.
