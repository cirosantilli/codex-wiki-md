↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the normalized average, including the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of its denominator. The [enthalpy fluctuation formula](../../../../../enthalpy-fluctuation-formula.md) follows from

$$
\partial_\beta\langle H\rangle_P
=-\langle H^2\rangle_P+\langle H\rangle_P^2
=-\operatorname{Var}(H).
$$

Since $d\beta/dT=-1/(k_BT^2)$,

$$
\boxed{C_P=\frac{\operatorname{Var}(H)}{k_BT^2}}.
$$

Thus a larger equilibrium spread of [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) corresponds to a larger response to heating at fixed [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). The [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is nonnegative, so this finite-system equilibrium description has $C_P\geq0$. The relation connects a response coefficient to fluctuations of the appropriate exchanged quantity; at fixed [volume](../../../../../volume.md) the corresponding quantity is [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md).
