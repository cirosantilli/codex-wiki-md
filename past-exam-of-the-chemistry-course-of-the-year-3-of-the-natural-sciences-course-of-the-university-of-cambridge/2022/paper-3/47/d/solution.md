<h1 id="47/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [volume](../../../../../volume.md) [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is a [gamma function](../../../../../gamma-function.md) [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md),

$$
\int_0^\infty V^N e^{-\beta PV}\,dV=\frac{N!}{(\beta P)^{N+1}},
\qquad
\boxed{Q_P=\frac{1}{\Lambda^{3N}(\beta P)^{N+1}}}.
$$

For the prescribed bridge relation, $\beta PQ_P=(\beta P\Lambda^3)^{-N}$, so $G=Nk_BT\ln(\beta P\Lambda^3)$. Since $\Lambda\propto T^{-1/2}$, this gives the macroscopic ideal-gas result

$$
\boxed{H=G-T(\partial_TG)_P=\frac52Nk_BT,\qquad C_P=\frac52Nk_B},
\qquad C_P-C_V=Nk_B.
$$

Heating at fixed [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) requires both an increase in [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) and expansion work; at fixed [volume](../../../../../volume.md) the latter contribution is absent.

The [finite-size normalization of the isothermal-isobaric ensemble](../../../../../finite-size-normalization-of-the-isothermal-isobaric-ensemble.md) matters here. Applying part (a) literally to the unprefactored $Q_P$ instead gives $\langle H\rangle_P=(5N/2+1)k_BT$ and $C_P=(5N/2+1)k_B$. The two answers differ because $-\partial_\beta\ln(\beta PQ_P)=-\partial_\beta\ln Q_P-k_BT$. The normalized continuous-volume distribution has $\langle PV\rangle=(N+1)k_BT$; its [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) agrees with the unprefactored result in part (b). The bridge convention subtracts an order-one contribution and recovers the usual extensive thermodynamic answer. Both agree to leading order in the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md), but their exact finite-$N$ derivatives should not be mixed.
