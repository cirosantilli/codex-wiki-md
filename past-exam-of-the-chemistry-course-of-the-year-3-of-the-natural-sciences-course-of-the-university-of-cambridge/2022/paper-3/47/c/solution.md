<h1 id="47/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Away from singular regimes, assume [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) and [heat capacity at constant pressure](../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) are extensive: $\langle H\rangle=Nh$ and $C_P=Nc_P$, with nonzero intensive $h$. The [enthalpy fluctuation formula](../../../../../enthalpy-fluctuation-formula.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(H)}}{|\langle H\rangle|}
=\frac{\sqrt{k_BT^2c_P}}{|h|\sqrt N}}.
$$

Absolute fluctuations grow as $\sqrt N$, while the [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) grows as $N$. In the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md), [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) per particle therefore concentrates around its [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md); it is relative, rather than absolute, fluctuations that vanish. This is the usual [relative fluctuations of an extensive observable](../../../../../relative-fluctuations-of-an-extensive-observable.md) scaling. It assumes ordinary extensive [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) and a nonzero [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) with a stated [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) reference; a critical divergence or an artificially zero [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) requires separate analysis.
