<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Open the cyclic [ester](../../../../ester.md) at its acyl–[oxygen](../../../../oxygen.md) bond. The resulting delta-hydroxy acid is

$$
\mathrm{HO_2C-CH(nPr)-CH_2-CH(iPr)-CH(OH)Ph}.
$$

Oxidation-state reversal at the benzylic [alcohol](../../../../alcohol.md) gives $\mathrm{HO_2C-CH(nPr)-CH_2-CH(iPr)-C(=O)Ph}$. This identifies the key **Michael disconnection between the central $\mathrm{CH_2}$ and $\mathrm{CH(iPr)}$ groups**:

$$
\mathrm{PhCOCH_2iPr}+\mathrm{CH_2=C(nPr)CO_2Et}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{PhCOCH(iPr)CH_2CH(nPr)CO_2Et}.
$$

The [nucleophile](../../../../nucleophile.md) is the [enolate](../../../../enolate.md) or [enamine](../../../../enamine.md) of the phenyl [ketone](../../../../ketone.md). The [electrophile](../../../../electrophile.md)'s propyl substituent must be alpha to its [ester](../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../carbonyl-group.md); using a beta-propyl acrylate would move the substituent to the wrong ring position.

Disconnect $\mathrm{PhCOCH_2iPr}$ further to the indicated **[acetophenone](../../../../acetophenone.md)**. Crossed aldol addition to acetone followed by dehydration gives $\mathrm{PhCOCH=CMe_2}$; selective conjugate reduction or controlled alkene [hydrogenation](../../../../hydrogenation.md) then gives $\mathrm{PhCOCH_2CHMe_2}$. This avoids requiring an efficient SN2 alkylation with a secondary isopropyl halide. The alpha-propyl acrylate can be obtained by alpha-hydroxymethylation/dehydration of [ethyl pentanoate](../../../../ethyl-pentanoate.md), $\mathrm{EtO_2CCH_2nPr}$, using formaldehyde.

In the forward direction, perform the Michael coupling, reduce the phenyl [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) selectively with [sodium borohydride](../../../../sodium-borohydride.md) while retaining the ordinary [ester](../../../../ester.md), hydrolyse the [ester](../../../../ester.md), and cyclize the delta-hydroxy acid by controlled acid-catalysed [lactonization](../../../../lactonization.md) or an activated-acid coupling. The six-membered ring then has the order **[carbonyl](../../../../carbonyl-group.md)–$\mathrm{CH(nPr)}$–$\mathrm{CH_2}$–$\mathrm{CH(iPr)}$–$\mathrm{CH(Ph)}$–O**. This atom order checks all three substituent positions. No relative configuration is specified, so the scheme does not imply a single [diastereomer](../../../../diastereomer.md) from the [Michael addition](../../../../michael-addition.md) or reduction.
