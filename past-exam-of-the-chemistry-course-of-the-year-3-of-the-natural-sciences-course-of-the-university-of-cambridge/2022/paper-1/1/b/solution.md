↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**C, the neutral [arachno](../../../../../arachno-cluster.md) $\mathrm{B_6H_{12}}$, is the most plausible [proton](../../../../../proton.md) donor among A, B and C.** Loss of an open-framework bridging [proton](../../../../../proton.md) produces the experimentally accessible, charge-delocalized conjugate base $[\mathrm{B_6H_{11}}]^-$. In contrast, deprotonating A or B would build a dianion and incurs additional electrostatic cost. The neutral, open hydrogen-rich cluster therefore has a more favourable route to a monoanionic conjugate base. This is a qualitative acidity prediction for the listed species, not a numerical solvent-independent $pK_a$ or a claim that all B–H hydrogens are equally acidic.
