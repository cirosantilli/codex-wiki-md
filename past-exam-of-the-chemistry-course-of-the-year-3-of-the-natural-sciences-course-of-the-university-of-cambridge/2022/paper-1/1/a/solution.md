<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**$\mathrm{A=K[B_5H_8]}$ is [nido](../../../../../nido-cluster.md); $\mathrm{B=K[B_6H_{11}]}$ and $\mathrm{C=B_6H_{12}}$ are [arachno](../../../../../arachno-cluster.md).** The initial [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) removes an acidic [bridging hydrogen](../../../../../bridging-hydrogen.md) as a [proton](../../../../../proton.md), releasing $\mathrm{H_2}$ and retaining the [potassium](../../../../../potassium.md) counterion. Addition of one $\mathrm{BH_3}$ unit expands the [boron](../../../../../boron.md) framework; final protonation supplies the neutral hexaborane and KCl. These assignments conserve all the atoms in the displayed sequence, without inserting an unshown dehydrogenation step.

For $\mathrm{B_nH_m^{q-}}$, take one terminal B–H bond per vertex and remove its $2n$ electrons from the valence total. The [skeletal electron count](../../../../../skeletal-electron-count.md) is $3n+m+q-2n=n+m+q$. Thus

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{cluster}&n&\text{skeletal electrons}&\text{skeletal pairs}\\\hline
[\mathrm{B_5H_8}]^-&5&5+8+1=14&7=n+2\\
[\mathrm{B_6H_{11}}]^-&6&6+11+1=18&9=n+3\\
\mathrm{B_6H_{12}}&6&6+12=18&9=n+3
\end{array}
$$

[Wade's rules](../../../../../polyhedral-skeletal-electron-pair-theory.md) assign $n+1$, $n+2$ and $n+3$ pairs to [closo](../../../../../closo-cluster.md), [nido](../../../../../nido-cluster.md) and [arachno](../../../../../arachno-cluster.md) frameworks respectively. A derives from a six-vertex [closo](../../../../../closo-cluster.md) octahedron by removal of one vertex, giving a square-pyramidal [boron](../../../../../boron.md) skeleton. B and C have six-vertex open frameworks formally derived from an eight-vertex [closo](../../../../../closo-cluster.md) polyhedron by removing two vertices. Their additional hydrogens are distributed over bridging and other open-face positions; counting every [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) as an independent terminal B–H vertex would give the wrong classification.

The [arachno](../../../../../arachno-cluster.md) assignment of $[\mathrm{B_6H_{11}}]^-$ has specific theoretical and NMR support. It should not be replaced by a permanently localized, unrearranged $[\mathrm{B_5H_8}]^-\!\cdot\!\mathrm{BH_3}$ picture: the corresponding symmetric adduct participates in fluxional rearrangement rather than being the lowest-energy structure.[2000, abstract: arachno minimum, bridging-hydrogen removal from B6H12 and alternative-adduct comparison](../../../../../the-structure-of-arachno-b6h11-at-25-degrees-c-in-cd3-2o-is-resolved-via-the-ab-initio-iglo-giao-nmr-procedure-by-matthias-hofmann-and-others.md)
