<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $[\mathrm{B_{12}H_{12}}]^{2-}$, twelve BH vertices supply $12(2)$ skeletal electrons and the charge adds two:

$$
N_{\rm skel}=26=2(12+1).
$$

For the [cobaltacarborane](../../../../../cobaltacarborane.md), nine BH vertices supply 18, two CH vertices supply six and CpCo supplies two by (a):

$$
N_{\rm skel}=9(2)+2(3)+2=26.
$$

**Both are 12-vertex [closo](../../../../../closo-cluster.md) icosahedra with 13 [skeletal electron pairs](../../../../../skeletal-electron-pair.md).** In the [cobalt](../../../../../cobalt.md) compound, the Cp ring is external to the cage. [cobalt](../../../../../cobalt.md) caps the five-membered open face of the 11-vertex dicarbollide fragment and is a vertex of the resulting icosahedron, giving the usual adjacent-carbon [cobaltacarborane](../../../../../cobaltacarborane.md) framework. Counting all five Cp carbons as additional polyhedral vertices would be incorrect.
