<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

React pentaborane with an alkyne under relatively mild conditions to obtain a **[nido](../../../../../nido-cluster.md) $\mathrm{C_2B_4H_8}$ framework**, conventionally nido-2,3-dicarbahexaborane(8) for acetylene. The two entering [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) atoms initially retain their adjacency. A specific mild example uses 2-butyne with triethylamine-assisted $\mathrm{B_5H_9}$ at or below room [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) to give nido-$\mathrm{Me_2C_2B_4H_6}$. This is a practical route to an explicitly [nido carborane](../../../../../nido-carborane.md), rather than an assumption that an alkyne supplies a [closo](../../../../../closo-cluster.md) cage under every condition.[1979, abstract and preparation discussion: amine-assisted substituted-alkyne reaction under mild conditions](../../../../../improved-synthetic-routes-to-small-carboranes-bench-scale-preparation-of-nido-2-3-c2b4h8-derivatives-from-b5h9-and-from-b3h8-salts-by-narayan-s-hosmane-and-russell-n-grimes.md)

More energetic gas-phase reaction or pyrolysis promotes [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) loss, boron-fragment loss and cage rearrangement, giving **[closo](../../../../../closo-cluster.md) [carboranes](../../../../../carborane.md)**, for example $1,5$-$\mathrm{C_2B_3H_5}$ and $1,6$-$\mathrm{C_2B_4H_6}$, with other cage sizes possible in the mixture. Thermolysis of a previously formed [nido carborane](../../../../../nido-carborane.md) is an alternative route to this stage. The point is not a clean same-composition nido-to-closo [isomerization](../../../../../isomerization.md): the change of [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) content and sometimes cage size must be retained in the formulas.

Milder conditions favour kinetically trapped adjacent-carbon products; higher temperatures permit migration to carbon-separated arrangements, often increasing favourable B–C contacts relative to adjacent C–C/B–B contacts. Conditions and Lewis bases also influence which separated-carbon cage is isolated; [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) alone does not prescribe a single quantitative isomer distribution. Primary thermolysis studies and the small-carborane literature document both cage rearrangement and reagent-dependent product mixtures.[1963, printed p. 3378, introduction and product summary](../../../../../synthesis-of-carboranes-from-dihydrocarboranes-by-thomas-p-onak-and-others.md)
