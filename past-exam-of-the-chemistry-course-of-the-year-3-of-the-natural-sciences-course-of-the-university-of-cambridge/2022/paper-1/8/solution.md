<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Disconnect the tertiary [amide](../../../../amide.md) into **cyclohex-3-ene-1-carboxylic acid** and the secondary amine $\mathrm{EtNHCH(Me)CH_2CH_2Ph}$. The acid-derived ring is an immediate Diels–Alder disconnection: [buta-1,3-diene](../../../../buta-1-3-diene.md) plus the indicated **[methyl acrylate](../../../../methyl-acrylate.md)** gives methyl cyclohex-3-ene-1-carboxylate. Heating, or suitable Lewis-acid activation of the [dienophile](../../../../dienophile.md), forms the two new [sigma bonds](../../../../sigma-bond.md) and leaves the [diene](../../../../diene.md)'s internal double bond as the ring alkene.

Hydrolyse this [ester](../../../../ester.md) to the acid. Convert it to its acid [chloride](../../../../chloride.md) with oxalyl [chloride](../../../../chloride.md) or thionyl [chloride](../../../../chloride.md), and react with the secondary amine in the presence of a base to capture HCl. The tertiary [amide](../../../../amide.md) now contains the complete [cyclohexene](../../../../cyclohexene.md) acyl fragment; direct conjugate addition of the amine to [methyl acrylate](../../../../methyl-acrylate.md) before ring construction would give the wrong connectivity.

Prepare the amine by [reductive amination](../../../../reductive-amination.md) of **4-phenylbutan-2-one, $\mathrm{MeCOCH_2CH_2Ph}$**, with ethylamine, using [sodium triacetoxyborohydride](../../../../sodium-triacetoxyborohydride.md) or another iminium-selective reducing system. This places [nitrogen](../../../../nitrogen.md) on $\mathrm{CH(Me)}$, not on a terminal methylene. If a route from simpler [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) compounds is wanted, crossed [aldol condensation](../../../../aldol-condensation.md) of acetone with [benzaldehyde](../../../../benzaldehyde.md) gives $\mathrm{MeCOCH=CHPh}$, and selective C=C reduction gives the required [ketone](../../../../ketone.md) before [reductive amination](../../../../reductive-amination.md).

The acid branch retains the acrylic [carbonyl](../../../../carbonyl-group.md) as the [amide](../../../../amide.md) [carbonyl](../../../../carbonyl-group.md); the amine branch supplies both N-ethyl and N-(4-phenylbutan-2-yl) substituents. The unlabelled stereogenic centres may be [racemic mixture](../../../../racemic-mixture.md). This sequence separates ring formation from [amide](../../../../amide.md) formation and avoids invoking uncontrolled alkylation of a tertiary [amide](../../../../amide.md) [nitrogen](../../../../nitrogen.md).
