<h1 id="11/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Both side chains have the order **ring–$\mathrm{CH_2CH=CHMe}$**, and both printed double bonds have the Z geometry. The two [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) side chains are cis to one another; equivalently the labelled ring H and ring Me are cis on the opposite face. A useful common late precursor therefore has two cis **ring–$\mathrm{CH_2CHO}$** substituents, which can undergo two Z-selective ethylidene Wittig reactions.

Use the starting 2-methylcyclohex-2-en-1-one in a **reductive alpha-alkylation**. Conjugate reduction under dissolving-metal/enolate-trapping conditions, followed by a primary [electrophile](../../../../../electrophile.md) $\mathrm{BrCH_2CH_2OTBS}$, installs a protected two-carbon [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) side chain at the original methyl-bearing alpha [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). This gives 2-methyl-2-(2-siloxyethyl)cyclohexanone. The [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) at that position is now quaternary, with its original methyl retained. Alternatively, reduce the enone to 2-methylcyclohexanone and deliberately form/select its more substituted [enolate](../../../../../enolate.md) before alkylation; its opposite [kinetic enolate](../../../../../kinetic-enolate.md) would give the wrong regioisomer.

Convert the remaining ring [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) to an exocyclic enoate by Horner–Wadsworth–Emmons [olefination](../../../../../olefination.md) with a [phosphonoacetate](../../../../../phosphonoacetate.md):

$$
\mathrm{ring\ C=O\longrightarrow ring\ C=CHCO_2Et}.
$$

Hydrogenate this sole alkene to give ring–$\mathrm{CH_2CO_2Et}$ at that [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). Select the [diastereomer](../../../../../diastereomer.md) with this substituent cis to the pre-existing protected hydroxyethyl side chain, so the newly installed ring H is cis to ring Me. Less hindered-face [hydrogenation](../../../../../hydrogenation.md) can assist this relationship, but the route should include separation/verification of the desired cis product rather than assert complete stereoselectivity without data.

Reduce the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) with [lithium aluminium hydride](../../../../../lithium-aluminium-hydride.md) to ring–$\mathrm{CH_2CH_2OH}$ and remove the TBS group from the other side chain. Both appendages are now two-carbon primary alcohols. Oxidize them to aldehydes under non-overoxidizing conditions, for example Dess–Martin oxidation, obtaining the cis dialdehyde with two ring–$\mathrm{CH_2CHO}$ groups. Treat with excess salt-controlled, nonstabilized ethylidenetriphenylphosphorane under conditions favouring kinetic Z [olefination](../../../../../olefination.md); isolate the **Z,Z product**. Each aldehyde [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) becomes the inner vinylic [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) of its crotyl group, while the [ylide](../../../../../ylide.md) supplies $\mathrm{CHMe}$.

This order postpones both stereodefined alkenes until after [hydrogenation](../../../../../hydrogenation.md), so they are not accidentally saturated. It also avoids assuming that a crotyl [grignard reagent](../../../../../grignard-reagent.md) adds without allylic transposition or that direct [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) deoxygenation fixes the required ring stereochemistry. An achiral synthesis gives a [racemic mixture](../../../../../racemic-mixture.md) pair with the specified relative configuration.
