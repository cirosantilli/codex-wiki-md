<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [alpha decay](../../../../../alpha-decay.md) decreases [mass number](../../../../../mass-number.md) by four and [atomic number](../../../../../atomic-number.md) by two; a [beta-minus decay](../../../../../beta-minus-decay.md) keeps the [mass number](../../../../../mass-number.md) fixed and increases [atomic number](../../../../../atomic-number.md) by one. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{A}={}^{239}_{93}\mathrm{Np},\qquad\mathrm{B}={}^{239}_{94}\mathrm{Pu},\qquad\mathrm{C}={}^{235}_{92}\mathrm U.}
$$

The beta-minus step converts a neutron to a [proton](../../../../../proton.md) with emission of an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and an [electron antineutrino](../../../../../electron-antineutrino.md). It is not capture of an orbital [electron](../../../../../electron.md), which would change the [atomic number](../../../../../atomic-number.md) in the opposite direction.
