<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Radiation changes the solution's chemistry by [radiolysis](../../../../../radiolysis.md); it does not turn Am(VI) nuclei into Am(V) nuclei.** An emitted alpha particle deposits energy into many water and solute molecules, generating ionized/excited species, hydrated electrons, [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) atoms and molecular products including [hydrogen peroxide](../../../../../hydrogen-peroxide.md). Reducing products can supply the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) needed for

$$
\mathrm{AmO_2^{2+}+e^-\longrightarrow AmO_2^+}.
$$

For example, [hydrogen peroxide](../../../../../hydrogen-peroxide.md) can be oxidized while reducing two americyl(VI) ions:

$$
\mathrm{2AmO_2^{2+}+H_2O_2\longrightarrow2AmO_2^++O_2+2H^+}.
$$

The $0.3$ mM loss of Am(VI) equals the $0.3$ mM appearance of Am(V), preserving the measured dissolved [americium](../../../../../americium.md) total while transferring $0.3\,\mathrm{mmol\,L^{-1}}$ of [electron](../../../../../electron.md) equivalents. Oxidizing [radiolysis](../../../../../radiolysis.md) products are also present, and the net reduction depends on acid, dissolved gases and scavengers; not every radiolytic event is reducing. In nitric acid, primary kinetic [work](../../../../../work.md) identifies [hydrogen peroxide](../../../../../hydrogen-peroxide.md) and radiolytically produced [nitrous acid](../../../../../nitrous-acid.md) as important reductants, with a rate controlled by total [americium](../../../../../americium.md) activity rather than necessarily by the instantaneous Am(VI) [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md).[2017, abstract: oxidation-state mass balance, UV–visible measurements and radiolytic reducing agents](../../../../../kinetics-of-the-autoreduction-of-hexavalent-americium-in-aqueous-nitric-acid-by-travis-s-grimes-and-others.md)
