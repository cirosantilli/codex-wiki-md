↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [character of a localized orbital basis](../../../../../../character-of-a-localized-orbital-basis.md), take the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of the transformation [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md): both permutation of centres and signs of [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) matter. The two metal centres lie on the principal axis and remain fixed under rotation about $z$. At either metal the $(p_x,p_y)$ [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) has the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
R(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The full four-dimensional [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) is block diagonal with two such blocks. Consequently $\chi(R)=4\cos\theta$, and for the $60^\circ$ rotation of the [D6h molecular point group](../../../../../../d6h-molecular-point-group.md), **$\chi(C_6)=4\cos(\pi/3)=2$**.
