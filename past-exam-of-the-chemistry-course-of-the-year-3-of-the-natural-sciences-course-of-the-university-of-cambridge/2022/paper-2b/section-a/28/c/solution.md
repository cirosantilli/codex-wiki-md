<h1 id="section-a/28/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose transverse directions parallel and perpendicular to the vertical mirror plane. At each metal the parallel [p orbital](../../../../../../p-orbital.md) is unchanged, whereas the perpendicular [p orbital](../../../../../../p-orbital.md) changes sign. Thus each two-dimensional block has [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) $1-1=0$. The total [character of a representation](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is **$\chi(\sigma_d)=0$**. A mirror plane passing through an orbital centre does not imply that every orbital on that centre contributes $+1$.
