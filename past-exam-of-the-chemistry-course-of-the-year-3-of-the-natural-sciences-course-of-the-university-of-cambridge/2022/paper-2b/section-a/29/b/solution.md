↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [C2v molecular point group](../../../../../../c2v-molecular-point-group.md) has four one-dimensional [irreducible representations](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md). In the operation order used above, the [character table](../../../../../../character-table.md) rows are $A_1=(1,1,1,1)$, $A_2=(1,1,-1,-1)$, $B_1=(1,-1,1,-1)$ and $B_2=(1,-1,-1,1)$. Their [character inner products](../../../../../../character-inner-product.md) with $\chi_\Gamma=(6,0,2,0)$ give

$$
m_{A_1}=\frac{6+2}{4}=2,\quad m_{A_2}=\frac{6-2}{4}=1,\quad m_{B_1}=2,\quad m_{B_2}=1.
$$

Hence **$\Gamma=2A_1\oplus A_2\oplus2B_1\oplus B_2$**, whose dimensions sum to six. The labels $B_1,B_2$ refer to the source axes; swapping $x$ and $y$ swaps these names.
