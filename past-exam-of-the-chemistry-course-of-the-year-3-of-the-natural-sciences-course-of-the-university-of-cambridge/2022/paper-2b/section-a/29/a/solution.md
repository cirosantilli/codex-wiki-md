<h1 id="section-a/29/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the axes actually printed: the two hydrogens lie in the $xz$ plane, $z$ is the principal twofold axis, and $y$ is perpendicular to that plane. For a Cartesian-displacement [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md), only atoms left at their own sites contribute to the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md); their vector components must also be transformed. In the order $(E,C_2(z),\sigma(xz),\sigma(yz))$, identity fixes six components, the twofold rotation exchanges the hydrogens, the $xz$ reflection fixes both hydrogens with vector [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1)$, and the $yz$ reflection exchanges them. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\chi_\Gamma=(6,0,2,0).}
$$

The nonzero mirror [character of a representation](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is $2(1-1+1)=2$, not six.
