<h1 id="section-a/27/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Within a specified [electron configuration](../../../../../../electron-configuration.md) in the [LS coupling](../../../../../../ls-coupling.md) regime, [Hund's rules](../../../../../../hund-s-rules.md) select the lowest [atomic term](../../../../../../atomic-term.md) as follows. First maximize the total [electron spin](../../../../../../electron-spin.md) $S$ subject to the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). Among the terms having that $S$, choose the largest total orbital [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $L$. Finally, [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) places the smallest allowed $J=|L-S|$ lowest for a subshell less than half full, and the largest $J=L+S$ lowest for a subshell more than half full. At half filling the maximum-spin term has $L=0$, hence $J=S$. These rules rank terms within configurations; the exceptional neutral-atom configurations of [chromium](../../../../../../chromium.md) and [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) must also be used.
