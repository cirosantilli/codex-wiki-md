<h1 id="section-a/31/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [31](../31.md)

Two contributions must be separated: the underlying contraction of the metal ion across the series and the configuration-dependent [crystal field stabilization energy](../../../../../crystal-field-stabilization-energy.md). Increasing [effective nuclear charge](../../../../../effective-nuclear-charge.md) across the relevant divalent ions generally contracts the metal orbitals and favours shorter metal–chlorine bonds. Superimposed on this, a weak-field tetrahedral chloride environment places the $e$ set below the $t_2$ set, at $-0.6\Delta_t$ and $+0.4\Delta_t$ relative to the barycentre. The high-spin occupancies and corresponding [crystal field stabilization energies](../../../../../crystal-field-stabilization-energy.md) are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|r}
d\text{ count}&\text{occupancy}&\mathrm{CFSE}/\Delta_t\\\hline
6&e^3t_2^3&-0.6\\
7&e^4t_2^3&-1.2\\
8&e^4t_2^4&-0.8\\
10&e^4t_2^6&0
\end{array}
$$

Because the [tetrahedral crystal field splitting](../../../../../tetrahedral-crystal-field-splitting.md) increases on bringing the ligands inward, the stabilization is especially effective at shortening the $d^7$ bond. Adding the next [electron](../../../../../electron.md) to the upper $t_2$ set reduces that stabilization; in a simple sigma-bonding description this set also has more metal–ligand antibonding character. That change explains the upward deviation from $d^7$ to $d^8$ despite the general contraction trend. By $d^{10}$ the [crystal field stabilization energy](../../../../../crystal-field-stabilization-energy.md) is zero, but the background contraction can still produce the shortest bond in the plotted set. Thus **the $d^7$ shortening and $d^8$ rebound reflect orbital filling, while the overall decrease reflects contraction**. A zero [crystal field stabilization energy](../../../../../crystal-field-stabilization-energy.md) does not mean no chemical bonding. Absolute distances also involve [covalent bonds](../../../../../covalent-bond-split.md), repulsion and ligand interactions; this qualitative model does not determine them numerically. No inference about the missing $d^9$ distance is required.
