<h1 id="section-a/25/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Quantum tunnelling](../../../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) is penetration of, or transmission through, a finite [potential barrier](../../../../../../potential-barrier.md) into a [classically forbidden region](../../../../../../classically-forbidden-region.md), where the total [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is less than the [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md). The [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) generally remains nonzero there, so the [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) assigns a nonzero [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of detection. This does not require an increase in total [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md); it is the classical relation between a definite position and a definite [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) that cannot be imposed on the [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md). For a bound [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), the relevant quantity is the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of finding the particle beyond its [classical turning points](../../../../../../classical-turning-point.md); it is not an escaping transmitted beam.
