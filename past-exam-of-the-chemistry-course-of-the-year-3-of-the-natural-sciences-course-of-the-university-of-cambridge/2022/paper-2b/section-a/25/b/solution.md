<h1 id="section-a/25/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the reduced [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), $V(q)=q^2/2$ and the ground-state [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is $E_0=1/2$. Thus the [classical turning points](../../../../../../classical-turning-point.md) satisfy $q^2/2=1/2$, giving $q=\pm1$. Normalizing the [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) gives $\phi_0(q)=\pi^{-1/4}e^{-q^2/2}$, because $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-q^2}\,dq=\sqrt\pi$. The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) therefore gives the [harmonic-oscillator forbidden-region probability](../../../../../../harmonic-oscillator-forbidden-region-probability.md)

$$
P(|q|>1)=\frac{2}{\sqrt\pi}\int_1^\infty e^{-q^2}\,dq
\simeq\frac{2(0.1394)}{\sqrt\pi}=\boxed{0.1573\simeq15.7\%}.
$$

The factor two includes both tails. Using the exact [complementary error function](../../../../../../complementary-error-function.md) gives $\operatorname{erfc}(1)=0.1572992$. A stationary real [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) has zero [probability current](../../../../../../probability-current.md), so this result describes forbidden-region occupancy, not continuous loss of the bound particle.
