<h1 id="section-a/26/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Inside the [infinite square well](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md), the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is purely the [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) operator,

$$
\boxed{\hat H=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2}{dx^2},\qquad 0<x<L.}
$$

Its admissible [wavefunctions](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) obey the [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) $\psi(0)=\psi(L)=0$ and vanish outside the interval. Equivalently write $\hat H=\hat p^2/(2m)+V(x)$ with $V=0$ inside and an infinite confining wall outside. The [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) are part of the specification of this [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md); the differential expression alone does not determine the spectrum.
