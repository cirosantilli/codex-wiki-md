<h1 id="section-a/26/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Twice differentiating gives $d^2\sin(kx)/dx^2=-k^2\sin(kx)$, so inside the [infinite square well](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md)

$$
\hat H\sin(kx)=\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m}\sin(kx).
$$

The [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) at zero is automatic. The other requires $\sin(kL)=0$, hence $kL=n\pi$. A nonzero [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) can therefore be labelled by

$$
\boxed{k_n=\frac{n\pi}{L},\qquad E_n=\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2mL^2},\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.}
$$

If desired, its normalized form is $\sqrt{2/L}\sin(n\pi x/L)$. The positive lowest [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) follows from confinement and the required spatial variation of a nonzero [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md).
