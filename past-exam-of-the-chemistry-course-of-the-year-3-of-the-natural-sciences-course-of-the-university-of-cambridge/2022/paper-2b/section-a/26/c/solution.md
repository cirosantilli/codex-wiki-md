<h1 id="section-a/26/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $n=0$ the proposed [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) vanishes identically and cannot be normalized. A negative integer gives $\sin(-|n|\pi x/L)=-\sin(|n|\pi x/L)$, which differs from the corresponding positive-integer [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) only by a physically irrelevant [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md). Both have the same positive [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md), proportional to $n^2$. Thus **zero is inadmissible and negative labels are redundant**. A negative integer is not forbidden by a separate negative-energy argument.
