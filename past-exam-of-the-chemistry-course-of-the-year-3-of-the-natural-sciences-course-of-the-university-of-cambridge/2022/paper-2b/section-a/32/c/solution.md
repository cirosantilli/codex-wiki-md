<h1 id="section-a/32/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For [hexa-1,5-diyne](../../../../../../hexa-1-5-diyne.md) in the extended anti conformation drawn, inversion at the midpoint of the central [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)–[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bond exchanges the two identical ends. A twofold axis through that midpoint perpendicular to the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) skeleton plane, together with the mirror plane of that skeleton, completes the [C2h molecular point group](../../../../../../c2h-molecular-point-group.md). The methylene hydrogens occur in the corresponding mirror-related pairs. Thus the answer is **$C_{2h}$**. The whole molecule is not linear simply because its terminal triple-bond fragments are linear; the conformation is essential to this assignment.
