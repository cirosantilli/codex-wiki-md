↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The planar [three-radialene](../../../../../../three-radialene.md) structure has a threefold axis normal to the plane, three in-plane twofold axes and the molecular plane as a horizontal mirror. Therefore its [molecular point group](../../../../../../molecular-point-group.md) is **$D_{3h}$**, the [D3h molecular point group](../../../../../../d3h-molecular-point-group.md). Its threefold connectivity does not supply a sixfold axis.
