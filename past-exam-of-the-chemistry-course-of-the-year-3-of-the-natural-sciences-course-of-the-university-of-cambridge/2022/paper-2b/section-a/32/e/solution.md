↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The folded [benzvalene](../../../../../../benzvalene.md) cage has one twofold axis and two mirror planes containing it. The double-bonded edge distinguishes it from cages having higher rotational [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md); it has neither an [inversion centre](../../../../../../inversion-centre.md) nor a horizontal mirror perpendicular to the twofold axis. Its [molecular point group](../../../../../../molecular-point-group.md) is **$C_{2v}$**, the [C2v molecular point group](../../../../../../c2v-molecular-point-group.md).
