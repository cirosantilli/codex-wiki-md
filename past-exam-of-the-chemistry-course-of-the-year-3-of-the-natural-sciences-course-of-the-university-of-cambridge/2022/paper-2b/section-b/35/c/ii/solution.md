<h1 id="section-b/35/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Express the [rotational constant](../../../../../../../rotational-constant.md) as a [spectroscopic wavenumber](../../../../../../../spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) $\widetilde B=h/(8\pi^2cI)$. The rotational absorption [selection rule](../../../../../../../selection-rule.md) $\Delta J=+1$ gives

$$
\widetilde\nu_{J\to J+1}=2\widetilde B(J+1).
$$

Successive lines therefore have spacing $2\widetilde B$, regardless of the starting $J$. Both observed spacings are $20.88\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, giving **$\widetilde B=10.44\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$**. The three lines are consequently the $5\to6$, $6\to7$ and $7\to8$ transitions within the stated precision; the first observed line is not the $0\to1$ line.

Using the given isotopic masses, the [reduced mass](../../../../../../../reduced-mass.md) is

$$
\mu=\frac{1.0078(34.969)}{1.0078+34.969}\,m_u
=1.62661\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg}.
$$

With $\widetilde B=1044\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and $c$ in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, the [bond length](../../../../../../../bond-length.md) follows from

$$
\boxed{r=\sqrt{\frac{h}{8\pi^2c\mu\widetilde B}}
=1.28390\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm m\simeq1.284\ \text{Å}.}
$$

The small common $0.01\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ offset relative to integer multiples of the spacing is immaterial at the precision of this model. A real ground-vibrational-state [rotational constant](../../../../../../../rotational-constant.md) determines an effective vibrationally averaged [bond length](../../../../../../../bond-length.md); it need not equal the exact equilibrium separation.
