<h1 id="section-b/35/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [linear rigid rotor](../../../../../../../linear-rigid-rotor.md), dividing the [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) squared [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) by twice the [moment of inertia](../../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) gives

$$
\boxed{E_J=\frac{\hbar^2}{2I}J(J+1),\qquad g_J=2J+1.}
$$

The [quantum degeneracy](../../../../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md) counts the values $M=-J,\ldots,J$ with the same [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md). It excludes nuclear-spin multiplicity and any fine or hyperfine splitting absent from this model.
