<h1 id="section-b/35/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalization of a [wavefunction](../../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) on the unit sphere uses solid angle $d\Omega=\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi$, not the flat measure $d\theta\,d\phi$. Since $|e^{-i\phi}|=1$,

$$
\int|\psi|^2d\Omega=\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi\int_0^\pi\sin^3\theta\,d\theta
=2\pi\int_{-1}^{1}(1-u^2)\,du=\frac{8\pi}{3},
$$

where $u=\cos\theta$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Psi(\theta,\phi)=\sqrt{\frac3{8\pi}}\sin\theta\,e^{-i\phi}.}
$$

With the usual phase convention this is the [spherical harmonic](../../../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md) $Y_{1,-1}$. An arbitrary global unit-modulus factor would not change normalization or any observable.
