<h1 id="section-b/33/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Exact solutions of the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) are unavailable for most interacting or realistic molecular systems. The [quantum variational principle](../../../../../../../quantum-variational-principle.md) turns the search for a ground-state [wavefunction](../../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) into optimization of a computable [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) quotient in a tractable trial family. The result is an upper bound: enlarging a nested trial space can only lower or preserve the optimized [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md), providing a systematic convergence check. Trial functions can incorporate [boundary conditions](../../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) and known [symmetry](../../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) before calculation. Near an exact normalized ground state, a small orthogonal [wavefunction](../../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) error changes the [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) only to second order, which explains why useful energies can result from imperfect trial functions. This does not ensure that every other observable is equally accurate, nor that a restricted trial family captures correlation adequately. Excited states require [orthogonality](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) or appropriate [symmetry](../../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) restrictions; unrestricted minimization seeks the ground state, as the preceding calculation demonstrates.
