<h1 id="section-b/33/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a self-adjoint [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) bounded below, every nonzero admissible trial [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) satisfies the [quantum variational principle](../../../../../../quantum-variational-principle.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E[\psi]=\frac{\langle\psi|\hat H|\psi\rangle}{\langle\psi|\psi\rangle}\ge E_0.}
$$

Here $E_0$ is the lowest exact [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md), and the trial function must obey the physical [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) and have finite relevant integrals. Equality holds for a [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) in the ground-state eigenspace. The quotient is unchanged by normalization or any nonzero overall scalar.
