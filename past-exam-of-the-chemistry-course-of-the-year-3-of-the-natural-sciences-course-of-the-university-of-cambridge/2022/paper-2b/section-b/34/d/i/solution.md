<h1 id="section-b/34/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The strong band is the **$v=0\to1$ vibrational fundamental**, and the weaker band near twice its [wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) is the **$v=0\to2$ first vibrational overtone**. At room temperature these high-frequency oscillators have overwhelmingly ground-state vibrational populations. In the harmonic-potential, linear-dipole limit the infrared [selection rule](../../../../../../../selection-rule.md) is $\Delta v=\pm1$; [anharmonicity](../../../../../../../anharmonicity.md) and nonlinear dipole dependence make the overtone weakly allowed. A $1\to2$ [hot band](../../../../../../../hot-band.md) would lie near, and slightly below, the fundamental [wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md), rather than near twice it.
