<h1 id="section-b/34/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The molecule with the larger inferred dissociation [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) is assigned the stronger [hydrogen](../../../../../../../hydrogen.md)–halogen bond: **$\mathrm A=\mathrm{HBr}$ and $\mathrm B=\mathrm{HI}$**. Moving from [bromine](../../../../../../../bromine.md) to the larger [iodine](../../../../../../../iodine.md) atom generally lengthens the bond and reduces effective overlap with the compact [hydrogen](../../../../../../../hydrogen.md) $1s$ orbital, so [hydrogen iodide](../../../../../../../hydrogen-iodide.md) is more weakly bound. Independently, the tabulated ground-state harmonic constants near $2649\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ for [hydrogen bromide](../../../../../../../hydrogen-bromide.md) and $2309\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ for [hydrogen iodide](../../../../../../../hydrogen-iodide.md) agree with the inferred assignment.[Ground X 1Sigma+ row, harmonic [wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) 2648.975 cm−1](../../../../../../../hydrogen-bromide-constants-of-diatomic-molecules-by-klaus-p-huber-and-others.md)[Ground X 1Sigma+ row, harmonic [wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) 2309.014 cm−1](../../../../../../../hydrogen-iodide-constants-of-diatomic-molecules-by-klaus-p-huber-and-others.md) The ordering is the identification criterion; the low-level [Morse oscillator](../../../../../../../morse-oscillator.md) extrapolations above should not be presented as measured dissociation energies.
