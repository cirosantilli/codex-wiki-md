<h1 id="section-b/34/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $s=v+1/2$ and complete the square in the [Morse oscillator](../../../../../../morse-oscillator.md) [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md):

$$
E(s)=hc\widetilde\omega(s-x_es^2)
=\frac{hc\widetilde\omega}{4x_e}
-hc\widetilde\omega x_e\left(s-\frac1{2x_e}\right)^2.
$$

The finite bound-level sequence approaches the dissociation limit at the vertex of this extrapolated parabola. Thus the [bond potential-well depth](../../../../../../bond-potential-well-depth.md) is

$$
\boxed{D_e=\frac{hc\widetilde\omega}{4x_e}.}
$$

The continuous vertex is a convenient way to identify the limit; it is not an additional normalizable bound level at the threshold.
