<h1 id="section-b/34/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $u=r-r_e$ measure displacement from the equilibrium [bond length](../../../../../../bond-length.md) and put the minimum of [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) at zero. The harmonic model and the [Morse potential](../../../../../../morse-potential.md) are

$$
V_{\rm harm}(r)=\frac12ku^2,\qquad
V_{\rm Morse}(r)=D_e(1-e^{-\alpha u})^2.
$$

The [harmonic approximation](../../../../../../harmonic-approximation.md) is a symmetric parabola and has no dissociation limit. The [Morse potential](../../../../../../morse-potential.md) has a steep compressed side and a shallower extended side, approaching $D_e$ as $r\to\infty$. Matching the curvatures at the minimum gives $k=2D_e\alpha^2$. With $\widetilde\omega$ denoting a [spectroscopic wavenumber](../../../../../../spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) rather than an angular frequency, the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) levels are

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_v^{\rm harm}&=hc\widetilde\omega(v+\tfrac12),\quad v=0,1,2,\ldots,\\
E_v^{\rm Morse}&=hc\widetilde\omega\left[(v+\tfrac12)-x_e(v+\tfrac12)^2\right],\quad v+\tfrac12<\frac1{2x_e}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $x_e$ is the dimensionless [spectroscopic anharmonicity constant](../../../../../../spectroscopic-anharmonicity-constant.md). The harmonic spacing is constant, while the [Morse oscillator](../../../../../../morse-oscillator.md) spacing is $E_{v+1}-E_v=hc\widetilde\omega[1-2x_e(v+1)]$ and decreases as the levels approach dissociation. Only finitely many [Morse oscillator](../../../../../../morse-oscillator.md) levels are bound. The exactly solvable potential was introduced to model this anharmonic behaviour.[Physical Review 34 (1929), pp. 57–64, potential and bound-level solution](../../../../../../diatomic-molecules-according-to-the-wave-mechanics-ii-vibrational-levels-by-philip-m-morse.md)

For the harmonic model put $q=\sqrt{\mu\omega/\hbar}\,u$, with $\omega=2\pi c\widetilde\omega$, and $N=\pi^{-1/4}$. The first three normalized reduced-coordinate [wavefunctions](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) are

$$
\phi_0=Ne^{-q^2/2},\qquad
\phi_1=\sqrt2\,Nq e^{-q^2/2},\qquad
\phi_2=\frac{N}{\sqrt2}(2q^2-1)e^{-q^2/2}.
$$

Their numbers of nodes are zero, one and two, and their parities are even, odd and even. The $v=2$ nodes are at $q=\pm1/\sqrt2$. The diagrams below use analytic curves and arbitrary illustrative scales, with both potentials given the same local curvature.
