<h1 id="section-b/34/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [ground-state dissociation energy](../../../../../../ground-state-dissociation-energy.md) starts at the lowest vibrational level, rather than the minimum of the potential. The [zero-point energy](../../../../../../zero-point-energy.md) of the [Morse oscillator](../../../../../../morse-oscillator.md) is

$$
E_0=hc\widetilde\omega\left(\frac12-\frac{x_e}{4}\right)=D_e(2x_e-x_e^2).
$$

Subtracting it from the [bond potential-well depth](../../../../../../bond-potential-well-depth.md) gives

$$
\boxed{D_0=D_e-E_0=D_e(1-2x_e+x_e^2)=D_e(1-x_e)^2.}
$$

This distinction matters whenever a dissociation [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is inferred from a vibrational spectrum.
