<h1 id="section-b/36/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $D=\sqrt{n^2-2n+5}=\sqrt{(n-1)^2+4}$. For $0<n<1$, $D>n+1$, since $D^2-(n+1)^2=4(1-n)>0$. Remembering $\beta<0$, the two lowest trapezium [molecular orbital](../../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) energies are

$$
\epsilon_1=\alpha+\frac\beta2(n+1+D)\quad(A_1),\qquad
\epsilon_2=\alpha+\frac\beta2(D-n-1)\quad(B_1).
$$

The remaining levels lie at $2\alpha-\epsilon_2$ and $2\alpha-\epsilon_1$. Filling the lowest spatial orbitals with opposite-spin pairs gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cc}
&\text{square}&\text{trapezium}\\\hline
2\ \text{electrons}&2\alpha+4\beta&2\alpha+\beta(n+1+D)\\
4\ \text{electrons}&4\alpha+4\beta&4\alpha+2\beta D
\end{array}}
$$

For the square four-electron case, two [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md) occupy the lowest orbital and two occupy the degenerate $E_u$ set. Their summed one-electron [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) is fixed here, but this [Hückel method](../../../../../../../huckel-method.md) calculation alone does not decide the spin or correlation within that open shell.
