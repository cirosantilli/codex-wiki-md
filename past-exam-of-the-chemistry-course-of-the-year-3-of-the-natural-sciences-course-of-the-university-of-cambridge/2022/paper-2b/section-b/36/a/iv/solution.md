The [molecular orbital](../../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) diagram has one level at $\alpha+2\beta$ labelled $A_{1g}$, two degenerate levels at $\alpha$ labelled $E_u$, and one level at $\alpha-2\beta$ labelled $B_{1g}$, in increasing [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) for $\beta<0$. Each spatial [molecular orbital](../../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) admits two opposite-spin [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md). The diagram below also shows how one representative distortion lifts the middle degeneracy; the distorted scale is illustrative, not a fitted molecular spectrum.
