<h1 id="section-b/36/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the trapezium's printed axes, with principal axis $z$ in its plane and $y$ perpendicular to the plane. For either site pair $(1,2)$ or $(3,4)$, the [group representation](../../../../../../../group-representation.md) has [characters of a representation](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $(2,0,2,0)$ in the order $(E,C_2(z),\sigma(xz),\sigma(yz))$: identity and the molecular-plane mirror fix both [s orbitals](../../../../../../../s-orbital.md), while the other two operations exchange them. The [character inner products](../../../../../../../character-inner-product.md) give **$\Gamma_{12}=\Gamma_{34}=A_1\oplus B_1$** in the [C2v molecular point group](../../../../../../../c2v-molecular-point-group.md).
