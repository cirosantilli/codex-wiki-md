<h1 id="section-b/36/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Within each pair the in-phase and out-of-phase combinations have $A_1$ and $B_1$ [symmetry](../../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) respectively. One normalized choice is

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
a&=(s_1+s_2)/\sqrt2,& b&=(s_3+s_4)/\sqrt2&& (A_1),\\
c&=(s_1-s_2)/\sqrt2,& d&=(s_4-s_3)/\sqrt2&& (B_1).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The sign convention for $d$ is chosen to make the off-diagonal interaction positive in the corresponding two-by-two block. It does not change the physical predictions. These are pair [symmetry-adapted linear combinations](../../../../../../../symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md); the actual [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) are [linear combinations](../../../../../../../linear-combination.md) within each of the two equal-symmetry pairs, found by diagonalizing the blocks.
