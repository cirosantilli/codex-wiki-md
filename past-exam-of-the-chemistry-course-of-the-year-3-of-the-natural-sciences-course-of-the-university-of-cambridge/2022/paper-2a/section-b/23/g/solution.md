<h1 id="section-b/23/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

In the [electronic state correlation diagram](../../../../../../electronic-state-correlation-diagram.md), the singly occupied reactant orbitals for triplet [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) have [symmetries](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) $B_1$ and $A_2$, so their spatial product is $B_2$. The reactant state is therefore a triplet ${}^3B_2$, whereas the closed-shell product is ${}^1A_1$. **A strictly spin-conserving path cannot correlate triplet reactants directly to the ground-state product.** It correlates to an excited triplet product configuration or requires [intersystem crossing](../../../../../../intersystem-crossing.md); the symmetric direct reaction consequently has a substantial electronic obstacle.

Simply changing the [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) spin does not repair the occupied carbon-orbital correlation. The lowest [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) singlet manifold has spatial components of $A_1$ and $B_2$ [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) in this subgroup. The $A_1$ singlet can have an avoided crossing with another $A_1$ configuration and reach the product singlet; the $B_2$ singlet cannot mix directly with the product ${}^1A_1$ while $C_{2v}$ is maintained. Following either closed-shell [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) singlet component in the orbital diagram still leaves the carbon-pi [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) pair in the rising $\sigma_+^*$ orbital. Its redistribution into the final occupied orbitals requires configuration mixing at an avoided crossing. **A barrier is therefore expected even for the symmetry-constrained concerted singlet addition; singlet does not imply barrierless.** The state curves in the original diagram are schematic, illustrating this avoided-crossing argument rather than computed energies.[1969, orbital and state correlation for concerted olefin additions](../../../../../../selection-rules-for-singlet-oxygen-reactions-concerted-addition-reactions-by-david-r-kearns.md)

The real singlet reaction can lower this obstacle through asymmetric or stepwise pathways that are absent from the stipulated direct approach. A primary multireference study explicitly distinguishes singlet and triplet addition through biradical intermediates, supporting that limitation of a one-coordinate [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) argument.[2010, abstract and calculated addition/isomerization surfaces](../../../../../../singlet-and-triplet-potential-surfaces-for-the-o2-plus-c2h4-reaction-by-kyoyeon-park-and-others.md) Neither correlation alone nor a spin label supplies a numerical [activation energy](../../../../../../activation-energy.md).
