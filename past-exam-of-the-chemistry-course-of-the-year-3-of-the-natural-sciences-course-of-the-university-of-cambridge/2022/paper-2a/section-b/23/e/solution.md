<h1 id="section-b/23/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The symmetric approach preserves the [C2v molecular point group](../../../../../../c2v-molecular-point-group.md) with its twofold axis z and mirror planes xz and yz. The molecular ring lies in xz. Classify orbitals by [reflection](../../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) signs $(\sigma_{xz},\sigma_{yz})$: $A_1=(+,+)$, $B_1=(+,-)$, $B_2=(-,+)$, $A_2=(-,-)$. With globally defined p-orbital phases the [molecular orbital correlation diagram](../../../../../../molecular-orbital-correlation-diagram.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{reactant orbital}&C_{2v}&\text{product orbital}\\ \hline
\pi_{O,z}&A_1&\sigma_+\\
\pi_C&A_1&\sigma_+^*\\
\pi_{O,z}^*&B_1&\sigma_-\\
\pi_C^*&B_1&\sigma_-^*\\
\pi_{O,y}&B_2&n_+\\
\pi_{O,y}^*&A_2&n_-
\end{array}}
$$

Within either two-orbital [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) block the lower member remains correlated with the lower product member; the [non-crossing rule](../../../../../../non-crossing-rule.md) prevents swapping their order through an ordinary same-symmetry crossing. Crossings between different [irreducible representations](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) are allowed. The crucial consequence is that the occupied [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) pi orbital correlates to a C–O-antibonding combination, rather than both initially occupied $A_1$ orbitals becoming the two product bonding orbitals.
