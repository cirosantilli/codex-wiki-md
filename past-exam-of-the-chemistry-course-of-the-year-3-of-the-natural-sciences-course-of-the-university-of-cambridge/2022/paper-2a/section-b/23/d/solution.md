<h1 id="section-b/23/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $\sigma_L,\sigma_R$ be localized bonding orbitals of the two new C–O bonds, and let $\sigma_L^*,\sigma_R^*$ be their antibonding partners. Their [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) combinations are

$$
\sigma_\pm=\frac{\sigma_L\pm\sigma_R}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\sigma_\pm^*=\frac{\sigma_L^*\pm\sigma_R^*}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The two remaining active orbitals are out-of-ring [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) lone-pair combinations, $n_\pm=(p_{y,L}\pm p_{y,R})/\sqrt2$. The opposite-phase combination $n_-$ is the HOMO in the supplied ordering and $n_+$ is HOMO−1. Both are filled, as are the two C–O bonding combinations, giving

$$
\boxed{(\sigma_+)^2(\sigma_-)^2(n_+)^2(n_-)^2(\sigma_+^*)^0(\sigma_-^*)^0.}
$$

The original active-orbital sketches show these phase relations, while the correlation diagram gives a qualitative energy ordering. Precise splittings between unrelated [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) blocks are not determined by the fragment [ionization energies](../../../../../../ionization-energy.md). The retained O–O [sigma bond](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md), the C–C [sigma bond](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md) and the other framework orbitals lie outside this six-orbital active comparison; the eight-electron count does not describe all valence [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) of the product.
