<h1 id="section-b/23/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The active set contains four [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) pi orbitals and two [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) pi orbitals, with **eight [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) in six orbitals**. The noninteracting levels, using the parameters inferred below, are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{fragment orbitals}&E\ /\mathrm{eV}&\text{occupation}\\ \hline
\pi_{O,z},\ \pi_{O,y}&-16.1&2,2\\
\pi_{O,z}^*,\ \pi_{O,y}^*&-12.1&1,1\quad(\text{parallel spins})\\
\pi_C&-10.5&2\\
\pi_C^*&-5.5&0
\end{array}
$$

Each bonding [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) orbital has equal-phase coefficients on its two O atoms; its antibonding partner reverses one coefficient. The two pi directions are z in the prospective ring plane and y perpendicular to it. [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) pi and pi-star use z-directed [p orbitals](../../../../../../p-orbital.md) with equal and opposite coefficient signs. In this separated-fragment comparison the occupations are those of neutral triplet O$_2$ plus neutral [ethene](../../../../../../ethylene.md). **Do not refill the combined list solely by energy**, which would move an [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) between infinitely separated fragments and describe a charge-transfer state instead.
