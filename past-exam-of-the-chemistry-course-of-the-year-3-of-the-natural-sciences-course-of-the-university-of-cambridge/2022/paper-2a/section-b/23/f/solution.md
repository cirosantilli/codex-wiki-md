↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

**Endpoint energies, endpoint phase patterns and a conserved [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) fix the qualitative connectivity.** Each initial orbital must end in the appropriate product [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) block, and the ordering within a generic block is preserved by avoided crossings. The two singleton blocks $A_2$ and $B_2$ have unique partners. No numerical calculation at each intermediate separation is required to draw those connections.

This conclusion is limited to a correlation diagram: it does not determine intermediate orbital energies, interaction strengths, the location or height of a [reaction energy barrier](../../../../../../reaction-energy-barrier.md), or the lowest route on a multidimensional [potential energy surface](../../../../../../potential-energy-surface.md). If the reaction breaks the assumed [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md), previously distinct [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) blocks can mix and the diagram must be reconsidered.
