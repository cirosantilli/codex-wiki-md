<h1 id="section-b/23/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use negative bonding [Hückel resonance integrals](../../../../../../huckel-resonance-integral.md), $\beta_{OO}=-2.0$ and $\beta_{CC}=-2.5\ \mathrm{eV}$. The [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) HOMO is pi-antibonding, whereas the [ethene](../../../../../../ethylene.md) HOMO is pi-bonding. The frozen-orbital Koopmans estimate therefore gives

$$
\alpha_O-\beta_{OO}\simeq-12.1,\qquad
\alpha_C+\beta_{CC}\simeq-10.5.
$$

Consequently **$\alpha_O\simeq-14.1\ \mathrm{eV}$ and $\alpha_C\simeq-8.0\ \mathrm{eV}$**. These [Hückel Coulomb parameters](../../../../../../huckel-coulomb-parameter.md) are effective parameters of this spectroscopically calibrated model. Real [ionization energies](../../../../../../ionization-energy.md) include relaxation and correlation, so they are not exact [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) elements of an isolated-atom [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md).
