<h1 id="section-b/24/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Taking the Hessian of the potential with respect to mass-weighted displacements gives

$$
\boxed{D_0=k_1\begin{pmatrix}1/m_a&-1/\sqrt{m_am_b}\\-1/\sqrt{m_am_b}&1/m_b\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The equations of motion are $\ddot{\boldsymbol q}=-D_0\boldsymbol q$, so [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are squared [angular frequencies](../../../../../../../angular-frequency.md). Normalized [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) and [frequencies](../../../../../../../frequency.md) are

$$
\boxed{u_T=\frac{(\sqrt{m_a},\sqrt{m_b})}{\sqrt{m_a+m_b}},\quad\omega_T=0;\qquad
u_S=\frac{(\sqrt{m_b},-\sqrt{m_a})}{\sqrt{m_a+m_b}},\quad
\omega_S=\sqrt{k_1\left(\frac1{m_a}+\frac1{m_b}\right)}=\sqrt{\frac{k_1}{\mu}}.}
$$

Both vectors are in the q basis, and $\mu=m_am_b/(m_a+m_b)$. The translation corresponds to equal physical displacements $\delta y_a=\delta y_b$. The stretch has $m_a\delta y_a+m_b\delta y_b=0$ and opposite physical displacements with magnitude ratio $|\delta y_a/\delta y_b|=m_b/m_a$. Ordinary [frequencies](../../../../../../../frequency.md) are $\nu=\omega/(2\pi)$; [spectroscopic wavenumbers](../../../../../../../spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) are $\omega/(2\pi c)$.
