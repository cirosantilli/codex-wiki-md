<h1 id="section-b/24/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For weak anchoring, $k_2\ll k_1$, use the normalized free-molecule [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $u_T=(1,1)/\sqrt2$ and $u_S=(1,-1)/\sqrt2$. The perturbation is

$$
\delta D=\frac{k_2}{m}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its diagonal [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) elements in this basis are both $k_2/(2m)$. First-order [time-independent perturbation theory](../../../../../../../time-independent-perturbation-theory.md) for the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\omega_T^2\simeq\frac{k_2}{2m},\qquad
\omega_S^2\simeq\frac{2k_1}{m}+\frac{k_2}{2m}.}
$$

Hence **$\omega_T\simeq\sqrt{k_2/(2m)}$** and **$\omega_S\simeq\sqrt{2k_1/m}\,[1+k_2/(8k_1)]$**. The zero free translation requires taking a square root of its new [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), rather than Taylor-expanding the [frequency](../../../../../../../frequency.md) about zero. It is not a degenerate [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) in the stipulated one-dimensional two-coordinate model.

As a check, the exact squared [frequencies](../../../../../../../frequency.md) are

$$
\omega_\pm^2=\frac{2k_1+k_2\pm\sqrt{4k_1^2+k_2^2}}{2m}.
$$

Expansion gives the same first-order results; the next lower-mode correction is $-k_2^2/(8k_1m)$. The perturbed [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) contain small translation/stretch admixtures of order $k_2/(4k_1)$, consistent with the failure of uniform translation to remain an exact mode in orientation (1).
