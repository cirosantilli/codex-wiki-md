<h1 id="section-b/22/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $T_{v',v''}=T_e+G'(v')-G''(v'')$. For a fixed row, moving one column to the right therefore gives

$$
T_{v',v''+1}-T_{v',v''}=-\big[G''(v''+1)-G''(v'')\big]
=-\widetilde\omega_e''+2\widetilde\omega_e''x_e''(v''+1).
$$

Thus **adjacent-column entries decrease by the lower-state vibrational spacing**, independent of the upper-state row in the ideal model. If the positive difference is instead defined as left minus right, it is $\widetilde\omega_e''-2\widetilde\omega_e''x_e''(v''+1)$. Stating the subtraction direction prevents an artificial negative vibrational [frequency](../../../../../../../frequency.md).
