<h1 id="section-a/17/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**The fixed localized [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is unsuitable near equilibrium.** It does not develop the correct shared bonding orbital and is not a pure singlet: for one alpha and one beta orbital, $\langle S^2\rangle/\hbar^2=1-|\langle A|B\rangle|^2$, which is nonzero unless the occupied [spatial orbitals](../../../../../../spatial-orbital.md) coincide. At dissociation it is an equal singlet/triplet mixture, though their energies become degenerate there. Near equilibrium the physical [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) is a bonding singlet. An optimized [UHF](../../../../../../unrestricted-hartree-fock-method.md) calculation can recover the restricted, spin-pure solution by making its two occupied [spatial orbitals](../../../../../../spatial-orbital.md) equal; that does not rescue the particular fixed atomic-orbital [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) written here. A spin-adapted multideterminant description is needed to combine good bonding near equilibrium with correct singlet dissociation.
