<h1 id="section-a/20/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $H=\alpha I+K$ with the same [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) diagonal parameter throughout, and define $P$ to have entries $+1$ on one sublattice and $-1$ on the other. Bipartite connectivity implies

$$
PKP=-K.
$$

Thus $Kc=\epsilon c$ implies $K(Pc)=-\epsilon(Pc)$. This proves **energy pairing about $\alpha$**, $E=\alpha\pm\epsilon$, and **equal site-probability magnitudes in each paired pair**, since $|(Pc)_i|^2=|c_i|^2$. This is the Coulson-Rushbrooke pairing property.[1940, pp. 193–200, alternant-system pairing and density properties](../../../../../../note-on-the-method-of-molecular-orbitals-by-charles-alfred-coulson-and-g-s-rushbrooke.md)

For the usual neutral half-filled closed-shell occupation, the lower member of each pair is doubly occupied. Combining paired coefficients with completeness of the [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) gives **one pi [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) per [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)**, so the model predicts no net pi charge separation. The same argument makes **off-diagonal [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md) orders between sites on the same sublattice zero**. These statements assume a neutral half-filled density and do not apply unchanged to the charged six-electron four-site ion considered separately.

An imbalance of sublattice sizes also guarantees **at least $|N_A-N_B|$ nonbonding orbitals at energy $\alpha$**, because the rectangular coupling block has insufficient [rank](../../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md) to remove all its [null space](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). None of the spectral pairing, equal paired densities, neutral charge uniformity or sublattice bond-order restrictions is required for a non-alternant graph. Long-range same-sublattice couplings or unequal diagonal parameters can likewise destroy the simple proof.
