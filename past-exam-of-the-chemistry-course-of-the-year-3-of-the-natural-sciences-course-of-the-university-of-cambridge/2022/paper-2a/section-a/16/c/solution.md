<h1 id="section-a/16/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A quencher-independent intersystem-crossing rate adds to the unquenched decay rate:

$$
\phi_Q=\frac{k_F}{k_F+k_{\rm ISC}+k_Q[Q]},\qquad
\tau_0=\frac1{k_F+k_{\rm ISC}}.
$$

The normalized [Stern-Volmer equation](../../../../../../stern-volmer-equation.md) retains the same form, so **[intersystem crossing](../../../../../../intersystem-crossing.md) does not bias $k_Q$ if the actual unquenched lifetime is used**. It reduces $\phi_0$ below one and changes the slope through $\tau_0$; assuming $\tau_0=1/k_F$ would then give the wrong result. A quencher that itself changes [intersystem crossing](../../../../../../intersystem-crossing.md) or returns triplet population to the singlet requires a larger kinetic scheme.
