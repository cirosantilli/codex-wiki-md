For conventional [CISD](../../../../../../configuration-interaction-with-singles-and-doubles.md), the leading matrix-vector contraction scales as $O(o^2v^4)$ in occupied and virtual orbital dimensions. If both grow proportionally to a system-size parameter $N$, the familiar estimate is **$O(N^6)$ work per iteration**; the iteration count and integral-storage strategy add implementation-dependent costs.

Calculated [potential energy surfaces](../../../../../../potential-energy-surface.md) can predict equilibrium geometries and harmonic force constants, permitting comparison with diffraction-derived structures, [rotational constants](../../../../../../rotational-constant.md) and vibrational spectra. Calculating several roots or different charge states can also estimate excitation or [ionization energies](../../../../../../ionization-energy.md). Such predictions depend on basis and truncation quality; truncated [CI](../../../../../../configuration-interaction.md) is not generally size extensive and is not automatically reliable for strongly multiconfigurational bond breaking. The quoted polynomial scaling is for [CISD](../../../../../../configuration-interaction-with-singles-and-doubles.md), not full [configuration interaction](../../../../../../configuration-interaction.md), whose [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) space grows combinatorially.
