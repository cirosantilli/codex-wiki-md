**[configuration interaction with singles and doubles](../../../../../../../configuration-interaction-with-singles-and-doubles.md) ([CISD](../../../../../../../configuration-interaction-with-singles-and-doubles.md))** uses a linear combination of the reference [Slater determinant](../../../../../../../slater-determinant.md) and singly and doubly excited [determinants](../../../../../../../determinant.md). Its many-electron trial state is therefore not restricted to one [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md); the orbitals may be held fixed while the configuration coefficients are varied.
