**[molecular mechanics](../../../../../../../molecular-mechanics.md)** evaluates a parametrized potential of atomic positions, with terms such as bond stretching, angle bending, torsion and nonbonded interactions. [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md) are represented implicitly through the fitted force field, rather than by electronic orbitals, a density or an electronic [wavefunction](../../../../../../../wave-function-split.md). This example is different from both preceding electronic methods.
