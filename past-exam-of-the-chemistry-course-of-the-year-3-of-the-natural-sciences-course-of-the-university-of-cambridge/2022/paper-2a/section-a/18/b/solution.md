<h1 id="section-a/18/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Hückel orbital charge density](../../../../../../huckel-orbital-charge-density.md) and [Hückel pi bond order](../../../../../../huckel-pi-bond-order.md) give

$$
n_i=\sum_k f_kc_{ik}^2,\qquad p_{ij}=\sum_k f_kc_{ik}c_{jk}.
$$

Each site receives $1/2$ [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) from the lowest orbital and one from the occupied nonbonding subspace, so **$n_i=3/2$ at every [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)**, summing to six. Relative to one neutral pi [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) per [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) this is a pi charge of $-e/2$ at each site.

For adjacent sites, only the lowest orbital contributes, giving **$p_{12}=p_{23}=p_{34}=p_{41}=1/2$**. For opposite sites the nonbonding contribution reverses the sign: **$p_{13}=p_{24}=-1/2$**. These non-neighbour negative density-matrix entries are not extra [sigma bonds](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md) or extra resonance integrals in the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md). If only skeletal nearest-neighbour [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md) orders are requested, the four values $1/2$ are the relevant ones.
