<h1 id="section-a/14/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [14](../14.md)

Use the [D3h molecular point group](../../../../../d3h-molecular-point-group.md) for [phosphorus pentafluoride](../../../../../phosphorus-pentafluoride.md), with $z$ along the two axial P–F bonds. For each [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) operation, only atoms left in place contribute to the character of the Cartesian displacement representation; each contributes the trace of the corresponding $3\times3$ spatial [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md). In the class order $E,2C_3,3C_2',\sigma_h,2S_3,3\sigma_v$ this gives

$$
\chi_{3N}=(18,0,-2,4,-2,4).
$$

For example a $C_2'$ fixes P and one equatorial F, and each fixed atom has Cartesian trace $-1$, giving $-2$. [reflection](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) in the equatorial plane fixes four atoms, each with trace $1$, giving $4$. Reduction with the [character table](../../../../../character-table.md) gives

$$
\Gamma_{3N}=2A_1'+A_2'+4E'+3A_2''+2E''.
$$

Subtract translations $E'+A_2''$ and rotations $A_2'+E''$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{\rm vib}=2A_1'+3E'+2A_2''+E''.}
$$

Its dimension is $2+6+2+2=12=3(6)-6$, as required for a nonlinear six-atom molecule. Thus there are eight symmetry-distinct fundamental [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md) in the harmonic picture, with each E mode doubly degenerate. This analysis concerns vibrations about one trigonal-bipyramidal minimum; it does not treat large-amplitude rearrangements between minima.
