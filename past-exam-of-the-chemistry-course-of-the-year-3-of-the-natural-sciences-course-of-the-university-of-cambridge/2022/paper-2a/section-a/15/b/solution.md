↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**The [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) changes from high-spin $t_{2g}^3e_g^2$, ${}^6A_{1g}$, to low-spin $t_{2g}^5$, ${}^2T_{2g}$.** The larger splitting eventually compensates the additional pairing cost. Because all plotted excitation energies are re-referenced to the new [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md), the branches change slope at the crossover. The break is not an infinite physical excitation energy or a discontinuity of the underlying [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md).
