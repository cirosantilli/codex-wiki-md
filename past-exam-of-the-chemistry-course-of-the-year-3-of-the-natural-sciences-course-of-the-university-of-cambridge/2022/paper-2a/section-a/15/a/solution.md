↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [octahedral splitting](../../../../../../octahedral-splitting.md) $\Delta_o$ separates the metal $t_{2g}$ and $e_g$ orbital sets. The [Racah parameter](../../../../../../racah-parameter.md) $B$ characterizes interelectronic repulsion and hence the spacing of many-electron d-shell terms; it is not another name for $\Delta_o$. A [Tanabe-Sugano diagram](../../../../../../tanabe-sugano-diagram.md) plots term excitation energies $E/B$ against $\Delta_o/B$, for specified values of the other repulsion-parameter ratios. Each ordinate is measured relative to whichever term is the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) at that field strength. The original many-electron treatment diagonalizes the relevant d-shell energy [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) rather than identifying every [absorption](../../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) with a one-electron $t_{2g}\to e_g$ gap.[1954, pp. 753–766, d-shell energy matrices in a cubic field](../../../../../../on-the-absorption-spectra-of-complex-ions-i-by-yukito-tanabe-and-satoru-sugano.md)
