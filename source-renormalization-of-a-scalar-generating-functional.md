# Source renormalization of a scalar generating functional

↑ **Parent:** [Connected generating functional](connected-generating-functional.md)

For $d=4-\epsilon$, write the bare field as $\phi_0=Z^{1/2}\phi$ and the renormalized source coupling as $\mu^{-\epsilon/2}h\phi$. In bare variables this is $h_0\phi_0$, with the displayed source. At fixed bare parameters, the source running is $\mu\,d\log h/d\mu=\epsilon/2+\tfrac12\widehat\beta\,\partial_\lambda\log Z$. Normalizing the [generating functional](generating-functional.md) by its zero-source value cancels source-independent vacuum diagrams and the constant measure factor from field rescaling. It does not remove source-dependent [ultraviolet divergences](ultraviolet-divergence.md) or [infrared divergences](infrared-divergence.md); the former require the ordinary field, mass and interaction [counterterms](counterterm.md).
