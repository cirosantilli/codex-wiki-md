# Extensional equations for a slender Newtonian column

↑ **Parent:** [Uniaxial extensional flow](uniaxial-extensional-flow.md)

A slender axisymmetric column of a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md), with radius $a(z,t)$, axial [velocity](velocity.md) $w$, external [pressure](pressure.md) $p_e$ and axial body-force density $f$, obeys $(a^2)_t+(a^2w)_z=0$ and $3\mu(a^2w_z)_z/a^2=p_{e,z}-f$ when inertia, [surface tension](surface-tension.md) and leading external shear are negligible. The factor three is the [Trouton ratio](trouton-ratio.md). Radial [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $u_r=-rw_z/2$; the [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md) gives $p=p_e-\mu w_z$ and axial excess [stress](stress.md) $3\mu w_z$. This reduces a three-dimensional [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) to one-dimensional evolving geometry.
