# Sphere gauge fixing for four string vertices

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-string scattering amplitude](closed-string-scattering-amplitude.md)

At genus zero the residual [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) group fixes three marked insertion points, leaving one complex position to integrate. The corresponding [worldsheet ghost fields](worldsheet-ghost-field.md) give $|z_{ij}z_{jk}z_{ki}|^2$ for the three fixed points. For on-shell weight-$(1,1)$ [string vertex operators](string-vertex-operator.md), these ghost factors cancel the coordinate dependence of the fixed-point normalization, including the limit at infinity. The remaining [Koba-Nielsen factor](koba-nielsen-factor.md) for four tachyons is the integrand of the [complex beta integral](complex-beta-integral.md), producing the [Virasoro–Shapiro amplitude](virasoro-shapiro-amplitude.md).
