# Riemann xi function

↑ **Parent:** [Completed Riemann zeta function](completed-riemann-zeta-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_xi_function)

Removing the completion's [poles](pole.md) produces an [entire function](entire-function.md) with $\xi(0)=\xi(1)=1/2$ and $\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)$. Its zeros are the nontrivial zeros of the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md). Its finite order allows [Hadamard factorization](hadamard-factorization-theorem.md), while the [xi modulus criterion for the Riemann hypothesis](xi-modulus-criterion-for-the-riemann-hypothesis.md) characterizes their horizontal location.
