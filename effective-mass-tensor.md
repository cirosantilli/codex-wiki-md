# Effective-mass tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Band effective mass](band-effective-mass.md)

For a semiclassical [wave packet](wave-packet.md) in an isolated [energy band](energy-band.md), $v_i=\hbar^{-1}\partial_{k_i}\varepsilon$ and $\hbar\dot k_j=F_j$ imply $\dot v_i=\sum_j(m^{*-1})_{ij}F_j$. The inverse [effective-mass tensor](effective-mass-tensor.md) is the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) of the dispersion divided by $\hbar^2$. Along a unit vector $\mathbf e$, $1/m^*_{\mathbf e}=\sum_{ij}e_i(m^{*-1})_{ij}e_j$. The [Laplacian](laplacian.md) is its trace: $\hbar^{-2}\nabla_k^2\varepsilon=\operatorname{tr}(m^{*-1})$. Thus in $d$ isotropic dimensions the scalar mass is $d\hbar^2/(\nabla_k^2\varepsilon)$, rather than $\hbar^2/(\nabla_k^2\varepsilon)$.
