# SEC void volume

↑ **Parent:** [Size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md)

The [SEC void volume](sec-void-volume.md) $V_0$ is the interparticle liquid volume, operationally measured by a sufficiently large excluded, nonbinding tracer. It excludes pore liquid. Choose a tracer that does not undergo [adsorption](adsorption.md) or change the packing; correct the measured elution for tubing and detector delay before identifying it with a column-only $V_0$.
