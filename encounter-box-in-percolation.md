# Encounter box in percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary counting proof of percolation uniqueness](boundary-counting-proof-of-percolation-uniqueness.md)

An encounter box is a finite connected set of open vertices whose removal from its [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md) leaves at least three infinite connected components. Unlike a [trifurcation vertex in percolation](trifurcation-vertex-in-percolation.md), it allows branching to occur across several vertices, which is useful for [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md) on graphs with local cycles. Contract disjoint encounter boxes to junctions. In a finite region, each junction must connect at least three different exits. Pruning to a forest gives at most as many junctions as boundary exits, by the identity $\sum_v(\deg(v)-2)=-2$ for a finite [tree](tree-graph-theory.md). Positive density of encounter boxes is therefore incompatible with an [amenable graph](amenable-graph.md).
