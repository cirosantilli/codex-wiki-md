# Kakeya Minkowski dimension conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [Kakeya set](kakeya-set.md)

The dimension assertion asks that every bounded [Kakeya set](kakeya-set.md) in $\mathbb R^n$ have full [Minkowski dimension](box-counting-dimension.md). The [Kakeya maximal conjecture](kakeya-maximal-conjecture.md) implies the stronger lower-dimension conclusion: its application to $E_\delta$ gives $|E_\delta|\gtrsim_{n,\varepsilon}\delta^{n\varepsilon}$, hence $N_\delta(E)\gtrsim_{n,\varepsilon}\delta^{-n+n\varepsilon}$ for every $\varepsilon>0$.
