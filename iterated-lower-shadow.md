# Iterated lower shadow

↑ **Parent:** [Lower shadow](lower-shadow.md)

Repeatedly take the [lower shadow](lower-shadow.md) of a [uniform set family](uniform-set-family.md). After $j$ steps the result consists of all subsets of its members whose size is $j$ smaller. Iterating the [Kruskal-Katona theorem](kruskal-katona-theorem.md) shows that [colexicographic initial segments](colexicographic-initial-segment.md) minimize every [iterated lower shadow](iterated-lower-shadow.md).
