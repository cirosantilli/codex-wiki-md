# Integrable random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Expected value](expected-value.md)

A real or complex [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ is integrable when $\mathbb E|X|<\infty$. Its [expected value](expected-value.md) is then finite, and [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) preserves integrability.
