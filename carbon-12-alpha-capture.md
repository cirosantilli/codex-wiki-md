# Carbon-12 alpha capture

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha capture](alpha-capture.md)

Capture of an [alpha particle](alpha-particle.md) by [carbon](carbon.md)-12 makes [oxygen](oxygen.md)-16. Competition between this reaction and the [Triple-alpha process](triple-alpha-process.md) helps determine the carbon-to-oxygen ratio after [core helium burning](core-helium-burning.md).
