<h1 id="normalized-short-time-schrodinger-kernel">Normalized short-time Schrödinger kernel</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum-mechanical propagator](quantum-mechanical-propagator.md)

For unit mass and $\hbar=1$, this prepoint kernel tends to the identity and generates the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md). The square-root branch follows the damped [Fresnel integral](fresnel-integral.md). Its normalized moments are $1$, $0$, and $i\Delta t$ at orders zero, one and two; these fix both the normalization and the kinetic sign.
