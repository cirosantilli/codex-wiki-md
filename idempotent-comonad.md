# Idempotent comonad

↑ **Parent:** [Comonad](comonad.md)

A [comonad](comonad.md) is idempotent when its comultiplication $G\to G^2$ is invertible. Its coalgebra category identifies with the [coreflective subcategory](coreflective-subcategory.md) of objects on which the counit is invertible. A coreflective inclusion produces such a comonad.
