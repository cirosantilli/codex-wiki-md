# Lubell mass

↑ **Parent:** [Lubell-Yamamoto-Meshalkin inequality](lubell-yamamoto-meshalkin-inequality.md)

The Lubell mass of a set family $\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal P([n])$ is

$$
\lambda(\mathcal A)=\sum_{A\in\mathcal A}\binom n{|A|}^{-1}.
$$

It is the [expected value](expected-value.md) of the number of members of $\mathcal A$ on a [Uniformly random maximal chain in a Boolean lattice](uniformly-random-maximal-chain-in-a-boolean-lattice.md).
