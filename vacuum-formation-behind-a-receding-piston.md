# Vacuum formation behind a receding piston

↑ **Parent:** [Receding-piston rarefaction wave](receding-piston-rarefaction-wave.md)

The gas state at a receding piston reaches vacuum when $\dot X_p=-2c_0/(\gamma-1)$, because then the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) and [mass density](density.md) both vanish. If the piston subsequently recedes faster, a vacuum gap opens between it and the gas boundary, which continues with velocity $-2c_0/(\gamma-1)$.
