# Triangular distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous probability distribution](continuous-probability-distribution-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_distribution)

A triangular [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) rises linearly from zero at $a$ to its mode $c$, then falls linearly to zero at $b$. Its peak is $2/(b-a)$. Its [inverse transform sampling](inverse-transform-sampling.md) rule is $a+\sqrt{u(b-a)(c-a)}$ for $u\le(c-a)/(b-a)$, and $b-\sqrt{(1-u)(b-a)(b-c)}$ otherwise.
