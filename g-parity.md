# G parity

↑ **Parent:** [Isospin](isospin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G_parity)

The product of [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) and a half-turn in [isospin](isospin.md) is G parity. Both conjugations act on the [isospin](isospin.md) generators by $(J_1,J_2,J_3)\mapsto(-J_1,J_2,-J_3)$, so their product commutes with every generator. The [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) therefore gives a common G-parity value throughout each irreducible [isospin](isospin.md) multiplet on which the operator acts internally. It can classify a charged multiplet even though individual charged states are not eigenstates of [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md).
