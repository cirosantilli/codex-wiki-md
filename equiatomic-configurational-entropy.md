# Equiatomic configurational entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Configurational entropy](configurational-entropy.md)

Randomly distributing $n$ species with equal fractions over $N$ sites gives $\Omega=N!/[(N/n)!]^n$. Stirling approximation gives $S=k_BN\ln n$, or $R\ln n$ per mole of sites. [Configurational entropy](configurational-entropy.md) alone does not decide whether a multicomponent alloy is a stable single [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md).
