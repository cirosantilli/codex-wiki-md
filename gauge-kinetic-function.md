# Gauge kinetic function

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric action](supersymmetric-action.md)

The gauge kinetic function is a symmetric [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) of [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md) multiplying the gauge field-strength [F-term](f-term.md). Its real part controls gauge kinetic coefficients and its imaginary part controls topological-angle terms. Its Wilsonian perturbative correction is constrained by [one-loop exactness of the holomorphic gauge kinetic function](one-loop-exactness-of-the-holomorphic-gauge-kinetic-function.md); canonical and one-particle-irreducible gauge couplings require separate normalization and infrared analysis.
