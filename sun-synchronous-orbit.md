# Sun-synchronous orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Polar orbit](polar-orbit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun-synchronous_orbit)

An [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) whose nodal precession tracks the central planet's annual motion, keeping approximately the same local solar time at corresponding crossings. Near-[Earth](earth.md) implementations are nearly polar and exploit the planet's nonspherical gravitational field. Fixed local-time sampling supports comparison between passes while limiting observations of the diurnal cycle.
