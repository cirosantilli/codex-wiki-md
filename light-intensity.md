# Light intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Intensity (physics)](intensity-physics.md)

Here [light intensity](light-intensity.md) means the time-averaged energy flux per perpendicular [area](surface-area.md) for [light](light.md). It is the propagation-direction component of the averaged [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md). For plane [electromagnetic waves](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) in the same medium it is proportional to squared [electric field](electric-field-split.md) [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md), giving the projection-squared factor in [Malus's law](malus-s-law.md).
