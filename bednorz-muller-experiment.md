<h1 id="bednorz-muller-experiment">Bednorz-Müller experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [High-temperature superconductivity](high-temperature-superconductivity.md)

In 1986, [Johannes Georg Bednorz](johannes-georg-bednorz.md) and [Karl Alexander Müller](karl-alexander-muller.md) precipitated mixed [oxalates](oxalate.md) from aqueous [barium](barium.md), [lanthanum](lanthanum.md) and [copper](copper.md) [nitrates](nitrate.md) using [oxalic acid](oxalic-acid.md). Heating at $900\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ for five hours preceded pressing at $4\,\mathrm{kbar}$ and reheating for [sintering](sintering.md). [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md) resolved three phases. Rectangular pieces carried [gold](gold.md) electrodes and [indium](indium.md) wires; [four-terminal sensing](four-terminal-sensing.md) in a continuous-flow [cryostat](cryostat.md) measured [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) versus [temperature](temperature-split.md). Annealing atmosphere and measuring [electric current density](current-density.md) were varied; larger [electric current densities](current-density.md) suppressed the drop. An onset near $35\,\mathrm K$ differed from the much lower [temperature](temperature-split.md) where the drop approached three orders of magnitude.[§II, pp.190–191; Figs.1–3](possible-high-tc-superconductivity-in-the-ba-la-cu-o-system-by-johannes-georg-bednorz-and-karl-alexander-muller.md)

This was evidence for possible granular [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md), with phase identification unfinished. The paper deferred [Meissner effect](meissner-effect.md) and [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) tests; its proof note corrected bulk [lanthanum](lanthanum.md)/[barium](barium.md) ratios. A resistive onset did not establish a uniform bulk [superconducting transition temperature](superconducting-transition-temperature.md).[§IV, p.192; proof note, p.193](possible-high-tc-superconductivity-in-the-ba-la-cu-o-system-by-johannes-georg-bednorz-and-karl-alexander-muller.md)

Their 1987 authored lecture describes later [magnetometer](magnetometer.md) checks on [lead](lead.md), followed by [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md) measurements: samples with a resistive drop developed [diamagnetism](diamagnetism.md) at somewhat lower [temperatures](temperature-split.md). Combining magnetic, transport and [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md) results identified [barium](barium.md)-substituted $\mathrm{La_2CuO_4}$ as the superconducting phase. These later results strengthened the case beyond the initial mixed-phase transport evidence.[Part 1, pp.436–438; Figs.1.10–1.11](perovskite-type-oxides-the-new-approach-to-high-tc-superconductivity-by-johannes-georg-bednorz-and-karl-alexander-muller.md)
