# Universality of an invariant speed under boost composition

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md)

Suppose standard collinear boosts have the [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) form, but initially assign speed scales $a$ and $b$ to two successive transformations with relative [velocities](velocity.md) $v$ and $u$. Their composition has spatial and temporal diagonal factors proportional to $1+uv/a^2$ and $1+uv/b^2$, respectively. Closure within the same calibrated transformation family requires these factors to agree, forcing $a^2=b^2$ for generic nonzero boosts.

This argument fixes a universal reciprocal squared speed; it does not establish that it is nonzero. The common limit $1/a^2=0$ gives the consistent [Galilean transformation](galilean-transformation.md) family. A finite invariant [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) is an additional empirical property of spacetime and electromagnetism, expressed by the second of the [postulates of special relativity](postulates-of-special-relativity.md). It cannot be deduced by excluding the Galilean limit through boost composition alone.
