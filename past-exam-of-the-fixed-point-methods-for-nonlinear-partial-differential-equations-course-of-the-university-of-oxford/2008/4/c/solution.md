<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [noncontact set](../../../../noncontact-set.md) $D=\{x\in(0,l):u(x)>g(x)\}$ is open. For $\varphi\in C_0^\infty(D)$, the positive gap above the obstacle has a positive minimum on the support of $\varphi$. Thus both $u+t\varphi$ and $u-t\varphi$ are admissible for sufficiently small $t>0$. Applying the [variational inequality](../../../../variational-inequality.md) to both signs yields

$$
\int_D\bigl(2au'\varphi'+\gamma\cos(u)\varphi\bigr)\,dx=0.
$$

Hence the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) on the noncontact set is

$$
\boxed{-2(au')'+\gamma\cos u=0\quad\text{in }D.}
$$

It holds classically under the assumed smoothness. On the [contact set](../../../../contact-set-of-an-obstacle-problem.md) the obstacle prevents the negative variations used to obtain this equality, so a variational inequality remains the appropriate global description.
