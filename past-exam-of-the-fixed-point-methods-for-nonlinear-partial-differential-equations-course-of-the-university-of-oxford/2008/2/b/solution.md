The [Schauder fixed-point theorem](../../../../schauder-fixed-point-theorem.md) states that a continuous compact self-map of a nonempty closed bounded [convex set](../../../../convex-set-split.md) in a [Banach space](../../../../banach-space-split.md) has a [fixed point](../../../../fixed-point.md). Equivalently in this bounded-domain version, it suffices for the image of the map to be relatively compact. The theorem guarantees existence, without requiring a contraction estimate or promising uniqueness.
