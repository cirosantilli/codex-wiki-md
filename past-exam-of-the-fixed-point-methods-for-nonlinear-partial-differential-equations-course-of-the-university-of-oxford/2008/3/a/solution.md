<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using the [dual pairing](../../../../dual-pairing.md), a [monotone operator](../../../../monotone-operator.md) satisfies $\langle A(u)-A(v),u-v\rangle\geq0$ for all $u,v\in M$. It is a [strongly monotone operator](../../../../strongly-monotone-operator.md) if some $m>0$ gives

$$
\boxed{\langle A(u)-A(v),u-v\rangle\geq m\|u-v\|^2\quad(u,v\in M).}
$$

A [nonlinear coercive operator](../../../../nonlinear-coercive-operator.md) with respect to $u_0\in M$ satisfies $\langle A(u),u-u_0\rangle/\|u-u_0\|\to+\infty$ as $\|u-u_0\|\to\infty$ in $M$.

Strong monotonicity, with $v=u_0$, gives

$$
\langle A(u),u-u_0\rangle\geq m\|u-u_0\|^2+\langle A(u_0),u-u_0\rangle
\geq m\|u-u_0\|^2-\|A(u_0)\|_{X^*}\|u-u_0\|.
$$

After division, the lower bound is $m\|u-u_0\|-\|A(u_0)\|_{X^*}\to+\infty$, proving coercivity. Reflexivity and separability are not needed for this particular estimate.
