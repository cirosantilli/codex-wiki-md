# Central simple algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_simple_algebra)

A central simple algebra over a [field](field.md) $k$ is a finite-dimensional associative $k$-algebra that is a [simple ring](simple-ring.md) and whose center is exactly $k$.
