# Sobolev interpolation inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

For $a<b$ and $0\leq\theta\leq1$, the displayed estimate holds on Euclidean space and a [torus](torus.md) with the Fourier definition of the [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md). In the squared norm, write the weighted squared Fourier coefficient as the product of its weight-$a$ value to the power $1-\theta$ and its weight-$b$ value to the power $\theta$. [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) then proves the estimate. On a [compact manifold](compact-manifold.md), a finite coordinate cover and a [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) give the same estimate up to a constant. [Young inequality](young-s-inequality-for-products.md) implies the useful absorption form $\|u\|_{H^{s+1}}\leq\varepsilon\|u\|_{H^{s+2}}+C_{s,\varepsilon}\|u\|_2$ for nonnegative $s$.
