# Transonic spherical flow in a power-law potential

↑ **Parent:** [Spherically symmetric adiabatic flow](spherically-symmetric-adiabatic-flow.md)

A smooth [transonic branch](transonic-branch.md) in a [power-law gravitational potential](power-law-gravitational-potential.md) has $u_*^2=c_*^2=A\beta/(2r_*^\beta)$ at its [sonic point](sonic-point.md). Substitution into the [Bernoulli function](bernoulli-function.md) gives $\mathcal B=c_*^2[1/(\gamma-1)+1/2-2/\beta]$. The local [sonic-point slope discriminant](sonic-point-slope-discriminant.md) distinguishes a genuine crossing from a degenerate everywhere-sonic scaling solution.
