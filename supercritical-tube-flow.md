# Supercritical tube flow

↑ **Parent:** [Collapsible tube flow](collapsible-tube-flow.md)

A positive [collapsible tube flow](collapsible-tube-flow.md) is supercritical when both [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) $u\pm c$ are positive relative to the wall. Information then propagates downstream on both branches. For a quadratic pressure-area law and fixed dimensionless flux $q$, the condition is $A/A_0<\sqrt q$.
