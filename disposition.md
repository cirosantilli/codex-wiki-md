# Disposition

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy](philosophy.md)

A tendency to respond in a specified way under specified conditions. A material's [hardness](hardness.md) or a surface's colour-producing power can be described dispositionally without treating the response as an illusion. Whether a disposition is realized, and which conditions matter, require separate investigation.
