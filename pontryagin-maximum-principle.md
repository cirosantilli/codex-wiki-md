# Pontryagin maximum principle

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal control](optimal-control.md)

For a regular finite-horizon [optimal control](optimal-control.md) problem, an optimal trajectory admits nontrivial multipliers and a [costate](costate.md) obeying adjoint equations, together with pointwise optimization of the [Hamiltonian of an optimal-control problem](hamiltonian-of-an-optimal-control-problem.md). In the normal minimization convention $H=L+p\cdot f$, the conditions are $\dot x=H_p$, $\dot p=-H_x$ and minimization of $H$ over admissible controls; endpoint and state constraints change the terminal conditions. Abnormal extremals use a zero cost multiplier and must not be silently excluded.
