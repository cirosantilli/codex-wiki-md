# Photonic structure

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)

A designed spatial arrangement of [dielectric](dielectric.md) or other electromagnetic material controls optical propagation, confinement or emission. Examples include [waveguides](waveguide.md), [distributed Bragg reflectors](distributed-bragg-reflector.md), [photonic crystals](photonic-crystal.md) and defect cavities. Spatial [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) and [mode](mode-statistics.md) [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) act on electromagnetic states; they are distinct from the [electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md) of the material itself.
