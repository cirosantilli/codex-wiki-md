# Total variation denoising of a disk

↑ **Parent:** [Total variation denoising](total-variation-denoising.md)

For data $g=\chi_{B(0,R)}$ on $\mathbb R^2$, the minimizer of $\alpha\operatorname{TV}(u)+\tfrac12\|u-g\|_2^2$ is the displayed amplitude shrinkage, including extinction at $\alpha=R/2$. The [total variation calibration](total-variation-calibration.md) uses $z(x)=x/R$ inside the disk and $z(x)=Rx/|x|^2$ outside. Its [continuous](continuous-function.md) normal component creates no boundary measure, and $\operatorname{div}z=(2/R)\chi_{B(0,R)}$. This [subgradient](subgradient.md) certifies the positive branch; scaling it down certifies the zero branch. The quadratic fidelity is [strictly convex](strictly-convex-function.md), ensuring uniqueness.
