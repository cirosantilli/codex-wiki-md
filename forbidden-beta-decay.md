# Forbidden beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay transition](beta-decay-transition.md)

A [beta decay transition](beta-decay-transition.md) for which the leading $l=0$ lepton orbital term vanishes by the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) or [parity](parity.md) selection rules. The least contributing orbital order $l=n\geq1$ gives the name $n$th forbidden. First-forbidden transitions change [parity](parity.md) and can carry total lepton [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $0,1,2$; second-forbidden transitions preserve [parity](parity.md) and can carry $1,2,3$, with Fermi-type $S=0$ contributions restricted to $J=l$. Forbidden is suppression by higher powers of lepton momentum times nuclear size, rather than an absolute prohibition.
