# Nonrelativistic particle field

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field theory](scalar-field-theory-split.md)

This first-order-in-time field theory has an annihilation-field expansion $\Psi=\int a_{\mathbf p}e^{-i\mathbf p^2t/(2m)+i\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf x}\,d^3p/(2\pi)^3$. Its adjoint creates particles; an [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) creation term is not required by a relativistic [mass shell](mass-shell.md) or Lorentz-invariant [microcausality](microcausality.md). It is often an effective low-energy sector of a relativistic charged theory after the [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) sector is omitted. This does not mean that antimatter cannot be treated nonrelativistically: one may introduce a separate nonrelativistic field for that species.
