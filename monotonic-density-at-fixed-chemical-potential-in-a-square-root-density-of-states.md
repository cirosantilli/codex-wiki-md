# Monotonic density at fixed chemical potential in a square-root density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-chemical-potential Sommerfeld density increase](fixed-chemical-potential-sommerfeld-density-increase.md)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) derivative of the Fermi occupation has opposite signs below and above $\mu$. Pairing energies $\mu\pm x$ leaves a positive contribution because the [density of states](density-of-states.md) increases with energy. The unpaired higher-energy tail is also positive. Hence the density increases at fixed $\mu$ for an unbounded three-dimensional parabolic band.
