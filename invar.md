# Invar

↑ **Parent:** [Alloy](alloy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invar)

A nickel–iron [alloy](alloy.md) with unusually small [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md) over a limited [temperature](temperature-split.md) range. Composition and treatment matter; its name does not mean every specimen has zero expansion. The [Guillaume thermal expansion experiment](guillaume-thermal-expansion-experiment.md) explains how the effect was measured.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1901–1921; Invar and elinvar, lecture 11 December 1920, pp. 451–453: expansion minimum and dependence on composition and treatment](nobel-lectures-physics-1901-1921/invar-and-elinvar-by-charles-edouard-guillaume.md)
