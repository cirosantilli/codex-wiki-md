# DNA double helix

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

A [DNA double helix](dna-double-helix.md) contains two antiparallel, base-paired DNA strands wound around a common axis. Handedness and groove geometry can change between conformational families without changing the basic fact of duplex pairing.
