# Relative state of a bipartite vector

↑ **Parent:** [Pure state](pure-state.md)

For a bipartite vector $|v\rangle\in\mathcal H_R\otimes\mathcal H_A$ and a chosen orthonormal basis of $R$, the generally unnormalized relative state on $A$ is obtained by the partial inner product with one basis vector. It gives the expansion $|v\rangle=\sum_j|j\rangle\otimes|\eta_j\rangle$. The squared norm of a relative state is the probability of its corresponding basis outcome when $|v\rangle$ is normalized. Relative states of eigenvectors of a [Choi matrix](choi-matrix.md) construct the columns of its [Kraus operators](kraus-operator.md).
