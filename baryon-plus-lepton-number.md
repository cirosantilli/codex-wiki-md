# Baryon plus lepton number

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon number](baryon-number.md)

Baryon plus lepton number is the sum of [baryon number](baryon-number.md) and [lepton number](lepton-number.md). It is an accidental classical symmetry of the renormalizable Standard Model but has an $SU(2)_L$ chiral anomaly, so electroweak instantons and sphalerons violate it.
