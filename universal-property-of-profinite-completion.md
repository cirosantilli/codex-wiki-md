# Universal property of profinite completion

↑ **Parent:** [Profinite completion](profinite-completion.md)

Every homomorphism from a group $G$ to a [profinite group](profinite-group.md) $P$ extends uniquely to a continuous homomorphism $\widehat G\to P$. Construct the extension on every finite quotient of $P$ and invoke the [universal property of an inverse limit](universal-property-of-an-inverse-limit.md); uniqueness follows from the density of $G$ in $\widehat G$.
