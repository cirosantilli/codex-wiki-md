# Cox process

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson process](poisson-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cox_process)

A counting process which, conditional on a random nonnegative locally integrable intensity $\Lambda$, is an [Inhomogeneous Poisson process](inhomogeneous-poisson-process.md). For $L_t=\int_0^t\Lambda(s)\,ds$, conditioning gives $\mathbb EN(t)=\mathbb EL_t$ and $\operatorname{Var}N(t)=\mathbb EL_t+\operatorname{Var}L_t$. Thus finite-variance counts are never underdispersed. Randomness of the intensity may induce dependence between increments which are independent conditionally.
