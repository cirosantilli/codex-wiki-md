# Ordinary line

↑ **Parent:** [Incidence geometry](incidence-geometry.md)

Relative to a finite point set, an [ordinary line](ordinary-line.md) is a line containing exactly two points of that set. It is an exact-richness condition, not a count of unordered pairs alone: a line with three points contains three point pairs but is not ordinary. [Point-line duality](point-line-duality.md) turns ordinary lines into intersections incident to exactly two dual lines.
