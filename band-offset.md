# Band offset

↑ **Parent:** [Heterojunction](heterojunction.md)

A [band offset](band-offset.md) is an interface discontinuity in a [conduction band](conduction-band.md) or [valence band](valence-band.md) edge.[§VIII, Fig. 12, pp. 790–791](nobel-lecture-quasielectric-fields-and-band-offsets-teaching-electrons-new-tricks-by-herbert-kroemer.md)

For a chosen two-material example, let the central material A have $E_{c,A}=1$ and $E_{v,A}=0$, and the surrounding material B have $E_{c,B}=1.6$ and $E_{v,B}=-0.4$, in stipulated [energy](energy-split.md) units. Define positive confinement barriers by $\Delta E_c=E_{c,B}-E_{c,A}$ and $\Delta E_v=E_{v,A}-E_{v,B}$. Then $\Delta E_c=0.6$, $\Delta E_v=0.4$, and the [band gaps](band-gap.md) obey

$$
E_{g,B}-E_{g,A}=\Delta E_c+\Delta E_v=1.
$$

This identity determines the sum of the [band offsets](band-offset.md); a [band gap](band-gap.md) difference alone cannot determine their partition. The chosen numbers illustrate the definition and do not specify a measured material pair.
