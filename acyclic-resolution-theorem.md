# Acyclic resolution theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md)

If $0\to\mathcal F\to\mathcal I^0\to\mathcal I^1\to\cdots$ is an exact resolution of [sheaves](sheaf-mathematics.md) and each $\mathcal I^j$ has vanishing positive [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md), then the cohomology of its global-section complex computes $H^q(X,\mathcal F)$. [Fine sheaves](fine-sheaf.md) provide acyclic terms on a paracompact manifold, giving the analytic [Dolbeault resolution of holomorphic differential forms](dolbeault-resolution-of-holomorphic-differential-forms.md).
