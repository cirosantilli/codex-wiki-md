# Added mass of a circular cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Added mass](added-mass.md)

An infinitely long [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) of radius $a$ translating across its axis through otherwise stationary, inviscid incompressible fluid has [added mass](added-mass.md) $m_a=\rho\pi a^2$ per unit length. The decaying, circulation-free [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) is $\phi=-Ua^2\cos\theta/r$. In moving centre coordinates, the fixed-laboratory derivative is $\phi_t=-\dot U a^2\cos\theta/r-U^2a^2\cos(2\theta)/r^2$. The [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md) gives $p-p_\infty=\rho a^2\dot U\cos\theta/r+\rho U^2a^2\cos(2\theta)/r^2-\rho U^2a^4/(2r^4)$. Integrating $-pa\cos\theta$ around $r=a$ yields $F_x=-\rho\pi a^2\dot U$; the transverse force vanishes. Taking a derivative at fixed centre coordinates would omit the necessary convective correction.
