# Pulse-height analyser

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

Electronics or software that classifies detected pulses by amplitude. A selected energy window around a [photopeak](photopeak.md) accepts candidate full-energy events while rejecting part of the scattered background.
