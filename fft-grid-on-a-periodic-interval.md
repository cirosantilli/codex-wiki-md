# FFT grid on a periodic interval

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier spectral method](fourier-spectral-method.md)

Use $N$ equally spaced samples on a period of length $L$, excluding the repeated endpoint. Their spacing is $L/N$, and the [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md) angular wavenumbers are $2\pi m/L$. Including both endpoints in an $N$-point array gives spacing $L/(N-1)$ and is inconsistent with that wavenumber assignment. It does not supply an extra independent periodic sample.
