# Pointed complete partial order

↑ **Parent:** [Directed-complete partial order](directed-complete-partial-order.md)

A [directed-complete partial order](directed-complete-partial-order.md) with a least element. Here directed suprema concern nonempty directed sets. Morphisms are [Scott continuous maps](scott-continuous-map.md) and need not preserve the least element. Requiring strictness would change the category and its cartesian-closure argument.
