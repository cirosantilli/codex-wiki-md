# Cusp-form divisor presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Meromorphic modular form](meromorphic-modular-form.md)

For a torsion-free modular curve with regular [modular cusps](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md), let $f$ be a nonzero meromorphic weight-$k$ form and $C$ the reduced sum of [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) points. The local-order divisor of $f$, minus $C$, imposes exactly holomorphy in the interior and vanishing at every [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) on the product $f\varphi$. Dividing any [cusp form](cusp-form.md) by $f$ gives the reverse identification with a [Riemann-Roch space](riemann-roch-space.md). The [regular-cusp valence formula on a torsion-free modular curve](regular-cusp-valence-formula-on-a-torsion-free-modular-curve.md) and [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) compute the dimension when the resulting divisor has degree greater than the canonical degree.
