# Planetary crust

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

The [planetary crust](planetary-crust.md) is the outer compositional rock layer of a differentiated rocky planet. Its thickness need not equal either the [lithosphere](lithosphere.md)'s thermal thickness or its [effective elastic thickness](effective-elastic-thickness.md).
