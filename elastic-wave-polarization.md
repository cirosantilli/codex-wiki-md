# Elastic wave polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave in an isotropic solid](elastic-wave-in-an-isotropic-solid.md)

The polarization of an [elastic wave in an isotropic solid](elastic-wave-in-an-isotropic-solid.md) specifies the direction of its [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) relative to its [wavevector](wavevector.md). The [longitudinal wave](longitudinal-wave.md) component is parallel to the [wavevector](wavevector.md); the [transverse wave](transverse-wave.md) component is perpendicular. For a real unit propagation vector $n$, $a_L=(a\cdot n)n$ and $a_T=a-a_L$. An evanescent constituent has a complex spatial [derivative](derivative.md) vector, so the corresponding amplitude ratio can be imaginary. The longitudinal and transverse constituents of a [Rayleigh wave](rayleigh-wave.md) combine into [ellipse](ellipse.md) particle motion at the free surface.
