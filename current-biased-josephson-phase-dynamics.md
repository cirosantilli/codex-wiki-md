# Current-biased Josephson phase dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md)

A [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md) can make a collective [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) behave as a measurable dynamical coordinate. In [current-biased Josephson phase dynamics](current-biased-josephson-phase-dynamics.md), the [Gauge-invariant Josephson phase](gauge-invariant-josephson-phase.md) $\phi$ moves in a [tilted Josephson washboard potential](tilted-josephson-washboard-potential.md); the junction's [capacitance](capacitance.md) supplies its effective inertia. A trapped phase gives zero mean [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md), while sustained phase advance produces a nonzero mean [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). [Quantized phase levels in a Josephson junction](quantized-phase-levels-in-a-josephson-junction.md) and [quantum escape of a current-biased Josephson junction](quantum-escape-of-a-current-biased-josephson-junction.md) test [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md) for this collective coordinate.

Begin with a stipulated parallel [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md): an ideal sinusoidal [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md), [capacitance](capacitance.md) $C>0$, and effective [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R>0$. The [Kirchhoff node law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) and [Josephson effect](josephson-effect.md) give

$$
I_b+I_\mu(t)+I_N(t)=I_c\sin\phi+\frac{\hbar}{2eR}\dot\phi+\frac{\hbar C}{2e}\ddot\phi.
$$

Here $I_b$ is the constant bias [electric current](electric-current.md), $I_\mu$ is a deliberately applied [microwave](microwave.md) [electric current](electric-current.md), and $I_N$ represents unwanted fluctuating [electric current](electric-current.md). Multiplication by $\hbar/(2e)$ puts the undriven equation into the mechanical form

$$
M_\phi\ddot\phi+\eta\dot\phi+U'(\phi)=0,\qquad
M_\phi=C\left(\frac{\hbar}{2e}\right)^2,\qquad
\eta=\frac{1}{R}\left(\frac{\hbar}{2e}\right)^2.
$$

The effective inertia is a coefficient for a dimensionless [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md), not a literal material [mass](mass.md) moving through the insulating layer. The capacitor's [energy](energy-split.md), $CV^2/2=M_\phi\dot\phi^2/2$, provides a direct check of that identification. The effective $R$ includes loading by the external [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md); a constant parallel $R$ is an approximation over the relevant [frequency](frequency.md) range.

The useful design freedom is that $I_b$ changes the confinement while $C$ changes its inertia. The same manufactured device can therefore connect a classical oscillation, discrete [energy levels](energy-level.md), and escape to an electrical readout. The [Martinis-Devoret-Clarke circuit experiments](martinis-devoret-clarke-circuit-experiments.md) separate these questions experimentally. [John Clarke](john-clarke.md), [Michel H. Devoret](michel-h-devoret.md) and [John M. Martinis](john-m-martinis.md) shared the 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics for circuit experiments establishing macroscopic [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) and discrete [energy levels](energy-level.md).[7 October 2025, p.1, award decision](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2025-press-release-by-royal-swedish-academy-of-sciences.md)
