# Optical halo

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric optics](atmospheric-optics.md)

An [optical halo](optical-halo.md) is a ring, arc or other light pattern formed by [refraction](refraction.md) or [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) in atmospheric ice crystals. It is distinct from a dark-matter halo.
