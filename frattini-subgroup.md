# Frattini subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Finite p-group](finite-p-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frattini_subgroup)

The Frattini subgroup of a finite p-group is the intersection of its maximal subgroups. It equals $G^p[G,G]$.
