# Product Laplacian eigenspace decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian product](riemannian-product.md)

For [closed manifolds](closed-manifold.md) with the product [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) is the sum of the two factor operators. Products of their [orthonormal eigenbases](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) form a complete basis, and [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) add. Hence the product [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) at $\lambda$ is the displayed finite orthogonal sum of [tensor products](tensor-product.md). The discrete assertion requires a discrete spectral setting.
