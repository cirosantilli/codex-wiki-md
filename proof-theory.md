# Proof theory

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_theory)

Proof theory studies [formal proofs](formal-proof.md) and the deductive systems that produce them. It examines which conclusions follow from [axioms](axiom.md), how proofs can be transformed, and what those transformations reveal about the chosen logic. [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) organizes a proof through introduction and elimination rules for [logical connectives](logical-connective.md) and quantifiers.
