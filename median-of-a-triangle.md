# Median of a triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

A median joins a [vertex of a polygon](vertex-of-a-polygon.md) to the midpoint of the opposite side of a [triangle](triangle.md). For side lengths $a,b,c$, the median to the side of length $c$ has length $m_c=\tfrac12\sqrt{2a^2+2b^2-c^2}$, by [Apollonius's theorem](apollonius-s-theorem.md).
