# Reflected-to-transmitted transverse-electric amplitude ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse-electric Fresnel reflection coefficient](transverse-electric-fresnel-reflection-coefficient.md)

For equal permeabilities on the two sides of an interface, tangential-field continuity and [Snell law for electromagnetic waves](snell-law-for-electromagnetic-waves.md) give

$$
\frac{E_R}{E_T}
=\frac12\left(1-\frac{\tan\theta_R}{\tan\theta_T}\right)
$$

for consistently signed electric amplitudes normal to the plane of incidence.
