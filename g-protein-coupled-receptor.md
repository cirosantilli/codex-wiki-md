# G protein-coupled receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Metabotropic receptor](metabotropic-receptor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G_protein-coupled_receptor)

A [G protein-coupled receptor](g-protein-coupled-receptor.md) is a [cell-surface receptor](cell-surface-receptor.md) that relays activation through a [G protein](g-protein.md) on the intracellular side of the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md). The activated [G protein](g-protein.md) can regulate an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) or [ion channel](ion-channel.md), so a [GPCR](g-protein-coupled-receptor.md) supplies a concrete [metabotropic receptor](metabotropic-receptor.md) example: [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) recognition and the eventual response are connected through distinct components. Different [G proteins](g-protein.md) couple to different targets. See [Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition: signaling through G-protein-linked cell-surface receptors](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26912/).
