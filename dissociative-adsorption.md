# Dissociative adsorption

↑ **Parent:** [Langmuir adsorption model](langmuir-adsorption-model.md)

In [dissociative adsorption](dissociative-adsorption.md), an arriving [molecule](molecule-split.md) splits into bound fragments. For the idealized process $A_2+2*\rightleftharpoons2A*$, let $*$ denote a vacant [adsorption site](adsorption-site.md) and let $\theta$ count sites occupied by $A$. A [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md) with [independent](independent-random-variables.md) vacancies takes the forward rate proportional to $P(1-\theta)^2$ and the reverse rate proportional to $\theta^2$. Equating them yields

$$
\frac{\theta}{1-\theta}=\sqrt{KP},\qquad
\theta=\frac{\sqrt{KP}}{1+\sqrt{KP}},
$$

with a positive coefficient $K$ of inverse-[pressure](pressure-split.md) units that includes the chosen pair-counting convention. This square-root dependence illustrates why the intact-[molecule](molecule-split.md) [Langmuir isotherm](langmuir-adsorption-model.md) cannot simply be assumed for splitting [molecules](molecule-split.md). Requirements for neighboring vacancies or interactions can further change the [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md).
