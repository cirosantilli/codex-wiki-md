# Under-moderated reactor

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md)

A [nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md) on the side of a specified moderation optimum where adding moderator increases neutron multiplication. Removing water then tends to reduce [reactor reactivity](reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md), under the comparison's other fixed conditions. The optimum and the derivative depend on composition, geometry and operating state.[Module NP-03, pp.24–25, Moderator Effects](nuclear-physics-and-reactor-theory-volume-2-by-the-united-states-department-of-energy.md)
