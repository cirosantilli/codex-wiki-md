# Rest mass of a coalescing relativistic collision

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md)

When two massive particles coalesce without additional outgoing radiation, [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) makes the final [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) their sum. For collinear incident [velocities](velocity.md), its [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) is the positive square root of the displayed invariant, and its final [velocity](velocity.md) equals the [center-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md) velocity $c^2p_{\rm total}/E_{\rm total}$. Relative [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) can become internal [rest energy](rest-energy.md), so the final [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) is generally larger than the sum of the incident [rest masses](invariant-mass.md).
