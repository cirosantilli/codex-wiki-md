# Size-exclusion characterization of chain polymers

↑ **Parent:** [Chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md)

[Size-exclusion characterization of chain polymers](size-exclusion-characterization-of-chain-polymers.md) separates dissolved chains using porous columns and records an elution signal. Calibrating that separation against appropriate standards supplies a size-related estimate of molecular masses. Detector response and calibration must accompany any claimed molecular distribution: an elution trace is not automatically a histogram of molecule counts or [degree of polymerization](degree-of-polymerization.md). Similar signals can conceal chemically different chain populations, while a shift toward larger apparent size alone does not show that every chain retained a [polymerization-active site](polymerization-active-site.md).
