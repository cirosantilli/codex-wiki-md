# Monodromy eigenfunction Laurent representation

↑ **Parent:** [Monodromy](monodromy.md)

For a linear [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) with [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) coefficients on a punctured disc, analytic continuation of a basis gives an invertible [monodromy](monodromy.md) matrix. A nonzero [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda=e^{2\pi i\sigma}$ selects a solution $w$ for which $z^{-\sigma}w$ is single-valued and [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md). Its [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) gives $w=z^\sigma\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}c_nz^n$. No regular-singular hypothesis is needed; infinitely many negative powers may occur.
