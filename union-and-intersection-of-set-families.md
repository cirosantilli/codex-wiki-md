# Union and intersection of set families

↑ **Parent:** [Set family](set-family.md)

For [set families](set-family.md), $\mathcal A\vee\mathcal B=\{A\cup B:A\in\mathcal A,B\in\mathcal B\}$ and $\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal B=\{A\cap B:A\in\mathcal A,B\in\mathcal B\}$. These are families of distinct sets, rather than multisets indexed by pairs. The [four functions theorem](ahlswede-daykin-inequality.md) gives $|\mathcal A\vee\mathcal B|\,|\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal B|\ge|\mathcal A|\,|\mathcal B|$. Complementing one input family yields the analogous product bound for pairwise [set differences](set-difference.md).
