# Amenability of a free product

↑ **Parent:** [Amenable group](amenable-group.md)

For nontrivial groups $A,B$, the [free product](free-product.md) $A*B$ is amenable exactly in the exceptional case $A\cong B\cong C_2$. In that case it is the [infinite dihedral group](infinite-dihedral-group.md), hence a [virtually cyclic group](virtually-cyclic-group.md) and amenable. In every other case the [normal form theorem for a free product](normal-form-theorem-for-a-free-product.md) and the [ping-pong lemma](ping-pong-lemma.md) produce a subgroup isomorphic to $F_2$; the fact that [a subgroup of an amenable group is amenable](a-subgroup-of-an-amenable-group-is-amenable.md) then obstructs amenability.
