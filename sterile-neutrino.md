# Sterile neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)

A neutral [fermion](fermion.md) without the ordinary active-neutrino weak gauge couplings. Mixing can still let it interact or decay. Its possible dark-matter role depends on lifetime, production and [free streaming](free-streaming.md), rather than on Majorana character alone.
