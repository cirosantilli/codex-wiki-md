# Discrete subset

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete space](discrete-space.md)

A subset of a [topological space](topological-space.md) is discrete when its [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md) is a [discrete topology](discrete-space.md): every point has an ambient neighbourhood meeting the subset only in that point. A discrete subset need not be closed; for example $\{1/n:n\geq1\}$ in the real line accumulates at zero. The group structure makes a [discrete subgroup](discrete-subgroup.md) of a Hausdorff [topological group](topological-group-split.md) closed, an additional property used in discontinuity arguments.
