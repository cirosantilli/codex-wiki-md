# Nonpolarizing beam splitter

↑ **Parent:** [Beam splitter](beam-splitter.md)

A [nonpolarizing beam splitter](nonpolarizing-beam-splitter.md) is designed for approximately equal power splitting ratios for the two orthogonal [linear polarizations](linear-polarization.md), at specified [wavelengths](wavelength.md) and [angle of incidence](angle-of-incidence.md). It need not be 50:50, lossless, or have equal transmission and reflection [wave phases](phase-waves.md) for both [linear polarizations](linear-polarization.md). A scalar [beam splitter scattering matrix](beam-splitter-scattering-matrix.md) therefore still requires a specified [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) and operating range. [Polarizing beam splitters](polarizing-beam-splitter.md) instead aim to separate those [linear polarizations](linear-polarization.md) into different paths.
