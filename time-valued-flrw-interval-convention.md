# Time-valued FLRW interval convention

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md)

A cosmological metric can measure the interval in units of [time](time-in-physics.md) rather than [length](length.md). With [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) comoving spatial coordinates, its [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) $R$ then has units of [length](length.md) and a comoving clock has $ds=dt$. Proper spatial [length](length.md) is $c\sqrt{-ds^2}$ at constant [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md). This convention is equivalent to the more common length-valued interval $d\ell^2=c^2dt^2-R^2d\Sigma_k^2$, not a different cosmology.
