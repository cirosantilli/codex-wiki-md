# Rabi frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Rabi cycle](rabi-cycle.md)

The coherent coupling frequency defined by the chosen driven [two-level system](two-level-system.md) [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). With $H=\hbar(-\Delta\sigma_z+\Omega\sigma_x)/2$, resonance gives population oscillations proportional to $\sin^2(\Omega t/2)$. Detuning replaces the oscillation frequency by $\sqrt{\Omega^2+\Delta^2}$ and reduces its contrast. Stating the Hamiltonian avoids factor-of-two ambiguities in the definition of $\Omega$.
