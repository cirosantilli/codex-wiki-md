# Basic statement

↑ **Parent:** [Falsificationism](falsificationism.md)

An intersubjectively testable report of an observable occurrence in specified circumstances, capable of participating in a test or attempted falsification. In [Karl Popper](karl-popper.md)'s account, accepting a basic statement is provisional and may be reopened by further testing.[Sections 28–30](the-logic-of-scientific-discovery-by-karl-popper.md)
