# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the optimisation for data science course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [optimisation for data science course of the University of Oxford](../optimisation-for-data-science-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, sat on 7 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 300–303; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=300). The original question numbering is retained without reproducing the question statements. Two printing errors on paper page 3 are addressed explicitly in parts 2(c) and 2(f).

Unless a subscript indicates the [L1 norm](../l1-norm.md), $\|\cdot\|$ denotes the [Euclidean norm](../euclidean-norm.md). In Question 1, the [regularization](../regularization.md) weight is $\lambda\geq0$ and the [step size](../step-size.md) is positive. The [big O notation](../big-o-notation.md) in part 1(d) is interpreted for fixed $x$ as the [step size](../step-size.md) tends to zero, with the necessary [subdifferential](../subdifferential.md) qualification explained there.
