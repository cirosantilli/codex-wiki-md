<h1 id="su-3-group">SU(3) group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md)

The [SU(3) group](su-3-group.md) consists of unitary complex three-by-three matrices of determinant one. Its defining action on $\mathbb C^3$ preserves the Hermitian norm. It contains block-diagonal copies of [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) and the real [SO(3) group](so-3-group.md). Its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) consists of traceless anti-Hermitian matrices.
