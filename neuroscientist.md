<h4 id="santiago-ramon-y-cajal">Santiago Ramón y Cajal</h4>

↑ **Parent:** [List of neuroanatomists](#list-of-neuroanatomists)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_Ramón_y_Cajal)

His drawings of [neurons](neuron.md) made their branching structure visible with extraordinary clarity. The historical illustration under [Purkinje cell](neuron.md#purkinje-cell) shows how different [neurons](neuron.md) can occupy the same part of the [cerebellum](neuroscience.md#cerebellum) while having strikingly different shapes.
