# Semi-empirical mass formula

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-empirical_mass_formula)

The [semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md) approximates [nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md) using volume, surface, electrostatic, neutron-proton asymmetry, and [nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md) contributions. At fixed [mass number](mass-number.md), its quadratic dependence on [atomic number](atomic-number.md) gives a [nuclear mass parabola](nuclear-mass-parabola.md). It describes broad trends and does not resolve individual [nuclear shell](nuclear-shell.md) effects.
