# Power Spectrum of Light Scattered by Two-Level Systems by B. R. Mollow

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by B. R. Mollow](work-by-b-r-mollow.md)

Published in Physical Review 188, starting at page 1969, on 25 December 1969: [https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.188.1969](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.188.1969)

The primary calculation obtains the near-resonant driven-atom fluorescence spectrum from two-time atomic dipole correlations, with radiation damping included through coupling to the quantized electromagnetic field. It provides the source for the [Mollow triplet](mollow-triplet.md).
