# Darcy thermal-time Rayleigh number

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy-Bénard convection](darcy-benard-convection.md)

With permeability $K$, depth $d$, [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu$ and the relevant effective [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md) $\kappa_T$, the Darcy [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md) compares [buoyancy](buoyancy.md)-driven Darcy [advection](advection.md) to thermal diffusion. It differs from the clear-fluid [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md) by replacing $d^3$ with $Kd$. Effective heat capacity and porosity factors may be absorbed into the definition of $\kappa_T$ and the thermal-time scale, and must be kept consistent with the chosen [Darcy law](darcy-law.md) convention.
