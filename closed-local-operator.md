# Closed local operator

↑ **Parent:** [Lawvere-Tierney topology](lawvere-tierney-topology.md)

For a [subterminal object](subterminal-object.md) classified by $u$, the closed local operator is $p\mapsto u\vee p$. Its closed monos contain the pullback of that subterminal, hence are exactly the monos dense for the corresponding [open local operator](open-local-operator.md).
