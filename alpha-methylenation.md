# Alpha-methylenation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Installation of an exocyclic or terminal methylene at the alpha position of a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) compound. Hydroxymethylation/dehydration or Mannich-base elimination can provide examples.
