# Periodic diffusion on a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

For a circle of circumference $2\pi$, the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) $u_t=Du_{xx}$ has periodic matching of both $u$ and $u_x$ at $x=\pm\pi$. For $D>0$, the displayed [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) solves the initial-value problem, with $\bar f=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{-\pi}^\pi f(x)dx$ and the usual real [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md). Every nonconstant [normal mode](normal-mode.md) decays, leaving the conserved mean. In particular $f(x)=(\pi-|x|)^2$ gives $\bar f=\pi^2/3$, $a_n=4/n^2$ and $b_n=0$. The initial cusp is smoothed for every positive time.
