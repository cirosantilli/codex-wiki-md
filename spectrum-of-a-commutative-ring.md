# Spectrum of a commutative ring

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

The spectrum $\operatorname{Spec}A$ of a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $A$ is the set of its [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md), with closed sets $V(I)=\{\mathfrak p:I\subseteq\mathfrak p\}$ and a structure sheaf whose sections locally look like fractions.
