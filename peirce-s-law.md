<h1 id="peirce-s-law">Peirce's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Classical propositional logic](classical-propositional-logic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peirce's_law)

Assume $H=(P\to Q)\to P$ and temporarily assume $\neg P$. A further assumption $P$ yields a contradiction and hence $Q$ by the [logical falsity elimination rule](logical-falsity-elimination-rule.md); discharge it to obtain $P\to Q$. Applying $H$ gives $P$, contradicting $\neg P$. Discharge the latter assumption to obtain $\neg\neg P$, apply [double-negation elimination](double-negation-elimination.md), and finally discharge $H$. This is a derivation from the primitive classical [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) rules. The principle is named for [Charles Sanders Peirce](charles-sanders-peirce.md).
