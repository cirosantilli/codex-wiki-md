# Worldvolume

↑ **Parent:** [Brane](brane.md)

The worldvolume is the history of a [brane](brane.md). An embedding $X:\Sigma\to\mathcal M$ maps its local coordinates into target [spacetime](spacetime.md). A string [worldvolume](worldvolume.md) is a [worldsheet](worldsheet.md).
