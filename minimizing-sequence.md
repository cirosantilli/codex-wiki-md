# Minimizing sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Direct method in the calculus of variations](direct-method-in-the-calculus-of-variations.md)

A [minimizing sequence](minimizing-sequence.md) satisfies $E(u_n)\to\inf E$. [Coercivity](coercive-function.md) bounds an appropriate [sublevel set](sublevel-set.md), [Compactness](compact-space.md) supplies a [convergent subsequence](convergent-subsequence.md), and [sequential lower semicontinuity](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md) turns its limit into a [global minimizer](global-minimizer.md).
