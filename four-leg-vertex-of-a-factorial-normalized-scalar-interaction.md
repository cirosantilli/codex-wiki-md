# Four-leg vertex of a factorial-normalized scalar interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman rule](feynman-rule.md)

An interaction $\mathcal L_{\rm int}=+\lambda\phi^4/4!$ produces a four-leg momentum-space vertex $i\lambda$. The $4!$ assignments of external legs cancel the coefficient factorial. With a negative interaction sign the vertex is $-i\lambda$. Graph [Feynman-diagram symmetry factors](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) are a separate combinatorial issue.
