# Conditional lognormal layer recovery

↑ **Parent:** [Limited excess of loss reinsurance](limited-excess-of-loss-reinsurance.md)

For a [lognormal distribution](log-normal-distribution.md), let $d_j(t)=(\log t-\mu-j\sigma^2)/\sigma$ for $j=0,1$. The [truncated lognormal moment](truncated-lognormal-moment.md) formula gives the recovery numerator

$$
e^{\mu+\sigma^2/2}[\Phi(d_1(M+A))-\Phi(d_1(M))]-M[\Phi(d_0(M+A))-\Phi(d_0(M))]+A[1-\Phi(d_0(M+A))].
$$

Divide by $1-\Phi(d_0(M))$ to condition on $X>M$. This handles both the partially penetrating claims and the capped payments beyond the upper attachment.
