# Hot isostatic pressing

↑ **Parent:** [Hot pressing](hot-pressing.md)

Consolidation under approximately equal applied pressure in all directions at elevated [temperature](temperature-split.md). In waste treatment it can produce a dense [ceramic](ceramic.md) inside a containment can.
