# Normalizer condition for nilpotent groups

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md)

Choose the first term of the [upper central series](upper-central-series.md) not contained in $H$. The preceding term lies in $H$, so an element of the new term outside $H$ commutes with $H$ modulo that preceding term and normalizes $H$.
