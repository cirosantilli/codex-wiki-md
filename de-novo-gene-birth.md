# De novo gene birth

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)

Origin of a new coding [gene](gene.md) from ancestrally [non-coding DNA](non-coding-dna.md), rather than divergence from a previously coding copy. Failure to detect a distant relative is insufficient evidence: comparative genomic context and evidence of ancestral coding status matter.
