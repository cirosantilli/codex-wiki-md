# Measure-preserving parametrization of one-dimensional transport maps

↑ **Parent:** [Transport map](transport-map.md)

When source and target have continuous strictly increasing cumulative distributions, all [transport maps](transport-map.md) are obtained by choosing a [Lebesgue-measure-preserving map](lebesgue-measure-preserving-map.md) $S$ on the unit interval. The maps $F$ and $G$ turn the two [measures](measure.md) into uniform [measure](measure.md). The choice $S=\mathrm{id}$ gives the [monotone rearrangement](monotone-rearrangement.md).
