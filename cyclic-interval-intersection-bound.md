# Cyclic interval intersection bound

↑ **Parent:** [Katona circle method](katona-circle-method.md)

An [intersecting family](intersecting-family.md) of cyclic intervals of length $r$ in a cyclic order of $n$ positions has at most $r$ members when $1\leq r\leq n/2$. Rotate one selected interval to end at position $n$. Intervals ending at $r,\ldots,n-r$ miss it. Pair the remaining endpoints, other than $n$, as $(j,j+n-r)$ for $1\leq j\leq r-1$; each pair represents two disjoint intervals, hence contributes at most one member. This gives $1+(r-1)=r$. Counting such intervals over all [permutations](permutation.md) proves the [Erdős-Ko-Rado theorem](erdos-ko-rado-theorem.md).
