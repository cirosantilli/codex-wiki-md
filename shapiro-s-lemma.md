<h1 id="shapiro-s-lemma">Shapiro's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Coinduced module](coinduced-module.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shapiro's_lemma)

[Shapiro's lemma](shapiro-s-lemma.md) gives a natural isomorphism

$$
H^n\!\left(G,\operatorname{Coind}_K^G X\right)\cong H^n(K,X).
$$

It follows by applying the [Hom functor adjunction for a coinduced module](hom-functor-adjunction-for-a-coinduced-module.md) to a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) and observing that restriction from $G$ to $K$ preserves [projective modules](projective-module.md).
