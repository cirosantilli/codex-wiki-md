# Bernstein inequality for Weyl algebra modules

↑ **Parent:** [Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module](bernstein-growth-dimension-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md)

For a nonzero finitely generated [module](module-mathematics.md) over the complex [Weyl algebra](weyl-algebra.md) $A_n$, its [Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module](bernstein-growth-dimension-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md) is at least $n$. The [faithful finite-step action of a Weyl algebra](faithful-finite-step-action-of-a-weyl-algebra.md) gives $\dim F_jA_n\leq(\dim M_j)(\dim M_{2j})$. Comparing degrees $2n$ and $2d(M)$ proves the inequality. The polynomial [module](module-mathematics.md) has dimension $n$, so the bound is sharp. This is an algebraic growth inequality, distinct from probabilistic Bernstein inequalities.
