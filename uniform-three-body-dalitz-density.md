# Uniform three-body Dalitz density

↑ **Parent:** [Dalitz plot](dalitz-plot.md)

After integrating the [overall orientation](rotation-mathematics.md) of a scalar-parent [three-body decay](relativistic-three-body-decay.md), the [Lorentz-invariant phase space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) is constant in two pairwise squared [invariant masses](invariant-mass.md). The two-body [factorization](factorization.md) $d\Phi_3=ds\,d\Phi_2(M;\sqrt s,m_2)d\Phi_2(\sqrt s;m_1,m_3)/(2\pi)$ and the relative-angle [Jacobian](jacobian-determinant.md) give the displayed density. The event density includes $|\mathcal M|^2/(2M)$ and need not be flat: a [resonance](resonance.md) produces bands. Identical-particle counting supplies its usual global [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md).
