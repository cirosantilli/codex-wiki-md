# Oil drop experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary charge](elementary-charge.md)

The [oil drop experiment](oil-drop-experiment.md) infers an oil drop's [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) from its motion under [gravity](gravity.md), [buoyancy](buoyancy.md), [drag](drag-physics.md) and a calibrated [electric field](electric-field-split.md). Repeated changes of a single drop's [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) reveal discrete steps; see the model, experimental procedure and limitations in [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md).
