# Moment of inertia of a uniform disc

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

For a uniform disc of radius $a$ and mass $M$, annuli of radius $r$ and thickness $dr$ have mass $(M/\pi a^2)2\pi r\,dr$. Their axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) sums to $I=\int_0^a r^2\,dm=Ma^2/2$.
