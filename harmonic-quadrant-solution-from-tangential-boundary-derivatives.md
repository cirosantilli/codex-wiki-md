# Harmonic quadrant solution from tangential boundary derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz integral formula](schwarz-integral-formula.md)

For a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) in the quadrant with boundary derivatives $u_y(0,y)=g_1(y)$ and $u_x(x,0)=g_2(x)$, integrating the data to equal corner values and applying the [Schwarz integral formula](schwarz-integral-formula.md) after the [conformal map](conformal-map.md) $w=z^2$ gives

$$
u_z(z)=\frac z{\pi i}\left[\int_0^\infty\frac{g_2(r)}{r^2-z^2}\,dr+\int_0^\infty\frac{g_1(r)}{r^2+z^2}\,dr\right].
$$

These are [tangential boundary derivatives](tangential-boundary-derivative.md), so uniqueness requires an additional normalization or growth restriction. A homogeneous contribution has the form $ih(z^2)/z$ with $h$ [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) in the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) and real on its boundary away from zero. For example, $u=xy$ has zero prescribed derivatives on both edges.
