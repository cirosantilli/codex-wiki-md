# Complementarity from two directed subspace angles

↑ **Parent:** [Directed subspace angle](directed-subspace-angle.md)

For [closed subspaces of a Hilbert space](closed-subspace-of-a-hilbert-space.md), set $T=P_{V^\perp}|_W$. The two positive cosines bound $T$ and its [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) $P_W|_{V^\perp}$ below. The first bound gives a closed range and [injectivity](injective-function.md); the second makes the range dense by [image-kernel orthogonality for an adjoint](image-kernel-orthogonality-for-an-adjoint.md). Thus $T$ is a bounded bijection, and $w=T^{-1}P_{V^\perp}f$ yields the unique [direct sum](direct-sum.md) decomposition $f=w+v$. In equal finite dimensions, a lower bound on $T$ alone suffices by the [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md).
