# Phonological linkage hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Phonics](phonics.md)

The proposal that phonological work benefits [reading](reading.md) most when linked to [reading instruction](reading-instruction.md). A matched-group intervention found greater [reading](reading.md) progress for integrated work, even though phonology-only work particularly improved phonological tasks.[Child Development 65, 41–57; matched groups of seven-year-old poor readers, reading/phonology/combined/control conditions](ameliorating-early-reading-failure-by-integrating-the-teaching-of-reading-and-phonological-skills-the-phonological-linkage-hypothesis-by-peter-j-hatcher-and-others.md)
