# Rank-one dephasing

↑ **Parent:** [Nonselective projective measurement](nonselective-projective-measurement.md)

In an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), rank-one dephasing is the [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) $\Delta(\rho)=\sum_i|\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|\rho|\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|$. It removes off-diagonal entries while preserving the basis probabilities. Its output [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) is their [Shannon entropy](information-entropy.md), and the [relative-entropy identity for rank-one dephasing](relative-entropy-identity-for-rank-one-dephasing.md) quantifies the entropy increase.
