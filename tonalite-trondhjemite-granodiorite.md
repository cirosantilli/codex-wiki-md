# Tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite

↑ **Parent:** [Petrology](petrology.md)

A family of felsic plutonic rocks prominent in studies of early [continental crust](continental-crust.md). Melting hydrous mafic sources can generate related compositions, but the rock name alone does not establish [subduction](subduction.md).
