# Nambu-Goto phase-space action

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu–Goto action](nambu-goto-action.md)

The first-order string action is

$$
I=\int dt\,d\sigma\left[\dot X\cdot P-\frac e2(P^2+T^2X'^2)-uX'\cdot P\right].
$$

The multipliers impose the two [Virasoro constraints](virasoro-constraint.md). The canonical equations give a spatial momentum flux $J_m=eT^2X'_m+uP_m$.
