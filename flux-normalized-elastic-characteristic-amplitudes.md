# Flux-normalized elastic characteristic amplitudes

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic impedance](seismic-impedance.md)

These characteristic [wave amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) split a one-dimensional [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md) into forward and backward components. Their instantaneous net [elastic-wave energy flux](elastic-wave-energy-flux.md) is $\phi_+^2-\phi_-^2$. For real-part harmonic phasors the mean flux is $(|\phi_+|^2-|\phi_-|^2)/2$. In a static graded medium with real positive [seismic impedance](seismic-impedance.md), put $g=(\log Z)'/2$; then $\partial_x\phi_\pm\pm\beta^{-1}\partial_t\phi_\pm=g\phi_\mp$. [Seismic impedance](seismic-impedance.md) [gradients](gradient.md) couple the two directions while preserving net flux.
