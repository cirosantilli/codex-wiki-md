# Monin-Obukhov length

↑ **Parent:** [Monin-Obukhov similarity theory](monin-obukhov-similarity-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monin–Obukhov_length)

The signed wall-turbulence length formed from stress velocity and the [vertical turbulent buoyancy flux](vertical-turbulent-buoyancy-flux.md). With upward buoyancy transport positive, stable cooling has $B_0<0$ and $\ell_O>0$; heating from below gives $\ell_O<0$. The magnitude marks the transition between shear-dominated and buoyancy-dominated scaling.
