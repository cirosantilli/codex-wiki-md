# Proper divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor](divisor.md)

Here a [proper divisor](proper-divisor.md) of a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$ means a positive [integer divisor](divisor.md) other than $n$ itself. Thus $1$ has no [proper divisors](proper-divisor.md), and the [proper divisors](proper-divisor.md) of $12$ are $1,2,3,4,6$.
