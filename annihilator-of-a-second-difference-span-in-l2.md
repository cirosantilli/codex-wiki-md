# Annihilator of a second-difference span in l2

↑ **Parent:** [Closed linear span](closed-linear-span.md)

Let $M$ be the closed span of these vectors in the real [l2 sequence space](l2-sequence-space.md). A vector $\beta$ is orthogonal to $M$ exactly when $\beta_{k+2}-2r\beta_{k+1}+\beta_k=0$. The characteristic roots are $r\pm\sqrt{r^2-1}$; one grows geometrically and the other, $\rho$, lies in $(0,1)$. Square summability eliminates the growing solution, so $M^\perp=\operatorname{span}\{(\rho^{j-1})_{j\geq1}\}$. Hence $M$ is a closed hyperplane, namely the kernel of the bounded functional $\alpha\mapsto\sum_j\alpha_j\rho^{j-1}$. This combines a recurrence with [density by annihilators](density-by-annihilators.md).
