# Coclosed differential form

↑ **Parent:** [Codifferential](codifferential.md)

A [differential form](differential-form-split.md) is coclosed when its [codifferential](codifferential.md) vanishes. On a closed [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), a form is a [harmonic differential form](harmonic-differential-form.md) exactly when it is both a [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) and coclosed: the identity $\langle\Delta\alpha,\alpha\rangle=\|d\alpha\|^2+\|\delta\alpha\|^2$ proves the converse as well as necessity.
