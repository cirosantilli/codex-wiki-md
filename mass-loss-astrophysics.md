# Mass loss (astrophysics)

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)

Loss of matter from a star or other astronomical system, for example through a [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md) or explosive ejection. [Mass loss rate](mass-loss-rate.md) conventions must distinguish the positive ejected rate $|\dot M|$ from the negative change of the retained [mass](mass.md), $\dot M$. In a [binary star](binary-star.md), the fate of an orbit also depends on the [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) carried away.
