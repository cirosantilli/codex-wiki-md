# Fermi-surface reconstruction in a weak square potential

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md)

For a free circle of radius $R$ and square reciprocal period $Q$ with $\sqrt2R<Q<2R$, the circle cuts the faces but not the corners of the first zone. Weak Bragg gaps reconnect the folded branches into two inequivalent edge-centred [electron pockets](electron-pocket.md) and one corner-centred [hole pocket](hole-pocket.md). The zero-potential limit retains band crossings instead of avoided crossings.
