# Arithmetic-harmonic bounds for dielectric capacitance

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitance](capacitance.md)

For a [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md) of area $A$ and separation $d$, filled by a spatially varying positive scalar [permittivity](permittivity-split.md), neglect edge fields. At fixed terminal voltage, the physical [electric potential](electric-potential.md) minimizes $\int\varepsilon|\nabla V|^2\,d\mathcal V/2$. A linear trial potential gives the arithmetic upper bound. At fixed plate charge, minimizing $\int|\mathbf D|^2/(2\varepsilon)\,d\mathcal V$ over divergence-free [electric displacement fields](electric-displacement-field.md) gives the harmonic lower bound by using a uniform trial displacement. The averages are volume averages. Layers parallel to the field attain the arithmetic bound; layers perpendicular to the field attain the harmonic bound. Equal volumes of vacuum and relative [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\epsilon$ therefore give $2\epsilon C_0/(1+\epsilon)\leq C\leq(1+\epsilon)C_0/2$, where $C_0=\varepsilon_0A/d$.
