# Effective radius

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effective_radius)

The projected [radius](radius.md) enclosing half an object's total [luminosity](luminosity.md) in a specified [photometric passband](photometric-passband.md). For circular apertures, the mean linear [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) inside it is $\mathcal I_e=L/(2\pi R_e^2)$. Elliptical apertures require an axis-ratio factor or a circularized [radius](radius.md); that convention must be stated when comparing calibrations.
