# Seifert circle

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert algorithm](seifert-algorithm.md)

A Seifert circle is a component of the crossing-free diagram obtained by [oriented smoothing](oriented-smoothing.md) at every crossing. These circles bound the disks used in the [Seifert algorithm](seifert-algorithm.md).
