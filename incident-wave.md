# Incident wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave scattering](wave-scattering.md)

The [incident wave](incident-wave.md) is the prescribed background solution that illuminates a scatterer. It solves the unperturbed [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md) in scalar time-harmonic models. The scattered contribution is defined by subtracting this field from the total field; reflected waves from a planar boundary are included unless a separate reference reflection is explicitly removed.
