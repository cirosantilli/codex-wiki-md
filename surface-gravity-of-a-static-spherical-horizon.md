# Surface gravity of a static spherical horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity](surface-gravity.md)

For a [static spacetime](static-spacetime.md) with $g_{tt}=-A(r)$, $g_{rr}=1/B(r)$ and simple simultaneous horizon zeros, the squared [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) of $\partial_t$ is $\lim BA'^2/(4A)=A'(r_+)B'(r_+)/4$. Choose the positive value for the outer horizon. A rescaling of the time generator rescales its [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md).
