# Centraliser of a fixed-point-free involution

↑ **Parent:** [Centralizer](centralizer.md)

Commutation with a fixed-point-free involution preserves its partner relation. A commuting [permutation](permutation.md) can permute the $m$ pairs and independently swap each pair. This gives the [permutation wreath product](permutation-wreath-product.md) with order $2^m m!$.
