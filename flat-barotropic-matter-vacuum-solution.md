# Flat barotropic matter-vacuum solution

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

For positive constant [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), zero [cosmological spatial curvature](spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) and a separate positive-density fluid with constant [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w>-1$, set $H_\Lambda=\sqrt{\Lambda/3}$ in the time-unit convention. Eliminating the fluid [density](density.md) gives $\dot H=-3(1+w)(H^2-H_\Lambda^2)/2$. On the expanding [Big Bang](big-bang.md) branch,

$$
H=H_\Lambda\coth\left[\frac32(1+w)H_\Lambda t\right],\qquad
a\propto\sinh^{2/[3(1+w)]}\left[\frac32(1+w)H_\Lambda t\right].
$$

The origin is the [Big Bang](big-bang.md). The fluid's early-time power-law expansion approaches exponential vacuum domination at late times.
