# Resonance-temperature dependence of dielectronic recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectronic recombination](dielectronic-recombination.md)

For temperature-independent atomic rates and an isolated positive-energy resonance, [dielectronic recombination](dielectronic-recombination.md) has the displayed dependence. Differentiating its logarithm gives a maximum at $kT=2E_d/3$. A sum of resonances can have several peaks, and near-threshold resonances change the low-temperature behavior. Collisional destruction of captured high levels can introduce density dependence.
