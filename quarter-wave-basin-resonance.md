# Quarter-wave basin resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Seiche](seiche.md)

An ideal uniform shallow basin, closed at one end and held at zero displacement at its open end, has its lowest [standing wave](standing-wave.md) with a displacement [node](node-dynamical-systems.md) at the opening and an [antinode](antinode.md) at the closed end. Its [wavelength](wavelength.md) is four times its length. Real mouths need not satisfy this perfectly reflecting idealization.
