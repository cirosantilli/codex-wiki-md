# Parallel RLC bandwidth

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel RLC circuit](parallel-rlc-circuit.md)

For the ratio of resistor-current [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) to total-current [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) to exceed $1/\sqrt2$, $|\omega C-1/(\omega L)|<1/R$. The exact positive endpoint difference is $\Delta\omega=1/(RC)$ and the [quality factor](q-factor.md) is $Q=R\sqrt{C/L}$. Greater shunt resistance means weaker dissipation, unlike increasing a series resistance.
