# Magnetic Reynolds number

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

The magnetic Reynolds number compares field advection with resistive [magnetic diffusion](magnetic-diffusion.md). With constant [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) and vacuum permeability, magnetic diffusivity is $\eta_m=1/(\mu_0\sigma)$ and $\operatorname{Rm}=UL/\eta_m$. The ideal induction approximation applies when this ratio is large on the scales of interest.
