# Functional necessity and sufficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Causal inference](causal-inference-split.md)

Necessity and sufficiency are conditional on the task, state and [intervention](treatment.md). A selective loss-of-function effect can support necessity under those conditions; an imposed pattern producing an effect can support sufficiency. Neither automatically proves the naturally used code, and compensation can make a null loss-of-function result inconclusive.
