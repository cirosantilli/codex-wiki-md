# Higher product bound for an approximate group

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate group](approximate-group.md)

If $A$ is a finite $K$-[approximate group](approximate-group.md), then

$$
A^m\subseteq X^{m-1}A,\qquad |A^m|\leq K^{m-1}|A|
$$

for every [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $m$, where $A^2\subseteq XA$ and $|X|\leq K$. This follows immediately by [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md).
