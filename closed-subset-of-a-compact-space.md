# Closed subset of a compact space

↑ **Parent:** [Compact space](compact-space.md)

Every [closed subset](closed-set.md) $F$ of a [compact space](compact-space.md) $X$ is [compact](compact-space.md) in its [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md). An [open cover](open-cover.md) of $F$ by ambient [open sets](open-set.md), together with $X\setminus F$, covers $X$. A [finite subcover](finite-subcover.md) for $X$ still covers $F$ after the extra member is discarded. The result does not require $X$ to be [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md).
