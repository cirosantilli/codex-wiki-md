# Unit ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)

The whole [ring](ring.md) $R$, viewed as an [ideal](ideal.md). Elements $f_1,\dots,f_r$ generate the [unit ideal](unit-ideal.md) exactly when coefficients $g_i\in R$ give $\sum_i g_if_i=1$.
