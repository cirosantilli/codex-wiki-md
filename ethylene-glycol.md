# Ethylene glycol

↑ **Parent:** [Alcohol](alcohol.md)

The diol with structure $\mathrm{HOCH_2CH_2OH}$. In a metal-nanostructure [polyol process](polyol-process.md) it can serve as a high-boiling [solvent](solvent.md) and part of the reducing chemistry; reaction conditions and additives determine the actual [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) and growth rates.
