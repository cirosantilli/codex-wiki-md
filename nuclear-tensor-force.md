# Nuclear tensor force

↑ **Parent:** [One-pion exchange](one-pion-exchange.md)

The [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $V_T(r)S_{12}$ couples [nucleon](nucleon.md) [spin](spin.md) orientations to their separation direction $\hat{\mathbf r}$. Here $\boldsymbol\sigma_i$ are [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md). A scalar radial [potential energy](potential-energy.md) alone omits this directional dependence.
