<h1 id="solovay-kitaev-theorem">Solovay--Kitaev theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Universal quantum gate set](universal-quantum-gate-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solovay–Kitaev_theorem)

For a suitable finite inverse-closed [universal quantum gate set](universal-quantum-gate-set.md), the Solovay--Kitaev theorem approximates a fixed-dimensional unitary to [operator norm](operator-norm.md) error $\varepsilon$ with a gate sequence of length polynomial in $\log(1/\varepsilon)$.
