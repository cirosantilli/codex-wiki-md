# Infinite-server queue transient distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson thinning](poisson-thinning.md)

For an [M-M-infinity queue](m-m-%E2%88%9E-queue.md) with empty initial state, Poisson arrivals of rate $\lambda$ and exponential lifetimes of rate $\mu$ produce a Poisson occupancy of mean $\lambda(1-e^{-\mu t})/\mu$ at time $t$.
