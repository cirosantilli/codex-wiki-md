# Triangular number recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Triangular number](triangular-number.md)

For a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$, adding the row of length $n$ to the previous arrangement gives $T_n=T_{n-1}+n$. Thus the first differences of the [triangular numbers](triangular-number.md) are $1,2,3,\ldots$. The [triangular number recurrence](triangular-number-recurrence.md) provides incremental computation: after $T_6=21$, adding seven gives $T_7=28$.
