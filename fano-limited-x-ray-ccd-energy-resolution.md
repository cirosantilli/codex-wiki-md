# Fano-limited X-ray CCD energy resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md)

A [photon](photon.md) of energy $E$ fully absorbed in [silicon](silicon.md) produces on average $E/w$ [electron-hole pairs](electron-hole-pair.md), with [variance](variance-split.md) $F E/w$ rather than necessarily a Poisson [variance](variance-split.md). Thus $\sigma_E=\sqrt{FwE}$ and Gaussian FWHM is $2\sqrt{2\ln2}\,\sigma_E$. Representative [silicon](silicon.md) values $w\simeq3.65$ eV and $F\simeq0.12$ give about $156$ eV FWHM at $10$ keV. [read noise](read-noise.md) in split-event [pixels](pixel.md), trapping and gain errors broaden that limit; optical [pixel](pixel.md) size alone does not specify the [energy resolution](energy-resolution.md).[§4.7, non-uniform CTI and Fano-limited FWHM expression](the-asca-data-reduction-guide-by-nasa-heasarc.md)
