# Binary fission (biology)

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell division](biological-cell-division.md)

A [biological cell division](biological-cell-division.md) that produces two daughter cells. It is an [asexual reproduction](asexual-reproduction.md) mechanism in many unicellular organisms and is distinct from the exchange or recombination of genetic material during sexual processes.
