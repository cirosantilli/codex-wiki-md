# Void probability for growing Poisson rain patches

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson germ-grain model](poisson-germ-grain-model.md)

For drops with intensity $\lambda(x,t,v)$, impact time $t$ and nonnegative [volume](volume.md) $v$, let their radius at time $\tau$ be $r(\tau-t,v)$. The dry [probability](probability.md) at $\xi$ is

$$
\exp\left[-\int_{t\le\tau}\int_{v\ge0}\int_{|x-\xi|\le r(\tau-t,v)}\lambda(x,t,v)\,dx\,dv\,dt\right].
$$

The exponent is the intensity of drops whose patch covers the point by that time. An infinite exponent means zero dry [probability](probability.md). No spatial-homogeneity assumption is needed.
