# Oxygen biosignature false positive

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric biosignature gas](atmospheric-biosignature-gas.md)

Nonbiological [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) can accumulate when photochemistry and planetary redox sinks allow it, for example after water photolysis and hydrogen loss. [Ozone](ozone.md) produced from that oxygen is not independent proof of a biological source. The stellar spectrum, water inventory, atmospheric escape and other gases constrain these interpretations.
