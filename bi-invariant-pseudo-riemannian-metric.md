# Bi-invariant pseudo-Riemannian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

A nondegenerate invariant symmetric [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) on a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) extends by translation to a metric preserved by both left and right translation. It may have indefinite signature. Its [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) is $\nabla_XY=\frac12[X,Y]$ for [left-invariant vector fields](left-invariant-vector-field.md), and its [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) is $-K/4$. Choosing the nondegenerate [Killing form](killing-form.md) of a semisimple [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) yields a canonical indefinite [Einstein manifold](einstein-manifold.md); compact groups instead admit the positive metric $-K$.
