# Multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A multiplet is a collection of components transformed into one another by a specified [group representation](group-representation.md). In [physics](particle-physics.md) it often means the states of one [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) of a symmetry [group](group-split.md); the [group](group-split.md) and [representation](group-representation.md) must be specified, since dimension alone does not identify them.
