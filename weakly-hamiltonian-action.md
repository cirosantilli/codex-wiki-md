# Weakly Hamiltonian action

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian group action](hamiltonian-group-action.md)

A symplectic [Lie group action](lie-group-action.md) is weakly Hamiltonian when every [fundamental vector field](fundamental-vector-field.md) is a [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md). A [Hamiltonian group action](hamiltonian-group-action.md) additionally supplies Hamiltonians as components of an equivariant [moment map](moment-map.md). Constants in the component Hamiltonians do not affect their [vector fields](vector-field.md), but the ability to choose them with the required equivariance is an additional global condition.
