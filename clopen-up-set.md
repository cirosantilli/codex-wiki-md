# Clopen up-set

↑ **Parent:** [Priestley dual space](priestley-dual-space.md)

A clopen up-set is both [clopen](clopen-set.md) in the topology and upward closed in the order. The clopen up-sets of a Priestley space form a [distributive lattice](distributive-lattice.md) under intersection and union.
