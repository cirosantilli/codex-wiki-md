# Remote preparation in conjugate bases

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum no-signalling](quantum-no-signalling.md)

A [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) on one half of a [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) prepares the complex-conjugate basis on the other half, conditional on the outcome. Measuring Alice in the columns of $U^*$ prepares Bob in the columns of $U$. Ignoring the equally likely outcomes leaves Bob's [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) equal to $I/2$ in either basis.
