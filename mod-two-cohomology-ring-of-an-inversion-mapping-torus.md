# Mod-two cohomology ring of an inversion mapping torus

↑ **Parent:** [Inversion mapping torus of a torus](inversion-mapping-torus-of-a-torus.md)

The base class $t$ and coordinate classes $x_i$ all have degree one. Pullback of the [mod-two intersection pairing of the Klein bottle](mod-two-intersection-pairing-of-the-klein-bottle.md) gives $x_i^2=t x_i$. The [Leray-Hirsch theorem](leray-hirsch-theorem.md) gives the basis consisting of square-free products $x_I$ and $t x_I$. Thus the additive dimensions match those of a [torus](torus.md), but the degree-one squares differ for $n\geq2$.
