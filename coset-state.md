# Coset state

↑ **Parent:** [Hidden subgroup problem](hidden-subgroup-problem.md)

For a finite group $G$, subgroup $H$, and $g\in G$, the coset state is the uniform superposition

$$
|gH\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt{|H|}}\sum_{h\in H}|gh\rangle.
$$
