# G-delta compactification invariance

↑ **Parent:** [Čech-complete space](cech-complete-space.md)

For the comparison map $\pi:\beta X\to K$, the [fibers of compactification comparison maps](fibers-of-compactification-comparison-maps.md) identity $\pi^{-1}(X)=X$ pulls a [G-delta set](g-delta-set.md) presentation of $X$ back from $K$. Conversely if $\beta X\setminus X=\bigcup_nF_n$ with $F_n$ closed compact, then $K\setminus X=\bigcup_n\pi(F_n)$. Each image is closed and misses $X$. Thus $X$ is a [G-delta set](g-delta-set.md) in one [Hausdorff compactification](hausdorff-compactification.md) exactly when it is in every one.
