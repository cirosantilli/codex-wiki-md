# Cyclic vector

↑ **Parent:** [Operator theory](linear-operator-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_vector)

For a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $T$, a cyclic vector is a vector whose iterates span the entire [vector space](vector-space-split.md). In finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $d$, the vectors $v,Tv,\ldots,T^{d-1}v$ then form a [basis](basis.md): the first linear dependence makes the preceding span invariant under $T$, so later iterates cannot enlarge it. In topological settings one often requires the iterates to have dense span instead; that closure convention must be stated.
