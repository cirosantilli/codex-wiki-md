# Intermediate cohomology vanishing for a projective complete intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Projective complete intersection](projective-complete-intersection.md)

For a length-$r$ [projective complete intersection](projective-complete-intersection.md) $X_r\subseteq\mathbb P^N_k$, the groups $H^q(X_r,\mathcal O_{X_r}(m))$ vanish for every integer $m$ and $0<q<N-r$. Starting with [cohomology of twisting sheaves on projective space](cohomology-of-twisting-sheaves-on-projective-space.md), induct through $0\to\mathcal O_{X_{j-1}}(m-d_j)\to\mathcal O_{X_{j-1}}(m)\to\mathcal O_{X_j}(m)\to0$. Both adjacent groups in the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) vanish precisely in the indicated range.
