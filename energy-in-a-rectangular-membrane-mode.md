# Energy in a rectangular membrane mode

↑ **Parent:** [Rectangular membrane normal mode](rectangular-membrane-normal-mode.md)

For a rectangular membrane [mode](mode-statistics.md) with peak [antinode](antinode.md) displacement A, the integrals of each squared sine factor are half the corresponding side length. Integrating local [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) [density](density.md) $\rho(\partial_t\psi)^2/2$ gives $K=\rho ab\omega^2A^2\sin^2(\omega t+\phi)/8$. The total [energy](energy-split.md) is $\rho ab\omega^2A^2/8$, and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) has the complementary cosine-squared factor. Its time-averaged [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $\rho ab\omega^2A^2/16$. At equal peak displacement, [frequency](frequency.md) and [density](density.md), such mean energies are proportional to area.
