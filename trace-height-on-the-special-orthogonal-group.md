# Trace height on the special orthogonal group

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md)

For positive distinct diagonal entries $c_1<\cdots<c_n$, the [critical points](critical-point.md) of this function are diagonal sign [matrices](matrix.md) $D=\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_n)$ with $\prod\varepsilon_i=1$. Criticality forces $CX$ symmetric; its square is $C^2$, so it commutes with the distinct-spectrum diagonal [matrix](matrix.md) $C^2$ and is diagonal. In the skew coordinates $a_{ij}$ its [Hessian](hessian-matrix.md) is $-\sum_{i<j}(c_i\varepsilon_i+c_j\varepsilon_j)a_{ij}^2$. The sign of each parenthesis is that of $\varepsilon_j$, giving [Morse index](morse-index.md) $\sum_{j:\varepsilon_j=1}(j-1)$.
