# Argonaute slicing

↑ **Parent:** [Argonaute](argonaute.md)

Endonucleolytic target cleavage directed by a small-[RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) guide and a catalytically competent [Argonaute](argonaute.md). Cleavage typically falls opposite guide positions 10 and 11; not all Argonautes can slice.
