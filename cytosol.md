# Cytosol

↑ **Parent:** [Cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cytosol)

The [cytosol](cytosol.md) is the aqueous part of the [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md) surrounding its structures, including [organelles](organelle.md). It contains [water](water.md), [ions](ion.md) and dissolved [molecules](molecule-split.md), including many [proteins](protein-split.md); it is a crowded working environment rather than pure [water](water.md). The liquid inside a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) or a membrane-bounded [organelle](organelle.md) is not the surrounding [cytosol](cytosol.md).
