<h1 id="so-3-lie-algebra">SO(3) Lie algebra</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md)

Differentiating $R^TR=I$ gives $\mathfrak{so}(3)=\{A\in M_3(\mathbb R):A^T=-A\}$. The generators $L_a v=e_a\times v$ satisfy $[L_a,L_b]=\epsilon_{abc}L_c$ by the [cross product](cross-product.md) identity. This identifies it with the [SU(2) Lie algebra](su-2-lie-algebra.md) by matching a skew-Hermitian Pauli basis.
