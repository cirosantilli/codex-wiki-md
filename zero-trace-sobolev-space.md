# Zero-trace Sobolev space

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

The zero-trace first-order [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) is the closure of $C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ in the $H^1$ norm. On a bounded Lipschitz domain it is the kernel of the [Sobolev trace operator](trace-operator.md). On an interval, its elements have absolutely continuous representatives with both endpoint values zero. The [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) makes the derivative norm equivalent to the full Sobolev norm. This is the natural form domain for an elliptic problem with homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md).
