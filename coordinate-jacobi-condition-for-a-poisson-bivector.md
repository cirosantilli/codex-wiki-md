# Coordinate Jacobi condition for a Poisson bivector

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson bivector](poisson-bivector.md)

The [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) for a [Poisson bivector](poisson-bivector.md) reduces to the displayed cyclic identity in every local chart. Necessity follows by applying the bracket to coordinate functions. Sufficiency follows because the second-derivative terms in an arbitrary Jacobiator cancel by antisymmetry, leaving this coefficient multiplying first derivatives of the three functions.
