# Noncommutative domain

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative algebra](noncommutative-algebra-split.md)

A noncommutative domain is a nonzero unital [ring](ring.md) without zero divisors; commutativity is not required, but commutative [integral domains](integral-domain.md) are included in this usage. Left and right cancellation hold for nonzero elements. A [right Noetherian domain](right-noetherian-domain.md) embeds in a [division ring](division-ring.md) by the [right Ore condition](right-ore-condition.md).
