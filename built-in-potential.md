# Built-in potential

↑ **Parent:** [P-n junction](p-n-junction.md)

The [built-in potential](built-in-potential.md) of a [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md) is the electrostatic potential difference established when carrier diffusion exposes ionized dopants until the opposing drift and diffusion currents balance. The equilibrium [electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md) is constant even though the [electric potential](electric-potential.md) and band edges vary. In a nondegenerate homojunction with neutral-edge majority densities $p_p,n_n$, $eV_b=k_BT\log(p_p n_n/n_i^2)$. This is [band bending](band-bending.md) within the same material, not necessarily a discontinuous offset of different materials' band edges.
