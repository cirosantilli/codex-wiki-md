# Four-flavour meson multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Quark model](quark-model.md)

Four [quark flavours](quark-flavor.md) in the defining [representation](group-representation.md) of flavour [SU(4)](su-4-group.md) and their [antiquarks](antiquark.md) give sixteen [meson](meson.md) flavour states. The [trace](matrix-trace.md) of $M^i{}_j=q^i\bar q_j$ is the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md); its traceless part is the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Under light-flavour [SU(3)](su-3-group.md), the full content is $\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus2\mathbf1$. Relative to the light-flavour nonet, the new states are $u\bar c,d\bar c,s\bar c,c\bar u,c\bar d,c\bar s,c\bar c$. Inside the traceless fifteen-dimensional multiplet the additional singlet is instead $(u\bar u+d\bar d+s\bar s-3c\bar c)/\sqrt{12}$. The pure $c\bar c$ state is a linear combination of this state and the full [SU(4)](su-4-group.md) singlet. The classification concerns flavour; it does not imply degenerate physical [masses](mass.md) or fix spin.
