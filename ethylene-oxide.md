# Ethylene oxide

↑ **Parent:** [Epoxide](epoxide.md)

The simplest [epoxide](epoxide.md), a three-membered $\mathrm{C_2H_4O}$ ring. Opening by a [carbon](carbon.md) nucleophile, followed by protonation, adds a two-carbon primary-alcohol unit.
