# Left Cauchy-Green deformation tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md)

The left Cauchy-Green tensor measures stretch on the [current configuration](current-configuration.md). It is a [positive-definite symmetric matrix](symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md) for an invertible [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md), with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) equal to the squares of the [principal stretches](principal-stretch.md).
