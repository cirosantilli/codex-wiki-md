# Maximum-volume open rectangular box at fixed surface area

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md)

For a lidless [rectangular parallelepiped](rectangular-parallelepiped.md) with positive dimensions $l,w,h$ and prescribed external [surface area](surface-area.md) $S=lw+2lh+2wh$, the largest [volume](volume.md) is attained at $l=w=2h=\sqrt{S/3}$. The [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) applied to $lw,2lh,2wh$ gives

$$
4(lwh)^2\leq(S/3)^3,
$$

with equality exactly when these three face-area terms are equal. This proves both the claimed global bound and its attainable equality case. [Lagrange multipliers](lagrange-multiplier.md) produce the same dimensions from the interior stationarity equations. The [weighted open-box minimization](weighted-open-box-minimization.md) is the corresponding fixed-volume formulation with general positive face weights.
