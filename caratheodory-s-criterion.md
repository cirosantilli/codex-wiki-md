<h1 id="caratheodory-s-criterion">Carathéodory's criterion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Outer measure](outer-measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carathéodory's_criterion)

A set $E$ is measurable for an [outer measure](outer-measure.md) $\mu^*$ when every set $A$ is split additively by $E$:

$$
\mu^*(A)=\mu^*(A\cap E)+\mu^*(A\setminus E).
$$

The measurable sets form a [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md), and the restriction of $\mu^*$ to it is a complete measure.
