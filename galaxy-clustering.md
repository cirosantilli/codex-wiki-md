# Galaxy clustering

↑ **Parent:** [Large-scale structure of the universe](large-scale-structure-of-the-universe-split.md)

The nonuniform spatial distribution of [galaxies](galaxy-split.md), quantified for example by a [two-point correlation function](two-point-correlation-function.md) or a [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md) related through [galaxy bias](galaxy-bias.md). Survey geometry and selection must be separated from physical clustering.
