# Gas-driven contraction of a planetesimal binary

↑ **Parent:** [Gas drag on a planetesimal binary](gas-drag-on-a-planetesimal-binary.md)

For a weakly perturbed circular [binary planetesimal](binary-planetesimal.md), the constant differential headwind does zero work averaged over the unperturbed orbit. [Orbit-averaged drag work](orbit-averaged-drag-work.md) gives

$$
\langle\dot a\rangle=-2\kappa a,\qquad
\tau=\frac1{2\kappa}=\frac{m_1+m_2}{2(m_1/t_2+m_2/t_1)}.
$$

With constant [aerodynamic stopping times](aerodynamic-stopping-time.md), $a(t)=a_0e^{-t/\tau}$. This averaging assumes drag is slow compared with the [orbital period](orbital-period.md) and does not appreciably distort the circular orbit within one period.
