# Demagnifying a distant diffraction pattern

↑ **Parent:** [Single-slit diffraction](single-slit-diffraction.md)

A [thin lens](thin-lens.md) placed immediately after an aperture can reproduce its free-propagated pattern from distance L on a nearer screen d if $1/f=1/d-1/L$. In the paraxial Fresnel integral, the lens changes the aperture's quadratic propagation [phase](phase-waves.md) from $k x^2/(2d)$ to $kx^2(1/d-1/f)/2=kx^2/(2L)$. The cross term $-kxy/d$ then matches free propagation at the old-screen coordinate $Y=L y/d$. [Intensities](intensity-physics.md) have the same shape under transverse scale d/L, with an overall brightness factor in a lossless lens of sufficient aperture. For a narrow uniformly illuminated slit, the far-field first-zero estimate on the new screen is $d\lambda/a$.
