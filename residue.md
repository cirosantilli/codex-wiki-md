# Residue

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Residue)

The residue of a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) $f$ at an [isolated singularity](isolated-singularity-split.md) $a$ is the coefficient of $(z-a)^{-1}$ in its [Laurent series](laurent-series.md). At a simple [pole](pole.md),

$$
\operatorname{Res}(f,a)=\lim_{z\to a}(z-a)f(z).
$$
