# Congruence subgroup of fractional ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Ray class group](ray-class-group.md)

A subgroup of the group of [fractional ideals](fractional-ideal.md) prime to a [modulus of a number field](modulus-of-a-number-field.md), containing the principal ray subgroup. It is the inverse image of a subgroup of the [ray class group](ray-class-group.md). The [existence theorem of global class field theory](existence-theorem-of-global-class-field-theory.md) realizes it as the kernel of an [Artin map](artin-reciprocity-law.md), and identifies its quotient with the corresponding [Galois group](galois-group.md). This is the ideal-theoretic class-field meaning of congruence subgroup.
