# Electrostatic force

↑ **Parent:** [Electric force](electric-force.md)

The [electric force](electric-force.md) exerted by a time-independent [electric field](electric-field-split.md). For a [point charge](point-charge.md), the external field excludes its own singular self-field. The [method of images](method-of-images.md) evaluates conductor-induced forces by the image field at the physical charge's position; the fictitious images need not be physical charges in the conductor interior.
