# Probability mass

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md)

The [probability mass](probability-mass.md) at an outcome $x$ is the probability of its [singleton](singleton-mathematics.md), $\mathbb P(X=x)$. It equals the jump $F_X(x)-F_X(x^-)$ of the [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md). For a [discrete probability distribution](discrete-probability-distribution-split.md) these masses form the [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md); a [continuous probability distribution](continuous-probability-distribution-split.md) gives every individual outcome mass zero. Positive [probability mass](probability-mass.md) at a point is distinct from the height of a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md).
