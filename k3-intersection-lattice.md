# K3 intersection lattice

↑ **Parent:** [K3 surface](k3-surface.md)

The [cup product](cup-product.md) on the second integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of a complex [K3 surface](k3-surface.md) is an [even unimodular lattice](even-unimodular-lattice.md) of signature $(3,19)$. Here $U$ is the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane-quadratic-form.md) and $E_8(-1)$ is the negative-definite form of the [E8 lattice](e8-lattice.md). In the [M-theory K3 duality](m-theory-k3-duality.md), this lattice matches the heterotic electric charge lattice. The self-dual harmonic two-forms of a [Ricci flat](ricci-flat-manifold.md) K3 metric span its positive three-plane.
