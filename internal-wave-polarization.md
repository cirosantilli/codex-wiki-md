# Internal-wave polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave phase and group velocity](internal-wave-phase-and-group-velocity.md)

For a propagating, non-cutoff two-dimensional [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) proportional to $e^{i(kx+mz-\omega t)}$, [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) gives $U=-mW/k$. Thus the oscillatory [velocity field](velocity-field.md) is perpendicular to the [wavevector](wavevector.md) and parallel to the [group velocity](group-velocity.md), whereas the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) is parallel to the [wavevector](wavevector.md).
