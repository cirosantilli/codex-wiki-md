# Semi-minor axis

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

For an ellipse of eccentricity $e$, the semi-minor axis is $b=a\sqrt{1-e^2}$.

This is one of the [semi-major and semi-minor axes](semi-major-and-semi-minor-axes.md); the two-axis geometry is the broader concept.
