# Triangular-rotation factorization of real determinant-one matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear group](special-linear-group.md)

Every $g\in SL_2(\mathbb R)$ factors into an upper triangular determinant-one matrix and a [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md). This follows because the triangular subgroup acts transitively on the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), a [group orbit](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) of the full group, while $SO(2)$ is the [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $i$. The intersection of the two subgroups is $\{\pm I\}$, giving exactly two matrix pairs $(h,k)$ and $(-h,-k)$. Requiring the diagonal of $h$ to be positive gives uniqueness. For $g=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$ and $q=\sqrt{c^2+d^2}$, the positive branch is $h=\begin{pmatrix}1/q&(ac+bd)/q\\0&q\end{pmatrix}$ and $k=q^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}d&-c\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$.
