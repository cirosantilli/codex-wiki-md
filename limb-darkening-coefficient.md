# Limb-darkening coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Linear limb-darkening law](linear-limb-darkening-law.md)

The parameter $u$ in the [linear limb-darkening law](linear-limb-darkening-law.md) specifies its contrast between disk centre and the limiting edge. Its interpretation requires the chosen law and interval of [frequency](frequency.md) or [wavelength](wavelength.md). A [limb-darkening coefficient](limb-darkening-coefficient.md) fitted to an [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md) may trade off against the [transit impact parameter](transit-impact-parameter.md) and [planet](planet.md) [radius](radius.md); it is not a direct measurement of one layer's [temperature](temperature.md).
