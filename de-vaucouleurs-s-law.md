<h1 id="de-vaucouleurs-s-law">de Vaucouleurs's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sérsic profile](sersic-profile.md)

The $n=4$ case of the Sérsic surface-brightness profile, approximately useful for many bright [elliptical galaxies](elliptical-galaxy.md): $I(R)=I_e\exp[-7.67((R/R_e)^{1/4}-1)]$. Real [galaxies](galaxy-split.md) can have other Sérsic indices.
