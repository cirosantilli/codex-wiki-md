# Local Artin map

↑ **Parent:** [Local Artin reciprocity](local-artin-reciprocity.md)

The local Artin map is the reciprocity homomorphism of [Local Artin reciprocity](local-artin-reciprocity.md). Its normalization is fixed by choosing whether a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) maps to arithmetic or geometric Frobenius.
