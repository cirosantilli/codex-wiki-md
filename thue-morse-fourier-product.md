<h1 id="thue-morse-fourier-product">Thue–Morse Fourier product</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thue–Morse sequence](thue-morse-sequence.md)

Each [binary digit](binary-digit.md) independently contributes either $1$ or $-e(2^l\theta)$, proving the product. Two adjacent factors have modulus $8|\cos(\pi t)|(1-\cos^2(\pi t))\le16/(3\sqrt3)<4$. Consequently the product is $O(2^{\sigma j})$, with $\sigma=2-\frac34\log_2 3<1$. This two-factor estimate suffices for a power saving, though its exponent is not optimal.
