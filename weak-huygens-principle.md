# Weak Huygens principle

↑ **Parent:** [Finite propagation speed](finite-propagation-speed.md)

The [weak Huygens principle](weak-huygens-principle.md) is the domain-of-dependence statement that initial data outside a backward light cone cannot influence its tip. It permits an interior tail and is weaker than the [Strong Huygens principle](strong-huygens-principle.md). For $u_{tt}-\Delta u+V(x)u=0$ with $V\ge0$, integrating the local [energy](energy.md) over shrinking balls proves this statement: the boundary contribution is $u_t\partial_\nu u-\tfrac12(u_t^2+|\nabla u|^2+Vu^2)\le0$.
