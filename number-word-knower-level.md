# Number-word knower level

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinality principle](cardinality-principle.md)

A description of which exact [number](number.md)-word meanings a child demonstrates in tasks such as giving a requested collection. Early knowledge of particular small quantities can precede a general use of the [counting](counting.md) system; task context and errors matter when assigning the description.
