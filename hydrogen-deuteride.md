# Hydrogen deuteride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance.md)

The diatomic molecule containing one ordinary hydrogen nucleus and one deuterium nucleus is an isotopic form of [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md). It has a weak electric dipole and can supply low-temperature [molecular line cooling](molecular-line-cooling.md) in primordial gas when its abundance is sufficient.
