# Unequal mixed partial derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed partial derivative](mixed-partial-derivative.md)

For $f(x,y)=xy(x^2-y^2)/(x^2+y^2)$ away from the origin and zero at the origin, $|f|\leq(x^2+y^2)/2$ proves [Fréchet differentiability](frechet-differentiability.md) with zero derivative. Yet $f_x(0,y)=-y$ and $f_y(x,0)=x$, giving the displayed unequal [mixed partial derivatives](mixed-partial-derivative.md). They exist but are not continuous at the origin, so [Clairaut's theorem](symmetry-of-second-derivatives.md) does not apply.
