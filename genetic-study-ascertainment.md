# Genetic-study ascertainment

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)

[Ascertainment in a genetic study](genetic-study-ascertainment.md) specifies how individuals or families enter a genetic study. If $A$ is the event of inclusion, then $P(X\mid A)=P(X)P(A\mid X)/P(A)$. Selection on disease [phenotype](phenotype.md) can change an unconditional [pedigree likelihood](pedigree-likelihood.md). When comparing [genetic marker](genetic-marker.md) [probabilities](probability.md) conditional on a specified disease pattern, selection depending only on that pattern cancels from the linkage [likelihood ratio](likelihood-ratio.md).
