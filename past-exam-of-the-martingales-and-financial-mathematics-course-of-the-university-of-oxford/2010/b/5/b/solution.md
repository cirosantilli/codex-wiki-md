↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For [geometric Brownian motion](../../../../../geometric-brownian-motion.md), $\log S_u=\log S_0+(\mu-\sigma^2/2)u+\sigma W_u$. Averaging in time gives

$$
\log A_t=\log S_0+\tfrac12(\mu-\sigma^2/2)t+\frac\sigma t I_t.
$$

Thus $\log A_t$ is normal with mean $\log S_0+(\mu-\sigma^2/2)t/2$ and variance $\sigma^2t/3$. The [moment-generating function of a normal distribution](../../../../../moment-generating-function-of-a-normal-distribution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb EA_t=S_0\exp\left[\left(\frac\mu2-\frac{\sigma^2}{12}\right)t\right].}
$$
