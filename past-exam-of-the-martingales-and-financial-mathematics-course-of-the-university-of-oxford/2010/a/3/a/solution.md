↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Borel-Cantelli second lemma](../../../../../borel-cantelli-second-lemma.md) says that independent events whose probabilities have divergent sum occur infinitely often almost surely. The events here are independent and have probabilities $\min\{1,\lambda/n\}$. Their sum diverges by the harmonic series, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(U_n\leq\lambda/n\text{ infinitely often})=1\quad(\lambda>0).}
$$
