↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) gives the event inclusion

$$
\{|f_n-f_m|>\varepsilon\}\subseteq
\{|f_n-f|>\varepsilon/2\}\cup\{|f_m-f|>\varepsilon/2\}.
$$

[Countable subadditivity of a measure](../../../../../../countable-subadditivity-of-a-measure.md) bounds its measure by the sum of the two terms on the right. Both tend to zero as $m,n\to\infty$ by [convergence in measure](../../../../../../convergence-in-measure.md). Thus **convergence in measure implies the Cauchy property in measure**.
