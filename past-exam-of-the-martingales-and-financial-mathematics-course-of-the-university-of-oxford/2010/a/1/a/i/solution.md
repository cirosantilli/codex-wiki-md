A [measurable space](../../../../../../measurable-space.md) is a set $E$ together with a [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal E$ of its subsets: it contains $E$ and is closed under complements relative to $E$ and countable unions. A [measure](../../../../../../measure-split.md) is a map $\mu:\mathcal E\to[0,\infty]$ with $\mu(\varnothing)=0$ and [countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md) on disjoint measurable sets:

$$
\mu\left(\bigcup_jA_j\right)=\sum_j\mu(A_j).
$$

A finite measure additionally satisfies $\mu(E)<\infty$.
