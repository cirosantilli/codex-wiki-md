<h1 id="a/2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Pointwise, the positive integers $n$ with $Z\geq n$ are exactly $1,\ldots,\lfloor Z\rfloor$. Thus $Y=\lfloor Z\rfloor=\sum_{n\geq1}\mathbf1_{\{Z\geq n\}}$. The [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) gives $\mathbb EY=\sum_{n\geq1}\mathbb P(Z\geq n)$. Since $Y\leq Z<Y+1$,

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n\geq1}\mathbb P(Z\geq n)\leq\mathbb EZ\leq1+\sum_{n\geq1}\mathbb P(Z\geq n).}
$$

These inequalities also hold for infinite expectations. This is the [tail-sum formula](../../../../../../tail-sum-formula.md) comparison for a nonnegative real variable.
