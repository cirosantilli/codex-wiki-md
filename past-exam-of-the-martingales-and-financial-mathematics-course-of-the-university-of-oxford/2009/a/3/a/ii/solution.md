<h1 id="a/3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each increment has the same law as its negative, and their independence makes the joint law of $(X_1,X_2,\ldots)$ invariant under simultaneous sign reversal. Under this transformation, the [simple symmetric random walk](../../../../../../simple-symmetric-random-walk.md) $S_n$ becomes $-S_n$, and the event $B_-$ becomes $B_+$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(B_-)=\mathbb P(B_+).}
$$

This uses symmetry of the entire increment sequence, rather than just the one-dimensional distributions of the positions.
