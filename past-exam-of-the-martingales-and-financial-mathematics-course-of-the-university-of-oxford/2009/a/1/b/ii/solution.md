↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By [convergence in probability](../../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md), choose recursively $n_k>n_{k-1}$ so that

$$
\mathbb P(|X_{n_k}-X|>2^{-k})<2^{-k}.
$$

The corresponding [events](../../../../../../event.md) have a summable sequence of probabilities. The [First Borel-Cantelli lemma](../../../../../../borel-cantelli-first-lemma.md), which requires no independence, therefore makes the probability of infinitely many of them zero. On the complementary event, $|X_{n_k}-X|\leq2^{-k}$ for all sufficiently large $k$, proving

$$
\boxed{X_{n_k}\longrightarrow X\quad\text{almost surely}.}
$$

The recursively chosen indices are strictly increasing and hence tend to infinity.
