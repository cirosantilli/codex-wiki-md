↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) satisfy, for every finite selection of distinct indices $n_1,\ldots,n_m$ and [Borel sets](../../../../../../borel-set.md) $C_1,\ldots,C_m$,

$$
\mathbb P\left(\bigcap_{j=1}^m\{X_{n_j}\in C_j\}\right)=\prod_{j=1}^m\mathbb P(X_{n_j}\in C_j).
$$

The [Second Borel-Cantelli lemma](../../../../../../borel-cantelli-second-lemma.md) says that if events $E_n$ are independent and $\sum_n\mathbb P(E_n)=\infty$, then

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(\limsup_nE_n)=1.}
$$

Here $\limsup_nE_n=\bigcap_N\bigcup_{n\geq N}E_n$ is the event that infinitely many of the $E_n$ occur.
