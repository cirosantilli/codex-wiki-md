[Almost sure convergence](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md) means

$$
\mathbb P\{\omega:\lim_{n\to\infty}X_n(\omega)=X(\omega)\}=1.
$$

Thus a single event of probability one supports ordinary pointwise convergence. [Convergence in probability](../../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md) means that for every $\epsilon>0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(|X_n-X|>\epsilon)\longrightarrow0.}
$$

The exceptional event is allowed to change with $n$ in the second definition.
