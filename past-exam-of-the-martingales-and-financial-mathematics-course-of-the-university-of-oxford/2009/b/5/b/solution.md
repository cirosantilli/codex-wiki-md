<h1 id="b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the homogeneity identity from part (a) with respect to $\lambda$ at $\lambda=1$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives [Euler homogeneous function theorem](../../../../../euler-theorem-for-homogeneous-functions.md) in the form

$$
xp_x+Kp_K=p.
$$

Using the supplied [put option](../../../../../put-option.md) spot derivative $p_x=-\Phi(-d_1)$ and the given price,

$$
Kp_K=p-xp_x
=Ke^{-r(T-t)}\Phi(-d_2).
$$

For positive strike, division by $K$ gives the [put-price strike derivative](../../../../../put-price-strike-derivative.md)

$$
\boxed{p_K=e^{-r(T-t)}\Phi(-d_2).}
$$

It is nonnegative, as increasing the strike increases the terminal put payoff.
