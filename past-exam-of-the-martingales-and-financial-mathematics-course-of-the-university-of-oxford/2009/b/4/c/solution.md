<h1 id="b/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

As insurance against [credit default](../../../../../credit-default.md), the payment is the positive shortfall of recovery below face value:

$$
H=\left(1-\frac{X_T}m\right)^+=\frac1m(m-X_T)^+.
$$

Thus the [credit default swap](../../../../../credit-default-swap.md) in this question is one $m$th of a [European put option](../../../../../european-put-option.md) on the firm value. Its price is

$$
\boxed{V_{\rm CDS}=e^{-rT}\Phi(-d_2)-\frac{x_0}m\Phi(-d_1).}
$$

Equivalently, adding this insurance to one corporate bond produces a sure unit payment, so its price is $e^{-rT}$ minus the bond price from part (b). This also fixes the positive orientation of the printed “difference”: insurance compensates face value minus recovery.
