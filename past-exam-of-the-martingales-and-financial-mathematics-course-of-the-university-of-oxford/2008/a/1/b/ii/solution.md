<h1 id="a/1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $A\in\mathcal F_S$, write

$$
A\cap\{S\leq T\}\cap\{T\leq n\}
=\bigcup_{k=0}^n\left[A\cap\{S=k\}\cap\{k\leq T\leq n\}\right].
$$

The first two factors together belong to $\mathcal F_k$, since $A\cap\{S=k\}$ is the difference of its intersections with $\{S\leq k\}$ and $\{S\leq k-1\}$. Also $\{k\leq T\leq n\}\in\mathcal F_n$ by the [stopping time](../../../../../../stopping-time.md) property. The filtration inclusion puts every term, and therefore the union, in $\mathcal F_n$. By definition,

$$
\boxed{A\cap\{S\leq T\}\in\mathcal F_T.}
$$
