A [filtration](../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) is an increasing sequence of sub-sigma-algebras $\mathcal F_n\subseteq\mathcal F$: if $m\leq n$, then $\mathcal F_m\subseteq\mathcal F_n$. It represents information available through each time.

A [stopping time](../../../../../stopping-time.md) $T:\Omega\to\mathbb N_0\cup\{\infty\}$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{\{T\leq n\}\in\mathcal F_n\quad\text{for every }n\geq0.}
$$

Thus whether stopping has already occurred is determined by the current information.
