The [strong law of large numbers](../../../../../../strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) states that if $X_n$ are independent and identically distributed real random variables with $\mathbb E|X_1|<\infty$, then

$$
\boxed{\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^NX_n\longrightarrow\mathbb EX_1
\quad\text{almost surely}.}
$$

The first absolute moment condition is part of the standard statement.
