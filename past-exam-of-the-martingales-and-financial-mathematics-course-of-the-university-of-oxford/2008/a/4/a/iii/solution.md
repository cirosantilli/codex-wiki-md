<h1 id="a/4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a [lower semicontinuous](../../../../../../lower-semicontinuity.md) function, each upper-ray inverse image is open, hence Borel, so part (a)(i) applies directly. For an [upper semicontinuous](../../../../../../upper-semicontinuity.md) $h$, the function $-h$ is lower semicontinuous because

$$
\{-h>y\}=\{h<-y\}
$$

is open. Negation is continuous and Borel measurable, so $h=-(-h)$ is Borel measurable too. Thus **both semicontinuity classes are Borel measurable**.
