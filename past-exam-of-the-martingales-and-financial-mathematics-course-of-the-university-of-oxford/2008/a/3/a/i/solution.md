↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A version of $\mathbb E(X\mid\mathcal G)$ is an integrable $\mathcal G$-measurable random variable $Y$ satisfying

$$
\boxed{\int_A Y\,d\mathbb P=\int_A X\,d\mathbb P
\quad\text{for every }A\in\mathcal G.}
$$

The measurability requirement restricts $Y$ to the available information; the integral identities preserve all averages visible to that information. This is the definition of [conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md).
