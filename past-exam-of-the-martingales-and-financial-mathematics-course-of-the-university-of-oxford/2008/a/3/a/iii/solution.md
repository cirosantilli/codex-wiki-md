A [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) is an adapted process $X_n$ with $\mathbb E|X_n|<\infty$ for every $n$ and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n)=X_n\quad\text{almost surely}.}
$$

Adaptedness means that $X_n$ is $\mathcal F_n$-measurable. Iterating the [tower property](../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) gives the analogous identity for any two deterministic times $m\leq n$.
