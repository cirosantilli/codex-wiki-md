<h1 id="a/3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The process $Y_n=X_n-Z_n$ is adapted and integrable. Since $Z_{n+1}=Z_n+G_{n+1}$ is already $\mathcal F_n$-measurable,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E(Y_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n)
&=\mathbb E(X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n)-Z_n-G_{n+1}\\
&=X_n+G_{n+1}-Z_n-G_{n+1}=Y_n.
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore **$Y$ is a martingale**, and $X=Y+Z$ is the [Doob decomposition in discrete time](../../../../../../doob-decomposition-theorem.md). Its normalization is $Z_0=0$, so the initial value is included in $Y_0=X_0$.
