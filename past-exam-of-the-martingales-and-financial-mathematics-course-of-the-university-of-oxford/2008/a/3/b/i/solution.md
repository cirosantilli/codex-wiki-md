<h1 id="a/3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md) property and linearity of [conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) give, for $n\geq1$,

$$
G_n=\mathbb E(X_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1})-X_{n-1}\leq0.
$$

Each $G_n$ is integrable and $\mathcal F_{n-1}$-measurable, while $G_0=0$. Thus $Z_n$ is integrable and

$$
\boxed{Z_{n+1}-Z_n=G_{n+1}\leq0.}
$$

Moreover, all terms $G_j$, $j\leq n+1$, are $\mathcal F_n$-measurable, so $Z_{n+1}\in m\mathcal F_n$. This is predictability of the decreasing component in the [Doob decomposition theorem](../../../../../../doob-decomposition-theorem.md).
