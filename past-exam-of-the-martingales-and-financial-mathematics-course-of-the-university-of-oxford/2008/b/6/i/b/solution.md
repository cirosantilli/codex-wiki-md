<h1 id="b/6/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A stock share plus a put has terminal value $S_T+(K-S_T)^+=\max(S_T,K)\geq K$. If $S_t+P(t,S_t)<Ke^{-r(T-t)}$, buy that package and borrow the price of a bond paying $K$. The package covers the debt at maturity, while the strictly positive initial surplus can be invested. This would be an [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{P(t,S_t)\geq Ke^{-r(T-t)}-S_t.}
$$

Together with payoff nonnegativity, the lower bound can be strengthened to the maximum of this expression and zero.
