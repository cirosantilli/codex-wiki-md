A [stopping time](../../../../../../stopping-time.md) is a random time $\tau$ taking values in the nonnegative integers together with infinity and satisfying $\{\tau\leq n\}\in\mathcal F_n$ for each $n$. Whether stopping has occurred by time $n$ can therefore be decided from information available then.
