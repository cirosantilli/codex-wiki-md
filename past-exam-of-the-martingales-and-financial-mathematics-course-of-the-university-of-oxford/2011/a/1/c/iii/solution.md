<h1 id="a/1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For each $i$, the collection of events $\{X_i\leq x\}$, together with the empty event, is a [pi-system](../../../../../../pi-system.md). Its generated [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) is $\sigma(X_i)$, because the half-lines generate the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md) and inverse images preserve intersections and the sigma-algebra operations.

The displayed probability identity says precisely that these two pi-systems are independent. Part (ii) makes $\sigma(X_1)$ and $\sigma(X_2)$ independent, which is the definition of independent [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md). Conversely independence of the random variables applies to the half-line events and gives the identity. Thus **the joint cumulative distribution function factors exactly when the variables are independent**.
