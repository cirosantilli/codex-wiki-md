<h1 id="a/1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each half-line $(-\infty,x]$ is a closed [Borel set](../../../../../../borel-set.md), and the empty set is Borel. Thus $\sigma(\pi(\mathbb R))\subseteq\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$.

Conversely,

$$
(-\infty,b)=\bigcup_{n\geq1}(-\infty,b-1/n],\qquad
(a,b)=(-\infty,b)\setminus(-\infty,a].
$$

The generated [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) contains both sets on the right and is closed under their difference, so it contains every open interval. By the given definition of the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md), the reverse inclusion follows. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\sigma(\pi(\mathbb R))=\mathcal B(\mathbb R).}
$$
