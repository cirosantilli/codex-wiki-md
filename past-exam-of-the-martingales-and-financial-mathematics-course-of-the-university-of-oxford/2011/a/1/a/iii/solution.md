The set function is [countably additive](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md) if, whenever $A_1,A_2,\ldots\in\mathcal I$ are pairwise disjoint and their union $A$ also belongs to $\mathcal I$,

$$
\boxed{\mu(A)=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\mu(A_j).}
$$

The equality is in the extended nonnegative reals. To be a [measure](../../../../../../measure-split.md) one also imposes $\mu(\varnothing)=0$ and an appropriate domain such as a sigma-algebra.
