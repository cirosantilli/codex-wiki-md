A [pi-system](../../../../../../pi-system.md) is a collection of subsets closed under binary intersections: $A,B\in\mathcal I$ implies $A\cap B\in\mathcal I$. Induction then gives closure under every nonempty finite intersection. A pi-system need not contain complements or unions.
