<h1 id="b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $\sigma=\sqrt{\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2}>0$. The linear combination $W_3$ starts at zero and has continuous paths. An increment over an interval of length $h$ is a linear combination of independent centred [normal random variables](../../../../../gaussian-random-variable.md), so is centred normal with variance

$$
\left(\frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma^2}+\frac{\sigma_2^2}{\sigma^2}\right)h=h.
$$

Increments on disjoint intervals are independent, since they depend on disjoint increments of the two independent [Brownian motions](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md). These are the defining properties, so **$W_3$ is a standard Brownian motion**.
