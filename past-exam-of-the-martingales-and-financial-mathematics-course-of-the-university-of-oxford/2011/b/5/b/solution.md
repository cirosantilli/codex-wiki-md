<h1 id="b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Cubing the supplied [geometric Brownian motion](../../../../../geometric-brownian-motion.md) solution gives $S_t^3=s^3\exp((3\mu-3\sigma^2/2)t+3\sigma W_3(t))$. The [moment-generating function of a normal distribution](../../../../../moment-generating-function-of-a-normal-distribution.md) yields $\mathbb E[e^{aW_3(t)}]=e^{a^2t/2}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[S_t^3]=s^3\exp\bigl((3\mu+3\sigma^2)t\bigr).}
$$
