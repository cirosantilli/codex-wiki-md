<h1 id="b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\Delta_t$ be the number of stock units and $\beta_t$ the number of bank-account units, with predictable holdings satisfying the integrability conditions for the price integrals. The portfolio value is $V_t=\Delta_tS_t+\beta_tB_t$. It is [self-financing](../../../../../self-financing-portfolio.md) if

$$
\boxed{V_t=V_0+\int_0^t\Delta_u\,dS_u+
\int_0^t\beta_u\,dB_u.}
$$

Thus changes in value come only from gains on the assets held. Rebalancing transfers money between the stock and [bank account](../../../../../bank-account.md); it does not inject or withdraw cash. The definition is an identity of [stochastic integrals](../../../../../stochastic-integral.md) and automatically includes the bookkeeping needed when holdings themselves vary randomly.
