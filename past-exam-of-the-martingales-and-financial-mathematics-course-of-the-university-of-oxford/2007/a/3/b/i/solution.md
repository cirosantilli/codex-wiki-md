A process is a [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md) relative to the given [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) if $X_n$ is $\mathcal F_n$-measurable and integrable for each $n$, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n)\leq X_n
\quad\text{almost surely for every }n\geq0.}
$$

Iteration gives the equivalent inequality $\mathbb E(X_m\mid\mathcal F_n)\leq X_n$ for every $m\geq n$. [adapted process](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) and integrability are part of the definition, not consequences of the displayed inequality alone.
