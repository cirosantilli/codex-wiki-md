A family of [sigma-algebras](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) is independent if for every finite collection of distinct indices $n_1,\ldots,n_k$ and sets $A_j\in\mathcal G_{n_j}$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P\!\left(\bigcap_{j=1}^kA_j\right)
=\prod_{j=1}^k\mathbb P(A_j).}
$$

A family of [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) is independent if its generated [sigma-algebras](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) $\sigma(X_n)$ are independent. Equivalently, the displayed product identity holds for the events $\{X_{n_j}\in B_j\}$ with arbitrary Borel $B_j$. Pairwise [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) alone is weaker and does not suffice for a family.
