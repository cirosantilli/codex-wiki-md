<h1 id="a/2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For two real-valued [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) and each real $a$,

$$
\{X+Y<a\}=
\bigcup_{q\in\mathbb Q}\bigl(\{X<q\}\cap\{Y<a-q\}\bigr).
$$

If $X+Y<a$, density of the rationals supplies $q$ with $X<q<a-Y$; conversely either pair of inequalities in the union implies $X+Y<a$. Every set in the countable union is measurable, so the half-line criterion proves [measurability](../../../../../../measurability.md) of $X+Y$. Its values are real, and induction gives **every finite sum $X_1+\cdots+X_n$ is a real-valued [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md)**.
