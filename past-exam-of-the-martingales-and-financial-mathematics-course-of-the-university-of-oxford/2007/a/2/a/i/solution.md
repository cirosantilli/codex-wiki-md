A real-valued [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) is a map $X:\Omega\to\mathbb R$ measurable from $(\Omega,\mathcal F)$ to the Borel real line. Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\{X<a\}\in\mathcal F\quad\text{for every real }a.}
$$

Its values are finite real numbers here; extended-real-valued [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) require a correspondingly enlarged target.
