<h1 id="a/1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the characterization of [measurability](../../../../../../measurability.md) by [preimages](../../../../../../preimage.md) of open half-lines, allowing intermediate extended-real-valued functions. Put $u_m=\inf_{n\geq m}f_n$. For each real $a$,

$$
\{u_m<a\}=\bigcup_{n\geq m}\{f_n<a\}\in\Sigma.
$$

Thus every $u_m$ is measurable. Countable suprema are measurable too, since $\{\sup_m u_m>a\}=\bigcup_m\{u_m>a\}$ and the upper half-lines also generate the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\liminf_n f_n=\sup_m\inf_{n\geq m}f_n}
$$

is measurable. Its values are real by the stated hypothesis, so it is the required ordinary real-valued [measurable function](../../../../../../measurable-function.md), rather than only an extended-real-valued one.
