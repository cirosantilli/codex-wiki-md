↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A map between the [measurable spaces](../../../../../../measurable-space.md) is measurable when

$$
\boxed{T^{-1}(A')\in\Sigma\quad\text{for every }A'\in\Sigma'.}
$$

A real-valued function $f$ on $S$ is $\Sigma$-measurable when it is measurable from $(S,\Sigma)$ to $(\mathbb R,\mathcal B(\mathbb R))$. Equivalently each set $\{f<a\}$ is in $\Sigma$, since open half-lines generate the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md).

A [Borel measurable function](../../../../../../borel-measurable-function.md) on the real line is a function measurable with respect to the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md) on both its domain and range. Continuity is sufficient for this property, but is not required.
