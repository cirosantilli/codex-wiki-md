# Dipolar perturbations of a critically rotating cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Critically rotating self-gravitating cylinder](critically-rotating-self-gravitating-cylinder.md)

The regular $m=1$ [velocity](velocity.md) family satisfies the displayed formulas. The surface [pressure](pressure.md) condition and gravitational matching give $W_s=-\Omega_s^2R_0\eta$, while the kinematic condition is $i(\omega+\Omega_s)\eta=u_R(R_0)$. If the material radial displacement $u_R/[i(\omega+kR)]$ is continued regularly to the boundary, then $\eta=-ia$ and $\omega^2=0$: translation of an isolated cylinder has no restoring force. Using only the Eulerian [velocity](velocity.md) conditions instead leaves $\omega^2(\omega+\Omega_s)=0$. The additional surface-corotation case has $\eta=0$, $\Phi'=0$, $u_R=a(kR-\Omega_s)$ and $W=iaR[(kR)^2-\Omega_s^2]$; it satisfies the [velocity](velocity.md) equations and zero surface [pressure](pressure.md). Excluding that degenerate branch is an extra admissibility assumption, not a valid division by a nonzero frequency at every surface.
