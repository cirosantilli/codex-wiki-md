# Vinyl group

↑ **Parent:** [Functional group](functional-group.md)

The group $\mathrm{CH_2{=}CH{-}}$, attached through an [alkene](alkene.md) [carbon](carbon.md). It differs from an [allyl group](allyl-group.md), whose attachment includes an intervening methylene.
