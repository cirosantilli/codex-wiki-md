# Collimator

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collimator)

A collimator turns light from a source near its focus into a nearly parallel beam. A slit of width $x$ produces an angular width approximately $x/f$, where $f$ is the [focal length](focal-length.md).
