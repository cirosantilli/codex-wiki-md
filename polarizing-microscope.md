# Polarizing microscope

↑ **Parent:** [Optical microscope](optical-microscope.md)

A microscope that uses controlled incident and analysed [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) to study specimens. Crossed [polarizers](polarizer.md) reveal orientation-dependent [phase retardation](phase-retardation.md) in a birefringent specimen.
