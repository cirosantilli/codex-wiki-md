<h1 id="fixed-edge-euler-lagrange-equation-for-mumford-shah">Fixed-edge Euler-Lagrange equation for Mumford–Shah</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mumford–Shah functional](mumford-shah-functional.md)

For a fixed sufficiently regular [image edge](image-edge.md) set, the [Mumford–Shah functional](mumford-shah-functional.md) is a [strictly convex](strictly-convex-function.md) quadratic energy on the [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) of its complement. A [first variation](first-variation.md) gives $u-\alpha\Delta u=g$ within each region and separate one-sided [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) on free edges. A jump need not vanish across an edge. The weak solution is unique by the [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md); this does not prove uniqueness when the edge set is also optimized.
