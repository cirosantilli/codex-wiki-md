# Wavefront error

↑ **Parent:** [Wavefront](wavefront.md)

A wavefront error is the difference in [optical path length](optical-path-length.md) from a reference wavefront, often an ideal plane or sphere. Its [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) should specify the illuminated pupil and which modes, such as constant phase or image displacement, have been removed.
