# Projected mass lower bound for a bound point-mass orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic-centre point-mass orbit model](galactic-centre-point-mass-orbit-model.md)

For a bound [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md), specific [orbital energy](orbital-energy.md) $v^2/2-\mu/r$ is negative. Therefore $\mu>v^2r/2$. A projected separation $R_\perp\le r$ and a systemic-motion-corrected line-of-sight speed $|v_{\rm los}|\le v$ give the displayed lower bound. One position and one spectral [velocity](velocity.md) already constrain the mass in this model; a fitted orbit gives much more. The bound assumes a bound orbit in a point-mass potential; it is not an unrestricted enclosed-mass formula for an arbitrary extended potential.
