# RNA pseudoknot

↑ **Parent:** [Ribonucleic acid](ribonucleic-acid.md)

An [RNA pseudoknot](rna-pseudoknot.md) forms when nucleotides in a loop pair with a region outside that loop, producing an interleaved pairing topology. Some pseudoknots resist ribosomal unwinding and contribute to programmed recoding.
