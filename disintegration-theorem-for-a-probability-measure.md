# Disintegration theorem for a probability measure

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional probability](conditional-probability.md)

For a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) on a standard Borel space and a measurable map $\pi$ to another standard Borel space, there is an almost-everywhere unique [measurable probability kernel](measurable-probability-kernel.md) $y\mapsto\mu_y$ supported on $\pi^{-1}\{y\}$. It integrates to $\mu$, and $\int f\,d\mu_{\pi(x)}$ realizes [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) given $\pi$. These measures are the [conditional measures of a factor](conditional-measures-of-a-factor.md).
