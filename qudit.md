# Qudit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state](quantum-state.md)

A [qudit](qudit.md) is a quantum subsystem with $d$ distinguishable basis states and [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $\mathbb C^d$. A [qubit](qubit.md) is the special case $d=2$. Tensor products of [qudits](qudit.md) give a natural setting for [local Hamiltonians](local-hamiltonian.md) and finite-dimensional [quantum circuits](quantum-circuit-split.md).
