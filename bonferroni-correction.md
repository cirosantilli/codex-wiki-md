# Bonferroni correction

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple hypothesis testing](multiple-hypothesis-testing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonferroni_correction)

The Bonferroni correction tests each of $m$ hypotheses at level $\alpha/m$. The [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) then controls the [familywise error rate](familywise-error-rate.md) by $\alpha$ under arbitrary dependence.
