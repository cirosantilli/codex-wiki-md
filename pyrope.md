# Pyrope

↑ **Parent:** [Garnet](garnet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrope)

The magnesian aluminium-bearing [garnet](garnet.md) [mineral endmember](mineral-endmember.md). In an ideal Mg–Fe site model with other positions fixed, its [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) is $X_{\rm Mg}^3$ because three magnesium positions occur per formula unit.
