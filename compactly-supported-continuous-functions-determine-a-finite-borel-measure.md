# Compactly supported continuous functions determine a finite Borel measure

↑ **Parent:** [Probability measure](probability-measure.md)

If finite [Borel measures](borel-measure.md) $\mu$ and $\nu$ on $\mathbb R$ satisfy

$$
\int f\,d\mu=\int f\,d\nu
$$

for every compactly supported [continuous function](continuous-function.md) $f$, then $\mu=\nu$. This follows from the uniqueness part of the [Riesz representation theorem](riesz-representation-theorem.md); alternatively, continuous functions that approximate indicators from above and below recover the measure of every interval and hence every Borel set.
