# Graph minor

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_minor)

A graph minor is obtained by deleting [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), deleting [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md), and performing [edge contractions](edge-contraction.md). Equivalently, its [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) can be represented by disjoint nonempty connected sets of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in the original [graph](graph-split.md), with an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) joining two sets whenever the corresponding [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) are adjacent. These are [branch sets of a graph minor](branch-set-of-a-graph-minor.md).
