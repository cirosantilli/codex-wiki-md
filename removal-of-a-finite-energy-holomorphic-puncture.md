# Removal of a finite-energy holomorphic puncture

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoholomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md)

A [J-holomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md) on a punctured disc with finite energy and image contained in a compact target extends smoothly across the puncture when the target has a smooth tame [almost complex structure](almost-complex-manifold.md) and compatible bounded geometry. Local monotonicity controls escape from small target balls, and the elliptic small-energy estimate followed by [elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md) gives the extension. This is the step turning a finite-energy plane obtained by rescaling into a [J-holomorphic sphere](j-holomorphic-sphere.md).
