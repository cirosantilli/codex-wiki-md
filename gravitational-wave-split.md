# Gravitational wave

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_wave)

A [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave-split.md) is a propagating disturbance of [spacetime](spacetime.md) [curvature](curvature.md) produced by changing [mass](mass.md)-[energy](energy-split.md) multipole moments. In [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md), its two transverse [gravitational wave polarizations](gravitational-wave-polarization.md) alternately stretch and squeeze freely falling separations. The leading radiation from a slowly moving source is governed by its [mass quadrupole moment](mass-quadrupole-moment.md), rather than by its total [mass](mass.md) alone.

The experimental breakthrough was [GW150914](gw150914.md): observed on 14 September 2015 and published on 11 February 2016. The [LIGO Scientific Collaboration](ligo-scientific-collaboration.md) and [Virgo Collaboration](virgo-collaboration.md) reported a merging pair of [black holes](black-hole-split.md), using the two [LIGO](ligo.md) detectors at Hanford and Livingston; Virgo was being upgraded and did not observe this event.[Abstract; II Observation, pp. 1–3; received and publication dates on p. 1](observation-of-gravitational-waves-from-a-binary-black-hole-merger-by-b-p-abbott-and-others.md)

The 2017 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) recognized [Rainer Weiss](rainer-weiss.md), [Barry C. Barish](barry-c-barish.md) and [Kip S. Thorne](kip-s-thorne.md) for contributions to [LIGO](ligo.md) and [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave-split.md) observation. [Rainer Weiss](rainer-weiss.md) developed interferometric detection and its noise analysis; [Barry C. Barish](barry-c-barish.md) led the project into construction and operation; [Kip S. Thorne](kip-s-thorne.md) developed its theoretical and astrophysical foundations. The experiment depended on a large collaboration.[3 October 2017 announcement, laureates and account of their complementary contributions](press-release-the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2017-by-royal-swedish-academy-of-sciences.md)
