# Deterministic boundary in an upper-tail chance constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Chance constraint](chance-constraint.md)

A deterministic demand has [probability](probability.md) one of meeting or exceeding its mean. Thus replacing a positive-variance [Gaussian chance constraint](exact-gaussian-chance-constraint.md) by its naive zero-variance limit with a non-strict capacity inequality loses the boundary condition. The example $X=0$, $C=0$, $\varepsilon=e^{-1}$ satisfies $m+\phi\sqrt v\leq C$ for finite $\phi$, but violates the chance requirement.
