# Codimension of an algebraic subvariety

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of an algebraic set](dimension-of-an-algebraic-set.md)

For an irreducible closed subvariety $Y$ of an irreducible variety $X$, its codimension is $\dim X-\dim Y$. In an affine chart it also equals the height of the corresponding [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md), by the dimension formula for finite-type domains over a field.
