# Fully observed Markov source

↑ **Parent:** [Markov information source](markov-information-source.md)

Each emitted symbol identifies the state of a stationary [finite Markov chain](finite-markov-chain.md). Here the [alphabet](alphabet.md) is the [state space](state-space.md) itself. Its [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) $P$ and [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) $\pi$ determine the [entropy rate of a fully observed Markov source](entropy-rate-of-a-fully-observed-markov-source.md).
