# Stellar pressure-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md)

This tensor integrates the local [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md), where $\sigma_{ij}=\langle(v_i-u_i)(v_j-u_j)\rangle$ is the [velocity](velocity.md) [covariance](covariance.md). Half its trace is the random [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). It enters the [tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md) with coefficient one.
