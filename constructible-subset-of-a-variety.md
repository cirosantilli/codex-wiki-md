# Constructible subset of a variety

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md)

A constructible subset is a finite union of locally closed subsets in the [Zariski topology](zariski-topology.md). A dense constructible subset contains a dense open subset. Constructibility alone is weaker than closedness, but a [constructible subgroup is closed](constructible-subgroup-is-closed.md).
