# Independence extended from generating pi-systems

↑ **Parent:** [Independent sigma-algebras](independent-sigma-algebras.md)

If two pi-systems satisfy the probability factorization pairwise, their generated sigma-algebras are independent. For one event in the first family, the events in the second sigma-algebra satisfying the factorization form a Dynkin system; apply [Dynkin lemma](dynkin-lemma.md), then repeat with the roles reversed.
