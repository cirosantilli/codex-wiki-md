# Atwood machine

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atwood_machine)

An [Atwood machine](atwood-machine.md) couples two hanging [masses](mass.md) by an inextensible [mechanical string](mechanical-string.md) over a [pulley](pulley.md). With ideal massless hardware, [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) gives the downward [acceleration](acceleration.md) of the heavier [mass](mass.md) as $a=g(m_2-m_1)/(m_1+m_2)$. To prove it, write $m_2g-T=m_2a$ and $T-m_1g=m_1a$, then add. The apparatus associated with [George Atwood](george-atwood.md) turns a small difference of weights into slow, measurable [acceleration](acceleration.md).
