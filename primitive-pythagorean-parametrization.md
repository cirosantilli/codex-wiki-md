# Primitive Pythagorean parametrization

↑ **Parent:** [Pythagorean triple](pythagorean-triple.md)

Every primitive [Pythagorean triple](pythagorean-triple.md), after exchanging its legs, has this form with $m>n>0$, $\gcd(m,n)=1$ and opposite parity. Its area is $mn(m-n)(m+n)$. The factors $m,n,m-n,m+n$ are pairwise coprime, because $m\pm n$ are odd. Conversely these conditions give a primitive triple. The parametrization follows by factoring the relatively prime integers $(c+b)/2$ and $(c-b)/2$ when $a$ is the even leg.
