# Hyperbolic metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_metric_space)

For $\delta\geq0$, a [geodesic metric space](geodesic-metric-space.md) is $\delta$-hyperbolic when each side of every [metric geodesic triangle](metric-geodesic-triangle.md) lies in the closed $\delta$-neighbourhood of the other two sides.
