# Vacuum-boundary degree as a defect charge

↑ **Parent:** [Topological charge](topological-charge.md)

A normalized vacuum field on a sphere surrounding a core can define $S^{d-1}\to S^{d-1}$ with integer [topological degree](topological-degree.md). Nonzero degree obstructs its extension through the enclosed ball while staying in the [vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md). This accounts for the [winding number](winding-number.md) of a [vortex](phase-vortex.md) and the spherical degree of a [magnetic monopole](magnetic-monopole.md), with the appropriate vacuum targets.
