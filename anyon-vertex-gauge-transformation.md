# Anyon vertex gauge transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon fusion tree](anyon-fusion-tree.md)

A change of the chosen [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of every [anyon fusion space](anyon-fusion-space.md). In a multiplicity-free theory each basis change is an independent [wave phase](phase-waves.md) $u^{ab}_c$. Splitting vertices acquire $u^{ab}_c$ and adjoint fusion vertices acquire its inverse. A scalar diagram is invariant under these choices when its splitting and fusion vertex factors cancel. A closed diagram with independently specified unmatched vertex tensors need not be invariant.
