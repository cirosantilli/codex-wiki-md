# Weinberg angle

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weinberg_angle)

The [Weinberg angle](weinberg-angle.md) rotates the neutral [gauge fields](gauge-field.md) into the [photon](photon.md) and [Z boson](z-boson.md):

$$
\begin{pmatrix}A_\mu\\ Z_\mu\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}\sin\theta_W&\cos\theta_W\\ \cos\theta_W&-\sin\theta_W\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}W^3_\mu\\ B_\mu\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\tan\theta_W=\frac{g'}{g},\qquad
e=g\sin\theta_W=g'\cos\theta_W.
$$

$g$ belongs to $SU(2)_L$, $g'$ to $U(1)_Y$, and $e$ is the positive magnitude of the [electron](electron.md) [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). These definitions use $Q=T_3+Y$; doubling the definition of [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) requires halving its coefficient in the [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md). The [electromagnetic coupling of a weak multiplet](electromagnetic-coupling-of-a-weak-multiplet.md) follows by substituting this rotation.

For a [fermion](fermion.md) component, the corresponding neutral coefficient is

$$
g\cos\theta_W T_3-g'\sin\theta_WY
=\frac{g}{\cos\theta_W}\left(T_3-Q\sin^2\theta_W\right).
$$

For a left-handed charged [lepton](lepton.md), $T_3=-1/2$ and $Q=-1$, giving $g_L=-1/2+\sin^2\theta_W$. Its right-handed component has $T_3=0$, giving $g_R=\sin^2\theta_W$. This algebra links the [neutral current](neutral-current.md) to the electromagnetic charge assignment. Together with the [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md), it also gives the [tree-level electroweak gauge-boson masses](tree-level-electroweak-gauge-boson-masses.md) with $m_W/m_Z=\cos\theta_W$; precise comparisons require a defined renormalization convention and [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md).
