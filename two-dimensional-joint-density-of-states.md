# Two-dimensional joint density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md)

For two isotropic parabolic subbands, the [joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md) per area and per independent channel is $\mu/(2\pi\hbar^2)\,\Theta(\hbar\omega-E_{\rm edge})$. The constant is a two-dimensional phase-space effect. Degeneracy and optical [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) determine the actual step weights.
