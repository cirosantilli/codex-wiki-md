# Componentwise bigness on a projective scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Big real divisor](big-real-divisor.md)

For a possibly reducible [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md), componentwise bigness means that a real Cartier class restricts to a [big real divisor](big-real-divisor.md) on every reduced irreducible component. This specifies the convention needed by positivity arguments that treat all curves. Maximal total section growth alone is weaker: on $\mathbb P^2\amalg\mathbb P^2$, the bundle $(\mathcal O(1),\mathcal O(-1))$ has quadratic total growth but negative degree on every line in the second component. Thus its negative curves cannot be confined to finitely many divisors.
