↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury(II)_oxide)

The [chemical compound](chemical-compound.md) $\mathrm{HgO}$. Thermal decomposition can release [oxygen](oxygen.md) and produce [mercury](mercury-element.md); historical preparations of the red oxide featured in [Joseph Priestley](joseph-priestley.md)'s and [Antoine Lavoisier](antoine-lavoisier.md)'s investigations.
