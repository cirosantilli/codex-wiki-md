# Chiral spinor superfield

↑ **Parent:** [Chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md)

A [chiral spinor superfield](chiral-spinor-superfield.md) carries a [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md) index and satisfies $\bar D_{\dot\alpha}\Psi_\beta=0$. The [chiral field-strength superfield](chiral-field-strength-superfield.md) is a fermionic example: its lowest component is the [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) [gaugino](gaugino.md). Its spinor index distinguishes it from a scalar [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md), even though both obey the same chirality constraint.
