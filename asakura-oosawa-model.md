# Asakura-Oosawa model

↑ **Parent:** [Depletion force](depletion-force.md)

The [Asakura-Oosawa model](asakura-oosawa-model.md) treats dilute nonadsorbing [polymer](polymer-split.md) coils as mutually interpenetrable ideal particles excluded from a layer around each [colloid](colloid.md). For colloid radius $a$ and polymer exclusion thickness $R_g$, two exclusion spheres of radius $R_e=a+R_g$ overlap when $2a\leq R<2R_e$. Adding their two [spherical cap](spherical-cap.md) volumes gives $V_{\mathrm{ov}}=\pi(4R_e+R)(2R_e-R)^2/12$. Multiplication by $-\Pi_p$ gives the displayed pair [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md), with zero depletion interaction beyond $2R_e$. The range is controlled by polymer size and the depth by reservoir polymer concentration. At larger polymer concentration, polymer interactions and many-body overlap effects require care.
