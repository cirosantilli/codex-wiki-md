# Multi-period dividend pricing identity

↑ **Parent:** [Discounted dividend gains](discounted-dividend-gains.md)

For an [integrable](integrability.md) discrete trading strategy under an [equivalent martingale measure](risk-neutral-measure.md), discounted [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md) plus accumulated discounted strategy payments is a [martingale](martingale-split.md). Conditioning its terminal value gives the displayed identity. A formula involving future payments alone requires terminal liquidation, $V_n=0$, or inclusion of that terminal value in the final payment. For an [attainable claim](attainable-european-contingent-claim.md) paid only at maturity, liquidation yields its usual conditional discounted [expected value](expected-value.md).
