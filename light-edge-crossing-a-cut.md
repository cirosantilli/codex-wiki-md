# Light edge crossing a cut

↑ **Parent:** [Cut (graph theory)](cut-graph-theory.md)

An [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) of minimum [edge weight](edge-weight.md) among those crossing a specified [cut of a graph](cut-graph-theory.md). It exists when the crossing set is nonempty. Several tied edges can all be light; lightness does not imply uniqueness or membership in every [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md).
