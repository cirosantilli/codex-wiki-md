# Source equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Equilibrium point of a dynamical system](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md)

A hyperbolic [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is a source when all its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) have positive real parts. It is a [sink equilibrium](sink-equilibrium.md) for the reversed flow. Every sufficiently close nonstationary trajectory leaves its neighborhood in forward time. This is distinct from an external forcing source.
