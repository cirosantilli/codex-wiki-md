# Parallelization of a Lie group by left translations

↑ **Parent:** [Left-invariant vector field](left-invariant-vector-field.md)

A basis $T_a$ of the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of a [Lie group](lie-group.md) gives a global [frame of a vector bundle](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) of its [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) by $X_a(g)=(dL_g)_eT_a$. Left translation is a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md), so the [left-invariant vector fields](left-invariant-vector-field.md) are independent everywhere. Every [Lie group](lie-group.md) is therefore a [parallelizable manifold](parallelizable-manifold.md).
