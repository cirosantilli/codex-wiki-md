# Chooser with positive exercise cost

↑ **Parent:** [Chooser option](chooser-option.md)

At its choice time this claim permits purchase of either of two later-expiring [European options](european-contingent-claim.md) for an exercise cost $K$, or abandonment. Its payoff is $\max(p-K,c-K,0)$. For common strike $K_3$ and remaining time $\tau$, [put-call parity](put-call-parity.md) places the branch crossing at $D=K_3e^{-r\tau}$. In the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md), both branch values there equal $D[2\Phi(\sigma\sqrt\tau/2)-1]$. An exercise cost above this minimum creates a central interval in which neither choice is worth exercising.
