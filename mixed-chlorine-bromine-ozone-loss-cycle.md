# Mixed chlorine-bromine ozone-loss cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

In the productive mixed-radical pathway, [chlorine monoxide](chlorine-monoxide.md) and [BrO](bromine-monoxide.md) yield [chlorine](chlorine.md) and [bromine](bromine.md) atoms; subsequent atom reactions with [ozone](ozone.md) regenerate the oxides. The net cycle consumes two [ozone](ozone.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) per radical-pair event, giving $L=2k[\mathrm{ClO}][\mathrm{BrO}]$ when the subsequent steps are fast. The branching of a real [chlorine monoxide](chlorine-monoxide.md)–[BrO](bromine-monoxide.md) reaction must be included if not already absorbed into its specified effective rate coefficient.
