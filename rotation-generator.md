# Rotation generator

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation in three dimensions](rotation-in-three-dimensions.md)

In the three-dimensional vector representation, the Hermitian [matrices](matrix.md) $(J_k)_{ij}=-i\epsilon_{kij}$ generate active [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) by the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) $R(\theta,\mathbf n)=\exp(-i\theta n^kJ_k)$. They satisfy $[J_i,J_j]=i\epsilon_{ijk}J_k$. The real antisymmetric [matrices](matrix.md) $A_k=-iJ_k$ are the corresponding [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) generators of $SO(3)$. Their [commutator](commutator.md) gives the leading change produced by interchanging two infinitesimal [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md).
