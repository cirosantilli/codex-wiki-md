# Nearly Hamiltonian system

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)

A [nearly Hamiltonian system](nearly-hamiltonian-system.md) is a weak perturbation of a [Hamiltonian system](hamiltonian-system.md). Along its paths, $\dot H=\epsilon\nabla H\cdot R$. Averaging this drift over an unperturbed [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) is the [energy balance method](energy-balance-method.md); a simple zero of the averaged drift selects a persistent nearby [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) for sufficiently small $\epsilon$. A multiple zero requires higher-order analysis.
