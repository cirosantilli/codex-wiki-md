# Alternating dyadic intervals contain disjoint IP sets

↑ **Parent:** [IP set](ip-set.md)

Let $E$ be the union of integer intervals $[2^{2j},2^{2j+1})$ for $j\geq0$. Every nonempty sum of distinct $4^j$ has largest summand $4^J$ and lies in $[4^J,2\cdot4^J)$, since the sum of all smaller summands is $(4^J-1)/3$. Thus $\operatorname{FS}(4^j)\subseteq E$. Doubling gives $\operatorname{FS}(2\cdot4^j)\subseteq E^c$. Both sides are [IP sets](ip-set.md). Each can be adjoined to the [IP-star filter](ip-star-filter.md) while preserving the [finite intersection property](finite-intersection-property.md), yielding two distinct [IP ultrafilters](ultrafilter-with-finite-sums-members.md).
