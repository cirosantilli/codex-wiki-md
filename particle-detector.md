# Particle detector

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_detector)

A [particle detector](particle-detector.md) converts an interaction involving a [particle](particle-physics.md) into a readable signal. A [cloud chamber](cloud-chamber.md) is a particularly direct example: tiny [liquid](liquid.md) drops turn an invisible [ion](ion.md) trail into a photographable line.
