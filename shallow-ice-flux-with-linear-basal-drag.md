# Shallow-ice flux with linear basal drag

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian shallow-ice flux](newtonian-shallow-ice-flux.md)

On a horizontal bed, the [shallow-ice approximation](shallow-ice-approximation.md) with a [linear basal drag law](linear-basal-drag-law.md) gives $u=-(\rho g/\mu)h_x(h/\beta+hz-z^2/2)$ and $q=-(\rho g/\mu)(h^3/3+h^2/\beta)h_x$. With no [ice accumulation](ice-accumulation.md) or [ice ablation](ice-ablation.md), [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) is $h_t+q_x=0$. Internal shear supplies the cubic mobility and [basal sliding](basal-sliding.md) the quadratic mobility.
