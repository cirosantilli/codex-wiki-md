# Absorption spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md)

The wavelength- or frequency-dependent absorption of a specified specimen. Composition, [optical path length](optical-path-length.md), [concentration](concentration.md), linewidths and illumination determine its transmitted colour; a single [band gap](band-gap.md) does not specify that colour.
