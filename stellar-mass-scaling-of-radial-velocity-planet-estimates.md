# Stellar-mass scaling of radial-velocity planet estimates

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum companion mass from radial velocity](minimum-companion-mass-from-radial-velocity.md)

At fixed observed period and semi-amplitude, $M_p\sin i\propto M_\star^{2/3}$ and $a\propto M_\star^{1/3}$. At fixed [planet](planet.md) [mass](mass.md) and amplitude threshold, the largest circular orbit instead scales as $M_\star^{-1}$. A revised stellar [radius](radius.md) affects transit-derived [planet](planet.md) radii but not these leading dynamical relations.
