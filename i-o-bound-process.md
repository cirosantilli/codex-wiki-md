<h1 id="i-o-bound-process">I/O-bound process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Process (computing)](process-computing.md)

An [operating-system process](process-computing.md) whose progress is mainly limited by [input-output](input-output.md) rather than useful [CPU](central-processing-unit.md) execution. It often has short [CPU bursts](cpu-burst.md) separated by blocking waits.
