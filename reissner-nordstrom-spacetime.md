# Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)

The four-dimensional charged spherically symmetric electrovacuum solution has radial function $f=1-2M/r+Q^2/r^2$. For $0<|Q|<M$, its outer and inner horizon radii are $r_\pm=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-Q^2}$. A complete two-ended spatial bridge has a [maximal Cauchy development](maximal-cauchy-development.md) ending at smoothly extendible inner [Cauchy horizons](cauchy-horizon.md) in the exact solution.
