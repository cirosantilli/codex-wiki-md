# Transit survey completeness

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit photometry](exoplanet-transit-photometry.md)

The probability that a genuinely transiting [planet](planet.md) with specified parameters is recovered by a survey and passes its selection procedure. It includes [light curve](light-curve.md) noise, observing gaps, search algorithms and vetting. It differs from [geometric transit probability](geometric-transit-probability.md); synthetic-transit injection and recovery can estimate it.
