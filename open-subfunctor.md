# Open subfunctor

↑ **Parent:** [Functor represented by a scheme](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md)

A [subfunctor](subfunctor.md) $H\subseteq F$ on the opposite of the [category](category-split.md) of [schemes](scheme.md) is open if every [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) $h_T\to F$ pulls it back to a [functor represented by a scheme](functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) $U$, with the projection represented by an [open immersion](open-immersion.md) $U\hookrightarrow T$. Thus membership in $H$ defines an [open subscheme](open-subscheme.md) in every family parameterized by $T$. This condition is preserved by further [pullbacks](pullback-category-theory.md).
