<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of the [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) is $r^2+2r+2=0$, with roots $-1\pm i$. A real pair of [homogeneous solutions](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{y_1(x)=e^{-x}\cos x,\qquad y_2(x)=e^{-x}\sin x}.
$$

Their [Wronskian](../../../../../wronskian.md), with the convention $W=y_1y_2'-y_1'y_2$, is

$$
W(x)=e^{-2x}\{\cos x(\cos x-\sin x)+(\sin x+\cos x)\sin x\}
=\boxed{e^{-2x}\ne0}.
$$

Thus the two functions are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md) throughout the interval and span its [homogeneous solutions](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md).
