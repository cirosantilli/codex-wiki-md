<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose the right-end [boundary layer](../../../../../boundary-layer.md) has width $\ell(\epsilon)$, and let $X=(1-x)/\ell$. An order-one change of $y$ over this width gives $\epsilon y''=O(\epsilon/\ell^2)$. Near $x=1$, the leading terms $y-x^2$ are order one. The [dominant balance](../../../../../dominant-balance.md) is therefore

$$
\frac\epsilon{\ell^2}\sim1.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\ell=\sqrt\epsilon,\qquad X=\frac{1-x}{\sqrt\epsilon}.}
$$

The [inner variable](../../../../../inner-variable.md) satisfies $X\geq0$ inside the domain and resolves the [reaction boundary layer with square-root thickness](../../../../../reaction-boundary-layer-with-square-root-thickness.md).
