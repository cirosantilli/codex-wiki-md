# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the differential equations 2 course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Differential Equations II paper, sat on 22 June 2023. Source: [released examination PDF, parent PDF pages 118–120; questions on pages 119–120](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_part_a_examinati/response/2820674/attach/3/FOI%20202410%20944%20Data.pdf#page=118). Question statements are omitted. In Question 2(b), the usual interpretation that the forcing extends continuously to the closed interval is distinguished from continuity only in the interior.
