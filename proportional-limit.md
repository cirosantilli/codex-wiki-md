# Proportional limit

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

The [proportional limit](proportional-limit.md) bounds the loading interval over which [stress](stress.md) is proportional to [strain](strain.md). It does not by itself establish the last load that permits complete recovery of [elastic deformation](elastic-deformation.md).
