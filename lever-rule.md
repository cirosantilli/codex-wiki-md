# Lever rule

↑ **Parent:** [Phase diagram](phase-diagram.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lever_rule)

The lever rule finds phase fractions from a horizontal tie line on a [phase diagram](phase-diagram.md). For solid and liquid [concentrations](concentration.md) $C_s,C_l$ and bulk [concentration](concentration.md) $\overline C$, conservation gives $\overline C=(1-\phi)C_s+\phi C_l$. Thus $\phi=(\overline C-C_s)/(C_l-C_s)$ is the [liquid fraction](liquid-fraction.md), provided $C_s\le\overline C\le C_l$. For pure solid, $\phi=\overline C/C_l$.
