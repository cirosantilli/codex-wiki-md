# Star (graph theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Complete bipartite graph](complete-bipartite-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_(graph_theory))

A star graph is the [complete bipartite graph](complete-bipartite-graph.md) with one centre and $m$ leaves. Any pair of its [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) meet at the centre.
