# Sub-Doppler cooling discovery thermometry

↑ **Parent:** [Sub-Doppler cooling](sub-doppler-cooling.md)

The 1988 experiment loaded [sodium](sodium.md) [optical molasses](optical-molasses.md) from a slowed [atomic beam](atomic-beam.md). After stopping loading, a shutter extinguished the six cooling beams. Released atoms crossed a lower probe; a [photomultiplier](photomultiplier.md) recorded [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) versus arrival time. Calculations integrated the measured initial cloud distribution and an assumed three-dimensional [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md), including [gravity](gravity.md) and detection geometry. The minimum was $43\pm20\ \mathrm{\mu K}$, versus the $240\ \mathrm{\mu K}$ [Doppler cooling limit](doppler-cooling-limit.md).[pp. 169–170, preparation, TOF apparatus, Fig. 1 and uncertainty](observation-of-atoms-laser-cooled-below-the-doppler-limit-by-paul-d-lett-and-others.md)

Probe separation, probe intensity, cloud density and shutter timing were varied. Fountain-height, horizontal-spreading and release/recapture measurements also gave below-limit results. Geometry remained an uncertainty; agreement among complementary thermometers made a detector artifact less plausible. These measured released atoms, rather than an in-situ [velocity](velocity.md) distribution.[pp. 170–171, controls, four thermometers and release limitation](observation-of-atoms-laser-cooled-below-the-doppler-limit-by-paul-d-lett-and-others.md)

[Steven Chu](steven-chu.md)'s account connects the explanation to [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md), [Stark effect](stark-effect.md) shifts and spatially changing [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md); [Claude Cohen-Tannoudji](claude-cohen-tannoudji.md)'s lecture separates this mechanism from subrecoil schemes. Their lectures and [William D. Phillips](william-d-phillips.md)'s describe complementary experimental and theoretical routes.[pp. 693–695, Fig. 6](the-manipulation-of-neutral-particles-by-steven-chu.md)[pp. 710–713, §§II–III](nobel-lecture-manipulating-atoms-with-photons-by-claude-cohen-tannoudji.md)
