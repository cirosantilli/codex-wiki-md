# Bounded below derived category of sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Derived category of sheaves](derived-category-of-sheaves.md)

The category $D^+(X)$ is obtained from bounded below [cochain complexes](cochain-complex.md) of [sheaves](sheaf-mathematics.md) by passing to cochain-homotopy classes of maps and then inverting [quasi-isomorphisms](quasi-isomorphism.md). It can equally be modeled by injective resolutions. The cohomology-sheaf functors descend to this localization, and an object is zero exactly when all its [cohomology sheaves](cohomology-sheaf.md) vanish.
