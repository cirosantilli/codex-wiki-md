# Near Infrared Imager and Slitless Spectrograph

↑ **Parent:** [James Webb Space Telescope](james-webb-space-telescope.md)

The NIRISS single-object slitless spectroscopy mode measures time-series spectra from $0.6$ to $2.8\,\mu\mathrm m$. It supports [exoplanet transmission spectra](exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md) and eclipse observations, as described in [the instrument guide](https://jwst-docs.stsci.edu/jwst-near-infrared-imager-and-slitless-spectrograph/niriss-observing-modes/niriss-single-object-slitless-spectroscopy).
