# Massless induced representation of the Poincare double cover

↑ **Parent:** [Massless particle little group](massless-particle-little-group.md)

For a future null standard momentum $k$, choose [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md) $L(p)k=p$ and a [unitary irreducible representation](unitary-irreducible-representation.md) $d$ of its [little group](little-group.md). The [Wigner rotation](wigner-rotation.md) $R(\Lambda,p)=L(\Lambda p)^{-1}\Lambda L(p)$ acts on the internal state. The resulting [induced representation](induced-representation.md) acts on wavefunctions with invariant measure $d^3p/(2p^0)$. Trivial little-group translations give fixed [helicity](helicity.md); nontrivial translations give a [continuous-spin representation](continuous-spin-representation.md) with infinitely many polarization states.
