# Sphere bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_bundle)

After choosing a fiber metric on a rank-$d$ [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E\to B$, its sphere bundle consists of the unit vectors and has fiber $S^{d-1}$. Different choices of metric give isomorphic sphere bundles.
