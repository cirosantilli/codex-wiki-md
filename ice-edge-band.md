# Ice-edge band

↑ **Parent:** [Marginal ice zone](marginal-ice-zone.md)

An ice-edge band is an elongated concentration of [ice floes](ice-floe.md) separated from neighbouring ice by open water. In one mechanism, an off-ice wind opens [polynyas](polynya.md); short wind waves then exert [wave radiation stress](radiation-stress.md) on their downwind floes. Wave forcing compacts floes into bands, opposed by incoming longer-period swell. Collisions and mergers can produce fewer, larger bands.
