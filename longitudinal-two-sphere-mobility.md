# Longitudinal two-sphere mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic interaction](hydrodynamic-interaction.md)

For well-separated spheres moving along their line of centres, the leading mobility matrix has diagonal entries $m_i=(6\pi\mu a_i)^{-1}$ and off-diagonal entries $h=(4\pi\mu\ell)^{-1}$. This is the axial [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) interaction. Finite-size and repeated-reflection corrections enter at higher order.
