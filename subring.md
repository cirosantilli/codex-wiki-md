# Subring

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)

A subset closed under addition, additive inverses and multiplication, with the induced operations. Under the unital convention used here it also contains the identity of $R$. A subring of an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) is again an integral domain. The [ring of invariants](ring-of-invariants.md) is one example: fixed elements remain fixed under all ring operations.
