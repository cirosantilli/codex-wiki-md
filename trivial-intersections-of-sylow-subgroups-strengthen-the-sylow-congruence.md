# Trivial intersections of Sylow subgroups strengthen the Sylow congruence

↑ **Parent:** [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md)

Suppose distinct [Sylow subgroups](sylow-subgroup.md) for $p$ in a [finite group](finite-group.md) have trivial intersections. Conjugation by one of them, $P$, fixes $P$. The [stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of any other $Q$ is $H=P\cap N_G(Q)$. Since $H$ normalizes $Q$, the product $HQ$ is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of $p$-power order; maximality of $Q$ gives $H\leq Q$. Thus $H\leq P\cap Q=1$. All other [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) have size $|P|$, proving the congruence.
