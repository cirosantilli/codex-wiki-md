# Universal covering by the modular lambda function

↑ **Parent:** [Modular lambda function](modular-lambda-function.md)

The [modular lambda function](modular-lambda-function.md) is invariant under the projective level-two [principal congruence subgroup](principal-congruence-subgroup.md). That group acts freely, and its quotient is the [level-two modular curve](level-two-modular-curve.md). Adding its three [modular cusps](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) gives a sphere, and $\lambda$ has cusp values $0,1,\infty$, with simple orders in exponential cusp coordinates. Therefore it identifies the quotient with the sphere minus those three points. Since the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) is simply connected, this is a [universal covering map](universal-cover.md), with the projective level-two group as its [deck transformation group](deck-transformation-group.md).
