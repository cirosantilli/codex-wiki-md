<h1 id="weyl-integration-formula-for-su-2">Weyl integration formula for SU(2)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Haar measure](haar-measure.md)

For a [class function](class-function.md) on [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) and normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md), the displayed formula reduces a group integral to its conjugacy-class angle. The weight follows by identifying [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) with the unit sphere in four real dimensions: writing a unit quaternion as $\cos\theta+\mathbf n\sin\theta$ gives spherical volume proportional to $\sin^2\theta\,d\theta\,d\mathbf n$. Since $\int_0^\pi\sin^2\theta\,d\theta=\pi/2$, normalization gives $2/\pi$.
