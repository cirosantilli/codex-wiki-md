# Coulomb bound state

↑ **Parent:** [Bound state](bound-state.md)

A Coulomb bound state is a physical normalizable eigenfunction for an attractive potential $V(r)=-\gamma/r$, $\gamma>0$. For the usual regular boundary condition at the origin its energies are the displayed values, $N=1,2,\ldots$. The spherically symmetric states have [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) zero; [series-termination quantization of the Coulomb radial equation](series-termination-quantization-of-the-coulomb-radial-equation.md) constructs them as a polynomial times a decaying exponential. Square integrability on the punctured radial interval alone does not replace the physical condition at the origin.
