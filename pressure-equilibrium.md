# Pressure equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanical equilibrium](mechanical-equilibrium.md)

Two [fluids](fluid.md) at a stationary, freely movable interface require equal [pressures](pressure-split.md) when [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) and other normal stresses are negligible. This local pressure equilibrium does not require equal [temperatures](temperature-split.md) or [mass densities](density.md). Curvature with nonzero [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) or an applied load changes the normal-stress balance. Comparing points at different heights also requires the [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) condition.
