# Du Bois-Reymond theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

There are [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) whose [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) diverges at a prescribed point. In contrast, the [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md) is nonnegative and has integral one, so [Fejér summation is a uniform-norm contraction](fejer-summation-is-a-uniform-norm-contraction.md). The resulting [Fejér sums](fejer-sum.md) need not be positive: for the [constant function](constant-function.md) $f=-1$, every sum is $-1$.
