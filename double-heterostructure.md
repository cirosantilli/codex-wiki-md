# Double heterostructure

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor heterostructure](semiconductor-heterostructure.md)

A [double heterostructure](double-heterostructure.md) sandwiches one [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) between two layers of another, for example [gallium arsenide](gallium-arsenide.md) between [aluminium gallium arsenide](aluminium-gallium-arsenide.md) layers. Suitable [band offsets](band-offset.md) confine injected [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md) centrally.[§II, Fig. 1, pp. 768–769](nobel-lecture-the-double-heterostructure-concept-and-its-applications-in-physics-electronics-and-technology-by-zhores-ivanovich-alferov.md)
