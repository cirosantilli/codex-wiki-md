# Neutrino-electron elastic scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md)

An elastic [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) of a [neutrino](neutrino.md) with an [electron](electron.md). For a stationary [electron](electron.md) of [mass](mass.md) $m$ and an effectively massless incident [neutrino](neutrino.md) of [energy](energy-split.md) $E$, the maximum recoil [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) in [natural units](natural-units.md) is $T_{\max}=2E^2/(m+2E)$. This kinematic limit does not specify the interaction probability.
