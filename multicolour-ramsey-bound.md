# Multicolour Ramsey bound

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey theory](ramsey-theory-split.md)

Let $R_k(3)$ be the least order of a [complete graph](complete-graph.md) whose every $k$-edge-colouring has a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md). At a vertex of a graph of order $k(R_{k-1}(3)-1)+2$, some colour joins at least $R_{k-1}(3)$ neighbours. An edge of that colour among these neighbours completes a triangle; otherwise the neighbours use at most $k-1$ colours and contain a triangle by induction. This proves the recurrence and the weaker explicit bound $R_k(3)\le3k!$.
