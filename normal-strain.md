# Normal strain

↑ **Parent:** [Strain](strain.md)

The fractional change in the length of a material line in a specified direction. For infinitesimal deformation with unit direction $\mathbf n$, it is $\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\varepsilon\mathbf n$, where $\boldsymbol\varepsilon$ is the small [strain](strain.md) tensor.
