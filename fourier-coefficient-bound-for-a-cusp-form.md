# Fourier coefficient bound for a cusp form

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant norm of a modular form](invariant-norm-of-a-modular-form.md)

If $f(\tau)=\sum_{n\geq1}a_nq^n$ is a weight-$k$ level-one cusp form, boundedness of its [invariant norm](invariant-norm-of-a-modular-form.md) gives

$$
|a_n|=O(n^{k/2}).
$$

Integrate $f(x+iy)e^{-2\pi inx}$ over one period and choose $y=1/n$.
