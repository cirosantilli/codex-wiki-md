# Band edges of a repulsive delta-comb potential

↑ **Parent:** [Kronig-Penney model](kronig-penney-model.md)

For $V(x)=\alpha\sum_j\delta(x-ja)$ with $\alpha>0$, put $P=m\alpha a/\hbar^2$ and $x=ka$. The [Floquet discriminant of the delta-comb Kronig-Penney model](floquet-discriminant-of-the-delta-comb-kronig-penney-model.md) is $f(x)=\cos x+P\sin x/x$. Extended [Bloch states](bloch-state.md) require $|f(x)|\leq1$. Since $f(0)=1+P>1$, there is a positive lower band edge; a repulsive potential does not support negative-energy states in this energy convention.

The $j$th positive-energy band ends at $x=j\pi$. Its lower edge lies in $((j-1)\pi,j\pi)$ and solves $f(x)=(-1)^{j-1}$. A finite periodic ring selects only the discrete allowed values of [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) within these bands. Nonzero [band gaps](band-gap.md) do not by themselves imply an insulator: the [band filling](band-filling.md) must also place the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) in a gap.
