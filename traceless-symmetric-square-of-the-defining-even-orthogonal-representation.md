# Traceless symmetric square of the defining even orthogonal representation

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric square](symmetric-square.md)

For $n\geq2$, contraction with the invariant [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) splits off the trivial line in the [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) of the defining [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md) [representation](group-representation.md). Its [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is the traceless [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md). It contains a [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md) of [weight](weight-representation-theory.md) $2e_1$, and the [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) gives $(n+1)(2n-1)$, exactly its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). [Complete reducibility of compact-group representations](complete-reducibility-of-compact-group-representations.md) therefore makes that [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md). The entire [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) is reducible because of the invariant line.
