# Degenerate Killing horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md)

A [degenerate Killing horizon](degenerate-killing-horizon.md) has zero [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md). In the radial quotient of an [extremal black hole](extremal-black-hole.md), a double horizon root produces a pole in the [tortoise coordinate](tortoise-coordinate.md) rather than the logarithm of a simple root. Such a horizon has no bifurcation surface of the usual nondegenerate type.
