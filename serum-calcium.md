# Serum calcium

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium in biology](calcium-in-biology.md)

Serum calcium is the concentration of calcium in blood serum. It can be an [exposure](exposure.md) in an [observational study](observational-study.md) or [Mendelian randomization](mendelian-randomization.md), but genetically changing it need not reproduce the effect of changing dietary [calcium intake](calcium-intake.md).
