<h1 id="exponential-martingale-of-a-levy-process">Exponential martingale of a Lévy process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic exponent of a Lévy process](characteristic-exponent-of-a-levy-process.md)

With the convention $\mathbb E e^{iuX_t}=e^{t\psi(u)}$, this complex process is a [martingale](martingale-split.md) for each real $u$. Its absolute value is the deterministic finite number $e^{-t\operatorname{Re}\psi(u)}$. Conditioning on the past at time $s$ and using [independent increments](independent-increments.md) multiplies $e^{iuX_s-t\psi(u)}$ by $e^{(t-s)\psi(u)}$, giving $M_s^u$. Under the opposite exponent convention the sign of the compensating term reverses.
