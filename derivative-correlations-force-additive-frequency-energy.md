# Derivative correlations force additive frequency energy

↑ **Parent:** [Additive energy of a frequency graph](additive-energy-of-a-frequency-graph.md)

Suppose $|f|\leq1$ on a finite cyclic group, $H$ has density $\beta$, and for each $h\in H$ the [multiplicative derivative](multiplicative-derivative.md) has a [Fourier coefficient on a finite abelian group](fourier-coefficient-on-a-finite-abelian-group.md) of magnitude at least $\eta$ at $\theta(h)$. Align the correlation phases, apply [Cauchy-Schwarz](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), group pairs by their shift and frequency differences, and apply [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md). This proves the displayed lower bound on [additive energy of a frequency graph](additive-energy-of-a-frequency-graph.md) without replacing exact frequency equalities by approximate ones.
