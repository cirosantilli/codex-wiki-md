# Eisenstein norm

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein integer](eisenstein-integer.md)

The product of an [Eisenstein integer](eisenstein-integer.md) $a+b\omega$ and its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md), where $\omega^2+\omega+1=0$. It is multiplicative and converts multiplication into composition identities for the quadratic form $a^2-ab+b^2$.
