# Eisenstein norm

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein integer](eisenstein-integer.md)

For an [Eisenstein integer](eisenstein-integer.md) $z=a+b\omega$, with $\omega=(-1+i\sqrt3)/2$, the norm is $N(z)=z\overline z=a^2-ab+b^2$. It is a positive [integer](integer-split.md) for $z\ne0$, since $N(z)=(a-b/2)^2+3b^2/4$, and is multiplicative by the multiplicativity of the [complex modulus](complex-modulus.md). An element is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) exactly when its norm is one: one direction follows from multiplicativity and integrality, and the other from the inverse $z^{-1}=\overline z$.
