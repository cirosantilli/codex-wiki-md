# Conserved cosmological relic abundance

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal decoupling in cosmology](thermal-decoupling-in-cosmology.md)

If relic particle number and reference-gas [entropy](entropy.md) are separately conserved in a [comoving volume](comoving-volume.md), their ratio $Y=n/s$ is constant. A decay or [entropy](entropy.md) injection can change it. The reference entropy is that of the specified thermal sector, not necessarily the entropy of the decoupled relic itself.
