# Elastic wave surface

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic slowness surface](elastic-slowness-surface.md)

The unit-time elastic [wave surface](elastic-wave-surface.md) is the locus of [group velocities](group-velocity.md) on the three branches of a homogeneous [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md). At time $t$, its scaled sheets describe the propagating [wavefronts](wavefront.md) from an impulsive point source when that source excites the corresponding modes. Homogeneity of the [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) gives $\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf g=1$, so each regular sheet is the envelope of planes $\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf x=1$. It is generally different from the radial reciprocal of the [elastic slowness surface](elastic-slowness-surface.md).
