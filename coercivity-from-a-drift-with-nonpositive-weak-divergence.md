# Coercivity from a drift with nonpositive weak divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md)

On a bounded open set, suppose $b\in L^\infty$ has [weak divergence](weak-divergence.md) in $L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}$ with $\operatorname{div}b\leq0$. For $\varphi\in C_0^\infty$, testing the divergence identity with $\varphi^2/2$ gives $\int(b\cdot\nabla\varphi)\varphi=-\tfrac12\int(\operatorname{div}b)\varphi^2\geq0$. The left side is continuous in the [zero-boundary Sobolev space](zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md) $H_0^1$, by the [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) and boundedness of $b$. Density therefore extends its nonnegativity to every $u\in H_0^1$. One need not assume that $(\operatorname{div}b)u^2$ is integrable. It follows that $a(u,v)=\int\nabla u\cdot\nabla v+\int(b\cdot\nabla u)v$ is a [coercive bilinear form](coercive-bilinear-form.md) in the gradient norm, giving unique weak Dirichlet solvability by the [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md).
