# Branching-process generating-function iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md)

Conditioning on the initial individual's children gives independent copies of the population $n$ generations later. Their [PGFs](probability-generating-function.md) multiply, and averaging the product over the offspring number yields $G(G_n(s))$. Conditioning on the last generation gives $G_n(G(s))$ as well; both agree because $G_n$ is the $n$fold iterate of $G$.
