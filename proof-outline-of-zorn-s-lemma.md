<h1 id="proof-outline-of-zorn-s-lemma">Proof outline of Zorn's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md)

Assume the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md) and suppose a [poset](partially-ordered-set.md) satisfying the hypothesis has no [maximal element](maximal-element-of-a-partially-ordered-set.md). Fix a [choice function](choice-function.md) on its nonempty [subsets](subset.md). By [transfinite recursion](transfinite-recursion.md), choose at each [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) stage an [upper bound](upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of the [chain in a partial order](chain-in-a-partial-order.md) already constructed, then choose a strictly larger member. Both choices are available, including the initial empty stage. This produces distinct members throughout the [Hartogs ordinal](hartogs-number.md) of $P$, contradicting the [Hartogs theorem](hartogs-theorem.md). Thus a [maximal element](maximal-element-of-a-partially-ordered-set.md) exists. Conversely, apply [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) to partial [choice functions](choice-function.md) on a family of nonempty [sets](set-split.md), ordered by extension. A [chain in a partial order](chain-in-a-partial-order.md) has the compatible [union](set-union.md) as an [upper bound](upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md), and a [maximal element](maximal-element-of-a-partially-ordered-set.md) must be defined on the whole family, since one missing value could be adjoined.

[Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) was introduced in 1935 to shorten [algebra](algebra-split.md) proofs using [choice](axiom-of-choice.md) or the [well-ordering theorem](well-ordering-theorem.md).[Zorn’s Lemma and Some Applications, §1, p. 4, paragraph on the 1935 introduction to shorten algebraic proofs](zorn%E2%80%99s-lemma-and-some-applications-by-keith-conrad.md)
