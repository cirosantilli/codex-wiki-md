# Osmotic stress in a symmetric electric double layer

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)

For a symmetric electrolyte with mobile charges $\pm q$, bulk density $n_0$ of each sign, and constant permittivity $\varepsilon$, the [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md) gives $n_\pm=n_0e^{\mp q\psi/(k_BT)}$. In planar geometry let $E=-\psi'$ and $\rho=q(n_+-n_-)$. Then $k_BT(n_++n_-)'=\rho E$ and $\varepsilon EE'=\rho E$ by [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md), proving that the displayed bulk-subtracted normal stress is constant. At the midpoint of identical equally charged plates, symmetry gives $E=0$ and $\Pi=2n_0k_BT[\cosh(q\psi_{\rm mid}/(k_BT))-1]$. Its ionic [osmotic pressure](osmotic-pressure.md) and electrostatic contributions together transmit the force; bare surface-charge repulsion alone omits essential terms.
