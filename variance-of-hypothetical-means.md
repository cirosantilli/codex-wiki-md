# Variance of hypothetical means

↑ **Parent:** [Bühlmann model](buhlmann-model.md)

The variance of hypothetical means in the [Bühlmann model](buhlmann-model.md) is the between-risk [variance](variance-split.md) of the conditional claim [expected value](expected-value.md). It creates the shared [covariance](covariance.md) $a$ between different years of the same risk and determines how much individual experience should influence the premium.
