# Transition relation

↑ **Parent:** [Transition system](transition-system.md)

A [binary relation](binary-relation.md) on the [states](state-computer-science.md) of a system, with $s\to t$ meaning that one permitted step changes $s$ to $t$. A [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md) can encode a [transition relation](transition-relation.md) after choosing bits for the current and next [states](state-computer-science.md).
