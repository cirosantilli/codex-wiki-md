# Saint-Venant equation

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

The one-dimensional open-channel [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md) express conservation of water volume and depth-averaged [momentum](momentum-split.md). Here $h$ is [water depth](water-depth.md), $u$ the mean [velocity](velocity.md), $\eta$ the free-surface elevation and $\tau_b$ the resisting bed [shear stress](shear-stress.md). The elevation includes the bed profile, so its gradient already includes gravitational driving by bed slope. A constant volume flux in space and time forces $h_t=0$ through the first equation; an unsteady reduction that imposes that flux while retaining momentum relaxation is consequently an approximation to this full system.
