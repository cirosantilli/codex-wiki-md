# Biosignature preservation

↑ **Parent:** [Biosignature](biosignature.md)

Retention of potential biological evidence through deposition, burial, alteration and exposure. [Organic molecules](organic-molecule.md), microscopic textures and [isotope fractionation](isotope-fractionation.md) have different sensitivities to heating, oxidation and [ionizing radiation](ionizing-radiation.md). A former habitable environment can lack a recoverable [biosignature](biosignature.md) if its record was destroyed; a preserved feature still requires tests against nonbiological origins and contamination.
