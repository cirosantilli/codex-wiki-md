# Fundamental theorem of calculus for Lebesgue integration

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md)

If $g\in L^1(a,b)$, then $F(x)=F(a)+\int_a^xg(s)\,ds$ is [absolutely continuous](absolutely-continuous-function.md) and satisfies $F'=g$ [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md). Conversely, every absolutely continuous function has this form.
