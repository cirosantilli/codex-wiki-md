# Largest component of a Poisson-Dirichlet partition

↑ **Parent:** [One-parameter Poisson-Dirichlet distribution](one-parameter-poisson-dirichlet-distribution.md)

For $a\ge1/2$, at most one component exceeds $a$, so $1-F_\theta(a)=\theta\int_a^1x^{-1}(1-x)^{\theta-1}dx$. Below one half several components can exceed the threshold. Put $N_a=\#\{i:p_i>a\}$ and use the finite identity $\mathbf1_{\{N_a=0\}}=\sum_m(-1)^m(N_a)_m/m!$, with the [factorial moment densities of Poisson-Dirichlet components](factorial-moment-densities-of-poisson-dirichlet-components.md). It gives the full [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) as finite [inclusion-exclusion](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) over $x_i>a$, $\sum_i x_i<1$. In contrast, a size-biased component is beta-distributed.
