# Homothetic gravitational motion

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian central configuration](newtonian-central-configuration.md)

For a [Newtonian central configuration](newtonian-central-configuration.md), set $\mathbf x_i(t)=a(t)\mathbf a_i$ with $a>0$. The gravitational force scales as $a^{-2}$, reducing all equations to the displayed scalar [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md). Its first integral is $\dot a^2/2-\Lambda/a=k/2$. The total [energy](energy.md) is $kI/2$, so the scalar energy constant exactly tracks the many-body energy for the fixed shape.
