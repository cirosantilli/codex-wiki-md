# Pressure upper bound for Hausdorff dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Hausdorff dimension](hausdorff-dimension.md)

Suppose shrinking interval covers of $F$ have length-power sums with logarithmic growth rate $P(\gamma)$, and $P$ is strictly decreasing with zero $\gamma_*$. For every $\gamma>\gamma_*$ those sums tend to zero exponentially. The definition of [Hausdorff measure](hausdorff-measure.md) then gives $\mathcal H^\gamma(F)=0$, hence the displayed upper bound. A matching lower bound requires additional information and does not follow from this covering argument alone.
