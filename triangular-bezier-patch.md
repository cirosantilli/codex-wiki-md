<h1 id="triangular-bezier-patch">Triangular Bézier patch</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Parametric surface](parametric-surface.md)

For nonnegative barycentric parameters, these total-degree [Bernstein basis](bernstein-basis.md) functions define a surface over a triangle. The triangular [control net](control-net.md) has $(n+1)(n+2)/2$ points. Its index constraint couples the three exponents, so it is not a tensor product of two independent univariate bases. The [multinomial theorem](multinomial-theorem.md) gives nonnegativity and [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md).
