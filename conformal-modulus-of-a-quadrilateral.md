# Conformal modulus of a quadrilateral

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal quadrilateral](conformal-quadrilateral.md)

Map a [conformal quadrilateral](conformal-quadrilateral.md) to a rectangle of width $w$ and height $h$, with the chosen opposite boundary arcs mapped to its vertical sides. Their [extremal length](extremal-length.md) is $M=w/h$; the other pair has [extremal length](extremal-length.md) $1/M$. The constant [conformal metric](conformal-metric.md) is extremal. For an arbitrary density, average horizontal joining lengths over height and apply the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) to obtain $L_\rho^2h\leq wA_\rho$, proving the upper bound. Vertical joining segments prove the dual formula. [Conformal invariance of extremal length](conformal-invariance-of-extremal-length.md) transports both equalities back to the quadrilateral.
