# Pore velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md)

[Pore velocity](pore-velocity.md) is the [fluid](fluid.md) [velocity](velocity.md) averaged over pore volume relative to the solid framework. For uniform [porosity](porosity.md) $\phi$, full saturation and all pores participating, $\mathbf v=\mathbf q/\phi$, with [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) $\mathbf q$ defined per bulk area. The identity follows from volume averaging: extending the relative [fluid](fluid.md) [velocity](velocity.md) by zero in the solid gives a bulk average equal to the pore fraction times the pore-volume average. It does not assert that every local passage has open area $\phi A$ or identical speed.

In a uniform advective model, travel over length $L$ takes $L/v=\phi L/q$. If some pore volume is inaccessible to transport, the corresponding estimate uses [effective porosity](effective-porosity.md) $\phi_e$ and $v=q/\phi_e$. A connected but nearly stagnant passage can exchange solute slowly, so even this mean does not describe all arrival times. [Flux-weighted residence time in a porous layer](flux-weighted-residence-time-in-a-porous-layer.md) explains one precise averaging convention.[https://books.gw-project.org/hydrogeologic-properties-of-earth-materials-and-principles-of-groundwater-flow/chapter/darcys-law/](https://books.gw-project.org/hydrogeologic-properties-of-earth-materials-and-principles-of-groundwater-flow/chapter/darcys-law/)
