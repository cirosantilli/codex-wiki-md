# Pore velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md)

The mean advective fluid speed within the connected pore volume, distinct from [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) defined per bulk cross-sectional area. For uniform [porosity](porosity.md) $\phi$ and saturated flow, $v=q/\phi$. Parcel travel times and solute [advection](advection.md) use this velocity, while pressure-flow relations normally use the bulk-area [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md).
