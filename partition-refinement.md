# Partition refinement

↑ **Parent:** [Partition of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md)

A refinement of a finite interval partition inserts additional points without removing original points. The union of two partitions is a common refinement. Refinement makes each subinterval smaller, increasing its infimum and decreasing its supremum; this proves [Darboux sum refinement monotonicity](darboux-sum-refinement-monotonicity.md).
