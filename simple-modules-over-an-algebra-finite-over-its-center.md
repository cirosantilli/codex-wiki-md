# Simple modules over an algebra finite over its center

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible module](irreducible-module.md)

If $A$ is a finite [module](module-mathematics.md) over a commutative central subring $R$ and $M$ is a simple left $A$-module, then $M=Am$ is finite over $R$. A central element not annihilating $M$ acts bijectively, since its kernel and image are $A$-submodules. Put $B=R/\operatorname{Ann}_R(M)$. For $0\ne r\in B$, write generators as $m_i=r\sum_jb_{ij}m_j$. The [adjugate matrix](adjugate-matrix.md) identity shows $\det(I-r(b_{ij}))$ annihilates $M$, hence is zero in $B$. Since this determinant has form $1-rq$, $r$ is invertible. Thus $B$ is a [field](field.md). If $R$ is a finite-type algebra over an algebraically closed [field](field.md) $k$, the residue [field](field.md) is $k$, and every such [simple module](irreducible-module.md) is finite-dimensional over $k$.
