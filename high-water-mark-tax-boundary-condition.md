# High-water mark tax boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [High-water mark investment taxation](high-water-mark-investment-taxation.md)

At an active maximum-raising boundary in [high-water mark investment taxation](high-water-mark-investment-taxation.md), the finite-variation part of the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) is $(V_{\bar w}-\tau V_w)d\bar w$, so smooth optimality sets this coefficient to zero. If the maximizing policy avoids increasing the maximum, the corresponding inequality $V_{\bar w}-\tau V_w\leq0$ is compatible with a [wealth-cap investment boundary](wealth-cap-investment-boundary.md) instead.
