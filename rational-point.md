# Rational point

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md)

For an [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) $X$ defined over a [field](field.md) $K$, a K-[rational point](rational-point.md) is a morphism $\operatorname{Spec}K\to X$ over $K$. In affine coordinates it is a solution of the defining equations with every coordinate in $K$; in projective coordinates it is a nonzero coordinate vector over $K$, considered up to common nonzero scaling. The set is denoted $X(K)$. Over a [number field](number-field.md), having points over every completion need not imply having a [rational point](rational-point.md); for genus-one coverings this failure is recorded by the [Tate–Shafarevich group](tate-shafarevich-group.md).
