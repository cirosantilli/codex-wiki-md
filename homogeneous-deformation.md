# Homogeneous deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation (mechanics)](deformation-mechanics.md)

A homogeneous [deformation](deformation-mechanics.md) has the same [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) at every material point. On a connected reference domain it is an affine map $y(X)=FX+b$ with constant matrix $F$ and translation $b$. Orientation preservation requires $\det F>0$. Its stretches and local volume ratio are spatially constant. A homogeneous deformation describes the kinematics; a [homogeneous hyperelastic material](homogeneous-hyperelastic-material.md) instead describes spatial independence of a constitutive law.
