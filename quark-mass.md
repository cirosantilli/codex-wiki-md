# Quark mass

↑ **Parent:** [Quark](quark.md)

A [quark mass](quark-mass.md) is the coefficient of its [Dirac mass](dirac-mass-term.md) term. This term couples left- and right-handed [quarks](quark.md), so nonzero [quark masses](quark-mass.md) explicitly break independent rotations of the two [chiralities](chirality-physics.md). For two equal masses, the vector [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) survives, while the nonsinglet [axial current](axial-current.md) obeys the [flavor-nonsinglet axial Ward identity with equal quark masses](flavor-nonsinglet-axial-ward-identity-with-equal-quark-masses.md). In the renormalized theory the [mass](mass.md) parameter depends on the subtraction scale and scheme, while measurable hadron masses are physical observables.
