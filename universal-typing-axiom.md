# Universal typing axiom

↑ **Parent:** [Simply typed lambda calculus](simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md)

An extension of [simple type](simple-type.md) assignment that assigns a distinguished atomic [type variable](type-variable.md) $\star$ to every [lambda term](lambda-term.md) in the empty [typing context](typing-context.md). The recorded assumptions therefore need not contain all [free variables](free-variable.md) of the subject. This is a rule admitting a [typing judgment](typing-judgment.md), rather than an implicit conversion to every [simple type](simple-type.md) or a general [subtyping](subtyping.md) rule.
