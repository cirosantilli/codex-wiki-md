# Stream tube

↑ **Parent:** [Streamline](streamline.md)

A [stream tube](stream-tube.md) is a narrow tube whose side boundary consists of [streamlines](streamline.md). In steady flow no fluid crosses its sides, so continuity makes its [mass flux](mass-flux.md) $A\rho u$ constant. If flow follows a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) with constant [magnetohydrodynamic mass loading](magnetohydrodynamic-mass-loading.md), its normal area obeys $A|\mathbf B|=\mathrm{constant}$.
