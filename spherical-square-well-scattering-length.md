# Spherical square-well scattering length

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical square well](spherical-square-well.md)

For an attractive [spherical square well](spherical-square-well.md), let $\kappa=\sqrt{2mV_0}/\hbar$. At zero [energy](energy-split.md), the outer reduced radial wave is proportional to $r-a_s$ and the regular inner wave to $\sin(\kappa r)$. Matching their [logarithmic derivatives](logarithmic-derivative.md) gives $a_s=a-\tan(\kappa a)/\kappa$. Away from a threshold and at sufficiently low [energy](energy-split.md), the total [S-wave scattering](s-wave-quantum-scattering.md) [scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md) tends to $4\pi a_s^2$. The length can be negative, zero or divergent.
