# Space-time Hermite polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilists' Hermite polynomial](probabilists-hermite-polynomial.md)

The space-time [Probabilists' Hermite polynomial](probabilists-hermite-polynomial.md) $H_n(x,t)=t^{n/2}\mathrm{He}_n(x/\sqrt t)$ extends polynomially to $t=0$ and solves $\partial_tH_n+\frac12\partial_x^2H_n=0$. Therefore $H_n(B_t,t)$ is a [martingale](martingale-split.md) for a standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), by the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) and Gaussian [moments](moment.md).
