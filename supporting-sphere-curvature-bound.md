# Supporting-sphere curvature bound

↑ **Parent:** [Supporting sphere](supporting-sphere.md)

If a compact regular surface lies in a closed Euclidean ball of radius $R$, maximize distance from the ball's centre. At a maximizing point, comparison of the [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) with the tangent [supporting sphere](supporting-sphere.md) makes both [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) have magnitude at least $R^{-1}$ and the same sign. The [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) there is therefore at least $R^{-2}$.
