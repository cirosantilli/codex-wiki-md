<h1 id="kahler-covariant-derivative-of-a-superpotential">Kähler covariant derivative of a superpotential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity F-term potential](supergravity-f-term-potential.md)

The Kähler covariant derivative of a [superpotential](superpotential.md) transforms by the same holomorphic factor as the [superpotential](superpotential.md) under a [Kähler transformation](kahler-transformation.md). This follows by differentiating $e^{-f}W$ and cancelling the extra derivative of $f$ against the shift of $K_i$.
