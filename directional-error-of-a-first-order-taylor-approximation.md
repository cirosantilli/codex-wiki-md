# Directional error of a first-order Taylor approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariable Taylor expansion](multivariable-taylor-expansion.md)

For a fixed unit direction $v$, the [multivariable Taylor expansion](multivariable-taylor-expansion.md) gives the displayed leading error. Equal [directional derivatives](directional-derivative.md) in two directions do not imply equal approximation errors: the linear changes depend on the [gradient](gradient.md), while the quadratic errors depend on the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md). For example, $f(x,y)=x+y+2x^2+y^2$ at the origin has equal derivatives in the two coordinate directions but errors $2s^2$ and $s^2$.
