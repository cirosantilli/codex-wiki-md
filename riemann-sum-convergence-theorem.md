# Riemann sum convergence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann sum](riemann-sum.md)

For a bounded [function](function-split.md) on a fixed closed [interval](interval-mathematics.md), [Riemann integrability](riemann-integrable-function.md) with [Riemann integral](riemann-integral.md) $I$ is equivalent to convergence of all tagged [Riemann sums](riemann-sum.md) to $I$ as the [mesh of a partition](mesh-of-a-partition.md) tends to zero. Precisely, for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $\delta>0$ such that every [partition of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md) of mesh less than $\delta$, with any tags in the corresponding closed subintervals, has its [Riemann sum](riemann-sum.md) within $\varepsilon$ of $I$.
