# Linear energy-absorption coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Linear attenuation coefficient](linear-attenuation-coefficient.md)

A coefficient describing the locally absorbed fraction of an incident photon energy flow per path length, under a stated averaging convention. It is distinct from the total [linear attenuation coefficient](linear-attenuation-coefficient.md), which counts removal from an uncollided beam. Dividing by [mass density](density.md) gives an energy-absorption coefficient per mass.
