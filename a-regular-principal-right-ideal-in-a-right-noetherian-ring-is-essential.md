# A regular principal right ideal in a right Noetherian ring is essential

↑ **Parent:** [Essential right ideal](essential-right-ideal.md)

If $c$ is a [regular element of a ring](regular-element-of-a-ring.md) in a [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md) and a nonzero [right ideal](right-ideal.md) $B$ had $B\cap cR=0$, cancellation would make $B+cB+c^2B+\cdots$ an infinite [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of nonzero [right ideals](right-ideal.md). Its finite partial sums contradict the [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md). Thus $cR$ is an [essential right ideal](essential-right-ideal.md).
