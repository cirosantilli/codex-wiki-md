# Affine rescaling of a null geodesic tangent

↑ **Parent:** [Affine parameter](affine-parameter.md)

A change $\lambda\mapsto A\lambda+B$ rescales a [geodesic](geodesic.md) tangent and all its linear Killing constants by $1/A$. A null norm remains zero and cannot fix $A$, unlike proper-time normalization of a material particle. Ratios of the constants, such as the [photon](photon.md) [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $b=|h|/(ck)$ in [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md), are independent of the affine scale. A physically normalized [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) fixes the [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) and hence selects a tangent scale.
