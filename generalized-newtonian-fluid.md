# Generalized Newtonian fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Non-Newtonian fluid](non-newtonian-fluid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_Newtonian_fluid)

A generalized Newtonian fluid has instantaneous scalar constitutive law $\tau=\eta(\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma$, with viscosity depending on the current [shear rate](shear-rate.md) but carrying no memory.
