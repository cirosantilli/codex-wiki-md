# Euler-product nonvanishing of the Riemann zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

Absolute convergence of the [Euler product](euler-product.md) gives $\zeta(s)\ne0$ for $\Re s>1$. Equivalently,

$$
\frac1{\zeta(s)}=\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{\mu(n)}{n^s}
$$

converges absolutely there.
