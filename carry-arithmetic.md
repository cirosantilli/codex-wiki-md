# Carry (arithmetic)

↑ **Parent:** [Addition](addition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carry_(arithmetic))

In [positional notation](positional-notation.md) with [integer](integer-split.md) base $b\geq2$, a carry transfers $b$ units in one position into one unit in the next position during [addition](addition.md). Each such transfer lowers the sum of the [numeral digits](numerical-digit.md) by $b-1$.
