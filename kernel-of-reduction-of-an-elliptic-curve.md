# Kernel of reduction of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Good reduction of an elliptic curve](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For an elliptic curve with good reduction over a local field, the kernel of reduction consists of the points reducing to the identity. The parameter $t=-x/y$ identifies it with the maximal ideal equipped with the [formal group of an elliptic curve](formal-group-of-an-elliptic-curve.md).
