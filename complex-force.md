# Complex force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

In planar [potential flow](potential-flow.md), encode a physical [force](force-split.md) $(F_x,F_y)$ by $F_x-iF_y$, using the same negative-imaginary-component convention as the [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) $u-iv$. The [Blasius force theorem](blasius-force-theorem.md) then expresses the force per unit span as a single [contour integral](contour-integral.md). The encoding is a convention: the physical vertical component is the negative [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) of this complex number.
