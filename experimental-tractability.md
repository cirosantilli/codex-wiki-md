# Experimental tractability

↑ **Parent:** [Model organism](model-organism.md)

[Experimental tractability](experimental-tractability.md) is the practical ability to cultivate a system, manipulate the variables of interest, and obtain an interpretable readout with appropriate [scientific controls](scientific-control.md). In [Saccharomyces cerevisiae](saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md), inexpensive cultivation, accessible [haploid](haploid.md) and [diploid](diploid.md) states, and tools for introducing [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) make controlled changes of [genotype](genotype.md) feasible. A [haploid](haploid.md) [biological strain](strain-biology.md) exposes a changed [allele](allele.md) without a second copy at that [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md), while crosses and [meiosis](meiosis.md) permit tests of inheritance. [Duina, Miller and Keeney (2014), introduction, Figure 2 and Experimental Toolkit and Related Resources](budding-yeast-for-budding-geneticists-a-primer-on-the-saccharomyces-cerevisiae-model-system-by-andrea-a-duina-and-others.md)

An accessible readout is not necessarily a direct measurement of the mechanism. Growth of a [biological colony](colony-biology.md) integrates many cellular functions. Failure to grow could reflect the intended [gene deletion](gene-deletion.md), unsuitable conditions, failed [genetic transformation](genetic-transformation.md), or another [mutation](mutation.md). Matching the [genetic background](genetic-background.md) and using [scientific controls](scientific-control.md) narrows these alternatives. Ease of doing an experiment and strength of its inference are distinct advantages.
