# Dual of a maximum of affine functions

↑ **Parent:** [Linear programming duality](linear-programming-duality.md)

Minimizing $\max_i(a_i^Tx+b_i)$ over unrestricted $x$ is a [linear program](linear-programming.md) after introducing an [epigraph](epigraph.md) variable $t$ and inequalities $a_i^Tx+b_i\leq t$. Its [optimization Lagrangian](optimization-lagrangian.md) has finite infimum over $x,t$ exactly when the nonnegative multipliers sum to one and their weighted slopes sum to zero. The displayed [Lagrangian dual problem](lagrangian-dual-problem.md) maximizes the resulting constant lower bound. It identifies the role of a zero-slope [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of the affine functions.
