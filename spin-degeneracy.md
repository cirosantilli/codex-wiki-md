# Spin degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [State degeneracy](state-degeneracy.md)

When a single-particle [energy](energy-split.md) is independent of its [spin](spin.md) projection and all projections are available, the [state degeneracy](state-degeneracy.md) due to [spin](spin.md) is $g_s=2S+1$. A spin-half gas has $g_s=2$ if both projections are available, while a fully polarized gas can have one populated [spin](spin.md) species. A [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) that resolves [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md) requires counting the separate energies instead of multiplying one common occupancy by $g_s$.
