# Quantified formula

↑ **Parent:** [First-order formula](first-order-formula.md)

A [quantified formula](quantified-formula.md) has a [quantifier](quantifier.md) as its outermost formation: it is of the form $\forall x\theta$ or $\exists x\theta$. The [quantifier](quantifier.md) binds the indicated variable occurrences according to the formation rules.
