# Scoring rule

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

A scoring rule assigns a reward or loss to an announced [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) and realized outcome. In the reward convention, a [proper scoring rule](proper-scoring-rule.md) makes truthful reporting maximize expected reward; the loss convention reverses the comparison.
