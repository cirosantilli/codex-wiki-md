# Symplectic Lagrangian stabilizer

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic parabolic subgroup](symplectic-parabolic-subgroup.md)

With a [Lagrangian subspace](lagrangian-subspace.md) first in a paired [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md), its stabilizer consists of $\begin{pmatrix}A&AS\\0&A^{-\mathsf T}\end{pmatrix}$ with $S$ symmetric. The additive symmetric-matrix [unipotent radical](unipotent-radical.md) has order $q^{m(m+1)/2}$; the [Levi subgroup](levi-subgroup.md) is $GL_m(q)$, acting by $S\mapsto ASA^{\mathsf T}$. Its index in $Sp_{2m}(q)$ is $\prod_{i=1}^m(q^i+1)$, the number of Lagrangian subspaces.
