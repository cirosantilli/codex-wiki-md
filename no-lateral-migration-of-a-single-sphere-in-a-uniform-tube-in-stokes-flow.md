# No lateral migration of a single sphere in a uniform tube in Stokes flow

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic reversibility of Stokes flow](kinematic-reversibility-of-stokes-flow.md)

Reflection of a uniform tube in a horizontal plane preserves an off-axis [sphere](sphere.md)'s transverse position but reverses the axial [force](force.md). By linearity, reversing the [force](force.md) reverses every translational [velocity](velocity.md). By reflection symmetry, it preserves the transverse [velocity](velocity.md). Thus the transverse [velocity](velocity.md) is zero. The [Minimum-dissipation theorem for Stokes flow](minimum-dissipation-theorem-for-stokes-flow.md) does not predict motion between different particle positions: it compares [velocity fields](velocity-field.md) in one fixed geometry with prescribed boundary [velocities](velocity.md). Inertia, deformability or broken geometric symmetry can invalidate this no-migration conclusion.
