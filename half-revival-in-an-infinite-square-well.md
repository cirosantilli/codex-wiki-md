# Half revival in an infinite square well

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md)

For the [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) of length $a$, the [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) are $E_n=n^2E_1$, with $E_1=\pi^2\hbar^2/(2ma^2)$. At $t_*=\pi\hbar/E_1$, the [dynamical phase](dynamical-phase.md) is $e^{-iE_nt_*/\hbar}=(-1)^n$. Since the [midpoint parity operator](midpoint-parity-operator.md) has eigenvalues $(-1)^{n+1}$ in the same complete [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), the [time-evolution operator](time-evolution-operator.md) is exactly $U(t_*)=-R_a$ on every state. At $2t_*$ every phase is one, giving a full revival.

Thus a state becomes its reflected copy, up to a [global phase](global-phase.md), at the half revival. Its position [probability density](probability-density.md) is reflected, while its mean momentum changes sign because $PR_a=-R_aP$. For an initially reflection-symmetric [probability density](probability-density.md), the initial and half-revival position distributions agree even when their [probability currents](probability-current.md) point in opposite directions.
