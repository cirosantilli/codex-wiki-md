# Sampling expansion by periodic Fourier projection

↑ **Parent:** [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md)

When the continuous [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) $F$ of an [integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) vanishes outside $[-\pi,\pi]$, its periodic [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) are the integer samples of the continuous representative of $f$, with the sign of the index reversed. Projecting $F$ in $L^2[-\pi,\pi]$ and then applying the [Fourier inversion theorem](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) gives the displayed reconstruction using the normalized [sinc function](sinc-function.md), with each quotient defined as one when $t=n$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) controls the reconstruction error uniformly in $t$. [Bessel's inequality](bessel-s-inequality.md) bounds the vector of shifted [sinc functions](sinc-function.md), giving absolute uniform tails when the sample sequence belongs to $\ell^2$.
