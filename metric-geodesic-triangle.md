# Metric geodesic triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md)

A metric geodesic triangle consists of three points of a [geodesic metric space](geodesic-metric-space.md) and a chosen [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) segment between each pair. Multiple choices are allowed, and coincident vertices give degenerate triangles. A [Gromov-hyperbolic metric space](hyperbolic-metric-space.md) has a uniform bound on the distance from each side to the other two sides for every such choice. This definition applies to [Cayley graphs](cayley-graph.md) and trees without tangent vectors or curvature assumptions; it does not invoke the surface angle formula for a [geodesic triangle](geodesic-triangle.md).
