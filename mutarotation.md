# Mutarotation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutarotation)

[Mutarotation](mutarotation.md) is a change in [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md) as [anomers](anomer.md) interconvert in a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). The [glucose mutarotation experiment](glucose-mutarotation-experiment.md) detects redistribution between cyclic forms through [monosaccharide ring-chain interconversion](monosaccharide-ring-chain-interconversion.md).
