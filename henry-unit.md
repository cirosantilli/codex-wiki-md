# Henry (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [Inductance](inductance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_(unit))

The [henry](henry-unit.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [inductance](inductance.md):

$$
1\,\mathrm H=1\,\frac{\mathrm{Wb}}{\mathrm A}
=1\,\frac{\mathrm{V\,s}}{\mathrm A}.
$$

A coil with [self-inductance](self-inductance.md) $1\,\mathrm H$ has [magnetic flux linkage](magnetic-flux-linkage.md) $1\,\mathrm{Wb}$ at [electric current](electric-current.md) $1\,\mathrm A$. A rate of change of [electric current](electric-current.md) $1\,\mathrm{A/s}$ produces induced [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) of magnitude $1\,\mathrm V$. The [weber](weber-unit.md) measures [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) and [magnetic flux linkage](magnetic-flux-linkage.md); the [henry](henry-unit.md) measures their ratio to [electric current](electric-current.md). [OpenStax University Physics 2, §14.2: the unit of self-inductance](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/14-2-self-inductance-and-inductors).
