# Smallest axial rotation group forcing second-rank transverse isotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Axially invariant second-rank tensor](axially-invariant-second-rank-tensor.md)

The six-element [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) generated by the displayed rotations already forces every invariant second-rank [tensor](tensor.md) to have the same form as an [axially invariant second-rank tensor](axially-invariant-second-rank-tensor.md). The axial threefold rotation eliminates mixed entries and leaves only a planar scalar plus a planar antisymmetric part; the transverse half-turn eliminates the latter. No smaller finite [subgroup](subgroup.md) suffices: cyclic rotation [groups](group-split.md) retain an axial antisymmetric [tensor](tensor.md), while a four-element noncyclic rotation [group](group-split.md) retains arbitrary diagonal [tensors](tensor.md) along its three mutually perpendicular half-turn axes.
