# Electroweak scale

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md)

The [electroweak scale](electroweak-scale.md) is the characteristic energy associated with [electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md). With the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) Higgs doublet normalized by $\langle H\rangle=(0,v/\sqrt2)^T$, its [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) satisfies $v=(\sqrt2G_F)^{-1/2}\simeq246\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $m_W=gv/2$. Its smallness compared with possible ultraviolet scales motivates the [hierarchy problem](hierarchy-problem.md).
