# Liquid-drop fission energy

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md)

For two spherical fragments with the same proton fraction as the parent, let $(A_1,Z_1)=y(A,Z)$ and $(A_2,Z_2)=(1-y)(A,Z)$. In the [semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md) without [nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md), nucleon rest energies, the volume term and the [nuclear symmetry energy](symmetry-energy.md) cancel in the parent-minus-fragment mass difference. The remaining [Q value](q-value.md) is the displayed competition between increased surface energy and reduced [nuclear Coulomb energy](nuclear-coulomb-energy.md). Its symmetric value is positive when

$$
\frac{Z^2}{A}>\frac{a_S}{a_C}\frac{2^{1/3}-1}{1-2^{-2/3}}.
$$

This is an energy-release condition, not the disappearance of a [fission barrier](fission-barrier.md) or a prediction of a short [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md).
