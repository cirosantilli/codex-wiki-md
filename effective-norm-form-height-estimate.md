# Effective norm-form height estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Baker lower bound for a homogeneous linear form in logarithms](baker-lower-bound-for-a-homogeneous-linear-form-in-logarithms.md)

Let $L$ be a fixed [number field](number-field.md) and let $V\subset L$ be a fixed rational linear subspace for which the associated [norm form](norm-form.md) has no unit-family degeneracy. There are effective constants $A,C>0$ such that every $z\in V\cap\mathcal O_L$ with nonzero [field norm](field-norm.md) $m$ satisfies

$$
H(z)\leq C|m|^A.
$$

To prove this, factor the principal ideal $(z)$, write its generators as a bounded factor times powers of fundamental units, use the linear relations defining $V$, and apply the [Baker lower bound for a homogeneous linear form in logarithms](baker-lower-bound-for-a-homogeneous-linear-form-in-logarithms.md) to bound the unit exponents by $O(\log|m|)$. For $L=\mathbb Q(\alpha)K$ with $[\mathbb Q(\alpha):\mathbb Q]=3$, $[K:\mathbb Q]=2$, and $V=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}\{1,\alpha,\omega\}$ for a basis $1,\omega$ of $K$, the coprime degrees rule out the degeneracy.
