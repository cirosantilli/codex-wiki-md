# Nuclease

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A [nuclease](nuclease.md) is an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that cleaves [nucleic acids](nucleic-acid.md). [ribonucleases](ribonuclease.md) act on [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md); other activities act on [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Nuclease sensitivity can reveal a nucleic-acid-dependent assembly, but loss of association can also reflect secondary structural disruption.
