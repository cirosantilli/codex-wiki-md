# Compact L2 potential perturbation of the Dirichlet Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Compact operator](compact-operator-split.md)

On a bounded open set, let $Tv\in W_0^{1,2}$ be the [weak solution](weak-solution.md) of $\Delta Tv=v$. The energy estimate bounds $T:L^2\to W_0^{1,2}$. For $p>n$, put $q=2p/(p-2)<2n/(n-2)$. The zero-boundary [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) gives compact embedding into $L^q$, and [Hölder's inequality](holder-s-inequality.md) gives $\|wz\|_2\leq\|w\|_p\|z\|_q$. Hence $Kv=wTv$ is compact on $L^2$, without a regularity assumption on the boundary. Writing $v=\Delta u$ makes $\Delta u-wu=f$ equivalent to $(I-K)v=f$. The [Fredholm alternative for a compact operator](fredholm-alternative.md) then identifies unique solvability with triviality of the homogeneous kernel.
