# Abrikosov vortex lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Abrikosov vortex](abrikosov-vortex.md)

A periodic array of [Abrikosov vortices](abrikosov-vortex.md). In the isotropic local theory near the [upper critical magnetic field](upper-critical-magnetic-field.md), the triangular array has a lower [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) than the square array.[p.976, equations3–4 and the two lattice geometries](nobel-lecture-type-ii-superconductors-and-the-vortex-lattice-by-alexei-alexeyevich-abrikosov.md)

The useful distinction is between **vortex existence, array geometry, and vortex mobility**. Quantized winding does not force every array to be triangular or defect-free. A stationary array and vortices moving under an applied [electric current](electric-current.md) also have different transport consequences; [flux pinning](flux-pinning.md) matters for carrying useful [electric currents](electric-current.md).
