# Dolbeault Green operator

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md)

On a compact [Hermitian manifold](hermitian-manifold.md) without boundary, this operator is the inverse of the [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md) on the orthogonal complement of its harmonic forms and is zero on the harmonic space. Elliptic theory gives $\Delta_{\bar\partial}G=G\Delta_{\bar\partial}=I-H$, with $H$ the harmonic projection, and ensures that $G$ sends smooth forms to smooth forms. It commutes with $\bar\partial$ and $\bar\partial^*$; their commutation with the Laplacian and orthogonality to its kernel give this by uniqueness of the inverse. If $\eta$ is $\bar\partial$-exact, then $\eta=\bar\partial\bar\partial^*G\eta$. On a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), the same operator serves the holomorphic Laplacian because $\Delta_\partial=\Delta_{\bar\partial}$.
