# Single-molecule magnet

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic relaxation](magnetic-relaxation.md)

A finite molecular [spin](spin.md) system exhibiting slow reversal of its magnetic moment on an experimental timescale. Blocking is timescale-dependent and does not require bulk long-range magnetic order.
