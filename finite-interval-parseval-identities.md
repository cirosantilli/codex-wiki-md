# Finite-interval Parseval identities

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonality of complex exponentials](orthogonality-of-complex-exponentials.md)

For the [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md) $F(t)=\sum_{k=1}^N b_ke(kt)$, [orthogonality of complex exponentials](orthogonality-of-complex-exponentials.md) gives

$$
\int_0^1|F|^2=\sum_{k=1}^N|b_k|^2,\qquad
\int_0^1|F\prime|^2=4\pi^2\sum_{k=1}^Nk^2|b_k|^2\leq4\pi^2N^2\sum_{k=1}^N|b_k|^2.
$$

Indeed, expand each square and use $\int_0^1e(jt)\,dt=0$ for every nonzero [integer](integer.md) $j$, and one for $j=0$.
