# Beryllium dimer

↑ **Parent:** [Diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md)

The neutral molecule Be-two. Its simplest filled sigma-two-s/bonding and antibonding description gives zero [bond order](bond-order.md), but mixing of accessible two-p configurations and [electron correlation](electron-correlation.md) produce a weak [covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md). Resolved vibrational levels establish a bound ground-state potential.[Beryllium dimer caught in the act of bonding by Jeremy M. Merritt and colleagues](beryllium-dimer-caught-in-the-act-of-bonding-by-jeremy-m-merritt-and-colleagues.md)
