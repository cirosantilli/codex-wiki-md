<h1 id="nadp-plus">NADP+</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate](nicotinamide-adenine-dinucleotide-phosphate.md)

[NADP+](nadp-plus.md) is the oxidized form of [nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate](nicotinamide-adenine-dinucleotide-phosphate.md). It can accept [Electrons](electron.md) to form [NADPH](nadph.md); the [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md) supply this conversion during [oxygenic photosynthesis](oxygenic-photosynthesis.md).
