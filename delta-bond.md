# Delta bond

↑ **Parent:** [Covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md)

A [delta bond](delta-bond.md) involves sideways [orbital overlap](orbital-overlap.md) with two nodal planes containing the internuclear axis. In suitable [metal-metal bonds](metal-metal-bond.md), d-[orbital overlap](orbital-overlap.md) can supply this component in addition to sigma and pi bonding.
