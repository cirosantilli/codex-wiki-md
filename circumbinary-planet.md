# Circumbinary planet

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circumbinary_planet)

A circumbinary planet orbits both members of a [binary star](binary-star.md) rather than just one. If its orbital radius greatly exceeds the binary separation, the stellar contributions to irradiation add approximately as luminosities at a common distance.
