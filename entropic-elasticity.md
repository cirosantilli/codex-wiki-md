# Entropic elasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Entropic spring](entropic-spring.md)

Entropic elasticity is a restoring response arising from the reduction of accessible molecular configurations under deformation. For an ideal [freely jointed chain](ideal-chain.md), weak extension has [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) $\Delta\mathcal F=3k_BTR^2/(2Nb^2)$ and [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k=3k_BT/(Nb^2)$. The proportionality to [temperature](temperature-split.md) assumes fixed segment geometry; temperature-dependent [Kuhn length](kuhn-length.md) modifies it.
