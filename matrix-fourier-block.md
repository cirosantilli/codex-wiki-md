# Matrix Fourier block

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix-valued Gowers U2 quantity](matrix-valued-gowers-u2-quantity.md)

For $f:G\to M_n(\mathbb C)$ and a degree-$d_\rho$ [unitary irreducible representation](unitary-irreducible-representation.md), the matrix Fourier block is the operator $T_\rho$ on $M_{n\times d_\rho}(\mathbb C)$ defined in the title. Its [singular values](singular-value.md) $\lambda_{\rho,j}$ satisfy

$$
\sum_{\rho,j}d_\rho\lambda_{\rho,j}^2=\mathbb E_x\|f(x)\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2,
\qquad
\sum_{\rho,j}d_\rho\lambda_{\rho,j}^4=\|f\|_{U^2}^4.
$$

Pointwise [operator norm](operator-norm.md) at most one implies $\|T_\rho\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq1$. These blocks are operators on a matrix space, rather than simply the entrywise scalar [Fourier transform on a finite group](fourier-transform-on-a-finite-group.md) without any reshaping.
