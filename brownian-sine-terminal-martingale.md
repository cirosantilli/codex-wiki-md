# Brownian sine terminal martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale representation theorem](martingale-representation-theorem.md)

The [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) of $\sin B_T$ at time $t$ is $e^{-(T-t)/2}\sin B_t$, by independent [Brownian increment](brownian-increment.md). [Itô's formula](ito-s-lemma.md) cancels its time derivative against its second spatial derivative and leaves the displayed [stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md). The integrand is bounded, so [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) verifies its square integrability.
