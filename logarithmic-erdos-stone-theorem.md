<h1 id="logarithmic-erdos-stone-theorem">Logarithmic Erdős-Stone theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Erdős-Stone theorem](erdos-stone-theorem.md)

An [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) density exceeding the [Turan theorem](turan-s-theorem.md) threshold for $K_{r+1}$ by a fixed positive amount forces a balanced $K_{r+1}(t)$ subgraph with logarithmic part size. Combine [clique supersaturation by sampling](clique-supersaturation-by-sampling.md) with the [dense clique family blow-up lemma](dense-clique-family-blow-up-lemma.md). Dense binomial random [graphs](graph-split.md) show that the logarithmic order is optimal for a uniform density guarantee.
