# Isothermal stellar and gas density equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal collisionless tracer](isothermal-collisionless-tracer.md)

Without an escape-energy cutoff, a [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) proportional to $\exp[(\Psi-v^2/2)/\sigma^2]$ gives $\rho\propto e^{\Psi/\sigma^2}$ and a constant one-dimensional [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md). Since $\Psi=\Phi_0-\Phi$, differentiation gives the displayed hydrostatic relation. An [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) at constant [temperature](temperature.md) has pressure $P=\rho k_BT/m_0$, so its [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) equation is identical at the displayed dispersion-temperature identification. Coupling either relation to the same [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md), with matched central density and boundary data, therefore gives the same density structure. Truncating the stellar [velocities](velocity.md) at escape changes this equivalence.
