# Laminar family of sets

↑ **Parent:** [Set family](set-family.md)

A [set family](set-family.md) is laminar if any two members are disjoint or one contains the other. Its distinct inclusion-maximal members are therefore disjoint. Applied to the true sets in a hierarchical family of [intersection hypotheses](intersection-hypothesis.md), this gives the disjoint-block bound used for [familywise error control for a laminar hypothesis family](familywise-error-control-for-a-laminar-hypothesis-family.md).
