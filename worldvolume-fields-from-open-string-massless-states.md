# Worldvolume fields from open-string massless states

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

A massless open-string vector polarization tangential to a planar [D-brane](d-brane.md) becomes a worldvolume gauge field. Transverse polarizations become [scalar fields](scalar-field.md) describing fluctuations of its position. This supports the interpretation of a Dirichlet plane as a dynamical extended object. The unprojected bosonic open-string [tachyon](tachyon.md) additionally signals that its brane ground state is unstable; the massless field interpretation does not remove that instability.
