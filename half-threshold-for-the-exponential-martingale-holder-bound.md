<h1 id="half-threshold-for-the-exponential-martingale-holder-bound">Half-threshold for the exponential-martingale Hölder bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder factorization of stochastic exponentials](holder-factorization-of-stochastic-exponentials.md)

For fixed $q>1$, the coefficient in the [Hölder factorization of stochastic exponentials](holder-factorization-of-stochastic-exponentials.md) exceeds $\sqrt q/(\sqrt q+1)>1/2$. Taking $q=1+\varepsilon$ and $p=1+\varepsilon^2$ approaches one-half. Thus a uniform stopped exponential moment at coefficient one-half bounds some $p$th moment of each strict scaling $\mathcal E(aM)$, $0<a<1$.
