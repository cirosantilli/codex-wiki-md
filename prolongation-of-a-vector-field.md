# Prolongation of a vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Lie point symmetry of an ordinary differential equation](lie-point-symmetry-of-an-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

For $V=\xi(x,u)\partial_x+\eta(x,u)\partial_u$, its prolongation to derivatives through order $n$ is

$$
\operatorname{pr}^{(n)}V
=V+\sum_{j=1}^n\eta^{(j)}\partial_{u^{(j)}},
\qquad
\eta^{(j)}=D_x\eta^{(j-1)}-u^{(j)}D_x\xi.
$$

It generates a symmetry of $\Delta=0$ exactly when $\operatorname{pr}^{(n)}V(\Delta)$ vanishes on the equation manifold.

Prolongation lifts a [vector field](vector-field.md) to the [jet bundle of maps](jet-bundle-of-maps.md) so that its flow acts on derivatives of the dependent variables as well as on their values.
