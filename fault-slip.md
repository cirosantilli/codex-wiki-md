# Fault slip

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake](earthquake.md)

The relative [displacement](displacement.md) across a fault surface. Its [vector](vector.md) and the fault normal determine an ideal double-couple seismic moment; a finite rupture can have spatially and temporally variable slip.
