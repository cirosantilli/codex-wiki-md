# Adiabatic exponents of a monatomic gas-radiation mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar adiabatic exponent](stellar-adiabatic-exponent.md)

For fixed-composition monatomic [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) plus equilibrium [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md), specific internal energy is $u=3\mathcal RT/2+a_{\rm r}T^4/\rho$. An [isentropic process](isentropic-process.md) satisfies $du=P\,d\rho/\rho^2$, giving $\Gamma_3-1=(8-6\beta)/(24-21\beta)$ and

$$
\Gamma_2=\frac{32-24\beta-3\beta^2}{24-18\beta-3\beta^2},\qquad
\nabla_{\rm ad}=\frac{8-6\beta}{32-24\beta-3\beta^2}.
$$

The [stellar gas-pressure fraction](stellar-gas-pressure-fraction.md) must be allowed to change along the adiabat. The pure-radiation and pure-gas limits give $\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2=4/3$ and $5/3$, respectively. Partial ionization or different gas heat capacities change these formulas.
