# Quantum state tomography

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_state_tomography)

Quantum state tomography estimates enough expectation values on many identically prepared systems to reconstruct their [density operator](density-matrix.md). A single ordinary specimen cannot provide arbitrarily many independent samples without disturbance.
