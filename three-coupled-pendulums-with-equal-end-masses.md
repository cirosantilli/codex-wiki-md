# Three coupled pendulums with equal end masses

↑ **Parent:** [Coupled oscillator](coupled-oscillator.md)

For equal lengths, end masses $m$, middle mass $M$, and equal springs between adjacent bobs, [reflection symmetry](reflection-symmetry.md) gives mode [vectors](vector.md) $(1,1,1)$, $(1,0,-1)$ and $(1,-2m/M,1)$. Their squared frequencies are displayed above. These [vectors](vector.md) are orthogonal in the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) weighted by $\operatorname{diag}(m,M,m)$. Equal end masses, rather than equality of all three masses, suffice for this [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) reduction.
