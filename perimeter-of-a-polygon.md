# Perimeter of a polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Polygon](polygon.md)

The [sum](sum.md) of the lengths of its [sides of a polygon](side-of-a-polygon.md). For a [triangle](triangle.md) with side lengths $a,b,c$, the [perimeter of a polygon](perimeter-of-a-polygon.md) is $a+b+c$.
