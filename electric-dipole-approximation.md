# Electric-dipole approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md)

For a localized charge distribution in an external field varying little across its extent, expand the external scalar potential about a reference point $\mathbf R$. The constant term couples to total charge; the first spatially varying term gives the [electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md) $-\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E(\mathbf R,t)$, where $\mathbf d$ is the [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md). Higher spatial multipoles are neglected.

For a passing charge at minimum distance $b$, the atomic extent must be small compared with $b$. For a plane electromagnetic wave, the analogous long-wavelength condition is $ka\ll1$, as in the [electric-dipole approximation for radiation](electric-dipole-approximation-for-radiation.md). A short interaction time and a spatially uniform field across the atom are different assumptions; neither implies the other.
