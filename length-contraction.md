# Length contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_contraction)

A rod of rest length $L_0$ moving parallel to its length has simultaneous laboratory length $L_0/\gamma$, where $\gamma$ is the [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md). Transverse lengths are unchanged by the corresponding [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md).
