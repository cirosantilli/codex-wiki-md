# Eccentricity vector

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eccentricity_vector)

For an attractive [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) with gravitational parameter $GM$, [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf v$ and [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $\mathbf h=\mathbf r\times\mathbf v$, the eccentricity vector is

$$
\mathbf e=\frac{\mathbf v\times\mathbf h}{GM}-\widehat{\mathbf r}.
$$

Its magnitude is the [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md), and it points toward [periapsis](periapsis.md) for a noncircular orbit. It is a normalized attractive [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md); it remains constant under the pure inverse-square force.
