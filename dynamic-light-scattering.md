# Dynamic light scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

[Dynamic light scattering](dynamic-light-scattering.md) analyses fluctuations in scattered light to infer diffusion and hydrodynamic size under a suitable model. Large particles can dominate the intensity, making it sensitive to aggregation but limiting simple interpretation as a number distribution.
