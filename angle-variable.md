# Angle variable

↑ **Parent:** [Action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md)

A periodic canonical coordinate paired with an [action variable](action-variable.md). In an integrable background [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H_0(\mathbf J)$, the angles advance uniformly at $\dot\theta_i=\partial H_0/\partial J_i$, while the [action variables](action-variable.md) remain constant. The periodicity permits an integer-indexed [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) on each regular orbital torus.
