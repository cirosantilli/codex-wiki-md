# Cell counting

↑ **Parent:** [Cell line](cell-line.md)

[Cell counting](cell-counting.md) estimates the number of [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) in a preparation. A count per calibrated [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) gives [number density](number-density.md); multiplying by the whole preparation's [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) estimates its total count. A [trypan blue exclusion test](trypan-blue-exclusion-test.md) adds an operational distinction between dye-excluding and dye-stained [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md).
