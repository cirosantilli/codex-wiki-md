# Fermat right triangle theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Congruent number](congruent-number.md)

There is no positive integer [right triangle](right-triangle.md) whose area is a square. For a hypothetical primitive triangle of minimal hypotenuse, its [primitive Pythagorean parametrization](primitive-pythagorean-parametrization.md) makes $m,n,m-n,m+n$ pairwise coprime squares. Write $m=r^2$, $n=s^2$, $m+n=u^2$, $m-n=v^2$. Modulo eight forces $m$ odd and $n$ even. The new integer legs $(u+v)/2,(u-v)/2$ have hypotenuse $r$ and area $(s/2)^2$, a smaller positive hypotenuse, contradicting [infinite descent](infinite-descent.md). Clearing denominators extends the conclusion to rational right triangles of area one.
