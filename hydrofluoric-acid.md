# Hydrofluoric acid

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

An aqueous solution of [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) fluoride. In silicon-processing mixtures it can dissolve oxidized silicon-containing surface material; its role should not be confused with an oxidant's role in creating that material.
