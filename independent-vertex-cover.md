# Independent vertex cover

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex cover](vertex-cover.md)

An [independent vertex cover](independent-vertex-cover.md) is a [vertex cover](vertex-cover.md) that is also an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md). Its complement is an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) as well, since the cover condition forbids [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) entirely in that complement. Thus such a cover is a side of a [bipartition](bipartition-of-a-graph.md) of the [graph](graph-split.md). This standard definition is stronger than [independent dominating set](independent-dominating-set.md) on [graphs](graph-split.md) without [isolated vertices](isolated-vertex.md), and remains a different notion when [isolated vertices](isolated-vertex.md) are present. In a nonempty [simple graph](simple-graph.md), two possibly repeated witnesses $x,y$ with $\neg E(x,y)$ and no [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) outside $\{x,y\}$ express existence of an [independent vertex cover](independent-vertex-cover.md) of size at most two.
