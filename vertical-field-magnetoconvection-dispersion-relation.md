# Vertical-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md)

For a stress-free, fixed-temperature layer with vertical magnetic perturbation at its boundaries, take vertical velocity and temperature proportional to $\sin\pi z$, vertical magnetic perturbation proportional to $\cos\pi z$, and horizontal magnetic perturbations proportional to $\sin\pi z$. With $s=k^2+\pi^2$, the vertical velocity, temperature and magnetic amplitudes form a three-variable linear system whose determinant is the stated polynomial. The [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) is $\sigma$, magnetic-to-thermal diffusivity ratio is $\zeta$, and field strength is the [Chandrasekhar number](chandrasekhar-number.md) $Q$. The steady marginal curve is $R^{(e)}=[s^3+Q\pi^2s]/k^2$.
