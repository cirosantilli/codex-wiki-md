# Vanishing odd correlators of a free Gaussian field

↑ **Parent:** [Wick's theorem](wick-s-theorem.md)

A centered free field in a Gaussian vacuum has only pair contractions, so every odd correlator vanishes by [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md). Equivalently, number parity reverses the field while preserving the vacuum. This statement applies to smeared or regulated fields; it does not assert that unregulated coincident products exist. Interactions or a nonzero field mean can invalidate it.
