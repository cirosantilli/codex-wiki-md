# Spherical-shell heat equation via radial rescaling

↑ **Parent:** [Radial heat equation in three dimensions](radial-heat-equation-in-three-dimensions.md)

On a shell $0<r_-<r<r_+$ with homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), put $L=r_+-r_-$ and $u=r\psi$. The [radial heat equation in three dimensions](radial-heat-equation-in-three-dimensions.md) becomes $u_t=\kappa u_{rr}$, with zero values at both endpoints. Expanding $u(0,r)=r\psi(0,r)$ in a [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md) gives the displayed solution, with $b_n=(2/L)\int_{r_-}^{r_+}r\psi(0,r)\sin[n\pi(r-r_-)/L]dr$. Each radial mode decays at the square of its wave number. The positive inner radius makes division by $r$ harmless throughout the shell.
