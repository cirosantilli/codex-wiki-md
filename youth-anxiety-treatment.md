# Youth anxiety treatment

↑ **Parent:** [Child and adolescent clinical psychology](child-and-adolescent-clinical-psychology.md)

Psychological intervention for [anxiety disorders](anxiety-disorder.md) in children and [adolescents](adolescent.md). Treatment studies should distinguish [diagnostic remission](diagnostic-remission.md), symptom change, global improvement and everyday functioning, as well as immediate outcomes and maintenance. A successful multicomponent intervention establishes an effect of that package under the tested conditions, not the necessity of each included technique.
