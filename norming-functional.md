# Norming functional

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md)

For a nonzero [vector](vector.md) in a real or complex [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md), a [norming functional](norming-functional.md) is a [continuous linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) satisfying the displayed conditions. The [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) extends the functional sending $av$ to $a\|v\|$ on its one-dimensional span. Consequently $\|w\|\geq\operatorname{Re}\ell(w)$, with equality at $v$, giving a supporting linear [lower bound](lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) for the [norm](norm.md). At zero the zero functional can be used as a supporting functional.
