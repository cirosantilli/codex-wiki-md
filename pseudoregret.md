# Pseudoregret

↑ **Parent:** [Multi-armed bandit](multi-armed-bandit.md)

The cumulative difference between the mean reward of an optimal fixed action and the mean rewards of the selected actions. For adaptive random actions, this sum is itself a [random variable](random-variable-split.md); taking its [expected value](expected-value-split.md) gives expected pseudoregret. Observation noise is absent from the sum, although earlier noise can influence later actions.
