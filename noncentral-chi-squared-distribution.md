# Noncentral chi-squared distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md)

If $Z_1,\ldots,Z_\nu$ are independent [standard normal random variables](standard-normal-random-variable.md) and $\lambda=\sum_j\mu_j^2$, then $\sum_j(Z_j+\mu_j)^2$ has a noncentral chi-squared distribution with $\nu$ degrees of freedom and noncentrality $\lambda$. For $\lambda=0$ it reduces to the [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md). Squaring a shifted [signed normal Wald statistic](signed-normal-wald-statistic.md) gives the case $\nu=1$.
