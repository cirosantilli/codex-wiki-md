# Dirichlet Green function inside a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

For the three-dimensional ball $|x|<a$, the displayed [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md) has convention $\Delta_xG_D=+\delta(x-y)$. Its [method of images](method-of-images.md) uses the exterior inverse point $y^*=a^2y/|y|^2$. On $|x|=a$, the distance identity $|x-y^*|=(a/|y|)|x-y|$ makes the boundary value zero. The image lies outside the ball and therefore adds no source singularity to the domain. At a central source the weighted image term tends to $1/a$, giving $G_D(x,0)=-(4\pi|x|)^{-1}+(4\pi a)^{-1}$. Spherical inversion with the required radial weight is the [Kelvin transform](kelvin-transform.md).
