# Parallel rovibrational band

↑ **Parent:** [Rovibrational spectroscopy](rovibrational-spectroscopy.md)

A symmetric-top vibrational band whose transition dipole is parallel to the [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) axis, with $\Delta K=0$ in the elementary model.
