# Normed representation of a Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a Banach algebra](representation-of-a-banach-algebra.md)

A normed representation acts by individually [bounded operators](continuous-linear-operator.md) on a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md). This condition alone does not assume that $a\mapsto\pi(a)$ is [continuous](continuous-function.md) in the [operator norm](operator-norm.md), nor that the representation space is complete. A [continuous](continuous-function.md) representation has a common bound $\|\pi(a)\|\le C\|a\|$.
