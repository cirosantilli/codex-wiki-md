# Preorder traversal

↑ **Parent:** [Depth-first traversal of a tree](depth-first-traversal-of-a-tree.md)

Visit a rooted tree's root before recursively visiting its child subtrees in their specified order. For a [binary tree](binary-tree.md), the order is root, left subtree, right subtree. Unlike [inorder traversal](inorder-traversal.md), this interpretation stores an internal node's label before either child's labels.
