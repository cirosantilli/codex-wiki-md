# Principal-root obstruction to A-stability

↑ **Parent:** [A-stability](a-stability.md)

Suppose a consistent [linear multistep method](linear-multistep-method.md) has a simple principal [polynomial root](root-of-a-polynomial.md) and its analytic [logarithm](logarithm.md) satisfies $\log w(z)=z+Cz^{2r}+O(z^{2r+1})$ near zero, with real coefficients. If $C(-1)^r>0$, then at $z=i\varepsilon$ the real part of $\log w$ is positive to leading order. The same remains true after giving $z$ a small negative real part of smaller magnitude than that leading term. Thus a growing [amplification factor](amplification-factor.md) occurs inside the left half-plane, ruling out [A-stability](a-stability.md). For an order-three method with $C>0$, choose $z=-C\varepsilon^4/2+i\varepsilon$.
