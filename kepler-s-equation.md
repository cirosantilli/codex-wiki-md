↑ **Parent:** [Mean anomaly](mean-anomaly.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler's_equation)

For an elliptic [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md), [Kepler's equation](kepler-s-equation.md) relates [mean anomaly](mean-anomaly.md) $M$ and [eccentric anomaly](eccentric-anomaly.md) $E$ by

$$
M=E-e\sin E.
$$
