# Lagrangian energy function

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

For a [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) $L(q,\dot q,t)$, its energy function satisfies $d\mathcal E/dt=-\partial L/\partial t$ along the [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md). This follows by differentiating and cancelling the $\ddot q_i\partial L/\partial\dot q_i$ terms. It is conserved when $L$ has no explicit time dependence. For imposed moving constraints it need not coincide with inertial [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md); a [Jacobi integral](jacobi-integral.md) is an important example.
