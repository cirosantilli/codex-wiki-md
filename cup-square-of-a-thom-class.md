# Cup square of a Thom class

↑ **Parent:** [Thom class](thom-class.md)

For an oriented rank-$r$ [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md), let $U\in H^r(D(E),S(E);\mathbb Z)$ be its [Thom class](thom-class.md). Forgetting relative supports sends $U$ to $\pi^*e(E)$, where $e(E)$ is the [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md). Compatibility of relative and mixed [cup products](cup-product.md) gives

$$
U\cup U=U\cup\pi^*e(E).
$$

For odd $r$, [graded commutativity of the cup product](graded-commutativity-of-the-cup-product.md) makes $2U^2=0$. The [Thom isomorphism theorem](thom-isomorphism-theorem.md) is injective, so $2e(E)=0$. This explains why an odd-rank [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md) can have only two-torsion.
