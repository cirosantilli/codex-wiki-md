<h1 id="normal-reversal-around-the-mobius-core">Normal reversal around the Möbius core</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius band](mobius-band.md)

For the model $F$ of the [Möbius band](mobius-band.md), the [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md) along its [core circle of a Möbius band](core-circle-of-a-mobius-band.md) are

$$
F_\theta(\theta,0)=(-R\sin\theta,R\cos\theta,0),\qquad F_w(\theta,0)=(\cos(\theta/2)\cos\theta,\cos(\theta/2)\sin\theta,\sin(\theta/2)).
$$

Their [cross product](cross-product.md), divided by its [norm](norm.md) $R$, gives the continuous [unit normal](unit-normal.md)

$$
n(\theta)=\bigl(\cos\theta\sin(\theta/2),\ \sin\theta\sin(\theta/2),\ -\cos(\theta/2)\bigr).
$$

Although $F(2\pi,0)=F(0,0)$, we have $n(2\pi)=-n(0)$. Any other continuous choice of [unit normal](unit-normal.md) along this parametrized interval is either $n$ everywhere or $-n$ everywhere: its relative sign is a continuous map into the [discrete space](discrete-space.md) $\{+1,-1\}$, and the interval is [connected](connected-space.md). Thus no choice can agree at the identified endpoints. This short calculation proves that the [Möbius band](mobius-band.md) has no global continuous [unit normal](unit-normal.md) and is not an [orientable surface](orientable-surface.md).

The plotted quantity is $n(\theta)\cdot n(0)=\cos(\theta/2)$. It compares ambient [vectors](vector.md) at generally different points; after one circuit it is $-1$ at the starting point, and after two circuits it is $+1$ there. Only these endpoint comparisons are needed for the [orientability](orientability.md) argument.
