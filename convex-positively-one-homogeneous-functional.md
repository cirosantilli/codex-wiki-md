# Convex positively one-homogeneous functional

↑ **Parent:** [Convex optimization](convex-optimization-split.md)

This is a [proper convex function](proper-convex-function.md) that has degree-one [positive homogeneity](positively-homogeneous-function-degree-one.md), with $J(0)=0$. It need not be even or nonnegative; linear functionals are examples. Its [subdifferential](subdifferential.md) satisfies

$$
p\in\partial J(u)\iff \langle p,u\rangle=J(u)\ \text{and}\ \langle p,z\rangle\leq J(z)\ \text{for every }z.
$$

The two tests $z=0$ and $z=2u$ prove equality; the [subgradient inequality](subgradient-inequality.md) then gives the global upper bound. Conversely that bound and equality prove the subgradient condition. Hence a [subgradient](subgradient.md) at $u$ also supports every nonnegative multiple of $u$. Requiring $J(-u)=J(u)$ gives an [absolutely one-homogeneous functional](absolutely-one-homogeneous-functional.md).
