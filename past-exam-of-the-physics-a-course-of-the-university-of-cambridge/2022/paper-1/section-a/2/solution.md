<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [protons](../../../../../proton.md) at the [Large Hadron Collider](../../../../../large-hadron-collider.md) are ultrarelativistic: their [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is much greater than their [rest energy](../../../../../rest-energy.md), about $0.938\,\mathrm{GeV}$. The [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) therefore gives $pc\simeq E$. Using the [de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../de-broglie-wavelength.md),

$$
\lambda=\frac hp\simeq\frac{hc}{E}
=\frac{1.240\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{eV\,m}}{6.5\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{eV}}
=1.91\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm m.
$$

Thus the conventional [de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../de-broglie-wavelength.md) estimate is **a distance of order $10^{-19}\,\mathrm m$**.

There is a conventional factor of $2\pi$ in interpreting this estimate. [Spatial resolution from momentum transfer](../../../../../spatial-resolution-from-momentum-transfer.md) is often expressed as $\ell\sim\hbar c/Q$, where $Q$ is the transferred [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) multiplied by the [speed of light](../../../../../speed-of-light.md). Taking $Q$ of order the beam [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) gives

$$
\ell\sim\frac{197.3\,\mathrm{MeV\,fm}}{6.5\times10^6\,\mathrm{MeV}}
=3.0\times10^{-20}\,\mathrm m.
$$

Both conventions describe the same characteristic scale up to $2\pi$. The beam [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) alone does not give an exact experimental [spatial resolution](../../../../../spatial-resolution.md): a particular collision probes a scale set by its actual [momentum transfer](../../../../../momentum-transfer.md).
