<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) uses the squared [modulus](../../../../../modulus.md) of the [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md), not its real part. Write $\psi=A[\sin x+i(1-\cos x)]$. Then

$$
|\psi|^2=|A|^2\bigl[\sin^2x+(1-\cos x)^2\bigr]
=2|A|^2(1-\cos x).
$$

[Wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) requires

$$
1=2|A|^2\int_0^{2\pi}(1-\cos x)\,dx=4\pi|A|^2,
\qquad |A|^2=\frac1{4\pi}.
$$

Integrating the [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) over the required interval gives the dimensionless [probability](../../../../../probability.md)

$$
\boxed{P=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{\pi/2}(1-\cos x)\,dx
=\frac{\pi/2-1}{2\pi}
=\frac14-\frac1{2\pi}\simeq0.09085.}
$$

Thus approximately **$9.08\%$** of [position measurements](../../../../../position-measurement.md) fall in this interval.
