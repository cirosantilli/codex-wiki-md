<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [canonical commutation relations](../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) $[\hat x,\hat p_x]=[\hat y,\hat p_y]=i\hbar$, with cross-coordinate [commutators](../../../../../../commutator.md) zero. For either coordinate,

$$
[\hat a,\hat a^\dagger]=2\alpha\beta\hbar,
\qquad
\hat a^\dagger\hat a=\alpha^2\hat x^2+\beta^2\hat p_x^2-\alpha\beta\hbar.
$$

Thus $2\alpha\beta\hbar=1$. To obtain the required [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md), the ratio of its [position operator](../../../../../../position-operator.md) and [momentum operator](../../../../../../momentum-operator.md) coefficients must be preserved:

$$
\frac{\alpha^2}{\beta^2}=m^2\omega^2,
\qquad \alpha=m\omega\beta,
$$

where the positive root follows from the requested signs. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\alpha_x=\alpha_y=\sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}},
\qquad \beta_x=\beta_y=\frac1{\sqrt{2m\hbar\omega}}.}
$$

The [annihilation operators](../../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) are dimensionless; $\alpha$ has units of inverse length and $\beta$ has units of inverse [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md). Operators belonging to different coordinates commute automatically. Writing the [number operators](../../../../../../number-operator.md) as $\hat N_j=\hat a_j^\dagger\hat a_j$, substitution gives

$$
\boxed{\hat H=\hbar\omega(\hat N_x+\hat N_y+1).}
$$

The constant is the sum of the two [zero-point energies](../../../../../../zero-point-energy.md) of this [two-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../two-dimensional-isotropic-harmonic-oscillator.md).
