<h1 id="section-b/b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Set $\hat B=\hat a_x^\dagger\hat a_y-\hat a_y^\dagger\hat a_x$. Taking the [Hermitian adjoint](../../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) reverses the order of products, so $\hat B^\dagger=-\hat B$. [Complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) also reverses the sign of $-i$, and therefore

$$
\hat Q^\dagger=i\hbar\hat B^\dagger=-i\hbar\hat B=\hat Q.
$$

Thus $\hat Q$ is a [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) on the usual [oscillator](../../../../../../oscillator.md) states.

Using the coefficients in part (a), products containing two [position operators](../../../../../../position-operator.md) or two [momentum operators](../../../../../../momentum-operator.md) cancel in the difference. Cross-coordinate operators commute, leaving

$$
\hat B=2i\alpha\beta(\hat x\hat p_y-\hat y\hat p_x)
=\frac i\hbar(\hat x\hat p_y-\hat y\hat p_x).
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\hat Q=\hat x\hat p_y-\hat y\hat p_x=\hat L_z.}
$$

It is the component of [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) perpendicular to the plane, with units $\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ (equivalently $\mathrm{J\,s}$). The [rotational symmetry](../../../../../../rotational-symmetry.md) of the [two-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../two-dimensional-isotropic-harmonic-oscillator.md) makes this a conserved [observable](../../../../../../observable.md); directly, each term in $\hat B$ preserves $N_x+N_y$, so $[\hat H,\hat Q]=0$.
