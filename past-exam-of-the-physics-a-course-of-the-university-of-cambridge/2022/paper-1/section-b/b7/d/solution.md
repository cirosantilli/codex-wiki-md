<h1 id="section-b/b7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Identical [spin one-half](../../../../../../spin-one-half.md) particles are [fermions](../../../../../../fermion.md). By [fermionic exchange symmetry](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md), the complete spatial-and-spin [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) must change sign when the two particle labels are exchanged. Define the normalized antisymmetric [spin-one-half singlet state](../../../../../../spin-one-half-singlet-state.md)

$$
\chi_S=\frac{|\uparrow\rangle_1|\downarrow\rangle_2-|\downarrow\rangle_1|\uparrow\rangle_2}{\sqrt2},
$$

and the three symmetric [spin-one-half triplet states](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md)

$$
\chi_+=|\uparrow\rangle_1|\uparrow\rangle_2,\qquad
\chi_0=\frac{|\uparrow\rangle_1|\downarrow\rangle_2+|\downarrow\rangle_1|\uparrow\rangle_2}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\chi_-=|\downarrow\rangle_1|\downarrow\rangle_2.
$$

The [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is independent of [spin](../../../../../../spin.md), so these spin functions carry no additional [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md).

Both particles can occupy $u_0$ if their [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) factor is antisymmetric. The unique [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Psi_g(\phi_1,\phi_2)=u_0(\phi_1)u_0(\phi_2)\chi_S
=\frac1{2\pi}\chi_S,\qquad E_g=0,\qquad g_g=1.}
$$

Two equal-spin particles could not share this orbital, by the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md).

At the first excited [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md), $E=\epsilon=\hbar^2/(2mR^2)$, one orbital is $u_0$ and the other is $u_s$ with $s=+1$ or $-1$. Define symmetric and antisymmetric normalized spatial functions

$$
\Phi_s^{\pm}(\phi_1,\phi_2)
=\frac{u_0(\phi_1)u_s(\phi_2)\pm u_s(\phi_1)u_0(\phi_2)}{\sqrt2}
=\frac{e^{is\phi_2}\pm e^{is\phi_1}}{2\pi\sqrt2}.
$$

For each $s$, all allowed [wavefunctions](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) are spanned by

$$
\boxed{\Phi_s^+\chi_S,\qquad
\Phi_s^-\chi_+,\quad\Phi_s^-\chi_0,\quad\Phi_s^-\chi_-,\qquad s=\pm1.}
$$

There are $2(1+3)$ [orthonormal](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) states, hence **first-excited-state degeneracy $g=8$**. In each product, exactly one factor is antisymmetric, ensuring the required total [fermionic exchange symmetry](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md).
