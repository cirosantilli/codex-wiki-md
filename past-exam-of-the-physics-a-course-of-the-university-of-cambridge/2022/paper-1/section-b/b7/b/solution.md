<h1 id="section-b/b7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Solving the [time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) with $\psi\propto e^{ik\phi}$ gives $E=\hbar^2k^2/(2mR^2)$. The periodic [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) requires $e^{2\pi ik}=1$, hence $k=n\in\mathbb Z$. With the angular integration measure $d\phi$, [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) gives

$$
\int_0^{2\pi}|A|^2\,d\phi=1,
$$

so a convenient [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) is

$$
\boxed{u_n(\phi)=\frac{e^{in\phi}}{\sqrt{2\pi}},
\qquad E_n=\frac{\hbar^2n^2}{2mR^2},\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The $n=0$ [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) is nondegenerate. For each $|n|>0$, $u_n$ and $u_{-n}$ span a two-dimensional [energy eigenspace](../../../../../../energy-eigenspace.md); real [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) and [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) combinations are an equally valid [basis](../../../../../../basis.md). These opposite-circulation [eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) have [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $n\hbar$ and $-n\hbar$.

If instead the [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is normalized with respect to physical arc length $ds=R\,d\phi$, its expression is $e^{in\phi}/\sqrt{2\pi R}$. The two conventions describe the same [probability](../../../../../../probability.md), since $|u_n|^2d\phi=|u_n/\sqrt R|^2ds$. The remaining parts use $d\phi$.
