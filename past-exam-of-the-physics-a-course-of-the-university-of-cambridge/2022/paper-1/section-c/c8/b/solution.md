<h1 id="section-c/c8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Rearrange the given [torsion balance](../../../../../../torsion-balance.md) equilibrium relation as

$$
\theta=\frac{GmML}{\kappa}\frac1{r^2}.
$$

A useful choice, preserving equal measurement uncertainties on the vertical axis, is

$$
\boxed{y=\theta,\qquad x=r^{-2},\qquad c=\frac{GmML}{\kappa}.}
$$

With [angle](../../../../../../angle.md) measured in radians, $y$ is dimensionless and $x$ has units $\mathrm{m^{-2}}$, so $c$ has units $\mathrm{m^2}$, as required. The four $x$ values, in the order of the original table, are

$$
(3906.25,\ 3086.41975,\ 2066.11570,\ 976.56250)\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}.
$$

The corresponding $y$ values are $(0.066,0.055,0.044,0.025)$, each with [standard uncertainty](../../../../../../standard-uncertainty.md) $\sigma_y=0.002$. The uncertainty of $x$ is negligible by assumption, making [linear regression through the origin](../../../../../../linear-regression-through-the-origin.md) appropriate for the proposed model.
