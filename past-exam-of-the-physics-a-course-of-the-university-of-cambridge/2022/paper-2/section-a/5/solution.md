<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

In an ordinary three-dimensional crystalline [electrical insulator](../../../../../electrical-insulator.md), heat is carried mainly by [phonons](../../../../../phonon.md). [Kinetic theory](../../../../../kinetic-theory-split.md) gives the estimate

$$
\kappa\simeq\frac13 c_Vv_s\ell,
$$

where $c_V$ is [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) per unit [volume](../../../../../volume.md), $v_s$ is a typical [sound speed](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md), and $\ell$ is the [phonon mean free path](../../../../../phonon-mean-free-path.md). The [thermal conductivity](../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md) $\kappa$ has units $\mathrm{W\,m^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$.

At low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), $T\ll\Theta_D$, the [Debye model](../../../../../debye-model.md) gives $c_V\propto T^3$. With phonon-phonon collisions frozen out, diffuse scattering at the sample boundaries makes $\ell$ approximately constant, and $v_s$ is nearly constant. Hence

$$
\boxed{\kappa\propto T^3\quad\text{at low }T\text{ with boundary-limited scattering}.}
$$

At high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), of order or above the [Debye temperature](../../../../../debye-temperature.md), the [Dulong-Petit law](../../../../../dulong-petit-law.md) makes $c_V$ approximately constant. Anharmonic [phonon scattering](../../../../../phonon-scattering.md), especially resistive [Umklapp scattering](../../../../../umklapp-scattering.md), has a collision rate approximately proportional to $T$. Thus $\ell\propto T^{-1}$ and

$$
\boxed{\kappa\propto T^{-1}\quad\text{at high }T\text{ with Umklapp-limited scattering}.}
$$

These two regimes usually give an intermediate maximum of [thermal conductivity](../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md). The assumptions matter: glassy materials, strong defects or specular boundary reflection can change the low-[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) law; these are not universal laws for every [electrical insulator](../../../../../electrical-insulator.md).
