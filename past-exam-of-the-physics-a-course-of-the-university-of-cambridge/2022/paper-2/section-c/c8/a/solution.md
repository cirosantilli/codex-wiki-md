<h1 id="section-c/c8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) $\psi_k(x)\propto e^{ikx}$, impose the cyclic or [periodic boundary condition](../../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) $\psi_k(x+L)=\psi_k(x)$. It requires

$$
e^{ikL}=1,\qquad k_n=\frac{2\pi n}{L},\quad n\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Adjacent allowed [wavenumbers](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) on the full signed $k$ axis are spaced by $\Delta k=2\pi/L$. Ignoring [spin](../../../../../../spin.md), the [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md) per unit $k$ is consequently

$$
\boxed{\rho_k=\frac1{\Delta k}=\frac{L}{2\pi}.}
$$

Both positive and negative $k$ are included in this density. It is not yet the density per unit magnitude $|k|$, which has an extra factor of two away from zero. In $d$ independent dimensions the same [periodic boundary conditions](../../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) give one spatial state per $k$-space cell of measure $(2\pi/L)^d$.
