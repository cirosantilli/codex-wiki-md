<h1 id="section-c/c8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $d=1,2,3$ and take a specimen of length, area or volume $L^d$. One [monovalent atom](../../../../../../monovalent-atom.md) per [unit cell](../../../../../../unit-cell.md) supplies

$$
N_e=(L/a)^d
$$

[electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). At zero [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) fills the lowest [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) states, with two [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) states per spatial [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md). The occupied region is an interval, disk or ball of radius $k_F$. Using the $k$-space [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md) gives, in one dimension,

$$
\frac La=2\frac L{2\pi}(2k_F),\qquad
\boxed{k_F^{(1)}=\frac\pi{2a},\quad E_F^{(1)}=\frac{\hbar^2\pi^2}{8ma^2}.}
$$

In two dimensions,

$$
\frac{L^2}{a^2}=2\left(\frac L{2\pi}\right)^2\pi k_F^2,\qquad
\boxed{k_F^{(2)}=\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}a,\quad E_F^{(2)}=\frac{\pi\hbar^2}{ma^2}.}
$$

In three dimensions,

$$
\frac{L^3}{a^3}=2\left(\frac L{2\pi}\right)^3\frac{4\pi k_F^3}3,\qquad
\boxed{k_F^{(3)}=\frac{(3\pi^2)^{1/3}}a,\quad E_F^{(3)}=\frac{\hbar^2(3\pi^2)^{2/3}}{2ma^2}.}
$$

Each [Fermi energy](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) follows from the [free electron model](../../../../../../free-electron-model.md) [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) $E_F=\hbar^2k_F^2/(2m)$. The [Fermi wavevectors](../../../../../../fermi-wavevector.md) have units $\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and the [Fermi energies](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) have units [joules](../../../../../../joule.md) for $a$ in metres and $m$ in kilograms. No numerical lattice spacing is supplied, so numerical energies in [electronvolts](../../../../../../electronvolt.md) cannot be uniquely calculated. The [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) factor of two is necessary here for actual monovalent [crystals](../../../../../../crystal.md); the spinless auxiliary count of part (a) was not an instruction to remove electron spin from these crystals.
