<h1 id="section-c/c8/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In three dimensions use the $k$-space [density of states](../../../../../../../density-of-states.md) $(L/2\pi)^3$ per [spin](../../../../../../../spin.md) species and the [spherical shell](../../../../../../../spherical-shell.md) volume $4\pi k^2dk$:

$$
g_3^{(1)}(\epsilon)=\left(\frac{L}{2\pi}\right)^3 4\pi k^2\frac{dk}{d\epsilon}
=\boxed{\frac{L^3}{4\pi^2}\left(\frac{2m}{\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2}\epsilon^{1/2}.}
$$

Thus the [three-dimensional free-electron density of states](../../../../../../../three-dimensional-free-electron-density-of-states.md) grows as $\epsilon^{1/2}$. For physical [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md), including both [spin](../../../../../../../spin.md) states gives

$$
g_3(\epsilon)=\frac{L^3}{2\pi^2}\left(\frac{2m}{\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2}\epsilon^{1/2}.
$$

All three total [density of states](../../../../../../../density-of-states.md) functions have units $\mathrm{J^{-1}}$ when [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) is in [joules](../../../../../../../joule.md); they count states per energy interval. The paper explicitly suppresses [spin](../../../../../../../spin.md) in (a) but does not repeat that convention in (b), so giving both conventions avoids a hidden factor-of-two ambiguity. Parts (c)--(f) below use the physical two-spin-state [electron gas](../../../../../../../electron-gas.md).
