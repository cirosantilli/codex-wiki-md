<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [nondispersive wave](../../../../../../nondispersive-wave.md) has a linear [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega=vk$ over the relevant range, so every [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) component has the same [phase velocity](../../../../../../phase-velocity.md) $v_p=\omega/k=v$ and [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) $v_g=d\omega/dk=v$. A [wave packet](../../../../../../wave-packet.md) then translates without spreading in an ideal homogeneous lossless medium.

For [wave dispersion](../../../../../../wave-dispersion.md), $v_p$ depends on [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) or [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md). Different components accumulate different [phases](../../../../../../phase-waves.md), and in general the [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) differs from the [phase velocity](../../../../../../phase-velocity.md); variation of [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) across the spectrum produces spreading of a [wave packet](../../../../../../wave-packet.md). The membrane material can be nondispersive while confinement in a [membrane waveguide](../../../../../../membrane-waveguide.md) introduces [wave dispersion](../../../../../../wave-dispersion.md) along its length.
