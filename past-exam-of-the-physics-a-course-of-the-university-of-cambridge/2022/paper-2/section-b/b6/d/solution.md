<h1 id="section-b/b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Different transverse [normal modes](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) have different cutoff [frequencies](../../../../../../frequency.md) and [group velocities](../../../../../../group-velocity.md). A pulse exciting several of them reaches the output in delayed modal components, causing [intermodal dispersion](../../../../../../intermodal-dispersion.md), pulse distortion and overlap of successive signals. Their [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) also makes the transverse [wave intensity](../../../../../../intensity-physics.md) pattern depend on propagation distance; irregularities can transfer [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) between modes.

Mitigations are to operate between the first and second [cutoff frequencies of a membrane waveguide](../../../../../../cutoff-frequency-of-a-membrane-waveguide.md), excite only the desired [mode shape](../../../../../../mode-shape.md) with a suitable source, and use smooth uniform boundaries or gradual transitions to reduce mode conversion. Reducing the signal bandwidth keeps its spectrum inside the single-mode interval and reduces [group velocity dispersion](../../../../../../group-velocity-dispersion.md) within the selected mode. Single-mode operation removes [intermodal dispersion](../../../../../../intermodal-dispersion.md) but does not remove the dispersion derived in part (b).
