<h1 id="section-b/b6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Twice differentiating the proposed [normal mode](../../../../../../../normal-mode.md) gives

$$
\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial x^2}=-k_x^2z,\qquad
\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial y^2}=-k_y^2z,\qquad
\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial t^2}=-\omega^2z.
$$

It therefore solves the two-dimensional [wave equation](../../../../../../../wave-equation-split.md) precisely when

$$
\omega^2=v^2(k_x^2+k_y^2).
$$

The fixed-edge [boundary conditions](../../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) require $z(x,0,t)=z(x,b,t)=0$. The first is automatic, and the second requires $\sin(k_yb)=0$. The nonzero [normal modes](../../../../../../../normal-mode.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{k_y=\frac{n\pi}{b},\quad n=1,2,\ldots;\qquad
k_x=\pm\sqrt{\frac{\omega^2}{v^2}-\frac{n^2\pi^2}{b^2}}.}
$$

The apparent $n=0$ choice gives identically zero [displacement](../../../../../../../displacement.md) for this sine solution. There are no imposed end conditions in $x$, so $k_x$ is continuous rather than quantized by a second length. For a propagating mode it is real; the plus sign in the stated $\cos(\omega t-k_xx)$ convention describes propagation in $+x$. Each positive integer $n$ labels a transverse [standing wave](../../../../../../../standing-wave.md) across the strip.
