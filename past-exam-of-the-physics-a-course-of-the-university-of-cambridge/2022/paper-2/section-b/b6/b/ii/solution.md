<h1 id="section-b/b6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a fixed transverse [normal mode](../../../../../../../normal-mode.md), define its cutoff [angular frequency](../../../../../../../angular-frequency.md)

$$
\omega_{c,n}=\frac{n\pi v}{b},\qquad
\omega^2=\omega_{c,n}^2+v^2k_x^2.
$$

The longitudinal [phase velocity](../../../../../../../phase-velocity.md) and [group velocity](../../../../../../../group-velocity.md), for propagation in $+x$, are

$$
\boxed{v_p=\frac\omega{k_x}=\frac{v}{\sqrt{1-(\omega_{c,n}/\omega)^2}},\qquad
v_g=\left.\frac{d\omega}{dk_x}\right|_n
=\frac{v^2k_x}{\omega}=v\sqrt{1-(\omega_{c,n}/\omega)^2}.}
$$

Thus $v_pv_g=v^2$, $v_p>v$ and $v_g<v$. The large longitudinal [phase velocity](../../../../../../../phase-velocity.md) reflects the geometry of the transverse [standing wave](../../../../../../../standing-wave.md) and does not imply faster transport of [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) than the membrane [wave speed](../../../../../../../wave-speed.md).

The [cutoff frequency of a membrane waveguide](../../../../../../../cutoff-frequency-of-a-membrane-waveguide.md) for mode $n$ is

$$
\boxed{f_{c,n}=\frac{\omega_{c,n}}{2\pi}=\frac{nv}{2b}.}
$$

At cutoff $k_x=0$ and $v_g=0$; the longitudinal [phase velocity](../../../../../../../phase-velocity.md) has no finite value. Below cutoff,

$$
k_x=i\gamma_n,\qquad \gamma_n=\sqrt{(n\pi/b)^2-\omega^2/v^2},
$$

and a bounded field excited at $x=0$ decays as $e^{-\gamma_nx}$. It is an [evanescent wave](../../../../../../../evanescent-wave.md), rather than a freely propagating mode carrying net time-averaged [energy flux](../../../../../../../energy-flux.md) down an indefinitely long uniform strip. A finite section can still transmit through coupling of evanescent fields.
