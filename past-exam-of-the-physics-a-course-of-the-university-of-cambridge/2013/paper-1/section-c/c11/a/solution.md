<h1 id="section-c/c11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [continuous symmetry](../../../../../../continuous-symmetry.md) is represented by a unitary family $U(\epsilon)=e^{-i\epsilon G/\hbar}$ with a self-adjoint [conserved generator of a continuous quantum symmetry](../../../../../../conserved-generator-of-a-continuous-quantum-symmetry.md) $G$. If $UHU^\dagger=H$ for all $\epsilon$, differentiating gives $[H,G]=0$. Thus, for no explicit time dependence,

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\langle G\rangle}{dt}=\frac{i}{\hbar}\langle[H,G]\rangle=0}.
$$

[translation](../../../../../../translation-geometry.md) invariance conserves the corresponding [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) component; rotational invariance about one axis conserves that angular-momentum component. A [central potential](../../../../../../central-potential.md) conserves all components and $L^2$, but the components need not be simultaneously measurable because $[L_x,L_y]=i\hbar L_z$. Time-translation invariance of a closed system gives a time-independent [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) and [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation.

A [discrete symmetry](../../../../../../discrete-symmetry.md) need not have a continuously varying generator. For [parity](../../../../../../parity.md), $[H,P]=0$ lets stationary states be chosen even or odd, and preserves [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) sectors. [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) does not force every state to be an eigenstate of its conserved [observable](../../../../../../observable.md). Explicit time dependence adds $\langle\partial G/\partial t\rangle$ to the conservation equation, and an external symmetry-breaking potential generally permits exchange with the environment. Degenerate [eigenspaces](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) require choosing a compatible common basis rather than assuming every eigenvector already diagonalizes every commuting operator.
