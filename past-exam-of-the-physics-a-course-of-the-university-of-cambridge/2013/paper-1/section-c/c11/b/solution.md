<h1 id="section-c/c11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a dilute linear [diatomic gas](../../../../../../diatomic-gas.md) with fixed molecular number, [translation](../../../../../../translation-geometry.md) contributes molar $C_V=3R/2$. Rotational levels are $E_J=\hbar^2J(J+1)/(2I)$ with degeneracy $2J+1$:

$$
Z_{\rm rot}=\sum_{J=0}^\infty(2J+1)e^{-\theta_rJ(J+1)/T},\qquad
\theta_r=\frac{\hbar^2}{2Ik_B}.
$$

At $T\ll\theta_r$ rotational excitation is frozen out; at $T\gg\theta_r$ the [partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is proportional to $T$, giving $U_{\rm rot}=Nk_BT$ and $C_{V,\rm rot}=Nk_B$. Nuclear-spin populations and molecular [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) modify detailed low-temperature behaviour. The high-temperature result corresponds to two quadratic rotational [degrees of freedom](../../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md); a nonlinear molecule has three and contributes $3Nk_B/2$.

For each independent harmonic vibration, write $x=\hbar\omega/(k_BT)$. Its [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) are

$$
U_{\rm vib}=N\hbar\omega\left(\frac12+\frac1{e^x-1}\right),\qquad
\boxed{C_{V,\rm vib}=Nk_B\frac{x^2e^x}{(e^x-1)^2}}.
$$

The contribution is exponentially small at low [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and approaches $Nk_B$ per mode at high [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). [zero-point energy](../../../../../../zero-point-energy.md) does not contribute to $dU/dT$. Typically [rotations](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) activate before bond stretching, producing diatomic plateaus **$C_V/R=3/2,5/2,7/2$** as [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) increases within the molecular regime; ideal-gas $C_P=C_V+R$. Dissociation, electronic excitation and nonideal interactions eventually invalidate this simple sequence. Polyatomic gases sum over their distinct vibrational [frequencies](../../../../../../frequency.md); no single activation [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) describes all modes.
