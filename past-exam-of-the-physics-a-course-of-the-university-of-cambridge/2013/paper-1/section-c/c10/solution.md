<h1 id="section-c/c10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C10](../c10.md)

A pure [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md) is a unit ray in a complex [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md): normalization fixes total probability, a common phase is unobservable, and [superpositions](../../../../../superposition-principle.md) retain relative phases. Observables are [self-adjoint operators](../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md). For an ideal [projective measurement](../../../../../projective-measurement.md) with [spectral projector](../../../../../spectral-projector.md) $P_a$, the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) gives $p(a)=\langle\psi|P_a|\psi\rangle$ and the [Lüders rule](../../../../../luders-rule.md) gives conditional state $P_a\psi/\sqrt{p(a)}$. This preserves an [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md), not necessarily a unique vector when the outcome is degenerate. Between measurements, a closed system evolves unitarily according to $i\hbar\partial_t\psi=H\psi$. Composite systems use [tensor products](../../../../../tensor-product.md); quantum statistics additionally restrict identical-particle states. These are a framework supported by many experiments, not separate logical deductions from any one experiment.

In the [photoelectric effect](../../../../../photoelectric-effect.md), monochromatic light illuminates a clean metal and a retarding potential measures the largest emitted [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) controls the stopping [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), $eV_s=h\nu-\Phi$, while in the one-photon regime intensity mainly controls the emission rate. A threshold [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) and a stopping-energy slope $h/e$ support quantized [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) exchange rather than gradual absorption of arbitrary classical-wave [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) by each [electron](../../../../../electron.md). Contact potentials, surface contamination, [dark current](../../../../../dark-current-physics.md) and spectral impurities require controls. [Robert Andrews Millikan](../../../../../robert-andrews-millikan.md) tested the [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md)–stopping-potential relation with careful surface and electrical control.[1916, apparatus and stopping-potential measurements, §§2–5](../../../../../a-direct-photoelectric-determination-of-planck-s-h-by-robert-andrews-millikan.md)

The [Stern-Gerlach experiment](../../../../../stern-gerlach-experiment.md) sends a collimated neutral [silver](../../../../../silver.md) beam through an inhomogeneous [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) onto a detector. The force is approximately $F_z=\mu_z\partial_zB$ in an appropriate strong-field electronic description. Two resolved components rather than a continuous classical orientation spread support discrete angular-momentum projections. Field-off beam profiles and magnetic-gradient/beam-width controls distinguish splitting from ordinary broadening. In the modern description the [silver](../../../../../silver.md) ground electronic state has $J=1/2$; the 1922 experiment preceded the electron-spin interpretation. Ideal sequential analyzers along different axes illustrate incompatible observables and state preparation, but were not the original apparatus.[1922; translated experimental setup and field-on/off deposition profiles](../../../../../der-experimentelle-nachweis-der-richtungsquantelung-im-magnetfeld-by-walther-gerlach-and-otto-stern.md)

In the [Davisson–Germer experiment](../../../../../davisson-germer-experiment.md), accelerated [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) scatter from a [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) single crystal and an angularly scanned collector measures the scattered intensity. Changing voltage and crystal orientation changes narrow [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) maxima. The inferred [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) agrees with $\lambda=h/p$, with nonrelativistic $p=\sqrt{2meV}$. [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md), crystal cleaning, [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) selection and background measurements matter: adsorbed surface gas can produce additional [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) features. This supports matter-wave amplitudes and [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md), with measured intensities related to their squared magnitude; it does not mean an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) must follow a classical orbit through the apparatus.[1927, abstract; apparatus and diffraction patterns](../../../../../diffraction-of-electrons-by-a-crystal-of-nickel-by-c-davisson-and-l-h-germer.md) Together these experiments motivate quantized outcomes, wave amplitudes and probabilistic measurement while leaving a distinction between observed evidence and the general postulates used to organize it.
