↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [free particle](../../../../../free-particle.md) [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) is $\hbar\omega=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{v_{\rm ph}=\omega/k=\hbar k/(2m),\qquad v_g=d\omega/dk=\hbar k/m}.
$$

The [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) follows constant-phase surfaces of a [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md). The [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) follows the envelope of a narrow-band [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) and equals its classical velocity $p/m$. Dispersion can spread the packet; the phase speed is not the particle's transport velocity. In three dimensions $\mathbf v_g=\hbar\mathbf k/m$ and the phase speed quoted is along $\mathbf k$.
