<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

In the [small-angle approximation](../../../../../small-angle-approximation.md) the [physical pendulum](../../../../../physical-pendulum.md) is a [harmonic oscillator](../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) with $\omega=2\pi/T$. Its classical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $E=m\omega^2A^2/2=3.55\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm J$. Equating this to $(n+1/2)\hbar\omega$ gives

$$
\boxed{n\simeq\frac{m\omega A^2}{2\hbar}=5.36\times10^{30}}.
$$

An oscillating bob is not a single stationary [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md). An ideal [coherent state](../../../../../coherent-state.md) has a [Poisson distribution](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) of oscillator occupations centred on this [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md), with relative spread $1/\sqrt{\bar n}$, exceptionally small here. This [quantum occupation scale of a classical oscillator](../../../../../quantum-occupation-scale-of-a-classical-oscillator.md) fixes the excitation scale without requiring the real, damped clock to be a pure [coherent state](../../../../../coherent-state.md).
