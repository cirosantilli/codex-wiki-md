<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

From [Snell's law](../../../../../snell-s-law.md), $n=\sin\theta_i/\sin\theta_r=1.5204$. Treating the quoted angle errors as independent one-standard-deviation uncertainties and using radians in [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md),

$$
\sigma_n^2=n^2\left(\cot^2\theta_i\,\sigma_i^2+\cot^2\theta_r\,\sigma_r^2\right),\qquad
\boxed{n=1.52\pm0.14}.
$$

This [refractive-index uncertainty from Snell's law](../../../../../refractive-index-uncertainty-from-snell-s-law.md) is first-order [propagation of uncertainty](../../../../../propagation-of-uncertainty.md), with air's index approximated by one. Correlated angular [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) errors add $-2n^2\cot\theta_i\cot\theta_r\operatorname{Cov}(\theta_i,\theta_r)$. If the quoted errors are hard bounds instead, their conservative first-order sum gives about $0.19$, rather than the statistical quadrature value.
