<h1 id="section-d/d13/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) changes alter dimensions, resistances, [refractive indices](../../../../../../refractive-index.md) and offsets; gradients can generate thermoelectric voltages. Identify the dominant heat paths and the experiment's [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) coefficients before choosing insulation. Reduce [convection](../../../../../../convection-split.md) with an enclosure or, where appropriate, [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md); reduce conduction through supports and leads with suitable geometry and [thermal anchoring](../../../../../../thermal-anchoring.md); use [thermal radiation shields](../../../../../../thermal-radiation-shield.md) and low-emissivity surfaces to reduce radiative exchange. [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) alone does not remove heat conduction through wires or [thermal radiation](../../../../../../thermal-radiation.md).

In [first-order thermal isolation](../../../../../../first-order-thermal-isolation.md), a sample with [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) $C$ and [thermal conductance](../../../../../../thermal-conductance.md) $G$ to its surroundings obeys

$$
C\,\delta\dot T_s+G\delta T_s=G\delta T_b+\delta P,\qquad
\boxed{\frac{\delta T_s}{\delta T_b}=\frac1{1+i\omega\tau},\quad\tau=C/G}.
$$

Thermal [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) and insulation suppress rapid fluctuations but only delay slow ambient drift: the zero-frequency gain remains one. Active [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) control, a stable reference environment, adequate equilibration time and an appropriate sensor location address long-term stability. Self-heating produces a steady offset $\delta T_s=\delta P/G$; increasing insulation can worsen that offset and settling time. Check [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) gradients rather than relying on a remote thermometer.

Match materials or use differential/reference measurements to reduce expansion and resistance drift; keep dissimilar-metal junctions isothermal to suppress thermoelectric offsets. Log [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) alongside the [signal](../../../../../../signal.md) and deliberately vary environmental [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) to measure residual coupling, using a physically justified correction with uncertainty. Interleave [calibrations](../../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) and allow equilibration after changing power. Feedback must remain stable, and heater/sensor electronics should not introduce electrical [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) into the measurement.
