↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) is $\lambda=c/f\simeq0.020\ \mathrm m$. The longest [interferometric baseline](../../../../../interferometric-baseline.md) measures the finest angular structure and the [shortest-baseline angular scale](../../../../../shortest-baseline-angular-scale.md) describes the coarsest:

$$
\boxed{\theta_{\rm res}\sim\frac\lambda{B_{\max}}=2.0\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{rad}\simeq41'',\qquad
\theta_{\rm large}\sim\frac\lambda{B_{\min}}=4.0\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{rad}\simeq14'}.
$$

These are order-of-magnitude scales, not sharp cutoffs. A commonly used maximum-recoverable-scale convention is $0.6\lambda/B_{\min}$, giving about $8.2'$; reconstruction also depends on baseline coverage and source structure. The individual antenna's [primary beam](../../../../../primary-beam.md) is a separate field-of-view limit.[“Interferometry”, “Field of view”, and “Imaging of extended structures”](../../../../../alma-basics-by-national-radio-astronomy-observatory.md)
