<h1 id="section-d/d13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Two parallel partially reflecting surfaces in a [Fabry–Pérot interferometer](../../../../../../fabry-perot-interferometer.md) produce a sequence of coherent transmitted beams. For equal lossless mirror intensity [reflectance](../../../../../../reflectance.md) $\mathcal R$ and round-trip phase $\delta=4\pi nd\cos\theta_t/\lambda$, summing the [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) of amplitudes gives

$$
\frac{E_t}{E_i}=\frac{(1-\mathcal R)e^{i\delta/2}}{1-\mathcal R e^{i\delta}},\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal T=\frac1{1+\mathcal F\sin^2(\delta/2)},\quad
\mathcal F=\frac{4\mathcal R}{(1-\mathcal R)^2}}.
$$

[resonances](../../../../../../resonance.md) have $2nd\cos\theta_t=m\lambda$ and unit peak transmission in this symmetric lossless model. Varying angle gives concentric equal-inclination rings; changing spacing scans the [resonance](../../../../../../resonance.md) [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md).

At [normal incidence](../../../../../../normal-incidence.md) with negligible dispersion, the [free spectral range](../../../../../../free-spectral-range.md) is $\Delta\nu_{\rm FSR}=c/(2nd)$. For high [reflectance](../../../../../../reflectance.md), the peak full width gives [finesse](../../../../../../finesse.md) and resolving [power](../../../../../../power.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathscr F\simeq\frac{\pi\sqrt{\mathcal R}}{1-\mathcal R},\qquad
\frac\nu{\delta\nu}=m\mathscr F}.
$$

The coefficient $\mathcal F$ in the transmission formula and the [finesse](../../../../../../finesse.md) $\mathscr F$ are different quantities. Multiple round trips sharpen a peak, allowing high-resolution spectroscopy, [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) selection and cavity measurements. Absorption, mirror mismatch, finite angular spread, imperfect parallelism and surface errors reduce usable transmission or [finesse](../../../../../../finesse.md). Adjacent orders can overlap in a broadband measurement, requiring order selection or prior spectral information.
