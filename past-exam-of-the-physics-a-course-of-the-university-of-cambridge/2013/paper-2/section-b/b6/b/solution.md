<h1 id="section-b/b6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At the zero-slope boundary, the rightgoing and leftgoing slopes cancel, so $A_r=A_i$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{r_\psi=+1,\qquad P_r=P_i,\qquad P_t=0}.
$$

This [free-end reflection of a transverse string wave](../../../../../../free-end-reflection-of-a-transverse-string-wave.md) uses a [Neumann boundary condition](../../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md): the displacement has an antinode and is not forced to vanish. The instantaneous flux $-T\psi_x\psi_t$ through the boundary is zero, confirming that it transmits no [power](../../../../../../power.md).
