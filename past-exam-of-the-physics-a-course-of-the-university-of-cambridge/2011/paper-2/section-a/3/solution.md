<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [canonical commutation relation](../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) is $[p_x,x]=-i\hbar I$. Applying the [commutator product rule](../../../../../commutator-product-rule.md) on a common [operator domain](../../../../../operator-domain.md),

$$
[p_x^2,x]=p_x[p_x,x]+[p_x,x]p_x
=\boxed{-2i\hbar p_x}.
$$

Equivalently, applying both sides to a twice differentiable [test function](../../../../../test-function.md) gives $-\hbar^2[(xf)''-xf'']=-2\hbar^2f'$, the same result.
