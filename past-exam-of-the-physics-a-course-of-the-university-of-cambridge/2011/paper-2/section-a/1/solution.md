<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a [normalizable](../../../../../normalizable-wavefunction.md) state take $a>0$. [inverse construction of a quantum potential](../../../../../inverse-construction-of-a-quantum-potential.md) gives $V=E+\hbar^2\Psi''/(2m\Psi)$. [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) yields $\Psi''/\Psi=16a^2x^6-12ax^2$, so the chosen [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) zero fixes $E=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{V(x)=\frac{8\hbar^2a^2}{m}x^6-\frac{6\hbar^2a}{m}x^2,\qquad E=0.}
$$

This [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) lies above the negative minima of the [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md); its zero value does not mean zero [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). The [nodeless eigenfunction](../../../../../nodeless-eigenfunction.md) is the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md).
