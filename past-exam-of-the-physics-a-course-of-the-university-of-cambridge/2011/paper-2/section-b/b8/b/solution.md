<h1 id="section-b/b8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The populations give

$$
\boxed{\langle S_z\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2}(c^2-s^2)=\frac{\hbar}{2}\cos(\Omega t),\qquad
\Omega=2\omega=\frac{geB_x}{2m}}.
$$

Together with $\langle S_y\rangle=(\hbar/2)\sin(\Omega t)$, this describes [Larmor precession](../../../../../../larmor-precession.md) of a [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) initially perpendicular to the field. The positive angular [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) is $|\Omega|$; the sign specifies the rotation sense through $d\langle\mathbf S\rangle/dt=\gamma\langle\mathbf S\rangle\times\mathbf B$. The factor of two between the [spinor](../../../../../../spinor.md) amplitudes and the measured [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) is essential. The model with [quantum coherence](../../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) predicts no decay; [spin relaxation](../../../../../../spin-relaxation.md) or [dephasing](../../../../../../dephasing-channel.md) in a real solid can damp the oscillation.
