<h1 id="section-b/b8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B8](../b8.md)

Write $\gamma=ge/(2m)$, retaining the printed [magnetic moment](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) convention. The displayed two-dimensional [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) are [Pauli matrices](../../../../../pauli-matrices.md), and the [spin](../../../../../spin.md) [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is

$$
\boxed{H=-\frac{\gamma\hbar}{2}
\begin{pmatrix}B_z&B_x-iB_y\\B_x+iB_y&-B_z\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

For an x-directed field let $\omega=\gamma B_x/2$. The [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) gives $i\dot c_+=-\omega c_-$ and $i\dot c_-=-\omega c_+$. With $c_+(0)=1,c_-(0)=0$, these equations yield

$$
\boxed{|\Psi(t)\rangle=\cos(\omega t)|+z\rangle+i\sin(\omega t)|-z\rangle,\qquad
\omega=\frac{geB_x}{4m}}.
$$

Equivalently, $U(t)=e^{i\omega t\sigma_x}=I\cos\omega t+i\sigma_x\sin\omega t$. If $e$ denotes a positive [elementary charge](../../../../../elementary-charge.md) magnitude, a free [electron](../../../../../electron.md) is conventionally assigned a minus sign in its [magnetic moment](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md); that alternative reverses the rotation sense. The expressions here consistently use the moment specified in the paper.
