<h1 id="section-b/b8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $c=\cos\omega t$, $s=\sin\omega t$. The real part of the off-diagonal coherence vanishes:

$$
\langle S_x\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2}(c\,is-is\,c)=\boxed{0}.
$$

This also follows from $[H,S_x]=0$ and its zero initial expectation. Individual measurements still give $\pm\hbar/2$, each with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$.
