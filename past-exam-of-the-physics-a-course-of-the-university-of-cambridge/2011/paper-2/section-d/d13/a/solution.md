↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A representative bipolar-input 741 amplifier has **$R_{\rm in}$ of order $10^6\,\Omega$**; the LM741 specification gives a typical $2\,\mathrm{M\Omega}$ at $25^\circ\mathrm C$. This is an illustrative device specification, not an identification of an unspecified practical-class part. Other input architectures can have much larger [electrical resistance](../../../../../../electrical-resistance.md), and finite input [capacitance](../../../../../../capacitance.md) makes the [input impedance](../../../../../../input-impedance.md) [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) dependent.[SNOSC25D, §6.5, p. 5, input-resistance row and its test conditions](../../../../../../lm741-operational-amplifier-by-texas-instruments.md)
