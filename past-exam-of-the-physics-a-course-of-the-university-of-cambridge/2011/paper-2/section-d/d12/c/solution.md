<h1 id="section-d/d12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Identify the physical coupling that causes a [systematic error](../../../../../../systematic-error.md) and change the apparatus so that it can be measured or cancelled. [measurement calibration](../../../../../../measurement-calibration.md) against known reference standards at several points reveals zero and gain errors; blanks and background runs measure additive contributions. Carry uncertainties of the standards and fitted corrections into the final result.

Reversal provides a particularly transparent test. If $D_+=S+b$ and $D_-=-S+b$, the combination $(D_+-D_-)/2$ isolates $S$ while $(D_++D_-)/2$ measures the [measurement zero offset](../../../../../../measurement-zero-offset.md). Interleave and repeat the reversals so drift can be detected. The cancellation assumes a stable [measurement zero offset](../../../../../../measurement-zero-offset.md) and a genuinely odd signal; a common gain error remains. [null measurements](../../../../../../null-measurement.md) and bridge comparisons can reduce dependence on detector gain. Swapping sensors or leads exposes asymmetry; checking alignment, shielding, grounding and [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) control tests specific unwanted couplings. Varying signal amplitude or measurement [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) can reveal heating, nonlinear response or pickup.

Use independent measurement principles where possible, and test the predicted response to each deliberate change. **Repeated readings alone do not remove a fixed [systematic error](../../../../../../systematic-error.md).** Correct identified significant effects and retain uncertainty for imperfectly known corrections; complete elimination cannot generally be demonstrated. Type A and Type B classify how uncertainty is evaluated, rather than simply meaning [random measurement errors](../../../../../../random-measurement-error.md) and [systematic errors](../../../../../../systematic-error.md).[§§2–5, especially §5.2; Appendix D.1.1.6–D.1.1.8](../../../../../../guidelines-for-evaluating-and-expressing-the-uncertainty-of-nist-measurement-results-by-barry-n-taylor-and-chris-e-kuyatt.md)
