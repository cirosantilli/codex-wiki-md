<h1 id="section-c/c11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a [classically forbidden region](../../../../../../classically-forbidden-region.md), the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) has evanescent solutions rather than forcing the [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) to vanish. Matching at both sides of a finite barrier permits nonzero transmission. For a broad barrier the [WKB approximation](../../../../../../wkb-approximation.md) gives

$$
T\simeq C\exp\left[-2\int_{x_1}^{x_2}\frac{\sqrt{2m(V(x)-E)}}{\hbar}\,dx\right],
$$

where $C$ is a matching prefactor. **Transmission is exponentially sensitive to barrier width and height.** A single decaying [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) carries no [probability current](../../../../../../probability-current.md); the matched scattering solution includes the combination needed for transmitted current. [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is conserved throughout.

In [alpha decay](../../../../../../alpha-decay.md), a quasibound alpha particle penetrates the nuclear [Coulomb barrier](../../../../../../coulomb-barrier.md). Multiplying the penetration [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) by an assault [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) estimates the decay rate; the strong dependence on alpha [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) explains the wide range of lifetimes. In a [scanning tunnelling microscope](../../../../../../scanning-tunneling-microscope.md), a [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) drives [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) [quantum tunnelling](../../../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) between a conducting tip and sample. Approximately $I\propto V e^{-2\kappa d}$, so constant-current feedback resolves changes in gap width, with electronic [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md) also affecting the image. Piezoelectric motion, vibration isolation and stable [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) are necessary; a bright feature is not automatically a taller atom. Atomic-scale imaging was demonstrated with a vacuum-tunnelling microscope in the original surface study.[Abstract and pp. 57–61: atomic-scale steps and surface reconstruction](../../../../../../surface-studies-by-scanning-tunneling-microscopy-by-gerd-binnig-and-others.md) In a [Josephson junction](../../../../../../josephson-junction.md), [Cooper pair](../../../../../../cooper-pair.md) [quantum tunnelling](../../../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) through a weak link gives $I=I_c\sin\varphi$, allowing supercurrent without an applied [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) and phase-sensitive interference. These are distinct applications of barrier penetration and coupling with [quantum coherence](../../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md), not particles borrowing [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) to climb over a barrier.
