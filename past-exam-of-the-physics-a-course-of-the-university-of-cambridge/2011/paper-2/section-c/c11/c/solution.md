<h1 id="section-c/c11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Addition of two spin-one-half angular momenta gives total [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) $S=0$ or $S=1$. The normalized [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md) and [triplet states](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) are

$$
|0,0\rangle=\frac{|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle-|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
|1,1\rangle=|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle,\quad
|1,0\rangle=\frac{|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle+|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\quad |1,-1\rangle=|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle.
$$

They have $S^2$ [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $0$ and $2\hbar^2$, and $S_z=M\hbar$. The [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md) is antisymmetric under a [particle exchange operator](../../../../../../particle-exchange-operator.md), whereas all three triplets have symmetric spin-exchange parity. For two identical [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) the full state must be antisymmetric: a [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md) pairs with a spatial state with symmetric exchange parity, a [triplet state](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) with an antisymmetric spatial state. Thus **two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) in the same [atomic orbital](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) must form a [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md)**.

The identity $\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2=(S^2-S_1^2-S_2^2)/2$ gives $-3\hbar^2/4$ in the [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md) and $+\hbar^2/4$ in the [triplet state](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md). Hence an [exchange interaction](../../../../../../exchange-interaction.md) distinguishes their [energies](../../../../../../energy-split.md), with ordering determined by its coupling sign. The [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md) has perfect opposite-spin correlations along any common measurement axis and is [entangled](../../../../../../entangled-state.md). The $M=0$ [triplet state](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) is also [entangled](../../../../../../entangled-state.md), while the $M=\pm1$ [triplet states](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) are [product states](../../../../../../product-state.md) along the chosen axis.
