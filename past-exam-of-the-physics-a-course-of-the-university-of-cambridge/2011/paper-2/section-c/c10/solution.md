<h1 id="section-c/c10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C10](../c10.md)

[identical particles](../../../../../indistinguishable-particles.md) have the same intrinsic properties. Exchanging their bookkeeping labels cannot distinguish a physical preparation or change a measurable prediction. This is stronger than merely failing to track classical trajectories. For ordinary [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) and [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md), the many-particle [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) has respectively [bosonic exchange symmetry](../../../../../bosonic-exchange-symmetry.md) or [fermionic exchange symmetry](../../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md) under every [particle exchange operator](../../../../../particle-exchange-operator.md), acting on spatial and [spin](../../../../../spin.md) coordinates together. The [Spin-statistics theorem](../../../../../spin-statistics-theorem.md) connects integer [spin](../../../../../spin.md) to [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) and half-integer [spin](../../../../../spin.md) to [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md); this connection uses relativistic quantum-field assumptions, rather than following from the nonrelativistic [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) alone.

For [one-particle states](../../../../../one-particle-state.md) $u,v$ satisfying [orthonormality](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md), the two alternatives form a [quantum superposition](../../../../../quantum-superposition.md):

$$
\Psi_\pm(1,2)=\frac{u(1)v(2)\pm v(1)u(2)}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Its [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) contains an exchange interference term, absent from an incoherent mixture of labelled alternatives. The antisymmetric expression vanishes when both [one-particle states](../../../../../one-particle-state.md) are identical: **one complete [one-particle state](../../../../../one-particle-state.md) can contain at most one [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md)**. This [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) explains [electron shell](../../../../../electron-shell.md) filling, the electronic [Fermi sea](../../../../../fermi-sea.md) and [degeneracy pressure](../../../../../degeneracy-pressure.md). Opposite [spins](../../../../../spin.md) are different [one-particle states](../../../../../one-particle-state.md), so two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) may share an [atomic orbital](../../../../../atomic-orbital.md). Spatially separated [wave packets](../../../../../wave-packet.md) with negligible overlap can often be treated as distinguishable for local measurements; the underlying exchange symmetry remains.

In [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md), occupation of a level of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\epsilon$ obeys

$$
\bar n_{\rm B}=\frac1{e^{(\epsilon-\mu)/(k_BT)}-1},\qquad
\bar n_{\rm F}=\frac1{e^{(\epsilon-\mu)/(k_BT)}+1}.
$$

[bosons](../../../../../boson.md) can accumulate macroscopically in one state, giving [Bose-Einstein condensation](../../../../../bose-einstein-condensation.md); [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) instead fill successive states up to the [Fermi energy](../../../../../fermi-energy.md) at zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). When occupation is dilute, both expressions reduce to the [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md). The identical-particle classical [partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) contains $1/N!$, avoiding spurious [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) from permutations and the [Gibbs paradox](../../../../../gibbs-paradox.md). Exchange effects become conspicuous when the [thermal de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) is comparable to the interparticle spacing.

An operational test is the [Hong-Ou-Mandel effect](../../../../../hong-ou-mandel-effect.md). Down-converted [photon](../../../../../photon.md) pairs enter separate ports of a balanced [beam splitter](../../../../../beam-splitter.md); indistinguishable alternatives for one [photon](../../../../../photon.md) per output cancel. Scanning the relative arrival delay restores coincidences away from overlap. Matching [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md), spectral filtering and spatial alignment controls distinguishability; measuring delayed accidental coincidences controls background. The observed dip tests two-photon interference, and its finite depth must not automatically be interpreted as a failure of bosonic symmetry. The original apparatus varied beam-splitter position and subtracted a separately measured accidental background.[pp. 2044–2046, Fig. 1, Eq. (2), coincidence-background procedure and Fig. 2](../../../../../measurement-of-subpicosecond-time-intervals-between-two-photons-by-interference-by-c-k-hong-and-others.md)
