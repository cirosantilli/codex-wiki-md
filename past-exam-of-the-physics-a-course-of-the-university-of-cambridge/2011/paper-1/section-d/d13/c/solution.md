<h1 id="section-d/d13/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Absorption of [photons](../../../../../../photon.md) with $h\nu\gtrsim E_g$ creates electron-hole pairs, the upward band-diagram arrow. In the [depletion region](../../../../../../depletion-region.md) the built-in field drives [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) towards n material and holes towards p material; [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) also delivers carriers generated within a [diffusion length](../../../../../../diffusion-length.md) outside that region. Selective contacts collect the two signs. Illumination maintains a nonequilibrium [quasi-Fermi-level splitting](../../../../../../quasi-fermi-level-splitting.md), allowing a voltage and useful current through an external load; it is the light, rather than the equilibrium barrier, that supplies the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md).

Taking forward diode current as positive, an ideal illuminated junction has

$$
\boxed{I=I_0(e^{qV/k_BT}-1)-I_{ph}},\qquad
\boxed{I_{sc}=-I_{ph},\quad V_{oc}=\frac{k_BT}{q}\ln(1+I_{ph}/I_0).}
$$

[power](../../../../../../power.md) is delivered where $VI<0$ in this convention. Reflection, below-gap transmission, carrier thermalization, [recombination](../../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md) and resistive losses limit real conversion; even an ideal single junction has radiative detailed-balance limits.[1961, Journal of Applied Physics 32, pp.510–519: radiative detailed-balance limit](../../../../../../detailed-balance-limit-of-efficiency-of-p-n-junction-solar-cells-by-william-shockley-and-hans-joachim-queisser.md)
