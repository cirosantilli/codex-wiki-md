<h1 id="section-d/d13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Joining p-type and n-type regions initially allows majority carriers to diffuse across and recombine. Exposed negative acceptor charge on the p side and positive donor charge on the n side form a [depletion region](../../../../../../depletion-region.md). Its [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) points from n to p and balances [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) at equilibrium. The [Fermi level](../../../../../../fermi-level.md) is then spatially constant, while the conduction and [valence band](../../../../../../valence-band.md) edges bend across the [built-in potential](../../../../../../built-in-potential.md); this equilibrium barrier cannot supply continuous [power](../../../../../../power.md) to a load.

[forward bias](../../../../../../forward-bias.md) makes p positive relative to n, reducing the barrier from $qV_{bi}$ to $q(V_{bi}-V)$. [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) enter p material and holes enter n material, becoming [minority carriers](../../../../../../minority-carrier.md) whose [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) and [recombination](../../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md) sustain the current. [reverse bias](../../../../../../reverse-bias.md) enlarges the barrier and leaves a much smaller minority-carrier current until breakdown. The ideal [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) model gives **$I=I_0[e^{qV/k_BT}-1]$**, the [Shockley diode equation](../../../../../../shockley-diode-equation.md); depletion-region [recombination](../../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md), series resistance and breakdown limit this approximation. Minority-carrier injection on both sides is essential to the junction theory.[1949, pp.435–436: rectification, electron and hole currents, and injection](../../../../../../the-theory-of-p-n-junctions-in-semiconductors-and-p-n-junction-transistors-by-william-shockley.md)
