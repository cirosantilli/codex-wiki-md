↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[forward bias](../../../../../../forward-bias.md) injects [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) and holes into a common active region. Their [electron-hole recombination](../../../../../../electron-hole-recombination.md) can emit a [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) as an [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) falls from a conduction-band state into an empty valence-band state, with **$h\nu$ approximately the [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md) $E_g$** near the band edges. A [direct band gap](../../../../../../direct-band-gap.md) makes this radiative transition much more probable than an indirect gap requiring simultaneous [phonon](../../../../../../phonon.md) participation. Carrier confinement improves their overlap; [nonradiative recombination](../../../../../../nonradiative-recombination.md) and [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) trapping reduce the external efficiency. This is spontaneous electroluminescence and does not require [population inversion](../../../../../../population-inversion.md). The biased carriers have separate [quasi-Fermi levels](../../../../../../quasi-fermi-level.md), rather than one global equilibrium [Fermi level](../../../../../../fermi-level.md). The band-diagram downward arrow shows [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) emission. As a concrete implementation, an InGaN/AlGaN double heterostructure was used for a bright blue junction LED.[1994, Applied Physics Letters 64, pp.1687–1689: double-heterostructure blue LED](../../../../../../candela-class-high-brightness-ingan%E2%81%84algan-double-heterostructure-blue-light-emitting-diodes-by-shuji-nakamura-and-others.md)
