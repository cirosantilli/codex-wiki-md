<h1 id="section-d/d12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Boundary conditions quantize the transverse [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) of an [electromagnetic waveguide](../../../../../../electromagnetic-waveguide.md). For an ideal hollow rectangular conducting guide of sides $a,b$, filled with a nondispersive medium of wave speed $u$, a [transverse electric mode of a rectangular waveguide](../../../../../../transverse-electric-mode-of-a-rectangular-waveguide.md) or [transverse magnetic mode of a rectangular waveguide](../../../../../../transverse-magnetic-mode-of-a-rectangular-waveguide.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=\omega_c^2+u^2\beta^2},\qquad
\omega_c=u\pi\sqrt{(m/a)^2+(n/b)^2}.
$$

The allowed integer pairs differ: TE permits one zero index, but not both; TM requires both positive. Here $\beta$ is the longitudinal propagation constant. Below cutoff $\beta$ is imaginary, so the mode is evanescent. Above cutoff,

$$
v_p=\frac\omega\beta=\frac{u}{\sqrt{1-(\omega_c/\omega)^2}},\qquad
v_g=\frac{d\omega}{d\beta}=u\sqrt{1-(\omega_c/\omega)^2},\qquad
\boxed{v_pv_g=u^2.}
$$

The positive branch approaches the straight line $\omega=u\beta$ far above cutoff, while near cutoff its slope tends to zero. Different [frequencies](../../../../../../frequency.md) acquire different group delays and a pulse can spread. A [wave phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) speed above $u$ does not convey superluminal information; in this ideal guide [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) transport follows the [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md). A single hollow conductor cannot support a TEM mode; two-conductor guides can have a zero-cutoff TEM branch. Material dispersion, dielectric fibres and lossy boundaries require their actual [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) and need not preserve the product rule.
