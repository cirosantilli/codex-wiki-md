↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [Fourier transform spectroscopy](../../../../../../fourier-transform-spectroscopy.md), a scanning [Michelson interferometer](../../../../../../michelson-interferometer.md) measures [intensity](../../../../../../intensity-physics.md) against [optical path difference](../../../../../../optical-path-difference.md) $\ell$ rather than selecting one narrow [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) at a time. After subtraction of the constant background, an ideal stationary source gives an [interferogram](../../../../../../interferogram.md)

$$
J(\ell)=\int_0^\infty S(\nu)\cos(2\pi\nu\ell/c)\,d\nu.
$$

A cosine [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) recovers the spectral [power](../../../../../../power.md) density $S(\nu)$ after instrumental calibration. A mirror [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md) $x$ changes the round-trip path by $2x$; the zero-path-difference burst aligns the scan, and a reference laser can calibrate the [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md).

Finite maximum path difference $L$ multiplies the [interferogram](../../../../../../interferogram.md) by a window and convolves the recovered [radiation spectrum](../../../../../../radiation-spectrum.md) with that window's [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform-split.md). Thus **$\delta\nu$ is of order $c/L$**; precise factors depend on scan range and resolution convention. Uniform path samples of separation $\Delta\ell$ need **$\nu_{\max}<c/(2\Delta\ell)$** to avoid [aliasing](../../../../../../aliasing.md) without bandpass information. [interferogram apodization](../../../../../../interferogram-apodization.md) reduces sidelobes at the price of broader lines. The broad accepted optical throughput can be advantageous; the multiplex signal-to-noise advantage depends on detector noise and is not universal in photon-noise-limited measurements. Source drift during a scan, finite beam divergence, imperfect sampling and [wave phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) errors require control. The finite-window reasoning is the [delay-range resolution in Fourier transform spectroscopy](../../../../../../delay-range-resolution-in-fourier-transform-spectroscopy.md).[NBSIR 86-3339, chapters 13–16, printed pp.51–80: basic equation, apodization, resolution and sampling](../../../../../../introduction-to-fourier-transform-spectroscopy-by-julius-cohen.md)
