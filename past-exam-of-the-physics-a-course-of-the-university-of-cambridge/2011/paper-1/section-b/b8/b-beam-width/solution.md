<h1 id="section-b/b8/b-beam-width/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B (beam width)](../b-beam-width.md)

A $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ beam illuminates only $N=300$ slits, giving $mN=900$ and **$\delta\lambda_R\simeq0.511\,\mathrm{nm}$**. The two main lobes broaden by $1000/300$ compared with the wider beam. Their $0.5\,\mathrm{nm}$ separation is slightly below the stated Rayleigh threshold: **marginally unresolved by that criterion**. This is not an assertion that the profile has only one maximum: the ideal equal-line sum still has two maxima and a modest central dip, as plotted. Inference from real data also requires adequate [signal-to-noise ratio](../../../../../../signal-to-noise-ratio.md) and a known instrumental profile.
