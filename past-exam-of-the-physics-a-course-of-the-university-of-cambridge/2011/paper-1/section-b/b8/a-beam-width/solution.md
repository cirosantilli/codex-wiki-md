<h1 id="section-b/b8/a-beam-width/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A (beam width)](../a-beam-width.md)

A $5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ beam illuminates $N=5.0\,\mathrm{mm}/p=1000$ slits. The [resolving power of a diffraction grating](../../../../../../resolving-power-of-a-diffraction-grating.md) in order $m=3$ is $mN=3000$, corresponding to a [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md) separation $\delta\lambda_R\simeq460/3000=0.153\,\mathrm{nm}$. The $0.5\,\mathrm{nm}$ separation therefore gives **two well separated main peaks**. The [finite-grating incoherent doublet profile](../../../../../../finite-grating-incoherent-doublet-profile.md) adds the mutually incoherent lamp [intensities](../../../../../../intensity-physics.md), not their amplitudes.
