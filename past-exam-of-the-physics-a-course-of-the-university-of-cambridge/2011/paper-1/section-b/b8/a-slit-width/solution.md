<h1 id="section-b/b8/a-slit-width/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A (slit width)](../a-slit-width.md)

With $w=1.0\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ the order positions are unchanged from the [grating equation](../../../../../../grating-equation.md), and the envelope's first zeros satisfy $|\sin\theta|=\lambda/w$. Equivalently the first missing integer orders are **$m=\pm5$**. The third-order peaks remain appreciable. The displayed order-coordinate $s=p\sin\theta/\lambda$ places all principal maxima at integers.
