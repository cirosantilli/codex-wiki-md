<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

By linear [scalar diffraction theory](../../../../../scalar-diffraction-theory.md), complementary [aperture](../../../../../aperture.md) transmissions $t$ and $1-t$ give $U_t+U_{1-t}=U_{\rm open}$ under identical illumination. In [Fraunhofer diffraction](../../../../../fraunhofer-diffraction.md), away from the direct forward beam $U_{\rm open}=0$, so $U_{1-t}=-U_t$: **the complementary screens have identical off-axis diffraction [intensities](../../../../../intensity-physics.md)**. This is [Babinet's principle](../../../../../babinet-s-principle.md). Their [complex amplitudes](../../../../../complex-amplitude.md), rather than their [intensities](../../../../../intensity-physics.md), add. The direct beam is the exception; real conducting-edge vector effects are outside this ideal scalar statement.
