<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

For source and screen distances $s,r$, the paraxial optical path excess at [aperture](../../../../../aperture.md) radius $u$ is $u^2(1/s+1/r)/2$. The outer boundary of [Fresnel half-period zone](../../../../../fresnel-half-period-zone.md) $n$ has excess $n\lambda/2$, hence

$$
u_n^2=\frac{n\lambda sr}{s+r},\qquad \boxed{u_2=\sqrt{\frac{2(500\times10^{-9})(0.5)^2}{1.0}}=0.500\,\mathrm{mm}.}
$$

This is the outer radius of the second zone, measured from the axis.
