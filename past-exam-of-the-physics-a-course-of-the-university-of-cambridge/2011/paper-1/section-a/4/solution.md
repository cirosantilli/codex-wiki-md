<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

A conventional [body-centred cubic](../../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) cell contains two atoms, so its edge $a$ obeys $\rho a^3=2(23m_u)$. Taking $m_u=1.66054\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$ gives $a\simeq4.29\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm m$. The [neighbour shells of a body-centred cubic lattice](../../../../../neighbour-shells-of-a-body-centred-cubic-lattice.md) have eight nearest neighbours that are at $\sqrt3a/2$; the six next-nearest neighbours lie along the cell axes. Thus **the next-nearest-neighbour distance is $a\simeq0.429\,\mathrm{nm}$**.
