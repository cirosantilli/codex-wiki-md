<h1 id="section-a/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $z=2$, **$k_F=\sqrt{4\pi}/a=3.545/a$**. The circle encloses one whole zone's area, crosses each face because $k_F>\pi/a$, and misses the corners because $k_F<\sqrt2\pi/a$. In a [reduced Brillouin zone](../../../../../../reduced-brillouin-zone.md), fold the portions outside the square into neighbouring band branches. A weak [periodic potential](../../../../../../periodic-potential.md) opens [band gaps](../../../../../../band-gap.md) at crossings and deforms these pieces; divalence alone does not establish a [band insulator](../../../../../../band-insulator.md).
