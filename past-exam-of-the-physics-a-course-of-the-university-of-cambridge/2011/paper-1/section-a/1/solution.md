↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For weak [damping](../../../../../damping.md), the [ringdown comparison bound for two resonator modes](../../../../../ringdown-comparison-bound-for-two-resonator-modes.md) gives $I(t)=I(0)e^{-\omega t/Q}$. Interpreting the stated loudness comparison as an [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) comparison,

$$
\frac{I_L(2)}{I_H(2)}=2\exp\!\left[-2\left(\frac{2\pi(700)}{Q_L}-\frac{2\pi(6000)}{10^5}\right)\right]<1.
$$

Therefore **the information gives an upper bound**, not a unique value:

$$
\boxed{Q_L<\frac{2\pi(700)}{2\pi(6000)/10^5+\ln2/2}\simeq6.08\times10^3.}
$$

Equality would describe equal [intensities](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) at two seconds; the stated strict inequality allows every smaller positive $Q_L$ consistent with the weak-damping model.
