<h1 id="section-c/c10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [elastic scattering](../../../../../../elastic-scattering.md), **$E_f=E_i$** and **$\mathbf k_f-\mathbf k_i=\mathbf G$**, where $\mathbf G$ is a [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md). In the ideal static crystal, coherent scattering obeys this lattice selection rule; the crystal as a whole can take the mechanical recoil with negligible [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md). [crystal momentum](../../../../../../crystal-momentum.md) is defined modulo $\hbar\mathbf G$, not as unconstrained free-particle [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md).
