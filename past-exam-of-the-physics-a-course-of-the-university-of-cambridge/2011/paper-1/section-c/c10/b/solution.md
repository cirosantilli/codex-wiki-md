<h1 id="section-c/c10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For annihilation of a [phonon](../../../../../../phonon.md) $(\mathbf q,\omega)$,

$$
\boxed{E_f=E_i+\hbar\omega,\quad\mathbf k_f=\mathbf k_i+\mathbf q+\mathbf G.}
$$

For [phonon](../../../../../../phonon.md) creation,

$$
\boxed{E_f=E_i-\hbar\omega,\quad\mathbf k_f=\mathbf k_i-\mathbf q+\mathbf G.}
$$

The signs follow whether the [neutron](../../../../../../neutron-split.md) gains or loses the [phonon](../../../../../../phonon.md)'s [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and [crystal momentum](../../../../../../crystal-momentum.md). These are one-phonon coherent [inelastic neutron scattering](../../../../../../inelastic-neutron-scattering.md) conditions, with $\mathbf G$ accounting for lattice periodicity.
