<h1 id="section-c/c11/b-heat-capacity/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B (heat capacity)](../b-heat-capacity.md)

For three-dimensional acoustic modes, $\omega\propto q$ and the mode count in a spherical shell is proportional to $q^2dq$, giving [phonon](../../../../../../phonon.md) DOS proportional to $\omega^2$. Thermally occupied modes have $\hbar\omega$ of order $k_BT$, so $U_{la}-U_0\propto\int\omega^3n_B(\omega)\,d\omega\propto T^4$. Differentiation gives **$C_{la}\propto T^3$**. At $T\gg\theta_D$, all $3N$ harmonic modes are classically populated; each has mean [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) $k_BT$ by equipartition. Thus the [molar heat capacity](../../../../../../molar-heat-capacity.md) tends to **$3R$**, the [Dulong-Petit law](../../../../../../dulong-petit-law.md). Anharmonic expansion and electronic contributions are not included in that lattice limit.
