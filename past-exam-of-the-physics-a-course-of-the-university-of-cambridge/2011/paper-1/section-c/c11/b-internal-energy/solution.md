<h1 id="section-c/c11/b-internal-energy/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B (internal energy)](../b-internal-energy.md)

In the harmonic approximation, sum over [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) $\mathbf q$ and [phonon](../../../../../../phonon.md) branch $s$:

$$
\boxed{U_{la}=\sum_{\mathbf q,s}\hbar\omega_{\mathbf q s}\left[\frac1{e^{\hbar\omega_{\mathbf q s}/k_BT}-1}+\frac12\right].}
$$

The occupation factor is the [Bose-Einstein distribution](../../../../../../bose-einstein-distribution.md) with zero [phonon](../../../../../../phonon.md) [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md); $\tfrac12\hbar\omega$ is the [zero-point energy](../../../../../../zero-point-energy.md). A temperature-independent zero-point term does not contribute to the [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) in this fixed-frequency approximation.
