<h1 id="section-c/c11/a-internal-energy/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A (internal energy)](../a-internal-energy.md)

For independent conduction-electron states labelled by band, [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) and spin, combine those labels into $j$. Then

$$
\boxed{U_{el}=\sum_j\epsilon_j f_{\rm FD}(\epsilon_j;\mu,T)=\int g(\epsilon)\,\epsilon\frac{d\epsilon}{e^{(\epsilon-\mu)/k_BT}+1}.}
$$

Here $g$ counts the same spin and band states as the sum, and $\mu(T)$ is fixed by the number constraint $N=\int g f_{\rm FD}\,d\epsilon$. The [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) zero is a fixed band reference. Use the total DOS for total [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) or the molar DOS for molar [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md); these conventions must not be mixed.
