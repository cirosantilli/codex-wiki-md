<h1 id="section-c/c11/a-heat-capacity/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A (heat capacity)](../a-heat-capacity.md)

Only states within an [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) shell of width roughly $k_BT$ around $E_F$ appreciably change occupation at low [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). There are roughly $g(E_F)k_BT$ such states and each excitation costs [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) of order $k_BT$. Thus $\Delta U\propto g(E_F)(k_BT)^2$, giving **$C_{el}\propto g(E_F)k_B^2T$**. This is the [thermal-shell estimate of electronic heat capacity](../../../../../../thermal-shell-estimate-of-electronic-heat-capacity.md); filled states far below $E_F$ are blocked by the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). A smooth DOS and fixed particle number give the more precise Sommerfeld result $C_{el}=(\pi^2/3)g(E_F)k_B^2T$ to leading order.
