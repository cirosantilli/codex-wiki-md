<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Write the one-electron Coulomb energies as $E_n=-C/n^2$, $C>0$. The [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) allows $2n^2$ [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) in shell $n$, including [spin](../../../../../spin.md). The ground configuration therefore has two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) in $n=1$ and seven in $n=2$, giving $E_g=-C(2+7/4)=-15C/4$. The least energy-increasing promotion is from $n=2$ to $n=3$:

$$
E_e=-C\left(2+\frac64+\frac19\right)=-\frac{65C}{18},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{E_e}{E_g}=\frac{26}{27}.}
$$

Rearranging the partly occupied degenerate $n=2$ shell does not produce a higher [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Both total energies are negative, so this ratio is below one even though $E_e>E_g$.
