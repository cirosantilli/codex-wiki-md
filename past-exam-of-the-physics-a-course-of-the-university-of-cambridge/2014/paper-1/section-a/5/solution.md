<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Interpret the quoted r.m.s. as the root-mean-square fluctuation about the [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md), namely the [standard deviation](../../../../../standard-deviation.md) $\sigma=10\,\mathrm K$. Standardization gives $z=(293-273)/10=2$. The supplied cumulative Gaussian curve gives $\Phi(2)\simeq0.977$, hence

$$
\boxed{P(T>293\,\mathrm K)=1-\Phi(2)\simeq0.023.}
$$

The exact Gaussian value is $0.02275$. A raw r.m.s. [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) of $10\,\mathrm K$ could not coexist with a [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) of $273\,\mathrm K$; the spread interpretation is required.
