<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Confinement to [length](../../../../../length.md) $L$ gives a [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) scale $p\sim\hbar/L$ by the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md). Using the alpha-particle [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $m_\alpha\simeq6.64\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$, its speed scale is $v\sim\hbar/(m_\alpha L)$. Counting encounters with either wall,

$$
\boxed{\nu\sim\frac vL\sim\frac{\hbar}{m_\alpha L^2}
\simeq1.6\times10^{20}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}.}
$$

This is an order-of-magnitude collision-rate estimate, not a classical trajectory of a stationary [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md). Taking $\Delta x\Delta p=\hbar/2$, a different geometric interpretation of the well size, or successive returns to the same wall changes the coefficient by factors of order unity; the robust scale is $10^{20}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
