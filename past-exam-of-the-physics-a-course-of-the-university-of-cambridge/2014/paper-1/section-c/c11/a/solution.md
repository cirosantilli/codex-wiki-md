<h1 id="section-c/c11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a normalized [top-hat wavefunction](../../../../../../top-hat-wavefunction.md) of width $L$ centred at zero, $\psi(x)=L^{-1/2}$ inside the interval and zero outside. Its [position](../../../../../../position.md) [distribution](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) has $\langle x\rangle=0$ and $\Delta x=L/\sqrt{12}$. With Fourier convention $(2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2}$, the [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) is

$$
\widetilde\psi(p)=\sqrt{\frac L{2\pi\hbar}}\,
\operatorname{sinc}\left(\frac{pL}{2\hbar}\right),\qquad
\operatorname{sinc}z=\frac{\sin z}{z}.
$$

The [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) density has long oscillatory $1/p^2$ tails. Consequently $\int p^2|\widetilde\psi|^2dp$ diverges: **the ideal discontinuous top hat has infinite r.m.s. [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) spread**. Its symmetric [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) density has formal principal-value [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) zero, but no finite absolute first moment either. The finite-variance form $\Delta x\Delta p\ge\hbar/2$ is therefore not saturated and cannot be evaluated using the width of the central diffraction lobe as $\Delta p$.

The first [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) zeros occur at $p=\pm2\pi\hbar/L$, showing the inverse-width scale. Smoothing the edges sufficiently gives a finite [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) and restores the ordinary finite-variance uncertainty comparison; sharper edges require larger high-momentum components. It is the ideal jump, not a violation of the uncertainty principle, that causes the divergence.
