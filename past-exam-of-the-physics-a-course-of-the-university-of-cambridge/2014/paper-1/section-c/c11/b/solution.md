<h1 id="section-c/c11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a [classically forbidden region](../../../../../../classically-forbidden-region.md) the local [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) becomes imaginary. For a slowly varying barrier, the WKB [transmission probability](../../../../../../transmission-probability.md) has leading exponential

$$
\mathcal T\sim e^{-2G},\qquad
G=\frac1\hbar\int_{r_1}^{r_2}\sqrt{2\mu[V(r)-Q]}\,dr,
$$

where $r_1,r_2$ are turning points and $\mu$ is the [reduced mass](../../../../../../reduced-mass.md). This is a [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) exponent; the wave [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) has only half that exponent. Near a turning point one uses matching formulas rather than the unmodified local WKB expression.

For [alpha decay](../../../../../../alpha-decay.md), an attractive nuclear interior supports a quasibound state behind the repulsive [Coulomb barrier](../../../../../../coulomb-barrier.md) of the daughter nucleus. For zero [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md), write the exterior potential as $C/r$, $C=2Z_de^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0)$, with nuclear matching radius $R$ and outer turning point $b=C/Q>R$. The exterior [action](../../../../../../action.md) can be integrated:

$$
G=\frac{\sqrt{2\mu Q}\,b}{\hbar}
\left[\arccos\sqrt{R/b}-\sqrt{(R/b)(1-R/b)}\right].
$$

The decay rate is approximately $\lambda=\nu P_{\rm pre}e^{-2G}$ and the [half-life](../../../../../../half-life.md) is $\ln2/\lambda$. Here $\nu$ is the interior assault-frequency scale and $P_{\rm pre}$ is the alpha preformation [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). They are model-dependent; barrier penetration alone does not determine them. Nonzero [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) adds a [centrifugal barrier](../../../../../../centrifugal-barrier.md).

For $R/b\ll1$, the leading dependence is $G\propto Z_d/\sqrt Q$. Thus a modest increase in decay [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) can cause an enormous increase in decay rate, giving the [Geiger-Nuttall law](../../../../../../geiger-nuttall-law.md) relating logarithmic [half-life](../../../../../../half-life.md) to inverse square-root [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) along a suitable isotope sequence. Tunnelling conserves the total [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md): the escaping [alpha particle](../../../../../../alpha-particle.md) does not acquire [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) from the barrier.
