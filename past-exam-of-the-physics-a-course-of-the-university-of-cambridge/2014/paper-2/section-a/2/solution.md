<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For a lossless single interface at [normal incidence](../../../../../normal-incidence.md), $R=1-T=0.04$ and the [Fresnel equations](../../../../../fresnel-equations.md) give $R=[(n-1)/(n+1)]^2$, taking air's [refractive index](../../../../../refractive-index.md) as one. Selecting the [glass](../../../../../glass.md) branch $n>1$ gives

$$
\boxed{n=\frac{1+\sqrt R}{1-\sqrt R}=1.50}.
$$

The reciprocal root $2/3$ is the mathematical lower-index branch, not ordinary [glass](../../../../../glass.md).
