<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Translating a scattering centre by any [lattice translation](../../../../../lattice-translation.md) $\mathbf R$ changes the scattered [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) by $e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf R}$, up to the immaterial overall sign convention. [constructive interference](../../../../../constructive-interference.md) requires $\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf R=2\pi$ times an integer. Applying this to $a\hat{\mathbf x},a\hat{\mathbf y},a\hat{\mathbf z}$ yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbf G=\frac{2\pi}{a}(h,k,l),\quad h,k,l\in\mathbb Z}.
$$

Thus $\mathbf G$ lies in the [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md). [elastic scattering](../../../../../elastic-scattering.md) additionally requires $|\mathbf k_i+\mathbf G|=|\mathbf k_i|=2\pi/\lambda$; not every reciprocal vector is accessible for a fixed incident beam.
