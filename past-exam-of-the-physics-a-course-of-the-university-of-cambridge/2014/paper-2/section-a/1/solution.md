<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a diffraction-limited circular aperture, the [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md) gives $\theta=1.22\lambda/D$. The [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) is $\lambda=hc/E=1.24\ \mu\mathrm m$, hence **$\theta\simeq1.5\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{rad}$**. This assumes adequate optical quality and sampling; being in space removes atmospheric seeing, not [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md).
