<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [power](../../../../../power.md) linewidth is $\Delta f=f_0/Q\simeq0.044\ \mathrm{Hz}$. [Fourier frequency resolution](../../../../../fourier-frequency-resolution.md) is of order $1/T_{\rm obs}$, so **$T_{\rm obs}\gtrsim Q/f_0\simeq23\ \mathrm{s}$**, and preferably longer to distinguish the intrinsic linewidth from the observation-window width. [zero padding](../../../../../zero-padding.md) does not improve this physical resolution. A freely decaying signal also needs sufficient microphone dynamic range.
