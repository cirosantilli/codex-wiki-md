<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [mean energy of a two-dimensional Fermi gas](../../../../../mean-energy-of-a-two-dimensional-fermi-gas.md) follows from its constant [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md) $g$ for nonrelativistic quadratic dispersion. At zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), states below $\epsilon_F$ are filled, so

$$
N=g\epsilon_F,\qquad U=\int_0^{\epsilon_F}g\epsilon\,d\epsilon
=\frac12g\epsilon_F^2,\qquad
\boxed{\frac UN=\frac{\epsilon_F}{2}}.
$$
