<h1 id="section-b/b7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B7](../b7.md)

Reflection at the fixed edges combines waves with opposite $k_y$ into a transverse [standing wave](../../../../../standing-wave.md) while retaining their common longitudinal propagation factor. The [membrane waveguide](../../../../../membrane-waveguide.md) scalar transverse displacement satisfies the [wave equation](../../../../../wave-equation-split.md) and vanishes at both edges:

$$
\psi=\operatorname{Re}\{A\sin(k_yy)e^{i(\omega t-k_xx)}\},\qquad
k_y=\frac{n\pi}{b},\quad n=1,2,\ldots.
$$

The $n=0$ sine gives no displacement. [separation of variables](../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=v_m^2\left[k_x^2+\left(\frac{n\pi}{b}\right)^2\right]},
\qquad
f_{c,n}=\frac{nv_m}{2b}.
$$

For a propagating branch with $k_x>0$,

$$
\boxed{v_p=\frac{\omega}{k_x}
=\frac{v_m}{\sqrt{1-(\omega_{c,n}/\omega)^2}},\qquad
v_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk_x}
=v_m\sqrt{1-(\omega_{c,n}/\omega)^2}}.
$$

Their product is $v_m^2$. The [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) can exceed the material [wave speed](../../../../../wave-speed.md); [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) transport follows the [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md).

Here the cutoff sequence is $10n\ \mathrm{kHz}$. At $15\ \mathrm{kHz}$ only $n=1$ propagates, with **$k_x=175.6\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}$** for travel toward positive $x$.
