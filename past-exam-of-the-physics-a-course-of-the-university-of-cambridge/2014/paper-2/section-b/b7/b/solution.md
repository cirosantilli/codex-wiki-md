<h1 id="section-b/b7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At $50\ \mathrm{kHz}$ **modes $n=1,2,3,4$ can propagate**. Their positive $k_x$ values are approximately $769.5,719.8,628.3,471.2\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Mode $n=5$ is a [nonpropagating mode at waveguide cutoff](../../../../../../nonpropagating-mode-at-waveguide-cutoff.md): $k_x=0$ and $v_g=0$, so it does not carry longitudinal [power](../../../../../../power.md) as a traveling mode. Higher modes are [evanescent](../../../../../../evanescent-wave.md). The injected transverse profile determines which permitted modes are actually excited.
