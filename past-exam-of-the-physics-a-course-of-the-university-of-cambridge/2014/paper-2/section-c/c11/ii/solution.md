<h1 id="section-c/c11/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For divalent atoms, $k_Fa=\sqrt{4\pi}$. The circle has the same area as the [first Brillouin zone](../../../../../../first-brillouin-zone.md) but crosses its faces and leaves its corners unfilled. Thus **the free-electron case remains conducting**: filling capacity alone does not remove band overlap. A nearly free-electron conductor has reconstructed [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) and [hole pockets](../../../../../../hole-pocket.md); a divalent [band insulator](../../../../../../band-insulator.md) instead has one completely filled band separated from all higher bands, with no [Fermi surface](../../../../../../fermi-surface.md) in the gap.
