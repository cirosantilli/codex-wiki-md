<h1 id="section-c/c11/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [first Brillouin zone](../../../../../../first-brillouin-zone.md) is $|k_x|,|k_y|\le\pi/a$. For one atom per primitive cell and two spin states, a monovalent free-electron gas has

$$
n_{2D}=\frac1{a^2}=\frac{k_F^2}{2\pi},\qquad
\boxed{k_Fa=\sqrt{2\pi}}.
$$

Its circular [Fermi surface](../../../../../../fermi-surface.md) lies inside the zone and encloses half its area: **a partially filled band is conducting**. Here [odd filling obstructs a spin-degenerate band insulator](../../../../../../odd-filling-obstructs-a-spin-degenerate-band-insulator.md): a monovalent [band insulator](../../../../../../band-insulator.md) is impossible in this spin-degenerate one-atom independent-electron model. It would require changed premises, such as unit-cell enlargement or physics beyond the free-electron picture; no insulating free-electron contour should be invented.
