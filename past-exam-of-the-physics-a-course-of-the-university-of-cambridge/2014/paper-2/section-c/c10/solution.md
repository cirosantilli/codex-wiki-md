<h1 id="section-c/c10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C10](../c10.md)

The [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) confines thermally excited conduction [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) to an [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) interval of order $k_BT$ around the [Fermi surface](../../../../../fermi-surface.md). Their excess [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is therefore proportional to $T^2$, giving an [electronic heat capacity](../../../../../electronic-heat-capacity.md) $\gamma T$. More precisely, $\gamma=(\pi^2/3)k_B^2g_{\rm mol}(\epsilon_F)$. In a three-dimensional [Debye model](../../../../../debye-model.md), [acoustic phonons](../../../../../acoustic-phonon.md) have [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md) proportional to $\omega^2$; their thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) scales as $T^4$, giving

$$
\boxed{C=\gamma T+\beta T^3,\qquad
\beta=\frac{12\pi^4R}{5\theta_D^3}}
$$

per mole of atoms. This assumes a normal [metal](../../../../../metal.md) without a lower-temperature superconducting or magnetic anomaly.

For $\theta_D\ll T\ll T_F$, the lattice approaches the Dulong-Petit value, so **$C_V\simeq3R+\gamma T$, usually dominated by $3R$**. Each classical vibrational mode contributes $k_B$ through kinetic and potential equipartition. This is a high-temperature solid-state regime, not a claim that the solid persists to arbitrarily high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md); anharmonicity and $C_P-C_V$ can matter.

The [body-centred cubic](../../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) cell has two atoms, so $n_{\rm at}=2/a^3$. Counting three acoustic branches gives $k_D^3=6\pi^2n_{\rm at}$. By the Debye-temperature definition,

$$
\boxed{\omega_D=\frac{k_B\theta_D}{\hbar}=1.18\times10^{13}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}},
\qquad
v_D=\frac{k_B\theta_D}{\hbar(12\pi^2/a^3)^{1/3}}
\simeq1.25\times10^3\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}.
$$

This is an effective [Debye sound speed](../../../../../debye-sound-speed.md); separate longitudinal and transverse speeds are not determined. For isotropic branches, $3/v_D^3=1/v_L^3+2/v_T^3$.
