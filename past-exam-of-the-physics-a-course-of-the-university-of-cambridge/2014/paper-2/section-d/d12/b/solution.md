<h1 id="section-d/d12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Reflections from the two film surfaces interfere with propagation phase $4\pi nd\cos\theta_t/\lambda_0$ plus the relative reflection phase. Reflection from a lower to a higher [refractive index](../../../../../../refractive-index.md) contributes a $\pi$ phase reversal. For a higher-index film surrounded by air there is one relative reversal, hence

$$
\boxed{\text{reflection maxima: }2nd\cos\theta_t=(m+\tfrac12)\lambda_0,\quad
\text{minima: }2nd\cos\theta_t=m\lambda_0}.
$$

Thickness or angle variation therefore produces fringes or colours under broadband illumination. An [antireflection coating](../../../../../../antireflection-coating.md) between indices $n_0,n_s$ instead uses, at [normal incidence](../../../../../../normal-incidence.md), $n_f=\sqrt{n_0n_s}$ and $n_fd=\lambda_0/4$: the two reflected [amplitudes](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) then cancel at the design [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md). Absorption, [polarization](../../../../../../polarization-waves.md), unequal [amplitudes](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) and multiple reflections determine the full contrast.
