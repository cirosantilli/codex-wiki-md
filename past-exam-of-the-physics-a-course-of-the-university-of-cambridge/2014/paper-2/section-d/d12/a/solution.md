<h1 id="section-d/d12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a plane sound wave, [acoustic impedance](../../../../../../acoustic-impedance.md) is $Z=p/u=\rho c$, with [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $p$ and particle-velocity [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $u$. Continuity of [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) and normal velocity at normal incidence on a lossless interface gives

$$
\boxed{r_p=\frac{Z_2-Z_1}{Z_2+Z_1},\quad
\mathcal R=r_p^2,\quad
\mathcal T=\frac{4Z_1Z_2}{(Z_1+Z_2)^2}}.
$$

Matching $Z_1=Z_2$ eliminates reflection. A quarter-wave intermediate layer with $Z_m=\sqrt{Z_1Z_2}$ transforms the load to the source impedance at its design [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md); its bandwidth is limited. For strings, the analogous [mechanical impedance](../../../../../../mechanical-impedance.md) is transverse force divided by velocity, $Z=Tv^{-1}=\sqrt{T\mu}$; continuity of displacement and transverse force gives the corresponding matching condition. These [amplitudes](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) use different force/velocity conventions from acoustic [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md), so reflection signs must be stated consistently.
