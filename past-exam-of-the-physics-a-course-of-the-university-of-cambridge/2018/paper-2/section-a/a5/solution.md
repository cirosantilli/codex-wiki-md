<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

Under the one-carrier [Drude model](../../../../../drude-model.md), the magnitude of the [Hall coefficient](../../../../../hall-coefficient.md) gives an effective [carrier density](../../../../../carrier-density.md)

$$
n_H=\frac1{e|R_H|}=2.60\times10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.
$$

Convert the material [mass density](../../../../../density.md) to $1850\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and its [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md) to $0.009\,\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}$. The atomic [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) is

$$
n_{\rm at}=\frac{1850}{0.009}N_A=1.238\times10^{29}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{n_H}{n_{\rm at}}=0.210\ \text{effective carriers per atom}}.
$$

Here $N_A=6.02214076\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}$ is the [Avogadro constant](../../../../../avogadro-constant.md). The positive [Hall coefficient](../../../../../hall-coefficient.md) gives a hole-like response in a one-carrier interpretation. The [Hall carrier count versus valence](../../../../../hall-carrier-count-versus-valence.md) distinction matters: [beryllium](../../../../../beryllium.md) has two [valence electrons](../../../../../valence-electron.md), but the result is neither two free [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) nor a literal fractional atom donating charge. Its [energy bands](../../../../../energy-band.md) and [Fermi surface](../../../../../fermi-surface.md) invalidate the simple isotropic one-carrier inference. Electron-like and hole-like contributions with different [charge carrier mobilities](../../../../../charge-carrier-mobility.md) can yield this sign and effective count.
