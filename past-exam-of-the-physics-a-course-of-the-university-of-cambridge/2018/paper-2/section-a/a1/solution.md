<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

For a [photon](../../../../../photon.md), $E=hc/\lambda$. Using [Planck constant](../../../../../planck-constant.md) $h=6.62607015\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$, [speed of light](../../../../../speed-of-light.md) $c=2.99792458\times10^8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $1\,\mathrm{eV}=1.602176634\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$ gives

$$
\lambda=\frac{hc}{2.5\,\mathrm{eV}}=4.96\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm m.
$$

The [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md) for a circular [aperture](../../../../../aperture.md) of diameter $D$ is $\theta_R=1.22\lambda/D$, set by the first zero of its [Airy disk](../../../../../airy-disk.md). Thus the ideal [diffraction-limited resolution](../../../../../diffraction-limited-resolution.md) is

$$
\boxed{\theta_R=2.52\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{rad}},\qquad
\boxed{s=R\theta_R=5.04\times10^{10}\,\mathrm m}.
$$

The conversion to transverse separation uses the [small-angle approximation](../../../../../small-angle-approximation.md). An order-of-magnitude estimate $\lambda/D$ instead gives $2.07\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{rad}$ and $4.13\times10^{10}\,\mathrm m$; the distinction is the chosen resolution criterion. These are [aperture](../../../../../aperture.md) limits, before instrumental aberrations and detector sampling.
