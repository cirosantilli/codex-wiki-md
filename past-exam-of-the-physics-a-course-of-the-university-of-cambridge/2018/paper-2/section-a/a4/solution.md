<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

Take nearest-neighbour separation $d$, a common [spring constant](../../../../../spring-constant.md) $K$, and one scalar displacement per atom. For a monatomic chain, the [harmonic approximation near a potential minimum](../../../../../harmonic-approximation-near-a-potential-minimum.md), [Newton's second law](../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) and a [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) give

$$
m\ddot u_j=K(u_{j+1}+u_{j-1}-2u_j),\qquad
u_j=u_0e^{i(kjd-\omega t)},\qquad
\boxed{\omega=2\sqrt{K/m}\,|\sin(kd/2)|}.
$$

The [monatomic-chain dispersion relation](../../../../../monatomic-chain-dispersion-relation.md) has one [acoustic phonon branch](../../../../../acoustic-phonon-branch.md), with $\omega\simeq d\sqrt{K/m}|k|$ near the origin. The first [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) ends at $\boxed{k=\pm\pi/d}$, where $\omega=2\sqrt{K/m}$ and the [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) vanishes.

For alternating [masses](../../../../../mass.md) $m,M$, choose a two-atom cell of length $2d$. The coupled [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md) give the [diatomic-chain dispersion relation with equal springs](../../../../../diatomic-chain-dispersion-relation-with-equal-springs.md),

$$
\boxed{\omega_\pm^2=K\left(\frac1m+\frac1M\right)\pm K\sqrt{\left(\frac1m+\frac1M\right)^2-\frac4{mM}\sin^2(kd)}}.
$$

The first [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) now ends at $\boxed{k=\pm\pi/(2d)}$. The minus branch is the [acoustic phonon branch](../../../../../acoustic-phonon-branch.md); its two atoms move together at long [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md). The plus branch is the [optical phonon branch](../../../../../optical-phonon-branch.md); at $k=0$ the atoms move oppositely with a stationary centre of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and $\omega_+^2=2K(1/m+1/M)$. For $M>m$ the edge frequencies are $\sqrt{2K/M}$ and $\sqrt{2K/m}$, with a gap between them.

If $M=m$, the two branches reduce to $2\sqrt{K/m}|\sin(kd/2)|$ and $2\sqrt{K/m}|\cos(kd/2)|$, touching at $\sqrt{2K/m}$. This is [zone folding in an equal-mass diatomic chain](../../../../../zone-folding-in-an-equal-mass-diatomic-chain.md): identical sites actually have a [primitive unit cell](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md) of length $d$. A doubled cell folds the monatomic branch but opens no gap. Equal masses alone do not specify all possible spring models; the sketches assume equal nearest-neighbour [spring constants](../../../../../spring-constant.md).
