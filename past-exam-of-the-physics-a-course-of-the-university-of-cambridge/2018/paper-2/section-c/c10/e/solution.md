<h1 id="section-c/c10/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Assume [sodium](../../../../../../sodium.md) supplies one conduction [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) per atom. The supplied [Fermi energy](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) gives

$$
T_F=\frac{3.24(1.602176634\times10^{-19})}{1.380649\times10^{-23}}
=3.760\times10^4\,\mathrm K,\qquad T/T_F=7.98\times10^{-3}.
$$

The gas is strongly degenerate at room [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). For one [mole](../../../../../../mole-unit.md), $N=N_A$ and $N_Ak_B=R=8.31446\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$. Using the requested estimate,

$$
\boxed{C_{V,\mathrm m}^{\rm el}\simeq\frac92R\frac{300}{T_F}=0.299\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}}.
$$

The supplied [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) determines the answer per unit [volume](../../../../../../volume.md): $N/V=(970/0.023)N_A=2.54\times10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{c_V^{\rm el}\simeq1.26\times10^4\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}\,K^{-1}}},\qquad
\boxed{c_{V,\rm mass}^{\rm el}\simeq13.0\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}}.
$$

The density is not needed for the [molar heat capacity](../../../../../../molar-heat-capacity.md) once $E_F$ is supplied. Using the precise [Sommerfeld expansion](../../../../../../sommerfeld-expansion.md) coefficient instead gives $0.327\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$.

Either electronic estimate is only about $1\%$ of the stated measured total. Most of the total [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) comes from lattice [phonons](../../../../../../phonon.md), whose high-temperature [Dulong-Petit law](../../../../../../dulong-petit-law.md) limit is $3R=24.94\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$. The remaining difference from $28\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ need not be electronic: anharmonic lattice effects and the difference between [heat capacity at constant pressure](../../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) and [heat capacity at constant volume](../../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) also contribute. The paper does not specify the measurement constraint, so the two totals should not be equated exactly.
