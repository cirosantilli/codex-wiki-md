<h1 id="section-c/c10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the quantum [free electron model](../../../../../../free-electron-model.md): conduction [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) move as independent particles in a three-dimensional box of [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) $V$, with a uniform positive background replacing the ionic lattice potential. Neglect the detailed periodic potential and explicit electron-electron interactions; the interior one-electron [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) is $\epsilon=\hbar^2k^2/(2m_e)$. The boundary confines the particles, and its detailed shape does not affect the bulk state count in a sufficiently large sample.

The [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) are spin-$1/2$ [fermions](../../../../../../fermion.md): each spatial [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md) accommodates two opposite spin states, with occupations given by the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) and the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). A fixed number $N$ of conduction [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) and thermal [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are assumed. Collisions can be represented by a phenomenological [momentum relaxation time](../../../../../../momentum-relaxation-time.md) for transport, but are unnecessary for the equilibrium state counting here. These assumptions distinguish the quantum model from a classical Maxwell-Boltzmann [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) gas.
