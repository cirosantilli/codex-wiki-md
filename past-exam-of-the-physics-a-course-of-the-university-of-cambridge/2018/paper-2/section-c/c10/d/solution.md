<h1 id="section-c/c10/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Only a shell of [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) width of order $k_BT$ near the [Fermi energy](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) can change occupation: much lower states are blocked by the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). The [thermal-shell estimate of electronic heat capacity](../../../../../../thermal-shell-estimate-of-electronic-heat-capacity.md) counts

$$
N_{\rm act}\sim g(E_F)k_BT=\frac32N\frac{T}{T_F}.
$$

To recover the rough coefficient requested in the paper, assign each of these active particles an excitation [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) of order $3k_BT/2$. Then

$$
\Delta U\sim\frac32N_{\rm act}k_BT=\frac94Nk_B\frac{T^2}{T_F},\qquad
\boxed{C_V^{\rm el}\sim\frac92Nk_B\frac{T}{T_F}}.
$$

The differentiation matters: the active shell itself widens as [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) rises. This argument explains the small linear-in-$T$ [electronic heat capacity](../../../../../../electronic-heat-capacity.md); its shell width and average excitation [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) are heuristic, so it does not establish a precise numerical prefactor.

A controlled calculation from the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) gives a nearby but different result. At fixed particle number, the [Sommerfeld expansion](../../../../../../sommerfeld-expansion.md) yields

$$
U(T)=\frac35NE_F+\frac{\pi^2}{4}N\frac{(k_BT)^2}{E_F}+O(T^4),\qquad
\boxed{C_V^{\rm el}=\frac{\pi^2}{2}Nk_B\frac{T}{T_F}+O((T/T_F)^3Nk_B)}.
$$

Thus $9/2$ is a rough estimate of the exact free-electron coefficient $\pi^2/2\simeq4.935$, not an exact consequence of the stated [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md). Both require $T\ll T_F$.
