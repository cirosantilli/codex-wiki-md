<h1 id="section-c/c11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $e>0$ denote the [elementary charge](../../../../../../elementary-charge.md), so an [electron](../../../../../../electron.md)'s charge is $-e$. In the relaxation-time [Drude model](../../../../../../drude-model.md) with positive transport [effective mass](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md),

$$
m^*\frac{d\mathbf v_d}{dt}=-e\mathbf E-\frac{m^*\mathbf v_d}{\tau}.
$$

The steady [drift velocity](../../../../../../drift-velocity.md) is $\mathbf v_d=-e\tau\mathbf E/m^*=-\mu\mathbf E$, defining positive [electron mobility](../../../../../../electron-mobility.md) $\mu=e\tau/m^*$. The [current density](../../../../../../current-density.md) is opposite to the [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) drift:

$$
\mathbf J=-ne\mathbf v_d=ne\mu\mathbf E.
$$

By the definition of [electrical conductivity](../../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md), $\mathbf J=\sigma\mathbf E$, so

$$
\boxed{\sigma=ne\mu}.
$$

Its units are $(\mathrm{m^{-3}})(\mathrm C)(\mathrm{m^2\,V^{-1}\,s^{-1}})=\mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$. The mobility definition alone establishes the result in the scalar linear-response regime; the relaxation-time equation supplies a microscopic approximation for $\mu$.
