<h1 id="section-b/b6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The original diagram is a [series RLC circuit](../../../../../../series-rlc-circuit.md), so the same [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) $i=\dot q$ passes through all three elements. Choose capacitor [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $q$ positive on the plate into which positive $i$ enters. [Kirchhoff's voltage law](../../../../../../kirchhoff-cycle-law.md) gives

$$
V(t)=V_L+V_R+V_C=L\frac{di}{dt}+Ri+\frac qC=L\ddot q+R\dot q+\frac qC.
$$

Dividing by [inductance](../../../../../../inductance.md) $L$ gives

$$
\boxed{\ddot q+\frac RL\dot q+\frac1{LC}q=\frac{V(t)}L}.
$$

The correspondence to the [forced harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../forced-harmonic-oscillator.md) is $m\leftrightarrow L$, $b\leftrightarrow R$, $k\leftrightarrow1/C$, and $x\leftrightarrow q$. The stored [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is $L i^2/2+q^2/(2C)$; its balance is $\dot E=Vi-Ri^2$, confirming the damping sign.
