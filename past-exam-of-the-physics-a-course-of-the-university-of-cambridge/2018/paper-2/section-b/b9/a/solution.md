<h1 id="section-b/b9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The symbol $\rho$ here is [linear mass density](../../../../../../linear-mass-density.md), with units $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}}$. A short element $[x,x+\delta x]$ has transverse [force](../../../../../../force-split.md)

$$
F_y=T\sin\theta(x+\delta x)-T\sin\theta(x)
\simeq T[\Psi_x(x+\delta x,t)-\Psi_x(x,t)],
$$

because small slopes give $\sin\theta\simeq\tan\theta=\Psi_x$. Its [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) is $\rho\delta x$. Applying [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md), dividing by $\delta x$, and taking the limit gives the [wave equation on a string](../../../../../../wave-equation-on-a-string.md),

$$
\rho\Psi_{tt}=T\Psi_{xx},\qquad
\boxed{\Psi_{tt}=v^2\Psi_{xx},\quad v=\sqrt{T/\rho}}.
$$

The derivation assumes constant positive [tension](../../../../../../tension.md), small transverse displacements and slopes, a flexible string with negligible bending stiffness, and negligible [damping](../../../../../../damping.md). Variations of [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) due to the motion enter beyond the linear approximation.
