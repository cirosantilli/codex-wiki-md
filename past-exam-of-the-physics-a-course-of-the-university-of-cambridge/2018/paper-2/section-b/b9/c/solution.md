<h1 id="section-b/b9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a rightgoing harmonic [travelling wave](../../../../../../travelling-wave.md) in the lossless string, its [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) and transverse-velocity [complex amplitudes](../../../../../../complex-amplitude.md) satisfy $F=Zu$ with real $Z=\sqrt{T\rho}$. Thus the supplied mean-power expression gives

$$
\boxed{\langle P\rangle=\frac12Z|u|^2=\frac{|F|^2}{2Z}}
=\boxed{\frac12\sqrt{T\rho}\,\omega^2|A|^2},
$$

where $A$ is the displacement [complex amplitude](../../../../../../complex-amplitude.md) and $u=-i\omega A$ for convention $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$. The same result follows directly from instantaneous string [power](../../../../../../power.md) $P=-T\Psi_x\Psi_t$ averaged over a cycle. The amplitudes here are peak amplitudes; with [root mean square](../../../../../../root-mean-square.md) [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) or velocity the factor $1/2$ is absent. If reflections return to the driver, its net mean input is still $\operatorname{Re}(Fu^*)/2$, but its input [mechanical impedance](../../../../../../mechanical-impedance.md) need not equal the travelling-wave value $Z$.
