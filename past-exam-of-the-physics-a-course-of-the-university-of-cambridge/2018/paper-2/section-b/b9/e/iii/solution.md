<h1 id="section-b/b9/e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

There is no reflected wave when the characteristic [mechanical impedances](../../../../../../../mechanical-impedance.md) match:

$$
\boxed{Z_1=Z_2\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\rho_1=\rho_2}
$$

for the same [tension](../../../../../../../tension.md). Then $r=0$, $C/A=1$ and $\mathcal T=1$. If tensions were independently adjustable, the general condition would be $T_1\rho_1=T_2\rho_2$, together with transverse-force matching; equality of densities alone would no longer be the universal criterion.
