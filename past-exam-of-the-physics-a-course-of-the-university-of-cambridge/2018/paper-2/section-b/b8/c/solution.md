<h1 id="section-b/b8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take the two interfering [complex amplitudes](../../../../../../complex-amplitude.md) to have equal intensities $I_0/2$ and relative [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) $kx$. The [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) cross term then gives

$$
I=\frac{I_0}{2}+\frac{I_0}{2}+2\sqrt{\frac{I_0}{2}\frac{I_0}{2}}\cos(kx)
=\boxed{I_0[1+\operatorname{Re}(e^{ikx})]}.
$$

Thus the mean intensity is $I_0$, the maximum is $2I_0$, and the minima are

$$
\boxed{x=(m+\tfrac12)\lambda},\qquad m\in\mathbb Z.
$$

The [optical path difference](../../../../../../optical-path-difference.md) period is $\lambda$ and the mirror-displacement period is $\lambda/2$. The assumed equal intensities refer to the two fields at the chosen output, with matching [polarization of waves](../../../../../../polarization-waves.md) and full [optical coherence](../../../../../../optical-coherence.md). They set the normalization; they should not additionally be interpreted as all incident light entering that output port. A physical lossless [Michelson interferometer](../../../../../../michelson-interferometer.md) has a complementary second output.
