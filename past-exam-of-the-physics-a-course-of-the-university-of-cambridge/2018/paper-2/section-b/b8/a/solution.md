↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [division of amplitude](../../../../../../division-of-amplitude.md), one incident beam is split into two or more parts at a partially reflecting interface; they follow distinct paths and are recombined. The [Michelson interferometer](../../../../../../michelson-interferometer.md) and [thin-film interference](../../../../../../thin-film-interference.md) use this principle. In [division of wavefront](../../../../../../division-of-wavefront.md), spatially distinct parts of the same [wavefront](../../../../../../wavefront.md) are selected and allowed to overlap, as in [double-slit interference](../../../../../../double-slit-interference.md). Both require adequate [optical coherence](../../../../../../optical-coherence.md); [wavefront](../../../../../../wavefront.md) division particularly exposes the need for [spatial optical coherence](../../../../../../spatial-optical-coherence.md) between the selected regions.
