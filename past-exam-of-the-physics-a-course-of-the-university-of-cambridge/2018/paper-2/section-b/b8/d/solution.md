<h1 id="section-b/b8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The two [sodium](../../../../../../sodium.md) lines are distinct optical [frequencies](../../../../../../frequency.md), so their rapidly oscillating mutual cross terms average to zero at the detector. Assuming equal line strengths and negligible individual linewidths, add their separate [interferograms](../../../../../../interferogram.md). Write $\bar k=(k_1+k_2)/2$ and $\Delta k=|k_1-k_2|$. With $I_0$ the total mean output intensity,

$$
I(x)=I_0\left[1+\frac12(\cos k_1x+\cos k_2x)\right]
=I_0[1+\cos(\bar kx)\cos(\Delta kx/2)].
$$

For mirror displacement $d=x/2$ this becomes

$$
\boxed{I(d)=I_0[1+\cos(2\bar kd)\cos(\Delta kd)]},\qquad
\mathcal V(d)=|\cos(\Delta kd)|.
$$

This is [spectral doublet from Michelson visibility revivals](../../../../../../spectral-doublet-from-michelson-visibility-revivals.md). Since $\Delta\lambda\ll\bar\lambda$, $\Delta k\simeq2\pi\Delta\lambda/\bar\lambda^2$. Numerically,

$$
\boxed{\Delta d_{\rm fringe}\simeq\bar\lambda/2=294.65\,\mathrm{nm}},\qquad
\boxed{\Delta d_{\rm revival}=\pi/\Delta k\simeq0.2894\,\mathrm{mm}}.
$$

The first [interference visibility](../../../../../../interferometric-visibility.md) zero occurs at $\boxed{d=0.1447\,\mathrm{mm}}$, with further zeros at odd multiples of that distance. Visibility is restored at integer multiples of $0.2894\,\mathrm{mm}$. The _signed_ envelope has period $0.5788\,\mathrm{mm}$: alternate high-visibility packets have opposite fringe [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md). Unequal line strengths leave a nonzero minimum visibility, and finite linewidths introduce a further decaying envelope; the wavelength data alone specify neither effect.
