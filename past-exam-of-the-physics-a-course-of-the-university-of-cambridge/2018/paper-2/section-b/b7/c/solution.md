<h1 id="section-b/b7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Represent equal narrow slits at $x=\pm D/2$ by two contributions with equal [complex amplitudes](../../../../../../complex-amplitude.md). In a direction $\theta$ the path difference is $D\sin\theta$, so

$$
\psi(\theta)\propto e^{iqD/2}+e^{-iqD/2}=2\cos(qD/2),\qquad q=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\sin\theta.
$$

Therefore [double-slit interference](../../../../../../double-slit-interference.md) gives

$$
\boxed{I(\theta)\propto\cos^2\!\left(\frac{\pi D\sin\theta}{\lambda}\right)}.
$$

The bright [interference fringes](../../../../../../interference-fringe.md) satisfy $D\sin\theta=m\lambda$, with a central maximum. In the small-angle region,

$$
\boxed{\Delta\theta\simeq\lambda/D=4.00\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}=4.00\,\mathrm{mrad}}.
$$

Dark fringes lie halfway between bright ones. Exactly, spacing is uniform in $\sin\theta$, not in $\theta$ at large angles. The first panel of the pattern figure below shows the narrow-slit result.
