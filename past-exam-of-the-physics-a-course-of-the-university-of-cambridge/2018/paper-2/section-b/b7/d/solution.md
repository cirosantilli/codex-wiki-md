<h1 id="section-b/b7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A relative [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) $\pi$ changes the sign of one slit field. Their sum becomes

$$
\psi(\theta)\propto e^{iqD/2}-e^{-iqD/2}=2i\sin(qD/2),\qquad
\boxed{I(\theta)\propto\sin^2\!\left(\frac{\pi D\sin\theta}{\lambda}\right)}.
$$

The central [interference fringe](../../../../../../interference-fringe.md) is now dark. The maxima move to $D\sin\theta=(m+1/2)\lambda$, while the minima occur at $D\sin\theta=m\lambda$. Thus the pattern shifts by half a fringe, $2.00\,\mathrm{mrad}$ near the axis, but **its angular fringe separation remains** $\boxed{4.00\,\mathrm{mrad}}$. This assumes the glass changes [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) uniformly and introduces negligible absorption or beam deflection.
