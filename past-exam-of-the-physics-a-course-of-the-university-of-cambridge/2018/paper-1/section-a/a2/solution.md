<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

The [infinite square well](../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) has [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md) $E_n=n^2h^2/(8m_eL^2)$, with $n=1,2,\ldots$. The longest absorption [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) corresponds to the smallest available [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) difference from the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md), namely $n=1\to2$. Its [position](../../../../../position.md) [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) is nonzero because the two states have opposite [parity](../../../../../parity.md) about the center of the well, so it is an allowed [electric dipole transition](../../../../../electric-dipole-transition.md).

Equating the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to this gap gives

$$
\frac{hc}{\lambda}=E_2-E_1=\frac{3h^2}{8m_eL^2},\qquad \boxed{L=\sqrt{\frac{3h\lambda}{8m_ec}}=8.53\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm m=0.853\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

Here the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) gap is approximately $1.55\,\mathrm{eV}$, not the [ground-state energy](../../../../../ground-state-energy.md) itself.
