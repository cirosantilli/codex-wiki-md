<h1 id="section-b/b9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) is $\boxed{[A,B]=AB-BA}$. To evaluate it for differential operators, apply it to a smooth test [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) $f$:

$$
[x,p_x]f=x(-i\hbar f')+i\hbar(xf)'=i\hbar f.
$$

Thus the [canonical commutation relation](../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) is $\boxed{[x,p_x]=i\hbar I}$. The associated [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](../../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) is $\Delta x\,\Delta p_x\geq\hbar/2$. [Position](../../../../../../position.md) and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) cannot both have arbitrarily sharp outcome distributions in the same prepared state, even with ideal measuring apparatus. This concerns quantum-state preparation, not merely a calibration error in a particular instrument.
