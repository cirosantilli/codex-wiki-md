<h1 id="section-b/b7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The one-dimensional time-independent [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2\Psi(x)}{dx^2}+V(x)\Psi(x)=E\Psi(x).}
$$

Here $m$ is the particle [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), $V(x)$ its [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md), $E$ the stationary [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md), and $\hbar=h/(2\pi)$ the reduced [Planck constant](../../../../../../planck-constant.md). The spatial [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is $\Psi$; the full stationary state is $\Psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$. Physical bound states satisfy the boundary conditions and [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) $\int|\Psi|^2dx=1$. The operator on the left is the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), the sum of kinetic and potential energies.
