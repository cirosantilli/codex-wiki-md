<h1 id="section-b/b7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For [removal of a half-oscillator wall](../../../../../../removal-of-a-half-oscillator-wall.md), the [sudden approximation](../../../../../../sudden-approximation.md) keeps the state unchanged during the change of potential. Its [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) in the new full-oscillator [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) $\Psi_0=\pi^{-1/4}\ell^{-1/2}e^{-x^2/(2\ell^2)}$ is the squared overlap:

$$
\langle0|\Psi_h\rangle=\int_0^\infty\frac{2x}{\sqrt\pi\ell^2}e^{-x^2/\ell^2}\,dx=\frac1{\sqrt\pi},
$$

where $\int_0^\infty xe^{-x^2/\ell^2}dx=\ell^2/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P_{\mathrm{new\ ground}}=\frac1\pi\simeq0.318.}
$$

The [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) runs only over the initially occupied half-line. The initial state is not the odd full-line [eigenstate](../../../../../../eigenstate.md): it is zero, rather than negative, on the left. Indeed its overlap with that final first excited state is $1/\sqrt2$, giving [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$, and the remaining [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) lies in higher states. Subsequent evolution under the new fixed [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) changes the [quantum phases](../../../../../../quantum-phase.md) of its [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) components without changing these energy-measurement probabilities.
