<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\rho=\psi^*\psi$ be the [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md). For a real [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) $V(x)$, the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) and its complex conjugate are

$$
i\hbar\partial_t\psi=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\partial_x^2\psi+V\psi,\qquad -i\hbar\partial_t\psi^*=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\partial_x^2\psi^*+V\psi^*.
$$

Differentiate $\rho$ and substitute both equations. The potential terms cancel, leaving

$$
\partial_t\rho=\frac{i\hbar}{2m}(\psi^*\partial_x^2\psi-\psi\partial_x^2\psi^*)=\frac{i\hbar}{2m}\partial_x(\psi^*\partial_x\psi-\psi\partial_x\psi^*).
$$

Comparison with the [probability continuity equation](../../../../../../probability-continuity-equation.md) $\partial_t\rho+\partial_xJ=0$ identifies

$$
\boxed{J=\frac{i\hbar}{2m}[(\partial_x\psi^*)\psi-\psi^*(\partial_x\psi)]=\frac\hbar m\operatorname{Im}(\psi^*\partial_x\psi).}
$$

For a [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) of [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $A$ and positive [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) $k$, $J=\hbar k|A|^2/m$, the density times the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md). Integrating the continuity equation over an interval makes its [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) change equal to the incoming current minus the outgoing current.
