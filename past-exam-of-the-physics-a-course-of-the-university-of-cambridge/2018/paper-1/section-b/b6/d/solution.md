<h1 id="section-b/b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

From the second-edge [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) in part (c),

$$
\boxed{\frac FA=i\frac{k_0}{k_1}=i\sqrt{\frac{k_0}{k_2}}.}
$$

This uses $\psi_{\mathrm R}=Fe^{ik_2(x-a)}$. If the transmitted wave is instead written $Te^{ik_2x}$, its coefficient is $\boxed{T/A=i\sqrt{k_0/k_2}\,e^{-ik_2a}}$; the extra factor only changes the [quantum phase](../../../../../../quantum-phase.md) convention.

Using the [probability current](../../../../../../probability-current.md) of a [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md),

$$
J_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{\hbar k_0}{m}|A|^2,\qquad J_{\mathrm{trans}}=\frac{\hbar k_2}{m}|F|^2=\frac{\hbar k_2}{m}\frac{k_0}{k_2}|A|^2=\boxed{J_{\mathrm{in}}.}
$$

Thus the flux [transmission probability](../../../../../../transmission-probability.md) is one. The larger transmitted density $|F|^2>|A|^2$ compensates for the smaller transmitted [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $\hbar k_2/m$; equal currents need not have equal wave amplitudes.
