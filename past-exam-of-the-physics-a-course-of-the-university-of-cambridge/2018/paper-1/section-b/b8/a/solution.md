<h1 id="section-b/b8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [quantum rotor](../../../../../../quantum-rotor.md) has orientation angle $\phi$ of period $2\pi$, so physical wavefunctions satisfy $\psi(\phi+2\pi)=\psi(\phi)$. The exponential solutions of $H u=Eu$ are therefore

$$
\boxed{u_m(\phi)=\frac{e^{im\phi}}{\sqrt{2\pi}},\qquad E_m=\frac{\hbar^2m^2}{2I},\qquad m\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

They are normalized and [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) on $[0,2\pi)$. The corresponding [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) operator is $L_z=-i\hbar\partial_\phi$, with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $m\hbar$. The $m=0$ ground level is nondegenerate, and every $|m|>0$ [energy level](../../../../../../energy-level.md) has the twofold degeneracy $m\leftrightarrow-m$. This uses ordinary periodic boundary conditions, with no flux or twisted [quantum phase](../../../../../../quantum-phase.md).
