<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

For [addition of angular momentum](../../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md), the allowed total [quantum number](../../../../../quantum-number.md) runs in unit steps from $|\ell-s|$ to $\ell+s$. With $\ell=2$ and $s=1$,

$$
\boxed{j=1,2,3,\qquad m_j=-j,-j+1,\ldots,j.}
$$

Thus the complete list of multiplets is

$$
\begin{array}{c|l}
j&m_j\\\hline
1&-1,0,1\\
2&-2,-1,0,1,2\\
3&-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3
\end{array}
$$

The [total angular momentum operator](../../../../../total-angular-momentum-operator.md) has squared magnitude [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\hbar^2j(j+1)$ and chosen-axis component [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\hbar m_j$. The number of states is $3+5+7=15$, matching the uncoupled dimension $(2\ell+1)(2s+1)=5\times3$.
