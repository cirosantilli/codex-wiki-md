<h1 id="section-c/c11/semiconductor-bands-and-efficient-light-emission">Semiconductor bands and efficient light emission</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

A [semiconductor](../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) has a filled [valence band](../../../../../valence-band.md) and an empty [conduction band](../../../../../conduction-band.md) at zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) in the intrinsic idealisation, separated by a modest [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md). Thermal excitation or deliberately introduced impurities can create mobile [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and [electron holes](../../../../../electron-hole.md). Its conductivity is consequently very sensitive to [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and doping. A [metal](../../../../../metal.md) has available electronic states at the occupied [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) boundary, through a partly filled band or overlapping bands, and need not thermally excite carriers across a gap.

For a [direct band gap](../../../../../direct-band-gap.md), the conduction-band minimum and valence-band maximum occur at the same [crystal momentum](../../../../../crystal-momentum.md). An [electron](../../../../../electron.md) can recombine with a hole near these extrema and emit a [photon](../../../../../photon.md) while conserving crystal momentum, since optical photon momentum is small on the lattice scale. For an [indirect band gap](../../../../../indirect-band-gap.md), the extrema lie at different crystal momenta. A band-edge radiative transition then ordinarily needs a [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) as well, making it less probable and allowing nonradiative processes to compete more effectively.

[gallium arsenide](../../../../../gallium-arsenide.md) has a direct fundamental gap, while [silicon](../../../../../silicon.md) has an indirect one. This makes efficient band-edge emission and hence a [light-emitting diode](../../../../../light-emitting-diode.md) easier to obtain from gallium arsenide. It does not mean [silicon](../../../../../silicon.md) has no optical transitions or that every direct-gap sample emits efficiently: defects, surfaces and carrier confinement also matter.
