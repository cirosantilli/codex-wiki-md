<h1 id="section-c/c11/doping-and-the-equilibrium-junction-sketch">Doping and the equilibrium junction sketch</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

[Semiconductor doping](../../../../../semiconductor-doping.md) introduces impurities with levels close to the band edges. A [donor impurity](../../../../../donor-impurity.md) can release an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) to the [conduction band](../../../../../conduction-band.md), leaving a fixed positive ion; an [n-type semiconductor](../../../../../n-type-semiconductor.md) has [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) as majority carriers. An [acceptor impurity](../../../../../acceptor-impurity.md) can take an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) from the [valence band](../../../../../valence-band.md), leaving a mobile hole and fixed negative ion; a [p-type semiconductor](../../../../../p-type-semiconductor.md) has holes as majority carriers. The opposite species remains as a [minority carrier](../../../../../minority-carrier.md) population, and a neutral bulk sample balances the ionised dopants with mobile carriers.

At a [p-n junction](../../../../../p-n-junction.md), concentration gradients initially drive [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and holes across the join. Their removal near the join exposes negative acceptor ions on the p side and positive donor ions on the n side, forming a [depletion region](../../../../../depletion-region.md). Its electric field opposes further majority-carrier diffusion. Equilibrium has a flat [Fermi level](../../../../../fermi-level.md), no net current and spatially bent band edges. With p on the left and n on the right, the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) band edges fall towards the n side; the difference is the equilibrium barrier in [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). A flat [Fermi level](../../../../../fermi-level.md) does not mean flat conduction and valence edges.
