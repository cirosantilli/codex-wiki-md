<h1 id="section-c/c10/spring-constant-from-the-original-graph">Spring constant from the original graph</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

The vertical axis of the original figure is **[angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md)**, in $10^{13}\,\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$, not ordinary [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md). Its zone-centre optical intercept is approximately $\omega_+(0)=4.8\times10^{13}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Convert the relative atomic masses using $m_u=1.66054\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$:

$$
m_A=23m_u=3.819\times10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg},\qquad
m_B=35.5m_u=5.895\times10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$

The [long-wavelength inference of diatomic-chain parameters](../../../../../long-wavelength-inference-of-diatomic-chain-parameters.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{m_Am_B\omega_+^2(0)}{2(m_A+m_B)}\simeq26.7\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}\simeq27\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}}.
$$

No factor $2\pi$ is needed because the graph already plots $\omega$.
