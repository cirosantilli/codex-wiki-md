<h1 id="section-c/c10/physical-meaning-of-a-phonon">Physical meaning of a phonon</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

A [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) is one quantum of a crystal's vibrational [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md). In the [harmonic approximation](../../../../../harmonic-approximation.md), each mode is a quantum [harmonic oscillator](../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) with [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) levels $(n+1/2)\hbar\omega$. A [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) carries excitation [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega$ and [crystal momentum](../../../../../crystal-momentum.md) $\hbar q$ modulo a [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) vector. It is a collective lattice excitation, not one atom travelling along the chain. [Phonons](../../../../../phonon.md) obey [Bose-Einstein statistics](../../../../../bose-einstein-statistics.md); their number need not be conserved in thermal equilibrium.
