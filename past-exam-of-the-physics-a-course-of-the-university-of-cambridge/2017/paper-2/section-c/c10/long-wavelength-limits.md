<h1 id="section-c/c10/long-wavelength-limits">Long-wavelength limits</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

Put $S=m_A+m_B$ and use $\sin qa\simeq qa$. Expanding the square root gives

$$
\sqrt{S^2-4m_Am_B\sin^2qa}\simeq S-\frac{2m_Am_B}{S}a^2q^2.
$$

Thus, to leading nonzero orders,

$$
\boxed{\omega_+\simeq\sqrt{\frac{2\alpha(m_A+m_B)}{m_Am_B}}},\qquad
\boxed{\omega_-\simeq|q|a\sqrt{\frac{2\alpha}{m_A+m_B}}}.
$$

The absolute value ensures a nonnegative [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) for either direction. The [speed of sound](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) is the initial acoustic slope $v_s=a\sqrt{2\alpha/(m_A+m_B)}$.
