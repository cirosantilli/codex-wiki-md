<h1 id="section-b/b6/practical-damping-choice">Practical damping choice</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

To settle quickly after loading, [critical damping](../../../../../critical-damping.md) is preferable to the supplied weak damper:

$$
\boxed{b_c=2\sqrt{k(m+M)}\simeq1.44\times10^4\,\mathrm{N\,s\,m^{-1}}}.
$$

Using the strict-bound [mass](../../../../../mass.md) instead changes this by less than $1\%$. This eliminates free ringing while avoiding the slow tail of severe [overdamping](../../../../../overdamping.md). In the stipulated external-force model, greater damping also reduces the steady forced amplitude; there is no finite optimum based solely on that amplitude. A critical or near-critical value is a practical compromise for rapid settling. If the disturbance were specified as floor displacement transmitted through the legs, damping would introduce an additional transmission tradeoff, but that is a different forcing model.
