<h1 id="section-b/b9/thickness-from-angular-fringe-spacing">Thickness from angular fringe spacing</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

Eliminate the internal angle using [Snell's law](../../../../../snell-s-law.md):

$$
\delta(\theta_i)=\frac{4\pi d}{\lambda}\sqrt{n^2-\sin^2\theta_i},\qquad
\frac{d\delta}{d\theta_i}=-\frac{4\pi d}{\lambda}\frac{\sin\theta_i\cos\theta_i}{\sqrt{n^2-\sin^2\theta_i}}.
$$

Neighbouring bright fringes differ in [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) by $2\pi$. The local [angular fringe spacing of a transparent film](../../../../../angular-fringe-spacing-of-a-transparent-film.md) therefore gives

$$
d\simeq\frac{\lambda\sqrt{n^2-\sin^2\theta_i}}{2\sin\theta_i\cos\theta_i\,\Delta\theta_i}.
$$

With $\Delta\theta_i=0.4\pi/180=6.981\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$, $\theta_i\simeq10^\circ$, $n=1.2$ and $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$,

$$
\boxed{d\simeq2.49\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm m\simeq0.25\,\mathrm{mm}}.
$$

The data specify a local spacing near $10^\circ$, so differentiating is appropriate; choosing particular neighbouring fringe angles slightly shifts this approximate result. The [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) decreases as incidence angle increases.
