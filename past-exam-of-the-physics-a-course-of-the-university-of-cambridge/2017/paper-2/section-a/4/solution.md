<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

In a conventional [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) cell of side $a_F$, touching nearest-neighbour [hard spheres](../../../../../hard-sphere.md) lie along a face diagonal. Four radii span that diagonal, so $\sqrt2a_F=4r$ and $a_F=2\sqrt2r$. In a conventional [body-centered cubic lattice](../../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) cell, the contact lies on the body diagonal: $\sqrt3a_B=4r$, giving $a_B=4r/\sqrt3$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{V_F}{V_B}=\left(\frac{2\sqrt2r}{4r/\sqrt3}\right)^3=\frac{3\sqrt6}{4}=1.837}.
$$

These are the nonprimitive cubic [unit cells](../../../../../unit-cell.md) requested. They contain four and two atoms respectively, so their volume ratio should not be confused with the volume-per-atom ratio, which is half as large, $3\sqrt6/8\simeq0.9186$. The common sphere radius cancels.
