<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [characteristic impedance of a stretched string](../../../../../characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md) is $Z=T/c=\rho c$, since its [wave speed](../../../../../wave-speed.md) obeys $c^2=T/\rho$. Put the endpoint at $x=0$ and use displacement [complex amplitudes](../../../../../complex-amplitude.md) $A$ incident and $B$ reflected:

$$
\Psi=(Ae^{ikx}+Be^{-ikx})e^{-i\omega t},\qquad k=\omega/c.
$$

At the massless end, the string's transverse [force](../../../../../force-split.md) $-T\Psi_x$ balances the damper's [force](../../../../../force-split.md) $-\alpha\Psi_t$. Thus $T\Psi_x=-\alpha\Psi_t$, or $Z(A-B)=\alpha(A+B)$. This [viscous termination of a stretched string](../../../../../viscous-termination-of-a-stretched-string.md) has

$$
r=\frac BA=\frac{Z-\alpha}{Z+\alpha},\qquad \mathcal R=|r|^2.
$$

Half the incident mean [power](../../../../../power.md) is reflected when $r=\pm1/\sqrt2$, giving **two positive solutions**:

$$
\boxed{\alpha=(3-2\sqrt2)\rho c\quad\text{or}\quad\alpha=(3+2\sqrt2)\rho c}.
$$

They are approximately $0.1716\rho c$ and $5.828\rho c$, in $\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$. The smaller coefficient produces no reflected displacement-phase reversal; the larger produces a $\pi$ reversal. Both dissipate the remaining half of the incident [power](../../../../../power.md). Choosing the impedance-matched value $\alpha=\rho c$ would instead absorb all of it.
