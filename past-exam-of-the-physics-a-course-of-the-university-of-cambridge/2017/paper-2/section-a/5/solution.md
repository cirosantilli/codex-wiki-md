<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

For fixed [electron](../../../../../electron.md) number $N$, the specified [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) scales as $P\propto V^{-5/3}$. Differentiating at fixed low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) gives the [degeneracy-pressure bulk modulus](../../../../../degeneracy-pressure-bulk-modulus.md),

$$
\boxed{K_T=-V\frac{\partial P}{\partial V}=\frac53P}.
$$

A monovalent sample has one conduction [electron](../../../../../electron.md) per atom. Its [carrier density](../../../../../carrier-density.md) is

$$
n=\frac{862\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}}{0.039\,\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}}N_A
=1.331\times10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}},
$$

using the [Avogadro constant](../../../../../avogadro-constant.md) $N_A=6.02214076\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}$. Substitution with [reduced Planck constant](../../../../../reduced-planck-constant.md) $\hbar=1.05457\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$ and [electron mass](../../../../../electron-mass.md) $m_e=9.10938\times10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$ gives

$$
P=1.747\times10^9\,\mathrm{Pa},\qquad
\boxed{K_T=2.91\times10^9\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq2.9\,\mathrm{GPa}}.
$$

Equivalently, $P=2nE_F/5$ and $K_T=2nE_F/3$ for this [free electron model](../../../../../free-electron-model.md). This is the assumed electronic contribution, not a calculation of the complete equation of state of real potassium: ionic cohesion and interaction corrections have been excluded as instructed. The supplied pressure is the zero-temperature form, appropriate to leading order for $T\ll T_F$.
