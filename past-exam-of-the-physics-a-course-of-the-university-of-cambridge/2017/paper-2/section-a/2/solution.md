<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For an object near the front focal plane, the objective's [numerical aperture](../../../../../numerical-aperture.md) in air is

$$
\mathrm{NA}=\sin\theta\simeq\frac{D}{2f}=\frac{0.005}{2(0.020)}=0.125.
$$

The [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md) for a circular objective gives object-space [diffraction-limited resolution](../../../../../diffraction-limited-resolution.md)

$$
\boxed{\Delta x\simeq\frac{0.61\lambda}{\mathrm{NA}}=\frac{1.22\lambda f}{D}=2.93\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm m\simeq2.9\,\mathrm{\mu m}}.
$$

The factor $1.22$ comes from the first zero of the [Airy disk](../../../../../airy-disk.md). Using the exact $\sin(\arctan(D/(2f)))$ changes the result by less than $1\%$. A coarser $\lambda f/D$ estimate gives $2.4\,\mathrm{\mu m}$; the chosen criterion must be stated. This estimates the [aperture](../../../../../aperture.md) limit, before aberrations and detector sampling.
