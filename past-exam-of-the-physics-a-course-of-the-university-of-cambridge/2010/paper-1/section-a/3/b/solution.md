↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At1000nm the first-zero radius is $5.083\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{rad}$, slightly larger than the $5.00\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{rad}$ separation. The images therefore overlap strongly and **just fail the conventional [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md)**, rather than forming two clean isolated peaks.

The summed Airy profile can still have a shallow central dip and two local maxima: Rayleigh's criterion is a resolution convention, not the exact condition for a profile to become single-peaked. The two-panel original plot in part(a) shows the correct intensity sums at both wavelengths.
