<h1 id="section-a/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The angular separation is $\Delta\theta=10^{11}/(2\times10^{17})=5.00\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{rad}$. Each star's angular diameter is below $5\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{rad}$, so treating it as a point source is justified at these diffraction scales.

At300nm the [Airy disk](../../../../../../airy-disk.md) first-zero radius is $1.22\lambda/D=1.525\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{rad}$. The separation is3.28 times this radius: **two well-separated bright peaks** occur. The stars are mutually incoherent, so their [point spread functions](../../../../../../point-spread-function-split.md) add as intensities, not amplitudes:

$$
I(\theta)=I_\star\left[A(\theta-\Delta\theta/2)+A(\theta+\Delta\theta/2)\right],\quad
A(u)=\left[\frac{2J_1(\pi Du/\lambda)}{\pi Du/\lambda}\right]^2.
$$

The value at u=0 is defined by continuity. Faint diffraction rings remain on either side of each principal peak. [§4.5, circular-aperture diffraction and Rayleigh angular scale](../../../../../../university-physics-volume-3-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others.md)
