<h1 id="section-c/c10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [Compton scattering](../../../../../../compton-scattering.md), a [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) transfers [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) to an initially stationary, approximately free [electron](../../../../../../electron.md). With incident and scattered [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda,\lambda'$ and [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) deflection angle $\theta$, [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) conservation gives

$$
p_e^2=h^2\left(\lambda^{-2}+\lambda'^{-2}-\frac{2\cos\theta}{\lambda\lambda'}\right).
$$

Combine this with $hc/\lambda+mc^2=hc/\lambda'+\sqrt{m^2c^4+p_e^2c^2}$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\lambda'-\lambda=\frac{h}{mc}(1-\cos\theta)}.
$$

The [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) loses [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) to [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) [recoil](../../../../../../recoil.md); the shift is independent of incident [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) in this free-electron model and is largest at backscattering. At [energies](../../../../../../energy-split.md) comparable with atomic binding, initial [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) motion, binding and coherent scattering from the atom require additional treatment. Classical elastic scattering from a fixed charge alone does not produce this [recoil](../../../../../../recoil.md) [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) shift.

In [Arthur Holly Compton](../../../../../../arthur-holly-compton.md)'s spectral test, molybdenum-target X-rays scattered from [graphite](../../../../../../graphite.md) at $90^\circ$ were compared with the primary spectrum using the same slits, crystal and tube [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md). A [calcite](../../../../../../calcite.md) crystal resolved the shifted line by [Bragg's law](../../../../../../bragg-s-law.md); lines on both sides established angular zero. The crystal glancing angle on the plotted axis differs from the [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) scattering angle. The compared curves had different intensity scales, and the paper did not directly observe the [recoil](../../../../../../recoil.md) [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) in this [X-ray](../../../../../../x-ray.md) test. These controls support the [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) comparison without converting it into a direct recoil-track measurement.[pp. 494–496, Experimental Test, Fig. 4 and opening of Velocity of recoil of the scattering electrons](../../../../../../a-quantum-theory-of-the-scattering-of-x-rays-by-light-elements-by-arthur-holly-compton.md)
