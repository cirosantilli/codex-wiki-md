<h1 id="section-c/c10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For independent angular momenta $\mathbf J_1,\mathbf J_2$, set $\mathbf J=\mathbf J_1+\mathbf J_2$. Since different subsystems commute, $\mathbf J$ satisfies the same angular-momentum algebra. The product basis $|j_1m_1\rangle|j_2m_2\rangle$ diagonalizes $J_1^2,J_2^2,J_{1z},J_{2z}$; the coupled basis diagonalizes $J_1^2,J_2^2,J^2,J_z$. Its labels obey

$$
\boxed{j=|j_1-j_2|,|j_1-j_2|+1,\ldots,j_1+j_2,\qquad m=m_1+m_2}.
$$

The [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](../../../../../../clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) are the unitary change of basis between them. Start from the maximum-weight state $j=j_1+j_2,m=j$, apply $J_-=J_{1-}+J_{2-}$ with [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md), and construct the remaining multiplets by [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md).

For two spin-one-half systems, the symmetric [triplet state](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) is $|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle$, $(|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle+|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle)/\sqrt2$, $|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle$, with $j=1$; the antisymmetric [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md) $(|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle-|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle)/\sqrt2$ has $j=0$. The dimension identity $\sum_j(2j+1)=(2j_1+1)(2j_2+1)$ checks completeness. Coupling orbital and intrinsic [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) similarly gives total atomic [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and organizes spin-orbit [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) splittings. Exchange symmetry is an additional restriction when the constituents are identical.
