<h1 id="section-c/c10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a time-independent [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), a [symmetry operator](../../../../../../symmetry-operator.md) $U$ satisfies $UHU^\dagger=H$, or $[U,H]=0$. Thus applying $U$ to an [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) preserves its [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md). Commuting Hermitian [observables](../../../../../../observable.md) can be diagonalized within each degenerate [energy eigenspace](../../../../../../energy-eigenspace.md), allowing states to be labelled by symmetry quantum numbers.

A continuous unitary symmetry $U(\varepsilon)=e^{-i\varepsilon Q/\hbar}$ has Hermitian generator $Q$. Differentiating the invariance condition gives $[H,Q]=0$. The [Heisenberg equation of motion](../../../../../../heisenberg-equation-of-motion.md) then yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\langle Q\rangle}{dt}=\frac{i}{\hbar}\langle[H,Q]\rangle=0}
$$

when $Q$ has no explicit time dependence. [spatial translations](../../../../../../spatial-translation.md) connect to [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) conservation, [rotations](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) to angular-momentum conservation, and time-translation invariance to [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation. A central [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) conserves all components of [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md), but they cannot all be assigned sharp simultaneous values because they do not commute; $H,L^2,L_z$ are a compatible choice. Discrete reflection symmetry gives [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) labels without a continuously variable generator. A [quantum degeneracy](../../../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md) can be protected by a symmetry, but a [quantum degeneracy](../../../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md) alone does not identify its physical origin.
