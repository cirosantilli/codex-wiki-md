<h1 id="section-b/b6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B6](../b6.md)

The [parity operator](../../../../../parity-operator.md) acts as $P\psi(x)=\psi(-x)$, with $P^2=I$. For an even [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) $[H,P]=0$, so [energy eigenstates](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) can be chosen even or odd, with [parity](../../../../../parity.md) [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $+1$ and $-1$. One-dimensional [bound states](../../../../../bound-state.md) are nondegenerate; the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) is [nodeless](../../../../../nodeless-eigenfunction.md) and even.

For the [finite square well](../../../../../finite-square-well.md), square integrability excludes the growing exterior exponential, while even [parity](../../../../../parity.md) excludes the interior sine. Thus the interior solution is proportional to $\cos kx$, with matching decaying tails and

$$
\boxed{k=\frac{\sqrt{2m(V_0+E_0)}}{\hbar},\qquad
q=\frac{\sqrt{-2mE_0}}{\hbar}}.
$$

Continuity at $a$ gives $B\cos ka=Ae^{-qa}$ and $-Bk\sin ka=-qAe^{-qa}$. Their ratio yields

$$
q=k\tan ka,\qquad
\boxed{\sqrt{-E_0}=\sqrt{V_0+E_0}\tan\left(\frac{a\sqrt{2m(V_0+E_0)}}{\hbar}\right)}.
$$

The conditions at $-a$ follow from the same [parity](../../../../../parity.md).

For a narrow weak well, $ka\ll1$, hence $q\simeq k^2a\simeq2mV_0a/\hbar^2$ and

$$
\boxed{E_0\simeq-\frac{2mV_0^2a^2}{\hbar^2}}.
$$

This is self-consistent because $|E_0|/V_0\simeq2mV_0a^2/\hbar^2\ll1$. It is the [shallow finite-square-well energy](../../../../../shallow-finite-square-well-energy.md), equivalently the attractive [delta potential](../../../../../delta-potential.md) limit with integrated strength $2aV_0$. An arbitrarily weak attractive well of this form still has its even bound [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md).

The first excited state must be odd. Replace the interior cosine by $\sin kx$ and choose exterior tails with opposite signs. The matching condition becomes $q=-k\cot ka$. Its first root requires $\pi/2<ka<\pi$. At the binding threshold $q\to0$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{a\sqrt{2mV_0}}{\hbar}>\frac{\pi}{2}}
$$

is necessary and sufficient for the first odd [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md). Equality gives a zero-energy nonnormalizable threshold solution, not an additional [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md).
