<h1 id="section-b/b9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The lowest [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Psi_{\rm s}=\frac1{2\pi}|0,0\rangle,\qquad E_{\rm s}=0,\quad g_{\rm s}=1}.
$$

For the lowest [triplet states](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) take $\lambda=\pm1$ and $m_s=-1,0,1$:

$$
\boxed{\Psi_{{\rm t},\lambda,m_s}(\phi_1,\phi_2)=
\frac{e^{i\lambda\phi_2}-e^{i\lambda\phi_1}}{2\pi\sqrt2}|1,m_s\rangle,\qquad
E_{\rm t}=\epsilon_R,\quad g_{\rm t}=6}.
$$

[orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of $u_0$ and $u_\lambda$ verifies [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) of the antisymmetric spatial factor. At this same [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) there are also two excited [singlet states](../../../../../../singlet-state.md), obtained by replacing its minus sign with a plus sign and the [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) factor by $|0,0\rangle$. Together they reproduce the eight states counted in part a.
