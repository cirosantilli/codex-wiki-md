<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Assuming independent Gaussian [measurement errors](../../../../../measurement-error.md) with the quoted [standard deviations](../../../../../standard-deviation.md), use the [inverse-variance weighted mean](../../../../../inverse-variance-weighted-mean.md):

$$
w_i=\sigma_i^{-2},\qquad
\bar x_w=\frac{\sum_iw_ix_i}{\sum_iw_i},\qquad
\sigma_{\bar x_w}=(\sum_iw_i)^{-1/2}.
$$

Here $\sum_iw_i=0.654514$ and $\sum_iw_ix_i=65.805556$, giving

$$
\boxed{\bar x_w=100.54\pm1.24}.
$$

The consistency statistic is $\chi^2=4.45$ for four [degrees of freedom](../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md), so there is no evident excess scatter requiring error rescaling. A shared [systematic error](../../../../../systematic-error.md) would require a [covariance matrix](../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) and would not be reduced by this averaging rule.
