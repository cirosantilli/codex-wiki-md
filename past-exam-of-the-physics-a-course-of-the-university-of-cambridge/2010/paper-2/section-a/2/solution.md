<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For normalized amplitudes $c_n,c_{n'}$ and a time-independent [observable](../../../../../observable.md) $A$, the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) gives

$$
\langle A\rangle(t)=|c_n|^2A_{nn}+|c_{n'}|^2A_{n'n'}
+2\operatorname{Re}\left[c_n^*c_{n'}A_{nn'}e^{-i(n'-n)\omega t}\right].
$$

The interference term therefore has

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{2\pi}{|n-n'|\omega}}\qquad(n\ne n').
$$

This is the [period of two-state quantum interference](../../../../../period-of-two-state-quantum-interference.md). If the off-diagonal [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) or either amplitude vanishes, the [expectation value](../../../../../expectation-value.md) is constant, so this is not its unique fundamental period. Equal [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) likewise give no beat.
