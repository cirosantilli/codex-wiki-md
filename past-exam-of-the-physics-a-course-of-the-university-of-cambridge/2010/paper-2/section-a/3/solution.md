<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [Sturm oscillation theorem](../../../../../sturm-oscillation-theorem.md) requires respectively **zero, one and two [nodes of a wavefunction](../../../../../node-of-a-wavefunction.md)**. Treating the vertical outer boundaries as hard walls, all three [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) vanish there. The [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) is asymmetric, so no even/odd [parity](../../../../../parity.md) should be imposed. At internal finite steps, both the [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) and its first [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) are continuous.

For $E_0$ and $E_1$, only the left well is classically allowed. Their [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) oscillate there and continue as evanescent combinations through the barrier and right region, reaching zero at the far wall. The first is [nodeless](../../../../../nodeless-eigenfunction.md); the second has one node in the left well. For $E_2$ both wells are allowed, while the central barrier remains forbidden: the [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) oscillates in both wells, with an evanescent bridge and two [nodes of a wavefunction](../../../../../node-of-a-wavefunction.md) overall. A node may lie inside a finite forbidden barrier when the matching coefficients have opposite signs. Precise node positions and relative amplitudes require dimensions and heights not specified by the qualitative drawing.
