<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Compare a directly transmitted ray emerging at $A$ with a ray that makes one extra round trip inside the [Fabry–Pérot interferometer](../../../../../fabry-perot-interferometer.md) and emerges at $C$. Both emerge parallel at angle $\theta$ to the plate normal. The extra internal distance is $2d/\cos\theta$, and their emergence points differ laterally by $AC=2d\tan\theta$. The first ray has already travelled $AC\sin\theta$ towards a common outgoing [wavefront](../../../../../wavefront.md) when the second reaches $C$. The [air-gap optical path difference in a Fabry–Pérot interferometer](../../../../../air-gap-optical-path-difference-in-a-fabry-perot-interferometer.md) follows because [optical path length](../../../../../optical-path-length.md) equals geometric path length in the air-gap approximation:

$$
\Delta=\frac{2d}{\cos\theta}-2d\tan\theta\sin\theta
=\frac{2d(1-\sin^2\theta)}{\cos\theta}
=\boxed{2d\cos\theta}.
$$

The subtraction is essential: comparing distances only inside the plates would compare different outgoing [wavefronts](../../../../../wavefront.md). The corresponding propagation [phase difference](../../../../../phase-difference.md) is $2\pi\Delta/\lambda$. Coating reflection phases can add a separate phase offset; they do not change this geometric [optical path difference](../../../../../optical-path-difference.md).
