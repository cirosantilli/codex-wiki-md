<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Count two [electron spin](../../../../../electron-spin.md) states in each reciprocal-space cell of volume $(2\pi)^3/V$. Filling a [Fermi sphere](../../../../../fermi-sphere.md) at zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) gives

$$
n=\frac{2}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{4\pi k_F^3}{3},\qquad
k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}=1.360\times10^{10}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
$$

The [free electron model](../../../../../free-electron-model.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{E_F=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e}(3\pi^2n)^{2/3}
=1.129\times10^{-18}\,\mathrm J\simeq7.05\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

The [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) zero is the bottom of the model's [conduction band](../../../../../conduction-band.md). The supplied [carrier density](../../../../../carrier-density.md) already incorporates the one-electron-per-atom assumption; it must not be multiplied by another atomic count.
