<h1 id="section-a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At distance $L$, the [small-angle approximation](../../../../../../small-angle-approximation.md) gives $\ell=L\theta$. Thus **the transverse resolution is approximately $2.5\times10^{10}\,\mathrm m$**, or $3.0\times10^{10}\,\mathrm m$ with the circular-aperture [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md). This is a transverse separation at the source, rather than a determination of its distance along the line of sight.
