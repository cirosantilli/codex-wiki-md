<h1 id="section-a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) relation gives $\lambda=hc/E=2.4797\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm m$. The diffraction-limited angular scale of an [aperture](../../../../../../aperture.md) of diameter $D$ is therefore

$$
\boxed{\theta\sim\lambda/D=2.48\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{rad}.}
$$

For a uniformly illuminated circular [aperture](../../../../../../aperture.md), the [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md) instead gives $1.22\lambda/D=3.03\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{rad}$. Both express the same requested estimate. This is an ideal [diffraction limit](../../../../../../diffraction-limit.md), conditional on the effective diameter behaving as a coherent imaging [aperture](../../../../../../aperture.md); [photon energy](../../../../../../photon-energy.md) and diameter alone do not specify the performance of a practical gamma-ray telescope.
