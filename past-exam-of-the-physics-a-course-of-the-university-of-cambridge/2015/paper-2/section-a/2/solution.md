<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Let $T$ be the common [tension](../../../../../tension.md) and $\mu_j$ the [linear mass densities](../../../../../linear-mass-density.md). The [characteristic impedance of a stretched string](../../../../../characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md) is $Z_j=T/c_j=\sqrt{T\mu_j}$. Continuity of [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) and transverse [force](../../../../../force-split.md) at a massless junction gives $1+r=t$ and $Z_1(1-r)=Z_2t$. Consequently

$$
r=\frac{Z_1-Z_2}{Z_1+Z_2}=\frac{1-\sqrt3}{1+\sqrt3},\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal R=|r|^2=(2-\sqrt3)^2=0.07180.}
$$

**The reflected [power](../../../../../power.md) is $7.18\%$.** The minus sign of $r$ represents a phase reversal, not negative [power](../../../../../power.md). Both incident and reflected [waves](../../../../../wave-split.md) travel in the first string, so their [power](../../../../../power.md) ratio is simply the squared displacement-amplitude ratio. As a check, $\mathcal T=(Z_2/Z_1)|t|^2=0.92820$ and $\mathcal R+\mathcal T=1$.
