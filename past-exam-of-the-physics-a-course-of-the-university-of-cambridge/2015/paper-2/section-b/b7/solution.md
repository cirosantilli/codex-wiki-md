<h1 id="section-b/b7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B7](../b7.md)

For a [monochromatic wave](../../../../../monochromatic-wave.md), a constant phase $kx-\omega t$ moves at [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) $v_p=\omega/k$. For a narrow [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) around $k$, expansion $\omega(k+\Delta k)\simeq\omega(k)+(d\omega/dk)\Delta k$ shows that its envelope moves at [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) $v_g=d\omega/dk$. The envelope interpretation requires a sufficiently narrow spectrum and weak distortion; it is not a general identification with a causal signal front. Since $\omega=kv_p$ and $\lambda=2\pi/k$,

$$
v_g=v_p+k\frac{dv_p}{dk}
=\boxed{v_p-\lambda\frac{dv_p}{d\lambda}}.
$$

For the [gravity-capillary wave](../../../../../gravity-capillary-wave.md), put $s=\sigma/\rho$ and take $k>0$. Direct differentiation gives

$$
v_p=\sqrt{g/k+sk},\qquad
v_g=\frac{g+3sk^2}{2\sqrt{gk+sk^3}},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{v_g}{v_p}=\frac{g+3sk^2}{2(g+sk^2)}
=1-\frac{g/k^2-s}{2(g/k^2+s)}.}
$$

Thus $v_g=v_p$ when $sk_*^2=g$, or

$$
\boxed{\lambda^*=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\sigma}{\rho g}}=2.89\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm m.}
$$

At this [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) the gravity and surface-tension restoring terms contribute equally. Also $d(v_p^2)/dk=-g/k^2+s=0$ with positive [second derivative](../../../../../second-derivative.md): this is the [minimum phase speed of a gravity-capillary wave](../../../../../minimum-phase-speed-of-a-gravity-capillary-wave.md), $v_{p,\min}=(4g\sigma/\rho)^{1/4}=0.214\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Locally the carrier crests and narrow envelope travel together. **The medium has not become nondispersive**: $d^2\omega/dk^2=k_*d^2v_p/dk^2\ne0$ there, so a finite [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) can still spread. In the gravity-dominated limit $v_g/v_p\to1/2$; in the capillary-dominated limit it tends to $3/2$.
