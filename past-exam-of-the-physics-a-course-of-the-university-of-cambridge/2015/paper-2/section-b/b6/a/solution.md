<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $b^2>4mk$, define $\beta=\sqrt{\gamma^2-\omega_0^2}$. The two [characteristic roots](../../../../../../characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) are real and negative:

$$
\boxed{x(t)=C_+e^{(-\gamma+\beta)t}+C_-e^{(-\gamma-\beta)t}.}
$$

This is [overdamping](../../../../../../overdamping.md): there is no repeated oscillation, and the slow root controls late-time return. A generic initial [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) can still cause a single crossing of [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), so “nonoscillatory” need not mean monotone for all initial data. For large $b$, the slow root approaches $-k/b$.
