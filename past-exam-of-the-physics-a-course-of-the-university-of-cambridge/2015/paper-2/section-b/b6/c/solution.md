<h1 id="section-b/b6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $b^2<4mk$, put $\omega_d=\sqrt{\omega_0^2-\gamma^2}$. Complex-conjugate roots give the real solution

$$
\boxed{x(t)=e^{-\gamma t}[C\cos(\omega_dt)+D\sin(\omega_dt)].}
$$

This is [underdamping](../../../../../../underdamping.md): oscillation at $\omega_d$ persists within a decaying exponential envelope. [damping](../../../../../../damping.md) reduces the oscillation [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) below $\omega_0$. Constants in all three cases are determined by two [initial conditions](../../../../../../initial-condition.md).
