↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $b^2=4mk$, the repeated [characteristic root](../../../../../../characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) requires a second linearly independent solution $te^{-\gamma t}$:

$$
\boxed{x(t)=(C_1+C_2t)e^{-\gamma t}.}
$$

This is [critical damping](../../../../../../critical-damping.md). If $x(0)=x_0$ and $\dot x(0)=0$, then $x=x_0(1+\gamma t)e^{-\gamma t}$, demonstrating the monotone return used in the suspension discussion.
