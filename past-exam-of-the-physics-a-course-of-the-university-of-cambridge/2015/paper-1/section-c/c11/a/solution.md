<h1 id="section-c/c11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With two coherent paths, the [probability amplitude](../../../../../../probability-amplitude.md) at a screen is $\psi_1(x)+e^{i\phi}\psi_2(x)$. The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
p(x)=|\psi_1|^2+|\psi_2|^2+
2\operatorname{Re}\left(e^{i\phi}\psi_1^*\psi_2\right).
$$

The final term shifts when the relative phase changes, producing [interference fringes](../../../../../../interference-fringe.md). For slit separation $d$ in the far field, the phase difference is approximately $2\pi d\sin\theta/\lambda$; constructive [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) occurs at $d\sin\theta=n\lambda$. Finite slit widths add a [diffraction](../../../../../../diffraction.md) envelope, so an ideal equally bright, infinitely narrow slit model does not predict the full observed envelope.

[single-electron interference pattern buildup](../../../../../../single-electron-interference-pattern-buildup.md) starts with isolated localized detector events at sufficiently low source intensity. Repeated events accumulate the [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) distribution. Thus detection is discrete while propagation is described by coherent [probability amplitudes](../../../../../../probability-amplitude.md). The effect cannot be explained by [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) between different particles if the apparatus is occupied by at most one particle at a time. A motivating realization used a field-emission [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) source, an [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) biprism to recombine two paths, [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) lenses and a position-sensitive [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) counter; it recorded individual arrivals building up the fringes. This was a biprism realization of two-path [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md), not a plate with two literal slits.[section III, American Journal of Physics 57, pp. 117–120](../../../../../../demonstration-of-single-electron-buildup-of-an-interference-pattern-by-akira-tonomura-and-others.md)

The crucial comparison is between both coherent paths open and a preparation that supplies which-path information. If the paths become correlated with detector states $|D_1\rangle,|D_2\rangle$, the joint state is $\psi_1|D_1\rangle+e^{i\phi}\psi_2|D_2\rangle$. Ignoring that detector multiplies the [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) term by $\langle D_1|D_2\rangle$. Orthogonal detector states remove the term completely, while identical detector states preserve it. This is a consequence of [entanglement](../../../../../../entangled-state.md) and the [partial trace](../../../../../../partial-trace.md); a human need not read the detector.

In a real test, check the single-path distributions, detector background, source coherence and stability of the relative phase. Classical phase drift can wash out fringes without supplying which-path information. Conversely, localized detections alone do not establish coherent two-path propagation; the controlled phase dependence of their accumulated distribution provides the discriminating evidence.
