<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), put $\ell_0=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$ and $\xi=x/\ell_0$. The normalized [energy eigenstates](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) are

$$
\boxed{E_n=\hbar\omega(n+\tfrac12)},\qquad
\psi_n(x)=\frac{H_n(\xi)e^{-\xi^2/2}}{\pi^{1/4}\sqrt{2^nn!\ell_0}},\qquad n=0,1,\ldots,
$$

where $H_n$ is a [Hermite polynomial](../../../../../hermite-polynomial.md). Successive [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) are equally spaced; the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) retains [zero-point energy](../../../../../zero-point-energy.md) $\hbar\omega/2$. The $n$th [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) has $n$ [nodes of a wavefunction](../../../../../node-of-a-wavefunction.md) and [parity](../../../../../parity.md) $(-1)^n$. In particular, the first three shapes are proportional to $e^{-\xi^2/2}$, $\xi e^{-\xi^2/2}$, and $(2\xi^2-1)e^{-\xi^2/2}$. The [classical turning points](../../../../../classical-turning-point.md) satisfy $x=\pm\ell_0\sqrt{2n+1}$; the [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) extend beyond them and decay, rather than ending abruptly.
