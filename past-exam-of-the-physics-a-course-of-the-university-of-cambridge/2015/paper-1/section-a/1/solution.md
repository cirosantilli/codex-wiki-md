<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) uses the squared modulus, so the imaginary term contributes positively: $|a+ix|^2=a^2+x^2$. For $a>0$, [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) would multiply the supplied expression by $N=\sqrt{3/(4a^3)}$. Equivalently, take the ratio of the integrals directly:

$$
P=\frac{\int_{a/2}^a(a^2+x^2)\,dx}{\int_0^a(a^2+x^2)\,dx}
=\frac{19a^3/24}{4a^3/3}
=\boxed{\frac{19}{32}}.
$$

Using $\psi^2$ instead of $|\psi|^2$ would fail to produce a real, nonnegative [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md).
