<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

A [Fresnel zone plate](../../../../../zone-plate.md) replaces a conventional refracting surface by concentric alternating transmitting and absorbing annuli. Successive annuli span half a [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) of optical-path excess to the chosen focus; blocking alternate zones prevents their approximately opposite [wave phases](../../../../../phase-waves.md) from cancelling. For a normally incident [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md),

$$
\sqrt{f^2+r_j^2}-f=\frac{j\lambda}{2},\qquad
r_j^2=j\lambda f+\frac{j^2\lambda^2}{4}
\simeq j\lambda f.
$$

The paraxial radii grow as $\sqrt j$, so the zones become narrower outwards.

Using the [photon energy](../../../../../photon-energy.md) relation $\lambda=hc/E$ gives **$\lambda=4.133\times10^{-11}\ {\rm m}=0.04133\ {\rm nm}$**. For $f=10^{-2}\ {\rm m}$,

$$
\boxed{r_j\simeq0.6429\sqrt j\ \mu{\rm m}.}
$$

The first three boundaries are approximately $0.6429$, $0.9092$ and $1.1135\ \mu{\rm m}$. This fixes the zone pattern but **does not fix a unique outside diameter**: the number of zones is a design choice. For example, $N=100$ gives radius $6.429\ \mu{\rm m}$, diameter $12.86\ \mu{\rm m}$ and outermost zone width about $32.2\ {\rm nm}$. A larger usable diameter requires finer outer zones.

One could pattern absorbing [metal](../../../../../metal.md) annuli on a thin X-ray-transparent support, choosing absorber thickness from its attenuation at the selected [photon energy](../../../../../photon-energy.md); that thickness cannot be determined from [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [focal length](../../../../../focal-length.md) alone. The design is chromatic: for fixed radii $f\propto1/\lambda$, so a narrow [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) band is needed for a sharp common focus.
