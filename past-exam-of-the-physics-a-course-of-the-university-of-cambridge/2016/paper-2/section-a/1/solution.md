<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a lossless [antireflection coating](../../../../../antireflection-coating.md), cancellation requires both equal reflected [wave amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) and an odd half-cycle difference in their round-trip [wave phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md). At [normal incidence](../../../../../normal-incidence.md) the interface amplitude coefficients are $r_{01}=(n_0-n)/(n_0+n)$ and $r_{12}=(n-n_s)/(n+n_s)$. Choosing $n_0<n<n_s$ makes both negative: both [reflections](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) acquire the same $\pi$ phase reversal. A quarter-wave optical thickness supplies their remaining relative phase $\pi$.

Equal amplitudes require $r_{01}=r_{12}$, which reduces to $n^2=n_0n_s$. Thus

$$
\boxed{n=\sqrt{1.4(1.6)}=1.49666,\qquad
d_{\min}=\frac{500\ {\rm nm}}{4n}=83.52\ {\rm nm}.}
$$

The exact multilayer [reflection](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) amplitude is zero for these choices, not merely the sum of the first two reflected rays: its numerator is $r_{01}+r_{12}e^{2i\beta}=0$, with $\beta=2\pi nd/\lambda_0=\pi/2$. Any positive odd multiple of this thickness has the same cancellation at the design [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md); the smallest is the thin-layer choice. [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md), index dispersion, angular spread and deviations from the selected [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) limit the practical cancellation.
