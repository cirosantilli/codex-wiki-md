<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Use periodic [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) temporarily to count the allowed [wavevectors](../../../../../wavevector.md). Each state occupies $(2\pi)^3/V$ in reciprocal space. For one acoustic polarization, the spherical shell between $k$ and $k+dk$ contains

$$
dN=\frac{V}{(2\pi)^3}4\pi k^2\,dk.
$$

Both positive and negative [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md) directions are already included in that sphere; no further factor of two is needed. With the linear [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) $k=\omega/v_s$,

$$
g_1(\omega)=\frac{dN}{d\omega}
=\frac{V\omega^2}{2\pi^2v_s^3}.
$$

A three-dimensional monatomic [crystal](../../../../../crystal.md) has three acoustic branches. Under the stated common-speed approximation,

$$
\boxed{g(\omega)=\frac{3V\omega^2}{2\pi^2v_s^3}.}
$$

This is a [phonon density of states](../../../../../phonon-density-of-states.md) per unit [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md), not per unit ordinary [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md). If longitudinal and transverse speeds differ, the isotropic expression becomes $g=V\omega^2(v_L^{-3}+2v_T^{-3})/(2\pi^2)$. In the [Debye model](../../../../../debye-model.md), imposing $\int_0^{\omega_D}g(\omega)\,d\omega=3N$ sets the cutoff; the linear approximation is not continued to arbitrarily high [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) in a finite [lattice](../../../../../lattice.md).
