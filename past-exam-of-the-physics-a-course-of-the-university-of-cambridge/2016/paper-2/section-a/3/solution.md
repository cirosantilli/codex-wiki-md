<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Let $x$ be [mirror](../../../../../mirror.md) [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) from equal optical paths in a [Michelson interferometer](../../../../../michelson-interferometer.md), so the [optical path difference](../../../../../optical-path-difference.md) is $2x$. Different spectral components add detected [light intensities](../../../../../light-intensity.md); each component interferes with its own delayed copy. For equal line strengths and ideally balanced arms, write

$$
I(x)=I_0[1+\cos(4\pi x/\lambda_1)]
+I_0[1+\cos(4\pi x/\lambda_2)].
$$

The [trigonometric identity](../../../../../trigonometric-identity.md) for the sum of cosines gives

$$
I(x)=2I_0\left[1+
\cos\!\left(2\pi x\left(\frac1{\lambda_1}-\frac1{\lambda_2}\right)\right)
\cos\!\left(2\pi x\left(\frac1{\lambda_1}+\frac1{\lambda_2}\right)\right)
\right].
$$

Thus the sketch consists of rapid fringes within a slowly varying contrast envelope. With $\bar\lambda$ the mean [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) and $|\Delta\lambda|\ll\bar\lambda$, the mirror-displacement fringe spacing is **$\bar\lambda/2$**. The [interference visibility](../../../../../interferometric-visibility.md) is

$$
\mathcal V(x)=\left|\cos(2\pi x\Delta s)\right|,
\qquad \Delta s=\left|\lambda_1^{-1}-\lambda_2^{-1}\right|.
$$

The first contrast zero is at $x=1/(4\Delta s)$; successive visibility maxima are separated by

$$
\boxed{\Delta x_{\rm revival}=\frac1{2\Delta s}
\simeq\frac{\bar\lambda^2}{2|\Delta\lambda|}.}
$$

Between successive contrast zeros the signed envelope changes sign, shifting the rapid fringes by half a cycle. Unequal line strengths leave a nonzero minimum contrast. Finite linewidth adds a longer-range coherence envelope, while arm imbalance and polarization mismatch can reduce contrast without indicating a different spectral spacing.
