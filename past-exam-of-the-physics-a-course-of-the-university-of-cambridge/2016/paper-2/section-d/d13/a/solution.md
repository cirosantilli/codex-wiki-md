<h1 id="section-d/d13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [free electron model](../../../../../../free-electron-model.md) replaces the detailed periodic potential by a constant background, treating conduction [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) as mobile particles with approximately parabolic dispersion $E=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$. It is useful to distinguish the classical [Drude model](../../../../../../drude-model.md) from the quantum [free electron gas](../../../../../../free-electron-model.md). They share a simple transport picture, but differ fundamentally in occupation statistics.

With a momentum-relaxation time $\tau$, both lead in a simple isotropic one-band approximation to

$$
\boxed{\sigma=\frac{nq^2\tau}{m},\qquad R_H=-\frac1{nq}.}
$$

This explains approximately Ohmic longitudinal conduction and a carrier-density scale from the [Hall effect](../../../../../../hall-effect.md). The relaxation time is phenomenological: introducing it does not itself predict scattering from defects or [phonons](../../../../../../phonon.md). A four-probe resistance measurement separates the sample's longitudinal response from current-contact voltage drops; field and current reversals isolate the transverse signal.

The classical model incorrectly assigns roughly $3Nk_B/2$ of electronic [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md), far too large in a degenerate [metal](../../../../../../metal.md). [Fermi-Dirac statistics](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md) fixes this: most occupied states cannot change occupation after a small thermal perturbation because nearby low-energy states are already filled. Only a fraction of order $k_BT/E_F$ near the [Fermi energy](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) participates. For $T\ll T_F$,

$$
C_e=\frac{\pi^2}{2}Nk_B\frac{T}{T_F}.
$$

The quantum model also gives a finite [Fermi velocity](../../../../../../fermi-velocity.md) even at zero [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [Pauli paramagnetism](../../../../../../pauli-paramagnetism.md). Under predominantly elastic scattering, its electronic heat and charge conductivities obey the [Wiedemann-Franz law](../../../../../../wiedemann-franz-law.md),

$$
\frac{\kappa_e}{\sigma T}=L_0,\qquad
L_0=\frac{\pi^2k_B^2}{3q^2}.
$$

This is a quantum success; classical equipartition does not give the correct small [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) and [Lorenz number](../../../../../../lorenz-number.md) together.

Its limitations are equally instructive. Without the periodic potential it has no [band gaps](../../../../../../band-gap.md), so it cannot distinguish a filled-band insulator from a [metal](../../../../../../metal.md) or explain [semiconductor doping](../../../../../../semiconductor-doping.md). The simplest single-electron Hall formula cannot explain positive [Hall coefficients](../../../../../../hall-coefficient.md); band curvature and [electron holes](../../../../../../electron-hole.md), or a multiband transport treatment, are needed. It neglects much Fermi-surface anisotropy and detailed optical interband [absorption](../../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md). Noninteracting [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) alone do not explain [ferromagnetism](../../../../../../ferromagnetism.md) or [superconductivity](../../../../../../superconductivity-split.md), and [lattice](../../../../../../lattice.md) thermal behavior needs [phonons](../../../../../../phonon.md). The [nearly-free electron model](../../../../../../nearly-free-electron-model.md), which allows reciprocal-lattice Fourier components of the potential to mix [plane waves](../../../../../../plane-wave.md), provides a controlled first correction near Bragg planes.

**Its main success is a simple quantitative baseline for degenerate conduction [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md); its main failure is treating the [crystal](../../../../../../crystal.md) and interactions as too featureless.** A fit of conductivity alone is not a test of the whole model: compare [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md), Hall sign, optical response and [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence using independently constrained parameters.
