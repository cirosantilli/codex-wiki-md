**[Quantum tunnelling](../../../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) is supported by transport and decay through barriers that classical particles of the same energy cannot traverse.** Three complementary examples are:

- In **[alpha decay](../../../../../../alpha-decay.md)**, an alpha cluster escapes the attractive nuclear region through a Coulomb barrier. The barrier penetration factor depends exponentially on a forbidden-region action, helping explain the strong dependence of lifetimes on emitted alpha energy. This is evidence from systematic decay rates, rather than direct imaging of an alpha inside the barrier.
- A **[scanning tunnelling microscope](../../../../../../scanning-tunneling-microscope.md)** measures current across a narrow [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) gap between a conducting tip and sample. At small bias an approximate dependence is $I\propto e^{-2\kappa s}$ with $\kappa\simeq\sqrt{2m\Phi}/\hbar$. The strong gap-distance sensitivity enables atomic-scale imaging and local spectroscopy. Electronic [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md) and barrier height also affect the current, so constant-current contrast is not an unqualified geometric atom map.[Vacuum-tunnelling measurement and surface imaging](../../../../../../surface-studies-by-scanning-tunneling-microscopy-by-gerd-binnig-and-others.md)
- A heavily doped **[Tunnel diode](../../../../../../tunnel-diode.md)** has a narrow p-n barrier. Bias changes the overlap of occupied and empty states across it; interband tunnelling produces a characteristic peak and [Negative differential resistance](../../../../../../negative-differential-resistance.md) interval in its current-voltage curve. The measured voltage dependence agrees with the changing band overlap, providing a solid-state transport example.[Germanium-junction tunnelling characteristic](../../../../../../new-phenomenon-in-narrow-germanium-junctions-by-leo-esaki.md)

Together these test barrier penetration in nuclear decay, vacuum-gap transport and semiconductor transport. Their detailed currents depend on state supply and matching as well as the barrier transmission probability.
