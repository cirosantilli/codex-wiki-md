<h1 id="section-c/c11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the nonrelativistic Coulomb [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) with reduced electron-proton mass $\mu$, separation in [Spherical coordinate system](../../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) gives **$\psi_{n\ell m}(r,\theta,\phi)=R_{n\ell}(r)Y_{\ell m}(\theta,\phi)$**. The [quantum numbers](../../../../../../quantum-number.md) obey $n\geq1$, $0\leq\ell\leq n-1$ and $-\ell\leq m\leq\ell$. These are joint [eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) of energy, $L^2$ and $L_z$, with the latter two [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\ell(\ell+1)\hbar^2$ and $m\hbar$.

The Coulomb bound-state energies and characteristic length are

$$
\boxed{E_n=-\frac{\mu e^4}{2(4\pi\epsilon_0)^2\hbar^2n^2},\qquad a_\mu=\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar^2}{\mu e^2}.}
$$

For example, $\psi_{100}=(\pi a_\mu^3)^{-1/2}e^{-r/a_\mu}$ is spherically symmetric and has no radial node. In general the radial part contains an exponential times a [Generalized Laguerre polynomial](../../../../../../generalized-laguerre-polynomial.md), with $n-\ell-1$ radial nodes. The [spherical harmonic](../../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md) specifies angular structure and azimuthal phase; real linear combinations of degenerate $m$ states give familiar directional orbitals.

The leading Coulomb energy depends on $n$ alone, giving $n^2$ spatial states for each principal shell, or $2n^2$ including electron [spin](../../../../../../spin.md). [Fine structure](../../../../../../fine-structure.md), [Lamb shift](../../../../../../lamb-shift.md) and [Hyperfine structure](../../../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) lift parts of this ideal degeneracy. An [atomic orbital](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) is a probability amplitude, not a classical planetary path.[Section 4: separation of the Coulomb problem and hydrogenic states](../../../../../../quantum-mechanics-by-david-tong.md)
