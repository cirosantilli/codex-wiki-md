<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Nonrelativistic acceleration from rest gives $p=\sqrt{2m_eeV}$ and $\lambda=h/p$. The observed deflection is the angle between incident and diffracted beams, **$2\theta=22^\circ$**, so [Bragg's law](../../../../../bragg-s-law.md) at first order gives

$$
\boxed{d=\frac{h}{2\sin11^\circ\sqrt{2m_ee(150\,\mathrm V)}}\simeq2.62\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm m=0.262\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

Here $\lambda\simeq0.100\,\mathrm{nm}$. The numerical evaluation uses the externally documented CODATA 2014 values $h=6.626070040\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$, $m_e=9.10938356\times10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $e=1.6021766208\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$; it does not assert which rounded constants were in the exam's handbook. Relativistic corrections at this voltage are negligible at the precision of the angle.[Planck constant, elementary charge and electron mass in the historical reference table](../../../../../codata-recommended-values-of-the-fundamental-physical-constants-2014-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)
