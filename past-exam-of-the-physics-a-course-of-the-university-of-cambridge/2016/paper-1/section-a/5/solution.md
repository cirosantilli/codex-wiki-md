<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

For independent charge arrivals described by a [Poisson distribution](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md), the one-sided [Shot noise](../../../../../shot-noise.md) current [Power spectral density](../../../../../power-spectral-density.md) is $S_I=2eI$. Treating the stated [bandwidth](../../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md) as the [Effective noise bandwidth](../../../../../effective-noise-bandwidth.md),

$$
\boxed{i_{\rm rms}=\sqrt{2eI\Delta f}=\sqrt{2(1.6021766208\times10^{-19})(10^{-9})(10^5)}=5.66\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm A.}
$$

The expected rms noise is **$5.66\,\mathrm{pA}$**, about $0.566\%$ of the mean current. This is the full [Shot noise](../../../../../shot-noise.md) value with [Fano factor](../../../../../fano-factor.md) one; correlations can suppress or enhance it. The elementary charge is from the external CODATA 2014 table.[Elementary charge](../../../../../codata-recommended-values-of-the-fundamental-physical-constants-2014-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)
