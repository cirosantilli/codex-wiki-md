<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

If $\hat A|a\rangle=\lambda|a\rangle$, the [Hermitian operator](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) property makes $\lambda$ real. Applying the operator identity to this [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) gives $\lambda^3=1$. Its only real root is **$\lambda=1$**; the other cube roots of unity are nonreal. In an orthonormal [eigenbasis](../../../../../eigenbasis.md), $\hat A=\sum_a|a\rangle\langle a|=\boxed{\hat I}$. Equivalently, the [spectral theorem](../../../../../spectral-theorem.md) places the entire spectrum of the self-adjoint observable at one, giving the same conclusion. The quantum-mechanical setting assumes the usual well-defined observable and operator domains.
