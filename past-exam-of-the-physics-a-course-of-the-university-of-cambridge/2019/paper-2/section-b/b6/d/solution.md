<h1 id="section-b/b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A high-[finesse](../../../../../../finesse.md) [Fabry–Pérot etalon](../../../../../../fabry-perot-etalon.md) obtains very narrow transmission peaks by summing many coherent round trips, allowing high resolving [power](../../../../../../power.md) with a compact optical path. It can transmit a substantial fraction of resonant [light](../../../../../../light.md) when coating absorption is small, and works well for precise relative line separations and narrow-line measurements. A [diffraction grating](../../../../../../diffraction-grating.md) may require many illuminated grooves to achieve the same resolving [power](../../../../../../power.md).

The etalon's [free spectral range](../../../../../../free-spectral-range.md) is limited, and repeated orders cause ambiguity across a broad [optical spectrum](../../../../../../optical-spectrum.md). It commonly needs scanning and an order-selecting [optical filter](../../../../../../optical-filter.md) or another dispersive element. Mirror alignment, flatness and illumination angle strongly affect peak positions and widths, and absorption lowers throughput. A [diffraction grating](../../../../../../diffraction-grating.md) instead separates a broad range of [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md) spatially for simultaneous detection, although grating orders can also overlap. The preferred instrument depends on whether narrow-band precision or broad spectral coverage is the priority.
