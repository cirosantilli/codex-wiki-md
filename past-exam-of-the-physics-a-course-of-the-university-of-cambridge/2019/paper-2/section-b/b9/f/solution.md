<h1 id="section-b/b9/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Resistance to bending adds a restoring contribution that grows more rapidly with spatial curvature than the tension contribution. Short-[wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) modes are therefore stiffened especially strongly, raising their [frequencies](../../../../../../frequency.md) and changing the large-[wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) behaviour.

For example, a thin plate of [flexural rigidity](../../../../../../bending-stiffness.md) $D_b$ has $\sigma z_{tt}=T\nabla^2z-D_b\nabla^4z$. When its edge conditions admit the same sine transverse mode, [bending stiffness in a membrane waveguide](../../../../../../bending-stiffness-in-a-membrane-waveguide.md) gives

$$
\omega_n^2=\frac T\sigma(k^2+q_n^2)+\frac{D_b}\sigma(k^2+q_n^2)^2.
$$

Thus the cutoffs increase, and bending-dominated propagation has $\omega\propto k^2$ at large $|k|$, rather than approaching $c_m|k|$. Its [phase velocity](../../../../../../phase-velocity.md) and [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) then grow with $|k|$. If “rigidly fixed” means a genuinely clamped plate edge, the slope must also vanish and the mode shapes change; the qualitative stiffening and stronger [wave dispersion](../../../../../../wave-dispersion.md) remain, but simple $q_n=n\pi/a$ need not remain exact.
