↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [waveguide](../../../../../../waveguide.md) confines the transverse structure of a wave while permitting propagation along a chosen route. It can deliver [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) to a distant receiver without the same geometric spreading as an unguided beam, protect a signal from unwanted coupling, and select transverse [normal modes](../../../../../../normal-mode.md). It also makes propagation paths and coupling to devices more controllable. Confinement does not eliminate [wave absorption](../../../../../../wave-absorption.md) or scattering loss, and it can introduce [wave dispersion](../../../../../../wave-dispersion.md) and [cutoff frequencies of a membrane waveguide](../../../../../../cutoff-frequency-of-a-membrane-waveguide.md) or corresponding cutoffs for other wave types.

Two examples are an [optical fiber](../../../../../../optical-fiber.md), which guides [light](../../../../../../light.md) by its core-and-cladding [refractive index](../../../../../../refractive-index.md) structure, and a hollow [electromagnetic waveguide](../../../../../../electromagnetic-waveguide.md) used to carry microwaves between a source and an antenna. A duct for [acoustic waves](../../../../../../acoustic-wave.md) is a further example. Each uses different material and [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md), but the common idea is controlled transverse confinement.
