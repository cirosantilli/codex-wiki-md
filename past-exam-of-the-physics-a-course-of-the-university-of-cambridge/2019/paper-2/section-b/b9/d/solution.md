<h1 id="section-b/b9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the $n$th transverse mode, substitute $q_n=n\pi/a$ into the [membrane waveguide](../../../../../../membrane-waveguide.md) [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md):

$$
k^2=\frac{\sigma\omega^2}{T}-\left(\frac{n\pi}{a}\right)^2.
$$

Longitudinal propagation requires real nonzero $k$, hence

$$
\boxed{\omega>\omega_{c,n}=\frac{n\pi}{a}\sqrt{\frac T\sigma}.}
$$

Below the [cutoff frequency of a membrane waveguide](../../../../../../cutoff-frequency-of-a-membrane-waveguide.md), $k=i\kappa$, with $\kappa=\sqrt{(n\pi/a)^2-\sigma\omega^2/T}$, and a bounded excitation decays as $e^{-\kappa x}$ rather than carrying a travelling wave along an infinite guide. At equality $k=0$, the displacement has no longitudinal phase advance and the [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) is zero; it is a cutoff oscillation, not a propagating mode carrying finite longitudinal [power](../../../../../../power.md).
