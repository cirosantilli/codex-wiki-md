<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Treat [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md) as monovalent, with one mobile [electron](../../../../../electron.md) per atom. Its [mass density](../../../../../density.md) is $\rho_m=0.97\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}=970\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, and its molar [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is $M=0.023\,\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}$. Using the [Avogadro constant](../../../../../avogadro-constant.md),

$$
n=\frac{\rho_mN_A}{M}=\frac{970}{0.023}(6.022\times10^{23})\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}
\simeq2.54\times10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.
$$

The [electron mobility](../../../../../electron-mobility.md) $\mu=e\tau/m_e$ relates the magnitude of [drift velocity](../../../../../drift-velocity.md) to [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md). The [Drude model](../../../../../drude-model.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{\sigma=ne\mu=(2.54\times10^{28})(1.602\times10^{-19})(5.2\times10^{-3})
\simeq2.1\times10^7\,\mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}.}
$$

Using $m_e=9.109\times10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$,

$$
\boxed{\tau=\frac{m_e\mu}{e}\simeq2.96\times10^{-14}\,\mathrm s\simeq3.0\times10^{-14}\,\mathrm s.}
$$

The inferred time is the [momentum relaxation time](../../../../../momentum-relaxation-time.md). Identifying it with the mean time between collisions is the intended simple isotropic [Drude model](../../../../../drude-model.md) assumption; small-angle scattering need not relax directed [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) in a single event.
