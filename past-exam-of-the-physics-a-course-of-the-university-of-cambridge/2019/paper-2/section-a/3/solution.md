<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

A [Michelson interferometer](../../../../../michelson-interferometer.md) splits a collimated beam at a partially reflecting [beam splitter](../../../../../beam-splitter.md). Two mutually perpendicular arms reflect from mirrors and recombine at the same [beam splitter](../../../../../beam-splitter.md); the superposed returning beams produce [interference fringes](../../../../../interference-fringe.md) at the detector. Translate one mirror along its arm while holding the other fixed.

<a id="section-a/3/image-original-michelson-geometry-for-measuring-wavelength-by-counting-fringes-while-one-mirror-moves"></a>
![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-physics-a-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2019/paper-2-a3-michelson.png)

**[Figure 1](#section-a/3/image-original-michelson-geometry-for-measuring-wavelength-by-counting-fringes-while-one-mirror-moves). Original Michelson geometry for measuring wavelength by counting fringes while one mirror moves**.

If that mirror moves a measured distance $\Delta x$, the round-trip [optical path difference](../../../../../optical-path-difference.md) changes by $2\Delta x$ in air, approximated as [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md). Every change of one [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) moves one complete bright-dark-bright fringe cycle past a fixed detector point. Count $N$ such cycles to obtain

$$
\Delta\phi=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}(2\Delta x)=2\pi N,\qquad
\boxed{\lambda=\frac{2\Delta x}{N}.}
$$

Use a [spectral line](../../../../../spectral-line.md) selected with a suitable [optical filter](../../../../../optical-filter.md), and count many [interference fringes](../../../../../interference-fringe.md) to reduce the fractional uncertainty of the distance and count. Keep the two arms within the source's [optical coherence length](../../../../../optical-coherence-length.md) and maintain overlap and alignment. If the [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) is required for an arm filled with air of known [refractive index](../../../../../refractive-index.md) $n$, the corrected relation is $\lambda_0=2n\Delta x/N$ at stable $n$.
