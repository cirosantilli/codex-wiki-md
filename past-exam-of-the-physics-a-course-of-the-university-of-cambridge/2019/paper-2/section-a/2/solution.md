<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Assume a massless junction and common [tension](../../../../../tension.md) $T$. The [characteristic impedance of a stretched string](../../../../../characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md) is $Z_j=\sqrt{T\mu_j}$, where $\mu_j$ is its [linear mass density](../../../../../linear-mass-density.md). The displacement [reflection coefficient](../../../../../reflection-coefficient.md) is $r=(Z_1-Z_2)/(Z_1+Z_2)$, so the reflected fraction of [power](../../../../../power.md) is

$$
\mathcal R=|r|^2=\left(\frac{1-z}{1+z}\right)^2,\qquad z=\frac{Z_2}{Z_1}=\sqrt{\frac{\mu_2}{\mu_1}}.
$$

Set $\mathcal R=1/2$. The positive solutions are $z=3\pm2\sqrt2$, which are reciprocals. Thus the [string density ratio for prescribed reflected power](../../../../../string-density-ratio-for-prescribed-reflected-power.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\mu_{\rm heavy}}{\mu_{\rm light}}=(3+2\sqrt2)^2=17+12\sqrt2\simeq34.0.}
$$

The impedance ratio is about $5.83$, rather than the required density ratio. Reversing which string is on the incident side changes the reflected displacement sign but not the reflected [power](../../../../../power.md) fraction.
