<h1 id="section-c/c11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [Bloch state](../../../../../../bloch-state.md) on the loop has $\psi(x)=u(x)e^{i\phi x}$, with $u(x+a)=u(x)$. Its circumference is $L=Na$, so $u(x+Na)=u(x)$. Single-valuedness after one complete circuit requires the [periodic boundary condition](../../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md)

$$
\psi(x+Na)=\psi(x),\qquad e^{i\phi Na}=1.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\phi=\frac{2\pi n}{Na},\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The allowed Bloch [wavevectors](../../../../../../wavevector.md) are spaced by $2\pi/(Na)$. Values of $\phi$ differing by $2\pi/a$ label the same [crystal momentum](../../../../../../crystal-momentum.md) modulo a [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) vector, so there are $N$ inequivalent choices in one [Brillouin zone](../../../../../../brillouin-zone.md). In this notation $\phi$ is the Bloch [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md), not a dimensionless phase; $\phi a$ is dimensionless. Spin gives additional states but does not alter this condition.
