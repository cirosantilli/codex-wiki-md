<h1 id="section-c/c11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The quantum [free electron model](../../../../../../free-electron-model.md) treats conduction [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) as independent particles moving in an approximately constant potential, with parabolic [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) $E=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$. [Fermi-Dirac statistics](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md) successfully explains important features such as a [Fermi energy](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) and a small [electronic heat capacity](../../../../../../electronic-heat-capacity.md), but a constant potential gives no [band gaps](../../../../../../band-gap.md) and cannot by itself explain why different band fillings produce metals, [semiconductors](../../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) and band insulators. It also misses lattice-dependent [effective masses](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md), detailed directional responses and the topology of real [Fermi surfaces](../../../../../../fermi-surface.md). Scattering and finite [electrical resistivity](../../../../../../electrical-resistivity.md) require additional physics.

The [nearly-free electron model](../../../../../../nearly-free-electron-model.md) introduces a weak periodic lattice potential. [Bloch's theorem](../../../../../../bloch-s-theorem-split.md) replaces individual free [plane waves](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) by [Bloch states](../../../../../../bloch-state.md), and Fourier components of the potential mix plane waves whose [wavevectors](../../../../../../wavevector.md) differ by a [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) vector. Near degeneracy at a [Brillouin zone](../../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) boundary, this coupling opens a [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md); for a two-wave crossing its size is $2|V_G|$. Away from the crossing, energies remain close to free-particle values. Band curvature produces [band effective masses](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md), and [band filling](../../../../../../band-filling.md) determines whether the [Fermi energy](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) lies inside an allowed band or a gap.

The improvement is the inclusion of lattice periodicity, rather than a switch from classical to quantum statistics: the quantum [free electron model](../../../../../../free-electron-model.md) already uses [Fermi-Dirac statistics](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md). The strong delta barriers in the later numerical example are treated exactly by the [Kronig-Penney model](../../../../../../kronig-penney-model.md), rather than by assuming that their strength is perturbatively small.
