<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Write $L=10^{-9}\,\mathrm m$ and use the [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) separately in the three Cartesian directions. Since a [position](../../../../../position.md) coordinate is confined to an interval of length $L$, [Popoviciu's inequality on variances](../../../../../popoviciu-s-inequality-on-variances.md) gives $\Delta x_i\leq L/2$. Consequently $\Delta p_i\geq\hbar/L$, and the [expectation value](../../../../../expectation-value.md) of the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) obeys

$$
\langle K\rangle=\sum_{i=1}^3\frac{(\Delta p_i)^2+\langle p_i\rangle^2}{2m_e}\geq\frac{3\hbar^2}{2m_eL^2}=1.83\times10^{-20}\,\mathrm J.
$$

Thus an uncertainty-based lower estimate is $\boxed{K\gtrsim0.11\,\mathrm{eV}}$. This [confinement energy bound from position support](../../../../../confinement-energy-bound-from-position-support.md) is not sharp: the exact [ground-state energy](../../../../../ground-state-energy.md) for a cubical [infinite square well](../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) is $3\pi^2\hbar^2/(2m_eL^2)=1.13\,\mathrm{eV}$. The [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) establishes the confinement scale $\hbar^2/(m_eL^2)$; identifying a box size with an approximate [position](../../../../../position.md) [standard deviation](../../../../../standard-deviation.md) changes its numerical coefficient.
