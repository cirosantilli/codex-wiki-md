<h1 id="section-d/d12/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A real [operational amplifier](../../../../../../operational-amplifier.md) has large but finite, frequency-dependent [open-loop gain](../../../../../../open-loop-gain.md); the gain falls and its phase changes at higher frequencies. Finite [bandwidth](../../../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md) and [slew rate](../../../../../../slew-rate.md) constrain small-signal and large-signal responses respectively. There are also finite [input impedance](../../../../../../input-impedance.md) and [output impedance](../../../../../../output-impedance.md), nonzero [input bias currents](../../../../../../input-bias-current.md) and [input offset voltage](../../../../../../input-offset-voltage.md), and internally generated [noise](../../../../../../noise-signal-processing.md). Offset and bias can drift with [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md).

The output is limited by the supply voltages and output-current capability, and the inputs have a permitted common-mode range. Rejection of common-mode and supply changes is finite. Consequently $v_+-v_-$ need not vanish exactly, and neither the virtual-ground approximation nor linear gain formulas apply after saturation. For the calculation below the stipulated infinite [input impedance](../../../../../../input-impedance.md) removes the input loading currents, while finite gain and optionally finite output resistance are retained.
