<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $S_i=(\hbar/2)\sigma_i$, with $\sigma_i$ the [Pauli matrices](../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md). Direct multiplication gives $\sigma_x^2=\sigma_y^2=\sigma_z^2=I$, and hence

$$
\boxed{S_x^2+S_y^2+S_z^2=\frac34\hbar^2I.}
$$

For the products in the other order,

$$
\sigma_x\sigma_y=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&-i\end{pmatrix}=i\sigma_z,\qquad \sigma_y\sigma_x=\begin{pmatrix}-i&0\\0&i\end{pmatrix}=-i\sigma_z.
$$

Their difference gives the [angular momentum commutation relations](../../../../../../angular-momentum-commutation-relations.md)

$$
\boxed{[S_x,S_y]=\frac{\hbar^2}{4}(2i\sigma_z)=i\hbar S_z.}
$$

The first identity corresponds to the [spin one-half](../../../../../../spin-one-half.md) value $s(s+1)\hbar^2$ with $s=1/2$.
