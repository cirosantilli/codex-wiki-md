<h1 id="section-b/b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [Stern-Gerlach experiment](../../../../../../stern-gerlach-experiment.md) uses an oven and slits to produce a collimated [atomic beam](../../../../../../atomic-beam.md), conventionally of neutral [silver](../../../../../../silver.md) atoms, and sends it through an inhomogeneous [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) onto a position-sensitive detector. The unpaired outer electron supplies the effective [spin one-half](../../../../../../spin-one-half.md) [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md); neutral atoms avoid the ordinary Lorentz deflection of a charged beam. For an analysing direction $z$, the spatial gradient produces a force approximately $F_z=\mu_z\partial_zB_z$. The resulting separated beam positions measure the allowed projections of [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md). A [spin one-half](../../../../../../spin-one-half.md) beam gives two branches, rather than the continuous distribution expected from classical dipoles with arbitrary orientations. A uniform [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) can rotate a [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) but does not itself provide this spatial separation.

To measure the rotation, first use a $z$-directed [Stern-Gerlach experiment](../../../../../../stern-gerlach-experiment.md) and an aperture to transmit only the $+z$ branch. Pass this prepared [spin eigenstate](../../../../../../spin-eigenstate.md) through a region of approximately uniform field in the $x$ direction, then use a second $z$ analyser to separate and count the two populations. For a field region of length $\ell$ and beam speed $v$, the precession angle is $\theta=\lambda B\ell/v$, and the predicted fractions are

$$
\boxed{P_+=\cos^2(\theta/2),\qquad P_-=\sin^2(\theta/2).}
$$

Varying $B$ or the transit time gives complementary oscillations, demonstrating coherent [Larmor precession](../../../../../../larmor-precession.md). Velocity selection and a sufficiently uniform rotation field keep different particles from accumulating different phases and washing out the contrast. Detection should compare the branch fractions after allowing for losses and analyser efficiency.
