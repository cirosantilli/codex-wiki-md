<h1 id="section-b/b6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $\Omega=\lambda B$, including its sign. The initial vector is the $+\hbar/2$ [eigenstate](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) of $S_z$; it is not an [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) of $H=\Omega S_x$. In terms of the [spin eigenstates](../../../../../../spin-eigenstate.md) found above, $|+z\rangle=(|+x\rangle+|-x\rangle)/\sqrt2$. Each [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) acquires its own [unitary time evolution](../../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md) phase:

$$
|\psi(t)\rangle=\frac{e^{-i\Omega t/2}|+x\rangle+e^{i\Omega t/2}|-x\rangle}{\sqrt2}=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\Omega t/2)\\-i\sin(\Omega t/2)\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Equivalently, $e^{-iHt/\hbar}=I\cos(\Omega t/2)-i\sigma_x\sin(\Omega t/2)$ follows from $\sigma_x^2=I$. The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(S_z=+\hbar/2;t)=|\langle+z|\psi(t)\rangle|^2=\cos^2(\Omega t/2).}
$$

The [expectation value](../../../../../../expectation-value.md) of the [spin angular momentum](../../../../../../spin.md) is $(\hbar/2)(0,-\sin\Omega t,\cos\Omega t)$, a rotation around the $x$ axis. Its direction follows the specified sign of the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md); changing the magnetic coupling sign reverses the precession.
