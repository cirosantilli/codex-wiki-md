<h1 id="section-b/b6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the $S_z$ [eigenbasis](../../../../../../eigenbasis.md), let a candidate [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) be $(a,b)^{\mathsf T}$. The [eigenvalue equation](../../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) is $b=(2s_x/\hbar)a$ and $a=(2s_x/\hbar)b$, so a nonzero vector requires $(2s_x/\hbar)^2=1$. Choosing normalized phases gives

$$
\boxed{|+x\rangle=\frac{|+z\rangle+|-z\rangle}{\sqrt2}=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},\quad s_x=\frac\hbar2,}
$$



$$
\boxed{|-x\rangle=\frac{|+z\rangle-|-z\rangle}{\sqrt2}=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix},\quad s_x=-\frac\hbar2.}
$$

The two [spin eigenstates](../../../../../../spin-eigenstate.md) are [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md), as required for distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of a [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md).
