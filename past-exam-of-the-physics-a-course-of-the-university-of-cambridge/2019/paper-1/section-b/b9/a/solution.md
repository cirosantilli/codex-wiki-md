<h1 id="section-b/b9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $c=\sqrt{m\omega/(2\hbar)}$ and $d=1/\sqrt{2m\hbar\omega}$, so $a=cx+idp$ and $a^\dagger=cx-idp$. The [canonical commutation relation](../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) $[x,p]=i\hbar$ gives

$$
a^\dagger a=c^2x^2+d^2p^2+icd[x,p]=\frac{m\omega x^2}{2\hbar}+\frac{p^2}{2m\hbar\omega}-\frac12.
$$

Hence $\boxed{H=\hbar\omega(a^\dagger a+1/2)}$. Similarly,

$$
[a,a^\dagger]=-2icd[x,p]=\boxed{1}.
$$

Using the product rule for a [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md), $[AB,C]=A[B,C]+[A,C]B$, gives

$$
\boxed{[H,a]=-\hbar\omega a,\qquad [H,a^\dagger]=\hbar\omega a^\dagger.}
$$

Thus $a^\dagger a$ is the [number operator](../../../../../../number-operator.md), while $a$ and $a^\dagger$ are the [annihilation operator](../../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) and [creation operator](../../../../../../creation-operator.md).
