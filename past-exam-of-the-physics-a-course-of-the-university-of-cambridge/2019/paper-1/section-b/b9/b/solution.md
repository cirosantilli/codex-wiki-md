<h1 id="section-b/b9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [commutators](../../../../../../commutator.md) imply

$$
H(a|n\rangle)=(E_n-\hbar\omega)a|n\rangle,\qquad H(a^\dagger|n\rangle)=(E_n+\hbar\omega)a^\dagger|n\rangle.
$$

Thus any nonzero resulting vector is the adjacent [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md). Since $a^\dagger a|n\rangle=n|n\rangle$ and $aa^\dagger=a^\dagger a+1$, their squared norms are

$$
\|a|n\rangle\|^2=n,\qquad \|a^\dagger|n\rangle\|^2=n+1.
$$

Choosing consistent phases for the [eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) yields

$$
\boxed{a|n\rangle=\sqrt n\,|n-1\rangle\quad(n\geq1),\qquad a^\dagger|n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}\,|n+1\rangle,\qquad a|0\rangle=0.}
$$

The vanishing last expression terminates the lowering sequence at the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md), rather than introducing a negative-energy oscillator state.
