<h1 id="section-b/b9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the [position](../../../../../../position.md) representation $p=-i\hbar\,d/dx$, so $a\psi_0=0$ becomes

$$
\left(\frac{d}{dx}+\frac{m\omega}{\hbar}x\right)\psi_0=0.
$$

Integrating and using the [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) for [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\psi_0(x)=\left(\frac{m\omega}{\pi\hbar}\right)^{1/4}\exp\left(-\frac{m\omega x^2}{2\hbar}\right).}
$$

The [creation operator](../../../../../../creation-operator.md) gives $|1\rangle=a^\dagger|0\rangle$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{\psi_1(x)=\sqrt{\frac{2m\omega}{\hbar}}\,x\psi_0(x).}
$$

Its [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) is one since $\int x^2|\psi_0|^2dx=\hbar/(2m\omega)$. Moreover $\psi_0$ is even and $\psi_1$ is odd, so the overlap integrand $\psi_0^*\psi_1$ is odd and integrable. Integration over the whole real line gives $\boxed{\langle0|1\rangle=0}$, establishing [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md).
