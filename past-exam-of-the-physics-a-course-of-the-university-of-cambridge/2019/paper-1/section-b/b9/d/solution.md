<h1 id="section-b/b9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Inverting the definitions of the [creation operator](../../../../../../creation-operator.md) and [annihilation operator](../../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) gives $x=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}(a+a^\dagger)$. Their actions and the [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of the oscillator [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) give

$$
\langle n_2|x|n_1\rangle=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}\left[\sqrt{n_1}\,\delta_{n_2,n_1-1}+\sqrt{n_1+1}\,\delta_{n_2,n_1+1}\right].
$$

Thus the [electric dipole transition](../../../../../../electric-dipole-transition.md) [selection rule](../../../../../../selection-rule.md) is

$$
\boxed{n_2=n_1\pm1,}
$$

with the lower alternative absent for $n_1=0$. The field exchanges one oscillator quantum in first-order dipole coupling. For a long weak monochromatic drive, its [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) must also be resonant with the level separation, here $\omega$, to give a substantial transition [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). Higher-order multiphoton processes are beyond the stated single-position-[matrix element](../../../../../../matrix-element.md) approximation.
