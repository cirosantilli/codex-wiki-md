<h1 id="section-b/b7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The normalized [radial probability density](../../../../../../radial-probability-density.md) gives

$$
\left\langle\frac1r\right\rangle=4\beta^3\int_0^\infty re^{-2\beta r}\,dr=\beta.
$$

Consequently the [expectation value](../../../../../../expectation-value.md) of the [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) is

$$
\langle V\rangle=-\frac{Ze^2\beta}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=-\frac{\hbar^2\beta^2}{m_e}=\boxed{2E}.
$$

Here the last equality uses $Ze^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0)=\hbar^2\beta/m_e$. Since this is an [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md), $E=\langle T\rangle+\langle V\rangle$, and so $\boxed{\langle T\rangle=-E}$. The [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is positive because $E<0$, in agreement with the [virial theorem](../../../../../../virial-theorem.md) for a [Coulomb potential](../../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md).
