<h1 id="section-b/b8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first two [probability densities](../../../../../../probability-density.md) are

$$
\boxed{\rho_1(x)=\frac2L\sin^2\frac{\pi x}L,\qquad \rho_2(x)=\frac2L\sin^2\frac{2\pi x}L.}
$$

Both vanish at the walls. The [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) has one maximum at $L/2$ and no interior node. The first excited state has a node at $L/2$ and equal maxima at $L/4$ and $3L/4$.

For a [finite square well](../../../../../../finite-square-well.md) whose exterior potential is $V_0>E_n$, the exterior [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) decays as $e^{-\kappa d}$, where $\kappa=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E_n)}/\hbar$ and $d$ is distance from the relevant edge. [Wavefunction matching at a finite potential step](../../../../../../wavefunction-matching-at-a-finite-potential-step.md) makes the [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) and, for constant [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), its first derivative continuous. Thus the [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) is nonzero at the edges and has evanescent tails outside, the interior wavelength increases, and the [energy levels](../../../../../../energy-level.md) are lower than for infinite walls at the same positions. Reflection symmetry still gives an even [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) and odd first excited state about the center, when both are bound. A shallow well may not support that excited bound state at all.
