<h1 id="section-b/b8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The infinite walls impose $\psi(0)=\psi(L)=0$, and the interior time-independent [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) is $\psi''+k^2\psi=0$ with $E=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$. Its general solution is $A\sin kx+B\cos kx$. The first [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) gives $B=0$, and the second gives $kL=n\pi$. A nonzero normalizable state needs $n=1,2,\ldots$; $n=0$ would give the zero function.

[Wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md), $\int_0^L\sin^2(n\pi x/L)\,dx=L/2$, gives the [infinite square well](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) states and their [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md):

$$
\boxed{\psi_n(x)=\sqrt{\frac2L}\sin\frac{n\pi x}L\quad(0<x<L),\qquad E_n=\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2mL^2}.}
$$

The [wavefunctions](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) vanish outside the well, and their full time dependence is $\psi_n(x)e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}$.
