<h1 id="section-c/c11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) of a particle is the operator $\mathbf L=\mathbf r\times\mathbf p$, with $\mathbf p=-i\hbar\nabla$ in the [position](../../../../../../position.md) representation. Its [angular momentum commutation relations](../../../../../../angular-momentum-commutation-relations.md) are

$$
[L_i,L_j]=i\hbar\epsilon_{ijk}L_k,\qquad [L^2,L_i]=0.
$$

Thus one may specify $L^2$ and one component, conventionally $L_z$, simultaneously. One generally cannot assign sharp values to all three components, because they do not commute. The common angular [eigenfunctions](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) are the [spherical harmonics](../../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md):

$$
\boxed{L^2Y_l^m=\hbar^2l(l+1)Y_l^m,\qquad L_zY_l^m=\hbar mY_l^m,\qquad l=0,1,2,\ldots,\quad m=-l,\ldots,l.}
$$

For a scalar orbital [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md), single-valuedness in the azimuthal angle gives integer $m$, and regularity on the sphere and termination of the ladder give integer $l\geq|m|$. Half-integer angular momenta describe intrinsic [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) rather than scalar orbital motion.

The [ladder operators](../../../../../../ladder-operator.md) $L_\pm=L_x\pm iL_y$ obey $[L_z,L_\pm]=\pm\hbar L_\pm$. Their normalized action is

$$
L_\pm|l,m\rangle=\hbar\sqrt{l(l+1)-m(m\pm1)}\,|l,m\pm1\rangle.
$$

The coefficient vanishes at the top or bottom of the multiplet. Its squared magnitude follows from $L_\mp L_\pm=L^2-L_z^2\mp\hbar L_z$, and positivity of the resulting [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) constrains the allowed $m$ values.

For a [central potential](../../../../../../central-potential.md), the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) commutes with $L^2$ and every component of $\mathbf L$. Stationary states can be separated as $R_{nl}(r)Y_l^m(\theta,\phi)$, and rotational symmetry makes the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) independent of $m$, giving a $2l+1$ [quantum degeneracy](../../../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md) at fixed radial label and $l$. Additional degeneracy between distinct $l$ values requires additional structure, as in the [Coulomb potential](../../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md). The [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) of $Y_l^m$ is $(-1)^l$. These results organize atomic orbitals and their [selection rules](../../../../../../selection-rule.md). They describe quantized rotation and symmetry, without assigning the electron a classical orbit or a simultaneously definite three-component [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) vector.
