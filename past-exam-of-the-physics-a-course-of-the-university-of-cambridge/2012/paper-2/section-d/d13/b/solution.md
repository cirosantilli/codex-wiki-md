<h1 id="section-d/d13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For degenerate carriers with approximately elastic scattering and a common transport [relaxation time](../../../../../../relaxation-time.md), $\sigma=ne^2\tau/m^*$ and $\kappa_e=(C_e/V)v_F^2\tau/3$. Using $C_e/V=\pi^2nk_B^2T/(2E_F)$ and $v_F^2=2E_F/m^*$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\kappa_e}{\sigma T}=L_0,\qquad
L_0=\frac{\pi^2k_B^2}{3e^2}=2.44\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{W\,\Omega\,K^{-2}}}.
$$

This is the Wiedemann–Franz law. The same mobile [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) carry charge and heat, but Pauli statistics set the [electronic heat capacity](../../../../../../electronic-heat-capacity.md) and hence the [Lorenz number](../../../../../../lorenz-number.md). The relation applies to electronic [thermal conductivity](../../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md), not automatically to total [electrical conductivity](../../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) including [phonons](../../../../../../phonon.md). Inelastic [electron-phonon scattering](../../../../../../electron-phonon-scattering.md) can relax [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and charge currents differently; nondegeneracy and strong [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) dependence of scattering also limit the simple form.

A meaningful test combines a four-terminal electrical measurement with a steady thermal-gradient measurement on the same sample and [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) range. Contact heat resistance, parasitic heat leaks, [phonon](../../../../../../phonon.md) conduction and sample geometry must be controlled before interpreting a measured Lorenz ratio as an electronic departure from the law.
