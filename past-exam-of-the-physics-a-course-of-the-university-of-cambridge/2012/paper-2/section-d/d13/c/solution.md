<h1 id="section-d/d13/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A carrier in a locally parabolic isotropic band obeys $m^*\dot{\mathbf v}=q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$, so

$$
\boxed{\omega_c=\frac{|q|B}{m_c},\qquad m_c=\frac{|q|B_{\rm res}}\omega}.
$$

Illuminate a high-mobility crystal with a transverse alternating [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md), sweep a calibrated static [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) at known [microwave](../../../../../../microwave.md) or far-infrared [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md), and detect resonant absorbed [power](../../../../../../power.md). Cooling lengthens the [relaxation time](../../../../../../relaxation-time.md); resolving the [resonance](../../../../../../resonance.md) generally needs $\omega_c\tau\gg1$. Varying field direction measures band anisotropy, while [circular polarization](../../../../../../circular-polarization.md) distinguishes the two senses of rotation for opposite carrier charges. This is orbital motion rather than [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) spin [resonance](../../../../../../resonance.md).

The classic silicon/germanium measurements used single crystals near $4\ \mathrm K$, light modulation and circularly polarized [microwaves](../../../../../../microwave.md), with directional scans to infer anisotropic carrier-energy surfaces.[Abstract: low-temperature single crystals, light modulation, circular polarization and anisotropy](../../../../../../cyclotron-resonance-of-electrons-and-holes-in-silicon-and-germanium-crystals-by-gene-dresselhaus-and-others.md) Carrier generation, calibration, scattering linewidth and inhomogeneity must be separated from a genuine [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) shift.

In a general band the cyclotron [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) is an orbit property, $m_c=\hbar^2(\partial A_k/\partial E)/(2\pi)$ for the appropriate field-normal orbit. It need not equal every component of the band-curvature [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) tensor. For an ellipsoidal parabolic band with field along a principal $z$ direction it is $\sqrt{m_xm_y}$. Thus a single [resonance](../../../../../../resonance.md) does not determine a whole effective-mass tensor without angular information.
