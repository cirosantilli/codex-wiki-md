<h1 id="section-b/b9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B9](../b9.md)

The string speed is $c=\sqrt{T/\rho}$. The [characteristic impedance](../../../../../characteristic-impedance.md) is the ratio of the forward-wave transverse force to transverse velocity, $Z=T/c=\sqrt{T\rho}$. With peak displacement amplitude $A$, the mean [power](../../../../../power.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle P\rangle=\tfrac12Z\omega^2A^2}.
$$

Displacement and transverse force are continuous at a massless junction. Since $Z_2=2Z_1$, the displacement amplitudes are $r=(Z_1-Z_2)/(Z_1+Z_2)=-1/3$ and $t=2Z_1/(Z_1+Z_2)=2/3$. Taking the incident amplitude real, the light-string motion is

$$
y=A\left[\cos(kx-\omega t)-\tfrac13\cos(kx+\omega t)\right]
=\frac{2A}{3}\cos(kx-\omega t)+\frac{2A}{3}\sin kx\sin\omega t.
$$

In this [standing-and-travelling decomposition of a reflected string wave](../../../../../standing-and-travelling-decomposition-of-a-reflected-string-wave.md), the first term travels and the second stands; the decomposition is not a separation into independent incident-power fractions because these fields interfere. The reflected wave has a phase reversal. [power](../../../../../power.md) coefficients and standing-wave ratio are

$$
\boxed{\mathcal R=|r|^2=\frac19,\quad
\mathcal T=\frac{Z_2}{Z_1}|t|^2=\frac89,\quad
\mathrm{SWR}=\frac{1+|r|}{1-|r|}=2}.
$$

For a uniform inserted matching section, unequal exterior impedances require a [quarter-wave impedance transformer](../../../../../quarter-wave-impedance-transformer.md): $Z_M=\sqrt{Z_1Z_2}$ and $k_Ma=(2j+1)\pi/2$. At equal [tension](../../../../../tension.md) this means **middle density $2\rho_0$ and an odd-quarter-wavelength length in that section**. Reflections at its two ends then cancel at the design [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md). An arbitrary density or an arbitrary half-wavelength segment cannot perfectly match these unequal exterior strings; the ideal match is frequency-selective.

[amplitude scaling along a slowly tapered string](../../../../../amplitude-scaling-along-a-slowly-tapered-string.md) instead follows from matching over an adiabatic [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) range. The source fixes [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), while conserved [power](../../../../../power.md) and the final [characteristic impedance](../../../../../characteristic-impedance.md) determine amplitude. In the heavy string,

$$
\boxed{f_2=f_1,\qquad A_2=A_1/\sqrt2,\qquad\lambda_2=\lambda_1/2}.
$$

Its [wave speed](../../../../../wave-speed.md) halves. The no-reflection approximation needs small fractional [characteristic impedance](../../../../../characteristic-impedance.md) change over each local [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md), rather than length alone for a taper containing abrupt features.
