<h1 id="section-d/d13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Zero [capacitance](../../../../../../capacitance.md) removes the [capacitor](../../../../../../capacitor.md) branches. No current enters the non-inverting input, so no current flows through $R_1$ or $R_2$ and $V_+=V_{\rm in}$. The inverting divider gives $V_-=V_{\rm out}R_3/(R_3+R_4)$. Equating the two input voltages in the linear solution gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{V_{\rm out}}{V_{\rm in}}=1+\frac{R_4}{R_3}=K}.
$$
