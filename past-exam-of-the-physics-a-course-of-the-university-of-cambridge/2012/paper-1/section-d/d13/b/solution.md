<h1 id="section-d/d13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $V_B$ be the non-inverting input voltage. The feedback divider gives $V_B=V_{\rm out}/K$. With $s=i\omega$, current conservation at this [wavefunction node](../../../../../../wavefunction-node.md) gives $(V_A-V_B)/R_2=sC_2V_B$, because the amplifier input draws no current. Hence

$$
\boxed{V_A=\frac{V_{\rm out}}K(1+i\omega R_2C_2)}.
$$
