<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

For independent [electron](../../../../../electron.md) arrivals, the shot-noise counting model gives mean transferred charge $Q=I\Delta t=10^{-15}\,\mathrm C$ and count $\bar N=Q/e\simeq6.24\times10^3$. Poisson statistics imply

$$
\boxed{\sigma_Q=e\sqrt{\bar N}=\sqrt{eI\Delta t}\simeq1.27\times10^{-17}\,\mathrm C},\qquad
\frac{\sigma_Q}{Q}\simeq1.27\%.
$$

This is about 79 [electron](../../../../../electron.md) charges rms. It assumes Poisson [shot noise](../../../../../shot-noise.md); charge discreteness by itself does not rule out correlations, [thermal noise](../../../../../thermal-noise.md) or other apparatus noise.
