<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Let $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$, $b=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$ and $\xi=x/b$. With $\psi_0=\pi^{-1/4}b^{-1/2}e^{-\xi^2/2}$, the next two normalized harmonic-oscillator [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) are $\psi_1=\sqrt2\xi\psi_0$ and $\psi_2=(2\xi^2-1)\psi_0/\sqrt2$. Their energies are $E_n=(n+1/2)\hbar\omega$, with respectively zero, one and two [wavefunction nodes](../../../../../wavefunction-node.md), alternating even and odd [parity](../../../../../parity.md). The [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) gives

$$
\psi_n''(x)=b^{-2}[\xi^2-(2n+1)]\psi_n(x).
$$

For [harmonic-oscillator wavefunction inflections](../../../../../harmonic-oscillator-wavefunction-inflections.md), **the inflections include both [classical turning points](../../../../../classical-turning-point.md) and simple [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) [wavefunction nodes](../../../../../wavefunction-node.md)**. In dimensionless coordinates the complete sets are

$$
\boxed{n=0:\ \xi=\pm1;\qquad
n=1:\ \xi=0,\pm\sqrt3;\qquad
n=2:\ \xi=\pm1/\sqrt2,\pm\sqrt5}.
$$

The curvature changes sign at every listed point; none of these states has a [wavefunction node](../../../../../wavefunction-node.md) coinciding with its turning points.
