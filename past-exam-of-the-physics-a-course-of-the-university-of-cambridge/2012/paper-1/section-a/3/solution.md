<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Resolving a length requires [momentum transfer](../../../../../momentum-transfer.md) $q$ of order $\hbar/\ell$. Taking a hard relativistic scale $qc\sim4\,\mathrm{TeV}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\ell\sim\frac{\hbar c}{E}\simeq4.9\times10^{-20}\,\mathrm m}.
$$

The full [de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../de-broglie-wavelength.md) convention gives $hc/E\simeq3.1\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm m$, differing by $2\pi$; the intended scale is roughly $10^{-19}\,\mathrm m$, far below a [proton](../../../../../proton.md) radius. Two opposing beams have a larger total collision [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), but [proton](../../../../../proton.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is shared among [partons](../../../../../parton.md): the actual resolution depends on the hard scattering [momentum transfer](../../../../../momentum-transfer.md), not simply on beam [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) alone.
