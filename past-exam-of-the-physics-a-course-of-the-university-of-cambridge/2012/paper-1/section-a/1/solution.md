<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Subtract the two [photoelectric effect](../../../../../photoelectric-effect.md) equations $K_{\max}=hc/\lambda-\Phi$ to eliminate the [work function](../../../../../work-function.md):

$$
h=\frac{(2.3-0.9)e}{c[(200\times10^{-9})^{-1}-(258\times10^{-9})^{-1}]}
=\boxed{6.66\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}}.
$$

Here $e$ converts the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) difference from [electronvolts](../../../../../electronvolt.md) to joules. The precision of the rounded stopping energies justifies about two significant figures, $6.7\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$.
