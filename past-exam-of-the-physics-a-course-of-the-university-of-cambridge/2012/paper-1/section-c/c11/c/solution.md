<h1 id="section-c/c11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) scattering through angle $\theta$ from a free [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) initially at rest, conserve [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md). Eliminating the final [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) by its relativistic [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) relation gives

$$
mc^2(E-E')=EE'(1-\cos\theta),\qquad
\boxed{\lambda'-\lambda=\frac h{mc}(1-\cos\theta)},
$$

or $E'=E/[1+(E/mc^2)(1-\cos\theta)]$. The recoil [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) gains [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $E-E'$. The shift is zero forward, one [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) [Compton wavelength](../../../../../../compton-wavelength.md) at $90^\circ$, and twice that [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) backward.

Compton's X-ray scattering measurements compared scattered spectra at different angles and identified an angle-dependent shifted component. This supports [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) $p=E/c$ and inelastic recoil, beyond the unchanged-frequency prediction of classical elastic [Thomson scattering](../../../../../../thomson-scattering.md). Bound [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) and coherent scattering can also produce an unshifted component; initial [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) and binding broaden or modify the ideal free-electron result. Treating an [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) as initially free and stationary is therefore a kinematic approximation appropriate when the relevant [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) transfer dominates binding scales.[Printed pp. 494–495, experimental angular-spectrum comparison; p. 496, recoil discussion](../../../../../../a-quantum-theory-of-the-scattering-of-x-rays-by-light-elements-by-arthur-holly-compton.md)
