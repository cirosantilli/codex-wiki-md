<h1 id="section-b/b6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The instantaneous rightward [energy flux](../../../../../../energy-flux.md) along a string is the transverse [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) times the transverse [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md),

$$
P=-Ty_xy_t.
$$

For a rightgoing [time-harmonic wave](../../../../../../time-harmonic-wave.md) of peak displacement [wave amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $|A|$, averaging over a [wave period](../../../../../../wave-period.md) gives

$$
\langle P\rangle=\frac12Tk\omega|A|^2=\frac12Z\omega^2|A|^2.
$$

A leftgoing [time-harmonic wave](../../../../../../time-harmonic-wave.md) has the opposite signed [energy flux](../../../../../../energy-flux.md). The positive ratios of reflected and transmitted [power](../../../../../../power.md) to incident [power](../../../../../../power.md) are therefore

$$
\mathcal R=|r|^2=\frac{(Z_L-Z_R)^2}{(Z_L+Z_R)^2},\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal T=\frac{Z_R}{Z_L}|t|^2=\frac{4Z_LZ_R}{(Z_L+Z_R)^2}.}
$$

All explicit [angular frequency](../../../../../../angular-frequency.md) factors cancel. In the ideal [wave equation on a string](../../../../../../wave-equation-on-a-string.md), $T$ and the two [linear mass densities](../../../../../../linear-mass-density.md) are constant, so the [mechanical impedances](../../../../../../mechanical-impedance.md) have no [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) or [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) dependence. Consequently $\mathcal T$ is independent of [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) even though the two string [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md) generally differ.

Finally,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal R+\mathcal T=
\frac{(Z_L-Z_R)^2+4Z_LZ_R}{(Z_L+Z_R)^2}=1.}
$$

This is [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) at a lossless, massless junction. The transmitted displacement [wave amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) can exceed the incident one when $Z_R<Z_L$; the factor $Z_R/Z_L$ is essential when comparing [power](../../../../../../power.md).
