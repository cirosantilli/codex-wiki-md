<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use transverse displacement $y(x,t)$, small slopes and constant [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) $T$. For a short string element of length $dx$, [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) gives $\rho_L dx\,y_{tt}=T[y_x(x+dx)-y_x(x)]$. Hence the [wave equation on a string](../../../../../../wave-equation-on-a-string.md) is

$$
y_{tt}=c_L^2y_{xx},\qquad c_L=\sqrt{\frac T{\rho_L}},
$$

where $\rho_L$ is the [linear mass density](../../../../../../linear-mass-density.md), in $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}}$.

The [mechanical impedance](../../../../../../mechanical-impedance.md) of a rightgoing [travelling wave](../../../../../../travelling-wave.md) is the transverse driving [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) divided by the transverse [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) at the driven section. Choose the [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) exerted by the material to the left on the material to the right, $F_y=-Ty_x$. For $y=f(x-c_Lt)$, the transverse [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is $v_y=y_t=-c_Ly_x$. Thus the positive [characteristic impedance of a stretched string](../../../../../../characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md) is

$$
\boxed{Z_L=\frac{F_y}{v_y}=\frac T{c_L}=\rho_Lc_L=\sqrt{T\rho_L},\qquad [Z_L]=\mathrm{N\,s\,m^{-1}}=\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

A leftgoing component has $F_y/v_y=-Z_L$ with this fixed orientation of [force](../../../../../../force-split.md). Specifying the orientation avoids confusing that propagation sign with a negative material [mechanical impedance](../../../../../../mechanical-impedance.md).
