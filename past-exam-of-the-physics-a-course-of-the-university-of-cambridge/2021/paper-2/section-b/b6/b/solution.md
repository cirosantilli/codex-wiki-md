<h1 id="section-b/b6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Place the junction at $x=0$ and use the [complex amplitude](../../../../../../complex-amplitude.md) convention $e^{-i\omega t}$. With incident displacement [wave amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $A_i$, write

$$
y_L=A_i(e^{ik_Lx}+r e^{-ik_Lx})e^{-i\omega t},\qquad
y_R=A_i t e^{ik_Rx}e^{-i\omega t}.
$$

Here $r$ and $t$ are displacement [wave reflection](../../../../../../wave-reflection.md) and [transmission coefficients](../../../../../../transmission-coefficient.md), and $k_j=\omega/c_j$. The junction is assumed massless, with no externally applied transverse [force](../../../../../../force-split.md), and both strings have the common equilibrium [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) $T$. Continuity of displacement and transverse [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) gives

$$
1+r=t,\qquad Tk_L(1-r)=Tk_Rt.
$$

Since $Tk_j=\omega Z_j$, these become $1+r=t$ and $Z_L(1-r)=Z_Rt$. Solving,

$$
\boxed{\frac{A_r}{A_i}=r=\frac{Z_L-Z_R}{Z_L+Z_R},\qquad
\frac{A_t}{A_i}=t=\frac{2Z_L}{Z_L+Z_R}.}
$$

A larger right-hand [mechanical impedance](../../../../../../mechanical-impedance.md) produces a reflected displacement with reversed sign, as expected in the fixed-end limit $Z_R\to\infty$. Equal [mechanical impedances](../../../../../../mechanical-impedance.md) give $r=0$ and $t=1$.
