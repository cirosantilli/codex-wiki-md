<h1 id="section-c/c8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Set $G=G_1$, $\kappa=\Delta k$, $U=|V_1|>0$ and $E_B=\hbar^2G^2/(8m)$. The two kinetic [energies](../../../../../../energy-split.md) satisfy

$$
\frac{\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2}{2}=E_B+\frac{\hbar^2\kappa^2}{2m},\qquad
\frac{\epsilon_1-\epsilon_2}{2}=\frac{\hbar^2G\kappa}{2m}.
$$

Consequently the [nearly-free electron dispersion near a one-dimensional band gap](../../../../../../nearly-free-electron-dispersion-near-a-one-dimensional-band-gap.md) becomes

$$
E_\pm\left(\frac G2+\kappa\right)=V_0+E_B+\frac{\hbar^2\kappa^2}{2m}
\pm\sqrt{U^2+\left(\frac{\hbar^2G\kappa}{2m}\right)^2}.
$$

The [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) $\sqrt{U^2+s^2}=U+s^2/(2U)+O(s^4/U^3)$ gives

$$
\boxed{E_\pm=V_0+E_B\pm U+
\left(\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\pm\frac{\hbar^4G^2}{8m^2U}\right)(\Delta k)^2
+O((\Delta k)^4).}
$$

For the paper's [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) convention, set $V_0=0$. There is no linear or cubic term because each branch is an even function of displacement from the crossing in this two-wave approximation. Each coefficient multiplying $(\Delta k)^2$ has units of [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) times length squared, as required.

The expansion requires

$$
\left|\frac{\hbar^2G\Delta k}{2m}\right|\ll U,
$$

a more restrictive condition than merely $|\Delta k|\ll G$ when the [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md) is small. If $U=0$, the square root is proportional to $|\Delta k|$ and this analytic expansion of the energy-ordered branches is unavailable.
