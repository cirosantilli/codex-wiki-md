<h1 id="section-c/c8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Twice the quadratic coefficient from the previous part is the [band curvature](../../../../../../band-curvature.md) $E_\pm''(G_1/2)$. Thus the [band effective masses](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) are

$$
\frac1{m_\pm^*}=\frac1{\hbar^2}E_\pm''\left(\frac{G_1}2\right)
=\frac1m\pm\frac{\hbar^2G_1^2}{4m^2U},\qquad
\boxed{m_\pm^*=\frac{m}{1\pm\hbar^2G_1^2/(4mU)}=\frac{m}{1\pm2E_B/U}.}
$$

Here $U=|V(G_1)|$ and $E_B=\hbar^2G_1^2/(8m)$. In the weak-potential regime $U\ll E_B$,

$$
\boxed{m_+^*\simeq+\frac{4m^2U}{\hbar^2G_1^2},\qquad
m_-^*\simeq-\frac{4m^2U}{\hbar^2G_1^2}.}
$$

Both have units of [mass](../../../../../../mass.md); their magnitudes are much smaller than $m$ in this asymptotic regime. The upper [energy band](../../../../../../energy-band.md) has a local minimum and positive [effective mass](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md), while the lower [energy band](../../../../../../energy-band.md) has a local maximum and negative [effective mass](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md). The exact lower-branch sign changes at $U=2E_B$ in the two-wave formula; the intended weak-coupling limit lies below that threshold.

The physical meaning follows from the semiclassical equations for an [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) [wave packet](../../../../../../wave-packet.md) in one [energy band](../../../../../../energy-band.md):

$$
v=\frac1\hbar\frac{dE}{dk},\qquad \hbar\dot k=F,\qquad \dot v=\frac F{m^*}.
$$

An upper-band [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) near its minimum accelerates in the direction of the applied [force](../../../../../../force-split.md), whereas a lower-band [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) near its maximum accelerates opposite to it. At the extremum itself the [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) is zero. A nearly filled lower [energy band](../../../../../../energy-band.md) is conveniently described by [electron holes](../../../../../../electron-hole.md) with positive [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $+e$ and positive [effective mass](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) $m_h^*=-m_-^*$. Negative curvature is a response of a crystal [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md), not a negative bare [electron mass](../../../../../../electron-mass.md). These statements assume slow forcing that does not induce transitions across the [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md).
