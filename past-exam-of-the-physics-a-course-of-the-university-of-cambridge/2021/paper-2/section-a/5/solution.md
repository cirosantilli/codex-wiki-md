<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Let $N=L/a$ be the integer number of [primitive unit cells](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md). A [Bloch state](../../../../../bloch-state.md) can be written as $\psi_k(x)=e^{ikx}u_k(x)$, with $u_k(x+a)=u_k(x)$. The [periodic boundary condition](../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) $\psi_k(x+L)=\psi_k(x)$ therefore requires

$$
e^{ikL}=1,\qquad k_j=\frac{2\pi j}{L},\qquad j\in\mathbb Z.
$$

The spacing of allowed [wavevectors](../../../../../wavevector.md) is $\Delta k=2\pi/L$. The first [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) has width $2\pi/a$, so

$$
\boxed{N_k=\frac{2\pi/a}{2\pi/L}=\frac La=N.}
$$

For an exact endpoint convention take a half-open [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md), such as $[-\pi/a,\pi/a)$. Its two endpoints differ by a [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) vector and must not both be counted as distinct values of [crystal momentum](../../../../../crystal-momentum.md). There are exactly $N$ inequivalent integers $j$ modulo $N$, irrespective of whether $N$ is even or odd. This is [state counting in a Bloch band](../../../../../state-counting-in-a-bloch-band.md): one allowed [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md) per [primitive unit cell](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md) per [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md). A separate twofold [spin](../../../../../spin.md) multiplicity gives $2N$ electronic states per [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md), without doubling the number of [wavevectors](../../../../../wavevector.md).
