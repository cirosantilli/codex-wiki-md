<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For the [deep-water gravity wave](../../../../../deep-water-gravity-wave.md), the [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) and [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) follow from its [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md):

$$
v_p=\frac{\omega}{k}=\sqrt{\frac gk},\qquad v_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk}=\frac12\sqrt{\frac gk}=\frac{v_p}{2}.
$$

The [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) travels at $v_g$, while the individual crests travel at $v_p$. Let its effective length be $D\simeq5\lambda$, where $\lambda=2\pi/k$ is the carrier [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md). A fixed observer is inside the [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) for $\Delta t=D/v_g$, and crests arrive with [wave period](../../../../../wave-period.md) $\lambda/v_p$. Thus

$$
\boxed{N_{\rm passing}=\frac{D/v_g}{\lambda/v_p}=5\frac{v_p}{v_g}=10\text{ crests}.}
$$

Crests enter the rear of the moving [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) and disappear at its front, so the five visible at one instant are not five permanently identifiable crests. The count uses the intended narrow-band, approximately unchanged [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) and neglects the precise visibility of its end crests.
