<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

A bubble is a thin water layer surrounded by air. At a locally flat patch, let its thickness be $d$, its [refractive index](../../../../../refractive-index.md) be $n$, and the internal angle of [refraction](../../../../../refraction.md) be $\theta_t$. The two principal reflected [light](../../../../../light.md) contributions are the reflection at the first surface and the contribution that enters the layer, reflects at its second surface and leaves again. The first reflection, from lower to higher [refractive index](../../../../../refractive-index.md), introduces a $\pi$ [phase shift](../../../../../phase-shift.md); the second does not. Their [optical path difference](../../../../../optical-path-difference.md) is $2nd\cos\theta_t$, so their [phase difference](../../../../../phase-difference.md) is, modulo $2\pi$,

$$
\Delta\phi=\frac{4\pi nd\cos\theta_t}{\lambda_0}+\pi,
$$

where $\lambda_0$ is the [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md). [Constructive interference](../../../../../constructive-interference.md) in reflected [light](../../../../../light.md) therefore occurs approximately when

$$
\boxed{2nd\cos\theta_t=(m+\tfrac12)\lambda_0,\qquad m=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$

[Destructive interference](../../../../../destructive-interference.md) occurs at $2nd\cos\theta_t=m\lambda_0$. In the weak-reflection approximation, the reflected [light intensity](../../../../../light-intensity.md) at each [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) is proportional to $\sin^2(2\pi nd\cos\theta_t/\lambda_0)$. This is a smooth spectral filter, with broad maxima and minima: it does not select three pure [wavelengths](../../../../../wavelength.md) corresponding to display primaries. Ordinary illumination contains many visible [wavelengths](../../../../../wavelength.md), and the eye combines their remaining contributions through [color vision](../../../../../color-vision.md). The result is a mixture of colours, often pastel or desaturated.

The layer thickness varies over the bubble because of drainage and flow; the bubble's curvature also changes the local angle of [refraction](../../../../../refraction.md) and the viewed [optical path difference](../../../../../optical-path-difference.md). The spectral filter therefore changes continuously across the surface and in time. Very thin regions become dark because the two reflected contributions nearly cancel across the visible [optical spectrum](../../../../../optical-spectrum.md). [Thin-film interference](../../../../../thin-film-interference.md) explains both the changing colours and this dark limit.
