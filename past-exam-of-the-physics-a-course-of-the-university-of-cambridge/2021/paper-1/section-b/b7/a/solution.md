<h1 id="section-b/b7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $a=a_-$ and $a^\dagger=a_+$. Inverting the [annihilation operator](../../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) and [creation operator](../../../../../../creation-operator.md) definitions gives

$$
x=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}(a+a^\dagger),\qquad
p=i\sqrt{\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}}(a^\dagger-a).
$$

The ladder operators take $|n\rangle$ to orthogonal [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) $|n-1\rangle$ and $|n+1\rangle$. Thus $\langle n|a|n\rangle=\langle n|a^\dagger|n\rangle=0$, and

$$
\boxed{\langle x\rangle_n=0,\qquad\langle p\rangle_n=0.}
$$

For $n=0$, the lowering term is simply zero. The conclusion concerns an individual [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md); interference between different eigenstates can produce nonzero [expectation values](../../../../../../expectation-value.md).
