<h1 id="section-b/b7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Each [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) picks up its own phase under [unitary time evolution](../../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md). The relative phase therefore changes at $(E_1-E_0)/\hbar=\omega$. After removing an irrelevant [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md), the state is

$$
|\Psi(t)\rangle=\cos\theta|0\rangle+
 e^{i(\phi-\omega t)}\sin\theta|1\rangle.
$$

The diagonal position [matrix elements](../../../../../../matrix-element.md) vanish, but the [annihilation operator](../../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) and [creation operator](../../../../../../creation-operator.md) give

$$
\langle0|x|1\rangle=\langle1|x|0\rangle
=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}.
$$

The two off-diagonal cross terms in the [expectation value](../../../../../../expectation-value.md) yield

$$
\boxed{\langle x\rangle_t=
\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}\sin(2\theta)\cos(\phi-\omega t).}
$$

Thus the [angular frequency](../../../../../../angular-frequency.md) is $\omega$, and the magnitude of the oscillation, meaning its amplitude, is

$$
\boxed{A=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}\,|\sin(2\theta)|.}
$$

The peak-to-peak displacement is $2A$. The phase $\phi$ fixes where the oscillation starts but does not change $A$. A pure $|0\rangle$ or $|1\rangle$ preparation has no mean oscillation, whereas an equal-weight [quantum superposition](../../../../../../quantum-superposition.md) maximizes its amplitude within this two-state family. The motion is produced by interference between [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md), rather than by a changing occupation of either one.
