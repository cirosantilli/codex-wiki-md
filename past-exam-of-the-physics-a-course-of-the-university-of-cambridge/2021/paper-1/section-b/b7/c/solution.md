<h1 id="section-b/b7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [standard deviations](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) defining the position and momentum uncertainties satisfy $(\Delta x)^2=\langle x^2\rangle-\langle x\rangle^2$ and $(\Delta p)^2=\langle p^2\rangle-\langle p\rangle^2$. Part (a) makes both means zero. Using the second moments from part (b),

$$
\Delta x=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar(n+1/2)}{m\omega}},\qquad
\Delta p=\sqrt{m\hbar\omega(n+1/2)}.
$$

Multiplication gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta x\,\Delta p=\hbar\left(n+\frac12\right).}
$$

The [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](../../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) requires only $\Delta x\Delta p\geq\hbar/2$. Among the oscillator [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md), equality occurs solely for the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) $n=0$; the excited states have larger uncertainty products.
