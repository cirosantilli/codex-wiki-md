<h1 id="section-b/b7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [harmonic-oscillator number-state second moments](../../../../../../harmonic-oscillator-number-state-second-moments.md), the [number operator](../../../../../../number-operator.md) gives $\langle a^\dagger a\rangle_n=n$, while the [canonical commutation relation](../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) gives $\langle aa^\dagger\rangle_n=n+1$. Terms with $a^2$ or $(a^\dagger)^2$ have zero diagonal [matrix elements](../../../../../../matrix-element.md), since they change the [occupation number](../../../../../../occupation-number.md) by two. Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle x^2\rangle_n
&=\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}\langle a^2+(a^\dagger)^2+aa^\dagger+a^\dagger a\rangle_n
=\frac{\hbar(2n+1)}{2m\omega},\\
\langle p^2\rangle_n
&=-\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}\langle (a^\dagger)^2+a^2-a^\dagger a-aa^\dagger\rangle_n
=\frac{m\hbar\omega(2n+1)}2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Consequently the [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) and [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) means are

$$
\boxed{\langle V\rangle_n=\frac12m\omega^2\langle x^2\rangle_n
=\frac12\hbar\omega\left(n+\frac12\right),\qquad
\langle T\rangle_n=\frac{\langle p^2\rangle_n}{2m}
=\frac12\hbar\omega\left(n+\frac12\right).}
$$

Their sum is the oscillator [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) $E_n=\hbar\omega(n+1/2)$. Their equality is also the [virial theorem](../../../../../../virial-theorem.md) result for a quadratic potential, here derived directly from the ladder operators.
