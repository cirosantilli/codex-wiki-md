<h1 id="section-c/c8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the [natural logarithm](../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) of the [exponential decay](../../../../../../exponential-decay.md) model. To keep the logarithm dimensionless, choose the reference rate $R_*=1\,\mathrm{count\,day^{-1}}$ and define

$$
\boxed{x=t,\qquad y=\ln(R/R_*),\qquad
m=-\frac1\tau,\qquad c=\ln(R_0/R_*).}
$$

Then $y=mx+c$. With time measured in picoseconds, the slope has units $\mathrm{ps}^{-1}$ and the intercept is dimensionless. A straight-line [linear regression](../../../../../../linear-regression-split.md) in these variables therefore estimates the inverse [mean lifetime](../../../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) and logarithmic normalization.
