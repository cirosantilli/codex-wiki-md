<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) gives the required [probability](../../../../../probability.md) by integrating the [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) over the central interval. Put $d=0.1\,\mathrm{nm}$. For this [infinite square well](../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md),

$$
P(|x|\leq d)=\frac2L\int_{-d}^{d}\cos^2\left(\frac{\pi x}L\right)dx
=\frac1L\int_{-d}^{d}\left[1+\cos\left(\frac{2\pi x}L\right)\right]dx
=\frac{2d}L+\frac1\pi\sin\left(\frac{2\pi d}L\right).
$$

Substitution of $d/L=0.1$ gives

$$
\boxed{P=0.2+\frac{\sin(0.2\pi)}\pi\simeq0.3871\simeq38.7\%.}
$$

This exceeds the interval's $20\%$ fraction of the box length because the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) is largest at the centre; it is not a uniform position distribution.
