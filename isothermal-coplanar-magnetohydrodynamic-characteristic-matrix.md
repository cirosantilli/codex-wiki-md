# Isothermal coplanar magnetohydrodynamic characteristic matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetosonic wave](magnetosonic-wave.md)

For one-dimensional flow obeying an [isothermal equation of state](globally-isothermal-equation-of-state.md) with $u_z=B_z=0$, the primitive variables $(\rho,u_x,u_y,B_y)$ form a four-variable hyperbolic system and $B_x$ is constant. The speeds relative to $u_x$ satisfy $c^4-(c_s^2+v_a^2)c^2+c_s^2v_{ax}^2=0$. These are the fast and slow [magnetosonic waves](magnetosonic-wave.md). The independent out-of-plane [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) polarization and a freely advected entropy mode are absent from this reduced isothermal system.
