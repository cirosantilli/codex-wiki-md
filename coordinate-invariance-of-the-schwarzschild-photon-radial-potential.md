# Coordinate invariance of the Schwarzschild photon radial potential

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md)

A stationary time-coordinate change $t'=t+F(r)$ leaves both the [areal radius](areal-radius.md) and the time-translation [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field-split.md) unchanged. The conserved [Killing energy](killing-energy.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) of a [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) therefore yield the same radial equation in either chart, $\dot r^2+h^2(1-2\mu/r)/r^2=c^2k^2$. The [effective potential](effective-potential.md) and radial [turning points](turning-point.md) agree, even when the coordinate time velocities and horizon regularity differ.
