# Archimedean property

↑ **Parent:** [Real number](real-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedean_property)

The Archimedean property says that the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) are unbounded in the [real numbers](real-number.md). In particular, for every positive [real number](real-number.md) $a$ there is a positive [integer](integer.md) $n$ with $1/n<a$. Otherwise a purported upper bound for all [natural numbers](natural-number.md) contradicts the [least-upper-bound property](least-upper-bound-property.md) by translating that bound by one.
