# Contact ion pair

↑ **Parent:** [Ion pair](ion-pair.md)

Oppositely charged ions remaining in direct contact without an intervening complete solvent shell. Their relative positions can bias substitution stereochemistry even when bond cleavage precedes [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) attack.
