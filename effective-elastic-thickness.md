# Effective elastic thickness

↑ **Parent:** [Lithospheric flexure](lithospheric-flexure.md)

The [effective elastic thickness](effective-elastic-thickness.md) is the thickness of a homogeneous [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) with the same effective bending stiffness as the loaded [lithosphere](lithosphere.md). In an isotropic thin-plate model, $D=ET_e^3/[12(1-\nu^2)]$, with [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md) $E$ and [Poisson's ratio](poisson-s-ratio.md) $\nu$. It depends on thermal state, [material creep](creep-deformation.md), loading history and yielding, and is not generally the [crust](planetary-crust.md)'s thickness or the full thermal thickness.
