# eIF2B

↑ **Parent:** [Translation (biology)](translation-biology.md)

The nucleotide-exchange factor that promotes replacement of [GDP](guanosine-diphosphate.md) by [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md) on [eIF2](eif2.md). Inhibition of this exchange reduces the supply of initiation-competent ternary complex.
