# Branches of the complex inverse sine

↑ **Parent:** [Complex inverse sine](complex-inverse-sine.md)

If $G$ is the principal [complex inverse sine](complex-inverse-sine.md), all values reached by [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) are

$$
2n\pi+G(z)
\quad\hbox{or}\quad
(2n+1)\pi-G(z),
\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.
$$

This follows from the [monodromy reflections](monodromy-reflection-at-a-square-root-branch-point.md) about the branch values $-\pi/2$ and $\pi/2$.
