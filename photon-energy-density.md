# Photon energy density

↑ **Parent:** [One-particle distribution function](one-particle-distribution-function.md)

The temporal-temporal component of the photon [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) in a local [orthonormal tetrad](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md). For an isotropic [one-particle distribution function](one-particle-distribution-function.md) expressed using comoving energy $\epsilon=aE$, it is $\bar\rho_\gamma=4\pi a^{-4}\int d\epsilon\,\epsilon^3\bar f(\epsilon)$. A thermal [photon temperature perturbation](photon-temperature-perturbation.md) gives a first-order directional energy perturbation four times its fractional temperature change. Boundary conditions on the distribution justify this factor through [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md).
