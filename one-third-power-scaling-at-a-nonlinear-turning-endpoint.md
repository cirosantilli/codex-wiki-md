# One-third-power scaling at a nonlinear turning endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear endpoint layer with a quadratic reaction](nonlinear-endpoint-layer-with-a-quadratic-reaction.md)

In $\epsilon y''+xy+y^2=0$, balancing all three terms where the outer branches $0$ and $-x$ meet gives the displayed [dominant balance](dominant-balance.md) and the inner equation $Y''+XY+Y^2=0$. Matching to $-X$ and writing $Y=-X+u$ gives $u''-Xu+u^2=0$. Its linearized decaying correction is an [Airy function](airy-function.md); the growing correction is excluded. Matching to zero instead gives $Y''+XY=0$, whose nonzero [Airy function](airy-function.md) combinations oscillate. Thus the acceptable matching branches differ qualitatively at the turning endpoint.
