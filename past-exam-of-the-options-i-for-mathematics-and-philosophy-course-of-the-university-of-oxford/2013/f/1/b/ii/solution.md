↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For every $z\in B$, $z_3+3>2$ and $|z_1+z_2|<2$. Consequently each of the three [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) in the preceding [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) has absolute value below $1/2$, so

$$
\|\nabla g(z)\|^2<\frac14+\frac14+\frac14=\frac34.
$$

The segment joining $x,y\in B$ lies inside $B$. The [mean value theorem](../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) and [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) therefore give

$$
|g(y)-g(x)|\leq\|\nabla g(x+s(y-x))\|\,\|y-x\|\leq\boxed{\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\|y-x\|}.
$$

For $x=y$ both sides vanish. This proves the required [Lipschitz continuity](../../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) uniformly on the ball.
