↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The scalar-valued multivariable [mean value theorem](../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) states that, if $f$ is differentiable on an [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) containing the closed line segment from $x$ to $y$, then for $x\ne y$ there exists $s\in(0,1)$ such that

$$
\boxed{f(y)-f(x)=Df(x+s(y-x))(y-x)=\nabla f(x+s(y-x))\cdot(y-x).}
$$

This follows by applying the one-dimensional [mean value theorem](../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) to $t\mapsto f(x+t(y-x))$ on $[0,1]$. The segment condition is necessary for this formulation on a possibly nonconvex domain.
