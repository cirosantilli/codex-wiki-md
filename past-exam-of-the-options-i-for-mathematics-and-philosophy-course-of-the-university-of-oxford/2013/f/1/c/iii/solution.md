<h1 id="f/1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The map $G$ is $C^1$ on the open ball $B$. Its [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) is

$$
DG(x)=\begin{pmatrix}(x_3+3)^{-1}&(x_3+3)^{-1}&-(x_1+x_2)(x_3+3)^{-2}\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

At the origin $G(0)=0$ and $\boxed{\det DG(0)=1/3\ne0}$. The [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) therefore supplies the required [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md) $V_1\subset B$ and $V_2\subset\mathbb R^3$, both containing zero, with $G:V_1\to V_2$ a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md). The explicit inverse formula on its image,

$$
G^{-1}(y_1,y_2,y_3)=(y_1(y_3+3)-y_2,\ y_2,\ y_3),
$$

also checks the local conclusion directly.
