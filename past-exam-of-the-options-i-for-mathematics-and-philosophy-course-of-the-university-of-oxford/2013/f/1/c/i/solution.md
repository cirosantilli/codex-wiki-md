A $C^1$ [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) $F:U\to V$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) for which both $F$ and $F^{-1}$ are continuously differentiable. If a higher regularity class is specified, both maps must belong to that class. In particular, a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md), and the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $D(F^{-1})(F(x))=(DF(x))^{-1}$.
