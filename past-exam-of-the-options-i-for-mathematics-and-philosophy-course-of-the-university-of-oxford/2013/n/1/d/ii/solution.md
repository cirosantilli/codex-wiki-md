<h1 id="n/1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^nX_j$ and apply the stated second-moment [Markov inequality](../../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) to $Y=\bar X_n-\mu$. The preceding calculation gives $\mathbb E Y^2=\sigma^2/n$. Thus for every $\varepsilon>0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(|\bar X_n-\mu|>\varepsilon)\leq\frac{\sigma^2}{n\varepsilon^2}\longrightarrow0.}
$$

This is [convergence in probability](../../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md) to the common mean, proving the finite-variance [weak law of large numbers](../../../../../../weak-law-of-large-numbers.md). It also covers $\sigma^2=0$, when the bound is identically zero.
