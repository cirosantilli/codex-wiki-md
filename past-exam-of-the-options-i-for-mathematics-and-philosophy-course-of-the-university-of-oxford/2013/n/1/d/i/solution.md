<h1 id="n/1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Linearity of [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and the vanishing of cross [covariances](../../../../../../covariance.md) for independent variables give

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E\left[\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^nX_j\right]=\mu,\qquad\operatorname{Var}\left(\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^nX_j\right)=\frac1{n^2}\sum_{j=1}^n\sigma^2=\frac{\sigma^2}{n}.}
$$

The [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) formula does not require independence; independence is used for the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) sum.
