[Independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) means that for all subsets $A,B\subseteq\mathbb Z$,

$$
\mathbb P(X\in A,Y\in B)=\mathbb P(X\in A)\mathbb P(Y\in B).
$$

For discrete [random variables](../../../../../random-variable-split.md), this is equivalent to the pointwise factorization

$$
\boxed{p_{X,Y}(x,y)=p_X(x)p_Y(y)\quad\text{for every }x,y\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

One direction takes singleton sets; the other sums the pointwise identity over $A\times B$.
