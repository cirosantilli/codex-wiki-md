<h1 id="n/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The five specified masses sum to one, so there are no other possible outcomes. Symmetry gives $\mathbb E X=0$, while $\mathbb E Y=1/4$. The two terms with $Y=1$ have opposite $X$ values and equal masses, so

$$
\mathbb E(XY)=\frac18-\frac18=0,\qquad\boxed{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=\mathbb E(XY)-\mathbb E X\,\mathbb E Y=0.}
$$

Nevertheless $\mathbb P(X=0,Y=1)=0$, whereas $\mathbb P(X=0)\mathbb P(Y=1)=(1/2)(1/4)=1/8$. Therefore **$X$ and $Y$ are not independent**. They are [uncorrelated random variables](../../../../../uncorrelated-random-variables.md), illustrating that zero [covariance](../../../../../covariance.md) alone does not imply independence.
