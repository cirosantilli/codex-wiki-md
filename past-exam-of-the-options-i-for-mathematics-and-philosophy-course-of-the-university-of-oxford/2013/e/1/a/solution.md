A [basis of a topology](../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) $\mathcal T$ is a collection $\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal T$ such that every [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) is a union of members of $\mathcal B$. Equivalently, for each open $U$ and every $x\in U$, there exists $B\in\mathcal B$ with $x\in B\subseteq U$. The empty union represents the empty set. In particular, the basis covers $X$, and any intersection of two basis elements is itself a union of basis elements.
