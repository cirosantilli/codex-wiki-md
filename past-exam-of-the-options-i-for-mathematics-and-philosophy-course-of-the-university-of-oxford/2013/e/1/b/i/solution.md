↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The following open rectangles form a [basis of a topology](../../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) for the [product topology](../../../../../../product-topology.md):

$$
\boxed{\{U\times V:U\in\mathcal T_X,\ V\in\mathcal T_Y\}.}
$$

These [open rectangles in a finite product](../../../../../../open-rectangles-in-a-finite-product.md) cover $X\times Y$, and intersections remain rectangles since $(U_1\times V_1)\cap(U_2\times V_2)=(U_1\cap U_2)\times(V_1\cap V_2)$. One may equivalently take products of elements from a chosen [basis of a topology](../../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) in each factor.
