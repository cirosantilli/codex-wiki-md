The [degree of a field extension](../../../../../degree-of-a-field-extension.md) is the [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the larger [field](../../../../../field.md) as a [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over the smaller one:

$$
\boxed{|K:F|=\dim_FK.}
$$

The [field extension](../../../../../field-extension.md) is a [finite field extension](../../../../../finite-field-extension.md) precisely when this [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is finite, equivalently when finitely many elements of $K$ span $K$ over $F$. Finiteness here concerns the [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md), not the number of elements of the [fields](../../../../../field.md); for example, $\mathbb C$ has degree two over $\mathbb R$ although both [fields](../../../../../field.md) are infinite.
