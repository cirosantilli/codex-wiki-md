<h1 id="b/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $v=P_2-P_1$ and $w=Q_2-Q_1$. They are nonzero [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) with the same [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md). Choose the [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) $R$ taking the direction $v/\|v\|$ to $w/\|w\|$; equal lengths then give $Rv=w$. Define

$$
\boxed{t(x)=Q_1+R(x-P_1).}
$$

This is a [translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) by $Q_1-P_1$ followed by a [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) about $Q_1$, so it is an [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md). Direct substitution gives $t(P_1)=Q_1$ and $t(P_2)=Q_1+w=Q_2$.
