↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md) of the [Euclidean plane](../../../../../euclidean-plane.md) is a map $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ preserving all [Euclidean distances](../../../../../euclidean-distance.md), $\|T(x)-T(y)\|=\|x-y\|$. Such a map of the whole plane is automatically bijective, and has the form $T(x)=Rx+b$ with $R$ an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md).

The classification is **identity, [translations](../../../../../translation-geometry.md), [rotations](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) about a point, [reflections](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) in a line, and glide [reflections](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md)**. Here a [glide reflection](../../../../../glide-reflection.md) is a [reflection](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) followed by a nonzero [translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) parallel to its mirror. The cases preserving [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) have $\det R=1$ and are [translations](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) or [rotations](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md); the orientation-reversing cases have $\det R=-1$ and are [reflections](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) or glide [reflections](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md). The identity is included by allowing a zero [translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) or [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) angle.
