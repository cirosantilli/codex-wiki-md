↑ **Parent:** [Boolean operation](boolean-operation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Morgan's_laws)

De Morgan's laws state

$$
\neg(P\wedge Q)=(\neg P)\vee(\neg Q),
\qquad
\neg(P\vee Q)=(\neg P)\wedge(\neg Q).
$$
