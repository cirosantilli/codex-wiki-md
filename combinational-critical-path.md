# Combinational critical path

↑ **Parent:** [Combinational logic](combinational-logic.md)

The [combinational critical path](combinational-critical-path.md) is a longest propagation-delay path through a [combinational logic](combinational-logic.md) circuit. Register-to-register clock timing also includes launch delay, setup time and clock uncertainty. Putting two functional units in series adds their dependent delays; operating them in parallel instead contributes the slower branch plus any subsequent selection delay.
