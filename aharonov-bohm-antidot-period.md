# Aharonov-Bohm antidot period

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hall antidot](quantum-hall-antidot.md)

For a fixed effective enclosed [area](surface-area.md) $S$, changing the magnetic flux by $h/e$ changes the single-electron [Aharonov-Bohm phase](aharonov-bohm-effect.md) by $2\pi$. Gate-dependent orbit area, spin branches and charging can modify the observed conductance-resonance pattern; the lithographic depleted area need not equal the exact enclosed edge-orbit area.
