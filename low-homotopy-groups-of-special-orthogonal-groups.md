# Low homotopy groups of special orthogonal groups

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md)

All the [special orthogonal groups](special-orthogonal-group.md) are connected, and their second [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) vanish. The rotation circle gives $SO(2)\cong S^1$. Conjugation by [unit quaternions](unit-quaternion.md) gives the twofold [universal cover](universal-cover.md) $S^3\to SO(3)$, so $\pi_1SO(3)=\mathbb Z/2$ and $\pi_3SO(3)=\mathbb Z$. The [special orthogonal sphere fibration](special-orthogonal-sphere-fibration.md) $SO(n-1)\to SO(n)\to S^{n-1}$ propagates the first two [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) for $n\geq4$. In dimension four its [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) has a zero map from the [torsion group](torsion-group.md) $\pi_4S^3=\mathbb Z/2$ to $\pi_3SO(3)=\mathbb Z$, giving $\pi_3SO(4)=\mathbb Z^2$. For $SO(1)$ all positive groups vanish, and $\pi_3SO(2)=0$.
