# Leopoldt conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopoldt_conjecture)

The Leopoldt conjecture says that the diagonal image of the global units of a [number field](number-field.md) in its local $p$-adic unit groups has $\mathbb Z_p$-rank $r_1+r_2-1$. A defect would measure a loss of rank after $p$-adic completion. It is a theorem for abelian extensions of $\mathbb Q$.
