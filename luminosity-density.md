# Luminosity density

↑ **Parent:** [Luminosity](luminosity.md)

The [luminosity](luminosity.md) per unit spatial [volume](volume.md). For a spherical source, its enclosed [luminosity](luminosity.md) is $L(r)=4\pi\int_0^r\lambda(s)s^2\,ds$, and its projected [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) is $\mu(R)=2\int_R^\infty\lambda(r)r\,dr/\sqrt{r^2-R^2}$.
