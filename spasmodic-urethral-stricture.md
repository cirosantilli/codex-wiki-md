# Spasmodic urethral stricture

↑ **Parent:** [Urethral stricture](urethral-stricture.md)

Historical description of transient functional obstruction attributed to muscular [spasm](spasm.md). It must be distinguished from a fixed fibrotic [urethral stricture](urethral-stricture.md) and from other causes of [urinary retention](urinary-retention.md).
