# Unit sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_sphere)

The unit sphere in $\mathbb R^{n+1}$ is

$$
S^n=\{x\in\mathbb R^{n+1}:\|x\|=1\}.
$$
