# Complex singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Complex analysis](complex-analysis-split.md)

A point where a specified complex [function](function-split.md) fails to be [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md), but which is approached by points of its [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md). An [isolated singularity](isolated-singularity-split.md) has a punctured [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) on which the [function](function-split.md) is [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md); a [non-isolated singularity](non-isolated-singularity.md) has other [singularities](complex-singularity.md) arbitrarily close to it. The classification as a [removable singularity](removable-singularity.md), a [pole](pole.md), or an [essential singularity](essential-singularity.md) applies to [isolated singularities](isolated-singularity-split.md).
