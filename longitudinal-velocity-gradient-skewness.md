# Longitudinal velocity-gradient skewness

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md)

For isotropic [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), directional averaging gives $\langle(\partial_xu_x)^3\rangle=(8/105)\langle\operatorname{tr}S^3\rangle=-(2/35)\langle\omega_i\omega_jS_{ij}\rangle$. Positive mean [vortex stretching](vortex-stretching.md) therefore implies negative longitudinal derivative [skewness](skewness.md). [Statistical homogeneity](statistical-homogeneity.md) alone does not determine the sign in a fixed direction.
