# Formal completion commutes with restriction to an open subscheme

↑ **Parent:** [Formal completion of a scheme](formal-completion-of-a-scheme.md)

Restricting the topological structure sheaf of a [formal completion of a scheme](formal-completion-of-a-scheme.md) to an [open subscheme](open-subscheme.md) commutes with its [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md): both sheaves are $\varprojlim_n(\mathcal O_U/(\mathcal I|_U)^{n+1})$ on $|U\cap Y|$. Equivalently, the identical [infinitesimal neighbourhoods](infinitesimal-neighbourhood-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) have identical restricted structure sheaves. On formal affine charts this uses [completed localization of an adic ring](completed-localization-of-an-adic-ring.md).
