# Nuclear resonance fluorescence

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma ray](gamma-ray.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_resonance_fluorescence)

Absorption of a [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) into an excited nuclear level followed by re-emission. The absorber-frame photon [energy](energy-split.md) must overlap the absorption line; nuclear recoil, [Doppler broadening](doppler-broadening.md) and the natural [decay width](decay-width.md) affect resonance. This nuclear process differs from atomic resonance fluorescence used in [quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md).
