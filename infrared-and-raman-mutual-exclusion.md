# Infrared and Raman mutual exclusion

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

In a centrosymmetric molecule, a fundamental vibration cannot be both electric-dipole [infrared](infrared.md) active and ordinary [Raman spectroscopy](raman-spectroscopy.md) active. Dipole components are odd under inversion; polarizability components are even. This selection rule concerns modes, not accidental numerical equality of unrelated frequencies.
