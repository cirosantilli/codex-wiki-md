# Variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease

↑ **Parent:** [Prion disease](prion-disease.md)

A human [prion disease](prion-disease.md) associated with exposure to the [BSE](bovine-spongiform-encephalopathy.md) agent. Exposure totals and clinical-onset totals measure different stages of the disease process; [incubation periods](incubation-period.md) and ascertainment affect their relationship.
