# Gaussian Dirichlet energy

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian number operator](gaussian-number-operator.md)

The energy form of the [Gaussian number operator](gaussian-number-operator.md) is the displayed squared [derivative](derivative.md) integral. Its form domain requires $\sum n|c_n|^2<\infty$, while the full [operator domain](operator-domain.md) requires $\sum n^2|c_n|^2<\infty$. It can be defined by $\lim_{t\downarrow0}t^{-1}(\|f\|_2^2-\langle f,P_tf\rangle)$ even when $Nf$ does not exist in $L^2$. This energy occurs in the [Gaussian Poincaré inequality](gaussian-poincare-inequality.md) and [Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality](gaussian-logarithmic-sobolev-inequality.md).
