# Necklace (combinatorics)

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necklace_(combinatorics))

A coloring necklace is an equivalence class of colorings of cyclically arranged positions under rotation. [Burnside lemma](burnside-s-lemma.md) counts necklaces by averaging the numbers of colorings fixed by each rotation.
