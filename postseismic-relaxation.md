# Postseismic relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Mantle viscosity](mantle-viscosity.md)

Time-dependent deformation after an [earthquake](earthquake.md), including viscous or viscoelastic flow as distinct from fault afterslip. Joint spatial and temporal observations are needed to distinguish those mechanisms and the layers that deform.
