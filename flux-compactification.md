# Flux compactification

↑ **Parent:** [Compactification in string theory](compactification-in-string-theory.md)

A compactification with quantized background form-field strengths on internal cycles. Fluxes can generate potentials for [moduli of a string compactification](modulus-of-a-string-compactification.md) and affect [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) and vacuum geometry. They must satisfy field equations, Bianchi identities and global charge constraints; choosing flux integers alone is not a complete consistency proof.
