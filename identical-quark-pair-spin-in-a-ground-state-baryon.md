# Identical-quark pair spin in a ground-state baryon

↑ **Parent:** [Three-quark colour singlet](three-quark-colour-singlet.md)

For an identical-flavour pair in the symmetric spatial ground state of a [baryon](baryon.md), the flavour factor is symmetric and the [three-quark colour singlet](three-quark-colour-singlet.md) is antisymmetric under their exchange. The [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) therefore requires a symmetric pair [spin](spin.md) factor. Two spin-one-half [quarks](quark.md) then form a [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md), $S_{qq}=1$, rather than a spin singlet. The conclusion applies to the identical pair in both spin-one-half and spin-three-half ground-state baryons.
