# Late-time CMB-matter cross correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md)

Cross-correlating a cosmic microwave background [temperature](temperature-split.md) map with a low-redshift matter tracer can isolate a late gravitational contribution from the much larger primordial anisotropy. Decaying potential wells generally produce a positive correlation of matter overdensity with [temperature](temperature-split.md). Tracer bias, overlapping redshift distributions, foreground emission, masks and chance correlations limit the inference. Potential evolution also depends on [curvature](curvature.md) and gravity assumptions, so this is not a dark-energy measurement independent of a cosmological model.
