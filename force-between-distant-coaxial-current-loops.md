# Force between distant coaxial current loops

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md)

For coaxial circular loops with radii $a_1,a_2\ll d$, the first loop's [on-axis magnetic field of a circular current loop](on-axis-magnetic-field-of-a-circular-current-loop.md) has far value $B_0\simeq\mu_0I_1a_1^2/(2d^3)$. Its [near-axis radial magnetic field](near-axis-radial-magnetic-field.md) at the second loop is $B_\rho\simeq3\mu_0I_1a_1^2a_2/(4d^4)$. Integrating the axial [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) from $I_2a_2\,d\phi\,\widehat{\boldsymbol\phi}\times B_\rho\widehat{\boldsymbol\rho}$ gives the displayed magnitude. Equal current senses attract; opposite senses repel. This is the leading dipole limit and is not an exact close-coil force law.
