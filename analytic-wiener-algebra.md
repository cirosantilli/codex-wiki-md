# Analytic Wiener algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Wiener algebra](wiener-algebra.md)

The analytic Wiener algebra consists of the [power series](power-series.md) $f(z)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nz^n$ with $\sum_{n\geq0}|a_n|<\infty$, identified with their values on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md). The [norm](norm.md) is $\|f\|=\sum|a_n|$. [Discrete convolution](discrete-convolution.md) gives multiplication and a submultiplicative [norm](norm.md); completeness follows from the [absolutely summable sequence space](absolutely-summable-sequence-space.md). Each series converges uniformly on the closed [unit disk](unit-disk.md) and is [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) in its interior.
