# Top compactly supported cohomology from orientation signs

↑ **Parent:** [Compactly supported cohomology](compactly-supported-cohomology.md)

For a connected n-manifold without boundary, a finite good cover presents its top [compactly supported cohomology](compactly-supported-cohomology.md) by one generator per disc and relations $u_i=\pm u_j$ on overlaps. This follows from the compact-support [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) and vanishing above dimension n. The overlap graph is connected, so the group is cyclic. Coherent local [orientations](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) give $\mathbb Z$; an orientation-reversing loop gives $\mathbb Z/2$.
