# Connected locally path-connected spaces are path-connected

↑ **Parent:** [Locally path-connected space](locally-path-connected-space.md)

In a [locally path-connected space](locally-path-connected-space.md), every [path component](path-component.md) is [open](open-set.md): a [path-connected](path-connected-space.md) open neighbourhood of a point lies within that point's [path component](path-component.md). The complement of a [path component](path-component.md) is a union of the other [open](open-set.md) [path components](path-component.md), hence is also [open](open-set.md). In a nonempty [connected space](connected-space.md), a nonempty subset that is both [open](open-set.md) and [closed](closed-set.md) must be the whole space. Thus a [connected space](connected-space.md) that is [locally path-connected](locally-path-connected-space.md) is [path-connected](path-connected-space.md).
