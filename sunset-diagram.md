# Sunset diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A sunset diagram is a two-point diagram with two interaction vertices joined by three internal propagators. Its dependence on the external momentum produces [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) in scalar quartic theory.
