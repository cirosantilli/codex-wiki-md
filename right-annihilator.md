# Right annihilator

↑ **Parent:** [Annihilator (ring theory)](annihilator-ring-theory.md)

The right annihilator of a subset $X$ of a [ring](ring.md) is the [right ideal](right-ideal.md) of elements killed on the left by every member of $X$. Its sidedness matters for a [noncommutative ring](noncommutative-ring.md).
