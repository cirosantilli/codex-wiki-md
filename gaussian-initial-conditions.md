# Gaussian initial conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Standard perturbation theory in cosmology](standard-perturbation-theory-in-cosmology.md)

Gaussian initial conditions make every connected primordial correlation function above second order vanish. [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md) then reduces perturbative late-time correlation functions to sums of products of the linear power spectrum.
