# Blasius force theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md)

For a steady two-dimensional [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) and [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) around an impermeable body, the displayed contour formula gives the [force](force-split.md) on the body per unit span. Here $W'=u-iv$ is the [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md), $\rho$ is the constant [mass density](density.md), and $C$ runs counterclockwise around the body, excluding other fluid singularities. To verify the sign, [Bernoulli's principle](bernoulli-s-principle.md) gives $p=p_0-\rho|q|^2/2$. On a counterclockwise boundary, the complex pressure force is $d(X-iY)=-ip\,d\bar z$. Tangency gives $q\,dz=\bar q\,d\bar z$, and therefore $|q|^2d\bar z=q^2dz$. Integrating removes the constant $p_0$ term and gives the formula. The theorem is named after [Heinrich Blasius](paul-richard-heinrich-blasius.md).
