# Morse index

↑ **Parent:** [Morse function](morse-function.md)

The Morse index of a [nondegenerate critical point](nondegenerate-critical-point.md) is the number of negative directions of its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md), counted with multiplicity. Equivalently, it is the maximal dimension of a subspace on which the second variation is negative definite. The same definition applies to a finite-index critical point of an infinite-dimensional energy.
