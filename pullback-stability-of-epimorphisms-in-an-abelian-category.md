# Pullback stability of epimorphisms in an abelian category

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian category](abelian-category.md)

In an [abelian category](abelian-category.md), the [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md) of an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md) $p:B\to C$ along $k:C'\to C$ is the kernel of the epimorphism $[p,-k]:B\oplus C'\to C$. If $v:C'\to D$ annihilates its projection, $[0,v]$ annihilates that kernel and therefore factors through $[p,-k]$. Restriction to $B$ forces the factor to vanish, giving $v=0$ and proving the projection epic. This uses the definition's normality of epimorphisms.
