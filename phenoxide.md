# Phenoxide

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

[Phenoxide](phenoxide.md) is the anion formed by deprotonating [phenol](phenol.md). Its [oxygen](oxygen.md) is an effective nucleophilic site, while conjugation distributes some [electron](electron.md) density into the [aromatic ring](aromatic-ring.md).
