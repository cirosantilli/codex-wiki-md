# Conductor of a Dirichlet character

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md)

The least divisor of a character's modulus through whose unit-group reduction the [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) factors. The [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) reduces its construction to choosing the least exponent at each [prime](prime-number.md). Surjectivity of reduction on units makes the descended [primitive Dirichlet character](primitive-dirichlet-character.md) unique.
