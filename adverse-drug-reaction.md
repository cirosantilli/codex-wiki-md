# Adverse drug reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology](pharmacology-split.md)

A harmful or unintended response attributed to a medicine under specified conditions. Temporal association alone does not establish drug causation; disease, concomitant [treatments](treatment.md) and contextual influences must also be assessed.
