# Sullivan minimal model

↑ **Parent:** [Rational homotopy theory](rational-homotopy-theory.md)

A free graded-commutative rational [differential graded algebra](differential-graded-algebra.md) with decomposable differentials and a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md) to the rational polynomial forms of a space. For [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) finite-type spaces, $V^i$ is dual to $\pi_i(X)\otimes\mathbb Q$. A proposed finite model must have its [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) and representing [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md) checked; matching a list of relations alone is insufficient.
