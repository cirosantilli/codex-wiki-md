# Empathy

↑ **Parent:** [Social cognition](social-cognition.md)

Understanding or sharing another person's state while maintaining a [self-other distinction](self-other-distinction.md). [Affective empathy](affective-empathy.md), [cognitive empathy](cognitive-empathy.md), [compassion](compassion.md) and [personal distress](personal-distress.md) should be separated when operationalizing the construct: neural evidence for one component does not automatically explain the others.
