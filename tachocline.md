# Tachocline

↑ **Parent:** [Sun](sun.md)

The solar tachocline is the rotational transition near the base of the convection zone, approximately at $0.7$ solar radii, between differential rotation above and more nearly uniform rotation in the radiative interior. Its shear can contribute to the [Omega effect](omega-effect.md), and stable stratification can help store strong [toroidal magnetic fields](toroidal-magnetic-field.md). It is a proposed site for important parts of the [solar dynamo](solar-dynamo.md), rather than a demonstrated unique location for all field generation.
