# Gamma point

↑ **Parent:** [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md)

The [Gamma point](gamma-point.md) is zero crystal wavevector, modulo reciprocal-lattice vectors. A [direct band gap](direct-band-gap.md) at this point allows a low-energy vertical optical transition with negligible photon momentum. Crystal symmetry constrains the degeneracies and symmetry labels of the band-edge states.
