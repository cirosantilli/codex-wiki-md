# Reduced expression in a Coxeter group

↑ **Parent:** [Coxeter system](coxeter-system.md)

A [reduced expression in a Coxeter group](reduced-expression-in-a-coxeter-group.md) has the least possible [Coxeter length](coxeter-length.md) among expressions in the generators of its [Coxeter system](coxeter-system.md).

A reduced expression for $w\in W$ is a product of the fewest possible simple generators representing $w$. The number of factors is its [Coxeter length](coxeter-length.md) $\ell(w)$.
