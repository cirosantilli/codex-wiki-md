# Trace of an elliptic-curve endomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md)

For $\phi\in\operatorname{End}(E)$, its trace is the integer characterized by

$$
\phi+\widehat\phi=[\operatorname{tr}(\phi)].
$$

Equivalently, $\operatorname{tr}(\phi)=1+\deg\phi-\deg(1-\phi)$.
