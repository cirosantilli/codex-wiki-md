# Integral-field spectrograph volume scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Integral field spectrograph](integral-field-spectrograph.md)

With fixed [spectral resolving power](spectral-resolving-power.md), [wavelength](wavelength.md) interval, [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md) geometry, [photodetector](photodetector.md) sampling and [collimator](collimator.md) [focal ratio](focal-ratio.md), a beam [diameter](diameter.md) scales as the [spaxel](spaxel.md) width $\theta$, but the [optical camera](optical-camera.md) [focal length](focal-length.md) stays fixed. A simple on-axis beam-envelope model therefore gives [collimator](collimator.md) and disperser volumes proportional to $\theta^3$ and [optical camera](optical-camera.md) volume proportional to $\theta^2$. With a fixed number of [spaxels](spaxel.md) per [photodetector](photodetector.md), replication multiplies these volumes by $M\propto\theta^{-2}$. A fixed [photodetector](photodetector.md) field adds transverse clearances and changes the small-$\theta$ limit; these exponents are not universal mechanical-volume laws.
