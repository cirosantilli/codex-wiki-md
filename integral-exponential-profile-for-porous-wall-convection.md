# Integral exponential profile for porous wall convection

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly heated vertical plate in a porous medium](uniformly-heated-vertical-plate-in-a-porous-medium.md)

Assume an exponentially decreasing [temperature](temperature.md) profile while imposing wall heat flux, impermeability and the exact integrated heat budget. For $f=C(1-e^{-\eta/d})$, the wall condition gives $C=d^2$ and $\int(f')^2d\eta=1$ gives $d^3=2$. This yields the displayed integral approximation and discharge $\int wdy\simeq2^{2/3}\kappa z/\delta$. It satisfies the boundary conditions and exact flux budget, but not the differential equation pointwise; its mass-flux coefficient is approximate.
