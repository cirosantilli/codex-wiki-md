# Voigt profile

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line](spectral-line.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voigt_profile)

A Voigt profile is the convolution of a [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md) and a [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md). For an [absorption line](absorption-line.md), thermal [Doppler broadening](doppler-broadening.md) contributes the Gaussian core while finite excited-state lifetime contributes the Lorentzian wings. Instrumental broadening and unresolved motions must also be modeled when inferring [column density](column-density.md) and [temperature](temperature.md).
