# Gillespie algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic chemical kinetics](stochastic-chemical-kinetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillespie_algorithm)

The Gillespie algorithm samples an exact path of a well-mixed stochastic reaction network. At state $x$, let $a_0(x)=\sum_ra_r(x)$; draw a waiting time with [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of rate $a_0$, choose reaction $r$ with probability $a_r/a_0$, and update $x\leftarrow x+\nu_r$.
