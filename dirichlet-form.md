# Dirichlet form

↑ **Parent:** [Potential theory](potential-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_form)

A Dirichlet form on a real $L^2$ space is a densely defined closed nonnegative symmetric [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) with the Markov contraction property. In the real case, truncating $u$ to $(0\vee u)\wedge1$ keeps it in the form domain and cannot increase $\mathcal E(u,u)$. The [Dirichlet form of a Markov chain](dirichlet-form-of-a-markov-chain.md) is a discrete energy example; Laplacian energy on a region supplies a continuous example.
