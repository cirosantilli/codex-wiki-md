# Finite-azimuth Schwarzschild photon plunge

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial null orbit in Schwarzschild spacetime](equatorial-null-orbit-in-schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

For a captured nonradial [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) with $u=1/r$, the large-$u$ orbit equation is $(du/d\phi)^2\sim2\mu u^3$. Integration on the inward branch gives

$$
\phi_*-\phi\sim\sqrt{2/(\mu u)},\qquad
r\sim\frac\mu2(\phi_*-\phi)^2.
$$

The [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md) is reached at finite azimuth. This does not prevent a near-critical ray from winding repeatedly near the [photon sphere](photon-sphere.md) before its final plunge. A radial ray has constant azimuth, so this asymptotic coordinate parametrization is inapplicable to it.
