# Radius of gyration of a uniform sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md)

For a uniform solid [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $R$, the squared [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md) about its centre is the mass-weighted mean of $r^2$:

$$
R_g^2=\frac{\int_0^Rr^2\,4\pi\rho r^2dr}{4\pi\rho R^3/3}=\frac35R^2.
$$

Thus its volume is $(4\pi/3)(5/3)^{3/2}R_g^3$, about $9.01R_g^3$. A nonuniform object or a different shape need not obey this conversion. This distinction matters when estimating molecular [number density](number-density.md) from scattering measurements of a [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md).
