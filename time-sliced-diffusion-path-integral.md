# Time-sliced diffusion path integral

↑ **Parent:** [Path integral](path-integral.md)

For [diffusion](diffusion.md) constant $D>0$, Gaussian increments have [variance](variance-split.md) $2D\Delta t$. Their [convolution](convolution.md) gives a path weight proportional to $\exp[-\sum(\Delta x)^2/(4D\Delta t)]$ with measure factor $(4\pi D\Delta t)^{-M/2}$ for $M$ slices. The continuum kinetic [action](action.md) is notation for this limiting measure; typical Brownian paths have no ordinary derivative.
