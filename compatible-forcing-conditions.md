# Compatible forcing conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Forcing](forcing-split.md)

Two forcing conditions are compatible when they have a common stronger extension. In standard notation this means that some $r$ satisfies $r\leq p,q$; in [Jerusalem notation for forcing](jerusalem-notation-for-forcing.md) it means that some $r$ satisfies $p,q\leq r$.
