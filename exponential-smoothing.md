# Exponential smoothing

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponential_smoothing)

With a fixed gain $0<K<1$, exponential smoothing recursively combines the newest observation with the preceding estimate. Iteration assigns geometric weights $K(1-K)^j$ to older observations, together with a diminishing weight on the initial estimate. The [steady-state local-level Kalman gain](steady-state-local-level-kalman-gain.md) gives a probabilistic interpretation of this recursion.
