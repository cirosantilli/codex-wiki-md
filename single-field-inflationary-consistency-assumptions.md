# Single-field inflationary consistency assumptions

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)

The leading relations $r=16\epsilon_V$ and $n_s-1=-6\epsilon_V+2\eta_V$ assume a canonical single slow-roll scalar, Einstein gravity and the usual vacuum initial fluctuations. More general inflation models need not obey these relations.
