# Normal number

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_number)

A real number is normal in base $K\geq2$ if every word of $r$ digits occurs in its base-$K$ expansion with limiting overlapping frequency $K^{-r}$, for every $r\geq1$.
