# Molar mass distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

The distribution of [molar masses](molar-mass.md) among the chains in a [polymer](polymer-split.md) sample. Number-weighted and mass-weighted descriptions assign different relative weights to long chains: for populations $N_i$ of chains with [molar mass](molar-mass.md) $M_i$, the [number-average molar mass](number-average-molar-mass.md) is $M_n=\sum_iN_iM_i/\sum_iN_i$, while the [weight-average molar mass](weight-average-molar-mass.md) is $M_w=\sum_iN_iM_i^2/\sum_iN_iM_i$. A single average does not determine the full distribution.
