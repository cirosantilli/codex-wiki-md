# Cantelli inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev inequality](chebyshev-inequality.md)

For a finite-variance [random variable](random-variable-split.md), shift its centred version by $b>0$ and apply [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) to the square. Minimizing $(\sigma^2+b^2)/(a+b)^2$ at $b=\sigma^2/a$ proves the bound. A two-point distribution attains equality. If the [variance](variance-split.md) is zero, the centred variable vanishes almost surely.
