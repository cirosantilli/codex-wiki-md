# Lindeberg replacement method

↑ **Parent:** [Invariance principle for a low-degree multilinear polynomial](invariance-principle-for-a-low-degree-multilinear-polynomial.md)

The Lindeberg replacement method, inspired by the [Lindeberg condition](lindeberg-condition.md), compares functions of two independent random vectors by replacing their coordinates one at a time. Matching moments cancel the corresponding terms in a [Taylor expansion](taylor-theorem.md).
