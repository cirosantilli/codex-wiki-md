# Finite-cost optimal transport converse

↑ **Parent:** [C-cyclical monotonicity](c-cyclical-monotonicity.md)

An optimal [transport plan](transport-plan.md) of finite cost for a finite continuous cost [function](function-split.md) is [c-cyclically monotone](c-cyclical-monotonicity.md). A strict violation at finitely many support points persists in small product neighborhoods; subtracting small normalized pieces and inserting cyclically re-paired product [measures](measure.md) preserves both marginals and strictly improves the finite cost. The finite-value hypothesis matters: if every competitor has infinite cost, a nonmonotone plan may still be an extended-value minimizer.
