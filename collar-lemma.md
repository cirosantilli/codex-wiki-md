# Collar lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md)

A [simple closed curve](simple-closed-curve.md) that is a [geodesic](geodesic.md) of length $\ell$ on a [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md) has an embedded collar of half-width $w$ with $\sinh w\,\sinh(\ell/2)=1$. In circumference-one coordinates its metric is $dr^2+\ell^2\cosh^2r\,dt^2$, and its [conformal modulus of an annulus](conformal-modulus-of-an-annulus.md) is $(2/\ell)\arctan(\operatorname{csch}(\ell/2))\sim\pi/\ell$.
