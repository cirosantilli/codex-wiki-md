# Dry atmospheric Ertel potential vorticity

↑ **Parent:** [Ertel potential vorticity](ertel-potential-vorticity.md)

For dry atmospheric thermodynamics, choose [potential temperature](potential-temperature.md) as the materially conserved scalar in [Ertel's theorem](ertel-s-theorem.md). The displayed density-normalized scalar has units $\mathrm{K\,m^2\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$. Its conservation in adiabatic inviscid motion depends on conservative body forces and a composition-fixed equation of state. It differs in normalization from the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md) convention using buoyancy without the variable density factor. Large-scale balanced weather can be organized by its distribution, with [potential-vorticity inversion](potential-vorticity-inversion.md) additionally requiring balance and boundary information.
