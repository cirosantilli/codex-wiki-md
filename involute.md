# Involute

↑ **Parent:** [Plane curve](plane-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Involute)

An [involute](involute.md) is the curve traced by the end of a taut string as it is unwound from a fixed curve. For a [circle](circle.md) of radius $R$, one parametrization is $\mathbf x(\theta)=R(\cos\theta+\theta\sin\theta,\sin\theta-\theta\cos\theta)$. The straight free [length](length.md) is $R\theta$, and differentiation shows that the traced [velocity](velocity.md) is perpendicular to the taut string. Reversing the motion winds the string back around the circle.
