# Parametrized post-Newtonian light deflection

↑ **Parent:** [Parametrized post-Newtonian formalism](parametrized-post-newtonian-formalism.md)

The leading [Schwarzschild light deflection](schwarzschild-light-deflection.md) of a distant ray passing an isolated spherical [mass](mass.md) is $\alpha=2(1+\gamma_{\rm PPN})GM/(bc^2)$. Thus measurements of angular displacement can constrain $\gamma_{\rm PPN}$ rather than merely detect a nonzero [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md). In [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) this becomes the [Schwarzschild light deflection](schwarzschild-light-deflection.md) $4GM/(bc^2)$.
