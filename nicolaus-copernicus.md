# Nicolaus Copernicus

↑ **Parent:** [List of astronomers](list-of-astronomers.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus)

Astronomer (1473–1543) whose 1543 book placed Earth among planets orbiting the Sun. The [heliocentric model](heliocentric-model.md) altered the geometric organization of planetary motion.
