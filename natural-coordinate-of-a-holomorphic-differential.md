# Natural coordinate of a holomorphic differential

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic differential form](holomorphic-differential-form.md)

For a nonvanishing local $m$-differential $\phi(z)\,dz^m$, choose $w=\int\phi^{1/m}\,dz$. Then the differential is $dw^m$. Different root choices give $w\mapsto\zeta w+b$, $\zeta^m=1$. For $m=1,2$ the permitted linear parts are translations or signs, producing [translation surfaces](translation-surface.md) and [half-translation surfaces](half-translation-surface.md).
