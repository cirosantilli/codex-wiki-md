# Maximum principle

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_principle)

A maximum principle bounds a solution through its boundary or initial data, often forbidding nonconstant solutions from attaining an interior extremum. Weak and strong versions depend on the differential operator and hypotheses. The [maximum principle for harmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md) and its strong counterpart are examples.
