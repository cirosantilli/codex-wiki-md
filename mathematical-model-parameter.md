# Mathematical model parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical model](mathematical-model.md)

A [mathematical model parameter](mathematical-model-parameter.md) is a quantity held fixed while solving a particular instance of a model, but allowed to vary between instances. Changing a [parameter](mathematical-model-parameter.md) can change [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), their [stability](lyapunov-stability.md) and the qualitative behavior of trajectories. [Parameter](mathematical-model-parameter.md) constancy does not mean probabilistic independence.
