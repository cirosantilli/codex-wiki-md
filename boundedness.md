# Boundedness

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded set](bounded-set.md)

A [subset](subset.md) of a [metric space](metric-space.md) has boundedness when it lies in some ball of finite [radius](radius.md), equivalently when its [diameter](diameter.md) is finite. A [function](function-split.md) is bounded when its range is a [bounded set](bounded-set.md). For real-valued [functions](function-split.md), this means that $|f|$ has a finite upper bound.
