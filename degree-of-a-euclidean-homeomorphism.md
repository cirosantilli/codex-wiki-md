# Degree of a Euclidean homeomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a continuous mapping](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md)

A Euclidean homeomorphism is a [proper map](proper-map.md) and extends to its [one-point compactification](alexandroff-extension.md) $S^n$. Its degree is the [degree of a continuous mapping](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) of this extension, and belongs to $\{1,-1\}$. A linear isomorphism has degree equal to the sign of its determinant.
