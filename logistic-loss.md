# Logistic loss

↑ **Parent:** [Logistic regression](logistic-regression.md)

For a signed margin $u=yh(x)$, the logistic loss is

$$
\phi(u)=\log(1+e^{-u}).
$$

It is convex and has derivative $\phi'(u)=-e^{-u}/(1+e^{-u})$.
