# Ping-pong lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Free group](free-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ping-pong_lemma)

Suppose nontrivial [groups](group-split.md) $G_1,G_2$ act on a [set](set-split.md) and there are disjoint nonempty [subsets](subset.md) $X_1,X_2$ such that every nonidentity element of $G_1$ maps $X_2$ into $X_1$, while every nonidentity element of $G_2$ maps $X_1$ into $X_2$. Assume at least one factor has at least three elements. Then the subgroup generated by $G_1$ and $G_2$ is their [free product](free-product.md). The cardinality hypothesis excludes the counterexample in which $G_1=G_2=C_2$ acts by swapping $X_1$ and $X_2$. Versions allowing both factors to have order two need an additional separation hypothesis.
