# Epanechnikov kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel for density estimation](kernel-for-density-estimation.md)

The Epanechnikov [kernel for density estimation](kernel-for-density-estimation.md) is a nonnegative symmetric truncated parabola with integral one, $\mu_2(K_E)=1/5$, and $R(K_E)=3/5$. It has the smallest optimized [asymptotic mean integrated squared error](asymptotic-mean-integrated-squared-error.md) among nonnegative second-order density kernels, up to rescaling. Its [canonical kernel](canonical-kernel-for-density-estimation.md) has scale $15^{1/5}$ and support $[-15^{1/5},15^{1/5}]$.
