# Continuum many one-types of the integer ring

↑ **Parent:** [Type space](type-space.md)

For every [subset](subset.md) of the standard [prime numbers](prime-number-split.md), prescribe exactly which of those primes divide a nonzero element. Every finite prescription is realized by the product of its finitely many positive [divisibility](divisibility-split.md) requirements. [Compactness theorem](compactness-theorem.md) extends each prescription to a [complete one-type](complete-one-type.md) of the [complete theory](complete-theory.md) of the [integer](integer-split.md) [ring](ring.md). Different [subsets](subset.md) give incompatible [partial types](partial-type.md). The [countable](countable-set.md) [first-order language](first-order-language.md) supplies the matching upper bound.
