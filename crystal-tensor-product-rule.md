# Crystal tensor-product rule

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of crystals](tensor-product-of-crystals.md)

Write $\varepsilon_i(b)$ and $\varphi_i(b)$ for the numbers of raising and lowering steps of color $i$ available from a vertex of a [crystal basis](crystal-basis.md). One convention for the [tensor product of crystals](tensor-product-of-crystals.md) is

$$
\widetilde f_i(a\otimes b)=\begin{cases}\widetilde f_i(a)\otimes b,&\varphi_i(a)>\varepsilon_i(b),\\a\otimes\widetilde f_i(b),&\varphi_i(a)\le\varepsilon_i(b).\end{cases}
$$

The raising operator uses the first factor for $\varphi_i(a)\ge\varepsilon_i(b)$ and the second otherwise. The different strictness makes raising and lowering inverse along every edge. An edge is absent if the chosen [Kashiwara operator](kashiwara-operator.md) vanishes. Stating the convention is essential when identifying the [highest-weight vectors](highest-weight-vector.md) as ordered tensor vertices.
