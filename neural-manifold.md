# Neural manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Neural population state space](neural-population-state-space.md)

A proposed lower-dimensional set around which recorded population states are organized. The estimated dimension depends on sampling, noise, preprocessing and the scale of variation. A visually smooth embedding is not by itself proof of an intrinsic biological manifold.
