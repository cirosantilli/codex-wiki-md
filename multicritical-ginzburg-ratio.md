# Multicritical Ginzburg ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg criterion](ginzburg-criterion.md)

For a tuned [multicritical even Landau potential](multicritical-even-landau-potential.md) with leading positive $M^{2n}$ term, $M^2\sim|t|^{1/(n-1)}$ and $\xi\sim|t|^{-1/2}$. Long-wavelength Gaussian fluctuations in a [correlation volume](correlation-volume.md) have variance proportional to $\xi^{2-D}$. Their ratio to $M^2$ therefore scales as $|t|^{(D-2)/2-1/(n-1)}$, vanishing only for $D>2n/(n-1)$. At equality the criterion is marginal, requiring further fluctuation analysis.
