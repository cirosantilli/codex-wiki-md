# Linear least squares

↑ **Parent:** [Probability and statistics](probability-and-statistics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_least_squares)

[Linear least squares](linear-least-squares.md) minimizes $\|Ax-b\|^2$ over a [vector](vector.md) $x$. Its [normal equations for linear least squares](normal-equations-for-linear-least-squares.md) are $A^TAx=A^Tb$; geometrically $Ax$ is the orthogonal [projection](projection-linear-algebra.md) of $b$ onto the range of $A$.
