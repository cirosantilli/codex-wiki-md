# Triangle cover construction for Sunada surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Sunada theorem](sunada-theorem.md)

Let a finite [group](group-split.md) $T$ be generated by elements of exact orders $p,q$ whose product has exact order $r$, with $p^{-1}+q^{-1}+r^{-1}<1$. The epimorphism from the [hyperbolic triangle group](hyperbolic-triangle-group.md) sending its generators to these elements has torsion-free kernel. Its quotient of the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) is a closed surface with a $T$ action. A subgroup acts freely precisely when it meets no conjugate of a nonidentity power of the three elliptic generators. Free [almost conjugate subgroups](gassmann-equivalence.md) give [isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md) by the [Sunada theorem](sunada-theorem.md).
