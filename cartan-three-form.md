# Cartan three-form

↑ **Parent:** [Bi-invariant differential form](bi-invariant-differential-form.md)

An adjoint-invariant symmetric [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) $B$ on a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) makes the displayed tensor alternating. If $B$ is invariant under the full group [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), its left-translated three-form is also right-invariant. On left-invariant fields, the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) is $d\eta(W,X,Y,Z)=-2B(W,[X,[Y,Z]]+[Y,[Z,X]]+[Z,[X,Y]])=0$ by the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md). The [Killing form](killing-form.md) supplies such a $B$ on every semisimple [Lie group](lie-group.md). An overall sign change in the lowered structure-constant convention negates the form but not its invariance or closedness.
