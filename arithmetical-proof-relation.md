# Arithmetical proof relation

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md)

An [arithmetical proof relation](arithmetical-proof-relation.md) represents the effective relation that a [natural number](natural-number.md) encodes a [formal proof](formal-proof.md) of the [logical formula](logical-formula.md) with a specified [Gödel number](godel-number.md). With the usual coding apparatus its proof-checking relation is bounded. [Existential quantification](existential-quantification.md) over the [formal proof](formal-proof.md) code gives the ordinary [arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md).
