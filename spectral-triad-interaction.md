# Spectral triad interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Energy cascade](energy-cascade.md)

The quadratic nonlinear term of the [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md) couples three [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) whose [wave vectors](wavevector.md) close to a triangle. These [spectral triad interactions](spectral-triad-interaction.md) transfer [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) between wave numbers. A [turbulence closure](turbulence-closure.md) can model their triple correlations while retaining more scale information than a prescribed local power law.
