# Close orthogonal projections identify their image bundles

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of complex vector bundles by a Grassmannian](classification-of-complex-vector-bundles-by-a-grassmannian.md)

Let P,Q be continuous orthogonal projection matrices of equal rank on a common trivial [complex vector bundle](complex-vector-bundle.md), with the displayed bound at every base point. Restricting Q to the image of P is injective: if Qv=0, then $v=(P-Q)v$ contradicts the norm bound unless v=0. Equal dimensions give a continuously invertible [vector bundle isomorphism](vector-bundle-isomorphism.md). Compactness applies this observation uniformly to a homotopy of image planes.
