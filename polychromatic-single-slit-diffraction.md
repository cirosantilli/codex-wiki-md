# Polychromatic single-slit diffraction

↑ **Parent:** [Single-slit diffraction](single-slit-diffraction.md)

For mutually incoherent [wavelengths](wavelength.md), [intensities](intensity-physics.md) add rather than [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md): $I(\theta)=\int S(\lambda)\operatorname{sinc}^2[\pi a\sin\theta/\lambda]d\lambda$, where $\operatorname{sinc}q=\sin q/q$ and S includes source strength and detector response. All [wavelengths](wavelength.md) share the central maximum but their minima occur at different angles. A smooth white spectrum therefore has a white centre, wavelength-dependent edge colours, no common first dark minimum and increasingly overlapped, low-contrast outer fringes. A particular hue needs the actual spectrum and colour response.
