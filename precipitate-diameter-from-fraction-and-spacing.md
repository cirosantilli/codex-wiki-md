# Precipitate diameter from fraction and spacing

↑ **Parent:** [Precipitation hardening](precipitation-hardening.md)

For one spherical particle per representative volume $L^3$, a [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) $f$ implies $d=L(6f/\pi)^{1/3}$. A mean nearest-neighbor distance in a random population does not determine this [number density](number-density.md) exactly, and an atomic [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) fraction requires a molar-volume conversion before it is used as $f$.
