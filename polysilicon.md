# Polysilicon

↑ **Parent:** [Silicon](silicon.md)

Polycrystalline [silicon](silicon.md), containing many crystalline grains. Deposited [polysilicon](polysilicon.md) can form structural layers in [surface micromachining](surface-micromachining.md); grain structure, doping and residual stress influence its properties.
