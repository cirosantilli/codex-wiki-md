<h1 id="bresenham-s-line-algorithm">Bresenham's line algorithm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Raster graphics](raster-graphics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bresenham's_line_algorithm)

[Jack Bresenham](jack-elton-bresenham.md)'s [raster graphics](raster-graphics.md) [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) selects a thin sequence of [pixels](pixel.md) to represent a [line segment](line-segment.md) by exact incremental [integer arithmetic](integer-arithmetic.md). For a first-octant segment with $0\leq\Delta y\leq\Delta x$, a scaled [midpoint line decision](midpoint-line-decision.md) chooses between the next same-row and next higher-row [pixels](pixel.md).
