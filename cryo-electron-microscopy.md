# Cryo-electron microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md)

[cryo-electron microscopy](cryo-electron-microscopy.md) images specimens at cryogenic [temperature](temperature-split.md). In biological [single-particle analysis](single-particle-analysis.md), many weak projections of vitrified particles are combined to estimate a three-dimensional scattering [electrostatic potential](electric-potential.md) map; specimen heterogeneity and [radiation damage](radiation-damage.md) limit the recoverable detail.
