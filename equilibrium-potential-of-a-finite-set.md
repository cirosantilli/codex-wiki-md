# Equilibrium potential of a finite set

↑ **Parent:** [Transient graph](transient-graph.md)

For a finite vertex set $A$, its equilibrium potential is $h_A(x)=P_x(H_A<\infty)$. It equals one on $A$, is harmonic off $A$, and belongs to the [Dirichlet energy space with zero boundary at infinity](dirichlet-energy-space-with-zero-boundary-at-infinity.md).
