# Polymer entanglement

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

A polymer entanglement is a constraint on a chain's motion caused by the inability of neighbouring chain contours to pass through one another. It is not a permanent chemical [cross-link](cross-link.md). In a dense [polymer melt](polymer-melt.md), these constraints can create long relaxation times and a transient [rubbery plateau](rubbery-plateau.md). Chains eventually release constraints through mechanisms such as [reptation](reptation.md) and motion of their neighbours. Entanglement therefore strongly affects dynamics even when the equilibrium size of a long chain remains approximately that of an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md) because excluded-volume interactions are screened.
