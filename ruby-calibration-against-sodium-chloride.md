# Ruby calibration against sodium chloride

↑ **Parent:** [Ruby pressure scale](ruby-pressure-scale.md)

The 1975 experiment mixed powdered [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) and 0.5%-[chromium](chromium.md) [ruby](ruby.md) in a 4:1 volume ratio. An Inconel [gasket](gasket.md), initially $0.15\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick with a $0.3\,\mathrm{mm}$ hole, held the powder and 4:1 [methanol](methanol.md)–[ethanol](ethanol.md). At $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md) rings on film $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ away gave salt compression; a reference [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) assigned [pressure](pressure-split.md). After decreasing load, they waited at least five hours and bracketed diffraction exposures with $R_1$ readings to check drift.[pp. 2774–2775, Experimental Procedures](calibration-of-the-pressure-dependence-of-the-r1-ruby-fluorescence-line-to-195-kbar-by-gasper-j-piermarini-and-others.md)

The recommended linear slope was $2.740\,\mathrm{GPa\,nm^{-1}}$, with a $95\%$ fit [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) of $\pm0.016\,\mathrm{GPa\,nm^{-1}}$, to $19.5\,\mathrm{GPa}$. Reference-scale systematic [measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md) remained separate. Above about $10.4\,\mathrm{GPa}$ the medium was only quasihydrostatic; separate range fits checked consistency.[pp. 2775–2776, Table I and recommended calibration; p. 2778, pressure-distribution discussion](calibration-of-the-pressure-dependence-of-the-r1-ruby-fluorescence-line-to-195-kbar-by-gasper-j-piermarini-and-others.md)

The logical chain is compression to reference [pressure](pressure-split.md), paired with optical shift, then a fitted response. [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md) supplies the reference comparison during calibration; subsequent [ruby](ruby.md) readings transfer that scale to other samples. The reference [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) is therefore part of the meaning of the reported [pressure](pressure-split.md). Changing it can move all calibrated readings together, even if the optical measurements repeat perfectly. A [regression residual](regression-residual.md) tests agreement with the chosen reference within the sampled conditions; it cannot independently certify that reference.
