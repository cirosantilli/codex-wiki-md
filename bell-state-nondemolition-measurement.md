# Bell-state nondemolition measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Bell-basis measurement](bell-basis-measurement.md)

The [Bell states](bell-state-split.md) are joint [eigenstates](eigenstate.md) of commuting $Z_AZ_B$ and $X_AX_B$. Two shared [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) meter pairs realize their two [entanglement-assisted nondemolition parity measurements](entanglement-assisted-nondemolition-parity-measurement.md) using spacelike local circuits. Their projector products give the four rank-one [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) outcomes and preserve each input [Bell state](bell-state-split.md). The globally identified outcome requires later [local operations and classical communication](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md).
