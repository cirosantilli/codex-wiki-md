# Fragmentation barrier to dust growth

↑ **Parent:** [Protoplanetary dust coagulation](protoplanetary-dust-coagulation.md)

A regime in which collisions disrupt aggregates faster than sticking can build larger bodies. The outcome depends on collision speed, porosity and material properties; it is distinct from loss of intact aggregates through [radial drift of solids in a gas disk](radial-drift-of-solids-in-a-gas-disk.md).
