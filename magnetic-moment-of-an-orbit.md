# Magnetic moment of an orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic invariant](adiabatic-invariant.md)

For slow variation of the field compared with the gyration period, the [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) $m v_\perp^2/(2B)$ is approximately conserved. Thus transverse random [energy](energy-split.md) scales with $B$, and remains unchanged only when the relevant field strength is unchanged.
