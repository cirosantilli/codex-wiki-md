<h1 id="idempotent-ultrafilter-proof-of-hindman-s-theorem">Idempotent-ultrafilter proof of Hindman's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hindman theorem](hindman-theorem.md)

Choose an [idempotent ultrafilter on the natural numbers](idempotent-ultrafilter-on-the-natural-numbers.md) $\mathcal U$ and a color class $A\in\mathcal U$. The set

$$
A^*=\{x\in A:A-x\in\mathcal U\}
$$

also belongs to $\mathcal U$, and $A^*-x\in\mathcal U$ for every $x\in A^*$. Recursively choosing each new term from the finitely many required translates of $A^*$ puts every nonempty finite sum in $A$.
