# Returning geodesic in a tractoid annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Tractoid](tractoid.md)

In unwrapped upper-half-plane coordinates, the annulus $b\leq y\leq a$ on a [tractoid](tractoid.md) identifies $x$ modulo $2\pi$. Connect $(x_0,b)$ and $(x_0+2\pi,b)$ by the upper circular [hyperbolic geodesic](geodesic-in-the-poincare-half-plane-model.md) with center $(x_0+\pi,0)$. Its largest height is $\sqrt{b^2+\pi^2}$. The displayed condition puts the whole arc in the annulus. Under the angular identification its endpoints coincide, and the resulting [geodesic](geodesic.md) segment winds once before returning. Its terminal tangent generally differs from the initial tangent, so this construction does not give a smooth periodic [closed geodesic](closed-geodesic.md). It explains how local hyperbolic geometry creates a returning segment on a [surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md).
