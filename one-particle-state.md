# One-particle state

↑ **Parent:** [Fock space](fock-space.md)

A [one-particle state](one-particle-state.md) belongs to the one-excitation sector of a [Fock space](fock-space.md). In a momentum basis with $[a_{\mathbf p},a_{\mathbf q}^\dagger]=(2\pi)^3\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$, the wavepacket $\int d^3p\,f(\mathbf p)a_{\mathbf p}^\dagger|0\rangle/(2\pi)^3$ has norm squared $\int d^3p\,|f(\mathbf p)|^2/(2\pi)^3$. Sharp-momentum states have distributional rather than unit normalization; factors of $\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf p}}$ convert to [relativistic normalization of a one-particle state](relativistic-normalization-of-a-one-particle-state.md).
