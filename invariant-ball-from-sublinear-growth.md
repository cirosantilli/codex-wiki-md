# Invariant ball from sublinear growth

↑ **Parent:** [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md)

A [continuous](continuous-function.md) Euclidean self-map with this growth has a [fixed point](fixed-point.md). Choose $R\ge(2A)^{1/(1-\alpha)}$ and $R\ge2B$. Then $|f(x)|\le R$ whenever $|x|\le R$, so the closed radius-$R$ ball is invariant and [Brouwer](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) applies. Existence does not imply convergence of iterating the original map: $x\mapsto1-x$ on the unit [interval](interval-mathematics.md) has a [fixed point](fixed-point.md) but a two-cycle from either endpoint.
