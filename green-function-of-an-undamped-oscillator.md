# Green function of an undamped oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function-split.md)

For zero initial value and slope, the causal [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) is $G_0(x,s)=H(x-s)\sin[k(x-s)]/k$, with $k\ne0$ and $H$ the [Heaviside step function](heaviside-step-function.md). For zero values at endpoints $a<b$, put $\kappa=|k|$. The [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md) is

$$
G_D(x,s)=-\frac{\sin[\kappa(x_<-a)]\sin[\kappa(b-x_>)]}{\kappa\sin[\kappa(b-a)]}.
$$

Both are continuous at the source and have a first-derivative jump of one, giving $L_xG=\delta(x-s)$. The Dirichlet inverse exists when $\sin[\kappa(b-a)]\ne0$; a zero denominator signals a nontrivial homogeneous mode and a [Fredholm solvability condition](fredholm-solvability-condition.md). The endpoint kernel is the zero-conjugation case of the [Dirichlet Green function for a conjugated harmonic oscillator](dirichlet-green-function-for-a-conjugated-harmonic-oscillator.md). Boundary conditions determine which kernel must be integrated against a forcing term.
