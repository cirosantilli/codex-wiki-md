# Legendre dual cone barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithmically homogeneous barrier](logarithmically-homogeneous-barrier.md)

For a [logarithmically homogeneous barrier](logarithmically-homogeneous-barrier.md) on a [proper cone](proper-cone.md), the [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md) with reversed argument defines a barrier on the interior [dual cone](dual-cone.md). The inverse gradient relation makes $y=-\mu\nabla F(s)$ equivalent to $s=-\mu\nabla F_\dagger(y)$. This gives a joint primal-dual [central path](central-path.md) without assuming the barrier is identical on a self-dual cone.
