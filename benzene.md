# Benzene

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[Benzene](benzene.md) has a planar ring of six [carbon](carbon.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), each bearing one [hydrogen](hydrogen.md). Its $\pi$ [electron density](electron-density.md) is distributed over the ring; the alternating [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md) in one [resonance structure](resonance-structure.md) do not describe alternating actual [bond lengths](bond-length.md). [McMurry: benzene structure](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/15-2-structure-and-stability-of-benzene)
