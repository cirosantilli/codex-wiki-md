# Subadditive sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

A real sequence $(a_n)$ is subadditive when $a_{m+n}\leq a_m+a_n$ for all positive $m,n$. [Fekete lemma](fekete-s-lemma.md) gives

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_n}{n}=\inf_{n\geq1}\frac{a_n}{n},
$$

allowing the value negative infinity.
