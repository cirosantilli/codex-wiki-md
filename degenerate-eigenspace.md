# Degenerate eigenspace

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenspace](eigenspace.md)

An [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) greater than one. In [degenerate perturbation theory](degenerate-perturbation-theory.md), first-order [energy](energy-split.md) corrections are the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the perturbing [linear operator](linear-operator.md) projected onto the whole unperturbed [eigenspace](eigenspace.md).
