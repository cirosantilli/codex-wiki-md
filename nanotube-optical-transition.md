# Nanotube optical transition

↑ **Parent:** [Single-walled carbon nanotube](single-walled-carbon-nanotube.md)

An optical excitation between nanotube electronic subbands, often labelled $E_{ii}$ for prominent axial-polarization transitions. One-dimensional [Van Hove singularities](van-hove-singularity.md) enhance the [joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md), but [selection rules](selection-rule.md), [oscillator strengths](oscillator-strength.md) and strong [excitons](exciton.md) interactions determine actual absorption peaks. [Dielectric](dielectric.md) environment, bundling, doping and defects can change those peaks.
