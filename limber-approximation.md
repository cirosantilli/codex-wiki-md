# Limber approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological perturbation theory](linear-cosmological-perturbation-theory-split.md)

For fields projected along the line of sight, the [Limber approximation](limber-approximation.md) replaces the oscillatory radial [Spherical Bessel function](spherical-bessel-function.md) coupling by a local radial contribution with $k\chi\simeq\ell+1/2$. The displayed [cross-power spectrum](cross-spectrum.md) formula applies to $u(\hat{\mathbf n})=\int d\chi\,q_u(\chi)U(\chi\hat{\mathbf n},z)$ and similarly for $v$, in a spatially flat background. It requires radial kernels and the three-dimensional [cross-power spectrum](cross-spectrum.md) to vary slowly across the radial coupling width. It suppresses correlations of disjoint radial kernels but can fail at low multipoles or for very narrow selections; exact unequal-time correlations can remain nonzero there.
