# Countable-state Markov chain ergodic theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Positive recurrent Markov chain](positive-recurrent-markov-chain.md)

For an [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md) on a countable [state space](state-space.md) with [positive recurrence](positive-recurrent-markov-chain.md) and [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) $\pi$, the displayed [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md) holds from every initial state whenever $\sum_i\pi_i|f(i)|<\infty$. In particular, the empirical fraction of visits to $i$ converges to $\pi_i$. No [aperiodicity](aperiodic-markov-chain.md) is needed. Regeneration at successive returns to a state reduces the result to the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) for independent excursion lengths and rewards. This extends the finite-state [Markov chain ergodic theorem](markov-chain-ergodic-theorem.md).
