# Meridional ray bend threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Step-index optical fiber](step-index-optical-fiber.md)

For a [light ray](light-ray.md) launched on the centreline in the plane of a circular [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md) bend, let $R$ be centreline radius, $r$ core radius and $\beta$ the internal launch [angle](angle.md) from the tangent. The straight segment's distance from the bend centre is $R\cos\beta$. Thus its outer-wall [angle of incidence](angle-of-incidence.md) satisfies $\sin i=R\cos\beta/(R+r)$. Equating this to $n_2/n_1$ gives the limiting [total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md) threshold in the title, provided $n_1\cos\beta>n_2$. Radially displaced or nonplanar launches need different geometry; this is not a universal wave-optical loss cutoff.
