# Balanced-splitter twin-Fock output distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Beam splitter scattering matrix](beam-splitter-scattering-matrix.md)

For two identical bosonic input populations of $N$ in a balanced two-mode mixer, only even occupancies occur at either output. Expanding $[(b_1^\dagger)^2-(b_2^\dagger)^2]^N$ and retaining normalized number-state factorials gives the displayed law. Its sum is one by the [generating function](generating-function.md) $(1-4z)^{-1/2}(1-4z)^{-1/2}=(1-4z)^{-1}$.
