# Electron-phonon scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-phonon interaction](electron-phonon-interaction.md)

In [electron-phonon scattering](electron-phonon-scattering.md), an [electron](electron.md) changes its energy by $\pm\hbar\omega_{\mathbf q}$ while its [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) changes by a phonon wavevector modulo a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector. Charge-transport relaxation weights a scattering angle $\theta$ by $1-\cos\theta$. Small-angle events can therefore relax heat efficiently while relaxing electrical current weakly. At temperatures above the phonon excitation scale, scattering becomes approximately elastic in the electron thermal shell; residual elastic disorder often dominates at very low temperature.
