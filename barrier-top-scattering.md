# Barrier-top scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md)

At energy equal to a finite rectangular barrier height, the stationary interior [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) has a linear solution $\psi=bx+c$. Its [probability current](probability-current.md) can be nonzero: $J=(\hbar/m)\operatorname{Im}(c^*b)$. Setting the interior classical kinetic energy to zero does not set the transmitted quantum flux to zero.
