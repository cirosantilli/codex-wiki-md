# Ricci scalar

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

The Ricci scalar is the full metric contraction of the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md):

$$
R=g^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu}.
$$
