# Orientation interval for transient energy growth

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional triangular model of transient growth](two-dimensional-triangular-model-of-transient-growth.md)

For $L=\begin{pmatrix}-1/R&1\\0&-1/(4R)\end{pmatrix}$ and $R>1$, write $\rho=x_2/x_1$. The energy derivative is $x_1^2[-1/R+\rho-\rho^2/(4R)]$, positive exactly between the displayed orientations. Linear trajectories cross the sector from $\rho^+$ to $\rho^-$ because $\dot\rho=\rho[3/(4R)-\rho]$. An [energy-neutral rotational nonlinearity](energy-neutral-rotational-nonlinearity.md) can reverse the crossing direction; the growth sector itself remains the same.
