# Split-coequalizer lifting for a fixed-point-domain operation

↑ **Parent:** [Free extension of nested partial unary operations](free-extension-of-nested-partial-unary-operations.md)

In the [nested partial unary operation category](nested-partial-unary-operation-category.md), suppose the lower-operation diagram $A\rightrightarrows B\xrightarrow{q}Q$ has a [split coequalizer](split-coequalizer.md) with $qs=1$, $ft=1$ and $gt=sq$. The section $s$ takes eligible [fixed points](fixed-point.md) to eligible fixed points, so the displayed next operation is defined on exactly the required domain. For eligible $x\in B$, the equations give

$$
q\beta_B(x)=qf\beta_A(tx)=qg\beta_A(tx)=q\beta_B(sqx).
$$

Thus $q$ preserves the next operation. Every factored [morphism](morphism.md) preserves it by evaluating at $sz$, giving creation of the specified [coequalizer](coequalizer.md). Together with reflection of [isomorphisms](isomorphism.md), the [Beck monadicity theorem](beck-s-monadicity-theorem.md) proves one-step monadicity.
