# Translational zero mode of a sine-Gordon kink

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon kink fluctuation operator](sine-gordon-kink-fluctuation-operator.md)

The normalized [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) $\psi_0(x)=\operatorname{sech}x/\sqrt2$ has zero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) under the [Sine-Gordon kink fluctuation operator](sine-gordon-kink-fluctuation-operator.md). It is proportional to $\partial_x\phi_K$ and comes from shifting the [collective coordinate](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md) of the [kink](scalar-field-kink.md). Its frequency is zero, so it contributes no oscillator [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md); it must be handled separately from a Gaussian [functional determinant](functional-determinant.md).
