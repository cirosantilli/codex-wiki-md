# Adiabatic damping of geometric emittance

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric emittance](geometric-emittance.md)

For slowly accelerated paraxial motion, $z'\simeq p_z/p_s$ decreases as longitudinal [momentum](momentum-split.md) grows while transverse canonical [phase-space](phase-space.md) area is conserved. The [normalized emittance](normalized-emittance.md) stays constant in the ideal limit, so the [geometric emittance](geometric-emittance.md) scales inversely with longitudinal [momentum](momentum-split.md). This is kinematic damping rather than dissipation of canonical area.
