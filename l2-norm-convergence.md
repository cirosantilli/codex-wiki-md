# L2 norm convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Norm convergence](norm-convergence.md)

A sequence in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) converges in its [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) when $\int|f_n-f|^2\,d\mu\to0$. This concerns the integral of the squared error and need not imply [pointwise convergence](pointwise-convergence.md). In particular a complete [Fourier sine basis](fourier-sine-basis.md) reconstructs each odd [L2 function](square-integrable-function.md) in this norm, while pointwise claims about its [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) require additional hypotheses.
