# Pro-p completion

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)

The [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) of the quotients of an abstract [group](group-split.md) by its [normal subgroups](normal-subgroup.md) of finite $p$-power index. Maps to [pro-p groups](pro-p-group.md) factor uniquely through the canonical dense image in this completion. Unlike [profinite completion](profinite-completion.md), only [finite p-groups](finite-p-group.md) are used.
