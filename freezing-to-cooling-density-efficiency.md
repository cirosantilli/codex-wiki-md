# Freezing-to-cooling density efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation of salt during freezing](conservation-of-salt-during-freezing.md)

For equal heat extraction, the density increase caused by [brine rejection](brine-rejection.md) can exceed that caused by cooling near the freezing point because the thermal density response is weak. Using [conservation of salt during freezing](conservation-of-salt-during-freezing.md), the ratio is $R=(\partial\rho/\partial S)S_0f_i/[(1-f_i)|\partial\rho/\partial T|\Delta T]$, with $f_i=c_{p,w}\Delta T/L_f$. This is a parcel comparison, not a guarantee that freezing overcomes any ambient [density stratification](density-stratification.md).
