# First adiabatic invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic invariant](adiabatic-invariant.md)

The magnetic-moment invariant of rapid [cyclotron motion](gyromotion.md) in slowly varying fields. Here the per-unit-[mass](mass.md) convention is used: the physical magnetic moment is $m\mu$. In [drift kinetics](drift-kinetics.md), using $(\mu,v_\parallel)$ as coordinates removes the leading perpendicular acceleration and leaves a [velocity-space measure](velocity-space-measure.md) proportional to the exact [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) strength.
