# Finite Coxeter group

↑ **Parent:** [Coxeter group](coxeter-group.md)

A finite Coxeter group is a [Coxeter group](coxeter-group.md) with finitely many elements. Its [Coxeter Gram matrix](coxeter-gram-matrix.md) is positive definite.
