# Indecomposable projectives of S3 in characteristic three

↑ **Parent:** [Block of S3 in characteristic three](block-of-s3-in-characteristic-three.md)

With $s=(12)$, the [idempotents](idempotent.md) $e_\pm=(1\pm s)/2$ split the right regular [module](module-mathematics.md) into two three-dimensional [projective modules](projective-module.md). Their tops are respectively the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) and [sign representation](sign-representation.md). The [nilpotent ideal](nilpotent-ideal.md) $J(kS_3)$ ensures that any nonzero [direct summand](direct-summand.md) has nonzero top, so the one-dimensional tops make these [projective modules](projective-module.md) indecomposable. Their successive [radical series of a module](radical-series-of-a-module.md) factors are trivial, sign, trivial and sign, trivial, sign. This decomposition concerns right [modules](module-mathematics.md); the [block of S3 in characteristic three](block-of-s3-in-characteristic-three.md) remains a single [algebra](algebra-split.md).
