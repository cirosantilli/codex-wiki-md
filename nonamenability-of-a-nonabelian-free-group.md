# Nonamenability of a nonabelian free group

↑ **Parent:** [Amenable group](amenable-group.md)

The [free group](free-group.md) $F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$ is not an [amenable group](amenable-group.md). Partitioning reduced words by their first letters gives two translated decompositions of $F_2$ whose invariance equations add to $2=1$, contradicting the existence of an [invariant mean](invariant-mean.md).
