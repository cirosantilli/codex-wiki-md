<h1 id="gelfand-tsetlin-algebra">Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory of the symmetric group](representation-theory-of-the-symmetric-group-split.md)

For the chain $S_0\subset\cdots\subset S_n$, the Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra in $\mathbb C S_n$ is generated by the centers of $\mathbb C S_r$ for $0\leq r\leq n$. [Multiplicity-free restriction](multiplicity-free-restriction.md) makes its joint eigenspaces one-dimensional in each [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md). Products of the central [primitive idempotents](primitive-idempotent.md) along restriction paths give its minimal projections. Thus it is a [maximal commutative subalgebra](maximal-commutative-subalgebra.md) and a [semisimple algebra](semisimple-algebra.md), isomorphic to a finite product of copies of $\mathbb C$.
