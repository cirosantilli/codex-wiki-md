# Root-space decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md)

For a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak t$, a semisimple [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) decomposes as

$$
\mathfrak g=\mathfrak t\oplus\bigoplus_{\alpha\in R}\mathfrak g_\alpha,
\qquad
\mathfrak g_\alpha=\{x:[h,x]=\alpha(h)x\text{ for every }h\in\mathfrak t\}.
$$

The nonzero functionals $\alpha$ are the roots.
