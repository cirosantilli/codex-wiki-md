# Thin-accretion-disk truncation error

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion disk](accretion-disk.md)

For smooth radial variation on scale $r$, $c_s\sim\Omega H$, $\nu\sim\alpha\Omega H^2$, $u_r\sim\nu/r$ and $u_z\sim Hu_r/r$. The exact point-mass vertical gravity differs from $\Omega_K^2z$ by relative order $(H/r)^2$. Radial [pressure](pressure.md) support changes the rotation and dominant shear heating by the same order. Meridional viscous forces, radial radiative-flux divergence, thermal [advection](advection.md) and compressional work are at most order $(H/r)^2$ relative to their retained vertical terms for $\alpha\lesssim1$. These estimates require secular disk evolution and exclude sharp radial [boundary layers](boundary-layer.md) or independently rapid vertical motion.
