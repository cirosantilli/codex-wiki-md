# Polybasic region

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

A [polybasic region](polybasic-region.md) is a sequence enriched in basic, usually positively charged residues. In a membrane-targeting sequence, it can cooperate with a lipid anchor to promote association with negatively charged [lipids](lipid.md).
