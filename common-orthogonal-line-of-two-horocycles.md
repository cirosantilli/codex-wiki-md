# Common orthogonal line of two horocycles

↑ **Parent:** [Horocycle](horocycle.md)

Two [horocycles](horocycle.md) have a unique common orthogonal [hyperbolic line](hyperbolic-line.md) when their [ideal centres of horocycles](ideal-centre-of-a-horocycle.md) differ: it is the line with those two [ideal endpoints](ideal-endpoint.md). If the centres agree, an [isometry](isometry.md) puts both [horocycles](horocycle.md) on horizontal lines; every vertical [hyperbolic line](hyperbolic-line.md) meets both orthogonally, so there are infinitely many. This classification remains valid for intersecting or tangent [horocycles](horocycle.md).
