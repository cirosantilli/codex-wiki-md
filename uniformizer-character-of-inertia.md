# Uniformizer character of inertia

↑ **Parent:** [Tame inertia quotient](tame-inertia-quotient.md)

For a finite Galois extension of non-Archimedean [local fields](local-field.md), this is a homomorphism from the [inertia group](inertia-group.md) to $k_L^\times$ with kernel the [wild inertia group](wild-inertia-group.md). Inertia acts trivially on residue units, which proves multiplicativity and independence of the chosen [uniformizer](uniformizer.md). Passing to the maximal unramified subextension reduces the kernel calculation to a totally ramified extension. Hence it embeds the [tame inertia quotient](tame-inertia-quotient.md) into the cyclic multiplicative group of the residue field.
