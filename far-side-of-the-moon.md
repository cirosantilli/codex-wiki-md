# Far side of the Moon

↑ **Parent:** [Moon](moon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_side_of_the_Moon)

The [far side of the Moon](far-side-of-the-moon.md) is the hemisphere directed approximately away from [Earth](earth.md). “Far” describes the direction to [Earth](earth.md); it does not describe illumination by the [Sun](sun.md). The [far side of the Moon](far-side-of-the-moon.md) experiences both sunlight and darkness during the [lunar phase](lunar-phase.md) cycle. [NASA: Moon facts, orbit and rotation](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/).
