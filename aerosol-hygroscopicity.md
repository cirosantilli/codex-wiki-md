# Aerosol hygroscopicity

↑ **Parent:** [Aerosol](aerosol.md)

A particle's tendency to take up [water vapor](water-vapor.md), which depends on composition, solubility and ambient humidity. In a common parameterization, a dimensionless $\kappa$ summarizes soluble-water uptake for use with dry diameter in [Köhler theory](kohler-theory.md). A single composition parameter does not remove the importance of particle size or parcel supersaturation.
