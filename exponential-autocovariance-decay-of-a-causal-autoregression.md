# Exponential autocovariance decay of a causal autoregression

↑ **Parent:** [Causality root criterion for an autoregressive model](causality-root-criterion-for-an-autoregressive-model.md)

The inverse autoregressive polynomial is an [analytic function](space-of-holomorphic-functions.md) on a disk larger than the unit disk. The [Cauchy estimate](cauchy-estimate.md) gives exponentially decaying coefficients of its [infinite moving-average representation](infinite-moving-average-representation.md). Summing their products gives $|\gamma(h)|\leq Cs^{|h|}$ for some $s\in(0,1)$.
