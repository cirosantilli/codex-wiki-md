# Elastic rod

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic filament](elastic-filament.md)

An [elastic rod](elastic-rod.md) is a slender elastic body represented by a centreline and an attached orthonormal director frame. Its [elastic energy](elastic-energy.md) penalizes stretching, bending and twisting relative to a preferred configuration. The [director kinematics of a rod](director-kinematics-of-a-rod.md) describe changes of the frame along the centreline. An unshearable [elastic rod](elastic-rod.md) has its third director parallel to the centreline [tangent vector](tangent-vector-split.md).
