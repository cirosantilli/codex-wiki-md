# Tafel slope

↑ **Parent:** [Tafel equation](tafel-equation.md)

The change in [overpotential](overpotential.md) per decade of [current density](current-density.md) in the activation-controlled regime of a [Tafel equation](tafel-equation.md). In a single-step [Butler-Volmer equation](butler-volmer-equation.md) with constant transfer coefficient, the anodic slope is $b_a=RT\ln10/(\alpha_a nF)$, where $T$ is [temperature](temperature-split.md), $R$ is the [molar gas constant](molar-gas-constant.md), $n$ is the number of transferred [electrons](electron.md) and $F$ is the [Faraday constant](faraday-constant.md). The magnitude of the cathodic slope is $b_c=RT\ln10/(\alpha_c nF)$. Inferring one from the other requires the model's relation between the two coefficients; the equality $\alpha_a+\alpha_c=1$ applies to this effective single-step model and need not describe a multistep reaction.
