# Hafnium

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical element](chemical-element.md)

[Hafnium](hafnium.md) is the heavier group-4 congener of [zirconium](zirconium.md). Changes in alkyl-bond energetics can change both propagation and chain-transfer rates in metallocene [polymerization](polymerization.md).
