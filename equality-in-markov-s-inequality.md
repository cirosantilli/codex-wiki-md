<h1 id="equality-in-markov-s-inequality">Equality in Markov's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Markov's inequality](markov-s-inequality.md)

For a finite-[expected value](expected-value-split.md) nonnegative [random variable](random-variable-split.md) and $a>0$, equality in the uncapped [Markov's inequality](markov-s-inequality.md) holds exactly when $Y$ takes only $0$ and $a$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). Indeed the nonnegative difference $Y-a\mathbf1_{\{Y\geq a\}}$ has zero [expected value](expected-value-split.md) exactly when it vanishes [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). Giving $a$ [probability](probability.md) $p$ yields $\mathbb E Y=pa$ and $\mathbb P(Y\geq a)=p$. This shows the bound cannot generally be reduced using only the nonnegative [random variable](random-variable-split.md)'s [expected value](expected-value-split.md).
