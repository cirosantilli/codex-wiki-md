# Dirac delta multiplication identity

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplication of a distribution by a smooth function](multiplication-of-a-distribution-by-a-smooth-function.md)

For smooth $a$ and a point $r$,

$$
a\delta_r=a(r)\delta_r,
\qquad a\delta'_r=a(r)\delta'_r-a'(r)\delta_r.
$$

These follow by applying both sides to a [test function](test-function.md) and using the [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md). In particular $(x-r)\delta'_r=-\delta_r$.
