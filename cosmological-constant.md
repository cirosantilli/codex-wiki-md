# Cosmological constant

↑ **Parent:** [Dark energy](dark-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmological_constant)

The cosmological constant $\Lambda$ is the constant term proportional to the metric in the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md). Interpreted as vacuum energy, it has $P_\Lambda=-\rho_\Lambda$, so its energy density is constant during expansion.
