# Wavefunction normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Wave function](wave-function.md)

Wavefunction normalization makes the total [Born rule](born-rule.md) probability equal to one. In an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md), $\psi=\sum_n c_n\phi_n$ is normalized precisely when $\sum_n|c_n|^2=1$.

For a proposed [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) mode $A\sin(n\pi x/L)$, the [integral](integral.md) of its squared [modulus](modulus.md) is $|A|^2L/2$. Thus [wavefunction normalization](wavefunction-normalization.md) requires $|A|=\sqrt{2/L}$; choosing $A$ real and positive fixes a convenient [global phase](global-phase.md). For the equal [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md) $(\phi_1+\phi_2)/\sqrt2$, [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) makes the integrated cross term zero, leaving $1/2+1/2=1$. The cross term can nevertheless be nonzero locally: normalization constrains total probability, not the shape of its [probability density](probability-density.md).
