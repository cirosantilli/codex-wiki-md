# Wavefunction normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Wave function](wave-function.md)

Wavefunction normalization makes the total [Born rule](born-rule.md) probability equal to one. In an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md), $\psi=\sum_n c_n\phi_n$ is normalized precisely when $\sum_n|c_n|^2=1$.
