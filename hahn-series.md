# Hahn series

↑ **Parent:** [Non-Archimedean analysis](non-archimedean-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hahn_series)

A [Hahn series](hahn-series.md) over a [field](field.md) $k$ with exponents in an ordered abelian group $\Gamma$ is a formal sum $\sum_{\gamma}a_\gamma t^\gamma$ whose nonzero exponents form a well-ordered set. Such series form the [Hahn series field](hahn-series-field.md).
