# Power-map group action obstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action-split.md)

Suppose a [binary operation](binary-operation.md) on the underlying [set](set-split.md) $\mathbb Z$ makes it a [group](group-split.md), and $\rho(n,x)=x^n$ is a [group action](group-action-split.md) on another [group](group-split.md) $G$. Every acting element must induce a [bijection](bijection.md), with [inverse function](inverse-function.md) given by its acting [inverse element](inverse-element.md). The integer $0$ instead induces the constant [function](function-split.md) $x\mapsto e$, where $e$ is the [identity element](identity-element.md) of $G$, so $G$ must be the [trivial group](trivial-group.md). Conversely, on the [trivial group](trivial-group.md), ordinary [addition](addition.md) on $\mathbb Z$ supplies such a [group action](group-action-split.md).
