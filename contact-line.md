# Contact line

↑ **Parent:** [Wetting](wetting.md)

The line where the interfaces of three phases meet, for example where a liquid film meets a solid and the surrounding gas. Continuum assumptions at this line can differ from the bulk [lubrication approximation](lubrication-theory.md).
