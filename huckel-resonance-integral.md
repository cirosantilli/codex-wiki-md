<h1 id="huckel-resonance-integral">Hückel resonance integral</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hückel method](huckel-method.md)

An off-diagonal [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) [matrix](matrix.md) element between selected neighbouring pi-basis functions. The usual bonding convention has $\beta<0$; non-neighbour [matrix](matrix.md) elements are neglected.
