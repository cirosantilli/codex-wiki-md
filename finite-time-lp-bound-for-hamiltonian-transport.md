# Finite-time Lp bound for Hamiltonian transport

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian Liouville equation](hamiltonian-liouville-equation.md)

The [Duhamel formula for Hamiltonian transport](duhamel-formula-for-hamiltonian-transport.md), [phase space](phase-space.md) preservation and [Minkowski integral inequality](minkowski-integral-inequality.md) give the displayed bound. A time-independent source in an [Lp space](lp-space.md) yields finite space-time norm on every bounded interval; it need not yield integrability on infinite time.
