# Lorentz-covariant rank-two loop reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md)

With an invariant integration measure and no external vector in $f(q^2)$, the rank-two integral must be proportional to the metric. Contracting its indices fixes the coefficient to $1/d$. In [dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md) this dimension is the continued $d$, rather than four; retaining its dependence matters when multiplying a divergent loop integral.
