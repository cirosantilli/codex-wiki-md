<h1 id="hadamard-s-lemma">Hadamard's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Differentiation under the integral sign](differentiation-under-the-integral-sign.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamard's_lemma)

On a star-shaped neighbourhood of $a$, a [smooth function](smooth-function.md) satisfies

$$
f(x)-f(a)=\sum_i(x_i-a_i)g_i(x),\qquad g_i(x)=\int_0^1\partial_i f(a+t(x-a))\,dt.
$$

The [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) along the segment proves the identity, and [differentiation under the integral sign](differentiation-under-the-integral-sign.md) proves smoothness of the coefficients.
