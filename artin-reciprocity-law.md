# Artin reciprocity law

↑ **Parent:** [Artin symbol](artin-symbol.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artin_reciprocity_law)

The Artin map sends an ideal prime to the ramified primes to the product of its Frobenius elements. For a suitable modulus, its kernel is the norm or congruence subgroup associated with the abelian extension.
