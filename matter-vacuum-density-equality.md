# Matter-vacuum density equality

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md)

For conserved [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) and a true [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), equality follows by comparing the matter density proportional to $(1+z)^3$ with the constant vacuum density. This is not the onset of accelerated expansion: in a matter-plus-vacuum model acceleration starts at $\rho_m=2\rho_\Lambda$.
