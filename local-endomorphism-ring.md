# Local endomorphism ring

↑ **Parent:** [Endomorphism ring](endomorphism-ring.md)

An [endomorphism ring](endomorphism-ring.md) is local when its nonunits form a proper two-sided [ideal](ideal.md); commutativity is not required. The [Fitting lemma](fitting-lemma.md) gives this property for an indecomposable finite-length [module](module-mathematics.md). If two nonunits had an invertible sum, multiplication by its inverse would give $I=f+g$ with noninvertible $f,g$. Fitting makes $f$ nilpotent, so $I-f=g$ would be invertible, a contradiction. Products with a nonunit are nonunits by finite-length injectivity/surjectivity. The quotient by the nonunit ideal is a [division ring](division-ring.md).
