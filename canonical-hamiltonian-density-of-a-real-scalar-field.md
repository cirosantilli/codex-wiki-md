# Canonical Hamiltonian density of a real scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian density](hamiltonian-density.md)

For a [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) with [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) $\mathcal L=\dot\phi^2/2-|\nabla\phi|^2/2-V(\phi)$, the [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) is $\pi=\dot\phi$. Its [Hamiltonian density](hamiltonian-density.md) is the sum of momentum energy, gradient energy and potential energy. Hamilton's equations recover the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md).
