# Debye sound speed

↑ **Parent:** [Debye model](debye-model.md)

The effective isotropic acoustic speed used to replace separate branches in the [Debye model](debye-model.md). With atomic number density $n$, counting three branches gives $k_D=(6\pi^2n)^{1/3}$ and $\omega_D=v_Dk_D=k_B\theta_D/\hbar$. Matching the low-frequency [density of states](density-of-states.md) for one longitudinal and two transverse branches gives the displayed inverse-cube average. A [Debye temperature](debye-temperature.md) alone does not determine both individual [sound speeds](speed-of-sound.md).
