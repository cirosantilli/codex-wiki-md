# Concave biconjugate

↑ **Parent:** [Lower conjugate](lower-conjugate.md)

The [concave biconjugate](concave-biconjugate.md) is an upper envelope of $F$, and equals $F$ for a proper closed [concave function](concave-function.md) under the usual duality hypotheses. It equals $-(-F)^{**}$ in terms of the ordinary [biconjugate](biconjugate.md). If $e=DF_*(\tau)$, differentiability and concavity of $F_*$ imply $F_*(\tau)+F_{**}(e)=\tau:e$. For a finite differentiable dual the equality follows because $\tau$ minimizes $s:e-F_*(s)$.
