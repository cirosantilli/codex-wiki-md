# Primitive exterior square

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic contraction of an exterior square](symplectic-contraction-of-an-exterior-square.md)

The primitive exterior square of a [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) is the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of its [symplectic contraction of an exterior square](symplectic-contraction-of-an-exterior-square.md). In [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) four, the invariant inverse-form bivector $\Omega$ has nonzero wedge square. Choose $\operatorname{vol}=\Omega\wedge\Omega/2$; the [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) $\xi\wedge\eta=B(\xi,\eta)\operatorname{vol}$ is [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) on $\Lambda^2V$. The primitive subspace is $\Omega^\perp$, and therefore inherits a nondegenerate form of dimension five. The [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md) acts on it by infinitesimal [orthogonal transformations](orthogonal-transformation.md).
