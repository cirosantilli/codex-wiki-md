# Semi-infinite Eady model

↑ **Parent:** [Eady model](eady-model.md)

A uniformly stratified [quasi-geostrophic approximation](quasi-geostrophic-approximation.md) fluid with constant vertical shear occupies $z>0$, has a flat rigid lower wall, and has decaying perturbations aloft. On an [f-plane](f-plane.md), the basic interior [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) is constant. Its smooth edge-wave modes have zero interior anomaly and propagate along the lower-boundary [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) gradient. The absence of an upper wall removes the pair of interacting edge waves that makes the finite-depth [Eady model](eady-model.md) unstable.
