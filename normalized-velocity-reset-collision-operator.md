# Normalized velocity-reset collision operator

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Boltzmann equation](linear-boltzmann-equation.md)

Let $M\geq0$ have velocity [integral](integral.md) one, and $\rho(g)(x)=\int g(x,v)dv$. On phase-space $L^1$, the normalized relaxation collision operator is $B=P-I$, where $Pg=\rho(g)M$. It obeys $\|B\|\leq2$ because $\|Pg\|_1\leq\|g\|_1$. It preserves mass at each spatial position. The corresponding [linear Boltzmann equation](linear-boltzmann-equation.md) combines this bounded collision operator with the [free-transport semigroup](free-transport-semigroup.md).
