# Alkenyl aluminate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

An [alkenyl aluminate](alkenyl-aluminate.md) has an anionic aluminium-containing unit attached to an [alkene](alkene.md) [carbon](carbon.md). Intramolecular [oxygen](oxygen.md) coordination can close a five-membered ring in directed [propargylic alcohol](propargylic-alcohol.md) reductions.
