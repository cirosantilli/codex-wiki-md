# Low-temperature Gibbs free-energy expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Third law of thermodynamics](third-law-of-thermodynamics.md)

At fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md) and material content, $S=-(\partial G/\partial T)_p$. If the equilibrium [entropy](entropy-split.md) tends to zero, the [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) approaches its zero-[temperature](temperature-split.md) value with zero slope. When $C_p=aT^r+o(T^r)$ with $r>0$ and zero residual [entropy](entropy-split.md), integrating $dS=C_p\,dT/T$ gives $S=(a/r)T^r+o(T^r)$ and $G=G(0,p)-aT^{r+1}/[r(r+1)]+o(T^{r+1})$. Nonzero residual [entropy](entropy-split.md) $S_0$ instead contributes $-S_0T$ to $G$; a horizontal limiting tangent requires the zero-[entropy](entropy-split.md) hypothesis.
