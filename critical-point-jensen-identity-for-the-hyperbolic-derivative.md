# Critical-point Jensen identity for the hyperbolic derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic characteristic of a disc map](hyperbolic-characteristic-of-a-disc-map.md)

For $g'(0)\ne0$, apply [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md) with the smooth curvature term to the [hyperbolic derivative density](hyperbolic-derivative-density.md). Its distributional [Laplacian](laplacian.md) is $H_g^2+2\pi\sum m_a\delta_a$, giving the displayed identity with [ramification points](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) defined by $g'(a)=0$. If $g'(z)=cz^m+\cdots$ at zero, use $m\log R$ as the origin counting term and replace the base constant by $\log\lim_{z\to0}H_g(z)/|z|^m$. Without this regularization the basepoint-critical formula is undefined.
