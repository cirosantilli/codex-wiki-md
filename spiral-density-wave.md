# Spiral density wave

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic disk](galactic-disk.md)

A collective perturbation of [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) and [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) in a [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md). Material can cross its spiral-shaped crests; the pattern angular speed need not equal the local material angular speed. A tightly wound fluid approximation has $(\omega-m\Omega)^2=\kappa^2+c_s^2k^2-2\pi G\Sigma|k|$, while a collisionless stellar disk requires its own response function.
