# Gravure printing

↑ **Parent:** [Thin film](thin-film.md)

Transfer of liquid from patterned recesses in a printing surface onto a substrate. It can repeatedly coat or pattern a moving flexible web. Ink rheology, cell filling, transfer, [wetting](wetting.md) and drying determine resolution and film uniformity.
