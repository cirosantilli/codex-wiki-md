# Diffeomorphism Noether identity for a scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Noether identity](noether-identity.md)

Simultaneously varying a [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) and a [scalar field](scalar-field.md) by the [Lie derivative](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) along a compactly supported [vector field](vector-field.md) leaves a covariant matter action invariant. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives the displayed [Noether identity](noether-identity.md). It implies [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) when the scalar equation holds without needing to impose the metric field equation.
