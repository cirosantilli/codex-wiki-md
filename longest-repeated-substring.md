# Longest repeated substring

↑ **Parent:** [String-searching algorithm](string-searching-algorithm.md)

A longest repeated substring is a maximum-length contiguous [string](string.md) occurring at two distinct starting positions in a text. The occurrences may overlap unless a nonoverlap constraint is explicitly imposed. It can be found by comparing all pairs of suffixes, or by taking the maximum longest-common-prefix length of adjacent entries of a [suffix array](suffix-array.md). The latter characterization concerns correctness; the time needed to build the [suffix array](suffix-array.md) depends on the construction algorithm.
