# Symplectic reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian group action](hamiltonian-group-action.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_reduction)

For a [Hamiltonian group action](hamiltonian-group-action.md), a [regular value](regular-value.md) level preserved by a free proper [group action](group-action.md) has a smooth quotient. The restricted [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) descends to a [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) on that quotient when the kernel is exactly the orbit directions; this is symplectic reduction.
