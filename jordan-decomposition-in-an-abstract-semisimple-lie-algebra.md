# Jordan decomposition in an abstract semisimple Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)

A complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) has an intrinsic [Jordan–Chevalley decomposition](jordan-chevalley-decomposition.md) $x=x_s+x_n$ characterized by $[x_s,x_n]=0$, a diagonalizable [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) operator $\operatorname{ad}x_s$, and a nilpotent [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) operator $\operatorname{ad}x_n$. The [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is faithful because the centre is zero. Closure of [semisimple matrix Lie algebras are closed under additive Jordan decomposition](semisimple-matrix-lie-algebras-are-closed-under-additive-jordan-decomposition.md) applied to this faithful representation supplies the parts and their uniqueness. For every finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) $\rho$, the operators $\rho(x_s)$ and $\rho(x_n)$ are the semisimple and nilpotent parts of $\rho(x)$. Thus the decomposition is independent of a matrix embedding; for a nonfaithful representation it passes to the semisimple quotient by its kernel.
