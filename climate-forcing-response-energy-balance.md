# Climate forcing-response energy balance

↑ **Parent:** [Climate feedback](climate-feedback.md)

With positive-downward net top-of-atmosphere flux N, imposed forcing F and positive stabilizing coefficient lambda, a simple global model has $N=F-\lambda\Delta T=C\,d\Delta T/dt$. A positive imbalance means energy storage, not an independent measurement of F. Ocean uptake and multiple response times complicate the one-capacity approximation. Feedback sign conventions differ between references; writing the full equation prevents confusion.
