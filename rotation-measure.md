# Rotation measure

↑ **Parent:** [Faraday effect](faraday-effect.md)

For the high-frequency [Faraday effect](faraday-effect.md) in a cold [plasma](plasma-physics.md), the rotation measure is the coefficient in $\Delta\phi=\lambda^2\mathrm{RM}$. It is a density-weighted line integral of the line-of-sight [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md); the coefficient $a_0$ depends on the units. A sign convention for the line of sight fixes its sign. Correlations across an extended source can constrain a foreground [magnetic energy spectrum](magnetic-energy-spectrum.md), subject to statistical assumptions.
