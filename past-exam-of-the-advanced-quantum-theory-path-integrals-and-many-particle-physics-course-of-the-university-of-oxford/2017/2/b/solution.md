<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Holstein–Primakoff transformation](../../../../holstein-primakoff-transformation.md) realizes a [spin algebra](../../../../spin-commutation-relations.md) using [bosonic creation operators](../../../../bosonic-creation-operator.md) and [bosonic annihilation operators](../../../../bosonic-annihilation-operator.md). A [boson](../../../../boson.md) counts a unit of [spin](../../../../spin.md) deviation from the local positive quantization axis: $\widetilde S^z=S-n$ with $n=a^\dagger a$. The square root in $\widetilde S^+$ supplies the correct [spin-ladder](../../../../spin-ladder-operator.md) [matrix elements](../../../../matrix-element.md). In particular,

$$
\widetilde S^+|n\rangle=\sqrt{n(2S-n+1)}|n-1\rangle,\qquad
\widetilde S^-|n\rangle=\sqrt{(n+1)(2S-n)}|n+1\rangle.
$$

Their [commutator](../../../../commutator.md) is $2(S-n)$ on every physical [number state](../../../../fock-state.md), reproducing the [Spin commutation relations](../../../../spin-commutation-relations.md). Although the [oscillator Fock space](../../../../bosonic-fock-space.md) is infinite, the [physical subspace](../../../../holstein-primakoff-occupation-constraint.md) is only

$$
\boxed{n=0,1,\ldots,2S.}
$$

The order of the square root and oscillator [operator](../../../../linear-operator.md) matters in obtaining these coefficients.

When the [spin deviations](../../../../magnon.md) are dilute, expand the square root in $n/(2S)$. The leading transverse [operators](../../../../linear-operator.md) are linear in the [bosonic creation operators](../../../../bosonic-creation-operator.md) and [bosonic annihilation operators](../../../../bosonic-annihilation-operator.md) and turn [exchange interactions](../../../../exchange-interaction.md) into a [quadratic Hamiltonian](../../../../quadratic-bosonic-hamiltonian.md), which can be diagonalized. Higher powers describe interactions between [spin waves](../../../../spin-wave.md). The method therefore organizes corrections by inverse powers of $S$ about an ordered classical configuration. Large $S$ alone is insufficient: the average [depletion](../../../../one-dimensional-planar-spin-wave-depletion.md) must remain small compared with $S$. Possible problems include the [occupation](../../../../bosonic-occupation-number.md) constraint after truncation, noncollinear order requiring site-dependent axis [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), [zero modes](../../../../zero-mode.md), and [infrared fluctuations](../../../../infrared-divergence.md) that destroy the assumed dilute expansion.
