<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume $Q\ge2$ and $L\ge2$; the trivial exceptions are described below. In this [periodic Potts chain](../../../../../periodic-potts-chain.md), every satisfied bond contributes $-J$ to the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), and no bond can contribute less. All $L$ bonds can be satisfied simultaneously by choosing the same state at every site. Conversely, satisfying every bond forces this uniform configuration. Therefore the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [degeneracy factor](../../../../../degeneracy-factor.md) are

$$
\boxed{E_0=-JL,\qquad d_0=Q.}
$$

The $Q$ choices are distinct configurations on the labelled lattice: a global relabelling of the states is a [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md), not an identification of microstates.
