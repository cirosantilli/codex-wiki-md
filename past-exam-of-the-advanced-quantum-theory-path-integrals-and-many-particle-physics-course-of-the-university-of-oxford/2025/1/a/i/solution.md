<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each neighboring pair of [Ising spins](../../../../../ising-spin-variable.md) contributes at least $-J$ to the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), with equality exactly when the two [Ising spins](../../../../../ising-spin-variable.md) are opposite. For even $N$, alternation closes consistently under the [periodic boundary conditions](../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md). The [ground states](../../../../../ground-state.md) are therefore

$$
S_j=\sigma(-1)^{j-1},\qquad \sigma\in\{+1,-1\},
\qquad \boxed{E_0=-JN,\quad g_0=2.}
$$

Here $g_0$ is the [ground-state degeneracy](../../../../../ground-state-degeneracy.md). Choosing $S_1$ fixes every other [Ising spin](../../../../../ising-spin-variable.md), so these are all the [ground states](../../../../../ground-state.md); translations do not give additional [spin configurations](../../../../../spin-configuration.md). The two [ground states](../../../../../ground-state.md) are exchanged by [spin inversion symmetry](../../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md).
