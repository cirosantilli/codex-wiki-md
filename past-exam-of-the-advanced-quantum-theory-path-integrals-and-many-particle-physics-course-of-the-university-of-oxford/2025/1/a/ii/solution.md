<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $m$ count the parallel neighboring pairs, or [frustrated bonds in an antiferromagnetic Ising ring](../../../../../frustrated-bond-in-an-antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md). The [periodic boundary conditions](../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) imply

$$
\prod_{j=1}^N(S_jS_{j+1})=\prod_{j=1}^NS_j^2=1.
$$

There are $N-m$ antiparallel pairs, so $(-1)^{N-m}=1$: the number of antiparallel pairs must be even. For odd $N$, $m$ must consequently be odd. Each [frustrated bond in an antiferromagnetic Ising ring](../../../../../frustrated-bond-in-an-antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md) raises the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) by $2J$ relative to a satisfied pair, giving

$$
H=-JN+2Jm.
$$

The minimum is attained at $m=1$. Choose its position among the $N$ labeled bonds, choose either orientation of one [Ising spin](../../../../../ising-spin-variable.md), then alternate across every other bond. This constructs exactly two [spin configurations](../../../../../spin-configuration.md) per position. Thus

$$
\boxed{E_0=-J(N-2),\qquad g_0=2N\quad(N\text{ odd}).}
$$

This is [geometric frustration](../../../../../geometrical-frustration.md): every local interaction prefers opposite [Ising spins](../../../../../ising-spin-variable.md), but the closed odd ring prevents all preferences being satisfied at once. No particular location is preferred for the single [frustrated bond in an antiferromagnetic Ising ring](../../../../../frustrated-bond-in-an-antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md).
