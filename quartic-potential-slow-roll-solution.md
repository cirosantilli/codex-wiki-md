# Quartic-potential slow-roll solution

↑ **Parent:** [Slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md)

For $V(\phi)=\lambda\phi^4/4$ with $\lambda>0$, use [natural units](natural-units.md) and the unreduced [Planck mass](planck-mass.md) $M_P=G^{-1/2}$. The [slow-roll equations](slow-roll-approximation.md) give $H\simeq\sqrt{2\pi\lambda/3}\,\phi^2/M_P$ and $\dot\phi\simeq-M_P\sqrt{\lambda/(6\pi)}\,\phi$. Integrating gives the displayed exponential field decay, valid on either sign branch, and

$$
\frac{a(t)}{a_i}\simeq\exp\left[\frac{\pi}{M_P^2}(\phi_i^2-\phi(t)^2)\right].
$$

The [potential slow-roll parameters](potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) require $\phi^2\gg M_P^2$. The expression is valid only during [slow-roll inflation](slow-roll-approximation.md); its formal late-time saturation does not describe the subsequent [reheating](reheating.md) or radiation era.
