# Preferential attachment

↑ **Parent:** [Random graph](random-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preferential_attachment)

In preferential attachment, new [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) favour existing [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in proportion to a prescribed attractiveness, often their current [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md). A complete model must specify initial conditions, [self-loop](loop-graph-theory.md) conventions and the normalization at each attachment. These details matter for the [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) distribution of the earliest [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md).
