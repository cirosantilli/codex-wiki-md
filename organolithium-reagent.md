# Organolithium reagent

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

An [organolithium reagent](organolithium-reagent.md) contains a [carbon](carbon.md)–[lithium](lithium.md) interaction with substantial carbanionic [carbon](carbon.md) reactivity. Such reagents can act as bases or [nucleophiles](nucleophile.md), and their solution aggregation affects both roles.
