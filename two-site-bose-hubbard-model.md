# Two-site Bose-Hubbard model

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Hubbard model](bose-hubbard-model.md)

Two localized [orbitals](single-particle-orbital.md) coupled by [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) and on-site interactions give the smallest [Bose-Hubbard model](bose-hubbard-model.md). A symmetric [double-well potential](double-well-potential.md) with lowest one-particle [energies](energy-split.md) $E_0,E_1$ has $t=(E_1-E_0)/2$ and common diagonal [energy](energy-split.md) $(E_0+E_1)/2$. At fixed total [particle number](particle-number.md), that common diagonal term is constant. A [fixed-number two-mode condensate](fixed-number-two-mode-condensate.md) gives a simple [variational method](variational-method.md) for this model, while stronger interactions can require occupation-correlated trial [quantum states](quantum-state.md).
