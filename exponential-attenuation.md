# Exponential attenuation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear attenuation coefficient](linear-attenuation-coefficient.md)

The uncollided component decays exponentially when its removal probability per unit path length is constant. Scattered contributions require a separate transport treatment or a specified [radiation buildup factor](radiation-buildup-factor.md).
