# Normal component

↑ **Parent:** [Normal and tangential components](normal-and-tangential-components.md)

The normal component is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) of a [vector](vector.md) onto the local normal space. For a [hypersurface](hypersurface.md) with [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $n$, the [vector](vector.md) component is $(w\cdot n)n$; the signed scalar component is $w\cdot n$. These two meanings must be distinguished. With the [principal normal vector](principal-normal-vector.md) chosen toward the turning side of a [curve](curve.md), the normal scalar coefficient of [acceleration](acceleration.md) is $\kappa v^2\ge0$.
