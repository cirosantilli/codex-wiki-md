# Complete linear system of a divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

This is the full [linear system of divisors](linear-system-of-divisors.md), obtained by using every global section of the associated [line bundle](line-bundle.md). For a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) on an integral projective variety, the complete linear system $|D|$ consists of its effective representatives under [linear equivalence of Cartier divisors](linear-equivalence-of-cartier-divisors.md). A basis of $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X(D))$ defines a rational map to projective space.
