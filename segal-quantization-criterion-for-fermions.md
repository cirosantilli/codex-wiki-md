# Segal quantization criterion for fermions

↑ **Parent:** [Polarized fermionic Fock space](polarized-fermionic-fock-space.md)

For the irreducible representation of the [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) determined by $P$, a unitary one-particle map $u$ is unitarily implementable precisely when $P-uPu^*$ is [Hilbert-Schmidt](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md), equivalently when $[P,u]$ is Hilbert-Schmidt. The implementer is unique up to a scalar phase. The equivalent representation criterion for two polarizations is that their projections differ by a Hilbert-Schmidt operator. This yields a continuous [projective unitary representation](projective-unitary-representation.md) of the [restricted unitary group](restricted-unitary-group.md).
