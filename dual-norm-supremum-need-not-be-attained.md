# Dual norm supremum need not be attained

↑ **Parent:** [Dual norm](dual-norm.md)

On the real [space of sequences converging to zero](space-of-sequences-converging-to-zero.md) $c_0$, use the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) and define the [linear functional](linear-functional.md)

$$
p(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2^{-n}x_n.
$$

The [geometric series](geometric-series.md) gives $|p(x)|\le\|x\|_\infty$, so $p$ is a [bounded linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) and $\|p\|_*\le1$. The [sequence](sequence.md) with its first $N$ entries equal to one and all later entries zero belongs to $c_0$, has unit [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md), and gives $p(x)=1-2^{-N}$. Hence $\|p\|_*=1$. But if $\|x\|_\infty\le1$ and $x_n\to0$, not all coordinates can equal one or all equal minus one. Since every weight $2^{-n}$ is positive, $-1<p(x)<1$. The [supremum](supremum.md) is approached but never attained on the [unit ball](unit-ball.md).
