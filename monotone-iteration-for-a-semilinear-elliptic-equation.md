# Monotone iteration for a semilinear elliptic equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ordered subsolution and supersolution](ordered-subsolution-and-supersolution.md)

For smooth non-increasing $V$ and an [ordered subsolution and supersolution](ordered-subsolution-and-supersolution.md), begin with $u_0=\varphi^-$ and solve successive [Poisson equations](poisson-equation.md) with forcing $V(u_k)$ and the fixed boundary data. The [weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) applied to the negatives of consecutive differences gives

$$
\varphi^-\leq u_1\leq u_2\leq\cdots\leq\varphi^+.
$$

The [global Schauder estimate](global-schauder-estimate.md) and the [Hölder interpolation inequality](holder-interpolation-inequality.md) bound the iterates uniformly in $C^{2,\alpha}$. Their bounded [monotone sequence](monotone-sequence.md) converges, and [compact embedding of Hölder spaces](compact-embedding-of-holder-spaces.md) permits passing to the equation. The uniform [Hölder seminorm](holder-seminorm.md) bounds retain $C^{2,\alpha}$ regularity of the limit.
