# Cosmological Euler equation in synchronous gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronous gauge in cosmology](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md)

For scalar perturbations of a generic fluid in cosmic time, momentum conservation in [synchronous gauge in cosmology](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md) gives

$$
\delta P+\nabla^2\pi^S
+\partial_t[(\bar\rho+\bar P)\delta u]
+3H(\bar\rho+\bar P)\delta u=0.
$$
