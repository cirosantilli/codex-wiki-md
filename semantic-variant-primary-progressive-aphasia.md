# Semantic variant primary progressive aphasia

↑ **Parent:** [Primary progressive aphasia](primary-progressive-aphasia.md)

A [PPA](primary-progressive-aphasia.md) pattern prominently affecting word and conceptual meaning. Failure to understand a sentence can arise from missing word knowledge even if some grammatical operations remain available.
