# Eigenfunction of an absolutely one-homogeneous functional

↑ **Parent:** [Absolutely one-homogeneous functional](absolutely-one-homogeneous-functional.md)

A nonzero vector $f$ is an eigenfunction of an absolutely one-homogeneous convex functional $J$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$ when $\lambda f\in\partial J(f)$. This nonlinear analogue of an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) underlies exact shrinkage formulas for several variational flows and proximal maps.
