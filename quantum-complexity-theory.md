# Quantum complexity theory

↑ **Parent:** [Computational complexity theory](computational-complexity-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_complexity_theory)

Quantum complexity theory studies resources needed by [quantum circuits](quantum-circuit-split.md) and quantum algorithms, including time, workspace, and [quantum query complexity](quantum-query-complexity.md). Its models specify which input oracles and gate implementations are available; counting oracle calls is different from counting all elementary gates.
