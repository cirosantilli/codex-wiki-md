# Condensate fluctuation power counting

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov theory](bogoliubov-theory.md)

Expand a weakly depleted [Bose gas](bose-gas.md) around its macroscopically occupied mode. Replacing a condensate operator by an amplitude of order $\sqrt N$ gives quadratic, cubic and quartic fluctuation interaction scales proportional to $NN_{\rm ex}$, $\sqrt N N_{\rm ex}^{3/2}$ and $N_{\rm ex}^2$, with the same interaction-volume factor. Thus the depletion fraction supplies formal amplitude counting. This is not a uniform bound on infrared modes or a substitute for checking the interaction regime. At fixed [particle number](particle-number.md), expansion of the condensate interaction must precede the final quadratic truncation; the resulting cancellation is the [number-conserving Bogoliubov quadratic Hamiltonian](number-conserving-bogoliubov-quadratic-hamiltonian.md).
