# Central limit theorem for a geometrically ergodic Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric ergodicity](geometric-ergodicity.md)

For an aperiodic positive Harris recurrent [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) with [geometric ergodicity](geometric-ergodicity.md), an observable with stationary moment $\pi(|h|^{2+\delta})<\infty$ for some $\delta>0$ satisfies a [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md). Its asymptotic [variance](variance-split.md) is $\gamma_h(0)+2\sum_{k\geq1}\gamma_h(k)$, with stationary covariances. Ergodicity alone does not guarantee this theorem.
