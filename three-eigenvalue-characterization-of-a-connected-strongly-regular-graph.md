# Three-eigenvalue characterization of a connected strongly regular graph

↑ **Parent:** [Strongly regular graph](strongly-regular-graph.md)

A connected regular graph is strongly regular exactly when its adjacency matrix has three distinct eigenvalues, apart from the complete graphs, which have two. The forward direction follows from the [adjacency-matrix relation for a strongly regular graph](adjacency-matrix-relation-for-a-strongly-regular-graph.md); conversely, the quadratic polynomial vanishing on the two nonconstant eigenspaces is a scalar multiple of the [all-ones matrix](all-ones-matrix.md).
