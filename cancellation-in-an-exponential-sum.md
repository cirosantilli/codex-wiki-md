# Cancellation in an exponential sum

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential sum](exponential-sum.md)

Cancellation occurs when different [complex numbers](complex-number.md) in an [exponential sum](exponential-sum.md) have directions that reduce the [absolute value](absolute-value.md) of their [sum](sum.md). For example, the [orthogonality of roots of unity](orthogonality-of-roots-of-unity.md) makes $\sum_{a=0}^{p-1}e(a/p)=0$ for every [integer](integer.md) $p>1$, despite all terms having [absolute value](absolute-value.md) one.
