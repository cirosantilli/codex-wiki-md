# Double empathy problem

↑ **Parent:** [Social communication](social-communication.md)

A proposal associated with [Damian Milton](damian-milton.md) that mutual understanding can be difficult across different experiential perspectives. It treats interaction as reciprocal rather than assigning every failure to one participant.
