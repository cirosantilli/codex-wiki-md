# Canonical feature space of a positive-semidefinite kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Feature map](feature-map.md)

On the finite formal span of symbols $e_x$, define $B(e_x,e_y)=k(x,y)$ and extend bilinearly. For a [positive-semidefinite kernel](positive-semidefinite-kernel.md), the null vectors of this [positive semidefinite bilinear form](positive-semidefinite-bilinear-form.md) form its [radical of a bilinear form](radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md). Quotienting by that [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) gives an [inner product space](inner-product-space.md) and [feature map](feature-map.md) $x\mapsto[e_x]$ with $\langle[e_x],[e_y]\rangle=k(x,y)$. This construction works for degenerate and zero kernels.
