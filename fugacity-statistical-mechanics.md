# Fugacity (statistical mechanics)

↑ **Parent:** [Grand canonical ensemble](grand-canonical-ensemble.md)

The dimensionless [quantum gas fugacity](fugacity-statistical-mechanics.md) weights [particle number](particle-number.md) in the [grand canonical ensemble](grand-canonical-ensemble.md): a state with [energy](energy-split.md) $E$ and [particle number](particle-number.md) $N$ has weight $z^Ne^{-\beta E}$. The [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) and one-particle [energies](energy-split.md) must use the same reference. When the lowest one-particle [energy](energy-split.md) is zero, $z\ll1$ makes both [fermion](fermion.md) and [boson](boson.md) occupations small throughout the low-energy spectrum. This dimensionless quantity must be distinguished from the pressure-valued fugacity used in chemical thermodynamics.
