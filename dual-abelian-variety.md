# Dual abelian variety

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_abelian_variety)

The dual of an [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md) represents algebraically trivial [line bundles](line-bundle.md) rigidified at the origin. Its group law is [tensor product](tensor-product.md). A [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $f:A\to B$ induces $\widehat f:\widehat B\to\widehat A$ by pullback of these [line bundles](line-bundle.md).
