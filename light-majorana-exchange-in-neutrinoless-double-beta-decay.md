# Light-Majorana exchange in neutrinoless double beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrinoless double beta decay](neutrinoless-double-beta-decay.md)

Two charged-current beta vertices can be connected by a virtual [Majorana neutrino](majorana-neutrino.md). With light exchanged [masses](mass.md) relative to nuclear [momentum](momentum-split.md) scales, the [chirality](chirality-physics.md) connection makes the amplitude proportional to $\sum_iU_{ei}^2m_i$, not an incoherent [mass](mass.md) average. Squaring introduces the [effective Majorana mass](effective-majorana-mass.md), [nuclear matrix elements](nuclear-matrix-element.md) and [phase space](phase-space.md). Other lepton-number-violating operators can also mediate the same final state, so translating a rate into a [mass](mass.md) requires the stated mechanism.
