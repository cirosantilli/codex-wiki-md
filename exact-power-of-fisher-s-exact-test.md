<h1 id="exact-power-of-fisher-s-exact-test">Exact power of Fisher's exact test</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fisher's exact test](fisher-s-exact-test.md)

For two independent binomial counts, form the [Fisher exact test](fisher-s-exact-test.md) rejection region from their conditional null [hypergeometric distribution](hypergeometric-distribution.md). Summing alternative joint binomial [probabilities](probability.md) over that region gives its exact power. For equal group sizes, the null allocation is symmetric, so the two-sided [probability](probability.md)-ordering test agrees with doubled lower-tail [probabilities](probability.md). Conservative test discreteness can make a normal-approximation [sample size](sample-size.md) underpowered even when both samples are large but event counts are low.
