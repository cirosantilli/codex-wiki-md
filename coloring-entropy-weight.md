# Coloring entropy weight

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring.md)

For a uniformly chosen vertex $Z$, minimize the [information entropy](information-entropy.md) of its color over proper colorings. Multiply by the number of vertices to define this weight. Minimizing the color entropy gives the strongest contribution from that graph in the [entropy weight of a graph cover](entropy-weight-of-a-graph-cover.md) inequality.
