# Tangent cone to a theta divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann singularity theorem](riemann-singularity-theorem.md)

The [tangent cone to a theta divisor](tangent-cone-to-a-theta-divisor.md) at a degree-$g-1$ [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $L$ is cut out in $H^1(\mathcal O_X)$ by the [determinant](determinant.md) of $\xi\smile-:H^0(L)\to H^1(L)$. Under [Serre duality](serre-duality.md) its [matrix](matrix.md) entries are $\langle\xi,s_i t_j\rangle$ for bases of $H^0(L)$ and $H^0(K_X\otimes L^{-1})$. For $h^0(L)=1$, its tangent hyperplane is annihilated by the nonzero [holomorphic differential form](holomorphic-differential-form.md) $s_1t_1$.
