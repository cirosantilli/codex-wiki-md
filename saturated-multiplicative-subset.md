# Saturated multiplicative subset

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicatively closed set](multiplicatively-closed-set.md)

A [multiplicative subset](multiplicatively-closed-set.md) is saturated if every factor of a product in the subset also lies in it. We include $1$ in a [multiplicative subset](multiplicatively-closed-set.md). A saturated subset containing $0$ is the whole ring. The units form a saturated subset in a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md).
