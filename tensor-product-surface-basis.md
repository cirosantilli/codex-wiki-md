# Tensor-product surface basis

↑ **Parent:** [Parametric surface](parametric-surface.md)

Products of two univariate bases form a surface basis. Nonnegativity, [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) and coordinate-wise [linear precision](linear-precision-of-a-geometric-basis.md) pass to the products by multiplication of the respective univariate identities. If the factors are $C^r$ and $C^s$, each mixed [derivative](derivative.md) through orders $r,s$ is the product of the corresponding [derivatives](derivative.md), giving continuous mixed [derivatives](derivative.md) and joint $C^{\min(r,s)}$ regularity.
