# Pseudoreal representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A pseudoreal [complex representation](complex-representation.md) is isomorphic to its conjugate but has an invariant antilinear map squaring to minus the identity. Such [group representations](group-representation.md) admit antisymmetric invariant bilinear forms and can support half-[hypermultiplets](hypermultiplet.md) in four-dimensional [extended supersymmetry](extended-supersymmetry.md).
