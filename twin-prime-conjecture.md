# Twin prime conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [Twin prime](twin-prime.md)

The conjecture asserts that infinitely many [prime numbers](prime-number.md) $p$ have $p+2$ prime. The [Hardy–Littlewood twin-prime asymptotic conjecture](hardy-littlewood-twin-prime-asymptotic-conjecture.md) predicts their quantitative frequency and is stronger. The [Selberg sieve](selberg-sieve.md) supplies an upper bound of the predicted order but does not prove infinitude.
