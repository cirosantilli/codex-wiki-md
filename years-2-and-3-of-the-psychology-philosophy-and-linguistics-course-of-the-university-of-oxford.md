# Years 2 and 3 of the psychology, philosophy and linguistics course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Psychology, philosophy and linguistics course of the University of Oxford](psychology-philosophy-and-linguistics-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

Normally two subjects; the three-subject route requires college approval. The Psychology component includes core choices, Experimental design and methods, advanced options and practical training.
