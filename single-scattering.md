# Single scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md)

A detected wave scatters only once in the sample. Multiple scattering mixes paths and [scattering vectors](scattering-vector.md), invalidating a simple one-vector [dynamic light scattering](dynamic-light-scattering.md) decay.
