# BCS gap equation

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BCS_gap_equation)

At zero temperature and weak coupling, the BCS gap equation gives $\Delta\simeq2\Lambda e^{-1/(\nu_F|V|)}$. Its exponential scale agrees with the renormalization-group scale of the [Cooper instability](cooper-instability.md).
