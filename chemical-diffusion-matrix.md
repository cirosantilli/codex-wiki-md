# Chemical diffusion matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Linear noise approximation](linear-noise-approximation.md)

A reaction's [stoichiometric vector](stoichiometric-vector.md) outer product multiplied by its [reaction propensity function](reaction-propensity-function.md) is its contribution to the infinitesimal state-increment covariance. Summing over channels gives $B$. Its normalized form $D$ divides by the two mean copy numbers. The [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md) writes the associated second-derivative term with $B/2$; the covariance evolution and normalized stationary Lyapunov equation instead use $B$ and $D$ themselves. A simultaneous unit loss of two species contributes positive cross noise even when their long-time stationary covariance is negative.
