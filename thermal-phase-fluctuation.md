# Thermal phase fluctuation

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluid order-parameter effective theory](superfluid-order-parameter-effective-theory.md)

A thermal phase fluctuation is a finite-temperature fluctuation of the phase of a complex [order parameter](order-parameter.md). For a linearly dispersing phase mode, its infrared variance is proportional to $T\int d^dk/k^2$, which prevents true finite-temperature long-range order when $d\leq2$.
