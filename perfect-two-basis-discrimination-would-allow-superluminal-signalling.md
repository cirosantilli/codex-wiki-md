# Perfect two-basis discrimination would allow superluminal signalling

↑ **Parent:** [Remote preparation in conjugate bases](remote-preparation-in-conjugate-bases.md)

Suppose two orthonormal [qubit](qubit.md) bases have disjoint physical rays. Alice chooses which conjugate basis to measure on her half of a [Bell state](bell-state-split.md). A device that perfectly labels Bob's individual member of either basis would reveal Alice's choice without her outcome being communicated. Spacelike operation would therefore violate relativistic causality, even though ordinary [projective measurements](projective-measurement.md) see the same [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) $I/2$.
