# Higgsino anomaly cancellation

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal supersymmetric Standard Model](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md)

A single weak-doublet [higgsino](higgsino.md) of [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) $+1/2$ contributes $1/4$ to the cubic [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md), $1/4$ to the weak-squared [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) coefficient with fundamental index $1/2$, and $1$ to the [mixed gauge-gravitational anomaly](mixed-gauge-gravitational-anomaly.md). A second [Higgs chiral doublet](higgs-chiral-doublet.md) of opposite [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) cancels all three through its [higgsino](higgsino.md). The pair also contributes an even number of weak doublets, avoiding a new [Witten SU(2) anomaly](witten-su-2-anomaly.md). Higgs [complex scalar fields](complex-scalar-field.md) do not cancel chiral fermion [gauge anomalies](gauge-anomaly.md).
