# Local tidal acceleration scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md)

The [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md) equation makes the relative gravitational acceleration proportional to the separation $L$ and a typical [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) component $\mathcal R$. Thus tidal acceleration vanishes as $L\to0$ at fixed bounded curvature. A uniform [electric field](electric-field.md) can give different accelerations $qE/m$ at the same point, independently of separation. Relative tidal changes accumulated over a fixed long time need not vanish as a fraction of the initial separation; a local spacetime experiment must control its duration as well as its spatial size.
