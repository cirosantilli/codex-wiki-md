# Henkin theory

↑ **Parent:** [Henkin construction](henkin-construction.md)

A [Henkin theory](henkin-theory.md) has named witnesses for its provable [existential sentences](existential-sentence.md). In a [Henkin construction](henkin-construction.md), fresh constants and the corresponding [Henkin witness axioms](henkin-witness-axiom.md) give this witness property. Completeness or maximal consistency can be imposed in an additional extension step.
