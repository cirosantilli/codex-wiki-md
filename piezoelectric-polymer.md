# Piezoelectric polymer

↑ **Parent:** [Piezoelectric energy harvesting](piezoelectric-energy-harvesting.md)

A polymer exhibiting [piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md). A compliant, low-density film can accept larger bending or strain than a brittle ceramic, while low [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) can yield a large [voltage](voltage.md) coefficient despite a modest charge coefficient. Extractable [energy](energy-split.md) still depends on mechanical input, [capacitance](capacitance.md) and electrical loading.
