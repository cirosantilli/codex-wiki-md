# Symmetric-matrix model of the two-sheeted hyperboloid

↑ **Parent:** [Two-sheeted hyperboloid](two-sheeted-hyperboloid.md)

The displayed coordinates identify determinant-one real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) of size two with the [two-sheeted hyperboloid](two-sheeted-hyperboloid.md). The [special linear congruence action on symmetric matrices](special-linear-congruence-action-on-symmetric-matrices.md) is transitive on each sheet, with stabilizer $SO(2)$. In particular the positive sheet is the homogeneous space $SL(2,\mathbb R)/SO(2)$, a model of the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md).
