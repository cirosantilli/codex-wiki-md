# Vinculin

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[vinculin](vinculin.md) is an adhesion-associated [protein](protein-split.md) that can bind exposed regions of [talin](talin.md) and contribute to coupling adhesions to [actin](actin.md). Recruitment and load depend on the state of the surrounding linkage.
