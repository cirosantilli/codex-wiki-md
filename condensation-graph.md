# Condensation graph

↑ **Parent:** [Strongly connected component](strongly-connected-component.md)

Contract each [strongly connected component](strongly-connected-component.md) of a [directed graph](directed-graph.md) to one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md), retaining [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) between different components. A [directed cycle](directed-cycle.md) between contracted [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) would make those components mutually reachable, contradicting their distinctness; thus the [condensation graph](condensation-graph.md) is a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md). Parallel [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) can be retained by [algorithms](algorithm-split.md) that count them consistently.
