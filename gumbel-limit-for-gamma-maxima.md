# Gumbel limit for gamma maxima

↑ **Parent:** [Gumbel distribution](gumbel-distribution.md)

For the [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) with fixed positive integer shape $m$ and rate one, the [survival function](survival-function.md) is $e^{-t}\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}t^j/j!$. Its [sample maximum](sample-maximum.md) centered by $\log n+(m-1)\log\log n-\log((m-1)!)$ has [convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md) to the standard [Gumbel distribution](gumbel-distribution.md), without an additional scale factor.
