# Quantum determinant

↑ **Parent:** [RTT construction](rtt-construction.md)

For the two-by-two [RTT construction](rtt-construction.md) with the standard [Yang-Baxter R-matrix](yang-baxter-r-matrix.md), the displayed determinant commutes with the matrix generators and satisfies $\Delta D_q=D_q\otimes D_q$ and $\varepsilon(D_q)=1$. Imposing $D_q=1$ gives the [coordinate Hopf algebra of quantum SL2](coordinate-hopf-algebra-of-quantum-sl2.md); adjoining its inverse instead gives the quantum general-linear coordinate algebra.
