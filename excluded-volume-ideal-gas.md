# Excluded-volume ideal gas

↑ **Parent:** [Excluded volume](excluded-volume.md)

For one mole, a simple repulsive-volume model uses $p(V-b)=RT$ with $b>0$ and domain $V>b$. The [Maxwell relation](maxwell-relations.md) and [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) give $(\partial U/\partial V)_T=T(\partial p/\partial T)_V-p=0$, so $U=U(T)$. If its molar [heat capacity at constant volume](heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) $C_V$ is [temperature](temperature-split.md) independent, it is constant, $U=C_VT+U_*$ and $C_p=C_V+R$. Reversible [adiabatic processes](adiabatic-process.md) obey $T(V-b)^{R/C_V}=\mathrm{constant}$ or $p(V-b)^{1+R/C_V}=\mathrm{constant}$. All positive-temperature [isotherms](isotherm.md) are strictly decreasing and convex; this single-phase model contains no attractive liquid-gas coexistence mechanism. The parameter $b$ is an effective unavailable molar [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md), not automatically the literal geometric [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) of the [molecules](molecule-split.md).
