# Period group of a function

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic function](periodic-function.md)

Include zero when forming this [set](set-split.md), even though a [period of a function](period-of-a-function.md) is normally required to be nonzero. For $f:\mathbb R\to Y$, $\operatorname{Per}(f)$ is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of the [additive group](additive-group.md) of the [real numbers](real-number.md): composing two preserving shifts adds them, and reversing a preserving shift negates it. A [constant function](constant-function.md) has period group $\mathbb R$. A [nonconstant](nonconstant-function.md) [continuous](continuous-function.md) [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) that is [periodic](periodic-function.md) has period group $T_0\mathbb Z$, where $T_0$ is its [fundamental period](fundamental-period-of-a-function.md).
