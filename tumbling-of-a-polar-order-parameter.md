# Tumbling of a polar order parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Flow alignment of a polar order parameter](flow-alignment-of-a-polar-order-parameter.md)

In [simple shear flow](simple-shear-flow.md), a persistent [polar order parameter](polar-order-parameter.md) with $|\xi|<1$ has no fixed angle and rotates continuously. Integrating $\dot\theta=g(\xi\cos2\theta-1)/2$ through $2\pi$ gives the displayed period. An unoriented [nematic director](nematic-director.md) repeats after half that time. At $\xi=0$, the magnitude relaxes independently while the angular speed is $-g/2$.
