# Intermediate axis theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Euler equations for a torque-free rigid body](euler-equations-for-a-torque-free-rigid-body.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermediate_axis_theorem)

For a [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) with three distinct positive [principal moments of inertia](principal-moment-of-inertia.md) $0<I_1<I_2<I_3$ and zero external [torque](torque.md) about its [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md), steady rotation about the first or third [principal axis](principal-axis.md) is [Lyapunov stable](lyapunov-stability.md) in [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md), while rotation about the intermediate [principal axis](principal-axis.md) is unstable. Here steady rotation means $\boldsymbol\omega=\Omega\hat e_i$ in the [principal body frame](principal-body-frame.md), with constant $\Omega\ne0$. A small sideways [perturbation](perturbation.md) remains small about the extreme [principal moments of inertia](principal-moment-of-inertia.md), but generically grows about the intermediate one. Stable does not mean that the wobble decays: without [damping](damping.md), there is no [asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md). Coincident [principal moments of inertia](principal-moment-of-inertia.md) and a stationary body are excluded.[Classical Dynamics by David Tong, §3.4.3, The Asymmetric Top: Stability, printed pp. 57–58, equations (3.40)–(3.45)](classical-dynamics-by-david-tong/3-the-motion-of-rigid-bodies.md)

No external [torque](torque.md) is needed for a body to tumble. [Conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) fixes $\mathbf L$ in the [fixed space frame](fixed-space-frame.md), but the body can turn relative to $\mathbf L$, so its [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) components in the [principal body frame](principal-body-frame.md) change. The [torque-free rigid-body invariants](torque-free-rigid-body-invariants.md) constrain this motion. A supported [gyroscope](gyroscope.md) with gravitational [torque](torque.md) has different governing assumptions.
