# Pointer basis

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum decoherence](quantum-decoherence.md)

A basis of apparatus records robust under the relevant environmental interaction. Its meaning is dynamical and depends on the coupling and the time scale, rather than on an arbitrary coordinate choice for the apparatus [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md).
