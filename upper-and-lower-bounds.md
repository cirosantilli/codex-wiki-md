# Upper and lower bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_and_lower_bounds)

An upper bound of a subset is an element above every member, and a [lower bound](lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) is below every member. They need not lie in the subset or be unique. [Infimum and supremum](infimum-and-supremum.md) are the greatest lower and least upper bounds when those exist.
