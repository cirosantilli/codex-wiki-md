# Gauge-covariant derivative of a charged scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Charged scalar field](charged-scalar-field.md)

For electric charge $q$ and [electromagnetic four-potential](electromagnetic-four-potential.md) $A_a$, the derivative $\mathcal D_a=\nabla_a-iqA_a$ transforms covariantly under an electromagnetic [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md).
