# Sodium saturation spectroscopy experiment of 1971

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler-free saturation spectroscopy](doppler-free-saturation-spectroscopy.md)

[Theodor W. Hänsch](theodor-w-hansch.md), [I. S. Shahin](i-s-shahin.md) and [Arthur Leonard Schawlow](arthur-leonard-schawlow.md) reported pulsed [dye laser](dye-laser.md) [saturation spectroscopy](doppler-free-saturation-spectroscopy.md) of [sodium](sodium.md) D lines in 1971. They resolved [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md); delaying the probe revealed persistent changes in ground-level distributions of [velocities](velocity.md) from [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md). Added [argon](argon.md) allowed observation of collisional recovery. These measurements probe population memory as well as line positions.[Hänsch, Shahin and Schawlow, 1971, abstract; published September 13](high-resolution-saturation-spectroscopy-of-the-sodium-d-lines-with-a-pulsed-tunable-dye-laser-by-theodor-w-hansch-and-others.md)

The [two-level saturation population model](two-level-saturation-population-model.md) explains depletion in a closed transition; the delayed multilevel [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) signal needs additional populations and time dependence. A measured [Doppler-free saturation spectroscopy](doppler-free-saturation-spectroscopy.md) feature can still contain [natural linewidth](natural-linewidth.md), [power broadening](power-broadening.md), [pressure broadening](pressure-broadening.md), finite interaction time, imperfect alignment and laser spectral width. The word “Doppler-free” removes a particular thermal broadening mechanism, not every limit on resolution.

[Arthur Leonard Schawlow](arthur-leonard-schawlow.md)'s 1981 recognition concerned [laser spectroscopy](laser-spectroscopy.md).[Stanford Physics, career history: 1981 Nobel Prize](professional-profile-of-arthur-leonard-schawlow.md) [Theodor W. Hänsch](theodor-w-hansch.md) received a distinct award in 2005 for precision [laser spectroscopy](laser-spectroscopy.md).[Max Planck Society, October 4, 2005, opening and award explanation](physik-nobelpreis-2005-an-theodor-w-hansch-by-max-planck-society.md)
