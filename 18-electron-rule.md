# 18-electron rule

↑ **Parent:** [Electron counting](electron-counting.md)

The [18-electron rule](18-electron-rule.md) is a useful closed-shell guideline for many low-valent transition-metal complexes, filling nine valence orbitals. It is not universal: many early-metal $d^0$ complexes have fewer [electrons](electron.md), while stable square-planar $d^8$ complexes are often 16-electron.
