# Reliable quantum source compression

↑ **Parent:** [Schumacher compression](schumacher-compression.md)

A quantum source-compression scheme has encoding and decoding [quantum channels](quantum-channel.md) with compressed space $\mathcal K_n$ and rate $\limsup_n n^{-1}\log_2\dim\mathcal K_n$. For a pure-state source ensemble, average reliability means that the mean squared [quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) after decoding tends to one. A stronger coherent criterion is $F_e(\pi^{\otimes n},\mathcal D_n\circ\mathcal E_n)\to1$, where [entanglement fidelity](entanglement-fidelity.md) also tests a purification reference. Encoding the [quantum typical subspace](quantum-typical-subspace.md) achieves both criteria above $S(\pi)$. [Schumacher's original quantum coding paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.51.2738) establishes quantum source coding.
