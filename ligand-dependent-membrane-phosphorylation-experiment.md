# Ligand-dependent membrane phosphorylation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Receptor phosphorylation assay](receptor-phosphorylation-assay.md)

A motivating experiment used isolated membranes from A-431 [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md), which supplied abundant [EGFR](epidermal-growth-factor-receptor.md). Separate membrane aliquots were incubated at $0\,{}^{\circ}\mathrm C$ with [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) carrying radioactive [phosphorus](phosphorus.md) in its terminal [phosphate](phosphate.md) group, plus [magnesium](magnesium.md) or [manganese](manganese.md) [ions](ion.md). Radioactivity in material recovered by [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md)-induced [precipitation](precipitation-chemistry.md) supplied the readout. Adding [EGF](epidermal-growth-factor.md) beforehand enhanced incorporation relative to the unstimulated preparation; this placed a ligand-dependent biochemical response in a cell-free membrane system.[Cohen, Epidermal Growth Factor, printed p. 339: A-431 membrane aliquots, terminally labeled ATP, divalent ions and trichloroacetic acid-insoluble incorporation](epidermal-growth-factor-by-stanley-cohen.md)

The follow-up comparisons separated increased addition from suppressed removal: [phosphate](phosphate.md) removal was rapid but unaffected by [EGF](epidermal-growth-factor.md), and anti-[EGF](epidermal-growth-factor.md) [antibody](antibody.md) reversed activation by removing the [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md). These observations support reversible regulation of membrane-associated [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) activity.[printed p. 341, numbered observations 1–4: rapid activation, dephosphorylation, lack of soluble kinase release and anti-EGF reversal](epidermal-growth-factor-by-stanley-cohen.md) Total incorporation alone cannot identify which [protein](protein-split.md) or [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md) received the label, prove intrinsic receptor [catalysis](catalysis.md), or predict an intact [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)'s response.

The same account records an instructive identification error: receptor [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) was initially assigned to the wrong [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md) because released modified [amino acids](amino-acid.md) migrated together in the electrophoretic [electric field](electric-field-split.md). Reanalysis identified [phosphotyrosine residues](phosphotyrosine-residue.md). Retained radioactivity is evidence about the assay's separation and detection, not unique chemical identity.[printed p. 341: co-migration in the electrophoretic system and subsequent identification of tyrosine phosphorylation](epidermal-growth-factor-by-stanley-cohen.md)
