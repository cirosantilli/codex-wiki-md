# One-loop scalar effective potential

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum effective potential](quantum-effective-potential.md)

For one canonically normalized [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) and $V''(\varphi)>0$, the [modified minimal subtraction scheme](modified-minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) gives the displayed one-loop term. The [Gaussian fluctuation approximation](gaussian-fluctuation-approximation.md) produces $\tfrac12\int d^dk_E\,(2\pi)^{-d}\log(k_E^2+V''(\varphi))$. Its derivative in $V''$ is half the [Euclidean massive loop integral](euclidean-massive-loop-integral.md) with $n=1$. Integrating and subtracting its pole yields the formula, up to a field-independent constant. Other finite [counterterms](counterterm.md) alter the scheme-dependent nonlogarithmic part. Negative curvature requires an analytic continuation and can yield an imaginary part indicating instability of that background.
