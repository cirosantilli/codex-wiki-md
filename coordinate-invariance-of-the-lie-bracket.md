# Coordinate invariance of the Lie bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md)

Under a change of coordinates, the second-derivative terms arising from the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) cancel because their coordinate indices are symmetric while the coefficient $X^iY^j-Y^iX^j$ is antisymmetric. Consequently $[X,Y]$ transforms as a [vector field](vector-field.md).
