# CRISPR interference

↑ **Parent:** [CRISPR genome editing](crispr-genome-editing.md)

Guide-directed reduction of [gene expression](gene-expression.md) using nuclease-inactive DNA targeting. In mammalian cells, [dCas9](dcas9.md) fused to a repressor can reduce transcription without requiring a DNA break.
