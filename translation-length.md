# Translation length

↑ **Parent:** [Isometry of a tree](isometry-of-a-tree.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translation_length)

The translation length of an isometry $g$ of a metric space is $\ell(g)=\inf_x d(x,gx)$. For a hyperbolic tree isometry, it is attained exactly on the [axis of a tree isometry](axis-of-a-tree-isometry.md).
