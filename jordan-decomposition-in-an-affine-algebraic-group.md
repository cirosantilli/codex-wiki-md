# Jordan decomposition in an affine algebraic group

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md)

This is the multiplicative [Jordan–Chevalley decomposition](jordan-chevalley-decomposition.md) in a [linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md). Every element $g$ of an affine algebraic group has unique commuting semisimple and unipotent parts $g_s,g_u$ such that $g=g_sg_u$. The construction agrees with the multiplicative [Jordan decomposition](jordan-normal-form.md) in every rational representation.
