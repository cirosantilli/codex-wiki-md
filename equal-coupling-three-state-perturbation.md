# Equal-coupling three-state perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate perturbation theory](degenerate-perturbation-theory.md)

A real perturbation with zero diagonal and every off-diagonal element $t$ has one [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $2t$ on the symmetric [vector](vector.md) and a twofold [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $-t$ on its [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md). It is $t(J_3-I)$, where $J_3$ is the [all-ones matrix](all-ones-matrix.md). Diagonal entries alone do not give first-order energies in a degenerate subspace.
