# Nonelectrolyte

↑ **Parent:** [Electrolyte](electrolyte.md)

A [nonelectrolyte](nonelectrolyte.md) supplies neutral [molecules](molecule.md) rather than [ions](ion.md) on dissolving in the specified [solvent](solvent.md), as illustrated by [sucrose](sucrose.md) in [water](water.md).
