# ADM boundary term of the Nester two-form

↑ **Parent:** [Nester two-form](nester-two-form.md)

For asymptotically constant spinors, the leading intrinsic [spin connection](spin-connection.md) term gives half the ADM metric integrand times $K^0_\infty$. The [Sen spinor connection](sen-spinor-connection.md) correction gives $-(k_{ij}-kh_{ij})K^j_\infty$. The constant-spinor derivative term integrates to zero by antisymmetry of the gamma matrices, and the remaining quadratic terms vanish under the standard decay. This identifies the [Nester two-form](nester-two-form.md) flux with the ADM energy-momentum contraction, including momentum rather than just energy.
