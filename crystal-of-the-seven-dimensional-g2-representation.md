# Crystal of the seven-dimensional G2 representation

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal basis](crystal-basis.md)

The seven-dimensional irreducible representation of the [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md) has [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\omega_1=2\alpha_1+\alpha_2$. Its [crystal basis](crystal-basis.md) is the chain of weights $2\alpha_1+\alpha_2,\alpha_1+\alpha_2,\alpha_1,0,-\alpha_1,-\alpha_1-\alpha_2,-2\alpha_1-\alpha_2$, with successive edge colors $1,2,1,1,2,1$. Each step subtracts its color's [simple root](simple-root.md). The nonzero weights are the six short roots, each of [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md) one; the zero weight also has multiplicity one.
