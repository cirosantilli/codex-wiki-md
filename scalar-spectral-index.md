# Scalar spectral index

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial scalar amplitude](primordial-scalar-amplitude.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_spectral_index)

The scalar spectral index describes the departure of the primordial scalar spectrum from scale invariance. To first order in potential slow-roll parameters,

$$
n_s-1\simeq-6\epsilon_V+2\eta_V.
$$
