# Pendulum approximation of a mean-motion resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Resonant-argument libration](resonant-argument-libration.md)

Keeping one resonant harmonic and treating [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) as fixed reduces [mean-motion resonance](mean-motion-resonance.md) dynamics to a [physical pendulum](physical-pendulum.md) equation. For the inner first-order term $\mathcal R=(GM_2/a_2)f(\alpha)e\cos\phi$, leading [Lagrange planetary equations](lagrange-planetary-equations.md) give $\ddot\phi\simeq3j^2\mu_2n^2\alpha f e\sin\phi$. Around its stable phase, the small-amplitude libration frequency is $jn\sqrt{3\mu_2\alpha|f|e}$. Evolution of eccentricity, other harmonics, and approach to a [separatrix](separatrix.md) change this approximation.
