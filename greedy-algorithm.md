# Greedy algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Algorithm](algorithm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greedy_algorithm)

A [greedy algorithm](greedy-algorithm.md) commits to a locally preferred next choice without revisiting earlier choices. Its rule needs a justification to guarantee a globally optimal result: the largest-individual-value rule in the [0-1 knapsack problem](0-1-knapsack-problem.md) example fails, whereas [Dijkstra's algorithm](dijkstra-algorithm.md) correctly settles the smallest tentative distance under its nonnegative-edge assumptions.
