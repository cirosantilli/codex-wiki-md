# Reflection intertwining of triangle-cover coset actions

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle cover construction for Sunada surfaces](triangle-cover-construction-for-sunada-surfaces.md)

Write triangle-group generators as products $a=s_1s_2$, $b=s_2s_3$ of adjacent side reflections. Conjugation by $s_2$ sends both to their inverses. A sheet relabelling satisfying the displayed two permutation identities therefore lifts this base reflection to an [orientation-reversing isometry](orientation-reversing-riemannian-isometry.md) between the two hyperbolic covers. Thus nonconjugate subgroups in the orientation-preserving finite group can still give isometric covers.
