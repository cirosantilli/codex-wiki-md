# Label switching

↑ **Parent:** [Finite mixture model](finite-mixture-model.md)

If component priors are exchangeable, permuting component labels leaves the mixture [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) and [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) unchanged. A sampling chain can visit these equivalent modes, making naive component-by-component averages misleading. Predictive densities and other permutation-invariant summaries avoid the issue; component-specific interpretation requires an explicit identification or relabelling rule. Nonexchangeable weight priors can break exact symmetry but do not remove every multimodality problem.
