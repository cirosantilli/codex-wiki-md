# Turing threshold of a basally forced quadratic activator-inhibitor model

↑ **Parent:** [Basally forced quadratic activator-inhibitor model](basally-forced-quadratic-activator-inhibitor-model.md)

Add diffusivities $1,p$ to the [basally forced quadratic activator-inhibitor model](basally-forced-quadratic-activator-inhibitor-model.md). A [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) with $s=k^2$ has matrix $J-s\operatorname{diag}(1,p)$ and [determinant](determinant.md) $D(s)=ps^2+(q-pa)s+q$. When $q>a$, its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is negative for every $s\geq0$. A positive-growth mode therefore occurs precisely when $D(s)<0$ for some positive $s$. The parabola has a positive minimizer only if $pa>q$, and its minimum is negative exactly when $(pa-q)^2>4pq$. Thus the ordinary [Turing instability](turing-instability.md) requires $a>0$, $q>a$ and $p>q/(\sqrt{1+a}-1)^2$. At onset, $k_c^2=(pa-q)/(2p)=\sqrt{q/p}$ and the critical [wavelength](wavelength.md) is $2\pi(p/q)^{1/4}$. A bounded spatial domain further requires an allowed mode inside the unstable band.
