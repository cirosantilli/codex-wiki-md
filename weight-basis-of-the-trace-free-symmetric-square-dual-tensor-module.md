# Weight basis of the trace-free symmetric-square dual tensor module

↑ **Parent:** [Sl3 decomposition of the symmetric-square dual tensor product](sl3-decomposition-of-the-symmetric-square-dual-tensor-product.md)

Let $t_{ij}=(e_i\otimes e_j+e_j\otimes e_i)/2$, and let $f_i$ be the dual [basis](basis.md). The equivariant contraction sends $t_{ij}\otimes f_k$ to $\delta_{ik}e_j+\delta_{jk}e_i$. Its kernel has a [weight basis](weight-basis.md) consisting of $t_{ii}\otimes f_k$ for $i\ne k$, $t_{ij}\otimes f_k$ for three distinct indices with $i<j$, and $t_{ij}\otimes f_j-\tfrac12t_{ii}\otimes f_i$ for $i\ne j$. The three families have sizes six, three and six. This is the irreducible fifteen-dimensional [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md) with [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $2L_1-L_3$.
