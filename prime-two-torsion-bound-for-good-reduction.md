# Prime-two torsion bound for good reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Reduction of torsion points on an elliptic curve](reduction-of-torsion-points-on-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) with good reduction over $\mathbb Q_2$, let $E_1$ be the reduction kernel and $E_2$ its formal subgroup with parameter in $4\mathbb Z_2$. The [deep logarithm subgroup of a formal group](deep-logarithm-subgroup-of-a-formal-group.md) makes $E_2$ torsion-free, while $[E_1:E_2]=2$. Thus the torsion of the reduction kernel has order at most $2$. Combined with the [Hasse theorem for elliptic curves](hasse-s-theorem-on-elliptic-curves.md), rational torsion of order $16$ forces bad reduction at $2$.
