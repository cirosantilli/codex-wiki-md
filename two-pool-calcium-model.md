# Two-pool calcium model

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium signaling](calcium-signaling.md)

A [two-pool calcium model](two-pool-calcium-model.md) couples cytosolic [calcium](calcium.md) and an intracellular store. With compatible reference volumes, uptake $J_+$ moves [calcium](calcium.md) into the store, release $J_-$ and leakage $k_sc_s$ return it, and $\dot c_s=J_+-J_--k_sc_s$. External supply $r$ and export $kc$ give $d(c+c_s)/dt=r-kc$. Thus an [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) has $c=r/k$ independently of the internal exchange law. Distinct physical reference volumes require their appropriate volume factors before adding the balances.
