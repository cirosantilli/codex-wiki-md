# Legendre differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order linear differential equation](second-order-linear-differential-equation.md)

The equation $(1-x^2)y\prime\prime-2xy\prime+\ell(\ell+1)y=0$ has polynomial solutions for nonnegative integer $\ell$.

The equation $(1-x^2)y\prime\prime-2xy\prime+\ell(\ell+1)y=0$ has the [Legendre polynomial](legendre-polynomial.md) of degree $\ell$ as its normalized regular polynomial solution when $\ell$ is a nonnegative integer.
