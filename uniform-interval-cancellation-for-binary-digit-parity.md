# Uniform interval cancellation for binary digit parity

↑ **Parent:** [Thue–Morse Fourier product](thue-morse-fourier-product.md)

An integer interval is a disjoint union of aligned binary blocks with at most two of each length. On $[a2^j,(a+1)2^j)$ the [Thue–Morse sequence](thue-morse-sequence.md) sign factors as $f(a)f(r)$, so the [Thue–Morse Fourier product](thue-morse-fourier-product.md) bounds its [exponential sum](exponential-sum.md) by $O(2^{\sigma j})$. Sum the geometric series over block lengths to obtain the uniform bound, for $\sigma=2-\frac34\log_2 3$.
