# Hyperbolic plane (quadratic form)

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic form](quadratic-form.md)

A hyperbolic plane is a two-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with a [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) and a [basis](basis.md) $e,f$ satisfying $q(e)=q(f)=0$ and $B(e,f)=1$, where $B$ is the [polar bilinear form of a quadratic form](polar-bilinear-form-of-a-quadratic-form.md). Equivalently, $q(xe+yf)=xy$. This definition also applies in characteristic two. Over the [real numbers](real-number.md), the identity $xy=((x+y)/2)^2-((x-y)/2)^2$ gives one positive and one negative square, hence signature zero. The signature statement is specific to real quadratic forms.
