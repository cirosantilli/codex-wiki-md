# Distributional regularity of a constant-coefficient ordinary differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

For a nonzero constant-coefficient polynomial differential operator in one variable, every homogeneous [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) solution is a [classical solution](classical-solution.md), indeed analytic. Factor the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) into powers of distinct [characteristic roots of a constant-coefficient differential equation](characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md). The [kernel decomposition for coprime polynomials](kernel-decomposition-for-coprime-polynomials.md) reduces the equation to $(D-\lambda)^m v=0$, and [multiplication of a distribution by a smooth function](multiplication-of-a-distribution-by-a-smooth-function.md) reduces this to $D^m(e^{-\lambda x}v)=0$. The fact that [a distribution with zero derivative is constant](a-distribution-with-zero-derivative-is-constant.md), iterated, makes the latter distribution a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree less than $m$.
