# Lever

↑ **Parent:** [Simple machine](simple-machine.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lever)

A body turning about a [fulcrum](fulcrum.md). In static balance, effort and load supply equal and opposite [torques](torque.md): $E d_E=L d_L$, where the distances are perpendicular [lever arms](lever-arm.md). Its ideal [mechanical advantage](mechanical-advantage.md) is $L/E=d_E/d_L$. Measuring distances along the body instead of perpendicular to the force lines can give the wrong answer.
