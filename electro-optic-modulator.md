# Electro-optic modulator

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electro-optic_modulator)

A device uses an applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md) to change an optical medium's [refractive index](refractive-index.md) and hence the phase, amplitude or polarization of light. A phase change followed by suitable polarization analysis can provide an intensity transmission gate for [active mode locking](active-mode-locking.md).
