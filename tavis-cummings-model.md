# Tavis-Cummings model

↑ **Parent:** [Jaynes-Cummings model](jaynes-cummings-model.md)

The [Tavis-Cummings model](tavis-cummings-model.md) extends the [Jaynes-Cummings model](jaynes-cummings-model.md) to several [two-level systems](two-level-system.md) exchanging excitations with a common quantized [normal mode](normal-mode.md) under the [rotating-wave approximation](rotating-wave-approximation.md). It conserves total excitation number. Equal coupling preserves permutation-symmetric atomic sectors, allowing finite-dimensional reductions for a fixed initial excitation number.
