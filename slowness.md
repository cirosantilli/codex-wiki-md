# Slowness

↑ **Parent:** [Wave speed](wave-speed.md)

[Slowness](slowness.md) is the reciprocal of [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md), measured in time per distance. A [plane wave](plane-wave.md) with [wavevector](wavevector.md) $\mathbf k$ and [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$ has vector [slowness](slowness.md) $\mathbf p=\mathbf k/\omega$. Its direction is the [wavefront](wavefront.md) normal, which need not be the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) direction in an [anisotropic](anisotropy.md) medium.
