# Diameter bound for a non-abelian finite simple group

↑ **Parent:** [Diameter of a finite group with respect to a generating set](diameter-of-a-finite-group-with-respect-to-a-generating-set.md)

For every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $\lambda(\varepsilon)$ such that every non-abelian finite [simple group](simple-group.md) $G$ and every finite symmetric generating set $S$ containing the identity satisfy

$$
\operatorname{diam}_S(G)\leq\max\{|G|^\varepsilon,\lambda(\varepsilon)\}.
$$

The proof applies the [one-scale virtual nilpotence theorem](one-scale-virtual-nilpotence-theorem.md) below the diameter and then uses simplicity to eliminate both the bounded-index subgroup and the finite normal subgroup.
