# Molecular mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)

An atomistic model using a fitted potential-energy function of nuclear positions, commonly with bond, angle, torsion and nonbonded terms. Electronic effects enter parametrically rather than through explicit electronic [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md) or densities.
