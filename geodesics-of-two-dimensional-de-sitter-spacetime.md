# Geodesics of two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime](two-dimensional-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

A [geodesic](geodesic.md) on the embedded [two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime](two-dimensional-de-sitter-spacetime.md) is the intersection with a two-plane through the ambient origin. For initial point $P$ and tangent $V$ satisfying $P^2=-1$, $P\cdot V=0$, $V^2=\varepsilon$, its affine parametrization is $P\cosh s+V\sinh s$ when timelike, $P+sV$ when null and $P\cos s+V\sin s$ when spacelike. These solve $X''=\varepsilon X$. The [spacelike geodesics](spacelike-geodesic.md) are closed; [timelike geodesics](timelike-geodesic.md) and [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) extend to both conformal infinities with infinite affine length.
