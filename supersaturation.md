# Supersaturation

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal graph theory](extremal-graph-theory.md)

Supersaturation strengthens an extremal existence theorem by forcing many copies of a forbidden graph when the edge count exceeds its extremal threshold by a fixed density. For a fixed clique, the [Erdős-Stone theorem](erdos-stone-theorem.md) and averaging over bounded-size random vertex subsets give a positive multiple of $n^{|V(F)|}$ copies.
