# Hydrogen radial second moment

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

For a nonrelativistic point-nucleus [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md), $\langle r^2\rangle_{nl}=a_0^2n^2[5n^2+1-3l(l+1)]/2$. Integrating the normalized radial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) squared with measure $r^4dr$ gives $3a_0^2,42a_0^2,30a_0^2$ for $1s,2s,2p$. For a [hydrogen-like ion](hydrogen-like-ion.md), replace $a_0$ by $a_0/Z$ in the infinite-nuclear-mass model. These moments determine first-order shifts under quadratic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) perturbations.
