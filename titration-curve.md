# Titration curve

↑ **Parent:** [Acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md)

A [titration curve](titration-curve.md) plots a measured or calculated property against added [titrant](titrant.md) [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) or [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md). In an [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md), plotting [pH](ph.md) exposes gradual changes and a steep endpoint region. A [potentiometric titration](potentiometric-titration.md) uses an [electrode](electrode.md) [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md); a calibrated [glass electrode](glass-electrode.md) and [reference electrode](reference-electrode.md) provide operational [pH](ph.md). Finite additions bracket a transition rather than measuring a continuous curve. A [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) fit can estimate the [equivalence point](equivalence-point.md); identifying the steepest sampled interval alone has a resolution set by the dose spacing and instrument response.
