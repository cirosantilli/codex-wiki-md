# Laplace integral solution

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace integral](laplace-integral.md)

A linear differential-equation solution may be sought as $\int_Ce^{xt}f(t)dt$. Integration by parts transfers the factor $x$ to [derivatives](derivative.md) of $f$, giving an equation for the amplitude and boundary terms. Admissible contours must cancel those terms and make the integral converge.
