# Gyromotion

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

Rapid circular particle motion perpendicular to a locally uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), superposed on parallel streaming when the parallel [velocity](velocity.md) is nonzero. In [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md), its signed [cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md) is given in the title and its [gyroradius](gyroradius.md) is $v_\perp/|\Omega_s|$. Slowly varying fields permit a [guiding center](guiding-center.md) description.
