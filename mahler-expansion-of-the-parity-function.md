# Mahler expansion of the parity function

↑ **Parent:** [Mahler coefficient](mahler-coefficient.md)

The [locally constant function](locally-constant-function.md) equal to one on $2\mathbb Z_2$ and minus one on its other coset has [Mahler coefficients](mahler-coefficient.md) $a_n=(-2)^n$: at nonnegative integers its values are $(-1)^j$, so the [forward difference operator](forward-difference-operator.md) multiplies it by $-2$. Since $|a_n|_2=2^{-n}$ and the [binomial polynomials](binomial-polynomial.md) have absolute value at most one on the [2-adic integers](2-adic-integer.md), the series converges uniformly. The [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md) verifies its values on the nonnegative integers, and [continuity](continuous-function.md) extends the identity to the entire domain.
