# Image-potential state

↑ **Parent:** [Surface state](surface-state.md)

An [electron](electron.md) state bound outside a conducting surface by image-charge attraction and the surface barrier. Its in-plane [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) is often close to the free-electron mass and its energy lies below the vacuum level.
