# Debye wavevector

↑ **Parent:** [Debye model](debye-model.md)

The spherical cutoff in the three-dimensional [Debye model](debye-model.md) preserves three vibrational modes per atom. For atom density $n_a$, $q_D=(6\pi^2n_a)^{1/3}$ and $\omega_D=c_s q_D$ in a single-speed approximation. A monovalent free-electron metal has $k_F=(3\pi^2n_a)^{1/3}$, giving $q_D=2^{1/3}k_F$. This cutoff is a mode-counting approximation, not an exact spherical boundary of the crystal's [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md).
