# Truth value

↑ **Parent:** [Proposition](proposition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truth_value)

In [classical propositional logic](classical-propositional-logic.md), a [truth value](truth-value.md) is false or true, encoded by a [bit](bit.md) as zero or one. A [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) assigns these values to primitive [propositions](proposition.md) and extends to compound [propositional formulas](propositional-formula.md) through [logical connectives](logical-connective.md).
