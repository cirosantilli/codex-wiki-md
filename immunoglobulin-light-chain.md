# Immunoglobulin light chain

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunoglobulin_light_chain)

An [immunoglobulin light chain](immunoglobulin-light-chain.md) accompanies an arm of the illustrated [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md), contributing to its [paratope](paratope.md).

Its [protein domains](protein-domain.md) are $V_L,C_L$, read from the [N-terminus](n-terminus.md); a [Fab fragment](fragment-antigen-binding.md) retains both.[Immunobiology: The Immune System in Health and Disease, 5th edition by Charles A. Janeway, Jr. and others; §§3-2–3-3: two light-chain domains and complete light chain in Fab](immunobiology-the-immune-system-in-health-and-disease-5th-edition-by-charles-a-janeway-jr-and-others/the-structure-of-a-typical-antibody-molecule.md)
