# Hahn spin echo experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Spin echo](spin-echo.md)

[Erwin Louis Hahn](erwin-louis-hahn.md)'s 1950 apparatus enclosed [liquid](liquid.md) samples in the coil of a tuned [LC circuit](lc-circuit.md), coupled to a gated [radio transmitter](radio-transmitter.md) and [radio receiver](radio-receiver.md). One transmitter quadrupled a crystal [oscillator](oscillator.md)'s 7.5 MHz to 30 MHz. A separate [proton](proton.md) resonance regulator stabilized the static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).[§IV, Figs. 12–13: transmitter, coil, receiver and regulator](spin-echoes-by-erwin-louis-hahn.md)

Waiting for [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) prepared [nuclear polarization](nuclear-polarization.md). [Oscilloscope](oscilloscope.md) photographs compared [glycerol](glycerol.md) [proton](proton.md) echoes at different field homogeneity: echo width changed; echo time followed twice the pulse separation. Receiver saturation obscured early [free induction decay](free-induction-decay.md); transmitter leakage required care. The delayed [signal](signal.md) tested refocusing; [diffusion](diffusion.md) could still attenuate it.[§II(B), Fig. 2, §IV: preparation, timing, homogeneity comparison and readout limits](spin-echoes-by-erwin-louis-hahn.md)

Hahn analyzed equal pulses as well as noting improved recovery with a second pulse twice as long; the ideal sequence above isolates that latter case.[§III(B), Fig. 1; footnote discussing a doubled second pulse and drive inhomogeneity](spin-echoes-by-erwin-louis-hahn.md)
