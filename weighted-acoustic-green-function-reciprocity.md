# Weighted acoustic Green-function reciprocity

↑ **Parent:** [Wave reciprocity](wave-reciprocity.md)

For an operator $c^{-2}\partial_t^2-a^{-1}\nabla\cdot(a\nabla)$ with positive stationary coefficients and reciprocal boundary conditions, its delta-normalized [Green function](green-s-function.md) satisfies

$$
a(x)G(x,y;s)=a(y)G(y,x;s).
$$

Multiplying its frequency-domain equation by $a$ and using the bilinear [Green second identity](green-second-identity.md) proves the relation. Both Green functions must use the same outgoing or causal prescription. Exchanging source and receiver preserves time lag, and involves no complex conjugation.
