# Elastic modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Elasticity (physics)](elasticity-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elastic_modulus)

A coefficient relating an appropriate stress and strain in the linear elastic regime. [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md), [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) and [bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md) describe different deformation modes.
