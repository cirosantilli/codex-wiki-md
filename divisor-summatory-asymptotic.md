# Divisor summatory asymptotic

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet hyperbola method](dirichlet-hyperbola-method.md)

The [Dirichlet hyperbola method](dirichlet-hyperbola-method.md) gives $D(x)=2\sum_{a\le r}\lfloor x/a\rfloor-r^2$, where $r=\lfloor\sqrt x\rfloor$. The [harmonic number](harmonic-number.md) estimate $H_r=\log r+\gamma+O(1/r)$, with $\gamma$ the [Euler's constant](euler-s-constant.md), yields $D(x)=2x(\log r+\gamma)-r^2+O(x/r+r)$. Substitution of $r=\sqrt x+O(1)$ gives the displayed [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md). Comparison of $\sum_{n\le x}\log n$ with its integral consequently gives $\sum_{n\le N}(\tau(n)-\log n-2\gamma)=O(\sqrt N)$ for integer $N$.
