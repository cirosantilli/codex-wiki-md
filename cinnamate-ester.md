# Cinnamate ester

↑ **Parent:** [Ester](ester.md)

An [ester](ester.md) containing the conjugated fragment Ph-CH=CH-C(=O)-O-R. Its trans [alkene](alkene.md) commonly has a large vicinal proton coupling, while [HMBC](hmbc.md) can connect an alkoxy methylene to the [ester](ester.md) [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md).
