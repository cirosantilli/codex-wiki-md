# Goal state

↑ **Parent:** [Search problem](search-problem.md)

A state satisfying a problem's [goal test](goal-test.md). Whether its generation or its later selection terminates a [search algorithm](search-algorithm.md) affects optimality for algorithms such as [A-star search](a-star-search.md). A [goal state](goal-state.md)'s remaining optimal cost is zero, whereas its accumulated [path cost](path-cost.md) need not be zero.
