# Global ocean freshwater budget

↑ **Parent:** [Sea level rise](sea-level-rise.md)

With positive freshwater runoff $R$, net [sea ice](sea-ice.md) melt $I$ and precipitation $P$, and evaporation loss $E$, the ocean water-mass budget is $\dot M=R+I+P-E$. With salt-free ice and fixed total dissolved salt mass, mean [salinity](salinity.md) satisfies $\dot S/S=-\dot M/M$. Gross regional ice melt must not be substituted for net global melt without accounting for ice formation elsewhere and the averaging period.
