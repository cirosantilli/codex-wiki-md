# Carl Wernicke

↑ **Parent:** [List of neurologists](list-of-neurologists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Wernicke)

A [neurologist](neurologist.md) whose work on [aphasia](aphasia.md) helped motivate anatomical and functional distinctions within language processing. Clinical syndromes bearing his name must still be explained through specific representations and computations.
