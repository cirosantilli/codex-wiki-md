# Geomorphic offset-age relation

↑ **Parent:** [Fault slip rate](fault-slip-rate.md)

A marker that starts with zero offset and accumulates displacement at a constant [fault slip rate](fault-slip-rate.md) $v$ has age $t=d/v$. More generally $d=\int_0^t v(s)\,ds$. The inferred age belongs to the establishment, abandonment or last resetting of that particular marker. A channel incised into an [alluvial fan](alluvial-fan.md) can be younger than the [sediment](sediment.md) it cuts. Uncertain channel correlation, [erosion](erosion.md), distributed slip and time-variable rates require corresponding age uncertainty; a numerical ratio alone does not date a fan’s deposition.
