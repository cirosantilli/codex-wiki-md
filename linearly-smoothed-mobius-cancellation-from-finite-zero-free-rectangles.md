<h1 id="linearly-smoothed-mobius-cancellation-from-finite-zero-free-rectangles">Linearly smoothed Möbius cancellation from finite zero-free rectangles</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Perron's formula](perron-s-formula.md)

The [Perron's formula](perron-s-formula.md) kernel $y^{s+1}/(s(s+1))$ integrates to $(y-1)_+$, giving the smoothed sum as an integral of $x^{s+1}/(\zeta(s)s(s+1))$. For each fixed height $T$, a zero-free rectangle permits shifting the central [contour](complex-integration-contour.md) to a real part below one; its vertical contribution is $o(x^2)$, and its horizontal contributions are $O_T(x^2/\log x)$ when the original real part is $1+1/\log x$. A bound $1/\zeta(\sigma+it)=O(\log^7(2+|t|))$ for $\sigma\geq1$ bounds the remaining tails by $Cx^2\int_T^\infty\log^7(2+t)t^{-2}dt$. Letting $x$ grow first and $T$ afterward proves the displayed cancellation, without uniform estimates to the left of one.
