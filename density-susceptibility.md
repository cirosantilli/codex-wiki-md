# Density susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Compressibility](compressibility.md)

The static [density susceptibility](density-susceptibility.md) is the linear density response to a chemical-potential perturbation. In the quadratic uniform contact [Bose gas](bose-gas.md), $\chi_\rho(q)=[g+q^2/(4m\rho_0)]^{-1}$. Its inverse is a density stiffness. At zero [wavevector](wavevector.md) $\partial\rho/\partial\mu=1/g$ at mean field, while the isothermal [compressibility](compressibility.md) includes the additional factor $1/\rho^2$.
