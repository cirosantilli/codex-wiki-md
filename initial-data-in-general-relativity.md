# Initial data in general relativity

↑ **Parent:** [Globally hyperbolic spacetime](globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md)

Gravitational [initial data](initial-data-in-general-relativity.md) on a spatial manifold consist of a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) $h$ and an [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md) $K$ satisfying the [Hamiltonian constraint](hamiltonian-constraint.md) and [momentum constraint](momentum-constraint.md). Matter theories require their own data and constraints as well. The data determine a [maximal Cauchy development](maximal-cauchy-development.md).
