# Character table of the quaternion group

↑ **Parent:** [Quaternion group](quaternion-group.md)

The [quaternion group](quaternion-group.md) has [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) $\{1\}$, $\{-1\}$, $\{\pm i\}$, $\{\pm j\}$, $\{\pm k\}$, with sizes $1,1,2,2,2$. Over the complex numbers, the number of classes and the [sum of squares of irreducible representation dimensions](sum-of-squares-of-irreducible-representation-dimensions.md) force four one-dimensional representations and one two-dimensional representation. For $\epsilon,\eta\in\{\pm1\}$, the linear character rows on these classes are $(1,1,\epsilon,\eta,\epsilon\eta)$. Orthogonality then gives the remaining row $(2,-2,0,0,0)$. A representation with character $(4,-4,0,0,0)$ contains two copies of the two-dimensional irrep and none of the linear irreps, by the [character inner product](character-inner-product.md). The choice of scalar field matters: the corresponding quaternionic four-dimensional real representation is irreducible over the reals.
