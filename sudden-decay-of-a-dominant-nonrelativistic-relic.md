# Sudden decay of a dominant nonrelativistic relic

↑ **Parent:** [Reheating](reheating.md)

The sudden-decay approximation converts a dominant cold relic's rest-mass density into radiation at $H\simeq\Gamma$, neglecting expansion during the conversion. Its pre-decay radiation temperature obeys $T_-^3\simeq3M_{\rm Pl}^2\Gamma^2/(c_1g_\star Ym)$, while the [reheating temperature](reheating-temperature.md) is set by the post-decay radiation energy density. Constant relativistic counts and prompt thermalization are additional assumptions.
