# Bufferless queue

↑ **Parent:** [Queue (queueing theory)](queue-queueing-theory.md)

A [queue](queue-queueing-theory.md) which retains no unfinished work between slots. Given nonnegative input $A$ and capacity $c$, its [bufferless queue output](bufferless-queue-output.md) is $\min(A,c)$ and its lost work is $(A-c)^+$.
