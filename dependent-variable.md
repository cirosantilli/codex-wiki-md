# Dependent variable

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)

A variable whose value is specified as a [function](function-split.md) of other variables. In an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md), the unknown [function](function-split.md) $y$ is the dependent variable, and its argument $x$ is the [independent variable](independent-variable.md).
