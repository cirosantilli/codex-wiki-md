# Identity matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_matrix)

The identity matrix has ones on its main diagonal and zeros elsewhere, and satisfies $IA=AI=A$ whenever the products are defined.
