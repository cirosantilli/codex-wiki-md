# Principles of Programming Languages examination paper by the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The eight-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) [computer science](computer-science-split.md) Finals paper dated Thursday 11 June 2015, 2:30 pm–4:00 pm. The released [examination archive supplied by the University of Oxford](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains it as `2015-popl.pdf`. Printed pp. 2–3 concern [monad transformers](monad-transformer.md); pp. 4–6 concern [continuations](continuation-computer-science.md), [coroutines](coroutine.md) and [semaphores](semaphore-programming.md); pp. 7–8 concern a [monadic interpreter](monadic-interpreter.md) and [recursive closures](recursive-closure.md).
