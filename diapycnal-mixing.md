# Diapycnal mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent mixing](turbulent-mixing.md)

Mixing across surfaces of constant [density](density.md), or [isopycnals](isopycnal.md). In the deep ocean, breaking [internal waves](internal-wave.md) can supply the mechanical [energy](energy-split.md) needed to mix against stable [stratification](density-stratification.md). The divergence of the turbulent [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) determines local [water-mass transformation](water-mass-transformation.md).
