# Interaction (statistics)

↑ **Parent:** [Regression model](regression-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interaction_(statistics))

A statistical interaction means that the effect of one predictor depends on another on the chosen response scale. In a [linear regression](linear-regression-split.md), an [interaction term](interaction-term.md) $\beta_3XZ$ changes the slope in $X$ to $\beta_1+\beta_3Z$. Whether interaction is present depends on the comparison scale; additive effects and multiplicative effects use different null contrasts. Comparing subgroup effects requires an [interaction contrast](interaction-contrast.md), rather than contrasting their separate significance labels.
