# Band effective mass

↑ **Parent:** [Energy band](energy-band.md)

In one dimension the [band effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) is defined by $1/m^*(k)=E''(k)/\hbar^2$. Together with $v=E'(k)/\hbar$ and $\hbar\dot k=F$, this gives $\dot v=F/m^*$. Negative band curvature produces negative [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) for the electron [wave packet](wave-packet.md); near a filled-band maximum the equivalent missing-electron description uses positively responding holes. In several dimensions the inverse mass is the Hessian $\partial_i\partial_jE/\hbar^2$.
