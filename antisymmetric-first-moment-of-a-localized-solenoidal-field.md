# Antisymmetric first moment of a localized solenoidal field

↑ **Parent:** [Solenoidal vector field](solenoidal-vector-field.md)

For a [solenoidal vector field](solenoidal-vector-field.md) $J$ with [compact support](compact-support.md) and suitable integrability, apply the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) to $x_ix_jJ$. It gives zero boundary term and hence the displayed antisymmetry. Equivalently, for any constant [vector](vector.md) $C$, the [vector triple product](vector-triple-product.md) identity yields $\int(C\cdot r)J\,dV=\tfrac12C\times\int(J\times r)\,dV$. A piecewise smooth field must have no normal jump at its support boundary; otherwise its [weak divergence](weak-divergence.md) contains a surface contribution and the argument fails.
