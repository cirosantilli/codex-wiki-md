# BCS electronic density of states

↑ **Parent:** [BCS quasiparticle spectrum](bcs-quasiparticle-spectrum.md)

For a particle-hole-symmetric normal [density of states](density-of-states.md) $g_F$ and isotropic [superconducting energy gap](superconducting-energy-gap.md) $\Delta>0$, the electron spectral density is zero for $|E|<\Delta$ and has the displayed form outside the gap. The [BCS coherence factors](bcs-coherence-factors.md) weight the two roots $\xi=\pm\sqrt{E^2-\Delta^2}$, where $\xi$ is normal-state energy relative to the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md). At positive $E$, their electron weights $(1+\xi/E)/2$ sum to one. Multiplying their sum by the Jacobian $|d\xi/dE|=E/\sqrt{E^2-\Delta^2}$ proves the formula. This electron spectral density differs from the unweighted count of all positive-energy [quasiparticles](quasiparticle.md), which receives both roots. Its gap-edge divergence is integrable and approaches the normal electronic density at large $|E|$.
