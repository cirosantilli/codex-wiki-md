# Small-interface-parameter growth with liquid superheat

↑ **Parent:** [Superheated two-phase Stefan similarity](superheated-two-phase-stefan-similarity.md)

The small-$\lambda$ limit of [superheated two-phase Stefan similarity](superheated-two-phase-stefan-similarity.md) gives $a\simeq A\sqrt{\kappa t}$, where $A=2/[\sqrt{\vartheta^2/\pi+2S}+\vartheta/\sqrt\pi]$ and $\vartheta=\Theta/\Delta T$. For fixed superheat at large [latent-to-sensible heat ratio](latent-to-sensible-heat-ratio.md), $A\sim\sqrt{2/S}$. For much larger superheat, $A\sim\sqrt\pi/\vartheta$. The approximation retains the leading effect of a hot interior and its coefficient need not be exact when $S$ is only order one.
