# FitzHugh-Nagumo model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)

The [FitzHugh-Nagumo model](fitzhugh-nagumo-model.md) represents an excitable cell by a fast activation variable and a slower recovery variable. A common nondimensional form is $\dot V=V-V^3/3-W+I$, $\dot W=\varepsilon(V+a-bW)$. Its cubic activation response and recovery feedback can produce an excitable resting state or sustained oscillations through [bifurcations](bifurcation.md). Particular reductions and travelling-wave equations can have additional state variables; their signs and parameter conventions must be stated separately.
