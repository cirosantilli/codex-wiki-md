# Self-centralizing property of a Cartan subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md)

For a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) $H$ of a finite-dimensional complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) $L$, $H$ is abelian and $C_L(H)=H$, so it is maximal among abelian subalgebras. Starting from the nilpotent self-normalizing definition, the [generalized-weight decomposition for a nilpotent Lie algebra](generalized-weight-decomposition-for-a-nilpotent-lie-algebra.md) and the [Engel theorem](engel-s-theorem.md) show that the zero generalized [weight space](weight-space.md) of its adjoint action is exactly $H$. Invariance of the [Killing form](killing-form.md) makes $H$ orthogonal to every nonzero generalized [weight space](weight-space.md). The [Lie theorem](lie-s-theorem.md) gives $B([H,H],H)=0$, and nondegeneracy then forces $[H,H]=0$. An element commuting with $H$ normalizes it, hence belongs to $H$.
