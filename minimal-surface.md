# Minimal surface

↑ **Parent:** [Mean curvature](mean-curvature.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_surface)

A minimal submanifold has vanishing mean curvature vector. For a surface in $\mathbb R^3$, this says that the scalar [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) is zero, equivalently that its two [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) sum to zero.
