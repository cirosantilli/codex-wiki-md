# Mach-Zehnder interferometer

↑ **Parent:** [Beam splitter](beam-splitter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mach–Zehnder_interferometer)

A [Mach-Zehnder interferometer](mach-zehnder-interferometer.md) splits an incident wave into two arms and recombines them at a second [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md). A relative [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) changes the interference at its output ports. With symmetric lossless 50:50 splitters, the ports can be calibrated to have detection [probabilities](probability.md) $\cos^2(\theta/2)$ and $\sin^2(\theta/2)$.
