# Restricted isometry property

↑ **Parent:** [Compressed sensing](compressed-sensing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restricted_isometry_property)

The restricted isometry property controls $\|Ax\|_2^2/\|x\|_2^2$ uniformly over [sparse vectors](sparse-vector.md). It supplies quantitative near-orthogonality of disjoint sparse coordinate combinations, not merely normalization of individual columns.
