# Halo abundance mass-budget bound

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md)

A population of distinct, nonoverlapping [dark matter halos](dark-matter-halo.md) cannot contain more total mass per [comoving volume](comoving-volume.md) than the cosmic mean matter density. A narrow mass population therefore obeys $n_cM_h\leq\bar\rho_{m,0}$. Do not sum subhalo masses and their inclusive host masses in the same bound, because that double-counts material. This check can reveal incompatible inferred absorption incidence, object size and halo-mass assumptions.
