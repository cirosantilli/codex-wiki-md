# Disk of convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md)

For a [power series](power-series.md) centred at $c$ with [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) $R$, its disk of convergence is the open [disk](disk-mathematics.md) $|z-c|<R$, on which the [series](series-mathematics.md) has [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md). Boundary points may or may not belong to the larger [domain of convergence](domain-of-convergence.md). When $R=0$ this open [disk](disk-mathematics.md) is empty, although the [power series](power-series.md) still converges at its centre; when $R=\infty$ it is the whole [complex plane](complex-plane.md).
