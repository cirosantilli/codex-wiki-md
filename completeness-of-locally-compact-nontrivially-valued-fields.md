# Completeness of locally compact nontrivially valued fields

↑ **Parent:** [Local compactness criterion for a complete non-Archimedean field](local-compactness-criterion-for-a-complete-non-archimedean-field.md)

In a locally compact nontrivially valued field, the [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) is compact. A Cauchy sequence has a tail inside a translate of this compact set, hence a convergent subsequence. The Cauchy property forces the full sequence to converge.
