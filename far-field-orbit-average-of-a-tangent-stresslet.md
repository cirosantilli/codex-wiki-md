# Far-field orbit average of a tangent stresslet

↑ **Parent:** [Orientation averaging of an axisymmetric stresslet](orientation-averaging-of-an-axisymmetric-stresslet.md)

A [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md) whose centre follows a circle and whose director stays tangent has a leading far-field mean stresslet centred at the circle centre. [Orientation averaging of an axisymmetric stresslet](orientation-averaging-of-an-axisymmetric-stresslet.md) gives normal-axis strength $-S/2$. Thus a [pusher microswimmer](pusher-microswimmer.md) becomes a leading mean [puller microswimmer](puller-microswimmer.md), and conversely. The radius and traversal frequency enter higher-order spatial corrections or averaging time, but not the leading dipole strength.
