<h1 id="adjoint-higgs-breaking-of-su-3-to-su-2-and-u-1">Adjoint-Higgs breaking of SU(3) to SU(2) and U(1)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)

For an adjoint [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) proportional to $\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2)$, the [centralizer](centralizer.md) is $(SU(2)\times U(1))/\mathbb Z_2$. Four [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md) remain massless and four acquire a common mass. This differs from [Fundamental-Higgs breaking of SU(3) to SU(2)](fundamental-higgs-breaking-of-su-3-to-su-2.md).
