# Solar granulation

↑ **Parent:** [Solar granule](solar-granule.md)

The evolving cellular intensity pattern on the [Sun](sun.md)'s [photosphere](photosphere.md), produced by [solar granules](solar-granule.md). Its radiative appearance and flow pattern are related but are not identical observables. Near-surface horizontal flow can redistribute weak magnetic field into the surrounding [intergranular lanes](intergranular-lane.md).
