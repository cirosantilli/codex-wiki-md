# Sea surface temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean](ocean.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_surface_temperature)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) of a specified near-surface [ocean](ocean.md) layer. Skin and bulk measurements refer to different sampling depths. Coupled variations in [sea surface temperature](sea-surface-temperature.md), winds and [thermocline](thermocline.md) depth are central to [El Niño–Southern Oscillation](el-nino-southern-oscillation.md).
