# Kramers-Moyal expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kramers–Moyal_expansion)

The Kramers-Moyal expansion Taylor-expands the gain terms of a jump-process master equation in the jump size. Keeping its first two terms yields a [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md) whose drift and diffusion coefficients are the first two infinitesimal jump moments.
