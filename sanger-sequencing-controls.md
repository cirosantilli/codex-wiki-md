# Sanger sequencing controls

↑ **Parent:** [Sanger sequencing](sanger-sequencing.md)

The endpoint argument identifies what its [scientific controls](scientific-control.md) must interrogate. A known-template comparison tests whether preparation, copying and detection can produce an interpretable ladder. A no-template comparison tests background arising without the intended template. Agreement from an independently primed opposite-strand read tests the proposed [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) through its [reverse complement](reverse-complement.md). These are distinct analytical checks; none follows merely from seeing a [gel band](gel-band.md).

An ambiguous ladder should retain its ambiguity. More than one band at a nominal position can reflect mixed templates or unwanted stops; a missing band can reflect failed detection rather than an absent [nucleotide](nucleotide.md). Four clean lanes are an assumption of the ideal example. They do not establish a unique [genome](genome.md), a biological function, or the frequency of a [mutation](mutation.md) in a population. The historical observations below show why a control for copying chemistry matters even before interpreting the letters.
