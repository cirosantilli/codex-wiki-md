# Affine projective twistor space

↑ **Parent:** [Projective twistor space](projective-twistor-space.md)

For affine [complexified Minkowski space](complexified-minkowski-spacetime.md), the relevant twistor domain is

$$
PT_{\rm aff}=\{[\omega^A,\pi_{A'}]\in\mathbb{CP}^3:\pi\ne0\}
=\mathbb{CP}^3\setminus\mathbb{CP}^1_\infty
\cong\operatorname{Tot}(\mathcal O(1)\oplus\mathcal O(1)).
$$

Incidence can be written $\omega^A=x^{AA'}\pi_{A'}$, absorbing the conventional factor $i$ into the definition of $x$. Each space-time point gives a [twistor line](twistor-line.md) $L_x\cong\mathbb{CP}^1$. On charts $\pi_{0'}\ne0$ and $\pi_{1'}\ne0$, the fibre coordinates transform by $\omega/\pi_{1'}=(\omega/\pi_{0'})/\lambda$, with $\lambda=\pi_{1'}/\pi_{0'}$, giving the two $\mathcal O(1)$ summands. The full [projective twistor space](projective-twistor-space.md) corresponds to [complexified compactified Minkowski space](complexified-compactified-minkowski-space.md).
