# Exponentially weighted supremum norm

↑ **Parent:** [Functional analysis](functional-analysis-split.md)

On continuous functions $u:[0,T]\to X$, the exponentially weighted supremum norm is $\|u\|_\alpha=\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}e^{-\alpha t}\|u(t)\|$. It is equivalent to the ordinary supremum norm and often turns a Volterra integral map into a [contraction mapping](contraction-mapping.md).
