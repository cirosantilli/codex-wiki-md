# Angle between intersecting regular surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Angle between two planes](angle-between-two-planes.md)

For smooth level surfaces $f=0$, $g=0$ meeting at a point where both [gradients](gradient.md) are nonzero, their acute angle is the [angle between two planes](angle-between-two-planes.md) tangent to the surfaces there. Each gradient is a [normal vector](normal-vector.md) to its tangent plane, proving the displayed formula. Vanishing gradients require further analysis because the level-set equation then need not specify a unique tangent plane.
