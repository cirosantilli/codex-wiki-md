# Potential Burgers equation

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

The nonlinear equation

$$
u_t=u_{xx}+u_x^2
$$

is the potential form of the viscous Burgers equation. The substitution $w=e^u$ converts it to the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) $w_t=w_{xx}$.
