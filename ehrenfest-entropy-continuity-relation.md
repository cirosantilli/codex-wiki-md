# Ehrenfest entropy-continuity relation

↑ **Parent:** [Phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md)

If two equilibrium [phases](phase-waves.md) have continuous [entropy](entropy-split.md) and [volume](volume.md) but jumps in second derivatives of [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md), differentiate their [entropy](entropy-split.md) equality along the transition line. Since $dS=(C_P/T)dT-V\alpha_TdP$, one obtains the title slope, for nonzero $\Delta C_P$. Before imposing equal [volume](volume.md), the numerator is $T\Delta(V\alpha_T)$. Equality of [Gibbs free energies](gibbs-free-energy.md) with equal [entropy](entropy-split.md) implies equal [volume](volume.md) along a differentiable coexistence curve on which pressure varies. A constant-pressure curve or zero numerator and denominator needs separate analysis. This entropy-continuity condition is one Ehrenfest relation; [volume](volume.md) continuity gives another independent relation.
