# Martingale compound Poisson process

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md)

A compound Poisson process with rate $\lambda$ and mark $Y$ is a [martingale](martingale-split.md) exactly when $\mathbb E|Y|<\infty$ and $\mathbb E Y=0$.
