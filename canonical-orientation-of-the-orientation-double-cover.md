# Canonical orientation of the orientation double cover

↑ **Parent:** [Orientation double cover](orientation-double-cover.md)

At $(p,o)$ in the [orientation double cover](orientation-double-cover.md), use the derivative of the covering projection to transport the chosen [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) $o$ to the tangent space of the cover. In local oriented sheets this prescription is smooth, so it defines a global [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md). The involution $(p,o)\mapsto(p,-o)$ reverses it.
