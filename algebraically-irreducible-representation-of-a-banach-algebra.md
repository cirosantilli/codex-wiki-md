# Algebraically irreducible representation of a Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a Banach algebra](representation-of-a-banach-algebra.md)

Algebraic irreducibility excludes every proper nonzero invariant [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md), including those that are not [closed](closed-set.md). It is stronger than topological irreducibility, which excludes only [closed](closed-set.md) [invariant subspaces](invariant-subspace.md). For a nonzero irreducible action, every nonzero [vector](vector.md) is cyclic in the algebraic sense: $A\xi=X$.
