# Rotating-frame effective potential

↑ **Parent:** [Effective potential](effective-potential.md)

For uniform rotation, the [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) plus the centrifugal potential generates the non-Coriolis part of the [equation of motion in a rotating frame](equation-of-motion-in-a-rotating-frame.md). Stationary particles obey $\nabla\Phi_{\rm eff}=0$. The [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) changes perturbation dynamics, so curvature of this potential alone is not a general rotating-frame stability criterion.
