# Finite-temperature electron equation of state

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md)

Neglecting interactions and thermal pairs, the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) $f(p)=[\exp((\varepsilon(p)-\mu_{\rm kin})/(k_BT))+1]^{-1}$ with kinetic energy $\varepsilon(p)=\sqrt{m_e^2c^4+p^2c^2}-m_ec^2$ determines the [Electron](electron.md) contribution to a stellar [equation of state](equation-of-state.md). The kinetic [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) is fixed by the electron [number density](number-density.md), not an independently chosen pressure fraction:

$$
n_e=\frac1{\pi^2\hbar^3}\int_0^\infty p^2f(p)\,dp,\qquad
P_e=\frac1{3\pi^2\hbar^3}\int_0^\infty\frac{p^4c^2}{\sqrt{m_e^2c^4+p^2c^2}}f(p)\,dp.
$$

The electron kinetic [internal energy](internal-energy.md) per volume is $(\pi^2\hbar^3)^{-1}\int_0^\infty p^2\varepsilon(p)f(p)\,dp$. In the nondegenerate limit $P_e=n_ek_BT$; at zero [temperature](temperature.md) the integrals become the [equation of state of a cold electron gas](equation-of-state-of-a-cold-electron-gas.md). These are limits of one component and must not be added together as independent electron pressures. When [electron-positron thermal pair abundance](electron-positron-thermal-pair-abundance.md) matters, both charge species must be included, with charge neutrality fixing their net density.
