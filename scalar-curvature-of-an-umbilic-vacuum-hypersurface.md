# Scalar curvature of an umbilic vacuum hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Totally umbilic hypersurface](totally-umbilic-hypersurface.md)

For a timelike unit normal with squared norm epsilon and vanishing ambient Ricci tensor, contraction of the [Gauss equation for a nonnull hypersurface](gauss-equation-for-a-nonnull-hypersurface.md) gives the displayed formula. In signature $(+---)$ the timelike normal has epsilon plus one, so the scalar of the induced negative-definite metric is nonnegative. In signature $(-+++)$ the positive-definite spatial metric instead has scalar $-6\lambda^2$. Replacing the induced metric by its negative reverses the scalar, so the inequality must not be transferred between these conventions without adjustment.
