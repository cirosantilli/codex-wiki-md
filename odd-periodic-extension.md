# Odd periodic extension

↑ **Parent:** [Odd extension](odd-extension.md)

Given a [function](function-split.md) on $(0,L)$, reflect it oddly onto $(-L,0)$ and repeat with [period](period-of-a-function.md) $2L$. Nonzero endpoint limits produce jumps at the joins. The resulting [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) is the [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md) of the original [function](function-split.md); its value at a jump converges to the mean of the one-sided limits under the [Dirichlet convergence theorem for Fourier series](dirichlet-s-theorem-on-fourier-series.md).
