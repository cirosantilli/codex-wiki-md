# Latent-heat polynya

↑ **Parent:** [Polynya](polynya.md)

A latent-heat polynya remains relatively open because winds or currents remove newly formed [sea ice](sea-ice.md). Sustained cooling can produce large amounts of ice even while local ice concentration remains low. Opening is maintained by export, not by latent heat warming the ocean enough to prevent freezing.
