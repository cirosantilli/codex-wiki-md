# Hardy operator

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

The Hardy operator is the averaging operator

$$
Au(x)=\frac1x\int_0^xu(t)\,dt
$$

on functions over the positive half-line or an interval beginning at zero.
