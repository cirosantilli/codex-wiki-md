# Endoplasmic reticulum

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle](organelle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endoplasmic_reticulum)

The [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) is a membrane-enclosed network of tubules and flattened sacs in a typical [eukaryotic](eukaryote.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md). Its continuous boundary encloses the [ER lumen](er-lumen.md), separate from the [cytosol](cytosol.md), and connects with the outer [nuclear envelope](nuclear-envelope.md). [Rough endoplasmic reticulum](rough-endoplasmic-reticulum.md) and [smooth endoplasmic reticulum](smooth-endoplasmic-reticulum.md) are regions of this network.[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; The Endoplasmic Reticulum, opening paragraph and Figure 9.1: continuous membrane and nuclear-envelope connection](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/the-endoplasmic-reticulum.md)
