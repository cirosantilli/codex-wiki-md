# Finiteness of fixed-degree extensions of a p-adic field

↑ **Parent:** [Krasner's lemma](krasner-s-lemma.md)

A [p-adic field](p-adic-field.md) has only finitely many extensions of a fixed degree up to isomorphism. There are finitely many possible residue degrees and a unique [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of each such degree. Over each maximal unramified subfield, a [totally ramified extension](totally-ramified-extension.md) is generated by a root of an [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md). The coefficient space of these polynomials of fixed degree is compact. [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) and [Krasner's lemma](krasner-s-lemma.md) show that sufficiently close polynomials generate isomorphic extensions, giving a finite cover by neighborhoods of a constant extension type.
