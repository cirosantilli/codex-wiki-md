# Dyadic slope martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

For a real [continuous function](continuous-function.md) $f$ on $[0,1]$, let $G_n$ be its secant slope on each length-$2^{-n}$ dyadic cell. Regard $([0,1],\mathcal B,dt)$ as a [probability space](probability-space.md) and use the [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) of half-open dyadic cells with the endpoint $\{1\}$ as a separate null cell. The mean of the two child slopes equals the parent slope, so $(G_n)$ is a [martingale](martingale-split.md). Its [integral](integral.md) gives the [linear interpolation](linear-interpolation.md) of $f$ on that grid. If $f$ is [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md), these slopes are bounded by its [Lipschitz constant](lipschitz-constant.md); the [Lp martingale convergence theorem](lp-martingale-convergence-theorem.md) then supplies a bounded [integral](integral.md) density for $f$.
