# Measurable probability kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional probability](conditional-probability.md)

A family of probability measures $K_y$ on a measurable space for which $y\mapsto K_y(A)$ is measurable for every measurable set $A$. This combines measurable dependence on a parameter with a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) at each parameter. The [conditional measures of a factor](conditional-measures-of-a-factor.md) form such a kernel, with the additional fiber-support property from disintegration.
