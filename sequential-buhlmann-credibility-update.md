<h1 id="sequential-buhlmann-credibility-update">Sequential Bühlmann credibility update</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bühlmann credibility premium](buhlmann-credibility-premium.md)

With fixed structural parameters and $K=v/a$, the [Bühlmann credibility estimate](buhlmann-credibility-premium.md) has form $(\sum_{i=1}^nx_i+Km_0)/(n+K)$. The next observation updates it by the displayed rule. Consequently the estimate decreases exactly when the new observation is less than the previous credibility estimate, with strict equality handled separately.
