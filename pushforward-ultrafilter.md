# Pushforward ultrafilter

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md)

For an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) $U$ on $X$ and a [function](function-split.md) $g:X\to Y$, define $g_*U=\{B\subseteq Y:g^{-1}[B]\in U\}$. This is an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) and inherits countable completeness from a [countably complete ultrafilter](countably-complete-ultrafilter.md). It is nonprincipal exactly when no single fibre of $g$ belongs to $U$. Restricting $U$ to a member of $U$ before pushing forward preserves these properties.
