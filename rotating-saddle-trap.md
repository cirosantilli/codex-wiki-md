# Rotating saddle trap

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)

A planar quadratic saddle $m\omega^2(x^2-y^2)/2$ rotated at angular [speed](speed.md) $\Omega$ becomes linearly confining for the displayed strict inequality. Its rotating-frame mode [frequencies](frequency.md) obey $\epsilon_\pm^2=\Omega^2\pm\omega^2$. Equality has a zero-frequency secular mode and is not robust bounded-motion stability. This is a rotating potential model; a rolling ball on an actual surface has additional constraints.
