# Curvature of a conformally Euclidean metric

↑ **Parent:** [Conformally flat metric](conformally-flat-metric.md)

For a positive conformal factor in dimension $n\geq3$, set $S_{ij}=-u_{ij}+u_i u_j-\tfrac12|\nabla u|^2\delta_{ij}$. These are the coordinate components of the [Schouten tensor](schouten-tensor.md). In the convention $R_{ijkl}=g(e_i,R(e_k,e_l)e_j)$, the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) is $R_{ijkl}=e^{2u}(\delta_{ik}S_{jl}+\delta_{jl}S_{ik}-\delta_{il}S_{jk}-\delta_{jk}S_{il})$. The [orthonormal coframe](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) $e^u dx^i$ has [connection 1-forms](connection-1-form-split.md) $\omega^i{}_j=u_jdx^i-u_idx^j$; substituting into [Cartan's second structure equation](cartan-s-second-structure-equation.md) proves this expression directly. Its contractions give $R_{ij}=(n-2)S_{ij}+\delta_{ij}\sum_kS_{kk}$ and $R=e^{-2u}[-2(n-1)\Delta u-(n-1)(n-2)|\nabla u|^2]$.
