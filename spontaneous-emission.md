# Spontaneous emission

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spontaneous_emission)

[Spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md) is radiative decay of an excited matter [quantum state](quantum-state.md) by emission of a [photon](photon.md), without an applied resonant drive. In a simple Markov decay model the excited-level population obeys $\dot P_e=-\Gamma P_e$. During driven control, the expected emission count is governed by $\Gamma\int P_e(t)dt$; suppressing the lossy level's population is therefore the purpose of dark-state adiabatic transfer.
