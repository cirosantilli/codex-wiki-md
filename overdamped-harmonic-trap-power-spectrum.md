# Overdamped harmonic-trap power spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck power spectrum](ornstein-uhlenbeck-power-spectrum.md)

In the classical thermal model, a coordinate in a [harmonic potential](harmonic-potential.md) obeys $\gamma\dot x+kx=\xi$, where $\gamma$ is the drag coefficient and $\langle\xi(t)\xi(t')\rangle=2\gamma k_BT\delta(t-t')$. Its stationary [one-sided spectral density](one-sided-spectral-density-of-a-real-stationary-time-series.md) per hertz is the displayed [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md). The integral over positive [frequencies](frequency.md) equals $k_BT/k$, consistent with the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md). On a [log-log plot](log-log-plot.md) the spectrum is flat for $f\ll f_c$ and has slope $-2$ for $f\gg f_c$. The crossover is determined by the restoring stiffness and drag; an added detector response multiplies the mechanical spectrum by the squared magnitude of its transfer function.
