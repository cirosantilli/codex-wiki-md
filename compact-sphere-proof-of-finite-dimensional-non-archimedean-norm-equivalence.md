# Compact-sphere proof of finite-dimensional non-Archimedean norm equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Unique extension of an absolute value to a finite extension](unique-extension-of-an-absolute-value-to-a-finite-extension.md)

On a finite-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb Q_p$, the triangle inequality gives an upper bound by a coordinate maximum norm. The coordinate unit sphere is compact. The other norm is continuous and nonzero there, so has a positive minimum. Scaling by powers of $p$ gives the lower bound. The resulting [equivalence of norms in finite dimensions](equivalence-of-norms-in-finite-dimensions.md) proves completeness of finite extensions, and applying the comparison to powers of a field element proves equality of two extending multiplicative [absolute values on a field](absolute-value-algebra.md).
