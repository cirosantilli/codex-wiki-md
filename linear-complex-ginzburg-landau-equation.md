# Linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation

↑ **Parent:** [Complex Ginzburg–Landau equation](complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md)

A [normal mode](normal-mode.md) $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$ has [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) $\omega=Uk+(c_d-i)k^2+i\mu$. The [absolute wavenumber](absolute-wavenumber.md) is the saddle where $d\omega/dk=0$. The [Green function of the linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation](green-function-of-the-linear-complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) distinguishes [convective hydrodynamic instability](convective-hydrodynamic-instability.md) from [absolute hydrodynamic instability](absolute-hydrodynamic-instability.md).
