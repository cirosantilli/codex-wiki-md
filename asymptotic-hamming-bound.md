# Asymptotic Hamming bound

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming bound](hamming-bound.md)

The [Hamming bound](hamming-bound.md) packs disjoint balls of radius approximately $\delta n/2$. Taking logarithms and using the [Hamming ball volume exponent](hamming-ball-volume-exponent.md) gives the bound for $0\le\delta\le1$.
