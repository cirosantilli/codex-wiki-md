# Noise gain of an operational amplifier

↑ **Parent:** [Operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md)

The closed-loop gain from an equivalent differential input-[voltage](voltage.md) disturbance to the output is the noise gain. For an ideal-input [inverting amplifier](inverting-amplifier.md), $\beta=R_1/(R_1+R_2)$ and $G_{\rm n}=1+R_2/R_1$, although the [voltage gain](voltage-gain.md) is $-R_2/R_1$. A dominant-pole amplifier has approximate closed-loop [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md) $f_T/G_{\rm n}$ when loop gain is large at low frequency and the circuit is stable.
