# Semiprime ring

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative algebra](noncommutative-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiprime_ring)

A [ring](ring.md) is semiprime when it has no nonzero [nilpotent ideals](nilpotent-ideal.md). Equivalently, a two-sided [ideal](ideal.md) with square zero must be zero. This is weaker than having no nonzero nilpotent elements: a matrix ring over a [field](field.md) is semiprime and can have nilpotent matrices.
