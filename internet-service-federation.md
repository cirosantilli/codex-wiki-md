# Internet service federation

↑ **Parent:** [Internet architecture](internet-architecture.md)

[Internet service federation](internet-service-federation.md) permits independently administered services to communicate through agreed [protocols](communication-protocol.md). [Internet](internet.md) mail is an example: separate domains can exchange messages without every user joining one provider. Federation differs from placing many replicas behind one mandatory account authority, and it does not itself establish [cryptographic confidentiality](cryptographic-confidentiality.md) or freedom from software failures.[§§2.1–2.3: communication model and domain-based delivery](simple-mail-transfer-protocol-rfc-5321.md)
