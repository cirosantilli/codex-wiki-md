# Relativistic head-on elastic collision with a stationary target

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

In units with $c=1$, a particle of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $M$ and total [energy](energy-split.md) $E$ strikes a stationary target of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $m$. For the nontrivial one-dimensional [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md), reverse the incident [momentum](momentum-split.md) in the [centre-of-mass frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md) and transform back to obtain the displayed outgoing projectile [energy](energy-split.md). The invariant squared total [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) is $s=M^2+m^2+2mE$. If $M\gg m$ and $Em\gg M^2$, then $E'\sim M^2/(2m)$. If $m\gg M$, retaining arbitrary $E/m$ gives $E'\simeq Em/(m+2E)$; only the further fixed-$E$, infinite-target-mass limit gives $E'\to E$.
