# Causal trapping between two spherical horizons

↑ **Parent:** [Five-dimensional Reissner--Nordstrom black hole](five-dimensional-reissner-nordstrom-black-hole.md)

In the regular metric $ds^2=-f\,dv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2d\Omega^2$, a future causal tangent has $\dot v\geq0$. For $\dot v>0$, causality gives $\dot r\leq f\dot v/2-r^2|\dot\Omega|^2/(2\dot v)$. If $f<0$ between two horizons, the areal radius must decrease. An inner event cannot reach the original exterior [future null infinity](future-null-infinity.md) without traversing that band in the forbidden direction.
