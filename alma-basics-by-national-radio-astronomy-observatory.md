# ALMA Basics by National Radio Astronomy Observatory

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by National Radio Astronomy Observatory](work-by-national-radio-astronomy-observatory.md)

The institutional observing guide explains interferometric Fourier sampling, the [primary beam](primary-beam.md) and synthesized resolution. Its “Imaging of extended structures” section gives the approximate $0.6\lambda/b_{\min}$ scale and explains the need for short-spacing measurements. This is an operational rule of thumb, not an exact source-independent cutoff.

[https://almascience.nrao.edu/about-alma/alma-basics](https://almascience.nrao.edu/about-alma/alma-basics)
