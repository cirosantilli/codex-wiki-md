# Network dual potential

↑ **Parent:** [Uncapacitated minimum-cost flow](uncapacitated-minimum-cost-flow.md)

A vertex potential in the dual of an [uncapacitated minimum-cost flow](uncapacitated-minimum-cost-flow.md) yields the edge inequalities $\pi_i-\pi_j\leq c_{ij}$. Its objective is $\sum_i\pi_i b_i$. Adding a common constant leaves all [network reduced costs](network-reduced-cost.md) and the objective unchanged when total net supply is zero. Potentials tight on a [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md) are fixed up to this common constant.
