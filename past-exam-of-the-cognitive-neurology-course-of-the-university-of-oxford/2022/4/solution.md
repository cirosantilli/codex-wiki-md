<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**Set shifting, [working-memory updating](../../../working-memory-updating.md) and [inhibitory control](../../../inhibitory-control.md) are useful distinctions, but patient evidence does not establish them as three exhaustive, anatomically isolated components of [executive function](../../../executive-function.md).** They specify important demands on control: changing a currently relevant rule, replacing or manipulating maintained information, and suppressing an inappropriate action. A useful model must explain selective deficits and shared impairments, as well as failures to organize behavior outside a tightly instructed test.

The influential three-component framework drew support from work modeling [latent variables](../../../latent-variable-split.md) in healthy participants, including [Akira Miyake](../../../akira-miyake.md) and colleagues' study. That evidence addresses [covariance](../../../covariance.md) across tasks while reducing some [executive task impurity](../../../executive-task-impurity.md). It is not itself a [brain lesion](../../../brain-lesion.md) experiment or proof that the same structure survives unchanged after [brain](../../../brain.md) injury. A factor identified in healthy performance need not correspond one-to-one with a cortical region, and two tasks sharing a deficit can share perceptual, [human memory](../../../human-memory.md) or general-demand requirements as well as an executive computation.[2000, healthy-participant latent-variable framework](../../../the-unity-and-diversity-of-executive-functions-and-their-contributions-to-complex-frontal-lobe-tasks-by-akira-miyake-and-others.md)

For [task switching](../../../task-switching.md), paradigms compare performance when repeating a rule with performance when changing it. [Tim Shallice](../../../tim-shallice.md) and colleagues studied 41 patients with focal [prefrontal cortex](../../../prefrontal-cortex.md) [brain lesions](../../../brain-lesion.md) and 38 controls, varying the interval between a task cue and the target. [Brain lesion](../../../brain-lesion.md) subgroups differed in general slowing, [reaction time](../../../reaction-time.md) switch costs and errors. Superior medial damage produced both slowing and increased [reaction time](../../../reaction-time.md) switch cost; the pattern across groups was not one uniform deficit shared by every frontal [brain lesion](../../../brain-lesion.md).[2008, focal-lesion groups, cue intervals and distinct speed/error effects](../../../multiple-effects-of-prefrontal-lesions-on-task-switching-by-tim-shallice-and-others.md)

This supports decomposing switching itself. A participant must perceive the cue, retrieve the rule, maintain readiness, resolve competing mappings and execute a response. A large switch-minus-repeat difference is evidence about the whole contrast, not a direct measurement of one indivisible shifting mechanism. General slowing can also affect raw difference scores. Patient comparisons that examine errors, cue preparation and baseline performance therefore constrain the interpretation more effectively than labeling every poor switching score a failure of flexibility.

For [working-memory updating](../../../working-memory-updating.md), it is essential to separate replacing obsolete information from simple maintenance and from rearranging retained material. [Aron K. Barbey](../../../aron-k-barbey.md) and colleagues compared patients with [dorsolateral prefrontal cortex](../../../dorsolateral-prefrontal-cortex.md) [brain lesions](../../../brain-lesion.md), other [brain lesions](../../../brain-lesion.md) and healthy controls using span-related and [N-back task](../../../n-back-task.md) measures. Their findings particularly implicated manipulation of verbal and spatial knowledge rather than supporting a universal loss of all [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) performance. This is evidence for differentiated control operations, but “manipulation” is not automatically identical to every operational definition of [working-memory updating](../../../working-memory-updating.md).[2013, focal-lesion comparison and maintenance/manipulation distinctions](../../../dorsolateral-prefrontal-contributions-to-human-working-memory-by-aron-k-barbey-and-others.md)

For [inhibitory control](../../../inhibitory-control.md), [Adam R. Aron](../../../adam-r-aron.md) and colleagues found impaired performance on the [stop-signal task](../../../stop-signal-task.md) after damage to the right [inferior frontal gyrus](../../../inferior-frontal-gyrus.md). In this task a frequent go response must occasionally be stopped following a signal. The stopping latency is inferred through a model rather than directly timed as an observable movement. The [brain lesion](../../../brain-lesion.md) result supports a contribution to successful stopping, while the paradigm also requires detecting the signal, maintaining its significance and coordinating competing responses. It consequently does not prove that the region is exclusively an inhibitory module or that every form of interference control is the same process.[2003, focal-lesion evidence from the stop-signal paradigm](../../../stop-signal-inhibition-disrupted-by-damage-to-right-inferior-frontal-gyrus-in-humans-by-adam-r-aron-and-others.md)

Broader patient findings challenge exhaustiveness. [María Roca](../../../maria-roca.md) and colleagues examined how deficits after frontal [brain lesions](../../../brain-lesion.md) related to [fluid intelligence](../../../fluid-intelligence.md). Some traditional executive-test deficits, including [Wisconsin Card Sorting Test](../../../wisconsin-card-sorting-test.md), were largely explained statistically by reduced [fluid intelligence](../../../fluid-intelligence.md), whereas other tasks retained additional deficits. This favors distinguishing general-demand contributions from more specific processes. Statistical adjustment does not, however, prove that [intelligence](../../../intelligence.md) is a separate causal mechanism upstream of [cognitive control](../../../cognitive-control.md): the measures can share neural and cognitive requirements.[2010, task-specific differences after accounting for fluid intelligence](../../../executive-function-and-fluid-intelligence-after-frontal-lobe-lesions-by-maria-roca-and-others.md)

Everyday [goal neglect](../../../goal-neglect.md) provides a further problem for a simple three-label account. In a study led by [Tim Shallice](../../../tim-shallice.md), three patients with traumatic injury to the [prefrontal cortex](../../../prefrontal-cortex.md) struggled with open-ended multiple-subgoal tasks over extended periods, often spending too long on individual activities. Their [perception](../../../perception.md), language and [intelligence](../../../intelligence.md) were relatively good, and two performed well on several conventional frontal tests. The authors argued for difficulty reactivating intentions when the situation did not directly cue them, rather than a general [human memory](../../../human-memory.md) or [motivation](../../../motivation.md) failure.[1991, three-patient comparison and open-ended subgoal management](../../../deficits-in-strategy-application-following-frontal-lobe-damage-in-man-by-tim-shallice-and-others.md)

Such behavior may draw on shifting, [working-memory updating](../../../working-memory-updating.md) and [inhibitory control](../../../inhibitory-control.md), but merely redescribing it as their combined failure does not explain why structured tests can be much better. A theory should specify how intentions are maintained, reactivated and applied over time, how effort is sustained and how progress is monitored. Additional levels of organization or interactions may be needed, even if some lower-level operations are captured by the three components.

Patient inference also has methodological limits. [Brain lesions](../../../brain-lesion.md) disrupt connections and neighboring processes; location covaries with etiology and severity. Apparent preservation can reflect low task sensitivity, and different [brain lesion](../../../brain-lesion.md) groups can differ in premorbid characteristics. Strong claims of separability need reliable tasks, matched nonexecutive requirements, convergent measures and formal comparisons, rather than one significant effect in one group and a nonsignificant effect in another.

**The three components remain a productive framework for designing contrasts and interpreting some selective deficits, but they are a working decomposition rather than a complete inventory of [cognitive control](../../../cognitive-control.md).** Patient studies reveal differentiation within each category, shared demand-related effects, and additional problems of sustained goal application. The most informative account links these behavioral distinctions to interacting computations and networks while predicting when the distinctions should and should not dissociate.
