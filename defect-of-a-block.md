# Defect of a block

↑ **Parent:** [Defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md)

The defect of a [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) is the [integer](integer.md) $d$ for which a [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) has order $p^d$. Conjugacy of [defect groups of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) makes this independent of the choice. A [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) has defect zero when its [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) is trivial.
