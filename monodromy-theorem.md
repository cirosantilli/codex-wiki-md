# Monodromy theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monodromy_theorem)

The monodromy theorem says that, when [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) exists throughout a [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md), its ambiguity depends on [homotopy](homotopy-split.md). Let $D\subset\mathbb C$ be a [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md), $a\in D$, and $g_a$ a [germ of a holomorphic function](germ-of-a-holomorphic-function.md) at $a$. Assume $g_a$ admits [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) along every [continuous path](continuous-path.md) in $D$ starting at $a$. Two such [paths](continuous-path.md) ending at $b$ give the same terminal [germ of a holomorphic function](germ-of-a-holomorphic-function.md) if they are [homotopic](homotopy-split.md) with endpoints fixed in $D$. [Krantz, A Guide to Complex Variables, sections 9.3.4–9.3.5, printed pp. 158–159](a-guide-to-complex-variables-by-steven-g-krantz.md)

Here a [homotopy](homotopy-split.md) with endpoints fixed is a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $H:[0,1]^2\to D$ satisfying

$$
H(0,t)=\gamma_0(t),\quad H(1,t)=\gamma_1(t),\quad H(s,0)=a,\quad H(s,1)=b.
$$

Every intermediate [path](continuous-path.md) must stay in $D$. Equality means agreement on a [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $b$, rather than merely equal values at $b$. [Sections 9.3.1–9.3.3, printed pp. 157–158: terminal elements and fixed endpoint homotopy](a-guide-to-complex-variables-by-steven-g-krantz.md)

The [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md) provides local rigidity; the [monodromy theorem](monodromy-theorem.md) shows precisely how [topology](topology-split.md) controls the remaining global freedom. Deforming a route without crossing a forbidden point cannot change the resulting [germ of a holomorphic function](germ-of-a-holomorphic-function.md). Thus a complicated [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) calculation can be replaced by one on a simpler [homotopic](homotopy-split.md) route. Distinct [homotopy classes](homotopy-class.md) can still give the same continuation: a [rational function](rational-function.md) on its [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) already gives a single-valued answer along every route, even around a [pole](pole.md).
