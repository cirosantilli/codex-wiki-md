<h1 id="stromgren-radius">Strömgren radius</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Strömgren sphere](stromgren-sphere.md)

For ionizing-photon rate $Q_{\rm H}$, uniform pure-hydrogen density $n$, and [Case B recombination](case-b-recombination.md) coefficient $\alpha_B$, the Strömgren radius is

$$
R_S=\left(\frac{3Q_{\rm H}}{4\pi\alpha_Bn^2}\right)^{1/3}.
$$
