# Smooth dual

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic contragredient representation](algebraic-contragredient-representation.md)

The smooth dual consists of the [smooth vectors](smooth-vector.md) of the [algebraic contragredient representation](algebraic-contragredient-representation.md). Their union is a vector subspace because finite intersections of open stabilizers remain open; it is invariant because translation conjugates stabilizers.
