# Between-subject design

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments-split.md)

A design in which distinct participants supply the observations for different levels of a factor. A study may combine [between-subjects](between-subject-design.md) factors with [within-subjects](within-subject-design.md) factors. The number of recruited participants alone does not determine which design was used.
