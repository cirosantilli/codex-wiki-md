# Radical of a module

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

The radical $J(M)$ of a module is the intersection of its maximal submodules. For a finite-dimensional module over a finite-dimensional algebra $A$,

$$
J(M)=J(A)M,
$$

and it is the smallest submodule $N$ for which $M/N$ is a [semisimple module](semisimple-module.md).
