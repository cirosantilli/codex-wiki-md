# Mutual information bound for separable states

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mutual information](quantum-mutual-information.md)

For a [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md) $\rho_{AB}=\sum_kp_k\sigma_A^k\otimes\tau_B^k$, [concavity of quantum conditional entropy](concavity-of-quantum-conditional-entropy.md) gives $S(A|B)_\rho\geq\sum_kp_kS(\sigma_A^k)\geq0$. Consequently $I(A:B)=S(A)-S(A|B)\leq S(A)$; swapping the parties gives the displayed bound. In finite dimension it is at most $\log_2\min(d_A,d_B)$. Thus more than one bit of mutual information witnesses entanglement of two qubits. Smaller positive mutual information need not be entanglement, because separable states can be classically correlated.
