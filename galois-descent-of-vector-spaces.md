# Galois descent of vector spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md)

For a [Finite Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) $L/K$ and a finite-dimensional $L$-space $V$ with a [semilinear action](semilinear-action.md) of its [Galois group](galois-group.md), the map $L\otimes_KV^G\to V$ is an isomorphism. The vectors $\sum_{\sigma}\sigma(a)\sigma(v)$ are invariant and span $V$ by the [Artin independence theorem](linear-independence-of-distinct-field-embeddings.md). Choosing an invariant $L$-basis then identifies the invariant space with its $K$-span. The proof does not divide by the extension degree.
