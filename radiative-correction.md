# Radiative correction

↑ **Parent:** [Perturbative quantum field theory](perturbative-quantum-field-theory-split.md)

A radiative correction is a quantum correction beyond the classical or tree approximation, represented in [perturbative quantum field theory](perturbative-quantum-field-theory-split.md) by [Feynman diagrams](feynman-diagram.md) of positive [loop order](loop-order.md). Such corrections can alter a [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) and its derivatives; symmetries and [renormalization](renormalization.md) constrain which changes are allowed.
