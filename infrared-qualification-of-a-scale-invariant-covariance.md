# Infrared qualification of a scale-invariant covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Scale-invariant inflationary power spectrum](scale-invariant-inflationary-power-spectrum.md)

An exact $P(k)=A/k^3$ has divergent unsubtracted point [variance](variance-split.md). Its finite isotropic [covariance](covariance.md) difference is the displayed logarithm, unchanged by simultaneous dilation of $r,r_0$. Thus the nonzero-mode scaling statement has a natural subtraction interpretation. Exact dilation invariance of a continuous finite-variance isotropic [covariance](covariance.md) itself would instead force a constant [covariance](covariance.md), corresponding only to a zero-mode spectral measure. Cutoffs or a zero-mode subtraction must accompany the nontrivial spectrum.
