# Smooth pasting

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal stopping](optimal-stopping.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_pasting)

At a regular exercise boundary for a diffusion [optimal stopping](optimal-stopping.md) problem, value matching is commonly accompanied by matching first derivatives. This makes the value $C^1$ across the boundary and eliminates a derivative-jump [local time of a semimartingale](local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md) term in the generalized [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md). It does not assert that second derivatives agree, or that smooth fit holds for every possible stopping problem.
