<h1 id="super-poincare-group">Super-Poincaré group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry](four-dimensional-n-1-supersymmetry.md)

The [Super-Poincaré group](super-poincare-group.md) combines [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md) and [supertranslations](supertranslation.md). Its even part acts by spin-group transformations and ordinary spacetime translations; odd parameters generate the [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md). Their graded commutator closes on translations through the [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md). A superspace tensor built from invariant coframes and Lorentz-invariant contractions is invariant under the full group.
