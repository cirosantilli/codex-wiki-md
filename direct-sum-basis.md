# Direct sum basis

↑ **Parent:** [Direct sum](direct-sum.md)

For [bases](basis.md) $(u_i)$ of $U$ and $(v_j)$ of $W$, the displayed pairs form a [basis](basis.md) of $U\oplus W$. Expanding each component proves spanning; comparing components of a vanishing [linear combination](linear-combination.md) proves [linear independence](linear-independence.md). Therefore $\dim(U\oplus W)=\dim U+\dim W$ for two [finite-dimensional vector spaces](finite-dimensional-vector-space.md).
