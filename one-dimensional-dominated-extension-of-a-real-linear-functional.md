# One-dimensional dominated extension of a real linear functional

↑ **Parent:** [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md)

A real [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $g$ on a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md), dominated by a [sublinear function](sublinear-function.md) $p$, can be extended across one new vector $v$ by setting $\widetilde g(m+tv)=g(m)+tc$. The displayed lower and upper bounds are compatible because $g(m+n)\leq p(m-v)+p(n+v)$. A value between them preserves domination for both signs of $t$. Chain unions and [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) then yield the full [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md).
