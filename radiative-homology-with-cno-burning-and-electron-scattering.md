# Radiative homology with CNO burning and electron scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Homology relations for radiative stars](homology-relations-for-radiative-stars.md)

Using the idealized [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md) law $\epsilon\propto\rho T^{16}$ and constant [electron-scattering opacity](electron-scattering-opacity.md), [homology relations for radiative stars](homology-relations-for-radiative-stars.md) give

$$
R\propto M^{15/19},\qquad L\propto M^3,\qquad T_{\rm eff}\propto L^{9/76}.
$$

These retain ideal-gas pressure support; significant [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) or convection changes the model.
