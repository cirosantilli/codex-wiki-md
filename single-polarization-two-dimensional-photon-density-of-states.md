# Single-polarization two-dimensional photon density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Density of states](density-of-states.md)

For an area $A$ with periodic in-plane [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), one polarization contributes $A\,2\pi k\,dk/(2\pi)^2$ [normal modes](normal-mode.md) of the [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md). For linear [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega=ck$ and effective physical volume $Ad$, this gives the [density of states](density-of-states.md) in the title. No extra factor two for polarization is allowed. Opposite in-plane directions are already counted in the full circle of [wavevectors](wavevector.md).
