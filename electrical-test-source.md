# Electrical test source

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical measurement](electrical-measurement.md)

A prescribed [voltage source](voltage-source.md) or [current source](current-source.md) used to probe a terminal response. For a suppressed [linear resistive circuit](linear-resistive-circuit.md), the ratio of terminal voltage to entering [electric current](electric-current.md) determines the [Thévenin resistance](thevenin-resistance.md) when finite.
