# Endpoint and continuous polarization thresholds of a contact electron gas

↑ **Parent:** [Contact-interaction Hartree-Fock spin energy](contact-interaction-hartree-fock-spin-energy.md)

Let $r=(\gamma/A)(n/2)^{1/3}$ and $P=(n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow)/n$. In units $2A(n/2)^{5/3}$ the spin-dependent [energy](energy-split.md) is $[(1+P)^{5/3}+(1-P)^{5/3}]/2-r(1+P^2)/2$. Comparing only $P=0$ and $P=1$ gives the endpoint crossing $r=2^{5/3}-2$. Expanding near $P=0$ gives a quadratic coefficient $5/9-r/2$, so the unpolarized state first loses local stability at $r=10/9$. The endpoint crossing is therefore not the actual continuous onset; intermediate polarizations matter.
