# Transverse optical coherence length

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial optical coherence](spatial-optical-coherence.md)

The [transverse optical coherence length](transverse-optical-coherence-length.md) summarizes the separation over which equal-time [spatial optical coherence](spatial-optical-coherence.md) remains appreciable. It depends on a contrast criterion and on the source brightness profile. For a distant incoherent source of angular diameter $\Theta$, its characteristic scale is $\lambda/\Theta$. A uniform circular source gives [uniform-disk stellar visibility](uniform-disk-stellar-visibility.md) $2J_1(x)/x$, with $x=\pi b\Theta/\lambda$ and baseline $b$. Its first zero is $b=1.21967\lambda/\Theta$. A uniformly bright one-dimensional strip instead has its first zero at $\lambda/\Theta$; the numerical factor is a source-model choice, not a universal constant.
