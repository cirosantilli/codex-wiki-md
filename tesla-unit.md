# Tesla (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla_(unit))

The [tesla](tesla-unit.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). From the magnetic [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md), $1\,\mathrm T=1\,\mathrm{N\,s\,C^{-1}\,m^{-1}}$. [OpenStax, magnetic-field units](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/11-2-magnetic-fields-and-lines).
