# Inertia tensor of a uniform cube

↑ **Parent:** [Inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md)

For a uniform [cube](cube.md) of side $s$ and [mass](mass.md) $M$, reflection [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) makes central off-diagonal inertia components zero. Equal coordinate second moments are $\langle x^2\rangle=\langle y^2\rangle=\langle z^2\rangle=s^2/12$, so each diagonal component is $Ms^2/6$. Thus every central axis is a [principal axis](principal-axis.md). A nonspherical shape can have an isotropic central [inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md): that tensor measures only quadratic moments of the [mass](mass.md) distribution, not the whole shape.
