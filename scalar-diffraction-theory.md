# Scalar diffraction theory

↑ **Parent:** [Physical optics](physical-optics-split.md)

[Scalar diffraction theory](scalar-diffraction-theory.md) approximates [light](light.md) by one complex field satisfying a scalar [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md). Aperture propagation is represented by an [integral](integral-split.md) of secondary contributions, with vector [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) and detailed electromagnetic edge conditions suppressed. The approximation is useful for many apertures large compared with [wavelength](wavelength.md), but is not an exact substitute for [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations-split.md) at arbitrary apertures.
