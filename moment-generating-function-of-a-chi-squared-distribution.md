# Moment-generating function of a chi-squared distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md)

A sum of the squares of $n$ [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) with the [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) has the [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $n$ degrees of freedom. A scaled [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) and factorization by independence give its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) $(1-2t)^{-n/2}$ for $t<1/2$. For positive $n$, this [expectation](expected-value.md) diverges when $t\geq1/2$.
