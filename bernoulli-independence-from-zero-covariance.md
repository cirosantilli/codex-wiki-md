# Bernoulli independence from zero covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Uncorrelated random variables](uncorrelated-random-variables.md)

For two [Bernoulli random variables](bernoulli-distribution.md) with joint probabilities $a,b,c,d$ in the order $(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)$, their [covariance](covariance.md) is $d-(c+d)(b+d)=ad-bc$. Zero covariance fixes the $(1,1)$ probability to the product of its marginals; subtracting this from each marginal fixes all three remaining joint probabilities to their products. Thus [uncorrelated random variables](uncorrelated-random-variables.md) taking only zero and one are independent, including degenerate marginals. The implication is special to this two-point setting and fails for general distributions.
