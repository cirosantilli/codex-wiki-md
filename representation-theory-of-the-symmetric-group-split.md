# Representation theory of the symmetric group

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory](representation-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representation_theory_of_the_symmetric_group)

The representation theory of $S_n$ is organized by a [partition of an integer](partition-of-an-integer.md). Over the complex numbers, partitions label the irreducible Specht modules; over positive-characteristic fields, the simple modules are labelled by regular partitions.
