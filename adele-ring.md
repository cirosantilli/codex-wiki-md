# Adele ring

↑ **Parent:** [Class field theory](class-field-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele_ring)

The adele ring is the restricted product of the completions $K_v$ with respect to the valuation rings $\mathcal O_v$ at finite places.
