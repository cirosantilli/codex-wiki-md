# Neutron-to-proton ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron-proton chemical equilibrium](neutron-proton-chemical-equilibrium.md)

Neglecting chemical potentials, the equilibrium abundance ratio is $n_n/n_p\simeq\exp[-(m_n-m_p)c^2/(k_BT)]$. Weak freeze-out fixes an approximate starting ratio, which is then reduced by [free-neutron decay after freeze-out](free-neutron-decay-after-freeze-out.md) before most remaining neutrons bind into helium.
