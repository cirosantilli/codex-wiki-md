# Outer-edge source of a viscous disk

↑ **Parent:** [Steady viscous disk supplied by a radial source](steady-viscous-disk-supplied-by-a-radial-source.md)

A narrow [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) source approaching the outer edge from inside, with $S=S_0\delta(r-r_2)$ normalized to have full weight, gives $\dot M=2\pi r_2S_0$ and the displayed steady interior profile. This follows by replacing the inner source integral by $r_2S_0$ and integrating $u^{-1/2}$. The flux jumps from $\dot M$ just inside to zero outside the injecting edge. Thus a zero exterior flux is consistent, whereas a continuous zero interior boundary trace is not. A half-weight convention for a symmetric endpoint [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) represents a different total injected mass; it must not be silently mixed with the full-weight boundary source.
