# Anisotropic renormalization group

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization group](renormalization-group.md)

A [renormalization-group transformation](renormalization-group-transformation.md) can scale different coordinate directions by different factors to keep distinct gradient terms fixed. If momentum component $p_i$ scales to $b^{w_i}p_i$, its coordinate scales by $b^{-w_i}$. The momentum/volume measure has total scaling weight $\sum_iw_i$, motivating the [anisotropic effective dimension](anisotropic-effective-dimension.md).
