# Martingale deflator

↑ **Parent:** [Risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md)

A martingale deflator is a strictly positive adapted process whose product with each cum-dividend asset gain is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). After normalization by a [numéraire](numeraire.md), a martingale deflator determines an [equivalent martingale measure](risk-neutral-measure.md).
