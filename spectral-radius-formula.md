# Spectral radius formula

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral radius](spectral-radius.md)

For an element $a$ of a complex [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md),

$$
r(a)=\max\{|\lambda|:\lambda\in\sigma(a)\}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\lVert a^n\rVert^{1/n}.
$$
