# Galaxy cluster

↑ **Parent:** [Large-scale structure of the universe](large-scale-structure-of-the-universe-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaxy_cluster)

A [galaxy cluster](galaxy-cluster.md) is a gravitationally bound collection of [galaxies](galaxy-split.md), diffuse gas and [dark matter](dark-matter.md). Much of its ordinary matter can reside in hot intracluster gas, visible through [thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) and the [Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md). The two signals weight the electron density differently, making the cluster useful both as a structure-formation probe and, with a geometrical model, as a distance indicator.
