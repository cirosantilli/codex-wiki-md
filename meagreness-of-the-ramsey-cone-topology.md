# Meagreness of the Ramsey cone topology

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey cone topology](ramsey-cone-topology.md)

The whole [space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers](space-of-infinite-subsets-of-the-natural-numbers.md) is a [meagre set](meagre-set.md) in the [Ramsey cone topology](ramsey-cone-topology.md). For $D_j=\{X:\min X=j\}$, the closure is $\{X:j\in X\}$, which has empty interior: every infinite ground set can be thinned to omit $j$. Thus all $D_j$ are [nowhere dense](nowhere-dense-set.md), while their countable union is the whole space. In contrast, the whole space is not meagre in either the [Ellentuck topology](ellentuck-topology.md) or the [ordinary topology on infinite subsets](ordinary-topology-on-infinite-subsets.md).
