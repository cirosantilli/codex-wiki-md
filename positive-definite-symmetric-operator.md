# Positive definite symmetric operator

↑ **Parent:** [Positive operator](positive-operator.md)

A [symmetric operator](symmetric-operator.md) $L$ on a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) is strictly positive definite when $\langle Lv,v\rangle>0$ for every nonzero $v$ in its [operator domain](operator-domain.md). Strict positivity proves uniqueness of a solution of $Lu=f$, but in infinite dimension it need not prove existence for every $f$. A [uniformly positive definite symmetric operator](uniformly-positive-definite-symmetric-operator.md) additionally has a positive lower bound independent of $v$. Symmetry must be stated separately when positivity is defined only by a real quadratic expression: adding a [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md) changes the operator without changing that expression.
