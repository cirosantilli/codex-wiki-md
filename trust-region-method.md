# Trust-region method

↑ **Parent:** [Trust region](trust-region.md)

A [trust-region method](trust-region-method.md) approximately minimizes a local model inside a bounded neighborhood, then tests the step using the [trust-region acceptance ratio](trust-region-acceptance-ratio.md). Poor agreement rejects the trial and shrinks the radius; sufficient agreement accepts it; very good agreement may justify expanding the radius. A [fraction of Cauchy decrease](fraction-of-cauchy-decrease.md) gives a quantitative minimum quality for approximate subproblem solves. The radius is an adaptive measure of the model's usable range, rather than a prescribed sequence tending to zero.
