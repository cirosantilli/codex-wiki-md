# Cotangent

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)

The cotangent is the quotient $\cot z=\cos z/\sin z$, wherever the [sine](sine.md) denominator is nonzero. Its meromorphic continuation has simple [poles](pole.md) at integer multiples of $\pi$.
