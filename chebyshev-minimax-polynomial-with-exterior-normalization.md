# Chebyshev minimax polynomial with exterior normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev polynomial domination lemma](chebyshev-polynomial-domination-lemma.md)

For $0<a<b$, the displayed polynomial minimizes $\max_{t\in[a,b]}|p(t)|$ among real polynomials of degree at most $k$ with $p(0)=1$. Its $k+1$ alternating extremal values prove the assertion: a strictly smaller competitor would make the difference change sign in each consecutive extremal interval and also vanish at zero outside $[a,b]$, giving too many roots. The normalizing factor makes either the monic or the usual [Chebyshev polynomial](chebyshev-polynomial.md) convention give the same ratio. For $\kappa=b/a$ and $q=(\sqrt\kappa-1)/(\sqrt\kappa+1)$, its maximum is $2q^k/(1+q^{2k})$. This yields an error bound for polynomial acceleration of [Richardson iteration](richardson-iteration.md).
