# Critical nucleus radius

↑ **Parent:** [Classical nucleation theory](classical-nucleation-theory.md)

In the spherical capillarity approximation, the nonzero maximum of $\Delta G=4\pi r^2\gamma+(4\pi/3)r^3\Delta g_V$ lies at $r^*=-2\gamma/\Delta g_V$ when $\Delta g_V<0$. Smaller [nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) tend to shrink and larger ones tend to grow. Surface anisotropy and molecular-scale structure limit the approximation.
