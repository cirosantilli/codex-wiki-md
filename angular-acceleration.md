# Angular acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Angular velocity](angular-velocity.md)

Angular acceleration is the time derivative of [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md), $\boldsymbol\alpha=d\boldsymbol\omega/dt$. For rotation about a fixed axis, its signed scalar component obeys $\tau=I\alpha$ when the axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) is constant and $\tau$ is the total applied [torque](torque.md) about that axis.
