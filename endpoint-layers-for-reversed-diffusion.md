# Endpoint layers for reversed diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Interior layer at a simple zero of advection](interior-layer-at-a-simple-zero-of-advection.md)

For $-\varepsilon^2y''+2(x-x_*)y'+\cdots=f$ on an interval straddling $x_*$, the bounded leading interior solution cannot connect unequal constants. A common bulk value is instead selected by an inner [solvability condition](solvability-condition.md), and width-$\varepsilon^2$ endpoint layers enforce the two boundary values. At endpoints $x=0,2$ with $x_*=1$, their leading decaying factors are $e^{-2x/\varepsilon^2}$ and $e^{-2(2-x)/\varepsilon^2}$. The interior layer still corrects derivative mismatches at smaller amplitude.
