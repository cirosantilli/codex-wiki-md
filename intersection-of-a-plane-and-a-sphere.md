# Intersection of a plane and a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

For a [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $r>0$ and a genuine [plane](plane.md) at perpendicular [distance](distance.md) $d\ge0$ from its centre, the intersection is a [circle](circle.md) of radius $\rho$ when $d<r$, a single point when $d=r$, and empty when $d>r$. The [circle](circle.md) centre is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) of the [sphere](sphere.md) centre onto the [plane](plane.md). Decomposing a radius into its perpendicular component of length $d$ and its planar component of length $\rho$ proves the formula by the [Pythagorean theorem](pythagorean-theorem.md).
