# Fourier decay in a bounded complex strip

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform of a compactly supported distribution](fourier-transform-of-a-compactly-supported-distribution.md)

For a [test function](test-function.md) $\varphi$ supported in a fixed ball of radius $R$ and a fixed height bound $H$, repeated [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
|\widehat\varphi(\xi+i\eta)|\leq C_{R,H,L}
\max_{|\alpha|\leq2L}\|\partial^\alpha\varphi\|_\infty(1+|\xi|^2)^{-L},
\qquad |\eta|\leq H.
$$

Apply $(1-\Delta)^L$ to the compactly supported smooth function $e^{x\cdot\eta}\varphi(x)$ inside the real-frequency Fourier integral. The bounded $\eta$ absorbs its derivative and exponential factors into the constant. This estimate makes the numerator of a [Hörmander staircase](hormander-staircase.md) integral absolutely integrable and makes truncated contour endpoints vanish.
