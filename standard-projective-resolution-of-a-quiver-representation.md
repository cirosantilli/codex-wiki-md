# Standard projective resolution of a quiver representation

↑ **Parent:** [Projective resolution](projective-resolution.md)

Set $A=kQ$, $P_i=Ae_i$, $X_i=e_iX$. There is an exact sequence

$$
0\to\bigoplus_{\rho:i\to j}P_j\otimes_kX_i\xrightarrow{d}\bigoplus_iP_i\otimes_kX_i\to X\to0,
$$

where $d(p\otimes x)=p\rho\otimes x-p\otimes f_\rho(x)$ in the source and target summands, and the augmentation is $p\otimes x\mapsto px$. Each $P_i$ is a summand of $A$, so the two terms are [projective modules](projective-module.md). This also works for infinite-dimensional modules.
