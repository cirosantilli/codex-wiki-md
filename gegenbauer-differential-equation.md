# Gegenbauer differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Gegenbauer polynomial](gegenbauer-polynomial.md)

The Gegenbauer differential equation is the displayed Sturm–Liouville eigenvalue equation defining the [Gegenbauer polynomial](gegenbauer-polynomial.md). Multiplication by $(1-x^2)^{\alpha-1/2}$ puts it in self-adjoint form.
