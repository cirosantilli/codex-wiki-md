# Emission spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md)

The distribution of emitted radiation with [wavelength](wavelength.md) or [frequency](frequency.md). Resolved atomic [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) correspond to transitions between energy levels; a source's excitation and optical depth affect the observed intensities.
