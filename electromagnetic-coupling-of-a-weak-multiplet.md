# Electromagnetic coupling of a weak multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md)

With the plus-sign convention $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+igT_3W_\mu^3+ig'YB_\mu$, let $\tan\theta_W=g'/g$ and $A_\mu=\sin\theta_WW_\mu^3+\cos\theta_WB_\mu$. The coefficient of $A_\mu$ is $g\sin\theta_WT_3+g'\cos\theta_WY=e(T_3+Y)$, where $e=g\sin\theta_W=g'\cos\theta_W$. The extra minus sign in the interaction Lagrangian comes from the $i$ in $\bar\psi i\gamma^\mu D_\mu\psi$. It reproduces the [electric charges](electric-charge.md) of every component of an [Standard Model representation](standard-model-representation.md).
