# Adjoint Chevalley group

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md)

Fix a [Chevalley basis](chevalley-basis.md) and its integral lattice. The adjoint Chevalley group over a [field](field.md) is generated by the root operators obtained from the integral divided-power polynomials $x_\alpha(t)=\sum_j t^j(\operatorname{ad}e_\alpha)^j/j!$ on that lattice. The coefficients are first computed over the integers and then reduced to the field; factorials need not be invertible there. This is the adjoint construction; other weight lattices give other isogeny forms of [groups](group-split.md) of Lie type.
