# Normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics](mathematics-split.md)

Choosing the multiplicative scale of a mathematical or physical quantity by a stated convention. Examples include a probability density integrating to one, a quantum state having unit norm, and relativistic [spinors](spinor.md) satisfying $\bar u u=2m$. A quoted [normalization](normalization.md) must specify the constraint and the underlying units or state convention.
