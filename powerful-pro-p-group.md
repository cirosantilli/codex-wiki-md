# Powerful pro-p group

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)

A [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) whose closed [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) lies in its closed power subgroup $G^{p^\epsilon}$, with $\epsilon=1$ for odd $p$ and $\epsilon=2$ for $p=2$. This is the profinite analogue of a [powerful p-group](powerful-p-group.md). Finitely generated torsion-free examples are exactly the [uniform pro-p groups](uniform-pro-p-group.md). The additive [group](group-split.md) of a finite product of [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md) is an abelian example.
