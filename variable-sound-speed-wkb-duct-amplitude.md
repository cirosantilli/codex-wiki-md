# Variable-sound-speed WKB duct amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [WKB amplitude in a slowly varying duct](wkb-amplitude-in-a-slowly-varying-duct.md)

For $\nabla\cdot(k_0(X)^{-2}\nabla p)+p=0$ in a slowly varying rigid duct, a real local transverse [normal mode](normal-mode.md) $F$ with squared norm $N(X)$ has longitudinal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k(X)$. The [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) is $p=A(X)F(y,X)\exp[-i\epsilon^{-1}\int^Xk(\xi)d\xi]$. The moving-wall [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) and next-order solvability give $2kN k_0^{-2}A_X+(kN k_0^{-2})_X A=0$, hence the displayed amplitude. For $F=\cos(n\pi y/R)$, $N=R$ for $n\ge1$ and $N=2R$ for $n=0$. The factor $k_0$ is essential when the coefficient in the divergence-form equation varies. This approximation requires a mode separated from cutoff and from other modes.
