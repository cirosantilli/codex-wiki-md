# Interior second-derivative estimate for the Poisson equation

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md)

If $u\in H^1(V)$ and $\Delta u=f\in L^2(V)$ weakly, then $u\in H^2_{\mathrm{loc}}(V)$. Test the weak equation with $-\delta_{-h}(\eta^2\delta_hu)$, move the first difference quotient onto $Du$, and bound the remaining quotient on the forcing side by the first derivative of $\eta^2\delta_hu$. [Young inequality](young-s-inequality-for-products.md) absorbs the localized second derivatives. Uniform bounds in $h$, together with the [Sobolev characterization by bounded difference quotients](sobolev-characterization-by-bounded-difference-quotients.md), give the assertion without differentiating $f$. The constant depends only on dimension and the positive distance from $U$ to the boundary of $V$.
