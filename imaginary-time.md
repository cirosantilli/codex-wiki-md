# Imaginary time

↑ **Parent:** [Wick rotation](wick-rotation.md)

Analytic continuation from real [time](time-in-physics.md) to $t=-i\tau$ turns unitary evolution into $e^{-\tau H/\hbar}$. A [thermal trace](thermal-trace.md) has extent $\beta\hbar$, or $\beta$ when $\hbar=1$. This converts oscillatory [action](action.md) phases into an [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md) where convergence permits it; it is not the actual laboratory [time](time-in-physics.md) of a dissipative process.
