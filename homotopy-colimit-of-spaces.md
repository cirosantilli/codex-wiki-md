# Homotopy colimit of spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)

A [homotopy colimit of spaces](homotopy-colimit-of-spaces.md) is the homotopy-invariant replacement for a [colimit](colimit.md) of spaces. For a filtered diagram of [CW complexes](cw-complex.md) with subcomplex inclusions, the union is a model: the inclusions are [cofibrations](cofibration.md), and every sphere map and homotopy has image in a finite subcomplex. A [suspension spectrum](suspension-spectrum.md) carries this construction to the corresponding [homotopy colimit of spectra](homotopy-colimit-of-spectra.md).
