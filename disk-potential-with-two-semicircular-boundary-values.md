# Disk potential with two semicircular boundary values

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson kernel for the unit disk](poisson-kernel-for-the-unit-disk.md)

On a disk of radius $a$, prescribe $V_1$ on the upper open semicircle and $V_2$ on the lower one. The bounded [harmonic extension](harmonic-extension.md) has the formula in the title for $r<a$. The boundary data have only a constant term and odd sine [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md), giving $u=(V_1+V_2)/2+[2(V_1-V_2)/\pi]\sum_{m\geq0}(r/a)^{2m+1}\sin((2m+1)\theta)/(2m+1)$. The complex series is half of $\log[(1+w)/(1-w)]$, where $w=(r/a)e^{i\theta}$. Its quotient has positive real part, so taking the imaginary part yields the displayed principal [arctangent](inverse-tangent.md) without an argument ambiguity. At the two boundary jumps, the radial limit is the mean; the harmonic function has no continuous extension matching both adjacent constant arcs there.
