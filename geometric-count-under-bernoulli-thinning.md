# Geometric count under Bernoulli thinning

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md)

Independently retain each event of a zero-based [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) count with probability $\alpha$. Conditional on the original count, the retained count is binomial, so its [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) is $p/[1-(1-p)(1-\alpha+\alpha z)]$. This is again a zero-based [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md), with success parameter $p_\alpha$ and mean $\alpha(1-p)/p$. In [excess of loss reinsurance](excess-of-loss-reinsurance.md), $\alpha$ is the probability that a claim exceeds the retention.
