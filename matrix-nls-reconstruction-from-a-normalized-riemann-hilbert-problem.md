# Matrix NLS reconstruction from a normalized Riemann-Hilbert problem

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann-Hilbert problem](riemann-hilbert-problem.md)

Assume a regular invertible normalized [Riemann-Hilbert problem](riemann-hilbert-problem.md) solution $\mu=I+\mu_1/k+\mu_2/k^2+\cdots$ with jump tending to the identity and conjugated by $e^{-i(kx+2k^2t)\sigma_3}$. Residuals of its two differential equations have no jump and vanish at infinity, so [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md) gives a [Lax pair](lax-pair.md). Coefficient comparison gives the displayed reconstruction and $A=2Q$; compatibility yields $iQ_t-Q_{xx}\sigma_3+2Q^3\sigma_3=0$. A jump matrix tending to zero instead is inconsistent with the normalization. Recovering the initial jump from a prescribed initial potential still requires direct scattering.
