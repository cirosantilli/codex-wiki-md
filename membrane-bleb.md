# Membrane bleb

↑ **Parent:** [Cell migration](cell-migration.md)

A [membrane bleb](membrane-bleb.md) is a rounded protrusion formed when the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) locally detaches from or loses support by the [cell cortex](cell-cortex.md). Pressure expands the initially cortex-poor region; cortical reassembly can then stabilize or retract it.
