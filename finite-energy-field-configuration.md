# Finite-energy field configuration

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field-theory soliton](classical-field-theory-soliton-split.md)

A finite-energy field configuration has integrable [energy density](energy-density.md) after the vacuum energy has been subtracted. The boundary conditions used for a localized [classical field-theory soliton](classical-field-theory-soliton-split.md) require approaching a vacuum at spatial infinity. In one dimension the two ends may approach distinct [scalar-field vacua](scalar-field-vacuum.md); in a gauge theory the asymptotic field can have nontrivial winding even when its local energy density tends to zero.
