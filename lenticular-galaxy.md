# Lenticular galaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenticular_galaxy)

A [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) with a stellar disc and a central bulge, but little of the prominent spiral structure characteristic of a [spiral galaxy](spiral-galaxy.md). Together with [elliptical galaxies](elliptical-galaxy.md), lenticular galaxies are conventionally grouped as [early-type galaxies](early-type-galaxy.md).
