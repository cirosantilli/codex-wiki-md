# Exchange economy

↑ **Parent:** [General equilibrium theory](general-equilibrium-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exchange_economy)

An [exchange economy](exchange-economy.md) specifies consumers, their [preference relations](preference-relation.md), their consumption sets and their initial endowments. A [consumption allocation](consumption-allocation.md) is feasible when aggregate consumption equals aggregate endowment; free-disposal models instead allow a coordinatewise inequality. These conventions must be distinguished when specifying supporting prices.
