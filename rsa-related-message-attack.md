# RSA related-message attack

↑ **Parent:** [RSA cryptosystem](rsa-cryptosystem.md)

An attack exploiting a known relation between [plaintexts](plaintext.md) encrypted using one textbook [RSA cryptosystem](rsa-cryptosystem.md) key. For public exponent three and a known nonzero additive difference, two cubic equations can yield a linear relation recovering a [plaintext](plaintext.md). A small unknown difference can be enumerated.
