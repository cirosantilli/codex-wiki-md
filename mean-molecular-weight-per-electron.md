# Mean molecular weight per electron

↑ **Parent:** [Mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md)

The mean molecular weight per electron is the mass carried per free electron in units of atomic mass $m_u$. Fully ionized hydrogen has $\mu_e=1$, while helium, carbon, and oxygen have approximately $\mu_e=2$. It converts [electron number density](electron-number-density.md) to bulk [mass density](density.md) in the [equation of state of a cold electron gas](equation-of-state-of-a-cold-electron-gas.md).
