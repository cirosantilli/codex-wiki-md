# Breathing excitation after a trap-frequency quench

↑ **Parent:** [Three-ion axial normal modes](three-ion-axial-normal-modes.md)

Changing the trap [frequency](frequency.md) changes the equilibrium spacing proportionally to $\omega^{-2/3}$ without shifting the centre. The old equilibrium displacement from the new one is parallel to the breathing [eigenvector](eigenvector.md). Starting at rest therefore excites that mode alone at linear order; the outer charges oscillate equally and oppositely while the central charge stays fixed.
