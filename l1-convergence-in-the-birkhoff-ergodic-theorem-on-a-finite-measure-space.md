# L1 convergence in the Birkhoff ergodic theorem on a finite measure space

↑ **Parent:** [Birkhoff ergodic theorem](birkhoff-ergodic-theorem.md)

On a [finite measure](finite-measure.md) space, the convergence in the [Birkhoff ergodic theorem](birkhoff-ergodic-theorem.md) also holds in $L^1$. Prove it first for bounded truncations by the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md), then use the $L^1$ contraction $\|A_nh\|_1\leq\|h\|_1$ and [Fatou lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) to remove the truncation.
