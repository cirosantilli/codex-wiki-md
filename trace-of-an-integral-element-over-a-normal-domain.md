# Trace of an integral element over a normal domain

↑ **Parent:** [Field trace](field-trace.md)

For a [normal domain](integrally-closed-domain.md) $A$ with [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) $K$, the [field trace](field-trace.md) of an [integral element](integral-element.md) in a finite [separable field extension](separable-extension.md) $L/K$ belongs to $A$. Its conjugates are integral, so their sum is integral; it lies in $K$ and hence in $A$ by normality. This places the [integral closure](integral-closure.md) inside a [trace-dual lattice](trace-dual-lattice.md).
