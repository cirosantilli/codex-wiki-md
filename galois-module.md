# Galois module

↑ **Parent:** [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md)

A discrete Galois module over $K$ is an abelian group with a continuous action of the [absolute Galois group](absolute-galois-group.md) $G_K$. Continuity means that each element has an open stabilizer. Examples are the algebraic points of an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md), its finite torsion module $E[m]$, and the group of roots of unity $\mu_m$. [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md) takes continuous cocycles with values in such a module.
