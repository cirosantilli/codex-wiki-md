# Thurston norm of a pair-of-pants product

↑ **Parent:** [Thurston norm](thurston-norm.md)

For $Y=P\times S^1$ with $P$ a [pair of pants](pair-of-pants-mathematics.md) and $h$ its circle fiber, a [horizontal surface in a Seifert fibered space](horizontal-surface-in-a-seifert-fibered-space.md) of degree $d$ has $\chi_- = |d|$; [vertical surfaces in a Seifert fibered space](vertical-surface-in-a-seifert-fibered-space.md) have zero cost. Cutting and pasting these representatives, and classifying essential surfaces, proves $\|u\|_T=|u\cdot h|$. The [dual Thurston polytope](dual-thurston-polytope.md) is therefore the segment between the two functionals $\pm h$ under the natural pairing.
