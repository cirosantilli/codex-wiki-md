# Disjunction and existence properties

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disjunction_and_existence_properties)

A theory has the disjunction property when provability of a closed disjunction implies provability of one disjunct. It has the existence property when a provable existential sentence has a witness whose substitution is provably valid. These are distinct constructive properties; [disjunction property of intuitionistic propositional logic](disjunction-property-of-intuitionistic-propositional-logic.md) is one instance of the former.
