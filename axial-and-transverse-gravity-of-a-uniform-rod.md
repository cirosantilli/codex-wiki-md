# Axial and transverse gravity of a uniform rod

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md)

Integrate Newtonian point-force elements along a uniform thin rod of total [mass](mass.md) $M$ and half-length $a$. The first title formula applies on the axis outside the endpoint, $b>a$; the second applies on its perpendicular bisector. Both approach the point-mass result at large distance, but at equal distance their ratio is $1+3a^2/(2b^2)+O(a^4/b^4)$. A nonuniform linear [mass](mass.md) profile cannot be replaced by these formulas using only total [mass](mass.md) and [length](length.md).
