# Magnetoelastic coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Elasticity (physics)](elasticity-physics-split.md)

[Magnetoelastic coupling](magnetoelastic-coupling.md) links a material's magnetic state to its [strain](strain.md) and [stress](stress.md). [Magnetostriction](magnetostriction.md) describes a resulting deformation; mechanical loading can also change the magnetic state.
