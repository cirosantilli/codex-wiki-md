# Fixed-target production threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic collision mass threshold](relativistic-collision-mass-threshold.md)

For an incident particle of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $m_1$ and total energy $E_1$ hitting a stationary target of mass $m_2>0$, [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) gives $W^2=m_1^2c^4+m_2^2c^4+2m_2c^2E_1$. Applying the [relativistic collision mass threshold](relativistic-collision-mass-threshold.md) gives the displayed necessary condition. The incoming energy must also satisfy $E_1\geq m_1c^2$; other conserved quantum numbers or reaction dynamics can impose further restrictions.
