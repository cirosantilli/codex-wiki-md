# Molecular formula

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical formula](chemical-formula.md)

A molecular formula gives the number of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) of each [chemical element](chemical-element.md) in a [molecule](molecule-split.md). For [urea](urea.md), $\mathrm{CH}_4\mathrm N_2\mathrm O$ counts one [carbon](carbon.md), four [hydrogen](hydrogen.md), two [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) and one [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). It records composition rather than the arrangement of [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md).
