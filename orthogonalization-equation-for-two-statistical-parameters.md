# Orthogonalization equation for two statistical parameters

↑ **Parent:** [Interest-respecting reparametrization](interest-respecting-reparametrization.md)

Write the old [nuisance parameter](nuisance-parameter.md) as $\lambda=g(\psi,\eta)$ with $g_\eta\ne0$. The transformed [score functions](informant-function.md) are $u_\psi^{\rm new}=u_\psi+g_\psi u_\lambda$ and $u_\eta=g_\eta u_\lambda$. Their expected cross product is $g_\eta(I_{\psi\lambda}+g_\psi I_{\lambda\lambda})$. Setting it to zero gives the displayed [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) along fixed-$\eta$ curves, assuming positive nuisance [Fisher information](fisher-information-matrix.md). Smooth solutions with distinct initial conditions give a local interest-respecting orthogonal coordinate system.
