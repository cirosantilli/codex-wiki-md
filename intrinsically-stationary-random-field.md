# Intrinsically stationary random field

↑ **Parent:** [Geostatistics](geostatistics.md)

An intrinsically stationary random field has constant mean and finite increment [variances](variance-split.md) depending only on displacement. Its [semivariogram](semivariogram.md) is $\gamma(h)=\tfrac12\operatorname{Var}[Z(s+h)-Z(s)]$. Second-order stationarity implies this property, but the converse can fail: subtracting the value at a fixed origin from a stationary field preserves increments and makes marginal [variance](variance-split.md) vary with location.
