# Total variation of an interval indicator

↑ **Parent:** [Total variation seminorm on a domain](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md)

For $a<b$ strictly inside a one-dimensional domain, the indicator $u=1_{[a,b]}$ has [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) $Du=\delta_a-\delta_b$, hence $\operatorname{TV}(u)=2$. Directly from the test-function definition, $\int u\varphi'=\varphi(b)-\varphi(a)\leq2$, and two disjoint normalized [smooth bump functions](smooth-bump-function.md) attain the bound. Values at the endpoints themselves do not change the $L^1$ function.
