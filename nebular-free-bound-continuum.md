# Nebular free-bound continuum

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary-nebula spectral energy distribution](planetary-nebula-spectral-energy-distribution.md)

A [nebular free-bound continuum](nebular-free-bound-continuum.md) is emitted when a free electron is captured into a bound atomic level and its excess energy leaves as a photon. The threshold energies produce recombination edges such as the Balmer discontinuity. Its spectrum depends on electron temperature, ionic abundances, capture cross-sections and emission measure; it is distinct from bound-bound recombination lines and free-free bremsstrahlung.
