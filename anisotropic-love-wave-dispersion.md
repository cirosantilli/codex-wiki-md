# Anisotropic Love-wave dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Love wave](love-wave.md)

For an aligned anisotropic surface layer, $q^2=(\omega^2-\beta_x'^2k^2)/\beta_z'^2$, while the isotropic substrate has $\kappa^2=k^2-\omega^2/\beta^2$. Zero top [traction](traction.md) and welded displacement/traction continuity give the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). The horizontal speed sets the limiting [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md); the vertical speed sets rigid-base cutoff frequencies. The effective contrast for the high-contrast transition is $\rho'\beta_x'\beta_z'/(\rho\beta^2)$.
