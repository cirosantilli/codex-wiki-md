# Equilibrium heat capacity of interconverting states

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)

For independent particles with interconverting state families $i$, the equilibrium [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is a sum $q=\sum_iq_i$ and the family probabilities are $x_i=q_i/q$. Let $u_i$ be the conditional mean one-particle [energy](energy-split.md) in family $i$ and $c_i=du_i/dT$. The total [canonical energy variance](canonical-energy-variance.md) separates into within-family and between-family contributions, giving

$$
C_V=N\sum_i x_ic_i+\frac{N}{k_{\rm B}T^2}\left[\sum_i x_iu_i^2-\left(\sum_i x_iu_i\right)^2\right].
$$

The final positive term is absent if family fractions are held fixed. A [frozen ortho-para mixture](frozen-ortho-para-mixture.md) and an equilibrating mixture can therefore have different [heat capacities](heat-capacity.md) at the same instantaneous composition.
