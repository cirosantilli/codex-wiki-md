# Joint spectral radius

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral radius](spectral-radius.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_spectral_radius)

The [joint spectral radius](joint-spectral-radius.md) measures the worst exponential growth under arbitrary products from a finite family of [matrices](matrix.md). Submultiplicativity gives its existence and invariance under changes of the chosen norm. Every fixed-length product $B$ satisfies $\rho(B)^{1/p}\le\widehat\rho(\mathcal A)$, while the maximum norm of all length-$p$ products to the power $1/p$ is an [upper bound](upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). A repeated product gives the first inequality; grouping an arbitrary long word into blocks proves the second. The [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md) of each individual [matrix](matrix.md) does not control growth of all switched products.
