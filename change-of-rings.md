# Change of rings

↑ **Parent:** [Module (mathematics)](module-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change_of_rings)

Changing the coefficient [ring](ring.md) of a [module](module-mathematics.md) along a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) can mean [restriction of scalars](restriction-of-scalars.md), [extension of scalars](extension-of-scalars.md), or [coextension of scalars](coextension-of-scalars.md). The underlying [module](module-mathematics.md) may be retained with a smaller ring action, or replaced by a tensor or Hom construction with the new ring.
