# Compensated summation

↑ **Parent:** [Floating-point arithmetic](floating-point-arithmetic-split.md)

[Compensated summation](compensated-summation.md) retains a correction for low-order information lost to [roundoff error](round-off-error.md) in successive [floating-point arithmetic](floating-point-arithmetic-split.md) additions. [Kahan summation](kahan-summation-algorithm.md) is a common implementation; it can substantially reduce accumulated error when many terms are added.
