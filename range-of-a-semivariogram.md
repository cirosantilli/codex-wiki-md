# Range of a semivariogram

↑ **Parent:** [Semivariogram](semivariogram.md)

The exact range is a separation beyond which the [semivariogram](semivariogram.md) reaches its sill. A [Gaussian semivariogram](gaussian-semivariogram.md) with nonzero structured [variance](variance-split.md) approaches its sill asymptotically and therefore has infinite exact range. Software range parameters may instead specify a scale.
