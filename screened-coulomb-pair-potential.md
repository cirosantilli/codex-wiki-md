# Screened Coulomb pair potential

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

A [screened Coulomb pair potential](screened-coulomb-pair-potential.md) models the effective electrostatic interaction of particles in an electrolyte. For identical weakly charged spheres of radius $a$, charge $Q$, and [Debye–Hückel screening length](debye-huckel-screening-length.md) $\kappa^{-1}$, a common pairwise approximation is $U(R)=Q^2e^{-\kappa(R-2a)}/[4\pi\varepsilon(1+\kappa a)^2R]$ for $R\geq2a$. The near-contact interaction can require more detailed boundary conditions or nonlinear screening. Charge, screening length and [temperature](temperature-split.md) all matter to a resulting [phase diagram](phase-diagram.md).
