# Maximal torus

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

A maximal torus is a connected compact abelian [Lie subgroup](lie-subgroup.md) maximal among such subgroups. Restricting a finite-dimensional [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md) to it splits into one-dimensional weight characters. For [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ it is the diagonal circle $\operatorname{diag}(z,z^{-1})$.
