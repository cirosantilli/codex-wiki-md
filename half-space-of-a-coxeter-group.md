# Half-space of a Coxeter group

↑ **Parent:** [Wall of a Coxeter group](wall-of-a-coxeter-group.md)

The two connected components of the complement of a [wall of a Coxeter group](wall-of-a-coxeter-group.md) are its half-spaces. A wall separates two group elements when they lie in opposite half-spaces.
