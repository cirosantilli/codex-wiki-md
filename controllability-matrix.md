# Controllability matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Controllability](controllability.md)

For the [linear time-invariant system](linear-time-invariant-system.md) $\dot x=Ax+Bu$, with $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ and $B\in\mathbb R^{n\times m}$, the [controllability matrix](controllability-matrix.md) has $n$ rows and $nm$ columns. The columns of $B$ are directions influenced directly by the [control inputs](control-input.md); $AB,A^2B,\ldots$ account for how the dynamics carry their influence into further directions. The [column space](column-space.md) of the [controllability matrix](controllability-matrix.md) is the zero-state [reachable set](reachable-set.md) for any $T>0$ when inputs are unrestricted. [Russ Tedrake, Underactuated Robotics: Controllability of linear systems](https://underactuated.csail.mit.edu/acrobot.html#controllability)
