# Dehn functions under a change of finite presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Dehn function](dehn-function.md)

Choose bounded-length substitutions $T$ from the first generating set to the second, and $U$ back. Each substituted [relator](relator.md) of the second [group presentation](group-presentation.md) has bounded area in the first, and every generator correction $a(UT(a))^{-1}$ has bounded area. Substitution into a minimal second-presentation diagram and filling the correction strips gives

$$
\delta_1(n)\le C\delta_2(Ln)+Dn.
$$

The reverse construction gives the opposite inequality. Thus the optimal [Dehn functions](dehn-function.md) are coarsely equivalent, and the existence of a recursive isoperimetric bound, hence solvability of the [word problem for a group](word-problem-for-groups.md), is independent of the [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md).
