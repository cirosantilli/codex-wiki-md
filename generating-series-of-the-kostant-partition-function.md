# Generating series of the Kostant partition function

↑ **Parent:** [Kostant partition function](kostant-partition-function.md)

The [Kostant partition function](kostant-partition-function.md) counts [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) combinations of the positive [roots of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md), without ordering the summands. Multiply the formal [geometric series](geometric-series.md) $\sum_{k\geq0}e^{-k\alpha}$ for every [positive root](positive-root.md). The coefficient of $e^{-\nu}$ counts precisely these combinations. Every coefficient is finite because a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) strictly positive on the [positive roots](positive-root.md) bounds every participating integer coefficient. The sign of each exponent in the cancelling product must agree with that of this series.
