# Electric-displacement angle from birefringent walk-off

↑ **Parent:** [Birefringent walk-off](birefringent-walk-off.md)

For a lossless nonmagnetic [uniaxial optical medium](uniaxial-optical-medium.md), the [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) of a [plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) is perpendicular to its [wavevector](wavevector.md). The magnetic field is perpendicular to the plane containing the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and [wavevector](wavevector.md), while the [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) is perpendicular to the electric field in that same plane. Thus the angle between $\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf D$ equals the walk-off angle between energy flow and phase propagation. At [normal incidence](normal-incidence.md) on a parallel slab of thickness $L$, a lateral separation $\Delta$ of the ordinary and extraordinary exiting rays gives $\alpha_e=\arctan(|\Delta|/L)$, while $\alpha_o=0$. The output rays can be parallel even though their internal energy paths differ.
