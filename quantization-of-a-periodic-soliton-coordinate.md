# Quantization of a periodic soliton coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Collective coordinate of a soliton](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md)

The conjugate [momentum](momentum.md) to a periodic internal soliton angle generates shifts of that angle. Single-valued wavefunctions, or [Bohr-Sommerfeld quantization](bohr-sommerfeld-quantization.md) with zero [Maslov index](maslov-index.md), quantize this [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md). A theta term can shift the relation between [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) and mechanical charge without changing integer canonical labels.
