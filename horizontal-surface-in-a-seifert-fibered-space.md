# Horizontal surface in a Seifert fibered space

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert fibered space](seifert-fibered-space.md)

A horizontal surface is a properly embedded [topological surface](topological-surface.md) transverse to every [Seifert fiber](seifert-fiber.md). Its projection to the base [orbifold](orbifold.md) is an orbifold covering. The covering degree is its intersection number with a regular [Seifert fiber](seifert-fiber.md).
