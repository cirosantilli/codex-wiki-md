# Coannihilation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological particle freeze-out](cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md)

Number-changing reactions involving a relic candidate and another nearly degenerate species. If reactions interchange the species rapidly enough, the effective [annihilation](annihilation.md) rate depends on their joint abundances and channels. A single-species relic estimate can then fail even for fixed candidate [mass](mass.md).
