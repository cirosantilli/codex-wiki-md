# Naive factorization of a nonleptonic meson decay

↑ **Parent:** [Four-fermion interaction](four-fermion-interaction.md)

Approximate a hadronic [matrix element](matrix-element.md) of a product of two currents by a product of separate current [matrix elements](matrix-element.md). For a decay to two [mesons](meson.md), one factor can create a [pion](pion.md) from the vacuum while the other gives a [pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar form factor](pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar-form-factor.md). Nonfactorizable [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md) interactions are omitted; tree-level weak vertices do not alone justify this approximation.
