# Pointwise minimax rate for Lipschitz regression

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-design nonparametric regression](fixed-design-nonparametric-regression.md)

At an interior target point in regular [fixed-design nonparametric regression](fixed-design-nonparametric-regression.md), mean functions with [Lipschitz bound](lipschitz-bound.md) $L$ have pointwise [minimax risk](minimax-risk.md) of order $(L\sigma^2/n)^{2/3}$ for fixed positive $L,\sigma$ as $n\to\infty$. A [smoothing bandwidth](smoothing-bandwidth.md) of order $(\sigma^2/(nL^2))^{1/3}$ balances squared [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md) and [variance](variance-split.md); the [two-point lower bound with a triangular bump](two-point-lower-bound-with-a-triangular-bump.md) gives the matching lower rate.
