# Ghost loop

↑ **Parent:** [Faddeev-Popov ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md)

A ghost loop is a closed cycle of ghost propagators in a [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md). Its Grassmann statistics contribute an additional minus sign.
