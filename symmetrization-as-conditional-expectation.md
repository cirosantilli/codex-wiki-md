# Symmetrization as conditional expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md)

If a finite group $\Gamma$ acts by measure-preserving maps on a [probability space](probability-space.md), let $\mathcal I$ be its invariant [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md). For an integrable [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $Z$, the displayed group average is $\mathcal I$-measurable. For $A\in\mathcal I$, changing variables under each group element gives $\int_A Z\circ T_g\,d\mathbb P=\int_A Z\,d\mathbb P$. The average therefore satisfies the defining integral identity for [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md). For permutations of $N$ exchangeable coordinates, averaging a product attached to $d$ distinct indices yields the sum over all ordered distinct $d$-tuples divided by the [falling factorial](falling-factorial.md) $(N)_d=N!/(N-d)!$.
