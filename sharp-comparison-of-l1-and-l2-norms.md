# Sharp comparison of l1 and l2 norms

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence of norms in finite dimensions](equivalence-of-norms-in-finite-dimensions.md)

For $x\in\mathbb R^n$, [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) applied to $(|x_i|)$ and $(1)$ gives the upper bound. Equal absolute coordinate values attain its factor $\sqrt n$. Expanding $(\sum_i|x_i|)^2$ gives the lower bound, and a vector with only one nonzero coordinate attains its factor one. Thus both constants are optimal. These [Lp norms](lp-norm.md) define the same finite-dimensional topology despite having different unit balls.
