# Informed consent

↑ **Parent:** [Medicine](medicine.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Informed_consent)

Informed consent is voluntary agreement after receiving and understanding relevant information about an intervention or participation in research. In a [clinical trial](clinical-trial.md), the participant is informed about the study procedures, potential benefits and risks, alternatives and the option to decline or withdraw. Consent precedes [randomization](randomization.md).
