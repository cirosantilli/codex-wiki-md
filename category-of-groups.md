# Category of groups

↑ **Parent:** [Category theory](category-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category_of_groups)

The category of groups has [groups](group-split.md) as objects and [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) as morphisms. Its product is the [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md), and its coproduct is the [free product](free-product.md), as expressed by the [universal property of a free product](universal-property-of-a-free-product.md).
