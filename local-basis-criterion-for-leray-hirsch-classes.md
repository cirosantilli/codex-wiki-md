# Local basis criterion for Leray-Hirsch classes

↑ **Parent:** [Leray-Hirsch theorem](leray-hirsch-theorem.md)

On a trivialized [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md) over $U$, assume the fiber has finite-dimensional total [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) over a [field](field.md). The [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) identifies the total-space cohomology with a finite free graded module over $H^*(U)$. Classes restricting to a basis on every fiber give an invertible change of generators: its degree-zero coefficient blocks are invertible on each path component, while its positive-base-degree terms strictly decrease fiber degree and are therefore nilpotent. Inverting the diagonal blocks and then using a finite geometric series proves that multiplication by the chosen classes is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) over $U$.
