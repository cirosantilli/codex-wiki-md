# Resonant pendulum energy

↑ **Parent:** [Pendulum approximation of a mean-motion resonance](pendulum-approximation-of-a-mean-motion-resonance.md)

For $\ddot\phi=\omega_0^2\sin\phi$ with constant $\omega_0$, multiplication by $\dot\phi$ gives the conserved energy $E=\dot\phi^2/2+\omega_0^2(1+\cos\phi)$. The stable center is $\pi$; the [separatrix](separatrix.md) has $E=2\omega_0^2$. Below it the [resonant argument](resonant-argument.md) librates and above it the angle circulates. This is an invariant of the constant-coefficient [pendulum approximation of a mean-motion resonance](pendulum-approximation-of-a-mean-motion-resonance.md), not of arbitrary coupled [orbital element](orbital-element.md) evolution.
