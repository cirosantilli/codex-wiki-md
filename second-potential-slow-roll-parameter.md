# Second potential slow-roll parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Potential slow-roll parameter](potential-slow-roll-parameter.md)

The second potential slow-roll parameter is

$$
\eta_V=M_{\rm Pl}^2\frac{V''}{V}.
$$

Slow roll usually requires both $\epsilon_V\ll1$ and $|\eta_V|\ll1$.
