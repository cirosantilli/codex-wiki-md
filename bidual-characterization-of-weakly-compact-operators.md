# Bidual characterization of weakly compact operators

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly compact operator](weakly-compact-operator.md)

A [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $T:X\to Y$ between [Banach spaces](banach-space-split.md) is a [weakly compact operator](weakly-compact-operator.md) exactly when its second adjoint takes values in the canonical copy of $Y$ inside $Y^{**}$. The [Goldstine theorem](goldstine-theorem.md) proves necessity; the [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](banach-alaoglu-theorem.md), continuity of the second adjoint, and the [Mazur theorem](mazur-theorem.md) prove sufficiency.
