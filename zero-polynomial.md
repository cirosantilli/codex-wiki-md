# Zero polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

The zero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) has every coefficient zero. It is the [zero vector](zero-vector.md) of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) and the additive [identity element](identity-element.md) of a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md). Its [degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) is often set to $-\infty$ when a formula requires one.
