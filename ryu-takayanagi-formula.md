<h1 id="ryu-takayanagi-formula">Ryu–Takayanagi formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Holographic entanglement entropy](holographic-entanglement-entropy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryu–Takayanagi_formula)

For a static holographic state and boundary region $A$,

$$
S(A)=\frac{\operatorname{Area}(\gamma_A)}{4G_N},
$$

where $\gamma_A$ is the least-area bulk surface homologous to $A$ and anchored on $\partial A$.
