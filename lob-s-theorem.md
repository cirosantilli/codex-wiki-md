<h1 id="lob-s-theorem">Löb's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Löb's_theorem)

Assume the [Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions](hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md) and the [diagonal lemma](diagonal-lemma.md). Choose $D$ with $T\vdash D\leftrightarrow(\Box D\to A)$. Necessitating the forward implication and using distribution and positive introspection gives $T\vdash\Box D\to\Box A$. If $T\vdash\Box A\to A$, then $T\vdash\Box D\to A$, so the reverse fixed-point implication gives $T\vdash D$. Necessitation gives $T\vdash\Box D$, and the preceding implications give $T\vdash A$. No consistency assumption is needed for this implication between provability assertions.
