# Positive random-environment path-weight martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

Let $\xi$ be a [random walk](random-walk.md), [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of centered, [independent](independent-random-variables.md) time-space signs $h$, and let $0<\varepsilon<1$. Averaging over the walk gives a positive [martingale](martingale-split.md) in the environment [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md): each new time layer has conditional mean-one weight. Hence $\mathbb EZ_t=1$ and the [martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md) gives a finite nonnegative almost-sure limit. Preserving its [mean](expected-value.md) requires an additional condition such as bounded [second moments](second-moment.md); positivity of finite-time weights alone does not suffice.
