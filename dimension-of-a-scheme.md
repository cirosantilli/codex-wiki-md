# Dimension of a scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

The dimension of a [scheme](scheme.md) is the supremum of the lengths of strict chains of irreducible closed subsets of its underlying topological space. On an affine scheme it is the [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) of its coordinate ring. Taking the reduced subscheme leaves the dimension unchanged.
