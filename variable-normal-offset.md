# Variable normal offset

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel surface](parallel-surface.md)

A [variable normal offset](variable-normal-offset.md) displaces a [parametric surface](parametric-surface.md) along its unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) by a scalar field $d(u,v)$. Unlike a constant-distance [parallel surface](parallel-surface.md), its derivatives also contain $d_u n$ and $d_v n$.
