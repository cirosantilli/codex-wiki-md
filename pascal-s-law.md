<h1 id="pascal-s-law">Pascal's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal's_law)

A [pressure](pressure-split.md) increment applied to a confined [fluid](fluid.md) in equilibrium is transmitted throughout it. The pressure increment is a [scalar](scalar.md), not a [force](force-split.md) vector copied in one direction. Comparing different heights also requires the [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) difference. Finite-speed [pressure wave propagation](pressure-wave-propagation.md) precedes the new equilibrium.
