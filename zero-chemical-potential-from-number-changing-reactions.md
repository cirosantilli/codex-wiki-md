# Zero chemical potential from number-changing reactions

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md)

For one particle species in [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) under $2\phi\leftrightarrow3\phi$, chemical balance gives $2\mu_\phi=3\mu_\phi$, hence $\mu_\phi=0$. Elastic $2\phi\leftrightarrow2\phi$ imposes no such constraint. In an expanding universe the number-changing reaction must remain fast compared with the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md); after chemical freeze-out a nonzero [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) can develop even if elastic collisions maintain [kinetic equilibrium](kinetic-equilibrium.md).
