# Chronological past

↑ **Parent:** [Causal hierarchy](causal-hierarchy.md)

The chronological past $I^-(S)$ is the set of points from which a future-directed [timelike curve](timelike-curve.md) reaches $S$. Equivalently, it is the time reverse of the [chronological future](chronological-future.md).
