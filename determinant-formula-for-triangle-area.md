# Determinant formula for triangle area

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)

Two side [vectors](vector.md) span a [parallelogram](parallelogram.md) whose signed area is their [determinant](determinant.md). Its two congruent triangles give the factor one-half, and the absolute value removes orientation. Reversing the vertex order changes the sign but not the area; collinear vertices give zero.
