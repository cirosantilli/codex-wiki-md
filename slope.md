# Slope

↑ **Parent:** [Straight line](straight-line.md)

In Cartesian coordinates, the [slope](slope.md) of a nonvertical [straight line](straight-line.md) is its vertical change divided by its horizontal change. The graph $y=mx+c$ has [slope](slope.md) $m$; a vertical [straight line](straight-line.md) has no finite [slope](slope.md). For a [tangent line](tangent-line.md) to a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md), the [slope](slope.md) is the [derivative](derivative.md).
