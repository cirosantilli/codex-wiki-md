# Commuting coordinate basis

↑ **Parent:** [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md)

Every pair of [coordinate basis](coordinate-basis.md) fields commutes:

$$
[\partial_i,\partial_j]=0.
$$

Therefore a frame containing two fields with nonzero [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) cannot be induced by a coordinate system.
