# Sub-Keplerian rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md)

Sub-Keplerian rotation has angular speed below that of a circular [Keplerian orbit](kepler-orbit.md) at the same radius. In a gaseous disk, an outwardly decreasing pressure supplies some radial support and makes the gas sub-Keplerian.
