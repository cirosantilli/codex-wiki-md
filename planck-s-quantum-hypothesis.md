<h1 id="planck-s-quantum-hypothesis">Planck's quantum hypothesis</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Black-body radiation](black-body-radiation.md)

The assumption that energy exchange with a mode of [frequency](frequency.md) $\nu$ occurs in discrete steps $h\nu$. A thermal oscillator with $E_n=nh\nu$ has $\langle E\rangle=h\nu/(e^{h\nu/(k_BT)}-1)$, recovering [equipartition](equipartition-theorem.md) for small $h\nu/(k_BT)$ and exponential suppression for large values.
