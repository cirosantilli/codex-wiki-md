# Equator

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

For a [sphere](sphere.md) with a chosen polar axis, its equator is the [great circle](great-circle.md) in the plane through its center perpendicular to that axis. On the unit [sphere](sphere.md) in [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md) it is $\theta=\pi/2$. Like every [great circle](great-circle.md), it is a [geodesic](geodesic.md).
