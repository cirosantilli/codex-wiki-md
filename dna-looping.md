# DNA looping

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

[DNA looping](dna-looping.md) brings separated sequence positions into spatial proximity through bending and folding of the DNA. In transcriptional regulation it can permit a distant regulatory protein to influence promoter-associated machinery, although proximity alone does not prove productive activation.
