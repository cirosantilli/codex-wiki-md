# Recoil angle in a two-body elastic collision

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

For a nonrelativistic [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md) with the target initially at rest, let $\theta^*$ be the outgoing projectile angle in the [centre-of-mass frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md). The laboratory target [velocity](velocity.md) is proportional to $(1-\cos\theta^*,-\sin\theta^*)$. Its unsigned recoil angle is therefore $(\pi-\theta^*)/2$, independent of the mass ratio. At zero deflection the target is stationary and its direction is undefined; the right-angle value is a limit.
