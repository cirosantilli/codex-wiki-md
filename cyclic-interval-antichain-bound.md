# Cyclic interval antichain bound

↑ **Parent:** [Katona circle method](katona-circle-method.md)

In any [cyclic ordering](cyclic-ordering.md) on $n$ points, an [antichain](antichain.md) contains at most $n$ nonempty proper [cyclic intervals](cyclic-interval.md). The intervals with one prescribed final position are nested, so at most one belongs to the [antichain](antichain.md). Summing over final positions proves the bound. Averaging it over [cyclic orderings](cyclic-ordering.md) proves the [LYM inequality](lubell-yamamoto-meshalkin-inequality.md); the empty set and full set must be handled separately.
