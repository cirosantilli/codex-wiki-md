# Suspension of spectra

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (topology)](spectrum-topology.md)

[Suspension of spectra](suspension-of-spectra.md) shifts the stable degree. Unlike [reduced suspension](reduced-suspension.md) of [based spaces](based-space.md), it is invertible in the [stable homotopy category](stable-homotopy-category.md), with inverse denoted $\Sigma^{-1}$.
