# Full-support probability distribution on a finite alphabet

↑ **Parent:** [Support of a probability distribution](support-of-a-probability-distribution.md)

A [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) $p$ has full support on a specified [finite alphabet](finite-alphabet.md) if $p_x>0$ for every symbol in that [alphabet](alphabet.md). This is relative to the chosen [alphabet](alphabet.md): deleting zero-probability symbols produces a smaller [alphabet](alphabet.md) on which the same law has full support. In a weighted [prefix code](prefix-code.md) problem, full support makes every shortening of a retained [codeword](codeword.md) strictly reduce [expected codeword length](expected-codeword-length.md). Without it, a minimum-cost [code tree](prefix-tree-of-a-code.md) may contain redundant zero-weight branches.
