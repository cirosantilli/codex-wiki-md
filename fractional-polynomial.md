# Fractional polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)

A [fractional polynomial](fractional-polynomial.md) uses selected real powers of a positive covariate to model a smooth regression effect. Power zero denotes a logarithm, and a repeated power can generate a term $x^p\log x$. Such terms permit a wider family than ordinary integer-power polynomials, but their selection adds modeling uncertainty and requires validation.
