# Paired simple and fractional distillation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Distillation](distillation.md)

McMaster University's 1997/98 laboratory manual compares [simple distillation](simple-distillation.md) and [fractional distillation](fractional-distillation.md) using charges of $25\,\mathrm{mL}$ each of [cyclohexane](cyclohexane.md) and [p-xylene](p-xylene.md). Part B uses a $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ round-bottom flask and a [fractionating column](fractionating-column.md). The protocol specifies a [boiling chip](boiling-chip.md), a heating mantle on a ring support, and the top of the [thermometer](thermometer.md) bulb midway in the opening toward the [laboratory condenser](laboratory-condenser.md). Heating is adjusted for roughly one drop per second. Students record head [temperature](temperature-split.md) every $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ collected, up to $45\,\mathrm{mL}$, and plot both runs against collected [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) on the same axes.[Chem2O06 Laboratory Manual by McMaster University; Parts A–B, Figure 3.5 position instruction, and Recording of Results](chem2o06-laboratory-manual-by-mcmaster-university/experiment-3-procedures.md)

Charge, atmospheric operation, and collection rate provide comparison conditions; the [fractionating column](fractionating-column.md) is changed. A sharper separation of [temperature](temperature-split.md) regions would support improved fractionation. It would not directly measure product [mole fractions](mole-fraction.md) or prove purity. The manual distinguishes collected [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) from composition and states that this pair forms no [azeotrope](azeotrope.md). No student dataset is supplied here, and the constant-[relative volatility](relative-volatility.md) example is not assigned to these liquids.[Chem2O06 Laboratory Manual by McMaster University; Recording of Results, distinction between temperature–volume and temperature–composition plots; comparison questions](chem2o06-laboratory-manual-by-mcmaster-university/experiment-3-procedures.md)
