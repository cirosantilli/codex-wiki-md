# Weakly null orthonormal sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Weak convergence in a Hilbert space](weak-convergence-in-a-hilbert-space.md)

Every [orthonormal sequence](orthonormal-sequence.md) in a Hilbert space converges weakly to zero, by [Bessel inequality](bessel-s-inequality.md), but no subsequence converges strongly because distinct terms remain distance $\sqrt2$ apart.
