# Central potential

↑ **Parent:** [Potential energy](potential-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_potential)

A central potential depends only on distance from a fixed centre, $V(\mathbf x)=V(|\mathbf x|)$. Its force is radial, and rotational symmetry implies conservation of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
