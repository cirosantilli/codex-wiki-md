# Herbrand quotient of the local multiplicative group

↑ **Parent:** [Herbrand quotient](herbrand-quotient.md)

For a cyclic extension of [p-adic fields](p-adic-field.md), sufficiently deep [principal units](principal-unit.md) are equivariantly isomorphic to an additive lattice by the [p-adic logarithm](p-adic-logarithm.md). The [normal basis theorem](normal-basis-theorem.md) makes its [Herbrand quotient](herbrand-quotient.md) one. Passing across finite unit quotients preserves it. The valuation exact sequence with quotient $\mathbb Z$ therefore gives the displayed formula.
