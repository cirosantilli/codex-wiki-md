# Orbit crossing

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary system dynamics](planetary-system-dynamics.md)

[Orbit crossing](orbit-crossing.md) occurs when two geometrical orbital paths intersect. It permits [collisions](collision.md) or close encounters when bodies also reach the intersection at compatible times; crossing alone does not imply a [collision](collision.md). For nearly circular coplanar nested orbits, a useful local test follows by differentiating their radial positions at fixed longitude.
