# Knot determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander polynomial](alexander-polynomial.md)

The knot determinant is $|\Delta_K(-1)|=|\det(A+A^T)|$. It is also the order of the first homology of the two-fold cover of $S^3$ branched over $K$.
