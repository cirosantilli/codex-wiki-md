# Normalized difference vegetation index

↑ **Parent:** [Multispectral imaging](multispectral-imaging.md)

The normalized contrast of red and [near-infrared](near-infrared.md) [reflectances](reflectance.md). Large positive values indicate a vegetation-like spectrum, but soil, atmosphere, shadows and canopy saturation affect its interpretation.
