# Mahalanobis distance

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md)

For a [positive-definite](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md) [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md), this is [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) after whitening. It accounts for both scales and correlations and is invariant under consistent nonsingular linear changes of coordinates. Estimating the covariance can be unstable with small samples, and a singular covariance requires restricting to a nonsingular subspace rather than claiming an ordinary [metric](metric.md) on the full space.
