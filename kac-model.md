# Kac model

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory.md)

This many-particle stochastic collision model rotates one uniformly selected velocity pair by a uniformly selected angle, conserving the sum of squared velocities. Its normalized angular averaging is the [Kac collision operator](kac-collision-operator.md), and its continuous-time evolution is the [Kac master equation](kac-master-equation.md). In this version velocities range over $\mathbb R^N$ and initial energy is not restricted to one sphere; the evolution preserves the initial energy distribution.
