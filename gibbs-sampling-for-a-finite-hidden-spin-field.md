# Gibbs sampling for a finite hidden spin field

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs sampler](gibbs-sampler.md)

For a strictly positive posterior on finitely many binary spins, a [Random-scan Gibbs sampler](random-scan-gibbs-sampler.md) uses the two local posterior weights to redraw one spin. Positive [full conditional distribution](full-conditional-distribution.md) probabilities allow every finite sequence of spin changes, giving an [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md) with positive self-transition probabilities.
