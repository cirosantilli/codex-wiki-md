# Radiation-supported inner-disk height

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure-opacity zones of a Shakura--Sunyaev thin disk](pressure-opacity-zones-of-a-shakura-sunyaev-thin-disk.md)

The inner [Shakura--Sunyaev thin disk](shakura-sunyaev-thin-disk.md) is supported mainly by [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) and has nearly constant [electron-scattering opacity](electron-scattering-opacity.md). Equating [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) with local dissipation and imposing vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives the characteristic half-thickness

$$
H(R)\simeq\frac{3\kappa_{\rm es}\dot M}{8\pi c}\left(1-\sqrt{R_{\rm in}/R}\right).
$$

The radiation-supported height approaches a plateau away from the inner boundary. The formal zero at that boundary is outside the reliable radiation-dominated approximation. At an [Eddington accretion rate](eddington-accretion-rate.md) defined using efficiency $0.1$, this height can make the inner flow only marginally thin.
