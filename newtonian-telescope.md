# Newtonian telescope

↑ **Parent:** [Reflecting telescope](reflecting-telescope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newtonian_telescope)

A Newtonian telescope uses a concave parabolic primary and a flat diagonal secondary to send the converging beam to a side focus. The primary avoids on-axis [spherical aberration](spherical-aberration.md), but has off-axis [coma](coma-optics.md).
