# Flasque resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Flasque sheaf](flasque-sheaf.md)

A flasque resolution is an [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) of [sheaves of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md) whose terms $\mathcal I^j$ are [flasque sheaves](flasque-sheaf.md). Every such sheaf embeds by its section germs into the sheaf $U\mapsto\prod_{P\in U}\mathcal F_P$. Its restriction maps are projections, so it is flasque. Apply this construction successively to the cokernels to obtain a resolution. The [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) of $\mathcal F$ is the cohomology of the [cochain complex](cochain-complex.md) $\Gamma(X,\mathcal I^\bullet)$. A quotient of two flasque sheaves in a [short exact sequence of sheaves](short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md) is flasque: the flasque kernel allows local lifts to be glued into a section of the middle sheaf over the smaller open set; extend that lift using flasqueness of the middle sheaf, and project. Consequently the resolution's successive cokernels are flasque when $\mathcal F$ is flasque, and exactness of sections shows that all its positive-degree cohomology vanishes.
