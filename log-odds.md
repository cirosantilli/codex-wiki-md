# Log odds

↑ **Parent:** [Logistic model](logistic-model.md)

For an event of probability $p$, the log odds are the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) of its [odds](odds.md):

$$
\operatorname{logit}(p)=\log\frac{p}{1-p}.
$$
