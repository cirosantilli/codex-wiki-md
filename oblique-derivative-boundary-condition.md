# Oblique derivative boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary value problem](boundary-value-problem.md)

An oblique derivative boundary condition prescribes a linear combination of the outward [normal derivative](normal-derivative.md) and the tangential [derivative](derivative.md) on a boundary. Its strictly oblique case has a nonzero normal coefficient; a zero normal coefficient is a limiting tangential condition. Both trace directions must be tracked when constructing a [global relation](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md). On unbounded domains, regularity at corners and a growth condition at infinity are separate parts of an admissible [boundary value problem](boundary-value-problem.md).
