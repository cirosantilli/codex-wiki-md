# Glaser bubble-chamber experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Bubble chamber](bubble-chamber.md)

[Donald Arthur Glaser](donald-arthur-glaser.md) developed the [bubble chamber](bubble-chamber.md) in 1952 during experimental [physics](particle-physics.md) research at Michigan.[Biography, research paragraph](donald-glaser-biography-submitted-by-dr-glaser-to-the-nobel-committee-by-donald-arthur-glaser.md)

His 1960 lecture, Elementary particles and bubble chambers, describes evacuated capillary bulbs containing pure [diethyl ether](diethyl-ether.md). Cooling one bulb reduced [pressure](pressure-split.md); the other remained hot. A [cobalt-60](cobalt-60.md) source initiated [boiling](boiling.md). Small glass chambers subsequently used hot-oil baths, hand-cranked [pistons](piston.md) and high-speed [photography](photography-split.md). Automatic cycling with counter-controlled flashes produced finer tracks. Local [ionization](ionization.md) deposits [energy](energy-split.md) that can initiate bubble [nucleation](nucleation.md).[pp. 531–538, Figs. 1–4](elementary-particles-and-bubble-chambers-by-donald-arthur-glaser.md)

Later metal-chamber photographs compared operation with and without a nearby radioactive source: wall [boiling](boiling.md) occurred without interior tracks; irradiation produced tracks before disruptive flow ended sensitivity.[pp. 539–543, Fig. 6](elementary-particles-and-bubble-chambers-by-donald-arthur-glaser.md)

These observations separate two questions: can radiation start [boiling](boiling.md), and can a detector preserve the geometry of a passing [charged particle](charged-particle.md)? Whole-vessel [boiling](boiling.md) alone answers only the first. Wall background, finite sensitivity and growth before [photography](photography-split.md) constrain the second.
