<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Schrödinger's cat](../../../../../schrodinger-s-cat.md) exposes the [quantum measurement problem](../../../../../measurement-problem.md) by correlating a microscopic alternative with macroscopically distinct records. Under unitary evolution a schematic state becomes

$$
\alpha|\text{undecayed}\rangle|\text{alive}\rangle
+\beta|\text{decayed}\rangle|\text{dead}\rangle.
$$

The difficulty is explaining why a definite macroscopic outcome is observed if the isolated total state is always governed by the linear [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md). It is not a claim that one classically contradictory record has literally been observed. Environmental interactions rapidly entangle the records with different environment states, making their reduced [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) approximately diagonal. That explains the practical loss of [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) but does not, by itself, turn a global pure superposition into a selected single branch.

[Wigner's friend](../../../../../wigner-s-friend.md) places an observer's physical memory inside the quantum description. A friend measuring $|+\rangle=(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ can become correlated as

$$
|+\rangle|F_{\rm ready}\rangle
\longrightarrow |\Psi\rangle=\frac{|0\rangle|F_0\rangle+|1\rangle|F_1\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Conditioned on one friend record, the corresponding [relative state](../../../../../relative-state-of-a-bipartite-vector.md) contains a definite outcome. An external observer applying unitary theory to the perfectly isolated entire laboratory instead assigns the entangled vector $|\Psi\rangle$. In principle, a coherent joint measurement could distinguish it from the collapsed mixture

$$
\rho_{\rm mix}=\frac12\bigl(|0F_0\rangle\langle0F_0|+|1F_1\rangle\langle1F_1|\bigr):
\qquad \langle\Psi|\rho_{\rm mix}|\Psi\rangle=\frac12,
$$

whereas projecting the pure state onto $|\Psi\rangle$ succeeds with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) one. Preserving that laboratory coherence, including all records and environment correlations, is an extreme idealization for macroscopic observers.

The paradox concerns how state assignment, definite records and universal unitary dynamics fit together. It does not show that an observer can send messages faster than light or that consciousness must be given a special dynamical role. The [many-worlds interpretation](../../../../../many-worlds-interpretation.md) retains the global unitary state with branch-relative records; interpretations with a physical collapse must specify when and how unitary evolution changes. Stating the chosen assumptions prevents the friend's conditional state and the external observer's unconditioned state from being treated as the same mathematical object.
