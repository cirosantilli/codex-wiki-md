<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Inverting the detector rotation at this time gives

$$
|\Psi\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2}\left[
|d_\nearrow\rangle\big(c|\uparrow E_\leftarrow\rangle+s|\downarrow E_\rightarrow\rangle\big)
+|d_\swarrow\rangle\big(s|\uparrow E_\leftarrow\rangle-c|\downarrow E_\rightarrow\rangle\big)
\right].
$$

For a representation with one system factor and one environment factor per displayed detector state, each parenthesized system–environment vector would have to be a [product state](../../../../../product-state.md). But the first has coefficient matrix $\operatorname{diag}(c,s)$ in the indicated orthonormal bases; the second has matrix $\operatorname{diag}(s,-c)$. Their [matrix ranks](../../../../../matrix-rank.md) are two whenever $cs\ne0$. Equivalently, their two nonzero [Schmidt coefficients](../../../../../schmidt-coefficient.md) are $|c|,|s|$, which no independent basis changes can remove. Thus **the proposed factorization is impossible when $\sin\theta\ne0$**.

The exception matters: at $\theta=m\pi$, the detector basis is the original basis up to phases or interchange, and a factorization does exist. In particular, the literal condition $\theta\ne0$ is insufficient if it includes $\theta=\pi$. The intended obstruction concerns genuinely mixed detector bases.

The environment has selected robust detector records: its interaction commutes with the original projectors, and orthogonal environmental records suppress their interference in the [reduced density matrix](../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md). A rotated detector branch instead leaves the system and environment entangled. This is [environment-induced pointer-basis selection](../../../../../environment-induced-pointer-basis-selection.md), the issue analyzed by [W. H. Zurek](../../../../../w-h-zurek.md) in his apparatus-environment model.[1981 abstract: the apparatus-environment interaction selects recorded observables through a commutation condition](../../../../../pointer-basis-of-quantum-apparatus-into-what-mixture-does-the-wave-packet-collapse-by-w-h-zurek.md)

This addresses the basis ambiguity but does not by itself select one realized outcome from the global superposition. Moreover, a two-state environment has recurrences: $\cos2A$ returns to unit modulus, so the toy model does not establish irreversible macroscopic collapse. A large environment can make records effectively persistent over experimental times, but that requires further dynamical assumptions. The distinction is part of the [measurement problem](../../../../../measurement-problem.md), not an algebraic error cured by a partial trace.
