<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The four required [dot products](../../../../../dot-product.md) are

$$
\mathbf a_1\cdot\mathbf b_1=\frac1{\sqrt2},\quad
\mathbf a_1\cdot\mathbf b_3=-\frac1{\sqrt2},\quad
\mathbf a_3\cdot\mathbf b_1=\frac1{\sqrt2},\quad
\mathbf a_3\cdot\mathbf b_3=\frac1{\sqrt2}.
$$

Consequently the specified [CHSH inequality](../../../../../chsh-inequality.md) combination is

$$
\boxed{S=-\frac1{\sqrt2}-\frac1{\sqrt2}-\frac1{\sqrt2}-\frac1{\sqrt2}=-2\sqrt2.}
$$

The magnitude reaches the quantum [Tsirelson bound](../../../../../tsirelson-s-bound.md) and exceeds the local bound two. The negative sign follows this singlet and the stated order of terms; other conventions can reverse it.
