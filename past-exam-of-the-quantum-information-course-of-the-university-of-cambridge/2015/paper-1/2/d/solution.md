<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let Eve obtain $u,v\in\{\pm1\}$. Applying part (b) to her axes gives $p(u,v)=[1-uv\mathbf n_a\cdot\mathbf n_b]/4$. Conditional on these rank-one local measurements, the forwarded particles are a [product state](../../../../../product-state.md) with individual spin directions $u\mathbf n_a$ and $v\mathbf n_b$. Hence

$$
\mathbb E[X\mid u]=u\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf n_a,\qquad
\mathbb E[Y\mid v]=v\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf n_b.
$$

Conditional independence gives the product of these means. Averaging over Eve's outcomes,

$$
C=(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf n_a)(\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf n_b)
\sum_{u,v}uv\frac{1-uv\mathbf n_a\cdot\mathbf n_b}{4}.
$$

The sum is $-\mathbf n_a\cdot\mathbf n_b$, so

$$
\boxed{C(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=-(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf n_a)
(\mathbf n_a\cdot\mathbf n_b)(\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf n_b).}
$$

Eve has turned the entangled source into an ensemble of product states by an [intercept-resend attack](../../../../../intercept-resend-attack.md). Ignoring her records does not restore the original singlet coherence.
