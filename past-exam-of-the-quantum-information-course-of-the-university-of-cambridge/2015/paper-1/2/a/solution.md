↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [spin singlet state](../../../../../spin-singlet-state.md) has zero local means and outcomes $X,Y\in\{+1,-1\}$, so both [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md) are one. Its [Pearson correlation coefficient](../../../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) is therefore $C=\mathbb E[XY]$. Summing over the four joint outcomes gives

$$
\boxed{C=P_{++}+P_{--}-P_{+-}-P_{-+}.}
$$

The equal-sign products contribute $+1$ and the opposite-sign products contribute $-1$. In a finite sample the corresponding relative frequencies estimate these probabilities; the formula is not an assertion that a finite random sample has exactly zero empirical means.
