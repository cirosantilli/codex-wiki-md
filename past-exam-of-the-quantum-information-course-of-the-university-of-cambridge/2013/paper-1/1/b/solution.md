<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the final splitter only to the first ket. The $|dd\rangle$ amplitudes from $i|uv\rangle$ and $|vv\rangle$ cancel, giving

$$
\boxed{|\Psi_{IR}\rangle=-\frac12|\gamma\rangle+\frac1{2\sqrt2}\left(-|cc\rangle+2i|cd\rangle+i|dc\rangle\right).}
$$

Thus detection at $D^+$ occurs only in the coincidence $D^+,C^-$, with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $1/8$. The other two nonzero coincidence probabilities are $P(C^+,C^-)=1/8$ and $P(C^+,D^-)=1/2$. The cancellation is quantum [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) between indistinguishable surviving alternatives.
