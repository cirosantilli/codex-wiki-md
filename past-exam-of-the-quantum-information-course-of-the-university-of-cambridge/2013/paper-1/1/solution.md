[Outcome determinism](../../../../outcome-determinism.md) means that a complete hidden state $\lambda$, together with the chosen measurement settings, fixes each outcome. The observed [probabilities](../../../../probability.md) can still arise from an ensemble of unknown $\lambda$. This differs from deterministic unitary evolution of a [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md), which does not by itself specify a unique measurement outcome. [Quantum nonlocality](../../../../quantum-nonlocality.md) means failure of a Bell-local account of the correlations, under assumptions including [measurement independence](../../../../measurement-independence.md); it is stronger than the existence of entanglement and does not imply controllable faster-than-light signaling.

Use the first ket for the [Positron](../../../../positron.md) and the second for the [electron](../../../../electron.md). Before either final [beam splitter](../../../../beam-splitter.md), the first pair of splitters and annihilation produce

$$
|\Psi\rangle=-\frac12|\gamma\rangle
+\frac i2|u^+v^-\rangle+\frac i2|v^+u^-\rangle+\frac12|v^+v^-\rangle.
$$

The [photon](../../../../photon.md) state $|\gamma\rangle$ is orthogonal to the surviving charged-particle sector. Its [probability](../../../../probability.md) is $1/4$, while the remaining sector has squared norm $3/4$. The states below retain that [photon](../../../../photon.md) amplitude and are normalized over **all trials**, without conditioning on nonannihilation. For compactness write $|cc\rangle=|c^+c^-\rangle$, and similarly for $|cd\rangle,|dc\rangle,|dd\rangle$.
