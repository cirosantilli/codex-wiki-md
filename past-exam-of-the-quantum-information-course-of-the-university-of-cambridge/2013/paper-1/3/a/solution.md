[Quantum complementarity](../../../../../complementarity-physics.md) concerns experimental arrangements or observables that cannot be combined into one sharp classical description. For a [qubit](../../../../../qubit.md), the [Pauli operators](../../../../../pauli-operator.md) $X$ and $Z$ have incompatible eigenbases: a definite $Z$ value gives equal probabilities for the two $X$ outcomes. One can measure either observable sharply on a given preparation, but a joint measurement cannot reproduce both sharp projective measurements on every state. Complementarity is not merely ignorance of two simultaneously definite quantum properties.

In a balanced two-path [interferometer](../../../../../interferometer.md), [which-path information](../../../../../which-path-information.md) reduces the [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) available when the paths recombine. If the path becomes entangled with detector states,

$$
|\Psi\rangle=\frac{|u\rangle|E_u\rangle+e^{i\varphi}|v\rangle|E_v\rangle}{\sqrt2},
$$

the path [reduced density matrix](../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) has off-diagonal magnitude $|\langle E_u|E_v\rangle|/2$. Its [interference visibility](../../../../../interferometric-visibility.md) is $V=|\langle E_u|E_v\rangle|$. For equally likely pure marker states, optimal path distinguishability is $D=\sqrt{1-|\langle E_u|E_v\rangle|^2}$, giving $D^2+V^2=1$ in this ideal model. Identical marker states preserve full [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md); orthogonal ones permit perfect path discrimination and erase the unconditional fringes.

A concrete optical realization puts different [polarizations](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) on the two arms of a [Mach-Zehnder interferometer](../../../../../mach-zehnder-interferometer.md). A detector that ignores [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) sees reduced [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) because [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) records the path. Projecting the outgoing light onto a common [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) erases that information in the selected subensemble and can recover conditional fringes; summing all outcomes again removes them. This is a physical change in which alternatives remain distinguishable, not a retrospective alteration of earlier detections.
