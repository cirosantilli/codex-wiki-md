<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Quantum decoherence](../../../../../quantum-decoherence.md) arises when different system alternatives correlate with distinguishable environmental states. For an initially uncorrelated state, a measurement-like [unitary time evolution](../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md) can produce

$$
(\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle)|E_0\rangle
\longmapsto\alpha|0\rangle|E_0(t)\rangle+\beta|1\rangle|E_1(t)\rangle.
$$

Taking the environmental [partial trace](../../../../../partial-trace.md) gives

$$
\rho_S(t)=\begin{pmatrix}
|\alpha|^2&\alpha\beta^*\langle E_1(t)|E_0(t)\rangle\\
\alpha^*\beta\langle E_0(t)|E_1(t)\rangle&|\beta|^2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The environmental overlap suppresses locally observable [quantum coherence](../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md). Scattered [photons](../../../../../photon.md), surrounding gas and uncontrolled apparatus variables can carry away such records. The interaction often singles out robust states forming a [pointer basis](../../../../../pointer-basis.md). Decoherence can be much faster than thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) relaxation because recording a macroscopic distinction need not transfer much [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).

The overall state can remain pure and evolve unitarily even when the system alone looks like a classical mixture. Discarding environmental information is therefore not, by itself, a proof of fundamental [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) collapse or a solution to the definite-outcome [measurement problem](../../../../../measurement-problem.md). A small controlled environment can return coherence, as in the [single-qubit environment dephasing model](../../../../../single-qubit-environment-dephasing-model.md); practical irreversibility arises when recovering the many dispersed records becomes inaccessible. In an interferometer, varying gas [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) or an independently controllable path marker tests the dependence of fringe contrast on this environmental coupling. Quantum computation must suppress or correct such unwanted system–environment entanglement to preserve the intended [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md).
