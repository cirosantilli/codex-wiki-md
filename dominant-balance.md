# Dominant balance

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

Rescale variables so that the terms responsible for the leading behaviour have comparable size. The resulting [distinguished limit](distinguished-limit.md) exposes a governing equation that may differ from the naive zero-parameter equation. Examples are [dominant balance for algebraic roots](dominant-balance-for-algebraic-roots.md) and the derivative balance in a [square-root boundary layer at a vanishing drift](square-root-boundary-layer-at-a-vanishing-drift.md).
