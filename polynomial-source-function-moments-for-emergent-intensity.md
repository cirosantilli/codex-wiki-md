# Polynomial source function moments for emergent intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Formal solution of the radiative transfer equation](formal-solution-of-the-radiative-transfer-equation.md)

For a semi-infinite plane-parallel atmosphere with polynomial [source function](radiative-transfer-source-function.md) $S(\tau)=\sum a_j\tau^j$, the outward [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) is $\int_0^\infty S(\tau)e^{-\tau/\mu}\,d\tau/\mu$. Substitution $u=\tau/\mu$ gives coefficients $A_j=j!a_j$ in the surface polynomial in $\mu$. The deep boundary term must vanish. A local truncated Taylor series instead gives this contribution plus a remainder, so its surface polynomial is not automatically exact.
