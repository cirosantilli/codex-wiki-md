# Hamming ball volume exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming ball](hamming-ball.md)

For $0\le\rho\le1-1/q$, the exponential growth rate of a q-ary Hamming ball is $H_q(\rho)$. [Stirling's formula](stirling-formula.md) gives the exponent of each summand $\binom ni(q-1)^i$, and the sum lies between its largest term and $n+1$ times that term. Above the entropy-maximizing radius fraction, the exponent is one.
