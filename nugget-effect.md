# Nugget effect

↑ **Parent:** [Semivariogram](semivariogram.md)

The nugget is the discontinuity of a distance [semivariogram](semivariogram.md) at zero. Independent location-specific noise of [variance](variance-split.md) $\tau^2$ contributes [covariance](covariance.md) $\tau^2$ at zero displacement and zero [covariance](covariance.md) at nonzero displacement, so its [semivariogram](semivariogram.md) is $\tau^2\mathbf1_{\{h\ne0\}}$.
