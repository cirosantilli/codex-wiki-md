# Virial current

↑ **Parent:** [Noether current](noether-current.md)

A [virial current](virial-current.md) $V^\mu$ represents the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of a conserved [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) as a divergence. Then the dilation [Noether current](noether-current.md) $D^\mu=x_\nu T^{\mu\nu}-V^\mu$ is conserved, since its divergence is $T^\mu{}_{\mu}-\partial_\mu V^\mu$. This establishes [scale invariance](scale-invariance.md); a traceless [stress-energy tensor improvement](stress-energy-tensor-improvement.md) is the extra condition needed for [conformal currents](conformal-current.md).
