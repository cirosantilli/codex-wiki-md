# Fixed-mean-number Bogoliubov variational energy

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov theory](bogoliubov-theory.md)

For a weakly depleted homogeneous [Bose gas](bose-gas.md) with repulsive [contact interaction](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md), set $g=uN/V$ and $\epsilon_k=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$. A zero-mode [coherent state](coherent-state.md) times a [two-mode squeezed vacuum](two-mode-squeezed-vacuum.md) for each opposite-[momentum](momentum-split.md) pair has mean [particle number](particle-number.md) $N=N_0+\sum_{k\ne0}\sinh^2\theta_k$. Substitution of $N_0=N-\sum\sinh^2\theta_k$ into the leading condensate [interaction energy](interaction-energy.md), followed by a consistent quadratic truncation, gives the displayed variational [energy](energy-split.md). Omitting this substitution loses the subtraction embodied in the [number-conserving Bogoliubov quadratic Hamiltonian](number-conserving-bogoliubov-quadratic-hamiltonian.md).

For each mode set $A_k=\epsilon_k+g$ and $E_k=\sqrt{A_k^2-g^2}$. If $A_k>|g|$, choose $\tanh(2\theta_k^*)=g/A_k$. The mode contribution equals $\tfrac12E_k\cosh[2(\theta_k-\theta_k^*)]-A_k/2$, proving a unique minimum $(E_k-A_k)/2$. At fixed [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md) this lowers the [energy](energy-split.md) relative to the uncorrelated condensate. The approximation requires small [quantum depletion of a Bose condensate](quantum-depletion-of-a-bose-condensate.md); an unregulated three-dimensional [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md) also requires [renormalization of a three-dimensional dilute-gas contact coupling](renormalization-of-a-three-dimensional-dilute-gas-contact-coupling.md).
