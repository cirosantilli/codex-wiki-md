# Mass second-moment tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

The mass second-moment tensor about a [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) is $I_{jk}=\int y_jy_k\,dm$. It measures the shape of a mass distribution and appears in [tidal torque theory](tidal-torque-theory.md). It differs from the mechanical [inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md), which is $\mathcal I_{jk}=\operatorname{tr}(I)\delta_{jk}-I_{jk}$. For a uniform [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) of mass $M$ and semiaxes $a_1,a_2,a_3$, its principal values are $Ma_j^2/5$.
