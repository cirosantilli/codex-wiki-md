# Weiss zone law

↑ **Parent:** [Miller index](miller-index.md)

A lattice direction $[uvw]$ lies parallel to a plane $(hkl)$ exactly when $hu+kv+lw=0$. Indeed, their direct and reciprocal lattice vectors have [dot product](dot-product.md) proportional to this expression. This statement holds for noncubic cells as well; angles between directions still require the direct-lattice [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md).
