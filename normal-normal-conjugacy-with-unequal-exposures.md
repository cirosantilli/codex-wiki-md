# Normal-normal conjugacy with unequal exposures

↑ **Parent:** [Normal-normal conjugacy with known observation variance](normal-normal-conjugacy-with-known-observation-variance.md)

For independent normal observations conditional on $\Theta$, with mean $\Theta$ and variances $v/m_j$, and normal prior mean $\mu$ and variance $a$, write $W=\sum_jm_j$. Multiplying likelihood and prior adds precisions $W/v+1/a$. Completing the square gives the displayed [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md). Its [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) is $Z\overline X_w+(1-Z)\mu$, where $Z=aW/(v+aW)$ and $\overline X_w=\sum_jm_jX_j/W$. Thus the [Bayes estimator under squared error loss](bayes-estimator-under-squared-error-loss.md) is exactly the [Bühlmann–Straub credibility estimate](buhlmann-straub-credibility-estimate.md), not merely an approximation to it. Its posterior variance is $av/(v+aW)$.
