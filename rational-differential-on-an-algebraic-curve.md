# Rational differential on an algebraic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical divisor](canonical-divisor.md)

A rational differential on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) is a rational section of its [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md). In a local coordinate $t$ it has the form $f(t)\,dt$ for a [rational function](rational-function.md) $f$.
