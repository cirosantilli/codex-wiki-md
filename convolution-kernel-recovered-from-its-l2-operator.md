# Convolution kernel recovered from its L2 operator

↑ **Parent:** [Convolution](convolution.md)

An integrable [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is uniquely determined by its [convolution](convolution.md) operator on $L^2$. Convolve it with a smooth compactly supported [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md) $\eta_\varepsilon$, whose members also lie in $L^2$. A zero operator makes $h*\eta_\varepsilon=0$, while [translation continuity in Lp](translation-continuity-in-lp.md) gives $h*\eta_\varepsilon\to h$ in $L^1$, proving the assertion. Since the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) of [convolution](convolution.md) by $f$ is [convolution](convolution.md) by $f^\sharp(s)=\overline{f(-s)}$, this also proves the exact self-adjointness criterion $f=f^\sharp$ almost everywhere, including necessity at the level of kernels.
