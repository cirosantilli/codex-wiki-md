# Piecewise integer-linear representation of a free Abelian lattice-ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Free Abelian lattice-ordered group](free-abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md)

Evaluate the generators as coordinate projections on $\mathbb R^n$. The [lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md) they generate consists of continuous [positively homogeneous](positively-homogeneous-function-degree-one.md) [piecewise linear functions](piecewise-linear-function.md) with finitely many integer-linear pieces. Equivalently its elements are finite [joins](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of finite [meets](greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of integer linear forms. The [universal property](universal-property.md) follows because the real line detects every identity of [Abelian lattice-ordered groups](abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md): if an identity fails, pass to a prime ordered quotient and select the finitely many active linear pieces. The resulting rational linear inequalities have a real solution by the alternative underlying [Farkas' lemma](farkas-lemma.md), giving a real witness to failure. This is a statement about integer coefficients and positive homogeneity, not arbitrary real coefficients or affine constant terms.
