# Peak-background split

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak-background_split)

The peak-background split decomposes density fluctuations into long- and short-wavelength parts. A long overdensity lowers the effective local collapse threshold and thereby changes the abundance of halos.
