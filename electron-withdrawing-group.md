# Electron-withdrawing group

↑ **Parent:** [Substituent](substituent.md)

A [substituent](substituent.md) that reduces [electron](electron.md) density in a specified molecular region by induction or [chemical resonance](resonance-chemistry.md). The sign and size of its effect depend on connectivity and the measured property.
