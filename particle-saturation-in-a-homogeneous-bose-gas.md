# Particle saturation in a homogeneous Bose gas

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous ideal Bose gas condensation](homogeneous-ideal-bose-gas-condensation.md)

At fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), the continuum excited population of a three-dimensional ideal [Bose gas](bose-gas.md) reaches a finite maximum as the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) approaches the ground [energy](energy-split.md). Adding particles beyond that maximum creates macroscopic ground occupation. Thus $N'=\min(N,N_c)$ and $N_0=\max(0,N-N_c)$ to leading thermodynamic-limit order. Finite systems retain nonmacroscopic ground occupation below the nominal threshold.
