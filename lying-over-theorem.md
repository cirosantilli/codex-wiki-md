# Lying-over theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Integral extension](integral-extension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lying-over_theorem)

If $A\subseteq B$ is an [integral extension](integral-extension.md), then every [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak p$ of $A$ is the contraction of a prime ideal $\mathfrak q$ of $B$. Thus the contraction map $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is surjective.
