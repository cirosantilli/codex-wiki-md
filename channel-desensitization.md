# Channel desensitization

↑ **Parent:** [Ion channel](ion-channel.md)

[Channel desensitization](channel-desensitization.md) is entry into a less responsive or nonconducting state during sustained stimulation. A ligand-bound channel can therefore be closed without the ligand having dissociated.
