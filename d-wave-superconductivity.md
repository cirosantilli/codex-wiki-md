# D-wave superconductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Unconventional superconductivity](unconventional-superconductivity.md)

Pairing with d-wave orbital structure, such as a crystal gap proportional to $k_x^2-k_y^2$. Its sign changes under a quarter-turn rotation, but a compensating overall phase change can leave gauge-invariant observables fourfold symmetric. Gap nodes and phase signs therefore do not by themselves establish nematicity or time-reversal breaking. A [phase-sensitive test of d-wave pairing](phase-sensitive-test-of-d-wave-pairing.md) probes relative signs through [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md).
