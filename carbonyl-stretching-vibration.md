# Carbonyl stretching vibration

↑ **Parent:** [Infrared spectroscopy](infrared-spectroscopy.md)

A [normal mode](normal-mode.md) with substantial variation of a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) bond length. Its [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) depends on the effective force constant and coupled motions; substitution, conjugation and measurement conditions matter.
