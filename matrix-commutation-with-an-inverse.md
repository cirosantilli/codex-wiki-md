# Matrix commutation with an inverse

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix inverse](matrix-inverse-split.md)

If two square [matrices](matrix.md) satisfy $AB=BA$ and $B$ is invertible, multiplication on the left and right by $B^{-1}$ gives $B^{-1}A=AB^{-1}$. Consequently a polynomial in a [matrix](matrix.md) commutes with the [matrix inverse](matrix-inverse-split.md) of any invertible polynomial in the same [matrix](matrix.md).
