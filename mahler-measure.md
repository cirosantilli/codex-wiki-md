# Mahler measure

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahler_measure)

If $f(X)=a_d\prod_{j=1}^d(X-\alpha_j)$, its Mahler measure is

$$
M(f)=|a_d|\prod_{j=1}^d\max\{1,|\alpha_j|\}.
$$
