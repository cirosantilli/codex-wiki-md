# Special orthogonal group in two dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md)

Every real orthogonal two-by-two matrix of determinant one has the unique form

$$
R(z)=\begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\b&a\end{pmatrix},\qquad z=a+ib,\qquad a^2+b^2=1.
$$

Indeed the first column is a [unit vector](unit-vector.md), the perpendicular second column has one of two signs, and positive determinant selects the displayed one. The map $z\mapsto R(z)$ from the [circle group](circle-group.md) is bijective and satisfies $R(zw)=R(z)R(w)$ by direct multiplication, proving a [group isomorphism](group-isomorphism.md). Writing $z=e^{i\theta}$ parametrizes these matrices as planar [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md), with the angle defined modulo $2\pi$.
