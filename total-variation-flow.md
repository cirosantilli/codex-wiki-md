# Total variation flow

↑ **Parent:** [Total variation seminorm on a domain](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md)

Total variation flow is the $L^2$ [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md) of the [total variation seminorm on a domain](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md). Formally it is $u_t=\operatorname{div}(\nabla u/|\nabla u|)$, but the [subgradient](subgradient.md) formulation handles zero gradients and jumps. Adding quadratic fidelity gives a [strictly convex](strictly-convex-function.md) stationary [total variation denoising](total-variation-denoising.md) problem.
