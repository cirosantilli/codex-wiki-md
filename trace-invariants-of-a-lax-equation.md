# Trace invariants of a Lax equation

↑ **Parent:** [Isospectral Lax equation](isospectral-lax-equation.md)

If

$$
\dot L=[L,A],
$$

then every positive power has constant trace:

$$
\frac d{dt}\operatorname{tr}(L^n)
=n\operatorname{tr}(L^{n-1}[A,L])=0.
$$

The final equality is the cyclic property of the [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md).
