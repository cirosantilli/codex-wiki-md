↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The symmetric [Lovász local lemma](../../../../../lovasz-local-lemma.md) states: if each bad [event](../../../../../event.md) has [probability](../../../../../probability.md) at most $p$, each [event](../../../../../event.md) is [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the collection of all but at most $d$ other [events](../../../../../event.md), and

$$
\boxed{ep(d+1)\leq1,}
$$

then the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) that none of the bad [events](../../../../../event.md) occurs is positive. [Independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) is from the [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) generated by the indicated nonneighbours, as in part (a). A sufficient sharper parameter condition is $p\leq x(1-x)^d$ for some $x\in(0,1)$; taking $x=1/(d+1)$ gives the displayed standard bound when $d\geq1$. When $d=0$, mutual [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) directly gives the conclusion under the displayed bound.
