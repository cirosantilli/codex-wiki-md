<h1 id="a/2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take a proper [colouring](../../../../../../colouring-of-a-set.md) $c:V(G)\to\{1,\ldots,k\}$ and orient every [edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) from its lower-coloured endpoint towards its higher-coloured endpoint. Along a [directed path](../../../../../../directed-path.md) the colour strictly increases, so such a [graph path](../../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md) has at most $k$ [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md). A [directed cycle](../../../../../../directed-cycle.md) is impossible for the same reason. Thus

$$
\boxed{D\text{ is acyclic and }\lambda(D)\leq k.}
$$
