↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) version of [Menger's theorem](../../../../../../menger-theorem.md) states that for two distinct nonadjacent [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) $s,t$ of a finite [graph](../../../../../../graph-split.md), the [maximum](../../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) number of internally vertex-disjoint $s$-$t$ [graph paths](../../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md) equals the [minimum](../../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) size of a [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $S\subseteq V\setminus\{s,t\}$ whose deletion separates $s$ from $t$.

$$
\boxed{\max\{\text{internally disjoint }s\text{-}t\text{ paths}\}=\min\{|S|:s,t\text{ disconnected in }G-S\}.}
$$

Internally disjoint [graph paths](../../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md) may share their endpoints but no other [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md). The nonadjacency hypothesis is needed in this separator formulation, since an [edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) $st$ cannot be destroyed by deleting other [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md).
