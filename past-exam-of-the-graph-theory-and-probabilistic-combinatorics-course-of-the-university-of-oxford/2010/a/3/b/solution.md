<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For disjoint nonempty [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) sets $X,Y$, let $e(X,Y)$ count edges with one endpoint in each. Their [density](../../../../../edge-density-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) is

$$
\boxed{d(X,Y)=\frac{e(X,Y)}{|X||Y|}.}
$$

For $0<\epsilon<1$, the pair is a [regular pair of vertex sets](../../../../../regular-pair-of-vertex-sets.md) if all $X'\subseteq X$ and $Y'\subseteq Y$ with $|X'|\geq\epsilon|X|$ and $|Y'|\geq\epsilon|Y|$ satisfy

$$
\boxed{|d(X',Y')-d(X,Y)|\leq\epsilon.}
$$

Using the convention with a strict bound less than $\epsilon$ instead gives the same argument below; the assumed density is strictly larger than $\epsilon$.
