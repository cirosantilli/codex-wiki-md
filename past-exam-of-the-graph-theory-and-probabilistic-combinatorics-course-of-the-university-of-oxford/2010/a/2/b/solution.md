<h1 id="a/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Colour the [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) $ij$ of the [complete graph](../../../../../complete-graph.md) on $\{1,\ldots,6\}$ by the class containing $|j-i|$. Part (a) gives $R(3,3)\leq6$, so there is a monochromatic triangle with vertices $i<j<k$. Its three [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) differences are

$$
x=j-i,\qquad y=k-j,\qquad z=k-i=x+y.
$$

They are positive integers in one class. Hence **every two-colouring of the positive integers has a monochromatic solution of $x+y=z$**. This is the [two-colour Schur theorem](../../../../../two-colour-schur-theorem.md). The two summands need not be distinct.
