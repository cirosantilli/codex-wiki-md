For a positive integer $x$, the [chromatic polynomial](../../../../../chromatic-polynomial.md) $p_G(x)$ counts proper vertex colorings from a palette of $x$ labelled colors, where adjacent vertices must have different colors. The counting function agrees with a polynomial, as proved by deletion-contraction in the next part.
