<h1 id="a/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An even cycle is bipartite and has [chromatic number](../../../../../chromatic-number.md) two. An odd cycle has [chromatic number](../../../../../chromatic-number.md) three. The extremal-density theorem therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{ex}(n;C_k)}{\binom n2}=\begin{cases}0,&k\text{ even},\\1/2,&k\text{ odd}.\end{cases}}
$$

For odd cycles the complete balanced [bipartite graph](../../../../../bipartite-graph-split.md) also exhibits the [matching](../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) asymptotic lower density one half.
