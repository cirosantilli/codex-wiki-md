↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [extremal number](../../../../../extremal-number.md) $\operatorname{ex}(n;H)$ is the maximum number of edges in a simple [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) on $n$ vertices containing no subgraph isomorphic to $H$. For a fixed [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) with $\chi(H)=r\ge2$, the [Erdős-Stone theorem](../../../../../erdos-stone-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ex}(n;H)=\left(1-\frac1{r-1}+o(1)\right)\frac{n^2}{2}}.
$$

Equivalently, the ratio to $\binom n2$ tends to $1-1/(r-1)$. The bipartite case has limiting density zero.
