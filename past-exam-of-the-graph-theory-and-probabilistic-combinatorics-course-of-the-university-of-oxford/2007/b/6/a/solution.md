<h1 id="b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a variable $Z$ of finite [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) and $t>0$, [Chebyshev's inequality](../../../../../chebyshev-s-inequality.md) is

$$
P(|Z-EZ|\ge t)\le\frac{\operatorname{Var}Z}{t^2}.
$$

On that event, $(Z-EZ)^2\ge t^2$. Thus $t^2P(|Z-EZ|\ge t)\le E[(Z-EZ)^2]$, proving the assertion. Equivalently apply [Markov's inequality](../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) to the squared deviation.
