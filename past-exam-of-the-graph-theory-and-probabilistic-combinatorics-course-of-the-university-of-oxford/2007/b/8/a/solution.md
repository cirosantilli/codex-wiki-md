On this product, use the Hamming metric $d(x,y)=|\{i:x_i\ne y_i\}|$. The relevant Lipschitz condition is $|f(x)-f(y)|\le d(x,y)$, equivalently changing any one coordinate changes the function by at most one. More generally an $L$-[Lipschitz function](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) has bound $L\,d(x,y)$. The unit version is the bounded-differences condition used below.
