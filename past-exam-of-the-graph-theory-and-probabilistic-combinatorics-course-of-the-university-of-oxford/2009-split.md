# 2009

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the graph Theory and Probabilistic Combinatorics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Graph Theory and Probabilistic Combinatorics, Trinity Term; Friday 5 June 2009, 9.30am–12.30pm. This combined Part C paper was shared by Mathematics, Mathematics and Computer Science, and Mathematics and Statistics. Both sections and all six questions are covered below; the complete four-page source is retained in a private editable transcription. The source was supplied through [the past-papers disclosure](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), in original Part C bundle pages 286–289. No exact worked-answer advertisement was found in the bounded check.
