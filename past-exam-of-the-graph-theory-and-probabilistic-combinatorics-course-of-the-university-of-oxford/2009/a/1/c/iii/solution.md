**False.** The [complete bipartite graph](../../../../../../complete-bipartite-graph.md) $K_{2009,2010}$ is $2009$-connected. Deleting fewer than $2009$ vertices leaves at least one vertex in each part and therefore a connected [complete bipartite graph](../../../../../../complete-bipartite-graph.md); deleting the smaller part disconnects the larger independent set. But its two parts have unequal sizes, so a [matching](../../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) cannot cover them both. Equivalently it has an odd number of vertices. Thus it has no [perfect matching](../../../../../../perfect-matching.md).
