↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Every cycle in a [bipartite graph](../../../../../../bipartite-graph-split.md) has even length because its vertices alternate between the two parts. On a [Hamiltonian cycle](../../../../../../hamilton-cycle.md), choose every other edge. These edges are disjoint and cover every vertex exactly once, so they form a [perfect matching](../../../../../../perfect-matching.md).
