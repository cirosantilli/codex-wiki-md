↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For disjoint nonempty vertex sets, their [edge density](../../../../../edge-density-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) is $d(X,Y)=e(X,Y)/(|X||Y|)$. The pair is [epsilon-regular](../../../../../regular-pair-of-vertex-sets.md) or epsilon-uniform if for all $X'\subseteq X$, $Y'\subseteq Y$ with $|X'|\geq\epsilon|X|$ and $|Y'|\geq\epsilon|Y|$,

$$
|d(X',Y')-d(X,Y)|<\epsilon.
$$

It controls density on every sufficiently large subpair, rather than merely on average over chosen subpairs.
