↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An epsilon-uniform partition has disjoint classes $V_0,V_1,\ldots,V_k$ covering the vertex set, with $|V_0|\leq\epsilon|V(G)|$, equal nonexceptional class sizes $|V_1|=\cdots=|V_k|$, and at most $\epsilon k^2$ unordered pairs of distinct nonexceptional classes failing epsilon-uniformity. The exceptional set need not have the common size.

[Szemerédi regularity lemma](../../../../../szemeredi-regularity-lemma.md) states that for every $\epsilon>0$ and positive integer $m_0$, there is $M=M(\epsilon,m_0)$ such that every sufficiently large finite [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md), for example every [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) of order at least $M$ after enlarging $M$ if needed, has such a partition with $m_0\leq k\leq M$. The bound depends only on the accuracy and minimum class count, not on the [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md)'s order.
