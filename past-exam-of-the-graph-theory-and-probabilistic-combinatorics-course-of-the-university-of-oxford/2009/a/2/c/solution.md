<h1 id="a/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Set $t=-x>0$. The coefficient formula gives

$$
(-1)^np_G(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_it^{n-i}.
$$

Every summand is nonnegative and the leading term is $t^n>0$, since $a_0=1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{(-1)^np_G(x)>0\quad(x<0)}.
$$
