<h1 id="b/4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each cyclic starting position let $I_i$ indicate that its length-$k$ block is increasing. All $k!$ relative orders of its distinct entries are equally likely, so $\mathbb EI_i=1/k!$. By [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbb EX_k=\frac n{k!}}.
$$

The indicators need not be independent for this calculation.
