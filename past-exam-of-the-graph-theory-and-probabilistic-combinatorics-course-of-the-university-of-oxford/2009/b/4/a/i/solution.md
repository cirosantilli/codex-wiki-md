[Chebyshev's inequality](../../../../../../chebyshev-s-inequality.md) states that if $X$ has finite mean $\mu$ and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, then for $t>0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(|X-\mu|\geq t)\leq\frac{\sigma^2}{t^2}}.
$$

Indeed $(X-\mu)^2\geq t^2\mathbf1_{\{|X-\mu|\geq t\}}$ pointwise. Taking expectations proves the result, including the zero-[variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) case.
