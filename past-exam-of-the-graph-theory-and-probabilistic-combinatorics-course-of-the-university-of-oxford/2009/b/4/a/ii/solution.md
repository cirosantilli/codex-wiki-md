<h1 id="b/4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The assumptions imply

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X_n)=\mathbb EX_n^2-\mu_n^2=o(\mu_n^2).
$$

Eventually $\mu_n>0$, and $X_n=0$ implies $|X_n-\mu_n|\geq\mu_n$. Thus [Chebyshev's inequality](../../../../../../chebyshev-s-inequality.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X_n=0)\leq\frac{\operatorname{Var}(X_n)}{\mu_n^2}\longrightarrow0}.
$$

No [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) between the different $X_n$ is needed; this is a [second moment method](../../../../../../second-moment-method.md).
