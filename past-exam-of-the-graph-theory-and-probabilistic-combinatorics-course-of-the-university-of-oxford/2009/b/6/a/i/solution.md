↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [dependency graph of events](../../../../../../dependency-graph-of-events.md), written here as a loopless directed [graph](../../../../../../graph-split.md) $D$ on the event indices, must satisfy a joint [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) condition: for each $i$, $A_i$ is independent of the [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) generated by all $A_j$ with $j\ne i$ and no arc $i\to j$. In particular this means [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) from every Boolean combination of those non-neighbour events, not merely pairwise [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md). The outgoing neighbours list the events for which that [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) is not required.
