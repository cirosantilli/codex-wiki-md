<h1 id="b/6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For distinct indices put arcs in both directions whenever $S_i\cap S_j\ne\varnothing$. If $j$ is not an outgoing neighbour of $i$, every variable determining $A_j$ lies outside $S_i$. The entire family of such events is measurable with respect to variables outside $S_i$, whose [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) is independent of the variables inside $S_i$. Thus $A_i$ is independent of their whole generated [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md), establishing the required [dependency graph of events](../../../../../../dependency-graph-of-events.md). This justifies joint [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) rather than just checking each pair.
