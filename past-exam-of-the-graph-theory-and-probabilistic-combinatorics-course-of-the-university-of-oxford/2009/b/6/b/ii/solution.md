<h1 id="b/6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the same independent coloring, now with events indexed by unoriented six-edge paths. A repetition fixes the second three-edge color block to equal the first, so each event has [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $k^{-3}$. A fixed edge belongs to at most $6\Delta^5$ such paths, and a six-edge path has at most $36\Delta^5$ events in its closed dependency neighbourhood. Thus $d+1\leq36\Delta^5$.

For $k\geq5\Delta^{5/3}$,

$$
e\,k^{-3}(d+1)\leq\frac{36e\Delta^5}{125\Delta^5}<1.
$$

The symmetric [Lovász local lemma](../../../../../../lovasz-local-lemma.md) again guarantees a coloring avoiding all six-edge repetition events. Thus **the required coloring exists**, with the empty event family handled trivially.
