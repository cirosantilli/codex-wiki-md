↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [graph Ramsey number](../../../../../graph-ramsey-number.md) $R(G,H)$ is the least $N$ such that every red-blue coloring of the edges of $K_N$ contains a red copy of $G$ or a blue copy of $H$. Copies are ordinary subgraphs, not necessarily induced. Equivalently every [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) on $N$ vertices contains $G$ or its complement contains $H$.
