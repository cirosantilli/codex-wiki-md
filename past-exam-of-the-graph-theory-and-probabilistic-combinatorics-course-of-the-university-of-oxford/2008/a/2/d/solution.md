<h1 id="a/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The degree of a [chromatic polynomial](../../../../../chromatic-polynomial.md) is the number of vertices and the negative of its next coefficient is the number of edges. Equality with $p_{K_n}$ therefore forces $n$ vertices and $\binom n2$ edges. A simple [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) on those vertices with that many edges contains every possible edge, so $\boxed{G\cong K_n}$.
