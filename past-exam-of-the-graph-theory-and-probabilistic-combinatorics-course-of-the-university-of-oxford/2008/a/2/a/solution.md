The [Turán graph](../../../../../turan-graph.md) $T_r(n)$ is the complete $r$-partite [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) whose nonempty part sizes differ by at most one. Write $n=qr+s$, $0\le s<r$: it has $s$ parts of size $q+1$ and $r-s$ parts of size $q$.

Turán's theorem states that every simple $n$-vertex [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) with no $K_{r+1}$ has at most $e(T_r(n))$ edges, with equality precisely for a [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) isomorphic to $T_r(n)$. Its edge number is half of $n^2$ minus the sum of the squared part sizes.
