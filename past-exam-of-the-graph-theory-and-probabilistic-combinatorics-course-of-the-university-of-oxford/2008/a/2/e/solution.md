↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [chromatic number](../../../../../chromatic-number.md) is the smallest positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $q$ for which $p_G(q)>0$. Thus equality of the polynomials implies $\chi(G)=r$, as well as $|V(G)|=n$ and $e(G)=e(T_r(n))$. In particular $G$ contains no $K_{r+1}$, since such a [clique](../../../../../clique-graph-theory.md) requires $r+1$ colors. The equality case in Turán's theorem now gives $\boxed{G\cong T_r(n)}$. This uses more than equality of triangle counts: the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) also determines colorability.
