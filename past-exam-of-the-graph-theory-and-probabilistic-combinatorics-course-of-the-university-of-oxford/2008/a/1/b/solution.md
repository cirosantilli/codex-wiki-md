<h1 id="a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Make a [bipartite graph](../../../../../bipartite-graph-split.md) whose two classes index the two partitions, joining $i$ on the left to $j$ on the right exactly when $A_i\cap B_j\ne\varnothing$. For a subset $U$ of left indices, its [union](../../../../../set-union.md) of blocks contains $2008|U|$ elements. They all lie in the blocks indexed by $N(U)$, whose [union](../../../../../set-union.md) has $2008|N(U)|$ elements. Thus $|N(U)|\ge|U|$. Hall's theorem gives a [matching](../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) covering all $n$ left vertices, hence also all $n$ right vertices. Its partner map is the required permutation $\pi$.
