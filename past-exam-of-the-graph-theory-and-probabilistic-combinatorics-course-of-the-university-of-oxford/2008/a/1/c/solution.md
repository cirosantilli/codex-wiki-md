<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the vertex form of [Menger's theorem](../../../../../menger-theorem.md), for distinct nonadjacent vertices $s,t$ of a finite [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md), the maximum number of pairwise internally vertex-disjoint $s$–$t$ paths equals the minimum size of an $s$–$t$ [vertex separator](../../../../../vertex-separator.md). A separator is a subset of vertices other than $s,t$ whose deletion leaves no $s$–$t$ path; internally disjoint paths may share their endpoints but no other vertices. Equivalently, between disjoint vertex sets the maximum number of mutually vertex-disjoint connecting paths equals the minimum size of a separating vertex set, where endpoints may be deleted in that set version.
