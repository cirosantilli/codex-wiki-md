For nonempty disjoint vertex sets, the edge density is $d(X,Y)=e(X,Y)/(|X||Y|)$. A pair is $\epsilon$-uniform, also called $\epsilon$-regular, if every $X'\subseteq X$ and $Y'\subseteq Y$ with $|X'|\ge\epsilon|X|$ and $|Y'|\ge\epsilon|Y|$ satisfies

$$
|d(X',Y')-d(X,Y)|<\epsilon.
$$

It says that restricting to reasonably large subsets does not noticeably change the edge density.
