↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the standard regularity-partition convention, the vertex sets form a disjoint partition, the nonexceptional sets $V_1,\ldots,V_k$ are nonempty and have a common size, $|V_0|<\epsilon|V(G)|$, and at most $\epsilon k^2$ unordered nonexceptional pairs fail to be $\epsilon$-uniform. One can instead use weak inequality for the exceptional-set bound without affecting the argument below once the size bound is strict. The exceptional set absorbs the rounding remainder when the [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) order is not divisible by $k$.
