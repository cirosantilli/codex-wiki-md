[Chebyshev's inequality](../../../../../chebyshev-s-inequality.md) says that for a random variable $Z$ with finite mean and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md), and every $t>0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(|Z-\mathbb EZ|\ge t)\le\frac{\operatorname{Var}Z}{t^2}}.
$$
