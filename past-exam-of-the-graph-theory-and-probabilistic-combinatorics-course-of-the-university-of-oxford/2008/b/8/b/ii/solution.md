<h1 id="b/8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $p=1/2$ and $k=\log_2n+\omega(n)$,

$$
\mathbb EX=n2^{-k}=2^{-\omega(n)}.
$$

If $\omega(n)\to\infty$, the mean tends to zero. [Markov's inequality](../../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) gives $\mathbb P(X\ge1)\le\mathbb EX\to0$, so $\boxed{\mathbb P(X=0)\to1}$.
