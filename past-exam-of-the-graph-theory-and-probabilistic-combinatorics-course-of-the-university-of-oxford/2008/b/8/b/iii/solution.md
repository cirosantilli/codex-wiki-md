↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Now $\omega(n)\to-\infty$ makes $\mathbb EX=2^{-\omega(n)}\to\infty$. [Chebyshev's inequality](../../../../../../chebyshev-s-inequality.md) and the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) bound give

$$
\mathbb P(X=0)\le\frac{\operatorname{Var}X}{(\mathbb EX)^2}\le\frac3{\mathbb EX}\longrightarrow0.
$$

For every fixed $\epsilon>0$, they likewise give

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P\left(\left|\frac X{\mathbb EX}-1\right|>\epsilon\right)\le\frac3{\epsilon^2\mathbb EX}\longrightarrow0}.
$$

Thus the count is concentrated about its growing mean, despite the overlapping runs.
