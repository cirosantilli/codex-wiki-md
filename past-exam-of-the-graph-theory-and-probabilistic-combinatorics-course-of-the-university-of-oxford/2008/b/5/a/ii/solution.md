<h1 id="b/5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $x-\log(1+x)$ has derivative $x/(1+x)$, is zero at zero, and is nonnegative for every $x>-1$. Therefore $\log(1+x)\le x$. Multiplying by $m>0$ and exponentiating gives

$$
\boxed{(1+x)^m\le e^{xm}}.
$$

Equality occurs at $x=0$.
