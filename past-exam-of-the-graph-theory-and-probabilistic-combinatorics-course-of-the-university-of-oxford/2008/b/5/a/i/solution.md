<h1 id="b/5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $k\ge1$, monotonicity of the logarithm gives

$$
\log(k!)=\sum_{j=1}^k\log j\ge\int_1^k\log t\,dt=k\log k-k+1\ge k\log k-k.
$$

Thus $k!\ge(k/e)^k$. Bounding the numerator of the [binomial coefficient](../../../../../../binomial-coefficient-split.md) by $n^k$ yields

$$
\boxed{\binom nk\le\frac{n^k}{k!}\le(en/k)^k}.
$$
