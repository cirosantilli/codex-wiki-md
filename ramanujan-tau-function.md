# Ramanujan tau function

↑ **Parent:** [Modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan_tau_function)

The Ramanujan tau function gives the [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) at positive indices of the [modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md): $\Delta(z)=\sum_{n\geq1}\tau(n)q^n$, with $\tau(1)=1$. The [Serre derivative](serre-derivative.md) and [vanishing of weight-fourteen level-one cusp forms](vanishing-of-weight-fourteen-level-one-cusp-forms.md) give $q\,d\Delta/dq=E_2\Delta$ and hence the convolution recurrence

$$
(1-n)\tau(n)=24\sum_{r=1}^{n-1}\sigma_1(r)\tau(n-r).
$$
