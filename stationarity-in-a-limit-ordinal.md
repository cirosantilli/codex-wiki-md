# Stationarity in a limit ordinal

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary set](stationary-set.md)

For any nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md) $\delta$, a [club set](club-set.md) is an unbounded [subset](subset.md) closed under limit points below $\delta$. A [subset](subset.md) is stationary if it meets every such [club set](club-set.md). Statements concerning the completeness of the [club filter](club-filter.md) or [Fodor lemma](fodor-lemma.md) require regular uncountable height; they should not be transferred to arbitrary singular or countable [limit ordinals](limit-ordinal.md) without checking their hypotheses.
