# Axis-angle representation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebra](linear-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axis–angle_representation)

An [axis-angle representation](axis-angle-representation.md) specifies a rotation of three-dimensional Euclidean space by an oriented unit axis and an angle. The axis is fixed, and the perpendicular plane undergoes a pure [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md), with no dilation.
