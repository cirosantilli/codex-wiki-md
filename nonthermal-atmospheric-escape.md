# Nonthermal atmospheric escape

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric escape](atmospheric-escape.md)

Nonthermal atmospheric escape supplies particle energy through processes other than the local thermal distribution. Examples include dissociation producing fast atoms, ion pickup by a stellar-wind electric field, and sputtering caused by impacting energetic particles. [Radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) can also accelerate material in an extended [exosphere](exosphere.md).
