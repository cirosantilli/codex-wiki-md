# Free-draining polymer friction

↑ **Parent:** [Rouse model](rouse-model.md)

With additive drag from $N$ identical [monomers](monomer.md), $\zeta_N=N\zeta_0$ and centre-of-mass [diffusion](diffusion.md) is $D_N=k_BT/(N\zeta_0)$. For [monomer](monomer.md) [Stokes law](stokes-s-law.md) $\zeta_0=6\pi\eta a$, this gives $D_N\propto N^{-1}$. The model neglects long-range hydrodynamic coupling and does not alone describe the long-time spatial [diffusion](diffusion.md) of a strongly entangled chain.
