# Predator

↑ **Parent:** [Predation](predation.md)

A [predator](predator.md) is an [organism](organism-split.md) that consumes [prey](prey.md) through [predation](predation.md). A [predator](predator.md) need not occupy the highest [trophic level](trophic-level.md) of an [ecosystem](ecosystem.md), and the label does not specify its size or guarantee that it has no [predators](predator.md) of its own. In the illustrative [food chain](food-chain.md), the small [fish](fish.md) is a [predator](predator.md) at level 3 and also [prey](prey.md) for the large [fish](fish.md) at level 4. Separating these relational labels from [species](species.md) names prevents a misleading division of the world into permanent [predator](predator.md) and [prey](prey.md) categories.
