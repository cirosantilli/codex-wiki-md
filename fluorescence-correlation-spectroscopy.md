# Fluorescence correlation spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md)

[fluorescence correlation spectroscopy](fluorescence-correlation-spectroscopy.md) analyzes fluctuations of detected [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) to infer quantities such as molecular number and movement through an observation volume. Photophysical fluctuations and background must be distinguished from transport.
