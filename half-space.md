# Half-space

↑ **Parent:** [Affine space](affine-space.md)

A closed half-space consists of a [hyperplane](hyperplane.md) and one of its two sides in a real [affine space](affine-space.md). Replacing the weak inequality by a strict inequality gives an open half-space. Half-spaces are [convex sets](convex-set.md), and finite [intersections](set-intersection.md) of closed [half-spaces](half-space.md) are [linear polyhedra](linear-polyhedron.md).
