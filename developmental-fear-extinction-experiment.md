# Developmental fear-extinction experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Extinction learning](extinction-learning.md)

Pattwell and colleagues compared conditioned responding and [extinction](extinction-learning.md) across [human](human.md) developmental groups, with parallel mouse investigations. Skin-conductance responses indexed [human](human.md) [fear](fear.md) [learning](learning.md). The developmental laboratory pattern motivates clinical hypotheses but does not directly estimate the efficacy of [exposure therapy](exposure-therapy.md) for [adolescents](adolescent.md).[PNAS 109, 16318–16323; human conditioned-fear/extinction experiment and parallel mouse work](altered-fear-learning-across-development-in-both-mouse-and-human-by-siobhan-s-pattwell-and-others.md)
