# Nebentypus character

↑ **Parent:** [Modular form on a finite-index subgroup](modular-form-on-a-finite-index-subgroup.md)

A [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) $\psi$ modulo $N$ specifying the displayed transformation law is the [nebentypus](nebentypus-character.md) of a [modular form](modular-form.md). The action of $-I$ forces $\psi(-1)=(-1)^k$ for a nonzero form. It describes a character eigenspace of the diamond action rather than an additional analytic condition at [modular cusps](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md).
