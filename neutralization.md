# Neutralization

↑ **Parent:** [Acid-base chemistry](acid-base-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutralization_(chemistry))

A [neutralization](neutralization.md) is a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) between a [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md) and a [chemical base](base-chemistry.md). In the [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md) example, [hydroxide](hydroxide.md) takes a [proton](proton.md) from the [weak acid](weak-acid.md) to form [water](water.md) and the [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md). The [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) updates the partner inventories before the approximate [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) calculation. The name does not require the resulting [pH](ph.md) to be $7$.
