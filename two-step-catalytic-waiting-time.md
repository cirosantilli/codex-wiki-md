# Two-step catalytic waiting time

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md)

The convolution of two independent sequential exponential waiting times has the displayed normalized [density](density.md) and mean $k_a^{-1}+k_b^{-1}$. At equal rates its limit is $k^2te^{-kt}$. A measured [density](density.md) of this form supports a multistep kinetic mechanism without uniquely identifying its hidden chemical intermediates.
