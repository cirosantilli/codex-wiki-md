# Exterior power of a vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

For a rank-$k$ smooth [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md), take the [exterior power](exterior-power.md) $\Lambda^rE_p$ in each fibre. A local frame gives a trivialization with fibre $\Lambda^r\mathbb R^k$, and a transition matrix $A$ acts by $\Lambda^rA$. Functoriality preserves the cocycle identities, producing a smooth vector bundle of rank $\binom{k}{r}$, or the zero bundle when $r>k$. Its top exterior power is the [determinant line bundle](determinant-line-bundle.md).
