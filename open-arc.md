# Open arc

↑ **Parent:** [Arc (topology)](arc-topology.md)

An open arc is a [topological space](topological-space.md) [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to an [open interval](open-interval.md). Unlike an [arc](arc-topology.md), which is [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to a [closed interval](closed-real-interval.md), an open arc does not include its two endpoints.
