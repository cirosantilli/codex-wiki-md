# Transit spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmosphere](exoplanet-atmosphere.md)

[Transit spectroscopy](transit-spectroscopy.md) infers the properties of an [exoplanet atmosphere](exoplanet-atmosphere.md) from the wavelength dependence of an [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md). Its measured product is an [exoplanet transmission spectrum](exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md), while an [annulus model for transmission spectroscopy](annulus-model-for-transmission-spectroscopy.md) connects the spectrum to atmospheric [optical depth](optical-depth.md). The observing technique is described by [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanets/how-we-find-and-characterize/).
