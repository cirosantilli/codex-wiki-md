# Doob-Meyer decomposition theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Submartingale](submartingale.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doob–Meyer_decomposition_theorem)

Under the usual filtration conditions, a càdlàg submartingale of [Class D](class-d-process.md) on a finite horizon has a unique decomposition into a uniformly integrable martingale and a predictable integrable nondecreasing process starting at zero. A general [continuous semimartingale decomposition](continuous-semimartingale-decomposition.md) instead allows an arbitrary continuous finite-variation drift. A convex function of a semimartingale need not be a submartingale when that drift has unrestricted sign.
