# Ritz-Galerkin equivalence for a symmetric coercive form

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Ritz method](rayleigh-ritz-method.md)

For a bounded symmetric [coercive bilinear form](coercive-bilinear-form.md) on a Hilbert space, a solution of $a(u,w)=\ell(w)$ minimizes $J(v)=a(v,v)/2-\ell(v)$. The displayed identity proves strict minimality and uniqueness. Restricting to a [conforming finite element space](conforming-finite-element-space.md) makes the discrete [Ritz method](rayleigh-ritz-method.md) equivalent to the [Galerkin method](galerkin-method.md). [Galerkin orthogonality](galerkin-orthogonality.md) gives exact best approximation in the [energy norm](energy-norm.md); the [Céa lemma](cea-s-lemma.md) translates it into a reference-norm estimate.
