# Busy period

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)

A [busy period](busy-period.md) is the interval from an arrival that finds the system empty until the next departure leaving it empty. It excludes the preceding idle waiting time. In a stable [M/G/1 queue](m-g-1-queue.md) with arrival rate $\lambda$ and mean service time $m$, its mean is $m/(1-\lambda m)$.
