# Branch path of a graph subdivision

↑ **Parent:** [Graph subdivision](graph-subdivision.md)

A [branch path of a graph subdivision](branch-path-of-a-graph-subdivision.md) is the replacement [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) for one original [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). Its endpoints are the corresponding [branch vertices of a graph subdivision](branch-vertex-of-a-graph-subdivision.md); its internal [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) have [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md) two within the [graph subdivision](graph-subdivision.md).
