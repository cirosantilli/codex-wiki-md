# Marginal incidence trend with clustered observations

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized estimating equation](generalized-estimating-equation.md)

A population-averaged log-rate model with exposure $P_{it}$ gives an annual [rate ratio](rate-ratio.md) $e^{\beta_1}$. A [generalized estimating equation](generalized-estimating-equation.md) can account for repeated observations within clusters through a working correlation and [sandwich covariance matrix](sandwich-covariance-matrix.md). Independent Poisson standard errors do not account for clustering. An exposure-weighted population rate and an equally weighted average of cluster rates are different estimands.
