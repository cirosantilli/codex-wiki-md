# Exponential reduction of a confluent hypergeometric equation with equal parameters

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer differential equation](kummer-differential-equation.md)

Putting $Y=e^zV$ in the displayed [Kummer differential equation](kummer-differential-equation.md) gives $zV''+(z+b)V'=0$. Thus $V'=Cz^{-b}e^{-z}$ on a chosen branch, yielding $Y=e^z[A+B\int z^{-b}e^{-z}dz]$. For $b=1/2$ and $z=x/2$, one solution is $e^{x/2}$ and a normalized second [Frobenius solution](frobenius-solution.md) is $\sqrt{x}\sum_{n\geq0}x^n/(2n+1)!!$. The analytic factors in both solutions have infinite [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md), since their coefficient ratios tend to zero. The second solution still has a square-root branch at zero; an entire series factor does not remove a nonintegral Frobenius exponent.
