# Gradient Estimation Using Stochastic Computation Graphs by John Schulman and others

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by John Schulman](work-by-john-schulman.md)

[Primary conference paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2015/file/de03beffeed9da5f3639a621bcab5dd4-Paper.pdf). Section 3.1 and Appendix A derive the general [gradient](gradient.md) identity; Section 4 treats baselines. The construction combines random-choice [probabilities](probability.md) and differentiable deterministic paths in a [stochastic computation graph](stochastic-computation-graph.md).
