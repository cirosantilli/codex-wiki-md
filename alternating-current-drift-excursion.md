# Alternating-current drift excursion

↑ **Parent:** [Drift velocity](drift-velocity.md)

For sinusoidal [current density](current-density.md) $J(t)=J_{\rm pk}\cos(\omega t)$ and constant [carrier density](carrier-density.md) $n$, the [electron](electron.md) [drift velocity](drift-velocity.md) is $v_d(t)=-J_{\rm pk}\cos(\omega t)/(ne)$. Its displacement has amplitude $J_{\rm pk}/(ne\omega)$ and zero net change over a full cycle. The distance accumulated by this drift alone in one cycle is $4v_{\rm pk}/\omega=2v_{\rm pk}/(\pi f)$. With an RMS current, $v_{\rm pk}=\sqrt2v_{\rm rms}$. This does not measure the much larger microscopic path due to [Fermi velocity](fermi-velocity.md) and [electron-phonon scattering](electron-phonon-scattering.md).
