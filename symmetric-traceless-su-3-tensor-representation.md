<h1 id="symmetric-traceless-su-3-tensor-representation">Symmetric traceless SU(3) tensor representation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed SU(3) tensor representation](mixed-su-3-tensor-representation.md)

Symmetrize the $p$ upper and $q$ lower indices separately, and remove every upper-lower [trace](matrix-trace.md). The resulting representation has [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $p\omega_1+q\omega_2$. Its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is

$$
\binom{p+2}{2}\binom{q+2}{2}-\binom{p+1}{2}\binom{q+1}{2}
=\frac{(p+1)(q+1)(p+q+2)}2.
$$

The subtraction is valid because contraction is onto: in the polynomial realization its adjoint, for the monomial-factorial [inner product](inner-product.md), is multiplication by the nonzero polynomial $\sum_ix_iy_i$, an injective map. The resulting kernel contains the highest-weight constituent and has precisely its [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md), hence is irreducible. This highest-weight constituent is not always the largest-dimensional constituent of the full mixed [tensor](tensor.md).
