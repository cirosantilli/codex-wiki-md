# Feedback alignment

↑ **Parent:** [Backpropagation](backpropagation.md)

A learning scheme replacing a transposed forward-weight matrix in the teaching signal with a separate fixed random matrix. Simulations show that adaptation of the forward weights can make this feedback useful on tested tasks. It need not equal the exact loss [gradient](gradient.md), and successful simulations alone do not establish the mechanism in the brain.[Results, Figure 1 and Figure 2; Discussion: random feedback, learning performance and biological limitations](random-synaptic-feedback-weights-support-error-backpropagation-for-deep-learning-by-timothy-p-lillicrap-and-others.md)
