# Unilateral contact complementarity

↑ **Parent:** [Contact mechanics](contact-mechanics.md)

For a nonadhesive obstacle, $g$ is the signed separation and $r$ its outward [normal contact force](normal-contact-force.md). Nonpenetration gives $g\geq0$, absence of adhesion gives $r\geq0$, and reaction is possible only at contact, giving $gr=0$. In a small-slope taut-string model, $g=w-f$ and force balance gives $r=p-Tw''$ for downward applied load $p$. The three conditions then become $(w-f)(Tw''-p)=0$, $w\geq f$, $Tw''-p\leq0$. Multiplication by admissible displacement differences and integration gives the associated [variational inequality](variational-inequality.md), connecting a local contact law to constrained energy minimization.
