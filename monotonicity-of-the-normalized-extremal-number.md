# Monotonicity of the normalized extremal number

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal number](extremal-number.md)

For every fixed graph $H$, the sequence

$$
\frac{\operatorname{ex}(n,H)}{\binom n2}
$$

is nonincreasing once $n\geq2$. Indeed, choose a uniformly random $m$-vertex [induced subgraph](induced-subgraph.md) of an extremal $H$-free graph on $n$ vertices. It remains $H$-free and has expected edge count

$$
\operatorname{ex}(n,H)\frac{\binom m2}{\binom n2},
$$

which cannot exceed $\operatorname{ex}(m,H)$.
