# Asymmetric absolute-error loss

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute-error loss](absolute-error-loss.md)

This loss assigns different positive costs to underestimation and overestimation of a scalar parameter. Here $\gamma$ penalizes estimates below the parameter and $\delta$ estimates above it. Equal costs give a multiple of [absolute-error loss](absolute-error-loss.md).
