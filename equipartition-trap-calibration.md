# Equipartition trap calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Optical tweezers](optical-tweezers.md)

A calibrated coordinate in a harmonic trap has equilibrium [variance](variance-split.md) $\langle\delta x^2\rangle=k_BT/k$. This determines stiffness only after removing detector noise and accounting for drift, exposure averaging and finite bandwidth. Position [variance](variance-split.md) does not supply a [force](force-split.md) calibration if the coordinate scale is unknown.
