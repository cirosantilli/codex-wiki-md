# Conjugate transpose

↑ **Parent:** [Operator theory](linear-operator-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_transpose)

The adjoint of a complex matrix is its conjugate transpose,

$$
A^\dagger=\overline A^T.
$$
