# Homogeneous free scalar as a stiff fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Stiff fluid](stiff-fluid.md)

For a spatially homogeneous [minimally coupled scalar field](minimally-coupled-scalar-field.md) with zero [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md), the [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) has $\rho=P=\dot\phi^2/2$. Its field equation is $\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi=0$, so $a^3\dot\phi$ is constant and its [energy density](energy-density.md) decreases as $a^{-6}$. Thus its homogeneous stress is precisely that of a [stiff fluid](stiff-fluid.md). Spatial gradients would introduce directional stresses, so this equivalence requires homogeneity.
