# Near and far field

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near_and_far_field)

The [near and far field](near-and-far-field.md) distinguish source-dependent electromagnetic field regions. Close to a localized source, reactive fields and induction terms contribute; sufficiently far away, the leading transverse fields decrease as the inverse distance and carry outgoing power. The [radiation zone](radiation-zone.md) describes this far-field limit.
