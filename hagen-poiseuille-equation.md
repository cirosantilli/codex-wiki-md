# Hagen-Poiseuille equation

↑ **Parent:** [Pipe flow](pipe-flow.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagen–Poiseuille_equation)

Steady pressure-driven flow through a circular pipe has the parabolic profile

$$
w(r)=-\frac1{4\mu}\frac{dp}{dz}(R^2-r^2).
$$
