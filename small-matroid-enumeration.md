# Small matroid enumeration

↑ **Parent:** [Matroid](matroid.md)

On labelled [ground sets](ground-set.md) of sizes $1,2,3$, there are respectively $2,5,16$ [matroids](matroid.md), or $2,4,8$ up to [matroid isomorphism](matroid-isomorphism.md). At [rank of a matroid](rank-of-a-matroid.md) one, a nonempty [subset](subset.md) of [nonloops](nonloop-of-a-matroid.md) specifies all [matroid independent sets](independent-set-of-a-matroid.md) of [cardinality](cardinality.md) one. On three labels at [rank of a matroid](rank-of-a-matroid.md) two, the three types are one [matroid loop](loop-of-a-matroid.md) with an independent pair, no [matroid loops](loop-of-a-matroid.md) with one pair of [parallel elements of a matroid](parallel-elements-of-a-matroid.md), and all pairs independent with the triple dependent.
