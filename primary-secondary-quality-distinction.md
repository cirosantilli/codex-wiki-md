# Primary-secondary quality distinction

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of science](philosophy-of-science.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary–secondary_quality_distinction)

In an account of perception, distinguish an object's physical constitution from its powers to produce experiences in perceivers. The distinction between [primary qualities](primary-quality.md) and [secondary qualities](secondary-quality.md) does not by itself prove an external world or imply that dispositions are unreal.[book II, chapter VIII, sections 8–15 and 23–26](an-essay-concerning-human-understanding-by-john-locke.md)
