<h1 id="internal-form-of-lob-s-theorem">Internal form of Löb's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Löb's theorem](lob-s-theorem.md)

In the [Löb's theorem](lob-s-theorem.md) proof, $T\vdash\Box D\to\Box A$ is obtained before any reflection assumption. Put $H=(\Box A\to A)$. Propositional reasoning and the diagonal equation give $T\vdash H\to D$. Necessitation and distribution yield $T\vdash\Box H\to\Box D$, hence the displayed implication. This is the arithmetic interpretation of the Löb axiom in [Gödel-Löb logic](godel-lob-logic.md), and avoids necessitating a temporary assumption.
