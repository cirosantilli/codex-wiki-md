# Pfaffian

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pfaffian)

For an antisymmetric $2n\times2n$ matrix, the Pfaffian is $\operatorname{Pf}(A)=(2^nn!)^{-1}\sum_{\sigma\in S_{2n}}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\prod_{j=1}^n A_{\sigma(2j-1),\sigma(2j)}$. Equivalently, it is the coefficient of the top exterior power of the associated alternating form, with the usual factorial normalization. Its square is the [determinant](determinant.md). In size four it is $a_{12}a_{34}-a_{13}a_{24}+a_{14}a_{23}$, so its vanishing characterizes the simple nonzero [bivectors](bivector.md) of the [Klein quadric](klein-quadric.md).
