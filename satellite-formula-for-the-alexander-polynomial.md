# Satellite formula for the Alexander polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Satellite knot](satellite-knot.md)

If a satellite knot has pattern $P$, companion $K$, and winding number $w$, then

$$
\Delta_{P(K)}(t)\doteq\Delta_{P(U)}(t)\Delta_K(t^w).
$$
