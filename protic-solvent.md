# Protic solvent

↑ **Parent:** [Solvent](solvent.md)

A solvent containing an appropriate proton-donating group, often an O–H or N–H group. It can [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) bond, shuttle [protons](proton.md) and quench strongly basic organometallic reagents.
