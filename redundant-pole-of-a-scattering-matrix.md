# Redundant pole of a scattering matrix

↑ **Parent:** [S-matrix](s-matrix.md)

A pole of a [scattering matrix](s-matrix.md) arising from the numerator $F(-k)$ rather than a zero of the incoming [Jost function](jost-function.md) $F(k)$ can fail to correspond to a [bound state](bound-state.md). For $F(k)=(k-i\kappa)/(k+i\alpha)$, the pole at $i\kappa$ is associated with the incoming zero; the pole at $i\alpha$ comes from the numerator. The regular Eckart realization has $\alpha>\kappa$. The distinction and this explicit example are discussed in [https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/373712/4/2306.12216.pdf.](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/373712/4/2306.12216.pdf.)
