# Reparametrization

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth curve](smooth-curve.md)

A reparametrization composes a parametrized curve with a smooth change of parameter. An orientation-preserving reparametrization has positive derivative.

The operation changes the [parametric equation](parametric-equation.md) while preserving the image of the curve.
