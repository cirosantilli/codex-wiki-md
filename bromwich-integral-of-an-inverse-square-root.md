# Bromwich integral of an inverse square root

↑ **Parent:** [Bromwich inversion with a square-root branch cut](bromwich-inversion-with-a-square-root-branch-cut.md)

For $\sigma,t>0$, take the [principal square root](principal-square-root-of-a-complex-number.md) and orient the [Bromwich contour](bromwich-contour.md) upward. Closing left around the negative-real [branch cut](branch-cut.md) gives the jump $2i\int_0^\infty e^{-tx}x^{-1/2}dx$. The substitution $u=tx$ and $\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt\pi$ prove the displayed formula. A rectangular closure has horizontal remainders $O(R^{-1/2})$, an exponentially small left edge, and an $O(\sqrt\varepsilon)$ small-circle term. These estimates also justify the conditionally convergent vertical [integral](integral-split.md).
