# Curvature of a tensor product connection

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product connection](tensor-product-connection.md)

The curvature of a [tensor product connection](tensor-product-connection.md) is

$$
F_{E\otimes F}=F_E\otimes I+I\otimes F_F.
$$

For [line bundles](line-bundle.md), this is simply $F_{E\otimes F}=F_E+F_F$.
