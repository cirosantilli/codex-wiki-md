# Superconducting quench

↑ **Parent:** [Superconducting magnet](superconducting-magnet.md)

A transition of part of a current-carrying superconducting system into a resistive state. Joule heating can spread the transition, and the stored magnetic [energy](energy-split.md) must be handled safely by the system's protection design. Zero steady winding resistance does not remove the finite magnetic [energy](energy-split.md) or the refrigeration requirement.
