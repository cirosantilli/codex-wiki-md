# Photofragment-ion imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Photodissociation](photodissociation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photofragment-ion_imaging)

[Photofragment-ion imaging](photofragment-ion-imaging.md) detects fragment [velocities](velocity.md) by ionizing neutral products and mapping their motion onto a position-sensitive detector using an [electric field](electric-field.md). In a velocity-mapping implementation, the image radius measures transverse speed under fixed lens settings. Selecting an arrival-time slice helps extract the central plane of the three-dimensional distribution. Species identification and electronic [quantum state](quantum-state.md) selection are essential for turning a radius into a particular dissociation threshold.
