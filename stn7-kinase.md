# STN7 kinase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[STN7 kinase](stn7-kinase.md) is required for plant LHCII phosphorylation associated with state transitions. Mutant and complementation experiments can separate its regulatory role from general loss of photosynthetic competence.
