# Diathermal wall

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal contact](thermal-contact.md)

A diathermal wall permits [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md). For a clean [zeroth law of thermodynamics](zeroth-law-of-thermodynamics.md) comparison, idealize it as rigid and impermeable, excluding boundary [work](work.md) and matter transfer. A thin metal partition approximates this arrangement.[§4.1, printed p. 109: rigid, particle-impermeable diathermal walls and the thin-metal example](statistical-physics-by-david-tong/4-classical-thermodynamics.md)
