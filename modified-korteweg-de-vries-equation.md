# Modified Korteweg-De Vries equation

↑ **Parent:** [Korteweg-De Vries equation](korteweg-de-vries-equation.md)

This nonlinear dispersive equation replaces the quadratic KdV nonlinearity by a cubic one. In the displayed sign convention, the [Miura transformation](miura-transformation.md) maps its solutions to $u_t-6uu_x+u_{xxx}=0$. Its scaling symmetry is $(x,t,v)\mapsto(e^s x,e^{3s}t,e^{-s}v)$.
