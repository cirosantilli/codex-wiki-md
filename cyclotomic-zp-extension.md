# Cyclotomic Zp-extension

↑ **Parent:** [Zp-extension](zp-extension.md)

For odd $p$, remove the finite torsion subgroup $\mu_{p-1}$ from the [Galois group](galois-group.md) of $\mathbb Q(\mu_{p^\infty})/\mathbb Q$ by taking its fixed field. For $p=2$, take the fixed field of $\{\pm1\}$ instead. The remaining [Galois group](galois-group.md) is $\mathbb Z_p$, using the [p-adic logarithm](p-adic-logarithm.md) on $1+p\mathbb Z_p$ or $1+4\mathbb Z_2$. For a [number field](number-field.md) $K$, the cyclotomic Zp-extension is the compositum with this rational tower.
