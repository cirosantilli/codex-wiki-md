# Logarithmic two-dimensional saddle joint density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Van Hove singularity](van-hove-singularity.md)

A single two-dimensional quadratic saddle with $A=\hbar^2/(2m^*)$ contributes this leading [joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md) when both contour branches are included and $g_s$ counts independent spin channels. The contour integral is proportional to $\int_{-\Lambda}^{\Lambda}dy/\sqrt{y^2+\delta/A}=2\operatorname{arsinh}(\Lambda\sqrt{A/\delta})$ above the saddle. Its asymptote gives the logarithm with $E_\Lambda$ of order $A\Lambda^2$. The cutoff-dependent constant is not universal. This is a density per area per energy; changing to a density per frequency requires a Jacobian.
