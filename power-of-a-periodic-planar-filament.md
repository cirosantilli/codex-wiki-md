# Power of a periodic planar filament

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive-force theory](resistive-force-theory.md)

The leading [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) due to a small planar filament stroke is $\dot W=\xi_\perp T^{-1}\int_0^T\int_0^\lambda y_t^2\,dx\,dt$. It follows from the work against the local [resistive-force theory](resistive-force-theory.md) force. The transverse velocity controls the leading power; the quadratic longitudinal velocity contributes only at higher order.
