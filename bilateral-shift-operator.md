# Bilateral shift operator

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate point spectrum](approximate-point-spectrum.md)

The bilateral shift $(Tx)_n=x_{n+1}$ is unitary. It has empty [point spectrum](point-spectrum.md), while its [approximate point spectrum](approximate-point-spectrum.md) is the unit circle. For $|\lambda|=1$, normalized vectors with entries $\lambda^n$ on an interval of length $N$ fail to satisfy $Tx=\lambda x$ only at the two endpoints, giving residual norm $\sqrt{2/N}$.

It is the two-sided sequence case of a [shift operator](shift-operator.md).
