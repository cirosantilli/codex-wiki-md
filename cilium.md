# Cilium

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cilium)

A cellular projection whose structure and functions differ from the bacterial flagellar apparatus. Motile cilia can move fluid; a transport estimate must compare advection with [diffusion](diffusion.md).
