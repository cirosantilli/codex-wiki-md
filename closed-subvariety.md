# Closed subvariety

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md)

A closed subvariety is an integral closed subscheme of an [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md). It supplies the lower-dimensional loci on which positivity is tested by the [Nakai–Moishezon criterion](nakai-moishezon-criterion.md).
