# Planck feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Climate feedback](climate-feedback.md)

The Planck feedback is the stabilizing increase in outgoing thermal radiation when temperatures rise, with other properties held fixed. For a [blackbody](blackbody.md) the local derivative is $4\sigma T^3$, so a colder emitter has a smaller absolute radiation increase per kelvin. The atmospheric response requires [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md), and cannot be obtained by replacing the entire climate system with a single surface blackbody.
