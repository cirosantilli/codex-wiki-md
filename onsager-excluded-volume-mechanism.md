# Onsager excluded-volume mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md)

For slender hard rods of length $L$ and diameter $D\ll L$, the leading pair [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md) is the displayed function of their mutual angle $\gamma$. Alignment lowers mutual obstruction and increases positional [entropy](entropy-split.md), while narrowing the orientation distribution lowers orientational [entropy](entropy-split.md). Their competition can drive an isotropic-to-nematic transition without attractive forces. The natural concentration scale is $cL^2D$ of order one. Finite rod diameter, flexibility and interactions beyond the hard-core second-virial approximation change quantitative predictions.
