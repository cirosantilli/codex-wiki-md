# Bandwidth limitation in fixed-power filament optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling-wave stationarity of planar filament propulsion](travelling-wave-stationarity-of-planar-filament-propulsion.md)

At fixed leading [power of a periodic planar filament](power-of-a-periodic-planar-filament.md), the family $a\sin(mkx+\Omega t)$ has [propulsive force of a periodic planar filament](propulsive-force-of-a-periodic-planar-filament.md) increasing with spatial harmonic $m$. The formal quadratic objective is unbounded without a spatial cutoff; large harmonics eventually invalidate the small-slope model. With a finite admissible [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) band, force divided by power is a weighted average of the signed ratios $-(\xi_\perp-\xi_\parallel)k_j/(\xi_\perp\Omega_j)$. An allowed extremal ratio is attained by a [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) mode, or by a superposition with one common phase speed.
