# Ray unit

↑ **Parent:** [Ray class group](ray-class-group.md)

A ray unit for a [modulus of a number field](modulus-of-a-number-field.md) is a unit congruent to one modulo every finite prime power in the modulus and positive at every real place in it. The group of [ray units](ray-unit.md) is the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of the [unit group](unit-group.md) map to the [residue and signature group of a modulus](residue-and-signature-group-of-a-modulus.md).
