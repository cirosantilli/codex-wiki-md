# DNA-RNA hybrid

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleic acid](nucleic-acid.md)

A [DNA-RNA hybrid](dna-rna-hybrid.md) pairs a DNA strand with an RNA strand. Its backbone constraints commonly favour A-like duplex geometry, which can alter accessibility relative to [B-DNA](b-dna.md).
