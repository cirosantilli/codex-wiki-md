# Quantum effective potential

↑ **Parent:** [Effective action](effective-action.md)

The quantum effective potential is the derivative-free part of the [quantum effective action](effective-action.md) evaluated on a spatially constant background. The displayed sign uses $\Gamma=-W+\int J\varphi$ for $Z=e^{iW}$ and the source term $+J\phi$; the opposite effective-action convention gives $-\int V_{\rm eff}$. Its derivatives give zero-momentum [one-particle-irreducible vertices](one-particle-irreducible-vertex.md). The value depends on a [renormalization scheme](renormalization-scheme.md) and scale; an arbitrary additive vacuum-energy constant may be fixed separately.
