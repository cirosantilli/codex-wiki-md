# Theta method

↑ **Parent:** [Runge-Kutta method](runge-kutta-method.md)

This one-step family interpolates between the [Forward Euler method](euler-method.md) at $a=0$, the [trapezoidal rule](trapezoidal-rule.md) at $a=1/2$, and the [Backward Euler method](backward-euler-method.md) at $a=1$. Its [stability function](stability-function.md) is $R(z)=[1+(1-a)z]/[1-az]$. It is first order except at one half, where it is second order. Applying it to spatial diffusion gives the [theta diffusion stability criterion](theta-diffusion-stability-criterion.md).
