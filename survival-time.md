# Survival time

↑ **Parent:** [Survival analysis](survival-analysis-split.md)

A survival time is the elapsed time to an event of interest, which may be death, failure, recovery or completion of a task. Its [survival function](survival-function.md) and [hazard function](hazard-function.md) describe the event-time law. [Right censoring](right-censoring.md) records only that the event time exceeds the observed follow-up time.
