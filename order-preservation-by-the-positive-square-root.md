# Order preservation by the positive square root

↑ **Parent:** [Square-root resolvent integral in a C-star algebra](square-root-resolvent-integral-in-a-c-star-algebra.md)

For [Positive elements of a C-star algebra](positive-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) $x\leq y$, [inversion reverses the order of strictly positive elements](inversion-reverses-the-order-of-strictly-positive-elements.md), so $(t1+x)^{-1}\geq(t1+y)^{-1}$ for $t>0$. Subtracting their [square-root resolvent integral in a C-star algebra](square-root-resolvent-integral-in-a-c-star-algebra.md) formulas gives

$$
y^{1/2}-x^{1/2}=\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty t^{1/2}\bigl((t1+x)^{-1}-(t1+y)^{-1}\bigr)\,dt\geq0.
$$

The integrand is positive, and the positive cone is closed, so the norm limit of these integrals is positive. No commutativity of $x$ and $y$ is required. In particular, $b^2\geq a^2$ for positive $a,b$ implies $b\geq a$, even though squaring itself is not generally order preserving.
