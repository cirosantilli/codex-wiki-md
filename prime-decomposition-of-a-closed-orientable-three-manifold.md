# Prime decomposition of a closed orientable three-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Prime three-manifold](prime-three-manifold.md)

Every closed connected orientable [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) is a finite [connected sum](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) of [prime three-manifolds](prime-three-manifold.md), unique up to order and the insertion of three-sphere factors. The corresponding [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is the free product of the summand groups. A prime orientable three-manifold is either irreducible or $S^2\times S^1$; this separates aspherical infinite-group cases from the infinite cyclic case.
