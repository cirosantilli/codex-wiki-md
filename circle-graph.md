# Circle graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

The [intersection graph](intersection-graph.md) of chords of a circle: each chord is a vertex, and two vertices are adjacent when their chords cross. Two chords with distinct endpoints cross exactly when their endpoints alternate around the circle.
