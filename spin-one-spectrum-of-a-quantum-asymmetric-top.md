# Spin-one spectrum of a quantum asymmetric top

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum asymmetric top](quantum-asymmetric-top.md)

Within the $l=1$ multiplet, $|1,0\rangle$ has [energy](energy-split.md) $\hbar^2(a_x+a_y)$. The symmetric and antisymmetric combinations of $|1,1\rangle$ and $|1,-1\rangle$ have the other two pairwise coefficient sums. Principal-axis permutation relates these energies; equality requires corresponding coefficient degeneracies rather than angular-momentum conservation alone.
