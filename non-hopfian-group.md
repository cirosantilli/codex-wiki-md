# Non-Hopfian group

↑ **Parent:** [Hopfian group](hopfian-group.md)

A non-Hopfian group admits a surjective [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) which is not injective. The [Baumslag-Solitar group](baumslag-solitar-group.md) $BS(2,3)$ has such an endomorphism $a\mapsto a^2$, $t\mapsto t$: its image contains $a^2$ and $a^3$, while the nonidentity [commutator](commutator.md) $[a,tat^{-1}]$ lies in its kernel by [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md).
