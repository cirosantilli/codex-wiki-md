# Quadrature weight

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md)

A [quadrature weight](quadrature-weight.md) multiplies a sampled [integrand](integrand.md) value in a [quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md). For ordinary [numerical integration](numerical-integration-split.md), its units match those of the integration variable. Positive [quadrature weights](quadrature-weight.md) that sum to $b-a$ make $Q(f)/(b-a)$ a weighted average of the samples; signed [quadrature weights](quadrature-weight.md) can magnify sample perturbations, as quantified by [noise sensitivity of a quadrature rule](noise-sensitivity-of-a-quadrature-rule.md).
