# Three-cumulant shifted gamma approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md)

Approximate a [compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md) of positive claims by a [shifted gamma distribution](shifted-gamma-distribution.md) whose first three [cumulants](cumulant.md) agree. Here $m_j=\mathbb E X^j$ are raw severity [moments](moment.md), while the compound [cumulants](cumulant.md) are $\lambda m_j$. Solve $k+\alpha/\nu=\lambda m_1$, $\alpha/\nu^2=\lambda m_2$, and $2\alpha/\nu^3=\lambda m_3$ to obtain the displayed parameters. The approximation captures positive [skewness](skewness.md), but not the atom $e^{-\lambda}$ at zero. Its shift can be negative; with exponential claims of mean $\mu$ it is $-\lambda\mu/3$. Matching three [cumulants](cumulant.md) alone supplies no general tail-error bound.
