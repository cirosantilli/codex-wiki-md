# Fractional support graph of a matching relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Matching (graph theory)](matching-graph-theory-split.md)

For the degree-constrained [linear programming relaxation](linear-programming-relaxation.md) of a [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory-split.md), retain precisely the [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) with fractional coordinates. In a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md), an even [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) in this [subgraph](subgraph.md) permits alternating positive and negative perturbations preserving all vertex sums. If the [subgraph](subgraph.md) is a [forest](forest.md), use a [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) between two [tree leaves](leaf-of-a-tree.md): its endpoints have positive slack and its internal sums remain unchanged. Either perturbation expresses the original [feasible point](feasible-point.md) as the midpoint of two distinct [feasible points](feasible-point.md). Hence a fractional [feasible point](feasible-point.md) cannot be an [extreme point](extreme-point.md), proving integrality of all [extreme points](extreme-point.md) of the bipartite degree relaxation.
