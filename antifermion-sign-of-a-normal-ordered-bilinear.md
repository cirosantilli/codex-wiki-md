# Antifermion sign of a normal-ordered bilinear

↑ **Parent:** [Fermionic sign](fermionic-sign.md)

In a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) mode expansion, the antiparticle contribution to $\bar\psi\Gamma\psi$ first appears with operator order $dd^\dagger$. [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) changes it to $-d^\dagger d$ using the fermionic anticommutation relations. Thus its scattering coefficient is $-\bar v_{\rm in}\Gamma v_{\rm out}$. Pair-annihilation and creation terms have the operator orders $db$ and $b^\dagger d^\dagger$. Keeping these orders and a fixed external-state convention determines interference signs, especially between exchange and annihilation graphs in a [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md).
