# Boundary closure of a difference scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary stability of a finite-difference method](boundary-stability-of-a-finite-difference-method.md)

A boundary closure supplies the boundary rows or ghost values needed by a spatial [finite difference method](finite-difference-method.md). It must reflect the PDE boundary data and preserve a mesh-uniform stability estimate. Interior [von Neumann stability analysis](von-neumann-stability-analysis.md) cannot detect an arbitrary unstable boundary row; for example a row $U_1^{n+1}=2U_1^n$ produces growth even beside an otherwise stable heat stencil.
