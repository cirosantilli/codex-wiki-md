# Ion-channel inactivation

↑ **Parent:** [Voltage-gated ion channel](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md)

[Ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md) is loss of availability for conduction, distinct from simply closing an otherwise activatable [ion channel](ion-channel.md). Recovery depends on channel type and [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md); incomplete recovery of [sodium](sodium.md)-selective [voltage-gated ion channels](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md) contributes to a [neuronal refractory period](neuronal-refractory-period.md).
