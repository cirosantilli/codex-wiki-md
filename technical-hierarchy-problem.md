# Technical hierarchy problem

↑ **Parent:** [Hierarchy problem](hierarchy-problem.md)

The technical [hierarchy problem](hierarchy-problem.md) asks why a small mass scale, once chosen, remains stable under quantum corrections from much larger scales. A symmetry can protect this radiative stability without explaining the numerical origin of the scale separation. For a fundamental [scalar mass](scalar-mass.md), heavy thresholds often give additive corrections proportional to the heavy mass squared; [supersymmetric cancellation of quadratic divergences](supersymmetric-cancellation-of-quadratic-divergences.md) supplies one protection mechanism when supersymmetry breaking is sufficiently soft.
