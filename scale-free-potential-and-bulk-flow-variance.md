# Scale-free potential and bulk-flow variance

↑ **Parent:** [Scale-free matter power spectrum](scale-free-matter-power-spectrum.md)

For a [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md) $P(k)=Ak^n$ at a fixed epoch, the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) gives potential Fourier amplitudes proportional to $\delta_k/k^2$, and the growing-mode [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md) is proportional to $\delta_k\mathbf k/k^2$. With a [spherical top-hat window function](spherical-top-hat-window-function.md), their variances are proportional to $\int k^{n-2}W(kR)^2dk$ and $\int k^nW(kR)^2dk$, respectively. Rescaling $kR$ gives the displayed powers when the integrals converge: $1<n<5$ for the averaged potential and $-1<n<3$ for the bulk velocity. Absolute averaged potential has infrared sensitivity; band-limited fluctuations or potential differences can have different convergence requirements. The density variance converges only for $-3<n<1$, so these three absolute averages cannot all converge for one unbroken power law at every scale.
