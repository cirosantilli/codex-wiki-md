# Spectral dbar equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dbar equation](dbar-equation.md)

A [dbar equation](dbar-equation.md) in a complex [spectral parameter](spectral-parameter.md), rather than the original spatial coordinate. In spectral reconstruction, its right-hand side is transformed data. A [Cauchy-Pompeiu formula](cauchy-pompeiu-formula.md) in the spectral plane reconstructs the auxiliary function; an asymptotic coefficient or the original differential equation then recovers the spatial field.
