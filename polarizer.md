# Polarizer

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polarizer)

A linear [polarizer](polarizer.md) transmits one [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) and suppresses the orthogonal one. Two ideal crossed [polarizers](polarizer.md) block light unless an intervening medium changes its [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md). A [twisted nematic field effect](twisted-nematic-field-effect.md) uses this selection to convert field-driven reorientation into brightness modulation.
