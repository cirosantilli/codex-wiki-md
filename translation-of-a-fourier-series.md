# Translation of a Fourier series

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

For a period-$L$ [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) written as $a_0/2+\sum_{n\geq1}(a_n\cos k_nx+b_n\sin k_nx)$, where $k_n=2\pi n/L$, translating the [function](function-split.md) by $\ell$ to $g(x)=f(x-\ell)$ rotates each pair of [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md):

$$
a_n^{(g)}=a_n\cos(k_n\ell)-b_n\sin(k_n\ell),\qquad
b_n^{(g)}=a_n\sin(k_n\ell)+b_n\cos(k_n\ell).
$$

The constant coefficient is unchanged. These formulas follow by the sine and cosine subtraction identities. In the [complex Fourier series](complex-fourier-series.md), the same statement is $\widehat g_n=e^{-ik_n\ell}\widehat f_n$. An integer-period translation changes no [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md).
