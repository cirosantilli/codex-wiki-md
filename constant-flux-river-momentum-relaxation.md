# Constant-flux river momentum relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Saint-Venant equation](saint-venant-equation.md)

This scalar [quasilinear partial differential equation](quasilinear-partial-differential-equation.md) results by imposing $h=Q/u$ in a frictional river momentum equation and measuring $X$ upstream. It retains a relaxation toward $u_0=Q^{1/3}$ and carries information at speed $c(u)=Q/(F^2u^2)-u$. Near equilibrium the relaxation time is $\varepsilon F^2u_0/3$. A slowly moving downstream boundary can generate an attached [boundary layer](boundary-layer.md) when $c(u)$ exceeds its speed throughout the relaxation. The model retains momentum dynamics only: exactly constant flux in both original [Saint-Venant equations](saint-venant-equation.md) would instead force time-independent depth.
