# Principal Cartier divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Linear equivalence of Cartier divisors](linear-equivalence-of-cartier-divisors.md)

A principal Cartier divisor is represented on every chart by the same nonzero [rational function](rational-function.md). It is linearly equivalent to zero and has zero [intersection number](intersection-number-of-a-cartier-divisor-with-a-curve.md) with every complete curve.
