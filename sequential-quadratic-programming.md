# Sequential quadratic programming

↑ **Parent:** [Constrained optimization](constrained-optimization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequential_quadratic_programming)

Sequential quadratic programming computes steps from a quadratic model of the [optimization Lagrangian](optimization-lagrangian.md) with linearized constraints. Its constrained [Newton system](newton-system.md) couples a primal step and multiplier estimates. A [line search](line-search.md) or [trust region](trust-region.md) then controls the use of that local model.
