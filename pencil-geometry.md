# Pencil (geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Geometry and topology](geometry-and-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pencil_(geometry))

A [pencil](pencil-geometry.md) is a one-parameter linear family of geometric objects. For linearly independent homogeneous [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) $F,G$ of equal degree, $sF+tG=0$, indexed by $[s:t]\in\mathbb P^1$, is a [pencil](pencil-geometry.md) of [plane curves](plane-curve.md) or [hypersurfaces](hypersurface.md). Lines through a fixed point give a simple example; a [pencil of plane curves](pencil-of-plane-curves.md) specializes the general construction.
