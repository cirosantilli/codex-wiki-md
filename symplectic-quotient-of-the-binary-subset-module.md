# Symplectic quotient of the binary subset module

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic group over a finite field](symplectic-group-over-a-finite-field.md)

For even n, the even-weight subspace of $F_2^n$ has radical $\langle\mathbf1\rangle$ for the dot product. Its quotient is a nondegenerate [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md) space of dimension n−2. The [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) acts by coordinate permutations. At n=6 this action is faithful and gives $S_6\cong Sp_4(2)$.
