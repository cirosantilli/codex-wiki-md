# Left-invariant vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Left and right translation on a Lie group](left-and-right-translation-on-a-lie-group.md)

For $\xi$ in the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of $G$, the left-invariant vector field is $l_\xi(g)=(dL_g)_e\xi$. Its global [flow](local-flow.md) is right translation $\Phi^t=R_{\exp(t\xi)}$.
