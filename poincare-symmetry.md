<h1 id="poincare-symmetry">Poincaré symmetry</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré group](poincare-group.md)

Invariance under [spacetime](spacetime.md) translations and [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md). A scalar action with constant coefficients and contracted Lorentz indices has this symmetry when its domain and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) also permit the transformations.
