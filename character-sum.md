# Character sum

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic number theory](analytic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character_sum)

A sum of character values over a set. Arithmetic cancellation, rather than the termwise absolute-value bound, is the useful feature. A [Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character](gauss-sum-of-a-dirichlet-character.md) is a weighted example, and the [Pólya–Vinogradov inequality](polya-vinogradov-inequality.md) bounds interval sums.
