# Large extra dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Compactification (physics)](compactification-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_extra_dimensions)

Large-extra-dimension models lower the fundamental higher-dimensional gravitational scale $M_*$ while obtaining a large four-dimensional [reduced Planck mass](reduced-planck-mass.md) from compact volume $V_n$. Ordinary matter can be localized on a [brane](brane.md) while gravity propagates in the bulk. The [Planck mass from compactification volume](planck-mass-from-compactification-volume.md) relation explains weak effective four-dimensional gravity but leaves volume stabilization and the origin of a large internal volume as separate problems.
