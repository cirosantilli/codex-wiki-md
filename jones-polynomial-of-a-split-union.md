# Jones polynomial of a split union

↑ **Parent:** [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md)

Placing the two [link diagrams](link-diagram.md) in disjoint disks keeps all state [circles](circle.md) separate. The reduced [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md) therefore acquires one extra loop factor $-A^2-A^{-2}$. Correcting [writhe of a link diagram](writhe-of-a-link-diagram.md) and setting $t=A^{-4}$ gives the displayed formula. It concerns a [split link](split-link.md), not an arbitrary union of linked components.
