<h1 id="de-caen-bound-for-hypergraph-turan-density">de Caen bound for hypergraph Turán density</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Turán density](turan-density.md)

For the complete $\ell$-uniform hypergraph $K_r^\ell$, with $r\ge\ell\ge2$, the bound is $\pi(K_r^\ell)\le1-1/\binom{r-1}{\ell-1}$. In the complementary covering formulation, every $r$-set containing an edge forces asymptotic edge density at least $1/\binom{r-1}{\ell-1}$. It follows from clique-extension incidence inequalities and [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) estimates on link degrees.
