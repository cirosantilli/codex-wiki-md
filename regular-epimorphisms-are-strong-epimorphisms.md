# Regular epimorphisms are strong epimorphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Regular epimorphism](regular-epimorphism.md)

The coequalizer universal property supplies the diagonal filler against any [monomorphism](monomorphism.md), so every [regular epimorphism](regular-epimorphism.md) is strong. In a [regular category](regular-category.md), every strong epimorphism is regular: factor it as a regular epimorphism followed by a monomorphism and use the lifting property to make the monomorphism an isomorphism.
