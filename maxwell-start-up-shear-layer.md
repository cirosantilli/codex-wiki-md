# Maxwell start-up shear layer

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Maxwell fluid](linear-maxwell-fluid.md)

For a half-space initially at rest and a wall given a step velocity $U$, [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) and the [Maxwell fluid](linear-maxwell-fluid.md) stress law give $\tau u_{tt}+u_t=(G_0\tau/\rho)u_{yy}$. The displayed decaying [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) solution obeys the wall condition. Inverting on a right-half-plane [Bromwich contour](bromwich-contour.md) yields a shear signal of speed $\sqrt{G_0/\rho}$. The limit $\tau\to0$ at fixed $G_0\tau=\eta$ gives viscous error-function penetration; $\tau\to\infty$ at fixed $G_0$ gives a sharp elastic front.
