# Two-mode Bose gas on a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Contact interaction (quantum mechanics)](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md)

Project a [bosonic](boson.md) field on a ring onto angular-momentum modes zero and one. The kinetic level spacing is $\mathcal E=\hbar^2/(2mR^2)$. The contact-interaction [integral](integral-split.md) enforces [angular momentum conservation](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md), leaving two same-mode terms and four mixed terms. The displayed projected [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) follows by normal ordering. Couplings to discarded modes belong to the full model and can limit the two-mode approximation.
