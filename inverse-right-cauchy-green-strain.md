# Inverse right Cauchy-Green strain

↑ **Parent:** [Finite strain theory](finite-strain-theory.md)

For $C=F^TF$, differentiation of $C^{-1}$ gives $\dot E^{(-2)}=F^{-1}DF^{-T}$. Its reference-power conjugate is $T^{(-2)}=F^T\tau F$. In a convected coordinate basis $g_I=Fe_I$, these are the covariant components of the [Kirchhoff stress tensor](kirchhoff-stress-tensor.md). The [second Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor](second-piola-kirchhoff-stress-tensor.md) instead gives its contravariant components, since $\tau=T^{(2)}_{IJ}g_I\otimes g_J$.
