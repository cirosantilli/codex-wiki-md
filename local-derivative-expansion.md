# Local derivative expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Landau-Ginzburg theory](landau-ginzburg-theory.md)

A [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) can be expanded in local powers of the [order parameter](order-parameter.md) and its spatial derivatives. Internal and spatial [symmetries](symmetry-physics.md) constrain the allowed terms; truncation assumes slowly varying fields.
