# Endoplasmic reticulum stress

↑ **Parent:** [Endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

[endoplasmic reticulum stress](endoplasmic-reticulum-stress.md) arises when ER demands exceed its processing or homeostatic capacity. It can alter folding, degradation and transport, confounding interpretation of a supposedly selective secretion defect.
