# Radiative relaxation time in a planetary atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmospheric dynamics](exoplanet-atmospheric-dynamics.md)

A column near [pressure](pressure.md) $p$ contains mass per area of order $p/g$. Linearizing its [blackbody](blackbody.md) cooling around [temperature](temperature.md) $T$ gives the estimate

$$
\tau_{\rm rad}\sim\frac{c_pp}{4g\sigma_{\rm SB}T^3},
$$

where $c_p$ is [specific heat capacity at constant pressure](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md). This estimate omits details of wavelength-dependent [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md), but captures faster relaxation in hotter and lower-pressure atmospheric layers.
