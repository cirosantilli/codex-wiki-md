# Coupling characterization of total variation distance

↑ **Parent:** [Total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md)

The total variation distance is the least possible mismatch probability over all couplings:

$$
\lVert\mu-\nu\rVert_{\mathrm{TV}}=\inf\mathbb P(X\ne Y).
$$
