# Trace-dual lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Trace pairing](trace-pairing.md)

For an $R$-lattice $M$ in $L$, its trace-dual lattice is

$$
M^\vee=\{x\in L:\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(xM)\subseteq R\}.
$$

For the integral closure $S$ of $R$, the trace dual $S^\vee$ is the [inverse different](inverse-different.md).
