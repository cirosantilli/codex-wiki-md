# Energy balance method

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

For a weak perturbation of a planar [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) system, integrate the first-order change of the unperturbed Hamiltonian around each unperturbed [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md). Zeros of this averaged energy drift select candidate perturbed periodic orbits, and a change from positive to negative drift indicates stability.
