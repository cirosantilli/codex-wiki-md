# Tangent space by point derivations

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent space](tangent-space.md)

A tangent vector is a [linear map](linear-map.md) on [germs](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) of smooth functions satisfying $D(ab)=a(x)D(b)+b(x)D(a)$. In a [manifold chart](manifold-chart.md), first-order expansion shows $Dh=\sum_iD(u^i)\partial_i h(x)$. Thus the coordinate [derivations](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) form a [basis](basis.md), and the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) equals the manifold [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). Composition of [germs](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) with a [smooth map](smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) defines its [differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) without choosing charts.
