# Conformal invariance of four-dimensional Yang-Mills action

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills action](yang-mills-action.md)

Under $g\mapsto\Omega^2g$ in dimension $d$, the [Hodge star](hodge-star-operator.md) on $p$-forms scales by $\Omega^{d-2p}$. For gauge [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md), $d=4,p=2$, so the star and the action $-g_{\rm YM}^{-2}\int\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge *F)$ are unchanged. Dilation therefore changes the [instanton size modulus](instanton-size-modulus.md) without changing the classical action. Quantum running of the coupling can break this classical scale invariance.
