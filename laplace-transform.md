# Laplace transform

↑ **Parent:** [Integral transform](integral-transform.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laplace_transform)

The Laplace transform is $\widehat f(p)=\int_0^\infty e^{-pt}f(t)dt$ and converts differentiation into multiplication by $p$ plus initial data.
