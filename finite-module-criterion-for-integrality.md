# Finite-module criterion for integrality

↑ **Parent:** [Integral element](integral-element.md)

An element $x$ of an $A$-algebra is an [integral element](integral-element.md) precisely when $A[x]$ is a finite $A$-[module](module-mathematics.md). More generally, a finite $A$-submodule containing one and stable under multiplication by $x$ proves integrality by the [determinant trick](determinant-trick.md). Conversely a [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) equation reduces all powers of $x$ to finitely many generators.
