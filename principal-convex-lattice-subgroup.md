# Principal convex lattice subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Convex lattice subgroup](convex-lattice-subgroup.md)

For $u\ge1$ in a [lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md), the displayed set is the smallest [convex lattice subgroup](convex-lattice-subgroup.md) containing $u$. Products of two such bounds are bounded by a larger power of $u$; inversion and the [lattice](lattice.md) operations preserve the bounds. This gives an explicit test for membership.
