<h1 id="gudden-s-ventral-tegmental-nucleus">Gudden's ventral tegmental nucleus</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Brain](brain.md)

A brainstem nucleus projecting to the [mammillary bodies](mammillary-body.md), named after [Bernhard von Gudden](bernhard-von-gudden.md). It is distinct from the commonly discussed dopaminergic ventral tegmental area.
