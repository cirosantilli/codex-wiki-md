# Almost holomorphic weight-two Eisenstein series

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein series of weight two](eisenstein-series-of-weight-two.md)

The [logarithmic derivative](logarithmic-derivative.md) of the [modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md) gives $E_2=\Delta'/(2\pi i\Delta)$. Differentiating its weight-twelve transformation yields the anomalous term $6c(c\tau+d)/(\pi i)$. Subtracting the displayed inverse-height term cancels that anomaly. The result transforms with weight two under the [modular group](modular-group.md) but is not holomorphic, so it is not a holomorphic [modular form](modular-form.md).
