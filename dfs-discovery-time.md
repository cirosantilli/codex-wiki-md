# DFS discovery time

↑ **Parent:** [Cut vertex](cut-vertex.md)

The [DFS discovery time](dfs-discovery-time.md) of a [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) is its first-visit number in [depth-first search](depth-first-search.md), assigned by an increasing counter. Discovery [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) form a [rooted tree](rooted-tree.md) in each [connected component of a graph](component-graph-theory.md). For the joined-$CD$ example, starting at $A$ and visiting [graph neighbours](neighbour-of-a-vertex.md) in [lexicographic order](lexicographic-order.md) gives first visits $A,B,C,D,E,F$, numbered $1,2,3,4,5,6$; its discovery [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) are $AB,BC,CD,DE,EF$. These numbers encode the chosen traversal, not an invariant of the [graph](graph-split.md).
