# Unsaturated receptor modification and imperfect adaptation

↑ **Parent:** [Barkai-Leibler model](barkai-leibler-model.md)

If the forward receptor-modification flux depends on remaining unmethylated substrate, it generally depends indirectly on ligand through the modified receptor pool. The steady output then need not be ligand independent. Near-perfect adaptation may remain in a saturation limit; finite modification capacity and completely inactive receptor populations also bound the ideal perfect-adaptation result.
