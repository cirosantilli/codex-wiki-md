# Multiquadratic field

↑ **Parent:** [Field extension](field-extension.md)

A [field extension](field-extension.md) obtained by adjoining finitely many square roots in characteristic different from two. If the classes of $a_1,\ldots,a_r$ are independent in $K^\times/(K^\times)^2$, the extension has degree $2^r$ and [Galois group](galois-group.md) $(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^r$: each square root's sign can be changed independently. The products of distinct square roots form a basis over $K$.
