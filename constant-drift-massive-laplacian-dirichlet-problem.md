# Constant-drift massive-Laplacian Dirichlet problem

↑ **Parent:** [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md)

Let $U\subset\mathbb R^d$ be bounded, $A\in\mathbb R^d$ satisfy $\lVert A\rVert_2<2$, and $f\in L^2(U)$. The [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md)

$$
B(u,v)=\int_U\bigl(\nabla u\mathbin\cdot\nabla v+uv+uA\mathbin\cdot\nabla v\bigr)
$$

on $H_0^1(U)$ is bounded. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and $2ab\leq a^2+b^2$ give

$$
B(u,u)\geq\left(1-\frac{\lVert A\rVert_2}{2}\right)\lVert u\rVert_{H^1}^2,
$$

so it is a [coercive bilinear form](coercive-bilinear-form.md). The [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) therefore gives a unique [weak solution](weak-solution.md) of $-\Delta v+v+A\cdot\nabla v=f$ with zero [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md).
