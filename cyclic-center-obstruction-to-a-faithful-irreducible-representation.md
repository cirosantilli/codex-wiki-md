# Cyclic-center obstruction to a faithful irreducible representation

↑ **Parent:** [Schur's lemma](schur-s-lemma.md)

If a finite group has a faithful irreducible complex representation, every central element acts as a scalar by [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md). Faithfulness embeds the centre into $\mathbb C^\times$, and every finite subgroup of $\mathbb C^\times$ is cyclic. Hence the group centre must be cyclic.
