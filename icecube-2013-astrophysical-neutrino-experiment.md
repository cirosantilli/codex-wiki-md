# IceCube 2013 astrophysical-neutrino experiment

↑ **Parent:** [IceCube Neutrino Observatory](icecube-neutrino-observatory.md)

In $662$ live days during 2010–2012, $28$ events exceeded the expected $10.6^{+5.0}_{-3.6}$ atmospheric events. [Energy](energy-split.md), direction and topology supported an extraterrestrial component. Combined searches gave $4.1\sigma$ with additional benchmark charm background; uncertain charm production affected significance. No source clustering was established.[Results; Discussion; Search for Neutrino Sources](evidence-for-high-energy-extraterrestrial-neutrinos-at-the-icecube-detector-by-icecube-collaboration.md)

A diffuse excess and an identified source answer different questions. The first compares populations against a background hypothesis; the second needs an association in direction, perhaps also in time.

Escaping [muons](muon.md) or [neutrinos](neutrino.md) can make deposited [energy](energy-split.md) smaller than incoming [neutrino](neutrino.md) [energy](energy-split.md).[Table 1; Event Reconstruction](evidence-for-high-energy-extraterrestrial-neutrinos-at-the-icecube-detector-by-icecube-collaboration.md)
