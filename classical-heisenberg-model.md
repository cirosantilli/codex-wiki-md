# Classical Heisenberg model

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg model](heisenberg-model.md)

The [classical Heisenberg model](classical-heisenberg-model.md) assigns a three-component unit [vector](vector.md) to each site and couples pairs by their [dot product](dot-product.md). Positive $J$ favors parallel alignment. With no applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) or anisotropy, simultaneous rotations of all vectors preserve the [energy](energy-split.md), so an ordered state chooses a direction and exhibits [spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md). The [vector order parameter](vector-order-parameter.md) is the spatially averaged orientation. A quartic [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) describes its mean-field onset near a continuous transition; fluctuations can change the critical exponents.[§4.2, $O(3)$ Heisenberg model](statistical-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
