# Virial mean escape speed

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

For an isolated equilibrium of equal-mass points, the [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) gives $\langle v^2\rangle=-W/M$. A particle's instantaneous [escape velocity](escape-velocity.md) satisfies $v_{\rm esc}^2=-2\Phi$ with zero potential at infinity. Since $W=\tfrac12\sum_i m\Phi_i$, averaging gives $\langle v_{\rm esc}^2\rangle=-4W/M$. These are three-dimensional mean-square speeds, not one-component [velocity dispersions](velocity-dispersion.md).
