# Normal monomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Pointed category](pointed-category.md)

A normal monomorphism is a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) that is the [kernel in a category](kernel-in-a-category.md) of some morphism. In a pointed category with kernels and cokernels, a monomorphism is normal exactly when it is the kernel of its own cokernel.
