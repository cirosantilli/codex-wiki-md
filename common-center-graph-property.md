# Common-center graph property

↑ **Parent:** [Graph property](graph-property.md)

All edges of a simple undirected [graph](graph-split.md) have at least one common endpoint. The empty [graph](graph-split.md) is included when there is at least one vertex, and isolated vertices are permitted. This is the star predicate with isolated vertices allowed; a connected [star graph](star-graph-theory.md) is a special case. Its [decision-tree depth](decision-tree-depth.md) is the full number $\binom n2$ of edge bits for $n\geq3$.
