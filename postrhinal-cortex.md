# Postrhinal cortex

↑ **Parent:** [Medial temporal lobe](medial-temporal-lobe.md)

Rodent cortex adjoining the [perirhinal cortex](perirhinal-cortex.md), important in contextual and spatial processing. A lesion including both regions is not evidence about the perirhinal region alone.
