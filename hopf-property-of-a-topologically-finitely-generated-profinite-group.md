# Hopf property of a topologically finitely generated profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Topological Hopf property](topological-hopf-property.md)

Every [topologically finitely generated group](topologically-finitely-generated-group.md) that is profinite has the [topological Hopf property](topological-hopf-property.md). There are only finitely many open subgroups of each given index, and inverse image under a surjective endomorphism permutes them. The endomorphism kernel consequently lies in every open normal subgroup and is trivial.
