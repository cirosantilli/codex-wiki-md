# Newtonian limit

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized gravity](linearized-gravity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newtonian_limit)

For a weak, static field produced by nonrelativistic matter, $T_{00}=\rho$ is the dominant stress-energy component. The field equations and the slow-particle [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) imply

$$
h_{00}=-2\Phi,
\qquad
\kappa=8\pi G,
$$

where the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) is $\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho$.
