# Convective instability of a uniform-density star

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar convective instability](stellar-convective-instability.md)

A uniform-density perfect-gas sphere supported by decreasing pressure has [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) squared $N^2=-2\omega_d^2r^2/[\gamma(R^2-r^2)]$ in the interior. Its [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md) decreases outward and adiabatic displacements are buoyantly unstable. For the nonradial polynomial family, the product of the two normalized squared mode frequencies is $-l(l+1)$, displaying one growing branch even when radial compression modes are stable.
