# Transport plan

↑ **Parent:** [Kantorovich optimal transport problem](kantorovich-optimal-transport-problem.md)

A transport plan is a [coupling of probability distributions](coupling.md) $\mu,\nu$, that is, a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) on the product space with those [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md). Unlike a [transport map](transport-map.md), it need not be concentrated on the graph of a function.
