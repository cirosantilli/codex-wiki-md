# Integrality of representation weights on coroots

↑ **Parent:** [Weight lattice](weight-lattice.md)

Restrict a finite-dimensional representation of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) to the [sl2 subalgebra associated with a root](sl2-subalgebra-associated-with-a-root.md) $\alpha$. Its Cartan element is the [coroot](coroot.md) $h_\alpha$. The [classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md) gives only integer eigenvalues for this element, so every [weight of a representation](weight-of-a-representation.md) has integral value on every coroot. This proves containment of all such representation weights in the [weight lattice](weight-lattice.md); it does not hold for unrestricted infinite-dimensional modules.
