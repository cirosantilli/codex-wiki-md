# Injective module sheaves are flasque

↑ **Parent:** [Injective sheaf of modules](injective-sheaf-of-modules.md)

For opens $U\subseteq V\subseteq X$, there is a monomorphism between the [extensions by zero](extension-by-zero.md) of $\mathcal O_U$ and $\mathcal O_V$. Morphisms from these sheaves into an [injective sheaf of modules](injective-sheaf-of-modules.md) $I$ identify with sections on $U$ and $V$, respectively. The [injective object](injective-object.md) extension property therefore makes the restriction $I(V)\to I(U)$ surjective, proving that $I$ is [flasque](flasque-sheaf.md). The argument applies to any [ringed space](ringed-space-split.md).
