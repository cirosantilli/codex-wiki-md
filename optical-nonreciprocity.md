# Optical nonreciprocity

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)

[Optical nonreciprocity](optical-nonreciprocity.md) means that exchanging source and detector paths need not restore the corresponding transmission when external conditions, including the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), are held fixed. The [Faraday effect](faraday-effect.md) gives a [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) rotation with the same laboratory sense on both traversals; see [Faraday rotation on a round trip](faraday-rotation-on-a-round-trip.md).
