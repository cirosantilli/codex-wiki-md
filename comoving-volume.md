# Comoving volume

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving coordinate](comoving-coordinate.md)

A comoving volume has fixed boundaries in [comoving coordinates](comoving-coordinate.md). Its physical volume grows as the cube of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md). In a homogeneous adiabatic gas, [cosmological entropy conservation](cosmological-entropy-conservation.md) makes $sV$ constant.
