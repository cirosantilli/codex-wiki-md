# Freudenthal suspension from two cones

↑ **Parent:** [Freudenthal suspension theorem](freudenthal-suspension-theorem.md)

Write the suspension of an $(n-2)$-connected based [CW complex](cw-complex.md) as two cones meeting in the original space. Each cone pair is $(n-1)$-connected because its relative [groups](group-split.md) shift the original [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) by one. [Homotopy excision theorem](homotopy-excision-theorem.md) compares the cone pair with the suspension relative to the other cone, giving the suspension [homomorphism](homomorphism.md) as an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) for $i<2n-2$ and a surjection at $i=2n-2$ in the indexing $\pi_{i-1}X\to\pi_i\Sigma X$.
