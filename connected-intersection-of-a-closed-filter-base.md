# Connected intersection of a closed filter base

↑ **Parent:** [Compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md)

A closed [filter base](filter-base.md) in a [compact space](compact-space.md) has nonempty [intersection](set-intersection.md). If the space is Hausdorff and every base member is [connected](connected-space.md), the [intersection](set-intersection.md) is [connected](connected-space.md). Otherwise [normality](normal-space.md) separates its two closed components by disjoint [open sets](open-set.md). [Compactness](compact-space.md) of the complement supplies finitely many base members whose [intersection](set-intersection.md) lies in that [union](set-union.md); directedness then gives a single base member meeting both sides, contradicting its [connectedness](connected-space.md).
