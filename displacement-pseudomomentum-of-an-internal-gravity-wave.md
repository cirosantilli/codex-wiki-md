# Displacement pseudomomentum of an internal gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Plane internal gravity wave](plane-internal-gravity-wave.md)

The displayed displacement convention is the negative of the usual positive-frequency [wave pseudomomentum](wave-pseudomomentum.md) $k\overline E/\omega$ for a rightgoing [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md). Here [energy](energy.md) per unit reference [mass density](density.md) is $\overline E=|\widehat\sigma|^2/(2N^2)$. From the [buoyancy polarization of a plane internal gravity wave](buoyancy-polarization-of-a-plane-internal-gravity-wave.md), horizontal averaging gives $\overline{\zeta_xp}=km|\widehat\sigma|^2/(2N^2K^2)=c_{gz}P$, where $p$ is [kinematic pressure](kinematic-pressure.md). The sign convention changes both density and flux and leaves their propagation at the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) unchanged.
