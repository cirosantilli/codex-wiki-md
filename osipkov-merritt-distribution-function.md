# Osipkov-Merritt distribution function

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md)

An Osipkov-Merritt spherical distribution has $F=f(Q)$ for $Q=E-L^2/(2r_a^2)>0$ and vanishes otherwise. Rescaling transverse velocities by $\sqrt{1+r^2/r_a^2}$ makes its velocity integral isotropic in the rescaled variables. Its [velocity-anisotropy parameter](velocity-anisotropy-parameter.md) is $\beta=r^2/(r^2+r_a^2)$.
