# Linear latent coordinates are not unique without constraints

↑ **Parent:** [Neural population state space](neural-population-state-space.md)

In the observation model $y=Cz+b$, let $Q$ be an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md). Replacing $z$ by $Qz$ and $C$ by $CQ^T$ leaves $y$ unchanged because $Q^TQ=I$. Thus reconstruction alone does not give each latent axis a unique biological meaning. A particular stochastic model can restrict this symmetry through its prior and parameter constraints; the algebra does not claim every fitted latent model has every rotational ambiguity.
