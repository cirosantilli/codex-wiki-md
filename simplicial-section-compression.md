# Simplicial section compression

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md)

Split a [set family](set-family.md) in the [hypercube graph](hypercube-graph.md) according to whether coordinate $i$ is present, and delete that coordinate from the present section. Replace both sections by [initial segments](initial-segment.md) of the [simplicial order on the discrete cube](simplicial-order-on-the-discrete-cube.md) with the same respective sizes. Induction and nesting of initial-segment [closed graph neighbourhoods](closed-graph-neighbourhood.md) show that this operation cannot enlarge the original [closed graph neighbourhood](closed-graph-neighbourhood.md). Repeated nontrivial compressions terminate because the sum of simplicial positions strictly decreases.
