# Schwarzschild time

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

[Schwarzschild time](schwarzschild-time.md) $t$ is the exterior time coordinate normalized to the [proper time](proper-time.md) of static observers at infinity. At fixed exterior radius, $d\tau=\sqrt{1-2M/r}\,dt$. A received signal and its emission occur at different values of this coordinate.
