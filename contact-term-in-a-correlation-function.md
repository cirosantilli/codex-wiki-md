# Contact term in a correlation function

↑ **Parent:** [Composite operator](composite-operator.md)

A contact term is a distribution supported where insertion points coincide. Its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) is local in momentum, typically polynomial. Renormalizing an operator product can require additive contact counterterms even if each operator has zero [anomalous dimension](anomalous-dimension.md).
