# Orthogonal involutions of the plane

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md)

For a real orthogonal involution, $A^{-1}=A=A^T$, so it is a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md). Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\pm1$. In two dimensions the possibilities are the identity, the half-turn $-I$, and reflections

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&\sin\theta\\\sin\theta&-\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The reflection fixes the line making angle $\theta/2$ with the first coordinate axis and negates its perpendicular direction. This follows either from the two corresponding [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) or by rotating $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$. A nonidentity element of order two in a [faithful representation](faithful-representation.md) must be either the half-turn or a reflection; the identity matrix is excluded by faithfulness.
