# Global section

↑ **Parent:** [Global section functor](global-section-functor.md)

A global section of a [sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md) $\mathcal F$ is an element of $\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)$, meaning a section defined over the whole space $X$.
