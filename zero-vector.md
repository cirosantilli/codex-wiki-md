# Zero vector

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)

The [zero vector](zero-vector.md) of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is its additive identity: $v+0=0+v=v$ for every [vector](vector.md) $v$. It is unique, because if $0'$ is another such identity then $0=0+0'=0'$. Distributivity gives $\alpha0=\alpha(0+0)=\alpha0+\alpha0$, so $\alpha0=0$ after cancellation. In a coordinate [vector](vector.md) space it has every coordinate zero.
