# Determining a lossy parallel coil from its admittance

↑ **Parent:** [Lossy parallel coil resonance](lossy-parallel-coil-resonance.md)

For series resistance R and [inductance](inductance.md) L in parallel with [capacitance](capacitance.md) C, suppose the applied [voltage](voltage.md) [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) V is fixed. The DC current gives $R=V/I_{\rm DC}$. If the high-frequency current is $I\sim\alpha\omega$, then $C=\alpha/V$. At the nonzero zero-phase [frequency](frequency.md), current [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $I_r=VRC/L$ determines $L=VRC/I_r$. The condition $R^2<L/C$ must hold. The inferred zero-phase [frequency](frequency.md) is $\sqrt{1/(LC)-R^2/L^2}$; it is not automatically the exact [frequency](frequency.md) of minimum current magnitude.
