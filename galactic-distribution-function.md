# Galactic distribution function

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

A galactic distribution function gives stellar mass or number per [phase space](phase-space.md) volume. Integrating over velocity gives spatial density, while velocity moments and projection along the line of sight predict kinematic observables.
