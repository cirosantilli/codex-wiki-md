# Policy iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Markov decision process](markov-decision-process.md)

Policy iteration alternates evaluation of a fixed decision policy with improvement of its actions using the resulting [value function](value-function.md). A stable policy satisfies the [Bellman equation](bellman-equation.md) when each chosen action attains the required optimum.
