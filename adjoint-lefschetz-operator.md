# Adjoint Lefschetz operator

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md)

The adjoint of $L=\omega\wedge-$ lowers real degree by two and bidegree by $(1,1)$. On a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) it acts on cohomology through [harmonic differential forms](harmonic-differential-form.md), because it commutes with the [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md). It does not generally preserve all closed forms.
