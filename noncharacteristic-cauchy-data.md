# Noncharacteristic Cauchy data

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy problem](cauchy-problem.md)

For $au_{x_1}+bu_{x_2}=c$ with data along $(\xi(s),\eta(s))$, the initial curve is noncharacteristic when its tangent is linearly independent of the projected characteristic vector $(a,b)$ evaluated at the initial value of $u$. The determinant is $a\eta'-b\xi'$, up to sign. For $C^1$ coefficients and data this permits the [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) to invert the local characteristic parametrization and gives a unique local [classical solution](classical-solution.md).
