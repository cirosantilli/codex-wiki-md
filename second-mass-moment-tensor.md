# Second mass moment tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Mass quadrupole moment](mass-quadrupole-moment.md)

The second mass moment tensor records the quadratic spatial distribution of mass. Its trace-free part is the [mass quadrupole moment](mass-quadrupole-moment.md) $Q_{ij}=I_{ij}-\delta_{ij}I_{kk}/3$. The mechanical moment-of-inertia tensor instead equals $J_{ij}=\delta_{ij}I_{kk}-I_{ij}$. A rigid rotation $L$ transforms the second mass moment tensor as $I(t)=L(t)I(0)L(t)^T$.
