<h1 id="cesaro-construction-of-a-banach-limit">Cesàro construction of a Banach limit</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Banach limit](banach-limit.md)

The displayed [sublinear functional](sublinear-function.md) on the real [l-infinity sequence space](l-infinity-sequence-space.md) agrees with the ordinary limit on [convergent sequences](convergent-sequence.md). A dominated extension by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) therefore defines a [generalized limit](generalized-limit.md) bounded between the lower and upper limits of the [Cesaro means](cesaro-mean.md). For the [left shift on bounded sequences](left-shift-on-bounded-sequences.md) $S$, the averages of $Sx-x$ equal $(x_{N+1}-x_1)/N$, which tend to zero. Domination applied to both signs gives $L(Sx-x)=0$, hence shift invariance. Positivity, agreement on constant sequences and the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) one follow from the bounds, so $L$ is a [Banach limit](banach-limit.md).
