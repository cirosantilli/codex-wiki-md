# Norm-compatible sequence of local units

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md)

Such a sequence $u=(u_n)$ satisfies $N_{n/n-1}u_n=u_{n-1}$ at every successive layer. The [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) uses the [field norm](field-norm.md), rather than inclusion of [units](unit-in-a-ring.md) from the lower [field](field.md). In a p-power cyclotomic tower a [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md) interpolates the sequence, and its [Coleman norm operator](coleman-norm-operator.md) fixes the interpolating series.
