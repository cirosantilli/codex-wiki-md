# Topological dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_dynamics)

Topological dynamics studies [continuous maps](continuous-map.md) and their [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) on [topological spaces](topological-space.md). For a compact [metric space](metric-space.md) $X$ and a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $T:X\to X$, the pair $(X,T)$ is a compact Hausdorff topological [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md).
