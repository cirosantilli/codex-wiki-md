# Measurement-based quantum feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-loop control](closed-loop-control.md)

[Measurement-based quantum feedback](measurement-based-quantum-feedback.md) adapts a [control input](control-input.md) from a measurement record or conditional [quantum state](quantum-state.md) estimate. A measurement contributes backaction and generally stochastic dynamics. Exact instantaneous expectation values cannot be read from a single system without these effects. A feedback law derived for deterministic closed-system evolution therefore needs a new conditional dynamical analysis before being applied as measurement feedback.
