# Two excited atoms in a resonant Tavis-Cummings mode

↑ **Parent:** [Tavis-Cummings model](tavis-cummings-model.md)

For two equally coupled atoms initially in $|ee;0\rangle$, excitation conservation confines the [interaction picture](interaction-picture.md) to $|ee;0\rangle$, $(|eg\rangle+|ge\rangle)|1\rangle/\sqrt2$ and $|gg;2\rangle$. The [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) divided by $\hbar$ has off-diagonal entries $\sqrt2g$ and $2g$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $0,\pm\sqrt6g$, so the amplitudes are $(2+\cos\sqrt6gt)/3$, $-i\sin(\sqrt6gt)/\sqrt3$ and $\sqrt2(\cos\sqrt6gt-1)/3$. An ideal one-[photon number](photon-number.md) projection therefore heralds a symmetric atomic [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) with probability $\sin^2(\sqrt6gt)/3$, at most $1/3$.
