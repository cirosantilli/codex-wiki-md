<h1 id="cauchy-riemann-operator">Cauchy–Riemann operator</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoholomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md)

This first-order operator vanishes exactly on [J-holomorphic curves](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md). Linearization at a map $u$ gives a [Fredholm operator](fredholm-operator.md) between suitable [Sobolev spaces](sobolev-space-split.md) of sections. At a constant map it is the [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md) on the trivial pulled-back tangent bundle.
