# Frailty distribution among survivors

↑ **Parent:** [Proportional frailty model](proportional-frailty-model.md)

In a [proportional frailty model](proportional-frailty-model.md), [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) tilts the original density $g(u)$ among subjects surviving to $t$ to

$$
g(u\mid T>t)=\frac{e^{-uH_0(t)}g(u)}{\int_0^\infty e^{-vH_0(t)}g(v)\,dv}.
$$

This survival selection progressively favors smaller frailties.
