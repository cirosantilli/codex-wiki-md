# Porosity

↑ **Parent:** [Permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porosity)

Porosity is the fraction of a representative material volume occupied by pore space. In a saturated medium, it is also the liquid volume fraction; it relates [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) to the mean pore-fluid velocity.
