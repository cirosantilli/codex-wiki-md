# Porosity

↑ **Parent:** [Permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porosity)

[Porosity](porosity.md) is the fraction of a representative bulk material volume occupied by voids:

$$
\phi=\epsilon=\frac{V_{\rm pore}}{V_{\rm bulk}},\qquad 0\leq\phi\leq1.
$$

It is dimensionless and measures storage space. A sample with bulk volume $1.0\,\mathrm L$ and pore volume $0.25\,\mathrm L$ has [porosity](porosity.md) $\phi=0.25=25\%$. If all its pores are saturated by one liquid, the liquid occupies $0.25\,\mathrm L$; partial [fluid saturation](fluid-saturation.md) $s$ instead gives liquid volume $s\phi V_{\rm bulk}$.

[Porosity](porosity.md) and [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) answer different questions: how much [fluid](fluid.md) fits, and how easily it flows. Isolated bubbles or cavities can contribute to total [porosity](porosity.md) while providing no throughgoing path. Even connected passages may be narrow enough to transmit very little discharge. In the simple fully participating saturated model, [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) is $\phi$ times [pore velocity](pore-velocity.md); changing [porosity](porosity.md) at a stipulated fixed [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) changes ideal advective travel time, but [porosity](porosity.md) alone cannot predict the [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md).
