<h1 id="ryu-takayanagi-surface-for-a-disk">Ryu–Takayanagi surface for a disk</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ryu–Takayanagi formula](ryu-takayanagi-formula.md)

For a round disk of radius $R$ in the vacuum of a holographic conformal field theory on flat space, the static Poincare-AdS Ryu--Takayanagi surface is the hemisphere $r^2+z^2=R^2$.
