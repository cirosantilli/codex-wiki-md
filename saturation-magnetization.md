# Saturation magnetization

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md)

[Saturation magnetization](saturation-magnetization.md) is the limiting [magnetization](magnetization.md) when the responding [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) are fully aligned within the stated model or experimental regime. In [independent-moment magnetic response](independent-moment-magnetic-response.md), $M_s=n\mu$. Reaching a large [magnetization](magnetization.md) does not imply a large [differential magnetic susceptibility](differential-magnetic-susceptibility.md): the remaining slope can be almost zero.
