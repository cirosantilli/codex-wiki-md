# Warsaw circle

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

The [Warsaw circle](warsaw-circle.md) joins the two [path components](path-component.md) of a closed [topologist's sine curve](topologist-s-sine-curve.md) by an [arc](arc-topology.md) attached at their outer endpoints. It is [path-connected](path-connected-space.md) and has trivial [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md), yet admits a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) to the [circle](circle.md) with no [continuous lift](continuous-lift.md) to its real-line [covering space](covering-space.md). Set the map equal to $1$ on the closed [topologist's sine curve](topologist-s-sine-curve.md) and wind once on the joining [arc](arc-topology.md). A lift would be constant on each [path component](path-component.md) of the sine curve, with the same constant on both by [continuity](continuous-function.md), but would change by $1$ along the joining [arc](arc-topology.md). This proves that the map is not [null-homotopic](null-homotopic-map.md), by the [homotopy lifting theorem for a covering map](homotopy-lifting-theorem-for-a-covering-map.md). Thus finiteness of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) alone cannot replace [local path connectedness](locally-path-connected-space.md) in the usual [lifting criterion for a covering space](lifting-criterion-for-a-covering-space.md).
