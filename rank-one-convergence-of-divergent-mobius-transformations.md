<h1 id="rank-one-convergence-of-divergent-mobius-transformations">Rank-one convergence of divergent Möbius transformations</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md)

A divergent sequence of [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) has a [subsequence](subsequence.md) converging uniformly on compact subsets of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) outside one exceptional point to a constant. Represent the transformations by determinant-one $2\times2$ [matrices](matrix.md) and divide each by its matrix norm. A convergent subsequence has a nonzero rank-one limit: its determinant tends to zero because the original norms tend to infinity. The kernel line is the exceptional point, and the image line is the constant limit. This converts the [limit set of a Kleinian group](limit-set-of-a-kleinian-group.md) into a tractable boundary dynamics problem.
