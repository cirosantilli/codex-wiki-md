# Counting-process martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Compensator of a counting process](compensator-of-a-counting-process.md)

If a [counting process](counting-process.md) $N$ has predictable conditional intensity $\lambda$, subtracting its [compensator of a counting process](compensator-of-a-counting-process.md) gives the associated martingale $M$. Conditional expected event increments and compensator increments agree, so their difference is conditionally centered. With the necessary integrability, $M$ is a mean-zero [martingale](martingale-split.md); for a simple no-ties process its predictable quadratic variation is $\int_0^t\lambda(u)du$. A predictable weighted martingale integral has mean zero and variance determined by the square of its weights. In [survival analysis](survival-analysis-split.md), $\lambda=Yh$ produces the event-minus-expected representation used to derive the [Nelson–Aalen estimator](nelson-aalen-estimator.md).
