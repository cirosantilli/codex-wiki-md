# Electron cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Beam cooling](beam-cooling.md)

Cooling an [ion](ion.md) beam through Coulomb interactions with a co-moving low-spread [electron](electron.md) beam. Friction reduces [ion](ion.md) motion relative to the [electron](electron.md) guiding flow while diffusion and external heating determine the eventual equilibrium.
