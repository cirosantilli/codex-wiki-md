# Cohen-Daubechies-Vial interval wavelet construction

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary wavelet](boundary-wavelet.md)

This construction adapts compactly supported orthonormal [wavelets](wavelet.md) to an interval by finite changes near its endpoints. It preserves local support, polynomial reproduction, nested approximation spaces and orthogonal detail spaces. The resulting basis contains coarse [scaling functions](scaling-function.md), unchanged interior [wavelets](wavelet.md), and finitely many [boundary wavelets](boundary-wavelet.md) per endpoint and per scale, without forcing periodic or zero boundary values.
