# Superhorizon scale

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological horizon exit](cosmological-horizon-exit.md)

A cosmological mode is on a superhorizon scale when its physical wavenumber is smaller than the Hubble expansion rate, $k/a\ll H$, equivalently $k\ll aH$.
