# Drifted Brownian interval-exit probability

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion with drift](brownian-motion-with-drift.md)

For [Brownian motion with drift](brownian-motion-with-drift.md) $dX=dB+\mu\,dt$ in $(-b,b)$, the displayed probability of exiting at $-b$ follows by stopping the harmonic function $e^{-2\mu X}$. A unit-time increment larger than $2b$ has a uniform positive probability and forces exit, so the exit time has a [geometric tail bound from a uniform escape probability](geometric-tail-bound-from-a-uniform-escape-probability.md) and all polynomial moments. At zero drift the limiting probability is $(b-x)/(2b)$.
