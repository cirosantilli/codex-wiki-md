# Pressureless homologous spherical collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational collapse](gravitational-collapse.md)

A uniform spherical cloud with negligible [pressure](pressure.md) and [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf u=(\dot R/R)\mathbf r$ remains uniform: the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) gives $\rho R^3=\rho_{00}R_0^3$. The [shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md) gives interior [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $-(4\pi G\rho/3)\mathbf r$, so its outer radius satisfies the displayed equation, with fixed mass $M=4\pi\rho_{00}R_0^3/3$. Collapse from rest has $\dot R^2=2GM(1/R-1/R_0)$. Writing $C^2=2GM/R_0$, its [cycloid](cycloid.md) parametrization is $R=R_0(1+\cos\phi)/2$ and $Ct=R_0(\phi+\sin\phi)/2$, for $0\le\phi\le\pi$. Complete collapse occurs after $\sqrt{3\pi/(32G\rho_{00})}$. This Newtonian dust solution ceases to be a regular fluid configuration at zero radius.
