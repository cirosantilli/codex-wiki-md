# Characters of the bilateral absolutely summable convolution algebra

↑ **Parent:** [L1 convolution algebra](l1-convolution-algebra.md)

Every [character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md) on $\ell^1(\mathbb Z)$ with [convolution](convolution.md) multiplication is uniquely of this form for $0\leq t<2\pi$. If $e_j$ is the point mass at $j$, then $e_je_k=e_{j+k}$. A character has [norm](norm.md) one and $e_1e_{-1}=1$, so $|\chi(e_1)|\leq1$ and $|\chi(e_1)^{-1}|\leq1$. Hence $\chi(e_1)$ lies on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md); multiplication determines all $\chi(e_j)$ and continuity determines the displayed series. Conversely [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md) permits rearrangement of the double series, proving multiplicativity. The [maximal ideals](maximal-ideal.md) are exactly their kernels: each consists of sequences whose [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) vanishes at one fixed point of the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md).
