# W. J. M. Rankine

↑ **Parent:** [List of engineers](list-of-engineers.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._J._M._Rankine)

A Scottish [engineer](engineer.md) and [physicist](physicist-split.md) who contributed to [thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md) and [classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md). Institutional historical profile: [Imperial College historical plaque booklet: William John Macquorn Rankine](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/media/imperial-college/faculty-of-engineering/civil/public/about/History_Plaques_Booklet.pdf).
