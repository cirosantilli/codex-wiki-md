# Merger at a magnetoconvection neutral-curve minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Steady-Hopf merger in vertical-field magnetoconvection](steady-hopf-merger-in-vertical-field-magnetoconvection.md)

At a steady-curve minimum, differentiation and the [steady-Hopf merger in vertical-field magnetoconvection](steady-hopf-merger-in-vertical-field-magnetoconvection.md) give the first displayed wavenumber. At an oscillatory-curve minimum they give the second. For fixed positive [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) $\sigma$ and large $Q_\kappa$, the corresponding merger diffusivity deficits are $1-\zeta_s\sim[(1+\sigma)/\sigma](\pi^2/(4Q_\kappa))^{1/3}$ and $1-\zeta_o\sim[(1+\sigma)\pi^2/(16\sigma Q_\kappa)]^{1/3}$. The two parameter surfaces are distinct: coincidence of neutral curves at one wavenumber does not imply coincidence of their minimizing wavenumbers.
