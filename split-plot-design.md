# Split-plot design

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments.md)

A [split-plot design](split-plot-design.md) randomizes one factor to whole plots and another to subplots within each whole plot. Whole-plot treatment effects and subplot treatment effects therefore use different error [ANOVA strata](anova-stratum.md). Whole plots provide [replication](replication-in-experimental-design.md) for the first factor; their subplots do not supply extra independent whole-plot replication. Within-whole-plot comparisons can remove a shared additive whole-plot [variance component](variance-component.md).
