# Vanishing harmonic moments of a polynomial conformal disc map

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

If a degree-$N$ [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $f(\zeta)=\sum_{j=1}^Na_j\zeta^j$ maps the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) conformally onto $D$, [Green's theorem](green-theorem.md) expresses $M_n=\iint_D z^n\,dx\,dy$ as the residue of $f^n f'\sum_j\bar a_j\zeta^{-j}$ times $\pi$. Its lowest possible power is $n-N$, so no residue occurs when $n\geq N$. For a quadratic map, $M_0=\pi(|a_1|^2+2|a_2|^2)$ and $M_1=\pi a_1^2\bar a_2$. The first moment can be complex; the zeroth is area.
