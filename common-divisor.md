# Common divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Divisibility](divisibility-split.md)

A positive [integer](integer-split.md) $d$ is a common divisor of [integers](integer-split.md) $a,b$ when $d\mid a$ and $d\mid b$. For positive $a,b$, their largest [common divisor](common-divisor.md) is their [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md). The identity $a=qb+r$ implies that $a,b$ and $b,r$ have the same positive [common divisors](common-divisor.md), because $r=a-qb$ and $a=qb+r$.
