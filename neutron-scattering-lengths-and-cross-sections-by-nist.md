# Neutron scattering lengths and cross sections by NIST

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

[https://www.ncnr.nist.gov/resources/n-lengths/list.html](https://www.ncnr.nist.gov/resources/n-lengths/list.html)

The NIST Center for Neutron Research table lists bound coherent and incoherent scattering lengths, cross sections and isotopic abundances, citing the 1992 Neutron News compilation. Natural-element coherent lengths include K $3.67$ fm, Cl $9.5770$ fm, Al $3.449$ fm and Si $4.1491$ fm. Nuclear contrast is not fixed by the number of atomic electrons.
