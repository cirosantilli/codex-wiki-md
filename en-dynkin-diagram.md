# En Dynkin diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md)

The finite [Dynkin diagrams](dynkin-diagram.md) $E_6,E_7,E_8$ are simply laced trees with one trivalent vertex. Their three arm lengths, counting edges, are respectively $(1,2,2)$, $(1,2,3)$ and $(1,2,4)$.
