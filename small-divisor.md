# Small divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine frequency vector](diophantine-frequency-vector.md)

A denominator such as $k\cdot\omega$ which may become small for large integer vectors $k$. Inverting a directional derivative on a [torus](torus.md) divides the nonzero [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) by $i k\cdot\omega$. A [Diophantine condition](diophantine-frequency-vector.md) bounds this loss polynomially, so exponential Fourier decay still gives convergence on a smaller complex strip.
