# Infimal convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Convex analysis](convex-analysis.md)

The [infimal convolution](infimal-convolution.md) is $(E\mathbin\square F)(u)=\inf_v\{E(v)+F(u-v)\}$. It optimizes a decomposition of $u$ between two costs. It is [convex](convex-function.md) when both costs are [convex functions](convex-function.md), and its [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md) is $E^*+F^*$ whenever the extended-real operations are well defined.
