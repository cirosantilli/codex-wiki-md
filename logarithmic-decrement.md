# Logarithmic decrement

↑ **Parent:** [Damping measurement from free decay](damping-measurement-from-free-decay.md)

For [underdamping](underdamping.md), let $x_n,x_{n+1}$ be successive same-sign extrema of [displacement](displacement.md) relative to [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), one full [oscillation period](period-of-an-oscillation.md) apart. The [logarithmic decrement](logarithmic-decrement.md) is

$$
\delta=\ln\frac{|x_n|}{|x_{n+1}|}=\alpha T_{\mathrm d}
=\frac{2\pi\zeta}{\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}},\qquad
\zeta=\frac{\delta}{\sqrt{4\pi^2+\delta^2}}.
$$

Comparing extrema $N$ full cycles apart instead gives $\delta=N^{-1}\ln(|x_n|/|x_{n+N}|)$. Adjacent extrema of opposite sign are only half a cycle apart and give half this decrement. This distinction matters when inferring a [damping ratio](damping-ratio.md). Near [critical damping](critical-damping.md), a full second same-sign extremum may be too small to resolve above [measurement noise](measurement-noise.md); a full-trace fit with uncertainty is then more informative than a peak ratio.
