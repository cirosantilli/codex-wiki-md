<h1 id="nist-digital-library-of-mathematical-functions/5-12-beta-function">5.12 Beta Function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions](../nist-digital-library-of-mathematical-functions.md)

[Official NIST DLMF section 5.12](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.12). Equation 5.12.1 gives Euler's integral for the [beta function](../beta-function.md) and its expression as a ratio of [gamma functions](../gamma-function.md), with positive real parts of the two parameters. The integral identity supplies normalization and shifted-parameter [moments](../moment.md) for the [beta distribution](../beta-distribution.md).
