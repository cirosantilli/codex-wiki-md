# E-fold count for the natural-inflation cosine potential

↑ **Parent:** [Natural inflation](natural-inflation.md)

For the cosine potential,

$$
N=\frac{2f^2}{M_{\rm Pl}^2}
\left[
\log\sin\frac{\phi_i}{2f}
-\log\sin\frac{\phi_f}{2f}
\right].
$$
