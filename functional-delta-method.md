# Functional delta method

↑ **Parent:** [Delta method](delta-method.md)

If $r_n(X_n-s)$ converges in distribution to $X$ and $\Phi$ is [Hadamard differentiable](hadamard-differentiability.md) at $s$ tangentially to a space containing the limit, the displayed transformation rule holds under the usual [measurability](measurability.md) and tight separable-support conditions. The variables $X_n$ must belong to the domain of $\Phi$.
