# Survival probability of a branching process

↑ **Parent:** [Extinction probability of a branching process](extinction-probability-of-a-branching-process.md)

For a [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md) started from one ancestor, this is the [probability](probability.md) that every generation is nonempty. It is the complement of the [branching extinction probability](extinction-probability-of-a-branching-process.md). The [binomial branching survival correction](binomial-branching-survival-correction.md) distinguishes finite-binomial reproduction from the [Poisson branching process](poisson-branching-process.md) near mean one.
