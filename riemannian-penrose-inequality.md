# Riemannian Penrose inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose inequality](penrose-inequality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_Penrose_inequality)

For complete asymptotically flat three-dimensional Riemannian initial data with nonnegative [scalar curvature](scalar-curvature.md) and an outermost compact [minimal surface](minimal-surface.md) boundary of area $A$, the [Riemannian Penrose inequality](riemannian-penrose-inequality.md) gives the displayed bound. Equality is realized by the spatial exterior of [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md). Time-symmetric gravitational initial data have this form when the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) and energy hypothesis give the required nonnegative curvature. This theorem does not justify substituting arbitrary non-time-symmetric [apparent horizon](apparent-horizon.md) area into the same formula.
