# High-angle annular dark-field imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Annular dark-field electron imaging](annular-dark-field-electron-imaging.md)

A [scanning transmission electron microscopy](scanning-transmission-electron-microscopy.md) mode collecting electrons scattered to sufficiently large angles. It often gives approximately monotonic atomic-number and mass-thickness contrast, with detector angles and multiple scattering still important.
