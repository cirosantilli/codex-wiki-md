# Free algebra functor

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra for a monad](algebra-for-a-monad.md)

For a [monad](monad.md) $(T,\eta,\mu)$, the free algebra functor $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C^T$ sends $X$ to $(TX,\mu_X)$ and $f$ to $Tf$. It is a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) to the forgetful [functor](functor.md) $U$, via $h\mapsto h\eta_X$ and $u\mapsto\gamma Tu$ between $\mathcal C^T(FX,(C,\gamma))$ and $\mathcal C(X,C)$.
