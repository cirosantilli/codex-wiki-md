# Totally geodesic submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)

For a [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) induced [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md), a [totally geodesic submanifold](totally-geodesic-submanifold.md) has vanishing [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md); equivalently, every ambient [geodesic](geodesic.md) initially tangent to it remains in it. This extends the notion of a [totally geodesic hypersurface](totally-geodesic-hypersurface.md) to arbitrary codimension.
