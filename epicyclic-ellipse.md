# Epicyclic ellipse

↑ **Parent:** [Epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md)

In a Cartesian frame rotating with an [epicyclic guiding center](epicyclic-guiding-center.md), a small planar oscillation obeys $x=X\cos(\kappa t+\chi)$ and $y=-(2\Omega X/\kappa)\sin(\kappa t+\chi)$. The ellipse is elongated azimuthally when the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) satisfies $\kappa<2\Omega$. [Conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) makes the motion around the guiding centre retrograde.
