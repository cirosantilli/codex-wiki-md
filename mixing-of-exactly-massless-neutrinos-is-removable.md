# Mixing of exactly massless neutrinos is removable

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)

If all [neutrinos](neutrino.md) are exactly massless, arbitrary unitary rotations among them preserve both their [kinetic terms](kinetic-term.md) and their zero mass matrix. After diagonalizing the charged-lepton [Yukawa matrix](yukawa-matrix.md), choose the neutrino basis to coincide with that rotation. The resulting [weak charged current](charged-current.md) has no physical lepton mixing matrix. With nondegenerate neutrino masses this extra basis freedom is lost, permitting observable [neutrino oscillations](neutrino-oscillation.md) and lepton mixing.
