# Ten-dimensional super Yang-Mills theory

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric gauge theory](supersymmetric-gauge-theory.md)

Minimal ten-dimensional [supersymmetric gauge theory](supersymmetric-gauge-theory.md) contains a [gauge field](gauge-field.md) and an adjoint [Majorana-Weyl spinor](majorana-weyl-spinor.md). They have eight propagating [bosonic](boson.md) and eight propagating [fermionic](fermion.md) states per gauge generator and sixteen real [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md). A toroidal zero-mode [dimensional reduction](dimensional-reduction.md) to four dimensions gives one [gauge field](gauge-field.md), six real adjoint [scalar fields](scalar-field.md), and four adjoint [Weyl spinors](weyl-spinor.md): a [four-dimensional N=4 super Yang-Mills theory](four-dimensional-n-4-super-yang-mills-theory.md). The internal field strength $F_{mn}=-ig[X_m,X_n]$ gives the nonnegative [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) $V=\frac{g^2}{4}\sum_{m,n}\lVert[X_m,X_n]\rVert^2$, where the norm uses a positive invariant inner product on the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). Its minima are commuting tuples of adjoint [scalar fields](scalar-field.md).
