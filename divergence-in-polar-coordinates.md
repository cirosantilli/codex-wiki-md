# Divergence in polar coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence](divergence.md)

For $\mathbf u=u_r\mathbf e_r+u_\theta\mathbf e_\theta$,

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf u
=\frac1r\frac{\partial(ru_r)}{\partial r}
+\frac1r\frac{\partial u_\theta}{\partial\theta}.
$$
