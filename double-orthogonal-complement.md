# Double orthogonal complement

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md)

For a linear subspace $F$ of a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md),

$$
F^{\perp\perp}=\overline F.
$$

Thus $F=F^{\perp\perp}$ exactly when $F$ is closed.
