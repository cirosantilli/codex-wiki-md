# Strength-gravity disruption transition

↑ **Parent:** [Catastrophic disruption threshold](catastrophic-disruption-threshold.md)

For $Q_D^*=Q_aD^{-a}+Q_bD^b$, $a,b>0$, [differentiation](differentiation.md) gives the minimum at the displayed diameter. Equal strength and gravity contributions occur instead at $D_{\rm eq}=(Q_a/Q_b)^{1/(a+b)}=(b/a)^{1/(a+b)}D_w$. These two transition conventions coincide only for $a=b$.
