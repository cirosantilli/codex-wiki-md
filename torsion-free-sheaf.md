# Torsion-free sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md)

On an integral scheme, a [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) is torsion-free when its [module](module-mathematics.md) on every [affine chart](affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) is a [torsion-free module](torsion-free-module.md) over that chart's [integral domain](integral-domain.md). It embeds into its generic fibre. Therefore a torsion-free sheaf with zero generic fibre is zero; [locally free sheaves](locally-free-sheaf.md) and their subsheaves are torsion-free.
