# Kemnitz theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Chevalley-Warning theorem](chevalley-warning-theorem.md)

Every sequence of $4p-3$ elements of $\mathbb F_p^2$, where $p$ is prime, contains $p$ terms whose sum is zero. A proof applies the [Chevalley-Warning theorem](chevalley-warning-theorem.md) to the cardinality and two coordinate sums and then double-counts zero-sum subsequences.
