# Lambda c baryon

↑ **Parent:** [Charmed baryon](charmed-baryon.md)

The lowest [Lambda c baryon](lambda-c-baryon.md) has valence content $cud$, [electric charge](electric-charge.md) $+1$, [isospin](isospin.md) zero and [spin angular momentum](spin.md) $1/2$. Its light pair has antisymmetric flavour and spin zero. This is the charm analogue of the [Lambda baryon](lambda-baryon.md).
