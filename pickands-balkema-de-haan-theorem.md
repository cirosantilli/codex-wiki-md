# Pickands-Balkema-de Haan theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Pareto distribution](generalized-pareto-distribution.md)

For a distribution in an extreme-value domain of attraction, the [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md) of the excess above a high threshold is asymptotically approximated by a [generalized Pareto distribution](generalized-pareto-distribution.md) with a threshold-dependent scale and a limiting shape parameter. This theorem justifies the [peaks-over-threshold method](peaks-over-threshold-method.md). Threshold selection must balance approximation error against the reduced number of excess observations.
