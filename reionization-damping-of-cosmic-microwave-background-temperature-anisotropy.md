# Reionization damping of cosmic microwave background temperature anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Reionization](reionization.md)

Ignoring velocities and evolving potentials, a thin [reionization](reionization.md) screen transmits the primary [Cosmic microwave background anisotropy](cosmic-microwave-background-anisotropy-split.md) with amplitude $e^{-\tau_{\rm re}}$ and adds a direction-averaged source. A plane wave propagating a distance $\Delta\chi$ from recombination to the screen has monopole factor $j_0(k\Delta\chi)=\sin(k\Delta\chi)/(k\Delta\chi)$. This suppresses the rescattered source at $k\Delta\chi\gg1$, while coherent large-scale modes are unchanged to leading order.
