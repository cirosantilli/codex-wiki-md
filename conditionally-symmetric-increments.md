# Conditionally symmetric increments

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md)

A process has conditionally symmetric increments if, for all deterministic $s\le t$ and bounded measurable $g$,

$$
\mathbb E[g(X_t-X_s)\mid\mathcal F_s]=\mathbb E[g(X_s-X_t)\mid\mathcal F_s].
$$

Equivalently, the [conditional characteristic function](conditional-characteristic-function.md) of every increment is invariant under changing the sign of its argument. This property is stronger than unconditional symmetry and is different from [independent increments](independent-increments.md). It supplies identities between positive and negative exponential terminal martingales.
