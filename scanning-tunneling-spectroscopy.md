# Scanning tunneling spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Scanning tunneling microscopy](scanning-tunneling-microscopy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scanning_tunneling_spectroscopy)

A conducting tip probes the energy-dependent local [density of states](density-of-states.md) by recording tunnel current versus applied bias, often measuring $dI/dV$ with a small bias modulation and lock-in detection. At low temperature and for slowly varying tip density and matrix element, $dI/dV$ is proportional to sample local density of states at the corresponding bias energy. Thermal broadening, surface structure and energy-dependent tunnelling selectivity limit a direct interpretation as bulk band structure.
