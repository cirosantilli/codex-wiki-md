# Descending chain condition

↑ **Parent:** [Artinian ring](artinian-ring.md)

A family of [ideals](ideal.md) satisfies the descending chain condition when every descending sequence of [ideals](ideal.md) eventually becomes constant. A [ring](ring.md) is [Artinian](artinian-ring.md) precisely when all its [ideals](ideal.md) satisfy this condition.
