# Spectral permanence for C-star algebras

↑ **Parent:** [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md)

If $A$ is a closed [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) inside a unital [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) $B$ and the two algebras share their identity, an element of $A$ is invertible in $A$ exactly when it is invertible in $B$. Consequently its [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md) does not depend on which of these two algebras is used.
