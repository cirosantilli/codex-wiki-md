<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

There is no allowed [spherical harmonic](../../../../spherical-harmonic.md) with $m=l+1$, so $L_+Y_l^l=0$. The equation $-i\hbar\partial_\phi Y=\hbar lY$ first gives $Y=f(\theta)e^{il\phi}$. Combining the differential expressions for $L_x,L_y$ yields

$$
L_+=\hbar e^{i\phi}(\partial_\theta+i\cot\theta\,\partial_\phi).
$$

Its annihilation of the highest-weight state becomes

$$
f'(\theta)-l\cot\theta\,f(\theta)=0.
$$

Integrating $f'/f=l\cot\theta$ gives $f=C\sin^l\theta$, with the constant solution included for $l=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{Y_l^l(\theta,\phi)=C\sin^l\theta\,e^{il\phi}.}
$$

The [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) $e^{il\phi}$ is single valued for [integral](../../../../integral-split.md) orbital [quantum number](../../../../quantum-number.md) $l$; [normalization](../../../../normalization.md) fixes only $|C|$, leaving a conventional constant [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md).
