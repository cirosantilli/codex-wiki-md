<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Hermiticity of the Cartesian [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) components gives

$$
\boxed{L_+^\dagger=L_-,\qquad L_-^\dagger=L_+.}
$$

The [angular momentum commutation relations](../../../../angular-momentum-commutation-relations.md) $[L_i,L_j]=i\hbar\epsilon_{ijk}L_k$ imply

$$
\boxed{[L^2,L_\pm]=0,\qquad [L_z,L_\pm]=\pm\hbar L_\pm.}
$$

For example, $[L_z,L_x+iL_y]=i\hbar L_y+\hbar L_x=\hbar L_+$. The two terms in $[L_x^2+L_y^2+L_z^2,L_i]$ cancel in pairs, giving the first [commutator](../../../../commutator.md). Also $[L_+,L_-]=2\hbar L_z$.
