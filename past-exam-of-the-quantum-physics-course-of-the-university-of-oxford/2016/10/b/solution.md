<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the ordered [basis](../../../../basis.md) $|+\rangle,|-\rangle$, the transverse [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) produces

$$
V(t)=\mu_BB_1(\sigma_x\cos\omega t+\sigma_y\sin\omega t)
=\boxed{\hbar\gamma\begin{pmatrix}0&e^{-i\omega t}\\e^{i\omega t}&0\end{pmatrix}},\qquad\gamma=\frac{\mu_BB_1}{\hbar}.
$$

The off-diagonal complex exponentials follow directly from $\cos\omega t\mp i\sin\omega t$. This [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) is [Hermitian](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) at every time.
