<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Insert the expansion with amplitudes $c_1,c_2$ and the two unperturbed [dynamical phases](../../../../dynamical-phase.md) into $i\hbar\dot\Psi=(H_0+V)\Psi$. Differentiation of the explicit [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md) cancels $H_0$, leaving

$$
i\dot c_1=\gamma e^{-i(\omega-\omega_0)t}c_2,\qquad
i\dot c_2=\gamma e^{i(\omega-\omega_0)t}c_1,
\qquad\omega_0=\frac{2\mu_BB_0}{\hbar}.
$$

Equivalently, with $\Omega=(\omega-\omega_0)/2$,

$$
\boxed{\dot c_1=-i\gamma e^{-2i\Omega t}c_2,\qquad\dot c_2=-i\gamma e^{2i\Omega t}c_1.}
$$

These interaction-picture equations conserve $|c_1|^2+|c_2|^2$. They are exact for the [circularly rotating magnetic drive](../../../../circularly-rotating-magnetic-drive.md); a [rotating-wave approximation](../../../../rotating-wave-approximation.md) has not been used.
