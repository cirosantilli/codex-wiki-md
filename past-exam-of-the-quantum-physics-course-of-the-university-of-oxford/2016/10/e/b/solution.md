<h1 id="10/e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For nonzero [Rabi detuning](../../../../../rabi-detuning.md),

$$
\boxed{P_-(t)=\frac{\gamma^2}{\gamma^2+\Omega^2}\sin^2\left(\sqrt{\gamma^2+\Omega^2}\,t\right).}
$$

The sketch starts at zero and oscillates between zero and $\gamma^2/(\gamma^2+\Omega^2)<1$, with [period](../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) $\pi/\sqrt{\gamma^2+\Omega^2}$ when the coupling is nonzero. Relative to resonance, the oscillations have a smaller maximum and a shorter [period](../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) at fixed coupling. The sign of the [Rabi detuning](../../../../../rabi-detuning.md) changes the amplitudes' [phases](../../../../../phase-waves.md) but leaves this [transition probability](../../../../../transition-probability.md) unchanged. For $|\Omega|\gg|\gamma|$, its maximum is approximately $\gamma^2/\Omega^2$, so population transfer is strongly suppressed. The plotted detuned example has $|\Omega|=|\gamma|$, giving maximum $1/2$.
