<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $\mathbf B=B_0\hat{\mathbf z}$ and $S_z=(\hbar/2)\sigma_z$,

$$
H_0=\mu_BB_0\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{E_+=\mu_BB_0,\quad |+\rangle=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix};\qquad E_-=-\mu_BB_0,\quad |-\rangle=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

For $B_0>0$, [spin](../../../../spin.md) down is the lower [energy eigenstate](../../../../energy-eigenstate.md). The negative [electron magnetic moment](../../../../electron-magnetic-moment.md) is already accounted for in the positive coefficient multiplying $\mathbf S\cdot\mathbf B$ in the given [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md).
