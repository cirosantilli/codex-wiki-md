<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Insert [hydrogen](../../../hydrogen.md) [energy eigenstates](../../../energy-eigenstate.md) on either side of the [commutator](../../../commutator.md). Because $L_z$ is [Hermitian](../../../hermitian-operator.md) and has [eigenvalue](../../../eigenvalue.md) $\hbar m$,

$$
0=\langle nlm|[L_z,z]|nl'm'\rangle=\hbar(m-m')\langle nlm|z|nl'm'\rangle.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\langle nlm|z|nl'm'\rangle\ne0\ \Longrightarrow\ m=m'.}
$$

This is a **necessary [electric-dipole selection rule](../../../electric-dipole-selection-rule.md)**, not a sufficient condition for a nonzero [matrix element](../../../matrix-element.md). For example, $\langle200|z|200\rangle=0$ by [parity](../../../parity.md) even though $m=m'=0$. The angular factor $z=r\cos\theta$ additionally requires $l'=l\pm1$: multiplication of a [spherical harmonic](../../../spherical-harmonic.md) by $\cos\theta$ gives only harmonics with the same $m$ and adjacent $l$. The [commutator](../../../commutator.md) alone establishes only the [magnetic quantum number](../../../magnetic-quantum-number.md) restriction; radial [integrals](../../../integral-split.md) and the remaining [electric-dipole selection rules for hydrogen](../../../electric-dipole-selection-rules-for-hydrogen.md) must still be considered.
