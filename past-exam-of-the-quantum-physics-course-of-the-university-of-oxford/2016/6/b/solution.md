<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Suppress the fixed individual [quantum numbers](../../../../quantum-number.md) $s_1=s_2=1/2$. In the coupled [basis](../../../../basis.md), ordered on both rows and columns as $|1,1\rangle,|1,0\rangle,|1,-1\rangle,|0,0\rangle$, the total-spin [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) give

$$
\boxed{[f]_{\mathrm{coupled}}=\operatorname{diag}\left(a+\frac{b\hbar^2}4,a+\frac{b\hbar^2}4,a+\frac{b\hbar^2}4,a-\frac{3b\hbar^2}4\right).}
$$

Here the first three entries form the [triplet state](../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) sector and the last is the [singlet state](../../../../singlet-state.md). For the product [basis](../../../../basis.md), use

$$
\mathbf s_1\cdot\mathbf s_2=s_{1z}s_{2z}+\frac12(s_{1+}s_{2-}+s_{1-}s_{2+}).
$$

The first term has diagonal entries $\hbar^2(1,-1,-1,1)/4$. Each nonzero exchange term flips $|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle$ to $|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle$ or back, with coefficient $\hbar^2/2$. With rows and columns ordered as $|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle,|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle,|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle,|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle$,

$$
\boxed{[f]_{\mathrm{product}}=aI+\frac{b\hbar^2}4\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0&0\\0&-1&2&0\\0&2&-1&0\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The [change of basis](../../../../change-of-basis.md) is made explicit by $|1,0\rangle=(|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle+|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and $|0,0\rangle=(|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle-|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle)/\sqrt2$, with $|1,\pm1\rangle=|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle,|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle$. The resulting [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) reproduce the diagonal coupled [matrix](../../../../matrix.md).
