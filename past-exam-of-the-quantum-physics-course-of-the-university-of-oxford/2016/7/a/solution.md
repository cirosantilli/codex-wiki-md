<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [spin](../../../../spin.md) [Hilbert space](../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) is a [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md) of two two-dimensional spaces and therefore has [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) four. [Angular momentum addition](../../../../angular-momentum-addition.md) decomposes it as

$$
\boxed{S=1:\ M_S=1,0,-1;\qquad S=0:\ M_S=0.}
$$

These are three [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) [triplet states](../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) and one [singlet state](../../../../singlet-state.md). For identical [electrons](../../../../electron.md) the **complete** [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) must also satisfy [fermionic exchange symmetry](../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md): a symmetric triplet [spin](../../../../spin.md) factor accompanies an antisymmetric spatial factor, whereas the antisymmetric singlet [spin](../../../../spin.md) factor accompanies a symmetric spatial factor. If both [electrons](../../../../electron.md) occupy the same [spatial orbital](../../../../spatial-orbital.md), only the singlet [spin](../../../../spin.md) factor is allowed. The four-dimensional [spin](../../../../spin.md) space does not assert four allowed [spin](../../../../spin.md) states for that particular spatial occupation.
