<h1 id="7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a [conditional spin measurement of identical fermions](../../../../conditional-spin-measurement-of-identical-fermions.md) addressing the $z$ component in one spatial mode, the [Born rule](../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(\uparrow)=\frac12.}
$$

Conditioned on this result, the mode [spin](../../../../spin.md) state is $|\uparrow_a\downarrow_b\rangle$; the other outcome produces $|\downarrow_a\uparrow_b\rangle$. The [spin](../../../../spin.md) product is not a spin-exchange [eigenstate](../../../../eigenstate.md). The [measurement](../../../../measurement.md) has selected an addressed mode and recorded an outcome, so this conditional [spin](../../../../spin.md) description distinguishes the measured subsystem.

The full [electron](../../../../electron.md) [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) still obeys [fermionic exchange symmetry](../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md). For example, the conditional spin-up outcome in spatial mode $a$ is represented in particle coordinates by

$$
\frac1{\sqrt2}\left[\phi_a(\mathbf r_1)\phi_b(\mathbf r_2)|\uparrow_1\downarrow_2\rangle-\phi_b(\mathbf r_1)\phi_a(\mathbf r_2)|\downarrow_1\uparrow_2\rangle\right].
$$

It is antisymmetric under full particle exchange. Thus the loss of a definite symmetry of the **conditional [spin](../../../../spin.md) product** does not mean that [measurement](../../../../measurement.md) turns identical [electrons](../../../../electron.md) into distinguishable particles. If the outcome is discarded, the mode [spin](../../../../spin.md) state is instead the equal [mixture](../../../../mixture-split.md) of the two opposite-spin products. The [measurement](../../../../measurement.md) must specify which spatial mode is addressed for the conditional statement to be operationally meaningful.
