<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Separate the [center-of-mass motion](../../../../center-of-mass-motion.md) from the [relative motion](../../../../relative-motion.md). Two equal [quark](../../../../quark.md) masses give [reduced mass](../../../../reduced-mass.md) $\mu=m_q/2$. Replacing the [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) Coulomb strength by $a>0$ gives the Coulomb binding energies and length scale

$$
\boxed{E_n^{(0)}=-\frac{m_q a^2}{4\hbar^2n^2},\qquad a_q=\frac{\hbar^2}{\mu a}=\frac{2\hbar^2}{m_q a}.}
$$

The factor of two in the [reduced mass](../../../../reduced-mass.md) matters: using $m_q$ directly would double the [binding energy](../../../../binding-energy.md). These are relative binding energies measured from the free [quark](../../../../quark.md)–[antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) threshold; the rest [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $2m_qc^2$ is excluded.
