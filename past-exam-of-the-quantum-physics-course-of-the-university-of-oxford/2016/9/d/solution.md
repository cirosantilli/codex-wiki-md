<h1 id="9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The unperturbed $n=2$ level **is degenerate**. The useful point is that a central perturbation is already diagonal within its usual orbital [eigenbasis](../../../../eigenbasis.md):

$$
\langle nlm|br|nl'm'\rangle
=b\left(\int_0^\infty r^3R_{nl}^*R_{nl'}\,dr\right)\delta_{ll'}\delta_{mm'}.
$$

Angular [orthogonality](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) removes the possible mixing of $2s$ and $2p$, and also the mixing of different [magnetic sublevels](../../../../magnetic-sublevel.md). Therefore the [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) required by [degenerate perturbation theory](../../../../degenerate-perturbation-theory.md) needs no further diagonalization: its diagonal entries are precisely the expectation values above. It would be incorrect to justify the calculation by claiming that the unperturbed spectrum was nondegenerate.

For $b=0$ the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) needs only $n$. For a nonzero central confinement term the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) generally needs **$n,l$**, but remains independent of $m$. The [magnetic quantum number](../../../../magnetic-quantum-number.md) is still needed to specify an individual orbital [eigenstate](../../../../eigenstate.md), rather than just its [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).
