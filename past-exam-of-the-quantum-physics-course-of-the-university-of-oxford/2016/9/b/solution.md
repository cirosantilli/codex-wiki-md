<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Ignoring [spin](../../../../spin.md) and working in the fixed [quarkonium](../../../../quarkonium.md) internal sector, the orbital degeneracy is $\sum_{l=0}^{n-1}(2l+1)=n^2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{g_1=1\quad(1s),\qquad g_2=4\quad(2s\text{ and three }2p\text{ states}).}
$$

No extra [spin](../../../../spin.md) or colour multiplicity is being counted. The degeneracy between different $l$ at fixed $n$ is special to the [Coulomb potential](../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md).
