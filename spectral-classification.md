# Spectral classification

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)

Classification of stellar spectra by features controlled mainly by [temperature](temperature-split.md) and also by [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) and composition. The O-B-A-F-G-K-M [temperature](temperature-split.md) sequence does not mean a corresponding sequence in bulk [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) abundance.
