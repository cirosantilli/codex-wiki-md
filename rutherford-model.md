# Rutherford model

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutherford_model)

The Rutherford model places a concentrated central [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) inside a much larger [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). In 1911, [Ernest Rutherford](ernest-rutherford.md) argued that rare large deflections were predominantly single close encounters, rather than accumulated small deviations in a diffuse-charge atom. He treated the central [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) as positive for calculation while noting that the scattering deductions did not establish its sign. He explicitly deferred atomic stability.[The Scattering of α and β Particles by Matter and the Structure of the Atom, §§1–3, printed pp. 669–675](the-scattering-of-alpha-and-beta-particles-by-matter-and-the-structure-of-the-atom-by-ernest-rutherford.md)

The physical inference is a compact region generating a strong [electric field](electric-field-split.md), not a miniature hard wall. Most [impact parameters](impact-parameter.md) miss that region, while a small subset give large deflections. The model supplies a way to infer interior structure from outgoing [angles](angle.md), a central use of scattering throughout [atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md) and [nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md). This inference does not by itself specify [electron](electron.md) [quantum states](quantum-state.md) or explain [spectral lines](spectral-line.md).

The repulsive model also sets a probing scale. At closest approach, [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) with zero radial [velocity](velocity.md) gives

$$
E=\frac{L^2}{2mr_{\min}^2}+\frac{\kappa}{r_{\min}},\qquad
r_{\min}=a+\sqrt{a^2+b^2}.
$$

For the numerical model above, even a head-on projectile turns at $r_{\min}=2a=45.504\,\mathrm{fm}$. This is a calculated closest-approach distance, not a measured [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) [radius](radius.md). A point-charge fit can constrain how compact the scattering centre must be, but cannot directly resolve structure at distances the projectiles never reach.

The [Rutherford differential cross-section](rutherford-differential-cross-section.md) must not be extrapolated to zero [angle](angle.md): the unscreened point model has a forward divergence, while real neutral [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) exhibit the [screening effect](screening-effect.md), finite beam acceptance, and accumulated small deflections. At very short separation, finite nuclear size and the [nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md) can change the interaction. [Nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md) changes the relation between laboratory and centre-of-mass [angles](angle.md) for lighter targets; appreciable [energy](energy-split.md) loss or repeated large deflections invalidates the simple [thin-foil scattering counts](thin-foil-scattering-counts.md). These limits delimit the calculation rather than supplying evidence for a nuclear contact collision in the early experiment.
