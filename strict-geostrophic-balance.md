# Strict geostrophic balance

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md)

For a homogeneous [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), strict [geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md) means that the [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) is exactly balanced by the [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md), after conservative body-force potentials have been absorbed into the pressure per unit density $\pi$. It neglects [material acceleration](material-acceleration.md) rather than merely assuming that its size is small. With constant vertical rotation, $\pi_z=0$ and taking the [curl](curl.md) gives $\mathbf u_z=0$, the [Taylor–Proudman theorem](taylor-proudman-theorem.md). Nonzero cross-contour motion over sloping rigid boundaries requires small departures from this strict limit.
