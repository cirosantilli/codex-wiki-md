# Mathematical definition

↑ **Parent:** [Foundations of mathematics](foundations-of-mathematics-split.md)

A specification of the meaning of a mathematical term or notation. A structural definition specifies conditions, such as the [group](group-split.md) axioms; a definition by a [universal property](universal-property.md) characterizes an object by its arrows. Such a characterization does not by itself prove that the object exists. For example, existence of an [exponential object](exponential-object.md) is an additional property of a [category](category-split.md).
