# Special sum set

↑ **Parent:** [Subset sum problem](subset-sum-problem.md)

A finite [set](set-split.md) $A$ of [positive integers](positive-integer.md) such that nonempty [disjoint sets](disjoint-sets.md) contained in $A$ have different [sums](sum.md), and the larger subset has the larger [sum](sum.md) when their sizes differ. For $a_1<\cdots<a_n$, the size condition is equivalent to $a_1+\cdots+a_{k+1}>a_{n-k+1}+\cdots+a_n$ for $1\le k\le\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor$: the left side is the smallest possible sum of $k+1$ elements and the right side the largest sum of $k$ elements.
