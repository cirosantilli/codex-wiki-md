# Failure of nested compact image intersection without closed fibers

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous images of nested compact intersections](continuous-images-of-nested-compact-intersections.md)

Take $X=[0,1]$ with its usual topology and $Y$ the [Sierpiński space](sierpinski-space.md) with open singleton $\{1\}$. The map $f(0)=0$, $f(x)=1$ for $x>0$ is continuous, since $f^{-1}(\{1\})=(0,1]$ is relatively open in $X$. The sets $K_n=[0,1/n]$ are nonempty, nested and compact, but

$$
f\!\left(\bigcap_nK_n\right)=\{0\},\qquad\bigcap_nf(K_n)=\{0,1\}.
$$

The fiber of $1$ is not closed, which is exactly where the compact-intersection proof fails. This distinguishes the valid Hausdorff-codomain theorem from a false extension to arbitrary codomains.
