# Rotational partition function

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md)

The [rotational partition function](rotational-partition-function.md) sums [Boltzmann factors](boltzmann-factor.md) over rotational [energy levels](energy-level.md), including their degeneracies and the appropriate indistinguishability convention. For a linear [rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md) at high temperature, $q_{\rm rot}=8\pi^2IkT/(\sigma h^2)$, with [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$ and [rotational symmetry number](rotational-symmetry-number.md) $\sigma$.
