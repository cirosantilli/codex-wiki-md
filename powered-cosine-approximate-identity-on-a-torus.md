# Powered-cosine approximate identity on a torus

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate identity](approximate-identity.md)

On the [torus](torus.md) $\mathbb T^2$ with normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md), choose $c_N$ so that $\int K_N=1$. The kernel is a nonnegative [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md). Its base $2+\cos s+\cos t$ has unique maximum four at the origin. Outside any fixed neighborhood its maximum is $q<4$, while in a smaller neighborhood of positive measure it is at least some $q'>q$. Normalization consequently bounds the exterior kernel by $C(q/q')^N$, which tends to zero. [Convolution](convolution.md) with these kernels gives uniform [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md) approximation to every [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on the torus.
