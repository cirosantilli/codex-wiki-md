# Jet stream

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)

A comparatively narrow band of strong atmospheric flow. Tropospheric jets are associated with large horizontal temperature gradients through the [thermal wind](thermal-wind.md) relation; strong [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) gradients can guide [Rossby waves](rossby-wave.md). The background speed and its spatial variation control Doppler shifting and refraction of wave packets. Not every atmospheric jet has the same origin or altitude.
