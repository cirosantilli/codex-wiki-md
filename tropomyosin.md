# Tropomyosin

↑ **Parent:** [Actomyosin](actomyosin.md)

A filament-associated regulatory protein whose position along actin helps control myosin access. In skeletal muscle its coupling to [troponin](troponin.md) connects calcium regulation to cross-bridge activity.
