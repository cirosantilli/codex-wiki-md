# Normalized emittance

↑ **Parent:** [Beam emittance](beam-emittance.md)

The [geometric emittance](geometric-emittance.md) multiplied by the relativistic velocity and Lorentz factor. For paraxial acceleration without dilution, this is proportional to transverse canonical [phase-space](phase-space.md) area and remains constant. Thus [geometric emittance](geometric-emittance.md) decreases approximately as inverse beam [momentum](momentum-split.md).
