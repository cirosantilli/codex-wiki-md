# Posterior predictive probability

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md)

A posterior predictive probability averages a future-event probability over the [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md). For a [Bernoulli distribution](bernoulli-distribution.md) with unknown parameter $p$, it is $\mathbb E[p\mid\mathcal D]$. Under [Beta-binomial conjugacy](beta-binomial-conjugacy.md) with posterior $\operatorname{Beta}(a,b)$, the predictive success probability is $a/(a+b)$.
