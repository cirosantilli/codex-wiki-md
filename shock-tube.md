# Shock tube

↑ **Parent:** [Normal shock wave](normal-shock-wave.md)

A tube in which releasing an initial [pressure](pressure-split.md) difference produces a shock, often accompanied by an expansion wave and a contact surface. Spatially separated [pressure](pressure-split.md) probes can measure shock arrival speed and jump size. The shock and the contact surface must be identified separately when interpreting the downstream state.
