<h1 id="section-ii/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**Through [heterochromatin spreading](../../../../../heterochromatin-spreading.md), a locally specified silent site becomes a larger protective [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) domain.** The essential distinction is between nucleation, which identifies where repression should begin, and propagation, which extends repression beyond that initial recognition site. [H3K9 methylation](../../../../../h3k9-methylation.md) provides a particularly clear example; its mechanism should not be equated with every kind of repressive [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md).

At a nucleation site, a sequence-specific factor or a complementary small [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) recruits a [histone methyltransferase](../../../../../histone-methyltransferase.md). Modification of nearby [histone H3](../../../../../histone-h3.md) at lysine 9 creates binding sites for reader [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), including [HP1 proteins](../../../../../hp1-protein.md) or the fission-yeast counterpart [Swi6](../../../../../swi6.md). A reader can recruit a writer to additional [nucleosomes](../../../../../nucleosome.md); in [Schizosaccharomyces pombe](../../../../../schizosaccharomyces-pombe.md), [Clr4](../../../../../clr4.md) itself combines [H3K9 methylation](../../../../../h3k9-methylation.md) activity with a methyl-mark-binding [chromodomain](../../../../../chromodomain.md). The resulting [positive feedback](../../../../../positive-feedback.md) allows an established patch to favor modification of surrounding [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md).[Discussion: Clr4 chromodomain requirement and reader–writer mechanism](../../../../../epigenetic-inheritance-uncoupled-from-sequence-specific-recruitment-by-kaushik-ragunathan-and-others.md) Recruitment of [histone deacetylases](../../../../../histone-deacetylase.md) and other repressive factors further reduces accessibility to [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) machinery. The causal scheme is

$$
\text{target recognition}\longrightarrow\text{local mark}\longrightarrow\text{reader recruitment}\longrightarrow\text{additional local marking}.
$$

The last two steps can repeat. This is an interaction network operating on dynamic [nucleosomes](../../../../../nucleosome.md), rather than a mark physically crawling along [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md).

Propagation can occur through nearby contacts along the [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) and contacts created by three-dimensional folding. Consequently, a domain need not advance as a perfectly uniform front. At the engineered mammalian locus studied using inducible [HP1](../../../../../hp1-protein.md) recruitment, [H3K9 methylation](../../../../../h3k9-methylation.md) and silencing extended beyond the recruitment site, while persistence depended on cellular context and additional stabilizing mechanisms.[Abstract and Results: inducible HP1 recruitment, domain propagation and context-dependent memory](../../../../../dynamics-and-memory-of-heterochromatin-in-living-cells-by-nathaniel-a-hathaway-and-others.md) This separates experimentally inducing a domain from merely finding correlated marks near an already silent [gene](../../../../../gene.md).

Spreading must be limited. Elements such as [chromatin boundaries](../../../../../chromatin-boundary.md), active [gene promoters](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md), [histone acetylation](../../../../../histone-acetylation.md), loss of modified [histones](../../../../../histone.md) through turnover and opposing [histone demethylases](../../../../../histone-demethylase.md) can interrupt the reinforcing cycle. A boundary can prevent access, recruit an antagonistic activity or maintain an unfavorable local substrate. Not every domain therefore requires a single physical barrier; propagation can also stop where mark deposition balances removal. During [DNA replication](../../../../../dna-replication.md), parental modified [histones](../../../../../histone.md) are diluted among new ones. Reader–writer feedback can restore a local state, but retention of a mark through one division does not establish indefinite [epigenetic inheritance](../../../../../epigenetic-inheritance.md). Recurrent nucleation may still be needed. Uncontrolled spreading risks silencing useful neighboring [genes](../../../../../gene.md), so genome defense requires both recognition specificity and restraint.

A well-defined targeting mechanism comes from [RNA interference](../../../../../rna-interference.md) at the pericentromeric repeats of [Schizosaccharomyces pombe](../../../../../schizosaccharomyces-pombe.md). Repeat transcripts enter a pathway involving [Dicer](../../../../../dicer.md), [RNA-dependent RNA polymerase](../../../../../rna-dependent-rna-polymerase.md) and [Argonaute](../../../../../argonaute.md). Small interfering [RNAs](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) in the [RITS complex](../../../../../rits-complex.md) recognize complementary nascent repeat transcripts; coupling to [H3K9 methylation](../../../../../h3k9-methylation.md) and [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) binding reinforces silencing at the corresponding locus. The original identification of [RITS](../../../../../rits-complex.md) physically connected small [RNAs](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), [Argonaute](../../../../../argonaute.md) and a [chromodomain](../../../../../chromodomain.md) component.[Abstract and Results: RITS composition and association with heterochromatic loci](../../../../../rnai-mediated-targeting-of-heterochromatin-by-the-rits-complex-by-andre-verdel-and-others.md) Some repeat [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) is therefore compatible with, and can help renew, a repressive domain. Complete elimination of all transcription is not a prerequisite for protection.

This defends the [genome](../../../../../genome.md) in two related ways. Repressing [transposable elements](../../../../../transposable-element.md) reduces production of the [RNAs](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) and [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) required for mobilization, limiting insertional [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md) and inappropriate recombination. Protecting repetitive regions also supports orderly [chromosome segregation](../../../../../chromosome-segregation.md). In fission yeast, loss of core [RNA interference](../../../../../rna-interference.md) components reduced centromeric [H3K9 methylation](../../../../../h3k9-methylation.md), relieved silencing and impaired centromere function.[Abstract and Results: RNAi mutants, centromeric repeats, H3K9 methylation and centromere function](../../../../../regulation-of-heterochromatic-silencing-and-histone-h3-lysine-9-methylation-by-rnai-by-thomas-a-volpe-and-others.md) These observations support a protective role for repeat-associated [heterochromatin](../../../../../heterochromatin.md), although a segregation defect alone would not prove a particular spreading mechanism.

Mammalian defense can use a different nucleation system. Sequence-specific [KRAB zinc-finger proteins](../../../../../krab-zinc-finger-protein.md) can recruit [KAP1](../../../../../kap1.md), which supports recruitment of [SETDB1](../../../../../setdb1.md) and other repressive machinery at [endogenous retroviruses](../../../../../endogenous-retrovirus.md). A small recognition region can thereby organize repression over a longer element. In mouse [embryonic stem cells](../../../../../embryonic-stem-cell.md), removal of [KAP1](../../../../../kap1.md) derepressed particular [endogenous retroviruses](../../../../../endogenous-retrovirus.md) and reduced their associated [H3K9 methylation](../../../../../h3k9-methylation.md); [DNA methylation](../../../../../dna-methylation.md) provided a cooperating layer of repression.[Abstract and Results: endogenous retroviral derepression and loss of H3K9me3 after KAP1 deletion](../../../../../kap1-controls-endogenous-retroviruses-in-embryonic-stem-cells-by-helen-m-rowe-and-others.md) The relevant factors and their relative importance vary with species, developmental state and element family; the fission-yeast pathway is not a universal mammalian template.

To demonstrate protective spreading directly, recruit a writer to one defined site beside several spaced [reporter genes](../../../../../reporter-gene.md), then follow mark distribution, nascent [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) and reporter output over time. Compare a catalytically inactive writer, a reader-binding [mutant](../../../../../mutant.md), a non-targeting recruitment control and insertion or deletion of a [chromatin boundary](../../../../../chromatin-boundary.md). Remove the initiating recruitment to distinguish continual establishment from inherited maintenance. Analyze individual [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) because a population-average gradient could represent different all-or-none domain lengths. Finally, measure the proposed defense itself: new [transposable element](../../../../../transposable-element.md) insertions, inappropriate recombination or [chromosome segregation](../../../../../chromosome-segregation.md), alongside [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) abundance. **A larger silent domain is mechanistic evidence; reduced genome damage is the functional test.**
