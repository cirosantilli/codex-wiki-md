<h1 id="section-ii/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**First establish whether X changes where [RNA polymerase II](../../../../../rna-polymerase-ii.md) initiates, rather than merely changing which initiated [RNAs](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) survive.** A dominant [transcription start site](../../../../../transcription-start-site.md) can shift without becoming less precise, and a population can appear broader because the proportions of two pre-existing start sites change. Treat altered position, within-promoter dispersion and switching between distinct [gene promoters](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md) as different phenotypes.

Use a dose and short time course of X with vehicle, biological replicates, washout and viability measurements. Map capped $5'$ ends by [CAGE](../../../../../cage.md) and independently map newly initiated [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) with [GRO-cap](../../../../../gro-cap.md) or another suitably validated nascent-start assay. [GRO-cap](../../../../../gro-cap.md) has been used to characterize initiation regions in mammalian [gene promoters](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md) and [enhancers](../../../../../enhancer.md).[Abstract and Methods: GRO-cap mapping of transcription initiation](../../../../../analysis-of-nascent-rna-identifies-a-unified-architecture-of-initiation-regions-at-mammalian-promoters-and-enhancers-by-leighton-j-core-and-others.md) Confirm selected $5'$ ends with an independent targeted method that distinguishes capped starts from processed [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) ends. Use external standards and control cap recovery; if X inhibits capping, a capped-end assay can lose genuine starts. Measure total nascent output separately from the start-position distribution, and compare libraries at matched effective coverage because low counts can make a broad [gene promoter](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md) appear artificially focused.

Within a fixed [gene promoter](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md) window, let $p_i$ be the fraction of initiation events at coordinate $x_i$. Quantify dominant position, central width and, if useful, [Shannon entropy](../../../../../information-entropy-split.md)

$$
\mu=\sum_i p_i x_i,\qquad \sigma^2=\sum_i p_i(x_i-\mu)^2,\qquad H=-\sum_i p_i\log p_i.
$$

A one-base shift of a single occupied start changes $\mu$ while leaving $\sigma^2=H=0$; splitting equally between two positions increases dispersion and gives $H=\ln 2$. Compare complete distributions with sampling uncertainty rather than interpreting one summary alone. Analyze separate [gene promoters](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md) independently, stratifying by sequence architecture and baseline output. If only mature [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) changes while nascent initiation remains fixed, investigate differential decay, capping, processing or selective [transcript isoform](../../../../../transcript-isoform.md) stabilization before claiming altered initiation.

One mechanism is altered recognition or placement of the [preinitiation complex](../../../../../preinitiation-complex.md). [TFIID](../../../../../tfiid.md), including [TATA-binding protein](../../../../../tata-binding-protein.md) and associated subunits, and [TFIIB](../../../../../tfiib.md) help position [RNA polymerase II](../../../../../rna-polymerase-ii.md) relative to the [core promoter](../../../../../core-promoter.md). X could disturb the [TATA box](../../../../../tata-box.md), [initiator element](../../../../../initiator-element.md) recognition, factor association or their geometry. Precise positioning of the human [initiator element](../../../../../initiator-element.md) at focused starts makes sequence-dependent sensitivity a useful test.[Abstract and Results: focused promoters and initiator positioning](../../../../../the-human-initiator-is-a-distinct-and-abundant-element-that-is-precisely-positioned-in-focused-core-promoters-by-long-vo-ngoc-and-others.md) Map factor occupancy, compare X-sensitive and insensitive [core promoters](../../../../../core-promoter.md), and use matched reporter constructs differing only in an [initiator element](../../../../../initiator-element.md), [TATA box](../../../../../tata-box.md) or their spacing. A reporter must have its $5'$ ends mapped: light output alone cannot reveal an altered [transcription start site](../../../../../transcription-start-site.md). Reconstitute initiation on naked [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) with purified factors and add X. Reproduction of the start redistribution without [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) would support a direct effect on the initiation machinery.

A second mechanism is altered [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) opening, template positioning or initial [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) synthesis. X might affect the [XPB](../../../../../xpb.md) translocase within [TFIIH](../../../../../tfiih.md), [RNA polymerase II](../../../../../rna-polymerase-ii.md) active-site function, or [TFIIB](../../../../../tfiib.md)-dependent selection of the first templating bases. Measure open-complex formation by an appropriate [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) accessibility or chemical-reactivity assay, initial short [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) products and precise starts under controlled [nucleoside triphosphate](../../../../../nucleoside-triphosphate.md) concentrations. A pre-opened [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) template can help distinguish a defect in opening from one that persists after opening. [triptolide](../../../../../triptolide.md) supplies a biochemical precedent for a small molecule targeting [XPB](../../../../../xpb.md), but does not establish that X shares that target or that every [XPB](../../../../../xpb.md) inhibitor broadens starts.[Abstract and biochemical target-identification experiments](../../../../../xpb-a-subunit-of-tfiih-is-a-target-of-the-natural-product-triptolide-by-denis-v-titov-and-others.md) Mammalian initiation should be tested directly; extensive scanning rules established in a different organism cannot simply be assumed to apply.

A third mechanism is a change in the [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) landscape or [DNA supercoiling](../../../../../dna-supercoiling.md). Altered [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) positioning could expose cryptic [gene promoters](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md), while changed [histone acetylation](../../../../../histone-acetylation.md) or [chromatin remodelling](../../../../../chromatin-remodelling.md) could broaden the region accessible to the [preinitiation complex](../../../../../preinitiation-complex.md). Map [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) accessibility, [nucleosomes](../../../../../nucleosome.md) and relevant [histone](../../../../../histone.md) modifications before and soon after X treatment. Compare naked and nucleosomal [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) templates carrying the same [core promoter](../../../../../core-promoter.md); mutate the proposed [chromatin remodelling](../../../../../chromatin-remodelling.md) factor or use acute depletion to test whether it is required. For a proposed [topoisomerase](../../../../../topoisomerase.md) effect, compare relaxed and supercoiled templates and measure [DNA supercoiling](../../../../../dna-supercoiling.md) directly. Occupancy changes that follow initiation changes can be consequences, so temporal order matters.

A fourth route is indirect alteration of factor supply or activity: X might change [nucleoside triphosphate](../../../../../nucleoside-triphosphate.md) pools, [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) of initiation machinery, or the abundance of a limiting [transcription factor](../../../../../transcription-factor.md). Measure candidate metabolites and [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) modifications, test rapid versus delayed onset, and restore the proposed limiting input. Altered promoter-proximal pausing can change the recovery of nascent products without moving the original start; compare initiation-specific maps with downstream engaged-[RNA polymerase II](../../../../../rna-polymerase-ii.md) profiles. A change that disappears after controlling [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) lifetime or pausing is not evidence for loss of start-site selection.

Finally, identify the direct molecular target through binding or engagement assays and a resistant [mutant](../../../../../mutant.md) that retains normal baseline function. Require the resistant version to rescue the start-position phenotype at an X concentration that affects the normal version; overexpression rescue alone can reflect sequestration of the compound. An acute genetic perturbation should phenocopy X, and purified biochemical assays should reproduce the relevant effect. **Convergent evidence from nascent starts, controlled templates and target-specific rescue distinguishes direct selection defects from secondary changes in [gene expression](../../../../../gene-expression.md).**
