<h1 id="section-ii/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**During [amino acid starvation](../../../../../amino-acid-starvation.md), cells reduce bulk [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) while selectively promoting an adaptive response.** It has both a direct substrate effect on elongation and regulated effects on initiation. Their balance depends on which [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) is limiting, how severely it is depleted, the organism and the duration of deprivation.

The immediate substrate problem is reduced charging of the relevant [transfer RNAs](../../../../../transfer-rna.md) by [aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases](../../../../../aminoacyl-trna-synthetase.md). A [ribosome](../../../../../ribosome.md) encountering a cognate [codon](../../../../../codon.md) waits longer for an appropriate charged adaptor, potentially creating queues or [ribosome collisions](../../../../../ribosome-collision.md). The response is not necessarily identical at every synonymous [codon](../../../../../codon.md), because [transfer RNA](../../../../../transfer-rna.md) isoacceptors, their charging and their competition differ. In mammalian [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), comparative [ribosome profiling](../../../../../ribosome-profiling.md) under particular [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) limitations demonstrated selective [codon](../../../../../codon.md)-associated pausing rather than an invariant slowdown at every sense [codon](../../../../../codon.md).[Summary and Results: amino-acid-specific pausing, tRNA charging and translational responses](../../../../../translational-control-through-differential-ribosome-pausing-during-amino-acid-limitation-in-mammalian-cells-by-alicia-m-darnell-and-others.md) Persistent stalls can engage [ribosome-associated quality control](../../../../../ribosome-associated-quality-control.md) to rescue stalled machinery and dispose of incomplete products. Increased [ribosome](../../../../../ribosome.md) occupancy at a site therefore need not mean increased productive synthesis.

In [eukaryotes](../../../../../eukaryote.md), [GCN2](../../../../../gcn2.md) links [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) limitation to the [integrated stress response](../../../../../integrated-stress-response.md). Deacylated [transfer RNA](../../../../../transfer-rna.md) and signals from starved [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md), supported by [GCN1](../../../../../gcn1.md), promote [GCN2](../../../../../gcn2.md) activation.[Abstract and Results: ribosome-dependent activation and relationship to translational stress](../../../../../activation-of-gcn2-by-the-ribosomal-p-stalk-by-alison-j-inglis-and-others.md) Its [protein kinase](../../../../../protein-kinase.md) activity phosphorylates the alpha subunit of [eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md) at serine 51. Normal [eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md) brings initiating [methionyl-tRNA](../../../../../methionyl-trna.md) to the small [ribosomal subunit](../../../../../ribosomal-subunit.md) as part of a ternary complex:

$$
\mathrm{eIF2\!\cdot\!GTP\!\cdot\!Met\!\! -\!tRNA_i}.
$$

After initiation, [eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md) must exchange bound [GDP](../../../../../guanosine-diphosphate.md) for [GTP](../../../../../guanosine-triphosphate.md), a reaction promoted by [eIF2B](../../../../../eif2b.md). Phosphorylated [eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md) inhibits this recycling activity, reducing ternary-complex supply and initiation on many [messenger RNAs](../../../../../messenger-rna.md). This conserves scarce substrates and limits addition of further [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md) to already stressed templates. Genetic experiments established that [GCN2](../../../../../gcn2.md)-dependent regulation can repress bulk synthesis while increasing production of [ATF4](../../../../../atf4.md).[Abstract and Results: eIF2 kinase dependence and selective ATF4 expression](../../../../../regulated-translation-initiation-controls-stress-induced-gene-expression-in-mammalian-cells-by-heather-p-harding-and-others.md)

The apparent paradox is resolved by [upstream open reading frames](../../../../../upstream-open-reading-frame.md). In the canonical mammalian [ATF4](../../../../../atf4.md) leader, a short permissive [upstream open reading frame](../../../../../upstream-open-reading-frame.md) is followed by an inhibitory one that overlaps the [ATF4](../../../../../atf4.md) coding region in a different [reading frame](../../../../../reading-frame.md). When ternary complex is plentiful, scanning small [ribosomal subunits](../../../../../ribosomal-subunit.md) that have completed the first short frame reacquire it rapidly and initiate at the inhibitory frame. After [eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md) [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md), reacquisition is delayed: some scanning subunits pass that inhibitory start and become competent before the downstream [ATF4](../../../../../atf4.md) start. **Reduced initiation capacity can therefore increase initiation at a particular downstream coding region.** Experiments using [upstream open reading frame](../../../../../upstream-open-reading-frame.md) [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md) and reporter assays directly tested this reinitiation mechanism.[Abstract and Results: uORF mutations, spacing and delayed reinitiation](../../../../../reinitiation-involving-upstream-orfs-regulates-atf4-mrna-translation-in-mammalian-cells-by-krishna-m-vattem-and-ronald-c-wek.md) The increase is not unlimited: if ternary complex becomes too scarce, initiation at [ATF4](../../../../../atf4.md) also fails.

[ATF4](../../../../../atf4.md) then changes [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) of adaptive [genes](../../../../../gene.md), including those supporting [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) supply, transport and stress management. Yeast uses the related [GCN2](../../../../../gcn2.md)–[eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md) logic to increase [GCN4](../../../../../gcn4.md) production through a leader with several [upstream open reading frames](../../../../../upstream-open-reading-frame.md); it should not be drawn as the identical two-frame [ATF4](../../../../../atf4.md) arrangement. The response can recover after replenishment through restoration of charging and dephosphorylation of [eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md). Prolonged, unresolved deprivation can instead favor damage and [apoptosis](../../../../../apoptosis.md). These later [gene expression](../../../../../gene-expression.md) changes complement the initial translational response rather than explaining its earliest onset.

A parallel mammalian pathway regulates [mTORC1](../../../../../mtorc1.md). In nutrient-replete conditions, [mTORC1](../../../../../mtorc1.md) promotes cap-dependent initiation partly by phosphorylating [4E-BP proteins](../../../../../4e-bp-protein.md) and activating [S6 kinase](../../../../../s6-kinase.md). Phosphorylated [4E-BP proteins](../../../../../4e-bp-protein.md) release [eIF4E](../../../../../eif4e.md), allowing it to associate with [eIF4G](../../../../../eif4g.md) and assemble an active [eIF4F](../../../../../eif4f.md) initiation complex. [amino acid starvation](../../../../../amino-acid-starvation.md) reduces [mTORC1](../../../../../mtorc1.md) activity, allowing hypophosphorylated [4E-BP proteins](../../../../../4e-bp-protein.md) to bind [eIF4E](../../../../../eif4e.md) and restrain initiation. A concrete upstream sensor is [Sestrin2](../../../../../sestrin2.md): loss of [leucine](../../../../../leucine.md) promotes its inhibitory interaction with [GATOR2](../../../../../gator2.md), influencing [Rag GTPases](../../../../../rag-gtpase.md) and nutrient-dependent recruitment of [mTORC1](../../../../../mtorc1.md) to the [lysosome](../../../../../lysosome.md).[Abstract and Results: leucine binding, sensor mutants and GATOR2 interactions](../../../../../sestrin2-is-a-leucine-sensor-for-the-mtorc1-pathway-by-rachel-l-wolfson-and-others.md) This route measures nutrient sufficiency through a different mechanism from the [GCN2](../../../../../gcn2.md) pathway; neither should be reduced to the other.

Reduced [mTORC1](../../../../../mtorc1.md) signaling also helps limit investment in [ribosome](../../../../../ribosome.md) production and permits [autophagy](../../../../../autophagy.md), which can recycle cellular material to supply [amino acids](../../../../../amino-acid.md). These adaptations operate on different timescales from waiting for a missing charged [transfer RNA](../../../../../transfer-rna.md). The outcome is selective redistribution of translational capacity, not simply uniform cessation of every [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) synthesis event. Some cap-dependent transcripts are especially sensitive to initiation-factor availability, while stress-regulated leaders can favor others.

To distinguish the mechanisms, compare a defined single-[amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) withdrawal with matched complete medium and replenishment. Control residual [amino acids](../../../../../amino-acid.md) in supplements and measure viability. Measure global nascent [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) synthesis, [transfer RNA](../../../../../transfer-rna.md) charging, [eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md) [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md), [ATF4](../../../../../atf4.md) synthesis, and [mTORC1](../../../../../mtorc1.md) substrate [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) over time. Combine [ribosome profiling](../../../../../ribosome-profiling.md) with [RNA sequencing](../../../../../rna-sequencing.md) and external normalization; otherwise globally reduced synthesis can disappear through library normalization. Compare normal [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) with [GCN2](../../../../../gcn2.md) loss or a nonphosphorylatable [eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md) variant, and use [ATF4](../../../../../atf4.md) leader reporters with separately mutated [upstream open reading frames](../../../../../upstream-open-reading-frame.md). Independently perturb [4E-BP proteins](../../../../../4e-bp-protein.md) or [mTORC1](../../../../../mtorc1.md) signaling. Recovery of initiation signaling without recovery of elongation would show that substrate shortage still limits output. A loss of large [polysomes](../../../../../polysome.md) suggests reduced initiation, whereas maintained or increased occupancy can accompany stalled elongation; neither pattern alone measures productive [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) synthesis.

In [bacteria](../../../../../bacteria.md), a related sensing principle feeds a different control system: in [Escherichia coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md), [RelA](../../../../../rela.md) recognizes starved [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md) containing deacylated [transfer RNA](../../../../../transfer-rna.md) and synthesizes [(p)ppGpp](../../../../../p-ppgpp.md), initiating the [stringent response](../../../../../stringent-response.md). Structural work explains how the starved [ribosome](../../../../../ribosome.md) activates [RelA](../../../../../rela.md).[Abstract and structural Results: deacylated tRNA, RelA activation and alarmone synthesis](../../../../../ribosome-dependent-activation-of-stringent-control-by-alan-brown-and-others.md) The [stringent response](../../../../../stringent-response.md) reallocates [gene expression](../../../../../gene-expression.md) and restrains investment in growth machinery; it is not an [eIF2](../../../../../eif2.md)-based mechanism. **Across these systems, the common strategy is to lower demand while preserving or increasing the capacity to restore [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) supply.**
