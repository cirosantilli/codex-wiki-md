**The regulation of [chloroplast gene expression](../../../../../chloroplast-gene-expression.md) includes substantial local control but depends on a largely nuclear-specified apparatus.** Distinguish where regulation occurs from where its regulator is encoded. A response executed rapidly inside the [chloroplast](../../../../../chloroplast.md) can use pre-existing, imported [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) and thus be operationally local while remaining genetically dependent on the [cell nucleus](../../../../../cell-nucleus.md). The clearest examples below concern land-plant [chloroplasts](../../../../../chloroplast.md); details vary among [plastids](../../../../../plastid.md) and [algae](../../../../../algae.md).

The [chloroplast genome](../../../../../chloroplast-genome.md) retains a limited set of [genes](../../../../../gene.md) for core [photosynthesis](../../../../../photosynthesis.md) components and its own expression machinery, including [ribosomal RNA](../../../../../ribosomal-rna.md), [transfer RNA](../../../../../transfer-rna.md), some [ribosomal proteins](../../../../../ribosomal-protein.md) and [RNA polymerase](../../../../../rna-polymerase.md) subunits. It supplies its own templates, local [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) and [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md). However, many components of those same systems, and most regulatory [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), are encoded in the nuclear [genome](../../../../../genome.md), synthesized on cytosolic [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md) and delivered by [organelle protein import](../../../../../organelle-protein-import.md). Retaining [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) therefore confers neither complete biochemical independence nor a self-contained regulatory program.

At [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md), the two major polymerase systems make the mixed control particularly apparent. [plastid-encoded RNA polymerase](../../../../../plastid-encoded-rna-polymerase.md), or [PEP](../../../../../plastid-encoded-rna-polymerase.md), contains bacterial-type core subunits encoded by plastid [genes](../../../../../gene.md), whereas [nuclear-encoded plastid RNA polymerase](../../../../../nuclear-encoded-plastid-rna-polymerase.md), or [NEP](../../../../../nuclear-encoded-plastid-rna-polymerase.md), is a nuclear-specified phage-type [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md). Their [gene promoter](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md) preferences differ and some [genes](../../../../../gene.md) have access to both systems; a simple photosynthetic-versus-housekeeping division has exceptions. Experiments with tobacco plastids lacking functional [PEP](../../../../../plastid-encoded-rna-polymerase.md) distinguished promoter classes and demonstrated continuing transcription by the other machinery.[Abstract and Results: plastid polymerase mutants and distinct promoter usage](../../../../../the-two-rna-polymerases-encoded-by-the-nuclear-and-the-plastid-compartments-transcribe-distinct-groups-of-genes-in-tobacco-plastids-by-p-t-hajdukiewicz-and-others.md) The core of [PEP](../../../../../plastid-encoded-rna-polymerase.md) is plastid encoded, but promoter-selecting [sigma factors](../../../../../sigma-factor.md) and important associated [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) are nuclear encoded. Even this apparently autonomous polymerase is embedded in nuclear control.

Nuclear regulation is therefore able to connect environmental and developmental signals to plastid [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md). In [Arabidopsis thaliana](../../../../../arabidopsis-thaliana.md), [SIG5](../../../../../sig5.md) contributes to activation of the blue-light-responsive [psbD gene promoter](../../../../../psbd-gene-promoter.md). The experiment provides a specific route from a nuclear-encoded [sigma factor](../../../../../sigma-factor.md) to expression of a chloroplast [gene](../../../../../gene.md), rather than merely correlating total greenness with nuclear state.[Abstract and Results: SIG5-dependent promoter activation](../../../../../the-multiple-stress-responsive-plastid-sigma-factor-sig5-directs-activation-of-the-psbd-blue-light-responsive-promoter-blrp-in-arabidopsis-thaliana-by-akitomo-nagashima-and-others.md) Such [anterograde signaling](../../../../../anterograde-signaling.md) can alter regulator availability, import or activity. Measuring total steady-state [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) alone would not establish which of those steps changed.

Nuclear influence is particularly extensive after [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md). Plastid transcripts often require cleavage, end maturation, [RNA splicing](../../../../../rna-splicing.md) and [RNA editing](../../../../../rna-editing.md); their stability and access to [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md) are also regulated. Nuclear-encoded [pentatricopeptide repeat proteins](../../../../../pentatricopeptide-repeat-protein.md) supply sequence-specific recognition for many of these events, although not every member has the same function. [PPR10](../../../../../ppr10.md) is a concrete stability mechanism: binding at a defined intergenic region can block degradation from either direction and define stable adjacent [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) ends.[Abstract and Results: PPR10 binding, overlapping transcript termini and barriers to exonucleolytic decay](../../../../../site-specific-binding-of-a-ppr-protein-defines-and-stabilizes-5%E2%80%B2-and-3%E2%80%B2-mrna-termini-in-chloroplasts-by-jeannette-pfalz-and-others.md) The functional instruction is a nuclear-encoded [RNA-binding protein](../../../../../rna-binding-protein.md) acting directly on a plastid transcript. The local [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) sequence is still essential because it determines where that instruction can act.

At [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md), chloroplast [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md) and their [RNAs](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) operate locally but rely on many imported initiation, elongation and transcript-specific factors. [RNA secondary structure](../../../../../rna-secondary-structure.md), transcript processing and binding of a nuclear-encoded regulator can expose or occlude the initiation region. Assembly of a complex can feed back on synthesis of its own components, preventing wasteful production of an unassembled subunit. This is useful because the final [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) complex can contain products of both nuclear and chloroplast [genes](../../../../../gene.md); neither [genome](../../../../../genome.md)'s output alone determines the final stoichiometry.

Local control is illustrated by synthesis of [photosystem II](../../../../../photosystem-ii.md) [D1 protein](../../../../../d1-protein.md) from chloroplast [psbA messenger RNA](../../../../../psba-messenger-rna.md). Light damages [D1 protein](../../../../../d1-protein.md), increasing demand for replacement during [photosystem II repair](../../../../../photosystem-ii-repair.md). Experiments separating photodamage from photosynthetic energy supply supported an assembly-linked circuit that changes recruitment of [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md) to [psbA messenger RNA](../../../../../psba-messenger-rna.md). This involves nuclear-encoded assembly and expression factors, including [HCF244](../../../../../hcf244.md), and demonstrates how imported machinery can implement a rapid local response.[Abstract and Results: photodamage, ribosome recruitment and assembly-linked autoregulation](../../../../../light-induced-psba-translation-in-plants-is-triggered-by-photosystem-ii-damage-via-an-assembly-linked-autoregulatory-circuit-by-prakitchai-chotewutmontri-and-alice-barkan.md) It would be too strong to infer that all light regulation has a single cause, or that this result establishes an identical circuit in every photosynthetic organism.

The nucleus also receives information back. In [retrograde signaling](../../../../../retrograde-signaling.md), the state of plastid expression, metabolism or stress alters nuclear [gene expression](../../../../../gene-expression.md), helping coordinate the two compartments. Genetic analysis of plastid [sigma factors](../../../../../sigma-factor.md) and [GUN1](../../../../../gun1.md) provides evidence that defects in plastid expression can affect nuclear photosynthetic [gene expression](../../../../../gene-expression.md).[Abstract and Results: sigma-factor mutants, GUN1 and regulation of nuclear photosynthesis-associated genes](../../../../../sigma-factor-mediated-plastid-retrograde-signals-control-nuclear-gene-expression-by-jesse-d-woodson-and-others.md) [GUN1](../../../../../gun1.md) should not be presented as a universal exported signal or as proof that all proposed plastid stress signals share one molecular route. These feedbacks make the two regulatory systems mutually coupled, rather than placing the [chloroplast](../../../../../chloroplast.md) at the end of a purely one-way command chain.

Test the degree of local control using a short perturbation in isolated intact [chloroplasts](../../../../../chloroplast.md), or a controlled change in illumination in intact [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), and follow nascent [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), newly synthesized [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) and complex assembly. Check preparation purity, membrane integrity and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) availability. A rapid response without new nuclear [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) supports operation by an existing local apparatus; it does not show independence of nuclear-encoded [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) already present. Conversely, acute depletion of a nuclear regulator followed by rescue with an import-competent or import-defective version tests whether its action inside the [chloroplast](../../../../../chloroplast.md) is necessary. Manipulating the matching plastid [cis-regulatory element](../../../../../cis-regulatory-element.md) can identify its direct target.

Finally, measure each layer separately. A fall in transcript abundance may reflect less [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) or faster decay; fewer assembled [photosystems](../../../../../photosystem.md) may reflect defective synthesis, import, assembly or increased turnover. Pair plastid run-on measurements with [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) decay assays, [ribosome profiling](../../../../../ribosome-profiling.md), nascent-[protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) labelling and assembly measurements. Long-term inhibition of cytosolic [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) or loss of a nuclear [gene](../../../../../gene.md) can disrupt the entire [organelle](../../../../../organelle.md), so use early time points and appropriate rescue to distinguish primary regulation from secondary damage. **Autonomy is strongest for immediate local sensing and execution, while the nucleus specifies much of the capacity, specificity and long-term developmental program that makes that local response possible.**
