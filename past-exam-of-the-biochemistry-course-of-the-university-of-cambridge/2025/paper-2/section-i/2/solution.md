<h1 id="section-i/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**Separate three causal steps: recognition of the modified [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), occupancy of the target [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md), and reduction of its degradation rate.** Call the newly identified [RNA-binding protein](../../../../../rna-binding-protein.md) R. Its recovery in a screen need not mean it contacts the modified [ribonucleotide](../../../../../ribonucleotide.md) directly; it could recognize a modification-dependent structure or arrive through another [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). Likewise, more target [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) could reflect increased [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) rather than greater stability.

First reproduce the interaction using endogenous R and the endogenous target. Determine R's [protein domains](../../../../../protein-domain.md), expression and subcellular distribution, then use reciprocal [RNA immunoprecipitation](../../../../../rna-immunoprecipitation.md) and [crosslinking and immunoprecipitation](../../../../../crosslinking-and-immunoprecipitation.md) to locate the occupied region. Normalize enrichment to input target abundance and R recovery, and include unrelated [RNAs](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), a non-specific [antibody](../../../../../antibody.md) and cells lacking R. Crosslink-based occupancy supports close contact, whereas recovery without crosslinking can preserve indirect complexes. Determine whether the relevant modification actually occurs near the binding site: [mass spectrometry](../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md) of purified target fragments can establish chemical identity, supplemented by a modification-specific mapping assay with known modified and unmodified standards. Bulk [mass spectrometry](../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md) of all cellular [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) cannot assign a modification to this particular transcript or position. Do not assume the modification is [N6-methyladenosine](../../../../../n6-methyladenosine.md) merely because it is familiar.

Prepare short synthetic [RNAs](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) identical in [nucleotide sequence](../../../../../nucleic-acid-sequence.md), length, ends and labelling except for the single modified [ribonucleotide](../../../../../ribonucleotide.md). Measure binding of purified R over a concentration range by [electrophoretic mobility shift assay](../../../../../electrophoretic-mobility-shift-assay.md) or [fluorescence anisotropy](../../../../../fluorescence-anisotropy.md), with competition by unlabelled cognate and unrelated [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md). The [dissociation constant](../../../../../dissociation-constant.md) distinguishes a quantitative preference from a binary pull-down impression. Verify that R is folded and sufficiently pure; include an RNA-binding-defective R [mutant](../../../../../mutant.md), several surrounding sequences and different modification positions. Modification-specific recovery from a cellular extract can disappear with purified R, revealing a missing partner rather than disproving the original screen.

Distinguish chemical recognition from structural recognition. Compare [RNA secondary structure](../../../../../rna-secondary-structure.md) with and without the modification by [RNA structure probing](../../../../../rna-structure-probing.md). Then disrupt the stem without changing the binding motif, or restore pairing through compensatory [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md). If an unmodified but constitutively exposed motif binds R, and restoring occlusion suppresses binding even when the modification remains, an [RNA modification switch](../../../../../rna-modification-switch.md) is plausible. Direct recognition is supported when modification discrimination persists after accessibility is matched and is lost upon a specific R binding-pocket [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md). Structural work can identify the contacts, but binding and structure controls remain essential. Modification-dependent exposure of a binding motif has an experimental precedent in the [N6-methyladenosine](../../../../../n6-methyladenosine.md)-dependent [RNA modification switch](../../../../../rna-modification-switch.md) for [HNRNPC](../../../../../hnrnpc.md).[Abstract; Figures 1–3: structural accessibility, binding and methylation dependence](../../../../../n6-methyladenosine-dependent-rna-structural-switches-regulate-rna-protein-interactions-by-nian-liu-and-others.md)

Establish stability by acute depletion of R, followed by a [metabolic RNA labelling](../../../../../metabolic-rna-labelling.md) pulse and chase. Measure loss of labelled target molecules using appropriate spike-ins and a time course spanning the expected [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md), while independently estimating newly synthesized target [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md). [SLAM-seq](../../../../../slam-seq.md) is one possible implementation: chemical treatment makes incorporated [4-thiouridine](../../../../../4-thiouridine.md) detectable through characteristic sequencing changes.[Abstract: metabolic RNA labelling and sequencing-based measurement of expression dynamics](../../../../../thiol-linked-alkylation-of-rna-to-assess-expression-dynamics-by-veronika-a-herzog-and-others.md) Include unlabelled controls, check label toxicity and correct for label reutilization or incomplete chase. When reporting molecules per [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), separate dilution through increasing cell number from chemical degradation. A [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) inhibitor time course provides an orthogonal check, but broad inhibition itself can change decay. Under a constant first-order degradation model, labelled molecules obey

$$
L(t)=L(0)e^{-k_{\mathrm d}t},\qquad t_{1/2}=\frac{\ln 2}{k_{\mathrm d}}.
$$

A shorter [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md) after R depletion, reversed by normal R but not its RNA-binding-defective [mutant](../../../../../mutant.md), establishes the proposed effect. Allow a more complex fit if the [exponential decay](../../../../../exponential-decay.md) model is inconsistent with the measured time course. At steady state, abundance is synthesis rate divided by degradation rate, so abundance alone cannot identify which rate changed.

Use a controlled target [reporter gene](../../../../../reporter-gene.md) carrying the occupied region, then remove or mutate that region and measure reporter [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) decay. Preserve [polyadenylation](../../../../../polyadenylation.md), [RNA splicing](../../../../../rna-splicing.md) and coding potential where possible, and distinguish site loss from altered [RNA secondary structure](../../../../../rna-secondary-structure.md). Manipulate the relevant modification-producing [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) acutely, rescuing with normal or catalytically inactive [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md); verify target-site modification after the manipulation. Cross the two perturbations: if R stabilizes the target specifically through the modification, removing the modification should reduce R occupancy and eliminate or substantially weaken the extra effect of removing R. This [epistasis](../../../../../epistasis.md) supports a shared pathway, but floor effects or redundant sites can obscure it. A site-specific chemically modified reporter helps separate local recognition from the many indirect consequences of removing a global modification [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md).

Identify the affected decay step using [poly(A) tail](../../../../../poly-a-tail.md) measurements, cap-state assays and mapping of decay intermediates. Test whether R excludes a [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md)-loaded [Argonaute](../../../../../argonaute.md) complex or a decay-promoting [RNA-binding protein](../../../../../rna-binding-protein.md), blocks an [endonuclease](../../../../../endonuclease.md), inhibits [deadenylation](../../../../../deadenylation.md) or [decapping](../../../../../decapping.md), or recruits a protective partner such as [poly(A)-binding protein](../../../../../poly-a-binding-protein.md). Use [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) interaction analysis with and without [ribonuclease](../../../../../ribonuclease.md) to distinguish RNA-bridged association from a more direct interaction. R [mutants](../../../../../mutant.md) that retain target binding but lose a partner interaction separate recruitment from recognition. Depleting the implicated decay factor should suppress the destabilization caused by R loss; purified-system decay assays can test whether R alone physically protects the substrate.

Measure [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) and subcellular location in parallel, using [ribosome profiling](../../../../../ribosome-profiling.md) or a controlled [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) reporter and [single-molecule fluorescence in situ hybridization](../../../../../single-molecule-fluorescence-in-situ-hybridization.md). R might stabilize the target indirectly by altering [ribosome](../../../../../ribosome.md) loading or compartment access. Inhibiting [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) can itself alter decay, and colocalization in a granule does not establish protection. The reported stabilizing action of [IGF2BP proteins](../../../../../igf2bp-protein.md) on modified targets provides a candidate mechanism to compare with, rather than an identity to assign to R.[Abstract and Results: modification-associated target recognition, stability and translation](../../../../../recognition-of-rna-n6-methyladenosine-by-igf2bp-proteins-enhances-mrna-stability-and-translation-by-huilin-huang-and-others.md) **The strongest mechanism links a verified target-site modification to R binding, a defined protective activity and a rescued change in target [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md).**
