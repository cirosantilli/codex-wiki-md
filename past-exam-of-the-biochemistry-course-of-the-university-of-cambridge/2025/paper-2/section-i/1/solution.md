<h1 id="section-i/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**A convincing [splicing code](../../../../../splicing-code.md) must predict [alternative splicing](../../../../../alternative-splicing.md) from both the [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) sequence and the cellular context, then survive causal perturbation.** A list of [exons](../../../../../exon.md) specific to [neurons](../../../../../neuron.md) or enriched sequence motifs is only a starting point. In the [central nervous system](../../../../../central-nervous-system.md), distinguish [neurons](../../../../../neuron.md) from [glial cells](../../../../../glial-cell.md), individual [neuron](../../../../../neuron.md) classes, anatomical regions, developmental stages and activity states. Otherwise a bulk difference can arise because two samples contain different proportions of [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), even when no individual [cell type](../../../../../cell-type.md) changes its [RNA splicing](../../../../../rna-splicing.md).

Start with replicated, well-matched samples from several [central nervous system](../../../../../central-nervous-system.md) regions and non-neural comparison [biological tissues](../../../../../tissue-biology.md), recording donor, age, sex, preparation batch and [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) integrity. Obtain deep, stranded [RNA sequencing](../../../../../rna-sequencing.md) with reads spanning [splice junctions](../../../../../splice-junction.md); combine this with [long-read RNA sequencing](../../../../../long-read-rna-sequencing.md) to establish complete [transcript isoforms](../../../../../transcript-isoform.md). Use purified [cell types](../../../../../cell-type.md) or sufficiently deep, full-length [single-cell RNA sequencing](../../../../../single-cell-rna-sequencing.md) to separate cellular composition from regulation. Routine assays that sequence only the $3'$ end cannot measure most internal [exons](../../../../../exon.md); [single-nucleus RNA sequencing](../../../../../single-nucleus-rna-sequencing.md) additionally enriches incompletely processed [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), so retained [introns](../../../../../intron.md) require careful interpretation. A matched bulk or purified-cell dataset supplies coverage that sparse individual-cell observations lack.

Align reads with a [splice-aware sequence alignment](../../../../../splice-aware-sequence-alignment.md) method and quantify cassette [exons](../../../../../exon.md), alternative [splice sites](../../../../../splice-site.md), mutually exclusive [exons](../../../../../exon.md) and retained [introns](../../../../../intron.md). Search explicitly for [microexons](../../../../../microexon.md), whose short length can defeat standard alignment filters. For a cassette [exon](../../../../../exon.md), summarize inclusion by [percent spliced in](../../../../../percent-spliced-in.md), written as a fraction

$$
\psi=\frac{I}{I+S},
$$

where $I$ and $S$ estimate abundances of included and skipped molecules after correcting the different informative read opportunities. Adding the two inclusion-junction counts and comparing their sum directly with a single skipping-junction count would bias this estimate. Report uncertainty and coverage, and model between-donor variation; select changes by both effect size and [false discovery rate](../../../../../false-discovery-rate.md). Validate selected [splice junctions](../../../../../splice-junction.md) using [RT-PCR](../../../../../reverse-transcription-polymerase-chain-reaction.md) and [Sanger sequencing](../../../../../sanger-sequencing.md). Distinguish genuine altered processing from differential destruction of [transcript isoforms](../../../../../transcript-isoform.md) through [nonsense-mediated decay](../../../../../nonsense-mediated-decay.md), using newly synthesized or nuclear [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) and a short, controlled perturbation of that pathway where needed.

Build a feature set around each regulated [exon](../../../../../exon.md): strengths of the two [splice sites](../../../../../splice-site.md), [RNA splicing branch point](../../../../../rna-splicing-branch-point.md) and [polypyrimidine tract](../../../../../polypyrimidine-tract.md); [exon](../../../../../exon.md) and [intron](../../../../../intron.md) lengths; position-specific [cis-regulatory elements](../../../../../cis-regulatory-element.md); predicted or measured [RNA secondary structure](../../../../../rna-secondary-structure.md); and evolutionary conservation. Include the abundance, [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) level, nuclear location and activity of candidate [RNA-binding proteins](../../../../../rna-binding-protein.md), rather than treating their [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) abundance as a perfect proxy. A [splicing code](../../../../../splicing-code.md) can combine many weak sequence features: the original tissue-prediction study demonstrated the value of combinations rather than one universal neural motif.[Abstract and Results: combinations of RNA features predicting tissue-dependent exon inclusion](../../../../../deciphering-the-splicing-code-by-yoseph-barash-and-others.md)

Fit an interpretable [statistical model](../../../../../statistical-model-split.md) first, allowing interactions between motif position and [RNA-binding protein](../../../../../rna-binding-protein.md) abundance. Compare it with more flexible [machine learning](../../../../../machine-learning.md), using [cross-validation](../../../../../cross-validation.md) that holds out whole [genes](../../../../../gene.md), donors and, for a strong transfer test, a [cell type](../../../../../cell-type.md). Randomly separating junctions from the same [gene](../../../../../gene.md) between training and testing risks information leakage. Compare against sequence-only, context-only and expression-matched baseline predictors. Successful prediction of held-out [percent spliced in](../../../../../percent-spliced-in.md) is more informative than classifying the training set. In particular, an enriched motif can reflect [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) composition, [intron](../../../../../intron.md) length or another factor with similar specificity; enrichment alone does not establish binding or function.

Map candidate [RNA-binding protein](../../../../../rna-binding-protein.md) occupancy with [crosslinking and immunoprecipitation](../../../../../crosslinking-and-immunoprecipitation.md) in the relevant preparations of [neurons](../../../../../neuron.md). Include input material, a no-crosslink control where appropriate, a validated [antibody](../../../../../antibody.md), and biological replication; account for transcript abundance and crosslinking preferences. Integrate binding positions with [RNA sequencing](../../../../../rna-sequencing.md) after acute depletion. [NOVA proteins](../../../../../nova-protein.md) give a concrete precedent: binding to YCAY clusters has position-dependent effects, with exonic binding able to inhibit inclusion and downstream intronic binding able to promote it. Here Y denotes a pyrimidine [nucleobase](../../../../../nucleotide-base.md). Thus the same motif can support opposite predictions at different positions.[Abstract; Figures 1–4 and 6: positional RNA map and effects on spliceosome assembly](../../../../../an-rna-map-predicting-nova-dependent-splicing-regulation-by-jernej-ule-and-others.md) Test other neural regulators, such as [RBFOX proteins](../../../../../rbfox-protein.md) and [PTBP proteins](../../../../../ptbp-protein.md), as potentially overlapping or antagonistic inputs rather than assuming every neural [exon](../../../../../exon.md) follows the [NOVA protein](../../../../../nova-protein.md) rule.

Finally, make the predictions experimentally vulnerable. For representative [exons](../../../../../exon.md), mutate or move individual [cis-regulatory elements](../../../../../cis-regulatory-element.md) in a [minigene splicing reporter](../../../../../minigene-splicing-reporter.md), preserving [splice sites](../../../../../splice-site.md) and controlling total reporter abundance. Test gain and loss of inclusion in both neural and non-neural [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Edit the endogenous element because a short [minigene splicing reporter](../../../../../minigene-splicing-reporter.md) can omit distant regulation within [introns](../../../../../intron.md) or [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) context. Acutely deplete the proposed [RNA-binding protein](../../../../../rna-binding-protein.md) and rescue with physiological amounts of normal [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) or an RNA-binding-defective [mutant](../../../../../mutant.md). Combined depletion tests redundancy; motif mutation plus factor depletion tests whether both act through the same element. A particularly strong result is the predicted reversal after moving a binding site from a repressive to an activating position. Measure [cell differentiation](../../../../../cellular-differentiation.md) and viability so that a change in cellular identity is not mistaken for direct [RNA splicing](../../../../../rna-splicing.md) regulation. **The resulting code is a tested, context-dependent set of regulatory rules, with an explicit domain of validity.**
