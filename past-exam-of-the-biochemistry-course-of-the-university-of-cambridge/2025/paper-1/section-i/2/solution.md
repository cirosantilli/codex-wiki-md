↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**Dynamic measurements explain which conformations a protein visits, how quickly it exchanges between them, and which transitions control biological activity.** A static [protein structure](../../../../../protein-structure.md) identifies possible contacts; [protein dynamics](../../../../../protein-dynamics.md) can establish whether those contacts form before [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) binding, during [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) turnover, or when a [membrane protein](../../../../../membrane-protein.md) switches between transport states. The useful comparison is therefore among [conformational ensembles](../../../../../conformational-ensemble.md), transition rates and function under matched conditions.

Consider two interconverting conformations $A$ and $B$. For an ideal two-state system at [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md), [detailed balance](../../../../../detailed-balance.md) gives $p_Ak_{AB}=p_Bk_{BA}$, so

$$
\frac{p_B}{p_A}=\frac{k_{AB}}{k_{BA}}=\exp\!\left(-\frac{G_B-G_A}{RT}\right),\qquad k_{\mathrm{ex}}=k_{AB}+k_{BA}.
$$

Here $G_A,G_B$ are molar [Gibbs free energies](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md), $R$ is the [molar gas constant](../../../../../molar-gas-constant.md), and $T$ is [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). Measuring only the population ratio cannot identify the exchange timescale: multiplying both rates by the same factor preserves that ratio. Conversely, a measured relaxation rate without populations does not determine the individual forward and reverse rates. Under this model, if $p_B=0.1$ and $k_{\mathrm{ex}}=100\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, then $k_{AB}=10\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ and $k_{BA}=90\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. These illustrative values are a consistency check, not measurements of a particular [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). A driven [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md)-consuming cycle need not satisfy [detailed balance](../../../../../detailed-balance.md) around the entire cycle.

[NMR spectroscopy](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) is particularly powerful for a soluble [globular protein](../../../../../globular-protein.md). Residue-resolved [chemical shifts](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) report local environments; [NMR relaxation dispersion](../../../../../nmr-relaxation-dispersion.md) measures exchange contributions to line broadening and can detect low-population states exchanging on microsecond-to-millisecond timescales. Different experiments probe faster local fluctuations or slower exchange. Analysis can estimate populations, rates and [chemical shift](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) differences, allowing a minor state's environment to be compared with a known [ligand](../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md)-bound state. [dihydrofolate reductase](../../../../../dihydrofolate-reductase.md) provides a concrete example: relaxation-dispersion measurements identified excited conformations resembling states used elsewhere in its catalytic cycle.[pp. 1638–1642, relation between excited conformations and catalytic intermediates](../../../../../the-dynamic-energy-landscape-of-dihydrofolate-reductase-catalysis-by-david-d-boehr-and-others.md) This supports access to functionally relevant conformations before the corresponding chemical step; it does not establish that every detected motion accelerates the bond-making reaction.

This distinction helps test [conformational selection](../../../../../conformational-selection.md) against [induced fit](../../../../../induced-fit.md). If an unbound [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) already samples a binding-competent conformation and the [ligand](../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) stabilizes it, [conformational selection](../../../../../conformational-selection.md) contributes. If substantial rearrangement follows initial binding, [induced fit](../../../../../induced-fit.md) contributes. Both can occur in the same mechanism. Measure binding and structural signals over a range of [ligand](../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) [concentrations](../../../../../concentration.md), include independently measured exchange rates, and fit explicit kinetic models. A matching [chemical shift](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) alone does not establish pathway flux, and different models can fit a single relaxation trace. Combining equilibrium populations, rapid-mixing kinetics and [site-directed mutagenesis](../../../../../site-directed-mutagenesis.md) can constrain which route dominates under the tested conditions.

[single-molecule Förster resonance energy transfer](../../../../../single-molecule-forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md) reveals heterogeneity concealed by ensemble averaging. For a calibrated label pair, the ideal distance dependence is

$$
E=\frac{1}{1+(r/R_0)^6},
$$

where $R_0$ is the [Förster distance](../../../../../forster-distance.md). Distinct efficiencies and transitions between them can reveal domain closure, opening and dwell times; $r=R_0$ gives $E=1/2$. A distribution of efficiencies is not automatically a distribution of structural states: dye orientation, blinking, label flexibility and [photobleaching](../../../../../photobleaching.md) also contribute. Use donor-only and acceptor-only samples, alternating excitation where appropriate, activity measurements after labeling, and more than one label geometry. For a single memoryless exit with rate $k$, the dwell survival is $e^{-kt}$ and the mean dwell is $1/k$; missed fast events and multiple hidden states violate that simple interpretation.

These measurements connect [protein dynamics](../../../../../protein-dynamics.md) to [allostery](../../../../../allostery.md). A [ligand](../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) at one site can change the populations or exchange barriers at a distant functional site, even if the mean [protein structure](../../../../../protein-structure.md) changes little. Measure both sites, compare their thermodynamic coupling and test whether an interface [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) changes coupling while preserving [protein folding](../../../../../protein-folding.md). [hydrogen-deuterium exchange mass spectrometry](../../../../../hydrogen-deuterium-exchange-mass-spectrometry.md) identifies changes in protection across a [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) and is useful for large assemblies. Interpretation needs matched [pH](../../../../../ph.md) and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), correction for exchange during analysis, and recognition that protection combines solvent access, [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) and structural opening. A protected region can be an interface or a stabilized internal region; it is not direct proof of contact.

For [membrane proteins](../../../../../membrane-protein.md), the environment is part of the experiment. [micelles](../../../../../micelle.md) of [detergents](../../../../../detergent.md), [lipid bilayer nanodiscs](../../../../../lipid-bilayer-nanodisc.md) and [lipid vesicles](../../../../../lipid-vesicle.md) can stabilize different [conformational ensembles](../../../../../conformational-ensemble.md). [NMR spectroscopy](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md), [electron paramagnetic resonance](../../../../../electron-paramagnetic-resonance.md) distance measurements and [single-molecule Förster resonance energy transfer](../../../../../single-molecule-forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md) can compare cytoplasmic domain movements in these environments. In the [beta-2 adrenergic receptor](../../../../../beta-2-adrenergic-receptor.md), combined fluorine [NMR spectroscopy](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) and [double electron-electron resonance](../../../../../double-electron-electron-resonance.md) showed that agonist occupancy did not fully stabilize one active conformation; interaction with a [G protein](../../../../../g-protein.md) or a mimetic further favored the active state.[pp. 1101–1111, comparison of unliganded, ligand-bound and intracellular-partner-bound receptor ensembles](../../../../../structural-insights-into-the-dynamic-process-of-beta-2-adrenergic-receptor-signaling-by-aashish-manglik-and-others.md) This explains how [ligands](../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) with similar binding can differ in signaling efficacy: binding occupancy and population of a productive signaling conformation are distinct quantities.

[Ion channels](../../../../../ion-channel.md) provide an unusually direct connection between molecular switching and function. [Patch clamp](../../../../../patch-clamp.md) records individual openings and closures, permitting measurement of conductance, open probability, dwell times and responses to [membrane potential](../../../../../membrane-potential-split.md) or [ligand](../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md). For $N$ identical independent channels with single-channel current $i$ and open probability $P_o$, the mean current is $NiP_o$. A changed whole-cell current could therefore reflect channel number, conductance or gating; single-channel measurements help separate them. Correlating a conformational probe with electrical activity can assign a structural motion to opening, but a multi-state gating model is usually more realistic than simply equating all structural fluctuations with current-producing transitions. Control membrane integrity, recording bandwidth, label effects and the relevant [electrochemical gradient](../../../../../electrochemical-gradient.md).

For a [carrier protein](../../../../../carrier-protein.md), structural dynamics tests [alternating access](../../../../../alternating-access.md): the [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md)-binding region becomes accessible successively from opposite sides without maintaining an open pore across the [lipid bilayer](../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md). Measure conformational exchange together with uptake into [lipid vesicles](../../../../../lipid-vesicle.md), varying [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md), [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) or the driving [electrochemical gradient](../../../../../electrochemical-gradient.md). A conformation trapped by a [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) may be an on-pathway intermediate, an off-pathway dead end or a misfolded state; compare binding, transport and the driving-energy reaction. [cryo-electron microscopy](../../../../../cryo-electron-microscopy.md) classification supplies candidate structural states, but particle-class fractions can be biased by preparation and classification and do not directly provide transition rates. [molecular dynamics](../../../../../molecular-dynamics.md) can suggest connecting routes and transient interactions; predictions must reproduce observables and are limited by sampling and the physical model.

The strongest functional inference comes from perturbing a particular transition and measuring the resulting activity. Preserve abundance, localization, [protein folding](../../../../../protein-folding.md) and binding where possible, then ask whether the measured rate change predicts the functional change. Slow [reaction product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md) release may determine [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) turnover even when chemical conversion is fast; a [membrane protein](../../../../../membrane-protein.md) can bind a [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) normally while failing to complete its transport cycle. **Biophysical dynamics turns a structural catalogue into a mechanism when state populations, transition rates and activity constrain the same model.**
