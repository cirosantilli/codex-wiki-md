<h1 id="section-i/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**Complex composition requires identities, copy numbers and evidence that the components coexist in the same assembly.** A list of nearby [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) is a useful starting point, but neither a direct [protein-protein interaction](../../../../../protein-protein-interaction.md) nor a uniquely defined [protein complex](../../../../../protein-complex.md) follows from that list. I would combine quantitative isolation, measurements in intact [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), and structural constraints, then repeat the measurements through a controlled perturbation time course.

An effective first experiment is [affinity purification-mass spectrometry](../../../../../affinity-purification-mass-spectrometry.md). Introduce an [affinity tag](../../../../../affinity-tag.md) at the endogenous [gene](../../../../../gene.md) encoding a candidate bait, verify normal [subcellular localization](../../../../../subcellular-localization.md) and biological activity, isolate associated material under conditions preserving the assembly, and identify digested [peptides](../../../../../peptide.md) by [tandem mass spectrometry](../../../../../tandem-mass-spectrometry.md). Matching fragmentation spectra to a sequence database assigns candidate [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md); control the [false discovery rate](../../../../../false-discovery-rate.md) at both [peptide](../../../../../peptide.md) and [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) levels and retain evidence from sequence-unique [peptides](../../../../../peptide.md) when related [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) share sequences. An endogenous tag reduces the risk that [gene overexpression](../../../../../gene-overexpression.md) creates interactions through excessive [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md). An untagged sample, a tag-only or localization-matched control, and independent [biological replicates](../../../../../biological-replicate.md) distinguish specific enrichment from bead-binding and abundant contaminants. Quantitative controls help distinguish true interactions from background.[pp. 70–73, statistical distinction between true interactions and background using quantitative purification data](../../../../../saint-probabilistic-scoring-of-affinity-purification-mass-spectrometry-data-by-hyungwon-choi-and-others.md)

Isolation has selective losses. A weak partner can dissociate during dilution or washing; a [membrane protein](../../../../../membrane-protein.md) assembly can be disrupted by its solubilizing agent. Compare extraction conditions, check recovery in the soluble and insoluble fractions, and use reciprocal [co-immunoprecipitation](../../../../../co-immunoprecipitation.md) from an independent subunit. Treatment with a [nuclease](../../../../../nuclease.md) tests whether the association depends on [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) or [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), but removal after extraction can itself destabilize a real nucleic-acid-dependent assembly. Purification from mixed tagged and untagged cell populations, with distinguishable [stable isotope labeling](../../../../../stable-isotope-labeling.md), can test whether partners exchange after cell disruption. A reciprocal enrichment supports coassociation; demonstrating a direct contact still requires additional evidence.

For [stoichiometry](../../../../../stoichiometry.md), raw [mass spectrometry](../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md) intensity is insufficient because different [peptides](../../../../../peptide.md) have different digestion and [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) efficiencies. Use calibrated [peptide](../../../../../peptide.md) standards or carefully normalized quantitative estimates, ideally with several reference subunits. [native mass spectrometry](../../../../../native-mass-spectrometry.md) measures intact assembly masses and can distinguish candidate subunit counts, while controlled dissociation helps assign subcomplexes. Bound [lipids](../../../../../lipid.md), [post-translational modifications](../../../../../post-translational-modification.md), gas-phase losses and heterogeneous occupancy complicate assignment, so check the mass against the summed masses of candidate subunits. [Size-exclusion chromatography](../../../../../size-exclusion-chromatography.md) coupled to [multi-angle light scattering](../../../../../multi-angle-light-scattering.md) independently constrains assembly mass, although an unresolved mixture produces an average and the hydrodynamic size alone is not a molecular mass. Quantitative [mass spectrometry](../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md) of [protein complexes](../../../../../protein-complex.md) can combine interaction strengths, abundance and stoichiometry.[pp. 712–723, integration of interaction strengths, abundance and stoichiometry](../../../../../a-human-interactome-in-three-quantitative-dimensions-organized-by-stoichiometries-and-abundances-by-marco-y-hein-and-others.md)

To avoid requiring a bait, fractionate native extracts by [size-exclusion chromatography](../../../../../size-exclusion-chromatography.md) or another separation and quantify [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) in every fraction. Correlated elution profiles suggest membership of the same [protein complex](../../../../../protein-complex.md); comparison across an independent separation helps distinguish coincidence. This [co-fractionation mass spectrometry](../../../../../co-fractionation-mass-spectrometry.md) can also reveal shifts from a large assembly into smaller forms after treatment. It may merge different assemblies sharing a subunit, and a failure to coelute does not exclude a transient interaction. [chemical cross-linking mass spectrometry](../../../../../chemical-cross-linking-mass-spectrometry.md) adds spatial information: identify pairs of linked residues, translate them into distance restraints appropriate to the reagent and residue accessibility, and ask whether a proposed assembly can satisfy them. A cross-link is a proximity constraint, not automatically a permanent interface; intermolecular assignment is especially difficult for identical subunits.

In living [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), [proximity labeling](../../../../../proximity-labeling.md) captures an interaction neighbourhood before disruptive extraction. A bait fused to [TurboID](../../../../../turboid.md) marks nearby [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) with [biotin](../../../../../biotin.md), allowing stringent enrichment and [mass spectrometry](../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md). The original [TurboID](../../../../../turboid.md) work demonstrated useful labeling over roughly ten minutes, which is much faster than older long-labeling implementations.[Fig. 2 and Supplementary Figs. 7–11, labeling-time and compartment comparisons](../../../../../efficient-proximity-labeling-in-living-cells-and-organisms-with-turboid-by-tess-c-branon-and-others.md) A localization-matched enzyme control, a no-added-[biotin](../../../../../biotin.md) control and matched fusion abundance are essential. The labeling pulse integrates both spatial proximity and residence time; ten-minute labeling cannot resolve a seconds-long interaction sequence. Changes in [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md), [biotin](../../../../../biotin.md) availability, enzyme activity or access to reactive residues can change the labeling signal independently of complex membership. A pulse followed by a chase does not make the covalent label disappear when the partner leaves.

Structural methods distinguish alternative interpretations of these measurements. [cryo-electron microscopy](../../../../../cryo-electron-microscopy.md) can assign ordered subunits, estimate occupancy of resolved features, and separate some compositionally different particle classes. Flexible or small subunits may remain invisible despite being present. [X-ray crystallography](../../../../../x-ray-crystallography.md) resolves stable interfaces but selects material that crystallizes. [Förster resonance energy transfer](../../../../../forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md) between appropriately positioned labels tests nanometre-scale proximity in intact [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md); [single-particle tracking](../../../../../single-particle-tracking.md) and [fluorescence correlation spectroscopy](../../../../../fluorescence-correlation-spectroscopy.md) can test comobility and exchange. Label placement, spectral cross-talk and accidental [colocalization](../../../../../colocalization.md) require controls. None of these techniques by itself reliably inventories every component.

The computational analysis should preserve these distinctions. Construct a [protein interaction network](../../../../../protein-interaction-network.md) with separate edge types for copurification, cross-linking and proximity labeling. Cluster reproducibly supported associations to propose modules, distinguish a stable core from variable accessory components, and integrate localization and measured [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) abundance. Models such as [AlphaFold 3](../../../../../alphafold-3.md) can propose interfaces for experimentally supported candidate components, but a confident predicted geometry does not demonstrate that an interaction occurs in the tested condition.[pp. 493–500, joint structural prediction of biomolecular complexes](../../../../../accurate-structure-prediction-of-biomolecular-interactions-with-alphafold-3-by-josh-abramson-and-others.md) Test interfaces by [site-directed mutagenesis](../../../../../site-directed-mutagenesis.md), confirming normal [protein folding](../../../../../protein-folding.md), expression and localization before interpreting a lost interaction. Evaluate a proposed assembly against independently held-out cross-links or other measurements to reduce circular validation.

For perturbations, use vehicle controls, several doses and early-to-late sampling; randomize preparation and instrument order. Quantify input abundance alongside bait-normalized partner recovery. An illustrative relative recruitment statistic for partner $i$ at time $t$ is

$$
R_i(t)=\frac{I_i^{\mathrm{IP}}(t)/I_b^{\mathrm{IP}}(t)}{I_i^{\mathrm{IP}}(0)/I_b^{\mathrm{IP}}(0)}\,\frac{A_i(0)}{A_i(t)},
$$

where $I^{\mathrm{IP}}$ is background-corrected recovered intensity, $b$ is the bait and $A_i$ is input abundance. This is an operational comparison under matched extraction and measurement response, not an absolute occupancy or [dissociation constant](../../../../../dissociation-constant.md). A doubled recovered partner signal with doubled input abundance and unchanged bait recovery gives $R_i=1$, illustrating why raw enrichment alone would mislead. Values near the detection limit need uncertainty bounds rather than unstable ratios.

A drug-induced loss of several core components could mean disassembly, degradation, changed localization or poor recovery. Distinguish them with input [proteomics](../../../../../proteomics.md), microscopy and native fractionation, then test reversibility after washout and rescue by a drug-resistant target when feasible. For stress, monitor viability and extraction-resistant material; for disease-associated [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md), compare otherwise matched genetic backgrounds and rescue with the normal allele. [post-translational modifications](../../../../../post-translational-modification.md) may recruit an accessory partner without changing the core. [hydrogen-deuterium exchange mass spectrometry](../../../../../hydrogen-deuterium-exchange-mass-spectrometry.md) can then test interface exposure, but its altered exchange reports accessibility and structural dynamics rather than a new subunit by itself. **A convincing remodeling claim is a reproducible change in assembly membership or occupancy, separated from changes in abundance, localization and assay recovery, and connected to a functional consequence.**
