↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**Atomic-resolution cryo-electron microscopy became possible by retaining high-frequency structural information through the whole experiment: specimen preparation, electron optics, detection and computation.** The decisive advances improved the information obtained per damaging [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and made it possible to align and average many weak images without averaging away structural differences. A short [de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../de-broglie-wavelength.md) had always been available; the practical limit was preserving and extracting the information carried by those [electrons](../../../../../electron.md).

First, [vitrification](../../../../../vitrification.md) preserves hydrated biological material in a thin noncrystalline layer of [water](../../../../../water.md). Rapid freezing avoids the strong periodic scattering and disruption associated with ordinary ice crystals, while cryogenic imaging slows some damage processes. The specimen still suffers [radiation damage](../../../../../radiation-damage.md), and a thicker layer adds background and multiple scattering. Reproducible thin ice, clean grids, stable supports and reduced mechanical drift are therefore prerequisites. The specimen must remain structurally intact and occupy enough orientations for a three-dimensional reconstruction. Adsorption at the air–water interface, preferred orientation and variable thickness can defeat an otherwise excellent microscope. Sample optimization should include independent checks of assembly integrity and activity where feasible.

Second, [direct electron detection](../../../../../direct-electron-detection.md) greatly improved the useful signal relative to earlier indirect cameras. In a counting detector, individual [electron](../../../../../electron.md) events can be localized and recorded without treating the deposited signal merely as a continuous intensity. [detective quantum efficiency](../../../../../detective-quantum-efficiency.md) measures how efficiently the detector preserves the input [signal-to-noise ratio](../../../../../signal-to-noise-ratio.md) as a function of [spatial frequency](../../../../../spatial-frequency.md). High efficiency matters particularly because the permissible exposure is limited. At excessive event rates, overlapping events cause coincidence losses, so faster readout permits useful counting at practical collection rates. A nominally smaller camera pixel alone cannot replace a detector that preserves high-frequency information.

Fast detectors also turn an exposure into a sequence of movie frames. Aligning those frames corrects specimen drift and beam-induced movement; local correction handles different movements across the imaged area. Uncorrected movement preferentially smears fine features. [dose weighting](../../../../../dose-weighting.md) then combines early and later frames according to the information they retain: later exposure can help low-frequency alignment even after fine structural details have been damaged. [pp. 331–332, local movie-based motion correction](../../../../../motioncor2-anisotropic-correction-of-beam-induced-motion-for-improved-cryo-electron-microscopy-by-shawn-q-zheng-and-others.md) exemplifies this computational use of detector movies. Frame alignment cannot restore chemistry already destroyed by [radiation damage](../../../../../radiation-damage.md), and extremely noisy individual frames require constrained motion estimates.

Third, stable and coherent electron optics increase contrast transfer at the finest spacings. A narrow source energy spread reduces [chromatic aberration](../../../../../chromatic-aberration.md), because electrons with different energies otherwise focus differently. A [cold field emission gun](../../../../../cold-field-emission-gun.md) provides one route; a [monochromator](../../../../../monochromator.md) provides another. An [energy filter](../../../../../energy-filter.md) removes many inelastically scattered [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) that otherwise contribute background. This does not remove all damage or every unwanted scattering event. Improved mechanical and electrical stability, reproducible lens settings, accurate coma-free alignment and calibrated magnification make the information more consistent across images.

The 2020 [apoferritin](../../../../../apoferritin.md) reconstructions illustrate the final hardware step. One study combined a cold source, improved energy filtering and a fast direct camera with advanced correction to obtain a map at about $1.22\,\text{Å}$. Another used an instrument with monochromation and aberration correction to reach about $1.25\,\text{Å}$.[Fig. 1, Fig. 3 and Extended Data Table 1; source, filter, camera and 1.22 Å apoferritin reconstruction](../../../../../single-particle-cryo-em-at-atomic-resolution-by-takanori-nakane-and-others.md)[main article, microscope configuration and 1.25 Å apoferritin reconstruction](../../../../../atomic-resolution-protein-structure-determination-by-cryo-em-by-ka-man-yip-and-others.md) The two instruments demonstrate complementary ways of preserving coherent high-frequency information. These unusually rigid, symmetric particles are favorable test specimens; their resolutions do not imply that a flexible cellular assembly will reach the same limit.

Fourth, modern reconstruction treats the noisy images statistically. [single-particle analysis](../../../../../single-particle-analysis.md) estimates how many randomly oriented projections correspond to a common three-dimensional structure. [Bayesian inference](../../../../../bayesian-statistics-split.md) and [maximum likelihood estimation](../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimation-split.md) permit uncertainty in image orientations and class membership to be handled rather than committing every weak image to a single exact alignment from the beginning. [pp. 519–530, probabilistic reconstruction and classification](../../../../../relion-implementation-of-a-bayesian-approach-to-cryo-em-structure-determination-by-sjors-h-w-scheres.md) provides a concrete implementation. Two-dimensional classes reject many damaged or overlapping particles; three-dimensional classification can separate different compositions or conformations. Appropriate symmetry increases the effective information, but an incorrect symmetry assumption can erase a real asymmetric feature.

Refinement increasingly corrects individual particle movements and optical imperfections. Per-particle defocus estimates accommodate height differences in ice. Beam tilt, higher-order aberrations and anisotropic magnification need correction when their accumulated phase errors become important. The [contrast transfer function](../../../../../contrast-transfer-function.md) changes the magnitude and sign of Fourier components; combining images recorded with different defocus values recovers information suppressed at particular transfer-function zeros. Near atomic spacings, accounting for [Ewald sphere curvature](../../../../../ewald-sphere-curvature.md) can further improve reconstruction. Such corrections require adequate signal and calibration; simply adding more adjustable parameters to weak data risks fitting [noise](../../../../../noise-signal-processing.md).

Automation and fast computation make these methods practical. Stable automated acquisition and beam-image-shift strategies collect large image sets; parallel computation permits repeated alignment, classification and refinement. More particles improve the average only if they carry consistent signal. Under an ideal independent-noise model, the amplitude [signal-to-noise ratio](../../../../../signal-to-noise-ratio.md) improves in proportion to $\sqrt{N}$; a systematic alignment error or persistent structural heterogeneity will not disappear by this scaling. For a target spacing $d$ and real-space pixel size $a$, [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](../../../../../nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md) requires $a\leq d/2$. For $d=1.2\,\text{Å}$ this gives $a\leq0.6\,\text{Å}$ per pixel, a necessary sampling condition rather than a guarantee of such resolution.

Validation is part of the technological achievement. Split particles into independent halves and refine them independently; calculate their [Fourier shell correlation](../../../../../fourier-shell-correlation.md), commonly reporting the spacing at the $0.143$ criterion for independently refined half-maps. Shared high-resolution references, poorly handled masks or correlated data can inflate agreement, so independence and mask correction matter. Inspect directional coverage and local resolution, compare models against held-out map information, and check stereochemistry. The global resolution is a summary: flexible loops, bound [lipids](../../../../../lipid.md) and minor components may be much worse than the ordered core.

Finally, distinguish an atomistic model from atomically resolved data. At ordinary near-atomic resolution, side chains and backbone features can support building an atomic model while individual neighboring [chemical atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) are not all separated. At true atomic resolution, many individual atomic peaks become distinguishable and some [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) contributions may be detectable under favorable conditions. [cryo-electron microscopy](../../../../../cryo-electron-microscopy.md) reconstructs scattering [electrostatic potential](../../../../../electric-potential.md), so interpretation of very fine features is not identical to interpreting [X-ray crystallography](../../../../../x-ray-crystallography.md) electron-density maps. **The breakthrough was the combined improvement of specimen quality, counting movies, optical coherence and statistical reconstruction, with independent validation showing that the fine detail was supported by the data.**
