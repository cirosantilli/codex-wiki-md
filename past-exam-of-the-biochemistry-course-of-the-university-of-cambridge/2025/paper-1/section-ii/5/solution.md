<h1 id="section-ii/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**The membrane supplies G proteins with an address, an orienting surface, a reaction environment and a route for spatial redistribution.** Its role therefore goes beyond retaining a [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) near a receptor. [Lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) composition, covalent anchors, membrane organization and trafficking can alter which partners meet, which interfaces are exposed and how long a signal persists.

For a [heterotrimeric G protein](../../../../../heterotrimeric-g-protein.md), an activated [G protein-coupled receptor](../../../../../g-protein-coupled-receptor.md) promotes release of [GDP](../../../../../guanosine-diphosphate.md) from the $\alpha$ subunit and binding of [GTP](../../../../../guanosine-triphosphate.md). The nucleotide switch changes interaction with the $\beta\gamma$ complex and downstream effectors; [GTP hydrolysis](../../../../../gtp-hydrolysis.md) favors termination and reassociation. This is not [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) of [GDP](../../../../../guanosine-diphosphate.md) by the receptor. Both the $\alpha$ subunit and the $\beta\gamma$ complex can participate in downstream signaling, and their activation need not entail complete physical separation in every receptor–subunit combination. The relevant question is whether the effector-binding surfaces and local interactions change.

Covalent [protein lipidation](../../../../../protein-lipidation.md) helps place this cycle at a membrane. Many $\alpha$ subunits undergo [S-palmitoylation](../../../../../s-palmitoylation.md), some also undergo [N-myristoylation](../../../../../n-myristoylation.md), and the $\gamma$ subunit commonly undergoes [prenylation](../../../../../prenylation.md) near its [C-terminus](../../../../../c-terminus.md). These modifications attach chemically different [hydrophobic](../../../../../hydrophobicity.md) groups; their distributions depend on the subunit, so no single anchor recipe describes every [G protein](../../../../../g-protein.md). Additional basic residues, contacts among subunits and membrane-facing surfaces stabilize association. The membrane anchor on $\gamma$ helps position the whole $\beta\gamma$ complex. Because one attachment can be individually weak or nonspecific, combined signals can determine both residence time and preference for a particular membrane.

[small GTPases](../../../../../small-gtpase.md) illustrate the same logic in a distinct signaling architecture. [Ras](../../../../../ras.md) isoforms have a [prenylation](../../../../../prenylation.md) anchor and additional targeting information. Palmitoylation contributes to targeting of some isoforms, while the [polybasic region](../../../../../polybasic-region.md) of [KRAS4B](../../../../../kras4b.md) promotes interaction with negatively charged inner-leaflet [lipids](../../../../../lipid.md). Reversible acylation and trafficking can therefore change localization without altering the nucleotide-binding core. Mutating an anchoring site should be interpreted with expression, processing and nucleotide-loading controls: an inactive cytosolic mutant does not by itself distinguish loss of a necessary location from a direct catalytic defect.

Membrane tethering can enhance productive encounters by restricting motion to a surface and increasing local availability near a membrane-associated partner. It also sets orientation and limits the orientations accessible to the [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). More surface density does not guarantee faster signaling: trapping in an inappropriate region, unfavorable orientation or crowding can reduce access. A clean experimental comparison is to retarget an anchor-deficient [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) with an independent membrane tether, confirming comparable surface density. Rescue would support the importance of localization; failure could indicate the original anchor also specifies orientation, lipid preference or trafficking, or that the replacement tether introduces an artifact.

The membrane can regulate even a correctly localized [G protein](../../../../../g-protein.md) through contacts with its folded domain. For [KRAS4B](../../../../../kras4b.md), [NMR spectroscopy](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) on [lipid bilayer nanodiscs](../../../../../lipid-bilayer-nanodisc.md) demonstrated membrane-associated orientations in which an effector-binding surface can be occluded. Some disease-associated [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md) changed this interaction and favored effector accessibility.[pp. 6625–6630, nanodisc NMR and membrane-dependent accessibility of the effector interface](../../../../../oncogenic-and-rasopathy-associated-k-ras-mutations-relieve-membrane-dependent-occlusion-of-the-effector-binding-site-by-mohammad-t-mazhab-jafari-and-others.md) Thus a [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) can remain attached while its signaling competence changes. The inference is specific to the tested constructs and [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) environments; it does not prove that every [Ras](../../../../../ras.md) isoform or every active state has one universal membrane orientation.

[Lipids](../../../../../lipid.md) can also act at the receptor–[G protein](../../../../../g-protein.md) interface. [Phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol-4-5-bisphosphate.md) is a negatively charged inner-leaflet signaling [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md), not simply an inert bulk solvent. Native [mass spectrometry](../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md) and complementary experiments showed that it can stabilize active [G protein-coupled receptor](../../../../../g-protein-coupled-receptor.md) complexes and influence [G protein](../../../../../g-protein.md) coupling selectivity.[pp. 423–427, lipid-dependent receptor stability and G-protein complex formation](../../../../../ptdins-4-5-p2-stabilizes-active-states-of-gpcrs-and-enhances-selectivity-of-g-protein-coupling-by-hsin-yung-yen-and-others.md) Specific lipid binding, general surface [electrostatics](../../../../../electrostatics-split.md) and changes in membrane mechanics are competing or cooperating explanations in any new system. Vary the relevant [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) while holding total charge and other physical properties as constant as possible; compare [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md) in the candidate binding site with control surface [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md). A bound lipid detected after purification is useful evidence but needs functional corroboration in a [lipid bilayer](../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md).

Bulk composition further affects [hydrophobic mismatch](../../../../../hydrophobic-mismatch.md), membrane thickness, local order and the conformational cost of receptor activation. [Cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md) and [sphingomyelin](../../../../../sphingomyelin.md) can alter the organization of a [lipid bilayer](../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md); acyl-chain diversity can affect [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) and anchor partitioning. Distinct [membrane nanodomains](../../../../../membrane-nanodomain.md) may enrich some receptors, [G proteins](../../../../../g-protein.md) and effectors while separating others. These are dynamic, context-dependent organizations. Detergent-resistant extraction does not demonstrate a pre-existing domain of a particular size in a living [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), and diffraction-limited [colocalization](../../../../../colocalization.md) cannot establish molecular interaction. Use live-cell tracking, proximity readouts and controlled membrane reconstitution, with caution that [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md) depletion also changes many unrelated membrane processes.

The membrane is also a chemical participant downstream. Activation of [phospholipase C](../../../../../phospholipase-c.md) hydrolyzes [phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol-4-5-bisphosphate.md), producing membrane-retained [diacylglycerol](../../../../../diacylglycerol.md) and soluble [inositol trisphosphate](../../../../../inositol-trisphosphate.md). This both generates distinct signals and changes the abundance of a receptor-associated [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md). Some [ion channels](../../../../../ion-channel.md) are regulated by $\beta\gamma$ and by [phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol-4-5-bisphosphate.md), linking local protein interactions to local lipid availability. Consequently, removing this [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) could impair receptor coupling, an effector, or both. Separate receptor-mediated nucleotide exchange from effector activity in a reconstituted assay before assigning the step affected.

Localization changes in time. Receptor activation can redistribute particular $\beta\gamma$ combinations from the [plasma membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) to internal membranes, creating opportunities for signaling at the [Golgi apparatus](../../../../../golgi-apparatus.md) and other sites. Experiments on receptor-induced $\beta\gamma$ movement linked this redistribution to [Golgi apparatus](../../../../../golgi-apparatus.md) structure and secretion.[pp. 11417–11422, receptor-driven redistribution, Golgi structure and secretion assays](../../../../../regulation-of-golgi-structure-and-secretion-by-receptor-induced-g-protein-beta-gamma-complex-translocation-by-deepak-kumar-saini-and-others.md) Subunit-dependent anchor interactions help determine the kinetics. Receptor trafficking can additionally move signaling platforms to [endosomes](../../../../../endosome.md): conformation-selective [antibody](../../../../../antibody.md) probes have detected active [beta-2 adrenergic receptors](../../../../../beta-2-adrenergic-receptor.md) and their [G proteins](../../../../../g-protein.md) there, with internalized receptors contributing to the cellular response.[Figs. 1–4, receptor and G-protein activation probes and the contribution of internalized receptors](../../../../../conformational-biosensors-reveal-gpcr-signalling-from-endosomes-by-roshanak-irannejad-and-others.md) A signal measured from the whole [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) may therefore persist after its surface component declines. These possibilities require compartment-resolved measurements rather than an assumption that all signaling occurs at the surface.

To separate these roles, reconstitute a receptor and a defined [heterotrimeric G protein](../../../../../heterotrimeric-g-protein.md) in [lipid bilayer nanodiscs](../../../../../lipid-bilayer-nanodisc.md) or [lipid vesicles](../../../../../lipid-vesicle.md) of controlled composition, verifying receptor orientation and active fraction. Measure nucleotide exchange, [GTP hydrolysis](../../../../../gtp-hydrolysis.md) and effector response separately, then compare live-cell localization and signaling biosensors. Quantify expression, anchor processing, membrane integrity and sensor specificity; include rescue of a depleted [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) and an independent method of perturbing the same variable. **The same G protein nucleotide state can generate different outputs because membrane attachment, orientation, lipid contacts and compartmental residence determine whether a productive signaling complex can form.**
