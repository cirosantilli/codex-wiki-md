↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**[COPII](../../../../../copii.md) couples membrane deformation to selective capture, with cargo size and shape influencing how the coat is organized and regulated.** The coat is not merely a rigid container assembled after sorting. Membrane insertion, inner-coat packing, outer-coat scaffolding and the [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) cycle cooperate at [endoplasmic reticulum exit sites](../../../../../endoplasmic-reticulum-exit-site.md).

Assembly begins when the exchange factor [Sec12](../../../../../sec12.md) promotes loading of [GTP](../../../../../guanosine-triphosphate.md) onto [Sar1](../../../../../sar1.md). The activated [small GTPase](../../../../../small-gtpase.md) exposes an N-terminal [amphipathic helix](../../../../../amphipathic-helix.md) that inserts shallowly into the cytosolic [membrane leaflet](../../../../../membrane-leaflet.md). Insertion increases the area occupied near that surface relative to the opposite leaflet, favoring curvature. Purified-component experiments and changes to the [Sar1](../../../../../sar1.md) helix connected this insertion to membrane deformation and vesicle production.[pp. 605–617, Sar1 helix perturbations, membrane deformation and budding assays](../../../../../sar1p-n-terminal-helix-initiates-membrane-curvature-and-completes-the-fission-of-a-copii-vesicle-by-marcus-c-s-lee-and-others.md) The evidence supports a direct contribution to bending; it does not mean the helix alone supplies every force needed for formation and release of a physiological carrier. Membrane [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) composition, tension and [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) density affect the outcome.

Membrane-bound [Sar1](../../../../../sar1.md) recruits the [Sec23-Sec24 complex](../../../../../sec23-sec24-complex.md). [Sec23](../../../../../sec23.md) contacts [Sar1](../../../../../sar1.md) and acts as a [GTPase-activating protein](../../../../../gtpase-activating-protein.md); [Sec24](../../../../../sec24.md) supplies multiple cargo-recognition surfaces. The outer [Sec13-Sec31 complex](../../../../../sec13-sec31-complex.md) links inner-coat assemblies into a scaffold. Its geometry constrains membrane shape, while contacts with the inner coat help couple assembly to the [Sar1](../../../../../sar1.md) [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) cycle. Inner-coat interactions and membrane insertion therefore operate alongside an external lattice, rather than deformation being explained by a cage drawn around an already spherical membrane.

[cryo-electron tomography](../../../../../cryo-electron-tomography.md) and [subtomogram averaging](../../../../../subtomogram-averaging.md) of reconstituted coats resolved ordered inner-coat arrangements and different assembly geometries, illustrating how coat organization can support distinct membrane shapes.[main structural results, membrane-associated inner-coat organization and coat geometry](../../../../../subtomogram-averaging-of-copii-assemblies-reveals-how-coat-organization-dictates-membrane-shape-by-joshua-hutchings-and-others.md) Reconstitution permits the [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) composition and [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) components to be controlled, but high surface densities or stabilized [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) states can favor structures that are not the dominant transport intermediates in cells. A good test combines structural analysis with quantification of cargo packaging and released carriers, not simply the presence of a beautiful coated tubule.

For a [transmembrane protein](../../../../../transmembrane-protein.md) cargo, cytoplasmic export signals can bind [Sec24](../../../../../sec24.md) directly. Different recognition sites and [Sec24](../../../../../sec24.md) family members allow chemically and structurally diverse cargos to use the same basic coat. Mutations in individual [Sec24](../../../../../sec24.md) cargo-binding surfaces selectively impaired packaging of particular membrane [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), establishing separable recognition sites rather than only a general coat-assembly defect.[pp. 497–509, cargo-binding sites and selective in vitro packaging phenotypes](../../../../../multiple-cargo-binding-sites-on-the-copii-subunit-sec24p-ensure-capture-of-diverse-membrane-proteins-into-transport-vesicles-by-elizabeth-a-miller-and-others.md) A convincing selectivity experiment verifies that coat recruitment, membrane bending and packaging of unaffected controls remain intact. Loss of all cargo after a destabilizing [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) would support a different interpretation.

Soluble lumenal cargo cannot contact a cytoplasmic coat directly. A [cargo receptor](../../../../../cargo-receptor.md) can bind it in the [endoplasmic reticulum lumen](../../../../../endoplasmic-reticulum-lumen.md) and present an export signal on its cytoplasmic face. Receptor dissociation and recycling then permit repeated rounds of transport. Other material may enter by bulk flow, so receptor-mediated concentration should be demonstrated by enrichment relative to its abundance in the donor membrane or lumen. Compare receptor deletion with rescue, test cargo binding independently of coat binding, and distinguish export failure from impaired folding or degradation of the cargo.

Selectivity includes exclusion. Resident [endoplasmic reticulum](../../../../../endoplasmic-reticulum.md) [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) may lack productive export signals, be retained through interactions, or be retrieved if they escape. Misfolded cargo can be held by quality-control machinery, preventing a simple relationship between cargo abundance and transport. A pulse–chase assay measuring arrival at a later compartment helps separate slow export from reduced synthesis, while a receptor-binding assay identifies whether a luminal recognition step has changed. A coat [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) that indirectly changes lumenal quality control should not automatically be classified as a cargo-recognition [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md).

Large cargo creates an additional geometrical problem. An extended [procollagen](../../../../../procollagen.md) molecule cannot simply fit into a conventional small spherical carrier without bending, changing carrier shape or using a different export arrangement. [TANGO1](../../../../../tango1.md) links lumenal cargo recognition to cytoplasmic coat machinery; its depletion selectively impaired [collagen](../../../../../collagen.md) export, providing a molecular connection between cargo type and exit-site organization.[pp. 891–902, TANGO1 localization, domain interactions and collagen export](../../../../../tango1-facilitates-cargo-loading-at-endoplasmic-reticulum-exit-sites-by-kota-saito-and-others.md) TANGO1-dependent organization can help delay premature closure and coordinate additional membrane recruitment. This supports adaptable export machinery, but does not prove that all [collagen](../../../../../collagen.md) travels in one universally defined oversized [COPII](../../../../../copii.md) vesicle. Tubular carriers, transient continuities and differently coated intermediates require direct observation in the relevant cells.

The [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) cycle provides timing rather than a simple irreversible timer. [GTP hydrolysis](../../../../../gtp-hydrolysis.md) on [Sar1](../../../../../sar1.md), stimulated by the coat, can weaken membrane association and facilitate remodeling or uncoating. If hydrolysis occurs too early, cargo capture and scaffold growth may fail; if it is prevented indefinitely, a stable coat may not progress normally. Accessory factors and cargo loading can alter this balance. Compare normal [Sar1](../../../../../sar1.md) with binding- and hydrolysis-altered variants, but interpret accumulated structures with caution: a trapped structure is not necessarily a productive intermediate, and nonhydrolyzable [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) experiments need transport readouts as well as morphology.

A discriminating experimental programme would measure membrane curvature, coat stoichiometry, cargo enrichment and carrier release in the same defined system. Independently vary the [Sar1](../../../../../sar1.md) insertion helix, a [Sec24](../../../../../sec24.md) recognition surface and outer-coat assembly. In living cells, compare small soluble cargo, a membrane cargo and [procollagen](../../../../../procollagen.md) at matched synthesis rates, with controls for [endoplasmic reticulum stress](../../../../../endoplasmic-reticulum-stress.md) and cell viability. Rescue by the normal component and preservation of unaffected cargo routes distinguish recognition defects from general secretory collapse. **[COPII](../../../../../copii.md) accommodates diversity by using multiple cargo interfaces and a regulated, geometrically adaptable assembly process; it does not require one identical coat shape or one recognition mechanism for every cargo.**
