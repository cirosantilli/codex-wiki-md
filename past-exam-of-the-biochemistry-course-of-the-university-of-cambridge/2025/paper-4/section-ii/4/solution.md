↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**[nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) sugars provide activated, recognizable sugar units whose chemistry can be coupled to controlled glycosidic-bond formation.** Their importance is shared across [protein glycosylation](../../../../../protein-glycosylation.md) and [polysaccharide biosynthesis](../../../../../polysaccharide-biosynthesis.md), although some final transfer reactions use lipid-linked donors or a preassembled oligosaccharide rather than a free [nucleotide sugar](../../../../../nucleotide-sugar.md) directly.

A [nucleotide sugar](../../../../../nucleotide-sugar.md) normally carries a [monosaccharide](../../../../../monosaccharide.md) linked through its anomeric center to a nucleotide-containing group. Examples include [UDP-glucose](../../../../../udp-glucose.md), [UDP-galactose](../../../../../udp-galactose.md), [UDP-N-acetylglucosamine](../../../../../udp-n-acetylglucosamine.md), [GDP-mannose](../../../../../gdp-mannose.md), [GDP-fucose](../../../../../gdp-fucose.md), [ADP-glucose](../../../../../adp-glucose.md) and [CMP-sialic acid](../../../../../cmp-sialic-acid.md). The [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) is part of the recognition and leaving-group apparatus; the sugar's stereochemistry and substituents determine the unit available for transfer. Most examples use a diphosphate linkage, but CMP-linked sialic acid makes clear why “[nucleotide sugar](../../../../../nucleotide-sugar.md)” should not be equated with “nucleoside diphosphate sugar” in every case.

Activation commonly uses a sugar 1-phosphate and a nucleoside triphosphate. For [UDP-glucose](../../../../../udp-glucose.md), a simplified reaction is

$$
\mathrm{glucose\ 1\!\!\!-phosphate+UTP\rightleftharpoons UDP\!\!\!-glucose+PP_i}.
$$

Hydrolysis of [inorganic pyrophosphate](../../../../../inorganic-pyrophosphate.md) to two inorganic phosphates favors net activation under suitable cellular conditions. Coupling to a nucleotide-maintaining metabolic system makes continued synthesis possible. This does not mean every isolated glycosyltransfer reaction is strongly favorable under all concentrations: the net [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) depends on reactants and products. With a suitable acceptor hydroxyl group, the transfer step can be written schematically as

$$
\mathrm{UDP\!\!\!-sugar+R\!\!\!-OH\longrightarrow R\!\!\!-O\!\!\!-sugar+UDP}.
$$

The transferred group, the acceptor atom and the stereochemical outcome depend on the [glycosyltransferase](../../../../../glycosyltransferase.md).

Early biochemical fractionation and isolation of the cofactor in galactose-phosphate metabolism established the nucleotide-sugar intermediate rather than a direct conversion of free sugars.[pp. 333–350, isolation and characterization of the nucleotide-containing coenzyme](../../../../../isolation-of-the-coenzyme-of-the-galactose-phosphate-glucose-phosphate-transformation-by-r-caputto-and-others.md) For a modern causal assay, supply a purified [glycosyltransferase](../../../../../glycosyltransferase.md), a defined [nucleotide sugar](../../../../../nucleotide-sugar.md) and an appropriate acceptor; omit each component separately and identify both the glycosylated product and released [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md). Incorporation of a labeled sugar demonstrates transfer, but a coupled assay detecting only [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) release can be confounded by donor hydrolysis without productive [glycosylation](../../../../../glycosylation.md). Product analysis by [mass spectrometry](../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md) or [NMR spectroscopy](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) can test connectivity and stereochemistry.

Common donors form an interconverting metabolic repertoire. Epimerization can relate [UDP-glucose](../../../../../udp-glucose.md) to [UDP-galactose](../../../../../udp-galactose.md); oxidation can generate [UDP-glucuronic acid](../../../../../udp-glucuronic-acid.md); other pathways generate amino sugars or deoxy sugars. This lets metabolism distribute carbon among multiple glycan products. The same donor does not dictate the final glycan: the [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) selects the acceptor, the position of attack, the linkage and often whether synthesis continues. Thus [UDP-glucose](../../../../../udp-glucose.md) can support different polymers in different enzymatic contexts. The donor pool supplies building blocks, while the transferase and its organization encode much of the assembly specificity.

For [N-linked glycosylation](../../../../../n-linked-glycosylation.md) in the [endoplasmic reticulum](../../../../../endoplasmic-reticulum.md), [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) sugars supply residues used to build a [dolichol](../../../../../dolichol.md)-linked oligosaccharide. The early cytoplasmic steps use [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) donors; later lumenal steps also use [dolichol phosphate](../../../../../dolichol-phosphate.md)-linked sugars derived from nucleotide-sugar metabolism. [oligosaccharyltransferase](../../../../../oligosaccharyltransferase.md) then transfers the assembled glycan to a suitable [asparagine](../../../../../asparagine.md) residue of a [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). The acceptor sequon and its accessibility matter, so donor availability is necessary but not sufficient. Structural analysis of the bacterial [oligosaccharyltransferase](../../../../../oligosaccharyltransferase.md) [PglB](../../../../../pglb.md) identified how a [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) acceptor is recognized at the catalytic machinery, providing a tractable example of this final lipid-linked transfer step.[pp. 350–355, acceptor-peptide recognition and catalytic architecture](../../../../../x-ray-structure-of-a-bacterial-oligosaccharyltransferase-by-christian-lizak-and-others.md) The bacterial system illustrates conserved catalytic principles without implying that its donor structure or complete pathway is identical to that in mammals.

In [O-linked glycosylation](../../../../../o-linked-glycosylation.md) and later Golgi processing, glycosyltransferases often add individual sugar residues directly from [nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) sugars to an existing protein-associated glycan or other acceptor. Compartmentation is critical: a donor synthesized outside the [Golgi lumen](../../../../../golgi-lumen.md) must reach lumen-facing catalytic sites through a [nucleotide-sugar transporter](../../../../../nucleotide-sugar-transporter.md). In transporter-deficient cultured cells, expression of a functional [UDP-galactose](../../../../../udp-galactose.md) transporter restored a [glycosylation](../../../../../glycosylation.md) defect, linking donor access to the observed product.[pp. 295–298, transporter expression and complementation of Lec8 cells](../../../../../schizosaccharomyces-pombe-udp-galactose-transporter-identification-of-its-functional-form-through-cdna-cloning-and-expression-in-mammalian-cells-by-hiroaki-segawa-and-others.md) A donor pool measured from the whole cell therefore cannot establish its concentration at the [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md). Transport, nucleotide-product clearance, [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) localization and acceptor availability are distinct possible bottlenecks.

Polymer synthesis uses the same logic repeatedly. [glycogen synthase](../../../../../glycogen-synthase.md) extends a glucan using [UDP-glucose](../../../../../udp-glucose.md), whereas [ADP-glucose](../../../../../adp-glucose.md) is an important donor in plant [starch](../../../../../starch.md) synthesis. [cellulose synthase](../../../../../cellulose-synthase.md) transfers [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) from [UDP-glucose](../../../../../udp-glucose.md) while coupling elongation to translocation through a membrane channel. A cellulose-synthase structure containing the growing polymer and a transport channel showed how synthesis and extrusion can be coordinated.[pp. 181–186, BcsA–BcsB architecture and nascent cellulose](../../../../../crystallographic-snapshot-of-cellulose-synthesis-and-membrane-translocation-by-jacob-l-w-morgan-and-others.md) Other polysaccharides require alternating donors or distinct [enzymes](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) for branching. Polymer properties therefore depend on linkage pattern and organization, not just the chemical identity of its monomers.

This arrangement makes biosynthesis controllable. Changing a nucleotide-sugar-generating [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) can affect several glycans at once; changing one transferase can selectively alter a particular linkage; changing a transporter can restrict a compartment. Distinguish those mechanisms by measuring donor pools, localization and glycan products, with rescue by the appropriate gene or metabolite where the uptake route is established. An increase in total sugar does not necessarily rescue a missing donor-activation or transport step.

**[nucleotide](../../../../../nucleotide.md) sugars are universal in the sense of a widely shared activation-and-transfer strategy, not a claim that every [glycosylation](../../../../../glycosylation.md) [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) takes the same donor or that every final sugar-transfer step directly consumes a [nucleotide sugar](../../../../../nucleotide-sugar.md).** Lipid-linked intermediates, donor transport and acceptor specificity explain how the same chemical currency supports both selectively glycosylated [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) and long structural or storage polymers.
