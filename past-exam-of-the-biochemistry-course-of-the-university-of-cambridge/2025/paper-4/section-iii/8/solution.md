<h1 id="section-iii/8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

**Photosynthetic exoelectrogenesis is measurable electron export, but the complete physiological export pathway is not established as one universal mechanism.** Experiments must distinguish the intracellular source of reducing power, passage across the cell envelope and the final electrochemical reaction at an external acceptor. A light-dependent current alone does not identify all three steps.

In oxygenic [photosynthesis](../../../../../photosynthesis.md), [photosystem II](../../../../../photosystem-ii.md) oxidizes [water](../../../../../water.md) and supplies electrons to a transport chain. [plastoquinone](../../../../../plastoquinone.md), a cytochrome complex, [photosystem I](../../../../../photosystem-i.md) and downstream carriers connect charge separation to production of [NADPH](../../../../../nadph.md). In [cyanobacteria](../../../../../cyanobacteria.md), respiratory and photosynthetic electron-transfer pathways can share components; reducing power can therefore reach the relevant pools from both [light](../../../../../light.md) reactions and metabolism of stored carbon. In a eukaryotic [microalga](../../../../../microalga.md), chloroplast and cell-surface membranes introduce additional compartment barriers. Electron export by one organism should not be assigned automatically to the architecture of another.

A [biophotovoltaic device](../../../../../biophotovoltaic-device.md) couples such cells to an electrode. At an [anode](../../../../../anode.md), oxidation of an exported reductant or a cell-associated redox component transfers electrons into the external circuit. At least three broad mechanisms are possible: direct transfer through cell-associated components at the interface; soluble endogenous [redox mediators](../../../../../redox-mediator.md); or added artificial mediators that access internal or surface-accessible carriers. A fourth practical possibility is release from damaged cells. These routes may coexist, and their relative contributions depend on electrode material, potential, growth state and preparation.

Evidence for a soluble route includes current from separated cell medium, loss of activity after removal of a candidate reductant and restoration by an appropriate add-back. In a cyanobacterial bioelectrochemical system, extracellular [NADPH](../../../../../nadph.md) accumulated under illumination and device operation, its removal suppressed current, and addition of [NADP+](../../../../../nadp-plus.md) enhanced current generation.[main results, extracellular NADPH measurements, depletion and NADP+ addition](../../../../../nadph-performs-mediated-electron-transfer-in-cyanobacterial-driven-bio-photoelectrochemical-cells-by-yaniv-shlosberg-and-others.md) Those observations support a mediated route in the tested system. They do not prove that freely released [NADPH](../../../../../nadph.md) is the principal mediator in every cyanobacterium or electrode arrangement. The process releasing it, its physiological relevance and possible leakage must be tested separately.

Cell-envelope experiments help locate a barrier. Comparing [cyanobacteria](../../../../../cyanobacteria.md) with systematically removed outer layers showed that the [periplasm](../../../../../periplasm.md) and [bacterial outer membrane](../../../../../bacterial-outer-membrane.md) strongly influence the photocurrent response.[main results, comparisons of intact cells and cell-envelope preparations](../../../../../a-biophotoelectrochemical-approach-to-unravelling-the-role-of-cyanobacterial-cell-structures-in-exoelectrogenesis-by-laura-t-wey-and-others.md) Greater current after permeabilization can indicate that a previously internal reductant or carrier has become accessible; it does not establish that intact cells normally export through the same route. Measure viability, photosynthetic competence and leakage of cytoplasmic markers, normalize to active cell material, and compare the time dependence. A damaged-cell preparation can make a useful bioelectrode while being a poor model of normal physiology.

Artificial mediators provide mechanistic probes but can create a new pathway. A membrane-impermeant acceptor such as [ferricyanide](../../../../../ferricyanide.md) reports reduction at an accessible site, whereas a membrane-permeant [quinone](../../../../../quinone.md) may intercept electrons deeper in the photosynthetic apparatus. Ultrafast spectroscopic experiments showed that an added [quinone](../../../../../quinone.md) can extract charge on very short timescales from photosynthetic reaction-center systems in living cells.[pp. 836–840, ultrafast charge extraction by an added mediator](../../../../../photosynthesis-re-wired-on-the-pico-second-timescale-by-tomi-k-baikie-and-others.md) This changes what is physically accessible for engineered extraction. It does not demonstrate that unmodified microorganisms use that compound or that the complete external current follows the same rapid timescale: intracellular interception and extracellular transport remain distinct steps.

Photosynthetic inhibitors and mutants can constrain the electron source. If inhibition of [photosystem II](../../../../../photosystem-ii.md) decreases photocurrent, that supports a contribution from water-derived electron flow under those conditions. Persistence of current in darkness can reflect oxidation of stored carbon or previously reduced mediator rather than a failure of the light-reaction model. Conversely, acute inhibition can change the redox balance of the shared chain and secondary metabolism, so a decrease does not uniquely identify the terminal export carrier. Use more than one perturbation, independently confirm target inhibition and monitor [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) evolution or another photosynthetic readout.

A strong electrochemical design includes an abiotic electrode under identical illumination, cell-free medium, killed-cell controls and fresh versus conditioned medium. Control electrode polarization and distinguish sustained faradaic current from capacitive transients when switching [light](../../../../../light.md) on and off. Compare a washed cell pellet with its separated medium, test molecular-size fractions, and identify active molecules analytically. An added compound that increases current might be an electron shuttle, a nutrient, a membrane perturbant or an antioxidant; reversible redox behavior and mass balance are needed to classify it as a mediator.

Charge accounting is especially useful. For integrated faradaic charge $Q$,

$$
n_{e^-}=\frac{Q}{F},\qquad Q=\int I(t)\,dt,
$$

where $F$ is the [Faraday constant](../../../../../faraday-constant.md). A two-electron oxidation of one reduced NADP molecule can supply at most $2F$ coulombs per mole for a single pass. Thus $1\,\mathrm{nmol}$ can supply about $0.193\,\mathrm{mC}$ before regeneration. If a measured charge greatly exceeds that amount, the reductant must be replenished, recycled, underestimated or supplemented by another source. This is an illustrative bookkeeping test, not a measured mediator concentration in any cited device.

It is also important to distinguish extraction efficiency from cell performance. A larger photocurrent after physiological stress may reflect diversion from [carbon fixation](../../../../../carbon-fixation.md), altered permeability or increased damage. Measure biomass production, carbon balance and sustained current over time. A useful engineered route must remain compatible with sufficient [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation and repair, while an ecological explanation requires evidence that extracellular acceptors are encountered and beneficial outside an electrode experiment.

**Photosynthetic and respiratory reducing pools can supply current through condition-dependent export routes.** The envelope gates accessibility, and mediators can connect internal redox chemistry to an electrode. Native carriers and export steps still require identification in each system.
