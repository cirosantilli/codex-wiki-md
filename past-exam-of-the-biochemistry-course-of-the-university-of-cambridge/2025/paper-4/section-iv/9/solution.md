<h1 id="section-iv/9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

**A parasite can retain a photosynthetic ancestor's organelle because that organelle supplies essential metabolites after [photosynthesis](../../../../../photosynthesis.md) itself has been lost.** Retained [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) genes or chlorophyll-pathway genes therefore need to be interpreted function by function. Their presence is evidence of evolutionary history and possible biochemical dependence, not proof that an animal parasite fixes carbon using [light](../../../../../light.md).

Many [apicomplexan parasites](../../../../../apicomplexan-parasite.md) possess an [apicoplast](../../../../../apicoplast.md), a reduced [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) derived from an algal endosymbiont. The surrounding membranes, [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) [genome](../../../../../genome.md) and imported nuclear-encoded [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) connect the organelle to an endosymbiotic ancestry. The nucleus must supply most of its working [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), using targeting information to deliver them across the relevant membranes. A small organelle [genome](../../../../../genome.md) can therefore coexist with a much larger nuclear investment in [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) maintenance. Loss of photosystem genes does not imply loss of every ancestral [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) pathway.

Comparative evidence is strengthened by close relatives that still photosynthesize. Discovery of the photosynthetic alveolate [Chromera velia](../../../../../chromera-velia.md) close to the apicomplexan lineage made a photosynthetic ancestry more directly testable using phylogeny and organelle comparisons.[pp. 959–963, organism characterization and phylogenetic relationship to apicomplexans](../../../../../a-photosynthetic-alveolate-closely-related-to-apicomplexan-parasites-by-robert-b-moore-and-others.md) Such relationships reconstruct common ancestry; they do not establish that a modern parasite passed through the exact ecological conditions of the sampled relative. Independent gene losses, horizontal transfer and uneven sampling need consideration when inferring the ancestral state from one sequence.

Selection acts on the products that remain useful. The [apicoplast](../../../../../apicoplast.md) can contain pathways for [isoprenoid precursor biosynthesis](../../../../../isoprenoid-precursor-biosynthesis.md), [type II fatty acid synthesis](../../../../../type-ii-fatty-acid-synthesis.md), parts of heme metabolism and other functions, with the requirement varying by organism and life-cycle stage. A host may supply abundant carbon and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) while failing to supply a particular essential precursor at the correct place or rate. Retaining that precursor pathway can make the [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) indispensable even in a cell that has no [chlorophyll](../../../../../chlorophyll.md) or photosystems. Conversely, pathway loss becomes possible when another compartment or the host supplies an adequate replacement.

The clearest functional dissection is chemical rescue. Blood-stage [Plasmodium falciparum](../../../../../plasmodium-falciparum.md) could be maintained after loss of its [apicoplast](../../../../../apicoplast.md) when supplied with [isopentenyl diphosphate](../../../../../isopentenyl-diphosphate.md), identifying production of isoprenoid precursors as the essential organelle contribution under those culture conditions.[main results, IPP rescue, loss of apicoplast genome and organelle-import function](../../../../../chemical-rescue-of-malaria-parasites-lacking-an-apicoplast-defines-organelle-function-in-blood-stage-plasmodium-falciparum-by-ellen-yeh-and-others.md) This is stronger evidence than inhibition by an antibiotic alone: a defined metabolite bypasses the need for the organelle. The conclusion is stage- and condition-specific. It does not mean that every apicomplexan needs only that product, or that the [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) is dispensable without supplementation. Loss of an organelle marker alone would also be insufficient without showing sustained propagation and loss of independent organelle functions.

An informative consequence is that some maintenance machinery is essential indirectly. [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) translation, [genome](../../../../../genome.md) replication, [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) import and division may be needed because they sustain a single indispensable output. An inhibitor can therefore cause a delayed phenotype as an existing organelle and its products are diluted or inherited abnormally. To distinguish a direct metabolic block from loss of organelle biogenesis, follow growth over several generations, assay the pathway product and monitor organelle inheritance. Add the proposed rescue metabolite before a generalized lethal collapse, and establish that it can enter the relevant compartment.

The boundary of this explanation is visible in lineages that have lost the [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md). The compact [genome](../../../../../genome.md) of [Cryptosporidium parvum](../../../../../cryptosporidium-parvum.md) lacked evidence for the characteristic plastid-related repertoire found in several other apicomplexans.[genome analysis, organelle reduction and metabolic inventory](../../../../../complete-genome-sequence-of-the-apicomplexan-cryptosporidium-parvum-by-mitchell-s-abrahamsen-and-others.md) Such comparative loss supports the idea that [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) retention depends on remaining metabolic requirements rather than an unavoidable structural rule. Absence of a sequence from a draft assembly alone would be weak evidence; a stronger claim combines a high-quality [genome](../../../../../genome.md), broad searches for imported [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) and independent cellular observations.

A more surprising case is [corallicolids](../../../../../corallicolid.md), coral-associated apicomplexans whose [plastid](../../../../../plastid.md) [genomes](../../../../../genome.md) retain chlorophyll-biosynthesis genes despite lacking photosystem genes.[main plastid-genome analysis and cellular characterization](../../../../../a-widespread-coral-infecting-apicomplexan-with-chlorophyll-biosynthesis-genes-by-waldan-k-kwong-and-others.md) This separates retention of part of pigment metabolism from retention of [photosynthesis](../../../../../photosynthesis.md) as a complete energy-conversion process. Possible explanations include an unrecognized metabolic role for the retained [enzymes](../../../../../enzyme-split.md), production of a useful tetrapyrrole-related intermediate, or constraints imposed by pathway interactions. The gene inventory alone does not choose among them. Nor should the ecological label “parasite” be inferred solely from occurrence inside an animal if the actual effect on host fitness has not been established.

To test residual photosynthetic capacity, look for assembled photosystems, pigment identity, light-driven charge separation, appropriate [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) or electron-transfer signals and [carbon fixation](../../../../../carbon-fixation.md) with proper dark controls. Host tissue and its other symbionts are major confounders in a coral sample: a bulk photosynthetic signal may come from a different organism. Isolate the relevant cells or obtain spatially resolved evidence, and distinguish the parasite's transcripts and [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) from host or symbiont contamination. A retained chlorophyll-pathway transcript demonstrates expression, not photosynthetic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation.

To test why an individual retained pathway is selected, perturb it specifically, identify the biochemical product that changes and attempt a mechanistically appropriate rescue. A metabolite rescue can distinguish loss of a useful product from a structural role of the organelle; a localization-specific rescue can test whether the product must be generated locally. Evolutionary interpretation should then compare multiple lineages with different losses, rather than narrating every retained gene as an adaptation without functional evidence.

**The main explanation is selective retention after evolutionary remodeling: plastids can remain useful as biosynthetic compartments while the energetically elaborate photosynthetic machinery disappears. Exceptional retention of pigment-pathway genes highlights unanswered functions, but does not overturn the distinction between a photosynthetic ancestry, a retained biochemical pathway and demonstrable [photosynthesis](../../../../../photosynthesis.md).**
