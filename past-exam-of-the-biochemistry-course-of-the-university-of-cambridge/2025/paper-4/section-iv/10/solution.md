<h1 id="section-iv/10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

**The [isocitrate](../../../../../isocitrate.md) branchpoint balances carbon conservation against oxidative metabolism, using both rapid [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) regulation and slower changes in [gene expression](../../../../../gene-expression.md).** In [Pseudomonas aeruginosa](../../../../../pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md), the oxidative arm has two distinct [isocitrate](../../../../../isocitrate.md) dehydrogenases, conventionally called [ICD](../../../../../icd-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) and [IDH](../../../../../idh-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md), alongside [isocitrate lyase](../../../../../isocitrate-lyase.md). Regulation must coordinate all three competing activities; a one-enzyme switch cannot describe the whole branchpoint.

The carbon logic comes first. The oxidative [citric acid cycle](../../../../../citric-acid-cycle.md) route takes six-carbon [isocitrate](../../../../../isocitrate.md) through oxidative decarboxylation toward a four-carbon intermediate, supplying reducing equivalents and releasing [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md). The [glyoxylate shunt](../../../../../glyoxylate-shunt.md) uses [isocitrate lyase](../../../../../isocitrate-lyase.md) to produce four-carbon [succinate](../../../../../succinate.md) and two-carbon [glyoxylate](../../../../../glyoxylate.md); [malate synthase](../../../../../malate-synthase.md) combines [glyoxylate](../../../../../glyoxylate.md) with another two-carbon [acetyl-CoA](../../../../../acetyl-coa.md) to make four-carbon [malate](../../../../../malate.md). A carbon-only bookkeeping summary is

$$
\underbrace{C_4}_{\mathrm{oxaloacetate}}+\underbrace{C_2}_{\mathrm{acetyl\!\!\!-CoA}}\longrightarrow C_6\longrightarrow C_4+C_2,
\qquad C_2+C_2\longrightarrow C_4.
$$

After regeneration of the original [oxaloacetate](../../../../../oxaloacetate.md), the shunt can provide a net four-carbon skeleton from two acetyl units without losing their carbon through the bypassed decarboxylations. This is a carbon balance, not a complete equation for cofactors, [water](../../../../../water.md) and proton stoichiometry.

Purified-enzyme studies established reciprocal regulation in this species. [pyruvate](../../../../../pyruvate.md) and [oxaloacetate](../../../../../oxaloacetate.md) activate [IDH](../../../../../idh-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) while inhibiting [isocitrate lyase](../../../../../isocitrate-lyase.md). [ICD](../../../../../icd-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) is inhibited through [AceK](../../../../../acek.md)-dependent [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md), whereas [IDH](../../../../../idh-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) is insensitive to that control. [acetyl-CoA](../../../../../acetyl-coa.md) favors [AceK](../../../../../acek.md) kinase action; [oxaloacetate](../../../../../oxaloacetate.md) and [pyruvate](../../../../../pyruvate.md) favor its phosphatase action, and fumarate can strongly stimulate dephosphorylation. The [enzymes](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) have broadly comparable [isocitrate](../../../../../isocitrate.md) kinetic constants, so partitioning is not determined simply by one overwhelmingly tighter-binding competitor.[Table 1 and Figs. 2–5, purified-enzyme kinetics, reciprocal allostery and AceK-dependent phosphorylation](../../../../../gluconeogenic-precursor-availability-regulates-flux-through-the-glyoxylate-shunt-in-pseudomonas-aeruginosa-by-audrey-crousilles-and-others.md) Thus precursor abundance favors the oxidative route, while precursor shortage and acetyl-unit availability favor conservation through the shunt. The inference concerns metabolic signals, not a direct measurement of every intracellular flux from an [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) assay.

Why is that feedback appropriate? [oxaloacetate](../../../../../oxaloacetate.md) and [pyruvate](../../../../../pyruvate.md) are useful indicators of the availability of carbon skeletons for biosynthesis and [gluconeogenesis](../../../../../gluconeogenesis.md). If those pools are scarce while [acetyl-CoA](../../../../../acetyl-coa.md) is abundant, continued oxidative loss of carbon can worsen the shortage. Favoring [isocitrate lyase](../../../../../isocitrate-lyase.md) conserves [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) for replenishment. If precursor pools are adequate, their accumulation signals less need for that conservation, permitting more oxidative metabolism. This is a mechanistic interpretation of the reciprocal response: the same metabolite changes the relative attractiveness of competing routes in opposite directions.

Covalent and allosteric regulation solve related problems. [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) changes the fraction of active [ICD](../../../../../icd-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md); allostery can change the activity of [IDH](../../../../../idh-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) and [isocitrate lyase](../../../../../isocitrate-lyase.md) without waiting for synthesis or degradation. Because the second dehydrogenase remains available, suppressing [ICD](../../../../../icd-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) alone need not force most flux into the shunt. Likewise, induction of [isocitrate lyase](../../../../../isocitrate-lyase.md) does not guarantee high flux if its current effectors inhibit it. A useful minimal flux definition is

$$
f_{\mathrm{shunt}}=\frac{v_{\mathrm{ICL}}}{v_{\mathrm{ICL}}+v_{\mathrm{ICD}}+v_{\mathrm{IDH}}}.
$$

This assumes the displayed reactions account for the relevant isocitrate-consuming fluxes. It is a flux fraction, not a ratio of transcript abundance, [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) concentration or maximal assay activities.

For example, illustrative fluxes $v_{\mathrm{ICL}}=2$, $v_{\mathrm{ICD}}=3$ and $v_{\mathrm{IDH}}=5$ give $f_{\mathrm{shunt}}=0.20$. Inactivating four fifths of [ICD](../../../../../icd-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) while holding the other two rates fixed gives $2/(2+0.6+5)\simeq0.263$, far from complete redirection. These numbers are not measured [Pseudomonas aeruginosa](../../../../../pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) fluxes; they demonstrate mathematically why the additional [IDH](../../../../../idh-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) and reciprocal control matter. In a real cell, [isocitrate](../../../../../isocitrate.md) and effectors would also change, so fixed-rate reasoning is only a diagnostic toy calculation.

Transcription changes the available capacities over longer times. The genes [aceA](../../../../../acea.md) and [glcB](../../../../../glcb.md) encode [isocitrate lyase](../../../../../isocitrate-lyase.md) and [malate synthase](../../../../../malate-synthase.md). Promoter reporters, DNA-affinity capture and genetic perturbation implicated [RccR](../../../../../rccr.md) in carbon-source-dependent repression of the shunt machinery.[Fig. 2 and supplementary DNA-capture, regulator-deletion and protein-expression experiments](../../../../../transcriptional-regulation-of-central-carbon-metabolism-in-pseudomonas-aeruginosa-by-stephen-k-dolan-and-others.md) A later study directly identified [KDPG](../../../../../kdpg.md) as an [RccR](../../../../../rccr.md) ligand and linked RccR-dependent control of central carbon genes to aminoglycoside tolerance.[main transcriptional, KDPG-binding, structural and antibiotic-response experiments](../../../../../a-kdpg-sensor-rccr-governs-pseudomonas-aeruginosa-carbon-metabolism-and-aminoglycoside-antibiotic-tolerance-by-yujue-wang-and-others.md) A metabolite-sensing transcription factor can therefore adjust [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) supply according to carbon metabolism, while the already present [enzymes](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) remain subject to rapid regulation. Reporter expression must be normalized to biomass and growth state; increased luminescence does not directly measure flux.

The branchpoint is important during growth on [acetate ion](../../../../../acetate-ion.md) or [fatty acids](../../../../../fatty-acid.md), which supply acetyl units without automatically replacing the four-carbon precursors withdrawn for biomass. The shunt enables net assimilation into gluconeogenic material. However, carbon conservation has a cost: bypassing oxidative steps changes the yield and distribution of reducing equivalents and metabolites such as [alpha-ketoglutarate](../../../../../alpha-ketoglutarate.md). An exclusive shunt would not meet every requirement of the cell. Flexible partitioning allows the bacterium to exploit changing nutrient mixtures and maintain biosynthetic and energetic demands together.

This flexibility can also influence infection-associated physiology. Changes in oxidative metabolism can alter respiration, [proton-motive force](../../../../../proton-motive-force.md) and the physiological response to some antibiotics. A survival phenotype is not equivalent to a higher resistance-conferring target-mutation frequency, and an [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) essential in one carbon source need not be essential in every infection niche. Compare carbon-defined cultures, measure growth and respiration, and test whether the same metabolic perturbation changes both flux and antibiotic killing. The [RccR](../../../../../rccr.md) evidence supports a connection to tolerance in the tested conditions rather than a universal therapeutic prediction.

A rigorous test combines [carbon-13 metabolic flux analysis](../../../../../carbon-13-metabolic-flux-analysis.md), intracellular metabolite measurements, [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) state and gene-expression measurements. Use individual and combined dehydrogenase perturbations, isocitrate-lyase controls and genetic rescue. Confirm that the flux model can distinguish the oxidative route from the shunt and account for other anaplerotic reactions. **The branchpoint is a regulated allocation decision: allostery, [ICD](../../../../../icd-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) and metabolite-responsive transcription cooperate to conserve carbon when precursors are limiting and support oxidative metabolism when their supply permits it.**
