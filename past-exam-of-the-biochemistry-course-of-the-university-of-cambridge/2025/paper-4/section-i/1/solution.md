<h1 id="section-i/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**[phosphoinositides](../../../../../phosphoinositide.md) provide changing membrane identities that recruit, activate and release the endocytic machinery.** The strongest evidence combines specific [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md)-binding measurements, acute [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) manipulation, genetic rescue and time-resolved imaging. An altered uptake endpoint alone cannot identify whether the defect is pit initiation, membrane deformation, [membrane fission](../../../../../membrane-fission.md), uncoating or later [endosome](../../../../../endosome.md) traffic.

[phosphoinositides](../../../../../phosphoinositide.md) are phosphorylated derivatives of [phosphatidylinositol](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol.md). Their headgroups differ in phosphate position, so chemically similar membrane anchors carry distinct recognition signals. At the [plasma membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md), [phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol-4-5-bisphosphate.md) supports recruitment and activation of multiple components of [clathrin-mediated endocytosis](../../../../../clathrin-mediated-endocytosis.md), including the [AP-2 complex](../../../../../ap-2-complex.md), [epsin](../../../../../epsin.md) and [dynamin](../../../../../dynamin.md). Binding can involve both electrostatic attraction and stereospecific recognition; showing binding to a highly charged artificial surface does not establish physiological selectivity. Compare equal-charge membranes with different headgroups, vary the target [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) at constant total charge, and verify that a binding-site [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) preserves [protein folding](../../../../../protein-folding.md).

A decisive experiment is to remove [phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol-4-5-bisphosphate.md) rapidly after the [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) has developed normally. Chemically induced recruitment of a [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) phosphatase to the [plasma membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) caused loss of endocytic coats and impaired uptake, giving a causal link on a timescale much shorter than a constitutive knockout.[Figs. 1–4, inducible phosphatase recruitment, coat dynamics and uptake](../../../../../loss-of-endocytic-clathrin-coated-pits-upon-acute-depletion-of-phosphatidylinositol-4-5-bisphosphate-by-roberto-zoncu-and-others.md) A catalytically inactive phosphatase, recruitment without the active [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) and monitoring of [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) depletion separate [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) action from recruitment artifacts. Because the phosphatase converts one [phosphoinositide](../../../../../phosphoinositide.md) into another, the perturbation changes both [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) and product; it is not equivalent to selectively deleting one molecular interaction. Recovery of [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) and coat assembly, or an independent depletion method, strengthens specificity.

That experiment supports a requirement for [phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol-4-5-bisphosphate.md), but does not by itself assign its action to one [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). Multiple coat and [actin](../../../../../actin.md) regulators bind the same [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md). To isolate a particular recruitment step, compare normal and lipid-binding-defective [AP-2 complexes](../../../../../ap-2-complex.md) at matched abundance, test cargo binding independently, and assay membrane association and internalization separately. In a reconstitution, purified adaptors, [clathrin](../../../../../clathrin.md), defined [lipid vesicles](../../../../../lipid-vesicle.md) and a cargo-bearing membrane can distinguish direct lipid-dependent assembly from secondary changes in cell physiology. The interpretation must also account for [membrane tension](../../../../../membrane-tension.md), which can change the requirement for [actin](../../../../../actin.md)-generated force.

Removal of the same [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) is needed later in the pathway. [synaptojanin](../../../../../synaptojanin.md) is a [phosphoinositide](../../../../../phosphoinositide.md) phosphatase whose loss in mouse neurons caused abnormal [phosphoinositide](../../../../../phosphoinositide.md) metabolism, accumulation of coated intermediates and defects in [synaptic vesicle recycling](../../../../../synaptic-vesicle-recycling.md).[pp. 179–188, synaptojanin-deficient neurons, lipid measurements and vesicle phenotypes](../../../../../essential-role-of-phosphoinositide-metabolism-in-synaptic-vesicle-recycling-by-ottavio-cremona-and-others.md) This establishes that productive traffic requires turnover as well as production of the [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md). A reasonable mechanism is that dephosphorylation weakens membrane binding of adaptors after budding, permitting coat release and component reuse. Constitutive loss can also affect development and several trafficking steps, so the morphological phenotype should be combined with acute interventions and rescue by normal versus catalytically defective [synaptojanin](../../../../../synaptojanin.md). Neither an increased number of coated profiles nor reduced synaptic transmission alone uniquely identifies defective uncoating.

Different [phosphoinositides](../../../../../phosphoinositide.md) can act sequentially. Experiments implicating [phosphatidylinositol 3,4-bisphosphate](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol-3-4-bisphosphate.md) and [PI3K-C2α](../../../../../pi3k-c2-alpha.md) in late coated-pit maturation linked local [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) production to recruitment of [SNX9](../../../../../snx9.md), a membrane-remodeling factor.[Figs. 1–4, lipid depletion, kinase perturbation and SNX9 recruitment](../../../../../spatiotemporal-control-of-endocytosis-by-phosphatidylinositol-3-4-bisphosphate-by-york-posor-and-others.md) The mechanistic point is a timed recruitment signal, rather than a permanently uniform membrane composition. Compare kinase depletion with rescue by a normal or catalytic mutant, measure pit lifetimes and morphology, and verify that cargo remains available at the surface. A fluorescent [clathrin](../../../../../clathrin.md) punctum can disappear by internalization, disassembly or movement out of the observation plane; an independent scission or uptake assay is necessary.

Live imaging provides temporal information that bulk [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) extraction lacks. Coincidence-detecting sensors were developed to report [phosphoinositides](../../../../../phosphoinositide.md) specifically at clathrin-associated structures and follow their conversion during traffic.[pp. 410–414, coincidence sensors and lipid dynamics in clathrin-associated structures](../../../../../dynamics-of-phosphoinositide-conversion-in-clathrin-mediated-endocytic-traffic-by-kangmin-he-and-others.md) A probe's fluorescence reports its binding equilibrium, geometry and accessibility, rather than an absolute local [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) count. Overexpressed probes can buffer the [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) being measured; some domains bind soluble headgroups as well. Use low expression, lipid-binding-defective probes, known enzymatic perturbations and an independent reporter. Sensor arrival after a coat marker is evidence about accessible [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) at that time, not necessarily the instant of synthesis.

Downstream, [phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate.md) helps recruit effectors to [early endosomes](../../../../../early-endosome.md), while [phosphatidylinositol 3,5-bisphosphate](../../../../../phosphatidylinositol-3-5-bisphosphate.md) is associated with later endolysosomal function. These roles should be separated experimentally from initial uptake. Pulse-label a surface cargo, remove extracellular label, and follow its delivery, recycling and degradation. If uptake is normal but degradation fails, the result supports a sorting or maturation defect rather than failed entry. General inhibition of all [phosphoinositide](../../../../../phosphoinositide.md) kinases is especially difficult to interpret because it perturbs several compartments simultaneously.

A useful synthesis is a regulated cycle: local [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) synthesis makes an assembly site competent; coat and curvature factors recognize that site; membrane deformation and scission proceed; [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) conversion changes the set of favored partners; uncoating and endosomal sorting follow. This is a model assembled from distinct experiments, not proof that every endocytic event follows one rigid [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) sequence. **The evidence supports both spatial identity and temporal switching: a [lipid](../../../../../lipid.md) can be required for assembling an endocytic structure, while its subsequent removal is required for that structure to complete its task.**
