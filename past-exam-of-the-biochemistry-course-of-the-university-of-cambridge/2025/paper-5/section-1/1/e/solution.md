<h1 id="section-1/1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In lane 3, detergent removes the [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) barrier while both partner preparations are present. Their binding regions can then encounter one another even if they originally faced inward or were on poorly apposed [lipid bilayers](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md). The strong remaining full-length signal is consistent with extensive formation of protected [SNARE complexes](../../../../../../snare-complex.md).

Thus **detergent does not prevent partner association under the displayed conditions**; it removes the topological restriction on that association. This lane is the normalization reference, not a direct measurement that 100% of all initially supplied [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) is active. The weak signal in the detergent-treated partner-free control also argues against detergent simply preventing all cleavage.
