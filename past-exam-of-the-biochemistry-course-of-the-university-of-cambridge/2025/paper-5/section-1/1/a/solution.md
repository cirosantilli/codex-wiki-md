<h1 id="section-1/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**Partial proteolysis is one plausible explanation:** intact [SNAP25](../../../../../../snap-25.md) and a slightly shortened form would migrate as two nearby bands in [SDS-PAGE](../../../../../../sds-page.md). Heterogeneous processing, a retained [affinity tag](../../../../../../affinity-tag.md), or an anomalously migrating species are other possibilities. The gel alone does not identify the chemical difference. Because the [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md) were expressed in [Escherichia coli](../../../../../../escherichia-coli.md), a mammalian [post-translational modification](../../../../../../post-translational-modification.md) should not simply be assumed. A [Western blot](../../../../../../western-blot.md) establishing that both bands contain [SNAP25](../../../../../../snap-25.md), followed by [mass spectrometry](../../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md), could distinguish these explanations.
