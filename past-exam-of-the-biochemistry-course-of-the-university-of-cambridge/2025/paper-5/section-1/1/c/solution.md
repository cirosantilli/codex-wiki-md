<h1 id="section-1/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The recovered vesicle fractions contain bands at the expected positions for the supplied [SNARE proteins](../../../../../../snare-protein.md). The [v-SNARE](../../../../../../v-snare.md) preparation contains its principal band, and the [t-SNARE](../../../../../../t-snare.md) preparation contains both [t-SNARE](../../../../../../t-snare.md) and the [SNAP25](../../../../../../snap-25.md) doublet. Thus **protein-containing vesicle fractions have been recovered**, consistent with successful reconstitution.

The [SDS-PAGE](../../../../../../sds-page.md) experiment does not establish the orientation of the [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md), their proportion facing outward, the [stoichiometry](../../../../../../stoichiometry.md) in an individual vesicle, or [membrane fusion](../../../../../../membrane-fusion.md). Nor does a stained band prove that all of its [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) is correctly folded and active. Those are separate functional and [membrane topology](../../../../../../membrane-topology.md) questions.
