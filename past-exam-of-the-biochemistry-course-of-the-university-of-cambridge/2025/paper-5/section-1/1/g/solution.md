<h1 id="section-1/1/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Use a [membrane topology](../../../../../../membrane-topology.md) measurement on [v-SNARE](../../../../../../v-snare.md) vesicles alone, so that partner binding cannot mask an outward-facing domain. A membrane-impermeant probe directed against the binding-domain region would label only the exposed population while vesicles remain intact. Compare that signal with total accessible [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) after [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) disruption, using a trapped-lumen marker to check that the intact vesicles exclude the probe.

With comparable detection efficiencies and background subtraction,

$$
\boxed{f_{\rm outward}=\frac{S_{\rm intact}-S_{\rm background}}{S_{\rm permeabilized}-S_{\rm background}}.}
$$

An analogous [protease protection assay](../../../../../../protease-protection-assay.md) compares external accessibility with total accessibility. In either approach, [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) leakage and steric masking must be distinguished from genuinely inward-facing [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md).
