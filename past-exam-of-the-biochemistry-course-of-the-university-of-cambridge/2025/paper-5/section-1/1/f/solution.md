<h1 id="section-1/1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Lane 4 combines detergent with the absence of the [t-SNARE](../../../../../../t-snare.md) preparation. The [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) no longer protects inward-facing [v-SNARE](../../../../../../v-snare.md), and there is no [t-SNARE](../../../../../../t-snare.md) partner to provide complex-dependent protection. Consequently little full-length labelled [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) remains. This distinguishes **membrane-dependent protection** from **partner-dependent protection** in a [protease protection assay](../../../../../../protease-protection-assay.md). It also helps interpret lane 3: its strong signal requires the partner rather than [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) enclosure alone.
