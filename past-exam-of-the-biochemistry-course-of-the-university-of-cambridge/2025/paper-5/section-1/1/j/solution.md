<h1 id="section-1/1/j/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [J](../j.md)

Cold preincubation can accumulate docked, fusion-ready intermediates. On warming, that population proceeds promptly to lipid mixing, whereas newly combined vesicles must first encounter, dock and assemble productive [SNARE complexes](../../../../../../snare-complex.md). This explains the faster initial rise and larger signal at the displayed endpoint without requiring a different total amount of fluorescent lipid.

The original reconstitution experiments likewise distinguish docking from subsequent [membrane fusion](../../../../../../membrane-fusion.md).[Docked unfused intermediates accumulated at low temperature and fused on warming](../../../../../../snarepins-minimal-machinery-for-membrane-fusion-by-t-weber-and-others.md)
