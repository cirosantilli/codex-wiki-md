↑ **Parent:** [K](../k.md)

Triton disperses the [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) into detergent-containing particles, separating the fluorescent lipids enough to remove most [fluorescence quenching](../../../../../../quenching-fluorescence.md). Both traces approach the maximum-dilution reference even though their earlier lipid-mixing histories differed. **The final [fluorescence](../../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) jump is detergent-induced dequenching, not an additional burst of physiological [membrane fusion](../../../../../../membrane-fusion.md).** It provides a useful normalization endpoint for the available dye signal.
