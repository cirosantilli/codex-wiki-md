**The N-terminal 1–29 fragment and the 1–15 fragment inhibit invadopodium formation.** Their values are around one fifth of the control, and gelatin degradation is strongly reduced too. Full-length [t-SNARE](../../../../../../t-snare.md), empty vector and the 15–29 fragment remain near control values. The images agree: the inhibitory fragments leave much more intact gelatin beneath the cells.
