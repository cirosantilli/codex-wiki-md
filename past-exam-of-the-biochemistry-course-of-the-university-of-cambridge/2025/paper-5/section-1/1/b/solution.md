<h1 id="section-1/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [liposomes](../../../../../../liposome.md) could be separated from free [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md) by [density-gradient flotation](../../../../../../density-gradient-flotation.md): lipid-containing particles float to a different position from soluble [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md). [Size-exclusion chromatography](../../../../../../size-exclusion-chromatography.md) is another possibility if it resolves the large vesicles from the unincorporated [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md). Simple sedimentation can also collect sufficiently large vesicles, but [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) aggregates may sediment too. The important control is to show that the recovered [SNARE proteins](../../../../../../snare-protein.md) associate with the [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md), rather than merely copurifying with it.
