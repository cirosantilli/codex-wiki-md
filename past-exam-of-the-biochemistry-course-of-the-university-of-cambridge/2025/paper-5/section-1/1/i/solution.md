↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**There is no evidence for substantial lipid mixing during the cold preincubation.** The trace begins near its low-fluorescence baseline when warming starts; a substantial pre-existing dilution of the dyes would already have raised that baseline. Nevertheless, [vesicle docking](../../../../../../vesicle-docking.md) or [trans-SNARE complex](../../../../../../trans-snare-complex.md) formation can accumulate while the later membrane-rearrangement step remains slow. Absence of dequenching does not mean that the vesicles have had no interaction.
