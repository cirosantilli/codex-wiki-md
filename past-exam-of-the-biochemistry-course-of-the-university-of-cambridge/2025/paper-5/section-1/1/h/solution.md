<h1 id="section-1/1/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

[NBD](../../../../../../nbd.md) and rhodamine are initially close together in the labelled vesicle [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md), allowing [Förster resonance energy transfer](../../../../../../forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md) and suppressing donor [fluorescence](../../../../../../fluorescence-split.md). Mixing labelled [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) with unlabelled [lipid bilayer](../../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) dilutes the dyes, decreases energy transfer and increases the [NBD](../../../../../../nbd.md) signal.

The rise with the complementary [SNARE proteins](../../../../../../snare-protein.md), but little rise with protein-free vesicles, therefore supports **SNARE-dependent lipid mixing**. It is consistent with [membrane fusion](../../../../../../membrane-fusion.md), but does not by itself distinguish complete fusion from [hemifusion](../../../../../../hemifusion.md) or other causes of dye dilution. A separate [content mixing assay](../../../../../../content-mixing-assay.md) with a leakage control would test merger of the aqueous compartments.
