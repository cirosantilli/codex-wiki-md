<h1 id="section-1/1/o/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [O](../o.md)

The fragment might sequester another factor required for [vesicle trafficking](../../../../../../vesicle-trafficking.md) or [actin](../../../../../../actin.md) organization, rather than acting through [Munc18](../../../../../../munc18.md). For example, binding a different SNARE-associated [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) could lower the [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) available for productive endogenous complexes. This is a possible [dominant-negative effect](../../../../../../dominant-negative-effect.md), not evidence that the isolated N terminus contains the entire [SNARE](../../../../../../snare-protein.md) interaction surface.

A less specific alternative is cellular stress or reduced viability. Those effects could suppress both invadopodium formation and matrix degradation without selectively inhibiting the proposed trafficking pathway.
