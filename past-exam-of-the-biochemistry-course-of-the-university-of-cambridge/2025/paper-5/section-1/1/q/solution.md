↑ **Parent:** [Q](../q.md)

The **S61/E62 and S75/Q76 pairs** are especially important for the extra stimulation attributable to [Munc18](../../../../../../munc18.md). Their replacement leaves basal lipid mixing broadly present but makes the red and blue traces much closer together. In contrast, replacement of R47/V48 or D57/Q58 retains strong stimulation. The S75/E78/T79 replacement retains a smaller enhancement, suggesting a partial contribution under these conditions.

Compare the Munc18-dependent increment with each construct's own baseline, rather than comparing red endpoints alone. These results identify functionally important regions; they do not prove that every altered side chain directly contacts [Munc18](../../../../../../munc18.md). The original reconstitution experiments also support cognate-SNARE recognition.[v-SNARE-dependent specificity of Munc18 stimulation](../../../../../../selective-activation-of-cognate-snarepins-by-sec1-munc18-proteins-by-jingshi-shen-and-others.md)
