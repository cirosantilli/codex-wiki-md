<h1 id="section-1/1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

After warming, more labelled [v-SNARE](../../../../../../v-snare.md) remains full length: lane 2 is approximately 80–85% of the reference signal, compared with about 40–45% in lane 1. Formation of a protected [SNARE complex](../../../../../../snare-complex.md) provides a consistent explanation: the susceptible part of [v-SNARE](../../../../../../v-snare.md) is less accessible after complex assembly.

Protection is not itself a unique readout of [membrane fusion](../../../../../../membrane-fusion.md). Some [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) may face the vesicle lumen, and [SNARE complexes](../../../../../../snare-complex.md) can protect [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) without proving that the two aqueous compartments have merged. The inference depends on comparing these alternatives with the detergent and partner-free controls.
