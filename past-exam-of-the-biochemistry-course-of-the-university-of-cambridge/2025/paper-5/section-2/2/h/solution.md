<h1 id="section-2/2/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

In [surface plasmon resonance](../../../../../../surface-plasmon-resonance.md), target [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) in the mobile phase passes over the immobilized [antibody](../../../../../../antibody.md) domain. Binding changes the optical response near the sensor surface; response increases during association and falls when unbound target is washed away. The successive injections give larger responses as the supplied target [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) increases.

For a simple 1:1 model,

$$
\frac{dR}{dt}=k_{\rm on}C(R_{\max}-R)-k_{\rm off}R,\qquad K_D=\frac{k_{\rm off}}{k_{\rm on}}.
$$

The reported fit gives **$K_D=78\,\mathrm{nM}$**. This is a binding estimate for the tested preparation and model. Surface heterogeneity, transport limitations or rebinding could cause deviations; the visible mismatch between parts of the trace and fit discourages treating the binary model as exact.
