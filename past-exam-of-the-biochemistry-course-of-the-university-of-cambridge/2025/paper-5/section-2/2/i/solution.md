<h1 id="section-2/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Antibody specificity](../../../../../../antibody-specificity.md) requires comparison with alternatives. Measure binding to unrelated [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md) and to appropriate related antigens under comparable conditions, with a reference surface controlling nonspecific sensor binding. Preferential binding to the intended target would support specificity within that tested panel.

Competition with a soluble target, or an independent interaction assay, can help establish that the signal reflects the intended interaction. Specificity for a particular [epitope](../../../../../../epitope.md) is a stronger claim than specificity for the target [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) and needs evidence localizing the contact. A single good-looking binding curve establishes neither universal absence of cross-reactivity nor [epitope](../../../../../../epitope.md) identity.
