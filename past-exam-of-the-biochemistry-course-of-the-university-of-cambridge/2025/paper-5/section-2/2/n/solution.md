↑ **Parent:** [N](../n.md)

**A: downward when [ligand](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) is present; B: downward; C: upward**, over the intended [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) and [ligand](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) range. [Ligand](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) should favour its bound Y state, and the ligand-bound subunits should favour tetramer assembly. Without [ligand](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md), the unassembled state should predominate, rather than forming a stable ligand-free tetramer.

These preferences are thermodynamically coupled. The [ligand](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) must bind preferentially to states participating in the assembled structure strongly enough to compensate for the cost of shifting the unliganded conformational and assembly equilibria. At extremely low [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md), association entropy can still favour monomers; “ligand-dependent” does not mean concentration-independent assembly. This is a [thermodynamic linkage](../../../../../../thermodynamic-linkage.md) argument, rather than three independently adjustable arrows.
