<h1 id="section-2/2/k/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [K](../k.md)

The unliganded and peptide-bound preparations elute at almost the same volume, near 14 ml. They therefore have similar effective [hydrodynamic sizes](../../../../../../hydrodynamic-size.md) in this separation. A conformational change can occur without a large change in that measurement. The GFP–peptide-containing complex elutes earlier, near 10.5–11 ml, consistent with a larger particle; the later peak is consistent with excess unbound GFP–[peptide](../../../../../../peptide.md).

**The experiment supports [ligand](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) association but does not alone determine subunit number.** [Size-exclusion chromatography](../../../../../../size-exclusion-chromatography.md) separates by access to pores, which depends on size and shape rather than mass alone. The similar peptide-free and peptide-bound elution volumes therefore do not establish identical conformations or exact molecular masses.
