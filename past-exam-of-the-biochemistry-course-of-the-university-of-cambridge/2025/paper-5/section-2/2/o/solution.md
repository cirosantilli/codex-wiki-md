<h1 id="section-2/2/o/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [O](../o.md)

**A single predicted monomer structure is insufficient.** It may suggest a possible binding pocket, domain hinge or subunit interface, and a confident multimer model may support a particular assembly hypothesis. Those features do not establish that alternative conformations are populated, that [ligand](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) binding shifts their populations, or that occupancy at one site changes another site's binding strength.

Cooperative [allostery](../../../../../../allostery.md) concerns free-energy relationships among states, not only one static geometry. Structural comparisons across conditions, independent oligomeric-mass measurements and ligand-dependent binding or activity curves are needed to test the proposal. A low-confidence inter-domain orientation is likewise uncertainty about a model, not a prediction that the [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) is necessarily an allosteric oligomer.
