<h1 id="section-2/2/l/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [L](../l.md)

The main particles are approximately **120 kDa in both conditions**. With 30 kDa per [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) subunit, this supports a tetramer:

$$
\boxed{N\simeq\frac{120\,\mathrm{kDa}}{30\,\mathrm{kDa}}=4.}
$$

The small [peptide](../../../../../../peptide.md) contribution is not clearly resolved as a separate mass increase at this scale. Together with the microscopy, the results support a conformational change of an existing tetramer, rather than a large change in oligomer number. [Mass photometry](../../../../../../mass-photometry.md) reports the scattering-derived mass distribution of detected particles; it does not by itself identify which conformation a particle adopts or count small bound [ligands](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) precisely.
