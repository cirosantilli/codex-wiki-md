↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

[Protein structure](../../../../../../protein-structure.md) can remain similar after extensive [protein sequence](../../../../../../protein-sequence.md) divergence has obscured a sequence-only relationship. A structural comparison may therefore reveal a conserved [protein fold](../../../../../../protein-fold.md), plausible binding pocket or spatial arrangement of candidate catalytic residues that a direct sequence search misses. Such evidence can suggest a functional family and useful hypotheses.

**A similar fold does not uniquely determine function.** The same architecture can support different activities, and a predicted pocket does not demonstrate binding or catalysis. Confidence in the model, residue-level chemistry and independent biochemical evidence must be considered together. Structural classification complements rather than replaces sequence and experimental analysis.
