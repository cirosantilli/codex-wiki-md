**Tumours gain support by changing the behaviour of genetically non-malignant neighbours, recruiting circulating [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) and conditioning distant [biological tissues](../../../../../tissue-biology.md).** These interactions create a [tumour microenvironment](../../../../../tumour-microenvironment.md) that can supply growth signals, modify physical barriers and suppress immune attack. Reprogramming here usually means a change of [cell state](../../../../../cell-state.md) through signalling or altered surroundings; it need not mean that the neighbouring [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) acquires the [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md)'s [driver mutations](../../../../../driver-mutation.md).

Resident [fibroblasts](../../../../../fibroblast.md) are major recipients of tumour-derived signals, including [TGF-beta signalling](../../../../../tgf-beta-signalling.md) inputs. Activated [cancer-associated fibroblasts](../../../../../cancer-associated-fibroblast.md) can produce [extracellular matrix](../../../../../extracellular-matrix.md), remodel its organisation and secrete growth factors and [chemokines](../../../../../chemokine.md). These changes support carcinoma-cell survival, provide tracks for [cell migration](../../../../../cell-migration.md) and alter mechanical signalling through [integrins](../../../../../integrin.md). Matrix accumulation can also impair access of drugs and immune [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Reciprocal feedback matters: [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) alter fibroblasts, and the changed fibroblasts then alter [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) behaviour.

A direct functional example used fibroblasts isolated from human breast [carcinomas](../../../../../carcinoma.md) and normal mammary fibroblasts from the same patients. In a coimplantation [xenograft](../../../../../xenograft.md) assay, carcinoma-associated fibroblasts enhanced [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) growth and [angiogenesis](../../../../../angiogenesis.md) more strongly. Increased [CXCL12](../../../../../cxcl12.md) [protein secretion](../../../../../protein-secretion.md) connected stromal signalling with tumour-cell stimulation and recruitment of vascular precursors. The matched normal-fibroblast comparison helps distinguish an associated stromal state from the mere presence of additional [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), while perturbing the ligand–receptor axis tests mechanism.[Cell 121, pp. 335–348; matched fibroblast isolates, coimplantation assays and CXCL12-dependent effects](../../../../../stromal-fibroblasts-present-in-invasive-human-breast-carcinomas-promote-tumor-growth-and-angiogenesis-through-elevated-sdf-1-cxcl12-secretion-by-akira-orimo-and-others.md) Because these were transplantation experiments, they establish a growth-promoting capacity in that setting rather than proving that every fibroblast population is tumour-promoting.

Tumours also recruit and educate [macrophages](../../../../../macrophage.md). Tumour-derived [CSF1](../../../../../csf1.md) can support macrophage recruitment and survival, while macrophage-derived [epidermal growth factor](../../../../../epidermal-growth-factor.md) stimulates carcinoma-cell movement. In mammary-tumour experiments, blocking either side of this paracrine interaction reduced coupled migration, and [intravital microscopy](../../../../../intravital-microscopy.md) connected the [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md)' behaviour in living [biological tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md). This supplies a mechanistic explanation for why non-malignant motile [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) can increase invasion by malignant ones.[Cancer Research 64, pp. 7022–7029; EGF/CSF1-dependent migration and receptor-pathway perturbations](../../../../../a-paracrine-loop-between-tumor-cells-and-macrophages-is-required-for-tumor-cell-migration-in-mammary-tumors-by-jeffrey-wyckoff-and-others.md) [tumour-associated macrophages](../../../../../tumour-associated-macrophage.md) can also promote vascular remodelling and immune suppression, but their states vary; a single binary inflammatory-versus-supportive classification is inadequate.

The vascular compartment is another target. [hypoxia](../../../../../hypoxia.md) and tumour-associated signalling promote [protein secretion](../../../../../protein-secretion.md) of [VEGF](../../../../../vegf.md), which acts on [endothelial cells](../../../../../endothelial-cell.md) to stimulate [angiogenesis](../../../../../angiogenesis.md). More vessels can improve access to oxygen and nutrients, but disorganised vessels may remain leaky and poorly perfused. This can maintain [hypoxia](../../../../../hypoxia.md), increase interstitial pressure and compromise drug delivery even as the [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) expands. The advantage to the [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) is therefore not equivalent to restoration of a normal, efficient circulation. Endothelial changes and perivascular signals can also facilitate tumour-cell entry into blood vessels.

Immune [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) may be redirected from elimination toward tolerance. Suppressive [cytokines](../../../../../cytokine.md), [regulatory T cells](../../../../../regulatory-t-cell.md), [tumour-associated macrophages](../../../../../tumour-associated-macrophage.md) and [myeloid-derived suppressor cells](../../../../../myeloid-derived-suppressor-cell.md) can reduce effective antitumour responses. Altered [dendritic cell](../../../../../dendritic-cell.md) maturation and [antigen presentation](../../../../../antigen-presentation.md) limit priming, while nutrient depletion and inhibitory metabolites can impair activated [T cells](../../../../../t-cell.md). Induction of [PD-L1](../../../../../pd-l1.md) on [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) or neighbouring [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) further restrains [PD-1](../../../../../pd-1.md)-expressing lymphocytes. A [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) can therefore benefit from changing non-malignant immune-cell behaviour without removing those [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) entirely. Finding immune [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) inside a [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) does not establish that they are recognising or killing malignant targets.

The same logic can operate at a distance. Tumour-derived [extracellular vesicles](../../../../../extracellular-vesicle.md) can be taken up by resident [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) in prospective metastatic organs, altering inflammatory and stromal programmes before tumour-cell arrival. In experimental models, distinct exosomal [integrins](../../../../../integrin.md) were associated with different target organs, and interference with those integrins reduced uptake and subsequent [metastasis](../../../../../metastasis.md). Pretreatment experiments helped connect vesicle exposure to formation of a [premetastatic niche](../../../../../premetastatic-niche.md).[Nature 527, pp. 329–335; organ-selective uptake, vesicle pretreatment and integrin-blocking experiments](../../../../../tumour-exosome-integrins-determine-organotropic-metastasis-by-ayuko-hoshino-and-others.md) This supports a mechanism for conditioning recipient [biological tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md); it does not mean that one vesicle code completely explains the organ preference of every human [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md).

Reprogramming also includes metabolic cooperation. In pancreatic-cancer experiments, [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) stimulated [autophagy](../../../../../autophagy.md) in [pancreatic stellate cells](../../../../../pancreatic-stellate-cell.md), which secreted [alanine](../../../../../alanine.md) used by malignant [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) as a carbon source for the [citric acid cycle](../../../../../citric-acid-cycle.md) and biosynthesis. Tracing carbon and interfering with stromal [autophagy](../../../../../autophagy.md) connected the changed support-cell behaviour to [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) metabolism.[Nature 536, pp. 479–483; metabolite tracing, autophagy perturbations and growth under nutrient limitation](../../../../../pancreatic-stellate-cells-support-tumour-metabolism-through-autophagic-alanine-secretion-by-cristovao-m-sousa-and-others.md) A shared metabolic-expression signature alone would not establish this material transfer. More generally, to establish a specific transfer, trace labelled substrates into recipient metabolites and biomass, distinguish exchange from independent use of the same nutrient, and block the proposed transport step with rescue by the downstream product. The [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md)'s advantage should be measured as survival, expansion or invasion under defined conditions.

A general causal design would compare tumour-conditioned and control-conditioned media on purified recipient [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), with matched [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) numbers and viability, then wash and retest recipients to distinguish a persistent state from residual secreted factors. Ligand deletion in [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), receptor deletion in recipients and recipient-specific rescue identify directionality. Add-back experiments, direct-contact versus barrier cultures and spatial lineage labels distinguish soluble signals, matrix effects and replacement by contaminating malignant [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). In vivo, use cell-type-specific interventions and immune-competent models where immune suppression is the proposed mechanism; a lymphocyte-deficient [xenograft](../../../../../xenograft.md) cannot establish the full immune interaction.

Importantly, supporting [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) can also restrain disease. Depletion of alpha-smooth-muscle-actin-positive myofibroblasts in a pancreatic-cancer mouse model reduced fibrosis yet accelerated aggressive disease and immune suppression. The result shows that removing a conspicuous stromal component can eliminate protective functions as well as supportive ones.[Cancer Cell 25, pp. 719–734; selective myofibroblast depletion and resulting tumour and immune phenotypes](../../../../../depletion-of-carcinoma-associated-fibroblasts-and-fibrosis-induces-immunosuppression-and-accelerates-pancreas-cancer-with-reduced-survival-by-berna-c-ozdemir-and-others.md) **The [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md)'s advantage emerges from particular reciprocal interactions, so the relevant question is which [cell state](../../../../../cell-state.md) and function are altered, rather than whether all surrounding normal [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) are uniformly harmful.**
