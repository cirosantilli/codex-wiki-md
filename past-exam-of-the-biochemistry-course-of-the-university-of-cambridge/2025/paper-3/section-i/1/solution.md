<h1 id="section-i/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**Genome management helps explain the architecture of the eukaryotic [cell cycle](../../../../../cell-cycle-split.md), but it does not establish a single historical cause for its origin.** Larger [genomes](../../../../../genome.md), multiple [chromosomes](../../../../../chromosome.md), [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) and compartmentalisation create problems of complete copying and accurate allocation. The [cell cycle](../../../../../cell-cycle-split.md) solves these problems while also coordinating growth, environmental responses and reproduction. A functional explanation of a modern mechanism must therefore be distinguished from a reconstruction of why that mechanism first evolved.

The basic requirement predates [eukaryotes](../../../../../eukaryote.md): a reproducing [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) must copy inherited information and distribute sufficient copies to its offspring. [Bacteria](../../../../../bacteria.md) also regulate initiation, resolve intertwined [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and coordinate [chromosome segregation](../../../../../chromosome-segregation.md) with division. The relevant evolutionary change is not the invention of any copying–division cycle from nothing, but the origin and elaboration of particular [eukaryotic](../../../../../eukaryote.md) solutions. Conversely, a large [genome](../../../../../genome.md) need not encode a proportionately larger number of functions: repetitive and other [non-coding DNA](../../../../../non-coding-dna.md) can expand without a matching increase in regulatory complexity.

In a typical proliferating [eukaryotic](../../../../../eukaryote.md) [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), [S phase](../../../../../s-phase.md) duplicates nuclear [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and [M phase](../../../../../m-phase.md) distributes the copies through [mitosis](../../../../../mitosis.md) and [cytokinesis](../../../../../cytokinesis.md). Separating these processes permits a clear sequence: establish competent replication machinery, replicate, finish and repair, segregate, then separate daughters. [G1 phase](../../../../../g1-phase.md) and [G2 phase](../../../../../g2-phase.md) provide opportunities to integrate growth and environmental information. Their duration and even their conspicuous presence vary: early embryonic cycles can alternate rapidly between [S phase](../../../../../s-phase.md) and [M phase](../../../../../m-phase.md), whereas other [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) stop dividing or undertake [endoreduplication](../../../../../endoreduplication.md). Thus the familiar four-phase diagram is one regulated implementation, not a universal definition of all possible [eukaryotic](../../../../../eukaryote.md) reproduction.

A larger [genome](../../../../../genome.md) places an immediate demand on replication capacity. In an idealised model, $N$ simultaneously active bidirectional [origin of replication](../../../../../origin-of-replication.md) sites, each producing two [replication forks](../../../../../replication-fork.md) moving at speed $v$, copy a [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount $G$ in time $T$ only if

$$
2NvT\geq G.
$$

Here $v$ is measured in [base pairs](../../../../../base-pair.md) per unit time and $G$ in [base pairs](../../../../../base-pair.md); both sides have the same units. Evenly distributed origins with synchronous firing and no interruptions make the bound attainable in the ideal model. Real [S phase](../../../../../s-phase.md) includes asynchronous firing, unequal origin spacing and [replication fork](../../../../../replication-fork.md) delays, so the inequality is a capacity requirement rather than a reliable prediction of completion time. Increasing origin number solves a throughput problem but creates a control problem: each region must be copied once, with gaps filled and rereplication prevented.

[replication origin licensing](../../../../../replication-origin-licensing.md) separates permission from execution. When [cyclin-dependent kinase](../../../../../cyclin-dependent-kinase.md) activity is low, the [origin recognition complex](../../../../../origin-recognition-complex.md), [Cdc6](../../../../../cdc6.md) and [Cdt1](../../../../../cdt1.md) load inactive [MCM2-7](../../../../../mcm2-7.md) double hexamers onto [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Later kinase activity permits firing while blocking renewed licensing. Reconstitution with purified budding-yeast components showed ATP-dependent loading of head-to-head [MCM2-7](../../../../../mcm2-7.md) complexes; structural and biochemical assays distinguished loaded [DNA helicase](../../../../../dna-helicase.md) precursors from active unwinding machinery.[Cell 139, pp. 719–730; purified licensing reaction and double-hexamer analysis](../../../../../concerted-loading-of-mcm2-7-double-hexamers-around-dna-during-dna-replication-origin-licensing-by-dirk-remus-and-others.md) An explicit separation between licensing and firing makes parallel replication compatible with a once-per-cycle constraint.

Physical complexity also matters. Multiple [sister chromatids](../../../../../sister-chromatids.md) must remain paired after copying, acquire suitable spindle attachments and then separate together. [Cohesin](../../../../../cohesin.md) maintains their association; [kinetochores](../../../../../kinetochore.md) connect [chromosomes](../../../../../chromosome.md) to the [spindle apparatus](../../../../../spindle-apparatus.md); regulated [separase](../../../../../separase.md) activation removes cohesion. [chromosome condensation](../../../../../chromosome-condensation.md) and [DNA topoisomerases](../../../../../dna-topoisomerase.md) help manage the long, interlinked polymers. The [spindle checkpoint](../../../../../spindle-checkpoint.md) delays [anaphase](../../../../../anaphase.md) while attachment is incomplete, rather than relying on a fixed waiting time. Additional [DNA damage checkpoints](../../../../../dna-damage-checkpoint.md) and the [replication checkpoint](../../../../../replication-checkpoint.md) coordinate entry into division with the state of the template and replication machinery. These mechanisms can buffer errors whose costs grow with the number of essential elements that must be inherited, although none guarantees perfect fidelity.

A shared biochemical controller ties these tasks together. Rising [cyclin-dependent kinase](../../../../../cyclin-dependent-kinase.md) activity favours replication and then mitotic events, while regulated [ubiquitin](../../../../../ubiquitin.md)-dependent destruction of [cyclins](../../../../../cyclin.md) and other substrates creates directionality and resets the system. In fission yeast, an engineered single [cyclin](../../../../../cyclin.md)–kinase module could support ordered progression using activity thresholds despite removal of much canonical regulation. This demonstrates that a relatively small control core can organise complex downstream events; it does not demonstrate that the engineered strain reconstructs an ancestral [eukaryote](../../../../../eukaryote.md).[Nature 468, pp. 1074–1079; engineered minimal network, kinase activity thresholds and ordered cell-cycle progression](../../../../../driving-the-cell-cycle-with-a-minimal-cdk-control-network-by-damien-coudreuse-and-paul-nurse.md)

The hypothesis also has limits. [cell growth](../../../../../cell-growth.md), nutrient availability and allocation of [organelles](../../../../../organelle.md) would still need coordination even for a smaller [genome](../../../../../genome.md). Multicellular organisms add requirements for developmental signals and restraint of inappropriate [cell proliferation](../../../../../cell-proliferation.md). Closed [mitosis](../../../../../mitosis.md) in some organisms shows that nuclear-envelope breakdown is not an obligatory solution; ongoing [organelle](../../../../../organelle.md) replication also need not follow the nuclear programme exactly. Moreover, regulation can become more elaborate without a proportional increase in [genome](../../../../../genome.md) size. Present-day correlations cannot decide which trait evolved first.

Testing the historical claim would require comparative analysis of regulatory components across diverse [eukaryotes](../../../../../eukaryote.md) and relevant outgroups, reconstruction of [gene](../../../../../gene.md) gains and losses, and experiments that alter replication burden independently of [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) size and growth rate. For example, increasing origin spacing while holding [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) content fixed tests replication completion, whereas adding dispensable [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) with compensatory origins tests increased [genome](../../../../../genome.md) size more specifically. Measure completion, segregation errors, [genotype fitness](../../../../../genotype-fitness.md) and stress responses; an engineered defect under laboratory conditions cannot by itself reveal ancient selection pressures. **The defensible conclusion is that [genome](../../../../../genome.md) size and physical organisation supplied important demands that cell-cycle regulation meets, while the evolutionary origin of that regulation remains a broader, multicausal question.**
