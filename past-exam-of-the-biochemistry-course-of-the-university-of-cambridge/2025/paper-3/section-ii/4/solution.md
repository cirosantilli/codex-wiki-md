<h1 id="section-ii/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**Bacterial and eukaryotic replication obey the same chemical constraints but use different replication machines, initiation controls and chromosome-management strategies.** Both perform [semiconservative replication](../../../../../semiconservative-replication.md), extend new [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) in the $5'\to3'$ direction from a paired $3'$ end, and therefore require repeated initiation on the lagging template. A useful comparison uses [Escherichia coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md) and nuclear replication in a conventional [eukaryote](../../../../../eukaryote.md), while recognising that neither represents every organism in its group.

At an [E. coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md) [origin of replication](../../../../../origin-of-replication.md), [DnaA](../../../../../dnaa.md) helps open [oriC](../../../../../oric.md) and promotes assembly of the initiation machinery. The [DnaB](../../../../../dnab.md) [DNA helicase](../../../../../dna-helicase.md) is loaded with the assistance of loading factors, and two [replication forks](../../../../../replication-fork.md) move away from the origin. Initiation responds to growth and the nucleotide-bound state of [DnaA](../../../../../dnaa.md), with additional controls limiting immediate reinitiation. A bacterial [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) need not always be single and circular: some bacteria have several replicons or linear [chromosomes](../../../../../chromosome.md). Fast-growing bacteria can also begin a new round before the preceding round has finished, so replication and [biological cell division](../../../../../biological-cell-division.md) do not have a universal one-round-to-one-division temporal relationship.

Nuclear [eukaryotic](../../../../../eukaryote.md) [chromosomes](../../../../../chromosome.md) generally use many [origin of replication](../../../../../origin-of-replication.md) sites. In [G1 phase](../../../../../g1-phase.md), the [origin recognition complex](../../../../../origin-recognition-complex.md), [Cdc6](../../../../../cdc6.md) and [Cdt1](../../../../../cdt1.md) load [MCM2-7](../../../../../mcm2-7.md) around double-stranded [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). This is [replication origin licensing](../../../../../replication-origin-licensing.md), not yet active [replication fork](../../../../../replication-fork.md) movement. During [S phase](../../../../../s-phase.md), [cyclin-dependent kinases](../../../../../cyclin-dependent-kinase.md) and [Dbf4-dependent kinase](../../../../../dbf4-dependent-kinase.md) promote assembly of the active [CMG helicase](../../../../../cmg-helicase.md), containing [Cdc45](../../../../../cdc45.md), [MCM2-7](../../../../../mcm2-7.md) and [GINS](../../../../../gins.md). Elevated kinase activity also suppresses new licensing, separating origin competence from origin firing.

Purified budding-yeast reconstitution demonstrated these opposing kinase functions: [cyclin-dependent kinase](../../../../../cyclin-dependent-kinase.md) could inhibit [DNA helicase](../../../../../dna-helicase.md) loading while promoting assembly of active replication machinery after licensing, and [Dbf4-dependent kinase](../../../../../dbf4-dependent-kinase.md) supplied another required input. Ordered addition, component omission and kinase-dependence experiments establish biochemical requirements more directly than colocalisation in an [S phase](../../../../../s-phase.md) [cell nucleus](../../../../../cell-nucleus.md).[Nature 519, pp. 431–435; origin-dependent reconstitution and regulation of loading versus firing](../../../../../regulated-eukaryotic-dna-replication-origin-firing-with-purified-proteins-by-joseph-t-p-yeeles-and-others.md) This protects a multi-origin [genome](../../../../../genome.md) against [DNA rereplication](../../../../../dna-rereplication.md). Not every licensed origin fires: neighbouring [replication forks](../../../../../replication-fork.md) may replicate a licensed site passively, and unused licensed origins can provide reserve initiation capacity under stress.

The two helicases are distinct molecular solutions. In the conventional comparison, bacterial [DnaB](../../../../../dnab.md) translocates on the [lagging strand](../../../../../lagging-strand.md) template, whereas the [CMG helicase](../../../../../cmg-helicase.md) travels on the [leading strand](../../../../../leading-strand.md) template. Their associated [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) also differ. [E. coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md) uses [DNA polymerase III](../../../../../dna-polymerase-iii.md) with the [beta sliding clamp](../../../../../beta-sliding-clamp.md), a [clamp loader](../../../../../clamp-loader.md), [DnaG](../../../../../dnag.md) primase and [single-stranded DNA-binding protein](../../../../../single-stranded-dna-binding-protein.md). In conventional nuclear [eukaryotic](../../../../../eukaryote.md) replication, [DNA polymerase alpha-primase](../../../../../dna-polymerase-alpha-primase.md) begins synthesis, [DNA polymerase epsilon](../../../../../dna-polymerase-epsilon.md) primarily extends the [leading strand](../../../../../leading-strand.md) and [DNA polymerase delta](../../../../../dna-polymerase-delta.md) primarily extends the [lagging strand](../../../../../lagging-strand.md), supported by [PCNA](../../../../../pcna.md), [RFC](../../../../../rfc.md) and [replication protein A](../../../../../replication-protein-a.md). These are predominant assignments in the standard system, not an assertion that polymerases can never exchange roles during initiation, repair or stress.

Both systems must repeatedly make and mature [Okazaki fragments](../../../../../okazaki-fragment.md). In [E. coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md), [DnaG](../../../../../dnag.md) supplies an [RNA primer](../../../../../rna-primer.md), and [DNA polymerase I](../../../../../dna-polymerase-i.md) participates in primer removal and gap filling before [DNA ligase](../../../../../dna-ligase.md) seals the backbone. Nuclear [eukaryotic](../../../../../eukaryote.md) initiation uses a short [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) segment followed by [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) made by [DNA polymerase alpha-primase](../../../../../dna-polymerase-alpha-primase.md). Subsequent strand-displacement synthesis, [FEN1](../../../../../fen1.md) and other processing activities remove primer-containing material, and [DNA ligase](../../../../../dna-ligase.md) completes joining. Eukaryotic fragments are generally shorter and their formation is integrated with [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) assembly; bacterial and eukaryotic [lagging strand](../../../../../lagging-strand.md) maturation should not be reduced to identical [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) names with different labels.

A shared [replication fork](../../../../../replication-fork.md) architecture does not imply a perfectly rigid, synchronised machine. [single-molecule fluorescence microscopy](../../../../../single-molecule-fluorescence-microscopy.md) of reconstituted [E. coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md) replication found that leading- and [lagging strand](../../../../../lagging-strand.md) polymerases could pause and change rates independently, while [DNA helicase](../../../../../dna-helicase.md) behaviour allowed recoupling. This shows why similar average synthesis rates do not prove lockstep molecular progression.[Cell 169, pp. 1201–1213.e17; real-time single-molecule trajectories and independent polymerase dynamics](../../../../../independent-and-stochastic-action-of-dna-polymerases-in-the-replisome-by-james-e-graham-and-others.md) Omission controls establish which reaction requires a component, but a purified short-template assay cannot reproduce every obstacle of a living [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md).

Chromosome context creates further differences. Nuclear [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is packaged into [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md), requiring [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) disassembly and reassembly and coordination with replication-associated inheritance of chromatin states. Multiple linear [chromosomes](../../../../../chromosome.md) also create an [end-replication problem](../../../../../end-replication-problem.md), commonly addressed by [telomerase](../../../../../telomerase.md) and specialised [telomere](../../../../../telomere.md) maintenance. Bacterial circular [chromosomes](../../../../../chromosome.md) avoid that particular end problem but still require resolution of [DNA supercoiling](../../../../../dna-supercoiling.md), [DNA catenanes](../../../../../dna-catenane.md) and [chromosome dimers](../../../../../chromosome-dimer.md). [DNA topoisomerases](../../../../../dna-topoisomerase.md) manage topology in both groups. Converging [replication forks](../../../../../replication-fork.md) terminate replication in both; the [Tus-Ter system](../../../../../tus-ter-system.md) is a specific feature of certain bacteria, including [E. coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md), rather than a universal bacterial termination mechanism.

Finally, replication is embedded in different cellular schedules. Bacterial synthesis shares the cellular compartment with [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) and must manage collisions with it; nuclear [eukaryotic](../../../../../eukaryote.md) replication also encounters [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) and other obstacles, despite the separation of nuclear [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) from cytoplasmic [translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md). [ATR](../../../../../atr.md)–[Chk1](../../../../../chk1.md) signalling in many [eukaryotes](../../../../../eukaryote.md) couples replication stress to origin use and cell-cycle progression. Bacteria use their own damage and fork-recovery programmes, including the [SOS response](../../../../../sos-response.md). Neither system is simply unregulated.

[Archaea](../../../../../archaea.md) should not be grouped indiscriminately with [bacteria](../../../../../bacteria.md) in this comparison: their lack of a [cell nucleus](../../../../../cell-nucleus.md) does not make their replication machinery bacterial, and several core components have closer relationships to the eukaryotic machinery. Comparative experiments should therefore ask separately about initiation, elongation, fidelity, chromatin handling and completion. Measure newly synthesised products as well as [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) recruitment, verify that an apparent initiation defect is not degradation of the template, and distinguish a [replication fork](../../../../../replication-fork.md)'s instantaneous speed from whole-genome completion time. **The deepest difference is how distinct molecular machines are organised around [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) architecture and cell-cycle control; the underlying templated chemistry and the need to preserve complete [genomes](../../../../../genome.md) are shared.**
