**A common transition into [anaphase](../../../../../anaphase.md) both releases [chromosomes](../../../../../chromosome.md) for separation and permits the spindle to organise [cytokinesis](../../../../../cytokinesis.md); later safeguards delay final separation when segregation remains incomplete.** Temporal coordination makes the events occur in the right order, while spatial coordination positions division between the separating [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) sets. The two forms of control interact but are experimentally distinguishable.

[S phase](../../../../../s-phase.md) establishes [sister chromatids](../../../../../sister-chromatids.md) and their association through [cohesin](../../../../../cohesin.md). During [mitosis](../../../../../mitosis.md), [chromosome condensation](../../../../../chromosome-condensation.md) and [DNA topoisomerase II](../../../../../dna-topoisomerase-ii.md) activity help prepare the copies for separation. Each sister's [kinetochore](../../../../../kinetochore.md) should attach to the opposite spindle pole, producing [biorientation](../../../../../biorientation.md). [Aurora B](../../../../../aurora-b.md) contributes to error correction by destabilising inappropriate attachments. Attachment and tension affect this process, but the [spindle checkpoint](../../../../../spindle-checkpoint.md) should not be described simply as a direct meter of equal tension on every [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md).

Unattached [kinetochores](../../../../../kinetochore.md) catalyse formation of the [mitotic checkpoint complex](../../../../../mitotic-checkpoint-complex.md), involving [Mad2](../../../../../mad2.md), [BubR1](../../../../../bubr1.md), [Bub3](../../../../../bub3.md) and [Cdc20](../../../../../cdc20.md). This inhibits productive [anaphase-promoting complex](../../../../../anaphase-promoting-complex.md) activity toward key substrates. In a classic causal experiment, destruction of the last unattached [kinetochore](../../../../../kinetochore.md) allowed [anaphase](../../../../../anaphase.md) to proceed, with chromosome-arm damage providing an important control. It was the inhibitory signalling structure, rather than the physical presence of one unaligned [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) somewhere in the [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), that maintained the delay.[Journal of Cell Biology 130, pp. 941–948; laser ablation of the last unattached kinetochore and chromosome-arm controls](../../../../../the-checkpoint-delaying-anaphase-in-response-to-chromosome-monoorientation-is-mediated-by-an-inhibitory-signal-produced-by-unattached-kinetochores-by-conly-l-rieder-and-others.md)

Once attachment-associated checkpoint signalling is silenced and inhibitory complexes are dismantled, [anaphase-promoting complex](../../../../../anaphase-promoting-complex.md) with [Cdc20](../../../../../cdc20.md) promotes [ubiquitin](../../../../../ubiquitin.md)-dependent destruction of [securin](../../../../../securin.md) and mitotic [cyclin B](../../../../../cyclin-b.md). [securin](../../../../../securin.md) destruction permits [separase](../../../../../separase.md) activity, which cleaves the remaining cohesion-bearing kleisin subunit. Non-cleavable [Scc1](../../../../../scc1.md) blocked sister separation in budding yeast, directly linking regulated proteolysis to the loss of [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) cohesion.[Nature 400, pp. 37–42; cleavage-resistant Scc1 and failure of sister-chromatid separation](../../../../../sister-chromatid-separation-at-anaphase-onset-is-promoted-by-cleavage-of-the-cohesin-subunit-scc1-by-frank-uhlmann-and-others.md) Chromosome movement can then proceed through shortening of kinetochore-associated [microtubules](../../../../../microtubule.md) and separation of the spindle poles.

Destruction of [cyclin B](../../../../../cyclin-b.md) also lowers [CDK1](../../../../../cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) activity. This changes the phosphorylation balance, allowing [protein phosphatases](../../../../../protein-phosphatase.md) to reverse mitotic modifications and permitting [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) decondensation, nuclear reassembly and cytokinetic organisation. Thus regulated proteolysis produces two coordinated outputs: removal of a physical [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) linkage and removal of the mitotic kinase state. Their sharing of a controller improves ordering, but it does not mean that [cytokinesis](../../../../../cytokinesis.md) physically waits for a sensor to count every [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) at its destination.

For animal [cytokinesis](../../../../../cytokinesis.md), overlapping [microtubules](../../../../../microtubule.md) of the [central spindle](../../../../../central-spindle.md) and signals associated with astral [microtubules](../../../../../microtubule.md) specify an equatorial cortical region. [centralspindlin](../../../../../centralspindlin.md) recruits and regulates the guanine-nucleotide exchange factor [ECT2](../../../../../ect2.md), which promotes local [RhoA](../../../../../rhoa.md) activation. [RhoA](../../../../../rhoa.md) stimulates [actin](../../../../../actin.md) assembly and [myosin II](../../../../../myosin-ii.md) activity, organising the [actomyosin ring](../../../../../actomyosin-ring.md) that drives furrow ingression. This geometry aligns the division plane between the segregating [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) masses. Spindle position can itself be set by cortical [cell polarity](../../../../../cell-polarity.md) cues, explaining how the same machinery supports either approximately symmetric or asymmetric division.

Depletion and localisation experiments distinguish the biochemical signal from a simple structural scaffold. Experiments in human [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) showed that [ECT2](../../../../../ect2.md) is required for cortical [RhoA](../../../../../rhoa.md) and furrow assembly, and that different [centralspindlin](../../../../../centralspindlin.md) subunits have distinguishable consequences for these events. Loss of central-spindle localisation need not abolish every equatorial signal, because additional spatial inputs can persist.[Journal of Cell Biology 170, pp. 571–582; ECT2 and centralspindlin depletion, interaction and cortical RhoA assays](../../../../../an-ect2-centralspindlin-complex-regulates-the-localization-and-function-of-rhoa-by-ozlem-yuce-and-others.md) Accordingly, central-spindle disruption should be assessed alongside astral signals, spindle orientation and direct measurements of the cortical activity zone.

Furrow ingression leaves an intercellular bridge with a [midbody](../../../../../midbody.md). Final [abscission](../../../../../abscission.md) involves [ESCRT-III](../../../../../escrt-iii.md)-dependent membrane-remodelling machinery and must be distinguished from the earlier contractile event. Chromatin trapped in the bridge can sustain [Aurora B](../../../../../aurora-b.md)-dependent signalling and delay abscission. Human-cell experiments connected persistent [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) bridges with continued kinase activity and delayed final separation, reducing the risk of unsuccessful division and furrow regression.[Cell 136, pp. 473–484; chromosome bridges, persistent Aurora B activity and delayed abscission](../../../../../aurora-b-mediated-abscission-checkpoint-protects-against-tetraploidization-by-patrick-steigemann-and-others.md) This safeguard can provide time to resolve a problem; persistent bridges can still lead to breakage, regression or abnormal descendants.

A decisive experimental series would image labelled [chromosomes](../../../../../chromosome.md), [microtubules](../../../../../microtubule.md), [cyclin B](../../../../../cyclin-b.md), active [RhoA](../../../../../rhoa.md) and the [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) boundary in the same [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Acute checkpoint inhibition predicts premature [anaphase](../../../../../anaphase.md) and increased segregation errors. Stabilised [securin](../../../../../securin.md) or cleavage-resistant cohesion should obstruct separation; persistent [cyclin B](../../../../../cyclin-b.md) should obstruct normal mitotic exit. Acute [ECT2](../../../../../ect2.md) loss can impair [cytokinesis](../../../../../cytokinesis.md) even when [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) segregation proceeds, demonstrating separable downstream machinery. Use timed interventions after spindle assembly, independent depletion reagents, appropriately expressed rescue constructs and controls for global [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) damage. Count [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) allocation, furrow initiation, ingression and abscission separately; a binucleate endpoint does not reveal which step failed.

The checkpoint also has blind spots. A [merotelic attachment](../../../../../merotelic-attachment.md), where one kinetochore binds microtubules from both poles, may evade an unattached-kinetochore signal and generate a lagging [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md). **Coordination is therefore layered rather than absolute: attachment surveillance, a shared proteolytic transition, spindle-directed cortical signalling and a late abscission checkpoint jointly reduce the chance of partitioning the [genome](../../../../../genome.md) incorrectly.** The detailed geometry differs in organisms that divide with a [cell plate](../../../../../cell-plate.md) or other cytokinetic mechanisms.
