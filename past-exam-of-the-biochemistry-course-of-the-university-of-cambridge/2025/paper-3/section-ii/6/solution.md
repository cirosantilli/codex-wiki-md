<h1 id="section-ii/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**Epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition can help [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) change adhesion, move, survive stress and enter new environments, but its contribution depends on the state reached and the stage of progression.** [Epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition](../../../../../epithelial-to-mesenchymal-transition.md) is a regulated change of phenotype and behaviour, often reversible. It should not be equated with every instance of invasion, with a single marker, or with a permanent conversion of a [carcinoma](../../../../../carcinoma.md) into an unrelated [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) lineage.

An [epithelial cell](../../../../../epithelial-cell.md) normally maintains apical–basal [cell polarity](../../../../../cell-polarity.md), organised [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md)–[biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) contacts and interactions with a [basement membrane](../../../../../basement-membrane.md). During a transition, regulatory factors including [Snail](../../../../../snail.md), [ZEB1](../../../../../zeb1.md) and [Twist1](../../../../../twist1.md) can alter expression of adhesion and cytoskeletal programmes. Reduced [E-cadherin](../../../../../e-cadherin.md)-mediated adhesion, junctional reorganisation and changed [integrin](../../../../../integrin.md) interactions can permit [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) to detach or rearrange while responding to their surroundings. [TGF-beta signalling](../../../../../tgf-beta-signalling.md), growth-factor pathways, inflammation and mechanical conditions can supply inducing inputs. Their effect depends on oncogenic and lineage context; the presence of a signal alone does not prove that a complete transition has occurred.

The clearest immediate advantage can be local dissemination. Altered adhesion and motility allow [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) to move through [biological tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md), interact with matrix tracks and reach vessels. A classic murine mammary-tumour study linked [Twist1](../../../../../twist1.md) suppression to reduced [metastasis](../../../../../metastasis.md) and linked ectopic activity to loss of epithelial adhesion and increased motility. Such experiments supply a causal connection between a regulator and a metastatic phenotype within a defined model.[Cell 117, pp. 927–939; Twist suppression, adhesion changes and mammary-to-lung metastasis assays](../../../../../twist-a-master-regulator-of-morphogenesis-plays-an-essential-role-in-tumor-metastasis-by-jing-yang-and-others.md) To attribute the result specifically to motility, rather than fewer viable [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), compare primary growth, survival and the number of [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) entering the circulation.

Dissemination has several stages: local invasion, [intravasation](../../../../../intravasation.md), survival in circulation, [extravasation](../../../../../extravasation.md) and growth in a distant organ. A transition that helps the first stage need not help the last. In an inducible squamous-carcinoma model, activation of [Twist1](../../../../../twist1.md) promoted dissemination, whereas turning it off at distant sites allowed proliferation and metastatic outgrowth. This demonstrates a stage-dependent benefit of [epithelial-mesenchymal plasticity](../../../../../epithelial-mesenchymal-plasticity.md) rather than an obligatory advantage of staying fully mesenchymal.[Cancer Cell 22, pp. 725–736; inducible Twist1, dissemination and reversal during metastatic outgrowth](../../../../../spatiotemporal-regulation-of-epithelial-mesenchymal-transition-is-essential-for-squamous-cell-carcinoma-metastasis-by-jeff-h-tsai-and-others.md) [mesenchymal-to-epithelial transition](../../../../../mesenchymal-to-epithelial-transition.md) can consequently contribute to colonisation, although its necessity is not universal across cancers.

Intermediate states are important. A [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) may retain epithelial contacts while acquiring motility, producing collective invasion or mixed epithelial–mesenchymal features. These states cannot be placed reliably on one axis using [E-cadherin](../../../../../e-cadherin.md) or [vimentin](../../../../../vimentin.md) alone. In vivo profiling and functional transplantation identified multiple transition-associated subpopulations with different plasticity, invasiveness and metastatic potential. Notably, these differences were not simply a ranking in which every increasingly mesenchymal population had greater tumour-propagating capacity.[Nature 556, pp. 463–468; phenotypic subpopulations, plasticity and metastatic-potential comparisons](../../../../../identification-of-the-tumour-transition-states-occurring-during-emt-by-ievgenia-pastushenko-and-others.md) The result argues for measuring behaviour and state jointly, rather than treating a score for [epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition](../../../../../epithelial-to-mesenchymal-transition.md) as a universal measure of malignancy.

Another contribution is treatment persistence. Transition-associated states can change proliferation, survival signalling and response to environmental stress, allowing some [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) to survive treatment and later re-expand. In a breast-cancer lineage-tracing study, [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) marked by the selected mesenchymal reporters were not required to account for all observed lung [metastases](../../../../../metastasis.md), but were associated with survival after chemotherapy and recurrent disease. The findings separate a contribution to [chemoresistance](../../../../../chemoresistance.md) from a universal requirement for metastatic seeding.[Nature 527, pp. 472–476; mesenchymal lineage reporters, lung metastases and chemotherapy-surviving populations](../../../../../epithelial-to-mesenchymal-transition-is-not-required-for-lung-metastasis-but-contributes-to-chemoresistance-by-kari-r-fischer-and-others.md) A surviving population may have been selected from a pre-existing state or induced by therapy; time-resolved tracing is needed to distinguish these possibilities.

Transition-associated changes can also alter communication with the [tumour microenvironment](../../../../../tumour-microenvironment.md), including immune-cell recruitment or inhibitory signalling in particular settings. Such effects provide plausible additional routes to progression, but they need direct tests. A correlation between an expression signature for [epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition](../../../../../epithelial-to-mesenchymal-transition.md) and low [T cell](../../../../../t-cell.md) activity could instead reflect shared induction by [TGF-beta signalling](../../../../../tgf-beta-signalling.md) or contamination of a bulk sample by [fibroblasts](../../../../../fibroblast.md). Cell-type-resolved measurements are essential before assigning a stromal transcript to a transitioning malignant [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md).

Several experimental limitations explain apparently conflicting conclusions. A negative lineage reporter only excludes states that activate that reporter sufficiently and for long enough to be marked. Different [transcription factors](../../../../../transcription-factor.md) can substitute for each other, and loss of one factor need not abolish every relevant transition. Conversely, a regulator of [epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition](../../../../../epithelial-to-mesenchymal-transition.md) can affect survival or [cell proliferation](../../../../../cell-proliferation.md) independently of the adhesion programme. Therefore, reduced [metastasis](../../../../../metastasis.md) after its deletion does not automatically identify the missing step. Orthogonal lineage markers, multi-marker [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) and [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) assays, morphology and live behaviour should be combined.

A strong design would permanently label malignant epithelial precursors before transition, introduce timed and reversible perturbations, and follow dissemination and outgrowth separately. Rescue with a perturbation-resistant construct helps establish specificity; matched primary [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) burden avoids confusing a smaller source [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) with reduced dissemination efficiency. Compare spontaneous [metastasis](../../../../../metastasis.md) with experimental vascular injection: injection bypasses local invasion and [intravasation](../../../../../intravasation.md), so it cannot test their requirement. Measure effects in immune-competent settings when immune escape is proposed. Count viable disseminated [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) before counting final [metastases](../../../../../metastasis.md) to separate arrival, survival and expansion.

These distinctions also complicate therapeutic inference. Inhibiting a transition might reduce escape from the primary [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) yet favour epithelial outgrowth of [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) already disseminated; enforcing a mesenchymal state might restrict growth but leave a persistent reservoir. Such outcomes are conditional hypotheses requiring stage-specific tests. **The main contribution is flexible deployment of partially separable programmes for dissemination and persistence, with reversibility allowing [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) to meet different demands during progression.** A complete EMT is neither a universal prerequisite for [metastasis](../../../../../metastasis.md) nor a sufficient description of all aggressive [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) behaviour.
