<h1 id="section-ii/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**Recurrent drivers reflect repeated selection of accessible changes in shared cellular control systems, filtered by [biological tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md) context and by how [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md) arise.** The same individual [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md), different alterations of one [gene](../../../../../gene.md), and changes in different components of one pathway are three distinct kinds of recurrence. They should be separated before proposing an explanation.

All proliferating human [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) must regulate growth, survival, resource use and inheritance of their [genomes](../../../../../genome.md). Disabling a restraint or making a growth-promoting signal constitutive can therefore confer an advantage in more than one [biological tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md). Activating [KRAS](../../../../../kras.md) or [BRAF](../../../../../braf.md) can increase [RAS-MAPK signalling](../../../../../ras-mapk-signalling.md); activating [PIK3CA](../../../../../pik3ca.md) can increase [PI3K-AKT signalling](../../../../../pi3k-akt-signalling.md); disruption of [TP53](../../../../../tp53.md) or the [RB pathway](../../../../../rb-pathway.md) can weaken responses that normally restrain proliferation. A relatively limited set of regulatory bottlenecks is reused across many differentiated [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) types. Pan-cancer analysis consequently finds repeated alteration of canonical growth and survival pathways, with frequencies and combinations differing across [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) lineages.[Cell 173, pp. 321–337.e10; pathway alterations across tumour types, co-occurrence and mutual exclusivity](../../../../../oncogenic-signaling-pathways-in-the-cancer-genome-atlas-by-francisco-sanchez-vega-and-others.md)

[positive selection](../../../../../positive-selection.md) amplifies advantageous clones after the initiating change occurs. A [driver mutation](../../../../../driver-mutation.md) raises reproductive success in a particular cellular environment; a [passenger mutation](../../../../../passenger-mutation.md) can accompany the expanding clone without causing that advantage. Independent [tumours](../../../../../tumour.md) can therefore converge on similar solutions even when their other [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md) differ. The relevant advantage can be escape from growth restraint, suppression of [apoptosis](../../../../../apoptosis.md), tolerance of stress or survival after therapy, rather than a simple increase in division rate. A driver at one stage need not be advantageous in every later environment.

Functional constraints help explain recurrence of exact positions. An [oncogene](../../../../../oncogene.md) often needs an altered [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) that retains a useful structure while escaping regulation. Certain [KRAS](../../../../../kras.md) substitutions impair normal switching toward the inactive state; [BRAF](../../../../../braf.md) V600E promotes abnormal kinase activity; particular [PIK3CA](../../../../../pik3ca.md) substitutions activate signalling. Many other changes in the same [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) would merely damage them and provide no comparable benefit. By contrast, a [tumour suppressor gene](../../../../../tumour-suppressor-gene.md) may be disabled by many different nonsense, frameshift, splice-site or deletion events. Consequently, clustered activating hotspots and dispersed inactivating changes can produce different [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) patterns even when both [genes](../../../../../gene.md) are recurrently selected. Some [TP53](../../../../../tp53.md) missense alleles also have dominant-negative or additional context-dependent effects, so not every tumour-suppressor lesion fits an identical two-hit mechanism.

Recurrence is nevertheless generated by [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) supply as well as [genotype fitness](../../../../../genotype-fitness.md). Local [nucleotide sequence](../../../../../nucleic-acid-sequence.md), methylated [CpG sites](../../../../../cpg-site.md), exposure-related damage, [DNA repair](../../../../../dna-repair.md) and replication-associated processes make some changes more likely than others. A hotspot can therefore recur partly because it is easy to generate. Large [genes](../../../../../gene.md), late-replicating regions and variable background [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) burdens can yield apparently recurrent passengers if the null model assumes uniform mutability. Analyses allowing for such heterogeneity substantially change which [genes](../../../../../gene.md) are identified as unusually mutated.[Nature 499, pp. 214–218; mutation-rate variation, replication timing, transcription and background-rate correction](../../../../../mutational-heterogeneity-in-cancer-and-the-search-for-new-cancer-associated-genes-by-michael-s-lawrence-and-others.md)

A conceptual decomposition is useful: the number of observed successful clones carrying an alteration depends on the number of opportunities for it to arise, its context-dependent chance of establishing an expanding lineage, and the chance of detecting that lineage. Thus counting cases with a [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) does not isolate its selective advantage. Gene-level recurrence tests should control [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) opportunity and sequence context; [dN/dS](../../../../../dn-ds.md) analyses compare observed nonsynonymous changes with a suitably calibrated expectation from synonymous changes. Applied to somatic evolution, such approaches provide evidence for selection beyond raw [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) counts.[Cell 171, pp. 1029–1041.e21; context-aware somatic dN/dS and driver-associated positive selection](../../../../../universal-patterns-of-selection-in-cancer-and-somatic-tissues-by-inigo-martincorena-and-others.md) The assumptions still require scrutiny: sequencing errors, biased ascertainment and an incorrect background model can mimic enrichment.

Tissue context explains why recurrence is broad but not universal. A particular [cell of origin](../../../../../cell-of-origin.md) expresses a particular set of receptors, lineage regulators and feedback circuits. The same [driver mutation](../../../../../driver-mutation.md) can cooperate with one background, be redundant in another, and cause intolerable stress in a third. [Epistasis](../../../../../epistasis.md) and [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) order alter its effect. Apparent mutual exclusivity can be consistent with alternative routes to the same pathway output, but it can also result from tumour-subtype structure or limited statistical power; it does not by itself prove that a double mutant is lethal. Similarly, co-occurrence can suggest cooperation without establishing a direct biochemical interaction.

The consequences of a shared lesion can also differ after treatment. An experimental screen in [BRAF](../../../../../braf.md) V600E colorectal-cancer [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) identified [epidermal growth factor receptor](../../../../../epidermal-growth-factor-receptor.md) feedback as an explanation for weak response to BRAF inhibition compared with the response seen in melanoma. Interfering with the feedback route improved pathway suppression in the tested models. This illustrates that the same driver is embedded in different circuits; recurrence across [biological tissues](../../../../../tissue-biology.md) does not imply identical drug sensitivity.[Nature 483, pp. 100–103; RNA-interference screen, EGFR feedback and combined pathway perturbation](../../../../../unresponsiveness-of-colon-cancer-to-braf-v600e-inhibition-through-feedback-activation-of-egfr-by-anirudh-prahallad-and-others.md) This is a mechanistic historical example, not a claim that the treatment landscape remains as it was in 2012.

Functional validation should recreate the candidate alteration at its endogenous locus in several relevant cellular backgrounds, compare it with matched wild-type and synonymous controls, and test growth under competitive conditions. Repairing the allele or restoring the affected restraint provides a complementary loss-of-advantage test. Use physiological expression: massive overexpression can make a normally weak effect appear transforming. Measure pathway output, survival and [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) formation separately, and test cooperation with plausible partner lesions. A dependency in an established [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) demonstrates a maintenance requirement, which need not be identical to the alteration's original role in initiation.

**The explanation is convergent somatic evolution acting on shared biological machinery, with [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) accessibility, functional constraints and lineage-specific interactions determining which solutions recur.** Genomic recurrence identifies candidates; calibrated statistics and controlled experiments establish which recurrent changes are causal and under what conditions.
