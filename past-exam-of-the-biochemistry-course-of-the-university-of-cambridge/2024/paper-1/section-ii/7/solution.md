**A [DNA library](../../../../../dna-library.md) synthesis method determines which variants can be reached, how often they are sampled, and which evolutionary paths remain available.** The nominal number of [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) molecules is less informative than the distribution of functional [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) sequences presented to the assay. [directed evolution](../../../../../directed-evolution.md) couples diversification to a [genotype-phenotype linkage](../../../../../genotype-phenotype-linkage.md), screening or selection, and recovery of successful genotypes. Construction controls the accessible moves through [protein sequence space](../../../../../protein-sequence-space.md) and therefore interacts with assay capacity, starting sequence and [epistasis](../../../../../epistasis.md); it is part of the evolutionary strategy rather than an interchangeable preliminary step.

[error-prone PCR](../../../../../error-prone-pcr.md) explores substitutions throughout an existing [gene](../../../../../gene.md) without requiring prior structural knowledge. [R. Craig Cadwell](../../../../../r-craig-cadwell.md) and [Gerald F. Joyce](../../../../../gerald-f-joyce.md) demonstrated controlled PCR [mutagenesis](../../../../../mutagenesis.md) using a ribozyme sequence, establishing a general route for generating variant templates.[Altered PCR fidelity and distribution of mutations](../../../../../randomization-of-genes-by-pcr-mutagenesis-by-r-craig-cadwell-and-gerald-f-joyce.md) For [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) evolution, polymerase preferences, sequence context and the [genetic code](../../../../../genetic-code.md) make [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) changes uneven: a substitution requiring two nucleotide changes is less accessible than a single-base neighbour. Increasing the [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) rate reaches more distant variants but often increases inactive products and makes attribution difficult. Insertions and deletions can also change the [reading frame](../../../../../reading-frame.md). Existing useful sequence combinations are usually retained at a low [mutation load](../../../../../mutation-load.md), but extensive remodelling is hard to reach by successive selections if intermediate genotypes are deleterious.

An idealized [Poisson distribution](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) with mean [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) count $\lambda$ makes this trade-off quantitative:

$$
P(m)=e^{-\lambda}\frac{\lambda^m}{m!}.
$$

At $\lambda=1$, about 37% of molecules have no change and 37% have one; at $\lambda=3$, only about 5% are unchanged and about 80% contain two or more changes. This is a planning model, not a guarantee that a real PCR [DNA library](../../../../../dna-library.md) is unbiased or independent at every site. Early PCR events can generate many related descendants, and the number of nucleotide [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md) is not the number of distinct [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) substitutions.

Oligonucleotide-directed [saturation mutagenesis](../../../../../saturation-mutagenesis.md) concentrates the assay budget at specified sites, for example an [active site](../../../../../active-site.md) pocket or an interface. [degenerate codons](../../../../../degenerate-codon.md) are convenient but biased. [NNK](../../../../../nnk-codon.md) and [NNS](../../../../../nns-codon.md) each encode 32 [codons](../../../../../codon.md), including one [stop codon](../../../../../stop-codon.md), with unequal multiplicities for different [amino acids](../../../../../amino-acid.md). At $k$ independently synthesized sites the ideal fraction lacking these introduced stops is $(31/32)^k$, even before assembly errors or deleterious substitutions. A [DNA library](../../../../../dna-library.md) of $20^k$ [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) combinations can therefore contain many redundant [codons](../../../../../codon.md) and uneven representation. Trinucleotide mixtures assemble complete chosen [codons](../../../../../codon.md) and can exclude stops, equalize [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) sampling, or deliberately favour a chemically relevant subset. [Bernhard Virnekas](../../../../../bernhard-virnekas.md) and colleagues developed such [trinucleotide phosphoramidites](../../../../../trinucleotide-phosphoramidite.md) for mixed-oligonucleotide synthesis.[Whole-codon synthesis for controlled amino-acid mixtures](../../../../../trinucleotide-phosphoramidites-ideal-reagents-for-the-synthesis-of-mixed-oligonucleotides-for-random-mutagenesis-by-bernhard-virnekas-and-others.md) Controlled diversity is valuable, but restricting sites according to an incomplete structural model can miss beneficial remote [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md).

[DNA shuffling](../../../../../dna-shuffling.md) offers a different move: [genetic recombination](../../../../../genetic-recombination.md) among already useful or homologous sequences. Random fragmentation followed by annealing and polymerase extension reconstructs [genes](../../../../../gene.md) from overlapping parental fragments. [Willem P. C. Stemmer](../../../../../willem-p-c-stemmer.md) demonstrated reassembly and [genetic recombination](../../../../../genetic-recombination.md), including chimeric sequences, providing a practical route to combine sequence blocks rather than relying only on isolated substitutions.[Fragmentation, gene reassembly and recombinant products](../../../../../dna-shuffling-by-random-fragmentation-and-reassembly-in-vitro-recombination-for-molecular-evolution-by-willem-p-c-stemmer.md) This can unite improvements acquired in separate lineages and remove unwanted changes by [genetic recombination](../../../../../genetic-recombination.md) with a parent. However, crossovers favour compatible homologous regions, closely related parents may add little diversity, and breaking a coadapted combination can lower fitness. Positive or negative [epistasis](../../../../../epistasis.md) means the fitness of a recombinant cannot be predicted by adding the effects of its parental substitutions.

Designed synthetic [genes](../../../../../gene.md) and pooled oligonucleotide assembly can specify more extensive combinations, deletions, insertions or carefully chosen paths between distant sequences. This can test combinations inaccessible to single-base steps and explicitly balance beneficial candidates against exploratory variants. The cost is that synthesis, assembly and amplification introduce errors and abundance differences; a designed sequence is not necessarily a correctly constructed or well represented molecule. Long constructs may need assembly from overlapping fragments, and different fragment yields can skew the [DNA library](../../../../../dna-library.md). A finite screen also prevents exhaustive exploration simply by purchasing a larger pool.

Sampling makes these differences concrete. If a target variant has [DNA library](../../../../../dna-library.md) frequency $p$, screening $n$ independent clones finds it with probability

$$
P(\mathrm{hit})=1-(1-p)^n.
$$

For $M$ equally frequent variants, the expected number of distinct variants observed is $M[1-(1-1/M)^n]$. Screening 1000 clones from a uniform two-site [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) [DNA library](../../../../../dna-library.md) with $M=400$ visits about 92% of the variants on average; from a three-site [DNA library](../../../../../dna-library.md) with $M=8000$, it visits only about 12%. These are expected fractions, not probabilities that every variant has been sampled. Roughly $3/p$ clones give a 95% chance of observing one specified rare variant when $p$ is small. Synthesis bias and clonal duplication can make the effective coverage substantially worse.

The course of evolution should therefore be planned alongside screening. Begin with a [mutation load](../../../../../mutation-load.md) or site set that the assay can actually cover, sequence the unselected [DNA library](../../../../../dna-library.md) to measure [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) spectra, stop frequency, parental carry-over and representation, and compare those frequencies with selected output. Use matched expression conditions and retain [genotype-phenotype linkage](../../../../../genotype-phenotype-linkage.md): selection for growth, expression or display can otherwise enrich a variant that performs poorly as a purified [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md). Reconstruct hits independently, sequence the complete [gene](../../../../../gene.md), measure soluble [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) and validate the desired activity or specificity at controlled [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) concentration. Include parental and known inactive controls and, where relevant, more than one [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) or environmental condition. Subsequent rounds can recombine verified hits, adjust [mutation load](../../../../../mutation-load.md) or target sites suggested by the first round. **The best synthesis method maximizes useful, testable diversity under the assay budget and enables appropriate paths through the [fitness landscape](../../../../../fitness-landscape.md); maximal random diversity alone is not the objective.**
