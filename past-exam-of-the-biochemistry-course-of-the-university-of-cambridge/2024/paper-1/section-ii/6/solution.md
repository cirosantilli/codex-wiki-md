↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**Biophysical measurements show that [enzymes](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) occupy [enzyme conformational ensembles](../../../../../enzyme-conformational-ensemble.md); establishing which motions control function requires linking those ensembles to particular kinetic steps.** Motions range from picosecond side-chain fluctuations to microsecond–millisecond loop rearrangements and slower domain transitions. They can govern [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) access, productive alignment, chemistry or product release. These roles must be distinguished: the steady-state turnover rate $k_{\mathrm{cat}}$ describes the entire catalytic cycle and need not equal the rate of bond making or breaking.

[crystallography](../../../../../crystallography.md) identifies the structural framework and can reveal alternative loop positions or ligand-dependent states. Room-temperature measurements and time-resolved experiments can capture changes hidden by a single cryogenic model. Nevertheless, a displacement parameter combines motion, static disorder and modelling uncertainty, and a series of structures is not itself a trajectory or a rate measurement. Spectroscopic measurements in solution are particularly useful because they measure fluctuations and exchange while the [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) remains in a defined chemical state.

[nuclear magnetic resonance](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) resolves dynamics at individual sites. [longitudinal nuclear spin relaxation](../../../../../longitudinal-nuclear-spin-relaxation.md) and [transverse relaxation](../../../../../transverse-relaxation.md) and heteronuclear [nuclear Overhauser effects](../../../../../nuclear-overhauser-effect.md) constrain fast fluctuations; a model-based [protein NMR order parameter](../../../../../protein-nmr-order-parameter.md) $S^2$ describes the amplitude of bond-vector motion after accounting for overall tumbling. [NMR relaxation dispersion](../../../../../nmr-relaxation-dispersion.md) measures how exchange contributes to relaxation as refocusing conditions change. A two-state analysis can constrain an exchange rate $k_{\mathrm{ex}}=k_{AB}+k_{BA}$, state populations and a [chemical shift](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) difference. Measurements at multiple fields and temperatures help separate these parameters, but alternative models and insensitive time windows remain limitations. The inferred minor-state [chemical shifts](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) can be compared with separately characterized ligand-bound structures; one should not equate a dispersion curve with a fully resolved molecular movie.

In [dihydrofolate reductase](../../../../../dihydrofolate-reductase.md), relaxation measurements detected sparsely populated states related to other intermediates of the catalytic cycle, supporting a pre-existing landscape from which [ligand binding](../../../../../ligand-binding.md) can select conformations.[Relaxation dispersion across catalytic intermediates](../../../../../the-dynamic-energy-landscape-of-dihydrofolate-reductase-catalysis-by-david-d-boehr-and-others.md) This connects [conformational selection](../../../../../conformational-selection.md) with enzymatic progression without excluding subsequent [induced fit](../../../../../induced-fit.md). In [cyclophilin A](../../../../../cyclophilin-a.md), comparison of the free [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) and catalytic conditions showed an intrinsic network of conformational exchange whose rates were associated with turnover.[NMR comparison of free enzyme and catalytic exchange](../../../../../intrinsic-dynamics-of-an-enzyme-underlies-catalysis-by-elan-z-eisenmesser-and-others.md) Matching rates is informative but not sufficient to prove that a particular motion limits chemistry: the measured exchange may be a coupled consequence, or another step can occur on a similar timescale.

Kinetic methods provide the essential bridge. [stopped-flow](../../../../../stopped-flow.md) mixing follows a rapid [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) or absorbance response after [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) addition; [rapid quench-flow](../../../../../rapid-quench-flow.md) terminates reactions at selected delays and measures chemical products. Binding, conformational change and chemical conversion can thus be fitted to competing kinetic schemes rather than all assigned to a single observed exponential. A [pre-steady-state burst](../../../../../pre-steady-state-burst.md) can reveal fast chemistry followed by slower release, although its amplitude also depends on the active [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) fraction and mechanism. [temperature jumps](../../../../../temperature-jump.md) perturb an existing equilibrium on shorter timescales and expose relaxation back toward it. [isotope](../../../../../isotope.md) substitution can help identify a chemical step, but changes in observed [isotope effects](../../../../../isotope-effect.md) must be interpreted within the complete kinetic scheme.

Single-molecule [Förster resonance energy transfer](../../../../../forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md) adds information lost in population averages. A donor–acceptor pair reports a distance-sensitive efficiency,

$$
E=\frac{1}{1+(r/R_0)^6},
$$

where the characteristic distance $R_0$ depends on the dyes and their photophysical environment. Trajectories can reveal state dwell times, heterogeneous pathways and ordering of domain transitions relative to a turnover reporter. Labels must preserve activity; donor-only and acceptor controls, corrections for detection efficiencies, and tests for blinking or bleaching are necessary. A [FRET](../../../../../forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md) change can arise from dye orientation or local quenching, so corroborating distances or structural data are required before assigning a specific domain movement. A mean efficiency also cannot simply be converted into the mean molecular distance because the relation is nonlinear.

[hydrogen-deuterium exchange mass spectrometry](../../../../../hydrogen-deuterium-exchange-mass-spectrometry.md) maps changes in protection and solvent accessibility across an [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md), usefully locating regions altered by [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) binding or [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md). Exchange also depends on [hydrogen bonding](../../../../../hydrogen-bonding.md), local opening equilibria and chemical conditions, so it is not a direct universal clock for internal motion. [molecular dynamics](../../../../../molecular-dynamics.md) can connect observed sites to candidate atomic pathways and predict further tests. Its force field, sampling and treatment of chemistry limit inference; a conventional classical trajectory does not demonstrate bond-breaking kinetics by itself.

Causal perturbation is stronger than correlation when coupled to these measurements. In the DHFR “dynamic knockout” study, the N23PP/S148A variant retained a closely similar [active site](../../../../../active-site.md) ground-state structure but lost much [active site](../../../../../active-site.md) millisecond exchange and showed slower hydride transfer. The S148A comparison was especially informative: it also altered the large closed-to-occluded transition yet retained more exchange and much faster chemistry.[Figures 3–4 and mutant comparison of hydride-transfer rates](../../../../../a-dynamic-knockout-reveals-that-conformational-fluctuations-influence-the-chemical-step-of-enzyme-catalysis-by-gira-bhabha-and-others.md) This argues against identifying the gross loop transition itself as the obligatory chemical event. Controls must still examine stability, binding, [active site](../../../../../active-site.md) geometry and altered electrostatic or solvation ensembles: a [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) is not an isolated dial for “motion”.

A coherent mechanistic account therefore combines structural states, populations, exchange rates, independently resolved [chemical kinetics](../../../../../chemical-kinetics-split.md) and perturbations that make successful predictions. In an activated-rate description $k\sim(k_BT/h)\exp(-\Delta G^\ddagger/RT)$, changing access to productive arrangements can alter the relevant free-energy barrier or pre-equilibrium population. Motions do not provide a new energy source outside thermal fluctuations and chemical [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). **Internal motions can gate access, organize reactive configurations and permit release; which role matters is an experimental question for each [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) and each step.**
