**[NMR](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) of [membrane proteins](../../../../../membrane-protein.md) became practical through coordinated improvements in sample preparation, [isotope labelling](../../../../../isotope-labelling.md), relaxation control, instrumentation and solid-state methods.** The obstacle is not simply that [cell membranes](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) are insoluble. Hydrophobic surfaces must remain shielded, while the [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) must retain function and form a sufficiently homogeneous sample. In solution, the [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) plus its detergent or lipid assembly tumbles slowly; rapid [transverse relaxation](../../../../../transverse-relaxation.md) then broadens resonances until assignments and structural restraints become difficult to obtain.

Recombinant expression and controlled [isotope labelling](../../../../../isotope-labelling.md) allow selective observation of the [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) against its environment. Uniform [nitrogen-15](../../../../../nitrogen-15.md) and [carbon-13](../../../../../carbon-13.md) labelling supports multidimensional assignments. [deuteration](../../../../../deuteration.md) reduces much proton–proton dipolar relaxation; reintroducing selected protons or labelled methyl groups retains sensitive reporters while limiting the relaxation network. [selective methyl labelling](../../../../../selective-methyl-labelling.md) is particularly useful for large complexes, but the sparse reporters do not automatically supply enough restraints for a unique complete structure. Exchangeable amide protons may also need back-exchange after growth in deuterated medium, and buried sites can exchange incompletely. Sample production, rather than the spectrometer alone, is therefore a central technical development.

The [membrane mimic](../../../../../membrane-mimic.md) must balance biological fidelity and spectroscopic tractability. Detergent [micelles](../../../../../micelle.md) shield hydrophobic surfaces and can produce relatively small assemblies; inappropriate detergents may alter packing, activity or oligomeric state. [bicelles](../../../../../bicelle.md) combine bilayer-forming lipids with components that stabilize their edges, and [lipid bilayer nanodiscs](../../../../../lipid-bilayer-nanodisc.md) use a scaffold to enclose a bilayer patch. Smaller, well-defined [nanodiscs](../../../../../lipid-bilayer-nanodisc.md) reduce the additional rotational burden. [Franz Hagn](../../../../../franz-hagn.md) and colleagues engineered optimized small [nanodiscs](../../../../../lipid-bilayer-nanodisc.md) and obtained high-resolution [NMR](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) information on the [membrane protein](../../../../../membrane-protein.md) [OmpX](../../../../../ompx.md), connecting scaffold design with a more tractable lipid-embedded sample.[Small scaffold design and OmpX structure determination](../../../../../optimized-phospholipid-bilayer-nanodiscs-facilitate-high-resolution-structure-determination-of-membrane-proteins-by-franz-hagn-and-others.md) No mimic is automatically native: compare [ligand binding](../../../../../ligand-binding.md), transport or catalytic activity with an appropriate [cell membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) preparation, and check concentration-dependent aggregation and oligomerization.

[transverse relaxation-optimized spectroscopy](../../../../../transverse-relaxation-optimized-spectroscopy.md) selects a slowly relaxing component of a coupled spin system. For suitable nuclei and fields, dipolar and [chemical shift anisotropy](../../../../../chemical-shift-anisotropy.md) contributions partly cancel for one component while reinforcing others. Combined with [deuteration](../../../../../deuteration.md), this preserves narrower lines in systems that would otherwise be inaccessible. The original [TROSY](../../../../../transverse-relaxation-optimized-spectroscopy.md) experiments established the relaxation-interference principle rather than merely increasing signal by averaging longer.[Theory and experimental demonstration of relaxation interference](../../../../../attenuated-t2-relaxation-by-mutual-cancellation-of-dipole-dipole-coupling-and-chemical-shift-anisotropy-indicates-an-avenue-to-nmr-structures-of-very-large-biological-macromolecules-in-solution-by-konstantin-pervushin-and-others.md) Methyl-based variants exploit favourable methyl relaxation properties and selective labelling. Increasing magnetic field improves sensitivity and frequency dispersion, but also changes relaxation contributions; it is not correct to assume that every resonance becomes narrower at every higher field.

High-field magnets, stable radiofrequency electronics and cryogenic detection probes increase accessible sensitivity and resolution. Three-dimensional and higher-dimensional experiments spread overlapping signals and connect resonances through known covalent pathways. Once assignments are secure, [nuclear Overhauser effects](../../../../../nuclear-overhauser-effect.md) provide distance information, while [residual dipolar couplings](../../../../../residual-dipolar-coupling.md) and [paramagnetic relaxation enhancement](../../../../../paramagnetic-relaxation-enhancement.md) can add orientational and longer-range constraints. [chemical shift](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) perturbations report changes in local environment on [ligand binding](../../../../../ligand-binding.md), but do not by themselves determine a contact geometry. Each additional measurement restricts the structural model in a different way, which is particularly valuable when only a sparse set of membrane-protein resonances remains visible.

[solid-state nuclear magnetic resonance](../../../../../solid-state-nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) provides a complementary route that does not require rapid overall tumbling. [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) can be studied in bilayers or other immobilized assemblies, using pulse sequences and sample orientation or [magic angle spinning](../../../../../magic-angle-spinning.md) to control anisotropic interactions. The magic angle satisfies $3\cos^2\theta-1=0$, giving $\theta\simeq54.74^\circ$. Spinning averages appropriate orientation-dependent terms, while decoupling, [isotope](../../../../../isotope.md) dilution or [deuteration](../../../../../deuteration.md) and fast spinning reduce broadening from dense spin networks. Proton detection at very high spinning rates improves sensitivity; [Loren B. Andreas](../../../../../loren-b-andreas.md) and colleagues demonstrated structures of fully protonated [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) using fast-spinning proton-detected experiments, an important general methodological advance rather than a demonstration that every [cell membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) sample is thereby solved.[Proton detection with approximately 100 kHz magic-angle spinning](../../../../../structure-of-fully-protonated-proteins-by-proton-detected-magic-angle-spinning-nmr-by-loren-b-andreas-and-others.md) [dynamic nuclear polarization](../../../../../dynamic-nuclear-polarization.md) can additionally enhance signal through electron-to-nuclear polarization transfer, although radicals and cryogenic conditions can affect line widths and the conformational states observed.

The outcome is both structural and functional information. Spectra can distinguish conformations, map [ligand](../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) responses and measure exchange even when no single static structure describes the system. Empty [micelles](../../../../../micelle.md) or scaffold-only [nanodiscs](../../../../../lipid-bilayer-nanodisc.md) help identify environmental signals; unlabelled samples, independent assignments and distance-restraint validation guard against incorrect peaks and overfitting. Compare preparations and activity before and after long acquisitions, because aggregation or degradation can selectively remove signals. A missing peak may reflect exchange broadening or overlap, not an absent residue. **The enabling combination is a functional and homogeneous [cell membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) environment with an [isotope](../../../../../isotope.md) and pulse-sequence strategy matched to the size and dynamics of the assembly; solution and solid-state [NMR](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) cover complementary regimes.**
