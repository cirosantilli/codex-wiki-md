<h1 id="section-ii/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**A defensible answer is a measured lower bound for a specified biological sample, accompanied by detection limits and unresolved ambiguities.** A [proteoform](../../../../../proteoform.md) is a molecular form of a [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) defined by its [amino acid](../../../../../amino-acid.md) sequence and covalent modifications, including processing. It is not simply a [gene](../../../../../gene.md), a [peptide](../../../../../peptide.md), a charge state in a mass spectrum or a conformational state. The number in one [cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) type at one time differs from the union across [cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) types, developmental stages, diseases and human genotypes. Before counting, specify whether rare molecular damage, incompletely processed intermediates and very low abundance species are included, and whether the question concerns individual [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) or a pooled population.

A theoretical inventory starts with the [genome](../../../../../genome.md), phased sequence variants and transcript structures. [whole-genome sequencing](../../../../../whole-genome-sequencing.md) identifies coding variation; [RNA sequencing](../../../../../rna-sequencing.md), especially full-length [long-read sequencing](../../../../../long-read-sequencing.md), can connect alternative exons within individual transcripts. [ribosome profiling](../../../../../ribosome-profiling.md) supplies evidence that candidate [reading frames](../../../../../reading-frame.md) are translated. However, an [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) isoform is not proof of a stable [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md), and none of these methods alone establishes the combination of [post-translational modifications](../../../../../post-translational-modification.md) on the mature chain. A schematic upper bound is

$$
N_{\mathrm{possible}}=\sum_i\prod_{j=1}^{m_i}(1+r_{ij}),
$$

where $i$ indexes distinct sequence forms and site $j$ permits an unmodified state plus $r_{ij}$ mutually exclusive modified states. This assumes independence between sites and that every combination is realizable. Steric constraints, processing, mutually exclusive pathways and low occupancy invalidate that assumption in real [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Multiplying all documented sites therefore estimates a possible space, not the number that exists.

[bottom-up proteomics](../../../../../bottom-up-proteomics.md) provides a broad experimental survey. Extract [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) under conditions that minimize [proteolysis](../../../../../proteolysis.md) and preserve labile modifications; fractionate, digest with complementary [proteases](../../../../../protease.md), and analyse [peptides](../../../../../peptide.md) by [liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry](../../../../../liquid-chromatography-mass-spectrometry.md) and [tandem mass spectrometry](../../../../../tandem-mass-spectrometry.md). Search against a sample-specific sequence database and relevant modification hypotheses, then validate sites with diagnostic fragments and, where needed, synthetic standards. Modification-specific enrichment increases sensitivity but changes the sampled population and cannot supply an unbiased count by itself. Control peptide- and protein-level [false discovery rates](../../../../../false-discovery-rate.md), distinguish unique from shared [peptides](../../../../../peptide.md), and report ambiguously localized modifications explicitly. Enlarging a search to every possible modification greatly increases the chance of a convincing but incorrect match.

The central limitation is loss of linkage. Suppose sites A and B occur on different digestion products. A mixture containing equal amounts of unmodified and doubly modified chains gives 50% modification at each site. A different mixture containing equal amounts of A-only and B-only chains gives exactly the same site occupancies, but contains different intact forms. No amount of more precise measurement of these two isolated [peptides](../../../../../peptide.md) distinguishes the populations. A spanning [peptide](../../../../../peptide.md), a larger linked fragment or intact-chain evidence is required.

**[Figure 1](#section-ii/3/image-different-intact-proteoform-populations-can-give-identical-modification-frequencies-after-digestion). Different intact proteoform populations can give identical modification frequencies after digestion**.

[top-down proteomics](../../../../../top-down-proteomics.md) addresses this by separating and measuring intact [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) before fragmenting their selected ions. High-resolution intact mass constrains the total composition, while sequence fragments connect modifications to the same molecule and localize them. Electron-based fragmentation can help retain modifications lost during energetic fragmentation. [middle-down proteomics](../../../../../middle-down-proteomics.md) uses larger fragments to recover partial linkage when complete intact-chain characterization is difficult. [John C. Tran](../../../../../john-c-tran.md) and colleagues demonstrated discovery-scale intact-protein analysis, illustrating that sequence variants and modification patterns can be mapped together rather than reconstructed solely from disconnected [peptides](../../../../../peptide.md).[Intact-protein fractionation and identification of linked isoforms](../../../../../mapping-intact-protein-isoforms-in-discovery-mode-using-top-down-proteomics-by-john-c-tran-and-others.md) Intact mass alone remains insufficient: positional [isomers](../../../../../isomer.md) with the same mass require informative fragmentation or additional separation, and one envelope can contain unresolved forms.

A practical programme would combine these measurements across well-defined human [cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) populations, using biological replicates, standardized handling and a documented range of physiological perturbations. It would use sequence-resolved [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) data to constrain searches, broad bottom-up data to locate promising variation, and targeted top-down or middle-down analysis to establish co-occurrence. Spike-in [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) spanning abundances and sizes would measure extraction loss and detection sensitivity. Time courses, immediate [protein denaturation](../../../../../denaturation-biochemistry.md), [protease](../../../../../protease.md) and [phosphatase](../../../../../phosphatase.md) inhibition, and processing blanks would help distinguish cellular modification from [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md), [protein deamidation](../../../../../protein-deamidation.md) or cleavage during preparation. Very large, hydrophobic and scarce [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) require specific extraction and separation strategies; their absence from a spectrum cannot be treated as biological absence.

Bulk and single-cell measurements answer different questions. A bulk sample may show two [proteoforms](../../../../../proteoform.md) expressed in different [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md); it does not show that both occur in each [cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Conversely, detecting two modifications in one [cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) does not demonstrate that they occupy the same [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) molecule. Single-cell sensitivity and molecular linkage must therefore be evaluated separately. Quantitative standards and targeted follow-up can establish whether a new low-abundance assignment is reproducible rather than a one-off incorrect match.

Finally, merge identifications by molecular identity rather than by [peptide](../../../../../peptide.md) count, search-engine entry or ion charge. Record fully characterized forms separately from groups sharing an unresolved mass or site pattern, and control identification error at the [proteoform](../../../../../proteoform.md) level. Plot discovery against sampling depth, tissues and perturbations; any extrapolation to unseen forms requires explicit assumptions about abundance and sampling heterogeneity. **Sequence prediction supplies candidates, [peptide](../../../../../peptide.md) analysis supplies coverage, and intact or linked-fragment evidence establishes combinations. A universal exact count cannot be inferred by multiplying modification sites or summing database entries.**
