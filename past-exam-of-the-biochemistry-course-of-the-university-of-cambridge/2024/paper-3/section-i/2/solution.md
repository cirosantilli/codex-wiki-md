<h1 id="section-i/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**[cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) largely reuses normal cellular machinery, but its organization and evolutionary behaviour are not adequately described as one homeostatic control simply being turned up or down.** Growth, survival, metabolism, plasticity and tissue remodelling all have normal functions. Malignancy arises when their coordination with the needs of the organism is lost, and selection can then generate new combinations, altered molecular activities and pathological ecological interactions.

Normal proliferation depends on extracellular signals and internal cell-cycle controls. Constitutive receptor or [Ras](../../../../../ras.md) signalling, increased cyclin activity or weakened [RB](../../../../../rb.md)–[E2F](../../../../../e2f.md) restraint can make a [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) divide under conditions in which its neighbours would arrest. These changes exploit existing [DNA replication](../../../../../dna-replication.md) and mitotic machinery rather than inventing a new way to duplicate a [genome](../../../../../genome.md). Equally, normal [apoptosis](../../../../../apoptosis.md) removes damaged or inappropriate [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md); a [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) can survive by suppressing this response. Loss of [p53](../../../../../p53.md) can compromise a network linking stress to arrest, repair, death and other restraints. Mice lacking [p53](../../../../../p53.md) develop spontaneous [tumours](../../../../../tumour.md) despite largely normal development, directly demonstrating the importance of an existing protective programme.[Tumour susceptibility after p53 loss](../../../../../mice-deficient-for-p53-are-developmentally-normal-but-susceptible-to-spontaneous-tumours-by-l-a-donehower-and-others.md) This does not mean that every [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) must eliminate every [p53](../../../../../p53.md) output or that all [p53](../../../../../p53.md) [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md) have the same effect.

Many other properties have similarly recognizable antecedents. [glycolysis](../../../../../glycolysis.md), nutrient uptake and [autophagy](../../../../../autophagy.md) support normal growth and stress adaptation; their deployment can help [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) tolerate fluctuating resources. Angiogenic signals normally support development and repair, but sustained production can recruit vessels into a growing [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md). Motility, matrix remodelling and reversible changes in adhesion participate in development and wound repair. [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) can recruit them for invasion, with surrounding [fibroblasts](../../../../../fibroblast.md) and immune [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) contributing signals and physical routes. Collective breast-cancer invasion, for example, can depend on a conserved basal epithelial programme rather than a completely new locomotor apparatus.[Basal-marker leader cells and functional invasion experiments](../../../../../collective-invasion-in-breast-cancer-requires-a-conserved-basal-epithelial-program-by-kevin-j-cheung-and-others.md) Immune suppression also uses normal regulatory pathways, although suppressing antitumour immunity conflicts with organismal protection.

The homeostatic interpretation is most useful when it emphasizes constraints and feedback. Normal tissue renewal couples production to [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) loss and spatial organization. A [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) increasing division, reducing death or changing competitive interactions can unbalance this system even without maximal activation of every pathway. Selection acts on net clonal success, not only on instantaneous proliferation. A slowly cycling population that survives therapy or colonizes a new site may be favoured under particular conditions. Conversely, an excessively strong oncogenic signal can trigger [senescence](../../../../../senescence.md) or [apoptosis](../../../../../apoptosis.md), so successful [cancers](../../../../../cancer.md) often combine activating changes with escape from compensating restraints. Different combinations can converge on similar phenotypes.

However, “nothing new” becomes misleading at the molecular level. Some alterations generate a qualitatively changed activity. Cancer-associated [IDH1](../../../../../idh1.md) mutants acquire the ability to reduce [alpha-ketoglutarate](../../../../../alpha-ketoglutarate.md) to R-2-hydroxyglutarate, rather than simply increasing the [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md)'s ordinary reaction. [metabolite](../../../../../metabolite.md) profiling and biochemical assays established this altered catalytic activity.[Tumour-associated metabolite and mutant-enzyme activity](../../../../../cancer-associated-idh1-mutations-produce-2-hydroxyglutarate-by-lenny-dang-and-others.md) [gene fusions](../../../../../gene-fusion.md) can join recognition and signalling domains in new regulatory arrangements, while new enhancer contacts or altered splicing can change which molecular combinations are expressed. These innovations still obey familiar chemistry and use inherited components, but that is true of biological innovation generally; it does not make them equivalent to a reversible normal homeostatic response.

[cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) is also an evolving population within a changing environment. Genetic and epigenetic variation permit selection for survival during [hypoxia](../../../../../hypoxia.md), immune attack or treatment. The selected phenotype can depend on the surrounding stroma and on interactions among [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) subclones. Normal wound-healing responses may stop when repair is complete, whereas a [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) can maintain the signals or continually regenerate the damage that recruits them. Altered metabolism, inflammation and genomic instability can create feedback that changes the environment itself. This is not a single [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) merely ignoring one stop signal, and one successful clone need not benefit either neighbouring [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) or the host.

Experimentally, the distinction matters. If an existing normal programme is misregulated, correcting the upstream input or restoring feedback may normalize behaviour, but normal tissues using the same programme may also be affected. If an altered [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) has a tumour-specific pocket or catalytic dependence, that difference may provide selectivity. Genotype-matched perturbations, measurements of the normal counterpart and tests in tissue context discriminate these cases. A list of cancer-associated phenotypes alone does not determine whether the causal change is quantitative, qualitative or ecological. **[cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) usually repurposes normal processes, but malignancy emerges from altered control, molecular changes and selection among [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md); “simply deregulation” understates that causal complexity.**
