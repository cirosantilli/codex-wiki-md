**I would establish whether the alteration drives [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md), whether the established [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) depends on it, and whether that dependence offers a selective intervention point.** These are different questions. A recurrent alteration can be a passenger; a genuine initiating driver can become dispensable after progression; and an essential [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) can be equally essential in normal tissue.

First validate the genomic observation with an independent assay and matched normal [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Distinguish a somatic [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) from inherited variation, sequencing error and contamination. For [gene amplification](../../../../../gene-amplification.md), resolve the altered interval, copy number and [tumour purity](../../../../../tumour-purity.md), and determine whether the candidate is the focus of a recurrent, narrowly amplified segment or simply accompanies a neighbouring driver in a broad gain. Measure [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) and relevant activity: increased [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) dosage need not increase active [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). For [mutations](../../../../../mutation.md), inspect the spectrum and allelic configuration. Recurrent clustered missense changes can suggest altered activity, whereas dispersed truncations and loss of the remaining allele can suggest tumour-suppressor inactivation. Neither pattern is conclusive without accounting for sequence context, background [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) rate and selection. Large [genes](../../../../../gene.md), highly mutable sites and poorly repaired regions can recur without being selected; analyses of mutational heterogeneity and somatic selection show why a simple recurrence ranking is insufficient.[Background heterogeneity and false candidate-driver discovery](../../../../../mutational-heterogeneity-in-cancer-and-the-search-for-new-cancer-associated-genes-by-michael-s-lawrence-and-others.md)[Selection estimates accounting for mutation opportunity](../../../../../universal-patterns-of-selection-in-cancer-and-somatic-tissues-by-inigo-martincorena-and-others.md)

Relate the alteration to [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) type, stage, co-occurring lesions and clonal architecture. Single-cell or spatial assays can establish whether it is in the malignant compartment and whether every [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) carries it. Correct variant fractions for purity and copy number before inferring clonality. A clonal lesion may be early, but clonality alone does not prove causality; a passenger can hitchhike in an expanding clone. Association with outcome should be assessed within appropriate clinical and molecular strata rather than interpreted from a mixture of unrelated [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) types.

For functional necessity, use several independently altered [cell lines](../../../../../cell-line.md) and patient-derived [organoids](../../../../../organoid.md), together with lineage-matched unaltered [cancers](../../../../../cancer.md) and nonmalignant controls. Reduce the candidate with orthogonal perturbations and rescue with a perturbation-resistant allele at a near-physiological level. Measure proliferation, cell-cycle distribution, [apoptosis](../../../../../apoptosis.md), differentiation and the proposed pathway output separately. In an amplified region, a particularly important control is to avoid equating CRISPR cutting toxicity with [gene](../../../../../gene.md) dependence: many [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) breaks at multiple copies can suppress growth independently of the encoded [gene](../../../../../gene.md). Aguirre and colleagues demonstrated this copy-number-dependent response.[Amplified targets and gene-independent antiproliferative effects](../../../../../genomic-copy-number-dictates-a-gene-independent-cell-response-to-crispr-cas9-targeting-by-andrew-j-aguirre-and-others.md) [CRISPR interference](../../../../../crispr-interference.md) or [RNA interference](../../../../../rna-interference.md), combined with rescue and appropriately matched cutting controls, helps distinguish [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) dependence from this artefact. Guide-specific off-target effects and incomplete depletion also require direct checks.

Test sufficiency and allele specificity by introducing the observed allele into a relevant nonmalignant background, preferably through endogenous editing, or by increasing expression to the measured [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) level. Compare wild type, each recurrent mutant and appropriate inactive variants at matched abundance. For a suspected [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) suppressor, recreate the observed heterozygous or biallelic loss rather than assuming every [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) is equivalent to a knockout. A kinase-domain mutant might activate signalling, a truncation might remove negative regulation, and another allele might abolish function. Physiological assays of acinar organization, growth-factor dependence or invasion can reveal transformation phenotypes missed by a two-dimensional growth assay. Failure to transform one [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) type alone does not exclude a driver requiring cooperating lesions or the correct developmental state.

Then connect genotype to mechanism. Identify affected enzymatic activity, binding partners, localization and downstream signalling, using time-resolved perturbation to separate primary changes from consequences of dying [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Restore a downstream effector to test whether it bypasses loss of the candidate, or inhibit that effector to test whether it is required for the mutant phenotype. Rescue with an inhibitor-resistant target allele is strong evidence for a drug's intended mechanism. A correlation between [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) and a distant transcriptional signature is weaker. Genetic and pharmacological results should agree at exposures that establish [target engagement](../../../../../target-engagement.md) rather than general cytotoxicity.

Finally test initiation and maintenance in suitable in vivo settings: tissue-appropriate engineered mice, orthotopic [organoid](../../../../../organoid.md) transplantation or [patient-derived xenografts](../../../../../patient-derived-xenograft.md), with the immune context chosen for the hypothesis. Inducing an allele before [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) development tests initiation; turning it off after established growth tests maintenance. Quantify [tumour latency](../../../../../tumour-latency.md), burden, invasion and metastatic outcome with random allocation, blinded assessment and independent [biological replicates](../../../../../biological-replicate.md). Measure effects on normal tissue to define a [therapeutic window](../../../../../therapeutic-window.md), and examine resistant outgrowth for bypass mechanisms. **The strongest conclusion combines statistically credible human selection, allele-specific causal perturbation, mechanistic rescue and disease-selective dependence across appropriate models.**
