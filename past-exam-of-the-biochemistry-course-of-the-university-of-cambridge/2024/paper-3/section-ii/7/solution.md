**Yes: a [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md) can suppress [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) formation or progression by restraining an oncogenic programme, but that role must be demonstrated for a defined [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md), tissue and genetic context.** Reduced expression in a [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) or growth inhibition after massive [microRNA mimic](../../../../../microrna-mimic.md) transfection is suggestive rather than sufficient evidence of an endogenous tumour-suppressor function.

[microRNAs](../../../../../microrna.md) are short regulatory RNAs whose guide strands associate with [Argonaute](../../../../../argonaute.md). Recognition through sequence complementarity, often involving a [microRNA seed region](../../../../../microrna-seed-region.md) and a target's [3′ untranslated region](../../../../../3%E2%80%B2-untranslated-region.md), can recruit translational repression and [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) decay machinery. Canonical precursor processing involves [Drosha](../../../../../drosha.md) and [Dicer](../../../../../dicer.md), although not every [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md) follows every canonical step. A [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md) can affect many transcripts, and a transcript can receive input from several [microRNAs](../../../../../microrna.md). Therefore modest coordinated repression of several growth or survival factors can have a substantial phenotype even without complete elimination of one target. Suppression is a property of the regulatory network, not of an [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) being universally “anticancer”.

The [let-7](../../../../../let-7.md) family supplies a mechanistic example. Experiments connecting let-7-responsive sequences in [Ras](../../../../../ras.md) transcripts with regulation of [Ras](../../../../../ras.md) expression linked a developmental regulatory [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) to a conserved growth pathway.[Target-site reporters, developmental genetics and human RAS regulation](../../../../../ras-is-regulated-by-the-let-7-microrna-family-by-steven-m-johnson-and-others.md) Reduced [let-7](../../../../../let-7.md) in lung [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) is compatible with loss of this restraint, but the inverse expression relationship alone would not show causation. Functional experiments restoring [let-7](../../../../../let-7.md) in relevant lung-cancer systems suppressed [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) development, providing a stronger test of tumour-suppressive capacity.[Functional let-7 manipulation in lung-tumour models](../../../../../suppression-of-non-small-cell-lung-tumor-development-by-the-let-7-microrna-family-by-manish-s-kumar-and-others.md) Expression should still be compared with a physiological range, and one must distinguish preventing [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) initiation from treating an established [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md).

Genetic loss gives a complementary direction of evidence. The [miR-15a](../../../../../mir-15a.md) and [miR-16-1](../../../../../mir-16-1.md) cluster lies within a recurrently deleted region associated with [chronic lymphocytic leukaemia](../../../../../chronic-lymphocytic-leukaemia.md). Deletion of the corresponding mouse regulatory region or the [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md) cluster increased B-cell proliferative abnormalities and produced clonal lymphoproliferative disease in a subset of animals.[Separate deletions of the broader region and the miRNA cluster; B-cell proliferation and disease](../../../../../the-dleu2-mir-15a-16-1-cluster-controls-b-cell-proliferation-and-its-deletion-leads-to-chronic-lymphocytic-leukemia-by-ulf-klein-and-others.md) Comparing the smaller cluster deletion with a larger deletion matters because neighbouring [genes](../../../../../gene.md) can contribute to a human copy-number association. Disease after specific [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md) loss supports endogenous restraint more directly than an expression signature does; incomplete penetrance and latency imply cooperation and context rather than an automatic one-step conversion of every [b cell](../../../../../b-cell.md).

The p53–miR-34 connection illustrates both strength and limitation of a proposed pathway. [p53](../../../../../p53.md) activation can induce miR-34-family [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md), and ectopic [miR-34 family](../../../../../mir-34-family.md) can restrain proliferation or promote death in suitable [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), connecting a transcriptional [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) suppressor with post-transcriptional repression.[p53-dependent miR-34 induction and growth-related responses](../../../../../a-microrna-component-of-the-p53-tumour-suppressor-network-by-lin-he-and-others.md) Yet mice lacking all three [miR-34 family](../../../../../mir-34-family.md) members retained substantial p53-dependent arrest and [apoptosis](../../../../../apoptosis.md) and did not show the predicted general increase in [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) susceptibility in the tested settings.[Triple-family deletion and in vivo tumour-suppression tests](../../../../../intact-p53-dependent-responses-in-mir-34-deficient-mice-by-carla-p-concepcion-and-others.md) This does not prove that [miR-34 family](../../../../../mir-34-family.md) cannot suppress a [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) in any setting. It shows that a gain-of-function phenotype and placement downstream of [p53](../../../../../p53.md) do not establish that the [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md) family is an indispensable general mediator of [p53](../../../../../p53.md) [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) suppression.

A convincing analysis combines several evidence types. Measure mature guide [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) in defined malignant and normal [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) populations, distinguishing it from precursor abundance and changes in stromal composition. Determine whether deletion, promoter silencing or altered processing explains loss. Reduce or delete the endogenous [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md) and test initiation or progression in a suitable model, with restoration from a controlled expression system. Compare related family members to expose redundancy. Use seed-mutant controls and multiple reagents to distinguish sequence-specific regulation from delivery toxicity or innate immune activation; deleting an entire processing [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) is not a specific test of one [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md) because it affects many regulatory RNAs.

To identify consequential targets, combine binding evidence, early [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md)/[protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) responses and target-site tests. A 3′-region reporter whose seed-complementary site is mutated supports direct sequence-dependent regulation, but the decisive endogenous test is to edit the site or express a suitably controlled [microRNA-resistant target](../../../../../microrna-resistant-target.md) and ask whether it rescues the phenotype. Failure of one target to rescue can reflect regulation of several cooperating targets rather than absence of specificity. Conversely, rescuing only bulk proliferation does not establish which target controls invasion or [metastasis](../../../../../metastasis.md). Measure relevant tissue phenotypes and target [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) levels, not merely a pathway enrichment score from predicted targets.

Tumour-suppressive activity also does not guarantee an effective therapy. Delivery must reach the relevant [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) and release the [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) into the correct compartment without excessive exposure of normal tissues. A clinical miR-34a [microRNA mimic](../../../../../microrna-mimic.md) study encountered serious immune-mediated toxicity, illustrating that an endogenous regulatory rationale does not establish safe systemic replacement.[Clinical safety findings and limits of replacement therapy](../../../../../phase-1-study-of-mrx34-a-liposomal-mir-34a-mimic-in-patients-with-advanced-solid-tumours-by-david-s-hong-and-others.md) Target abundance, isoform-specific 3′ regions and competing pathways can change the effect of the same [microRNA](../../../../../microrna.md) between [tumours](../../../../../tumour.md). **[microRNAs](../../../../../microrna.md) can be genuine [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) suppressors; the strongest evidence is specific endogenous loss, controlled restoration and mechanistic target tests, interpreted alongside genetic redundancy and tissue context.**
