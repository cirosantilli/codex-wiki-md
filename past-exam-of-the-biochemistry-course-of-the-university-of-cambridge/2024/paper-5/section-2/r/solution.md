↑ **Parent:** [R](../r.md)

**The weak [phalloidin](../../../../../phalloidin.md) signal could include sparse or very short residual [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md), together with nonspecific background; it is not a direct image of [G-actin](../../../../../actin-monomer.md).** [phalloidin](../../../../../phalloidin.md) preferentially binds [filamentous actin](../../../../../actin-filament.md), so genuinely specific residual signal would indicate some polymeric [actin](../../../../../actin.md). Long exposure also amplifies [autofluorescence](../../../../../autofluorescence.md), out-of-focus light and nonspecific staining. Vehicle controls, unstained [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), matched acquisition settings and background subtraction are needed before assigning a small residual signal to [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md).

For wavelength $\lambda$ and [numerical aperture](../../../../../numerical-aperture.md) $\mathrm{NA}$, the [Rayleigh criterion](../../../../../rayleigh-criterion.md) gives a conventional lateral resolution of roughly $d\approx0.61\lambda/\mathrm{NA}$, typically a few hundred nanometres under these conditions, far larger than the roughly 7-nm [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md) diameter. It cannot resolve every short [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md), distinguish all closely spaced [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md), or infer [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) mass directly from the displayed intensity. The tenfold exposure change is not itself a calibrated tenfold decrease in [F-actin](../../../../../actin-filament.md). Fixation, dye accessibility and detector saturation introduce further uncertainty.

Super-resolution [fluorescence microscopy](../../../../../fluorescence-microscopy.md) could localize remaining labelled structures more precisely, while [electron microscopy](../../../../../electron-microscopy.md) or [cryo-electron tomography](../../../../../cryo-electron-tomography.md) could test ultrastructure, subject to preservation and sampling limits. Biochemical separation of polymeric and soluble [actin](../../../../../actin.md), with the mass-balance and aggregation controls already discussed, supplies an independent population measurement. A live [actin](../../../../../actin.md) reporter can test dynamics but must be checked for perturbation of assembly. Agreement between these approaches would be stronger than concluding complete [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md) absence from weak conventional staining alone.
