<h1 id="section-2/m/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [M](../m.md)

**Experiment 2:** relative to untreated wild-type [actin](../../../../../actin.md), [actin monomer sequestration](../../../../../actin-monomer-sequestration.md) predicts a longer apparent [actin nucleation](../../../../../actin-nucleation.md) lag, a smaller elongation slope, and a lower steady-state [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) mass. At sufficiently strong binding the trace could remain nearly flat rather than displaying clearly separated phases. The wild-type [sedimentation](../../../../../sedimentation.md) data support a lower endpoint, but do not establish the exact kinetic changes or whether the time to plateau is longer or shorter.

**Experiment 3:** the halo result does not determine the drug-free [actin polymerization](../../../../../actin-polymerization.md) curve of act1-129. A [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) that mainly raises drug affinity could have a nearly wild-type curve without drug. A [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) that intrinsically destabilizes [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md) or impairs [actin nucleation](../../../../../actin-nucleation.md) could instead prolong the lag, reduce growth and lower the plateau. Thus a wild-type-like untreated curve is a useful conditional reference prediction, not a result that follows from cellular hypersensitivity.

**Experiment 4:** act1-129 is about sixfold more sensitive in the cellular assay. If that reflects a stronger direct effect of ToxB on its [actin monomers](../../../../../actin-monomer.md), predict greater suppression than in experiment 2: a longer or effectively absent [actin nucleation](../../../../../actin-nucleation.md) phase, slower or undetectable elongation, and a still lower plateau relative to its own drug-free control. It could be almost flat. However, cellular hypersensitivity could also arise from reduced functional reserve or altered interactions with other [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md); it does not guarantee stronger inhibition of purified [actin](../../../../../actin.md). These alternatives are exactly what the comparison with experiment 3 resolves. No numerical lag, slope or plateau can be calculated from halo sizes alone.
