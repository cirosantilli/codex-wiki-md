<h1 id="section-2/q/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Q](../q.md)

**Some [cell membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) delivery and fusion must continue in the [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) whose buds actually grow, but it need not use normal long-range cable transport.** [secretory vesicles](../../../../../secretory-vesicle.md) can move by [molecular diffusion](../../../../../molecular-diffusion.md) or other non-directed motion and be preferentially captured, tethered and fused where a surviving polarity cue concentrates the relevant [vesicle docking](../../../../../vesicle-docking.md) machinery. Spatial selectivity can therefore reside in the destination rather than in directed travel over the entire route. Less efficient delivery is consistent with delayed budding and small buds.

Short residual [actin](../../../../../actin.md) tracks could also contribute; the experiment does not prove that every [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md) is absent. Neither bud shape nor [Con-A](../../../../../concanavalin-a.md) staining directly measures vesicle trajectories or fusion events. Tracking [secretory vesicles](../../../../../secretory-vesicle.md) together with a membrane-fusion reporter and a polarity marker would distinguish directed runs from [molecular diffusion](../../../../../molecular-diffusion.md) followed by localized capture, with expression and imaging controls for reporter effects. Random arrival with selective [vesicle docking](../../../../../vesicle-docking.md) is a published mechanistic proposal for this physiological context, rather than a demonstrated trajectory in the present images.[Discussion: vesicle delivery and preferential docking during polarized growth](../../../../../polarized-growth-in-the-absence-of-f-actin-in-saccharomyces-cerevisiae-exiting-quiescence-by-annelise-sahin-and-others.md)
