<h1 id="section-2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**ToxB greatly reduces recognizable [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md) and [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md).** Untreated [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) contain concentrated cortical structures, especially in buds, whereas treated [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) show only weak diffuse staining even at ten times the exposure. The disappearance concerns [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md) organization and detectable [F-actin](../../../../../actin-filament.md), not necessarily destruction of the [actin](../../../../../actin.md) [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) itself. [actin monomer sequestration](../../../../../actin-monomer-sequestration.md) could leave substantial total [actin](../../../../../actin.md) present while eliminating most visible [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md).

Loss of [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md) should impair directed secretory-vesicle delivery and [polarized cell growth](../../../../../polarized-cell-growth.md); loss of [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) should impair actin-dependent [endocytosis](../../../../../endocytosis.md). Disruption of the [actomyosin](../../../../../actomyosin.md) machinery can also compromise [cytokinesis](../../../../../cytokinesis.md). The rounded, poorly polarized appearance is consistent with these effects, but the images do not independently measure secretion, division rate or viability. A [vehicle control](../../../../../vehicle-control.md) and equal-exposure images are needed for quantitative [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) comparison; a longer exposure is useful for finding residual structures but prevents direct comparison of the displayed brightnesses.
