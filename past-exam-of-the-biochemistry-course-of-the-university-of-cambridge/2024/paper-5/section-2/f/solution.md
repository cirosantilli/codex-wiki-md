<h1 id="section-2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

**ToxB strongly reduces sedimentable wild-type [actin](../../../../../actin.md), whereas act1-117 shows little additional reduction from its already low baseline.** Wild-type [actin](../../../../../actin.md) is mainly in the pellet without drug and mainly in the [supernatant](../../../../../supernatant.md) with drug. Using the approximate bar heights, the pelleted fraction changes from $740/(740+120)\approx0.86$ to $280/(280+820)\approx0.25$. Thus the drug suppresses [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) accumulation but does not eliminate the measured pellet entirely.

For act1-117, the pellet is only about one-third of the recovered signal even without drug, and remains near that fraction with drug: approximately $340/(340+570)\approx0.37$ and $360/(360+660)\approx0.35$. The mutant is therefore relatively resistant to the drug's further effect under this assay, but it is not a wild-type-quality polymerizer with an unchanged high [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) mass. These are approximate within-pair fractions; unequal total recoveries and loading limit precise quantitative comparison. [sedimentation](../../../../../sedimentation.md) also requires confirmation that pellet material is filamentous rather than aggregated.
