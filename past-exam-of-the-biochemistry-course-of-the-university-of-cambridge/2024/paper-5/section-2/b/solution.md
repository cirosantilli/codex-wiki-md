<h1 id="section-2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**The detectable [cytoskeleton](../../../../../cytoskeleton.md) effect is substantially reversible after washout.** Depolarized [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) reappear first, reaching roughly 85% of [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) near 20 minutes. Polarized [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) subsequently increase to about 80%, while detectable [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md) recover later, reaching roughly 70–75% by the end of the time course. This argues against irreversible destruction of all cellular capacity to assemble [actin](../../../../../actin.md).

Recovery is not instantaneous, and the population measurements do not prove that every previously treated [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) recovers. Nor do they distinguish reuse of the existing [actin](../../../../../actin.md) pool from a contribution of new [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) synthesis. A time-matched vehicle culture and a continuously treated culture would separate washout-dependent recovery from changes caused by handling or time alone. Reversibility of visible structures also does not by itself establish recovery of every physiological function.
