<h1 id="section-2/o/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [O](../o.md)

**Efficient and timely budding requires the normal [actin](../../../../../actin.md) network, but some initial [polarized cell growth](../../../../../polarized-cell-growth.md) remains possible when visible [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md) are greatly depleted.** Around 40% of control [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) have new buds by two hours, rising to nearly 60% by six hours. Drug-treated [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) show almost no budding at two hours but roughly 15–20% at four to six hours, and the phase images confirm small buds despite loss of normal bright [actin](../../../../../actin.md) structures.

The result therefore supports a large delay and reduction, rather than an absolute block to every initiation event. Polarity cues or localized membrane-fusion machinery could remain spatially organized without normal long [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md). The images and bud counts do not establish completion of a daughter-cell cycle, normal [cytokinesis](../../../../../cytokinesis.md) or indefinite proliferation. Residual [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md) below detection remain an alternative contributor. This distinction between initial bud emergence and completion of growth was directly examined in a quiescence-exit study.[Bud-emergence and cell-cycle-completion experiments](../../../../../polarized-growth-in-the-absence-of-f-actin-in-saccharomyces-cerevisiae-exiting-quiescence-by-annelise-sahin-and-others.md)
