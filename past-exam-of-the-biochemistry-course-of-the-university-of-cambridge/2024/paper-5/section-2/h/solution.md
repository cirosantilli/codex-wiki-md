**A [pyrene-actin assay](../../../../../pyrene-actin-assay.md) can follow assembly continuously.** A small labelled fraction mixed with otherwise unlabelled [actin](../../../../../actin.md) gives an increasing signal as the labelled subunits enter [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md), allowing comparison of lag, growth and plateau. Drug-only and labelled-monomer controls are essential because ToxB might alter [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) directly; comparable label incorporation and a polymer-mass calibration are needed to compare wild-type and mutant [actin](../../../../../actin.md). An institutional description explains this polymerization-dependent [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) readout.[Principle: enhanced fluorescence on polymerization](../../../../../preparation-of-pyrenyl-actin-by-the-maciver-laboratory.md)

Direct single-filament imaging by [total internal reflection fluorescence microscopy](../../../../../total-internal-reflection-fluorescence-microscopy.md) can separately measure [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md) appearance, elongation and shortening, rather than inferring all three from bulk [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) mass. It requires matched surface and label conditions, since immobilization can perturb assembly. [light scattering](../../../../../scattering-of-light.md) or [turbidity](../../../../../turbidity.md) can also follow assembly, but aggregates and changes in [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md) length or bundling complicate mass estimates. [electron microscopy](../../../../../electron-microscopy.md) can verify [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md) morphology and length at selected times, although sampling and preparation make it less convenient for a continuous kinetic curve. Combining a bulk assay with direct [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md) imaging addresses different ambiguities.
