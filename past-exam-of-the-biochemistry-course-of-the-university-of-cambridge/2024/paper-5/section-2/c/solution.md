**Both structures turn over rapidly, with visible [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md) disappearing faster than [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) under these conditions.** Cable-positive [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) fall from about 80% to essentially none within roughly two minutes. Polarized [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) disappear over several minutes, while a transient depolarized-patch population persists before nearly all [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) are lost by the end of the treatment time course.

Because ToxB binds [actin monomers](../../../../../actin-monomer.md) rather than existing [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md), a straightforward explanation is continuing normal disassembly without adequate replacement from assembly-competent [actin monomers](../../../../../actin-monomer.md). The disappearance therefore reveals dynamic maintenance of structures that can look static in a fixed image. It does not show that the drug directly severs [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md). Percentages of [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) above a visibility threshold are not exponential decay curves for the lifetimes of individual [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md): cable thickness, staining sensitivity, [actin monomer](../../../../../actin-monomer.md) access and secondary loss of polarity can all affect the apparent timing. An analogous monomer-sequestering inhibitor established rapid [yeast](../../../../../yeast.md) [actin turnover](../../../../../actin-turnover.md) in the classical study by [Kathryn R. Ayscough](../../../../../kathryn-r-ayscough.md) and colleagues.[Actin-disassembly and recovery time courses](../../../../../high-rates-of-actin-filament-turnover-in-budding-yeast-and-roles-for-actin-in-establishment-and-maintenance-of-cell-polarity-revealed-using-the-actin-inhibitor-latrunculin-a-by-kathryn-r-ayscough-and-others.md)
