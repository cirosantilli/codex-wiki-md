<h1 id="section-2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**[actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) can re-form, and can become polarized, before detectable [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md) return.** Depolarized [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) are abundant around 20 minutes after washout, when the cable curve remains essentially zero. By roughly 40 minutes many [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) already have polarized [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md), still with almost no visible [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md). Thus a strict requirement for an already established visible cable network in patch assembly or initial patch polarization is inconsistent with these population data.

The converse is not demonstrated: [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md) recover only after [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) have returned, so this experiment cannot establish that [actin cables](../../../../../actin-cable.md) assemble independently of [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md). It also cannot exclude sparse or very short [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md) below the cable-detection threshold. The percentages do not follow the same individual [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), and temporal order alone does not prove a direct molecular dependence. The strongest conclusion is the one-way independence actually resolved by the graph, rather than complete mutual independence.
