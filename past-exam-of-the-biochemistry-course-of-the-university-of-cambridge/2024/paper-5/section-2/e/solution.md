↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**[actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) initially return in a depolarized distribution and then become concentrated at a growth pole.** The depolarized fraction peaks near 20 minutes and subsequently falls as the polarized fraction rises. Patch assembly therefore recovers before normal spatial organization is restored.

A plausible cellular interpretation is recovery from disrupted trafficking and growth polarity, followed by establishment of a bud-associated growth site and renewed polarized secretion and [endocytosis](../../../../../endocytosis.md). Continued cell-cycle progression can also redistribute [actin patches](../../../../../actin-patch.md) as [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) grow and prepare to divide. The graph alone does not show that all [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) pass synchronously through one particular cell-cycle stage, or that the recovered pole is the same pole used before treatment. Time-lapse imaging of the same [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) and a bud or cell-cycle marker would distinguish restoration of an old site from selection of a new one.
