<h1 id="section-2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The major problems are unequal recovered totals, absence of replication/error estimates, and ambiguity about what pellets.** Wild-type [supernatant](../../../../../supernatant.md) plus pellet signal is roughly 860 units without drug and 1,100 with drug; the mutant totals also differ. Equal starting [actin](../../../../../actin.md) should give comparable total recovery after correcting for fraction volumes and loading. The graph does not specify such corrections, so comparing raw pellet heights alone is misleading. Fractions $P/(S+P)$ are more informative but still require comparable staining response and unbiased recovery.

The mutant's poor drug-free pelleting is biologically important and should not be hidden by calling it simply resistant. Independent preparations and replicate assays would establish whether that baseline is reproducible. One drug [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) and one nominal steady-state time cannot establish potency, [binding affinity](../../../../../binding-affinity.md), assembly kinetics or attainment of a true steady state. The gel confirms an actin-sized band but does not preserve [actin filament](../../../../../actin-filament.md) structure: denaturation occurs after separation. Short [actin filaments](../../../../../actin-filament.md) can remain in the [supernatant](../../../../../supernatant.md), and aggregates can pellet. Direct imaging, mass-balance controls, a monomer-only control and a linear [densitometry](../../../../../densitometry.md) calibration would make the interpretation substantially stronger.
