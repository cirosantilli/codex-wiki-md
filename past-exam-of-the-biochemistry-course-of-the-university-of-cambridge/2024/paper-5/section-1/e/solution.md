<h1 id="section-1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**Latent viral [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) has regularly spaced [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) protection, whereas much of the nuclear viral [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) during productive infection lacks that regular protection.** In the latent sample, increasing digestion resolves a ladder of protected fragments, as expected when [micrococcal nuclease](../../../../../micrococcal-nuclease.md) cuts exposed linker [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) between [nucleosomes](../../../../../nucleosome.md). In the productive sample, viral signal is lost rapidly into heterogeneous small material rather than accumulating as a comparable ladder. That is consistent with a large pool of newly replicated or otherwise differently packaged viral [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) being more accessible.

The total-DNA ladders show that host [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) remains nucleosomal and that the assay can recover protected fragments in each cellular condition. The virus-specific [Southern blot](../../../../../southern-blot.md) distinguishes viral [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) from this abundant host background, and the uninfected lane controls probe background. The conclusion concerns the dominant detectable viral population: productive cultures can contain both chromatinized templates in [viral latency](../../../../../viral-latency.md) and accessible replication products. Absence of a ladder does not establish absence of every [histone](../../../../../histone.md) molecule, and differences in [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) recovery or [nuclease](../../../../../nuclease.md) accessibility should be considered before assigning a precise fraction of chromatinized [genomes](../../../../../genome.md).
