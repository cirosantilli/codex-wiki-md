<h1 id="section-1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

About 80% of full-length-expressing [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) have two conspicuous nuclear foci, whereas roughly 90% of [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) expressing the deletion [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) have none. Both [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) show nuclear [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md), so the result is not explained simply by complete exclusion of the deletion [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) from the nucleus. **The [DNA-binding domain](../../../../../dna-binding-domain.md) is needed for efficient focal accumulation at a small number of host sites.** A plausible explanation is recognition of repeated host [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequences resembling the viral binding sites; clustered repeats and homotypic contacts would concentrate the [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) enough to create visible foci.

Two spots are compatible with one locus on each [homologous chromosome](../../../../../homologous-chromosome.md) in a diploid [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). They do not prove diploidy, identify a [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md), or exclude unresolved sister loci and cell-cycle effects. An alternative is DNA-dependent recruitment through a host complex. Matching [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) abundance and confirming stability of the deletion construct would make the domain comparison stronger. Punctate [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) alone does not demonstrate a liquid condensate or show that a focus identifies an [origin of replication](../../../../../origin-of-replication.md).
