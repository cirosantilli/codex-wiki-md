↑ **Parent:** [P](../p.md)

**NVNA1 could contribute to [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) by increasing the chance of host [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) damage and heritable rearrangements.** Persistent occupancy of a [chromosomal fragile site](../../../../../chromosomal-fragile-site.md) could obstruct replication or normal [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) reassembly. Incompletely replicated or damaged [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) can break, mis-segregate and be repaired into deletions, [gene fusions](../../../../../gene-fusion.md) or [copy-number alterations](../../../../../copy-number-alteration.md). Particular surviving rearrangements might remove a [tumour suppressor gene](../../../../../tumour-suppressor-gene.md) or deregulate an [oncogene](../../../../../oncogene.md), after which [clonal selection](../../../../../clonal-selection.md) could expand that lineage. A viral [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) can therefore promote [genomic instability](../../../../../genomic-instability.md) without the viral [genome](../../../../../genome.md) having to integrate at every breakpoint.

The tumour-sequencing association is consistent with this mechanism but does not establish causality by itself. [tumour](../../../../../tumour.md) type, pre-existing instability, infection detection sensitivity and cellular composition can confound an NV-positive/NV-negative comparison. The induction experiment strengthens the case that NVNA1 can damage the locus, while its high doses limit direct estimation of risk during ordinary [viral latency](../../../../../viral-latency.md). Very common infection is compatible with rare [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) because susceptible [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) types, expression fluctuations, time and cooperating alterations all matter. A documented biological analogue links EBNA1 occupancy of a [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) 11 repeat cluster to [chromosome breakage](../../../../../chromosome-breakage.md) and rearrangement.[Figures 3–4 and tumour-genome analysis: locus damage and chromosome 11 structural variants](../../../../../chromosomal-fragile-site-breakage-by-ebv-encoded-ebna1-at-clustered-repeats-by-julia-su-zhou-li-and-others.md)
