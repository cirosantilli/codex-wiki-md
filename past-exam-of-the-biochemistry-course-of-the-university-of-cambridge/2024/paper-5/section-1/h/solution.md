<h1 id="section-1/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

With [nucleosomes](../../../../../nucleosome.md) alone, neither competitor substantially removes the main [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) band, although some free labelled [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is present. With nucleosome–NVNA1 complexes, increasing [specific competitor DNA](../../../../../specific-competitor-dna.md) removes the extra shifts and restores the [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) band. **The starting shifted complexes therefore contained [histones](../../../../../histone.md) as well as NVNA1:** competitor recognition sites draw NVNA1 away while leaving labelled [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) associated with [histones](../../../../../histone.md).

[nonspecific competitor DNA](../../../../../nonspecific-competitor-dna.md) gives a different endpoint. The shifted population changes to a band matching the far-right NVNA1–naked-DNA control, rather than returning mainly to the [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) position. The labelled [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) remains bound to NVNA1 while [histones](../../../../../histone.md) can transfer to the excess competitor [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). **NVNA1 binding destabilizes the [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) against competitor-induced [histone](../../../../../histone.md) displacement.** Remaining shifted [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is not evidence that the [histone octamer](../../../../../histone-octamer.md) remained: its mobility must be compared with both controls. The nucleosome-only competition lanes show that competitor [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) alone is insufficient to cause the same conversion. Histone-specific detection would confirm the inferred component loss. A closely analogous ternary-complex and competition analysis was demonstrated for EBNA1; it provides a methodological precedent without establishing the identity of NV.[Figures 2–4: ternary complexes, specific competition and histone displacement](../../../../../epstein-barr-nuclear-antigen-1-binds-and-destabilizes-nucleosomes-at-the-viral-origin-of-latent-dna-replication-by-tina-m-avolio-hunter-and-others.md)
