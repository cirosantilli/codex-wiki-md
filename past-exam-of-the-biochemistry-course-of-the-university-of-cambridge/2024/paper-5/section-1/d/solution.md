<h1 id="section-1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At low [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md), the array with more and higher-affinity binding sites is preferentially occupied, giving one visible [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) cluster on an otherwise extended [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) molecule. At higher [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) the weaker origin array also becomes occupied. **[homotypic protein interactions](../../../../../homotypic-protein-interaction.md) between [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) at the two arrays can bridge them, trapping the intervening [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) through [DNA looping](../../../../../dna-looping.md).** This explains why the electron-dense [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) cluster lies at the loop junction rather than uniformly coating the entire molecule.

The high-affinity array can stabilize weak-site occupancy through tethering: once one partner is anchored, the second site experiences a high effective local [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) and several contacts can contribute [avidity](../../../../../avidity.md). This reconciles stable looped assemblies with the vulnerability of the isolated weak site to [nonspecific competitor DNA](../../../../../nonspecific-competitor-dna.md). The images support intramolecular [DNA bridging](../../../../../dna-bridging.md), but selected micrographs do not measure its frequency or exclude some intermolecular associations. A high local density of [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) need not imply [biomolecular phase separation](../../../../../biomolecular-phase-separation.md). The printed scale-bar unit appears implausible for a kilobase-scale [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) substrate; the mechanistic inference uses topology and site positions rather than that unit.
