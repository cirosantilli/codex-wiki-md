↑ **Parent:** [M](../m.md)

[aphidicolin](../../../../../aphidicolin.md) increases the abnormal-locus fraction from roughly 40% to about 55–60% in all three lines. **Inhibition of replicative [DNA polymerases](../../../../../dna-polymerase.md) increases [replication stress](../../../../../replication-stress.md) at an intrinsically difficult-to-complete region.** Tandem repeats can complicate fork progression or [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md) organization. Slower or incomplete synthesis leaves [underreplicated DNA](../../../../../underreplicated-dna.md), unresolved replication intermediates or abnormal condensation as [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) approach [mitosis](../../../../../mitosis.md); these can appear as split signals or gaps and can predispose to actual breakage.

This is an increase of approximately 15–20 percentage points, not proof of a 15–20-fold change. The two independent measurements per condition support a reproducible direction, but the graph does not provide enough information for a precise significance calculation or a quantitative model of fork failure. [aphidicolin](../../../../../aphidicolin.md) need not bind NVNA1 or the repeat sequence directly. Because these are uninfected [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), the result establishes susceptibility of the host locus to [replication stress](../../../../../replication-stress.md) independently of viral [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) expression.
