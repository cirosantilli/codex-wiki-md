<h1 id="section-1/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

The naked labelled [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) runs fastest. Reconstitution creates a slower [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) band, with a little residual unassembled [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Adding NVNA1 progressively generates several additional, more slowly migrating labelled species and reduces the unbound [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) population. **These are consistent with [ternary protein–DNA complexes](../../../../../ternary-protein-dna-complex.md) containing [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), [histones](../../../../../histone.md) and NVNA1, with increasing occupancy or altered assembly.** The naked-DNA titration also shows several NVNA1–DNA species, reflecting different occupancy states on the repeat-containing fragment.

A mobility shift reports changes in charge, size and shape together. It cannot by itself establish that [histones](../../../../../histone.md) remain in every shifted species, or identify exact [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) stoichiometries. In particular, similarity to a band in the naked-DNA lanes could indicate [histone](../../../../../histone.md) loss rather than merely another occupancy state. The specific-competitor experiment in the next part supplies the important compositional test: removal of NVNA1 can regenerate a [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) band, demonstrating retention of [histones](../../../../../histone.md) in the starting ternary population.
