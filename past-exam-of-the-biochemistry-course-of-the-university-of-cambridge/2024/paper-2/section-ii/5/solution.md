**I would test three separable claims: that a distinct assembly exists, that particular mRNAs are recruited or retained there, and that this recruitment causally alters their [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md).** Establishing one claim does not establish the others. In particular, a fluorescent punctum need not be a phase-separated liquid, and enrichment of untranslated [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) need not mean that the assembly caused repression.

Begin by tagging a candidate resident at its endogenous locus, verifying that the tagged [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) retains its normal abundance and function. Identify additional resident [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) and RNAs with independent probes, assess ultrastructure or [cell membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) exclusion where necessary, and follow assembly and dissolution over physiological time courses. Measure [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) dependence, partitioning and exchange rather than classifying the structure from roundness alone. [fluorescence recovery after photobleaching](../../../../../fluorescence-recovery-after-photobleaching.md) reports mobility and exchange, but recovery can occur in non-liquid assemblies; fusion and rapid recovery are supporting observations rather than decisive tests. Avoid interpreting dissolution by 1,6-hexanediol as specific evidence, because it can perturb other cellular structures and processes. If phase separation is proposed, purified-component reconstitution should establish appropriate [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md), salt and [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) dependences, including sequence and valency controls and comparison with estimated cellular concentrations.

To identify recruited mRNAs, combine granule enrichment with [RNA sequencing](../../../../../rna-sequencing.md), controlling nonspecific recovery and input transcript abundance. Validate candidates by [single-molecule fluorescence in situ hybridization](../../../../../single-molecule-fluorescence-in-situ-hybridization.md) using endogenous RNAs, negative-control RNAs and probes spanning different transcript regions. Three-dimensional segmentation and randomized spatial controls help distinguish genuine enrichment from chance overlap in a crowded [cytoplasm](../../../../../cytoplasm.md). A useful [RNA partition coefficient](../../../../../rna-partition-coefficient.md) is $K_p=c_{\mathrm{in}}/c_{\mathrm{out}}$, estimated from molecule numbers and accessible volumes rather than uncorrected total intensity. A small assembly can have high [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) but contain only a small fraction of the cellular transcript, so report both enrichment and the fraction of molecules recruited.

Live imaging using an MS2- or PP7-tagged [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) can measure entry, exit, dwell time and transport relative to the assembly. Compare the labelled transcript with the untagged endogenous distribution: repeated hairpins, coat-protein binding and fluorescent-protein expression can alter processing or mobility. Track whole particles in three dimensions where possible to separate actual residence from transient passage through an imaging plane. A localization element identified in a candidate 3′ region can be deleted or transferred to a neutral reporter, with matched [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) abundance and integrity, to test sequence-dependent targeting. [crosslinking and immunoprecipitation](../../../../../crosslinking-and-immunoprecipitation.md) of the proposed recruitment factor then tests direct contact rather than mere colocalization.

[biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) should be measured on the same [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) molecules wherever possible. A reporter bearing RNA-label sites and an N-terminal repeated peptide tag, such as [SunTag](../../../../../suntag.md), makes an [mRNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) visible separately from its nascent chains. A bright nascent-chain focus at an [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) can report ongoing [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md), while completed [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) diffuse away. Puromycin-mediated release and initiation-block/run-off controls validate the translation-dependent signal; tag maturation, antibody-fragment availability and altered peptide behaviour need controls. Fixed-cell [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) abundance or a ribosomal-protein stain is inadequate: either can be present without active [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) at the identified [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md).

This caution has experimental force. Single-molecule work by [Daniel Mateju](../../../../../daniel-mateju.md) and colleagues showed that some stress-granule-associated mRNAs undergo [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md), including complete [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) cycles, rather than being obligatorily silent.[Simultaneous RNA tracking and nascent-chain imaging](../../../../../single-molecule-imaging-reveals-translation-of-mrnas-localized-to-stress-granules-by-daniel-mateju-and-others.md) For the proposed new assembly, compare the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) and rate of [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) before entry, during residence and after exit, with matched cytoplasmic molecules of the same transcript. If loss of [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) consistently precedes recruitment, the assembly may sort previously repressed [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md). If recruitment precedes a reproducible [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) change, that supports a different direction of action but still requires perturbation to exclude a common upstream cause.

Causality requires a perturbation that separates recruitment or condensation from other functions. Acutely deplete an assembly scaffold and rescue with wild-type [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) or a separation-of-function variant that retains [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) binding and normal abundance but fails to assemble, or conversely assembles but cannot recruit the chosen [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md). Verify those properties directly. Studies of [G3BP1](../../../../../g3bp1.md)/G3BP2-deficient [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) showed that stress-granule assembly requirements depend on the initiating stress, motivating matched tests of the upstream [stress response](../../../../../stress-response.md) rather than assuming one knockout removes every granule type.[Double-deficient cells, rescue and stress-specific assembly](../../../../../g3bp-caprin1-usp10-complexes-mediate-stress-granule-condensation-and-associate-with-40s-subunits-by-nancy-kedersha-and-others.md) Measure global [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md), stress signalling, [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) stability and [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) viability alongside the local reporter. Loss of a granule accompanied by wholesale loss of [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) machinery is not a specific test of granule function.

An orthogonal manipulation could inducibly recruit the same [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) to the assembly or release it, using inert recruitment controls and a non-condensing scaffold. Artificial clustering can itself obstruct [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md), so this establishes conditional sufficiency only. Reversible manipulation and rescue, together with endogenous cis-element edits, provide stronger evidence. **A convincing result connects a defined RNA-recruitment mechanism to a reversible [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) change while preserving [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) abundance and upstream cellular state; phase behaviour and regulatory function should be concluded separately.**
