<h1 id="section-ii/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**[RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) couples [iron](../../../../../iron.md) availability to the production of [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) that acquire, store, export and use [iron](../../../../../iron.md).** The clearest mammalian mechanism uses structured [iron-responsive elements](../../../../../iron-responsive-element.md) in untranslated regions and iron-regulated binding [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md). The [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) element encodes a regulatory address and its position determines the response; the main sensing machinery is protein-based, so this system should not be described as an autonomous iron-binding [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) riboswitch.

An [iron-responsive element](../../../../../iron-responsive-element.md) is a [stem-loop](../../../../../stem-loop.md) with conserved sequence and structural features recognized by [iron regulatory proteins](../../../../../iron-regulatory-protein.md). At low available [iron](../../../../../iron.md), RNA-binding [IRP1](../../../../../irp1.md) and [IRP2](../../../../../irp2.md) are active. [IRP1](../../../../../irp1.md) is bifunctional: an iron–sulfur-cluster-containing form acts as cytosolic [aconitase](../../../../../aconitase.md), whereas loss of the functional cluster permits high-affinity [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) binding. Biochemical disassembly of its cluster connected these two activities directly.[Cluster state and RNA-binding activity](../../../../../cellular-regulation-of-the-iron-responsive-element-binding-protein-disassembly-of-the-cubane-iron-sulfur-cluster-results-in-high-affinity-rna-binding-by-david-j-haile-and-others.md) [IRP2](../../../../../irp2.md) does not use the same [aconitase](../../../../../aconitase.md) switch. Under iron- and oxygen-replete conditions, the [FBXL5](../../../../../fbxl5.md) ubiquitin-ligase pathway promotes [IRP2](../../../../../irp2.md) degradation; instability of the iron-sensitive [FBXL5](../../../../../fbxl5.md) regulator during [iron](../../../../../iron.md) depletion helps preserve [IRP2](../../../../../irp2.md).[FBXL5 sensing, stability and IRP2 degradation](../../../../../an-e3-ligase-possessing-an-iron-responsive-hemerythrin-domain-is-a-regulator-of-iron-homeostasis-by-ameen-a-salahudeen-and-others.md) Redox and oxygen conditions can therefore modify the relation between total cellular [iron](../../../../../iron.md) and RNA-binding activity.

The canonical 5′ example is [ferritin](../../../../../ferritin.md) [mRNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md). Binding of an [IRP](../../../../../iron-regulatory-protein.md) near the cap prevents efficient recruitment of the translation-initiation machinery, lowering [ferritin](../../../../../ferritin.md) synthesis when [iron](../../../../../iron.md) is scarce. This avoids committing a limited resource to storage. When [iron](../../../../../iron.md) is plentiful, reduced [IRP](../../../../../iron-regulatory-protein.md) occupancy releases repression, allowing more [ferritin](../../../../../ferritin.md) to store [iron](../../../../../iron.md) and limit its reactive pool. Deletion analysis and transfer of a synthetic [ferritin](../../../../../ferritin.md) element to another reporter established a cis-acting signal sufficient to confer iron-responsive [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md).[Ferritin leader deletion and synthetic-element transfer](../../../../../identification-of-the-iron-responsive-element-for-the-translational-regulation-of-human-ferritin-mrna-by-matthias-w-hentze-and-others.md) This response can change [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) before a major change in transcript abundance occurs.

At the 3′ end of [transferrin receptor 1](../../../../../transferrin-receptor-1.md) [mRNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md), multiple IREs participate in a stability-control region. [IRP](../../../../../iron-regulatory-protein.md) binding during [iron](../../../../../iron.md) shortage protects the message from destabilization, increasing receptor production and [iron](../../../../../iron.md) uptake. At higher [iron](../../../../../iron.md), loss of protection permits more rapid decay and reduces uptake. [John L. Casey](../../../../../john-l-casey.md) and colleagues showed that the transferrin-receptor 3′ region conferred iron-regulated [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) abundance and that isolated elements placed in a 5′ reporter context instead conferred translational control.[Context-dependent reporter regulation by ferritin-like stem-loops](../../../../../iron-responsive-elements-regulatory-rna-sequences-that-control-mrna-levels-and-translation-by-john-l-casey-and-others.md) Thus similar recognition chemistry supports opposite outputs through different transcript positions and associated machinery.

The network also adjusts use and release. A 5′ [IRE](../../../../../iron-responsive-element.md) in erythroid [ALAS2](../../../../../alas2.md) [mRNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) couples synthesis of a haem-pathway enzyme to [iron](../../../../../iron.md) availability, helping limit production of [porphyrin](../../../../../porphyrin.md) precursors when metal incorporation is restricted.[Erythroid ALAS leader element and iron-dependent translation](../../../../../identification-of-a-novel-iron-responsive-element-in-murine-and-human-erythroid-delta-aminolevulinic-acid-synthase-mrna-by-thomas-dandekar-and-others.md) IRE-containing [ferroportin](../../../../../ferroportin.md) transcripts can reduce [iron](../../../../../iron.md) export through translational repression during deficiency.[IRE deletion reporters distinguish protein-output regulation from RNA abundance](../../../../../the-role-of-the-iron-responsive-element-in-the-control-of-ferroportin1-ireg1-mtp1-gene-expression-by-athina-lymboussaki-and-others.md) These are transcript-specific rules, not universal predictions from a [gene](../../../../../gene.md) name: alternative transcripts can lack an [IRE](../../../../../iron-responsive-element.md), and [ferroportin](../../../../../ferroportin.md) is also regulated at the [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) level by systemic signals. The central [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) logic coordinates uptake with conservation, rather than simply maximizing the expression of every iron-related [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md).

Regulation beyond the mammalian [IRE](../../../../../iron-responsive-element.md) system illustrates a broader eukaryotic principle. In budding yeast, [iron](../../../../../iron.md) deficiency induces the RNA-binding regulator [Cth2](../../../../../cth2.md), which recognizes AU-rich elements in selected 3′ regions and accelerates degradation of messages encoding iron-consuming metabolic functions. This remodels resource allocation rather than protecting an uptake message. [Sergi Puig](../../../../../sergi-puig.md) and colleagues combined expression and RNA-turnover analysis with RNA-element recognition to establish targeted degradation during deficiency.[Cth2-dependent turnover of iron-utilization transcripts](../../../../../coordinated-remodeling-of-cellular-metabolism-during-iron-deficiency-through-targeted-mrna-degradation-by-sergi-puig-and-others.md) It is therefore misleading to generalize that [iron](../../../../../iron.md) shortage always stabilizes iron-related [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md): the direction depends on the transcript and its physiological role.

To establish these mechanisms experimentally, compare [iron](../../../../../iron.md) chelation with [iron](../../../../../iron.md) restoration, measuring available [iron](../../../../../iron.md), IRP–RNA binding, transcript half-life and newly synthesized [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) separately. Match reporter [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) and mutate recognition features without inadvertently changing general [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) stability. For a [stem-loop](../../../../../stem-loop.md), a compensatory paired [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) helps distinguish loss of structure from loss of a specific sequence contact. Electrophoretic binding assays with purified [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md), cellular crosslinking and rescue with functional versus binding-defective [IRP](../../../../../iron-regulatory-protein.md) variants connect recognition to regulation. RNA-abundance measurements alone cannot establish translational repression, and chelators can cause broad stress, so unrelated control reporters and viability measurements are needed. **The decisive principle is position-dependent [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) control: low [iron](../../../../../iron.md) can repress storage and export [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) while preserving uptake [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) and reducing expendable iron-consuming programmes.**
