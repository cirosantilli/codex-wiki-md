**[chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) has organization across overlapping length scales, and each assay measures a particular aspect rather than a universal “openness”.** Local [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) exposure, [histone](../../../../../histone.md) occupancy, chemical state, long-range proximity and position within the nucleus are related but distinct. A strong [experimental design](../../../../../design-of-experiments-split.md) chooses the relevant scale and combines a genomic assay with an independent structural or functional measurement.

At the molecular scale, [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) wraps around a [histone octamer](../../../../../histone-octamer.md) to form a [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) core, with roughly 147 base pairs protected; [linker DNA](../../../../../linker-dna.md) connects neighbouring units, and linker [histones](../../../../../histone.md) can affect their organization. The core contains two copies each of H2A, H2B, H3 and H4. Crystallography established the detailed DNA–histone arrangement using a defined [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) core particle.[Atomic structure of the reconstituted core particle](../../../../../crystal-structure-of-the-nucleosome-core-particle-at-2-8-a-resolution-by-karolin-luger-and-others.md) Such structures explain local contacts, but a purified core does not specify the arrangement of an entire chromosome in a living nucleus. [histone](../../../../../histone.md) variants, tail modifications and ATP-dependent remodelling change stability and access without requiring DNA-sequence changes.

For local accessibility, [ATAC-seq](../../../../../atac-seq.md) uses transposase-mediated insertion of sequencing adapters into accessible [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). It maps accessible [promoters](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md) and regulatory elements, and its fragment-length distribution can contain signatures of nucleosome-free regions and neighbouring [nucleosomes](../../../../../nucleosome.md). The original assay combined rapid accessibility mapping with information about [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) organization.[ATAC-seq development and accessible-region profiling](../../../../../transposition-of-native-chromatin-for-fast-and-sensitive-epigenomic-profiling-of-open-chromatin-dna-binding-proteins-and-nucleosome-position-by-jason-d-buenrostro-and-others.md) DNase I hypersensitivity provides a related cleavage-based measure. Both have sequence bias and depend on preparation and enzyme exposure; a footprint can reflect enzyme preferences or protected binding, and is not a direct identification of the occupying [transcription factor](../../../../../transcription-factor.md). Compare [biological replicates](../../../../../biological-replicate.md), enzyme-dose controls, appropriate background and [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) composition. Accessibility does not prove that an element regulates a particular [gene](../../../../../gene.md).

[nucleosome mapping by MNase-seq](../../../../../nucleosome-mapping-by-mnase-seq.md) addresses occupancy and spacing more directly: partial micrococcal-nuclease digestion preferentially removes exposed [linker DNA](../../../../../linker-dna.md), and sequencing protected fragments maps nucleosome-associated regions. Digestion titration is essential because over-digestion can preferentially lose fragile [nucleosomes](../../../../../nucleosome.md) and create apparent depletion; MNase also has sequence preferences and can recover other protected particles. Compare fragment sizes, [histone](../../../../../histone.md) enrichment and an independent accessibility assay. Chemical mapping or defined in vitro reconstitution can refine particular [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) positions, while single-molecule measurements can expose heterogeneity hidden in a population average.

Chemical states require different assays. [histone acetylation](../../../../../histone-acetylation.md) and methylation at particular residues can correlate with distinct regulatory states; they are not interchangeable with physical accessibility. [chromatin immunoprecipitation](../../../../../chromatin-immunoprecipitation.md) followed by sequencing, or targeted [CUT&RUN](../../../../../cut-and-run.md), maps association of a [histone modification](../../../../../histone-modification.md) or [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) with genomic regions. [antibody](../../../../../antibody.md) specificity, [histone](../../../../../histone.md) abundance, input/background and calibrated normalization matter, especially when the modification changes globally. In [CUT&RUN](../../../../../cut-and-run.md), an [antibody](../../../../../antibody.md) recruits a nuclease to release nearby [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), reducing some backgrounds associated with bulk [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) fragmentation.[Principle and validation of targeted chromatin cleavage](../../../../../an-efficient-targeted-nuclease-strategy-for-high-resolution-mapping-of-dna-binding-sites-by-peter-j-skene-and-steven-henikoff.md) [DNA methylation](../../../../../dna-methylation.md) can be measured by appropriately controlled bisulfite-based or direct-read approaches; standard bisulfite measurements do not automatically distinguish every modified cytosine species. These assays locate a chemical mark, not the geometric folding of the fibre.

At the level of [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) arrays, regular compact fibres can form in some reconstituted or specialized conditions. A universal hierarchy in which every interphase chromosome passes through a uniform 30-nm fibre is not supported. [electron tomography](../../../../../electron-tomography.md) and related imaging can examine the distribution and packing of [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) directly. [ChromEMT](../../../../../chromemt.md) combined DNA-selective staining with [electron tomography](../../../../../electron-tomography.md) and revealed irregular [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) chains and variable compaction in [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md).[In situ chromatin ultrastructure and compaction](../../../../../chromemt-visualizing-3d-chromatin-structure-and-compaction-in-interphase-and-mitotic-cells-by-horng-d-ou-and-others.md) Fixation, staining, sectioning and limited sampled volumes must be considered; cryogenic and super-resolution approaches provide complementary views. Dense staining also need not imply a distinct biochemical [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) state.

Longer-range folding produces loops and domains of enhanced contact. [chromosome conformation capture](../../../../../chromosome-conformation-capture.md) crosslinks, digests and ligates spatially associated fragments; targeted variants test selected loci, whereas [Hi-C](../../../../../hi-c.md) samples contacts genome-wide. Contact matrices reveal local domains and broad A/B compartmentalization. High-resolution in situ [Hi-C](../../../../../hi-c.md) mapped [chromatin loops](../../../../../chromatin-loop.md) and their association with [CTCF](../../../../../ctcf.md) orientation and [cohesin](../../../../../cohesin.md).[In situ contact maps, loop anchors and domain structure](../../../../../a-3d-map-of-the-human-genome-at-kilobase-resolution-reveals-principles-of-chromatin-looping-by-suhas-s-p-rao-and-others.md) A [topologically associating domain](../../../../../topologically-associating-domain.md) is a region with preferential internal contacts under a stated analysis, not necessarily a stable, sharply bounded object in every [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Contact frequency is neither an absolute distance nor proof that two sites directly interact. Restriction-site distribution, crosslinking, ligation, coverage and bin size affect resolution and interpretation.

Perturbation can distinguish mechanisms underlying similar-looking domains. Acute [cohesin](../../../../../cohesin.md) loss eliminated loop domains while compartment organization persisted, showing that local loop organization and large-scale compartmental segregation are separable.[Acute depletion, loss of loops and persistence of compartments](../../../../../cohesin-loss-eliminates-all-loop-domains-by-suhas-s-p-rao-and-others.md) Imaging by [DNA fluorescence in situ hybridization](../../../../../dna-fluorescence-in-situ-hybridization.md) can measure distance distributions between chosen loci and test heterogeneity; live labels track dynamics but can perturb loci. Agreement between contact enrichment and a shift in distance distributions is stronger than either assay alone, although the observables still differ.

At nuclear scale, chromosomes occupy territories and regions associate preferentially with the [nuclear lamina](../../../../../nuclear-lamina.md) or [nucleolus](../../../../../nucleolus.md). [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) imaging maps territories and radial positions, while lamina-association assays such as [DamID](../../../../../damid.md) map sequences near a labelled nuclear compartment over an integration interval. The original [DamID](../../../../../damid.md) approach tethered a [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) adenine methyltransferase to a [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) to record nearby genomic regions.[Targeted methylation as a proximity record](../../../../../identification-of-in-vivo-dna-targets-of-chromatin-proteins-using-tethered-dam-methyltransferase-by-bas-van-steensel-and-steven-henikoff.md) Such maps record proximity and history rather than a single fixed structure. Mitotic condensation is a distinct organization with additional constraints and chromosome-shaping machinery, not simply maximally closed interphase [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md). **The appropriate pairing is exposure assays for local access, protected-fragment assays for [nucleosomes](../../../../../nucleosome.md), [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md)/mark profiling for composition, contact assays for genomic proximity and imaging for spatial organization, with perturbations to connect organization to function.**
