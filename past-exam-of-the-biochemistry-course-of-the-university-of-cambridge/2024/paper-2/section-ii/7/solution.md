**[ribosome profiling](../../../../../ribosome-profiling.md) turned initiation from a small set of reporter-based examples into a genome-wide, condition-dependent map, while specialized variants extended observation from translating [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md) to start-site selection and scanning.** The central distinction is that ordinary footprints locate occupied [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md); they do not directly label every initiation event or measure the movement of a scanning small subunit.

In a typical experiment, rapidly preserve [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) complexes, digest exposed [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), isolate [ribosome-protected fragments](../../../../../ribosome-protected-fragment.md) and sequence them alongside matched total [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md). Assign each footprint to a ribosomal position using length-specific offsets calibrated on established coding regions. Enrichment over coding sequences and [three-nucleotide periodicity](../../../../../three-nucleotide-periodicity.md) support active elongation. The initial yeast work demonstrated genome-wide [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) measurements and widespread regulation not inferable from [mRNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) abundance alone.[Footprint method and translational responses in yeast](../../../../../genome-wide-analysis-in-vivo-of-translation-with-nucleotide-resolution-using-ribosome-profiling-by-nicholas-t-ingolia-and-others.md) Counts normalized to transcript abundance provide an operational translation-efficiency measure,

$$
\mathrm{TE}_i\propto\frac{\text{normalized coding-region footprints}_i}{\text{normalized mRNA abundance}_i}.
$$

For treatment comparisons, a count model testing the interaction between assay type and treatment is preferable to unstable ratios at low counts. More occupancy can result from increased initiation or slower passage, so changes in elongation, premature termination and global normalization must be considered before assigning a TE change specifically to initiation.

A major advance was recognition of pervasive translated [upstream open reading frames](../../../../../upstream-open-reading-frame.md) and alternative starts. Mammalian stem-cell profiling found many leader-region [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) events, including near-cognate starts, and alternative coding products.[Mammalian footprints, leader translation and alternative initiation](../../../../../ribosome-profiling-of-mouse-embryonic-stem-cells-reveals-the-complexity-and-dynamics-of-mammalian-proteomes-by-nicholas-t-ingolia-and-others.md) Initiation upstream of an annotated start can add an N-terminal extension; downstream in-frame initiation can remove targeting or regulatory sequences, while another frame creates a different product. This exposed a coding and regulatory landscape missed by annotation based only on a single longest [open reading frame](../../../../../open-reading-frame.md). A footprint-supported short [ORF](../../../../../open-reading-frame.md) is not automatically a stable functional microprotein: start-codon [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md), peptide evidence and [phenotype](../../../../../phenotype.md) are separate tests.

Ordinary elongation profiles often make the exact starting site ambiguous. Brief [harringtonine](../../../../../harringtonine.md) treatment permits much ongoing elongation to run off while newly initiating [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md) accumulate near starts, generating start-associated peaks. [lactimidomycin](../../../../../lactimidomycin.md)-based initiation profiling preferentially enriches early [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md) and, when compared with an elongation-inhibitor profile, improves discrimination of initiation sites. [Sooncheol Lee](../../../../../sooncheol-lee.md) and colleagues used this strategy to map alternative mammalian start sites at high resolution.[Parallel initiation- and elongation-inhibitor profiling](../../../../../global-mapping-of-translation-initiation-sites-in-mammalian-cells-at-single-nucleotide-resolution-by-sooncheol-lee-and-others.md) These approaches reveal that the first AUG is not invariably chosen: local sequence context, near-cognate [codons](../../../../../codon.md) and competing upstream sites influence selection. However, drug uptake, exposure time and sequence-dependent trapping can change the observed distribution. A high peak can reflect capture efficiency or dwell time rather than a proportionate initiation flux, so compare time courses, untreated profiles and reporters with mutated candidate starts.

The regulatory significance of upstream [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) is particularly clear during stress. In the [GCN4](../../../../../gcn4.md) leader, [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md) of a short upstream frame can be followed by retention of a scanning small subunit. Reacquisition of the eIF2–GTP–initiator-tRNA ternary complex determines whether it reinitiates at an inhibitory upstream frame or proceeds to the main coding region. Amino-acid limitation lowers ternary-complex availability and changes that competition. Profiling measures occupancy on upstream and main regions simultaneously, testing redistribution across endogenous transcripts rather than relying only on reporter output. The reinitiation mechanism predates profiling; the advance is transcriptome-wide evidence for its deployment and condition dependence. Similarly, changes in cap-dependent recruitment can be mapped by perturbing [mTORC1](../../../../../mtorc1.md) signalling: profiling identified selectively regulated transcript programmes rather than a uniform effect on every [mRNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md).[mTOR perturbation and selective translational programmes](../../../../../the-translational-landscape-of-mtor-signalling-steers-cancer-initiation-and-metastasis-by-andrew-c-hsieh-and-others.md)

An important limit is that standard 80S footprints do not directly demonstrate scanning. [translation complex profiling](../../../../../translation-complex-profiling.md) addresses this by preserving and separately profiling small-subunit-associated and 80S complexes. In yeast, [Stuart K. Archer](../../../../../stuart-k-archer.md) and colleagues mapped small-subunit occupancy along leaders and around initiation and recycling transitions, providing evidence for otherwise difficult-to-trap intermediates.[Small-subunit and ribosome profiling across the translation cycle](../../../../../dynamics-of-ribosome-scanning-and-recycling-revealed-by-translation-complex-profiling-by-stuart-k-archer-and-others.md) This makes different predictions from a model that treats every leader footprint as elongation. Nevertheless, the measurement is a distribution of captured complexes, not a movie of one subunit; crosslinking and footprint-length choices can affect which states are recovered.

Validation is therefore integral to the mechanistic conclusions. Use rapid, consistent harvesting, [biological replicates](../../../../../biological-replicate.md), multiple footprint lengths and matched RNA-seq; inspect rRNA contamination, mapping ambiguity and frame periodicity. Test start-site edits at the endogenous locus or in reporters preserving the relevant leader. Measure the predicted N termini or [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) isoforms where possible, and use run-off or independent synthesis measurements to separate initiation changes from altered elongation. A structured [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) or another protected ribonucleoprotein can also produce a short sequencing fragment, especially in a very short proposed [ORF](../../../../../open-reading-frame.md). [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) mixtures and transcript-isoform changes can create apparent alternative initiation, so transcript architecture must be resolved. **Profiling has revealed the extent and plasticity of initiation-site choice and upstream [biological translation](../../../../../translation-biology.md); perturbations and specialized capture methods are needed to turn an occupancy map into an initiation mechanism.**
