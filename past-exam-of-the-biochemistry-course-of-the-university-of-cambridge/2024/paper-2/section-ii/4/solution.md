↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**Shared preference for pyrimidine-rich [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) does not prescribe one splicing outcome: position, multivalent binding and interactions with the [spliceosome](../../../../../spliceosome.md) determine the regulated step.** [Sex-lethal](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) acts in the Drosophila sex-determination programme, whereas [PTBP1](../../../../../ptbp1.md) regulates many mammalian precursor RNAs in a cell-type-dependent manner. Their effects must be interpreted against competing [splice sites](../../../../../splice-site.md) and the [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) normally recognizing them.

During early [spliceosome](../../../../../spliceosome.md) assembly, [U1 snRNP](../../../../../u1-snrnp.md) recognizes a 5′ [splice site](../../../../../splice-site.md), while factors including [U2AF](../../../../../u2af.md) help recognize a [polypyrimidine tract](../../../../../polypyrimidine-tract.md) and 3′ splice-site region and promote assembly around the branch point. Alternative sites compete within this context. Binding of another [RNA-binding protein](../../../../../rna-binding-protein.md) can occlude an essential interaction, organize a repressive complex or favour use of a competing site; recognition need not be determined solely by an isolated site's consensus strength.

A particularly clear [SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) mechanism operates on precursor [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) encoding [Transformer protein](../../../../../transformer-protein.md). In the absence of [SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md), [U2AF](../../../../../u2af.md) recognizes a strong default 3′ site, producing an unproductive transcript. In female [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), [SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) binds the pyrimidine-rich region at that site and competes with [U2AF](../../../../../u2af.md). The weaker alternative female site can then be used, producing functional [Transformer protein](../../../../../transformer-protein.md). Reconstituted splicing experiments showed the antagonism, and adding a [U2AF](../../../../../u2af.md) effector domain to [SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) converted its activity from repression toward activation.[In vitro splice-site switch and effector-domain fusion](../../../../../the-protein-sex-lethal-antagonizes-the-splicing-factor-u2af-to-regulate-alternative-splicing-of-transformer-pre-mrna-by-juan-valcarcel-and-others.md) The domain-fusion result is important: related [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) recognition can direct different outcomes because the [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) recruit different downstream activities. [SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) need not directly activate the female acceptor to shift usage toward it; repression of the stronger competitor is sufficient in this substrate.

[SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) also maintains its own productive expression through exclusion of a male-specific [exon](../../../../../exon.md) containing a premature stop. This provides positive feedback in female [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). It is not adequately described as another instance of simple [U2AF](../../../../../u2af.md) displacement. Work on the [SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) autoregulatory substrate identified a specialized arrangement of AG dinucleotides and a pyrimidine tract: [U2AF](../../../../../u2af.md) and [SPF45](../../../../../spf45.md) recognize different features, and interaction with [SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) inhibits SPF45-dependent progression at the second catalytic step.[Autoregulatory exon 3 and second-step inhibition](../../../../../splicing-regulation-at-the-second-catalytic-step-by-sex-lethal-involves-3%E2%80%B2-splice-site-recognition-by-spf45-by-maria-jose-lallena-and-others.md) [SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) can consequently alter site selection after early assembly has occurred. Accumulation of a first-step intermediate in an appropriate biochemical assay distinguishes such a block from failure to recruit the [spliceosome](../../../../../spliceosome.md) initially. The same regulator can use different mechanisms on different RNAs.

[PTBP1](../../../../../ptbp1.md) has four [RNA recognition motifs](../../../../../rna-recognition-motif.md) and commonly binds CU-rich sequences at more than one position. Structural work showed distinct RNA-recognition arrangements and an organization of its third and fourth domains compatible with bridging separated [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) segments.[RNA-bound domain structures and implications for recognition](../../../../../structure-of-ptb-bound-to-rna-specific-binding-and-implications-for-splicing-regulation-by-florian-c-oberstrass-and-others.md) [Förster resonance energy transfer](../../../../../forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md), [NMR](../../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) and splicing assays subsequently supported [RNA looping](../../../../../rna-looping.md) by these domains: contacts on either side of a segment can organize an [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) geometry that disfavors [exon](../../../../../exon.md) use.[Looping measurements and functional tests](../../../../../rna-looping-by-ptb-evidence-using-fret-and-nmr-spectroscopy-for-a-role-in-splicing-repression-by-rajan-lamichhane-and-others.md) These observations support a physical repression mechanism, but a short model [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) loop does not prove that every endogenous PTBP1-regulated [exon](../../../../../exon.md) is looped out.

Other [PTBP1](../../../../../ptbp1.md) arrangements can interfere with early assembly or communication between sites without directly replacing [U2AF](../../../../../u2af.md) at the same tract. Binding near or within an alternative [exon](../../../../../exon.md) can impair its definition, whereas in another arrangement [PTBP1](../../../../../ptbp1.md) can favour [exon](../../../../../exon.md) inclusion, including by suppressing a competing event. Genome-wide perturbation and positional analyses revealed both activation and repression associated with different binding contexts.[Global splicing responses and position-dependent activity](../../../../../position-dependent-alternative-splicing-activity-revealed-by-global-profiling-of-alternative-splicing-events-regulated-by-ptb-by-miriam-llorian-and-others.md) Thus neither “all pyrimidine binders are repressors” nor “[PTBP1](../../../../../ptbp1.md) always causes skipping” is a valid general rule. [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) abundance, [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) architecture, neighbouring factors and related PTBP family members change the effective regulatory complex.

To discriminate mechanisms, map direct contacts by [crosslinking and immunoprecipitation](../../../../../crosslinking-and-immunoprecipitation.md), measure exon-junction usage after acute depletion, and rescue with wild-type or selected RNA-binding/interface mutants. Use a [minigene splicing reporter](../../../../../minigene-splicing-reporter.md) to mutate individual tracts, change their spacing or move them relative to a regulated [exon](../../../../../exon.md), then confirm important effects at the endogenous locus. Assays of [U2AF](../../../../../u2af.md) or [U1 snRNP](../../../../../u1-snrnp.md) association and native spliceosomal assembly can reveal an early block; time-resolved splicing-intermediate analysis can reveal a late one. Control reporter [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) and precursor abundance, and account for [nonsense-mediated decay](../../../../../nonsense-mediated-decay.md), because an unstable mature isoform can conceal the splice choice that generated it. Mutants must retain expression and the intended remaining contacts. **[SXL](../../../../../sex-lethal.md) supplies examples of both competition and late catalytic control; [PTBP1](../../../../../ptbp1.md) illustrates multivalent, context-dependent organization of splice-site recognition. [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) affinity is only one part of specificity.**
