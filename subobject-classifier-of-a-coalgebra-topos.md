# Subobject classifier of a coalgebra topos

↑ **Parent:** [Category of coalgebras for a comonad](category-of-coalgebras-for-a-comonad.md)

Let $\kappa:G\Omega\to\Omega$ classify $G\top:1\hookrightarrow G\Omega$. In the [cofree coalgebra](cofree-coalgebra.md) $R\Omega$, equalize the identity and $G\kappa\,\delta_\Omega$. A map $\chi:X\to\Omega$ classifies a subcoalgebra exactly when $\chi=\kappa G\chi\,x$, since this says the subobject equals the inverse image of its image under $G$. Transposition gives precisely the displayed equalizer condition.
