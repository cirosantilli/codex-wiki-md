# Supersymmetry generator

↑ **Parent:** [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md)

A supersymmetry generator is an odd [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) generator of the [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md). Acting on a physical state reverses [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) while preserving its [four-momentum](four-momentum.md).
