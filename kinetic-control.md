# Kinetic control

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md)

Under [kinetic control](kinetic-control.md), the observed product distribution reflects competing formation rates rather than an equilibrated distribution. Heating may speed access to a lower-[Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) product, but does not itself prove that the equilibrium favors it.
