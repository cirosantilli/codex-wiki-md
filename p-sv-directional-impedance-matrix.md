# P-SV directional impedance matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic-wave energy flux](elastic-wave-energy-flux.md)

For real [frequency](frequency.md) $\omega>0$ and both vertical wavenumbers $p=k_\alpha>0$, $s=k_\beta>0$, let $D=k^2+ps$, $A=\rho\omega p/D$, $B=\rho\omega s/D$, and $C=k(2\mu D-\rho\omega^2)/(\omega D)$. With [traction](traction.md) $\mathbf t=(\sigma_{xz},\sigma_{zz})$ and particle [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf v=(v_x,v_z)$, each directional [P wave](p-wave.md)/[SV-wave](sv-wave.md) field obeys $\mathbf t=-Z_\pm\mathbf v$. Direct displacement-potential substitution yields $Z_-=-Z_+^\dagger$. The [Hermitian part](hermitian-part-of-a-matrix.md) of $Z_+$ is positive diagonal; thus forward/backward fluxes have opposite signs and mixed directional flux cross terms cancel.
