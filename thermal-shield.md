# Thermal shield

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal insulation](thermal-insulation.md)

A [thermal shield](thermal-shield.md) reduces coupling to unwanted environmental [temperature](temperature-split.md) changes. Low-conductance supports and [thermal insulation](thermal-insulation.md) reduce heat transport, low-emissivity surfaces reduce [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md), and a conducting inner enclosure can reduce [temperature](temperature-split.md) gradients. Combined with [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) these give a thermal low-pass response. Passive shielding cannot indefinitely reject a slow change in the entire environment and may increase the temperature rise from internal dissipation; active regulation addresses a different [frequency](frequency.md) range.
