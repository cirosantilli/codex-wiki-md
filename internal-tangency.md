# Internal tangency

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent circles](tangent-circles.md)

Two distinct positive-[radius](radius.md) [circles](circle.md) have internal tangency when their boundaries meet at exactly one point and one [disk](disk-mathematics.md) contains the other. Their centre separation equals the absolute difference of their [radii](radius.md).
