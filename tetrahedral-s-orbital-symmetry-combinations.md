# Tetrahedral s-orbital symmetry combinations

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry-adapted linear combination](symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md)

Four equivalent $s$ [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) at the vertices of a regular [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md) carry the [tetrahedral symmetry](tetrahedral-symmetry.md) representation $A_1\oplus T_2$. The normalized equal sum is $A_1$; three coordinate-sign combinations span $T_2$. Every normalized coefficient vector has entries $\pm1/2$ in a suitable orthonormal real basis. These functions simplify a [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) invariant under the tetrahedral operations, but equivalent symmetry copies on other sites may still mix.
