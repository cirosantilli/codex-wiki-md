# Polynomial ring of level-one modular forms

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of level-one modular forms](dimension-of-level-one-modular-forms.md)

Subtract the constant term of a [modular form](modular-form.md) times a same-weight monomial in $E_4,E_6$. The remainder is a [cusp form](cusp-form.md), hence divisible by the [modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md). Induction on weight and $1728\Delta=E_4^3-E_6^2$ express every form as a weighted homogeneous polynomial in these two [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md). The number of monomials of a given weight equals the [dimension of level-one modular forms](dimension-of-level-one-modular-forms.md), so they form a basis and the graded ring has no polynomial relation.
