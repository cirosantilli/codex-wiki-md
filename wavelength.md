# Wavelength

↑ **Parent:** [Wavenumber](wavenumber.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavelength)

The wavelength is the spatial period of a monochromatic wave. For angular [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$, it is $\lambda=2\pi/|k|$.
