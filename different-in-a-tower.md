# Different in a tower

↑ **Parent:** [Different ideal](different-ideal.md)

For finite separable extensions $L/E/K$,

$$
\mathfrak D_{L/K}
=\mathfrak D_{L/E}\mathfrak D_{E/K}\mathcal O_L.
$$

For normalized valuations this says

$$
d(L/K)=d(L/E)+e(L/E)d(E/K).
$$
