# Invariant manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invariant_manifold)

An invariant manifold is a [manifold](topological-manifold.md) preserved by a [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md): a trajectory beginning on it stays on it for as long as that trajectory is defined. The [stable manifold](stable-manifold.md) and [unstable manifold](unstable-manifold.md) of a [hyperbolic equilibrium](hyperbolic-equilibrium-point.md) are examples.
