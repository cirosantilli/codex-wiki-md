# Henkin construction

↑ **Parent:** [Gödel's completeness theorem](godel-s-completeness-theorem.md)

A Henkin construction enlarges a consistent [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) by fresh constants, decides sentences while preserving consistency, and supplies constant witnesses for existential sentences. Countably many additional dense requirements can be interleaved, as in the [omitting types theorem](omitting-types-theorem.md). A complete consistent extension with witnesses has a [term model](term-model.md).
