↑ **Parent:** [Mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md)

[co-fractionation mass spectrometry](co-fractionation-mass-spectrometry.md) quantifies [proteins](protein-split.md) across fractions from a native separation. Similar elution profiles suggest shared assemblies; coincident migration and assemblies that exchange partners are alternative explanations.
