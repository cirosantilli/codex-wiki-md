<h1 id="self-consistency-of-kaplan-meier-estimation">Self-consistency of Kaplan–Meier estimation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md)

The [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md) is self-consistent under fractional allocation of censored event times according to its own conditional fitted event distribution. Adding the corresponding fractional event counts and [risk set](risk-set.md) weights leaves the estimator unchanged.
