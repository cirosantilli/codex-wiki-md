# Jeans modes of a four-thirds polytropic cosmological fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Linear density modes with polytropic pressure](linear-density-modes-with-polytropic-pressure.md)

For a nonrelativistic [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md) $P=K\rho^{4/3}$ during [matter domination](matter-domination.md), $c_s^2\propto a^{-1}$ and the comoving [Jeans wavenumber](jeans-wavenumber.md) is constant. The leading density equation becomes $\ddot\delta+4\dot\delta/(3t)+[2(q-1)/(3t^2)]\delta=0$, giving the displayed powers. A growing mode exists for $q<1$; at $q=1$ one mode is constant. At $q=25/24$ the repeated root has the second solution $t^{-1/6}\ln t$. For $q>25/24$ both real solutions oscillate in $\ln t$ with envelope $t^{-1/6}$. Thus the instability threshold and the onset of oscillatory solutions are distinct in an expanding background.
