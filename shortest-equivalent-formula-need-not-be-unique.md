# Shortest equivalent formula need not be unique

↑ **Parent:** [Logical equivalence](logical-equivalence.md)

Lengths of formulas [logically equivalent](logical-equivalence.md) to a fixed [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) form a nonempty subset of the [natural numbers](natural-number.md), so a minimum exists. With variables counted as atomic symbols and no truth constants, $(p\to p)$ and $(p\leftrightarrow p)$ are distinct shortest [tautologies](tautology.md). A formula with no binary connective is a variable preceded by negations and cannot be a tautology. Thus minimization need not give a canonical representative.
