# Cutoff frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Linear filter](linear-filter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cutoff_frequency)

A chosen boundary in a [frequency response](frequency-response.md), commonly where the voltage [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) gain falls to $1/\sqrt2$ of its pass-region value. The convention must specify the reference gain.
