# OPERA experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OPERA_experiment)

A long-baseline tau-appearance experiment with the CERN-to-Gran-Sasso [muon neutrino](muon-neutrino.md) beam. Lead targets and nuclear emulsion resolve a short [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) decay after a [tau neutrino](tau-neutrino.md) [charged current](charged-current.md) interaction.
