<h1 id="gell-mann-nishijima-formula">Gell-Mann--Nishijima formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor hypercharge](flavor-hypercharge.md)

For the light [quark](quark.md) flavors, [electric charge](electric-charge.md) is $Q=I_3+Y/2$, with [flavor hypercharge](flavor-hypercharge.md) $Y=B+S$. On $(u,d,s)$ it gives $(2/3,-1/3,-1/3)$, and additivity extends it to the [baryon octet](baryon-octet.md) and [meson octet](meson-octet.md).
