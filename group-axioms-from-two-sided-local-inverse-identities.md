# Group axioms from two-sided local inverse identities

↑ **Parent:** [Group axioms](group-axioms.md)

Suppose a nonempty [semigroup](semigroup.md) has, for each $a$, elements $l_a,r_a$ satisfying $bl_aa=b$ and $ar_ab=b$ for every $b$. Then it is a [group](group-split.md). For any fixed $a$, the element $e_R=l_aa$ is a right [identity element](identity-element.md), and $e_L=ar_a$ is a left [identity element](identity-element.md). Since $e_Le_R=e_L=e_R$, they coincide in a two-sided identity $e$. For each $a$, putting $b=e$ in the two assumed identities gives $l_aa=e$ and $ar_a=e$. [Associativity](associative-property.md) then yields $l_a=l_ae=l_a(ar_a)=(l_aa)r_a=er_a=r_a$, a two-sided [inverse element](inverse-element.md). Thus the usual [group axioms](group-axioms.md) follow without an assumed identity or cancellation law.
