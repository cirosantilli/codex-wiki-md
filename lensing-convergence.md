# Lensing convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Weak gravitational lensing](weak-gravitational-lensing.md)

The trace of the angular derivative of the reduced deflection defines convergence in the standard [thin gravitational lens equation](thin-gravitational-lens-equation.md) convention. It equals projected [surface mass density](projected-surface-mass-density.md) divided by the critical surface density, $\Sigma_{\rm crit}=c^2D_s/(4\pi GD_dD_{ds})$. If deflection is instead defined as source minus image angle, the divergence relation has a minus sign.
