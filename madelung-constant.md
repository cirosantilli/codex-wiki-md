# Madelung constant

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatic model of ionic binding](electrostatic-model-of-ionic-binding.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madelung_constant)

The [Madelung constant](madelung-constant.md), named for [Erwin Madelung](erwin-madelung.md), is a dimensionless geometrical factor in the electrostatic binding of an [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md). For a neutral lattice of [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) $\pm e$ with nearest unlike-ion distance $r_0$, [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md) gives the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) per ion pair as $-\alpha_M e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0r_0)$. At a positive reference site, $\alpha_M=-\sum_{j\ne0}z_jr_0/r_j$, with neighboring [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) $z_je$. Both attraction and repulsion enter this lattice sum; summing with a neutral-bulk prescription is essential. Short-range overlap repulsion and other contributions must be added before inferring a stable spacing or a thermodynamic [lattice energy](lattice-energy.md).
