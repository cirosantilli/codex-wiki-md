# Between-study heterogeneity

↑ **Parent:** [Random-effects meta-analysis](random-effects-meta-analysis.md)

Variation in the underlying study effects, additional to sampling error. The usual normal [random-effects meta-analysis](random-effects-meta-analysis.md) models it by a between-study [variance](variance-split.md) $\tau^2$. It can reflect effect modification, design differences or [within-study bias](within-study-bias.md); it is not automatically a biological treatment difference.
