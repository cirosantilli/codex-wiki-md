# Critical freshwater forcing in a two-box overturning model

↑ **Parent:** [Two-box overturning model](two-box-overturning-model.md)

For $q|q|-A|q|+BF=0$ with positive $A,B$, the two positive roots $q_\pm=(A\pm\sqrt{A^2-4BF})/2$ merge at the displayed [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md). The negative root $q_{\rm rev}=(A-\sqrt{A^2+4BF})/2$ exists for every positive $F$. Under standard fixed-temperature salt-contrast dynamics, the stronger positive and reversed branches are stable, while the weaker positive branch is unstable. A linear ramp $F=F_0(1+st)$ reaches the fold after $(F_c/F_0-1)/s$, but the equilibrium flow changes before that time; a specified closeness tolerance generally gives an earlier limit.
