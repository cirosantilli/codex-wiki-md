# Gromov compactness for spheres

↑ **Parent:** [J-holomorphic sphere](j-holomorphic-sphere.md)

For a compact [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md), smooth convergence of tame [almost complex structures](almost-complex-manifold.md) and a uniform area bound give a subsequence of [J-holomorphic spheres](j-holomorphic-sphere.md) converging, after reparametrization, to a finite tree of [J-holomorphic spheres](j-holomorphic-sphere.md), with convergence smooth away from finitely many bubbling points. The total [homology class](homology-class.md) and [symplectic area](symplectic-area.md) are preserved. Constant components may be added for stability. In a spherical class which cannot split into two positive-area spherical classes, there is only one nonconstant component.
