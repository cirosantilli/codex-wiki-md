# Einstein-de Sitter photon flight time

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md)

For an earlier emission epoch $z_1>z_2$, the matter-only flat model gives $R\propto t^{2/3}$ and $t(z)=2(1+z)^{-3/2}/(3H_0)$. Subtraction gives the displayed elapsed [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md). It is not the comoving travel [distance](distance.md) divided by $c$, because expansion changes the spatial scale during flight. An Einstein-de Sitter approximation fixed to the same $H_0$ differs from a matter-era approximation normalized to $H_0\sqrt{\Omega_{m0}}$.
