# Taper extinction experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Combustion](combustion.md)

A controlled test in which a taper is exposed to a specified [gas mixture](gas-mixture.md) to determine whether its [candle flame](candle-flame.md) persists under a fixed protocol. The result depends on composition, flow, time, taper and thermal conditions; it is not a general test of whether the gas can safely be breathed.
