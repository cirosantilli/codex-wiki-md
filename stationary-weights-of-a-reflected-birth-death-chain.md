# Stationary weights of a reflected birth-death chain

↑ **Parent:** [Birth-death chain](birth-death-chain.md)

Consider an [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md) on the nonnegative integers with up-step probabilities $u_k>0$ and down-step probabilities $d_k>0$ for $k\geq1$, reflected at zero. The displayed weights satisfy [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md). Every [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) is proportional to them: the stationary equation at zero sets the edge flow $\pi_0u_0-\pi_1d_1$ to zero, and the remaining stationary equations make the flow constant along all edges. Hence [positive recurrence](positive-recurrent-markov-chain.md) is equivalent to $\sum_kw_k<\infty$, and then $\pi_k=w_k/\sum_jw_j$. This also allows [holding probabilities of a Markov chain](holding-probability-of-a-markov-chain.md).
