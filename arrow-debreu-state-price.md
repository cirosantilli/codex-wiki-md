<h1 id="arrow-debreu-state-price">Arrow–Debreu state price</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [State-price density](state-price-density.md)

On a finite state space, the Arrow–Debreu state price is the current price of a payoff equal to one in state $s$ and zero elsewhere. It equals the physical state probability times the [state-price density](state-price-density.md) in that state. With a riskless asset of deterministic prices $B_0,B_1$, the [Arrow state prices](arrow-debreu-state-price.md) sum to $B_0/B_1$. Normalizing them gives the [risk-neutral probabilities](risk-neutral-probability.md), while division by the physical probabilities instead recovers the pricing density.
