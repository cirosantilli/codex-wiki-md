# Optical Activity Laboratory, Truman State University

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry bibliography](chemistry-bibliography-split.md)

Laboratory handout hosted by Truman State University. The first page gives the laboratory attribution AEMoody and the revision July 2013. The attribution is consistent with [Anne Erwin Moody](anne-erwin-moody.md) in the institutional context, but is not an explicit full-name author byline. The attached apparatus description on p. 3 explicitly credits an adaptation from I2R literature; no original instrument-design authorship is assigned to Moody or Truman.

Online text: [https://chemlab.truman.edu/files/2015/07/Optical-Activity-Lab-2013.pdf](https://chemlab.truman.edu/files/2015/07/Optical-Activity-Lab-2013.pdf)

Cited passages: p. 1: title, Revised July 2013, Organic Chemistry Lab: AEMoody; pp. 1–2, Experiment 1: preparation, actual final volume, water blank extinction, sample extinction, corrected rotations and concentration plot; p. 2, Experiment 2: varying pathlength and corrected-rotation plot; p. 3, The Design and General Use of Simple Polarimeters: I2R adaptation, vertical path, yellow-filter incandescent/tungsten light, sheet polarizers, extinction eyepiece, coarse temperature/pathlength/angle tolerances.
