# Phase-contrast microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Optical microscope](optical-microscope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase-contrast_microscopy)

[Phase-contrast microscopy](phase-contrast-microscopy.md) converts specimen-dependent [phase differences](phase-difference.md) into visible [light intensity](light-intensity.md) differences. It can reveal structures in living, unstained [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md), allowing their changes to be followed without first making an absorbing preparation.[Opening description of the phase-contrast microscope and biological motivation](phase-contrast-microscope-by-university-of-groningen.md) The key question is how an [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) can read a [phase](phase-waves.md) when an ordinary detector records [light intensity](light-intensity.md).

Consider a transparent specimen with [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) transmission $t(x,y)=e^{i\phi(x,y)}$. In ideal in-focus [coherent imaging](coherent-imaging.md) with all relevant [spatial frequencies](spatial-frequency.md) transmitted, its image has $I=|t|^2=1$: varying $\phi$ alone produces no brightness detail. This is a particular model, not a claim that all transparent specimens are invisible. [Diffraction](diffraction.md), defocus, [absorption](absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) and a finite [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) can already change a real image.

A [phase-contrast microscope](phase-contrast-microscope.md) images an illuminating annulus onto a [phase plate](phase-plate.md) ring near the objective's rear [focal plane](focal-plane.md). The aligned ring attenuates and shifts direct light relative to most diffracted light; their [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) changes image brightness.[Figure 1; Phase Plates and Phase Annuli](introduction-to-phase-contrast-microscopy-by-nikon-instruments-inc.md)

**[Figure 9](#image-separating-the-reference-light-before-recombining-the-image). Separating the reference light before recombining the image**. Original functional diagram of [phase-contrast microscopy](phase-contrast-microscopy.md). The annulus and ring indicate their optical correspondence, not an exact ray prescription. The lower [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) construction uses the original [weak phase object](weak-phase-object.md) model below, with chosen positive specimen [phase](phase-waves.md) and two opposite quarter-cycle reference shifts.

The method is beautiful because the bright direct beam becomes its own reference: a small specimen-dependent [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) can interfere with it and change the image at first order. Merely magnifying an invisible [phase](phase-waves.md) pattern would leave $|t|^2$ unchanged.
