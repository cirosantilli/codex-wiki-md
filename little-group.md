# Little group

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré group](poincare-group.md)

The little group of a momentum $p$ is its stabilizer in the Lorentz group. Its [unitary irreducible representations](unitary-irreducible-representation.md) label the internal degrees of freedom of one-particle states on the orbit of $p$.

These stabilizer representations are used in [Wigner's classification](wigner-s-classification.md) of relativistic particle states.
