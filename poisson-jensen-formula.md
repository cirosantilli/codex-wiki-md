# Poisson-Jensen formula

↑ **Parent:** [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md)

For a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) with no zero or [pole](pole.md) on $|w|=R$, set $G_R(z,a)=\log|(R^2-\overline az)/(R(z-a))|$. At source points that are neither zeros nor [poles](pole.md), the displayed identity expresses its logarithmic modulus as the Poisson integral of its boundary values, minus its zero Green potentials plus its [pole](pole.md) Green potentials. The Poisson kernel is $(R^2-|z|^2)/|Re^{i\theta}-z|^2$ with normalized angular measure. Subtracting the corresponding logarithmic singularities leaves a harmonic function, so the harmonic Poisson formula proves the identity. At zero it reduces to [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md).
