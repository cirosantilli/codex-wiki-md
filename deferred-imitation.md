# Deferred imitation

↑ **Parent:** [Imitation](imitation.md)

Reproduction of an observed action after a delay without continuous access to its demonstration. It supplies a nonverbal [memory](memory-in-animals.md) measure, with baseline and control conditions needed to exclude actions likely to occur without the prior demonstration.
