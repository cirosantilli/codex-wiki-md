# Invariant vector of a module over a Hopf algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md)

For a left [module](module-mathematics.md) over a [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md), a vector $v$ is invariant when $hv=\varepsilon(h)v$. In the [internal hom for modules over a Hopf algebra](internal-hom-for-modules-over-a-hopf-algebra.md), invariant maps are exactly the [R-module homomorphisms](module-homomorphism.md) for the acting algebra.
