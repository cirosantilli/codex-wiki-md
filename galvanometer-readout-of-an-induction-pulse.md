# Galvanometer readout of an induction pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Galvanometer](galvanometer.md)

A small-angle [galvanometer](galvanometer.md) model combines its [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $J_g$, [damping](damping.md) coefficient $b_g$ and restoring coefficient $\kappa_g$ with a torque per [electric current](electric-current.md) $K_g$. The resulting [damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md) responds to the current pulse through the displayed equation. Its angular deflection $\theta$ can continue after the driving [electric current](electric-current.md) has ceased. Inferring the current's detailed time dependence requires the instrument response, rather than equating needle angle with instantaneous current. This is a generic readout model, not a calibration of Faraday's historical instrument.
