# Schmidt rank

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt_rank)

The Schmidt rank is the number of nonzero terms in a [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md), equivalently the rank of either [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) of a bipartite pure state.
