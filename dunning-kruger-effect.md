# Dunning-Kruger effect

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive bias](cognitive-bias.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunning–Kruger_effect)

A pattern in which low observed performers overestimate their performance, often accompanied by hypotheses about deficits in evaluating their own answers. Task skill, comparison-group beliefs, measurement noise and grouping can all affect the observed relation; the pattern alone does not identify one cause.[1999, original empirical proposal](unskilled-and-unaware-of-it-by-justin-kruger-and-david-dunning.md)
