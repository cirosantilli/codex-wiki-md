# Confining-potential energy space

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear analysis](nonlinear-analysis-split.md)

For a nonnegative potential $V(x)\to\infty$ as $|x|\to\infty$, the confining-potential energy space is

$$
\Sigma_V=\{u:\nabla u\in L^2,\ V^{1/2}u\in L^2\},
\qquad
\|u\|_{\Sigma_V}^2=\|\nabla u\|_2^2+\|V^{1/2}u\|_2^2.
$$

A local [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) controls the missing $L^2$ term, so this energy norm makes $\Sigma_V$ a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md).
