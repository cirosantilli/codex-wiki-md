# Separated reciprocal-distance sum

↑ **Parent:** [Distance to the nearest integer](distance-to-the-nearest-integer.md)

If finitely many points on $\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ are pairwise separated by [distance modulo one](distance-to-the-nearest-integer.md) at least $\delta>0$, then the displayed bound holds for $Q\ge2$. An arc of length $2r$ contains at most $1+2r/\delta$ points. First sum over $\|\theta_j\|\le Q^{-1}$, then over the dyadic annuli $2^j/Q<\|\theta_j\|\le2^{j+1}/Q$. Their contributions are $O(Q+\delta^{-1})$ and $O(Q2^{-j}+\delta^{-1})$, respectively. Summing proves the estimate. A point at zero contributes $Q$, using the convention $\min(Q,\infty)=Q$.
