# Coideal

↑ **Parent:** [Coalgebra](coalgebra.md)

A [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) $I$ of a [coalgebra](coalgebra.md) satisfying the displayed conditions. These are exactly the conditions for the [comultiplication](comultiplication.md) and [counit](counit.md) to descend to the quotient [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $C/I$. An ideal that is also a coideal in a [bialgebra](bialgebra.md) gives a quotient [bialgebra](bialgebra.md).
