# Reciprocal coordinate at infinite coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Real-space renormalization group](real-space-renormalization-group.md)

An infinite positive coupling coordinate $v$ can be studied through $w=1/v$, with $v=\infty$ represented by $w=0$. For example, $v'=v^4/A(u)$ with $A(u)>0$ becomes $w'=A(u)w^4$, a smooth map at zero. This gives a well-defined boundary [renormalization-group fixed point](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md) whose derivative in the reciprocal direction is zero. One should use this chart rather than substituting infinity into an ordinary finite-coordinate Jacobian.
