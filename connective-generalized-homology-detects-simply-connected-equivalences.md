# Connective generalized homology detects simply connected equivalences

↑ **Parent:** [Bottom-degree generalized Hurewicz isomorphism](bottom-degree-generalized-hurewicz-isomorphism.md)

For a [connective spectrum](connective-spectrum.md) with $\pi_0E\cong\mathbb Z$, an $E$-homology isomorphism of [simply connected spaces](simply-connected-space.md) is a [weak homotopy equivalence](weak-homotopy-equivalence.md). Its simply connected homotopy [mapping cone](mapping-cone-homological-algebra.md) is $E$-acyclic. If it had a first nonzero integral [homology](homology-split.md) group, the [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) and the [bottom-degree generalized Hurewicz isomorphism](bottom-degree-generalized-hurewicz-isomorphism.md) would produce a nonzero $E$-homology group. Thus the cone is integrally acyclic and the [homological Whitehead theorem](homological-whitehead-theorem.md) applies.
