# Metalloligand

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

A [coordination complex](coordination-complex.md) that retains donor sites capable of binding additional metal [ions](ion.md). Distinguishable internal and external coordination sites permit hierarchical construction of heterometallic assemblies.
