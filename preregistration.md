# Preregistration

↑ **Parent:** [Open science](open-science.md)

A time-stamped record of a research plan made before relevant results are examined. It distinguishes planned decisions from result-informed changes; it need not prohibit [exploratory analysis](exploratory-analysis.md). For existing data, disclose previous access and knowledge.[Definition and FAQ on existing data](preregistration-by-center-for-open-science.md)
