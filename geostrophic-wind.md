# Geostrophic wind

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md)

The [geostrophic wind](geostrophic-wind.md) is the horizontal flow for which the [pressure gradient force](pressure-gradient-force.md) balances the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md). It runs along [atmospheric isobars](atmospheric-isobar.md) and has speed proportional to their [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md), at fixed [density](density.md) and nonzero [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md). Curvature, [friction](friction.md) and small spatial scales can invalidate that approximation.
