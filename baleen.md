# Baleen

↑ **Parent:** [Mysticeti](mysticeti.md)

Keratinous plates used in filtering food in living [mysticetes](mysticeti.md). Its presence in a fossil may be inferred from associated anatomy but must not be confused with direct preservation of the plates.
