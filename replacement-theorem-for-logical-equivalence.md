# Replacement theorem for logical equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Subformula replacement theorem](subformula-replacement-theorem.md)

Replacing a designated [subformula](subformula.md) by a logically equivalent [formula](logical-formula.md) preserves [logical equivalence](logical-equivalence.md) of the surrounding [propositional formulas](propositional-formula.md). For any [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) the two expressions at the replaced node have the same [truth value](truth-value.md). Each [logical connective](logical-connective.md) preserves this agreement when its other inputs are unchanged. Induction from that node to the root proves agreement of the whole [formulas](logical-formula.md) under every [valuation](boolean-valuation.md).
