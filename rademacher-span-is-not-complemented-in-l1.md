# Rademacher span is not complemented in L1

↑ **Parent:** [Grothendieck theorem for L1 to Hilbert operators](grothendieck-theorem-for-l1-to-hilbert-operators.md)

If the closed span of the [Rademacher functions](rademacher-function.md) were a [complemented subspace](complemented-subspace.md) of $L^1(0,1)$, compose a projection with the inverse of its [l2 sequence space](l2-sequence-space.md) parametrization. The [Grothendieck theorem for L1 to Hilbert operators](grothendieck-theorem-for-l1-to-hilbert-operators.md) would make this map an [absolutely summing operator](absolutely-summing-operator.md). Its composition with the parametrization would make the identity on the infinite-dimensional [l2 sequence space](l2-sequence-space.md) absolutely summing, contradicting its coordinate-vector test.
