<h1 id="ramification-break-of-an-artin-schreier-pole">Ramification break of an Artin–Schreier pole</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups](uniformizer-criterion-for-lower-ramification-groups.md)

Let $K$ be an equal-characteristic-p [local field](local-field.md) and let $c\in K$ have $v_K(c)=-m<0$, with $p\nmid m$. If $\alpha^p-\alpha=c$, the [Artin–Schreier extension](artin-schreier-extension.md) $L=K(\alpha)$ has degree $p$ and is totally ramified: a root in $K$ would force the pole order to be divisible by $p$, and $p v_L(\alpha)=-m e(L/K)$ forces $e=p$. Choose $1\leq s<p$ and an integer $r$ with $rp-sm=1$. For a base [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $t$, the element $\pi_L=t^r\alpha^s$ has [valuation](valuation.md) one. Under $\sigma_a(\alpha)=\alpha+a$, $a\in\mathbb F_p^\times$, the leading term in $\sigma_a(\pi_L)-\pi_L$ has [valuation](valuation.md) $rp-(s-1)m=m+1$. The [uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups](uniformizer-criterion-for-lower-ramification-groups.md) therefore gives the single lower break $m$. The [Herbrand function](herbrand-function.md) is the identity up to that break, so the upper break is also $m$.
