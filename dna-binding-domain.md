# DNA-binding domain

↑ **Parent:** [Protein domain](protein-domain.md)

A [DNA-binding domain](dna-binding-domain.md) is a region of a protein contributing to DNA recognition or binding. Its binding specificity can be separable from an [activation domain](activation-domain.md), allowing experimental tests of recognition and transcriptional stimulation.
