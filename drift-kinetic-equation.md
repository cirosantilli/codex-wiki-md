# Drift-kinetic equation

↑ **Parent:** [Drift kinetics](drift-kinetics.md)

The collisionless equation in the title transports a gyrophase-independent [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) at fixed [first adiabatic invariant](first-adiabatic-invariant.md). Its leading [guiding center](guiding-center.md) position has $\dot{\mathbf R}=\mathbf u_\perp+v_\parallel\mathbf b$. The parallel acceleration includes $(q/m)E_\parallel-\mu\mathbf b\cdot\nabla B$ and, when required, terms from motion of the local frame. Both $v_\parallel$ and the parallel derivative refer to the exact perturbed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).
