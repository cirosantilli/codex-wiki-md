# Decalin

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Decalin](decalin.md) contains two fused six-membered [carbon](carbon.md) rings. Trans fusion permits a relatively rigid double-chair geometry, while cis fusion admits interconverting double-chair conformers.
