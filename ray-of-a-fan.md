# Ray of a fan

↑ **Parent:** [Face of a polyhedral cone](face-of-a-polyhedral-cone.md)

A [ray of a fan](ray-of-a-fan.md) is a one-dimensional cone in a [fan in toric geometry](fan-in-toric-geometry.md). It has the form $\mathbb R_{\geq0}v$, where $v$ is a primitive [lattice](lattice.md) vector, and occurs as a [face of a polyhedral cone](face-of-a-polyhedral-cone.md).

A ray of a fan is a one-dimensional cone. It has a unique primitive lattice generator $v_\rho$ pointing along it.
