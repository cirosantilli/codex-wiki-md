# Twin image in holography

↑ **Parent:** [In-line holography](in-line-holography.md)

For a uniform on-axis [plane wave](plane-wave.md) reference, choose its constant [wave phase](phase-waves.md) to be zero, so $R=A_R>0$. The reconstructed cross terms are then proportional to $O$ and its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) $O^*$. This pair creates the [twin image in holography](twin-image-in-holography.md). An outgoing curved [wavefront](wavefront.md) and its conjugate have opposite curvature: under forward propagation, one can appear to diverge from a [virtual image](virtual-image.md) while the other converges to a real image. In [in-line holography](in-line-holography.md) their depths differ, but a defocused member still overlaps the other. This is the same conjugation of spatial [wave phase](phase-waves.md) used in [phase conjugation](phase-conjugation.md), although a transmission [hologram](hologram.md) here launches both components forward rather than automatically creating a returning [phase-conjugate mirror](phase-conjugate-mirror.md).
