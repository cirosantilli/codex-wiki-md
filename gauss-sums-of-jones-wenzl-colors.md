# Gauss sums of Jones-Wenzl colors

↑ **Parent:** [Kirby color](kirby-color.md)

These sums are the evaluations of the plus- and minus-one-framed unknots with [Kirby color](kirby-color.md). Twist balancing and the fusion-dimension identity give $H(\theta_jd_j)_j=p_+(\theta_j^{-1}d_j)_j$. Conjugating this identity and using $H^2=\mathcal D^2I$ proves $p_+p_-=\mathcal D^2$, so neither stabilization factor vanishes.
