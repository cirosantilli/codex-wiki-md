# Rossby-wave teleconnection

↑ **Parent:** [Teleconnection](teleconnection.md)

A spatially localized forcing, such as persistent tropical heating, can excite [Rossby waves](rossby-wave.md) that propagate wave activity into distant regions. Mean winds Doppler-shift the waves, and background [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) gradients and jets guide or refract the packets. A stationary pattern can thus connect remote ridges and troughs even though the underlying wave energy propagates. [Group velocity](group-velocity.md), rather than phase velocity alone, diagnoses packet propagation; damping, seasonality and eddy feedbacks affect the response.
