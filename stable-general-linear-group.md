# Stable general linear group

↑ **Parent:** [K1 of a ring](k1-of-a-ring.md)

The union of finite invertible [matrices](matrix.md) under $g\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(g,1)$. Every element and relation occurs in a finite [matrix](matrix.md) size; adding identity blocks permits comparisons between different sizes without changing the stable class.
