# Constant-risk binomial proportion estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Minimax estimator](minimax-estimator.md)

For $X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,\theta)$, the [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) under $\operatorname{Beta}(\sqrt n/2,\sqrt n/2)$ has constant squared-error risk $1/[4(\sqrt n+1)^2]$. Its unique Bayes property and constant risk make it the unique [minimax estimator](minimax-estimator.md).
