# Finite-quotient criterion for conjugacy in a profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Profinite group](profinite-group.md)

Elements $g,h$ of a profinite group $G=\varprojlim_jG_j$ are conjugate exactly when their images are conjugate in every $G_j$. For the nontrivial direction, the finite nonempty sets of conjugators in the $G_j$ form an inverse system, whose inverse limit supplies a conjugator in $G$.
