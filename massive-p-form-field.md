# Massive p-form field

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian p-form gauge field](abelian-p-form-gauge-field.md)

A free massive $p$-form obeys the displayed equation, with the divergence constraint following by applying the [codifferential](codifferential.md). For $m>0$ and $0\le p\le D-1$, a rest frame leaves an antisymmetric tensor on $D-1$ spatial directions, giving $\binom{D-1}{p}$ physical polarizations. The mass term removes the ordinary gauge invariance, unless a [Stueckelberg mechanism](stueckelberg-mechanism.md) restores a redundant description.
