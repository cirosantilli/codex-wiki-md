# Unbounded self-adjoint operator

↑ **Parent:** [Self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md)

A densely defined [linear operator](linear-operator.md) on a complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) is self-adjoint when its [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) has exactly the same domain and agrees there. Symmetry only requires $A\subseteq A^*$ and does not guarantee self-adjointness. A self-adjoint operator is a [closed operator](closed-linear-operator.md) with real [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md). Domains matter for differential operators and unbounded [multiplication operators](multiplication-operator.md).
