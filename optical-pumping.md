# Optical pumping

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic measurement methods](atomic-measurement-methods.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_pumping)

[Optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) redistributes populations among internal [quantum states](quantum-state.md) by selective [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) followed by [spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md). It can prepare atoms for measurement and participate in [sub-Doppler cooling](sub-doppler-cooling.md).

The practical aim is to make otherwise almost equally populated [magnetic sublevels](magnetic-sublevel.md) behave differently. Repeated absorption and emission can store a population imbalance in the long-lived [ground state](ground-state.md); a change in [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) or [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) then reveals a perturbation of that prepared distribution. This combines preparation and measurement: a weak [radio wave](radio-wave.md) can reorganize the populations, while abundant optical [photons](photon.md) supply the readout.

An imbalance among [ground state](ground-state.md) [magnetic sublevels](magnetic-sublevel.md) need not be a [population inversion](population-inversion.md) between optical [energy levels](energy-level.md). [Optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) also supplies excitation in some [lasers](laser-split.md), where an appropriate [population inversion](population-inversion.md) matters for gain. The preparation problem here concerns the distribution within the [ground state](ground-state.md). A [two-level system](two-level-system.md) with only one lower [quantum state](quantum-state.md) cannot describe this redistribution.
