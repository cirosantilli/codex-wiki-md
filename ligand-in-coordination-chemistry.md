# Ligand in coordination chemistry

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

A [ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) is an atom, [ion](ion.md) or [molecule](molecule-split.md) attached to a central atom through one or more donor sites. This use is distinct from a protein-binding [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md). A neutral CO ligand and an anionic chloride ligand can both donate two [electrons](electron.md) in ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md).
