# Maximum power transfer theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Impedance matching](impedance-matching.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_power_transfer_theorem)

A [Thévenin equivalent](thevenin-equivalent.md) voltage source $V_s$ with positive source resistance $R_s$ supplies load power $P_L=V_s^2R_L/(R_s+R_L)^2$ to a real resistive load. Differentiating gives its maximum at $R_L=R_s$. With reactive impedances and fixed source impedance, conjugate matching maximizes the average delivered power. Maximum delivered power is distinct from maximum efficiency; at a real resistive match half of the source's internally generated power is dissipated in $R_s$.
