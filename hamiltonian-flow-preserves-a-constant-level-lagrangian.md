# Hamiltonian flow preserves a constant-level Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md)

If a Hamiltonian is constant on a Lagrangian $L$, then $dH$ vanishes on $TL$. Hence its Hamiltonian vector field belongs to $(TL)^\omega=TL$, and its flow preserves $L$.
