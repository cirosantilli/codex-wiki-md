# Convection amplitude coupled to a conserved field

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md)

A representative scaled amplitude model has complex $A$ and real $B$ with

$$
A_T=A+A_{XX}-A|A|^2-AB,\qquad B_T=\sigma B_{XX}+\mu(|A|^2)_{XX},\qquad\sigma>0.
$$

[Periodic boundary conditions](periodic-boundary-conditions.md) conserve $\int B\,dX$, because the right-hand side is a total second derivative. The slow field can change long-wave [convection roll](convection-roll.md) stability and permit localized pulses. For zero mean $B$, the uniform [convection rolls](convection-roll.md) are $A=Re^{iqX}$, $R^2=1-q^2$, $B=0$.
