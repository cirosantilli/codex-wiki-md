# Transported step forcing in an advection-diffusion equation

↑ **Parent:** [Advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md)

In coordinates $s=t$, $y=x-t$, the transport derivative $u_t+u_x$ becomes $U_s$. A [Heaviside step function](heaviside-step-function.md) moving at that speed becomes stationary, and its twice-integrated profile gives the displayed continuously differentiable solution of $u_t+u_x=u_{xx}+H(x-t)$. Its second spatial derivative has a jump, so the equation holds away from the moving interface and in the distributional sense there.
