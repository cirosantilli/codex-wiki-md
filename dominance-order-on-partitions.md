# Dominance order on partitions

↑ **Parent:** [Partition of an integer](partition-of-an-integer.md)

For partitions of the same integer, $\lambda$ dominates $\mu$ when

$$
\sum_{i=1}^r\lambda_i\geq\sum_{i=1}^r\mu_i
$$

for every $r$.
