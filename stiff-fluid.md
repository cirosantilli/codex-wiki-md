# Stiff fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md)

A [perfect fluid](perfect-fluid.md) with [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) $P=\rho$ is called a stiff fluid. For positive [energy density](energy-density.md), its constant equation-of-state parameter is $w=1$. The [cosmological continuity equation](cosmological-continuity-equation.md) gives $\rho\propto a^{-6}$. In a flat universe dominated by this fluid, the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) then gives $a(t)\propto(t-t_B)^{1/3}$. This statement assumes a nonzero fluid density and negligible other components.
