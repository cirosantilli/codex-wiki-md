# Heegaard Floer homology

↑ **Parent:** [Heegaard splitting](heegaard-splitting.md)

Heegaard Floer homology assigns groups $HF^\circ(Y,\mathfrak s)$ to a closed oriented three-manifold with a [Spin-c structure](spin-c-structure.md). A pointed [Heegaard diagram](heegaard-diagram.md) of genus $g$ gives product [Lagrangian tori](lagrangian-torus.md) $\mathbb T_\alpha,\mathbb T_\beta\subset\operatorname{Sym}^g(\Sigma)$; the [chain complex](chain-complex.md) is generated by their intersection points and its differential counts [Whitney disk classes](whitney-disk-class.md) represented by holomorphic disks. The minus, infinity, plus and hat versions use different powers or quotients of the variable $U$. The [Heegaard Floer cobordism maps](heegaard-floer-cobordism-map.md) and the [Mixed Heegaard Floer invariant](mixed-heegaard-floer-invariant.md) relate these groups to smooth four-dimensional topology.
