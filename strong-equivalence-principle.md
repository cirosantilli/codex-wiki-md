# Strong equivalence principle

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md)

The strong [Equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md) extends universality of free fall to bodies with appreciable gravitational self-energy and requires local experiments, including gravitational ones, in a freely falling laboratory to be independent of the laboratory's location and velocity. This statement neglects external tidal variation across the laboratory; it does not remove [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md). In [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md), universal metric coupling, fixed local gravitational coupling and the absence of extra preferred-frame gravitational fields implement this principle.
