# Planet-synchronous orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Circumplanetary orbit](circumplanetary-orbit.md)

A [circumplanetary orbit](circumplanetary-orbit.md) whose [orbital period](orbital-period.md) equals the central planet's spin period. Its [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) follows from [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md). Matching periods alone does not fix the satellite above one ground location; that additionally requires a [planet-stationary orbit](planet-stationary-orbit.md).
