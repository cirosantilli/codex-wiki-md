# Uniaxial strain modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

The [uniaxial strain modulus](uniaxial-strain-modulus.md) relates longitudinal [normal stress](normal-stress.md) to longitudinal [strain](strain.md) when both transverse normal strains vanish. For isotropic [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md), solving the transverse compliance equations gives the transverse stresses $\nu\tau_1/(1-\nu)$ and hence the displayed modulus in terms of [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md) and [Poisson's ratio](poisson-s-ratio.md). It differs from [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md), which applies when the transverse stresses vanish. Their difference is crucial for thin elastic layers bonded to rigid faces.
