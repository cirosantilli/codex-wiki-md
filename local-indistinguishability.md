# Local indistinguishability

↑ **Parent:** [Topological quantum matter](topological-quantum-matter-split.md)

Two [quantum states](quantum-state.md) are [locally indistinguishable](local-indistinguishability.md) at a prescribed scale if all operators of norm at most one supported below that scale have nearly equal expectation values. This is a statement about restricted observables, not equality of the global states. An orthogonal pair can be locally indistinguishable on macroscopically large regions.
