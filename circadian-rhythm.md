# Circadian rhythm

↑ **Parent:** [Physiology](physiology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circadian_rhythm)

A [circadian rhythm](circadian-rhythm.md) is an endogenous biological [oscillation](oscillation.md) with a [period](period-of-a-function.md) near 24 hours, persisting without a repeating external signal. A [circadian clock](circadian-clock.md) times biological activities and anticipates daily changes without continual external instructions. A [free-running circadian rhythm](free-running-circadian-rhythm.md) reveals its intrinsic [period](period-of-a-function.md); [entrainment of circadian rhythm](entrainment-of-circadian-rhythm.md) coordinates it with an external cycle.[Vitaterna, Takahashi and Turek (2001), Overview of Circadian Rhythms, pp. 85–86, Biological Rhythms and Basic Properties of Circadian Rhythms](overview-of-circadian-rhythms-by-martha-hotz-vitaterna-and-others.md)

In 1994, [Martha Hotz Vitaterna](martha-hotz-vitaterna.md) and colleagues screened offspring of mutagen-treated C57BL/6J mice (the [house mouse](house-mouse.md)). Each mouse lived in a cage with a running wheel, inside a ventilated, light-tight chamber with timer-controlled lighting. Wheel revolutions were recorded electronically or on an event recorder. The initial screen used 14:10 LD for the first 41 animals and 12:12 LD thereafter; LD denotes hours of light and darkness. Transfer to constant darkness, DD, omitted the next scheduled lights-on; recording continued for at least three weeks. The first 41 animals also received brief light pulses, so those records were not uninterrupted DD.[Vitaterna and others (1994), Mutagenesis and Mapping of a Mouse Gene, Clock, Essential for Circadian Behavior, Figure 1 and References and Notes 6–10](mutagenesis-and-mapping-of-a-mouse-gene-clock-essential-for-circadian-behavior-by-martha-hotz-vitaterna-and-others.md)

The subsequent-generation comparison in Figure 2 used 12:12 LD before DD. Animals without the [Clock mutation](clock-mutation-mouse.md) and with mutant [genotypes](genotype.md) supplied the comparison: the B6 examples in panels A–C had initial DD [periods](period-of-a-function.md) of 23.6, 24.8 and 27.1 hours, respectively; the [homozygote](homozygote.md) later lost its sustained [circadian rhythm](circadian-rhythm.md). Total activity remained comparable across [genotypes](genotype.md) in the interstrain comparison. The [mutation](mutation.md) therefore altered timing, beyond merely reducing running. Wheel activity is a behavioral readout; it does not directly measure every [circadian clock](circadian-clock.md) or establish a human [period](period-of-a-function.md).[Figure 2A–C; Table 2 and discussion of activity counts and loss of rhythmicity; References and Notes 10 and 14](mutagenesis-and-mapping-of-a-mouse-gene-clock-essential-for-circadian-behavior-by-martha-hotz-vitaterna-and-others.md)

Daily activity under LD alone cannot establish [entrainment of circadian rhythm](entrainment-of-circadian-rhythm.md). Light can directly change activity without resetting the [circadian clock](circadian-clock.md): [masking of circadian rhythm](masking-of-circadian-rhythm.md). Removing the light cycle helps distinguish these effects; they can coexist.[p. 92, glossary entries Entrainment, Free running and Masking](overview-of-circadian-rhythms-by-martha-hotz-vitaterna-and-others.md) In an experiment, other repeating inputs must also be controlled: a nominally dark room with daily handling is not automatically an environment without timing cues.
