# Inner NFW contraction to a flat rotation curve

↑ **Parent:** [Circular-shell adiabatic contraction](circular-shell-adiabatic-contraction.md)

An inner [NFW profile](navarro-frenk-white-profile.md) has $M_i(r_i)=Ar_i^2$, where $A=2\pi\rho_s r_s$. If the final total [circular speed](circular-speed.md) is $V$, [circular-shell adiabatic contraction](circular-shell-adiabatic-contraction.md) gives $r_i=(V^2/(GA))^{1/3}r^{2/3}$ and $M'_D=A^{1/3}(V^2/G)^{2/3}r^{4/3}$. Thus $\rho'_D=A^{1/3}(V^2/G)^{2/3}r^{-5/3}/(3\pi)$. The total density producing the flat curve is $V^2/(4\pi Gr^2)$, so the dark fraction tends to zero as $r^{1/3}$ despite its increase relative to the original cusp.
