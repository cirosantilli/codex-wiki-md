# Radiation-induced creep

↑ **Parent:** [Irradiation creep](irradiation-creep.md)

Stress-directed deformation generated by irradiation-defect processes that can operate where ordinary [creep](creep-deformation.md) is small. The terminology overlaps with [irradiation creep](irradiation-creep.md) in the literature; the mechanism and reference condition should be stated.
