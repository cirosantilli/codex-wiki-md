# Characteristic of a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)

The characteristic of a [ring](ring.md) $R$ is the least positive integer $n$ satisfying $n\cdot1_R=0$, or zero if no such integer exists. A nonzero ring of positive characteristic has prime characteristic when it is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md).
