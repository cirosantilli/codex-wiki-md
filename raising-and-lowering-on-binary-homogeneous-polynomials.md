# Raising and lowering on binary homogeneous polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Commutator](commutator.md)

On the degree-$n$ [homogeneous polynomials](homogeneous-polynomial.md) use $e_k=x^{n-k}y^k$. Then $Pe_k=ke_{k-1}$ and $Qe_k=(n-k)e_{k+1}$; both maps are nilpotent of index $n+1$. Their [commutator](commutator.md) is diagonal in this basis with distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $n,n-2,\ldots,-n$. Thus the raising and lowering operators are not [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md) for $n\geq1$, while their [commutator](commutator.md) is.
