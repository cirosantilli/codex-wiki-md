# Stable pair potential

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md)

A [pair potential](pair-potential.md) is stable when its total interaction [energy](energy.md) obeys this lower bound for every allowed configuration, with a constant $B$ independent of $N$. It prevents collapse with an arbitrarily negative [energy](energy.md) per particle. Stability alone does not control the long-distance contribution needed for the ordinary [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md).
