# Fermionic Einstein-coefficient relations

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein coefficients](einstein-coefficients.md)

For an ideal emitter able to exchange one [fermion](fermion.md) with a zero-[chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) bath, [Pauli blocking](pauli-blocking.md) gives downward rate $A(1-f)$ rather than the bosonic $A(1+f)$. If $u_\nu=h\nu\mathcal D_\nu f$ and $C=A/(h\nu\mathcal D_\nu)>0$, the rate is $A-Cu_\nu$. [Detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) with a [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) implies $g_1B_{12}=g_2C$ and $A/C=h\nu\mathcal D_\nu$. For $d$ massless helicity states, $\mathcal D_\nu=d\,4\pi\nu^2/c^3$. Thus an additive induced-emission coefficient would be negative, expressing blocking rather than a negative total rate. The emitter must change its [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md); two ordinary electronic [energy levels](energy-level.md) cannot exchange a single [neutrino](neutrino.md) this way.
