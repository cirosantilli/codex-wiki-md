# Diffraction bandwidth and detector pitch

↑ **Parent:** [Optical resolution](optical-resolution.md)

For incoherent imaging with an unobstructed circular [aperture](aperture.md) of diameter $D$, the intensity-image spatial-frequency cutoff is $D/(\lambda f)$ in the focal plane. The [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md) therefore gives the displayed pitch limit. The [Rayleigh criterion](rayleigh-criterion.md) instead gives a point-image separation $1.22\lambda f/D$. Consequently two samples per Rayleigh separation and sampling the full optical [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md) differ by the factor $1.22$. Neither sampling estimate guarantees useful contrast at arbitrarily low signal-to-noise ratio.
