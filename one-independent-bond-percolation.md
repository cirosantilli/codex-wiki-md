# One-independent bond percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Dependent percolation](dependent-percolation.md)

A [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) law is one-independent if the bond states in any two sets with disjoint endpoint sets are independent as collections. In particular, states of bonds in a [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md) are mutually independent. The law need not be invariant under translations or positively associated.
