# Out-of-male hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Evolutionary developmental biology](evolutionary-developmental-biology.md)

A proposed origin of a bisexual reproductive structure through regional modification of a formerly male structure. In one developmental scenario, restriction of B-class activity permits female identity in part of a C-active reproductive axis. Accessibility in a model system does not establish the historical precursor.
