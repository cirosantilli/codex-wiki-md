# Positive-definite scalar function

↑ **Parent:** [Positive definiteness of a Lyapunov function](positive-definiteness-of-a-lyapunov-function.md)

Near an equilibrium, a [positive-definite scalar function](positive-definite-scalar-function.md) has value zero at that point and is strictly positive at every other nearby point. This scalar notion differs from positive definiteness of a kernel.
