# Hot-carrier solar cell

↑ **Parent:** [Solar cell](solar-cell.md)

A device concept that seeks to extract carriers through energy-selective contacts before their excess photon [energy](energy-split.md) is lost by [thermalization](thermalization.md). Suppressing cooling while maintaining extraction is the central challenge.
