# Cossee-Arlman mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerization](polymerization.md)

The [Cossee-Arlman mechanism](cossee-arlman-mechanism.md) propagates a [polymer](polymer-split.md) through [alkene](alkene.md) coordination followed by insertion into a [metal](metal.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) bond. Repetition extends the [metal](metal.md)-bound chain. [Regioselectivity](regioselectivity.md) controls monomer connectivity; it does not by itself specify [tacticity](tacticity.md).
