# Integer division

↑ **Parent:** [Division (mathematics)](division-mathematics.md)

[Division](division-mathematics.md) of [integers](integer-split.md) that returns an [integer](integer-split.md) quotient rather than an exact [fraction](fraction.md). A rounding convention is required for negative operands. For $n\ge0$ and $d>0$, the quotient is $q=\lfloor n/d\rfloor$ and the [remainder](remainder.md) is $r=n-qd$ with $0\le r<d$.
