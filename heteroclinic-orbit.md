# Heteroclinic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

A heteroclinic orbit is a trajectory tending to one invariant equilibrium or orbit in backward time and a different one in forward time. Coupled convection amplitudes can have such trajectories describing replacement of one [convection roll](convection-roll.md) orientation by another.
