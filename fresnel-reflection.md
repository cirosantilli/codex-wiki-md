# Fresnel reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md)

The specular reflection of a wave at a planar dielectric interface with amplitudes described by the [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md). Below the [critical angle](critical-angle-for-total-internal-reflection.md), the reflected fraction is generally nonzero even when transmission is allowed; above the critical threshold, ideal lossless [total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md) has unit reflected power. The reflected electric-field sign depends on polarization and the chosen basis conventions.
