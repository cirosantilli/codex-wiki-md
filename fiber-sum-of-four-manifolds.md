# Fiber sum of four-manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth four-manifold](smooth-four-manifold.md)

Remove [tubular neighborhoods](tubular-neighborhood.md) of diffeomorphic embedded oriented surfaces with opposite normal Euler numbers, and glue their boundary circle bundles by a fiber-reversing identification. The resulting smooth [manifold](topological-manifold.md) is a fiber sum. The square-zero case uses trivial [normal bundles](normal-bundle.md). Additional symplectic hypotheses give a [symplectic fiber sum along a square-zero surface](symplectic-fiber-sum-along-a-square-zero-surface.md).
