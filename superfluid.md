# Superfluid

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluidity](superfluidity.md)

A superfluid exhibits [superfluidity](superfluidity.md), with [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md) and a phase-gradient current. In an appropriate ordered or quasi-ordered regime it supports low-speed flow without viscous dissipation, and its long-wavelength [Goldstone boson](goldstone-boson.md) has a sound-like dispersion. True thermodynamic phase selection and [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md) need separate infrared conditions.
