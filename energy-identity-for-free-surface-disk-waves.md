# Energy identity for free-surface disk waves

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric waves of a homogeneous incompressible disk](axisymmetric-waves-of-a-homogeneous-incompressible-disk.md)

For radial and vertical [Lagrangian fluid displacements](lagrangian-fluid-displacement.md) of a local axisymmetric mode, integrate the momentum equations and use [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) plus the [free-surface pressure condition of an incompressible disk](free-surface-pressure-condition-of-an-incompressible-disk.md). The result is

$$
\omega^2\int_{-H}^H(|\xi_r|^2+|\xi_z|^2)dz
=\kappa^2\int_{-H}^H|\xi_r|^2dz+\Omega_z^2H\bigl(|\xi_z(H)|^2+|\xi_z(-H)|^2\bigr).
$$

For nonnegative $\kappa^2$ and positive $\Omega_z^2$, every nontrivial oscillatory mode has real nonnegative $\omega^2$. The surface term is the gravitational restoring energy of the displaced vacuum interfaces.
