# Brownian endpoint sufficiency for a constant drift

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian Brownian drift filter](gaussian-brownian-drift-filter.md)

For $Y_s=\lambda s+W_s$ and fixed $t>0$, subtracting $(s/t)Y_t$ leaves the [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md) $W_s-(s/t)W_t$. It is independent of $(\lambda,Y_t)$, since it is independent of $\lambda$ and has zero [covariance](covariance.md) with $W_t$ in their jointly [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) law. The observed path is determined by this bridge and its endpoint, so the bridge gives no further information about $\lambda$. Hence conditioning on the full observation [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) at $t$ gives the same posterior as conditioning on $Y_t$ alone.
