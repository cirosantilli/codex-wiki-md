# Density-gradient centrifugation

↑ **Parent:** [Centrifugation](centrifugation.md)

[Density-gradient centrifugation](density-gradient-centrifugation.md) uses a liquid whose [density](density.md) varies along the separation direction. A band does not by itself establish which mechanism produced it: [rate-zonal centrifugation](rate-zonal-centrifugation.md) separates by migration rate before equilibrium, whereas [isopycnic centrifugation](isopycnic-centrifugation.md) uses equilibrium positions set by [buoyant density](buoyant-density.md).

The distinction is between a moving clock and a restoring balance. In [rate-zonal centrifugation](rate-zonal-centrifugation.md), two species may occupy different positions because their [sedimentation coefficients](sedimentation-coefficient.md) differ, even when their [buoyant densities](buoyant-density.md) agree. In [isopycnic centrifugation](isopycnic-centrifugation.md), two differently sized species can converge to the same band because their [buoyant densities](buoyant-density.md) agree. Conversely, equal [mass](mass.md) does not ensure equal migration [velocity](velocity.md): shape affects [drag force](drag-physics.md), and medium [density](density.md) affects [buoyancy](buoyancy.md).[The Physics Behind the Technology; Rate Zonal Centrifugation; Isopycnic Centrifugation: migration properties versus matching density](an-introduction-to-ultracentrifugation-by-beckman-coulter-life-sciences.md)

The worked [finite-time rate-zonal separation](finite-time-rate-zonal-separation.md) and [linear-gradient isopycnic focusing](linear-gradient-isopycnic-focusing.md) examples isolate these mechanisms in chosen [mathematical models](mathematical-model.md). The actual [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) experiment described under [isopycnic centrifugation](isopycnic-centrifugation.md) illustrates the second mechanism; its initial collection of [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) into [centrifugation pellets](centrifugation-pellet.md) is a different operation from the later molecular banding. Collecting liquid fractions from a tube can recover material at different positions, but the position alone does not identify its [molecules](molecule-split.md) or establish purity.
