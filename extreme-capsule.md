# Extreme capsule

↑ **Parent:** [White matter](white-matter.md)

A white-matter region between the claustrum and insular cortex associated with a ventral temporofrontal route in some [language](language.md)-connectivity studies. It is a specific anatomical term, not a synonym for every ventral [language](language.md) connection.
