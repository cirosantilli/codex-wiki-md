# Solar cell

↑ **Parent:** [Photovoltaic effect](photovoltaic-effect.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_cell)

A [solar cell](solar-cell.md) converts incident [light](light.md) into electrical [power](power.md) through the [photovoltaic effect](photovoltaic-effect.md). A [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md) separates and collects photogenerated [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md). An ideal illuminated junction has output [current density](current-density.md)

$$
J(V)=J_{\rm sc}-J_0\left(e^{eV/(k_BT)}-1\right),\qquad
V_{\rm oc}=\frac{k_BT}{e}\log\left(1+\frac{J_{\rm sc}}{J_0}\right).
$$

Here $J_{\rm sc}$ is the short-circuit [current density](current-density.md), $J_0$ is the [diode saturation current](diode-saturation-current.md), and $V_{\rm oc}$ is the open-circuit [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). The conversion efficiency is the largest delivered $VJ$ divided by the incident [power](power.md) per unit area; the short-circuit and open-circuit operating points themselves deliver zero [power](power.md).
