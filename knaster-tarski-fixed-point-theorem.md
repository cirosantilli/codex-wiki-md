<h1 id="knaster-tarski-fixed-point-theorem">Knaster–Tarski fixed-point theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Complete lattice](complete-lattice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knaster–Tarski_fixed-point_theorem)

Every [order-preserving function](order-preserving-function.md) from a [complete lattice](complete-lattice.md) to itself has a least and a greatest fixed point. More strongly, its set of fixed points is itself a complete lattice.
