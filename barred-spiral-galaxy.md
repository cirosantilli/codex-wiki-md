# Barred spiral galaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Spiral galaxy](spiral-galaxy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barred_spiral_galaxy)

A [spiral galaxy](spiral-galaxy.md) with a central elongated stellar bar. Gas, young spiral-arm populations, a bulge and older disk populations can coexist; the bar is a nonaxisymmetric structure and can drive gas motion different from a purely circular-orbit model.
