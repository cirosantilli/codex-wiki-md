# Pair-number statistics of polarization-entangled down-conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Two-pair component of polarization-entangled down-conversion](two-pair-component-of-polarization-entangled-down-conversion.md)

For an ideal undepleted pump and one collected pair of spatial modes, the two orthogonal polarization-pair creation terms generate two commuting [two-mode squeezed vacua](two-mode-squeezed-vacuum.md). If $\lambda=\tanh^2r$, either squeezed pair has geometric occupation probability $(1-\lambda)\lambda^k$. Adding their pair counts gives $n+1$ equal-weight possibilities, hence $P_n=(n+1)(1-\lambda)^2\lambda^n$. Thus $P_1=2(1-\lambda)^2\lambda$ and $P_2=3(1-\lambda)^2\lambda^2\simeq3P_1^2/4$ at weak gain. Different unresolved temporal or spectral modes need not obey this single-mode formula.
