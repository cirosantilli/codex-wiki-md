# Sheaf of units of the structure sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Structure sheaf of a scheme](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md)

The units in the [structure sheaf of a scheme](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) form a multiplicative [sheaf of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md). On a [smooth algebraic curve](smooth-algebraic-curve.md), a nonzero [rational function](rational-function.md) is a unit at a point precisely when its [discrete valuation](discrete-valuation.md) there is zero. Its inclusion in the [sheaf of nonzero rational functions on an irreducible variety](sheaf-of-nonzero-rational-functions-on-an-irreducible-variety.md) relates [divisor on an algebraic curve](divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) to the [Picard group](picard-group.md) through the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md).
