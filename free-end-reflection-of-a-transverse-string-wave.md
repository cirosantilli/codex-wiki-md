# Free-end reflection of a transverse string wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md)

At a free termination of a taut string, zero transverse [force](force-split.md) imposes a [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md). Incident and reflected displacement amplitudes add with equal sign so their slopes cancel. The endpoint is a displacement antinode, all incident [power](power.md) is reflected, and the flux $-T\psi_x\psi_t$ through the boundary vanishes. This is a transverse-wave [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md), distinct from the quantum open-string usage of similar notation.
