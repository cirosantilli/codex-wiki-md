<h1 id="orbit-volume-in-an-axisymmetric-stackel-potential">Orbit volume in an axisymmetric Stäckel potential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stäckel potential](stackel-potential.md)

For fixed $E,l,I$, separated motion obeys $p_\tau^2=[E\tau+G(\tau)-I+(\alpha-\beta)l^2/(2(\tau+\alpha))]/[2(\tau+\alpha)(\tau+\beta)]$. Allowed intervals are where this is nonnegative, with turning points and coordinate-fold endpoints treated separately. A generic nonresonant bound [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) with $l\ne0$ circulates azimuthally and densely samples a projected [volume](volume.md) bounded by confocal spheroids and hyperboloids. It is not uniformly distributed in [volume](volume.md). Resonant or zero-angular-momentum [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) can project to lower-dimensional regions; integrability alone does not imply that every [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) fills a three-dimensional shell.
