# Maximal dilatation

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiconformal mapping](quasiconformal-mapping.md)

The essential supremum of the ratio of the largest to the smallest infinitesimal [singular value](singular-value.md) of a [quasiconformal map](quasiconformal-mapping.md). It equals $(1+\|\mu_f\|_\infty)/(1-\|\mu_f\|_\infty)$ in terms of the [Beltrami coefficient](beltrami-coefficient.md).
