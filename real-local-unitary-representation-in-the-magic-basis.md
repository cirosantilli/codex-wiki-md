# Real local-unitary representation in the magic basis

↑ **Parent:** [Magic basis](magic-basis.md)

The [anti-Hermitian](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) generators of local [qubit](qubit.md) rotations become real [antisymmetric matrices](skew-symmetric-matrix.md) in the [magic basis](magic-basis.md). Their exponentials preserve $b^{\mathsf T}b$, and hence its modulus and pure-state [concurrence](concurrence.md). A common [phase](phase-waves.md) changes that bilinear quantity by a [phase](phase-waves.md) but not its modulus.
