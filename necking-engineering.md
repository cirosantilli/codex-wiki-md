# Necking (engineering)

↑ **Parent:** [Plasticity (physics)](plasticity-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necking_(engineering))

Localization of tensile deformation into a narrowing region of a specimen. The onset depends on the competition between declining cross-sectional area and [work hardening](work-hardening.md).
