# Projective base change group of a quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Base change action on quiver representations](base-change-action-on-quiver-representations.md)

For a nonzero dimension vector, quotient $\prod_i\operatorname{GL}_{n_i}(k)$ by the common scalar subgroup $(\lambda I_{n_i})_i$. It acts trivially, so the quotient acts algebraically on the [quiver representation space](quiver-representation-space.md). This is one scalar quotient, rather than a product of the individual projective general linear groups. At dimension vector zero the action is trivial.
