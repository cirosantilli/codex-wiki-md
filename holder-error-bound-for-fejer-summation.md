<h1 id="holder-error-bound-for-fejer-summation">Hölder error bound for Fejér summation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fejér sum](fejer-sum.md)

For a periodic [Hölder continuous function](holder-condition.md) of [Hölder exponent](holder-exponent.md) $0<\alpha<1$ and [Hölder seminorm](holder-seminorm.md) at most $M$, the degree-$n-1$ [Fejér sum](fejer-sum.md) has the displayed [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) error bound. In the half-kernel normalization $F_{n-1}=(2n)^{-1}\sin^2(nt/2)/\sin^2(t/2)$, use $F_{n-1}(t)\le\min\{n/2,\pi^2/(2nt^2)\}$. Splitting the convolution error integral at $1/n$ gives the valid constant $C_\alpha=1/[\pi(\alpha+1)]+\pi/(1-\alpha)$. At the endpoint $\alpha=1$ this argument instead gives $O(M\log n/n)$.
