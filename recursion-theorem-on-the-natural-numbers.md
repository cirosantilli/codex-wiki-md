# Recursion theorem on the natural numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md)

Given an initial value $a$ and a specified successor rule on a [set](set-split.md), there is a unique [function](function-split.md) on the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) with that initial value and successor rule. Finite compatible approximations give existence; induction gives agreement of any two approximations and uniqueness. Recursive definitions of [addition](addition.md) and [multiplication](multiplication.md) apply this theorem with the other argument fixed.
