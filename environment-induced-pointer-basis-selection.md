# Environment-induced pointer-basis selection

↑ **Parent:** [Pointer basis](pointer-basis.md)

If record projectors commute with an apparatus-environment interaction, their populations are preserved while the corresponding environmental states can become distinguishable. Their [conditional environment overlap](conditional-environment-overlap.md) then suppresses interference in the apparatus [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md). A nondegenerate record coupling can thereby select robust sectors; commutation alone does not select a unique basis in a degenerate or trivial interaction.[1981 abstract: recorded observables and the apparatus-environment interaction commutation condition](pointer-basis-of-quantum-apparatus-into-what-mixture-does-the-wave-packet-collapse-by-w-h-zurek.md)
