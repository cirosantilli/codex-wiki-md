<h1 id="doob-s-martingale-inequality">Doob's martingale inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doob's_martingale_inequality)

For a nonnegative submartingale $X$, the maximal tail bound is $\lambda\mathbb P(\max_{k\leq n}X_k\geq\lambda)\leq\mathbb E[X_n;\max_{k\leq n}X_k\geq\lambda]$. Applied to the absolute value of a martingale and integrated over $\lambda$, it gives the [Doob Lp maximal inequality](doob-lp-maximal-inequality.md); at $p=2$ this is the [Doob L2 maximal inequality](doob-l2-maximal-inequality.md).
