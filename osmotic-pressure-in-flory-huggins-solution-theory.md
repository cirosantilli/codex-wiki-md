# Osmotic pressure in Flory-Huggins solution theory

↑ **Parent:** [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md)

Let $f(\phi)$ be the mixing [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per lattice site in [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md), $v_0$ the site volume, and $\phi$ the polymer [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md). The solvent mixing [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is $f-\phi f'$, so its balance against the pure-solvent pressure contribution gives $\Pi=(\phi f'-f)/v_0$. For composition-independent $\chi$,

$$
\frac{\Pi v_0}{k_BT}=\frac\phi N-\ln(1-\phi)-\phi-\chi\phi^2
=\frac\phi N+\left(\frac12-\chi\right)\phi^2+\frac{\phi^3}{3}+O(\phi^4).
$$

The first term is the ideal chain translational contribution; the second encodes the effective two-segment [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md). At the [theta temperature](theta-temperature.md) condition it vanishes but the positive third-order term remains. Treating $f$ as a free-energy density without dividing by $v_0$ loses the physical dimensions of [osmotic pressure](osmotic-pressure.md).
