# 2-Mercaptoethanol

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2-Mercaptoethanol)

2-Mercaptoethanol is the [chemical compound](chemical-compound.md) $\mathrm{HOCH}_2\mathrm{CH}_2\mathrm{SH}$; see the [NIST Chemistry WebBook, 2-mercaptoethanol: structure and names](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C60242&Units=SI). In the [ribonuclease A refolding experiment](ribonuclease-a-refolding-experiment.md), it breaks [protein disulfide bonds](protein-disulfide-bond.md), while the [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md) of the [protein backbone](protein-backbone.md) remain intact. This separates a change in crosslinks from cutting the [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md).
