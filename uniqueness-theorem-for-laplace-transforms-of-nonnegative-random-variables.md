# Uniqueness theorem for Laplace transforms of nonnegative random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform of a nonnegative random variable](laplace-transform-of-a-nonnegative-random-variable.md)

The [Laplace transform of a nonnegative random variable](laplace-transform-of-a-nonnegative-random-variable.md) determines its [probability law](probability-distribution.md). Indeed, $U=e^{-X}$ takes values in $(0,1]$, and $\mathbb E[U^n]=\psi_X(n)$. Thus the transform determines every moment of $U$; [moment determinacy on a compact interval](moment-determinacy-on-a-compact-interval.md) determines the law of $U$, and the injective inverse $X=-\log U$ determines the law of $X$.
