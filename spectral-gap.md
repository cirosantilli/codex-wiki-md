# Spectral gap

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral decomposition](spectral-decomposition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_gap)

A spectral gap is a positive separation between designated parts of the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) of a [linear operator](linear-operator.md). For the [power method](power-method.md), the relevant gap separates the modulus of the [dominant eigenvalue](dominant-eigenvalue.md) from the other eigenvalue moduli.
