# Formal power series module

↑ **Parent:** [Formal power series](formal-power-series.md)

For a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $M$ over a [field](field.md) $k$, its formal power series module is $M[[t]]=\varprojlim_n M\otimes_k k[t]/(t^n)$. It is complete for the filtration by $t^nM[[t]]$. The ordinary [tensor product](tensor-product.md) $M\otimes_k k[[t]]$ embeds in it and consists of series whose coefficients span a finite-dimensional [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md); it equals $M[[t]]$ if $M$ is finite-dimensional. The inclusion is proper for infinite-dimensional $M$, as shown by a series with linearly independent coefficients.
