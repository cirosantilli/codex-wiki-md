# Constant-phase line of an internal gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave phase and group velocity](internal-wave-phase-and-group-velocity.md)

For a two-dimensional [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) with phase $kx+\int m(z)\,dz-\omega t$, a constant-phase line at fixed time has slope $dz/dx=-k/m(z)$. Its normal spacing from the next crest is $2\pi/(k^2+m^2)^{1/2}$; the [wavevector](wavevector.md) $(k,m)$ is normal to the line. The intrinsic energy ray is tangent to these phase lines, while mean-flow [advection](advection.md) changes the laboratory ray direction.
