# Abelianization of an odd dihedral group

↑ **Parent:** [Abelianization](abelianization.md)

For odd $n\geq3$, the [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) $D_{2n}$ of order $2n$ has rotation generator $r$ and reflection generator $s$, with $srs=r^{-1}$. The [group commutator](group-commutator.md) $[r,s]=r^{-2}$ generates the rotation [subgroup](subgroup.md), while the quotient by that [subgroup](subgroup.md) is $C_2$. Hence the [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is exactly $\langle r\rangle$ and the [abelianization](abelianization.md) is $C_2$. The only [abelian quotients](abelian-quotient.md) are $C_2$ and the trivial group.
