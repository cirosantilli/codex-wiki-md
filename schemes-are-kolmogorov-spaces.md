# Schemes are Kolmogorov spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

The underlying [topological space](topological-space.md) of every [scheme](scheme.md) is a [Kolmogorov space](kolmogorov-space.md): any two distinct points are distinguished by an [open subset](open-set.md) containing just one. Choose an [affine open subscheme](affine-open-subscheme.md) containing the first point. If it omits the second, it already distinguishes them. Otherwise, the two points correspond to distinct [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md), and an element in one but not the other defines a [principal open subscheme](principal-open-subscheme.md) distinguishing them. Thus distinct points of a [scheme](scheme.md) have distinct [closures](closure-topology.md).
