# Frequency-dependent payoff

↑ **Parent:** [Evolutionary game theory](evolutionary-game-theory.md)

A [frequency-dependent payoff](frequency-dependent-payoff.md) is an [expected payoff](expected-payoff.md) depending on the population composition $x$. Under random pairwise encounters with a fixed [payoff matrix](payoff-matrix.md) it is the [linear function](linear-function.md) $f_i(x)=(Ax)_i$. The relative advantage $f_i(x)-f_j(x)$ can change sign as opponents become more or less common. The [replicator equation](replicator-equation.md) converts this feedback into changes of frequency; nonlinear [frequency-dependent payoffs](frequency-dependent-payoff.md) allow interactions not represented by a fixed pairwise table.
