# Outgoing boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering state](scattering-state.md)

A [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) for stationary scattering is chosen to contain waves travelling away from its source. In one dimension at positive [energy](energy-split.md) it is proportional to $e^{ik|x-x^{\prime}|}$; the condition selects the $E+i0$ [resolvent](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) boundary value.
