# Degenerate metric tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Metric tensor](metric-tensor.md)

A [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) on a tangent space is degenerate when some nonzero [vector](vector.md) pairs to zero with every [vector](vector.md); equivalently its matrix has zero [determinant](determinant.md). At such a point there is no inverse [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md), and the usual [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) construction of a unique [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) does not apply. A coordinate expression that degenerates must be checked to distinguish a removable coordinate failure from a genuinely excluded point of the geometric domain.
