# Symplectic cotangent lift with a closed momentum shift

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent lift of a diffeomorphism](cotangent-lift-of-a-diffeomorphism.md)

If $x=\chi(x')$ is an angular [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) and $\eta\cdot dx$ is a [closed differential one-form](closed-differential-one-form.md), the map $y=\eta(\chi(x'))+D\chi(x')^{-T}y'$ is a [symplectomorphism](symplectomorphism.md). It combines a [cotangent lift of a diffeomorphism](cotangent-lift-of-a-diffeomorphism.md) with translation by a [closed differential one-form](closed-differential-one-form.md). In particular $\eta=du+a$ is allowed on a [torus](torus.md) even when the constant one-form $a\cdot dx$ is not exact.
