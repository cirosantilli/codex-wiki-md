# Norm-compact unit ball criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md)

The closed [unit ball](unit-ball.md) of a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) is compact in norm exactly when the space is finite-dimensional. Finite-dimensional compactness follows from the [Heine-Borel theorem](heine-borel-theorem.md). In infinite dimension, an [orthonormal sequence](orthonormal-sequence.md) has pairwise distance $\sqrt2$ and no convergent subsequence. Restricting a [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md) that equals a nonzero multiple of the identity on a subspace therefore forces that subspace to be finite-dimensional.
