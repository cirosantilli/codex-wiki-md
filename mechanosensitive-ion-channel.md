# Mechanosensitive ion channel

↑ **Parent:** [Ion channel](ion-channel.md)

A [mechanosensitive ion channel](mechanosensitive-ion-channel.md) changes gating in response to mechanical conditions transmitted through the membrane or associated structures. A changed [calcium](calcium.md) signal alone does not identify which channel or mechanical coupling was responsible.
