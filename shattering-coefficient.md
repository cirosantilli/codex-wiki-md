# Shattering coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical learning theory](statistical-learning-theory.md)

The shattering coefficient is

$$
s(\mathcal H,n)=\sup_{x_1,\ldots,x_n}
\left|\{(h(x_1),\ldots,h(x_n)):h\in\mathcal H\}\right|.
$$
