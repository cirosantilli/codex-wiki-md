# Escape and circular speeds inside an isolated uniform sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Escape velocity](escape-velocity.md)

For a sphere of radius $r_0$ and uniform [mass density](density.md) $\rho_0$, with no surrounding [mass](mass.md) and [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) zero at infinity, the [spherical shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md) gives $V_c^2=4\pi G\rho_0r^2/3$ inside. Matching to the exterior point-mass potential gives $\Phi(r)=-(2\pi G\rho_0/3)(3r_0^2-r^2)$. Since $v_e^2=-2\Phi$, the displayed relation follows for $0<r\leq r_0$. Exterior shells leave the interior [circular speed](circular-speed.md) unchanged but deepen the potential and change the [escape velocity](escape-velocity.md); a uniform core alone is insufficient to determine escape.
