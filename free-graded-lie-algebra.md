# Free graded Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Graded Lie algebra](graded-lie-algebra.md)

For a positively graded [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $V$, the free [graded Lie algebra](graded-lie-algebra.md) $\mathbb L(V)$ receives a linear map from $V$ and extends every graded linear map $V\to L$ uniquely to a Lie homomorphism. Over $\mathbb Q$, it is the Lie subalgebra generated by $V$ inside the [tensor algebra](tensor-algebra.md) $T(V)$, using $[u,v]=uv-(-1)^{|u||v|}vu$. Its [universal enveloping algebra](universal-enveloping-algebra.md) is $T(V)$: both have the same universal property for maps from $V$ into associative algebras. The primitives of the tensor [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md) with primitive generators are exactly $\mathbb L(V)$.
