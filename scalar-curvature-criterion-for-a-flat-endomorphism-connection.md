# Scalar-curvature criterion for a flat endomorphism connection

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature of an endomorphism bundle connection](curvature-of-an-endomorphism-bundle-connection.md)

The [endomorphism bundle connection](endomorphism-bundle-connection.md) of a rank-$r>0$ bundle is flat if and only if the original curvature satisfies $F_A=\omega\,\operatorname{id}$. By the [curvature of an endomorphism bundle connection](curvature-of-an-endomorphism-bundle-connection.md), flatness means each $F_A(X,Y)$ commutes with every fiber endomorphism. The centre of the full matrix algebra consists of scalar matrices, and $\omega=r^{-1}\operatorname{tr}F_A$ is the required smooth two-form. The original connection can still have nonzero scalar curvature.
