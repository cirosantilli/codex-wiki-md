# J-irreducible object of a site

↑ **Parent:** [Coverage on a category](coverage-on-a-category.md)

An object of a [site](site-category-theory.md) is J-irreducible when its only covering [sieve on a category](sieve-category-theory.md) is the maximal sieve. In particular the empty sieve cannot cover it. Every arrow from a J-irreducible object belongs to any covering sieve on its codomain: the pulled-back sieve must contain the identity. This makes the induced topology on the full subcategory of J-irreducible objects trivial.
