# Docosahexaenoic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Fatty acid](fatty-acid.md)

[docosahexaenoic acid](docosahexaenoic-acid.md) is a 22-carbon omega-3 fatty acid with six double bonds. Production and storage can require additional steps beyond an EPA-producing pathway.
