# Prime counting

↑ **Parent:** [Prime-counting function](prime-counting-function.md)

Prime counting evaluates the [prime-counting function](prime-counting-function.md): it determines how many [prime numbers](prime-number-split.md) do not exceed a given bound. A [number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md) can construct the whole table up to a moderate bound, while methods such as the [Lehmer prime-counting algorithm](lehmer-prime-counting-algorithm.md) group whole classes of [integers](integer-split.md) to evaluate much larger arguments without listing every [prime number](prime-number-split.md).
