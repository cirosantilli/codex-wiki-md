# Photonic-crystal defect localization

↑ **Parent:** [Photonic band gap](photonic-band-gap.md)

A local departure from periodicity can support a discrete mode inside a bulk [photonic band gap](photonic-band-gap.md). An isolated defect makes a cavity; a line of defects may guide light. Finite surrounding crystals allow leakage.
