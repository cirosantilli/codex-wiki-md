# Domain (mathematical analysis)

↑ **Parent:** [Open set](open-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_(mathematical_analysis))

A domain is a nonempty connected [open set](open-set.md) in the ambient space under discussion. In [complex analysis](complex-analysis-split.md) this normally means an open connected subset of $\mathbb C$. A [star-shaped set](star-shaped-set.md) that is open is a domain, but a domain need not be a [simply connected domain](simply-connected-domain.md) and can have a [period obstruction to a holomorphic antiderivative](period-obstruction-to-a-holomorphic-antiderivative.md).
