# Eutectic composition

↑ **Parent:** [Eutectic system](eutectic-system.md)

The eutectic composition is the liquid [concentration](concentration.md) at the [eutectic temperature](eutectic-temperature.md), where liquid and two solid phases coexist. It is a composition coordinate on a [phase diagram](phase-diagram.md), not the overall [concentration](concentration.md) of every sample in an [eutectic system](eutectic-system.md).
