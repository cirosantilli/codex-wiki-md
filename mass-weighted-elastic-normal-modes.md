# Mass-weighted elastic normal modes

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

For a conservative elastic body, the stiffness operator is a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) under compatible [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). The dynamical operator $\rho^{-1}\hat H$ is self-adjoint in the mass [inner product](inner-product-split.md) $\langle\mathbf u,\mathbf v\rangle_\rho=\int\rho\mathbf u^*\cdot\mathbf v\,dV$. Choosing mass-normalized [normal modes](normal-mode.md) converts the field equation into independent forced oscillators. If the [normal modes](normal-mode.md) have mass norm $N_\alpha$ instead, each projected force must be divided by $N_\alpha$.
