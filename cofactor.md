# Cofactor

↑ **Parent:** [Cofactor matrix](cofactor-matrix.md)

The [cofactor](cofactor.md) of a [matrix](matrix.md) entry is the signed [determinant](determinant.md) of the minor obtained by deleting its row and column. These scalars form the [cofactor matrix](cofactor-matrix.md), and their transpose forms the [adjugate matrix](adjugate-matrix.md). They give row and column expansions of the [determinant](determinant.md), including for singular [matrices](matrix.md).
