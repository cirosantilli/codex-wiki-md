# Thermal reduction of a two-mode squeezed vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Two-mode squeezed vacuum](two-mode-squeezed-vacuum.md)

Tracing out one mode leaves geometric occupation [probabilities](probability.md) with $q=|\lambda|^2$. In an oscillator basis of [frequency](frequency.md) $\omega$, they are [Boltzmann weights](boltzmann-factor.md) with $k_BT=\hbar\omega/\ln(1/q)$. The joint state can be pure while each reduced state is mixed.
