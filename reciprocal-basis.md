# Reciprocal basis

↑ **Parent:** [Multilinear algebra](multilinear-algebra.md)

For a basis $e_1,e_2,e_3$ with [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md) $\Delta$, the reciprocal vectors are cyclic cross products divided by $\Delta$ and satisfy $f_i\cdot e_j=\delta_{ij}$. This construction is used for the basis of a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md).
