<h1 id="metropolis-hastings-acceptance-probability">Metropolis–Hastings acceptance probability</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Metropolis–Hastings algorithm](metropolis-hastings-algorithm.md)

For target density $\pi$ and proposal density $q$, the Metropolis–Hastings acceptance probability is $1\wedge\{\pi(y)q(y,x)/(\pi(x)q(x,y))\}$.
