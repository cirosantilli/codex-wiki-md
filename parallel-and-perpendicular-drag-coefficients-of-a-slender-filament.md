# Parallel and perpendicular drag coefficients of a slender filament

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive-force theory](resistive-force-theory.md)

The local [resistive-force theory](resistive-force-theory.md) coefficients $\xi_\parallel$ and $\xi_\perp$ describe drag along and across a filament tangent. For very slender bodies their leading logarithmic values have ratio $\xi_\perp/\xi_\parallel\simeq2$. Geometry, ends and nonlocal fluid interactions affect the corrections. Anisotropy enables propulsion by rotating [helices](helix.md).
