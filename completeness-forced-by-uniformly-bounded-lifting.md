# Completeness forced by uniformly bounded lifting

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric correction for approximate surjectivity](geometric-correction-for-approximate-surjectivity.md)

If a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) from a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) onto a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) admits the displayed lift for every $y$, then its target is complete. Given a [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md), choose a subsequence with summable successive differences. Lift those differences to a summable sequence in the source, and use its convergent sum to produce a limit of the target subsequence. The original Cauchy sequence has the same limit.
