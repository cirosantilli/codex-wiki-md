# Scherzer defocus

↑ **Parent:** [Contrast transfer function](contrast-transfer-function.md)

An underfocus setting that balances objective-lens [spherical aberration](spherical-aberration.md) against defocus to provide a broad first phase-contrast band for a weak phase object. A common approximate convention is $\Delta f_S\simeq-1.2\sqrt{C_S\lambda}$ for positive $C_S$; numerical optimization conventions differ slightly.
