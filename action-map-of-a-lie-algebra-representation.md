# Action map of a Lie algebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of Lie algebra representations](tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md)

Give $\mathfrak g$ its [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). The representation identity gives $a(y\cdot(x\otimes v))=[y,x]v+xyv=y(xv)$, so the action map is a [Lie algebra representation homomorphism](lie-algebra-representation-homomorphism.md). For a nontrivial simple module it is surjective. If the acting algebra is semisimple and the module is finite dimensional, [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md) splits the surjection and makes $V$ a direct summand.
