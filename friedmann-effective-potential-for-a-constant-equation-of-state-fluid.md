# Friedmann effective potential for a constant-equation-of-state fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

For a [perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) of constant [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) $P=w\rho$, conservation gives $\rho=\rho_0a^{-3(1+w)}$. In units $8\pi G=1$, the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) becomes $\dot a^2/2+V(a)=0$. Allowed scales have $V\leq0$, and the [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) is $\ddot a=-V'(a)$. The curvature term shifts the potential vertically; positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) bends it downward and negative [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) bends it upward.
