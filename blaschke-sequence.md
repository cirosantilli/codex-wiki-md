# Blaschke sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Blaschke condition](blaschke-condition.md)

A sequence in the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), counted with [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md), is a Blaschke sequence when it satisfies the [Blaschke condition](blaschke-condition.md). The zeros of every nonzero bounded [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) form such a sequence: [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md) bounds $\sum_{|a_n|<r}\log(r/|a_n|)$ uniformly as $r\uparrow1$, and $1-|a|\le-\log|a|$ gives the assertion. There are only finitely many zeros at the origin, which can be factored out first.
