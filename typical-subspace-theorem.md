# Typical subspace theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum typical subspace](quantum-typical-subspace.md)

For each fixed $\delta>0$, the [quantum typical subspace](quantum-typical-subspace.md) projector selects eigenvalues of $\pi^{\otimes n}$ between $2^{-n(S(\pi)+\delta)}$ and $2^{-n(S(\pi)-\delta)}$. Its probability tends to one by the [weak law of large numbers](weak-law-of-large-numbers.md) for the spectral information variable. If its probability is at least $1-\epsilon$, its dimension is between $(1-\epsilon)2^{n(S(\pi)-\delta)}$ and $2^{n(S(\pi)+\delta)}$. These statements give the size and concentration estimates behind [Schumacher compression](schumacher-compression.md).
