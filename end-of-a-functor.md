# End of a functor

↑ **Parent:** [Categorical limit](categorical-limit.md)

For $T:\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}\times\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$, an end is a universal object $E$ with maps $e_C:E\to T(C,C)$ satisfying $T(1_C,f)e_C=T(f,1_D)e_D$ for every $f:C\to D$. Every other such family factors uniquely through $E$. For small $\mathcal C$ in a [complete category](complete-category.md), it is the [equalizer](equaliser.md) of the two maps $\prod_CT(C,C)\rightrightarrows\prod_{f:C\to D}T(C,D)$.
