# Convergent evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Evolution](evolution.md)

Independent evolution of similar outcomes in different lineages. Similar [phenotypes](phenotype.md) can arise through the same [gene](gene.md), different [genes](gene.md) in a shared pathway, or distinct mechanisms; independence of the evolutionary events must first be established.
