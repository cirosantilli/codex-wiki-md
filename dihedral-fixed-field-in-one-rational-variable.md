# Dihedral fixed field in one rational variable

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed field](fixed-field.md)

Automorphisms $\sigma(z)=\zeta_nz$ and $\tau(z)=1/z$ generate a [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of order $2n$. The invariant $w=z^n+z^{-n}$ has rational map degree $2n$, so $[\mathbb C(z):\mathbb C(w)]=2n$. The fixed-field theorem for a finite automorphism group gives the same degree over its [fixed field](fixed-field.md), proving equality. For odd prime $n$, the nontrivial proper subgroups are the rotation group and the $n$ reflection groups. Their fixed fields are $\mathbb C(z^n)$ and $\mathbb C(z+\zeta_n^{-j}/z)$ for reflections $\sigma^j\tau$, respectively.
