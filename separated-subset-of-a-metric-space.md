# Separated subset of a metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)

A subset is $r$-separated if distinct points have distance at least $r$. Such a subset is a packing by points; small balls around them have disjoint interiors at suitable radius. A large [separated set](separated-subset-of-a-metric-space.md) supplies many distinguishable alternatives in statistical lower-bound arguments.
