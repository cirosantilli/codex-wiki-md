# Binary mass-ratio reversal

↑ **Parent:** [Algol binary](algol-binary.md)

During binary mass-ratio reversal, an initially more massive [donor star](donor-star.md) loses enough material that its companion becomes the more massive member. The stars retain their different prior nuclear evolution, resolving the [Algol paradox](algol-paradox.md). [Conservative binary mass transfer](conservative-binary-mass-transfer.md) shrinks the orbit before equal masses and widens it after reversal, because $a\propto(M_dM_a)^{-2}$ at fixed total mass and orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
