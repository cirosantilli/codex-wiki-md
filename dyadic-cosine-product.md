# Dyadic cosine product

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic function of a uniform distribution](characteristic-function-of-a-uniform-distribution.md)

The [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) of a [Dyadic Rademacher series](dyadic-rademacher-series.md) is $\sin t/t$, interpreted as one at zero. The [characteristic function of a sum of independent variables](characteristic-function-of-a-sum-of-independent-variables.md) gives the finite cosine products, and [weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) gives their displayed limit for every real $t$. This argument includes zeros of the [sinc function](sinc-function.md) without taking logarithms of the factors.
