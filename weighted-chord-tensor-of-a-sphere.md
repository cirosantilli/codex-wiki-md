# Weighted chord tensor of a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric symmetric rank-two tensor](axisymmetric-symmetric-rank-two-tensor.md)

For $|y|=a$ and $n>-1$, this surface integral is finite: near $x=y$ its integrand has size $O(|x-y|^{2n})$ and the two-dimensional area element supplies one further power of distance. Rotational covariance and symmetry make it an [axisymmetric symmetric rank-two tensor](axisymmetric-symmetric-rank-two-tensor.md). Put $K=\pi(2a)^{2n+2}$. Polar integration about the $y$ axis gives $\operatorname{tr}T=K/(n+1)$ and $y^TTy=a^2K/(n+2)$. Solving $3\alpha+\beta a^2=\operatorname{tr}T$ and $\alpha+\beta a^2=y^TTy/a^2$ gives $\alpha=K/[2(n+1)(n+2)]$ and $\beta=\alpha(2n+1)/a^2$. Thus the weighted chord tensor becomes an [isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md) exactly at $n=-1/2$.
