# Stack alphabet

↑ **Parent:** [Alphabet](alphabet.md)

The finite [alphabet](alphabet.md) of symbols permitted on the [stack](stack-data-structure.md) of a [pushdown automaton](pushdown-automaton.md). It may contain [nonterminal symbols](nonterminal-symbol.md), [terminal symbols](terminal-symbol.md), and a fresh [stack bottom marker](stack-bottom-marker.md), independently of the input [alphabet](alphabet.md).
