# Angular fringe spacing of a transparent film

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film interference](thin-film-interference.md)

For a film of [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n$ and thickness $d$ in air, its round-trip propagation [phase](phase-waves.md) is $\delta=(4\pi d/\lambda_0)\sqrt{n^2-\sin^2\theta_i}$. Two neighbouring same-type [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md) differ by $2\pi$. At small angular spacing in radians,

$$
\Delta\theta_i\simeq\frac{\lambda_0\sqrt{n^2-\sin^2\theta_i}}{2d\sin\theta_i\cos\theta_i}
=\frac{\lambda_0 n\cos\theta}{2d\sin\theta_i\cos\theta_i}.
$$

The relation assumes slowly varying interface [reflection coefficients](reflection-coefficient.md) and no rapidly varying reflection phase. At normal incidence the first derivative vanishes, so a local linear angular-spacing formula must be replaced by a quadratic expansion.
