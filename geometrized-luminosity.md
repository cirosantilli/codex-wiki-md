# Geometrized luminosity

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrized units](geometrized-units.md)

A [geometrized luminosity](geometrized-luminosity.md) is dimensionless because in [geometrized units](geometrized-units.md) an energy and a time have the same dimension. Starting from SI [luminosity](luminosity.md), multiply by $G/c^5$. The corresponding SI power unit is $c^5/G$. Radiating a fraction $f$ of a mass $M$ at constant SI [luminosity](luminosity.md) $L$ takes $fMc^2/L$, or $f(GM/c^3)/L_{\rm geom}$ in seconds.
