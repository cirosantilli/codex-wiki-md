# Ulam matrix on omega-one

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary set](stationary-set.md)

A family $(A_{\alpha,n})_{\alpha<\omega_1,n<\omega}$ whose row at $\alpha$ partitions $(\alpha,\omega_1)$ and whose fixed-column cells are pairwise disjoint. Choose [injections](injective-function.md) $e_\beta:\beta\to\omega$ and put $A_{\alpha,n}=\{\beta>\alpha:e_\beta(\alpha)=n\}$. This gives a matrix used to split [stationary sets](stationary-set.md) into many stationary pieces.
