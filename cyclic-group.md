# Cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_group)

A cyclic group is generated by one element. A cyclic group of order $n$ has exactly $d$ solutions of $x^d=1$ for every positive [integer divisor](divisor.md) $d$ of $n$.
