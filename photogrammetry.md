# Photogrammetry

↑ **Parent:** [Remote sensing](remote-sensing.md)

Recovery of object positions and shape from photographs with overlapping views. Known or estimated camera geometry and matched image features constrain three-dimensional point positions. A metric reconstruction requires scale and a coordinate reference; image pixel size is not automatically the positional accuracy of the reconstructed surface. [Stereo photogrammetry](stereo-photogrammetry.md) and [structure-from-motion photogrammetry](structure-from-motion-photogrammetry.md) use related geometry with different acquisition and camera-estimation strategies.
