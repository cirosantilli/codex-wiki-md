# Hexagonal close packing

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal](crystal.md)

Close packing of triangular layers with ABAB stacking. Its nearest-layer geometry agrees with [face-centered cubic](face-centered-cubic.md) stacking, but the further-layer registry differs.
