# Vertical bound pair in the point-force sedimentation model

↑ **Parent:** [Leading interaction of two sedimenting spheres](leading-interaction-of-two-sedimenting-spheres.md)

If $\delta D<0$, the [leading interaction of two sedimenting spheres](leading-interaction-of-two-sedimenting-spheres.md) has a formal vertical [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) at $r_*=-3D/(2\delta)$. Choose labels with $\delta>0,D<0$ and put [sphere](sphere.md) one above [sphere](sphere.md) two. On that invariant vertical line, $\dot r=-\delta+\delta r_*/r$, so both sides approach $r_*$ and the radial [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $-\delta/r_*$. A small tilt instead grows at rate $\delta/(2r_*)$: the full configuration-space [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is a saddle, not an attracting node. A physically valid far-field bound pair requires $r_*\gg a_1+a_2$, or $|D|\gg(a_1+a_2)|\delta|$. Reversing both driving [forces](force.md) reverses the [vector](vector.md) field, interchanging radial attraction and repulsion, consistent with [kinematic reversibility of Stokes flow](kinematic-reversibility-of-stokes-flow.md).
