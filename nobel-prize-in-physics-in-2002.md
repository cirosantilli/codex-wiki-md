# Nobel Prize in Physics in 2002

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

One quarter each went to [Raymond Davis Jr.](raymond-davis-jr.md) and [Masatoshi Koshiba](masatoshi-koshiba.md) for pioneering [astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md) through detection of cosmic [neutrinos](neutrino.md). One half went to [Riccardo Giacconi](riccardo-giacconi.md) for pioneering [astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md) leading to the discovery of [cosmic X-ray sources](cosmic-x-ray-source.md).[Individual official 2002 summary: Davis and Koshiba one quarter each for cosmic neutrinos; Giacconi one half for cosmic X-ray sources.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2002-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
