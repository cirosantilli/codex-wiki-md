# Conjugacy separable group

↑ **Parent:** [Residually finite group](residually-finite-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugacy_separable_group)

A group $G$ is conjugacy separable when any two nonconjugate elements remain nonconjugate in some finite quotient. Equivalently, every [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) is closed in the [profinite topology](profinite-topology.md).
