# Froude number

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity current](gravity-current.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Froude_number)

The Froude number compares inertial speed with a gravity-wave speed. For a shallow [gravity current](gravity-current.md) of depth $h$ and [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $g'$, $\operatorname{Fr}=U/\sqrt{g'h}$.
