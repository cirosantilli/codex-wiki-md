# Mendelian IBD sharing of full siblings

↑ **Parent:** [Identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md)

In an [outbred pedigree](outbred-pedigree.md), the two [full siblings](full-sibling.md) receive the same paternal copy with probability $1/2$, and independently the same maternal copy with probability $1/2$. Thus the number $J$ of shared copies has a [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) with probabilities $(1/4,1/2,1/4)$, and $E[J/2]=1/2$.
