# Normal-normal credibility with unequal exposures

↑ **Parent:** [Normal-normal credibility](normal-normal-credibility.md)

For conditionally independent annual averages $X_j\mid\theta\sim N(\theta,v/m_j)$ and prior $\theta\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$, the [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) is $Z\overline X_w+(1-Z)\mu$, with $\overline X_w=\sum_jm_jX_j/W$. Completing the quadratic [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) gives posterior precision $\sigma^{-2}+W/v$. This is an exact [Bayesian credibility](bayesian-credibility.md) version of the [Bühlmann–Straub credibility estimate](buhlmann-straub-credibility-estimate.md). Increasing the between-risk prior variance increases the experience weight; increasing process variance decreases it. The next total pure premium is $m_{n+1}$ times the estimated per-life mean.
