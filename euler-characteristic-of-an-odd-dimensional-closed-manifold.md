# Euler characteristic of an odd-dimensional closed manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md)

Every closed odd-dimensional manifold has Euler characteristic zero. For an orientable manifold this follows by pairing complementary Betti numbers using [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md); the general case follows from the orientable double cover and [Euler characteristic under a finite covering](euler-characteristic-under-a-finite-covering.md).
