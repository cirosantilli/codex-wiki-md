# Biproduct-induced addition of morphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Biproduct](biproduct.md)

In a [pointed category](pointed-category.md) with finite [product in a category](product-category-theory.md) and [coproducts in a category](coproduct.md) and invertible canonical maps $c:A+B\to A\times B$, the unique [commutative-monoid enrichment](commutative-monoid-enrichment.md) is $f+g=[1_B,1_B]c_{B,B}^{-1}\langle f,g\rangle$. Associativity and commutativity follow by comparing fold maps on triple and swapped coproduct injections. Composition distributes by the universal properties. For uniqueness, bilinearity forces $i_1p_1+i_2p_2=1$ on each biproduct, and composing with the fold and the paired morphism forces the addition formula.
