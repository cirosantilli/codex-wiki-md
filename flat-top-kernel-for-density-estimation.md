# Flat-top kernel for density estimation

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel for density estimation](kernel-for-density-estimation.md)

Choose a real even smooth compactly supported [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) $\eta$ equal to one near zero and let $K$ be its inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md). Repeated integration by parts makes $K$ a [Schwartz function](schwartz-function.md). It is real, integrable and square-integrable, with unit integral. [Moment differentiation of the Fourier transform](moment-differentiation-of-the-fourier-transform.md) gives $\int x^jK(x)dx=i^j\eta^{(j)}(0)=0$ for every positive integer $j$. Such a [kernel for density estimation](kernel-for-density-estimation.md) therefore satisfies arbitrary finite-order moment cancellation, and necessarily takes negative values. If the transform plateau contains the frequency support of a [bandlimited function](bandlimited-function.md) $f$, the [convolution theorem](convolution-theorem.md) proves $K*f=f$.
