# Triangular-channel hydrostatic pressure head

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md)

For a fixed symmetric triangular channel with depth $H$ and [area](surface-area.md) $A=kH^2$, the hydrostatic first moment is $I=\int_A(H-z)\,dA=kH^3/3=AH/3$. Since $dI/dH=A$, the depth-averaged [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) is $gI_x/A=gH_x$. A mean geometric depth $H/2$ cannot replace $H$ in that gradient without an extra factor two. This distinction prevents confusion between [pressure](pressure-split.md) head, centroid depth, and [hydraulic radius](hydraulic-radius.md).
