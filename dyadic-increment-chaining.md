# Dyadic increment chaining

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov continuity theorem](kolmogorov-continuity-theorem.md)

For a function on the [dyadic rationals](dyadic-rational.md) in $[0,1]$, let $A_n$ be its largest absolute adjacent increment on the level-$n$ grid. If $K_\alpha<\infty$ for $\alpha>0$, the function extends uniquely to a [Hölder continuous function](holder-condition.md) with

$$
|X_t-X_s|\leq K_\alpha|t-s|^\alpha.
$$

Choose $n$ with $2^{-n}\leq|t-s|<2^{1-n}$. The level-$n$ left approximations differ by at most two grid steps, and each finer approximation adds at most one level increment. Thus the difference is bounded by $2\sum_{j\geq n}A_j\leq2^{-n\alpha}K_\alpha$. For a [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) with $\|\xi_t-\xi_s\|_p\leq C|t-s|^\beta$, the bound $\|A_n\|_p\leq C2^{-n(\beta-1/p)}$ makes this series converge in the [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) for $0<\alpha<\beta-1/p$.
