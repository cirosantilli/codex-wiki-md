# Ratio definition of cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Cutoff for Markov chains](cutoff-for-markov-chains.md)

The ratio convention for [cutoff for Markov chains](cutoff-for-markov-chains.md) requires $t_{\mathrm{mix}}^{(n)}(\varepsilon)/t_{\mathrm{mix}}^{(n)}(1-\varepsilon)\to1$ for every fixed $0<\varepsilon<1/2$, with positive denominators eventually. Equivalently, any two fixed levels have asymptotically equal [mixing times](mixing-time-of-a-markov-chain.md). This convention permits a bounded-time jump; definitions which additionally require diverging [mixing times](mixing-time-of-a-markov-chain.md) exclude such a jump. For nonlazy [simple random walk](simple-random-walk.md) on the [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $K_n$, every fixed-level [mixing time](mixing-time-of-a-markov-chain.md) is eventually one.
