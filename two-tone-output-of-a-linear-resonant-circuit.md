# Two-tone output of a linear resonant circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Beat](beat.md)

A linear time-invariant circuit sends each sinusoidal input to an output at the same [frequency](frequency.md), multiplied by its complex transfer function. Two inputs therefore give the sum of two phase-shifted [sinusoids](sinusoid.md). If their output [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) are equal, the sum factors into a carrier at the mean [frequency](frequency.md) and a signed cosine envelope at half the [frequency](frequency.md) difference. The envelope magnitude repeats at the full difference [frequency](frequency.md). Unequal [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) give a nonzero minimum; a nonlinear detector is needed to generate a separate difference-frequency signal.
