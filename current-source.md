# Current source

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_source)

An ideal current source prescribes an [electric current](electric-current.md); its [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) is determined by the connected [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md). A [dependent electrical source](dependent-electrical-source.md) can prescribe a current controlled by another circuit variable instead.
