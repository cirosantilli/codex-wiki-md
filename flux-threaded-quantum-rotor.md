# Flux-threaded quantum rotor

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum rotor](quantum-rotor.md)

For the Gaussian-unit flux term $e\Phi_B\dot\phi/(2\pi c)$, [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) is shifted by $e\Phi_B/(2\pi c)$. Periodic wavefunctions then give the displayed spectrum for integer $n$. The flux period is $hc/|e|$ in these units; the corresponding SI expressions omit $c$. A gauge change transfers the flux to a [twisted boundary condition](twisted-boundary-condition.md) rather than removing it from the physical problem. Half-integer $f$ gives a pair of degenerate nearest-momentum levels.
