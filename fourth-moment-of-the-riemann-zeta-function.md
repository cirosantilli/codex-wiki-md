# Fourth moment of the Riemann zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

The [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md) satisfies $\int_0^T|\zeta(1/2+it)|^4\,dt\ll T(\log T)^4$ for $T\ge2$. Squaring a short [Dirichlet polynomial](dirichlet-polynomial.md) produces the [divisor function](divisor-function.md); the [divisor-square summatory bound](divisor-square-summatory-bound.md) controls the off-diagonal and diagonal terms.
