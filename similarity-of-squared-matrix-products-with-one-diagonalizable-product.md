# Similarity of squared matrix products with one diagonalizable product

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal polynomials of AB and BA](minimal-polynomials-of-ab-and-ba.md)

The identity $(BA)^{j+1}=B(AB)^jA$ gives $m_{BA}(t)\mid t\,m_{AB}(t)$. If $AB$ is [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md), its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) has simple roots. Hence all nonzero Jordan blocks of $BA$ have size one and its zero blocks have size at most two. Squaring kills these nilpotent blocks, making both squared products diagonalizable. Their eigenvalue multisets agree by [Characteristic polynomials of AB and BA](characteristic-polynomials-of-ab-and-ba.md), so the squared products are [similar matrices](matrix-similarity.md).
