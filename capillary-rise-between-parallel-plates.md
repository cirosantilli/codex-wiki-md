# Capillary rise between parallel plates

↑ **Parent:** [Capillary action](capillary-action.md)

Two wide vertical plates separated by gap $d$ provide two contact lines per unit width. Upward surface-tension [force](force-split.md) $2\gamma\cos\theta$ balances column weight $\Delta\rho gdh$. Compared with a circular tube of radius $r$, the height ratio is $r/d$ for the same [fluid](fluid.md) and [contact angle](contact-angle.md). Equal plate gap and tube diameter give half the tube rise.
