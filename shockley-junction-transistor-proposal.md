# Shockley junction transistor proposal

↑ **Parent:** [Bipolar junction transistor](bipolar-junction-transistor.md)

[William Shockley](william-shockley.md)'s proposal used transport through the bulk of a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md), rather than coupling between two surface points. The base must be thin enough for injected [minority carriers](minority-carrier.md) to reach the collector. Two separately wired [diodes](diode.md) do not supply that shared transport region. The proposal was a design principle; producing a working device required further control of material properties.[Printed pp. 368–370](transistor-technology-evokes-new-physics-by-william-shockley.md)
