# Three-sphere bundle over the four-sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md)

For an oriented rank-four real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E\to S^4$, its [sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md) has fibre $S^3$. Let $m=\langle e(E),[S^4]\rangle$ be its [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md) evaluated on the orientation class. The [Gysin sequence](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) gives integral [homology](homology-split.md) $\mathbb Z$ in degrees $0,7$, $\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z$ in degree $3$, and a copy of $\mathbb Z$ in degree $4$ exactly when $m=0$, with all other groups zero. Here $\mathbb Z/0\mathbb Z=\mathbb Z$. A transition function $v\mapsto q^k vq^l$ on the [unit quaternions](unit-quaternion.md) has Euler number $k+l$, up to an overall orientation sign: evaluating it at a fixed unit vector gives the power map $q\mapsto q^{k+l}$, whose [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) is $k+l$. The same homology calculation follows by applying the [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) to the two trivializations over the hemispheres.
