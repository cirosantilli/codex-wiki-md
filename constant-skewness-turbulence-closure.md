# Constant-skewness turbulence closure

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence closure](turbulence-closure.md)

This [turbulence closure](turbulence-closure.md) assumes the [velocity increment](velocity-increment.md) [skewness](skewness.md) is the same throughout the [equilibrium range](equilibrium-range.md), with its value fixed by the [Kolmogorov two-thirds law](kolmogorov-two-thirds-law.md) and [Kolmogorov four-fifths law](kolmogorov-four-fifths-law.md). It yields a simple crossover between differentiable and inertial-scale increments, but suppresses the scale dependence of [skewness](skewness.md) and has no independent spectral-transfer dynamics.
