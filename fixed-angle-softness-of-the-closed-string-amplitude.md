# Fixed-angle softness of the closed-string amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro–Shapiro amplitude](virasoro-shapiro-amplitude.md)

For $t\simeq-as$, $u\simeq-(1-a)s$ with $0<a<1$, the smooth high-energy envelope satisfies $\log|A|\simeq-2s[-a\log a-(1-a)\log(1-a)]$. The [Gamma function](gamma-function.md) asymptotics cancel the leading $s\log s$ terms, leaving exponential suppression. Exact real-axis resonance poles must be excluded or treated by analytic continuation.
