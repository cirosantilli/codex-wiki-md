# Short-rate diffusion hedging

↑ **Parent:** [Short-rate bond pricing equation](short-rate-bond-pricing-equation.md)

In a [one-factor short-rate model](one-factor-short-rate-model.md), a derivative $V(t,r)$ can be hedged locally by a nondegenerate traded [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) $P(t,r;T_1)$. Match Brownian exposures by holding $V_r/P_r$ bonds and putting the remaining value into the [bank account](bank-account.md). Substitution into the two pricing equations proves the gain identity. This requires $\eta P_r\ne0$; completeness at a degenerate boundary cannot be inferred from the dimension count alone.
