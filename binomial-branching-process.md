# Binomial branching process

↑ **Parent:** [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md)

A [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md) whose offspring law is a [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) $\operatorname{Bin}(n,p)$ has mean $np$ and the displayed offspring [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md). Its [branching extinction probability](extinction-probability-of-a-branching-process.md) is the smallest root of $q=f(q)$ in $[0,1]$. It dominates a [breadth-first exploration of a binomial random graph](breadth-first-exploration-of-a-binomial-random-graph.md) by allowing each explored [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) up to $n$ independent possible children, including previously exposed or already discovered [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) as phantom children.
