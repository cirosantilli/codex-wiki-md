# Zero-capital survival probability

↑ **Parent:** [Classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md)

In a [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md) with positive [relative safety loading](relative-safety-loading.md), zero initial capital has ultimate [survival probability](survival-probability.md) $1-\lambda\mu/c$, where $\lambda$ is the claim-arrival rate, $\mu$ the claim [expected value](expected-value.md) and $c$ the premium rate. It depends on the claim law through its mean alone. It is not the event of never receiving a claim: premium accumulates between arrivals.
