# Pressure-balanced plasma perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic magnetohydrodynamics](kinetic-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

A low-[frequency](frequency.md), strongly oblique [plasma](plasma-physics.md) perturbation in which perpendicular [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) equilibrates rapidly compared with the retained dynamics. In [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md), its leading total transverse [pressure](pressure-split.md) perturbation vanishes as shown. Thus $\delta B/B_0=-(\beta/2)\delta p_\perp/p_0$, where $\beta$ is [plasma beta](plasma-beta.md). A small fractional [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) perturbation can still exert a leading [force](force-split.md).
