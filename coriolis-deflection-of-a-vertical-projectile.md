# Coriolis deflection of a vertical projectile

↑ **Parent:** [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md)

At colatitude $\theta$, ignore terms quadratic in Earth angular speed and use uniform vertical gravity. A vertically launched particle has zeroth-order height $z=vt-gt^2/2$. Its eastward [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) is $-2\omega\sin\theta\,\dot z$, so its horizontal velocity is $-2\omega\sin\theta\,z$. Integrating over the flight time $2v/g$ gives the westward displacement displayed above. Descent reverses the horizontal acceleration but does not cancel the accumulated displacement.
