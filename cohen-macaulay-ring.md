# Cohen-Macaulay ring

↑ **Parent:** [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohen-Macaulay_ring)

A commutative Noetherian ring is Cohen-Macaulay when every localization at a prime has [depth of a Noetherian local ring](depth-of-a-noetherian-local-ring.md) equal to its Krull dimension. A regular local ring is Cohen-Macaulay, and quotienting one by a nonzero divisor preserves Cohen-Macaulayness while lowering dimension and depth by one. In particular a hypersurface in a regular ambient variety satisfies the depth condition $(S_2)$ in the [Serre criterion for normality](serre-s-criterion-for-normality.md).
