# Restricted unitary group

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary group](unitary-group.md)

For an orthogonal projection $P$ on a complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), this is the group of unitary operators whose off-diagonal blocks relative to $PH\oplus(I-P)H$ are [Hilbert-Schmidt operators](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md). Its usual topology combines strong operator convergence with Hilbert-Schmidt convergence of $[P,u]$. It is the group implementable in the corresponding [polarized fermionic Fock space](polarized-fermionic-fock-space.md).
