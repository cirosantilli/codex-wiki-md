# Rotationally symmetric surface metric

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric-split.md)

An intrinsic two-dimensional metric locally written $ds^2=dr^2+f(r)^2d\phi^2$ with positive $f$. Rotation of the angular coordinate is an [isometry](isometry.md). Such a metric need not have a global realization as a surface of revolution in three-dimensional Euclidean space.
