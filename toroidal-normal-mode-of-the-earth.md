# Toroidal normal mode of the Earth

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

A [toroidal normal mode of the Earth](toroidal-normal-mode-of-the-earth.md) is a free oscillation with tangential, divergence-free displacement. It is the spherical counterpart of the transverse shear motion in a [Love wave](love-wave.md). A bounded spherical [Earth](earth.md) has discrete angular orders and [eigenfrequencies](eigenfrequency.md), so the arbitrarily low-frequency continuum of a flat infinite layer is not a literal statement about global nonrigid oscillations.
