# Prime lattice ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice ideal](lattice-ideal.md)

A proper [lattice ideal](lattice-ideal.md) $I$ is prime if $x\wedge y\in I$ implies $x\in I$ or $y\in I$. Its complement is a [prime filter of a distributive lattice](prime-filter-of-a-distributive-lattice.md) when the lattice is distributive: downward closure of the ideal makes the complement upward closed, primeness makes it meet-closed, and join closure makes that filter prime. Equivalently, its complement indicator is a [homomorphism of lattices](homomorphism-of-lattices.md) to the two-element lattice. Given $b\nleq a$ in a distributive lattice, maximize an ideal containing $a$ and avoiding $b$ using [Zorn lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md). If $x,y$ lie outside it, the two enlarged ideals contain $b$, so $b\leq i\vee x$ and $b\leq j\vee y$ for some $i,j\in I$. Distributivity shows that $x\wedge y\in I$ would force $b\in I$, a contradiction. Thus this maximal avoiding ideal is prime and separates the two specified elements.
