# Covariance of two uniform order statistics

↑ **Parent:** [Order statistic](order-statistic.md)

For two [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) with the [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $[0,1]$, put $U=\min(X,Y)$ and $V=\max(X,Y)$. Then $P(U>u)=(1-u)^2$, so $\mathbb EU=1/3$. Since $U+V=X+Y$ and $UV=XY$, $\mathbb EV=2/3$ and $\mathbb E(UV)=1/4$. Thus their [covariance](covariance.md) is $1/36$, although the original variables are independent. Rescaling the interval to $[a,b]$ multiplies the covariance by $(b-a)^2$.
