# Dark-field transmission electron microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Objective aperture](objective-aperture.md)

[Dark-field transmission electron microscopy](dark-field-transmission-electron-microscopy.md) selects a diffracted beam while excluding the central beam. Regions contributing to that selected [electron diffraction](electron-diffraction.md) beam can appear bright.[Definition: selected diffracted beam and bright contributing regions](dark-field-image-by-jeol.md)
