# Jacobi fields from conjugation at a central endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md)

For a [Lie group](lie-group.md) with a [bi-invariant Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-riemannian-metric.md), vary $\gamma(t)=e^{tA}$ by conjugation: $F(s,t)=e^{sX}e^{tA}e^{-sX}$. Its [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) is $J_X(t)=Xe^{tA}-e^{tA}X$. If $e^A$ is central, all these fields vanish at both endpoints. Their space has dimension $\dim\mathfrak g-\dim\mathfrak z(A)$, since the kernel is the [centralizer of an element of a Lie algebra](centralizer-of-an-element-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
