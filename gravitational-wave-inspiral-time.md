# Gravitational-wave inspiral time

↑ **Parent:** [Circular gravitational-wave inspiral](circular-gravitational-wave-inspiral.md)

The leading circular, constant-mass [gravitational-wave inspiral time](gravitational-wave-inspiral-time.md) follows by integrating the quadrupole radiation-reaction equation $\dot A=-64G^3\mu M^2/(5c^5A^3)$. It assumes initially weak gravity and neglects eccentricity, mass exchange and finite contact separation. It is $3/8$ of the associated instantaneous period-decay timescale.
