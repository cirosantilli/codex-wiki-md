# Sum of mutually orthogonal projectors

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md)

If [idempotent](idempotent.md) [matrices](matrix.md) $P_1,\ldots,P_k$ satisfy $P_iP_j=0$ whenever $i\ne j$, then $(\sum_iP_i)^2=\sum_{i,j}P_iP_j=\sum_iP_i$. Thus the sum is [idempotent](idempotent.md). For a real [orthonormal set](orthonormal-set.md) $q_i$, the [matrices](matrix.md) $P_i=q_iq_i^T$ meet this condition. Their sum is the [orthogonal projector](orthogonal-projection.md) onto their [span](linear-span.md); the [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) property follows by transposing the sum. Arbitrary sums of [idempotent](idempotent.md) [matrices](matrix.md) need not be [idempotent](idempotent.md).
