# Subpartition of unity for B-splines

↑ **Parent:** [B-spline](b-spline.md)

A finite collection of the standard partition-normalized [B-splines](b-spline.md) is nonnegative and has sum at most one on the entire real line. Extend the knot sequence in both directions. The full order-one collection sums to one, and summing the [Cox-de Boor recurrence](cox-de-boor-recursion-formula.md) preserves that sum because the two coefficients of each lower-order function add to one. The finite collection is a subset. On the basic knot interval the finite sum equals one, also following from [Marsden's identity](marsden-identity.md).
