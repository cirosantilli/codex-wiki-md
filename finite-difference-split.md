# Finite difference

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_difference)

A finite difference compares the values of a [function](function-split.md) at finitely separated arguments. For example, the forward finite difference with step $h$ is

$$
\Delta_hf(x)=f(x+h)-f(x).
$$

It is the discrete analogue of a [derivative](derivative.md) and lowers the degree of a nonconstant [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) by one.
