# Finite CW complex

↑ **Parent:** [CW complex](cw-complex.md)

A finite [CW complex](cw-complex.md) has finitely many cells. Its [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) consists of finitely generated free [abelian groups](abelian-group.md) and vanishes outside finitely many degrees, so its [integral homology](integral-homology.md) groups are finitely generated.
