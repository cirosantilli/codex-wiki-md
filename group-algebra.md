# Group algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_algebra)

The group algebra $k[G]$ is the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with basis $G$ and multiplication obtained by extending the group law $k$-bilinearly.
