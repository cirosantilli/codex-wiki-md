# RT Lac

↑ **Parent:** [Semidetached binary](semidetached-binary.md)

RT Lac is a named interacting binary used in studies of [binary star](binary-star.md) evolution. Conservative-progenitor tests and [nonconservative mass transfer](nonconservative-binary-mass-transfer.md) provide ways to interpret a stripped, mass-losing component.
