# Collinearity

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collinearity)

Points are collinear when they lie on a single [line](straight-line.md). Two [vectors](vector.md) are collinear when their [linear span](linear-span.md) has [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) at most one. For a nonzero vector $v$, this means that the other vector is $\lambda v$ for some [scalar](scalar.md) $\lambda$. This algebraic convention includes the zero vector; the directional use of “parallel” sometimes assumes both vectors are nonzero.
