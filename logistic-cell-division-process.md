# Logistic cell-division process

↑ **Parent:** [Birth process](birth-process.md)

For a carrying capacity $N$, each of $k$ cells splits into two at rate $1-k/N$, so the population increases by one at the displayed total rate and is absorbed at $N$. Starting from population density $p\in(0,1)$, its normalized density converges on finite horizons to $p e^t/(1-p+p e^t)$ by the [Kurtz fluid limit theorem](kurtz-fluid-limit-theorem.md).
