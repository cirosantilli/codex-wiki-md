# Homologous recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md)

Exchange or repair using corresponding [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequences as templates. It can restore a broken molecule and can yield crossover or noncrossover products; sequence repair and reshuffling of inherited [alleles](allele.md) are compatible outcomes.
