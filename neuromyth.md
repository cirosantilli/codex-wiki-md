# Neuromyth

↑ **Parent:** [Educational neuroscience](educational-neuroscience.md)

A misconception presented as a consequence of brain knowledge or used as a brain-based educational explanation. Not every unsupported educational claim is specifically neuroscientific, so survey categories and definitions need examination.
