# Half-density

↑ **Parent:** [Line bundle](line-bundle.md)

A [half-density](half-density.md) transforms by the square root of the absolute Jacobian under a coordinate change. The product of a [half-density](half-density.md) with its complex conjugate is an integrable density, so its [norm](norm.md) is coordinate-independent without choosing a separate volume form. [Half-densities](half-density.md) provide an intrinsic configuration-space [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) for a real cotangent polarization.
