# Massive induced representation of the Poincare double cover

↑ **Parent:** [Wigner's classification](wigner-s-classification.md)

Fix $m>0$, $k=(m,\mathbf0)$, and a rotationless [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) $L(p)$ taking $k$ to $p$ on the positive-energy [mass shell](mass-shell.md). The [little group](little-group.md) on the [Lorentz spinor double cover](lorentz-spinor-double-cover.md) is [SU(2)](su-2-group.md). Its spin-$s$ irreducible representation induces a unitary representation on $L^2(d^3p/(2p^0))\otimes\mathbb C^{2s+1}$. Here $W(\Lambda,p)=L(\Lambda p)^{-1}\Lambda L(p)$ is the [Wigner rotation](wigner-rotation.md), lifted to [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) when necessary, and the translation convention is $U(I,a)|p\rangle=e^{ip\cdot a}|p\rangle$. The little-group cocycle proves the group law, and [Wigner's classification](wigner-s-classification.md) gives irreducibility. Every nonnegative integer or half-integer spin is allowed on the double cover; descent to the ordinary proper orthochronous [Poincaré group](poincare-group.md) requires integer spin, because its $2\pi$ rotation must act as the identity.
