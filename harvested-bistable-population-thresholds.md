# Harvested bistable population thresholds

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-effort harvesting](constant-effort-harvesting.md)

For $\dot N=N[P(N)-E]$ and $P(N)=\prod_{i=1}^3(a_i-N)$ with $0<a_1<a_2<a_3$, extinction changes stability at $E_T$, while the upper stable population and an unstable separator merge at $E_M$. If $E_T<E_M$, an interval of efforts supports stable extinction and a stable positive population simultaneously; at $E_M$ the positive state collapses. If the thresholds have the opposite order, the high-population pair disappears before the lower stable branch reaches extinction. At a positive [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) stability follows from the sign of $NP'(N)$.
