# Line bundle associated to a divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

For a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$, the associated line bundle $\mathcal O(D)$ has local sections given by [meromorphic functions](meromorphic-function.md) $f$ satisfying $(f)+D\geq0$. Its canonical meromorphic section has divisor $D$.
