# Stellar radius from transit duration and a linear mass-radius relation

↑ **Parent:** [Total duration of a circular planetary transit](total-duration-of-a-circular-planetary-transit.md)

Assume [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md), a negligible planet [mass](mass.md), and $M_\star/M_\odot=R_\star/R_\odot$. Define $A_\odot=[(GM)_\odot P^2/(4\pi^2)]^{1/3}/R_\odot$. The [total duration of a circular planetary transit](total-duration-of-a-circular-planetary-transit.md) determines $a/R_\star$, giving the displayed [stellar radius](stellar-radius.md). Unknown [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md), [transit dilution](transit-dilution.md) or a different stellar mass-radius calibration introduces additional uncertainty.
