# Linearized chord diagram model

↑ **Parent:** [Preferential attachment](preferential-attachment.md)

Start with one [self-loop](loop-graph-theory.md), and at step $t$ add one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) and one [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). An old [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) $i$ is chosen with [probability](probability.md) $d_i(t-1)/(2t-1)$, and the new [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) is chosen with [probability](probability.md) $1/(2t-1)$, producing a [self-loop](loop-graph-theory.md). [Self-loops](loop-graph-theory.md) contribute two to the [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md). There are $n$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and total [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) $2n$ at time $n$. This exact normalization is distinct from treating the total [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) as the number of [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md).
