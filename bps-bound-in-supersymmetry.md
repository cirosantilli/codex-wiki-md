# BPS bound in supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Central charge in supersymmetry](central-charge-in-supersymmetry.md)

Use $\{Q^A_\alpha,Q^B_\beta\}=\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}Z^{AB}$. [Unitary skew-diagonalization of an antisymmetric matrix](unitary-skew-diagonalization-of-an-antisymmetric-matrix.md) gives two-by-two blocks with entries $Z_r=2z_r$. At rest, the paired [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) have [anticommutators](anticommutator.md) $2(M\pm|z_r|)$. The squared [norms](norm.md) of an operator and its adjoint sum to the expectation of their [anticommutator](anticommutator.md), so positive [norm](norm.md) requires $M\ge|z_r|$ for every block. A convention with $2Z$ in the algebra instead writes $M\ge\max|Z_r|$.
