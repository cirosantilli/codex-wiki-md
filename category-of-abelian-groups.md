# Category of abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian category](abelian-category.md)

Objects are [abelian groups](abelian-group.md) and morphisms are [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md). Addition on hom-sets is pointwise, kernels are subgroup kernels, and cokernels are quotients by images. Thus this is an [abelian category](abelian-category.md). Products are Cartesian products; coproducts are direct sums of finite-support families. The underlying-set [functor](functor.md) creates directed colimits, giving a concrete description of their elements by eventual equality of representatives.
