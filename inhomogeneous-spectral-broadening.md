# Inhomogeneous spectral broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line broadening](spectral-line-broadening.md)

An ensemble-wide distribution of [resonance](resonance.md) frequencies, such as velocity-dependent Doppler shifts or spatially varying static-field shifts. The observed profile averages the individual responses. A velocity class need not have the full width of the ensemble's [thermal Doppler broadening](thermal-doppler-broadening.md).
