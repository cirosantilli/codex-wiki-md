<h1 id="los-theorem">Łoś theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ultraproduct](ultraproduct.md)

This theorem characterizes truth in an [ultraproduct](ultraproduct.md). Łoś's theorem says that a first-order formula holds in an ultraproduct exactly when it holds in a set of factors belonging to the ultrafilter.
