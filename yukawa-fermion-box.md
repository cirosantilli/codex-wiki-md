# Yukawa fermion box

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion loop](fermion-loop.md)

Four scalar [Yukawa interactions](yukawa-interaction.md) on a closed [fermion loop](fermion-loop.md) generate a scalar four-point [one-particle-irreducible vertex](one-particle-irreducible-vertex.md). At high momentum four [dirac propagators](dirac-propagator.md) contribute $k^{-4}$, leaving a logarithmic four-dimensional [ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md). Its local scalar-fourth-power term requires a quartic scalar coupling; scalar mass and field [counterterms](counterterm.md) cannot cancel it.
