# British Pharmacopoeia by the General Medical Council (1864)

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology and toxicology bibliography](pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography-split.md)

[Wellcome catalogue identifying the 1864 first edition](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/nd3cg4gk).

Digitized original: [https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ef/British_pharmacopoeia_%28electronic_resource%29_%28IA_b20405558%29.pdf](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ef/British_pharmacopoeia_%28electronic_resource%29_%28IA_b20405558%29.pdf)

Cited primary monograph: Morphiæ Hydrochloras, Characters and Tests, pp. 93–94. Its facsimile is reproduced on [PDF page 37 of the 2022 Hong Kong Society for Quality presentation](https://hksq.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Quality-Sustainability-AS-Watson-220826b.pdf#page=37); the indexed visual description of that reproduction was inspected. The original whole PDF exceeded the browser retrieval limit. The 1864 edition is kept distinct from the 1867 edition; no later pharmaceutical recipe is substituted.
