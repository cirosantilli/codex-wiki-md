# Nonzero spectra of products in opposite orders

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md)

In a unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), for $\lambda\ne0$ the existence of $R=(\lambda1-ab)^{-1}$ gives $(\lambda1-ba)^{-1}=\lambda^{-1}(1+bRa)$, as verified by multiplying on either side. Interchanging $a,b$ proves the converse. Zero may differ: for the [unilateral shift operator](unilateral-shift-operator.md) $S$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb N_0)$, $S^*S=1$ has spectrum $\{1\}$ and $SS^*=1-P_0$ has spectrum $\{0,1\}$.
