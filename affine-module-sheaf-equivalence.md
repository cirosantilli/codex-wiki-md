# Affine module-sheaf equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md)

On an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) or [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md) with [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) $A$, the [global section functor](global-section-functor.md) and $M\mapsto\widetilde M$ are inverse equivalences between [modules](module-mathematics.md) and [quasi-coherent sheaves](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md). On every [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md), sections are the appropriate [localization of a module](localization-of-a-module.md). [Localization](localization-of-a-ring.md) detects exactness at maximal ideals, so these functors are exact even for [modules](module-mathematics.md) which are not finitely generated.
