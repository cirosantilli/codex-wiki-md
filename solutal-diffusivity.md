# Solutal diffusivity

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md)

Solutal diffusivity is the [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) governing transport of a dissolved constituent down its [concentration](concentration.md) gradient. For uniform $D$ and a stationary solvent, the [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) is $C_t=D\nabla^2 C$. It need not equal the [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md).
