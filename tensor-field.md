# Tensor field

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of vector bundles](tensor-product-of-vector-bundles.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_field)

A [tensor field](tensor-field.md) is a smooth [section of a vector bundle](section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) formed from [tensor products](tensor-product.md) of the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) and [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md). With $k$ covector factors and $l$ vector factors it belongs to

$$
\Gamma\bigl((T^*M)^{\otimes k}\otimes(TM)^{\otimes l}\bigr).
$$

Its coefficients in any local frame vary smoothly. Different conventions list the two counts in different orders, so the factor order should be specified. [Tensor contractions](tensor-contraction.md) use the canonical evaluation of a covector on a vector and are independent of the frame.
