# Sonic density of a polytropic nozzle

↑ **Parent:** [Isentropic nozzle flow](isentropic-nozzle-flow.md)

For steady [polytropic flow](polytropic-flow.md) with $p=K\rho^\gamma$, $K>0$, $\gamma>1$, a sonic section has $u_*=c_*=(\gamma K\rho_*^{\gamma-1})^{1/2}$. Substitution in [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) $\dot M=A_*\rho_*u_*$ gives the displayed density. These conditions fix the sonic state for a prescribed flux and throat area, subject to compatible reservoir and downstream [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
