# Coulomb angular-momentum intertwining

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen radial Hamiltonian factorization](hydrogen-radial-hamiltonian-factorization.md)

The Coulomb radial factors satisfy $[A_l,A_l^\dagger]=a^2(l+1)/r^2=ma^2(H_{l+1}-H_l)/\hbar^2$. Commuting $A_l$ through its factorized [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) gives the displayed intertwining relation. A nonzero transformed radial [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) has the same [energy](energy-split.md) in the next angular-momentum sector; this changes the radial sector, rather than being the usual magnetic-quantum-number raising operator within a fixed $l$ multiplet.
