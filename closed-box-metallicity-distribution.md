# Closed-box metallicity distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution](closed-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md)

For a pristine [closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution](closed-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md), $M_g\,dZ=Y\,dM_s$ and $dM_s=-dM_g$ imply $M_g=M_0e^{-Z/Y}$ independently of the [star formation rate](star-formation-rate.md). The resulting [metallicity distribution function](metallicity-distribution-function.md) is exponential on $0\le Z\le Z_f$. Its normalized density is $e^{-Z/Y}/[Y(1-e^{-Z_f/Y})]$. A fixed [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) and survival selection are needed to turn locked stellar mass into a stellar number distribution. Logarithmic abundance intervals have the additional factor $(\ln10)Z$.
