# Spherical circle

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical geometry](spherical-geometry.md)

On a sphere of radius $R$, a spherical circle of radius $r\in(0,\pi R)$ is the locus at [spherical distance](great-circle-distance.md) $r$ from its center. Its polar angle about that center is $r/R$, so parametrization by longitude has speed $R\sin(r/R)$. Its circumference is therefore $2\pi R\sin(r/R)$. For small $r$, this is $2\pi r-\pi r^3/(3R^2)+O(r^5)$, with the deficit determined by the sphere's positive [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md).
