# Projective modules are direct summands of free modules

↑ **Parent:** [Projective module](projective-module.md)

Every [module](module-mathematics.md) $P$ is a quotient of a [free module](free-module.md) $F=\bigoplus_{p\in P}Ae_p$, via $e_p\mapsto p$. If $P$ is a [projective module](projective-module.md), lift $1_P$ through this [surjection](surjective-function.md) to obtain a section $s:P\to F$. The resulting [split short exact sequence](split-short-exact-sequence.md) identifies $F$ with $\ker\pi\oplus P$. Conversely, if $P$ is a summand of a [free module](free-module.md), extend a prescribed map out of $P$ to the [free module](free-module.md) using the summand projection, lift each [basis](basis.md) image through the given [surjection](surjective-function.md), and restrict the lift to $P$. This proves the equivalence with the lifting definition of a [projective module](projective-module.md), for arbitrary ranks.
