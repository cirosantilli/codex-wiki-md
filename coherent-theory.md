# Coherent theory

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent logic](coherent-logic.md)

A coherent theory is a [first-order signature](first-order-signature.md) with a set of [coherent sequents](coherent-sequent.md) as axioms. It has a [coherent syntactic category](coherent-syntactic-category.md) containing a [conservative syntactic model](conservative-syntactic-model.md). Theories of groups, rings and integral domains admit coherent axiomatizations.
