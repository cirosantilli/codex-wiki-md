# Entropy dissipation identity for Ornstein-Uhlenbeck flow

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Fokker-Planck equation](ornstein-uhlenbeck-fokker-planck-equation.md)

For a sufficiently regular unit-mass solution, the [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Fokker-Planck equation](ornstein-uhlenbeck-fokker-planck-equation.md) is $\partial_tf=\nabla\cdot(f\nabla\log(f/\gamma))$. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) proves the displayed identity. Consequently [relative entropy](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) decreases, and its dissipation is [relative Fisher information](relative-fisher-information.md).
