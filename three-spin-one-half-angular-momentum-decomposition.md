# Three spin-one-half angular-momentum decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md)

For three distinguishable [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) systems, the [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition](clebsch-gordan-decomposition.md) first combines two spins into $0\oplus1$, then couples the third: $0\otimes1/2=1/2$ and $1\otimes1/2=1/2\oplus3/2$. Thus the [tensor product](tensor-product.md) has one quartet and two doublets. A [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) depending only on the total $S^2$ and $S_z$ acts identically on the two doublet copies. In particular $H=\epsilon_tS^2/\hbar^2+\epsilon_zS_z/\hbar$ has [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) $\epsilon_tS(S+1)+\epsilon_zM$, generically once for each quartet projection and twice for each doublet projection. Equal numerical energies have their multiplicities added.
