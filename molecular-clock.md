# Molecular clock

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)

A [molecular clock](molecular-clock.md) relates accumulated [nucleotide substitutions](nucleotide-substitution.md) to elapsed evolutionary time through an assumed rate. Two lineages evolving at the same constant rate $r$ for time $T$ have total expected substitution count $2rT$ per site before accounting for repeated changes obscured in endpoint sequences. Rate variation among sites or branches requires a more general clock model.
