# Massless chiral RNS spectrum after GSO projection

↑ **Parent:** [Critical dimension of the RNS superstring](critical-dimension-of-the-rns-superstring.md)

In one critical chiral [RNS string](spinning-string.md) sector, [light-cone gauge in string theory](light-cone-gauge-in-string-theory.md) leaves eight transverse directions. The [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md) massless states $b_{-1/2}^i|0;k\rangle$ give eight spacetime bosonic polarizations. The [Ramond zero-mode Clifford algebra](ramond-zero-mode-clifford-algebra.md) gives sixteen ground-state polarizations, split into two eight-dimensional spinor chiralities. The supersymmetric [GSO projection](gso-projection.md) keeps one chirality, giving eight spacetime fermionic polarizations. Without that projection the massless counts do not agree. Counting a chiral sector is distinct from tensoring left and right sectors into closed-string states.
