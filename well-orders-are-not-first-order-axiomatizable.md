# Well-orders are not first-order axiomatizable

↑ **Parent:** [Compactness theorem](compactness-theorem.md)

If a theory axiomatized the [well-orders](well-order.md), adjoin constants $c_0,c_1,\ldots$ and the sentences $c_{n+1}<c_n$. Every finite fragment has a finite well-ordered model, but [compactness](compactness-theorem.md) would produce a model with an infinite descending chain.
