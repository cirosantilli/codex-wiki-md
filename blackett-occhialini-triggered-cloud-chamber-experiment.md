# Blackett-Occhialini triggered cloud-chamber experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Triggered cloud chamber](triggered-cloud-chamber.md)

[Patrick Maynard Stuart Blackett](patrick-maynard-stuart-blackett.md) and [Giuseppe Occhialini](giuseppe-paolo-stanislao-occhialini.md) began their cosmic-ray collaboration in 1931. They used the coincidence methods of [Walther Bothe](walther-bothe.md) and [Bruno Rossi](bruno-benedetto-rossi.md) to make a particle initiate its own recording. Blackett later reported tracks on about $80\%$ of their counter-controlled photographs, compared with roughly $2$–$5\%$ for random expansions in small chambers. Those figures describe the reported apparatus and conditions, not a universal [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md).[Cloud chamber researches in nuclear physics and cosmic radiation, lecture 13 December 1948, pp. 104–105: collaboration, coincidence precedent and photographic yield](cloud-chamber-researches-in-nuclear-physics-and-cosmic-radiation-by-patrick-maynard-stuart-blackett.md)

In their 1933 account, [Geiger counters](geiger-counter.md) above and below the chamber fed a [coincidence circuit](coincidence-circuit.md). Its pulse operated a [thyratron](thyratron.md), magnet and [spring](spring.md)-release [valve](valve.md), letting a light [piston](piston.md) expand the [gas](gas.md). A [mercury-vapor lamp](mercury-vapor-lamp.md) then illuminated the drops. A water-cooled [solenoid](solenoid.md) supplied the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md); two oblique [cameras](camera.md) allowed track reconstruction. Moving the lower counter changed which trajectories could be recorded.[Some Photographs of the Tracks of Penetrating Radiation, pp. 699–701, section 1 and Fig. 2; p. 719, Description of Plates: expansion sequence, cameras and alternate counter position](some-photographs-of-the-tracks-of-penetrating-radiation-by-patrick-maynard-stuart-blackett-and-giuseppe-paolo-stanislao-occhialini.md)

Two timing problems must be separated. The [coincidence circuit](coincidence-circuit.md)'s window distinguishes nearly simultaneous counter signals. The mechanical expansion latency determines how much the existing [ionization](ionization.md) trail spreads before droplets form. For a chosen one-coordinate [diffusion](diffusion.md) model with initial [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma_0^2$, [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$ and expansion delay $t_e$,

$$
\sigma_{\rm track}^2=\sigma_0^2+2Dt_e.
$$

Taking $\sigma_0=0$, $D=2.0\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$ and $t_e=20\,\mathrm{ms}$ gives $\sigma_{\rm track}\simeq0.283\,\mathrm{mm}$. This is an original illustrative calculation, not the historical track width. It shows why a fast electronic pulse does not by itself guarantee a sharp photograph: the subsequent mechanical response also matters.

The photographs revealed groups containing positive and negative electron-like tracks, complementing the [Anderson positron track experiment](anderson-positron-track-experiment.md). Their interpretation depended on track curvature, direction, [ionization](ionization.md) and [particle range](particle-range.md), not simply on the presence of a coincidence.[Some Photographs of the Tracks of Penetrating Radiation, pp. 702–708 and p. 718, Summary: associated tracks and evidence for positive electrons](some-photographs-of-the-tracks-of-penetrating-radiation-by-patrick-maynard-stuart-blackett-and-giuseppe-paolo-stanislao-occhialini.md) Blackett explicitly noted that the counter control favored larger [particle showers](particle-shower.md).[Cloud chamber researches in nuclear physics and cosmic radiation, p. 108: multiplicity-dependent triggering and overrepresentation of large showers](cloud-chamber-researches-in-nuclear-physics-and-cosmic-radiation-by-patrick-maynard-stuart-blackett.md) The [multiplicity bias from a detector trigger](multiplicity-bias-from-a-detector-trigger.md) gives a small model of that effect: a recording method can improve the study of rare interactions while making its photographs unrepresentative of the incident population.
