# Tensor product of group representations

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

For representations $V$ and $W$ of a group $G$, the tensor product representation is the [tensor product](tensor-product.md) $V\otimes W$ with diagonal action

$$
g(v\otimes w)=(gv)\otimes(gw).
$$
