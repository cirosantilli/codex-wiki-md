# Weyl curvature spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Two-component spinor calculus](two-component-spinor-calculus.md)

The totally symmetric unprimed four-index spinor represents one chiral part of the [Weyl tensor](weyl-tensor.md). The other part is represented by a primed spinor, conjugate to the unprimed one on a Lorentzian real slice. The trace-free symmetry separates conformal curvature from the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) and scalar-curvature terms.
