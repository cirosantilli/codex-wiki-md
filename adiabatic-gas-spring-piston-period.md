# Adiabatic gas-spring piston period

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md)

A frictionless piston of area $A$ and mass $m$ supported by gas has $p_0=p_{\rm ext}+mg/A$. Small displacement $x$ changes its [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) by $Ax$. The local [isentropic compressibility](isentropic-compressibility.md) gives $\delta p=-\gamma p_0Ax/V_0$, so [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) becomes $m\ddot x=-\gamma p_0A^2x/V_0$. The period follows. Gas inertia and heat leakage are neglected, and internal equilibration must be faster than the oscillation.
