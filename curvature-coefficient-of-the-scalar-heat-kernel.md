# Curvature coefficient of the scalar heat kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel expansion](heat-kernel-expansion.md)

For $L=-\nabla^a\nabla_a$ on scalar functions, the leading coefficient of the [heat parametrix](heat-parametrix.md) in [normal coordinates](normal-coordinates.md) is the inverse square root of the metric volume density: $u_0=1+R_{ab}\xi^a\xi^b/12+O(|\xi|^3)$. The [heat-kernel transport equations](heat-kernel-transport-equations.md) give $u_1(x,x)=\nabla^a\nabla_a u_0(x,x)=R(x)/6$. This gives the displayed diagonal expansion. The coefficient is specific to the minimally coupled scalar operator; a bundle connection or a curvature-dependent potential changes the general Laplace-type coefficients.
