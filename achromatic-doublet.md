# Achromatic doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Achromatic lens](achromatic-lens.md)

An [achromatic doublet](achromatic-doublet.md) is an [achromatic lens](achromatic-lens.md) with two [optical lens](lens-optics.md) elements. In the contact [thin lens](thin-lens.md) model, their [optical powers](optical-power.md) add and their primary colour changes cancel according to [achromatic doublet power balance](achromatic-doublet-power-balance.md). The conventional positive [achromatic doublet](achromatic-doublet.md) uses a converging [crown glass](crown-glass-optics.md) element and a weaker diverging [flint glass](flint-glass.md) element; these names describe a common choice, not a universal ordering of every modern [optical glass](optical-glass.md) composition.
