# Logarithmic clique blow-up from minimum degree

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithmic Erdős-Stone theorem](logarithmic-erdos-stone-theorem.md)

A fixed positive excess over the [Turan theorem](turan-s-theorem.md) threshold forces logarithmic balanced [complete multipartite graphs](complete-multipartite-graph.md). There is an elementary induction using [common neighbourhood from bipartite density](common-neighbourhood-from-bipartite-density.md): first obtain a logarithmic $r$-partite subgraph from the induction hypothesis, and select disjoint transversal $r$-cliques in it. Each such clique has at least $r\varepsilon n$ [common neighbours](common-neighbour.md), by the [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) on non-neighbour sets. Apply the bipartite common-neighbour count to these cliques as left-side objects. Their selected union and a large common neighbourhood form the extra part.
