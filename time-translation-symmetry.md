# Time-translation symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Noether theorem](noether-theorem.md)

Invariance under a shift of the time coordinate, with other required background structures held fixed. A continuous [time-translation symmetry](time-translation-symmetry.md) gives an [energy](energy-split.md) [conservation law](conservation-law.md) through [Noether theorem](noether-theorem.md). A generic expanding cosmological background is not stationary and need not supply a corresponding globally conserved total [energy](energy-split.md).
