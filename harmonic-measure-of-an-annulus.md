# Harmonic measure of an annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md)

For $0<r<1$, the [harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md) of the outer circle of $r<|z|<1$ is the title expression. The function $\log|z|$ is harmonic away from zero; the normalization gives boundary values zero on the inner circle and one on the outer circle. Uniqueness of the [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md) proves the formula. A [conformal map](conformal-map.md) to an annulus transports it to a [doubly connected domain](doubly-connected-domain.md).
