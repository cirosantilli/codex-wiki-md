<h1 id="cramer-s-v">Cramér's V</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Contingency table](contingency-table.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cramér's_V)

For an $r\times c$ [contingency table](contingency-table.md) with $n$ observations and positive row and column totals, [Cramér's V](cramer-s-v.md) rescales the [Pearson chi-squared statistic](pearson-chi-squared-statistic.md) to a dimensionless measure of [statistical association](statistical-association.md). It lies between zero and one and is unchanged by reordering category labels. It therefore describes the magnitude of dependence, without an ordinal direction. A small [p-value](p-value.md) can accompany a small $V$ in a large sample: evidence against [independence](independent-random-variables.md) and the magnitude of the departure answer different questions. The displayed quantity is the usual uncorrected sample measure.
