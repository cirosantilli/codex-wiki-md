# Third central velocity moment

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity moment](velocity-moment.md)

For unit particle [mass](mass.md), this symmetric third-order [tensor](tensor.md) transports the [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md) in a kinetic fluid description. Its contraction gives the [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) $q_i=Q_{ijj}/2$. It vanishes for a [local Maxwellian](local-maxwellian.md), whose odd central [velocity moments](velocity-moment.md) vanish, but it need not vanish for a general anisotropic or skewed [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md).
