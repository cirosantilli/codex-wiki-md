# Vibration isolation

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

[Vibration isolation](vibration-isolation.md) reduces the transmission of unwanted mechanical [oscillations](oscillation.md) between an apparatus and its surroundings. Compliant supports and [damping](damping.md) can reduce transmitted motion, but their effectiveness depends on the disturbance [frequency](frequency.md) and the support's [resonance](resonance.md).
