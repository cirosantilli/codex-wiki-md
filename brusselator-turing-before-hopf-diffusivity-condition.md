# Brusselator Turing-before-Hopf diffusivity condition

↑ **Parent:** [Turing threshold of the Brusselator](turing-threshold-of-the-brusselator.md)

For the [Brusselator](brusselator.md), the stationary spatial threshold is $b_T=(1+a\sqrt{D_u/D_v})^2$ and the homogeneous oscillatory threshold is $b_H=1+a^2$. A genuine [Turing instability](turing-instability.md) appears first only if $b_T<b_H$, which rearranges to the displayed diffusivity-ratio bound. Equality gives simultaneous stationary spatial and homogeneous oscillatory neutral modes. Otherwise homogeneous loss of stability occurs first.
