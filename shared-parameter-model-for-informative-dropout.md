# Shared-parameter model for informative dropout

↑ **Parent:** [Selection model for informative longitudinal dropout](selection-model-for-informative-longitudinal-dropout.md)

A response model and a dropout model share latent [random effects](random-effect.md). Conditional on those effects and observed predictors, the two processes may be independent, yet marginally dropout is [missing not at random](missing-not-at-random.md) because the shared effects also predict unseen outcomes. Joint [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) fitting integrates the product of both models over the shared effects; integrating the outcome model alone is insufficient. This is an explicit identifying model, and its latent-dependence assumptions should be assessed by [sensitivity analysis](sensitivity-analysis.md).
