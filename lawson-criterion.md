# Lawson criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fusion](nuclear-fusion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawson_criterion)

A fusion energy-balance requirement on [density](density.md), confinement time and [temperature](temperature-split.md) under a stated loss and reaction model. A numerical density-time threshold is conditional on fuel and [temperature](temperature-split.md); it must not be silently identified with an alpha-stopping areal-density threshold.
