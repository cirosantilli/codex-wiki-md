# Logarithmically homogeneous barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Self-concordant barrier](self-concordant-barrier.md)

A cone barrier with this scaling law satisfies $\langle\nabla F(s),s\rangle=-\nu$ and $\nabla F(ts)=t^{-1}\nabla F(s)$. Therefore a [central path](central-path.md) with $y=-\mu\nabla F(s)$ has primal-dual gap $\nu\mu$. A product of orthant and [Lorentz cone](second-order-cone.md) canonical barriers adds their parameters.
