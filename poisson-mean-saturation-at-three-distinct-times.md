# Poisson mean saturation at three distinct times

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.md)

For three distinct times, the columns $1,t,t^2$ are linearly independent by the [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md). Thus a quadratic [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.md) for the log mean can reproduce any three positive time-group means. A grouped goodness-of-fit test of that mean form has zero residual degrees of freedom. Replicated observations still allow checks of the [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) and [overdispersion](overdispersion.md) within each time group; saturation of the mean does not establish the response distribution.
