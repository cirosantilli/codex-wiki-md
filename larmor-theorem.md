# Larmor theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

For particles with the same charge-to-mass ratio, rotationally invariant mutual [central forces](central-force.md), and a weak uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), a frame with the displayed [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) cancels the velocity-dependent magnetic [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) against the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md). The remaining rotational and magnetic terms are quadratic in $\mathbf B$. Thus, to first order in the field, the rotating-frame equations coincide with the zero-field equations. This is the classical dynamical basis of [Larmor precession](larmor-precession.md); equality is an approximation rather than an exact removal of the magnetic field.
