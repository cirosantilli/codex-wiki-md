# Poisson integral

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson kernel for the upper half-space](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-space.md)

The Poisson integral convolves boundary data with the [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md) to produce a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) in a half-space. In the upper half-plane,

$$
u(x,y)=\frac1\pi\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{y f(v)}{(x-v)^2+y^2}\,dv.
$$
