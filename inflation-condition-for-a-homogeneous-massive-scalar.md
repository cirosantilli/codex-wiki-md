# Inflation condition for a homogeneous massive scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)

In flat general relativity with no separate [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), a canonical massive scalar has $\rho+3P=2\dot\phi^2-m^2\phi^2$. Accelerated expansion therefore requires $\dot\phi^2<m^2\phi^2/2$. Initial potential dominance is an instantaneous condition and does not alone fix the duration.
