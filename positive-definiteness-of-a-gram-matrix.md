# Positive definiteness of a Gram matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md)

For real [vectors](vector.md) $x_i$ in a Euclidean [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), their [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) $G_{ij}=x_i^Tx_j$ satisfies the displayed identity by expanding the squared [norm](norm.md). Thus every [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) is a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md), and it is a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) exactly when the vector family is [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). The complex version replaces transposes by [conjugate transposes](conjugate-transpose.md) and uses $c^*Gc=\|\sum_i c_ix_i\|^2$.
