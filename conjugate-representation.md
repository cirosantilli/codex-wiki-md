# Conjugate representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

For a complex [group representation](group-representation.md) with [matrices](matrix.md) $R(g)$, its conjugate representation has [matrices](matrix.md) $\overline{R(g)}$ on the conjugate [vector space](vector-space-split.md). In a [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md) it is equivalent to the [dual representation](dual-representation.md), since $(R(g)^{-1})^T=\overline{R(g)}$. On a diagonal [maximal torus](maximal-torus.md), its [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) are the negatives of the original [weights](weight-representation-theory.md).
