# Crystal facet

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal](crystal.md)

A macroscopically planar crystal boundary characterized by a crystallographic orientation. Its [Miller index](miller-index.md) specifies that orientation; visible facet area depends on surface thermodynamics and growth kinetics. A low surface energy and a slow growth rate are different properties.
