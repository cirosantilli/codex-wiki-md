# Fourier filtering of overlapping amplitude-modulation sidebands

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude modulation sidebands](amplitude-modulation-sidebands.md)

The [modulation property of the Fourier transform](modulation-property-of-the-fourier-transform.md) adds shifted copies of the message spectrum. If those copies overlap, their complex amplitudes add before taking a magnitude; a frequency filter then acts on this sum. For a real even triangular message spectrum proportional to $(1-|\omega|)_+$ and carrier frequency $1/2<\Omega<1$, the translated spectra overlap over $|\omega|<1-\Omega$. A filter retaining $|\omega|<\Omega$ therefore leaves a central plateau and sloping outer sections, rather than two disjoint triangular bands. Spectral [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md) lines at the cutoff need an explicitly specified filter gain: multiplying a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) by a discontinuous mask is not defined by pointwise reasoning alone.
