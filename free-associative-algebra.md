# Free associative algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

The free associative algebra has a basis of finite words in its generators, including the empty word as identity. Multiplication concatenates words without commutativity relations. It is the [path algebra](path-algebra.md) of the [quiver with r loops](quiver-with-r-loops.md). With one generator it is a polynomial algebra.
