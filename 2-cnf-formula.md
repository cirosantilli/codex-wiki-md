# 2-CNF formula

↑ **Parent:** [Conjunctive normal form](conjunctive-normal-form.md)

A [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md) in [conjunctive normal form](conjunctive-normal-form.md) with at most two [Boolean literals](boolean-literal.md) per [Boolean clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md). Shorter [Boolean clauses](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) are allowed. The [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md) restricted to these [Boolean formulas](boolean-formula.md) is [2-SAT](2-satisfiability.md).
