# Ideal sheaf of a closed subscheme

↑ **Parent:** [Closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md)

For a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md) $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$, the ideal sheaf is the kernel $\mathcal I_{Z/X}=\ker(\mathcal O_X\to i_*\mathcal O_Z)$. It therefore fits into the [short exact sequence of sheaves](short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal I_{Z/X}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X\longrightarrow i_*\mathcal O_Z\longrightarrow0.
$$
