# Sarcopterygii

↑ **Parent:** [Osteichthyes](osteichthyes.md)

In crown-based usage, the [clade](clade.md) comprising the most recent common ancestor of living [coelacanths](coelacanth.md), [lungfish](lungfish.md) and [tetrapods](tetrapod.md), and all its descendants. Lobed, internally supported paired appendages provide a traditional diagnostic character; inherited organization can persist despite conversion of [fins](fin.md) into [limbs](vertebrate-limb.md).
