# First-order slip independence of a transverse sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Navier-slip Taylor swimming sheet](navier-slip-taylor-swimming-sheet.md)

For a dimensionless [Navier-slip Taylor swimming sheet](navier-slip-taylor-swimming-sheet.md), the decaying first-harmonic [streamfunction](stream-function.md) is $(1+Y)e^{-Y}\sin(X-\tau)$. Its tangential surface velocity and surface shear both vanish at $Y=0$. Hence it obeys the first-order [Navier slip boundary condition](navier-slip-boundary-condition.md) for every nonnegative slip length, including the [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md).
