# Symplectic invariant of a density operator

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic dynamical symmetry in quantum control](symplectic-dynamical-symmetry-in-quantum-control.md)

For $U^TJU=J$ with real orthogonal skew-symmetric $J$, the dual $\widetilde\rho=J\rho^TJ^\dagger$ transforms as $\widetilde{U\rho U^\dagger}=U\widetilde\rho U^\dagger$. Hence $I_J(\rho)$ is constant on the reachable [unitary orbit of a density operator](unitary-orbit-of-a-density-operator.md). Different values can obstruct [density operator controllability](density-operator-controllability.md) even when the ordinary spectra agree. For a four-dimensional $J$ pairing indices $1,4$ and $2,3$, the two diagonal arrangements $(a,a,b,b)$ and $(a,b,b,a)$ have invariants $4ab$ and $2(a^2+b^2)$.
