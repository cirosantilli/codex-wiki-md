# Propagation of uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wavelength measurement](acoustic-wavelength-measurement.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propagation_of_uncertainty)

[Propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md) estimates how [measurement errors](measurement-error.md) in inputs affect a calculated result. For small input errors with [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $\Sigma$, linearizing a differentiable [function](function-split.md) $g$ gives the displayed approximation for its [variance](variance-split.md). For independent errors in a product $g=2fd$, the squared relative [standard deviations](standard-deviation.md) add. Correlated errors need the covariance terms; systematic offsets require separate assessment.
