# Humidity-temperature response of OH production

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

At fixed [relative humidity](relative-humidity.md) and fixed excited-oxygen [concentration](concentration.md), a water-reaction [hydroxyl radical](hydroxyl-radical.md) source scales as $e_s(T)/T$ when [concentrations](concentration.md) are number densities. Thus $d\ln P_{\rm OH}/dT=\Delta H_{\rm vap}/(RT^2)-1/T$ under constant [latent heat](latent-heat.md) and rate coefficient. Using only $e_s$ instead gives a [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) or fixed-pressure mole-fraction approximation. Neither derivative follows without a [humidity](humidity.md) condition.
