# Cosmic microwave background temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md)

The [blackbody](blackbody.md) temperature of freely propagating [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) photons scales inversely with the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md): cosmological redshifting changes every [frequency](frequency.md) by the same factor and preserves the [Planck spectrum](blackbody-spectrum.md) shape. Its present value is approximately $2.73\,\mathrm K$. Measuring that absolute spectral temperature is distinct from measuring [temperature fluctuations](temperature-fluctuation.md) about it.
