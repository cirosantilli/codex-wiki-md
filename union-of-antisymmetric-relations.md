# Union of antisymmetric relations

↑ **Parent:** [Antisymmetric relation](antisymmetric-relation.md)

The union of two [antisymmetric relations](antisymmetric-relation.md) need not be an [antisymmetric relation](antisymmetric-relation.md). On $\{0,1\}$, take $R=\{(0,1)\}$ and $S=\{(1,0)\}$. Each is an [antisymmetric relation](antisymmetric-relation.md), but $R\cup S$ contains both directions between distinct elements. This [counterexample](counterexample.md) shows why testing each [binary relation](binary-relation.md) separately does not control cross-pairs in their union.
