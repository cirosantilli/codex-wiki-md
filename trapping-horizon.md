# Trapping horizon

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

A trapping horizon is a hypersurface foliated by marginal two-surfaces with one [null expansion](null-expansion.md) zero and the other nonzero. A future outer trapping horizon has $\theta_+=0$, $\theta_-<0$ and $\mathcal L_{n}\theta_+<0$ for a future ingoing null normal $n$. Unlike an [event horizon](event-horizon.md), this is a quasi-local definition. Under the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md), a regular future outer horizon is spacelike or null. The ingoing derivative condition distinguishes its outer type from an inner trapping horizon.
