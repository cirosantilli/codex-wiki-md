# Periodic point of an interval map

↑ **Parent:** [Interval map](interval-map.md)

A point $x$ is periodic for an [interval map](interval-map.md) $F$ when $F^n(x)=x$ for some [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$. Its least such $n$ is its period, and the finite set

$$
\{x,F(x),\ldots,F^{n-1}(x)\}
$$

is its periodic orbit.

For an [interval map](interval-map.md), this is the usual [periodic point](periodic-point.md) condition. Its least positive return time is the period.
