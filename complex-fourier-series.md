# Complex Fourier series

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

The complex form of a [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) for period $2L$ uses coefficients $c_n=(2L)^{-1}\int_{-L}^Lf(x)e^{-in\pi x/L}dx$ and complex exponentials $e^{in\pi x/L}$. For [square-integrable functions](square-integrable-function.md) it converges in the $L^2$ norm, and the [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md) is $(2L)^{-1}\int|f|^2=\sum_n|c_n|^2$. Real functions satisfy $c_{-n}=\overline{c_n}$.
