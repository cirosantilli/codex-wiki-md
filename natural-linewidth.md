# Natural linewidth

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md)

For a closed [two-level system](two-level-system.md) with [spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md) population decay $P_e(t)=P_e(0)e^{-\Gamma t}$ and no additional [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md), the [natural linewidth](natural-linewidth.md) has [full width at half maximum](full-width-at-half-maximum.md) $\Gamma$ in [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), or $\Gamma/(2\pi)$ in [hertz](hertz.md). The excited-state lifetime is $1/\Gamma$.
