# Inverse norm divergence at noninvertible boundary points

↑ **Parent:** [Group of invertible elements of a Banach algebra](group-of-invertible-elements-of-a-banach-algebra.md)

In a unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), if a subsequence of inverse norms remained bounded, $a=a_n[1+a_n^{-1}(a-a_n)]$ would be invertible for large subsequence indices by the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md). This contradicts noninvertibility of $a$. If $A$ is a closed unital subalgebra of a larger [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) $B$ and the $a_n$ lie in $G(A)$, then such a limit cannot be invertible in $B$ either: [continuity of inversion in a Banach algebra](continuity-of-inversion-in-a-banach-algebra.md) would make their inverses converge in $B$ to $a^{-1}$, which closedness puts in $A$.
