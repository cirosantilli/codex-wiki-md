# Ciliary ganglion

↑ **Parent:** [Ganglion](ganglion.md)

A small [ganglion](ganglion.md) between the [optic nerve](optic-nerve.md) and [lateral rectus muscle](lateral-rectus-muscle.md). Preganglionic fibres of the [parasympathetic nervous system](parasympathetic-nervous-system.md) relay here; sensory and already postganglionic fibres of the [sympathetic nervous system](sympathetic-nervous-system.md) traverse it without another relay.[Learning objective 4, autonomic fibres: ciliary-ganglion position, roots and relays](dissector-answers-eye-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso.md)
