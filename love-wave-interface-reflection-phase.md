# Love-wave interface reflection phase

↑ **Parent:** [Love wave](love-wave.md)

For a slower surface layer supporting propagating [SH-waves](sh-wave.md) with vertical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $q>0$ above an [evanescent](evanescent-wave.md) half-space with decay exponent $p>0$, continuity of [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) and [traction](traction.md) gives the displayed [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) for a downward incident [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) wave. Its modulus is one and its [wave phase](phase-waves.md) is $-2\arctan(\mu p/(\mu_0q))$. A traction-free top reflects [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) with coefficient $+1$. The round-trip condition $R_he^{2iqh}=1$ then gives $\tan(qh)=\mu p/(\mu_0q)$, equivalent to direct [Love wave](love-wave.md) mode matching.
