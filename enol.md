# Enol

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

An [enol](enol.md) has an OH group bonded to a [carbon](carbon.md) of a C=C bond. Protonation and pi-bond rearrangement can interconvert it with a carbonyl compound through [keto-enol tautomerism](keto-enol-tautomerism.md).
