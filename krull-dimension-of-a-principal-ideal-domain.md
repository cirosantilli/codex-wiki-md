# Krull dimension of a principal ideal domain

↑ **Parent:** [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md)

In a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md), every nonzero [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) is maximal. Indeed, write it as $(p)$ with $p\ne0$. If $(p)\subseteq(a)$, then $p=ab$. Primality gives $a\in(p)$, hence $(a)=(p)$, or $b\in(p)$, say $b=pc$, in which case cancellation gives $1=ac$ and $(a)=R$.

Thus every [prime chain](prime-chain.md) has length at most one. If the [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md) is not a [field](field.md), choose a nonzero [nonunit](nonunit.md) $a$ and a [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) $\mathfrak m\supseteq(a)$. Then $(0)\subsetneq\mathfrak m$ is a [prime chain](prime-chain.md) of length one, so the [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) is one. A [field](field.md) instead has [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) zero.
