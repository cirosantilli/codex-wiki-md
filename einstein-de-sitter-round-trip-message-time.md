# Einstein-de Sitter round-trip message time

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological round-trip communication horizon](cosmological-round-trip-communication-horizon.md)

For two [comoving observers](comoving-observer.md) in an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), light observed at redshift $z$ today fixes their separation through the past [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md). A newly sent signal needs the same [comoving distance](comoving-radial-distance.md) for every one-way leg. Since $a=(t/t_0)^{2/3}$, each leg adds a fixed [conformal time](conformal-time.md) interval, producing the displayed reception time after $n$ legs when replies are immediate. At $z=1$ the first round-trip wait is $[(3-\sqrt2)^3-1]t_0$. There is no finite count limit because future [conformal time](conformal-time.md) diverges, though infinitely many legs require infinite cosmic time.
