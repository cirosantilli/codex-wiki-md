# Equalization with a controlled exceptional set

↑ **Parent:** [Equitable regularity energy](equitable-regularity-energy.md)

In a proof of the [Szemerédi regularity lemma](szemeredi-regularity-lemma.md), suppose $k$ equal classes have size $L$ and witness splitting creates at most $2^k$ atoms per class. Divide every atom into blocks of size $\ell=\lfloor L/4^k\rfloor$, putting leftovers into the exceptional set. At most $k2^k\ell\leq kL2^{-k}$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) are lost. When $L\geq2\cdot4^k$, at most $2k4^k$ blocks remain. For [equitable regularity energy](equitable-regularity-energy.md) defined by omitting exceptional [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), the loss from discarding a fraction $r$ is at most $2r$: only ordered pairs incident with discarded [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) are removed, and every squared [edge density of a bipartite graph](edge-density-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) is at most one. This provides a quantitative way to restore equal class sizes after each energy increment.
