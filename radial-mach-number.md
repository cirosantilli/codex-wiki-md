# Radial Mach number

↑ **Parent:** [Mach number](mach-number.md)

The radial [Mach number](mach-number.md) is the source [velocity](velocity.md) projected toward the observer, divided by the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md). It enters the [moving-surface retarded Jacobian](moving-surface-retarded-jacobian.md) through $d|x-y(\tau)|/d\tau=-\widehat r\cdot v$. The sign is directional: a source moving toward the observer has positive $M_r$ and compressed arrival times.
