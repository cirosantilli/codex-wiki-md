# Zirconium hydride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

A hydrogen-containing zirconium phase that can precipitate from hydrogen-bearing [zirconium alloys](zirconium-alloys.md). Brittle hydride platelets and their orientation affect cracking resistance; their behavior differs from dissolved hydrogen.
