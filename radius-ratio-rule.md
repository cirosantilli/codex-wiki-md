# Radius ratio rule

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic bonding](ionic-bonding-split.md)

A [hard-sphere model](hard-sphere-model.md) relating the [radius](radius.md) ratio of opposite-charge [ions](ion.md) to a possible [coordination number](coordination-number.md). For a [cube](cube.md) of eight mutually touching [anions](anion.md) around a touching [cation](cation.md), $r_c/r_a=\sqrt3-1$; real bonding and coordination-dependent [radii](radius.md) limit its predictive power.
