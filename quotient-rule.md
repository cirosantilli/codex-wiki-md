# Quotient rule

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotient_rule)

If $f$ and $g$ are [differentiable](differentiable-function.md) and $g\ne0$, then

$$
\left(\frac fg\right)'
=\frac{f'g-fg'}{g^2}.
$$
