# Settling-velocity radius scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Settling velocity](settling-velocity.md)

For spheres with the same density contrast, balance submerged weight, proportional to $r^3$, against [Stokes drag law](stokes-s-law.md), proportional to $rW_s$, to obtain $W_s\propto r^2$. With constant [drag coefficient](drag-coefficient.md), [quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md) is proportional to $r^2W_s^2$, giving $W_s\propto r^{1/2}$. The two radius laws require their corresponding [particle Reynolds number](particle-reynolds-number.md) limits.
