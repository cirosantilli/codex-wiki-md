# AAPCS32

↑ **Parent:** [Calling convention](calling-convention.md)

The 32-[bit](bit.md) [calling convention](calling-convention.md) for the [ARM architecture family](arm-architecture-family.md). Its base variant passes initial arguments in `r0`–`r3`, returns a word in `r0`, preserves `r4`–`r8`, `r10`, `r11`, and `sp`, and requires eight-byte [stack pointer](stack-pointer.md) alignment at public calls.[Sections 6.1.1, 6.2.1.2, 6.4 and 6.5](procedure-call-standard-for-the-arm-architecture-by-arm-limited.md)
