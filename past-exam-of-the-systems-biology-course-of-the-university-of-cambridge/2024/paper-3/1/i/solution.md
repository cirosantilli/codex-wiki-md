<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the **[log link](../../../../../logarithmic-link-function.md)**:

$$
\boxed{\log\lambda_i=\beta_0+\beta_T T_i},\qquad
\lambda_i=\mathbb E[N_i\mid T_i],
$$

where $N_i$ is the offspring count and $T_i$ is [testosterone](../../../../../testosterone.md) concentration. This [link function](../../../../../link-function.md) keeps the fitted conditional mean positive.
