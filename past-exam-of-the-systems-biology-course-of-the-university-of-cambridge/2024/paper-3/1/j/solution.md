↑ **Parent:** [J](../j.md)

A one-unit increase in [testosterone](../../../../../testosterone.md) concentration, here $1\,\mathrm{ng/dL}$, multiplies the expected offspring count by **$\boxed{e^{\beta_T}}$** under the [Poisson regression](../../../../../poisson-regression.md) with a [log link](../../../../../logarithmic-link-function.md). Thus the exact percentage change in the conditional mean is **$\boxed{100(e^{\beta_T}-1)\%}$**. The [regression coefficient](../../../../../regression-coefficient.md) $\beta_T$ itself is the change in the logarithm of the expected count, not an additive change in offspring number.
