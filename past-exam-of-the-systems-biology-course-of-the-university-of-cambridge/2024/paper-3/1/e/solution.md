Two assumptions of the usual [normal linear model](../../../../../normal-linear-model-split.md) used for the reported coefficient [Student t-tests](../../../../../student-s-t-test.md) are:

- The errors are [independent random variables](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) across mice.
- Conditional on the predictors, each error has a [normal distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) with mean zero and the same [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$: $\varepsilon_i\mid w_i,s_i\sim N(0,\sigma^2)$.

The second statement includes [homoscedasticity](../../../../../homoscedasticity.md). Normality concerns the conditional errors, rather than requiring the weights themselves to have a [normal distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md).
