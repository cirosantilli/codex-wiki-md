<h1 id="1/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

Use **[Poisson regression](../../../../../poisson-regression.md)** for the offspring count, with [testosterone](../../../../../testosterone.md) as a predictor. It models a nonnegative integer response through its conditional mean. If the conditional [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) substantially exceeds that mean, a [negative binomial regression](../../../../../negative-binomial-regression.md) is a possible alternative for [overdispersion](../../../../../overdispersion.md).
