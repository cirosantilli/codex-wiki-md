<h1 id="section-i/1-1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1.1](../1-1.md)

**[biology](../../../../../biology-split.md) is physically realized, but this does not make its explanations merely technological applications of physics.** The strongest defensible position combines [physicalism](../../../../../physicalism.md) with qualified [explanatory autonomy](../../../../../explanatory-autonomy.md): biological mechanisms must respect physical constraints, while biological questions can require concepts, experiments and theories organized at other levels.

The contention associated with [J. J. C. Smart](../../../../../j-j-c-smart.md) should first be separated into different claims. [Ontological reductionism](../../../../../ontological-reductionism.md) concerns what living systems are made of; [explanatory reductionism](../../../../../explanatory-reductionism.md) concerns whether explanations in terms of organisms, populations or regulatory organization can always be replaced without loss by descriptions of their physical constituents. A methodological preference for investigating smaller components is a further claim. Accepting the first does not logically establish the other two. The cited article supplies the historical setting for the contention; the distinctions here provide a way of assessing it.[Synthese 11 (1959), pp. 359–368; article identified in the examination](../../../../../can-biology-be-an-exact-science-by-j-j-c-smart.md)

The reductionist case has substantial scientific force. [DNA replication](../../../../../dna-replication.md), [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) [catalysis](../../../../../catalysis.md) and transport across a [cell membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md) involve material interactions, not an extra vital substance. [thermodynamics](../../../../../thermodynamics-split.md) constrains the energy available for [metabolism](../../../../../metabolism.md); [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) limits transport; [chemical kinetics](../../../../../chemical-kinetics-split.md) constrains the speeds of reactions. Describing a [gene regulatory network](../../../../../gene-regulatory-network.md) without specifying how [transcription factors](../../../../../transcription-factor.md) bind and how their concentrations change can conceal an impossible mechanism. [systems biology](../../../../../systems-biology.md) benefits from these constraints because a diagram of arrows alone does not guarantee that the proposed dynamics can occur. Nor should [emergence](../../../../../emergence.md) be used as a name for an unexplained exemption from physical law.

Nevertheless, physical consistency leaves many explanatory questions open. Knowing the chemical composition of a population does not immediately explain why an [allele frequency](../../../../../allele-frequency.md) changes. In a simple haploid [natural selection](../../../../../natural-selection.md) model with two alleles and reproductive outputs $w_A,w_a$, the next-generation frequency satisfies

$$
p' = \frac{p w_A}{p w_A+(1-p)w_a},\qquad
p'-p=\frac{p(1-p)(w_A-w_a)}{\bar w},\quad
\bar w=pw_A+(1-p)w_a.
$$

This relation identifies differential reproduction as the relevant comparison. It applies to many distinct molecular causes of the reproductive difference, provided the model's assumptions hold. An explanation of why an allele spreads can therefore be both physically implemented and usefully stated at the population level. [genetic drift](../../../../../genetic-drift.md), migration or frequency dependence require changes to the model, rather than a conclusion that population-level explanation was never scientific.

Similarly, [Mendelian segregation](../../../../../mendelian-segregation.md) makes a conditional probabilistic claim about inheritance. For a heterozygote cross at one locus, equal segregation, random fertilization and complete dominance produce genotype probabilities $1/4,1/2,1/4$ and a phenotype ratio of $3:1$. A molecular description explains how chromosomes segregate and why exceptions occur; it does not remove the utility of the inheritance-level relation. Molecular detail and the higher-level relation answer connected questions at different resolutions. The experimental importance of tracking characters across controlled crosses is already clear in [Gregor Mendel](../../../../../gregor-mendel.md)'s original work.[experimental design and the segregation of characters in successive hybrid generations](../../../../../experiments-in-plant-hybridization-by-gregor-mendel.md)

Organization also matters. The same components can form a stable [negative feedback](../../../../../negative-feedback.md) circuit or an amplifying [positive feedback](../../../../../positive-feedback.md) circuit, depending on how interactions are connected. Conversely, distinct biochemical implementations can produce the same adaptation behavior. This is [multiple realizability](../../../../../multiple-realizability.md): it supports explanations in terms of a relationship or control architecture that remains useful across several implementations. It does not establish that every implementation has identical dynamics, or that a sufficiently complete physical description could never recover the behavior. The claim is about what makes an explanation informative and generalizable, not a proof of nonphysical causation.

A further distinction concerns historical questions and [biological function](../../../../../biological-function.md). To ask how a receptor transduces a signal is to ask for a present [mechanistic explanation](../../../../../mechanistic-explanation.md). To ask why a lineage retained that receptor may require evidence about ancestry, past environments and [natural selection](../../../../../natural-selection.md). Present physical laws alone do not supply those historical boundary conditions. A functional claim must still be tested: the existence of a useful feature does not prove that it evolved because of that use, and [natural selection](../../../../../natural-selection.md) has no foresight. Explanations of evolutionary history complement rather than contradict physical accounts of current operation.

The objection that [biology](../../../../../biology-split.md) lacks exceptionless laws is also too quick. Many useful biological generalizations are conditional on explicitly stated assumptions, but physical models also idealize away interactions, specify boundary conditions and use statistical descriptions. [stochasticity](../../../../../stochasticity.md) in a biological model need not mean absence of regularity: a [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) can be a precise prediction even when one individual outcome is uncertain. The scientific issue is whether assumptions, predictions and domains of applicability are clear enough to be tested.

There are limits to this defense of autonomy. A higher-level explanation should not be protected from evidence about its mechanism, and an attractive organizing principle should not substitute for quantitative testing. Conversely, merely listing every molecular event can fail to reveal which changes would alter the outcome. A productive investigation moves between levels: identify a population or cellular regularity, propose a mechanism, perturb its components, and test whether the higher-level prediction survives. **[biology](../../../../../biology-split.md)'s autonomy is therefore explanatory and methodological, not independence from the material world; calling it only technology understates its role in discovering and testing such regularities.**
