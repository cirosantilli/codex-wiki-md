↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Bulk [RNA sequencing](../../../../../rna-sequencing.md) mixes the [gene expression](../../../../../gene-expression.md) of all sampled cells, while dissociated-cell measurements retain individual profiles but usually lose their original neighbours and positions. [Spatial transcriptomics](../../../../../spatial-transcriptomics.md) preserves those relationships. The same [cell type](../../../../../cell-type.md) can behave differently at a tissue boundary, near a blood vessel or beside a tumour, so position can explain a state that a cell-type label alone cannot.

Spatial measurements can reveal tissue layers, developmental boundaries, tumour–immune interfaces and subcellular [RNA localization](../../../../../rna-localization.md). They can distinguish a change in cell abundance from a change in expression within a particular anatomical niche. Dissociation can also lose fragile cells or induce stress responses.

**Spatial proximity supplies context, not proof of communication.** Adjacent expression of a ligand and receptor suggests an interaction to test; establishing signalling requires additional evidence and perturbation.
