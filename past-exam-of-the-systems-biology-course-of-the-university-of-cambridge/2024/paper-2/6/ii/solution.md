<h1 id="6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Start with the biological quantity to measure: compartment location, molecular colocalization, fast cell dynamics, deep-tissue activity or nanometre-scale organization. Specify the required field, depth, lateral and axial resolution, temporal sampling and acceptable perturbation before choosing the instrument.

For a thin fixed specimen or a shallow live preparation, [confocal microscopy](../../../../../confocal-microscopy.md) provides flexible multicolour imaging and effective [optical sectioning](../../../../../optical-sectioning.md) with widely available labels. Adjust the pinhole and dwell time against [signal-to-noise ratio](../../../../../signal-to-noise-ratio.md), bleaching and speed. Repeatedly scanning a large volume can become too slow, and out-of-focus excitation can damage regions that contribute little useful signal.

For a living scattering specimen, [two-photon excitation microscopy](../../../../../two-photon-excitation-microscopy.md) often offers better access to depth and confinement of excitation. Longer excitation [wavelengths](../../../../../wavelength.md) scatter less strongly in many tissues, and broad collection can recover scattered emission without a detection pinhole. These benefits do not eliminate depth-dependent [optical aberrations](../../../../../optical-aberration.md), absorption, heating or focal [phototoxicity](../../../../../phototoxicity.md). Pulsed excitation can also make instrumentation and suitable multicolour excitation more demanding.

If the priority is rapid, gentle volumetric observation of a suitable specimen, consider [light-sheet fluorescence microscopy](../../../../../light-sheet-fluorescence-microscopy.md) rather than forcing either point-scanning method to cover every use case. If subdiffraction molecular organization is required, consider a suitable super-resolution method and assess its labeling, speed and dose costs.

Finally, calibrate the [point spread function](../../../../../point-spread-function-split.md) in a sample-relevant medium, sample finely enough in all three dimensions, and check registration between colour channels. Match photon collection and exposure to the question, with controls for label-induced changes and [photobleaching](../../../../../photobleaching.md). **The best method meets the biological resolution and timing requirements with sufficient signal and acceptable perturbation; the smallest nominal resolution number alone does not decide.**
