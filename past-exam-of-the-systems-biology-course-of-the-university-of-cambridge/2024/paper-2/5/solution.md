<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**Test metabolic response and verified exposure, rather than using body weight as the sole definition of efficacy.** Equal body weight can coexist with altered liver [triglycerides](../../../../triglyceride.md), altered circulating [lipids](../../../../lipid.md) or different substrate use. A fair cross-species comparison must also distinguish failure to deliver [docosahexaenoic acid](../../../../docosahexaenoic-acid.md) from failure to respond after it reaches the relevant compartments.

**Parallel randomized interventions.** In mice, compare a standardized high-fat/high-sucrose diet containing [docosahexaenoic acid](../../../../docosahexaenoic-acid.md) with an otherwise matched control diet in which an equal energy amount of control fat replaces it. Keep total fat, sugar, protein, micronutrients and energy density constant; measure actual food intake. Include a normal-diet reference group to contextualize the diet-induced disturbance and, if intake differs, a separate [pair feeding](../../../../pair-feeding.md) control to address intake-mediated effects. The pair-fed group answers a different question from the freely fed control, so it should not replace it. Standardize age and housing, include both sexes with a planned analysis, and randomize across cages and processing batches. If diet is assigned by cage, replicate and analyze the cage-level assignment rather than treating all cage-mates as independent treatment allocations.

In humans, use a parallel, double-blind [randomized controlled trial](../../../../randomized-controlled-trial.md) of a characterized [docosahexaenoic acid](../../../../docosahexaenoic-acid.md) preparation versus a matched, isoenergetic control preparation in adults with obesity. Choose the exposure under an approved clinical protocol, maintain background diet and activity as consistently as practical, and record medication, alcohol and other supplement use. Avoid combining one arm with an unbalanced weight-loss programme. The control oil is a defined comparator with its own possible biology, not an assumed metabolically inert substance. Block randomization on important baseline factors and blind clinical assessors and laboratory staff.

Choose dose and duration to compare **achieved exposure**, not simply the same milligrams per kilogram across species. Analyze the administered diets and preparations for fatty-acid composition and oxidation. Measure [docosahexaenoic acid](../../../../docosahexaenoic-acid.md) incorporation in plasma and longer-lived cellular lipid fractions to assess adherence and delivery. A predeclared study duration of roughly twelve weeks in humans, with a comparable mouse time course chosen for its biology, is an example design rather than a claim that identical calendar time implies identical metabolic exposure.

**Samples and timing.** Collect standardized fasting plasma before treatment and at early, intermediate and final visits, for example weeks 2, 6 and 12 in humans. In a subset, add a standardized-meal time course before and after intervention to measure postprandial trajectories rather than only a fasting endpoint. Record fasting duration, collection time, handling delay and recent diet. Collect urine for complementary small-molecule measurements. In mice, obtain serial blood within appropriate sampling limits, and collect liver, [adipose tissue](../../../../adipose-tissue.md) and muscle from randomized terminal cohorts at selected early and final time points. These terminal tissues are from different animals and must not be analyzed as within-animal repeated measurements.

Use comparable plasma fractions in both species for the primary cross-species analysis. Mouse liver gives a mechanistic view of [hepatic steatosis](../../../../hepatic-steatosis.md); human liver fat can be followed noninvasively by a suitable [magnetic resonance imaging](../../../../magnetic-resonance-imaging.md) measurement of liver fat. Do not require research liver biopsies solely to make the matrices match. Optional tissue collection must have separate justification. Measure body composition, liver fat and relevant clinical metabolic traits alongside [metabolomics](../../../../metabolomics.md), so molecular changes can be related to a physiological outcome.

**Analytical measurements.** Use broad [liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry](../../../../liquid-chromatography-mass-spectrometry.md) [metabolomics](../../../../metabolomics.md) to survey polar metabolites, together with dedicated [lipidomics](../../../../lipidomics.md) separations and [tandem mass spectrometry](../../../../tandem-mass-spectrometry.md) for intact lipid classes and molecular species. Different extraction and chromatographic conditions are needed for polar compounds and hydrophobic [lipids](../../../../lipid.md). [Gas chromatography-mass spectrometry](../../../../gas-chromatography-mass-spectrometry.md) of appropriately prepared fatty-acid derivatives provides an independent quantitative fatty-acid composition measurement. [NMR spectroscopy](../../../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) can complement these assays with robust quantification of abundant soluble metabolites, although it usually detects fewer low-abundance compounds than [mass spectrometry](../../../../mass-spectrometry.md).

Prespecify molecular outcomes such as [docosahexaenoic acid](../../../../docosahexaenoic-acid.md) incorporation into [phospholipids](../../../../phospholipid.md) and [triglycerides](../../../../triglyceride.md), then exploratory responses in other lipid classes, [acylcarnitines](../../../../acylcarnitine.md) and central [metabolic pathways](../../../../metabolic-pathway.md). A possible response is incorporation of more highly unsaturated lipid species without weight loss; reduced liver fat or altered postprandial handling could then reveal a metabolic effect missed by body weight. Conversely, altered precursor pools do not themselves prove increased pathway flux. [Acylcarnitine](../../../../acylcarnitine.md) changes should be interpreted with other measurements, not automatically equated with increased fatty-acid oxidation. These are hypotheses to test, not assumed treatment outcomes.

Use isotope-labeled [internal standards](../../../../internal-standard.md), extraction blanks and a [pooled quality-control sample](../../../../pooled-quality-control-sample.md) injected throughout each analytical batch. Randomize sample order while balancing species, arm and time; include shared reference material across batches. Standardize rapid cooling, extraction, storage and freeze–thaw exposure, because highly unsaturated [lipids](../../../../lipid.md) are susceptible to oxidation. Verify key metabolite identities with retention behaviour, fragmentation and authentic standards where available. An accurate mass alone does not uniquely establish a lipid's double-bond positions or distinguish all structural isomers.

**Statistical plan.** Power the study for a limited set of primary biochemical or physiological endpoints, using anticipated variance and independent treatment units. Keep discovery [lipidomics](../../../../lipidomics.md) separate from confirmatory endpoints. Inspect [principal component analysis](../../../../principal-component-analysis.md) plots for outliers and [batch effects](../../../../batch-effect.md); an apparent separation is not by itself a treatment test. For repeated plasma measurements, use a [mixed model](../../../../mixed-model.md) with participant or animal [random effects](../../../../random-effect.md), baseline adjustment and treatment-by-time terms, with cage effects where relevant. Report [effect sizes](../../../../effect-size.md) and [confidence intervals](../../../../confidence-interval.md); use a prespecified [Benjamini-Hochberg procedure](../../../../benjamini-hochberg-procedure.md) for exploratory [multiple testing](../../../../multiple-hypothesis-testing.md).

For matched molecular endpoints, model the log abundance or change using species, treatment, time and their interactions, or compare harmonized treatment [effect sizes](../../../../effect-size.md) across species. **A species-by-treatment interaction addresses differing responses.** A significant result in mice and a nonsignificant result in humans does not establish that the responses differ. Differences in achieved exposure, sampled tissue, starting diet or baseline disease can still explain an apparent interaction; document these before attributing the difference to species physiology. Use [intention-to-treat analysis](../../../../intention-to-treat-analysis.md) for the primary human trial, with adherence-based analyses clearly secondary, and validate exploratory molecular findings in independent samples.

**Ethics.** Obtain informed consent and independent ethical review, explain uncertainty about benefit, protect identifiable molecular and clinical data, monitor adverse events and allow withdrawal. Do not induce obesity or require a harmful diet merely to mimic the mouse model, and do not withhold established care. Minimize blood and tissue sampling and justify any optional biopsy separately. Animal work likewise needs justified numbers, humane endpoints and refinement of sampling. The design should answer the cross-species question with the least invasive measurements that can distinguish exposure, molecular response and physiology.
