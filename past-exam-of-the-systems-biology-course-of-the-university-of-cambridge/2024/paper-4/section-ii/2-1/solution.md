<h1 id="section-ii/2-1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2.1](../2-1.md)

**Central hypothesis and objectives.** PUF50 participates in one or more [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) assemblies whose composition or activity affects [Wnt signalling](../../../../../wnt-signalling.md). The proposal should distinguish membership, direct binding and causal pathway regulation. The three aims are to nominate plausible partners, identify reproducible cellular complexes, and test whether a supported interaction explains the phenotype.

**Computational starting point.** Inspect the verified PUF50 sequence for [protein domains](../../../../../protein-domain.md), localization signals, conserved residues and predicted disordered regions. Use [sequence alignment](../../../../../sequence-alignment.md) and [orthology](../../../../../orthology.md) to compare well-annotated homologues, recording uncertainty in functional transfer. Public interaction and expression resources can nominate partners expressed in the experimental cell line. Structural predictions can prioritize testable interfaces, but a confident fold alone does not establish a cellular interaction or tumour-suppressor function.

**Discovery [proteomics](../../../../../proteomics.md).** Use [affinity purification-mass spectrometry](../../../../../affinity-purification-mass-spectrometry.md) to compare PUF50-associated material with matched negative controls across independent [biological replicates](../../../../../biological-replicate.md). An endogenous or functionally validated tagged preparation is preferable to assuming that strong overexpression preserves normal localization and complex [stoichiometry](../../../../../stoichiometry.md). Tag-only controls, input abundance measurements and an unrelated bait help distinguish reproducible association from abundant or sticky contaminants. Quantitative enrichment should be evaluated together with replicate consistency and a controlled [false discovery rate](../../../../../false-discovery-rate.md).

Complexes may vary with pathway state. Compare a relevant baseline and a defined Wnt-responsive state, checking that changes in recovered partner abundance are not explained solely by input abundance, cell number or viability. If transient or compartmentalized partners are missed, [proximity labeling](../../../../../proximity-labeling.md) can provide complementary neighbourhood evidence. Its hits should be labelled as proximity associations rather than automatically promoted to direct complex subunits.

**Independent validation and mechanism.** Validate leading associations by reciprocal [co-immunoprecipitation](../../../../../co-immunoprecipitation.md) and localization evidence. Where a direct interaction is important to the proposed mechanism, test it with an independent assay suited to the relevant [protein domains](../../../../../protein-domain.md). A genetic perturbation and rescue comparison can ask whether loss of PUF50 changes pathway output and whether restoring PUF50 restores it. Compare pathway-proximal measurements with downstream transcription and longer-term growth phenotypes, because a growth defect alone is not specific to Wnt regulation.

A separation-of-function comparison is especially informative: a PUF50 variant that retains abundance and localization but loses a supported partner association could help connect the interaction to function. This interpretation requires checking folding and stability, since an inactive misfolded [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) does not isolate one interface's role. Genetic interaction analysis can then place PUF50 relative to known pathway components without treating every correlated expression change as a direct target.

**Analysis, deliverables and alternatives.** Integrate quantitative interaction evidence, localization and perturbation responses into an annotated [protein interaction network](../../../../../protein-interaction-network.md), retaining evidence type on each edge. Report [effect sizes](../../../../../effect-size.md) and uncertainty, include unsuccessful validation, and release analysis code with processed data. A negative stable-complex result would motivate a transient or condition-specific interaction hypothesis rather than proving that PUF50 acts alone. The principal deliverable is a ranked, experimentally qualified set of complexes and a causal test of the most supported pathway mechanism.
