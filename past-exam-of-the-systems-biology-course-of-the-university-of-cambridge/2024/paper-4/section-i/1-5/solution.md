<h1 id="section-i/1-5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1.5](../1-5.md)

Define the evaluation universe first, including which sufficiently covered loci were eligible for both methods. Relative to the reference assay's labels,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{TPR}=\frac{TP}{TP+FN},\qquad\operatorname{FPR}=\frac{FP}{FP+TN}.}
$$

The [true positive rate](../../../../../recall-classification.md) is the fraction of reference-positive loci retained by [xPore](../../../../../xpore.md); the [false positive rate](../../../../../false-positive-rate.md) is the fraction of reference-negative loci incorrectly retained. The latter is not the fraction of [xPore](../../../../../xpore.md) calls that fail validation.

An imperfect reference assay can miss real modifications. “False positive” here therefore [means](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) disagreement with that reference, not independently demonstrated absence of a modification. Restricting the universe differently for the two denominators would invalidate the comparison.
