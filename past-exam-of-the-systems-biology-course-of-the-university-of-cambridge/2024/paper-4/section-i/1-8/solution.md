<h1 id="section-i/1-8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1.8](../1-8.md)

**The diagonal [means](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) no ranking discrimination against the reference labels, with ROC area 0.5.** At every operating point on that diagonal, the fraction of positives accepted equals the fraction of negatives accepted. A score independent of the true label has this expected behaviour.

This does not imply that exactly half of all individual predictions are correct: an imbalanced dataset can have high accuracy by always predicting the majority class. It also does not establish biological absence of signal; it describes what the evaluated score extracts relative to the chosen reference and dataset.
