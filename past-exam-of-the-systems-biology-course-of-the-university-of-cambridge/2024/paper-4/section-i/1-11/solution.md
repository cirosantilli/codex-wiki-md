<h1 id="section-i/1-11/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1.11](../1-11.md)

Pooling can erase [between-replicate variation](../../../../../between-replicate-variation.md). One unusually well-covered or highly modified sample can dominate the pooled estimate, hiding an inconsistent biological response. If individual reads are then treated as independent [biological replicates](../../../../../biological-replicate.md), uncertainty can be understated through [pseudoreplication](../../../../../pseudoreplication.md).

A better analysis retains replicate identity and models sample-to-sample variation, while allowing information to be shared about suitable common component parameters. Pooled performance on a larger dataset can be useful, but should not substitute for evidence that the result reproduces across independent biological samples.
