<h1 id="section-i/1-3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1.3](../1-3.md)

**A five-base current measurement alone does not guarantee identification of one modified base in every sequence.** At GGACT there is only one [adenosine](../../../../../adenosine.md) in the window, so attributing an established adenosine-specific modification to that A is plausible. That does not establish a universal resolution claim.

For a counterexample, consider a window containing two candidate [adenosines](../../../../../adenosine.md). If modifying either position produces indistinguishable current distributions, its signal cannot identify which A changed. Overlapping windows, sequence context, calibrated modification signatures or external evidence may resolve some ambiguities, but they are extra information. This is an [identifiability](../../../../../identifiability.md) issue: estimating the fraction in a shifted current component is not automatically the same as identifying its molecular cause or exact position.
