<h1 id="section-i/1-10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1.10](../1-10.md)

**It is a useful depletion control, but not an automatically modification-free negative control.** A nominal knockout may retain functional products, and loss of one writer does not establish absence of every relevant [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) modification. Calling the sample completely negative requires independent validation of the molecular state being used as ground truth.

This distinction has direct experimental support: alternatively spliced functional [METTL3](../../../../../mettl3.md) products have been found in some nominal knockout lines.[Abstract and Results: residual functional METTL3 isoforms in purported knockout cells](../../../../../alternative-splicing-of-mettl3-explains-apparently-mettl3-independent-m6a-modifications-in-mrna-by-hui-xian-poh-and-others.md) That result identifies a possible failure mode; it does not prove that this particular sample has the same defect. A modification-free reference preparation and an independent chemical measurement would strengthen the interpretation.
