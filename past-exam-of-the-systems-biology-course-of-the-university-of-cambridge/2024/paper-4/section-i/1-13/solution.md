<h1 id="section-i/1-13/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1.13](../1-13.md)

**The panel supports feasibility in these samples, but not the full strength or generality of the claim.** The method produces modification estimates for three patient samples, and the displayed selected [genes](../../../../../gene.md) generally have higher estimates than the knockout controls. That is evidence that an analysis can be performed and yields biological-looking contrasts.

The comparison simultaneously changes cell background, disease status and potentially sequencing conditions. The displayed [genes](../../../../../gene.md) were selected for high estimated modification, creating [selection bias](../../../../../selection-bias.md); the same estimates do not independently validate their accuracy. The panel also does not itself show input-RNA amounts, uncertainty, or performance across controlled input levels. It therefore cannot alone establish reliable low-input performance across genetically diverse clinical samples or disease-specific modification differences.
