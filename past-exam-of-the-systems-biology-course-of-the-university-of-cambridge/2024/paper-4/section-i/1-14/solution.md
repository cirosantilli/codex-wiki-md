<h1 id="section-i/1-14/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1.14](../1-14.md)

Two useful changes would be:

- Show patient estimates together with matched, relevant cell-type controls and an independent measurement of modification at prespecified or held-out loci. Display uncertainty and all evaluated loci, or state a selection rule independent of the reported contrast. This separates genuine agreement from selection of high [xPore](../../../../../xpore.md) values and from unrelated cell-background effects.
- Add performance across measured RNA-input levels for the same biological materials, with biological and technical replication, [read coverage](../../../../../read-coverage.md) and failure rates reported. This tests the low-input part of the claim directly rather than inferring it from the heatmap.

A single additional knockout row would not address both independent accuracy and input dependence.
