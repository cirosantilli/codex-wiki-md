# Conducting plane

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md)

An ideal planar [conductor](electrical-conductor.md) is an equipotential boundary in [electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md). The [electric field](electric-field-split.md) just outside is normal to it, while the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) inside its metal vanishes. If grounded and infinite, the [method of images](method-of-images.md) implements the boundary by reflected opposite [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md).
