# Second variation of geodesic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian index form](riemannian-index-form.md)

For $E=\tfrac12\int|\dot\gamma|^2$ and a geodesic base curve, a variation field $V$ satisfies

$$
E''(0)=[\langle\nabla_sV,\dot\gamma\rangle]_a^b+
\int_a^b\left(|\nabla_tV|^2-\langle R(V,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma,V\rangle\right)dt.
$$

The endpoint term vanishes for fixed endpoints and for periodic variations of closed geodesics. The integral is the [Riemannian index form](riemannian-index-form.md). This uses the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) and the convention $R(X,Y)=\nabla_X\nabla_Y-\nabla_Y\nabla_X-\nabla_{[X,Y]}$.
