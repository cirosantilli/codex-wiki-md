# Compact continuous-derivative representation of a distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Structure theorem for compactly supported distributions](structure-theorem-for-compactly-supported-distributions.md)

Every [compactly supported distribution](compactly-supported-distribution.md) on an [open set](open-set.md) $X$ is a finite sum $u=\sum_\alpha\partial^\alpha f_\alpha$ with continuous $f_\alpha$ compactly supported inside $X$. One can first write $u=\partial^\gamma F$ for a continuous global [convolution](convolution.md) primitive, then multiply by a [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) equal to one near the support. The identity $\chi\partial^\gamma F=\sum_{\beta\leq\gamma}(-1)^{|\beta|}\binom\gamma\beta\partial^{\gamma-\beta}[(\partial^\beta\chi)F]$ localizes every coefficient. Finite global representation can fail for a [distribution of unbounded order](distribution-of-unbounded-order.md).
