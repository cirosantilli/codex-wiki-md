# Line bundles trivialized by an open cover

↑ **Parent:** [Line bundle](line-bundle.md)

A [line bundle](line-bundle.md) trivial on each [open set](open-set.md) is determined by unit-valued transition functions on pairwise intersections. Their [cocycle](cocycle.md) identities make the local trivial bundles glue; changing frames changes the [cocycle](cocycle.md) by a [coboundary](coboundary.md). The [tensor product of sheaves](tensor-product-of-sheaves.md) multiplies the transition functions, giving the displayed group [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) with [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md).
