# Carleson box

↑ **Parent:** [Carleson measure](carleson-measure.md)

A boundary neighborhood over an arc $I$ of the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), with radial depth equal to its normalized angular length $m(I)$. Changing that depth by any fixed positive factor gives an equivalent [Carleson measure](carleson-measure.md) condition. Large arcs and central mass can be handled separately by finitely many fixed neighborhoods covering the [unit disc](unit-disc.md).
