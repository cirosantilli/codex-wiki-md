# Rate of strain and vorticity of a Stokeslet

↑ **Parent:** [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md)

Differentiating the [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) gives its [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md) and [vorticity](vorticity.md), displayed above for $r=|\mathbf x|>0$. The pressure is $p=\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf x/(4\pi r^3)$. Integrating the [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md) over a sphere enclosing the origin gives traction resultant $-\mathbf F$, fixing the point-force normalization.
