# Membrane capacitive ion budget

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane capacitance](membrane-capacitance.md)

When a single [ion](ion.md) species supplies the net charging of a [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md), its bulk [concentration](concentration.md) increment is $\Delta c=C_mA\Delta V/(zF\mathcal V)$. Opposing ionic [Electric currents](electric-current.md) can make gross entry exceed this minimum net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) budget. A large [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) change can coexist with approximate bulk [electroneutrality](electroneutrality.md).
