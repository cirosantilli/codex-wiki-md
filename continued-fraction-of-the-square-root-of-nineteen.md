# Continued fraction of the square root of nineteen

↑ **Parent:** [Continued fraction](continued-fraction.md)

For a [quadratic irrational](quadratic-irrational-number.md) $\sqrt D$, write its [complete quotient](complete-quotient.md) as $(\sqrt D+m_j)/d_j$. The recurrence $m_{j+1}=d_ja_j-m_j$, $d_{j+1}=(D-m_{j+1}^2)/d_j$, $a_{j+1}=\lfloor(\sqrt D+m_{j+1})/d_{j+1}\rfloor$ computes its [continued fraction](continued-fraction.md). For $D=19$, the successive pairs after the initial quotient are $(4,3),(2,5),(3,2),(3,5),(2,3),(4,1)$, and then the cycle repeats. They give the displayed period of length six.
