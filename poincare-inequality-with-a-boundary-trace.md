# Poincare inequality with a boundary trace

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md)

On a bounded connected Lipschitz domain $U$,

$$
\|u\|_{L^2(U)}\leq C\left(\|Du\|_{L^2(U)}+\|\operatorname{Tr}u\|_{L^2(\partial U)}\right)
$$

for every $u\in H^1(U)$. If this failed, the [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) would produce a normalized strong $L^2$ limit with zero [gradient](gradient.md), hence a [constant function](constant-function.md); continuity of the [Sobolev trace theorem](sobolev-trace-theorem.md) would force that constant to vanish, contradicting its unit [norm](norm.md).
