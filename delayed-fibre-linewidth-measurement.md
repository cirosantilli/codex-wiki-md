# Delayed-fibre linewidth measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal optical coherence](temporal-optical-coherence.md)

Two delayed copies of the same laser field probe its [first-order optical coherence](first-order-optical-coherence.md). With equal detected intensities and matched [polarizations](polarization-waves.md), the [interferometric visibility](interferometric-visibility.md) is $V=|g^{(1)}(\tau)|$. A [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md) of frequency full width at half maximum $\Delta\nu$ gives $V=e^{-\pi\Delta\nu|\tau|}$. For a fibre length difference $\Delta l$, the delay is $\tau=n_g\Delta l/c$, where $n_g$ is the [group index](group-refractive-index.md). Hence $\Delta\nu=-\log V/(\pi|\tau|)$. Replacing $n_g$ by the effective phase index requires negligible dispersion. Polarization mismatch, unequal intensities and uncontrolled phase drift can reduce visibility independently of linewidth.
