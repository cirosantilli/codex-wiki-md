# Unfolding a reflected interval trajectory

↑ **Parent:** [Method of images](method-of-images.md)

Reflecting an interval repeatedly across its endpoints maps a reflected path to a free path in an image interval. A return trajectory from $q\in(0,L)$ ends at $q+2rL$ after an even number of wall reflections, or $-q+2rL$ after an odd number. An [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) has reflection amplitude $-1$, so the corresponding [Dirichlet heat kernel on an interval](dirichlet-heat-kernel-on-an-interval.md) is the difference of direct and mirrored free kernels. Individual straight paths illustrate the geometry; the [path integral](path-integral.md) sums all time-sliced paths with the same endpoints.
