# Step-index fiber normalized frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Step-index optical fiber](step-index-optical-fiber.md)

The dimensionless [waveguide](waveguide.md) parameter $V$ combines radius, vacuum [wavelength](wavelength.md) and the [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md) factor. In the weak-guidance scalar model the first additional spatial family appears at $V=j_{0,1}=2.404825\ldots$, the first positive zero of a [Bessel function of the first kind](bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md). The fundamental family has no positive cutoff. Its two [polarizations](polarization-waves.md) are not two distinct spatial profiles.
