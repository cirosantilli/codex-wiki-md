# Stationary set

↑ **Parent:** [Club set](club-set.md)

A subset of a regular uncountable [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$ is stationary if it meets every [club set](club-set.md) in $\kappa$. Intersecting a stationary set with a [club set](club-set.md) preserves stationarity. Every member of the [club filter](club-filter.md) is stationary. At a regular infinite $\theta<\kappa$, the [stationarity of ordinals of prescribed cofinality](stationarity-of-ordinals-of-prescribed-cofinality.md) supplies important examples.
