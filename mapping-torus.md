# Mapping torus

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapping_torus)

The mapping torus of $f:F\to F$ is

$$
T_f=(F\times[0,1])/((x,1)\sim(f(x),0)).
$$

If $f$ is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md), the projection to the [circle](circle.md) $S^1$ is a [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md) with fiber $F$. For a general [continuous map](continuous-map.md), the [mapping torus](mapping-torus.md) need not be a [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md), but its [homology](homology-split.md) still has the [Wang sequence](wang-sequence.md).
