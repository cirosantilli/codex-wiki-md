# Type scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Type variable](type-variable.md)

A [type scheme](type-scheme.md) binds [type variables](type-variable.md) that may be instantiated independently at different uses. The [identity function](identity-function.md) has [type scheme](type-scheme.md) $\forall\alpha.\alpha\to\alpha$: choose one type per use, then use that same choice on both sides of its [function type](function-type.md). A free [type variable](type-variable.md) shared with the [typing context](typing-context.md) is not automatically such an independently selectable parameter.
