# Linear valley

↑ **Parent:** [Geomorphology](geomorphology.md)

An unusually straight valley, sometimes localized along weakened [rock](rock-geology.md) in a [fault zone](fault-zone.md). Its alignment can suggest a [fault](geological-fault.md) trace, but erosion along bedding or other structural weaknesses can produce similar forms.
