# Image edge

↑ **Parent:** [Image processing](image-processing.md)

An [image edge](image-edge.md) is a rapid grey-value transition, modeled as a large [gradient](gradient.md) or a jump discontinuity. It differs from an [edge of a graph](edge-of-a-graph.md). Variational models can penalize [image edge](image-edge.md) length, while [diffusion image processing](diffusion-image-processing.md) estimates [image edge](image-edge.md) directions to limit [image smoothing](image-smoothing.md) across them.
