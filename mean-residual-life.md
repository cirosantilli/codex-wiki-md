# Mean residual life

↑ **Parent:** [Survival function](survival-function.md)

For a nonnegative [random variable](random-variable-split.md) with finite [expected value](expected-value.md) and $\mathbb P(X>t)>0$, its mean residual life is the remaining conditional [expected value](expected-value.md) after surviving past $t$. The [tail integral formula for moments](tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md) gives $m_X(t)=\int_t^\infty\mathbb P(X>x)\,dx/\mathbb P(X>t)$. The [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) has constant mean residual life, equal to its original mean. In [excess of loss reinsurance](excess-of-loss-reinsurance.md), the [total variance stationary condition for excess of loss](total-variance-stationary-condition-for-excess-of-loss.md) sets a candidate retention equal to this quantity.
