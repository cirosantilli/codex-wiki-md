# Double-opponent cell

↑ **Parent:** [Primary visual cortex](primary-visual-cortex.md)

A color-responsive [neuron](neuron-split.md) with opposite chromatic tuning in different spatial subregions. A simple filter is $r=(c_L-c_M)_{\rm center}-(c_L-c_M)_{\rm surround}$. It cancels a spatially uniform opponent signal but responds when the regions differ. Such filtering can support chromatic boundaries without by itself solving every illumination ambiguity.
