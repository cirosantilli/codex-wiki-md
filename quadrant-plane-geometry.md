# Quadrant (plane geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md)

The two coordinate axes divide a [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) in the plane into four open quadrants, distinguished by the signs of their coordinates. The [positive quadrant](positive-quadrant.md) is the first quadrant.
