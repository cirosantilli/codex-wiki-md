# Superfluid fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluid number density](superfluid-number-density.md)

In the [two-fluid model of superfluidity](two-fluid-model-of-superfluidity.md), the superfluid fraction is $\rho_s/\rho$, where $\rho_s$ is the superfluid mass density and $\rho$ the total mass density. For a one-component fluid of particles of the same mass this also equals $n_s/n$, using the [superfluid number density](superfluid-number-density.md) $n_s$ and total [number density](number-density.md) $n$. It measures the superfluid response and is distinct from the [condensate fraction](condensate-fraction.md), which measures occupation of a single-particle state. Interactions can make the two fractions very different.
