# Morse-Bott critical manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Morse theory](morse-theory.md)

A Morse-Bott critical manifold is a smooth submanifold $C$ of [critical points](critical-point.md) such that the kernel of the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) at every $x\in C$ is exactly $T_xC$. The Hessian is therefore nondegenerate in normal directions. Repeated based great-circle geodesics on a round [sphere](sphere.md) form such critical manifolds, parametrized by their unit initial directions.
