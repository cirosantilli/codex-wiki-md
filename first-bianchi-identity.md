# First Bianchi identity

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md)

For a torsion-free connection, $R^\mu{}_{\alpha\beta\gamma}+R^\mu{}_{\beta\gamma\alpha}+R^\mu{}_{\gamma\alpha\beta}=0$.
