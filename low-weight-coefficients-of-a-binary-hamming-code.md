# Low-weight coefficients of a binary Hamming code

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming code weight enumerator](hamming-code-weight-enumerator.md)

For $n=2^\ell-1$, a [codeword](codeword.md) in a [Hamming code](hamming-code.md) is a subset of the nonzero [vectors](vector.md) of $\mathbb F_2^\ell$ whose sum is zero. Ordered weight-three [supports of a vector](support-of-a-vector.md) are determined by two distinct nonzero [vectors](vector.md), giving $n(n-1)$ choices. For weight four, the third [vector](vector.md) must avoid the three nonzero [vectors](vector.md) in the [linear span](linear-span.md) of the first two, giving $n(n-1)(n-3)$ choices. For weight five, the first three must be linearly independent, and the fourth must avoid all seven nonzero [vectors](vector.md) in their [linear span](linear-span.md), giving $n(n-1)(n-3)(n-7)$ choices. The final [vector](vector.md) is their sum. Dividing by $3!$, $4!$, and $5!$ counts unordered [supports of a vector](support-of-a-vector.md) and proves the displayed [weight enumerator](weight-enumerator.md) coefficients.
