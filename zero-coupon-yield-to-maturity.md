# Zero-coupon yield to maturity

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md)

The continuously compounded yield of a unit-face [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) is the constant rate producing its current discount factor over the remaining maturity. It equals the maturity average of the [instantaneous forward rate](instantaneous-forward-rate.md). Differentiation gives $f(t,T)=R(t,T)+(T-t)R_T(t,T)$, and both rates tend to the [short rate](short-rate.md) as $T\downarrow t$. This definition uses continuous compounding; quoted coupon-bond yields require a different cash-flow equation.
