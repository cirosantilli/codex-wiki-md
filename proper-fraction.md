# Proper fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Fraction](fraction.md)

A nonnegative [fraction](fraction.md) with positive [denominator](denominator.md) and [numerator](numerator.md) smaller than its denominator, so its value lies in $[0,1)$. The zero value has the [reduced fraction](reduced-fraction.md) representation $0/1$. The term is sometimes extended to negative fractions by comparing absolute values; the nonnegative convention is the one used here.
