# Two-mode approximation for a Bose gas

↑ **Parent:** [Second quantization](second-quantization.md)

Project a [quantized field](quantized-field.md) onto two low-[energy](energy-split.md) [orbitals](single-particle-orbital.md). A sufficient regime has [temperature](temperature-split.md), hopping and interaction shifts small compared with the [energy gap](energy-gap.md) to discarded [orbitals](single-particle-orbital.md). Small interwell [contact interaction](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md) integrals can then be neglected while retaining a small [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) matrix element. The projected field's [operator commutator](operator-commutator.md) is the two-mode [projection operator](projection-linear-algebra.md) kernel, rather than a complete spatial [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md).
