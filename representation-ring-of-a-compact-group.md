# Representation ring of a compact group

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory](representation-theory-split.md)

The representation ring is the [Grothendieck group](grothendieck-group.md) of finite-dimensional complex [unitary representations](unitary-representation.md) of a [compact group](compact-group.md), with addition induced by [direct sum](direct-sum.md) and multiplication by [tensor product](tensor-product.md). As an abelian group it is freely generated by the irreducible [unitary representations](unitary-representation.md). Taking dimension gives a ring homomorphism $R(G)\to\mathbb Z$.
