# Scalar surface integral over an obliquely cut cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar surface integral](scalar-surface-integral.md)

Let $S$ be the whole boundary of $x^2+y^2\le R^2$, $a\le z-\alpha x\le b$, where $R>0$ and $a<b$. On the curved [cylinder](cylinder-geometry.md), the [parametrization](parametric-equation.md) $(R\cos\theta,R\sin\theta,\alpha R\cos\theta+s)$ has [surface area element](surface-area-element.md) $R\,d\theta\,ds$ and $\alpha x-z=-s$. Each planar cap has [area](surface-area.md) $\pi R^2\sqrt{1+\alpha^2}$. Summing the side and both caps gives the [scalar surface integral](scalar-surface-integral.md)

$$
\boxed{\int_S(\alpha x-z)\,dS=\pi R(a^2-b^2)-\pi R^2\sqrt{1+\alpha^2}(a+b).}
$$

The [surface area element](surface-area-element.md) is unsigned; cap orientation does not change the signs of the scalar integrand. This separates the geometric area factor from the field values and avoids mistaking a [scalar surface integral](scalar-surface-integral.md) for a [flux](flux.md).
