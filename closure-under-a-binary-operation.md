# Closure under a binary operation

↑ **Parent:** [Binary operation](binary-operation.md)

A [subset](subset.md) $A\subseteq S$ is closed under a [binary operation](binary-operation.md) on $S$ when the result of combining any two elements of $A$ is again in $A$. The [even integers](even-number.md) are closed under [addition](addition.md), but the [odd integers](odd-integer.md) are not: $1+3=4$ is even. Closure permits restricting the [binary operation](binary-operation.md) to $A\times A\to A$. It is different from being a [closed set](closed-set.md) in [topology](topology-split.md).
