# Finite language

↑ **Parent:** [Formal language theory](formal-language-theory.md)

A [formal language](formal-language.md) with finitely many [words](string.md). Every [finite language](finite-language.md) is a [regular language](regular-language.md): take a finite [union](set-union.md) of the [regular expressions](regular-expression.md) spelling its members, with the [empty language](empty-language.md) represented separately when there are no members.
