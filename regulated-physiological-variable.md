# Regulated physiological variable

↑ **Parent:** [Homeostasis](homeostasis.md)

A [regulated physiological variable](regulated-physiological-variable.md) is a specified internal quantity whose departures elicit actions tending to restore an appropriate range. Examples include [core body temperature](core-body-temperature.md), [blood glucose](blood-sugar.md) [concentration](concentration.md), [blood pressure](blood-pressure.md), and the [pH](ph.md) and [osmolality](osmolality.md) of [extracellular fluid](extracellular-fluid.md). Specify the compartment and readout: [skin](skin.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [core body temperature](core-body-temperature.md) are different quantities, and an [insulin](insulin.md) [concentration](concentration.md) is a regulatory message rather than the [blood glucose](blood-sugar.md) it helps control.
