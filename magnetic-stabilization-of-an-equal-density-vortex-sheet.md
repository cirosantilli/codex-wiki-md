# Magnetic stabilization of an equal-density vortex sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic interface wave](magnetohydrodynamic-interface-wave.md)

For two incompressible streams of equal density with a common tangential [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) parallel to their velocities, decaying [normal modes](normal-mode.md) and interface [displacement](displacement.md)/total-pressure continuity give $\rho(\sigma+k_xU_1)^2+\rho(\sigma+k_xU_2)^2=2k_x^2B^2$ in units $\mu_0=1$. The displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) follows, with $v_A^2=B^2/\rho$. Thus $|\Delta U|<2v_A$ excludes exponential [Kelvin-Helmholtz instability](kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md); equality is marginal. Above threshold the [growth rate](growth-rate.md) is $|k_x|\sqrt{(\Delta U)^2/4-v_A^2}$. The transverse horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) affects the decay length through $k=(k_x^2+k_y^2)^{1/2}$ but not this growth threshold. [Magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) supplies the stabilizing restoring force.
