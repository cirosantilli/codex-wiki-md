# Worldsheet moduli

↑ **Parent:** [Polyakov path integral](polyakov-path-integral.md)

Worldsheet moduli parameterize inequivalent conformal structures and marked insertion points after [worldsheet diffeomorphisms](worldsheet-diffeomorphism.md) and [Weyl invariance](weyl-transformation.md) are fixed. At each [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md), [string perturbation theory](string-perturbation-theory.md) integrates over the corresponding moduli space.
