# Abelian Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

An abelian [Lie group](lie-group.md) has commutative multiplication. Its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is an [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md), and its [Lie exponential map](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md) is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from the additive [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). Conversely, an additive [Lie exponential map](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md) forces the [identity component of a Lie group](identity-component-of-a-lie-group.md) to be abelian, but does not force all disconnected components to commute.
