# Electronic heat capacity coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic heat capacity](electronic-heat-capacity.md)

For a [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md), $C_{\rm el}=\gamma T+o(T)$ at low temperature. In a simple quasiparticle description, $\gamma=(\pi^2/3)k_B^2g(E_F)$, where the [density of states](density-of-states.md) includes both spin states and uses the same per-volume or per-mole normalization as $\gamma$. A fit $C/T=\gamma+\beta T^2$ separates this contribution from a Debye lattice term over an appropriate low-temperature range. Magnetic or other low-energy excitations can require extra fit terms.
