# CDN edge

↑ **Parent:** [Content delivery network](content-delivery-network.md)

A [CDN edge](cdn-edge.md) is a delivery [endpoint](communication-endpoint.md) of a [content delivery network](content-delivery-network.md). It may use [caching](caching.md) or fetch content, and a provider can authorise it to terminate a [client](network-client.md) connection. Its location near demand and its administrative authority are separate properties.
