# Epsilon-delta definition of a limit

↑ **Parent:** [Limit of a function](limit-of-a-function.md)

The assertion $f(x)\to L$ as $x\to a$ means that for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $\delta>0$ such that $0<|x-a|<\delta$ implies $|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon$.
