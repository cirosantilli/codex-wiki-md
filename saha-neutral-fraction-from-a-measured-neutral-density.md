# Saha neutral fraction from a measured neutral density

↑ **Parent:** [Saha ionization equation](saha-ionization-equation.md)

For charge-neutral pure [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md), write the [Saha equation](saha-ionization-equation.md) as $n_en_p/n_{\rm HI}=S(T)$. A measured neutral [number density](number-density.md) fixes $n_e=n_p=\sqrt{S(T)n_{\rm HI}}$, so the neutral fraction is $f_{\rm HI}=n_{\rm HI}/(n_p+n_{\rm HI})=(1+\sqrt{S/n_{\rm HI}})^{-1}$. At high ionization it scales as $\sqrt{n_{\rm HI}/S}$, rather than $n_{\rm HI}/S$. The latter mistake confuses measured neutral [number density](number-density.md) with total [number density](number-density.md). [Photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md) with an external nonthermal field generally requires a rate equation instead of the [Saha equation](saha-ionization-equation.md).
