# Suprafaciality

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)

Bond-making or bond-breaking at the same face of the interacting orbital array in a [pericyclic reaction](pericyclic-reaction.md). It is a local stereochemical relation, not a statement that all substituents end on one molecular face or a specification of an absolute clockwise rotation.
