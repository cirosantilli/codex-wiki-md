# Charged-particle equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Absorbed dose](absorbed-dose.md)

A dosimetric condition in which the charged-particle energy entering a small region balances the corresponding energy leaving it. Under appropriate equilibrium conditions, photon collision kerma equals [absorbed dose](absorbed-dose.md). It can fail near material boundaries or in the buildup region of a high-energy photon beam.
