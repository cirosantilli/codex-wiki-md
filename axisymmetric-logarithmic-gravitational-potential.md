# Axisymmetric logarithmic gravitational potential

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md)

An axisymmetric logarithmic [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) has spheroidal equipotential surfaces and a [flat galaxy rotation curve](flat-galaxy-rotation-curve.md): $R\Phi_R(R,0)=V^2$. The singular model is defined away from the origin; a fixed reference length makes the logarithm dimensionless. Its central $r^{-2}$ [mass density](density.md) cusp is locally integrable, but its total [mass](mass.md) diverges at large radius.
