# Hook-content formula

↑ **Parent:** [Schur polynomial](schur-polynomial.md)

For an [integer partition](integer-partition.md) $\lambda$, $s_\lambda(1^m)=\prod_{(i,j)\in\lambda}(m+j-i)/h_{ij}$, where $h_{ij}$ is the cell's [hook length](hook-length.md). It counts [Semistandard Young tableaux](semistandard-young-tableau.md) of shape $\lambda$ whose entries belong to $\{1,\ldots,m\}$.
