# Prime implicant

↑ **Parent:** [Implicant](implicant.md)

An [implicant](implicant.md) from which no [literal](boolean-literal.md) can be removed while preserving the implication to the [Boolean function](boolean-function.md). A [prime implicant](prime-implicant.md) is essential when it covers a one-input assignment that no other [prime implicant](prime-implicant.md) covers.
