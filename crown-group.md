# Crown group

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)

The [clade](clade.md) comprising the [last common ancestor](last-common-ancestor.md) of a stated set of living lineages and all its descendants. An older fossil closely related to that group may instead belong to its [stem group](stem-group.md).
