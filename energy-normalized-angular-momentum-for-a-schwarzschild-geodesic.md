# Energy-normalized angular momentum for a Schwarzschild geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md)

For an unbound equatorial [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md) [geodesic](geodesic.md), let $E_\infty$ and $L_\infty$ be its conserved [energy](energy-split.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) measured relative to infinity. The ratio $J=c^2L_\infty/E_\infty$ has dimensions of area divided by time. For a massive [test particle](test-particle.md), $E_\infty=m c^2\gamma_\infty$ and $L_\infty=m\ell$, so $J=\ell/\gamma_\infty=bv_\infty$, where $\ell$ is [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) and $b$ the [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md). For a [photon](photon.md), $E_\infty=cp_\infty$ and $L_\infty=bp_\infty$, giving $J=bc$ without assigning a finite [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) or a [proper time](proper-time.md) to the [photon](photon.md). This ratio removes the arbitrary normalization of a null [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md).
