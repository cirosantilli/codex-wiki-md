# Hard-sphere limit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering.md)

The hard-sphere limit of a repulsive spherical potential makes penetration into a sphere of radius $a$ vanish. Its low-energy [S wave](s-wave.md) scattering length tends to $a$, and its quantum total cross-section tends to $4\pi a^2$.
