# Mitochondrial membrane potential

↑ **Parent:** [Inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md)

The [electric potential](electric-potential.md) difference across the [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md), contributing to the [proton-motive force](proton-motive-force.md). A change in potential can alter both useful phosphorylation and leak behavior.
