# Inverse-clock proof of the Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md)

For a continuous strictly increasing unbounded bracket $V$, the inverse times $T_u=\inf\{t:V_t>u\}$ satisfy $V_{T_u}=u$ and $T_{V_t}=t$. Optional sampling of the original martingale stopped at bounded bracket levels makes $M_{T_u}$ a continuous local martingale in $\mathcal F_{T_u}$. Time-changing the square-minus-bracket martingale gives its bracket $u$; the [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md) identifies it as Brownian.
