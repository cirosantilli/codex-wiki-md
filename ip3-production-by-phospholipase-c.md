# IP3 production by phospholipase C

↑ **Parent:** [Inositol trisphosphate](inositol-trisphosphate.md)

[Phospholipase C](phospholipase-c.md) catalyzes [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) of membrane [PIP2](phosphatidylinositol-4-5-bisphosphate.md), producing soluble [IP3](inositol-trisphosphate.md) and membrane-associated [diacylglycerol](diacylglycerol.md). This consumes a [phospholipid](phospholipid.md) [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md), rather than manufacturing [calcium](calcium.md). [G protein](g-protein.md)-regulated and [receptor tyrosine kinase](receptor-tyrosine-kinase.md)-regulated forms of [PLC](phospholipase-c.md) provide different routes to this reaction.[Phospholipids and Ca2+, Figures 13.24–13.25: PIP2 hydrolysis and distinct PLC activation routes](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/pathways-of-intracellular-signal-transduction.md)
