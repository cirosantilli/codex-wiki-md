# Fore-aft symmetry of a sedimenting-sphere encounter

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic reversibility of Stokes flow](kinematic-reversibility-of-stokes-flow.md)

For a smooth passing encounter of a force-driven [sphere](sphere.md) with a fixed [sphere](sphere.md), isotropy and reflection symmetry imply $\mathbf U=[m_\perp(R)I+(m_\parallel(R)-m_\perp(R))\mathbf n\mathbf n]\mathbf F$. Along a [force](force.md) axis $X$, $U_X$ is even and the transverse [velocity](velocity.md) is odd in $X$. Uniqueness then makes the trajectory fore-aft symmetric and restores the incoming impact parameter downstream. This consequence of [kinematic reversibility of Stokes flow](kinematic-reversibility-of-stokes-flow.md) remains valid with [lubrication resistance](lubrication-resistance.md), provided the particles remain smooth, noncontacting and subject only to the stated [forces](force.md). Surface roughness and contact can invalidate these assumptions.
