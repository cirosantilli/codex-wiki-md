# Magnetic-field-dependent Langmuir adsorption

↑ **Parent:** [Langmuir adsorption model](langmuir-adsorption-model.md)

For independent spin-one-half adsorbates whose two [spin](spin.md) states are split only at the surface, the one-site [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) contains $2\cosh(\mu_{\rm B}B/k_{\rm B}T)$. Relative to the unsplit bulk [spin degeneracy](spin-degeneracy.md), this increases the [Langmuir adsorption model](langmuir-adsorption-model.md) affinity by a hyperbolic-cosine factor. At fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) and gas [pressure](pressure-split.md), increasing the magnitude of the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) increases [surface coverage](surface-coverage.md) in this model.
