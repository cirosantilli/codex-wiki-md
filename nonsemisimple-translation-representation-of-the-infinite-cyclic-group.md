# Nonsemisimple translation representation of the infinite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Complex representation](complex-representation.md)

On polynomials of degree at most one, let the generator of $\mathbb Z$ act by $p(x)\mapsto p(x+1)$. In the basis $(1,x)$ its matrix is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

This nontrivial [Jordan block](jordan-block.md) is not diagonalizable, so the representation is not a direct sum of one-dimensional subrepresentations.
