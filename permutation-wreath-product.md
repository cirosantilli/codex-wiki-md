# Permutation wreath product

↑ **Parent:** [Wreath product](wreath-product.md)

If a [group](group-split.md) $K$ acts on a finite set $X$, it acts on the [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) $H^X$ by permuting its factors. The resulting [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md) is the permutation wreath product. For $K=S_m$ acting on $m$ two-point blocks and $H=C_2$, it describes independent within-block swaps combined with a permutation of blocks, and has order $2^m m!$. The action on $X$ is part of the definition; it need not be the regular action used in a regular [wreath product](wreath-product.md).
