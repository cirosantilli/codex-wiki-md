# Finite-thickness magnetorotational instability criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation](ideal-magnetorotational-dispersion-relation.md)

If the horizontal [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) perturbations vanish at $z=\pm H$, their allowed [vertical wavenumbers](vertical-wavenumber.md) are $k_m=m\pi/(2H)$, $m=1,2,\ldots$. The lowest [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) is unstable exactly when

$$
0<\frac{\pi^2B_z^2}{8q\mu_0\rho H^2\Omega^2}<1.
$$

If that magnetic mode is stable, all higher magnetic modes are stable as well. A weak field admits longer unstable wavelengths that fit inside the slab; sufficiently strong [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) stabilizes every allowed wavelength.
