# H1 L3 interpolation inequality in three dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md)

On a bounded three-dimensional domain, interpolation between $L^2$ and the [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md) $H^1\hookrightarrow L^6$ gives

$$
\|u\|_{L^3}\leq C\|u\|_{L^2}^{1/2}\|u\|_{H^1}^{1/2}.
$$
