# Helium dimer

↑ **Parent:** [Diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md)

A very weakly bound van der Waals molecule, observed in cold [helium](helium.md) expansions. The absence of an ordinary [covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md) in the elementary [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) model must not be confused with absence of every possible bound dimer.[The weakest bond: experimental observation of helium dimer by Fei Luo and colleagues](the-weakest-bond-experimental-observation-of-helium-dimer-by-fei-luo-and-colleagues.md)
