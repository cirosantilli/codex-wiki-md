# Four-velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-velocity)

A massive observer's [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) is the tangent $U^\mu=dX^\mu/d\tau$ to its [worldline](world-line.md), parametrized by [proper time](proper-time.md). Its invariant squared [Minkowski norm](minkowski-norm.md) is $c^2$ in the $+---$ [metric signature](metric-signature.md), and $-c^2$ in the opposite convention. Unlike the coordinate values $X^\mu$, the tangent transforms as a [vector](vector.md) under a general coordinate change.
