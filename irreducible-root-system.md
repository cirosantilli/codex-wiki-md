# Irreducible root system

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

A [root system](root-system.md) spanning a real [inner product space](inner-product-space.md) is irreducible when it cannot be partitioned into two nonempty mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) [root systems](root-system.md). Its [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) is connected. This condition concerns the roots themselves, rather than an arbitrary ambient space larger than their span.
