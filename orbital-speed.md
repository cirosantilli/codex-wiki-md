# Orbital speed

↑ **Parent:** [Celestial mechanics](celestial-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_speed)

Orbital [speed](speed.md) is the magnitude of the [velocity](velocity.md) of an orbiting body in a specified reference frame. For a Newtonian [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) around a point source of [mass](mass.md) $M$, equating $v^2/r$ to $GM/r^2$ gives $v=\sqrt{GM/r}$. In a curved [spacetime](spacetime.md), a speed inferred from coordinate radius and coordinate time generally differs from a speed measured by a local observer. For a circular orbit in the [Schwarzschild metric](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md), a static local observer measures $v_{\rm local}=r|d\phi/dt|/\sqrt{1-2GM/(c^2r)}$; $r|d\phi/dt|$ uses the clock normalization at infinity instead.
