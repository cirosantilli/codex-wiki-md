# Double cochain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Double complex](double-complex.md)

Two degree-raising differentials square to zero and anticommute. If an initial convention uses commuting differentials, introduce a sign in one of them before forming the [total cochain complex](total-cochain-complex.md).
