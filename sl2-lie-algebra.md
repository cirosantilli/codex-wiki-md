# sl2 Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md)

The Lie algebra $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ has generators $e,h,f$ with $[h,e]=2e$, $[h,f]=-2f$, and $[e,f]=h$.

This is the $n=2$ instance of the [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md). Classifying its modules is a separate representation-theory problem.
