# Central path

↑ **Parent:** [Self-concordant barrier](self-concordant-barrier.md)

For a strictly feasible primal-dual [conic optimization](conic-optimization.md) problem and a logarithmically homogeneous barrier, the central path consists of solutions

$$
s=Ax-b\in\operatorname{int}K,\quad A^\top y=c,\quad y\in\operatorname{int}K^*,\quad y=-\mu\nabla F(s),\quad\mu>0.
$$

The primal-dual gap is $\langle s,y\rangle=\nu\mu$. Existence requires appropriate feasibility and boundedness hypotheses, rather than merely a full-rank constraint matrix. Linearizing these equations gives a [central-path Newton system](central-path-newton-system.md).
