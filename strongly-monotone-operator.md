# Strongly monotone operator

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone operator](monotone-operator.md)

A map from a subset of a real [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) to its [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md) is strongly monotone if the displayed inequality holds for every pair in its domain, with one constant $m>0$. The brackets denote the [dual pairing](dual-pairing.md). This implies strict [monotonicity](monotonic-function.md) for distinct arguments. Fixing $v=u_0$ and applying the dual norm inequality gives $\langle A(u),u-u_0\rangle\ge m\|u-u_0\|^2-\|A(u_0)\|\|u-u_0\|$, so it is a [nonlinear coercive operator](nonlinear-coercive-operator.md) with respect to $u_0$.
