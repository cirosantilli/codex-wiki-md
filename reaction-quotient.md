# Reaction quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md)

For a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) with signed [stoichiometric vector](stoichiometric-vector.md) entries $\nu_i$, positive for products and negative for reactants, the reaction quotient is the product of the species' [thermodynamic activities](thermodynamic-activity.md) raised to these powers. It equals the [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md) at [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md), with all quantities using the same [standard state](standard-state.md) convention.
