# Exponential substitution removing a constant first derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md)

Differentiating $y=e^{ax}u$ twice proves the displayed identity. It converts an exponentially weighted forced equation into a [simple harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) equation. If $b=0$, the reduced homogeneous solutions are $1,x$ rather than $\cos bx,\sin bx$ as an independent pair. If the forcing contains a term at frequency $|b|$, [resonance](resonance.md) requires an additional polynomial factor in a particular solution.
