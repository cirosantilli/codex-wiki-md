# Spherical inversion preserves angular derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin transform](kelvin-transform.md)

For $\overline{\mathbf r}=a^2\mathbf r/r^2$, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $\nabla_{\mathbf r}=(a^2/r^2)(I-2\widehat{\mathbf r}\widehat{\mathbf r}^{\,T})\nabla_{\overline{\mathbf r}}$. The radial term is killed by crossing with $\mathbf r$, giving the identity. Purely angular [vector spherical harmonics](vector-spherical-harmonic.md) are therefore unchanged as angular basis functions. This is distinct from pushing forward a physical vector field by the inversion Jacobian.
