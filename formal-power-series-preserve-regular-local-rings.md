# Formal power series preserve regular local rings

↑ **Parent:** [Regular local ring](regular-local-ring.md)

Let $(A,\mathfrak m)$ be a [regular local ring](regular-local-ring.md) of [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) $d$. Its [formal power series ring](formal-power-series.md) $B=A[[x]]$ is [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md) and local, with [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) $I=\mathfrak mB+(x)$ and [embedding dimension](embedding-dimension.md) $d+1$. Since $I$ is generated by $d+1$ elements, the [Krull height theorem](krull-height-theorem.md) gives $\dim B\leq d+1$. For the reverse inequality choose a strict prime chain $\mathfrak p_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq\mathfrak p_d=\mathfrak m$ in $A$. Finite generation of each $\mathfrak p_i$ gives $B/\mathfrak p_iB\cong(A/\mathfrak p_i)[[x]]$, an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), so each extended [ideal](ideal.md) is prime. The inclusions remain strict by contraction to $A$, and $\mathfrak mB\subsetneq I$ adds one more step since $x\notin\mathfrak mB$. This proves $\dim B=d+1$, equal to its [embedding dimension](embedding-dimension.md), and hence regularity.
