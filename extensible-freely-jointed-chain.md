# Extensible freely jointed chain

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal chain](ideal-chain.md)

A [freely jointed chain](ideal-chain.md) supplemented with axial segment compliance. A simple phenomenological additive law is $z=L\mathcal L(fb/(k_BT))+Lf/S$, where $L=Nb$, $\mathcal L$ is the [Langevin function](langevin-function.md) and $S$ is an axial stretch modulus with units of force. This adds energetic compliance to the [entropic spring](entropic-spring.md); it is not an exact angular average for arbitrary harmonic-link statistics.
