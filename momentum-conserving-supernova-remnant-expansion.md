# Momentum-conserving supernova-remnant expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Supernova remnant](supernova-remnant.md)

In the ideal cooled-shell limit with negligible continuing interior pressure, swept-up mass in a uniform medium scales as $R^3$. Constant radial shell [momentum](momentum-split.md) then gives $R^3\dot R={\rm const}$ and $R\propto t^{1/4}$. This differs from an energy-conserving blast wave or a pressure-driven snowplough.
