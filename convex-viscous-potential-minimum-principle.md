# Convex viscous potential minimum principle

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous dissipation potential](viscous-dissipation-potential.md)

An inertialess [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md) with symmetric [stress](stress.md) generated by a convex [viscous dissipation potential](viscous-dissipation-potential.md) minimizes $\int\Omega(D)\,dx-\int b\cdot v\,dx-\int_{S_t}t\cdot v\,dS$ among fields satisfying the prescribed [velocities](velocity.md), and any [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) constraint. The supporting-hyperplane inequality bounds the difference of two functional values below by $\int S:\nabla w-\int b\cdot w-\int_{S_t}t\cdot w$. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md), equilibrium $\operatorname{div}S+b=0$ and $w=0$ on the [velocity](velocity.md) boundary make this lower bound zero. A [pressure](pressure.md) reaction has zero contraction with divergence-free variations. Convexity proves global minimality without asserting uniqueness.
