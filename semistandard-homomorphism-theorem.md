# Semistandard homomorphism theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

A filling $T$ of shape $\lambda$ and content $\alpha$ defines a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) $\Theta_T:M^\lambda\to M^\alpha$: given a numbered tableau, sum over distinct assignments obtained by permuting $T$ within its rows, and put each numbered entry into output row $j$ when its assigned label is $j$. Restricting to $S^\lambda$ gives $\widehat\Theta_T$. For [Semistandard Young tableaux](semistandard-young-tableau.md) $T$, these restrictions are linearly independent over every [field](field.md). They form a basis of $\operatorname{Hom}_{FS_n}(S^\lambda,M^\alpha)$ if the [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md) is not two, or if $\lambda$ is a two-regular [partition of an integer](partition-of-an-integer.md). For two-singular shapes in [characteristic two](characteristic-two.md), they need not span. Column antisymmetrization and triangular leading [tabloids](tabloid.md) establish independence; straightening the images of a cyclic [polytabloid](polytabloid.md) establishes spanning under the stated hypotheses.
