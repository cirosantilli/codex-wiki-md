# Synaptojanin

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[synaptojanin](synaptojanin.md) is a [phosphoinositide](phosphoinositide.md) phosphatase involved in endocytic lipid turnover. Loss of its activity can affect coat removal and [synaptic vesicle recycling](synaptic-vesicle-recycling.md); genetic defects need to be separated from secondary developmental changes.
