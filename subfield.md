# Subfield

↑ **Parent:** [Field](field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subfield)

A subfield of a [field](field.md) $K$ is a subset containing the additive and multiplicative identities and closed under addition, subtraction, multiplication, and inversion of nonzero elements. It is itself a field under the operations inherited from $K$.
