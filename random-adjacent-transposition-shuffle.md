# Random adjacent transposition shuffle

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain.md)

On the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_n$, this [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) chooses uniformly from the identity and the $n-1$ adjacent [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md), and multiplies on the right. Its [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) is the [uniform distribution on a finite set](discrete-uniform-distribution.md), because the generators are self-inverse. A fixed label's position is a [lumped Markov chain](lumped-markov-chain.md) on a [path graph](path-graph.md) with off-diagonal [transition probabilities](transition-probability.md) $1/n$ to existing neighbours.
