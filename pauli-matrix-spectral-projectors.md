# Pauli-matrix spectral projectors

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md)

For $H=\mathbf d\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ with $\mathbf d\ne0$, the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\pm|\mathbf d|$ and the projectors are $P_s=(I+s\widehat{\mathbf d}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)/2$. A normalized [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) has $\langle\boldsymbol\sigma\rangle=s\widehat{\mathbf d}$. At $\mathbf d=0$ the spectrum is degenerate and that direction is undefined.
