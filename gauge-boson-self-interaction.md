# Gauge boson self-interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

[Gauge boson self-interactions](gauge-boson-self-interaction.md) arise from the quadratic-field term in a non-Abelian [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md). Squaring that field strength in the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) yields cubic and quartic pure-gauge [interaction vertices](interaction-vertex.md). Higher-point scattering can occur by connecting these vertices, even without a corresponding fundamental higher-valence vertex.
