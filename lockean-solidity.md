# Lockean solidity

↑ **Parent:** [Primary quality](primary-quality.md)

In [John Locke](john-locke.md)'s terminology, a body's exclusion of another body from the space it occupies. This is not the material-science notion of [hardness](hardness.md): [John Locke](john-locke.md) treats water as solid in this sense while distinguishing its yielding response from a diamond's resistance.[Book II, chapter IV, especially section 4: [https://barryfvaughan.org/text/philtext/locke/bk2/4.html](https://barryfvaughan.org/text/philtext/locke/bk2/4.html)](an-essay-concerning-human-understanding-by-john-locke.md)
