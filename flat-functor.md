# Flat functor

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor.md)

A covariant [functor](functor.md) into a [Grothendieck topos](grothendieck-topos.md) is flat when its tensor extension from presheaves preserves [finite limits](finite-limit.md). For set values, its [category of elements](category-of-elements.md) with arrows carrying source elements to target elements is cofiltered; equivalently the [functor](functor.md) is a filtered [colimit](colimit.md) of covariant representables. If the indexing category has [finite limits](finite-limit.md), flatness is equivalent to preservation of [finite limits](finite-limit.md). For a [site](site-category-theory.md), cover-to-joint-epimorphism continuity adds the condition needed by the [Diaconescu equivalence for geometric morphisms](diaconescu-equivalence-for-geometric-morphisms.md).
