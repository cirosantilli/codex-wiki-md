# Paleoclimate

↑ **Parent:** [Climate](climate.md)

The climate of an earlier geological interval, inferred from dated geological, geochemical or biological indicators. Spatially coherent glacial deposits, striations and climate-sensitive rocks can test a [continental reconstruction](continental-reconstruction.md), provided their ages and original tectonic contexts are established.
