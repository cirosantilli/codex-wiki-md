# Islet perifusion experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Glucagon](glucagon.md)

[Intraislet glucagon signaling is critical for maintaining glucose homeostasis by Lu Zhu and others](intraislet-glucagon-signaling-is-critical-for-maintaining-glucose-homeostasis-by-lu-zhu-and-others.md) tested whether local [glucagon](glucagon.md) supports [insulin](insulin.md) release. For human [pancreatic islets](pancreatic-islets.md), a computer-controlled flow system delivered a [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md) at $1\,\mathrm{ml}/\mathrm{min}$ through a chamber holding 500 [pancreatic islets](pancreatic-islets.md) on a nylon filter at $37\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. A fraction collector sampled the outgoing fluid. With an [amino acid](amino-acid.md) mixture present, the experiment changed [glucose](glucose.md) from $3$ to $16.7\,\mathrm{mM}$ and compared [GLP-1 receptor](glucagon-like-peptide-1-receptor.md) blockade with fluid lacking the blocker. The readout was released [insulin](insulin.md) and [glucagon](glucagon.md): blockade reduced [glucose](glucose.md)-stimulated [insulin](insulin.md) release without significantly changing [glucagon](glucagon.md) release. [Intraislet glucagon signaling is critical for maintaining glucose homeostasis by Lu Zhu and others](intraislet-glucagon-signaling-is-critical-for-maintaining-glucose-homeostasis-by-lu-zhu-and-others.md)

Complementary mouse experiments suppressed [alpha cell](alpha-cell.md) activity using an engineered [G protein-coupled receptor](g-protein-coupled-receptor.md). [ELISA](elisa.md) assays of minute-by-minute effluent samples measured [insulin](insulin.md) and [glucagon](glucagon.md) [concentrations](concentration.md), providing the [hormone](hormone.md) release readout. Drug-treated control [pancreatic islets](pancreatic-islets.md) lacking that engineered [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) checked for drug effects; adding [glucagon](glucagon.md) restored [insulin](insulin.md) release in the suppressed preparation. Together, blockade and rescue support local [signal transduction](signal-transduction-split.md) from [alpha cells](alpha-cell.md) to [beta cells](beta-cell.md). Isolated [pancreatic islets](pancreatic-islets.md) omit intact circulation and nervous input; the human preparation came from five donors, and blocker specificity and donor variation limit generalization. A chamber response does not directly measure whole-body [blood glucose](blood-sugar.md) control. [Intraislet glucagon signaling is critical for maintaining glucose homeostasis by Lu Zhu and others](intraislet-glucagon-signaling-is-critical-for-maintaining-glucose-homeostasis-by-lu-zhu-and-others.md)
