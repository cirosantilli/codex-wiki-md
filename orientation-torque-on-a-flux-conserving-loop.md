# Orientation torque on a flux-conserving loop

↑ **Parent:** [Flux conservation in a superconducting loop](flux-conservation-in-a-superconducting-loop.md)

For a loop of area $A$ and [self-inductance](self-inductance.md) $L$, negligible resistance conserves $LI+\Phi_{\rm ext}$. With zero trapped total flux and $\Phi_{\rm ext}=AB_0\sin(\omega t)\cos\theta$, the [electric current](electric-current.md) is $I=-AB_0\sin(\omega t)\cos\theta/L$. The [magnetic torque](surface-magnetic-torque-on-an-accretion-disk.md) generalized to the angle between loop normal and field is $\tau_\theta=-IAB\sin\theta$. For an orientation varying slowly over a field cycle, averaging gives the displayed torque and effective potential $U_{\rm av}=A^2B_0^2\cos^2\theta/(4L)$. Its stable orientations have loop normal perpendicular to the applied field. Without [damping](damping.md), a freely released loop librates around such an orientation instead of settling there.
