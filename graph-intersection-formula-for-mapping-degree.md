# Graph intersection formula for mapping degree

↑ **Parent:** [Relative mapping degree](relative-mapping-degree.md)

For a smooth map of disk pairs whose [graph of a function](graph-of-a-function.md) is transverse to the zero slice, orient the graph by its domain. With the tangent space of the zero slice ordered before that of the graph, the local [smooth intersection number](smooth-intersection-number.md) at a zero is $\operatorname{sgn}\det DF$. Summing these signs gives the relative [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) and hence the boundary [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md). Reversing the order of these two $m$-dimensional tangent spaces changes the sign by $(-1)^m$.
