# Phonon dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon](phonon.md)

A phonon dispersion relation gives its angular frequency $\omega$ as a function of its [wavevector](wavevector.md) $k$. For an isotropic power law, $\omega=C|k|^\alpha$.
