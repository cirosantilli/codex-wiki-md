# Product-state obstruction to SWAP universality

↑ **Parent:** [SWAP gate](swap-gate.md)

The [SWAP gate](swap-gate.md) and local [unitary operators](unitary-operator.md) preserve [product states](product-state.md). The displayed identity moves every exchange past a pair of local gates, reducing any such two-[qubit](qubit.md) [quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md) to a local operation, optionally followed by [SWAP gate](swap-gate.md). On more wires the same argument leaves a permutation of the tensor factors. By induction, a fully factored input remains factored. Since [universal quantum computation](universal-quantum-computation.md) includes operations that create [quantum entanglement](entangled-state.md) from a [product state](product-state.md), these gates cannot form a [universal quantum gate set](universal-quantum-gate-set.md).
