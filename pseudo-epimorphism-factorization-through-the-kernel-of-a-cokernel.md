# Pseudo-epimorphism factorization through the kernel of a cokernel

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudo-epimorphism](pseudo-epimorphism.md)

In a [pointed category](pointed-category.md) with [categorical kernels](kernel-in-a-category.md) and [categorical cokernels](cokernel-in-a-category.md), where every [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) is normal, every $h$ factors through $i=\ker(\operatorname{coker}h)$ as $h=ie$, with $e$ a [pseudo-epimorphism](pseudo-epimorphism.md). If $te=0$, write $k=\ker t$. Then $e$ factors through $k$, and the monic $ik$ is a [categorical kernel](kernel-in-a-category.md) of some $q$ by normality. Since $qh=0$, the cokernel of $h$ forces $qi=0$. Hence $i$ factors through $ik$, forcing $k$ to be an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) and therefore $t=0$. This establishes the factorization without assuming the stronger [abelian category](abelian-category.md) axioms.
