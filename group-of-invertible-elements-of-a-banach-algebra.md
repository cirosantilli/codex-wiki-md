# Group of invertible elements of a Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

The invertible elements of a unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) form an open multiplicative [group](group-split.md). For $a\in G(A)$, every $b$ satisfying $\|a^{-1}\|\|b-a\|<1$ is invertible, because $b=a(1+a^{-1}(b-a))$ and the second factor has a [Neumann series](neumann-series.md) inverse.
