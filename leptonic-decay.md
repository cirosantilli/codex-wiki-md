# Leptonic decay

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)

A leptonic decay has only [leptons](lepton.md) in its non-radiative final state. For example, a charged [pion](pion.md) can decay as $\pi^+\to\mu^+\nu_\mu$. A [semileptonic decay](semileptonic-decay.md) instead retains at least one [hadron](hadron.md) alongside the leptons. This classification concerns the final particles, not whether the initial particle is a hadron or a lepton.
