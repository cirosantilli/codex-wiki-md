# Reciprocal lattice vector

↑ **Parent:** [Reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md)

A reciprocal lattice vector $\mathbf G$ has the displayed property for every direct [lattice vector](lattice-vector.md) $\mathbf R$. Thus $e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf R}=1$. Fourier components of a lattice-periodic potential carry these wavevectors and couple plane-wave electronic states whose momenta differ by $\hbar\mathbf G$. This is the origin of the Bragg-plane mixing in a [nearly-free electron model](nearly-free-electron-model.md).
