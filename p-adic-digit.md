# p-adic digit

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic integer](p-adic-integer.md)

The [p-adic digits](p-adic-digit.md) are the unique coefficients in $x=\sum_{j\geq0}b_jp^j$ for a [p-adic integer](p-adic-integer.md) $x$. Taking least nonnegative representatives of its [congruence classes](congruence-class.md),

$$
r_{n+1}=r_n+b_np^n,
$$

so each new residue determines one more [p-adic digit](p-adic-digit.md). For $13$ in $\mathbb Z_2$, the low-to-high digits are $1,0,1,1,0,\ldots$, giving $13=1+0\cdot2+1\cdot4+1\cdot8$. A finite prefix describes a whole [coset](coset.md), not a unique infinite expansion.
