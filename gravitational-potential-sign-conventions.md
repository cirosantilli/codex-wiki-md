# Gravitational potential sign conventions

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md)

The usual [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) satisfies $\mathbf g=-\nabla\Phi$ and $\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho$. An equally consistent convention sets $\varphi=-\Phi$, giving $\mathbf g=\nabla\varphi$ and $\nabla^2\varphi=-4\pi G\rho$. With zero potential at infinity, a [point mass](point-mass.md) has $\Phi=-GM/r$ and $\varphi=GM/r$. Both conventions describe the same inward [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) and the same [Gauss's law for gravity](gauss-s-law-for-gravity.md). Only the signs of the potential and its differences change. The gravitational [potential energy](potential-energy.md) per unit test [mass](mass.md) is $\Phi$, or $-\varphi$ in the alternative convention. A formula for a potential difference must therefore be read together with its relation to the [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md).
