# Separation of number sets in an arithmetic theory

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md)

A formula separates the number [set](set-split.md) $A$ from $B$ in $S$ if the displayed numeralwise proof implications hold. No decision is required outside $A\cup B$. In a [consistent](consistent-first-order-theory.md) [theory](first-order-theory.md) the [sets](set-split.md) must be disjoint. A diagonal sentence asserting the [negation](negation.md) of its membership in a separator of provable from refutable codes is undecidable by the two direct [contradiction](contradiction.md) cases.
