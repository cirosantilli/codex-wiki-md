# Newton-Cotes multistep methods attaining the first Dahlquist barrier

↑ **Parent:** [First Dahlquist barrier](first-dahlquist-barrier.md)

Using [Newton-Cotes closed quadrature](newton-cotes-closed-quadrature.md) for the integrated derivative yields the displayed [linear multistep method](linear-multistep-method.md). Its first [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\rho(z)=z^s-1$, whose unit-circle roots are all simple, proving [zero-stability](zero-stability.md). Quadrature exactness gives order $s+1$ for odd $s$ and at least $s+2$ for even $s$. The [first Dahlquist barrier](first-dahlquist-barrier.md) gives equality, while the [Dahlquist equivalence theorem](dahlquist-equivalence-theorem.md) gives convergence with consistent starts. These are finite-time convergence statements, not claims of [A-stability](a-stability.md).
