# Proper time

↑ **Parent:** [Spacetime](spacetime.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proper_time)

Proper time is the time measured by a clock following a timelike worldline. With metric signature $(-,+,+,+)$ and units $c=1$,

$$
d\tau^2=-ds^2
$$

along that worldline.

Restoring the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md), the same [metric signature](metric-signature.md) gives $d\tau=\sqrt{-ds^2}/c=dt\sqrt{1-|\mathbf v|^2/c^2}$ for a future-directed [timelike curve](timelike-curve.md). Integrating along a clock's [world line](world-line.md) gives its elapsed [proper time](proper-time.md), even when its [velocity](velocity.md) varies. For uniform motion this becomes the [time dilation](time-dilation.md) formula; it is not generally the square root of the interval between endpoints of a curved [world line](world-line.md).
