# Physical address

↑ **Parent:** [Computer memory](computer-memory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_address)

An address in the physical address system, obtained from a [virtual address](virtual-address.md) by [address translation](address-translation.md) in the examples here. Physical address ranges can also identify devices, so not every [physical address](physical-address.md) denotes ordinary [computer memory](computer-memory.md).
