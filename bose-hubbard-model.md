# Bose-Hubbard model

↑ **Parent:** [Second quantization](second-quantization.md)

A lattice [boson](boson.md) [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) combining hopping and on-site repulsion. The on-site interaction counts distinct particle pairs through $n_i(n_i-1)/2$. It is the bosonic counterpart of the [Hubbard model](hubbard-model.md); projecting a continuum [contact interaction](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md) onto localized [orbitals](single-particle-orbital.md) gives $U=g\int|\varphi_i|^4$ when intersite overlaps are negligible.
