# Reversibility of an autonomous differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Time reversal of an autonomous differential equation](time-reversal-of-an-autonomous-differential-equation.md)

A reversing involution $R$ maps a trajectory $x(t)$ to another trajectory $Rx(-t)$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives the displayed condition. In contrast, an ordinary equivariance [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) satisfies $F(Sx)=DS(x)F(x)$ and maps trajectories with time unchanged. Oddness $F(-x)=-F(x)$ expresses half-turn equivariance, not reversal by the half-turn. Damping usually breaks the mechanical candidate $R(x,y)=(x,-y)$.
