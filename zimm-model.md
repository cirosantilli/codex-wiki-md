# Zimm model

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

A dilute-solution bead-spring model including solvent-mediated [hydrodynamic interactions](hydrodynamic-interaction.md). In its scaling regime, a chain of size $R$ has translational [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) of order $k_BT/(\eta_sR)$ and longest relaxation time of order $\eta_sR^3/(k_BT)$. Intermediate-time segment mean-square displacements scale as $t^{2/3}$. Screening of hydrodynamic interactions in a concentrated solution changes the regime toward [Rouse model](rouse-model.md) behaviour; constraints from [polymer entanglement](polymer-entanglement.md) require additional physics.
