# Matching number

↑ **Parent:** [Matching (graph theory)](matching-graph-theory.md)

The matching number $\nu(G)$ is the largest number of edges in a matching of $G$.

The matching number $\nu(G)$ is the maximum cardinality of a [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md).
