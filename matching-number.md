# Matching number

↑ **Parent:** [Matching (graph theory)](matching-graph-theory-split.md)

For a finite [graph](graph-split.md) $G$, the [matching number](matching-number.md) is

$$
\nu(G)=\max\{|M|:M\text{ is a matching of }G\}.
$$

It is the [cardinality](cardinality.md) of a [maximum cardinality matching](maximum-cardinality-matching.md). The vertex capacity constraint gives $0\leq\nu(G)\leq\lfloor |V(G)|/2\rfloor$. The worked [path graph](path-graph.md) examples have $\nu(P_4)=2$ and $\nu(P_6)=3$. A [perfect matching](perfect-matching.md) reaches $|V(G)|/2$; the empty [graph](graph-split.md) has [matching number](matching-number.md) zero.
