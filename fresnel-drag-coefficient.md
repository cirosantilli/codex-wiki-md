# Fresnel drag coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Fizeau experiment](fizeau-experiment.md)

The [Fresnel drag coefficient](fresnel-drag-coefficient.md) is the factor $f=1-1/n^2$ multiplying a transparent medium's [velocity](velocity.md) in the first-order change of collinear [light](light.md) [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md), when [optical dispersion](dispersion-optics.md) is neglected. Here $n$ is the medium's [refractive index](refractive-index.md) in its [rest frame](rest-frame.md). The name recalls [Augustin Jean Fresnel](augustin-jean-fresnel.md)'s partial-drag law; [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) obtains the same coefficient from the [velocity-addition formula](velocity-addition-formula.md) without introducing a [luminiferous aether](luminiferous-aether.md).

Let the [water](water.md) move at signed [velocity](velocity.md) $u$ along the positive laboratory axis, with $|u|\ll c/n$, where $c$ is the vacuum [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). Assume a transparent medium with constant $n>1$, uniform flow and negligible [optical dispersion](dispersion-optics.md). In the [water](water.md)'s local [rest frame](rest-frame.md), a collinear constant-phase surface travels at $v'=c/n$. Dividing the [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) differentials along that surface gives the same longitudinal [velocity-addition formula](velocity-addition-formula.md) as for a particle:

$$
v_{\rm lab}=\frac{c/n+u}{1+u/(nc)}
=\left(\frac cn+u\right)\left(1-\frac{u}{nc}+O\left(\frac{u^2}{c^2}\right)\right)
=\frac cn+u\left(1-\frac1{n^2}\right)+O\left(\frac{u^2}{c}\right).
$$

The correction comes from the transformed time denominator. Simply adding $u$ to $c/n$ would omit it. This argument concerns collinear [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md); in a medium with [optical dispersion](dispersion-optics.md), [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is a different quantity.[Section III, pp. 498–499, equation (1) and discussion of the collinear wave-four-vector derivation](fizeau%E2%80%99s-%E2%80%9Caether-drag%E2%80%9D-experiment-in-the-undergraduate-laboratory-by-thierry-lahaye-and-others.md)

For $n=4/3$, the [Fresnel drag coefficient](fresnel-drag-coefficient.md) is $f=7/16$. A chosen flow [speed](speed.md) of $6\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ therefore changes the forward [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) by approximately $2.625\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, even though the stationary-medium [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) is about $2.25\times10^8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. These are model values. The invariant vacuum [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) remains $c$; $c/n$ describes propagation through matter in its own [rest frame](rest-frame.md).
