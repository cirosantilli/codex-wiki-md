# Genetic variance

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

The component of variation in a specified trait attributable to differences among [genotypes](genotype.md) in the stated environment and population. Additive [genetic variance](genetic-variance.md) is distinguished from contributions due to [dominance](dominance-genetics.md) and [epistasis](epistasis.md).
