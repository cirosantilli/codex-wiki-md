# Velocity-alignment energy dissipation

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field alignment equation](mean-field-alignment-equation.md)

For the [mean-field alignment equation](mean-field-alignment-equation.md), multiply by $|\mathbf v|^2/2$ and use [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). Exchanging $z$ with $z_\star$ and averaging the two results gives the displayed nonpositive [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) derivative. The factor $1/2$ accounts for counting ordered pairs twice. Total [momentum](momentum-split.md) is conserved, so the dissipated part is motion relative to the global [mean velocity](mean-velocity-of-a-kinetic-distribution.md).
