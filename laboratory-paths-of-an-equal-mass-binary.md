# Laboratory paths of an equal-mass binary

↑ **Parent:** [Two-body problem](two-body-problem.md)

For equal masses and relative [position](position.md) $\mathbf r$, the individual positions are $\mathbf r_A=\mathbf R-\mathbf r/2$ and $\mathbf r_B=\mathbf R+\mathbf r/2$, where the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) has uniform [velocity](velocity.md) in an isolated system. Bound [inverse-square force](inverse-square-force.md) motion makes $\mathbf r$ an [ellipse](ellipse.md); each mass follows a half-size ellipse in the center-of-mass frame. Adding a nonzero center-of-mass drift generally makes their laboratory [trajectories](trajectory.md) nonclosed. The relative orbital period is not necessarily a period of either laboratory position.
