<h1 id="dirichlet-s-unit-theorem">Dirichlet's unit theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet's_unit_theorem)

If a number field has $r$ real embeddings and $s$ conjugate pairs of complex embeddings, then

$$
\mathcal O_K^\times\cong\mu(K)\times\mathbb Z^{r+s-1},
$$

where $\mu(K)$ is the finite group of roots of unity in $K$.
