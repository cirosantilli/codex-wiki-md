# Euclidean magnitude-limited source count

↑ **Parent:** [Apparent magnitude](apparent-magnitude.md)

For identical [luminosities](luminosity.md), uniform spatial density, and no extinction, the [inverse-square law](inverse-square-law.md) gives limiting distance $r\propto F_{\min}^{-1/2}$. The number in the enclosed volume is therefore $N\propto F_{\min}^{-3/2}\propto10^{0.6m_{\lim}}$. Increasing the sample by a factor $b$ needs a magnitude-limit increase $(5/3)\log_{10}b$. This relation need not hold with evolving source populations, dust, or cosmological distance effects.
