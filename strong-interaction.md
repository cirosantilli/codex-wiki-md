# Strong interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_interaction)

The strong interaction is the $SU(3)_c$ gauge interaction described by [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md). It couples quarks and gluons through color charge.
