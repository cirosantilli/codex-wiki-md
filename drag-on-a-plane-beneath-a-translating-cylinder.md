# Drag on a plane beneath a translating cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-boundary lubrication flux](moving-boundary-lubrication-flux.md)

For a full Newtonian liquid film in the [lubrication approximation](lubrication-theory.md), a cylinder translating parallel to a stationary plane with minimum gap $h_0\ll a$ exerts tangential force $2\pi\mu U\sqrt{2a/h_0}$ per unit axial length on the plane. The leading normal force vanishes by the odd symmetry of excess [pressure](pressure.md).
