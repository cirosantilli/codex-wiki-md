# Log-posterior

↑ **Parent:** [Posterior density](posterior-density.md)

The log-posterior is the logarithm of a positive [posterior density](posterior-density.md). Its gradient is the [posterior score control variate](posterior-score-control-variate.md) when boundary terms vanish. An additive constant independent of the parameter does not affect optimization or differentiation.
