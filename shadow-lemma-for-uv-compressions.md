# Shadow lemma for UV-compressions

↑ **Parent:** [UV-compression](uv-compression.md)

Suppose that for every $x\in U$ there is a $y\in V$ such that $\mathcal A$ is fixed by the smaller compression $C_{U\setminus\{x\},V\setminus\{y\}}$. Then

$$
\left|\partial C_{U,V}(\mathcal A)\right|\leq|\partial\mathcal A|.
$$

Indeed, deleting an element outside $U$ from a newly compressed member gives the $UV$-compression of an old shadow member. If the deleted element is $x\in U$, stability under the chosen smaller compression shows that the resulting set already belongs to the old [lower shadow](lower-shadow.md). Thus

$$
\partial C_{U,V}(\mathcal A)\subseteq C_{U,V}(\partial\mathcal A),
$$

and compression preserves cardinality.
