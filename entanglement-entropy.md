# Entanglement entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entanglement_entropy)

For a bipartite pure state, the entanglement entropy is the Von Neumann entropy of either [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md). The two reduced states have the same nonzero eigenvalues, so their entropies agree.
