# Risk factor

↑ **Parent:** [Epidemiology](epidemiology.md)

A characteristic associated with increased probability of an outcome. Association can arise from causal influence, [confounding](confounding.md) or selection; being predictive does not automatically make a factor a modifiable causal target.
