# Neumann eigenfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md)

A Neumann eigenfunction is an [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) of a spatial [differential operator](differential-operator.md) whose normal [derivative](derivative.md) vanishes on the boundary. A [Sturm-Liouville eigenfunction expansion](sturm-liouville-eigenfunction-expansion.md) in these functions gives a [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) for homogeneous [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md).
