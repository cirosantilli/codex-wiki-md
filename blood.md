# Blood

↑ **Parent:** [Circulatory system](circulatory-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood)

Blood is a fluid containing [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) that transports substances through a [circulatory system](circulatory-system.md). Its [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) depends on its cellular content and deformation rate. Treating it as a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) in the [Hagen-Poiseuille equation](hagen-poiseuille-equation.md) is an approximation that depends on the vessel size and flow conditions.
