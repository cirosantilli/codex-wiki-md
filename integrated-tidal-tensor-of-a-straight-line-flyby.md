# Integrated tidal tensor of a straight-line flyby

↑ **Parent:** [Impulse approximation](impulse-approximation.md)

For a perturber following $(p,0,vt)$, the [impulse approximation](impulse-approximation.md) integrates its [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md) to $2Gm_p\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)/(vp^2)$. A fixed target position therefore receives $\Delta\mathbf v=2Gm_p(x,-y,0)/(vp^2)$. The longitudinal kick vanishes after integrating the symmetric trajectory.
