# Electrophoretic mobility shift assay

↑ **Parent:** [Gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md)

A binding assay that detects a change in electrophoretic mobility when a ligand associates with a labelled target, such as an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md)–[protein](protein-split.md) complex. A concentration series and competition controls help distinguish specific binding from aggregation or non-specific retention.
