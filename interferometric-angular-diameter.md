# Interferometric angular diameter

↑ **Parent:** [Angular diameter](angular-diameter.md)

An angular diameter inferred from the [interferometric visibility](interferometric-visibility.md) of an extended source. The displayed monochromatic formula is for a uniformly bright circular disk, with projected baseline $B$, diameter $\Theta$ and wavelength $\lambda$. Its first visibility zero gives $B\Theta/\lambda\simeq1.22$. [Stellar angular diameter from a visibility envelope](stellar-angular-diameter-from-a-visibility-envelope.md) describes this calibration; real photospheres require limb-darkening corrections.
