# G2 dimension polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md)

For the [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md) with $\alpha_1$ short and $\alpha_2$ long, the [fundamental weights](fundamental-weight.md) are $\omega_1=2\alpha_1+\alpha_2$ and $\omega_2=3\alpha_1+2\alpha_2$. The positive [coroots](coroot.md), relative to the [simple coroots](simple-coroot.md), have coordinates $(1,0),(0,1),(1,3),(2,3),(1,1),(1,2)$. The [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) therefore gives, for nonnegative [integers](integer.md) $a,b$,

$$
\dim L(a\omega_1+b\omega_2)=\frac{(a+1)(b+1)(a+b+2)(a+2b+3)(a+3b+4)(2a+3b+5)}{120}.
$$

The denominator is the product of the pairings of the [Weyl vector](half-sum-of-positive-roots.md) with those coroots. The [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md) at $\omega_1,\omega_2,2\omega_1$ are seven, fourteen and twenty-seven.
