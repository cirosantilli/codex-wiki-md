# First-order Horn clause

↑ **Parent:** [Horn clause](horn-clause.md)

A [first-order clause](first-order-clause.md) containing at most one positive [first-order literal](first-order-literal.md). A clause with exactly one positive literal is definite; with no positive literal it is a constraint or goal clause.
