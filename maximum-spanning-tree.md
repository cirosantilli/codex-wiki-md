# Maximum spanning tree

↑ **Parent:** [Spanning tree](spanning-tree.md)

A [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md) with greatest total [edge weight](edge-weight.md). Apply [Kruskal's algorithm](kruskal-s-algorithm.md) in decreasing weight order, or negate every [edge weight](edge-weight.md) and compute a [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md). Every [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) within a [maximum spanning tree](maximum-spanning-tree.md) solves the [widest path problem](widest-path-problem.md) between its endpoints.
