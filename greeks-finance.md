# Greeks (finance)

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeks_(finance))

The Greeks are sensitivities of a financial claim value to model inputs, expressed through [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md). The [option delta](option-delta.md) measures sensitivity to the underlying price; a [delta hedge](delta-hedge.md) uses that sensitivity to cancel first-order price exposure.
