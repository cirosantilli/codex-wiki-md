# Papapetrou electromagnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md)

For a [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) $K$, its metric-dual [differential one-form](one-form.md) produces the [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md) $F_{ab}=2\nabla_{[a}K_{b]}=2\nabla_aK_b$. The [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) gives $dF=0$, while the [second covariant derivative of a Killing vector](second-covariant-derivative-of-a-killing-vector.md) gives $\nabla^aF_{ab}=-2R_{bd}K^d$. Thus this construction solves the source-free [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations.md) in a [vacuum spacetime](vacuum-spacetime.md) with zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md). The field is a test field: adding its [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) would generally change the original [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md).
