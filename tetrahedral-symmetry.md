# Tetrahedral symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group theory](finite-group-theory-split.md)

A regular tetrahedron has a proper rotational symmetry group $T$ of order 12, isomorphic to the [alternating group](alternating-group.md) $A_4$ acting on its four vertices. Its full spatial symmetry group $T_d$ has order 24 and is isomorphic to the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_4$; the additional operations reverse orientation. Three-fold vertex/face-axis rotations and half-turns about opposite-edge midpoints generate the proper group. The distinction matters when a field symmetry uses only proper rotations, but a scalar density also admits reflections.
