# Periodicity

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic function](periodic-function.md)

Periodicity is invariance under translation by a nonzero period $P$: a [periodic function](periodic-function.md) satisfies $f(x+P)=f(x)$ at every point in its domain, with translation preserving the domain. For an angular coordinate on a full [circle](circle.md), single valuedness requires $2\pi$-periodicity. The [sine](sine.md) and [cosine](cosine.md) factors $\sin(k\theta)$ and $\cos(k\theta)$ are $2\pi$-periodic for nonzero real $k$ precisely when $k$ is an integer. Their [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) supply the angular modes for the [harmonic expansion on a disk](harmonic-expansion-on-a-disk.md).
