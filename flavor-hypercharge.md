# Flavor hypercharge

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md)

The additive [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) quantum number $Y=B+S$ combines [baryon number](baryon-number.md) with [strangeness](strangeness.md). For the $(u,d,s)$ [quark](quark.md) triplet its values are $(1/3,1/3,-2/3)$. The [Gell-Mann--Nishijima formula](gell-mann-nishijima-formula.md) is $Q=I_3+Y/2$. This is distinct from the normalization of [electroweak hypercharge](hypercharge.md).
