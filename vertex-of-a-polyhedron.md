# Vertex of a polyhedron

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)

A corner of a [polyhedron](polyhedron.md), stored with its spatial [coordinates](coordinate-linear-algebra.md) and incidences with boundary edges and faces. In a closed manifold [boundary representation](boundary-representation.md), the incident faces form a single cyclic fan. The [vertices of a polyhedron](vertex-of-a-polyhedron.md) and edges define a [graph](graph-split.md), but geometric embedding and valid face incidence require additional information.
