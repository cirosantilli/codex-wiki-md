# Center of a tensor product of associative algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of associative algebras](tensor-product-of-associative-algebras.md)

For [associative algebras](associative-algebra-split.md) of finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) over a [field](field.md) $k$, write a central [tensor](tensor.md) as $x=\sum_j a_j\otimes b_j$ in a [basis](basis.md) of $B$. Commutation with every $a\otimes1$ forces each $a_j$ into the [center of an associative algebra](center-of-an-associative-algebra.md) $Z(A)$. Reexpand in a [basis](basis.md) of $Z(A)$ and commute with every $1\otimes b$ to force the second coefficients into $Z(B)$. The reverse inclusion follows by multiplying [pure tensors](pure-tensor.md).
