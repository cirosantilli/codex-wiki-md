# Marine atmospheric boundary layer

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric boundary layer](atmospheric-boundary-layer.md)

The [atmospheric boundary layer](atmospheric-boundary-layer.md) over an ocean surface. A box-model [ozone](ozone.md) budget can include in-layer production, chemical loss, [dry deposition](dry-deposition.md), [entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md) from the [free troposphere](free-troposphere.md) and horizontal [Advection](advection.md). Fixed-depth local steady-state assumptions must be checked against transport and day–night variation.
