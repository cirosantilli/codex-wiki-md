# Exotic subset-cover class of a framed bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Framed cobordism](framed-cobordism.md)

The degree-zero [framed cobordism](framed-cobordism.md) class is defined by the transfer of the [subset cover of a framed vector bundle](subset-cover-of-a-framed-vector-bundle.md). With the canonical relative framing it equals $\binom nk\cdot1$ for a globally ordered framing of rank $n$. Put $l_0=1$ and $l_k=0$ outside the rank range. Disjoint-union additivity and fiber-product multiplicativity of transfer give $l_k(\eta\oplus\zeta)=\sum_{i+j=k}l_i(\eta)l_j(\zeta)$.
