# Atiyah-Singer index theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac operator](dirac-operator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atiyah–Singer_index_theorem)

For a [Twisted Dirac operator](twisted-dirac-operator.md) on a closed even-dimensional [spin manifold](spin-manifold.md), the theorem identifies its analytic [Fredholm index](fredholm-index.md) with the integral of the top-degree part of the [A-hat form](a-hat-form.md) times the [Chern character](chern-character.md). This is one geometric specialization of the general theorem for [elliptic differential operators](elliptic-differential-operator.md), which expresses the analytic index in terms of the topological class of the [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md). The heat proof pairs all positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) by the odd [Dirac operator](dirac-operator.md), leaving only the index in the [supertrace](supertrace.md); [Getzler rescaling](getzler-rescaling.md) computes its local limit from curvature.
