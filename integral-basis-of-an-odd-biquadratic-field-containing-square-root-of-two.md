# Integral basis of an odd biquadratic field containing square root of two

↑ **Parent:** [Integral basis](integral-basis.md)

For square-free $\delta\equiv3\pmod4$, the field $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt\delta)$ has basis $1,\sqrt2,\sqrt\delta,(\sqrt2+\sqrt{2\delta})/2$ as an [integral basis](integral-basis.md) of its ring of [integers](integer.md). [Relative traces](relative-trace.md) force all rational coefficients to be half-integers; the [norm](norm.md) to $\mathbb Q(\sqrt\delta)$ forces the constant and $\sqrt\delta$ coefficients to be [integers](integer.md) and the two other numerators to have equal parity. The half-sum is integral because it satisfies $X^4-(1+\delta)X^2+(\delta-1)^2/4=0$.
