# Lead (sea ice)

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead_(sea_ice))

A [sea-ice lead](lead-sea-ice.md) is a fracture or elongated opening in a sea-ice cover, containing open water or young thin ice. Divergence and shear create leads, while freezing and convergence close them.
