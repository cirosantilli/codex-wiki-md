# Trace of Frobenius

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius isogeny of an elliptic curve](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For an elliptic curve over $\mathbb F_q$, the trace of Frobenius is

$$
a_q=q+1-\#E(\mathbb F_q).
$$

It is the trace of the [Frobenius isogeny of an elliptic curve](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) on every prime-to-$q$ Tate module.
