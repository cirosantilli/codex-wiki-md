# Kerr black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)

The Kerr black hole is the asymptotically flat stationary axisymmetric vacuum black hole with mass $M$ and angular momentum $J=Ma$.

A Kerr black hole is the [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md) with mass $M>0$ and spin parameter satisfying $|a|\leq M$ in geometrized units; its outer horizon is $r_+=M+\sqrt{M^2-a^2}$.
