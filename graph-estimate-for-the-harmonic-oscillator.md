# Graph estimate for the harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [One-dimensional harmonic oscillator form domain](one-dimensional-harmonic-oscillator-form-domain.md)

For $c>0$ and smooth compactly supported $u$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\|-u''+(c+x^2)u\|_2^2
=\|u''\|_2^2+\|(c+x^2)u\|_2^2+2\int(c+x^2)|u'|^2\,dx-2\|u\|_2^2.
$$

Dropping the nonnegative weighted derivative term proves the displayed bound. For a weak resolvent solution in the [one-dimensional harmonic oscillator form domain](one-dimensional-harmonic-oscillator-form-domain.md), apply the bound to $\chi_Ru$, where $\chi_R$ is a smooth cutoff. The cutoff error $-2\chi_R'u'-\chi_R''u$ is bounded in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) and tends to zero. The [Fatou lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) then shows that $u''$ and $x^2u$ belong to [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md), identifying the strong operator domain.
