# Path-integral perturbation by source derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field path integral](scalar-field-path-integral.md)

An interaction depending on the field can be represented by substituting $(1/i)\delta/\delta J$ into its action and acting on the free [generating functional](generating-functional.md). Expanding that exponential produces [Feynman diagrams](feynman-diagram.md), contractions and vertex weights. Normalize by the resulting value at $J=0$ to cancel vacuum diagrams.
