# Brane-world scenario

↑ **Parent:** [Compactification (physics)](compactification-physics.md)

In a brane-world model some fields, typically ordinary matter and gauge fields, are localized on a lower-dimensional [brane](brane.md), while gravity propagates through the higher-dimensional bulk. Compact volume or a warped metric can then separate the observed gravitational scale from local matter scales. A model must specify field localization, boundary conditions and stabilization rather than identifying a brane with an arbitrary compactification.
