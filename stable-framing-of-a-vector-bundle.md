# Stable framing of a vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

A framing is a continuous ordered basis in every fiber, equivalently a trivialization of the [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md). A stable framing trivializes the bundle after adding trivial real summands, up to further stabilization and homotopy. For a smooth map it is the stable normal data that is framed, not necessarily the tangent bundle of either manifold.
