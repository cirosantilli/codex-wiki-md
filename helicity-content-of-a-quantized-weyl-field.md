# Helicity content of a quantized Weyl field

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md)

A left-handed massless [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) has a positive-energy [plane wave](plane-wave.md) transforming by $e^{i\alpha/2}$ under a rotation about its [momentum](momentum.md). Its particle has [helicity](helicity.md) $-1/2$. The negative-frequency term multiplies an antiparticle [fermionic creation operator](fermionic-creation-operator.md), so its corresponding one-particle state carries the conjugate rotation phase and [helicity](helicity.md) $+1/2$. Thus a left-handed field has one particle and one antiparticle helicity; the right-handed field interchanges the signs. A full massless [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) contains both chiral fields and hence both particle helicities.
