# Singular projection of an implicit curve

↑ **Parent:** [Implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md)

For two constraints $F(x,y,z)=G(x,y,z)=0$, the [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md) gives differentiable functions $y(x),z(x)$ when the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $\partial(F,G)/\partial(y,z)$ is nonzero. At its zeros this particular projection can fail even when a geometric [curve](curve.md) remains. A nonzero [tangent vector](tangent-vector-split.md) with zero $x$ component gives a vertical tangent in that projection. Singular constraint representations require further care: a determinant of zero alone does not prove an infinite derivative or even a regular intersection.
