# Isogeny of elliptic curves

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

An [isogeny](isogeny.md) between [elliptic curves](elliptic-curve.md) is a nonconstant morphism preserving their group identities; it is finite and surjective with finite kernel.

An isogeny is a nonconstant morphism of elliptic curves preserving their identity points. It is a finite surjective group homomorphism.
