# Least quadratic nonresidue

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic nonresidue](quadratic-nonresidue.md)

For an [odd prime](odd-prime.md) $p$, the least quadratic nonresidue $n(p)$ is the smallest [positive integer](positive-integer.md) whose nonzero [residue class](residue-class.md) is a [quadratic nonresidue](quadratic-nonresidue.md) modulo $p$.
