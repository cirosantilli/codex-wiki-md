# Investment portfolio

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)

A collection of holdings in traded assets. Its value is the [dot product](dot-product.md) of the holdings with the asset prices. A [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) is an [investment portfolio](investment-portfolio.md) whose trading requires no external funding.
