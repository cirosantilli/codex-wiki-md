# Rainbow

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric optics](atmospheric-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainbow)

A rainbow is an atmospheric optical arc produced by [refraction](refraction.md), internal reflection and [wave dispersion](wave-dispersion.md) of light in water droplets. Wavelength-dependent [refractive index](refractive-index.md) separates the colours. The primary arc involves one internal reflection, and the secondary arc involves two.
