# Constant-coefficient differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md)

A [linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) has constant coefficients when the multipliers $a_j$ of the unknown function and its [derivatives](derivative.md) do not depend on the independent variable. The forcing $f$ may still vary. For the homogeneous equation $f=0$, its [characteristic equation of a constant-coefficient differential equation](characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) determines exponential solutions.
