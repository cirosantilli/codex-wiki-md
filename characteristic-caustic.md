# Characteristic caustic

↑ **Parent:** [Method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md)

A characteristic caustic is a locus where the coordinate map from initial-data and evolution parameters to physical coordinates has zero [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md). The [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) then fails, and different characteristic branches can reach the same point. A classical solution constructed from initial data must be restricted to its appropriate invertible branch before such a fold. In [geometrical optics](geometrical-optics-split.md) the corresponding ray-envelope phenomenon is a [caustic](wave-caustic.md); in nonlinear first-order equations a fold can signal the onset of multiple gradients or [shock formation by characteristic intersection](shock-formation-by-characteristic-intersection.md).
