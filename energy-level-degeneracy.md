# Energy-level degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

The number of independent quantum states sharing one [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md). A level of angular momentum $J$ has $2J+1$ magnetic substates when no interaction splits them.
