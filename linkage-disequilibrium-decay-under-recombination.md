# Linkage-disequilibrium decay under recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md)

In a random-mating two-locus population with recombination fraction $r$, and no [mutation](mutation.md), selection, migration or [genetic drift](genetic-drift.md), the next-generation frequency of haplotype $AB$ is $(1-r)p_{AB}+rp_Ap_B$. The marginal allele frequencies do not change, so subtracting $p_Ap_B$ gives the displayed recursion for [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md). Observed disequilibrium also reflects population history and sampling; it is not a deterministic function of physical distance.
