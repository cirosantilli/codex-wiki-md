<h1 id="kohler-theory">Köhler theory</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cloud condensation nucleus](cloud-condensation-nucleus.md)

The equilibrium water-vapor saturation ratio above a solution droplet combines a curvature effect, which raises [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md), with dissolved-solute reduction of [water](water.md) activity. Its maximum defines a [critical supersaturation](critical-supersaturation.md) for activation. In a dilute hygroscopicity approximation using droplet diameter $D$ and dry diameter $D_d$, $s(D)\simeq A_K/D-\kappa D_d^3/D^3$, where $A_K=4\sigma M_w/(RT\rho_w)$. Then $s_c\simeq\sqrt{4A_K^3/(27\kappa D_d^3)}$. These approximate forms assume the stated diameter convention and dilute solution; composition and [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) must be consistent.[Equations describing hygroscopic growth and critical activation supersaturation](a-single-parameter-representation-of-hygroscopic-growth-and-cloud-condensation-nucleus-activity-by-m-d-petters-and-s-m-kreidenweis.md)
