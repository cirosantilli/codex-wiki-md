# Dirichlet spectral formula for half-line constant drift

↑ **Parent:** [Fokas method](fokas-method.md)

Let $I_C[F]=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_Ce^{ikx-(k^2-i\alpha k)t}F(k)dk$. The [drift-diffusion half-line global relation](drift-diffusion-half-line-global-relation.md) and the [half-line drift null contour identity](half-line-drift-null-contour-identity.md) give the formula in the secondary title. Its real-space kernel is the difference of two drifted [Gaussian heat kernels](gaussian-heat-kernel.md), and its boundary-forcing kernel is $x(2\sqrt\pi t^{3/2})^{-1}\exp[-(x+\alpha t)^2/(4t)]$. This kernel is an [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md) in time as $x\downarrow0$, which verifies the [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md).
