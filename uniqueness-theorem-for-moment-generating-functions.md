# Uniqueness theorem for moment-generating functions

↑ **Parent:** [Moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md)

If two random vectors have moment-generating functions that are finite and equal on a neighborhood of the origin, then they have the same [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md).
