<h1 id="vinogradov-s-three-primes-theorem">Vinogradov's three-primes theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hardy-Littlewood circle method](hardy-littlewood-circle-method.md)

Every sufficiently large odd integer is a sum of three primes, with repetitions allowed. The weighted count is asymptotic to $\mathfrak S(n)n^2/2$. [Siegel–Walfisz theorem](siegel-walfisz-theorem.md) controls the [major arcs](major-arc.md), and Type I/II estimates for the prime [exponential sum](exponential-sum.md) make the [minor arcs](minor-arc.md) negligible. Removing prime-power contributions gives genuine prime representations. This is the sufficiently-large theorem, not by itself a proof for every small odd integer.
