# Harmonic decomposition of homogeneous polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic polynomial](harmonic-polynomial.md)

If $\mathcal P_\ell$ is the space of degree-$\ell$ [homogeneous polynomials](homogeneous-polynomial.md) and $\mathcal H_\ell$ its [harmonic polynomials](harmonic-polynomial.md), then $\mathcal P_\ell=\mathcal H_\ell\oplus|x|^2\mathcal P_{\ell-2}$. Under the [Fischer inner product](fischer-inner-product.md), the second summand is orthogonal to the kernel of the [Laplacian](laplacian.md). Iteration expresses every polynomial restricted to the unit [sphere](sphere.md) as a sum of [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md).
