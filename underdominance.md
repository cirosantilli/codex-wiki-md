# Underdominance

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underdominance)

[Underdominance](underdominance.md) means that the [heterozygote](heterozygote.md) has lower [genotype fitness](genotype-fitness.md) than both corresponding [homozygotes](homozygote.md). In a deterministic randomly mating biallelic population, it produces an unstable interior allele-frequency threshold separating the two fixation outcomes, unlike [heterozygote advantage](heterozygote-advantage.md).
