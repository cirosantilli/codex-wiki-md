# Beckmann potential

↑ **Parent:** [Wardrop equilibrium](wardrop-equilibrium.md)

The Beckmann potential integrates each link's delay function. Its [gradient](gradient.md) with respect to route flows is the corresponding vector of route delays, making [Wardrop equilibrium](wardrop-equilibrium.md) a [convex optimization](convex-optimization-split.md) problem.
