# Composition length

↑ **Parent:** [Composition series](composition-series.md)

The composition length of a finite-length module or group is the number of simple factors in a [composition series](composition-series.md). The [Jordan–Hölder theorem](jordan-holder-theorem.md) makes this independent of the chosen composition series.
