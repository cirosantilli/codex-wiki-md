# Cycles-per-time spectral density

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md)

In cycles per observation, frequency lies in $[-1/2,1/2]$ and $\gamma(h)=\int e^{2\pi ih\omega}f_c(\omega)\,d\omega$. Compared with angular-frequency density $f_a$, one has $f_c(\omega)=2\pi f_a(2\pi\omega)$. In particular, [white noise](white-noise.md) of [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$ has $f_c=\sigma^2$.
