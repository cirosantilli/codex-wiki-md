# Quartic Yukawa interaction in three spacetime dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md)

A nonderivative interaction of two real scalar legs with a fermion pair in $2+1$ dimensions. The [engineering dimensions of relativistic fields](engineering-dimensions-of-relativistic-fields.md) are $[\phi]=1/2$ and $[\psi]=1$, so $g$ is a [marginal coupling](marginal-coupling.md) by classical [power counting](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md). The internal symmetry $\phi\mapsto-\phi$ forbids odd-scalar interactions, but allows a sextic scalar interaction. The two-component [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) representation must be distinguished from four-component [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) in four spacetime dimensions.
