Official University of Illinois semiconductor-device lecture slides. Slide 26 distinguishes carrier-counting, conductivity and cyclotron [effective masses](band-effective-mass.md), and gives the multiple-valley [density-of-states effective mass](density-of-states-effective-mass.md) for silicon. This is useful when interpreting a supplied scalar mass in an elementary [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) model.
