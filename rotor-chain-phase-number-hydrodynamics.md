# Rotor-chain phase-number hydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Coupled quantum rotor chain](coupled-quantum-rotor-chain.md)

With $\delta n=\ell/\hbar$, number and compact phase are conjugate variables. Their long-wavelength [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) has compressibility coefficient $I/\hbar^2$ and [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md) J. The canonical equations $\dot\phi=\hbar\delta n/I$ and $\dot{\delta n}=J\partial_x^2\phi/\hbar$ combine into $\phi_{tt}=(J/I)\phi_{xx}$. The resulting phase-density wave has physical sound speed $\sqrt{J/I}$ at unit spacing. A [superfluid](superfluid.md) has the same low-energy structure even though the rotor coordinate is not the displacement of a mass on a spring.
