# Sharpness of the Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev constant

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality](gaussian-logarithmic-sobolev-inequality.md)

For standard one-dimensional [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md), $\int e^{sx}\,d\gamma=e^{s^2/2}$. Thus $\operatorname{Ent}_\gamma(e^{sx})=(s^2/2)e^{s^2/2}$, while $\int|(e^{sx/2})'|^2d\gamma=(s^2/4)e^{s^2/2}$. Every nonzero $s$ therefore attains ratio two, proving optimality of the Gaussian constant.
