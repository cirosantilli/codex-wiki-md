# Allele fixation

↑ **Parent:** [Allele frequency](allele-frequency.md)

An [allele](allele.md) fixes when all copies at the relevant locus in the population are that [allele](allele.md). Fixation can occur through selection or [genetic drift](genetic-drift.md). Spatial polymorphism means that fixation within an individual habitat need not imply fixation across a [metapopulation](metapopulation.md).
