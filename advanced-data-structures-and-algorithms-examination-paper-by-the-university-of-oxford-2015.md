# Advanced Data Structures and Algorithms examination paper by the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

Examination dated 5 June 2015, Trinity term, [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md). The public archive contains the paper as the member `2015-adsa.pdf` of [Lawrence Attachment.zip](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1). The three original pages contain the cover and thirteen numbered subparts on [amortized analysis](amortized-analysis.md), [flow networks](flow-network-split.md), and [linear programming](linear-programming-split.md).
