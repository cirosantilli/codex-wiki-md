# Support and Gram bandwidth of B-splines

↑ **Parent:** [B-spline](b-spline.md)

An order-$k$ [B-spline](b-spline.md) is supported on $[t_i,t_{i+k}]$. Below this interval its truncated-power knot data agree with a degree-$k-1$ polynomial, annihilated by the order-$k$ [divided difference](divided-difference.md); above it all data vanish. Therefore its ordinary or mixed [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) has entries zero when $|i-j|\geq k$. The half-bandwidth is $k-1$, and for strictly increasing knots this bound is sharp.
