# Category of topological manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold](topological-manifold-split.md)

The [category](category-split.md) of topological manifolds has [topological manifolds](topological-manifold-split.md) without boundary as its [objects of a category](object-of-a-category.md) and all [continuous maps](continuous-map.md) between them as its [categorical morphisms](morphism-in-a-category.md). Allowing every [dimension of a manifold](dimension-of-a-manifold.md) lets maps connect spaces of different dimensions. [Identity functions](identity-function.md) are [continuous](continuous-function.md), and [function composition](function-composition.md) preserves [continuity](continuous-function.md), so the [category](category-split.md) laws are inherited from [functions](function-split.md). Its [isomorphisms](isomorphism.md) are exactly [homeomorphisms](homeomorphism.md).

In [coordinate charts](manifold-chart.md) $(U,\varphi)$ on $M$ and $(V,\psi)$ on $N$, a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $f:M\to N$ is represented locally by $\psi\circ f\circ\varphi^{-1}$ on $\varphi(U\cap f^{-1}(V))$. Changing [coordinate charts](manifold-chart.md) changes this expression through [topological transition maps](topological-transition-map.md), while $f$ itself stays fixed. This viewpoint separates the underlying [continuous map](continuous-map.md) from the coordinates used to describe it; restricting to [smooth manifolds](smooth-manifold-split.md) and [smooth maps](smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) gives the corresponding smooth [category](category-split.md).
