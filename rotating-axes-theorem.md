# Rotating-axes theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)

The [time derivative](time-derivative.md) of a [vector](vector.md) in inertial axes equals its component derivative in rotating axes plus the change of the rotating basis itself. Each basis vector obeys $\dot{\mathbf e}_j=\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf e_j$, proving the displayed identity by differentiating $\mathbf a=\sum_ja_j\mathbf e_j$. Applying it twice to position produces the [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) $2\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf u$ and the centripetal term $\boldsymbol\Omega\times(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf x)$. A time-dependent rotation adds $\dot{\boldsymbol\Omega}\times\mathbf x$; constant angular velocity removes that last term.
