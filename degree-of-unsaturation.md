# Degree of unsaturation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical formula](chemical-formula.md)

The number of rings and pi-bond equivalents needed by a conventional closed-shell formula. For a neutral formula containing C, H, N and halogens, it is $(2C+2+N-H-X)/2$; [oxygen](oxygen.md) does not change the count. A [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) contributes one and a triple bond two.
