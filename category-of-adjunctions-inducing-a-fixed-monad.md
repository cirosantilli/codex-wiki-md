# Category of adjunctions inducing a fixed monad

↑ **Parent:** [Monad induced by an adjunction](monad-induced-by-an-adjunction.md)

Fix a [monad](monad.md) on $\mathcal C$. Objects are [adjunctions](adjoint-functors.md) with left-hand [category](category-split.md) $\mathcal C$ whose induced monads are identified with the fixed monad. A [morphism](morphism.md) between two such adjunctions is a [functor](functor.md) between their right-hand [categories](category-split.md) commuting with both adjoints and respecting the specified units and counits. Strict identifications give a [category](category-split.md); coherent identifications give the analogous [universal property](universal-property.md) up to compatible [natural isomorphism](natural-isomorphism.md).
