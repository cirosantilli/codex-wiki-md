# Noninteracting Coulomb-shell filling

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

In a fixed attractive [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md), neglecting electron-electron interactions leaves hydrogenic one-particle levels with spin-inclusive degeneracy $2n^2$. The [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) fills these levels in increasing [energy](energy-split.md). A partially filled degenerate shell gives many states at the same total [energy](energy-split.md); the first higher [energy](energy-split.md) requires promotion to a different principal shell. This model does not describe the screening and configuration-dependent energies of a real many-electron atom.
