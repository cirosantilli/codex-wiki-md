<h1 id="darcy-benard-convection">Darcy-Bénard convection</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy law](darcy-law.md)

Darcy-Bénard convection is [thermal convection](thermal-convection.md) in a porous layer heated from below, with velocity governed by [Darcy's law](darcy-law.md). With fixed temperatures and impermeable boundaries, a normalized two-dimensional model uses [streamfunction](stream-function.md) $\psi$ and temperature $\theta$:

$$
\nabla^2\psi=-R\theta_x,\qquad \theta_t+\psi_z\theta_x-\psi_x\theta_z=\nabla^2\theta.
$$

The control parameter is a porous-medium [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md). On $0<z<1$ with temperature values $0,-1$, the conductive state is $\psi=0$, $\theta=-z$.
