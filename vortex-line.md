# Vortex line

↑ **Parent:** [Vorticity](vorticity-split.md)

A [vortex line](vortex-line.md) is an [integral](integral-split.md) curve of the [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) vector field: its tangent is parallel to $\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf u$. In a two-dimensional [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), these lines are perpendicular to the flow plane and the [vorticity equation](vorticity-equation.md) gives $D\omega/Dt=0$. Since material cross-sectional area is preserved, the flux of [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) through a material [vortex tube](vortex-tube.md), equal to its [circulation](circulation-physics.md), is constant.
