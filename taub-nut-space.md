<h1 id="taub-nut-space">Taub–NUT space</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

The single-centre Euclidean Taub–NUT geometry is a gravitational instanton with a circle fibre over three-dimensional space. Its fibre shrinks smoothly at the centre when its period is chosen appropriately. Adding flat worldvolume directions gives the local geometry of a [Kaluza-Klein monopole](kaluza-klein-monopole.md).
