<h1 id="mohorovicic-discontinuity">Mohorovičić discontinuity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary crust](planetary-crust.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohorovičić_discontinuity)

The seismic boundary between [crust](planetary-crust.md) and [mantle](planetary-mantle.md), commonly identified by a marked increase in elastic [wave speeds](wave-speed.md). It is a compositional boundary and need not coincide with the base of the [lithosphere](lithosphere.md).
