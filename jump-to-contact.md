# Jump-to-contact

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

An instability in a compliant [force](force-split.md) probe when the attractive [force](force-split.md) gradient exceeds the restoring stiffness of the [cantilever](cantilever.md). The tip then jumps from a noncontact branch into contact; the missing branch cannot be followed quasistatically with that stiffness.
