# Two-factor interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Interaction term](interaction-term.md)

A two-factor interaction means that changing one factor has a different effect at different levels of another. For a two-level [factorial design](factorial-design.md), its sign column is $x_ix_j$, and four times its coefficient is the corresponding difference of differences. It can be confounded with other [factorial contrasts](factorial-contrast.md) in a [fractional factorial design](fractional-factorial-design.md).
