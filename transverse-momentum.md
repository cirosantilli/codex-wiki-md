# Transverse momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

Choose the beam as the $z$ direction. A [particle](particle-physics.md)'s [transverse momentum](transverse-momentum.md) is its [momentum](momentum-split.md) projected onto the perpendicular plane; $p_T=\sqrt{p_x^2+p_y^2}$. A [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) along the beam changes [energy](energy-split.md) and longitudinal [momentum](momentum-split.md), while leaving [transverse momentum](transverse-momentum.md) unchanged. Large [transverse momentum](transverse-momentum.md) identifies motion substantially across the beam rather than merely along it.
