# Castelnuovo contraction criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth projective surface](smooth-projective-surface.md)

A [smooth rational curve](smooth-rational-curve.md) with self-intersection $-1$ on a [smooth projective surface](smooth-projective-surface.md) over an algebraically closed field contracts to a smooth point of another [smooth projective surface](smooth-projective-surface.md). The contraction is a [birational morphism](birational-morphism.md) and an isomorphism off the curve, with inverse a [blowup of a smooth algebraic surface](blowup-of-a-smooth-algebraic-surface.md). This algebraic criterion is valid in arbitrary characteristic and should not be confused with Castelnuovo's rationality criterion.
