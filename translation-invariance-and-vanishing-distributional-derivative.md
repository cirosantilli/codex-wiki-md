# Translation invariance and vanishing distributional derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Translation of a distribution](translation-of-a-distribution.md)

A distribution is invariant under every translation parallel to the $i$th coordinate axis exactly when its $i$th [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) vanishes. Differentiate the translated pairing for one direction; integrate that derivative in the translation parameter for the converse.
