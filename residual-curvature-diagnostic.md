# Residual curvature diagnostic

↑ **Parent:** [Regression residual](regression-residual.md)

A systematic curved pattern in [regression residuals](regression-residual.md) plotted against [fitted values](fitted-values.md) suggests a misspecified conditional mean. Positive residuals at both ends and negative residuals in the middle motivate trying [quadratic regression](quadratic-regression.md). The diagnostic does not by itself identify the error [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) or prove [heteroscedasticity](heteroscedastic.md).
