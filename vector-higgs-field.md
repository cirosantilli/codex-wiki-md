# Vector Higgs field

↑ **Parent:** [SO(3) vector Higgs model](so-3-vector-higgs-model.md)

In the [SO(3) vector Higgs model](so-3-vector-higgs-model.md), a real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) $\Phi\in\mathbb R^3$ transforms as $\Phi\mapsto R\Phi$. Its [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md) is $D_\mu\Phi=\partial_\mu\Phi+g\mathbf A_\mu\times\Phi$ for generators acting by the [cross product](cross-product.md).
