<h1 id="nelson-aalen-variance-estimator">Nelson–Aalen variance estimator</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nelson–Aalen estimator](nelson-aalen-estimator.md)

For untied events at $a_j$ with predictable [risk set](risk-set.md) size $Y_j$, the estimated predictable variation of the [Nelson–Aalen estimator](nelson-aalen-estimator.md) is $\sum_{a_j\leq t}Y_j^{-2}$. It comes from the martingale event increment variance $Y\,dH$ and the estimator increment $dN/Y$. It applies on the range with individuals at risk; it is not an exact finite-sample unbiasedness statement.
