# Radial logarithmic mean of a Blaschke product

↑ **Parent:** [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md)

For $B(z)=\lambda z^m\prod_n b_{a_n}(z)$ with nonzero zeros $a_n$ counted with [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md), [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md) gives the displayed identity. The negative logarithms of partial products increase, so the [monotone convergence theorem](monotone-convergence-theorem.md) permits termwise integration. The [Blaschke condition](blaschke-condition.md) implies $\sum_n-\log|a_n|<\infty$, which dominates the absolute summands. The [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) therefore gives a limit of zero as $r\uparrow1$. Since $|B|\le1$, the mean of $|\log|B||$ tends to zero as well.
