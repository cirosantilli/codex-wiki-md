# Finite-time free-surface approach of a point stresslet

↑ **Parent:** [Free-surface image of a force dipole](free-surface-image-of-a-force-dipole.md)

A positive-strength parallel point [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md) below a flat shear-free impermeable plane is attracted by its identical reflected image. Its height satisfies $\dot h=-S/(32\pi\mu h^2)$ and formally reaches zero in finite time. Negative strength reverses the drift. Finite-body near-contact physics lies outside this singularity prediction.
