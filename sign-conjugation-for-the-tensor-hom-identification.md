# Sign conjugation for the tensor-Hom identification

↑ **Parent:** [Graded Hom complex of chain complexes](graded-hom-complex-of-chain-complexes.md)

For finite free total [chain complexes](chain-complex.md), evaluation identifies the underlying graded [tensor product](tensor-product.md) $X\otimes C'$ with the [graded Hom complex of chain complexes](graded-hom-complex-of-chain-complexes.md), where $X$ is the [reversed dual chain complex](reversed-dual-chain-complex.md). Under the precomposition-first Hom differential, conjugation by the sign $(-1)^{j(j-1)/2}$ makes this a chain isomorphism. The identity $\rho(j)-\rho(j-1)=j-1$ works for every integer degree. If the grading is unbounded and only degreewise finite, products on the Hom side need not equal direct sums on the tensor side.
