# Asteroidal core growth by crustal sinking

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

At zero liquid superheat, dense solid released by [viscous overturn of a dense crust](viscous-overturn-of-a-dense-crust.md) can sink and remain solid. For a thin crust on an asteroid of fixed radius $R$, efficient deposition and the equal-density volume approximation give $b^3-b_0^3=3R^2J_s(t-t_0)$. If surface gravity scales as $R$, the crustal flux scales as $R^{1/3}$ and the core radius as $R^{7/9}t^{1/3}$. The approximation ends when remaining liquid is exhausted or the renewed crust is no longer thin.
