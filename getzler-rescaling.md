# Getzler rescaling

↑ **Parent:** [Atiyah-Singer index theorem](atiyah-singer-index-theorem.md)

Getzler rescaling combines the change $x\mapsto\sqrt t\,x$ in [geodesic normal coordinates](geodesic-normal-coordinates.md) with the [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) filtration, assigning degree one to a derivative and to a Clifford generator and degree minus one to a coordinate. The associated graded [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) is the [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md). This is useful because the spinor [supertrace](supertrace.md) annihilates every Clifford monomial except the top-degree one. Applied to the [Lichnerowicz formula for a twisted Dirac operator](lichnerowicz-formula-for-a-twisted-dirac-operator.md), it retains a quadratic curvature model, whose [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) is computed by a Gaussian ansatz; all terms of smaller rescaling degree disappear from the local index limit. Thus the cancellations in a supersymmetric heat trace can be computed before solving the complete curved heat equation.
