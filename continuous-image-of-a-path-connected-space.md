# Continuous image of a path-connected space

↑ **Parent:** [Path-connected space](path-connected-space.md)

If $f:X\to Y$ is continuous and $X$ is [path-connected](path-connected-space.md), then $f(X)$ is path-connected. Choose preimages of any two image points and a [continuous path](continuous-path.md) between them in $X$. Its composition with $f$ supplies the required path inside $f(X)$. The conclusion uses the [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md) on the image, not an assumption that $f$ maps onto the whole codomain.
