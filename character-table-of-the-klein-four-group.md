# Character table of the Klein four-group

↑ **Parent:** [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md)

Write the [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md) as $\{1,a,b,ab\}$. Its four one-dimensional [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) independently assign signs to $a,b$, so the displayed [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) have $p,q\in\{0,1\}$. The [character table](character-table.md) is

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrr}
 &1&a&b&ab\\\hline
\chi_{00}&1&1&1&1\\
\chi_{01}&1&1&-1&-1\\
\chi_{10}&1&-1&1&-1\\
\chi_{11}&1&-1&-1&1
\end{array}.
$$

Every [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) is a singleton because the group is abelian. The four rows exhaust the complex irreducibles by the equality between their number and the number of classes. If $H$ is this table, $HH^T=4I$ proves [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md). The diagonal three-dimensional representation by half-turns about the coordinate axes is the [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of the three nontrivial characters, with total character $(3,-1,-1,-1)$.
