# Forced Airy boundary-value problem

↑ **Parent:** [Airy ordinary differential equation](airy-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

On the unit interval, the weak form is $a(u,v)=\int_0^1fv$, with $a(u,v)=\int_0^1(u'v'+xuv)$ on the [zero-boundary Sobolev space](zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md). The nonnegative potential makes this form coercive in the derivative [norm](norm.md), and the [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) makes that [norm](norm.md) equivalent to the [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md). The [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) gives a unique solution. A conforming [Ritz method](rayleigh-ritz-method.md) minimizes the corresponding strictly convex quadratic energy and produces a symmetric positive-definite [tridiagonal](tridiagonal-matrix.md) [matrix](matrix.md) for piecewise-linear hats.
