# Viscous torque in an accretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

For vertically integrated kinematic viscosity $\bar\nu$, the viscous torque across a cylinder of radius $r$ is

$$
\mathcal G=-2\pi\bar\nu\Sigma r^3\frac{d\Omega}{dr}.
$$
