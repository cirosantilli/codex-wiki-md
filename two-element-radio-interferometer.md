# Two-element radio interferometer

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomical interferometer](astronomical-interferometer.md)

A [two-element radio interferometer](two-element-radio-interferometer.md) coherently combines signals from two receiving [antennas](antenna-radio.md). With equal single-arm [wave intensity](intensity-physics.md) $I_0$, projected baseline $b$, free-space [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda$ and imposed [phase difference](phase-difference.md) $\delta$, its directional response is the displayed formula. It follows by adding the two [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md) and taking the squared [modulus](modulus.md). Near the normal to the baseline, the first null is $\lambda/(2b)$ from a maximum. This narrow response repeats in [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md), so a long baseline alone does not give an unambiguous image.
