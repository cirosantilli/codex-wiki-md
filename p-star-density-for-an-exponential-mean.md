# P-star density for an exponential mean

↑ **Parent:** [P-star approximation](p-star-approximation.md)

For a size-$n$ sample from an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of mean $\theta$, the likelihood in the [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) coordinate $v=\bar Y$ has difference $\ell(\theta;v)-\ell(v;v)=n\log(v/\theta)-nv/\theta+n$, and the fitted [observed information](observed-fisher-information.md) is $n/v^2$. Their [P-star approximation](p-star-approximation.md) is the displayed gamma density shape. Its normalization gives exactly the sampling density $n^nv^{n-1}e^{-nv/\theta}/(\Gamma(n)\theta^n)$. This illustrates an exact transformation-model case rather than a general claim of exactness.
