<h1 id="weighted-identity-for-howard-s-semicircle-theorem">Weighted identity for Howard's semicircle theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Howard's semicircle theorem](howard-s-semicircle-theorem.md)

For a nonreal regular [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md) mode vanishing at the walls, put $F=\widehat w/(U-c)$. The equation becomes $[(U-c)^2F']'-k^2(U-c)^2F=0$. Multiplication by $\overline F$ and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives the displayed identity. With $Q=|F'|^2+k^2|F|^2$, its imaginary and real parts give $\int UQ=c_r\int Q$ and $\int U^2Q=|c|^2\int Q$. Since $(U-U_{\min})(U_{\max}-U)\ge0$, these imply [Howard's semicircle theorem](howard-s-semicircle-theorem.md).
