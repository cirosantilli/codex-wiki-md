# Valuation equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Valuation](valuation.md)

Two additive [valuations](valuation.md) are equivalent when they have the same [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md). Their ordered value-group images then identify compatibly with the [valuations](valuation.md); for nontrivial real-valued rank-one [valuations](valuation.md) this is multiplication by a positive constant.
