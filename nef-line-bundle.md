# Nef line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Positivity of divisors](positivity-of-divisors.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nef_line_bundle)

A [line bundle](line-bundle.md) is nef when its degree on every integral complete curve is nonnegative. Equivalently a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$ is nef when $D\cdot C\geq0$ for every such curve. On a projective scheme, adding a positive ample class to a nef class makes it ample; taking limits in the [intersection product](intersection-product-of-cartier-divisors.md) shows that a nef divisor has nonnegative top [self-intersection number](self-intersection-number.md).
