# Nucleoprotein filament

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

A [nucleoprotein filament](nucleoprotein-filament.md) is an extended assembly containing [protein](protein-split.md) and [nucleic acid](nucleic-acid.md). In [RecA](reca.md)-family reactions, repeated subunits organize [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and couple neighboring nucleotide states.
