# Inertial modes of an incompressible disk

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric waves of a homogeneous incompressible disk](axisymmetric-waves-of-a-homogeneous-incompressible-disk.md)

For $0<\omega^2<\kappa^2$, set $S=kH\omega/\sqrt{\kappa^2-\omega^2}$. Even-pressure modes satisfy $\omega^2=-\Omega_z^2S\tan S$, while odd-pressure modes satisfy $\omega^2=\Omega_z^2S\cot S$. In the case $\Omega_z>\kappa>0$, the branches approach $\kappa$ as $kH\to0$. For each fixed vertical order $n\geq1$ and large $kH$, $S\to n\pi/2$ and $\omega\sim\kappa n\pi/(2kH)$. Even orders have even [pressure](pressure.md) and odd orders odd [pressure](pressure.md). The limiting [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) is zero normal derivative of [pressure](pressure.md) at both interfaces.
