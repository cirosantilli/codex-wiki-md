# Settling rarefaction fan

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic hindered-settling flux](quadratic-hindered-settling-flux.md)

If lower initial concentration $b$ is less than upper concentration $a$, the [entropy solution](entropy-solution.md) expands into a fan with $\phi=[1+(z-z_m)/(u_st)]/2$. Its characteristic speeds range from $u_s(2b-1)$ to $u_s(2a-1)$. The fan can be clipped by a clearing or deposition [shock](shock-wave.md).
