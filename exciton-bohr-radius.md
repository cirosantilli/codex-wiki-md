# Exciton Bohr radius

↑ **Parent:** [Wannier-Mott exciton](wannier-mott-exciton.md)

The characteristic relative-motion length of a hydrogenic [Wannier-Mott exciton](wannier-mott-exciton.md), with dielectric screening and [exciton reduced mass](exciton-reduced-mass.md) $\mu^*$. Comparing a [quantum dot](quantum-dot.md)'s dimensions with this scale helps distinguish strong carrier confinement from confinement of a comparatively intact bound pair.
