# Capillary instability of an annular liquid lining

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh–Plateau instability](rayleigh-plateau-instability.md)

A thin liquid lining inside a rigid cylindrical tube has [pressure](pressure.md) perturbation $p_1=-\sigma(a^{-2}h_1+h_{1,xx})$ at leading order in $h_0/a$. [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) gives flux $q=-h_0^3p_x/(3\mu)$, so [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives the displayed growth rate. Long waves with $0<k^2a^2<1$ grow, while short waves are stabilized by [curvature](curvature.md). The maximum occurs at $k^2a^2=1/2$.
