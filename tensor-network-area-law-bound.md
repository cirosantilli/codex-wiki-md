# Tensor-network area-law bound

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement area law](entanglement-area-law.md)

If a bipartition cuts $n_\partial$ tensor-network bonds of dimension at most $\chi$, its Schmidt rank is at most $\chi^{n_\partial}$ and its [entanglement entropy](entanglement-entropy.md) is at most $n_\partial\log\chi$.
