# Two adjacent transitions generate the qutrit unitary Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical Lie algebra](dynamical-lie-algebra.md)

Let $H_0=\operatorname{diag}(-\omega_1,0,-\omega_2)$ and $H_1=d_1(E_{12}+E_{21})+d_2(E_{23}+E_{32})$, with nonzero $d_j$, distinct positive $\omega_j$. The squared adjoint action of $-iH_0$ has different [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $-\omega_1^2,-\omega_2^2$ on the two adjacent skew-Hermitian couplings. Linear combinations of $-iH_1$ and its double [commutator](commutator.md) with $-iH_0$ isolate both couplings. Further [commutators](commutator.md) give their real antisymmetric partners, two traceless diagonal generators, and both $1$–$3$ generators, hence the [special unitary Lie algebra](special-unitary-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{su}(3)$. The drift has nonzero [trace](matrix-trace.md); subtracting its traceless part supplies $iI$, giving the [Lie algebra of the unitary group](unitary-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak u(3)$ and [unitary operator controllability](unitary-operator-controllability.md) under the usual freely timed real-control assumptions.
