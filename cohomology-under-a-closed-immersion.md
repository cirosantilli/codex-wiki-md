# Cohomology under a closed immersion

↑ **Parent:** [Leray's theorem](leray-s-theorem.md)

If $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ is a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md) of [Noetherian schemes](noetherian-scheme.md), $X$ is separated, and $\mathcal F$ is [quasi-coherent](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) on $Z$, then

$$
H^q(X,i_*\mathcal F)\cong H^q(Z,\mathcal F).
$$

Indeed, an affine cover of $X$ induces an affine cover of $Z$, and the two associated [Čech cochain complexes](cech-cochain-complex.md) are termwise identical.
