# Distance

↑ **Parent:** [Geometry](geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distance)

A measure of separation between locations or objects. In a [metric space](metric-space.md), distance is specified by a [metric](metric.md) satisfying positivity, symmetry and the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md). The familiar straight-line separation in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) is [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md); other metrics can assign different distances to the same pair of points.
