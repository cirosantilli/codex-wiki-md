# Tube lens

↑ **Parent:** [Infinity-corrected microscope](infinity-corrected-microscope.md)

The [optical lens](lens-optics.md) assembly that focuses the objective's parallel bundles into the [intermediate microscope image](intermediate-microscope-image.md) in an [infinity-corrected microscope](infinity-corrected-microscope.md). Its [focal length](focal-length.md) participates in the objective-system [transverse magnification](transverse-magnification.md) $-f_t/f_o$.
