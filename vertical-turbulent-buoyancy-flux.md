# Vertical turbulent buoyancy flux

↑ **Parent:** [Eddy diffusion](eddy-diffusion.md)

The covariance of vertical velocity and buoyancy fluctuations has dimensions length squared per time cubed. It supplies buoyancy production in the [local turbulent kinetic energy balance](local-turbulent-kinetic-energy-balance.md). This local flux density differs dimensionally from the section-integrated [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) of a plume, which includes an area or width integration.
