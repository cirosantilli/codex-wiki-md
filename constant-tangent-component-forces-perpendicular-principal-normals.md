# Constant tangent component forces perpendicular principal normals

↑ **Parent:** [Principal normal vector](principal-normal-vector.md)

For a regular [curve](curve.md) with positive [curvature of a space curve](curvature-of-a-space-curve.md), the [Frenet-Serret formulas](frenet-serret-formulas.md) give $dT/ds=\kappa N$. If the [unit tangent vector](unit-tangent-vector.md) has constant component along a fixed [vector](vector.md) $a$, differentiation of $T\cdot a$ gives $\kappa N\cdot a=0$. Hence every [principal normal vector](principal-normal-vector.md) is perpendicular to that direction. Nonzero curvature is needed to define the principal normal.
