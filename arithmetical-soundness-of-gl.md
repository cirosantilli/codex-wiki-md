# Arithmetical soundness of GL

↑ **Parent:** [Gödel-Löb logic](godel-lob-logic.md)

Map propositional letters to arithmetic sentences, map falsity to a refutable sentence, commute with implication and interpret $\Box$ by an [arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md). Propositional axioms remain theorems. The K axiom uses the second of the [derivability conditions](hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md), and the Löb axiom uses the [Internal form of Löb's theorem](internal-form-of-lob-s-theorem.md). [Modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) is preserved directly, and the [necessitation rule](necessitation-rule.md) uses the first derivability condition. Induction on a GL proof therefore proves the displayed soundness implication for [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md) with the stipulated provability predicate.
