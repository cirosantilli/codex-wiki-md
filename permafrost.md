# Permafrost

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permafrost)

Permafrost is ground that remains at or below its freezing threshold for at least two consecutive years. Thaw can expose previously frozen organic material to decomposition, with [methanogenesis](methanogenesis.md) where conditions are anaerobic.
