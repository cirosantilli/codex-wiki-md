# Semiample and curve-positive ampleness criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Semiample divisor](semiample-divisor.md)

A [semiample divisor](semiample-divisor.md) on an integral [projective variety](projective-variety.md) is [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) if it has positive degree on every integral [projective curve](projective-curve.md). Its [Kodaira map](kodaira-map.md) cannot contract a positive-dimensional fibre, since such a fibre contains a curve and the pulled-back hyperplane bundle has degree zero there. Thus the morphism is proper and a [quasi-finite morphism](quasi-finite-morphism.md), hence a [finite morphism](finite-morphism.md). The [finite pullback of an ample line bundle](finite-pullback-of-an-ample-line-bundle.md) is [ample](ample-line-bundle.md).
