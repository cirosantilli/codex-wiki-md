# Aho-Corasick automaton

↑ **Parent:** [Aho-Corasick algorithm](aho-corasick-algorithm.md)

An Aho-Corasick automaton stores a [trie](trie.md) of patterns together with failure links and pattern-output information. It supports simultaneous pattern matching by the [Aho-Corasick algorithm](aho-corasick-algorithm.md).
