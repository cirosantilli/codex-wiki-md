# Meridian transit

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial coordinate system](equatorial-coordinate-system.md)

A [meridian transit](meridian-transit.md) is a direction's crossing of the observer's [celestial meridian](celestial-meridian.md). Upper [meridian transit](meridian-transit.md) occurs at [hour angle](hour-angle.md) $0$ and gives the greatest geometric [altitude](altitude.md) in the fixed-direction model; lower [meridian transit](meridian-transit.md) occurs at [hour angle](hour-angle.md) $12^{\rm h}$ and gives the least. Either crossing can lie below the [astronomical horizon](astronomical-horizon.md). [US Naval Observatory: Upper and lower culmination](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/asa_glossary).
