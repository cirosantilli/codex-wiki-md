# Statistics and symmetry factors of a two-propagator loop

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md)

A closed Dirac loop gives a minus sign and a [spinor](spinor.md) [trace](matrix-trace.md). Replacing it by two distinguishable real scalar species removes both, with no extra interchange factor. One identical real scalar species instead has [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) two. A cubic interaction $g\phi\chi^2/2$ has vertex $ig$, whereas $g\phi\chi^2$ has vertex $2ig$; those choices give different numerical coefficients even though their drawn loop topology is the same.
