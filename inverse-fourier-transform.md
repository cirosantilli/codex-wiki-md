# Inverse Fourier transform

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md)

With the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) convention $\widehat f(k)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e^{-ikx}\,dx$, the inverse Fourier transform is the displayed [linear operator](linear-operator.md). The integral is defined directly for an [integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) $g$. The [Fourier inversion theorem](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) ensures $\mathcal F^{-1}\widehat f=f$ under its hypotheses, in particular for [Schwartz functions](schwartz-function.md), and for integrable $f$ and $\widehat f$ at continuity points of $f$. The operation also extends to [tempered distributions](tempered-distribution.md); the normalization and sign depend on the chosen [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) convention.
