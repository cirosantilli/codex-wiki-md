# Sarcoplasmic reticulum

↑ **Parent:** [Endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

The [sarcoplasmic reticulum](sarcoplasmic-reticulum.md) is the specialized [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) of muscle cells that stores and releases [calcium](calcium.md). Pump uptake, channel-mediated release and passive leak exchange [calcium](calcium.md) with the [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md); these internal transfers cancel in the appropriately volume-weighted total-calcium balance. They can nevertheless change cytoplasmic [calcium](calcium.md) rapidly and control contraction.
