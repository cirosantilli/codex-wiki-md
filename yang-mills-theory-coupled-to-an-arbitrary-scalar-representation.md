# Yang-Mills theory coupled to an arbitrary scalar representation

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

For a compact simple [gauge group](gauge-group.md), use $B=-\kappa$ on its algebra and an averaged positive invariant [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md) $h$ on any supplied finite-dimensional complex representation. With anti-Hermitian connection and signature $(+---)$, $\mathcal L=-B(\mathcal F_{\mu\nu},\mathcal F^{\mu\nu})/(4g^2)+h(D_\mu\Phi,D^\mu\Phi)-V(\Phi)$ is [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md) whenever $V$ is invariant. A real scalar representation instead has a symmetric form and kinetic prefactor $1/2$.
