# Angular momentum deficit

↑ **Parent:** [Secular perturbation](secular-perturbation.md)

The difference between the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) of circular coplanar orbits at fixed [semi-major axes](semi-major-axis.md) and the system's actual angular momentum along the reference normal. With circular momenta $L_j=m_j\sqrt{GM_\star a_j}$ it is $\sum_jL_j(1-\sqrt{1-e_j^2}\cos I_j)$. For small planar [orbital eccentricities](orbital-eccentricity.md) its quadratic part is $\frac12\sum_jL_j|z_j|^2$. Its conservation in $\dot z=iAz$ requires $L_jA_{jk}=L_kA_{kj}$.
