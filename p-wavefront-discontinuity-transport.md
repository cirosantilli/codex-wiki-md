# P-wavefront discontinuity transport

↑ **Parent:** [P wave](p-wave.md)

Let $t=T(\mathbf x)$ be a smooth [wavefront](wavefront.md) and suppose the first jump in time [derivatives](derivative.md) of [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) is $[\partial_t^k\mathbf u]=a^{[k]}\mathbf n$, with $k\geq2$. Substitution of $\mathbf u=a^{[k]}\mathbf n(t-T)_+^k/k!+\cdots$ into the elastic equation first gives the [eikonal equation](eikonal-equation.md) $|\nabla T|=1/\alpha$ and longitudinal polarization $\mathbf n=\alpha\nabla T$. Projecting the next-order compatibility condition onto $\mathbf n$ gives $2\rho\alpha\mathbf n\cdot\nabla a^{[k]}+a^{[k]}\nabla\cdot(\rho\alpha\mathbf n)=0$, hence the displayed conservation law. Coefficients and [wavefront](wavefront.md) must be sufficiently differentiable for this classical transport equation; its local form fails at a [caustic](wave-caustic.md).
