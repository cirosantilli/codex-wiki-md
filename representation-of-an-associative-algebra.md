# Representation of an associative algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

An algebra representation is a unital [algebra homomorphism](algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md) into the [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md). It is equivalent to making that [vector space](vector-space-split.md) a left [module](module-mathematics.md) over the algebra, with $av=\rho(a)v$.
