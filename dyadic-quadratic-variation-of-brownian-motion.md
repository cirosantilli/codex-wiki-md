# Dyadic quadratic variation of Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md)

On $[0,1]$, the sum $Q_n$ of squared adjacent dyadic increments of standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) has $\mathbb E Q_n=1$ and $\operatorname{var}(Q_n)=2^{1-n}$. Therefore $Q_n\to1$ in $L^2$. On an interval of length $t$, scaling gives mean $t$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $2t^2 2^{-n}$. [Independence](independent-random-variables.md) is used within each grid, not between different grids.
