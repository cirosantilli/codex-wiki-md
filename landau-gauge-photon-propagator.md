# Landau gauge photon propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Landau gauge (quantum field theory)](landau-gauge-quantum-field-theory.md)

The [covariant Landau gauge](landau-gauge-quantum-field-theory.md) propagator is the transverse limit of the [inversion of the gauge-fixed Maxwell kinetic operator](inversion-of-the-gauge-fixed-maxwell-kinetic-operator.md). Contracting with $k^\mu$ gives zero away from the pole. The [Lorenz gauge](lorenz-gauge-condition.md) condition still has residual transformations satisfying $\Box\chi=0$, so boundary conditions and the pole prescription complete the choice.
