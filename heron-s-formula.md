<h1 id="heron-s-formula">Heron's formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

For a [triangle](triangle.md) with side lengths $a,b,c$ and [semiperimeter](semiperimeter.md) $s=(a+b+c)/2$, its [area](surface-area.md) is $A=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$. This expresses the [area](surface-area.md) using only the three side lengths.
