# Polynomial progression in a high-energy fourfold difference set

↑ **Parent:** [Additive energy](additive-energy.md)

For every $\theta>0$, some $\gamma(\theta)>0$ has this property: any nonempty finite $A\subseteq\mathbb Z$ with $E(A)\geq\theta|A|^3$ has an [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) of length at least $|A|^{\gamma(\theta)}$ in $2A-2A$. The [small-difference-set form of the Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](small-difference-set-form-of-the-balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md), [Ruzsa modelling lemma](ruzsa-modelling-lemma.md), [cyclic Bogolyubov lemma](cyclic-bogolyubov-lemma.md) and [nonwrapping progression in a cyclic Bohr set](nonwrapping-progression-in-a-cyclic-bohr-set.md) prove it. An order-eight [Freiman s-isomorphism](freiman-s-isomorphism.md) suffices to lift the progression because its consecutive second-difference equations expand into equalities of eight-term sums.
