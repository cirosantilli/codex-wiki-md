# Universe for spectra

↑ **Parent:** [Stable homotopy theory](stable-homotopy-theory.md)

A [universe for spectra](universe-for-spectra.md) is a countably infinite-dimensional real [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), normally with its direct-limit topology, used to index finite-dimensional suspension coordinates. A cofinal indexing family contains arbitrarily large finite-dimensional subspaces.
