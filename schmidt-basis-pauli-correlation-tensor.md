# Schmidt-basis Pauli correlation tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md)

For the [pure state](pure-state.md) $\lambda_0|00\rangle+\lambda_1|11\rangle$ with real normalized [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md), the two-body [Pauli correlators](pauli-correlator.md) form $T=\operatorname{diag}(s,-s,1)$, $s=2\lambda_0\lambda_1$. Thus arbitrary local axes give $\langle(\mathbf a\cdot\sigma)\otimes(\mathbf b\cdot\sigma)\rangle=s(a_xb_x-a_yb_y)+a_zb_z$. The negative $yy$ component follows from the phases in the [Pauli operators](pauli-operator.md).
