# Phase averaging in a linkage likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Pedigree likelihood](pedigree-likelihood.md)

An unphased multilocus [genotype](genotype.md) can have several pairs of [haplotypes](haplotype.md). Its [pedigree likelihood](pedigree-likelihood.md) must average over these possible phases with prior weights $\pi_s$, rather than maximize over phases after observing the offspring. Under [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md), the weights need not be equal. They can be obtained by conditioning population haplotype frequencies on the observed parental [genotype](genotype.md); different choices of what is conditioned on change only parameter-independent factors when those frequencies are held fixed.
