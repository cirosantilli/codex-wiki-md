# Conical flux by closing with a cap

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md)

To evaluate outward [flux](flux.md) through the lateral surface of a finite [right circular cone](right-circular-cone.md), add its planar cap and apply the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) to the enclosed solid. Subtract the cap flux, whose outward normal points away from the solid. For a cone $r=az$ with $0\leq z\leq h$, the volume element is $r\,dr\,d\varphi\,dz$ and the cap has radius $ah$. The cone's vertex has no unique normal, but is a set of area zero; one can remove a small tip and take a limit when the field is regular there.
