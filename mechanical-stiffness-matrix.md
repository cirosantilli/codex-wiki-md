# Mechanical stiffness matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized eigenvalue problem for small oscillations](generalized-eigenvalue-problem-for-small-oscillations.md)

The [Hessian](hessian-matrix.md) of [potential energy](potential-energy.md) at an [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). With a positive [mechanical mass matrix](mechanical-mass-matrix.md), its generalized [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) give squared small-oscillation frequencies. Distinct [normal modes](normal-mode.md) are orthogonal in the mass-weighted [inner product](inner-product-split.md).
