# Kernel list-coloring lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel-perfect directed graph](kernel-perfect-directed-graph.md)

In a [kernel-perfect directed graph](kernel-perfect-directed-graph.md), lists of size at least $d^+(v)+1$ admit a proper [graph coloring](graph-coloring.md). Choose a color $a$ and a [digraph kernel](kernel-of-a-directed-graph.md) in the subgraph on vertices whose lists contain $a$. Color that kernel $a$, delete it, and remove $a$ from the remaining lists. Every vertex losing a color also loses an outgoing neighbour; hence the inequality persists. Induction on the [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) count finishes the proof.
