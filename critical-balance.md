# Critical balance

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic turbulence](magnetohydrodynamic-turbulence.md)

[Critical balance](critical-balance.md) is a scale-dependent ordering in which the wave-propagation time and nonlinear interaction time are comparable. For strong [Alfvénic turbulence](alfvenic-turbulence.md), $k_\parallel v_A\sim k_\perp\delta u_\perp$. For [electron magnetohydrodynamics](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md), the linear rate is $v_Ad_i k_\parallel k_\perp$ and the nonlinear rate is $v_Ad_i k_\perp^2\delta B/B_0$, so [critical balance](critical-balance.md) instead gives $\delta B/B_0\sim k_\parallel/k_\perp$. This assumption is an estimate of decorrelation dynamics, not an exact equality at every point.
