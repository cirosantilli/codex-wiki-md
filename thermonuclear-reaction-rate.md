# Thermonuclear reaction rate

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md)

A thermal distribution of relative velocities weights a nuclear cross-section to determine the reaction rate per volume. Identical reactants require the denominator two to avoid counting each pair twice. Specific deposited power is $r_{12}Q_{\rm deposited}/\rho$. Charged-particle [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) through a [Coulomb barrier](coulomb-barrier.md) produces a [Gamow peak](gamow-peak.md), while resonances and plasma screening can alter the rate.
