# Boryl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [boryl ligand](boryl-ligand.md) attaches through boron as an X-type ligand. In ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md) it is formally anionic and contributes two [electrons](electron.md). It is distinct from a neutral borylene and from attachment to an entire [borazine](borazine.md) pi face.
