# Proximal operator of Poisson data fidelity

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson data fidelity](poisson-data-fidelity.md)

For $y>0$ and $\gamma>0$, minimizing $|x-z|^2/2+\gamma[y\log(y/x)+x-y]$ on $x>0$ gives $x^2+(\gamma-z)x-\gamma y=0$. The roots have opposite signs, so only the displayed positive root belongs to the effective domain. Strict positivity of the second derivative proves uniqueness. For an integral [Poisson data fidelity](poisson-data-fidelity.md), the [proximal operator](proximal-operator.md) separates pointwise under the appropriate integrability assumptions.
