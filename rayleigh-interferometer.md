# Rayleigh interferometer

↑ **Parent:** [Interferometer](interferometer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayleigh_interferometer)

A Rayleigh interferometer compares optical paths through a sample and a reference. A path-length difference $\Delta\ell$ shifts the interference phase by $2\pi\Delta\ell/\lambda$. In [analytical ultracentrifugation](analytical-ultracentrifugation.md), sample and reference sectors produce concentration-dependent fringe shifts, allowing radial concentration scans without requiring the solute to absorb at the probe wavelength.[Rayleigh interference optics: reference sector, refractive-index difference and fringe displacement](optical-systems-used-for-auc-by-beckman-coulter-life-sciences.md)
