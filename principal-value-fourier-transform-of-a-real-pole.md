# Principal-value Fourier transform of a real pole

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform of a tempered distribution](fourier-transform-of-a-tempered-distribution.md)

A real [simple pole](simple-pole.md) is not locally integrable in the ordinary improper sense. Its symmetric [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) defines a [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md). For the $e^{-ikx}$ transform convention, contour indentation or sine integration gives the displayed nondecaying oscillatory contribution. Upper and lower boundary-value prescriptions instead change it by $\mp i\pi\delta(x-a)$ before transforming. A [pole](pole.md) prescription must therefore be stated before claiming a Fourier [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md).
