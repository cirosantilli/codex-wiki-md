# Second derivatives under a two-variable coordinate change

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariable chain rule](multivariable-chain-rule.md)

For twice continuously differentiable coordinate functions $u,v$ and an outer scalar function $f$, apply the [multivariable chain rule](multivariable-chain-rule.md) twice along an original coordinate $s$. The first three terms differentiate the outer function, while the last two differentiate the coordinate coefficients. They cannot be omitted for a nonlinear coordinate map. In matrix notation this is the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) formula $H_{f\circ g}=J_g^TH_fJ_g+f_uH_u+f_vH_v$ for $g=(u,v)$. The extra terms vanish for an [affine map](affine-map.md).
