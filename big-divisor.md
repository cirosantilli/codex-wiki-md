# Big divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Positivity of divisors](positivity-of-divisors.md)

A [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$ on an integral $n$-dimensional projective variety is big when $h^0(X,mD)\geq c m^n$ for some $c>0$ and infinitely many positive integers $m$. Equivalently, its [Iitaka dimension](iitaka-dimension.md) is $n$. [Kodaira's lemma](kodaira-s-lemma.md) characterizes bigness by an ample-plus-effective decomposition, and the [birational linear system criterion for bigness](birational-linear-system-criterion-for-bigness.md) shows why this growth captures the full variety.
