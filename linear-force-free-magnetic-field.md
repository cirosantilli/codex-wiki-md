# Linear force-free magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Force-free magnetic field](force-free-magnetic-field.md)

A [force-free magnetic field](force-free-magnetic-field.md) is linear when the proportionality between its [curl](curl.md) and itself is a spatial constant. The [curl of the curl identity](curl-of-the-curl-identity.md) and zero [divergence](divergence.md) give the vector [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md) $\nabla^2\mathbf B+\lambda^2\mathbf B=0$. Here $\lambda$ has inverse-length units; specifying a constant current-to-field coefficient with another normalization changes its numerical definition.
