# Faithful module

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

A [module](module-mathematics.md) is faithful when its [annihilator of a module](annihilator-of-a-module.md) is zero. Equivalently, the action [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $R\to\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb Z}(M)$ to the [endomorphism ring](endomorphism-ring.md) of its additive [abelian group](abelian-group.md) is [injective](injective-function.md): every nonzero scalar acts nontrivially somewhere on $M$. A [primitive ring](primitive-ring.md) has a faithful [simple module](irreducible-module.md).
