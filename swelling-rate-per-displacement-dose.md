# Swelling rate per displacement dose

↑ **Parent:** [Void swelling](void-swelling.md)

The derivative of volume [void swelling](void-swelling.md) with respect to displacement dose, $dS/dD$. It differs from a time rate; conversion requires a dose rate and a specified irradiation history.
