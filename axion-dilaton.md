# Axion-dilaton

↑ **Parent:** [Type IIB superstring theory](type-iib-superstring-theory.md)

The complex scalar combines the IIB axion and [dilaton](dilaton.md). Its imaginary part is $1/g_s$ for a constant background. [S-duality](s-duality.md) acts by fractional linear transformations, corresponding to changes of torus basis in the [M-theory torus duality](m-theory-torus-duality.md).
