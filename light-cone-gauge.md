# Light cone gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_cone_gauge)

In gauge field theory its standard form is $A^+=n_\mu A^\mu=0$ for a fixed null direction $n$. A light-cone gauge uses [light-cone coordinates](light-cone-coordinates.md) to fix a longitudinal variable, often choosing $x^+$ as the evolution parameter for a [relativistic particle phase-space action](relativistic-particle-phase-space-action.md). Related field gauges set selected minus components to zero when a field has the required [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md). A massive field without that gauge freedom must instead eliminate dependent components through its equations of motion.
