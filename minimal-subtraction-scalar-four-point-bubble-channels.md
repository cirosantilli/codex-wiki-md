# Minimal-subtraction scalar four-point bubble channels

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar bubble integral](scalar-bubble-integral.md)

For one real [phi-fourth theory](quartic-interaction.md) field, each of the three labeled one-loop four-point channels has [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) $1/2$. In $d=4-2\epsilon$, the displayed expression is its amplitude through the finite part. The three poles require $\delta\lambda=3\lambda^2/(32\pi^2\epsilon)$, and differentiating $\lambda_B=\mu^{2\epsilon}(\lambda+\delta\lambda)$ at fixed bare coupling gives $\beta(\lambda)=3\lambda^2/(16\pi^2)+O(\lambda^3)$ in four dimensions. The [minimal subtraction scheme](minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) retains $\log(4\pi)-\gamma_E$; [modified minimal subtraction](modified-minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) absorbs that constant. The Feynman-parameter logarithm is left unevaluated.
