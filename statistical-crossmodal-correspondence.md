# Statistical crossmodal correspondence

↑ **Parent:** [Crossmodal correspondence](crossmodal-correspondence.md)

A correspondence proposed to reflect learned co-occurrence or covariation of sensory attributes. A discriminating test changes the experienced relation while controlling exposure and tests transfer; a familiar pairing alone does not establish how it was acquired.
