# Strong-pseudoprime bases for a product of two primes with common predecessor divisor ten

↑ **Parent:** [Strong pseudoprime](strong-pseudoprime.md)

Let $N=pq$ for distinct odd [prime numbers](prime-number.md) $p,q$ with $\gcd(p-1,q-1)=10$. Exactly $100$ unit bases make $N$ a [Fermat pseudoprime](fermat-pseudoprime.md), and exactly $50$ of these make it a [strong pseudoprime](strong-pseudoprime.md). The Fermat bases form $C_{10}\times C_{10}$ under the [Chinese remainder theorem for unit groups](chinese-remainder-theorem-for-unit-groups.md). If $N-1=2^sm$, then $5\mid m$ and $m$ is odd, so the $m$th-power map sends five elements of each $C_{10}$ to $1$ and five to $-1$. The strong condition requires equal signs in both components, giving $5^2+5^2=50$.
