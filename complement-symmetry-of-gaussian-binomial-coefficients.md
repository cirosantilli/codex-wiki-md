# Complement symmetry of Gaussian binomial coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Partition interpretation of Gaussian binomial coefficients](partition-interpretation-of-gaussian-binomial-coefficients.md)

Pad a [partition in a rectangle](partition-in-a-rectangle.md) with zero rows until it has $k$ entries, and put $w=n-k$. The complementary rotated diagram has row lengths $\lambda'_i=w-\lambda_{k+1-i}$. This is again a [partition in a rectangle](partition-in-a-rectangle.md), and its size is $kw-|\lambda|$. Repeating the operation recovers the original [integer partition](integer-partition.md), so it gives a [bijection](bijection.md) between diagrams of size $a$ and diagrams of size $kw-a$. Consequently the [coefficients](coefficient.md) of the [Gaussian polynomial](gaussian-binomial-coefficient.md) read the same in either direction. The displayed identity can be interpreted as an identity of [Laurent polynomials](laurent-polynomial.md); it does not require substituting $t^{-1}$ at $t=0$.

For the two-by-two [rectangle](rectangle.md), the empty diagram pairs with $(2,2)$, and $(1)$ pairs with $(2,1)$; $(2)$ and $(1,1)$ each map to themselves. This establishes symmetry, while [unimodality of Gaussian binomial coefficients](unimodality-of-gaussian-binomial-coefficients.md) is a stronger statement about the growth and decrease of successive [coefficients](coefficient.md).
