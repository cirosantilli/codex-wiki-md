# Complex cell

↑ **Parent:** [Primary visual cortex](primary-visual-cortex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_cell)

A [complex cell](complex-cell.md) typically responds selectively to an oriented edge or bar over a finite spatial region with less dependence on spatial phase and contrast polarity than a [simple cell](simple-cell.md). It need not have the simple cell's separated linear ON and OFF subregions. Phase tolerance is local, not invariance to arbitrary displacement outside its [receptive field](receptive-field.md).
