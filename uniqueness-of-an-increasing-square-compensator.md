# Uniqueness of an increasing square compensator

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md)

For a [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) $M$, at most one continuous adapted increasing process $A$ starting at zero makes $M^2-A$ a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). The difference of two candidates is a continuous [finite-variation process](finite-variation-process.md) and a [local martingale](local-martingale.md), hence constant; its initial value is zero. Without the initial-value normalization only uniqueness up to an initial constant is possible.
