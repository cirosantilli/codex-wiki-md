# p-biased Fourier coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [P-biased product measure](p-biased-product-measure.md)

The $p$-biased Fourier coefficient of $f$ at $S$ is $\widehat f_p(S)=\mathbb E_{\mu_p}[f\phi_S]$.
