# Genetic transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)

[Genetic transformation](genetic-transformation.md) introduces [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) into recipient [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). It can change their [genotype](genotype.md) and sometimes their [phenotype](phenotype.md); the [yeast cross-species complementation experiment](yeast-cross-species-complementation-experiment.md) separates detecting introduction of a [plasmid](plasmid.md) from detecting its functional effect.
