# BCH and parity-extension proof of the binary Golay distance

↑ **Parent:** [Binary Golay code](binary-golay-code.md)

A root $\beta$ of the binary [Golay generator polynomial](golay-generator-polynomial.md) has order $23$, and the [Frobenius endomorphism](frobenius-endomorphism.md) shows that $\beta,\beta^2,\beta^3,\beta^4$ are roots: $2^8\equiv3\pmod{23}$. The [BCH bound](bch-bound.md) gives [minimum Hamming distance](minimum-distance-of-a-code.md) at least five. The [circular autocorrelation of the binary Golay generator](circular-autocorrelation-of-the-binary-golay-generator.md) makes its [parity extension](parity-extension.md) [doubly even](doubly-even-code.md), excluding original weights five and six. The generator itself has [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md) seven, proving that the distance is exactly seven.
