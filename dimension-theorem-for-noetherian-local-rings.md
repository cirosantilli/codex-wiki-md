# Dimension theorem for Noetherian local rings

↑ **Parent:** [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md)

For a Noetherian local ring $(A,\mathfrak m)$, the Krull dimension of $A$, the Krull dimension of the [associated graded ring](associated-graded-ring.md) $\operatorname{gr}_{\mathfrak m}(A)$, and the order of the pole at $t=1$ of its [Hilbert series](hilbert-series.md) are equal.
