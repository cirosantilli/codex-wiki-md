# Membrane permeability

↑ **Parent:** [Selective permeability](selective-permeability.md)

[membrane permeability](membrane-permeability.md) describes passage of a specified substance through a membrane under stated conditions. It is distinct from lateral [diffusion](diffusion.md) within the membrane and from the mechanical force needed to rupture the membrane.
