# Posterior mean

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md)

The posterior mean is the [expected value](expected-value.md) of a parameter under its [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md); under squared-error loss it is the Bayes estimator.
