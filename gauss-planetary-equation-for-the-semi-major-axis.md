# Gauss planetary equation for the semi-major axis

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange planetary equations](lagrange-planetary-equations.md)

For perturbing [acceleration](acceleration.md) $R\widehat{\boldsymbol r}+T\widehat{\boldsymbol\theta}$ on an [osculating orbital element](osculating-orbital-element.md) system with fixed central parameter $\mu$,

$$
\dot a=2\sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu(1-e^2)}}[Re\sin f+T(1+e\cos f)].
$$

To prove it, differentiate the [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) $-\mu/(2a)$, obtaining $\dot a=(2a^2/\mu)(R\dot r+Th/r)$. Substitute $\dot r=\mu e\sin f/h$, $h/r=\mu(1+e\cos f)/h$ and $h^2=\mu a(1-e^2)$. Here $f$ is the [true anomaly](true-anomaly.md); a normal perturbing [acceleration](acceleration.md) does no instantaneous [work](work.md).
