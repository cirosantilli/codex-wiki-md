# Cauchy reconstruction from a jump across a finite interval

↑ **Parent:** [Sokhotski–Plemelj theorem](sokhotski-plemelj-theorem.md)

Suppose $w$ is [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) off $[0,1]$, tends to zero at [infinity](infinity.md), has sufficiently regular [boundary values](boundary-value.md) in the interval interior, and has endpoint growth $O(|z-a|^{-\alpha})$ for some $\alpha<1$ at each endpoint $a$. If its jump is integrable, the displayed reconstruction follows by deforming a [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md) [contour](complex-integration-contour.md) onto the two sides of the interval. An endpoint circle of radius $\varepsilon$ contributes $O(\varepsilon^{1-\alpha})$, which vanishes. The two oppositely oriented banks leave precisely the jump. Without decay at [infinity](infinity.md), an entire remainder can survive; a nonzero constant is the simplest example.
