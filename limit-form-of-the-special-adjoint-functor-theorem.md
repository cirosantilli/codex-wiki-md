# Limit form of the special adjoint functor theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Special adjoint functor theorem](special-adjoint-functor-theorem.md)

A [functor](functor.md) $G:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ from a [locally small category](locally-small-category.md) that is complete and [well-powered](well-powered-category.md), with a [small cogenerating family](cogenerating-set.md), to a [locally small category](locally-small-category.md) has a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) exactly when it preserves small [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md). For sufficiency, the [cogenerator bound for comma-category solution sets](cogenerator-bound-for-comma-category-solution-sets.md) produces a [weakly initial set](weakly-initial-set.md) in each complete [comma category](comma-category.md) $(B\downarrow G)$. The [initial-object lemma for complete categories with a weakly initial set](initial-object-lemma-for-complete-categories-with-a-weakly-initial-set.md) supplies its [initial object](initial-object.md), a [universal arrow from an object to a functor](universal-arrow-from-an-object-to-a-functor.md). Necessity is limit preservation by a [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md).
