# Finite-horizon exponential-utility portfolio

↑ **Parent:** [Constant absolute risk aversion utility](constant-absolute-risk-aversion-utility.md)

For [exponential utility](constant-absolute-risk-aversion-utility.md) $(1-e^{-ax})/a$ on unrestricted terminal [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md) in the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md), the optimal dollar holding in the [stock](stock.md) is the displayed deterministic amount. Set $\vartheta=(\mu-\rho)/\sigma$ and let $W^Q=W+\vartheta t$ be the [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) under the [risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md). The terminal optimizer is $X^*=w_0e^{\rho T}+\vartheta W_T^Q/a$. Its [marginal utility](marginal-utility.md) is proportional to the [state-price density](state-price-density.md), and the [concave supporting-tangent inequality](concave-supporting-tangent-inequality.md) proves global optimality under the [state-price budget constraint](state-price-budget-constraint.md). The [expected utility](expected-utility.md) at the optimum is $(1-\exp(-aw_0e^{\rho T}-\vartheta^2T/2))/a$. A nonnegative-wealth constraint changes this optimizer.
