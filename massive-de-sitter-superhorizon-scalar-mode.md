# Massive de Sitter superhorizon scalar mode

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical rescaling of a scalar cosmological perturbation](canonical-rescaling-of-a-scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md)

In the [de Sitter approximation](de-sitter-approximation.md), a canonically rescaled scalar has superhorizon powers $(-\tau)^{1/2\pm\nu}$. The physical field has powers $(-\tau)^{3/2\pm\nu}$. For $H^2<m^2<2H^2$, both physical branches decay, while the slower rescaled branch grows. The generic late-time [dimensionless power spectrum](dimensionless-cosmological-power-spectrum.md) is proportional to $(k/(aH))^{3-2\nu}$.
