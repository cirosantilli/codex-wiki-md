# Prime-character sum near one

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet's theorem on arithmetic progressions](dirichlet-s-theorem-on-arithmetic-progressions.md)

For real $s>1$, the logarithm defined by the [Euler product](euler-product.md) of a [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md) differs from $F_\chi(s)$ by a quantity of absolute value at most $\sum_{n\geq2}1/(n(n-1))=1$. If $L(\chi,1)\ne0$, a local [holomorphic logarithm](holomorphic-logarithm.md) differs from this continuous logarithm by one fixed multiple of $2\pi i$ on a short real interval, so $F_\chi(s)$ stays bounded as $s\downarrow1$. For the [principal Dirichlet character](principal-dirichlet-character.md), the pole of the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md) instead gives $F_{\chi_0}(s)=\log(1/(s-1))+O(1)$. [Orthogonality of Dirichlet characters](orthogonality-of-dirichlet-characters.md) then makes the sum over any reduced residue class diverge, proving infinitude of its [primes](prime-number.md).
