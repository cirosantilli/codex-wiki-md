# Information velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Phase velocity and group velocity](phase-velocity-and-group-velocity.md)

An [information velocity](information-velocity.md) must specify what counts as a newly distinguishable message and how it is detected. In a causal model, receivers cannot distinguish two otherwise identical preparations before their difference can arrive. [Front velocity](front-velocity.md) therefore bounds the arrival of genuinely new changes when the model has a finite [domain of dependence](domain-of-dependence.md). [Group velocity](group-velocity.md) can estimate the delay of a weakly distorted narrow [wave packet](wave-packet.md), but it is not a universal speed of information: [damping](damping.md), amplification and reshaping can move the peak without advancing the first possible response. A Gaussian peak shift alone does not test [relativistic causality](relativistic-causality.md).[equations 871–873 and discussion after equation 887: earliest response versus phase propagation](wave-front-propagation-by-richard-fitzpatrick.md)
