# Interior adjustment coefficient ruin prefactor

↑ **Parent:** [Cramér–Lundberg ruin asymptotic](cramer-lundberg-ruin-asymptotic.md)

When the [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) lies inside the finite domain of the claim [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md), the tilted kernel has finite mean $(\lambda M'(R)-c)/(cR)$. The forcing integral is $(c-\lambda\mu)/(cR)$. Their ratio is the constant $C$ in $\psi(u)\sim Ce^{-Ru}$, by the [key renewal theorem](key-renewal-theorem.md). An extra exponential moment controls the forcing tails and proves [direct Riemann integrability](direct-riemann-integrability.md).
