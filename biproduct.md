# Biproduct

↑ **Parent:** [Semi-additive category](semi-additive-category.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biproduct)

A biproduct $A\oplus B$ is simultaneously a [product in a category](product-category-theory.md) and a [coproduct in a category](coproduct.md), with projections $p_i$ and injections $i_i$ satisfying

$$
p_ii_j=\delta_{ij},
\qquad
i_1p_1+i_2p_2=1_{A\oplus B}.
$$
