# Dimensional transmutation

↑ **Parent:** [Strong-coupling scale](strong-coupling-scale.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimensional_transmutation)

Dimensional transmutation replaces a dimensionless coupling by a dimensionful renormalization-group invariant scale. In an asymptotically free theory, specifying the coupling at one scale is equivalent to specifying its [strong-coupling scale](strong-coupling-scale.md).
