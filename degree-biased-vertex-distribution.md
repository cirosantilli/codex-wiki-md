# Degree-biased vertex distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Degree (graph theory)](degree-graph-theory.md)

In a finite graph with at least one edge, the degree-biased vertex distribution assigns probability proportional to the [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md). It is the distribution of an endpoint on a specified side of a uniformly random edge.
