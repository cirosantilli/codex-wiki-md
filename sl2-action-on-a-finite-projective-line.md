# SL2 action on a finite projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear group over a finite field](special-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md)

A matrix $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$ acts on the [projective line](projective-line.md) by the [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $x\mapsto(ax+b)/(cx+d)$, with the pole sent to infinity and infinity sent to $a/c$ when $c\ne0$. The action is transitive because $\begin{pmatrix}t&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ sends infinity to any finite $t$. The [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of infinity consists of $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&a^{-1}\end{pmatrix}$ with $a\ne0$. The [orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives $|\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb F_q)|=q(q^2-1)$. Its kernel is the scalar matrices of determinant one: a transformation fixing infinity has $c=0$, fixing zero then forces $b=0$, and fixing one gives $a=d$. Thus the kernel is $\{I,-I\}$, with these matrices coinciding in characteristic two.
