# Homozygosity mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic linkage](genetic-linkage.md)

Homozygosity mapping searches for shared homozygous marker regions in individuals with a rare recessive condition, often in an [inbred](inbreeding.md) pedigree. Under a single ancestral disease copy, affected individuals inherit two copies identical by descent. Marker homozygosity alone gives [identity by state](identity-by-state.md), so founder haplotype frequencies matter. If a particular haplotype has founder frequency $p$ and $K$ distinct founder chromosome copies underlie all selected descendant copies, their marker-pattern probability is $E[p^K]$. For complete linkage and a single disease copy, the affected-only numerator is $p$, yielding a [LOD score](lod-score.md) $\log_{10}(p/E[p^K])$ under linkage-equilibrium founder assumptions.
