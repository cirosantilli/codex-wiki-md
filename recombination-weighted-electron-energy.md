# Recombination-weighted electron energy

↑ **Parent:** [Recombination cooling of ionized hydrogen](recombination-cooling-of-ionized-hydrogen.md)

The mean [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) removed per recombination is $\langle E\rangle_{\rm rec}=\int E f_T(E)\sigma_{\rm rec}(E)v(E)\,dE/\int f_T(E)\sigma_{\rm rec}(E)v(E)\,dE$. The capture-rate weighting generally differs from averaging an unselected Maxwell distribution, so $\langle E\rangle_{\rm rec}=3k_BT/2$ is only an additional approximation.
