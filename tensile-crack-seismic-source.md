# Tensile-crack seismic source

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md)

A small planar crack of area $S$ opening normally by $d(t)$ has volume change $V=Sd$ and the displayed [seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md). Its principal dipoles are $\lambda V,\lambda V,(\lambda+2\mu)V$: two act in the crack plane and the largest acts normally for positive opening and ordinary positive Lamé parameters. The definition follows by contracting the isotropic [elastic stiffness tensor](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md) with two copies of the crack normal.
