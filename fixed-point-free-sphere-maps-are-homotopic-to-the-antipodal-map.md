# Fixed-point-free sphere maps are homotopic to the antipodal map

↑ **Parent:** [Antipodal map](antipodal-map.md)

If a continuous map $f:S^d\to S^d$ has no fixed point, the formula $H_t(x)=((1-t)f(x)-tx)/\|(1-t)f(x)-tx\|$ is a [homotopy](homotopy.md) from $f$ to the [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md). The denominator could vanish only at $t=1/2$ and $f(x)=x$, which is excluded. The [homotopy invariance of mapping degree](homotopy-invariance-of-mapping-degree.md) therefore gives $\deg f=(-1)^{d+1}$.
