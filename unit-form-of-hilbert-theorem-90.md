# Unit form of Hilbert theorem 90

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert's theorem 90](hilbert-s-theorem-90.md)

For an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of non-Archimedean [local fields](local-field.md), let $\phi$ generate its cyclic [Galois group](galois-group.md). The ordinary [Hilbert theorem 90](hilbert-s-theorem-90.md) supplies $b\in L^\times$ with $x=\phi(b)/b$. A common [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\pi\in K$ gives $y=\pi^{-v_L(b)}b\in\mathcal O_L^\times$, retaining the same quotient because $\phi$ fixes $\pi$. The reverse implication follows by telescoping the [field norm](field-norm.md).
