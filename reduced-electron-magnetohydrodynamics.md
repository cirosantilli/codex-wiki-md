# Reduced electron magnetohydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Electron magnetohydrodynamics](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

With $k_\parallel/k_\perp\sim\delta B/B_0\ll1$, define $b=\delta B_\parallel/B_0$ and $\delta\mathbf B_\perp/B_0=\hat{\mathbf z}\times\nabla_\perp\Psi/v_A$ to leading order. Let $\nabla_\parallel=\partial_z+\{\Psi,\cdot\}/v_A$, where $\{f,g\}=f_xg_y-f_yg_x$. The leading [Electron](electron.md) [velocities](velocity.md) are $\mathbf u_{e\perp}=v_Ad_i\hat{\mathbf z}\times\nabla_\perp b$ and $u_{e\parallel}=-d_i\nabla_\perp^2\Psi$. Substitution into frozen-in induction yields

$$
\partial_t\Psi=v_A^2d_i\nabla_\parallel b,\qquad \partial_tb=-d_i\nabla_\parallel\nabla_\perp^2\Psi.
$$

[Linearization](linearization.md) gives $\omega^2=v_A^2d_i^2k_\parallel^2k_\perp^2$, agreeing with the full [EMHD](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md) relation to leading anisotropic order. If $b$ varies along the guide field, an order-smaller perpendicular gradient correction restores exact solenoidality; the two-field representation is not an exact decomposition at arbitrary anisotropy.
