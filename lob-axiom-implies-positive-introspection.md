<h1 id="lob-axiom-implies-positive-introspection">Löb axiom implies positive introspection</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gödel-Löb logic](godel-lob-logic.md)

The Löb axiom $\Box(\Box A\to A)\to\Box A$ over normal [modal logic](modal-logic.md) already implies $\Box X\to\Box\Box X$. Put $C=\Box X\wedge X$. Distribution gives $\Box C\to\Box X$, hence $X\to(\Box C\to C)$. Necessitation and distribution give $\Box X\to\Box(\Box C\to C)$; the Löb axiom gives $\Box C$, which implies $\Box\Box X$.
