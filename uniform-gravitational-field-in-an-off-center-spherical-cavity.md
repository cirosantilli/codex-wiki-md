# Uniform gravitational field in an off-center spherical cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

Remove a spherical void centered at $c$ from a uniform sphere centered at the origin. At points inside the void, [superposition](superposition-principle.md) combines the sphere's interior field $-4\pi G\rho x/3$ with the removed sphere's field $+4\pi G\rho(x-c)/3$. Their sum is the displayed constant [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md). A second removed mass outside this void adds a nonuniform contribution.
