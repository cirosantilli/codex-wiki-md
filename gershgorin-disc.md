# Gershgorin disc

↑ **Parent:** [Gershgorin circle theorem](gershgorin-circle-theorem.md)

For row $i$ of a matrix, the [Gershgorin disc](gershgorin-disc.md) has centre $M_{ii}$ and radius $\sum_{j\ne i}|M_{ij}|$. The [Gershgorin disc theorem](gershgorin-circle-theorem.md) places every [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) in the union of these discs. For a Hermitian matrix the real intervals cut out by the discs bound energies; an isolated disc contains exactly one [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md).
