# Full-row-rank convergence of multiplicative resource prices

↑ **Parent:** [Relative-entropy Lyapunov function for resource prices](relative-entropy-lyapunov-function-for-resource-prices.md)

For [multiplicative resource-price dynamics](multiplicative-resource-price-dynamics.md) with positive capacities, nonempty routes and strictly positive initial prices, full row rank of the [link-route incidence matrix](link-route-incidence-matrix.md) makes

$$
V(\mu)=\sum_rw_r\log(A^T\mu)_r-C^T\mu
$$

strictly concave. There is a unique optimizing price vector, possibly on the boundary, and the [relative-entropy Lyapunov function for resource prices](relative-entropy-lyapunov-function-for-resource-prices.md) proves convergence to it. Row-rank deficiency still gives unique optimal route prices and rates, but resource prices may depend on the initial state. Full column rank alone and unrestricted zero initial prices do not yield the same uniqueness theorem.
