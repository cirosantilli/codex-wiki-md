# Semiclassical quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

[Semiclassical quantization](semiclassical-quantization.md) uses classical trajectories and actions to approximate quantum states in a regime where relevant actions are large compared with $\hbar$. Phase consistency on a periodic orbit leads to [Bohr-Sommerfeld quantization](bohr-sommerfeld-quantization.md). Soliton [collective coordinates](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md) can be quantized this way, while fluctuations supply additional corrections to the leading classical [mass](mass.md).
