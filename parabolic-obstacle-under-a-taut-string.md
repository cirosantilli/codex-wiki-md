# Parabolic obstacle under a taut string

↑ **Parent:** [Obstacle problem](obstacle-problem.md)

For a string of positive tension $T$ with fixed endpoints $w(\pm1)=0$, zero transverse loading and obstacle $f=h-x^2$, $0\leq h\leq1$, equilibrium minimizes $T\int_{-1}^1(w')^2/2\,dx$ among functions above $f$. On a free interval $w''=0$, while in contact $w=f$. Smooth tangency of the right line through $(1,0)$ to the parabola at $x=s$ requires $h-s^2=2s(1-s)$, yielding the displayed root in $[0,1]$.

The two pieces join with equal value and slope. On the free portions $w-f=(|x|-s)^2\geq0$; in contact $-Tw''=2T$ is the nonnegative reaction. These facts verify the [complementarity](unilateral-contact-complementarity.md) conditions. The associated [variational inequality](variational-inequality.md) and the exact energy difference prove global minimality; strict convexity with fixed endpoints proves uniqueness. At $h=0$ contact reduces to one point; at $h=1$ it fills the whole interval.
