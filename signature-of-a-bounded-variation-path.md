# Signature of a bounded variation path

↑ **Parent:** [Rough path](rough-path.md)

The [path signature](signature-of-a-bounded-variation-path.md) collects the ordered iterated [Riemann-Stieltjes integrals](riemann-stieltjes-integral.md) of a continuous path of [bounded variation](total-variation-of-a-function.md). Its truncation belongs to the [free step-N nilpotent Lie group](free-step-n-nilpotent-lie-group.md). It multiplies under concatenation by the [Chen identity](chen-identity.md), is independent of orientation-preserving reparametrisation of the whole path, and records more than its endpoint displacement.
