# Vector addition

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)

Vector addition is the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) operation combining two [vectors](vector.md) into their sum. It is commutative and associative, has the [zero vector](zero-vector.md) as identity, and distributes with [scalar multiplication](scalar-multiplication.md) as specified by the [vector space axioms](vector-space-axioms.md). Coordinatewise addition describes it after choosing a [basis](basis.md).
