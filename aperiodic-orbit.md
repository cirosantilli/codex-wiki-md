# Aperiodic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete dynamical system](discrete-dynamical-system-split.md)

An [aperiodic orbit](aperiodic-orbit.md) does not repeat with a finite period. An orbit that is not eventually periodic never enters a finite cycle after any number of transients. Aperiodicity alone is insufficient for [chaos](chaos.md): an irrational circle rotation is aperiodic but has no exponential separation of nearby states. Chaotic interval maps can have both uncountably many [aperiodic orbits](aperiodic-orbit.md) and [dense periodic points](dense-periodic-points.md).
