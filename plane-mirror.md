# Plane mirror

↑ **Parent:** [Mirror](mirror.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_mirror)

A [plane mirror](plane-mirror.md) has a flat reflecting [surface](topological-surface-split.md). The [law of reflection](law-of-reflection.md) sends incident unit direction $\mathbf d$ to $\mathbf d-2(\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf n)\mathbf n$, where $\mathbf n$ is its [unit normal](unit-normal.md). It forms a [virtual image](virtual-image.md) of an object the same perpendicular distance behind the reflecting plane as the object is in front. Two successive [plane mirrors](plane-mirror.md) whose planes meet at [angle](angle.md) $\gamma$ rotate the ray geometry through $2\gamma$ about their intersection.
