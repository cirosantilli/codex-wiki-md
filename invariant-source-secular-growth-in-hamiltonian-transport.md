# Invariant-source secular growth in Hamiltonian transport

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian Liouville equation](hamiltonian-liouville-equation.md)

If $f_0=0$ and a nonzero time-independent source is invariant along the [Hamiltonian flow](hamiltonian-flow.md), then the [Duhamel formula for Hamiltonian transport](duhamel-formula-for-hamiltonian-transport.md) gives $f_t=t h$. For the nonzero-frequency oscillator, $h=e^{-H_\omega}$ is smooth and belongs to all finite [Lp spaces](lp-space.md), yet its resulting solution has infinite space-time norm on $(0,\infty)$.
