<h1 id="total-stiefel-whitney-class-of-the-projectivization-of-copies-of-the-tautological-line">Total Stiefel–Whitney class of the projectivization of copies of the tautological line</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md)

Under

$$
\mathbb P(k\gamma_{\mathbb R}^{1,n+1})
\cong\mathbb{RP}^n\times\mathbb{RP}^{k-1},
$$

let $x$ and $v$ be the degree-one generators. Then

$$
w(T\mathbb P(k\gamma_{\mathbb R}^{1,n+1}))
=(1+x)^{n+1}(1+v)^k.
$$
