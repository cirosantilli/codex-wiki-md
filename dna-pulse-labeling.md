# DNA pulse labeling

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle analysis](cell-cycle-analysis.md)

[DNA pulse labeling](dna-pulse-labeling.md) exposes [biological cells](biological-cell.md) to a detectable precursor for a bounded interval. After removal, later sampling can follow the labeled material and descendants. A short pulse asks about synthesis during that interval; overnight labeling instead accumulates evidence across many hours. Detectable synthesis does not by itself prove completion of a [cell cycle](cell-cycle.md), and [DNA repair](dna-repair.md) can also incorporate precursors.
