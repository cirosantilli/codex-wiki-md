# Observational study

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical association](statistical-association.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observational_study)

An observational study measures exposures and outcomes without assigning the exposure by an intervention. Its causal interpretation therefore requires assumptions that address [confounding](confounding.md) and [selection bias](selection-bias.md).
