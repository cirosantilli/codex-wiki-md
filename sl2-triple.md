# sl2 triple

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md)

An sl2 triple consists of elements of a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) satisfying the displayed brackets. It defines a homomorphism from $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ by sending its standard generators to $h,e,f$. A nonzero homomorphism is injective because $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ is a [simple Lie algebra](simple-lie-algebra.md). Root vectors and their coroot give such triples in a [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), and their representations supply finite [weight strings](weight-string.md).
