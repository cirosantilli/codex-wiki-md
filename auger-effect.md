# Auger effect

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auger_effect)

A transition filling an inner atomic vacancy can transfer its released [energy](energy-split.md) to another bound [electron](electron.md), ejecting it instead of emitting a [photon](photon.md). This provides an atomic relaxation channel following [electron capture](electron-capture.md) and ionization.
