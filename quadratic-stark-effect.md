# Quadratic Stark effect

↑ **Parent:** [Stark effect](stark-effect.md)

The quadratic [Stark effect](stark-effect.md) is an [energy level](energy-level.md) shift whose leading term is quadratic in an applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md). For a nondegenerate [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) of definite parity, the diagonal [matrix element](matrix-element.md) of an odd electric-dipole perturbation vanishes. [Second-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](second-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) gives

$$
\delta E_n^{(2)}=\sum_{k\ne n}\frac{|\langle k|V|n\rangle|^2}{E_n^{(0)}-E_k^{(0)}}.
$$

Every term is negative for the ground state. If field reversal is implemented by the [parity operator](parity-operator.md), an analytic nondegenerate energy branch is even in the field. This symmetry explains the absence of odd powers; it does not apply without qualification to a degeneracy split linearly by the field.
