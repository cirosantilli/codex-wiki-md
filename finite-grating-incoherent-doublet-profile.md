# Finite-grating incoherent doublet profile

↑ **Parent:** [Resolving power of a diffraction grating](resolving-power-of-a-diffraction-grating.md)

For narrow slits and equally bright mutually incoherent spectral lines, $u_j=\pi p\sin\theta/\lambda_j$ and the [intensities](intensity-physics.md) add as displayed. The [Rayleigh criterion](rayleigh-criterion.md) uses a main-peak-to-first-zero separation $\delta\lambda\simeq\lambda/(|m|N)$. A slightly sub-Rayleigh doublet can still have two local maxima: the criterion is not a theorem of statistical non-identifiability. [photon](photon.md) counts, background, unequal strengths and uncertainty in the instrumental profile govern actual inference.
