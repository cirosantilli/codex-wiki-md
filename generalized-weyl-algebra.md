# Generalized Weyl algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative algebra](noncommutative-algebra-split.md)

For a ring $D$, an automorphism $\sigma$ and central $t$, adjoin $X,Y$ with $Xd=\sigma(d)X$, $Yd=\sigma^{-1}(d)Y$, $YX=t$ and $XY=\sigma(t)$. This construction includes [Weyl algebras](weyl-algebra.md) and rank-one [universal enveloping algebras](universal-enveloping-algebra.md).
