# Spreading coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Wetting](wetting.md)

The excess interfacial [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) released per newly wetted area when a liquid film replaces a solid-vapour interface is the displayed coefficient. Positive $S$ favors macroscopic spreading in the ideal equilibrium surface-energy model, whereas negative $S$ permits a finite [contact angle](contact-angle.md) obeying [Young's equation](young-s-equation.md). Surface preparation, chemical composition and adsorption can change all three interfacial energies.
