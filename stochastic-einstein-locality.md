# Stochastic Einstein locality

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of physics](philosophy-of-physics.md)

A formulation of [stochastic Einstein locality](stochastic-einstein-locality.md) restricts an event's objective chance to its relevant causal past, rather than spacelike-separated interventions or facts. Different versions constrain either marginal chances or joint conditional probabilities. A one-wing marginal condition alone does not supply [outcome independence](outcome-independence.md). To derive a [Bell theorem](bell-theorem.md) by this route, the proposed locality assumptions must include the appropriate screening-off relation for the two outcomes, together with a common setting-independent past distribution.
