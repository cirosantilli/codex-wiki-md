# Isobaric analog state

↑ **Parent:** [Isospin](isospin.md)

Nuclear [quantum states](quantum-state.md) in different [nuclear isobars](isobar-nuclide.md) belonging to the same [isospin multiplet](isospin-multiplet.md), with equal total [isospin](isospin.md) and approximately the same spatial and spin structure. An [isospin](isospin.md) raising or lowering operator connects the states in the limit of exact isospin symmetry. [Nuclear Coulomb energy](nuclear-coulomb-energy.md) and other isospin-breaking effects shift their [energies](energy-split.md) and modify this ideal relationship.
