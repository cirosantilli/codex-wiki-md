# Low-spin complex

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

A [low-spin complex](low-spin-complex.md) has the smaller-spin occupancy favored when ligand-field splitting outweighs the cost of pairing [electrons](electron.md) in the lower orbitals. An octahedral low-spin $d^6$ center has $t_{2g}^6e_g^0$ and total spin zero.
