# Reflexive closure

↑ **Parent:** [Reflexive relation](reflexive-relation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflexive_closure)

The reflexive closure of a [binary relation](binary-relation.md) $R\subseteq A\times A$ is $R\cup\Delta_A$, where $\Delta_A=\{(a,a):a\in A\}$. Every [reflexive relation](reflexive-relation.md) containing $R$ must contain the diagonal, so this is the least such extension.
