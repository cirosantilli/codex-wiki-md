# Projectivization by quotients

↑ **Parent:** [Projective bundle](projective-bundle.md)

This convention for a [projective bundle](projective-bundle.md) parametrizes one-dimensional quotients of the fibres of a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E$. Its universal quotient is $\pi^*E\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal O_{\mathbb P(E)}(1)$, and $\pi_*\mathcal O(1)=E$. Tensoring $E$ by a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) on the base leaves the projective bundle unchanged but tensors its universal quotient by the pullback of that line bundle. The alternative convention of [holomorphic projectivization by lines](holomorphic-projectivization-by-lines.md) replaces $E$ by its dual and reverses the tautological sign.
