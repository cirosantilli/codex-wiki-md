# Unramified halving fields for a split cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Halving cocycle with rational two-torsion](halving-cocycle-with-rational-two-torsion.md)

Let $E:y^2=(x-\alpha)(x-\beta)(x-\gamma)$ with distinct [algebraic integers](algebraic-integer.md) in a [number field](number-field.md) $K$. For a rational point $P$, its halving field is unramified outside the finite set $S$ of primes dividing $2(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha-\gamma)(\beta-\gamma)$. At $v\notin S$, the [elliptic-curve discriminant](elliptic-curve-discriminant.md) is a unit, so there is [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). A half of the reduction of $P$ is defined over a finite extension of the [residue field](residue-field.md). Pass to the corresponding [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of the completion and lift this half using the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). The doubling error lies in the [formal kernel of a minimal Weierstrass equation](formal-kernel-of-a-minimal-weierstrass-equation.md). Since two is a unit, [prime-to-residue-characteristic multiplication on a formal group](prime-to-residue-characteristic-multiplication-on-a-formal-group.md) corrects this error uniquely. Every half differs by rational [2-torsion](2-torsion.md), so all halves are unramified at $v$. The [halving cocycle with rational two-torsion](halving-cocycle-with-rational-two-torsion.md) has image of order at most four, making the halving field a [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of degree at most four. The [bounded-degree extensions with restricted ramification](bounded-degree-extensions-with-restricted-ramification.md) are finite in number; there are only finitely many homomorphisms from their finite [Galois groups](galois-group.md) to $E[2]$. Thus $E(K)/2E(K)$ is finite, without assuming any form of the [Mordell-Weil theorem](mordell-weil-group.md).
