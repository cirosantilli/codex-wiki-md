# Kinetic helicity conservation law

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic helicity density](kinetic-helicity-density.md)

For smooth inviscid [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md) motion without gravity or magnetic forces, let $w$ be the [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) with $\nabla w=\rho^{-1}\nabla p$, and set $q=\mathbf u\cdot\boldsymbol\omega$. Then

$$
\partial_tq+\nabla\cdot\left[q\mathbf u+\left(w-\frac{u^2}{2}\right)\boldsymbol\omega\right]=0.
$$

This follows by dotting the [barotropic vorticity transport](barotropic-vorticity-transport.md) equation with $\mathbf u$ and using the [divergence of a cross product](divergence-of-a-cross-product.md). A volume integral is constant when the resulting boundary flux vanishes.
