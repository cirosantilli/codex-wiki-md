# Primary state

↑ **Parent:** [Primary field](primary-field.md)

A [primary state](primary-state.md) is a simultaneous eigenvector of $L_0,\widetilde L_0$ with eigenvalues $h,\widetilde h$ that is annihilated by all positive modes of both [Virasoro algebras](virasoro-algebra.md). Negative modes generate descendants. The [state–operator correspondence](state-operator-correspondence.md) identifies it with a [primary operator](primary-field.md) inserted at the origin.
