# Totally unordered locale

↑ **Parent:** [Locale](locale.md)

A [locale](locale.md) is totally unordered when $f^*\leq g^*$ for two maps into it implies $f=g$, for every common domain. This tests generalized points rather than just ordinary points. Every [Hausdorff locale](hausdorff-locale.md) has this property: comparable maps annihilate the complement of the diagonal and hence factor through the diagonal.
