# Gaussian integer norm

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian integer](gaussian-integer.md)

The product of a [Gaussian integer](gaussian-integer.md) with its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md). It is multiplicative: $N(zw)=N(z)N(w)$, which gives the [Brahmagupta-Fibonacci identity](brahmagupta-fibonacci-identity.md).
