# Stunted real projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md)

Collapse the indicated standard skeleton to a point. Besides its basepoint, the quotient has one cell in dimensions $k+1$ through $m$. Its integral cellular boundary is two in even dimensions and zero in odd dimensions. Steenrod operations on its relative [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) are inherited from real projective space.
