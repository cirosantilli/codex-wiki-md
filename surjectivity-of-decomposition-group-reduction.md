# Surjectivity of decomposition group reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Decomposition group](decomposition-group.md)

For a finite [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of [number fields](number-field.md), choose a nonzero [residue field](residue-field.md) generator at $\mathfrak q$ and lift it to an integral element $a$ whose residues at the other primes above $\mathfrak p$ are zero, using the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md). The orbit polynomial $\prod_{\sigma\in G}(T-\sigma a)$ has coefficients in the base [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md). Modulo $\mathfrak q$, its nonzero roots are precisely the orbit of $\overline a$ under the [decomposition group](decomposition-group.md). Its coefficients belong to $k_{\mathfrak p}$, so [finite-field Frobenius automorphism](finite-field-frobenius-automorphism.md) permutes these roots. Consequently some decomposition-group element acts as Frobenius on the generator, and therefore on the entire [residue field](residue-field.md). Frobenius generates the finite-field [Galois group](galois-group.md), proving surjectivity.
