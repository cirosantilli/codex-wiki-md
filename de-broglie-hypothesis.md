# de Broglie hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [de Broglie wavelength](de-broglie-wavelength.md)

A material particle carries a quantum [phase](phase-waves.md) whose wave vector and [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) obey $\mathbf p=\hbar\mathbf k$ and $E=\hbar\omega$. [Interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) and [diffraction](diffraction.md) test this relation; a localized particle requires a [wave packet](wave-packet.md).
