# Phase centre

↑ **Parent:** [Radio interferometry](radio-interferometry.md)

The phase centre is the reference sky direction for removing geometric delay and its associated [wave phase](phase-waves.md). An unresolved source exactly there ideally has constant [complex visibility](complex-visibility.md) after calibration; a displaced source has a baseline-dependent [wave phase](phase-waves.md).
