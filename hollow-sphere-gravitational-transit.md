# Hollow-sphere gravitational transit

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md)

For a spherical shell of uniform [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, inner radius $a$ and outer radius $2a$, suppose a negligible-width radial tunnel permits a small [mass](mass.md) $m$ to fall from rest at the outer surface. The enclosed [mass](mass.md) at radius $r$ within the material is $4\pi\rho(r^3-a^3)/3$. The [spherical shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md) gives inward [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $g(r)=4\pi G\rho(r-a^3/r^2)/3$. Integrating from $a$ to $2a$ gives released [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) $4\pi G\rho ma^2/3$, hence speed $a\sqrt{8\pi G\rho/3}$ at the cavity. The cavity has zero [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md), so the diameter transit time is the title expression, independent of $a$ and $m$.
