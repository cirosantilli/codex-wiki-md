# Stagnation pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Static pressure](static-pressure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stagnation_pressure)

The [pressure](pressure.md) obtained by bringing a flow to rest without losses; in [compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md) the usual definition specifies an [isentropic process](isentropic-process.md) for stopping. For the constant-[mass density](density.md), same-height [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) discussed under [dynamic pressure](dynamic-pressure.md), $p_0=p+q$.
