# Angular dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Wave dispersion](wave-dispersion.md)

The change of an optical propagation angle with [wavelength](wavelength.md). A [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md) at normal incidence has $d\sin\theta=m\lambda$, so differentiating gives $d\theta/d\lambda=m/(d\cos\theta)$. This converts a small spectral-line separation into [angular separation](angular-separation.md), but resolving the two images also requires sufficient aperture and a narrow source image.
