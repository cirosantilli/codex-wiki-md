# Powers of the identity plus a square-zero matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent matrix](nilpotent-matrix.md)

The [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md) for the commuting [matrices](matrix.md) $I,N$ proves the formula for nonnegative integers: every term containing $N^2$ vanishes. Since $(I+N)(I-N)=I$, the [matrix inverse](matrix-inverse-split.md) is $I-N$; applying the same argument to its powers gives the negative integers as well. The same identity shows that $I+N$ is invertible even when the [nilpotent matrix](nilpotent-matrix.md) $N$ is nonzero. In dimension two, the example $N=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&p\\0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ describes repeated [shear mappings](shear-mapping.md), whose displacement parameters add under composition.
