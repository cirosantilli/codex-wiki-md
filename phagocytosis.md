# Phagocytosis

↑ **Parent:** [Endocytosis](endocytosis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phagocytosis)

[Phagocytosis](phagocytosis.md) encloses a large extracellular particle by extending and closing the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) around it. It is one possible conceptual route for internalizing a symbiotic partner, but a modern phagocytic pathway should not be assumed to have existed before eukaryotic organization evolved.
