# List-decoding Fano inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Fano's inequality](fano-s-inequality.md)

If a uniform index $J$ has $N$ possibilities and every proposed success neighbourhood contains at most $B<N$ possibilities, then any estimator has failure [probability](probability.md) at least the displayed expression. Condition on its success indicator: [conditional entropy](conditional-entropy.md) is at most $\log2+(1-p_e)\log B+p_e\log N$. Subtract this from $\log N$ to bound the [mutual information](mutual-information.md) and rearrange. A metric [Hamming ball](hamming-ball.md) gives approximate recovery rather than exact index decoding.
