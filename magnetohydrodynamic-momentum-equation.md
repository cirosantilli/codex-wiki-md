# Magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

For constant [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu$ and [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) $\mu_0$, the incompressible momentum equation is

$$
\partial_t\mathbf u+(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=-\rho^{-1}\nabla p+(\mu_0\rho)^{-1}(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\times\mathbf B+\nu\nabla^2\mathbf u.
$$

It is the [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md) with [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md). The magnetic force decomposes into [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) and a [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) gradient. A uniform streamwise pressure gradient need not make ordinary pressure independent of transverse coordinates.
