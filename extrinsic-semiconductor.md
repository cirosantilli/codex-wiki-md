# Extrinsic semiconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

An [extrinsic semiconductor](extrinsic-semiconductor.md) has mobile [electron](electron.md) and [electron hole](electron-hole.md) populations substantially controlled by electrically active impurities rather than solely by intrinsic thermal pair production. [Donor impurities](donor-impurity.md) favor electron conduction and [acceptor impurities](acceptor-impurity.md) favor hole conduction. Ionization, compensation and the temperature-dependent intrinsic population determine whether a given doped specimen is actually in its extrinsic transport regime.
