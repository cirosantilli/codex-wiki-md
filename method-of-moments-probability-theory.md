# Method of moments (probability theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Method_of_moments_(probability_theory))

If all moments of real [random variables](random-variable-split.md) converge to those of a moment-determinate limit law, then the variables converge to that law in the sense of [convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md). [Moment determinacy](moment-determinacy.md) is essential; the [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) meets this condition.
