# Neumann eigenfunctions of the Chebyshev differential operator

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev differential equation](chebyshev-differential-equation.md)

With the [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md) $\int_{-1}^1\overline f g\,dx/\sqrt{1-x^2}$, the [Chebyshev differential equation](chebyshev-differential-equation.md) has the [Sturm-Liouville form](sturm-liouville-form.md) with coefficients $p=\sqrt{1-x^2}$, $w=(1-x^2)^{-1/2}$ and $q=0$. The substitution $x=\cos\theta$ converts its operator to $-d^2/d\theta^2$ on $(0,\pi)$ and its inner product to the ordinary one in $d\theta$. The natural endpoint conditions $py'\to0$ become zero derivative conditions at $\theta=0,\pi$.

Consequently the [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) are the [Chebyshev polynomials](chebyshev-polynomial.md) $T_n(x)=\cos(n\arccos x)$ with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $n^2$, $n=0,1,2,\ldots$. Their squared norms are $\pi$ for $n=0$ and $\pi/2$ otherwise. In particular $T_1=x$ and $T_3=4x^3-3x$ are orthogonal. The endpoints of the original differential expression are singular; specifying these transformed [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) defines its self-adjoint realization. The [Dirichlet eigenfunctions of the Chebyshev differential operator](dirichlet-eigenfunctions-of-the-chebyshev-differential-operator.md) use a different endpoint condition and have sine rather than cosine profiles.
