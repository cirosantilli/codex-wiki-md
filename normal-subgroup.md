# Normal subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Group theory](group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_subgroup)

A subgroup $N\le G$ is normal when $gNg^{-1}=N$ for every $g\in G$.
