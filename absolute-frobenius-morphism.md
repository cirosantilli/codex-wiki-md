# Absolute Frobenius morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme of characteristic p](scheme-of-characteristic-p.md)

The absolute Frobenius morphism of a [scheme of characteristic p](scheme-of-characteristic-p.md) is the identity on the underlying topological space and raises every local function to its $p$th power. On an affine chart $\operatorname{Spec}A$ it is induced contravariantly by the [Frobenius endomorphism](frobenius-endomorphism.md) $A\to A$, $a\mapsto a^p$. It need not be an [isomorphism of schemes](isomorphism-of-schemes.md), even when it acts invertibly on global sections.

The algebraic [Frobenius endomorphism](frobenius-endomorphism.md) induces this scheme map contravariantly on affine charts.
