# Antiperiplanar migration in the Beckmann rearrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Beckmann rearrangement](beckmann-rearrangement.md)

A [carbon](carbon.md) [substituent](substituent.md) opposite the activated N–O leaving group migrates onto [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) as the N–O bond cleaves. The [oxime](oxime.md) geometry therefore determines which bond is replaced by a [carbon](carbon.md)–[nitrogen](nitrogen.md) bond.
