# Thermal anchoring

↑ **Parent:** [Cryogenics](cryogenics.md)

A thermal connection to a controlled cold stage reduces the [temperature](temperature-split.md) difference caused by heat leaking through measurement leads or supports. [Johnson-Nyquist noise](thermal-electrical-noise.md) still remains at the maintained [temperature](temperature-split.md); reducing drift and reducing the noise amplitude are separate aims.
