# Thermal timescale of an accretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal stability of an accretion disk](thermal-stability-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

The thermal timescale compares the internal energy stored in a disc annulus with its local heating or cooling. In a gas-pressure-supported [alpha disk](alpha-disk.md), $t_{\rm th}\sim(\alpha\Omega_K)^{-1}$, whereas the [viscous timescale](viscous-timescale.md) is longer by approximately $(R/H)^2$. A local thermal perturbation is therefore studied at fixed [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md), rather than fixed steady-state [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md).
