# Associated Legendre differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre differential equation](legendre-differential-equation.md)

This second-order linear differential equation extends the [Legendre differential equation](legendre-differential-equation.md) by its order parameter $m$. Its solutions are [associated Legendre functions](associated-legendre-function.md). In a degree-one potential problem, with $x=\tanh\eta$, two explicit solutions are $e^{m\eta}(m-\tanh\eta)$ and $e^{-m\eta}(m+\tanh\eta)$. Their [Wronskian](wronskian.md) with respect to $\eta$ is $2m(1-m^2)$, so the two formulas are not a fundamental pair at $m=0$ or $m=\pm1$.
