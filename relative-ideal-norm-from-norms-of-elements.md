# Relative ideal norm from norms of elements

↑ **Parent:** [Norm of a fractional ideal](norm-of-a-fractional-ideal.md)

For a nonzero [fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md) of a [number field](number-field.md), the displayed generated ideal equals the relative [norm of a fractional ideal](norm-of-a-fractional-ideal.md). Locally, multiplication by $x$ on the integral lattice has determinant $N_{L/K}(x)$. A [Smith normal form](smith-normal-form.md) calculation and the residue filtrations give $v_{\mathfrak p}(N(x))=\sum_{\mathfrak P\mid\mathfrak p}f_{\mathfrak P/\mathfrak p}v_{\mathfrak P}(x)$. The [Chinese remainder theorem for ideals](chinese-remainder-theorem-for-ideals.md) chooses $x\in I$ simultaneously attaining the minimum valuation of $I$ at each prime above a fixed $\mathfrak p$. Thus the generated ideal has valuation $\sum f_{\mathfrak P/\mathfrak p}v_{\mathfrak P}(I)$. Adding these valuations proves multiplicativity, including [fractional ideals](fractional-ideal.md) after clearing denominators.
