# Extremal infinite-volume Ising measures

↑ **Parent:** [Ising boundary condition](ising-boundary-condition.md)

For the ferromagnetic binary [Ising model](ising-model.md), all-zero boundary measures increase and all-one boundary measures decrease as boxes exhaust the [lattice](lattice.md), in [stochastic domination](stochastic-domination-of-probability-measures.md). The nearest-neighbor spatial conditional [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) reduces each domain comparison to a mixture of boundary conditions for the smaller box. [Probabilities](probability.md) that a specified finite set is all one consequently converge; [inclusion-exclusion](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) gives convergence of every finite [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) pattern. Their consistent limits define the two extreme infinite-volume measures. Existence does not assert that the two limits coincide.
