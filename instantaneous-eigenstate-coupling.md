# Instantaneous eigenstate coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic theorem](adiabatic-theorem.md)

Expanding a [quantum state](quantum-state.md) in instantaneous [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) separates [dynamical phases](dynamical-phase.md) from the coupling $A_{mn}$. [orthonormality](orthonormal-set.md) makes $A$ anti-Hermitian. For distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), differentiating the [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) equation gives $A_{mn}=\langle m|\dot H|n\rangle/(E_n-E_m)$. Its off-diagonal entries drive nonadiabatic transitions; diagonal entries describe phase changes of the basis and generate the [Berry phase](berry-phase.md).
