# Epicyclic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md)

For [epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md) in a [Keplerian shearing sheet](keplerian-shearing-sheet.md), write $x-x_0=\operatorname{Re}(Ae^{-i\Omega t})$. The energy of the radial [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) per unit mass is $E_{\rm epi}=\Omega^2|A|^2/2$. The full horizontal [Jacobi energy in a shearing sheet](jacobi-energy-in-a-shearing-sheet.md) also includes the negative guiding-center term $-3\Omega^2x_0^2/8$.
