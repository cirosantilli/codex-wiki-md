# Nuclear separation energy

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md)

The [energy](energy-split.md) needed to remove specified [nucleons](nucleon.md) from a bound [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) in its [ground state](ground-state.md). It compares neighboring nuclear [masses](mass.md), and so can reveal a [nuclear shell closure](nuclear-shell-closure.md) more clearly than a smoothly varying total [nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md).
