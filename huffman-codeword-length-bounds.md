# Huffman codeword length bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Huffman coding](huffman-coding.md)

For a largest binary-symbol probability $p_1$, $p_1<1/3$ prevents a length-one word, while $p_1>2/5$ forces one in a Huffman tree.
