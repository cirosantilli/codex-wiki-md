# Wheel graph

↑ **Parent:** [Cycle graph](cycle-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheel_graph)

A wheel graph is the [join of graphs](join-graph-theory.md) of a [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md) and a single [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). Every wheel with a rim of at least three [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) has a $K_4$ [graph minor](graph-minor.md): partition the rim into three nonempty consecutive connected sets and use its centre as the fourth [branch set of a graph minor](branch-set-of-a-graph-minor.md).
