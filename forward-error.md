# Forward error

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

The [forward error](forward-error.md) is the difference between a computed result $\widehat{x}$ and the exact result $x_*$ for the original data. Its size can be measured by a [norm](norm.md), or relative to $\|x_*\|$ when $x_*\ne0$. A [linear-system residual](linear-system-residual.md) measures the equation imbalance instead; the [condition number](condition-number.md) relates that imbalance to the [forward error](forward-error.md).
