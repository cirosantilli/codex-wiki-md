# Relative homology of a simplex and its boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Relative homology](relative-homology.md)

For $n\geq1$,

$$
H_k(\Delta^n,\partial\Delta^n;\mathbb Z)
\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&k=n,\\
0,&k\ne n.
\end{cases}
$$

In the [relative simplicial chain complex](relative-simplicial-chain-complex.md), every proper face vanishes, leaving one generator in degree $n$ and zero chain groups in all other degrees.
