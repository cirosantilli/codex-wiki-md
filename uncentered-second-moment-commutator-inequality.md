# Uncentered second-moment commutator inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Robertson uncertainty principle](robertson-uncertainty-principle.md)

For [self-adjoint operators](self-adjoint-operator.md) $A,B$ and a normalized [quantum state](quantum-state.md) in their relevant product [operator domains](operator-domain.md), put $u=A\psi$, $v=B\psi$, $z=\langle u,v\rangle$. With the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) linear in its second entry, $\langle C\rangle=2\operatorname{Im}z$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives

$$
\langle A^2\rangle\langle B^2\rangle=\|u\|^2\|v\|^2\geq|z|^2\geq(\operatorname{Im}z)^2=\frac14\langle C\rangle^2.
$$

Equality holds exactly when $u,v$ are [linearly dependent](linear-dependence.md) and $\operatorname{Re}z=0$. This includes either zero vector; if both vectors are nonzero it is equivalent to $B\psi=i\lambda A\psi$ for real nonzero $\lambda$. Applying this inequality to the centred [observables](observable.md) $A-\langle A\rangle I$ and $B-\langle B\rangle I$ gives the [Robertson uncertainty principle](robertson-uncertainty-principle.md).
