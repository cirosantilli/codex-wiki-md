# Resonant heating

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

Transfer of fluctuation [energy](energy-split.md) to particles selected by a wave-particle [resonance](resonance.md). For a weakly damped [Langmuir wave](langmuir-wave.md), particles near its [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) absorb net [energy](energy-split.md) from a stable distribution by [Landau damping](landau-damping.md). Under weak random forcing, a smaller damping rate produces a larger stationary wave intensity; a very long waiting time may be required before that stationary balance holds.
