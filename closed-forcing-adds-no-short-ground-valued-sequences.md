# Closed forcing adds no short ground-valued sequences

↑ **Parent:** [Closed forcing](closed-forcing.md)

If a [forcing](forcing-split.md) is $\kappa$-closed in the ground model, then it adds no [functions](function-split.md) from a ground [ordinal](ordinal.md) $\alpha<\kappa$ into a ground [set](set-split.md) $B$. Recursively decide each value inside the ground model and take common stronger bounds at limit stages and after the final step. Conditions deciding a whole ground [function](function-split.md) are dense below any condition asserting this type of [function](function-split.md). The [dense-below generic meeting lemma](dense-below-generic-meeting-lemma.md) ensures the [generic filter](generic-filter.md) meets that [dense subset of a forcing order](dense-subset-of-a-forcing-order.md). One arbitrary bound need not be in the [filter in an ordered set](filter-mathematics.md).
