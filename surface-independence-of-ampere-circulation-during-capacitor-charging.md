<h1 id="surface-independence-of-ampere-circulation-during-capacitor-charging">Surface independence of Ampère circulation during capacitor charging</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement current](displacement-current.md)

A spanning surface that cuts a charging wire carries free current $I$. A surface passing through the [capacitor](capacitor.md) gap instead carries displacement flux $\int\partial_t\mathbf D\cdot d\mathbf A=\dot Q=I$. Their common boundary has the same magnetic circulation only when both terms are included in [Ampère's law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md).
