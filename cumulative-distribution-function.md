# Cumulative distribution function

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumulative_distribution_function)

The cumulative distribution function of a real [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ is

$$
F_X(x)=\mathbb P(X\leq x).
$$

It is nondecreasing, right-continuous, tends to zero at $-\infty$, and tends to one at $+\infty$.
