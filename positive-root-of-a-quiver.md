# Positive root of a quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Tits form of a quiver](tits-form-of-a-quiver.md)

In the positive definite case this is a nonnegative integer vector with $q_Q(\mathbf n)=1$, so it is nonzero. The [positive definite Tits form indecomposable classification](positive-definite-tits-form-indecomposable-classification.md) gives one indecomposable isomorphism class per positive root.
