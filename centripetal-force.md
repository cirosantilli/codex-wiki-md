# Centripetal force

↑ **Parent:** [Centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md)

A [centripetal force](centripetal-force.md) is the inward resultant [force](force-split.md) needed for circular motion. [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) and [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md) give magnitude $mv^2/r$ for a particle of [mass](mass.md) $m$, speed $v$, and path radius $r$. It is a role played by an actual interaction, such as [tension](tension.md) or an [electric force](electric-force.md), rather than an additional interaction to add to a [free-body diagram](free-body-diagram.md). A purely radial [centripetal force](centripetal-force.md) is perpendicular to the [velocity](velocity.md), so it does no [work](work.md) and leaves the speed unchanged.
