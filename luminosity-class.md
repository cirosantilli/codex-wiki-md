# Luminosity class

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral classification](spectral-classification.md)

A stellar spectral category based on gravity-sensitive line widths and ratios, distinguishing supergiants, giants and dwarfs at comparable spectral temperature. Examples are I for supergiants, III for giants and V for main-sequence dwarfs. It is inferred spectroscopically and is not a direct substitute for measuring absolute [luminosity](luminosity.md) in every individual star.
