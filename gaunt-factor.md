# Gaunt factor

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaunt_factor)

The [Gaunt factor](gaunt-factor.md) is the dimensionless quantum correction to a classical free-free radiation calculation. Its weak temperature and frequency dependence modifies the approximate [thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) scaling; a frequency-averaged value appears in the bolometric cooling coefficient.
