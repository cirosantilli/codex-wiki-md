<h1 id="kepler-harmonic-radial-duality">Kepler–harmonic radial duality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Radial orbit time transformation](radial-orbit-time-transformation.md)

Taking $\bar E=GM\,r/q^2>0$ in a [radial orbit time transformation](radial-orbit-time-transformation.md) gives $r=(\bar E/GM)q^2$ and $\bar\Phi(q)=-E(\bar E/GM)^2q^2$. Bound [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md) with $E<0$ map to a confining [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md); positive [energy](energy.md) maps to an inverted oscillator. Full angular reconstruction halves the original orbital angle.
