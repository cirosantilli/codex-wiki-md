[Executive functions](../../../executive-function.md) should contribute most when [learning](../../../learning.md) or assessment requires keeping several pieces of information active, suppressing an attractive error, coordinating successive operations or changing strategy. This predicts particularly strong links with multistep [mathematics](../../../mathematics-split.md), mathematical problem solving and [reading comprehension](../../../reading-comprehension.md), alongside classroom engagement and following instructions. It predicts a smaller _direct_ contribution to well-practised fact retrieval or fluent decoding once those operations are automated. That is a task-demand [hypothesis](../../../hypothesis.md), not a claim that every [mathematics](../../../mathematics-split.md) score must correlate more strongly with [executive functions](../../../executive-function.md) than every literacy score.

[Working memory](../../../working-memory.md) maintains goals and intermediate results: a [child](../../../child.md) doing a [subtraction](../../../subtraction.md) with borrowing must retain both the original problem and its updated intermediate state. [Inhibitory control](../../../inhibitory-control.md) helps suppress a larger-number answer that is tempting but irrelevant, or a familiar operation when the problem requires another. [Cognitive flexibility](../../../cognitive-flexibility.md) supports switching between representations or abandoning an unsuccessful strategy. In [reading](../../../reading.md), maintaining earlier propositions and rejecting an initially plausible interpretation should matter especially for comprehension and inference; knowledge of letter–sound correspondences is not itself an [executive function](../../../executive-function.md). Repeated practice can change which process is the bottleneck, so developmental stage and familiarity matter.

Historically, the unity-and-diversity framework helped replace an undifferentiated “executive ability” with correlated but distinguishable processes. [Akira Miyake](../../../akira-miyake.md) and colleagues used several tasks per proposed component and [latent variable](../../../latent-variable-split.md) analysis to separate common executive [variance](../../../variance-split.md) from individual task demands. Their influential study tested adults: its factor structure cannot simply be assumed to describe preschool [children](../../../child.md). It nevertheless supplies a crucial methodological lesson. A single executive task measures the intended control process together with [perception](../../../perception.md), [language](../../../language.md), motor speed and task-specific strategies. Treating a raw score as a pure capacity risks attributing the wrong source of [variance](../../../variance-split.md) to school achievement.[Cognitive Psychology 41, 49–100; latent-variable analyses](../../../the-unity-and-diversity-of-executive-functions-and-their-contributions-to-complex-frontal-lobe-tasks-by-akira-miyake-and-others.md)

More directly, [Clancy Blair](../../../clancy-blair.md) and [Rachel Peters Razza](../../../rachel-peters-razza.md) followed 141 [children](../../../child.md) from low-income homes, measuring preschool [self-regulation](../../../self-regulation.md) and later kindergarten [mathematics](../../../mathematics-split.md) and literacy. Their [inhibition](../../../inhibitory-control.md) measure required [children](../../../child.md) to tap once when the experimenter tapped twice and twice after one tap: the apparatus is simple, but the rule requires suppressing [imitation](../../../imitation.md) and maintaining an opposite response mapping. [Inhibitory control](../../../inhibitory-control.md) was associated with emerging [mathematics](../../../mathematics-split.md) and [reading](../../../reading.md) after accounting for [intelligence](../../../intelligence.md). This supports the importance of regulation during early [learning](../../../learning.md), but the design was observational, not random assignment to different inhibitory capacities. Shared [language](../../../language.md), family circumstances or classroom experience could influence both performances.[Child Development 78, 647–663; Measures and academic-outcome regressions](../../../relating-effortful-control-executive-function-and-false-belief-understanding-to-emerging-math-and-literacy-ability-in-kindergarten-by-clancy-blair-and-rachel-peters-razza.md)

[Lucy Cragg](../../../lucy-cragg.md) and colleagues provided a more differentiated mathematical analysis. Their sample of 293 participants aged eight to twenty-five completed executive tasks and measures of factual knowledge, procedures and conceptual understanding. [Working memory](../../../working-memory.md) had both an association with overall [mathematics](../../../mathematics-split.md) and indirect associations through mathematical component skills; [inhibition](../../../inhibitory-control.md) measured in a numerical context was linked to factual and procedural knowledge. This is more informative than treating “[mathematics](../../../mathematics-split.md)” as one outcome. However, [statistical mediation](../../../statistical-mediation.md) in an age-spanning observational sample does not establish that changing the putative mediator would change attainment. Numerically framed executive tasks can also share content with the achievement measures, increasing the apparent specificity of the association.[Cognition 162, 12–26; abstract, executive-function battery and mediation models](../../../direct-and-indirect-influences-of-executive-functions-on-mathematics-achievement-by-lucy-cragg-and-others.md)

[Kate Cain](../../../kate-cain.md) and colleagues supply a corresponding literacy result. In assessments at ages eight, nine and eleven, [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) and comprehension components such as inference and monitoring predicted [reading comprehension](../../../reading-comprehension.md) after controls for word [reading](../../../reading.md) and verbal ability. Sentence-span and digit-working-memory measures helped avoid defining the entire construct by one task. This supports a contribution beyond decoding, but the reported concurrent relations at successive ages are not an experimental demonstration of causal direction; comprehension itself can affect performance on verbally loaded [memory](../../../memory-in-animals.md) tasks.[Journal of Educational Psychology 96, 31–42; longitudinal assessments at eight, nine and eleven years, concurrent prediction models](../../../children-s-reading-comprehension-ability-concurrent-prediction-by-working-memory-verbal-ability-and-component-skills-by-kate-cain-and-others.md)

Evidence across populations qualifies the temptation to make [executive functions](../../../executive-function.md) the master explanation of achievement. [Greg J. Duncan](../../../greg-j-duncan.md) and colleagues compared six longitudinal datasets while controlling earlier [child](../../../child.md) skills and family background. School-entry [mathematics](../../../mathematics-split.md) and [reading](../../../reading.md) were strong predictors of subsequent attainment; [attention](../../../attention.md) added prediction, whereas several socioemotional measures did not. [Attention](../../../attention.md) ratings are not interchangeable with laboratory [inhibition](../../../inhibitory-control.md) or [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) measures. The result supports a classroom-regulation pathway while showing that already acquired domain knowledge remains central. [Learning](../../../learning.md) also exercises executive control, so baseline-adjusted longitudinal associations do not eliminate reciprocal influence or unmeasured [confounding](../../../confounding.md).[Developmental Psychology 43, 1428–1446; abstract and cross-dataset models; corrected version](../../../school-readiness-and-later-achievement-by-greg-j-duncan-and-others.md)

Intervention offers a sharper causal test, but its target and outcome must be distinguished. [Darren L. Dunning](../../../darren-l-dunning.md) and colleagues randomly allocated seven- to nine-year-olds with low [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) to adaptive computerised training, low-load nonadaptive training or no training. The adaptive condition improved several untrained [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) tests, with some gains maintained over time, but did not produce corresponding academic or classroom-analogue benefits. The active comparison helps separate memory-load effects from [attention](../../../attention.md), computer practice and expectations. Crucially, improvement on an untrained but structurally similar [memory](../../../memory-in-animals.md) test is [near transfer](../../../near-transfer.md); improved [reading](../../../reading.md) or [mathematics](../../../mathematics-split.md) would be [far transfer](../../../far-transfer.md). One does not entail the other.[Developmental Science 16, 915–925; three-arm randomised trial, Results and Discussion](../../../does-working-memory-training-lead-to-generalized-improvements-in-children-with-low-working-memory-by-darren-l-dunning-and-others.md)

This trial does not show that [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) is irrelevant to achievement. A resource can constrain performance even when practising isolated tasks fails to change how it is deployed in a classroom. Nor does one intervention test all executive components, age groups or teaching methods. It does undermine the stronger inference from [correlation](../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) to a promise that generic executive training will raise school attainment. Supporting instruction through shorter directions, external reminders and staged problems can reduce executive demands; whether any particular classroom support improves [learning](../../../learning.md) still requires an appropriately controlled outcome study.

The most defensible answer is therefore conditional and specific. Executive differences are especially plausible influences on managing novel, attentionally demanding, multistep [learning](../../../learning.md) and demonstrating that [learning](../../../learning.md) under load. They operate alongside knowledge, [language](../../../language.md), motivation and opportunity. Strong evidence would combine reliable multi-task executive measures, differentiated academic outcomes, repeated measurements with comparable meaning across ages, and interventions with [active controls](../../../active-control.md) and delayed achievement tests. Existing prospective and experimental evidence supports [executive functions](../../../executive-function.md) as contributors to [learning](../../../learning.md), while limiting claims that they are a single cause of educational success.
