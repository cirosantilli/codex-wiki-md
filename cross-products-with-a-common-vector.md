# Cross products with a common vector

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md)

The [cross product](cross-product.md) of two [cross products](cross-product.md) sharing their first vector is parallel to that vector, with scalar factor equal to the [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md). In [suffix notation](einstein-notation.md), use $\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{kpq}=\delta_{ip}\delta_{jq}-\delta_{iq}\delta_{jp}$. The component of the double [cross product](cross-product.md) becomes $A_i\epsilon_{q\ell m}C_qA_\ell B_m-C_i\epsilon_{p\ell m}A_pA_\ell B_m$. The second term vanishes by antisymmetry of the [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md); the first equals $A_i(A\cdot(B\times C))$, proving the identity even when the vectors are dependent.
