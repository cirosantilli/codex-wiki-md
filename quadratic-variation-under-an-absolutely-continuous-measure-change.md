# Quadratic variation under an absolutely continuous measure change

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic covariation under an absolutely continuous measure change](quadratic-covariation-under-an-absolutely-continuous-measure-change.md)

If $Q\ll P$ and a [continuous semimartingale](continuous-semimartingale.md) is a [semimartingale](semimartingale.md) under both measures, its quadratic variations agree $Q$-indistinguishably. The same squared-increment sums converge uniformly on compacts in probability under both measures: absolute continuity transfers the original convergence, and uniqueness of the limit identifies the two continuous versions. Equivalence of measures is unnecessary.
