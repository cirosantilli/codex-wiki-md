# Parameter-shift remainder bound for a logarithmic Laplace integral

↑ **Parent:** [Watson's lemma](watson-s-lemma.md)

For $g(s)=-(1/a)e^{ias}\int_0^\infty x^{s+\sigma-1}e^{-tx}/(ia+\log x)\,dx$ with positive $a,t,\sigma$ and $s\ge0$, differentiation cancels the logarithmic denominator and gives $g_s=-(1/a)e^{ias}\Gamma(s+\sigma)t^{-s-\sigma}$. Integrating this [derivative](derivative.md) expresses $g(0)$ as a finite parameter-Laplace [integral](integral.md) plus $g(s)$. The elementary lower bound $|ia+\log x|\ge a$ gives the displayed remainder, smaller than every logarithmic term at fixed $s>0$. [Watson's lemma](watson-s-lemma.md) then determines all coefficients from the finite [integral](integral.md)'s endpoint Taylor series.
