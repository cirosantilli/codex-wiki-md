# System of distinct representatives

↑ **Parent:** [Hall's marriage theorem](hall-s-marriage-theorem.md)

A [system of distinct representatives](system-of-distinct-representatives.md) for a finite indexed family of finite [sets](set-split.md) $(A_i)_{i\in I}$ chooses one element $x_i\in A_i$ per index, with all choices distinct. Form a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md) with left [vertex set](vertex-set.md) $I$, right [vertex set](vertex-set.md) $\bigcup_{i\in I}A_i$, and [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) $ix$ whenever $x\in A_i$. [Matching saturation](matching-saturation.md) of $I$ is precisely a [system of distinct representatives](system-of-distinct-representatives.md). Hence [Hall's marriage theorem](hall-s-marriage-theorem.md) says that such choices exist exactly when $|\bigcup_{i\in J}A_i|\geq|J|$ for every [subset](subset.md) $J\subseteq I$. Repeated equal [sets](set-split.md) still have separate indices and separate demands.
