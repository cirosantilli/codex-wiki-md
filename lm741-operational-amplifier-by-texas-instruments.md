# LM741 Operational Amplifier by Texas Instruments

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Texas Instruments](work-by-texas-instruments.md)

Manufacturer's data sheet, revision D, October 2015.

[https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm741.pdf](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm741.pdf)

Section 6.5, p. 5, gives typical [input impedance](input-impedance.md) $2\,\mathrm{M\Omega}$ at $25^\circ\mathrm C$ and typical large-signal [voltage gain](voltage-gain.md) $200\,\mathrm{V/mV}$. Section 7.4.2 describes closed-loop feedback. These are device-specific examples for interpreting a real [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md).
