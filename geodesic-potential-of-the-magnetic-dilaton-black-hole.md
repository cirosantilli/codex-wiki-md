# Geodesic potential of the magnetic dilaton black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetically charged dilaton black hole](magnetically-charged-dilaton-black-hole.md)

Rotate any [geodesic](geodesic.md) into an equatorial plane of the [magnetically charged dilaton black hole](magnetically-charged-dilaton-black-hole.md). Conserved energy and angular momentum are $E=V\dot t$ and $L=R^2\dot\phi$. Normalizing its tangent to $g(\dot x,\dot x)=-\epsilon$, with $\epsilon=1,0,-1$ for timelike, null and spacelike geodesics, gives $\dot r^2=E^2-V(\epsilon+L^2/R^2)$. Thus its radial equation is $\dot r^2/2+\widetilde V=0$. Radial null rays have $\dot r=\pm E$ and use the same [Schwarzschild tortoise coordinate](schwarzschild-tortoise-coordinate.md) with $2M$ replaced by $r_+$.
