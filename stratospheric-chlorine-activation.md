# Stratospheric chlorine activation

↑ **Parent:** [Polar stratospheric cloud](polar-stratospheric-cloud.md)

Conversion of relatively unreactive [chlorine](chlorine.md) reservoirs into forms that produce active [radicals](radical-chemistry.md) in sunlight. Heterogeneous reactions on appropriate particle surfaces can turn [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md) and [chlorine nitrate](chlorine-nitrate.md) into photolabile species. Available surface composition, [temperature](temperature-split.md), reservoir supply and subsequent illumination all matter; adding arbitrary particles does not guarantee activation.
