# Right circular conical surface

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)

This [surface](topological-surface.md) consists of rays from a vertex $\mathbf a$ whose directions make a fixed angle $\beta$ with a unit axis [vector](vector.md) $\widehat{\mathbf n}$. The displayed equation follows from the [dot product](dot-product.md) formula for that angle. For $0<\beta<\pi$, $\beta\ne\pi/2$, it gives one circular nappe; $\beta=\pi/2$ gives a [plane](plane.md), and $\beta=0,\pi$ gives a degenerate ray. Squaring the equation produces both nappes, so the unsquared sign distinguishes them. [Generalized angular momentum in a radial monopole field](generalized-angular-momentum-in-a-radial-monopole-field.md) confines particle directions to such a surface.
