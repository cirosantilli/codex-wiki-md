# Frictional heating

↑ **Parent:** [Friction](friction.md)

In the ideal [kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md) model, the loss of combined [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) of the two contacting bodies becomes thermal [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) at rate

$$
\dot E_{\mathrm{thermal}}=-f_k(v-U)=\mu_kN|v-U|\ge0.
$$

This follows by adding the contact [powers](power.md) $f_kv$ and $-f_kU$ from [Newton's third law](newton-s-third-law.md). It depends on [contact slip](contact-slip.md), whereas the contact [power](power.md) received by either body separately depends on its [velocity](velocity.md) in the chosen [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md). Thus positive [frictional heating](frictional-heating.md) is compatible with increasing [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) of one body, as the moving-belt example in [kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md) shows. Heating need not cause a [temperature](temperature-split.md) rise if thermal [energy](energy-split.md) escapes by [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md).
