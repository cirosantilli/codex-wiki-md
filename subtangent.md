# Subtangent

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent](tangent.md)

For a point $B$ of a [curve](curve.md), let its ordinate meet the chosen coordinate axis at $C$, and its [tangent](tangent.md) meet that axis at $E$. The segment $CE$ is the [subtangent](subtangent.md). It depends on the chosen axis. For the standard [parabola](parabola.md), $CE=2CD$, where $D$ is its [vertex of a parabola](vertex-of-a-parabola.md).
