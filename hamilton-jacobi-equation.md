# Hamilton-Jacobi equation

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian mechanics](hamiltonian-mechanics.md)

A principal function $S(q,t)$ obeys the [Hamilton-Jacobi equation](hamilton-jacobi-equation.md) $\partial_tS+H(q,\partial_qS,t)=0$. A complete solution generates [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) components $p_j=\partial_jS$ and local canonical coordinates constant along motion. For a time-independent [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), $S=W(q)-Et$ reduces the equation to $H(q,\partial_qW)=E$. Additive separation of $W$ yields conserved separation constants, subject to local regularity and independence.
