# Inverse-gamma distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse-gamma_distribution)

An inverse-gamma random variable is the reciprocal of a [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) random variable up to scale. In shape-scale parameters its density is proportional to $x^{-a-1}e^{-b/x}$ on $x>0$.
