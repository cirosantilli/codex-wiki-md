# Irreducible augmentation criterion for a transitive group action

↑ **Parent:** [Augmentation subrepresentation of a permutation representation](augmentation-subrepresentation-of-a-permutation-representation.md)

For a transitive finite $G$-set $X$ with at least two points, the [augmentation subrepresentation of a permutation representation](augmentation-subrepresentation-of-a-permutation-representation.md) is [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md) if and only if the action is [two-transitive](two-transitive-group-action.md). Indeed, writing the permutation character as $\pi=1+\sum_i m_i\chi_i$ gives

$$
\langle\pi,\pi\rangle_G=1+\sum_i m_i^2.
$$

The augmentation subrepresentation is irreducible exactly when this norm is two. By the [character norm of a permutation representation](character-norm-of-a-permutation-representation.md), that means that the diagonal and the off-diagonal are the only two orbits on $X\times X$, which is exactly two-transitivity.
