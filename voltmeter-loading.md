# Voltmeter loading

↑ **Parent:** [Voltmeter](voltmeter.md)

[Voltmeter loading](voltmeter-loading.md) is the change in an [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) caused by the [electric current](electric-current.md) drawn by the measuring [voltmeter](voltmeter.md).

For a [loaded voltage divider](loaded-voltage-divider.md) in steady [direct current](direct-current.md), a [voltmeter](voltmeter.md) connected across the lower [resistor](resistor.md) places its finite input [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R_m$ in parallel with the lower arm's [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R_2$ and any intended [electrical load](electrical-load.md) of [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R_L$. With positive, fixed [electrical resistances](electrical-resistance.md), an upper [resistor](resistor.md) of [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R_1$, and an ideal [voltage source](voltage-source.md) supplying [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V_{\mathrm{in}}$, [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) and [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) give

$$
\frac{V_{\mathrm{in}}-V_m}{R_1}=\frac{V_m}{R_2}+\frac{V_m}{R_L}+\frac{V_m}{R_m},\qquad
R_p=\left(\frac1{R_2}+\frac1{R_L}+\frac1{R_m}\right)^{-1},\qquad
V_m=V_{\mathrm{in}}\frac{R_p}{R_1+R_p}.
$$

Here $V_m$ is the connected circuit's [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) across the [voltmeter](voltmeter.md); omit $1/R_L$ if there is no intended [electrical load](electrical-load.md). The parallel connection and need for large input [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) are standard [voltmeter](voltmeter.md) operating principles.[§10.4, Measuring Voltage with a Voltmeter: parallel connection and large input resistance](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/10-4-electrical-measuring-instruments.md)

[Voltmeter loading](voltmeter-loading.md) is a bias relative to the target [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) with the [voltmeter](voltmeter.md) absent and any intended [electrical load](electrical-load.md) still present. [Voltage divider loading error](voltage-divider-loading-error.md) instead compares the attached-load output with the wholly unloaded output. An accurate readout of $V_m$, supported by [measurement calibration](measurement-calibration.md), can still differ from either target: calibration accuracy concerns the indication of the connected [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md), whereas [voltmeter loading](voltmeter-loading.md) changes that [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) itself.
