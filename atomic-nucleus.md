# Atomic nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_nucleus)

The central system of [protons](proton.md) and [neutrons](neutron.md) in an atom. Its mass number $A$ is its [baryon number](baryon-number.md) in ordinary nuclear states; $Z$ is the proton number and $N$ the neutron number.
