# Graph excess

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

Here excess means edges minus vertices. For a [connected graph](connected-graph.md) it is one less than the number of independent cycles. Deleting a leaf and its incident edge, or suppressing a degree-two vertex, preserves excess. This convention differs by one from the cyclomatic number for connected graphs.
