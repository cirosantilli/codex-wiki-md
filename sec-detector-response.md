# SEC detector response

↑ **Parent:** [SEC chromatogram](sec-chromatogram.md)

A [SEC detector response](sec-detector-response.md) must be specified before interpreting a peak area. [Ultraviolet](ultraviolet.md) [absorbance](absorbance.md) depends on a species' response factor and [concentration](concentration.md), as described by the [Beer-Lambert law](beer-lambert-law.md); differential [refractive index](refractive-index.md) detection depends on how the dissolved species changes [refractive index](refractive-index.md). Equal amounts need not produce equal signal areas across unlike species.

Even a calibrated mass-proportional trace is not a chain-count [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md). In an original two-population example, equal recovered [masses](mass.md) of chains with [molar masses](molar-mass.md) $20{,}000$ and $80{,}000\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ contain four times as many chains in the lighter population. Thus equal signal areas, assuming equal response per unit [mass](mass.md), correspond to number fractions $4/5$ and $1/5$: their [number-average molar mass](number-average-molar-mass.md) is $32{,}000\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$, while their [weight-average molar mass](weight-average-molar-mass.md) is $50{,}000\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. Converting a continuous trace additionally requires response correction and the appropriate [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) between elution and [molar mass](molar-mass.md) bins.
