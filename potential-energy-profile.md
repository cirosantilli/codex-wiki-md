# Potential-energy profile

↑ **Parent:** [Energy profile (chemistry)](energy-profile-chemistry.md)

A [potential-energy profile](potential-energy-profile.md) plots the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) of the reacting system along a specified path from [reactants](reactant.md) through a [transition state](transition-state.md) to [reaction products](product-chemistry.md). It is a particular kind of [energy profile](energy-profile-chemistry.md). For an [elementary reaction](elementary-reaction.md), a suitable [reaction coordinate](reaction-coordinate.md) parametrizes the path; a multi-step [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md) needs the coordinates of its successive steps. See the [IUPAC Gold Book: potential-energy profile](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/P04779).
