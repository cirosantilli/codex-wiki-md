<h1 id="adjoint-representation-of-su-3">Adjoint representation of SU(3)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

The complex [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) is the action by conjugation on traceless $3\times3$ matrices. With $H_1=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)$ and $H_2=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,-1)$, its [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md) is $E_{13}$ of [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) $(1,1)$. The six off-diagonal matrix units have weights $(2,-1),(-2,1),(-1,2),(1,-2),(1,1),(-1,-1)$, and the two-dimensional [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) has weight zero. In coordinates $(h_1,(h_1+2h_2)/\sqrt3)$, the [weight diagram](weight-diagram.md) is a regular hexagon together with a central point of [weight multiplicity](weight-multiplicity.md) two. This representation describes both the [gluon](gluon.md) colour multiplet and the approximate flavour [baryon octet](baryon-octet.md), under different physical [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) symmetries.
