# Self-dual Yang-Mills equations in temporal gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Self-dual Yang-Mills equations](self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md)

With orientation $dx^1\wedge dx^2\wedge dx^3\wedge dt$, $\epsilon_{123}=1$, and [temporal gauge](temporal-gauge.md) $A_t=0$, the mixed [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) is $F_{ti}=\partial_tA_i$. The [self-dual Yang-Mills equations](self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md) then have the displayed form. Placing $dt$ first in the orientation reverses the sign, so the metric alone does not specify it.
