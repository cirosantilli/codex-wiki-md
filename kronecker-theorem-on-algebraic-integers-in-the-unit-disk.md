# Kronecker theorem on algebraic integers in the unit disk

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)

A nonzero algebraic integer whose conjugates all have modulus at most one is a [root of unity](root-of-unity.md). Its norm forces every conjugate to have modulus one, and bounded integral coefficient vectors show that two positive powers coincide.
