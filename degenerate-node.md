# Degenerate node

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability of a planar equilibrium](linear-stability-of-a-planar-equilibrium.md)

A [node equilibrium](node-dynamical-systems.md) with a repeated nonzero real [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). If the planar [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has a one-dimensional [eigenspace](eigenspace.md), its linearized solutions contain a term $t e^{\lambda t}$ and the node is also called improper. A negative repeated [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) still gives [asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md); a repeated value does not by itself indicate a [bifurcation](bifurcation.md).
