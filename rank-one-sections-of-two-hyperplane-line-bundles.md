# Rank-one sections of two hyperplane line bundles

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic section](holomorphic-section.md)

A section of $\mathcal O(1)\oplus\mathcal O(1)$ on the [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md) is a pair of homogeneous linear forms $\ell_1=aZ_0+bZ_1$, $\ell_2=cZ_0+dZ_1$. It vanishes at exactly one projective point precisely when its coefficient matrix has rank one, equivalently $ad-bc=0$ and the matrix is nonzero. Its kernel is then one line in $\mathbb C^2$. Equivalently, $s=v\ell$ for a constant nonzero vector $v$ and one nonzero linear form $\ell$. Rank two gives no zero, while rank zero gives the identically zero section.
