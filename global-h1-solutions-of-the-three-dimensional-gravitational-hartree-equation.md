# Global H1 solutions of the three-dimensional gravitational Hartree equation

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational Hartree equation](gravitational-hartree-equation.md)

In three dimensions, the [Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev inequality](hardy-littlewood-sobolev-inequality.md) and [Gagliardo-Nirenberg interpolation inequality](gagliardo-nirenberg-interpolation-inequality.md) give

$$
\int|\nabla\phi|^2
\lesssim\|u\|_{12/5}^4
\lesssim\|u\|_2^3\|\nabla u\|_2.
$$

Conserved mass and energy therefore bound the $H^1$ norm by [Young inequality](young-s-inequality-for-products.md), and the blowup alternative gives global existence.
