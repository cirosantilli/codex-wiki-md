# Goal interaction in planning

↑ **Parent:** [Classical planning](classical-planning.md)

Two subgoals interact when actions needed for one can establish, obstruct or delete facts needed for the other. A [plan](plan.md) for each subgoal separately need not combine into a [plan](plan.md) for their [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md). Preparing the world before committing to a subgoal, revising an earlier subplan or temporarily undoing an achieved fact can all be necessary. [Goal-by-goal planning](goal-by-goal-planning.md) may therefore be incomplete.
