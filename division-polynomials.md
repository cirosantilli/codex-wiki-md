# Division polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion point of an elliptic curve](torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_polynomials)

The division polynomial $\psi_n$ has roots at the nonzero $n$-torsion points of an elliptic curve, with the two points sharing an $x$-coordinate represented by the same root when $n$ is odd.
