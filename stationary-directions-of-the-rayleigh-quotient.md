# Stationary directions of the Rayleigh quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md)

For a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $A$ and nonzero real [vector](vector.md) $x$, the [quotient rule](quotient-rule.md) and the [quadratic form gradient](quadratic-form-gradient.md) give the displayed [gradient](gradient.md). It vanishes exactly when $Ax=R_A(x)x$, so the [stationary points](stationary-point.md) are [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md). Equivalently, the [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) condition for optimizing $x^TAx$ on the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md) is $Ax=\lambda x$. The smallest and largest [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) give the global extrema; other [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) can be stationary without being extrema, as a direction mixing larger and smaller [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) changes the quotient in opposite ways.
