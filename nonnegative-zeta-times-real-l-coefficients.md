# Nonnegative zeta-times-real-L coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md)

The coefficients of $\zeta(s)L(s,\chi)$ are multiplicative. On $p^k$, their values are $k+1$ when $\chi(p)=1$, alternating one and zero when $\chi(p)=-1$, and one when $\chi(p)=0$. In particular $a_{m^2}\ge1$. Their logarithmic Euler coefficients $\Lambda(n)(1+\chi(n))$ are also nonnegative, supporting [uniqueness of a possible exceptional real Dirichlet zero](uniqueness-of-a-possible-exceptional-real-dirichlet-zero.md).
