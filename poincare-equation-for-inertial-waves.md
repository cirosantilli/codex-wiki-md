<h1 id="poincare-equation-for-inertial-waves">Poincaré equation for inertial waves</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial wave](inertial-wave.md)

Linear incompressible motion about rest in a frame rotating with $\boldsymbol\Omega=\Omega\mathbf k$ obeys $\mathbf u_t+2\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf u=-\nabla P/\rho$. If $\zeta=v_x-u_y$ is its vertical [vorticity](vorticity-split.md), taking [curl](curl.md) gives $\zeta_t=2\Omega w_z$, while eliminating [pressure](pressure-split.md) using continuity gives $\partial_t\nabla^2w+2\Omega\zeta_z=0$. Differentiating in time yields the displayed scalar equation. All three [velocity](velocity.md) components must be retained, even when the fields depend on only two spatial coordinates. The [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) generally generates a transverse [velocity](velocity.md) that is absent in a strictly two-component description.
