# Equatorial reachability of a detuned spin pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Driven two-level Rabi model](driven-two-level-rabi-model.md)

For an initial pole of the [Bloch sphere](bloch-sphere.md), one fixed-amplitude harmonic pulse detuned by $\Delta$ transfers at most $\Omega^2/(\Omega^2+\Delta^2)$ of the population. A pure equatorial state requires half transfer, so some pulse duration reaches the equator exactly when $|\Delta|\leq\Omega$. Adjusting drive [phase](phase-waves.md) then selects its transverse azimuth. Exact inversion to the opposite pole is unavailable for nonzero detuning with that single fixed pulse. This is a restriction on the specified controls, rather than on composite-pulse or frequency-swept state preparation.
