# Particle attraction in an elliptical shearing-sheet vortex

↑ **Parent:** [Kida vortex](kida-vortex.md)

The material-boundary and [vorticity](vorticity.md) conditions give $\alpha=S/[r(r-1)]$ and $\beta=Sr/(r-1)$. A pressureless particle subject to [linear drag](linear-drag.md), [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) and tidal gravity has the [drag-polynomial stability for all positive stopping rates](drag-polynomial-stability-for-all-positive-stopping-rates.md) with $K=2\Omega(2\Omega-S)$, $L=\Omega(\alpha+\beta-S)$ and $P=\alpha\beta$. In [Keplerian shear](keplerian-shear.md), $K/\Omega^2=1$, $L/\Omega^2=3(r+1)/[2r(r-1)]$ and $P/\Omega^2=9/[4(r-1)^2]$. The exact criterion is $r\geq3$, including the boundary $K=L>P$ at $r=3$. Attraction is local to the centre under the prescribed interior velocity field; a trajectory leaving the patch cannot be followed using that field alone.
