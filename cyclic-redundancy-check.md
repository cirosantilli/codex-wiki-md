# Cyclic redundancy check

↑ **Parent:** [Error detection and correction](error-detection-and-correction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_redundancy_check)

A [cyclic redundancy check](cyclic-redundancy-check.md) uses a [polynomial remainder](remainder-of-polynomial-division.md) over the [field with two elements](field-with-two-elements.md) as checking information. In the basic systematic convention, appending the remainder of $x^rM$ modulo the [CRC generator polynomial](crc-generator-polynomial.md) makes the transmitted [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) divisible by that generator.
