# Weak holomorphy implies norm holomorphy

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly holomorphic Banach-valued function](weakly-holomorphic-banach-valued-function.md)

For a [weakly holomorphic Banach-valued function](weakly-holomorphic-banach-valued-function.md), choose a closed disk contained in its domain. Each continuous functional is bounded on the image of that disk by scalar continuity. The [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md), applied to its evaluation functionals in the bidual, gives a common bound $M$ on the [norms](norm.md) of the image vectors. Cauchy's formula on the disk's boundary then bounds the scalar difference between difference quotients at increments $h,k$ by a constant times $M\|\varphi\||h-k|$, uniformly for sufficiently small increments. Explicitly the difference kernel is

$$
\frac{h-k}{(\zeta-z)(\zeta-z-h)(\zeta-z-k)}.
$$

The [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) dual [norm](norm.md) formula converts this into the same [norm](norm.md) bound for the vector difference quotients. They are Cauchy, so completeness gives a [norm](norm.md) derivative. This also explains why weak holomorphy suffices without a separately assumed [norm](norm.md)-continuity hypothesis.
