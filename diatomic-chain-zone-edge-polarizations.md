# Diatomic-chain zone-edge polarizations

↑ **Parent:** [Diatomic-chain dispersion relation with equal springs](diatomic-chain-dispersion-relation-with-equal-springs.md)

In an alternating-mass chain with nearest-neighbour spacing $a$ and cell length $2a$, the inter-sublattice Bloch coupling is proportional to $\cos qa$. At the first-zone edge $q=\pi/(2a)$ it vanishes. For $M>m$, the acoustic edge mode moves only the heavy sublattice and the optical edge mode only the light one. Their [frequency](frequency.md) difference is $\sqrt{2\alpha/m}-\sqrt{2\alpha/M}$. At long [wavelength](wavelength.md) the acoustic displacements are in phase, whereas the optical mode has zero centre-of-mass displacement.
