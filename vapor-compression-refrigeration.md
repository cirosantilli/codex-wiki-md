# Vapor-compression refrigeration

↑ **Parent:** [Refrigerator](refrigerator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vapor-compression_refrigeration)

Vapor-compression refrigeration circulates a working [fluid](fluid.md) through cold and hot coils. In the cold coil, [liquid](liquid.md) becomes [gas](gas.md) while absorbing [heat](heat-split.md) from the refrigerated region. Compression supplies [work](work.md) and raises the [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md); the hot coil releases [heat](heat-split.md) as the [gas](gas.md) returns to [liquid](liquid.md). A restriction reduces the [pressure](pressure-split.md) before the next cold-side passage. Changing the [pressure](pressure-split.md) changes the [temperature](temperature-split.md) of the [phase transition](phase-transition.md), letting one circulating [fluid](fluid.md) receive and release [heat](heat-split.md) at different [temperatures](temperature-split.md). This physical implementation serves both [refrigerators](refrigerator.md) and [heat pumps](heat-pump.md); its ordinary dissipative operation differs from the reversible [Carnot cycle](carnot-cycle.md) used to bound performance.
