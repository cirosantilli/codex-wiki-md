# Within-study bias

↑ **Parent:** [Meta-analysis](meta-analysis.md)

Systematic displacement of an individual study estimate caused by design, conduct, analysis or selective reporting. Combining estimates can reduce random error without reducing this bias. [Bias-adjusted meta-analysis](bias-adjusted-meta-analysis.md) and analyses restricted or stratified by relevant bias domains can assess its impact.
