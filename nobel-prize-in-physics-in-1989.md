# Nobel Prize in Physics in 1989

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

[Norman Foster Ramsey, Jr.](norman-foster-ramsey-jr.md) received one half for inventing the separated oscillatory fields method and applying it to the [hydrogen maser](hydrogen-maser.md) and other [atomic clocks](atomic-clock.md). [Hans G. Dehmelt](hans-georg-dehmelt.md) and [Wolfgang Paul](wolfgang-paul.md) received one quarter each for developing [ion trap](ion-trap.md) techniques.[Individual official 1989 summary: Ramsey one half, Dehmelt and Paul one quarter each; separated fields and clocks versus ion trapping.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1989-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
