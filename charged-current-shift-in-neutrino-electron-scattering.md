# Charged-current shift in neutrino-electron scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Massless neutrino-electron contact cross-section](massless-neutrino-electron-contact-cross-section.md)

Electron-neutrino scattering has a charged-current contribution absent for muon neutrinos. [Fierz rearrangement](fierz-identity.md) combined with the external [fermionic sign](fermionic-sign.md) adds a left-electron current: $c_V\to c_V+1$, $c_A\to c_A+1$. The right-electron coupling stays unchanged; the left-electron coupling increases by two.
