# Characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module

↑ **Parent:** [Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module](bernstein-growth-dimension-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md)

A good [Bernstein filtration](bernstein-filtration.md) on a finitely generated module produces a finite graded module over $\operatorname{gr}A_n\cong\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n,\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n]$. Its support is the characteristic variety for this filtration. Its algebraic dimension is the [Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module](bernstein-growth-dimension-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md), which is independent of the choice of good filtration.
