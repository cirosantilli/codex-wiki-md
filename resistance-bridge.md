# Resistance bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical measurement](electrical-measurement.md)

A resistance bridge compares [electrical resistances](electrical-resistance.md) by making two paths through an [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) produce the same intermediate [electric potential](electric-potential.md). The [Wheatstone bridge](wheatstone-bridge.md) turns this into a [null measurement](null-measurement.md): adjust one arm until a detector joining the two intermediate points reads zero, then infer the unknown from a ratio. This is useful because the balance relation does not require an accurately calibrated detector gain or supply [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md), provided the supply is nonzero and the [resistors](resistor.md) obey [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md).
