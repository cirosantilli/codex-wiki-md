# Biconditional introduction rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

Derive each side of a [logical biconditional](logical-biconditional.md) under a [temporary assumption](temporary-assumption.md) of the other, then discharge both assumptions. The surrounding assumptions $\Gamma$ remain. This is equivalent to deriving the two implications and combining them as the two directions of equivalence.
