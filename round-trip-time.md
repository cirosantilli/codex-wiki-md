# Round-trip time

↑ **Parent:** [Network latency](network-latency.md)

The [round-trip time](round-trip-time.md) measures the elapsed time for an exchange to travel to a peer and for a response to return. Feedback-based sending can be limited by this delay as well as by link capacity.
