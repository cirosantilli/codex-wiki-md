# Water hardness

↑ **Parent:** [Water quality](water-quality.md)

The capacity of [water](water.md) to consume [soap](soap.md), chiefly because of dissolved [calcium](calcium.md) and [magnesium](magnesium.md) ions. Quantitative results are commonly expressed as a [calcium carbonate](calcium-carbonate.md) equivalent; this does not mean all the hardness originally existed as dissolved [calcium carbonate](calcium-carbonate.md).
