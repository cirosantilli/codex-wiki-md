# Noncuspidal level-one Hecke eigenform

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md)

A simultaneous eigenfunction of all normalized level-one [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md) in positive even weight, with nonzero constant [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md), is a multiple of the normalized [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md). The constant coefficient forces its $T_n$ eigenvalue to be $\sigma_{k-1}(n)$, and $a_1(T_nf)=a_n(f)$ forces $a_n(f)=a_1(f)\sigma_{k-1}(n)$. Subtracting the matching constant multiple of the [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md) leaves a [cusp form](cusp-form.md). Unless every coefficient vanishes, its prime-index coefficients grow like $p^{k-1}$, contradicting the [Fourier coefficient bound for a cusp form](fourier-coefficient-bound-for-a-cusp-form.md) $O(p^{k/2})$ when $k\geq4$. Weight two has no nonzero [modular forms](modular-form.md), by [vanishing of weight-two level-one modular forms](vanishing-of-weight-two-level-one-modular-forms.md).
