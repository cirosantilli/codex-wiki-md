# Open slicing of de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md)

With no matter or radiation, $k<0$ and $H_\Lambda=\sqrt{\Lambda/3}>0$, the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) in units $c=1$ is $\dot a^2=H_\Lambda^2a^2-k$. Separating variables on the expanding branch and choosing its coordinate origin at $a=0$ gives the displayed [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md). Normalization $a(t_0)=1$ imposes $\sinh(H_\Lambda t_0)=H_\Lambda/\sqrt{-k}$ and $H_0^2=H_\Lambda^2-k$. The zero of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) is the boundary of this open coordinate patch, rather than a curvature singularity of [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md).
