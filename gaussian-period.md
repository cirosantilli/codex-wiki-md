# Gaussian period

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

A Gaussian period is a sum of [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) indexed by a multiplicative [coset](coset.md) in the group of nonzero residues modulo a [prime number](prime-number-split.md). Such sums describe [subfields](subfield.md) of a [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md) and occur as values of [induced characters](induced-character.md) of certain [semidirect products](semidirect-product.md).
