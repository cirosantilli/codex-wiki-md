# Normality criterion for a two-mode shear model

↑ **Parent:** [Normal matrix](normal-matrix.md)

For $A=\begin{pmatrix}-i\omega_1&a\\c&-i(\omega_1-b)\end{pmatrix}$ with real parameters,

$$
AA^\dagger-A^\dagger A=\begin{pmatrix}a^2-c^2&-ib(a+c)\\ib(a+c)&c^2-a^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus the displayed conditions are necessary and sufficient for a [normal matrix](normal-matrix.md). In the family $a=1$, $b=c=q$, normality requires $q=-1$, not $q=1$. If $q=10/Re$, this is the formal value $Re=-10$ and no positive [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) qualifies. At that value $A$ is a [skew-Hermitian matrix](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) and its exponential is unitary, so every initial direction has [optimal energy amplification of a linear system](optimal-energy-amplification-of-a-linear-system.md) equal to one.
