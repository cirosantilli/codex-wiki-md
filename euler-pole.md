# Euler pole

↑ **Parent:** [Plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md)

The direction of the instantaneous angular-velocity [vector](vector.md) describing relative rigid-plate motion on a [sphere](sphere.md). At position $\mathbf r$, the relative [velocity](velocity.md) is $\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r$. The antipodal pole represents the same physical [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) if the angular-velocity sign is reversed.
