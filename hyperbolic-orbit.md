# Hyperbolic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

An unbound [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) under attractive [inverse-square law](inverse-square-law.md) [gravity](gravity.md) is a [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) with the attracting center at a [focus of a conic](focus-of-a-conic.md). Writing $\mu=GM$ and $h$ for [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) per unit [mass](mass.md), its [eccentricity](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) satisfies $e^2=1+2\varepsilon h^2/\mu^2$, where $\varepsilon>0$ is the [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md). A nonzero incoming [speed](speed.md) $v$ at infinity gives $\varepsilon=v^2/2$.
