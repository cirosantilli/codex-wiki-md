# Linearly graded junction depletion width

↑ **Parent:** [Linearly graded p-n junction](linearly-graded-p-n-junction.md)

Integrating the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) across a [linearly graded p-n junction](linearly-graded-p-n-junction.md) gives $V_b-V=e\alpha w^3/(12\varepsilon_s)$, where positive $V$ means [forward bias](forward-bias.md). Thus the [depletion region](depletion-region.md) width depends on the cube root of the remaining barrier voltage. Both the space-charge density and integration range grow with width; this differs from the square-root width law of an abrupt uniformly doped junction.
