# Cumulative incidence update with tied censoring

↑ **Parent:** [Aalen–Johansen estimator](aalen-johansen-estimator.md)

For [competing risks](competing-risks.md), a [cumulative incidence function](cumulative-incidence-function.md) jumps by the displayed [Aalen–Johansen estimator](aalen-johansen-estimator.md) increment. A person [right-censored](right-censoring.md) at exactly the event time remains in the immediately preceding [risk set](risk-set.md) under the usual events-before-censoring tie convention. Censoring changes the next risk set, not the current event numerator or survival jump.
