# Huang-Rhys factor

↑ **Parent:** [Displaced-oscillator Franck-Condon progression](displaced-oscillator-franck-condon-progression.md)

The dimensionless displacement strength for the equal-frequency single-vibration model in [displaced-oscillator Franck-Condon progression](displaced-oscillator-franck-condon-progression.md). It equals the mean number of excited vibrational quanta in its zero-temperature overlap distribution.
