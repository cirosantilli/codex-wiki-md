# Predicate symbol

↑ **Parent:** [First-order language](first-order-language.md)

A [predicate symbol](predicate-symbol.md) of arity $k$ is interpreted as a subset of the $k$th Cartesian power of a structure's domain. Applying it to $k$ [first-order terms](first-order-term.md) gives an [atomic formula](atomic-formula.md). In a singleton domain there is only one possible argument tuple at each arity, but a predicate may still be interpreted as either the empty relation or the full relation.
