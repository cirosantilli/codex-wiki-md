# Taylor series

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor theorem](taylor-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_series)

The Taylor series of a sufficiently regular [function](function-split.md) about $a$ is

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n.
$$
