# Six-dimensional cubic scalar field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Phi cubed theory](phi-cubed-theory.md)

A canonically normalized [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) in six dimensions has [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) two. The cubic coupling is classically a [marginal coupling](marginal-coupling.md), whereas a local quartic coupling has [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) minus two. This theory is perturbatively renormalizable by [power counting in quantum field theory](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md), but a real cubic Euclidean potential is unbounded below. Its expansion about a massive Gaussian theory is therefore formal perturbation theory, not a convergent positive functional integral at nonzero real $g$.
