# Euler characteristics of closed oriented four-manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md)

[Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) gives the displayed formula for connected closed oriented four-manifolds. Every integer occurs. A [connected sum](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) of $r$ copies of the [Complex projective plane](complex-projective-plane.md) and $s$ copies of $S^1\times S^3$ has Euler characteristic $2+r-2s$, because the summand characteristics are three and zero. Given any integer $h$, choose $s$ large enough that $r=h-2+2s\geq0$; when both counts are zero use $S^4$. If simple connectivity is additionally required, the possible values instead begin at two, since $b_1=0$; connected sums of projective planes realize every such value.
