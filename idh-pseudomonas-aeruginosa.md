# IDH (Pseudomonas aeruginosa)

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

In the [Pseudomonas aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) branchpoint discussed here, [IDH](idh-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) denotes the [isocitrate](isocitrate.md) dehydrogenase activity insensitive to [AceK](acek.md) [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) and regulated allosterically by metabolic effectors. The abbreviation is context-dependent across organisms.
