# Finite-interval Airy spectral determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-interval Airy global relation](finite-interval-airy-global-relation.md)

Here $\alpha=e^{2\pi i/3}$. The missing-trace matrix has determinant $-\sqrt3k\Delta(k)$. Its nonzero zeros are the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) in the parametrization $Av=ik^3v$ of $A=-d^3/dx^3$ with $v(0)=v(L)=v'(L)=0$. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $\operatorname{Re}\langle v,Av\rangle=-|v'(0)|^2/2$. No nonzero zero lies in $\operatorname{Re}(-ik^3)\leq0$: on its boundary the determinant equation reduces, after rotation, to an equality between a unit-modulus exponential and $\cosh u-i\sqrt3\sinh u$, whose modulus exceeds one unless $u=0$. The zero at the origin is removable in the original boundary problem. There are nevertheless infinitely many genuine negative [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $A$, obtained from $e^{-3s/2}=2\cos(\sqrt3s/2+\pi/3)$, $s>0$, with $k=-is/L$. Absence of contour poles in the [Fokas method](fokas-method.md) does not imply absence of [discrete spectrum](discrete-spectrum.md) of the operator.
