# Logarithmic derivative inside the zeta zero-free region

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-free region of the Riemann zeta function](zero-free-region-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md)

After shrinking the zero-free-region constant, one has

$$
\frac{\zeta'(\sigma+it)}{\zeta(\sigma+it)}\ll\log|t|
$$

in the half-width region $\sigma>1-c/(2\log|t|)$ for $|t|$ large. Compare the local partial-fraction expansion at $s$ with one at a point just to the right of one, where the [Euler product](euler-product.md) controls the logarithmic derivative.
