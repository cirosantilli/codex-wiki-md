# CRC generator polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic redundancy check](cyclic-redundancy-check.md)

The [CRC generator polynomial](crc-generator-polynomial.md) is the agreed divisor in a [cyclic redundancy check](cyclic-redundancy-check.md). Under the basic convention, undetected error [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) are precisely its nonzero multiples.
