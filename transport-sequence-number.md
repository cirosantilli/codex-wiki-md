# Transport sequence number

↑ **Parent:** [Transport protocol](transport-protocol.md)

A [transport sequence number](transport-sequence-number.md) identifies a data position within an exchange, supporting ordering, duplicate rejection and precise [acknowledgements](acknowledgement.md). [TCP](transmission-control-protocol.md) numbers [bytes](byte.md) rather than simply numbering [packets](network-packet.md).
