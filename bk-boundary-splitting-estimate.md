# BK boundary-splitting estimate

↑ **Parent:** [One-arm probability](one-arm-probability.md)

For [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on the [square lattice](square-lattice.md), put $g_n=\mathbb P_p(0\leftrightarrow\partial[-n,n]^2)$. Splitting an open [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md) at its first visit to the radius-$m$ boundary gives disjoint certificates for reaching a boundary [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) and then reaching radius $n$ about that [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). The [Van den Berg-Kesten inequality](van-den-berg-kesten-inequality.md) and the [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) give $g_{m+n}\leq8m g_mg_n$ for positive $m,n$. With $g_0=1$ and $|\partial\Lambda_0|=1$, the boundary-cardinality form holds for zero indices as well.
