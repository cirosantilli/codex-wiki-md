# Relative homology of a fibration over a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md)

For a [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md) over $S^r$, cover the base by a contractible punctured sphere and a contractible ball. [Excision](excision-theorem.md) relative to the preimage of the punctured sphere identifies the relative pair with the product of $F$ and $(D^r,S^{r-1})$ up to [homotopy](homotopy.md). The base pair has a single free [homology](homology-split.md) group in degree $r$, so the [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) gives the displayed shift without a torsion term. The relative [long exact sequence](long-exact-sequence.md) then gives $\cdots\to H_i(F)\to H_i(E)\to H_{i-r}(F)\to H_{i-1}(F)\to\cdots$. The connecting map retains the twisting of the original [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md).
