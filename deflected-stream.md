# Deflected stream

↑ **Parent:** [Displaced drainage](displaced-drainage.md)

A stream whose course changes direction where deformation displaces or obstructs its former route. Along a [strike-slip fault](strike-slip-fault.md), matching upstream and downstream channel segments can constrain [shear sense](shear-sense.md) and [fault offset](fault-offset.md). A bend alone is not a unique tectonic marker: [erosion](erosion.md), capture and pre-existing relief also alter drainage. A reliable offset estimate restores identified piercing features rather than equating all along-fault channel length to slip.
