# Turing threshold of the Brusselator

↑ **Parent:** [Brusselator](brusselator.md)

When $u$ and $v$ have diffusivities $D$ and $1$, respectively, and the homogeneous Brusselator equilibrium is stable, a [Turing instability](turing-instability.md) occurs precisely when

$$
\beta>(1+\alpha\sqrt D)^2.
$$

At onset,

$$
D_c=\frac{(\sqrt\beta-1)^2}{\alpha^2},
\qquad
k_*^2=\frac{\alpha^2}{\sqrt\beta-1}.
$$
