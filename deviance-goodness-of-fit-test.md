# Deviance goodness-of-fit test

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized linear model](generalized-linear-model.md)

For a suitably regular [generalized linear model](generalized-linear-model.md), a residual deviance $D$ can be compared approximately with a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) on $n-p$ degrees of freedom to assess fit relative to the saturated model. Sparse or very small expected cell counts can invalidate this reference distribution. A large [p-value](p-value.md) means no detected lack of fit, not proof of correctness; dependence, wrong mean structure or [overdispersion](overdispersion.md) still require diagnostics.
