# Potential barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

A potential barrier is a region where the potential energy exceeds that in the surrounding regions.

A [rectangular potential barrier](rectangular-potential-barrier.md) is the piecewise-constant special case used for exact transmission and reflection calculations.
