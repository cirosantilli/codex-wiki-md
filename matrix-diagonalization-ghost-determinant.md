# Matrix diagonalization ghost determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian matrix model](hermitian-matrix-model.md)

Imposing vanishing off-diagonal entries with auxiliary integrations and an off-diagonal [Grassmann Gaussian integral](grassmann-gaussian-integral.md) gives a determinant of the commutator map on the off-diagonal matrix units. Its eigenvalues are $\lambda_i-\lambda_j$, $i\ne j$. Up to a constant sign, their product is the squared [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md). It contributes $-2\sum_{i<j}\log|\lambda_i-\lambda_j|$ to the eigenvalue action, encoding repulsion between distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). Diagonal zero modes and constant normalization factors must be treated separately.
