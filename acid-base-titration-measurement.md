# Acid-base titration measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md)

The institutional experiment The Potentiometric Titration of an Acid Mixture, from Massachusetts Institute of Technology's 5.310 Laboratory Chemistry, determines [hydrochloric acid](hydrochloric-acid.md) and [phosphoric acid](phosphoric-acid.md) inventories. Dried [potassium hydrogen phthalate](potassium-hydrogen-phthalate.md) standardizes [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md). A [volumetric pipette](volumetric-pipette.md) selects an acid [aliquot](aliquot.md), diluted with [water](water.md) in a beaker. A [burette](burette.md) feeds a stirred sample beside a [glass electrode](glass-electrode.md); the apparatus isolates the sample from stirrer warming. The [burette](burette.md) is conditioned with [titrant](titrant.md) and its tip freed of bubbles. Stable [pH](ph.md) and added [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) are recorded, with smaller additions near the transition. Controls include replicate standardizations, a consistent pale-pink [phenolphthalein](phenolphthalein.md) endpoint, and a standard [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md) check between sample runs. The readout supports inventory estimates through known [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md); it does not directly count [molecules](molecule-split.md).[The Potentiometric Titration of an Acid Mixture, printed Titration-5–8, §IV.2 and Day \#2; Figure 3; standardization, aliquot, bubbles, stable readings, and buffer check](the-potentiometric-titration-of-an-acid-mixture-mit-5-310-laboratory-handout.md)

The same protocol requires drainage time for [burette](burette.md) readings and warns that an undelivered hanging drop spoils the association between [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) and [pH](ph.md). Its color-persistence wording differs between the main paragraph and footnote, so a reproducible [titration endpoint](titration-endpoint.md) needs an explicitly chosen criterion. Its acid mixture is not the single-[strong acid](strong-acid.md) ideal model above.[Printed Titration-6, main endpoint paragraph and footnote 6; Titration-8, hanging-drop warning](the-potentiometric-titration-of-an-acid-mixture-mit-5-310-laboratory-handout.md)

A distinct control appears in Process Measurement Procedures: Sulfuric Acid Emissions, §1.4.4.2: the [phenolphthalein](phenolphthalein.md) standardization is repeated with [water](water.md) replacing the acid, matching the sample [aliquot](aliquot.md) and indicator quantity. The [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) needed for the same pink endpoint is subtracted from the sample reading.[Process Measurement Procedures: Sulfuric Acid Emissions, printed pp. 26–27, §1.4.4.2, indicator-blank paragraph, NOTE, and procedure steps 1–3](process-measurement-procedures-sulfuric-acid-emissions-by-r-maddalone-and-n-garner.md)

The independent bookkeeping for such a matched blank is $n_A=c_B(V_{\mathrm{sample}}-V_{\mathrm{blank}})$, assuming one-to-one [neutralization](neutralization.md) and equal background consumption. A blank cannot correct an unmatched [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) or guarantee exact coincidence of endpoint and [equivalence point](equivalence-point.md). Subtracting [pH](ph.md) readings would not give this inventory correction, because [pH](ph.md) is a [logarithm](logarithm.md). Repeatability constrains scatter; an independently known check sample also tests systematic bias. [Carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) uptake, incomplete mixing, [electrode](electrode.md) drift, and uncertain [titrant](titrant.md) [concentration](concentration.md) limit the inference.
