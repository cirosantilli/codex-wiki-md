# Beheaded stream

↑ **Parent:** [Displaced drainage](displaced-drainage.md)

A downstream channel segment cut off from its former upstream catchment, for example by [fault slip](fault-slip.md) or stream capture. An abruptly terminating gully beside a [fault](geological-fault.md) is a candidate; a reconstructed upstream counterpart, stratigraphy or field evidence helps distinguish true beheading from an ordinary small drainage head.
