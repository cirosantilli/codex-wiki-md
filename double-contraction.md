# Double contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md)

A [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md) over two paired indices. For real second-order [tensors](tensor.md), it gives the displayed [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md). For a fourth-order [elasticity tensor](elasticity-tensor.md) and a second-order [infinitesimal strain tensor](infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md), $(C:\epsilon)_{ij}=\sum_{kl}C_{ijkl}\epsilon_{kl}$ remains a second-order [tensor](tensor.md); double contraction need not always give a scalar.
