# Volterra convolution algebra on a finite interval

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

Truncated [convolution](convolution.md) makes $L^1(0,1)$ a commutative nonunital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md). The [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) gives $\|f*g\|_1\leq\|f\|_1\|g\|_1$, and the [Fubini theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) gives associativity over the integration simplex. Its [unitization](unitization-of-an-algebra.md) is $\mathbb C\oplus L^1(0,1)$ with norm $|\alpha|+\|f\|_1$ and product $(\alpha,f)(\beta,g)=(\alpha\beta,\alpha g+\beta f+f*g)$. The constant function one has convolution powers $t^{n-1}/(n-1)!$, of norm $1/n!$. Thus it is a nonnilpotent [quasinilpotent element](quasinilpotent-element.md) in this unitization.
