# Minimax risk

↑ **Parent:** [Minimax estimator](minimax-estimator.md)

The [minimax risk](minimax-risk.md) is the infimum over estimators of the supremum of their [risk functions](risk-function.md) over a parameter class. An upper bound supplies an estimator; a lower bound applies to every estimator, often using the [Le Cam two-point lemma](le-cam-two-point-lemma.md).
