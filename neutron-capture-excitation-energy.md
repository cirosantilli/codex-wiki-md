# Neutron-capture excitation energy

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron capture](neutron-capture.md)

After neutron capture on $(A,Z)$, the compound nucleus is $(A+1,Z)$. With negligible incoming kinetic energy, conservation of [energy](energy-split.md) gives $E^*=M(A,Z)+m_n-M(A+1,Z)$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). This is the [neutron separation energy](neutron-separation-energy.md) of the compound nucleus. At finite incident energy, add the kinetic energy in the initial [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md); in a nonrelativistic heavy-target approximation it is $A/(A+1)$ times the laboratory neutron kinetic energy. [Nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md) can significantly change capture excitation between neighboring even- and odd-mass targets.
