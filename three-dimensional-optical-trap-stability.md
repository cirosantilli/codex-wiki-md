# Three-dimensional optical trap stability

↑ **Parent:** [Optical tweezers](optical-tweezers.md)

At an [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $\mathbf r_*$, the total [force](force-split.md) vanishes. A small transverse [displacement](displacement.md) must produce a transverse [restoring force](restoring-force.md); a small axial [displacement](displacement.md) must also restore the bead. Downstream of a tight focus, the backward [optical gradient force](optical-gradient-force.md) can oppose the forward [optical scattering force](optical-scattering-force.md). Equality at one point is insufficient: the total axial [force](force-split.md) must decrease as the bead moves downstream through that point. The [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) can therefore lie downstream of the intensity maximum.

For uncoupled overdamped coordinates, $\zeta_i\dot{\delta r_i}=-k_i\delta r_i$ with $\zeta_i>0$ decays only when each $k_i>0$. More generally, linearizing gives $\dot{\delta\mathbf r}=M J\delta\mathbf r$, where $M$ is the [hydrodynamic mobility matrix](hydrodynamic-mobility-matrix.md) and $J_{ij}=\partial F_i/\partial r_j$ at [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). All [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $MJ$ must have negative real parts for linear asymptotic stability. For a conservative [force](force-split.md) with symmetric positive-definite mobility, a positive-definite [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) of the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) supplies this condition. A general optical [force](force-split.md) need not possess a global [potential energy](potential-energy.md).

Increasing [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md) admits more strongly converging [light rays](light-ray.md) and a steeper axial variation of the field. But a larger nominal [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md) alone does not establish trapping: illumination of the objective pupil, [optical aberration](optical-aberration.md), particle properties and the actual in-liquid focus also matter. A [harmonic optical trap](harmonic-optical-trap.md) describes local confinement after this stability question has been answered; it does not predict capture from arbitrary positions or an infinite holding [force](force-split.md).
