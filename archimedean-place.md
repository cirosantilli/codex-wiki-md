# Archimedean place

↑ **Parent:** [Place of a number field](place-of-a-number-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedean_place)

A real embedding $K\hookrightarrow\mathbb R$ gives a real Archimedean place of local degree $1$. A conjugate pair of complex embeddings $K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ gives a complex Archimedean place of local degree $2$.
