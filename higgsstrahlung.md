# Higgsstrahlung

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md)

[Higgsstrahlung](higgsstrahlung.md) produces a [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) together with a massive [electroweak gauge boson](electroweak-gauge-boson.md). At an electron-positron collider the process $e^+e^-\to Z^*\to ZH$ uses the [Higgs boson coupling to Z bosons](higgs-boson-coupling-to-z-bosons.md). The associated [Z boson](z-boson.md) provides a recoil and identifiable decay products. This was the dominant [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) Higgs-production channel used at LEP2.
