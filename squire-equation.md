# Squire equation

↑ **Parent:** [Orr-Sommerfeld equation](orr-sommerfeld-equation.md)

The Squire equation governs wall-normal [vorticity](vorticity.md) $\eta=\partial_zu-\partial_xw$ in the same [normal mode](normal-mode.md) convention as the [Orr-Sommerfeld equation](orr-sommerfeld-equation.md). It follows by taking $\partial_z$ of the streamwise momentum equation minus $\partial_x$ of the spanwise momentum equation. At a rigid no-slip wall, $\widehat\eta=0$.
