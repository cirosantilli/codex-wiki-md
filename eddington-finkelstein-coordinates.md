# Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddington–Finkelstein_coordinates)

The advanced time $v=t+r_*$ or retarded time $u=t-r_*$ combines Schwarzschild time with the [Schwarzschild tortoise coordinate](schwarzschild-tortoise-coordinate.md) to remove one horizon-coordinate singularity. The advanced choice gives [ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md); the retarded choice is outgoing.
