# Counterflow heat exchanger

↑ **Parent:** [Heat exchanger](heat-exchanger.md)

A [heat exchanger](heat-exchanger.md) in which the two streams move in opposite directions. Returning cold [helium](helium.md) can cool incoming compressed [helium](helium.md) before its expansion in [helium liquefaction](helium-liquefaction.md).
