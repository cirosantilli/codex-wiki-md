# Methyl abstraction

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

[Methyl abstraction](methyl-abstraction.md) transfers a [metal](metal.md)-bound methyl group to an [electrophile](electrophile.md). A trityl cation can convert a neutral metallocene dimethyl into a cationic monomethyl, forming $\mathrm{Ph_3CMe}$ and opening a site for an [alkene ligand](alkene-ligand.md).
