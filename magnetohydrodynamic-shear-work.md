# Magnetohydrodynamic shear work

↑ **Parent:** [Plane-parallel magnetohydrodynamic flow with imposed shear](plane-parallel-magnetohydrodynamic-flow-with-imposed-shear.md)

The energy of the residual flow and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) in a maintained [shear flow](shear-flow.md) has source $S=-aT_{xy}$, where $T_{xy}=\rho v_xv_y-B_xB_y/\mu_0$. The kinetic and magnetic stresses can exchange energy with the imposed background. The source vanishes when their off-diagonal contributions cancel.
