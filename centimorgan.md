# Centimorgan

↑ **Parent:** [Morgan (unit)](morgan-unit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centimorgan)

A centimorgan is one hundredth of a [morgan](morgan-unit.md). For a sufficiently short [genetic map](genetic-map.md) interval, one centimorgan corresponds approximately to a $1\%$ [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md), rather than an exact identity for all intervals.
