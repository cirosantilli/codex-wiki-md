# Turbulent mixing-layer similarity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Planar turbulent mixing layer](planar-turbulent-mixing-layer.md)

For a [planar turbulent mixing layer](planar-turbulent-mixing-layer.md), let $U_c=(U_a+U_b)/2$, $\Delta U=U_a-U_b$, $\nu_T=C\Delta U\delta$, $\delta=S\Delta U x/U_c$, and $\xi=\sqrt{S/C}\,y/\delta$. The [stream function](stream-function.md) $\psi=U_c[y+\delta\sqrt{C/S}F(\xi)]$ enforces mean [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md). Substitution into $UU_x+VU_y=\nu_TU_{yy}$ gives the displayed [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md), with $F'(\pm\infty)=\pm\Delta U/(2U_c)$. When $\Delta U/U_c$ is small, its linear approximation is $F'''+\xi F''=0$, so $F''$ is a [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) and $F'$ an [error function](error-function.md).
