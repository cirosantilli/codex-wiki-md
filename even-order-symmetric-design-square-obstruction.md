# Even-order symmetric design square obstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric balanced incomplete block design](symmetric-balanced-incomplete-block-design.md)

In a nontrivial [symmetric balanced incomplete block design](symmetric-balanced-incomplete-block-design.md), $\det(N)^2=r^2(r-\lambda)^{t-1}$. If $t$ is even, $t-1$ is odd. For every prime, the exponent in the left side is even and that in $r^2$ is even, so its exponent in $r-\lambda$ must be even. Thus $r-\lambda$ is a perfect square. This supplies an elementary necessary existence condition without claiming it is sufficient.
