# Half-range Fourier cosine series

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier cosine series](fourier-cosine-series.md)

For a function prescribed on $0<x<L$, its half-range [Fourier cosine series](fourier-cosine-series.md) is the [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) of the even extension, made $2L$-periodic. Its coefficients are $a_n=(2/L)\int_0^L f(x)\cos(n\pi x/L)\,dx$, including $n=0$. At a jump the usual piecewise-smooth convergence theorem gives the average of the two one-sided limits of the periodic extension.
