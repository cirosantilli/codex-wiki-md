# Fourth-moment tightness of polygonal random walks

↑ **Parent:** [Donsker's theorem](donsker-s-theorem.md)

For centered unit-variance [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) increments with $m_4=\mathbb E\xi^4<\infty$, an interpolated increment is $N^{-1/2}\sum_jc_j\xi_j$. Here $c_j$ is the length of the overlap of $(Ns,Nt)$ with the $j$th unit cell, so $0\le c_j\le1$ and $\sum c_j=N(t-s)$. [Independence](independent-random-variables.md) gives its [fourth moment](fourth-moment.md) as $N^{-2}[3(\sum c_j^2)^2+(m_4-3)\sum c_j^4]$. This is at most $\max(3,m_4)N^{-2}(\sum c_j^2)^2$, and $\sum c_j^2\le N(t-s)$ gives the bound, including intervals crossing a mesh point. A uniform fourth-moment continuity criterion yields [tightness of probability measures](uniform-tightness.md) on $C_0[0,1]$.
