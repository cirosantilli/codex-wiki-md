# Stationary phase of topographic internal waves

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary topographic internal gravity wave](stationary-topographic-internal-gravity-wave.md)

For a localized terrain source in uniform flow, the [stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md) applied to $kx+m(k)z$ gives phase $\Phi=(N/U)\sqrt{x^2+z^2}$ on the downstream branch. Permanent phase lines are circular arcs, whereas an individual monochromatic component has straight phase lines. Reflected arcs use an image source; transmitted phase is continuous across the velocity interface.
