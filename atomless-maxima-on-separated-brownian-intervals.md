# Atomless maxima on separated Brownian intervals

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian running maximum](brownian-running-maximum.md)

For [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), write the later maximum as $B_b+Z+H$, where $Z=B_c-B_b$ has a nondegenerate [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) and $H=\sup_{0\leq u\leq d-c}(B_{c+u}-B_c)$. The [independent increments](independent-increments.md) make $Z$ independent of the earlier history and of $H$. Conditional on those two objects, equality of the two maxima prescribes one value of $Z$, an event of probability zero. This argument uses the positive gap $c-b$ and does not assume independence of the two absolute maxima.
