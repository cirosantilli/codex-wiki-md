# Exponential rate with a small known additive hazard

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential-rate estimation from censored exposure](exponential-rate-estimation-from-censored-exposure.md)

Under [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md), a model $H_i(t)=\lambda t+G_i(t)$ with known $G_i$ has score $\sum_i v_i/(\lambda+G_i'(x_i))-X$, where $X=\sum_i x_i$ and $d=\sum_i v_i$. Expanding about the ordinary exponential estimate $d/X$ gives the displayed first-order correction when every added event-time hazard is small compared with $d/X$. The integrated offsets contribute constants to the [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md). If all $G_i'(x_i)=g$, the positive interior estimate is exactly $d/X-g$.
