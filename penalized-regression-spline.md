# Penalized regression spline

↑ **Parent:** [Regression spline](regression-spline.md)

A penalized [regression spline](regression-spline.md) estimates basis coefficients by minimizing a residual or likelihood criterion plus a curvature penalty. Basis size limits the available complexity; the penalty controls how much of that complexity is used.
