# Three-dimensional point source

↑ **Parent:** [Point source](point-source.md)

A point injection of volume at rate $Q$ produces the displayed radial [potential flow](potential-flow.md) in three dimensions. Its [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) is $-Q/(4\pi r)$, and every enclosing [sphere](sphere.md) has outward [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $Q$. The [divergence](divergence.md) vanishes away from the source and is $Q$ times the three-dimensional [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md) distribution at it. The field is also a [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) with constant [pressure](pressure.md) away from its singularity.
