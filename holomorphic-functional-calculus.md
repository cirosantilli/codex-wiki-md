# Holomorphic functional calculus

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_functional_calculus)

For $x$ in a unital Banach algebra and $f$ holomorphic near $\sigma(x)$, the holomorphic functional calculus defines

$$
f(x)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_\Gamma f(z)(z1-x)^{-1}\,dz,
$$

where $\Gamma$ winds once around the spectrum. It is a continuous unital algebra homomorphism and satisfies the [spectral mapping theorem](spectral-mapping-theorem.md) $\sigma(f(x))=f(\sigma(x))$.
