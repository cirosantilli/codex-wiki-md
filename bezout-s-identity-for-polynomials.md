<h1 id="bezout-s-identity-for-polynomials">Bézout's identity for polynomials</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Coprime polynomials](coprime-polynomials.md)

For [univariate polynomials](univariate-polynomial.md) $f,g$ over a [field](field.md), not both zero, there are [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) $A,B$ such that $Af+Bg=d$, where $d$ is their monic [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md). In particular, [coprime polynomials](coprime-polynomials.md) admit $Af+Bg=1$. The [extended Euclidean algorithm](extended-euclidean-algorithm.md) constructs the coefficients.
