# Driven string standing wave experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Standing wave](standing-wave.md)

The University of California, Santa Barbara's cord demonstration uses a [function generator](function-generator.md) and mechanical driver loosely attached near a clamped cord end. The other end passes over a pulley to a mass hanger, setting [tension](tension.md). Its photograph shows operation with a strobe; the page reports measured and calculated lowest-mode [frequencies](frequency.md). Varying drive [frequency](frequency.md) at fixed loading selects higher [normal modes](normal-mode.md). This clamp arrangement differs from a string tied directly to a moving driver.[Standing waves on cord, opening apparatus description, operating photograph, fixed-end explanation, and calculated versus measured frequency table](40-75-standing-waves-on-cord-ucsb-department-of-physics.md)

A controlled [frequency](frequency.md) scan holds the cord, endpoint separation and hanging load fixed, then varies [frequency](frequency.md) slowly to locate large steady [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md). Read stationary [nodes of a standing wave](node-physics.md) and count loops; compare the inferred $\lambda=2L/n$ with $c=f\lambda$. As additional checks, compare small drive settings, repeat the scan in reverse, and change only the hanging load to test the predicted square-root [tension](tension.md) dependence. These checks test the ideal model rather than adding measurements to the demonstration.

An elastic cord's stretched [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) can differ from its relaxed value; the published calculation uses the relaxed value. A strobe can freeze motion at misleading settings, so identify [nodes of a standing wave](node-physics.md) across several snapshots. A moving attachment need not be an exact [node of a standing wave](node-physics.md). These limits qualify any comparison with ideal fixed-end [eigenfrequencies](eigenfrequency.md).[Standing waves on cord, clamp and nearby driver distinction, optional strobe, and relaxed-cord linear-density assumption](40-75-standing-waves-on-cord-ucsb-department-of-physics.md)
