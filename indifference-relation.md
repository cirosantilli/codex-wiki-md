# Indifference relation

↑ **Parent:** [Preference relation](preference-relation.md)

For a [preorder](preorder.md), the [indifference relation](indifference-relation.md) is an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md). Passing to its equivalence classes removes ties and produces a [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md).
