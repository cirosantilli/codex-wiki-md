# Metric realization of a countable star

↑ **Parent:** [Metric realization of a simplicial complex](metric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md)

In the [metric realization of a simplicial complex](metric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md), the point on arm $n$ at parameter $t$ is $(1-t)e_v+te_{u_n}$. Its distance from the common vertex is $\sqrt2\,t$. Consequently the vertex has a [neighbourhood basis](neighbourhood-basis.md) consisting of one uniformly short initial interval on every arm. Away from that vertex, each arm is locally isolated from the other arms.

The map $(n,t)\mapsto te^{i/n}$, with all $t=0$ identified, is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) onto

$$
\{0\}\cup\{te^{i/n}:n\geq1,\ 0<t\leq1\}\subset\mathbb R^2.
$$

It is a bijection, preserves the one-dimensional topology on each arm away from zero, and matches the uniform-radius neighbourhood bases at zero. The positive real limiting ray is omitted. The outer endpoints form an infinite [closed discrete subset](closed-discrete-subset.md) in the original metric realization, so the image is not compact. This planar fan and the [weak realization of a countable star](weak-realization-of-a-countable-star.md) have different topologies at their common vertex.
