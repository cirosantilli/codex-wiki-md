# Separable aquifer drawdown

↑ **Parent:** [Boussinesq equation for an unconfined aquifer](boussinesq-equation-for-an-unconfined-aquifer.md)

After recharge stops on a finite aquifer connected to a river, the late solution of the [Boussinesq equation for an unconfined aquifer](boussinesq-equation-for-an-unconfined-aquifer.md) has the form $h\sim t^{-1}F(x/L)$. The profile solves a nonlinear eigenvalue problem, and river discharge decays as $t^{-2}$.
