# Proper base change for sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Derived direct image of sheaves](derived-direct-image-of-sheaves.md)

For a proper map of locally compact spaces and a constructible complex $E$, the derived stalk satisfies $(Rf_*E)_y\cong R\Gamma(f^{-1}(y),E|_{f^{-1}(y)})$. In particular $(R^if_*\mathbb Q)_y\cong H^i(f^{-1}(y);\mathbb Q)$. Properness is what prevents fibre [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) from missing nearby sections.
