# c-src

↑ **Parent:** [Gene](gene.md)

[c-src](c-src.md) is the normal cellular counterpart of viral src, encoding a regulated protein tyrosine kinase. Conservation in normal cells distinguishes a proto-oncogene from an exclusively virus-specific genetic invention.
