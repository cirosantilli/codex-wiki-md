# Radiative electron-hole recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-hole recombination](electron-hole-recombination.md)

In a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md), a conduction [electron](electron.md) fills a vacant valence state and emits a [photon](photon.md). In a simple nondegenerate model the recombination rate is $Bnp$, while the equilibrium reverse generation rate is $Bn_i^2$; their difference gives the displayed net pair-loss rate. This subtraction enforces [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) at thermal equilibrium. Photon momentum is small on the scale of a [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md), so a [direct band gap](direct-band-gap.md) permits a direct transition while an [indirect band gap](indirect-band-gap.md) generally requires a [phonon](phonon.md) or another source of momentum.
