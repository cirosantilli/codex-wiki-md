# Angular differential imaging

↑ **Parent:** [High-contrast imaging](high-contrast-imaging.md)

Angular differential imaging keeps the instrument pupil fixed while the sky rotates. A stellar reference [point spread function](point-spread-function.md) is subtracted, then the residuals are derotated and combined. Insufficient rotation and imperfect reference selection cause [differential-imaging self-subtraction](differential-imaging-self-subtraction.md).
