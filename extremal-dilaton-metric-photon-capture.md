# Extremal dilaton metric photon capture

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic potential of the magnetic dilaton black hole](geodesic-potential-of-the-magnetic-dilaton-black-hole.md)

For $f=1-2M/r$ and squared areal radius $C=r(r-b)$, an equatorial [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) obeys $\dot r^2=E^2[1-B^2f/C]$, with [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $B=L/E$. At $b=2M$, $f/C=1/r^2$ in the exterior. Its supremum is $1/(4M^2)$ at the null singular boundary, rather than at a regular exterior [photon sphere](photon-sphere.md). Rays with $B>2M$ turn at $r=B$; rays with $B<2M$ reach the boundary. Assuming those rays are absorbed gives the displayed geometric-optics cross-section despite zero limiting horizon area.
