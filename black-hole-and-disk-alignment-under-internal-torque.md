# Black-hole and disk alignment under internal torque

↑ **Parent:** [Lense-Thirring precession](lense-thirring-precession.md)

For the opposite internal torques on a [black hole](black-hole.md) and its [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md), the total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $\mathbf J_t$ and the black-hole spin magnitude $J_h$ are constant. If $A=K_2|\mathbf J_h\times\mathbf J_d|^2$, then $d(\mathbf J_h\cdot\mathbf J_t)/dt=A$ and $dJ_d^2/dt=-2A$. The $K_1$ term produces [precession](precession.md); the positive $K_2$ term increases the alignment of the hole with the total vector while decreasing the disk angular-momentum magnitude. The exact direction equation is $\dot c=K_2J_hJ_t(1-c^2)$ for $c=\cos\angle(\mathbf J_h,\mathbf J_t)$.
