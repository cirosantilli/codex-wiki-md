# Weakly inaccessible cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Large cardinal](large-cardinal.md)

A weakly inaccessible cardinal is an uncountable [regular cardinal](regular-cardinal.md) that is a [limit cardinal](limit-cardinal.md). It need not be a [strong limit cardinal](strong-limit-cardinal.md). Under the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md), it is a [strongly inaccessible cardinal](strongly-inaccessible-cardinal.md), since every smaller infinite $\lambda$ satisfies $2^\lambda=\lambda^+<\kappa$.
