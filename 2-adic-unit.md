# 2-adic unit

↑ **Parent:** [P-adic unit](p-adic-unit.md)

A 2-adic unit is an odd element of $\mathbb Z_2$. It is a square in $\mathbb Z_2^\times$ exactly when it is congruent to $1$ modulo $8$.
