# Normal ordering identity for up and down operators

↑ **Parent:** [Differential poset](differential-poset.md)

If $DU-UD=I$, commuting every $D$ past every $U$ gives $(D+U)^\ell=\sum_{i+j+2m=\ell}\ell!U^iD^j/(2^m i!j!m!)$. The recurrence follows from $DU^i=U^iD+iU^{i-1}$. Applied to the empty diagram in the [Young lattice](young-s-lattice.md), it counts [oscillating tableaux](oscillating-path-in-the-young-lattice.md).
