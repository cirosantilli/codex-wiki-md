# Damped wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

A [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) with a linear [damping](damping.md) term and positive [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $c$ separates into spatial [normal modes](normal-mode.md) and [damped harmonic oscillators](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md). For a spatial [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $k^2$, the time factor obeys $T''+\alpha T'+c^2k^2T=0$. Its characteristic roots are $-\alpha/2\pm\sqrt{\alpha^2/4-c^2k^2}$. Thus [underdamping](underdamping.md) requires $|\alpha|<2c|k|$, [critical damping](critical-damping.md) gives a repeated root and the second solution $te^{-\alpha t/2}$, and [overdamping](overdamping.md) gives two real exponential rates. Negative $\alpha$ amplifies rather than damps the motion.
