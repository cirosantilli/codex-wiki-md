# Cartier-divisor description of the Picard group

↑ **Parent:** [Picard group](picard-group.md)

On an [irreducible variety](irreducible-variety.md), a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) is a section of $\mathcal K_X^*/\mathcal O_X^*$: its local rational equations differ by regular units. Their ratios define an [invertible sheaf](line-bundle.md), and a global rational equation gives the trivial class. Conversely a rational trivialization of an [invertible sheaf](line-bundle.md) gives local equations. The rational-function sheaf is [flasque](flasque-sheaf.md), so its [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) also identifies the quotient with $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X^*)$.
