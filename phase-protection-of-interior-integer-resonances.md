# Phase protection of interior integer resonances

↑ **Parent:** [Resonant conjunction geometry](resonant-conjunction-geometry.md)

In a high-[orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) interior $k:1$ [mean-motion resonance](mean-motion-resonance.md), the [resonant argument](resonant-argument.md) $\phi=k\lambda_p-\lambda-(k-1)\varpi$ places successive rotating-frame [apocentres](apoapsis.md) at $\pi-[\phi+(2j+1)\pi]/k$. Avoiding [astronomical conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md) at these slow outer excursions favors $\phi=0$ for $k=2$ and $\phi=\pi$ for $k=3$. This geometrical phase protection suggests stable [resonant-argument libration](resonant-argument-libration.md) centres; actual dynamical stability requires the resonant perturbation problem.
