# Archosauria

↑ **Parent:** [Diapsida](diapsida.md)

The [clade](clade.md) comprising the most recent common ancestor of living [crocodilians](crocodilian.md) and [birds](bird.md) and all its descendants, including extinct [dinosaurs](dinosaur.md). Being sister to this [clade](clade.md) does not make [turtles](turtle.md) [archosaurs](archosauria.md).
