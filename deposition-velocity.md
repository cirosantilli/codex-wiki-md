# Deposition velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Dry deposition](dry-deposition.md)

The ratio of a specified downward [dry deposition](dry-deposition.md) flux to its reference near-surface [concentration](concentration.md). It has units of speed but need not be an actual particle settling speed: transfer resistance and surface uptake can both control gas [dry deposition](dry-deposition.md).
