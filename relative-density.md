# Relative density

↑ **Parent:** [Density](density.md)

The [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) ratio of a material's [density](density.md) to a specified reference [density](density.md), with reference conditions stated. Gases are commonly compared with dry air and liquids with [water](water.md).
