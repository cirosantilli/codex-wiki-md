# Valence and sea decomposition of proton-antiproton W production

↑ **Parent:** [Parton distribution function](parton-distribution-function.md)

For [charged current](charged-current.md) production $u\bar d\to W^+$, [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) of the antiproton distributions gives $u(x_+)d(x_-)+\bar d(x_+)\bar u(x_-)$. With a common [sea quark](sea-quark.md) distribution $S$, the [valence quark](valence-quark.md) terms are $u_vd_v$, the mixed terms are $u_vS+Sd_v$, and the two sea assignments give $2S(x_+)S(x_-)$. Omitting the second beam assignment undercounts the pure sea contribution by two.
