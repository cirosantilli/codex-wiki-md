# Exponential variational trial for a square-root half-line well

↑ **Parent:** [Variational method](variational-method.md)

The trial $xe^{-bx}$, $b>0$, satisfies the wall condition in the [half-line square-root potential well](half-line-square-root-potential-well.md). Its norm is $1/(4b^3)$, kinetic [energy](energy-split.md) is $\hbar^2b^2/(2m)$ and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is $(15a/16)\sqrt{\pi/2}\,b^{-1/2}$. The optimal $b$ satisfies $b^{5/2}=(ma/\hbar^2)(15/32)\sqrt{\pi/2}$. At the minimum, $\langle V\rangle=4\langle T\rangle$ and $E_{\rm var}=5\hbar^2b^2/(2m)=1.876493E_*$. The [quantum variational principle](quantum-variational-principle.md) makes this an upper bound on the [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md).
