# Church numeral zero test

↑ **Parent:** [Church numeral](church-numeral.md)

The [lambda term](lambda-term.md)

$$
\mathsf{Zero}=\lambda n.n(\lambda z.\mathsf F)\mathsf T
$$

returns the [Church Boolean](church-boolean.md) $\mathsf T$ on $c_0$ and $\mathsf F$ on every positive [Church numeral](church-numeral.md). Zero iterations leave the initial true value, while at least one iteration replaces it by false. [Normal-order beta reduction](normal-order-beta-reduction.md) evaluates a numeral conditional without evaluating an unchosen branch.
