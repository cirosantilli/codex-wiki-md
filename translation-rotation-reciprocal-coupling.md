# Translation-rotation reciprocal coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry and positivity of a rigid-body resistance matrix](symmetry-and-positivity-of-a-rigid-body-resistance-matrix.md)

For a fixed rigid-body shape and origin in an unbounded stationary [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md), the [Lorentz reciprocal theorem](lorentz-reciprocal-theorem-for-stokes-flow.md) makes the off-diagonal blocks of the six-dimensional [hydrodynamic resistance matrix](hydrodynamic-resistance-matrix.md) transposes. Here $\mathbf F,\mathbf T$ are the fluid's force and torque on the body. If every pure translation has zero [torque](torque.md) about the chosen origin, then $B^T=0$, hence $B=0$: every pure rotation about that origin has zero resultant [force](force-split.md). This conditional statement does not require such an origin to exist for every body.
