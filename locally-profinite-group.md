# Locally profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)

A locally profinite group is a Hausdorff, locally compact, totally disconnected [topological group](topological-group-split.md). It has a neighbourhood basis at the identity consisting of [compact-open subgroups](compact-open-subgroup.md). In smooth representation theory this is also called an $\ell$-group; that terminology is unrelated to a lattice-ordered group.
