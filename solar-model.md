# Solar model

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

A model of the Sun obtained from [stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md) equations with specified composition, opacity and energy generation. Uniform [density](density.md) is useful for scale estimates but cannot reproduce the strong central concentration.
