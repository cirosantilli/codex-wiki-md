# Radiofrequency pseudopotential of a Paul trap

↑ **Parent:** [Paul trap](paul-trap.md)

When the radiofrequency drive is much faster than the [Paul trap secular motion](paul-trap-secular-motion.md) and [Paul trap micromotion](paul-trap-micromotion.md) is small, averaging the oscillatory force produces the displayed [effective potential](effective-potential.md). The [electric field](electric-field-split.md) here is its peak oscillating amplitude. It is an [energy](energy-split.md), not a static electrostatic potential, so confinement does not contradict the static electrostatic no-trapping theorem. Excess [Paul trap micromotion](paul-trap-micromotion.md) from displacement from the radiofrequency null is distinct from the intended slow [Paul trap secular motion](paul-trap-secular-motion.md).
