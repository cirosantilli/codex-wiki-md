# Multiplicative cancellation in an integral domain

↑ **Parent:** [Integral domain](integral-domain.md)

In an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), $ab=ac$ with $a\ne0$ gives $a(b-c)=0$ by the [distributive property](distributive-property.md). Since there are no [zero divisors](zero-divisor.md), $b-c=0$. Conversely, in a nonzero [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md), this cancellation property rules out [zero divisors](zero-divisor.md) by comparing $ab=0$ with $a0=0$.
