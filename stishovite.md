# Stishovite

↑ **Parent:** [Silicon dioxide](silicon-dioxide.md)

A dense structural polymorph of [silicon dioxide](silicon-dioxide.md). Comparing its unit-cell volume per formula unit with that of a coexisting phase supplies the specific-volume difference needed in a [Clapeyron equation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) calculation.
