# Linear dispersive Stokes equation

↑ **Parent:** [Airy equation](airy-equation.md)

The scalar [linear partial differential equation](linear-partial-differential-equation.md) $u_t+u_x+u_{xxx}=0$ is an [Airy equation](airy-equation.md) with a first-order transport term. It is a linearization of the [Korteweg-De Vries equation](korteweg-de-vries-equation.md) with an advective term retained. It is distinct from the viscous incompressible-flow [Stokes equation](stokes-equation.md). On the whole line its spatial [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) evolves by $e^{i(k^3-k)t}$.
