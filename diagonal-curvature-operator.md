# Diagonal curvature operator

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature 2-form](curvature-2-form.md)

The curvature operator is diagonal in an orthonormal coframe $\{e^a\}$ when every [curvature 2-form](curvature-2-form.md) satisfies $\mathcal R^{ab}=K_{ab}e^a\wedge e^b$. Its matrix on the corresponding basis of [2-forms](2-form.md) is then diagonal.
