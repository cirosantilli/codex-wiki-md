# Covariance of Grassmann bilinears

↑ **Parent:** [Ordered Grassmann Gaussian contractions](ordered-grassmann-gaussian-contractions.md)

For $C=B^{-1}$, [ordered Grassmann Gaussian contractions](ordered-grassmann-gaussian-contractions.md) give $\langle\bar\theta M\theta\rangle=-\operatorname{tr}(MC)$ and $\langle(\bar\theta M\theta)(\bar\theta N\theta)\rangle=\operatorname{tr}(MC)\operatorname{tr}(NC)-\operatorname{tr}(MCNC)$. The disconnected contribution has two [fermion loops](fermion-loop.md); the connected contribution has one, giving its minus sign. This covariance is an algebraic normalized [Berezin integral](berezin-integral.md), rather than a positivity statement about a probability measure.
