# Sea-ice lead-width distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Lead (sea ice)](lead-sea-ice.md)

[Sea-ice lead-width distributions](sea-ice-lead-width-distribution.md) depend on observational thresholds, resolution and whether leads are counted individually or weighted by area. Observations commonly exhibit a broad, approximately [power law](power-law.md) range, with many narrow leads and a relatively heavy tail.
