# Cyclic unitary representation of a positive-definite function

↑ **Parent:** [Positive-definite function](positive-definite-function.md)

For an Abelian group, on finite formal sums of group points, define $\langle[x],[y]\rangle=p(xy^{-1})$, with the first variable linear. Quotient by the null space of this [positive semidefinite](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) form and complete to a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). Left translation is unitary, and $v=[e]$ generates a dense span of translated vectors. For continuous $p$, the equality $\|U_x[y]-[y]\|^2=2(p(e)-\operatorname{Re}p(x))$ gives [strong continuity](strong-continuity.md). This realizes $p$ as a [matrix coefficient](matrix-coefficient.md) without assuming its integral representation.
