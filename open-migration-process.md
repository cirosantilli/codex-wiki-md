# Open migration process

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic network](stochastic-network.md)

An open migration process is a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) of colony populations with external [Poisson processes](poisson-process.md), state-dependent departure rates, and fixed probabilistic routing that eventually exits the system. Its [traffic equations of an open migration process](traffic-equation-of-an-open-migration-process.md) determine the total arrival rates, and its equilibrium has a [product-form stationary distribution of an open migration process](product-form-stationary-distribution-of-an-open-migration-process.md) when the normalizing sums are finite.
