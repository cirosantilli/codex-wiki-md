# Slow circular inspiral under tangential drag

↑ **Parent:** [Chandrasekhar dynamical friction](chandrasekhar-dynamical-friction.md)

For a slowly evolving [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) with tangential deceleration $D>0$, the [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) is $j=rV_c(r)$ and its [torque](torque.md) is $\dot j=-rD$. Differentiating $j$ gives the displayed drift when $1+q\ne0$. The positive local decay time $r/(2|\dot r|)$ is $(1+q)V_c/(2D)$. In a uniform-density core $q=1$, so it is $V_c/D$; for a flat [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md) $q=0$, it is $V_c/(2D)$. Simply setting $\dot V_c=-D$ neglects the radial contribution to [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) change.
