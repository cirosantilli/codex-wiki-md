# Spectral geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian geometry](riemannian-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_geometry)

[Spectral geometry](spectral-geometry.md) studies which properties of a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) can be recovered from the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) of geometric [differential operators](differential-operator.md), especially the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md). Explicit [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md), [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) on a [flat torus](flat-torus.md), and [isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md) illustrate three different aspects of the problem.
