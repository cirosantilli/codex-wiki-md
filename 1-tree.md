# 1-tree

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling salesman problem](travelling-salesman-problem.md)

For a designated root vertex in an undirected complete graph, a 1-tree consists of a [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md) on all other vertices together with two distinct root-incident edges. It is connected and has exactly one cycle, which passes through the root. Its minimum weight is the cost of an independent [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md) plus the two cheapest root edges, computable in quadratic time on a dense graph by [Prim's algorithm](prim-s-algorithm.md).
