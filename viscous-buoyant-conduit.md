# Viscous buoyant conduit

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

A slender low-viscosity fluid column inside a much more viscous fluid rises under a density contrast. At negligible inertia, its axial flux is approximately $q=\pi a^4(\Delta\rho g-P_{i,z})/(8\mu_i)$. Radial expansion strains the outer fluid and gives the normal-stress [pressure](pressure.md) difference $P_i-P_o=2\mu_o a_t/a$ at leading order when inner normal viscous stress and interfacial tension are negligible. Coupling that [pressure](pressure.md) to volume conservation yields the [conduit equation](conduit-equation.md). This two-fluid viscous model differs from a turbulent entraining thermal plume.
