# Solid ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md)

The interior bounded by an [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) is a solid ellipsoid. Positive coordinate dilations multiply its [symplectic capacity](symplectic-capacity.md) by the square of the dilation factor.
