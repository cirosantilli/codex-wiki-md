# Sectional curvatures from Ricci curvature in dimension three

↑ **Parent:** [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md)

For an orthonormal triple and distinct $i,j,k$, tracing [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) gives $\operatorname{Ric}_{ii}=K_{ij}+K_{ik}$. Solving the three equations gives the displayed formula. Equivalently $K_{ij}=\operatorname{Ric}_{ii}+\operatorname{Ric}_{jj}-\operatorname{Scal}/2$. This pointwise assertion does not require the triple to diagonalize the [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md).
