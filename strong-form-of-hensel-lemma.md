# Strong form of Hensel lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Hensel's lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md)

Let $K$ be complete for a [discrete valuation](discrete-valuation.md) $v$. If $f\in\mathcal O_K[X]$ and

$$
v(f(a))>2v(f'(a)),
$$

then [Newton iteration over a valued field](newton-iteration-over-a-valued-field.md) converges to a root $\alpha\in\mathcal O_K$ with $v(\alpha-a)>v(f'(a))$.
