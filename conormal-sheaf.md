# Conormal sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Conormal exact sequence for Kähler differentials](conormal-exact-sequence-for-kahler-differentials.md)

For a closed immersion, this [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) measures the first-order equations of the subvariety. Its local [module](module-mathematics.md) is $I/I^2$, and its map to the restricted [Kähler differential sheaf](sheaf-of-kahler-differentials-over-a-field.md) sends the class of $f$ to $df$. This is the definition in [Stacks Project, Section 29.32](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01R1).
