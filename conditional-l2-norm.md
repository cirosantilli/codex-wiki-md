# Conditional L2 norm

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md)

Disintegrate over a [factor map](factor-map-between-measure-preserving-systems.md) and take the $L^2$ norm using the conditional probability on each fiber. Its square integrates to the global squared norm. The [uniform conditional L2 norm](uniform-conditional-l2-norm.md) takes the essential supremum of these fiber norms.
