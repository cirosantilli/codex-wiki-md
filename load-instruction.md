# Load instruction

↑ **Parent:** [Machine instruction](machine-instruction.md)

A [machine instruction](machine-instruction.md) that reads a value from [computer memory](computer-memory-split.md) into a [processor register](processor-register.md). A demand [cache miss](cache-miss.md) may delay its result; a [software prefetch](software-prefetch.md) has no such architectural result.
