# Point of subjective simultaneity

↑ **Parent:** [Simultaneity judgment](simultaneity-judgment.md)

An offset at which events are judged most simultaneous under a specified measurement procedure. A fitted peak in simultaneity reports need not coincide with the equal-response point of an order task or with equal neural onset latencies.
