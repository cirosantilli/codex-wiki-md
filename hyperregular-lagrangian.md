# Hyperregular Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Regular Lagrangian](regular-lagrangian.md)

A [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is hyperregular when its fiber derivative, sending a velocity to its conjugate momentum, is a global [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) from the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) to the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md). This gives a globally defined [Legendre transform in mechanics](legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) and a global equivalence between the [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) and [Hamilton's equations](hamilton-s-equations.md). With time dependence this requirement is imposed at each time, with a smooth inverse depending on time.
