# Bicelle

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md)

A lipid assembly containing a bilayer-like region whose edges are stabilized by other components, often short-chain lipids or detergents. Size and composition affect its use as a [membrane mimic](membrane-mimic.md).
