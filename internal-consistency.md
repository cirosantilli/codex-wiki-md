# Internal consistency

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement reliability](measurement-reliability.md)

The degree of association among items or components of a measure under a specified model. High [internal consistency](internal-consistency.md) can arise from overlapping content or [common method variance](common-method-variance.md); it does not by itself establish one substantive dimension or [construct validity](construct-validity.md).
