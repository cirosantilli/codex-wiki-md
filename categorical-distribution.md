# Categorical distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_distribution)

A [categorical distribution](categorical-distribution.md) describes one trial with one of $k$ labelled outcomes and [probabilities](probability.md) $p_1,\ldots,p_k$ summing to one. The labels need not be numbers or have an order. It is the single-trial building block of the [multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md): $n$ [independent](independent-random-variables.md) draws with the same [categorical distribution](categorical-distribution.md) produce multinomial category counts.

For a trial choosing labels $A,B,C$ with [probabilities](probability.md) $1/2,3/10,1/5$, the category-indicator [random vector](random-vector.md) is $(1,0,0)$, $(0,1,0)$, or $(0,0,1)$. Its [expected value](expected-value-split.md) is $(1/2,3/10,1/5)$; its components are dependent because exactly one is one. A numerical encoding of $A,B,C$ is a separate modelling choice: the category labels themselves do not define an [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md).
