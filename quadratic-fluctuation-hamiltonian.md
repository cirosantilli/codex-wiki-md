# Quadratic fluctuation Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian fluctuation approximation](gaussian-fluctuation-approximation.md)

Expanding the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) about a static classical solution removes the linear term by its [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md). For a canonical [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) with potential $U$, the quadratic fluctuation operator is $\mathcal H=-\partial_X^2+U''(\varphi_{\rm cl})$. Positive-frequency [normal modes](normal-mode.md) become [quantum harmonic oscillators](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) under [canonical quantization](canonical-quantization.md), yielding the Gaussian contribution to a [one-loop soliton mass correction](one-loop-soliton-mass-correction.md). A negative [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) signals an instability, while a physical [zero mode in field theory](zero-mode-in-field-theory.md) must be replaced by a [collective coordinate](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md).
