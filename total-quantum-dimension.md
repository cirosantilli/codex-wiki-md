# Total quantum dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum dimension](quantum-dimension.md)

The positive square root of the sum of the squared [quantum dimensions](quantum-dimension.md) of all simple [anyons](anyon.md). The [vacuum anyon](vacuum-anyon.md) has $d_1=1$ and [antianyons](antianyon.md) have equal [quantum dimensions](quantum-dimension.md).
