# Fault offset

↑ **Parent:** [Fault slip](fault-slip.md)

The observed separation of an originally correlated marker across a [fault](geological-fault.md). Its value depends on marker geometry and measurement direction; apparent map separation is not automatically the full [fault slip](fault-slip.md) vector. Matching channel reaches, beds or dated surfaces supplies candidate piercing constraints.
