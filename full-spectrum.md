# Full spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md)

The full spectrum of an element is its spectrum together with every bounded component of its complement. Its complement is the unbounded resolvent component, and the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md) controls a holomorphic function on each filled hole by its values on the spectral boundary.
