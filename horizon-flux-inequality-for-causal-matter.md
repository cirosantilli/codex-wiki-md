# Horizon flux inequality for causal matter

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose process](penrose-process.md)

For a future-directed causal four-momentum $p$ crossing a future [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md) with generator $\xi=t+\Omega_H\varphi$, the Lorentzian inner product obeys $p\cdot\xi\le0$. With $E=-p\cdot t$ and $L=p\cdot\varphi$, this is $E\ge\Omega_HL$. A massive particle has strict inequality. The same bound follows in the nonextremal quasistationary regime from the [Physical-process first law for a rotating black hole](physical-process-first-law-for-a-rotating-black-hole.md) and the [second law of black-hole mechanics](second-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md). Negative Killing energy in a [Penrose process](penrose-process.md) is allowed, but its angular momentum must satisfy this bound.
