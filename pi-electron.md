# Pi electron

↑ **Parent:** [Pi conjugation](pi-conjugation.md)

An [electron](electron.md) occupying a pi-type [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md). In a conjugated planar [molecule](molecule-split.md), the pi subspace is commonly built from [p orbitals](p-orbital.md) perpendicular to the molecular plane; its electron population excludes the sigma framework.
