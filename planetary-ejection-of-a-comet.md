# Planetary ejection of a comet

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary scattering](planetary-scattering.md)

A [gravitational assist](gravitational-assist.md) ejects a weakly bound [comet](comet.md) when the increase in its [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) exceeds its binding energy. For near-parabolic coplanar encounters, the weak-deflection ejection [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) scales as $(M_p/M_\star)Q$. Combining this with the [phase-mixed comet surface density](phase-mixed-comet-surface-density.md) gives a one-encounter ejection time proportional to $Q^{1/2}$, within the local two-body encounter approximation.
