# Static-surface Schwarzschild redshift

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md)

A static emitter at radius $R>2GM/c^2$ and a static receiver at infinity compare [photon](photon.md) wavelengths with $1+z=(1-2GM/(Rc^2))^{-1/2}$. Conservation of the time-translation [Killing energy](killing-energy.md) and the static clock rate $d\tau=\sqrt{1-2GM/(rc^2)}\,dt$ prove the relation. Motion of the emitter adds a [Doppler factor](doppler-factor.md); a rotating stellar exterior may require a different metric.
