# Optical reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Light](light.md)

Redirection of incident [light](light.md) into outgoing directions at a material boundary or through scattering near it. [Specular reflection](specular-reflection.md) concentrates light around a geometric reflection direction; diffuse reflection distributes it more broadly. This physical process is distinct from reflection as a mathematical transformation.
