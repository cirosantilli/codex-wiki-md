# Dynamic continuation of a static incompressible elastic deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Finite elasticity](finite-elasticity.md)

Let $\mathbf x_0(\mathbf X;\tau)$ be a family of [static elastic equilibrium](static-elastic-equilibrium.md) solutions for a homogeneous material with [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) under a fixed spatial [body force](body-force.md). Replacing $\tau$ by time gives a dynamic solution of the elastic field equations exactly when the resulting [material acceleration](material-acceleration.md) has a global [acceleration potential](acceleration-potential.md) $\xi$. At the same [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md), the [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) can change only by the scalar [pressure](pressure-split.md) reaction. Subtracting static and dynamic [balance of linear momentum](balance-of-linear-momentum.md) gives $\nabla q=\rho\mathbf a$, and constant [mass density](density.md) then gives $q=-\rho\xi+C(t)$. Surface [tractions](traction.md) must match the adjusted [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md).
