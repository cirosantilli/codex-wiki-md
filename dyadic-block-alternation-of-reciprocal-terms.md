# Dyadic-block alternation of reciprocal terms

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence and series](sequence-and-series.md)

For $0\le r<2^n$ and $n\ge0$, the displayed [sequence](sequence.md) tends to zero, but its [series](series-mathematics.md) diverges. The signed sum across the $n$th dyadic block has magnitude $\sum_{r=0}^{2^n-1}(2^n+r)^{-1}\ge1/2$. Differences of arbitrarily late [partial sums](partial-sum.md) therefore fail the [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md) condition on [partial sums](partial-sum.md). Alternating signs between blocks do not rescue convergence when the total contribution of an entire block stays away from zero.
