# Coulomb-gauge vector potential of a long solenoid

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md)

For an ideal infinitely long [solenoid](solenoid.md) with interior [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) $B_0\hat{\mathbf z}$ and radius $R$, an axisymmetric [Coulomb gauge](coulomb-gauge.md) representative of the [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) is

$$
\mathbf A=A_\phi(r)\hat{\boldsymbol\phi},\qquad A_\phi(r)=\begin{cases}B_0r/2,&r<R,\\B_0R^2/(2r),&r>R.\end{cases}
$$

This follows from $2\pi rA_\phi=\Phi(r)$ by [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md). The exterior has zero [curl](curl.md) but nonzero circulation around the enclosed [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md). The [Coulomb gauge](coulomb-gauge.md) condition alone does not eliminate every residual [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md); symmetry and regularity select this customary representative.
