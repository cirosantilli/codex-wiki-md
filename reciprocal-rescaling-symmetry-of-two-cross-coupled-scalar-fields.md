# Reciprocal rescaling symmetry of two cross-coupled scalar fields

↑ **Parent:** [Internal symmetry of a classical field theory](internal-symmetry-of-a-classical-field-theory.md)

For $\mathcal L=\phi_{1,t}\phi_{2,t}-c^2\phi_{1,x}\phi_{2,x}$, the real linear internal transformations preserving the density are $\operatorname{diag}(r,r^{-1})$ and $\begin{pmatrix}0&r\\r^{-1}&0\end{pmatrix}$ with $r\ne0$. There are four [connected components](connected-component.md); the [identity component](identity-component.md) is the displayed reciprocal rescaling with $r=e^s>0$. In the sum-and-difference basis it preserves a [bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md) of signature $(1,1)$, with the identity component isomorphic to that of the [One-dimensional Lorentz group](one-dimensional-lorentz-group.md). Its [Noether current](noether-current.md) in coordinates $(t,x)$ is $j^0=\phi_1\phi_{2,t}-\phi_2\phi_{1,t}$ and $j^x=c^2(\phi_2\phi_{1,x}-\phi_1\phi_{2,x})$. The two wave equations give $\partial_tj^0+\partial_xj^x=0$. The energy quadratic form is indefinite: the fields $\phi_\pm=(\phi_1\pm\phi_2)/\sqrt2$ have equal and opposite kinetic signs.
