# Homotopy sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Homology sphere](homology-sphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_sphere)

A [homotopy](homotopy.md) [sphere](sphere.md) is a closed [manifold](topological-manifold.md) with the [homotopy type](homotopy-type.md) of a [sphere](sphere.md) of the same dimension. In dimensions at least two, a closed [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [integral homology sphere](homology-sphere.md) is a [homotopy](homotopy.md) [sphere](sphere.md): successive applications of the [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) produce a map from the [sphere](sphere.md) inducing an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) on integral [homology](homology-split.md), and the [homological Whitehead theorem](homological-whitehead-theorem.md) makes it a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md). The [topological generalized Poincare theorem](topological-generalized-poincare-theorem.md) identifies its topological type, while prescribed smooth structures require a separate question.
