# Non-Archimedean place of a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Place of a number field](place-of-a-number-field.md)

If a prime ideal $\mathfrak p$ of $K$ lies over the [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$ with [ramification index of a prime ideal](ramification-index-of-a-prime-ideal.md) $e_{\mathfrak p}$, its normalized absolute value is

$$
|x|_{\mathfrak p}=p^{-\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak p}(x)/e_{\mathfrak p}}.
$$

It extends the usual [p-adic absolute value](p-adic-absolute-value.md) on $\mathbb Q$. Its local degree is $[K_{\mathfrak p}:\mathbb Q_p]=e_{\mathfrak p}f_{\mathfrak p}$, where $f_{\mathfrak p}$ is the [residue-field degree](residue-field-degree.md).
