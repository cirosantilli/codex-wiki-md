# Codimension of a submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of a manifold](dimension-of-a-manifold.md)

The codimension is the difference between the [dimension of a manifold](dimension-of-a-manifold.md) and that of its submanifold. For [complex submanifolds](complex-submanifold.md), complex codimension one means real codimension two.
