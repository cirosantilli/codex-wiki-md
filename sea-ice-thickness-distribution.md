# Sea-ice thickness distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)

A [sea-ice thickness distribution](sea-ice-thickness-distribution.md) assigns fractional horizontal area to ice thickness intervals: $g(h)\,dh$ is the fraction between $h$ and $h+dh$. Include open water as a mass at $h=0$ when normalizing over the entire ocean area. The first moment is ice volume per ocean area. With growth rate $f$ and velocity $v$, its conservative evolution is $\partial_tg+\nabla\cdot(vg)+\partial_h(fg)=\psi$, with [mechanical redistribution of sea ice](mechanical-redistribution-of-sea-ice.md) represented by $\psi$.
