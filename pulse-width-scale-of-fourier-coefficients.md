# Pulse-width scale of Fourier coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series of a periodic rectangular pulse](fourier-series-of-a-periodic-rectangular-pulse.md)

For the [Fourier series of a periodic rectangular pulse](fourier-series-of-a-periodic-rectangular-pulse.md), the [sinc function](sinc-function.md) is nearly one when $nw/T\ll1$ and has its first zero at $nw/T=1$. Thus narrowing a pulse increases the range of significant [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md). Only integer harmonic indices occur, so an envelope zero need not be an exactly vanishing coefficient.
