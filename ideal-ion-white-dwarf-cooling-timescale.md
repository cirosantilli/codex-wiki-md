# Ideal-ion white-dwarf cooling timescale

↑ **Parent:** [White dwarf](white-dwarf.md)

With carbon-ion [thermal energy](thermal-energy.md) $U=Mk_BT_c/(8m_u)$ and [luminosity](luminosity.md) $L=A(M/M_\odot)T_c^{7/2}$, the time between two core [temperatures](temperature-split.md) is $k_BM_\odot(T_f^{-5/2}-T_i^{-5/2})/(20m_uA)$. Late cooling is slow in this approximation. Extrapolating the formula does not prove a categorical observational absence of ultracool remnants, since atmosphere, crystallization and heat-capacity physics alter the simple model.
