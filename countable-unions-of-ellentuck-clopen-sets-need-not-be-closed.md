# Countable unions of Ellentuck clopen sets need not be closed

↑ **Parent:** [Ellentuck topology](ellentuck-topology.md)

In the [space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers](space-of-infinite-subsets-of-the-natural-numbers.md), the families $C_n=\{X:n\notin X\}$ are [clopen sets](clopen-set.md) in the [ordinary topology on infinite subsets](ordinary-topology-on-infinite-subsets.md) and thus in the finer [Ellentuck topology](ellentuck-topology.md). Their union is $[\mathbb N]^\omega\setminus\{\mathbb N\}$. Every basic [Ellentuck topology](ellentuck-topology.md) neighborhood of $\mathbb N$ contains $\mathbb N\setminus\{n\}$ for some sufficiently large $n$, so this union is not a [closed set](closed-set.md).
