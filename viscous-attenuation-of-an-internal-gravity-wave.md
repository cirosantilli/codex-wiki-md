# Viscous attenuation of an internal gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement pseudomomentum of an internal gravity wave](displacement-pseudomomentum-of-an-internal-gravity-wave.md)

With weak [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu$, zero buoyancy diffusion and locally unchanged [plane internal gravity wave](plane-internal-gravity-wave.md) polarization, the [displacement pseudomomentum of an internal gravity wave](displacement-pseudomomentum-of-an-internal-gravity-wave.md) obeys $P_t+\partial_z(c_{gz}P)=-\nu K^2P$. For constant [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md) and a stationary wave envelope, $P\propto|\widehat\sigma|^2$ proves the displayed amplitude decay. Decay is along the [group velocity](group-velocity.md), so $c_{gz}<0$ describes a source above the receiving level. In a slowly varying [shear flow](shear-flow.md), use [wave action](wave-action-fluid-dynamics.md) and the changing [intrinsic frequency](intrinsic-frequency.md) rather than treating $P/|\widehat\sigma|^2$ as constant.
