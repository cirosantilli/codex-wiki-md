# Heart rate variability

↑ **Parent:** [Heart rate](heart-rate.md)

Variation in the intervals between successive heartbeats. Its interpretation depends on the time scale, [breathing](pulmonary-ventilation.md) and recording conditions; it is not a context-free measure of a particular [emotion](emotion.md).
