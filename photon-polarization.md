# Photon polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Photon](photon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photon_polarization)

For a [photon](photon.md) in a fixed spatial mode, the two transverse [photon polarizations](photon-polarization.md) span a [qubit](qubit.md) space. A pure [photon polarization](photon-polarization.md) state is $\alpha|H\rangle+\beta|V\rangle$ with $|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1$, and a general state is a two-dimensional [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md). Its [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) gives the normalized Stokes parameters. Polarization-dependent path separation can correlate this internal degree of freedom with a path [qubit](qubit.md).
