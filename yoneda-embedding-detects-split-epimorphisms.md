# Yoneda embedding detects split epimorphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Yoneda embedding](yoneda-embedding.md)

For $f:A\to B$, the [Yoneda embedding](yoneda-embedding.md) sends $f$ to postcomposition $\mathcal C(-,A)\to\mathcal C(-,B)$. If this is epic in the [presheaf category](presheaf-category.md), its component at $B$ is surjective, so $1_B=fg$ for some $g:B\to A$. Conversely such a section makes the natural transformation split epic. Pointwise surjectivity follows directly by doubling the target presheaf along the image subpresheaf: the two inclusions agree on the image but differ on any element outside it.
