# Primary submodule

↑ **Parent:** [Primary decomposition](primary-decomposition.md)

A proper submodule $N\subsetneq M$ is primary when $rm\in N$ and $m\notin N$ imply $r^kM\subseteq N$ for some positive integer $k$.
