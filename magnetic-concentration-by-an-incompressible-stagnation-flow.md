# Magnetic concentration by an incompressible stagnation flow

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamo action](dynamo-action.md)

The linear [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) $\mathbf u=(-\omega x,-\omega y,2\omega z)$ compresses the transverse plane and stretches the axial direction. An axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) obeys $B_t-\omega rB_r=2\omega B+\eta\Delta_\perp B$. In [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md), its amplitude grows as $e^{2\omega t}$ while its transverse width decreases as $e^{-\omega t}$. Thus its [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) per unit axial length grows as $e^{2\omega t}$, even when opposite-polarity angular sectors give zero signed [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md). Finite [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) limits the amplification of localized finite-flux Gaussian fields.
