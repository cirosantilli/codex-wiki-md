# Reaction intermediate

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reaction_intermediate)

A [reaction intermediate](reaction-intermediate.md) is formed in one step of a [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md) and consumed in a later step, cancelling from the net [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md). A [reaction intermediate](reaction-intermediate.md) can connect the transformations in [energy coupling](energy-coupling.md); it is distinct from a [transition state](transition-state.md). See the [IUPAC Gold Book: intermediate](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/I03096).
