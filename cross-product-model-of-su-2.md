<h1 id="cross-product-model-of-su-2">Cross-product model of su(2)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Skew-Hermitian matrix](skew-hermitian-matrix.md)

The real vector space of traceless [skew-Hermitian matrices](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) of size two is the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak{su}(2)$. For the basis $M_1=\frac12\operatorname{diag}(i,-i)$, $M_2=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $M_3=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\i&0\end{pmatrix}$, cyclic [commutators](commutator.md) satisfy $[M_1,M_2]=M_3$. The map $a\mapsto\sum a_jM_j$ carries the [cross product](cross-product.md) to the [commutator](commutator.md). Every target is a single commutator: choose a unit vector perpendicular to its associated vector $c$ and use $u\times(c\times u)=c$.
