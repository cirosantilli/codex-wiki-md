# Homogeneous treatment effect

↑ **Parent:** [Causal effect](causal-effect.md)

A treatment effect is homogeneous when the relevant contrast of [potential outcomes](potential-outcome.md) is constant across units. For an additive treatment, this takes the form $Y(a)-Y(0)=\beta a$.
