<h1 id="real-determinant-one-mobius-orbits">Real determinant-one Möbius orbits</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md)

The [special linear group](special-linear-group.md) $SL_2(\mathbb R)$ acting by [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) has exactly three [group orbits](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md): the extended real line and the two open half-planes. The identity $\operatorname{Im}((az+b)/(cz+d))=\operatorname{Im}z/|cz+d|^2$ preserves these sets. Upper triangular determinant-one matrices send $i$ to any prescribed point of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), and similarly send $-i$ through the lower half-plane. The [stabilizer subgroups](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $i$ and $-i$ are both $SO(2)$.
