# Direct image sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md)

For a continuous map $f:X\to Y$ and a sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $X$, the direct image is defined by $(f_*\mathcal F)(V)=\mathcal F(f^{-1}V)$ for every open $V\subseteq Y$.

The direct image sheaf is the result of the [direct image functor](direct-image-functor.md): $(f_*\mathcal F)(V)=\mathcal F(f^{-1}V)$.
