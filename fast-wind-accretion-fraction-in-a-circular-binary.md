# Fast-wind accretion fraction in a circular binary

↑ **Parent:** [Wind mass transfer in a binary star](wind-mass-transfer-in-a-binary-star.md)

For a fast isotropic cold wind, the [mass density](density.md) at a companion at separation $a$ is $(-\dot M_d)/(4\pi a^2v_w)$. The ballistic capture model gives $\dot M_a/(-\dot M_d)=G^2M_a^2/(a^2v_w^4)$. Fast relative to orbital motion also makes the capture radius small compared with the separation.
