# Dust tail

↑ **Parent:** [Interplanetary dust](interplanetary-dust.md)

A dust tail is an extended distribution of grains released from a localized moving source. [Radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) changes the grains' orbits; their release times, grain properties and survival probabilities determine the spatial profile.
