# Collapse map of degree one onto a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md)

Choose an oriented embedded closed disc in a closed oriented positive-dimensional manifold and collapse its complement to a point. A target point away from the collapsed value has one preimage with local degree $+1$, proving global degree one by the [degree as a sum of local degrees](degree-as-a-sum-of-local-degrees.md).
