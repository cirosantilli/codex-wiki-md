# Electroweak doublet gauge-boson mass matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md)

For a [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md) with [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) $1/2$ and [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) $(0,v/\sqrt2)^T$, its [gauge-covariant kinetic term](gauge-covariant-kinetic-term.md) gives the neutral [gauge-boson mass matrix](gauge-boson-mass-matrix.md)

$$
M_{\mathrm{neutral}}^2=\frac{v^2}{4}\begin{pmatrix}g^2&-gg'\\-gg'&g'^2\end{pmatrix}
$$

in the $(W^3,B)$ basis. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are zero and $(g^2+g'^2)v^2/4$, and the [Weinberg angle](weinberg-angle.md) rotation gives the [photon](photon.md) and Z boson. The two charged [electroweak gauge bosons](electroweak-gauge-boson.md) have $m_W^2=g^2v^2/4$. The zero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) expresses the unbroken electric-charge [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md).
