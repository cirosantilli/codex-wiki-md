# Positive square root in a C-star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Positive element of a C-star algebra](positive-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md)

Every [Positive element of a C-star algebra](positive-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) $a$ has a unique positive element $a^{1/2}$ satisfying $(a^{1/2})^2=a$. It is obtained by applying the continuous functional calculus to $t\mapsto\sqrt t$ on $\sigma(a)\subseteq[0,\infty)$.
