# Minimally coupled scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field](scalar-field.md)

A real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) is minimally coupled to a [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) when its kinetic term uses the metric contraction of first derivatives and its [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) depends only on the field, without an explicit [scalar curvature](scalar-curvature.md) coupling. For [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$ the displayed [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) and volume density $\sqrt{-g}$ give $\Box\phi-V'(\phi)=0$. Its [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) is $T_{ab}=\nabla_a\phi\nabla_b\phi-g_{ab}[\tfrac12(\nabla\phi)^2+V]$. The [scalar stress-energy divergence identity](scalar-stress-energy-divergence-identity.md) explains its conservation on the field equation.
