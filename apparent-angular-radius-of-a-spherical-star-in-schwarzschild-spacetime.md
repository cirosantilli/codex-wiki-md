# Apparent angular radius of a spherical star in Schwarzschild spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md)

A spherical surface with [areal radius](areal-radius.md) $R>3m$, where $m=GM/c^2$, has apparent angular radius $\psi$ for a static exterior observer at $r_0>R$. Its grazing [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) has [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $b=R/\sqrt{1-2m/R}$. Projecting the photon tangent into the observer's local [rest frame](rest-frame.md) gives $\sin\psi=b\sqrt{1-2m/r_0}/r_0$, yielding the displayed result. The condition $R>3m$ ensures the grazing ray escapes, since the surface is outside the [photon sphere](photon-sphere.md). For $R=4m$, the distant image radius is $\sqrt2$ times the flat-space prediction.
