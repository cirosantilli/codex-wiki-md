# Charged-lepton Yukawa matrix diagonalization

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa matrix](yukawa-matrix.md)

A [singular value decomposition](singular-value-decomposition.md) of the charged-lepton [Yukawa matrix](yukawa-matrix.md) uses independent unitary rotations of left- and right-handed [leptons](lepton.md). Their [gauge-covariant kinetic terms](gauge-covariant-kinetic-term.md) are unchanged because each rotation mixes identical [Standard Model representations](standard-model-representation.md). After [electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md), the nonnegative singular values give [Dirac masses](dirac-mass-term.md) $m_i=y_iv/\sqrt2$ and diagonal [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) couplings $m_i/v$. When the [neutrinos](neutrino.md) are massless, rotating their left-handed fields with the charged partners preserves the diagonal [weak charged current](charged-current.md).
