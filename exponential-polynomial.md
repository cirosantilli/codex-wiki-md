# Exponential polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Entire function](entire-function.md)

An [exponential polynomial](exponential-polynomial.md) is a finite sum of [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) multiples of exponentials. For distinct exponents, its terms are linearly independent over the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) ring: to isolate one term, apply the product of operators $(D-\lambda_j)^{\deg P_j+1}$ for all the other terms. On the remaining [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) each operator becomes $D+\lambda_0-\lambda_j$, which is injective because its nonzero constant preserves the leading coefficient. A nonzero [exponential polynomial](exponential-polynomial.md) is an [entire function](entire-function.md) of exponential type. Its maximum-modulus [logarithm](logarithm.md) is $O(R+\log R)$, and [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md) gives at most $O(R)$ zeros, counted with multiplicity, in a disk of radius $R$.
