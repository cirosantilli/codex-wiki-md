# Baroclinic vorticity generation

↑ **Parent:** [Baroclinity](baroclinity.md)

Vorticity is generated when density and pressure gradients are not parallel. Under the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), the source is $\nabla(\rho'/\rho_0)\times\mathbf g$.
