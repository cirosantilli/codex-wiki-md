# Scaling violation

↑ **Parent:** [Bjorken scaling](bjorken-scaling.md)

Dependence of a [deep-inelastic structure function](deep-inelastic-structure-function.md) on $Q^2$ at fixed [Bjorken scaling variable](bjorken-scaling-variable.md). In [QCD](quantum-chromodynamics.md), radiation and [gluon splitting](gluon-splitting.md) evolve the [parton distribution functions](parton-distribution-function.md) with the resolution scale, producing logarithmic departures from the simplest [parton model](parton-model.md) scaling limit.
