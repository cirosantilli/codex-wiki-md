# Geodesic convexity

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)

A subset of a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) is geodesically convex when any two of its points can be joined by a minimizing [geodesic segment](geodesic-segment.md) lying in the subset. In the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) the joining [geodesic segment](geodesic-segment.md) is unique. Thus in the [Beltrami-Klein model](beltrami-klein-model.md), geodesically convex subsets correspond to Euclidean [convex sets](convex-set.md).
