# Sullivan model of the connected sum of two complex projective planes

↑ **Parent:** [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md)

The two quadratic relations form a [regular sequence](regular-sequence.md) in $\mathbb Q[a,b]$, so their [Koszul complex](koszul-complex.md) has only the quotient-ring [cohomology](cohomology-split.md). Represent the two degree-two classes by rational polynomial forms and choose primitives for the exact relations to obtain a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md). There are no further generators.
