# Salvetti complex

↑ **Parent:** [Right-angled Artin group](right-angled-artin-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvetti_complex)

The Salvetti complex $S_\Gamma$ has one vertex, one oriented loop for each vertex of $\Gamma$, and an $n$-torus cubulated by one $n$-cube for every $n$-clique of $\Gamma$. It is nonpositively curved and $\pi_1S_\Gamma\cong A_\Gamma$.
