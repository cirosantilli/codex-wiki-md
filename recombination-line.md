# Recombination line

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative recombination](radiative-recombination.md)

A recombination line is a [spectral line](spectral-line.md) produced after a free [electron](electron.md) is captured, often through a subsequent [radiative cascade](radiative-cascade.md) among bound levels. Its emissivity is computed with an [effective recombination coefficient](effective-recombination-coefficient.md), distinct from the free-bound recombination continuum.
