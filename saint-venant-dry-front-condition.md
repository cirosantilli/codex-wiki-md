# Saint-Venant dry-front condition

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-current front condition](gravity-current-front-condition.md)

The [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md) at a dry bed, with negligible ambient [mass density](density.md), end in a vanishing-depth edge, rather than a finite-depth [gravity current](gravity-current.md) head. In a prismatic triangular channel the [Riemann invariant](riemann-invariant.md) $u+4\sqrt{g'h/2}$ gives the speed $2\sqrt{2g'h_0}$ after release of a resting semi-infinite layer of depth $h_0$. In a rectangular channel the corresponding speed is $2\sqrt{g'h_0}$; the channel geometry matters.
