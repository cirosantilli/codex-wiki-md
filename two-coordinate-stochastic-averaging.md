# Two-coordinate stochastic averaging

↑ **Parent:** [Doubly stochastic matrix](doubly-stochastic-matrix.md)

This [doubly stochastic matrix](doubly-stochastic-matrix.md) replaces two values $a,b$ by $ta+(1-t)b$ and $(1-t)a+tb$, preserving their sum. It fixes all other coordinates when embedded in a larger [matrix](matrix.md). A finite product of such averaging [matrices](matrix.md) and [permutation matrices](permutation-matrix.md) is again doubly stochastic.
