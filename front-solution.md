# Front solution

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling wave](travelling-wave.md)

A front is a spatial profile connecting different asymptotic states on its two sides. A [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) front moves with a constant speed; a stationary front has zero speed. In a one-dimensional stationary reduction it is a [heteroclinic orbit](heteroclinic-orbit.md) between the corresponding homogeneous [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). Equal first-integral values are necessary for a stationary front in a scalar conservative spatial equation.
