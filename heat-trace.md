# Heat trace

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral geometry](spectral-geometry.md)

For the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) on a closed compact [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), the [heat trace](heat-trace.md) is $Z_M(t)=\sum_j e^{-t\lambda_j}=\int_M K_M(t,x,x)\,dV(x)$. It records every [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) with multiplicity. In a finite free isometric quotient it equals $|U|^{-1}\sum_{u\in U}\int_NK_N(t,x,ux)\,dV(x)$. The orbital integrals form a [class function](class-function.md) of the ambient finite isometry group, so [Gassmann equivalence](gassmann-equivalence.md) gives equal quotient [heat traces](heat-trace.md) and hence [Sunada theorem](sunada-theorem.md).
