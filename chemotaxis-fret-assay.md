# Chemotaxis FRET assay

↑ **Parent:** [Chemotaxis feedback regulation](chemotaxis-feedback-regulation.md)

The [chemotaxis FRET assay](chemotaxis-fret-assay.md) uses [Förster resonance energy transfer](forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md) between fluorescent tags on [CheY](chey.md) and [CheZ](chez.md) to report their association. Interpreting this emission [ratio](ratio.md) as [CheA](chea.md) activity requires the coupling between [CheY](chey.md) [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) and [CheZ](chez.md) binding described in the experimental sources above.
