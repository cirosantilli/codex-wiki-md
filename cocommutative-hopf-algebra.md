# Cocommutative Hopf algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md)

A [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md) is cocommutative when flipping the two tensor factors leaves its coproduct unchanged. A [group algebra](group-algebra.md) has coproduct $g\mapsto g\otimes g$, so it is cocommutative even for a nonabelian group, whose group algebra is not commutative.
