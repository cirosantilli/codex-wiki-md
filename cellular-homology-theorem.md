# Cellular homology theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md)

For a [CW complex](cw-complex.md), the [homology](homology-split.md) of its [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) naturally equals its [singular homology](singular-homology.md). The relative groups of successive skeleton pairs, free on the cells in the corresponding dimension and zero in other dimensions, yield this identification through their connecting maps.
