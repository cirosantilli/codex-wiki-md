# Main-sequence mass-radius relation

↑ **Parent:** [Main sequence](main-sequence.md)

An approximate relation between the mass and radius of [main-sequence stars](main-sequence-star.md) in a specified composition and mass range. It is not determined by [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) alone. For gas-pressure [stellar homology](stellar-homology.md) with [Kramers' opacity law](kramers-opacity-law.md), [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) gives $L\propto\mu^{15/2}M^{11/2}R_*^{-1/2}$. Substitution of $R_*\propto M^q$ gives $L\propto M^{(11-q)/2}$ at fixed composition, explaining precisely which extra radius assumption produces any claimed exponent.
