# Classification of subgroups of a dihedral group

↑ **Parent:** [Dihedral group](dihedral-group.md)

Write $D_{2n}=\langle r,s:r^n=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle$. A subgroup contained in the rotations is a [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md) $\langle r^d\rangle$, with $d\mid n$. If it contains a reflection, every other reflection differs from a chosen $r^js$ by a member of the rotational intersection, so it is $\langle r^d,r^js\rangle$, with $j$ specified modulo $d$. These subgroups have orders $n/d$ and $2n/d$ respectively. This gives a complete list, including the trivial and whole subgroups; see [Peter Cameron's group-theory exercise solutions, question1](https://maths.qmul.ac.uk/~pjc/MTH714U/so3.pdf).
