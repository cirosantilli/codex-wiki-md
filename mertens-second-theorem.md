# Mertens second theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Mertens' theorems](mertens-theorems.md)

There is an absolute constant $B_1$, the Meissel–Mertens constant, such that

$$
\sum_{p\leq x}\frac1p
=\log\log x+B_1+O\left(\frac1{\log x}\right).
$$
