# Standard skew tableau determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Standard skew Young tableau](standard-skew-young-tableau.md)

Here $n=|\lambda|-|\mu|$, with zero entries for negative factorial arguments. Extract the squarefree [monomial](monomial.md) $x_1\cdots x_n$ from the skew [Jacobi–Trudi identity](jacobi-trudi-identity.md). A product $h_{r_1}\cdots h_{r_\ell}$ contributes $n!/\prod r_i!$ because its distinct labels are assigned to the factors in that many ways. Summing [determinant](determinant.md) signs proves the formula, since a squarefree [semistandard Young tableau](semistandard-young-tableau.md) is a [standard skew Young tableau](standard-skew-young-tableau.md).
