# Liquid-drop model of the nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md)

A collective nuclear model that treats the nucleus as a nearly uniform fluid drop with short-range cohesive binding, surface energy and electrostatic self-energy. The [semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md) incorporates these contributions, together with asymmetry and pairing terms, to describe broad binding trends. Discrete [nuclear shell](nuclear-shell.md) structure requires additional physics.
