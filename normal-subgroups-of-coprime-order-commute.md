# Normal subgroups of coprime order commute

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)

If finite [normal subgroups](normal-subgroup.md) $H,K$ of a [group](group-split.md) have coprime orders, their intersection is trivial by [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md). For $h\in H$ and $k\in K$, the [commutator](commutator.md) $hkh^{-1}k^{-1}$ belongs to both subgroups: write it as $h(kh^{-1}k^{-1})$ to use normality of $H$, and as $(hkh^{-1})k^{-1}$ to use normality of $K$. Hence it is the identity and $hk=kh$. The product subgroup is therefore isomorphic to the [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) $H\times K$.
