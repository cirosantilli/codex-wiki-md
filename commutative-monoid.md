# Commutative monoid

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid](monoid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative_monoid)

A commutative monoid is a [monoid](monoid.md) whose operation is commutative. The natural numbers with addition form a commutative monoid that is not an [abelian group](abelian-group.md), because positive elements have no additive inverses.
