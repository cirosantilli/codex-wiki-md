# Square-pulse resonance profile

↑ **Parent:** [Time-dependent perturbation theory](time-dependent-perturbation-theory.md)

A constant-amplitude drive lasting $T$ gives the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) in the title, where $\operatorname{sinc}x=\sin x/x$ and its value at zero is one. The [transition probability](transition-probability.md) in weak driving has a finite resonant maximum, first zeros at $\delta=\pm2\pi/T$, and decreasing side lobes. Longer duration increases the peak as $T^2$ and narrows its width as $1/T$. The [rotating-wave approximation](rotating-wave-approximation.md) discards a rapidly oscillating counterrotating term; it can have a small absolute error but a large relative error at the resonant term's zeros.
