# Similarity ansatz

↑ **Parent:** [Similarity solution](similarity-solution.md)

A similarity ansatz assumes a profile invariant under a prescribed rescaling, commonly in the displayed power-law form. Substitution into a [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) fixes compatible exponents and can reduce it to an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) for $F$. Boundary conditions and conserved quantities must also be compatible with the scaling; the ansatz alone does not establish existence of a solution.
