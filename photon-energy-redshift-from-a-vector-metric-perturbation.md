# Photon energy redshift from a vector metric perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Vector cosmological perturbation](vector-cosmological-perturbation.md)

For the metric in [vector cosmological perturbation](vector-cosmological-perturbation.md), an [orthonormal tetrad](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) is $E_0=a^{-1}\partial_\eta$, $E_i=a^{-1}(B_i\partial_\eta+\partial_i)$ to first order. A [photon](photon.md) has $p^0=(\epsilon/a^2)(1+B_ie^i)$ and $p^i=(\epsilon/a^2)e^i$, so the covariant component is $p_0=-\epsilon+O(B^2)$. The covariant [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) gives $dp_0/d\eta=(2p^0)^{-1}\partial_\eta g_{\alpha\beta}p^\alpha p^\beta=\epsilon e^i\dot B_i$. The [derivative](derivative.md) of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) cancels by the [null vector](null-vector.md) condition, proving the redshift formula.
