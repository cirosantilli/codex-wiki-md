# Angular distribution of electric dipole radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Electric dipole radiation](electric-dipole-radiation.md)

If $\theta$ is the angle between the observation direction and $\ddot p$, then

$$
\frac{dP}{d\Omega}
=\frac{\mu_0}{16\pi^2c}|\ddot p|^2\sin^2\theta.
$$

Integrating over [solid angle](solid-angle.md) uses $\int\sin^2\theta\,d\Omega=8\pi/3$ and gives the total [electric dipole radiation](electric-dipole-radiation.md) power.
