# Foot of a perpendicular

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md)

The foot from $\mathbf c$ to the [line](straight-line.md) $\mathbf a+t\mathbf u$, with $\mathbf u\ne0$, is its [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto that [line](straight-line.md). Solving $(\mathbf c-\mathbf a-t\mathbf u)\cdot\mathbf u=0$ gives the displayed formula and proves uniqueness. It belongs to the [line segment](line-segment.md) with endpoints $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf a+\mathbf u$ exactly when $0\leq(\mathbf c-\mathbf a)\cdot\mathbf u\leq\|\mathbf u\|^2$.
