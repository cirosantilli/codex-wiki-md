# Bott-Chern cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md)

For a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md), Bott-Chern cohomology is

$$
H_{BC}^{p,q}(M)=\frac{\ker\partial\cap\ker\bar\partial\subseteq\mathcal A^{p,q}(M)}{\partial\bar\partial\mathcal A^{p-1,q-1}(M)}.
$$

Its classes are represented by $d$-closed forms of pure type. A natural map sends a Bott-Chern class to its [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md) class.
