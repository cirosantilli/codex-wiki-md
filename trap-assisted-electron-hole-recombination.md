# Trap-assisted electron-hole recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Nonradiative recombination](nonradiative-recombination.md)

A localized electronic defect can capture a conduction [electron](electron.md) and subsequently an [electron hole](electron-hole.md), or the reverse sequence, returning to its original charge state. Energy is transferred to the lattice rather than an emitted [photon](photon.md). The two-step process can remove an [electron-hole pair](electron-hole-pair.md) even when direct band-to-band photon emission is inefficient. Its rate depends on trap density, occupation and both capture rates; a single trapping event is not necessarily completed recombination.
