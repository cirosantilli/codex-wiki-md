# Density-power cylindrical moment of inertia

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

For a solid cylinder with [mass density](density.md) $\rho(r)=kr^p$ and $p>-2$, integrating cylindrical shells gives $M=2\pi LkR^{p+2}/(p+2)$ and axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I_z=2\pi LkR^{p+4}/(p+4)$. Their ratio proves the displayed formula. It gives the uniform-cylinder result $MR^2/2$ at p=0 and $2MR^2/3$ at p=2. The radial density weighting must enter both mass and inertia integrals.
