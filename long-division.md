# Long division

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean division](euclidean-division.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_division)

Repeated [division with remainder](euclidean-division.md) that determines successive digits of a [quotient](quotient.md). To generate the fractional digits in base $b$, multiply the current [remainder](remainder.md) by $b$, divide by the [denominator](denominator.md), and keep the resulting [remainder](remainder.md) for the next step.
