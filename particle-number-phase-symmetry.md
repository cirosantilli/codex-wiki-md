# Particle-number phase symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md)

Conservation of particle number corresponds to global [circle group](circle-group.md) phase rotations of a nonrelativistic Bose field. A positive-density uniform mean-field saddle chooses a phase, with degenerate saddle manifold a circle. The associated [Goldstone boson](goldstone-boson.md) is the long-wavelength phase mode. This is a thermodynamic symmetry-selection statement; an exact finite-volume number [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) does not itself choose a phase.
