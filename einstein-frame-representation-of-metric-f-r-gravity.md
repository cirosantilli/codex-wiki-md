<h1 id="einstein-frame-representation-of-metric-f-r-gravity">Einstein-frame representation of metric f(R) gravity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [f(R) gravity](f-r-gravity.md)

In the mostly-minus convention with vacuum gravitational action proportional to $\int\sqrt{-g}f(R)$ and $f'<0$, the displayed [conformal transformation](conformal-map.md) turns its vacuum equation into $\widetilde G_{\mu\nu}=T_{\mu\nu}(\phi)$, with potential $V=(Rf'-f)/(2f'^2)$ and the [stress-energy tensor of a canonical scalar field](stress-energy-tensor-of-a-canonical-scalar-field.md). The conformal Ricci formula cancels the second derivatives of $f'$ against the original field equation. Its remaining derivative tensor is $\partial_\mu\phi\partial_\nu\phi-\widetilde g_{\mu\nu}(\widetilde\nabla\phi)^2/2$. Expressing $V$ as a function of $\phi$ requires a locally invertible $R\mapsto f'(R)$. When $f''=0$, this scalar-tensor parametrization degenerates and introduces no new propagating scalar degree of freedom.
