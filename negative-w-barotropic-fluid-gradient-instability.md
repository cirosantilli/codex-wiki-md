# Negative-w barotropic-fluid gradient instability

↑ **Parent:** [Dark energy](dark-energy.md)

A propagating adiabatic fluid with the constant closure $p=w\rho$ has $c_s^2=dp/d\rho=w$. For $-1<w<0$, a short-wavelength Fourier perturbation obeys $\omega^2\simeq wk_{\rm phys}^2<0$, producing gradient growth. This does not exclude a negative-pressure perfect-fluid stress tensor with nonadiabatic pressure response, or a canonical scalar with rest-frame [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) one. A [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) has no independent propagating fluid density mode.
