# Scaling function

↑ **Parent:** [Multiresolution analysis](multiresolution-analysis.md)

A scaling function generates the approximation space $V_0$ of a [multiresolution analysis](multiresolution-analysis.md) by its integer translates. In an orthonormal construction those translates form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), and $\varphi_{j,k}(x)=2^{j/2}\varphi(2^jx-k)$ generate $V_j$. Integrable orthonormal [scaling functions](scaling-function.md) have $|\widehat\varphi(0)|=1$, as follows from density and the [MRA projection Fourier identity](mra-projection-fourier-identity.md).
