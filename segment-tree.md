# Segment tree

↑ **Parent:** [Data structure](data-structure-split.md)

A [binary tree](binary-tree.md) organizing a fixed sequence into recursively subdivided contiguous intervals. Each node stores an aggregate for its interval. Range queries and point updates visit logarithmically many nodes; range-update variants store additional information such as a coverage count to maintain the length of a union of active intervals.
