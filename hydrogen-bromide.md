# Hydrogen bromide

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen halide](hydrogen-halide.md)

The gaseous [hydrogen halide](hydrogen-halide.md) consisting of one [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) atom and one [bromine](bromine.md) atom. Its [molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md) can be studied through its [vibrational fundamental](vibrational-fundamental.md) and weak [vibrational overtones](vibrational-overtone.md). The aqueous acid and the isolated [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md) should be distinguished when specifying a dissociation process.[Ground-state diatomic constants](hydrogen-bromide-constants-of-diatomic-molecules-by-klaus-p-huber-and-others.md)
