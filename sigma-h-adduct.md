# Sigma-H adduct

↑ **Parent:** [Vicarious nucleophilic substitution](vicarious-nucleophilic-substitution.md)

A [sigma-H adduct](sigma-h-adduct.md) forms by nucleophilic addition at an aromatic [carbon](carbon.md) that initially bears [hydrogen](hydrogen.md). Both the original [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and the incoming [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) remain attached at that [carbon](carbon.md) before the rearomatization steps.
