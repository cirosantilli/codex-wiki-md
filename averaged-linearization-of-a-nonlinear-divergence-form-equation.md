# Averaged linearization of a nonlinear divergence-form equation

↑ **Parent:** [Quasilinear partial differential equation](quasilinear-partial-differential-equation.md)

For a $C^1$ flux $F$ and a [weak solution](weak-solution.md) of $\operatorname{div}F(Du)=0$, [discrete integration by parts](discrete-integration-by-parts.md) and the [fundamental theorem of calculus along a line segment](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus-along-a-line-segment.md) show that $w_h=\delta_{\ell,-h}u$ solves $\operatorname{div}(A_hDw_h)=0$, with $p_-=Du(x-he_\ell)$ and $p_+=Du(x)$. Coercive quadratic-form bounds and [operator norm](operator-norm.md) bounds for $DF$ on all segments between these [gradients](gradient.md) pass to $A_h$. This produces a [divergence-form elliptic operator](divergence-form-elliptic-operator.md) without initially assuming second [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) of $u$.
