# Velocity autocorrelation function

↑ **Parent:** [Mean squared displacement](mean-squared-displacement.md)

The unnormalized [autocorrelation function](autocorrelation.md) of a stationary [velocity](velocity.md). For zero [mean](expected-value-split.md), $\chi(0)$ is the [velocity](velocity.md) [variance](variance-split.md). It is different from the normalized [autocorrelation function](autocorrelation.md) $\chi(u)/\chi(0)$ often used in [time series](time-series-split.md).

If $X(t)-X(0)=\int_0^tV(s)\,ds$, integrating the two-time [covariance](covariance.md) over the square $[0,t]^2$ gives

$$
\mathbb E[(X(t)-X(0))^2]=2\int_0^t(t-u)\chi(u)\,du.
$$

This identity connects a [mean squared displacement](mean-squared-displacement.md) to the [Green-Kubo formula for diffusion](green-kubo-formula-for-diffusion.md).
