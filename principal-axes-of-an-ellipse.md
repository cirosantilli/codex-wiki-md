# Principal axes of an ellipse

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipse](ellipse.md)

The [principal axes of an ellipse](principal-axes-of-an-ellipse.md) are the two perpendicular symmetry axes through its [center of an ellipse](center-of-an-ellipse.md). For a [symmetric positive-definite matrix](symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md) $M$, its orthonormal [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) give those directions and its positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_1,\lambda_2$ give semiaxis lengths $\lambda_1^{-1/2},\lambda_2^{-1/2}$. This follows by [orthogonal diagonalization](orthogonal-diagonalization-of-a-real-symmetric-matrix.md) of the defining [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md). In $\xi^2/A^2+\upsilon^2/B^2=1$, the axes are already horizontal and vertical, with semiaxes $|A|,|B|$ and full lengths $2|A|,2|B|$. Equal semiaxes give a [circle](circle.md), for which every perpendicular pair through the center can serve.
