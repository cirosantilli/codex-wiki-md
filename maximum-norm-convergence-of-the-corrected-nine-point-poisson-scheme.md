# Maximum-norm convergence of the corrected nine-point Poisson scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Fourth-order correction of the nine-point Poisson stencil](fourth-order-correction-of-the-nine-point-poisson-stencil.md)

On the unit square with exact [Dirichlet boundary data](dirichlet-boundary-data.md), let $D_9U=B_hf$ and suppose the normalized residual on the exact smooth solution has [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) at most $\varepsilon$. The error satisfies $D_9e=-\tau$ and vanishes on the boundary. The barrier $q=[x(1-x)+y(1-y)]/4$ has $D_9q=-1$, $q\ge0$ on the boundary, and maximum $1/8$. Apply the [discrete maximum principle](discrete-maximum-principle.md) to $e-\varepsilon q$ and $-e-\varepsilon q$, obtaining $|e|\le\varepsilon q$. Thus $\|e\|_\infty\le\varepsilon/8$, and fourth-order residual gives fourth-order [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) nodal convergence. The proof needs a uniform smoothness bound and a compatible boundary discretization.
