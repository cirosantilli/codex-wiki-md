<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [spin commutation relations](../../../../../spin-commutation-relations.md) give $[S_{\rm tot}^z,S_n^\pm]=\pm S_n^\pm$. Using the product rule for a [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md),

$$
[S_{\rm tot}^z,S_n^+S_{n+1}^-]
=S_n^+S_{n+1}^--S_n^+S_{n+1}^-=0,
$$

and the conjugate term also vanishes. The longitudinal field commutes with every $S_n^z$, so **$[H,S_{\rm tot}^z]=0$**. The [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is invariant under the global [spin rotation](../../../../../spin-rotation.md) $U(\theta)=e^{-i\theta S_{\rm tot}^z}$: its [continuous symmetry](../../../../../continuous-symmetry.md) group is $U(1)$. Simultaneous [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and total-magnetization [eigenstates](../../../../../eigenstate.md) can be chosen, and evolution preserves each fixed-magnetization sector. Local [magnetization](../../../../../magnetization.md) can still flow between sites; generally $[H,S_n^z]\ne0$. In the [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) the conserved number will be $N_f=N/2-S_{\rm tot}^z$.
