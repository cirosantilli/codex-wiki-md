<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a bulk bond, $K_{n+1}=K_n(1-2n_n)$ and $c_n(1-2n_n)=-c_n$. Reordering the remaining different-site operators then gives

$$
S_n^+S_{n+1}^-=c_{n+1}^\dagger c_n,\qquad
S_n^-S_{n+1}^+=c_n^\dagger c_{n+1}\quad(n<N).
$$

The periodic [spin](../../../../../spin.md) [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) requires separate attention. Set $P=\prod_{n=1}^N(1-2n_n)=(-1)^{N_f}$. The end bond becomes $S_N^+S_1^-=-P c_1^\dagger c_N$, with the conjugate relation for its adjoint. Consequently the exact finite-ring [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is

$$
\boxed{H=-\frac{hN}{2}+h\sum_{n=1}^Nn_n
-\frac J2\sum_{n=1}^{N-1}(c_n^\dagger c_{n+1}+c_{n+1}^\dagger c_n)
+\frac{JP}{2}(c_N^\dagger c_1+c_1^\dagger c_N).}
$$

[fermion parity](../../../../../fermion-parity.md) is conserved, so diagonalize in a sector $P=\pm1$. There the end bond can be written like every other bond provided $c_{N+1}=-Pc_1$. This [parity-dependent boundary condition of the Jordan-Wigner chain](../../../../../parity-dependent-boundary-condition-of-the-jordan-wigner-chain.md) is **antiperiodic for even [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) number and periodic for odd [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) number**. Simply assuming periodic [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) in both sectors changes the finite-size spectrum.
