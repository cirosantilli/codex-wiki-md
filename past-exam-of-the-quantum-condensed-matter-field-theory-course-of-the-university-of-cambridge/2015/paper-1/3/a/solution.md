<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a normal-ordered bosonic [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md), insert coherent-state resolutions between short imaginary-time evolution factors. The overlaps generate $\bar\psi\partial_\tau\psi$ and the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) symbol is obtained by replacing creation and [annihilation operators](../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) by $\bar\psi$ and $\psi$. Including the [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) gives, in the stated $\hbar=1$ convention,

$$
\boxed{S=\int_0^\beta d\tau\int_Vd^dr\left[
\bar\psi\left(\partial_\tau-\frac{\nabla^2}{2m}-\mu\right)\psi
+\frac g2(\bar\psi\psi)^2\right].}
$$

The [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) imposes **periodic imaginary-time [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md)**,

$$
\psi(\mathbf r,\beta)=\psi(\mathbf r,0),\qquad
\bar\psi(\mathbf r,\beta)=\bar\psi(\mathbf r,0),
$$

so bosonic [Matsubara frequencies](../../../../../matsubara-frequency.md) are $\omega_n=2\pi n/\beta$. The two complex coherent-state integration variables are treated independently before imposing the physical saddle contour. There is no extra zero-point ordering term: the supplied interaction is already normal ordered.

The spatial confinement is not specified beyond the volume. The uniform bulk results below use a homogeneous box with periodic spatial boundaries, or the interior of a large vessel where boundary effects can be neglected. A hard wall instead requires a vanishing field at that wall and an inhomogeneous saddle near it. Temporal periodicity follows from the [thermal trace](../../../../../thermal-trace.md) and does not by itself impose periodic spatial boundaries.
