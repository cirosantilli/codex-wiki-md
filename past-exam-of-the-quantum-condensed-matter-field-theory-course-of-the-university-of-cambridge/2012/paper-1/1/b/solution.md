<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $n_n=c_n^\dagger c_n$ and $P_n=\prod_{m<n}(1-2n_m)$. The [Jordan–Wigner transformation](../../../../../jordan-wigner-transformation.md) gives $2S_n^x=P_n(c_n+c_n^\dagger)$ and $P_{n+1}=P_n(1-2n_n)$. The [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) imply

$$
(c_n+c_n^\dagger)(1-2n_n)=c_n^\dagger-c_n.
$$

Consequently the [bondwise cancellation of a Jordan-Wigner string](../../../../../bondwise-cancellation-of-a-jordan-wigner-string.md) yields

$$
4S_n^xS_{n+1}^x=(c_n^\dagger-c_n)(c_{n+1}^\dagger+c_{n+1}),\qquad
2S_n^z=1-2n_n.
$$

Substitution therefore gives

$$
\boxed{H=-J\sum_n\left[(c_n^\dagger-c_n)(c_{n+1}^\dagger+c_{n+1})+g(1-2n_n)\right].}
$$

The [finite-ring parity qualification of an Ising quasiparticle gap](../../../../../finite-ring-parity-qualification-of-an-ising-quasiparticle-gap.md) matters here. For an exact periodic spin chain, the closing bond retains total [fermion parity](../../../../../fermion-parity.md). The [parity-dependent boundary condition of the Jordan-Wigner chain](../../../../../parity-dependent-boundary-condition-of-the-jordan-wigner-chain.md) is $c_{N+1}=-Pc_1$, with $P=(-1)^{N_f}$. Fixing that parity determines the allowed Fourier grid, and the resulting many-body states must still have the chosen parity. Dropping this boundary issue preserves the thermodynamic energy density, but can lose a degeneracy or the correct finite-ring excitation gap.
