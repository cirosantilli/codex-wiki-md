<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a real, even interaction $V_{-\mathbf k}=V_{\mathbf k}$. Let $\mathcal V=L^3$ and $N_{\rm ex}=\sum_{\mathbf k\ne0}b^\dagger_{\mathbf k}b_{\mathbf k}$. In the [Bogoliubov theory](../../../../../bogoliubov-theory.md) expansion, keep terms with zero or two noncondensed operators. The zero-[momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) term gives $V_0N_0(N_0-1)/(2\mathcal V)$. The terms with two noncondensed operators give

$$
\sum_{\mathbf k\ne0}\left[\frac{N_0}{\mathcal V}(V_0+V_{\mathbf k})b^\dagger_{\mathbf k}b_{\mathbf k}+\frac{N_0V_{\mathbf k}}{2\mathcal V}\left(b^\dagger_{\mathbf k}b^\dagger_{-\mathbf k}+b_{-\mathbf k}b_{\mathbf k}\right)\right].
$$

Here $V_0$ is the direct condensate interaction and $V_{\mathbf k}$ is its exchange contribution. At fixed total [particle number](../../../../../particle-number.md), $N_0=N-N_{\rm ex}$, so

$$
\frac{V_0N_0^2}{2\mathcal V}=\frac{V_0N^2}{2\mathcal V}-nV_0N_{\rm ex}+O(N_{\rm ex}^2/\mathcal V).
$$

The second term cancels the direct normal term. Adding the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) yields the [number-conserving Bogoliubov quadratic Hamiltonian](../../../../../number-conserving-bogoliubov-quadratic-hamiltonian.md)

$$
\boxed{H_2=E_{\rm mf}+\sum_{\mathbf k\ne0}\left[A_k b^\dagger_{\mathbf k}b_{\mathbf k}+\frac{B_k}{2}\left(b^\dagger_{\mathbf k}b^\dagger_{-\mathbf k}+b_{-\mathbf k}b_{\mathbf k}\right)\right],\quad
E_{\rm mf}=\frac{\mathcal V V_0n^2}{2},\quad A_k=\epsilon_k+nV_k,\quad B_k=nV_k.}
$$

The constant is the condensate [mean-field theory](../../../../../mean-field-theory.md) interaction [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md); the normal term describes moving a particle out of the condensate, while the anomalous term creates or annihilates an opposite-[momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) pair by exchanging two particles with the condensate. Total [particle number](../../../../../particle-number.md) remains fixed in the underlying theory; the condensate amplitudes in this notation are treated as classical amplitudes.

For [condensate fluctuation power counting](../../../../../condensate-fluctuation-power-counting.md), put $\delta=N_{\rm ex}/N\ll1$. Formal interaction-amplitude counting gives $H_2-E_{\rm mf}\sim E_{\rm mf}\delta$, cubic terms $\sim E_{\rm mf}\delta^{3/2}$ and quartic terms $\sim E_{\rm mf}\delta^2$; these are not uniform bounds on individual modes. Thus the latter are down by $\sqrt\delta$ and $\delta$ relative to the quadratic interaction terms. The $N_0-1$ correction is relative $1/N$. Homogeneity eliminates terms linear in nonzero-[momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) operators. Small depletion and weak interactions, not low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) alone, justify the truncation.
