<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the [Bogoliubov quasiparticle](../../../../../bogoliubov-quasiparticle.md) vacuum, $\alpha_{\mathbf k}|0_\alpha\rangle=0$, but the inverse [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md) gives $\langle b^\dagger_{\mathbf k}b_{\mathbf k}\rangle=v_k^2$. Consequently the [quantum depletion of a Bose condensate](../../../../../quantum-depletion-of-a-bose-condensate.md) is

$$
\boxed{n-n_0=\frac1{\mathcal V}\sum_{\mathbf k\ne0}v_k^2=\frac1{2\mathcal V}\sum_{\mathbf k\ne0}\left(\frac{\epsilon_k+nV_k}{\sqrt{\epsilon_k(\epsilon_k+2nV_k)}}-1\right).}
$$

**This expression assumes zero temperature.** At nonzero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), thermal [Bogoliubov quasiparticles](../../../../../bogoliubov-quasiparticle.md) add to the depletion:

$$
 n-n_0=\frac1{\mathcal V}\sum_{\mathbf k\ne0}\left[v_k^2+\frac{A_k}{E_k}\frac1{e^{\beta E_k}-1}\right].
$$

Thus the printed formula is the quantum contribution, or a low-temperature approximation when the thermal contribution is negligible. It is nonnegative for a stable quadratic [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) and vanishes in the noninteracting zero-temperature limit.
