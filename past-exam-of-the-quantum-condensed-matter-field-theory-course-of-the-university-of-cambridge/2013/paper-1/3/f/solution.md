<h1 id="3/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The constant term in the spin sum cancels. Linearizing the [saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md) equation gives

$$
M=UM\chi_0+O(M^3),\qquad \chi_0=\int d\epsilon\,\nu(\epsilon)[-\partial_\epsilon n_F(\epsilon)].
$$

The [paramagnetic auxiliary-field curvature](../../../../../paramagnetic-auxiliary-field-curvature.md) is

$$
\frac{S_{\rm eff}(M)-S_{\rm eff}(0)}{\beta N}=\frac12(U^{-1}-\chi_0)M^2+O(M^4).
$$

Consequently the [Stoner criterion](../../../../../stoner-criterion.md) for the loss of local paramagnetic stability is

$$
\boxed{U>U_c,\qquad U_c\chi_0=1,\qquad U_c\nu(\mu)\simeq1\quad(T\to0).}
$$

The printed $\nu(0)$ uses energy measured from the [Fermi level](../../../../../fermi-level.md). For a smooth [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md), $-n_F'$ becomes a narrow unit-area peak at $\mu$; a singular density of states or appreciable temperature requires the integral form. The curvature establishes local instability, not the order or location of every possible thermodynamic transition.

With exact spin-rotation symmetry, [itinerant ferromagnetism](../../../../../itinerant-ferromagnetism.md) selects a magnetization direction. The two broken transverse generators form **one quadratic [type-B Goldstone boson](../../../../../type-b-goldstone-boson.md)**, a long-wave [ferromagnetic magnon](../../../../../ferromagnetic-magnon.md) with $E_q\sim Dq^2$. Longitudinal amplitude motion is not symmetry-protected to be gapless. In a metallic ordered state, particle-hole excitations remain near each surviving exchange-split [Fermi surface](../../../../../fermi-surface.md); these are distinct from the collective magnon. The [itinerant spin-flip particle-hole continuum](../../../../../itinerant-spin-flip-particle-hole-continuum.md) starts at exchange scale $2|M|$ at $q=0$ in this simple mean-field spectrum, while its finite-$q$ boundaries differ. Spin anisotropy, spin-orbit coupling or an external field can gap the transverse mode; none is present in the stated model.
