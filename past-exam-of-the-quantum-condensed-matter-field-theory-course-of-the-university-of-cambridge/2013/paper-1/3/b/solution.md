<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expand the [Grassmann fields](../../../../../grassmann-field.md) in fermionic [Matsubara frequencies](../../../../../matsubara-frequency.md) $\omega_l=(2l+1)\pi/\beta$. For $U=0$, the [Grassmann Gaussian integral](../../../../../grassmann-gaussian-integral.md) factorizes into thermal [fermionic one-level thermal determinants](../../../../../fermionic-one-level-thermal-determinant.md):

$$
Z_0=\prod_{k,\sigma}(1+e^{-\beta\xi_k})=\prod_k(1+e^{-\beta\xi_k})^2.
$$

Each factor counts the empty and occupied states of one spin orbital. Equivalently, the regularized [Matsubara sum](../../../../../bosonic-matsubara-sum.md) gives the logarithm of that factor; the raw infinite determinant requires [thermal normalization of a fermion determinant](../../../../../thermal-normalization-of-a-fermion-determinant.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Omega_0=-\frac1\beta\ln Z_0=-\frac2\beta\sum_k\ln(1+e^{-\beta(\epsilon_k-\mu)}).}
$$

**The printed $F$ is the grand potential $\Omega$**, because the trace contains $H-\mu\hat N$. The [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) at fixed mean [particle number](../../../../../particle-number.md) is $F_{\rm H}=\Omega+\mu\langle\hat N\rangle$.
