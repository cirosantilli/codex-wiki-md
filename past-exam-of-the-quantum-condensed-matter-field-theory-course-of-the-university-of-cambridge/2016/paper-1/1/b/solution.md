<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $[a,a^\dagger]=1$, $n=a^\dagger a$, and take $S^+=(S^-)^\dagger=\sqrt{2S-n}\,a$. The physical [Holstein–Primakoff occupation constraint](../../../../../holstein-primakoff-occupation-constraint.md) limits the [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), which contains only $n=0,1,\ldots,2S$. Acting in this number basis gives

$$
S^-|n\rangle=\sqrt{(n+1)(2S-n)}|n+1\rangle,\qquad S^+|n\rangle=\sqrt{n(2S-n+1)}|n-1\rangle.
$$

The upper and lower endpoints are annihilated as required. Consequently

$$
[S^+,S^-]|n\rangle=\{(n+1)(2S-n)-n(2S-n+1)\}|n\rangle=2(S-n)|n\rangle=\boxed{2S^z|n\rangle}.
$$

The change in $n$ also gives $[S^z,S^\pm]=\pm S^\pm$. These [matrix elements](../../../../../matrix-element.md) prove the [spin](../../../../../spin.md) algebra on the full physical spin-$S$ space. Extending the oscillator space to arbitrary occupation would not give a physical finite [spin](../../../../../spin.md): the occupation constraint and operator ordering in the square root matter. [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) on distinct sites have $[a_m,a_n^\dagger]=\delta_{mn}$ and all other site [commutators](../../../../../commutator.md) zero.
