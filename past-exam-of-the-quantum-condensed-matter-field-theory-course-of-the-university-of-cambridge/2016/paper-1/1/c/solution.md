<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Rotate the [spin](../../../../../spin.md) on one sublattice through $\pi$ about its $x$ axis, using the unitary product of the corresponding spin-rotation operators. On a rotated site the old components obey $S^x_{\rm old}=S^x$, $S^y_{\rm old}=-S^y$, $S^z_{\rm old}=-S^z$. Every bond joins opposite sublattices, so

$$
S^x_{m,\rm old}S^x_{m+1,\rm old}+S^y_{m,\rm old}S^y_{m+1,\rm old}=S^x_mS^x_{m+1}-S^y_mS^y_{m+1}=\frac12(S_m^+S_{m+1}^++S_m^-S_{m+1}^-).
$$

The longitudinal product changes sign. Hence **the rotated bond is $-J\Delta S_m^zS_{m+1}^z+\tfrac J2(S_m^+S_{m+1}^++S_m^-S_{m+1}^-)$**, giving the requested form. The canonical [spin](../../../../../spin.md) algebra is unchanged because the transformation is unitary. A staggered easy-axis reference configuration is now the uniform all-up state. At $\alpha=0$ one first chooses a Néel direction, here $z$, before performing the same rotation.
