# Convergence theorem for moment-generating functions

↑ **Parent:** [Moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md)

Suppose the [moment-generating functions](moment-generating-function.md) $M_n$ are finite on a common open [interval](interval-mathematics.md) $(-a,a)$ containing zero and converge there pointwise to the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) $M_X$ of a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$, with $M_X$ finite there. Then $X_n$ tends to $X$ in [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Equivalently, a finite pointwise limit on such an interval is the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) of a unique limiting [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md). This theorem requires control on a neighborhood of zero; convergence of isolated [moments](moment.md) is a different hypothesis.
