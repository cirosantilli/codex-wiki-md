# Completed nonholomorphic Eisenstein series

↑ **Parent:** [Nonholomorphic Eisenstein series](nonholomorphic-eisenstein-series.md)

This [modular-invariant function](modular-invariant-function.md), initially defined for $\Re s>1$, has [Fourier expansion](fourier-series-split.md)

$$
\mathcal E(z,s)=\Xi(2s)y^s+\Xi(2s-1)y^{1-s}+2\sqrt y\sum_{n\ne0}|n|^{s-1/2}\sigma_{1-2s}(|n|)K_{s-1/2}(2\pi|n|y)e^{2\pi inx},
$$

where $\Xi(u)=\pi^{-u/2}\Gamma(u/2)\zeta(u)$ is the [completed Riemann zeta function](completed-riemann-zeta-function.md), without the entire-xi polynomial factor. [Poisson summation](poisson-summation-formula.md) and the [integral representation of the modified Bessel function of the second kind](integral-representation-of-the-modified-bessel-function-of-the-second-kind.md) prove the expansion. It gives [meromorphic continuation](meromorphic-continuation.md), $\mathcal E(z,s)=\mathcal E(z,1-s)$, and simple [poles](pole.md) at zero and one with residues $-1/2,1/2$. Apparent poles at $s=1/2$ cancel between the two constant terms.
