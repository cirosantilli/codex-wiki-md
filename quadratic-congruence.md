# Quadratic congruence

↑ **Parent:** [Modular congruence](modular-congruence.md)

A quadratic congruence is a [modular congruence](modular-congruence.md) defined by a degree-two [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), with solutions sought among [residue classes](residue-class.md). For odd $n$ and $a$ invertible modulo $n$, completing the square gives $(2ax+b)^2\equiv b^2-4ac\pmod n$. A [Jacobi symbol](jacobi-symbol.md) of $-1$ for a [coprime](coprime-integers.md) proposed square rules out solutions, whereas a [Jacobi symbol](jacobi-symbol.md) of $1$ alone does not establish solubility for a composite modulus.
