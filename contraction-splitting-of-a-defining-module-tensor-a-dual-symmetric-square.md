# Contraction splitting of a defining module tensor a dual symmetric square

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric powers of the defining sln representation](symmetric-powers-of-the-defining-sln-representation.md)

For the defining $n$-dimensional [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md) module, with $n\geq2$, write $T_i(p)=e_i\otimes p(y_1,\ldots,y_n)$. The equivariant contraction is $C(T_i(p))=\partial_{y_i}p$. The map $\iota(f)=\sum_i e_i\otimes y_i f$ is also equivariant, since $\sum_i e_i\otimes y_i$ is an invariant identity tensor. Direct differentiation gives $C\iota=(n+1)I$, so the displayed [direct sum](direct-sum.md) follows. Its trace-free part is an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) of [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\omega_1+2\omega_{n-1}$. Its [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md) is $e_1\otimes y_n^2$; the [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) gives dimension $n(n-1)(n+2)/2$, exactly $\dim\ker C$.
