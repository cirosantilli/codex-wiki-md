# Lower convex hull

↑ **Parent:** [Convex hull](convex-hull.md)

The lower boundary of the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of points in the plane: the portion visible from points sufficiently far in the negative vertical direction. Sorting its vertices by horizontal coordinate gives a chain with nondecreasing edge slopes; collinear interior vertices can be omitted.
