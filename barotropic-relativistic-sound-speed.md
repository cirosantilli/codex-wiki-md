# Barotropic relativistic sound speed

↑ **Parent:** [Specific enthalpy per particle](specific-enthalpy-per-particle.md)

In units $c=1$, an isentropic [perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) has sound speed $c_s^2=dp/d\rho$. Writing $n=p'(\epsilon)$ gives $d\rho=\epsilon\,dn$ and $c_s^2=n/(\epsilon p''(\epsilon))$. Linearized [particle number conservation in a relativistic fluid](particle-number-conservation-in-a-relativistic-fluid.md) and the [relativistic Euler equation](relativistic-euler-equation.md) then yield acoustic waves with this speed. Positive $c_s^2$ is needed for ordinary acoustic stability; a causal fluid model additionally requires $c_s^2\le1$.
