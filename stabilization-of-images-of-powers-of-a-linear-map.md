# Stabilization of images of powers of a linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Linear endomorphism](linear-endomorphism.md)

For a [linear endomorphism](linear-endomorphism.md) $h$ of an $n$-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), the [images of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) formed by its powers decrease and stabilize by exponent $n$. Indeed, $h^{m+k}v=h^m(h^kv)$ proves inclusion. If equality occurs at one step, applying $h$ to both sides proves equality at the next step, and induction gives equality forever after. Before stabilization, every inclusion is strict and lowers [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) by at least one. Starting at [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $n$ allows at most $n$ such decreases.

By the [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md), the increasing [null spaces](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of the same powers stabilize at the same time. This gives a short proof of the [finite-dimensional Fitting decomposition](finite-dimensional-fitting-decomposition.md).
