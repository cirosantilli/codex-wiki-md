# Factorial series

↑ **Parent:** [Series (mathematics)](series-mathematics.md)

A factorial series with digits $0\leq a_n<n$ is an absolutely convergent [series](series-mathematics.md) of [real numbers](real-number.md) between zero and one. The [telescoping series](telescoping-series.md) identity

$$
\frac{n-1}{n!}=\frac1{(n-1)!}-\frac1{n!}
$$

shows that its largest possible tail after place $N$ is $1/N!$. Multiplying this tail by $N!$ gives a number between zero and one, a useful arithmetic constraint on [rational numbers](rational-number.md).
