# Relaxation technique

↑ **Parent:** [Anxiety management](anxiety-management.md)

An activity intended to reduce muscular tension or [physiological arousal](physiological-arousal.md). A reduction in immediate [arousal](physiological-arousal.md) is a different endpoint from durable change in an [anxiety disorder](anxiety-disorder.md).
