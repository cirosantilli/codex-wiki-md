# Conserved energy of a cylinder-averaged torsional wave

↑ **Parent:** [Cylinder-averaged torsional Alfvén wave](cylinder-averaged-torsional-alfven-wave.md)

For a [cylinder-averaged torsional Alfvén wave](cylinder-averaged-torsional-alfven-wave.md) with time-independent $H>0$, differentiating this quadratic form gives $dE/dt=[rUT]_0^a$. Regularity at the axis and vanishing magnetic torque at the outer endpoint therefore imply conservation. By [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), $T^2/H\leq\int B_\phi^2\,dz$; equality holds for the torsional-wave magnetic component proportional to $B_r$ along each cylinder. A stationary magnetic component orthogonal to $B_r$ is invisible to $T$.
