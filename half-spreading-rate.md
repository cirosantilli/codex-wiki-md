# Half-spreading rate

↑ **Parent:** [Spreading ridge](spreading-ridge.md)

The [speed](speed.md) of one side of newly formed crust relative to the ridge axis in a symmetric spreading model. It is half the full relative opening [speed](speed.md); the width formed during a polarity interval is this [speed](speed.md) times the interval duration.
