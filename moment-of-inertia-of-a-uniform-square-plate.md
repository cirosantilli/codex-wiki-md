# Moment of inertia of a uniform square plate

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

A uniform square of side $s$ has the displayed [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about the perpendicular axis through its centre. Integrating $x^2+y^2$ over $[-s/2,s/2]^2$ gives $s^4/6$; multiplying by the [surface mass density](projected-surface-mass-density.md) $M/s^2$ gives the result. A square prism of any uniform thickness has the same axial formula when expressed in terms of its total mass. If the side is denoted $2a$ instead, the result is $2Ma^2/3$, so the side-length convention must be specified before substituting numerical data into a [normal mode](normal-mode.md) calculation.
