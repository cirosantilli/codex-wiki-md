# Ideal-theoretic decomposition law

↑ **Parent:** [Artin symbol](artin-symbol.md)

For an unramified prime $\mathfrak p$ in a finite abelian extension $L/K$, the residue degree equals the order of its [Artin symbol](artin-symbol.md). Consequently the number of primes above $\mathfrak p$ is $[L:K]$ divided by that order.
