# Proper filter

↑ **Parent:** [Filter (set theory)](filter-set-theory.md)

A [proper filter](proper-filter.md) is an upward-closed family of [subsets](subset.md) [closed](closed-set.md) under finite [intersections](set-intersection.md) and excluding the [empty set](empty-set.md). This excludes the improper family of all [subsets](subset.md). An [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) is a maximal [proper filter](proper-filter.md).
