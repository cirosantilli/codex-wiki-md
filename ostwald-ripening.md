# Ostwald ripening

↑ **Parent:** [Binary fluid mixture](binary-fluid-mixture.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ostwald_ripening)

In Ostwald ripening, the higher interfacial [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) of small droplets drives diffusion toward larger droplets, increasing the characteristic domain size. The [Gibbs--Thomson relation](gibbs-thomson-relation.md) supplies the curvature-dependent surface value, while [conserved order-parameter dynamics](conserved-order-parameter-dynamics.md) controls transport through the surrounding phase.
