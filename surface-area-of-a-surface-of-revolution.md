# Surface area of a surface of revolution

↑ **Parent:** [Surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md)

For the radial profile $(\rho(t),z(t))$, with $\rho>0$, use the [parametrized surface](parametrized-surface.md) $\mathbf R(t,\theta)=(\rho\cos\theta,\rho\sin\theta,z)$, $0\leq\theta<2\pi$. The two [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md) are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) and have [Euclidean norms](euclidean-norm.md) $\sqrt{\rho'^2+z'^2}$ and $\rho$. Their [cross product](cross-product.md) gives the displayed [surface area](surface-area.md). Geometric [surface area](surface-area.md) assumes a single covering of the radial profile. A general three-dimensional generating curve must first be replaced by its actual [radius](radius.md) $\rho(t)=\sqrt{x(t)^2+y(t)^2}$; its spatial [arc length](arc-length.md) cannot simply replace the radial profile's [arc length](arc-length.md).
