# Delta hedge

↑ **Parent:** [Option delta](option-delta.md)

A delta hedge holds $\Delta_t=\partial_sV(t,S_t)$ units of the risky asset. In the Black-Scholes model, this choice cancels the claim's Brownian exposure.

The [option delta](option-delta.md) is one of the [Greeks (finance)](greeks-finance.md); hedging is the trading procedure that uses this sensitivity.
