# Hydrofoil

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrofoil)

A submerged lifting surface that develops [lift](lift-force-split.md) when water moves relative to it. A thin two-dimensional model relates its motion-induced [lift](lift-force-split.md) per unit span to bound [circulation](circulation-physics.md); [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) is a separate contribution to the upward fluid [force](force-split.md).
