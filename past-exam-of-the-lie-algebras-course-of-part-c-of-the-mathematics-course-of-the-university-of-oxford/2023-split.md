# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the lie algebras course of Part C of the mathematics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [Lie algebras course of Part C of the mathematics course of the University of Oxford](../lie-algebras-course-of-part-c-of-the-mathematics-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) examination, sat on 6 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 307–309; paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=307). Questions 1 and 2 occupy paper page 2, and Question 3 occupies paper page 3. Module C2.1 and formal paper code A12189W1 identify this Part C paper. The original question numbering is retained.

Throughout, $k$ is an [algebraically closed field](../algebraically-closed-field.md) of [characteristic zero](../characteristic-zero.md). All [Lie algebras](../lie-algebra-split.md) and [vector spaces](../vector-space-split.md) are [finite-dimensional vector spaces](../finite-dimensional-vector-space.md) except the [Lie algebra representation](../lie-algebra-representation.md) explicitly considered in Question 2(c). We use the [operator commutator](../operator-commutator.md) $[A,B]=AB-BA$ and the standard [sl2 triple](../sl2-triple.md) convention $[H,E]=2E$, $[H,F]=-2F$, $[E,F]=H$. The printed operators in Question 2(c)(i) have a sign inconsistency with that convention; its solution explains the correction. The displayed definition of the [symplectic group](../symplectic-group.md) on paper page 3 also lacks a closing set brace; its intended meaning is the group of invertible [symplectic linear maps](../symplectic-linear-map.md).
