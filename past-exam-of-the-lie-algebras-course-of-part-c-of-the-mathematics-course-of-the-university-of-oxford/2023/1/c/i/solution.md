<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

First put the nonabelian two-dimensional [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) in a convenient form. For any [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $p,q$, let $u=[p,q]=ap+bq\ne0$. Then $[p,u]=bu$ and $[q,u]=-au$. Since $(a,b)\ne(0,0)$, some [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) $x$ of $p,q$ has $[x,u]=u$. In particular, $x,u$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md). Taking $y=u$ gives a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) with

$$
[x,y]=y.
$$

This is the [affine Lie algebra of the line](../../../../../affine-lie-algebra-of-the-line.md).

Every [alternating bilinear form](../../../../../alternating-bilinear-form.md) on this [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) is

$$
\alpha_c(rx+sy,tx+uy)=c(ru-st)\qquad(c\in k).
$$

For any [alternating bilinear form](../../../../../alternating-bilinear-form.md) $\alpha$, the expression

$$
J_\alpha(v,w,t)=\alpha([v,w],t)+\alpha([w,t],v)+\alpha([t,v],w)
$$

is an [alternating trilinear form](../../../../../alternating-trilinear-form.md): it is trilinear, and setting any two arguments equal makes the terms cancel. Every [alternating trilinear form](../../../../../alternating-trilinear-form.md) on a two-dimensional [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) vanishes, since any triple is [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md). Hence every $\alpha_c$ satisfies the [Lie algebra two-cocycle](../../../../../lie-algebra-two-cocycle.md) condition, and there are no others:

$$
\boxed{Z^2(\mathfrak s,k)=\{\alpha_c:c\in k\}\cong k,\qquad\alpha_c(x,y)=c.}
$$
