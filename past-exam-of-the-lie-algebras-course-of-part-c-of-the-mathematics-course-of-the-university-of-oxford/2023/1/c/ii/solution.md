<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [Lie algebra two-cocycle](../../../../../lie-algebra-two-cocycle.md) $\alpha_c$ of part (i), the [central extension of a Lie algebra](../../../../../central-extension-of-a-lie-algebra.md) has a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $x,y,z$ and [Lie brackets](../../../../../lie-bracket.md)

$$
[x,y]=y+cz,\qquad[x,z]=[y,z]=0.
$$

Put $y'=y+cz$. This is still a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) together with $x,z$, and

$$
[x,y']=[x,y]+c[x,z]=y+cz=y'.
$$

Consequently $\langle x,y'\rangle$ is a [Lie subalgebra](../../../../../lie-subalgebra.md) isomorphic to the [affine Lie algebra of the line](../../../../../affine-lie-algebra-of-the-line.md). It commutes with $kz$ and has zero intersection with $kz$, so both summands are [ideals of a Lie algebra](../../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md) and

$$
\boxed{\widetilde{\mathfrak s}\cong\mathfrak s\oplus kz:\quad\text{exactly one class, which is trivial}.}
$$

This also holds for the stricter [equivalence of central extensions of a Lie algebra](../../../../../equivalence-of-central-extensions-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Indeed, choose the [linear functional](../../../../../linear-functional.md) $t$ with $t(x)=0$, $t(y)=c$. Since $[x,y]=y$ in $\mathfrak s$, the change of section in part (a) gives $\alpha'_c(x,y)=c-t(y)=0$. Thus every [Lie algebra two-cocycle](../../../../../lie-algebra-two-cocycle.md) here is a [Lie algebra two-coboundary](../../../../../lie-algebra-two-coboundary.md), so $H^2(\mathfrak s,k)=0$ in [Lie algebra cohomology](../../../../../lie-algebra-cohomology.md). The explicit map from the split [central extension of a Lie algebra](../../../../../central-extension-of-a-lie-algebra.md) sends $x$ to $x$, $y$ to $y+cz$, and $z$ to $z$; it fixes the central line and induces the identity on the [quotient Lie algebra](../../../../../quotient-lie-algebra.md).
