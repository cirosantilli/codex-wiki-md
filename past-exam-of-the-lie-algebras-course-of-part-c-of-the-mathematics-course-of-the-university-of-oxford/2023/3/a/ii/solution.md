<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the general definition of a [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md): a [nilpotent Lie algebra](../../../../../nilpotent-lie-algebra.md) that is a [Lie subalgebra](../../../../../lie-subalgebra.md) and equals its [normalizer of a Lie subalgebra](../../../../../normalizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md). In a [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), the stated structural fact says every [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak h$ is a [toral subalgebra](../../../../../toral-subalgebra.md) and an [abelian Lie algebra](../../../../../abelian-lie-algebra.md). Part (i) therefore gives

$$
C_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak h)=N_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak h)=\mathfrak h.
$$

Conversely, suppose $\mathfrak h$ is a [toral subalgebra](../../../../../toral-subalgebra.md) and an [abelian Lie algebra](../../../../../abelian-lie-algebra.md) with $C_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak h)=\mathfrak h$. An [abelian Lie algebra](../../../../../abelian-lie-algebra.md) is a [nilpotent Lie algebra](../../../../../nilpotent-lie-algebra.md), because its [lower central series of a Lie algebra](../../../../../lower-central-series-of-a-lie-algebra.md) vanishes at the second term. Part (i) gives $N_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak h)=C_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak h)=\mathfrak h$. Thus $\mathfrak h$ satisfies the defining conditions of a [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md), proving

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak h\text{ is Cartan}\iff\begin{gathered}\mathfrak h\text{ is toral and abelian},\\C_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak h)=\mathfrak h.\end{gathered}}
$$
