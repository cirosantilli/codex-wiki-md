<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [normalizer of a Lie subalgebra](../../../../../normalizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md) and the [centralizer of a Lie subalgebra](../../../../../centralizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md) are

$$
N_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak a)=\{x:[x,\mathfrak a]\subseteq\mathfrak a\},\qquad C_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak a)=\{x:[x,\mathfrak a]=0\}.
$$

The second is always contained in the first. Conversely, let $x\in N_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak a)$ and $a\in\mathfrak a$. Then $[a,x]\in\mathfrak a$, and, because $\mathfrak a$ is an [abelian Lie algebra](../../../../../abelian-lie-algebra.md),

$$
(\operatorname{ad}a)^2x=[a,[a,x]]=0.
$$

By the definition of a [toral subalgebra](../../../../../toral-subalgebra.md), $\operatorname{ad}a$ is [diagonalizable](../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md). A [diagonalizable](../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $T$ satisfies $\ker T^2=\ker T$: in an [eigenbasis](../../../../../eigenbasis.md), both [kernels](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) consist of exactly the [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) whose [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is zero. Thus $[a,x]=0$. This holds for every $a\in\mathfrak a$, so $x\in C_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak a)$ and

$$
\boxed{N_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak a)=C_{\mathfrak g}(\mathfrak a).}
$$

This is the [normalizer-centralizer equality for a toral abelian subalgebra](../../../../../normalizer-centralizer-equality-for-a-toral-abelian-subalgebra.md); it does not require $\mathfrak g$ to be a [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md).
