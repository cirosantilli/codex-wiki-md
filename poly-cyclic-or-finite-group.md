# Poly-(cyclic or finite) group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)

A group is poly-(cyclic or finite) when it has a finite [subnormal series](subnormal-series.md) $1=G_0\triangleleft G_1\triangleleft\cdots\triangleleft G_r=G$ in which each quotient $G_i/G_{i-1}$ is a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) or a [finite group](finite-group.md). Each term need only be normal in the next term. The [polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md) condition instead requires every factor to be cyclic.
