# Displacement equation for a parallel magnetic shear flow

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized ideal magnetohydrodynamic equations](linearized-ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-equations.md)

For nonresonant incompressible [normal modes](normal-mode.md) of a uniform-density [shear flow](shear-flow.md) with aligned uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), let $S=\sigma+k_xU(z)$, $k^2=k_x^2+k_y^2$ and $f=w/S$. The normal [displacement](displacement.md) is $f/i$. The linearized horizontal equations and incompressibility give $ik^2p_1=\rho(Sw'-S'w)=\rho S^2f'$. Substituting the [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md) into normal [momentum](momentum.md) gives the displayed conservative equation. Across a zero-thickness [shear layer](shear-layer.md), continuity of $f$ and of $(\rho S^2-k_x^2B^2)f'$ supplies the interface conditions. They express common normal [displacement](displacement.md) and continuous perturbed [magnetohydrodynamic total pressure](magnetohydrodynamic-total-pressure.md), avoiding undefined products of distributional shear gradients.
