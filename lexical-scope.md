# Lexical scope

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language semantics](programming-language-semantics.md)

Name resolution determined by the nesting of declarations in the source program. An activation of a nested procedure obtains its [nonlocal variables](nonlocal-variable.md) from its defining environment, which can differ from the newest activation of the same enclosing procedure when [procedural parameters](procedural-parameter.md) are used.
