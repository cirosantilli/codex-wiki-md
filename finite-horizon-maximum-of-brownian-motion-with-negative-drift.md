# Finite-horizon maximum of Brownian motion with negative drift

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion with drift](brownian-motion-with-drift.md)

For $a,b>0$,

$$
\mathbb P\!\left(\sup_{0\leq s\leq t}(W_s-as)\leq b\right)
=\Phi\!\left(\frac{b+at}{\sqrt t}\right)
-e^{-2ab}\Phi\!\left(\frac{at-b}{\sqrt t}\right).
$$

This follows from the [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) weighted by the [Cameron-Martin theorem for a linear drift](cameron-martin-theorem-for-a-linear-drift.md).
