# Beta barium borate

↑ **Parent:** [Barium borate](barium-borate.md)

The low-temperature crystalline phase of [barium borate](barium-borate.md), used as a [nonlinear optical crystal](nonlinear-optical-crystal.md). A properly phase-matched and compensated source can use it for [spontaneous parametric down-conversion](spontaneous-parametric-down-conversion.md); the choice of collected modes and compensation determines the resulting [polarization entanglement](polarization-entanglement.md).
