# Dynamic boundary condition for an inviscid interface

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

At an interface between two inviscid fluids without surface tension, the pressure is continuous. For potential flows of equal density, the [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md) therefore equates

$$
\partial_t\phi+\frac12|\nabla\phi|^2
$$

on the two sides, up to a removable function of time.
