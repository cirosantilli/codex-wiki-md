# Spatial summation

↑ **Parent:** [Synaptic integration](synaptic-integration.md)

[Spatial summation](spatial-summation.md) combines inputs arriving at different [synapses](synapse.md). Location matters because a [dendrite](dendrite.md) has axial [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) and leaky [membrane capacitance](membrane-capacitance.md): a local [synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md) need not arrive at the [soma](soma-biology.md) with its original amplitude or time course. A [passive dendritic cable](passive-dendritic-cable.md) makes this attenuation explicit. The [superposition principle](superposition-principle.md) applies to fixed [electric current](electric-current.md) inputs in a linear passive model, but adding [synaptic conductances](synaptic-conductance.md) changes the model's coefficients. Compare voltage changes relative to the same baseline, rather than adding absolute [membrane potentials](membrane-potential.md).

Original calculations, not biological recordings. A: equal imposed [electric current](electric-current.md) steps at the [soma](soma-biology.md) and a [dendrite](dendrite.md) in two coupled passive compartments yield different somatic voltage traces; applying both gives their exact sum relative to rest. B: activating two excitatory [synaptic conductances](synaptic-conductance.md) together gives less [depolarization](depolarization.md) than adding the separate voltage responses. C: a [synaptic conductance](synaptic-conductance.md) whose [reversal potential](reversal-potential.md) equals rest suppresses the same excitation. All steps last from $10$ to $60\,\mathrm{ms}$. There are no active [ion channels](ion-channel.md), [action potentials](action-potential.md) or resets. The equations and parameters are under [passive spatial and conductance integration](passive-spatial-and-conductance-integration.md).

**A motivating experiment.** A 2004 study used $300$–$350\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ cortical slices from $18$–$28$-day-old rats at $35$–$36\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Somatic [whole-cell recordings](whole-cell-recording.md) measured [EPSPs](excitatory-postsynaptic-potential.md) in layer-5 [pyramidal neurons](pyramidal-cell.md). Two fine stimulating [electrodes](electrode.md) were positioned beside imaged [dendrites](dendrite.md); [calcium](calcium.md)-sensitive dye localized activation. Each site was stimulated alone, then both together, usually with paired pulses $20\,\mathrm{ms}$ apart. Comparing the combined voltage trace with the sum of individual changes tested the summation rule. Nearby inputs on one branch showed strong amplification at intermediate strengths; inputs on different branches were approximately linear.[pp. 622–623, Figures 1–2; p. 626, Methods](computational-subunits-in-thin-dendrites-of-pyramidal-cells-by-alon-polsky-and-others.md)

Blocking both excitatory [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) classes eliminated voltage and [calcium](calcium.md) responses, checking against direct electrical activation. [NMDA receptor](nmda-receptor.md) blockers eliminated or markedly reduced amplification; comparisons with inhibition intact and blocked addressed inhibitory contributions. These controls support local active integration. Passing [axons](axon.md) could still recruit unintended [synapses](synapse.md); somatic voltage does not measure every local [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md). The study tested subthreshold slice responses and pairs of branches, not arbitrary activity in an intact brain.[pp. 622–624: controls; pp. 625–626: limitations](computational-subunits-in-thin-dendrites-of-pyramidal-cells-by-alon-polsky-and-others.md)
