# RNA immunoprecipitation

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

Recovery of an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md)-associated complex using an [antibody](antibody.md) against a chosen [protein](protein-split.md). The associated [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) need not contact that [protein](protein-split.md) directly, especially when partners remain intact during isolation.
