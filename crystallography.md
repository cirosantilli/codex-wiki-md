# Crystallography

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallography)

[Crystallography](crystallography.md) studies [crystal structures](crystal-structure.md) and their [symmetries](symmetry-physics.md). A structure determination connects measurements such as [crystal scattering](crystal-scattering.md) patterns to a model of the [unit cell](unit-cell.md) and its [atomic basis of a crystal](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md).

[X-ray crystallographic structure inference](x-ray-crystallographic-structure-inference.md) makes that connection explicit: compare proposed [crystal structures](crystal-structure.md) through the positions and [wave intensities](intensity-physics.md) of their predicted reflections. The main difficulty is that a [diffraction](diffraction.md) pattern encodes [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md), so it must be interpreted through a model rather than read as a direct picture of the [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md).
