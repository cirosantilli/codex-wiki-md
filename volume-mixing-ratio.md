# Volume mixing ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical species](chemical-species.md)

For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) mixture, the volume mixing ratio $x_i$ equals the [mole fraction](mole-fraction.md), number fraction, and ratio of partial to total [pressure](pressure.md): $x_i=N_i/N=n_i/n=P_i/P$, with $\sum_i x_i=1$. This equivalence fails for general nonideal mixtures.
