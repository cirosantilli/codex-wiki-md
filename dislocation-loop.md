# Dislocation loop

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

A closed [dislocation](dislocation.md) line. Vacancy or interstitial clustering can form a platelet bounded by a loop; its Burgers vector, habit plane and possible stacking fault specify its crystallographic character.
