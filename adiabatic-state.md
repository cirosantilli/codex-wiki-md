# Adiabatic state

↑ **Parent:** [Born-Oppenheimer approximation](born-oppenheimer-approximation.md)

An electronic [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) at a fixed nuclear geometry. Its [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) and energy change with nuclear positions. Two same-symmetry states can exchange their dominant electronic character through an [avoided crossing](avoided-crossing.md).
