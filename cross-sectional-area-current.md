# Cross-sectional-area current

↑ **Parent:** [Line density on a Kepler orbit](line-density-on-a-kepler-orbit.md)

The [geometric cross-section](geometric-collision-cross-section.md) passing a fixed point of a steady orbital wire per unit time is $J_\sigma=\lambda_\sigma v$, where $\lambda_\sigma=d\sigma/ds$. Conservation of fragments requires $J_\sigma$ constant and $\lambda_\sigma\propto1/v$. For total area $\sigma_{\rm tot}$ and [orbital period](orbital-period.md) $P$,

$$
J_\sigma=\frac{\sigma_{\rm tot}}P.
$$

With the [fractional luminosity of a phase-mixed eccentric wire](fractional-luminosity-of-a-phase-mixed-eccentric-wire.md), this becomes $J_\sigma=2fh$. The current describes transported area, not the swept area enclosed by the [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md).
