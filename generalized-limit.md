# Generalized limit

↑ **Parent:** [L-infinity sequence space](l-infinity-sequence-space.md)

A generalized limit on real bounded sequences is a [positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md) of [operator norm](operator-norm.md) one that extends the ordinary [limit of a sequence](limit-of-a-sequence.md). Such a functional can be chosen by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md): extend the limit on the [convergent sequence space](convergent-sequence-space.md) while dominating it by the [sublinear functional](sublinear-function.md) $p(x)=\limsup_n x_n$. Applying the same domination to $-x$ gives the displayed lower bound. A [Banach limit](banach-limit.md) additionally has invariance under the [left shift on bounded sequences](left-shift-on-bounded-sequences.md).
