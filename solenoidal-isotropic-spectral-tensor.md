# Solenoidal isotropic spectral tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral tensor](spectral-tensor.md)

For a statistically homogeneous, isotropic, reflection-symmetric [solenoidal](solenoidal-vector-field.md) vector [random field](random-field.md) in three dimensions, the nonzero-wavevector [spectral tensor](spectral-tensor.md) is a scalar multiple of the [transverse projector of a vector field](transverse-projector-of-a-vector-field.md). Isotropy gives $A(k)\delta_{ij}+D(k)k_i k_j$, while zero [divergence](divergence.md) gives $A+Dk^2=0$. Its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is $2A$, giving the displayed normalization. Without reflection symmetry an additional antisymmetric, helical term can occur.
