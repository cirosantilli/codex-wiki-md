# Infinitesimal

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus](calculus-split.md)

In Cauchy's 1821 terminology, an [infinitesimal](infinitesimal.md) is a variable quantity tending to zero. Other mathematical frameworks give different precise meanings to the word; the intended framework must be specified.[Chapter II](cours-d-analyse-by-augustin-louis-cauchy.md)
