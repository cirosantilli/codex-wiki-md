# Vector noise normalization in inertial Langevin dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Fluctuation-dissipation relation for a Langevin particle](fluctuation-dissipation-relation-for-a-langevin-particle.md)

Divide the inertial [Langevin equation](langevin-dynamics.md) by mass and let $\zeta=\gamma/m$. An isotropic three-dimensional equilibrium velocity has $\langle|\mathbf u|^2\rangle=3k_BT/m$ by the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md). Its white acceleration dot-covariance amplitude is therefore $6\zeta k_BT/m$, while the amplitude per Cartesian component is $2\zeta k_BT/m$. A scalar moment formula must use the component variance rather than the trace.
