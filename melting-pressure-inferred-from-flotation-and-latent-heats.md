# Melting pressure inferred from flotation and latent heats

↑ **Parent:** [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md)

Floating ice with submerged volume fraction $f$ has [density](density.md) $f\rho_l$ by [Archimedes' principle](archimedes-principle.md). Its volume change on melting is therefore $v_l-v_s=(1-1/f)/\rho_l<0$. An [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) cycle gives $L_f=L_s-L_v$ for quantities consistently expressed per [mass](mass.md) at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md). Treating these [latent heats](latent-heat.md) and specific volumes as constant and integrating the [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) gives the displayed [pressure](pressure-split.md) estimate. Using a vaporization [latent heat](latent-heat.md) extrapolated from a different [temperature](temperature-split.md) carries that approximation into the inferred fusion [heat](heat-split.md).
