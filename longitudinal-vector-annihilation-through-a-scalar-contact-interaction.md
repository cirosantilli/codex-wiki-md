# Longitudinal-vector annihilation through a scalar contact interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Proca field](proca-field.md)

For a vertex $2ig^2\eta_{\mu\nu}$ and two identical outgoing real [scalar](scalar.md) [particles](particle-physics.md), the longitudinal-pair amplitude is $g^2(s-2m_A^2)/m_A^2$. The high-energy [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) is the displayed expression, including the final-state factor $1/2!$. An [unpolarized](unpolarized-cross-section.md) incoming pair averages over nine [spin](spin.md) states and has leading [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) $g^4s/(288\pi m_A^4)$. A written interaction $g^2A_\mu A^\mu h^2$ instead gives vertex $4ig^2\eta_{\mu\nu}$ and four times these [cross sections](cross-section-physics.md). The distinction separates a vertex normalization from [polarization](polarization-waves.md) averaging and identical-particle phase-space counting.
