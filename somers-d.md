# Somers' D

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal categorical variable](ordinal-categorical-variable.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somers'_D)

Here $C,D$ count [concordant pairs](concordant-pair.md) and [discordant pairs](discordant-pair.md), and $T_Y$ counts pairs tied on response $Y$ but not on predictor $X$. [Somers' D](somers-d.md) divides by the number of pairs with distinct $X$ values, including pairs that the predictor separates but the response ties. Thus it is directional and generally changes when predictor and response are interchanged. For an $r\times c$ [contingency table](contingency-table.md) with row predictor totals $n_{i+}$, that denominator is $[n^2-\sum_i n_{i+}^2]/2$. The double-counting convention for concordances and discordances cancels the factor two.[DescTools documentation, Details: column response given row predictor and the row-total denominator](https://andrisignorell.github.io/DescTools/reference/SomersDelta.html)
