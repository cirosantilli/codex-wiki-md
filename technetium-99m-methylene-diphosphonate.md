# Technetium-99m methylene diphosphonate

↑ **Parent:** [Radiopharmaceutical](radiopharmaceutical.md)

A diagnostic bone tracer combining [technetium-99m](technetium-99m.md) with a diphosphonate carrier. Uptake reflects bone turnover and delivery, rather than a unique disease diagnosis.
