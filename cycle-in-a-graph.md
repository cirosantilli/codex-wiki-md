# Cycle in a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Path in a graph](path-in-a-graph.md)

A cycle is a closed path: its final vertex is joined back to its initial vertex, and no other vertex is repeated.

A [closed walk](closed-walk.md) can revisit other [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and need not contain a [simple cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) with every property of the whole [closed walk](closed-walk.md), such as using both of two edge colours.
