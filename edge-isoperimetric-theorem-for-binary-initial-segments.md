# Edge-isoperimetric theorem for binary initial segments

↑ **Parent:** [Edge-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube](edge-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md)

Among subsets of the $n$-dimensional [hypercube graph](hypercube-graph.md) with $a$ vertices, initial segments of the [binary order on the discrete cube](binary-order-on-the-discrete-cube.md) minimize the [edge boundary](edge-boundary-in-a-graph.md). Its exact minimum is $na-2\sum_{j=0}^{a-1}s_2(j)$. This strengthens the entropy lower bound on the same boundary.
