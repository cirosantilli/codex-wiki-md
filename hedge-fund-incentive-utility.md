# Hedge fund incentive utility

↑ **Parent:** [Expected utility maximization](expected-utility-maximization.md)

An incentive fee above a hurdle changes a manager’s [utility function](utility-function-split.md) into $F(y)=U(\varepsilon y+\alpha(y-w_0)_+)$. For [constant absolute risk aversion utility](constant-absolute-risk-aversion-utility.md), each branch is strictly [concave](concave-function.md), but the marginal reward jumps upward at the hurdle. Thus the effective terminal-wealth utility is not concave, and [concavification of incentive utility](concavification-of-incentive-utility.md) can reveal optimal risk-taking lotteries.
