# Dual-phase xenon detector

↑ **Parent:** [Time projection chamber](time-projection-chamber.md)

A liquid target with a gaseous charge-readout layer. An interaction produces prompt [particle scintillation](scintillation-physics.md), conventionally S1, and [ionization](ionization.md) electrons that drift to the liquid surface. Extracted electrons produce gas [electroluminescence](electroluminescence.md), conventionally S2. Timing and light-pattern information give three-dimensional position; the S2/S1 distribution helps distinguish [electron recoils](electron-recoil.md) from [nuclear recoils](nuclear-recoil.md). It does not remove neutron-induced single recoils, and the discrimination requires [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md).
