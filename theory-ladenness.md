# Theory-ladenness

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of science](philosophy-of-science.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory-ladenness)

The dependence of scientific observation, description or interpretation on background concepts and assumptions. Dependence on some theory is weaker than dependence on the truth of the particular theory being tested.
