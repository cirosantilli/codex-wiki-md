# Constraint reaction in hyperelastic stress

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)

For a regular material constraint $F(A)=0$, admissible rates of the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) lie in the kernel of $F_{,A}$. The difference between the [nominal stress](nominal-stress-tensor.md) and the derivative of the [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) annihilates that tangent space by [virtual work](virtual-work.md), so it is a [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) times $F_{,A}$. For [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md), the derivative of the [determinant](determinant.md) is its [cofactor matrix](cofactor-matrix.md), and the material-first reaction is $qA^{-1}$ when $\det A=1$.
