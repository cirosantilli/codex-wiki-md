# Cluster distance from Sunyaev-Zeldovich and X-ray signals

↑ **Parent:** [Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md)

For a uniform spherical cluster of depth $\ell\propto\theta d_A$, [thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) gives received bolometric flux $S_X\propto n_e^2T_e^{1/2}\ell^3/d_L^2$, while the [Rayleigh-Jeans law](rayleigh-jeans-law.md) limit of the [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) gives $|\Delta T|\propto n_eT_e\ell$. Eliminating $n_e$ and using $d_L=(1+z)^2d_A$ yields the displayed [angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md). A model for the density profile fixes its numerical coefficient. Clumping, nonsphericity and [temperature](temperature.md) structure can bias this distance because the two observables weight the gas differently.
