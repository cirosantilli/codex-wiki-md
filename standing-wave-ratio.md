# Standing wave ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission line](transmission-line.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_wave_ratio)

On a uniform lossless [transmission line](transmission-line.md), incident and reflected sinusoidal waves give [voltage](voltage.md) maxima $|V^+|(1+|\Gamma|)$ and minima $|V^+|(1-|\Gamma|)$. Their ratio is the [voltage](voltage.md) [standing wave ratio](standing-wave-ratio.md), $(1+|\Gamma|)/(1-|\Gamma|)$, where $\Gamma$ is the voltage-wave [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md). A matched termination gives one, while perfect reflection gives an infinite ratio. These expressions assume a common real [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md) and no propagation attenuation between the compared extrema.
