# Cubic planar flow with diagonal approach directions

↑ **Parent:** [First integral of an ordinary differential equation](first-integral-of-an-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

For this cubic [vector field](vector-field.md), every non-axis trajectory has the [first integral](first-integral.md) $I(x,y)=1/y-y/x^2$. Direct differentiation gives $\nabla I\cdot(\dot x,\dot y)=0$ where $xy\ne0$. Equivalently the trajectories satisfy $x^2-y^2=Cx^2y$. The axes are separate invariant trajectories, and the diagonals are the cases $C=0$. For each fixed finite $C$, approach to the origin forces $y^2/x^2=1-Cy\to1$, so non-axis trajectories have diagonal limiting directions.

The origin is repelling in forward time. For $V=x^2+y^2$, $\dot V=2x^4+2x^2y^2+4y^4\geq V^2$, so every nonzero trajectory eventually exits any small disk. The zero [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) at the origin leaves the linearized system constant, demonstrating why nonlinear terms are needed for this stability classification.
