# Constant sheaf of sets

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf (mathematics)](sheaf-mathematics.md)

The sections are [locally constant functions](locally-constant-function.md) into a discrete set $T$, with ordinary restriction. It is the [sheafification](sheafification.md) of the [constant presheaf of sets](constant-presheaf-of-sets.md). Its value on an empty open set is a singleton, even if $T$ is empty. For a topological space, this construction is [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) to the [global sections functor](global-sections-functor.md): a map from it to a sheaf is a family of global sections indexed by $T$.
