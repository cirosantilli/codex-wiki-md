# Ray (geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Straight line](straight-line.md)

A half-line beginning at $p$ with a nonzero [direction vector](direction-vector.md) $v$. Removing the endpoint gives an [open ray](open-ray.md); allowing every real parameter gives the whole [straight line](straight-line.md).
