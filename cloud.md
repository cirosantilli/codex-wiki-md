# Cloud

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)

A suspension of liquid-water droplets, ice particles or both in an atmosphere. [Clouds](cloud.md) interact with solar and thermal radiation; their net radiative effect depends on height, [water](water.md) content, particle size distribution and coverage.
