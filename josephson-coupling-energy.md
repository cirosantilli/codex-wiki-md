# Josephson coupling energy

↑ **Parent:** [Josephson effect](josephson-effect.md)

The ideal [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md) has a phase-dependent [energy](energy-split.md)

$$
U_J(\phi)=-E_J\cos\phi,\qquad E_J=\frac{\hbar I_c}{2e}.
$$

Differentiating and using the voltage–phase relation yields

$$
\frac{dU_J}{dt}=E_J\sin\phi\,\dot\phi=VI_s.
$$

Thus the ideal pair element stores and returns [energy](energy-split.md). At $V=0$ it carries a static [electric current](electric-current.md) without electrical power loss. Under a nonzero periodic drive its instantaneous power need not vanish, but its [energy](energy-split.md) returns after a full phase cycle. Real junctions also have a [capacitance](capacitance.md), [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) transport and an external circuit; their total losses cannot be inferred from this conservative pair term alone.
