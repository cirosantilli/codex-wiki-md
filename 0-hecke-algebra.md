# 0-Hecke algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Iwahori-Hecke algebra](iwahori-hecke-algebra.md)

The 0-Hecke algebra is the [specialization](specialization-of-an-algebra.md) $a_i=0$ of a [Generic Hecke algebra of a Coxeter system](iwahori-hecke-algebra.md). Its generators obey $T_i^2=-T_i$.
