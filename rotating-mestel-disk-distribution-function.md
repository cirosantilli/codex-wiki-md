# Rotating Mestel disk distribution function

↑ **Parent:** [Mestel disk](mestel-disk.md)

In the midplane [Mestel disk](mestel-disk.md) potential $\Phi=v_c^2\log(R/R_0)$, take specific [energy](energy.md) $E=(v_R^2+v_\phi^2)/2+\Phi$ and $L_z=Rv_\phi$. A prograde [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) $f=A L_z^qe^{-E/s}$ for $L_z>0$, with $s>0$, has [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) proportional to $R^{q-v_c^2/s}$. Matching $\Sigma=\Sigma_0R_0/R$ gives $q=v_c^2/s-1>-1$ and

$$
A=\frac{\Sigma_0R_0^{-q}}{\sqrt\pi\,2^{q/2}s^{(q+2)/2}\Gamma((q+1)/2)}.
$$

The denominator is the product of the full radial Gaussian integral and the positive-azimuthal half-line moment. Here $s$ is a squared-velocity scale, not a [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) itself. Changing the potential's additive constant changes $A$. With positive exponent $e^{\varepsilon/s}$, $\varepsilon$ must instead be the negative of the specific total energy.
