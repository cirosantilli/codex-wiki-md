# Gravitationally induced entanglement

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum gravity](quantum-gravity.md)

When two masses occupy spatial superpositions, their branch-dependent [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md) produces relative phases. If those phases cannot be separated into one phase for each mass, the interaction turns an initial [product state](product-state.md) into an [entangled state](entangled-state.md).
