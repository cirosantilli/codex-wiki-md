# Unit sphere orbit of the defining special unitary action

↑ **Parent:** [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md)

For $n\geq2$, the [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) acts transitively on the complex unit sphere. Extend any unit vector to an orthonormal basis, then adjust the phase of a remaining column to give determinant one. The [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of the first vector consists of special unitary transformations of its orthogonal complement. Thus the sphere is a homogeneous quotient, not just a set with a norm-preserving action.
