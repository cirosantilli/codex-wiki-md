# Photon arrival from an Einstein-de Sitter particle horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving particle horizon](comoving-particle-horizon.md)

For an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) normalized by $a(t_0)=1$, $a=(t/t_0)^{2/3}$. Its current [comoving particle horizon](comoving-particle-horizon.md) is $3ct_0$. A photon emitted inward at that location at $t_0$ covers $3ct_0[(t/t_0)^{1/3}-1]$ in comoving distance by time $t$. Equating these distances gives arrival at $8t_0$, a travel time of $7t_0$.
