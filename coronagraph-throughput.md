# Coronagraph throughput

↑ **Parent:** [Coronagraph](coronagraph.md)

For a specified [photodetector](photodetector.md) measurement, [coronagraph throughput](coronagraph-throughput.md) $T(\theta)$ is the fraction of a source's [light](light.md) transmitted into that measurement at angular separation $\theta$ from the blocked source, relative to a stated reference configuration. Total transmitted [power](power.md) and [power](power.md) inside a small image opening can give different fractions; one must specify which is used. Throughput usually depends on [wavelength](wavelength.md) and separation. A lower stellar residual is useful only in relation to the retained companion signal and noise. Guyon's worked designs plot transmitted off-axis [light](light.md) against separation to expose that tradeoff. [Olivier Guyon, High Performance Lyot and PIAA Coronagraphy for Arbitrarily shaped Apertures, §3.4](https://subarutelescope.org/staff/guyon/04research.web/10coronagraphy.web/60complexApertures.web/content_nomenu.html).
