# Natural visibility weighting

↑ **Parent:** [Radio interferometry](radio-interferometry.md)

Independent visibility samples are combined with weights proportional to inverse noise [variance](variance-split.md). This maximizes the point-source sensitivity for correctly calibrated samples. Dense regions of the sampled Fourier plane receive more total weight; tapering the long baselines can further emphasize extended structure at the cost of [angular resolution](angular-resolution.md). A source-template filter instead also weights by predicted source response.
