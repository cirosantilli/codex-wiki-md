# Semiheavy water

↑ **Parent:** [Spectroscopic measurement of hydration dynamics](spectroscopic-measurement-of-hydration-dynamics.md)

[Semiheavy water](semiheavy-water.md) contains one ordinary [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) nucleus and one [deuterium](deuterium.md) nucleus. Dilute [semiheavy water](semiheavy-water.md) in [heavy water](heavy-water.md) provides relatively isolated O–H groups for [infrared spectroscopy](infrared-spectroscopy.md).
