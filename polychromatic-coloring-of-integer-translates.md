# Polychromatic coloring of integer translates

↑ **Parent:** [Polychromatic coloring of a hypergraph](polychromatic-coloring-of-a-hypergraph.md)

For finite $S\subset\mathbb Z$, use vertices $\mathbb Z$ and hyperedges $S+t$. Independent uniform $k$-coloring gives missing-color probability at most $ke^{-|S|/k}$, and the [dependency graph of events](dependency-graph-of-events.md) has degree at most $|S|(|S|-1)$. For $k\ge20$ and $|S|\ge6k\log k$, the symmetric [Lovász local lemma](lovasz-local-lemma.md) condition holds. The [compactness extension of the Lovász local lemma](compactness-extension-of-the-lovasz-local-lemma.md) gives a coloring in which every translate contains every color.
