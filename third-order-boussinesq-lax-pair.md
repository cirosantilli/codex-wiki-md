# Third-order Boussinesq Lax pair

↑ **Parent:** [Isospectral Lax equation](isospectral-lax-equation.md)

The Lax equation $L_t=[L,A]$ for these operators is equivalent to

$$
q_t=3p_x,
\qquad
p_t=q_{xxx}-4(q^2)_x.
$$

Eliminating $p$ gives the [Boussinesq equation](boussinesq-equation.md)

$$
q_{tt}=3q_{xxxx}-12(q^2)_{xx}.
$$
