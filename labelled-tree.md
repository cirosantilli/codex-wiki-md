# Labelled tree

↑ **Parent:** [Tree (graph theory)](tree-graph-theory-split.md)

A [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md) whose [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) carry distinct labels from a fixed [finite set](finite-set.md), as a [labelled combinatorial structure](labelled-combinatorial-structure.md). Two [labelled trees](labelled-tree.md) on the same [vertex set](vertex-set.md) are equal when their sets of [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) are equal: any comparison must keep every label fixed. [Cayley's formula](cayley-s-formula.md) counts these [trees](tree-graph-theory-split.md).
