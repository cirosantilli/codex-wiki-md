# Lyman-alpha forest

↑ **Parent:** [Intergalactic medium](intergalactic-medium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyman-alpha_forest)

The Lyman-alpha forest is the collection of foreground [Lyman-alpha absorption](lyman-alpha-absorption.md) features in a distant [quasar](quasar.md) spectrum. Each observed wavelength identifies a [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md). Weak forest lines usually sample diffuse [intergalactic medium](intergalactic-medium.md) at modest [density contrast](density-contrast.md); strong, self-shielding absorbers need a different density interpretation.
