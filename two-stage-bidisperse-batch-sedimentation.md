# Two-stage bidisperse batch sedimentation

↑ **Parent:** [Bidisperse kinematic sedimentation](bidisperse-kinematic-sedimentation.md)

Initially, separate clearing fronts of fast and slow species coexist with a mixed deposition front. After the fast clearing front meets that deposit, the remaining suspension contains only the slow species. Its later [sedimentation shock](sedimentation-shock.md) builds a pure-slow layer above the earlier mixed deposit. Final layer thicknesses and composition follow from separate particle-volume conservation.
