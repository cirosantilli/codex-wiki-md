# Neuromesodermal progenitor

↑ **Parent:** [Progenitor cell](progenitor-cell.md)

A [progenitor cell](progenitor-cell.md) with competence for neural and mesodermal development, particularly [spinal cord](spinal-cord.md) and [paraxial mesoderm](paraxial-mesoderm.md). Shared markers indicate [cell state](cell-state.md); demonstrating realised bipotency requires [clonal lineage tracing](clonal-lineage-tracing.md).
