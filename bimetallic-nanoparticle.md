# Bimetallic nanoparticle

↑ **Parent:** [Nanoparticle](nanoparticle.md)

A nanoscale particle containing two metallic elements. Spatial arrangements include disordered [alloys](alloy.md), chemically ordered [alloys](alloy.md), core-shell structures and segregated domains; composition alone does not specify the arrangement.
