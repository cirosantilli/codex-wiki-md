# BCS pairing-field effective action

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

An attractive local pairing interaction can be decoupled with a complex [Hubbard–Stratonovich transformation](hubbard-stratonovich-transformation.md). Integrating the fermionic [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md) gives an [effective action](effective-action.md) for the pair field. Its uniform saddle is the [BCS gap equation](bcs-gap-equation.md), and its slow-field expansion near the transition gives the [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md). A [trace](matrix-trace.md) over a fully doubled particle-hole basis requires the corresponding factor of one half; the displayed formula uses one independent pairing block.
