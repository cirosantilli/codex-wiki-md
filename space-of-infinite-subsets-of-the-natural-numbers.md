# Space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey theory](ramsey-theory-split.md)

The space $[\mathbb N]^\omega$, also written $\mathbb N^{(\omega)}$, consists of all infinite subsets of the [positive integers](positive-integer.md). An element is identified with its strictly increasing enumeration. If $s$ is finite, write $s\sqsubset X$ when $s$ is the finite [initial segment](initial-segment.md) of this enumeration.
