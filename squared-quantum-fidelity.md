# Squared quantum fidelity

↑ **Parent:** [Fidelity of quantum states](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md)

This is the square of the [unsquared quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md), another common convention for [fidelity of quantum states](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md). If one state is the normalized [pure state](pure-state.md) $|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$, then

$$
F_{\mathrm{sq}}(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|,\sigma)=\langle\psi|\sigma|\psi\rangle,\qquad
F(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|,\sigma)=\sqrt{\langle\psi|\sigma|\psi\rangle}.
$$

By the [Born rule](born-rule.md), the [squared quantum fidelity](squared-quantum-fidelity.md) here is the [probability](probability.md) that a [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) with acceptance [orthogonal projector](orthogonal-projection.md) $|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ accepts $\sigma$. For two [pure states](pure-state.md) it is $|\langle\psi|\varphi\rangle|^2$: the comparison of $|0\rangle$ with $|+\rangle$ has acceptance [probability](probability.md) $1/2$ and [unsquared quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) $1/\sqrt2$.

For two general [mixed states](mixed-state.md), neither [quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) nor [squared quantum fidelity](squared-quantum-fidelity.md) is a probability that the states are equal. Equality of two specified [density operators](density-matrix-split.md) is a definite mathematical condition. The [measurement characterization of quantum fidelity](measurement-characterization-of-quantum-fidelity.md) instead describes overlap between outcome [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md). The ensemble average used in [reliable quantum source compression](reliable-quantum-source-compression.md) is normally the average [squared quantum fidelity](squared-quantum-fidelity.md) with the emitted [pure state](pure-state.md); the convention must be stated when comparing formulas.
