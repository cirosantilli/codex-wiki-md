# Partial pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal gas](ideal-gas.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_pressure)

The [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md) of a component of a [gas](gas.md) [mixture](mixture.md) is the [pressure](pressure.md) it would exert alone at the same [temperature](temperature.md) and total [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). For an ideal [mixture](mixture.md), $p_i=n_iRT/V$ and $p=\sum_i p_i$. The [chemical potential of an ideal gas](chemical-potential-of-an-ideal-gas.md) uses $p_i$ for that component rather than the total [pressure](pressure.md) $p$.
