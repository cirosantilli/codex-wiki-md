# Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski model](ehrenfeucht-mostowski-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehrenfeucht–Mostowski_theorem)

Let $M$ be an infinite [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) and $I$ a [total order](total-order.md). After choosing a [Skolem expansion](skolem-expansion.md) of $M$, an [elementary extension](elementary-extension.md) contains distinct elements $(a_i)_{i\in I}$ forming an [order-indiscernible sequence](order-indiscernible-sequence.md) in that expanded language. Their [Skolem hull](skolem-hull.md) is an [elementary substructure](elementary-substructure.md) in the expanded language, and its reduct is a [first-order model](model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $\operatorname{Th}(M)$. Every [order automorphism](order-automorphism.md) of $I$ extends uniquely to a [structure automorphism](automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md) of the hull preserving the chosen [Skolem expansion](skolem-expansion.md), by transporting terms in the generators.

Uniqueness need not hold among all [structure automorphisms](automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md) of the reduct. For an infinite structure in the pure [logical equality](logical-equality.md) language, take two distinct nullary [Skolem function](skolem-function.md) values $c_0,c_1$ in the hull. For an infinite [order-indiscernible sequence](order-indiscernible-sequence.md), neither can equal a generator: the expanded formula $x=c_j$ would otherwise hold at every generator, contradicting their distinctness. Swapping $c_0,c_1$ fixes every generator and preserves the pure [logical equality](logical-equality.md) reduct, while failing to preserve the [Skolem expansion](skolem-expansion.md). Thus the identity [order automorphism](order-automorphism.md) already has two extensions in that reduct.
