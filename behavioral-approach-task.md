# Behavioral approach task

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological assessment](psychological-assessment.md)

A structured opportunity to approach a feared stimulus or situation, with behavioural performance and [fear](fear.md) measured according to a specified protocol. Novel-context tasks can assess transfer; willingness, safety constraints and task demands still affect the result.
