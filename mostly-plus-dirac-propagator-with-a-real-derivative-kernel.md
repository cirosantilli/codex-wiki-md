# Mostly-plus Dirac propagator with a real derivative kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac propagator](dirac-propagator.md)

For action $-\int\bar\psi(\gamma\cdot\partial+m)\psi$, choose [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) with $\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2\eta^{\mu\nu}$ and signature $(-,+,\ldots,+)$. The Fourier kernel of $D=\gamma\cdot\partial+m$ is $i\gamma\cdot p+m$. Its algebraic inverse is $(-i\gamma\cdot p+m)/(p^2+m^2)$. The [Grassmann Gaussian integral](grassmann-gaussian-integral.md) for $e^{-i\bar\psi D\psi}$ has covariance $-iD_F^{-1}$, giving the displayed [Dirac propagator](dirac-propagator.md). Its denominator has positive-energy poles below and negative-energy poles above the real energy axis by the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md). Both the metric and the factor of $i$ in the derivative kernel must be specified when comparing propagator formulas.
