# One-period Gram-matrix replication formula

↑ **Parent:** [Claim replication](claim-replication.md)

In a one-period [complete market](complete-market.md), an invertible payoff [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) identifies the unique holdings replicating each claim. The pricing weight is $Z=P_0^TQ^{-1}P_1$ and the random portfolio kernel is $W=Q^{-1}P_1$. Positive definiteness gives uniqueness of holdings; positivity of $Z$ requires an additional no-arbitrage condition.
