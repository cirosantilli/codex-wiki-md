# Coimage

↑ **Parent:** [Image and coimage in an abelian category](image-and-coimage-in-an-abelian-category.md)

In an [abelian category](abelian-category.md), the coimage of a [morphism](morphism.md) is its domain modulo its [kernel in a category](kernel-in-a-category.md), expressed as a [cokernel in a category](cokernel-in-a-category.md). The canonical arrow from the coimage to the image is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md), giving the [image factorization in an abelian category](image-factorization-in-an-abelian-category.md).
