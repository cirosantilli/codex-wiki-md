# Dual differentiability with nonvanishing utility curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Utility conjugate](utility-conjugate.md)

For an increasing [strictly concave](strictly-concave-function.md) differentiable utility satisfying [Inada conditions](inada-conditions.md), its utility conjugate has a unique optimizer $I(y)=(U')^{-1}(y)$ and [derivative](derivative.md) $-I(y)$. The dual is strictly decreasing and [strictly convex](strictly-convex-function.md). Twice [differentiability](differentiability.md) of the dual additionally follows when $U''$ exists and is strictly negative everywhere. [Strict concavity](strict-concavity.md) alone allows $U''$ to vanish and does not imply this additional assertion.
