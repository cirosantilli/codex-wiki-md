# Stability-plasticity dilemma

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical learning](statistical-learning-split.md)

The need to adapt to new evidence while preserving useful information from prior experience. Freezing everything prevents learning; changing shared representations without appropriate constraints can damage retention. [Complementary learning systems](complementary-learning-systems.md) and replay address aspects of this trade-off.
