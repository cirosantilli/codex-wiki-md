# One-sided sudden expansion of an infinite square well

↑ **Parent:** [Sudden approximation](sudden-approximation.md)

A normalized [ground state](ground-state.md) initially supported on $(0,a)$ is $\sqrt{2/a}\sin(\pi x/a)$. Expanding the [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) to $(0,2a)$ leaves this [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) unchanged during an ideal sudden switch. Its overlap with the new [ground state](ground-state.md) $a^{-1/2}\sin(\pi x/(2a))$ is $4\sqrt2/(3\pi)$, so the [Born rule](born-rule.md) gives $32/(9\pi^2)$. This differs from [ground-state overlap after sudden expansion of a square well](ground-state-overlap-after-sudden-expansion-of-a-square-well.md) with a fixed midpoint, because translation of the new well changes the overlap. Under a fixed final [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), all final [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) [probabilities](probability.md) remain constant during subsequent [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md).
