# Spin-boson model

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of quantum systems](tensor-product-of-quantum-systems-split.md)

A spin-boson model couples a two-state [quantum system](quantum-system-split.md) to a collection of [quantum harmonic oscillators](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md). The oscillator modes provide environmental degrees of freedom that can exchange energy with the [spin](spin.md) and change its [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md). One transverse-coupling convention is

$$
H=\frac{\hbar\Omega}2\sigma_z+\sum_j\hbar\omega_j(a_j^\dagger a_j+1/2)
+\sigma_x\sum_j\lambda_j(a_j^\dagger+a_j).
$$

The choice of coupling axis matters: here the [Pauli matrix](pauli-matrices.md) $\sigma_x$ flips the energy-basis [spin](spin.md) states. Coupling only through $\sigma_z$ has a different effect, since it commutes with the free spin [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md).
