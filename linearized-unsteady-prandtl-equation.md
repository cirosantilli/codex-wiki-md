# Linearized unsteady Prandtl equation

↑ **Parent:** [Unsteady Prandtl equation](unsteady-prandtl-equation.md)

For a fixed outer pressure and slip [velocity](velocity.md), retain terms linear in a disturbance of a base flow $(U,V)$. The tangential equation has the displayed form and continuity is $\tilde u_x+\tilde v_y=0$. Disturbances vanish at the wall and the tangential disturbance vanishes in the far field. A local parallel-profile approximation may freeze the coefficients even when the profile is not an exact global steady solution.
