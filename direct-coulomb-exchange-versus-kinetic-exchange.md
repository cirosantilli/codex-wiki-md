# Direct Coulomb exchange versus kinetic exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Direct exchange](direct-exchange.md)

A direct intersite Coulomb exchange integral acts without an intermediate doubly occupied state. In a repulsive one-band [Hubbard model](hubbard-model.md), by contrast, virtual hopping into a doubly occupied state lowers the singlet [energy](energy-split.md) by $4t^2/U$ relative to the triplet. The latter is antiferromagnetic kinetic exchange, even when hopping is directly between neighbouring magnetic orbitals. Ligand-mediated [superexchange](superexchange.md) introduces an intermediate ligand orbital as well.
