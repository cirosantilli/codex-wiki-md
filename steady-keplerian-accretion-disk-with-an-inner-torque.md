# Steady Keplerian accretion disk with an inner torque

↑ **Parent:** [Keplerian accretion disk](keplerian-accretion-disk.md)

For a positive inner [magnetic torque](surface-magnetic-torque-on-an-accretion-disk.md) $T_m$, define $\lambda=T_m/(\dot M h_{\rm in})$. Integrating steady [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $\mathcal G=T_m+\dot M(h-h_{\rm in})$. With [Keplerian rotation](keplerian-disk.md), this determines the [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) in the displayed formula.
