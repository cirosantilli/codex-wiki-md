# Boundedness extends across closure under a continuous map

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous map](continuous-map.md)

For a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) into a [metric space](metric-space.md), $f(\overline A)\subseteq\overline{f(A)}$: an [open neighborhood](open-neighbourhood.md) of an image point pulls back to a [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) meeting $A$. A [bounded set](bounded-set.md) lies in a [closed ball](closed-ball.md), and its [closure](closure-topology.md) lies in the same ball because [distance](distance.md) from the centre is a [continuous function](continuous-function.md). The displayed boundedness conclusion follows. Neither [compactness](compact-space.md) of the domain nor uniform [continuity](continuous-function.md) of the map is needed.
