# Sample principal component

↑ **Parent:** [Principal component analysis](principal-component-analysis.md)

Let the columns of a centered [design matrix](design-matrix.md) $X\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$ be variables and write $X^TX=V\Lambda V^T$ by the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md). The columns $u_i$ of $U=XV$ are the sample principal-component score vectors. They satisfy $u_i^Tu_j=\Lambda_{ij}$, so distinct score vectors have zero [sample covariance](sample-covariance.md) and $u_i$ has [sample variance](sample-variance.md) $\Lambda_{ii}/n$ under the divisor-$n$ convention.
