# Phasor

↑ **Parent:** [Complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md)

A [phasor](phasor.md) is a [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) representing a [sinusoid](sinusoid.md) at one specified [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md): $a(t)=\operatorname{Re}(\widehat A e^{\mathrm i\omega t})$. Its [modulus](modulus.md) gives the peak [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and its [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) gives the [wave phase](phase-waves.md) in this convention. Both may instead use [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) magnitudes if that normalization is stated.
