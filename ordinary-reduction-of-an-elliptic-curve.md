# Ordinary reduction of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Good reduction of an elliptic curve](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

A good reduction in characteristic $p$ is ordinary when the geometric $p$-torsion has $p$ points, equivalently its [formal group of an elliptic curve](formal-group-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) has height one. For a [CM elliptic curve](cm-elliptic-curve.md), a good prime split in its CM field is ordinary. One of the two roots of $X^2-a_pX+p$ is a $p$-adic unit, enabling the bounded ordinary [p-adic L-function](p-adic-l-function.md).
