# Infinitely many prime quadratic nonresidues of a nonsquare integer

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi reciprocity law](jacobi-reciprocity-law.md)

For an odd positive nonsquare integer $a$, there are infinitely many [primes](prime-number.md) $p$ with [Legendre symbol](legendre-symbol.md) $(a/p)=-1$. Choose a residue $n$ modulo $a$ with [Jacobi symbol](jacobi-symbol.md) $(n/a)=-1$ by making it a [quadratic nonresidue](quadratic-nonresidue.md) at one prime occurring to odd multiplicity and a square at every other prime. The [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) also imposes $n\equiv1\pmod4$ and $n\equiv1$ at any finite list of previously found primes. Then [Jacobi reciprocity](jacobi-reciprocity-law.md) gives $(a/n)=-1$, so a prime factor of $n$ has $(a/p)=-1$ and is outside that list.
