# Cross-dispersed echelle spectrograph

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrograph](spectrograph.md)

A cross-dispersed echelle spectrograph uses an [echelle grating](echelle-grating.md) for strong dispersion and a [cross-disperser](cross-disperser.md) in the perpendicular direction to separate overlapping [diffraction orders](diffraction-order.md). It records many orders simultaneously in an echellogram.
