# Doubly stochastic matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubly_stochastic_matrix)

A square real [matrix](matrix.md) is doubly stochastic if its entries are nonnegative and every row and column sums to $1$. Its positive-entry support satisfies the condition of the [Hall marriage theorem](hall-s-marriage-theorem.md): for a row subset $S$, $|S|\leq|N(S)|$ by summing column capacities. Consequently its support contains a [permutation matrix](permutation-matrix.md).
