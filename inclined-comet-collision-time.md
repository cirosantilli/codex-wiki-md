# Inclined comet collision time

↑ **Parent:** [Coplanar comet collision time](coplanar-comet-collision-time.md)

For a population with randomized vertical phases, [orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md) $I$, and $R_p\ll a_p I$, the effective volume probability near the planetary plane is proportional to $\Sigma_c/(a_p I)$. The collision time then scales as $I Q^{3/2}M_p^{-2/3}a_p^2$, at fixed stellar mass and planetary [mass density](density.md). An individual inclined [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) whose [orbital nodes](orbital-node.md) do not intersect the [planet](planet.md)'s orbit need not collide at all.
