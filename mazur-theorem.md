# Mazur theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Mazur's lemma](mazur-s-lemma.md)

The weak closure and norm closure of a convex subset of a real or complex normed space coincide. If a point lies outside the norm closure, the [Hahn-Banach separation theorem](hahn-banach-separation-theorem.md) supplies a weakly continuous linear functional that separates it from the convex set.
