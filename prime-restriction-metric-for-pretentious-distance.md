# Prime-restriction metric for pretentious distance

↑ **Parent:** [Pretentious distance](pretentious-distance.md)

For unit-modulus [prime](prime-number.md) values, [pretentious distance](pretentious-distance.md) is the Euclidean [metric](metric.md) on the finite vectors $(f(p)/\sqrt{2p})_{p\leq X}$. On whole [arithmetic functions](arithmetic-function.md) it is only a [pseudometric](pseudometric.md): it sees neither values on [prime powers](prime-power.md) nor [primes](prime-number.md) above the cutoff. Even for every cutoff, the functions one and $\mu^2$ have distance zero but differ on nonsquarefree integers. The [norm](norm.md) representation proves the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) and identifies exactly the quotient on which separation holds.
