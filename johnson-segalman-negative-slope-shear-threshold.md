# Johnson-Segalman negative-slope shear threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Johnson-Segalman steady viscometric functions](johnson-segalman-steady-viscometric-functions.md)

For nonzero slip parameter with $|\alpha|<1$, scale shear rate as $x=\tau\sqrt{1-\alpha^2}\dot\gamma$ and stress as $S=x[b+1/(1+x^2)]$, where $b=\mu_0/(\alpha^2G_0\tau)$. The derivative is $b+(1-x^2)/(1+x^2)^2$ and its least value is $b-1/8$, attained at $x^2=3$. A decreasing homogeneous constitutive segment therefore exists exactly under the displayed condition. It permits different shear rates at the same stress and motivates [shear banding](shear-banding.md); the local curve alone does not select a band-interface stress.
