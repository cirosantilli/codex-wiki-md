# Superlinear convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence of a numerical method](convergence-of-a-numerical-method.md)

An iterative sequence converges superlinearly to its [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) when its nonzero error norms satisfy the displayed ratio condition, or when it reaches the [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) in finitely many steps. [Quadratic convergence](quadratic-convergence.md) is a stronger local rate. In contrast, exact [linear convergence](linear-convergence.md) has a positive asymptotic error ratio below one. A superlinear method need not have [quadratic convergence](quadratic-convergence.md).
