# Left-exact reflective subcategory

↑ **Parent:** [Reflective subcategory](reflective-subcategory.md)

A reflective subcategory is left-exact when its [reflector](reflector.md) preserves [finite limits](finite-limit.md). The fixed objects of a left-exact reflector are closed under finite limits.
