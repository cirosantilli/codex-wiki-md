# Constant-opacity radiative-zero giant envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate-core shell-burning mass-luminosity relation](degenerate-core-shell-burning-mass-luminosity-relation.md)

A negligible-mass [radiative envelope](radiative-envelope.md) surrounding a core of mass $M_c$, carrying constant luminosity and having constant [opacity](opacity.md), obeys $dP/dT=16\pi acGM_cT^3/(3\kappa_0L)$. Neglecting the outer pressure integration constant gives $\Lambda=4\pi acGM_c/(3\kappa_0L)$. The [gas pressure](gas-pressure.md) fraction $\beta$ is constant because $P_{\rm rad}=aT^4/3$, and $\rho=\mu\beta\Lambda T^3/\mathcal R$. Hydrostatic balance gives $T=T_s+\mu\beta GM_c(1/r-1/R_s)/(4\mathcal R)$; dropping this outer-boundary constant gives the displayed deep-envelope approximation. A positive gas-pressure fraction requires $L<4\pi cGM_c/\kappa_0$.
