# Basis selection theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Basic sequence](basic-sequence.md)

If a bounded subset $K$ of a Banach space is bounded away from zero and zero lies in its [weak closure](weak-closure.md), then $K$ contains a [basic sequence](basic-sequence.md). More generally, weak closure may be replaced by closure in any Hausdorff locally convex vector topology weaker than the weak topology.
