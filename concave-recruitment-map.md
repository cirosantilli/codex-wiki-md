# Concave recruitment map

↑ **Parent:** [Population dynamics](population-dynamics.md)

For $g(0)=0$ and [strict concavity](strict-concavity.md), per-capita recruitment $g(N)/N$ strictly decreases because $g(N)=\int_0^Ng'(s)\,ds>Ng'(N)$. Thus a positive [fixed point](fixed-point.md) is unique if it exists. A slope $g'(0)>1$ makes extinction unstable but does not guarantee a positive intersection: $g(N)=2N+1-e^{-N}$ stays above the identity. A large-population restriction supplies existence; the local fixed-point stability condition remains $|g'(N_*)|<1$.
