# Selenoxide elimination

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

A syn elimination in which [oxidation](oxidation.md) of an organoselenide permits intramolecular beta-hydrogen removal and C–Se cleavage, producing an [alkene](alkene.md).
