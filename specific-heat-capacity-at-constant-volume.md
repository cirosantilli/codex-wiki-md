# Specific heat capacity at constant volume

↑ **Parent:** [Specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md)

The specific heat capacity at constant volume is the temperature derivative of [specific internal energy](specific-internal-energy.md) at fixed density and composition: $c_V=(\partial u/\partial T)_\rho$. It is the corresponding extensive [heat capacity at constant volume](heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) divided by material mass.
