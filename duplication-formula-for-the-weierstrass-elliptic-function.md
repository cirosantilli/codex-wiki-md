# Duplication formula for the Weierstrass elliptic function

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

The [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) satisfies the displayed [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) identity. To prove it, the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) shows that its derivative has exactly three simple zeros modulo its [period lattice](period-lattice.md), at the nonzero [two-torsion points of a complex torus](two-torsion-point-of-a-complex-torus.md). At each such half-period $h$, evenness around $h$ gives $\wp''/\wp'=1/(z-h)+O(z-h)$, so the right side has principal part $1/(4(z-h)^2)$, matching $\wp(2z)$. At zero, $\wp=z^{-2}+O(z^2)$ and $\wp''/\wp'=-3/z+O(z^3)$ give the same principal part and zero constant term. The difference is a holomorphic [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md), hence constant by compactness, and its limit at zero is zero.
