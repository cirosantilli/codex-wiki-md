# Indirect optical transition

↑ **Parent:** [Interband transition](interband-transition.md)

An optical [interband transition](interband-transition.md) that requires another momentum source, commonly a phonon, to connect initial and final electronic wavevectors. Near an [indirect band gap](indirect-band-gap.md), phonon emission gives a threshold photon energy $E_g+\hbar\Omega$, while phonon absorption gives $E_g-\hbar\Omega$ and requires an occupied phonon mode. The temperature dependence of the absorption therefore contains phonon-population information in addition to electronic occupations.
