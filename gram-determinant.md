# Gram determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md)

The determinant of a [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) is nonnegative and vanishes precisely when its vectors are [linearly dependent](linear-dependence.md). In Euclidean three-dimensional space, three column vectors form a square [matrix](matrix.md) $V$, whose [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) is $V^TV$. Its determinant is $(\det V)^2=[v_1\cdot(v_2\times v_3)]^2$, the square of the [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md) and of the parallelepiped volume.
