# Microsomal protein import experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Cotranslational protein translocation](cotranslational-protein-translocation.md)

In 1975, [Günter Blobel](gunter-blobel.md) and [Bernhard Dobberstein](bernhard-dobberstein.md) combined [cell-free translation](cell-free-translation.md) with dog-pancreas [microsomes](microsome.md). [Density-gradient centrifugation](density-gradient-centrifugation.md) isolated the membranes; removal of resident [ribosomes](ribosome.md) allowed reconstitution with rabbit-reticulocyte [ribosome](ribosome.md) subunits. Added [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) encoded an [immunoglobulin light chain](immunoglobulin-light-chain.md); [globin](globin.md) provided a comparison. Products made with or without membranes were challenged with [proteases](protease.md) and analyzed by [SDS-PAGE](sds-page.md) and [autoradiography](autoradiography.md). Membranes present during synthesis yielded processed, protected light chain; [globin](globin.md) remained susceptible. Adding membranes after precursor synthesis failed to reproduce import. These comparisons support selective passage during synthesis in this preparation.[Transfer of proteins across membranes. II, Methods p. 853; Figure 6 and text pp. 856–859; Figure 7 pp. 860–861; Figure 8 and text pp. 861–862](transfer-of-proteins-across-membranes-ii-by-gunter-blobel-and-bernhard-dobberstein.md)

The companion paper specifies radioactive [amino acid](amino-acid.md) incorporation, ice-cold [trypsin](trypsin.md) plus [chymotrypsin](chymotrypsin.md) treatment, a slab-gel electrophoresis apparatus and dried-gel exposure to X-ray film. Product-size analysis distinguishes a preserved full-size product from residual labeled fragments.[Methods pp. 837–838: proteolysis, SDS-PAGE and autoradiography; Discussion p. 847](transfer-of-proteins-across-membranes-i-by-gunter-blobel-and-bernhard-dobberstein.md)

Protection was incomplete. The authors considered leaky vesicles, and warned that heat treatment could damage processing as well as [ribosome](ribosome.md) attachment. Their experiments did not identify [SRP](signal-recognition-particle.md) or the [Sec61 complex](sec61-complex.md). Nor does failure of late membrane addition establish that all [organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md) must occur during synthesis.[pp. 858–859: incomplete protection; p. 861: heat-inactivation caveat; p. 862: alternatives for failed processing](transfer-of-proteins-across-membranes-ii-by-gunter-blobel-and-bernhard-dobberstein.md)
