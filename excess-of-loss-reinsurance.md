# Excess of loss reinsurance

↑ **Parent:** [Reinsurance](reinsurance.md)

Per-claim [reinsurance](reinsurance.md) under which the insurer retains each claim up to a level $M$ and the reinsurer pays its [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) $(x-M)_+$. The annual retained amount is a sum of capped claims, so it is generally different from the cap of the annual sum in [aggregate stop loss reinsurance](aggregate-stop-loss-reinsurance.md).
