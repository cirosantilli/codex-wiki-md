# Catalytic efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme kinetics](enzyme-kinetics.md)

For simple Michaelis–Menten kinetics at low substrate concentration, $v/[E]\simeq(k_{\mathrm{cat}}/K_M)[S]$. This efficiency combines productive substrate capture and turnover; it should be interpreted with the assay conditions and mechanism.
