# Relativistic kinetic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md)

For a free particle of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $m$, subtract its [rest energy](rest-energy.md) $mc^2$ from its total [energy](energy-split.md) $\gamma mc^2$, where $\gamma$ is the [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md). The low-[speed](speed.md) [binomial series](binomial-series.md) gives $K=mv^2/2+3mv^4/(8c^2)+O(mv^6/c^4)$, recovering classical [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md).
