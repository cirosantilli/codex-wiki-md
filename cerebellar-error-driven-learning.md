# Cerebellar error-driven learning

↑ **Parent:** [Cerebellum](cerebellum.md)

A family of models in which an instructive signal changes the influence of recently active inputs on cerebellar output. In a smooth-pursuit experiment, complex-spike duration predicted subsequent simple-spike depression and eye-movement learning; matching preceding simple-spike activity provided a control. This supports graded instruction during behaviour without proving an exact [delta rule](delta-rule.md) at every [synapse](synapse.md).[Figures 1–4 and Methods; author manuscript at PMC4132823: smooth pursuit, trial-over-trial plasticity and controls for prior simple-spike activity](purkinje-cell-plasticity-and-cerebellar-motor-learning-are-graded-by-complex-spike-duration-by-yan-yang-and-others.md)
