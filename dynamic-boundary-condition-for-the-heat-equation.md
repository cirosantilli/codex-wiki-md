# Dynamic boundary condition for the heat equation

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary condition](boundary-condition.md)

A dynamic [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) includes a time derivative of the boundary trace. For a half-line [heat equation](heat-equation.md), $u_t(0,t)+\alpha u_x(0,t)+\beta u(0,t)=f(t)$ gives a separate boundary evolution coupled to the interior flux. Its [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) contains the initial trace $u_0(0)$. The sign of $u_x$ differs from that of the outward [normal derivative](normal-derivative.md) at a left endpoint.
