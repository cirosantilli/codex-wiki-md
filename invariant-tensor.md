# Invariant tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md)

An invariant [tensor](tensor.md) is fixed by every element of the represented group, so it spans or belongs to a trivial [group representation](group-representation.md). The [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) preserves its identity [tensor](tensor.md) and the upper and lower volume [tensors](tensor.md). Such [tensors](tensor.md) make [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md) equivariant and permit invariant inner products and real structures.
