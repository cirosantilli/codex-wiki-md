# Finite presentation from a synchronous combing

↑ **Parent:** [Combable group](combable-group.md)

Attach [relators](relator.md) for every identity word of length at most $2K+2$, where $K$ is an integer fellow-traveller constant. For any null loop, connect its consecutive [combing](combing-of-a-group.md) [paths](continuous-path.md) at integer heights by rungs of length at most $K$. Each resulting quadrilateral has perimeter at most $2K+2$ and thus is a [relator](relator.md). The bottom row is collapsed at the identity and the top row is the original loop, so these finitely many [relators](relator.md) fill every null word and present the group.
