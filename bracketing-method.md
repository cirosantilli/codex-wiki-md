# Bracketing method

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

A [bracketing method](bracketing-method.md) finds a [zero of a function](zero-of-a-function.md) by maintaining a [root bracket](root-bracket.md) and shrinking it as new values of the [function](function-split.md) become available. The [bisection method](bisection-method.md) always tries the [midpoint](midpoint.md); its special attraction is a predictable [bisection error bound](bisection-error-bound.md) without any [derivative](derivative.md) evaluations.
