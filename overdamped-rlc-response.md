# Overdamped RLC response

↑ **Parent:** [Series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md)

When $R^2>4L/C$, the homogeneous [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md) has two distinct negative [characteristic roots](characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md), $[-R\pm\sqrt{R^2-4L/C}]/(2L)$. Its current is a [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of the corresponding decaying [exponential functions](exponential-function.md).
