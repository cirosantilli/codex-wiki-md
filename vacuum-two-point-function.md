# Vacuum two-point function

↑ **Parent:** [Two-point correlation function](two-point-correlation-function.md)

A two-field [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md). Its ordered, time-ordered and anticommutator versions are different distributions. For a free [Dirac field](dirac-field.md), [fermionic time ordering](fermionic-time-ordering.md) combines the positive-energy [particle](particle-physics.md) and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) contributions into a [Dirac propagator](dirac-propagator.md). Its nonzero value at spacelike separation does not violate [microcausality](microcausality.md), which constrains the field [anticommutator](anticommutator.md).
