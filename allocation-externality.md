# Allocation externality

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanism design](mechanism-design.md)

Dependence of a participant's [utility](utility.md) on assignments made to other participants, beyond the identity of that participant's own assigned object. In [room allocation with friendship externalities](room-allocation-with-friendship-externalities.md), the same room can become more valuable when a friend receives an adjacent room. An early participant in a sequential allocation must therefore anticipate later choices.
