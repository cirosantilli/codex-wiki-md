# Unrestricted Hartree-Fock method

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock method](hartree-fock-method.md)

A Hartree-Fock ansatz allowing different [spatial orbitals](spatial-orbital.md) for alpha and beta spin. This can lower the energy of a stretched bond, but the [determinant](determinant.md) need not be an eigenstate of total spin squared.
