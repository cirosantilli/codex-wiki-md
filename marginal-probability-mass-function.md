# Marginal probability mass function

↑ **Parent:** [Probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md)

A [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) obtained by summing a [joint probability mass function](joint-probability-mass-function.md) over the unretained coordinates. Nonnegative summands ensure that iterated sums are well defined.
