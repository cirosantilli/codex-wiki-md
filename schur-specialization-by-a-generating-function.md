# Schur specialization by a generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy identity for symmetric functions](cauchy-identity-for-symmetric-functions.md)

Given $F(t)=\sum_{r\ge0}f_rt^r$ with $f_0=1$, put $f_r=0$ for $r<0$. The [Cauchy identity for symmetric functions](cauchy-identity-for-symmetric-functions.md) and [Jacobi–Trudi identity](jacobi-trudi-identity.md) give $\prod_iF(t_i)=\sum_\lambda s_\lambda^Fs_\lambda(t)$, degree by degree in the completion. For $F(t)=\prod_j(1-x_jt)^{-1}$ the coefficients are $s_\lambda(x)$; for $F(t)=\prod_j(1+x_jt)$ they are $s_{\lambda'}(x)$.
