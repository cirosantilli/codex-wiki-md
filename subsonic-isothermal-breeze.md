# Subsonic isothermal breeze

↑ **Parent:** [Parker wind](parker-wind.md)

The steady [isothermal Parker wind](isothermal-parker-wind.md) differential equation also has positive solutions describing [subsonic flow](subsonic-flow.md) at every radius. Write their first integral as $w-\ln w=4\ln x+4/x+C$, where $w=u^2/c^2$ and $x=r/r_s$. On the small-$w$ branch at large radius, $w\sim e^{-C}x^{-4}$, so $u\sim ce^{-C/2}x^{-2}$. Constant spherical [mass flow rate](mass-flow-rate.md) then gives nonzero limiting [mass density](density.md) and hence finite external [pressure](pressure-split.md). This distinguishes a pressure-confined breeze from an escaping wind into vanishing pressure. Specifying outward motion alone does not select the supersonic asymptotic branch.
