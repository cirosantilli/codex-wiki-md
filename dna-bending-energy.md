# DNA bending energy

↑ **Parent:** [Worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md)

The curvature-dependent [elastic energy](elastic-energy.md) of a semiflexible [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [polymer](polymer-split.md) in the isotropic [worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md) model. Here $l_p$ is the [persistence length](persistence-length.md), $\kappa$ the curvature and $\ell$ the [polymer contour length](polymer-contour-length.md). A complete circular loop of radius $R$ costs $\pi k_BT l_p/R$, obtained by integrating constant curvature $1/R$ over length $2\pi R$.
