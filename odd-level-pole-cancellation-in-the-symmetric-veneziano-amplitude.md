# Odd-level pole cancellation in the symmetric Veneziano amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Veneziano amplitude pole residue](veneziano-amplitude-pole-residue.md)

For identical open-string tachyons, $s+t+u=-4/\alpha'$. At $\alpha's=n-1$, the ordered [Veneziano amplitude](veneziano-amplitude.md) residues in the $s,t$ and $s,u$ terms differ by $(-1)^n$, so their symmetric sum cancels odd-level $s$-channel poles at generic $t$. In particular the massless vector does not couple to this identical-scalar singlet amplitude. It remains a state of the open-string spectrum and is visible in suitable ordered or color-dependent amplitudes. Pole absence due to an external-state selection rule must not be interpreted as absence of the exchanged state from the theory.
