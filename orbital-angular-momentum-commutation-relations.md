# Orbital angular momentum commutation relations

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md)

The canonical position-momentum commutators imply

$$
[L_i,L_j]=i\hbar\varepsilon_{ijk}L_k,
\qquad
[L^2,L_i]=0.
$$
