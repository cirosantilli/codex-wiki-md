# Integral extensions preserve Krull dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Integral extension](integral-extension.md)

If $A\subseteq B$ is an [integral extension](integral-extension.md), then $\dim A=\dim B$. The [incomparability theorem for integral extensions](incomparability-theorem-for-integral-extensions.md) contracts every strict prime chain in $B$ to a strict chain in $A$, giving $\dim B\leq\dim A$. Conversely, the [Lying-over theorem](lying-over-theorem.md) starts over the bottom of any prime chain in $A$, and repeated use of the [going-up theorem](going-up-theorem.md) lifts the whole chain to $B$.
