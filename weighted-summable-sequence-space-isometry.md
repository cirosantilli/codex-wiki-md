# Weighted summable sequence space isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Absolutely summable sequence space](absolutely-summable-sequence-space.md)

For positive weights $w_j$, the [sequence space](sequence-space.md) with [norm](norm.md) $\|\alpha\|=\sum_jw_j|\alpha_j|$ is isometrically isomorphic to $\ell_1$: multiplication by $w_j$ is a linear bijection onto the [absolutely summable sequence space](absolutely-summable-sequence-space.md), with inverse multiplication by $w_j^{-1}$. Thus it is a separable [Banach space](banach-space-split.md). Density can also be seen directly by truncating a weighted summable sequence and then approximating its finitely many coordinates by rational numbers.
