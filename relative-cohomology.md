# Relative cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)

Relative cohomology is the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of cochains on $X$ vanishing on chains in $A$. A pair has a natural [long exact sequence in cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md), and [excision](excision-theorem.md) identifies relative groups for suitable neighborhood pairs.
