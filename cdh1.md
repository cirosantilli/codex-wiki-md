# Cdh1

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)

A regulatory coactivator of the [anaphase-promoting complex](anaphase-promoting-complex.md). Its activity helps maintain low mitotic-cyclin abundance during late mitosis and G1, before the next cell-cycle programme.
