# Weierstrass sufficient condition

↑ **Parent:** [Strengthened Weierstrass condition](strengthened-weierstrass-condition.md)

Under the usual $C^2$ integrand regularity, an admissible $C^1$ curve satisfying the weak [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md), a coercively positive [second variation](second-variation.md), and the [strengthened Weierstrass condition](strengthened-weierstrass-condition.md) is a [strong local minimizer of a variational integral](strong-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md). Strict excess away from the reference slope gives strict minimality. Coercive positivity means $\delta^2I(u)[\eta,\eta]\geq c\|\eta\|_{W^{1,2}}^2$ for every zero-trace variation and some $c>0$.

The [second variation](second-variation.md) provides a neighboring field of extremals with slope $p(x,y)$. The associated endpoint-invariant Hilbert integral yields $I(v)-I(u)=\int E(x,v,p(x,v),v')\,dx$ for uniformly close admissible $v$. The tube condition makes this integral nonnegative even when $v'$ is large, explaining the passage from weak to strong minimality.
