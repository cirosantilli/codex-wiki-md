<h1 id="fernique-s-theorem">Fernique's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernique's_theorem)

A [Gaussian random variable in a Banach space](gaussian-random-variable-in-a-banach-space.md) taking values in a [separable Banach space](separable-banach-space.md) satisfies the displayed exponential-integrability bound for some positive $a$. In particular every finite power of its [norm](norm.md) has a finite [expected value](expected-value.md). The value of $a$ depends on the [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md); the theorem does not assert this for every positive $a$.
