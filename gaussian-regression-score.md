# Gaussian regression score

↑ **Parent:** [Informant function](informant-function.md)

For a [regression function](regression-function.md) $g_\theta$, let $h_\theta=\partial_\theta g_\theta$. With [independent](independent-random-variables.md) centered [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) error of known [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, the parametric [score function](informant-function.md) is $h_\theta(X)\varepsilon/\sigma^2$. It has zero [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) given the [covariate](covariate.md), so unknown covariate-density nuisance directions do not remove any of its [Fisher information](fisher-information-matrix.md).
