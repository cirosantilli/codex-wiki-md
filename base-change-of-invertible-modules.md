# Base change of invertible modules

↑ **Parent:** [Invertible module](invertible-module.md)

For a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $A\to B$, [extension of scalars](extension-of-scalars.md) takes an [invertible module](invertible-module.md) to an invertible $B$-module. A direct-summand presentation in a finite [free module](free-module.md) remains a direct-summand presentation after tensoring. At a prime $\mathfrak q\subset B$ contracting to $\mathfrak p$, localization gives $B_{\mathfrak q}\otimes_{A_{\mathfrak p}}L_{\mathfrak p}\cong B_{\mathfrak q}$. Compatibility with tensor products, identities and composition makes the [Picard group of a ring](picard-group-of-a-ring.md) a covariant [functor](functor.md).
