# Furstenberg correspondence principle

↑ **Parent:** [Upper Banach density](upper-banach-density.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furstenberg_correspondence_principle)

The Furstenberg correspondence principle turns a subset $S\subseteq\mathbb Z$ of positive [upper Banach density](upper-banach-density.md) into a shift-invariant probability system and a positive-measure cylinder $A$ such that recurrence times of $A$ produce differences of elements of $S$.
