# Supersymmetric mass degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Boson-fermion degeneracy in a supermultiplet](boson-fermion-degeneracy-in-a-supermultiplet.md)

In an unbroken [supersymmetric vacuum](supersymmetric-vacuum.md), physical [boson](boson.md) and [fermion](fermion.md) partners have equal masses because [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) commute with [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). For one canonical [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md), fluctuations about $W'(v)=0$ have mass $|W''(v)|$ for both the [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) and [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md).
