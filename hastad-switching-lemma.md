<h1 id="hastad-switching-lemma">Håstad switching lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-depth circuit complexity](constant-depth-circuit-complexity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Håstad_switching_lemma)

The Håstad switching lemma says that a small-width DNF or CNF becomes representable by a shallow [decision tree](decision-tree.md) with high probability under a sufficiently sparse random restriction. Iterating it is a central method for proving lower bounds against constant-depth circuits.
