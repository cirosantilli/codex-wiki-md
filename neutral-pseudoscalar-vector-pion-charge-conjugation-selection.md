# Neutral-pseudoscalar vector-pion charge-conjugation selection

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md)

A neutral self-conjugate pseudoscalar with $C=+1$ cannot decay through charge-conjugation-conserving interactions into a neutral $C=-1$ vector and a neutral $C=+1$ [pion](pion.md). The two final particles are distinct and each maps to itself under [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md), so their total C eigenvalue is the product, without an exchange factor $(-1)^L$. Their relative orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) still enters their total [parity](parity.md). For $0^-\to0^-1^-$, $L=1$ gives a CP-violating transition if a weak amplitude is retained.
