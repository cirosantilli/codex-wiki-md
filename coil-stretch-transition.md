# Coil-stretch transition

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

A [coil-stretch transition](coil-stretch-transition.md) is a sharp change from a coiled to a substantially extended [polymer](polymer-split.md) conformation under sufficiently strong [extensional flow](extensional-flow.md). The driving [extensional strain rate](extensional-strain-rate.md) must compete with the chain's relaxation rate, and the available residence [time](time-in-physics.md) matters. A long [polymer relaxation time](polymer-relaxation-time.md) lets a weak but sustained flow produce a large effect. The [Hookean dumbbell in planar extension](hookean-dumbbell-in-planar-extension.md) below isolates this competition; its unbounded growth signals a model limitation, rather than a prediction of infinitely long [molecules](molecule-split.md).
