# Total-effort formula for a proportional contest with outside effort

↑ **Parent:** [Proportional contest with outside effort](proportional-contest-with-outside-effort.md)

With $k>0$ active players of [harmonic mean](harmonic-mean.md) valuation $\bar v_k$, unit-cost [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md) effort satisfies $R+\delta=\frac{\bar v_k}{2k}[(k-1)+\sqrt{(k-1)^2+4k\delta/\bar v_k}]$. Sum the active-player first-order equations $b_i=(R+\delta)(1-(R+\delta)/v_i)$ to obtain the quadratic. If $\delta$ is at least the largest valuation, no one is active and $R=0$. With no outside effort, at least two players must be active.
