# Second-class constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint (mechanics)](constraint-mechanics.md)

A collection of Hamiltonian [constraints](constraint-mechanics.md) is second class when its [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md) matrix $C_{ab}=\{\chi_a,\chi_b\}$ is invertible on the constraint surface. These conditions eliminate canonical variables rather than generate a [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md). Their consistent reduced bracket is the [Dirac bracket](dirac-bracket.md), $\{F,G\}_D=\{F,G\}-\{F,\chi_a\}(C^{-1})^{ab}\{\chi_b,G\}$. The zero temporal [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) of a massive [Proca field](proca-field.md) and its massive [Gauss law](gauss-s-law.md) form such a pair; imposing an independent temporal [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) would contradict the constraint.
