# Antiperiplanar geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Conformation](conformation.md)

[Antiperiplanar geometry](antiperiplanar-geometry.md) places two specified bonds at a torsion near $180^\circ$. It often enables favorable orbital alignment in [E2 elimination](e2-elimination.md) and supports large vicinal [proton](proton.md) couplings in an appropriate calibrated [Karplus relation](karplus-equation.md).
