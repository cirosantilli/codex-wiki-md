# Nullity of a cyclic linear operator

↑ **Parent:** [Rational canonical form](rational-canonical-form.md)

For a nonzero finite-dimensional cyclic [linear operator](linear-operator.md), identify its [module](module-mathematics.md) with $K[X]/(d)$, where $d$ is the monic [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md). Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) consists of classes of $h$ with $d\mid Xh$. If $d(0)\ne0$, coprimality gives $d\mid h$, so the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is zero. If $d=Xe$, it consists precisely of the multiples of $e$ modulo $Xe$, a one-dimensional space. Thus the multiplicity of zero as a root of $d$ does not increase the [nullity](nullity-of-a-linear-map.md). The same companion block reads off invertibility from the constant term and gives the characteristic and minimal [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) directly, while explicitly listing [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) may require factoring $d$.
