# Photon polarization vector

↑ **Parent:** [Photon](photon.md)

For a real [on shell](on-shell.md) [photon](photon.md) of null momentum $k$, a physical polarization satisfies $k\cdot\epsilon=0$ and is defined modulo $\epsilon\mapsto\epsilon+\alpha k$. The two-dimensional quotient carries the two physical [photon](photon.md) helicities. In a transverse gauge choose $\epsilon^0=0$ and $\epsilon^*\cdot\epsilon=-1$. A [Ward identity](ward-identity.md) ensures that replacing an external polarization by its momentum gives zero in the summed physical amplitude.
