# Loss network

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic network](stochastic-network.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loss_network)

A loss network admits a call only when all its required resources have enough free capacity. Rejected calls do not queue. Under [fixed routing](fixed-routing.md) and independent [Poisson processes](poisson-process.md), its exact occupancy law has a [product-form stationary distribution of a loss network](product-form-stationary-distribution-of-a-loss-network.md).
