# Nitrone cycloaddition

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

A [1,3-dipolar cycloaddition](1-3-dipolar-cycloaddition.md) joining a [nitrone](nitrone.md) to an [alkene](alkene.md). Intramolecular tethering controls regioselectivity and relative approach; the [nitrone](nitrone.md) [nitrogen](nitrogen.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md) bond can later be cleaved reductively.
