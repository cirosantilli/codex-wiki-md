# Free scalar field with quartic spatial dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field theory](scalar-field-theory-split.md)

A local [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) with [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) $\tfrac12[(\partial_t\phi)^2-\kappa^2(\nabla^2\phi)^2-m^2\phi^2]$ has the [equation of motion](equation-of-motion.md) $\partial_t^2\phi+\kappa^2\nabla^4\phi+m^2\phi=0$. Two spatial [integrations by parts](integration-by-parts.md) produce the positive [biharmonic operator](biharmonic-operator.md) in this equation. Its [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is a sum of positive squares and each nonzero-frequency spatial [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) quantizes as an oscillator with the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). Higher spatial derivatives do not require higher time derivatives. The theory has a preferred time and does not possess [Lorentz invariance](lorentz-invariance.md).
