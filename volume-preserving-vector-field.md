# Volume-preserving vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Volume form](volume-form.md)

A [vector field](vector-field.md) preserves a chosen [volume form](volume-form.md) $\omega$ when its [local flow](local-flow.md) pulls $\omega$ back to itself. Infinitesimally this is $\mathcal L_X\omega=0$, using the [Lie derivative](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md). The condition depends on the chosen volume form.
