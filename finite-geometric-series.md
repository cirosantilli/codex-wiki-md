# Finite geometric series

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric series](geometric-series.md)

For $r\ne1$, a finite geometric series satisfies

$$
\sum_{j=m}^n r^j=r^m\frac{1-r^{n-m+1}}{1-r}.
$$
