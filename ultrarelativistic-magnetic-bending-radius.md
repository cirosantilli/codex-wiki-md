# Ultrarelativistic magnetic bending radius

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetism](magnetism.md)

For motion perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field, the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) gives $r=p/(|q|B)$. An ultrarelativistic massive particle has $pc\simeq E$, hence $r\simeq E/(|q|Bc)$. Total energy and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) differ by the rest energy.
