# Minimal cyclic factorization criterion for powerful p-groups

↑ **Parent:** [Powerful p-group](powerful-p-group.md)

For an odd [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$, a finite [p-group](p-group.md) that is a product of $d(G)$ [cyclic subgroups](cyclic-subgroup.md) is a [powerful p-group](powerful-p-group.md). Indeed, $H=G/G^p$ has [exponent of a finite group](exponent-of-a-finite-group.md) $p$ and the same minimal generator number $d$. Its cyclic factorization gives $|H|\leq p^d$, while its [Frattini quotient](frattini-quotient.md) has order $p^d$. Thus $\Phi(H)=1$, $H$ is an [elementary abelian group](elementary-abelian-group.md), and $[G,G]\subseteq G^p$. The condition on the number of factors is essential: the nonabelian [Heisenberg group](heisenberg-group.md) over $\mathbb F_p$ has exponent $p$ and is a product of three cyclic subgroups, although its minimal generator number is two.
