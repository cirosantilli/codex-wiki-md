# Scaling vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Commutation vector field for the wave equation](commutation-vector-field-for-the-wave-equation.md)

The [vector field](vector-field.md) $S=t\partial_t+x\cdot\nabla$ generates simultaneous spacetime dilation. For the flat [d'Alembert operator](d-alembert-operator.md), $[\Box,S]=2\Box$. Consequently it commutes with the homogeneous [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) at the level of its solution set, although its operator [commutator](commutator.md) is nonzero.
