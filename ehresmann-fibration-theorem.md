# Ehresmann fibration theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehresmann_fibration_theorem)

Every proper surjective [submersion](submersion.md) is a locally trivial smooth [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md). In particular, the fibers in a proper smooth family are diffeomorphic.
