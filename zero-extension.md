# Zero extension

↑ **Parent:** [Immediate operand](immediate-operand.md)

Widen an $m$-[bit](bit.md) [binary representation](binary-number.md) to $w>m$ [bits](bit.md) by retaining the original low positions and filling every new high position with zero. This preserves the [unsigned interpretation](unsigned-interpretation-of-a-bit-string.md). A [MIPS instruction](mips-instruction.md) such as `ori` uses [zero extension](zero-extension.md) of its [immediate operand](immediate-operand.md), whereas `addi` uses [sign extension](sign-extension.md).
