# Sulfation

↑ **Parent:** [Lead-acid battery](lead-acid-battery.md)

In lead-acid batteries, development of lead-sulfate deposits that are difficult to reconvert during ordinary charging. Persistent coarse deposits reduce accessible area and capacity; [sulfate](sulfate.md) formation during normal discharge is not by itself a failure.
