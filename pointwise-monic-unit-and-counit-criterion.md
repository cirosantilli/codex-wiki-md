# Pointwise-monic unit-and-counit criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Unit and counit of an adjunction](unit-and-counit-of-an-adjunction.md)

Suppose $\mathcal C$ is [balanced](balanced-category.md) and every morphism of $\mathcal D$ factors as a [strong epimorphism](strong-epimorphism.md) followed by a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md). For $F:\mathcal C\rightleftarrows\mathcal D:G$, both unit and counit are pointwise monic exactly when $F$ is [full and faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md) and its essential image is closed under [strong quotients](strong-quotient.md).
