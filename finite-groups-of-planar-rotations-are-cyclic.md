# Finite groups of planar rotations are cyclic

↑ **Parent:** [Planar rotation](planar-rotation.md)

A finite [group](group-split.md) of [planar rotations](planar-rotation.md) about one point is a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md). For a nontrivial group, choose its smallest positive rotation angle $\alpha$. Subtracting multiples of $\alpha$ from any other group angle leaves a group angle in $[0,\alpha)$, hence zero. Applying the same argument to $2\pi$ shows that $\alpha=2\pi/m$ for a positive integer $m$. The identity-only group is cyclic as well.
