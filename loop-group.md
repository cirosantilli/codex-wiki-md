# Loop group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_group)

The loop group of a [Lie group](lie-group.md) is the group of smooth maps from the circle to that group, with pointwise multiplication and the smooth-function topology. Rotation of the source circle acts by group automorphisms. For $G=U(1)$, every loop has the form $z^w e^{ih(z)}$, where $w\in\mathbb Z$ is its [winding number](winding-number.md) and $h$ is a smooth real periodic function determined up to addition of an integer multiple of $2\pi$.
