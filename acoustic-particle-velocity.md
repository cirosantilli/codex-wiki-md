# Acoustic particle velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)

The local fluid [velocity](velocity.md) perturbation in an [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md). It describes the oscillating material, whereas the [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md) describes propagation of the disturbance. A fluid parcel need not travel one [wavelength](wavelength.md) during a [wave period](wave-period.md).
