# Conditional information function

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy of a countable measurable partition](entropy-of-a-countable-measurable-partition.md)

For a [measurable partition](measurable-partition.md) $\xi$ and a [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal G$, the conditional information function is

$$
I_\mu(\xi\mid\mathcal G)
=-\sum_{A\in\xi}\mathbf1_A\log\mathbb E_\mu[\mathbf1_A\mid\mathcal G].
$$

The [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) in this formula is positive [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) on $A$. Without conditioning, the information is $I_\mu(\xi)(x)=-\log\mu(\xi(x))$.
