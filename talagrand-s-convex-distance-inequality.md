<h1 id="talagrand-s-convex-distance-inequality">Talagrand's convex distance inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Talagrand convex distance](talagrand-convex-distance.md)

On a finite product of standard [probability spaces](probability-space.md), for any measurable event $A$ of positive probability,

$$
\mathbb E\exp(d_T(X,A)^2/4)\leq1/\mathbb P(A).
$$

Consequently $\mathbb P(A)\mathbb P(d_T(X,A)\geq s)\leq e^{-s^2/4}$. Here $d_T$ is the [Talagrand convex distance](talagrand-convex-distance.md). The distance-event conclusion can be read with outer probability when necessary.
