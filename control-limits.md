# Control limits

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical process control](statistical-process-control.md)

Control limits bound the sampling outcomes expected under a specified reference process. An observation outside a limit is a [statistical hypothesis testing](statistical-hypothesis-test.md) signal to investigate the reference model. These are limits for observations under that model, rather than a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) constructed around each observed estimate. Their calibration may be pointwise, simultaneous or sequential.
