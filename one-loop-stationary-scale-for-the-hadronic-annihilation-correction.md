# One-loop stationary scale for the hadronic annihilation correction

↑ **Parent:** [Principle of minimal sensitivity](principle-of-minimal-sensitivity.md)

Suppose $K=1+a+a^2[C-b\ln(q^2/\mu^2)]$, with $da/d\ln\mu^2=-ba^2$. Differentiation gives $dK/d\ln\mu^2=-2ba^3[C-b\ln(q^2/\mu^2)]$. For nonzero $a,b$, the [principle of minimal sensitivity](principle-of-minimal-sensitivity.md) therefore gives $\boxed{\mu_*^2=q^2e^{-C/b}}$. The derivative vanishes identically through order $a^2$: this prescription retains the residual $a^3$ term generated by one-loop running of the truncated expression. Higher-order running and coefficients can shift the stationary scale. For $b=0$ or $a=0$, no unique scale is selected.
