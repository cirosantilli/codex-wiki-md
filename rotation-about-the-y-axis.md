# Rotation about the y-axis

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation gate](rotation-gate.md)

The one-qubit [rotation gate](rotation-gate.md)

$$
R_y(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\theta/2)&-\sin(\theta/2)\\\sin(\theta/2)&\cos(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix}
$$

satisfies $R_y(2\theta)|0\rangle=\cos\theta|0\rangle+\sin\theta|1\rangle$ and $R_y(\alpha)R_y(\beta)=R_y(\alpha+\beta)$. The latter identity enables a [binary-angle implementation of a quantum variable rotation](binary-angle-implementation-of-a-quantum-variable-rotation.md).
