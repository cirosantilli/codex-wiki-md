# Weighted path length

↑ **Parent:** [Path in a graph](path-in-a-graph.md)

The weighted length of a [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) is the sum of its [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) weights. With positive weights, removing a closed excursion from a [walk](walk-in-a-graph.md) strictly decreases its length. Hence a shortest walk between two vertices is a [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md), and minimizing weighted length is the [shortest path problem](shortest-path-problem-split.md).
