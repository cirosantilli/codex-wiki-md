# Gravimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesy](geodesy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravimeter)

An instrument measuring gravitational acceleration or its change. Drift, tide, platform motion and reference-height corrections are needed to interpret small [gravity anomalies](gravity-anomaly.md).
