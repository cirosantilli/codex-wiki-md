# Inertial coordinates inside a slowly rotating spherical shell

↑ **Parent:** [Frame dragging inside a slowly rotating spherical shell](frame-dragging-inside-a-slowly-rotating-spherical-shell.md)

At linear order in $\epsilon=GM/R$ and the leading rotation term, this coordinate change takes the interior shell metric to [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md). Consequently the retained interior [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) vanishes although inertial axes rotate relative to distant reference axes. A freely moving particle has $\mathbf X=\mathbf X_0+\mathbf V T$, while its original coordinates obey $d^2\mathbf x/dt^2=2\boldsymbol\omega\times d\mathbf x/dt$ to first rotation order. Terms of order $\omega^2$ would include centrifugal acceleration and are outside this approximation. A [gyroscope](gyroscope.md) at rest to the retained order has its parallel-transported spin precessing at $\boldsymbol\omega$ in the original coordinates.
