# Random-to-top card shuffle

↑ **Parent:** [Cutoff for Markov chains](cutoff-for-markov-chains.md)

The random-to-top shuffle chooses one uniformly random card and moves it to the top of the deck. The time at which every card has been chosen is a [strong stationary time](strong-stationary-time.md), yielding cutoff at $n\log n$ with a window of order $n$.
