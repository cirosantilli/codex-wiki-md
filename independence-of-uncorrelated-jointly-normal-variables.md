# Independence of uncorrelated jointly normal variables

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md)

Two subvectors of a jointly [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) are independent exactly when their cross-covariance matrix is zero.
