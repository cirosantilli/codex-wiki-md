# Pseudo spin valve

↑ **Parent:** [Spin valve](spin-valve.md)

A [spin valve](spin-valve.md) with two ferromagnetic layers of different [magnetic coercivities](magnetic-coercivity.md), allowing separate reversal without requiring one layer to be rigidly exchange-pinned. A continuous weakly coupling spacer permits a controllable antiparallel state.
