# Mean photon energy

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein distribution](bose-einstein-distribution.md)

The number and energy integrals of a zero-chemical-potential [photon](photon.md) gas give $n=2\zeta(3)T^3/\pi^2$ and $\rho=\pi^2T^4/15$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). Their ratio is the mean energy per [photon](photon.md). It is useful for converting a relic number-density ratio into an energy-density ratio.
