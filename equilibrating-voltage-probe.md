# Equilibrating voltage probe

↑ **Parent:** [Landauer-Büttiker formula](landauer-buttiker-formula.md)

An ideal [voltage](voltage.md) probe is a reservoir that equilibrates incident carriers but draws zero net average current. Its [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is fixed by that zero-current condition, and can differ from a nearby geometrical point's inferred diffusive potential in a ballistic device. Reading the probe therefore requires the transmission network, not just a local Ohm law.
