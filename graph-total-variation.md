# Graph total variation

↑ **Parent:** [Total variation denoising](total-variation-denoising.md)

The graph total variation measures absolute variation of a vertex signal across [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). It is independent of the orientation used for the [oriented incidence matrix](oriented-incidence-matrix.md). It vanishes exactly on signals constant on each [connected component of a graph](component-graph-theory.md). A [graph total variation denoising](graph-total-variation-denoising.md) estimator combines this penalty with squared error, favouring signals that are piecewise constant on connected regions.
