# Quantum uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Observable](observable.md)

For a normalized state with finite second moment of a [quantum observable](observable.md) $Q$, its uncertainty is the standard deviation of the measurement outcome: $(\Delta Q)^2=\langle Q^2\rangle-\langle Q\rangle^2$. This equals $\|(Q-\langle Q\rangle)\psi\|^2$. The [Robertson uncertainty principle](robertson-uncertainty-principle.md) bounds products of uncertainties using the expectation of the [commutator](commutator.md).
