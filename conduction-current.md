# Conduction current

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)

A [conduction current](conduction-current.md) is transport of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) by mobile carriers through matter. For a local isotropic ohmic medium, its [current density](current-density.md) is $\mathbf J_c=\sigma\mathbf E$, with [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) $\sigma$. It differs from [displacement current](displacement-current.md) $\partial\mathbf D/\partial t$, which can be nonzero in a capacitor's insulating gap without carriers crossing the gap.
