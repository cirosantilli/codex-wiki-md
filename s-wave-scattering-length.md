# S-wave scattering length

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering length from a partial-wave S-matrix](scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md)

The zero-energy parameter of the angular-momentum-zero partial wave. For a short-range interaction in three dimensions, its leading elastic [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) is $f_0(k)\simeq- a_s/(1+ika_s)$ when effective-range corrections are negligible. For identical dilute [bosons](boson.md) this parameter fixes the leading contact coupling $g_B=4\pi\hbar^2a_s/m_B$.
