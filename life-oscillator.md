# Life oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Conway Game of Life](conway-game-of-life.md)

A [Life oscillator](life-oscillator.md) is a nonconstant [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) of the [Conway's Game of Life](conway-game-of-life.md) update [function](function-split.md) $F$. Its period is the least positive [integer](integer.md) $p$ for which $F^p(A)=A$, comparing the entire live-cell [set](set-split.md) at the same coordinates. A configuration that never changes is instead a [fixed point](fixed-point.md); a [Life glider](life-glider.md) is not a [Life oscillator](life-oscillator.md) on the infinite board because it returns at displaced coordinates. The [Life blinker](life-blinker.md) has period two.[Johnston and Greene, chapter 3: oscillators and their periods](https://github.com/nathanieljohnston/game-of-life-book/blob/master/oscillators.tex)
