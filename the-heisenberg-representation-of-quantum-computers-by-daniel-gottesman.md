# The Heisenberg Representation of Quantum Computers by Daniel Gottesman

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Daniel Gottesman](work-by-daniel-gottesman.md)

[Primary preprint record](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9807006) and [full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9807006). Section 3 defines the [Clifford group](clifford-group-quantum-computing.md) as the normalizer of the [Pauli group](pauli-group.md); Table 1 gives the generator conjugations used to track [Clifford circuits](clifford-circuit.md). The preprint was submitted on 1 July 1998 and the conference contribution was published in 1999, preceding the examination.
