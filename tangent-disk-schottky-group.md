# Tangent-disk Schottky group

↑ **Parent:** [Schottky group](schottky-group.md)

A [Tangent-disk Schottky group](tangent-disk-schottky-group.md) uses side-pairing disks with disjoint interiors but permits boundaries to meet. This convention must be distinguished from the strictly separated, purely loxodromic [Schottky group](schottky-group.md) convention. For example, the maps $A(z)=z+2$ and $B(z)=z/(iz+1)$ pair the half-plane disks $\Re z<-1,\Re z>1$ and the disks $|z-i|<1,|z+i|<1$. The [ping-pong lemma](ping-pong-lemma.md) gives a free discrete group. With $C=ABA^{-1}B^{-1}$, the subgroup generated by $B,C$ is conjugate by $M(z)=iz/(2-z)$ to the principal level-two modular group, generated by $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$ and $\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$. Thus its [Kleinian limit set](limit-set-of-a-kleinian-group.md) contains the circle $|z-1|=1$ and its translate $|z-3|=1$. Its [Hausdorff dimension](hausdorff-dimension.md) is at least one and it cannot be [homeomorphic](local-homeomorphism.md) to a single circle.
