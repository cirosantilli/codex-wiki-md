# Nuclear gamma-ray multipole selection rules

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma decay](gamma-decay.md)

A real [photon](photon.md) emitted in [gamma decay](gamma-decay.md) carries a radiation multipole of integer [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $L\geq1$. The triangle rule gives the displayed range. An electric multipole $EL$ requires $\pi_f/\pi_i=(-1)^L$, while a magnetic multipole $ML$ requires $\pi_f/\pi_i=(-1)^{L+1}$. There is no real single-photon $E0$ decay; an electric monopole transition can instead proceed through [internal conversion](internal-conversion.md) or, above threshold, pair creation. In the long-wavelength regime $kR\ll1$, increasing $L$ usually strongly suppresses the rate, but nuclear transition matrix elements and collective enhancement can make an allowed lower multipole weaker than a higher one.
