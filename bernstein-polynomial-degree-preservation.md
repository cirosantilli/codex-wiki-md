# Bernstein polynomial degree preservation

↑ **Parent:** [Bernstein polynomial](bernstein-polynomial.md)

For $K$ with a binomial distribution of parameters $n,x$, the Bernstein image of a monomial is $B_n(x^m)=\mathbb E(K/n)^m$. Expand $K^m$ in the monic [falling factorial](falling-factorial.md) [basis](basis.md). Since $\mathbb E K^{\underline r}=n^{\underline r}x^r$, the [leading coefficient](leading-coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md) becomes $n^{\underline m}/n^m$, which is nonzero for $m\leq n$. Thus an actual degree-$m$ [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) retains its degree, even though its lower [coefficients](coefficient.md) generally change.
