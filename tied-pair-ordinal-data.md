# Tied pair (ordinal data)

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal categorical variable](ordinal-categorical-variable.md)

Two observations tied on a variable have equal recorded values of that variable. For observations of two [ordinal categorical variables](ordinal-categorical-variable.md), distinguish pairs tied only on the first, only on the second, or on both. [Goodman and Kruskal's gamma](goodman-and-kruskal-s-gamma.md) excludes all such pairs, whereas [Somers' D](somers-d.md) retains ties on the response when measuring directional predictive association. Consequently these coefficients can differ markedly for the same [contingency table](contingency-table.md).
