# Piezoelectric ceramic

↑ **Parent:** [Piezoelectric energy harvesting](piezoelectric-energy-harvesting.md)

A ceramic material used for [piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md), often offering large charge response and electromechanical coupling with comparatively high stiffness. Brittle fracture, mechanical impedance matching and mounting conditions can limit a vibration harvester. A high charge coefficient alone is not a device [power](power.md) ranking.
