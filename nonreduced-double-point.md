# Nonreduced double point

↑ **Parent:** [Nonreduced scheme](nonreduced-scheme.md)

The spectrum of the [dual number](dual-number.md) ring $k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ is a [nonreduced scheme](nonreduced-scheme.md) of length two supported at one point. Its nonzero nilpotent $\epsilon$ retains first-order information that the underlying singleton topological space forgets. It differs from the disjoint union of two reduced $k$-points.
