# Gaussian initial data for the heat equation

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian heat kernel](gaussian-heat-kernel.md)

For $D,\gamma>0$, the displayed [function](function-split.md) solves the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) $u_t=Du_{xx}$ and has [initial condition](initial-condition.md) $u(x,0)=Ae^{-\gamma(x-x_0)^2}$. Set $s=1+4\gamma Dt$. Direct [differentiation](differentiation.md) gives

$$
u_t=\left(-\frac{2\gamma D}{s}+\frac{4\gamma^2D(x-x_0)^2}{s^2}\right)u,
\qquad
u_{xx}=\left(-\frac{2\gamma}{s}+\frac{4\gamma^2(x-x_0)^2}{s^2}\right)u.
$$

These identities prove the equation. The total [integral](integral-split.md) is $A\sqrt{\pi/\gamma}$, independent of time. The real smooth formula is valid for $t>-1/(4\gamma D)$, including a finite interval of negative time for this particular initial profile. For nonzero $A$, its value at $x_0$ diverges as the lower time endpoint is approached. The [superposition principle](superposition-principle.md) combines finitely many such profiles with different centers or widths.
