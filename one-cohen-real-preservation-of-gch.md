# One-Cohen-real preservation of GCH

↑ **Parent:** [Countable forcing](countable-forcing.md)

Over a [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) ground model, adding one Cohen real preserves every infinite power $2^\lambda=\lambda^+$. For the countable [forcing](forcing-split.md), nice [subset](subset.md) [forcing names](forcing-name.md) for $\lambda$ number at most $(2^{\aleph_0})^\lambda=2^\lambda$ in the ground model. Old [subsets](subset.md) give the matching lower bound and the [countable chain condition for forcing](countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) preserves [cardinals](cardinal-number.md). Starting from the [constructible universe](constructible-universe.md), the new real is nonconstructible while the constructible levels remain unchanged.
