# Finite-range partial-wave threshold law

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering phase shift](scattering-phase-shift.md)

For regular low-energy elastic scattering by a finite-range [central potential](central-potential.md), higher [partial waves](partial-wave.md) are suppressed by their [centrifugal barriers](centrifugal-barrier.md). Matching the regular interior radial solution to small-argument exterior spherical Bessel functions gives the displayed scaling of [scattering phase shift](scattering-phase-shift.md), in the generic absence of a threshold singularity. Since the channel amplitude is proportional to $\sin\delta_\ell/k$, it scales as $k^{2\ell}$. The $\ell=0$ term is isotropic and normally leads. A tuned vanishing leading amplitude or a threshold resonance requires examining the next terms rather than applying the generic statement blindly.
