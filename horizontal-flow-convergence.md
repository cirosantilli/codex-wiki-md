# Horizontal flow convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence](divergence.md)

Horizontal flow convergence is the negative horizontal [divergence](divergence.md) of a [velocity](velocity.md) field. Positive convergence reduces the horizontal area of a material patch; for constant-depth linear [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md), the surface equation is $\eta_t=HC$, so convergence raises the free surface and divergence lowers it. This is a fluid-flow notion, distinct from convergence of a numerical sequence.
