# Derivative expansion of a four-point vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Four-point one-particle-irreducible correlation function](four-point-one-particle-irreducible-correlation-function.md)

The small-external-momentum expansion of a [four-point one-particle-irreducible correlation function](four-point-one-particle-irreducible-correlation-function.md) generates a local quartic term followed by four-field terms with derivatives, for example $\phi^2(\partial\phi)^2$ and higher-derivative operators, modulo [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). This is an analytic expansion when there is an appropriate gap and regulator smoothness. A sharp projector on every internal momentum can instead generate nonanalytic boundary artifacts; then the local expansion is a smooth-regulator or fixed-loop-cutoff convention, not an automatic theorem about the entire sharp-cutoff vertex.
