# Self-adjoint endpoint conditions for filament bending

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic filament](elastic-filament.md)

For the [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md) operator $K=A\partial_x^4$, two [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) operations give boundary form $A[\overline u v'''-\overline{u'}v''+\overline{u''}v'-\overline{u'''}v]$. Four elementary homogeneous choices at each endpoint are $h=h'=0$ (clamped), $h=h''=0$ (hinged), $h''=h'''=0$ (free), and $h'=h'''=0$ (slope-constrained and transverse-force-free). Each defines a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) on a finite interval when imposed at both ends. More general real [Robin boundary conditions](robin-boundary-condition.md) also give [self-adjointness](self-adjoint-operator.md); the four choices are not an exhaustive classification of all boundary subspaces.
