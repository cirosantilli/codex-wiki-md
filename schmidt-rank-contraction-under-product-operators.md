# Schmidt-rank contraction under product operators

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt rank](schmidt-rank.md)

The coefficient matrix of a bipartite [pure state](pure-state.md) has rank equal to its [Schmidt rank](schmidt-rank.md), by [singular value decomposition](singular-value-decomposition.md). A local product operation $A\otimes B$ sends it to $AMB^{\mathsf T}$, whose rank cannot increase. A complete classical transcript of an [LOCC](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md) protocol still selects one product [Kraus operator](kraus-operator.md), so every nonzero branch obeys the bound. Discarding the transcript gives a mixed output with [Schmidt number](schmidt-number.md) at most the initial rank.
