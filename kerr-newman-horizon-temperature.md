# Kerr-Newman horizon temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr-Newman metric](kerr-newman-metric.md)

The outer-horizon [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) is $(r_+-r_-)/(2(r_+^2+a^2))$. [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md) or nondegenerate [Euclidean black-hole regularity condition](euclidean-black-hole-regularity-condition.md) gives $T_H=\kappa/(2\pi)$ in units $G=\hbar=c=k_B=1$. The zero-temperature extremal value is the nonextremal limit; conical regularity alone does not fix a Euclidean period at a degenerate horizon.
