# Killing field is determined by its value and first covariant derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md)

On a [connected](connected-space.md) [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), these data determine a [Killing field](killing-vector-field.md) uniquely, without a completeness assumption. The [second covariant derivative of a Killing vector](second-covariant-derivative-of-a-killing-vector.md) makes its restriction to every [geodesic](geodesic.md) a [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md). The difference of two fields with identical data has zero Jacobi initial values and vanishes in a [convex normal neighborhood](convex-normal-neighbourhood.md). The set on which both its value and [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) vanish is closed by continuity and open by the same local argument, so connectedness makes it the whole manifold.
