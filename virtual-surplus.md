# Virtual surplus

↑ **Parent:** [Virtual valuation](virtual-valuation.md)

Virtual surplus weights allocation amounts by the corresponding [virtual valuations](virtual-valuation.md). Under the hypotheses of the [virtual-surplus revenue identity](virtual-surplus-revenue-identity.md), maximizing it subject to implementability and feasibility yields a revenue-optimal mechanism when lowest-type utilities can be normalized to zero.
