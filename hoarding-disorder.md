# Hoarding disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Mental disorder](mental-disorder.md)

A [clinical syndrome](clinical-syndrome.md) characterized by persistent difficulty discarding possessions and accumulation that compromises use of living space, with relevant distress or impairment. Differential assessment asks whether the appearance is better accounted for by another condition or context; excessive acquisition and insight can vary.
