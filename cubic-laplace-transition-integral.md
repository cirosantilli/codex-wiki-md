# Cubic Laplace transition integral

↑ **Parent:** [Scorer Hi function](scorer-hi-function.md)

The integral $I(x)=\int_0^\infty e^{xt-t^3/3}\,dt$ is a rescaled [Scorer Hi function](scorer-hi-function.md). It has the three useful limits

$$
I(-M)\sim M^{-1},\qquad I(0)=3^{-2/3}\Gamma(1/3),\qquad I(M)\sim\sqrt\pi M^{-1/4}e^{2M^{3/2}/3},\quad M\to\infty.
$$

For the negative-argument limit, scale $t=s/M$ and use the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md). At zero, substitute $s=t^3/3$ and use the [Gamma integral](gamma-integral.md). For the positive-argument limit, the exponent has its maximum at $t=\sqrt M$, with second [derivative](derivative.md) $-2\sqrt M$; [Laplace's method](laplace-s-method.md) gives the displayed factor. These estimates describe a [cubic endpoint-to-saddle transition](cubic-endpoint-to-saddle-transition.md).
