# Auxiliary-metric brane action

↑ **Parent:** [Auxiliary worldvolume metric](auxiliary-worldvolume-metric.md)

The auxiliary-metric brane action is a quadratic embedding action with an independent [worldvolume metric](auxiliary-worldvolume-metric.md). Its embedding equation is $\partial_\mu(\sqrt{-\gamma}\gamma^{\mu\nu}\partial_\nu X^a)=0$. Its metric equation is $h_{\mu\nu}-\gamma_{\mu\nu}(\gamma^{\rho\sigma}h_{\rho\sigma}-(p-1))/2=0$. For $p\ne1$, tracing determines $\gamma^{\rho\sigma}h_{\rho\sigma}=p+1$ and hence $\gamma=h$; eliminating the auxiliary field gives twice the invariant [worldvolume](worldvolume.md) volume, before including the overall [brane tension](brane-tension.md). The [Weyl-invariance exception for the string among branes](weyl-invariance-exception-for-the-string-among-branes.md) prevents unique elimination at $p=1$.
