# Tanabe-Sugano diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

A plot of many-electron ligand-field term energies relative to the [ground state](ground-state.md) against a normalized ligand-field strength. Axes commonly use $E/B$ and $\Delta_o/B$, with the ratio of other interelectronic parameters stipulated. A change of ground-state term can create a break in the plotted reference.
