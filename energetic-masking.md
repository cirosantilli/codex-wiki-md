# Energetic masking

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic masking](acoustic-masking.md)

Reduced target detectability associated with overlap between target and interfering acoustic energy in the listener's representation. It is distinguished conceptually from [informational masking](informational-masking.md), although both can occur in one task and the distinction does not identify one anatomical site automatically.
