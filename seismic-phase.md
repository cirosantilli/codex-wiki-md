# Seismic phase

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

A [seismic phase](seismic-phase.md) labels a propagation type and path, such as direct P, direct S, a reflection or a converted wave. Misidentifying the phase assigns the wrong travel-time function even if its arrival is picked precisely.
