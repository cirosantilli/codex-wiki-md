# Spin-spin interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

A [spin-spin interaction](spin-spin-interaction.md) couples two [spin angular momenta](spin.md). In an isotropic model it is proportional to their [dot product](dot-product.md), whose expectation in a total-pair-spin state follows from $2\mathbf S_i\cdot\mathbf S_j=S_{ij}^2-S_i^2-S_j^2$. Magnetic interactions generate examples in atoms and effective colour-magnetic interactions in [hadron](hadron.md) models.
