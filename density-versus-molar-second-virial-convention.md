# Density versus molar second virial convention

↑ **Parent:** [Second virial coefficient](second-virial-coefficient.md)

In the number-density [virial expansion](virial-expansion.md), $p/(nk_BT)=1+B_2n+\cdots$, so $B_2$ has units of [volume](volume.md). With [molar concentration](molar-concentration.md) instead, the coefficient is $B_{2,\rm mol}=N_AB_2$, where $N_A$ is the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md).
