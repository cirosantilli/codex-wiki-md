# Electromagnetic momentum density

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md)

The electromagnetic momentum density in vacuum is $\mathbf g=\epsilon_0\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$. It equals the [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) divided by $c^2$, expressing the relativistic relation between energy flux and momentum density.
