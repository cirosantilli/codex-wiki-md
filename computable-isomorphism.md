# Computable isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Recursive presentation of a structure](recursive-presentation-of-a-structure.md)

A computable isomorphism is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) between presented structures whose underlying map is a [total computable function](total-computable-function.md). For presentations on $\mathbb N$, its inverse is computable too: enumerate inputs until the required output occurs. Two abstractly [isomorphic](isomorphism.md) structures with a [recursive presentation of a structure](recursive-presentation-of-a-structure.md) need not admit a [computable isomorphism](computable-isomorphism.md) between the chosen presentations.
