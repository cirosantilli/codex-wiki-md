# Biomagnification

↑ **Parent:** [Ecology](ecology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomagnification)

Increase in the concentration of some substances along feeding relationships between successive [trophic levels](trophic-level.md). It depends on uptake, transfer and elimination and is not a universal property of all chemicals.
