# Prime-power conjugacy-class obstruction to simplicity

↑ **Parent:** [Burnside's theorem](burnside-s-theorem.md)

A nonabelian [simple group](simple-group.md) has no nonidentity [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) of prime-power size. Coprime-degree [irreducible characters](irreducible-character.md) vanish on such an element: the [conjugacy-class sum](conjugacy-class-sum.md) makes the character-to-degree ratio an [algebraic integer](algebraic-integer.md), and the [Kronecker theorem on algebraic integers in the unit disk](kronecker-theorem-on-algebraic-integers-in-the-unit-disk.md) makes a nonzero ratio a [root of unity](root-of-unity.md), forcing a scalar in a faithful [group representation](group-representation.md). Column [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md) then makes $1/p$ an [algebraic integer](algebraic-integer.md), impossible. This is the character-theoretic ingredient in [Burnside's theorem](burnside-s-theorem.md).
