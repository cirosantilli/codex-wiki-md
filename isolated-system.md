# Isolated system

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md)

An isolated thermodynamic system exchanges neither matter nor [energy](energy.md) with its surroundings. Its total [internal energy](internal-energy.md) is fixed, while the [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics.md) constrains its total [entropy](entropy.md).
