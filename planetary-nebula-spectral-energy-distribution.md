# Planetary-nebula spectral energy distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary nebula](planetary-nebula.md)

The [spectral energy distribution](spectral-energy-distribution.md) of a [planetary nebula](planetary-nebula.md) combines hot central-star radiation, nebular free-free/free-bound/two-photon continua, recombination and collisionally excited lines, infrared dust emission and sometimes molecular emission. Ionizing-photon supply, gas thermal/ionization balance, density, composition, geometry and extinction determine their relative strengths. A line-dominated broad-band flux is not by itself a continuum temperature measurement.
