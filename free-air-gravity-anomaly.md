# Free-air gravity anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md)

[gravity](gravity.md) reduced to a reference elevation while retaining the gravitational contribution of the intervening topographic [mass](mass.md). The local elevation correction is approximately $2g h/R$ for a spherical reference, with sign chosen to undo the measured decrease with height.
