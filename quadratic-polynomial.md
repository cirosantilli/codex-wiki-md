# Quadratic polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

A quadratic polynomial in one variable over a [field](field.md) has [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) two and the displayed form. Its [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) satisfy a [quadratic equation](quadratic-equation.md). The [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) and its associated [polynomial function](polynomial-function.md) are distinct objects: over a [finite field](finite-field.md), different [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) can define the same [function](function-split.md).
