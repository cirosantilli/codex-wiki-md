<h1 id="kleene-post-incomparability-theorem">Kleene–Post incomparability theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Turing degree](turing-degree.md)

There are incomparable [Turing degrees](turing-degree.md) strictly between the computable degree and the degree of the [diagonal halting set](diagonal-halting-set.md). An [oracle-assisted finite-extension construction](oracle-assisted-finite-extension-construction.md) alternately defeats each [Turing functional](turing-functional.md) in the two directions. Since the sets are computable in the halting oracle, their degrees are at most $0'$; incomparability makes them nonzero and strictly below $0'$. This theorem does not require the constructed sets to be [computably enumerable](recursively-enumerable-set.md). The [Friedberg–Muchnik theorem](friedberg-muchnik-theorem.md) supplies that stronger conclusion.
