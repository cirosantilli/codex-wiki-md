# Lipid divide

↑ **Parent:** [Cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md)

The [lipid divide](lipid-divide.md) is the contrast between characteristic archaeal membrane lipids and characteristic bacterial/eukaryotic membrane lipids, including glycerol stereochemistry and ether versus ester linkages. It constrains hypotheses for eukaryotic origins without uniquely determining the sequence of ancestral membrane changes.
