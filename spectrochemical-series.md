# Spectrochemical series

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

The [spectrochemical series](spectrochemical-series.md) orders ligands empirically by the splitting they produce in comparable complexes. [Pi-donor ligands](pi-donor-ligand.md) tend toward smaller splittings and [pi-acceptor ligands](pi-acceptor-ligand.md) toward larger ones. Metal, geometry and [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) still matter; a sigma-basicity scale is not an interchangeable substitute.
