# Charles Rundle Davidson

↑ **Parent:** [List of astronomers](list-of-astronomers.md)

Astronomer at the Royal Observatory, Greenwich, and coauthor of the 1920 solar-deflection report with [Frank Watson Dyson](frank-watson-dyson.md) and [Arthur Eddington](arthur-eddington.md). His institutional name record is [Royal Society fellow record NA1855](https://catalogues.royalsociety.org/CalmView/Record.aspx?id=NA1855&src=CalmView.Persons)
