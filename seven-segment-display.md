# Seven-segment display

↑ **Parent:** [Digital electronics](digital-electronics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven-segment_display)

A display whose seven separately controlled bars can form digit shapes. A [combinational logic](combinational-logic.md) decoder maps an encoded digit to seven output [bits](bit.md). Active-high versus active-low control and the required behaviour for unused input codes belong to the decoder's specification.
