# Pure-target upper bound on trace distance

↑ **Parent:** [Trace distance](trace-distance.md)

For two normalized [pure states](pure-state.md), the difference of their projectors has two [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\pm\sqrt{1-|\langle u|v\rangle|^2}$, giving that [trace distance](trace-distance.md). Write a mixed [density operator](density-matrix.md) as $\rho=\sum_jq_j|u_j\rangle\langle u_j|$. Convexity of the [trace norm](trace-norm.md) and weighted [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) give $D(\rho,|v\rangle\langle v|)\leq\sum_jq_j\sqrt{1-|\langle u_j|v\rangle|^2}\leq\sqrt{1-\langle v|\rho|v\rangle}$. Thus a small [probability](probability.md) of failing the pure-state projector test forces trace-distance proximity, including for correlated multipartite outputs.
