# Pair production

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)

[Pair production](pair-production.md) creates a [particle](particle-physics.md) and its [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) while conserving [energy](energy-split.md), [momentum](momentum-split.md) and the relevant charges. The available [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) must reach the sum of their rest masses.
