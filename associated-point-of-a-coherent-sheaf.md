# Associated point of a coherent sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md)

An associated point of a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) is a point whose corresponding prime is the annihilator of an element of the module on an affine chart. There are finitely many on a [Noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md). A section of a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) avoiding them acts injectively on the sheaf, even when the scheme is nonreduced. Over an infinite field a sufficiently ample linear system supplies such a section.
