# Radially layered coaxial cable

↑ **Parent:** [Coaxial cable](coaxial-cable.md)

For a [coaxial cable](coaxial-cable.md) with inner and outer radii $a,b$, a radial interface $c$, and [permittivities](permittivity-split.md) $\epsilon,\epsilon'$, define $T=\ln(c/a)/\epsilon+\ln(b/c)/\epsilon'$. With terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V$, the [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) is $D_\rho=V/(T\rho)$. The [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is $E_\rho=V/(T\epsilon_i\rho)$ in each layer $i$. If the outward and return [electric currents](electric-current.md) have magnitudes $I$, the [magnetic field strength](magnetic-field-strength.md) in the gap is $H_\varphi=I/(2\pi\rho)$ and the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) is $B_\varphi=\mu_iI/(2\pi\rho)$.

The proof uses [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) without free interface [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), the [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) integral, and the [magnetostatic Ampère law](magnetostatic-ampere-law.md). The axial [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) is $VI/(2\pi T\epsilon_i\rho^2)$, so its cross-sectional [surface integral](surface-integral.md) is $VI$, independently of the radial material layering and [magnetic permeabilities](permeability-electromagnetism.md). The empty inner and exterior regions have zero [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) for the regular solution with bounded exterior [electric potential](electric-potential.md) and no applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).
