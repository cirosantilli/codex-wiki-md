# Rough-surface far-field intensity per illuminated length

↑ **Parent:** [First-order rough-surface scattered field](first-order-rough-surface-scattered-field.md)

First-order Dirichlet roughness has mean-plane trace $2iq_iA_i h(x)e^{ip_ix}$, where $p_i^2+q_i^2=k^2$ and $q_i>0$. [Stationary phase](stationary-phase-method.md) applied to the [outgoing angular spectrum](outgoing-angular-spectrum.md) evaluates the far-field amplitude at tangential wavenumber $k\sin\theta_s$. For a stationary surface illuminated over length $D$, the mean squared height transform divided by $D$ tends to its [power spectrum of surface height](power-spectrum-of-surface-height.md). Thus the diffuse intensity samples the spectrum at the tangential momentum transfer. An infinite stationary surface has no finite total far-field amplitude without a window or an intensity-density convention.
