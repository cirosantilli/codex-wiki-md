# AC Stark shift

↑ **Parent:** [Stark effect](stark-effect.md)

The [energy](energy-split.md) shift produced by an oscillating [electric field](electric-field-split.md), often called a [light shift](ac-stark-shift.md). For a far-detuned two-level drive with coupling $\hbar\Omega/2$ and $\Delta=\omega_L-\omega_{eg}$, the ground-state shift is approximately $\hbar|\Omega|^2/(4\Delta)$. Multilevel atoms can have polarization-dependent differential shifts.
