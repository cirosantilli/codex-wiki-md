# Magnetic spin equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetically channelled accretion](magnetically-channelled-accretion.md)

The spin at which the net material and [magnetic torque](surface-magnetic-torque-on-an-accretion-disk.md) on an accreting star vanishes. The [corotation radius](corotation-radius.md) is typically comparable to the [magnetospheric truncation radius](magnetospheric-truncation-radius.md), but the precise torque balance depends on the coupling and any outflow.
