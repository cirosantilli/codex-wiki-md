# Vibrational progression

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic transition](electronic-transition.md)

A sequence of vibronic transitions sharing a lower vibrational level and reaching successive vibrational levels of another electronic state. [frequency](frequency.md) differences remove the common electronic origin and reveal vibrational spacings.
