# Weighted essential spectrum of a Fisher travelling front

↑ **Parent:** [Exponentially weighted L2 space](exponentially-weighted-l2-space.md)

The [linearization](linearization.md) at a [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) $w_c$ is $A=\partial_z^2+c\partial_z+k-2w_c$. Conjugation by $e^{\gamma z}$ changes this to $\partial_z^2+(c-2\gamma)\partial_z+\gamma^2-c\gamma+k-2w_c$. The choice $\gamma=c/2$ removes the first derivative and makes the operator [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md). Its two limiting potentials are $-k-c^2/4$ and $k-c^2/4$, so [essential-spectrum invariance under relatively compact perturbations](essential-spectrum-invariance-under-relatively-compact-perturbations.md) gives $\sigma_{\mathrm{ess}}=(-\infty,k-c^2/4]$. This lies strictly below zero for $c>2\sqrt{k}$ but touches zero at the critical speed. Stability of this [essential spectrum](essential-spectrum-of-a-closed-operator.md) alone is not a general nonlinear stability theorem.
