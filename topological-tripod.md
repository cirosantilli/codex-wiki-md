# Topological tripod

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold](topological-manifold.md)

Three intervals joined at one endpoint form a [compact](compact-space.md) [connected](connected-space.md) metric space. It is [second countable](second-countable-space.md), [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) and [paracompact](paracompact-space.md), but is not a [topological manifold](topological-manifold.md). At regular arm points a putative manifold has [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) one; deleting the junction from a small neighborhood instead gives three components, incompatible with an interval chart. This local obstruction precedes any question about a smooth structure.
