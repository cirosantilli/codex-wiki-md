# Electron counting

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

[Electron counting](electron-counting.md) records metal valence [electrons](electron.md) plus ligand donations using a declared convention. In ionic counting, subtract metal [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) from its group number, then add donor [electrons](electron.md); in neutral counting, combine neutral metal and ligand fragments consistently. An Fe–Fe single bond contributes one [electron](electron.md) to the count at each metal.
