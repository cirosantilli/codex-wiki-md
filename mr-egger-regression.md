# MR-Egger regression

↑ **Parent:** [Mendelian randomization](mendelian-randomization.md)

MR-Egger regression fits genetic outcome associations to genetic [exposure](exposure.md) associations with a free intercept. Its slope estimates a [causal effect](causal-effect.md) under the [InSIDE assumption](inside-assumption.md) and appropriate instrument-strength assumptions; its intercept assesses average directional [horizontal pleiotropy](horizontal-pleiotropy.md). A nonsignificant intercept, especially with few [genetic variants](genetic-variant.md), does not prove the [exclusion restriction](exclusion-restriction.md) for every instrument.
