# Planar rotational symplectic reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic reduction](symplectic-reduction.md)

For a planar [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md), rotation has [moment map](moment-map.md) $\ell=xp_y-yp_x$. At $\ell\ne0$ its level is regular and the circle action is free. Polar canonical coordinates give $\omega=dr\wedge dp_r+d\theta\wedge d\ell$. Fixing $\ell$ and quotienting $\theta$ leaves $T^*\mathbb R_{>0}$ with the displayed form. The unit-mass free [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) becomes $p_r^2/2+\ell^2/(2r^2)$.
