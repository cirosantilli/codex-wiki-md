# Second fundamental form of a ring torus

↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)

For major radius $a>b>0$ and minor radius $b$, let $R=a+b\cos u$. With the inward normal determined by the usual ordered $(u,v)$ parametrization, the [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md) and [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) are

$$
I=b^2du^2+R^2dv^2,\qquad II=bdu^2+R\cos u\,dv^2.
$$

Reversing the normal reverses $II$. The [Gaussian curvature of a ring torus](gaussian-curvature-of-a-ring-torus.md) is independent of this orientation.
