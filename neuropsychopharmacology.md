# Neuropsychopharmacology

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology](pharmacology-split.md)

Study of how pharmacological manipulations interact with [neural circuits](neural-circuit.md) and psychological functions. A change in task performance can arise through altered [learning](learning.md), retrieval, [motivation](motivation.md), sensation or motor execution; the mechanism must be distinguished experimentally.
