# Albert Einstein

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Einstein)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md) whose work connects [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md), [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md), [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) and the [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md). He worked at the Institute for Advanced Study from 1933 until his death in 1955.[Institutional profile: physicist, life dates and Institute appointment](albert-einstein-institutional-profile-by-institute-for-advanced-study.md)

In 1905, [Albert Einstein](albert-einstein.md) used the [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md) to explore quantized [light](light.md), related [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) to molecular agitation, formulated [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md), and connected changes in [mass](mass.md) and [energy](energy-split.md). These problems link visible measurements to otherwise inaccessible structure: emitted [electron](electron.md) energies, suspended-particle displacements and comparisons of clocks.[March, May, June and September 1905: the four papers and their physical questions](albert-einstein-the-great-works-by-center-for-history-of-physics.md)

The [Millikan photoelectric experiment](millikan-photoelectric-experiment.md) follows one of those predictions into the laboratory. Its stopping-voltage measurements test a quantitative relation; the experiment's apparatus, surface preparation and electrical corrections matter to that inference. The [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations-split.md) and [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) lead to a different set of tests involving [gravity](gravity.md) and [spacetime](spacetime.md).

The [Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921](nobel-prize-in-physics-in-1921.md) recognized his theoretical work, particularly the law of the [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md); the award was presented in 1922.[Prize motivation and the note that Einstein received the 1921 award in 1922](albert-einstein-facts-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
