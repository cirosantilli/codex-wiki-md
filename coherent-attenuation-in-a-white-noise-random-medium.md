# Coherent attenuation in a white-noise random medium

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent and diffuse wave fields](coherent-and-diffuse-wave-fields.md)

For a [parabolic wave equation](parabolic-wave-equation.md) driven by longitudinal [Gaussian white noise](gaussian-white-noise.md) with transverse [covariance kernel](covariance-kernel.md) $B$, the mean envelope obeys $\partial_x m=i\Delta_\perp m/(2k_0)-\gamma m$. This closure requires [independent increments](independent-increments.md) in the longitudinal direction; [statistical homogeneity](statistical-homogeneity.md) and one-point [Gaussian distributions](normal-distribution.md) alone are insufficient.
