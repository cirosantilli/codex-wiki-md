# Zero-handle

↑ **Parent:** [Handle attachment](handle-attachment.md)

A zero-handle in an $n$-dimensional [handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md) is $D^0\times D^n=D^n$. Its attaching region is empty, so it starts a connected component of the manifold. In dimension four it is a four-ball; adding [one-handles](one-handle.md) and [two-handles](two-handle.md) gives a four-dimensional handlebody with a two-dimensional CW spine.
