# Projective conic

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane](projective-plane.md)

A [projective conic](projective-conic.md) is the zero locus in a [projective plane](projective-plane.md) of a nonzero homogeneous degree-two [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). The equation is considered up to multiplication by a nonzero scalar. Over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) for the [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) has rank three, two, or one: these give a nonsingular conic, two distinct [projective lines](projective-line.md), or one line counted twice. Diagonalization of a [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) and rescaling its nonzero diagonal entries prove this classification. The smooth model $XZ-Y^2=0$ is parametrized by the degree-two [Veronese map](veronese-map.md) $[s:t]\mapsto[s^2:st:t^2]$.
