# Schlieren photography

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schlieren_photography)

[Schlieren photography](schlieren-photography.md) makes spatial changes of [refractive index](refractive-index.md) visible by converting deflections of [light rays](light-ray.md) into contrast at an [optical camera](optical-camera.md). In a [gas](gas.md), [mass density](density.md) variations can produce these deflections through [refraction](refraction.md). A knife edge in the focused beam is a common implementation.
