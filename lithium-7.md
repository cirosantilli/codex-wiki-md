# Lithium-7

↑ **Parent:** [Lithium](lithium.md)

Lithium-7 has three [protons](proton.md) and four [neutrons](neutron.md). In [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) it can form directly or through [beryllium-7](beryllium-7.md), which later undergoes [electron capture](electron-capture.md). Competition between direct lithium production, lithium destruction and beryllium production produces a valley in the primordial lithium abundance versus [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md). Its measured stellar surface abundance need not equal its birth abundance because stellar transport and nuclear burning can remove lithium.
