# Random slope

↑ **Parent:** [Random effect](random-effect.md)

A [random slope](random-slope.md) multiplies a predictor by a group-specific latent coefficient. With $b_j\sim N(0,\tau^2)$, a contribution $b_jt_i$ induces [covariance](covariance.md) $\tau^2t_it_k$ for two observations in group $j$. A [random slope](random-slope.md) does not necessarily include a [random intercept](random-intercept.md).
