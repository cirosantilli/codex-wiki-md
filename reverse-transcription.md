# Reverse transcription

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)

[Reverse transcription](reverse-transcription.md) makes [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) from an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) template using a [reverse transcriptase](reverse-transcriptase.md). It reverses the template/product relationship of ordinary [transcription](transcription-biology.md), which makes [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) from [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). The resulting [Complementary DNA](complementary-dna.md) can supply a template for [PCR amplification](polymerase-chain-reaction.md).[RNA as the Starting Material and Reverse Transcriptase Enzymes](basic-principles-of-rt-qpcr-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)
