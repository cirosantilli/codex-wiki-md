# Basal traction on a tectonic plate

↑ **Parent:** [Plate motion](plate-motion.md)

[Traction](traction.md) transmitted between the base of a [tectonic plate](tectonic-plate.md) and the [mantle](planetary-mantle.md). Its component parallel to the plate can drive or resist [plate motion](plate-motion.md), depending on the relative motion and surrounding [mantle flow](mantle-flow.md). Its direction should not be inferred from the word “drag” alone.
