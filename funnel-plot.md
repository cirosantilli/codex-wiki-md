# Funnel plot

↑ **Parent:** [Meta-analysis](meta-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funnel_plot)

A [funnel plot](funnel-plot.md) displays study effect estimates against a measure of their precision, often [standard error](standard-error.md) increasing down the vertical axis. Under a common-effect model, imprecise estimates spread more widely around the effect. Asymmetry is evidence of a [small-study effect](small-study-effect.md) and can be consistent with [publication bias](publication-bias.md), but it has other possible explanations.
