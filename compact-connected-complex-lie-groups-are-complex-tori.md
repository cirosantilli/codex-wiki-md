# Compact connected complex Lie groups are complex tori

↑ **Parent:** [Complex Lie group](complex-lie-group.md)

Every scalar [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on a compact connected [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) is constant by the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md). Apply this to each entry of the holomorphic [Adjoint representation of a Lie group](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md); its value is constantly the identity. Differentiation makes the complex [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) abelian, hence connectedness makes the [group](group-split.md) abelian. The holomorphic [Lie exponential map](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md) identifies it with $\mathbb C^n$ modulo a [discrete subgroup](discrete-subgroup.md). Compactness forces that [subgroup](subgroup.md) to span the underlying [real vector space](real-vector-space.md), giving a full-rank [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) and a [complex torus](complex-torus.md).
