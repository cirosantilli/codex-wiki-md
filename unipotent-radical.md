# Unipotent radical

↑ **Parent:** [Unipotent algebraic group](unipotent-algebraic-group.md)

It is the largest connected normal unipotent subgroup of a [linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md). A connected [reductive algebraic group](reductive-group.md) has trivial unipotent radical. The subgroup $1+J(E)$ of a finite-dimensional algebra's unit group is closed, connected and unipotent.
