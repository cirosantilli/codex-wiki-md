# Chiral gauge spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Chirality (physics)](chirality-physics.md)

A chiral gauge spectrum has independent left-handed [Weyl spinors](weyl-spinor.md) in complex [gauge group representations](gauge-group-representation.md) without an obligatory conjugate pair of left-handed [Weyl spinors](weyl-spinor.md). The right-handed antiparticles required by the [CPT theorem](cpt-theorem.md) do not themselves make a spectrum vectorlike.
