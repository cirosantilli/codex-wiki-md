# Solar granule

↑ **Parent:** [Sun](sun.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_granule)

A solar granule is a photospheric [convection cell](convection-cell.md), with relatively hot rising material in its interior and cooler descending material near its edges. The pattern formed by their bright interiors and darker edges is [solar granulation](solar-granulation.md). Their evolving horizontal outflows can sweep weak [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) toward surrounding lanes.
