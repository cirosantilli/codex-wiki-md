# Operator formalism

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

The [operator formalism](operator-formalism.md) describes a quantum system using states in a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) and observables represented by [self-adjoint operators](self-adjoint-operator.md) with suitable domains on that [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). Evolution and correlation functions are computed from [Hamiltonian operators](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) and their [time-ordered products](time-ordered-product.md). For free [quantized fields](quantized-field.md), the oscillator construction gives a [Fock space](fock-space.md). The [operator-path-integral equivalence](operator-path-integral-equivalence.md) relates these matrix elements to integrals over field histories.
