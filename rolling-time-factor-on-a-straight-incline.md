# Rolling-time factor on a straight incline

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

For a rigid body rolling from rest down a straight incline with fixed rolling radius $R$ and axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$, [translation](translation-geometry.md) and [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) give $(M+I/R^2)\ddot s=Mg\sin\theta$. A smooth slide has $\ddot s=g\sin\theta$. Travel over the same distance from rest therefore gives the displayed time ratio. [Static friction](static-friction.md) must suffice for [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md); its contact point is at rest, so this ideal friction does no work. A uniform solid cylinder has $I=MR^2/2$ and ratio $\sqrt{3/2}$.
