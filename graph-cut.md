# Graph cut

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

A graph cut consists of the [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) joining a vertex subset to its complement. In a two-group [stochastic block model](stochastic-block-model.md), crossing and within-group [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) have different [Bernoulli distributions](bernoulli-distribution.md). The [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) between two such models is the sum of the changed-edge divergences, with their order determined by which partition regards the [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) as crossing.
