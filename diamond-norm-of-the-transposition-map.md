# Diamond norm of the transposition map

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum channel discrimination](quantum-channel-discrimination.md)

For the [transposition map](transpose.md) $\Theta:M_d\to M_d$, the [induced trace norm](induced-trace-norm.md) is $\lVert\Theta\rVert_1=1$, while stabilization gives

$$
\lVert\Theta\rVert_\diamond=d.
$$

Thus an entangled [quantum ancilla](quantum-ancilla.md) can amplify the distinguishability witnessed by transposition by a factor of $d$.
