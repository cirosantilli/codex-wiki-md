# Delta baryon pion branching ratios

↑ **Parent:** [Delta baryon](delta-baryon.md)

Couple the nucleon [isospin](isospin.md) $1/2$ to the [pion](pion.md) isospin $1$. The highest state $|3/2,3/2\rangle=|p\pi^+\rangle$ is normalized. One application of the total [isospin](isospin.md) lowering operator gives $|3/2,1/2\rangle=\sqrt{2/3}|p\pi^0\rangle+\sqrt{1/3}|n\pi^+\rangle$. For a common [isospin-invariant two-body decay amplitude](isospin-invariant-two-body-decay-amplitude.md), the partial [decay rates](decay-width.md) relative to the $\Delta^{++}$ channel are $2/3$ and $1/3$. The lowest state gives $\Delta^-\to n\pi^-$. Exact relations assume isospin-degenerate masses and common kinematic factors; electromagnetic and quark-mass differences cause corrections.
