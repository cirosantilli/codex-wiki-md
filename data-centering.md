# Data centering

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)

[Data centering](data-centering.md) subtracts the [sample mean](sample-mean.md) from every observation, separately for each coordinate of multivariate data. The resulting coordinate [sample means](sample-mean.md) are zero.
