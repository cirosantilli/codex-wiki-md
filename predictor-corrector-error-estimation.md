# Predictor-corrector error estimation

↑ **Parent:** [Local error estimator](local-error-estimator.md)

A predictor and corrector give distinct approximations from the same local history. Their difference, scaled by their leading error constants and orders, can estimate a local defect. Raw differences without that calibration need not estimate the corrected solution's error. Variable-step [multistep methods](linear-multistep-method.md) also require updated coefficients and controlled history handling.
