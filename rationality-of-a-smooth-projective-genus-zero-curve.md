# Rationality of a smooth projective genus-zero curve

↑ **Parent:** [Genus of a smooth projective curve](genus-of-a-smooth-projective-curve.md)

Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), choose a point $P$ on the [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md). The [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives $\dim L(P)=2$: the complementary [canonical divisor](canonical-divisor.md) term has negative degree. A nonconstant function in $L(P)$ has exactly one simple pole, so it defines a degree-one [morphism of algebraic curves](morphism-of-algebraic-curves.md) to the [projective line](projective-line.md). A degree-one map between [smooth projective curves](smooth-projective-curve.md) is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). The existence of a rational point is essential over a field which is not algebraically closed.
