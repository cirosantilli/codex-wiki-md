# Borel subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel_subalgebra)

For a choice of positive roots, the corresponding Borel subalgebra is $\mathfrak b=\mathfrak t\oplus\mathfrak n^+$, the sum of a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) and all positive root spaces.
