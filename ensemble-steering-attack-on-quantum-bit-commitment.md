# Ensemble-steering attack on quantum bit commitment

↑ **Parent:** [Bit commitment](bit-commitment.md)

If the two honest [quantum state ensembles](quantum-state-ensemble.md) have the same [density operator](density-matrix.md), their independently prepared blocks are perfectly hiding. A dishonest sender can instead send one half of a [purification of a density operator](purification-of-a-density-operator.md) and retain the other. The [Hughston–Jozsa–Wootters theorem](hughston-jozsa-wootters-theorem.md) allows a measurement of the retained system to prepare either honest ensemble after commitment. If verification checks only the declared states, either opening is then accepted with the honest probabilities. This gives a concrete failure of binding while preserving perfect hiding.
