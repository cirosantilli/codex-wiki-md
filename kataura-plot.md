# Kataura plot

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon nanotube](carbon-nanotube.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kataura_plot)

A plot of optical transition energies against [carbon nanotube](carbon-nanotube.md) diameter, usually organized by wrapping indices and electronic type. It relates optical and resonant [Raman spectroscopy](raman-spectroscopy.md) observations to nanotube structure.
