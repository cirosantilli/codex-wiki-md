# CSP interrupt

↑ **Parent:** [CSP process](csp-process.md)

Allow $P$ to proceed while $Q$ is ready to interrupt. The first visible [CSP event](csp-event.md) of $Q$ discards $P$ and leaves the derivative of $Q$. A [CSP internal action](csp-internal-action.md) of $Q$ alone does not discard $P$.
