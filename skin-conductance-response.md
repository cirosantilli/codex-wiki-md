# Skin conductance response

↑ **Parent:** [Psychophysics](psychophysics.md)

A transient change in electrical conductance of the [skin](skin.md), often used as an index of autonomic [arousal](physiological-arousal.md). Electrode recordings provide a physiological response measure, not a direct reading of threat [beliefs](belief.md) or diagnostic status.
