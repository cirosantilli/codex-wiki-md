# Humorism

↑ **Parent:** [History of medicine](history-of-medicine.md)

A historical explanatory system in which health and [disease](disease.md) depend on the mixture and balance of bodily fluids. The four-fluid scheme includes [blood](blood.md), [humoral phlegm](humoral-phlegm.md), yellow [bile](bile.md) and [black bile](black-bile.md). These categories are not a modern analysis of [blood](blood.md) composition. [Selection 1, four distinct fluids](on-the-nature-of-man-in-the-hippocratic-corpus.md)
