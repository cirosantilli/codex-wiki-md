# Climate

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate)

The statistical properties of atmospheric and related oceanic conditions over an extended period, including means, variability and extremes. [Radiation budgets](radiation-budget.md), [ocean circulation](ocean-circulation.md) and atmospheric transport help determine its large-scale structure.
