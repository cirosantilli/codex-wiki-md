# Distributive property

↑ **Parent:** [Binary operation](binary-operation.md)

For two [binary operations](binary-operation.md), multiplication distributes over addition on the left or right when the respective displayed identity holds. If multiplication is [commutative](commutativity.md), either law gives the other. Repeated distribution expands expressions in an [incline](incline.md) into finite sums of [monomials](monomial.md). This property is distinct from [associativity](associative-property.md) of either operation.
