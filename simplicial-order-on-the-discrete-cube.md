# Simplicial order on the discrete cube

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md)

Order subsets first by increasing [cardinality](cardinality.md) and, within each level, by [lexicographic order](lexicographic-order.md), with the smallest differing coordinate belonging to the earlier set. This convention makes simplicial [initial segments](initial-segment.md) the extremizers in [Harper theorem](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md).
