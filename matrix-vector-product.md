# Matrix-vector product

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md)

The product of an $m\times n$ [matrix](matrix.md) with an $n$-component [vector](vector.md) forms one row [inner product](inner-product-split.md) per output component. A dense evaluation costs $O(mn)$ [arithmetic operations](arithmetic-operation.md) in [Big O notation](big-o-notation.md).
