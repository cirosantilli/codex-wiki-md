# BCS coherence factors

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

For real gap and the particle-hole block $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\xi_k&-\Delta_k\\-\Delta_k&-\xi_k\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, the BCS coherence factors obey $u_k^2=(1+\xi_k/E_k)/2$, $v_k^2=(1-\xi_k/E_k)/2$ and $u_kv_k=\Delta_k/(2E_k)$, where $E_k=\sqrt{\xi_k^2+\Delta_k^2}$. They diagonalize the [Bogoliubov--de Gennes Hamiltonian](bogoliubov-de-gennes-hamiltonian.md) and give the empty/pair amplitudes of the [BCS ground state](bcs-ground-state.md). Their relative sign depends on the pairing convention.

For an original check, write $x=\xi_k/\Delta$ with constant real $\Delta>0$. At $x=0$, the [BCS coherence factors](bcs-coherence-factors.md) give $u_k^2=v_k^2=1/2$. At $x=1$, they give $(u_k^2,v_k^2)=(0.853553,0.146447)$; at $x=-1$ these values exchange places. Thus the zero-[temperature](temperature-split.md) occupation $v_k^2$ rounds the normal [Fermi sea](fermi-sea.md)'s step over an [energy](energy-split.md) interval of order $\Delta$. This mixing is strongest at the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md), where the [BCS quasiparticle spectrum](bcs-quasiparticle-spectrum.md) has its minimum.
