# BCS coherence factors

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

For real gap and the particle-hole block $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\xi_k&-\Delta_k\\-\Delta_k&-\xi_k\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, the BCS coherence factors obey $u_k^2=(1+\xi_k/E_k)/2$, $v_k^2=(1-\xi_k/E_k)/2$ and $u_kv_k=\Delta_k/(2E_k)$, where $E_k=\sqrt{\xi_k^2+\Delta_k^2}$. They diagonalize the [Bogoliubov--de Gennes Hamiltonian](bogoliubov-de-gennes-hamiltonian.md) and give the empty/pair amplitudes of the [BCS ground state](bcs-ground-state.md). Their relative sign depends on the pairing convention.
