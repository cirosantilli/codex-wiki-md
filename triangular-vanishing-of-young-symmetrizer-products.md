# Triangular vanishing of Young-symmetrizer products

↑ **Parent:** [Column-reading order of standard Young tableaux](column-reading-order-of-standard-young-tableaux.md)

For [standard tableaux](standard-young-tableau.md) of the same shape and $h_t=c_tr_t$, $t>u$ in [column-reading order of standard Young tableaux](column-reading-order-of-standard-young-tableaux.md) implies $h_th_u=0$. If there were no row-column collision, each column of $u$ would select one entry from each eligible row of $t$. Its sorted first column is componentwise at least the row minima of $t$; equality fixes those selected entries and permits induction after deleting the column. Thus no collision implies $u\geq t$. A collision gives $r_tc_u=0$.
