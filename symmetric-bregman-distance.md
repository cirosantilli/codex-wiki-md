# Symmetric Bregman distance

↑ **Parent:** [Bregman divergence](bregman-divergence.md)

For $p_u\in\partial J(u)$ and $p_v\in\partial J(v)$, add the two [Bregman distances](bregman-divergence.md) to get $D_J^{p_v}(u,v)+D_J^{p_u}(v,u)=\langle p_u-p_v,u-v\rangle\geq0$. The choice of [subgradients](subgradient.md) matters. This is generally not a metric: it need not separate points or satisfy a [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md).
