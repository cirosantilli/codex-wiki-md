# Ice-shelf buttressing

↑ **Parent:** [Ice shelf](ice-shelf.md)

Ice-shelf buttressing is the restraint that a floating [ice shelf](ice-shelf.md), through its contact with sidewalls or pinning points, can exert on upstream grounded ice flow. Thinning or loss of the shelf can reduce this restraint and increase discharge, but the response depends on geometry and cannot be inferred from floating-ice area alone.
