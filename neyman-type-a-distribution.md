# Neyman Type A distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neyman_Type_A_distribution)

Let $N$ have a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) with parameter $\lambda$, and let $Y_1,Y_2,\ldots$ be [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) with a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) of common parameter $\theta$, independent also of $N$. Then $Z=\sum_{i=1}^N Y_i$ has a [compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md), with $Z\mid N\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\theta N)$. The [law of total expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md) yields the displayed [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) and [expected value](expected-value-split.md) $\lambda\theta$. The [law of total variance](law-of-total-variance.md) gives [variance](variance-split.md) $\lambda\theta(1+\theta)$, exceeding the mean when both parameters are positive. This extra variation comes from the random number of groups. [Jerzy Neyman](jerzy-neyman.md) is the statistician for whom the distribution is named.
