# Dilute-polymer free-energy expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md)

For a single-site solvent and polymer length $N$, expand $(1-\phi)\ln(1-\phi)=-\phi+\phi^2/2+\phi^3/6+O(\phi^4)$ in [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md). The displayed result follows. Its linear term changes reference [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md); its quadratic term sets the effective two-segment [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md). The latter vanishes at the [theta temperature](theta-temperature.md), while the positive cubic term remains. Taking $\phi f'-f$ yields the [osmotic pressure in Flory-Huggins solution theory](osmotic-pressure-in-flory-huggins-solution-theory.md), whose cubic coefficient is $1/3$ rather than the free energy's $1/6$.
