# Universal cover of two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime](two-dimensional-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

The unrestricted spatial coordinate unwraps the spatial circle of [two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime](two-dimensional-de-sitter-spacetime.md). The [conformal time](conformal-time.md) $\eta=\arcsin(\tanh t)$ lies in $(-\pi/2,\pi/2)$, and [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) have slopes $d\xi/d\eta=\pm1$. Constant-$\eta$ lines are [Cauchy hypersurfaces](cauchy-surface.md): along any causal curve $|d\xi/d\eta|\leq1$, so a curve cannot escape to spatial infinity in a bounded conformal-time interval. The covering spacetime is therefore [globally hyperbolic](globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md), despite its observer-dependent cosmological horizons. Identifying $\xi$ modulo $2\pi$ gives the de Sitter cylinder, also globally hyperbolic.
