# Doubling time

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential growth](exponential-growth.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubling_time)

The interval required to double a quantity under constant-rate [exponential growth](exponential-growth.md). Solving $e^{rt_2}=2$ with the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) gives $t_2=\ln2/r$. At $r=0.2$ per time unit, it is approximately $3.46574$ time units; every subsequent doubling takes the same interval.
