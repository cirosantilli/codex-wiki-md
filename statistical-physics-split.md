# Statistical physics

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics)

[Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md) explains collective behavior using [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md) over microscopic possibilities. Its central surprise is that incomplete microscopic knowledge can still produce precise predictions for [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md), [entropy](entropy-split.md) and [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). The bridge is [statistical mechanics](statistical-mechanics.md): specify the available [microstates](microstate.md), assign their [probabilities](probability.md), then compute the [expected values](expected-value-split.md) of measured quantities.

A [two-level system](two-level-system.md) already displays the idea. Warming it makes its higher [energy](energy-split.md) more likely; its [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) first rises, then falls as the two [probabilities](probability.md) approach equality. With two distinguishable units, merely counting [microstates](microstate.md) explains why the middle [energy](energy-split.md) has twice the [state degeneracy](state-degeneracy.md). These small examples expose the machinery before the much larger sums needed for a [photon gas](photon-gas.md) or an [Ising model](ising-model.md).

The appeal is economical: the same microscopic [energies](energy-split.md) and [probabilities](probability.md) explain both an average reading and its [variance](variance-split.md). A measurement's spread therefore becomes useful physical information rather than an inconvenience to discard.
