# Positronium Zeeman spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Positronium](positronium.md)

For the dimensionless-spin [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $A\mathbf S_e\cdot\mathbf S_p+2\mu_BB(S_{ez}-S_{pz})$, the two aligned-spin states remain at $A/4$. The two states of total [spin](spin.md) projection zero mix and have the displayed energies. The parameter $4\mu_BB/A$ compares off-diagonal Zeeman mixing in the singlet-triplet basis with half the zero-field splitting. A crossover is gradual, not a level-crossing [phase transition](phase-transition.md).
