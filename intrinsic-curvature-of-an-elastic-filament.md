# Intrinsic curvature of an elastic filament

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic filament](elastic-filament.md)

The intrinsic [signed curvature](signed-curvature.md) is the curvature of a stress-free filament, measured along material [arc length](arc-length.md). An [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md) with actual curvature $\kappa$ stores bending energy $\frac B2\int(\kappa-\kappa_0)^2ds$, with [filament bending modulus](filament-bending-modulus.md) $B$. In a planar small-slope [Monge representation](monge-representation.md), $\kappa\simeq\zeta''$ and the leading energy is quadratic in $\zeta''-\kappa_0$.
