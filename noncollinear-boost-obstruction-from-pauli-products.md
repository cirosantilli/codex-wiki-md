# Noncollinear boost obstruction from Pauli products

↑ **Parent:** [Wigner rotation](wigner-rotation.md)

For boost [matrices](matrix.md) $A=cI+s\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ and $A'=c'I+s'\mathbf n'\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$, the [Pauli matrix multiplication law](pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md) gives the displayed anti-Hermitian part. If both rapidities are nonzero and the axes are noncollinear, it cannot vanish, while either lift of a pure boost is Hermitian. Thus an additional [Wigner rotation](wigner-rotation.md) is necessary. A zero boost is a degenerate exception.
