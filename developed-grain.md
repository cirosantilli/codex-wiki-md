# Developed grain

↑ **Parent:** [Photographic emulsion](photographic-emulsion.md)

A [developed grain](developed-grain.md) is a visible deposit of [silver](silver.md) produced during [photographic processing](photographic-processing.md). The chain of [developed grains](developed-grain.md) in a [nuclear emulsion](nuclear-emulsion.md) records interactions along a [charged particle](charged-particle.md)'s path. Its width is a property of the recording and viewing process, not a measurement of the particle's diameter.
