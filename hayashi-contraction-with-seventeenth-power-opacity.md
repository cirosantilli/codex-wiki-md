# Hayashi contraction with seventeenth-power opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Convective contraction with power-law opacity](convective-contraction-with-power-law-opacity.md)

Match an $n=3/2$ [stellar polytrope](stellar-polytrope.md) to the [grey-atmosphere convection onset with seventeenth-power opacity](grey-atmosphere-convection-onset-with-seventeenth-power-opacity.md). At each instant the convective entropy coefficient obeys $P=KT^{5/2}$, $K\propto M^{-1/2}R^{-3/2}$ at fixed composition, while the boundary gives $K\propto g^{1/2}T_e^{-21/2}$. Thus $T_e^{21/2}\propto MR^{1/2}$ and the first displayed [luminosity](luminosity.md) law follows. The [stellar virial theorem](stellar-virial-theorem.md) for a monatomic gas gives total energy $-3GM^2/(7R)$, so [Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction](kelvin-helmholtz-mechanism.md) has $\dot R=-7LR^2/(3GM^2)$. Integrating at fixed mass gives $R^{-67/21}-R_0^{-67/21}\propto t$. The second displayed relation holds after the initial-radius term is negligible. The entropy coefficient is spatially constant but changes during contraction.
