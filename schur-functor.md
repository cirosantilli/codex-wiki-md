# Schur functor

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial representation of the general linear group](polynomial-representation-of-the-general-linear-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schur_functor)

For a [partition of an integer](partition-of-an-integer.md) $\lambda\vdash n$, applying a [Young symmetrizer](young-symmetrizer.md) of that shape to the [tensor power](tensor-power.md) of each [vector space](vector-space-split.md) gives a Schur functor. The construction respects [linear maps](linear-map.md) because their [tensor powers](tensor-power.md) commute with place permutations. For single rows it gives [symmetric powers](symmetric-power.md), and for single columns [exterior powers](exterior-power.md).
