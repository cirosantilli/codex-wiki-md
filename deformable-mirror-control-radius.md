# Deformable-mirror control radius

↑ **Parent:** [Deformable mirror](deformable-mirror.md)

With $N$ actuators across diameter $D$, the projected pitch is approximately $D/N$. The [Nyquist spatial frequency](nyquist-spatial-frequency.md) is $N/(2D)$, so the largest correctable angular speckle displacement along an actuator row is $N\lambda/(2D)$. A square actuator array gives a square ideal spatial-frequency region. See the [Bordé–Traub wavefront-control derivation](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0510597).
