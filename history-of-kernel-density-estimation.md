# History of kernel density estimation

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel density estimation](kernel-density-estimation.md)

Murray Rosenblatt's 1956 paper studied [density estimation](density-estimation.md) from a one-dimensional [statistical sample](statistical-sample.md), the unavoidable [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md) under its stated conditions, and [asymptotic mean squared error](asymptotic-mean-squared-error.md). It is an early foundation of [kernel density estimation](kernel-density-estimation.md). [Rosenblatt, Remarks on Some Nonparametric Estimates of a Density Function, Annals of Mathematical Statistics 27(3), 832-837 (1956)](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177728190). Emanuel Parzen's 1962 paper developed [statistical consistency](consistency-statistics.md) and [asymptotic normality](asymptotic-normality.md) for [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) estimates and studied estimation of the [mode](mode-statistics.md). [Parzen, On Estimation of a Probability Density Function and Mode, Annals of Mathematical Statistics 33(3), 1065-1076 (1962)](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177704472).

The authors' interests help explain the connection with smoothing. In a conversation recorded in 2006 and published in 2009, Rosenblatt recalled that his [density estimation](density-estimation.md) work grew out of estimation of the [time-series spectral density](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) and emphasized the connection with a smoothed [histogram](histogram.md). This is his retrospective account of the motivation. [Brillinger and Davis, A conversation with Murray Rosenblatt, passage immediately before The Chicago, Indiana and Brown years](https://celebratio.org/Rosenblatt_M/article/891/). Parzen's original paper lists Stanford University as his affiliation; its opening frames the problem as recovering both a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) and its [mode](mode-statistics.md) from [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md). [Parzen's original paper, p. 1065](https://bayes.wustl.edu/Manual/Parzen_1962.pdf).
