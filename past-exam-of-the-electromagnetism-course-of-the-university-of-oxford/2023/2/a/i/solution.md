<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [constitutive relations](../../../../../constitutive-relations.md) $\mathbf D=\epsilon\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf H=\mathbf B/\mu$, where $\mathbf D$ is the [electric displacement field](../../../../../electric-displacement-field.md), $\mathbf H$ the [magnetic field strength](../../../../../magnetic-field-strength.md), $\epsilon$ the [permittivity](../../../../../permittivity-split.md), and $\mu$ the [magnetic permeability](../../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md). Let $\rho_f$ be the [charge density](../../../../../charge-density.md) of [free charge](../../../../../free-charge.md) and $\mathbf J_f$ its [current density](../../../../../current-density.md). In this homogeneous medium, [Gauss's law](../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) and the [magnetostatic Ampère law](../../../../../magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) are, respectively,

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\rho_f,\qquad
\oint_{\partial W}\mathbf D\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=Q_f(W)=\int_W\rho_f\,d^3r,}
$$



$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf H=\mathbf J_f,\qquad
\oint_{\partial S}\mathbf H\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=I_f(S)=\int_S\mathbf J_f\cdot\mathbf n\,dS.}
$$

Here $W$ is a volume with closed boundary $\partial W$, and $S$ is an oriented spanning surface whose boundary orientation follows the right-hand rule. Thus the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) form is $\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\rho_f/\epsilon$ and $\oint_{\partial W}\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=Q_f/\epsilon$; the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) form is $\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu\mathbf J_f$ and $\oint_{\partial S}\mathbf B\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=\mu I_f$.

The remaining static [Maxwell equations](../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) are

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0,\qquad\oint_{\partial W}\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=0,
\qquad
\nabla\times\mathbf E=0,\qquad\oint_{\partial S}\mathbf E\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=0.
$$

These express [Gauss's law for magnetism](../../../../../gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) and the static limit of [Faraday's law](../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md). The volume and circulation forms follow from the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) and [Stokes theorem](../../../../../stokes-theorem.md). The [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) and [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) on the right of the macroscopic laws are free sources; material [electric polarization](../../../../../polarization-density.md) and [magnetization](../../../../../magnetization.md) are already included in $\mathbf D$ and $\mathbf H$.
