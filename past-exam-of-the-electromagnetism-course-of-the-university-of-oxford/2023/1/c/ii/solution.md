<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write the constant [spherical harmonic](../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md) $Y_{00}=C$. Its [orthonormality](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) gives $4\pi|C|^2=1$, independently of its phase convention. [Orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $Y_{00}$ gives

$$
\int_{S^2}\overline{Y_{\ell m}(\Omega)}\,d\Omega=0\quad(\ell>0),\qquad
\int_{S^2}\overline{Y_{00}(\Omega)}Y_{00}(\Omega_0)\,d\Omega=1.
$$

Only the $\ell=m=0$ term contributes to the [surface integral](../../../../../surface-integral.md) of the [surface charge density](../../../../../surface-charge-density.md). Multiplying by the surface-area factor $a^2$ gives

$$
Q_a=-qa^2\frac{a^{-1}}{1-a/b}\left(\frac1{r_0}-\frac1b\right).
$$

Thus the [spherical harmonic](../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md) calculation reproduces the total induced [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md):

$$
\boxed{Q_a=-q\frac{a(b-r_0)}{r_0(b-a)}.}
$$
