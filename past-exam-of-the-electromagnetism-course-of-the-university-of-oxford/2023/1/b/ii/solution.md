<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose a spherical [Gaussian surface](../../../../../gaussian-surface.md) of radius $s$ with $a<s<r_0$. Its physical enclosed [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) is the total induced [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $Q_a$ on the inner sphere. The [method of images](../../../../../method-of-images.md) produces the same [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) on that [Gaussian surface](../../../../../gaussian-surface.md), so [Gauss's law](../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) identifies $Q_a$ with the sum of the enclosed [image charges](../../../../../image-charge.md). These are the first family with $n\leq-1$ and the second family with $n\leq0$:

$$
Q_a=q\sum_{n=-\infty}^{-1}k^n-\frac{qa}{r_0}\sum_{n=-\infty}^{0}k^n
=q\frac{k^{-1}-a/r_0}{1-k^{-1}}.
$$

Summing these [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md) gives

$$
\boxed{Q_a=-q\frac{a(b-r_0)}{r_0(b-a)}.}
$$

The [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) in the empty central cavity vanishes by [electrostatic shielding](../../../../../electrostatic-shielding.md), so it contributes no additional inner-face [surface charge density](../../../../../surface-charge-density.md). The formula tends to $-q$ as $r_0\downarrow a$ and to zero as $r_0\uparrow b$, as expected when the [point charge](../../../../../point-charge.md) approaches one grounded boundary or the other.
