# Scalar-field stress projections

↑ **Parent:** [Normal scalar-field momentum](normal-scalar-field-momentum.md)

For a [minimally coupled scalar field](minimally-coupled-scalar-field.md), put $q^2=h^{ij}D_i\phi D_j\phi$ using the [spatial covariant derivative](spatial-covariant-derivative.md). Spatial and normal projections of its [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) give $\rho=\Pi^2/2+q^2/2+V$, $\mathcal J_i=-\Pi D_i\phi$, and $S_{ij}=D_i\phi D_j\phi+h_{ij}(\Pi^2/2-q^2/2-V)$. The trace-free stress is $D_i\phi D_j\phi-h_{ij}q^2/3$, with coefficient one. This makes the scalar [anisotropic stress](anisotropic-stress.md) second order in a spatial gradient expansion.
