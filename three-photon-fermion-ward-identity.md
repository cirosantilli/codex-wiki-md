# Three-photon fermion Ward identity

↑ **Parent:** [Ward identity](ward-identity.md)

For tree [Electron](electron.md)-[Positron](positron.md) annihilation into three [photons](photon.md), all six orders along the [fermion](fermion.md) line must be summed. Write $D(r)=\not r-m$ and $S(r)=D(r)^{-1}=(\not r+m)/(r^2-m^2)$. Contracting an internal photon with its momentum gives $S(R)\not q S(r)=S(R)-S(r)$ when $q=r-R$. Contracting an end photon removes its adjacent [Dirac propagator](dirac-propagator.md) by the external [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md), with a minus sign at the electron end and a plus sign at the positron end. For either fixed order of the two uncontracted photons, the three insertion positions therefore telescope to zero. Summing their two orders proves the [Ward identity](ward-identity.md). The mass terms in both remaining [Dirac propagators](dirac-propagator.md) are essential when $m\ne0$. The result expresses the invariance of the [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) under $\epsilon_\ell\mapsto\epsilon_\ell+cq_\ell$.
