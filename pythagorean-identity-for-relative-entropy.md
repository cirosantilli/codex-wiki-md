# Pythagorean identity for relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Three-point identity for relative entropy](three-point-identity-for-relative-entropy.md)

The Pythagorean identity is

$$
D(P\Vert Q)=D(P\Vert R)+D(R\Vert Q)
$$

whenever the residual term in the [three-point identity for relative entropy](three-point-identity-for-relative-entropy.md) is zero. Convex information projections replace equality by the corresponding Pythagorean inequality.
