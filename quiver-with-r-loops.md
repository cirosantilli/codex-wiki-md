# Quiver with r loops

↑ **Parent:** [Quiver](quiver.md)

This [quiver](quiver.md) has one vertex and $r$ loops. Paths are words in the loops, so its [path algebra](path-algebra.md) is a [free associative algebra](free-associative-algebra.md). A representation assigns an arbitrary endomorphism to each loop.
