# Measurement of linear photon polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Projective measurement](projective-measurement.md)

With $|V\rangle=|0\rangle$ and $|H\rangle=|1\rangle$, an analyzer oriented at angle $\phi$ from vertical has positive-outcome state $\cos\phi|0\rangle+\sin\phi|1\rangle$. Subtracting the two orthogonal rank-one [projection operators](projection-linear-algebra.md) gives $M(\phi)=\cos2\phi\,Z+\sin2\phi\,X$, where $X,Z$ are [Pauli operators](pauli-operator.md). Physical polarization angles are therefore half their corresponding [Bloch sphere](bloch-sphere.md) angles.
