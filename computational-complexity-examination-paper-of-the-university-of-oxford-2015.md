# Computational Complexity examination paper of the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) [computer science](computer-science-split.md) finals examination dated Wednesday 3 June 2015, Trinity Term 2015. The four-page original has three questions, on [computability theory](computability-theory-split.md), [time complexity](time-complexity.md) and [logarithmic space](logarithmic-space.md). The public [examination-paper archive supplied by the University of Oxford](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains it as `2015-complex.pdf`. Questions 2 and 3 are marked corrected. Question 3(e) uses the word [path in a graph](path-in-a-graph.md) without specifying whether repeated [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) are permitted; the [even-length directed reachability](even-length-directed-reachability.md) proof uses directed [walks](walk-in-a-graph.md) with length counted with multiplicity.
