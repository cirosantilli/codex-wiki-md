# Spin contamination

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

A [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) intended for a specified total-spin state contains components of other total spins. For an unrestricted [determinant](determinant.md) with one alpha and one beta orbital, $\langle S^2\rangle=1-|\langle\phi_\alpha|\phi_\beta\rangle|^2$ in units of $\hbar^2$.
