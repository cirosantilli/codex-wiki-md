# Magnetic circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostatics](magnetostatics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_circuit)

A [magnetic circuit](magnetic-circuit.md) approximates a narrow core and its gaps by sections carrying a common [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) $\Phi$. With negligible leakage and approximately uniform [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) across each section, [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) gives $B=\Phi/A$. The [magnetostatic Ampère law](magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) then gives $NI=\int H\,dl=\Phi\int dl/(\mu_0\mu_rA)$. This requires an appropriate material relation; constant [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) is an approximation for an actual magnetic core.
