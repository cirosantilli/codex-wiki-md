# Planet-induced debris-disk warp

↑ **Parent:** [Differential nodal precession](differential-nodal-precession.md)

An inclined inner [planet](planet.md) causes exterior [debris disk](debris-disk.md) rings to undergo [nodal precession](nodal-precession.md) at different rates. In the [quadrupole approximation](quadrupole-approximation.md), $\nu\simeq(3/4)n(M_p/M_\star)(a_p/a)^2\propto a^{-7/2}$. Rings that have precessed appreciably tilt away from their original [orbital plane](orbital-plane.md), whereas distant rings remain near it, making an evolving warp. Conservative [secular perturbations](secular-perturbation.md) alone do not damp every ring into the planet's plane; unresolved [phase mixing](phase-mixing.md) can instead produce a thick inner disk whose mean normal is near the forced plane.
