# Proximity photolithography

↑ **Parent:** [Photolithography](photolithography.md)

Mask-based optical pattern transfer across a small noncontact gap. [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md) across the gap blurs edges; a common resolution estimate is $w=k\sqrt{\lambda g}$ for wavelength $\lambda$, gap $g$ and process factor $k$.
