# Euler elastica

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic beam](elastic-beam.md)

The planar equilibrium of an inextensible slender beam can have large tangent [angle](angle.md) while retaining small elastic [strain](strain.md) across its thickness. For constant horizontal compressive [force](force-split.md) $F$, moment balance and [curvature](curvature.md) $\theta'=d\theta/ds$ give the displayed nonlinear equation. Multiplication by $\theta'$ gives the first integral $EI\theta'^2/2-F\cos\theta=C$. A tensile load has $F<0$ in this convention.
