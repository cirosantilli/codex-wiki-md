# Zinc-air battery

↑ **Parent:** [Electric battery](electric-battery.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinc–air_battery)

A metal-air cell that oxidizes zinc while reducing oxygen at a gas-accessible positive [electrode](electrode.md). Primary and electrically rechargeable designs exist; reversible zinc morphology and durable oxygen-evolution catalysis are major requirements for the latter.
