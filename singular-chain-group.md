# Singular chain group

↑ **Parent:** [Singular chain](singular-chain.md)

The singular chain group is the free [abelian group](abelian-group.md) on all continuous maps $\Delta^q\to X$, the [singular simplices](singular-simplex.md). In particular it is free even when the set of simplices is infinite. This freeness makes applying $\operatorname{Hom}(C_q(X),-)$ to a short exact coefficient sequence exact.
