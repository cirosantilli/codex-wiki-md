<h1 id="cech-complete-space">Čech-complete space</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hausdorff compactification](hausdorff-compactification.md)

A [Tychonoff space](completely-regular-hausdorff-space.md) is Čech-complete if it is a [G-delta set](g-delta-set.md) in a [Hausdorff compactification](hausdorff-compactification.md). The [G-delta compactification invariance](g-delta-compactification-invariance.md) makes this independent of the chosen compactification, and equivalent to being a [G-delta set](g-delta-set.md) in its [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md).
