# Stress-induced preferential absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Irradiation creep](irradiation-creep.md)

An [irradiation creep](irradiation-creep.md) mechanism in which applied [stress](stress.md) makes differently oriented [dislocations](dislocation.md) or [dislocation loops](dislocation-loop.md) absorb [point defects](point-defect.md) at different rates. The resulting directional strain is distinct from stress-free [void swelling](void-swelling.md).
