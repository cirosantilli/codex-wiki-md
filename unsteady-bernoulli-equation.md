# Unsteady Bernoulli equation

↑ **Parent:** [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md)

For time-dependent [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) with $u=\nabla\phi$, constant [mass density](density.md), and no body force, the [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) integrate to

$$
\partial_t\phi+\frac12|\nabla\phi|^2+\frac p\rho=C(t).
$$

A time-dependent change of gauge in $\phi$ may set $C(t)=0$.

This is the unsteady potential-flow version of [Bernoulli's principle](bernoulli-s-principle.md), with a time-dependent potential gauge.
