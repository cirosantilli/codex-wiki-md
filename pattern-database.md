# Pattern database

↑ **Parent:** [Abstraction heuristic](abstraction-heuristic.md)

A table of precomputed exact abstract goal distances used as an [abstraction heuristic](abstraction-heuristic.md). Retaining a subset of the disks of a [Tower of Hanoi](tower-of-hanoi.md) instance gives a smaller abstract puzzle: moving an ignored disk does not change the abstract state, and moving a retained disk projects to a legal abstract move. Taking maxima of [pattern database](pattern-database.md) estimates preserves admissibility; summing them requires an additional cost-partition argument.
