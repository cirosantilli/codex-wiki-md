# Radiative pion and kaon decay form factors in the 2025 Review of Particle Physics

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

[Form Factors for Semileptonic Kaon, Radiative Pion and Kaon Decays](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-form-factors-radiative-pik-decays.pdf)

Section 62.1 distinguishes inner [bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md) from structure-dependent [radiative pion decay](radiative-pion-decay.md) and their [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md). Equations (62.3)–(62.4) give the double differential [decay width](decay-width.md) in terms of the scaled [photon](photon.md) and charged-lepton [energies](energy-split.md) and the squared lepton-to-meson [mass](mass.md) ratio.
