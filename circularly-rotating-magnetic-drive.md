# Circularly rotating magnetic drive

↑ **Parent:** [Rabi cycle](rabi-cycle.md)

For positive [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md) in static field $B\hat z$, transverse components $B_1\cos(\omega t+\varphi)$ and $-B_1\sin(\omega t+\varphi)$ rotate with the [spin](spin.md) precession. Their rotating-frame [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is time independent, with transverse [Rabi angular frequency](rabi-angular-frequency.md) $\Omega=\gamma B_1$. A linearly polarized field of peak amplitude $B_{\rm peak}$ contains two oppositely rotating components and gives resonant $\Omega=|\gamma|B_{\rm peak}/2$ in the [rotating-wave approximation](rotating-wave-approximation.md). Negative [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md) requires opposite rotation sense.
