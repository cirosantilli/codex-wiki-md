Publisher record for the reprint in _Classics in Game Theory_, pp. 46–68: [https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/9781400829156-011/html](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/9781400829156-011/html)
