# Initial topology

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

For a family of [functions](function-split.md) $f_i:X\to Y_i$ into [topological spaces](topological-space.md), the [initial topology](initial-topology.md) on $X$ is the coarsest [topology](topology-split.md) making them all [continuous](continuous-function.md). Its [subbasis](subbasis-of-a-topology.md) consists of $f_i^{-1}(U)$ for open [subsets](subset.md) $U\subseteq Y_i$. A map into $X$ is [continuous](continuous-function.md) exactly when all its composites with the $f_i$ are [continuous](continuous-function.md).
