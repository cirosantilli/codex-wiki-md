This is the sum of content [monomials](monomial.md) of colorings up to the [group action](group-action.md). It equals the [cycle index](cycle-index.md) specialized at $t_r=p_r(x)$. One may first use finitely many colors; stabilization defines the corresponding homogeneous [symmetric function](symmetric-function.md). Calling the specialized cycle index a cycle indicator makes $Z_G=F_G$ an equality in the same variables.
