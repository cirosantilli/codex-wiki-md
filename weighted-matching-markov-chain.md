# Weighted matching Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Reversible Markov chain](reversible-markov-chain.md)

On the [matchings in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md), a [lazy Markov chain](lazy-markov-chain.md) proposes adding an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md), deleting an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md), or exchanging an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) for the unique matching [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) incident with it. A proposal incident with two matching [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) is rejected. Select the proposed [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) uniformly and use the [Metropolis–Hastings acceptance probability](metropolis-hastings-acceptance-probability.md) for the displayed weights. Symmetric proposals give [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md); deletions connect every state to the empty [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md). The normalizing constant is the [matching generating function](matching-generating-function.md).
