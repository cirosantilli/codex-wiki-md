# Monotonicity and bounds of effective bandwidth

↑ **Parent:** [Effective bandwidth](effective-bandwidth.md)

For $s>0$ in the finite [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) domain and integrable $X$, [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) gives the lower bound and $X\le\operatorname{ess\,sup}X$ almost surely gives the upper bound. The [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) makes $K(s)=\log\mathbb Ee^{sX}$ convex with $K(0)=0$. Thus $K(t)\le(t/s)K(s)$ for $0<t<s$, and the [effective bandwidth](effective-bandwidth.md) is nondecreasing. It need not be strictly increasing: deterministic traffic gives a constant bandwidth. Infinite peaks or exponential moments require extended-value conventions.
