# Heat exchanger

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_exchanger)

A [heat exchanger](heat-exchanger.md) transfers [heat](heat-split.md) between a [fluid](fluid.md) and another [fluid](fluid.md) or its surroundings. In [measuring the Joule-Thomson effect with a porous plug](measuring-the-joule-thomson-effect-with-a-porous-plug.md), an upstream coil restores the inlet [temperature](temperature-split.md) before the measurement throttle; it belongs outside the approximately insulated measurement [control volume](control-volume.md).
