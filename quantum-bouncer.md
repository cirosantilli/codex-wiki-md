# Quantum bouncer

↑ **Parent:** [Bound state](bound-state.md)

A [quantum particle](quantum-particle.md) above a hard reflecting wall in a linearly increasing potential $V(x)=Fx$ for $x>0$. The [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) reduces to the [Airy equation](airy-equation.md). Decay at infinity selects $\operatorname{Ai}$, while the wall requires $\operatorname{Ai}(-z_n)=0$. Thus $\psi_n\propto\operatorname{Ai}(x/\ell-z_n)$ and the energies are the displayed values. The $n$th state has $n-1$ interior nodes and a decaying tail beyond its [classical turning point](classical-turning-point.md). A gravitational realization has $F=mg$; [gravitational neutron bound-state transmission](gravitational-neutron-bound-state-transmission.md) tests such confinement experimentally.
