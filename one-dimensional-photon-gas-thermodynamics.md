# One-dimensional photon-gas thermodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Photon gas](photon-gas.md)

A cavity with two transverse [photon](photon.md) [polarizations](polarization-waves.md) and standing-wave spacing $\pi/L$ has [energy](energy-split.md) [density of states](density-of-states.md) $2L/(\pi\hbar c)$. In the continuum regime, integrating the Bose occupation at zero [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) gives $U=bLT^2$, where $b=\pi k_B^2/(3\hbar c)$. The thermal quantities are $C_L=S=2bLT$, Helmholtz free [energy](energy-split.md) $A=-bLT^2$ and generalized [pressure](pressure-split.md) $P=bT^2=U/L$. The last quantity has units of [force](force-split.md). These thermal formulas exclude a separate boundary-dependent vacuum [energy](energy-split.md) and require [temperature](temperature-split.md) above the mode-spacing scale.
