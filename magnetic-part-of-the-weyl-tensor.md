# Magnetic part of the Weyl tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl tensor](weyl-tensor.md)

In four spacetime dimensions, relative to a timelike [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $n$, define $B_{ab}=\tfrac12\epsilon_{acde}C^{de}{}_{bf}n^cn^f$, with the chosen spacetime [metric volume tensor](metric-volume-tensor.md). This is the dual tidal part of the [Weyl tensor](weyl-tensor.md). Normal contractions vanish by curvature and volume-tensor antisymmetry. Its sign depends on the specified orientation and dualization convention.
