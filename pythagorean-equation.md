# Pythagorean equation

↑ **Parent:** [Pythagorean theorem](pythagorean-theorem.md)

The equation $a^2+b^2=c^2$. Its [positive integer](positive-integer.md) solutions are [Pythagorean triples](pythagorean-triple.md); [primitive Pythagorean triples](primitive-pythagorean-triple.md) are those with no common factor greater than 1.
