# Surface coverage

↑ **Parent:** [Adsorption site](adsorption-site.md)

In a one-[molecule](molecule-split.md)-per-[adsorption site](adsorption-site.md) description, [surface coverage](surface-coverage.md) is $\theta=N_{\rm occupied}/N_{\rm sites}$, with $0\leq\theta\leq1$. For a macroscopic sample at [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md), $\theta$ usually denotes the mean occupied fraction. Inferring it from total uptake also requires the capacity of a complete [monolayer](monolayer.md).
