# Stolz region

↑ **Parent:** [Nontangential limit](nontangential-limit.md)

A fixed-aperture approach region at a boundary point of the [unit disc](unit-disc.md). Its points approach the boundary point without becoming tangent to the circle. Convergence inside every such fixed region is a [nontangential limit](nontangential-limit.md); the corresponding supremum defines a [non-tangential maximal function](non-tangential-maximal-function.md).
