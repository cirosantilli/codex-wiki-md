# Dopamine D2 receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Dopamine](dopamine.md)

A subtype of receptor for [dopamine](dopamine.md) involved in neural signalling and targeted by many [antipsychotic](antipsychotic.md) medications. Receptor blockade is a pharmacological action, not a complete psychological description of [treatment](treatment.md) response.
