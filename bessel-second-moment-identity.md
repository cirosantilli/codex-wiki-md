# Bessel second-moment identity

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi-Anger expansion](jacobi-anger-expansion.md)

For real $z$, the [probabilities](probability.md) $J_m(z)^2$ over integer angular-momentum modes satisfy $\sum_m m^2J_m(z)^2=z^2/2$. Differentiate the unit-modulus function $e^{iz\cos\theta}$ and use Parseval to obtain the identity; its zero first moment follows from the symmetry of opposite orders.
