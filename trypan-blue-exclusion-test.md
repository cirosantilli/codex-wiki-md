# Trypan blue exclusion test

↑ **Parent:** [Cell counting](cell-counting.md)

The [trypan blue exclusion test](trypan-blue-exclusion-test.md) reads [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) integrity through [trypan blue](trypan-blue.md) exclusion. Prolonged dye exposure can itself increase staining.[Cell viability, dye exclusion and trypan-blue counting window](animal-cell-culture-guide-by-atcc.md)

An unstained [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) is dye-excluding at the time of this assay. That observation alone does not demonstrate future division, sustained [metabolism](metabolism.md) or long-term renewal of a [cell line](cell-line.md). Conversely, a smaller unstained count can reflect both staining and loss of [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) from the recovered preparation. Report the denominator and recovery procedure alongside the fraction.
