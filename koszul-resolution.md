# Koszul resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Projective resolution](projective-resolution.md)

For a [regular sequence](regular-sequence.md) $f_1,\ldots,f_r$ in a commutative [ring](ring.md) $R$, its Koszul complex is the finite free [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) of $R/(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$ with terms $\bigwedge^jR^r$ and [boundary map](boundary-operator.md) contracting by $(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$. Exactness follows by induction: append $f_r$ using the [mapping cone](mapping-cone-homological-algebra.md) for multiplication by $f_r$, which is injective on the preceding quotient. For a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) viewed as a [bimodule](bimodule.md), use the [regular sequence](regular-sequence.md) $X_{i,\ell}-X_{i,r}$ in the enveloping [algebra](algebra-split.md).
