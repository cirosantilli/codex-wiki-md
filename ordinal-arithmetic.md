# Ordinal arithmetic

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal](ordinal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_arithmetic)

Ordinal arithmetic defines addition, multiplication and exponentiation of [ordinals](ordinal.md) through order types or [transfinite recursion](transfinite-recursion.md). The usual operations need not be commutative. Natural sum and natural product instead combine [Cantor normal forms](cantor-normal-form.md) and are commutative; the [Hessenberg natural sum](hessenberg-natural-sum.md) is the natural addition operation.
