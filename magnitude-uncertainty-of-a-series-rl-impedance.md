# Magnitude uncertainty of a series RL impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md)

For $z=\sqrt{R^2+X^2}$ with [reactance](electrical-reactance.md) $X=\omega L$, linear differentiation gives $dz=(R\,dR+X\,dX)/z$. [Independent](independent-random-variables.md) [standard uncertainties](standard-uncertainty.md) give the title formula; correlated inputs add $2RX\operatorname{Cov}(R,X)/z^2$. Hard error bounds instead add absolute linear contributions. The meaning of an error bar is an additional measurement assumption, not a consequence of the impedance formula.
