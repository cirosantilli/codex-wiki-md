# Mental neutralization

↑ **Parent:** [Compulsion](compulsion.md)

A covert mental act intended to cancel a threat, remove a thought's significance or prevent a feared outcome. [Mental neutralization](mental-neutralization.md) can preserve a [compulsion](compulsion.md) cycle even when visible rituals have decreased.
