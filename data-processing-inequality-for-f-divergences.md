# Data processing inequality for f-divergences

↑ **Parent:** [f-divergence](f-divergence.md)

For a [measurable function](measurable-function.md) $T$, every f-divergence contracts under [pushforward](pushforward-measure.md):

$$
D_f(T_*P\Vert T_*Q)\leq D_f(P\Vert Q).
$$

This follows from conditional [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) applied to the jointly convex perspective $(a,b)\mapsto bf(a/b)$.
