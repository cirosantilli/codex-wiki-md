# Fiber-fed spectrograph

↑ **Parent:** [Multi-object spectrograph](multi-object-spectrograph.md)

A fiber-fed spectrograph places [optical fibers](optical-fiber.md) on selected targets and reformats their output into a common entrance slit. Dedicated sky fibers sample [sky brightness](sky-brightness.md). Fiber coupling, transmission, and output beam spreading affect throughput and resolution.
