# Semiconductor source-detector double-beta search

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrinoless double beta decay](neutrinoless-double-beta-decay.md)

A search in which isotope-enriched detector material is also the decay source. [germanium](germanium.md) detectors combine a contained [energy](energy-split.md) deposit with [energy](energy-split.md) resolution, pulse information and material-background controls. Background peaks and resolution tails must be tested before assigning a signal.
