# Nabarro-Herring creep

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion creep](diffusion-creep.md)

Diffusional [material creep](creep-deformation.md) dominated by transport through grain interiors. For an equiaxed-grain model, $\dot\varepsilon\sim C D_V\Omega\sigma/(k_BT d^2)$, with geometric factor $C$, atomic [volume](volume.md) $\Omega$ and [grain size](grain-size.md) $d$. It is linear in [stress](stress.md) in this ideal limit.
