# Cold collisionless plasma dielectric response

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md)

In an unmagnetized [plasma](plasma-physics.md), an electron displacement driven by $\mathbf E e^{-i\omega t}$ obeys $-m_e\omega^2\mathbf x_e=-e\mathbf E$. Its dipole moment is $-e\mathbf x_e$, giving [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) $\mathbf P=-ne^2\mathbf E/(m_e\omega^2)$. With $\mathbf D=\varepsilon_0\mathbf E+\mathbf P$, this yields the displayed [relative permittivity](relative-permittivity.md) and $\omega_p^2=ne^2/(m_e\varepsilon_0)$. Ions contribute a further negative term smaller by $Zm_e/m_i$ for one ion species of charge $Ze$. Neglecting it requires heavy ions at electron-wave frequencies; collisions, background magnetization and pressure dispersion are excluded. The transverse [electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) relation is $c^2k^2=\omega^2-\omega_p^2$.
