# Mechanism (mechanism design)

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanism design](mechanism-design.md)

A mechanism specifies participants' available messages, an allocation rule, and any payment rule. [Mechanism design](mechanism-design.md) chooses these rules to achieve an objective while respecting participants' [incentive compatibility](incentive-compatibility.md) and [individual rationality](individual-rationality.md).
