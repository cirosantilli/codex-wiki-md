# Centrifugal potential of cylindrical rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md)

For [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) depending only on cylindrical radius, the centrifugal acceleration is a [gradient](gradient.md). Steady barotropic balance is $\nabla p/\rho=-\nabla\Psi$, hence [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) satisfies $h(\rho)+\Psi=\text{constant}$. On an invertible equation-of-state branch, [mass density](density.md) and [pressure](pressure.md) are functions of $\Psi$. Cylindrical rotation is essential to this scalar centrifugal-potential representation.
