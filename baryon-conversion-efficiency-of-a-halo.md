# Baryon conversion efficiency of a halo

↑ **Parent:** [Hierarchical galaxy formation](hierarchical-galaxy-formation.md)

The [baryon conversion efficiency of a halo](baryon-conversion-efficiency-of-a-halo.md) is the fraction of its baryonic mass incorporated into stars. If the halo retains the [cosmic baryon fraction](cosmic-baryon-fraction.md) $f_b$, its stellar-to-total-mass ratio is $f_bf_*$. This efficiency depends on halo mass, epoch, gas supply and feedback and must not be confused with a star-formation efficiency per free-fall time.
