# Power-law comparison from a refined ratio bound

↑ **Parent:** [Ratio test](ratio-test.md)

Suppose $a_n>0$ and, for all sufficiently large $n$, $a_{n+1}/a_n\le1-c/n$ with fixed $c>0$. Choose an initial index $N>c$. Since $\log(1-x)\le-x$ for $0<x<1$, summing logarithmic ratios gives $\log(a_n/a_N)\le-c\sum_{k=N}^{n-1}1/k\le-c\log(n/N)$. Thus $a_n\le a_NN^c n^{-c}$. For $c>1$, the [comparison test for series](comparison-test-for-series.md) with a [P-series](p-series.md) proves convergence. This supplies a useful refinement when a limiting ratio of one makes the ordinary [ratio test](ratio-test.md) inconclusive.
