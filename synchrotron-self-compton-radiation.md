# Synchrotron self-Compton radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse Compton scattering](inverse-compton-scattering.md)

A population of relativistic electrons emits [synchrotron radiation](synchrotron-radiation.md) and then scatters some of those same photons through [inverse Compton scattering](inverse-compton-scattering.md). The emitting region supplies both the scatterers and the seed radiation field. Comparing the two spectral components can constrain electron energies and the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), but source geometry and relativistic beaming affect the inference.
