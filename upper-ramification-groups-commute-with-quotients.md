# Upper ramification groups commute with quotients

↑ **Parent:** [Upper ramification numbering](upper-ramification-numbering.md)

For a normal subgroup $H$ of a finite local [Galois group](galois-group.md), the [upper ramification numbering](upper-ramification-numbering.md) on its quotient is obtained by projecting the upper groups. The [Herbrand function](herbrand-function.md) is the change of variable that ensures this compatibility. In contrast, the [lower ramification numbering](lower-ramification-numbering.md) restricts directly to subgroups.
