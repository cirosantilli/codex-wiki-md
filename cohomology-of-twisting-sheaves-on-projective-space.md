# Cohomology of twisting sheaves on projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Projective space](projective-space-split.md)

For every integer $d$, the cohomology of a [twisting sheaf on projective space](twisting-sheaf-on-projective-space.md) is

$$
H^q(\mathbb P_k^n,\mathcal O(d))\cong
\begin{cases}
k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]_d,&q=0\text{ and }d\geq0,\\
k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]_{-d-n-1}^*,&q=n\text{ and }d\leq-n-1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

Thus all intermediate cohomology vanishes. The top line follows from [Serre duality](serre-duality.md) and $\omega_{\mathbb P^n}\cong\mathcal O(-n-1)$.
