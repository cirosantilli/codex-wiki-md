# Nurse cell

↑ **Parent:** [Oogenesis](oogenesis.md)

A [germline](germline.md)-associated [cell](biological-cell-split.md) supplying macromolecules to an [oocyte](oocyte.md) in systems such as [Drosophila](drosophila.md). Maternal [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) synthesis and its eventual localization are distinct processes.
