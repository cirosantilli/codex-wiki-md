# Independent coordinates of a compound Poisson process

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md)

The coordinates of a vector-valued [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md) are independent exactly when its [Lévy measure](levy-measure.md) is supported on the union of the coordinate axes. For marks $(g_1(U),g_2(U))$ with $U$ uniform on $[0,1]$ and continuous $g_i$, this is equivalent to

$$
g_1(y)g_2(y)=0
$$

for every $y\in[0,1]$.
