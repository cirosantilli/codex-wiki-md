# Localizing sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Local martingale](local-martingale.md)

A localizing sequence for a [local martingale](local-martingale.md) $M$ is an increasing sequence of [stopping times](stopping-time.md) $T_n\uparrow\infty$ almost surely such that every stopped process $M^{T_n}$ is a true martingale. One can often choose the times so that the stopped processes are bounded.
