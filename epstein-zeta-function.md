# Epstein zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epstein_zeta_function)

For a full-rank [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) in $\mathbb R^n$, its Epstein zeta function is $E_\Lambda(s)=\sum_{0\ne x\in\Lambda}|x|^{-2s}$, initially convergent for $\operatorname{Re}s>n/2$. A [Mellin transform](mellin-transform.md) of its [theta function](theta-function.md) gives its [completed Epstein zeta function](completed-epstein-zeta-function.md) and a meromorphic continuation. Its sole pole has residue $\pi^{n/2}/[m(\Lambda)\Gamma(n/2)]$ at $s=n/2$.
