# Transitive increasing event

↑ **Parent:** [Increasing event](increasing-event.md)

An [increasing event](increasing-event.md) on a finite product of identically distributed coordinates is transitive if a [group action](group-action.md) preserving the event is transitive on the coordinate set. Its [influences](influence-of-a-variable.md) are all equal. Transitivity concerns coordinates, and does not assert transitivity on successful configurations.
