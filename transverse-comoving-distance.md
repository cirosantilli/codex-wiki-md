# Transverse comoving distance

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological distance measure](cosmological-distance-measure.md)

The distance that converts a small angular separation into a transverse [comoving distance](comoving-radial-distance.md) at a specified [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md). It equals the radial comoving distance in spatially flat geometry; [cosmological spatial curvature](spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) changes that equality. A comoving acoustic ruler has angular size $r_d/D_M$.
