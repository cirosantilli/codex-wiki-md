# Anti-de Sitter timelike geodesic refocusing

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

Embed radius-$L$ [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) as $X\cdot X=-L^2$ in signature with two timelike directions. Initial tangent $V_P$ satisfies $X_P\cdot V_P=0$ and $V_P\cdot V_P=-1$. The displayed curve remains on the hyperboloid and has unit timelike tangent; its ambient acceleration is normal, proving it is a [geodesic](geodesic.md). Every such curve reaches $-X_P$ after proper time $\pi L$. On the [universal cover](universal-cover.md), this is a later antipodal event, not a closed causal loop. Global time advances by $2\pi$ over a complete ambient period.
