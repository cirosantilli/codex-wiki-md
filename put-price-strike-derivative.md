# Put-price strike derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Digital put option](digital-put-option.md)

For a [European put option](european-put-option.md) with deterministic [discount factor](discount-factor.md) $D$ and an integrable terminal [stock](stock.md) price, $P(K)=D\mathbb E_{\mathbb Q}[(K-S_T)^+]$. If $\mathbb Q(S_T=K)=0$, bounded difference quotients and the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) give the displayed derivative, the price of a [digital put option](digital-put-option.md). At an atom, the left derivative is $D\mathbb Q(S_T<K)$ and the right derivative is $D\mathbb Q(S_T\leq K)$, so the ordinary derivative need not exist.
