# Propargylic alcohol

↑ **Parent:** [Alcohol](alcohol.md)

A [propargylic alcohol](propargylic-alcohol.md) has its hydroxyl-bearing [carbon](carbon.md) adjacent to a [carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) triple bond. Its alkoxide can coordinate a metal hydride and direct [alkyne](alkyne.md) reduction.
