# Dual graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_graph)

For a [graph](graph-split.md) with a cellular embedding on a surface, the dual has one vertex for each face and an edge crossing each original edge between its adjacent faces. Each edge is counted, including a loop when the two incident faces coincide. The [planar dual graph](planar-dual-graph.md) is the case of a planar embedding.
