# Two-dimensional quantum-gravity interpretation of strings

↑ **Parent:** [Polyakov path integral](polyakov-path-integral.md)

The [Polyakov path integral](polyakov-path-integral.md) integrates over [string worldsheet](worldsheet.md) metrics as well as embedding fields, modulo [worldsheet diffeomorphisms](worldsheet-diffeomorphism.md) and [Weyl transformations](weyl-transformation.md). Summing surface topologies gives perturbative [two-dimensional quantum gravity](two-dimensional-quantum-gravity.md) coupled to the coordinate matter fields. Gauge fixing retains [worldsheet moduli](worldsheet-moduli.md) and introduces ghosts; a conformal gauge is locally flat, not a global flat metric on every genus. Cancellation of the [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) makes the critical bosonic version consistent.
