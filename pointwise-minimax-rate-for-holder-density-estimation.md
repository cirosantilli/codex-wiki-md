<h1 id="pointwise-minimax-rate-for-holder-density-estimation">Pointwise minimax rate for Hölder density estimation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel density estimation](kernel-density-estimation.md)

Over a one-dimensional Hölder density class of smoothness $\beta$, the optimized pointwise mean-squared error has order $n^{-2\beta/(2\beta+1)}$. A kernel of sufficiently high order balances squared bias $h^{2\beta}$ against variance $(nh)^{-1}$.
