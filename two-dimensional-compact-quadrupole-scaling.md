# Two-dimensional compact quadrupole scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Compact acoustic quadrupole Mach-number scaling](compact-acoustic-quadrupole-mach-number-scaling.md)

In two dimensions, the outgoing harmonic [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md) kernel has magnitude proportional to $(k_0r)^{-1/2}$. Combining this spreading with the compact [acoustic quadrupole](acoustic-quadrupole.md) source and $k_0\ell=O(M)$ changes the density-amplitude [Mach number](mach-number.md) power to $7/2$ when geometric range is separated.
