# Diffusive nose of an advected porous gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-flux porous gravity current](constant-flux-porous-gravity-current.md)

The downstream edge of a continuously fed [porous gravity current with background flow](porous-gravity-current-with-background-flow.md) has a transition width proportional to $\sqrt{a h_{\mathrm{int}}t}$ around drift position $ut$. Its [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) satisfies $(FF\prime)\prime+\eta F\prime/2=0$, $F(-\infty)=1$, and $F(\eta_N)=0$. A finite front has $F\sim(\eta_N/2)(\eta_N-\eta)$. A clipped linear ramp is a useful mass-preserving approximation, but not an exact solution of this nonlinear transition equation.
