# Light-cone decomposition of a massive two-form

↑ **Parent:** [Massive two-form field](massive-two-form-field.md)

If $\partial_-$ is invertible, the divergence constraint of a [massive two-form field](massive-two-form-field.md) makes $A_{-i}$ and $A_{ij}$ independent. It gives $A_{+-}=-\partial_-^{-1}\partial_iA_{-i}$ and $A_{+i}=\partial_-^{-1}(-\partial_+A_{-i}+\partial_jA_{ji})$. All independent components obey the [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md). Their count is $(D-2)+\binom{D-2}{2}=\binom{D-1}{2}$.
