<h1 id="kantorovich-rubinstein-theorem">Kantorovich–Rubinstein theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Wasserstein distance](wasserstein-distance.md)

On a [Polish space](polish-space.md) $(X,\rho)$, [probability measures](probability-measure.md) with finite first [moments](moment.md) satisfy

$$
W_1(\mu,\nu)=\sup_{\operatorname{Lip}(f)\leq1}\left(\int f\,d\mu-\int f\,d\nu\right).
$$

The supremum runs over functions with [Lipschitz constant](lipschitz-constant.md) at most one. Fixing $f(x_0)=0$ ensures integrability from the moment assumption.
