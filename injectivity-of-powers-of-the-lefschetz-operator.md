# Injectivity of powers of the Lefschetz operator

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz operator on a Hermitian manifold](lefschetz-operator-on-a-hermitian-manifold.md)

The [Hermitian Lefschetz operator](lefschetz-operator-on-a-hermitian-manifold.md) has injective powers in the indicated range. The [commutator formula for powers of the Lefschetz operator](commutator-formula-for-powers-of-the-lefschetz-operator.md) proves this by induction on $r+k$: a form in the kernel can be written as $L\alpha$ using injectivity of the smaller power, and the lower-degree form $\alpha$ is then in the kernel of another power covered by the induction hypothesis.
