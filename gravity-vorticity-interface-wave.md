# Gravity-vorticity interface wave

↑ **Parent:** [Jump conditions for stratified inviscid shear flow](jump-conditions-for-stratified-inviscid-shear-flow.md)

At an isolated interface with continuous base [velocity](velocity.md), a stable density drop $\Delta\rho/2$ and a jump $[U']$ of base shear, decaying disturbances $w\propto e^{-k|z-z_i|}$ obey the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). It follows by inserting their derivative jump $[w']=-2kw$ into the [jump conditions for stratified inviscid shear flow](jump-conditions-for-stratified-inviscid-shear-flow.md). Both the density jump and the [vorticity](vorticity.md) jump contribute to the intrinsic [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) $c-U_i$.
