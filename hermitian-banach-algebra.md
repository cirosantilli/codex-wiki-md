# Hermitian Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

A Hermitian [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) is a [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) with an [algebra involution](algebra-involution.md) for which every [Hermitian element of a star algebra](hermitian-element-of-a-star-algebra.md) has real [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md). A [closed](closed-set.md) unital [star-subalgebra](star-subalgebra.md) has [spectral permanence for Hermitian Banach algebras](spectral-permanence-for-hermitian-banach-algebras.md). The assumption is weaker than being a [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) and does not require the C-star [norm](norm.md) identity.
