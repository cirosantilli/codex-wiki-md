# Ideal class group of Q of square root of minus one hundred sixty-three

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski's bound](minkowski-s-bound.md)

The imaginary quadratic field $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-163})$ has discriminant $-163$, and its [Minkowski bound for ideal classes](minkowski-s-bound.md) is

$$
\frac2\pi\sqrt{163}<9.
$$

The polynomial $X^2-X+41$ defining its [ring of integers of a quadratic field](ring-of-integers-of-a-quadratic-field.md) has no root modulo $2,3,5,$ or $7$, so these rational primes are inert. Factoring an integral ideal of norm at most eight into [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md) then shows that its only possible nontrivial factor is the principal ideal $(2)$, of norm four. Every ideal class is therefore principal, and

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(K)=1.
$$
