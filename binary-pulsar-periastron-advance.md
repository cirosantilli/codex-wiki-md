# Binary-pulsar periastron advance

↑ **Parent:** [Binary pulsar](binary-pulsar.md)

A gradual rotation of the direction of [periapsis](periapsis.md) within the [orbital plane](orbital-plane.md). A closed [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) has fixed [argument of periapsis](argument-of-periapsis.md) $\omega$; relativistic [gravity](gravity.md) can make $\omega$ advance between successive orbits. The angle change and [binary-pulsar orbital decay](binary-pulsar-orbital-decay.md) describe different changes of the [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md).
