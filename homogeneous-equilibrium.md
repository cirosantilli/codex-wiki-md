# Homogeneous equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Equilibrium point of a dynamical system](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md)

A [homogeneous equilibrium](homogeneous-equilibrium.md) in a spatial evolution equation is a steady state independent of position. Its stability against homogeneous perturbations need not imply stability against spatially varying modes.
