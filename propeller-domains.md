# Propeller domains

↑ **Parent:** [Transplantation theorem](transplantation-theorem.md)

A propeller domain consists of seven congruent reflected triangles: one central triangle and three arms of two triangles each. There are different coloured gluing patterns whose [Dirichlet Laplacian](dirichlet-laplacian.md) and [Neumann Laplacian](neumann-laplacian.md) spectra agree by the [transplantation theorem](transplantation-theorem.md). Varying the three side lengths of a scalene triangle in a suitable open region gives three parameters; the central triangle is identifiable from the three reflex corners, allowing a direct nonisometry proof.
