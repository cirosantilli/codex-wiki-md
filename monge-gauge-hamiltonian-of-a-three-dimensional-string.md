# Monge-gauge Hamiltonian of a three-dimensional string

↑ **Parent:** [Monge gauge](monge-gauge.md)

With unit tension and $X^0=t$, $X^1=\sigma$, the [Virasoro constraints](virasoro-constraint.md) give $P_1=-\Pi\Phi'$ and $P_0=-\sqrt{(1+\Phi'^2)(1+\Pi^2)}$. The reduced energy density is $\mathcal H=-P_0$. On the chiral branch $\Pi=\Phi'$, the [Hamiltonian field equation](hamiltonian-field-equation.md) becomes $\dot\Phi=\Phi'$.
