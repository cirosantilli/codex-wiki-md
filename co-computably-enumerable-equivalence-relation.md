# Co-computably enumerable equivalence relation

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md)

An [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) on the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) is co-computably enumerable when its [complement](complement-of-a-set.md) as a set of pairs is [computably enumerable](recursively-enumerable-set.md). Inequivalence can then be positively recognized, even when equivalence cannot be decided. This is sufficient for a [semidecidable least-representative transversal](semidecidable-least-representative-transversal.md).
