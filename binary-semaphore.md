# Binary semaphore

↑ **Parent:** [Semaphore (programming)](semaphore-programming.md)

A [semaphore](semaphore-programming.md) with at most one available permit. Initially unavailable instances support event signalling; initially available instances can protect one-at-a-time access under a balanced acquisition-and-release discipline.
