# Van der Corput lemma (Hilbert space sequences)

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodic theory](ergodic-theory.md)

For a bounded sequence $(u_n)$ in a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), put $c_h=\limsup_{N\to\infty}|N^{-1}\sum_{n=0}^{N-h-1}\langle u_{n+h},u_n\rangle|$. If $H^{-1}\sum_{h=1}^Hc_h\to0$, then $N^{-1}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}u_n\to0$ in norm. Since the $c_h$ are bounded, it suffices that $(c_h)$ has [convergence in density of a sequence](convergence-in-density-of-a-sequence.md) to zero. The proof applies the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) to an average of shifted sums.
