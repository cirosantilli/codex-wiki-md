# Conditionality principle

↑ **Parent:** [Fisherian conditional inference](fisherian-conditional-inference.md)

When an experiment's configuration is observed, inference conditions on the experiment actually performed rather than averaging over its possible configurations. Conditioning on an [ancillary statistic](ancillary-statistic.md) is a central application; freedom from a [nuisance parameter](nuisance-parameter.md) must still be checked in the resulting [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md).
