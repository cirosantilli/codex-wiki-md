# Mass change in a viscously damped spring oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For fixed [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k$ and linear [damping](damping.md) coefficient $b$, replacing [mass](mass.md) $m$ by $\lambda m$ changes the exponential envelope rate from $\gamma=b/(2m)$ to $\gamma/\lambda$. The damped [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) becomes $\omega_{d,\lambda}^2=(\omega_d^2+\gamma^2)/\lambda-\gamma^2/\lambda^2$. Thus envelope decay times scale exactly as [mass](mass.md), while the familiar inverse-square-root [frequency](frequency.md) scaling is a weak-damping approximation. A constant gravitational [force](force-split.md) shifts equilibrium without changing either rate.
