# Cumulative coherence bound for restricted isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Cumulative coherence](cumulative-coherence.md)

For normalized columns and $1\le s\le N$, the [restricted isometry constant](restricted-isometry-constant.md) is bounded by the [cumulative coherence](cumulative-coherence.md) at $s-1$. A restricted [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) has diagonal one and every off-diagonal row sum at most $\mu_1(s-1)$. The [Gershgorin circle theorem](gershgorin-circle-theorem.md) and the [finite-dimensional spectral theorem](finite-dimensional-spectral-theorem.md) bound its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) between $1-\mu_1(s-1)$ and $1+\mu_1(s-1)$. At $s=1$ the distortion is zero, and at $s=2$ it is exactly the [mutual coherence](coherence-of-a-normalized-matrix.md).
