# Spatial resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)

The ability of an imaging or measurement system to distinguish nearby spatial features. A resolution criterion must specify the features and measurement conditions: [diffraction](diffraction.md), [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) and sampling can all limit the distinction. A small pixel spacing alone does not establish high spatial resolution; [electron microscope resolution](electron-microscope-resolution.md) illustrates the role of [wavelength](wavelength.md) and [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md).
