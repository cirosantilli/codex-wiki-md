# Greville abscissa

↑ **Parent:** [Monomial B-spline coefficients](monomial-b-spline-coefficients.md)

For an order-$k$ [B-spline](b-spline.md) with $k\geq2$, its Greville abscissa is the arithmetic mean of its $k-1$ interior knots. These are precisely the coefficients reproducing the linear function in the [monomial B-spline coefficients](monomial-b-spline-coefficients.md) formula. The irregular plural is Greville abscissae.
