# Powerful-squarefree coprime decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Powerful number](powerful-number.md)

Every positive integer has a unique factorization $n=as$ with $a$ [powerful](powerful-number.md), $s$ [squarefree](square-free-integer.md), and $\gcd(a,s)=1$: assign each prime power of exponent at least two to $a$, and each prime of exponent one to $s$. In general $s$ differs from the [squarefree part](squarefree-part.md), which instead retains every prime of odd exponent.
