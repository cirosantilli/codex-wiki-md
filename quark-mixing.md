# Quark mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Charged current](charged-current.md)

The up-type and down-type [fermion mass matrices](fermion-mass-matrix.md) need different left-handed transformations represented by [unitary matrices](unitary-matrix.md) to diagonalize them. Their relative transformation appears in the [charged weak current](charged-current.md) as the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md). The [weak neutral currents](neutral-current.md) remain diagonal in the minimal [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md), since the same-charge couplings are generation-independent. Three nondegenerate generations give three mixing angles and one physical weak [CP](cp-symmetry.md) phase after [quantum field rephasings](quantum-field-rephasing.md).
