# Perfect-fluid alignment in a static spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md)

For [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(+---)$, a [perfect fluid](perfect-fluid.md) has $T_{ab}=(\rho+p)u_au_b-pg_{ab}$. The [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) and [static Killing field is a Ricci eigenvector](static-killing-field-is-a-ricci-eigenvector.md) imply $(\rho+p)(k\cdot u)u_{[a}k_{b]}=0$. Two nonzero timelike vectors cannot be orthogonal, so $k\cdot u\ne0$. If $\rho+p\ne0$, the [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) is parallel to the static [Killing vector](killing-vector-field.md). Choosing both future-directed gives the displayed normalization. For $\rho+p=0$, the [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) is proportional to the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) and does not determine a preferred fluid velocity; the strict enthalpy hypothesis is therefore material.
