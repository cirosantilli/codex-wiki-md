# Whistler wave

↑ **Parent:** [Electron magnetohydrodynamics](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

In the inertia-free, constant-density [electron magnetohydrodynamics](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md) model with uniform guide field $B_0\hat{\mathbf z}$, a transverse magnetic [plane wave](plane-wave.md) obeys $\omega\mathbf b=i v_Ad_i k_\parallel\mathbf k\times\mathbf b$. The [helicity decomposition of a transverse Fourier mode](helicity-decomposition-of-a-transverse-fourier-mode.md) gives the displayed dispersive branches with opposite [circular polarizations](circular-polarization.md). This electron-current mode is called a whistler wave; see [Lyutikov's analysis of electron magnetohydrodynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4544). Its pressure-free induction equation should not be identified with a general kinetic Alfvén model.
