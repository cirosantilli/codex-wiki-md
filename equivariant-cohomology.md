# Equivariant cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant_cohomology)

The Borel version of equivariant cohomology is the ordinary [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of the [Borel construction](borel-construction.md). It retains information about both the space and its [group action](group-action.md). For a point it is the cohomology of the [classifying space](classifying-space.md); for a free action with the usual bundle hypotheses it is the cohomology of the orbit space.
