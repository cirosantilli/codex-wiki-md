# Triangle count

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md)

The triangle count of a finite simple [graph](graph-split.md) is the number of unordered triples of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) inducing a [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md). In a [tripartite graph](tripartite-graph.md) with parts $X,Y,Z$ and adjacency [indicator functions](indicator-function.md) $g_{XY},g_{YZ},g_{XZ}$, it equals $|X||Y||Z|\mathbb E_{x,y,z}g_{XY}(x,y)g_{YZ}(y,z)g_{XZ}(x,z)$, using uniform [expectations](expected-value.md) on nonempty parts.
