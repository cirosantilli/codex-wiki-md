# Trapped ion

↑ **Parent:** [Ion trap](ion-trap.md)

An ion confined by an electromagnetic trap has both internal atomic states and quantized motional modes. Coherent internal-state control, state-dependent [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) and resolved-sideband operations allow these degrees of freedom to serve as quantum-information resources.
