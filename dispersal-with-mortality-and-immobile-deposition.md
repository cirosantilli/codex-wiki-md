# Dispersal with mortality and immobile deposition

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)

For diffusing organisms of density $n$ that die at rate $\mu$ and deposit an immobile material of density $e$ at rate $\lambda$,

$$
n_t=Dn_{xx}-\mu n,
\qquad
e_t=\lambda n.
$$

An initial point population $N\delta(x)$ leaves the [remnant density from diffusion with mortality and deposition](remnant-density-from-diffusion-with-mortality-and-deposition.md)

$$
e(x,\infty)=\frac{N\lambda}{2\sqrt{D\mu}}e^{-|x|\sqrt{\mu/D}}.
$$
