# Symmetric inverse-power Poisson field on a line

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md)

Place equal masses at points of a homogeneous [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md) of intensity $\rho$ on the line, and truncate their signed inverse-power forces symmetrically at radius $R$. For force $m\operatorname{sign}(x)/|x|^p$, with $\alpha=1/p\in(0,2)$, the characteristic exponent tends to $-2\rho\int_0^\infty(1-\cos(mt/x^p))\,dx$. The [cosine integral for a symmetric stable exponent](cosine-integral-for-a-symmetric-stable-exponent.md) evaluates it as displayed. Thus the weak limit is a [symmetric stable distribution](symmetric-stable-distribution.md). Symmetric truncation is important when the far-field force series is not absolutely summable.
