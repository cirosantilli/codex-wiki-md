# Cohomology group

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)

A degree-$q$ cohomology group is the quotient of degree-$q$ cocycles by coboundaries in a [cochain complex](cochain-complex.md). Topological cohomology groups arise from singular or cellular cochains. Their direct sum carries the [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md) structure when equipped with the [cup product](cup-product.md).
