# One-dimensional harmonic oscillator form domain

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)

This [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) has norm $\|u\|_X^2=\|u\|_2^2+\|u'\|_2^2+\|xu\|_2^2$. Completeness follows from completeness of [H1 space](h1-space.md) and closedness of the [multiplication operator](multiplication-operator.md) $u\mapsto xu$. Smooth cutoffs followed by convolution with a [mollifier](mollifier.md) show that smooth compactly supported functions are dense. The space is the natural energy domain of the dimensionless [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md), whereas the operator domain additionally requires $u''$ and $x^2u$ to lie in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md).
