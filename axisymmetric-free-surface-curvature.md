# Axisymmetric free-surface curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Young–Laplace equation](young-laplace-equation.md)

For an axisymmetric graph $z=h(r)$ with the normal pointing out of the liquid, the sum of principal [curvatures](curvature.md) is the displayed expression. In the small-slope limit it becomes $\kappa=-(h''+h'/r)$, giving the [capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md) $p_{\mathrm{liquid}}-p_{\mathrm{air}}=\gamma\kappa$. Regularity at the axis gives $h'(0)=0$ and $h'/r\to h''(0)$: the azimuthal contribution does not disappear at the axis. For example, the paraboloid $h=H(1-r^2/R^2)$ has $\kappa\approx4H/R^2$, twice the meridional contribution.
