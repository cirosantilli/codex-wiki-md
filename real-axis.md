# Real axis

↑ **Parent:** [Complex plane](complex-plane.md)

The [real axis](real-axis.md) consists of the points of the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) whose [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) is zero. These points represent the [real numbers](real-number.md).
