# Impulse-momentum theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Impulse](impulse.md)

Integrating the closed-collection balance in [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) and applying the [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) gives

$$
\mathbf J_{\mathrm{ext}}
=\int_{t_i}^{t_f}\sum_i\mathbf F_i^{\mathrm{ext}}(t)\,dt
=\mathbf P(t_f)-\mathbf P(t_i).
$$

For one fixed-[mass](mass.md) [point mass](point-mass.md), this becomes $\mathbf J_{\mathrm{net}}=\Delta\mathbf p=m\Delta\mathbf v$. Zero external [impulse](impulse.md) implies equal initial and final total [momenta](momentum-split.md); [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) throughout the interval requires zero net external [force](force-split.md) throughout it.
