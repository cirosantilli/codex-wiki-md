# Chiral proof of rigid worldsheet supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid worldsheet supersymmetry boundary term](rigid-worldsheet-supersymmetry-boundary-term.md)

Let $\partial_\pm=\partial_\tau\pm\partial_\sigma$ and $\mathcal L=\partial_+X\cdot\partial_-X+i\psi_1\cdot\partial_+\psi_1+i\psi_2\cdot\partial_-\psi_2$. For constant Grassmann-odd parameters, set $\delta X=i(\epsilon_2\psi_1-\epsilon_1\psi_2)$, $\delta\psi_1=-\epsilon_2\partial_-X$, $\delta\psi_2=\epsilon_1\partial_+X$. In the first chiral sector the variation of the fermionic term cancels $i\epsilon_2\partial_+\psi_1\cdot\partial_-X$ from the bosonic term, leaving $i\epsilon_2\partial_-(\psi_1\cdot\partial_+X)$. The second sector gives the other [total derivative](total-derivative.md). This proves off-shell invariance when the boundary flux vanishes. Closure on [fermions](fermion.md) uses their free [equations of motion](equation-of-motion.md). A constant parameter must also preserve the chosen boundary conditions and [spin structure](spin-structure.md).
