# Standard skew Young tableau

↑ **Parent:** [Skew Young diagram](skew-young-diagram.md)

A standard skew Young tableau fills the $k$ cells of a [skew Young diagram](skew-young-diagram.md) bijectively with $1,\ldots,k$, increasing along each row and column. Equivalently it is a [linear extension of a partially ordered set](linear-extension.md) formed from the row and column inequalities. Interchanging consecutive labels preserves standardness exactly when their cells are incomparable in that partial order.
