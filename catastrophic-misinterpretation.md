# Catastrophic misinterpretation

↑ **Parent:** [Threat appraisal](threat-appraisal.md)

Interpreting an ambiguous or relatively benign event as evidence of imminent disaster. In cognitive accounts of [panic disorder](panic-disorder.md), [anxiety](anxiety.md)-induced bodily sensations can appear to confirm the threatening interpretation, forming a feedback loop.
