# NMR coupling constant

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar spin-spin coupling](scalar-spin-spin-coupling.md)

The frequency spacing $J$ of a first-order multiplet, expressed in [hertz](hertz.md). For a spectrum at $\nu_0$ MHz, $J/\mathrm{Hz}=(\Delta\delta/\mathrm{ppm})(\nu_0/\mathrm{MHz})$. A bond-count superscript, such as ${}^3J$, specifies the coupling path.
