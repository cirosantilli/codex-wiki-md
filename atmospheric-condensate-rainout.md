# Atmospheric condensate rainout

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet cloud](exoplanet-cloud.md)

Condensation followed by gravitational settling removes chemical elements from an atmospheric layer. The remaining gas may be in local [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) with a depleted elemental inventory, while the column is not a closed equilibrium system.
