# Normal subgroups of a compact group with irreducible adjoint action

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint representation of a Lie group](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md)

Let $G$ be [connected](connected-space.md) and [compact](compact-space.md) with nonzero [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md) real [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) has no proper invariant [Lie algebra ideal](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md). If it is [Abelian](abelian-group.md), irreducibility forces [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) one, so $G$ is the [circle group](circle-group.md). Otherwise its centre has zero-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) and is finite. A proper closed [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) likewise has zero-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), hence is finite; connectedness of $G$ makes [conjugation](conjugation.md) on that finite subgroup trivial, so the subgroup is central. A [Lie group homomorphism](lie-group-homomorphism.md) onto a nontrivial [connected](connected-space.md) target with [surjective](surjective-function.md) differential consequently has finite central [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md). The qualifications “proper” and “nontrivial” are essential.
