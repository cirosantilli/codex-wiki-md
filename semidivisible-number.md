# Semidivisible number

↑ **Parent:** [Divisibility](divisibility-split.md)

A [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$ lying strictly between consecutive [prime](prime-number-split.md) squares $p^2<n<q^2$ that is divisible by exactly one of $p,q$. Within a fixed band, the sum is the sum of multiples of $p$ plus the sum of multiples of $q$ minus twice the sum of multiples of $pq$, an [inclusion-exclusion](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) calculation using [arithmetic progressions](arithmetic-progression.md).
