# L1 space

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

The [Lp space](lp-space.md) for $p=1$ consists of equivalence classes of [integrable functions](lebesgue-integrable-function.md), with [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) $\|f\|_1=\int|f|d\mu$. [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) is a contraction in this norm. Convergence in this space is [L1 convergence](convergence-in-l1.md), which controls the change in every integral over a [measurable](measurability.md) subset.
