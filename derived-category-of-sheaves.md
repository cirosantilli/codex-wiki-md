# Derived category of sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Homological algebra](homological-algebra.md)

The derived category of sheaves is formed by localizing cochain complexes of sheaves at quasi-isomorphisms, equivalently localizing their homotopy category. The bounded, bounded below and bounded above variants are denoted $D^b(X)$, $D^+(X)$ and $D^-(X)$. Cohomology sheaves are invariant under quasi-isomorphism and therefore define functors on these categories. Constructible bounded complexes form a natural subcategory on which [Verdier duality](verdier-duality.md) reverses arrows and, under the usual finiteness hypotheses, is an involution.
