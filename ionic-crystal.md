# Ionic crystal

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic bonding](ionic-bonding.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ionic_crystal)

An [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md) repeats an arrangement of [ions](ion.md) in three dimensions. A [unit cell](unit-cell.md) describes that repetition, whereas a [formula unit](formula-unit.md) describes composition. The two accounting devices answer different questions. [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md) is not restricted to an arbitrarily drawn line connecting one [cation](cation.md) to one [anion](anion.md): every [ion](ion.md) contributes to the [electric field](electric-field.md) experienced by the others.
