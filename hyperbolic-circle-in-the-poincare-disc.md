# Hyperbolic circle in the Poincare disc

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic geometry](hyperbolic-geometry.md)

In the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md), a [hyperbolic circle in the Poincare disc](hyperbolic-circle-in-the-poincare-disc.md) consists of points at one fixed hyperbolic distance from a center. At center zero it is the Euclidean circle $|z|=\tanh(R/2)$, since radial hyperbolic distance is $2\operatorname{artanh}|z|$. A [Poincare disc automorphism](poincare-disc-automorphism.md) sending a general center to zero transports this description. Conversely every Euclidean circle entirely inside the disc is such a circle: the product of inversion in that circle and unit-circle inversion has reciprocal fixed points, one inside; sending the inner fixed point to zero makes the circle concentric with the unit circle.
