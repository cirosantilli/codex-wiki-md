# Basic emotion theory

↑ **Parent:** [Emotion](emotion.md)

A family of theories proposing a limited set of biologically grounded [basic emotions](basic-emotion.md). Versions differ in how strongly they predict distinctive facial, physiological and behavioral patterns. A claim of fixed one-to-one patterns is stronger than a claim of inherited response biases that interact with context.
