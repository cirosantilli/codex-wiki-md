↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**The proposed asymmetry is not generally defensible: [animal behaviour](../../../../animal-behaviour.md) and [immunity](../../../../immunity-medical.md) form a reciprocal system, and “more” has no common meaning without an outcome, scale and intervention.** Avoiding [infection](../../../../infection.md), controlling [pathogen](../../../../pathogen.md) growth, tolerating tissue damage and changing social contact are different outcomes. Their causal effect sizes cannot be ranked by counting examples in either direction.

The definition of [immunity](../../../../immunity-medical.md) matters first. In its narrower physiological sense, it concerns cellular and molecular defences. A [behavioural immune system](../../../../behavioural-immune-system.md) describes actions that reduce exposure before those defences are challenged, while [social immunity](../../../../social-immunity.md) includes protection generated by interactions among group members. If avoidance is defined as part of [immunity](../../../../immunity-medical.md), a statement that [animal behaviour](../../../../animal-behaviour.md) influences [immunity](../../../../immunity-medical.md) becomes partly true by definition. A useful analysis instead specifies whether an action changes exposure, immune activity, [pathogen](../../../../pathogen.md) burden or the damage associated with that burden. [disease resistance](../../../../disease-resistance.md) reduces burden; [disease tolerance](../../../../disease-tolerance.md) reduces damage at a given burden. Lower illness alone does not distinguish these routes.

One strong route from [animal behaviour](../../../../animal-behaviour.md) to defence is the management of exposure. Choice of food, mates and social partners determines contact with infectious material. [grooming](../../../../grooming.md) can remove external [parasites](../../../../parasite.md), and [hygienic behaviour](../../../../hygienic-behaviour.md) can remove contaminated material from a nest. Conversely, aggregation and some forms of close social contact can increase opportunities for transmission. These effects depend on the [pathogen](../../../../pathogen.md)'s transmission route: removing one kind of contact need not reduce [infection](../../../../infection.md) spread by an environmental reservoir. An observed association between sociability and [infection](../../../../infection.md) can also arise because [infection](../../../../infection.md) changes sociability, making temporal or experimental evidence essential.

[honeybee](../../../../honeybee.md) colonies selected for [hygienic behaviour](../../../../hygienic-behaviour.md) provide a causal evolutionary and experimental example. Selection based on brood-removal assays was combined with exposure to [American foulbrood](../../../../american-foulbrood.md) [disease](../../../../disease.md), allowing [disease](../../../../disease.md) outcomes in selected and comparison colonies to be assessed. Removal of infected brood can reduce a colony's infectious reservoir and protect other individuals. [Apidologie 32, pp. 555–565; selected hygienic colonies, brood assays and pathogen challenge](../../../../resistance-to-american-foulbrood-disease-by-honey-bee-colonies-apis-mellifera-bred-for-hygienic-behavior-by-marla-spivak-and-gary-s-reuter.md) The result concerns colony protection, not a demonstration that each worker's cellular [immunity](../../../../immunity-medical.md) is stronger. Selection can change correlated traits as well as brood removal, so a mechanistic follow-up should match colony size, brood age, exposure and background, and manipulate the opportunity for removal. Colonies are independent [experimental units](../../../../experimental-unit.md); scoring many cells within one colony does not create many independently selected colonies.

Choice of an offspring’s food plant can also change the chemical environment encountered by [parasites](../../../../parasite.md). Infected [monarch butterflies](../../../../monarch-butterfly.md) preferentially laid eggs on plants that reduced [parasite](../../../../parasite.md) growth and [disease](../../../../disease.md) in their offspring. This supported [transgenerational medication](../../../../transgenerational-medication.md): neither a simple cure of the mother nor merely a demonstration that a toxic plant can inhibit a [parasite](../../../../parasite.md). [Ecology Letters 13, pp. 1485–1493; infected monarch mothers, oviposition choice and offspring parasite growth](../../../../evidence-for-trans-generational-medication-in-nature-by-thierry-lefevre-and-others.md) The inference needs both the infection-dependent change in [oviposition](../../../../oviposition.md) and the offspring benefit. Tests should include infected and uninfected mothers offered the same plant alternatives, standardized [parasite](../../../../parasite.md) exposure of offspring, and measurements of larval growth and survival as well as [parasite](../../../../parasite.md) burden. Nutritional quality and general plant preference are competing explanations. A cost of the medicinal plant in parasite-free conditions would additionally help explain why every individual does not always choose it.

[animal behaviour](../../../../animal-behaviour.md) can influence physiological [immunity](../../../../immunity-medical.md) as well as exposure. Activity, feeding and [sleep](../../../../sleep.md) affect resource acquisition and allocation, while social interactions can alter endocrine and neural signals. These are plausible routes, but a study must measure the relevant immune process and not infer it from survival alone. An immune assay can increase because the animal is more infected, rather than because it has greater protective capacity. [randomization](../../../../randomization.md), controlled nutrition and repeated observations can help separate these interpretations. Any claim of a trade-off should identify the limiting resource and the competing [biological fitness](../../../../biological-fitness.md) demands; a change in one circulating marker is insufficient.

The reverse direction is equally substantive. [Immune challenges](../../../../immune-challenge.md) can change activity, appetite, social investigation and priorities, producing [sickness behaviour](../../../../sickness-behaviour.md). Such changes may conserve resources, reduce exposure to further hazards or alter transmission, but some can instead reflect damaging [inflammation](../../../../inflammation.md) or a [pathogen](../../../../pathogen.md)'s manipulation. Reduced activity after [infection](../../../../infection.md) is therefore not automatically adaptive host defence. Comparing host [biological fitness](../../../../biological-fitness.md) with and without the response, and separating immune activation from direct [pathogen](../../../../pathogen.md) action, is necessary for an evolutionary interpretation.

[lipopolysaccharide](../../../../lipopolysaccharide.md) provides one experimental way to induce an inflammatory response without a replicating [pathogen](../../../../pathogen.md). Work on lactating [house mice](../../../../house-mouse.md) showed that the resulting effects depended on the activity and environmental context: pup retrieval could be maintained, and cold conditions elicited nest building despite the challenge. [Brain, Behavior, and Immunity 11, pp. 107–118; immune challenge, maternal behaviour and ambient temperature](../../../../differential-effects-of-lipopolysaccharide-on-pup-retrieving-and-nest-building-in-lactating-mice-by-arnaud-aubert-and-others.md) This supports a reorganization of competing motivations rather than simple global paralysis. It does not show that all illness-related inactivity is strategic. Controls should match handling and injection, assess motor capacity and temperature, and distinguish loss of appetite from inability to acquire food. Dose and timing also matter: an artificial inflammatory challenge need not reproduce a natural [infection](../../../../infection.md)'s trajectory. Blocking a candidate immune mediator, followed by a rescue intervention, would provide stronger evidence for a specific immune-to-neural pathway than a challenge alone.

Immune influences are not confined to overt sickness. In [house mice](../../../../house-mouse.md), work on [interferon-gamma](../../../../interferon-gamma.md) linked immune deficiency and altered social preference to changes in neural connectivity and inhibitory physiology. Immune restoration and targeted signalling interventions strengthened a mechanistic interpretation beyond a correlation between illness and social withdrawal. [Nature 535, pp. 425–429, Figs. 1–2; immune deficiency, IFN-γ interventions, neural physiology and social preference](../../../../unexpected-role-of-interferon-gamma-in-regulating-neuronal-connectivity-and-social-behaviour-by-anthony-j-filiano-and-others.md) The experiments support a role for immune signalling in the tested neural and behavioural context; they do not establish a universal explanation of sociability or justify extrapolating a [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md) social-preference assay directly to human psychiatric conditions. Sensory ability, general activity, anxiety-related responses, housing and genetic background remain important controls when interpreting such assays.

The two directions can form the same feedback loop. An [immune response](../../../../immune-response.md) changes social contact; changed contact alters subsequent exposure and [infection](../../../../infection.md); the new [infection](../../../../infection.md) state changes the [immune response](../../../../immune-response.md) again. The medication example similarly links infection-dependent action to offspring defence. Neither direction is an independent input fixed while the other acts. A useful factorial design would manipulate a specific action or opportunity for action and a specific [immune challenge](../../../../immune-challenge.md), then measure both behavioural and physiological outcomes over time. Baseline health, age, sex and reproductive state should be controlled, and the manipulation of action should avoid introducing injury or [stress](../../../../physiological-stress.md) that independently changes [immunity](../../../../immunity-medical.md).

A claim that one direction is larger would require comparable interventions and a shared endpoint, such as lifetime [reproductive success](../../../../reproductive-success.md) or [pathogen](../../../../pathogen.md) transmission, with uncertainty over an explicitly chosen time horizon. Different answers may follow for individual survival, colony persistence and epidemic spread. **The evidence supports bidirectional, context-dependent regulation: actions govern exposure and defence, while immune signals govern behavioural priorities and sometimes normal neural function.** A universal ranking conceals precisely the causal distinctions needed to understand this system.
