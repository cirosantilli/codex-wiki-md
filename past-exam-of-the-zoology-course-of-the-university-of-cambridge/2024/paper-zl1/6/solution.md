**Genetic data provide relationships, demographic histories and mechanistic hypotheses that can be tested against [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) anatomy and dates; they do not make the synapsid [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) record redundant.** Most deep synapsid evolution is much older than recoverable [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Molecular biology therefore often contributes indirectly through living descendants, calibrated models and developmental experiments, while exceptional younger [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) yield [Ancient DNA](../../../../ancient-dna.md) or proteins themselves.

A major contribution is an independent framework for relationships. Superficially similar “ungulates” or insectivore-like [mammals](../../../../mammal.md) need not form natural groups. Molecular analyses recovered large placental groupings such as [Afrotheria](../../../../afrotheria.md) and [Laurasiatheria](../../../../laurasiatheria.md), each containing independently specialized ecological forms. [Nature 409, pp. 610–614; molecular placental groups and parallel ecological forms](../../../../parallel-adaptive-radiations-in-two-major-clades-of-placental-mammals-by-ole-madsen-and-others.md) That result changes interpretation of the [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) record: similarities in feeding or locomotion must be tested for [convergent evolution](../../../../convergent-evolution.md), and extinct taxa must be placed using broader character combinations. Living [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) does not itself score the missing anatomy of an extinct animal, but it changes which ancestral reconstructions and comparisons are plausible.

Whale origins show a particularly clear exchange between molecules and [fossils](../../../../fossil.md). Molecular evidence placed [Cetacea](../../../../cetacea.md) within the artiodactyl radiation and allied it with [hippopotamuses](../../../../hippopotamus.md), challenging interpretations driven strongly by dental resemblance to mesonychians. Analyses of independent [retrotransposon insertion](../../../../retrotransposon-insertion.md) loci supported the whale–hippopotamus relationship. [PNAS 96, pp. 10261–10266; independent insertion loci and artiodactyl relationships](../../../../phylogenetic-relationships-among-cetartiodactyls-based-on-insertions-of-short-and-long-interspersed-elements-hippopotamuses-are-the-closest-extant-relatives-of-whales-by-masato-nikaido-and-others.md) Subsequently described early whale limb skeletons supplied artiodactyl ankle characters consistent with that relationship. [Nature 413, pp. 277–281; pakicetid postcranial skeletons](../../../../skeletons-of-terrestrial-cetaceans-and-the-relationship-of-whales-to-artiodactyls-by-j-g-m-thewissen-and-others.md) [Science 293, pp. 2239–2242; protocetid ankle anatomy](../../../../origin-of-whales-from-early-artiodactyls-hands-and-feet-of-eocene-protocetidae-from-pakistan-by-philip-d-gingerich-and-others.md) This is stronger than agreement among several reanalyses of the same [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) characters because the anatomical and molecular character systems have different sources of error. A living hippopotamus remains a relative, not a whale ancestor.

Rare genomic changes can be particularly useful, but no character type is infallible. A [retrotransposon insertion](../../../../retrotransposon-insertion.md) requires orthology and correct reconstruction of insertion presence or absence. Ancestral polymorphism and [incomplete lineage sorting](../../../../incomplete-lineage-sorting.md) can produce discordance among loci; [hybridization](../../../../hybridization-biology.md) can produce a genuinely non-tree-like ancestry. The same concerns apply to nucleotide data: one [gene tree](../../../../gene-tree.md), especially a mitochondrial genealogy, need not equal a [species tree](../../../../species-tree.md). Broad genomic sampling and explicit models are therefore preferable to declaring a relationship settled by a single attractive marker.

Molecular clocks add time to the framework. Under the simplest two-lineage model, expected sequence divergence is approximately:

$$
d\simeq2rt,
$$

with substitution rate $r$ per site per unit time and divergence time $t$. The sequences alone constrain a rate–time product, not both independently. [Fossil calibration](../../../../fossil-calibration.md), other external time information and assumptions about rate variation are needed. [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) age, stem-versus-crown placement and the difference between a minimum occurrence and an origin are consequently central molecular inputs, rather than inconvenient qualifications added afterwards.

The timing of placental diversification illustrates the consequences. Some combined anatomical and molecular reconstructions emphasized a major post-[Cretaceous–Palaeogene boundary](../../../../cretaceous-palaeogene-boundary.md) radiation, whereas calibrated phylogenomic timelines support pre-boundary divergences for important deep placental lineages. [Science 339, pp. 662–667; combined morphology and molecular data and post-boundary interpretation](../../../../the-placental-mammal-ancestor-and-the-post-k-pg-radiation-of-placentals-by-maureen-a-o-leary-and-others.md) [Nature 602, pp. 263–267; calibrated molecular divergence estimates](../../../../a-species-level-timeline-of-mammal-evolution-integrating-phylogenomic-data-by-sandra-alvarez-carretero-and-others.md) These results need not disagree about every aspect of the event: separation of deep lineages, diversification within orders and expansion in ecological disparity can occur at different times. [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) identified only as [Eutheria](../../../../eutheria.md) do not automatically establish a [Cretaceous](../../../../cretaceous.md) crown placental order. Conversely, failure to sample an earlier crown member is not automatically a hard maximum on its age.

The resulting [ghost lineages](../../../../ghost-lineage.md) formulate useful [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) predictions. A molecular hypothesis placing two living branches apart before their oldest known [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) predicts unsampled history, potentially in different regions or ecological settings. Appropriate tests compare collecting intensity and preservation, search suitable deposits and assess whether putative older members possess the required characters. Calibration sensitivity should be reported: changing maximum-age assumptions, rates or [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) placement can move the estimate. A date inferred using a particular [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) as its calibration is not an independent molecular confirmation of that same [fossil](../../../../fossil.md)'s age.

Genomic and developmental work can also test a proposed transformation of anatomy. The mammalian middle ear incorporates bones homologous to components of the ancestral [jaw](../../../../jaw.md) apparatus. Living mammalian development retains a connection involving [Meckel's cartilage](../../../../meckel-s-cartilage.md), and experimental study of its breakdown, including interference with cartilage-resorbing cells, links developmental removal to the transition from a jaw-connected to a detached ear apparatus. [Nature Ecology & Evolution 1, 0093; developmental breakdown of the jaw–ear connection](../../../../meckel%E2%80%99s-cartilage-breakdown-offers-clues-to-mammalian-middle-ear-evolution-by-neal-anthwal-and-others.md) These experiments offer a mechanism consistent with [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) intermediates. They do not show that every [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) lineage used an identical gene-regulatory change or that evolution simply repeated the entire embryonic sequence. To test a specific mechanism, compare matched developmental stages and relevant tissues and distinguish a targeted persistence of the connection from generalized developmental delay.

Younger [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) sometimes allow a more direct molecular test. [collagen](../../../../collagen.md) sequences from [Toxodon](../../../../toxodon.md) and [Macrauchenia](../../../../macrauchenia.md) supported affinities with the perissodactyl side of the placental tree, clarifying the relationships of South American ungulate-like [mammals](../../../../mammal.md) for which convergent anatomy had been problematic. [Nature 522, pp. 81–84; collagen sequences from Toxodon and Macrauchenia](../../../../ancient-proteins-resolve-the-evolutionary-history-of-darwin-s-south-american-ungulates-by-frido-welker-and-others.md) [Palaeoproteomics](../../../../palaeoproteomics.md) recovers amino-acid information rather than a complete [genome](../../../../genome.md), so the number and independence of informative positions differ from [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) studies. Authentication, contamination checks and comparison with plausible alternatives remain necessary, and the result should not be generalized automatically to every extinct South American ungulate lineage.

Ancient mammoth [genomes](../../../../genome.md) extend direct inference into the Early [Pleistocene](../../../../pleistocene.md). More-than-million-year-old [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) revealed distinct mammoth lineages and supported hybrid ancestry in the history of Columbian mammoths. [Nature 591, pp. 265–269; Early Pleistocene genomes and hybrid ancestry of Columbian mammoths](../../../../million-year-old-dna-sheds-light-on-the-genomic-history-of-mammoths-by-tom-van-der-valk-and-others.md) This helps test whether a morphology-based [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) grouping corresponds to one ancestry lineage, reveals population processes invisible in an isolated bone, and connects some adaptive changes to dated specimens. Preservation in a favourable environment does not imply that [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) can be recovered from Permian or Mesozoic [synapsids](../../../../synapsid.md). The deep [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) record must therefore remain accessible chiefly through anatomy, chemistry and comparative models.

Finally, genetic data help separate the origin of an underlying pathway from the origin of a visible trait. A conserved developmental [gene](../../../../gene.md) can be older than a particular ear structure or locomotor specialization; a [gene](../../../../gene.md) duplication or regulatory change is not, by itself, a dated [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) of a phenotype. Functional experiments establish what a mechanism can do in a living context. [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) distribution and comparative anatomy establish when and where the corresponding construction is actually evidenced. [Total-evidence phylogenetics](../../../../total-evidence-phylogenetics.md) can combine these sources, but must retain their different uncertainties and avoid counting correlated characters as independent confirmations.

**The strongest accounts are reciprocal: molecular hypotheses guide [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) searches and interpretations, while [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) calibrate and challenge molecular histories. Genetic data have exposed convergence, refined timescales and revealed reticulate population histories; developmental experiments make some anatomical transitions mechanistically testable without pretending that living [genomes](../../../../genome.md) directly record every ancient synapsid form.**
