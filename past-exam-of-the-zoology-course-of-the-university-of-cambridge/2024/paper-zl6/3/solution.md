<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Quantitative [lineage tracing](../../../../lineage-tracing.md) shifted the interpretation of squamous renewal from a necessarily rigid stem-cell hierarchy toward population-level balance, while [live-cell imaging](../../../../live-cell-imaging.md) and injury experiments revealed local coordination and context-dependent plasticity.** The resulting picture is neither that every basal cell is identical nor that one universal hierarchy applies to every anatomical site. Methods measure different properties, and their assumptions help explain apparently conflicting conclusions.

A classical model of [stratified epithelium](../../../../stratified-epithelium.md) places long-lived [stem cells](../../../../stem-cell.md) above a finite sequence of [transit-amplifying cells](../../../../transit-amplifying-cell.md), whose descendants leave the basal compartment and differentiate. Spatial columns of epidermal cells and slow label dilution were interpreted in terms of [epidermal proliferative units](../../../../epidermal-proliferative-unit.md). Such observations are compatible with a hierarchy, but do not establish it uniquely. A [label-retaining cell](../../../../label-retaining-cell.md) may divide slowly, be terminally differentiated, or retain label because of assay kinetics. Conversely, an actively cycling [stem cell](../../../../stem-cell.md) can lose a dilution-sensitive label rapidly. Clonogenicity after culture or transplantation measures potential in a perturbed environment rather than necessarily identifying the cells that sustain undamaged tissue.

Sparse, inducible permanent [clonal lineage tracing](../../../../clonal-lineage-tracing.md) allowed the descendants of individual basal cells to be followed without relying on label retention. Clone numbers, basal-cell contents and differentiated descendants could be quantified at multiple chase times and compared with explicit stochastic models. In mouse [epidermis](../../../../epidermis.md), such analysis supported a model in which one progenitor class generated both renewing and differentiating daughters, with balance achieved across the population rather than one obligatory asymmetric division for every [stem cell](../../../../stem-cell.md). [Nature 446, pp. 185–189; quantitative sparse clonal fate mapping and population self-renewal](../../../../a-single-type-of-progenitor-cell-maintains-normal-epidermis-by-elizabeth-clayton-and-others.md) Clones disappeared while surviving clones expanded, even though the tissue remained stable. That pattern is not evidence that disappearing clones were necessarily a separate short-lived class.

The bookkeeping is informative. Suppose a proliferative cell divides to give two proliferative daughters with probability $r$, one proliferative and one differentiating daughter with probability $1-2r$, and two differentiating daughters with probability $r$, where $0\leq r\leq1/2$. The expected number of proliferative daughters is:

$$
2r+(1-2r)=1.
$$

Thus the proliferative population can maintain itself even when individual divisions have different outcomes. In a simplified [critical branching process](../../../../critical-branching-process.md) with division rate $\lambda$, the effective rates of clone-size increase and decrease are both $\lambda r$ per proliferative cell. Starting from one such cell:

$$
P(N_t>0)=\frac{1}{1+\lambda rt},\qquad
\mathbb E[N_t\mid N_t>0]=1+\lambda rt.
$$

The product remains one: increasing mean size among surviving clones can accompany unchanged expected labelled cell number across all initial clones. These formulae illustrate survivor conditioning; actual tissue geometry, noncycling basal cells, finite compartments and local interactions can require a different quantitative model.

Work on mouse oesophageal [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md) combined label-retention assays with [lineage tracing](../../../../lineage-tracing.md) and found that a single proliferative population could account for maintenance. After wounding, its outcomes became biased toward producing proliferating daughters, then returned toward balance after repair. [Science 337, pp. 1091–1093; label retention, stochastic lineage dynamics and reversible wound responses](../../../../a-single-progenitor-population-switches-behavior-to-maintain-and-repair-esophageal-epithelium-by-david-p-doupe-and-others.md) Changing the division rate and changing the probability of daughter fates are different responses. More divisions with unchanged balance can accelerate replacement without expanding the renewing pool; biased outcomes can expand that pool. Neither observation alone requires conversion of a terminally differentiated cell into a [stem cell](../../../../stem-cell.md).

Evidence for heterogeneity also remained important. Marker-selected tracing and proliferation analysis in mouse [epidermis](../../../../epidermis.md) supported distinct slow-cycling stem and committed progenitor compartments. [Nature 489, pp. 257–262; marker-selected populations, proliferation kinetics and hierarchy](../../../../distinct-contribution-of-stem-and-progenitor-cells-to-epidermal-maintenance-by-guilhem-mascre-and-others.md) Such results should not be dismissed simply because a [single-progenitor model](../../../../single-progenitor-model.md) fits another dataset. Anatomical site, promoter expression, induction dose, chase duration and which cells are scored can change the sampled population. A driver that preferentially labels a slow-cycling subset and a broadly induced driver need not produce the same clone distribution. Models should be compared against joint measurements of clone survival, [cell cycle](../../../../cell-cycle-split.md) kinetics and labelled differentiated output rather than one convenient summary statistic.

Live [intravital imaging](../../../../intravital-imaging.md) added information that destructive sampling cannot supply. Tracking the same cells shows which event precedes which: division, loss of basal contact, movement into a suprabasal layer, or neighbour expansion. In adult mouse [epidermis](../../../../epidermis.md), local differentiation was followed by neighbour expansion and compensatory [cell cycle](../../../../cell-cycle-split.md) progression, supporting a mechanism in which cell loss locally stimulates [self-renewal](../../../../self-renewal.md). [Cell Stem Cell 23, pp. 677–686.e4; intravital tracking, local cell loss and compensatory division](../../../../homeostatic-epidermal-stem-cell-self-renewal-is-driven-by-local-differentiation-by-kailin-r-mesa-and-others.md) Balanced stochastic clone statistics therefore need not mean that cells make independent random decisions insulated from their neighbours. A spatially coordinated system can generate population-level fate balance and apparent randomness at the level of individual retrospective clones.

Modern molecular methods refine, but do not replace, those functional tests. [single-cell RNA sequencing](../../../../single-cell-rna-sequencing.md) identifies heterogeneous molecular states and pathway activity; spatial measurements locate them relative to [basement membrane](../../../../basement-membrane.md), follicles or wounds. A trajectory inferred from transcriptomes is not a directly observed lineage, and an intermediate expression profile can reflect a transient response rather than a cell reversing fate. Combining molecular profiling with permanent labels, repeated imaging and appropriately timed perturbations distinguishes state similarity, ancestry and causal requirement. [cell cycle](../../../../cell-cycle-split.md) state and injury-associated inflammation also need to be separated from a genuinely distinct renewing population.

Direct evidence for [cell fate plasticity](../../../../cell-fate-plasticity.md) requires more than changing proliferation within an existing progenitor class. Pre-injury labelling of [GATA6](../../../../gata6.md)-expressing differentiated sebaceous-duct cells showed that their descendants could move into wounded [interfollicular epidermis](../../../../interfollicular-epidermis.md), regain basal renewing properties and produce a broader range of epidermal descendants. Long-term tracing and live observations supported [dedifferentiation](../../../../dedifferentiation.md) under the wound conditions. [Nature Cell Biology 19, pp. 603–613; pre-injury lineage labelling, live imaging and sebaceous-duct cell plasticity](../../../../wounding-induces-dedifferentiation-of-epidermal-gata6-plus-cells-and-acquisition-of-stem-cell-properties-by-giacomo-donati-and-others.md) The key comparison is the fate of the same pre-labelled population in undamaged and damaged tissue. It is not sufficient to find Gata6-lineage [fluorescence](../../../../fluorescence-split.md) in a cell that has just begun expressing the marker after injury.

Several controls make such a claim persuasive. Verify the initial labelled cell identities before damage; allow the inducer to clear; exclude rare pre-labelled [stem cells](../../../../stem-cell.md) or reporter leakage; and demonstrate sustained renewal rather than transient basal residence. Imaging a labelled cell's transition and showing its later functional output is stronger than a static double-positive image. Exclude cell fusion and selective expansion of an unrecognized initial subpopulation where relevant. Perturbing a proposed reprogramming regulator should preserve source-cell viability, since losing the source is not the same as preventing its identity change.

The general conclusion is a flexible hierarchy of evidence. Label retention measures division history, clonal tracing measures descendant output, [live-cell imaging](../../../../live-cell-imaging.md) measures event order and local interactions, and molecular profiling measures state. **Together they support dynamic [population self-renewal](../../../../population-self-renewal.md), site-dependent heterogeneity and injury-induced epithelial plasticity, while leaving normal maintenance distinct from regenerative potential.** Claims about one mouse skin region or oesophagus should be tested independently before being generalized to every squamous tissue or to humans.
