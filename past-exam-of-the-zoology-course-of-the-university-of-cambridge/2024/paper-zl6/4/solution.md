**I would test whether the candidate changes [digit identity](../../../../digit-identity.md) while separating that effect from changes in limb axes, digit number, growth and survival.** A convincing result would combine an early, position-appropriate requirement, a complementary gain-of-function phenotype and specific rescue, with positive evidence that an affected digit has adopted an alternative identity. Expression alone or an absent digit would not establish the hypothesis.

First define the experimental system and the identity being measured. In a chosen species and limb, map the normal skeletal pattern, digit positions, phalangeal structure, associated soft tissues and combinations of early molecular features. Record anatomical stage as well as chronological age. Serial imaging of condensations and later cartilage and bone distinguishes a developmental change from delayed ossification. Do not assume that a single marker labels the same identity in every digit or across all tetrapods: comparative digit transcriptomes have challenged universal digit-specific signatures. [Nature Communications 10, 3244; digit transcriptional comparisons and limits of universal identity signatures](../../../../evidence-against-tetrapod-wide-digit-identities-and-for-a-limited-frame-shift-in-bird-wings-by-thomas-a-stewart-and-others.md) Identity should therefore be defined using several independent features in the actual experimental species.

Establish the candidate's spatial and temporal expression before, during and after the proposed identity-specification window. Use validated [in situ hybridization](../../../../in-situ-hybridization.md), protein detection or an endogenous reporter, and compare anterior–posterior position, proximal–distal level and dorsal–ventral side. Determine whether expression is in [mesenchyme](../../../../mesenchyme.md), ectoderm, a signalling centre or differentiating cartilage. Co-expression with a digit marker suggests an association; expression preceding that marker makes an instructive role plausible but still does not demonstrate it. A reporter must be checked against endogenous expression and function, since insertion or overexpression can change dosage or cellular localization.

The normal patterning context supplies crucial controls. Posterior [limb bud](../../../../limb-bud.md) [Sonic hedgehog](../../../../sonic-hedgehog.md) signalling has a demonstrated ability to polarize the limb: ectopic expression can generate altered anterior–posterior patterns. [Cell 75, pp. 1401–1416; ectopic Shh expression and limb polarization](../../../../sonic-hedgehog-mediates-the-polarizing-activity-of-the-zpa-by-robert-d-riddle-and-others.md) The distal [apical ectodermal ridge](../../../../apical-ectodermal-ridge.md) also supports outgrowth, while other signals establish dorsal–ventral properties. The candidate might alter one of these organizers and only indirectly alter digit identities. I would therefore measure organizer position and pathway activity, [limb bud](../../../../limb-bud.md) size and overall axis specification before treating an abnormal skeleton as a direct digit-identity phenotype.

For necessity, generate at least two independent loss-of-function alleles or validated independent targeting reagents, then establish that candidate protein or activity is actually lost. A constitutive lethal or globally malformed embryo is uninformative about a specific limb function. [tissue-specific gene perturbation](../../../../tissue-specific-gene-perturbation.md) in limb [mesenchyme](../../../../mesenchyme.md) or ectoderm, combined with [temporal gene perturbation](../../../../temporal-gene-perturbation.md), can distinguish an early role in establishing the bud from a later role in assigning digit character. Include littermate or sibling controls, driver-only and inducer-only controls, and assess possible effects of the delivery method itself. Recombination, protein depletion and pathway-response delays must be measured rather than assumed to coincide with the time of induction.

The strongest predicted loss-of-function outcome is a reproducible change in a digit's position-associated character while a digit remains present in the corresponding lineage and location. A smaller digit, delayed cartilage, [apoptosis](../../../../apoptosis.md) or reduced progenitor number could instead explain the phenotype. I would measure [cell proliferation](../../../../cell-proliferation.md), [apoptosis](../../../../apoptosis.md), condensation number, local tissue movement and chondrogenic differentiation at early stages, before secondary degeneration. If a digit is missing, fate-map its prospective territory to determine whether cells died, failed to expand, contributed elsewhere or retained an alternative identity. That analysis can identify a real developmental requirement without overcalling it specification.

For sufficiency, activate the candidate at a normally negative position during the relevant window, using regulated expression near physiological levels. Compare equivalent expression after the window has closed. A position-appropriate identity transformation induced early but not late would support an instructive, competence-dependent function. A broad expansion of tissue or induction of ectopic organizer activity would require a different interpretation. Include vector and inactive-product controls where appropriate, verify dose and distribution, and avoid equating high-level toxicity with a patterned effect. Mosaic activation can reveal whether only expressing cells change or whether neighbouring cells respond through an induced signal.

Timing is particularly important because digit patterning integrates signal duration and tissue expansion. Lineage and perturbation studies of Shh supported an expansion-based temporal contribution to [digit identity](../../../../digit-identity.md). [Cell 118, pp. 517–528; Shh lineage history, expansion and duration-dependent digit patterning](../../../../evidence-for-an-expansion-based-temporal-shh-gradient-in-specifying-vertebrate-digit-identities-by-brian-d-harfe-and-others.md) An intervention can consequently change which cells experience a signal, for how long, or where they end up. I would permanently label candidate-expressing and neighbouring cells before perturbation and track them through condensation. Measure both pathway duration and cell displacement, rather than inferring an identity transformation merely from a digit's final ordinal position.

Rescue should restore the relevant candidate function with its appropriate timing and location. Re-expression of a targeting-resistant wild-type product that corrects the phenotype supports specificity. Domain mutants can test whether DNA binding, secretion or another biochemical activity is required, but their stability and localization must be confirmed. A rescue that restores total limb growth but leaves the identity pattern abnormal would separate these functions. Conversely, restoring a downstream organizer signal may rescue the phenotype while indicating that the candidate normally acts upstream rather than directly in digit cells.

To position the candidate mechanistically, combine its perturbation with controlled manipulation of established pathways. If the candidate requires a Shh response, ask whether its activity can bypass pathway inhibition or whether the candidate itself is required for a Shh-induced transformation. Such [epistasis](../../../../epistasis.md) tests must use doses and stages that preserve a limb competent to respond. Transcript profiling can identify early consequences before overt shape changes. For a putative transcription factor, binding assays and a targeted perturbation of a candidate regulatory element can test direct regulation. Binding or differential expression alone is not proof that the target causes the identity phenotype.

Finally, analyse the data with the animal or independently manipulated limb as the biological replicate, accounting for the shared environment of digits within one limb. Randomize treatments, blind anatomical scoring and use predefined identity criteria. A contralateral limb is useful but not always independent if the perturbation is systemic or changes whole-embryo timing. Test reproducibility across developmental batches and, if arguing for a conserved function, validate it in another suitably staged species rather than assuming identical digit programmes.

**Support for specification would require necessity, sufficiency and rescue tied to a defined spatial and temporal window, plus evidence for an alternative identity rather than simple tissue loss.** Other outcomes would still be informative: they might place the [gene](../../../../gene.md) in growth, competence, organizer maintenance or later differentiation instead of digit-identity assignment itself.
