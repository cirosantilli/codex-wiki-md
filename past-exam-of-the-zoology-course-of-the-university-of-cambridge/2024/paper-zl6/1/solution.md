**[cell specification](../../../../cell-specification.md) and [morphogenesis](../../../../morphogenesis.md) can form a reciprocal causal loop: cell identity changes force production and tissue material properties, while the resulting geometry and loading alter signalling and differentiation.** A molecular programme need not first assign every identity and then leave mechanics merely to implement a predetermined shape. Conversely, correlation between a new shape and a new marker does not establish that mechanical input instructed the fate. Developing intestine, avian skin and lung illustrate distinct mechanisms and experimental tests.

Cells generate forces through [actomyosin contractility](../../../../actomyosin-contractility.md), transmit them through [cell adhesion](../../../../cell-adhesion.md) and the [extracellular matrix](../../../../extracellular-matrix.md), and respond through [mechanotransduction](../../../../mechanotransduction.md). Changes in cell number, volume, polarity and matrix composition modify the mechanical problem. A deformed tissue can change ligand distribution, receptor exposure or intracellular signalling without any change in a ligand's production rate. One must therefore distinguish force generation, transmission and sensing. A contractility inhibitor can disrupt all three as well as [cell division](../../../../cell-division.md), so an abnormal organ alone is not a mechanistic explanation.

In the embryonic chick intestine, differentiation of surrounding [smooth muscle](../../../../smooth-muscle.md) changes the constraints on epithelial growth. Successive muscle layers restrict expansion in different directions, helping a growing epithelial–mesenchymal composite form ridges, zigzags and eventually villi through [buckling](../../../../buckling.md). Muscle perturbations and artificial confinement support a causal role for physical boundary conditions rather than a purely epithelial prepattern. [Science 342, pp. 212–218; muscle-layer perturbation, external confinement and folding](../../../../villification-how-the-gut-gets-its-villi-by-amy-e-shyer-and-others.md) Here an initial differentiation event supplies a mechanical constraint that changes tissue shape. Equivalent external confinement is especially informative because it can replace a mechanical function without restoring all molecular products of the missing muscle.

The new geometry then feeds back into regional cell-state regulation. Epithelial [Sonic hedgehog](../../../../sonic-hedgehog.md) signals become concentrated beneath the curved villus tips, promoting mesenchymal [BMP signalling](../../../../bmp-signalling.md); the resulting signals restrict the stem-cell-associated proliferative programme away from those tips. [Cell 161, pp. 569–580; tissue geometry, epithelial Shh concentration and mesenchymal BMP induction](../../../../bending-gradients-how-the-intestinal-stem-cell-gets-its-home-by-amy-e-shyer-and-others.md) Thus spatially patterned signalling can arise from deformation of a source that was initially more uniform. The result concerns localization of a developmental stem-cell programme, not proof that every cell has irreversibly acquired one terminal identity. Comparing curved and flattened explants, measuring ligand production as well as local pathway activity, and changing pathway activity downstream of deformation distinguish altered geometry from a change in total ligand supply.

This chick mechanism should not be imposed unchanged on mouse intestine. Mesenchymal patterning and surface deformation have different temporal relationships in different species. The reusable principle is a feedback between differentiated supporting tissue, physical geometry and local signalling; the exact sequence is an empirical question. A useful control compares developmental stage and viability, since a tissue that has simply stopped developing may lack both folds and differentiation markers without either being the direct cause of the other.

Avian skin supplies a different route from mechanics to identity. Contractile dermal cells aggregate beneath the [epidermis](../../../../epidermis.md), mechanically compressing it. Experiments linked this dermal self-organization to activation of epidermal [beta-catenin](../../../../beta-catenin.md) and initiation of the feather-follicle programme. Altering contractility or tissue mechanical conditions changed aggregate formation and pattern. [Science 357, pp. 811–815; dermal aggregation, contractility and epidermal β-catenin activation](../../../../emergent-cellular-self-organization-and-mechanosensation-initiate-follicle-pattern-in-the-avian-skin-by-amy-e-shyer-and-others.md) This makes the [feather placode](../../../../feather-placode.md) an emergent epithelial–mesenchymal structure rather than requiring a complete molecular placode map to exist before any cell movement.

The important distinction is between [beta-catenin](../../../../beta-catenin.md)'s junctional and transcriptional roles. Redistribution of an adhesion-associated protein alone is not evidence for a fate change; nuclear activity and appropriate target expression, followed by placode development, provide a stronger chain. To test ordering, one can alter dermal force production while preserving epidermal viability, apply controlled compression, and perturb the epithelial response separately. Restoration of placode [gene](../../../../gene.md) activity by a downstream intervention would place that activity after mechanical input, but would not necessarily rescue normal spacing. Molecular identity and periodic geometry are related outputs that can be experimentally separated.

In the developing lung, differences in how [epithelial cells](../../../../epithelial-cell.md) experience a common physical input help generate different differentiated states. [live-cell imaging](../../../../live-cell-imaging.md) and perturbations showed that forces associated with [fetal breathing movements](../../../../fetal-breathing-movement.md) and fluid displacement contribute to [type I alveolar cell](../../../../type-i-alveolar-cell.md) differentiation. A subset of progenitors protrudes toward [mesenchyme](../../../../mesenchyme.md) through [FGF10](../../../../fgf10.md)–[FGFR2](../../../../fgfr2.md)-dependent signalling; enrichment of apical myosin in those cells resists flattening and supports [type II alveolar cell](../../../../type-ii-alveolar-cell.md) fate. [Developmental Cell 44, pp. 297–312.e5; mechanical-force perturbation, cell shape and alveolar differentiation](../../../../the-strength-of-mechanical-forces-determines-the-differentiation-of-alveolar-epithelial-cells-by-jiao-li-and-others.md) Thus a local biochemical signal changes the cell's mechanical response, while loading helps determine whether it becomes thin and extended or retains the alternative morphology and differentiation programme.

A decisive lung experiment must therefore vary force and growth-factor signalling independently. If a receptor perturbation changes fate, it could act through competence, proliferation, survival or the physical protection from flattening. Cell tracking and early marker measurements distinguish transformation of an existing progenitor from selective loss of one population. Controlled loading, matched fluid and culture conditions, measurement of cell shape, and restoration of the relevant contractile response can separate these alternatives. Flattening alone is not a sufficient endpoint: the molecular and functional differentiation of the two alveolar classes must also be assessed.

These systems demonstrate three forms of coupling: differentiation changes a tissue constraint, geometry reorganizes an extracellular signal, and local signalling tunes a cell's response to a physical force. They need not all use the same sensor or transcription factor. Nor does mechanics replace biochemical information: the same deformation can elicit different responses in cells with different [developmental competence](../../../../developmental-competence.md). A fate programme can also alter adhesion or matrix properties and thereby change the mechanical input experienced by neighbouring cells, closing a [mechanochemical feedback](../../../../mechanochemical-feedback.md) loop.

The strongest general strategy combines time-resolved [live-cell imaging](../../../../live-cell-imaging.md), fate or lineage reporters, a physical measurement and an independently targeted perturbation. Initial recoil after [laser ablation](../../../../laser-ablation.md) reports load relative to resistance rather than an absolute force without a material model; substrate deformation and pressure measurements likewise need calibrated assumptions. Acute and tissue-restricted perturbations, sham procedures, independent genetic alleles and appropriate rescue help avoid generalized damage as the explanation. **Fate and form are coupled through specific causal chains, but identifying a mechanically instructive step requires measuring both the physical intermediate and the resulting change in developmental state.**
