**The terms describe contrasting palatal architectures; their literal “old” and “new” implications do not establish which architecture was ancestral.** A [palaeognathous palate](../../../../palaeognathous-palate.md) has extensive bracing of the palatal apparatus against the [skull](../../../../skull.md) base, involving the [vomer](../../../../vomer.md), [pterygoid bone](../../../../pterygoid-bone.md), [palatine bone](../../../../palatine-bone.md) and prominent [basipterygoid processes](../../../../basipterygoid-process.md). In a [neognathous palate](../../../../neognathous-palate.md), reduced bracing and a more mobile pterygoid–palatine connection permit greater relative movement. These are arrangements of [joints](../../../../joint-of-a-line-collection.md) and contacts, not simply differences in lower-jaw shape. The distinction concerns the structural basis of [cranial kinesis](../../../../cranial-kinesis.md), movement within the [skull](../../../../skull.md) beyond ordinary opening of the lower [jaw](../../../../jaw.md).[Results and Discussion; vomer contacts, morphological disparity and limits of functional reconstruction](../../../../evolution-of-the-vomer-and-its-implications-for-cranial-kinesis-in-paraves-by-han-hu-and-others.md)

Living [Palaeognathae](../../../../palaeognathae.md), including [ostriches](../../../../ostrich.md) and [tinamous](../../../../tinamou.md), and living [Neognathae](../../../../neognathae.md), including [landfowl](../../../../landfowl.md), [waterfowl](../../../../waterfowl.md) and other [birds](../../../../bird.md), form the deepest two surviving branches of crown [Aves](../../../../aves.md). Historically, the heavily braced condition was commonly interpreted as retention of a primitive nonavian configuration, followed by [evolution](../../../../evolution.md) of a more mobile [palate](../../../../palate.md) in [Neognathae](../../../../neognathae.md). That interpretation combined resemblance to [outgroups](../../../../outgroup.md) with the misleading intuition that a lineage branching early must preserve the ancestral [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md). A sister lineage has had just as much time to change; its living members are not ancestors of the other branch.

The difficulty is establishing [character polarity](../../../../character-polarity.md) when delicate palatal elements are absent, crushed or wrongly identified in [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) near the crown divergence. Comparisons of the [vomer](../../../../vomer.md) across living and extinct [birds](../../../../bird.md) documented meaningful differences in contact geometry but also substantial variation and repeated reduction. The 2019 analysis by [Han Hu](../../../../han-hu.md) and colleagues emphasized how incomplete contacts restrict inferences about extinct [cranial kinesis](../../../../cranial-kinesis.md). A single robust-looking element cannot establish an immobile [skull](../../../../skull.md), and one potentially mobile [joint](../../../../joint-of-a-line-collection.md) cannot establish the full range of movement. Constraints elsewhere in the kinetic chain remain relevant.[Results and Discussion; vomer contacts, morphological disparity and limits of functional reconstruction](../../../../evolution-of-the-vomer-and-its-implications-for-cranial-kinesis-in-paraves-by-han-hu-and-others.md)

The [Ichthyornis](../../../../ichthyornis.md) material described in 2018 helped separate components of the supposedly modern avian head. Three-dimensionally preserved [skull](../../../../skull.md) elements combined a small beak at the [jaw](../../../../jaw.md) tips and retained [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) with a reconstructed kinetic apparatus, while other cranial regions remained markedly dinosaurian. Thus [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) loss, beak expansion and acquisition of a mobile feeding apparatus did not form one indivisible evolutionary event. This example of [mosaic evolution](../../../../mosaic-evolution.md) also weakens the argument that a toothed stem [bird](../../../../bird.md) must have a fully rigid, palaeognath-like [palate](../../../../palate.md). Its importance is the combination of characters, rather than an inference that every modern kinetic mechanism was already identical.[Nature 557, pp. 96–100; mosaic cranial construction and inferred kinetic apparatus](../../../../complete-ichthyornis-skull-illuminates-mosaic-assembly-of-the-avian-head-by-daniel-j-field-and-others.md)

The strongest recent change came from [Janavis finalidens](../../../../janavis-finalidens.md), a toothed [bird](../../../../bird.md) from approximately 66.7 million years ago, described in 2022. [micro-computed tomography](../../../../micro-computed-tomography.md) exposed a well-preserved [pterygoid bone](../../../../pterygoid-bone.md) within the specimen. Its articular construction and comparative shape resemble those of [Galloanserae](../../../../galloanserae.md), supporting a mobile pterygoid–palatine connection. The animal was recovered outside the living-bird [crown group](../../../../crown-group.md), close to [Ichthyornis](../../../../ichthyornis.md), rather than inside [Neognathae](../../../../neognathae.md). Consequently, [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md) previously treated as a [neognath](../../../../neognathae.md) innovation occurred on an earlier branch. On that reconstruction, mobility evolved before crown [Aves](../../../../aves.md), and the tightly integrated [palaeognathous palate](../../../../palaeognathous-palate.md) is a subsequent specialization of the [palaeognath](../../../../palaeognathae.md) lineage.[Nature 612, pp. 100–105; Janavis pterygoid, comparative shape and ancestral-palate interpretation](../../../../cretaceous-ornithurine-supports-a-neognathous-crown-bird-ancestor-by-juan-benito-and-others.md)

The inference is best understood by separating observation, placement and reconstruction. The observation is the morphology of a [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) [pterygoid bone](../../../../pterygoid-bone.md). Its placement outside crown [Aves](../../../../aves.md) comes from a wider analysis of [phylogenetic characters](../../../../phylogenetic-character.md) using [phylogenetics](../../../../phylogenetics.md). The inference that the crown ancestor had a relatively mobile [palate](../../../../palate.md) combines those findings with distributions of [phylogenetic characters](../../../../phylogenetic-character.md) in living groups. None alone proves the entire history. Convergence in the [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) lineage remains a logical alternative; it would require a different pattern of character acquisition from ancestral mobility followed by increased bracing. Distinguishing these alternatives requires additional palatal characters and [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) sampling, not the age of the specimen alone. A 66.7-million-year-old stem [bird](../../../../bird.md) need not predate the crown ancestor chronologically: stem and crown lineages can coexist.

Functional and developmental caution also matters. [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) generally lack [ligaments](../../../../ligament.md), [muscles](../../../../muscle.md) and direct measurements of movement. Comparative [joint](../../../../joint-of-a-line-collection.md) geometry can support potential mobility, but cannot yield exact excursions or feeding performance without further assumptions. The [palaeognathous palate](../../../../palaeognathous-palate.md) should not be reduced to total [skull](../../../../skull.md) immobility, nor every [neognathous palate](../../../../neognathous-palate.md) to one uniform kinetic mechanism. **The defensible revision is that important neognath-like palatal features originated before the living-bird crown split, making [palaeognath](../../../../palaeognathae.md) specializations a plausible derived condition.** The two living [clades](../../../../clade.md) remain valid; what changes is the inferred history of the anatomical states used to describe them.
