**The two classical anatomical hypotheses are derivation from [gill](../../../../gill.md) arches and differentiation of continuous lateral [fin](../../../../fin.md) folds. Developmental co-option supplies an additional explanation for shared patterning mechanisms.** The histories of the shoulder support, appendage blade and developmental programme need not be identical. Evaluating them separately allows evidence that initially appears contradictory to describe different components of the same evolutionary transition.

The **[gill-arch hypothesis of paired fin origins](../../../../gill-arch-hypothesis-of-paired-fin-origins.md)**, associated with [Carl Gegenbaur](../../../../carl-gegenbaur.md), proposes transformation of a posterior branchial apparatus into a girdle and projecting appendage skeleton. In living [cartilaginous fishes](../../../../chondrichthyes.md), [branchial rays](../../../../branchial-ray.md) project from the [gill](../../../../gill.md) skeleton, inviting comparison with skeletal supports extending from a [pectoral girdle](../../../../pectoral-girdle.md). The historical archipterygium interpretation treated these as evidence for a gill-to-fin transformation. Positional similarity alone is insufficient: the relevant branchial construction must be ancestral, and a plausible transformation must explain both the supporting girdle and distal [fin](../../../../fin.md).

Developmental experiments provide stronger evidence for shared mechanisms than simple adult resemblance. Work on the little skate, [Leucoraja erinacea](../../../../leucoraja-erinacea.md), showed interactions among [Sonic hedgehog](../../../../sonic-hedgehog.md), [FGF signalling](../../../../fgf-signalling.md) and [retinoic acid](../../../../retinoic-acid.md) in branchial-ray outgrowth and patterning, resembling regulatory interactions in paired appendages. The crucial inference is reuse of a spatial patterning system; expression of the same molecule by itself would be weaker evidence. Functional perturbation helps distinguish an active requirement from a coincidental expression domain.[PNAS 106, pp. 5720–5724; retinoic acid/Shh/Fgf8 regulation of skate branchial rays](../../../../shared-developmental-mechanisms-pattern-the-vertebrate-gill-arch-and-paired-fin-skeletons-by-j-andrew-gillis-and-others.md)

A 2016 study used timed inhibition of [Hedgehog signalling](../../../../hedgehog-signalling.md) with [cyclopamine](../../../../cyclopamine.md), lineage labelling and measurements of [cell proliferation](../../../../cell-proliferation.md) in skate [embryos](../../../../embryo.md). Earlier inhibition disrupted branchial-ray polarity, while later inhibition reduced their production or expansion. [EdU labeling](../../../../dna-synthesis-labeling-with-edu.md) provided a proxy for proliferative activity through new [dNA synthesis](../../../../dna-synthesis.md), and labelled precursor positions related early arch territories to later [cartilage](../../../../cartilage.md). Solvent-treated controls are necessary because handling and drug vehicle can alter development; stage matching and general growth measurements are needed to distinguish pattern-specific effects from generalized retardation. These results support parallel roles for the signal in axial organization and progenitor expansion. They do not alone demonstrate that an ancestral [gill](../../../../gill.md) arch physically moved to become a [fin](../../../../fin.md).[Development 143, pp. 1313–1317; cyclopamine timing, fate mapping and EdU proliferation assays](../../../../a-shared-role-for-sonic-hedgehog-signalling-in-patterning-chondrichthyan-gill-arch-appendages-and-tetrapod-limbs-by-j-andrew-gillis-and-brian-k-hall.md)

The [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) argument for a pharyngeal contribution was strengthened before this examination by the 2023 analysis of [Kolymaspis sibirica](../../../../kolymaspis-sibirica.md). [computed tomography](../../../../computed-tomography.md) and comparative [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md) identified a craniothoracic relationship interpreted as connecting the shoulder to a posterior, sixth [branchial arch](../../../../branchial-arch.md). This provides evidence for the origin of a component of the [pectoral girdle](../../../../pectoral-girdle.md), rather than a complete [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) sequence converting [branchial rays](../../../../branchial-ray.md) into the entire free [fin](../../../../fin.md). Homology of the articulation and the reconstruction of the head–trunk boundary remain important assumptions. **A pharyngeal contribution to the girdle does not require a wholly pharyngeal origin for every appendicular tissue.**[Nature 623, pp. 550–554; Kolymaspis tomography, craniothoracic articulation and sixth-branchial-arch hypothesis](../../../../fossil-evidence-for-a-pharyngeal-origin-of-the-vertebrate-pectoral-girdle-by-martin-d-brazeau-and-others.md)

The **[lateral fin-fold hypothesis](../../../../lateral-fin-fold-hypothesis.md)** proposes that the two localized pairs of [fins](../../../../fin.md) arose through differentiation and regional restriction of continuous bilateral folds along the body wall. It predicts possible precursors extending through intervening pectoral and pelvic regions, with increased regional differentiation and locomotor control later. It does not require an ancestral animal to possess fully developed pectoral and [pelvic girdles](../../../../pelvic-girdle.md) throughout the fold. A persistent developmental field capable of forming appendages could be evolutionarily restricted even if a continuous fold is not retained in living [embryos](../../../../embryo.md).

The 2022 description of the jawless [galeaspid](../../../../galeaspid.md) [Tujiaaspis vividus](../../../../tujiaaspis-vividus.md) supplied a striking [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) comparison. Articulated specimens preserve continuous paired ventrolateral [fins](../../../../fin.md) extending from the branchial region toward the tail. They occur outside the group of [osteostracans](../../../../osteostracan.md) and jawed [vertebrates](../../../../vertebrate.md) in the reported tree. Models using [computational fluid dynamics](../../../../computational-fluid-dynamics.md) indicated that the lateral surfaces could provide passive lift. This supports an evolutionary stage with extended lateral appendages before differentiation of localized, actively controlled [pectoral fins](../../../../pectoral-fin.md); it does not prove powered swimming by the folds or show that the [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) itself was a direct ancestor. Surface reconstruction, swimming conditions and the assumed body geometry constrain the functional model.[Nature 609, pp. 959–963, Figs. 1–4; Tujiaaspis lateral fins, fluid dynamics and ancestral-state reconstruction](../../../../galeaspid-anatomy-and-the-origin-of-vertebrate-paired-appendages-by-zhikun-gai-and-others.md)

Other jawless stem relatives sharpen the proposed sequence. [osteostracans](../../../../osteostracan.md) have differentiated [pectoral fins](../../../../pectoral-fin.md) without an equivalent localized pelvic pair; jawed [vertebrates](../../../../vertebrate.md) subsequently include both. This distribution argues against assuming simultaneous origin of the two appendage pairs. In a fin-fold interpretation, anterior differentiation can precede restriction of the remaining competent body-wall region to a pelvic position. [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) supporting that sequence still have to distinguish flexible [fins](../../../../fin.md) from rigid body-armour extensions and demonstrate their relation to the enclosing skeleton.

The **[median-fin co-option hypothesis](../../../../median-fin-co-option-hypothesis.md)** addresses where the developmental machinery came from. [median fins](../../../../median-fin.md) are evolutionarily older than the paired appendages. Comparative work on [shark](../../../../shark.md) and [lamprey](../../../../lamprey.md) [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) found that median-fin development uses aspects of the appendage-patterning toolkit, including regional [Hox gene](../../../../hox-gene.md) expression and boundary-associated regulation. In [sharks](../../../../shark.md), the median-fin skeleton develops largely from [paraxial mesoderm](../../../../paraxial-mesoderm.md), whereas paired appendage [mesenchyme](../../../../mesenchyme.md) has major contributions from [lateral plate mesoderm](../../../../lateral-plate-mesoderm.md). Similar patterning despite different tissue sources supports redeployment of an older regulatory programme. It does not imply that a dorsal [fin](../../../../fin.md) literally split and migrated down the body wall.[Nature 442, pp. 1033–1037; shark and lamprey median fins, tissue origins and appendage-patterning genes](../../../../evidence-that-mechanisms-of-fin-development-evolved-in-the-midline-of-early-vertebrates-by-renata-freitas-and-others.md)

This is the distinction between [deep homology](../../../../deep-homology.md) of mechanisms and a claim of whole-organ [biological homology](../../../../biological-homology.md). Shared regulation could reflect inherited [serial homology](../../../../serial-homology.md), recruitment of the same programme to a novel site, or a mixture of both. Testing alternatives requires spatial and temporal perturbations, embryonic [lineage tracing](../../../../lineage-tracing.md), comparison across jawless and jawed [outgroups](../../../../outgroup.md), and [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) evidence locating the relevant structures. Tissue origins are informative but can change during [evolution](../../../../evolution.md); neither a shared gene nor a different germ-layer contribution is a self-sufficient verdict.

**The strongest synthesis is component-based:** [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) lateral [fins](../../../../fin.md) support a body-wall appendage precursor; developmental comparisons support recruitment of an older fin-patterning programme; and developmental plus 2023 [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) evidence supports a pharyngeal contribution to the shoulder. The remaining problem is to establish how those components were assembled and when regional pectoral and pelvic control evolved. This synthesis retains the testable content of the classical hypotheses without treating either a complete gill-to-fin conversion or a literal universal [fin](../../../../fin.md) fold as already demonstrated.
