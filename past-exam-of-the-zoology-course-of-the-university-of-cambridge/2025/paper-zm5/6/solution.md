↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**I would combine quantitative-genetic [cross-fostering](../../../../cross-fostering.md) with controlled transfer of maternal brood-chamber material in the dung beetle Onthophagus taurus.** The hypothesis is that maternal genetic differences influence offspring development partly through the amount of food provided and partly through the composition of material transferred from the mother's body. These pathways must be separated: a larger brood provision, a beneficial microorganism and an inherited offspring [allele](../../../../allele.md) can all increase offspring mass, but imply different mechanisms.

The system has three relevant precedents. A paternal half-sib study of [Onthophagus taurus](../../../../onthophagus-taurus.md) detected genetic variation in maternal brood provisioning and substantial maternal contributions to offspring [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md). [PNAS 99, pp. 6828–6832; paternal half-sib design, brood mass and maternal contributions](../../../../the-genetics-of-maternal-care-direct-and-indirect-genetic-effects-on-phenotype-in-the-dung-beetle-onthophagus-taurus-by-john-hunt-and-others.md) A separate study reared this species with sterile dung and detected maternal contributions and shared bacterial sequences in the brood chamber and offspring, supporting transmission through the provisioned environment. [PLoS ONE 8, e79061; sterile dung, brood-chamber material and parent–offspring bacterial sequence comparisons](../../../../brood-ball-mediated-transmission-of-microbiome-members-in-the-dung-beetle-onthophagus-taurus-coleoptera-scarabaeidae-by-anne-m-estes-and-others.md) Experimental developmental benefits of pedestal-associated [microbiota](../../../../microbiota.md) were demonstrated in [Onthophagus gazella](../../../../onthophagus-gazella.md), including context dependence under environmental stress; those functional results should not be assumed automatically to hold in [Onthophagus taurus](../../../../onthophagus-taurus.md). [The American Naturalist 188, pp. 679–692; Onthophagus gazella, pedestal removal and environmental stress](../../../../developmental-and-ecological-benefits-of-the-maternally-transmitted-microbiota-in-a-dung-beetle-by-daniel-b-schwab-and-others.md)

The conceptual link is an [indirect genetic effect](../../../../indirect-genetic-effect.md): the provider's [genotype](../../../../genotype.md) affects the recipient's [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md) through a rearing environment. The transferred mediator would be maternal faecal or secreted material, including associated viable [microorganisms](../../../../microorganism.md) and soluble products. This fits the [socially transferred material](../../../../socially-transferred-material.md) framework because the mother contributes material processed in or produced by her body; collecting external dung alone would not establish such a transfer. [Trends in Ecology & Evolution 38, pp. 446–458; framework and distinction from merely collecting external food](../../../../socially-transferred-materials-why-and-how-to-study-them-by-sanja-maria-hakala-and-others.md) The actual molecular mediator is an experimental question. Finding bacterial DNA or a protein in a brood chamber does not establish that it is alive, active, beneficial, or responsible for an [indirect genetic effect](../../../../indirect-genetic-effect.md).

First establish a common-garden breeding population with a recorded [pedigree](../../../../pedigree.md) and [genotype](../../../../genotype.md) markers. Use a [paternal half-sib design](../../../../paternal-half-sib-design.md) with several dams per sire and several adult daughter providers per dam, distributed among independently randomized rearing containers. Standardize donor age, mating history, developmental nutrition and adult diet, and block dung source, moisture and temperature. Because maternally inherited microbes can themselves produce family resemblance, rear replicate provider families with a standardized microbial inoculum in an additional common-rearing arm. Persistence of a family difference alone is insufficient evidence for host genetic causation.

For the initial [cross-fostering](../../../../cross-fostering.md) experiment, allow each provider to construct several [brood balls](../../../../brood-ball.md) on weighed, standardized dung. Measure construction time, final dry-mass estimate from matched material, egg-chamber properties and maternal [faecal pedestal](../../../../faecal-pedestal.md) quantity. Remove the original egg before hatching and replace it with an age-matched egg from a separately bred recipient family. Randomize recipient families across provider families, with reciprocal assignments and repeated providers; include handled own-egg and untouched controls. Record egg size and biological-mother identity so that egg provisioning and other pre-transfer [maternal effects](../../../../maternal-effect.md) are not silently attributed to the foster provider.

One possible balanced allocation has 20 sire families, two dams per sire and two daughter providers per dam, giving 80 providers. Four independently prepared [brood balls](../../../../brood-ball.md) per provider yield 320 offspring-rearing units, allocated across multiple recipient families and collection blocks. This is an illustrative feasible structure, not a claim of adequate power. The effective replication for a sire-level genetic component is much smaller than 320; repeated balls estimate provider consistency and within-provider variation. A pilot-based simulation of the planned [pedigree](../../../../pedigree.md), variance components, mortality and missing samples should determine the final scale before outcomes are examined.

Measure larval growth trajectories, survival, development time and adult size; subsequently measure reproductive performance where feasible. Score provisioning behaviour blind to family and recipient treatment. Collect matched donor and provision samples for absolute microbial load, community composition and soluble chemical profiles. Include extraction blanks, reagent controls and bacterial-load standards, especially because low-biomass sequence samples are vulnerable to contamination. Shared 16S sequences provide limited discrimination among strains; culture or higher-resolution sequencing can test whether a donor-associated strain actually reaches and persists in a recipient.

For an offspring $i$ reared in a ball prepared by provider $j$, an [animal model in quantitative genetics](../../../../animal-model-in-quantitative-genetics.md) could use:

$$
y_{ij}=\mu+a_i^{D}+a_j^{I}+c_j+b_{\mathrm{birth}(i)}+B_{ij}+\varepsilon_{ij}.
$$

Here $a_i^{D}$ is the [direct genetic effect](../../../../direct-genetic-effect.md) of the offspring, $a_j^{I}$ the additive provider contribution, $c_j$ a repeatable non-genetic provider effect, $b_{\mathrm{birth}(i)}$ a biological-mother effect, and $B_{ij}$ prespecified batch and rearing terms. Genetic effects use the [pedigree](../../../../pedigree.md) or genomic relationship matrix; provider identity alone cannot identify $a_j^{I}$. If the [pedigree](../../../../pedigree.md) contains informative relatives in both roles, direct–indirect [genetic covariance](../../../../genetic-covariance.md) can also be estimated. Otherwise that covariance is not identified by the first experiment and should not be assumed known. Crossed recipient and provider families distinguish a general provider effect from matching between a particular recipient [genotype](../../../../genotype.md) and provider background.

The second experiment tests the transferred pathway rather than merely adding a microbial measurement to that model. Construct uniform artificial [brood balls](../../../../brood-ball.md) with the same treated dung, food mass and chamber geometry, and transfer equal donor-equivalent pedestal material. Compare intact material, material separated into a cell-free soluble fraction, a preparation containing non-viable microbial cells, and a live-cell add-back or rescue preparation. Include material-free, medium and sham-processing controls, together with untreated natural balls as a benchmark. Test viability rather than inferring it from a sterilization label. All processed-dung groups must share the same processing, because sterilization can also change nutritional and chemical properties.

Within a subset, exchange washed live-cell fractions and soluble fractions independently between donor families. A crossed donor-cell-source by donor-soluble-source design asks whether offspring differences track microbial cells, soluble maternal products, or their interaction. Hold total food quantity and transfer volume constant. Include both a standardized live-cell dose, testing compositional differences, and a naturally varying dose arm, testing a possible maternal-genotype effect on delivered quantity. An equal-dose experiment alone could erase a real dose-mediated pathway. Test a candidate cultured community or identified molecule in a dose range anchored to measured natural transfers, rather than interpreting a large artificial supplement as evidence for normal function.

The predicted pattern is specific. If food mass explains the provider effect, equalizing mass should greatly reduce it. If viable maternal microbes mediate an additional effect, removing viability at equal food quantity should reduce the relevant donor-associated growth difference, and a compatible live add-back should restore it. An effect retained in a cell-free fraction instead motivates identifying soluble maternal or microbial products. A failed rescue can reflect processing damage or a missing microbial partner, rather than absence of any natural pathway. If all provider differences disappear after standardized rearing, a non-genetic maternal or microbial inheritance explanation becomes more plausible than the proposed host genetic effect.

Analyse the [cross-fostering](../../../../cross-fostering.md) data first for total provider genetic contributions, then analyse the randomized material manipulations for mechanism. Simply adjusting for a measured mediator can block part of the effect or introduce bias; [causal mediation](../../../../causal-mediation.md) needs assumptions and experimental support. Prespecify the primary outcomes, appropriate survival analysis, family and donor clustering, planned contrasts and treatment-by-recipient-background interactions. Offspring within a maternal [pedigree](../../../../pedigree.md) and repeated chemical measurements are not independent genetic replicates. Measure provider costs, such as future fecundity and provisioning effort, because faster-growing offspring alone do not establish the evolutionary advantage of the provisioning behaviour.

**The decisive result would connect provider [genotype](../../../../genotype.md) to a measured transfer difference and then experimentally connect that difference to recipient performance, independently of offspring [genotype](../../../../genotype.md) and food quantity.** Negative outcomes remain informative: maternal influence without additive provider variance is a [maternal effect](../../../../maternal-effect.md) without the demonstrated genetic component, while heritable provisioning effects without a material-mediated mechanism leave a genuine [indirect genetic effect](../../../../indirect-genetic-effect.md) whose pathway is still unresolved.
