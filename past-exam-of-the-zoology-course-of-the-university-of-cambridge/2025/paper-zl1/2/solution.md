**Repeated dietary adaptations reflect recurring mechanical and digestive demands, but a similar [diet](../../../../diet.md) need not produce identical [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md), and anatomical resemblance need not represent an independent evolutionary origin.** A useful comparison places each example on a [phylogenetic tree](../../../../phylogenetic-tree.md), identifies the inherited structure being modified, and separates food acquisition, oral processing and digestion.

Among dental adaptations, shearing blades recur in meat-eating [mammals](../../../../mammal.md). In [Carnivora](../../../../carnivora.md), a placental order within [Laurasiatheria](../../../../laurasiatheria.md), the main [carnassial](../../../../carnassial.md) pair comprises the upper fourth [premolar](../../../../premolar.md) and lower first [molar](../../../../molar.md). Their opposing blades slice flesh, while relative development of crushing surfaces varies with [diet](../../../../diet.md). Predatory [marsupials](../../../../marsupial.md), including the extinct [thylacine](../../../../thylacine.md) within [Dasyuromorphia](../../../../dasyuromorphia.md), also emphasize shearing, but do not reproduce precisely the same specialized [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) pair. The [jaw](../../../../jaw.md) and basic [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) classes are inherited mammalian structures; the highly blade-like feeding apparatus represents [convergent evolution](../../../../convergent-evolution.md) of performance in separate [therian](../../../../theria.md) branches. Within [Carnivora](../../../../carnivora.md), resemblance between close relatives may instead reflect shared ancestry, and specialized insect-eating or plant-eating members demonstrate that membership of the order does not establish a carnivorous [diet](../../../../diet.md).

Repeated elaboration of grinding surfaces provides a complementary case. [lophodonty](../../../../lophodonty.md) joins [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) cusps into ridges, and [selenodonty](../../../../selenodonty.md) produces crescentic ridges, maintaining effective cutting edges as the surface wears. Herbivorous [perissodactyls](../../../../perissodactyla.md), including [horses](../../../../horse.md), contrast with [ruminants](../../../../ruminant.md) such as bovids and cervids in [Artiodactyla](../../../../artiodactyla.md); both are within [Laurasiatheria](../../../../laurasiatheria.md). Complex grinding [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) also evolved in [proboscideans](../../../../proboscidea.md) within [Afrotheria](../../../../afrotheria.md) and in [macropods](../../../../macropod.md), a [marsupial](../../../../marsupial.md) lineage within [Diprotodontia](../../../../diprotodontia.md). These positions rule out treating every complex herbivore [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) as one recent innovation inherited from a common grazing ancestor. Three-dimensional measurements of occlusal surface complexity in [carnivorans](../../../../carnivora.md) and [rodents](../../../../rodentia.md) show that dietary demands can produce comparable patterns across very different [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) constructions. The useful prediction concerns functional surface geometry, not identical cusp names in every group. [Nature 445, pp. 78–81; three-dimensional occlusal complexity and diet](../../../../high-level-similarity-of-dentitions-in-carnivorans-and-rodents-by-alistair-r-evans-and-others.md)

Resistance to abrasion repeatedly modifies [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) growth as well as [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) shape. [hypsodonty](../../../../hypsodonty.md) supplies a tall reserve crown that is progressively exposed by wear; it occurs in several ungulate groups and in some [marsupials](../../../../marsupial.md). It differs from [hypselodonty](../../../../hypselodonty.md), in which [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) continue growing, as in rodent [incisors](../../../../incisor.md). Self-sharpening [incisors](../../../../incisor.md), with uneven distribution of [tooth enamel](../../../../tooth-enamel.md) and differential wear of dental tissues, facilitate gnawing. [Rodentia](../../../../rodentia.md) and [Lagomorpha](../../../../lagomorpha.md) are both within [Glires](../../../../glires.md), so their [incisor](../../../../incisor.md) similarities must be analysed against that relationship rather than automatically counted as two independent origins of the whole dental system.

A high crown is a wear-management adaptation, not an infallible grass detector. Abrasive particles can come from vegetation or ingested external dust and grit; food toughness and abrasiveness are also distinct. North American [horse](../../../../horse.md) [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) show substantial variation in [dental mesowear](../../../../dental-mesowear.md) within populations and temporal relationships between dietary abrasion and crown-height changes that are more complicated than an instantaneous switch from browsing to grazing. [Science 331, pp. 1178–1181; mesowear compared with crown-height evolution](../../../../dietary-change-and-evolution-of-horses-in-north-america-by-matthew-c-mihlbachler-and-others.md) A defensible palaeodiet reconstruction combines [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) form with [dental microwear](../../../../dental-microwear.md), [dental mesowear](../../../../dental-mesowear.md), [stable isotope analysis](../../../../stable-isotope-analysis.md) where suitable, and environmental evidence. [dental microwear](../../../../dental-microwear.md) emphasizes relatively recent meals, whereas crown form reflects inherited [morphology](../../../../morphology-biology.md) across generations. Disagreement can therefore be informative rather than an analytical failure.

A major non-dental recurrence is an enlarged gut compartment supporting microbial [fermentation](../../../../fermentation.md). [mammals](../../../../mammal.md) do not obtain the full energy value of structural plant carbohydrates through their own [enzymes](../../../../enzyme-split.md) alone; microbial partners release usable [fermentation](../../../../fermentation.md) products, notably [short-chain fatty acids](../../../../short-chain-fatty-acid.md). [foregut fermentation](../../../../foregut-fermentation.md) evolved in [ruminants](../../../../ruminant.md), [colobine monkeys](../../../../colobine-monkey.md) within [Primates](../../../../primates.md), and [macropods](../../../../macropod.md). The forestomach compartments are not identical organs merely because each houses [microbial digestion](../../../../microbial-digestion.md): the relevant independent changes concern enlargement, compartmentalization, retention and mixing in phylogenetically separated lineages. In [ruminants](../../../../ruminant.md), [rumination](../../../../rumination.md) additionally returns food for further [mastication](../../../../mastication.md), increasing the surface available to [microorganisms](../../../../microorganism.md).

[hindgut fermentation](../../../../hindgut-fermentation.md) provides another solution. [horses](../../../../horse.md), within [Perissodactyla](../../../../perissodactyla.md), use enlarged caecal and colonic compartments; [rabbits](../../../../rabbit.md), within [Lagomorpha](../../../../lagomorpha.md), combine caecal processing with [cecotrophy](../../../../cecotrophy.md), reingesting selected soft material and thereby recovering microbial nutrients through a second passage. [foregut fermentation](../../../../foregut-fermentation.md) allows microbial products and microbial biomass to encounter later digestive regions, whereas [hindgut fermentation](../../../../hindgut-fermentation.md) can accommodate high intake and rapid passage of some food fractions. Neither arrangement is universally superior: retention time, food quality, [body size](../../../../body-size.md), intake rate and the ability to select particles affect the balance. The comparative explanation of equid success therefore requires throughput as well as percentage extraction from a single meal. [Evolution 30, pp. 757–774; retention, throughput and digestive strategies](../../../../the-evolutionary-strategy-of-the-equidae-and-the-origins-of-rumen-and-cecal-digestion-by-christine-m-janis.md)

Food acquisition supplies a further non-dental example. [anteaters](../../../../anteater.md), within [Xenarthra](../../../../xenarthra.md), and [pangolins](../../../../pholidota.md), within [Pholidota](../../../../pholidota.md) in [Laurasiatheria](../../../../laurasiatheria.md), independently combine reduction or loss of [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) with an elongated, protrusible [tongue](../../../../tongue.md) and a feeding apparatus suited to taking small social insects. Powerful [forelimbs](../../../../forelimb.md) and [claws](../../../../claw.md) can open nests, but digging also serves many other functions; [claws](../../../../claw.md) alone do not identify ant feeding. Conversely, absence of grinding [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) need not signify nutritional simplicity: prey capture, protection from prey defences and [microbial digestion](../../../../microbial-digestion.md) can remain specialized.

Comparative dissection of primate digestive tracts supplies an independent anatomical test: gut compartments and their relative dimensions can be compared with [diet](../../../../diet.md) rather than guessed from the [teeth](../../../../tooth.md). Such comparisons must account for [body size](../../../../body-size.md) and the non-independence of related taxa. [Journal of Morphology 166, pp. 337–386; comparative gut measurements and dietary categories](../../../../morphology-of-the-gastrointestinal-tract-in-primates-comparisons-with-other-mammals-in-relation-to-diet-by-david-j-chivers-and-others.md) Testing these interpretations requires more than a gallery of look-alikes. Record [diet](../../../../diet.md) in wild animals, compare anatomical performance under relevant loads, measure gut retention or digestive products, and include relatives with different [diets](../../../../diet.md). Control for [body size](../../../../body-size.md), age, sex, [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) wear stage and shared ancestry, since ten related grazers are not ten independent evolutionary experiments. For [fossils](../../../../fossil.md), preserve the distinction between direct anatomical observation and an inferred gut or behaviour. **The repeated solutions are cutting or grinding surfaces, mechanisms that sustain those surfaces under wear, specialized acquisition structures and microbial digestive chambers; their phylogenetic distribution reveals both convergence and inherited constraints.**
