<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**Living [great apes](../../../../great-ape.md) are indispensable comparative evidence, but none is a preserved version of the Pan–Homo last common ancestor.** The ancestor was a population with its own combination of traits, and every living descendant has subsequently evolved. A defensible reconstruction is therefore a set of trait-specific hypotheses constrained by [phylogenetics](../../../../phylogenetics.md), functional comparison and [fossils](../../../../fossil.md).

The comparison starts with relationships. [Homo](../../../../homo.md) and [Pan](../../../../pan.md) are closest living sister lineages; [Pan](../../../../pan.md) contains [chimpanzees](../../../../chimpanzee.md) and [bonobos](../../../../bonobo.md). [Gorilla](../../../../gorilla.md) provides a more distant comparison, and [Pongo](../../../../pongo.md) a more distant one again. Humans are themselves [great apes](../../../../great-ape.md), rather than a group standing outside them. A character shared by [Pan](../../../../pan.md) and [Homo](../../../../homo.md) may have been inherited from their [last common ancestor](../../../../last-common-ancestor.md), but it can also have changed in either lineage or arisen in parallel. A shared feature of [Pan](../../../../pan.md) and [Gorilla](../../../../gorilla.md) does not automatically reconstruct the ancestor either: it may be ancestral, convergent, or retained in some branches and lost in others.

Living [great apes](../../../../great-ape.md) are especially valuable because their soft tissues, growth, behaviour and mechanical performance can be studied. Muscle paths, joint motion and hand loading can make competing functional interpretations of a [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) [wrist](../../../../wrist.md) testable. Observing climbing and terrestrial movement establishes which combinations are mechanically possible; it also exposes the inadequacy of labelling a whole animal simply “arboreal” or “terrestrial”. Appropriate controls compare individuals of different ages and sexes, adjust for [body size](../../../../body-size.md), record substrate and loading, and distinguish performance in captivity from behaviour in the wild. A surface that resembles another surface is weak functional evidence unless its position in the complete joint and the range of loads are considered.

The same accessibility creates temptations. [chimpanzee](../../../../chimpanzee.md) locomotion, male coalitions or territorial behaviour cannot be transferred wholesale to the common ancestor. [bonobos](../../../../bonobo.md) demonstrate variation within [Pan](../../../../pan.md), not a second unchanged ancestral template. Ecology, group composition, developmental plasticity and histories of selection differ among living populations. Current behaviour can generate hypotheses about feasible ancestral social systems, but proving that a particular ancestral behaviour existed requires additional evidence; most social behaviours do not fossilize. Treating the contrast as a choice between a uniformly aggressive [chimpanzee](../../../../chimpanzee.md) ancestor and a uniformly peaceful [bonobo](../../../../bonobo.md) ancestor replaces biology with caricature.

[knuckle-walking](../../../../knuckle-walking.md) illustrates the genuine scientific alternatives. One reconstruction takes the shared use of knuckle support in [Pan](../../../../pan.md) and [Gorilla](../../../../gorilla.md) as evidence for an ancestral condition, with later loss or modification in the [hominin](../../../../hominin.md) lineage. [wrist](../../../../wrist.md) features in early [hominins](../../../../hominin.md) have been interpreted as retained support for that hypothesis. [Nature 404, pp. 382–385; wrist characters interpreted as retained knuckle-walking adaptations](../../../../evidence-that-humans-evolved-from-a-knuckle-walking-ancestor-by-brian-g-richmond-and-others.md) A competing study emphasized differences in the development and functional organization of [chimpanzee](../../../../chimpanzee.md) and [Gorilla](../../../../gorilla.md) [wrists](../../../../wrist.md), consistent with independent origins of their knuckle-walking systems. [PNAS 106, pp. 14241–14246; contrasts between chimpanzee and gorilla wrist development and function](../../../../independent-evolution-of-knuckle-walking-in-african-apes-shows-that-humans-did-not-evolve-from-a-knuckle-walking-ancestor-by-tracy-l-kivell-and-others.md) Similar external posture can therefore conceal distinct load-bearing arrangements. Neither simply counting two living knuckle-walking genera nor quoting the conclusion of one comparative study settles the ancestral condition. The inference depends on [biological homology](../../../../biological-homology.md), functional interpretation and inclusion of relevant extinct forms.

[Ardipithecus ramidus](../../../../ardipithecus-ramidus.md), approximately 4.4 Ma, is particularly important because its skeleton combines a grasping great toe and arboreal capabilities with [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md) interpreted as supporting terrestrial [bipedalism](../../../../bipedalism.md). The authors argued that its hands and feet did not represent an unchanged modern [chimpanzee](../../../../chimpanzee.md) locomotor package. [Science 326, pp. 75–86; 4.4 Ma skeleton and locomotor mosaic](../../../../ardipithecus-ramidus-and-the-paleobiology-of-early-hominids-by-tim-d-white-and-others.md) This weakens a reconstruction that derives early [hominins](../../../../hominin.md) by merely standing a modern [chimpanzee](../../../../chimpanzee.md) upright. However, [Ardipithecus](../../../../ardipithecus.md) lived after the inferred [Pan](../../../../pan.md)–[Homo](../../../../homo.md) divergence; its features may include specializations of its own. Its proposed [hominin](../../../../hominin.md) position and functional reconstruction must also be evaluated against incomplete preservation and distortion, especially in the [pelvis](../../../../pelvis.md). It is evidence about a nearby branch, not direct observation of the common ancestor.

Older [Miocene](../../../../miocene.md) [apes](../../../../ape.md) extend the comparison beyond surviving lineages. [Pierolapithecus](../../../../pierolapithecus.md), approximately 12.5–13 Ma, combines an upright-trunk construction ([orthogrady](../../../../orthogrady.md)) with hand [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md) lacking the complete suspensory specialization of some living [apes](../../../../ape.md). [Science 306, pp. 1339–1344; orthograde trunk and contrasting hand morphology](../../../../pierolapithecus-catalaunicus-a-new-middle-miocene-great-ape-from-spain-by-salvador-moya-sola-and-others.md) The example shows that trunk posture, climbing and [suspensory locomotion](../../../../suspensory-locomotion.md) can evolve as a mosaic. Its exact position is debated, and it is not thereby identified as the [Pan](../../../../pan.md)–[Homo](../../../../homo.md) ancestor. Rather, extinct combinations expand the set of plausible ancestral forms and can reveal that a feature widespread among living [apes](../../../../ape.md) was assembled in stages.

Genetic evidence establishes relationships and helps constrain timing, but a molecular divergence estimate is not a [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) anatomical reconstruction. Estimates are sensitive to [mutation rate](../../../../mutation-rate.md), calibration and [generation interval](../../../../generation-interval.md). Pedigree observations of wild [chimpanzees](../../../../chimpanzee.md) and [Gorilla](../../../../gorilla.md) demonstrate why substituting one universal generation length can shift the inferred dates of great-ape divergences. [PNAS 109, pp. 15716–15721; pedigrees, generation intervals and divergence-time sensitivity](../../../../generation-times-in-wild-chimpanzees-and-gorillas-suggest-earlier-divergence-times-in-great-ape-and-human-evolution-by-kevin-e-langergraber-and-others.md) A [gene tree](../../../../gene-tree.md) can also coalesce before a population split, and [incomplete lineage sorting](../../../../incomplete-lineage-sorting.md) means that not every genomic region follows the species branching order. There is consequently no reason to force every candidate [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) into an overly precise split date selected from one molecular estimate.

The best procedure is to specify competing ancestral states and their predictions: for example, whether a particular [wrist](../../../../wrist.md) character should accompany habitual knuckle support, or whether an inferred climbing apparatus requires suspensory finger proportions. Score those characters across [Pan](../../../../pan.md), [Homo](../../../../homo.md), [Gorilla](../../../../gorilla.md), [Pongo](../../../../pongo.md) and well-preserved [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) taxa; examine sensitivity to uncertain character coding and alternative [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) placements. Related features must not be counted as a large number of independent confirmations when they are mechanically or developmentally coupled. Extant-only [ancestral state reconstruction](../../../../ancestral-state-reconstruction.md) is especially vulnerable to the [extinction](../../../../extinction.md) of lineages that possessed combinations absent today.

**Living [great apes](../../../../great-ape.md) supply the comparative mechanics, development and diversity needed to interpret [fossils](../../../../fossil.md); [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) prevent the living sample from dictating a false ancestral stereotype.** A cautious composite reconstruction, with separate uncertainty for posture, hand use, [diet](../../../../diet.md) and behaviour, is more defensible than choosing one living species as the ancestor's complete anatomical and social model.
