**Southern [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) have changed the geography, timing and anatomical character of mammaliaform evolution, while exposing how strongly a northern [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) record had shaped earlier expectations.** Their importance is not that every southern lineage arose in the south. A dated occurrence establishes presence and a minimum age; an evolutionary origin requires an argument from [phylogenetics](../../../../phylogenetics.md) and [biogeography](../../../../biogeography.md). Nor do modern continental outlines reproduce the connections of [Gondwana](../../../../gondwana.md).

A particularly consequential example is [Ambondro mahabo](../../../../ambondro-mahabo.md), represented by a [jaw](../../../../jaw.md) fragment with lower [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) from Middle [Jurassic](../../../../jurassic.md) Madagascar, approximately $167\pm2$ Ma. The [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) possess a basined heel associated with the crushing component of [tribosphenic molars](../../../../tribospheny.md), which combine shearing and crushing in a single occlusal system. Its age extended the then-known record of this dental construction substantially backwards and challenged a simple northern origin inferred from better-sampled northern localities. The material nevertheless consists of a very restricted part of the animal, and the matching upper [dentition](../../../../dentition.md) was not directly observed. Assigning the entire skeleton, physiology or reproductive mode from these [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) would go beyond the evidence. [Nature 401, pp. 57–60; Ambondro lower jaw, approximately 167 ± 2 million years old](../../../../a-middle-jurassic-mammal-from-madagascar-by-john-j-flynn-and-others.md)

The discovery also changed the character of the phylogenetic question. One hypothesis groups several southern forms in [Australosphenida](../../../../australosphenida.md), associated with the ancestry of [monotremes](../../../../monotreme.md), and proposes that a tribosphenic-like construction arose independently of northern [Boreosphenida](../../../../boreosphenida.md), including [therians](../../../../theria.md). In that interpretation a highly functional [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) arrangement is [convergent evolution](../../../../convergent-evolution.md), not a unique certificate of therian ancestry. A competing analysis supports a single origin of [tribospheny](../../../../tribospheny.md) and rearranges the relationships of the relevant southern taxa. [Nature 409, pp. 53–57; alternative placements of southern tribosphenic mammals](../../../../dual-origin-of-tribosphenic-mammals-by-zhe-xi-luo-and-others.md) [phylogenetic analysis supporting a single origin of tribospheny](../../../../the-evolution-of-tribospheny-and-the-antiquity-of-mammalian-clades-by-michael-o-woodburne-and-others.md) The important lesson is not to declare one historical topology established by the striking [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) alone. The choice depends on which cusps and basins are judged instances of [biological homology](../../../../biological-homology.md), character coding, inclusion of other anatomical regions, and the taxon sample. Nor does a Middle [Jurassic](../../../../jurassic.md) tribosphenic [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) by itself date the split between living [marsupials](../../../../marsupial.md) and [placental mammals](../../../../placental-mammal.md).

South American [monotreme](../../../../monotreme.md) material further undermined the equation of present distribution with evolutionary birthplace. Early [Paleocene](../../../../paleocene.md) [Monotrematum](../../../../monotrematum.md), approximately 60–65 Ma, has [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) with affinities to the [platypus](../../../../platypus.md) lineage. Living [monotremes](../../../../monotreme.md) are restricted to Australasia, but that restriction cannot be projected unchanged into the past. [Nature 356, pp. 704–706; Paleocene Monotrematum teeth](../../../../first-discovery-of-monotremes-in-south-america-by-rosendo-pascual-and-others.md) South America, Antarctica and Australia offered changing opportunities for connection, fragmentation and movement. A South American occurrence is compatible with a geographically extensive southern history; it does not uniquely establish the direction of dispersal or prove that [Monotrematum](../../../../monotrematum.md) was the direct ancestor of the living [platypus](../../../../platypus.md). The distinction between a [crown group](../../../../crown-group.md) and related members of its [stem group](../../../../stem-group.md) is essential when attaching such [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) to divergence-time estimates.

Latest [Cretaceous](../../../../cretaceous.md) Madagascar has contributed more than isolated [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) surprises. The cranium of [Vintana](../../../../vintana.md), around 70 Ma, supplied extensive new characters for the formerly tooth-dominated [Gondwanatheria](../../../../gondwanatheria.md). Its robust feeding apparatus and unusual cranial combination permit tests of relationships and ecology that were unavailable from a few [teeth](../../../../tooth.md). Yet a mosaic of familiar and unusual features is precisely the situation in which a single apparently diagnostic character can mislead. The placement of [gondwanatherians](../../../../gondwanatheria.md) therefore needs broad anatomical and taxonomic comparison, including other extinct groups, rather than assignment to a living order by superficial resemblance. [Nature 515, pp. 512–517; Vintana cranium and phylogenetic character sampling](../../../../first-cranial-remains-of-a-gondwanatherian-mammal-reveal-remarkable-mosaicism-by-david-w-krause-and-others.md)

The approximately 66 Ma skeleton of [Adalatherium](../../../../adalatherium.md) expanded that evidence into the postcranial skeleton. It documents a relatively large, several-kilogram non-therian mammal with an unusual combination of cranial, dental, vertebral and limb features. This contradicts the caricature that all [Mesozoic](../../../../mesozoic.md) [mammaliaforms](../../../../mammaliaform.md) were anatomically interchangeable tiny insect-eaters. Madagascar's long isolation provides a plausible context for its evolutionary distinctiveness, but the claim that isolation caused a particular feature remains an explanatory hypothesis. One needs comparative island and continental samples to distinguish an island effect from inherited lineage-specific [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md). [Nature 581, pp. 421–427; Adalatherium skeleton and insularity hypothesis](../../../../skeleton-of-a-cretaceous-mammal-from-madagascar-reflects-long-term-insularity-by-david-w-krause-and-others.md)

Australia's early [Eocene](../../../../eocene.md) [Djarthia](../../../../djarthia.md), approximately 55 Ma, addresses a different issue: the relationships and movement of [marsupials](../../../../marsupial.md). Its ankle material supports a position close to the base of [Australidelphia](../../../../australidelphia.md), a group containing the living Australian orders and the South American [Microbiotheria](../../../../microbiotheria.md). This is stronger evidence than assuming that every small Australian [jaw](../../../../jaw.md) belongs inside a modern Australian family. [PLoS ONE 3, e1858; Djarthia tarsals and australidelphian relationships](../../../../australia-s-oldest-marsupial-fossils-and-their-biogeographical-implications-by-robin-m-d-beck-and-others.md) It is compatible with a history involving South America and Antarctica, but a branching order is not a map of a single migration. An extinct distribution can be extensive, intermediate populations can disappear, and the oldest sampled [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) need not occur near the place where a lineage originated. Also, the older and more inclusive [Metatheria](../../../../metatheria.md) must not be confused with the living marsupial [crown group](../../../../crown-group.md).

Together these discoveries change how large-scale history is tested. First, expanding southern sampling can overturn a supposed absence that arose from collecting intensity, exposure of suitable rocks or preservation. [taphonomy](../../../../taphonomy.md) and the concentration of many early records on durable [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) must be modelled before comparing continental diversity. Second, new skeletons can change the inferred [phylogenetic tree](../../../../phylogenetic-tree.md), and therefore whether a [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) adaptation counts as one origin or several. Third, [fossil calibration](../../../../fossil-calibration.md) constrains the minimum age of a lineage, with explicit uncertainty in both geological age and phylogenetic assignment. Fourth, [continental drift](../../../../continental-drift.md) changes which dispersal routes were physically plausible. Shared distributions across former [Gondwana](../../../../gondwana.md) may reflect [vicariance](../../../../vicariance.md), later dispersal, or a mixture; the geological separation must be compared with a defensible lineage age rather than used as its automatic explanation.

From the [Jurassic](../../../../jurassic.md) through the [Palaeogene](../../../../palaeogene.md), these independent discoveries have cumulatively changed both the anatomical evidence and its interpretation. **They reveal a more diverse southern mammaliaform history and force explicit tests of convergence, missing data and geographic sampling, rather than replacing an assumed northern centre with an equally assumed southern centre.**
