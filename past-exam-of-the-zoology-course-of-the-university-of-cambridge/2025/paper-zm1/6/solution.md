<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**The [turtle problem](../../../../turtle-problem.md) is the difficulty of placing [turtles](../../../../turtle.md) on the reptile [phylogenetic tree](../../../../phylogenetic-tree.md), historically intensified by conflict between their unusual [skulls](../../../../skull.md) and other anatomical or molecular evidence.** Their closed or extensively roofed temporal region once appeared to identify a primitively [anapsid](../../../../anapsid.md) lineage outside [Diapsida](../../../../diapsida.md). Evidence instead places living [turtles](../../../../turtle.md) within the [diapsid](../../../../diapsida.md) radiation, commonly as the sister lineage to [archosaurs](../../../../archosauria.md). The inference requires distinguishing retention of an ancestral state from secondary modification.

The traditional scheme counts openings behind the [orbit (anatomy)](../../../../orbit-anatomy.md) on each side of the [skull](../../../../skull.md). An [anapsid](../../../../anapsid.md) condition lacks the characteristic temporal openings; a [synapsid](../../../../synapsid.md) condition has one, classically lower; a [diapsid](../../../../diapsida.md) condition has upper and lower openings separated by bony structures. These descriptions originally provided convenient anatomical categories. The [eye](../../../../eye.md) socket is not a temporal opening, and a notch open to a [skull](../../../../skull.md) margin, or [temporal emargination](../../../../temporal-emargination.md), must not automatically be equated with a completely bounded [temporal fenestra](../../../../temporal-fenestra.md). [bone](../../../../bone.md) identities and contacts matter as much as the count.

On a literal state-based scheme, a roofed [turtle](../../../../turtle.md) [skull](../../../../skull.md) resembles the inferred primitive [amniote](../../../../amniote.md) condition. That resemblance helped motivate positions outside the main [diapsid](../../../../diapsida.md) groups and comparisons with extinct anapsid-grade reptiles. Other anatomical analyses allied [turtles](../../../../turtle.md) with different reptile branches. Their shell and strongly modified [skull](../../../../skull.md) make character comparison difficult: several apparently correlated characters may belong to one functional transformation, and absence of an opening can conceal a more complex history. A shared absence is especially weak evidence when the opening can be closed independently.

Molecular evidence provides an independent character system. [Nicholas G. Crawford](../../../../nicholas-g-crawford.md) and colleagues sampled more than a thousand [ultraconserved elements](../../../../ultraconserved-element.md) with associated variable sequence, using representatives of major living reptile lineages, including the [tuatara](../../../../tuatara.md). Their analyses supported [turtles](../../../../turtle.md) as the sister group of [archosaurs](../../../../archosauria.md), rather than the sister group of all other living reptiles or a lineage within [lepidosaurs](../../../../lepidosaur.md).[Nuclear-locus sampling, inclusion of tuatara and inferred turtle–archosaur relationship](../../../../more-than-1000-ultraconserved-elements-provide-evidence-that-turtles-are-the-sister-group-of-archosaurs-by-nicholas-g-crawford-and-others.md) In this hypothesis, [crocodilians](../../../../crocodilian.md) and [birds](../../../../bird.md) are closer to one another than either is to [turtles](../../../../turtle.md). The schematic in solution 4 shows that distinction: “sister to [archosaurs](../../../../archosauria.md)” does not mean “a living [archosaur](../../../../archosauria.md)”.

A large number of aligned bases is not a substitute for checking the analysis. Appropriate outgroups, broad taxon sampling, correct [orthology](../../../../orthology.md) and models that address rate or composition differences are needed. Analyses across loci and model choices test sensitivity; [gene](../../../../gene.md) histories may also differ from the [species tree](../../../../species-tree.md). Molecular data from living [species](../../../../species.md) cannot directly locate every extinct turtle-like animal. They provide a living backbone against which the anatomical placement of [fossils](../../../../fossil.md) can be tested.

Stem-turtle discoveries provide a particularly discriminating anatomical test. [Pappochelys](../../../../pappochelys.md), a Middle [Triassic](../../../../triassic.md) animal described by [Rainer R. Schoch](../../../../rainer-r-schoch.md) and [Hans-Dieter Sues](../../../../hans-dieter-sues.md), combines broadened ribs and robust ventral elements relevant to shell origins with temporal [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md) retaining a small upper opening and a ventrally open lower region. This supports a [diapsid](../../../../diapsida.md) ancestry even though the animal lacks a complete modern shell.[Skull openings, ribs, gastralia and phylogenetic interpretation of Pappochelys](../../../../a-middle-triassic-stem-turtle-and-the-evolution-of-the-turtle-body-plan-by-rainer-r-schoch-and-hans-dieter-sues.md) Its significance is the associated character combination, not merely a hole selected because it agrees with a molecular tree. It shows that the shell and temporal roof need not have evolved as one indivisible package.

A study using [computed tomography](../../../../computed-tomography.md) of [Eunotosaurus](../../../../eunotosaurus.md) supplied another proposed part of the transition, interpreting hidden or ontogenetically altered temporal [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md) as evidence of [diapsid](../../../../diapsida.md) ancestry.[Computed-tomographic anatomy, ontogenetic interpretation and placement of Eunotosaurus](../../../../evolutionary-origin-of-the-turtle-skull-by-g-s-bever-and-others.md) The proposed placement of such early forms and the [biological homology](../../../../biological-homology.md) of particular openings remain matters for character analysis. One should not claim that every specimen retains an unambiguous pair of textbook [diapsid](../../../../diapsida.md) holes, or that a single [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) resolves all deeper reptile relationships. Comparing growth stages, multiple specimens and independent postcranial characters is necessary to separate developmental closure from damage, preservation and mistaken [bone](../../../../bone.md) identification.

The general limitation is [homoplasy](../../../../homoplasy.md). Openings can expand, merge with a margin, lose separating bars or become secondarily roofed; apparently similar counts can consequently have different histories. A derived closed roof cannot be classified reliably as primitively [anapsid](../../../../anapsid.md) without a tree and additional characters. Conversely, changing the temporal condition does not remove a descendant from [Diapsida](../../../../diapsida.md). The same principle applies to [synapsids](../../../../synapsid.md): [mammals](../../../../mammal.md) remain members of that lineage despite extensive transformation of their [skull](../../../../skull.md) architecture. An anatomical grade is not automatically a [clade](../../../../clade.md).

Even perfect recognition of a [diapsid](../../../../diapsida.md) origin would not, by itself, decide whether [turtles](../../../../turtle.md) are nearest to [lepidosaurs](../../../../lepidosaur.md) or [archosaurs](../../../../archosauria.md), since both are [diapsid](../../../../diapsida.md) lineages. Resolving that question requires informative derived characters within the radiation, rather than recounting the ancestral pair of openings. Temporal architecture remains valuable when scored through explicit [bone](../../../../bone.md) relationships and combined with other evidence; it fails when used as a stand-alone ancestry detector.

**[turtles](../../../../turtle.md) demonstrate that temporal fenestration is an evolving character complex, not an immutable classification key.** An ancestry-based classification can recognize [turtles](../../../../turtle.md) as modified [diapsids](../../../../diapsida.md) while explaining their [skull](../../../../skull.md) roof as a derived transformation. The strongest resolution brings molecular comparisons, [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md) and developmental change into agreement without assuming that any one evidence source is infallible.
