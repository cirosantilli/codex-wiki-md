**The transition places [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) origins within a prolonged assembly of animal body plans, ecological interactions and preservational opportunities.** It does not identify one boundary at which a complete modern [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) suddenly appeared. Three questions must be separated: when the relevant lineages diverged, when diagnostic features evolved, and when those features became recoverable as [fossils](../../../../fossil.md).

The [Ediacaran](../../../../ediacaran.md) precedes the [Cambrian](../../../../cambrian.md), whose beginning is approximately 539 million years ago. [Ediacaran](../../../../ediacaran.md) communities already contained macroscopic organisms and evidence of animal movement and feeding. [Kimberella](../../../../kimberella.md), for example, has been interpreted from its body organization as a mollusc-like [bilaterian](../../../../bilaterian.md); its relevance is that substantial animal differentiation predates the boundary, rather than that it is a [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) ancestor.[Body reconstruction and bilaterian interpretation](../../../../the-late-precambrian-fossil-kimberella-is-a-mollusc-like-bilaterian-organism-by-mikhail-a-fedonkin-and-benjamin-m-waggoner.md) [trace fossils](../../../../trace-fossil.md) record activities that may otherwise leave no body remains. Increasingly elaborate movement and sediment disturbance reveal ecological capacities, but an isolated burrow does not identify a [notochord](../../../../notochord.md), a head or a particular branch of the animal [phylogenetic tree](../../../../phylogenetic-tree.md).

Across the transition, animal skeletons, [predation](../../../../predation.md), burrowing and water-column communities became more prominent. Their appearance changes both biology and observation. Resistant skeletons improve preservation, while [bioturbation](../../../../bioturbation.md) can destroy the sedimentary surfaces that had preserved some earlier soft-bodied communities. Different preservational windows reveal different subsets of life. An apparent replacement in the [fossil record](../../../../fossil-record.md) can therefore combine real ecological turnover with [taphonomic bias](../../../../taphonomic-bias.md). One should compare [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md) and environments across several deposits rather than treating all [Ediacaran](../../../../ediacaran.md) absences as biological absences.

The [Cambrian](../../../../cambrian.md) exceptional deposits provide positive evidence. [Haikouichthys](../../../../haikouichthys.md) and [Myllokunmingia](../../../../myllokunmingia.md) from [Chengjiang](../../../../chengjiang.md) reveal small fish-like animals with combinations of axial, muscular and branchial structures interpreted as early [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) organization.[Descriptions and reconstruction of the two Chengjiang taxa](../../../../lower-cambrian-vertebrates-from-south-china-by-d-g-shu-and-others.md) [Metaspriggina](../../../../metaspriggina.md) from the [Burgess Shale](../../../../burgess-shale.md) adds evidence from a distinct assemblage, including prominent [eyes](../../../../eye.md), segmented musculature, organized into [myomeres](../../../../myomere.md), and serial branchial supports.[Anatomical description and reconstruction of Metaspriggina](../../../../a-primitive-fish-from-the-cambrian-of-north-america-by-simon-conway-morris-and-jean-bernard-caron.md) These are more informative than an undifferentiated claim that “fish existed”: they allow particular characters to be mapped onto alternative [phylogenetic trees](../../../../phylogenetic-tree.md).

A plausible sequence involves a swimming [chordate](../../../../chordate.md) organization with a [notochord](../../../../notochord.md) and segmental musculature, followed by elaboration of anterior sensory and feeding systems along the [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) lineage. Nevertheless, [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) compressions can obscure boundaries or superimpose unrelated structures. Confidence should come from repeated specimens, bilateral agreement, positional relationships and combinations of characters, rather than resemblance of one dark patch to an organ. A structure absent from a compressed specimen may be unpreserved. Nor does a plausible early [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) have to be a member of the living [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) [crown group](../../../../crown-group.md), still less a direct ancestor of a living [species](../../../../species.md). A [stem group](../../../../stem-group.md) can contain extinct side branches showing different combinations of ancestral and derived states.

Living organisms constrain the reconstruction without forming a ladder. Molecular analyses place [tunicates](../../../../tunicate.md), rather than [cephalochordates](../../../../cephalochordate.md), as the closest living relatives of [vertebrates](../../../../vertebrate.md).[Phylogenomic comparison of chordate relationships](../../../../tunicates-and-not-cephalochordates-are-the-closest-living-relatives-of-vertebrates-by-frederic-delsuc-and-others.md) A modern lancelet is consequently a comparative relative with its own history, not an unchanged stage through which [vertebrates](../../../../vertebrate.md) passed. The same warning applies to living [hagfish](../../../../hagfish.md) and [lampreys](../../../../lamprey.md). Their [anatomy](../../../../anatomy.md) can reveal inherited features and secondary specializations; simplicity alone cannot polarize a character.

The [New Head hypothesis](../../../../new-head-hypothesis.md) connects cranial sensory and feeding innovations with [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md) and [ectodermal placodes](../../../../ectodermal-placode.md). This is a developmental explanation for assembling a more elaborate head. Experiments on living [chordates](../../../../chordate.md) can test where cells originate, which structures they form and which regulatory interactions are needed. The [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) presence of [eyes](../../../../eye.md) or branchial supports cannot directly demonstrate the embryonic migration of [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md). Conversely, a conserved regulatory [gene](../../../../gene.md) alone does not establish that a complete modern head existed in an [Ediacaran](../../../../ediacaran.md) ancestor. Developmental, molecular and anatomical evidence address complementary parts of the argument.[Proposed developmental basis and functional significance of head innovation](../../../../neural-crest-and-the-origin-of-vertebrates-a-new-head-by-carl-gans-and-r-glenn-northcutt.md)

Ecological context helps explain why these capacities might diversify. More active swimming, prey detection and food processing can create opportunities and selective pressures for improved sensory and locomotor coordination. [molecular oxygen](../../../../molecular-oxygen.md) availability constrains the metabolic activities that can be sustained. [Erik A. Sperling](../../../../erik-a-sperling.md) and colleagues compared modern low-oxygen animal communities and related the scarcity of carnivory at low oxygen levels to hypotheses about [Cambrian](../../../../cambrian.md) ecological expansion.[Modern oxygen-gradient observations and the proposed ecological connection](../../../../oxygen-ecology-and-the-cambrian-radiation-of-animals-by-erik-a-sperling-and-others.md) This motivates a mechanism; environmental correlation alone neither dates the [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) ancestor nor establishes oxygenation as a sufficient single cause.

**The useful conclusion is a constrained historical sequence, with explicit gaps.** [Ediacaran](../../../../ediacaran.md) evidence establishes antecedent animal complexity; [Cambrian](../../../../cambrian.md) exceptional preservation documents recognizable early [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) combinations; comparative [phylogenetics](../../../../phylogenetics.md) and [evolutionary developmental biology](../../../../evolutionary-developmental-biology.md) test their placement and assembly. [molecular clocks](../../../../molecular-clock.md) can estimate earlier divergences, but their dependence on rates and [fossil calibration](../../../../fossil-calibration.md) prevents them from turning every absence into a precise origin date.
