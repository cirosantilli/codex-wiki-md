<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**[Sarcopterygii](../../../../sarcopterygii.md) is the [clade](../../../../clade.md) comprising the most recent common ancestor of living [coelacanths](../../../../coelacanth.md), [lungfish](../../../../lungfish.md) and [tetrapods](../../../../tetrapod.md), together with all its descendants.** In this crown-based usage, [humans](../../../../human.md) are [sarcopterygians](../../../../sarcopterygii.md): acquisition of [limbs](../../../../vertebrate-limb.md) and loss of a fish-like outline do not remove descendants from a [clade](../../../../clade.md). Extinct taxa assigned along the stem require an explicitly broader total-group usage. A diagnosis lists characters useful for recognizing a group; a [synapomorphy](../../../../synapomorphy.md) is a shared derived character whose position has been inferred on a [phylogenetic tree](../../../../phylogenetic-tree.md). Those are related but not interchangeable statements.

Two particularly instructive traditional diagnostic features are the fleshy, internally supported [paired appendages](../../../../paired-appendage.md) and true [enamel](../../../../enamel.md) associated with the dentition. The first survives conspicuously in our arms and legs. The second survives in our [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) crowns, although modern evidence requires qualification of its traditional status as an exclusively [sarcopterygian](../../../../sarcopterygii.md) innovation.

**The paired-appendage transformation.** A lobed [fin](../../../../fin.md) contains musculature and a substantial [endoskeleton](../../../../endoskeleton.md) extending into the appendage. In the relevant ancestral pattern, a single proximal skeletal element connects to the girdle: the [humerus](../../../../humerus.md) in the pectoral appendage, and the [femur](../../../../femur.md) in the pelvic appendage. Subsequent elements elaborate the jointed internal axis. The important comparison is historical organization and articulations, not merely that both a [fin](../../../../fin.md) and an arm contain [bone](../../../../bone.md). [fins](../../../../fin.md) in other lineages also possess internal supporting elements, so the presence of any [fin](../../../../fin.md) skeleton is too broad a diagnosis.

Within the lineage leading to [tetrapods](../../../../tetrapod.md), the pectoral series can be recognized as [humerus](../../../../humerus.md), [radius bone](../../../../radius-bone.md) and [ulna](../../../../ulna.md), followed by increasingly elaborate distal elements. [Tiktaalik](../../../../tiktaalik.md) retains [fin rays](../../../../fin-ray.md) but has a distal jointed skeleton capable of limb-like positioning against a substrate. Its combination of characters shows that parts of a wrist-like support system preceded a fully digit-bearing hand. Comparing several articulated specimens and the geometry of joint surfaces constrains the reconstruction better than matching isolated [bones](../../../../bone.md) by outline.[Pectoral anatomy, joint configurations and proposed substrate-supported postures](../../../../the-pectoral-fin-of-tiktaalik-roseae-and-the-origin-of-the-tetrapod-limb-by-neil-h-shubin-and-others.md)

Further changes included [anatomical digits](../../../../anatomical-digit.md), loss of external [fin rays](../../../../fin-ray.md) in the [limb](../../../../vertebrate-limb.md), changes to the girdles and altered force transmission through the trunk. They did not all arise simultaneously. Early digit-bearing [tetrapods](../../../../tetrapod.md) were not uniformly five-fingered terrestrial walkers; aquatic settings and [polydactyly](../../../../polydactyly.md) make it misleading to present the modern [human](../../../../human.md) hand as the immediate purpose or original endpoint of the transition. [Acanthostega](../../../../acanthostega.md), with eight [anatomical digits](../../../../anatomical-digit.md) in its [forelimb](../../../../forelimb.md), supplies a concrete counterexample to an ancestrally universal five-digit condition.[Digit counts and implications for the ancestral tetrapod limb](../../../../polydactyly-in-the-earliest-known-tetrapod-limbs-by-michael-i-coates-and-jennifer-a-clack.md)

Our own upper [limb](../../../../vertebrate-limb.md) retains the proximal single-bone and paired-bone organization, but the [shoulder](../../../../shoulder.md), [elbow](../../../../elbow.md), [wrist](../../../../wrist.md) and [fingers](../../../../finger.md) are extensively specialized. Rotation of the [radius bone](../../../../radius-bone.md) relative to the [ulna](../../../../ulna.md) contributes to turning the forearm; an opposable [thumb](../../../../thumb.md) and independently controlled [fingers](../../../../finger.md) support grasping. In the lower [limb](../../../../vertebrate-limb.md), the [femur](../../../../femur.md), [tibia](../../../../tibia.md) and [fibula](../../../../fibula.md) show the corresponding serial organization, while the [pelvis](../../../../pelvis.md), [hip](../../../../hip.md), [knee](../../../../knee.md) and [foot](../../../../foot.md) transmit loads in habitual [bipedalism](../../../../bipedalism.md). These are derived modifications within [tetrapods](../../../../tetrapod.md), rather than defining traits of all [Sarcopterygii](../../../../sarcopterygii.md). Comparing our arm with a fish pectoral [fin](../../../../fin.md) concerns [biological homology](../../../../biological-homology.md) between organisms; comparing arm and leg also involves [serial homology](../../../../serial-homology.md) within the body.

**The dental transformation and its qualification.** [human](../../../../human.md) [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) crowns have an outer layer of [enamel](../../../../enamel.md) over [dentine](../../../../dentine.md). [enamel](../../../../enamel.md) is highly mineralized and is produced by epithelial [ameloblasts](../../../../ameloblast.md), whereas [dentine](../../../../dentine.md) is a different tissue formed by [odontoblasts](../../../../odontoblast.md). Identifying a [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) surface as [enamel](../../../../enamel.md) therefore requires tissue architecture and developmental or molecular comparison, not simply a shiny hard covering. In our [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) the coating has specialized organization into [enamel rods](../../../../enamel-rod.md) and contributes to wear resistance during biting and chewing. [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) classes and crown shapes diversify the ancestral feeding apparatus; they are not themselves basal [sarcopterygian](../../../../sarcopterygii.md) [synapomorphies](../../../../synapomorphy.md). After eruption, loss of the enamel-producing cellular layer severely limits biological replacement of worn crown [enamel](../../../../enamel.md).

The traditional contrast between [sarcopterygian](../../../../sarcopterygii.md) true [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) [enamel](../../../../enamel.md) and other hard coverings needs an explicit historical correction. [Qingming Qu](../../../../qingming-qu.md) and colleagues combined [fossil](../../../../fossil.md) [histology](../../../../histology.md) with evidence for enamel-matrix [genes](../../../../gene.md) in the skin of [gar](../../../../gar.md). In [Psarolepis](../../../../psarolepis.md), external [odontodes](../../../../odontode.md) carried [enamel](../../../../enamel.md) while the [teeth](../../../../tooth.md) lacked an [enamel](../../../../enamel.md) coating. Their evidence supported an origin in dermal coverings followed by expansion into the mouth, and an ancestry deeper than [Sarcopterygii](../../../../sarcopterygii.md) alone.[Fossil tissue distribution and gar enamel-matrix gene expression](../../../../new-genomic-and-fossil-data-illuminate-the-origin-of-enamel-by-qingming-qu-and-others.md) Thus [enamel](../../../../enamel.md) and its oral deployment must be coded separately; [tooth](../../../../tooth.md) [enamel](../../../../enamel.md) remains a useful traditional diagnostic comparison, but **the tissue itself is not a uniquely [sarcopterygian](../../../../sarcopterygii.md) [synapomorphy](../../../../synapomorphy.md)**. Shared tissue can undergo [heterotopy](../../../../heterotopy.md), loss and later modification.

The two histories illustrate different forms of transformation visible in our bodies: an internally supported paired [fin](../../../../fin.md) became a jointed, digit-bearing, specialized [limb](../../../../vertebrate-limb.md), and an inherited mineralized epithelial covering acquired a complex role in our dentition. A rigorous diagnosis states the anatomical level being compared and distinguishes inherited organization from later functional specialization. Neither the modern function nor the external [human](../../../../human.md) appearance determines membership of [Sarcopterygii](../../../../sarcopterygii.md).
