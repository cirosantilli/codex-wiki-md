<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Ubiquitin](../../../../../ubiquitin.md) is a central regulator of selective protein turnover, but “the most important” has no universal meaning without specifying substrate, cell state and the quantity being measured. Selective destruction of a short-lived regulator, bulk recycling during starvation, and removal of a damaged [mitochondrion](../../../../../mitochondria.md) are different problems. The ubiquitin system is extensive, yet it neither encompasses every degradation pathway nor makes all [ubiquitination](../../../../../ubiquitination.md) a destruction signal.

In the canonical pathway, an [E1 enzyme](../../../../../ubiquitin-activating-enzyme.md) uses [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) to activate ubiquitin, an [E2 enzyme](../../../../../ubiquitin-conjugating-enzyme.md) carries it, and an [E3 ligase](../../../../../ubiquitin-ligase.md) supplies much of the substrate specificity. Repeated conjugation can build chains on a substrate, while [deubiquitinating enzymes](../../../../../deubiquitinating-enzyme.md) edit or remove those modifications. Linkage and chain architecture influence recognition; a K48-linked chain is a common proteasomal signal, but “polyubiquitinated” does not specify a single outcome. Some [ubiquitination](../../../../../ubiquitination.md)s regulate location or signalling instead of degradation.

The [proteasome](../../../../../proteasome.md) recognizes suitable substrates, removes or recycles ubiquitin, unfolds accessible protein regions using ATP-dependent machinery, and threads the polypeptide into a proteolytic core. Substrate tagging is therefore one control point among several: accessibility, engagement and unfolding matter as well. This permits rapid removal of regulatory proteins and disposal of misfolded soluble proteins without digesting an entire organelle.

A particularly clear exception is [ornithine decarboxylase](../../../../../ornithine-decarboxylase.md). In a cell-free system, proteasome depletion blocked its ATP- and [antizyme](../../../../../antizyme.md)-dependent degradation; purified 26S proteasomes supported degradation without ubiquitin. This provides direct biochemical evidence that proteasomal destruction need not always require substrate ubiquitination.[Immunodepletion and purified-proteasome assays, Nature 360, pp. 597–599](../../../../../ornithine-decarboxylase-is-degraded-by-the-26s-proteasome-without-ubiquitination-by-yasuko-murakami-and-others.md) It is a specific exception rather than evidence that ubiquitin is generally dispensable.

[Lysosomes](../../../../../lysosome.md) provide another major route. [Autophagy](../../../../../autophagy.md) can sequester cytoplasm, aggregates and organelles in membrane carriers for lysosomal degradation. Some selective autophagy receptors recognize ubiquitinated cargo, so this route is partly coordinated with ubiquitin; bulk starvation-induced autophagy and other cargo-recruitment mechanisms show why it is not equivalent to proteasomal tagging. A yeast genetic screen used failure to accumulate autophagic bodies and failure of starvation-induced vacuolar protein turnover to identify autophagy-defective mutants. The experiment established a genetically separable membrane-delivery programme essential to this recycling response.[Mutant isolation, complementation groups, and nitrogen-starvation-induced vacuolar protein degradation](../../../../../isolation-and-characterization-of-autophagy-defective-mutants-of-saccharomyces-cerevisiae-by-miki-tsukada-and-yoshinori-ohsumi.md)

Membrane proteins can also be delivered through [endocytosis](../../../../../endocytosis.md) and endosomal sorting, including [ESCRT](../../../../../endosomal-sorting-complexes-required-for-transport.md)-dependent entry into internal vesicles before lysosomal destruction. Here ubiquitin often helps specify sorting, while receptor internalization, membrane traffic and lysosomal activity supply additional control points. Dedicated organellar proteases add further pathways.

Experimentally, compare proteasome inhibition, lysosomal inhibition and perturbation of a defined [E3 ligase](../../../../../ubiquitin-ligase.md) or autophagy factor while following a labelled substrate over time. A steady-state increase is insufficient: it can result from increased synthesis rather than reduced degradation. Measure turnover and pathway flux, check inhibitor toxicity and use genetic rescue. The defensible conclusion is that ubiquitin is a major, versatile selectivity signal whose importance is context-dependent; complete protein clearance depends on several regulated degradation systems.
