<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Coat-associated adaptors couple three tasks: recognizing the correct membrane, selecting cargo, and assembling a carrier. Each interaction can be modest on its own; coincidence detection creates a selective assembly site. A sorting motif on a cargo's cytoplasmic tail can contact an adaptor directly, whereas soluble luminal cargo requires a transmembrane receptor to expose an adaptor-binding signal to the cytoplasm. Thus cargo recognition respects membrane orientation.

In [clathrin](../../../../../clathrin.md)-mediated [endocytosis](../../../../../endocytosis.md), the [AP2 adaptor complex](../../../../../ap2-adaptor-complex.md) recognizes plasma-membrane determinants and cargo motifs while recruiting the clathrin scaffold. Other clathrin adaptors operate at internal membranes, so clathrin alone does not specify an itinerary. [AP1 adaptor complex](../../../../../ap1-adaptor-complex.md)-associated carriers, for example, rely on a different local molecular context. Cargo, lipid and small-GTPase interactions collectively distinguish productive assembly from accidental coat binding.

[COPII](../../../../../copii.md) illustrates the same logic at [endoplasmic reticulum](../../../../../endoplasmic-reticulum.md) exit sites. Membrane-associated [Sar1 GTPase](../../../../../sar1-gtpase.md) recruits the inner [Sec23-Sec24 complex](../../../../../sec23-sec24-complex.md) layer, in which Sec24 recognizes cargo and cargo receptors; [Sec13-Sec31 complex](../../../../../sec13-sec31-complex.md) builds an outer scaffold. [COPI](../../../../../copi.md) uses a different coat assembly, recruited by [ARF-family GTPases](../../../../../arf-gtpase.md), and participates in retrieval toward the [endoplasmic reticulum](../../../../../endoplasmic-reticulum.md) and traffic within the [Golgi apparatus](../../../../../golgi-apparatus.md). These systems need not have identical subunits to share the architecture of membrane recruitment, cargo selection and scaffold assembly.

An especially useful mechanistic test is biochemical reconstitution. Purified COPII coat components and defined liposomes were sufficient to produce coated vesicles, demonstrating that assembly itself can deform membranes rather than merely decorate carriers made by a wholly independent mechanism.[Reconstitution with Sar1, Sec23/Sec24 and Sec13/Sec31 on defined liposomes](../../../../../copii-coated-vesicle-formation-reconstituted-with-purified-coat-proteins-and-chemically-defined-liposomes-by-ken-matsuoka-and-others.md) Sufficiency in this minimal system does not imply that every regulator and cargo in a living cell is dispensable.

Coat polymerization, membrane-inserting regions and accessory factors contribute to curvature, while fission machinery and details of neck geometry differ among pathways. The nucleotide cycles of [Sar1 GTPase](../../../../../sar1-gtpase.md) and [ARF-family GTPases](../../../../../arf-gtpase.md) regulate coat recruitment and turnover; they should not be portrayed as identical engines executing every membrane-cutting step. After budding, substantial uncoating permits subsequent targeting and fusion. Destination specificity then depends on additional factors, including tethering machinery, [Rab-family GTPases](../../../../../rab-gtpase.md) and [SNAREs](../../../../../snare-protein.md); a coat is not a complete postal address by itself.

To test the common design, mutate a cargo motif, its adaptor-binding site and the membrane-recruitment determinant separately. Measure recruitment, cargo concentration and productive transport as separate outputs, and rescue with the corresponding wild-type component. A cargo-selective defect without complete loss of carrier formation is particularly informative because it separates selection from the physical formation of a carrier.
