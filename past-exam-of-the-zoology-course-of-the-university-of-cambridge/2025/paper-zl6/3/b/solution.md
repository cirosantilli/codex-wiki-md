<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**[reactive oxygen species](../../../../../reactive-oxygen-species.md) can alter competitive fitness, but neither high ROS nor an antioxidant response universally identifies a loser.** [cell competition](../../../../../cell-competition.md) is relative: cells that survive in one neighbourhood are preferentially lost in another, with fitter neighbours occupying their place. An experiment showing oxidant-induced death in a homogeneous tissue establishes toxicity, not necessarily [cell competition](../../../../../cell-competition.md). In epithelia, loss can occur through [apoptosis](../../../../../apoptosis.md), extrusion or differentiation followed by departure from a proliferative layer.

[reactive oxygen species](../../../../../reactive-oxygen-species.md) include [superoxide](../../../../../superoxide.md) and [hydrogen peroxide](../../../../../hydrogen-peroxide.md), which differ in reactivity, localization and transport. They can arise from mitochondrial metabolism or regulated oxidase activity. At controlled levels they participate in [redox signalling](../../../../../redox-signalling.md); excess or poorly buffered production damages [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), proteins and membranes. Competitive fitness can therefore change through altered damage, stress responses, growth or differentiation. [NRF2](../../../../../nrf2.md) and its fly counterpart [CncC](../../../../../cncc.md) regulate antioxidant and detoxification programmes. Their activation is an integrated response to cellular conditions, not a direct quantitative measurement of one ROS species.

An instructive fly study compared prospective losers carrying a ribosomal-protein defect or loss of [Mahjong protein](../../../../../mahjong-protein.md). Both activated antioxidant-response genes before elimination. In ribosomal-protein-deficient cells, two ROS dyes did not reveal a uniform increase, whereas reduced-thiol staining was lower. Mild [CncC](../../../../../cncc.md) activation was tolerated across an entire disc but caused boundary-enriched death in clones surrounded by normal cells; those clones survived better among less-fit neighbours. Conversely, reducing [CncC](../../../../../cncc.md) impaired stressed-cell survival. Thus a protective response can also confer relative loser status. The study separated this module from [JNK signalling](../../../../../jnk-signalling.md) effects on growth and from [JAK-STAT signalling](../../../../../jak-stat-signalling.md) effects on tissue expansion; it did not establish a compulsory linear chain from ROS through JNK to elimination.[Figures 5–7, redox measurements and neighbourhood-dependent CncC experiments](../../../../../chronic-activation-of-jnk-jak-stat-and-oxidative-stress-signalling-causes-the-loser-cell-status-by-iwo-kucinski-and-others.md)

This distinction prevents two errors. First, a high antioxidant-response reporter can coexist with successful buffering and relatively low measured oxidant levels. Second, knocking down a protective factor may kill cells autonomously, making their apparent competitive loss worse even if that factor also participates in neighbour-dependent recognition. To identify a competitive mechanism, compare the same genotype in a homogeneous tissue, in a mosaic with normal neighbours and in a mosaic with an alternative neighbour genotype. Quantify clone growth, death at boundaries and in centres, and replacement by neighbours, normalized to the corresponding cell numbers or areas. A clone that is small because its cells divide slowly has not necessarily been actively eliminated.

The adult mammalian oesophagus supplies a striking environmental example. In transgenic mouse [epithelium](../../../../../epithelium.md), low-dose [ionizing radiation](../../../../../ionizing-radiation.md) induced an oxidative response that shifted normal basal cells toward differentiation. Cells carrying mutant [p53](../../../../../p53.md) were relatively insensitive and expanded at their neighbours' expense. Combining antioxidant treatment with irradiation reversed the population outcome, promoting normal-cell proliferation and mutant-cell differentiation. The selective advantage was consequently conditional on the redox environment, not a fixed property of the mutation. This also shows that competitive replacement need not be executed primarily by [apoptosis](../../../../../apoptosis.md).[Mouse lineage tracing, irradiation and antioxidant-intervention experiments](../../../../../outcompeting-p53-mutant-cells-in-the-normal-esophagus-by-redox-manipulation-by-david-fernandez-antoran-and-others.md)

A separate mouse experiment generated a mosaic of [NRF2](../../../../../nrf2.md)-deficient and NRF2-intact oesophageal cells. Deficient cells persisted under unstressed conditions but accumulated damage and were preferentially lost during exposure to the carcinogen [4-nitroquinoline 1-oxide](../../../../../4-nitroquinoline-1-oxide.md). Tumours predominantly arose from the surviving NRF2-intact population. This supports a context-dependent role for stress protection in population selection, while not proving that every disappearing cell was directly recognized and killed by a neighbour. Carcinogen detoxification and [DNA damage](../../../../../dna-damage.md) are additional variables, so the experiment is not a chemically pure ROS titration.[Mosaic deletion, unstressed controls and 4NQO exposure experiments](../../../../../cellular-nrf2-levels-determine-cell-fate-during-chemical-carcinogenesis-in-esophageal-epithelium-by-makoto-horiuchi-and-others.md)

To test a proposed ROS mechanism in an epithelial mosaic, image a calibrated, compartment-targeted [redox biosensor](../../../../../redox-biosensor.md) before clone loss, along with cell boundaries, a proliferation readout and a death or differentiation reporter. Include sensor controls for [pH](../../../../../ph.md), expression level and dynamic range; general oxidant dyes have loading, localization and chemical-specificity limitations. Perturb the source and scavenging machinery independently and restrict the perturbation to prospective losers or winners. For example, a validated [catalase](../../../../../catalase.md) manipulation tests peroxide buffering, while a source-specific perturbation tests whether that peroxide is required. A matched rescue should restore both the measured redox change and the competitive outcome. Monitor cellular energy and survival in homogeneous controls because a mitochondrial perturbation can affect fitness independently of ROS. Perturb downstream stress signalling separately to distinguish oxidant production, stress detection and execution of cell loss.

The tissue consequences can be beneficial or harmful. Elimination of damaged cells can preserve a barrier, but a stress-resistant mutant can also exploit environmental oxidants to displace normal cells. Persistent stress signals from damaged or dying cells may alter neighbour proliferation and inflammation, producing further changes in fitness. The experimentally supported conclusion is that redox state reshapes the conditions under which cells persist, differentiate or are eliminated. A universal prescription to raise ROS or administer antioxidants does not follow: the relevant species, tissue compartment, neighbour genotype and exposure history determine the direction of selection.
