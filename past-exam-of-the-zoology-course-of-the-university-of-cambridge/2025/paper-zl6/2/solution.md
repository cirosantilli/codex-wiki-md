**Intestinal development constructs a patterned renewing organ; intestinal homeostasis continually replaces its cells while preserving that organization.** Both depend on reciprocal signalling between [endoderm](../../../../endoderm.md)-derived [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md) and [mesenchyme](../../../../mesenchyme.md), but they solve different problems. Development expands and regionalizes the tissue, establishes architecture and produces its first mature [cell types](../../../../cell-type.md). Adult [tissue homeostasis](../../../../tissue-homeostasis.md) balances cell production, [cell differentiation](../../../../cellular-differentiation.md), movement and loss within an already established architecture. The mammalian small intestine provides the main comparison; its villi and crypts should not be indiscriminately attributed to the colon or to all vertebrates.

Early [intestinal development](../../../../intestinal-development.md) begins with a broadly proliferative epithelial tube whose regional identity is established in its embryonic context. Factors including [CDX2](../../../../cdx2.md) help impose intestinal identity, while local signals distinguish the future small intestine and colon. Growth is coordinated with the formation of [smooth muscle](../../../../smooth-muscle.md), connective tissue, the [enteric nervous system](../../../../enteric-nervous-system.md) and vasculature. The emergence of [intestinal villi](../../../../intestinal-villus.md) transforms a relatively simple luminal surface into an absorptive one. In mouse, epithelial [Hedgehog signalling](../../../../hedgehog-signalling.md) promotes underlying mesenchymal clusters, and [BMP signalling](../../../../bmp-signalling.md) helps space those clusters. In explants, increased BMP activity suppresses clusters whereas BMP inhibition produces merged clusters and abnormal villus patterns. This supports a role in pattern generation, not simply in the terminal differentiation of enterocytes.[Explant BMP perturbations and the comparison with chick villification](../../../../villification-in-the-mouse-bmp-signals-control-intestinal-villus-patterning-by-katherine-d-walton-and-others.md)

The mechanics and signalling must be distinguished across species. In chick, sequential [smooth muscle](../../../../smooth-muscle.md) differentiation constrains growth, generating ridges, zigzags and villi through [buckling](../../../../buckling.md). In mouse, patterned mesenchymal clusters arise before equivalent surface deformation, so the chick sequence is not a general account of mammalian villus initiation. The common principle is reciprocal communication between layers; the ordering of mechanical and chemical patterning differs. In development, therefore, an abnormal villus pattern could arise from defective mesenchymal organization, epithelial growth or mechanical coupling, even if the final epithelial [cell differentiation](../../../../cellular-differentiation.md) markers are present.

As villi emerge, proliferative cells become concentrated in intervillus regions, and [intestinal crypts](../../../../intestinal-crypt.md) develop chiefly around the perinatal and postnatal transition in mouse. The fetal proliferative [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md) is consequently not simply a miniature adult crypt. [Paneth cells](../../../../paneth-cell.md) and other components of the mature niche also appear on their own developmental schedules. Demonstrating an adult stem-cell marker in an embryonic cell would not alone establish that the embryonic lineage hierarchy or niche requirements are identical to the adult ones.

In adult small-intestinal [tissue homeostasis](../../../../tissue-homeostasis.md), cycling [Lgr5](../../../../lgr5.md) positive [intestinal stem cells](../../../../intestinal-stem-cell.md) at crypt bases generate renewed [stem cells](../../../../stem-cell.md) and [transit-amplifying cells](../../../../transit-amplifying-cell.md). Descendants differentiate into absorptive [enterocytes](../../../../enterocyte.md) and secretory populations including [goblet cells](../../../../goblet-cell.md), [enteroendocrine cells](../../../../enteroendocrine-cell.md), [tuft cells](../../../../tuft-cell.md) and [Paneth cells](../../../../paneth-cell.md). Most differentiated descendants move toward the villus tip and are shed, whereas [Paneth cells](../../../../paneth-cell.md) occupy the crypt base. [lineage tracing](../../../../lineage-tracing.md) from the [Lgr5](../../../../lgr5.md) locus generated persistent contributions to all epithelial lineages, providing functional evidence for [self-renewal](../../../../self-renewal.md) and [multipotency](../../../../multipotency.md) rather than relying on marker expression alone.[Inducible lineage tracing and persistence of labelled epithelial lineages](../../../../identification-of-stem-cells-in-small-intestine-and-colon-by-marker-gene-lgr5-by-nick-barker-and-others.md)

The adult niche spatially restricts developmental pathways. High local [Wnt signalling](../../../../wnt-signalling.md) and [R-spondin](../../../../r-spondin.md) support crypt stemness and growth, while [BMP signalling](../../../../bmp-signalling.md) restrains crypt-like programmes outside their appropriate domain. [Notch signalling](../../../../notch-signalling.md) favours absorptive over secretory lineage allocation partly by opposing [ATOH1](../../../../atoh1.md)-dependent secretory specification. Thus a single pathway can control both population maintenance and cell composition: stimulating proliferation is not sufficient if the resulting [cell differentiation](../../../../cellular-differentiation.md) balance is wrong. Stromal signals are essential in vivo, and [Paneth cells](../../../../paneth-cell.md) provide additional local support in small intestine; the colon maintains [stem cells](../../../../stem-cell.md) without a small-intestinal Paneth-cell arrangement. Niche dependence cannot therefore be equated with dependence on one invariant supporting [cell type](../../../../cell-type.md).

The adult organ is dynamically stable rather than cellularly static. A stem-cell clone can expand while neighbouring stem-cell clones disappear through [neutral drift](../../../../neutral-drift.md), with the total compartment maintained.[Multicolour clonal tracing and neutral-drift analysis](../../../../intestinal-crypt-homeostasis-results-from-neutral-competition-between-symmetrically-dividing-lgr5-stem-cells-by-hugo-j-snippert-and-others.md) At the organ level, sustained excess production generates expansion or neoplasia; excess loss causes barrier failure. Development deliberately changes total cell number, surface area and proportions over time, whereas adult maintenance constrains their long-term averages. Nevertheless, adult [tissue homeostasis](../../../../tissue-homeostasis.md) remains responsive to feeding, microbes, immune signals and injury, and is not a completely fixed endpoint.

Experimental models expose both continuity and differences. A single sorted [Lgr5](../../../../lgr5.md) positive cell can produce a self-renewing [intestinal organoid](../../../../intestinal-organoid.md) containing organized differentiated progeny in a suitable matrix and supplied growth factors. This establishes epithelial self-organization under defined conditions, not niche independence in the absence of extracellular signals: the medium replaces important niche functions.[Single-cell cultures, lineage organization and defined culture requirements](../../../../single-lgr5-stem-cells-build-crypt-villus-structures-in-vitro-without-a-mesenchymal-niche-by-toshiro-sato-and-others.md) Embryonic explants retain the [mesenchyme](../../../../mesenchyme.md) needed to study villus patterning, while adult [intestinal organoids](../../../../intestinal-organoid.md) are especially useful for stem-cell maintenance and lineage allocation. Neither alone reproduces an intact organ's circulation, innervation, immune environment and longitudinal regionalization.

Finally, injury reveals an important bridge between development and maintenance. [cell fate plasticity](../../../../cell-fate-plasticity.md) can restore missing [stem cells](../../../../stem-cell.md), so the normal adult lineage flow is not an irreversible restriction of all descendants. Committed [progenitor cells](../../../../progenitor-cell.md) can recover stem-cell function under particular injury conditions, but this does not establish that all mature villus cells routinely do so. A decisive comparison uses stage-specific [lineage tracing](../../../../lineage-tracing.md), matched marker analysis and timed pathway perturbation, measuring architecture and functional output as well as [cell proliferation](../../../../cell-proliferation.md). Development and adult renewal reuse molecular machinery while differing in tissue geometry, developmental competence and the direction of net population change.
