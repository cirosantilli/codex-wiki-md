**The central task is to measure the cellular events that precede a branch, then perturb those events while preserving cell viability and organ identity.** Similar final trees can arise through very different combinations of [cell proliferation](../../../../cell-proliferation.md), [cell migration](../../../../cell-migration.md), [cell intercalation](../../../../cell-intercalation.md), [cell shape change](../../../../cell-shape-change.md) and [lumen formation](../../../../lumen-formation.md). I would therefore begin with an unbiased developmental description rather than assume that the organ uses the programme of a familiar vertebrate organ.

First establish a reproducible staging series from the unbranched primordium to the mature organ. Use serial sections and whole-mount [confocal microscopy](../../../../confocal-microscopy.md) to distinguish [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md), surrounding [mesenchyme](../../../../mesenchyme.md), [extracellular matrix](../../../../extracellular-matrix.md), nerves and blood vessels. Validate candidate [epithelial cell](../../../../epithelial-cell.md) and [cell polarity](../../../../cell-polarity.md) markers by their localization and sequence rather than assuming that mammalian antibodies cross-react. A fluorescent luminal tracer, introduced without excessive [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), would establish which spaces are continuous. Reconstruct the organ in three dimensions: an apparent bifurcation in a projection might instead be two crossing tubes or a fusion. Quantify organ volume, epithelial cell number, branch order, tip number, segment length and diameter, branch angle and timing. Distinguish lateral budding, tip bifurcation and branch fusion. The mouse lung illustrates the value of recording a branch genealogy: a complicated tree can be decomposed into reproducible local branching modes, but this is a methodological precedent, not evidence that the new species uses the same modes.[Developmental reconstruction and identification of local branching modes](../../../../the-branching-programme-of-mouse-lung-development-by-ross-j-metzger-and-others.md)

Next establish intact-animal [live-cell imaging](../../../../live-cell-imaging.md), preferably with [light-sheet fluorescence microscopy](../../../../light-sheet-fluorescence-microscopy.md) if optical access allows it, and a complementary explant culture containing both [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md) and [mesenchyme](../../../../mesenchyme.md). Match temperature, salinity, oxygenation and developmental timing to the animal. Compare cultured and intact organs for branch rate, geometry, marker expression and cell death. Introduce membrane and nuclear [fluorescent proteins](../../../../fluorescent-protein.md) at low expression and validate that imaging, immobilization and labels do not change development. Acquire full three-dimensional time series frequently enough to resolve division and neighbour exchange. Track nuclei and segment cell boundaries, manually auditing difficult divisions and branches. Express movement relative to the local tissue, so passive displacement by growth is not mistaken for active [cell migration](../../../../cell-migration.md).

Measure separately the sources of tissue expansion. Short [DNA synthesis labeling with EdU](../../../../dna-synthesis-labeling-with-edu.md) pulses locate [DNA replication](../../../../dna-replication.md), pulse-chase experiments reveal subsequent displacement, and direct imaging records completed [cytokinesis](../../../../cytokinesis.md). A high mitotic fraction alone cannot establish rapid cell production because a prolonged [mitosis](../../../../mitosis.md) also raises that fraction. Measure cell volume to distinguish [cell growth](../../../../cell-growth.md) from [cell proliferation](../../../../cell-proliferation.md), and use a validated [caspase](../../../../caspase.md) reporter plus morphology to detect [apoptosis](../../../../apoptosis.md) or [cell extrusion](../../../../cell-extrusion.md). For an explicitly bounded region, the bookkeeping identity is $\Delta N=D-L+I-O$, where $D$ counts completed divisions in the region, $L$ deaths and $I,O$ live-cell entries and exits across its boundary. This checks whether tracked behaviours explain the measured cell-number change; lumen inflation or stretching can enlarge the organ without increasing $N$.

Sparse permanent [lineage tracing](../../../../lineage-tracing.md) or local photolabelling before branch initiation would test whether tip and stalk populations are stable or exchange. Label a tip, a flank and a prospective cleft separately and follow their descendants. Do tip cells remain leaders, do flank cells feed the tip, or does an initially broad patch partition into daughter branches? Image [oriented cell division](../../../../oriented-cell-division.md), cell elongation, protrusions and junction turnover alongside branch emergence. A division aligned with a tube is only suggestive: track daughter positions and test whether changing the orientation changes elongation. Quantify neighbour exchanges to identify [cell intercalation](../../../../cell-intercalation.md). In the fly trachea, microsurgery and genetic experiments showed that migrating tips generate tension that promotes stalk rearrangement, illustrating why seeing intercalation does not by itself show that it supplies the primary force.[Genetic and microsurgical tests of tip-generated tension](../../../../tip-cell-migration-controls-stalk-cell-intercalation-during-drosophila-tracheal-tube-elongation-by-emmanuel-caussinus-and-others.md)

Then make acute, staged causal tests. Transiently inhibit the [cell cycle](../../../../cell-cycle-split.md) after specification, verify suppression of completed divisions, and ask whether existing tips still extend or new branches initiate. Independently perturb [actomyosin contractility](../../../../actomyosin-contractility.md), [cell adhesion](../../../../cell-adhesion.md) or a candidate migration regulator identified by the movies. Use short observation windows before generalized disorganization, and quantify cell number, [apoptosis](../../../../apoptosis.md), [cell polarity](../../../../cell-polarity.md) and organ markers in parallel. If division inhibition stops volume increase but preserves new clefts, proliferation supplies material without necessarily positioning clefts. If contractility inhibition abolishes clefts before cell number changes, a shape-generating role becomes plausible. Local [laser ablation](../../../../laser-ablation.md) of a tip, a stalk junction or adjacent matrix, with sham irradiation controls, can test force transmission; recoil reports pre-existing load relative to resistance, not an absolute force without a mechanical model.

Use [RNA interference](../../../../rna-interference.md) for candidate screening and independent [CRISPR genome editing](../../../../crispr-genome-editing.md) alleles for confirmation. Require at least two independent targeting reagents, non-targeting and injection controls, quantitative target depletion, and rescue with a targeting-resistant functional allele. Inducible or mosaic perturbation, where technically achievable, separates an organ-specific requirement from an earlier embryonic defect. Mix labelled mutant and normal cells to distinguish a [cell-autonomous](../../../../cell-autonomous.md) defect from altered neighbouring signals or matrix. Separate [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md) and [mesenchyme](../../../../mesenchyme.md), recombine control and perturbed partners, and test matrix composition or localized candidate growth-factor delivery only after confirming that separated tissues remain viable. Use [single-cell RNA sequencing](../../../../single-cell-rna-sequencing.md) and spatial expression to nominate pathways such as [FGF signalling](../../../../fgf-signalling.md), but treat expression as a hypothesis generator rather than proof of behaviour.

Finally compare explicit alternative models of [branching morphogenesis](../../../../branching-morphogenesis.md) against held-out movies: localized cell production, directed collective movement, active rearrangement, or growth constrained by the surrounding matrix. Each should predict both branch geometry and the observed cell trajectories, and its response to a spatially restricted perturbation. Randomize treatment, blind segmentation and use animals from independent clutches as biological replicates; many cells within one animal do not replace independent animals. The strongest outcome is a causal chain from molecular perturbation to altered cell behaviour to altered branch geometry, supported by rescue and by measurements showing that general developmental delay cannot explain it.
