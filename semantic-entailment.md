# Semantic entailment

↑ **Parent:** [Propositional logic](propositional-logic.md)

A [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) $\varphi$ semantically entails $\psi$ when every [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) satisfying $\varphi$ satisfies $\psi$. Equivalently, $\varphi\land\neg\psi$ is an [unsatisfiable Boolean formula](unsatisfiable-boolean-formula.md). Deciding [semantic entailment](semantic-entailment.md) is [co-NP](co-np.md)-complete: a counterexample [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) certifies failure in [polynomial time](polynomial-time.md), while $\theta\mapsto\langle\theta,p\land\neg p\rangle$ reduces unsatisfiability to [semantic entailment](semantic-entailment.md).
