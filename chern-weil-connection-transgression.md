# Chern-Weil connection transgression

↑ **Parent:** [Chern-Weil homomorphism](chern-weil-homomorphism.md)

A variation of the [connection one-form](connection-one-form.md) has $\dot F=D_A\dot A$. The [Bianchi identity](bianchi-identity.md) and graded cyclic [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) imply $\partial_t\operatorname{Tr}(F\wedge F)=2d\operatorname{Tr}(\dot A\wedge F)$. Thus the [Chern-Weil theory](chern-weil-homomorphism.md) four-form changes by an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md), and its integral on a closed four-cycle is connection-independent. For a general unitary bundle, include the corresponding trace-product term in the [Second Chern form](second-chern-form.md).
