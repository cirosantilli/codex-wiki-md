# Finite intersection of finite-index subgroups

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-index subgroup](finite-index-subgroup.md)

For [finite-index subgroups](finite-index-subgroup.md) $H,K$ of a [group](group-split.md) $G$, the map of sets of [left cosets](left-coset.md)

$$
g(H\cap K)\longmapsto(gH,gK)
$$

is well defined and injective: equality of both components is equivalent to the difference $g'^{-1}g$ belonging to $H\cap K$. Hence the displayed [subgroup index](index-of-a-subgroup.md) bound holds, without a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) hypothesis. By [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md), the [index of a subgroup](index-of-a-subgroup.md) for a finite intersection is at most the product of the individual [subgroup indices](index-of-a-subgroup.md).
