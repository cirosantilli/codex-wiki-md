# Splice-aware sequence alignment

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence alignment](sequence-alignment.md)

A [sequence alignment](sequence-alignment.md) that permits gaps corresponding to removed [introns](intron.md) when matching processed [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) reads to a [genome](genome.md). Short [microexons](microexon.md) require particular care because their supporting aligned segments can be very small.
