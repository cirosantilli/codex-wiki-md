# Junction capacitance

↑ **Parent:** [Depletion region](depletion-region.md)

The incremental [capacitance](capacitance.md) associated with changes of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) around a [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md). Its depletion contribution is often estimated by $C_j\simeq\varepsilon_s A/W$, so an independent [junction capacitance](junction-capacitance.md) measurement can constrain the width $W$ of the [depletion region](depletion-region.md) under the model assumptions. Other stored [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), including injected [minority carriers](minority-carrier.md), can also contribute to a measured terminal [capacitance](capacitance.md).
