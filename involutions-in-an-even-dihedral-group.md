# Involutions in an even dihedral group

↑ **Parent:** [Dihedral group](dihedral-group.md)

For the [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of a regular $2n$-gon, $n\ge2$, use $r^{2n}=s^2=1$ and $srs=r^{-1}$. Its [involutions](involution.md) are the central half-turn $r^n$ and all reflections $sr^j$. The half-turn has singleton [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) and [normal closure](normal-closure.md) $\{1,r^n\}$. Conjugating a reflection changes its exponent by an even integer or negates it, so its [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) is $\{sr^{j+2k}:0\le k<n\}$. Products of consecutive reflections generate $r^2$, giving [normal closure](normal-closure.md) $\langle r^2,sr^j\rangle$, of order $2n$ and index two. The two reflection classes are distinguished by exponent parity.
