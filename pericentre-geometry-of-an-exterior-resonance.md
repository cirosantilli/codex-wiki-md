# Pericentre geometry of an exterior resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Resonant trapping of dust](resonant-trapping-of-dust.md)

At pericentre, [mean longitude](mean-longitude.md) equals [longitude of periapsis](longitude-of-periapsis.md), so [resonant argument](resonant-argument.md) $\phi=(p+q)\lambda-p\lambda_p-q\varpi$ becomes $\phi=p(\varpi-\lambda_p)$. The pericentre angle in the planet's rotating frame is $\phi/p$ modulo $2\pi/p$. For coprime $p,q$, there are $p$ distinct pericentre directions before the trajectory closes. [Resonant-argument libration](resonant-argument-libration.md) restricts these directions and supplies [resonance protection](resonance-protection.md).
