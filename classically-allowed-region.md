# Classically allowed region

↑ **Parent:** [Time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md)

For a particle of fixed [energy](energy-split.md) $E$, a [classically allowed region](classically-allowed-region.md) has positive classical [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $E-V(x)$. For a constant [potential energy](potential-energy.md), the stationary [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) is a combination of $e^{ikx}$ and $e^{-ikx}$, where $k=\sqrt{2m(E-V)}/\hbar$ is the [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). In a varying [potential energy](potential-energy.md) this local value underlies the [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) away from turning points.
