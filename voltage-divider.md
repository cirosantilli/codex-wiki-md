# Voltage divider

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage_divider)

A voltage divider takes an [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) across two series [resistors](resistor.md) and measures the share across one. With no [electric current](electric-current.md) drawn from the junction, [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) gives $V_{\mathrm{out}}=V_sR_b/(R_a+R_b)$ for the lower [resistor](resistor.md) $R_b$. Drawing [electric current](electric-current.md) from that junction changes the ratio through [voltmeter loading](voltmeter-loading.md); the [Wheatstone bridge](wheatstone-bridge.md) compares two such junctions.
