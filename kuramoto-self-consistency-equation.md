# Kuramoto self-consistency equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kuramoto order parameter](kuramoto-order-parameter.md)

For a symmetric stationary frequency distribution, locked oscillators contribute $r=\int_{-Kr}^{Kr}\sqrt{1-\omega^2/(Kr)^2}\,g(\omega)\,d\omega$. The drifting oscillators' real contribution is zero by integrating a periodic logarithmic [derivative](derivative.md), and imaginary contributions cancel by symmetry. Substituting $\omega=Kr\sin\theta$ and dividing by $r>0$ gives the equation. The incoherent solution must be retained before division. For finite positive continuous $g(0)$ the branch reaches it at $K_c=2/[\pi g(0)]$.
