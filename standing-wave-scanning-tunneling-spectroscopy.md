# Standing-wave scanning tunneling spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Scanning tunneling spectroscopy](scanning-tunneling-spectroscopy.md)

Interference between incident and elastically reflected surface-state waves gives density oscillations proportional to $\cos(2kx+\phi)$. Their real-space period is $\pi/k$, half the travelling-wave [wavelength](wavelength.md).
