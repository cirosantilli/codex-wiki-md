# Rational covering maps of the projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Rational map (complex analysis)](rational-map-complex-analysis.md)

A degree-$m$ [rational map](rational-map-complex-analysis.md) of the [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md) is represented by two degree-at-most-$m$ polynomials without a common root, modulo common scalar multiplication. This is an open set of $\mathbb{CP}^{2m+1}$. Quotienting by domain [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) subtracts three complex dimensions, giving real dimension $4m-4$ for degree $m\geq2$. These are the local covering parameters of a multiple-covered [J-holomorphic sphere](j-holomorphic-sphere.md).
