# Love-wave cutoff frequencies

↑ **Parent:** [Love wave](love-wave.md)

For a slow uniform layer over a faster half-space, the nth [Love wave](love-wave.md) branch reaches the half-space shear [wave speed](wave-speed.md) at the displayed threshold. Its half-space decay exponent then vanishes, so the threshold is a limiting nonlocalized state, not a trapped finite-energy mode. The fundamental has zero threshold and exists at every positive [frequency](frequency.md); overtones require [frequency](frequency.md) strictly above their positive threshold. At high [frequency](frequency.md) each fixed branch approaches the layer shear [wave speed](wave-speed.md) from above.
