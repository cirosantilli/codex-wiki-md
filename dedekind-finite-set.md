# Dedekind-finite set

↑ **Parent:** [Set](set-split.md)

A [set](set-split.md) $X$ is Dedekind-finite when no [injective function](injective-function.md) $\mathbb N\to X$ exists. In classical [Zermelo–Fraenkel set theory](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) this is equivalent to $X$ not being in [bijection](bijection.md) with a proper subset: an injective non-surjective self-map generates distinct iterates from an element outside its image, and a countably infinite subset permits a shift fixing its complement. A [finite set](finite-set.md) is Dedekind-finite. Without the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md), some [infinite sets](infinite-set.md) can also be Dedekind-finite.
