<h1 id="timelike-geodesics-in-a-warped-poincare-patch">Timelike geodesics in a warped Poincaré patch</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime](poincare-coordinates-on-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

For unit-radius metric $dy^2+e^{-2y}\eta_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu$, translations conserve $p^\mu=e^{-2y}\dot x^\mu$. Timelike normalization gives $K^2=-\eta_{\mu\nu}p^\mu p^\nu>0$ and $\dot y^2=K^2e^{2y}-1$. Setting $w=e^{-y}$ yields $\dot w^2+w^2=K^2$, hence the displayed proper-time solution where the cosine is positive. Its two patch-horizon crossings are separated by proper time $\pi$, although the globally extended [geodesic](geodesic.md) is complete.
