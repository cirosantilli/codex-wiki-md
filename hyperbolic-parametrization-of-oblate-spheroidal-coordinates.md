# Hyperbolic parametrization of oblate spheroidal coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Oblate spheroidal coordinates](oblate-spheroidal-coordinates.md)

Set $x=a\cosh\rho\cos u\cos v$, $y=a\cosh\rho\cos u\sin v$, $z=a\sinh\rho\sin u$, with $a>0$, $\rho\geq0$ and $|u|\leq\pi/2$. Differentiating the position vector gives pairwise orthogonal coordinate tangents and the displayed [scale factors of orthogonal coordinates](scale-factors-of-orthogonal-coordinates.md). Thus the positive volume density is $a^3\cosh\rho\cos u(\sinh^2\rho+\sin^2u)$. The ordered [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is negative for the order $(\rho,u,v)$; volume uses its absolute value. Positive constant $\rho$ surfaces are [oblate spheroids](oblate-spheroid.md). For $0<|u|<\pi/2$, constant $u$ gives the half of the [one-sheet hyperboloid](one-sheet-hyperboloid.md) $(x^2+y^2)/(a^2\cos^2u)-z^2/(a^2\sin^2u)=1$ with the sign of $z$ fixed by $u$. At $\rho=0$ the coordinates cover the equatorial disk, while $u=0$ covers the exterior equatorial plane.
