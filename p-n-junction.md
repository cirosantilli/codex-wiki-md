# P-n junction

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

A [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md) joins regions of a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) with opposite majority [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md): [electrons](electron.md) on one side and [electron holes](electron-hole.md) on the other. It provides the junction modeled by the [Shockley diode equation](shockley-diode-equation.md).

Near an unbiased [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md), [diffusion](diffusion.md) leaves exposed ionized [donor impurities](donor-impurity.md) and [acceptor impurities](acceptor-impurity.md). Their [electric field](electric-field-split.md) opposes further majority-carrier transfer. At [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md), drift and [diffusion](diffusion.md) balance; there is no net [electric current](electric-current.md). [Forward bias](forward-bias.md) reduces the barrier to [carrier injection](carrier-injection.md); [reverse bias](reverse-bias.md) enlarges it. A heavily doped [tunnel diode](tunnel-diode.md) adds a different transport route, [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md), that the ordinary [Shockley diode equation](shockley-diode-equation.md) omits.
