# Mixed-axis trajectory of a prestressed spring pair

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

Two equal [masses](mass.md) linked in a line by three identical [springs](spring.md), each stretched to twice its natural [length](length.md), have longitudinal [stiffness matrix](stiffness-matrix.md) $k\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$ and transverse matrix half as large. Equal displacements of both [masses](mass.md) select the symmetric modes. Equal longitudinal and transverse release [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) produce the title trajectory. It is not closed after one transverse [period](period-of-a-function.md) because the two mode [frequencies](frequency.md) have irrational ratio $\sqrt2$. Higher-order geometric terms are neglected in this small-displacement solution.
