# Restricted maximum likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restricted_maximum_likelihood)

Restricted maximum likelihood estimates [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) parameters from linear combinations of the data whose distribution does not involve the fixed mean coefficients. It differs from a [restricted maximum-likelihood estimator](restricted-maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) obtained by maximizing an ordinary [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) under a null hypothesis.
