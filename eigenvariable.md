# Eigenvariable

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvariable condition](eigenvariable-condition.md)

A variable used as an arbitrary parameter in a [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) subproof, subject to an [eigenvariable condition](eigenvariable-condition.md). For [universal introduction](universal-quantifier-introduction-rule.md), it must be free in no undischarged assumption. For [existential elimination](existential-quantifier-elimination-rule.md), a temporary witness must also be free in no conclusion exported from its subproof. These restrictions distinguish an arbitrary object from one constrained by a special hypothesis.
