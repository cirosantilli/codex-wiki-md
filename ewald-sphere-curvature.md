# Ewald sphere curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction](diffraction.md)

[Ewald sphere curvature](ewald-sphere-curvature.md) is the curvature of the reciprocal-space surface sampled by elastic scattering. Accounting for its departure from a plane can improve very high-resolution [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md) reconstructions.
