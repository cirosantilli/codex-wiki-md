# Hoeffding projection

↑ **Parent:** [U-statistic central limit theorem](u-statistic-central-limit-theorem.md)

The displayed sum is the first-order projection of a centred [U-statistic](u-statistic.md) onto sums of functions of individual observations. Its summands are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) and have mean zero. Subtracting it leaves a remainder with conditional [expectation](expected-value.md) zero in each individual observation, a useful decomposition for [variance](variance-split.md) and normal-limit calculations.
