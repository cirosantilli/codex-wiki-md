# Right translation of a group function

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis on a finite group](fourier-analysis-on-a-finite-group.md)

For a scalar function on a [group](group-split.md), use $(R_af)(x)=f(xa)$ as the right-translation convention. For [Fourier analysis on a finite group](fourier-analysis-on-a-finite-group.md) this gives $\widehat{R_af}(\rho)=\widehat f(\rho)\rho(a)^*$. Specifying whether $a$ or $a^{-1}$ appears in the definition avoids sign and multiplication-order ambiguity.
