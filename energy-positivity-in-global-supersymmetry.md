# Energy positivity in global supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md)

For $\{Q_\alpha,Q_\beta^\dagger\}=2\sigma^\mu_{\alpha\dot\beta}P_\mu$, summing diagonal components gives $4H$. The expectation of each [anticommutator](anticommutator.md) is $\|Q_\alpha v\|^2+\|Q_\alpha^\dagger v\|^2$, so $H$ is a [positive semidefinite operator](positive-operator.md). A vacuum has zero energy exactly when every [supercharge](supersymmetry-generator.md) annihilates it. Spontaneously broken global [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) with an existing vacuum therefore gives positive [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md) density. This statement fixes the energy zero through the algebra and does not apply to the general [supergravity F-term potential](supergravity-f-term-potential.md).
