# Saturn-ring defect

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic disclination](nematic-disclination.md)

An equatorial disclination loop surrounding a roughly spherical inclusion with [homeotropic anchoring](homeotropic-anchoring.md). A meridional section cuts the loop twice. It can match a radial near-surface director to a uniform far field.
