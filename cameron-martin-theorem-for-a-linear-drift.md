# Cameron-Martin theorem for a linear drift

↑ **Parent:** [Cameron-Martin theorem](cameron-martin-theorem.md)

For the [Cameron-Martin space of Wiener measure](cameron-martin-space-of-wiener-measure.md) path $h(s)=\mu s$, the [Cameron-Martin theorem](cameron-martin-theorem.md) says that translation of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) by this [linear function](linear-function.md) changes its law through time $t$ by the density

$$
\exp\!\left(\mu W_t-\frac12\mu^2t\right).
$$
