# MSc in mathematical and computational finance course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-mathematical-and-computational-finance).

Introductory week followed by core courses: stochastic [calculus](calculus-split.md); financial derivatives; [numerical methods](numerical-method.md); [statistics](statistics.md) and financial data analysis; financial computing with C++ in each of the first two terms; deep learning; quantitative risk management; [stochastic control](stochastic-control.md); fixed income. Four electives complete the taught programme. Third-term dissertation, potentially prepared in connection with an industry internship.
