# Matched filter

↑ **Parent:** [Signal processing](signal-processing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matched_filter)

A linear filter that correlates data with a proposed signal, weighting frequencies according to their noise. In stationary Gaussian noise it maximizes the expected [signal-to-noise ratio](signal-to-noise-ratio.md) for that known signal.
