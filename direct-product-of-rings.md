# Direct product of rings

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)

The direct product of a family of [rings](ring.md) consists of all tuples of their elements, with coordinatewise addition and multiplication. Unlike a direct sum, tuples may have infinitely many nonzero coordinates.
