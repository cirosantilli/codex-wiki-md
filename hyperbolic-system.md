# Hyperbolic system

↑ **Parent:** [Quasilinear system](quasilinear-system.md)

A quasilinear system is hyperbolic where its principal coefficient matrix has real eigenvalues and enough independent eigenvectors. The eigenvalues are its [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md).
