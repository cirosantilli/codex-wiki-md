# B2 weight lattice

↑ **Parent:** [B2 root system](b2-root-system.md)

For simple roots $(1,0)$ and $(-1,1)$, [coroot](coroot.md) integrality gives the [weight lattice](weight-lattice.md) $P=\mathbb Z^2\cup(\mathbb Z+1/2)^2$. This is the lattice for the [Spin group](spin-group.md) $\operatorname{Spin}(5)$ and its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). Representations descending to $SO(5)$ use the integer coset, because a $2\pi$ rotation in either coordinate plane must act trivially.
