# Myotonia congenita

↑ **Parent:** [Ion-channel disease](ion-channel-disease.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myotonia_congenita)

A skeletal-muscle excitability disorder associated with variants of CLCN1, encoding the ClC-1 [chloride channel](chloride-channel.md). Reduced stabilizing resting conductance can permit repetitive [action potentials](action-potential.md) and delayed relaxation after contraction.
