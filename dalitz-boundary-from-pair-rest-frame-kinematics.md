# Dalitz boundary from pair-rest-frame kinematics

↑ **Parent:** [Dalitz plot](dalitz-plot.md)

At fixed $s=s_{13}$, use $E_2^*=(M^2-m_2^2-s)/(2\sqrt s)$ and $E_3^*=(s+m_3^2-m_1^2)/(2\sqrt s)$ in the 13-pair rest frame. The endpoint values of its relative cosine give the displayed [Dalitz plot](dalitz-plot.md) boundary. The [momenta](momentum-split.md) are fixed by the [Källén function](kallen-function.md); $(m_1+m_3)^2\le s\le(M-m_2)^2$. Integrating $s_{23}^+-s_{23}^-$ gives the [invariant-coordinate area](dalitz-plot.md), whose [phase-space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) [normalization](normalization.md) still contains $M^{-2}$.
