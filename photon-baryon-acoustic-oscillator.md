# Photon-baryon acoustic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Tight-coupling approximation](tight-coupling-approximation.md)

With $v_b=v_\gamma$ at leading order and $c_s^2=1/[3(1+R)]$, the [photon continuity equation](photon-continuity-equation.md) and total Euler equation give $\ddot\delta_\gamma+\dot R\dot\delta_\gamma/(1+R)+k^2\delta_\gamma/[3(1+R)]=4\ddot\phi+4\dot R\dot\phi/(1+R)-4k^2\psi/3$. The [baryon loading parameter](baryon-loading-parameter.md) changes inertia and shifts the gravitational equilibrium. The leading equations omit diffusion damping.
