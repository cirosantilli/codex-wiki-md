# Normalized adjacency operator of a bipartite graph

↑ **Parent:** [Bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md)

Equip the function spaces of the two nonempty parts of a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) with uniform [inner products](inner-product.md). Its normalized adjacency operator is $(\theta_Gv)(x)=\mathbb E_yG(x,y)v(y)$. In orthonormal coordinate [bases](basis.md), its matrix is $G/\sqrt{|X||Y|}$, not the unscaled adjacency matrix. For a [biregular graph](biregular-graph.md) of [edge density of a bipartite graph](edge-density-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) $\gamma$, constant functions give a largest [singular value](singular-value.md) $\gamma$, and the remaining [singular values](singular-value.md) belong to the centered kernel $G-\gamma$.
