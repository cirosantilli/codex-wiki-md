<h1 id="probabilistic-use-of-holder-s-inequality">Probabilistic use of Hölder's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder's inequality](holder-s-inequality.md)

On a [probability space](probability-space-split.md), the same [inequality](inequality-mathematics.md) reads

$$
\mathbb E|XY|\leq
(\mathbb E|X|^p)^{1/p}(\mathbb E|Y|^q)^{1/q}.
$$

If the displayed [moments](moment.md) are finite, the product is an [integrable random variable](integrable-random-variable.md). The [random variables](random-variable-split.md) can be dependent: the estimate controls their possible overlap without assuming a factorization of the [expected value](expected-value-split.md). This is useful when a calculation needs the product to be integrable but the joint [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) is complicated.
