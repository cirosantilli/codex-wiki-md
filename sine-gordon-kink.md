# Sine-Gordon kink

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon theory](sine-gordon-theory.md)

The static [kink](scalar-field-kink.md) $\phi_K(x)=4\arctan e^{x-a}$ joins adjacent [scalar-field vacua](scalar-field-vacuum.md) $0$ and $2\pi$. It obeys $\phi_K'=2\operatorname{sech}(x-a)$ and has [topological charge](topological-charge.md) $Q=[\phi(+\infty)-\phi(-\infty)]/(2\pi)=1$. In the [Sine-Gordon theory](sine-gordon-theory.md) normalization with physical mass scale $m$ and coupling $\beta$, its classical [mass](mass.md) is $8m/\beta^2$. A [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) gives $\phi_K=4\arctan\exp[(x-vt-a)/\sqrt{1-v^2}]$. Spatial reflection gives an [antikink](antikink.md).
