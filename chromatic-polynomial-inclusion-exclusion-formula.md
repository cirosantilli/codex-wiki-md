# Chromatic polynomial inclusion-exclusion formula

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md)

Here $c(F)$ is the number of [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md) in the spanning [subgraph](subgraph.md) $(V(G),F)$, including isolated [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). Apply the [inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) to the conditions that the endpoints of an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) have equal colours. Imposing all such conditions for $F$ forces a single colour on each [connected component of a graph](component-graph-theory.md), yielding $x^{c(F)}$ for positive [integer](integer-split.md) $x$. The equality extends to a [polynomial identity](polynomial-identity.md).
