# Fourier transform of a reciprocal positive quadratic form

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform of a homogeneous distribution](fourier-transform-of-a-homogeneous-distribution.md)

For a real symmetric positive-definite three-dimensional [matrix](matrix.md), the reciprocal [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) is locally integrable and defines a regular [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md). With the Fourier kernel $e^{-ix\cdot\xi}$, radial Abel regularization gives $\mathcal F(|x|^{-2})=2\pi^2/|\xi|$. The linear change of variables $y=G^{1/2}x$ gives the displayed formula. No [principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) or origin-supported correction is required. The result is homogeneous of degree $-1$ on the frequency side.
