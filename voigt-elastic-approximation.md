# Voigt elastic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Effective elastic energy](effective-elastic-energy.md)

The [Voigt elastic approximation](voigt-elastic-approximation.md) assumes uniform [strain](strain.md). Differentiating its averaged [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) gives the averaged local [stress](stress.md). For [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) the predicted [elastic stiffness tensor](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md) is $C_V=\langle C\rangle$. The uniform [strain](strain.md) is an admissible affine [displacement](displacement.md) trial field, so this gives an upper [elastic energy](elastic-energy.md) bound for convex local energy.
