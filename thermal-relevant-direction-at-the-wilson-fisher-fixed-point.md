# Thermal relevant direction at the Wilson-Fisher fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md)

The scalar [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md) is attractive within its [critical surface](critical-surface.md), but has a relevant thermal direction. With $r=m^2\Lambda^{-2}$, $\lambda=g\Lambda^{-\epsilon}$, and $K_D=\Omega_D/(2\pi)^D$, its one-loop equations are $\dot r=2r+K_D\lambda/[2(1+r)]$ and $\dot\lambda=\epsilon\lambda-3K_D\lambda^2/[2(1+r)^2]$. Their [stability matrix](stability-matrix.md) at $r_*=-\epsilon/6+O(\epsilon^2)$ and $\lambda_*=16\pi^2\epsilon/3+O(\epsilon^2)$ has the displayed [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). Thus an unrestricted claim of infrared attraction is false: the bare mass must be tuned to the critical surface.
