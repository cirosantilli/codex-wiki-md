# Nuclear-burning qualification of the helium expansion-rate response

↑ **Parent:** [Helium sensitivity to the expansion rate](helium-sensitivity-to-the-expansion-rate.md)

A moderately enhanced [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) raises the [primordial helium mass fraction](primordial-helium-mass-fraction.md) through earlier [cosmological weak freeze-out](cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md) and less [free-neutron decay after freeze-out](free-neutron-decay-after-freeze-out.md), provided nuclear burning still incorporates nearly all surviving neutrons into helium-4. This provision matters: if expansion carries the gas through the nuclear-burning temperature range faster than the reaction times, [neutron-limited helium synthesis](neutron-limited-helium-synthesis.md) no longer applies. More neutrons surviving the bottleneck alone does not prove more final helium in that limit.
