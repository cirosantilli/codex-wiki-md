# Western blot band interpretation

↑ **Parent:** [Western blot](western-blot.md)

Band position gives an apparent [mass](mass.md), not unique [protein](protein.md) identity. Intensity depends on retention, [epitope](epitope.md) recognition and detection response; it does not establish folded state, [enzyme](enzyme.md) activity or successful [protein targeting](protein-targeting.md). Missing bands can reflect missing targets or failed [Western blot transfer](western-blot-transfer.md) or [Western blot antibody detection](western-blot-antibody-detection.md). [McDonough and others, “Consider the Specificity of the Antibody for the Target” and “Consider the Linearity of the Detection System”](considerations-when-quantitating-protein-abundance-by-immunoblot-by-alicia-a-mcdonough-and-others.md)

The [yeast cross-species complementation experiment](yeast-cross-species-complementation-experiment.md) consequently combines two logically different readouts: [Western blot](western-blot.md) evidence about production of an [antibody](antibody.md)-recognized [protein](protein.md), and growth evidence about [functional complementation](functional-complementation.md). A detectable [protein](protein.md) need not perform the biological task required for growth.
