# Transcendence theory

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine approximation](diophantine-approximation.md)

Transcendence theory proves that particular numbers are not [algebraic numbers](algebraic-number.md), or that several numbers satisfy no nonzero polynomial relation with algebraic coefficients. A recurring method constructs an auxiliary expression which is simultaneously too small analytically and too large arithmetically if the proposed values were algebraic.
