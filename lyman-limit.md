# Lyman limit

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyman_limit)

The [Lyman limit](lyman-limit.md) is the short-wavelength limit of transitions to the [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) ground state. Its [photon](photon.md) energy, approximately $13.6\,\mathrm{eV}$, is the ground-state [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md).
