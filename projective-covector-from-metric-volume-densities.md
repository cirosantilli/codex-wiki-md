# Projective covector from metric volume densities

↑ **Parent:** [Projective equivalence of affine connections](projective-equivalence-of-affine-connections.md)

For two [projectively equivalent connections](projective-equivalence-of-affine-connections.md) that are [Levi-Civita connections](levi-civita-connection.md), contraction of their difference gives $(n+1)V_c=\bar\Gamma^a{}_{ac}-\Gamma^a{}_{ac}$. The [Jacobi formula](jacobi-s-formula.md) gives $\Gamma^a{}_{ac}=\partial_c\log\sqrt{|\det g|}$. Subtraction proves the displayed expression. The two [determinant](determinant.md) densities transform by the same coordinate Jacobian factor, so their ratio is a [scalar](scalar.md) and its [derivative](derivative.md) a [covector](covector.md). The formula is a necessary consequence of projective equivalence, not a test asserting that arbitrary [metric tensors](metric-tensor.md) have identical [geodesics](geodesic.md).
