# Stopping potential

↑ **Parent:** [Photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md)

A [stopping potential](stopping-potential.md) $V_s\geq0$ is the magnitude of the retarding [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) just sufficient to stop collection of even the fastest relevant [electrons](electron.md) in an ideal collection geometry. Since the [electron](electron.md)'s [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) is $-e$, the positive [energy](energy-split.md) barrier is $eV_s$. After the [photoelectric contact-potential correction](photoelectric-contact-potential-correction.md),

$$
V_s=\frac{h}{e}\nu-\frac{\Phi}{e}.
$$

The maximum here is an emission [energy](energy-split.md) edge, not the mean [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). In an ideal planar retarding geometry, transmission instead selects the component normal to the plates; identifying its endpoint with the full edge requires collection of trajectories that attain that edge in the relevant direction.

To make a complete illustrative curve, choose a uniform distribution of outward normal [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $K_\perp$ on $0\leq K_\perp\leq K_{\max}$, complete collection at zero barrier, and zero background. This is a deliberately chosen distribution, not a prediction of the [Einstein photoelectric equation](einstein-photoelectric-equation.md). For an effective retarding barrier $eV_r$, the collected fraction is

$$
F(V_r)=\begin{cases}
1,&V_r\leq0,\\
1-V_r/V_s,&0<V_r<V_s,\\
0,&V_r\geq V_s.
\end{cases}
$$

Integrating that uniform [probability density](probability-density.md) above $eV_r$ proves the middle line. Thus $I(V_r)=I_{\rm sat}F(V_r)$ has a common endpoint when incident [light intensity](light-intensity.md) changes, even though its vertical scale changes. Real emission distributions need not give straight segments.
