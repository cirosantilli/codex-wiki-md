# Stratum of holomorphic one-forms

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic one-form](holomorphic-one-form.md)

The locus of nonzero [holomorphic one-forms](holomorphic-one-form.md) with a fixed list of zero multiplicities $k_1,\ldots,k_r$, summing to $2g-2$. The [SL2R action on differentials](sl2r-action-on-differentials.md) preserves this list. For example $\mathcal H(2)$ and $\mathcal H(1,1)$ are distinct [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-two strata.
