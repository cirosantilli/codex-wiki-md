# Crest crossings through a dispersive wave packet

↑ **Parent:** [Wave packet](wave-packet.md)

For a nearly monochromatic packet of length $L$ travelling past a stationary observer, the snapshot count is $L/\lambda$ but the crossing count is $fL/v_g=(L/\lambda)(v_p/v_g)$. In the envelope frame the observer moves at $-v_g$ and the crests at $v_p-v_g$. For [deep-water gravity waves](deep-water-gravity-wave.md), $v_p=2v_g$, so the observer counts twice as many crests as appear in an instantaneous snapshot. A sharply bounded monochromatic packet is an idealization; the count describes the approximately uniform central train.
