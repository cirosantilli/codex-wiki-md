# Katabatic wind

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katabatic_wind)

A katabatic wind is a downslope flow of cold dense air accelerated by gravity. Over Antarctic coasts its offshore component can export [sea ice](sea-ice.md) and maintain a [latent-heat polynya](latent-heat-polynya.md).
