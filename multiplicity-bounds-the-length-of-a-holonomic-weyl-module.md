# Multiplicity bounds the length of a holonomic Weyl module

↑ **Parent:** [Holonomic Weyl algebra module](holonomic-weyl-algebra-module.md)

Every nonzero subquotient of a [holonomic Weyl algebra module](holonomic-weyl-algebra-module.md) over a characteristic-zero [field](field.md) has dimension $n$, by [Bernstein inequality for Weyl algebra modules](bernstein-inequality-for-weyl-algebra-modules.md) and [dimension and multiplicity in a filtered exact sequence](dimension-and-multiplicity-in-a-filtered-exact-sequence.md). Each strict step in a [submodule](submodule.md) chain consumes positive [multiplicity of a filtered module](multiplicity-of-a-filtered-module.md). With a [standard graded algebra](standard-graded-algebra.md) normalization these multiplicities are integral. There are therefore at most $m(M)$ strict steps. The [module](module-mathematics.md) is [Artinian](artinian-ring.md) and [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md), and its [module length](length-of-a-module.md) is bounded by that multiplicity.
