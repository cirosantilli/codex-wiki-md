# Nested matching criterion for a regular subgraph

↑ **Parent:** [Regular graph](regular-graph.md)

Let $p$ be [prime](prime-number.md) and set $s=2p-2$. If a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) has edge-disjoint [perfect matchings](perfect-matching.md) $M_0,\ldots,M_{\sigma-1}$ on nested nonempty [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) sets, and $|V(M_{i+s})|>s|V(M_i)|/(s+1)$, then the union of $s+1$ consecutive [matchings in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md) has maximum degree at most $2p-1$ and more than $(p-1)|V(M_i)|$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). The [prime-regular subgraph from Boolean polynomial constraints](prime-regular-subgraph-from-boolean-polynomial-constraints.md) gives a $p$-regular subgraph. Its [perfect matching](perfect-matching.md) decomposition gives a $k$-regular subgraph for every $k\leq p$. If none exists, every $s$ steps shrink the [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) set by factor at most $s/(s+1)$, bounding $\sigma$ by a constant times $\log n$.
