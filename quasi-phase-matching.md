# Quasi-phase-matching

↑ **Parent:** [Phase matching](phase-matching.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-phase-matching)

Compensation of [phase mismatch](phase-mismatch.md) by a spatial modulation of the coupling coefficient. Write $d(z)=\sum_m d_m e^{imKz}$. The driven amplitude contains $e^{i(\Delta k+mK)z}$, so the selected Fourier component accumulates coherently when $\Delta k+mK=0$. For first-order compensation the full spatial period is $2\pi/|\Delta k|$, twice the [nonlinear optical coherence length](nonlinear-optical-coherence-length.md).

An ideal equal-duty pattern alternating $+d$ and $-d$ has first Fourier coefficient of magnitude $2d/\pi$. Hence its conversion intensity is $4/\pi^2$ of a uniformly phase-matched crystal using that same coefficient, under the same undepleted plane-wave assumptions. The modulation can nevertheless allow a larger tensor coefficient or an otherwise inaccessible wavelength. [Periodically poled lithium niobate](periodically-poled-lithium-niobate.md) implements such sign reversals using ferroelectric domains.
