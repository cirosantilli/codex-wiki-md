<h1 id="reidemeister-schreier-theorem">Reidemeister–Schreier theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schreier's lemma](schreier-s-lemma.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reidemeister–Schreier_theorem)

Given a presentation of a group and a Schreier transversal for a subgroup, the Reidemeister–Schreier theorem gives a presentation of the subgroup. Its generators are the Schreier generators $ts\overline{ts}^{-1}$, and its relators are rewritten conjugates of the original relators.
