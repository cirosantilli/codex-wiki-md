# Log modulus has no global harmonic conjugate on the punctured plane

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic conjugate](harmonic-conjugate.md)

The function $\log|z|$ is [harmonic](harmonic-function.md) on the [punctured complex plane](punctured-complex-plane.md), but it is not globally the real part of a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) there. Such a function would have derivative $1/z$, whose integral around the unit circle is $2\pi i$, whereas the integral of a derivative around a closed curve is zero.
