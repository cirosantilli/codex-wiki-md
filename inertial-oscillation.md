# Inertial oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inertial_oscillation)

An inertial oscillation is the horizontal circular motion of an unforced parcel in a rotating fluid. On an [f-plane](f-plane.md), $\dot u-fv=0$ and $\dot v+fu=0$, so the speed is constant and the angular frequency is $|f|$.
