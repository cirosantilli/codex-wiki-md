# Nematic director

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md)

A nematic director is a unit vector specifying the local unoriented axis of a [nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md). Head-tail symmetry identifies $\mathbf n$ with $-\mathbf n$, so the order-parameter space is a [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) rather than a sphere.
