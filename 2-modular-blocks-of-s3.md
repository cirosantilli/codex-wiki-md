# 2-modular blocks of S3

↑ **Parent:** [Block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md)

Over a [splitting field for finite group representations](splitting-field-for-finite-group-representations.md) of characteristic $2$, put $a=(123)$. The [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) idempotents of $kS_3$ are $b_0=1+a+a^2$ and $b_1=a+a^2$. Their block algebras are $b_0kS_3\cong kC_2$ and $b_1kS_3\cong M_2(k)$, with a [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) given by $C_2$ and $1$, respectively. The first is a [local ring](local-ring.md); for the second, the representation $a\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(\omega,\omega^2)$, $(12)\mapsto\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ generates the full matrix algebra. The [Cartan matrix of a group algebra](cartan-matrix-of-a-group-algebra.md) is $\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$, and the ordinary trivial, sign, and two-dimensional characters give [decomposition matrix](decomposition-matrix-modular-representation-theory.md) $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&0\\1&0\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
