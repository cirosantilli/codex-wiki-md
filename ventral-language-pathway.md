# Ventral language pathway

↑ **Parent:** [Language processing](language-processing.md)

Temporal and temporofrontal systems supporting transformations from [speech](speech.md) signals toward lexical and conceptual interpretation. Models differ on the precise anatomy and levels of representation included; bilateral [speech](speech.md) analysis is compatible with stronger lateralization of some higher-level operations.
