# Shifted gamma distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md)

A [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) translated by a real constant $k$. With shape $\alpha>0$ and rate $\nu>0$, its support is $(k,\infty)$ and its first three [cumulants](cumulant.md) are $k+\alpha/\nu$, $\alpha/\nu^2$, and $2\alpha/\nu^3$. Its [skewness](skewness.md) is $2/\sqrt\alpha$. A negative shift permits negative values; introducing a shift does not automatically respect the nonnegative support of an insurance loss.
