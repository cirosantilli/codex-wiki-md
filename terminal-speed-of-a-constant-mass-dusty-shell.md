# Terminal speed of a constant-mass dusty shell

↑ **Parent:** [Dusty radiation-pressure-driven shell](dusty-radiation-pressure-driven-shell.md)

A fixed-mass shell with ultraviolet opacity $\kappa_{\rm UV}$ has [dust transparency radius](dust-transparency-radius.md) $R_{\rm UV}=\sqrt{\kappa_{\rm UV}M_{\rm sh}/(4\pi)}$. For an initially expanding shell driven by constant ultraviolet luminosity, a sharp opaque-to-transparent approximation gives

$$
v_\infty^2=v_0^2+\frac{2GM}{R_0}\left[\Gamma_{\rm ss}(2X-1)+\Gamma_{\rm IR}(1-X^{-1})-1\right],
\qquad X=R_{\rm UV}/R_0.
$$

Here $\Gamma_{\rm ss}=LR_0^2/(GM M_{\rm sh}c)$ is the initial single-scattering force ratio and $\Gamma_{\rm IR}=\kappa_{\rm IR}L/(4\pi GMc)$. The trajectory must actually reach $R_{\rm UV}$. Large initial ultraviolet [optical depth](optical-depth.md) enhances the radiative work at fixed initial single-scattering ratio; the same conclusion does not follow unconditionally at fixed electron-scattering luminosity.
