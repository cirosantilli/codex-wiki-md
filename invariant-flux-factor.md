# Invariant flux factor

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic scattering cross-section](relativistic-scattering-cross-section.md)

For two incident particles in relativistic normalization, this is $2\sqrt{\kappa(s,m_1^2,m_2^2)}$, where $\kappa$ is the [Källén function](kallen-function.md). For equal masses it is $2\sqrt{s(s-4m^2)}$.
