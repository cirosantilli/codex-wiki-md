# Uniqueness theorem for the electrostatic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Uniqueness of an electrostatic dielectric boundary-value problem](uniqueness-of-an-electrostatic-dielectric-boundary-value-problem.md)

In a vacuum domain with specified [charge density](charge-density.md), fixed boundary [electric potential](electric-potential.md) and the appropriate condition at infinity, two solutions of the [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) have difference $w$ satisfying $\nabla^2w=0$ with zero boundary data. Integration by parts gives $\int|\nabla w|^2dV=0$; hence their [electric fields](electric-field-split.md) coincide, and their potentials coincide after fixing the constant. This is the Dirichlet case of the more general dielectric uniqueness result. It justifies the exterior field constructed by the [method of images](method-of-images.md).
