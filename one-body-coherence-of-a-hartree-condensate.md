# One-body coherence of a Hartree condensate

↑ **Parent:** [One-body density matrix](one-body-density-matrix.md)

For the normalized [Hartree product state](hartree-product-state.md) $|N:\phi\rangle$, applying the [bosonic annihilation operator](bosonic-annihilation-operator.md) gives $\psi(y)|N:\phi\rangle=\sqrt N\phi(y)|N-1:\phi\rangle$. Taking the inner product proves the displayed factorization of the [one-body density matrix](one-body-density-matrix.md). Its diagonal is the total [number density](number-density.md) in this approximation. For a real positive [orbital](single-particle-orbital.md), $G^{(1)}(0,r)=\sqrt{n(0)n(r)}$. Restoring the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) in a [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) can smooth a [Thomas–Fermi approximation for a condensate](thomas-fermi-approximation-for-a-condensate.md) edge while retaining this factorization. Describing [quantum depletion of a Bose condensate](quantum-depletion-of-a-bose-condensate.md) additionally requires noncondensate mode occupations and their contribution to the [one-body density matrix](one-body-density-matrix.md).
