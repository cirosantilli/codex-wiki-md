# Tortoise coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md)

A [tortoise coordinate](tortoise-coordinate.md) straightens radial null directions. For a static radial metric $-F(r)dt^2+G(r)dr^2$ outside a horizon, choose $dr_*/dr=\sqrt{G/F}$; then $t\pm r_*$ are constant along the corresponding radial [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md). Continuation across horizons requires choosing branches consistently rather than replacing an analytic derivative by its absolute value.
