# Substitutional semiconductor doping

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md)

[Substitutional semiconductor doping](substitutional-semiconductor-doping.md) replaces selected host atoms with impurity atoms on ordinary lattice sites. A shallow [donor impurity](donor-impurity.md) can supply a mobile conduction electron and remain positively charged; a shallow [acceptor impurity](acceptor-impurity.md) can capture an electron and leave a mobile valence hole, remaining negatively charged. In nondegenerate, substantially ionized material, donor-dominated doping raises the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) toward the conduction edge and acceptor-dominated doping lowers it toward the valence edge. Dopants do not create net bulk charge in a neutral sample: fixed impurity charge compensates the mobile carrier charge. Freeze-out, compensation and degeneracy qualify the simplest relation between dopant and carrier densities.
