# Trypsinogen

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

A precursor of a digestive [enzyme](enzyme-split.md). Portions of an ancestral trypsinogen [gene](gene.md) contributed to the origin of an Antarctic fish [antifreeze glycoprotein](antifreeze-glycoprotein.md); this does not mean that the new product retained the original digestive function.
