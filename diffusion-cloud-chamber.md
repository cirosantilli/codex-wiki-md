# Diffusion cloud chamber

↑ **Parent:** [Cloud chamber](cloud-chamber.md)

A [diffusion cloud chamber](diffusion-cloud-chamber.md) continuously supplies [vapor](vapor.md) from a warmer reservoir toward a colder base. A [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md) permits [supersaturated vapor](supersaturated-vapor.md) in a layer near the base, where new [ionization](ionization.md) trails can continually form drops. There is no repeated bulk [piston](piston.md) expansion cycle. The two instruments share [ion](ion.md)-assisted [condensation](condensation.md), but their sensitive regions and timing differ.

CERN's modern teaching apparatus uses alcohol-soaked felt, a sealed transparent container, a dark metal base cooled with dry [solid](solid.md) [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md), and a side light. The view is a thin sensitive region near the cold base, rather than a chamber-wide expansion photograph.[CERN PER Team, DIY Cloud Chamber, Description and Material list](diy-cloud-chamber-by-cern-per-team.md)[Barradas-Solas and Alameda-Meléndez, Bringing particle physics to life: build your own cloud chamber (29 April 2010), “The continuously sensitive diffusion cloud chamber” and “Method”, steps 5–6](bringing-particle-physics-to-life-build-your-own-cloud-chamber-by-francisco-barradas-solas-and-paloma-alameda-melendez.md)
