# Sheaf of differential forms of type (p, q)

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of smooth differential forms](sheaf-of-smooth-differential-forms.md)

On a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md), this [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) consists of smooth [differential forms of type (p, q)](differential-form-of-type-p-q.md). The [type decomposition of the complexified tangent bundle](type-decomposition-of-the-complexified-tangent-bundle.md) gives $\mathcal A^k=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}\mathcal A^{p,q}$.
