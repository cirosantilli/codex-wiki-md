# Cyclically reduced sequence in an HNN extension

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced sequence in an HNN extension](reduced-sequence-in-an-hnn-extension.md)

A cyclically reduced sequence has no [pinch in an HNN extension](pinch-in-an-hnn-extension.md) either internally or across its cyclic junction. If its stable-letter length is positive, every positive power remains reduced, so [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md) gives infinite order. Repeated cyclic conjugation and pinch removal shows that any finite-order element of an [HNN extension](hnn-extension.md) is conjugate into its base [group](group-split.md).
