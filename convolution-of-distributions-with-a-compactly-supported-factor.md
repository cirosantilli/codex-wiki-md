# Convolution of distributions with a compactly supported factor

↑ **Parent:** [Convolution](convolution.md)

If $v$ is a [compactly supported distribution](compactly-supported-distribution.md) and $u$ is any [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md), define $\langle u*v,\phi\rangle=\langle u_x,\langle v_y,\phi(x+y)\rangle\rangle$. The inner function is smooth and supported in $\operatorname{supp}\phi-\operatorname{supp}v$, hence is a [test function](test-function.md) for $u$. Finite-order estimates prove continuity, while the [tensor product of distributions](tensor-product-of-distributions.md) proves commutativity after inserting compact cutoffs. For every [test function](test-function.md) $\psi$, $(u*v)*\psi=u*(v*\psi)$; evaluating at zero against reflected [test functions](test-function.md) proves uniqueness. If both [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) have [compact support](compact-support.md), $\operatorname{supp}(u*v)\subset\operatorname{supp}u+\operatorname{supp}v$ and the [convolution theorem](convolution-theorem.md) gives $\widehat{u*v}=\widehat u\widehat v$. Without a compact factor or another support/growth condition, arbitrary distributional [convolution](convolution.md) is not generally defined.
