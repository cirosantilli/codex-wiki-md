# Rouse model

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

An overdamped bead-spring model of [polymer](polymer-split.md) dynamics with local friction and no long-range [hydrodynamic interaction](hydrodynamic-interaction.md). For an ideal unentangled chain of $N$ identical frictional units, the centre-of-mass [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) is proportional to $N^{-1}$ and the longest internal relaxation time to $N^2$. A tagged segment has an intermediate-time mean-square displacement proportional to $t^{1/2}$ before ordinary whole-chain diffusion dominates. Entanglement adds constraints beyond this model.
