# Vector areas of a closed polyhedron sum to zero

↑ **Parent:** [Vector area](vector-area.md)

For the faces of a closed [polyhedron](polyhedron.md), take outward [unit normal vectors](unit-normal.md). Applying the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) to an arbitrary constant [vector field](vector-field.md) $c$ gives

$$
c\cdot\sum_f A_f\hat{\mathbf n}_f
=\int_{\partial\Omega}c\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,dA
=\int_\Omega\nabla\cdot c\,dV=0.
$$

Since $c$ is arbitrary, the [vector areas](vector-area.md) sum to zero. This is an oriented statement; the positive scalar [surface areas](surface-area.md) do not cancel. The same proof works for every piecewise smooth closed boundary to which the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) applies.
