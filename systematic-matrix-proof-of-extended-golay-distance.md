# Systematic-matrix proof of extended Golay distance

↑ **Parent:** [Extended binary Golay code](extended-binary-golay-code.md)

Suppose a binary [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $A$ satisfies $A^2=I$, has one row of [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md) eleven and eleven rows of [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md) seven, and every sum of two distinct rows has [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md) six. Then $(I\mid A)$ generates a [self-dual code](self-dual-code.md). Its rows have weights twelve or eight; their mutual orthogonality makes every generated word [doubly even](doubly-even-code.md). A weight-four word $(u,uA)$ would have left-half weight one, two, three or four. The first two cases are excluded by the stated row weights; weight three would force $uA$ to be a unit vector and hence $u$ to be a row of $A$; weight four would force $uA=0$. Thus weight four is impossible and the [minimum Hamming distance](minimum-distance-of-a-code.md) is eight.
