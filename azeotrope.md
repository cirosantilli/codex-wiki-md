# Azeotrope

↑ **Parent:** [Distillation](distillation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azeotrope)

An [azeotrope](azeotrope.md) has equal coexisting [liquid](liquid.md) and [vapor](vapor.md) compositions: $y=x$. Ordinary [fractional distillation](fractional-distillation.md) cannot cross this composition boundary at fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md); more packing does not remove it. A constant head [temperature](temperature-split.md) therefore does not establish a pure [chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md).[Chem2O06 Laboratory Manual by McMaster University; Liquids that Form Azeotropes, constant-temperature mixtures and limits on separation](chem2o06-laboratory-manual-by-mcmaster-university/distillation-introduction.md) The stipulated $\alpha=2$ model has $y>x$ throughout $0<x<1$, so it has no interior [azeotrope](azeotrope.md) and cannot represent this limit.
