# Newtonian normalization of the Einstein field equations

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md)

Assume a metric theory in four dimensions, a [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), covariant [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) and field equations linear in second metric derivatives. The [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md) selects $G_{ab}=R_{ab}-g_{ab}R/2$. Its trace-reversed equation is $R_{ab}=\kappa(T_{ab}-g_{ab}T/2)+\Lambda g_{ab}$. In the convention $R_{00}\simeq-\nabla^2\varphi$ and signature $(+---)$, nonrelativistic matter gives $R_{00}\simeq\kappa\rho/2$. The [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) $\nabla^2\varphi=4\pi G\rho$ fixes $\kappa=-8\pi G$. Reversing the curvature convention reverses this displayed coupling sign without changing the physical theory. The [strong equivalence principle](strong-equivalence-principle.md) alone does not establish uniqueness without the stated additional assumptions.
