# Zeldovich approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological structure formation](cosmological-structure-formation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeldovich_approximation)

The Zeldovich approximation maps a fluid element from its [Lagrangian coordinate](lagrangian-coordinate.md) $\mathbf q$ to the comoving position $\mathbf x=\mathbf q+D(t)\mathbf s(\mathbf q)$, where $D$ is the [linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md). It follows the initial gravitational displacement along a straight comoving trajectory until trajectories cross.
