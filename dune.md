# Dune

↑ **Parent:** [Sediment transport](sediment-transport.md)

A [dune](dune.md) is a sediment bedform. In the elementary [subcritical flow](subcritical-flow.md) description of river dunes, bed undulations and free-surface undulations are out of phase. This phase relation identifies a hydraulic response; instability additionally requires a positive real part of the relevant [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). Contrast the in-phase surface of an [antidune](antidune.md).
