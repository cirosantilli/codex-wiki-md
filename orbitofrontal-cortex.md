# Orbitofrontal cortex

↑ **Parent:** [Cerebral cortex](cerebral-cortex.md)

A frontal cortical region involved in several sensory and value-related processes. Taste–odor mixture contrasts can reveal interactions within its measured activity, but voxel overlap does not demonstrate convergence in every [neuron](neuron-split.md) and a value correlation is not itself a causal account.
