# Boron trichloride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

Molecular $\mathrm{BCl_3}$ has a trigonal-planar sigma framework and ligand-to-boron [pi donation](pi-donation.md). Association energetics differ from those of [aluminium chloride](aluminium-chloride.md).
