# Doubly connected edge list

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary representation](boundary-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubly_connected_edge_list)

A mesh representation with two oppositely directed records per ordinary edge. Each record stores its origin [vertex of a polyhedron](vertex-of-a-polyhedron.md), incident face, next face-boundary record and opposite record; previous records are often cached. The opposite record's origin gives the destination. This usage differs from a dangling [half-edge of a graph](half-edge-of-a-graph.md).
