# Imaginary quadratic field

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic field](quadratic-field.md)

An imaginary quadratic field is a [quadratic number field](quadratic-field.md) with no real embedding. Its two complex embeddings are interchanged by complex conjugation. The [endomorphism ring of an elliptic curve](endomorphism-ring-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) in characteristic zero, when larger than the integers, is an order in such a field; this is [complex multiplication](complex-multiplication.md).
