# Fugacity coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical fugacity](chemical-fugacity.md)

The dimensionless ratio of a gas species' [chemical fugacity](chemical-fugacity.md) to its pressure in the pure-fluid convention. Its [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) relative to a gas reference pressure $P^\circ$ is $\phi P/P^\circ$. For mixtures the pressure denominator uses the species' partial pressure. The ideal-gas limit has $\phi=1$.
