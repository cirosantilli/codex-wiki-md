<h1 id="a/4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Suppose $T_n$ are compact and $\|T_n-T\|\to0$. For any $\varepsilon>0$ take $n$ with $\|T_n-T\|<\varepsilon$. A finite $\varepsilon$-net of $T_n$ of the [unit ball](../../../../../unit-ball.md) is then a finite $2\varepsilon$-net of its $T$ image. That image is [totally bounded](../../../../../totally-bounded-space.md), so $T$ is compact by the stated criterion. Hence **$\mathcal K(X)$ is [norm](../../../../../norm.md) closed in $B(X)$**.
