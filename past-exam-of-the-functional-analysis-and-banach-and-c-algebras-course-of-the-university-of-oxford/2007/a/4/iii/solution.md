<h1 id="a/4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Extract a subsequence whose $T$ images converge, and apply continuity of the bounded operator $S$ to those images. This proves **$ST$ is compact**. The reverse composition $TS$ is compact as well, since $S$ sends a bounded sequence to a bounded sequence to which compactness of $T$ applies.
