<h1 id="a/4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Given a bounded sequence, first pass to a subsequence on which the images under $T_1$ converge. From that subsequence extract another on which the images under $T_2$ converge. Both image sequences, and hence their sum, converge on the latter subsequence. Thus **$T_1+T_2$ is compact**.
