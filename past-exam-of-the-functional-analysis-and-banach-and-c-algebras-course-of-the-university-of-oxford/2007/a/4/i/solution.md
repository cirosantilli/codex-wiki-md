<h1 id="a/4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [bounded linear operator](../../../../../continuous-linear-operator.md) is compact when the closure of the image of its [unit ball](../../../../../unit-ball.md) is compact. Equivalently every bounded sequence has a subsequence whose images converge; on a Banach target it also suffices that the image of its [unit ball](../../../../../unit-ball.md) be [totally bounded](../../../../../totally-bounded-space.md).

For a [finite-rank operator](../../../../../finite-rank-operator.md) that image is bounded in a finite-dimensional subspace, whose closed bounded sets are compact. Thus **every [finite-rank operator](../../../../../finite-rank-operator.md) is compact**.
