↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**Compact.** Let $P_N$ keep the first $N$ coordinates. The operator $P_NT$ has finite rank and $\|T-P_NT\|\leq1/(N+1)$, since the remaining diagonal coefficients are at most that number. [Norm](../../../../../norm.md)-closedness of [compact operators](../../../../../compact-operator-split.md) proves the claim.
