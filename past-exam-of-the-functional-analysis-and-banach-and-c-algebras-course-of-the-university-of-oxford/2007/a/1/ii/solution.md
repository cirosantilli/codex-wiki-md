<h1 id="a/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The kernel is globally Lipschitz with constant $1/\delta^2$: this holds on each of its linear pieces, and summing across the finitely many intervening breakpoints proves it across pieces as well. Consequently

$$
\boxed{|(f*g)(t_1)-(f*g)(t_2)|\leq\int|f(s)|\frac{|t_1-t_2|}{\delta^2}ds=\|f\|_1\frac{|t_1-t_2|}{\delta^2}.}
$$
