<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If the two ambient topologies are not comparable, neither is contained in the other. By the definition of inclusion of topologies, there is a $\tau_1$-open set absent from $\tau_2$, and a $\tau_2$-open set absent from $\tau_1$. These are the requested $U_1,U_2$, giving alternative (b). Together with the comparable case, this proves that at least one of the two alternatives always holds; it does not assume that the alternatives are mutually exclusive.
