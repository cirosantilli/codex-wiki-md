In the infimum defining $q$, choose $k=1,j_1=1$. A finite shift leaves the [limit superior](../../../../../limit-superior.md) unchanged, so

$$
\boxed{q(x)\leq\limsup_n\alpha_{n+1}=\limsup_n\alpha_n.}
$$
