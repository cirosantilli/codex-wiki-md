↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The extension already agrees with the usual limit on $c$. Alternatively, applying the domination inequality to both $x$ and $-x$ gives

$$
\liminf_n\alpha_n\leq\varphi(x)\leq\limsup_n\alpha_n.
$$

When the sequence converges, both endpoints are its limit, proving **$\varphi(x)=\lim\alpha_n$**. These bounds also give $\|\varphi\|=1$ and positivity on nonnegative sequences, but do not by themselves assert shift invariance.
