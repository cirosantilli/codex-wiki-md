The natural [canonical embedding into the bidual](../../../../../canonical-embedding-into-the-bidual.md) is $J_Xx(f)=f(x)$. It is linear and $\|J_Xx\|\leq\|x\|$. A norming functional from the [Hahn-Banach theorem](../../../../../hahn-banach-theorem.md) gives the reverse inequality, so it is an [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md). A [reflexive Banach space](../../../../../reflexive-banach-space.md) is one for which this canonical embedding is onto.

**A nonreflexive example is $c_0$.** Its dual is $\ell^1$ and its bidual is $\ell^\infty$; the canonical map is the natural inclusion, which misses the constant sequence one. This also shows why merely having an isometric embedding in the bidual is insufficient.
