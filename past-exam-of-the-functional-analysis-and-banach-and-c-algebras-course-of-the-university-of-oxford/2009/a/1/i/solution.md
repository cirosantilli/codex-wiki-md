<h1 id="a/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [quotient norm](../../../../../quotient-norm.md) is

$$
\boxed{\|x+Y\|_{X/Y}=\inf_{y\in Y}\|x-y\|.}
$$

Changing the representative does not change the infimum. The [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) and absolute homogeneity follow from those of the original [norm](../../../../../norm.md). Vanishing means $x$ belongs to the closure of $Y$, hence to $Y$ because the subspace is closed. Thus this is a [norm](../../../../../norm.md).

Use the absolutely convergent series criterion for completeness. If $\sum\|\xi_n\|_{X/Y}<\infty$, choose representatives $z_n$ of $\xi_n$ satisfying $\|z_n\|\leq\|\xi_n\|_{X/Y}+2^{-n}$. The series $\sum z_n$ converges in the [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md) $X$ to some $z$. Since the [quotient map](../../../../../quotient-map.md) is contractive, $\sum\xi_n$ converges to $z+Y$. Consequently **the quotient is a [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md)**.
