↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**(iii) implies (ii):** the preliminary bounds show $\sup_k\|P_kx\|<\infty$ for every $x$. Applying the [Uniform boundedness principle](../../../../../uniform-boundedness-principle.md) to the bounded maps $P_k$ gives $\sup_k\|P_k\|<\infty$.

**(iii) implies (iv):** the normalization $\|x_n\|=1$ gives

$$
|\varphi_n(x)|=\|(P_n-P_{n-1})x\|\leq(\|P_n\|+\|P_{n-1}\|)\|x\|.
$$

Hence every coordinate functional is bounded.
