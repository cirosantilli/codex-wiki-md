↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**(v) implies (iv).** Fix $n$ and consider any sequence $y_r\to y$ with $\varphi_n(y_r)\to\gamma$. Apply (v) to $y_r-y$ and $\beta=\gamma-\varphi_n(y)$. It gives $\gamma=\varphi_n(y)$. Thus the graph of $\varphi_n:X\to\mathbb K$ is closed. Both spaces are Banach, so the [closed graph theorem](../../../../../closed-graph-theorem.md) makes $\varphi_n$ bounded.
