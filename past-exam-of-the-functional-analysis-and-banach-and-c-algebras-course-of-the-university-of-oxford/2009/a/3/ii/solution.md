**(ii) implies (i):** together with the preliminary estimate, (ii) gives [equivalent norms](../../../../../equivalent-norms.md),

$$
\|x\|\leq\|x\|'\leq C\|x\|.
$$

An equivalent [norm](../../../../../norm.md) on a [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md) is complete: a primed-[norm](../../../../../norm.md) [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) has an original-[norm](../../../../../norm.md) limit, and the upper bound makes convergence hold also in the primed [norm](../../../../../norm.md). **(ii) implies (iii)** immediately because $\|P_k\|\leq C$ for every $k$.
