↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

**(vi) implies (iv)** without assuming beforehand that any coordinate functional is continuous. Define the everywhere-defined [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md)

$$
\Phi:X\to\ell^\infty,\qquad\Phi(x)=(\varphi_n(x))_{n\geq1}.
$$

The preliminary coordinate bound ensures it really takes values in $\ell^\infty$. Condition (vi) states exactly that its graph is sequentially closed: $y_r\to x$ and $\Phi(y_r)\to\beta$ in the supremum [norm](../../../../../norm.md) imply $\Phi(x)=\beta$. The product is a metric space, so sequentially closed means closed. The [closed graph theorem](../../../../../closed-graph-theorem.md) therefore makes $\Phi$ bounded. Every coordinate projection on $\ell^\infty$ is bounded, hence so is each $\varphi_n$.

Together with the preceding implications this proves **all six conditions are equivalent**. No continuity of the coordinate functionals was assumed during the preliminary bounds.
