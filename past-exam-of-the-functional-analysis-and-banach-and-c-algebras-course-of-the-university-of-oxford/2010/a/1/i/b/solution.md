<h1 id="a/1/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [quotient operator](../../../../../../quotient-operator.md) is a [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) [bounded linear operator](../../../../../../continuous-linear-operator.md) that is an [open map](../../../../../../open-map.md). For a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md), being an [open map](../../../../../../open-map.md) is equivalent to the existence of $c>0$ such that

$$
B_Y(0,c)\subseteq T(B_X(0,1)).
$$

The inclusion gives a neighbourhood of $Tx$ inside the [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of every sufficiently small ball about $x$, by translation and scaling. Conversely, an [open map](../../../../../../open-map.md) sends $B_X(0,1)$ to a neighbourhood of zero. A linear [open map](../../../../../../open-map.md) onto $Y$ is automatically [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), since its [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) contains a ball and is a [linear subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md).

The [Banach-space adjoint](../../../../../../transpose-of-a-bounded-linear-operator.md) is $T^*g=g\circ T$. For $g\in Y^*$, the ball inclusion gives

$$
\|T^*g\|=\sup_{\|x\|<1}|g(Tx)|\geq\sup_{\|y\|<c}|g(y)|=c\|g\|.
$$

Also $\|T^*g\|\leq\|T\|\|g\|$. Thus **the [Banach-space adjoint](../../../../../../transpose-of-a-bounded-linear-operator.md) is an [isomorphic embedding](../../../../../../isomorphic-embedding.md)**, with lower bound $c$.
