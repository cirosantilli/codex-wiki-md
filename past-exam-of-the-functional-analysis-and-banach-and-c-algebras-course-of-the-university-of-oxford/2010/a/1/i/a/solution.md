<h1 id="a/1/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [isomorphic embedding](../../../../../../isomorphic-embedding.md) of [normed vector spaces](../../../../../../normed-vector-space-split.md) is a [bounded linear operator](../../../../../../continuous-linear-operator.md) that is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) and whose [inverse function](../../../../../../inverse-function.md) on its [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) is bounded. Equivalently, **$T$ is bounded below**: there is $c>0$ such that

$$
\boxed{c\|x\|\leq\|Tx\|\quad(x\in X).}
$$

Indeed this inequality gives [injectivity](../../../../../../injective-function.md) and $\|T^{-1}z\|\leq c^{-1}\|z\|$ for $z\in T(X)$; the converse follows by applying the boundedness of $T^{-1}$ to $Tx$. Together with $\|Tx\|\leq\|T\|\|x\|$, it makes $T$ an [isomorphic embedding](../../../../../../isomorphic-embedding.md) onto its [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) with the inherited [norm](../../../../../../norm.md). The [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) is necessarily closed when $X$ is a [Banach space](../../../../../../banach-space-split.md); that extra conclusion requires completeness.
