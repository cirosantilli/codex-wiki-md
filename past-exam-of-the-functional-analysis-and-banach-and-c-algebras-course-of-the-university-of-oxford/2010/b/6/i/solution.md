<h1 id="b/6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $c=x(1_A)\geq0$. Positivity on $a^*a$ implies positivity on every [Positive element of a C-star algebra](../../../../../positive-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md), by taking its positive [square root](../../../../../square-root.md). For a [self-adjoint C-star element](../../../../../hermitian-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) $h$, the [positive cone of a C-star algebra](../../../../../positive-cone-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) gives

$$
-\|h\|1_A\leq h\leq\|h\|1_A.
$$

Both $x(\|h\|1_A+h)$ and $x(\|h\|1_A-h)$ are nonnegative real numbers. Their difference shows $x(h)$ is real, and the same inequalities give $|x(h)|\leq c\|h\|$.

For an arbitrary $a\in A$, write $a=h+ik$ with $h=(a+a^*)/2$ and $k=(a-a^*)/(2i)$ self-adjoint. Since the [involution](../../../../../involution.md) is isometric, $\|h\|,\|k\|\leq\|a\|$. Hence

$$
|x(a)|\leq|x(h)|+|x(k)|\leq2c\|a\|.
$$

Thus **every [positive functional on a C-star algebra](../../../../../positive-functional-on-a-c-star-algebra.md) is bounded**, with the preliminary bound $\|x\|\leq2x(1_A)$. This proof does not presuppose the [Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for positive C-star functionals](../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality-for-positive-c-star-functionals.md). It also gives $x(a^*)=\overline{x(a)}$, since $x(h)$ and $x(k)$ are real.
