# Teleological response of a Vaidya event horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Vaidya metric](vaidya-metric.md)

For a small bounded mass perturbation $m(v)=M_0+\mu(v)$ that becomes constant in the future, write the [event horizon](event-horizon.md) radius as $r_H(v)=2M_0+\rho(v)$. The outgoing [radial null geodesics of the Vaidya metric](radial-null-geodesics-of-the-vaidya-metric.md) give $\rho'-\rho/(4M_0)=-\mu/(2M_0)$ to first order. Future stationarity selects the displayed solution and removes the exponentially growing homogeneous solution. If $\mu'\geq0$, integration by parts gives $\rho-2\mu=2\int_v^\infty e^{-(s-v)/(4M_0)}\mu'(s)\,ds\geq0$. Thus the [event horizon](event-horizon.md) can grow before local matter arrives, lying outside the instantaneous spherical [apparent horizon](apparent-horizon.md) $r=2m(v)$. This is a consequence of the [event horizon](event-horizon.md)'s global definition, not propagation of a local signal into the past.
