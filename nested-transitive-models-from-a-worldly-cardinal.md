# Nested transitive models from a worldly cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Worldly cardinal](worldly-cardinal.md)

A [worldly cardinal](worldly-cardinal.md) permits a [countable transitive model](countable-transitive-model.md) $M$ of [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) and a [transitive model](transitive-model.md) $N$ of [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) with $M\in N$, $|N|=\aleph_1$, and $(\omega_1)^N=\omega_1$. First collapse a countable [elementary substructure](elementary-substructure.md) of $V_\kappa$. Then collapse an $\aleph_1$-sized [Skolem hull](skolem-hull.md) containing every countable ordinal and every member of $M$, as well as $M$ itself. The [transitive collapse fixes transitive subsets](transitive-collapse-fixes-transitive-subsets.md) lemma preserves $M$ and those ordinals.
