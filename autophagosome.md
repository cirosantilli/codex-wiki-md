# Autophagosome

↑ **Parent:** [Macroautophagy](macroautophagy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autophagosome)

An [autophagosome](autophagosome.md) is the membrane-enclosed carrier formed around [macroautophagy](macroautophagy.md) cargo. Enclosing a [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md) does not immediately digest it: subsequent delivery must expose the cargo to digestive [enzymes](enzyme-split.md) in [lysosomes](lysosome.md).[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Phagocytosis and Autophagy, autophagosome fusion and digestion, Figure 9.37](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/lysosomes.md)
