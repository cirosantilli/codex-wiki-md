# Fock-Darwin spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum dot](quantum-dot.md)

For a circular two-dimensional parabolic dot in perpendicular [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), orbital levels are $\hbar\Omega(2n+|\ell|+1)\pm\ell\hbar\omega_c/2$, with $\Omega^2=\omega_0^2+\omega_c^2/4$. The sign depends on the angular-momentum and signed-charge convention. At zero field the shell degeneracy is $q+1$ before [spin](spin.md); Zeeman and interaction terms require separate treatment.
