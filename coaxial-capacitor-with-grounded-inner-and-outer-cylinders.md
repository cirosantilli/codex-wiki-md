# Coaxial capacitor with grounded inner and outer cylinders

↑ **Parent:** [Coaxial cylindrical capacitor](coaxial-cylindrical-capacitor.md)

A driven cylindrical conductor between two grounded coaxial conductors forms two [coaxial cylindrical capacitors](coaxial-cylindrical-capacitor.md) in parallel. For radii $a,\lambda a,2a$, the [capacitance per unit length](capacitance-per-unit-length.md) is the sum $C'=2\pi\epsilon_0[1/\log\lambda+1/\log(2/\lambda)]$. Writing $u=\log\lambda$ and $L=\log2$, the function $1/u+1/(L-u)$ is strictly [convex](convex-function.md) on $(0,L)$ and symmetric about $L/2$. Hence $C'$ has its unique minimum at $\lambda=\sqrt2$, with value $8\pi\epsilon_0/\log2$. As $\lambda=1+\delta$ approaches the inner conductor, $C'\sim2\pi\epsilon_0/\delta$, agreeing with the local [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md) approximation.
