# Local-density sound speed of a condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Thomas–Fermi approximation for a condensate](thomas-fermi-approximation-for-a-condensate.md)

In occupied bulk regions where density-gradient [energy](energy-split.md) is negligible, the stationary relation $g\rho=\mu-V$ gives [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md) $c=\sqrt{g\rho/m}$. Empty regions do not have an imaginary physical [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md); the bulk condensate formula is simply inapplicable there.
