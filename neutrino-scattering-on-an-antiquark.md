# Neutrino scattering on an antiquark

↑ **Parent:** [Weak charged-current quark scattering](weak-charged-current-quark-scattering.md)

For a massless [neutrino](neutrino.md) scattering on an unpolarized up [antiquark](antiquark.md) through the [Fermi interaction](fermi-interaction.md), $d\sigma/dy=G_F^2s(1-y)^2/\pi$. The corresponding down-quark channel has $d\sigma/dy=G_F^2s/\pi$. Their difference follows from the [chiral weak-current trace contraction](chiral-weak-current-trace-contraction.md). The [parton distribution functions](parton-distribution-function.md) weight these rates in a hadron.
