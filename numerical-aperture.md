# Numerical aperture

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction-limited system](diffraction-limited-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_aperture)

The [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md) is $n\sin\alpha$, where $n$ is the [refractive index](refractive-index.md) of the medium between object and objective, and $\alpha$ is the half-[angle](angle.md) of the admitted cone of [light](light.md). Specify object-side or image-side [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md); the microscope convention usually means the object side.[Numerical Aperture by Michael W. Davidson](numerical-aperture-by-michael-w-davidson.md)
