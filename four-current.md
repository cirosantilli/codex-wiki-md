# Four-current

↑ **Parent:** [Current density](current-density.md)

The four-current combines charge density and [current density](current-density.md) into the [four-vector](four-vector.md) $J^\mu=(c\rho,\boldsymbol J)$. With $x^\mu=(ct,\boldsymbol x)$, its [four-divergence](four-divergence.md) vanishes by the charge [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md): $\partial_\mu J^\mu=\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol J=0$. It is the source in the covariant [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations-split.md).
