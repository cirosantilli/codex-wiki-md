# Light-scalar box matching for a charge-two field

↑ **Parent:** [Charge-two cubic complex-scalar interaction](charge-two-cubic-complex-scalar-interaction.md)

For the [charge-two cubic complex-scalar interaction](charge-two-cubic-complex-scalar-interaction.md), integrating out a [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) of mass $m$ generates the displayed local [effective Lagrangian](effective-lagrangian.md) term when external energies and momenta are small compared with $m$. A constant $\Phi$ gives two real squared masses $m^2\pm g|\Phi|$. Expanding half the sum of their one-loop logarithms gives $V_1\supset-g^4|\Phi|^4I_4/4$, with the [Euclidean massive loop integral](euclidean-massive-loop-integral.md) $I_4=1/(96\pi^2m^4)$. Since $\mathcal L_{\rm eff}=-V_1$ for a constant field, $2!2!$ external attachments give the [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) $g^4/(96\pi^2m^4)$ for either heavy-heavy or heavy-antiparticle elastic scattering. This agrees with the unique charge-allowed light [box diagram](box-diagram.md).
