# Dense sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Sieve (category theory)](sieve-category-theory.md)

A sieve $R$ on $A$ is dense when every arrow $f:B\to A$ has a further composite $fh$ in $R$. Equivalently, every pullback sieve $f^*R$ is nonempty. This condition is stable under pullback and transitive refinement and defines the [double-negation topology](double-negation-topology.md).
