# Exponential-kernel half-line eigenfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Wiener-Hopf equation](wiener-hopf-equation.md)

The equation $\int_0^\infty e^{-|x-t|}f(t)\,dt=f(x)$ has this solution family in the ordinary bounded-growth transform class. Differentiating twice gives $f''=-f$, and the integral's right derivative at zero gives $f'(0)=f(0)$. Alternatively its positive-support [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) is $A(ik-1)/(k^2-1)$; inversion above both real poles gives the same answer.
