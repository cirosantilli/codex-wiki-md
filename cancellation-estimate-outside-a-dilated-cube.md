# Cancellation estimate outside a dilated cube

↑ **Parent:** [Hörmander integral kernel condition](hormander-integral-kernel-condition.md)

If $K$ obeys the [Hörmander integral kernel condition](hormander-integral-kernel-condition.md) with constant $C$, and an integrable $b$ is supported in a cube $Q$ with $\int b=0$, then $\int_{(LQ)^c}|K*b|\leq C\|b\|_1$ for $L=4\sqrt d$. Here $LQ$ is the concentric cube with multiplied side length. Subtract the kernel value at the cube center inside the [convolution](convolution.md); outside $LQ$, every source displacement is smaller than half the observation displacement. [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) and the integral kernel condition give the bound.
