# Structure isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Structure embedding](structure-embedding.md)

A structure isomorphism is a bijective [structure embedding](structure-embedding.md): it preserves constants and functions, and both preserves and reflects relations. Its inverse has the same properties. It therefore preserves truth of every [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md), by [structural induction](structural-induction.md), so it preserves realization and omission of parameter-free types.
