# Subgame perfect equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subgame_perfect_equilibrium)

A strategy profile that is a [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md) in every subgame, including those outside the realized play path. In finite sequential games, [backward induction](backward-induction.md) constructs such profiles by solving continuation games before earlier decisions. When a stage is an [all-pay contest](all-pay-auction.md), its solution can involve [mixed strategies](mixed-strategy.md).
