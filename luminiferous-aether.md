# Luminiferous aether

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminiferous_aether)

A historical proposed medium for the propagation of [light](light.md). The [Fizeau experiment](fizeau-experiment.md) tested how moving transparent matter affects light propagation; its positive signal did not uniquely establish such a medium.
