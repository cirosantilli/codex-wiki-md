# Broad-crested weir

↑ **Parent:** [Weir](weir.md)

For reservoir head $\delta$ above a broad crest, negligible approach [velocity](velocity.md), constant [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md), and no head loss, [hydraulic control](hydraulic-control.md) gives $\delta=3h_c/2$ and the displayed discharge. Reflecting the vertical coordinate gives the same result for a light layer escaping beneath a lowered roof. An orifice discharge coefficient is a separate closure and should not be silently inserted into this inviscid weir law.
