Here $s_2(n)$ is the sum of the [binary digits](binary-digit.md) of the nonnegative [integer](integer.md) $n$. The sign version is $f(n)=(-1)^{s_2(n)}$. Concatenating binary digits gives $f(a2^j+r)=f(a)f(r)$ for $0\le r<2^j$. It supplies cancellation controlled by binary blocks rather than multiplicative factorization.
