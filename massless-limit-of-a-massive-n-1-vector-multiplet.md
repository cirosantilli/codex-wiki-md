<h1 id="massless-limit-of-a-massive-n-1-vector-multiplet">Massless limit of a massive N=1 vector multiplet</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Massive N=1 vector multiplet](massive-n-1-vector-multiplet.md)

The [massive N=1 vector multiplet](massive-n-1-vector-multiplet.md) contains [spins](spin.md) $1,1/2,1/2,0$. Its massless [helicity](helicity.md) content splits into a CPT-complete [vector multiplet](supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) with helicities $(1,1/2)$ and $(-1/2,-1)$, and a CPT-complete [chiral multiplet](chiral-multiplet.md) with helicities $(1/2,0)$ and $(0,-1/2)$. The longitudinal vector polarization becomes one of the two scalar polarizations; the other comes from the original real scalar. This is the representation-theoretic inverse of the [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md).
