# Complex quadratic Abelian gauge condition

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear Abelian gauge fixing](nonlinear-abelian-gauge-fixing.md)

The formal variation of this condition is $\delta F=(\partial^2+2iA\cdot\partial)\alpha$. Thus its [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) operator can be chosen as $\mathcal O_A=-\partial^2-2iA\cdot\partial$. With $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+iA_\mu$, one has $D^2=\partial^2+2iA\cdot\partial+iF[A]$, so $\mathcal O_A=-D^2$ on the formal gauge slice. The [reality obstruction for a complex Euclidean gauge condition](reality-obstruction-for-a-complex-euclidean-gauge-condition.md) prevents interpreting this slice as an ordinary real Euclidean [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) without an additional complex-contour prescription.
