# Congruence (general relativity)

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congruence_(general_relativity))

A congruence in [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) is a family of curves through an open spacetime region such that each point lies on exactly one member. Its tangent field describes the family locally. A [geodesic congruence](geodesic-congruence-split.md) is the special case in which every member is a [geodesic](geodesic.md).
