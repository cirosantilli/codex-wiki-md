# Planar isoperimetric inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Isoperimetric inequality](isoperimetric-inequality.md)

This is the planar case of the [isoperimetric inequality](isoperimetric-inequality.md). Every bounded planar domain with a sufficiently regular boundary of length $L$ and area $A$ satisfies

$$
4\pi A\leq L^2.
$$

Equality holds exactly for circular domains.
