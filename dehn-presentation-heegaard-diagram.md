# Dehn presentation Heegaard diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Heegaard diagram](heegaard-diagram.md)

Thicken the planar graph underlying a [knot diagram](knot-diagram.md). Its boundary supports a [Heegaard diagram](heegaard-diagram.md) with face disks on one side and crossing-tunnel disks on the other. The face generators and crossing curves give a [Dehn presentation of a knot group](dehn-presentation-of-a-knot-group.md).
