# Kolyvagin derivative operator for a cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Root-of-unity Euler system](root-of-unity-euler-system.md)

For a cyclic [group](group-split.md) of order $m$ with generator $\sigma$, let $N=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\sigma^i$. Subtracting adjacent coefficients and using $\sigma^m=1$ proves the displayed group-ring identity. If $p^r\mid m$, then $(\sigma-1)D\equiv-N\pmod{p^r}$. Together with the norm relations of a [root-of-unity Euler system](root-of-unity-euler-system.md), this makes derivative classes invariant modulo p-power powers and permits Kummer descent, with roots-of-unity corrections where necessary. The congruence axiom controls the associated residue and valuation maps.
