# Gauge-invariant scalar-vector-tensor perturbations of Minkowski spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized gravity](linearized-gravity.md)

For signature $(+---)$, decompose a [metric perturbation](linearized-gravity.md) into $h_{00}=2A$, $h_{0i}=\partial_iB+B_i$ and $h_{ij}=2C\delta_{ij}+2(\partial_i\partial_j-\delta_{ij}\Delta/3)E+\partial_iE_j+\partial_jE_i+h_{ij}^{\rm TT}$, where the spatial vectors are divergence-free and $h^{\rm TT}$ is a [transverse-traceless tensor](transverse-traceless-tensor.md). Under a passive coordinate shift $\xi^0=\tau$, $\xi^i=\partial_iL+L_i$, with $\partial_iL_i=0$, the changes are $\delta A=-\dot\tau$, $\delta B=\dot L-\tau$, $\delta C=\Delta L/3$, $\delta E=L$, $\delta B_i=\dot L_i$, and $\delta E_i=L_i$. The displayed combinations and $h^{\rm TT}$ are therefore gauge invariant. The decomposition is unique after appropriate spatial decay or mode restrictions; homogeneous and boundary modes require separate conventions.
