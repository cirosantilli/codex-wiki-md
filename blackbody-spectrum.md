# Blackbody spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Black-body radiation](black-body-radiation.md)

A [blackbody spectrum](blackbody-spectrum.md) is the spectrum of ideal [black-body radiation](black-body-radiation.md) in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md), given by [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md). Thus [Planck spectrum](blackbody-spectrum.md) and [blackbody spectrum](blackbody-spectrum.md) name the same ideal spectrum.
