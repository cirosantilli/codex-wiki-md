# Closed-string physical-state Virasoro conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro constraint](virasoro-constraint.md)

Critical [closed bosonic strings](closed-string.md) have independent left- and right-moving [Virasoro constraints](virasoro-constraint.md). Positive modes in both sectors annihilate physical kets, while both zero-mode conditions impose the mass shell and [closed-string level matching](closed-string-level-matching.md). Quotient by physical [null string states](null-string-state.md), equivalently use the appropriate [BRST cohomology](brst-cohomology.md). With $L_0=\alpha'k^2/4+N$, the result is $N=\widetilde N$ and $M^2=4(N-1)/\alpha'$. Negative matter modes are adjoints rather than additional ket-annihilation constraints.
