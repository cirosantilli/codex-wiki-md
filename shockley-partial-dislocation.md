# Shockley partial dislocation

↑ **Parent:** [Partial dislocation](partial-dislocation.md)

An FCC [partial dislocation](partial-dislocation.md) with [Burgers vector](burgers-vector.md) $a\langle112\rangle/6$ lying in a close-packed $\{111\}$ plane. Two suitable Shockley vectors can sum to an FCC perfect vector $a\langle110\rangle/2$.
