# Anosov flow

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth flow](smooth-flow.md)

A nonsingular [smooth flow](smooth-flow.md) on a compact [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) is Anosov if there is a continuous invariant splitting $TN=E^s\oplus\mathbb RF\oplus E^u$ and constants $C,\lambda>0$ with $\|D\phi_t v_s\|\le Ce^{-\lambda t}\|v_s\|$ and $\|D\phi_{-t}v_u\|\le Ce^{-\lambda t}\|v_u\|$ for $t\ge0$. Uniform contraction and expansion are transverse to the neutral flow direction, which distinguishes the splitting from that of an [Anosov diffeomorphism](anosov-diffeomorphism.md).
