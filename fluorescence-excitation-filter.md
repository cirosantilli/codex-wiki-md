# Fluorescence excitation filter

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence microscope](fluorescence-microscope.md)

A [fluorescence excitation filter](fluorescence-excitation-filter.md) selects the illumination [wavelength](wavelength.md) band reaching the specimen. Its finite transmission changes the absorbed [photon](photon.md) rate and thus image brightness. Specify that band when interpreting a [fluorescence excitation spectrum](fluorescence-excitation-spectrum.md) or comparing images.
