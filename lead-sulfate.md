# Lead sulfate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

The sparingly soluble sulfate $\mathrm{PbSO_4}$. Its precipitation can retain [lead](lead.md) in [anode slime](anode-slime.md) in sulfate-based [copper](copper.md) [electrorefining](electrorefining.md), but an adherent layer can also contribute to [passivation](passivation.md).
