# Cosmological-constant domination

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md)

An expanding-universe regime in which a positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) supplies most of the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md). The approximately constant positive expansion rate gives exponential growth of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md). Since matter, [radiation](radiation.md), and curvature contributions decay with expansion, a positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) eventually dominates a perpetually expanding model containing them; this does not guarantee that curvature dominates at an intermediate time.
