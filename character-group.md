# Character group

↑ **Parent:** [Pontryagin duality](pontryagin-duality.md)

The character group of a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) that is an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) consists of its continuous [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) to the [circle group](circle-group.md), with pointwise multiplication. For a [finite group](finite-group.md), all characters are continuous and their values are [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md), so this agrees with the [character group of a finite abelian group](character-group-of-a-finite-abelian-group.md).
