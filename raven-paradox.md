# Raven paradox

↑ **Parent:** [Scientific confirmation](scientific-confirmation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raven_paradox)

“All ravens are black” is [logically equivalent](logical-equivalence.md) to “all nonblack things are nonravens”. An unrestricted rule that positive instances confirm universal generalizations would make a white shoe confirm the first statement. A [Bayesian confirmation](bayesian-confirmation.md) account instead conditions on background knowledge and the sampling procedure.[section 5](studies-in-the-logic-of-confirmation-i-by-carl-hempel.md)
