# Poisson equation with a spherical harmonic source

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson's equation](poisson-s-equation.md)

Let $Y_\ell$ be a [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) of degree $\ell$ on the unit [sphere](sphere.md). The [Laplacian](laplacian.md) in [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md) separates its angular part from a radial power, giving the displayed identity for $r>0$. Thus a [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) $\Delta\Phi=r^qY_\ell$ has the particular solution $\Phi=r^{q+2}Y_\ell/[(q+2)(q+3)-\ell(\ell+1)]$ when the denominator is nonzero. Add $Ar^\ell Y_\ell+Br^{-\ell-1}Y_\ell$ to impose the radial [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). If the denominator vanishes at $p=q+2$, differentiating the displayed identity with respect to $p$ gives instead $\Delta(r^p\log r\,Y_\ell)=(2p+1)r^{p-2}Y_\ell$. Regularity at the origin and decay at infinity must be checked separately; the radial identity alone is local away from zero.
