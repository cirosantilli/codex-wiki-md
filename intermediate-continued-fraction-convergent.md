# Intermediate continued fraction convergent

↑ **Parent:** [Continued fraction convergent](continued-fraction-convergent.md)

Between consecutive [continued fraction convergents](continued-fraction-convergent.md), intermediate convergents have the form $(t p_{k-1}+p_{k-2})/(t q_{k-1}+q_{k-2})$ for $1\le t\le a_k$. The final value $t=a_k$ is the next convergent.
