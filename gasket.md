# Gasket

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanical engineering](mechanical-engineering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gasket)

A [gasket](gasket.md) is a deformable component that closes a leakage path between contacting surfaces. In a [diamond anvil cell](diamond-anvil-cell.md), its hole also confines the sample laterally.
