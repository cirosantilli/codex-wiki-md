# Instantaneous stretching rate

↑ **Parent:** [Lyapunov exponent](lyapunov-exponent.md)

For a unit perturbation direction $e$ and [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) $J$, the instantaneous logarithmic growth rate of its Euclidean norm is $e^TSe$, where $S=(J+J^T)/2$. Indeed $d|\eta|^2/dt=2\eta^TS\eta$ for $\dot\eta=J\eta$. The [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $S$ give the principal rates and its orthonormal [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) give the principal directions. These directions generally rotate as the trajectory evolves; their instantaneous rates need not equal asymptotic [Lyapunov exponents](lyapunov-exponent.md).
