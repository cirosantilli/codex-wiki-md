<h1 id="folner-sequence-for-the-integers">Følner sequence for the integers</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Følner sequence](folner-sequence.md)

The intervals $F_n=\{-n,\ldots,n\}$ form a [Følner sequence](folner-sequence.md) for the additive group $\mathbb Z$, because

$$
\frac{|(F_n+1)\mathbin\triangle F_n|}{|F_n|}=\frac{2}{2n+1}\longrightarrow0.
$$
