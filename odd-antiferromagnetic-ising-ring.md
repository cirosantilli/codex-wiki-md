# Odd antiferromagnetic Ising ring

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetic Ising ring](antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md)

An odd periodic nearest-neighbour [Ising model](ising-model.md) with antiferromagnetic coupling has unavoidable [geometrical frustration](geometrical-frustration.md). For $k$ parallel bonds, $H=-NJ+2Jk$, while $\prod_jS_jS_{j+1}=1$ forces $N-k$ to be even. Thus odd $N$ requires at least one parallel bond. A [ground state](ground-state.md) has exactly one, with $N$ possible locations and two global [spin inversion symmetry](spin-inversion-symmetry.md) choices, proving the displayed [ground-state degeneracy](ground-state-degeneracy.md) and [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md). The zero-[temperature](temperature-split.md) [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md) distributes that defect uniformly. A directed arc of $n$ bonds contains it with probability $n/N$, giving [two-point correlation function](two-point-correlation-function.md) $(-1)^n(1-2n/N)$. The [finite antiferromagnetic Ising-ring correlation](finite-ring-antiferromagnetic-ising-correlation.md) gives the corresponding positive-[temperature](temperature-split.md) result.
