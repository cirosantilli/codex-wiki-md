# Portfolio diversification

↑ **Parent:** [Investment portfolio](investment-portfolio.md)

Spreading a [portfolio](investment-portfolio.md) across assets can reduce risk that is not shared by them. For centered asset risks with [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $\Gamma$, the [variance](variance-split.md) of the weighted risk is $w^T\Gamma w$. Equal weights across $n$ independent risks with common [variance](variance-split.md) $s^2$ give [variance](variance-split.md) $s^2/n$, whereas equal weights across risks that are all the same [random variable](random-variable-split.md) leave [variance](variance-split.md) $s^2$. Thus small individual weights do not by themselves remove common risk; the [covariance criterion for diversification of factor residuals](covariance-criterion-for-diversification-of-factor-residuals.md) identifies sufficient dependence and weight conditions.
