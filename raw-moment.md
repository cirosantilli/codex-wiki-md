# Raw moment

↑ **Parent:** [Moment](moment.md)

The $r$th raw moment of a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ is $\mathbb E[X^r]$, when it exists. A [central moment](central-moment.md) instead uses $\mathbb E[(X-\mathbb E[X])^r]$.
