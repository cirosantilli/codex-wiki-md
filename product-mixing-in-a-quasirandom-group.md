# Product mixing in a quasirandom group

↑ **Parent:** [Quasirandom group](quasirandom-group.md)

For an $m$-[quasirandom group](quasirandom-group.md), uniform [expectations](expected-value.md), and the [normalized convolution on a finite group](normalized-convolution-on-a-finite-group.md), a scalar [mean-zero function](mean-zero-function.md) $f$ satisfies

$$
\|f*g\|_2\leq m^{-1/2}\|f\|_2\|g\|_2.
$$

The [Fourier analysis on a finite group](fourier-analysis-on-a-finite-group.md) proof bounds each nontrivial matrix component of $f$ in [operator norm](operator-norm.md) using its weighted [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md). For subsets of [subset density](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) values $a,b,c$, the error in their normalized product count is at most $\sqrt{a(1-a)b(1-b)c(1-c)/m}$. In particular $abc>1/m$ guarantees a solution of $xy=z$ in the three subsets.
