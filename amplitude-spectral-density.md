# Amplitude spectral density

↑ **Parent:** [Power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md)

An [amplitude spectral density](amplitude-spectral-density.md) is the [square root](square-root.md) of a [power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md). For an [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) expressed in volts and [frequency](frequency.md) in [hertz](hertz.md), its units are $\mathrm V/\sqrt{\mathrm{Hz}}$.
