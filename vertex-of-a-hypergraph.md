# Vertex of a hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A vertex of a [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) $H=(V,E)$ is an element of its ground set $V$. Each [hypergraph edge](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) is a subset of $V$, and isolated vertices may belong to no edge. In a [multipartite hypergraph](multipartite-hypergraph.md), each vertex additionally lies in exactly one of the specified parts. This is the hypergraph analogue of a [vertex of a graph](vertex-graph-theory.md), allowing edges of cardinality other than two.
