# Cylinder rolling on a freely translating wedge

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

For a uniform solid cylinder of mass $M_c$ and a frictionlessly sliding wedge of mass $M_b$, take the horizontal wedge position $X$ and uphill contact coordinate $s$. Contact and [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) leave two [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md). Up to a constant, the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is $L=\tfrac12(M_b+M_c)\dot X^2+M_c\cos\alpha\dot X\dot s+\tfrac34M_c\dot s^2-M_cgs\sin\alpha$.

The [cyclic coordinate](cyclic-coordinate.md) $X$ gives $(M_b+M_c)\ddot X+M_c\cos\alpha\ddot s=0$. The other [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) is $\cos\alpha\ddot X+(3/2)\ddot s=-g\sin\alpha$. Elimination proves the displayed acceleration, with $\ddot X=-M_c\cos\alpha\ddot s/(M_b+M_c)$. For $M_b\gg M_c$, the cylinder approaches the fixed-incline acceleration $-(2/3)g\sin\alpha$.
