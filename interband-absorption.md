# Interband absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Interband transition](interband-transition.md)

Absorption in which a [photon](photon.md) excites an [electron](electron.md) between different [energy bands](energy-band.md), requiring an occupied initial state, an available final state and a nonzero transition matrix element. Indirect band-edge transitions also need momentum transfer, commonly through a [phonon](phonon.md). Such absorption can prevent optical transmission even above a metal's free-carrier plasma edge.
