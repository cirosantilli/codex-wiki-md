# Cylindrical screening charge

↑ **Parent:** [Counterion condensation](counterion-condensation.md)

The positive mobile charge per unit axial length accumulated from cylinder radius $a$ to $r$ is $Q(r)=2\pi\int_a^r s\rho(s)\,ds$. For fixed negative line charge $-\lambda$, [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) gives signed radial [electric field](electric-field.md) $E_r=2[-\lambda+Q(r)]/(\epsilon r)$ in [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md). Its absolute value is the field magnitude. An integral over a local infinite-dilution profile measures condensed charge, rather than every [ion](ion.md) in a finite neutral cell.
