<h1 id="bohm-tree">Böhm tree</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Head normal form](head-normal-form.md)

The Böhm tree of an [unsolvable lambda term](unsolvable-lambda-term.md) is $\bot$. If $M\to_\beta^*\lambda x_1\cdots x_n.yM_1\cdots M_r$ is a [head normal form](head-normal-form.md), its Böhm tree has root label $\lambda x_1\cdots x_n.y$ and ordered children $BT(M_1),\ldots,BT(M_r)$. The tree may be infinite. [Church-Rosser theorem](church-rosser-theorem.md) and [alpha equivalence](alpha-equivalence.md) make this independent of the selected head reduct.
