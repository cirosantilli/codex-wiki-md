# Independent-spin magnetic moment

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-only magnetic moment](spin-only-magnetic-moment.md)

For uncoupled centres with approximately equal $g=2$, squared [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) add: $\mu_{\rm eff}/\mu_B=2\sqrt{\sum_i S_i(S_i+1)}$. This does not assume that the individual [spins](spin.md) add to a definite total [spin](spin.md); appreciable [exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md) invalidates the independent-centre approximation.
