# Lipschitz truncation preserves zero-boundary Sobolev spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-boundary Sobolev space](zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md)

If $v\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ and $g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) with $g(0)=0$, then $g(v)\in H_0^1(\Omega)$. The Lipschitz version of the [Sobolev chain rule](sobolev-chain-rule.md) controls its [weak derivatives](weak-derivative.md), and its boundary trace is $g(0)=0$. Equivalently, approximate $v$ by compactly supported smooth functions, apply the derivative bound to their compositions, and pass weakly in $H^1$ and strongly in $L^2$. The closed linear space $H_0^1$ is weakly closed. This allows positive-part and bounded truncations in weak formulations without losing the [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md).
