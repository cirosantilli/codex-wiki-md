# Ligand field

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

A [ligand field](ligand-field.md) describes how ligands split and mix the central metal orbitals. [Sigma donation](sigma-donation.md), pi donation and [pi backbonding](pi-backbonding.md) explain trends that a purely electrostatic picture misses.
