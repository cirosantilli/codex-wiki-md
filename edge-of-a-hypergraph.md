# Edge of a hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

An edge of a [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) is one of its specified subsets of the vertex set. In a [uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) all edges have the same cardinality; unlike an ordinary [edge of a graph](edge-of-a-graph.md), an edge may contain more than two vertices.
