# Airport problem

↑ **Parent:** [Cooperative cost game](cooperative-cost-game.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport_problem)

Share a runway's cost among users needing different runway lengths. If user $i$ needs a facility costing $C_i\geq0$, a nonempty [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) pays $c(S)=\max_{i\in S}C_i$, and $c(\varnothing)=0$. This is a [subadditive cost game](subadditive-cost-game.md) because the maximum of two nonnegative costs does not exceed their sum. A cost-core allocation always exists: charge the whole cost to one longest-runway user and zero to all others. The [Shapley value of an airport game](shapley-value-of-an-airport-game.md) gives a symmetric alternative that also lies in the cost [core](core-game-theory.md).
