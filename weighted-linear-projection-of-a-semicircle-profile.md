# Weighted linear projection of a semicircle profile

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial least-squares approximation](polynomial-least-squares-approximation.md)

For the weight $w(x)=x$ on $[0,1]$, the [normal equations](normal-equation.md) for projecting $\sqrt{1-x^2}$ onto $\operatorname{span}\{1,x\}$ have [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1/2&1/3\\1/3&1/4\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and right side $(1/3,\pi/16)^T$. Its determinant is $1/72$, and inversion gives the displayed [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) of the residual to both [basis](basis.md) functions proves [best approximation](best-approximation-in-a-normed-space.md) in the weighted [norm](norm.md).
