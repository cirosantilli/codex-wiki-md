# Polycyclic groups are virtually poly-infinite cyclic

↑ **Parent:** [Polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md)

Every [polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md) has a finite-index subgroup with a series whose nontrivial factors are infinite cyclic. This is the polycyclic finite-index lemma; it also supplies torsion-free finite-index subgroups. Such a subgroup is a [poly-infinite cyclic group](poly-infinite-cyclic-group.md). Combining its series with the extension rule for [Poincare duality groups](poincare-duality-group.md) makes it a duality group of dimension its [Hirsch length](hirsch-length.md).
