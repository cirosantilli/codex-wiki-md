# Theta vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_vacuum)

A theta vacuum is a coherent state $|\theta\rangle=\sum_n e^{in\theta}|n\rangle$ formed from gauge vacua of different winding numbers. Large gauge transformations shift $n$ and act on this state by a phase. The [Yang-Mills theta term](yang-mills-theta-term.md) gives the corresponding weighting of spacetime topological sectors.
