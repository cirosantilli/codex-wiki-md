# Fixed-length stationary surface of revolution

↑ **Parent:** [Isoperimetric constraint](isoperimetric-constraint.md)

The area of a [surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md) about the horizontal axis is $2\pi\int|y|\sqrt{1+y'^2}\,dx$. For a curve below the axis with endpoints $(\pm a,0)$ and fixed [arc length](arc-length.md), its stationarity is equivalent to stationarity of $\int(y+\lambda)\sqrt{1+y'^2}\,dx$. The [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md) gives $(y+\lambda)/\sqrt{1+y'^2}=C$. Choosing $C=1/k$, imposing the endpoints and integrating the length gives the displayed curve. The function $\sinh s/s$ increases strictly from $1$ to infinity on $s>0$, so every $l>2a$ determines exactly one positive $k$. Stationarity alone does not establish a global minimum.
