# Basal water pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Subglacial drainage](subglacial-drainage.md)

The water [pressure](pressure-split.md) at a [glacier](glacier.md)-bed interface affects the effective normal [pressure](pressure-split.md) $N$, the difference between [ice](ice.md) overburden [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p_i$ and water [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p_w$. Higher water [pressure](pressure-split.md) at fixed overburden reduces effective compression of the bed contact and can alter cavitation and basal resistance. It is not identical to basal [shear stress](shear-stress.md), and a sliding law depending only on shear suppresses this independent hydraulic influence.
