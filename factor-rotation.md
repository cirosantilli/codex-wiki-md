# Factor rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal factor model](orthogonal-factor-model.md)

An orthogonal rotation of factors leaves $LL^T$ unchanged and hence preserves the model covariance. It proves rotational nonidentifiability of the unrestrained [factor loadings](factor-loading.md). Choose an orientation for interpretability, for example [varimax rotation](varimax-rotation.md), or impose identifying restrictions in a confirmatory model. An oblique transformation permits correlated factors: if $L$ becomes $LH$, their covariance becomes $H^{-1}H^{-T}$, preserving the same shared covariance.
