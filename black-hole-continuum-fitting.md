# Black-hole continuum fitting

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole spin measurement](black-hole-spin-measurement.md)

A fit of a thermal accretion-disk spectrum that infers the inner emitting [radius](radius.md) and identifies it with the [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md). A relativistic thin-disk model requires independently constrained [mass](mass.md), distance and inclination, together with spectral hardening and [absorption](absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md). Use of a suitable disk-dominated state is an essential physical check, not merely a statistical cut.
