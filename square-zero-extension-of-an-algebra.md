# Square-zero extension of an algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Square-zero ideal](square-zero-ideal.md)

For a unital [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) $A$ and prescribed $A$-[bimodule](bimodule.md) $M$, an extension is $0\to M\to E\to A\to0$ where $E$ is unital, $E\to A$ is unital, $M$ is a [square-zero ideal](square-zero-ideal.md), and the induced actions are the prescribed ones. Equivalences induce the identity on $A$ and $M$. A unital linear section produces a normalized [Hochschild cocycle](hochschild-cocycle.md) $\mu(a,b)=s(a)s(b)-s(ab)$. Conversely, $(a,m)(b,n)=(ab,an+mb+\mu(a,b))$ on $A\oplus M$ defines the extension. Changing section changes $\mu$ by a coboundary, giving classification by $HH^2(A,M)$.
