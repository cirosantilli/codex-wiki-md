# Weighted hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) with a specified numerical weight $w(e)$ for each [hyperedge](hyperedge.md) $e\in E$. In a [hypergraph cut function](hypergraph-cut-function.md), the weight records the cost or benefit of splitting a [hyperedge](hyperedge.md) between two sides. Signed weights and nonnegative weights lead to different [submodularity](submodular-set-function.md) properties.
