# Langevin function

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal chain](ideal-chain.md)

The [Langevin function](langevin-function.md) is the mean projection of a freely orientable three-dimensional unit vector weighted by $e^{\xi\cos\theta}$. Integrating over solid angle gives $Z_1=4\pi\sinh\xi/\xi$, and differentiation gives $\langle\cos\theta\rangle=\partial_\xi\log Z_1=\coth\xi-1/\xi$. It behaves as $\xi/3$ at small argument and $1-1/\xi$ at large positive argument.
