# Mitochondria

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle](organelle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitochondria)

A [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md) is an [organelle](organelle.md) with an [outer mitochondrial membrane](outer-mitochondrial-membrane.md) and an [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md). The latter encloses the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md) and folds into [cristae](crista.md); the [intermembrane space](intermembrane-space.md) lies on its opposite side. This architecture separates a chemical workspace from a membrane that couples [electron](electron.md) transport to [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) formation.[Biology 2e by Mary Ann Clark and others; §4.3, Mitochondria and Figure 4.14: two membranes, matrix and cristae](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/4-3-eukaryotic-cells.md)

For an original location exercise, place soluble label A in the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md) and soluble label B in the interior of a [crista](crista.md). In the depicted geometry, B can reach the peripheral [intermembrane space](intermembrane-space.md) through the fold's opening without crossing a [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md). A must cross the [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md) to reach either location. A fold projecting into the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md) therefore does not make its enclosed fluid part of the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md). Following a continuous path through the colored regions proves the distinction; merely asking whether a label is “inside the [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md)” loses it.

The functional connection is equally spatial. The [mitochondrial respiratory chain](mitochondrial-respiratory-chain.md) pumps [protons](proton.md) from the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md) side across the [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md). Their return through [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) can drive [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) formation. [Oxidative phosphorylation](oxidative-phosphorylation.md) develops the mechanism and [chemiosmosis](chemiosmosis.md) gives its [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) bookkeeping. Compartmentalization is useful because it lets a shared [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) couple different [proteins](protein-split.md) without direct transfer of a chemical intermediate between each pair.

Under aerobic conditions, [mitochondria](mitochondria.md) supply much of the [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) in many [eukaryotic](eukaryote.md) [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md); the contribution varies with [biological tissue](tissue-biology.md), demand and [oxygen](oxygen.md) supply. [Glycolysis](glycolysis.md) also makes [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) in the [cytosol](cytosol.md). Mature mammalian [red blood cells](red-blood-cell.md) lack [mitochondria](mitochondria.md), and [bacteria](bacteria.md) have no [mitochondria](mitochondria.md) at all. Neither all [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) production nor all [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) fit the familiar mitochondrial picture.[§4.3, Mitochondria; §7.2, glycolysis; §40.2, Red Blood Cells: mature mammalian cells lack mitochondria](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)

[Mitochondria](mitochondria.md) descend from a bacterial [endosymbiosis](endosymbiosis.md) and retain a [genome](genome.md) and [ribosomes](ribosome.md), although many required [proteins](protein-split.md) are encoded by nuclear [genes](gene.md). [Endosymbiotic theory](endosymbiotic-theory.md) develops the ancestry argument; [organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md) explains delivery of nuclear-encoded [proteins](protein-split.md). A functioning isolated [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md) demonstrates biochemical autonomy under supplied conditions, rather than independently establishing that ancestry.
