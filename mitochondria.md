# Mitochondria

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle](organelle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitochondria)

A [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md) is an [organelle](organelle.md) whose inner membrane houses machinery for much of aerobic [cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md) and [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) production in typical [eukaryotic](eukaryote.md) cells. [Mitochondria](mitochondria.md) descend from a bacterial [endosymbiosis](endosymbiosis.md) and retain a [genome](genome.md) and [ribosomes](ribosome.md), although many required [proteins](protein.md) are encoded by nuclear [genes](gene.md). This combination connects compartmentalized function to [evolution](evolution.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 23.1: mitochondrial ancestry](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/23-1-eukaryotic-origins).
