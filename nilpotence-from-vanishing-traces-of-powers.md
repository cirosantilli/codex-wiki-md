# Nilpotence from vanishing traces of powers

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix trace](matrix-trace.md)

For a complex $n$ by $n$ [matrix](matrix.md), triangularization gives $\operatorname{tr}(A^k)=\sum_j\lambda_j^k$. The [zero power sums force a finite complex multiset to vanish](zero-power-sums-force-a-finite-complex-multiset-to-vanish.md) criterion makes every diagonal eigenvalue zero. A strictly upper-triangular $n$ by $n$ matrix has zero $n$th power: every nonzero product entry would require $n$ strictly increasing index steps. [Similarity](matrix-similarity.md) then gives $A^n=0$.
