# Canonical vorticity of a relativistic fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md)

The canonical vorticity two-form is $\omega_{ab}=\nabla_a(\epsilon u_b)-\nabla_b(\epsilon u_a)$, with [specific enthalpy per particle](specific-enthalpy-per-particle.md) $\epsilon$. Potential flow $\epsilon u_a=\nabla_a\chi$ has $\omega=0$ for a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md). Contracting this identity with $u^a$ yields $\epsilon u^a\nabla_au_b=\nabla_b\epsilon-u_bu^a\nabla_a\epsilon$, the isentropic [relativistic Euler equation](relativistic-euler-equation.md) in signature $(+---)$. This canonical condition does not mean the unweighted four-velocity one-form is closed.
