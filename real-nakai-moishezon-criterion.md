<h1 id="real-nakai-moishezon-criterion">Real Nakai–Moishezon criterion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nakai–Moishezon criterion](nakai-moishezon-criterion.md)

A real Cartier class on a [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md) is [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) if and only if its top self-intersection on every positive-dimensional integral [subvariety](closed-subvariety.md) is positive. For the converse curve tests give nefness. Small [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) perturbations and rational approximation give [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) rational $B,C$ with $D-A=B-C$ satisfying the [algebraic Morse inequality for ample divisors](algebraic-morse-inequality-for-ample-divisors.md), hence bigness. Induction gives [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) restrictions on codimension-one [subvarieties](closed-subvariety.md); [uniform ample subtraction from a big divisor with ample exceptional restrictions](uniform-ample-subtraction-from-a-big-divisor-with-ample-exceptional-restrictions.md) makes $D-\epsilon A$ [nef](nef-line-bundle.md). The [nef-plus-ample ampleness lemma](nef-plus-ample-ampleness-lemma.md) concludes. For reducible schemes perform the finite component tests simultaneously.
