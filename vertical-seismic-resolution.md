# Vertical seismic resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic reflection survey](seismic-reflection-survey.md)

The ability to distinguish vertically adjacent reflectors. A quarter-[wavelength](wavelength.md) thickness is a useful wavelet-tuning scale, rather than a universal exact limit; bandwidth, [seismic wavelet](seismic-wavelet.md) shape and noise also matter.
