# Measurement uncertainty budget

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md)

A record of uncertainty contributions in a specified measurement model, their evaluation methods, sensitivity coefficients and relevant [correlations](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md). For $y=g(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$, first-order [propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md) gives $u_y^2=\sum_{ij}(\partial_i g)(\partial_j g)\operatorname{Cov}(x_i,x_j)$. A correction for a known [systematic error](systematic-error.md) can itself have uncertainty; applying the correction does not remove that contribution.[sections 4–5 and Appendix A](guidelines-for-evaluating-and-expressing-the-uncertainty-of-nist-measurement-results-by-barry-n-taylor-and-chris-e-kuyatt.md)
