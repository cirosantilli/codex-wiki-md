# Cosmological wavelength-redshift relation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md)

Cosmic expansion stretches an emitted or absorbed spectral feature's wavelength by $1+z$. A small observed wavelength interval at fixed transition wavelength gives a redshift interval by division by the rest wavelength. Cosmological radial distances then require the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) and, for proper distance at that epoch, an extra factor $1/(1+z)$.
