# Momentum variance of a discontinuous top hat

↑ **Parent:** [Top-hat wavefunction](top-hat-wavefunction.md)

The [momentum](momentum-split.md) density of a [top-hat wavefunction](top-hat-wavefunction.md) decays as an oscillatory $1/p^2$ envelope. Its [momentum](momentum-split.md) second moment therefore diverges, although the [probability density](probability-density.md) itself is normalizable. The central diffraction-lobe width is not the [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md). Even the first moment requires care: the symmetric [principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) is zero, but the absolute first moment diverges logarithmically.
