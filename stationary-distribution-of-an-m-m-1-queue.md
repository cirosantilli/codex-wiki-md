# Stationary distribution of an M-M-1 queue

↑ **Parent:** [M/M/1 queue](m-m-1-queue.md)

When $\rho<1$, the equilibrium queue length has distribution $\pi_n=(1-\rho)\rho^n$ and mean $\rho/(1-\rho)$.
