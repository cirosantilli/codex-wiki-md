# Sideband instability

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability](linear-stability.md)

A sideband instability is growth of disturbances with [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md) slightly displaced from a carrier pattern's [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). A real physical perturbation uses the displaced mode together with its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md); a [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) equation can couple those two sidebands through its cubic term. The [Eckhaus instability](eckhaus-instability.md) is a longitudinal phase-modulation example.
