# Initiality of the Kleisli adjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Kleisli category](kleisli-category.md)

For a [monad](monad.md) $(T,\eta,\mu)$, the free functor $J:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C_T$ and functor $U:\mathcal C_T\to\mathcal C$ with $UA=TA$ and $U(f)=\mu_B T(f)$ form an [adjunction](adjoint-functors.md) inducing that monad. Every other adjunction inducing the same monad receives the unique [Kleisli comparison functor](kleisli-comparison-functor.md) commuting with its left and right adjoints and preserving the adjunction structure. This is initiality among adjunctions inducing the specified monad, with morphisms required to respect that structure.
