# Measurement of the Xi0 to Lambda gamma Decay Asymmetry and Branching Fraction by NA48 Collaboration

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by NA48 Collaboration](work-by-na48-collaboration.md)

[https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0401027](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0401027)

Physical Letters B 584, 251–259. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2004.01.041](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2004.01.041)

The collaboration selected 730 candidates with an estimated $58\pm8$ background events and measured a [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md) $(1.16\pm0.05_{\rm stat}\pm0.06_{\rm syst})\times10^{-3}$. This is an observed weak radiative channel, not an electromagnetic flavour-conserving decay.
