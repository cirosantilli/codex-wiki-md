# Thermal representation of a reduced coupled-oscillator ground state

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix-split.md)

Tracing one oscillator out of a symmetric coordinate-coupled Gaussian [ground state](ground-state.md) gives a thermal density operator for an effective oscillator of [frequency](frequency.md) $\nu$. Its geometric ratio is $q=[(\sqrt{\omega_+}-\sqrt{\omega_-})/(\sqrt{\omega_+}+\sqrt{\omega_-})]^2$. It is generally squeezed relative to the original bare-frequency number basis. The covariance ratio fixes the effective [frequency](frequency.md) rather than permitting an arbitrary thermal-basis identification.
