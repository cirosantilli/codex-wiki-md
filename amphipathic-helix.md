# Amphipathic helix

↑ **Parent:** [Protein secondary structure](protein-secondary-structure.md)

An [amphipathic helix](amphipathic-helix.md) presents chemically different faces, often a relatively polar face and a hydrophobic face. Shallow membrane insertion can change leaflet packing without making a full transmembrane segment.
