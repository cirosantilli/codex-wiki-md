# Construction of a principal sl2 triple

↑ **Parent:** [Principal sl2 subalgebra](principal-sl2-subalgebra.md)

Choose [simple root](simple-root.md) vectors with $[e_i,f_i]=h_i=\alpha_i^\vee$, and let $\rho^\vee$ be the half-sum of positive [coroots](coroot.md). It satisfies $\alpha_i(\rho^\vee)=1$. Expand $h=2\rho^\vee=\sum_i c_i h_i$. For $i\ne j$, $[e_i,f_j]=0$, since the difference of distinct simple roots is not a [root of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md). Thus $[e,f]=h$, while $[h,e]=2e$ and $[h,f]=-2f$. These identities construct an embedded [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md) in any nonzero complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md). Its raising vector is the [principal nilpotent element](principal-nilpotent-element.md) with a nonzero component in each simple-root space.
