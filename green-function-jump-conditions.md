# Green function jump conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function-split.md)

For $L=a_2(x)D^2+a_1(x)D+a_0(x)$ with $a_2(\xi)\ne0$, a [Green's function](green-s-function-split.md) solving $L_xG=\delta(x-\xi)$ is homogeneous on either side of $\xi$, continuous there, and has first [derivative](derivative.md) jump $[G_x]_-^+=1/a_2(\xi)$. A nonzero jump in $G$ itself would produce an unwanted [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) of the [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md). Once continuity removes that term, integrating the equation across $\xi$ fixes the derivative jump. Endpoint [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) complete the classical characterization.
