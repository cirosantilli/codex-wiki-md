# Semiconductor doping

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doping_(semiconductor))

The controlled introduction of impurities to change a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)'s [charge carrier](charge-carrier.md) populations. [Donor impurities](donor-impurity.md) and [acceptor impurities](acceptor-impurity.md) can produce an [n-type semiconductor](n-type-semiconductor.md) or a [p-type semiconductor](p-type-semiconductor.md), respectively; compensation and [temperature](temperature-split.md) also matter.
