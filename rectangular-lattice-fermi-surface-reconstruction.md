# Rectangular-lattice Fermi-surface reconstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Nearly-free electron model](nearly-free-electron-model.md)

Consider a spin-degenerate two-dimensional [free electron model](free-electron-model.md) with rectangular periods $a>b$ and two [electrons](electron.md) per [unit cell](unit-cell.md). Write $K=\pi/a$, $L=\pi/b$, and $k_F^2=4\pi/(ab)$. If $K<k_F<L$, the circular [Fermi contour](fermi-contour.md) crosses only the vertical boundaries of the first [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md). Folding translates its protruding caps back by a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector $(2K,0)$. A weak [periodic potential](periodic-potential.md) mixes the intersecting [plane waves](plane-wave.md), reconnecting the contours into two open first-band sheets and a closed second-band pocket. The pocket is centred on a zone boundary: its halves on opposite sides of a reduced-zone plot are identified periodically and belong to one closed sheet.
