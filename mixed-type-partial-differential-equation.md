# Mixed-type partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)

A second-order [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) is of mixed type when the type of its principal part changes across its domain. For $A u_{xx}+2B u_{xy}+C u_{yy}=0$, the sign of $B^2-AC$ distinguishes a [hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md), [parabolic type](parabolic-partial-differential-equation.md) and an [elliptic partial differential equation](elliptic-partial-differential-equation.md). For example, $u_{xx}+\operatorname{sign}(y)u_{yy}=0$ is elliptic above $y=0$, hyperbolic below it, and degenerate on the interface. On its hyperbolic side, the change $\xi=x+y$, $\eta=x-y$ reduces it to $u_{\xi\eta}=0$; above it is the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md). Type classification alone supplies no matching rule for the normal derivative.
