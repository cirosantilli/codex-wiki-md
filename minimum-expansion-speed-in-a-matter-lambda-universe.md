# Minimum expansion speed in a matter-Lambda universe

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

For pressureless matter and a positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $\dot a^2=H_0^2[\Omega_{m,0}a_0^3/a+\Omega_{k,0}a_0^2+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}a^2]$. Differentiating with respect to $a$ gives the displayed unique minimum of this expression for positive matter density. It is a minimum of the actual expanding solution's speed only if that branch reaches it; sufficient positive spatial curvature can cause recollapse first. At the minimum, $\rho_m=2\rho_\Lambda$ and the [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) changes sign.
