# Lagrangian (field theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrangian_(field_theory))

Lagrangian field theory specifies an [action](action.md) by integrating a Lagrangian density $\mathcal L(\phi,\partial\phi,x)$ over spacetime. Requiring its [first variation](first-variation.md) to vanish for compactly supported field variations yields the field [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md). The variational [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is the integrand, whereas this field-theoretic formalism specifies the continuum fields and their dynamics.
