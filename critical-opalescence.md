# Critical opalescence

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_opalescence)

[Critical opalescence](critical-opalescence.md) is enhanced [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md) by large fluctuations near a [thermodynamic critical point](thermodynamic-critical-point.md). The [carbon-dioxide viewing-cell phase-transition experiment](carbon-dioxide-viewing-cell-phase-transition-experiment.md) gives an observable finite-sample example; cloudiness alone does not establish a divergent [correlation length](correlation-length.md).
