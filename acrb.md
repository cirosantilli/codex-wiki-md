# AcrB

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport protein](membrane-transport-protein.md)

[AcrB](acrb.md) is a bacterial proton-coupled multidrug transporter that functions as part of the AcrAB–TolC export assembly. Its asymmetric trimer and multiple drug-binding regions are structural models for studying broad specificity.[Figs. 1–5, drug-bound asymmetric AcrB trimer](crystal-structures-of-a-multidrug-transporter-reveal-a-functionally-rotating-mechanism-by-satoshi-murakami-and-others.md)
