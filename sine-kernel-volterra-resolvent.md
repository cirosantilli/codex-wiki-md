# Sine-kernel Volterra resolvent

↑ **Parent:** [Volterra integral equation](volterra-integral-equation.md)

For causal [convolution](convolution.md) on $t\ge0$, the [Laplace transform convolution theorem](convolution-theorem-for-laplace-transforms.md) converts $x+\sin*x=F$ into $\bar x=[(p^2+1)/(p^2+2)]\bar F$. Since this multiplier is $1-1/(p^2+2)$, inversion gives the displayed resolvent kernel. The formula remains valid for locally integrable forcing on finite intervals: substitution and the [Fubini theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) reduce verification to an identity between the two continuous sine kernels. Thus a global Laplace transform is a convenient derivation, rather than an essential growth restriction on every local forcing.
