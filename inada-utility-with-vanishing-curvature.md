# Inada utility with vanishing curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Inada conditions](inada-conditions.md)

This smooth utility has $U'(x)=x^{-1}-x^{-2}+x^{-3}/3>0$ and $U''(x)=-(x-1)^2/x^4$. Its [derivative](derivative.md) is strictly decreasing, diverges at zero and tends to zero at infinity, so the utility is [strictly concave](strictly-concave-function.md) and satisfies the [Inada conditions](inada-conditions.md). Yet its curvature vanishes at one. The [inverse marginal utility](inverse-marginal-utility.md) cannot have a finite [derivative](derivative.md) at $y=1/3$, since differentiating $U'(I(y))=y$ there would give zero equal to one. Its dual is differentiable but not twice differentiable at that price.
