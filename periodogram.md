# Periodogram

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md)

For a centered record of length $T$, the periodogram is the squared modulus of its [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform.md), with normalization

$$
I_T(\lambda)=\frac1{2\pi T}\left|\sum_{t=1}^TX_te^{-it\lambda}\right|^2.
$$

Under suitable short-memory assumptions its expectation approaches the [spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md), but its variance generally does not vanish. Smoothing nearby frequencies can provide an estimate with [statistical consistency](consistency-statistics.md).
