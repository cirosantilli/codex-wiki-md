# Number variance of an interacting defect

↑ **Parent:** [Single-orbital interacting defect](single-orbital-interacting-defect.md)

With $z=e^{\beta(\mu-\epsilon)}$, $q=e^{-\beta U}$ and $\Xi=1+2z+qz^2$, direct four-state averaging gives

$$
\operatorname{Var}n=\frac{2z(1+2qz+qz^2)}{\Xi^2}=k_BT\,\partial_\mu\bar n.
$$

For $\beta U\gg1$ it has two separated peaks of height $1/4$ at the spin-degeneracy-shifted charge steps. At the lower bare crossing $\mu=\epsilon$, three [microstates](microstate.md) share equal limiting weight, giving $\bar n=2/3$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $2/9$. The upper bare crossing similarly gives mean $4/3$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $2/9$. Between crossings occupation is pinned near one and fluctuations are suppressed. The identity is grand-canonical; fixing total [particle number](particle-number.md) would change the fluctuation question.
