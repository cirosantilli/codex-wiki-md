# Time normalization in the Cole-Hopf transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Cole-Hopf transformation](cole-hopf-transformation.md)

Substituting $u=-2\nu\partial_x\log\Psi$ into the [viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md) gives $\partial_x[(\Psi_t-\nu\Psi_{xx})/\Psi]=0$. Integration permits the arbitrary function $C(t)$ in the title. Multiplication of $\Psi$ by $e^{-\int C(t)dt}$ preserves $u$ and reduces its equation to the [heat equation](heat-equation.md). Thus the heat-equation representation uses a permissible time normalization rather than a condition uniquely imposed on every representative $\Psi$.
