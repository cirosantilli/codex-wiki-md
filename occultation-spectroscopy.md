# Occultation spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet secondary eclipse](exoplanet-secondary-eclipse.md)

The difference between [star](star.md)-plus-[planet](planet.md) light outside secondary eclipse and [star](star.md)-only light during eclipse isolates the [planet](planet.md)'s dayside spectrum. Thermal radiation probes its [temperature](temperature-split.md)-[pressure](pressure-split.md) structure and [opacity](opacity.md), while optical reflected light probes scattering and albedo. Contrast and the subtraction of stellar photons limit sensitivity.
