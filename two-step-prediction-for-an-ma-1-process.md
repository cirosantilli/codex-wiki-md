<h1 id="two-step-prediction-for-an-ma-1-process">Two-step prediction for an MA(1) process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Best linear prediction from a finite past](best-linear-prediction-from-a-finite-past.md)

In a centered MA(1) process, $X_{T+2}$ is uncorrelated with every observation through time $T$. Its best [linear predictor](linear-predictor.md) from that information is therefore zero, with error [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2(1+\theta^2)$. One-step innovation formulas using $X_{T+1}$ cannot be substituted when that observation is unavailable.
