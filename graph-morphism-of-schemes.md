# Graph morphism of schemes

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md)

For [morphisms of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$, the graph is the displayed map. It is the pullback of the [diagonal morphism](diagonal-morphism.md) $Y\to Y\times_ZY$ along $(f\circ p_X,p_Y)$. Thus a [separated morphism](separated-morphism.md) $g$ makes $\Gamma_f$ a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md). If also $g\circ f$ is a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md), the projection $X\times_ZY\to Y$ is a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md) by [base change of a morphism of schemes](base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md), and its composite with $\Gamma_f$ proves that $f$ is a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md).
