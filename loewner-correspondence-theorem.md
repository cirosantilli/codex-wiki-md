# Loewner correspondence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Loewner local growth property](loewner-local-growth-property.md)

An increasing family of [compact H-hulls](compact-h-hull.md) of [half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md) $2t$ with the [Loewner local growth property](loewner-local-growth-property.md) corresponds uniquely to a continuous real [Loewner driving function](loewner-driving-function.md). Its [mapping-out functions](mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md) solve the [Chordal Loewner equation](chordal-loewner-equation.md) up to the maximal lifetime of each point. Conversely each continuous driver generates that hull family. A continuous [Loewner trace](trace-of-a-loewner-chain.md) is an additional property, not a consequence for every continuous driver. For [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) it is supplied by the trace-existence theorem.
