# Fourier transform of an algebraic cusp

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier decay from a derivative jump](fourier-decay-from-a-derivative-jump.md)

For $p>-1$ this formula is interpreted away from $k=0$ as a [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md) or by exponential regularization. It also supplies local high-frequency cusp terms after multiplying by a smooth cutoff. Even nonnegative integer $p$ give smooth polynomials and only contact terms at $k=0$, so their localized forms have no algebraic tail. For example, the cusp expansion of $(1+|x|^3)^{-1}$ gives $-12|k|^{-4}+2\,9!|k|^{-10}+O(|k|^{-16})$.
