# Linear spin-wave approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Spin wave](spin-wave.md)

The [linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md) expands a [Holstein–Primakoff transformation](holstein-primakoff-transformation.md) about a classical ordered state and retains the quadratic oscillator [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). It gives the leading order-$S$ excitation energies above the order-$S^2$ classical energy. In a [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md), first make a [bipartite spin rotation](bipartite-spin-rotation.md). The approximation also predicts a zero-point reduction of the order parameter; infrared-divergent [quantum depletion of Néel order](quantum-depletion-of-neel-order.md) signals that the assumed ordered state is not a valid thermodynamic starting point.
