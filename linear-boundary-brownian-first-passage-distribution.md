# Linear-boundary Brownian first-passage distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Drifted Brownian first-passage density](drifted-brownian-first-passage-density.md)

For $a>0$, real $b$ and standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), the [first-passage time](first-passage-time.md) to a linear boundary has

$$
\mathbb P(T_{a,b}\leq t)=e^{-2ab}\Phi((bt-a)/\sqrt t)+\Phi(-(a+bt)/\sqrt t),\qquad t>0.
$$

Here $\Phi$ is the [standard normal distribution function](standard-normal-distribution-function.md). Differentiation gives $a\exp(-(a+bt)^2/(2t))/\sqrt{2\pi t^3}$, the [drifted Brownian first-passage density](drifted-brownian-first-passage-density.md). Its [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) is $\exp[-a(b+\sqrt{b^2+2\theta})]$ for $\theta>0$. There is mass $1-e^{-2ab}$ at infinity when $b>0$; for $b\leq0$ the hitting time is finite [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md).
