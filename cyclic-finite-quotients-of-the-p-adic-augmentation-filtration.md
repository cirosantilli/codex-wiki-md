# Cyclic finite quotients of the p-adic augmentation filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md)

In the ordinary [group algebra](group-algebra.md) of the additive [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md), let $I$ be the [augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md). The [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of the map to the [group algebra](group-algebra.md) of $\mathbb Z_p/p^r\mathbb Z_p$ is generated by $[p^rh]-1=([h]-1)^{p^r}$, so it is contained in $I^{p^r}$. The quotient is the cyclic-group algebra $\mathbb F_p[T]/(T^{p^r})$, so its [augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md) has zero $p^r$th power and the reverse inclusion holds. Every finite degree of the [associated graded ring](associated-graded-ring.md) can be read from such a quotient, giving $\operatorname{gr}_I\mathbb F_p[\mathbb Z_p]\cong\mathbb F_p[X]$. The completion is $\mathbb F_p[[T]]$, but completion is not needed for the graded calculation.
