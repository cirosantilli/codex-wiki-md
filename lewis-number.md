# Lewis number

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion](diffusion.md)

The ratio of thermal [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md) $\kappa$ to molecular or solutal [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$. In coupled [heat equation](heat-equation.md) and solute [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) similarity problems, the thermal coordinate $z/(2\sqrt{\kappa t})$ becomes $\sqrt{Le}$ times larger in the solute [complementary error function](complementary-error-function.md) profile. This follows by substituting the thermal coordinate into $C_t=DC_{zz}$, giving $C''+2Le\eta C'=0$. The definition must specify the direction of this ratio because reciprocal conventions occasionally occur.
