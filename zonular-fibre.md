# Zonular fibre

↑ **Parent:** [Ciliary muscle](ciliary-muscle.md)

A suspensory fibre transmitting tension to the [crystalline lens](crystalline-lens.md). Reduced zonular tension during near [ocular accommodation](accommodation-vertebrate-eye.md) permits the [crystalline lens](crystalline-lens.md) to become more rounded.
