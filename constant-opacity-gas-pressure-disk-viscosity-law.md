# Constant-opacity gas-pressure disk viscosity law

↑ **Parent:** [Homologous vertical structure of a constant-opacity alpha disk](homologous-vertical-structure-of-a-constant-opacity-alpha-disk.md)

The [density-weighted mean kinematic viscosity](density-weighted-mean-kinematic-viscosity.md) of the universal vertical profile is $\bar\nu=\alpha\Omega h^2\int_{-\zeta_s}^{\zeta_s}P\,d\zeta$. Substitution of the height scale gives $\bar\nu=C\alpha^{4/3}(\kappa/\sigma)^{1/3}\mathcal R^{4/3}\Sigma^{2/3}\Omega^{-2/3}$. For [Keplerian rotation](keplerian-disk.md), $\Omega^{-2/3}=(GM)^{-1/3}r$. The positive numerical constant depends only on the dimensionless vertical profile and the convention for $\alpha$.
