# Half-turn flat three-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Holonomy groups of closed orientable flat three-manifolds](holonomy-groups-of-closed-orientable-flat-three-manifolds.md)

This closed [flat Riemannian manifold](flat-manifold.md) is the [mapping torus](mapping-torus.md) of $-I$ on a two-dimensional [flat torus](flat-torus.md). Equivalently, take planar lattice translations and the screw $(v,z)\mapsto(-v,z+1/2)$ in Euclidean three-space. Its square is a unit axial translation, its holonomy is $C_2$, and its first [Betti number](betti-number.md) is one.
