# Lagrange dual function

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian dual problem](lagrangian-dual-problem.md)

The [Lagrange dual function](lagrange-dual-function.md) is the infimum of a [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) over its primal variables. It is concave in the multipliers, even before convexity of the primal problem is assumed. Maximizing it over sign-compatible multipliers gives a lower bound on a minimization problem by [weak duality](weak-duality.md); appropriate convex qualifications give [strong duality](strong-duality.md).
