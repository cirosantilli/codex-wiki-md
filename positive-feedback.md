# Positive feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Signaling feedback](signaling-feedback.md)

[Positive feedback](positive-feedback.md) closes a causal loop whose net effect reinforces an initiating change. A loop with two inhibitory links can therefore have [positive feedback](positive-feedback.md). In [signal transduction](signal-transduction.md), a product that increases the activity of the [enzyme](enzyme.md) making it illustrates reinforcement; the consequences depend on the full [mathematical model](mathematical-model.md), not just the loop sign. See [signaling feedback](signaling-feedback.md).

A biological example is [cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md). [Cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) binds [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md), whose activity promotes activation of [Cdc25](cdc25.md); [Cdc25](cdc25.md) removes inhibitory [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) from [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md), permitting further activation. [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) also inhibits [Wee1](wee1.md), an [enzyme](enzyme.md) that inhibits [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) through [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md). That second return contains two inhibitions and therefore also has [positive feedback](positive-feedback.md). These are regulatory paths, not a claim that every connection is a single direct molecular encounter. [Multisite phosphoregulation of Cdc25 activity refines the mitotic entrance and exit switches by Lucy X. Lu and others](multisite-phosphoregulation-of-cdc25-activity-refines-the-mitotic-entrance-and-exit-switches-by-lucy-x-lu-and-others.md)

Reinforcement can preserve a decision after its initiating stimulus weakens: returning from the high state may require a lower input than entering it. The [cell-cycle hysteresis experiment](cell-cycle-hysteresis-experiment.md) below tests this history dependence at matched [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) [concentrations](concentration.md). A steep response alone is insufficient evidence of [bistability](bistability.md), and finite observation times can make slow relaxation look like memory. A proposed switch must specify its input, readout and observation interval.
