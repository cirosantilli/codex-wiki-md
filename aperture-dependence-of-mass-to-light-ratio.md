# Aperture dependence of mass-to-light ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md)

A dynamical [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md) must specify its aperture and whether the mass includes [dark matter](dark-matter.md). A growing dark halo can increase $\Upsilon(<R)$ outside the bright stellar body even when the stellar ratio is constant. Comparing a tidal-radius satellite mass with a large-aperture host mass is different from comparing both within their half-light radii. From $M\propto\sigma^2R$ and $L\propto\sigma^4$, the inferred ratio scales as $R/\sigma^2$, so equal ratios additionally require homologous radius scaling.
