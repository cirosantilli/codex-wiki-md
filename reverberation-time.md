# Reverberation time

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustics](acoustics.md)

The conventional [reverberation time](reverberation-time.md) $T_{60}$ is the time for the level of decaying acoustic [energy](energy-split.md) to decrease by $60$ [decibels](decibel.md), a factor $10^6$ in [energy](energy-split.md) or $10^3$ in pressure [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md). For exponential [energy](energy-split.md) decay $E(t)=E(0)e^{-t/\tau_E}$, $T_{60}=6\ln(10)\tau_E$. It is not the [exponential relaxation time](exponential-relaxation-time.md) itself and cannot be inferred exactly from an unspecified audibility threshold.
