# Nuclear fuel cladding

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fuel](nuclear-fuel.md)

The structural layer enclosing [nuclear fuel](nuclear-fuel.md), retaining fuel and [fission products](fission-product.md) while transferring heat to a coolant. Its pressure loading, chemical compatibility and irradiation response must remain consistent with its required geometry and integrity.
