# Habit

↑ **Parent:** [Instrumental conditioning](instrumental-conditioning.md)

A learned tendency to respond that can persist despite a change in the current value of the outcome. Devaluation insensitivity is informative only under conditions in which the outcome change is understood and performance is otherwise possible. It need not imply exceptionally rapid [habit](habit.md) acquisition.[American Journal of Psychiatry 168, 718–726; Method, instructed outcome devaluation and slips-of-action test](disruption-in-the-balance-between-goal-directed-behavior-and-habit-learning-in-obsessive-compulsive-disorder-by-claire-m-gillan-and-others.md)
