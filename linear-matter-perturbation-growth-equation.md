# Linear matter perturbation growth equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md)

For pressureless sub-Hubble perturbations, combine the [linearized cosmological continuity equation](linearized-cosmological-continuity-equation.md), [cosmological Euler equation](cosmological-euler-equation.md) and gravitational [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) with matter as its source. The expansion contributes a $2H$ friction term and matter self-gravity contributes the negative coefficient of $\delta$. The background expansion can include radiation or dark energy; treating their perturbations as additional sources requires a more complete system.
