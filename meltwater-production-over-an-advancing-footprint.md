# Meltwater production over an advancing footprint

↑ **Parent:** [Melting-source gravity-current equation](melting-source-gravity-current-equation.md)

For constant water-production rate per wetted area $s_m$, dry zero-flux noses and footprint length $\mathcal L(t)$, the moving-boundary [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) gives $\dot{\mathcal V}=s_m\mathcal L$ per out-of-plane span. Consequently $\ddot{\mathcal V}=s_m\dot{\mathcal L}$. Equivalently the melt volume is $s_m\int_0^t\mathcal L(t\prime)dt\prime$; a point begins producing melt only after the front arrives.
