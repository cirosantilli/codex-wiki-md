# Smectic A phase

↑ **Parent:** [Smectic phase](smectic-phase.md)

A smectic A phase has layered positional order with the average molecular orientation along the layer normal. Molecular positions within each layer remain liquid-like. Its orientational axis is described by a [nematic director](nematic-director.md).
