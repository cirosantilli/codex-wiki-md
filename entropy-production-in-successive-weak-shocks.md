# Entropy production in successive weak shocks

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy production in a perfect-gas shock](entropy-production-in-a-perfect-gas-shock.md)

For $N$ equal [weak shocks](weak-shock.md) with individual [pressure](pressure.md) ratio $1+\delta$ and total ratio $P_s=(1+\delta)^N$, [entropy production in a perfect-gas shock](entropy-production-in-a-perfect-gas-shock.md) gives

$$
\frac{\Delta s}{c_v}
=\frac{\gamma^2-1}{12\gamma^2}\delta^2\log P_s\,[1+O(\delta)].
$$

At fixed $P_s>1$, this tends to zero as the compression is divided into increasingly many smaller [weak shocks](weak-shock.md). A single finite [normal shock wave](normal-shock-wave.md) instead gives the positive value $\log P_s-\gamma\log D(P_s)$. Gradual compression can consequently approach reversible [isentropic flow](isentropic-flow.md) while an abrupt compression generates finite [entropy](entropy.md).
