# Equal Sigma and Xi hyperfine splittings

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon hyperfine mass formula](baryon-hyperfine-mass-formula.md)

Use dimensionless [spin angular momentum](spin.md) operators with $\hbar=1$ and the common-coefficient [baryon hyperfine mass formula](baryon-hyperfine-mass-formula.md). The identical pair has total spin one, so its pair expectation is $1/4$. For total baryon spin $1/2$, the two mixed-flavour pair expectations sum to $-1$; for total spin $3/2$, they sum to $1/2$. Their difference is $3/2$, giving the title formula for either repeated-flavour choice. The equality assumes the same effective coefficient $A'$ in the two flavour configurations; different spatial overlaps can change it.
