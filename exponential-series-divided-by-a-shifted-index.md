# Exponential series divided by a shifted index

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential series](exponential-series.md)

For a positive integer $k$, multiply the [exponential series](exponential-series.md) by $t^{k-1}$ and integrate term by term from zero to $x$. Its [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) on every compact interval justifies the operation and proves the title identity. The resulting [power series](power-series.md) converges absolutely for every real or complex $x$, by the [ratio test](ratio-test.md). Repeated [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) evaluates the right side as an exponential times a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), with its constant chosen to make the integral vanish at zero.
