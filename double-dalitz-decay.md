# Double Dalitz decay

↑ **Parent:** [Dalitz decay](dalitz-decay.md)

[Double Dalitz decay](double-dalitz-decay.md) converts both virtual [photons](photon.md) from a two-photon hadronic transition into charged-lepton pairs. The neutral-[pion](pion.md) four-electron final state avoids the specific two-lepton [helicity suppression](helicity-suppression.md) of $\pi^0\to e^+e^-$, despite its more restricted [phase space](phase-space.md).
