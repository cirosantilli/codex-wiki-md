# Phase-space density of an atomic gas

↑ **Parent:** [Dilute-gas Bose-Einstein condensation](dilute-gas-bose-einstein-condensation.md)

The dimensionless occupancy scale $n\lambda_T^3$, with [number density](number-density.md) $n$ and [thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) $\lambda_T=h/\sqrt{2\pi mk_BT}$. Increasing it approaches the regime where quantum statistics matters. This use of degeneracy must not be confused with degeneracy of an individual [energy](energy-split.md) eigenspace.
