# Hydraulic radius

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

The [hydraulic radius](hydraulic-radius.md) is cross-sectional flow [area](surface-area.md) divided by wetted perimeter. A perimeter-averaged boundary [shear stress](shear-stress.md) $\tau$ exerts drag per unit fluid volume $\tau\ell/A=\tau/R$. It differs from both actual depth and the radius of a noncircular channel.
