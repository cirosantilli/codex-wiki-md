# Stationary density for a Fokker-Planck equation

↑ **Parent:** [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md)

A [probability density](probability-density.md) left unchanged by the [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md) $\partial_tf=L^*f$. Equivalently its [Fokker-Planck probability current](fokker-planck-probability-current.md) has zero [divergence](divergence.md); the current itself need not vanish. For the [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Fokker-Planck equation](ornstein-uhlenbeck-fokker-planck-equation.md), the normalized standard [Gaussian density](multivariate-normal-density.md) has zero current and is stationary.
