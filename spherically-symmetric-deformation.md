# Spherically symmetric deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation map](deformation-map.md)

A radial [deformation map](deformation-map.md) preserving each particle's direction from a common centre. In an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md), its [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) has [principal stretches](principal-stretch.md) $f'(R),f(R)/R,f(R)/R$. Thus [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) gives $f'(R)f(R)^2=R^2$, and integration gives $f(R)^3=R^3+C$.
