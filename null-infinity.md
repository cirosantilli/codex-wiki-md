# Null infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Null_infinity)

[Null infinity](null-infinity.md) is the ideal boundary reached by asymptotically escaping [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) in a suitable conformal completion. It has future and past components. A finite location on a [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md) need not represent a finite [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md).
