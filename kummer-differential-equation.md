# Kummer differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order linear differential equation](second-order-linear-differential-equation.md)

Kummer's equation is

$$
xy''+(b-x)y'-ay=0.
$$

Its solution analytic at zero is the [confluent hypergeometric function of the first kind](confluent-hypergeometric-function-of-the-first-kind.md)

$$
M(a,b,x)=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}
\frac{(a)_m}{(b)_m\,m!}x^m.
$$

When $b$ is not an integer, a second local solution is

$$
x^{1-b}M(a-b+1,2-b,x).
$$
