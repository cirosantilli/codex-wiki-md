# Growth tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md)

The factor describing local stress-free growth in the [morphoelasticity](morphoelasticity.md) convention $\mathbf F=\mathbf A\mathbf G$. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is the local grown-volume ratio. For $\mathbf G=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,\gamma)$, growth changes only the preferred third-direction length, by factor $\gamma$.
