# Inflaton equation of motion

↑ **Parent:** [Inflaton](inflaton.md)

For a homogeneous canonical inflaton in an expanding [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md),

$$
\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi+V'(\phi)=0.
$$

The expansion contributes the damping term called [Hubble friction](hubble-friction.md).
