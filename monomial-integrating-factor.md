# Monomial integrating factor

↑ **Parent:** [Integrating factor for a differential one-form](integrating-factor-for-a-differential-one-form.md)

For a [differential one-form](one-form.md) $M\,dx+N\,dy$, a monomial [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) exists on a chosen domain away from the axes when constants $\alpha,\beta$ make

$$
M_y-N_x=\frac{\alpha}{x}N-\frac{\beta}{y}M.
$$

This follows by expanding the exactness condition $\partial_y(\mu M)=\partial_x(\mu N)$. When $M,N$ are sums of [monomials](monomial.md), matching their coefficients often reduces the search for $\alpha,\beta$ to a small linear system. Integrating $dF=\mu M\,dx+\mu N\,dy$ then gives the implicit solution $F=C$. Curves excluded by the multiplying factor, especially solutions on coordinate axes, must be tested in the original equation.
