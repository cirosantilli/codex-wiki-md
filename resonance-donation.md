# Resonance donation

↑ **Parent:** [Resonance (chemistry)](resonance-chemistry.md)

[Resonance donation](resonance-donation.md) is electron-pair delocalization from a substituent into an adjacent conjugated system. Drawing electron-pair arrows into the system gives contributing charge-separated structures; their stabilization can change reaction rates and preferred sites.
