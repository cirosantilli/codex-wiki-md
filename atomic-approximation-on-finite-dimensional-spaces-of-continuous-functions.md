# Atomic approximation on finite-dimensional spaces of continuous functions

↑ **Parent:** [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md)

A finite regular [complex measure](complex-measure.md) of total variation one can be approximated on a finite-dimensional [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) of continuous functions by a finite sum of phased point evaluations with $\sum_i|t_i|=1$. The [Krein-Milman theorem](krein-milman-theorem.md) and the [extreme points of the dual unit ball of C(K)](extreme-points-of-the-dual-unit-ball-of-c-k.md) give weak-star approximation by convex combinations of phased point masses. A finite [norm](norm.md) net of the [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) [unit ball](unit-ball.md) turns finitely many scalar approximations into one uniform estimate. Repeated nodes may be kept separate to retain the exact coefficient-magnitude sum.
