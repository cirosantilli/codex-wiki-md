# Oscillation decay estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Weak Harnack inequality](weak-harnack-inequality.md)

For a weak solution of a uniformly elliptic divergence-form equation, local boundedness and the [Weak Harnack inequality](weak-harnack-inequality.md) imply

$$
\operatorname{osc}_{B_r}u\leq\theta\operatorname{osc}_{B_{2r}}u
$$

for a universal $0<\theta<1$. Iteration gives local [Hölder continuity](holder-condition.md).
