<h1 id="newton-s-rings">Newton's rings</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film interference](thin-film-interference.md)

A curved transparent surface resting on a plane traps a thin film whose thickness increases radially, producing circular [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md). For near-normal reflected [light](light.md) and one relative $\pi$ reflection [wave phase](phase-waves.md) change, contact is dark and bright rings obey $2nt=(m+1/2)\lambda_0$ for $m=0,1,\ldots$. Here $n$ is the film's [refractive index](refractive-index.md) and $\lambda_0$ the vacuum [wavelength](wavelength.md). For spherical curvature radius $R$, $t\simeq r^2/(2R)$ gives $r_m^2=R\lambda_0(m+1/2)/n$. Consequently $D_{m+s}^2-D_m^2=4sR\lambda_0/n$ for ring diameters $D_m=2r_m$. A contact gap, altered reflection phase, or significant oblique incidence changes the formula.
