# Moment closure

↑ **Parent:** [Moment hierarchy](moment-hierarchy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_closure)

A moment closure replaces higher moments in a [moment hierarchy](moment-hierarchy.md) by functions of retained lower moments. It converts an infinite hierarchy into a finite approximate system.
