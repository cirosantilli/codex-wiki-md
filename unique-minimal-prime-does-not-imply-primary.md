# Unique minimal prime does not imply primary

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal prime ideal](minimal-prime-ideal.md)

In $k[u,v]$, the [ideal](ideal.md) $I=(u^2,uv)$ has [radical of an ideal](radical-of-an-ideal.md) $(u)$, a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md), and hence a unique [minimal prime ideal](minimal-prime-ideal.md). Nevertheless $uv\in I$, $u\notin I$, and $v^j\notin I$ for all $j\ge1$, so $I$ is not a [primary ideal](primary-ideal.md). The same example works after adjoining inverses of $1+u$ and $1+v$; thus it also gives an example in a two-variable [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md).
