# Complexified Minkowski spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md)

Allow the four affine Minkowski coordinates to take independent complex values, obtaining $M_{\mathbb C}\simeq\mathbb C^4$ with the holomorphic extension of the flat conformal metric. In two-spinor coordinates $x^{AA'}$, the metric is proportional to $\epsilon_{AB}\epsilon_{A'B'}dx^{AA'}dx^{BB'}$. The [twistor incidence relation](twistor-incidence-relation.md) associates a [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md) to each affine point; compactification adds the points at conformal infinity.
