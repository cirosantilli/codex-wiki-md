# Entropy bound for graphs with isolated-vertex-free intersections

↑ **Parent:** [Shearer's inequality](shearer-s-inequality.md)

If every pair of [graphs](graph-split.md) in a family on $[n]$ has a [graph intersection](graph-intersection.md) without an [isolated vertex](isolated-vertex.md), the family has at most $2^{n(n-2)/2}$ members. At each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md), its possible [graph neighbourhoods](graph-neighbourhood.md) form an [intersecting family](intersecting-family.md), of size at most $2^{n-2}$. Every [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) occurs in two such neighbourhood projections. [Shearer inequality](shearer-s-inequality.md) bounds twice the full [information entropy](information-entropy.md) by the sum of their [information entropies](information-entropy.md).
