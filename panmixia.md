# Panmixia

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panmixia)

Panmixia is random mating within a breeding population, without systematic choice by genotype or phenotype. In a homogeneous genetic model it makes the two parental gamete draws independent, yielding the [Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium](hardy-weinberg-principle.md) genotype probabilities $p_i^2$ and $2p_ip_j$. It is a modelling assumption; [assortative mating](assortative-mating.md) and [population stratification](population-stratification.md) are distinct ways a population can depart from it.
