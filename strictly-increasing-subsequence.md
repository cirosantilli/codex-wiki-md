# Strictly increasing subsequence

↑ **Parent:** [Increasing subsequence](increasing-subsequence.md)

A [strictly increasing subsequence](strictly-increasing-subsequence.md) selects indices $i_1<\cdots<i_m$ of a [sequence](sequence.md) with $x_{i_1}<\cdots<x_{i_m}$. Equal values cannot both occur in it. Its [maximum](maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) possible length is $L_{<}(x)$, with $L_{<}(())=0$; in general $L_{<}(x)\leq L(x)$ for the weak [longest increasing subsequence](longest-increasing-subsequence.md). Use strict comparisons in the [dynamic programming](dynamic-programming.md) [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md) and replace the first tail at least as large as the next value in the [binary search](binary-search.md) [algorithm](algorithm-split.md).
