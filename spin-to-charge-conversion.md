# Spin-to-charge conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatically defined quantum dot](electrostatically-defined-quantum-dot.md)

A measurement method in which spin-dependent tunnelling or allowed charge transfer maps a spin outcome onto a distinguishable dot charge state. [Quantum-dot charge sensing](quantum-dot-charge-sensing.md) then records that charge. The measurement axis or singlet/triplet observable must be specified.
