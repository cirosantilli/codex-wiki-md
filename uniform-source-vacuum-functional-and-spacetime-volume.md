# Uniform-source vacuum functional and spacetime volume

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized vacuum generating functional](normalized-vacuum-generating-functional.md)

A translation-invariant connected [generating functional](generating-functional.md) with a constant [external source](source-quantum-field-theory.md) is extensive in spacetime volume $\mathcal V_d$. Its total logarithm is dimensionless, while its density has [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) $d$. Consequently its dimensionless representation at fixed physical volume must retain $\mu^d\mathcal V_d$. For a free massive scalar with source coupling $\mu^{-\epsilon/2}h\phi$, [completing the square](completing-the-square.md) gives $F=\mathcal V_d\mu^{-\epsilon}h^2/(2m^2)$. This already disproves a literal rewriting of the full uniform-source logarithm using only $\lambda,m^2/\mu^2,h/\mu^3$ at fixed volume. In infinite volume the total functional is generally infinite; use the density, a source of compact support, or an explicit volume regulator. After writing the density as $\mu^d f$, its [renormalization group](renormalization-group.md) equation has the engineering term $+df$. Ultraviolet subtraction alone does not establish infrared finiteness for a massless constant source.
