# Subbasis of a topology

↑ **Parent:** [Basis of a topology](basis-of-a-topology.md)

A family of open subsets is a [subbasis of a topology](subbasis-of-a-topology.md) when its finite intersections form a [basis of a topology](basis-of-a-topology.md), including the empty [intersection](set-intersection.md) equal to the entire space. For the [product topology](product-topology.md), [inverse images](preimage.md) of [open sets](open-set.md) under the [product projections](product-projection.md) form the standard [subbasis of a topology](subbasis-of-a-topology.md). A map into the space is continuous if the [inverse image](preimage.md) of every subbasic set is open.
