# Radial and angular modes of an orthogonally invariant scalar potential

↑ **Parent:** [Goldstone theorem](goldstone-theorem.md)

For $V=m^2\phi^2/2+\lambda(\phi^2)^2/4$, $m^2<0$, the [vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md) is a sphere of radius $v=\sqrt{-m^2/\lambda}$. At a chosen vacuum the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is $2\lambda v^2nn^T$, where $n$ is its unit direction. The radial scalar has mass squared $2\lambda v^2$; the $n-1$ angular modes are physical [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md) when the symmetry is global.
