# Local fourth-moment restriction estimate for the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier extension estimate](fourier-extension-estimate.md)

On the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), the arclength-measure transform satisfies $|\widehat\sigma(x)|\lesssim(1+|x|)^{-1/2}$. This follows by removing angular neighborhoods of width $|x|^{-1/2}$ around the two stationary points and integrating by parts on the remainder. [Gaussian positivity for even extension moments](gaussian-positivity-for-even-extension-moments.md) bounds the weighted fourth moment for any bounded $f$ by that for $f=1$. Radial integration of $(1+r)^{-2}e^{-\pi r^2/R^2}$ with area element $r\,dr$ costs only $\log R$, proving the displayed local estimate. Modulated disjoint caps and random signs convert it into the [planar Kakeya neighborhood lower bound](planar-kakeya-neighborhood-lower-bound.md).
