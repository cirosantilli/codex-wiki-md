# ABCD law for Gaussian beams

↑ **Parent:** [Complex beam parameter](complex-beam-parameter.md)

A real [paraxial](paraxial-approximation.md) ray [matrix](matrix.md) transforms a [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md) parameter by the displayed fractional-linear map. For a lossless round trip with [determinant](determinant.md) one, a fixed point with positive imaginary part generically requires $|A+D|<2$. Scalar [matrices](matrix.md) $\pm I$ are degenerate exceptions: their fractional-linear map fixes every physical beam parameter even at the boundary. Finite apertures and resonance phases impose additional physical constraints.
