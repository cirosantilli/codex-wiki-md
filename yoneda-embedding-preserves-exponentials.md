# Yoneda embedding preserves exponentials

↑ **Parent:** [Yoneda embedding](yoneda-embedding.md)

For a small [Cartesian closed category](cartesian-closed-category.md), the [Yoneda embedding](yoneda-embedding.md) into its [presheaf category](presheaf-category.md) preserves [exponential objects](exponential-object.md). At $C$, the [exponential in a presheaf category](exponential-in-a-presheaf-category.md) has value $\operatorname{Nat}(YC\times YA,YB)$. Since $YC\times YA\cong Y(C\times A)$, the [Yoneda lemma](yoneda-lemma.md) identifies this with $\mathcal C(C\times A,B)\cong\mathcal C(C,B^A)$. Every identification is natural in all three objects.
