# Hyperbolic one-vortex at curvature minus one half

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic vortex](hyperbolic-vortex.md)

For metric $8|dz|^2/(1-|z|^2)^2$, these smooth fields satisfy the [Bogomolny vortex equations](bogomolny-vortex-equation.md) with one simple zero at the origin. Their curvature coefficient is $F_{12}=4/(1+|z|^2)^2$, and their physical [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) is $(1-|z|^2)^2/[2(1+|z|^2)^2]=(1-|\Phi|^2)/2$. The [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) is $2\pi$, and the critically coupled energy in the [conformal-surface vortex square completion](conformal-surface-vortex-square-completion.md) normalization is $\pi$.
