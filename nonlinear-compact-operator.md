# Nonlinear compact operator

↑ **Parent:** [Compact operator](compact-operator-split.md)

A [nonlinear compact operator](nonlinear-compact-operator.md) between [Banach spaces](banach-space-split.md) is a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) that sends every [bounded set](bounded-set.md) to a [relatively compact subset](relatively-compact-subset.md). Thus every bounded [sequence](sequence.md) of inputs has a [subsequence](subsequence.md) whose images converge in the target [norm](norm.md). No [linearity](linearity.md) is assumed. On a bounded domain of definition it suffices that the entire image be [relatively compact](relatively-compact-subset.md). For [linear operators](linear-operator.md), this recovers the usual [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md) notion.
