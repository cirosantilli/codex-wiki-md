# Caro-Wei bound

↑ **Parent:** [Independence number](independence-number.md)

For a finite simple [graph](graph-split.md), $\alpha(G)\geq\sum_{v\in V(G)}1/(1+\deg v)\geq |V(G)|^2/(|V(G)|+2|E(G)|)$. Randomly order its [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and retain each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) preceding all its [graph neighbours](neighbour-of-a-vertex.md). This gives an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) with the first expression as its [expected value](expected-value.md). The second inequality is the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md).
