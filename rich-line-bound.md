# Rich line bound

↑ **Parent:** [Szemerédi–Trotter theorem](szemeredi-trotter-theorem.md)

For $n$ distinct points in the real plane, $L_{\geq k}$ counts [lines](straight-line.md) containing at least $k\geq2$ points. The [Szemerédi–Trotter theorem](szemeredi-trotter-theorem.md) applied to these [lines](straight-line.md) gives the bound by absorbing its additive line-count term; bounded $k$ follows by counting point pairs. Consequently, summing occupancies of [lines](straight-line.md) with at least $k$ points by dyadic ranges gives $O(n^2/k^2+n\log n)$.
