# Cyclotriphosphazene

↑ **Parent:** [Phosphazene](phosphazene.md)

A [cyclotriphosphazene](cyclotriphosphazene.md) contains an alternating six-membered $\mathrm{P_3N_3}$ ring. Substitution at its phosphorus atoms can generate geminal, non-geminal and spiro derivatives.
