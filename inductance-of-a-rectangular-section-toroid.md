# Inductance of a rectangular-section toroid

↑ **Parent:** [Inductance of a toroidal winding](inductance-of-a-toroidal-winding.md)

For a uniformly permeable [toroid](toroid.md) with inner [radius](radius.md) $a$, outer [radius](radius.md) $b$ and axial height $h$, an ideal poloidal winding gives $H_\phi=NI/(2\pi r)$. Integrating the [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) across its section gives $\Phi=\mu NIh\ln(b/a)/(2\pi)$ and hence the displayed [self-inductance](self-inductance.md). It retains radial field variation when $b-a$ is not small. For two fully linked windings, replace $N^2$ by $N_1N_2$ to obtain their [mutual inductance](mutual-inductance.md). Discrete turns, leakage and nonlinear core response limit the ideal formula.
