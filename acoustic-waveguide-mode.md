# Acoustic waveguide mode

↑ **Parent:** [Waveguide](waveguide.md)

A separated [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) in a straight rigid channel $0<y<H$ has transverse [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md), giving factors $\cos(n\pi y/H)$ for integers $n\geq0$. The [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) satisfies the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md), and its axial factor obeys

$$
\Phi''+q_n^2\Phi=0,\qquad q_n^2=\omega^2/c^2-(n\pi/H)^2.
$$

Here $c$ is the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md). Real $q_n$ permits propagation; positive imaginary $q_n$ produces an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md). A prescribed wall [velocity](velocity.md) proportional to one transverse factor excites that mode alone in the linear straight-channel model. The physical field is the [real part](real-part.md) of the complex expression.
