# Prime decomposition of knots

↑ **Parent:** [Connected sum of knots](connected-sum-of-knots.md)

Every nontrivial oriented [knot](knot.md) is a finite [connected sum of knots](connected-sum-of-knots.md) of [prime knots](prime-knot.md), uniquely up to permutation and oriented [link isotopy](link-isotopy.md). Existence follows by induction from [additivity of Seifert genus](additivity-of-seifert-genus.md). For uniqueness, compare complete [decomposing sphere systems for a knot](decomposing-sphere-system-for-a-knot.md) and remove their intersection [circles](circle.md) by the [prime-ball exchange lemma](prime-ball-exchange-lemma.md). Once a prime ball is disjoint from both systems, its factor can be removed from both; induction then matches all factors.
