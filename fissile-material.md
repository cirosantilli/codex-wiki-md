# Fissile material

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fissile_material)

Material capable of sustaining a [nuclear chain reaction](nuclear-chain-reaction.md) with slow neutrons in a suitable configuration. Fissility involves capture and fission probabilities, neutron production and losses, rather than positive fission [Q value](q-value.md) alone. [Uranium-235](uranium-235.md) and [plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md) are common examples; fissile is more specific than fissionable by sufficiently energetic neutrons.
