# Waterbag distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md)

A [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) constant inside a prescribed region and zero outside. The one-dimensional example in the title has a [velocity plateau](velocity-plateau.md) and distributional derivatives at its edges. Its simple kinetic response can support undamped collective modes, illustrating why absence of growing modes does not imply [Landau damping](landau-damping.md). For electrons with fixed ions, $\epsilon(\omega,k)=1-\omega_p^2/(\omega^2-k^2V^2)$, so collective roots lie outside the populated [velocity](velocity.md) interval.
