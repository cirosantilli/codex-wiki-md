# Even-polynomial approximation with exact endpoint interpolation

↑ **Parent:** [Stone-Weierstrass theorem](stone-weierstrass-theorem.md)

On $[0,1]$, polynomials in $t^2$ form a unital algebra closed under [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) separating points, so the [Stone-Weierstrass theorem](stone-weierstrass-theorem.md) makes them dense in the continuous complex functions. Their approximation can preserve both endpoint values exactly. Given $\|a-p(t^2)\|_\infty<\eta$, add $[a(0)-p(0)](1-t^2)+[a(1)-p(1)]t^2$. This is still an even [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), fixes both endpoints, and changes the approximation by at most $\eta$, since its correction is a [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of the endpoint errors. Taking $\eta<\varepsilon/2$ yields any prescribed uniform error $\varepsilon$.
