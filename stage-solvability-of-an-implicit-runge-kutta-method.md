# Stage solvability of an implicit Runge-Kutta method

↑ **Parent:** [Implicit Runge-Kutta method](implicit-runge-kutta-method.md)

For a vector field uniformly [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) in the state with constant $L$, the stage fixed-point map of an [implicit Runge-Kutta method](implicit-runge-kutta-method.md) is a contraction in the maximum stage norm if $hL\max_i\sum_j|a_{ij}|<1$. The [contraction mapping theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md) then gives unique stages. This is a sufficient small-step condition; a [dissipative vector field](dissipative-vector-field.md) may permit solvability for larger steps. An [algebraic stability](algebraic-stability-of-a-runge-kutta-method.md) or [B-stability](b-stability.md) estimate compares existing stage solutions and should not be mistaken for an unqualified stage-existence theorem.
