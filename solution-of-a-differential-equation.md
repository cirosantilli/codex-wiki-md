# Solution of a differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Differential equation](differential-equation-split.md)

A classical solution is a sufficiently differentiable [function](function-split.md) that satisfies a [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) and any prescribed initial or boundary conditions. A [weak solution](weak-solution.md) instead satisfies an appropriate integral or distributional identity in a specified [function space](function-space.md). For a smooth locally [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) vector field, an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) has a unique local solution for each initial state; this does not by itself ensure global existence or boundedness.
