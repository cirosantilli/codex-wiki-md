# Two-state logarithmic portfolio with a borrowing constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithmic utility](logarithmic-utility.md)

Let a [portfolio](investment-portfolio.md) hold $x\in[0,T]$ in a [risk-free asset](risk-free-asset.md) with net return $r$ and $T-x$ in an asset returning $g$ or $b$, with probabilities $p$ and $1-p$. Assume $T>0$, $-1<b<r<g$, $0<p<1$, and $pg+(1-p)b>r$. Its state wealths are $w_g=T(1+g)-(g-r)x$ and $w_b=T(1+b)+(r-b)x$. The [expected utility](expected-utility.md) $p\log w_g+(1-p)\log w_b$ has strictly negative second [derivative](derivative.md). Setting its first [derivative](derivative.md) to zero gives the displayed unconstrained root; the [derivative](derivative.md) at $x=T$ is negative, so only the lower constraint can bind. Thus the displayed formula is the unique optimum. All equity is optimal exactly when $p\ge (r-b)(1+g)/[(g-b)(1+r)]$. The positivity of both state wealths is part of the [logarithmic utility](logarithmic-utility.md) domain.
