# Planetary vorticity

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute vorticity](absolute-vorticity.md)

The planetary contribution to absolute [vorticity](vorticity.md) is supplied by the rotating frame. Its local vertical component is the [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md) $f=2\Omega\sin\phi$. On a [beta plane](beta-plane.md), motion with northward velocity $v$ changes this contribution at the rate $\beta v$.
