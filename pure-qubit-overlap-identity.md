# Pure-qubit overlap identity

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch sphere](bloch-sphere.md)

Write each pure [density matrix](density-matrix.md) as $(I+r\cdot\sigma)/2$. The [trace](matrix-trace.md) identities of the [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md), $\operatorname{Tr}\sigma_i=0$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_i\sigma_j)=2\delta_{ij}$, make the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of their product equal to the displayed overlap. Antipodal unit vectors represent [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) states; vectors at right angles correspond to squared overlap one half. For mixed states the same [trace](matrix-trace.md) formula gives Hilbert–Schmidt overlap, not in general quantum fidelity.
