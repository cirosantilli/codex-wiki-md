# Travelling-wave stationarity of planar filament propulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Propulsive force of a periodic planar filament](propulsive-force-of-a-periodic-planar-filament.md)

For the leading periodic [propulsive force of a periodic planar filament](propulsive-force-of-a-periodic-planar-filament.md) and [power of a periodic planar filament](power-of-a-periodic-planar-filament.md) functionals, the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) for $F_x+\Gamma(\dot W-\dot W_0)$ gives $(\xi_\perp-\xi_\parallel)y_{xt}+\Gamma\xi_\perp y_{tt}=0$. For nonzero thrust, $y_t$ obeys an advection equation and the active [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) is $f(x-ct)$, with $c=(\xi_\perp-\xi_\parallel)/(\Gamma\xi_\perp)$. A static periodic shape may be added without changing leading force or power. Stationarity is a necessary extremum condition, not by itself an existence theorem for a global maximum.
