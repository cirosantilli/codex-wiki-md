# Weierstrass approximation does not preserve derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass approximation theorem](weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md)

On $[0,1]$, the [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) $p_n(x)=x^n/n$ converge in [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) to the zero [function](function-split.md), since $\|p_n\|_\infty=1/n$. But $p_n'(1)=1$ for every $n$, whereas the zero [function](function-split.md) has left [derivative](derivative.md) zero at that endpoint. Thus [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) of function values does not imply [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) of [derivatives](derivative.md). This does not contradict [uniform convergence of Bernstein polynomial derivatives](uniform-convergence-of-bernstein-polynomial-derivatives.md), which has a particular approximation construction and additional [differentiability](differentiability.md) hypotheses.
