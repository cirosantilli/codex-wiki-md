# Global qualifications of Abelian T-duality

↑ **Parent:** [T-duality](t-duality.md)

A local [Buscher procedure](buscher-procedure.md) sets a flat auxiliary connection equal to a coordinate derivative only on a simply connected patch. A full compact-circle [Polyakov path integral](polyakov-path-integral.md) must also sum flat-connection holonomies, match the period of the dual multiplier, and include [momentum and winding modes](momentum-and-winding-modes.md). A free circle isometry then gives the radius relation $R'=\alpha'/R$. Fixed points, noncompact orbits and global identifications require additional analysis; a local spacelike [Killing vector](killing-vector-field.md) does not specify these data.
