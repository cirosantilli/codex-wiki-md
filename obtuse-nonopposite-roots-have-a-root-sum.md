# Obtuse nonopposite roots have a root sum

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

For nonproportional roots in a [reduced root system](reduced-root-system.md) which is a [crystallographic root system](crystallographic-root-system.md), the negative [Cartan integers](cartan-integer.md) $m=2(\alpha,\beta)/(\alpha,\alpha)$ and $n=2(\alpha,\beta)/(\beta,\beta)$ satisfy $mn=4\cos^2\theta\in\{1,2,3\}$. At least one of $m,n$ equals $-1$, so reflecting one root in the other produces their sum. The exclusion of opposite roots is essential: their inner product is negative but their sum is zero, which is not a [root](root-of-a-root-system.md).
