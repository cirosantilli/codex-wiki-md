# Decision theory

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_theory)

The study of choices under uncertainty and consistency conditions on preferences. In a quantum application, recovering [Born rule](born-rule.md) weights from preferences requires specifying how physically equivalent games and irrelevant branch refinements affect value; it is not a consequence of [decoherence](quantum-decoherence.md) alone.
