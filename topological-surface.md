# Topological surface

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_surface)

A topological surface is a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) with a countable basis in which every point has a neighborhood homeomorphic to an open subset of $\mathbb R^2$.
