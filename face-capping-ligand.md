# Face-capping ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Bridging ligand](bridging-ligand.md)

A ligand interacting with the vertices of a cluster face. In an Mo6Cl8 core, each inner [chloride](chloride.md) caps a triangular face of the metal octahedron.
