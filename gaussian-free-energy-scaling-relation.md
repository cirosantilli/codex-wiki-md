# Gaussian free-energy scaling relation

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian momentum-shell scaling](gaussian-momentum-shell-scaling.md)

For a stable massive [Gaussian field theory](gaussian-field-theory.md), a finite blocking step yields $F(t,h)=F_{\mathrm{shell}}(t)+b^{-d}F(b^2t,b^{(d+2)/2}h)$ for [free-energy density](free-energy-density.md). The finite-step shell and measure terms are analytic backgrounds. Generic-dimension singular scaling is homogeneous after subtracting those backgrounds; resonances such as [Gaussian free-energy logarithm at effective dimension two](gaussian-free-energy-logarithm-at-effective-dimension-two.md) need additive logarithmic terms.
