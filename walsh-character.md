# Walsh character

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier-Walsh transform](fourier-walsh-transform.md)

For $S\subseteq[n]$, the Walsh character on the [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md) is $\chi_S(x)=\prod_{i\in S}x_i$, equivalently $(-1)^{\sum_{i\in S}x_i}$ in the zero-one convention.
