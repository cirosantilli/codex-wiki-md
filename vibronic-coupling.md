# Vibronic coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)

Coupling between electronic and nuclear vibrational motion. Dependence of an electronic [electric dipole transition moment](electric-dipole-transition-moment.md) on a [normal mode](normal-mode.md) coordinate can give intensity to a transition whose purely electronic matrix element vanishes at the equilibrium geometry.
