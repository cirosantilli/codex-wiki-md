# Magnetic coenergy

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md)

For reversible quasistatic magnetic circuits with [flux linkages](magnetic-flux-linkage.md) $\lambda_i$, currents $I_i$ and mechanical coordinate $q$, write $dW_B=\sum_i I_i\,d\lambda_i-F_q\,dq$. The [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md) $W'_B=\sum_iI_i\lambda_i-W_B$ obeys $dW'_B=\sum_i\lambda_i\,dI_i+F_q\,dq$. Thus $F_q=(\partial W'_B/\partial q)_{I_i}$, while $F_q=-(\partial W_B/\partial q)_{\lambda_i}$. In a linear reciprocal system $W'_B=W_B=\tfrac12\mathbf I^{\mathsf T}L(q)\mathbf I$ numerically, but the variables held fixed in their [force](force-split.md) [derivatives](derivative.md) differ. The coenergy formulation includes the effect of electrical sources maintaining the currents; it avoids reversing the sign of the magnetic [force](force-split.md) by differentiating stored [energy](energy-split.md) under the wrong [constraint](constraint-mechanics.md).
