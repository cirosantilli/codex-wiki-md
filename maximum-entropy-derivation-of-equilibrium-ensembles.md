# Maximum-entropy derivation of equilibrium ensembles

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs entropy](gibbs-entropy.md)

Maximizing the [Gibbs entropy](gibbs-entropy.md) under normalization alone gives a uniform distribution. Adding a fixed mean-energy constraint gives the Boltzmann distribution $p_n=Z^{-1}e^{-\beta E_n}$.
