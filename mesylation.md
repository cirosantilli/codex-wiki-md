# Mesylation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

[Mesylation](mesylation.md) installs a methanesulfonyl group on a heteroatom, often alcohol [oxygen](oxygen.md). An alcohol attacks electrophilic [sulfur](sulfur.md) of a sulfonylating reagent, and base removes the O–H [proton](proton.md).
