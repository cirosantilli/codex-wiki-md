# Uniform convergence in probability

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence in probability](convergence-in-probability.md)

Uniform convergence in [probability](probability.md) means that the supremum of the absolute discrepancy on the entire index set tends to zero in [probability](probability.md). It controls evaluation at random indices in that set, unlike pointwise [convergence in probability](convergence-in-probability.md). On a fixed compact index set it is a special case of [uniform convergence on compacts in probability](uniform-convergence-on-compacts-in-probability.md).
