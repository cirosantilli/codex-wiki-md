# Exoplanet thermal phase curve

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmospheric dynamics](exoplanet-atmospheric-dynamics.md)

An exoplanet thermal phase curve measures infrared brightness throughout the orbit. Its amplitude constrains [day-night heat redistribution](day-night-heat-redistribution.md), while its maximum's displacement from secondary eclipse constrains the offset of the hottest atmospheric region. Wavelength-dependent [opacity](opacity.md) makes different channels probe different pressures.
