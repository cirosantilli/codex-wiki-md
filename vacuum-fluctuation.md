# Vacuum fluctuation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum fluctuation](quantum-fluctuation.md)

A [vacuum fluctuation](vacuum-fluctuation.md) is a nonzero quantum variance in a [vacuum state](vacuum-state.md). For a single electromagnetic [normal mode](normal-mode.md), dimensionless [optical quadratures](optical-quadrature.md) $a+a^\dagger$ and $-i(a-a^\dagger)$ have zero means and unit [variances](variance-split.md) in the [vacuum state](vacuum-state.md), despite zero [photon number](photon-number.md). The mode's [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) is $\hbar\omega/2$.
