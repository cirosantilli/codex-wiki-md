# Dilaton

↑ **Parent:** [String coupling](string-coupling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilaton)

The dilaton is a scalar closed-string background field coupled to the worldsheet Ricci scalar. Its constant mode determines the [string coupling](string-coupling.md), while spacetime variation modifies the background-field equations.
