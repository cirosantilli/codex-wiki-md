# General circulation model

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_circulation_model)

A general circulation model evolves rotating fluid dynamics together with heating/cooling and suitable transport parameterizations. Atmospheric applications can couple [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md), clouds and chemistry to resolve longitudinal and latitudinal structure. For an [exoplanet atmosphere](exoplanet-atmosphere.md), it can predict winds and an [exoplanet thermal phase curve](exoplanet-thermal-phase-curve.md). Unresolved turbulence and cloud microphysics still require approximate closures.
