# Remainder

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean division](euclidean-division.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remainder)

For an [integer](integer-split.md) $a$ and positive [integer](integer-split.md) $b$, the remainder in [Euclidean division](euclidean-division.md) is the unique [integer](integer-split.md) $r$ in $0\leq r<b$ for which $a=bq+r$ with an [integer](integer-split.md) $q$. It is not the full [quotient](quotient.md) $a/b$.
