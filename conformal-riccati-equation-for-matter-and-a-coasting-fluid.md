# Conformal Riccati equation for matter and a coasting fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Flat matter-coasting-fluid Friedmann solution](flat-matter-coasting-fluid-friedmann-solution.md)

The conformal [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) is $\mathcal H^2=H_0^2[\Omega_m/a+1-\Omega_m]$. Differentiating and using $a'=a\mathcal H$ eliminates the matter term and yields a first-order [Riccati equation](riccati-equation.md). The expanding Big-Bang branch for $0<\Omega_m<1$ has $\mathcal H=\alpha\coth(\alpha\tau/2)$.
