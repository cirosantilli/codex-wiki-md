# Crystal interface anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Phase boundary](phase-boundary.md)

A crystal's interfacial free energy and attachment kinetics depend on the interface normal $\mathbf n$. The orientation-dependent energy $\gamma$ and mobility $M$ select directions even when bulk diffusion is isotropic. This supplements the transport mechanism of [crystal dendrites](dendrite-crystal.md); isotropic transport alone does not specify the number of preferred arms.
