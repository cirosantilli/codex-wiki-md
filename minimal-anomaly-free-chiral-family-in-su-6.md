<h1 id="minimal-anomaly-free-chiral-family-in-su-6">Minimal anomaly-free chiral family in SU(6)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Grand unified theory](grand-unified-theory.md)

The [SU(6)](su-6.md) [representations](group-representation.md) $\mathbf{15}=\Lambda^2\mathbf6$ and $\overline{\mathbf6}$ branch under [SU(5)](su-5.md) as $\mathbf{10}\oplus\mathbf5$ and $\overline{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf1$. Their cubic [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md) coefficients are $2$ and $-1$ in fundamental normalization. Thus $\mathbf{15}\oplus2\overline{\mathbf6}$ is anomaly-free and gives one [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) family $\mathbf{10}\oplus\overline{\mathbf5}$, a [vectorlike gauge spectrum](vectorlike-gauge-spectrum.md) $\mathbf5\oplus\overline{\mathbf5}$, and two singlets. With these [representations](group-representation.md) and their conjugates, precisely one net chiral family requires $m_{15}-m_{\overline{15}}=1$ and $m_{\bar6}-m_6=2$, so this is the smallest such choice.
