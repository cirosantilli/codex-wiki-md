# Asthenosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary mantle](planetary-mantle.md)

The [asthenosphere](asthenosphere.md) is a relatively weak region of the upper [mantle](planetary-mantle.md) below the mechanically strong [lithosphere](lithosphere.md). A seismic [low-velocity zone](low-velocity-zone.md) often occurs in this region, particularly beneath oceanic [lithosphere](lithosphere.md), but mechanical weakness and seismic [speed](speed.md) are distinct properties.
