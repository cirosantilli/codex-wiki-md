# Critical supersaturation

↑ **Parent:** [Köhler theory](kohler-theory.md)

The maximum equilibrium supersaturation along a solution-droplet growth curve. Above this threshold, an [aerosol](aerosol.md) particle can grow into an activated [cloud droplet](cloud-droplet.md) under the conditions of the parcel. In [Köhler theory](kohler-theory.md), larger dry diameter or greater hygroscopicity generally lowers the threshold; [aerosol](aerosol.md) number alone does not specify it.
