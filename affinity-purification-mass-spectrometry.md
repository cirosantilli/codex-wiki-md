# Affinity purification-mass spectrometry

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

In [affinity purification-mass spectrometry](affinity-purification-mass-spectrometry.md), a bait [protein](protein-split.md) is isolated with associated material, and [mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md) identifies candidate partners. Enrichment relative to matched controls supports association; it does not alone establish a direct [protein-protein interaction](protein-protein-interaction.md) or the number of copies per assembly.
