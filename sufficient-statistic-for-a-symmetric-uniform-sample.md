# Sufficient statistic for a symmetric uniform sample

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal sufficient statistic](minimal-sufficient-statistic.md)

For independent samples uniform on $[-\theta,\theta]$ with $\theta>0$, the absolute maximum $M$ is a [minimal sufficient statistic](minimal-sufficient-statistic.md), since the likelihood is $(2\theta)^{-n}\mathbf1_{\{\theta\geq M\}}$. The pair of sample minimum and maximum is a [sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md) but is not minimal: simultaneous sign reversal preserves $M$ and generally changes that pair.
