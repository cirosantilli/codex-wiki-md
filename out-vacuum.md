# Out-vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [In-vacuum and out-vacuum](in-vacuum-and-out-vacuum.md)

The state annihilated by every annihilation operator of the positive-frequency mode basis chosen in the asymptotic future. It need not coincide with the [in-vacuum](in-vacuum.md) on a time-dependent spacetime.
