# Signal power

↑ **Parent:** [Signal processing](signal-processing.md)

For a periodic [signal](signal.md), the mean squared magnitude over a [period](period-of-a-function.md). Its units are the square of the signal's units: a voltage signal has mean-square power in $\mathrm{V^2}$. Physical electrical [power](power.md) in a resistive load additionally divides this quantity by [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). Subtracting the squared mean gives the [variance](variance-split.md) of the signal over a uniformly sampled phase; the two quantities coincide for a zero-mean signal.
