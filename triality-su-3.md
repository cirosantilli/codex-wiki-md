<h1 id="triality-su-3">Triality (SU(3))</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(3) group](su-3-group.md)

The central element $\omega I$ of [SU(3)](su-3-group.md), with $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$, acts on an irreducible representation of [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) $(a,b)$ as $\omega^{a+2b}$. This residue is its triality. In a tensor realization the representation has $a+2b$ fundamental boxes, and a complete column of three is a singlet; removing such columns preserves the residue. Tensor products add trialities and complex conjugation reverses them.
