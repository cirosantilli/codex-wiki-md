# Relative constructible level recognition

↑ **Parent:** [Relative constructible hierarchy](relative-constructible-hierarchy.md)

For [transitive sets](transitive-set.md) $M,X$ with $X\in M$, a single [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) $\Phi(X)$ recognises the nonzero limit levels $L_\alpha(X)$. Require [finite relation closure for set-theoretic coding](finite-relation-closure-for-set-theoretic-coding.md), transitivity of $X$, that every element belongs to a [coded relative constructible stage](coded-relative-constructible-stage.md), and that every represented stage index has a larger represented index. Correct history codes have downward-closed indices with no maximum, hence form a nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md) $\alpha$. The exhaustion assertion and transitivity then give $M=\bigcup_{\xi<\alpha}L_\xi(X)$. Conversely every limit level has the required closure, correct histories cofinal in its index, and exhaustion. This formulation does not assume [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) for an arbitrary input $M$ and does not replace finite coding closure with an unjustified internal constructibility assertion.
