# Euler characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial homology](simplicial-homology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_characteristic)

For a finite complex,

$$
\chi(X)=\sum_i(-1)^i\dim_\mathbb QH_i(X;\mathbb Q).
$$
