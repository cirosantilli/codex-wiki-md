# Momentum uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md)

The [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) of the [momentum](momentum-split.md) measurement distribution of a [quantum state](quantum-state.md). A zero mean momentum need not imply zero momentum uncertainty. For a normalized state with finite moments, position and momentum uncertainties obey the [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md).
