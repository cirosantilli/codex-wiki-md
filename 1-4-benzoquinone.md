<h1 id="1-4-benzoquinone">1,4-Benzoquinone</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ketone](ketone.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1,4-Benzoquinone)

A planar conjugated molecule with two opposite carbonyl groups and molecular [point group](point-group.md) $D_{2h}$. A simple eight-site [Hückel method](huckel-method.md) model uses one perpendicular $p$ orbital on each of its six carbon and two oxygen atoms.
