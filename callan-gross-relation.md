# Callan-Gross relation

↑ **Parent:** [Parton model](parton-model.md)

For massless spin-one-half [partons](parton.md) at leading order, the unpolarized electromagnetic [deep-inelastic structure functions](deep-inelastic-structure-function.md) obey this relation. Radiative [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections and target-mass effects can modify it.
