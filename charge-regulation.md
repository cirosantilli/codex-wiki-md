# Charge regulation

↑ **Parent:** [Electric double layer](electric-double-layer.md)

A surface can change its [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) as its local [electric potential](electric-potential.md) and solution composition change, for example through reversible ionization or [ion](ion.md) adsorption. This couples the surface reaction equilibrium to the surrounding [electric double layer](electric-double-layer.md). Fixed-charge and fixed-potential models are limiting boundary conditions rather than interchangeable descriptions. Salt or [pH](ph.md) changes can therefore alter both electrostatic screening and the repulsion amplitude.
