# Mean absolute error bound for local polynomial regression

↑ **Parent:** [Local polynomial regression](local-polynomial-regression.md)

For equally spaced [fixed-design nonparametric regression](fixed-design-nonparametric-regression.md), independent mean-zero uniformly finite-variance errors, a bounded compactly supported kernel, and a uniformly invertible local [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md), degree $\ell\ge s-1$ gives the displayed bound for $s$ bounded [derivatives](derivative.md). Kernel weights have absolute sum $O(1)$ and squared sum $O((nh)^{-1})$. [Polynomial reproduction](polynomial-reproduction-property-of-local-polynomial-regression.md) removes a degree-$s-1$ [Taylor polynomial](taylor-polynomial.md). Without the degree requirement the general [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md) is only $O(h^{\min(s,\ell+1)})$.
