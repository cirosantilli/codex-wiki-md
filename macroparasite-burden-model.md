# Macroparasite burden model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical epidemiology](mathematical-epidemiology.md)

A [macroparasite burden model](macroparasite-burden-model.md) tracks the distribution of [within-host parasite burdens](within-host-parasite-burden.md) and couples it to transmission or environmental infectious stages. [Mean](expected-value.md) burden alone is insufficient when worm mating, nonlinear fecundity, [immunity](immunity-medical.md) or [host](host-biology.md) mortality depend on that distribution.
