# Fold and Hopf thresholds of a directly forced cubic amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Directly forced cubic amplitude equation](directly-forced-cubic-amplitude-equation.md)

For the [directly forced cubic amplitude equation](directly-forced-cubic-amplitude-equation.md), a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md) requires $\Lambda^2=1/s-1/(4s^4)$ and $\mu=s+1/(2s^2)$. The right side has its unique maximum $3/4$ at $s=1$, giving a [cusp bifurcation](cusp-bifurcation.md) at $(\Lambda,\mu,s)=(\sqrt3/2,3/2,1)$. The [stability matrix](stability-matrix.md) has [trace](matrix-trace.md) $2(\mu-2s)$ and [determinant](determinant.md) $(\mu-3s)(\mu-s)+\Lambda^2$. A [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md) requires $\mu=2s$, $s(s^2+\Lambda^2)=1$ and $\Lambda^2-s^2>0$. These imply $\Lambda>2^{-1/3}$; equality is a double-zero linear degeneracy rather than an ordinary [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md).
