# Linear spectral dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrograph](spectrograph.md)

[Linear spectral dispersion](linear-spectral-dispersion.md) is the derivative of the [photodetector](photodetector.md) coordinate in the dispersion direction with respect to [wavelength](wavelength.md). Near the [optical axis](optical-axis.md), $q=f_{\rm cam}\,d\beta/d\lambda$.
