# Countable subadditivity of Lebesgue outer measure

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue outer measure](lebesgue-outer-measure.md)

If the sum of [Lebesgue outer measures](lebesgue-outer-measure.md) on the right is infinite, the inequality is automatic. Otherwise, fix $\varepsilon>0$ and cover each $A_n$ by a countable family of [intervals](interval-mathematics.md) whose total length is less than $m^*(A_n)+\varepsilon2^{-n}$. These covers exist by the definition of [infimum](infimum.md). The combined countable family covers $\bigcup_nA_n$ and has total length at most $\sum_nm^*(A_n)+\varepsilon$. Take the infimum over covers of the union, then let $\varepsilon$ tend to zero. No [measurability](measurability.md) assumption on the $A_n$ is needed.
