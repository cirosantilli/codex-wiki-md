# Vertex-disjoint paths

↑ **Parent:** [Menger theorem](menger-theorem.md)

[Graph paths](path-in-a-graph.md) are vertex-disjoint if no [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) belongs to more than one of them, including their endpoints. In contrast, [internally vertex-disjoint paths](internally-vertex-disjoint-paths.md) between common endpoints are allowed to share exactly those endpoints. The [Set version of Menger theorem](set-version-of-menger-theorem.md) uses vertex-disjointness, while the two-terminal form uses internal vertex-disjointness.
