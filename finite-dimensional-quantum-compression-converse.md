# Finite-dimensional quantum compression converse

↑ **Parent:** [Reliable quantum source compression](reliable-quantum-source-compression.md)

If encoding and decoding [quantum channels](quantum-channel.md) factor through a $k$-dimensional space, their composite [Kraus operators](kraus-operator.md) all have [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) at most $k$. The [rank bound for a weighted operator trace](rank-bound-for-a-weighted-operator-trace.md) and the [Kraus formula for entanglement fidelity](kraus-formula-for-entanglement-fidelity.md) imply

$$
F_e(\rho,\mathcal D\mathcal C)\leq\sum_{j<k}\lambda_j(\rho).
$$

Thus high [operation fidelity](operation-fidelity.md) requires the leading $k$ [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the source [density operator](density-matrix.md) to carry almost all its probability. The bound applies to arbitrary completely positive trace-preserving encoders and decoders.
