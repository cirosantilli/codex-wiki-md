# Median-fin co-option hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Paired fin](paired-fin.md)

The proposal that developmental mechanisms first operating in [median fins](median-fin.md) were redeployed in paired appendages. Shared mechanisms do not by themselves specify the anatomical precursor or migration route.
