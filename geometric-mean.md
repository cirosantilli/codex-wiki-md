# Geometric mean

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md)

The geometric mean of finitely many nonnegative real numbers is the nonnegative $n$th root of their product. For two positive numbers it is $\sqrt{ab}$. The [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) compares it with their [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md). Unlike an arithmetic average, the geometric mean respects multiplicative scaling and is natural for growth factors.
