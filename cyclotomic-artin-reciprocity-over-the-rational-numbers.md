# Cyclotomic Artin reciprocity over the rational numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

For the [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md) $\mathbb Q(\zeta_n)$, arithmetic [Frobenius automorphisms](frobenius-automorphism.md) at primes $\ell\nmid n$ send $\zeta_n$ to $\zeta_n^\ell$: reduction distinguishes the nth [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md), and [Frobenius](frobenius-automorphism.md) raises residues to their $\ell$th powers. Multiplicativity therefore sends a [fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md) with positive rational generator $a$, prime to $n$, to the exponent $a\bmod n$. Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) consists exactly of positive generators congruent to one modulo $n$. This proves [Artin reciprocity](artin-reciprocity-law.md) for the [ray class group](ray-class-group.md) of modulus $n\infty$ without assuming the general reciprocity theorem.
