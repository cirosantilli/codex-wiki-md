# Interaction vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

An [interaction vertex](interaction-vertex.md) joins the field insertions from one interaction term in a [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md). Its number of incident lines is the number of fields in that term, and its momentum-space [Feynman rule](feynman-rule.md) includes the interaction coefficient and [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md).
