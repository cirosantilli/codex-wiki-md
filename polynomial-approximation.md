# Polynomial approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)

Polynomial approximation represents a function by [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of bounded [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md). On a compact real interval, the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md) guarantees [uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md) of every [continuous function](continuous-function.md) by such polynomials. The displayed best error quantifies the accuracy available at degree at most $n$.
