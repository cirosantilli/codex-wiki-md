# Nonnegativity

↑ **Parent:** [Real number](real-number.md)

The condition that a [real number](real-number.md) is at least zero. A nonnegative [flow](flow.md) in a directed [flow network](flow-network-split.md) follows the specified orientation or has zero value; a negative algebraic solution would describe the opposite orientation instead.
