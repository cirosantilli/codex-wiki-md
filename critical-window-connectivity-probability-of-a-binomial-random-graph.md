# Critical-window connectivity probability of a binomial random graph

↑ **Parent:** [Connectivity threshold in the Erdős-Rényi model](connectivity-threshold-in-the-erdos-renyi-model.md)

For fixed real $c$, the number of [isolated vertices](isolated-vertex.md) in a [binomial random graph](binomial-random-graph.md) with $p=(\log n+c)/n$ converges to a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) of mean $e^{-c}$. Its fixed [factorial moments](factorial-moment.md) tend to $(e^{-c})^k$. A [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) for small [connected components](connected-component.md), together with exclusion of intermediate components, shows that [isolated vertices](isolated-vertex.md) are asymptotically the only obstruction to connectivity. Thus the displayed [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) is the zero-mass of that [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md).
