# Higgs mode

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic Lagrangian](quadratic-lagrangian.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higgs_mode)

A Higgs mode is a fluctuation in the magnitude of a complex or multicomponent [order parameter](order-parameter.md), as distinct from a phase or orientational fluctuation.
