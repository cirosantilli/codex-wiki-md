# Neutron Stern-Gerlach splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Stern-Gerlach experiment](stern-gerlach-experiment.md)

An electrically neutral [neutron](neutron-split.md) still has a [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) $\boldsymbol\mu=\gamma_n\mathbf S$. A collimated beam passing through an inhomogeneous [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) experiences the force derived from $H=-\boldsymbol\mu\cdot\mathbf B$. In a region whose field axis is approximately fixed, a [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) neutron has two field-axis [spin eigenstates](spin-eigenstate.md), producing two allowed transverse forces and two separated beam components after a drift. A homogeneous field can cause [Larmor precession](larmor-precession.md) but cannot separate positions through this force.

The neutron's negative [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md) interchanges which spin projection moves toward increasing field; it does not change the number of branches. An unpolarized beam gives equal ideal populations. A beam prepared in one field-axis eigenstate populates only one branch, so splitting into two observed spots presupposes both components and sufficient spatial resolution.
