<h1 id="maxwell-boltzmann-statistics">Maxwell–Boltzmann statistics</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum ideal-gas statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell–Boltzmann_statistics)

Maxwell–Boltzmann statistics describes a dilute classical gas whose single-particle occupation factors have the displayed exponential form. It is the low-occupation [classical limit of Bose--Einstein and Fermi--Dirac statistics](classical-limit-of-bose-einstein-and-fermi-dirac-statistics.md). Integrating this factor over momentum gives the [nonrelativistic Maxwell--Boltzmann number density](nonrelativistic-maxwell-boltzmann-number-density.md) when the particle energy is $mc^2+p^2/(2m)$.
