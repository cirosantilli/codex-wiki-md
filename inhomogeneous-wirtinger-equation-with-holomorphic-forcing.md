# Inhomogeneous Wirtinger equation with holomorphic forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Wirtinger derivatives](wirtinger-derivatives.md)

Let $g$ be a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) on an [open set](open-set.md) $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C$. Among [continuously differentiable functions](continuously-differentiable-function.md), all solutions of $\partial_{\bar z}f=g$ are

$$
f(z)=\bar z\,g(z)+h(z),
$$

where $h$ is an arbitrary [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) on $\Omega$. The [Wirtinger derivatives](wirtinger-derivatives.md) satisfy $\partial_{\bar z}\bar z=1$ and $\partial_{\bar z}g=0$, so the [product rule](product-rule.md) verifies the particular solution $\bar z\,g(z)$. Subtracting it from any other solution leaves a [continuously differentiable function](continuously-differentiable-function.md) with vanishing [Wirtinger derivative](wirtinger-derivatives.md), hence a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) by the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md). Conversely adding such a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) changes no prescribed derivative. For $g(z)=z$ on the [complex plane](complex-plane.md), the solutions are $|z|^2+h(z)$ with $h$ any [entire function](entire-function.md).
