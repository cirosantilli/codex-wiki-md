# Northern blot

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_blot)

A [Northern blot](northern-blot.md) separates [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) by size and detects selected sequences through hybridization with a probe. Detection of a [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) supports [transcription](transcription-biology.md) of a [gene](gene.md), but does not alone establish a functional [protein](protein.md) or its destination.
