# Irregular solid harmonic

↑ **Parent:** [Solid harmonic](solid-harmonic.md)

An [irregular solid harmonic](irregular-solid-harmonic.md) is harmonic on punctured three-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) and generally singular at the origin. The [Kelvin transform](kelvin-transform.md) maps a degree-$n$ [regular solid harmonic](regular-solid-harmonic.md) to a constant multiple of its irregular counterpart.
