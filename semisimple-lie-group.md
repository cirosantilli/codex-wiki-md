# Semisimple Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

A real or complex [Lie group](lie-group.md) is semisimple when its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is semisimple. For a real group, the [Killing form](killing-form.md) then defines a nondegenerate [bi-invariant pseudo-Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-pseudo-riemannian-metric.md). Compact semisimple groups have negative-definite [Killing form](killing-form.md); noncompact examples such as $SL(2,\mathbb R)$ have indefinite [Killing form](killing-form.md). A disconnected group can also have a [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), so connectedness is a separate hypothesis when integrating infinitesimal actions.
