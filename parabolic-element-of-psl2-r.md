<h1 id="parabolic-element-of-psl2-r">Parabolic element of PSL2(R)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [PSL2(R)](psl2-r.md)

A nonidentity real [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) represented by $A\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ with $|\operatorname{tr}A|=2$. It has one ideal boundary fixed point and is conjugate to a nonzero translation on the [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md).
