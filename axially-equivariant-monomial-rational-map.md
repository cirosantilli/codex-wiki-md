# Axially equivariant monomial rational map

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational symmetry of a rational map](rotational-symmetry-of-a-rational-map.md)

For positive integer $n$, $R(e^{i\alpha}z)=e^{in\alpha}R(z)$ and $R(1/z)=1/R(z)$. For $n>1$ its [angular Jacobian of a rational map](angular-jacobian-of-a-rational-map.md) vanishes at the poles and has axial symmetry. Its combined rotational group is the group of [rotations preserving an axis](rotations-preserving-an-axis.md); for $n=1$ it enlarges to all domain rotations paired with identical target rotations.
