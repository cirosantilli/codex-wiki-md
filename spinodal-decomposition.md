# Spinodal decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Spinodal](spinodal.md)

[Spinodal decomposition](spinodal-decomposition.md) is spontaneous growth of composition fluctuations in a homogeneous mixture whose local mixing [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) has negative composition curvature. For a mean $\phi$, equal regions at $\phi\pm\delta\phi$ change the mean free energy by $f''(\phi)(\delta\phi)^2/2+O((\delta\phi)^4)$, which is negative inside the [spinodal](spinodal.md). Interfacial costs suppress sufficiently short wavelengths, while conservation of composition constrains the dynamics. Outside the spinodal but inside the [binodal](binodal.md), the uniform state is instead [metastable](metastability.md) and requires [nucleation](nucleation.md) to begin separation.
