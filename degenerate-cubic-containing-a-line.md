# Degenerate cubic containing a line

↑ **Parent:** [Plane cubic](plane-cubic.md)

A projective line $L=0$ is contained in the zero locus of the nonzero cubic $L^3$. The union of three lines is given by their product, and a conic together with a line is likewise a [plane cubic](plane-cubic.md). Reducibility and repeated factors are permitted in polynomial coverings used in [incidence geometry](incidence-geometry.md); a covering by cubics need not consist of smooth irreducible curves.
