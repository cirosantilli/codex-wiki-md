# Stoichiometric coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md)

The signed amount of species $i$ in a written [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md), positive for products and negative for reactants. Multiplying all coefficients by a factor multiplies the [standard Gibbs energy of reaction](standard-gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) by that factor and raises the [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md) to that power.
