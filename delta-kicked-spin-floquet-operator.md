# Delta-kicked spin Floquet operator

↑ **Parent:** [Floquet operator](floquet-operator.md)

For an instantaneous [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) about $x$ followed by free precession about $z$, the just-before-kick one-period operator is $U=e^{-i\Omega TS^z}e^{-i\Phi S^x}$. Sampling just after kicks reverses the displayed order. The [spin](spin.md) generators are [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) and the pulse area $\Phi$ is an angle.
