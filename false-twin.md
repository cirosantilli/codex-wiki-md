# False twin

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

Distinct [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) with equal open neighbourhoods are false twins. They are nonadjacent: adjacency would put one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) in the other's open neighbourhood but not its own. Equal open neighbourhoods define [false-twin classes](false-twin-class.md), which are independent sets and have uniform adjacency to every other such class.
