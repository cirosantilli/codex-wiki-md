# Concentration of discrete cubes at diameter scale

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy family of graphs](levy-family-of-graphs.md)

Equip the [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md) with uniform [probability measure](probability-measure.md) and [graph distance](distance-graph-theory.md) divided by its [graph diameter](graph-diameter.md). These spaces form a [Lévy family of graphs](levy-family-of-graphs.md). The [vertex-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md) reduces the complement of a positive-density [set](set-split.md)'s radius-$\varepsilon n$ [graph neighbourhood](graph-neighbourhood.md) to a [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) tail. For fixed density $\delta>0$, [Chebyshev's inequality](chebyshev-inequality.md) puts a lower density quantile at least $n/2-C_\delta\sqrt n-O(1)$. After enlarging by $\varepsilon n$, the omitted mass is at most $n/[4(\varepsilon n-C_\delta\sqrt n-O(1))^2]$, which tends to zero.
