# Orthogonalization of a compact-group representation

↑ **Parent:** [Unitarization of a compact-group representation](unitarization-of-a-compact-group-representation.md)

For a continuous real representation $\rho:G\to GL(V)$ of a compact group, average any positive-definite real inner product against normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md):

$$
(v,w)_G=\int_G(\rho(g)v,\rho(g)w)\,dg.
$$

The result is positive definite and $G$-invariant. An orthonormal basis for it conjugates the representation into an [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md).
