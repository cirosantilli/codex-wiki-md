# Minimal primes are associated primes

↑ **Parent:** [Associated prime of a module](associated-prime-of-a-module.md)

For a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) and a proper [ideal](ideal.md) $I$, every minimal prime $P$ over $I$ is an [associated prime of a module](associated-prime-of-a-module.md) $R/I$. The localized quotient has one prime, so its finitely generated [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) is nilpotent and its [socle](socle-mathematics.md) is nonzero. An element with [annihilator](annihilator-ring-theory.md) $PR_P$ can be lifted to the quotient. Clearing denominators for a finite generating set of $P$ gives a nonzero element with [annihilator](annihilator-ring-theory.md) exactly $P$.
