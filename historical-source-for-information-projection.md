# Historical source for information projection

↑ **Parent:** [Information projection](information-projection.md)

In [Information Theory and Statistics: A Tutorial by Imre Csiszár and Paul C. Shields](information-theory-and-statistics-a-tutorial-by-imre-csiszar-and-paul-c-shields.md) (2004, Historical Notes, §3, printed p. 522), the authors connect systematic studies of [information projection](information-projection.md) with earlier [relative entropy](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) minimization principles and geometric work, including [Imre Csiszár](imre-csiszar.md)'s [I-Divergence Geometry of Probability Distributions and Minimization Problems by Imre Csiszár](i-divergence-geometry-of-probability-distributions-and-minimization-problems-by-imre-csiszar.md) (1975). This is the tutorial's historical account, rather than a claim of one isolated invention. Its finite-[alphabet](alphabet.md) treatment makes the projection identities available without the additional machinery needed for general [probability spaces](probability-space-split.md).
