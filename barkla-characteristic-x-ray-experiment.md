# Barkla characteristic X-ray experiment

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray emission spectroscopy](x-ray-emission-spectroscopy.md)

[Charles Glover Barkla](charles-glover-barkla.md)'s 1911 paper describes irradiating a target with an [X-ray tube](x-ray-tube.md) and observing secondary [X-rays](x-ray.md) using an [electroscope](electroscope.md). For soft [Characteristic X-rays](characteristic-x-ray.md) from [tin](tin.md), he used a soft primary beam and placed the [electroscope](electroscope.md) close to the target to limit loss in air. Successive [aluminium](aluminium.md) absorbers changed the gas-[ionization](ionization.md) signal. Thick absorbers suppressed the soft component; a [carbon](carbon.md) radiator supplied a comparison for the surviving scattered component. Subtracting this estimated contribution left approximately equal fractional losses through successive sheets, evidence for a nearly homogeneous component. Changing primary-beam hardness could suppress excitation of the more penetrating characteristic component.[Barkla, The spectra of the fluorescent Röntgen radiations (1911), p. 396: soft tube, nearby electroscope, aluminium sheets, carbon comparison and subtraction](the-spectra-of-the-fluorescent-rontgen-radiations-by-charles-glover-barkla.md)

An [electroscope](electroscope.md) reads loss of stored [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md); [ionization](ionization.md) in its detecting [gas](gas.md) enables discharge. An [ionization chamber](ionization-chamber.md) with an [electrometer](electrometer.md) instead measures the collected [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) or [electric current](electric-current.md). These are integrating readouts: neither directly assigns an [energy](energy-split.md) to each arriving [photon](photon.md). The diagram separates these functions without claiming a surviving apparatus drawing.

The K and L components were distinguished by their penetrating power and dependence on the emitting [chemical element](chemical-element.md). Barkla's Nobel lecture, delivered on 3 June 1920 for the 1917 prize, explains that absorber-defined K radiation had seemed homogeneous, whereas later [diffraction](diffraction.md) experiments resolved neighbouring [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) in both K and L groups. Absorber tests therefore distinguished groups without resolving every line.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1901–1921; Barkla, Characteristic Röntgen radiation, printed pp. 392 and 395, PDF pp. 132 and 135: lecture date and limitations of absorption analysis](nobel-lectures-physics-1901-1921/characteristic-rontgen-radiation-by-charles-glover-barkla.md)

[Henry Gwyn Jeffreys Moseley](henry-gwyn-jeffreys-moseley.md) subsequently related the [frequencies](frequency.md) of corresponding [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) to [atomic number](atomic-number.md). The approximate relation $\sqrt{\nu}=a(Z-b)$, with series-dependent constants $a,b$, is [Moseley's law](moseley-s-law.md); its discovery should be distinguished from Barkla's identification of characteristic secondary radiation.[University of Oxford Department of Physics, Moseley and X rays: Moseley's graph and the story of Moseley and X-rays](moseley-and-x-rays-by-university-of-oxford-department-of-physics.md)

A continuous incident [frequency spectrum](frequency-spectrum.md) can contribute a continuous scattered background alongside discrete [Characteristic X-rays](characteristic-x-ray.md). Here “scattered” identifies a production process, not a universal spectral shape: scattering of a narrow incident line need not make a continuum. In particular, the characteristic component is [photon emission](photon-emission.md) after vacancy creation, rather than simply the primary beam redirected. Source hardness, target substitution and absorber thickness probe different parts of that distinction. A surviving signal alone establishes neither an additional [electron shell](electron-shell.md) nor a fully resolved [spectral line](spectral-line.md).
