# Trace-distance-preserving binary measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Variational characterization of trace distance](variational-characterization-of-trace-distance.md)

For two [density operators](density-matrix.md) $\rho,\sigma$, let $P_+$ project onto the positive spectral subspace of $\rho-\sigma$. The binary [POVM](positive-operator-valued-measure.md) $\{P_+,I-P_+\}$ has output probability difference $(D,-D)$, where $D=D(\rho,\sigma)$. Its [measurement channel](measurement-channel.md) therefore preserves this pair's [trace distance](trace-distance.md) exactly. The optimizing [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) depends on the pair; a single fixed measurement need not preserve every pair's distance.
