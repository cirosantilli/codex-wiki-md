# Mean curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_curvature)

[Mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) measures the average signed bending of a [smooth surface](smooth-surface.md). Unlike [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md), it adds the two [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) rather than multiplying them. A [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) therefore has nonzero [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) despite zero [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md), whereas a [catenoid](catenoid.md) has zero [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) despite negative [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md). The cancellation on a [catenoid](catenoid.md) explains how a curved [minimal surface](minimal-surface.md) can be stationary for [surface area](surface-area.md).

Choose a [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $N$ and the [shape operator](shape-operator.md) $S=-dN$, as in the [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) convention $II(v,w)=\langle Sv,w\rangle$. For a [smooth surface](smooth-surface.md) in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) of dimension three, define the signed scalar [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) by

$$
H=\frac{k_1+k_2}{2}=\frac12\operatorname{tr}S=\frac{eG-2fF+gE}{2(EG-F^2)}.
$$

Here $E,F,G$ are the coefficients of the [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md), and $e,f,g$ those of the [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md). The factor $1/2$ makes $H$ an average; the scalar trace convention instead calls $k_1+k_2=2H$ the [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md). Authors who use $S=+dN$ reverse both signs. A formula involving [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) is therefore incomplete unless its [shape operator](shape-operator.md), [unit normal](unit-normal.md), and averaging convention are specified.

In [orientation reversal of surface curvature](orientation-reversal-of-surface-curvature.md), both [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) and $H$ change sign, while [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) and the [mean curvature vector](mean-curvature-vector.md) do not. The units of [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) are inverse [length](length.md), compared with inverse squared [length](length.md) for [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md). The distinction matters in [soap film pressure balance](soap-film-pressure-balance.md): [pressure](pressure-split.md) couples to the sum of the signed [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md).
