# Sink equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Equilibrium point of a dynamical system](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md)

A hyperbolic [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is a sink when all its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) have negative real parts. Nearby trajectories converge exponentially to it. In a planar system this includes both [stable nodes](stable-node.md) and [stable foci](stable-spiral.md). The condition is stronger than neutral [stability](stability-of-a-numerical-method.md): a centre equilibrium is not a sink.
