# Cosmic inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_inflation)

Cosmic inflation is an early period of accelerated expansion, often modeled by a scalar field whose potential energy dominates its kinetic energy.
