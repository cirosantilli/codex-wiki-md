# Nodal plane of a double couple

↑ **Parent:** [Double-couple radiation pattern](double-couple-radiation-pattern.md)

For orthogonal slip and fault-normal unit [vectors](vector.md) $s,n$, the P radiation amplitude is $2M_0(\hat r\cdot s)(\hat r\cdot n)$. Its zeros are the two planes normal to $s$ and $n$, producing four alternating polarity quadrants.
