# Sheaf of holomorphic sections of a vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

For a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) $E$, $\mathcal O(E)(U)$ is its space of [holomorphic sections](holomorphic-section.md) on $U$, with their restrictions. It is the kernel of the bundle [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md) on smooth sections.
