# Hill equations

↑ **Parent:** [Circular restricted three-body problem](circular-restricted-three-body-problem.md)

The unforced planar [equation of motion in a rotating frame](equation-of-motion-in-a-rotating-frame.md) near a circular [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) has components $\ddot x-2n\dot y-3n^2x=0$ and $\ddot y+2n\dot x=0$. They describe [epicyclic motion](epicyclic-motion.md) and [Keplerian shear](keplerian-shear.md); mutual gravity adds close-encounter forcing.
