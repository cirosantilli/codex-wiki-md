# Pseudomonas aeruginosa

↑ **Parent:** [Bacteria](bacteria.md)

[Pseudomonas aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) is a bacterium with flexible central carbon metabolism. Its [isocitrate](isocitrate.md) branchpoint includes two regulated dehydrogenase routes and a [glyoxylate shunt](glyoxylate-shunt.md).
