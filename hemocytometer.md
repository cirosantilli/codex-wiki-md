# Hemocytometer

↑ **Parent:** [Cell counting](cell-counting.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemocytometer)

A [hemocytometer](hemocytometer.md) has a calibrated grid and depth. ATCC describes $1\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ squares $0.1\,\mathrm{mm}$ deep, each sampling $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{mL}$.[Cell counting, chamber geometry; Figure 2](animal-cell-culture-guide-by-atcc.md)

An original constructed [hemocytometer](hemocytometer.md) count uses four such squares after mixing the [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) preparation with the same [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) of stain. The dye-excluding counts are $36,44,40,40$; the dye-stained counts are $4,6,5,5$. The dilution factor is $D=2$. With $v$ the sampled [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) per square and $\bar n$ the [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md) count per square,

$$
v=(1\,\mathrm{mm}^2)(0.1\,\mathrm{mm})=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{mL},\qquad c=D\frac{\bar n}{v}.
$$

Hence the undiluted preparation has dye-excluding [number density](number-density.md) $2(40)/10^{-4}=800\,000\,\mathrm{mL}^{-1}$ and total [number density](number-density.md) $2(45)/10^{-4}=900\,000\,\mathrm{mL}^{-1}$. Its dye-excluding fraction is $160/180=8/9\approx88.9\%$. If the well-mixed preparation contains $3\,\mathrm{mL}$, it contains an estimated $2\,400\,000$ dye-excluding [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). The chamber sample itself contains only $160$ counted dye-excluding [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md).

**[Figure 3](#image-a-sampled-count-becomes-a-whole-preparation-estimate-through-volume-thermodynamics-and-dilution). A sampled count becomes a whole-preparation estimate through volume and dilution**. Original constructed [hemocytometer](hemocytometer.md) example: four $1\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ counting squares at $0.1\,\mathrm{mm}$ depth. Numbers report hypothetical counts, not recorded [HeLa](hela.md) measurements. Correcting for the equal-volume stain mixture doubles both [number densities](number-density.md) and leaves the dye-excluding fraction unchanged.

The relation $c=D\bar n/v$ follows from counting material in a known [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md), then undoing dilution. It assumes a representative mixed sample and correctly identified single [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). Clumps, inconsistent decisions at grid edges, wrong chamber depth and nonrepresentative sampling introduce [measurement error](measurement-error.md). A fixed convention such as including top and left edges while excluding bottom and right edges assigns shared-edge centers to just one square. Adding ten times as much suspension to a correctly filled chamber does not make each square's calibrated [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) ten times larger.
