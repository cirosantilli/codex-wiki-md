# Poultice

↑ **Parent:** [History of medicine](history-of-medicine.md)

A material applied locally to the body, often with warmth. In the assigned [Aretaeus of Cappadocia](aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md) passage it prepares the site for [wet cupping](wet-cupping.md); that historical purpose must be distinguished from a palliative application for comfort.
