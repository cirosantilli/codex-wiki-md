# Desolvation

↑ **Parent:** [Solvation](solvation.md)

[Desolvation](desolvation.md) removes some or all of the [solvent](solvent.md) environment of a species. Removing a [hydration shell](hydration-shell.md) does not remove an [ion](ion.md)'s [electric charge](electric-charge.md). Complete reversal of a specified [solvation](solvation.md) transfer reverses its [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) and [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) changes when the same states and conventions are used. Partial [desolvation](desolvation.md), exchange of one [solvent](solvent.md) for another, and creation of a gaseous species have different endpoints; their costs need not match.
