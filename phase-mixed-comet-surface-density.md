# Phase-mixed comet surface density

↑ **Parent:** [Comet](comet.md)

With uniformly distributed apsidal orientations, the [phase-mixed radial probability of a Kepler orbit](phase-mixed-radial-probability-of-a-kepler-orbit.md) gives the surface probability per [comet](comet.md)

$$
\Sigma_c(r)=\frac{1}{\pi^2(Q+q)\sqrt{(r-q)(Q-r)}}.
$$

For $Q\gg r>q$, this is $\pi^{-2}Q^{-3/2}r^{-1/2}(1-q/r)^{-1/2}$. Multiply by the population size for a number [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md).
