# First moment method

↑ **Parent:** [Probability inequality](probability-inequality-split.md)

This is a [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) argument, complementary to the [second moment method](second-moment-method.md). For a nonnegative integer-valued random variable $N$, [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) gives

$$
\mathbb P(N\geq1)\leq\mathbb EN.
$$

Consequently, $\mathbb EN<1$ proves that some outcome has $N=0$, while $\mathbb EN\to0$ shows that $N=0$ with probability tending to one.
