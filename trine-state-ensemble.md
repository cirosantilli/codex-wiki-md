# Trine state ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state ensemble](quantum-state-ensemble.md)

Three equally weighted pure [qubit](qubit.md) states with coplanar unit [Bloch vectors](bloch-vector.md) separated by $120$ degrees. One example is $|t_j\rangle=(|0\rangle+\omega^j|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, with $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$ and $j=0,1,2$. The roots-of-unity identity $1+\omega+\omega^2=0$ cancels the average [off-diagonal matrix entries](off-diagonal-matrix-entry.md), so $\frac13\sum_j|t_j\rangle\langle t_j|=I/2$.

This is an ensemble of preparation states, whereas a [trine qubit POVM](trine-qubit-povm.md) consists of measurement effects $E_j=\frac23|t_j\rangle\langle t_j|$. The [POVM–ensemble duality for the maximally mixed state](povm-ensemble-duality-for-the-maximally-mixed-state.md) relates them but does not identify their operational roles.
