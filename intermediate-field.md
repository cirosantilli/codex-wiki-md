# Intermediate field

↑ **Parent:** [Field extension](field-extension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermediate_field)

For a field extension $L/K$, an intermediate field is a field $M$ satisfying $K\subseteq M\subseteq L$.
