# Local survival threshold of the contact process

↑ **Parent:** [Survival probability of the contact process](survival-probability-of-the-contact-process.md)

Local survival means repeated infection of a fixed [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) at arbitrarily large times in the [contact process](contact-process.md). It implies global survival, but infection can survive by moving away from every fixed [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) on an infinite [graph](graph-split.md). On a transitive [graph](graph-split.md) the threshold is independent of the chosen [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md), and $\lambda_1\le\lambda_2$, with $\lambda_1$ the [global survival threshold of the contact process](global-survival-threshold-of-the-contact-process.md).
