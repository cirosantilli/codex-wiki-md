# Born approximation for a one-dimensional reflection coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [KdV scattering data](kdv-scattering-data.md)

For a weak potential $u=\epsilon q$, the first [Born approximation](born-approximation.md) in the KdV Schrödinger convention is

$$
R(k)=\frac{\epsilon}{2ik}\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-2ikx}q(x)\,dx+O(\epsilon^2).
$$
