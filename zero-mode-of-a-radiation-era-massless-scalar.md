# Zero mode of a radiation-era massless scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Zero mode in field theory](zero-mode-in-field-theory.md)

For a minimally coupled [massless scalar field](massless-scalar-field.md) in a flat radiation-dominated universe, [conformal time](conformal-time.md) gives $a=\alpha\tau$ and $(a\phi)''-\nabla^2(a\phi)=0$. Every nonzero [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) is therefore a free oscillator divided by $a$. The zero-wavenumber equation instead gives $a\phi_0=A+B\tau$, hence the displayed constant-plus-decaying solution. Substituting $k=0$ into a fixed-amplitude oscillatory formula retains only the $1/a$ term and loses the constant field solution. A truly general solution must include this zero mode or explicitly exclude a homogeneous background.
