# Dolbeault cohomology of the projective line times the affine line

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md)

For $X=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb C$, the two standard affine charts and their intersection form an [acyclic cover](acyclic-cover.md) for the [sheaves](sheaf-mathematics.md) of holomorphic forms. Their [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md) gives $H^{0,0}_{\bar\partial}=\mathcal O(\mathbb C)$ and $H^{2,1}_{\bar\partial}=\mathcal O(\mathbb C)$, with all other groups for $p\in\{0,2\}$ zero. For the top-form [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md), the transition $d(1/z)=-z^{-2}dz$ removes every [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) power except $z^{-1}$ from the first cohomology quotient; its coefficient may be an arbitrary [entire function](entire-function.md) on the affine factor.
