# s-p mixing in a one-dimensional tight-binding chain

↑ **Parent:** [Tight binding](tight-binding.md)

Opposite [parity](parity.md) of same-centre [s orbitals](s-orbital.md) and axial [p orbitals](p-orbital.md) makes their left and right nearest-neighbour [hopping integrals](hopping-integral.md) opposite in sign. Their [Bloch wave](bloch-state.md) sum is consequently odd in wavevector and vanishes at the zone centre and boundary. Away from those points, [orbital hybridization](orbital-hybridization.md) repels coupled levels and can produce an [avoided crossing](avoided-crossing.md).
