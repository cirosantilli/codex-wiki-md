<h1 id="constant-fayet-iliopoulos-terms-in-conventional-n-1-supergravity">Constant Fayet–Iliopoulos terms in conventional N=1 supergravity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](fayet-iliopoulos-term.md)

In the conventional two-derivative [supergravity](supergravity.md) action described by a [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md), [superpotential](superpotential.md) and [gauge kinetic function](gauge-kinetic-function.md), a constant [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](fayet-iliopoulos-term.md) cannot be added independently of the gauging. It makes the Abelian gauging an [R-symmetry](r-symmetry.md) gauging, and the superpotential must have the corresponding gauge transformation. Thus the rigid Abelian constant is subject to extra local consistency conditions. This statement concerns the conventional matter-coupled action, not distinct nonstandard FI constructions.
