# Three-axis obstruction to unitary qubit orthogonalization

↑ **Parent:** [Universal NOT gate](universal-not-gate.md)

No fixed [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) maps both [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) rays of each of the three [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) to their orthogonal partners. Such a map would give $U^\dagger XU=-X$, $U^\dagger YU=-Y$ and $U^\dagger ZU=-Z$. But [unitary conjugation](unitary-conjugation.md) preserves multiplication. The relation $XY=iZ$ would imply both $U^\dagger(XY)U=iZ$ and $U^\dagger(iZ)U=-iZ$, a contradiction.

Two axes alone do not suffice: $U=Y$ flips the X- and Z-basis labels while preserving the Y-basis labels. Thus six specified input rays already obstruct a unitary implementation, without requiring tests on every point of the [Bloch sphere](bloch-sphere.md).
