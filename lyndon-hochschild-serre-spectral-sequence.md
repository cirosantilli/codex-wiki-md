<h1 id="lyndon-hochschild-serre-spectral-sequence">Lyndon–Hochschild–Serre spectral sequence</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyndon–Hochschild–Serre_spectral_sequence)

For a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) $H\triangleleft G$, quotient $Q=G/H$, and a $G$-module $M$, the Lyndon–Hochschild–Serre spectral sequence has

$$
E_2^{p,q}=H^p(Q,H^q(H,M))\Longrightarrow H^{p+q}(G,M).
$$
