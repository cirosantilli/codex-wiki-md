# Risk-neutral probability

↑ **Parent:** [Risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md)

The probability of a market event under an [equivalent martingale measure](risk-neutral-measure.md) for a specified [numéraire](numeraire.md). It can differ from the physical [probability](probability.md). For a random [numéraire](numeraire.md), impose the [martingale](martingale-split.md) condition on asset-price ratios rather than treating the [numéraire](numeraire.md) return as deterministic.
