# Instanton size modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Instanton moduli space](instanton-moduli-space.md)

The scale $\rho>0$ in a [BPST instanton](bpst-instanton.md) changes the width of its action density while leaving total action unchanged. This reflects conformal invariance of four-dimensional classical [Yang-Mills action](yang-mills-action.md). As $\rho\to0$, [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) concentrates at the center and the smooth family approaches a bubbling boundary, not another smooth zero-size [instanton](instanton.md).
