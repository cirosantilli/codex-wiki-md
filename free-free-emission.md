# Free-free emission

↑ **Parent:** [Bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md)

Radiation from unbound charged particles during a scattering encounter, notably [electrons](electron.md) accelerated by [ions](ion.md) in a plasma. Both the initial and final electron states are free, distinguishing this process from emission involving an atomic [bound state](bound-state.md).
