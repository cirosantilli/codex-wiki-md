# Metal nanowire percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Transparent conducting electrode](transparent-conducting-electrode.md)

Formation of a sample-spanning conducting path in a network of metal wires. Connectivity depends on wire number density, [aspect ratio](aspect-ratio.md), orientation and the sample's finite dimensions; resistance also depends on the quality and number of wire junctions. A barely spanning network can have poor reliability even when individual wire resistivity is low.
