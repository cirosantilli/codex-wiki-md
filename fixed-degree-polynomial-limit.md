# Fixed-degree polynomial limit

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

If [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $d$ converge at $d+1$ distinct points, their coefficients converge, by invertibility of the [Vandermonde matrix](vandermonde-matrix.md). Their limit is a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $d$, and convergence is uniform on every compact subset of $\mathbb C$. Under convergence on compact sets, [Rouché's theorem](rouche-s-theorem.md) further locates a unique nearby zero for each simple root of the limit, for all sufficiently large indices. Finite initial polynomials need not have any root.
