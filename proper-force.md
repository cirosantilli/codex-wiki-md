# Proper force

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic force](relativistic-force.md)

The three-force measured in a particle's instantaneous [rest frame](rest-frame.md). For a [pure force](pure-force.md), $\mathbf f_0=m_0\mathbf a_0$, where $\mathbf a_0$ is the rest-frame [acceleration](acceleration.md). In that frame the [four-force](four-force.md) has zero temporal component and spatial part $\mathbf f_0$; the [four-force](four-force.md) itself is a different object.
