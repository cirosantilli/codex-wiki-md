# Worldsheet area

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet](worldsheet.md)

The area of a [string worldsheet](worldsheet.md) is computed from its [induced worldsheet metric](induced-worldsheet-metric.md). The [Nambu–Goto action](nambu-goto-action.md) is minus [string tension](string-tension.md) times Lorentzian worldsheet area.
