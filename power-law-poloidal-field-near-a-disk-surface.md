# Power-law poloidal field near a disk surface

↑ **Parent:** [Poloidal magnetic flux function](poloidal-magnetic-flux-function.md)

If $\Psi(R,0)=\Psi_1+\Psi_0(R/R_0)^\delta$ and the surface inclination to the vertical is a radius-independent $\alpha$, then $B_z=\delta\Psi_0R^{\delta-2}/R_0^\delta$ and $B_R=B_z\tan\alpha$. The [solenoidal vector field](solenoidal-vector-field.md) condition gives

$$
\left.\partial_zB_z\right|_{0}=-\frac{\delta-1}{R}B_z(R,0)\tan\alpha.
$$

The constant offset in the [poloidal magnetic flux function](poloidal-magnetic-flux-function.md) has no effect on the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md).
