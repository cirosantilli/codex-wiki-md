# Normal-stress smallness in lubrication

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

For gap $h\ll\ell$ and speed $U$, shear [stress](stress.md) scales as $\mu U/h$ and viscoelastic [normal stresses](normal-stress.md) as $\psi_iU^2/h^2$. Their force contributions scale respectively as $\mu U/h^2$ and $\psi_iU^2/(h^2\ell)$. Thus the displayed criterion permits neglecting the normal-stress corrections in the leading [lubrication approximation](lubrication-theory.md). It is weaker than requiring the normal stresses themselves to be smaller than the shear stress. Rapid time dependence can impose an additional material-time criterion.
