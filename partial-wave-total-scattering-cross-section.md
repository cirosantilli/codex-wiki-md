# Partial-wave total scattering cross-section

↑ **Parent:** [Partial-wave expansion of a scattering amplitude](partial-wave-expansion-of-a-scattering-amplitude.md)

For elastic scattering by a central potential,

$$
\sigma_T=\frac{4\pi}{k^2}\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}(2l+1)\sin^2\delta_l.
$$

At low momentum, the [S wave](s-wave.md) normally supplies the leading term.
