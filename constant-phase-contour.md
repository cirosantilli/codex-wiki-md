# Constant-phase contour

↑ **Parent:** [Method of steepest descent](method-of-steepest-descent.md)

For an exponential integral with phase $\phi$, a constant-phase contour holds the imaginary part of $\phi$ fixed. Along it, decreasing real part of $\phi$ gives [steepest descent](method-of-steepest-descent.md), while increasing real part gives steepest ascent. At a cubic stationary point, six rays alternate between descent and ascent.
