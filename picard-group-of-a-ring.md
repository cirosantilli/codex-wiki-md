# Picard group of a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Picard group](picard-group.md)

The [Picard group](picard-group.md) of a commutative [ring](ring.md) $A$ consists of isomorphism classes of finitely generated [projective modules](projective-module.md) that have rank one at every [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md). Tensor product is the group law, $[A]$ is the identity, and the module dual supplies the inverse. For an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), every such module can be represented by an [invertible fractional ideal](invertible-fractional-ideal.md) after choosing a basis over the [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md).
