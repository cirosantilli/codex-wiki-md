# Totient chain

↑ **Parent:** [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md)

The sequence $n,\varphi(n),\varphi(\varphi(n)),\ldots,1$ obtained by iterating [Euler's totient function](euler-totient-function.md) on a [positive integer](positive-integer.md). Since $1\le\varphi(n)<n$ for $n>1$, the sequence reaches 1. Counting both endpoints gives the length [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md) $L(1)=1$ and $L(n)=1+L(\varphi(n))$; increasing order of $n$ therefore suffices for [dynamic programming](dynamic-programming.md).
