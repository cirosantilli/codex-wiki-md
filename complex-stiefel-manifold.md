# Complex Stiefel manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Stiefel manifold](stiefel-manifold.md)

The space of ordered orthonormal complex $k$-frames in $\mathbb C^n$, for $0\leq k\leq n$. It is a compact [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) of real dimension $2nk-k^2$. Completing a frame to a unitary basis gives the displayed homogeneous-space description; $V_0$ is a point and $V_n(\mathbb C^n)$ is the [unitary group](unitary-group.md) $U(n)$.
