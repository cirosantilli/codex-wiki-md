# MSc in autonomous robotics course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-autonomous-robotics).

Six compulsory modules: programming for robotics; robotics foundations; autonomous robotic systems; [machine learning](machine-learning.md) for robotics; robotics research review; robotics project proposal. Hands-on robotics project spanning Michaelmas and Hilary terms. Independent research during Trinity term and the summer; maximum 40 pages.
