# Essential infimum and essential supremum

↑ **Parent:** [Infimum and supremum](infimum-and-supremum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essential_infimum_and_essential_supremum)

The essential bounds disregard [null sets](null-set.md). For a real [measurable function](measurable-function.md) $f$, its [essential supremum](essential-supremum.md) is $\inf\{a:f\leq a\text{ almost everywhere}\}$ and its [essential infimum](essential-infimum.md) is $\sup\{a:f\geq a\text{ almost everywhere}\}$. Unlike pointwise [infimum](infimum.md) and [supremum](supremum.md), these values are unchanged by changing $f$ on a [null set](null-set.md).
