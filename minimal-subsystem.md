# Minimal subsystem

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal dynamical system](minimal-dynamical-system.md)

A [minimal subsystem](minimal-subsystem.md) is a nonempty closed forward-invariant [subset](subset.md) on which the restricted [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md) is a [minimal dynamical system](minimal-dynamical-system.md). Every [continuous map](continuous-map.md) on a nonempty [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md) has one: intersections of chains of nonempty closed invariant sets remain nonempty by [compactness](compact-space.md), so the [Zorn lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) gives a minimal member.
