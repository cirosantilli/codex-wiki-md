# Climatological normal

↑ **Parent:** [Climatology](climatology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climatological_normal)

A climate statistic calculated over a stated long reference period, conventionally often 30 years. Specify the variable, site, period and averaging procedure; a changing climate makes the choice of baseline consequential.[Conventional 30-year normals](u-s-climate-normals-by-noaa.md)
