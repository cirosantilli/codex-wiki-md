# Anode slime

↑ **Parent:** [Electrorefining](electrorefining.md)

Solid residue formed or collected during anodic dissolution in [electrorefining](electrorefining.md). It can contain noble metals, precipitated salts and undissolved inclusions. Slime can retain valuable impurities, block transport or contaminate a deposit by entrainment.
