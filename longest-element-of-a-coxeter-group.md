# Longest element of a Coxeter group

↑ **Parent:** [Finite Coxeter group](finite-coxeter-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longest_element_of_a_Coxeter_group)

The longest element $w_0$ is the unique element of maximal [Coxeter length](coxeter-length.md) in a finite [Coxeter group](coxeter-group.md). It is an involution and satisfies $\ell(w_0w)=\ell(w_0)-\ell(w)$.
