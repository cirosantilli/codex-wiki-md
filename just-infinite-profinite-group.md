# Just-infinite profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Profinite group](profinite-group.md)

An infinite [profinite group](profinite-group.md) is just infinite if every nontrivial closed [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) has finite index, equivalently is open. Requiring closedness is part of the topological convention. This property says that every proper [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) quotient is finite.
