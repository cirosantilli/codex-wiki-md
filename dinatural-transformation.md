# Dinatural transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Natural transformation](natural-transformation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinatural_transformation)

For $F,G:\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}\times\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$, a dinatural transformation has components $\alpha_C:F(C,C)\to G(C,C)$ satisfying, for $f:C\to D$,

$$
G(1_C,f)\alpha_CF(f,1_C)=G(f,1_D)\alpha_DF(1_D,f).
$$

Both composites have domain $F(D,C)$ and codomain $G(C,D)$. It is the compatibility used for [categorical ends](end-of-a-functor.md) and [categorical coends](coend-of-a-functor.md), rather than ordinary naturality on just one variance.
