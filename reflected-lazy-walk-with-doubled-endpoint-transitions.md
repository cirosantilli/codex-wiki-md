# Reflected lazy walk with doubled endpoint transitions

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md)

On the finite path $\{0,\ldots,m\}$, take interior neighbor probabilities $p$, endpoint inward probability $2p$, and holding probability $1-2p$, with $0<p\leq1/2$. [Detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) gives the displayed invariant distribution: endpoint weights are half the interior weights. For $p<1/2$ the chain is irreducible and aperiodic, so transition probabilities converge to it. At $p=1/2$ parity alternates and the period is two.
