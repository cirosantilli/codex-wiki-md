# Population (biology)

↑ **Parent:** [Ecology](ecology.md)

A [biological population](population-biology.md) is a set of [organisms](organism.md) of a [species](species.md) occupying a specified area or experimental setting at a specified time. State the boundary for the question being studied: a flask, a breeding locality, or a wider region connected by [gene flow](gene-flow.md). A [biological population](population-biology.md) need not be perfectly mixed or isolated.
