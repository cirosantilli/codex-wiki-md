# Dense sublocale

↑ **Parent:** [Sublocale](sublocale.md)

A [sublocale](sublocale.md) is dense when its closure is the whole [locale](locale.md). For a map $f$, its image is dense when $f^*(a)=0$ implies $a=0$. Classically, a dense point belongs to every nonzero open.
