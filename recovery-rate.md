# Recovery rate

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical epidemiology](mathematical-epidemiology.md)

In a continuous-time epidemic model, a constant [recovery rate](recovery-rate.md) $\gamma$ gives an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of infectious duration with [mean](expected-value.md) $1/\gamma$. Other duration distributions require additional stages or infection-age structure.
