# Conformal group

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

The [conformal group](conformal-group.md) of a metric manifold consists of [diffeomorphisms](diffeomorphism.md) $f$ with $f^*g=\Omega^2g$ for a positive function $\Omega$. Its infinitesimal generators are [Conformal Killing vector fields](conformal-killing-vector-field.md). The choice of manifold matters: in flat Euclidean dimension greater than two, [special conformal transformations](special-conformal-transformation.md) can be regular on the [conformal compactification of Euclidean space](conformal-compactification-of-euclidean-space.md) while having finite poles in the uncompactified space.
