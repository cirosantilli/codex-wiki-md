# Three-subspace quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Subspace quiver](subspace-quiver.md)

The [subspace quiver](subspace-quiver.md) with three sources reduces to the problem of three subspaces in one vector space. The [three-subspace decomposition](three-subspace-decomposition.md) gives eleven indecomposables with vertex dimensions at most one, and one [exceptional indecomposable of the three-subspace quiver](exceptional-indecomposable-of-the-three-subspace-quiver.md).
