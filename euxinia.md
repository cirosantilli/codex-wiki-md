# Euxinia

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean anoxia](ocean-anoxia.md)

Anoxic, sulfide-bearing conditions in a water body. [Euxinia](euxinia.md) combines oxygen absence with dissolved sulfide, potentially adding toxicity to the habitat loss caused by [ocean anoxia](ocean-anoxia.md). It is a chemical state rather than a synonym for every low-oxygen environment.
