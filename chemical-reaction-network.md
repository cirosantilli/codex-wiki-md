# Chemical reaction network

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_reaction_network)

A chemical reaction network specifies [chemical species](chemical-species.md), reactions between them, and their [stoichiometric vectors](stoichiometric-vector.md). It can be modelled deterministically by [reaction-rate equations](rate-equation.md) or stochastically by molecule-copy-number jumps.
