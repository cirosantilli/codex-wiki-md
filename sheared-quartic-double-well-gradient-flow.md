# Sheared quartic double-well gradient flow

↑ **Parent:** [Gradient flow](gradient-flow.md)

The completed-square form gives global minima at $(1,-1)$ and $(-1,1)$, both with value $-1$, and a [saddle point](saddle-point.md) at $(0,0)$ with value zero. Negative levels split into two closed components; the zero contour joins their lobes at the saddle. The [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md) $\dot x=-V_x,\dot y=-V_y$ decreases $V$ by the [gradient-flow dissipation identity](gradient-flow-dissipation-identity.md). A negative initial level traps each trajectory on its original side of $x=0$, because $V(0,y)\geq0$.
