# Vortex (fluid mechanics)

↑ **Parent:** [Vorticity](vorticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vortex)

A fluid vortex is a region in which [velocity field](velocity-field.md) circulates about an axis or center. [Vorticity](vorticity.md) measures local rotation, while [circulation](circulation-physics.md) measures the integral of velocity around a closed path. A [phase vortex](phase-vortex.md) is instead a [topological defect](topological-defect.md) defined by winding of an order parameter; superfluid examples connect the two notions.
