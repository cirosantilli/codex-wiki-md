# Periodicity of the complex sine

↑ **Parent:** [Complex sine function](complex-sine-function.md)

The kernel $2\pi i\mathbb Z$ of the [complex exponential function](complex-exponential-function.md) gives

$$
\sin(z+2\pi)
=\frac{e^{iz}e^{2\pi i}-e^{-iz}e^{-2\pi i}}{2i}
=\sin z.
$$

The same period appears as the translation generated by the [monodromy of the complex inverse sine](monodromy-of-the-complex-inverse-sine.md).
