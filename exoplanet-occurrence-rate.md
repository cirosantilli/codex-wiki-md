# Exoplanet occurrence rate

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md)

The mean number of [planets](planet.md) per surveyed [star](star.md) in a specified parameter bin. It need not equal the fraction of [stars](star.md) hosting at least one such [planet](planet.md). Inferring it from transits requires accounting for [geometric transit probability](geometric-transit-probability.md), [transit survey completeness](transit-survey-completeness.md) and false positives.
