# Gaussian positive-part expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Truncated normal distribution](truncated-normal-distribution.md)

For $Z$ with [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md), $m\in\mathbb R$, and $a>0$, integrate $(m+az)\phi(z)$ over $z>-m/a$. The identity $\phi'(z)=-z\phi(z)$ gives the displayed [expectation](expected-value-split.md) of the [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md). This is useful for [option pricing](option-pricing.md) when a [financial payoff](contingent-claim-payoff.md) is the positive part of a [normal random variable](gaussian-random-variable.md), including a [log-call option](log-call-option.md). At $a=0$ the formula is interpreted by its [limit](limit-of-a-function.md), $m^+$.
