# Lebesgue measurable function

↑ **Parent:** [Measurable function](measurable-function.md)

A [Lebesgue measurable function](lebesgue-measurable-function.md) $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ has a [Lebesgue measurable set](lebesgue-measurable-set.md) as the [preimage](preimage.md) of every open [subset](subset.md) of $\mathbb R$. Equivalently, use every [Borel set](borel-set.md) in the target: the sets whose preimages are measurable form a [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) containing all open sets. The source carries the [Lebesgue sigma-algebra](lebesgue-sigma-algebra.md) and the target its [Borel sigma-algebra](borel-sigma-algebra.md). A [Borel measurable function](borel-measurable-function.md) on $\mathbb R$ instead requires the preimages to be Borel in the source as well. Restricting the source to a [Lebesgue measurable set](lebesgue-measurable-set.md) uses its induced sigma-algebra.
