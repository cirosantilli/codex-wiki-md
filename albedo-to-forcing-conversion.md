# Albedo-to-forcing conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Albedo](albedo.md)

For [solar constant](solar-constant.md) $S_0$ and planetary [albedo](albedo.md) change $\Delta A$, the global-mean absorbed shortwave perturbation is $\Delta F=-S_0\Delta A/4$. The factor four comes from intercepted disk area versus total spherical area. The incoming top-of-atmosphere sunlight itself is unchanged.
