# Hydroxo ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [hydroxo ligand](hydroxo-ligand.md) is coordinated $\mathrm{OH^-}$. [Deprotonation](deprotonation.md) of coordinated [water](water.md) can make it without first breaking the metal–[oxygen](oxygen.md) bond.
