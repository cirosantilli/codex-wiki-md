# RNA partition coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Messenger RNA localization](messenger-rna-localization.md)

The ratio of [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) [concentration](concentration.md) inside an assembly to that outside, with accessible volumes and molecule detection specified. A high [concentration](concentration.md) ratio does not mean a large fraction of all cellular molecules is recruited.
