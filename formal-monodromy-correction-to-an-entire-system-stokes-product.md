# Formal monodromy correction to an entire-system Stokes product

↑ **Parent:** [Formal monodromy](formal-monodromy.md)

When Stokes factors act on solution coefficient vectors, a full positive turn gives their ordered product equal to the inverse of [formal monodromy](formal-monodromy.md) for a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) system with no finite singularities. The true [fundamental matrix](fundamental-matrix-of-a-linear-differential-equation.md) is entire, so its actual [monodromy](monodromy.md) is trivial, but its normalized formal branch changes by $M_f$. Indeed, write $Y_{j+1}=Y_jS_j^{-1}$; then $Y_{N+1}=Y_1(S_N\cdots S_1)^{-1}$, whereas normalized continuation after the positive turn gives $Y_{N+1}=Y_1M_f$. Cancelling the invertible [fundamental matrix](fundamental-matrix-of-a-linear-differential-equation.md) proves the identity. The uncorrected product is identity only when $M_f=I$. For a cubic-exponential two-by-two system with $U=1,V=-1,P=1,R=2,d=3/2$, the formal exponent is $\theta=1/2$, so $M_f=-I$ and the Stokes product is $-I$.
