# Logarithmic divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Improper integral](improper-integral.md)

A [logarithmic divergence](logarithmic-divergence.md) grows like a [logarithm](logarithm.md) as a cutoff is removed. For example $\int_\epsilon^1dx/x=\log(1/\epsilon)\to\infty$ as $\epsilon\to0^+$. An integrand $f(x)=C/x+O(1)$ near zero gives $C\log(1/\epsilon)+O(1)$. This behavior distinguishes logarithmic sensitivity to an infrared or ultraviolet cutoff from power-law divergence.
