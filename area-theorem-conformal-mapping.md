# Area theorem (conformal mapping)

↑ **Parent:** [Univalent function](univalent-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_theorem_(conformal_mapping))

For a [univalent](univalent-function.md) exterior map $G(\zeta)=\zeta+b_0+\sum_{n\ge1}b_n\zeta^{-n}$, the image of $|\zeta|=R>1$ is a positively oriented Jordan curve. [Green's theorem](green-theorem.md) gives its enclosed area as $\pi(R^2-\sum_{n\ge1}n|b_n|^2R^{-2n})$. Nonnegativity and $R\downarrow1$ give the displayed bound. Laurent-series differentiation and integration on a [circle](circle.md) of radius greater than one are justified by [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) there.
