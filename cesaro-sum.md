<h1 id="cesaro-sum">Cesàro sum</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md)

The finite [limit of a sequence](limit-of-a-sequence.md) of averaged [partial sums](partial-sum.md) in the declared [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md) convention, when that [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) exists. It can exist when an ordinary sum does not, but every ordinarily convergent [series](series-mathematics.md) has the same [Cesàro sum](cesaro-sum.md) as its ordinary sum.
