# Tropopause

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary troposphere](planetary-troposphere.md)

The transition above a [planetary troposphere](planetary-troposphere.md), usually near a temperature minimum or the change to a weakly varying or outward-increasing temperature profile. The overlying [planetary stratosphere](planetary-stratosphere.md) is stable against ordinary thermal [convection](convection.md). Its pressure is planet- and location-dependent.
