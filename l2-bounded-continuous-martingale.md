# L2-bounded continuous martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time martingale](continuous-time-martingale.md)

A continuous [martingale](martingale-split.md) $M$ is L2-bounded when $\sup_{t\geq0}\mathbb E|M_t|^2<\infty$. The [L2 martingale convergence theorem](l2-martingale-convergence-theorem.md) gives a terminal value $M_\infty$ with convergence in $L^2$ and [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md). The resulting [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) identity is $M_t=\mathbb E[M_\infty\mid\mathcal F_t]$. Boundedness on each separate finite horizon is a weaker condition. The subspace with $M_0=0$ is commonly denoted $\mathcal M_0^2$.
