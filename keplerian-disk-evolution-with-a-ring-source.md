# Keplerian disk evolution with a ring source

↑ **Parent:** [Keplerian viscous diffusion equation](keplerian-viscous-diffusion-equation.md)

For an axisymmetric [thin disc](thin-disk.md) supplied at $R_0$ with the local [Keplerian rotation](keplerian-disk.md) specific angular momentum, the displayed source augments the [Keplerian viscous diffusion equation](keplerian-viscous-diffusion-equation.md). The radial velocity remains $v_R=-3(\Sigma R^{1/2})^{-1}\partial_R(\nu\Sigma R^{1/2})$: subtracting local specific angular momentum times [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) from [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) cancels the source term. The normalization of the [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) makes the integrated supplied mass per unit time equal to $\dot M(t)$.
