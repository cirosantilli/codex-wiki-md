# Spacelike infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md)

[Spacelike infinity](spacelike-infinity.md) is the ideal endpoint of spatial directions in the [Minkowski conformal compactification](minkowski-conformal-compactification.md), represented by $(T,R)=(0,\pi)$. Unlike [null infinity](null-infinity.md), it is a vertex of the radial [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md).
