# Quantum premeasurement

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md)

The coherent interaction that correlates a system observable with detector records before selecting an outcome. An ideal coupling maps $|s_j\rangle|d_0\rangle$ to $|s_j\rangle|d_j\rangle$. Linearity then maps a superposition to $\sum_j c_j|s_jd_j\rangle$. The full state can remain pure and entangled, whereas a nonselective [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) removes coherence between different record sectors. Conditioning on one observed record is a third, selective operation.
