# McMillan inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Kraft–McMillan inequality](kraft-mcmillan-inequality.md)

The lengths of a finite [uniquely decodable code](decipherable-code.md) satisfy $\sum_a2^{-l(a)}\leq1$, even when the code is not a [prefix code](prefix-code.md). Distinct concatenations of $r$ codewords remain distinct; counting the possible binary strings of each total length bounds $(\sum_a2^{-l(a)})^r$ by $r\max_a l(a)$. Taking $r$th roots proves the claim.
