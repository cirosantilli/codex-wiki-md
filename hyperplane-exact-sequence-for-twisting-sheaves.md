# Hyperplane exact sequence for twisting sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Twisting sheaf on projective space](twisting-sheaf-on-projective-space.md)

For a hyperplane $H\subset\mathbf P^n$ with inclusion $i$, multiplication by its defining linear [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) gives the [short exact sequence of sheaves](short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md)

$$
0\to\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^n}(m-1)\to\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^n}(m)\to i_*\mathcal O_H(m)\to0.
$$

The [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) relates twists in consecutive degrees and consecutive dimensions. Surjectivity of restriction on global homogeneous polynomials, together with eventual positive-twist vanishing, proves vanishing of intermediate cohomology by descending induction. The top-degree dimensions satisfy $h_n(m-1)=h_n(m)+h_{n-1}(m)$ for $n\geq2$, where $h_r(m)=\dim H^r(\mathbf P^r,\mathcal O(m))$.
