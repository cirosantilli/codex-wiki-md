# Periodic extension

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic function](periodic-function.md)

For $T>0$, values of a [function](function-split.md) $f$ on a half-open interval $[a,a+T)$ determine its periodic extension to the [real line](real-line.md): choose the unique [integer](integer-split.md) $k$ with $x-kT\in[a,a+T)$ and set $F(x)=f(x-kT)$. The resulting [periodic function](periodic-function.md) has [period](period-of-a-function.md) $T$. If the endpoint limits of $f$ differ, the extension has a [jump discontinuity](jump-discontinuity.md) at every period boundary. Under the usual piecewise-smooth convergence hypotheses, its [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) takes the average of the two one-sided limits there; an assigned value at an isolated endpoint does not change the [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md).
