# Wigner small-d matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Wigner D-matrix](wigner-d-matrix.md)

The rotation matrix about the $y$ axis in a spin-$j$ angular-momentum representation. For $j=1$, $d^1_{-1,+1}=(1-\cos\theta)/2$; its square gives the polar dependence of a transition between those [spin projections](spin.md).
