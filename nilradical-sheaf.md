# Nilradical sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Structure sheaf of a scheme](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md)

The nilradical sheaf of a [scheme](scheme.md) is the [ideal sheaf](ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) of locally [nilpotent](nilpotent.md) sections in its [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md). On an [affine open subscheme](affine-open-subscheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}A$ it is the [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) associated to the [nilradical](nilradical.md) of $A$, since the [nilradical](nilradical.md) commutes with [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md). Its quotient defines the [reduced induced subscheme](reduced-induced-subscheme.md). On a [Noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md) it has a uniform nilpotence exponent: take nilpotence bounds on a finite [affine open cover](affine-open-cover.md) and use their maximum. On a general [scheme](scheme.md), a locally [nilpotent](nilpotent.md) section need not have a single nilpotence exponent over a non-quasi-compact [open subset](open-set.md).
