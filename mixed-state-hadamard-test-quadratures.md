# Mixed-state Hadamard-test quadratures

↑ **Parent:** [Hadamard test](hadamard-test.md)

For the [Hadamard test](hadamard-test.md) with a [density operator](density-matrix.md) $\rho$ in the auxiliary register, the output control [qubit](qubit.md) has [Pauli Z gate](pauli-z-gate.md) expectation $\operatorname{Re}m$ and [Pauli Y gate](pauli-y-gate.md) expectation $-\operatorname{Im}m$, where $m=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho U)$. The minus sign comes from the final [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md), since $HYH=-Y$. Measuring in $(|0\rangle\pm i|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ therefore estimates the imaginary part using the minus-outcome frequency minus the plus-outcome frequency.
