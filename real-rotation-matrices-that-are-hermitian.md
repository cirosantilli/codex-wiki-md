# Real rotation matrices that are Hermitian

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md)

A real [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) is a [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) because it is an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md). It is a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) precisely when it is symmetric, equivalently when its square is the identity. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are then $\pm1$. In three dimensions a proper rotation is therefore Hermitian only for the identity or a half-turn. A half-turn about a unit vector $n$ has matrix $2nn^T-I$: it fixes $n$ and reverses the perpendicular plane. For every real rotation $R$, the difference $R-R^T$ is [anti-Hermitian](skew-hermitian-matrix.md); it is also Hermitian only in the zero case.
