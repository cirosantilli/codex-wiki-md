# Seed-bank population recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Age-structured population model](age-structured-population-model.md)

A [seed-bank population recurrence](seed-bank-population-recurrence.md) includes delayed recruitment from seeds that survive more than one season. For $a,b\ge0$, recruitment function $N$ with $N(0)=0$ and $N'(0)=1$ gives extinction multipliers solving $\lambda^2-a\lambda-b=0$. If $N$ is strictly concave, bounded and increasing, a positive fixed point exists uniquely exactly when $a+b>1$; it solves $M=N((a+b)M)$. The dominant extinction multiplier then exceeds one.
