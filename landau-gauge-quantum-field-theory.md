# Landau gauge (quantum field theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant gauge](covariant-gauge.md)

The $\xi\to0$ limit of [covariant gauge](covariant-gauge.md) fixing by $-(\partial_\mu A^\mu)^2/(2\xi)$. It imposes the divergence condition sharply and gives a transverse [photon propagator](photon-propagator.md). This is distinct from the vector-potential choice called [Landau gauge for a uniform magnetic field](landau-gauge-for-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md) when computing [Landau levels](landau-level.md).
