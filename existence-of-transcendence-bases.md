# Existence of transcendence bases

↑ **Parent:** [Transcendence basis](transcendence-basis.md)

Apply [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) to subsets of a [field extension](field-extension.md) with [algebraic independence](algebraic-independence.md), ordered by inclusion. The union of a chain is independent because any polynomial relation involves only finitely many distinct elements, all of which lie in one member of the chain. A maximal independent set $B$ is a [transcendence basis](transcendence-basis.md): if any element were transcendental over $K(B)$, adjoining it would preserve independence, by clearing denominators, contradicting maximality. Tuple entries must be distinct in the set-based independence definition; otherwise the nonzero polynomial $X_1-X_2$ vanishes on a repeated pair and excludes every nonempty set.
