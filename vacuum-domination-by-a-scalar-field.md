# Vacuum domination by a scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Inflaton](inflaton.md)

A homogeneous canonical [scalar field](scalar-field.md) with positive [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) has [energy density](energy-density.md) $\rho_\phi=\dot\phi^2/2+V$ and [pressure](pressure.md) $P_\phi=\dot\phi^2/2-V$. When its [potential energy](potential-energy.md) dominates its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), the [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) approaches $-1$. If other components and spatial curvature are negligible, the [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives [accelerating expansion](accelerating-expansion-of-the-universe.md). Small initial [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is not enough to ensure long [cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md); the field must also remain on a sufficiently flat part of the [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md), as measured by the [potential slow-roll parameters](potential-slow-roll-parameter.md).
