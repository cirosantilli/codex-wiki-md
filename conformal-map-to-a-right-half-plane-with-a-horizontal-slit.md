# Conformal map to a right half-plane with a horizontal slit

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz-Christoffel mapping](schwarz-christoffel-mapping.md)

The [principal square root](principal-square-root-of-a-complex-number.md) maps the upper $Z$ [half-plane](half-plane.md) bijectively onto $\Re z>0$ with the ray $[1,\infty)$ removed. The [prevertex](prevertex-of-a-schwarz-christoffel-map.md) zero is the slit tip, and $\pm1$ represent the two ends at infinity. The inverse is the unique root $Z^2=1-z^{-2}$ with positive [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md). Pulling back $\pi^{-1}\arg((Z-1)/(Z+1))$ gives the bounded [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) with value one on the slit banks and zero on the imaginary axis. The [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $\Im[Z/(1-Z^2)]$ has zero finite [boundary values](boundary-value.md) but grows at infinity, explaining the role of boundedness.
