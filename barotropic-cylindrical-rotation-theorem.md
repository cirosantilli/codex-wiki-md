# Barotropic cylindrical rotation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md)

In a steady axisymmetric [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md) with purely azimuthal velocity $r\Omega\mathbf e_\phi$ and a conservative body force, the [barotropic enthalpy function](barotropic-enthalpy-function.md) obeys $\nabla(w+\Phi)=r\Omega^2\mathbf e_r$. Taking its curl yields the displayed cylindrical rotation law. The first integral is $w+\Phi-\int r\Omega^2dr=C$ in a connected fluid region. Baroclinic pressure and density need not satisfy this conclusion.
