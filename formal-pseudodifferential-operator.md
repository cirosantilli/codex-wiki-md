# Formal pseudodifferential operator

↑ **Parent:** [Differential operator](differential-operator.md)

A [formal pseudodifferential operator](formal-pseudodifferential-operator.md) is a series in [integer](integer.md) powers of $\partial=d/dx$, bounded above but possibly infinite towards negative powers. Its [coefficients](coefficient.md) are [smooth functions](smooth-function.md). Multiplication uses the [formal pseudodifferential composition rule](formal-pseudodifferential-composition-rule.md). Negative powers are formal inverses, not globally defined integration operators on all [smooth functions](smooth-function.md). Every [coefficient](coefficient.md) in a product is a finite sum, so multiplication is defined without analytic convergence.
