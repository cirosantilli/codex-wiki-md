# Detritus

↑ **Parent:** [Ecosystem](ecosystem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detritus)

[Detritus](detritus.md) is nonliving organic material, including dead remains and organic wastes. It is a resource for [decomposers](decomposer.md), not itself an [organism](organism-split.md) or a [predator](predator.md). In the illustrative [food web](food-web.md), the dashed arrow from [algae](algae.md) to [detritus](detritus.md) denotes a material transfer; the solid arrow from [detritus](detritus.md) to [decomposers](decomposer.md) denotes resource use.
