# Intensity measure of a point process

↑ **Parent:** [Point process](point-process.md)

The [intensity measure of a point process](intensity-measure-of-a-point-process.md) $\Pi$ is the measure $\mu(A)=\mathbb E[\Pi(A)]$. It can have infinite total mass even when counts on bounded or finite-intensity sets are finite. For a [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md), this measure also specifies every finite-set count distribution.
