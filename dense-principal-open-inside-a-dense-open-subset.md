# Dense principal open inside a dense open subset

↑ **Parent:** [Principal open subscheme](principal-open-subscheme.md)

In an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) with [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) $A$, every dense open subset $U$ contains a dense [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md). Write its closed complement as $V(I)$. Density says that $I$ is contained in none of the finitely many [minimal prime ideals](minimal-prime-ideal.md) of the [reduced ring](reduced-ring.md) $A$. [Prime avoidance](prime-avoidance.md) supplies $s\in I$ outside all these primes. Thus $D(s)\subseteq U$, and $s$ is a [non-zero-divisor](non-zero-divisor.md). [Noetherian Zariski topology](noetherian-zariski-topology.md) also makes $U$ a finite union of principal opens; one can add $D(s)$ to this cover. An arbitrary existing cover need not contain a dense member when the [variety](algebraic-variety.md) is reducible.
