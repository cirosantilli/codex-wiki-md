# Planning horizon

↑ **Parent:** [SATPlan](satplan.md)

The number of action layers allowed in a bounded [automated planning](automated-planning.md) encoding. Sequential layers permit at most one physical action, while a parallel semantics may permit several compatible actions. A sufficiently large horizon guarantees finding a solution only for a solvable input; shorter [plans](plan.md) require idle layers or testing the successive smaller horizons.
