# Dense clique family blow-up lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Graph blow-up](graph-blow-up.md)

A family of positively many $s$-cliques forces a logarithmic balanced [graph blow-up](graph-blow-up.md), which can also contain a matching of that many members of the family. To induct on $s$, prune faces with few extensions, apply induction to the remaining $(s-1)$-faces, and use a matching of those faces as one side of a bipartite incidence [graph](graph-split.md). The [common neighbourhood from bipartite density](common-neighbourhood-from-bipartite-density.md) estimate selects logarithmically many disjoint faces with polynomially many common extension [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md).
