# Countable forcing preserves Suslin trees

↑ **Parent:** [Countable forcing](countable-forcing.md)

A ground-model [Suslin tree](suslin-tree.md) remains Suslin after countable forcing. A new uncountable chain or antichain would contain an uncountable ground-model subset by the [ground-model uncountable subset lemma for countable forcing](ground-model-uncountable-subset-lemma-for-countable-forcing.md).
