# Strong epimorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Epimorphism](epimorphism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_epimorphism)

A strong epimorphism has the left lifting property with respect to every [monomorphism](monomorphism.md). Thus every commutative square with the strong epimorphism on the left and a monomorphism on the right has a diagonal filler.
