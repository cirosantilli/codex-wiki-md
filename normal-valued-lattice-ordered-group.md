# Normal-valued lattice-ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md)

A [lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md) is normal-valued when every [value in a lattice-ordered group](value-in-a-lattice-ordered-group.md) is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of its cover. Normality in the cover is weaker than normality in the entire group. Every [linearly ordered group](linearly-ordered-group.md) is normal-valued.
