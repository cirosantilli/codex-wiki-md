# Stationary wave amplitude in a stratified wavespeed

↑ **Parent:** [Wave-action transport for a variable-speed wave](wave-action-transport-for-a-variable-speed-wave.md)

For an individual monochromatic component in $c=c(Y)$, conserved $\omega,k$ give $\ell=\sqrt{\omega^2/c^2-k^2}$. With amplitude independent of $X,T$, [wave-action transport for a variable-speed wave](wave-action-transport-for-a-variable-speed-wave.md) conserves $c^2\ell A^2$ on a positive-$\ell$ branch, hence $A=\mu/(c\sqrt\ell)$. The divergence at $\ell=0$ signals failure of the outer [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md).
