# Polarization perpendicular to the plane of incidence

↑ **Parent:** [Plane of incidence](plane-of-incidence.md)

For reflection or refraction at a flat isotropic interface, [s-polarization](polarization-perpendicular-to-the-plane-of-incidence.md) has its [electric field](electric-field-split.md) perpendicular to the plane containing the incident [wavevector](wavevector.md) and interface normal. At an isotropic [dielectric](dielectric.md) boundary it is an eigenpolarization of the Fresnel reflection problem. The label is relative to the specified interface, not an absolute direction in space.
