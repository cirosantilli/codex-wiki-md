# Separability criterion using a pth power

↑ **Parent:** [Separable algebraic element](separable-algebraic-element.md)

For an [algebraic element](algebraic-element.md) $\alpha$ over a [field](field.md) $F$ of characteristic $p>0$, suppose first that $\alpha$ is a [separable algebraic element](separable-algebraic-element.md). Its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) over $E=F(\alpha^p)$ divides its original [separable polynomial](separable-polynomial.md) and also $T^p-\alpha^p=(T-\alpha)^p$. It is therefore separable with only one distinct root, so has degree one and $\alpha\in E$. Conversely, if the irreducible [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) $m$ is inseparable, its [formal derivative](formal-derivative.md) vanishes and $m(T)=g(T^p)$ for some $g$ of degree $\deg(m)/p$. Thus $[F(\alpha^p):F]\leq\deg(m)/p<[F(\alpha):F]$, proving the converse.
