# Holonomic constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint (mechanics)](constraint-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holonomic_constraint)

A [holonomic constraint](holonomic-constraint.md) is a relation $f(q,t)=0$ involving generalized coordinates and possibly time, rather than a genuinely nonintegrable velocity relation. Independent smooth constraints of full row rank define a local configuration submanifold. Ideal constraint forces are normal to its tangent virtual displacements and can be represented by [Lagrange multipliers](lagrange-multiplier.md). A time-dependent [holonomic constraint](holonomic-constraint.md) need not conserve mechanical [energy](energy.md).
