# Theta-solvent brush height

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer brush](polymer-brush.md)

In the [Alexander-de Gennes brush model](alexander-de-gennes-brush-model.md), a [theta solvent](theta-solvent.md) gives $D\sim ag^{1/2}$. Stacking $N/g$ blobs then gives the displayed height, with mean [polymer](polymer-split.md) [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) $\phi\sim(sa^2)^{1/2}$ for segment [volume](volume.md) $a^3$. Cancellation of the two-segment [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md) does not remove three-segment repulsion or make the crowded layer freely interpenetrable at no cost.
