# Oxyanion

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

An [oxyanion](oxyanion.md) carries negative charge on [oxygen](oxygen.md), often with delocalization onto neighboring atoms. Stabilization of a developing [oxyanion](oxyanion.md) is important in many hydrolytic [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) pathways.
