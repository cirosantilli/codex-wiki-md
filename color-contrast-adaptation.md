# Color contrast adaptation

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic adaptation](chromatic-adaptation.md)

Changes following exposure to variation around a mean [color](color.md) or [luminance](luminance.md). Holding the mean input fixed helps distinguish adaptation to variation from a simple account based on changed average cone catch, while receptor nonlinearities and task factors still require controls.
