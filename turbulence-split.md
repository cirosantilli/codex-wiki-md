# Turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turbulence)

Turbulence is irregular fluid motion with interacting velocity fluctuations over a range of scales. It can mix a [particle suspension](suspension-chemistry.md) and promote [fluid entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md) into a [turbulent plume](turbulent-plume-split.md).
