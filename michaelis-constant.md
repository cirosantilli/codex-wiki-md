# Michaelis constant

↑ **Parent:** [Michaelis-Menten equation](michaelis-menten-equation.md)

The [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) [concentration](concentration.md) giving half the limiting [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md) in the [Michaelis-Menten equation](michaelis-menten-equation.md). In the elementary mechanism $E+S\rightleftharpoons ES\to E+P$, it is $K_M=(k_{-1}+k_{\mathrm{cat}})/k_1$ under the [quasi-steady-state approximation](quasi-steady-state-approximation.md), whereas the binding [dissociation constant](dissociation-constant.md) is $K_d=k_{-1}/k_1$.
