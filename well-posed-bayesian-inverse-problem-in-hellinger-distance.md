# Well-posed Bayesian inverse problem in Hellinger distance

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian inverse problem](bayesian-inverse-problem.md)

A Bayesian inverse problem is well posed in Hellinger distance when every datum determines a unique posterior and the posterior depends continuously on the data in [Hellinger distance](hellinger-distance.md). Since posterior measures are dominated by the prior and squared Hellinger distance is at most twice [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md), total-variation well-posedness implies Hellinger well-posedness.
