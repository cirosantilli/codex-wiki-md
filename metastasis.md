# Metastasis

↑ **Parent:** [Medicine](medicine.md)

The establishment of malignant growth at a site distant from the original [tumour](tumour.md), requiring successful dissemination, survival and expansion. Circulating [tumour](tumour.md) cells alone do not establish that a [metastasis](metastasis.md) has formed.
