# Axial strain rate

↑ **Parent:** [Uniaxial extensional flow](uniaxial-extensional-flow.md)

The axial strain rate is the axial component of the [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md). For axial [velocity](velocity.md) $w(z)$ it is $E=\partial w/\partial z$. In a uniform [uniaxial extensional flow](uniaxial-extensional-flow.md), $w=Ez$ and [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) requires $u_r=-Er/2$; positive $E$ stretches the axial direction and contracts the transverse directions.
