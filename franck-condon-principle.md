# Franck-Condon principle

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic transition](electronic-transition.md)

Electronic excitation occurs rapidly compared with nuclear motion. In the Condon approximation, vibronic intensities are proportional to squared overlaps of initial and final vibrational [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md); vertical arrows on a potential diagram summarize this overlap rather than a nuclear trajectory.
