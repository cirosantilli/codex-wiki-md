# Linear low-temperature heat capacity of a two-dimensional Fermi gas

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic low-temperature energy correction for constant density of states](quadratic-low-temperature-energy-correction-for-constant-density-of-states.md)

For a two-dimensional [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md) of $N$ spin-one-half particles with [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) $E_F$, the constant [density of states](density-of-states.md) makes the low-temperature [internal energy](internal-energy.md) correction and [heat capacity at constant area](heat-capacity-at-constant-area.md)

$$
U(T)-U(0)=\frac{\pi^2N}{6E_F}(k_BT)^2+o(T^2),
\qquad
C_A=\frac{\pi^2Nk_B^2}{3E_F}T+o(T).
$$

Thus its low-temperature heat capacity obeys a [power law](power-law.md) with exponent one.
