# Spectral measure of a random harmonic oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral measure of a stationary time series](spectral-measure-of-a-stationary-time-series.md)

For uncorrelated zero-mean unit-variance amplitudes $A,B$, the process $A\cos(\omega_0t)+B\sin(\omega_0t)$ has covariance $\cos(\omega_0k)$ and hence the displayed two-atom [spectral measure of a stationary time series](spectral-measure-of-a-stationary-time-series.md). Independence or Gaussian amplitudes are unnecessary for [weak stationarity](weakly-stationary-process.md). A phase shift need not preserve the whole joint amplitude distribution, so strict stationarity cannot be inferred from these second moments alone.
