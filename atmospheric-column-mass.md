# Atmospheric column mass

↑ **Parent:** [Atmosphere](atmosphere.md)

The [mass](mass.md) per unit horizontal area above a level in an [atmosphere](atmosphere.md). With constant [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md), [hydrostatic balance](hydrostatic-balance.md) gives $m=(p_s-p_{\rm top})/g$. This relation connects an observed surface [pressure](pressure-split.md) to the total reservoir [mass](mass.md) without assuming an isothermal [temperature](temperature-split.md) profile.
