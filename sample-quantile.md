# Sample quantile

↑ **Parent:** [Quantile function](quantile-function.md)

For $0<p<1$, the [sample quantile](sample-quantile.md) is the generalized inverse of the [empirical distribution function](empirical-distribution-function.md): $\inf\{x:\widehat F_n(x)\geq p\}$. With ordered observations this is $X_{(\lceil np\rceil)}$, including ties. If the population [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is continuous and positive at its unique $p$th [quantile](quantile-function.md), the centered quantile has asymptotic variance $p(1-p)/f(F^{-1}(p))^2$ under scaling by $\sqrt n$.
