# Van Vleck determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Semiclassical propagator](semiclassical-propagator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Vleck_determinant)

The Van Vleck determinant is the determinant of mixed endpoint derivatives of a classical action. It measures how nearby classical trajectories focus and supplies the fluctuation prefactor of the [semiclassical propagator](semiclassical-propagator.md).
