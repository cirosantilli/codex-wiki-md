# Normal-ordered boson pair count

↑ **Parent:** [Number operator](number-operator.md)

For a [bosonic](boson.md) mode satisfying the [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) $[a,a^\dagger]=1$, the [number operator](number-operator.md) $n=a^\dagger a$ obeys $n^2=a^\dagger aa^\dagger a=(a^\dagger)^2a^2+n$. Thus a two-body on-site interaction is proportional to $n(n-1)$, with no self-interaction. In a [fixed-number two-mode condensate](fixed-number-two-mode-condensate.md) with probability $q$ of occupying this mode, $\langle n(n-1)\rangle=N(N-1)q^2$ by the [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md).
