# Rocket propellant

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

[Rocket propellant](rocket-propellant.md) is the onboard material expelled to produce [rocket thrust](rocket-thrust.md). A chemical rocket normally carries both fuel and oxidizer; both contribute to its [mass](mass.md) and to the expelled material. In the ideal [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md), $M(t)=M_{\mathrm{dry}}+M_{\mathrm{propellant}}(t)$, with fixed [rocket dry mass](rocket-dry-mass.md) and decreasing remaining [rocket propellant](rocket-propellant.md). [NASA Glenn Research Center: Rocket Thrust](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/rocket-thrust/)
