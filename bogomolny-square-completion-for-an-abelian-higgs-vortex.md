# Bogomolny square completion for an Abelian Higgs vortex

↑ **Parent:** [Bogomolny vortex equation](bogomolny-vortex-equation.md)

With $D_i=\partial_i-iA_i$ and $B=\partial_1A_2-\partial_2A_1$, use the critical-coupling energy $E=\int[B^2/2+|D_i\phi|^2/2+(1-|\phi|^2)^2/8]d^2x$. For positive [vortex number](vortex-number.md), integration by parts gives

$$
E=\int\left[\frac12|(D_1+iD_2)\phi|^2+\frac12\left(B-\frac{1-|\phi|^2}{2}\right)^2\right]d^2x+\frac12\int B\,d^2x.
$$

The boundary divergence vanishes for the usual decaying vortex fields, and [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) is $2\pi N$. The two squares vanish exactly at the [Bogomolny vortex equations](bogomolny-vortex-equation.md). Reversing both signs treats negative $N$. Energy coefficients and the numerical bound change together under other normalizations.
