<h1 id="finite-condition-levy-collapse">Finite-condition Lévy collapse</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Forcing](forcing-split.md)

The finite-condition Lévy collapse $\operatorname{Lv}(\kappa)$ consists of finite [partial functions](partial-function.md) $p$ with $\operatorname{dom}p\subseteq\kappa\times\omega$ and $p(\alpha,n)<\alpha$, ordered by reverse inclusion. The generic union gives a surjection $\omega\to\alpha$ for every infinite $\alpha<\kappa$. If $\kappa$ is regular and uncountable, the [Delta-system lemma at a regular uncountable cardinal](delta-system-lemma-at-a-regular-uncountable-cardinal.md) proves the $\kappa$-chain condition, so the extension makes $\kappa=\aleph_1$.
