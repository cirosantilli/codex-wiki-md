# Cohomology ring of quaternionic projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Quaternionic projective space](quaternionic-projective-space.md)

The cell structure gives one integral cohomology generator in each degree divisible by four through $4n$. The [Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) for the [quaternionic tautological line bundle](quaternionic-tautological-line-bundle.md) makes multiplication by its [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md) an isomorphism between successive such degrees. Choose $u$ to be the negative of that Euler class; its powers generate the displayed [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md).
