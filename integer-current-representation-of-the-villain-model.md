# Integer-current representation of the Villain model

↑ **Parent:** [Villain model](villain-model.md)

Integrating every angle in the bond [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) of the [Villain model](villain-model.md) imposes the Kronecker constraint $\sum_\mu[n_{x,\mu}-n_{x-\hat\mu,\mu}]=0$. Thus its dual variables are divergence-free integer currents. On a planar lattice such currents are rotated gradients of integer heights on faces. Noncontractible current sectors must additionally be retained on a torus.
