# Neural phase locking

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal coding](temporal-coding.md)

A tendency for neural spikes to occur at preferred phases of a periodic stimulus. It supplies timing information without requiring one spike on every cycle. Its precision and usable frequency range depend on the cell population and conditions.
