# Graphical corner frequency of a Lorentzian spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Overdamped harmonic-trap power spectrum](overdamped-harmonic-trap-power-spectrum.md)

For [power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md) $P(f)=C/(f^2+f_c^2)$, the low-frequency plateau is $P_0=C/f_c^2$ and the high-frequency asymptote is $C/f^2$. Their intersection on a [log-log plot](log-log-plot.md) gives the displayed corner [frequency](frequency.md). Equivalently $P(f_c)=P_0/2$. Use the range unaffected by additional low-frequency [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) or high-frequency filtering; a measurement chain can otherwise shift an apparent corner.
