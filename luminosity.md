# Luminosity

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminosity)

Luminosity is the total radiative energy emitted by an astronomical source per unit time. It differs from the received [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md), which also depends on distance and geometry.
