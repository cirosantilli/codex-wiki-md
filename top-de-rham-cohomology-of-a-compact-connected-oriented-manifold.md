# Top de Rham cohomology of a compact connected oriented manifold

↑ **Parent:** [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md)

For a compact connected oriented boundaryless $n$-dimensional [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), choose a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md). Harmonic functions are constant because $\langle f,\Delta f\rangle=\|df\|^2$, and [Hodge star commutes with the Hodge Laplacian](hodge-star-commutes-with-the-hodge-laplacian.md). Thus the [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) identifies the constant functions with the [harmonic differential forms](harmonic-differential-form.md) of top degree, exactly $\mathbb R\omega_g$. The [Hodge decomposition theorem](hodge-decomposition-theorem.md) identifies these with the top [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md). The [metric volume form](metric-volume-form.md) has nonzero class by [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md). The harmonic-representative theorem is stated in [Denis Auroux's Hodge theory lecture](https://www.ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-966-geometry-of-manifolds-spring-2007/d6848bb391c032ef27993e984fef4558_lect15.pdf).
