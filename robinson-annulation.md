# Robinson annulation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinson_annulation)

A [Robinson annulation](robinson-annulation.md) combines a [Michael addition](michael-addition.md) with an intramolecular [aldol reaction](aldol-reaction.md) and dehydration. Tracking the atoms shows which carbonyl survives and which new bond closes the six-membered ring.
