# Real torus

↑ **Parent:** [Compact Lie group](compact-lie-group.md)

A real torus is the compact [Lie group](lie-group.md) obtained by identifying real vectors differing by integer vectors, with addition induced from Euclidean addition. It is a finite product of circle groups and has normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md). Dimension two is the familiar surface [torus](torus.md), but toral dynamics and Fourier characters naturally use arbitrary finite dimension.
