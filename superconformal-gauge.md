# Superconformal gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md)

The local gauge choice $h_{\mu\nu}=e^{2\omega}\eta_{\mu\nu}$ and $\chi_\mu=0$ for an [RNS string](spinning-string.md). Diffeomorphisms and Weyl symmetry fix the metric, while local [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) and [super-Weyl symmetry](super-weyl-symmetry.md) fix the gravitino. Stress-tensor and supercurrent constraints survive the gauge choice and eliminate longitudinal degrees of freedom. Higher-genus surfaces retain metric and spin-structure moduli.
