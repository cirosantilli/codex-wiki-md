# Acyclic quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Quiver](quiver.md)

An [acyclic quiver](acyclic-quiver.md) has no oriented cycle. For a finite [quiver](quiver.md), this is equivalent to finite-dimensionality of its [path algebra](path-algebra.md): paths have bounded length without cycles, while powers of a cycle are infinitely many distinct basis elements.
