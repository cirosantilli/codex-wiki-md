# Sphere spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Suspension spectrum](suspension-spectrum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_spectrum)

The [sphere spectrum](sphere-spectrum.md) is $\mathbb S=\Sigma^\infty S^0$. It is the unit of the [smash product of spectra](smash-product-of-spectra.md). Its integer [suspensions of spectra](suspension-of-spectra.md) represent [stable homotopy groups](stable-homotopy-group.md).
