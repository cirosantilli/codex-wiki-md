# Direct sum of sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md)

The direct sum of sheaves of abelian groups is the sheafification of the presheaf $U\mapsto\bigoplus_i\mathcal F_i(U)$. It need not equal that presheaf: sections are locally represented using finitely many summands, while globally infinitely many can occur. For point-supported [skyscraper sheaves](skyscraper-sheaf.md) at closed points, allowable point supports are a [locally finite family of subsets](locally-finite-family-of-subsets.md).
