# Photoelectron

↑ **Parent:** [Photoionization](photoionization.md)

An [electron](electron.md) ejected following [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md). For a free residual ion with negligible recoil, the emitted electron's [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) equals incident [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) minus the ionization [binding energy](binding-energy.md).
