# Canonical quantization of a complex scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md)

A free [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) has independent particle and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) mode operators. With $d\Pi_p=d^3\mathbf p/[(2\pi)^3 2E_p]$, the expansion $\phi=\int d\Pi_p(ae^{-ipx}+b^\dagger e^{ipx})$ and its adjoint reproduce equal-time [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) when $[a(\mathbf p),a^\dagger(\mathbf q)]=[b(\mathbf p),b^\dagger(\mathbf q)]=(2\pi)^3 2E_p\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$ and cross commutators vanish. The [canonical momenta](canonical-momentum.md) are $\pi=\dot\phi^\dagger$, $\pi^\dagger=\dot\phi$. An overall phase on $b$ can reverse the sign of the [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) term without changing the theory.
