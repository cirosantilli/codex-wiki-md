# Uniform spin field and magnetization

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-vector Hubbard–Stratonovich decoupling](spin-vector-hubbard-stratonovich-decoupling.md)

For the auxiliary-field convention with cost $M^2/(2U)$ and coupling $-M\bar\psi\sigma_z\psi$, the homogeneous [saddle point](saddle-point.md) equation equates M to U times spin polarization per site. M has energy units. It is not itself a magnetic moment, and its coefficient changes if the contact interaction or density-of-states spin convention is changed. The exact three-component decoupling must be distinguished from selecting one constant spin direction as a [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md).
