# IGF2BP protein

↑ **Parent:** [RNA-binding protein](rna-binding-protein.md)

An insulin-like growth factor 2 messenger RNA-binding [protein](protein-split.md) family member. Stabilization and enhanced [biological translation](translation-biology.md) of modified targets have been reported; this does not identify every modification-dependent stabilizer as an [IGF2BP protein](igf2bp-protein.md).[Abstract](recognition-of-rna-n6-methyladenosine-by-igf2bp-proteins-enhances-mrna-stability-and-translation-by-huilin-huang-and-others.md)
