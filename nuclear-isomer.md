# Nuclear isomer

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear excited state](nuclear-excited-state.md)

A [nuclear isomer](nuclear-isomer.md) is a relatively long-lived metastable nuclear state. Gamma de-excitation can be hindered by a large angular-momentum change, high electromagnetic multipolarity or small overlap with lower states. Its lifetime and decay channels depend on the actual level structure; metastability is not an absolute prohibition on decay.
