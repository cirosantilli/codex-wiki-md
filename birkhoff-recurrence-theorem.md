# Birkhoff recurrence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Recurrent point](recurrent-point.md)

Every continuous self-map of a nonempty compact [metric space](metric-space.md) has a [recurrent point](recurrent-point.md). Choose a [minimal subsystem](minimal-subsystem.md). The forward orbit of every point in it is dense, and the same is true of every tail of that orbit; consequently each neighborhood is revisited at arbitrarily large times. A nonempty subsystem is all that is guaranteed: the identity map on a singleton has no larger subsystem.
