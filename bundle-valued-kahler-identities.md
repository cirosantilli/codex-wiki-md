<h1 id="bundle-valued-kahler-identities">Bundle-valued Kähler identities</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler identities](kahler-identities.md)

On a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) the [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) components satisfy $[\Lambda,\bar\partial_E]=-i(D'_E)^*$ and $[\Lambda,D'_E]=i\bar\partial_E^*$. Holomorphic normal coordinates and a [Chern connection in a normal holomorphic frame](chern-connection-in-a-normal-holomorphic-frame.md) reduce them to the scalar identities pointwise. Closedness of the Kähler form also gives $[\Lambda,\bar\partial_E^*]=0$.
