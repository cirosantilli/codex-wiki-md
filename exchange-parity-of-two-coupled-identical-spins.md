# Exchange parity of two coupled identical spins

↑ **Parent:** [Addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md)

Exchanging the factors in the [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition](clebsch-gordan-decomposition.md) of two equal [spins](spin.md) gives the phase $(-1)^{2s-j}$. This follows from the exchange symmetry of the [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md). The total exchange parity includes the spatial state and any other internal factors: the spin parity alone does not impose the full [symmetrization postulate](symmetrization-postulate.md). For two half-integer [spins](spin.md), even $j$ is spin-antisymmetric and odd $j$ spin-symmetric.
