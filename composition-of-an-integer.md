# Composition of an integer

↑ **Parent:** [Stars and bars (combinatorics)](stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md)

A [composition of an integer](composition-of-an-integer.md) $N>0$ is an ordered [tuple](finite-tuple.md) of [positive integers](positive-integer.md) with [sum](sum.md) $N$. Its length $k$ counts the parts. For example, $(3,2)$ and $(2,3)$ are different [compositions of an integer](composition-of-an-integer.md), though they give the same [integer partition](integer-partition.md). [Stars and bars](stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md) counts [compositions of an integer](composition-of-an-integer.md) with fixed length. [Gotti, Lecture 6, definition of a composition](https://math.mit.edu/~fgotti/docs/Courses/C.%20Combinatorial%20Analysis/6.%20Compositions/Compositions.pdf)
