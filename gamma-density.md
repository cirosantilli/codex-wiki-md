# Gamma density

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md)

The [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) of a [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) with shape $k>0$ and rate $\theta>0$. Integration against $y^{-j}$ gives $\mathbb E[Y^{-j}]=\theta^j\Gamma(k-j)/\Gamma(k)$ when $j<k$; the integral diverges for $j\geq k$. Rate and scale are reciprocal conventions and must be distinguished.
