# Two-layer internal deformation radius

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation radius](deformation-radius.md)

The [two-layer quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](two-layer-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) coupling defines $R_d^{-2}=F_1+F_2$. With $F_i=f_0^2/(g\prime H_i)$ this gives the stated radius. The [baroclinic energy ratio and deformation scale](baroclinic-energy-ratio-and-deformation-scale.md) depends on $(l/R_d)^2$, so the thinner layer matters when the depths are strongly unequal.
