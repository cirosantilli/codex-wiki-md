# Dissipated energy per oscillatory shear cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Loss modulus](loss-modulus.md)

For [strain](strain.md) $\gamma=\gamma_0\cos\omega t$ and [stress](stress.md) $\sigma=\gamma_0(G'\cos\omega t-G''\sin\omega t)$, integrating supplied [power](power.md) $\sigma\dot\gamma$ over one period gives the displayed dissipated [energy](energy-split.md) per unit [volume](volume.md). The storage term integrates to zero; passivity requires nonnegative [loss modulus](loss-modulus.md) for positive [frequency](frequency.md) in this convention.
