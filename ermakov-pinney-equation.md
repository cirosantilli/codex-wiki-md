# Ermakov-Pinney equation

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear ordinary differential equation](nonlinear-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

This nonlinear [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) governs the amplitude of a complex oscillator solution. Independent real solutions of $y''+p(x)y=0$ can be combined into a nonvanishing complex solution; its modulus satisfies the equation with $c$ equal to the squared [Wronskian](wronskian.md). A real positive amplitude and a conserved phase flux give an exact representation, not merely a [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md).
