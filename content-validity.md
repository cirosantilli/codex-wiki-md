# Content validity

↑ **Parent:** [Construct validity](construct-validity.md)

The adequacy with which a measure's items cover the intended domain of content. Expert review, target-user input and [cognitive interviewing](cognitive-interviewing.md) can identify missing or irrelevant material. Good numerical fit cannot compensate for a questionnaire that omits essential parts of its intended construct.
