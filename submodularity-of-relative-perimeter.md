# Submodularity of relative perimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Set of finite perimeter](set-of-finite-perimeter.md)

The BV lattice inequality $|D\min(v,w)|+|D\max(v,w)|\le|Dv|+|Dw|$ applied to two [indicator functions](indicator-function.md) gives the perimeter inequality. For smooth approximations, minima and maxima partition the two [gradients](gradient.md); [sequential lower semicontinuity](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md) passes the estimate to BV. It prevents a crossing of [minimizers](global-minimizer.md) with strictly ordered set forcing.
