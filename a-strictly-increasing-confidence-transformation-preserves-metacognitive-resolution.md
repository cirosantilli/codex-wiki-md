# A strictly increasing confidence transformation preserves metacognitive resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Metacognitive sensitivity](metacognitive-sensitivity.md)

If confidence $C$ is replaced by a strictly increasing function $g(C)$, the attainable threshold partitions have the same ordering and ties. Consequently the [type 2 receiver operating characteristic](type-2-receiver-operating-characteristic.md) and its [area under the ROC curve](area-under-the-roc-curve.md) are unchanged. Equivalently, $P(C_{\rm correct}>C_{\rm error})+\tfrac12P(C_{\rm correct}=C_{\rm error})$ is invariant. Calibration need not be preserved: adding a positive constant to probabilities within their allowed range changes their absolute values while preserving ranks. A merely nondecreasing transformation can merge distinct reports and requires additional care.
