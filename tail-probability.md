# Tail probability

↑ **Parent:** [Probability](probability.md)

For a real [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$, an upper-tail probability has the form $\mathbb P(X\geq t)$ and a lower-tail probability has the form $\mathbb P(X\leq t)$. A [concentration inequality](concentration-inequality.md) bounds such probabilities.
