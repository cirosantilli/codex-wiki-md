# Prime ideals and local embedding orbits

↑ **Parent:** [Local factorization and extended absolute values](local-factorization-and-extended-absolute-values.md)

For a finite extension of [number fields](number-field.md) $L/K$ and a nonzero [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak p$ of $K$, the primes of $L$ above $\mathfrak p$ correspond to the orbits of

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(\overline K_{\mathfrak p}/K_{\mathfrak p})\quad\text{on}\quad\operatorname{Hom}_K(L,\overline K_{\mathfrak p}).
$$

An embedding determines a prime by pulling back the local [absolute value on a field](absolute-value-algebra.md). Embeddings with the same prime extend to embeddings of the same completion and are conjugate. If $L=K(\alpha)$, these orbits are the irreducible factors of its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) over $K_{\mathfrak p}$.
