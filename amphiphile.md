# Amphiphile

↑ **Parent:** [Physical chemistry](physical-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphiphile)

An [amphiphile](amphiphile.md) is a [molecule](molecule.md) containing distinct [hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md) and [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) regions. [Amphiphilic](amphiphile.md) and [amphipathic](amphiphile.md) describe this combination. Membrane-forming [phospholipids](phospholipid.md) are examples; their two regions give different parts of one [molecule](molecule.md) different affinities for [water](water.md). The word describes a structure, not a guarantee of a particular aggregate shape. See [IUPAC Gold Book: amphiphilic](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/A00303).
