# Quantum enveloping algebra action on the quantum plane

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum plane](quantum-plane.md)

For $yx=qxy$, the [quantum enveloping algebra of sl2](quantum-enveloping-algebra-of-sl2.md) acts by $K(x^ry^s)=q^{r-s}x^ry^s$, $E(x^ry^s)=[s]_qx^{r+1}y^{s-1}$ and $F(x^ry^s)=[r]_qx^{r-1}y^{s+1}$. Negative exponents here have zero coefficients and are omitted. The [quantum integer](quantum-integer.md) identity $[r][s+1]-[s][r+1]=[r-s]$ verifies the commutator. These operations preserve total degree and make the [quantum plane](quantum-plane.md) a [module algebra](module-algebra.md).
