# Qp-Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

A Qp-Banach algebra is a $\mathbb Q_p$-algebra with a complete non-Archimedean submultiplicative norm satisfying $\lVert\lambda a\rVert=|\lambda|_p\lVert a\rVert$.
