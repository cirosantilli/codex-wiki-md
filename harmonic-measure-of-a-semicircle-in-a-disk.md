# Harmonic measure of a semicircle in a disk

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md)

The bounded [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) on the open unit disk with boundary values one on its upper semicircle and zero on its lower semicircle is the displayed function. The [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $w=i(1-z)/(1+z)$ has real part $2y/|1+z|^2$ and imaginary part $(1-|z|^2)/|1+z|^2$. Pulling back the upper-half-plane step-data solution proves the formula by [conformal invariance of harmonicity](conformal-invariance-of-harmonicity.md). The discontinuity points $z=\pm1$ have no path-independent boundary limit.
