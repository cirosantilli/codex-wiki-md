# Thermal expansion coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md)

For a fluid at fixed [pressure](pressure.md), the volumetric thermal expansion coefficient obeys $\rho(T_0+\theta)\simeq\rho(T_0)(1-\alpha\theta)$. Under the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md) it determines the [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) proportional to $g\alpha\theta$, while [mass density](density.md) is otherwise held constant.
