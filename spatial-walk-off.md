# Spatial walk-off

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md)

Loss of spatial overlap between interacting finite beams because their [energy flux](energy-flux.md) directions differ. In an anisotropic crystal, the direction of the [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) need not coincide with the [wave vector](wavevector.md). For beam radius $w$ and relative walk-off angle $\rho$, an overlap length is of order $w/|\tan\rho|$. A full conversion calculation also includes beam profiles, [diffraction](diffraction.md) and [phase mismatch](phase-mismatch.md).
