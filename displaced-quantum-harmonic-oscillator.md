# Displaced quantum harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

Adding $-Fx$ to the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) potential gives $\frac12m\omega^2(x-d)^2-F^2/(2m\omega^2)$, where $d=F/(m\omega^2)$. Translation preserves the oscillator spectrum, apart from the displayed constant shift. Eigenfunctions are translated by $d$ and the energy spacing is unchanged.
