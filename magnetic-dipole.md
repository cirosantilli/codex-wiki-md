# Magnetic dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostatics](magnetostatics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_dipole)

A localized magnetic source described at leading nonvanishing dipole order by a [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). A small current loop is one classical model. The source object, its moment vector and the resulting [magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md) are distinct concepts. At large distance from a source with nonzero dipole moment, higher multipoles give smaller corrections; if the dipole moment vanishes, a higher-order term can lead instead.
