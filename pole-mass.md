# Pole mass

↑ **Parent:** [Self-energy](self-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pole_mass)

The pole mass is the value of the invariant momentum at a pole of the full propagator. It is independent of the renormalization scheme, whereas a running mass parameter generally depends on the [renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md).
