# Fibonacci Nim

↑ **Parent:** [Impartial game](impartial-game.md)

A heap game in which, after the first move, a player may remove at most twice the number removed on the preceding move. Under the usual first-move restriction of fewer than all counters, [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md) and the [Zeckendorf representation](zeckendorf-representation.md) characterize the winning moves.
