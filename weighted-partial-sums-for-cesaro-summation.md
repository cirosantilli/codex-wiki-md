<h1 id="weighted-partial-sums-for-cesaro-summation">Weighted partial sums for Cesàro summation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md)

In the average of [partial sums](partial-sum.md) $s_0,\ldots,s_N$, the term $a_k$ occurs exactly $N-k+1$ times. Reordering this finite [sum](sum.md) gives the displayed identity. Early terms have weights closer to one, and the newest term has weight $1/(N+1)$. The triangular weight expresses the cumulative averaging rule; it is not a claim that one may freely rearrange an infinite [series](series-mathematics.md).
