The [character formula for an induced representation](../../../../character-formula-for-an-induced-representation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\chi_{\operatorname{Ind}_H^GW}(g)
=\frac1{|H|}\sum_{\substack{x\in G\\x^{-1}gx\in H}}\chi_W(x^{-1}gx).}
$$

Equivalently, extend the [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $\chi_W$ by zero outside the [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ and sum over all $x\in G$. The factor $1/|H|$ accounts for passing from elements of $G$ to representatives of left [cosets](../../../../coset.md) of $H$.
