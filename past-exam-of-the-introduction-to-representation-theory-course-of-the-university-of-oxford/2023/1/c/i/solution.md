<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the left $A$-[module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) structure $c\cdot(a\otimes b)=ca\otimes b$ on the [tensor product of associative algebras](../../../../../tensor-product-of-associative-algebras.md). Expanding each $b\in B$ in the [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $b_1,\ldots,b_m$ shows that every [tensor](../../../../../tensor.md) can be written

$$
x=\sum_{j=1}^m a_j\otimes b_j
=\sum_{j=1}^m a_j\cdot(1\otimes b_j).
$$

Thus the displayed family generates the [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md).

For uniqueness, let $\beta_j:B\to k$ be the corresponding [dual basis](../../../../../dual-basis.md), with $\beta_j(b_i)=\delta_{ij}$. Applying the [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\beta_j$ to a [linear relation](../../../../../linear-relation.md) $\sum_i a_i\otimes b_i=0$ gives $a_j=0$. Hence the family is [linearly independent in a module](../../../../../linear-independence-in-a-module.md), and

$$
\boxed{A\otimes_kB\cong A^{\oplus m}\quad\text{as left }A\text{-modules}.}
$$

In particular $1\otimes b_1,\ldots,1\otimes b_m$ is a [basis of a module](../../../../../basis-of-a-module.md), with coefficients uniquely in $A$.
