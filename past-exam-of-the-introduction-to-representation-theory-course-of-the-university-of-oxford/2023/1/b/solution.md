<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $A$ is a nonzero [associative algebra](../../../../associative-algebra-split.md) of finite [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md), it has a nonzero [left ideal](../../../../left-ideal.md) $I$ of least positive [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md). Such a [minimal left ideal](../../../../minimal-left-ideal.md) is a [simple module](../../../../irreducible-module.md) under left multiplication.

For $a\in A$, right multiplication defines the [module homomorphism](../../../../module-homomorphism.md)

$$
R_a:I\longrightarrow A,\qquad x\longmapsto xa.
$$

Its [kernel](../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is a [submodule](../../../../submodule.md) of $I$, so its [image](../../../../image-of-a-function.md) $Ia$ is either zero or a [simple module](../../../../irreducible-module.md) [isomorphic](../../../../isomorphism.md) to $I$. Each $Ia$ is a [left ideal](../../../../left-ideal.md), since $c(xa)=(cx)a$.

Consider

$$
J=\sum_{a\in A}Ia.
$$

It is a [left ideal](../../../../left-ideal.md). It is also stable under right multiplication, because $(Ia)b=I(ab)$, so it is a [two-sided ideal](../../../../two-sided-ideal.md). Moreover $I=I1\subseteq J$, giving $J\ne0$. The assumption that $A$ is a [simple ring](../../../../simple-ring.md) therefore forces $J=A$.

Only finitely many summands are needed: express each member of a finite [basis](../../../../basis.md) of $A$ as a finite sum of elements of the $Ia$, and collect the finitely many indices used. After discarding zero summands, $A$ is a finite sum of [simple modules](../../../../irreducible-module.md). Part (a)(ii) makes its [left regular module](../../../../left-regular-module.md) a [semisimple module](../../../../semisimple-module.md). This is exactly the definition of a [semisimple algebra](../../../../semisimple-algebra.md), proving that a [finite-dimensional simple algebra is semisimple](../../../../finite-dimensional-simple-algebra-is-semisimple.md). The finite [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) assumption is essential to this assertion about a [simple ring](../../../../simple-ring.md).
