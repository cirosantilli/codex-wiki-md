<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $J\ne0$ be a [two-sided ideal](../../../../../two-sided-ideal.md) of $A\otimes_kB$. Choose a nonzero $x\in J$ of least possible [two-factor tensor rank](../../../../../two-factor-tensor-rank.md) $n$, and take a shortest expression

$$
x=\sum_{i=1}^n a_i\otimes b_i.
$$

Both families $(a_i)$ and $(b_i)$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md): a [linear dependence](../../../../../linear-dependence.md) in either family would combine terms and shorten the expression. In particular $a_1,b_1\ne0$.

Because $A$ is a [simple ring](../../../../../simple-ring.md), the [two-sided ideal](../../../../../two-sided-ideal.md) generated by $a_1$ is $A$. Thus there are finitely many $u_j,v_j\in A$ with $\sum_j u_ja_1v_j=1$. Form

$$
y=\sum_j(u_j\otimes1)x(v_j\otimes1)
=\sum_{i=1}^n c_i\otimes b_i\in J,\qquad
c_i=\sum_j u_ja_iv_j,\qquad c_1=1.
$$

Since the $b_i$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md), applying a [linear functional](../../../../../linear-functional.md) selecting $b_1$ shows $y\ne0$. Its [two-factor tensor rank](../../../../../two-factor-tensor-rank.md) is at most $n$ and hence, by the choice of $x$, exactly $n$. Consequently $c_1=1,c_2,\ldots,c_n$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md).

Similarly, simplicity of $B$ gives finitely many $p_j,q_j\in B$ with $\sum_jp_jb_1q_j=1$. Now

$$
z=\sum_j(1\otimes p_j)y(1\otimes q_j)
=\sum_{i=1}^n c_i\otimes d_i\in J,\qquad
d_i=\sum_jp_jb_iq_j,\qquad d_1=1.
$$

The [linear independence](../../../../../linear-independence.md) of the $c_i$ shows $z\ne0$, and minimality again makes its [two-factor tensor rank](../../../../../two-factor-tensor-rank.md) exactly $n$. We have obtained

$$
\boxed{z=1\otimes1+\sum_{i=2}^n c_i\otimes d_i\in J\setminus\{0\}.}
$$

Both coefficient families in this expression are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md). Retaining this additional property is the key to the next part. This is [coefficient normalization in an ideal of a tensor product](../../../../../coefficient-normalization-in-an-ideal-of-a-tensor-product.md).
