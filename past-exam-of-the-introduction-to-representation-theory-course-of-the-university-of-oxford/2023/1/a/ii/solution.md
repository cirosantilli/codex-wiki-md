<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Construct the sum successively, retaining a [simple module](../../../../../irreducible-module.md) only when it adds a new [direct summand](../../../../../direct-summand.md). Suppose $S=V_1+\cdots+V_{j-1}$ is already expressed as a [direct sum of modules](../../../../../direct-sum-of-modules.md) selected from these [simple modules](../../../../../irreducible-module.md). The intersection $S\cap V_j$ is a [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) of the [simple module](../../../../../irreducible-module.md) $V_j$, so it is either zero or all of $V_j$. In the latter case $S+V_j=S$. In the former case

$$
S+V_j=S\oplus V_j.
$$

Starting with $S=0$ and proceeding by [mathematical induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) gives a [direct sum of modules](../../../../../direct-sum-of-modules.md) equal to $V_1+\cdots+V_n$. Thus the sum is a [semisimple module](../../../../../semisimple-module.md). The argument proves the useful [finite sum of simple submodules](../../../../../finite-sum-of-simple-submodules.md) lemma without claiming that the originally listed [submodules](../../../../../submodule.md) are themselves independent.
