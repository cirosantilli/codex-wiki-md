<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An $A$-[module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is **completely reducible** if it is a [direct sum of modules](../../../../../direct-sum-of-modules.md) each of which is a [simple module](../../../../../irreducible-module.md):

$$
V=\bigoplus_{j\in J}S_j.
$$

Here a [simple module](../../../../../irreducible-module.md) is nonzero and has no [submodules](../../../../../submodule.md) other than zero and itself. This is precisely the definition of a [semisimple module](../../../../../semisimple-module.md). The indexing set need not be finite unless $V$ has finite [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md); the zero [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is included as the empty [direct sum of modules](../../../../../direct-sum-of-modules.md).
