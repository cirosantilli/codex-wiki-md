# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the introduction to representation theory course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part B [Introduction to representation theory course of the University of Oxford](../introduction-to-representation-theory-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) examination, held on 29 May 2023. Source: [released examination PDF, bundle pages 212–213; paper pages 1–2](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=212). The original numeric, letter and roman numbering is retained. All [associative algebras](../associative-algebra-split.md) and [modules](../module-mathematics.md) below are unital; the [characters](../character-of-a-representation.md) in Questions 2 and 3 are those of finite-dimensional [complex representations](../complex-representation.md).
