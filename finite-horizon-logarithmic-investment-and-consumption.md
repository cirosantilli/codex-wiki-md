# Finite-horizon logarithmic investment and consumption

↑ **Parent:** [Investment-consumption problem](investment-consumption-problem.md)

For terminal [logarithmic utility](logarithmic-utility.md) $a\log V_T$ and running [logarithmic utility](logarithmic-utility.md) $b\log c_t$, with $a,b,w_0>0$, constant [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md) coefficients give $\lambda=(a+bT)/w_0$ and $V_t=[a+b(T-t)]/(\lambda\xi_t)$, where $\xi$ is the [state-price density](state-price-density.md). The optimal terminal wealth and [consumption](consumption.md) are $a/(\lambda\xi_T)$ and $b/(\lambda\xi_t)$. Their discounted budget adds to $(a+bT)/\lambda=w_0$. The [concave supporting-tangent inequality](concave-supporting-tangent-inequality.md), integrated over time, proves optimality; the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives the displayed dollar stock holding. The time-dependent consumption-to-wealth ratio reflects both remaining consumption opportunities and the terminal utility weight.
