# Chiral constraint algebra of a closed string

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu–Goto phase-space constraints](nambu-goto-phase-space-constraints.md)

For a [closed string](closed-string.md), the currents $J_\pm=P\pm TX'$ have vanishing mixed [Poisson brackets](poisson-bracket.md). The densities $\mathcal H_\pm=J_\pm^2/(4T)$ therefore generate commuting constraint algebras. With opposite Fourier orientations their modes satisfy $\{L_m,L_n\}=-i(m-n)L_{m+n}$ and the identical relation for $\widetilde L_n$. This is a [direct sum of Lie algebras](direct-sum-of-lie-algebras.md) consisting of two [Witt algebras](witt-algebra.md).
