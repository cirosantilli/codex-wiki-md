# Unstable manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

At a hyperbolic equilibrium, the unstable manifold is formed by nearby trajectories approaching that equilibrium as time tends to minus infinity. It is tangent to the span of [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) whose [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) have positive real part. Its dimension counts departing amplitudes; translation along an autonomous trajectory can remove one parameter when comparing orbit shapes. The [stable manifold](stable-manifold.md) instead approaches the equilibrium forward in time.
