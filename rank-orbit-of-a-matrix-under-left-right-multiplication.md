# Rank orbit of a matrix under left-right multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix rank](matrix-rank.md)

Two rectangular matrices are related by invertible left and right multiplication exactly when their ranks agree. The rank-$r$ orbit in $\operatorname{Mat}_{b\times a}(k)$ has dimension $r(a+b-r)$: choose its image in a [Grassmannian](grassmannian.md), then a surjective map to that image. These are the representation orbits of the [one-arrow quiver](kronecker-quiver-with-one-arrow.md).
