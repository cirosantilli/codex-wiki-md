# Magnetic second-moment tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md)

For a resolved [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), the unaveraged tensor $B_iB_j$ describes [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md); the isotropic [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) is treated separately in the [magnetohydrodynamic total pressure](magnetohydrodynamic-total-pressure.md). Its spatial or ensemble average need not have [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) one. [Statistical isotropy](statistical-isotropy.md) in three dimensions gives $\langle B_iB_j\rangle=\langle B^2\rangle\delta_{ij}/3$, by taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md). In [Alfvén velocity](alfven-velocity.md) units this tensor has dimensions of squared [velocity](velocity.md).
