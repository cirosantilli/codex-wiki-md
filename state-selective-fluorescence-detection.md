# State-selective fluorescence detection

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum measurement](quantum-measurement-split.md)

A readout in which one logical [quantum state](quantum-state.md) is coupled to an optical cycling transition and scatters many [photons](photon.md), while another is dark. An ideal bright/dark record implements a [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) in the logical basis. Neglecting that record removes their relative [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md). Missed photons, off-resonant excitation and pumping out of the logical subspace qualify the ideal model.
