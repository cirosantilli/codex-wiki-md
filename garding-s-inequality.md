<h1 id="garding-s-inequality">Gårding's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic boundary value problem](elliptic-boundary-value-problem-split.md)

For a uniformly elliptic second-order [Dirichlet](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) form with [bounded](boundedness.md) coefficients and lower-order terms, there are $a>0$ and $b\ge0$ giving the displayed lower bound. [Cauchy-Schwarz](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and Young's inequality absorb the drift term into part of the principal [gradient](gradient.md) energy plus an $L^2$ correction. Adding a sufficiently large nonnegative multiple of the $L^2$ [inner product](inner-product-split.md) therefore makes the form coercive, permitting Lax-Milgram. The shift restores coercivity even when the unshifted form is not positive.
