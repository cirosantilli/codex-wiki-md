# Charged-particle conserved quantity from a Killing symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic conserved quantity from a Killing vector](geodesic-conserved-quantity-from-a-killing-vector.md)

Suppose $V$ is a [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md), $F$ is an [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) satisfying $dF=0$ and $\mathcal L_VF=0$, and a particle of nonzero mass $m$ obeys the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) equation $\nabla_uu=(q/m)F^a{}_bu^b$. [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md) gives $d(\iota_VF)=0$, so the [local potential of a closed differential one-form](local-potential-of-a-closed-differential-one-form.md) proof gives $d\Phi=\iota_VF$ locally. The [Killing equation](killing-equation.md) eliminates $u^au^b\nabla_aV_b$, and the force term gives $d(V\cdot u)/d\tau=(q/m)d\Phi/d\tau$. Therefore $mV\cdot u-q\Phi$ is locally conserved. The sign assumes contraction in the first argument of $F$. A global potential additionally requires the closed one-form to have zero periods; invariance alone is insufficient without the homogeneous [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations.md).
