# Box norm

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)

For nonempty [finite sets](finite-set.md) $X,Y$ and a [function](function-split.md) $f:X\times Y\to\mathbb C$ on their [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md), using uniform [expectations](expected-value.md), define

$$
\|f\|_{\square}^4=\mathbb E_{x,x',y,y'}f(x,y)\overline{f(x',y)}\overline{f(x,y')}f(x',y').
$$

This equals $\mathbb E_{y,y'}|\mathbb E_xf(x,y)\overline{f(x,y')}|^2$. Hence it is nonnegative, and vanishing forces $f=0$ by taking $y=y'$. Absolute homogeneity and the [box Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](box-cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) establish that it is a [norm](norm.md). For a [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) the complex conjugates can be omitted. The [bilinear correlation bound for the box norm](bilinear-correlation-bound-for-the-box-norm.md) also holds for complex functions.
