# Fan subdivision

↑ **Parent:** [Fan in toric geometry](fan-in-toric-geometry.md)

A subdivision of a fan replaces its cones by smaller cones with the same support and compatible intersections. It induces a proper birational [toric morphism](toric-morphism.md).
