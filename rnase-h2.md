# RNase H2

↑ **Parent:** [Ribonucleotide excision repair](ribonucleotide-excision-repair.md)

A ribonuclease H system capable of recognizing a single [ribonucleotide](ribonucleotide.md) embedded in [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and initiating its removal. This substrate is distinct from a longer RNA–DNA hybrid.
