# Triangle inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle_inequality)

A metric satisfies $d(x,z)\leq d(x,y)+d(y,z)$. A norm satisfies the corresponding inequality $\|u+v\|\leq\|u\|+\|v\|$.
