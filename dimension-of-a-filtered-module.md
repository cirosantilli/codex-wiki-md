# Dimension of a filtered module

↑ **Parent:** [Good filtration of a module](good-filtration-of-a-module.md)

For a [good filtration of a module](good-filtration-of-a-module.md) over a fixed finite-dimensional [filtration of a ring](filtration-of-a-ring.md) whose associated graded algebra is commutative and finitely generated, the cumulative Hilbert function is eventually a [quasipolynomial](quasipolynomial.md) by the [Hilbert-Serre theorem](hilbert-serre-theorem.md). Its leading asymptotic degree is the dimension of the nonzero filtered [module](module-mathematics.md). Monotonicity forces the leading coefficients in its residue classes to agree. If the associated graded algebra is a [standard graded algebra](standard-graded-algebra.md), the Hilbert function is eventually polynomial. Two good module filtrations bound one another after fixed shifts, so they have the same growth degree. Set $d(0)=-\infty$. For the [Bernstein filtration](bernstein-filtration.md) this is the [Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module](bernstein-growth-dimension-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md).
