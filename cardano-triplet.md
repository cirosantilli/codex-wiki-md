# Cardano triplet

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine equation](diophantine-equation.md)

A triple of [positive integers](positive-integer.md) $(a,b,c)$ satisfying $\sqrt[3]{a+b\sqrt c}+\sqrt[3]{a-b\sqrt c}=1$, with real cube roots. Cubing the equality yields the equivalent [integer](integer-split.md) identity $27b^2c=(a+1)^2(8a-1)$. In particular $a=3k-1$ for a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $k$, and the condition becomes $b^2c=k^2(8k-3)$.
