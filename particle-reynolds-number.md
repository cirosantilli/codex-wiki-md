# Particle Reynolds number

↑ **Parent:** [Settling velocity](settling-velocity.md)

A [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) based on particle diameter and relative speed in the carrier fluid. Small values justify [Stokes drag law](stokes-s-law.md); a high-Reynolds-number regime with nearly constant [drag coefficient](drag-coefficient.md) instead gives [quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md). The drag law must be evaluated in the appropriate regime for every species.
