# Genetic variant

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_variant)

A genetic variant is a heritable difference in a DNA sequence relative to a chosen reference. Genetic variants can be used as [instrumental variables](instrumental-variable.md) in [Mendelian randomization](mendelian-randomization.md) when the required causal assumptions are credible.
