# Residual electron freeze-out

↑ **Parent:** [Recombination (cosmology)](recombination-cosmology.md)

When the effective recombination rate per [Electron](electron.md) becomes smaller than the expansion rate, the small remaining ionized fraction can no longer track [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md). A nonzero [Electron](electron.md) fraction remains, exceeding the exponentially small [Saha equation](saha-ionization-equation.md) prediction. The effective rate includes atomic radiative bottlenecks; chemical freeze-out is distinct from [photon decoupling](photon-decoupling.md) by scattering.
