# Generating family for a uniform set family

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform set family](uniform-set-family.md)

A generating family consists of sets of size at most $r$ whose $r$-element supersets are exactly the prescribed [uniform set family](uniform-set-family.md). Small-support generators express dependence on a bounded prefix rather than on the full ground set. Taking the entire family itself as generators always works, but extremal intersection problems admit much smaller supporting intervals.
