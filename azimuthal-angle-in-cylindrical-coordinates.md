# Azimuthal angle in cylindrical coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical coordinate system](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md)

The azimuthal [angle](angle.md) $\phi$ in [cylindrical coordinates](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) is measured in the horizontal plane from a chosen reference axis. In the usual mathematical convention $x=s\cos\phi$, $y=s\sin\phi$, so positive $\phi$ runs counterclockwise from positive $x$ when viewed from positive $z$. This same angular coordinate occurs in [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md). It is defined modulo $2\pi$ away from the polar axis and is nonunique on the axis.
