# Spectral measure of a stationary time series

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md)

The spectral measure of a [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) is the finite nonnegative measure whose Fourier coefficients are its [autocovariances](autocovariance.md). Its total mass is the process variance. A [time-series spectral density](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) exists exactly when this measure is [absolutely continuous with respect to](absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md).
