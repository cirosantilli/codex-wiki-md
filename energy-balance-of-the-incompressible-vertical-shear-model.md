# Energy balance of the incompressible vertical-shear model

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical shear instability](vertical-shear-instability.md)

For constant density $\rho_0$ and body force $\Omega^2(3x-2qz)\mathbf e_x$, dotting the [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) momentum equation with $\rho_0\mathbf u$ gives the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) flux $(K+P)\mathbf u$ and source $\rho_0\Omega^2(3x-2qz)u_x$, where $K=\rho_0|\mathbf u|^2/2$. [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) does no work. Absorbing $\Phi_t=-3\Omega^2x^2/2$ gives the displayed balance. For nonzero $q$, the remaining force has [curl](curl.md) $-2\Omega^2q\mathbf e_y$, so it cannot be incorporated into a scalar mechanical potential. The model can exchange energy with its maintained vertical-shear background; at $q=0$ ordinary [Jacobi energy in a shearing sheet](jacobi-energy-in-a-shearing-sheet.md) conservation is recovered under zero boundary flux.
