# Hydrogen ground-state nuclear-volume probability

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

For the point-Coulomb [ground state](ground-state.md), radial [probability density](probability-density.md) is $4r^2e^{-2r/a_0}/a_0^3$. Integrating to $r_p$ gives $1-e^{-2u}(1+2u+2u^2)$, with $u=r_p/a_0$, whose small-$u$ expansion is the displayed result. A finite [proton](proton.md) charge distribution changes the interior potential, and a [proton](proton.md) radius is not literally a sharp material boundary; the expression is an estimate of a small overlap region.
