# Ultraluminous X-ray source

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray binary](x-ray-binary.md)

An [ultraluminous X-ray source](ultraluminous-x-ray-source.md) is an apparently off-nuclear compact X-ray source with inferred isotropic luminosity above roughly $10^{32}$ W. Luminosity alone does not establish an [intermediate-mass black hole](intermediate-mass-black-hole.md): supercritical accretion, beaming and magnetized neutron-star accretion can also produce large apparent luminosities. Spatial association, spectra, timing and dynamical constraints help distinguish models.
