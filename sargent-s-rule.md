<h1 id="sargent-s-rule">Sargent's rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Weak interaction](weak-interaction.md)

In a low-energy allowed weak three-body decay, negligible final-state masses and a nearly constant effective matrix element give a fifth-power energy-release dependence. For muon decay in [natural units](natural-units.md), $\Gamma\simeq G_F^2m_\mu^5/(192\pi^3)$. This four-fermion approximation requires momentum transfers small compared with the [W boson](w-boson.md) mass. Extrapolation to a heavy particle able to emit a real W changes the decay kinematics and the mass scaling, so it is not a reliable heavy-particle lifetime prediction.
