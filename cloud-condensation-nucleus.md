# Cloud condensation nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Aerosol](aerosol.md)

An [aerosol](aerosol.md) particle able to activate into a growing [cloud droplet](cloud-droplet.md) at the supersaturation attained by a [cloud](cloud.md) parcel. Activity depends on particle dry size, hygroscopicity and interfacial properties. The number active at a specified supersaturation differs from total [aerosol](aerosol.md) number.
