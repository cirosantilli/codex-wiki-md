# Dimensionless cosmological power spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial perturbation](primordial-perturbation.md)

The dimensionless spectrum is the contribution to a statistically isotropic field variance per logarithmic [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) interval: $\langle X^2\rangle=\int d\log k\,\Delta_X(k)$, with any necessary smoothing or regulators. Some conventions denote this same quantity $\Delta_X^2$. The definition, not the superscript convention, fixes its meaning.
