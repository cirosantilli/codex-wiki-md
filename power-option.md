# Power option

↑ **Parent:** [European contingent claim](european-contingent-claim.md)

A power option is a [European contingent claim](european-contingent-claim.md) whose payoff is a fixed power of the terminal underlying price. In the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md), its value is $S_t^p\exp((p-1)r(T-t)+\frac12p(p-1)\sigma^2(T-t))$ when the required [moments](moment.md) are finite.
