# Depletion approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Depletion region](depletion-region.md)

The [depletion approximation](depletion-approximation.md) replaces the junction's transition region by a region without mobile carriers, leaving only fixed ionized dopants. For a uniformly doped abrupt [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md), $\rho=-eN_A$ on the p side, $\rho=+eN_D$ on the n side and $\rho\simeq0$ outside. [Charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) gives $N_Ax_p=N_Dx_n$. Integrating [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) gives the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and the electrostatic [band bending](band-bending.md).
