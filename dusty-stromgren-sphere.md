<h1 id="dusty-stromgren-sphere">Dusty Strömgren sphere</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Strömgren sphere](stromgren-sphere.md)

A dusty Strömgren sphere includes ionizing photons absorbed by dust before they can ionize hydrogen. Dust therefore decreases the ionized radius below the dust-free [Strömgren radius](stromgren-radius.md) at fixed source luminosity and gas density.
