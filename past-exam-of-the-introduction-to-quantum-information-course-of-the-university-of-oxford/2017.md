## 3

↑ **Parent:** [2017](2017.md)

<h3 id="3/a">a</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

<h4 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h4>

↑ **Parent:** [A](#3/a)

The [Pauli matrices](../algebra.md#pauli-matrices) are [Hermitian matrices](../hilbert-space.md#hermitian-operator) with zero [trace](../linear-algebra.md#matrix-trace), so a real [Bloch vector](../density-matrix.md#bloch-vector) makes $\varrho$ [Hermitian](../hilbert-space.md#hermitian-operator) and gives $\operatorname{tr}\varrho=1$. Their [Pauli multiplication identity](../algebra.md#pauli-multiplication-identity) implies $(\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)^2=|\mathbf s|^2I$. Consequently the two [eigenvalues](../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) of $\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ are $\pm|\mathbf s|$, including the zero-vector case, and

$$
\boxed{\lambda_\pm(\varrho)=\frac{1\pm|\mathbf s|}{2}.}
$$

A [density matrix](../density-matrix.md) must also be [positive semidefinite](../quadratic-form.md#positive-semidefinite-matrix). The displayed [eigenvalues](../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) are nonnegative exactly when $\boxed{|\mathbf s|\leq1}$. Thus real coordinates alone are insufficient: the allowed vectors fill the [Bloch ball](../density-matrix.md#bloch-ball). Its boundary consists of [pure states](../quantum-theory.md#pure-state), with [eigenvalues](../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) one and zero; its center is the [maximally mixed state](../density-matrix.md#maximally-mixed-state) $I/2$.

<h3 id="3/b">b</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

<h4 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h4>

↑ **Parent:** [B](#3/b)

For any column vector $v$ and [positive semidefinite](../quadratic-form.md#positive-semidefinite-matrix) input $\varrho$, the [Kraus representation](../quantum-information-theory.md#kraus-representation) gives

$$
v^\dagger\mathcal E(\varrho)v=\sum_k(A_k^\dagger v)^\dagger\varrho(A_k^\dagger v)\geq0.
$$

Thus the output is [positive semidefinite](../quadratic-form.md#positive-semidefinite-matrix). The [cyclic property of the trace](../linear-algebra.md#cyclic-property-of-the-trace) and completeness of the [Kraus operators](../quantum-information-theory.md#kraus-operator) give

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}\mathcal E(\varrho)=\operatorname{tr}\!\left(\varrho\sum_kA_k^\dagger A_k\right)=\operatorname{tr}\varrho.}
$$

More strongly, tensoring with an arbitrary [ancilla](../quantum-information-theory.md#quantum-ancilla) replaces $A_k$ by $A_k\otimes I$, so the same argument proves [complete positivity](../quantum-information-theory.md#completely-positive-map) rather than only positivity on single-system [density matrices](../density-matrix.md).

If $\mathcal F$ has [Kraus operators](../quantum-information-theory.md#kraus-operator) $B_j$, the [composition of quantum channels](../quantum-information-theory.md#composition-of-quantum-channels) has

$$
(\mathcal F\circ\mathcal E)(\varrho)=\sum_{j,k}B_jA_k\varrho A_k^\dagger B_j^\dagger,
\qquad
\sum_{j,k}(B_jA_k)^\dagger B_jA_k
=\sum_kA_k^\dagger\!\left(\sum_jB_j^\dagger B_j\right)A_k=I.
$$

This is again a trace-preserving [Kraus representation](../quantum-information-theory.md#kraus-representation). The composition of [completely positive maps](../quantum-information-theory.md#completely-positive-map) is [completely positive](../quantum-information-theory.md#completely-positive-map) even without the trace-preserving assumption; in the general case, the product [Kraus operators](../quantum-information-theory.md#kraus-operator) still establish [complete positivity](../quantum-information-theory.md#completely-positive-map).

<h3 id="3/c">c</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

<h4 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h4>

↑ **Parent:** [C](#3/c)

Conjugation by one [Pauli matrix](../algebra.md#pauli-matrices) fixes that same matrix and reverses the other two. For example, $X X X=X$, $Y X Y=-X$, and $Z X Z=-X$. Applying this to each component of the [Bloch vector](../density-matrix.md#bloch-vector) gives

$$
\sum_{j=x,y,z}\sigma_j\varrho\sigma_j
=\frac12(3I-\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma).
$$

Hence the [quantum depolarizing channel](../quantum-information-theory.md#quantum-depolarizing-channel) is

$$
\mathcal E_p(\varrho)
=\frac12\left[I+\left(1-\frac{4p}{3}\right)\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\right],
\qquad
\boxed{\mathbf s'=\frac{3-4p}{3}\mathbf s.}
$$

The [Kraus operators](../quantum-information-theory.md#kraus-operator) $\sqrt{1-p}\,I$ and $\sqrt{p/3}\,\sigma_j$ establish [complete positivity](../quantum-information-theory.md#completely-positive-map) for $0\leq p\leq1$. At $p=3/4$ every state becomes [maximally mixed](../density-matrix.md#maximally-mixed-state). For $p>3/4$ the factor is negative: it reverses the vector as well as reducing its length. Its most negative value is $-1/3$, consistent with the complete-positivity range of the [isotropic qubit map](../quantum-information-theory.md#isotropic-qubit-map).

<h3 id="3/d">d</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

<h4 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h4>

↑ **Parent:** [D](#3/d)

The [universal NOT gate](../quantum-information-theory.md#universal-not-gate) is the [isotropic qubit map](../quantum-information-theory.md#isotropic-qubit-map) with factor $-1$. It sends

$$
\varrho=\frac12(I+\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)
\quad\longmapsto\quad
\mathcal N(\varrho)=\frac12(I-\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)=I-\varrho.
$$

For $|\mathbf s|\leq1$, the output has the same two nonnegative [eigenvalues](../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) as the input, exchanged, and still has [trace](../linear-algebra.md#matrix-trace) one. Thus **it maps every single-qubit density matrix to a density matrix**. Its linear extension to all operators is $\mathcal N(A)=\operatorname{tr}(A)I-A$, not $I-A$ for arbitrary unnormalized $A$. Positivity on one [qubit](../quantum-system.md#qubit) does not establish [complete positivity](../quantum-information-theory.md#completely-positive-map) when an [ancilla](../quantum-information-theory.md#quantum-ancilla) is present.

<h3 id="3/e">e</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

<h4 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h4>

↑ **Parent:** [E](#3/e)

The input is the [Bell state](../bell-state.md) $\rho=|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|$, where $|\Phi^+\rangle=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$. Acting on the first [qubit](../quantum-system.md#qubit) reverses the signs of all three terms containing its [Pauli matrices](../algebra.md#pauli-matrices), so in [computational basis](../quantum-theory.md#computational-basis) order $00,01,10,11$ the output is

$$
\boxed{(\mathcal N\otimes\operatorname{id})(\rho)
=\frac14(I\otimes I-X\otimes X+Y\otimes Y-Z\otimes Z)
=\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&0&-1/2\\
0&1/2&0&0\\
0&0&1/2&0\\
-1/2&0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its [eigenvalues](../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) are $\boxed{-1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2}$: in particular, its [expectation value](../quantum-system.md#expectation-value) in $|\Phi^+\rangle$ is $-1/2$. It therefore fails to be [positive semidefinite](../quadratic-form.md#positive-semidefinite-matrix), although its [trace](../linear-algebra.md#matrix-trace) remains one. This single [entangled state](../bell-state.md#entangled-state) proves that the [universal NOT gate](../quantum-information-theory.md#universal-not-gate) is **positive but not completely positive**, so it cannot be a deterministic physical [quantum channel](../quantum-information-theory.md#quantum-channel) on an arbitrary input.
