<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Pauli matrices](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) are [Hermitian matrices](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) with zero [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md), so a real [Bloch vector](../../../../bloch-vector.md) makes $\varrho$ [Hermitian](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and gives $\operatorname{tr}\varrho=1$. Their [Pauli multiplication identity](../../../../pauli-multiplication-identity.md) implies $(\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)^2=|\mathbf s|^2I$. Consequently the two [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ are $\pm|\mathbf s|$, including the zero-vector case, and

$$
\boxed{\lambda_\pm(\varrho)=\frac{1\pm|\mathbf s|}{2}.}
$$

A [density matrix](../../../../density-matrix-split.md) must also be [positive semidefinite](../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). The displayed [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are nonnegative exactly when $\boxed{|\mathbf s|\leq1}$. Thus real coordinates alone are insufficient: the allowed vectors fill the [Bloch ball](../../../../bloch-ball.md). Its boundary consists of [pure states](../../../../pure-state.md), with [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) one and zero; its center is the [maximally mixed state](../../../../maximally-mixed-state.md) $I/2$.
