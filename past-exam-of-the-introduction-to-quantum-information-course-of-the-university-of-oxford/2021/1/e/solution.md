<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

While the sequence is remembered, the conditional [quantum state](../../../../quantum-state.md) is the known pure endpoint $C|0\rangle$, so its [Bloch vector](../../../../bloch-vector.md) is one of the six Pauli vertices. Partial forgetting leaves a probability distribution $p_j$ over possible endpoints, giving

$$
\rho=\sum_{j=1}^6p_j|v_j\rangle\langle v_j|,\qquad
\mathbf s=\sum_jp_j\mathbf v_j.
$$

Hence the vector lies in the [stabilizer octahedron](../../../../stabilizer-octahedron.md). The mixing describes lost classical information, not a different unitary evolution of the physical qubit.

Under complete unbiased uncertainty over the six endpoints, $p_j=1/6$, the opposite vectors cancel and

$$
\boxed{\mathbf s=0,\qquad\rho=I/2.}
$$

This is the [maximally mixed state](../../../../maximally-mixed-state.md) at the center. Mere loss of the particular sequence does not mathematically force uniform probabilities: a known biased distribution of endpoint states would leave its corresponding noncentral mixture. The central conclusion uses the unbiased randomization implicit in complete forgetting in this setting.
