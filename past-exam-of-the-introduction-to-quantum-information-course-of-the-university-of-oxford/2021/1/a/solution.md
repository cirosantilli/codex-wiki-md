<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Pauli multiplication identity](../../../../pauli-multiplication-identity.md) gives $(\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)^2=|\mathbf s|^2I$. Its [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are therefore $\pm|\mathbf s|$, and those of the [density matrix](../../../../density-matrix-split.md) are

$$
\lambda_\pm=\frac{1\pm|\mathbf s|}{2}.
$$

Hermiticity and trace one are automatic; [positive semidefiniteness](../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) holds exactly for $|\mathbf s|\leq1$. Thus physical qubit states form the [Bloch ball](../../../../bloch-ball.md), with [pure states](../../../../pure-state.md) on the [Bloch sphere](../../../../bloch-sphere.md) and mixed states inside.

For the mixture of the two standard-basis states,

$$
\boxed{\rho=\begin{pmatrix}p_0&0\\0&p_1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf s=(0,0,p_0-p_1),\quad p_0+p_1=1.}
$$

These form the entire $z$-axis diameter, shown in the left panel of the diagram in part [b](../b.md). Generate the state by selecting $|0\rangle$ with classical [probability](../../../../probability.md) $p_0$ and $|1\rangle$ with probability $p_1$, then discarding the classical selection record. A coherent [superposition](../../../../superposition-principle.md) is generally a different state: the forgotten classical record removes off-diagonal coherence.
