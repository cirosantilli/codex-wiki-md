<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The output of a state of Bloch radius $u$ has [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $(1\pm ru)/2$. These are nonnegative for every $0\leq u\leq1$ exactly when

$$
\boxed{-1\leq r\leq1.}
$$

If $|r|>1$, a pure input already gives a negative eigenvalue. Conversely, $|r|\leq1$ preserves the [Bloch ball](../../../../../bloch-ball.md), hence positivity for states and, by positive scaling, for all positive operators. This is positivity of the map alone; physical implementation on part of an entangled system needs [complete positivity](../../../../../completely-positive-map.md).
