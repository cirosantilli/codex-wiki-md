<h1 id="3/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [Bell state](../../../../../bell-state-split.md) $|\Omega\rangle$ is an eigenvector of $J_r$ with eigenvalue $(1+3r)/2$. Its three-dimensional orthogonal complement has eigenvalue $(1-r)/2$. The [Choi criterion for complete positivity](../../../../../choi-s-theorem-on-completely-positive-maps.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{-\frac13\leq r\leq1.}
$$

For sufficiency one can also write a [Kraus representation](../../../../../kraus-representation.md) explicitly:

$$
\mathcal E_r(\rho)=\frac{1+3r}{4}\rho+
\frac{1-r}{4}(X\rho X+Y\rho Y+Z\rho Z).
$$

In this range the four coefficients are nonnegative and sum to one, giving a physical mixture of Pauli conjugations. This is the complete positivity range of the [depolarizing channel](../../../../../quantum-depolarizing-channel.md) in the Bloch-scaling parameterization.
