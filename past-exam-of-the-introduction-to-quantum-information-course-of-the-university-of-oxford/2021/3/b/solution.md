**No: the advertised pure joint state is incompatible with these marginals.** Their eigenvalue multisets are $\{0.9,0.1\}$ and $\{0.7,0.3\}$, which differ. Part [a](../a.md) shows that a bipartite pure state must give the same nonzero [Schmidt spectrum](../../../../schmidt-spectrum.md) on both sides.

This excludes Eve's purity claim, not every possible joint state or every possibility of entanglement. A mixed state can have unequal marginal spectra; the product $\rho_A\otimes\rho_B$, for example, has exactly the stated marginals.
