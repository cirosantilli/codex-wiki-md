<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [partial trace](../../../../partial-trace.md) removes the other orthonormal factor in the [Schmidt decomposition](../../../../schmidt-decomposition.md):

$$
\boxed{\rho_A=\sum_kp_k|a_k\rangle\langle a_k|,\qquad
\rho_B=\sum_kp_k|b_k\rangle\langle b_k|.}
$$

For instance, tracing $|a_kb_k\rangle\langle a_\ell b_\ell|$ over $B$ gives $\langle b_\ell|b_k\rangle|a_k\rangle\langle a_\ell|$, which vanishes unless $k=\ell$. Consequently the nonzero [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the two [reduced density operators](../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) agree, with multiplicity. If the subsystem dimensions differ, the orthonormal Schmidt families can be completed to full bases and the extra eigenvalues are zero.
