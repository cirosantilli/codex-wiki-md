# 2021

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the introduction to quantum information course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source: [original released examination PDF, bundle pages 93–96; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/oxford_physics_past_papers_2/response/2340696/attach/9/Physics%20Papers%202020.pdf?cookie_passthrough=1#page=93). The printed sitting is 7 June 2021 despite the bundle filename.

Solutions to all three questions. We use the usual [Pauli matrices](../pauli-matrices.md), column [state vectors](../state-vector.md), and the [density operator](../density-matrix-split.md) convention $\rho\mapsto U\rho U^\dagger$ for a gate. The CHSH conclusions require independent, uniformly chosen settings and no communication between the devices during a run. In Question 3, a reliable unknown-basis transformation means one deterministic operation that works for every common input basis.
