# Extrapolation

↑ **Parent:** [Interpolation](interpolation-split.md)

Estimating values beyond the range of the supplied sample points. For two distinct points $P,Q$, the [affine combination](affine-combination.md) $(1-t)P+tQ$ lies beyond an endpoint of their [line segment](line-segment.md) when $t<0$ or $t>1$. The construction specifies an extension of the chosen model; it does not show that an unknown underlying [function](function-split.md) follows that extension.
