# Lithium diisopropylamide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Lithium diisopropylamide](lithium-diisopropylamide.md) is a strong, sterically hindered base used to generate [enolates](enolate.md). Its low [nucleophilicity](nucleophilicity.md) toward many [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) substrates helps separate [deprotonation](deprotonation.md) from [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) addition.
