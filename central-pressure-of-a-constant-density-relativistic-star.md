# Central pressure of a constant-density relativistic star

↑ **Parent:** [Interior Schwarzschild metric](interior-schwarzschild-metric.md)

The [Interior Schwarzschild metric](interior-schwarzschild-metric.md) has central pressure

$$
p(0)=\rho
\frac{1-\sqrt{1-2M/R}}
{3\sqrt{1-2M/R}-1}.
$$

It diverges when $M/R=4/9$, the limiting compactness in [Buchdahl's theorem](buchdahl-s-theorem.md).
