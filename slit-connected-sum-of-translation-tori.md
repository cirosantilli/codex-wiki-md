# Slit connected sum of translation tori

↑ **Parent:** [Translation surface](translation-surface.md)

Cut equal straight slits in two [translation surfaces](translation-surface.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) one and cross-glue their banks by translation. The [connected sum of oriented manifolds](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) has [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) two and two cone points of angle $4\pi$, hence two simple zeros of its [holomorphic one-form](holomorphic-one-form.md). Choosing one [torus](torus.md) of side $\delta$ and a slit of length $\delta^2$ creates a flat-small handle without forcing its generator to have small [extremal length](extremal-length.md).
