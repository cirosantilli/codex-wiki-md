# Profinite Frattini subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Profinite group](profinite-group.md)

The intersection of the maximal proper open [subgroups](subgroup.md) of a [profinite group](profinite-group.md). A subset topologically generates the [profinite group](profinite-group.md) exactly when its image topologically generates its quotient by this intersection: a proper closed generated [subgroup](subgroup.md) is contained in a maximal open [subgroup](subgroup.md), obtained from a suitable finite quotient. For a [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md), $\Phi(G)=\overline{G^p[G,G]}$, where the bar denotes [closure](closure-topology.md) of the algebraically generated power and [commutator subgroups](commutator-subgroup.md). Closedness cannot simply be discarded in infinite rank.
