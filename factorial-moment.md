# Factorial moment

↑ **Parent:** [Birth-death master equation](birth-death-master-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factorial_moment)

The $r$th falling factorial moment is $\mathbb{E}[(N)_r]$, where $(N)_r=N(N-1)\cdots(N-r+1)$.
