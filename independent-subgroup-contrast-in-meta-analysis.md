# Independent subgroup contrast in meta-analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Meta-analysis](meta-analysis.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) subgroup estimates $\widehat\mu_1,\widehat\mu_2$ with [variances](variance-split.md) $v_1,v_2$, the null contrast has [Wald statistic](wald-test.md) $Z=(\widehat\mu_1-\widehat\mu_2)/\sqrt{v_1+v_2}$. If evidence overlaps, include minus twice the [covariance](covariance.md) in the contrast variance. A nonsignificant comparison is not proof of equivalence.
