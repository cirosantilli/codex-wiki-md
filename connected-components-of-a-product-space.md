# Connected components of a product space

↑ **Parent:** [Connected component](connected-component.md)

The [product of connected spaces](product-of-connected-spaces.md) is connected, so the product of the two [connected components](connected-component.md) lies in the component of $(x,y)$. Conversely, continuous projections carry that component to connected sets in the factors, which must lie in $C_x$ and $C_y$. These two inclusions prove equality in arbitrary [topological spaces](topological-space.md).
