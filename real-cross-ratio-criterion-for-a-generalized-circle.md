# Real cross-ratio criterion for a generalized circle

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md)

Normalize three distinct points of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) by a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $f$ sending them to $\infty,0,1$. A fourth point lies on their [generalized circle](generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md) exactly when $f$ takes a real value there: the image of that [generalized circle](generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md) is the extended real line. Other standard [cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md) ordering conventions differ by real fractional-linear transformations and preserve this reality criterion.
