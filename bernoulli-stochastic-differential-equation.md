# Bernoulli stochastic differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md)

On the positive half-line, divide by the [stochastic exponential](doleans-dade-exponential.md) $F_t=e^{\alpha B_t-\alpha^2t/2}$. The [Itô product rule](ito-product-rule.md) reduces the equation to the random [Bernoulli differential equation](bernoulli-differential-equation.md) $d(X/F)/dt=F^{\delta-1}(X/F)^\delta$. For $\delta\ne1$ this gives $X_t=F_t[x_0^{1-\delta}+(1-\delta)\int_0^tF_s^{\delta-1}ds]^{1/(1-\delta)}$, until the bracket vanishes; for $\delta=1$, $X_t=x_0F_te^t$.
