# Fermi wavelength

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi wavevector](fermi-wavevector.md)

The wavelength associated with a specified [Fermi wavevector](fermi-wavevector.md). A local-density response normally requires variation on a scale large compared with $k_F^{-1}$; numerical factors such as $2\pi$ must be kept explicit when comparing a variation scale with the full wavelength.
