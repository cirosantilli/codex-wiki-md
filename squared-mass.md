# Squared mass

↑ **Parent:** [Mass term](mass-term.md)

The parameter multiplying a scalar quadratic [mass term](mass-term.md). A negative value describes an instability of the expansion point, rather than a negative value of the physical [mass](mass.md) of a stable excitation. Nonnegative quartic terms do not stabilize a negative quadratic term along an exactly flat quartic ray.
