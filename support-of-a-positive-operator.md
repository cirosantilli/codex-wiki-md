# Support of a positive operator

↑ **Parent:** [Positive operator](positive-operator.md)

The support of a [positive operator](positive-operator.md) is the [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) of its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md). In finite dimension it is the span of [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) with positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), equivalently the operator's image. [Quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md) is finite only when the first state's support lies in the reference state's support.
