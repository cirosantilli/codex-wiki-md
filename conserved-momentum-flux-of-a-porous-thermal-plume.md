# Conserved momentum flux of a porous thermal plume

↑ **Parent:** [Porous thermal plume](porous-thermal-plume.md)

For a slender [porous thermal plume](porous-thermal-plume.md), [Darcy law](darcy-law.md) gives $w=\beta(T-T_0)$, with $\beta=Kg\alpha/\nu$. If $C_h=\rho_0c_p$ and $F_h=C_h\int w(T-T_0)dx$ is the conserved excess convective [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) per span, then the kinematic [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) is $M=\int w^2dx=\beta F_h/C_h$, and the [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) is $B=\int g\alpha(T-T_0)w dx=g\alpha F_h/C_h$. The [hyperbolic-secant porous plume profile](hyperbolic-secant-porous-plume-profile.md) has $Q=(36\kappa Mz)^{1/3}$. Multiplication of $Q$ and $M$ by $\rho_0$ gives physical [mass flux](mass-flux.md) and the density-weighted Darcy second moment. Intrinsic pore-velocity momentum flux instead has an additional inverse-porosity factor. Momentum conservation here follows from the heat/velocity proportionality, not from an inertia balance: porous drag balances buoyancy locally.
