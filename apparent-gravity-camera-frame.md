# Apparent-gravity camera frame

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design.md)

If $g$ is gravitational [acceleration](acceleration.md) and $a$ is the camera's [acceleration](acceleration.md), the locally felt upward vector is $U=a-g$. Choose the travel [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $X$, set $W=U-(U\cdot X)X$, and, when $W\ne0$, take $Z=W/\|W\|$ and $Y=Z\times X$. These form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) with $U$ in the $XZ$ plane. If $U$ is parallel to $X$, the condition leaves the roll angle free even when $U\ne0$; transport a previous transverse axis or choose a well-conditioned coordinate axis and project it into $X^\perp$.
