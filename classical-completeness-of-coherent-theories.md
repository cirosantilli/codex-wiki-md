# Classical completeness of coherent theories

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent theory](coherent-theory.md)

A coherent sequent true in all set models of a coherent theory is coherently derivable. A generic underivable sequent remains false after the [Boolean cover by a generic quasi-closed subtopos](boolean-cover-by-a-generic-quasi-closed-subtopos.md). Slice over its nonzero Boolean counterexample to show classical consistency of the theory with a named tuple satisfying the antecedent and negated consequent. The [Henkin construction](henkin-construction.md) then gives a set-valued countermodel. This argument does not assume that every Boolean topos has points.
