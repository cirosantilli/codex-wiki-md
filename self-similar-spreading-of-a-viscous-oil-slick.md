# Self-similar spreading of a viscous oil slick

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric viscous-sheet stretching equations](axisymmetric-viscous-sheet-stretching-equations.md)

For external pressure $\Delta\rho\,gh$ and edge condition $h\sigma_{rr}=-\Delta\rho\,gh^2/2$, the fixed-volume [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) is a uniformly thick disc:

$$
h=\frac{6\mu}{\Delta\rho\,gt},\qquad u=\frac r{2t},\qquad R^2=\frac{\Delta\rho\,gVt}{6\pi\mu}.
$$

[Conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) fixes the similarity velocity. The radial equation then forces a differentiable thickness profile to be constant, and the edge [stress](stress.md) fixes that constant. The singular point-release solution describes the spreading scaling; a finite initial uniform disc corresponds to shifting time.
