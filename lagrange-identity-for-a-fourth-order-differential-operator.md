# Lagrange identity for a fourth-order differential operator

↑ **Parent:** [Self-adjoint fourth-order scalar differential operator](self-adjoint-fourth-order-scalar-differential-operator.md)

For $L=D^4+q(x)$ where $q$ is a real [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on $[a,b]$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\int_a^b\bigl(\overline u Lv-\overline{Lu}v\bigr)\,dx
=\left[\overline u v'''-\overline{u'}v''+\overline{u''}v'-\overline{u'''}v\right]_a^b.
$$

The potential terms cancel, and differentiating the boundary expression verifies the identity. Under [clamped boundary conditions](clamped-boundary-condition.md) $u=u'=v=v'=0$ at both endpoints, this form vanishes. Conversely, requiring its vanishing against every clamped test [function](function-split.md) forces the adjoint [function](function-split.md) and its first [derivative](derivative.md) to vanish at both endpoints, because the second and third test-[function](function-split.md) [derivatives](derivative.md) are independent endpoint traces. Thus, on a regular finite [interval](interval-mathematics.md) with the usual fourth-order Sobolev domain, the clamped realization is [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md). The equality of domains is part of this conclusion; formal [self-adjointness](self-adjoint-operator.md) of the expression alone does not supply it.
