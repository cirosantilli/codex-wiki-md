<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use $F$ for the maximized [objective function](../../../../objective-function.md), and adopt [optimization constraints](../../../../optimization-constraint.md) $g_i\geq0$ with

$$
g_1=2-x_1,\qquad g_2=20-x_1^2-x_2^2,\qquad g_3=x_2-x_1^2.
$$

The [feasible set](../../../../feasible-region.md) is nonempty, since it contains $(0,0)$. It is closed as an intersection of inverse images of [closed sets](../../../../closed-set.md) under [continuous functions](../../../../continuous-function.md). It lies in the disk of radius $\sqrt{20}$, so is bounded and therefore compact by the [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md). The [objective function](../../../../objective-function.md) is a [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md), hence continuous. The [extreme value theorem](../../../../extreme-value-theorem.md) now guarantees **a global maximizer exists**.
