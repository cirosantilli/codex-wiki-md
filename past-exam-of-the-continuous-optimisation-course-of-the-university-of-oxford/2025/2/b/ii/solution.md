<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $G=\prod_i y_i^{a_i}>0$ be the weighted [geometric mean](../../../../../geometric-mean.md) and set $x_i=y_i/G$. Since the weights sum to one,

$$
\prod_i x_i^{a_i}=\frac{\prod_i y_i^{a_i}}{G^{\sum_i a_i}}=1.
$$

Part (i) implies $\sum_i a_iy_i/G\geq1$, proving the [weighted arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](../../../../../weighted-arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md):

$$
\boxed{\prod_i y_i^{a_i}\leq\sum_i a_iy_i}.
$$

Equality holds exactly when $x_i=1$ for every $i$, or equivalently when all the $y_i$ are equal.
