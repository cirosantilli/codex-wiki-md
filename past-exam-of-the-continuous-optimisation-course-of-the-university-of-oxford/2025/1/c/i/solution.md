<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $g=\nabla f(x)\ne0$, the [positive-definite symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md) $B$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../invertible-matrix.md) and $s=-B^{-1}g\ne0$. Multiplying $Bs=-g$ by $s^T$ gives

$$
\boxed{g^Ts=-s^TBs<0}.
$$

Hence $s$ is a [descent direction](../../../../../descent-direction.md). If $g=0$, then $s=0$ and the algorithm has already reached a [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md); the strict descent assertion applies before termination.
