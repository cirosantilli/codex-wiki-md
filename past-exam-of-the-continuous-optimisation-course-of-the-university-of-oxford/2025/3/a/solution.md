<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $x,y$ are [feasible points](../../../../feasible-point.md) and $0\leq t\leq1$, [concavity](../../../../concave-function.md) gives

$$
c_i(tx+(1-t)y)\geq tc_i(x)+(1-t)c_i(y)\geq0
$$

for every $i$. Thus the [feasible set](../../../../feasible-region.md) is a [convex set](../../../../convex-set-split.md). Since the [objective function](../../../../objective-function.md) is [convex](../../../../convex-function.md), this is a [convex optimization](../../../../convex-optimization-split.md) problem.
