# Morphological instability of a solidification front

↑ **Parent:** [Freezing](freezing.md)

A morphological instability amplifies shape perturbations of a [solidification](freezing.md) interface. Protrusions may receive a larger diffusive supply or encounter larger undercooling, while [curvature-induced melting-temperature depression](curvature-induced-melting-temperature-depression.md) suppresses fine corrugations. Solute rejection can produce [constitutional supercooling](constitutional-supercooling.md), destabilizing a planar front and leading to cells or [crystal dendrites](dendrite-crystal.md).
