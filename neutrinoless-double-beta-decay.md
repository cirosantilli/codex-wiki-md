# Neutrinoless double beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Double beta decay](double-beta-decay.md)

A lepton-number-violating nuclear transformation emitting two electrons without [neutrinos](neutrino.md). A signal appears near the nuclear [Q value](q-value.md) in summed electron [energy](energy-split.md), after recoil and detector response are accounted for. Its interpretation requires the decay mechanism and nuclear [matrix elements](matrix-element.md).
