# Fourier cosine series of a triangular wave

↑ **Parent:** [Triangular wave](triangular-wave.md)

The period-two extension of $1-|x|$ on $[-1,1]$ is even. Its [Fourier cosine series](fourier-cosine-series.md) has $a_0=1$ and $a_n=2\int_0^1(1-x)\cos(n\pi x)dx=2[1-(-1)^n]/(n^2\pi^2)$ by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). This proves the displayed series, with only odd nonconstant harmonics. Continuity and piecewise smoothness give pointwise equality through the [Dirichlet-Jordan convergence theorem](dirichlet-jordan-convergence-theorem.md); the summable coefficient magnitudes also imply [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) by the [Weierstrass M-test](weierstrass-m-test.md). At $x=0$, it gives $\sum_{m\geq0}(2m+1)^{-2}=\pi^2/8$.
