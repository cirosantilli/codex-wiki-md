# Coherence of an affine module sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Affine module sheaf](affine-module-sheaf.md)

For a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) $A$, a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) $M$ has a [finite presentation of a module](finite-presentation-of-a-module.md), and kernels of maps from [finite free modules](finite-free-module.md) to $M$ are finitely generated. Its [affine module sheaf](affine-module-sheaf.md) is therefore a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md). Conversely, if $\widetilde M$ is coherent, choose a finite [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md) cover on which its section [modules](module-mathematics.md) are finitely generated. Lift their finitely many generators to numerators in $M$. The [submodule](submodule.md) generated by these numerators becomes all of $M$ on every chart, and hence is all of $M$: its quotient has zero [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md) at every point. This proves finite generation without assuming that [global sections](global-section.md) preserve arbitrary [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) [surjections](surjective-function.md).
