# Hausdorff property of a nonempty product

↑ **Parent:** [Product topology](product-topology.md)

For nonempty [topological spaces](topological-space.md) $X,Y$, their [product topology](product-topology.md) is [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) exactly when both factors are [Hausdorff spaces](hausdorff-space.md). Distinct pairs differ in some coordinate, and disjoint [open](open-set.md) [neighbourhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) in that factor give disjoint [open](open-set.md) rectangles in the [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md). Conversely, fixing $y_0\in Y$ gives a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) from $X$ onto the [topological subspace](topological-subspace.md) $X\times\{y_0\}$; similarly for $Y$. The [Hausdorff condition](hausdorff-space.md) passes to [topological subspaces](topological-subspace.md). Nonemptiness matters: an [empty set](empty-set.md) product is [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) regardless of the other factor.
