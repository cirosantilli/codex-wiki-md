# Scalar propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md)

For a free massive scalar in a [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md), inversion of the quadratic kernel gives $\Delta_E(p)=1/(p^2+m^2)$. The Minkowski version follows by [Wick rotation](wick-rotation.md).
