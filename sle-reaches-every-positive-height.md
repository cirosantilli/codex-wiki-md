# SLE reaches every positive height

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling invariance of SLE](scaling-invariance-of-sle.md)

For a chordal [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) trace $\gamma$, the [Scaling invariance of SLE](scaling-invariance-of-sle.md) makes $\mathbb P(\sup_t\operatorname{Im}\gamma(t)>r)$ independent of $r>0$. The trace must enter the upper half-plane because its positive-time hull has positive [half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md). Letting $r\downarrow0$ therefore makes this probability one, and continuity implies that every positive height is reached. This argument does not assume [Transience of chordal SLE](transience-of-chordal-sle.md).
