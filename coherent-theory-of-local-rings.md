# Coherent theory of local rings

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent theory](coherent-theory.md)

Use the signature of unital [commutative rings](commutative-ring.md), its equational axioms, and the [coherent sequents](coherent-sequent.md) $0=1\vdash\bot$ and $\top\vdash_x\operatorname{Inv}(x)\vee\operatorname{Inv}(1-x)$, where $\operatorname{Inv}(x)$ abbreviates $\exists y\,(xy=1)$. In a set-based model the nonunits form its unique [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md); this is the usual nonzero [local ring](local-ring.md). In a [topos](elementary-topos.md) the disjunction expresses local existence of a unit witness. Its [classifying topos](classifying-topos.md) is defined by the [Zariski coverage on finitely presented rings](zariski-coverage-on-finitely-presented-rings.md).
