# Exchange-correlation functional

↑ **Parent:** [Density functional theory](density-functional-theory.md)

The part of the exact density functional left after subtracting the auxiliary noninteracting kinetic energy and classical [Hartree electrostatic energy](hartree-electrostatic-energy.md). It includes fermionic exchange, correlation and the difference between interacting and noninteracting kinetic energies. Its functional derivative enters the [Kohn-Sham equations](kohn-sham-equations.md). Practical approximations to this functional, rather than the existence of a density description itself, are a central source of error.
