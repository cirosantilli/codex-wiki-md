# Half-plane-capacity parameterization

↑ **Parent:** [Half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md)

A growing hull is parameterized by half-plane capacity when $\operatorname{hcap}(K_t)=2t$. The factor two makes its [Chordal Loewner equation](chordal-loewner-equation.md) take the conventional form $\partial_tg_t=2/(g_t-U_t)$.
