# Thermal Landau quasiparticle energy shift

↑ **Parent:** [Landau quasiparticle energy functional](landau-quasiparticle-energy-functional.md)

For a symmetric interaction kernel, $\delta E/\delta n_a=\epsilon_a+V^{-1}\sum_bf_{ab}\delta n_b$. Smooth Fermi-surface kernels and fixed-density chemical-potential shifts give an $O(T^2)$ thermal correction by the [Sommerfeld expansion](sommerfeld-expansion.md). The reference dispersion already includes zero-temperature interactions.
