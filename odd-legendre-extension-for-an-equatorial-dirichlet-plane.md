# Odd Legendre extension for an equatorial Dirichlet plane

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric harmonic extension from a sphere](axisymmetric-harmonic-extension-from-a-sphere.md)

Suppose the exterior of a sphere also has zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) on the part of its equatorial plane outside the sphere. The plane separates the upper and lower domains. Extend the sphere data of each hemisphere oddly across the equator, and apply the [axisymmetric harmonic extension from a sphere](axisymmetric-harmonic-extension-from-a-sphere.md) separately to these two odd extensions. Only odd-degree [Legendre polynomials](legendre-polynomial.md) occur, and every such polynomial vanishes at $u=\cos\theta=0$. Thus the resulting [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) vanishes on the plane. For upper data $f(u)$ on $0\le u\le1$, the exterior coefficients are $(2\ell+1)\int_0^1f(u)P_\ell(u)\,du$ for odd $\ell$. If $f(0)=0$, the plane and sphere data agree at their shared edge. The same construction with the lower data gives the lower solution; their normal [derivatives](derivative.md) need not match through a conducting sheet.
