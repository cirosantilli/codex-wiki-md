# Generic coordinate reals for finite-function forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Fn forcing](fn-forcing.md)

For [Fn forcing](fn-forcing.md) on $\kappa\times\omega$ with values in $2$, the [generic filter](generic-filter.md) union is total by the dense coordinate-domain requirements. For each pair of distinct rows, the [set](set-split.md) of conditions giving them opposite bits at some column is dense: choose a column untouched in both finite rows. Thus the rows are distinct [subsets](subset.md) of $\omega$ and provide an [injective function](injective-function.md) from the ground-model [ordinal](ordinal.md) $\kappa$ into the extension's [power set](power-set.md) of $\omega$. This construction alone does not prove preservation of its ground-model [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) status.
