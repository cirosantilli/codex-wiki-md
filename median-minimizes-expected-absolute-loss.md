# Median minimizes expected absolute loss

↑ **Parent:** [Median](median.md)

For a finite first absolute moment, the minimizers of expected absolute loss are the [medians](median.md). With a density, differentiating the loss gives $2F(a)-1$, so every point where the [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) equals one half minimizes it. A density gap may give a whole minimizing interval. In contrast, finite squared loss is uniquely minimized by the [expectation](expected-value.md).
