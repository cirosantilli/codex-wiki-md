# Null dust

↑ **Parent:** [Dust stress-energy tensor](dust-stress-energy-tensor.md)

Null dust is matter with a [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) $T_{ab}=\varepsilon k_ak_b$ for a future-directed [null vector](null-vector.md) $k$. For every future-directed [timelike vector](timelike-vector.md) $t$, the energy current is $-T^a{}_bt^b=\varepsilon(-k\cdot t)k^a$. Therefore its [dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md) holds exactly when $\varepsilon\geq0$, allowing the zero current. Rescaling $k$ rescales $\varepsilon$ inversely by the square and leaves the [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) unchanged. Ingoing [Vaidya metric](vaidya-metric.md) matter has $k=-\partial_r$, $k_a=-(dv)_a$, and $\varepsilon=\dot m/(4\pi r^2)$.
