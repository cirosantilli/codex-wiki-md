# Closed graph of a map into a Hausdorff space

↑ **Parent:** [Graph of a function](graph-of-a-function.md)

For a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $f:X\to Y$ with [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) $Y$, its [graph of a function](graph-of-a-function.md) is closed in the [product topology](product-topology.md). If $y\ne f(x)$, choose disjoint neighborhoods of those two points in $Y$. Continuity supplies a neighborhood of $x$ whose image lies in the neighborhood of $f(x)$; its product with the other neighborhood misses the graph.
