# Dielectric loss tangent

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric loss](dielectric-loss.md)

For a [capacitor](capacitor.md) represented by ideal [capacitance](capacitance.md) $C$ and series [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R_s$, the [complex impedance](electrical-impedance.md) is $R_s-i/(\omega C)$. The ratio of its dissipative part to the magnitude of its reactive part is $\tan\delta=\omega CR_s$. This dimensionless ratio characterizes loss at a specified [frequency](frequency.md); equivalent parallel parameters generally differ from the series parameters.
