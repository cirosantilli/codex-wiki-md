# Bounded Girsanov density for exit of an unstable linear diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md)

Under a reference measure where $X$ is [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at zero, let $T$ be its exit time from $(-1,1)$. The displayed density is the stochastic exponential with integrand $X\mathbf1_{[0,T]}$, by the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md). It is bounded by $e^{1/2}$, hence defines a uniformly integrable density process with terminal density $Z_T$. Under that measure change, $X_t-\int_0^{t\wedge T}X_sds$ is [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). At exit, $Z_T\ge e^{1/2-T}$, implying $\mathbb P(T\le t)\ge e^{1/2-t}\widetilde{\mathbb P}(T\le t)$. This is a terminal density on the whole probability space, not merely an unspecified finite-horizon change of measure.
