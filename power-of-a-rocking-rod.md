# Power of a rocking rod

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillating rigid rod in resistive-force theory](oscillating-rigid-rod-in-resistive-force-theory.md)

The leading mean [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) of a translating and rotating rigid rod is $c_\perp\epsilon^2\omega^2(La^2+L^3/12)/2$ for equal dimensionless translation and angle amplitudes. Both terms are quadratic in amplitude; rotational material [velocity](velocity.md) cannot be omitted simply because the angle is small.
