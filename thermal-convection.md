# Thermal convection

↑ **Parent:** [Convection](convection.md)

Thermal convection is fluid motion driven by temperature-dependent [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). It transports [heat](heat.md) by bulk motion in addition to [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md).
