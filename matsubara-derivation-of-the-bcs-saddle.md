# Matsubara derivation of the BCS saddle

↑ **Parent:** [BCS gap equation](bcs-gap-equation.md)

Differentiate the [subtracted grand potential of the reduced BCS model](subtracted-grand-potential-of-the-reduced-bcs-model.md) with respect to the squared pairing amplitude. The displayed fermionic [Matsubara sum](bosonic-matsubara-sum.md) converts the derivative of the trace logarithm into $\sum_k\tanh(\beta E_k/2)/(2E_k)$. Setting it equal to $V/\lambda$ gives the uniform nonzero-gap saddle. The limit E→0 is finite at positive temperature, $\beta/4$. The sum relies on antiperiodic temporal boundary conditions from the [fermionic coherent-state trace](fermionic-coherent-state-trace.md).
