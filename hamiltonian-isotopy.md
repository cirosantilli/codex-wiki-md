# Hamiltonian isotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md)

A Hamiltonian isotopy is the flow of a time-dependent [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md). Its time-one map is a Hamiltonian diffeomorphism.
