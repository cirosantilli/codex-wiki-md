# Special cube complex

↑ **Parent:** [Cubical complex](cubical-complex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_cube_complex)

A nonpositively curved cube complex is special when every hyperplane is embedded and two-sided, no hyperplane self-osculates, and no two hyperplanes interosculate. Equivalently, it admits a cubical local isometry to a [Salvetti complex](salvetti-complex.md).
