# Stellar age

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

The stellar age is the elapsed time since a [star](star.md) formed. A constant number formation rate on a finite interval gives a [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) of present ages if every formed object remains counted, including stellar remnants. An age distribution inferred only from surviving [main sequence](main-sequence.md) stars generally differs because their lifetimes depend on [mass](mass.md).
