# Rank map in topological K-theory

↑ **Parent:** [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md)

The rank of a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) is locally constant on the base. Taking the difference of the ranks extends to the [Grothendieck group](grothendieck-group.md) of [vector bundles](vector-bundle.md), giving the rank map in [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md). For a [finite CW complex](finite-cw-complex.md), its kernel consists of classes of rank zero on every [connected component](connected-component.md).
