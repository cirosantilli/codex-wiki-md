# Explosive seismic source

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic source](seismic-source.md)

A [seismic source](seismic-source.md) producing an impulsive disturbance by a professionally managed subsurface explosion. Its applications include surveys needing strong, broad-band energy, but source coupling, repeatability, ground disturbance, site access and specialist operational requirements influence suitability. [Vibroseis](vibroseis.md) instead distributes controlled energy over a known [frequency](frequency.md) sweep.
