# Magnetic angular-momentum invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Charged particle in a uniform magnetic field](charged-particle-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md)

For a particle obeying $m\dot{\mathbf v}=q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$ in a constant uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), the displayed combination is conserved. The derivative of mechanical [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) projected onto the field is canceled by the quadratic position term. This is the rotational analogue of the distinction between mechanical and [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) in an electromagnetic field.
