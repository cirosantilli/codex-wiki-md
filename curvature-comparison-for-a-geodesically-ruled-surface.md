# Curvature comparison for a geodesically ruled surface

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss equation in a curved ambient manifold](gauss-equation-in-a-curved-ambient-manifold.md)

A surface locally swept out by ambient [geodesics](geodesic.md) has $II(T,T)=0$ in its unit ruling direction $T$. For a perpendicular unit tangent $E$, the [Gauss equation in a curved ambient manifold](gauss-equation-in-a-curved-ambient-manifold.md) therefore gives the displayed identity. Its [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) is at most the ambient [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) in its tangent plane, with equality precisely when $II(T,E)=0$. An independent variation argument applies the [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) equation to the length of a perpendicular connecting field; its ambient derivative has the extra normal component $II(T,E)$. The Euclidean surface $(t,s,ts)$ is ruled by straight lines and has [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) $-(1+t^2+s^2)^{-2}<0$.
