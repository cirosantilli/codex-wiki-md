# Prime coefficient detection of quasi-isomorphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md)

Let $f$ be a [chain map](chain-map.md) between degreewise finitely generated [free abelian groups](free-abelian-group.md). If its reduction modulo every [prime number](prime-number.md) is a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md), the [mapping cone acyclicity criterion](mapping-cone-acyclicity-criterion.md) makes $M(f)\otimes\mathbb F_p$ acyclic. The [short exact sequence of chain complexes](short-exact-sequence-of-chain-complexes.md) $0\to M\xrightarrow{p}M\to M\otimes\mathbb F_p\to0$ implies that multiplication by $p$ is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) on every $H_i(M)$. These are [finitely generated abelian groups](finitely-generated-abelian-group.md). Their free summands would make multiplication by $p$ fail to be surjective, and any nonzero finite summand would make it fail to be injective for a prime dividing its order. Thus $H_i(M)=0$ and $f$ is a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md). Degreewise finite generation is essential to this argument.
