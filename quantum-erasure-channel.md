# Quantum erasure channel

↑ **Parent:** [Memoryless quantum channel](memoryless-quantum-channel.md)

A quantum erasure channel transmits an input state unchanged with probability $1-p$ and replaces it by an orthogonal, recognizable erasure flag with probability $p$. Its output space has one more dimension than the input. This is a [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) for $0\le p\le1$ and preserves all input coherences on the nonerased branch.
