# Reciprocal-factorial series remainder bound

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential function](exponential-function.md)

The positive tail after term $n$ in the reciprocal-[factorial](factorial.md) series for [Euler's number](e-mathematical-constant.md) satisfies $1/(n+1)!<e-\sum_{i=0}^n1/i!\leq(n+2)/((n+1)(n+1)!)$. The upper bound replaces each subsequent denominator factor by $n+2$ and sums the resulting [geometric series](geometric-series.md); the lower bound retains only the first omitted term. This supplies an explicit error certificate and, with [Stirling's formula](stirling-formula.md), a term count of order $d/\log d$ for $d$ decimal digits. The arithmetic evaluation also has its own rounding error, distinct from this truncation bound.
