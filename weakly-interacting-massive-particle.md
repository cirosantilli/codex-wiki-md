# Weakly interacting massive particle

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter](dark-matter.md)

A [weakly interacting massive particle](weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) is a massive dark-matter candidate whose interaction strength permits thermal production and later [cosmological particle freeze-out](cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md). Its relic abundance depends on the annihilation rate and cosmological history, not on its mass alone. At nucleosynthesis temperatures a 50 GeV example is nonrelativistic: its equilibrium density is reduced by [Boltzmann suppression](boltzmann-suppression.md), while an already frozen relic population need not follow the equilibrium density. Its effect on nucleosynthesis depends on whether the surviving energy density is appreciable or whether it injects reaction-altering products.
