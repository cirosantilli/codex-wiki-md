# Gaussian perturbation of a quantum harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Time-independent perturbation theory](time-independent-perturbation-theory.md)

For perturbation $V_0e^{-\beta^2x^2}$ and oscillator inverse length $\alpha=\sqrt{m\omega/\hbar}$, direct Gaussian expectation values give $\Delta E_0=V_0\alpha/(\alpha^2+\beta^2)^{1/2}$ and $\Delta E_1=V_0\alpha^3/(\alpha^2+\beta^2)^{3/2}$. A narrow perturbation samples the odd state's [node](node-dynamical-systems.md) and suppresses its shift by two extra [powers](power.md) of $\alpha/\beta$. A broad perturbation approaches a constant [energy](energy-split.md) offset for both states.
