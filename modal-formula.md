# Modal formula

↑ **Parent:** [Modal logic](modal-logic.md)

A [modal formula](modal-formula.md) is built from [propositional variables](propositional-variable.md) and logical constants using Boolean connectives and [modal operators](modal-operator.md), such as the box operator. In [provability logic](provability-logic.md), arithmetic realization replaces propositional letters by arithmetic sentences and interprets a box as the corresponding arithmetic provability assertion. Modal validity, theoremhood and truth under a particular arithmetic realization are distinct notions.
