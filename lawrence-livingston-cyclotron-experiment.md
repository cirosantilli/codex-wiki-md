# Lawrence-Livingston cyclotron experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotron](cyclotron.md)

[Ernest Orlando Lawrence](ernest-orlando-lawrence.md) and [Milton Stanley Livingston](milton-stanley-livingston.md) reported repeated [ion](ion.md) acceleration in 1932. Their evacuated brass chamber used one driven hollow [cyclotron dee](cyclotron-dee.md); the grounded box and slotted divider supplied the other electrode. Filament [electrons](electron.md) ionized [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) near the gap. A perpendicular [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) bent the [ions](ion.md); a high-frequency [oscillator](oscillator.md) drove the gap. Deflecting plates and slits selected particles for a [Faraday cup](faraday-cup.md), read by an [electrometer](electrometer.md).[Lawrence and Livingston, 1932, pp. 25–28, Figs. 2–4: actual accelerating electrode, source, collector and oscillator](the-production-of-high-speed-light-ions-without-the-use-of-high-voltages-by-ernest-orlando-lawrence-and-milton-stanley-livingston.md)

Scanning the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) produced narrow [resonance](resonance.md) peaks. The optimum deflecting voltage tracked the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) predicted from field and orbit [radius](radius.md), supporting the reported $1.22\,\mathrm{MeV}$ [protons](proton.md). Source width and slit geometry limited this check; it was not a direct [energy](energy-split.md) measurement of every particle.[pp. 31–33, Figs. 7–9: resonance scans, field and wavelength measurements, deflection-voltage energy check and geometric uncertainty; p. 19: maximum reported proton energy](the-production-of-high-speed-light-ions-without-the-use-of-high-voltages-by-ernest-orlando-lawrence-and-milton-stanley-livingston.md)

This combination distinguishes producing a circulating [electric current](electric-current.md) from establishing its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). The [electrometer](electrometer.md) measures collected [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) per unit time; it cannot by itself tell whether the collected particles are fast. The separate deflection selection supplies a speed-dependent test. Agreement of two consequences of the model, the [cyclotron resonance condition](cyclotron-resonance-condition.md) and the [cyclotron energy and orbit radius](cyclotron-energy-and-orbit-radius.md) relation, makes the inference stronger than either a current signal or a predicted orbit alone.

[Milton Stanley Livingston](milton-stanley-livingston.md) built the early [cyclotrons](cyclotron.md) with [Ernest Orlando Lawrence](ernest-orlando-lawrence.md); the 1939 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) went to [Ernest Orlando Lawrence](ernest-orlando-lawrence.md). His lecture, The evolution of the cyclotron, was delivered on 11 December 1951.[Ernest Lawrence entry: 1939 prize and delayed lecture on 11 December 1951](more-timelines-by-lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory.md)[M. Stanley Livingston: co-inventor of the cyclotron and early Berkeley work](m-stanley-livingston-by-fermilab.md)
