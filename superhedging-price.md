# Superhedging price

↑ **Parent:** [Superhedging](superhedging.md)

The infimum of initial costs of admissible [superhedging](superhedging.md) portfolios. Positive [pricing kernels](state-price-density.md) supply lower bounds: if $\mathbb E[ZP]=p$ and $H\cdot P\geq X$, then $H\cdot p\geq\mathbb E[ZX]$ whenever these [expectations](expected-value.md) exist.
