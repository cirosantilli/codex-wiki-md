# Cole-Hopf transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole-Hopf_transformation)

The Cole-Hopf transformation $u=-2\nu\phi_x/\phi$ converts the [viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md) into the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) $\phi_t=\nu\phi_{xx}$. Initial data $u(x,0)=g(x)$ correspond to $\phi(x,0)=C\exp[-(2\nu)^{-1}\int^xg(s)\,ds]$.
