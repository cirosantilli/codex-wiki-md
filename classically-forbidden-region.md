# Classically forbidden region

↑ **Parent:** [Time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md)

A [classically forbidden region](classically-forbidden-region.md) would require negative classical [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), but a quantum [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) need not vanish there. For constant [potential energy](potential-energy.md), it is $Ae^{qx}+Be^{-qx}$ with $q=\sqrt{2m(V-E)}/\hbar$. Boundary matching determines both coefficients. Beside a hard wall at $D$, a nonzero forbidden-region solution is proportional to $\sinh[q(D-x)]$, and therefore has no interior [nodes](node-dynamical-systems.md). Across a finite barrier, both exponentials are generally needed; a pure decaying exponential is appropriate only when the other boundary condition permits it.
