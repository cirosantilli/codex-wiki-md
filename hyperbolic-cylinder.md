# Hyperbolic cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Quadric surface](quadric-surface.md)

For $a,b>0$, this [quadric surface](quadric-surface.md) is independent of its third coordinate and is formed by translating a [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) along that direction. Its two connected components correspond to $u\geq a$ and $u\leq-a$. In [elliptic cylindrical coordinates](elliptic-cylindrical-coordinates.md), fixing a generic angular coordinate selects a half-branch of such a cylinder because the signs of both planar coordinates are fixed by that angular value.
