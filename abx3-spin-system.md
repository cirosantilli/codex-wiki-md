# ABX3 spin system

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

A [spin](spin.md) system with two distinguishable, mutually coupled [spins](spin.md) A and B, each coupled to three equivalent X [spins](spin.md). An ethoxy methylene with nonequivalent hydrogens can display this pattern; small A/B chemical-shift separation relative to their coupling produces second-order distortion.
