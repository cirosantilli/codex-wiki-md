# Hom representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

For [Lie algebra representations](lie-algebra-representation.md) $V$ and $W$, the Hom representation on $\operatorname{Hom}(V,W)$ is

$$
(x\cdot f)(v)=x\cdot f(v)-f(x\cdot v).
$$

Its invariant vectors are exactly the intertwining maps.
