# Kappa-filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)

For a [set](set-split.md) $A$ of size an uncountable [regular cardinal](regular-cardinal.md) $\kappa$, an increasing sequence $(A_\alpha)_{\alpha<\kappa}$ with union $A$, each $|A_\alpha|<\kappa$, and $A_\delta=\bigcup_{\alpha<\delta}A_\alpha$ at every nonzero limit stage.
