# Polar-coordinate T-dual background

↑ **Parent:** [Buscher dilaton shift](buscher-dilaton-shift.md)

The flat polar metric $dr^2+r^2dz^2$ has a circle isometry away from $r=0$. Its local [Buscher rules](buscher-rules.md) give the displayed dual metric and [dilaton](dilaton.md). The dual [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) has components $R_{rr}=-2/r^2$ and $R_{\widetilde z\widetilde z}=-2/r^4$, while the dilaton Hessian has components $1/r^2$ and $1/r^4$. Thus the [leading metric-dilaton Weyl condition](leading-metric-dilaton-weyl-condition.md) $R_{ab}+2\nabla_a\nabla_b\Phi=0$ holds despite nonzero curvature. Flat spectators complete a critical target; the scalar anomaly condition also holds because $|\nabla\Phi|^2=1/r^2$, $\Box\Phi=2/r^2$ and $R=-4/r^2$. The construction is local on $r>0$, excluding the degenerating circle.
