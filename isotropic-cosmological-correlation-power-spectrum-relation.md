# Isotropic cosmological correlation-power-spectrum relation

↑ **Parent:** [Correlation function (astronomy)](correlation-function-astronomy.md)

For the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) convention $\delta_{\mathbf k}=\int d^3x\,\delta(\mathbf x)e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}$ and covariance $\langle\delta_{\mathbf k}\delta_{\mathbf k'}\rangle=(2\pi)^3P(k)\delta_D(\mathbf k+\mathbf k')$, inversion gives $\xi(\mathbf r)=\int d^3k\,P(k)e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}/(2\pi)^3$. [Isotropy](isotropy.md) reduces the angular integral to $4\pi j_0(kr)$, where $j_0(x)=\sin x/x$ is the order-zero [Spherical Bessel function](spherical-bessel-function.md). At zero separation this gives the [variance](variance-split.md) if the spectrum integral converges. A nonnegative spectrum can produce negative correlations away from the origin.
