# Cyclotomic local different exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Different exponent](different-exponent.md)

For $K_r=\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_{p^r})$, the [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\zeta_{p^r}-1$ has [different exponent](different-exponent.md) $p^{r-1}(r(p-1)-1)$. Differentiate the [cyclotomic polynomial](cyclotomic-polynomial.md) at $\zeta_{p^r}$: the numerator contributes $r p^{r-1}(p-1)$ to the [valuation](valuation.md), while the denominator $\zeta_p-1$ contributes $p^{r-1}$.
