# Rossby-gravity waves

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial wave](equatorial-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rossby-gravity_waves)

The Yanai wave is the $n=0$ branch of the [equatorial shallow-water dispersion relation](equatorial-shallow-water-dispersion-relation.md) with positive frequency:

$$
k=\frac\omega c-\frac\beta\omega,\qquad v\propto e^{-\beta y^2/(2c)}.
$$

Its [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $d\omega/dk=c\omega^2/(\omega^2+\beta c)>0$. At low frequency it has westward [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) but transports energy eastward; at high frequency its phase and group velocities approach $c$.
