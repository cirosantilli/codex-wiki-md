# Normal modes of two spring-coupled coaxial rotors

↑ **Parent:** [Small oscillation](small-oscillation.md)

For point masses on concentric horizontal rings in parallel planes, set $I_1=ma^2$, $I_2=MA^2$, and $\delta=\theta-\varphi$. A spring of zero natural length gives $V=\text{constant}-kaA\cos\delta$. The inertia-weighted angle $q=(I_1\theta+I_2\varphi)/(I_1+I_2)$ separates the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) into a free common rotor with inertia $I_1+I_2$ and a relative rotor with inertia $I_r=I_1I_2/(I_1+I_2)$. Static equilibria have $\delta_0=0$ or $\pi$. Their [normal modes](normal-mode.md) are the common displacement $(1,1)$ with zero frequency and the relative displacement $(I_2,-I_1)$ with the displayed squared frequency. The aligned family has a restoring relative mode; the antipodal family has an exponentially growing one. The zero mode is required by [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md). Small nonzero total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) produces common drift, so stability of the aligned family modulo rotations should be distinguished from [Lyapunov stability](lyapunov-stability.md) of one fixed equilibrium point.
