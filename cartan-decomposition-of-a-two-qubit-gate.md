# Cartan decomposition of a two-qubit gate

↑ **Parent:** [Two-qubit gate](two-qubit-gate.md)

Every [two-qubit gate](two-qubit-gate.md) has the displayed form with local elements of the [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) and real interaction parameters. The three Pauli-product generators commute, so the central exponential is the product of three interactions. Local basis changes turn an [Ising coupling](ising-coupling-of-two-qubits.md) into either of the other Pauli-product couplings. A fixed entangling controlled phase together with arbitrary local rotations can also synthesize each variable interaction, for example through two [CNOT gates](controlled-not-gate.md) enclosing a variable $z$ rotation.
