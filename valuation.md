# Valuation

↑ **Parent:** [Field](field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valuation_(algebra))

A valuation assigns sizes to elements of a [field](field.md) in a way compatible with multiplication and addition. In additive notation, $v(xy)=v(x)+v(y)$ and $v(x+y)\geq\min\{v(x),v(y)\}$.
