# Ionic strength

↑ **Parent:** [Solution (chemistry)](solution-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ionic_strength)

The concentration-based [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md) is $I_c=\frac12\sum_i c_i z_i^2$, where $c_i$ is each dissolved [ion](ion.md)'s [concentration](concentration.md) and $z_i$ its signed [electric charge](electric-charge.md) number. It has the same units as $c_i$; some formulas instead use the [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) ratio $I_c/c^\circ$. All dissolved [ions](ion.md) contribute, including those that balance the [electric charge](electric-charge.md) of a [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md)'s [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md). [Ionic strength](ionic-strength.md) helps describe the interactions represented by [activity coefficients](activity-coefficient.md). See [Hippler and Metcalfe, activity coefficients and dimensionless ionic strength](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/167121/1/BM_5_2020_Unterricht_Hippler_Metcalfe.pdf).
