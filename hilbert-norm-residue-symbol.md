# Hilbert norm residue symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Local Artin reciprocity](local-artin-reciprocity.md)

For a [local field](local-field.md) $F$ containing $\mu_m$, the symbol is $\operatorname{rec}_F(b)(a^{1/m})/a^{1/m}\in\mu_m$, after fixing a convention for [Local Artin reciprocity](local-artin-reciprocity.md). It is independent of the chosen root and bilinear. Its kernel in the second variable is the norm subgroup of $F(a^{1/m})/F$. The [quadratic Hilbert symbol](quadratic-hilbert-symbol.md) is the sign-valued case relevant to quadratic forms.
