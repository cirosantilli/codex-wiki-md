# Riemann vanishing theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Theta divisor](theta-divisor.md)

For the period matrix of a genus-$g$ [compact Riemann surface](compact-riemann-surface.md), there is a vector $\kappa$ depending on the marking and base point such that the [Riemann theta function](riemann-theta-function.md) vanishes exactly on this translate of the image of the [Abelian sum map](abel-map-of-an-algebraic-curve.md). Pairing sides of the cut surface proves that a nonzero pullback $\theta(u(p)-z)$ has a degree-$g$ zero [divisor](divisor.md) whose Abel sum is $z+\kappa$. At a theta zero that pullback includes the base point. Irreducibility and dimension of the effective-divisor locus give equality of supports; the primitive [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md) of the theta [line bundle](line-bundle.md) makes the [divisor](divisor.md) reduced.
