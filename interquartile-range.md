# Interquartile range

↑ **Parent:** [Quartile](quartile.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interquartile_range)

The [interquartile range](interquartile-range.md) measures the width between the lower and upper [quartiles](quartile.md), in the same units as the observations. It describes central spread rather than the distance between the observed extremes. In our toy [box plot](box-plot.md), $\mathrm{IQR}=6.25-2.75=3.5$; moving the largest value from $20$ to $200$ would leave the [quartiles](quartile.md) and [interquartile range](interquartile-range.md) unchanged under the chosen rule, because the [order statistics](order-statistic.md) used in their interpolation would not change.
