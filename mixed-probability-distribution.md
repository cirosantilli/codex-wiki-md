# Mixed probability distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

A [mixed probability distribution](mixed-probability-distribution.md) combines a [discrete probability distribution](discrete-probability-distribution-split.md) with a [continuous probability distribution](continuous-probability-distribution-split.md). For example, put probability $1/4$ at zero and probability $3/4$ into a [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(0,1)$. Its [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is $0$ for $x<0$, $1/4+3x/4$ for $0\leq x<1$, and $1$ for $x\geq1$. The jump at zero and the continuous increase both contribute; neither an ordinary [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) alone nor a [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) alone describes the whole law.
