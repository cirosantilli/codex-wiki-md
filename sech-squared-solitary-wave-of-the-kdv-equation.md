# Sech-squared solitary wave of the KdV equation

↑ **Parent:** [Korteweg-De Vries equation](korteweg-de-vries-equation.md)

For the sign convention $u_t-6uu_x+u_{xxx}=0$ and any $\kappa>0$, the displayed negative [solitary wave](solitary-wave.md) travels at [speed](speed.md) $4\kappa^2$. To verify it, put $\xi=x-ct$ and write $u=U(\xi)$. The equation becomes $(-cU-3U^2+U'')'=0$. A decaying profile has zero integration constant, so $U''=cU+3U^2$. Direct differentiation of the displayed profile gives $U''=4\kappa^2U+3U^2$, proving the result. Its depth is $2\kappa^2$, so narrower and deeper pulses travel faster. Changing the sign of the nonlinear term reverses the amplitude sign. This wave belongs to the [soliton](soliton.md) family of the [Korteweg-De Vries equation](korteweg-de-vries-equation.md).
