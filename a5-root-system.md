# A5 root system

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

The [root system](root-system.md) of the complex [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sl}_6(\mathbb C)$ is $\{e_i-e_j:1\le i\ne j\le6\}$ in $\sum_ix_i=0$. Its [simple roots](simple-root.md) $e_i-e_{i+1}$ give a five-node [An Dynkin diagram](an-dynkin-diagram.md). The defining [fundamental representation](fundamental-representation.md) has six [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) $w_i=e_i-\tfrac16\sum_je_j$. The [dual representation](dual-representation.md) has [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) $-w_i$, and the [two-index antisymmetric representation](two-index-antisymmetric-representation.md) has fifteen [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) $w_i+w_j$, $i<j$, all with [weight multiplicity](weight-multiplicity.md) one.
