# Bare mass

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

The [bare mass](bare-mass.md) is the mass parameter in a regulated field-theory action before a [renormalization condition](renormalization-condition.md) relates it to a measured mass. For a scalar, loop [self-energy](self-energy.md) corrections change the propagator denominator to $p^2-m_0^2-\Sigma(p^2)$. The [pole mass](pole-mass.md) solves $m^2-m_0^2-\Sigma(m^2)=0$ for a stable particle. The [bare mass](bare-mass.md) generally depends on the regulator when the [pole mass](pole-mass.md) is held fixed.
