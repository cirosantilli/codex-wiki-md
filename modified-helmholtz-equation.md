# Modified Helmholtz equation

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)

The modified Helmholtz equation is

$$
\Delta u-m^2u=0.
$$

It reduces to the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md) when $m=0$.
