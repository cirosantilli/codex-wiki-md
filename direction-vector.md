# Direction vector

↑ **Parent:** [Straight line](straight-line.md)

A nonzero [vector](vector.md) $d$ is a direction vector of a [line](straight-line.md) if the [line](straight-line.md) has a parametrization $r=p+td$, $t\in\mathbb R$, for some point $p$. Replacing $d$ by a nonzero [scalar multiple](scalar-multiple.md) describes the same [line](straight-line.md); dividing by its [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) gives a [unit vector](unit-vector.md) in one of the two orientations. A [cross product](cross-product.md) of two nonparallel [normal vectors](normal-vector.md) supplies a direction vector for the intersection of their [planes](plane.md).
