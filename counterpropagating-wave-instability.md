# Counterpropagating wave instability

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

Waves travelling in opposite directions relative to their local background currents can share a laboratory [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md). Their interaction can then lock their phases and extract energy from the mean shear, producing growing disturbances. In a layered flow, the coupling is often exponentially weak when the interface separation exceeds the waves' decay length. A [dispersion relation for two density interfaces in uniform shear](dispersion-relation-for-two-density-interfaces-in-uniform-shear.md) gives a simple explicit example.
