# Jacobi identity

↑ **Parent:** [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_identity)

The Jacobi identity is

$$
[x,[y,z]]+[y,[z,x]]+[z,[x,y]]=0.
$$
