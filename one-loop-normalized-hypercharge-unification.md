# One-loop normalized hypercharge unification

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge coupling unification](gauge-coupling-unification.md)

The normalization of an Abelian [gauge coupling](gauge-coupling.md) matters in testing [gauge coupling unification](gauge-coupling-unification.md). If $\mu\,dg_i/d\mu=b_ig_i^3$, then $\alpha_i^{-1}(\mu)=\alpha_i^{-1}(\mu_0)-8\pi b_i\log(\mu/\mu_0)$. For normalized hypercharge $\alpha_Y^{\mathrm{GUT}}=(5/3)\alpha_Y$, the inverse slope is $(3/5)b_Y$. Subtracting pairs of inverse couplings at the common scale and eliminating its logarithm gives a low-energy consistency relation. Division by the difference of two slopes requires that difference to be nonzero; a scale above $\mu_0$ additionally requires a positive inferred logarithm.
