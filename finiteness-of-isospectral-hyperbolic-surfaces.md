# Finiteness of isospectral hyperbolic surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md)

For a fixed closed [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md), there are only finitely many [isometry](isometry.md) classes with its [Laplacian](laplacian.md) spectrum. The [Selberg trace formula](selberg-trace-formula.md) gives a common discrete [length spectrum](length-spectrum.md) and common positive [hyperbolic systole](hyperbolic-systole.md). [Mumford's compactness theorem](mumford-s-compactness-theorem.md) confines this family to a compact subset of [moduli space](moduli-space.md). If distinct members accumulated, choose nearby markings at an accumulation point and use [finite length coordinates for hyperbolic surfaces](finite-length-coordinates-for-hyperbolic-surfaces.md). Each of these finitely many lengths converges while remaining in one discrete spectrum, so it is eventually constant. The length coordinates then force eventual equality, a contradiction. This finiteness theorem is weaker than the [Wolpert generic spectral rigidity theorem](wolpert-generic-spectral-rigidity-theorem.md).
