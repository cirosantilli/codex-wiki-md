# Cauchy exit law from a Brownian half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion](conformal-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md)

Planar [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at $(-x,0)$ exits the left half-plane with ordinate distributed as $x$ times a standard [Cauchy random variable](cauchy-random-variable.md). Conditional on the independent horizontal hitting time, the ordinate is normal with variance equal to that time. The [inverse-square Gaussian law of Brownian first passage](inverse-square-gaussian-law-of-brownian-first-passage.md) turns this into a ratio of independent standard normals. Its exit density is $x/(\pi(x^2+y^2))$.
