# Precessing orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit (dynamical system)](orbit-dynamical-system.md)

A bound [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) whose direction of closest approach changes between radial periods. For integrable central motion, closure requires a rational ratio of radial and azimuthal frequencies.
