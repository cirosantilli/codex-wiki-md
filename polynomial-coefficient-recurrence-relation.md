# Polynomial-coefficient recurrence relation

↑ **Parent:** [Recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md)

A [linear recurrence](linear-recurrence-relation.md) $\sum_{j=0}^r p_j(n)a_{n+j}=0$ whose coefficients $p_j(n)$ are [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) in the index. A sequence satisfying such a relation is [P-recursive](p-recursive-sequence.md).
