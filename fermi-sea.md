# Fermi sea

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi_sea)

The Fermi sea is the zero-temperature many-[fermion](fermion.md) [quantum state](quantum-state.md) with every one-particle state below the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) occupied and every state above it empty. For a uniform isotropic [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md), the occupied region in [momentum space](momentum-space.md) is a solid [sphere](sphere.md); its boundary is the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md). These are [momenta](momentum-split.md), not positions: filling the [sphere](sphere.md) does not put the particles in a ball in ordinary space. Opposite [momentum](momentum-split.md) contributions cancel, so the gas at rest has zero total [momentum](momentum-split.md) even though its total [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is positive.

The filled states are forced upward in [energy](energy-split.md) by the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md). This is different from the [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) of one particle confined by a boundary. At fixed [number density](number-density.md), the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) remains positive as the box grows to the [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md), while the lowest box confinement [energy](energy-split.md) tends to zero. Low-[energy](energy-split.md) excitations move particles from occupied states near the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) into nearby empty states; deep states have no nearby available destination. This explains why only a small fraction of [electrons](electron.md) contribute to the low-[temperature](temperature-split.md) [electron heat capacity](electron-heat-capacity.md).[Statistical Physics by David Tong; §§3.6.2–3.6.3, filled sea and thermally active surface shell](statistical-physics-by-david-tong/3-quantum-gases.md)
