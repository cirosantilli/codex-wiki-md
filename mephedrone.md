# Mephedrone

↑ **Parent:** [Psychoactive drug](psychoactive-drug.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mephedrone)

Mephedrone is 4-methylmethcathinone, a synthetic stimulant [psychoactive drug](psychoactive-drug.md). The chemical identity is described in [the EUDA synthetic-cathinone profile](https://www.euda.europa.eu/publications/drug-profiles/synthetic-cathinones_en). In [causal inference](causal-inference-split.md), an association between increased use and fewer deaths involving another drug does not itself establish substitution: changes in use, population composition and ascertainment must be distinguished.
