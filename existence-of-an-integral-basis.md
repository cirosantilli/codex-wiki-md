# Existence of an integral basis

↑ **Parent:** [Integral basis](integral-basis.md)

Every degree-$n$ [number field](number-field.md) has a [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) that is a [free abelian group](free-abelian-group.md) of rank $n$. To prove this without assuming finite generation, scale a rational [basis](basis.md) to [algebraic integers](algebraic-integer.md) $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n$ and set $M=\sum_j\mathbb Z\alpha_j$. Their [trace pairing](trace-pairing.md) has an invertible [integer](integer-split.md) [matrix](matrix.md) $T$, since the [trace form of a number field](trace-form-of-a-number-field.md) is nondegenerate. For $x\in\mathcal O_K$, all $\operatorname{Tr}(x\alpha_j)$ are [integers](integer-split.md). The [adjugate matrix](adjugate-matrix.md) formula gives $M\subseteq\mathcal O_K\subseteq(\det T)^{-1}M$. The [submodule theorem for free modules over a principal ideal domain](submodule-theorem-for-free-modules-over-a-principal-ideal-domain.md) makes the middle group free of rank $n$, and a group [basis](basis.md) is an [integral basis](integral-basis.md).
