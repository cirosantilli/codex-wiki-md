# K1 of a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic K-theory](algebraic-k-theory.md)

Stable invertible [matrices](matrix.md), with stabilization $g\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(g,1)$, form the [stable general linear group](stable-general-linear-group.md). Its elementary subgroup is its [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md), and the displayed quotient is its [abelianization](abelianization.md). [Elementary matrices](elementary-matrix.md) lift along any surjective [ring](ring.md) map, even when arbitrary invertible [matrices](matrix.md) do not lift. Over a [field](field.md), [determinant](determinant.md) identifies $K_1$ with its multiplicative unit [group](group-split.md).
