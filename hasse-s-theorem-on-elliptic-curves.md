↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius isogeny of an elliptic curve](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse's_theorem_on_elliptic_curves)

For an elliptic curve over $\mathbb F_q$,

$$
\left|\#E(\mathbb F_q)-(q+1)\right|\leq2\sqrt q.
$$
