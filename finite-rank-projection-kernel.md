# Finite-rank projection kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Integral kernel](integral-kernel.md)

An [orthonormal set](orthonormal-set.md) $\phi_0,\ldots,\phi_{m-1}$ in an $L^2$ space defines the [integral kernel](integral-kernel.md) of the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto its span by this sum. It satisfies $\int K(x,y)K(y,z)\,d\mu(y)=K(x,z)$ and $\int K(x,x)\,d\mu(x)=m$. Its evaluation [matrices](matrix.md) are positive semidefinite Gram [matrices](matrix.md).
