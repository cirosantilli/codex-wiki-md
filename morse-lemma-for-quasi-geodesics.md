# Morse lemma for quasi-geodesics

↑ **Parent:** [Quasigeodesic](quasigeodesic.md)

In a Gromov-hyperbolic geodesic metric space, every quasigeodesic segment stays within a uniformly bounded Hausdorff distance of a geodesic segment with the same endpoints. The bound depends only on the hyperbolicity and quasigeodesic constants.

The bounded-distance conclusion is a property of a [Gromov-hyperbolic metric space](hyperbolic-metric-space.md), under the geodesic and quasigeodesic hypotheses stated here.
