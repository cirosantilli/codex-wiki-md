# Far-field P and S radiation from a point force

↑ **Parent:** [Elastodynamic Green tensor](elastodynamic-green-tensor.md)

In a homogeneous [isotropic](isotropy.md) solid, the far-field [P wave](p-wave.md) is the radial projection of the force and the [S wave](s-wave.md) is its transverse projection. For a unit force axis $d$, the magnitudes are $|\cos\theta|$ and $\sin\theta$. The approximation requires distance large compared with both reduced wavelengths; angular nodes can still be controlled by a smaller near-field contribution.
