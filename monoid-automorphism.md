# Monoid automorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid](monoid.md)

A monoid automorphism is a bijective operation- and identity-preserving self-map of a [monoid](monoid.md). Its inverse is also a monoid homomorphism. Permuting [prime numbers](prime-number.md) and extending by [prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) gives all automorphisms of the multiplicative monoid of positive natural numbers; extending by $0\mapsto0$ gives automorphisms of the multiplicative monoid including zero.
