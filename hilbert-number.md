# Hilbert number

↑ **Parent:** [Integer](integer-split.md)

A Hilbert number is a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) congruent to $1$ modulo $4$. The Hilbert numbers form a multiplicative set: a product of two of them is again congruent to $1$ modulo $4$.
