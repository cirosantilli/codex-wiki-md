# Four-vertex obstruction to polychromatic three-colouring

↑ **Parent:** [Polychromatic hypergraph colouring](polychromatic-hypergraph-colouring.md)

Take every three-element subset of a four-element vertex set as a [hypergraph edge](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md). Any three-colouring repeats a colour on two vertices. A triple containing that pair cannot contain all three colours. Thus this [uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) has no polychromatic three-colouring, although each edge meets only three other edges.
