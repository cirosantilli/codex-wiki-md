# Angular Jacobian of a rational map

↑ **Parent:** [Rational map (complex analysis)](rational-map-complex-analysis.md)

For a nonconstant [rational map](rational-map-complex-analysis.md) between round unit [Riemann spheres](riemann-sphere.md), $R^*d\Omega=J_Rd\Omega$. Counting inverse images with multiplicity gives $\int J_Rd\Omega=4\pi\deg R$. The [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) vanishes at [ramification points of a holomorphic map](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md), but the integral still records the global [degree of a holomorphic map](degree-of-a-holomorphic-map.md).
