# Torus action

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group action](lie-group-action.md)

A smooth action of the compact abelian [Lie group](lie-group.md) $\mathbb R^n/(2\pi\mathbb Z)^n$. Commuting [vector fields](vector-field.md) with a common periodic normalization give such an action. In [action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md), the torus acts by translation of the angles while leaving the actions fixed.
