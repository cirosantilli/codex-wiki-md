# Minimal deterministic finite automaton

↑ **Parent:** [DFA minimization](dfa-minimization.md)

A [minimal deterministic finite automaton](minimal-deterministic-finite-automaton.md) is the result of [DFA minimization](dfa-minimization.md). An irreducible deterministic automaton has the fewest states among deterministic automata for its language and is unique up to isomorphism. Its states are the Myhill--Nerode classes.
