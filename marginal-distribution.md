# Marginal distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Joint probability distribution](joint-probability-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marginal_distribution)

A marginal distribution is the distribution of a selected coordinate or subvector of a [random vector](random-vector.md), obtained from the joint distribution by integrating or summing over the remaining coordinates.
