# Sverdrup

↑ **Parent:** [Volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sverdrup)

A unit of [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) used for [ocean circulation](ocean-circulation.md). Its symbol is $\mathrm{Sv}$; this denotes a volume transported per time, not a [velocity](velocity.md).
