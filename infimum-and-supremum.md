# Infimum and supremum

↑ **Parent:** [Real analysis](real-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infimum_and_supremum)

The [infimum](infimum.md) of a subset of an ordered set is its greatest lower bound; its [supremum](supremum.md) is its least upper bound. Neither bound must belong to the subset. For bounded nonempty subsets of the real numbers both bounds exist by [completeness of the real numbers](completeness-of-the-real-numbers.md).
