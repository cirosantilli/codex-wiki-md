# B2 spin crystal

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal basis](crystal-basis.md)

For the [B2 root system](b2-root-system.md) with $\alpha_1=\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2$ long and $\alpha_2=\varepsilon_2$ short, the spin [fundamental weight](fundamental-weight.md) is $\Lambda_2=(\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2)/2$. Its four-dimensional [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md) has the [minuscule weight](minuscule-weight.md) orbit $\tfrac12(\pm\varepsilon_1\pm\varepsilon_2)$, each of multiplicity one. The [crystal basis](crystal-basis.md) is the chain $++\xrightarrow{2}+-\xrightarrow{1}-+\xrightarrow{2}--$, where signs denote the coordinates. Each [Kashiwara operator](kashiwara-operator.md) subtracts its indicated simple root; the [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md) strings have length one. This fixes every edge and gives a connected highest-weight graph with the correct character.
