# Pythagorean theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagorean_theorem)

For a right triangle with perpendicular side lengths $a,b$ and hypotenuse $c$, the Pythagorean theorem states $a^2+b^2=c^2$.
