# Meta-regression

↑ **Parent:** [Between-study heterogeneity](between-study-heterogeneity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta-regression)

Meta-regression relates study effect estimates to study-level explanatory variables. A common [random-effects meta-analysis](random-effects-meta-analysis.md) extension is $y_i=\alpha+\beta x_i+u_i+\varepsilon_i$, where $\operatorname{Var}(u_i)=\tau^2$ and $\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_i)=v_i$. For [log odds ratios](log-odds-ratio.md), $e^\beta$ is a ratio of underlying odds ratios per unit of $x$. Few studies, correlated design changes and exploratory variable selection limit interpretation; a study-level association does not identify an individual-level [causal effect](causal-effect.md). The interpretation of study-level predictors is also discussed in [the Cochrane methods handbook, section 10.11](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-10).
