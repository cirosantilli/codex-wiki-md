# Rubbery plateau

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic modulus](dynamic-modulus.md)

The rubbery plateau is a frequency or time range in which a flexible polymer network has an approximately constant elastic [storage modulus](storage-modulus.md) dominated by chain entropy. A permanently crosslinked [elastomer](elastomer.md) retains a nonzero long-time modulus. An entangled uncrosslinked [polymer melt](polymer-melt.md) can have a transient plateau but ultimately reaches terminal fluid flow at sufficiently low frequency. The plateau therefore must be interpreted with the network lifetime and observation window specified.
