# Dominant weight

↑ **Parent:** [Weight lattice](weight-lattice.md)

A weight is dominant when all its [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) are nonnegative.

Dominance is a nonnegativity condition on a [Weight](weight-representation-theory.md); integrality additionally requires lattice membership.
