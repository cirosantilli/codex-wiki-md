# Density of continuous functions in Lp on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

For a finite interval $(a,b)$ with [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md), restrictions of [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) on $[a,b]$ form a [dense subspace](dense-subspace.md) of the [Lp space](lp-space.md) $L^p((a,b))$ for $1\leq p<\infty$. One proof first approximates by [simple functions](simple-function.md). The [regularity of Lebesgue measure](regularity-of-lebesgue-measure.md) approximates each of their [measurable sets](measurable-set.md) in measure by a finite union of intervals. An interval's [indicator function](indicator-function.md) is approximated by [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) taking values in $[0,1]$ and differing from that [indicator function](indicator-function.md) only in endpoint strips of total length tending to zero. Their [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) error is at most the $1/p$ power of that length. The [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) completes the approximation of each [simple function](simple-function.md).
