# Dialysis (biochemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Biochemistry](biochemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialysis_(biochemistry))

[Dialysis](dialysis-biochemistry.md) exchanges small [solutes](solute.md) between a sample and an external bath through a [semipermeable membrane](semipermeable-membrane.md), while retaining suitably large [macromolecules](macromolecule.md), such as [proteins](protein-split.md). The transport is passive [diffusion](diffusion.md): random motion carries permeant [molecules](molecule-split.md) both ways, with net transport down their [concentration](concentration.md) [gradient](gradient.md). The bath is the [dialysate](dialysate.md). At equal permeant [solute](solute.md) [concentrations](concentration.md), microscopic exchange continues but net exchange stops.[Dialysis: an overview, Tech Tip 20, p. 1, Dialysis is Diffusion Through a Semi-Permeable Membrane](dialysis-an-overview-pierce-biotechnology-technical-note.md)

For example, a [protein](protein-split.md) sample containing excess [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) can lose the small [ions](ion.md) to a low-[salt](salt-chemistry.md) [dialysate](dialysate.md) without deliberately transferring the [protein](protein-split.md). Conversely, components of a new [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md) can enter from the bath. This is useful before studying [enzyme kinetics](enzyme-kinetics.md): changing the surrounding [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) can remove an interfering small [solute](solute.md) while keeping the [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) available for the next experiment.

**[Figure 1](#image-small-salt-ions-exchange-across-a-semipermeable-membrane-while-a-protein-is-retained). Small salt ions exchange across a semipermeable membrane while a protein is retained**. Original schematic of a sample bag immersed in a [dialysate](dialysate.md), with an enlarged membrane region. Arrows indicate exchange in both directions and an initially outward net [diffusive flux](diffusive-flux.md). Symbols and openings are not to scale; the drawn gap is not a literal [molecular weight cut-off](molecular-weight-cut-off.md) measurement. Replacing the bath removes escaped [salt](salt-chemistry.md) and renews the [concentration](concentration.md) [gradient](gradient.md).

The striking feature is that the retained [protein](protein-split.md) does not have to be captured and released again to change its surroundings. However, retention is conditional on the [semipermeable membrane](semipermeable-membrane.md) and the particular [macromolecule](macromolecule.md), and [dialysis](dialysis-biochemistry.md) is poorly suited to separating [proteins](protein-split.md) of nearly the same size. The [molecular weight cut-off](molecular-weight-cut-off.md) is a nominal retention rating, not a sharp geometrical boundary.
