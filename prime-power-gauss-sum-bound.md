# Prime-power Gauss sum bound

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character](gauss-sum-of-a-dirichlet-character.md)

For a character of modulus $p^k$, a [primitive Dirichlet character](primitive-dirichlet-character.md) attains the bound by finite [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md). An imprimitive character with $k\ge2$ has periodic values modulo $p^{k-1}$, so its frequency-one sum vanishes. The [principal Dirichlet character](principal-dirichlet-character.md) modulo $p$ has unnormalized sum $-1$. The sign convention of the exponential does not affect its magnitude.
