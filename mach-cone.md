# Mach cone

↑ **Parent:** [Mach angle](mach-angle.md)

The [Mach cone](mach-cone.md) forms the [geometric envelope](envelope-mathematics.md) of [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) [wavefronts](wavefront.md) from a source moving at a [supersonic](supersonic-flow.md) [Mach number](mach-number.md) $M>1$. In the source [reference frame](reference-frame.md) the ray-[velocity](velocity.md) [sphere](sphere.md) is centered at $-Mc_0\mathbf e_x$ and has radius $c_0$. Its tangent rays make [angle](angle.md) $\sin^{-1}(1/M)$ to the backward flight direction; all other emitted acoustic rays lie inside that [Mach cone](mach-cone.md).

The [Mach cone](mach-cone.md) is the [geometric envelope](envelope-mathematics.md) of the [spheres](sphere.md) in [moving-point acoustic wavefronts](moving-point-acoustic-wavefronts.md). To obtain its axial boundary without a diagram, put $s=-\tau>0$ and impose the [geometric envelope](envelope-mathematics.md) conditions $F=0$ and $\partial_sF=0$ on

$$
F(x,y,s)=(x+Us)^2+y^2-c^2s^2.
$$

The [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md) condition gives $x=-(U^2-c^2)s/U$. Substituting into $F=0$ gives $y^2=c^2(U^2-c^2)s^2/U^2$, hence

$$
y=\pm\frac{-x}{\sqrt{M^2-1}},\quad x<0,
\qquad
\sqrt{y^2+z^2}=\frac{-x}{\sqrt{M^2-1}}quad\text{in three dimensions}.
$$

For $M=2$, the [Mach cone](mach-cone.md) section has slope magnitude $1/\sqrt3$, consistent with a [Mach angle](mach-angle.md) of $30^\circ$. If emission begins at a finite [time](time-in-physics.md), the reached region is limited by the oldest [wavefront](wavefront.md); the infinite cone describes the steady geometric boundary obtained with an indefinitely long source history. In a uniformly moving [gas](gas.md), [advection](advection.md) shifts each emission's [sphere](sphere.md) by the gas [velocity](velocity.md) times its age; working with the source's [relative velocity](relative-velocity.md) through the [gas](gas.md) restores the same construction.
