# Mach cone

↑ **Parent:** [Mach angle](mach-angle.md)

The cone forming the envelope of sound wavefronts from a source moving supersonically at [Mach number](mach-number.md) $M>1$. In the source frame the ray-velocity sphere is centered at $-Mc_0\mathbf e_x$ and has radius $c_0$. Its tangent rays make angle $\sin^{-1}(1/M)$ to the backward flight direction; all other emitted acoustic rays lie inside that cone.
