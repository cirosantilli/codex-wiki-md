# Classification of complete positive constant-curvature manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md)

For a connected complete [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) of dimension $n\geq2$ and constant [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) $k>0$, its complete simply connected [universal cover](universal-cover.md) is the round [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $k^{-1/2}$. This is the constant-curvature case of [Cartan-Ambrose-Hicks theorem](cartan-ambrose-hicks-theorem.md). Thus the manifold is a quotient by a finite group of [isometries](isometry.md) acting freely. In even dimension, the determinant embeds this group in $\{1,-1\}$: every determinant-one orthogonal transformation in odd ambient dimension has eigenvalue one and therefore a fixed point. The only possible nonidentity element is the antipodal involution, giving [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md). In odd dimensions, free cyclic scalar actions on complex unit spheres instead produce [lens spaces](lens-space.md).
