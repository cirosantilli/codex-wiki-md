# Comoving radial distance

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal time](conformal-time.md)

Comoving radial distance uses the coordinate $\chi=\int dr/\sqrt{1-kr^2}$ and removes the radial curvature factor.

This radial coordinate is the comoving member of the [comoving and proper distances](comoving-and-proper-distances.md) comparison; multiplying by the scale factor gives proper radial distance at fixed cosmic time.
