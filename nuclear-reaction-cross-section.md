# Nuclear reaction cross-section

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md)

The effective [area](surface-area.md) for a specified [nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md) channel at given incident [energy](energy-split.md). Multiplying incident [particle flux](particle-flux.md), target count and this [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) gives an ideal event rate; target losses and [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md) require separate corrections.
