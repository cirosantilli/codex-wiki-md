# Norms of a rank-one operator

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-one operator](rank-one-operator.md)

For the [rank-one operator](rank-one-operator.md) $Tf=\langle f,v\rangle u$ on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) with a countable [Hilbertian basis](hilbertian-basis.md) $(e_k)$, the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) and [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md) both equal $\|u\|\|v\|$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\|Tf\|\leq\|u\|\|v\|\|f\|$, with equality at $f=v/\|v\|$ when $v\ne0$. The [Parseval identity for a Hilbertian basis](parseval-identity-for-a-hilbertian-basis.md) gives $\sum_k\|Te_k\|^2=\|u\|^2\sum_k|\langle e_k,v\rangle|^2=\|u\|^2\|v\|^2$. The zero-vector cases are immediate.
