# Same-resonance entrance-channel cross-section ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md)

Compare two entrance channels feeding the same isolated resonance and the same selected exit channel, each at its own resonance [energy](energy-split.md). The exit [partial decay width](partial-decay-width.md) and the resonant denominator cancel. Different entrance momenta, [spin](spin.md) weights and entrance widths remain. The formula assumes the same resonance, the stated unpolarized [spin](spin.md) average and negligible nonresonant [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md); a ratio at one shared laboratory [energy](energy-split.md) would generally be different.
