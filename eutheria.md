# Eutheria

↑ **Parent:** [Theria](theria.md)

The lineage comprising [placental mammals](placental-mammal.md) and extinct relatives closer to them than to [marsupials](marsupial.md). A fossil assignment to [Eutheria](eutheria.md) does not automatically assign it to the placental [crown group](crown-group.md).
