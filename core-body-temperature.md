# Core body temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoregulation](thermoregulation.md)

[Core body temperature](core-body-temperature.md) refers to internal [temperature](temperature.md), distinguished from [skin](skin.md) [temperature](temperature.md). Different measurement sites are proxies with their own limitations, not interchangeable readings of a perfectly uniform interior.
