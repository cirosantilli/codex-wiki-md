# Taylor-Couette flow

↑ **Parent:** [Couette flow](couette-flow.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor–Couette_flow)

[Taylor-Couette flow](taylor-couette-flow.md) is the flow between coaxial rotating cylinders. Its simplest steady homogeneous purely azimuthal viscous state has [steady circular Couette profile](steady-circular-couette-profile.md) $v_\phi=Ar+B/r$. Stability of that base state depends on rotation rates, geometry and viscosity; the inviscid [Rayleigh centrifugal stability criterion](rayleigh-centrifugal-stability-criterion.md) addresses axisymmetric centrifugal disturbances, not every viscous instability.
