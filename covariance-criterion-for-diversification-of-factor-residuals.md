# Covariance criterion for diversification of factor residuals

↑ **Parent:** [Arbitrage pricing theory](arbitrage-pricing-theory.md)

For centered residual [random variables](random-variable-split.md) $\epsilon_i$ with [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $\Gamma$, the residual of a [portfolio](investment-portfolio.md) has [variance](variance-split.md) $w^T\Gamma w$. Pairwise uncorrelated residuals with uniformly bounded [variances](variance-split.md) and weights $|w_i|\le W/n$ give $\operatorname{Var}(w^T\epsilon)\le s^2W^2/n$, hence convergence to zero in [mean-square convergence](convergence-in-l2.md). For nonnegative weights summing to one, the sharper bound is $s^2W/n$. With correlated residuals, $|w_i|\le W/n$ and $\sum_{i,j}|\Gamma_{ij}|=o(n^2)$ also suffice. Individual bounded [variances](variance-split.md) alone do not: if every residual equals the same nondegenerate centered $Z$, every fully invested [portfolio](investment-portfolio.md) retains residual $Z$. A one-sided upper bound on signed weights is insufficient even for uncorrelated residuals because it allows a fixed large [short selling](short-finance.md).
