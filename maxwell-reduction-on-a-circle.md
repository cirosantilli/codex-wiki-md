# Maxwell reduction on a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein theory](kaluza-klein-theory.md)

A real five-dimensional [Maxwell field](electromagnetic-field.md) on a circle of radius $r$ has a [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) with coefficients $A_M^{(n)}$, $A_M^{(-n)}=(A_M^{(n)})^*$. Its mixed field-strength component is $F_{\mu5}^{(n)}=\partial_\mu A_5^{(n)}-inA_\mu^{(n)}/r$. The nonzero modes transform as $\delta A_\mu^{(n)}=\partial_\mu\Lambda^{(n)}$, $\delta A_5^{(n)}=in\Lambda^{(n)}/r$. Thus $A_5^{(n)}$ is a [Stueckelberg mechanism](stueckelberg-mechanism.md) field and the gauge-invariant massive vector is $A_\mu^{(n)}-r\partial_\mu A_5^{(n)}/(in)$. Its mass is $|n|/r$ and it has three physical polarizations. The zero modes instead consist of a massless four-dimensional [Maxwell field](electromagnetic-field.md) and a real massless [scalar field](scalar-field.md); the latter cannot be removed by a periodic infinitesimal [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md). A positive Fourier level and its negative level are conjugate, not independent real fields.
