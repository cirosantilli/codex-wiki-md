# Scalar interactions after complete SU2 breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Complete breaking by an SU2 scalar doublet](complete-breaking-by-an-su2-scalar-doublet.md)

In [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md), $\phi=(0,(v+h)/\sqrt2)^T$ gives $\frac{g^2}{8}(v+h)^2A_\mu^aA^{a\mu}$. With potential $\frac{\lambda}{2}(\phi^\dagger\phi-v^2/2)^2$, the physical scalar has $m_h^2=\lambda v^2$ and interactions $-\lambda vh^3/2-\lambda h^4/8$. This normalization matters when extracting both masses and identical-leg [Feynman vertices](interaction-vertex.md).
