# Wave steepness

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)

The dimensionless product of surface-wave amplitude and [wavenumber](wavenumber.md), $ka$, measures orbital displacement relative to the wavelength. Small [wave steepness](wave-steepness.md) permits a [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md) of the velocity sampled by moving parcels. For a [deep-water gravity wave](deep-water-gravity-wave.md), orbital amplitudes and the local steepness decrease exponentially with depth.
