# Orbits in a self-gravitating isothermal slab

↑ **Parent:** [Self-gravitating isothermal slab](self-gravitating-isothermal-slab.md)

For a [self-gravitating isothermal slab](self-gravitating-isothermal-slab.md) with $\rho=\rho_0\operatorname{sech}^2(z/H)$ and $H^2=\sigma^2/(2\pi G\rho_0)$, [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives the displayed [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md). A test particle has constant in-plane velocity and conserved vertical energy $E_z=\dot z^2/2+\Phi(z)-\Phi(0)$. Its vertical motion is periodic for every finite $E_z>0$, with turning height $H\operatorname{arcosh}\exp[E_z/(2\sigma^2)]$. Near the plane it is a [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) of squared frequency $4\pi G\rho_0$; at large height the force tends to the constant $2\pi G\Sigma$, where $\Sigma=2\rho_0H$. The ideal infinite slab has no finite vertical escape energy.
