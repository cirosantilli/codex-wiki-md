# Future-null spinor factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian matrix representation of Minkowski four-vectors](hermitian-matrix-representation-of-minkowski-four-vectors.md)

A nonzero Hermitian rank-one [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) has zero [determinant](determinant.md) and positive [trace](matrix-trace.md), representing a future-directed [null vector](null-vector.md). It factors as $\chi\chi^\dagger$, with an irrelevant [spinor](spinor.md) phase. A past-directed [null vector](null-vector.md) instead requires a minus sign; a positive outer product alone does not cover both [time](time-in-physics.md) directions.
