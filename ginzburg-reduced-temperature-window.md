# Ginzburg reduced-temperature window

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg criterion](ginzburg-criterion.md)

For dimensionless scalar [Landau-Ginzburg theory](landau-ginzburg-theory.md) with quadratic coefficient $r=a_t t$, [gradient](gradient.md) coefficient $c>0$ and quartic term $u\phi^4/4$, the [correlation-volume derivation of the scalar Ginzburg ratio](correlation-volume-derivation-of-the-scalar-ginzburg-ratio.md) gives $\mathcal G\sim u c^{-d/2}a_t^{(d-4)/2}|t|^{-(4-d)/2}$. For $d<4$, solving $\mathcal G\sim1$ gives the displayed nonuniversal window. Mean-field self-consistency requires $|t|\gg\mathrm{Gi}$ while still staying in the domain of the local expansion. In three dimensions $\mathrm{Gi}\sim u^2/(c^3a_t)$. Numerical prefactors depend on the averaging convention and microscopic normalization; no universal material value follows from dimensional power counting. At four dimensions use the [marginal Ginzburg criterion](marginal-ginzburg-criterion.md) instead of this singular exponent formula.
