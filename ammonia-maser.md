# Ammonia maser

↑ **Parent:** [Maser](maser-split.md)

An ammonia maser uses an [ammonia](ammonia.md) [molecular beam](molecular-beam.md), [maser state selection](maser-state-selection.md) and a [maser cavity](maser-cavity.md) to obtain amplification or sustained [microwave](microwave.md) [oscillation](oscillation.md). In the 1954 experiment by [James Power Gordon](james-power-gordon.md), [Herbert Jack Zeiger](herbert-jack-zeiger.md) and [Charles Hard Townes](charles-hard-townes.md), a quadrupolar static [electric field](electric-field-split.md) focused upper-state [molecules](molecule-split.md) toward the cavity and deflected lower-state [molecules](molecule-split.md) away. The reported $J=K=3$ [ammonia inversion transition](ammonia-inversion-transition.md) lies near $23.87\,\mathrm{GHz}$.[pp. 282–283, Fig. 1 and Fig. 2 caption; 23 870 Mc/sec](molecular-microwave-oscillator-and-new-hyperfine-structure-in-the-microwave-spectrum-of-nh3-by-james-power-gordon-and-others.md)
