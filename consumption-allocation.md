# Consumption allocation

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange economy](exchange-economy.md)

In an economy with a [measure space](measure-space.md) of consumers, a [consumption allocation](consumption-allocation.md) is a [measurable](measurability.md), [integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) assigning each consumer a feasible bundle. Exact aggregate feasibility means $\int_Af\,d\mu=\int_A\omega\,d\mu$.
