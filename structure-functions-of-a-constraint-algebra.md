# Structure functions of a constraint algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint algebra](constraint-algebra.md)

The coefficients $C_{ij}{}^k(q,p)$ in $\{\varphi_i,\varphi_j\}=C_{ij}{}^k\varphi_k$ are structure functions. A finite real span of [first-class constraints](first-class-constraint.md) is a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) when it closes with constant coefficients. General structure functions instead describe closure with phase-space-dependent coefficients, while still permitting [canonical gauge transformations](canonical-gauge-transformation.md).
