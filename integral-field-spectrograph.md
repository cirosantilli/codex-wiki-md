# Integral field spectrograph

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrograph](spectrograph.md)

An integral field spectrograph records a spectrum for each of many spatial elements across a two-dimensional field, producing a [spectral data cube](spectral-data-cube.md). Field reformatters include [lenslet arrays](lenslet-array.md), [optical fibers](optical-fiber.md) and [image slicers](image-slicer.md).
