# Constructible universe theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible universe](constructible-universe.md)

[ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) proves that the [constructible universe](constructible-universe.md) is an [inner model](inner-model.md) of [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) and satisfies the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md). Relativizing to $L$ yields the corresponding relative-consistency implication.
