# Nonlinear wavenumber

↑ **Parent:** [Standard perturbation theory in cosmology](standard-perturbation-theory-in-cosmology.md)

The nonlinear [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) is the inverse scale at which linear [density contrast](density-contrast.md) fluctuations become of order unity. For the one-dimensional convention $\langle\delta(k)\delta(k')\rangle=2\pi\delta^{(D)}(k+k')P_L(k)$, a usual estimate is $k_{\rm NL}P_L(k_{\rm NL})/\pi\sim1$. Loop momenta above this scale require the [effective field theory of large-scale structure](effective-field-theory-of-large-scale-structure.md) even when external modes are perturbative.
