# Fair sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Detection loophole](detection-loophole.md)

For a specified [Bell test](bell-test.md) statistic, [fair sampling](fair-sampling.md) assumes that conditioning on successful detections preserves the correlations needed to represent the intended ensemble. Constant detector efficiency in an independently specified loss model can ensure this; constant observed count rates alone do not establish independence from the hidden state. The [Polarization CHSH count model](polarization-chsh-count-model.md) explicitly assumes independent losses.
