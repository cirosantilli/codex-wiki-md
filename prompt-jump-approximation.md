# Prompt jump approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Point kinetics model](point-kinetics-model.md)

A timescale-separation approximation that eliminates the rapidly adjusting neutron amplitude in favour of the slower [delayed neutron precursor](delayed-neutron-precursor.md) reservoir. It describes behaviour after the short prompt transient and need not resolve the initial adjustment.
