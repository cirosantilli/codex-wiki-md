# Clomipramine

↑ **Parent:** [Psychopharmacology](psychopharmacology.md)

A tricyclic antidepressant with serotonin-reuptake-inhibiting action, studied in [obsessive-compulsive disorder](obsessive-compulsive-disorder.md). Comparative efficacy must be interpreted alongside tolerability and the treatment schedule; results for it do not automatically describe every [SSRI](selective-serotonin-reuptake-inhibitor.md).
