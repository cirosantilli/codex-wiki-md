# Commuting positive operators with equal squares are equal

↑ **Parent:** [Positive square root of an operator](positive-square-root-of-an-operator.md)

For bounded [positive operators](positive-operator.md) satisfying the hypotheses, $(S-T)(S+T)=0$, so $S-T$ vanishes on the closure of the range of $S+T$. The [kernel of a sum of positive operators](kernel-of-a-sum-of-positive-operators.md) identity shows that it also vanishes on $\ker(S+T)$. Self-adjointness and the [Hilbert projection theorem](hilbert-projection-theorem.md) decompose the whole [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) as that closed range plus that kernel. Thus $S-T=0$. This proves the commuting case of uniqueness of a positive square root without using a spectral representation.
