# Lowest-weight coupling of two angular momenta

↑ **Parent:** [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition](clebsch-gordan-decomposition.md)

Put $s=j_1+j_2$, with both $j_i>0$. At magnetic quantum number $-s+1$, let $|a\rangle$ raise only the first factor of the lowest product state and $|b\rangle$ raise only the second. Applying total $J_+$ to the lowest product gives the normalized $J=s$ combination $\sqrt{j_1/s}|a\rangle+\sqrt{j_2/s}|b\rangle$. Its orthogonal combination is annihilated by total $J_-$ and therefore has $J=s-1$. This obtains a useful [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) without a table.
