<h1 id="dade-s-embedding-theorem-for-monomial-groups">Dade's embedding theorem for monomial groups</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Monomial group](monomial-group.md)

Every finite [solvable group](solvable-group.md) is isomorphic to a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of a finite [monomial group](monomial-group.md). This is an embedding assertion and does not assert that every solvable group is monomial, nor that the embedding is normal.
