# Upper semicontinuity

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous function](continuous-function.md)

A real or extended-real [function](function-split.md) is [upper semicontinuous](upper-semicontinuity.md) when every strict sublevel set is open. Equivalently its negative is [lower semicontinuous](lower-semicontinuity.md). An arbitrary infimum of continuous [functions](function-split.md) is [upper semicontinuous](upper-semicontinuity.md), even when the infimum is over an uncountable family; its strict sublevel sets are unions of the corresponding open sublevel sets.
