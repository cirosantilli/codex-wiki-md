<h1 id="su-4-group">SU(4) group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md)

The four-by-four unitary [matrices](matrix.md) of [determinant](determinant.md) one form this [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md). Its [two-index antisymmetric representation](two-index-antisymmetric-representation.md) has complex dimension six and the [real structure of the SU4 exterior square](real-structure-of-the-su4-exterior-square.md) realizes it on a six-dimensional real space.
