# Chemical Treatise on Air and Fire by Carl Wilhelm Scheele

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry bibliography](chemistry-bibliography-split.md)

[https://web.lemoyne.edu/Giunta/scheele77.html](https://web.lemoyne.edu/Giunta/scheele77.html)  
[https://library.si.edu/digital-library/book/carlwilhelmsche00sche](https://library.si.edu/digital-library/book/carlwilhelmsche00sche)

Primary extracts in English and the original German edition. The numbered experimental sections distinguish fire-air from the remaining air and describe its preparation. Publication in 1777 does not date every experiment to that year.
