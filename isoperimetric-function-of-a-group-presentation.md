# Isoperimetric function of a group presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Dehn function](dehn-function.md)

A nondecreasing function bounding the area of every null word of length at most $n$ is an [isoperimetric function](isoperimetric-function-of-a-group-presentation.md) for a [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md). The [Dehn function](dehn-function.md) is the minimal such bound. A subrecursive bound means one dominated by a total computable function. Arbitrarily enlarged bounds are not invariants: even a [group presentation](group-presentation.md) with zero area function admits both linear and exponential upper bounds, which are not equivalent under the usual coarse comparison.
