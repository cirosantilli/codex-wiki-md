# Wiener-Hopf kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Wiener-Hopf equation](wiener-hopf-equation.md)

The scalar coefficient multiplying an unknown half-line transform in a [Wiener-Hopf equation](wiener-hopf-equation.md) is its kernel. This is a transform-domain [function](function-split.md), distinct from the [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of a [linear map](linear-map.md). Its analytic zeros, [poles](pole.md) and [winding number](winding-number.md) govern [Wiener-Hopf factorization](wiener-hopf-factorization.md). For mixed scalar Helmholtz half-plane diffraction a typical kernel is the outgoing vertical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $\sqrt{k^2-\alpha^2}$.
