# Squaring and zig-zag iteration yields bounded-degree expanders

↑ **Parent:** [Zig-zag product](zig-zag-product.md)

Choose $D=d^2$ and a fixed $d$-regular [graph](graph-split.md) $H$ on $D^2$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) with absolute nonconstant [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) at most $1/10$. Start with the loop-inclusive [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $G_0$ on $D$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and iterate $G_{t+1}=G_t^2\mathbin{\mathrm{zigzag}}H$. The degree stays $D$, [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) counts are $D^{2t+1}$, and $\rho_{t+1}\leq\rho_t^2+21/100$. Since $\rho_0=0$, all $\rho_t\leq1/2$. The spectral cut bound gives multiplicity-counted [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) expansion at least $D/4$. Removing loops and merging parallel [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) loses at most a factor $D$, producing simple [expander graphs](expander-graph.md) with maximum degree $D$ and [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) expansion at least $1/4$.
