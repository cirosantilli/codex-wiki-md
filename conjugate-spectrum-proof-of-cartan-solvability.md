# Conjugate-spectrum proof of Cartan solvability

↑ **Parent:** [Cartan solvability criterion](cartan-solvability-criterion.md)

For $x$ in the [derived algebra](derived-algebra.md) of a complex matrix [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $L$, define $y$ by multiplication by $\overline\lambda$ on each [generalized eigenspace](generalized-eigenspace.md) $V_\lambda$ of $x$. [Polynomial interpolation](polynomial-interpolation.md) and [adjoint compatibility of additive Jordan decomposition](adjoint-compatibility-of-additive-jordan-decomposition.md) make $\operatorname{ad}y$ a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) in $\operatorname{ad}x$ with zero constant term, so $[y,L]\subseteq[L,L]$. Trace orthogonality of $[L,L]$ and $L$ forces the displayed sum to vanish. Thus $x$ is a [nilpotent endomorphism](nilpotent-linear-map.md), and the [Engel theorem](engel-s-theorem.md) implies solvability. The auxiliary $y$ and the Jordan components are not required to lie in $L$.
