# Thermal exponent from a discrete renormalization map

↑ **Parent:** [Relevant direction of a fixed point](relevant-direction-of-a-fixed-point.md)

A discrete [renormalization group](renormalization-group.md) with length factor $b>1$ and positive relevant thermal multiplier $\lambda_t>1$ sends $t$ to $\lambda_t t$ and the [correlation length](correlation-length.md) to $\xi/b$. Combining this with $\xi\sim|t|^{-\nu}$ gives the displayed [correlation-length critical exponent](correlation-length-critical-exponent.md). The corresponding [scaling dimension](scaling-dimension.md) is $y_t=\log\lambda_t/\log b=1/\nu$. Relevance of discrete multipliers is determined by magnitude relative to one, unlike the sign criterion for continuous-flow [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md).
