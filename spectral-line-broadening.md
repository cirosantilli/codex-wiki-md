# Spectral line broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line](spectral-line.md)

The finite width of a [spectral line](spectral-line.md) can arise from finite optical coherence or a distribution of [resonance](resonance.md) frequencies. [lifetime broadening](lifetime-broadening.md) and impact-regime [pressure broadening](pressure-broadening.md) often give [Lorentzian line shapes](lorentzian-line-shape.md); [thermal Doppler broadening](thermal-doppler-broadening.md) gives a Gaussian component. The instrument response must also be included when interpreting a measured spectrum.
