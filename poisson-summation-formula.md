# Poisson summation formula

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_summation_formula)

For a sufficiently rapidly decreasing function $f$, the Poisson summation formula relates its samples to those of its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md):

$$
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}f(n)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}\widehat f(2\pi k)
$$

under the convention $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e^{-ix\xi}\,dx$.
