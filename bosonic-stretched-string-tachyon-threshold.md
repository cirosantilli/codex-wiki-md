# Bosonic stretched-string tachyon threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Open string stretched between D-branes](open-string-stretched-between-d-branes.md)

For two static parallel [D-branes](d-brane.md) separated transversely by $d$, the [open bosonic string mass spectrum](open-bosonic-string-mass-spectrum.md) becomes $M^2=d^2/(4\pi^2\alpha'^2)+(N-1)/\alpha'$. The classical stretching contribution is the square of the [string tension](string-tension.md) times the separation, while the oscillator vacuum contributes the intercept term. Thus the ground state is a [tachyon](tachyon.md) for $d<2\pi\sqrt{\alpha'}$, massless at equality, and massive above it. Its real rest energy, when stable, is $\sqrt{d^2/(4\pi^2\alpha'^2)-1/\alpha'}$; the vacuum correction belongs to energy squared and is not an additive fixed subtraction from the stretching energy. Angled branes change oscillator moding and require a different formula.
