# Kripke semantics

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kripke_semantics)

[Kripke semantics](kripke-semantics.md) interprets logical formulas at worlds related by an accessibility relation or an information order. Modal logics use accessibility to interpret modal operators; intuitionistic logics use persistent valuations and quantify over stronger worlds for implication.
