# Surface modes of a self-gravitating incompressible slab

↑ **Parent:** [Self-gravitating incompressible slab](self-gravitating-incompressible-slab.md)

For $q=kH$, the even and odd scalar-potential branches have $\omega_e^2=(2\pi G\Sigma/H)[q\tanh q-1/(1+\coth q)]$ and $\omega_o^2=(2\pi G\Sigma/H)[q\coth q-1/(1+\tanh q)]$. Even potential symmetry moves the surfaces oppositely; odd potential symmetry bends them together. Hydrostatic free-surface [pressure](pressure.md) gives the positive term and perturbed self-gravity the negative term.
