# Writhe

↑ **Parent:** [Link](link.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writhe)

For an oriented closed curve, geometric [writhe](writhe.md) is the signed crossing number averaged over generic viewing directions. For an individual [link diagram](link-diagram.md), [writhe of a link diagram](writhe-of-a-link-diagram.md) is its signed crossing sum. Both depend on geometry or projection rather than only the [link isotopy](link-isotopy.md) class.
