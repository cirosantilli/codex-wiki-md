# Integrand

↑ **Parent:** [Integral](integral-split.md)

In $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$, the [function](function-split.md) $f$ is the [integrand](integrand.md). It is distinct from the integration variable, the bounds and the resulting [integral](integral-split.md). In [contour integration](contour-integration.md), the transformed [integrand](integrand.md) includes the parametrization derivative.
