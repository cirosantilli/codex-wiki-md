# Finite-exponential Cole-Hopf solution

↑ **Parent:** [Cole-Hopf transformation](cole-hopf-transformation.md)

For the negative-flux [viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md) $q_Z-qq_\theta=\epsilon q_{\theta\theta}$, set $q=2\epsilon\partial_\theta\log\psi$. A sum of exponentials in the initial heat data evolves into $\psi=\sum_je^{A_j}$, giving the displayed [softmax function](softmax-function.md) average. Two terms produce a traveling tanh shock from the lower to the higher state, with speed minus their mean.
