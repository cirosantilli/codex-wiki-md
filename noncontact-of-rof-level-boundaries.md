# Noncontact of ROF level boundaries

↑ **Parent:** [ROF level-set formulation](rof-level-set-formulation.md)

For bounded [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) data and $s<t$, nested [ROF level-set formulation](rof-level-set-formulation.md) minimizing sets cannot share an interface of positive surface [measure](measure.md) away from data jumps. On regular graph patches, upward and downward [first variations](first-variation.md) use the [BV traces on a hypersurface](bv-trace-on-a-hypersurface.md). At contacts outside $J_f$ the forcing [BV trace on a hypersurface](bv-trace-on-a-hypersurface.md) agrees, whereas the two levels differ by $t-s$. Graph regularity and contact differentiation give the contradiction. Bounded forcing is a hypothesis of this argument; arbitrary unbounded $L^2\cap BV$ data are not covered by this statement.
