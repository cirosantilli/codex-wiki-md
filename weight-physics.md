# Weight (physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weight)

Here [gravitational weight](weight-physics.md) means the [force](force-split.md) on a body due to [gravity](gravity.md). In the Newtonian model, a body of [mass](mass.md) $m$ in a uniform [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $\mathbf g$ has [gravitational weight](weight-physics.md) $\mathbf W=m\mathbf g$, with magnitude $W=mg$. Its direction follows $\mathbf g$; a [buoyant force](buoyancy.md) is a separate contribution to the body's net [force](force-split.md). [OpenStax: mass and gravitational weight](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/5-4-mass-and-weight).
