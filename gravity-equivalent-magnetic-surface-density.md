# Gravity-equivalent magnetic surface density

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic scalar potential](magnetic-scalar-potential.md)

For an isolated razor-thin disk, rescale its exterior [magnetic scalar potential](magnetic-scalar-potential.md) to satisfy $\sqrt{4\pi G/\mu_0}\mathbf B=-\nabla\Phi_M$. Comparing the normal magnetic boundary derivative with $\partial_z\Phi=2\pi G\Sigma$ identifies the displayed equivalent signed [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md). Both exterior potentials satisfy [Laplace's equation](laplace-equation.md). The equivalent [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) is a representation of the magnetic boundary data, rather than physical negative mass.
