# Stem-supported Ramsey family need not be completely Ramsey

↑ **Parent:** [Completely Ramsey set](completely-ramsey-set.md)

Let E be the even [positive integers](positive-integer.md), and take a [finite-symmetric-difference parity colouring](finite-symmetric-difference-parity-colouring.md) $\varepsilon$ of $[E]^\omega$. The family $\{\{1\}\cup X:X\in[E]^\omega,\ \varepsilon(X)=0\}$ is Ramsey even under the every-ground-set convention, since every infinite ground set can be thinned to omit 1. It is not [completely Ramsey](completely-ramsey-set.md): inside $[\{1\},E]$, every infinite tail has two subfamilies of opposite parity. Its supporting [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) is closed and [nowhere dense](nowhere-dense-set.md) in the [ordinary topology on infinite subsets](ordinary-topology-on-infinite-subsets.md), but open in the [Ellentuck topology](ellentuck-topology.md).
