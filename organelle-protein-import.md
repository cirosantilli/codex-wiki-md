# Organelle protein import

↑ **Parent:** [Protein targeting](protein-targeting.md)

[Organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md) brings externally synthesized [proteins](protein-split.md) into an [organelle](organelle.md). Many [plastid](plastid.md) [proteins](protein-split.md) made from nuclear [genes](gene.md) carry targeting information recognized by machinery that transports them across the envelope membranes. This delivery enables nuclear [genes](gene.md) to supply functions inside a [plastid](plastid.md), but the presence of targeting information is not itself evidence that a [gene](gene.md) was acquired through [endosymbiotic gene transfer](endosymbiotic-gene-transfer.md). [“How to tame your symbiont”: targeting, membrane passage and host-derived proteins](origin-and-evolution-of-plastids-and-photosynthesis-in-eukaryotes-by-geoffrey-i-mcfadden.md)

For the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md), [cotranslational protein translocation](cotranslational-protein-translocation.md) illustrates how [organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md) can begin before a [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) is complete. Import into the [ER lumen](er-lumen.md) and insertion as a [transmembrane protein](transmembrane-protein.md) are different outcomes; neither alone establishes subsequent secretion from the [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md).
