# Organelle protein import

↑ **Parent:** [Protein targeting](protein-targeting.md)

[Organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md) brings externally synthesized [proteins](protein.md) into an [organelle](organelle.md). Many [plastid](plastid.md) [proteins](protein.md) made from nuclear [genes](gene.md) carry targeting information recognized by machinery that transports them across the envelope membranes. This delivery enables nuclear [genes](gene.md) to supply functions inside a [plastid](plastid.md), but the presence of targeting information is not itself evidence that a [gene](gene.md) was acquired through [endosymbiotic gene transfer](endosymbiotic-gene-transfer.md).
