# Pointwise order

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)

Given a [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) $B$ and a [set](set-split.md) $A$, the pointwise order on the [function space](function-space.md) of [functions](function-split.md) $A\to B$ compares their values at every argument. It is a [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md): [reflexivity](reflexive-relation.md) and [transitivity](transitive-relation.md) hold at every argument, and [antisymmetry](antisymmetry.md) follows because two [functions](function-split.md) equal at every argument are equal. Even if $B$ has a [total order](total-order.md), this [function space](function-space.md) need not: the two [functions](function-split.md) on $\{0,1\}$ with values $(0,1)$ and $(1,0)$ are incomparable when $B=\mathbb R$.
