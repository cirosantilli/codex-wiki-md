# Preferred longitude of an unknot

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert longitude](seifert-longitude.md)

The exterior of an [unknot](unknot.md) is a [solid torus](solid-torus.md). Inclusion of its boundary kills exactly the primitive [Seifert longitude](seifert-longitude.md), so every essential simple closed curve bounding a disk in that exterior is isotopic, as an unoriented curve, to that longitude. The bounding disk need not avoid other link components.
