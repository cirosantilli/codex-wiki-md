# Minimum Toomre parameter in an exponential flat-rotation disk

↑ **Parent:** [Toomre parameter](toomre-parameter.md)

For $\Omega=\Omega_0r_0/r$, [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $\Sigma=\Sigma_0e^{1-r/r_0}$ and constant [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) $a_0$, the [Toomre parameter](toomre-parameter.md) is $Q(x)=Q_0e^{x-1}/x$, where $x=r/r_0$ and $Q_0=\sqrt2\Omega_0a_0/(\pi G\Sigma_0)$. Its [logarithmic derivative](logarithmic-derivative.md) is $1-1/x$, so the minimum is $Q_0$ at $r_0$. The formal inner and outer limits both diverge; if $Q_0<1$, local axisymmetric instability occupies a bounded intermediate annulus. A central departure from the assumed flat [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) law can change the inner conclusion.
