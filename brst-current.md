# BRST current

↑ **Parent:** [BRST operator](brst-operator.md)

The BRST current is the conserved worldsheet current whose contour integral is the [BRST operator](brst-operator.md). For the bosonic string it is built from the reparameterization ghost and the matter and ghost stress tensors; its self-OPE is nonsingular only when the total central charge vanishes.
