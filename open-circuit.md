# Open circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)

An ideal [open circuit](open-circuit.md) permits no conduction [electric current](electric-current.md) through a terminal pair, corresponding to infinite load [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md). At the end of a lossless [transmission line](transmission-line.md), its voltage-wave [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) is $+1$ and the terminal current is zero. Real open terminals may retain parasitic [capacitance](capacitance.md) and radiation loss.
