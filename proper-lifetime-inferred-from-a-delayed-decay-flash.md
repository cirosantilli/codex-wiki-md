# Proper lifetime inferred from a delayed decay flash

↑ **Parent:** [Proper time](proper-time.md)

An inertial particle passes a stationary detector, then recedes at [speed](speed.md) $v=\beta c$ before decaying. The detector's reception time obeys $t_{\rm receive}=t_d+vt_d/c$. Dividing the decay time $t_d$ by its [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) gives the title formula for elapsed [proper time](proper-time.md). A reception event at the detector and a decay event at the particle are not interchangeable endpoints for a time-dilation calculation.
