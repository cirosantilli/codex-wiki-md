# Endomembrane system

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endomembrane_system)

The [endomembrane system](endomembrane-system.md) comprises functionally connected internal membranes such as the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md), [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md), [endosomes](endosome.md) and [lysosomes](lysosome.md), together with their transport carriers. Connections through carrier traffic differ from direct continuity of the membrane sheets.
