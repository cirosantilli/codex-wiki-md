# Periodic Toda lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable systems](integrable-systems-split.md)

The periodic Hamiltonian chain $H=\tfrac12\sum p_i^2+\sum e^{q_i-q_{i+1}}$, with cyclic indices, is a [Liouville integrable](integrable-hamiltonian-system.md) system. Exponentials of neighbouring coordinate differences lead to a [Lax pair](lax-pair.md) and conserved characteristic coefficients.
