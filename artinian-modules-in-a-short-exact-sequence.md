# Artinian modules in a short exact sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Artinian module](artinian-module.md)

For a [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) $0\to N\to M\to Q\to0$, the middle [module](module-mathematics.md) is [Artinian](artinian-ring.md) exactly when both outer [modules](module-mathematics.md) are [Artinian](artinian-ring.md). Submodules and quotients inherit the [descending chain condition](descending-chain-condition.md). Conversely, for a descending chain $M_i$, both $M_i\cap N$ and its image in $Q$ eventually stabilize. If $x\in M_i$, choose $y\in M_{i+1}$ with the same image in $Q$; then $x-y\in M_i\cap N=M_{i+1}\cap N$, giving $M_i=M_{i+1}$. Induction shows that finite direct sums of [Artinian modules](artinian-module.md) are [Artinian](artinian-ring.md).
