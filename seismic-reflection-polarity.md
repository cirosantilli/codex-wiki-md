# Seismic reflection polarity

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic reflection](seismic-reflection.md)

The sign of a reflected seismic [seismic wavelet](seismic-wavelet.md) relative to an incident or reference wave under a stated recording convention. A negative normal-incidence stress-amplitude [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) reverses that polarity.
