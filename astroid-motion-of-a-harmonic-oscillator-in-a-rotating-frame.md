# Astroid motion of a harmonic oscillator in a rotating frame

↑ **Parent:** [Equation of motion in a rotating frame](equation-of-motion-in-a-rotating-frame.md)

For an isotropic [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) with inertial frequency $2\omega$, viewed in a frame rotating at $\omega$, initial rotating coordinates $(1,0)$ and zero relative [velocity](velocity.md) produce the displayed [astroid](astroid.md) trajectory. The complex coordinate satisfies $\ddot\zeta+2i\omega\dot\zeta+3\omega^2\zeta=0$, with solution $\zeta=\tfrac34e^{i\omega t}+\tfrac14e^{-3i\omega t}$. The relative [speed](speed.md) is $(3|\omega|/2)|\sin2\omega t|$, attaining its first maximum at $\pi/(4|\omega|)$ for nonzero $\omega$.
