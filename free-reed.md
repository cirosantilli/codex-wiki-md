# Free reed

↑ **Parent:** [Free reed aerophone](free-reed-aerophone.md)

An elastic tongue fixed at one end and moving through a close-fitting opening without regularly striking its frame. An airflow can maintain oscillation by exchanging [energy](energy-split.md) with the reed's elastic motion. This mechanism is an analogy for some aspects of [phonation](phonation.md), not an anatomical identity.
