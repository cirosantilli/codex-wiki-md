# Strong law for submartingales with bounded increments

↑ **Parent:** [Doob decomposition theorem](doob-decomposition-theorem.md)

If a [submartingale](submartingale.md) has uniformly [bounded increments](bounded-increments.md), then $\liminf_n Y_n/n\geq0$ almost surely. In its [Doob decomposition in discrete time](doob-decomposition-theorem.md), the predictable compensator is nondecreasing, while the martingale part has [bounded increments](bounded-increments.md). The [strong law for martingales with bounded increments](strong-law-for-martingales-with-bounded-increments.md) makes the latter negligible on the linear scale.
