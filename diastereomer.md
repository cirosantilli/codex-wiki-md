# Diastereomer

↑ **Parent:** [Stereoisomer](stereoisomer.md)

[Diastereomers](diastereomer.md) are different [stereoisomers](stereoisomer.md) that are not [enantiomers](enantiomer.md). The [meso compound](meso-compound.md) form of [tartaric acid](tartaric-acid.md) is a [diastereomer](diastereomer.md) of either member of its [enantiomer](enantiomer.md) pair.
