# Geodesic circle

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic polar coordinates](geodesic-polar-coordinates.md)

For $r$ smaller than the normal-coordinate radius, the [geodesic circle](geodesic-circle.md) on a [Riemannian surface](riemannian-surface.md) consists of points at distance $r$ from $p$. The [exponential map](exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md) restricted to the radius-$r$ circle in $T_pS$ gives its smooth embedded parameterization. Outside the injectivity range a distance sphere need not be smooth.
