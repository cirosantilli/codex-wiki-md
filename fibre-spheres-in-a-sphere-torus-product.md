# Fibre spheres in a sphere-torus product

↑ **Parent:** [Gromov compactness for spheres](gromov-compactness-for-spheres.md)

In a product [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) $S^2(a)\times T^{2n-2}$, every [compatible almost complex structure](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md) admits a [J-holomorphic sphere](j-holomorphic-sphere.md) in class $F$ through each point. Here $S^2(a)$ has area $a$, and the [torus](torus.md) carries a constant [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md). All spherical classes are integer multiples of $F$, so a curve in class $F$ cannot split into positive-area nonconstant spheres. For the product [complex structure](complex-structure.md) the fibre through a point is unique and regular. Its one-point count modulo two is one; generic [transversality](transversality-of-a-map-to-a-submanifold.md) and the compact one-dimensional parameterized [moduli spaces](moduli-space.md) preserve this count. Approximation and [Gromov compactness for spheres](gromov-compactness-for-spheres.md) remove the genericity requirement. This gives the area-$a$ curve used in the proof of the [Gromov non-squeezing theorem](non-squeezing-theorem.md).
