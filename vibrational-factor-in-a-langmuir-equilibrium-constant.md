# Vibrational factor in a Langmuir equilibrium constant

↑ **Parent:** [Langmuir adsorption model](langmuir-adsorption-model.md)

Equating the site [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) with that of an internally structureless classical vapour gives $\theta=b_sp/(1+b_sp)$. The coefficient $b_s$ includes the bound vibrational partition factor, the translational [thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md), and the positive binding [Boltzmann factor](boltzmann-factor.md). It has inverse-pressure units. Half coverage occurs at $p=1/b_s$; an unspecified $z_s(T)$ prevents a numerical [pressure](pressure-split.md) scale, but not the functional isotherm.
