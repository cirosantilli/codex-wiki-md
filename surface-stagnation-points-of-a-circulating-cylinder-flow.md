# Surface stagnation points of a circulating cylinder flow

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow around a circular cylinder with circulation](potential-flow-around-a-circular-cylinder-with-circulation.md)

For [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) $w=U(z+a^2/z)-i\Gamma\log z/(2\pi)$, zeros of the [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) solve a quadratic. If $0<\gamma<1$, the displayed two roots are distinct and both have modulus $a$; positive [circulation](circulation-physics.md) places them above the centreline. Their polar angles satisfy $\sin\theta=\gamma$. At the threshold $\gamma=1$ they merge at the top of the cylinder. This follows also from the surface tangential velocity $-2U\sin\theta+\Gamma/(2\pi a)$. The shifted [stream function](stream-function.md) $U(r-a^2/r)\sin\theta-\Gamma\log(r/a)/(2\pi)$ is constant on the cylinder.
