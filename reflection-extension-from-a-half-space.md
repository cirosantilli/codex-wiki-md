# Reflection extension from a half-space

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev extension operator](sobolev-extension-operator.md)

For $U=(0,\infty)\times\mathbb R^{n-1}$, the formula $Eu(x_1,x')=u(|x_1|,x')$ defines a bounded [Sobolev extension operator](sobolev-extension-operator.md). Its weak normal derivative changes sign across the boundary, while its tangential weak derivatives are reflected unchanged.
