# Spreadsheet cell

↑ **Parent:** [Spreadsheet](spreadsheet.md)

An addressed entry of a [spreadsheet](spreadsheet.md), such as `A1`. Here it stores either a literal numerical [input](input-computer-science.md) or a [spreadsheet formula](spreadsheet-formula.md); its current value is distinct from the stored formula. A [cached cell value](cached-cell-value.md) records a previously computed value, not an instruction to compute it again.
