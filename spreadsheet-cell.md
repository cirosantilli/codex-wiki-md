# Spreadsheet cell

↑ **Parent:** [Spreadsheet](spreadsheet-split.md)

An addressed entry of a [spreadsheet](spreadsheet-split.md), such as `A1`. Here it stores either a literal numerical [input](input-computer-science.md) or a [spreadsheet formula](spreadsheet-formula.md); its current value is distinct from the stored formula. A [cached cell value](cached-cell-value.md) records a previously computed value, not an instruction to compute it again.

In a [spreadsheet tabular model](spreadsheet-tabular-model.md), `D2` can store `=C2*(1+$F$1)` while displaying `9`. The [spreadsheet cell reference](spreadsheet-cell-reference.md) `D2` identifies the location, the [spreadsheet formula](spreadsheet-formula.md) describes the calculation, and `9` is the value obtained from the present [inputs](input-computer-science.md). Keeping those three ideas distinct makes copying and checking a model much easier.
