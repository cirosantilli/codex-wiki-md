# SEC fractionation range

↑ **Parent:** [Size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md)

The [SEC fractionation range](sec-fractionation-range.md) has differential [SEC pore access](sec-pore-access.md). Larger excluded species share $V_e\simeq V_0$; fully included species share $V_e\simeq V_t$. Neither group can be size-ranked by elution. A broader pore distribution can trade separation per size increment for range.[GE Healthcare, Size Exclusion Chromatography: Principles and Methods, printed pp. 17–18, Figures 1.9–1.10: selectivity curves, fractionation and exclusion limits](size-exclusion-chromatography-principles-and-methods-by-ge-healthcare.md)
