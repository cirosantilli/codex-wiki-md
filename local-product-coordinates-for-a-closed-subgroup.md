# Local product coordinates for a closed subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-subgroup theorem](closed-subgroup-theorem.md)

Choose a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $\mathfrak m$ complementary to the [Lie algebra of a closed subgroup](lie-algebra-of-a-closed-subgroup.md) $H$. The differential of $F$ at $(0,0)$ is $(U,V)\mapsto U+V$, so the [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) makes $F$ a local [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md). Sufficiently small nonzero $U\in\mathfrak m$ cannot satisfy $\exp U\in H$: otherwise [limit directions of a closed subgroup](limit-directions-of-a-closed-subgroup.md) give a nonzero element of $\mathfrak m\cap\mathfrak h$. Consequently $H$ is locally exactly the coordinate slice $U=0$, proving that it is an [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md).
