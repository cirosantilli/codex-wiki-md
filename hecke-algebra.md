# Hecke algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Coxeter group](coxeter-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hecke_algebra)

A Hecke algebra is a deformation of the [group algebra](group-algebra.md) of a [Coxeter group](coxeter-group.md), obtained by deforming the quadratic relations for its simple generators while retaining the braid relations.
