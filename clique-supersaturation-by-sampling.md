# Clique supersaturation by sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Supersaturation](supersaturation.md)

Choose a fixed sample size $m$ for which the normalized [Turan theorem](turan-s-theorem.md) bound lies below the given density by a positive margin. The expected [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) count in a random $m$-vertex subset then forces a positive proportion of subsets to contain a forbidden-size [clique](clique-graph-theory.md). Each [clique](clique-graph-theory.md) belongs to a known number of subsets, so double counting gives a positive constant times $n^{r+1}$ [cliques](clique-graph-theory.md).
