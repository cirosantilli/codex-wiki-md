# Particle decay cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)

A [particle decay cascade](particle-decay-cascade.md) follows successive unstable daughters to longer-lived or stable particles. Probabilities of specified independent decay paths multiply their [branching fractions](branching-fraction.md). Waiting times are governed by each particle's [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md), with [time dilation](time-dilation.md) relating proper and laboratory times. A particle which appears stable on one observation timescale can be an intermediate on another.
