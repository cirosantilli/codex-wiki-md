# Outbred pedigree

↑ **Parent:** [Pedigree](pedigree.md)

For calculations of [identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md), an outbred pedigree has unrelated, noninbred [founders in a pedigree](founder-in-a-pedigree.md), and no additional ancestral relationships beyond those explicitly specified. Founder gene copies are treated as distinct ancestral copies even if they have [identity by state](identity-by-state.md).
