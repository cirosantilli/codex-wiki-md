# Plane model y plus x cubed plus xy cubed equals zero of the Klein quartic

↑ **Parent:** [Klein quartic](klein-quartic.md)

The [projective completion](projective-completion.md) of

$$
y+x^3+xy^3=0
$$

is the [smooth plane quartic](smooth-plane-quartic.md)

$$
X^3Z+XY^3+YZ^3=0.
$$

The coordinate relabelling $[A:B:C]=[Y:X:Z]$ turns this equation into the standard [Klein quartic](klein-quartic.md) equation $A^3B+B^3C+C^3A=0$.
