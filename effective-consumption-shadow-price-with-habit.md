# Effective consumption shadow price with habit

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicative habit utility](multiplicative-habit-utility.md)

When habit evolves by $dh=\lambda(c-h)dt$, increasing consumption both costs wealth and increases future habit. Consequently the consumption coefficient in the [Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation](hamilton-jacobi-bellman-equation.md) is $V_w-\lambda V_h$. A finite interior optimum requires this effective shadow price to be positive.
