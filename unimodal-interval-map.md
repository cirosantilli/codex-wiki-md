# Unimodal interval map

↑ **Parent:** [Interval map](interval-map.md)

A unimodal interval map is a [interval map](interval-map.md) that is increasing to one turning point and decreasing afterwards. For a differentiable map with a maximum at zero, a [critical point](critical-point.md) of even order $d$ means $f(x)=f(0)+c x^d+o(x^d)$ with $c<0$. The critical order distinguishes universality classes of [period-doubling cascades](period-doubling-cascade.md).
