# Inclusive fitness

↑ **Parent:** [Natural selection](natural-selection.md)

A framework combining an actor’s effects on its own reproduction with effects on others, weighted by appropriate [genetic relatedness](genetic-relatedness.md) and reproductive values. Coefficients and marginal effects depend on the model and population structure.
