# Strong-field limit of a two-spin exchange spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Two-spin exchange spectrum with unequal signed magnetic moments](two-spin-exchange-spectrum-with-unequal-signed-magnetic-moments.md)

If the differential Zeeman [energy](energy-split.md) $|w|=|\mu_0B(g_1-g_2)|$ greatly exceeds $|\Delta|$, the two mixed levels are $-\Delta/4\pm|w|/2+O(\Delta^2/|w|)$ and approach opposite-spin product states. Four energies are generically distinct. Equal signed moments have $w=0$, so that block remains singlet/triplet rather than being unmixed by a stronger field.
