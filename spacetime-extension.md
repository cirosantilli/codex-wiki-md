# Spacetime extension

↑ **Parent:** [Spacetime](spacetime.md)

A [spacetime extension](spacetime-extension.md) is an isometric embedding of a spacetime as a proper open subset of a larger connected spacetime with a nondegenerate Lorentzian metric. The allowed differentiability class must be specified: analytic, smooth and low-regularity extensions are different questions. Divergent curvature can obstruct a regular extension even when an incomplete geodesic has finite affine length.
