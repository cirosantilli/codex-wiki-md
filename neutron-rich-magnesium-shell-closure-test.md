# Neutron-rich magnesium shell-closure test

↑ **Parent:** [Empirical evidence for nuclear shells](empirical-evidence-for-nuclear-shells.md)

A 2013 [RIKEN](riken.md) experiment used a fast [calcium](calcium.md) beam on [beryllium](beryllium.md), selected radioactive [aluminium](aluminium.md) and [silicon](silicon.md) fragments, then struck [carbon](carbon.md) to produce [magnesium](magnesium.md) [isotopes](isotope.md) with $N=22,24,26$. Emitted [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md) supplied excitation [energies](energy-split.md); comparison across these [isotopes](isotope.md) supported deformation extending beyond the $N=20$ region toward $28$. This tests the limits of traditional [magic numbers](magic-number.md), not merely their arithmetic. The inferred shape depends on nuclear-structure interpretation of the [energy levels](energy-level.md), rather than direct imaging.[20 November 2013, beam preparation, gamma-ray readout and deformation inference](magic-numbers-disappear-and-expand-area-of-nuclear-deformation-by-riken.md)
