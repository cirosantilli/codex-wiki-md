# Spherical law of sines

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical trigonometry](spherical-trigonometry.md)

For a nondegenerate convex minor [spherical triangle](spherical-triangle.md), its [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md) $\tau=u\cdot(v\times w)$ satisfies $|\tau|=\sin b\sin c\sin A$, and cyclically. Dividing by $\sin a\sin b\sin c$ proves the [spherical law of sines](spherical-law-of-sines.md).
