# Morphisms from projective space to smaller projective space are constant

↑ **Parent:** [Projective space](projective-space-split.md)

A nonconstant morphism $\mathbb P^m\to\mathbb P^n$ with $m>n$ would have a positive-dimensional [fiber of a morphism](fiber-of-a-morphism.md) by the [fiber dimension theorem](fiber-dimension-theorem.md). A target hyperplane avoiding its image point meets the positive-dimensional projective image, so its pullback is a nonempty divisor in $\mathbb P^m$ disjoint from that fibre. Every positive-dimensional projective subvariety meets every hypersurface: on its affine cone, a homogeneous equation cuts a positive-dimensional zero set containing a nonzero cone point. This contradiction proves constancy.
