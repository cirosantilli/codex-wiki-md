# Glycerol

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glycerol)

[Glycerol](glycerol.md) has three [carbon](carbon.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) and three $\mathrm{OH}$ groups. It supplies the scaffold of [triglycerides](triglyceride.md) and [glycerophospholipids](glycerophospholipid.md), but is not itself a long [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) chain. See [Biology 2e: glycerol](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-3-lipids).
