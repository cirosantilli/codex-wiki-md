# Dense jumps do not prevent Riemann integrability

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann-integrable function](riemann-integrable-function.md)

A [left-continuous cumulative function of an atomic measure](left-continuous-cumulative-function-of-an-atomic-measure.md) with positive masses at a dense countable set has dense discontinuities. Nevertheless it is bounded and monotone on a compact interval, hence a [Riemann-integrable function](riemann-integrable-function.md). Finite step-function sums approximate it uniformly because the positive masses are summable. Thus dense discontinuities do not imply failure of the [Riemann integral](riemann-integral.md).
