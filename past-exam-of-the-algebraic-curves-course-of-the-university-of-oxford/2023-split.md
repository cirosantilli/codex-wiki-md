# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the algebraic curves course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part B [algebraic curve](../algebraic-curve.md) paper, sat on 6 June 2023. Source: [Oxford's released examination PDF, compilation pages 218–220; paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=218). The solutions use the paper's labels. Curves in Question 1 are understood to be [reduced plane curves](../reduced-plane-curve.md); the qualification concerning lines in 2(a), and the unnecessary restriction on the half-period values in 3(d), are discussed where they arise.
