<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose a nonzero [meromorphic differential on a Riemann surface](../../../../meromorphic-differential-on-a-riemann-surface.md) $\eta$ and write $K=(\eta)$. Multiplication defines a linear map

$$
\mathcal L(K)\longrightarrow\operatorname{HD}(C),\qquad f\longmapsto f\eta.
$$

It takes values in the [holomorphic differential forms](../../../../holomorphic-differential-form.md) because $(f\eta)=(f)+K\geq0$. It is injective since $\eta\ne0$. It is surjective because, for any [holomorphic differential form](../../../../holomorphic-differential-form.md) $\omega$, the ratio $\omega/\eta$ is a [meromorphic function](../../../../meromorphic-function.md) and has $(\omega/\eta)+K=(\omega)\geq0$. Thus this map identifies $\operatorname{HD}(C)$ with the [canonical Riemann-Roch space](../../../../canonical-riemann-roch-space.md).

Furthermore, $\mathcal L(0)=\mathbb C$: a [meromorphic function](../../../../meromorphic-function.md) with no [poles](../../../../pole.md) is [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the [compact Riemann surface](../../../../compact-riemann-surface.md) $C$, and hence constant by the [maximum modulus principle](../../../../maximum-modulus-principle.md). Applying the [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) with $D=0$ gives

$$
1-\ell(K)=1-g.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb C}\operatorname{HD}(C)=\ell(K)=g.}
$$
