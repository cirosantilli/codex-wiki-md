<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [divisor on an algebraic curve](../../../../divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) is a finite formal sum

$$
D=\sum_{p\in C}n_pp,\qquad n_p\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Its [degree of a divisor](../../../../degree-of-a-divisor.md) is $\deg D=\sum_pn_p$. It is an [effective divisor](../../../../effective-cartier-divisor.md), written $D\geq0$, when every $n_p\geq0$.

A [meromorphic differential on a Riemann surface](../../../../meromorphic-differential-on-a-riemann-surface.md) is a one-form represented in every local [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) coordinate $t$ by $a(t)\,dt$, where $a$ is a [meromorphic function](../../../../meromorphic-function.md). Under a coordinate change $t=t(u)$, its coefficient becomes $a(t(u))t'(u)$, so these expressions agree on overlapping charts. Since $t'(u)$ is a nonvanishing [holomorphic function](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) for a valid coordinate change, the order of a zero or [pole](../../../../pole.md) of the differential is independent of coordinate. Equivalently, for a fixed nonconstant [meromorphic function](../../../../meromorphic-function.md) $h$, any such differential can be written $f\,dh$ with $f$ meromorphic.

A [canonical divisor](../../../../canonical-divisor.md) is the [divisor on an algebraic curve](../../../../divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md)

$$
K=(\eta)=\sum_{p\in C}\operatorname{ord}_p(\eta)p
$$

of a nonzero [meromorphic differential on a Riemann surface](../../../../meromorphic-differential-on-a-riemann-surface.md) $\eta$. If $\eta'=f\eta$, then $(\eta')=(\eta)+(f)$, so all [canonical divisors](../../../../canonical-divisor.md) are related by [linear equivalence of divisors](../../../../linear-equivalence-of-divisors.md). Their degree is

$$
\boxed{\deg K=2g-2.}
$$

The [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) states that, for every [divisor on an algebraic curve](../../../../divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) $D$ and any [canonical divisor](../../../../canonical-divisor.md) $K$ on the nonsingular [projective curve](../../../../projective-curve.md) $C$ of [genus](../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$,

$$
\boxed{\ell(D)-\ell(K-D)=\deg D+1-g,}
\qquad
\ell(D)=\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal L(D).
$$

The term $\ell(K-D)$ measures the failure of the naive count of conditions on [meromorphic functions](../../../../meromorphic-function.md).
