# Completely prime filter

↑ **Parent:** [Complete Heyting algebra](complete-heyting-algebra.md)

An upward-closed subset of a [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) containing $1$, excluding $0$ and closed under finite meets is completely prime if membership of an arbitrary join implies membership of some summand. Its characteristic map is a [point of a frame](point-of-a-frame.md). Constructively the map takes values in the [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) of truth values.
