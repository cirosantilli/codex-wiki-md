# Stratospheric cooling by carbon dioxide

↑ **Parent:** [Stratosphere](stratosphere.md)

An increase of atmospheric [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) can cool the [stratosphere](stratosphere.md) through enhanced infrared cooling relative to non-infrared heating and through changes in the spectrum of upwelling radiation. A local emissivity-only explanation is incomplete because absorption and emission both change. The response depends on [ozone](ozone.md) solar heating, spectral opacity, altitude and adjustments of the layers below.[Abstract: window-grey model, blocking and indirect solar effects](why-co2-cools-the-middle-atmosphere-a-consolidating-model-perspective-by-helge-f-goessling-and-sebastian-bathiany.md)
