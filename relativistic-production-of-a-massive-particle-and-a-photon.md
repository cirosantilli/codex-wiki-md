# Relativistic production of a massive particle and a photon

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic two-body decay](relativistic-two-body-decay.md)

In the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md) with total energy $\mathcal E$, [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) for a final massive particle of rest mass $m>0$ and one [photon](photon.md) gives equal and opposite three-momenta. The [energy–momentum relation](energy-momentum-relation.md) and $|\mathbf p_\gamma|=E_\gamma/c$ give the displayed energies, and

$$
\frac{v_B}c=\frac{\mathcal E^2-m^2c^4}{\mathcal E^2+m^2c^4}.
$$

The photon has positive energy when $\mathcal E>mc^2$. Kinematics fixes the speed and the opposition of the two final directions, while an angular distribution requires information about the interaction.
