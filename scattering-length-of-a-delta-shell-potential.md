# Scattering length of a delta-shell potential

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering length from a partial-wave S-matrix](scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md)

The zero-energy limit of the [phase shift of a delta-shell potential](phase-shift-of-a-delta-shell-potential.md) gives the displayed [scattering length](scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md). It vanishes at zero coupling and has a pole at $g=-1$. For repulsion $g>0$, $0<a_0<R$; for weak attraction $-1<g<0$, $a_0<0$; for $g<-1$, $a_0>R$. Both infinite-coupling limits tend to $R$. The pole is the threshold for an S-wave [bound state](bound-state.md): writing its energy as $-\hbar^2\kappa^2/(2m)$ gives $g=-2\kappa R/[1-e^{-2\kappa R}]$, with threshold $g\to-1$ as $\kappa\downarrow0$. The [delta-shell Born amplitude](delta-shell-born-amplitude.md) agrees with the exact low-energy amplitude only in their common weak-coupling and low-energy regime, $|g|\ll1$ and $kR\ll1$, where both tend to $-gR$.
