# Three-dimensional torus

↑ **Parent:** [Torus](torus.md)

The product of three circles is a compact smooth three-manifold. Quotient coordinates allow constant commuting [vector fields](vector-field.md). A rank-two plane field can have dense immersed leaves, as in a [dense immersed cylinder in a three-dimensional torus](dense-immersed-cylinder-in-a-three-dimensional-torus.md).
