<h1 id="two-dimensional-thomas-fermi-condensate">Two-dimensional Thomas–Fermi condensate</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thomas–Fermi approximation for a condensate](thomas-fermi-approximation-for-a-condensate.md)

In a two-dimensional isotropic harmonic trap with repulsive effective coupling $g>0$, neglecting density-gradient [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) gives the displayed parabolic [number density](number-density.md). Normalization to $N$ particles yields $\mu=\sqrt{Ngm\omega^2/\pi}$, radius $R^2=2\mu/(m\omega^2)$ and central density $n_c=\mu/g$. The bulk approximation requires $\mu\gg\hbar\omega$, equivalently $m gN/(\pi\hbar^2)\gg1$. It fails near the outer edge and inside [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md) cores. The coupling must have two-dimensional units; an unreduced three-dimensional contact coupling cannot be inserted without a normalization convention.
