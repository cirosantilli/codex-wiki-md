# Diphoton invariant mass

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant mass](invariant-mass.md)

Adding two null [photon](photon.md) four-momenta gives the displayed [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md), with opening angle $\theta$ measured in the same frame as the energies. A nonzero [mass](mass.md) of the pair does not give either [photon](photon.md) a [rest mass](invariant-mass.md). At fixed energies it vanishes for parallel [photons](photon.md) and is greatest for opposite directions. A reconstructed [mass](mass.md) peak must be assessed with background, resolution and identification controls before assigning a parent particle.
