<h1 id="a/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Map each bounded sequence $a=(a_n)$ to the continuous function that takes value $a_n$ at the integer $n\geq1$ and is affine on every interval $[n,n+1]$. Convexity of absolute value gives $|F_a(t)|\leq\sup_n|a_n|$, and evaluation at integers gives equality of suprema. Thus $a\mapsto F_a$ is an [isometric embedding](../../../../../isometric-embedding.md) of the [l-infinity sequence space](../../../../../l-infinity-sequence-space.md) into $C^b([1,\infty))$.

The former is not [separable](../../../../../separable-topological-space.md) by the allowed fact. If the latter were [separable](../../../../../separable-topological-space.md), the subspace result just proved would make its isometric copy of $\ell_\infty$ [separable](../../../../../separable-topological-space.md), a contradiction. Hence

$$
\boxed{C^b([1,\infty))\text{ is not separable}.}
$$
