<h1 id="a/1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $1<p<\infty$, truncation makes the $\ell^p$ tail arbitrarily small, since $\sum_j|a_j|^p$ converges. Approximate the finite remaining coordinates by numbers in $\mathbb Q+i\mathbb Q$. The resulting countable set of [finitely supported sequences](../../../../../finitely-supported-sequence.md) is dense. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\ell^p\text{ is separable for }1<p<\infty.}
$$

The same construction also works for $p=1$, though that endpoint is not needed here.
