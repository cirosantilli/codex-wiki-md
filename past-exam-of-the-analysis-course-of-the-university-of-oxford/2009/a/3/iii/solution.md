<h1 id="a/3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The weighted right shift $T$ is injective, so zero is not an eigenvalue. If $Ta=\lambda a$ with $\lambda\ne0$, the first coordinate forces $a_1=0$ and subsequent coordinates force every $a_j=0$. Thus its [point spectrum](../../../../../point-spectrum.md) is empty.

For $m\geq1$,

$$
T^me_j=\frac{e_{j+m}}{j(j+1)\cdots(j+m-1)},\qquad\|T^m\|=\frac1{m!},
$$

since the largest column sum is attained at $j=1$. Therefore the [spectral radius formula](../../../../../spectral-radius-formula.md) gives $\nu(T)=\lim_m(m!)^{-1/m}=0$. The limit follows, for example, from $m!\geq(m/2)^{m/2}$ for large $m$. Zero is in the spectrum because the output first coordinate is always zero, so $T$ is not onto. Hence

$$
\boxed{p\sigma(T)=\varnothing,\qquad\sigma(T)=\{0\}.}
$$

This is a nonzero [quasinilpotent element](../../../../../quasinilpotent-element.md), specifically the [factorial weighted right shift](../../../../../factorial-weighted-right-shift.md).
