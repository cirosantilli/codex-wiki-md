<h1 id="b/6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) to the convergent expansion of $x$:

$$
\boxed{\langle x,y\rangle=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\langle x,e_n\rangle\langle e_n,y\rangle.}
$$

The series is absolutely convergent because the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) for scalar sequences and [Bessel's inequality](../../../../../../bessel-s-inequality.md) give

$$
\sum_n|\langle x,e_n\rangle\langle e_n,y\rangle|\leq\left(\sum_n|\langle x,e_n\rangle|^2\right)^{1/2}\left(\sum_n|\langle y,e_n\rangle|^2\right)^{1/2}\leq\|x\|\|y\|.
$$

This is the polarized [Parseval identity](../../../../../../parseval-identity.md).
