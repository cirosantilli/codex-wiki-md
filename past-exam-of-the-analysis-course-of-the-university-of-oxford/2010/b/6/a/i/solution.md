<h1 id="b/6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For finite subsets $F\subset\mathbb Z$, orthonormality gives

$$
\left\|\sum_{n\in F}\alpha_ne_n\right\|^2=\sum_{n\in F}|\alpha_n|^2.
$$

For $S_N=\sum_{|n|\leq N}\alpha_ne_n$, the squared [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) of $S_N-S_M$ is the corresponding finite tail sum. Since $\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}|\alpha_n|^2<\infty$, these tails tend to zero. Thus $(S_N)$ is a [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) in the complete [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) and converges.

The same estimate for arbitrary finite tails proves that the sum is unconditional, so **the bilateral orthogonal series converges in $X$ independently of its ordering**. This is the [Riesz-Fischer theorem for an orthonormal system](../../../../../../riesz-fischer-theorem-for-an-orthonormal-system.md).
