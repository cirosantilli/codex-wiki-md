<h1 id="b/6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $a_n=\langle x,e_n\rangle$, the finite [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) $P_Nx=\sum_{|n|\leq N}a_ne_n$ satisfies

$$
0\leq\|x-P_Nx\|^2=\|x\|^2-\sum_{|n|\leq N}|a_n|^2.
$$

This is [Bessel's inequality](../../../../../../bessel-s-inequality.md), so the coefficients are square summable. The preceding result gives a limit $s=\sum_na_ne_n$. For each fixed $k$, [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) gives $\langle x-s,e_k\rangle=0$. The [completeness](../../../../../../completeness.md) assumption on the [orthonormal system](../../../../../../orthonormal-sequence.md) forces $x-s=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{x=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\langle x,e_n\rangle e_n.}
$$

Taking squared norms also gives [Parseval's identity](../../../../../../parseval-identity.md), $\|x\|^2=\sum_n|\langle x,e_n\rangle|^2$.
