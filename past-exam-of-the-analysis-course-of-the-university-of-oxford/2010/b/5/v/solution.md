<h1 id="b/5/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For $x\in\ker(S+T)$,

$$
0=\langle(S+T)x,x\rangle=\langle Sx,x\rangle+\langle Tx,x\rangle.
$$

Both terms are nonnegative because $S,T$ are [positive operators](../../../../../positive-operator.md). Their sum is zero, so each vanishes. In particular,

$$
\boxed{\langle Tx,x\rangle=0.}
$$
