<h1 id="b/5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [bounded linear operator](../../../../../continuous-linear-operator.md) on a complex [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) is [self-adjoint](../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) if $T=T^*$, equivalently $\langle Tx,y\rangle=\langle x,Ty\rangle$ for every $x,y$.

If $Tx=0$, then $\langle x,Ty\rangle=\langle Tx,y\rangle=0$ for all $y$, so $x\in(\operatorname{Ran}T)^\perp$. Conversely, membership in that [orthogonal complement](../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) gives $\langle Tx,y\rangle=0$ for every $y$, and choosing $y=Tx$ yields $Tx=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\ker T=(\operatorname{Ran}T)^\perp.}
$$
