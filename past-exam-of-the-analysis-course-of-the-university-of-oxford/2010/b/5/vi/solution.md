<h1 id="b/5/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

By the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for a positive operator](../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality-for-a-positive-operator.md), for every $y$,

$$
|\langle Tx,y\rangle|^2\leq\langle Tx,x\rangle\langle Ty,y\rangle=0.
$$

Taking $y=Tx$ gives $Tx=0$, and $(S+T)x=0$ then gives $Sx=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\ker(S+T)=\ker S\cap\ker T.}
$$

The reverse inclusion is immediate. This is the [kernel of a sum of positive operators](../../../../../kernel-of-a-sum-of-positive-operators.md) identity.
