<h1 id="b/5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Self-adjointness and conjugate symmetry of the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) give

$$
\langle Tx,x\rangle=\langle x,Tx\rangle=\overline{\langle Tx,x\rangle}.
$$

A complex number equal to its conjugate is real, so **$\langle Tx,x\rangle\in\mathbb R$** for every $x$.
