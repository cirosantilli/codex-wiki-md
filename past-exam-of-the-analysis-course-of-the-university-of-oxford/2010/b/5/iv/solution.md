<h1 id="b/5/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Set $B(x,y)=\langle Tx,y\rangle$. It is a positive semidefinite [Hermitian form](../../../../../hermitian-form.md). Write $A=B(x,x)$, $C=B(y,y)$ and $c=B(x,y)$. For every complex $t$,

$$
0\leq B(x+ty,x+ty)=A+\bar t c+t\bar c+|t|^2C.
$$

If $C>0$, choosing $t=-c/C$ gives $A-|c|^2/C\geq0$. If $C=0$ and $c\ne0$, choosing $t=-sc$ for large positive $s$ makes the expression negative, a contradiction. Thus $c=0$ in that case. In all cases,

$$
\boxed{|\langle Tx,y\rangle|^2\leq\langle Tx,x\rangle\langle Ty,y\rangle.}
$$

This is the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for a positive operator](../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality-for-a-positive-operator.md).
