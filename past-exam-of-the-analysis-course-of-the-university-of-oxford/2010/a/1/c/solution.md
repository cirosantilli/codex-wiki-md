<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $D$ be the set of [finitely supported sequences](../../../../../finitely-supported-sequence.md) with rational coordinates. It is countable: it is the union over $N$ of copies of $\mathbb Q^N$. Given $\alpha\in\ell_{1,2}$ and $\varepsilon>0$, choose $N$ with $\sum_{j>N}j^2|\alpha_j|<\varepsilon/2$. Choose rational numbers $q_j$ for $1\leq j\leq N$ so that $\sum_{j=1}^Nj^2|q_j-\alpha_j|<\varepsilon/2$, and put $q_j=0$ thereafter. Then $q\in D$ and $\|q-\alpha\|_{1,2}<\varepsilon$. Thus **$D$ is countable and dense, so the space is [separable](../../../../../separable-topological-space.md)**.
