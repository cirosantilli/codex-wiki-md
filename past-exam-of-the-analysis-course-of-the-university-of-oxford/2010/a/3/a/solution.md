↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [spectrum of a bounded operator](../../../../../spectrum-of-a-bounded-operator.md) is $\sigma(T)=\{\lambda\in\mathbb C:T-\lambda I\text{ has no bounded inverse}\}$. The [point spectrum](../../../../../point-spectrum.md) consists of those $\lambda$ for which $\ker(T-\lambda I)\ne0$, namely the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). The [spectral radius](../../../../../spectral-radius.md) is $r(T)=\sup_{\lambda\in\sigma(T)}|\lambda|$.

If $\|T\|<1$, the [Neumann series](../../../../../neumann-series.md) $S=\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-T)^n$ converges in the [Banach space of bounded linear operators](../../../../../banach-space-of-bounded-linear-operators.md), since its term norms are bounded by a convergent [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md). Multiplying the finite sums on either side by $I+T$ gives $I-(-T)^{N+1}$, which tends in [operator norm](../../../../../operator-norm.md) to $I$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{(I+T)^{-1}=\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-T)^n.}
$$
