<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The normalization gives

$$
\widehat{e_n}(m)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}e^{i(n-m)t}\,dt=\delta_{nm}.
$$

Thus $\alpha_ne_n$ has exactly the coefficients prescribed for $Te_n$. Uniqueness yields

$$
\boxed{Te_n=\alpha_ne_n\quad(n\in\mathbb Z).}
$$

These are [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md), including the zero-[eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) cases.
