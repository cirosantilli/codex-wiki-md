<h1 id="4/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Since every $e_n$ is nonzero, boundedness gives

$$
|\alpha_n|\,\|e_n\|_\infty=\|Te_n\|_\infty
\leq\|T\|\,\|e_n\|_\infty.
$$

Cancel its [norm](../../../../../norm.md) to obtain $\boxed{\sup_{n\in\mathbb Z}|\alpha_n|\leq\|T\|}$. Thus **the multiplier sequence is bounded**.
