<h1 id="4/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Evaluation at zero is a [bounded linear functional](../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) for the uniform [norm](../../../../../norm.md). Define

$$
\boxed{\phi(f)=\sqrt{2\pi}\,(Tf)(0).}
$$

It is linear and satisfies $|\phi(f)|\leq\sqrt{2\pi}\|T\|\|f\|_\infty$. Since $e_n(0)=(2\pi)^{-1/2}$,

$$
\phi(e_n)=\sqrt{2\pi}\,\alpha_ne_n(0)=\alpha_n.
$$

The factor $\sqrt{2\pi}$ is required by the source's basis normalization.
