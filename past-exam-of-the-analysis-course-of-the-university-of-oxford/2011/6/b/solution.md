<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The four claims below show that $I+T$ has a bounded inverse and that the [Cayley transform of a bounded accretive operator](../../../../cayley-transform-of-a-bounded-accretive-operator.md) is contractive. For an arbitrary $z\in X$, write $z=(I+T)x$. Then

$$
\|Sz\|=\|(I-T)x\|\leq\|(I+T)x\|=\|z\|.
$$

Taking the supremum over the unit ball gives

$$
\boxed{S=(I-T)(I+T)^{-1},\qquad\|S\|\leq1.}
$$
