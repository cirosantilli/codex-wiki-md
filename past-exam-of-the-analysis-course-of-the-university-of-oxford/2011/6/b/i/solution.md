<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expanding both squared [norms](../../../../../norm.md) gives

$$
\|(I+T)x\|^2-\|(I-T)x\|^2
=4\operatorname{Re}\langle Tx,x\rangle\geq0.
$$

Both [norms](../../../../../norm.md) are nonnegative, so taking square roots proves $\boxed{\|(I+T)x\|\geq\|(I-T)x\|}$.
