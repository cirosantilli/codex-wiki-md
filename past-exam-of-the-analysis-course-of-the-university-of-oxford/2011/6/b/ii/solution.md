<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A separate expansion gives

$$
\|(I+T)x\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|Tx\|^2+2\operatorname{Re}\langle Tx,x\rangle
\geq\|x\|^2.
$$

Hence $\boxed{\|(I+T)x\|\geq\|x\|}$. In particular $I+T$ is injective and bounded below by one.
