↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Show that $c$ is a closed [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $\ell^\infty$. Suppose $a^{(j)}\in c$ and $\|a^{(j)}-a\|_\infty\to0$. Given $\varepsilon>0$, choose $j$ with uniform error below $\varepsilon/3$. Since $a^{(j)}$ converges, choose $N$ such that $|a_m^{(j)}-a_n^{(j)}|<\varepsilon/3$ for $m,n\geq N$. Then

$$
|a_m-a_n|\leq|a_m-a_m^{(j)}|+|a_m^{(j)}-a_n^{(j)}|+|a_n^{(j)}-a_n|<\varepsilon.
$$

Thus $(a_n)$ is a real [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) and converges, so $a\in c$. A closed [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of a [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md) is complete in the inherited [norm](../../../../../norm.md). Therefore $\boxed{(c,\|\cdot\|_\infty)\text{ is Banach}}$.
