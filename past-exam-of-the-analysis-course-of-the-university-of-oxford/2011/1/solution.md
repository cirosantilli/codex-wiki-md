A [normed vector space](../../../normed-vector-space-split.md) over $\mathbb R$ is a real [vector space](../../../vector-space-split.md) $X$ with a function $\|\cdot\|:X\to[0,\infty)$ satisfying

$$
\|x\|=0\iff x=0,\qquad\|ax\|=|a|\|x\|,
\qquad\|x+y\|\leq\|x\|+\|y\|.
$$

Its [distance](../../../distance.md) is $d(x,y)=\|x-y\|$. It is a [Banach space](../../../banach-space-split.md) if this [metric](../../../metric.md) is complete: **every [Cauchy sequence](../../../cauchy-sequence.md) converges to an element of $X$**.
