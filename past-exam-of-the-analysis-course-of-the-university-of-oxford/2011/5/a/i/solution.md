<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $d=\inf_{y\in Y}\|x-y\|$ and choose $y_n\in Y$ with $\|x-y_n\|\to d$. The [parallelogram law](../../../../../parallelogram-law.md) gives

$$
\|y_n-y_m\|^2
=2\|x-y_n\|^2+2\|x-y_m\|^2
-4\left\|x-\frac{y_n+y_m}{2}\right\|^2
\leq2\|x-y_n\|^2+2\|x-y_m\|^2-4d^2.
$$

The right side tends to zero. Thus $(y_n)$ is Cauchy. The closed subspace $Y$ of a [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) is complete, so $y_n\to y_0\in Y$. Continuity of the [norm](../../../../../norm.md) gives $\|x-y_0\|=d$, proving **the [distance](../../../../../distance.md) is attained**.
