<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $w=x-y_0$. For any $y\in Y$ and any real $t$, minimality gives

$$
\|w-ty\|^2-\|w\|^2=-2t\operatorname{Re}\langle w,y\rangle+t^2\|y\|^2\geq0.
$$

The derivative at zero must vanish, so $\operatorname{Re}\langle w,y\rangle=0$. In a complex [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), apply the same argument to $iy$ to get the imaginary part zero as well. In the real case the first conclusion is already enough. Hence

$$
\boxed{x-y_0\in Y^\perp.}
$$
