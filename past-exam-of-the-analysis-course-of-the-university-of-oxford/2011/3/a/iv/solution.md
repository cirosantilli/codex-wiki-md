<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The factorization

$$
T^n-\lambda^nI=(T-\lambda I)
\left(T^{n-1}+\lambda T^{n-2}+\cdots+\lambda^{n-1}I\right)
$$

has commuting factors because both are polynomials in $T$. If $\lambda^n\notin\sigma(T^n)$, their product is invertible. The preceding part would then make $T-\lambda I$ invertible, contradicting $\lambda\in\sigma(T)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\lambda\in\sigma(T)\Longrightarrow\lambda^n\in\sigma(T^n).}
$$

This proves the required inclusion without assuming the full [spectral mapping theorem](../../../../../spectral-mapping-theorem.md).
