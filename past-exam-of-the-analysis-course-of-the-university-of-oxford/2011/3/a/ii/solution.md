<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The bounded operators on a [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md) form a [Banach algebra](../../../../../banach-algebra-split.md) in the [operator norm](../../../../../operator-norm.md). If $\|T\|<1$, the [Neumann series](../../../../../neumann-series.md) $A=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}T^k$ converges absolutely in that [norm](../../../../../norm.md). Its [partial sums](../../../../../partial-sum.md) satisfy

$$
(I-T)\sum_{k=0}^NT^k=I-T^{N+1}
=\left(\sum_{k=0}^NT^k\right)(I-T).
$$

Taking limits proves $\boxed{(I-T)^{-1}=\sum_{k\geq0}T^k}$.

If $|\lambda|>\|T\|$, factor $\lambda I-T=\lambda(I-T/\lambda)$ and apply the same argument. Thus such $\lambda$ are in the [resolvent set](../../../../../resolvent-set-of-an-operator.md) and

$$
\boxed{\sigma(T)\subseteq\{\lambda:|\lambda|\leq\|T\|\}.}
$$
