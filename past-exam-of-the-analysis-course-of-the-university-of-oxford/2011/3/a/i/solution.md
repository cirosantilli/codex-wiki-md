↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [bounded linear operator](../../../../../continuous-linear-operator.md) on a complex [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md), its [spectrum](../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sigma(T)=\{\lambda\in\mathbb C:\lambda I-T
\text{ has no bounded two-sided inverse}\}.}
$$

The complement is the [resolvent set](../../../../../resolvent-set-of-an-operator.md). For a real space, one may use its complexification when speaking of the complex [spectrum](../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md); the algebraic arguments below apply over the underlying scalar field as well.
