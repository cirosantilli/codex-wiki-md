The [continuous dual space](../../../continuous-dual-space-split.md) $X'$ is the [vector space](../../../vector-space-split.md) of bounded scalar-valued [linear functionals](../../../linear-functional.md), with [operator norm](../../../operator-norm.md)

$$
\boxed{\|f\|=\sup_{\|x\|\leq1}|f(x)|.}
$$

This makes it a normed space, and in fact a [Banach space](../../../banach-space-split.md) whether or not $X$ is complete.

The [norm](../../../norm.md)-preserving [Hahn-Banach theorem](../../../hahn-banach-theorem.md) states that a [bounded linear functional](../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) $f_0$ on any [linear subspace](../../../vector-subspace.md) $M\subseteq X$ extends to a [bounded linear functional](../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) $f$ on all of $X$ with $f|_M=f_0$ and $\|f\|=\|f_0\|$. The subspace need not be closed. This holds over both the real and complex scalar fields, using the complex version when necessary.
