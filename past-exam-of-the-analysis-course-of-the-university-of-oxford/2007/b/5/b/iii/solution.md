<h1 id="b/5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Additivity applied to $u+(-u)=0$ gives $P(-u,v)=-P(u,v)$. Multiplication by $i$ preserves the [norm](../../../../../../norm.md), so

$$
B(iu,v)=-B(u,iv),\qquad B(iu,iv)=B(u,v).
$$

For example the first follows by multiplying $iu\pm v$ by $-i$ inside each [norm](../../../../../../norm.md). Consequently

$$
P(iu,v)=-B(u,iv)+iB(u,v)=iP(u,v).
$$

This proves **the two required identities**, with the convention of linearity in the first argument.
