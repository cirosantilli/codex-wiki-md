<h1 id="b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expand both squared [norms](../../../../../norm.md) using sesquilinearity and [conjugate symmetry](../../../../../conjugate-symmetry.md). Their mixed terms $\langle u,v\rangle+\langle v,u\rangle$ appear with opposite signs and cancel. Hence

$$
\boxed{\|u+v\|^2+\|u-v\|^2=2\|u\|^2+2\|v\|^2,}
$$

the [parallelogram identity](../../../../../parallelogram-law.md).
