# Forcing antichain

↑ **Parent:** [Forcing](forcing-split.md)

A [subset](subset.md) of a [forcing](forcing-split.md) order whose distinct members have no common stronger extension. A maximal [forcing antichain](forcing-antichain.md) has a compatible member for every condition. For [forcing](forcing-split.md) by nodes of a [set-theoretic tree](set-theoretic-tree.md), ordered by extension, this coincides with a [tree antichain](tree-antichain.md).
