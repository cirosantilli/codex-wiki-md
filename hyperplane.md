# Hyperplane

↑ **Parent:** [Vector subspace](vector-subspace.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperplane)

A hyperplane is a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) of codimension one. A projective hyperplane in $\mathbb P^n$ is the zero set of one nonzero homogeneous linear form.
