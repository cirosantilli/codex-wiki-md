# Conservation of etendue

↑ **Parent:** [Etendue](etendue.md)

Lossless reversible propagation preserves the [etendue](etendue.md) of the same bundle of [light rays](light-ray.md). Here the model uses stationary isotropic media, [specular reflection](specular-reflection.md) and [refraction](refraction.md), with every selected [light ray](light-ray.md) reaching the output and with no [optical gain](optical-gain.md) or [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md). An [aperture](aperture.md) that blocks some incident [light rays](light-ray.md) changes which bundle is counted; the full incident and transmitted [etendue](etendue.md) need not be equal. [Étendue, pp. 1–2, lossless-system and entrance-pupil restrictions; p. 3, Figure 1, preserved generalized phase-space element](etendue-photon-snacks-by-r-john-koshel.md)

The underlying [phase space](phase-space.md) has four coordinates. In a planar section with constant [refractive index](refractive-index.md), let $(x,y)$ locate a [light ray](light-ray.md) and set

$$
p_x=n\sin\theta\cos\phi,\qquad p_y=n\sin\theta\sin\phi.
$$

The [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) from [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md) gives

$$
dp_x\,dp_y=n^2\sin\theta\cos\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi
=n^2\cos\theta\,d\Omega,
\qquad dG=dx\,dy\,dp_x\,dp_y.
$$

Thus [etendue](etendue.md) is a four-dimensional volume, preserved by the optical counterpart of [Liouville theorem in Hamiltonian mechanics](liouville-s-theorem-hamiltonian.md). Individual position and direction extents may change, and correlations between them matter. The [Lagrange optical invariant](lagrange-optical-invariant.md) is a related [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md) invariant for pairs of [light rays](light-ray.md), rather than the full four-dimensional [measure](measure.md).
