# Magnetically charged conical pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Conical pendulum](conical-pendulum.md)

A conducting [conical pendulum](conical-pendulum.md) connected to a grounded pivot develops a motional [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $B\omega r^2/2$ between the rotation axis and a bob at radius $r$. If the bob dominates the [capacitance](capacitance.md), its [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) is $CB\omega r^2/2$. An additional uniform vertical [electric field](electric-field-split.md) changes both the force on that charge and, while the grounding connection remains, the background [electric potential](electric-potential.md) entering the charging relation. These effects must be treated consistently when calculating a first-order mechanical response.
