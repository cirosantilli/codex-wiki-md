# Moduli space

↑ **Parent:** [Geometry and topology](geometry-and-topology-split.md)

A space parametrizing isomorphism classes of geometric objects. Its topology and possible stabilizers are part of the definition; a [moduli space of Riemann surfaces](moduli-space-of-riemann-surfaces.md) forgets the marking retained by [Teichmüller space](teichmuller-space.md).
