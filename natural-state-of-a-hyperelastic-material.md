# Natural state of a hyperelastic material

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)

An unconstrained natural state is a stress-free homogeneous [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md). For $J>0$, its equivalent conditions are $DW(F)=0$ and $J^{-1}DW(F)F^T=0$. An interior differentiable minimum of the [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) is a natural state, although a stationary state need not minimize the energy. Constraints require allowing their reaction stress; the unconstrained equivalence must then be modified. Under strict [Baker-Ericksen inequalities](baker-ericksen-inequalities.md), an isotropic natural state is a dilation times a proper rotation.
