# Equality-pattern orbits of ordered pairs

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit of a group action](orbit-of-a-group-action.md)

The diagonal [group action](group-action.md) of the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) on ordered pairs has two [orbits of a group action](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) when $n\geq2$: equal pairs, of size $n$, and distinct pairs, of size $n(n-1)$. Equality is preserved by a [bijection](bijection.md), and a prescribed bijection on one or two distinct letters extends to a [permutation](permutation.md) of all letters. At $n=1$ only the equal-pair orbit remains.
