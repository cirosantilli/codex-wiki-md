# Brownian excursion in the upper half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md)

The excursion from $0$ to infinity is the [Doob h-transform](doob-h-transform.md) of killed [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) with $h(z)=\operatorname{Im}z$, using the entrance law at zero. Equivalently, its real part is a standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) and its independent imaginary part is a [Bessel process](bessel-process.md) of dimension three started at zero. This planar, infinite-duration excursion differs from the one-dimensional excursion on a finite time interval.
