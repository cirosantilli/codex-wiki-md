# Circumgalactic medium

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)

The [circumgalactic medium](circumgalactic-medium.md) is gas surrounding a galaxy's main stellar body and interstellar gas, typically associated with its halo. [accretion](accretion.md), outflow and recycling exchange matter through it. Its observational boundary and inferred [mass](mass.md) depend on the adopted halo convention and the gas phases sampled by absorption or emission.
