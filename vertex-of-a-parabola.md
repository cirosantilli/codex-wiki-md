# Vertex of a parabola

↑ **Parent:** [Parabola](parabola.md)

For a [parabola](parabola.md) described by the [function graph](graph-of-a-function.md) $y=a(x-h)^2+k$, with nonzero $a$ in the [real numbers](real-number.md), its [vertex of a parabola](vertex-of-a-parabola.md) is the [point](point-geometry.md) $(h,k)$. This point gives its [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) or [maximum](maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) value of $y$ in these coordinates.
