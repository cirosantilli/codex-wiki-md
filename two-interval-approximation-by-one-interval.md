# Two-interval approximation by one interval

↑ **Parent:** [VC dimension of interval indicators](vc-dimension-of-interval-indicators.md)

For a target positive [set](set-split.md) consisting of two disjoint intervals of total [probability](probability.md) $p$, choosing its heavier component has [misclassification risk](misclassification-risk.md) at most $p/2$. Choosing the spanning interval has [misclassification risk](misclassification-risk.md) at most $1-p$. The smaller bound is at most $1/3$. The constant is sharp: on three ordered atoms with equal masses and labels $1,0,1$, every single interval makes a mistake on at least one atom. Together with the [VC uniform convergence theorem](vc-uniform-convergence-theorem.md), this gives [weak PAC learning](weak-pac-learning.md) using an interval [hypothesis class](concept-class.md).
