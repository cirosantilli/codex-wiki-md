# Absolute error bound for a ratio estimator with a controlled denominator

↑ **Parent:** [Ratio estimator](ratio-estimator.md)

Set $G_n=U_n-\theta V_n$, and define the estimator on zero-denominator events as part of the model. On $V_n>\delta>0$, its error is at most $|G_n|/\delta$. Centering $G_n$ and applying the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives the displayed bound, where $r_n$ is the expected error on $V_n\le\delta$. This controls a [ratio estimator](ratio-estimator.md) without falsely assuming that its numerator and denominator are independent.
