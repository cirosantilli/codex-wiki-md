# Circle-plane relation under stereographic projection

↑ **Parent:** [Stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md)

For north-pole [stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md) $z=(X+iY)/(1-Z)$ on the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md), substitute $|z|^2=(1+Z)/(1-Z)$ into a planar circle equation. The displayed plane equation follows, and its intersection with the sphere is a [circle](circle.md). The plane contains the sphere's centre exactly when $r^2=1+|c|^2$, giving a [great circle](great-circle.md).
