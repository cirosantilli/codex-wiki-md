<h1 id="ito-integrand-of-a-finite-sum-of-gaussian-exponentials">Itô integrand of a finite sum of Gaussian exponentials</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Itô integral](ito-integral.md)

For deterministic [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) $h_j$ on a finite interval, their stochastic exponentials $M(h_j)$ are [square-integrable martingales](square-integrable-martingale.md) and satisfy $dM(h_j)=h_jM(h_j)dB$. Thus $F=\sum_jc_jM(h_j)_T$ has mean $\sum_jc_j$ and stochastic-integral representation with the displayed [integrand](integrand.md). [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) proves uniqueness up to time-probability [null sets](null-set.md) among square-integrable predictable integrands. It does not give pointwise uniqueness, since changing a value at one deterministic time changes no [integral](integral-split.md).
