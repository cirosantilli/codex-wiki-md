<h1 id="mixed-su-3-tensor-representation">Mixed SU(3) tensor representation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(3) group](su-3-group.md)

A mixed [tensor](tensor.md) has $k$ defining indices and $l$ dual indices. The [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) action applies $U$ to every upper index and $U^{-1}$ to every lower index. [Young symmetrizers](young-symmetrizer.md) select permutation types, while invariant [Kronecker deltas](kronecker-delta.md) take [traces](matrix-trace.md) and invariant alternating [tensors](tensor.md) convert an antisymmetric pair of defining indices into a dual index, or conversely. Repeated fundamental and dual [tensor](tensor.md) rules decompose it with multiplicities: $(p,q)\otimes(1,0)=(p+1,q)\oplus(p-1,q+1)\oplus(p,q-1)$ and the conjugate rule, omitting negative labels.
