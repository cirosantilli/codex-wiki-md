# Critical surface density for gravitational lensing

↑ **Parent:** [Lensing convergence](lensing-convergence.md)

The geometrical projected density scale converting a thin lens [surface mass density](projected-surface-mass-density.md) into convergence. Its value depends on the angular-diameter distances to lens and source, not only on lens matter.
