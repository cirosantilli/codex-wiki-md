# Brusselator periodic-orbit criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Brusselator](brusselator.md)

The spatially homogeneous Brusselator has its unique positive equilibrium at $(r,s/r)$. Its Jacobian there has determinant $r^2$ and trace $s-1-r^2$. If $s-1>r^2$, the equilibrium is a repeller inside the [Brusselator trapping region](brusselator-trapping-region.md); the [Poincaré-Bendixson theorem](poincare-bendixson-theorem.md) then supplies a periodic orbit.
