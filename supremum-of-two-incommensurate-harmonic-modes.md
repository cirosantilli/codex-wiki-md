# Supremum of two incommensurate harmonic modes

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiperiodic motion](quasiperiodic-motion.md)

For $x(t)=A\cos\omega_1t+B\cos\omega_2t$ with irrational [frequency](frequency.md) ratio and nonzero [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md), $|x|\le|A|+|B|$. The [phase](phase-waves.md) trajectory is dense on the two-torus, so this bound is a [supremum](supremum.md). The required exact [phase](phase-waves.md) alignment may never occur at finite time. Thus an exam's maximum-amplitude estimate can be a limiting bound rather than an attained displacement; finite observation intervals have smaller maxima.
