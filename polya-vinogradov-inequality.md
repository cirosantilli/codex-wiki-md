<h1 id="polya-vinogradov-inequality">Pólya–Vinogradov inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Character sum](character-sum.md)

For a primitive nonprincipal [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) modulo $q$, finite [Fourier inversion theorem](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) and the primitive Gauss magnitude give this bound uniformly in the interval. Bound the exponential sums by $C\|a/q\|^{-1}$ and sum the resulting [harmonic series](harmonic-series.md). The general Gauss identity follows from the prime-power identity by the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md).
