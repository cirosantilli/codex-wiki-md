# Contingent claim payoff

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent claim](contingent-claim.md)

The monetary amount delivered by a [contingent claim](contingent-claim.md) at its payment time, represented by a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) measurable with respect to the information then available. For a [European call option](european-call-option.md) it is $(S_T-K)^+$, and for a [European put option](european-put-option.md) it is $(K-S_T)^+$. A payoff is the delivered amount, whereas the claim is the contract and its current price is the cost of acquiring or replicating that contract.
