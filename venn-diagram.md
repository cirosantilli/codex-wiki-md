# Venn diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Set](set-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venn_diagram)

A Venn diagram represents [sets](set-split.md) by closed curves, with each region recording a possible pattern of membership. For two [sets](set-split.md), the regions mean “first only”, “both”, “second only”, and “neither”; the enclosing rectangle can represent the [universal set](universal-set.md). Shading regions displays [union](set-union.md), [intersection](set-intersection.md), [set difference](set-difference.md), or [complement](complement-of-a-set.md). In the numbered examples in [set union](set-union.md) and [complement](complement-of-a-set.md), positions encode membership, while curve shapes and region areas do not encode [cardinalities](cardinality.md).
