# Minimal submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian geometry](riemannian-geometry-split.md)

A minimal submanifold has vanishing [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) vector, equivalently zero first variation of volume for compactly supported normal variations. A [minimal surface](minimal-surface.md) is its two-dimensional case. A [calibrated submanifold](calibrated-submanifold.md) satisfies the stronger property of minimizing volume in its relative homology class.
