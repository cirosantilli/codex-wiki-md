# Kernel of a group homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md)

The [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is the group version of a [kernel](kernel-algebra.md). For $\varphi:G\to H$, it is

$$
\ker\varphi=\{g\in G:\varphi(g)=e_H\}.
$$

It is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of the domain: for $n$ in the [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md), $\varphi(gng^{-1})=\varphi(g)e_H\varphi(g)^{-1}=e_H$. The [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) describes inputs whose effect disappears after mapping. For reduction modulo three from $\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z$, it is $\{0,3\}$; for the [sign of a permutation](sign-of-a-permutation.md) on $S_3$, it is $A_3$. A [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) is [injective](injective-function.md) exactly when its [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) contains only the [identity element](identity-element.md).
