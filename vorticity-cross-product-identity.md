# Vorticity cross-product identity

↑ **Parent:** [Vorticity](vorticity.md)

For differentiable vector fields, the displayed identity relates [vorticity](vorticity.md) to the antisymmetric part of the [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md). Together with the [curl of a cross product](curl-of-a-cross-product.md), it converts the [adjoint linearized Navier-Stokes evolution](adjoint-linearized-navier-stokes-evolution.md) between component and rotational forms.
