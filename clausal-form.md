# Clausal form

↑ **Parent:** [First-order clause](first-order-clause.md)

A representation by a set of [first-order clauses](first-order-clause.md), interpreted as their [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md). General [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) can be transformed by [Skolemization](skolemization.md) and conversion to [conjunctive normal form](conjunctive-normal-form.md), preserving satisfiability in an expanded [first-order signature](first-order-signature.md).

In an expanded signature, [Skolemization](skolemization.md) preserves [equisatisfiability](equisatisfiability.md) rather than equivalence under arbitrary interpretations of the new function symbols.
