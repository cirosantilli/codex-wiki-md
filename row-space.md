# Row space

↑ **Parent:** [Row and column spaces](row-and-column-spaces.md)

The row space of a [matrix](matrix.md) is the [linear span](linear-span.md) of its rows. It is the [column space](column-space.md) of the [matrix transpose](transpose.md).
