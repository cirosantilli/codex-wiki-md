# Frobenius conjugacy class

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius automorphism](frobenius-automorphism.md)

In a finite [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) $L/K$, choose a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak P$ above an unramified $\mathfrak p$. The [Frobenius automorphism](frobenius-automorphism.md) at $\mathfrak P$ is characterized on its residue field by $x\mapsto x^{N\mathfrak p}$. Changing $\mathfrak P$ conjugates this element, so its [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) is well-defined. It is the identity class exactly at a [completely split prime](completely-split-prime.md).
