# PET coincidence

↑ **Parent:** [Positron emission tomography](positron-emission-tomography.md)

A pair of nearly simultaneous detector events treated as a candidate two-photon [positron annihilation](electron-positron-annihilation.md). True unscattered pairs define a [coincidence line of response](coincidence-line-of-response.md). For attenuation along one such line, the complementary survival path integrals add to the full chord and are independent of the annihilation depth.
