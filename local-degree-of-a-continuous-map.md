# Local degree of a continuous map

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a continuous mapping](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md)

Let $f:M\to N$ be a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) between oriented $d$-dimensional [manifolds](topological-manifold.md), with $d\geq1$. Suppose $x$ is an isolated point of the fiber over $y=f(x)$. Choose an open neighborhood $U$ with $U\cap f^{-1}(y)=\{x\}$. The map of pairs

$$
(U,U\setminus\{x\})\longrightarrow(N,N\setminus\{y\})
$$

induces a map between rank-one degree-$d$ [local homology](local-homology.md) groups. Relative to the [local orientation of a manifold](local-orientation-of-a-manifold.md), this map is multiplication by an integer, the local degree. [Excision](excision-theorem.md) makes it independent of the neighborhood. A [smooth map between manifolds](smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) with invertible derivative at $x$ has local degree $\operatorname{sign}\det Df_x$, by the [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) and the chosen orientations.
