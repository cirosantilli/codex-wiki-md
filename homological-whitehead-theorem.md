# Homological Whitehead theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Whitehead theorem](whitehead-theorem.md)

An integral [homology](homology-split.md) equivalence between [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) spaces is a [weak homotopy equivalence](weak-homotopy-equivalence.md). A first nonzero relative [homotopy group](homotopy-group.md) would contradict the [Relative Hurewicz theorem](relative-hurewicz-theorem.md). Between [CW complexes](cw-complex.md) the ordinary [Whitehead theorem](whitehead-theorem.md) then gives a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md).
