# Entrained-head approximation for a helical microswimmer

↑ **Parent:** [Helical microswimmer with a spherical head](helical-microswimmer-with-a-spherical-head.md)

Suppose a small spherical head co-translates with the large-scale flagellar flow and rotates at $\Omega-\omega$, while the local ambient rotation is approximated by $\Omega$. Its force is neglected and its torque on the fluid is $-8\pi\mu a^3\omega$. Balance this against the [axial resistance matrix of a slender helix](axial-resistance-matrix-of-a-slender-helix.md), with local coefficient $C=4\pi\mu/|\log\epsilon|$, to obtain the displayed swimming speed and motor power $8\pi\mu a^3\omega^2$. This is an entrainment approximation, distinct from treating the head as an isolated sphere in stationary fluid.
