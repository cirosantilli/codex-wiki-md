# Quartic hilltop inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Hilltop inflation](hilltop-inflation.md)

Near a flat quartic maximum, $V\simeq\alpha\sigma^4-3\alpha\phi^4$. With [reduced Planck mass](reduced-planck-mass.md) $M$, the [slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md) gives $N\simeq\sigma^4/(24M^2\phi_N^2)$, $\eta_V\simeq-3/(2N)$ and $\epsilon_V\simeq\sigma^4/(192M^4N^3)$. Hence $n_s\simeq1-3/N$, $n_T\simeq-2\epsilon_V$, and $\mathcal P_{\mathcal R}\simeq8\alpha N^3/\pi^2$. The result is dominated by the near-maximum part of the trajectory; extending slow roll to a distant minimum is unnecessary.
