# Handle

↑ **Parent:** [Handle attachment](handle-attachment.md)

An index-$\lambda$ [handle](handle.md) in dimension $d$ is this product of [disks](disk-mathematics.md), attached along $S^{\lambda-1}\times D^{d-\lambda}$. Its [core disk](core-disk-of-a-handle.md) is $D^\lambda\times\{0\}$, and its [belt sphere](belt-sphere.md) is $\{0\}\times S^{d-\lambda-1}$. The complementary boundary piece is $D^\lambda\times S^{d-\lambda-1}$. Crossing a [critical point](critical-point.md) in the [Morse handle-attachment theorem](morse-handle-attachment-theorem.md) produces precisely this local change.
