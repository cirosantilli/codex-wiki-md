# Exponential moment of the hyperbolic secant

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic secant](hyperbolic-secant.md)

For real $|a|<\pi$, integrate $g(z)=e^{az}/\cosh\pi z$ around a rectangle of height $i$ and width $2R$. Only the [simple pole](simple-pole.md) $i/2$ lies inside, with [residue](residue.md) $e^{ia/2}/(\pi i)$. Since $g(x+i)=-e^{ia}g(x)$, the reversed top edge adds $e^{ia}$ times the bottom integral. The two vertical edges vanish as $R\to\infty$, using $|a|<\pi$. The [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md) gives $(1+e^{ia})J=2e^{ia/2}$, proving the formula. The condition is also the exact convergence range: the two tails decay with exponents $\pi-a$ and $\pi+a$.
