# Cartesian powers of cap sets

↑ **Parent:** [Cap set](cap-set.md)

Every [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) of [cap sets](cap-set.md) is a [cap set](cap-set.md). In [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) three, $x+y+z=0$ with any two equal forces all three equal. A zero-sum triple in a [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) of [cap sets](cap-set.md) is therefore diagonal in every coordinate block, hence diagonal overall.
