# Adjoint Dirac contribution to the Yang-Mills beta function

↑ **Parent:** [Charge renormalization](charge-renormalization.md)

One adjoint [Dirac spinor](dirac-spinor.md) contributes the displayed screening term to the four-dimensional [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md) in the [modified minimal subtraction scheme](modified-minimal-subtraction-scheme.md). The fermion contribution to the gluon [self-energy](self-energy.md) insertion has pole $-8g^2C_A(k^2\delta_{\mu\nu}-k_\mu k_\nu)/(3\epsilon(4\pi)^2)$. The corresponding background gauge-field [counterterm](counterterm.md) is $\delta Z_A=-8g^2C_A/(3\epsilon(4\pi)^2)$; the [background gauge-field renormalization identity](background-gauge-field-renormalization-identity.md) $Z_g=Z_A^{-1/2}$ gives $g_0=\mu^{\epsilon/2}(g+4C_Ag^3/[3\epsilon(4\pi)^2])$. Differentiation at fixed $g_0$ yields the screening coefficient. The mass-independent subtraction includes massive fields until effective-theory threshold matching is performed.
