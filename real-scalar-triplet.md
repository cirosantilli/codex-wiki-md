# Real scalar triplet

↑ **Parent:** [Real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md)

A [real scalar triplet](real-scalar-triplet.md) groups three real scalar components into a vector under [SO(3)](so-3-group.md). Equivalently it is the three-dimensional [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of [SU(2)](su-2-group.md), whose center acts trivially. Its rotationally invariant quadratic and quartic potentials depend on $\Phi\cdot\Phi$. A nonzero vacuum direction leaves rotations around that direction unbroken.
