# Ising-chain decimation constant

↑ **Parent:** [Exact zero-field Ising-chain decimation](exact-zero-field-ising-chain-decimation.md)

For a periodic even chain, eliminating alternate spins gives $Z_N(K)=A^{N/2}Z_{N/2}(K')$. Thus dimensionless [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per original spin obeys $f(K)=-\tfrac12\log A+\tfrac12 f(K')$. The exact solution $f(K)=-\log(2\cosh K)$ satisfies this relation. The multiplicative constant cancels from normalized retained-spin [probabilities](probability.md) but records eliminated-spin [entropy](entropy-split.md); discarding it loses thermodynamics even if the interaction recursion is correct.
