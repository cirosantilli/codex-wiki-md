# Positive functional on a C-star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_functional_on_a_C-star_algebra)

A bounded linear functional $\tau$ on a [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) is positive when $\tau(x)\geq0$ for every [Positive element of a C-star algebra](positive-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md). Positivity implies

$$
|\tau(y^*x)|^2\leq\tau(x^*x)\tau(y^*y)
$$

and $\|\tau\|=\tau(1)$; conversely, $\|\tau\|=\tau(1)$ implies positivity.
