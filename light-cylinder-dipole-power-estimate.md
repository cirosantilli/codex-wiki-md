# Light-cylinder dipole power estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Pulsar spin-down](pulsar-spin-down.md)

Extrapolate an anchored [magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md) to the [light cylinder](light-cylinder.md) $R_c=c/\Omega$ and estimate outward power as [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) density [times](time-in-physics.md) $cR_c^2$. The result fixes the displayed scaling but not the geometric coefficient. An aligned vacuum dipole does not radiate, while a current-carrying plasma magnetosphere can have nonzero aligned spin-down. Corotation at the [light cylinder](light-cylinder.md) is a notional limiting construction, not a material surface moving rigidly at light speed.
