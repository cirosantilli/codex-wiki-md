# Isothermal Bondi accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Bondi accretion](bondi-accretion.md)

Isothermal Bondi accretion has $p=c_s^2\rho$ with constant sound speed. Its regular transonic solution crosses the [Bondi sonic point](bondi-sonic-point.md) and approaches free fall near the accretor.
