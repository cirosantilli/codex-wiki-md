# Continuity point

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous function](continuous-function.md)

A point $c$ at which a [function](function-split.md) $f$ is [continuous](continuous-function.md). For a real [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F$, this is equivalent to the associated [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) having no [atom of a measure](atom-measure-theory.md) at $c$.
