# Defining representation of a matrix Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

For a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak g\subseteq\mathfrak{gl}(V)$, its defining representation is its given action on the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $V$. The [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) identity $[X,Y]v=X(Yv)-Y(Xv)$ verifies the representation property. For the [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md), diagonal trace-zero matrices have coordinate [weights of a representation](weight-of-a-representation.md) $L_i$ on the standard basis; for a [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md), the same action preserves the defining symmetric [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md).
