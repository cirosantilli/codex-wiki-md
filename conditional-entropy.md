# Conditional entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Information theory](information-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conditional_entropy)

For discrete random variables, conditional entropy is

$$
H(Y\mid X)=\sum_x\mathbb P(X=x)H(Y\mid X=x).
$$
