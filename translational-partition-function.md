# Translational partition function

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md)

For a free molecule of mass $m$ in a large three-dimensional volume $V$, replacing the translational state sum by an integral gives the displayed [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md). For identical classical molecules the many-molecule [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $q_{\rm trans}^N/N!$, not simply $q_{\rm trans}^N$. Differentiating its [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) at fixed volume gives the ideal-gas translational [entropy](entropy-split.md).
