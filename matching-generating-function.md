# Matching generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Matching (graph theory)](matching-graph-theory.md)

The coefficient $m_k$ counts [matchings in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md) with $k$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). For a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) with $n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) on each side, $m_n$ counts [perfect matchings](perfect-matching.md) and equals the [permanent of a matrix](permanent-mathematics.md) of its bipartite adjacency [matrix](matrix.md). Positive activity $\lambda$ makes $Z_G$ the normalizing constant of the [weighted matching Markov chain](weighted-matching-markov-chain.md).
