# Bernoulli saturated log-likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Saturated statistical model](saturated-statistical-model.md)

For individual [Bernoulli](bernoulli-distribution.md) observations, the [statistical saturated model](saturated-statistical-model.md) assigns one independent probability to each response. Each [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) factor is at most one and is exactly one at $\pi_i=y_i$. Hence the maximum [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md) is zero, with $0\log0=0$. This boundary maximum is attained when probabilities zero and one are allowed; restricting them to the open interval gives the same supremum.
