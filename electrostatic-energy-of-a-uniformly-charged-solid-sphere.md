# Electrostatic energy of a uniformly charged solid sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md)

A solid sphere of radius $R$ and uniform [charge density](charge-density.md) has total [electric charge](electric-charge.md) $Q$. [Gauss law](gauss-s-law.md) gives the radial [electric field](electric-field.md) $E_r=Qr/(4\pi\epsilon_0R^3)$ inside and $E_r=Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^2)$ outside. The [electric potential](electric-potential.md) vanishing at infinity is $Q(3-r^2/R^2)/(8\pi\epsilon_0R)$ inside and $Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0r)$ outside. Integrating the [electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md) density $\epsilon_0E^2/2$ over both regions gives $U=3Q^2/(20\pi\epsilon_0R)$. In a fixed-density family with $Q\propto Z$ and $R^3\propto Z$, the [electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md) scales as $Z^{5/3}$.
