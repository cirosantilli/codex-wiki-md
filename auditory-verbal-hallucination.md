# Auditory verbal hallucination

↑ **Parent:** [Auditory hallucination](auditory-hallucination.md)

An experience of a voice without an ordinary corresponding external speaker. Location, frequency, content, control, interpretation and distress are separable dimensions; the experience is not specific to [schizophrenia](schizophrenia.md).
