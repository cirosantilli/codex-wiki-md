# Polytrope of index zero

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar polytrope](stellar-polytrope.md)

The regular $n=0$ solution of the [Lane-Emden equation](lane-emden-equation.md) is $\theta=1-\xi^2/6$, with first zero $\sqrt6$. Its interior density is constant and its pressure varies as $\theta$. This is the structural incompressible limit; $P=K\rho^{1+1/n}$ itself is singular at $n=0$, and does not assign the perturbative [stellar adiabatic exponent](stellar-adiabatic-exponent.md) of a thermally stratified ideal-gas model.
