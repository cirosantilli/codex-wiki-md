# Variation of inverse metric

↑ **Parent:** [Metric tensor](metric-tensor.md)

Differentiating $g^{ac}g_{cb}=\delta^a_b$ and multiplying by the inverse gives the displayed [variation](variation.md). It is the [matrix inverse](matrix-inverse.md) [derivative](derivative.md), expressed with the covariant and contravariant components of a [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md).
