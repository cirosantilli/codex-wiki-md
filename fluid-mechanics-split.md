# Fluid mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_mechanics)

[Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md) studies the motion and [forces](force-split.md) of [liquids](liquid.md) and [gases](gas.md). Its central simplification is the [continuum approximation](continuum-approximation.md): replace individual [molecules](molecule-split.md) by bulk properties defined at every position and time. The averaging region must contain many [molecules](molecule-split.md) while remaining small compared with the distances over which those properties vary. A [fluid element](fluid-element.md) is such a small material parcel, rather than a single [molecule](molecule-split.md). This connects [fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md) to [continuum mechanics](continuum-mechanics-split.md) and makes [calculus](calculus-split.md) useful for describing flow. [MIT, A. Techet, Hydrodynamics reading 1, pp. 2–3](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-016-hydrodynamics-13-012-fall-2005/dc299d2ff05553b3515dccc23fc0f7d2_2005reading1.pdf)

Three basic fields are the [velocity field](velocity-field.md) $\mathbf u(\mathbf x,t)$, the [mass density](density.md) $\rho(\mathbf x,t)$ and the [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p(\mathbf x,t)$. The first gives a parcel's instantaneous [velocity](velocity.md); the second gives [mass](mass.md) per unit [volume](volume.md). The [pressure](pressure-split.md) contributes an inward normal [traction](traction.md) $-p\mathbf n$ across a surface with outward unit normal $\mathbf n$. Thus [pressure](pressure-split.md) is a [stress](stress.md), measured in $\mathrm{Pa}=\mathrm{N\,m^{-2}}$, and may vary with position even in [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md). Additional [viscous stresses](viscous-stress-tensor.md) can act tangentially. [MIT, A. Techet, Hydrodynamics reading 1, p. 5](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-016-hydrodynamics-13-012-fall-2005/dc299d2ff05553b3515dccc23fc0f7d2_2005reading1.pdf)

The guiding questions are what must be conserved and which [forces](force-split.md) matter. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) constrains the [velocity field](velocity-field.md); [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) determines its response to [pressure gradients](pressure-gradient.md), [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) and body [forces](force-split.md). An [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) model, a [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) estimate and [Bernoulli's principle](bernoulli-s-principle.md) answer different parts of a problem. Their appeal is that a few carefully stated assumptions can determine useful quantities before the entire [velocity field](velocity-field.md) is known.
