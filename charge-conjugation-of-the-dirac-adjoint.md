# Charge conjugation of the Dirac adjoint

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation of a Dirac field](charge-conjugation-of-a-dirac-field.md)

For $\psi^c=C\bar\psi^T$, use a unitary [charge-conjugation matrix](charge-conjugation-matrix.md) with $C^{-1}\gamma^0C=-(\gamma^0)^T$ and Hermitian $\gamma^0$ of square one. The [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md) then satisfies

$$
\overline{\psi^c}=\psi^T(\gamma^0)^*C^{-1}\gamma^0=-\psi^TC^{-1}.
$$

If the unitary [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) operator sends $\psi$ to $\eta_C\psi^c$, it sends $\bar\psi$ to $-\eta_C^*\psi^TC^{-1}$. Antisymmetry $C^T=-C$ also gives $(\psi^c)^c=\psi$. The unitary operator acting on fields is not an [antiunitary operator](antiunitary-operator.md) acting on numerical coefficients.
