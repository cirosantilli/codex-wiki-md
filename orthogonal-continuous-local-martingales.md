# Orthogonal continuous local martingales

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md)

Two [continuous local martingales](continuous-local-martingale.md) are orthogonal when their [quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md) is zero. With zero initial values, the [martingale product identity](martingale-product-identity.md) says that their product is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). For two [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md) in a common [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md), orthogonality and the [Lévy characterization of multidimensional Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-multidimensional-brownian-motion.md) make the pair a two-dimensional [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) and imply independence. Merely having the two marginal [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) laws on the same space does not imply orthogonality.
