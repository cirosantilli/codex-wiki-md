# Reversibility of an optical ray

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics.md)

In a stationary reciprocal optical medium, reversing the direction of a ray produces a ray along the same path. [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) is unchanged when its two media and ray directions are interchanged.
