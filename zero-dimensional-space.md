# Zero-dimensional space

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

A space is zero-dimensional when its topology has a base of [clopen sets](clopen-set.md). In the Hausdorff setting these sets separate distinct points, so the space is [totally disconnected](totally-disconnected-space.md). Conversely a compact Hausdorff [totally disconnected space](totally-disconnected-space.md) has a clopen base, by the [Šura-Bura component lemma](sura-bura-component-lemma.md) and finite subcovers of the complement of a neighborhood.
