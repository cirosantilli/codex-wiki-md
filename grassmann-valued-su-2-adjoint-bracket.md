<h1 id="grassmann-valued-su-2-adjoint-bracket">Grassmann-valued SU(2) adjoint bracket</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [BRST symmetry](brst-symmetry.md)

For adjoint fields whose coefficients have [Grassmann parity](grassmann-parity.md), $X\times Y=-(-1)^{|X||Y|}Y\times X$. Thus two odd ghost fields have a symmetric adjoint bracket, and $c\times c$ need not vanish. The [graded Jacobi identity](graded-jacobi-identity.md) and this symmetry are the algebraic mechanisms behind [BRST nilpotence](brst-nilpotence.md).
