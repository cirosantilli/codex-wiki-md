# Rooted plane tree

↑ **Parent:** [Plane tree](plane-tree.md)

A [plane tree](plane-tree.md) with a distinguished root and root corner, equivalently a [rooted tree](rooted-tree.md) with a linear order on the children at each vertex. Trees with $n$ edges are counted by the [Catalan number](catalan-number.md) $C_n$.
