# Travelling solidification front in a pure supercooled melt

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

For equal thermal properties and bounded travelling profiles on two deep half-spaces, the solid [temperature](temperature.md) is $T_m$ and the liquid profile is exponential. The [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) requires $L=c_p\Delta T$. This balances the [latent heat](latent-heat.md) of [solidification](freezing.md) with warming of the initially supercooled liquid.
