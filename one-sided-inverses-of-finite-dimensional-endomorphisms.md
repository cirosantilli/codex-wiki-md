# One-sided inverses of finite-dimensional endomorphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Linear map](linear-map.md)

For endomorphisms $S,T$ of the same finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), $ST=I$ makes $S$ surjective and $T$ injective. The [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) makes both invertible, hence $TS=I$. The finite-dimensional and equal-domain assumptions matter; unilateral shift operators on an infinite-dimensional sequence space give one-sided inverses that are not two-sided.
