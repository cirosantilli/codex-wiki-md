# Pioneer transcription factor

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md) able to engage certain nucleosomal or otherwise inaccessible [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) contexts and facilitate subsequent regulatory changes. Opening after expression does not alone prove direct pioneer action.
