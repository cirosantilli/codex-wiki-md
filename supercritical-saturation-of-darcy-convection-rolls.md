# Supercritical saturation of Darcy convection rolls

↑ **Parent:** [Onset of convection in a horizontal Darcy layer](onset-of-convection-in-a-horizontal-darcy-layer.md)

For $R=4\pi^2+\epsilon^2$, fixed roll phase, $T=\epsilon^2t$ and normalization $\psi=\epsilon A(T)\cos(\pi x)\sin(\pi z)+\cdots$, the [Landau amplitude equation](landau-amplitude-equation.md) is

$$
A_T=\frac12A-\frac{\pi^2}{8}A^3.
$$

The [mean-temperature correction in weakly nonlinear Darcy convection](mean-temperature-correction-in-weakly-nonlinear-darcy-convection.md) supplies the cubic negative feedback. Projection of the third-order equations onto the critical [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) via the [Fredholm solvability condition for a self-adjoint operator](fredholm-solvability-condition-for-a-self-adjoint-operator.md) fixes both coefficients. The stable nonzero amplitudes are $\pm2/\pi$ within the chosen phase, giving a supercritical [pitchfork bifurcation](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md) in this real-amplitude reduction.
