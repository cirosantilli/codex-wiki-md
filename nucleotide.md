# Nucleotide

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)

A [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) contains a [nucleobase](nucleotide-base.md), a [sugar](sugar.md), and one or more [phosphate](phosphate.md) groups. In [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md), linked [nucleotides](nucleotide.md) form a [polymer](polymer-split.md) whose [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) carries information. The [sugar](sugar.md) is [deoxyribose](deoxyribose.md) in [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and [ribose](ribose.md) in [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md). See [NHGRI: nucleotide](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Nucleotide).
