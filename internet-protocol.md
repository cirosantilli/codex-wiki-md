# Internet Protocol

↑ **Parent:** [Internet](internet.md)

[Internet Protocol](internet-protocol.md) supplies an inter-network datagram interface. Its basic [packet](network-packet.md) delivery does not itself guarantee arrival, order, absence of duplicates or completion of an application operation. [IPv4](ipv4.md) and [IPv6](ipv6.md) are versions of this network-layer [protocol](communication-protocol.md).
