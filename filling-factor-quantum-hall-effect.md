# Filling factor (quantum Hall effect)

↑ **Parent:** [Landau level](landau-level.md)

For a [two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md) in a perpendicular [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) of magnitude $B>0$, the [filling factor](filling-factor-quantum-hall-effect.md) is the [electron](electron.md) number per unit [area](surface-area.md) $n_s$ divided by the orbital degeneracy per unit [area](surface-area.md) $eB/h$ of one [spin](spin.md) branch of a [Landau level](landau-level.md). Here $e$ is the positive [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md) and $h$ the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md). The nominal [filling factor](filling-factor-quantum-hall-effect.md) varies continuously with $n_s/B$; it need not be an integer.
