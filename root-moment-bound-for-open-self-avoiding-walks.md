# Root-moment bound for open self-avoiding walks

↑ **Parent:** [Self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md)

In independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on a [graph](graph-split.md), let $c_n$ be the number of $n$-step [self-avoiding walks](self-avoiding-walk.md) from a fixed [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) and $\kappa_n$ the number that are open. Then $\mathbb E\kappa_n=c_np^n$. The [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) for the [concave function](concave-function.md) $t^{1/n}$ gives $\mathbb E(\kappa_n^{1/n})\leq p c_n^{1/n}$. No [independence](independent-random-variables.md) between different [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md) indicators is needed. When $c_n^{1/n}\to\mu$, the [limit superior](limit-superior.md) is at most $p\mu$.
