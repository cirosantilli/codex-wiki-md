# Seismic ray parameter in horizontal layering

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic ray theory](seismic-ray-theory.md)

In horizontally layered isotropic media, horizontal slowness is conserved along a ray. The incidence angle $i$ is measured from vertical. A smooth turning depth satisfies $pv=1$. The range and [travel time](travel-time.md) are $x=2\int pv\,dz/\sqrt{1-p^2v^2}$ and $T=2\int dz/[v\sqrt{1-p^2v^2}]$ for a symmetric surface-to-surface turning ray.
