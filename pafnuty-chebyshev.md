# Pafnuty Chebyshev

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematician](mathematician.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pafnuty_Chebyshev)

[Pafnuty Chebyshev](pafnuty-chebyshev.md)'s 1867 paper “Des valeurs moyennes” develops bounds for deviations of sums in terms of their [expected values](expected-value-split.md) and [second moments](second-moment.md). This connects his historical work with the modern [Chebyshev's inequality](chebyshev-s-inequality.md) and its use in the [weak law of large numbers](weak-law-of-large-numbers.md). [Chebyshev's original paper in French translation](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1867_2_12__177_0.pdf).
