# Exponential martingale for Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

For every $\theta\in\mathbb R$ and standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) $B$,

$$
\exp\left(\theta B_t-\frac12\theta^2t\right)
$$

is a positive martingale. Applying [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) cancels the drift, and its expectation is one by the moment-generating function of the [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md).
