# Outer expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Expected value](expected-value.md)

For a bounded possibly nonmeasurable real function $Z$ on a [probability space](probability-space.md), its outer expectation is the infimum of [expected values](expected-value.md) of measurable majorants. Its inner expectation is $\mathbb E_*Z=-\mathbb E^*(-Z)$. They agree with the usual [expected value](expected-value.md) for measurable $Z$. This permits function-space [convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md) for an [empirical distribution process](empirical-distribution-process.md) whose paths are not Borel-measurable in a nonseparable norm topology: both expectations of each bounded continuous test functional must approach its ordinary expectation under the tight measurable limiting law.
