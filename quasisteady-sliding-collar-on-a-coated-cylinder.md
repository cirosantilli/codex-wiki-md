# Quasisteady sliding collar on a coated cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Capillary instability of a thin film on a rigid cylinder](capillary-instability-of-a-thin-film-on-a-rigid-cylinder.md)

A large collar translating slowly at speed $c$ over a film $h_+$ and leaving a thinner film $h_-$ must grow: its leading excess volume increases at rate $c(h_+-h_-)$. An exact [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) connecting unequal stationary films is impossible, since integration of the [thin-film equation](thin-film-equation.md) would give a common constant $q-ch=-ch_+=-ch_-$. In a slowly evolving collar core the leading [pressure](pressure.md) gradient vanishes. Therefore $h'+h'''=0$, and a centred, reflection-symmetric collar has $h=A(1+\cos x)+B$ on $-\pi<x<\pi$. Its leading volume is $2\pi A$, so for constant small $B$, $2\pi\dot A\simeq c(h_+-h_-)$. The speed is supplied by [collar-to-film capillary matching](collar-to-film-capillary-matching.md).
