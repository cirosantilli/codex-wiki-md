# Chiral flavour symmetry of massless Dirac fields

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary flavour symmetry of degenerate Dirac fields](unitary-flavour-symmetry-of-degenerate-dirac-fields.md)

The free kinetic terms of left- and right-handed [Weyl fields](weyl-field.md) are separately invariant under [unitary group](unitary-group.md) rotations in flavour space. For $N$ massless [Dirac fields](dirac-field.md), the subgroup preserving common [fermion number](fermion-number.md) is $U(N)_L\times U(N)_R$. A common nonzero [Dirac mass term](dirac-mass-term.md) couples the two [chirality](chirality-physics.md) sectors and leaves its diagonal $U(N)$ subgroup. Gauge interactions can obstruct some of the classical [axial currents](axial-current.md) through a [chiral anomaly](chiral-anomaly.md).
