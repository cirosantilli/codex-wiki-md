# Galactic gas fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution](closed-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md)

This fraction compares gas [mass](mass.md) with gas plus permanently locked stellar [mass](mass.md) in a stated galactic reservoir. Under instantaneous recycling with a constant locking fraction and [star formation](star-formation.md) proportional to gas [mass](mass.md), it decays as $e^{-\alpha\epsilon t}$. Gross cumulative [star formation](star-formation.md) is not the denominator's surviving stellar [mass](mass.md).
