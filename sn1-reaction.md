# SN1 reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophilic substitution](nucleophilic-substitution.md)

A limiting unimolecular [nucleophilic substitution](nucleophilic-substitution.md) mechanism with rate-determining ionization, commonly producing a [carbocation](carbocation.md), followed by nucleophile capture. Its ideal rate law is first order in substrate.
