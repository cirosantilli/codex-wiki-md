# Omnivore

↑ **Parent:** [Consumer (food chain)](consumer-food-chain.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnivore)

An [omnivore](omnivore.md) feeds on both [primary producers](primary-producer.md) and [consumers](consumer-food-chain.md). Its mixed diet explains why a [trophic level](trophic-level.md) need not be a whole number or a fixed label throughout an [organism](organism-split.md)'s life.
