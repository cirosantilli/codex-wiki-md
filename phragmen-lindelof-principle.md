<h1 id="phragmen-lindelof-principle">Phragmén–Lindelöf principle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phragmén–Lindelöf_principle)

A maximum-modulus extension for unbounded domains with suitable growth restrictions. In its polynomial-growth vertical-strip form, bounds $|F(a+it)|\ll(1+|t|)^u$ and $|F(b+it)|\ll(1+|t|)^v$ for a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) imply the interpolated exponent $((b-\sigma)u+(\sigma-a)v)/(b-a)$. It is the strip-convexity tool used to move [subpower zeta bounds to the right of the critical line](subpower-zeta-bound-to-the-right-of-the-critical-line.md) back onto that line.
