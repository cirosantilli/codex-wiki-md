# Polynomial greatest common divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

A polynomial greatest common divisor of two [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) over a [field](field.md) divides both and is divisible by every common polynomial divisor. It is unique after requiring it to be monic, and the polynomial [Euclidean algorithm](euclidean-algorithm.md) computes it.
