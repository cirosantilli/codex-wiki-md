# Mapping cone exact sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping cone (topology)](mapping-cone-topology.md)

For nonempty $X$, the [topological mapping cone](mapping-cone-topology.md) has this [long exact sequence in homology](long-exact-sequence-in-homology.md), with ordinary [homology](homology-split.md) for $X,Y$ and [reduced homology](reduced-homology.md) for $C_f$. In degree zero, the augmentation of $X$ is accounted for by the cone vertex. The sequence follows from the [Mayer–Vietoris theorem](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) applied to neighborhoods of the cone and $Y$.
