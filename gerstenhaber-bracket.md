# Gerstenhaber bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Hochschild cohomology](hochschild-cohomology.md)

Write $f\circ g=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}(-1)^{i(q-1)}f\circ_i g$ for insertion of a degree-$q$ cochain into a degree-$p$ one. With the unsigned [Hochschild cup product](hochschild-cup-product.md) and the left [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md), take $[f,g]=(-1)^{(p-1)(q-1)}f\circ g-g\circ f$. It descends to [Hochschild cohomology](hochschild-cohomology.md) and gives a [Gerstenhaber algebra](gerstenhaber-algebra.md). The alternative insertion bracket $f\circ g-(-1)^{(p-1)(q-1)}g\circ f$ differs by the displayed degree sign and obeys the corresponding right rule. Degree-one brackets are [commutators](commutator.md) of [derivations](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) in either convention.
