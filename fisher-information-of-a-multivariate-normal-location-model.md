# Fisher information of a multivariate normal location model

↑ **Parent:** [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md)

For $X\sim N_p(\theta,\Sigma)$ with known nonsingular covariance matrix, the [score function](informant-function.md) is $\Sigma^{-1}(X-\theta)$ and the per-observation [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md) is $\Sigma^{-1}$. In particular, covariance $I_p$ gives information $I_p$.
