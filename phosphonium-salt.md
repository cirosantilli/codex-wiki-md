# Phosphonium salt

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

A salt containing a tetracoordinate positively charged phosphorus centre. Deprotonation of a suitable [carbon](carbon.md) substituent gives a [phosphorus ylide](phosphorus-ylide.md).
