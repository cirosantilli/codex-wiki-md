# Conformal mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian mechanics](hamiltonian-mechanics.md)

A one-dimensional particle with $H=p^2/2+g/x^2$ admits conserved time-translation, dilation and special-conformal charges. Their [Poisson brackets](poisson-bracket.md) form the [sl2R Lie algebra](sl2r-lie-algebra.md). The homogeneity of the [inverse-square potential](inverse-square-potential.md) is what allows the symmetry.
