# Wien displacement constant

↑ **Parent:** [Wien's displacement law](wien-s-displacement-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wien_displacement_constant)

The [Wien displacement constant](wien-displacement-constant.md) is the [constant of proportionality](constant-of-proportionality.md) $b$ in $\lambda_{\max}T=b$. From the exact [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md) values of the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md), [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) and [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md),

$$
b=\frac{(6.62607015\times10^{-34})(299792458)}{(1.380649\times10^{-23})(4.9651142317\ldots)}\,\mathrm{m\,K}
\simeq2.897771955\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m\,K}.
$$

The units follow from $hc/k_B$: $(\mathrm{J\,s})(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}})/(\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}})=\mathrm{m\,K}$. The defining constants are exact; the displayed decimal is rounded.
