# Stem cell

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stem_cell)

A [stem cell](stem-cell.md) can produce both renewed [stem cells](stem-cell.md) and differentiated descendants. Its developmental range depends on the type and setting; self-renewal does not imply unrestricted [cell fate](cell-fate.md).
