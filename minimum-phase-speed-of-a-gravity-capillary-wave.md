# Minimum phase speed of a gravity-capillary wave

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-capillary wave](gravity-capillary-wave.md)

For a deep-water [gravity-capillary wave](gravity-capillary-wave.md), $c_p^2=g/k+\sigma k/\rho$ for $k>0$. Differentiation gives the unique minimum at $k_*=\sqrt{\rho g/\sigma}$, or [wavelength](wavelength.md) $2\pi\sqrt{\sigma/(\rho g)}$. At this point the gravitational and capillary contributions are equal and $c_{\min}=(4g\sigma/\rho)^{1/4}$. Since $c_g=c_p+k\,dc_p/dk$, the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) equals the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) at this minimum, despite their usual difference in a dispersive wave.
