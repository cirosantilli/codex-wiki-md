# Buckling of a synthetic film

↑ **Parent:** [Buckling](buckling.md)

A flat, unsupported film's bending and [tension](tension.md) energies give modal stiffness $\kappa q^4+\sigma q^2$. Negative [tension](tension.md) represents compression, destabilizing sufficiently long waves. A finite sheet restricts allowed [wave numbers](wavenumber.md), while a supporting foundation adds stiffness and can select a wrinkle wavelength. This is generic continuum mechanics, independent of biological membrane structure.
