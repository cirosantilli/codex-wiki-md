# Photon-rocket fuel for acceleration and braking

↑ **Parent:** [Photon rocket](photon-rocket.md)

Accelerating from rest to [rapidity](rapidity.md) $\eta$ and braking to rest requires two separate photon-rocket burns. Their mass ratios multiply, giving $M_f/M_i=e^{-2\eta}$. For ideal equal-mass matter–[antimatter](antimatter.md) annihilation, the initial [antimatter](antimatter.md) mass fraction is $(1-e^{-2\eta})/2$. Braking fuel must be carried and accelerated during the first burn.
