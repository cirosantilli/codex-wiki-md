# Dendritic growth of a snowflake

↑ **Parent:** [Snowflake](snowflake.md)

An exposed tip receives vapour more efficiently than a sheltered surface and can amplify its lead, producing [crystal dendrites](dendrite-crystal.md) and side branches. The planar thermal [solidification](freezing.md) mechanism is an analogy, not a complete vapour-growth model. The [hexagonal ice crystal anisotropy](hexagonal-ice-crystal-anisotropy.md) selects six preferred directions; an isotropic linear instability alone does not select six arms.
