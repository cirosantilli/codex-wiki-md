# Grain size

↑ **Parent:** [Grain in a polycrystal](grain-in-a-polycrystal.md)

A measure of the spatial scale of [crystal grains](grain-in-a-polycrystal.md). Its definition must be stated for elongated [crystal grains](grain-in-a-polycrystal.md); size, aspect ratio and orientation distribution have distinct physical effects.
