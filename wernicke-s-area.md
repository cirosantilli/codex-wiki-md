<h1 id="wernicke-s-area">Wernicke's area</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal lobe](temporal-lobe.md)

A historical anatomical label associated with [language comprehension](language-comprehension.md), commonly centered on posterior superior temporal cortex. Its boundaries and functional interpretation vary across usages; a clinical syndrome is not confined automatically to this one area.
