# Curvature of a pullback connection

↑ **Parent:** [Pullback connection](pullback-connection.md)

For a [smooth map](smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) $\phi:M'\to M$ and a [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) $E\to M$, the [curvature form of a connection](curvature-form.md) on the [pullback vector bundle](pullback-vector-bundle.md) satisfies

$$
R^{\phi^*\nabla}_p(u,v)=R^\nabla_{\phi(p)}(d\phi_pu,d\phi_pv)
$$

as an endomorphism of $E_{\phi(p)}$. In a [frame of a vector bundle](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) written as a row, with section components written as columns, the [connection one-form](connection-one-form.md) is $\Omega$ and its [curvature form of a connection](curvature-form.md) is $d\Omega+\Omega\wedge\Omega$. The [pullback connection](pullback-connection.md) has matrix $\phi^*\Omega$; commutation of the [pullback of a differential form](pullback-of-a-differential-form.md) with the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) and [wedge product of differential forms](wedge-product-of-differential-forms.md) proves the identity. No pushforward to an ambient [vector field](vector-field.md) is required: $d\phi(X)$ is a [vector field along a map](vector-field-along-a-map.md).
