<h1 id="huckel-orbital-charge-density">Hückel orbital charge density</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hückel method](huckel-method.md)

In an orthonormal [Hückel method](huckel-method.md) basis, an occupied [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) with occupancy $f_k$ contributes $f_k|c_{ik}|^2$ electrons to site $i$. Adding one electron to a fixed orbital changes the site population by $|c_i|^2$. A coefficient sign indicates orbital phase, not charge sign. A net atomic charge also needs a reference population and treatment of the electrons outside the model.
