# Fixed-sign null space condition

↑ **Parent:** [Nullspace property](nullspace-property.md)

For a real vector $x_0$ with support $S$, uniqueness in [basis pursuit](basis-pursuit.md) is equivalent to $|\langle\operatorname{sgn}(x_{0,S}),h_S\rangle|<\|h_{S^c}\|_1$ for every nonzero $h\in\ker A$. The supporting-line inequality for the [absolute value](absolute-value.md) proves sufficiency. Taking small positive and negative multiples of $h$ before any active sign changes proves necessity.
