# Strong continuity of functional calculus through resolvents

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous functional calculus](continuous-functional-calculus.md)

For bounded [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) $a_i,a$, no uniform bound on $\|a_i\|$ is needed. The [resolvent identity](resolvent-identity.md) gives $(a_i-i)^{-1}-(a-i)^{-1}=(a_i-i)^{-1}(a-a_i)(a-i)^{-1}$, and the first factor has [norm](norm.md) at most one. Thus both [resolvents](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) at $i$ and $-i$ converge strongly. Products preserve strong convergence for uniformly bounded nets; uniform approximation by the algebra of these [resolvent](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) functions then gives the displayed statement for every continuous function vanishing at infinity.
