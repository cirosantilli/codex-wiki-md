# Focal length

↑ **Parent:** [Lens (optics)](lens-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focal_length)

The focal length measures the focusing power of an [optical lens](lens-optics.md) or mirror. In a thin converging lens, parallel paraxial rays meet a distance $f$ beyond the lens.
