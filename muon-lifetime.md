# Muon lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Muon decay](muon-decay.md)

The [proper mean lifetime](proper-mean-lifetime.md) of a free [muon](muon.md), approximately $2.2\,\mu\mathrm s$. Independent [particle decays](particle-decay.md) give survival [probability](probability.md) $\exp(-\Delta\tau/\tau_\mu)$. In a frame where the [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) is constant, $\Delta\tau=\Delta t/\gamma$; interactions of a stopped negative [muon](muon.md) in matter can add capture channels.
