# Energy transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md)

The [energy](energy-split.md) component of a transferred [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) in a specified frame. In fixed-target [deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md) the target rest frame gives $\nu=E-E'$, where $E,E'$ are incoming and scattered [electron](electron.md) [energies](energy-split.md). It is frame dependent even though $Q^2=-q^2$ is [Lorentz invariant](lorentz-invariance.md).
