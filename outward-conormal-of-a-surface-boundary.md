# Outward conormal of a surface boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Orientable surface](orientable-surface.md)

On the smooth boundary of an [orientable surface](orientable-surface.md), let $n$ be the chosen unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) and $t$ the positively oriented unit [tangent vector](tangent-vector.md) used by [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md). The outward conormal is $u=t\times n$. It is a [unit vector](unit-vector.md), tangent to the surface and orthogonal to its boundary. This convention turns $(n\times v)\cdot d\mathbf r$ into $u\cdot v\,ds$ by cyclic invariance of the [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md).
