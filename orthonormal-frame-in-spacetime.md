# Orthonormal frame in spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

An orthonormal frame $\{e_a\}$ satisfies $g(e_a,e_b)=\eta_{ab}$. Any two such frames differ by a local Lorentz transformation.
