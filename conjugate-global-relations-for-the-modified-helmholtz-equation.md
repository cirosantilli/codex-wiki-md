# Conjugate global relations for the modified Helmholtz equation

↑ **Parent:** [Global relation for a linear boundary value problem](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md)

The two [modified Helmholtz adjoint plane waves](modified-helmholtz-adjoint-plane-wave.md) $v_\lambda$ and $\widetilde v_\lambda=\overline{v_{\overline\lambda}}$ give boundary identities $\mathcal G(\lambda)=0$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal G}(\lambda)=0$. For real solution traces, $\widetilde{\mathcal G}(\lambda)=\overline{\mathcal G(\overline\lambda)}$. Reality is needed for this conjugation argument; the parametrization separately yields the reciprocal identity $\widetilde{\mathcal G}(\lambda)=\mathcal G(1/\lambda)$.
