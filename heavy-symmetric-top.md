# Heavy symmetric top

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric top](symmetric-top.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_symmetric_top)

A heavy symmetric top is a [symmetric top](symmetric-top.md) with one point fixed in a uniform gravitational field. Its vertical and body-axis rotational symmetries give two cyclic Euler angles.
