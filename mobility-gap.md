# Mobility gap

↑ **Parent:** [Anderson localization](anderson-localization.md)

An [energy](energy-split.md) interval with no extended bulk [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) available for transport. It can contain [localized eigenstates](localized-eigenstate.md) and have a nonzero [density of states](density-of-states.md); a [mobility gap](mobility-gap.md) need not be a [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md).
