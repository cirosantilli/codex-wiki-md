# Finite-subset lifting of a well-quasi-order

↑ **Parent:** [Hoare domination preorder](hoare-domination-preorder.md)

Finite subsets of a [well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md), compared by [Hoare domination preorder](hoare-domination-preorder.md), form a [well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md). Enumerate each finite subset as a [word](string.md) and apply [Higman lemma](higman-s-lemma.md): a subsequence embedding with increased letters supplies a domination witness for every source element. This does not assert the same result for the full [power set](power-set.md).
