<h1 id="egorov-s-theorem">Egorov's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egorov's_theorem)

On a finite-measure measurable [set](set-split.md), a [sequence](sequence.md) of [measurable functions](measurable-function.md) converging pointwise almost everywhere converges uniformly outside a [set](set-split.md) of arbitrarily small measure. For tolerance $1/k$, the [sets](set-split.md) on which every index after $N$ has error at most $1/k$ increase to full measure as $N\to\infty$. Choose $N_k$ with exceptional measure at most $\varepsilon2^{-k-1}$ and intersect the good [sets](set-split.md). [Countable subadditivity](countable-subadditivity-of-a-measure.md) bounds the discarded measure, and the resulting tail estimates prove [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md). Finite measure is essential: $\mathbf1_{[n,\infty)}$ on $[0,\infty)$ is a counterexample.
