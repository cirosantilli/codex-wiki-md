# Frequency spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)

A [frequency spectrum](frequency-spectrum.md) describes the [frequency](frequency.md) components of a [function](function-split.md). A [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) supplies discrete [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md); the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) of an [integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) supplies a continuous, generally complex-valued description. An [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) plot shows magnitudes and omits [wave phases](phase-waves.md), so it need not determine the original [function](function-split.md). In the example with three [Fourier harmonics](fourier-harmonic.md) above, the one-sided [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) plot has heights $1$, $1/2$, and $1/4$. The corresponding complex [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) at positive and negative [frequencies](frequency.md) each have half those heights: $\cos(2\pi\nu t)=(e^{2\pi i\nu t}+e^{-2\pi i\nu t})/2$.
