# Restricted mean survival time

↑ **Parent:** [Survival function](survival-function.md)

The restricted mean survival time to a fixed cutoff $\tau$ is $\mathbb E[\min(T,\tau)]=\int_0^\tau S(t)\,dt$ for a nonnegative event time. This follows by the [tail integral formula for moments](tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md). Unlike the unrestricted mean, it uses no tail beyond $\tau$; under [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md) it can be estimated by integrating a [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md) over adequately observed follow-up.
