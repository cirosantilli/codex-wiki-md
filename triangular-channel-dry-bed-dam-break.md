# Triangular-channel dry-bed dam break

↑ **Parent:** [Prismatic-channel shallow water equations](prismatic-channel-shallow-water-equations.md)

In a prismatic triangular channel with wetted area proportional to $h^2$, the [Riemann invariants](riemann-invariant.md) are $u\pm4c$, with $c^2=gh/2$. Releasing water initially at rest into a dry channel produces a [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md) with $u+4c=4c_0$ and $x/t=u-c$. Hence $c=(4c_0-x/t)/5$, $u=4(c_0+x/t)/5$ and the displayed depth profile for $-c_0t<x<4c_0t$. The dry front advances at $4c_0$ before finite-reservoir or varying-channel effects become important.
