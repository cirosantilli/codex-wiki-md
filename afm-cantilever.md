# AFM cantilever

↑ **Parent:** [AFM probe](afm-probe.md)

A compliant beam whose deflection senses tip–sample [force](force-split.md) in [atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md). In its calibrated linear range, normal [force](force-split.md) is $F=k_c\delta_c$.
