# Geodesic metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_metric_space)

A [metric space](metric-space.md) in which every two points are joined by a [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) segment. Equivalently, they admit a [continuous path](continuous-path.md) whose [metric path length](metric-path-length.md) equals their distance. In this general setting no [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) or [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) is required.
