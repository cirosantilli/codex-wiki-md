# Signed Cauchy boundary operators

↑ **Parent:** [Sokhotski–Plemelj theorem](sokhotski-plemelj-theorem.md)

With $Hv(\rho)=\pi^{-1}\operatorname{PV}\int v(s)/(\rho-s)ds$, the upper and lower limiting [Cauchy integrals](cauchy-transform.md) are $P^\pm v=\pm v/2+iHv/2$. They obey $P^+-P^-=I$. The actual complementary idempotent [projections](projection-linear-algebra.md) are $P^+$ and $-P^-$; confusing this signed convention with two positive projectors changes reconstruction signs. These boundary operators naturally enter the [spectral reconstruction of an attenuated Radon transform](spectral-reconstruction-of-an-attenuated-radon-transform.md).
