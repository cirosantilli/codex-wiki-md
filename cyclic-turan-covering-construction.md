<h1 id="cyclic-turan-covering-construction">Cyclic Turán covering construction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal hypergraph theory](extremal-hypergraph-theory.md)

With $r$ balanced cyclically ordered vertex classes and $2\le\ell\le r+1$, include an $\ell$-set if some start has at least $k+1$ vertices in its first $k$ classes for every $1\le k\le\ell-1$. The [cycle lemma](cycle-lemma.md) implies every $(r+1)$-set contains an edge. Its asymptotic density is $((\ell-1)/r)^{\ell-1}$; taking the [hypergraph complement](hypergraph-complement.md) gives a lower bound for the [Turán density](turan-density.md) of a complete uniform hypergraph.
