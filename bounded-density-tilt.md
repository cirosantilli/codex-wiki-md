# Bounded density tilt

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical tangent set](statistical-tangent-set.md)

For bounded measurable $g$ with $Pg=0$, the formula $p_t=p(1+tg)$ defines a normalized positive [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) for $|t|\|g\|_\infty<1$. A uniform [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md) of the square root proves [differentiability in quadratic mean](differentiability-in-quadratic-mean.md) with [score function](informant-function.md) $g$: the squared remainder is $O(t^4Pg^4)$. Such paths realize all bounded centered directions of an unrestricted density model.
