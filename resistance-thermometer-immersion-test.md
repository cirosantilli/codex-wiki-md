# Resistance thermometer immersion test

↑ **Parent:** [Resistance thermometer](resistance-thermometer.md)

A probe's sheath and leads carry [heat](heat-split.md) between the sensing region and the surroundings through [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md). In a stable, sufficiently uniform [heat bath](heat-bath.md), compare the indicated [temperature](temperature-split.md) as insertion depth increases, allowing settling each time. A plateau supports adequate immersion for that apparatus and required uncertainty; it does not prove that every depth or medium works. Keep the sensing element and reference [thermometer](thermometer.md) at comparable depths, and check bath gradients independently, so moving the probe does not merely sample a different [temperature](temperature-split.md). The documented [platinum resistance thermometer comparison calibration](platinum-resistance-thermometer-comparison-calibration.md) includes these controls.
