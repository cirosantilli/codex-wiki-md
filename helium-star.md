# Helium star

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

A helium star has lost most of its hydrogen-rich envelope and is made predominantly of helium. If massive enough, its core can ignite helium; a lower-mass stripped core instead becomes a [helium white dwarf](helium-white-dwarf.md).
