# Solid torus

↑ **Parent:** [Torus](torus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_torus)

A solid torus is the product of a circle with a closed disk. Its boundary is the [torus](torus.md) $S^1\times S^1$. It deformation retracts onto its central circle, so its degree-one [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) is one dimensional and injects into that of its boundary.
