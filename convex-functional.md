# Convex functional

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

A [functional](functional.md) on a real [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is convex when it is a [convex function](convex-function.md): $J(\theta v+(1-\theta)w)\leq\theta J(v)+(1-\theta)J(w)$ for $0\leq\theta\leq1$. For a symmetric nonnegative [bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md) $a$ and a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $\ell$, the quadratic functional $J(v)=\tfrac12a(v,v)-\ell(v)$ is convex, since its convexity difference is $\tfrac12\theta(1-\theta)a(v-w,v-w)\geq0$.
