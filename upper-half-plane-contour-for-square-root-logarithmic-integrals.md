# Upper-half-plane contour for square-root logarithmic integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Residue theorem](residue-theorem.md)

Set $I=\int_0^\infty x^{1/2}/(1+x^2)\,dx$ and $J=\int_0^\infty x^{1/2}\log x/(1+x^2)\,dx$. In an indented upper semicircle use the [principal complex logarithm](principal-complex-logarithm.md) and $z^{1/2}=e^{\log z/2}$. The negative real boundary contributes $iJ-\pi I$, the positive boundary contributes $J$, and both circular arcs vanish. The pole at $i$ has [residue](residue.md) $\pi e^{i\pi/4}/4$. Thus $(1+i)J-\pi I=(\pi^2/(2\sqrt2))(-1+i)$; comparing real and imaginary parts yields the displayed integrals.
