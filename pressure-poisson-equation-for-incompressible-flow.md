# Pressure Poisson equation for incompressible flow

↑ **Parent:** [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md)

Taking the divergence of the constant-density [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md) and using [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) gives the displayed elliptic [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) for pressure. Its [Green function](green-s-function.md) is nonlocal. For an isolated eddy, the far pressure has a quadrupolar contribution proportional to $r^{-3}$, obtained by differentiating the kernel $1/r$ twice. Boundary conditions can contribute additional pressure fields.
