# Repeated characteristic root of a linear recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic equation of a linear recurrence](characteristic-equation-of-a-linear-recurrence.md)

A nonzero root $\mu$ of multiplicity $m$ supplies $m$ independent solutions $n^j\mu^n$ to a constant-coefficient [linear recurrence relation](linear-recurrence-relation.md). Substitution $y_n=\mu^nz_n$ reduces its repeated factor to the $m$th [forward difference operator](forward-difference-operator.md). A zero root needs separate initial-data treatment: for characteristic polynomial $\lambda^m$, the recurrence is $y_{n+m}=0$, leaving the first $m$ values arbitrary.
