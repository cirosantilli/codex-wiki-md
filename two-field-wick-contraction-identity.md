# Two-field Wick contraction identity

↑ **Parent:** [Wick's theorem](wick-s-theorem.md)

Split a free scalar field into its annihilation and creation parts. For $x^0>y^0$, changing the product into [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) adds $[\phi^{(+)}(x),\phi^{(-)}(y)]$, the positive-frequency two-point function. For the opposite ordering interchange the two points. Their combination is the [Feynman propagator](feynman-propagator.md), equal to the vacuum expectation of the [time-ordered product](time-ordered-product.md). Iterating this identity gives the scalar [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md).
