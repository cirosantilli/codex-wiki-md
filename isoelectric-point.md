# Isoelectric point

↑ **Parent:** [Acid-base chemistry](acid-base-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isoelectric_point)

The [isoelectric point](isoelectric-point.md) is the [pH](ph.md) at which a specified [molecule](molecule.md)'s mean net [electric charge](electric-charge.md) is zero. Different [protonation](protonation.md) states can still carry nonzero [electric charges](electric-charge.md); their contributions cancel in the average. For the two-step [glycine speciation model](glycine-speciation-model.md), this gives $\mathrm{pI}=(\mathrm pK_{a1}+\mathrm pK_{a2})/2$. Additional ionizable [amino acid side chains](amino-acid-side-chain.md) require their own [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) calculations; averaging every [pKa](pka.md) is not a general rule. [§26.2, discussion following Figure 26.2: isoelectric points and the dissociations surrounding the neutral form](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry.md)

Below its [isoelectric point](isoelectric-point.md), free [glycine](glycine.md) has positive mean [electric charge](electric-charge.md) and an [electric field](electric-field.md) drives it toward the negative [electrode](electrode.md); above its [isoelectric point](isoelectric-point.md), the mean [electric charge](electric-charge.md) is negative and the direction reverses. At its [isoelectric point](isoelectric-point.md) the charge-driven contribution vanishes. [Diffusion](diffusion.md) and bulk movement of the [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) can still move the sample. [Gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md) applies this charge dependence to separation, although [protein](protein.md) size, shape and interactions also affect migration. The [pKa](pka.md) values of free [glycine](glycine.md) do not determine a [protein](protein.md)'s [isoelectric point](isoelectric-point.md): its free [N-terminus](n-terminus.md), [C-terminus](c-terminus.md) and ionizable [amino acid side chains](amino-acid-side-chain.md) supply a different inventory.
