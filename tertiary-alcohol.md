# Tertiary alcohol

↑ **Parent:** [Alcohol](alcohol.md)

An [alcohol](alcohol.md) whose hydroxyl-bearing carbon is bonded to three carbon substituents. Its oxygenated carbon carries no directly attached proton, so it lacks an ordinary carbon-proton [HSQC](hsqc.md) cross peak.
