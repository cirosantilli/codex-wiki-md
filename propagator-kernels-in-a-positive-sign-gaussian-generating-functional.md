# Propagator kernels in a positive-sign Gaussian generating functional

↑ **Parent:** [Generating functional](generating-functional.md)

With source term $J\phi+\bar\eta\psi+\bar\psi\eta$ and left [functional derivatives](functional-derivative.md), a free functional written as $Z_0\propto\exp(iJ\Delta J/2+i\bar\eta S\eta)$ has physical time-ordered contractions $D=-i\Delta$ and $F=-iS$. The kernels in this exponent must not be identified with contractions already carrying factors of $i$. Four contractions in a second-order Yukawa scalar two-point graph introduce $(-i)^4=1$, leaving its kernel formula's overall coefficient unchanged.
