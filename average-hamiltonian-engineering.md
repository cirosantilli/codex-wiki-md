# Average Hamiltonian engineering

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian engineering](hamiltonian-engineering.md)

Fast prescribed pulses change the frame in which the system evolves during successive intervals. To leading order over a short control cycle, the effective [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is the duration-weighted mean of these conjugated generators. The [Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula](baker-campbell-hausdorff-formula.md) supplies [commutator](commutator.md) corrections. Symmetrizing the cycle can cancel unwanted interactions or retain a desired one. The approximation requires control cycles short compared with the relevant dynamical timescales and a suitable account of finite pulse duration.
