# Rationalization of a topological space

↑ **Parent:** [Rational space](rational-space.md)

For [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) $X$, [rationalization of a topological space](rationalization-of-a-topological-space.md) is a map $r:X\to X_{\mathbb Q}$ to a [rational space](rational-space.md) inducing $\pi_n(X_{\mathbb Q})=\pi_n(X)\otimes\mathbb Q$. It is also a [rational homology](rational-homology.md) equivalence. Its universal property is the bijection $r^*:[X_{\mathbb Q},Z]\to[X,Z]$ for every [rational space](rational-space.md) $Z$. A [Postnikov tower](postnikov-tower.md) constructs it by replacing each fibre group with its rational [tensor product](tensor-product.md) and transporting the [Postnikov invariant](postnikov-invariant.md) through the resulting [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) isomorphism, then taking the [homotopy inverse limit](homotopy-inverse-limit-of-a-tower.md).
