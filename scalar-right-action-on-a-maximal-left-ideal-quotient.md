# Scalar right action on a maximal-left-ideal quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Maximal left ideal](maximal-left-ideal.md)

In a complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), right multiplication by $a$ induces a bounded [module endomorphism](module-endomorphism.md) $T$ of $A/L$ when $La\subseteq L$. Every nonzero module endomorphism of this [simple module](irreducible-module.md) is algebraically bijective, since its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) and range are [submodules](submodule.md). Its inverse is bounded by the [bounded inverse theorem](bounded-inverse-theorem.md). Choosing $\lambda$ in the nonempty [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md) of $T$ forces $T-\lambda I=0$. Applying this identity to $1+L$ gives $a-\lambda1\in L$, with uniqueness because $1\notin L$.
