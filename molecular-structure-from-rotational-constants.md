# Molecular structure from rotational constants

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational spectroscopy](rotational-spectroscopy.md)

Recovering [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md) from [rotational constants](rotational-constant.md) is an [inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md). In the synthetic assignment, $B=5\times10^9\,\mathrm{Hz}$ gives $I=h/(8\pi^2B)\simeq1.6784\times10^{-45}\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$. For a [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md) with known [masses](mass.md), [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md) explains how to infer an effective [bond length](bond-length.md). For a longer [linear molecule](linear-molecule.md), one $I$ cannot determine every [bond length](bond-length.md): several different arrangements can have the same [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md).

For a fixed three-atom linear model with positions $0,a,a+b$ and positive [masses](mass.md) $m_1,m_2,m_3$, subtracting the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) gives

$$
I=\frac{m_1m_2a^2+m_2m_3b^2+m_1m_3(a+b)^2}{m_1+m_2+m_3}.
$$

This is one equation for two unknown [bond lengths](bond-length.md). Even fixing the order of the [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) leaves a family of solutions. Additional [isotope](isotope.md) substitutions change known [masses](mass.md) and supply independent equations, provided the effective [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md) can be treated as shared. For nonlinear [molecules](molecule-split.md), three principal [moments of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) give frequency-valued constants $A=h/(8\pi^2I_a)$, $B=h/(8\pi^2I_b)$, and $C=h/(8\pi^2I_c)$; a single evenly spaced comb is generally insufficient.

Vibrational averaging, unresolved [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md), internal motion, and [centrifugal distortion](centrifugal-distortion.md) limit structural inference. A ground-vibrational fitted [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md) is not automatically the equilibrium constant. Ordinary field-free [rotational spectroscopy](rotational-spectroscopy.md) also gives identical spectra for opposite [enantiomers](enantiomer.md) under the usual parity-symmetric molecular [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md); assigning composition and conformation does not by itself determine handedness. Molecular identification from a rich fingerprint can therefore succeed even when a unique full [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md) cannot be reconstructed.
