# Horizontal-vibration averaged pendulum potential

↑ **Parent:** [Pendulum with a prescribed translating support](pendulum-with-a-prescribed-translating-support.md)

For a small, rapid horizontal displacement $a\cos\omega t$, the leading fast angular excursion is $\xi=-(a/l)\cos\Theta\cos\omega t$. Correlating that excursion with the driving [torque](torque.md) gives the averaged force $\ddot\Theta=-(g/l)\sin\Theta+(a^2\omega^2/2l^2)\sin\Theta\cos\Theta$. Its [effective potential](effective-potential.md) is $-mgl\cos\Theta+ma^2\omega^2\cos^2\Theta/4$. The downward state loses [linear stability](linear-stability.md) above $a\omega=\sqrt{2gl}$ and two tilted minima appear. Small $a/l$ and slow dynamics compared with $\omega$ are required; horizontal and vertical support vibration produce different [effective potentials](effective-potential.md).
