# Place cell

↑ **Parent:** [Neural coding](neural-coding.md)

A [neuron](neuron-split.md) whose activity is spatially selective for the animal’s own location under specified conditions. Its field can depend on environment, task and sensory context; a visual view and self-location must be disentangled experimentally.
