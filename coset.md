# Coset

↑ **Parent:** [Group theory](group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coset)

For a subgroup $H\leq G$, a left coset is $gH=\{gh:h\in H\}$. The left cosets partition $G$ and all have cardinality $|H|$.
