<h1 id="homology-after-attaching-a-mobius-band-to-the-real-projective-plane">Homology after attaching a Möbius band to the real projective plane</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental group after attaching a Möbius band](fundamental-group-after-attaching-a-mobius-band.md)

Glue the boundary of a [Möbius band](mobius-band.md) to the projective line in the [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md) by a [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) $n$. The band is the [mapping cylinder](mapping-cylinder.md) of the degree-two boundary covering of its core. Collapsing the connecting tree in the resulting double mapping cylinder gives a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) with one vertex, edges $a,b$, and two attaching words $a^2$ and $a^{-n}b^2$. Its [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) has degree-two boundary matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2&-n\\0&2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its determinant is four, so $H_2=0$. The [Smith normal form](smith-normal-form.md) gives $H_1\cong\mathbb Z/4$ for odd $n$ and $(\mathbb Z/2)^2$ for even $n$; $H_0=\mathbb Z$ and higher [homology](homology-split.md) vanishes. The space cannot be [homotopy equivalent](homotopy-inverse.md) to a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md): mod-two [homology](homology-split.md) forces a potential manifold to be a surface, and its [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) is one, whereas the only such closed connected surface is the projective plane, with different integral $H_1$.
