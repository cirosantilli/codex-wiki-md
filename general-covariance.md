# General covariance

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

The ability to express a theory consistently under arbitrary changes of spacetime coordinates. A [tensor equation](tensor-equation.md) has this property when all its fields, including any [background structures](background-structure.md), transform appropriately. It does not by itself say that every active transformation is a [dynamical symmetry](dynamical-symmetry.md), or that no preferred [background structure](background-structure.md) exists. [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md) has the additional substantive feature that its [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) is dynamical.
