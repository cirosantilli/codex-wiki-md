# Chromatin immunoprecipitation

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

Enrichment of chromatin associated with a selected [protein](protein-split.md), followed by measurement of the recovered [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Association with a fragment need not mean direct base-specific binding; sequential enrichment can test co-association while retaining those resolution limits.
