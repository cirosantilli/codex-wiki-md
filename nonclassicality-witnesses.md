# Nonclassicality witnesses

↑ **Parent:** [Nonclassical state of light](nonclassical-state-of-light.md)

Sufficient single-mode witnesses include a [Fano factor](fano-factor.md) $\operatorname{Var}n/\langle n\rangle<1$, an [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md) variance below $1/4$ for $[X,P]=i/2$, or [photon antibunching](photon-antibunching.md) with $g^{(2)}(0)<1$. Classical mixtures obey $\operatorname{Var}n\ge\langle n\rangle$, and $g^{(2)}(0)=\mathbb E(|\alpha|^4)/\mathbb E(|\alpha|^2)^2\ge1$ by nonnegative variance. The tests are sufficient rather than necessary and exclude the vacuum case when dividing by zero mean occupation.
