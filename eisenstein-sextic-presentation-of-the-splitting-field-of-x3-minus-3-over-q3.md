# Eisenstein sextic presentation of the splitting field of X3 minus 3 over Q3

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification groups of the splitting field of Xp minus p over the p-adic numbers](ramification-groups-of-the-splitting-field-of-xp-minus-p-over-the-p-adic-numbers.md)

If $a^3=3$ and $\zeta_3$ is a primitive cube root of unity, then $\pi^3=1+2\zeta_3$ and $\pi^6=-3$. The [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md) $X^6+3$ therefore presents the entire degree-six [splitting field](splitting-field.md), and $\pi$ is a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md). Its normalised [valuation](valuation.md) gives $v_L(3)=6$, $v_L(a)=2$ and $v_L(\zeta_3-1)=3$. The order-three automorphisms displace $\pi$ with [valuation](valuation.md) four, whereas transpositions displace it with [valuation](valuation.md) one.
