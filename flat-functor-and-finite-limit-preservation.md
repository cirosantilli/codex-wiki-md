# Flat functor and finite-limit preservation

↑ **Parent:** [Flat functor](flat-functor.md)

For a [small category](small-category.md) with [finite limits](finite-limit.md), a set-valued [functor](functor.md) preserving them has a [category of elements](category-of-elements.md) with finite limits, so its opposite is a [filtered category](filtered-category.md). The [covariant density presentation](covariant-density-presentation.md) is therefore a filtered [colimit](colimit.md) of [representable functors](representable-functor.md). Conversely, representable functors preserve [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md), and [filtered colimits commute with finite limits in sets](filtered-colimits-commute-with-finite-limits-in-sets.md), proving the criterion.
