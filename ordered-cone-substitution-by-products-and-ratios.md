# Ordered-cone substitution by products and ratios

↑ **Parent:** [Change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md)

On $x>0$, $0<y<1$, $0<z<x$, this [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) is a [bijection](bijection.md) onto $0<\gamma<\beta<\alpha$. Its inverse is $x=\sqrt{\alpha\beta}$, $y=\sqrt{\beta/\alpha}$, $z=\gamma\sqrt{\alpha/\beta}$, and its forward [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $2x$. Thus $x\,dx\,dy\,dz=\tfrac12\,d\alpha\,d\beta\,d\gamma$. Passing to increments $(\alpha-\beta,\beta-\gamma,\gamma)$ then maps the ordered cone to the positive octant, turning an exponential with linear exponent into a product of three one-dimensional [integrals](integral.md).
