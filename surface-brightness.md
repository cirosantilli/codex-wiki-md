# Surface brightness

↑ **Parent:** [Observational astronomy](observational-astronomy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_brightness)

[Surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) is received flux per unit solid angle on the sky. It describes the brightness of an extended source independently of a particular aperture's area. An [isophote](isophote.md) is a contour of constant [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md); central [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) sets the available photons for resolved galaxy spectroscopy.
