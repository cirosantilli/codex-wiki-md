# Instantaneous forward rate

↑ **Parent:** [Interest rate](interest-rate.md)

The continuously compounded rate inferred for an infinitesimal investment interval at future maturity $T$, as seen at time $t$. For a unit-face-value [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md), $P(t,T)=\exp(-\int_t^T f(t,u)du)$ and the [short rate](short-rate.md) is $f(t,t)$.
