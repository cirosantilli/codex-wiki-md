# Low circular-orbit period measures mean density

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital period](orbital-period.md)

For a spherical body of radius $R$, mass $M$ and [mean density](mean-density.md) $\bar\rho=3M/(4\pi R^3)$, a low [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) has period $P\simeq2\pi\sqrt{R^3/(GM)}$, by equating the gravitational acceleration from [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md) to the [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md). Substitution proves the displayed relation. Thus similar low-orbit periods indicate similar mean densities, even for bodies with very different masses and radii. At altitude $h$, the right side is multiplied by $(1+h/R)^3$; the simple density inference requires $h\ll R$ and neglects rotation, nonsphericity and other gravitating bodies.
