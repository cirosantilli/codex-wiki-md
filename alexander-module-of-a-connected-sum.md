# Alexander module of a connected sum

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander module of a knot](alexander-module-of-a-knot.md)

Boundary-connected-summing [Seifert surfaces](seifert-surface.md) gives a block-diagonal [Seifert matrix](seifert-matrix.md), because basis curves in the separated summands have zero linking. The [Seifert-matrix presentation of the Alexander module](seifert-matrix-presentation-of-the-alexander-module.md) is block diagonal too. Thus the Alexander [modules](module-mathematics.md) form a direct sum and their [Alexander polynomials of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md) multiply up to a unit.
