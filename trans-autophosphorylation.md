# Trans-autophosphorylation

↑ **Parent:** [Autophosphorylation](autophosphorylation.md)

In [trans-autophosphorylation](trans-autophosphorylation.md), one [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) chain phosphorylates another partner chain. “Auto” therefore does not require the modified site and catalytic [active site](active-site.md) to be on the same chain. The [insulin receptor](insulin-receptor.md) provides an activation-loop example; [EGFR](epidermal-growth-factor-receptor.md) shows why [autophosphorylation](autophosphorylation.md) and catalytic activation need not be the same event.
