# Line section of a smooth plane quartic

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth plane quartic](smooth-plane-quartic.md)

For a [smooth plane quartic](smooth-plane-quartic.md) $C\subset\mathbb P^2$, the [adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md) gives $K_C\cong\mathcal O_C(1)$. Hence every line section $C\cap\ell$ is a [canonical divisor](canonical-divisor.md).
