# Monomial equivalence of linear codes

↑ **Parent:** [Linear code](linear-code.md)

Two [linear codes](linear-code.md) are monomially equivalent if a permutation of coordinates followed by multiplication of individual coordinates by nonzero [field](field.md) elements takes one to the other. This preserves [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) and [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md). Choosing different representatives for projective parity-check columns produces this equivalence.
