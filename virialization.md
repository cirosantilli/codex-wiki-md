# Virialization

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

[virialization](virialization.md) is relaxation toward a gravitationally supported bulk state satisfying an appropriate virial balance. For an isolated approximately stationary Newtonian system with negligible boundary terms, $2K+W\simeq0$. It does not require every collisionless constituent to reach collisional [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md), and ongoing infall, tides or pressure at a boundary modify the balance.
