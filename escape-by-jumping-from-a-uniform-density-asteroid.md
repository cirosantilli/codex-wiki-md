# Escape by jumping from a uniform-density asteroid

↑ **Parent:** [Escape velocity](escape-velocity.md)

For a spherical nonrotating asteroid of the same mean [mass density](density.md) as a reference planet of radius R and surface [gravity](gravity.md) g, the [mass](mass.md) scales as r cubed. Its surface [escape speed](escape-velocity.md) therefore obeys $v_{\rm esc}^2=2gr^2/R$. A take-off [speed](speed.md) $v_J$ permits escape for $r\leq v_J\sqrt{R/(2g)}$. If that same take-off [speed](speed.md) gives ballistic height H above the reference planet, $v_J^2=2gH$, and the limiting asteroid radius is $\sqrt{RH}$. A chosen H is an explicit performance estimate, not a universal human constant.
