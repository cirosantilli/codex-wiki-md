# Extraordinary phase-index angular dependence

↑ **Parent:** [Birefringence](birefringence.md)

For a lossless uniaxial [dielectric](dielectric.md), $n_{\rm ex}^{-2}=\cos^2\vartheta/n_o^2+\sin^2\vartheta/n_e^2$, where $\vartheta$ is the angle between the [wave vector](wavevector.md) and [optic axis](optic-axis.md). Along the axis $n_{\rm ex}=n_o$, while perpendicular propagation gives $n_{\rm ex}=n_e$.
