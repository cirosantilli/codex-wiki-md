# Spherical Hankel function

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical Bessel function](spherical-bessel-function.md)

These combinations of [Spherical Bessel functions](spherical-bessel-function.md) solve the radial [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md). For time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$, $h_n^{(1)}(kr)$ is outgoing and $h_n^{(2)}(kr)$ incoming. At fixed order and large argument, $h_n^{(1)}(t)\sim(-i)^{n+1}e^{it}/t$. Their recurrence is $h_{n+1}=(2n+1)h_n/t-h_{n-1}$.
