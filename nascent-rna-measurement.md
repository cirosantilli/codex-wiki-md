# Nascent RNA measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

Measurement of newly synthesized [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) to distinguish early transcriptional changes from accumulated [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) abundance. Short-interval perturbations help prioritize direct regulatory responses.
