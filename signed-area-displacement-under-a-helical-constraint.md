# Signed-area displacement under a helical constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Helical symmetry](helical-symmetry.md)

If zero conserved helical momentum reduces to $r^2\dot\theta+b\dot z=0$, a closed transverse projection $C$ has axial displacement $\Delta z=-b^{-1}\oint_C(x\,dy-y\,dx)=-2A/b$. Here $b\ne0$ and $A$ is the [signed area](signed-area.md), with winding multiplicity for self-intersections. This is a path-dependent displacement even when the transverse coordinates return to their initial values. It follows from the momentum constraint and [Green's theorem](green-theorem.md); it is not a prediction that every chosen closed curve is dynamically realizable.
