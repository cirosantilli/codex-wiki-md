# Chiral multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md)

A four-dimensional N=1 [chiral multiplet](chiral-multiplet.md) consists off shell of a [complex scalar](complex-scalar-field.md) $\phi$, a Weyl [fermion](fermion.md) $\psi_\alpha$, and a complex auxiliary scalar $F$. A [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md) packages their [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) transformations. Eliminating the auxiliary scalar leaves two bosonic and two fermionic physical polarizations; an allowed supersymmetric mass gives equal scalar and [fermion](fermion.md) masses.
