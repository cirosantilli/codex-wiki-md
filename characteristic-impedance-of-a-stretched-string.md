# Characteristic impedance of a stretched string

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md)

For the lossless small-slope [wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md), [tension](tension.md) $T$ and [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\mu$ give [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $c=\sqrt{T/\mu}$. With transverse [force](force-split.md) $F_y=-Ty_x$ exerted across a cut toward increasing $x$, a rightgoing displacement $y=f(x-ct)$ has [velocity](velocity.md) $v_y=-cy_x$. Its positive [mechanical impedance](mechanical-impedance.md) is therefore $Z=F_y/v_y=T/c=\sqrt{T\mu}$. A leftgoing component has $F_y/v_y=-Z$ under the same fixed orientation. A rightgoing [time-harmonic wave](time-harmonic-wave.md) with peak displacement [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $|A|$ carries mean [power](power.md) $Z\omega^2|A|^2/2$.
