# Tetrahedral quotient onto the cyclic group of order three

↑ **Parent:** [Tetrahedral symmetry](tetrahedral-symmetry.md)

The identity and three half-turns form a normal [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md) $V_4$ inside the proper rotational [tetrahedral symmetry](tetrahedral-symmetry.md) group $A_4$. Conjugation preserves double-transposition cycle type, establishing normality. The [quotient group](quotient-group.md) has order three and is therefore cyclic. If $r$ is any 3-cycle, every element is uniquely $r^jk$ with $j\in\{0,1,2\}$ and $k\in V_4$. The formula $r^jk\mapsto e^{2\pi ij/3}$ defines a surjective [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) with [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) $V_4$. Normality allows the factors from $V_4$ to be moved past powers of $r$, making exponents add modulo three and proving the homomorphism property directly.
