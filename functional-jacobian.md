# Functional Jacobian

↑ **Parent:** [Functional determinant](functional-determinant.md)

For a change of field variables $\phi\mapsto F[\phi]$, the functional Jacobian is the [functional determinant](functional-determinant.md) of the linearized change. At a finite regulator it is an ordinary [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) on the finitely many field coordinates; the continuum expression denotes the regulated limit. It changes the [functional integral](functional-measure.md) measure and can depend on the background field. The [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md) is the example obtained by replacing the integration coordinate along a [gauge orbit](gauge-orbit.md) by a chosen [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) condition.
