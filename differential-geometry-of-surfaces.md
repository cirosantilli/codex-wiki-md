# Differential geometry of surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_geometry_of_surfaces)

The differential geometry of smooth [smooth surfaces](smooth-surface.md) studies their induced [Riemannian metrics](riemannian-metric.md) and intrinsic or extrinsic [curvature](curvature.md). The [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md) measures lengths, while the [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) and [shape operator](shape-operator.md) describe bending in an ambient space.
