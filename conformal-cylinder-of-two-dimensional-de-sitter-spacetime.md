# Conformal cylinder of two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime](two-dimensional-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

The [conformal time](conformal-time.md) $T=2\arctan(e^t)-\pi/2$ satisfies $\sin T=\tanh t$ and $\cos T=\operatorname{sech}t$. Multiplying the physical metric by $\cos^2T$ gives a finite [conformal compactification](conformal-compactification.md) with periodic spatial coordinate. Its past and future conformal boundaries are spacelike circles. [Null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) have slopes $d\chi/dT=\pm1$.
