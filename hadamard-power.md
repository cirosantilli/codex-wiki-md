# Hadamard power

↑ **Parent:** [Hadamard product](hadamard-product.md)

For integer $k\geq1$, the entrywise power has entries $X_{ij}^k$ and equals the repeated [Hadamard product](hadamard-product.md). The zero power is the all-ones [matrix](matrix.md), even at zero entries, because it represents the constant function one. Every nonnegative integer [Hadamard power](hadamard-power.md) of a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) is [positive semidefinite](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) by the [Schur product theorem](schur-product-theorem.md). It is not an ordinary [matrix](matrix.md) power.
