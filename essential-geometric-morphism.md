# Essential geometric morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md)

A [geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md) is essential when its inverse image has an additional [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md). For a [functor](functor.md) of small categories, precomposition on presheaves has [left Kan extension](left-kan-extension.md) and [Right Kan extension](right-kan-extension.md) as its two adjoints. Precomposition preserves [finite limits](finite-limit.md) pointwise, so this is an essential [geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md).
