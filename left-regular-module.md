# Left regular module

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

The left regular [module](module-mathematics.md) of a unital [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) $A$ is its underlying additive group with $A$ acting by left multiplication. Its [submodules](submodule.md) are exactly the [left ideals](left-ideal.md) of $A$. The [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) is a [semisimple algebra](semisimple-algebra.md) precisely when this [module](module-mathematics.md) is a [semisimple module](semisimple-module.md).
