# Unwrapped reflectionless sine-Gordon transmission phase

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon breather spectrum at reflectionless couplings](sine-gordon-breather-spectrum-at-reflectionless-couplings.md)

For the transmitting product with factors $\cosh[(\theta-i\pi j/N)/2]/\cosh[(\theta+i\pi j/N)/2]$ and prefactor $(-1)^N$, the continuous phase on $\theta\geq0$ is $\pi N-2\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}\arctan[\tanh(\theta/2)\tan(\pi j/(2N))]$. Its derivative is $-\sum_j\sin(\pi j/N)/[\cosh\theta+\cos(\pi j/N)]$. A [Riemann sum](riemann-sum.md) at fixed positive [rapidity](rapidity.md) gives $(2N/\pi)\log\tanh(\theta/2)$ at leading order. The endpoints depend on the stated unwrapped branch; setting the high-energy value to zero silently changes the constant.
