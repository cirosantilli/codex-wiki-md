# Log odds ratio variance from a two-by-two table

↑ **Parent:** [Log odds ratio](log-odds-ratio.md)

For two independent [binomial distributions](binomial-distribution.md) with event and nonevent counts $a,b$ and $c,d$, the [odds ratio](odds-ratio.md) is $ad/(bc)$. The [delta method](delta-method.md) applied to $\log(p/(1-p))$, whose derivative is $1/[p(1-p)]$, gives a group variance $1/[Np(1-p)]$. Substitution of the fitted proportion gives $1/a+1/b$ in the first group and $1/c+1/d$ in the second. Their independence gives the displayed sum. A [normal approximation](normal-approximation.md) then gives a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) on the [log odds ratio](log-odds-ratio.md) scale; exponentiation produces an odds-ratio interval. Zero cells require another approximation or model.
