# Representable presheaves are the tiny objects of an idempotent-complete finite-product category

↑ **Parent:** [Tiny object](tiny-object.md)

If a small finite-product category $\mathcal C$ splits idempotents, the tiny objects of its [presheaf topos](presheaf-topos.md) are exactly the [representable presheaves](representable-functor.md). Exponentiation by $yA$ is precomposition with $-\times A$ and therefore has a right adjoint given by [Right Kan extension](right-kan-extension.md). Conversely, if $P$ is tiny, then $\operatorname{Nat}(P,-)$ preserves all colimits; idempotent completeness makes every such presheaf representable.
