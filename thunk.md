# Thunk

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language semantics](programming-language-semantics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunk)

A suspended computation together with the information needed to evaluate it later. In [lazy evaluation](lazy-evaluation.md) by need, forcing a [thunk](thunk.md) computes and records its result, so subsequent demands can reuse it. Deferral and memoization are distinct: a merely delayed computation need not share its result unless its evaluation mechanism provides that sharing.
