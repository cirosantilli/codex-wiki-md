# Uniform forcing of a periodic heat equation

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial mean of a periodic heat equation](spatial-mean-of-a-periodic-heat-equation.md)

For a spatially constant [PDE source term](source-term-in-a-partial-differential-equation.md) $Q(t)$, subtract its time integral from $u$. The result obeys the homogeneous [heat equation](heat-equation.md) with the same initial condition. This proves the displayed formula and shows that uniform heating changes only the [zero Fourier mode](zero-fourier-mode.md). In an unnormalised $N_x\times N_y$ [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md), a constant increment $q$ adds $N_xN_yq$ to the zero-frequency coefficient.
