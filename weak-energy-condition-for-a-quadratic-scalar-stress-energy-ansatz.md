# Weak energy condition for a quadratic scalar stress-energy ansatz

↑ **Parent:** [Weak energy condition](weak-energy-condition.md)

For the general scalar ansatz in [Conserved Klein-Gordon scalar stress-energy ansatz](conserved-klein-gordon-scalar-stress-energy-ansatz.md), work in a unit timelike frame. Then

$$
2T_{00}=(1+A)(\partial_0\phi)^2
-A|\nabla_{\rm s}\phi|^2-B\phi^2.
$$

The weak energy condition for arbitrary local field data is therefore equivalent to

$$
-1\leq A\leq0,
\qquad B\leq0.
$$
