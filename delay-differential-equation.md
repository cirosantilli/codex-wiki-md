# Delay differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Differential equation](differential-equation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delay_differential_equation)

A [delay differential equation](delay-differential-equation.md) relates a derivative at the current argument to values at earlier arguments. For example, the [Buchstab function](buchstab-function.md) obeys $\frac{d}{du}(u w(u))=w(u-1)$ for $u>2$.

The defining feature is memory: a [derivative](derivative.md) can depend on earlier values of the unknown [function](function-split.md). Constant delays, time-dependent delays, and delays depending on the unknown [function](function-split.md) belong to this wider class. The [retarded delay differential equation](retarded-delay-differential-equation.md) below isolates a single positive constant delay.[Skip Thompson, Delay-differential equations, opening definition and equation (1): constant and state-dependent delays](delay-differential-equations-by-skip-thompson.md)

The [Malthusian delay differential equation](malthusian-delay-differential-equation.md) is a simple linear member of this class. The [method of steps for a delay differential equation](method-of-steps-for-a-delay-differential-equation.md) constructs its future from a [history function of a delay differential equation](history-function-of-a-delay-differential-equation.md), and the [characteristic equation of a delay differential equation](characteristic-equation-of-a-delay-differential-equation.md) studies its [exponential function](exponential-function.md) modes. The [exact polynomial steps for a delay equation](exact-polynomial-steps-for-a-delay-equation.md) below make that construction explicit without approximating the [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md). Applications include [population dynamics](population-dynamics.md) and [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md); the [delayed negative feedback model](delayed-negative-feedback-model.md) and [feedback delay](feedback-delay.md) explain why the timing of a return matters as well as its sign.
