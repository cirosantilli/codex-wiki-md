# Causal cone for the constant-speed wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Finite propagation speed](finite-propagation-speed.md)

For $c>0$, the backward causal cone at $(x_*,t_*)$ is

$$
\mathcal C^-_c(x_*,t_*)=\{(x,t):0\leq t\leq t_*,\ |x-x_*|\leq c(t_*-t)\}.
$$

Its boundaries are [characteristic lines for the one-dimensional wave equation](characteristic-line-for-the-one-dimensional-wave-equation.md); its base on $t=0$ is the initial-data [domain of dependence](domain-of-dependence.md). The forward cone from $(s,0)$ is $t\geq0$, $|x-s|\leq ct$. Here the cone is set by the model's [wave speed](wave-speed.md), which may be acoustic or a string [wave speed](wave-speed.md); it does not assert propagation at the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md).
