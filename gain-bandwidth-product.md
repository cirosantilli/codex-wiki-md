# Gain-bandwidth product

↑ **Parent:** [Open-loop gain](open-loop-gain.md)

For a dominant-pole voltage-feedback [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md) with $A(s)=A_0/(1+s/\omega_a)$ and $A_0\gg1$, the unity-gain [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is approximately $\omega_t=A_0\omega_a$. In the range where this pole dominates, a roughly constant closed-loop noise gain $G_N$ gives a bandwidth approximately $f_t/G_N$. This approximation depends on the amplifier model and [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md) circuit; it is not a universal statement about all amplifiers or all frequency-dependent feedback networks. [Analog Devices, Activity: Op-Amp Gain Bandwidth Product, dominant-pole gain and unity-gain frequency](https://wiki.analog.com/university/courses/alm1k/alm-lab-gbw)
