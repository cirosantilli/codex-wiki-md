# Singlet helium state

↑ **Parent:** [Helium atom](helium-atom.md)

A [helium atom](helium-atom.md) electronic state with total [spin](spin.md) zero. Its antisymmetric two-spin function requires a symmetric spatial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md). Its approximate spectroscopic family is distinct from the triplet family under spin-conserving [electric dipole transitions](electric-dipole-transition.md).
