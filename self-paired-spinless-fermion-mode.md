# Self-paired spinless fermion mode

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu spinor](nambu-spinor.md)

In a unit-spacing periodic spinless chain, self-paired [momenta](momentum.md) are $k=0$ and, when available, $k=\pi$. An anomalous pair of the same [creation operator](creation-operator.md) vanishes by the [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md). Such a mode is a single normal number level, filled or empty according to its energy sign, rather than a two-distinct-mode [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md). This excludes the paired-angle construction at those special [momenta](momentum.md).
