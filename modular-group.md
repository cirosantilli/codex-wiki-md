# Modular group

↑ **Parent:** [Modular form](modular-form.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_group)

The modular group is $SL_2(\mathbb Z)$ acting on the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). Its central element $-I$ acts trivially on points but contributes the factor $(-1)^k$ in weight $k$.
