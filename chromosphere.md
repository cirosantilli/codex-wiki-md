# Chromosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar atmosphere](stellar-atmosphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromosphere)

A heated layer above a photosphere can have an outward [temperature](temperature.md) increase. Strong lines formed there may show emission where an outward-cooling photosphere would produce absorption. The energy maintaining the [temperature](temperature.md) inversion is additional atmospheric heating; the exact height and transfer regime vary between stars.
