# Quadratic finite-field norm and trace surjectivity

↑ **Parent:** [Finite field extension](finite-field-extension.md)

For $\mathbb F_{q^2}/\mathbb F_q$, the [field norm](field-norm.md) maps the nonzero elements onto $\mathbb F_q^*$: the [multiplicative group of a finite field is cyclic](multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field-is-cyclic.md), and the power $q+1$ has image of order $q-1$. The [field trace](field-trace.md) is also onto. Indeed it is an $\mathbb F_q$-linear map into $\mathbb F_q$, and is not zero because its polynomial $X+X^q$ has degree $q$ and cannot vanish at all $q^2$ field elements. Consequently $a\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}(ac)$ is onto whenever $c\ne0$.
