# Predictive discrepancy statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test.md)

A measurable summary of a possible dataset is chosen to detect disagreement with a specified predictive model. Compare its observed value with its distribution under replicated datasets. Large-discrepancy statistics can be checked with $\mathbb P(T(X^{\mathrm{rep}})\ge T(x))$. A [posterior predictive check](posterior-predictive-check.md) integrates over posterior parameter uncertainty; a fully specified null instead supplies a fixed reference law.
