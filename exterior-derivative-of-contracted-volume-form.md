# Exterior derivative of contracted volume form

↑ **Parent:** [Interior contraction of the Riemannian volume form](interior-contraction-of-the-riemannian-volume-form.md)

At a point in a [normal orthonormal frame](normal-orthonormal-frame.md), torsion-freeness makes the frame brackets vanish there, hence the dual one-forms have zero [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) there. Differentiating the [interior product](interior-product.md) formula then leaves only $\sum_iE_i(f_i)\omega_g$, the [divergence of a Riemannian vector field](divergence-of-a-riemannian-vector-field.md). Since the point is arbitrary, the identity holds globally.
