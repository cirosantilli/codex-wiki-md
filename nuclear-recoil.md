# Nuclear recoil

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)

Movement of an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) after it receives [momentum](momentum-split.md), for example in a collision. A finite nuclear [mass](mass.md) requires sharing [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) as well as [momentum](momentum-split.md); the fixed-target approximation neglects this motion.
