# Gravitational stress contribution to angular-momentum transport

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational stress tensor](newtonian-gravitational-stress-tensor.md)

In cylindrical coordinates, the [Newtonian gravitational stress tensor](newtonian-gravitational-stress-tensor.md) gives radial [angular-momentum flux](angular-momentum-flux.md) $rT_{r\phi}=r(\partial_r\Phi)(r^{-1}\partial_\phi\Phi)/(4\pi G)$. Nonaxisymmetric structure can correlate the field components and transport [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). The isotropic negative-pressure part has zero off-diagonal component, so it gives no direct radial transport. This separates a shear flux from a contribution to normal force balance.
