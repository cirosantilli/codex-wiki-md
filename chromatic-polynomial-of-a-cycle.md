# Chromatic polynomial of a cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md)

For a [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md) of length $\ell\geq3$, the [chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md) is $p_{C_\ell}(x)=(x-1)^\ell+(-1)^\ell(x-1)$. The [graph triangle](triangle-in-a-graph.md) gives the base case $x(x-1)(x-2)$. For $\ell\geq4$, the [deletion-contraction recurrence for the chromatic polynomial](deletion-contraction-recurrence-for-the-chromatic-polynomial.md) gives $p_{C_\ell}(x)=x(x-1)^{\ell-1}-p_{C_{\ell-1}}(x)$, since deleting an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) leaves a [path graph](path-graph.md) and contracting it gives the shorter [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md). Substitution proves the formula by [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md).
