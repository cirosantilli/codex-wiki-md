# Hypervirial density-potential family

↑ **Parent:** [Hypervirial model](hypervirial-model.md)

For $p>0$, the relative potential $GM/(a^p+r^p)^{1/p}$ generates density $(p+1)Ma^pr^{p-2}/[4\pi(a^p+r^p)^{2+1/p}]$. In unit scales the separable [augmented density](augmented-stellar-density.md) is $(p+1)r^{p-2}\psi^{2p+1}/(4\pi)$. Its [Jeans moments from an augmented density](jeans-moments-from-an-augmented-density.md) give constant anisotropy $\beta=1-p/2$.
