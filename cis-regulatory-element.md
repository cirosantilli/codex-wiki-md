# Cis-regulatory element

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

A sequence element acting on expression or processing of the molecule or locus carrying it. A binding motif in an [intron](intron.md) can regulate nearby [alternative splicing](alternative-splicing.md), with its effect depending on position and the available [RNA-binding proteins](rna-binding-protein.md).
