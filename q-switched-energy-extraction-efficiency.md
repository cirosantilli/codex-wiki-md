# Q-switched energy extraction efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Q-switching](q-switching.md)

For an ideal pulse with negligible stored seed [photons](photon.md) and useful output accounting for all [cavity](optical-cavity.md) [photon](photon.md) loss, the extracted [energy](energy-split.md) is $(N_i^*-N_f^*)V_g\hbar\omega_L/\beta$. The factor $\beta=1+g_2/g_1$ converts inversion depletion into stimulated transitions. With over-pumping $r=N_i^*/N_{\rm th}^*$, the nonzero extraction branch satisfies $r\beta\eta=-\ln(1-\beta\eta)$. A fraction $0.95$ of the maximum [energy](energy-split.md) requires $r=-\ln(0.05)/0.95$.
