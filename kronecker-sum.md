# Kronecker sum

↑ **Parent:** [Kronecker product](kronecker-product.md)

The [Kronecker sum](kronecker-sum.md) of square [matrices](matrix.md) combines their [Kronecker products](kronecker-product.md) with [identity matrices](identity-matrix.md). If $Ax=\lambda x$ and $By=\mu y$, then $x\otimes y$ is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda+\mu$. Tensor-product spatial grids therefore turn separated coordinate Laplacians into a [Kronecker sum](kronecker-sum.md). Two [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) give a Hermitian [Kronecker sum](kronecker-sum.md), allowing an [energy method](energy-method.md) without computing every eigenvector.
