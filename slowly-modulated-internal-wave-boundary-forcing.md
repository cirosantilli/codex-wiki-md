# Slowly modulated internal-wave boundary forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary-forced internal gravity wave](boundary-forced-internal-gravity-wave.md)

For a [plane internal gravity wave](plane-internal-gravity-wave.md) in uniform [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md), substitute $\zeta=A(\mu t,\mu z)e^{i(kx+mz-\omega t)}$ into the displacement equation. The leading order gives $\omega^2=N^2k^2/(k^2+m^2)$; the next order gives the displayed transport equation with $c_{gz}=-\omega m/(k^2+m^2)$. A boundary amplitude $a(T)$ radiates as $A=a(T-Z/c_{gz})$, choosing the sign of $m$ so the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) points into the fluid. The wavelength-averaged energy is $N^2|A|^2/2$ and its vertical flux is $c_{gz}$ times that energy. A causal ramp therefore creates a translating front, not an instantaneous monochromatic field throughout the half-space.
