# Pathwise quadratic variation distinguishes Brownian speeds

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md)

On $[0,T]$, a standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) has pathwise [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) $t$, whereas the time-rescaled process $B_{ct}$ has quadratic variation $ct$. Therefore their path laws are mutually singular when $c\ne1$.
