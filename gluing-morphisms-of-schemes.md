# Gluing morphisms of schemes

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md)

If $X=\bigcup_i U_i$ is an [open cover](open-cover.md) by [open subschemes](open-subscheme.md), and [morphisms of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) $f_i:U_i\to Y$ agree on every overlap, there is a unique [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) $f:X\to Y$ restricting to them. The underlying [continuous maps](continuous-map.md) glue because their restrictions agree. The maps of [structure sheaves](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) glue using the [sheaf gluing axiom](sheaf-gluing-axiom.md), and the required local condition on [stalks](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) can be checked on the original cover. No affineness or separation assumption on $X$, $Y$, or the overlaps is needed. This is the local construction behind [affine test schemes determine scheme morphisms](affine-test-schemes-determine-scheme-morphisms.md).
