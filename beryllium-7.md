# Beryllium-7

↑ **Parent:** [Beryllium](beryllium.md)

Beryllium-7 contains four [protons](proton.md) and three [neutrons](neutron.md). It forms in reactions including $^3\mathrm{He}+{}^4\mathrm{He}\to{}^7\mathrm{Be}+\gamma$ and becomes [lithium-7](lithium-7.md) through [electron capture](electron-capture.md). In the early universe its ionization state affects when capture occurs, so the final primordial lithium inventory includes the earlier beryllium inventory rather than just lithium present when nuclear burning ends.
