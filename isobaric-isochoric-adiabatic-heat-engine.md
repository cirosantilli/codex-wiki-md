# Isobaric-isochoric-adiabatic heat engine

↑ **Parent:** [Heat engine](heat-engine.md)

Consider an ideal-gas cycle with constant heat-capacity ratio $\gamma>1$: expand isobarically from $V_1$ to $rV_1$, cool isochorically, then compress reversibly and adiabatically to the initial state. With $P_*=p_1V_1=nRT_1$, closure gives $T_2=rT_1$ and $T_3=r^{1-\gamma}T_1$. The [heat](heat-split.md) input is $Q_h=\gamma P_*(r-1)/(\gamma-1)$ and the rejected [heat](heat-split.md) magnitude is $Q_c=P_*(r-r^{1-\gamma})/(\gamma-1)$. Thus $\eta=1-(r-r^{1-\gamma})/[\gamma(r-1)]$. Reservoir temperatures determine feasibility and [entropy](entropy-split.md) generation but need not coincide with the gas temperatures at heat-exchange endpoints.
