# Finite subgroups of the plane orthogonal group

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md)

A finite [subgroup](subgroup.md) of $O(2)$ is cyclic or dihedral. Its orientation-preserving part consists of [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) and is cyclic: choose the smallest positive rotation angle, divide any other angle by it with remainder, and use minimality to force zero remainder. The smallest angle is therefore $2\pi/n$ for some $n$. A trivial rotational subgroup is included by $n=1$.

If there is no orientation-reversing element, this is the whole [group](group-split.md). Otherwise choose a [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) $s$. Every orientation-reversing element is $r^js$, where $r$ generates the rotations; these form the second [coset](coset.md). Since $srs=r^{-1}$, the group is a [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of order $2n$. Its involutions are all $n$ reflections and, when $n$ is even, the extra half-turn $r^{n/2}$. Consequently it has $n$ order-two [cyclic subgroups](cyclic-subgroup.md) for odd $n$, and $n+1$ for even $n$.
