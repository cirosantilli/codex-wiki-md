# Differential Manchester encoding

↑ **Parent:** [Line code](line-code.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_Manchester_encoding)

[Differential Manchester encoding](differential-manchester-encoding.md) has a transition at every [bit](bit.md) midpoint and encodes the data by the presence or absence of a boundary transition. A consistent signal polarity reversal preserves those transition decisions.
