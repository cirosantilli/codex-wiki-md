# Sampling with replacement

↑ **Parent:** [Probability sampling](probability-sampling.md)

[Sampling with replacement](sampling-with-replacement.md) returns each selected label to the available population before the next draw. Independent uniform draws from a fixed population are [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md); a label can recur. Replacement by itself does not imply uniformity or [independence](independent-random-variables.md).
