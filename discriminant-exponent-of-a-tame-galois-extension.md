# Discriminant exponent of a tame Galois extension

↑ **Parent:** [Tamely ramified extension](tamely-ramified-extension.md)

If $L/K$ is a finite tamely ramified [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of local fields with degree $n$ and [ramification index](ramification-index.md) $e$, then its different exponent is $e-1$ and the discriminant exponent is

$$
v_K(\operatorname{disc}(L/K))=n\left(1-\frac1e\right).
$$
