# IP-star filter

↑ **Parent:** [IP-star set](ip-star-set.md)

The [IP-star sets](ip-star-set.md) form a proper [filter on a set](filter-set-theory.md) over the [positive integers](positive-integer.md). Indeed, sets that are not [IP sets](ip-set.md) are closed under taking subsets. They are also closed under finite unions, because the [partition regularity of IP sets](partition-regularity-of-ip-sets.md) would otherwise put an IP set in one of the union's constituent sets. Taking complements gives upward closure and closure under finite intersections for the [IP-star sets](ip-star-set.md). The full set belongs to this filter and the empty set does not.
