# Constant-tension balloon discharge

↑ **Parent:** [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md)

A spherical balloon feeding an inviscid uniform tube plug, with entrance excess [pressure](pressure.md) $2\gamma/R$, satisfies $L\dot u=2\gamma/(\rho R)$ and $4\pi R^2\dot R=-\pi a^2u$. Eliminating $u$ yields the displayed equation. Its [first integral](first-integral.md) is $R^4\dot R^2+KR^2=C$. From rest at radius $R_0$, $C=KR_0^2$; integration gives $t=R_0^2K^{-1/2}(\pi/4-\theta/2+\sin(2\theta)/4)$, where $\theta=\arcsin(R/R_0)$. The ideal emptying time is $\pi R_0^2/(4\sqrt K)$. Independently prescribed initial flow changes $C$ and therefore this time.
