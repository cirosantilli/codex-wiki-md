<h1 id="broca-s-area">Broca's area</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Inferior frontal gyrus](inferior-frontal-gyrus.md)

A traditional label for left inferior-frontal territory, commonly associated with areas 44 and 45. It is heterogeneous: [language](language.md)-selective and domain-general regions can lie beside one another, and an anatomical label does not establish a single [syntax](syntax.md) function. The name commemorates [Paul Broca](paul-broca.md).
