# Fused Lasso

↑ **Parent:** [Lasso](lasso.md)

The fused Lasso adds an [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) penalty on adjacent coefficient differences to a squared-error objective, optionally together with an [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) penalty on the coefficients themselves. The differences penalty encourages neighbouring coefficients to coincide.
