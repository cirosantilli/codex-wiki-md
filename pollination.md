# Pollination

↑ **Parent:** [Sexual reproduction](sexual-reproduction.md)

In [angiosperms](angiosperm.md), [pollination](pollination.md) transfers [pollen](pollen.md) from an [anther](anther.md) to a [stigma](stigma-botany.md). [Fertilization](fertilization.md) is a later fusion of [gametes](gamete.md); [seed](seed.md) development follows. [Pollination](pollination.md) may use animals, wind or water, so an insect visit is neither required for every [flower](flower.md) nor sufficient evidence of [fertilization](fertilization.md).[Biology 2e, §32.2 opening definition, Pollination by Wind and Pollination by Water](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/32-2-pollination-and-fertilization.md)
