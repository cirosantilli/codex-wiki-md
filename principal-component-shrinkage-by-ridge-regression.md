# Principal-component shrinkage by ridge regression

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-form ridge regression estimator](closed-form-ridge-regression-estimator.md)

For $X^TX=V\Lambda V^T$, [ridge regression](ridge-regression.md) multiplies the fitted response along the $i$th [normalized sample principal component](normalized-sample-principal-component.md) by the shrinkage factor $\Lambda_{ii}/(\Lambda_{ii}+\lambda)$. Directions with variance much larger than $\lambda$ are nearly retained, while directions with variance much smaller than $\lambda$ are nearly removed.
