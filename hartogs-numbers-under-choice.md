# Hartogs numbers under choice

↑ **Parent:** [Hartogs number](hartogs-number.md)

For infinite [sets](set-split.md) in [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md), the [Hartogs number](hartogs-number.md) is the successor of the input cardinal. Every smaller ordinal injects into the input, whereas that successor does not. An infinite [successor cardinal](successor-cardinal.md) $\kappa^+$ is regular: a cofinal family of at most $\kappa$ ordinals below it would have union of cardinal at most $\kappa\cdot\kappa=\kappa$, a contradiction. Finite inputs give finite successor cardinals, so the formulation in terms of alephs requires an infinite input. The general [Hartogs theorem](hartogs-theorem.md) itself does not require [choice](axiom-of-choice.md).
