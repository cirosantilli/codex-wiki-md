# Hoyle state

↑ **Parent:** [Triple-alpha process](triple-alpha-process.md)

This excited carbon-12 resonance lies close to the three-alpha threshold and enables the [Triple-alpha process](triple-alpha-process.md) to make carbon efficiently. A transient beryllium-8 nucleus captures an alpha particle into the resonance; electromagnetic decay then stabilizes carbon-12. A small radiative branching probability still matters because the resonance enhances formation.
