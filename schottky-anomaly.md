# Schottky anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schottky_anomaly)

A [Schottky anomaly](schottky-anomaly.md) is a peak in [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) caused by thermal occupation of a small set of separated [energy levels](energy-level.md). For $N$ independent two-level units with equal degeneracies and gap $\Delta>0$, write $x=\Delta/(k_BT)$; their [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) is $C=Nk_Bx^2e^{-x}/(1+e^{-x})^2$, with [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md) $k_B$. It vanishes at both low and high [temperature](temperature-split.md) and can spoil a lattice $T^3$ fit in between. Localized electronic or nuclear levels can contribute, including in [calorimeter addenda](calorimeter-addenda.md); their presence must be checked rather than attributed automatically to [phonons](phonon.md).
