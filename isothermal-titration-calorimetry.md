# Isothermal titration calorimetry

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

[Isothermal titration calorimetry](isothermal-titration-calorimetry.md) measures heat accompanying additions of one binding partner to another at controlled temperature. With suitable concentration ranges, stoichiometry and a binding model, it can estimate affinity and binding enthalpy; dilution and buffer effects need controls.
