# Truncated normal distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Truncated distribution](truncated-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_normal_distribution)

A truncated normal distribution is a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) conditioned to lie in a specified subset of the real line. Its density is the original Gaussian density divided by the probability of the retained set.
