# Concavity bound on past FLRW evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Strong energy condition in a Friedmann universe](strong-energy-condition-in-a-friedmann-universe.md)

In an [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) with zero separate [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), $\varrho+3p\ge0$ makes the [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) imply $\ddot a\le0$. If $a_0=a(t_0)>0$ and $v_0=\dot a(t_0)>0$, then the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) is a [concave function](concave-function.md) and

$$
a(t)\le a_0-v_0(t_0-t)
$$

throughout its regular past interval. Thus a regular positive [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) cannot persist earlier than $t_0-a_0/v_0$. If the solution continues regularly whenever $a>0$, its first past zero occurs within $a_0/v_0=H(t_0)^{-1}$, where $H$ is the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md).

The continuation condition matters: without restrictions on the [equation of state](equation-of-state.md), an earlier [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md) can occur at positive $a$. For example, in fixed units $a(t)=1+\sqrt t$, $t>0$, with $k=0$, defines [energy density](energy-density.md) $\varrho=3(\dot a/a)^2/(8\pi G)$ and [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p=-\varrho/3-\ddot a/(4\pi Ga)$. These satisfy the [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md) and $\varrho+3p>0$, yet $a\to1$ at the singular past boundary. Conversely the [Milne model](milne-model.md) reaches zero without divergent [curvature](curvature.md). A finite duration, a zero of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md), and a [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md) are distinct conclusions.
