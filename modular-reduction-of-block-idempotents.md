# Modular reduction of block idempotents

↑ **Parent:** [Block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md)

For a complete [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) $\mathcal O$ with residue [field](field.md) $k$, class sums give bases of $Z(\mathcal OG)$ and $Z(kG)$, so reduction of the [center of an associative algebra](center-of-an-associative-algebra.md) is surjective. [Idempotent lifting](idempotent-lifting.md) in this complete commutative [algebra](algebra-split.md) gives a bijection of primitive [central idempotents](central-idempotent.md), hence of [blocks of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md). Corresponding [blocks of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) have the same [defect groups of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md): [relative traces](relative-trace.md) reduce, and a lifted trace whose reduction is the block identity is a unit in its complete block center.
