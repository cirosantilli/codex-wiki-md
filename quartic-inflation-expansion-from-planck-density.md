# Quartic-inflation expansion from Planck density

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic-potential slow-roll solution](quartic-potential-slow-roll-solution.md)

With unreduced [Planck mass](planck-mass.md) $M_P$ and initial potential-dominated density $\lambda\phi_i^4/4\sim M_P^4$, one has $\phi_i^2\sim2M_P^2/\sqrt\lambda$. The [slow-roll e-fold count](slow-roll-e-fold-count.md) is $N\simeq\pi(\phi_i^2-\phi_e^2)/M_P^2$. Thus small positive $\lambda$ produces a very large expansion factor $e^N$ in this classical quartic model. The order-one endpoint correction is negligible compared with $2\pi/\sqrt\lambda$; initial [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), spatial curvature and the model's validity at trans-Planckian field values are independent assumptions.
