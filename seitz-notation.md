# Seitz notation

↑ **Parent:** [Space group](space-group.md)

Write a Euclidean [isometry](isometry.md) as $\{R\mid t\}$, acting by $x\mapsto Rx+t$. Composition is $\{R\mid t\}\{R^{\prime}\mid t^{\prime}\}=\{RR^{\prime}\mid Rt^{\prime}+t\}$. The [translation subgroup](translation-subgroup.md) is normal; the quotient is the [crystallographic point group](crystallographic-point-group.md).
