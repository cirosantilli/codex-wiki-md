# Normal modes of two arc-spring-coupled beads on a hoop

↑ **Parent:** [Small oscillation](small-oscillation.md)

Two frictionless beads of positive [masses](mass.md) $m_1,m_2$ on a circular hoop are joined by two identical [springs](spring.md) of [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k$, constrained to follow the two arcs. At their diametrically opposite equilibrium, each [spring](spring.md) has natural length equal to half the circumference. For signed [arc length](arc-length.md) displacements in the same rotational sense, the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is $U=k(x_1-x_2)^2$. The [normal mode](normal-mode.md) of common [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) has [mode shape](mode-shape.md) $(1,1)$ and zero [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), while the other [normal mode](normal-mode.md) has [mode shape](mode-shape.md) $(1,-m_1/m_2)$ and the positive [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) in the title.

Adding the equations of motion shows that $X=(m_1x_1+m_2x_2)/(m_1+m_2)$ satisfies $\ddot X=0$. Subtracting them gives $\ddot\delta+\omega_1^2\delta=0$ for $\delta=x_1-x_2$. Thus the complete [zero mode](zero-mode.md) is $X=C+Dt$, including uniform [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md), and the relative motion is a [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md). In the limit $m_2/m_1\to\infty$ at fixed $m_1,k$, the oscillatory motion of the heavier bead vanishes for fixed lighter-bead [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md), and $\omega_1\to\sqrt{2k/m_1}$. A common drift still survives if its initial [velocity](velocity.md) is held fixed.
