# Prefix of a list

↑ **Parent:** [List (abstract data type)](list-abstract-data-type.md)

An initial segment of a [list](list-abstract-data-type.md), including the empty segment and, for finite lists, the whole list. A proper prefix excludes the whole list. Fully materialising all prefixes of a length-$n$ list needs $\Theta(n^2)$ element constructors, although the outer list of prefixes has only $n+1$ entries.
