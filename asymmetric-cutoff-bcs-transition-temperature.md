# Asymmetric-cutoff BCS transition temperature

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

For a constant effective [density of states](density-of-states.md) and attractive coupling $\lambda$, let the linearized [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md) gap equation integrate $\tanh(\xi/(2k_BT_c))/(2\xi)$ over $-\sigma<\xi<\theta$, with positive cutoffs. Its even integrand makes the result the mean of two positive-cutoff logarithms. When $k_BT_c\ll\sigma,\theta$, $1/\lambda\simeq\frac12\ln(1.13\theta/(k_BT_c))+\frac12\ln(1.13\sigma/(k_BT_c))$, giving the displayed geometric mean of cutoffs.

If a band bottom truncates the lower side, $\sigma=\min(\mu,\theta)$ for positive [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) $\mu$. The weak-coupling limit at fixed $\mu/\theta>0$ must not be interchanged with an uncontrolled $\mu\to0$ extrapolation: the lower logarithm requires $\mu\gg k_BT_c$ when $\mu<\theta$.
