# Group of order eight

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group theory](finite-group-theory-split.md)

Up to [group isomorphism](group-isomorphism.md), the [groups of order eight](group-of-order-eight.md) are $C_8$, $C_4\times C_2$, $C_2^3$, $D_8$ and $Q_8$, with $D_8$ here denoting the eight-element [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md). An element of order eight gives the first case. If every nonidentity element has order two the [group](group-split.md) is an [elementary abelian group](elementary-abelian-group.md). Otherwise, after excluding elements of order eight, a [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md) of order four has index two; an element outside it either commutes with its generator or inverts it. In the latter case its square is either the identity or the central [involution](involution.md), giving the [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) or [quaternion group](quaternion-group.md).
