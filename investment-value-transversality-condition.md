# Investment value transversality condition

↑ **Parent:** [Investment-consumption problem](investment-consumption-problem.md)

A discounted-value transversality condition rules out residual value at infinity in an infinite-horizon [Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation](hamilton-jacobi-bellman-equation.md) verification. Under appropriate integrability, admissibility, and localization assumptions, $\mathbb E[e^{-\rho T}V(w_T,s_T)]\to0$ identifies the economic value among formal differential-equation solutions.
