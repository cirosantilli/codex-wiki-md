# Generalized momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

The generalized, or conjugate, momentum associated with a coordinate $q_i$ is

$$
p_i=\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_i}.
$$

In [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md), the coordinate variable describes the configuration; its conjugate momentum need not equal the Cartesian mechanical momentum.
