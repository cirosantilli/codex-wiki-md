# Kronecker lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Series (mathematics)](series-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kronecker_lemma)

Let $(b_n)$ be positive and increase to infinity. If the [series](series-mathematics.md) $\sum_{n\geq1}x_n/b_n$ converges, then

$$
\frac1{b_n}\sum_{k=1}^nx_k\longrightarrow0.
$$

This follows from [summation by parts](abel-s-summation-formula.md): write $x_k/b_k$ as a convergent series and average its tails with the increasing weights $b_k$.
