# Anoxygenic photosynthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anoxygenic_photosynthesis)

[Anoxygenic photosynthesis](anoxygenic-photosynthesis.md) captures [light](light.md) without producing [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md). It occurs in some [bacteria](bacteria.md) and uses [electron](electron.md) sources other than [water](water.md). Whether [carbon fixation](carbon-fixation.md) accompanies this [light](light.md) capture depends on the [organism](organism-split.md) and conditions; [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md) does not imply one universal [carbon](carbon.md)-assimilation pathway. See [Alberts et al.: chloroplasts and photosynthetic bacteria](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26819/).
