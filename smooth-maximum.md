# Smooth maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Log-sum-exp function](log-sum-exp-function.md)

Composing the [log-sum-exp function](log-sum-exp-function.md) with finitely many [affine functions](affine-function.md) gives a differentiable approximation to their pointwise maximum. Increasing the inverse-temperature parameter $\beta$ reduces the uniform approximation error while increasing the [Lipschitz gradient](lipschitz-gradient.md) constant.
