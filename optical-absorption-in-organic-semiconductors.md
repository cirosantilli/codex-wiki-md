# Optical absorption in organic semiconductors

↑ **Parent:** [Organic semiconductor](organic-semiconductor.md)

Molecular excitations in [organic semiconductors](organic-semiconductor.md) often retain strong local character. Weak screening supports [Frenkel excitons](frenkel-exciton.md) or intermolecular charge-transfer admixtures, so a photon can create a bound neutral excitation rather than free carriers. Transition dipoles, [spin selection rules](spin-selection-rule.md), molecular packing and [Franck-Condon factors](franck-condon-factor.md) set the bright spectral features. Disorder and thermal vibrations broaden them. Electronic overlap can also produce more delocalized excitons; the molecular limit is a useful model, not a definition covering every organic solid.
