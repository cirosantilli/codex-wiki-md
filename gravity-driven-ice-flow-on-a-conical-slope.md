# Gravity-driven ice flow on a conical slope

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow-ice approximation](shallow-ice-approximation.md)

For a thin [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) film on a conical slope at angle $\alpha$, neglecting the thickness-gradient contribution to the driving pressure gives [volume flux per unit width](volume-flux-per-unit-width.md) $q=Dh^3$, where $D=g\sin\alpha/(3\nu)$. Radial [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) is

$$
h_t+\frac1x\partial_x(xDh^3)=a(x).
$$

The distance $x$ is measured along the slope; the horizontal radius is $x\cos\alpha$, so total volume is $2\pi\cos\alpha\int xh\,dx$.
