# Gaussian saddle coalescing with a simple pole

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes phenomenon](stokes-phenomenon.md)

For $z=2n+i\mu\sqrt n+O(1)$, the factor $t^{2n}e^{z(1-t)}$ has saddle scale $t=1+u/\sqrt n$ and becomes $e^{-u^2-i\mu u}$. If the amplitude has a simple pole there, its leading local integral contains $e^{-u^2-i\mu u}/u$. Differentiating its [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) with respect to $\mu$ reduces it to a [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md), producing an [error function](error-function.md). The nonsingular amplitude $(1+t)^{-1}$ gives $\frac12\sqrt{\pi/n}e^{-\mu^2/4}$.
