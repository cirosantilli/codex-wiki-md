↑ **Parent:** [Countable set](countable-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor's_diagonal_argument)

Cantor's diagonal argument proves that the infinite binary sequences form an [uncountable set](uncountable-set.md): a sequence obtained by changing the $n$th digit of the $n$th listed sequence differs from every sequence in the list.
