# Alternating-radius square circle packing

↑ **Parent:** [Circle packing](circle-packing.md)

Place equal numbers of radii $r_1,r_2$ on alternating sites of a square nearest-neighbour lattice of spacing $d$. Nonoverlap requires $d\ge\max(r_1+r_2,\sqrt2r_1,\sqrt2r_2)$. A two-circle cell has area $2d^2$, giving the displayed [packing fraction](packing-fraction.md). Its maximum within this geometry occurs at large-to-small radius ratio $1+\sqrt2$ and is $\pi(1-1/\sqrt2)$; smaller interstitial circles leave unused space, while larger ones force the large-circle lattice apart.
