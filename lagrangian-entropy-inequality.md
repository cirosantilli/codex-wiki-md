# Lagrangian entropy inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Clausius-Duhem inequality](clausius-duhem-inequality.md)

The local [entropy](entropy.md) balance has nonnegative production, with heat supply $\rho_0r/\theta$ and [thermodynamic entropy flux](thermodynamic-entropy-flux.md) $q/\theta$ at positive [temperature](temperature.md) $\theta$. Its integral version holds on every material reference subvolume.
