# Sar1

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[Sar1](sar1.md) is a small GTPase that initiates [COPII](copii.md) assembly. In its activated state an N-terminal amphipathic helix inserts into the cytosolic membrane leaflet, contributing to curvature and coat recruitment.
