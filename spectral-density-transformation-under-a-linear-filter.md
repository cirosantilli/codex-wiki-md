# Spectral density transformation under a linear filter

↑ **Parent:** [Linear filter of a stationary time series](linear-filter-of-a-stationary-time-series.md)

Filtering multiplies the [time-series spectral density](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) by the squared modulus of the frequency response. Insert the spectral integral into the [covariance](covariance.md) double sum and interchange sums using absolute summability of the filter. This holds even when the input [covariances](covariance.md) are not absolutely summable, provided the spectral measure has a density.
