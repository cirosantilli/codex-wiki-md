# Uniformized sample range

↑ **Parent:** [Order statistic](order-statistic.md)

For an iid sample of size $n\geq2$ from a continuous [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md), the [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md) makes the transformed sample uniform. Integrating the joint density of its minimum and maximum gives range density $n(n-1)r^{n-2}(1-r)$ on $0<r<1$. Its distribution function is $nr^{n-1}-(n-1)r^n$. The result describes population content between the extreme [order statistics](order-statistic.md), independently of the underlying continuous distribution.
