# Closure of a family of statistical hypotheses

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection hypothesis](intersection-hypothesis.md)

The closure is the collection of all nonempty [intersection hypotheses](intersection-hypothesis.md) from the original elementary family, including singletons. It is not a topological closure. The [closed testing procedure](closed-testing-procedure.md) requires local level-$\alpha$ tests for these intersections and rejects an elementary hypothesis only when all intersections containing it reject.
