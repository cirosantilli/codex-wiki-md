# Strong phase

↑ **Parent:** [Hadronic final-state interaction](hadronic-final-state-interaction.md)

A phase in a [hadronic matrix element](hadronic-matrix-element.md) generated by [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md) dynamics, including rescattering of final states. It does not change sign under conjugation of the weak [coupling constant](coupling-constant-physics.md). Interference measurements depend on relative [strong phases](strong-phase.md), rather than their common overall value.
