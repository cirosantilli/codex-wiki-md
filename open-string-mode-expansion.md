# Open-string mode expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Open string](open-string.md)

In [conformal gauge](conformal-gauge.md), a free open-string coordinate with [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) has a cosine expansion. Canonical quantization turns its nonzero Fourier coefficients into [string oscillators](string-oscillator.md).
