# Minimal supported subobject

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection of subobjects](intersection-of-subobjects.md)

For a limit-preserving [functor](functor.md) $U$ and an arrow $d:D\to UC$, a supported [subobject](subobject.md) is $m:M\hookrightarrow C$ through which $d$ factors after applying $U$. In a complete well-powered domain, their intersection remains supported. At the resulting pair $(C_0,d_0)$, every supported [subobject](subobject.md) of $C_0$ is invertible. This minimality makes $q\mapsto U(q)d_0$ injective on morphisms to each cogenerator.
