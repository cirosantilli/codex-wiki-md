# Moment of inertia of a uniform solid hemisphere

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

The centroid of a uniform solid hemisphere of radius $a$ lies $3a/8$ from its flat face. About a diameter of the underlying sphere parallel to that face, its [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) is $2ma^2/5$. The [parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md) therefore gives $I_G=2ma^2/5-m(3a/8)^2=83ma^2/320$ about the parallel centroidal axis. A thin hemispherical shell has a different centroid and inertia.
