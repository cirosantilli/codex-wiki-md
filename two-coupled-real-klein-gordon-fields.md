# Two coupled real Klein-Gordon fields

↑ **Parent:** [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md)

For interaction [density](density.md) $g\phi_1\phi_2$, the combinations $\phi_\pm=\phi_1\pm\phi_2$ diagonalize the equations into independent [Klein-Gordon equations](klein-gordon-equation.md) with the displayed [mass](mass.md) squares. When $0<g<m^2$, all Fourier [frequencies](frequency.md) are real and nonzero. [Initial data](initial-data-in-general-relativity.md) in one original field generally excite both normal combinations.
