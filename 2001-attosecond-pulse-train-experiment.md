# 2001 attosecond pulse-train experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Reconstruction of attosecond beating by interference of two-photon transitions](reconstruction-of-attosecond-beating-by-interference-of-two-photon-transitions.md)

[Pierre Agostini](pierre-agostini.md)'s experiment illuminated an [argon](argon.md) jet with an annular $800\,\mathrm{nm}$ beam. A pinhole removed the annulus; a tungsten-coated mirror relayed harmonics and weak central reference into another [argon](argon.md) jet. A magnetic-bottle [electron spectrometer](electron-spectrometer.md) used [time of flight](time-of-flight.md) and [microchannel plate detectors](microchannel-plate-detector.md). Glass plates varied delay. Reference-on sidebands contrasted with reference-off peaks. Atomic-phase-corrected reconstruction yielded $250$-attosecond bursts.[pp.1690–1691, Figs.1–4 and Table 1](observation-of-a-train-of-attosecond-pulses-from-high-harmonic-generation-by-p-m-paul-and-others.md)

The [two-path attosecond phase reconstruction](two-path-attosecond-phase-reconstruction.md) explains the control's purpose: adding the reference opens an interference channel. Its fitted oscillation tests phase relationships under the weak-field and atomic-response model. It does not independently reveal every target phase or an arbitrary nonperiodic [optical pulse](optical-pulse.md).
