# Four-wavevector

↑ **Parent:** [Four-vector](four-vector.md)

The [four-wavevector](four-wavevector.md) combines the [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) and spatial [wavevector](wavevector.md) of a plane wave. Phase invariance gives $K'^\mu=\Lambda^\mu{}_{\nu}K^\nu$. Under a boost $\mathbf v$, $\omega'=\gamma(\omega-\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf k)$, $\mathbf k'_\parallel=\gamma(\mathbf k_\parallel-\omega\mathbf v/c^2)$ and $\mathbf k'_\perp=\mathbf k_\perp$. For metric signature $(+,-,-,-)$, $R_\mu K^\mu=\omega t-\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r$ is a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md). A vacuum light wave has $K_\mu K^\mu=0$.
