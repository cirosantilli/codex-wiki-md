# Classical Ising chain as a quantum particle

↑ **Parent:** [Transfer matrix for a classical spin chain](transfer-matrix-for-a-classical-spin-chain.md)

Hubbard-Stratonovich decoupling of an exponentially coupled Ising chain gives a scalar-field action with a nearest-neighbor [gradient](gradient.md) term and $U(m)=\tanh(\kappa/2)m^2/J-\log[2\cosh(2m+h)]$. Its continuum [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is the [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md) of the displayed quantum particle. At zero field the potential can have two wells, but a finite barrier still permits [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) and gives a unique symmetric [ground state](ground-state.md). The excitation gap produces a finite classical [correlation length](correlation-length.md), so there is no finite-temperature spontaneous magnetization for fixed positive $\kappa$. One classical spatial coordinate becomes imaginary time; a $d$-dimensional classical model corresponds to a quantum model with $d-1$ spatial dimensions.
