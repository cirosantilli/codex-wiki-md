# Random-access list

↑ **Parent:** [List (abstract data type)](list-abstract-data-type.md)

A persistent [list](list-abstract-data-type.md) representation supporting positional lookup more efficiently than a conventional [linked list](linked-list.md). Different implementations trade lookup, insertion and deletion costs; “random-access” does not itself guarantee constant-time indexing. A [skew-binary random-access list](skew-binary-random-access-list.md) supports worst-case constant-time front insertion and removal with logarithmic worst-case lookup.
