# Diffuse attenuation coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean colour](ocean-colour.md)

The logarithmic rate at which downwelling spectral [irradiance](irradiance.md) decreases with depth. It depends on absorption, scattering and the angular light field, rather than equaling the [absorption coefficient](absorption-coefficient.md).
