# Bilinear cross-effect of a line bundle on an abelian variety

↑ **Parent:** [Theorem of the Cube](theorem-of-the-cube.md)

For [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) $f,g:A\to B$, this [Picard group](picard-group.md) class is symmetric and additive in both arguments. The [Theorem of the Cube](theorem-of-the-cube.md) says precisely that the third additive difference of $h\mapsto[h^*L]$ vanishes, proving additivity of the cross-effect. The [Poincaré line bundle](poincare-line-bundle.md) expresses it as $(f,\phi_Lg)^*P_B$, so it depends only on the [Néron-Severi group](neron-severi-group.md) class of $L$. Its associated homomorphism is $\widehat f\phi_Lg+\widehat g\phi_Lf$.
