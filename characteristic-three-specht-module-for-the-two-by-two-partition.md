# Characteristic-three Specht module for the two-by-two partition

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

Use the standard [polytabloids](polytabloid.md) for tableaux with rows $12\mid34$ and $13\mid24$, as an ordered [basis](basis.md) $e_1,e_2$. Adjacent [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md) act by

$$
\rho(12)=\rho(34)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad\rho(23)=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

In characteristic three their common fixed line is $F(e_1+e_2)$. On its quotient every [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) acts by minus one, giving the [sign representation](sign-representation.md). Thus the displayed sequence has the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) and sign as its [composition factors](composition-factor.md), each once. It is nonsplit: the minus-one [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of $\rho(12)$ is $Fe_2$, which is not preserved by $\rho(23)$.

The natural [tabloid](tabloid.md) form restricts to [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}4&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$. Its radical in characteristic three is exactly that fixed line. Therefore $D^{(2,2)}$ is the sign quotient, while the trivial factor is $D^{(4)}$. This gives an explicit small failure of semisimplicity in modular symmetric-group [representation](group-representation.md) theory.
