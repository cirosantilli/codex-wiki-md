# Lindblad resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Type I migration](type-i-migration.md)

A rotating gravitational perturbation drives an epicyclic disk response where $m(\Omega-\Omega_p)=\pm\kappa$. The launched [density](density.md) waves carry [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). For a [planet](planet.md), inner and outer waves exert torques of opposite signs, and their imbalance contributes to migration.
