# Van der Pol amplitude evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Averaged amplitude for position-dependent damping](averaged-amplitude-for-position-dependent-damping.md)

For $x''+\varepsilon(x^2-1)x'+x=0$, the [averaged amplitude for position-dependent damping](averaged-amplitude-for-position-dependent-damping.md) has the displayed logistic-type equation. With $A(0)=1$, its positive solution is $A(T)=2/\sqrt{1+3e^{-T}}$. Small amplitudes grow and large ones decay toward the stable value two; the leading oscillation is $A(\varepsilon t)\cos t$.
