# Gauge transform

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear Schrödinger blowup analysis](nonlinear-schrodinger-blowup-analysis.md)

This local phase change is a [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) of the wave function. Multiplication by $e^{ia\psi(x)}$ is a gauge transform that preserves pointwise modulus and mass while shifting the gradient by $ia(\nabla\psi)u$.
