# Conjugate reflection of a holomorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md)

If $f$ is [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) on an [open set](open-set.md) $U$, its conjugate reflection $f^*$ is [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) on $U^*=\{z:\overline z\in U\}$, with $(f^*)'(z)=\overline{f'(\overline z)}$. The identity follows by conjugating the difference quotient with increment $\overline h$; the two conjugations restore [complex differentiability](complex-differentiability.md).

If $U$ is invariant under [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) and is [connected](connected-space.md), and $f$ is real at a set of real points accumulating inside $U$, then $f=f^*$ by the [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md). In particular, an [entire function](entire-function.md) real at every $1/n$ has this symmetry. This is a symmetry statement on an already defined domain; [Schwarz reflection](schwarz-reflection-principle.md) also constructs extensions across a boundary.
