# Parallel-spin degeneracy for equal-and-opposite moments

↑ **Parent:** [Two-spin exchange spectrum with unequal signed magnetic moments](two-spin-exchange-spectrum-with-unequal-signed-magnetic-moments.md)

If $g_2=-g_1$ in a shared magneton convention, the Zeeman [energy](energy-split.md) of both parallel-spin states cancels. Their [energy](energy-split.md) stays $\Delta/4$, with exact degeneracy two for every field. Opposite charges alone do not enforce this equality because the masses and magnetic factors may differ.
