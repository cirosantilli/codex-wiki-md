# BV mollification error estimate

↑ **Parent:** [BV translation estimate](bv-translation-estimate.md)

For a nonnegative normalized [mollifier](mollifier.md) supported in the radius-$\epsilon$ ball, integrate the [BV translation estimate](bv-translation-estimate.md) with weight $\rho_\epsilon(h)$. The full-space [derivative](derivative.md) variation controls the full-space error. Replacing it by variation on a smaller domain is invalid without a support condition: a nonconstant bump outside that domain has zero [derivative](derivative.md) [measure](measure.md) inside but a nonzero mollification error.
