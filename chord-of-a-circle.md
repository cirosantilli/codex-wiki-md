# Chord of a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

A chord joins two points of a [circle](circle.md) by a straight segment. If the circle has radius $r$ and the chord midpoint lies a distance $d$ from its center, the perpendicular bisector and [Pythagorean theorem](pythagorean-theorem.md) give the displayed length. The length exceeds that of an inscribed [equilateral triangle](equilateral-triangle.md) precisely when $d<r/2$.
