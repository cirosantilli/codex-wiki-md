# Vortex charge neutrality

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex Coulomb gas](vortex-coulomb-gas.md)

For the [Villain model](villain-model.md) on a periodic two-dimensional lattice, the vortex charge is the oriented curl of its integer bond variable. Summing over all faces cancels every bond twice, proving neutrality. In an infinite plane, a configuration with net winding $Q$ has far-field [spin wave](spin-wave.md) action $\pi\beta Q^2\log(L/a)$, so finite-energy localized configurations must have $Q=0$. At an open boundary the total winding instead equals the boundary circulation; neutrality cannot be imposed without a boundary condition.
