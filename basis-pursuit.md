# Basis pursuit

↑ **Parent:** [Compressed sensing](compressed-sensing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basis_pursuit)

Basis pursuit minimizes an $\ell^1$ [norm](norm.md) subject to exact linear measurements. Basis pursuit with a noise tolerance replaces the equality by $\|Ax-y\|_2\le\eta$. A unique minimizer has linearly independent active columns, and hence at most $\operatorname{rank}A$ nonzero coordinates: a null direction on its support would make the objective locally affine on a feasible line, contradicting minimality or uniqueness.
