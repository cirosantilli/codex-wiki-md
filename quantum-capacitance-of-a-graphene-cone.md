# Quantum capacitance of a graphene cone

↑ **Parent:** [Gate control of graphene carrier density](gate-control-of-graphene-carrier-density.md)

The [quantum capacitance](quantum-capacitance.md) per unit [area](surface-area.md) is $c_q=e^2D(E_F)$. Differentiating the gate equation gives

$$
\frac{1}{c_{\rm diff}}=\frac{1}{c_g}+\frac{1}{c_q},\qquad
c_{\rm diff}=e\frac{dn}{dV_g}.
$$

Thus the available electronic states act as a second contribution in series with geometric [capacitance](capacitance.md). At exact neutrality the ideal zero-[temperature](temperature-split.md) model has $c_q=c_{\rm diff}=0$; actual finite-[temperature](temperature-split.md) or spatially nonuniform samples need a broader description. A capacitance measurement can therefore probe the [density of states](density-of-states.md), rather than geometry alone.
