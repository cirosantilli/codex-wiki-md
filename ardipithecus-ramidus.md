# Ardipithecus ramidus

↑ **Parent:** [Ardipithecus](ardipithecus.md)

An approximately 4.4-million-year-old fossil hominin with an informative, incomplete skeleton. Its inferred locomotor mosaic differs from treating a living chimpanzee as an unchanged human ancestor. [Science 326, pp. 75–86; 4.4 Ma skeleton and locomotor mosaic](ardipithecus-ramidus-and-the-paleobiology-of-early-hominids-by-tim-d-white-and-others.md)
