# Rotational diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion](diffusion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotational_diffusion)

Rotational diffusion randomizes orientation. For a unit orientation vector on a sphere, isotropic rotational diffusion contributes $D_r\nabla_p^2f$ to the [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md), where $\nabla_p^2$ is the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md). Its coefficient has units inverse time. It differs from the translational [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md), whose units are length squared per time.
