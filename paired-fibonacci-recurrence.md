# Paired Fibonacci recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md)

Compute adjacent [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md) together, starting at $(0,1)$ and applying $(a,b)\mapsto(b,a+b)$. The shared pair avoids repeating the same recursive subproblem and gives $n$ additions for $F_n$. This counts arithmetic operations; exact [integers](integer-split.md) grow with $n$.
