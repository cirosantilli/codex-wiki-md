# Cosmic coincidence problem

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md)

Nonrelativistic matter dilutes as $a^{-3}$, while a [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) has constant [energy density](energy-density.md). The ratio of [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md) density to [matter density](matter-density.md) consequently scales as $a^3$, so comparable contributions occur during a limited epoch. The [cosmic coincidence problem](cosmic-coincidence-problem.md) asks why observers find these very different components comparable near their own epoch. This timing puzzle is distinct from the [cosmological constant problem](cosmological-constant-problem.md), which concerns the tiny absolute vacuum-energy scale.
