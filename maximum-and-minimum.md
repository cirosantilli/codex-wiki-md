# Maximum and minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_and_minimum)

Maxima and minima are [extrema](maximum-and-minimum.md) of a real-valued function. A global maximum of $f$ on its domain $D$ is a point $x_*$ with $f(x)\leq f(x_*)$ for every $x\in D$; a global minimum reverses the inequality. A local extremum requires the inequality only in a [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of the point within $D$. In [constrained optimization](constrained-optimization.md), $D$ is the feasible set, so an extremum is tested relative to the constraints rather than in every ambient direction. Thus a constrained [local maximum](local-maximum.md) or [local minimum](local-minimum.md) can occur at a boundary point without being an interior [critical point](critical-point.md).
