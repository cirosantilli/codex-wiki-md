# Hanle spin-precession measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Spin relaxation](spin-relaxation.md)

A transverse magnetic field rotates a nonequilibrium [spin polarization](spin-polarization.md) while diffusion and [spin relaxation](spin-relaxation.md) redistribute it. The field dependence of a detector signal can constrain spin lifetime and transport parameters. A distribution of diffusion times and rotation of the injecting/detecting electrodes must be included when relevant.
