# Conductors force finite support in a smooth dual

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth dual](smooth-dual.md)

If one-dimensional smooth [linear characters](linear-character.md) of a local multiplicative group have conductors tending to infinity, their [direct sum](direct-sum.md) can be admissible. Its algebraic dual is the product of inverse [linear characters](linear-character.md), but a [smooth vector](smooth-vector.md) in this product has only finitely many nonzero coordinates. A common open stabilizer contains one fixed principal-unit subgroup, on which every sufficiently ramified [linear character](linear-character.md) is nontrivial. Thus the smooth dual is again a [direct sum](direct-sum.md).
