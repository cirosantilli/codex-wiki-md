# Averaged-operator inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Averaged operator](averaged-operator.md)

The defining averaging representation is equivalent to $\|Tz-Tw\|^2\leq\|z-w\|^2-(1-\alpha)\|(I-T)z-(I-T)w\|^2/\alpha$. Expanding the norm of the convex combination proves both directions. Substituting a fixed point for $w$ gives [Fejér monotonicity](fejer-monotonicity.md) and summability of squared residuals.
