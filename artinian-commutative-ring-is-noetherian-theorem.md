# Artinian commutative ring is Noetherian theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Artinian ring](artinian-ring.md)

Every commutative [Artinian ring](artinian-ring.md) is [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md). Its [nilradical](nilradical.md) $N$ is nilpotent by the [nilradical of a commutative Artinian ring](nilradical-of-a-commutative-artinian-ring.md), and the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) identifies the reduced quotient $R/N$ with the finite product of the residue fields at the finitely many [maximal ideals](maximal-ideal.md). Each $N^i/N^{i+1}$ is an Artinian module over that product of [fields](field.md) and hence a [finite-dimensional vector space](finite-dimensional-vector-space.md). The finite [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) by these layers makes every [ideal](ideal.md) finitely generated.
