# Quantum reflection probability

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering.md)

For one-dimensional scattering from a real potential, write the left exterior [wavefunction](wave-function.md) as $e^{ikx}+re^{-ikx}$ with $k>0$. The reflected-to-incident probability-current ratio is $R=|r|^2$, since both components have the same speed and their currents have opposite signs. This probability differs from the complex reflection amplitude $r$. With transmission into a right exterior [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $q$, the [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md) is $T=(q/k)|t|^2$ and conservation of current gives $R+T=1$ when there is no absorption.
