# Reaction-rate elasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Rate equation](rate-equation.md)

The displayed logarithmic derivative compares the loss and production flux sensitivities of species $i$ to the mean amount $\mu_j$. At a steady state $v_i^+=v_i^-=v_i$, it converts the normalized negative drift Jacobian into $M_{ij}=H_{ij}/\tau_i$, where $\tau_i=\mu_i/v_i$ is the [mean molecular lifetime](mean-molecular-lifetime.md). For constant production and loss proportional to $\mu^\alpha$, the scalar elasticity is $H=\alpha$.
