# Cosmic neutrino background last-scattering surface

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic neutrino background](cosmic-neutrino-background.md)

The cosmic neutrino background last-scattering surface is the locus from which relic neutrinos that reach an observer underwent their final appreciable scattering. Although [neutrino decoupling](neutrino-decoupling.md) occurs before [photon decoupling](photon-decoupling.md), a massive neutrino travels slower than light after becoming nonrelativistic, so its last-scattering surface need not be farther away than the [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background.md) last-scattering surface.
