# Relative boundary rigidity from a hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Three-manifold hierarchy](three-manifold-hierarchy.md)

A map between [compact](compact-space.md) [connected](connected-space.md) [orientable](orientable-surface.md) irreducible [3-manifolds](3-manifold.md) with nonempty boundaries, [injective](injective-function.md) on the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) and on the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md), is [homotopic](homotopy.md) to a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) relative to the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md). The [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) determines degree $\pm1$, and the cover corresponding to the image of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) consequently has one sheet. Straighten the preimage of an incompressible hierarchy [surface](topological-surface.md), cut both manifolds, and repeat. At the terminal balls, extend the prescribed [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) [homeomorphisms](homeomorphism.md) by coning. The [homotopies](homotopy.md) and [homeomorphisms](homeomorphism.md) glue across the straightened [surface](topological-surface.md) collars.
