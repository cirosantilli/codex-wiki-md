# Deterministic quadratic variation characterizes a Gaussian continuous local martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian continuous martingale](gaussian-continuous-martingale.md)

If a continuous local martingale starts at zero and has deterministic continuous [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) $[M]_t=f(t)$, the [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) gives $M_t=B_{f(t)}$. It is therefore a centered [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md). The converse follows from [independent increments of a Gaussian martingale](independent-increments-of-a-gaussian-martingale.md).
