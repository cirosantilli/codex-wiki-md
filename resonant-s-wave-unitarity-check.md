# Resonant s-wave unitarity check

↑ **Parent:** [Optical theorem](optical-theorem.md)

For an isotropic elastic amplitude normalized by an incident unit [plane wave](plane-wave.md) and outgoing $fe^{ikr}/r$, total cross section is $4\pi|f|^2$. The [optical theorem](optical-theorem.md) gives the displayed identity. At a [resonance](resonance.md) where the real part of $1/f$ vanishes, $f=i/k$ and the cross section is $4\pi/k^2$.
