# Genetic assimilation

↑ **Parent:** [Evolution](evolution.md)

Evolution of expression of a formerly induced [phenotype](phenotype.md) in the absence of the original inducing condition. It is a particular outcome of [genetic accommodation](genetic-accommodation.md), not direct copying of acquired bodily modifications into [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md).
