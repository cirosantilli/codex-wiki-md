# Dimethyl sulfide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimethyl_sulfide)

An organic sulfur gas emitted through biological processing, including marine production associated with sulfur-containing metabolites. It is a [secondary metabolic byproduct](secondary-metabolic-byproduct.md) example and a possible [atmospheric biosignature gas](atmospheric-biosignature-gas.md) whose detectability depends on production and photochemical destruction.
