# Hyperbolic motion

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)

Hyperbolic motion in [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) is rectilinear motion with constant [proper acceleration](proper-acceleration.md). A representative positive-$x$ [world line](world-line.md) is $x=ct_0\cosh(\tau/t_0)$, $t=t_0\sinh(\tau/t_0)$, with proper acceleration $c/t_0$. This describes a hyperbola in spacetime, not a [hyperbolic Kepler orbit](hyperbolic-kepler-orbit.md).
