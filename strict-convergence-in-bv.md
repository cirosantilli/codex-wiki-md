# Strict convergence in BV

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-star convergence in BV](weak-star-convergence-in-bv.md)

Strict convergence combines [strong convergence](norm-convergence.md) in $L^1$ with convergence of the [total variation seminorm](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md). It implies [weak-star convergence in BV](weak-star-convergence-in-bv.md) but does not make jump sets stable: [mollifications](mollification.md) of an interior step can converge strictly to the step while every approximant has an empty [jump set of a bounded-variation function](jump-set-of-a-bounded-variation-function.md).
