# Hitting-zero classification for a Bessel process

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel process](bessel-process.md)

A [Bessel process](bessel-process.md) of dimension $d>0$ started away from zero hits zero almost surely exactly when $d<2$. For $d\ne2$, its [scale function of a one-dimensional diffusion](scale-function-stochastic-processes.md) is $s(x)=x^{2-d}$; for $d=2$ it is $s(x)=\log x$. The [boundary hitting probability from a diffusion scale function](boundary-hitting-probability-from-a-diffusion-scale-function.md) then gives the classification by taking the inner boundary to zero and the outer boundary to infinity.
