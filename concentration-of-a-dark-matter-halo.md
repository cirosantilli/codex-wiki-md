# Concentration of a dark-matter halo

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md)

The concentration of a spherical dark-matter halo compares its outer radius with an inner scale radius. For a [Navarro--Frenk--White profile](navarro-frenk-white-profile.md), $c=r_v/r_s$.
