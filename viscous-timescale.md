# Viscous timescale

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

The viscous timescale is the radial diffusion time $t_\nu\sim R^2/\nu$ associated with a kinematic viscosity $\nu$ on length scale $R$.
