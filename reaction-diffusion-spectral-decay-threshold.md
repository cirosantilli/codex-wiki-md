# Reaction-diffusion spectral decay threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

On the unit interval with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), $u_t=u_{xx}+\kappa u$ has sine-mode growth rates $\kappa-j^2\pi^2$. Every finite-energy initial condition decays exactly when the largest rate is negative, namely $\kappa<\pi^2$. A centered [finite difference](finite-difference-split.md) grid with $h=1/(M+1)$ has eigenvectors $\sin(j\pi m/(M+1))$ and growth rates $\kappa-4h^{-2}\sin^2(j\pi/(2(M+1)))$. Its all-data decay threshold is the displayed $\kappa_h$, strictly less than $\pi^2$ and approaching it from below. Equality leaves a stationary lowest mode; exceeding the threshold allows growth.
