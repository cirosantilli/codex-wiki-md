# Abelian image of a cubing homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md)

Suppose the cubing map $T:G\to G$ is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md). Expanding $(ab)^3=a^3b^3$ and cancelling gives $(ba)^2=a^2b^2$. Also

$$
gz^3g^{-1}=(gzg^{-1})^3=g^3z^3g^{-3},
$$

so $g^2$ commutes with every cube. For $x,y\in T(G)$, therefore, $x^2y=yx^2$ and $yxyx=x^2y^2$. Multiplying the latter identity on the left by $y^{-1}$ yields $xyx=x^2y$; cancelling $x$ gives $yx=xy$. Thus the [image of a group homomorphism](image-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) $T(G)$ is an [abelian group](abelian-group.md).
