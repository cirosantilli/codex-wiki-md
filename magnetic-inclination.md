# Magnetic inclination

↑ **Parent:** [Geomagnetic field](geomagnetic-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_inclination)

The angle of the [geomagnetic field](geomagnetic-field.md) to the horizontal, conventionally positive downwards. It controls the skew and polarity pattern of an induced [total-field magnetic anomaly](total-field-magnetic-anomaly.md).
