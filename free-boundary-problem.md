# Free boundary problem

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)

A [free boundary problem](free-boundary-problem.md) determines both a field and part of its domain boundary. A glacier terminus, for example, separates positive [ice](ice.md) thickness from bare bed. [Mass conservation](mass-conservation.md), nonnegative thickness, a rule for [ice ablation](ice-ablation.md) only where [ice](ice.md) exists, and suitable interface or [entropy solution](entropy-solution.md) conditions are needed; a formal zero of one [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) branch alone need not give the selected physical boundary.
