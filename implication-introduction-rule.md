# Implication introduction rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

The implication introduction rule discharges a temporary assumption $A$ from a derivation of $B$, producing $A\to B$. Under the [Curry-Howard correspondence](curry-howard-correspondence.md), a body $t:B$ under $a:A$ gives the [lambda abstraction](lambda-abstraction.md) $\lambda a^A.t:A\to B$. Unused assumptions may also be discharged.
