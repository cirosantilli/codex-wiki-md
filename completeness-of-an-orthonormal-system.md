# Completeness of an orthonormal system

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md)

An [orthonormal set](orthonormal-set.md) is complete in a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) when the [closure](closure-topology.md) of its [linear span](linear-span.md) is the whole space. Equivalently, the only vector orthogonal to every member is zero: project any vector onto the closed span, and its remaining orthogonal component must vanish. This is completeness of an expansion system, distinct from completeness of the ambient metric space.
