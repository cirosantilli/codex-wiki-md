# Spin group

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_group)

The spin group $\operatorname{Spin}(n)$ is the connected double cover of $SO(n)$ constructed inside the units of a [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md).
