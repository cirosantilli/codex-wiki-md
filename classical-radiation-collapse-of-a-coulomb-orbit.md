# Classical radiation collapse of a Coulomb orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb orbit](coulomb-orbit.md)

An accelerating classical charge radiates according to the [Larmor formula](larmor-formula.md). For a slowly evolving nonrelativistic circular electron orbit, energy balance gives $d(r^3)/dt=-4cr_0^2$, with $r_0$ the [classical electron radius](classical-electron-radius.md). The finite formal collapse time exposes classical atomic instability; it is not a model of a stable quantum atom.
