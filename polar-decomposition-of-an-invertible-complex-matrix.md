# Polar decomposition of an invertible complex matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Polar decomposition of an invertible real matrix](polar-decomposition-of-an-invertible-real-matrix.md)

Every invertible complex matrix has the unique polar decomposition

$$
M=PU,
\qquad
P=\sqrt{MM^*},
\qquad
U=P^{-1}M,
$$

where $P$ is a positive-definite [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) and $U$ is a [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md). Writing $P=e^B$ gives $B=\frac12\log(MM^*)$, which is Hermitian.
