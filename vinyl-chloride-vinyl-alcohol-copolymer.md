# Vinyl chloride-vinyl alcohol copolymer

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical copolymer](statistical-copolymer.md)

A [copolymer](copolymer.md) containing $-\mathrm{CH_2-CHCl}-$ and $-\mathrm{CH_2-CH(OH)}-$ units. It describes the intended chemical composition, not a guarantee that unselective alkaline treatment yields an undegraded polymer.
