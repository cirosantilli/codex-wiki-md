# Holomorphic square root outside all polynomial roots

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic square root](holomorphic-square-root.md)

An even-degree [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) with all roots in $|z|<R$ admits a [holomorphic square root](holomorphic-square-root.md) throughout $|z|>R$. Factor out its even power of $z$ and use the convergent logarithm of each $1-a_j/z$ to construct it. Even degree removes the annular winding obstruction. The two choices differ by a global sign.
