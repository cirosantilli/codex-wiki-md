# Quantum witness

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum complexity theory](quantum-complexity-theory.md)

A [quantum witness](quantum-witness.md) is a polynomial-size [quantum state](quantum-state.md) supplied to a verifier as evidence for a YES instance. The verifier fixes its own [ancilla qubits](ancilla-qubit.md) independently. Pure [quantum witnesses](quantum-witness.md) suffice when maximizing a linear acceptance functional on [density operators](density-matrix.md). [Quantum witnesses](quantum-witness.md) are not generally restricted to basis vectors or product states.
