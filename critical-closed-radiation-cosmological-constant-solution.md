<h1 id="critical-closed-radiation-cosmological-constant-solution">Critical closed radiation--cosmological-constant solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Closed radiation--cosmological-constant turning polynomial](closed-radiation-cosmological-constant-turning-polynomial.md)

For $\beta=4$ and $a(0)=0$, the expanding branch is

$$
a(t)=\sqrt{2(1-e^{-2H_0t})}.
$$

It grows as $2\sqrt{H_0t}$ near zero and approaches $\sqrt2$ exponentially at late times.
