# Particle-laden current with suction

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md)

A steady well-mixed dilute [particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md) above an absorbing porous bed loses carrier fluid at speed $W$ and particles at total downward speed $W+V$, where $V$ is their relative [settling velocity](settling-velocity.md) magnitude. [Volume conservation](volume-conservation.md) and the particle budget give $Q=Q_0-Wx$ and $\phi=(Q/Q_0)^{V/W}$ for $W>0$. The [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) equation is $(Q^2/h+g'_0\phi h^2/2)'=-Wu$ if the withdrawn fluid carries its local horizontal [velocity](velocity.md). Consequently $uu'+g'_0\phi h'+g'_0h\phi'/2=0$. At $W=0$, $\phi=e^{-Vx/Q_0}$ and the momentum flux is constant.
