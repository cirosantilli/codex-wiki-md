# Lone pair

↑ **Parent:** [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lone_pair)

A [lone pair](lone-pair.md) is an [electron pair](electron-pair.md) assigned to one [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) without a connecting line to another [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) in a [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md). It is usually shown as two dots. [Water](water.md) has two [lone pairs](lone-pair.md) on [oxygen](oxygen.md), accounting for four [valence electrons](valence-electron.md); [ammonia](ammonia.md) has one [lone pair](lone-pair.md) on [nitrogen](nitrogen.md), accounting for two. In $\mathrm{O=C=O}$, each [oxygen](oxygen.md) has two [lone pairs](lone-pair.md), and [carbon](carbon.md) has none.

A [lone pair](lone-pair.md) can be reactive. In [protonation](protonation.md) of [ammonia](ammonia.md), the [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) [lone pair](lone-pair.md) becomes a new [bond pair](bond-pair.md) to a [proton](proton.md):

$$
\mathrm{NH}_3+\mathrm H^+\longrightarrow\mathrm{NH}_4^+.
$$

The [proton](proton.md) supplies no [electron](electron.md), so the eight [valence electrons](valence-electron.md) are retained: three [bond pairs](bond-pair.md) plus one [lone pair](lone-pair.md) become four [bond pairs](bond-pair.md). The resulting [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) has [formal charge](formal-charge.md) $5-0-8/2=+1$; each [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) has [formal charge](formal-charge.md) zero. This is why identifying an available [lone pair](lone-pair.md) helps explain the [chemical base](base-chemistry.md) behaviour of [ammonia](ammonia.md), rather than merely decorating its formula.

Lewis already used the [ammonia](ammonia.md) to $\mathrm{NH}_4^+$ example in [The Atom and the Molecule (1916), pp. 777–778](https://zenodo.org/records/1429068). In modern terms the dots identify an allocation of [electrons](electron.md), not two stationary particles or an isolated patch unaffected by the rest of the [molecule](molecule-split.md). A [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) description can spread electronic amplitude over several [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) while a localized [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md) remains useful for [acid-base chemistry](acid-base-chemistry-split.md).
