# Duality of simple and projective Brauer characters

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer character inner product](brauer-character-inner-product.md)

If $S_i$ are the simple modules of a split group algebra and $P_i$ are their [projective covers](projective-cover.md), then

$$
\langle\chi_{P_i},\chi_{S_j}\rangle_{p'}=\delta_{ij}.
$$
