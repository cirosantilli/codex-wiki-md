# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the functional analytic methods for partial differential equations course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all three questions of the [functional analytic methods for partial differential equations course of the University of Oxford](../functional-analytic-methods-for-partial-differential-equations-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part C examination, held on 2 June 2023. Source: [released parent PDF, bundle pages 339–342; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=339). The native PDF confirms three substantive printing issues: the [cutoff function](../cutoff-function.md) bound in 2(b)(ii), the missing boundary restriction in 3(a)(ii), and the [Sobolev space](../sobolev-space-split.md) used for the asserted convergence in 3(b)(iv). Each is addressed explicitly at the relevant leaf.
