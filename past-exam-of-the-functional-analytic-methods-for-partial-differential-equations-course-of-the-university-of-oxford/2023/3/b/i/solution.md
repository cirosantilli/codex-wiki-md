<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $w_m=|E_m|^{5/6}u_m$. By linearity of the [weak formulation](../../../../../weak-formulation.md), $w_m$ is the zero-boundary [weak solution](../../../../../weak-solution.md) with forcing $\chi_{E_m}$. The estimate in (a)(iii) therefore gives

$$
\|w_m\|_{H^1(B)}\leq C|E_m|^{5/6},\qquad
\boxed{\|u_m\|_{H^1(B)}\leq C.}
$$

The [change of variables](../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $x=r_my$ gives $|E_m|=r_m^3|E|>0$, so the division is legitimate. Since $H^1(B)$ is a [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), [weak sequential compactness in a Hilbert space](../../../../../weak-sequential-compactness-in-a-hilbert-space.md) now gives a [subsequence](../../../../../subsequence.md) and some $u\in H^1(B)$ such that

$$
\boxed{u_{m_j}\rightharpoonup u\quad\text{in }H^1(B).}
$$

In fact $u\in H_0^1(B)$: the [Sobolev trace operator](../../../../../trace-operator.md) is continuous and linear, so its zero kernel is preserved by [weak convergence](../../../../../weak-convergence.md).
