<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [locally integrable function](../../../../../locally-integrable-function.md) $u$ has [weak derivative](../../../../../weak-derivative.md) $g_i$ in direction $i$ when $g_i$ is locally integrable and

$$
\boxed{\int_{B_1}u\,\partial_i\phi\,dx=-\int_{B_1}g_i\phi\,dx
\qquad\text{for every }\phi\in C_c^\infty(B_1).}
$$

The [test functions](../../../../../test-function.md) have [compact support](../../../../../compact-support.md) inside the domain, so no boundary values enter this definition. Such $g_i$ is unique [almost everywhere](../../../../../almost-everywhere.md) and is denoted $\partial_i u$.

For $1\leq p<\infty$, the first-order [Sobolev space](../../../../../sobolev-space-split.md) consists of the [Lp space](../../../../../lp-space.md) classes whose first [weak derivatives](../../../../../weak-derivative.md) are also in that [Lp space](../../../../../lp-space.md):

$$
\boxed{W^{1,p}(B_1)=\{u\in L^p(B_1):\partial_i u\in L^p(B_1),\ i=1,\ldots,n\},\qquad
\|u\|_{W^{1,p}}=\left(\|u\|_p^p+\sum_{i=1}^n\|\partial_i u\|_p^p\right)^{1/p}.}
$$

This [Sobolev norm](../../../../../sobolev-norm.md) is equivalent to $\|u\|_p+\|\nabla u\|_p$; either convention is acceptable. When $p=2$, this is the [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) $H^1(B_1)$.
