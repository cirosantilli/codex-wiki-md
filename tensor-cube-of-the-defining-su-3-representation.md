<h1 id="tensor-cube-of-the-defining-su-3-representation">Tensor cube of the defining SU(3) representation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental representations of sl3](fundamental-representations-of-sl3.md)

The [tensor product of Lie algebra representations](tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md) $\mathbf3\otimes\mathbf3$ splits into the symmetric $\mathbf6$ and antisymmetric $\overline{\mathbf3}$. Then $\mathbf6\otimes\mathbf3=\mathbf{10}\oplus\mathbf8$ and $\overline{\mathbf3}\otimes\mathbf3=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf1$, as [highest-weight character subtraction](highest-weight-character-subtraction.md) verifies. The decuplet is fully symmetric under quark permutations, the singlet fully antisymmetric, and the two octets constitute the two-dimensional mixed permutation multiplicity space. Applying the [Pauli constraint on three-quark flavour multiplets](pauli-constraint-on-three-quark-flavour-multiplets.md) gives one ground-state spin-one-half octet and one spin-three-halves decuplet.
