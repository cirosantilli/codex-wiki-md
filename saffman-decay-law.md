# Saffman decay law

↑ **Parent:** [Saffman integral](saffman-integral.md)

With nonzero invariant $L$, a self-preserving [integral scale of turbulence](integral-scale-of-turbulence.md) gives $L\propto\mathcal E\ell^3$. Combining this with freely decaying high-Reynolds-number [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) $\epsilon\propto\mathcal E^{3/2}/\ell$ gives $\mathcal E\propto(t-t_*)^{-6/5}$ and $\ell\propto(t-t_*)^{2/5}$. The closure assumptions, not the invariant alone, determine this exponent.
