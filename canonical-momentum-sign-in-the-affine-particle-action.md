# Canonical momentum sign in the affine particle action

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md)

For the affine action $S=-\tfrac m2\int g_{\mu\nu}\dot x^\mu\dot x^\nu d\tau$ with signature $(-+++)$, differentiating with respect to velocity gives the displayed [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md). Future-oriented proper time in a conformally flat metric yields $P_i^c=-a\gamma mv_i$, whereas the conventional future-directed mechanical momentum has $q_i=a\gamma mv_i$. Changing the action's overall sign interchanges these canonical labels but leaves its classical geodesics unchanged. Both labels are conserved by spatial [translation invariance](translation-invariance.md), and either gives the same physical momentum magnitude proportional to $a^{-1}$. The sign should be stated when comparing a worldline canonical covector with a positive-frequency field-mode label.
