# Euclidean algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_algorithm)

The Euclidean algorithm repeatedly replaces $(a,b)$ by $(b,a\bmod b)$ and terminates at $\gcd(a,b)$.
