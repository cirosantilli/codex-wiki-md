# Non-Boussinesq triangular-profile line-plume balances

↑ **Parent:** [Triangular-profile line plume](triangular-profile-line-plume.md)

For a [triangular-profile line plume](triangular-profile-line-plume.md) with centreline density $\rho$, speed $W$, half-width $b$, ambient density $\rho_0$, and inward relative edge speed $e$, [volume](volume.md), [mass](mass.md) and [momentum](momentum.md) conservation give

$$
(2b)_t+(bW)_z=2e,\quad [b(\rho+\rho_0)]_t+[bW(\rho_0+2\rho)/3]_z=2\rho_0e,
$$



$$
[bW(\rho_0+2\rho)/3]_t+[bW^2(\rho_0+3\rho)/6]_z=gb(\rho_0-\rho).
$$

Subtracting [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) from $\rho_0$ times [volume](volume.md) conservation also gives buoyancy conservation. [Batchelor entrainment](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md) supplies a closure for $e$.
