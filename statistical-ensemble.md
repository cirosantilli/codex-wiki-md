# Statistical ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_ensemble)

A [statistical ensemble](statistical-ensemble.md) describes hypothetical copies of the same system under specified constraints, with a [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) over their [microstates](microstate.md). An [ensemble average](expected-value-split.md) is an [expected value](expected-value-split.md) across those copies. The [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md) fixes [temperature](temperature-split.md) and particle number while allowing [energy](energy-split.md) exchange; the [microcanonical ensemble](microcanonical-ensemble.md) fixes [energy](energy-split.md), and the [grand canonical ensemble](grand-canonical-ensemble.md) also allows particle exchange. These choices specify different physical preparations. [Gibbs (1902), preface: ensembles of systems with different microscopic conditions](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Elementary_Principles_in_Statistical_Mechanics)
