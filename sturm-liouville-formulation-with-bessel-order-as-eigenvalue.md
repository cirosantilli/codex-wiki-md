# Sturm-Liouville formulation with Bessel order as eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel differential equation](bessel-differential-equation.md)

For fixed $\beta$, move the order term of the [Bessel equation](bessel-differential-equation.md) to the right: $x^2y''+xy'+\beta^2x^2y=n^2y$. The [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) for its leading differential expression is $1/x$, giving the displayed [Sturm-Liouville theory](sturm-liouville-theory-split.md) form with coefficient $p=x$, potential $q=\beta^2x$, weight $w=1/x$ and [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $n^2$. The origin is a singular endpoint. The [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) must be normalized from a positive reference point; its defining logarithmic [integral](integral-split.md) cannot literally start at zero. [Boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) are needed before this differential identity becomes a specified self-adjoint spectral problem.
