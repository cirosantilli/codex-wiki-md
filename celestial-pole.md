# Celestial pole

↑ **Parent:** [Celestial sphere](celestial-sphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestial_pole)

The [celestial poles](celestial-pole.md) are the two intersections of the rotation axis of Earth with the [celestial sphere](celestial-sphere.md). The northern pole has [declination](declination.md) $+90^\circ$.
