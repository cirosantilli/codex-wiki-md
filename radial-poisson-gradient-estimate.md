# Radial Poisson gradient estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md)

For a radial Dirichlet solution on a fixed three-dimensional shell $a<r<b$, $(r^2U')'=r^2F$. Thus $r^2U'=C_0+\int_a^rs^2F(s)\,ds$, and $U(a)=U(b)=0$ bounds $|C_0|$ by the supremum of that integral. [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) in the radial volume measure bounds the integral by $C\|f\|_2$. Since $r\ge a>0$, the displayed [gradient](gradient.md) bound follows. The estimate exploits radiality and does not extend to arbitrary forcing in three dimensions.
