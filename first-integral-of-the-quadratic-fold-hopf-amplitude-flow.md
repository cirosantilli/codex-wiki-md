# First integral of the quadratic fold-Hopf amplitude flow

↑ **Parent:** [Fold-Hopf bifurcation](fold-hopf-bifurcation.md)

The amplitude equations $u'=-2uv$, $v'=\lambda_2+v^2+u^2$ satisfy $u'=F_v$, $v'=-F_u$, hence preserve the displayed [first integral](first-integral.md). For $\lambda_2=-a^2<0$ and $u\ge0$, the [centre equilibrium](center-equilibrium.md) is $(a,0)$ with $F=2a^3/3$, while the two boundary [saddle equilibria](saddle-equilibrium.md) are $(0,\pm a)$ with $F=0$. The level $F=0$ contains the boundary connection and the arc $u^2=3(a^2-v^2)$ joining the two [saddle equilibria](saddle-equilibrium.md). Levels $0<F<2a^3/3$ form closed [periodic orbits](periodic-orbit.md). Adding an independent rotating phase turns these closed amplitude trajectories into invariant [tori](torus.md).
