# Matter-era photon endpoint solution

↑ **Parent:** [Line-of-sight solution for free-streaming photons](line-of-sight-solution-for-free-streaming-photons.md)

In the negative-spatial-perturbation convention, a matter-era scalar solution $h=h_s+C=Ak^{1/2}\tau^2/4$ gives the displayed exact [photon brightness perturbation](photon-brightness-perturbation.md). Integration gives emission bracket $\Delta_*+iAk^{-1/2}\mu\tau_*-Ak^{-3/2}$ times the propagation phase, plus observer monopole $Ak^{-3/2}$ and dipole $-iAk^{-1/2}\mu\tau_0$. Under ordinary adiabatic growing-mode initial conditions and $k\tau_*\ll1$, the emission gravitational term dominates higher angular multipoles. Arbitrary freely specified initial brightness need not obey this dominance.
