# Interior gradient estimate near a zero Dirichlet side

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md)

For a smooth [uniformly elliptic](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) homogeneous equation away from other boundaries, an interior [gradient](gradient.md) estimate on a ball of radius comparable to the distance $s$ from a flat side gives $|\nabla u|\leq Cs^{-1}\sup_{B_{cs}}|u|$. If $u$ extends continuously with value zero on that side, $s|\nabla u|\to0$ locally along it. This justifies vanishing boundary terms such as $u_x\sin(nx)$ in a distributional Fourier sine projection.
