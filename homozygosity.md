# Homozygosity

↑ **Parent:** [Genotype](genotype.md)

[Homozygosity](homozygosity.md) means that the two [allele](allele.md) states at a diploid [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) are the same; an individual with that property is a [homozygote](homozygote.md). Observed [homozygosity](homozygosity.md) does not by itself establish [identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md).
