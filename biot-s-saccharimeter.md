<h1 id="biot-s-saccharimeter">Biot's saccharimeter</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Polarimeter](polarimeter.md)

An angular [polarimeter](polarimeter.md) associated with [Jean-Baptiste Biot](jean-baptiste-biot.md), measuring the rotation produced by a sugar solution of known optical path length. The original form used a black-glass polarizing mirror and a calcite analyser; it should not be conflated with a later quartz-wedge compensating instrument.[pp.84–85, Biot polariscope; chapter VI, compensating saccharimeters](a-handbook-of-sugar-analysis-by-charles-albert-browne-jr.md)
