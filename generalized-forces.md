# Generalized forces

↑ **Parent:** [Virtual work](virtual-work.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_forces)

[Generalized forces](generalized-forces.md) are the coefficients of [virtual work](virtual-work.md) in a set of [generalized coordinates](generalized-coordinate.md). A translational coordinate has a [force](force-split.md) as its coefficient, while an angular coordinate has a [torque](torque.md). A generalized [force](force-split.md) conjugate to $q_i$ has units of [energy](energy-split.md) divided by the units of $q_i$. [Forces](force-split.md) not already included in the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) enter the [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) as $d(\partial L/\partial\dot q_i)/dt-\partial L/\partial q_i=Q_i$.
