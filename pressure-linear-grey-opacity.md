# Pressure-linear grey opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md)

A [grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md) model with [mass absorption coefficient](mass-absorption-coefficient.md) proportional to [pressure](pressure-split.md). Under [hydrostatic balance](hydrostatic-balance.md), $\kappa=\kappa_sp/p_s$ gives downward [optical depth](optical-depth.md) $t=\kappa_sp^2/(2gp_s)$. For $T=T_s(p/p_s)^a$, a non-scattering optically thick column emits hemispheric [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) $F\sim\sigma T_s^4t_s^{-2a}\Gamma(2a+1)/(a+1)$. The angular factor follows from integrating ray attenuation over the upward hemisphere; a vertical-ray approximation omits it. The extension of the emitting-depth [integral](integral-split.md) to infinity requires fixed $a$ as $t_s\to\infty$.
