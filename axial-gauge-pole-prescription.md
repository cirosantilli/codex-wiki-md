# Axial-gauge pole prescription

↑ **Parent:** [Axial gauge](axial-gauge.md)

The inverse of $n\cdot\partial$ and the [axial-gauge propagator](axial-gauge-propagator.md) are singular when $n\cdot k=0$. A pole prescription and residual-gauge boundary conditions specify these inverses. They must be compatible throughout a calculation; formal ghost decoupling alone does not specify them.
