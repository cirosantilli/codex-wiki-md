# Semiprime

↑ **Parent:** [Composite number](composite-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiprime)

A [semiprime](semiprime.md) is a [composite number](composite-number.md) that is a product of exactly two [prime numbers](prime-number.md), counted with multiplicity. For example, $6=2\cdot3$ and $9=3^2$ are semiprimes. Distinct factors give $\varphi(pq)=(p-1)(q-1)$ for the [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md), whereas equal factors give $\varphi(p^2)=p(p-1)$. This distinction matters when constructing a [uniform coprime state for a semiprime](uniform-coprime-state-for-a-semiprime.md).
