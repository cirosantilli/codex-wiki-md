# Recovery after a finite-duration stress load

↑ **Parent:** [Series spring and Voigt-branch creep](series-spring-and-voigt-branch-creep.md)

Apply constant [stress](stress.md) $\sigma_0$ at $t=0$ and remove it at $t=t_h$. For [series spring and Voigt-branch creep](series-spring-and-voigt-branch-creep.md), the series [spring](spring.md) recovers its [strain](strain.md) $\sigma_0/E_0$ immediately on unloading. The [Kelvin-Voigt model](kelvin-voigt-model.md) branch has [retardation time](retardation-time-viscoelasticity.md) $\tau=\eta/E_1$ and must keep continuous [strain](strain.md) because an instantaneous dashpot displacement would require an impulse. Its post-unloading equation is $\eta\dot\varepsilon+E_1\varepsilon=0$. Solving with the branch's strain at $t_h$ gives the title formula. Thus all remaining [strain](strain.md) eventually recovers: this [standard linear solid model](standard-linear-solid-model.md) has no permanent set.
