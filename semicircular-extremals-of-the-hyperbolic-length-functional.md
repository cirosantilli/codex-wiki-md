# Semicircular extremals of the hyperbolic length functional

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md)

For positive twice differentiable [functions](function-split.md) $y$, the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) for this [functional](functional.md) reduces to $yy''+1+y'^2=0$. Thus $(yy')'=-1$ and integration gives $(x-h)^2+y^2=R^2$. The upper [circular arc](circular-arc.md) is a graph wherever its radicand is positive. A fixed horizontal endpoint with free height imposes $F_{y'}=y'/(y\sqrt{1+y'^2})=0$, so that endpoint must lie above the centre. Distinct horizontal endpoints cannot both satisfy this [natural boundary conditions for a free endpoint](natural-boundary-conditions-for-a-free-endpoint.md). The [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md) alone also admits constant functions, which are excluded by the full [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md).
