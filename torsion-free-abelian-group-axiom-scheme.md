# Torsion-free abelian group axiom scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion-free abelian group](torsion-free-abelian-group.md)

For an [abelian group](abelian-group.md), the scheme $\forall x(nx=0\to x=0)$ for every positive integer $n$ expresses absence of nonzero elements of [finite order](finite-order.md). Each fixed $n$ gives one sentence, while the entire scheme expresses the [torsion-free abelian group](torsion-free-abelian-group.md) property.
