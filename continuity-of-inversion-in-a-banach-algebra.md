# Continuity of inversion in a Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Group of invertible elements of a Banach algebra](group-of-invertible-elements-of-a-banach-algebra.md)

Inversion is continuous on the [group of invertible elements of a Banach algebra](group-of-invertible-elements-of-a-banach-algebra.md). The [Neumann series](neumann-series.md) gives a local bound on inverse norms, while $b^{-1}-a^{-1}=b^{-1}(a-b)a^{-1}$ makes the difference tend to zero as $b\to a$.
