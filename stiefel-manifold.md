# Stiefel manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Unit tangent bundle](unit-tangent-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stiefel_manifold)

The real Stiefel manifold $V_k(\mathbb R^n)$ consists of ordered orthonormal $k$-frames in $\mathbb R^n$. In particular, $V_2(\mathbb R^{m+1})$ is naturally the [unit tangent bundle](unit-tangent-bundle.md) of $S^m$.
