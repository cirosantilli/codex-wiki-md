# Blood circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Circulatory system](circulatory-system.md)

The repeated transport of [blood](blood.md) through connected pathways. An argument based on cardiac throughput compares the amount propelled over many beats with the amount which could be newly supplied; throughput is not the instantaneous [blood](blood.md) stock.
