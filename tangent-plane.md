# Tangent plane

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent space](tangent-space.md)

For a regular surface given locally by $F(\mathbf x)=0$, its [tangent plane](tangent-plane.md) at $\mathbf x_0$ is the affine plane $\nabla F(\mathbf x_0)\cdot(\mathbf x-\mathbf x_0)=0$. A [normal vector](normal-vector.md) is the nonzero [gradient](gradient.md) at that point. Differentiating $F$ along each surface curve proves that its [tangent vector](tangent-vector.md) lies in this plane.
