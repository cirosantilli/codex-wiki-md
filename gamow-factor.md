# Gamow factor

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb barrier](coulomb-barrier.md)

The semiclassical penetration factor for two repelling point charges is approximately $\exp(-2\pi\eta)$, where $\eta=Z_1Z_2\alpha_{\rm fs}\sqrt{\mu c^2/(2E)}$. It represents the long-range Coulomb suppression, not the [nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md) probability after penetration.
