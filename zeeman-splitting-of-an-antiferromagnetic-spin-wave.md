# Zeeman splitting of an antiferromagnetic spin wave

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetic spin wave](antiferromagnetic-spin-wave.md)

[Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md) is the separation of [energy](energy-split.md) levels or mode [frequencies](frequency.md) by a background [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). For an [antiferromagnetic spin wave](antiferromagnetic-spin-wave.md), opposite circular [spin](spin.md) polarizations receive opposite [frequency](frequency.md) shifts.
