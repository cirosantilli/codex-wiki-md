# Complex orthogonal matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md)

An invertible matrix over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md) preserving the symmetric bilinear form $v^Tw$: $A^TA=I$. The transpose here does not include [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md), unlike the defining condition for a [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md). For example, $\begin{pmatrix}\cosh t&i\sinh t\\-i\sinh t&\cosh t\end{pmatrix}$ is complex orthogonal for real $t$, but is not unitary when $t\ne0$.
