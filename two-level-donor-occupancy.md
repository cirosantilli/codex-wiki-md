# Two-level donor occupancy

↑ **Parent:** [Donor impurity](donor-impurity.md)

A nondegenerate donor-bound state of [energy](energy-split.md) zero and an excited representative conduction level of [energy](energy-split.md) $\Delta>0$ and degeneracy $g$ have the displayed bound [probability](probability.md) by canonical state counting. The inferred degeneracy from one measurement is $g=(P_b^{-1}-1)e^{\Delta/(k_BT)}$. At finite fixed $g$, the [probability](probability.md) decreases from1 toward $1/(1+g)$ as [temperature](temperature-split.md) grows. A temperature-independent effective degeneracy is an assumed two-level approximation; a full conduction-band [density of states](density-of-states.md) can behave differently.
