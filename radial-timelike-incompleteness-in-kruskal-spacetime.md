# Radial timelike incompleteness in Kruskal spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Kruskal spacetime](kruskal-spacetime.md)

Every maximal radial [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) in positive-mass [Kruskal spacetime](kruskal-spacetime.md) is incomplete in at least one direction. Its equations are $\dot r^2=E^2-1+2M/r$ and $\ddot r=-M/r^2$. A finite turning point is a maximum, and each trajectory reaches $r=0$ at one or both ends. The remaining [proper time](proper-time.md) is finite because $d\tau\sim\sqrt{r/(2M)}\,dr$. Complete circular timelike geodesics exist, but they have nonzero [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
