<h1 id="scheffe-lemma">Scheffé lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md)

For nonnegative [integrable](integrability.md) functions converging [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) to an integrable $f$, convergence of their integrals implies [L1 convergence](convergence-in-l1.md). Indeed $\min(f_n,f)\to f$ almost everywhere and is dominated by $f$, so the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) gives convergence of its integral. Use $|f_n-f|=f_n+f-2\min(f_n,f)$. This applies to [probability densities](probability-density.md), giving convergence in [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md).
