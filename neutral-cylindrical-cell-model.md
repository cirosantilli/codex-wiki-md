# Neutral cylindrical cell model

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)

Confine the [counterions](counterion.md) of a uniformly charged infinite cylinder within a coaxial cylinder of radius $R$, with total mobile line charge $\lambda$. The [electric field](electric-field.md) vanishes at $R$, while its surface value at $a$ is fixed by the bare line charge. This gives $a\phi'(a)=2\lambda/\epsilon$ and $R\phi'(R)=0$ in [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md). Sending $R/a\to\infty$ defines the [infinite-dilution limit of cylindrical counterions](infinite-dilution-limit-of-cylindrical-counterions.md).
