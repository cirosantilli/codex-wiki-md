# TurboID

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

[TurboID](turboid.md) is an engineered promiscuous [biotin](biotin.md) ligase used for [proximity labeling](proximity-labeling.md). A fusion can label nearby [proteins](protein-split.md) during a short pulse, while localization-matched and no-added-biotin controls help distinguish the intended neighbourhood from background.[Fig. 2, short-pulse compartment labeling and specificity comparisons](efficient-proximity-labeling-in-living-cells-and-organisms-with-turboid-by-tess-c-branon-and-others.md)
