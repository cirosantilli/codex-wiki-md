<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $u_1,u_2$ satisfy the same [boundary value problem](../../../../boundary-value-problem-split.md), their difference $w=u_1-u_2$ satisfies $Lw=0$ in $D$ and $w=0$ on the boundary, by linearity. Part (b)(ii) gives

$$
w\leq\max_{\partial D}w=0.
$$

Apply it again to $-w$, which also satisfies $L(-w)=0$ and zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). This gives $-w\leq0$. Hence $w=0$ throughout $\overline D$, proving

$$
\boxed{u_1=u_2.}
$$

This proves uniqueness whenever a solution exists; existence is the separately supplied assumption. When the drift coefficients vanish, the same argument uses part (a).
