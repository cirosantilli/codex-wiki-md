<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the [elliptic differential operator](../../../../../elliptic-differential-operator.md) as $L=\Delta+r\partial_x+s\partial_y$. At an interior maximum, $u_x=u_y=0$ and $u_{xx}+u_{yy}\leq0$. Therefore $Lu\leq0$ there, contradicting $Lu=f>0$.

The [extreme value theorem](../../../../../extreme-value-theorem.md) ensures that a maximum exists on the compact closed disk. It cannot lie in the interior, so **the maximum is attained on $\partial D$**. The drift terms disappear at a stationary point, which is why the strict-sign proof is so short.
