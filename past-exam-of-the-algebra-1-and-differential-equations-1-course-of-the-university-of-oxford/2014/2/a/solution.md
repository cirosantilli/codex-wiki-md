<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a [basis](../../../../basis.md) and let $A$ be the matrix of $T$. Use the monic convention

$$
\boxed{\chi_T(x)=\det(xI_n-A).}
$$

Under a [change of basis](../../../../change-of-basis.md), $A$ becomes $B=P^{-1}AP$ for an [invertible matrix](../../../../invertible-matrix.md) $P$. Then

$$
xI-B=P^{-1}(xI-A)P,
$$

so [multiplicativity of the determinant](../../../../multiplicativity-of-the-determinant.md) gives $\det(xI-B)=\det(xI-A)$. Therefore the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is independent of the chosen [basis](../../../../basis.md) and is well-defined for the [linear transformation](../../../../linear-map.md) itself.
