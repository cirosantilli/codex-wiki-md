<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Rearrange $M^TJM=J$ as

$$
\boxed{M^{-1}=J^{-1}M^TJ.}
$$

Thus $M^{-1}$ is similar to $M^T$. Similar [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) have the same [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) by part (a), and transposition also preserves it:

$$
\det(xI-M^T)=\det((xI-M)^T)=\det(xI-M).
$$

Therefore $\boxed{\chi_{M^{-1}}=\chi_M}$. This is the underlying algebraic source of the [reciprocal spectrum of a symplectic matrix](../../../../../reciprocal-spectrum-of-a-symplectic-matrix.md).
