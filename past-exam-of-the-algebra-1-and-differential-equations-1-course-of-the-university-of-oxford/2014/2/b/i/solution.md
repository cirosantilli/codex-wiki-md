<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The given $J$ satisfies $J^2=-I_{2m}$ and is invertible. Taking [determinants](../../../../../determinant.md) in the defining identity of a [symplectic matrix](../../../../../symplectic-matrix.md) gives

$$
\det(M^T)\det J\det M=\det J.
$$

Since $\det(M^T)=\det M$ and $\det J\ne0$, cancellation yields $(\det M)^2=1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\det M=1\text{ or }-1.}
$$

In particular every [symplectic matrix](../../../../../symplectic-matrix.md) is invertible. This argument establishes the requested alternatives; the later parts explicitly supply the determinant-one hypothesis that they use.
