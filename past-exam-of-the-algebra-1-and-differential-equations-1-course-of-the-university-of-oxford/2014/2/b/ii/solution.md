<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The identity is symplectic because $I^TJI=J$. For two [symplectic matrices](../../../../../symplectic-matrix.md) $M,N$,

$$
(MN)^TJ(MN)=N^T(M^TJM)N=N^TJN=J.
$$

Thus the set is closed under matrix multiplication. Every member is invertible by part (i). Multiplying $M^TJM=J$ on the left by $M^{-T}$ and on the right by $M^{-1}$ gives

$$
(M^{-1})^TJM^{-1}=J,
$$

so it is also closed under inverses. Associativity is inherited from the [general linear group](../../../../../general-linear-group.md). These facts prove that **the [symplectic group](../../../../../symplectic-group.md) is a [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) of $\operatorname{GL}(2m,\mathbb R)$**.
