<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For fixed $a\ne0$ and $B>0$, the rectangle

$$
K=[|a|/2,3|a|/2]\times[-B,B]
$$

is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) and stays away from $x=0$. The [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $f$ in part (i) is continuous there. The [extreme value theorem](../../../../../extreme-value-theorem.md) makes $|f|$ attain a finite maximum $M_0$ on $K$; choosing $\boxed{M=M_0+1}$ gives the requested strict bound.

The absolute values make this a positive-$x$ rectangle even when $a<0$. Its boundedness is still true, but for a negative initial point the [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](../../../../../picard-lindelof-theorem.md) must instead be applied to a rectangle centered at that negative point, as in part (iii).
