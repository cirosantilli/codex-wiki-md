<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Dividing by $x^2$, which is allowed precisely away from zero, gives

$$
\boxed{f(x,y)=\frac{1-2x}{x^2}y+\frac{e^{-1/x}}{x^2(1+x^2)},\qquad x\ne0.}
$$

This [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is smooth on each of the two half-planes $x>0$ and $x<0$, and is affine in the dependent variable $y$. Its derivative with respect to $y$ is $(1-2x)/x^2$.
