<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [Fourier transform](../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) convention

$$
\boxed{\widehat v(\omega)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}v(x)e^{-i\omega x}\,dx.}
$$

The [absolute integrability](../../../../integrability.md) of $v$ makes this integral exist for every real $\omega$, with $|\widehat v(\omega)|\leq\|v\|_1$. With this convention the [inverse Fourier transform](../../../../inverse-fourier-transform.md) has factor $1/(2\pi)$; maintaining that normalization is essential in part (e).
