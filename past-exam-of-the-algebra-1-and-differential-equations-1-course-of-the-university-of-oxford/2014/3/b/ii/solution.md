<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The restriction of a [positive definite](../../../../../positive-definiteness.md) form to each $V_m$ is positive definite. By the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md), choose an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) $P$ such that $P^TA_mP=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_m)$.

Each $d_i=\varphi(f_i,f_i)>0$, since a basis vector is nonzero. Taking [determinants](../../../../../determinant.md) in part (i) gives

$$
\det(P^TA_mP)=(\det P)^2\det A_m=\det A_m,
$$

because an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) has determinant $\pm1$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\det A_m=\prod_{i=1}^m d_i>0\quad(1\leq m\leq n).}
$$

These determinants are the leading [principal minors](../../../../../principal-minor.md) in [Sylvester's criterion](../../../../../sylvester-s-criterion.md).
