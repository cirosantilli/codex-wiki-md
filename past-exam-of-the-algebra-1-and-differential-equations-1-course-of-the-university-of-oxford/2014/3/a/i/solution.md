The **[rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md)** states that for a [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $A:X\to Y$ with finite-dimensional domain,

$$
\boxed{\dim X=\dim\ker A+\dim\operatorname{im}A.}
$$

Equivalently, nullity plus rank equals the dimension of the domain. One proof extends a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $\ker A$ to a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $X$; the images of the added vectors form a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $\operatorname{im}A$.
