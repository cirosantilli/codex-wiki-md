<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Under the [bidual evaluation map](../../../../../bidual-evaluation-map.md), a vector $y$ lies in $(\operatorname{im}\Phi|_U)^\circ$ exactly when

$$
j(y)(\varphi_x)=\varphi_x(y)=\varphi(x,y)=0\quad\text{for all }x\in U.
$$

That is precisely membership of the [orthogonal complement for a bilinear form](../../../../../orthogonal-complement-for-a-bilinear-form.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{U^\perp=(\operatorname{im}\Phi|_U)^\circ.}
$$

The dimension formula in part (ii) gives

$$
\dim U^\perp=n-\operatorname{rank}(\Phi|_U)\geq n-\dim U,
$$

so $\boxed{\dim U+\dim U^\perp\geq n}$. More precisely, the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives

$$
\dim U+\dim U^\perp=n+\dim(U\cap\ker\Phi).
$$

This is the [orthogonal complement dimension for a degenerate bilinear form](../../../../../orthogonal-complement-dimension-for-a-degenerate-bilinear-form.md). The possible excess comes from vectors annihilated by the form against all of $V$.
