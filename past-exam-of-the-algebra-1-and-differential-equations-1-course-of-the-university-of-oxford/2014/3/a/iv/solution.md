<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [dimension formula for a sum of subspaces](../../../../../dimension-formula-for-a-sum-of-subspaces.md) and the zero intersection imply

$$
\dim(U+U^\perp)=\dim U+\dim U^\perp\geq n.
$$

But $U+U^\perp$ is a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of the $n$-dimensional space $V$, so its dimension is at most $n$. Equality follows, and the sum is all of $V$. Since the intersection is zero, it is a [direct sum](../../../../../direct-sum.md):

$$
\boxed{V=U\oplus U^\perp.}
$$

The argument remains valid for a degenerate ambient [symmetric bilinear form](../../../../../symmetric-bilinear-form.md); it uses the stated intersection hypothesis rather than assuming nondegeneracy everywhere.
