<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $E^2=E$. Every [vector](../../../../../vector.md) has the decomposition

$$
v=Ev+(v-Ev),\qquad Ev\in\operatorname{im}E,\quad v-Ev\in\ker E.
$$

If $w=Ez$ also belongs to $\ker E$, then $w=E^2z=Ew=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{V=\operatorname{im}E\oplus\ker E.}
$$

On its [image of a linear map](../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md), $E$ acts as the identity; on its [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), it acts as zero. Concatenate a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of the image with a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of the kernel. The resulting [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is $\operatorname{diag}(I_r,0)$, where $r=\operatorname{rank}E$.

Conversely, this block [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) squares to itself, so any [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) having that matrix in a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) satisfies $E^2=E$. Thus **[projection operators](../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) are precisely the [linear maps](../../../../../linear-map.md) with this block form**. This is an algebraic projection; no [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) or orthogonality is required.
