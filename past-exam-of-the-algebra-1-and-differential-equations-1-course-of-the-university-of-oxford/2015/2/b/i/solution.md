The [primary decomposition theorem for linear maps](../../../../../primary-decomposition-theorem-for-linear-maps.md) states that if

$$
m_T(x)=\prod_{j=1}^r p_j(x)^{e_j}
$$

with distinct monic [irreducible polynomials](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) $p_j$, then

$$
\boxed{V=\bigoplus_{j=1}^r\ker\bigl(p_j(T)^{e_j}\bigr).}
$$

Each summand is a $T$-[invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md). More generally the same [direct sum](../../../../../direct-sum.md) conclusion holds for any factorization of an annihilating [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) into pairwise [coprime polynomials](../../../../../coprime-polynomials.md), using the corresponding kernels. The underlying mechanism is the [Bézout's identity for polynomials](../../../../../bezout-s-identity-for-polynomials.md), which separates the mutually coprime factors.
