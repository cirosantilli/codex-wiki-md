<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $v\in V_\lambda$, use commutation to obtain

$$
T(Sv)=S(Tv)=S(\lambda v)=\lambda Sv.
$$

Thus $Sv\in V_\lambda$, proving that **every $T$-[eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) is an $S$-[invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md)**. This does not assert that an arbitrary [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $T$ is already an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $S$.
