<h1 id="2/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Suppose a single [basis](../../../../../basis.md) gives the [diagonal matrices](../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md) $D_S,D_T$. Entrywise multiplication gives $D_SD_T=D_TD_S$. If $P$ is the [change-of-basis matrix](../../../../../change-of-basis-matrix.md), then

$$
ST=P D_SD_TP^{-1}=P D_TD_SP^{-1}=TS.
$$

Thus **[simultaneous diagonalization](../../../../../simultaneous-diagonalization.md) implies commutation**.
