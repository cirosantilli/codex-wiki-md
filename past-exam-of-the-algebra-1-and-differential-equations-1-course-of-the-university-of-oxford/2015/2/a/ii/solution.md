↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [quadratic polynomial](../../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) $x^2+4x+10=(x+2)^2+6$ has no real [roots of a polynomial](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md). Thus the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) does not split over the [real numbers](../../../../../real-number.md). A real [diagonal matrix](../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md) would have only real [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), and [similar matrices](../../../../../matrix-similarity.md) have the same [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md). Therefore **$A$ is not [diagonalizable](../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) over $\mathbb R$**.
