<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [linear endomorphism](../../../../linear-endomorphism.md) is [diagonalizable](../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) over its [field](../../../../field.md) precisely when its [minimal polynomial](../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is a product of **distinct [linear polynomials](../../../../linear-polynomial.md) over that same [field](../../../../field.md)**. Splitting alone is insufficient: repeated factors permit nontrivial [Jordan blocks](../../../../jordan-block.md).

For the displayed [matrix](../../../../matrix.md), a [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) expansion gives

$$
\chi_A(x)=x^3+3x^2+6x-10=(x-1)(x^2+4x+10).
$$

These formulas can be reduced in each requested [characteristic](../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md). The four cases below distinguish failure to split from failure to have distinct factors.
