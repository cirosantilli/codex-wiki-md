<h1 id="2/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In the [finite field](../../../../../finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_2$, the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) becomes

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

On the first two coordinate vectors it is a nonzero [nilpotent](../../../../../nilpotent.md) [Jordan block](../../../../../jordan-block.md) of size two, and on the last it is the [identity map](../../../../../identity-function.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{m_A(x)=x^2(x-1)\quad\text{over }\mathbb F_2.}
$$

The repeated zero root prevents [diagonalizability](../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md). Directly, the only possible [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are zero and one, so [diagonalizability](../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) would imply $A^2=A$, whereas the displayed off-diagonal entry disappears on squaring.
