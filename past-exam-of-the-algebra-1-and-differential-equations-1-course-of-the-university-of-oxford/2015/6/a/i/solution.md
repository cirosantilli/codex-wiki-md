<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Introduce the position and velocity variables $y_1=y$ and $y_2=y'$. Then the required [system of ordinary differential equations](../../../../../system-of-ordinary-differential-equations.md) and [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf y'=\mathbf f(x,\mathbf y),\qquad
\mathbf f(x,(y_1,y_2))=\begin{pmatrix}y_2\\F(x,y_1,y_2)\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf y(a)=\begin{pmatrix}c\\d\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Thus $f_1(x,\mathbf y)=y_2$. Conversely, the first equation identifies $y_2=y_1'$, and the second recovers the original second-order [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) and both initial data.
