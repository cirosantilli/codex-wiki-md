The [dual basis](../../../../../dual-basis.md) is the collection of [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md) characterized by

$$
\boxed{e'_i(e_j)=\delta_{ij}.}
$$

Explicitly, $e'_i(\sum_j\alpha_je_j)=\alpha_i$. Every [linear functional](../../../../../linear-functional.md) $\ell\in V'$ satisfies $\ell=\sum_i\ell(e_i)e'_i$, proving that the displayed functionals span the [dual space](../../../../../dual-space-split.md). Evaluating a zero [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) at each $e_j$ makes all its coefficients zero, so they are also [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md). Thus they form a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $V'$.
