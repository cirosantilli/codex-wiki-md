<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take $v\in U^\perp$ and $u\in U$. Because $U$ is an [invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md), $Tu\in U$. The [adjoint operator](../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) relation yields

$$
\langle Tv,u\rangle=\langle v,T^*u\rangle=-\langle v,Tu\rangle=0.
$$

This holds for every $u\in U$, so $Tv\in U^\perp$. Therefore **the [orthogonal complement](../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) is also an [invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md)**.
