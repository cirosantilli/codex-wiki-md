<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the convention of part (a): the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) is linear in its first argument. If $Tv=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$, the [adjoint operator](../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) identity and $T^*=-T$ give

$$
\lambda\langle v,v\rangle=\langle Tv,v\rangle
=\langle v,T^*v\rangle=-\langle v,Tv\rangle
=-\overline\lambda\langle v,v\rangle.
$$

[Positive definiteness](../../../../../positive-definiteness.md) makes $\langle v,v\rangle>0$, so

$$
\boxed{\lambda=-\overline\lambda,\qquad\operatorname{Re}\lambda=0.}
$$

Thus every [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of a [skew-adjoint operator](../../../../../skew-adjoint-generator.md) is purely imaginary, including zero.
