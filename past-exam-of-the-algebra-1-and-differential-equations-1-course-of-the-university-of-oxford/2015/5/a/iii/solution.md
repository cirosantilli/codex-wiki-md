<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Integrate the [canonical form of a second-order partial differential equation](../../../../../canonical-form-of-a-second-order-partial-differential-equation.md) first in $\eta$ and then in $\xi$. The first integration gives $U_\xi=\eta+g'(\xi)$, and the second gives

$$
U(\xi,\eta)=\xi\eta+g(\xi)+h(\eta).
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{u(x,y)=y^2-\ln^2|x|+g\bigl(y+\ln|x|\bigr)+h\bigl(y-\ln|x|\bigr).}
$$

The two arbitrary twice continuously differentiable [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) describe the two [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) families. Conversely, substituting this expression into $U_{\xi\eta}=1$ verifies every such solution. The formula applies on $x>0$ or $x<0$; independent choices can be made on the two half-planes. Smooth extension across the degenerate line $x=0$ requires extra compatibility and is not supplied by the logarithmic formula alone.
