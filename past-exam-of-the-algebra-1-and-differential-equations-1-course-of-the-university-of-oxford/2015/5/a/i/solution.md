<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a second-order [linear partial differential equation](../../../../../linear-partial-differential-equation.md) written $A u_{xx}+2B u_{xy}+C u_{yy}+\cdots=0$, the discriminant is $B^2-AC$. Here

$$
A=-x^2,\qquad B=0,\qquad C=1,\qquad
\boxed{B^2-AC=x^2.}
$$

It is positive exactly when $x\ne0$, proving that the equation is a [hyperbolic partial differential equation](../../../../../hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) on each half-plane. At $x=0$ its principal part degenerates and it is not hyperbolic.
