<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md), $\Delta u=0$ and $\Delta(-u)=0$. Apply the preceding minimum argument to $u$ and to $-u$:

$$
\boxed{\min_{\overline D}u=\min_{\partial D}u,\qquad
\max_{\overline D}u=\max_{\partial D}u.}
$$

These are the weak minimum and [maximum principle for harmonic functions](../../../../../maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md).
