<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The difference $w=v-u$ is a [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md), because the [Laplacian](../../../../../laplacian.md) is a [linear differential operator](../../../../../linear-differential-operator.md):

$$
\Delta w=\Delta v-\Delta u=0.
$$

The boundary inequality says $w\geq0$ on $\partial D$. The minimum principle from part (ii) gives

$$
\boxed{v(p)-u(p)=w(p)\geq\min_{\partial D}w\geq0\quad(p\in D).}
$$

This is the [comparison principle for harmonic functions](../../../../../comparison-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md). Applying it in both directions also shows why fixed [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) determine at most one [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md).
