<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**The proposed equivalence is false.** On a nonzero [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) over any [field](../../../../field.md) of [characteristic](../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two, choose

$$
E=I,\qquad F=I,\qquad G=0.
$$

These [linear projections](../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) commute and satisfy $EFG=0$. Nevertheless their sum is $2I$, and

$$
\boxed{(2I)^2-2I=2I\ne0.}
$$

Thus the proposed conditions are not sufficient. For commuting [linear projections](../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) the actual obstruction is the sum of the pairwise products:

$$
(E+F+G)^2-(E+F+G)=2(EF+EG+FG).
$$

The single condition on the triple product does not remove this obstruction.
