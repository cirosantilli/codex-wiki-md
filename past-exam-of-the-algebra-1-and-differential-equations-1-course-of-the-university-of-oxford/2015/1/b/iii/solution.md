↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**No.** On any nonzero [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over a [field](../../../../../field.md) of [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) two, take $E=F=I$. Both are [linear projections](../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md), and

$$
\boxed{E+F=0\text{ is a linear projection},\qquad EF=FE=I\ne0.}
$$

The correct condition from part (i) in this [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) is $EF=FE$, because minus and plus coincide. Vanishing of both products remains sufficient, but is no longer necessary.
