<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expand the square using $E^2=E$ and $F^2=F$:

$$
(E+F)^2-(E+F)=EF+FE.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{E+F\text{ is a linear projection}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad EF=-FE.}
$$

This [sum of two linear projections](../../../../../sum-of-two-linear-projections.md) criterion holds in every [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md); it uses an equality of [linear maps](../../../../../linear-map.md), not cancellation of factors in the noncommutative [associative algebra](../../../../../associative-algebra-split.md) of [linear endomorphisms](../../../../../linear-endomorphism.md).
