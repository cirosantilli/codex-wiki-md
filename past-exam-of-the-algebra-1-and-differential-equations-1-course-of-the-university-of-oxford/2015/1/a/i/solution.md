<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The identity $T^2=T$ gives the decomposition

$$
v=(v-Tv)+Tv,\qquad T(v-Tv)=0.
$$

The first summand lies in the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) $\ker T$, and the second in the [image of a linear map](../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) $\operatorname{im}T$. If $w$ belongs to their intersection, write $w=Tu$; then $Tw=T^2u=Tu=w$, while $Tw=0$. Thus $w=0$, proving the [direct sum](../../../../../direct-sum.md)

$$
\boxed{V=\ker T\oplus\operatorname{im}T.}
$$

On these two [invariant subspaces](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md), $T$ is respectively zero and the [identity map](../../../../../identity-function.md). Joining a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of each produces a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md), with [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{diag}(0,\ldots,0,1,\ldots,1).}
$$

Hence every [linear projection](../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) is [diagonalizable](../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md), over the original [field](../../../../../field.md), including in [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) two.
