<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Factor each component of the [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md). The horizontal-velocity [nullclines](../../../../../nullcline.md) and vertical-velocity [nullclines](../../../../../nullcline.md) are, respectively,

$$
\boxed{\dot x=0:\ x=0,\ y=1,\ x+y=1;\qquad
\dot y=0:\ y=0,\ xy=2.}
$$

The last curve is a [hyperbola](../../../../../hyperbola.md). In the nonnegative quadrant its branch is $y=2/x$ for $x>0$.
