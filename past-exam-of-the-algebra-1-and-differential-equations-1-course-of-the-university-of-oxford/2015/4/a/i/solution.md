An [autonomous differential equation](../../../../../autonomous-system-mathematics.md) has no explicit dependence on the independent time variable: its [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) depends only on the current state. The two families of [nullclines](../../../../../nullcline.md) are

$$
\boxed{X(x,y)=0\quad\text{and}\quad Y(x,y)=0.}
$$

On the first family the horizontal velocity is zero; on the second the vertical velocity is zero. A [critical point of a dynamical system](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is a state where both components vanish. It gives a constant solution, also called an [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md).
