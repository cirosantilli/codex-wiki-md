# Nuclear chain reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_chain_reaction)

A sequence in which particles released by one [nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md) induce further reactions. In a neutron-driven fission chain, [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md), leakage and non-fission absorption compete with neutron production. A sustained chain requires an effective reproduction factor of one; whether material is [fissile material](fissile-material.md) cannot be determined from a single positive fission [Q value](q-value.md).
