# Place of a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)

A place of a [number field](number-field.md) $K$ is an equivalence class of [absolute values on a field](absolute-value-algebra.md). Its Archimedean places come from real embeddings and conjugate pairs of complex embeddings. Its non-Archimedean places correspond to nonzero prime ideals of the [ring of integers of a number field](ring-of-integers.md).

Equivalence is taken among [absolute values on a field](absolute-value-algebra.md), so changing a representative within one place does not change the associated topology.
