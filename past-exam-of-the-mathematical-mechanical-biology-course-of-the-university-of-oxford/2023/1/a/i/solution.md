<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [material frame of a rod](../../../../../material-frame-of-a-rod.md) obeys the [director kinematics of a rod](../../../../../director-kinematics-of-a-rod.md)

$$
\mathbf d_i'=\mathbf u\times\mathbf d_i.
$$

In the stress-free [reference configuration](../../../../../reference-configuration.md), the [constitutive equation](../../../../../constitutive-equation.md) gives $u_1=0$, $u_2=\hat u$, and $u_3=0$, since all three stiffnesses are positive. Thus $\mathbf u=\hat u\mathbf d_2$. Its [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) with $\mathbf d_2$ vanishes, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf d_2'=\hat u\mathbf d_2\times\mathbf d_2=\mathbf0.}
$$

Consequently this director is a fixed vector in space, even though the other two directors rotate along the [elastic rod](../../../../../elastic-rod.md).
