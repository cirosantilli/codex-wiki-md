<h1 id="1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Zero applied end [force](../../../../../force-split.md) and the [force balance](../../../../../force-balance.md) $\mathbf n'=\mathbf0$ imply $\mathbf n(S)=\mathbf0$ everywhere. The [moment balance](../../../../../moment-balance.md) then reduces to $\mathbf m'=\mathbf0$.

Choose an untwisted straight configuration: all three directors are constant, $\mathbf r'=\mathbf d_3$, and $\mathbf u=\mathbf0$. The [constitutive equation](../../../../../constitutive-equation.md) gives

$$
\mathbf m=-EI_2\hat u\mathbf d_2,
$$

which is constant in space and therefore satisfies the [moment balance](../../../../../moment-balance.md). The required end [bending moment](../../../../../bending-moment.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{m_2=-EI_2\hat u.}
$$

Its sign opposes the positive [intrinsic curvature of an elastic filament](../../../../../intrinsic-curvature-of-an-elastic-filament.md). If the initial position and frame from part (ii) are retained, this solution is simply $\mathbf r(S)=S\mathbf e_3$ with $\mathbf d_i=\mathbf e_i$; the clamp supplies the opposite reaction [moment](../../../../../moment.md) at the other end.
