<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $s=\sigma$ up to an irrelevant additive constant, $r=a$ constant, $z=z_0-\sigma$, and $\theta=\pi/2$. These satisfy the geometric relations and give the [principal stretches](../../../../../principal-stretch.md)

$$
\lambda_s=1,\qquad\lambda_\varphi=\frac a{a_0}.
$$

The [constitutive equations](../../../../../constitutive-equation.md) therefore produce constant [membrane tensions](../../../../../membrane-tension.md)

$$
t_s=f_s(1,a/a_0),\qquad t_\varphi=f_\varphi(1,a/a_0).
$$

The tangential [mechanical equilibrium](../../../../../mechanical-equilibrium.md) equations hold automatically, while the normal equation reduces to

$$
\boxed{Pa=f_\varphi\left(1,\frac a{a_0}\right).}
$$

Every positive root of this algebraic equation provides a cylindrical solution with no axial [stretch ratio](../../../../../stretch-ratio.md) change. There is no further constraint on $t_s$ in the interior equations when end [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) are discarded. In particular, a closed-end pressure balance would impose additional axial loading data that are absent here. Existence of a positive root for an arbitrary specified $P$ depends on the given [constitutive equation](../../../../../constitutive-equation.md).
