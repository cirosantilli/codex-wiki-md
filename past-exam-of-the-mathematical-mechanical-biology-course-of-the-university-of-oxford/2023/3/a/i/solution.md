<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Before contact with both walls, the [elastic rod](../../../../../elastic-rod.md) can grow freely, so its elastic [stretch ratio](../../../../../stretch-ratio.md) is $\alpha=1$ and its length is its stress-free grown length,

$$
\ell(t)=L_0\gamma(t)=L_0(1+ct).
$$

The first contact occurs when $\ell(t_c)=L_1$. For the intended growth case $c>0$,

$$
\boxed{t_c=\frac{L_1/L_0-1}{c}=\frac{L_1-L_0}{cL_0}.}
$$

The condition $L_1>L_0$ ensures $t_c>0$. If $c=0$, contact never occurs; negative $c$ describes shrinkage rather than the stated continuing growth.
