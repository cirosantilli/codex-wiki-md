<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The kinematic relation is unchanged: $\alpha=1$ before contact and $\alpha=\lambda/(1+ct)$ afterwards. Therefore the nonlinear [constitutive equation](../../../../../constitutive-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{n(t)=\begin{cases}
0,&0\le t\le t_c,\\
E_s\left[\dfrac{\lambda^2}{(1+ct)^2}-\dfrac{1+ct}{\lambda}\right],&t>t_c.
\end{cases}}
$$

The first term in the post-contact expression is $O(t^{-2})$. Hence its [asymptotic expansion](../../../../../asymptotic-expansion-split.md) is

$$
n(t)=-\frac{E_sc}{\lambda}t-\frac{E_s}{\lambda}+O(t^{-2}),\qquad
\boxed{n(t)\sim-\frac{E_scL_0}{L_1}t\quad(t\to\infty).}
$$

In this model the compressive [axial stress](../../../../../axial-stress.md) diverges in magnitude, because the $-\alpha^{-1}$ term penalizes vanishing elastic [stretch ratio](../../../../../stretch-ratio.md) without bound.
