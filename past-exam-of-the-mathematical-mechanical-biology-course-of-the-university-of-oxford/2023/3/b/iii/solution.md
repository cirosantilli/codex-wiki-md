<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The end displacement relative to the initial unit axial length is

$$
\delta=\ell-1=\alpha\gamma-1.
$$

By [Hooke's law](../../../../../hooke-s-law.md), the total resisting [force](../../../../../force-split.md) supplied by the spring is $F_3=-K\delta$. To convert this resultant into the axial [Cauchy stress tensor](../../../../../cauchy-stress-tensor.md), divide by the current end [cross-sectional area](../../../../../cross-sectional-area.md). The transverse total stretches are both $\alpha^{-1/2}$, so that area is

$$
\mathcal A=\alpha^{-1/2}\alpha^{-1/2}=\alpha^{-1}.
$$

Uniform [traction](../../../../../traction.md) over that face consequently gives

$$
t_3=\frac{F_3}{\mathcal A}=-K\alpha(\alpha\gamma-1).
$$

Equate this to the expression from part (ii):

$$
\boxed{-K\alpha(\alpha\gamma-1)=\alpha\hat W'(\alpha).}
$$

The extra factor $\alpha$ on the left is essential: the spring law specifies a total [force](../../../../../force-split.md), whereas the material [constitutive equation](../../../../../constitutive-equation.md) specifies [force](../../../../../force-split.md) per current area. The reference end area is one, so the same spring resultant can also be read as a nominal end [traction](../../../../../traction.md).
