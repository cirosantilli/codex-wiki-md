# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the mathematical mechanical biology course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions and all sixteen terminal parts of the [mathematical mechanical biology course of the University of Oxford](../mathematical-mechanical-biology-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) examination, sat on 7 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, inclusive bundle pages 335–338; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=335). In Question 1(b), [bending strain of a rod](../bending-strain-of-a-rod.md) is measured per reference [arc length](../arc-length.md), exactly as in the printed kinematic identities. In Question 3(b), [elastic incompressibility](../elastic-incompressibility.md) constrains the elastic part of the [deformation gradient](../deformation-gradient.md); growth can still increase the total volume.
