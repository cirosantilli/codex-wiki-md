# Closure of the inverse of a nondegenerate Poisson bivector

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson bivector](poisson-bivector.md)

A nondegenerate [Poisson bivector](poisson-bivector.md) inverts to a [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) of degree two. Differentiating $S=P^{-1}$ gives $\partial_dS=-S(\partial_dP)S$. Contracting the [coordinate Jacobi condition for a Poisson bivector](coordinate-jacobi-condition-for-a-poisson-bivector.md) with three copies of $S$ yields $\partial_iS_{jk}+\partial_jS_{ki}+\partial_kS_{ij}=0$, which is precisely closure of the resulting [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md). An overall sign in the inverse convention does not change closure.
