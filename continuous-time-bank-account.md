# Continuous-time bank account

↑ **Parent:** [Bank account](bank-account.md)

The value of an account that continuously reinvests at the [short rate](short-rate.md) $r$, with $B_0=1$. Its reciprocal is the [discount factor](discount-factor.md) $D_t=B_t^{-1}$.
