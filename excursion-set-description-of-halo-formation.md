# Excursion-set description of halo formation

↑ **Parent:** [Press-Schechter formalism](press-schechter-formalism.md)

A halo is associated with the first upcrossing of the [linear spherical-collapse threshold](linear-spherical-collapse-threshold.md) by the smoothed [density contrast](density-contrast.md) as its variance increases. With a [sharp-k smoothing filter](sharp-k-smoothing-filter.md), the [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) makes the crossing probability twice the one-scale Gaussian upper tail. This handles trajectories that crossed earlier but finish below the barrier and leads to the [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md).
