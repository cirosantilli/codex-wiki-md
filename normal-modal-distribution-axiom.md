# Normal modal distribution axiom

↑ **Parent:** [Modal logic](modal-logic.md)

The normal distribution axiom says that a boxed implication and its boxed antecedent give the boxed consequent. Together with the [necessitation rule](necessitation-rule.md), it permits boxed versions of propositional deductions. Under an arithmetic interpretation it is the internal modus-ponens condition among the [Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions](hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md). It does not permit dropping a box to assert the enclosed sentence.
