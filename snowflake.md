# Snowflake

↑ **Parent:** [Ice](ice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowflake)

A snowflake is an [ice](ice.md) crystal or an aggregate of [ice](ice.md) crystals grown in atmospheric conditions. Its morphology depends on vapour supersaturation, transport, [temperature](temperature.md) and crystal anisotropy. Not every snowflake is dendritic.
