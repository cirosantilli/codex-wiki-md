# Polymer mass fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

The fraction of measured material assigned to [polymer](polymer-split.md) under a stated separation or detection criterion. For corrected pellet and [supernatant](supernatant.md) measurements, $f_P=P/(P+S)$; the assignment is valid only if [sedimentation](sedimentation.md) separates the intended species.
