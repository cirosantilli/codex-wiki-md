# Absoluteness of infinitude between transitive models

↑ **Parent:** [Set-theoretic absoluteness](set-theoretic-absoluteness.md)

Transitive models of [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) have the same [natural numbers](natural-number.md) and agree on whether a shared set is a [finite set](finite-set.md). Consequently they also agree on whether it is an [infinite set](infinite-set.md).
