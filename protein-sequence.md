# Protein sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md)

A [protein sequence](protein-sequence.md) records the order of [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) in a [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md). The sequence alone does not specify every modification, covalent crosslink, assembly state or conformation present in a purified sample.
