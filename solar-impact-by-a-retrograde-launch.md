# Solar impact by a retrograde launch

↑ **Parent:** [Patched conic approximation](patched-conic-approximation.md)

In the circular-Earth-orbit, unpowered [patched conic approximation](patched-conic-approximation.md), the least Earth-relative excess [speed](speed.md) for direct solar-surface contact lowers the heliocentric tangential [speed](speed.md) to that of an [ellipse](ellipse.md) with [apocentre](apoapsis.md) $a$ and [pericentre](periapsis.md) $R_\odot$. The [vis-viva equation](vis-viva-equation.md) gives $v_a=v_E\sqrt{2R_\odot/(a+R_\odot)}$, so the retrograde excess magnitude is $v_E-v_a$ and the terrestrial surface launch [speed](speed.md) is the title expression. Cancelling all heliocentric [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) instead produces radial fall to a point Sun and the larger estimate $\sqrt{v_{e,E}^2+v_E^2}$. Rotation, planetary gravity assists and atmospheric [drag](drag-physics.md) are outside this direct-flight model.
