# Parallel spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor covariant derivative](spinor-covariant-derivative.md)

A parallel [spinor field](spinor-field.md) is annihilated by the [spinor covariant derivative](spinor-covariant-derivative.md). Its value at one point determines it on a connected domain by [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md). The [spinor curvature identity](spinor-curvature-identity.md) implies $R_{\mu\nu AB}\gamma^{AB}\epsilon=0$, but this local condition alone need not settle global existence: the [spin structure](spin-structure.md) and [holonomy](holonomy.md) also matter. Lorentzian parallel spinors need not force the whole [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) to vanish, unlike the positive-definite case.
