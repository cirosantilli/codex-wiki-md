# Christos Papadimitriou

↑ **Parent:** [List of computer scientists](list-of-computer-scientists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christos_Papadimitriou)

A [computer scientist](computer-scientist.md) working on [algorithms](algorithm-split.md), [computational complexity theory](computational-complexity-theory.md) and [game theory](game-theory-split.md). Co-author with [Xiaotie Deng](xiaotie-deng.md) of [On the Complexity of Cooperative Solution Concepts by Xiaotie Deng and Christos Papadimitriou](on-the-complexity-of-cooperative-solution-concepts-by-xiaotie-deng-and-christos-papadimitriou.md).

Primary profile: [Columbia University faculty profile](https://www.engineering.columbia.edu/faculty-staff/directory/christos-papadimitriou).
