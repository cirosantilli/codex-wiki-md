# Bragg-Williams free energy of the Ising model

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field theory of the Ising model](mean-field-theory-of-the-ising-model.md)

A product trial distribution with actual mean [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) $m$ gives

$$
\frac{A_{\rm BW}}N=-\frac{qJ}{2}m^2-hm+k_BT\left[\frac{1+m}{2}\log\frac{1+m}{2}+\frac{1-m}{2}\log\frac{1-m}{2}\right].
$$

Its [entropy](entropy.md) is the sum of independent-spin entropies, with $0\log0=0$. Differentiation gives $k_BT\operatorname{arctanh}m=qJm+h$, the [self-consistency equation](self-consistency-equation.md). The variational mean parameter is distinct from the assumed field parameter of the [Ising auxiliary mean-field free energy](ising-auxiliary-mean-field-free-energy.md) until the equilibrium condition is imposed.
