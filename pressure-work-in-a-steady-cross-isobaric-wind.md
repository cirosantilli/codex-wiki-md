# Pressure work in a steady cross-isobaric wind

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic wind with linear drag](geostrophic-wind-with-linear-drag.md)

Dot the steady linear-drag momentum equation with the velocity. The [Coriolis force does no work](coriolis-force-does-no-work.md), so pressure-gradient work balances frictional dissipation. In a strictly geostrophic flow the wind is along isobars and pressure work vanishes. Cross-isobar motion supplies the positive pressure work needed to maintain a dissipative equilibrium.
