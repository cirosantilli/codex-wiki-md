# Alternant character formula for the general linear group

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md)

For a dominant integer tuple $\lambda$ and $\delta=(m-1,\ldots,0)$, the rational Schur [character](character-of-a-representation.md) is $\det(x_i^{\lambda_j+m-j})/\det(x_i^{m-j})$. For partitions it is a [symmetric polynomial](symmetric-polynomial.md); for general tuples it is Laurent on the diagonal torus. The formula follows by comparing the [trace of a permuted tensor power](trace-of-a-permuted-tensor-power.md) with the [Frobenius alternant character formula](frobenius-alternant-character-formula.md) and using independence of symmetric-group [characters](character-of-a-representation.md). Apparent poles at coincident nonzero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are removable.
