# Haar scaling function

↑ **Parent:** [Haar wavelet](haar-wavelet.md)

At level $J$, a [Haar scaling function](haar-scaling-function.md) is the normalized [indicator function](indicator-function.md) $2^{J/2}\mathbf1_{I_{J,k}}$ of a [dyadic interval](dyadic-interval.md). The [functions](function-split.md) at that level form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of the cellwise-constant subspace. Their normalization is what turns white-noise coefficient variances into $1/n$ and cellwise variances into $2^J/n$.
