# Torus-invariance of the singular locus of a toric variety

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit-cone correspondence](orbit-cone-correspondence.md)

The action of the [algebraic torus](algebraic-torus.md) on a [toric variety](toric-variety.md) is by automorphisms, and automorphisms preserve regular local rings. The smooth and singular loci are therefore unions of torus orbits.
