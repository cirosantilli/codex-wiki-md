# Three-site Abelian Higgs chain

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-boson mass matrix](gauge-boson-mass-matrix.md)

Two canonically normalized complex [scalar fields](scalar-field.md) of [vacuum](vacuum.md) modulus $v$ and charge rows $R_1=(1,-q,0)$, $R_2=(0,1,-q)$ couple to three canonically normalized Abelian vector [fields](field.md). The scalar [kinetic terms](kinetic-term.md) give $g^2v^2|RB|^2$, so the physical squared-mass [matrix](matrix.md) includes the factor two shown. Its nonzero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $2g^2v^2(1+q^2\pm q)$ and its normalized null vector is $(q^2,q,1)/\sqrt{q^4+q^2+1}$. The two scalar phases are absorbed into the massive [vector bosons](vector-boson.md), leaving one unbroken local Abelian generator. A compact global gauge-group interpretation additionally requires a compatible charge lattice.
