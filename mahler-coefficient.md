# Mahler coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Mahler's theorem](mahler-s-theorem.md)

For a [continuous function](continuous-function.md) $f:\mathbb Z_p\to\mathbb Q_p$, its nth Mahler coefficient is

$$
a_n=\sum_{j=0}^n(-1)^{n-j}\binom nj f(j).
$$

This is the explicit iterate of the [forward difference operator](forward-difference-operator.md). These coefficients are exactly those of its [Mahler expansion](mahler-s-theorem.md).
