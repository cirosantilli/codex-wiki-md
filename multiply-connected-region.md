# Multiply connected region

↑ **Parent:** [Path-connected space](path-connected-space.md)

A [path-connected](path-connected-space.md) region is multiply connected when some closed [path](continuous-path.md) cannot contract to a point within the region. An [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) and the complement of a solid [torus](torus.md) are examples. The accessible region in the ideal magnetic [Aharonov-Bohm effect](aharonov-bohm-effect.md) has this obstruction. Such a region is not a [simply connected space](simply-connected-space.md).
