# Induced magnetosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetosphere](magnetosphere.md)

An induced magnetosphere is maintained by the interaction of an external magnetized plasma flow with a conducting ionosphere or atmosphere. Its currents and draped [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) do not establish an internal [planetary dynamo](planetary-dynamo.md). [Venus](venus.md) is a central example; [Mars](mars.md) combines this interaction with localized crustal fields.
