# Separately conserved visible and dark entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological entropy conservation](cosmological-entropy-conservation.md)

After [thermal decoupling in cosmology](thermal-decoupling-in-cosmology.md), two homogeneous sectors that evolve adiabatically without entropy injection satisfy $s_{\rm SM}a^3=\mathrm{constant}$ and $s_\phi a^3=\mathrm{constant}$, hence their [entropy density](entropy-density.md) ratio $\xi$ is fixed. At a common relativistic [temperature](temperature.md) before decoupling, $\xi=g_{*s}^{\rm SM}/g_{*s}^\phi$. Internal annihilations can change each sector's [temperature](temperature.md) and effective entropy degrees of freedom without changing its total comoving [entropy](entropy.md).
