# Single-local-gate Bell readout information

↑ **Parent:** [Superdense coding](superdense-coding.md)

For four uniformly distributed [Bell states](bell-state-split.md), measure one [qubit](qubit.md) along [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) $\boldsymbol n$ and the other along $z$. The sign within each $\Phi^\pm$ or $\Psi^\pm$ pair is invisible and the displayed [mutual information](mutual-information.md) is at most one bit. A local [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md) before a computational detector changes $n_z$ from one to zero and destroys the remaining information. An entangling Bell decoder falls outside this product-measurement restriction and recovers two bits.
