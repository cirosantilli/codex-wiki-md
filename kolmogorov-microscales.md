# Kolmogorov microscales

↑ **Parent:** [Dissipation range](dissipation-range.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolmogorov_microscales)

The Kolmogorov length, velocity and time are $\eta=(\nu^3/\epsilon)^{1/4}$, $v_\eta=(\nu\epsilon)^{1/4}$ and $\tau_\eta=(\nu/\epsilon)^{1/2}$. With $\epsilon\sim u^3/\ell$, their ratios to the [integral scales of turbulence](integral-scale-of-turbulence.md) are powers of the [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md). They describe a mean scale balance; intermittent dissipative structures can have other local scales.
