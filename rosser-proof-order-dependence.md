# Rosser proof-order dependence

↑ **Parent:** [Rosser provability predicate](rosser-provability-predicate.md)

The internal [derivability conditions](hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md) of a [Rosser provability predicate](rosser-provability-predicate.md) depend on the proof numbering, even when the ordinary provability predicate is unchanged up to provable equivalence. Joining proofs of $A$ and $A\to B$ can increase their codes beyond the two bounds in the Rosser hypotheses, leaving the absence of earlier proofs of $\neg B$ unestablished. Internal [modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) therefore needs a separate argument for the chosen numbering. Proof predicates can be constructed with different combinations of closure and introspection properties. [Provability Logic, printed page 87, Rosser provability and proof-numbering dependence](https://sartemov.ws.gc.cuny.edu/files/2012/10/Artemov-Beklemishev.-Provability-logic.pdf). [Rosser provability and the second incompleteness theorem, constructions of differing derivability conditions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06863).
