# Central-commutator exponential identity

↑ **Parent:** [Commutator expansion for exponential conjugation](commutator-expansion-for-exponential-conjugation.md)

For finite-dimensional complex [linear operators](linear-operator.md) with $C=[A,B]$ commuting with both $A$ and $B$, differentiating $e^{-vB}Ae^{vB}$ gives the constant derivative $C$. Hence $e^{-B}Ae^B=A+C$, and conjugating the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) gives $e^{-B}e^{-A}e^B=e^{-A-C}$. Since $[A,C]=0$, multiplication by $e^A$ proves the identity. The commutator order determines the minus sign.
