# Loitsyansky integral

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md)

The Loitsyansky integral is the second spatial moment of the trace [velocity correlation tensor](velocity-correlation-tensor.md), with the displayed sign convention. If correlations decay sufficiently rapidly, it is the coefficient of $k^4/(24\pi^2)$ in the [turbulent energy spectrum](turbulent-energy-spectrum.md). In terms of the normalized [longitudinal velocity correlation](longitudinal-velocity-correlation.md) it is $8\pi u^2\int_0^\infty r^4f(r)dr$ when the boundary terms vanish; conventions omitting $8\pi$ also occur. Its evolution is $\dot I=8\pi[r^4u^3K]_\infty-12\nu L$, where $L$ is the [Saffman integral](saffman-integral.md). Thus it is invariant when $L=0$ and the longitudinal triple correlation is $o(r^{-4})$, not under arbitrary turbulent conditions.
