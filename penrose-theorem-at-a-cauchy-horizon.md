# Penrose theorem at a Cauchy horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose singularity theorem](penrose-singularity-theorem.md)

The globally hyperbolic [maximal Cauchy development](maximal-cauchy-development.md) of appropriate [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md) data obeys the [Penrose singularity theorem](penrose-singularity-theorem.md) when it contains a closed [trapped surface](trapped-surface.md). Its resulting [null geodesic incompleteness](null-geodesic-incompleteness.md) can include generators reaching a smoothly extendible inner [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md) in finite [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md). The full analytic extension is not globally hyperbolic and cannot be substituted into that version of the theorem. Incompleteness alone does not establish curvature blowup at every endpoint.
