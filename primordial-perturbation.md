# Primordial perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)

A primordial perturbation is an early-universe fluctuation that seeds later cosmic microwave background anisotropy and large-scale structure. Inflation generates scalar curvature perturbations and tensor perturbations from quantum fluctuations.
