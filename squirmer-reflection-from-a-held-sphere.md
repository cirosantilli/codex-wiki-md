# Squirmer reflection from a held sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Method of reflections for Stokes flow](method-of-reflections-for-stokes-flow.md)

For a [two-mode tensorial squirmer flow](two-mode-tensorial-squirmer-flow.md) of radius $a$ and a passive equal [sphere](sphere.md) held at $X=Re$, with $a/R\ll1$, let $s=e\cdot Be$. [Faxén's first law](faxen-s-first-law.md) gives holding [force](force.md) $F_h=-9\pi\mu a^3s e/R^2+O(\mu a^4|A|/R^3+\mu a^5\|B\|/R^4)$. Its reflected [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) changes swimming [velocity](velocity.md) by $-9a^3s e/(4R^3)$. Since this leading [force](force.md) is radial, its [vorticity](vorticity.md) at the swimmer vanishes. The next holding [force](force.md), generated by the [potential dipole](potential-dipole.md), yields rotation $a^4 A\times X/(4R^6)$. Terms from the passive [sphere](sphere.md)'s finite radius, reflected [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md) and [rotlet](rotlet.md) are needed at higher order. These formulas assume fixed slip and no externally applied swimmer [force](force.md) or [torque](torque.md).
