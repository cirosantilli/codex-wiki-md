# Anosov homeomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Nielsen–Thurston classification](nielsen-thurston-classification.md)

An Anosov surface homeomorphism has nonsingular invariant expanding and contracting foliations. In the closed orientable surface setting it occurs on the [torus](torus.md), with a hyperbolic integral linear model.
