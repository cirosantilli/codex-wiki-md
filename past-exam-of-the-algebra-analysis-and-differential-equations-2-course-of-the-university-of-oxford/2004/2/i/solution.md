<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If a [basis](../../../../basis.md) consists of [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md), let $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r\in K$ be the distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md). A [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) annihilates the diagonal action exactly when it vanishes at each $\lambda_j$. Consequently the [minimal polynomial](../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is $\prod_j(X-\lambda_j)$, a product of distinct linear factors. It therefore has no repeated irreducible factor, proving **(i) implies (ii)**.
