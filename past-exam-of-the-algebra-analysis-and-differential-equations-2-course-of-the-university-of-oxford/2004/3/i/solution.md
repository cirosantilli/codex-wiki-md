↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [ideal](../../../../ideal.md) is an additive subgroup $I\subseteq R$ with $rI\subseteq I$ for all $r\in R$. A [ring homomorphism](../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) preserves addition and multiplication; for the [rings](../../../../ring.md) with identity here use the standard unital convention $\theta(1_R)=1_S$. If $a,b\in\ker\theta$, then $\theta(a-b)=0$, and if $r\in R$ then $\theta(ra)=\theta(r)\theta(a)=0$. Thus the kernel is an [ideal](../../../../ideal.md).

A [maximal ideal](../../../../maximal-ideal.md) is a proper [ideal](../../../../ideal.md) $I\ne R$ such that an [ideal](../../../../ideal.md) $J$ satisfying $I\subseteq J\subseteq R$ is either $I$ or $R$.
