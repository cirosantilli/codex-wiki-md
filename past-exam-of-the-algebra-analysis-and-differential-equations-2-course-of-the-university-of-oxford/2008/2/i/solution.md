<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The functional $\ell(u)=\langle u,v\rangle$ is linear and nonzero because $\ell(v)=\langle v,v\rangle>0$. It therefore has rank one, and [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{v^\perp=\ker\ell,\qquad\dim v^\perp=n-1.}
$$

No Gram–Schmidt argument is needed. Every vector decomposes uniquely as $u=w+av$, where $w\perp v$ and $a=\langle u,v\rangle/\langle v,v\rangle$. Substitution gives $S_v(w+av)=w-av$. Thus the [eigenspace](../../../../eigenspace.md) for $-1$ is $\mathbb Rv$, and that for $+1$ is $v^\perp$. The latter is an actual [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) when $n\ge2$; when $n=1$ its [eigenspace](../../../../eigenspace.md) is zero and only $-1$ occurs. In the plane the map is **[reflection](../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) in the line perpendicular to $v$**.
