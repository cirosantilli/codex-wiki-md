↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The given distinct zeros converge to $i$, which lies inside the connected [upper half-plane](../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). The [identity theorem for holomorphic functions](../../../../identity-theorem.md) forces the function to vanish identically. Thus $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
