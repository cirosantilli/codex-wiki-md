A [critical point](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is an [equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md): $\boxed{f(a,b)=g(a,b)=0}$. Put $X=x-a$, $Y=y-b$. The [Jacobian matrix](../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) $J$ gives the first-order linearized equation $(\dot X,\dot Y)^\mathsf T=J(X,Y)^\mathsf T$. The following [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) tests classify that linearized system. For a continuously differentiable nonlinear system, the cases with nonzero real parts persist as the usual local node, [saddle](../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) or spiral classifications. The purely imaginary case needs extra nonlinear information, as explained in (iv).
