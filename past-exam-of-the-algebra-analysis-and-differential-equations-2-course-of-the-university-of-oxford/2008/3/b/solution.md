A [Euclidean domain](../../../../euclidean-domain.md) is an [integral domain](../../../../integral-domain.md) equipped with a function $\delta:R\setminus\{0\}\to\mathbb N$ such that for every $a\in R$ and nonzero $b\in R$ there are $q,r\in R$ with $a=qb+r$ and either $r=0$ or $\delta(r)<\delta(b)$. One may additionally require $\delta(a)\le\delta(ab)$ for nonzero $a,b$; degree on a [polynomial ring](../../../../polynomial-ring.md) also satisfies this convention.
