<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For this forcing the [integral](../../../../integral-split.md) in part (a) simplifies to

$$
y_p(x)=e^{-2x}\int_0^x\sin(x-t)\,dt=e^{-2x}(1-\cos x),\qquad C=-\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos t\,dt=-1.
$$

Consequently the unique [boundary value problem](../../../../boundary-value-problem-split.md) solution is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=e^{-2x}(1-\sin x-\cos x).}
$$

It vanishes at zero and at $\pi/2$. To check the [differential equation](../../../../differential-equation-split.md) directly, set $y=e^{-2x}w$. Then $Ly=e^{-2x}(w''+w)$. For $w=1-\sin x-\cos x$, one has $w''+w=1$, as required.
