<h1 id="5/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose, towards a contradiction, that for some $r,w,\varepsilon$ the punctured-disk image misses the open disk of radius $\varepsilon$ about $w$. Then

$$
h(z)=\frac1{f(z)-w}
$$

is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) and bounded by $1/\varepsilon$ on $0<|z|<r$. Part (i) extends it to a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) $H$ on the full disk. If $H(0)\ne0$, then $w+1/H$ extends $f$ holomorphically at zero, making zero a [removable singularity](../../../../../removable-singularity.md).

If $H(0)=0$, the function $H$ is not identically zero, since it is nonzero on the punctured disk. The [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) and [identity theorem](../../../../../identity-theorem.md) give a finite zero order $k\geq1$, so $H(z)=z^kq(z)$ with $q$ holomorphic and $q(0)\ne0$. Hence

$$
f(z)=w+z^{-k}/q(z)
$$

has a [pole](../../../../../pole.md) of order $k$. Both alternatives contradict an [essential singularity](../../../../../essential-singularity.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{\overline{f(\{z:0<|z|<r\})}=\mathbb C\quad\text{for every }0<r<s.}
$$

This is the [Casorati-Weierstrass theorem](../../../../../casorati-weierstrass-theorem.md): every complex value can be approximated arbitrarily closely in every punctured neighbourhood of an essential singularity.
