<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The point is a **[removable singularity](../../../../../removable-singularity.md)** if assigning a value to $f(z_0)$ makes $f$ [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on a full neighbourhood of $z_0$. Equivalently, its [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) has no nonzero [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md) of negative powers. The assigned value is its constant [coefficient](../../../../../coefficient.md), or equivalently the finite limit $\lim_{z\to z_0}f(z)$.
