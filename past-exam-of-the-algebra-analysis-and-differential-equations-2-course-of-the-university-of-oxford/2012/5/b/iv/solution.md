↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

An **[essential singularity](../../../../../essential-singularity.md)** is an [isolated singularity](../../../../../isolated-singularity-split.md) that is neither a [removable singularity](../../../../../removable-singularity.md) nor a [pole](../../../../../pole.md). In its [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md), infinitely many [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md) of negative powers are nonzero. The alternatives follow because no negative terms give a removable singularity, while finitely many with a lowest nonzero power give a pole.
