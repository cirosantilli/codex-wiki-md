↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An **[isolated singularity](../../../../../isolated-singularity-split.md)** at $z_0$ means that some radius $r>0$ has $f$ [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the punctured disk $0<|z-z_0|<r$, while $f$ is not initially defined as a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) at $z_0$. The adjective isolated refers to the absence of other singularities in this punctured neighbourhood; it permits a [removable singularity](../../../../../removable-singularity.md).
