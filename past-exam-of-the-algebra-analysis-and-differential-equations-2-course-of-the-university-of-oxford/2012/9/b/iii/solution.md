<h1 id="9/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For fixed $t$, eliminate $s=x-t$ to obtain the projected [parabola](../../../../../parabola.md)

$$
\boxed{y=x-2t+(x-t)^2.}
$$

Along that curve,

$$
x+y+1=(s+1)^2\geq0.
$$

Equality occurs at $s=-1$, where the point is $(t-1,-t)$. Also $dy/dx=y_s/x_s=1+2s=-1$ there, so the [tangent line](../../../../../tangent-line.md) has the same slope as the line $x+y=-1$ and passes through a point of that line. Thus every projected characteristic is tangent to that line, with no crossing of it. Because $t\geq0$, the attained tangency points form only the half-line $x\geq-1$, $y=-x-1$.
