<h1 id="9/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Differentiating the projected parameterization gives

$$
\boxed{J=\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(s,t)}=\det\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1+2s&-1\end{pmatrix}=-2(s+1).}
$$

It vanishes exactly when $s=-1$. The image is the tangency half-line from part (iii), $x=t-1$, $y=-t$, $t\geq0$. This is a [fold caustic](../../../../../fold-caustic.md): the parameterized surface itself remains regular, but its projection ceases to be locally invertible, so it cannot there be expressed as a differentiable single-valued graph over the plane.
