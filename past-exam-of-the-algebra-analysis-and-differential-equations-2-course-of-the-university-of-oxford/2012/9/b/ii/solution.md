<h1 id="9/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use $t\geq0$ to label the initial half-line, with $(x,y,z)=(t,-t,1)$ at $s=0$. In the general characteristic from part (i), this sets $a=t$, $b=-t$, $c=1$. The parametric [solution surface](../../../../../solution-surface.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{x=t+s,\qquad y=-t+s+s^2,\qquad z=1+2s,\qquad t\geq0.}
$$

At $s=0$ this recovers exactly the given data. Moreover $(x_s,y_s,z_s)=(1,1+2s,2)=(1,z,2)$, so the surface follows the required characteristic [vector](../../../../../vector.md) field. The branch defining a classical graph through the data is determined below by restricting to the side of the projection fold containing $s=0$.
