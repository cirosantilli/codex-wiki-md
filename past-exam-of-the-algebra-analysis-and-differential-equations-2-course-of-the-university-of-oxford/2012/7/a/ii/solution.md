<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An [unstable node](../../../../../unstable-node.md) has

$$
\boxed{\lambda_1,\lambda_2\in\mathbb R,\qquad\lambda_1>0,\quad\lambda_2>0.}
$$

Both linear modes grow forward in time. Nearby nonconstant orbits move away from the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), and approach it under reversed time; their local directions come from the two real [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md).
