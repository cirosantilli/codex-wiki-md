<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [stable node](../../../../../stable-node.md) has

$$
\boxed{\lambda_1,\lambda_2\in\mathbb R,\qquad\lambda_1<0,\quad\lambda_2<0.}
$$

Both exponential modes decay as $t\to\infty$, so the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is [asymptotically stable](../../../../../asymptotic-stability.md). Generic linear orbits approach tangent to the [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) for the less negative [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md); the [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) directions themselves are invariant straight lines. There is no rotation because both [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are real.
