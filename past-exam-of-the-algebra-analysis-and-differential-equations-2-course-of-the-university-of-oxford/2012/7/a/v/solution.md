↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

A [stable spiral](../../../../../stable-spiral.md) has the complex conjugate [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
\boxed{\lambda_{1,2}=\alpha\pm i\beta,\qquad\alpha<0,\quad\beta\ne0.}
$$

In suitable real coordinates the radius decays like $e^{\alpha t}$ while the angle changes at rate $\beta$. The orbit therefore spirals towards the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), which is asymptotically stable. This is also called a [stable focus](../../../../../stable-spiral.md).
