A [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) has

$$
\boxed{\lambda_1,\lambda_2\in\mathbb R,\qquad\lambda_1\lambda_2<0.}
$$

The negative [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) gives a [stable manifold](../../../../../stable-manifold.md) direction and the positive [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) an [unstable manifold](../../../../../unstable-manifold.md) direction. Perturbations along the latter grow, so the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is unstable even though there are special trajectories approaching it.
