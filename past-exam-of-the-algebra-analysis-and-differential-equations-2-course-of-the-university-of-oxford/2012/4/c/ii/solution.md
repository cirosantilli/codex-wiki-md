<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Either equality condition says that the [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) $h$ from part (i) has modulus one at an interior point: at zero in the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) case, or at the given nonzero point in the other case. Since $|h|\leq1$ everywhere, the [maximum modulus principle](../../../../../maximum-modulus-principle.md) makes $h$ a constant $w$ with $|w|=1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(z)=wz,\qquad |w|=1.}
$$

These maps are rotations of the [unit disk](../../../../../unit-disk.md) and do achieve both equalities, so this also identifies all equality cases of the [Schwarz lemma](../../../../../schwarz-lemma.md).
