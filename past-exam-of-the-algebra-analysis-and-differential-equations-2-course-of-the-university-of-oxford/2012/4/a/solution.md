<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Cauchy integral formula](../../../../cauchy-integral-formula.md) says that if $f$ is [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the [simply connected](../../../../simply-connected-space.md) [open set](../../../../open-set.md) $U$, and $\gamma$ is a positively oriented [simple closed curve](../../../../simple-closed-curve.md) in $U$, then, for every $z$ in its interior,

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_\gamma\frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta.}
$$

Here the contour and its enclosed region lie in $U$; the contour can be taken piecewise continuously differentiable. More generally, the same formula needs only [holomorphic function](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on an [open neighbourhood](../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) of that closed region. The orientation is counterclockwise, so the [winding number](../../../../winding-number.md) about an interior point is one.
