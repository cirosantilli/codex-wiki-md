A **[unit in a ring](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md)** is an element $u$ with a multiplicative inverse in $R$, so $uv=1$ for some $v\in R$.

An **[irreducible element](../../../../irreducible-element.md)** is a nonzero nonunit $a$ such that every factorization $a=bc$ has at least one of $b,c$ a [unit in a ring](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md).

A **[prime element](../../../../prime-element.md)** is a nonzero nonunit $p$ such that $p\mid bc$ implies $p\mid b$ or $p\mid c$. Equivalently its [principal ideal](../../../../principal-ideal.md) $(p)$ is a nonzero [prime ideal](../../../../prime-ideal.md). These are definitions in the given commutative [ring](../../../../ring.md); the equivalence between irreducibility and primality requires additional hypotheses on the [ring](../../../../ring.md).
