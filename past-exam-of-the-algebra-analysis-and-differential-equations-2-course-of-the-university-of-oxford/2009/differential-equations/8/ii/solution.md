<h1 id="differential-equations/8/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The equation is parabolic at a point when $\boxed{b^2-ac=0}$ and at least one of $a,b,c$ is nonzero. The principal [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) then has rank one, with a repeated [characteristic direction](../../../../../characteristic-direction.md). If all three vanish, there is no second-order [principal part](../../../../../principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md) there and the usual parabolic classification does not apply.
