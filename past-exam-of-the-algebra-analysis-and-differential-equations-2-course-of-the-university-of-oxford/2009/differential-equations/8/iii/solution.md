<h1 id="differential-equations/8/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The equation is hyperbolic at a point when $\boxed{b^2-ac>0}$. The principal form has one positive and one negative [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and two distinct real [characteristic directions](../../../../../characteristic-direction.md). In the given equation $a=x^2$, $b=0$, $c=-y^2$, so $b^2-ac=x^2y^2>0$ throughout the [positive quadrant](../../../../../positive-quadrant.md), consistent with the two canonical characteristic families used above.
