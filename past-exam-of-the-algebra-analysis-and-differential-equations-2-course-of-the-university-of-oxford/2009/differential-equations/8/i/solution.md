<h1 id="differential-equations/8/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The principal [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$. The equation is elliptic at a point when $\boxed{b^2-ac<0}$, equivalently the principal form has [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the same nonzero sign and no real [characteristic directions](../../../../../characteristic-direction.md).
