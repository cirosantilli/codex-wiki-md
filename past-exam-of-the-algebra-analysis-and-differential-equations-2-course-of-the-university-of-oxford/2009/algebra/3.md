Use ordinary [matrix](../../../matrix.md) addition and multiplication on $R=K[A]$. Sums and products of [polynomials](../../../polynomial-split.md) in $A$ remain such [polynomials](../../../polynomial-split.md), and they commute because powers of the same [matrix](../../../matrix.md) commute. The identity is $A^0=I$. Evaluation $\phi(\sum c_iX^i)=\sum c_iA^i$ is a surjective unital [algebra homomorphism](../../../algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md) over $K$. It is unique among maps fixing the scalar [matrices](../../../matrix.md) and sending $X$ to $A$.

The scalar condition is needed for the word “unique”: as an unrestricted ring-homomorphism assertion over an arbitrary [field](../../../field.md), prescribing only the image of $X$ does not suffice. For example, over $\mathbb C$ with a real scalar [matrix](../../../matrix.md) $A$, conjugating every coefficient gives another unital [ring homomorphism](../../../ring-homomorphism.md) with the same image of $X$. The intended [polynomial](../../../polynomial-split.md)-evaluation map is the $K$-algebra map just defined.

The [kernel of a ring homomorphism](../../../kernel-of-a-ring-homomorphism.md) is an [ideal](../../../ideal.md) of the [principal ideal domain](../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) $K[X]$. It is nonzero because finitely many powers of $A$ must become linearly dependent in the finite-dimensional [matrix](../../../matrix.md) space. Its unique [monic](../../../monic-polynomial.md) generator is precisely the [minimal polynomial](../../../minimal-polynomial.md) $m$: division by $m$ shows that a [polynomial](../../../polynomial-split.md) killing $A$ has zero remainder, by minimality. Hence $\ker\phi=(m)$ and $R\cong K[X]/(m)$.

If $\gcd(f,m)=1$, [Bézout's identity](../../../bezout-s-identity.md) gives $uf+vm=1$; evaluation gives $u(A)f(A)=I$. Conversely an inverse in $R$ has form $g(A)$, so $fg-1\in(m)$, which forces $\gcd(f,m)=1$. Thus invertibility in this ring is exactly [coprimality](../../../coprime-integers.md) with the [minimal polynomial](../../../minimal-polynomial.md).
