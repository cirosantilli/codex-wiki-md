<h1 id="algebra/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Reduce the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) modulo two. It is $A=1\oplus\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, so $A\ne I$ but $(A-I)^2=0$. Thus the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is $\boxed{m=(X+1)^2}$. Its monic divisors are $1,X+1,(X+1)^2$, giving exactly $\boxed{3\text{ ideals}}$ in the quotient.

This ring has the four elements $0,I,A,I+A$. [Coprimality](../../../../../coprime-integers.md) with $X+1$ is equivalent to the representing [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) taking value one at $1$. Thus precisely $I$ and $A$ are [units](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md): $\boxed{2\text{ invertible matrices}}$. Indeed $A^2=I$, whereas $(I+A)^2=0$.
