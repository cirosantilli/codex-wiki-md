<h1 id="algebra/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The displayed [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is block diagonal with a $1\times1$ block $1$ and a $2\times2$ block $B$ having [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) $X^2-4X-5=(X-5)(X+1)$. The latter block is not scalar, so its [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) has degree two and is exactly that [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md). Taking the least common multiple of the block minimal [polynomials](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) gives $m=(X-1)(X-5)(X+1)$ over $\mathbb R$.

[Ideals](../../../../../ideal.md) of $K[X]/(m)$ correspond to [ideals](../../../../../ideal.md) of $K[X]$ containing $(m)$, namely the principal [ideals](../../../../../ideal.md) generated by the monic divisors of $m$. Every one of the three distinct linear factors may be included or omitted, so

$$
\boxed{\#\{\text{ideals of }R\}=2^3=8.}
$$

This includes both the zero [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) and the whole ring. Equivalently the [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md) identifies this ring with $\mathbb R^3$.
