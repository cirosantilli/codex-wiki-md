<h1 id="algebra/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The assumption $v\notin W^\perp$ means $p=Pv\ne0$. For every [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) $w\in W$, [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) and Cauchy–Schwarz give $\langle v,w\rangle=\langle p,w\rangle\le\|p\|$. Equality is attained at

$$
\boxed{\hat w=p/\|p\|,\qquad\sup_{w\in W,\ \|w\|=1}\langle v,w\rangle=\|p\|.}
$$

Equality in the real [Cauchy–Schwarz inequality](../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) requires $w$ to be a scalar multiple of $p$, and attaining the positive [supremum](../../../../../supremum.md) fixes its sign; the [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) [norm](../../../../../norm.md) fixes its magnitude. This proves uniqueness.
