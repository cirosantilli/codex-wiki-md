<h1 id="algebra/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For any $w\in W$, the decomposition $v-w=Tv+(Pv-w)$ is [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md). The [Pythagorean identity](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem-in-an-inner-product-space.md) gives

$$
\|v-w\|^2=\|Tv\|^2+\|Pv-w\|^2\ge\|Tv\|^2.
$$

Equality is attained at $w=Pv$. Thus $\boxed{\inf_{w\in W}\|v-w\|=\|Tv\|}$, and the minimizer in $W$ is uniquely $Pv$.
