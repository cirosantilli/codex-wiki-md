<h1 id="algebra/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An element $a$ of an [integral domain](../../../../../integral-domain.md) is an [irreducible element](../../../../../irreducible-element.md) when it is nonzero and not a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md), and any [factorization](../../../../../factorization.md) $a=bc$ has at least one [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) factor. The exclusions of zero and [units](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) are part of the definition.
