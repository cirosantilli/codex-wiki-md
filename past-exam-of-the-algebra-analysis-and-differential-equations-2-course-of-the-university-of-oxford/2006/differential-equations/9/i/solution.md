<h1 id="differential-equations/9/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a function for which the integral converges, the [Laplace transform](../../../../../laplace-transform.md) is

$$
\bar f(p)=\int_0^\infty e^{-pt}f(t)\,dt.
$$

For a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) function of [exponential order](../../../../../exponential-order.md) this exists in a sufficiently far right half-plane. For the first input,

$$
\boxed{\overline1(p)=\frac1p\qquad(\operatorname{Re}p>0).}
$$

This follows by evaluating the exponential antiderivative and using its decay at infinity.
