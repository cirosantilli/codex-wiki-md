<h1 id="differential-equations/9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), whose boundary term vanishes for $\operatorname{Re}p>0$, gives

$$
\boxed{\overline t(p)=\int_0^\infty te^{-pt}\,dt=\frac1p\int_0^\infty e^{-pt}\,dt=\frac1{p^2}.}
$$
