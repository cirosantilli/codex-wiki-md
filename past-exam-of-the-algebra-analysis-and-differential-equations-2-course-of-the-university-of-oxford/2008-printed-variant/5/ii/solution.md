<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use $\sin z=z+O(z^3)$ and

$$
(1-e^{-z})^{-3}=z^{-3}(1-z/2+O(z^2))^{-3}=z^{-3}(1+3z/2+O(z^2)).
$$

Their product is $z^{-2}+(3/2)z^{-1}+O(1)$. Thus the singularity is a **[pole](../../../../pole.md) of order two**, with $\boxed{\operatorname{Res}_0 f=3/2}$.
