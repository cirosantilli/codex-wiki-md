<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Both numerator and denominator have simple zeros at zero, and $1-e^{-z}=z+O(z^2)$. Their quotient tends to one and extends holomorphically with that value. Hence the singularity is **removable**, and $\boxed{\operatorname{Res}_0 f=0}$ because its [Laurent series](../../../../laurent-series.md) has no negative powers.
