<h1 id="9/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are a nonreal conjugate pair $\boxed{\alpha\pm i\beta,\ \alpha<0,\ \beta\ne0}$. The factor $e^{\alpha t}$ contracts while the imaginary part produces rotation, giving a [stable spiral](../../../../../stable-spiral.md).
