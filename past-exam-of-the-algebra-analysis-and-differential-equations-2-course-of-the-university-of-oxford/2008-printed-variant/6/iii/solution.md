<h1 id="6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The domain has two [connected components](../../../../connected-component.md). The zeros force the function to vanish on the [upper half-plane](../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) but impose nothing on the lower one. Define

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\begin{cases}0,&\operatorname{Im}z>0,\\1,&\operatorname{Im}z<0.\end{cases}}
$$

This function is locally constant and hence [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the stated [open set](../../../../open-set.md), and is not identically zero on that whole set. Thus the answer is **yes**. [Connectedness](../../../../connected-space.md) is exactly the missing hypothesis for a global identity conclusion.
