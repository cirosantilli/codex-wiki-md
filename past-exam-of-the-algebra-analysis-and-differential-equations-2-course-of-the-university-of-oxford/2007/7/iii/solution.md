<h1 id="7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Translation in $x$ leaves this autonomous [differential equation](../../../../differential-equation-split.md) unchanged. For every $c\ge1$, the same formula

$$
u_c(x)=\begin{cases}0,&x\le c,\\{}[3(x-c)/4]^{4/3},&x\ge c\end{cases}
$$

satisfies $u_c(1)=0$, and the zero function does too. Thus **solutions exist and are not unique**, exactly as for the initial point $(0,0)$. The relevant failure is the [Lipschitz condition](../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) at $y=0$; moving the initial value of $x$ does not remove it.
