<h1 id="9/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Integrating the exponential directly gives

$$
\mathcal L[e^{-nt}](p)=\int_0^\infty e^{-(p+n)t}\,dt
=\boxed{\frac1{p+n}},\qquad\operatorname{Re}p>-n.
$$

The inequality is the absolute-convergence domain of this [Laplace transform](../../../../laplace-transform.md).
