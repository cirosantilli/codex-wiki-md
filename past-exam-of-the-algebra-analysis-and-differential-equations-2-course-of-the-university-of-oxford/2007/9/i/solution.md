<h1 id="9/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}p>a$, differentiation under the integral is justified by the [dominated convergence theorem](../../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md): on a smaller closed half-plane $\operatorname{Re}p\ge a+\delta$, the additional factor $t$ is controlled by $te^{-\delta t}$. Hence

$$
Y'(p)=\int_0^\infty -ty(t)e^{-pt}\,dt,
\qquad\boxed{\mathcal L[ty](p)=-Y'(p).}
$$

This is the multiplication rule for the [Laplace transform](../../../../laplace-transform.md).
