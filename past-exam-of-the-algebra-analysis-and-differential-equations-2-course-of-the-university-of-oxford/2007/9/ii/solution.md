<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}p>0$, the starting integral is $\int_0^\infty e^{-pt}\,dt=1/p$. Repeated [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md), with vanishing boundary terms, gives

$$
\mathcal L[t^n](p)=\frac np\mathcal L[t^{n-1}](p)
=\boxed{\frac{n!}{p^{n+1}}}\qquad(n\ge0).
$$

This also follows from the [gamma function](../../../../gamma-function.md). The stated open half-plane ensures absolute convergence.
