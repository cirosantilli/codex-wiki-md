↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [adjoint operator](../../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) is characterized by

$$
\boxed{\langle Tu,v\rangle=\langle u,T^*v\rangle\quad\text{for all }u,v\in V.}
$$

For each fixed $v$, the map $u\mapsto\langle Tu,v\rangle$ is a [linear functional](../../../../../../linear-functional.md). Part (i) gives a representing vector, which we define to be $T^*v$. Hence such a map exists on every vector of $V$. Part (b) proves that this constructed map is linear.
