<h1 id="analysis/4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Cauchy's integral theorem](../../../../../cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) states that if a function is holomorphic on a simply connected open set, its integral around every closed piecewise-smooth path in that set is zero. A local form sufficient here is: if a function is holomorphic on an open set containing a triangle together with its interior, then

$$
\boxed{\int_{\partial\Delta}f(z)\,dz=0.}
$$

For a general domain with holes, an arbitrary closed path need not have zero integral; the triangle or simple-connectedness hypothesis supplies the required interior.
