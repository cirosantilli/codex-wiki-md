<h1 id="algebra/2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $D=T^*T-TT^*$. This operator is [self-adjoint](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md), and

$$
\langle Dx,x\rangle=\|Tx\|^2-\|T^*x\|^2.
$$

If condition (iii) holds, this [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) vanishes. For a real [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md), [polarization](../../../../../../polarization-identity.md) gives

$$
2\langle Dx,y\rangle=\langle D(x+y),x+y\rangle-\langle Dx,x\rangle-\langle Dy,y\rangle=0.
$$

Therefore $D=0$, which is condition (i). This supplies the nontrivial reverse implication needed for the equivalence.
