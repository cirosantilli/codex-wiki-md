<h1 id="algebra/2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By the definition of the [adjoint](../../../../../../adjoint-operator.md),

$$
\langle Tx,Ty\rangle-\langle T^*x,T^*y\rangle
=\langle x,(T^*T-TT^*)y\rangle.
$$

If (i) holds, the expression is zero for all $x,y$, giving (ii). Conversely, if this expression vanishes for every $x$, nondegeneracy of the [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) gives $(T^*T-TT^*)y=0$ for every $y$, so (ii) also implies (i).
