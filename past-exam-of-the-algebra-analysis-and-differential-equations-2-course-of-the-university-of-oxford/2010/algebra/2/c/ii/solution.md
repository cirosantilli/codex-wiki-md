↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Restricting $T^*T=TT^*$ to $V_i$, using (i), gives $T_i^*T_i=T_iT_i^*$, so the global [normal operator](../../../../../../normal-operator.md) property implies the [normal operator](../../../../../../normal-operator.md) property of every restriction. Conversely, if the restrictions are normal and the [adjoint](../../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) preserves each summand, then for $x=\sum_i x_i$,

$$
(T^*T-TT^*)x=\sum_i(T_i^*T_i-T_iT_i^*)x_i=0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{T\text{ is normal}\iff\text{every }T_i\text{ is normal}}$, with condition (i) automatic under the stated decomposition.
