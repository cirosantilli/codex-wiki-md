<h1 id="algebra/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take a reduced fraction $a/b\in R$. If $a\ne0$, its only possible inverse in $\mathbb Q$ is $b/a$. Since $\gcd(a,b)=1$, this is in lowest terms up to sign and belongs to $R$ exactly when $\gcd(a,30)=1$. Zero is not invertible. Therefore

$$
\boxed{R^\times=\{a/b:\gcd(a,b)=1,\ \gcd(ab,30)=1\}.}
$$

Equivalently, these are the [rational numbers](../../../../../rational-number.md) whose [valuations](../../../../../valuation.md) at $2,3,5$ are all zero.
