<h1 id="analysis/4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $f=u+iv$ and evaluate all [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) of $\psi$ at $(u,v)$. The real [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\Delta(\psi\circ f)=\psi_{uu}(u_x^2+u_y^2)+2\psi_{uv}(u_xv_x+u_yv_y)+\psi_{vv}(v_x^2+v_y^2)+\psi_u\Delta u+\psi_v\Delta v.
$$

The [Cauchy–Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give equal squared lengths $u_x^2+u_y^2=v_x^2+v_y^2=|f'|^2$ and zero cross term $u_xv_x+u_yv_y=0$. Part (ii) removes the last two terms. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta(\psi\circ f)=|f'|^2(\Delta\psi)\circ f=0.}
$$

The composition is $C^2$, so it is [harmonic](../../../../../harmonic-function.md). No assumption that $f'$ is nonzero is required.
