<h1 id="analysis/4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [complex exponential function](../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) $E(z)=e^z$ maps the strip bijectively and holomorphically to the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md): its modulus is $e^{\Re z}>0$ and its argument is $\Im z\in(0,\pi)$. Its inverse is the [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) [logarithm](../../../../../logarithm.md) $L(w)=\log|w|+i\operatorname{Arg}w$ with $\operatorname{Arg}w\in(0,\pi)$.

Part (iii) applies to both maps. The required inverse correspondences are

$$
\boxed{h\in\mathcal H(\Omega_2)\longmapsto h\circ E\in\mathcal H(\Omega_1),\qquad k\longmapsto k\circ L.}
$$

They compose to the identity because $L\circ E$ and $E\circ L$ do. This proves injectivity and surjectivity, not merely a map between the two sets.
