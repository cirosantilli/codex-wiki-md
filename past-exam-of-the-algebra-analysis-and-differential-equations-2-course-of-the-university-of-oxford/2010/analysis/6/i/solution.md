<h1 id="analysis/6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On $D=\mathbb C\setminus[0,\infty)$ choose the [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) argument $0<\arg z<2\pi$, and put

$$
\boxed{z^w=\exp\bigl(w(\log|z|+i\arg z)\bigr).}
$$

This is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the slit [plane](../../../../../plane.md) for every fixed $w$. The upper boundary value at $x>0$ is $x^w$, whereas the lower boundary value is $e^{2\pi iw}x^w$. This difference is exactly what the [keyhole contour](../../../../../keyhole-contour.md) measures.
