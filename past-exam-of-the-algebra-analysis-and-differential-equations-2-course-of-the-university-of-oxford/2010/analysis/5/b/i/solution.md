↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [removable singularity](../../../../../../removable-singularity.md) means that $f$ has a [holomorphic](../../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) extension to the full disk, assigning a finite value at zero. Equivalently its [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) has no negative-power terms. One direction follows by the [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) of the extension; the other by the convergent nonnegative-power series. Thus $\boxed{a_k=0\text{ for all }k<0}$ is the coefficient criterion.
