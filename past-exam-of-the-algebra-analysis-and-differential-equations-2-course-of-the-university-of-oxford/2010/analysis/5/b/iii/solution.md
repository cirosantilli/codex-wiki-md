An [essential singularity](../../../../../../essential-singularity.md) is an [isolated singularity](../../../../../../isolated-singularity-split.md) that is neither removable nor a [pole](../../../../../../pole.md). In terms of the [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) this means $\boxed{\text{infinitely many negative-power coefficients are nonzero}}$: no finite multiplication by a power of $z$ removes the singularity.
