<h1 id="analysis/5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $|z|<1$, differentiate the [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md) to obtain $(1-t)^{-2}=\sum_{k\ge0}(k+1)t^k$. Since $(z-i)^2=-(1-z/i)^2$,

$$
\boxed{\frac1{z(z-i)^2}=-\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(k+1)(-i)^kz^{k-1}=-\frac1z+2i+3z-4iz^2-5z^3+\cdots.}
$$

The convergence region is precisely $0<|z|<1$, the specified punctured disk; the other [pole](../../../../../pole.md) is on its boundary at $i$.
