<h1 id="algebra/3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $J=\bigcup_nI_n$. It contains [zero](../../../../../zero-of-a-function.md). If $a,b\in J$, both belong to $I_N$ for some common $N$, so $a-b\in I_N\subseteq J$. If $r\in R$, then $ra\in I_N\subseteq J$. These are the [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) axioms, proving $J$ is an [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md).

In a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) write $J=(g)$. Since $g\in J$, there is $n_0$ with $g\in I_{n_0}$. For $n\geq n_0$,

$$
(g)\subseteq I_{n_0}\subseteq I_n\subseteq J=(g).
$$

All inclusions are equalities. Hence **the chain stabilizes at $I_{n_0}$**. This includes $J=(0)$ and is the [ascending chain condition](../../../../../ascending-chain-condition.md) for [ideals](../../../../../ideal.md).
