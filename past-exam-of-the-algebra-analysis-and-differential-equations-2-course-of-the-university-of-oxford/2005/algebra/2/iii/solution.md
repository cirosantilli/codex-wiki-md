<h1 id="algebra/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The sum in question is $\langle Pu,Pv\rangle$. Applying Cauchy-Schwarz and then [Bessel's inequality](../../../../../bessel-s-inequality.md) gives

$$
|\langle Pu,Pv\rangle|^2\leq\|Pu\|^2\|Pv\|^2\leq\|u\|^2\|v\|^2.
$$

If either original [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is [zero](../../../../../zero-of-a-function.md), equality holds. If both are nonzero, equality forces both projection [norms](../../../../../norm.md) to equal their original [norms](../../../../../norm.md) and also forces dependence of the projected [vectors](../../../../../vector.md). Thus **apart from the [zero](../../../../../zero-of-a-function.md)-[vector](../../../../../vector.md) cases, equality holds exactly when $u,v\in W$ and $u,v$ are [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md)**.

For the final unnumbered request, apply Cauchy-Schwarz in $\mathbb R^k$ to $p_i=a^{i/2}$ and $q_i=a^{-i/2}$. Their [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) is $k$, so

$$
\boxed{\left(\sum_{i=1}^ka^i\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^ka^{-i}\right)\geq k^2.}
$$

Equality requires the positive [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) $p,q$ to be proportional, equivalently $a^i$ constant for $i=1,\ldots,k$. For $k\geq2$ this is **$a=1$**. If $k=1$, equality holds for every $a>0$.
