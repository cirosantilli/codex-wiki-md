<h1 id="algebra/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A real [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) is a symmetric bilinear map $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle:V\times V\to\mathbb R$ with $\langle v,v\rangle>0$ for $v\ne0$. It defines $\|v\|=\sqrt{\langle v,v\rangle}$. Set $W=\operatorname{span}\{e_1,\ldots,e_k\}$ and $Pv=\sum_i\langle v,e_i\rangle e_i$. Orthonormality gives $v-Pv\perp W$, hence the [Pythagorean identity](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem-in-an-inner-product-space.md) gives

$$
\|v\|^2=\|v-Pv\|^2+\sum_i\langle v,e_i\rangle^2.
$$

This proves [Bessel's inequality](../../../../../bessel-s-inequality.md). **Equality holds exactly when $v\in W$.**
