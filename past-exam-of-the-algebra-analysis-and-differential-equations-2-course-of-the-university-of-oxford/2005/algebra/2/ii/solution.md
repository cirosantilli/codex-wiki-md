<h1 id="algebra/2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $v=0$, equality is immediate. If $v\ne0$, the nonnegative squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md)

$$
0\leq\left\|u-\frac{\langle u,v\rangle}{\|v\|^2}v\right\|^2=\|u\|^2-\frac{\langle u,v\rangle^2}{\|v\|^2}
$$

proves the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Equality holds precisely when the displayed [vector](../../../../../vector.md) vanishes. Thus **equality is equivalent to [linear dependence](../../../../../linear-dependence.md) of $u,v$**, including the cases with a [zero](../../../../../zero-of-a-function.md) [vector](../../../../../vector.md).
