<h1 id="analysis/4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $f$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on a neighborhood of the closed disc, the differentiated [Cauchy integral formula](../../../../../cauchy-integral-formula.md) is

$$
f^{(k)}(a)=\frac{k!}{2\pi i}\int_{|z-a|=r}\frac{f(z)}{(z-a)^{k+1}}\,dz,
$$

with positive orientation. On the circle the integrand has modulus at most $M/r^{k+1}$ and the [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) has length $2\pi r$. Thus

$$
\boxed{|f^{(k)}(a)|\leq\frac{k!M}{r^k}.}
$$
