# Secondary antibody

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)

A [secondary antibody](secondary-antibody.md) recognizes another [antibody](antibody.md), rather than the proposed target [protein](protein.md) directly. In [Western blot antibody detection](western-blot-antibody-detection.md), its attached label makes retained [primary antibodies](primary-antibody.md) visible. A [secondary antibody](secondary-antibody.md) must recognize the kind of [primary antibody](primary-antibody.md) used.
