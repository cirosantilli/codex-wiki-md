# Phenylalanine

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid composition](amino-acid-composition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenylalanine)

[Phenylalanine](phenylalanine.md) is an [amino acid](amino-acid.md), represented by Phe or F for an incorporated [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md). It was identified at the other [insulin](insulin.md) [N-terminus](n-terminus.md) in [Sanger's insulin sequencing experiment](sanger-s-insulin-sequencing-experiment.md). See [IUPAC Blue Book, P-103.1: amino-acid names and symbols](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/P10.html).
