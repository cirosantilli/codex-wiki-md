# Prime element

↑ **Parent:** [Integral domain](integral-domain.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_element)

A nonzero nonunit $p$ in an integral domain is prime when $p\mid ab$ implies $p\mid a$ or $p\mid b$.
