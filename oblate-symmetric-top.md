# Oblate symmetric top

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric top](symmetric-top.md)

An [oblate symmetric top](oblate-symmetric-top.md) has two equal smaller principal [moments of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) and a larger [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about its symmetry axis. A planar trigonal [molecule](molecule-split.md), such as [boron trifluoride](boron-trifluoride.md), is an example. Its axial [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md) is smaller than its transverse [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md).
