# Bayesian confirmation

↑ **Parent:** [Scientific confirmation](scientific-confirmation.md)

Evidence $E$ confirms $H$ relative to background $K$ when it increases its [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md). With $0<P(H\mid K)<1$ and positive evidence [probability](probability.md), [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) gives

$$
P(H\mid E,K)>P(H\mid K)\iff P(E\mid H,K)>P(E\mid\neg H,K).
$$

Indeed, writing $p=P(H\mid K)$, $a=P(E\mid H,K)$ and $b=P(E\mid\neg H,K)$ gives the [posterior probability](posterior-probability.md) $pa/(pa+(1-p)b)$; comparison with $p$ reduces to $a>b$. Thus [scientific confirmation](scientific-confirmation.md) depends on the alternatives and background, not just on a statement's logical form.
