# Reionization

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reionization)

Reionization returns some neutral cosmic gas to an ionized state after [cosmological recombination](recombination-cosmology.md). The resulting free electrons increase the [cosmological optical depth](cosmological-optical-depth.md) through [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) and create a second contribution to the [cosmological visibility function](cosmological-visibility-function.md).
