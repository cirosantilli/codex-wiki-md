# Solvent

↑ **Parent:** [Solution (chemistry)](solution-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solvent)

A [solvent](solvent.md) is the component designated as the medium of a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), commonly the component present in greatest proportion. [Water](water.md) is a common [solvent](solvent.md), not a necessary one. A [mixture](mixture-split.md) of mostly [ethanol](ethanol.md) with a little [water](water.md) can designate [ethanol](ethanol.md) as [solvent](solvent.md) and [water](water.md) as [solute](solute.md). [Chemical solutions](solution-chemistry.md) can also be [gas](gas.md) or [solid](solid.md) [mixtures](mixture-split.md); [molecular nitrogen](molecular-nitrogen.md) is the principal medium of ordinary well-mixed air. When proportions are comparable, state the convention explicitly.[Chemistry 2e, §11.1, paragraph beginning Water is used so often as a solvent; Table 11.1; solvent convention](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/11-1-the-dissolution-process.md)

The [solvent](solvent.md) is part of the final sample. Choosing it influences [solubility](solubility.md) and [solvation](solvation.md); measuring its added [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) alone does not determine the final [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). [Solvent](solvent.md) and [solute](solute.md) [masses](mass.md) add in a closed account, whereas the final [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) should be measured rather than inferred by adding pre-mixing [volumes](volume.md).
