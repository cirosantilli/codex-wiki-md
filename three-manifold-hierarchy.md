# Three-manifold hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible surface](incompressible-surface.md)

A hierarchy is a finite sequence of cuts along properly embedded two-sided [incompressible surfaces](incompressible-surface.md), without spherical components, ending in disjoint three-balls. [Disks](disk-mathematics.md) are allowed. For an ambient manifold which is not [orientable](orientable-surface.md) the two-sided cutting [surfaces](topological-surface.md) need not themselves be [orientable](orientable-surface.md). The existence of a hierarchy does not imply that every choice of incompressible cuts terminates.
