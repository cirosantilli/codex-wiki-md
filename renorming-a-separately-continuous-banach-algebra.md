# Renorming a separately continuous Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

If an associative unital [algebra](algebra-split.md) has a complete [norm](norm.md) and separately continuous multiplication, the [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md) applied to $L_x(y)=xy$, for $\|x\|\leq1$, yields $\|xy\|\leq C\|x\|\|y\|$. The [operator norm](operator-norm.md) of $L_x$ then defines an equivalent [algebra norm](algebra-norm.md): $\|x\|/\|e\|\leq\|L_x\|\leq C\|x\|$, and $L_{xy}=L_xL_y$ proves [submultiplicativity](submultiplicativity.md). It normalizes the identity to norm one.
