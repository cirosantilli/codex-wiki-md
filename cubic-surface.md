# Cubic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic surface](algebraic-surface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_surface)

A cubic surface is a degree-three [projective hypersurface](projective-hypersurface.md) in the [projective space](projective-space-split.md) $\mathbb P^3$. Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) every cubic surface contains a projective line. The smooth case has finitely many lines, although the incidence proof of existence does not require smoothness.
