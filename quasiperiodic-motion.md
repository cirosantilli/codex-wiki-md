# Quasiperiodic motion

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant torus](invariant-torus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasiperiodic_motion)

A quasiperiodic orbit on a torus has angular coordinates $\theta(t)=\theta(0)+\omega t$ modulo $2\pi$. It is [periodic](periodic-orbit.md) exactly when the nonzero frequencies are commensurate; rationally independent frequencies produce a dense orbit.
