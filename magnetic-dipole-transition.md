# Magnetic dipole transition

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)

A transition driven by an oscillating [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) through $H'=-\boldsymbol\mu\cdot\mathbf B$. The rank-one magnetic operator preserves parity and allows changes in total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) of zero or one, excluding zero-to-zero. Its spherical components select changes in the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) component of $0,\pm1$. Hyperfine clock states can therefore couple even when each has zero first-order static magnetic moment.
