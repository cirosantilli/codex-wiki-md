# Komar angular momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Komar charge](komar-charge.md)

[Komar angular momentum](komar-angular-momentum.md) is the [Komar charge](komar-charge.md) associated with an axial [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) in four-dimensional [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md). In geometrized units its normalization is $(16\pi)^{-1}\int_S\star dm$, where $m$ is the metric-dual [differential one-form](one-form.md). With zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), vacuum [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) make $d\star dm=0$, so homologous surrounding surfaces in the vacuum region give the same charge.
