# Multiplication of complex numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Complex number](complex-number.md)

Multiplication is defined by $(a+bi)(c+di)=(ac-bd)+(ad+bc)i$. In polar form, it multiplies magnitudes and adds arguments. This arithmetic operation is distinct from elliptic-curve [complex multiplication](complex-multiplication.md).
