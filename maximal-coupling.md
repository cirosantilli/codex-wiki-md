# Maximal coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Coupling](coupling.md)

A [maximal coupling](maximal-coupling.md) of two [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md) maximizes the agreement [probability](probability.md) $\mathbb P(X=Y)$ among all their [couplings](coupling.md). Equivalently, it minimizes $\mathbb P(X\ne Y)$. The [coupling characterization of total variation distance](coupling-characterization-of-total-variation-distance.md) identifies the minimum mismatch with the [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md) of the two marginal laws. The term maximal concerns agreement, rather than the size of a [correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) or closeness in a geometric [metric](metric.md).

On a [finite set](finite-set.md), retain all common [probability mass](probability-mass.md) at equal pairs and match the excesses elsewhere. The explicit four-outcome example in [coupling characterization of total variation distance](coupling-characterization-of-total-variation-distance.md) realizes this construction and checks its [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md). A [maximal coupling](maximal-coupling.md) need not be unique: different joint arrangements of the residual mass can have the same optimal mismatch.
