# Equation of motion

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

An equation of motion determines how a physical state evolves. A variational action gives [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md); a [phase-space action](phase-space-action.md) instead gives canonical [Hamiltonian mechanics](hamiltonian-mechanics.md) equations together with any imposed [constraints](constraint-mechanics.md).
