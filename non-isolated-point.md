# Non-isolated point

↑ **Parent:** [Isolated point](isolated-point.md)

A non-isolated point $x$ of a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$ is one for which $\{x\}$ is not [open](open-set.md), equivalently every [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $x$ contains another point of $X$. A [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) preserves this property because it and its inverse preserve [open sets](open-set.md), in particular [open](open-set.md) [singletons](singleton-mathematics.md). In a [product space](product-space.md) $X\times Y$, a point $(x,y)$ is [isolated](isolated-point.md) exactly when both coordinates are [isolated](isolated-point.md): use basic [open](open-set.md) [rectangles](rectangle.md) and fix the other coordinate. Thus the non-isolated points of $X\times Y$ are $(X'\times Y)\cup(X\times Y')$, where the primes denote the non-isolated points in the spaces themselves.
