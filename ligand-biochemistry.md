# Ligand (biochemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ligand_(biochemistry))

A biochemical [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) is an [ion](ion.md) or [molecule](molecule-split.md) that binds a [protein](protein-split.md). For a [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md), a [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) may activate signaling, prevent activation, or have another effect. This usage concerns [protein](protein-split.md) binding; it is distinct from the coordination-chemistry use of the word. See the protein-binding reference under [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md).
