# Power Caccioppoli inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Caccioppoli inequality](caccioppoli-inequality.md)

For a nonnegative bounded [weak subsolution](weak-subsolution-of-a-divergence-form-elliptic-equation.md) and $\beta>1$, test with $\eta^2(u+\varepsilon)^{\beta-1}$. Uniform ellipticity and [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) give $\int |Du|^2u^{\beta-2}\eta^2\leq4\Lambda_*^2/[\lambda^2(\beta-1)^2]\int u^\beta|D\eta|^2$, with $\Lambda_*$ an [operator norm](operator-norm.md) bound for the coefficient [matrix](matrix.md). The [Sobolev chain rule](sobolev-chain-rule.md) and $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ justify fractional powers and zero values. An entrywise bound $\Lambda$ yields $\Lambda_*\leq n\Lambda$.
