# Warm neutral medium

↑ **Parent:** [Multiphase interstellar medium](multiphase-interstellar-medium.md)

Diffuse mostly neutral atomic gas with [temperatures](temperature-split.md) below about $10^4\ \mathrm K$. It can share a [pressure](pressure-split.md) range with the [cold neutral medium](cold-neutral-medium.md) where the [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) curve has two stable branches.
