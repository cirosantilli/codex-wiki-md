# Mertens function bound from the classical zeta zero-free region

↑ **Parent:** [Mertens function](mertens-function.md)

The classical [zero-free region of the Riemann zeta function](zero-free-region-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md) and a [truncated Perron formula](truncated-perron-formula.md) give

$$
M(x)\ll x\exp(-c\sqrt{\log x})
$$

for some absolute constant $c>0$. Take contour height $T=\exp(\sqrt{\log x})$ and move the Perron contour to $\Re s=1-c_1/\log T$.
