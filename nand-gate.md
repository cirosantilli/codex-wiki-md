# NAND gate

↑ **Parent:** [Logic gate](logic-gate.md)

A [logic gate](logic-gate.md) computing the [logical negation](negation.md) of the [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md) of its inputs. Thus $\operatorname{NAND}(x_1,\ldots,x_k)=\neg(x_1\land\cdots\land x_k)$. It implements a [logical disjunction](logical-disjunction.md) of complemented inputs by [De Morgan laws](de-morgan-s-laws.md).
