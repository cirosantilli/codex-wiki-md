# Umklapp scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon scattering](phonon-scattering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umklapp_scattering)

An [Umklapp process](umklapp-scattering.md) conserves reduced [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) modulo a nonzero [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector. It can occur in electron-phonon scattering as well as phonon-phonon scattering; the lattice takes up the reciprocal-lattice momentum.

In a three-phonon [Umklapp scattering](umklapp-scattering.md) process, the first-zone [wavevectors](wavevector.md) obey the displayed equation for a nonzero [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector $\mathbf G$, together with [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md). The [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) transferred to the lattice allows these processes to relax a phonon heat current. They are suppressed at sufficiently low [temperature](temperature-split.md) and often determine the approximate $1/T$ high-temperature [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) of a crystalline [electrical insulator](electrical-insulator.md).
