# Attenuation in bacterial gene regulation

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

[Attenuation in bacterial gene regulation](attenuation-in-bacterial-gene-regulation.md) regulates downstream transcription through formation of alternative RNA structures during transcription. In the tryptophan example, translation of a leader peptide influences which structure forms; the immediate decision is transcription termination.
