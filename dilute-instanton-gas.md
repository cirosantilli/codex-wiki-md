# Dilute instanton gas

↑ **Parent:** [Instanton](instanton.md)

When individual [instantons](instanton.md) are much narrower than their typical separation, multi-crossing paths factor at leading semiclassical order. Integrating $n$ ordered centers over time $\tau$ contributes $\tau^n/n!$. In a double well, crossings alternate direction and even/odd counts yield hyperbolic cosine/sine amplitudes. This approximation requires large action relative to $\hbar$, matched local well structure and control of corrections from interactions and local anharmonicity.
