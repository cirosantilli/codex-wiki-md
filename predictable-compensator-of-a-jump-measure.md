# Predictable compensator of a jump measure

↑ **Parent:** [Jump measure of a Markov jump process](jump-measure-of-a-markov-jump-process.md)

The [predictable](predictable-process.md) random measure $\nu$ matching the expected integrals of the jump measure $N$ against every nonnegative [predictable](predictable-process.md) integrand. For displacement marks of a [Markov jump process](markov-jump-process.md), $\nu(ds,dy)=K(X_{s-},dy)ds$. Multiplying the integrand by an event known before an interval proves that the compensated integral has conditionally centered increments; expected absolute [integrability](integrability.md) makes it a true [martingale](martingale-split.md). Squared-integrand [integrability](integrability.md) gives the square-integrable compensation isometry.
