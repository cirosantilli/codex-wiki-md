# Wave absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave-split.md)

[Wave absorption](wave-absorption.md) transfers [energy](energy-split.md) from a propagating [wave](wave-split.md) to other [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md) of a medium. For a [plane wave](plane-wave.md) that is a [monochromatic wave](monochromatic-wave.md), with amplitude proportional to $e^{i(k'+ik'')z}$ and $k''>0$, its [wave intensity](intensity-physics.md) decreases as $I(z)=I(0)e^{-2k''z}$. The intensity absorption coefficient is therefore $2k''$ when this loss is due to absorption. A decreasing transmitted [wave intensity](intensity-physics.md) can also result from [wave reflection](wave-reflection.md) or [wave scattering](wave-scattering.md), which redistribute [energy](energy-split.md) rather than necessarily converting it into [heat](heat-split.md).
