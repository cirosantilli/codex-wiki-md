# Phosphodiester bond

↑ **Parent:** [Covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphodiester_bond)

A [phosphodiester bond](phosphodiester-bond.md) connects two groups through a [phosphate](phosphate.md) diester. In a [nucleic acid](nucleic-acid.md) backbone, the bridging [phosphate](phosphate.md) is connected through [oxygen](oxygen.md) to the $3'$ [carbon](carbon.md) position of one [sugar](sugar.md) and the $5'$ [carbon](carbon.md) position of another. These [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md) connect successive [nucleotides](nucleotide.md) along a strand; they are distinct from the [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md) between paired [nucleobases](nucleotide-base.md).
