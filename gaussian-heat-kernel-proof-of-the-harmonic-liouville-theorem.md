# Gaussian heat-kernel proof of the harmonic Liouville theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic Liouville theorem](harmonic-liouville-theorem.md)

A bounded entire [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) satisfies $u(x)=\mathbb E_xu(B_t)$ because $u(B)$ is a bounded [local martingale](local-martingale.md). The [Gaussian heat kernel](gaussian-heat-kernel.md) has a directional derivative with $L^1$ norm $\sqrt{2/\pi}/\sqrt t$. Consequently $|u(x)-u(y)|\leq\|u\|_\infty\sqrt{2/\pi}|x-y|/\sqrt t$; letting $t\to\infty$ proves the [harmonic Liouville theorem](harmonic-liouville-theorem.md) in every dimension.
