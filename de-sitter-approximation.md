# de Sitter approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)

Approximately constant positive [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) gives a nearly [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md) expansion in flat slicing. Its scale factor can be approximated by $a=-1/(H\tau)$ with negative [conformal time](conformal-time.md) increasing toward zero. Slow variation of the expansion rate limits the accuracy of this approximation.
