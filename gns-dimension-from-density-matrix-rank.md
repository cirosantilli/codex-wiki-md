# GNS dimension from density matrix rank

↑ **Parent:** [Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction](gelfand-naimark-segal-construction.md)

For the [state on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md) $a\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}(\rho a)$ on $M_d(\mathbb C)$, the [GNS construction](gelfand-naimark-segal-construction.md) has the displayed Hilbert-space dimension. Here $\rho$ is a [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md). Its null space for the GNS seminorm consists of $b$ with $b\rho^{1/2}=0$, because $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho b^*b)=\|b\rho^{1/2}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$. Thus the GNS space is the space of matrices $b\rho^{1/2}$ with the [Hilbert-Schmidt inner product](hilbert-schmidt-inner-product.md). If $\rho$ has [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md) $r$, diagonalization identifies these matrices with $r$ arbitrary columns, giving dimension $dr$. Left multiplication is the representation and $\rho^{1/2}$ is its [cyclic vector](cyclic-vector.md). In particular a pure [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md)-one matrix state gives the defining $d$-dimensional representation, while a full-[rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md) state gives a $d^2$-dimensional space.
