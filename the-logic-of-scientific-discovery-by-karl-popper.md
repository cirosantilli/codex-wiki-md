↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy bibliography](philosophy-bibliography.md)

Sections 29–30 of the full book distinguish acceptance of [basic statements](basic-statement.md) from conclusive justification; sections 82–84 discuss [scientific corroboration](scientific-corroboration.md).
