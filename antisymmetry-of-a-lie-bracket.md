# Antisymmetry of a Lie bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md)

The relation $[x,y]=-[y,x]$ for a [bilinear map](bilinear-map.md) proposed as a [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md). An [Alternating Lie bracket](alternating-lie-bracket.md) always satisfies it. Conversely, setting $y=x$ gives $2[x,x]=0$, so antisymmetry implies alternation over a [field](field.md) of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two.

In [characteristic two](characteristic-two.md), the sign disappears and this relation just says $[x,y]=[y,x]$. It does not force $[x,x]=0$, even when the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) holds. For example, on a two-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) with [basis](basis.md) $u,v$, set $[u,u]=v$ and every other [basis](basis.md) bracket to zero, then extend bilinearly. Every nested bracket vanishes, so the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) holds, and the product is symmetric. Nevertheless it is not a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), because $[u,u]\ne0$.
