# Ramification groups of the splitting field of T3 minus 2 over Q3

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups](uniformizer-criterion-for-lower-ramification-groups.md)

For $L=\mathbb Q_3(\zeta_3,\alpha)$ with $\alpha^3=2$, the shifted cubic for $\alpha+1$ and quadratic for $\zeta_3-1$ are [Eisenstein](eisenstein-criterion.md). Their compositum is totally ramified of degree six. The element $\pi=(\zeta_3-1)/(\alpha+1)$ has [valuation](valuation.md) one. Order-three automorphisms move it with [valuation](valuation.md) two and transpositions with [valuation](valuation.md) one. The lower breaks are zero and one, the upper breaks zero and one-half, and the [different exponent from ramification groups](different-exponent-from-ramification-groups.md) is seven.
