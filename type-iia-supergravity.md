# Type IIA supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_IIA_supergravity)

Massless type IIA supergravity is the nonchiral ten-dimensional maximal [supergravity](supergravity.md) with metric, [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md), [dilaton](dilaton.md), Ramond-Ramond one-form and three-form potentials, two opposite-chirality Majorana-Weyl [gravitini](gravitino.md), and two opposite-chirality Majorana-Weyl [dilatini](dilatino.md). Its massless spectrum has $128$ bosonic and $128$ fermionic polarizations. The two [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) have opposite ten-dimensional [chirality](chirality-physics.md).
