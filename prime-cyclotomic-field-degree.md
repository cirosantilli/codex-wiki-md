# Prime cyclotomic field degree

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

For a prime $p$, the [cyclotomic polynomial](cyclotomic-polynomial.md) of a primitive $p$th root of unity is

$$
\Phi_p(X)=1+X+\cdots+X^{p-1}.
$$

It is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$, so adjoining one root gives a field of degree $p-1$.
