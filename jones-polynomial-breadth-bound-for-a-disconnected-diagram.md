# Jones polynomial breadth bound for a disconnected diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Kauffman bracket breadth bound](kauffman-bracket-breadth-bound.md)

Here $n$ is the crossing count and $k$ is the number of connected components of the planar [link diagram](link-diagram.md), not necessarily the number of [link](link.md) components. The all-$A$ and all-$B$ state counts satisfy $s_A+s_B\leq n+2k$, by summing the [Turaev surface](turaev-surface.md) [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) bound over projection components. The [Kauffman bracket breadth bound](kauffman-bracket-breadth-bound.md) and $t=A^{-4}$ give the displayed bound. It is sharp for an [unlink](unlink.md) diagram with $n=0$, whose [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md) has [breadth of a Laurent polynomial](breadth-of-a-laurent-polynomial.md) $k-1$.
