# Photon shot noise

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

Independent photon arrivals have a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md): a mean count $\mu$ has variance $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sqrt\mu$. Subtraction of independent counting measurements adds their variances.
