<h1 id="finite-order-mobius-orbit-sizes">Finite-order Möbius orbit sizes</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius conjugacy normal forms](mobius-conjugacy-normal-forms.md)

A nonidentity [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) of finite order $n$ is conjugate to multiplication by a primitive $n$th [root of unity](root-of-unity.md). Zero and infinity are fixed; for any other point, a return after $k$ iterates requires $\mu^k=1$, whose least positive solution is $n$. Thus its only orbit sizes are one and $n$. For the identity, every orbit has size one. This is a direct orbit computation, independent of an appeal to the orbit-stabilizer theorem.
