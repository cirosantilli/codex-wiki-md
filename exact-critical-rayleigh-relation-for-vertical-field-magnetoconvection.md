# Exact critical Rayleigh relation for vertical-field magnetoconvection

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation](vertical-field-magnetoconvection-dispersion-relation.md)

At the minimum of the steady [vertical-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation](vertical-field-magnetoconvection-dispersion-relation.md), stationarity gives $R_c=2(k_c^2+\pi^2)^3/\pi^2$. With nonmagnetic value $R_0=27\pi^4/4$, eliminating the critical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) gives the stated relation and $k_c^2=\pi^2[\tfrac32(R_c/R_0)^{1/3}-1]$. These hold for every nonnegative [Chandrasekhar number](chandrasekhar-number.md), including its zero-field limit.
