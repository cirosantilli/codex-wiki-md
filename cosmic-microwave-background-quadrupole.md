# Cosmic microwave background quadrupole

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background anisotropy](cosmic-microwave-background-anisotropy-split.md)

The [cosmic microwave background quadrupole](cosmic-microwave-background-quadrupole.md) is the degree-two [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) part of the observed [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) sky. It has five real coefficients. The axisymmetric example $Q P_2(\cos\theta)$ selects only one orientation and shape from this space; $Q$ is its chosen amplitude, not a normalization for every possible [cosmic microwave background quadrupole](cosmic-microwave-background-quadrupole.md).
