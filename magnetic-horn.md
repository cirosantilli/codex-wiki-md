# Magnetic horn

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino beam](neutrino-beam.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_horn)

A [magnetic horn](magnetic-horn.md) uses a pulsed magnetic field around a high-current conductor to focus charged secondary [pions](pion.md) and [kaons](kaon.md) of a selected sign. Their decays then create a directed [neutrino beam](neutrino-beam.md). The field acts on the charged parents, not on the neutral [neutrinos](neutrino.md).
