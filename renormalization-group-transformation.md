# Renormalization-group transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization group](renormalization-group.md)

A renormalization-group transformation integrates out selected short-distance degrees of freedom and restores chosen cutoff and field-normalization conventions. It defines a map $R_b$ on couplings at a length factor $b$. Its discrete [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) multipliers differ from [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the generator of a continuous [renormalization-group flow](renormalization-group-flow.md); the relation for a scaling direction is $\lambda=b^y$.
