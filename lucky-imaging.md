# Lucky imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomical seeing](astronomical-seeing.md)

Many short frames freeze atmospheric image fluctuations. Selecting the sharpest frames, registering them and combining them can improve [angular resolution](angular-resolution.md). Selection discards [photons](photon.md) and can create a read-noise penalty, so adequate reference-source [photon](photon.md) counts are essential. It does not guarantee that every high-order [wavefront error](wavefront-error.md) disappears.
