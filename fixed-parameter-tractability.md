# Fixed-parameter tractability

↑ **Parent:** [Parameterized complexity](parameterized-complexity.md)

A parameterized [decision problem](decision-problem.md) is [fixed-parameter tractable](fixed-parameter-tractability.md) if an [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) decides every instance of encoded length $N$ and parameter $k$ in [running time](running-time.md) at most $f(k)N^c$, for a computable [function](function-split.md) $f$ and a constant $c$ independent of $k$. The distinction is in the exponent: $2^kN^3$ has this form, whereas an $N^k$ bound alone does not establish [fixed-parameter tractability](fixed-parameter-tractability.md). Branching on an uncovered [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) gives a [fixed-parameter tractable](fixed-parameter-tractability.md) [vertex cover decision problem](vertex-cover-decision-problem.md).
