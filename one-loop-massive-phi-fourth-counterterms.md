# One-loop massive phi-fourth counterterms

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic interaction](quartic-interaction.md)

With interaction $-\mu^\epsilon\lambda\phi^4/4!$ and $d=4-\epsilon$, the two-point [tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md) has [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) $1/2$. The [Euclidean massive loop integral](euclidean-massive-loop-integral.md) gives its pole through $I_{d,1}=-m^2/(8\pi^2\epsilon)+O(1)$. The three four-point [bubble diagrams](bubble-diagram.md) each have symmetry factor $1/2$, and the [massive scalar bubble pole in four dimensions](massive-scalar-bubble-pole-in-four-dimensions.md) gives the quartic counterterm. The tadpole is independent of external [momentum](momentum.md), so no one-loop [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) is required. A vacuum-energy counterterm is additionally needed if vacuum diagrams are retained.
