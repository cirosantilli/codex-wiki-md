# Relative quantum phase

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum phase](quantum-phase.md)

The phase difference between nonzero amplitudes in a specified basis, for example between $a$ and $b$ in $a|1\rangle+b|2\rangle$. Unlike a [global phase](global-phase.md), it affects [quantum interference](quantum-interference.md) when the two basis components are recombined. It is encoded in the off-diagonal [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) elements and depends on the phase convention for the basis states.
