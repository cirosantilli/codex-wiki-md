# Unramified cyclic cubic extension from a prime-discriminant cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md)

Suppose an irreducible monic integral cubic has discriminant $-p$ for a prime $p>3$, and let $L$ be its [splitting field](splitting-field.md). Its [Galois group](galois-group.md) is $S_3$ and its quadratic subfield is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-p})$. The extension over that subfield is cyclic cubic and everywhere unramified. Away from $p$, separable reduction and [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) put the roots in an unramified local extension. At $p$, discriminant [valuation](valuation.md) one gives a double residue root and a separate simple root; lift the simple root, and the remaining quadratic's discriminant has square class $-p$. Its splitting field is already the quadratic subfield's completion. The imaginary quadratic base has no real places.
