<h1 id="hawking-s-area-theorem">Hawking's area theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Second law of black-hole mechanics](second-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawking's_area_theorem)

Hawking's area theorem states that the total area of classical future event horizons cannot decrease when the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md) and the required global assumptions hold. It is the geometric content of the [second law of black-hole mechanics](second-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md).
