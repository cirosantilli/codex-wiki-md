# Antibody variable region

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)

The paired [antibody variable regions](antibody-variable-region.md) of an [immunoglobulin heavy chain](immunoglobulin-heavy-chain.md) and an [immunoglobulin light chain](immunoglobulin-light-chain.md) contribute to an [antibody binding site](paratope.md). Variation refers to differences between [antibody](antibody.md) sequences, not to a tip continually changing identity while binding.
