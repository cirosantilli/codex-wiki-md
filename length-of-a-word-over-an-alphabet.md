# Length of a word over an alphabet

↑ **Parent:** [String](string.md)

The [length of a word over an alphabet](length-of-a-word-over-an-alphabet.md) is the number of symbol positions in that finite [string](string.md), counting repetitions. Thus $\ell(abba)=4$ and $\ell(\varepsilon)=0$ for the [empty word](empty-word.md). [Concatenation](concatenation.md) satisfies $\ell(uv)=\ell(u)+\ell(v)$. This differs from the [word length](word-length.md) of a [group](group-split.md) element, which minimizes over representing [words](string.md) using a chosen [generating set of a group](generating-set-of-a-group.md).
