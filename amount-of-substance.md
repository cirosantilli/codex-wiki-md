# Amount of substance

↑ **Parent:** [Physical quantity](physical-quantity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amount_of_substance)

Amount of substance measures a count of specified entities, such as [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), [molecules](molecule.md), [ions](ion.md), or [Electrons](electron.md), using the [mole](mole-unit.md) as its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) base unit. It differs from [mass](mass.md): equal amounts of substance of different [chemical substances](chemical-substance.md) contain equal entity counts but generally have different [masses](mass.md). If $N$ entities are present, $n=N/N_{\mathrm A}$, where $N_{\mathrm A}$ is the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md). The specified entity matters: one [mole](mole-unit.md) of [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule.md) contains two [moles](mole-unit.md) of [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). [BIPM, definition of amount of substance and the mole](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/mole)
