# Symmetric coactivity weights do not distinguish reversed episodes

↑ **Parent:** [Cell assembly](cell-assembly.md)

Suppose storing activities $a_1,\ldots,a_T$ changes a matrix by $\Delta W=\eta\sum_{t=1}^T a_ta_t^T$. Any permutation of the activities leaves this sum unchanged. Therefore the update alone cannot distinguish an episode from its reversal. This is a restriction on the stated order-insensitive update, not on all [Hebbian learning](hebbian-learning.md): [trace learning](trace-learning.md), an evolving context or directed transitions can introduce order information.
