# Linear-system residual norm

↑ **Parent:** [Linear-system residual](linear-system-residual.md)

The [norm](norm.md) of a [linear-system residual](linear-system-residual.md), used to measure how closely an approximate solution satisfies its [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md). It differs from the solution-error norm $\|x_*-x\|$. Their relationship depends on the [matrix inverse](matrix-inverse-split.md) and the [condition number](condition-number.md), so a small residual alone is not a scale-free relative-error guarantee.
