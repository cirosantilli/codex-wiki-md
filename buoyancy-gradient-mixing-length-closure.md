# Buoyancy-gradient mixing-length closure

↑ **Parent:** [Eddy diffusion](eddy-diffusion.md)

For positive dense-fluid [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $G$ and upward coordinate $z$, an unstable gradient $G_z>0$ supplies buoyant work of order $d^2G_z$ over mixing length $d$. [velocity](velocity.md) $u\sim d\sqrt{G_z}$ and [eddy diffusivity](eddy-diffusivity.md) $K\sim ud$ follow. Order-one constants depend on the [turbulence](turbulence-split.md) model. This closure is for an unstable gradient; its square root must not be applied to a stable negative gradient.
