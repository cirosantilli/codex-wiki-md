# Boiling point

↑ **Parent:** [Boiling](boiling.md)

At a specified external [pressure](pressure-split.md), the equilibrium [temperature](temperature-split.md) at which a pure liquid's saturation [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) equals that external [pressure](pressure-split.md). Bubble nucleation, hydrostatic head and [capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md) can affect the observed onset of [boiling](boiling.md) without redefining the equilibrium property.
