# Atomic line cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical cooling function](astrophysical-cooling-function.md)

[Atomic line cooling](atomic-line-cooling.md) removes gas kinetic energy by [collisional excitation](collisional-excitation.md) followed by photon escape. It is strongest where atoms or ions have bound [Electrons](electron.md) and thermally accessible transitions. If photons are trapped, or if the relevant atoms are fully ionized, the simple optically thin line-cooling rate no longer gives the same loss.
