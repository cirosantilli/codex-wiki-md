# CNOT control reversal in the Hadamard basis

↑ **Parent:** [Controlled-NOT gate](controlled-not-gate.md)

Writing a [CNOT gate](controlled-not-gate.md) as $P_0\otimes I+P_1\otimes X$ gives $I\otimes Q_++Z\otimes Q_-$, where $Q_\pm=(I\pm X)/2$. In the [Hadamard basis](hadamard-basis.md), $Z$ flips the sign label and $X$ is diagonal. Thus the same gate matrix reverses its control and target roles under the two local basis changes.
