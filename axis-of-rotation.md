# Axis of rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation (mathematics)](rotation-mathematics.md)

The fixed [straight line](straight-line.md) of a nonidentity three-dimensional [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md). For a [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) $R$, its [direction vector](direction-vector.md) is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $1$. A [unit vector](unit-vector.md) along it also specifies the positive sense of the [angle](angle.md) through the right-hand convention.
