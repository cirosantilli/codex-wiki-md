# Finite-dimensional subspace is closed

↑ **Parent:** [Normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md)

Every [finite-dimensional vector space](finite-dimensional-vector-space.md) subspace of a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) is closed. Coordinates in a finite basis identify it homeomorphically with a finite-dimensional scalar space; it is therefore complete, and every complete subspace of a metric space is closed.
