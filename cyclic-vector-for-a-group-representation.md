# Cyclic vector for a group representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A vector $v$ is cyclic for a [group representation](group-representation.md) $\rho:G\to GL(V)$ if the [linear span](linear-span.md) of $\{\rho(g)v:g\in G\}$ is all of $V$. Equivalently $v$ generates $V$ as a module over the [group algebra](group-algebra.md). Its projection to the invariant subspace generates that subspace, so a cyclic representation of a [finite group](finite-group.md) has at most one copy of the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md).
