<h1 id="canonical-stability-of-a-schwarzschild-anti-de-sitter-black-hole">Canonical stability of a Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter black hole</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter black hole](schwarzschild-anti-de-sitter-black-hole.md)

The Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter heat capacity has the sign of $dT_H/dr_+$. It is positive precisely for $r_+>L/\sqrt3$, so these large black holes are locally stable in a fixed-temperature reservoir.
