# Two-spin XY Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

The two-site quantum version of an [XY model](xy-model.md) couples the transverse components of two [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) degrees of freedom. In the ordered basis $|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle,|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle,|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle,|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle$, its only off-diagonal entries are $H_{23}=H_{32}=-2J$. The parallel states have [energies](energy-split.md) $-2h$ and $2h$; the symmetric and antisymmetric opposite-spin combinations have [energies](energy-split.md) $-2J$ and $2J$ respectively. The latter is the [singlet state](singlet-state.md). The formulas remain valid at accidental degeneracies.
