# Long exact sequence in relative homology

↑ **Parent:** [Relative homology](relative-homology.md)

For a pair $A\subseteq X$, the [short exact sequence of chain complexes](short-exact-sequence-of-chain-complexes.md) $0\to C_*(A)\to C_*(X)\to C_*(X,A)\to0$ induces

$$
\cdots\to H_n(A)\to H_n(X)\to H_n(X,A)\xrightarrow{\partial}H_{n-1}(A)\to\cdots.
$$
