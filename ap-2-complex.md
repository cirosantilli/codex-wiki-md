# AP-2 complex

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

The [AP-2 complex](ap-2-complex.md) is an adaptor complex that links cargo and membrane recognition to [clathrin](clathrin.md)-coat assembly at the [plasma membrane](cell-membrane-split.md). [phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate](phosphatidylinositol-4-5-bisphosphate.md) contributes to its recruitment and activation.
