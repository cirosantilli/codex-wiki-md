# Hecke eigenform

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md)

A Hecke eigenform is a nonzero [modular form](modular-form.md) that is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) for every [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md). A cuspidal eigenform has nonzero first [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md): $a_1(T_nf)=a_n(f)$ proves this. Normalize $a_1=1$, and then $T_nf=a_n(f)f$. The [Hecke multiplication relations](hecke-multiplication-relations.md) give multiplicativity and the prime-power recurrence of the coefficients.
