# Siegbahn precision X-ray wavelength spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray emission spectroscopy](x-ray-emission-spectroscopy.md)

Precision [X-ray emission spectroscopy](x-ray-emission-spectroscopy.md) turns closely spaced [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) into quantitative constraints on atomic [energy levels](energy-level.md). [Bragg's law](bragg-s-law.md) makes a [crystal](crystal.md) an angular ruler for [wavelength](wavelength.md): resolving nearby directions can reveal components hidden inside an apparently single line. Accurate line positions and the ability to separate two lines are related, but distinct, experimental achievements.

[Karl Manne Georg Siegbahn](karl-manne-georg-siegbahn.md) received the 1924 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md); his lecture was delivered on 11 December 1925.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; The X-ray Spectra and the Structure of the Atoms, heading, printed p. 81](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/the-x-ray-spectra-and-the-structure-of-the-atoms-by-karl-manne-georg-siegbahn.md)

His son [Kai Siegbahn](kai-manne-borje-siegbahn.md) received the 1981 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) for electron [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md).[Family Nobel Prize laureates in physics, Father and son: Manne and Kai Siegbahn](facts-on-the-nobel-prize-in-physics-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)

The [Barkla characteristic X-ray experiment](barkla-characteristic-x-ray-experiment.md) distinguished element-dependent K and L groups through absorption and excitation. [Moseley's law](moseley-s-law.md) related corresponding line [frequencies](frequency.md) across [chemical elements](chemical-element.md) to [atomic number](atomic-number.md). Measuring the components within those groups asks a further question: which distinct atomic [energy](energy-split.md) differences produce the neighbouring [spectral lines](spectral-line.md)?

Siegbahn's lecture organizes measured [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) into [energy levels](energy-level.md) and resolved double levels.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; Printed pp. 84–86, Figs. 1–2](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/the-x-ray-spectra-and-the-structure-of-the-atoms-by-karl-manne-georg-siegbahn.md) The payoff is a map of atomic [energy](energy-split.md) differences as well as an element's position in the [periodic table](periodic-table.md).
