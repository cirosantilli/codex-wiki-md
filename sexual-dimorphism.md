# Sexual dimorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Phenotypic trait](phenotypic-trait.md)

A systematic difference in [phenotypic traits](phenotypic-trait.md) between sexes within a [species](species.md), such as body proportions or coloration. It can make specimen-based [species delimitation](species-delimitation.md) difficult if sexes are mistaken for separate species. The degree of dimorphism can also supply an empirical proxy for reproductive investment in particular fossil groups.
