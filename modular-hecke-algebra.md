# Modular Hecke algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md)

A modular Hecke algebra is the algebra generated by [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md) acting on a specified space of [modular forms](modular-form.md), with level, weight and coefficient ring fixed. Its [Eisenstein ideal](eisenstein-ideal.md) encodes congruences with Eisenstein eigenvalues. This arithmetic algebra should not be confused with the generic Hecke algebra of a Coxeter group.
