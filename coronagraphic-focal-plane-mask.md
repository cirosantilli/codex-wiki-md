# Coronagraphic focal plane mask

↑ **Parent:** [Lyot coronagraph](lyot-coronagraph.md)

A [coronagraphic focal plane mask](coronagraphic-focal-plane-mask.md) modifies the [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) or [wave phase](phase-waves.md) in an intermediate [focal plane](focal-plane.md). Here it is a perfectly opaque circular disk centered on the bright source. A finite [aperture](aperture.md) produces an extended [point spread function](point-spread-function.md), so a finite disk blocks only part of the stellar [light](light.md). Its sharp edge also changes the transmitted field and hence the later [diffraction](diffraction.md) pattern. The exact model below makes both effects visible.
