# P-SV displacement potentials

↑ **Parent:** [Helmholtz separation of elastic waves](helmholtz-separation-of-elastic-waves.md)

In a uniform isotropic material, these scalar [P-SV displacement potentials](p-sv-displacement-potentials.md) separate longitudinal [P waves](p-wave.md) from vertically polarized [SV-waves](sv-wave.md) in an $x,z$ plane. They satisfy $\phi_{tt}=\alpha^2\Delta\phi$ and $\psi_{tt}=\beta^2\Delta\psi$, with compressional and shear speeds $\alpha,\beta$. Reversing the sign convention for $\psi$ changes its polarization column but not the physical [P-SV directional impedance matrix](p-sv-directional-impedance-matrix.md) or the separate modal [energy](energy.md) fluxes.
