# Energy decay criterion for a viscous-resistive shearing box

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing box](shearing-box.md)

For zero mean velocity and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) in a [shearing box](shearing-box.md), the perturbation energy obeys $\dot E\le2(|A|-4\pi^2\eta/L^2)E$ when kinematic viscosity and magnetic diffusivity agree. Bound the work of [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md) and [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md) by $2|A|E$, and use the [spectral gap of a shearing-periodic box](spectral-gap-of-a-shearing-periodic-box.md) to bound dissipation below. Thus the displayed bound on [magnetic Reynolds number](magnetic-reynolds-number.md) guarantees exponential decay and excludes sustained turbulence.
