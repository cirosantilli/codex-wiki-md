# Two-torsion square-class homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer map of an elliptic curve](kummer-map-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Let $E:y^2=f(x)$ with $f$ monic and separable, and let $T=(\theta,0)$. Over $K$ containing $\theta$, the map $\alpha_T(P)=[x(P)-\theta]$ for $P\ne O,T$, completed by $\alpha_T(O)=1$ and $\alpha_T(T)=[f^{\prime}(\theta)]$, is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) to the [square-class group of a field](square-class-group-of-a-field.md). A nonvertical chord has the product of its three $x-\theta$ values a square; a chord through $T$ instead has the product of the other two values equal to $f^{\prime}(\theta)$. These identities include tangencies.
