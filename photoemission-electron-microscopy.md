# Photoemission electron microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photoemission_electron_microscopy)

[Electron](electron.md) optics forms a magnified spatial image of [photoelectrons](photoelectron.md) emitted from an illuminated surface. Emission yield and electron-energy selection can supply work-function, electronic or chemical contrast; brightness is not necessarily geometrical height.
