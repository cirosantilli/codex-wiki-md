# Monochromatic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave-split.md)

A [monochromatic wave](monochromatic-wave.md) has a single [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). Its sinusoidal components can have different [wave amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) and [wave phases](phase-waves.md) while sharing that [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), as in the [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) examples.
