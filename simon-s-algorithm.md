<h1 id="simon-s-algorithm">Simon's algorithm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Simon's problem](simon-s-problem.md)

Simon's algorithm prepares hidden-subgroup [coset states](coset-state.md), applies the [quantum Fourier transform](quantum-fourier-transform.md) over $(\mathbb Z_2)^n$, and samples vectors $y$ satisfying $y\cdot p=0$ over $\mathbb F_2$. Repeated samples and [Gaussian elimination](gaussian-elimination.md) recover $p$ with $O(n)$ oracle queries and polynomial computation.
