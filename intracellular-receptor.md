# Intracellular receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Receptor (biochemistry)](receptor-biochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intracellular_receptor)

An [intracellular receptor](intracellular-receptor.md) recognizes a [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) inside a [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md), including in the [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md) or [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md). Some [intracellular receptors](intracellular-receptor.md) regulate [transcription](transcription-biology.md) after binding a membrane-permeable signal; an [intracellular receptor](intracellular-receptor.md) does not universally imply direct control of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). See the receptor-location reference under [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md).
