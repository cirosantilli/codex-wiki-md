# Bracket smoothing state

↑ **Parent:** [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md)

A bracket smoothing state chooses one of the two unoriented smoothings at every crossing of a [link diagram](link-diagram.md). Its weight is $A^{a-b}\delta^{s-1}$ in the reduced [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md), where $a,b$ count the smoothing types and $s$ counts resulting circles. The all-$A$ and all-$B$ states choose the same smoothing type at every crossing.
