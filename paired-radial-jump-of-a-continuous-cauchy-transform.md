# Paired radial jump of a continuous Cauchy transform

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy transform](cauchy-transform.md)

For continuous boundary data on the counterclockwise unit circle and its [Cauchy transform](cauchy-transform.md) $\Phi$, $\Phi(r\omega)-\Phi(r^{-1}\omega)=P\phi(r\omega)$ for $0<r<1$. This follows by subtracting the two Cauchy kernels and obtaining the [Poisson kernel on the circle](poisson-kernel-on-the-circle.md). Consequently the paired interior-minus-exterior difference tends to $\phi(\omega)$ as $r\uparrow1$. For $r\downarrow1$ from above, the same written difference has the opposite sign. This paired limit does not require each individual boundary value of the transform to exist.
