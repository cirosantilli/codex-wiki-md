# Bond moving

↑ **Parent:** [Migdal-Kadanoff approximation](migdal-kadanoff-approximation.md)

In the bond-moving-first hypercubic construction with blocking [length](length.md) $b$, combine $b^{d-1}$ transverse-equivalent bonds into each link of a surviving $b$-link chain. Summing that chain gives the displayed [Migdal-Kadanoff approximation](migdal-kadanoff-approximation.md). For $b=2$, it becomes $K'=\tfrac12\log\cosh(2^dK)$. The $d=1$ specialization is exact; the higher-dimensional formula is a convention-dependent approximation, not an exact closure of the original hypercubic model.
