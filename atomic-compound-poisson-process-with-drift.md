# Atomic compound Poisson process with drift

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy process](levy-process.md)

With finitely many nonzero marks $z_j$ and independent [Poisson processes](poisson-process.md) $N^j$ of rates $\lambda_j$, the displayed process is a [Lévy process](levy-process.md) with no Gaussian component. Its [Lévy measure](levy-measure.md) is $\sum_j\lambda_j\delta_{z_j}$, its mean is $t(\gamma+\sum_j\lambda_jz_j)$, and its [variance](variance-split.md) is $t\sum_j\lambda_jz_j^2$. Its paths have finitely many jumps on every bounded interval and deterministic slope $\gamma$ between jumps. Coincident mark sizes combine their rates; zero marks have no effect. The drift here uses the uncompensated finite-sum convention, which differs from the drift parameter in a compensated [Lévy–Khintchine formula](levy-khintchine-formula.md).
