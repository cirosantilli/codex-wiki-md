# Thermal and compositional interfacial buoyancy criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Compositional convection](compositional-convection.md)

For two [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) layers at a common interfacial [pressure](pressure-split.md), the lower layer has smaller [density](density.md) precisely when $M_-/T_-<M_+/T_+$, where $M$ is mean [molar mass](molar-mass.md). That ordering is unstable to an infinitesimal interchange. A heavy condensable constituent can therefore inhibit [thermal convection](thermal-convection.md) in a light background gas even when the lower layer is hotter. This local criterion assumes no phase change during the initial displacement.
