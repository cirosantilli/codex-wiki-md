# Epicyclic frequency bounds for monotone spherical density

↑ **Parent:** [Radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md)

For a nonnegative, radially nonincreasing spherical [mass density](density.md), $\Omega^2=GM(r)/r^3$ and $\kappa^2=\Omega^2+4\pi G\rho(r)$. Since $\rho(r)$ does not exceed the mean enclosed density, the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) lies between $\Omega$ and $2\Omega$. General axisymmetric disks need not satisfy this bound.
