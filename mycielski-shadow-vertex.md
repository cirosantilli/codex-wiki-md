# Mycielski shadow vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Mycielskian](mycielskian.md)

A [Mycielski shadow vertex](mycielski-shadow-vertex.md) $u_i$ has [graph neighbourhood](graph-neighbourhood.md) $N_G(v_i)\cup\{w\}$ in $\mu(G)$. Its neighbours other than the [Mycielski apex](mycielski-apex.md) are original [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). All [Mycielski shadow vertices](mycielski-shadow-vertex.md) form an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md).
