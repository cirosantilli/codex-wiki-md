# Physical law

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)

A physical law expresses a reproducible relation used to describe or predict physical phenomena within its domain of applicability. Applying it requires a model specifying the system and conditions: [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md), together with a [force](force.md) model, predicts a body's [acceleration](acceleration.md); [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) constrains exchanges within a specified system. Its predictions must be compared with physical observations.
