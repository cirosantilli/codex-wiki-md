# Canonical quantization of a real scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md)

The equal-time [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) is $[\phi(t,\mathbf x),\pi(t,\mathbf y)]=i\delta^3(\mathbf x-\mathbf y)$ in units $\hbar=1$, with the two field-field and momentum-momentum commutators zero. The quadratic [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) gives $\dot\phi=\pi$ and $\dot\pi=\nabla^2\phi-m^2\phi$, hence the [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) as an operator identity.
