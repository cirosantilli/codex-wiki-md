# Hodge number

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md)

The Hodge number of a compact complex manifold is $h^{p,q}=\dim_{\mathbb C}H^{p,q}_{\bar\partial}(X)$.

For a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), these dimensions are the graded dimensions in its [Hodge structure](hodge-structure.md).
