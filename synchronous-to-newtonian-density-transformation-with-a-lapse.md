# Synchronous-to-Newtonian density transformation with a lapse

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar gauge transformations with a background lapse](scalar-gauge-transformations-with-a-background-lapse.md)

For spatial scalar convention $g_{ij}=a^2[(1-2\Psi)\delta_{ij}-2E_{,ij}]$ and $g_{0i}=a^2B_{,i}$, a passive gauge displacement gives $\widetilde E=E+\lambda$ and $\widetilde B=B-\dot\lambda+\bar N^2\xi^0/a^2$. Moving from [synchronous gauge](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md) to [Newtonian gauge](newtonian-gauge.md) therefore requires $\lambda=-E_S$ and $\xi^0=-a^2\dot E_S/\bar N^2$. A scalar density transforms by $\delta\widetilde\rho=\delta\rho-\dot{\bar\rho}\xi^0$, giving the displayed [density contrast](density-contrast.md) relation. Keeping the background [lapse function](lapse-function.md) explicit prevents cosmic-time and conformal-time factors from being confused.
