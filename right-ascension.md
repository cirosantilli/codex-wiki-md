# Right ascension

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial coordinate system](equatorial-coordinate-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_ascension)

Right ascension is the eastward angular coordinate measured around the celestial equator from the reference equinox. It is often quoted in hours, with 24 hours corresponding to a full turn.
