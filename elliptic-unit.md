# Elliptic unit

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)

Elliptic units are algebraic units constructed by evaluating suitably normalized elliptic functions at CM torsion points and taking products or norms into ray class fields. Their distribution relations make them norm compatible in CM division towers. They generate an [Euler system of elliptic units](euler-system-of-elliptic-units.md) and their local [Coleman power series for a CM formal group](coleman-power-series-for-a-cm-formal-group.md) have logarithmic derivatives encoding critical [Hecke L-function](hecke-l-function.md) values.
