# Maximal unramified subextension of a local field extension

↑ **Parent:** [Unramified extension](unramified-extension.md)

Every finite extension $L/K$ of non-Archimedean [local fields](local-field.md) has a unique intermediate field $K_0$ whose residue field is $k_L$ and for which $K_0/K$ is unramified. The extension $L/K_0$ is totally ramified.
