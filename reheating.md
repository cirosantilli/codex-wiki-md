# Reheating

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)

Reheating transfers the [inflaton](inflaton.md) energy into other particles after [cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md), eventually establishing a hot thermal bath. It requires interactions in addition to a background [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md). The duration and averaged [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) of reheating affect the [number of e-folds](number-of-e-folds.md) between a chosen scale's horizon exit and the end of inflation.
