# Space clamp

↑ **Parent:** [Whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md)

The [space clamp](space-clamp.md) problem is the failure to maintain the commanded [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) throughout a spatially extended [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md). A soma [voltage clamp](voltage-clamp.md) can coexist with substantial voltage changes in distant [dendrites](dendrite.md) or an [axon](axon.md). Reducing [patch-clamp series resistance](patch-clamp-series-resistance.md) improves access at the recording site, but does not eliminate electrical separation within the [neuron](neuron.md).
