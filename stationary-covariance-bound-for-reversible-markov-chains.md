# Stationary covariance bound for reversible Markov chains

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute L2 spectral gap of a reversible Markov chain](absolute-l2-spectral-gap-of-a-reversible-markov-chain.md)

If $\|P\|_{L^2_0(\mu)}\leq r<1$, a stationary [reversible Markov chain](reversible-markov-chain.md) satisfies

$$
|\operatorname{Cov}(f(X_0),f(X_k))|\leq r^k\operatorname{Var}_\mu(f).
$$

Consequently the [variance](variance-split.md) of a sum of $N$ consecutive values is at most $N(1+r)/(1-r)$ times the stationary [variance](variance-split.md) of one value. This bounds the [Markov chain Monte Carlo asymptotic variance](markov-chain-monte-carlo-asymptotic-variance.md) uniformly over square-integrable functions.
