# Jacobi truncation of two singular isothermal spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi tidal radius](jacobi-tidal-radius.md)

For a low-mass [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md) satellite on a circular orbit of radius $D$ in a [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md) host, $\Omega^2=2\sigma_h^2/D^2$ and $\Phi_h''=-2\sigma_h^2/D^2$. The radial differential effective acceleration is $(\Omega^2-\Phi_h'')r$. Balancing it with satellite gravity $2\sigma_d^2/r$ gives the displayed [Jacobi tidal radius](jacobi-tidal-radius.md) and bound mass $m_t=\sqrt2D\sigma_d^3/(G\sigma_h)$. Equal mean density gives a different order-one approximation, $r_t=D\sigma_d/\sigma_h$; eccentric orbits require an orbital-history prescription.
