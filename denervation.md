# Denervation

↑ **Parent:** [Nerve injury](nerve-injury.md)

Interruption or loss of a structure's [nerve](nerve.md) supply. Its physiological consequences can include direct motor or secretory changes and indirect effects through altered [perfusion](perfusion.md).
