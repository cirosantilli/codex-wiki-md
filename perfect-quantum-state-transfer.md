# Perfect quantum state transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md)

A [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) has perfect transfer between [basis](basis.md) states $|a\rangle,|b\rangle$ at time $t_*$ if $e^{-iHt_*}|a\rangle=e^{i\chi}|b\rangle$ for a known [global phase](global-phase.md) $\chi$. In an excitation-conserving [qubit](qubit.md) network whose vacuum is fixed, the initial [product state](product-state.md) with $\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle$ at site $a$ and zeros elsewhere evolves to $\alpha|\mathrm{vac}\rangle+\beta e^{i\chi}|b\rangle$. A local [phase gate](phase-gate.md) at $b$ removing $\chi$ restores the unknown [qubit](qubit.md) there. This explains why transfer of the single-excitation [basis](basis.md) state suffices for [qubit](qubit.md) transfer when the vacuum phase is also accounted for.
