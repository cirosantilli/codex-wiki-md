# Character table of the nonabelian group of order twenty-one

↑ **Parent:** [Character table](character-table.md)

For the nontrivial action of $C_3$ on $C_7$, the [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) sizes are $1,7,7,3,3$. Three [linear characters](linear-character.md) factor through the quotient $C_3$, and the remaining two [irreducible characters](irreducible-character.md) have degree three. Put $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$ and let $\alpha,\beta=(-1\pm i\sqrt7)/2$. With suitable class labels the table is

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
&1&g_2&g_3&g_4&g_5\\\hline
\text{class size}&1&7&7&3&3\\
\chi_1&1&1&1&1&1\\
\chi_2&1&\omega&\omega^2&1&1\\
\chi_3&1&\omega^2&\omega&1&1\\
\chi_4&3&0&0&\alpha&\beta\\
\chi_5&3&0&0&\beta&\alpha
\end{array}
$$

One can derive the last two rows by inducing nontrivial [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) of $C_7$ along the two orbits of the order-three [automorphism](automorphism.md) on their exponents. Their degree-three orbit sums vanish off $C_7$. On a generator they give the two sums of seventh roots over quadratic residues and nonresidues. Their sum is $-1$ and their product is $2$: the nine pairwise exponent sums between the two triples contain zero three times and every nonzero residue once. Thus they are the roots of $X^2+X+2$. Alternatively [column orthogonality](column-orthogonality.md) forces the zeros on the [centralizers](centralizer.md) of order three, and orthogonality with the identity column interchanges $\alpha$ and $\beta$ in the last row. This finite table illustrates how [centralizer](centralizer.md) sizes and a small number of given values determine missing [character](character-of-a-representation.md) values.
