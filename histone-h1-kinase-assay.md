# Histone H1 kinase assay

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclin-dependent kinase 1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md)

A [histone H1 kinase assay](histone-h1-kinase-assay.md) measures [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) action on supplied [histone H1](histone-h1.md), a [protein](protein-split.md) [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md). When the transferred [phosphate](phosphate.md) carries a radioactive [isotope](isotope.md) label, label incorporated into [histone H1](histone-h1.md) reports catalytic action over the assay interval. It does not count [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md), and a crude preparation can contain other [protein kinases](protein-kinase.md) acting on [histone H1](histone-h1.md). Attribution therefore needs controls.

A 2010 study prepared human [HeLa](hela.md) S3 extracts after a [double thymidine block](double-thymidine-block.md), using swelling, a [Dounce homogenizer](dounce-homogenizer.md), [centrifugation](centrifugation.md), [dialysis](dialysis-biochemistry.md) and concentration. Added recombinant [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md)1 incubated for $20$ minutes.[Deibler and Kirschner (2010), Experimental Procedures, “Tissue Culture, Flow Cytomtery, and Somatic Cell Extract Preparation”; Figure 2A](quantitative-reconstitution-of-mitotic-cdk1-activation-in-somatic-cell-extracts-by-richard-w-deibler-and-others.md)

Aliquots received [histone H1](histone-h1.md) and terminal-$^{32}\mathrm{P}$-labeled [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) for a $15$-minute room-[temperature](temperature-split.md) reaction, stopped with Laemmli buffer. Dried [SDS-PAGE](sds-page.md) gels exposed a [phosphorimager](phosphorimager.md) screen; a BioRad Molecular Imager FX quantified labeling. No-added-[cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) activity set the normalization baseline; RO-3306 reduced H1 activity approximately $85\%$. Companion [Western blots](western-blot.md) monitored Tyr15 [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md), with [actin](actin.md) [Western blot loading control](western-blot-loading-control.md).[Deibler and Kirschner (2010), “H1 Kinase Assays”; Results, “A Three-Stage Activation Profile of CDK1 by Cyclin B”, reported Figure S2A inhibitor result; Figure 2B](quantitative-reconstitution-of-mitotic-cdk1-activation-in-somatic-cell-extracts-by-richard-w-deibler-and-others.md)

Mixed extracts lose compartment boundaries; inhibitor sensitivity supports a major [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) contribution, with residual signal.[Results, “Establishing a Somatic Cell Extract System for Mitotic Entry”; “A Three-Stage Activation Profile of CDK1 by Cyclin B”](quantitative-reconstitution-of-mitotic-cdk1-activation-in-somatic-cell-extracts-by-richard-w-deibler-and-others.md) [Substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) choice and inhibitor specificity limit attribution. The [cell-cycle hysteresis experiment](cell-cycle-hysteresis-experiment.md) supplies a separate history comparison with this kind of [histone H1](histone-h1.md) readout; its dose thresholds should not be transferred to human [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md).
