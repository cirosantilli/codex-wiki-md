# Stoichiometry of ionic compounds

↑ **Parent:** [Salt (chemistry)](salt-chemistry.md)

For a neutral [formula unit](formula-unit.md), let $\nu_i$ count [ions](ion.md) of type $i$ with [electric charge](electric-charge.md) $z_i e$, where $e$ is the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). The [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) must satisfy

$$
\sum_i\nu_i z_i=0.
$$

For $\mathrm{Na}^+$ and $\mathrm{Cl}^-$, the smallest positive counts are $1:1$. For $\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}$ and $\mathrm{Cl}^-$, they are $1:2$, giving [calcium chloride](calcium-chloride.md) $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$: $1(2)+2(-1)=0$. The formula counts [ions](ion.md), not the number of nearest neighbours. A [counterion](counterion.md) is the oppositely charged partner needed to balance a charged species; its identity can change [solubility](solubility.md) and other properties without changing the identity of that species.[§7.1, neutral ionic formulas and charge balance; §2.6, ionic compound examples](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)
