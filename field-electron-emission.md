# Field electron emission

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_electron_emission)

[Field electron emission](field-electron-emission.md) releases [electrons](electron.md) from matter through electric-field-assisted [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) at the surface. The emission mechanism and its rate are distinct from the subsequent acceleration and [Larmor formula](larmor-formula.md) radiation of an electron already travelling through an approximately uniform [electric field](electric-field-split.md).
