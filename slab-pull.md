# Slab pull

↑ **Parent:** [Subduction](subduction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slab_pull)

A gravitational driving force associated with a dense descending slab. The slab's buoyancy and ability to remain coherent matter, so increased planetary gravity alone does not determine whether [subduction](subduction.md) occurs.
