# Heat capacity at constant volume

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)

For a fixed-composition [closed system](closed-system.md), the [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) at fixed [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) is $C_V=(\partial U/\partial T)_V$, with [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) $U$. Under the [quasistatic process](quasistatic-process.md) assumptions in [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), only [pressure](pressure-split.md)-[volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) [work](work.md) is permitted, so $dV=0$ gives $\delta Q=dU=C_V\,dT$. A rigid vessel therefore needs $Q=\Delta U$, or $Q=C_V\Delta T$ when $C_V$ is constant. Its per-unit-[mass](mass.md) counterpart is [specific heat capacity at constant volume](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md).
