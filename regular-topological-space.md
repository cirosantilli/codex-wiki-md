# Regular topological space

↑ **Parent:** [Separation axiom](separation-axiom.md)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is regular when a [point](point-geometry.md) and a [closed set](closed-set.md) not containing it have disjoint [open neighborhoods](open-neighbourhood.md). Equivalently, for every [point](point-geometry.md) $x$ in an [open set](open-set.md) $U$, there is an open $W$ with $x\in W\subseteq\overline W\subseteq U$. To obtain shrinking, separate $x$ from the closed complement of $U$; the [closure](closure-topology.md) of the [point](point-geometry.md)'s [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) avoids the other [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md). Conversely use such a shrinking and the complement of its [closure](closure-topology.md). This definition alone does not require $T_1$.
