# Two-node Gaussian quadrature with linear weight

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md)

For weight $w(x)=x$ on $[0,1]$, the two-node [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md) nodes are roots of $x^2-6x/5+3/10$, the monic quadratic orthogonal to all linear [polynomials](polynomial-split.md). Its weights come from integrating the [Lagrange interpolation](lagrange-polynomial.md) cardinal functions. Orthogonality gives exactness on cubic [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) and positive weights.
