# Hessian criterion for flexes of a plane cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Inflection point of a plane cubic](inflection-point-of-a-plane-cubic.md)

For a smooth homogeneous cubic in characteristic zero, its [plane cubic flexes](inflection-point-of-a-plane-cubic.md) are precisely its intersections with the determinant of its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md). Choose coordinates with a given smooth point $(0:0:1)$ and its tangent $y=0$. Up to a nonzero coefficient, the cubic starts as $yz^2+a x^2z+bxyz+cy^2z$ plus cubic terms in $x,y$. Its tangent contact has order three exactly when $a=0$, and the Hessian determinant at the point is a nonzero constant times $a$. This proves the criterion.
