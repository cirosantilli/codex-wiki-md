# Symmetric sixth-order two-derivative method

↑ **Parent:** [Multiderivative multistep method](multiderivative-multistep-method.md)

For $y'=f(y)$ and $g=f'f$, inserting a smooth exact solution into the displayed recurrence gives defect $h^7y^{(7)}(t_n)/4725+O(h^9)$. This follows by expanding $2\sinh z-(14/15)z\cosh z-(16/15)z+(2/15)z^2\sinh z$. The terms through $z^6$ vanish and the $z^7$ coefficient is nonzero. Its zero-step polynomial is $\zeta^2-1$, whose roots are simple and of unit modulus. Thus it is [zero-stable](zero-stability.md) and has order six, provided starting values and derivative evaluations have corresponding accuracy.
