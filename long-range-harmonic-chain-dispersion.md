# Long-range harmonic-chain dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon dispersion relation](phonon-dispersion-relation.md)

For a translation-invariant monatomic chain with symmetric pair force constants, $M\ddot u_p=\sum_{n>0}C_n(u_{p+n}+u_{p-n}-2u_p)$. Substituting a traveling [normal mode](normal-mode.md) gives the displayed even, reciprocal-periodic [phonon dispersion relation](phonon-dispersion-relation.md). If $\sum n^2C_n$ converges and the chain is stable, its long-wavelength speed is $a\sqrt{\sum n^2C_n/M}$. A truly long-range interaction need not have a finite second moment or a finite linear acoustic slope.
