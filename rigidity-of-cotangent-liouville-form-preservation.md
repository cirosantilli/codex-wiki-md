# Rigidity of cotangent Liouville-form preservation

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent lift of a diffeomorphism](cotangent-lift-of-a-diffeomorphism.md)

A [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) of the entire [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md) preserving the [Liouville one-form](canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) is the [cotangent lift of a diffeomorphism](cotangent-lift-of-a-diffeomorphism.md) of the base. It preserves the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) and the [Liouville vector field](liouville-vector-field.md), hence commutes with the [cotangent fiber-dilation flow](cotangent-fiber-dilation-flow.md). The limit as the fiber contracts forces it to cover its restriction to the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md). Evaluation of the preserved one-form then forces the inverse-transpose derivative action on each covector.
