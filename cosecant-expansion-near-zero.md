# Cosecant expansion near zero

↑ **Parent:** [Cosecant](cosecant.md)

Factor the [sine](sine.md) expansion as $\sin z=z[1-z^2/6+z^4/120+O(z^6)]$. To invert the bracket, put $1+Az^2+Bz^4+O(z^6)$ and equate coefficients after multiplication. The $z^2$ coefficient gives $A=1/6$, while the $z^4$ coefficient gives $B=A/6-1/120=7/360$. Dividing by $z$ proves the displayed [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) truncation for $z\to0$, $z\ne0$. This reciprocal-series calculation is useful when a small [sine](sine.md) argument is itself an inverse power of a large variable; multiply the remainder by any outside prefactor when assigning its final order.
