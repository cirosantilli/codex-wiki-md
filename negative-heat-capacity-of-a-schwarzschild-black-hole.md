# Negative heat capacity of a Schwarzschild black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

In units $G=\hbar=c=k_B=1$, a [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) has [Hawking temperature](hawking-temperature.md) $T=1/(8\pi M)$, so its [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) is $dM/dT=-8\pi M^2$. It heats up as it loses [mass](mass.md), preventing stable canonical equilibrium with an unlimited thermal reservoir.
