<h1 id="parkinson-s-disease">Parkinson's disease</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Neurology](neurology-split.md)

A neurodegenerative disorder with motor and nonmotor manifestations, including motivational difficulties in some patients. Comparisons across [dopamine](dopamine.md)-related medication states can inform mechanisms but do not selectively manipulate a single cognitive process. The disease name commemorates [James Parkinson](james-parkinson.md).
