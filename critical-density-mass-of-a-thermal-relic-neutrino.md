# Critical-density mass of a thermal relic neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Relic-neutrino energy density](relic-neutrino-energy-density.md)

A standard thermal [Cosmic neutrino background](cosmic-neutrino-background.md) with one populated [neutrino](neutrino.md) and one [antineutrino](antineutrino.md) helicity per species has $n_\nu+n_{\bar\nu}=3n_\gamma/11$ after [electron-positron annihilation in cosmology](electron-positron-annihilation-in-cosmology.md). If this species is nonrelativistic today and dominates a spatially flat matter universe, equate $m_\nu(n_\nu+n_{\bar\nu})$ to the [critical density](critical-density.md) $3H_0^2M_P^2/(8\pi)$, using the unreduced [Planck mass](planck-mass.md). The [photon number density](photon-number-density.md) gives the displayed conditional mass. Extra populated states or several massive species change the abundance count; vacuum-dominated cosmology changes the density available for matter.
