# Product over all Dirichlet characters

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md)

For real $s>1$, [Orthogonality of Dirichlet characters](orthogonality-of-dirichlet-characters.md) gives $\log\mathcal P_q(s)=\varphi(q)\sum_{p\nmid q}\sum_{j\ge1,\ p^j\equiv1\pmod q}1/(jp^{js})\ge0$. Thus $\mathcal P_q(s)\ge1$. Only the [principal Dirichlet character](principal-dirichlet-character.md) contributes a [pole](pole.md) at one, and that [pole](pole.md) is simple. A zero of a nonreal [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md) at one brings a zero of its conjugate too; their two zeros overpower that [pole](pole.md) and would make the product tend to zero. This proves nonvanishing for nonreal [Dirichlet characters](dirichlet-character.md) and is a useful complement to arguments for [real Dirichlet characters](real-dirichlet-character.md).
