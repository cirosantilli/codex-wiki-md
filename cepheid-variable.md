# Cepheid variable

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cepheid_variable)

A Cepheid variable is a pulsating star whose period is tightly related to its luminosity, allowing its distance to be inferred from its measured period and [apparent magnitude](apparent-magnitude.md).
