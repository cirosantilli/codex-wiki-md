# D1-brane

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

A [D1-brane](d1-brane.md) has one spatial dimension and couples to the two-form [Ramond–Ramond potential](ramond-ramond-potential.md). Its tension differs from the [fundamental string](fundamental-string.md) tension by a factor $g_s^{-1}$ at zero axion.
