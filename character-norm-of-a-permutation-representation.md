# Character norm of a permutation representation

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation representation](permutation-representation.md)

If $\pi$ is the character of $\mathbb C[X]$, then [Burnside lemma](burnside-s-lemma.md) applied to the diagonal action on $X\times X$ gives

$$
\langle\pi,\pi\rangle_G
=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}|X^g|^2
=|G\backslash(X\times X)|.
$$

For a transitive action $X\simeq G/H$, this also equals the number of [double cosets](double-coset.md) in $H\backslash G/H$.
