# Cell-cycle activation feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Positive feedback](positive-feedback.md)

[Cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md) is the reinforcement of [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) activation through [Cdc25](cdc25.md) and suppression of [Wee1](wee1.md). Its role is to help make transitions in the [cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md) decisive; the [cell-cycle hysteresis experiment](cell-cycle-hysteresis-experiment.md) tests the resulting states rather than inferring them solely from circuit arrows.
