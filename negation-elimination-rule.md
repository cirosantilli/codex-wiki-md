# Negation elimination rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

From a [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) and its [negation](negation.md), infer [logical falsity](logical-falsity.md). This identifies a contradiction inside a [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) derivation; a later [negation introduction rule](negation-introduction-rule.md) may discharge the assumption that led to it.
