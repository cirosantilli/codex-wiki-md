# Sub-step estimation with uniform dither

↑ **Parent:** [Dither](dither.md)

Let a constant input lie a fraction $f$ of the way between two consecutive quantizer levels separated by $\Delta$. With independent uniform additive [dither](dither.md) spanning one step and nearest-level rounding, the two output codes occur with [probabilities](probability.md) $1-f$ and $f$. Their mean equals the input and their sample-average [variance](variance-split.md) is the displayed expression. This simple model shows both the sub-step information and the remaining [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) cost.
