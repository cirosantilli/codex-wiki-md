# Root bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Bracketing method](bracketing-method.md)

A [root bracket](root-bracket.md) is a [closed interval](closed-real-interval.md) known to contain at least one [zero of a function](zero-of-a-function.md). For a real-valued [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on $[a,b]$, a strict change of sign between $f(a)$ and $f(b)$ certifies such an enclosure by the [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md). An endpoint with value zero already supplies a [zero of a function](zero-of-a-function.md). For example, $[1,2]$ is a [root bracket](root-bracket.md) for the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $f(x)=x^2-2$, since $f(1)=-1$ and $f(2)=2$. A [root bracket](root-bracket.md) need not contain a unique [zero of a function](zero-of-a-function.md).
