<h1 id="u-1-connection">U(1) connection</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (principal bundle)](connection-principal-bundle.md)

A connection on a principal circle bundle has local real [one-form](one-form.md) representatives $A$ transforming by $A\mapsto A+d\chi$. Its curvature is the globally defined [two-form](2-form.md) $F=dA$. In unit-charge normalization its [holonomy](holonomy.md) around a closed loop is $e^{i\oint A}$, and curvature periods are integer multiples of $2\pi$ on closed surfaces. A circulation representative is defined modulo $2\pi$ under large [gauge transformations](gauge-transformation.md).
