# Polynomial representation of the Heisenberg Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg Lie algebra](heisenberg-lie-algebra.md)

On $\mathbb C[x]$, the operators $d/dx$, multiplication by $x$, and the identity satisfy $[d/dx,x]=1$. Sending $a,b,c$ to these operators gives a faithful irreducible infinite-dimensional representation of the [Heisenberg Lie algebra](heisenberg-lie-algebra.md).
