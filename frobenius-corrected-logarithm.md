# Frobenius-corrected logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman power series for a CM formal group](coleman-power-series-for-a-cm-formal-group.md)

Assume $K_\mathfrak p=\mathbb Q_p$, $p>2$, $f(t)\in\mathbb Z_p[[t]]^\times$, and $[\pi]t\equiv t^p\pmod p$. Then the displayed ratio lies in $1+p\mathbb Z_p[[t]]$, so its logarithm divided by $p$ is integral. A formal-group isomorphism over the completed unramified coefficient ring transfers it to an integral [Amice transform](amice-transform.md). A norm relation makes the corresponding [p-adic measure](p-adic-measure.md) supported on units. Its $k$th moment has the Euler correction $1-\pi^k/p$.
