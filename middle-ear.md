# Middle ear

↑ **Parent:** [Hearing](hearing.md)

The tympanic membrane and ossicular coupling system that transfers airborne vibration toward the fluid-filled inner ear. Its mechanical transformation helps address an [acoustic impedance](acoustic-impedance.md) mismatch; it is not an ideal, lossless, frequency-independent amplifier.
