# Floating-catalyst chemical vapor deposition

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical vapor deposition](chemical-vapor-deposition.md)

A [chemical vapor deposition](chemical-vapor-deposition.md) route in which catalyst particles form or travel in the gas stream rather than remaining on a macroscopic support. For [carbon nanotubes](carbon-nanotube.md), particle nucleation, tube growth and aggregation can occur in one flowing reactor. Product withdrawal becomes possible when the growing network is sufficiently connected.
