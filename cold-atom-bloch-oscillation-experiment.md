# Cold-atom Bloch oscillation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch oscillation](bloch-oscillation.md)

In a 1996 experiment, ultracold cesium atoms were loaded into the lowest band of an optical standing wave made with $852\,\mathrm{nm}$ light. Chirping the frequency difference of its counterpropagating beams accelerated the lattice, producing a constant inertial force in its frame. Switching off the lattice after different evolution times allowed momentum-distribution measurements. The observed oscillations tested the periodic band velocity underlying a [Bloch oscillation](bloch-oscillation.md); the lattice period and imposed acceleration controlled the oscillation frequency.[Physical Review Letters 76, pp. 4508–4509: optical preparation, accelerating lattice, time-dependent momentum distribution](bloch-oscillations-of-atoms-in-an-optical-potential-by-maxime-dahan-and-others.md)
