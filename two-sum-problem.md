# Two-sum problem

↑ **Parent:** [Search algorithm](search-algorithm.md)

Find two distinct input positions whose values sum to a target, or establish that no such pair exists. Distinct values are not required: two equal values may form a pair when they occur at different positions. On a [sorted array](sorted-array.md), [distinct-index two-sum by suffix lower bounds](distinct-index-two-sum-by-suffix-lower-bounds.md) gives $O(N\log N)$ [running time](running-time.md), while a [two-pointer technique](two-pointer-technique.md) can give linear time.
