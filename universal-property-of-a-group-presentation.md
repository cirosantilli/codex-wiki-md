# Universal property of a group presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Group presentation](group-presentation.md)

A map from the generators $S$ of $\langle S\mid R\rangle$ to a group $G$ extends to a homomorphism exactly when every relator in $R$ evaluates to the identity. The extension is then unique.
