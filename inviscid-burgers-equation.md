# Inviscid Burgers equation

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inviscid_Burgers_equation)

The inviscid Burgers equation is the [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md)

$$
u_t+\left(\frac{u^2}{2}\right)_x=0.
$$

Its discontinuities obey the [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) $\sigma=(u_-+u_+)/2$. Restricting to [entropy solutions](entropy-solution.md) restores uniqueness for bounded initial data.
