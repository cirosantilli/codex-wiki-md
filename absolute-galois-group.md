# Absolute Galois group

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_Galois_group)

The absolute Galois group of a field $K$ is $G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{sep}}/K)$. It is a [profinite group](profinite-group.md), the inverse limit of the Galois groups of finite Galois extensions. Its continuous actions on discrete modules are the coefficients for [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md).
