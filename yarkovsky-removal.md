# Yarkovsky removal

↑ **Parent:** [Yarkovsky effect](yarkovsky-effect.md)

Drift driven by the [Yarkovsky effect](yarkovsky-effect.md) can carry a body out of a belt or into an unstable orbital region. A useful two-limit removal law is $t_y=BD+C/D$, with slow escape at both large and small diameters.
