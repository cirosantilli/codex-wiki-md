# Oscillation of a function on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Darboux sum](darboux-sum.md)

For a bounded real function, its oscillation on an interval is its full range width there. An [upper Darboux sum](upper-darboux-sum.md) minus a [lower Darboux sum](lower-darboux-sum.md) is the sum of these widths multiplied by their interval lengths. This gives the [Riemann integrability criterion](riemann-integrability-criterion.md) in terms of oscillation.
