# Free straight edge of an isotropic bending plate

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic thin-plate bending energy](isotropic-thin-plate-bending-energy.md)

For a plate occupying $x>0$, the boundary part of the [first variation](first-variation.md) of its [isotropic thin-plate bending energy](isotropic-thin-plate-bending-energy.md) is, up to an overall nonzero sign,

$$
D\int_{x=0}[\eta_x(w_{xx}+\nu w_{yy})-
\eta(w_{xxx}+(2-\nu)w_{xyy})],dy.
$$

At a free edge the variation and its normal derivative can be prescribed independently. Their coefficients must therefore vanish, giving the two displayed natural boundary conditions: zero bending moment and zero effective transverse shear. The mixed-derivative coefficient includes the derivative along the edge of the twisting moment; keeping only the normal derivative of $w_{xx}+\nu w_{yy}$ would miss that contribution.
