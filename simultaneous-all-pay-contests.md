# Simultaneous all-pay contests

↑ **Parent:** [Contest theory](contest-theory.md)

Players choose efforts in several [all-pay contests](all-pay-auction.md) at the same time. With additive [quasilinear utility](quasilinear-utility.md) and no shared effort budget, a player's expected payoff is the sum of its per-contest payoffs. An entry restriction couples the choices of contests even though the subsequent effort optimization is separable.
