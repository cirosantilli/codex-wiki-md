<h1 id="valiant-s-permanent-reduction">Valiant's permanent reduction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Counting complexity](counting-complexity.md)

Valiant's permanent reduction encodes satisfying assignments as [cycle covers](cycle-cover-of-a-directed-graph.md) so that their weighted sum is the [permanent of a matrix](permanent-mathematics.md). Local gadgets enforce variable consistency and clause satisfaction, while signed contributions cancel invalid covers.
