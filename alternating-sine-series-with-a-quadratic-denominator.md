# Alternating sine series with a quadratic denominator

↑ **Parent:** [Jordan's lemma](jordan-s-lemma.md)

For real $a\ne0$ and $|x|<\pi$, integrate $z\sin(xz)/((a^2+z^2)\sin\pi z)$ around a thin positively oriented strip containing the integers. Its integer [residues](residue.md) give the series. Closing the two strip sides outward instead gives minus the sum of the residues at $z=\pm ia$, each $\sinh(ax)/(2\sinh(a\pi))$. The factor $|x|<\pi$ ensures exponential decay on those closures. The bilateral series is interpreted by symmetric partial sums; at $a=0$ its continuous extension is $-x$ with the zero-index summand defined to be zero.
