# Future domain of dependence

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy surface](cauchy-surface.md)

The set $D^+(\Sigma)$ of events for which every past-inextendible [causal curve](causal-curve.md) meets $\Sigma$. This causal characterization permits an initial sign condition on a [null geodesic congruence](null-geodesic-congruence.md) to propagate throughout the regular region by integrating a focusing inequality from $\Sigma$.
