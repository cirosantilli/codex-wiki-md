# Stellar electron-scattering Eddington factor

↑ **Parent:** [Eddington luminosity](eddington-luminosity.md)

This factor is the ratio of acceleration due to [electron-scattering opacity](electron-scattering-opacity.md) to gravitational acceleration in a spherical luminous star. The remaining effective gravity is approximately $GM(1-\Gamma_e)/R^2$ when other radiative forces are neglected. Being below the electron-scattering limit does not preclude a line-driven [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md), because additional [opacity](opacity.md) can provide more acceleration.
