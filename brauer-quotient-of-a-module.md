# Brauer quotient of a module

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer morphism](brauer-morphism.md)

For a $p$-subgroup $P$, the Brauer quotient removes all fixed vectors produced by proper-subgroup [relative traces](relative-trace.md). On a [permutation module](permutation-module.md) it has a basis of the permutation-basis elements fixed by $P$: nonsingleton orbit sums are traces and disappear. The construction commutes with direct sums and idempotent projections. For an indecomposable [trivial source module](trivial-source-module.md), it is nonzero exactly when a vertex contains a conjugate of $P$.
