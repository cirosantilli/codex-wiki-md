# Shaken harmonic trap

↑ **Parent:** [Displaced quantum harmonic oscillator](displaced-quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)

Displacing a one-dimensional [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) produces a linear force term and a scalar shift relative to the unshifted [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). The scalar adds only a common phase. The linear term couples [number states](number-state.md) separated by one excitation. Arbitrary linear driving of the [ground state](ground-state.md) creates a [coherent state](coherent-state.md); a first-order expansion of that state contains only the vacuum and first excited state.[§4.4.2, printed pp.132–133: a forcing term creates coherent states](topics-in-quantum-mechanics-by-david-tong.md)
