# Spectral radius criterion for matrix powers

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral radius](spectral-radius.md)

For a finite square matrix, powers tend to zero exactly when every [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) has modulus less than one. In a complex [Jordan normal form](jordan-normal-form.md), the entries of a block power are sums of factors $\binom{k}{j}\lambda^{k-j}$, which tend to zero when $|\lambda|<1$; a zero-eigenvalue block becomes zero after finitely many powers. Conversely, an eigenvector gives $G^kv=\lambda^kv$, so convergence to zero excludes $|\lambda|\geq1$. This criterion underlies the convergence theorem for a [stationary iterative method for a linear system](stationary-iterative-method-for-a-linear-system.md).
