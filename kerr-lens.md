# Kerr lens

↑ **Parent:** [Optical Kerr effect](optical-kerr-effect.md)

A beam's transverse [intensity](intensity-physics.md) changes its own [refractive index](refractive-index.md). For positive nonlinear index and a bright centre, the accumulated phase has the curvature of a converging lens. For $I(r)=I_0e^{-2r^2/w^2}$ over a short length $L$, the in-medium thin-lens approximation gives $1/f=4n_2I_0L/(n_0w^2)$. Negative $n_2$ instead produces [self-defocusing](self-defocusing.md).
