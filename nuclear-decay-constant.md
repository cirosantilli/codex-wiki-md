# Nuclear decay constant

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md)

In the constant-rate [mathematical model](mathematical-model.md), an undecayed [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) has conditional probability

$$
\Pr(T\le t+\Delta t\mid T>t)=1-e^{-\lambda\Delta t}
=\lambda\Delta t+O((\lambda\Delta t)^2)
$$

of decaying during the next positive interval $\Delta t$. The [nuclear decay constant](nuclear-decay-constant.md) has units of inverse [time](time-in-physics.md). Its value describes a rate; it does not specify a scheduled death time for one [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md). The conditional probability is independent of the age $t$, the [memorylessness of the exponential distribution](memorylessness-of-the-exponential-distribution.md).
