# William Thomas Tutte

↑ **Parent:** [List of mathematicians](list-of-mathematicians.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._T._Tutte)

A [mathematician](mathematician.md) whose work includes [graph theory](graph-theory-split.md) and [matroids](matroid.md); [Tutte's theorem on perfect matchings](tutte-s-theorem-on-perfect-matchings.md) is one of his fundamental results.
