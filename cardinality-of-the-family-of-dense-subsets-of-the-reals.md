# Cardinality of the family of dense subsets of the reals

↑ **Parent:** [Dense set](dense-set.md)

There are $2^{\mathfrak c}$ dense subsets of $\mathbb R$, where $\mathfrak c=|\mathbb R|$. For a fixed [Cantor set](cantor-set.md) $C$ of size $\mathfrak c$, its complement $D$ is dense, and $A\mapsto D\cup A$ injects $\mathcal P(C)$ into the family of dense subsets. The reverse injection is the inclusion into $\mathcal P(\mathbb R)$. The [Cantor-Schröder-Bernstein theorem](cantor-schroder-bernstein-theorem.md) gives equality.
