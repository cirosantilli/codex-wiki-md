# Unit disk

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_disk)

The open unit disk in the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-geometry-split.md) is

$$
\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:x^2+y^2<1\}.
$$
