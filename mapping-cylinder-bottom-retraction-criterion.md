# Mapping-cylinder bottom retraction criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping cylinder](mapping-cylinder.md)

Use the [mapping cylinder](mapping-cylinder.md) convention $(x,1)\sim f(x)$ for $f:X\to Y$. Its bottom copy of $X$ is a [retract](retract.md) precisely when $f$ has a left [homotopy inverse](homotopy-inverse.md) $g:Y\to X$. A [retraction](retraction.md) restricted to $Y$ supplies $g$, and its values along the cylinder supply the [homotopy](homotopy-split.md) from $\operatorname{id}_X$ to $gf$. Conversely use that [homotopy](homotopy-split.md) on the cylinder and $g$ on $Y$; agreement at the attached end makes the map descend by the [universal property of the quotient topology](universal-property-of-the-quotient-topology.md).
