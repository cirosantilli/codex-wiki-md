# Volume of a solid with affine thickness

↑ **Parent:** [Volume integral](volume-integral.md)

Let a bounded planar region $D$ have positive [area](surface-area.md) $A$ and [centroid](centroid.md) $\overline{\mathbf x}=A^{-1}\int_D\mathbf x\,dA$. If a solid has nonnegative thickness $h(\mathbf x)=c+\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf x$ above $D$, its [volume integral](volume-integral.md) gives

$$
V=\int_D h(\mathbf x)\,dA=A\left(c+\mathbf v\cdot\overline{\mathbf x}\right)=A\,h(\overline{\mathbf x}).
$$

Thus the average of an [affine function](affine-function.md) over a region is its value at the [centroid](centroid.md). The proof is the [linearity of integration](linearity-of-integration.md). For a [disk](disk-mathematics.md) the [centroid](centroid.md) is its centre, making the formula particularly useful for a cylindrical bore cut through a solid whose width varies linearly with height.
