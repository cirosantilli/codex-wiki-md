# Degenerate semiconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degenerate_semiconductor)

A [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) whose mobile [charge carrier](charge-carrier.md) population requires [Fermi-Dirac statistics](fermi-dirac-statistics.md) rather than a dilute [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md). In a strongly degenerate [n-type semiconductor](n-type-semiconductor.md), the [Fermi level](fermi-level.md) lies inside the [conduction band](conduction-band.md); in a strongly degenerate [p-type semiconductor](p-type-semiconductor.md), it lies inside the [valence band](valence-band.md). The occupied and empty [quantum states](quantum-state.md) on opposite sides of a [tunnel diode](tunnel-diode.md) can then overlap in [energy](energy-split.md). Heavy [semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md) both changes these occupations and narrows the [depletion region](depletion-region.md); a narrow barrier and accessible final [quantum states](quantum-state.md) serve different roles.
