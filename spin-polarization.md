# Spin polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_polarization)

For a spin-one-half ensemble, [spin polarization](spin-polarization.md) measures unequal occupation or coherent alignment of [spin](spin.md) states. A spin-dependent energy splitting does not alone determine the ensemble's response: occupation, [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [spin relaxation](spin-relaxation.md) matter. A [spin polarization](spin-polarization.md) is distinct from a net change of [charge density](charge-density.md) and from the optical [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md).
