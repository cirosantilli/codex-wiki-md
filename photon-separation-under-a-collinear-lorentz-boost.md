# Photon separation under a collinear Lorentz boost

↑ **Parent:** [Relativity of simultaneity](relativity-of-simultaneity.md)

For two [photons](photon.md) emitted simultaneously a distance $d$ apart in one frame and moving in the same positive direction, the world-line intercept $x-ct$ transforms to $\gamma(1+v/c)(x-ct)$. Their equal-time separation in the boosted frame is therefore the displayed constant $d'$. It is different from length contraction of the emitters, because the emission events are not simultaneous after the boost.
