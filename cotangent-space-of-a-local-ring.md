# Cotangent space of a local ring

↑ **Parent:** [Local ring](local-ring.md)

For a [local ring](local-ring.md) $(R,\mathfrak m)$ with [residue field](residue-field.md) $\kappa=R/\mathfrak m$, its cotangent space is the $\kappa$-[vector space](vector-space-split.md) $\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$. In a [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md), its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is the minimal number of generators of $\mathfrak m$, by the [Nakayama lemma](nakayama-lemma.md). Equality of this [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) with the [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) defines a [regular local ring](regular-local-ring.md).
