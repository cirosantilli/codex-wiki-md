# Tidal water loading of a spring balance

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational field of an infinite plane sheet](gravitational-field-of-an-infinite-plane-sheet.md)

A [mass](mass.md) above a sufficiently wide estuary experiences additional downward [gravity](gravity.md) when extra water is present. Approximating the tide as a plane slab of [density](density.md) $\rho$ and added thickness $h$ gives $\Delta g=2\pi G\rho h$. A linear [spring](spring.md) with initial extension $x_0=mg/k$ changes by $\Delta x=(m/k)\Delta g=x_0\Delta g/g$. This isolates direct water attraction; it omits finite shoreline geometry, buoyancy, structural motion and terrestrial tides.
