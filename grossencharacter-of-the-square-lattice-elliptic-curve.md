<h1 id="grossencharacter-of-the-square-lattice-elliptic-curve">Grössencharacter of the square-lattice elliptic curve</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke character of a CM elliptic curve](hecke-character-of-a-cm-elliptic-curve.md)

For $E:y^2=x^3-x$ over $\mathbb Q(i)$, the [Hecke character](hecke-character.md) has conductor $(1+i)^3$ and ideal infinity type $(1,0)$. Its value on an ideal prime to $2$ is its unique [primary Gaussian integer](primary-gaussian-integer.md) generator. Rational $(1+i)^3$-torsion fixes the unit ambiguity of the Frobenius value. At a split prime $\mathfrak p$, its value reduces to the degree-$p$ [Frobenius isogeny](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md); at an inert rational prime $p$, its value is $-p$, reducing to the $p^2$-power [Frobenius isogeny](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md).
