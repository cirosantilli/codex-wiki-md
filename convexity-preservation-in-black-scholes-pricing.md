# Convexity preservation in Black-Scholes pricing

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes parameter sensitivities](black-scholes-parameter-sensitivities.md)

In the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md) the terminal [stock](stock.md) price conditional on current price $x$ is $xM$ for a positive [lognormal](log-normal-distribution.md) multiplier $M$. Differentiation under the [risk-neutral pricing](risk-neutral-pricing.md) expectation gives the displayed [option gamma](option-gamma.md). Its sign follows that of $f''$, so [convexity](convex-function.md) and [concavity](concave-function.md) of the terminal payoff are preserved. An analogous first-derivative identity preserves monotonicity. With appropriate growth conditions, [Gaussian integration by parts](stein-s-lemma-probability.md) also gives $V_\sigma=\sigma(T-t)x^2V_{xx}$, fixing the sign of [option vega](option-vega.md) for these payoff classes.
