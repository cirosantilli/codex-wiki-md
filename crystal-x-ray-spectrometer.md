# Crystal X-ray spectrometer

↑ **Parent:** [Siegbahn precision X-ray wavelength spectroscopy](siegbahn-precision-x-ray-wavelength-spectroscopy.md)

A [crystal X-ray spectrometer](crystal-x-ray-spectrometer.md) selects a narrow [wavelength](wavelength.md) interval by [diffraction](diffraction.md) at known [interplanar spacing](interplanar-spacing.md) $d$ and [Bragg angle](bragg-angle.md) $\theta$. For fixed [diffraction order](diffraction-order.md) $n$, a known $d$ lets an angular measurement determine $\lambda=2d\sin\theta/n$. This reverses the [crystallography](crystallography.md) problem of finding $d$ from a known [wavelength](wavelength.md).

Siegbahn's laboratory developed [X-ray tubes](x-ray-tube.md) and evacuated spectrographs for longer [wavelengths](wavelength.md). Rotating the [crystal](crystal.md) selected reflections; [photographic plates](photographic-plate.md) recorded positions. Opposite-side exposures measured angles on both sides of the direct beam. Later tube instruments mounted a beam-defining slit opposite the plate holder. A separate double instrument compared two reflected beams with two [ionization chambers](ionization-chamber.md). These were different instruments, not one universal assembly.[Hugo Atterling, Manne Siegbahn: Development of precision spectroscopy; Work at Uppsala 1923–1927](manne-siegbahn-his-research-by-hugo-atterling.md)

The Nobel biography describes constant-[temperature](temperature-split.md) spectrographs and a precision angular scale read by diametrically opposed microscopes.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; Biographical, paragraph beginning As a measure of the high precision; inspected collected volume pp. 90–91](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/manne-siegbahn-biographical.md) [Temperature](temperature-split.md) control matters because [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md) changes $d$; an accurately read angle cannot compensate for an unknown [interplanar spacing](interplanar-spacing.md).

![](siegbahn-crystal-spectrometer-functions.png)

**[Figure 7](#image-siegbahn-crystal-spectrometer-functions). Functions of a crystal X-ray spectrometer**. Original functional schematic, not a reconstruction of a particular Siegbahn instrument. Two slits illustrate [collimation](collimation.md); the selected incident direction, rotatable [crystal](crystal.md) and movable readout define the geometry. The [Bragg angle](bragg-angle.md) $\theta$ is measured from the [lattice planes](lattice-plane.md); the beam deflection is $2\theta$. The [photographic plate](photographic-plate.md) and scanning [ionization chamber](ionization-chamber.md) are alternative readouts. Component sizes, angles and positions have no historical scale.

The schematic separates preparation, selection and readout. An [X-ray tube](x-ray-tube.md) target supplies [Characteristic X-rays](characteristic-x-ray.md) with a possible [bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md) background. A [collimator](collimator.md) limits incident directions; evacuation reduces air [X-ray attenuation](x-ray-attenuation.md). Rotating the [crystal](crystal.md) brings different [wavelengths](wavelength.md) into reflection. A [photographic plate](photographic-plate.md) stores line positions over an exposure, whereas an [ionization chamber](ionization-chamber.md) can measure collected [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) at successive angles. Neither readout alone establishes absolute [wavelength](wavelength.md): an angular zero, angular-scale calibration, [diffraction order](diffraction-order.md), $d$ and any required [refraction](refraction.md) correction enter the inference. Narrower slits reduce accepted angular spread but also reduce signal; a weak line can become harder to locate despite a narrower instrumental response.

A control reported in Siegbahn's lecture photographed a line system with copper-sulphate and calcium-sulphate [crystals](crystal.md). Irregular relative shifts around copper's characteristic [frequency](frequency.md) supported [anomalous dispersion](anomalous-dispersion.md); the arrangement excluded differing absorption across that [frequency](frequency.md) as the explanation.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; Printed pp. 88–89, final experiment](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/the-x-ray-spectra-and-the-structure-of-the-atoms-by-karl-manne-georg-siegbahn.md) A finer angular scale alone cannot remove this physical [refraction](refraction.md) correction.
