# Phase-plane construction of a Fisher travelling front

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling wave](travelling-wave.md)

For $u_t=u_{xx}+u(k-u)$ with $k>0$, a decreasing [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) connecting $k$ to $0$ satisfies $w'=p$, $p'=-cp-w(k-w)$. If $c\geq2\sqrt{k}$, choose $a>0$ with $a(c-a)\geq k$. The triangle $0\leq w\leq k$, $-aw\leq p\leq0$ is forward invariant: on its sloping side, $(p+aw)'=w[a(c-a)-k+w]\geq0$. The descending [unstable manifold](unstable-manifold.md) of the [saddle equilibrium](saddle-equilibrium.md) $(k,0)$ stays in this triangle and tends to $(0,0)$, producing a positive [heteroclinic orbit](heteroclinic-orbit.md). Below the threshold the origin is a [stable spiral](stable-spiral.md) for positive $c$, forcing sign changes; at $c=0$ conserved energy also excludes the connection.
