# Formally real field

↑ **Parent:** [Field](field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formally_real_field)

A [field](field.md) is formally real when $-1$ is not a finite sum of squares. The [Artin-Schreier ordering criterion](artin-schreier-ordering-criterion.md) says this is equivalent to the existence of an ordering compatible with the field operations. Such a field has characteristic zero and embeds into a [real closed field](real-closed-field.md).
