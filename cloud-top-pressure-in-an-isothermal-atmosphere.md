# Cloud-top pressure in an isothermal atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet cloud deck](exoplanet-cloud-deck.md)

With constant gravity and [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md), [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $P_c=P_s\exp(-H_c/h)$, where $h$ is the [atmospheric scale height](atmospheric-scale-height.md). A flat [exoplanet transmission spectrum](exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md) alone does not determine $H_c$ or a unique cloud-top [pressure](pressure.md).
