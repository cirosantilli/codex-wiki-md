# Morphism of locally ringed spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Locally ringed space](locally-ringed-space.md)

A morphism is a continuous map $f:X\to Y$ together with a homomorphism $\mathcal O_Y\to f_*\mathcal O_X$ of [sheaves of rings](sheaf-of-rings.md) whose induced map on every [stalk](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) is a [local homomorphism](local-homomorphism-of-local-rings.md). Applied to [schemes](scheme.md), this is exactly a [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md).
