# Stagnant-lid planet

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary science](planetary-science-split.md)

A planet whose outer mechanically strong shell does not participate continuously in [mantle convection](mantle-convection.md) as a connected system of independently moving [tectonic plates](tectonic-plate.md).
