# Anyon fusion space

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon fusion rule](anyon-fusion-rule.md)

The [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) of ways to combine the specified [anyon](anyon.md) charges to the specified total charge. For two inputs its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is the [anyon fusion multiplicity](anyon-fusion-multiplicity.md) $N^c_{ab}$. Repeated [anyon fusion](anyon-fusion-rule.md) is described by an [anyon fusion tree](anyon-fusion-tree.md).
