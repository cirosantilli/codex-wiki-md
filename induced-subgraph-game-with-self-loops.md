# Induced subgraph game with self-loops

↑ **Parent:** [Induced subgraph game](induced-subgraph-game.md)

An [induced subgraph game](induced-subgraph-game.md) extended by one [self-loop](loop-graph-theory.md) weight $\ell_i$ per player. Each loop is counted once, so $v(\{i\})=\ell_i$ and

$$
w_{ij}=v(\{i,j\})-v(\{i\})-v(\{j\}).
$$

Singleton and pair [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) values determine all weights; larger [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) values must then agree with their sums. Signed weights are allowed. For example, two loops of weight $1$ joined by an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) of weight $-1$ represent the [simple cooperative game](simple-cooperative-game.md) in which any [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) meeting those two players wins.
