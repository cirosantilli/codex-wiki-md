# Triweight kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel for density estimation](kernel-for-density-estimation.md)

The triweight kernel is a compactly supported symmetric probability density with variance $1/9$. Its value and first two derivatives vanish at the support endpoints. Its integrated squared second derivative is $35$, and it minimizes that curvature among twice continuously differentiable densities with zero mean and the same variance, by the [triweight density minimizes integrated squared curvature](triweight-density-minimizes-integrated-squared-curvature.md) inequality.
