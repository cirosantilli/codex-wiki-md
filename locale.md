# Locale

↑ **Parent:** [Complete Heyting algebra](complete-heyting-algebra.md)

A locale is an object of the opposite of the [category](category-split.md) of [frames](complete-heyting-algebra.md). A map $f:X\to Y$ is specified by a [frame homomorphism](frame-homomorphism.md) $f^*:\mathcal O(Y)\to\mathcal O(X)$. This makes open-set algebra primary and allows spaces without enough ordinary points. A [point of a frame](point-of-a-frame.md) is the inverse-image map of a point of a locale.
