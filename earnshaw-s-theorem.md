<h1 id="earnshaw-s-theorem">Earnshaw's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earnshaw's_theorem)

For a point [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) in a charge-free neighbourhood, a static [electric potential](electric-potential.md) obeys [Laplace's equation](laplace-equation.md). The [maximum principle for harmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md) excludes a strict interior minimum or maximum of that [electric potential](electric-potential.md), hence a strict minimum of the charge's [potential energy](potential-energy.md). A static [electric field](electric-field-split.md) alone therefore cannot provide stable three-dimensional confinement under these assumptions.
