# Stabilizer as a hidden subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Hidden subgroup problem](hidden-subgroup-problem.md)

For a [group action](group-action.md) $F:G\times X\to X$ and fixed $x$, the orbit map $f_x(g)=F(g,x)$ hides the [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) $G_x$: $f_x(g)=f_x(h)$ exactly when $h^{-1}g\in G_x$, equivalently when $g$ and $h$ lie in the same left coset of $G_x$.
