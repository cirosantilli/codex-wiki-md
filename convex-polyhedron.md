# Convex polyhedron

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_polyhedron)

A [polyhedron](polyhedron.md) that is also a [convex set](convex-set-split.md). A bounded full-dimensional [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of finitely many [points](point-geometry.md) is a [convex polyhedron](convex-polyhedron.md); each face lies in a supporting [plane](plane.md), with the whole solid in its inward [half-space](half-space.md).
