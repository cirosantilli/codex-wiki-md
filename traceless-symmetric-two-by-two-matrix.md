# Traceless symmetric two-by-two matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md)

For real $a,b$, the displayed [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) satisfies $M^2=(a^2+b^2)I$. If $s=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}>0$, choose $\theta$ with $\cos2\theta=a/s$ and $\sin2\theta=b/s$. The [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) $(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$ and $(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta)$ are [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) and have [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $s$ and $-s$. The half-angle description works even when $b=0$, avoiding an artificial division by an off-diagonal entry. If $s=0$, the matrix is zero and every direction is an eigenvector. The normalized matrix $M/s$ is reflection in the axis at angle $\theta$.
