↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a set $A$, its [definable power set](../../../../../definable-power-set-split.md) consists of the subsets of $A$ definable by a [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) over $(A,\in)$ using finitely many parameters from $A$. The quantifiers range over $A$; this is generally much smaller than its full [power set](../../../../../power-set.md).

The [constructible universe](../../../../../constructible-universe.md) is defined by [transfinite recursion](../../../../../transfinite-recursion.md):

$$
\boxed{L_0=\varnothing,\qquad L_{\alpha+1}=\operatorname{Def}(L_\alpha),\qquad
L_\lambda=\bigcup_{\alpha<\lambda}L_\alpha\quad(\lambda\text{ a limit ordinal}),\qquad
L=\bigcup_{\alpha\in\mathrm{On}}L_\alpha.}
$$

The last [union](../../../../../set-union.md) denotes a definable [proper class](../../../../../proper-class.md), not a set-sized [union](../../../../../set-union.md) over a set of all [ordinals](../../../../../ordinal-split.md). At a successor stage, only definitions over the previously constructed stage are permitted.
