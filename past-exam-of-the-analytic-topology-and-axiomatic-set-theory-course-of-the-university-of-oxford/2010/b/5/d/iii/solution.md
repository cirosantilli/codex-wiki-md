<h1 id="b/5/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $\kappa=\aleph_\omega$, monotonicity of [cardinal exponentiation](../../../../../../cardinal-exponentiation.md) gives

$$
2^\kappa\leq\aleph_0^\kappa\leq(2^{\aleph_0})^\kappa
=2^{\aleph_0\cdot\kappa}=2^\kappa.
$$

The middle equality uses the [currying law for cardinal exponentiation](../../../../../../currying-law-for-cardinal-exponentiation.md), and the final one uses $\aleph_0\cdot\kappa=\kappa$. Hence $\aleph_0^\kappa=2^\kappa$, even before assuming GCH. Applying the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\aleph_0^{\aleph_\omega}=\aleph_{\omega+1}.}
$$
