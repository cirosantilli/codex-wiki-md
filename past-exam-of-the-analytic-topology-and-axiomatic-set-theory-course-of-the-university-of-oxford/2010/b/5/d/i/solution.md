<h1 id="b/5/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Constant functions give the lower bound $\aleph_2\leq\aleph_2^{\aleph_0}$. By the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md), $\aleph_2=2^{\aleph_1}$. The [currying law for cardinal exponentiation](../../../../../../currying-law-for-cardinal-exponentiation.md) and infinite [cardinal arithmetic](../../../../../../cardinal-arithmetic.md) therefore give

$$
\aleph_2^{\aleph_0}=(2^{\aleph_1})^{\aleph_0}
=2^{\aleph_1\cdot\aleph_0}=2^{\aleph_1}=\aleph_2.
$$

Hence $\boxed{\aleph_2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_2}$.
