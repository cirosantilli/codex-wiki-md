↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [cardinal sum](../../../../../cardinal-sum.md) is the [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of a disjoint [union](../../../../../set-union.md), and the [cardinal product](../../../../../cardinal-product.md) is the [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of the set of choice functions:

$$
\boxed{\sum_{\alpha<\beta}\kappa_\alpha=
\left|\{(\alpha,\xi):\alpha<\beta,\ \xi<\kappa_\alpha\}\right|,\qquad
\prod_{\alpha<\beta}\kappa_\alpha=
\left|\{f:\operatorname{dom}f=\beta,\ f(\alpha)<\kappa_\alpha\}\right|.}
$$

Here the cardinals are represented by [initial ordinals](../../../../../initial-ordinal.md). Tagging by $\alpha$ makes the summands disjoint even when their underlying sets overlap. For an empty index set the sum is zero and the product is one, because there is exactly one [empty function](../../../../../empty-function.md). If any factor in a nonempty product is zero, that product is empty.
