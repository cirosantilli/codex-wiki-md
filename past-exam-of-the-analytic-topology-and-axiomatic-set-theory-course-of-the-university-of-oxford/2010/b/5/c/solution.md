<h1 id="b/5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [aleph numbers](../../../../../aleph-number.md) enumerate the infinite [initial ordinals](../../../../../initial-ordinal.md) by [transfinite recursion](../../../../../transfinite-recursion.md):

$$
\boxed{\aleph_0=\omega,\qquad\aleph_{\alpha+1}=(\aleph_\alpha)^+,\qquad
\aleph_\lambda=\sup_{\alpha<\lambda}\aleph_\alpha\quad(\lambda\text{ a nonzero limit ordinal}).}
$$

Here $\kappa^+$ is the least cardinal strictly larger than $\kappa$. The [Generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) is

$$
\boxed{2^\kappa=\kappa^+\quad\text{for every infinite cardinal }\kappa,}
$$

equivalently $2^{\aleph_\alpha}=\aleph_{\alpha+1}$ for every [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) $\alpha$ in ZFC.
