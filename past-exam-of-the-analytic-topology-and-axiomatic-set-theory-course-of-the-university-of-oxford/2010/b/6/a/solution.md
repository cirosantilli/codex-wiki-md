The [membership induction theorem](../../../../../membership-induction-theorem.md) is the following schema, for every formula $\varphi(x)$ with any fixed set parameters:

$$
\boxed{\left[\forall x\left((\forall y\in x\,\varphi(y))\to\varphi(x)\right)\right]
\ \Longrightarrow\ \forall x\,\varphi(x).}
$$

Its hypothesis says the property is progressive under membership. In ZF it follows from the [Axiom of foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md): if a counterexample $x$ exists, take the nonempty set of counterexamples in the [transitive closure](../../../../../transitive-closure.md) $\operatorname{TC}(\{x\})$, which contains $x$. A membership-minimal counterexample supplied by the [Axiom of foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) has no counterexample among its elements, contradicting progressivity. The transitive [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) ensures that all elements relevant to this argument remain in the set being considered.
