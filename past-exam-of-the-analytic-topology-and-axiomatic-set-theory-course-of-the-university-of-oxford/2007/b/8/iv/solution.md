<h1 id="b/8/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use the [constructible universe theorem](../../../../../constructible-universe-theorem.md) that $L$ satisfies $ZFC$ and [GCH](../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md). In particular, internally it has a [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md)

$$
b:\omega_1^L\longrightarrow\mathcal P(\omega)^L.
$$

This [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) belongs to $L$ and is also an actual [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) in the ambient universe, because its domain, range and graph are transitive-model sets with the same membership relation.

We must compare the [ordinals](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) rather than assume [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) is absolute. Every [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) below $\omega_1^L$ is [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) in $L$, and its witnessing enumeration belongs to the ambient universe too. Thus every such [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) is [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) externally, proving $\omega_1^L\leq\omega_1$. Consequently the ambient [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of the constructible reals is at most $\aleph_1$.

If every subset of $\omega$ were constructible, the displayed [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) would enumerate the full ambient [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) using at most $\aleph_1$ [points](../../../../../point-geometry.md). This contradicts the assumption $2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\exists X\subseteq\omega\quad X\notin L.}
$$

The argument permits $\omega_1^L$ to be strictly smaller than the ambient $\omega_1$, exactly the [cardinal](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md)-absoluteness issue highlighted in the source.
