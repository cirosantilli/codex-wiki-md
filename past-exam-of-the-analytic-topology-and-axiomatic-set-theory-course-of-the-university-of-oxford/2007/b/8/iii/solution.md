<h1 id="b/8/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Fix $a\in L$. In the ambient universe, [Separation](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md) forms the set

$$
S=\{x\in\mathcal P(a):x\in L\}.
$$

It is a set of constructible sets, so the previous part gives an [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) bound on all its members. Increase the bound to a level $L_\beta$ also containing $a$. [Transitivity](../../../../../transitive-relation.md) ensures $a\subseteq L_\beta$. Then

$$
S=\{x\in L_\beta:x\subseteq a\}.
$$

The inclusion test is absolute for the transitive level $L_\beta$, since all members of $x$ and $a$ lie there. The right side is consequently a subset definable over $L_\beta$ with parameter $a$, so

$$
\boxed{S\in\operatorname{Def}(L_\beta)=L_{\beta+1}\subseteq L.}
$$

For $x\in L$, transitivity of the [constructible universe](../../../../../constructible-universe.md) makes internal and ambient subsethood of $a$ agree. Hence $L$ satisfies that $S$ consists exactly of all its subsets of $a$. This is the [Power set](../../../../../power-set.md) Axiom in $\langle L,\in\rangle$. It is not the assertion that every ambient subset of $a$ belongs to $L$.
