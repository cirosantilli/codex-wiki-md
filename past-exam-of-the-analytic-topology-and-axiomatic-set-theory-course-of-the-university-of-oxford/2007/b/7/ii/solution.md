<h1 id="b/7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[König theorem for cardinal numbers](../../../../../konig-s-theorem-set-theory.md) says that for families of [cardinals](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md) $\kappa_i<\lambda_i$ indexed by a set $I$,

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i\in I}\kappa_i<\prod_{i\in I}\lambda_i.}
$$

The sum is the [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of the disjoint union of sets $A_i$ of sizes $\kappa_i$, and the product is the [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of $\prod_iB_i$ for sets of sizes $\lambda_i$.

Consider any function $F:\bigsqcup_iA_i\to\prod_iB_i$. For each $i$, the set of $i$th coordinates of images of [points](../../../../../point-geometry.md) from the $i$th block has [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) at most $\kappa_i<\lambda_i$. Choose $b_i\in B_i$ outside it. Then $b=(b_i)_i$ is not the image of any [point](../../../../../point-geometry.md): a [point](../../../../../point-geometry.md) from block $i$ has the wrong $i$th coordinate. Thus there is no [surjection](../../../../../surjective-function.md) from the sum set onto the product set. [Cardinal](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md) comparability in $ZFC$ makes the inequality strict in the indicated direction. For the empty index set the statement is $0<1$, also true. This is the diagonal argument underlying the inequality.
