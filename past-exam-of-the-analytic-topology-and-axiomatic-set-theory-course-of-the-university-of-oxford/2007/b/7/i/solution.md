A [cardinal number](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md) is an [initial ordinal](../../../../../initial-ordinal.md): an [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) not in [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) with any smaller [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md). In $ZFC$ every set has the [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of a unique such [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md).

For an infinite [cardinal](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md) $\kappa$, its [cofinality](../../../../../cofinality.md) is the least [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of an unbounded subset of the [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) $\kappa$. Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)=\min\{\theta:\exists\text{ a cofinal map }f:\theta\to\kappa\}.}
$$

A subset is unbounded when no [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) below $\kappa$ bounds all its elements. A [cofinal](../../../../../cofinal-subset.md) sequence can be taken increasing, and $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)$ is an infinite [regular cardinal](../../../../../regular-cardinal.md).
