↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The preceding inclusion is exactly $V_\alpha\subseteq\mathcal P(V_\alpha)$. Therefore every $x\in V_\alpha$ is a subset of $V_\alpha$. Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{y\in x\in V_\alpha\Longrightarrow y\in V_\alpha,}
$$

which says that $V_\alpha$ is a [transitive set](../../../../../../transitive-set.md). This uses the [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) definition and the proved inclusion, without any ambient use of [Foundation](../../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md).
