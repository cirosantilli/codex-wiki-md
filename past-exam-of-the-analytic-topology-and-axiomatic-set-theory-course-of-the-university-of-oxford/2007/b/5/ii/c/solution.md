<h1 id="b/5/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

We prove the stronger [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) identity

$$
V_\alpha\cap\operatorname{Ord}=\alpha
$$

by [transfinite induction](../../../../../../transfinite-induction.md). It holds at zero. At a successor stage an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) $\gamma$ belongs to $\mathcal P(V_\beta)$ exactly when every $\delta<\gamma$ belongs to $V_\beta$. By induction this means every $\delta<\gamma$ is less than $\beta$, equivalently $\gamma\leq\beta$. Thus the [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) in $V_{\beta+1}$ are precisely those less than $\beta+1$. At a limit the identity follows by taking unions.

Every $\beta<\alpha$ consequently belongs to $V_\alpha$, so $\alpha\subseteq V_\alpha$ and $\alpha\in\mathcal P(V_\alpha)=V_{\alpha+1}$. But $\alpha\notin V_\alpha$, since an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) is not less than itself. Hence

$$
\boxed{\alpha\in V_{\alpha+1}\setminus V_\alpha.}
$$

The only well-foundedness used is that of the [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) order, available independently of [Foundation](../../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) for arbitrary ambient sets.
