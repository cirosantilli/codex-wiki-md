<h1 id="b/5/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [transfinite induction](../../../../../../transfinite-induction.md). The inclusion at zero is immediate. If $V_\beta\subseteq V_{\beta+1}$, monotonicity of the [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) gives

$$
V_{\beta+1}=\mathcal P(V_\beta)\subseteq\mathcal P(V_{\beta+1})=V_{\beta+2}.
$$

If $\alpha$ is a limit and $x\in V_\alpha$, choose $\beta<\alpha$ with $x\in V_\beta$. The inductive inclusion $V_\beta\subseteq\mathcal P(V_\beta)$ means that $x\subseteq V_\beta\subseteq V_\alpha$. Thus $x\in\mathcal P(V_\alpha)=V_{\alpha+1}$. This proves **$V_\alpha\subseteq V_{\alpha+1}$ for every [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md)**. These successive inclusions and the union definition also give $V_\beta\subseteq V_\alpha$ whenever $\beta\leq\alpha$.
