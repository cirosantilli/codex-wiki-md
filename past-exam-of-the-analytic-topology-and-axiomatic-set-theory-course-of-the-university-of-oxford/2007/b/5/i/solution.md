Here $ZF^*$ denotes [ZF without foundation](../../../../../zf-without-foundation.md). [Transfinite recursion](../../../../../transfinite-recursion.md) on [ordinals](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) defines the [cumulative hierarchy](../../../../../cumulative-hierarchy.md) by

$$
\boxed{V_0=\varnothing,\qquad V_{\alpha+1}=\mathcal P(V_\alpha),\qquad V_\lambda=\bigcup_{\beta<\lambda}V_\beta\quad(\lambda\text{ a nonzero limit}).}
$$

The associated class is $V=\{x:\exists\alpha\in\operatorname{Ord}\ (x\in V_\alpha)\}$. Each level is a set, while $V$ is a definable class. Without [Foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) it need not contain every ambient set; it consists of the sets built by this [well-founded](../../../../../well-founded-relation.md) hierarchy.
