<h1 id="a/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Hausdorff compactification](../../../../../hausdorff-compactification.md) of $X$ is a pair $(h,Y)$ in which $Y$ is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) and $h:X\to Y$ is a [topological embedding](../../../../../topological-embedding.md) with [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) image.

For a locally [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) noncompact [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md), form $X^*=X\cup\{\infty\}$ with one new point. Keep all [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) of $X$, and take the [neighborhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of infinity to contain [sets](../../../../../set-split.md)

$$
\{\infty\}\cup(X\setminus K),\qquad K\subseteq X\text{ compact}.
$$

[Compact subsets](../../../../../compact-space.md) of a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) are [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md). Finite [unions](../../../../../set-union.md) of [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) are [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md), so finite [intersections](../../../../../set-intersection.md) of these basic [neighborhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) are again of the same form; their [intersections](../../../../../set-intersection.md) with ordinary [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) are [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in $X$. This specifies a [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) restricting to the original one on $X$.

It is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md): from an [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) choose a member containing infinity. Its [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) is contained in some [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) $K$, and finitely many more members cover that [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) remainder. It is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md): two points of $X$ are separated as before; to separate $x\in X$ from infinity, choose an [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighborhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $U$ of $x$ lying inside a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [neighborhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $K$. The [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $U$ and the infinity [neighborhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $X^*\setminus K$ are disjoint.

The inclusion of $X$ is an embedding. Every basic [neighborhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of infinity meets $X$, because otherwise its [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) $K$ would equal all of $X$, contrary to noncompactness. Thus $X$ is [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) in $X^*$. This proves that **$X^*$ is the [one-point compactification](../../../../../alexandroff-extension.md)**.
