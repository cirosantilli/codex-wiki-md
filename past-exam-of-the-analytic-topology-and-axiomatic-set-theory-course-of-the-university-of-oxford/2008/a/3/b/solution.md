<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

First prove the suggested density fact. If $Z$ is [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) in $Y$, $V$ is [open](../../../../../open-set.md) and $y\in V$, then every [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighborhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $W$ of $y$ has $W\cap V$ nonempty [open](../../../../../open-set.md). It meets $Z$, so $W$ meets $V\cap Z$. Hence

$$
V\subseteq\overline{V\cap Z}^{\,Y}.
$$

Identify $X$ with its [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) embedded image in the [hausdorff compactification](../../../../../hausdorff-compactification.md). For $x\in X$, local compactness supplies an [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighborhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $U$ in $X$ and a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $K\subseteq X$ with $x\in U\subseteq K$. Choose an [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $V$ in $Y$ with $V\cap X=U$. The inclusion into $Y$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md), so $K$ is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) in $Y$ and therefore [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md), since $Y$ is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md). The density fact now yields

$$
x\in V\subseteq\overline{V\cap X}^{\,Y}=\overline U^{\,Y}\subseteq K\subseteq X.
$$

Thus every point of the embedded $X$ has a $Y$-open [neighborhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) contained in $X$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{h[X]\text{ is open in }Y.}
$$
