↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [topological space](../../../../../../topological-space.md) is [separable](../../../../../../separable-topological-space.md) if it has a [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) [dense subset](../../../../../../dense-set.md). A [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) $D$ is [dense](../../../../../../dense-set.md) when its [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md) is the whole [topological space](../../../../../../topological-space.md), equivalently when every nonempty [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) intersects $D$. [Countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) here permits a [finite set](../../../../../../finite-set.md), so finite [topological spaces](../../../../../../topological-space.md) and the empty [topological space](../../../../../../topological-space.md) fit the same definition.
