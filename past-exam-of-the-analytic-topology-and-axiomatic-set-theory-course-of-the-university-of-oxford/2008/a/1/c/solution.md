<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Since $D$ is a [discrete subset](../../../../../discrete-subset.md) in the [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md), for each $d\in D$ choose an [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $U_d\subseteq Z$ with $U_d\cap D=\{d\}$. Closedness of $D$ makes $Z\setminus D$ [open](../../../../../open-set.md). The family

$$
\{Z\setminus D\}\cup\{U_d:d\in D\}
$$

is therefore an [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) of $Z$. A [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) subcover exists by the [Lindelöf](../../../../../lindelof-space.md) property. Each point $d$ can be covered only by its own $U_d$ among this family: the [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) misses all of $D$, and every other $U_e$ meets $D$ only at $e$. Hence the [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) subcover must include $U_d$ for every $d\in D$. This forces

$$
\boxed{D\text{ is countable}.}
$$

Both closedness and discreteness matter: they provide the [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) and the single-point traces, respectively.
