<h1 id="a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) $D_X\subseteq X$ and $D_Y\subseteq Y$. Their [Cartesian product](../../../../../cartesian-product.md) is [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md). Every nonempty basic [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) of the [product topology](../../../../../product-topology.md) has the form $U\times V$ with $U,V$ nonempty [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in their respective [topological spaces](../../../../../topological-space.md). Density gives $d_X\in D_X\cap U$ and $d_Y\in D_Y\cap V$, hence $(d_X,d_Y)\in(D_X\times D_Y)\cap(U\times V)$.

Every nonempty [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) in the product contains such a basic [open set](../../../../../open-set.md). Thus $D_X\times D_Y$ is [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) in $X\times Y$, proving **[topological separability](../../../../../separable-topological-space.md) of the product**. If one factor is empty the product is empty and the conclusion holds directly.
