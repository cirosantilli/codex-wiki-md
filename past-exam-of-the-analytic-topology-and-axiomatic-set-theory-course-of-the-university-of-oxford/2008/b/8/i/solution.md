<h1 id="b/8/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $M$, let $\operatorname{Def}(M)$ consist of the [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) of $M$ definable over the structure $\langle M,\in\rangle$ by a [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) with finitely many parameters from $M$. It is a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) contained in $\mathcal P(M)$: satisfaction for a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) structure and the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) codes give a uniform [set](../../../../../set-split.md) construction of these definable [subsets](../../../../../subset.md). If $M$ is transitive, every $a\in M$ is one such [subset](../../../../../subset.md), defined over $M$ by $z\in a$, so $M\subseteq\operatorname{Def}(M)$.

Define the [constructible hierarchy](../../../../../constructible-hierarchy.md) by [transfinite recursion](../../../../../transfinite-recursion.md):

$$
L_0=\varnothing,\qquad L_{\alpha+1}=\operatorname{Def}(L_\alpha),\qquad
L_\lambda=\bigcup_{\alpha<\lambda}L_\alpha\quad(\lambda\text{ a nonzero limit ordinal}).
$$

Gödel's [constructible universe](../../../../../constructible-universe.md) is the definable [class in set theory](../../../../../class-set-theory.md)

$$
\boxed{L=\{x:\exists\alpha\text{ ordinal }(x\in L_\alpha)\}.}
$$

At successor stages only [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) definable over the previous stage are admitted, in contrast to the full power-set construction of the [cumulative hierarchy](../../../../../cumulative-hierarchy.md). The stages are transitive and increasing, as permitted in the question, and their [union](../../../../../set-union.md) contains every [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md). Finite parameters are essential in the definition of $\operatorname{Def}$.
