A bounded [quantifier](../../../../../../quantifier.md) has the form $\exists u\in v$ or $\forall u\in v$, abbreviating $\exists u(u\in v\wedge\cdots)$ or $\forall u(u\in v\to\cdots)$. A $\Sigma_0$ [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md), also called $\Delta_0$, is built from atomic membership and equality [logical formulas](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) using Boolean connectives and only bounded [quantifiers](../../../../../../quantifier.md). It has no unbounded [quantifier](../../../../../../quantifier.md).

For example $z\subseteq y$ abbreviates the bounded [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) $\forall u\in z\,(u\in y)$. [Quantifiers](../../../../../../quantifier.md) are bounded by an existing variable or parameter, not by an unrestricted [class term](../../../../../../class-term.md) such as the [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) of a parameter unless that [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) has separately been supplied as a parameter.
