A $\Sigma_1$ [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) has the form

$$
\boxed{\exists z_1\cdots\exists z_n\,\psi,\qquad\psi\text{ a }\Sigma_0\text{ formula},}
$$

up to the standard logical transformations defining the hierarchy. The initial existential [quantifiers](../../../../../../quantifier.md) may be unbounded; all [quantifiers](../../../../../../quantifier.md) inside the matrix are bounded. A finite tuple of witnesses can be encoded by one [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) if desired, and [logical formulas](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) with no initial unbounded [quantifier](../../../../../../quantifier.md) are included as the level-zero special case.

The dual $\Pi_1$ [class in set theory](../../../../../../class-set-theory.md) has an initial universal block with a bounded matrix. Negation interchanges these two classes, but does not in general preserve the existential [class in set theory](../../../../../../class-set-theory.md). Part (iii) supplies an explicit reason for the failure in this setting.
