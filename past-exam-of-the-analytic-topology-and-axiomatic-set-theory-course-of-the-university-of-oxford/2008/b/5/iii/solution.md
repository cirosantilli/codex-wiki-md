<h1 id="b/5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Fix a [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) $\theta(x,y,\vec p)$ with parameters in $V$, and a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $a\in V$. Suppose $\langle V,\in\rangle$ satisfies that each $x\in a$ has a unique $y$ with $\theta(x,y,\vec p)$. Relativize every unbounded [quantifier](../../../../../quantifier.md) in $\theta$ to the definable [class in set theory](../../../../../class-set-theory.md) $V$, obtaining an ambient [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\theta^V$.

Transitivity of $V$ ensures that every member of $a$ belongs to $V$. The ambient relation

$$
y\in V\ \wedge\ \theta^V(x,y,\vec p)
$$

therefore defines a unique ambient [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $y$ for every $x\in a$. Apply ambient [replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) to obtain its range [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $b$. By construction every element of $b$ belongs to $V$, so part (ii) gives $b\in V$.

Membership in this [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $b$ is exactly the range relation required by [replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) inside the structure $\langle V,\in\rangle$. Since the [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) and parameters were arbitrary, **every instance of the [replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) scheme holds in the hierarchy [class in set theory](../../../../../class-set-theory.md)**. The proof uses [formula relativization to a class](../../../../../formula-relativization-to-a-class.md) and the [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) property from part (ii), not the unsupported assertion that $V$ is the whole ambient universe.
