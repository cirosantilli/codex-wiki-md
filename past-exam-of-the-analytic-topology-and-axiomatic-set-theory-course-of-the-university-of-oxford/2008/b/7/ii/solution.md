<h1 id="b/7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an infinite [cardinal](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md) regarded as an [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md), its [cofinality](../../../../../cofinality.md) $\theta=\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)$ is the least [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of a [cofinal subset](../../../../../cofinal-subset.md) of $\kappa$. Equivalently there is a family $(\alpha_i)_{i<\theta}$ of [ordinals](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) below $\kappa$ whose [supremum](../../../../../supremum.md) is $\kappa$, and no smaller indexed family has that property. Since an infinite [initial ordinal](../../../../../initial-ordinal.md) is a [limit ordinal](../../../../../limit-ordinal.md), $\theta$ is infinite and in particular nonzero.

Use the diagonal mechanism of [König theorem for cardinal numbers](../../../../../konig-s-theorem-set-theory.md) directly. Suppose $F:\kappa\to{}^{\theta}\kappa$ were [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md). For each $i<\theta$, the [set](../../../../../set-split.md)

$$
E_i=\{F(\alpha)(i):\alpha<\alpha_i\}
$$

has [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) at most $|\alpha_i|<\kappa$. Choose the least $b_i\in\kappa\setminus E_i$. The resulting [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $b:\theta\to\kappa$ is not in the range: for any $\alpha<\kappa$, [cofinality](../../../../../cofinality.md) supplies $i$ with $\alpha<\alpha_i$, and then $b_i\ne F(\alpha)(i)$.

Thus no such surjection exists. Constant [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) provide an [injection](../../../../../injective-function.md) $\kappa\to{}^{\theta}\kappa$, and an [injection](../../../../../injective-function.md) in the reverse direction would give a surjection by the same inverse-and-default construction as in part (i). Consequently

$$
\boxed{\kappa^{\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)}>\kappa.}
$$

This argument also handles $\kappa=\aleph_0$, when [cofinal](../../../../../cofinal-subset.md) initial segments are finite; it does not incorrectly assume that smaller [cardinals](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md) themselves must form a [cofinal](../../../../../cofinal-subset.md) sequence in every infinite [cardinal](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md).
