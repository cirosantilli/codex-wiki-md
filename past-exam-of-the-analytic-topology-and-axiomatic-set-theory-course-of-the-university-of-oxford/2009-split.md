# 2009

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the analytic topology and axiomatic set theory course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part C paper combines Analytic [Topology](../topology-split.md) in section A and Axiomatic [Set](../set-split.md) Theory in section B, cross-listed for Mathematics, Mathematics and Computer Science, and Mathematics and Philosophy. [Source release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3) The archive retains the complete editable four-page transcription. Bounded official-source searches found no advertised answers for this exact paper. The solution to A1(iv) replaces a faulty printed radial hint with a proved rectangular construction.
