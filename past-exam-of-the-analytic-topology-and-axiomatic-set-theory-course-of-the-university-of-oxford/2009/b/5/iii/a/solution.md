<h1 id="b/5/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [GCH arithmetic at aleph omega](../../../../../../gch-arithmetic-at-aleph-omega.md), let $\kappa=\aleph_\omega$. Use monotonicity of [cardinal exponentiation](../../../../../../cardinal-exponentiation.md), the law $(a^b)^c=a^{bc}$, and $\kappa\cdot\kappa=\kappa$ for infinite cardinals in [ZFC](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md). Since $2\le\kappa\le2^\kappa$,

$$
2^\kappa\le\kappa^\kappa\le(2^\kappa)^\kappa=2^{\kappa\cdot\kappa}=2^\kappa.
$$

Thus $\kappa^\kappa=2^\kappa$, and the [generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\aleph_\omega^{\aleph_\omega}=\aleph_{\omega+1}.}
$$

The lower bound embeds binary-valued [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) into $\kappa$-valued ones; the upper bound codes each value by a [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) and combines the resulting pairs of indices. In particular the equality before applying the hypothesis is a [ZFC](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) equality.
