<h1 id="b/5/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The product from the preceding part injects into $\kappa^{\aleph_0}$, where $\kappa=\aleph_\omega$, because each coordinate value is an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) below $\kappa$. Combining its strict König lower bound with the exponentiation upper bound gives

$$
\kappa<\kappa^{\aleph_0}\le\kappa^\kappa=\kappa^+.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\aleph_\omega^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_{\omega+1}.}
$$

Only the instance $2^{\aleph_\omega}=\aleph_{\omega+1}$ of the [generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) was needed in these three computations, though the question assumes the full hypothesis. No assumption that [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) exponentiation leaves this [singular cardinal](../../../../../../singular-cardinal.md) unchanged is valid: König's theorem rules that out.
