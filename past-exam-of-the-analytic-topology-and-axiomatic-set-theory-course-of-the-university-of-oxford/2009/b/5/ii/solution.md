The [generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) is

$$
\boxed{2^\kappa=\kappa^+\quad\text{for every infinite cardinal }\kappa.}
$$

Equivalently, for every [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) $\alpha$, $2^{\aleph_\alpha}=\aleph_{\alpha+1}$. Here $2^\kappa$ is the [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of the [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) of a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) $\kappa$, and $\kappa^+$ is the least strictly larger cardinal. The hypothesis says that no intermediate cardinal lies between an [infinite set](../../../../../infinite-set.md) and its [power set](../../../../../power-set.md).
