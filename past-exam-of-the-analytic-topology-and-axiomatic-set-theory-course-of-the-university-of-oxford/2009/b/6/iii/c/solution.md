<h1 id="b/6/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A bounded expression for the successor relation is

$$
\boxed{y\in x\ \land\ (\forall z\in y\ z\in x)\ \land\ (\forall z\in x\ (z\in y\lor z=y)).}
$$

The first two clauses put $y$ and all its elements into $x$, while the last forbids any additional member of $x$. [Extensionality](../../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md) identifies this exactly with $x=y\cup\{y\}$. Since the formula is $\Delta_0$, it is absolute in both directions between the transitive classes and therefore upward absolute. This bounded description also justifies the successor computations used earlier in the rank-stage [Replacement](../../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) counterexample.
