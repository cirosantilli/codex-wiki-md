<h1 id="b/6/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Equality with the [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) is expressed by

$$
\boxed{\bigl(\forall z\in x\ \exists u\in y\ z\in u\bigr)\ \land\ \bigl(\forall u\in y\ \forall z\in u\ z\in x\bigr).}
$$

The first conjunct says that every member of $x$ lies in some member of $y$; the second gives the reverse inclusion. Every [quantifier](../../../../../../quantifier.md) is bounded by a parameter or by an element of a previously bounded parameter. Therefore bounded absoluteness applies, proving full absoluteness and hence upward absoluteness of $x=\bigcup y$. This only compares the relation for existing parameters; it does not require either class to contain the [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) of every one of its [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md).
