<h1 id="b/6/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [bounded formula](../../../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md)

$$
\boxed{\forall z\in x\ (z\in y).}
$$

It expresses $x\subseteq y$ with no unbounded [quantifier](../../../../../../quantifier.md). For parameters $x,y\in U_1$, transitivity puts every member of $x$ in $U_1$ already, and membership in $y$ has the same meaning in both classes. Thus the formula is $\Delta_0$ and is absolute in both directions, in particular upward absolute.
