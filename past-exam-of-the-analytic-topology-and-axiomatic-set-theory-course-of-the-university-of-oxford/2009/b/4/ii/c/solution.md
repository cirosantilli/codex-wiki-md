<h1 id="b/4/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Induct on $\alpha$. For every $\beta<\alpha$, the induction hypothesis says $\beta\in V_{\beta+1}$, and monotonicity gives $V_{\beta+1}\subseteq V_\alpha$. Thus every element of the [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) $\alpha$ lies in $V_\alpha$, so $\alpha\subseteq V_\alpha$. Taking its [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\alpha\in\mathcal P(V_\alpha)=V_{\alpha+1}.}
$$

For $\alpha=0$ the inclusion is vacuous and $\varnothing\in\mathcal P(\varnothing)$ supplies the base case. This argument uses [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) membership as strict order and the previously proved hierarchy monotonicity.
