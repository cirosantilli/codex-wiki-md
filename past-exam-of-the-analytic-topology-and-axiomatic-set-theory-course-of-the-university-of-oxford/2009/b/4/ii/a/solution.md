<h1 id="b/4/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $T$ is transitive if every element of an element of $T$ is itself in $T$, equivalently each element of $T$ is a [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of $T$. Proceed by [transfinite induction](../../../../../../transfinite-induction.md). The [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) is transitive. If $T$ is transitive and $u\in v\in\mathcal P(T)$, then $v\subseteq T$ gives $u\in T$, and transitivity of $T$ gives $u\subseteq T$. Thus $u\in\mathcal P(T)$, proving the successor stage transitive. A [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) of [transitive sets](../../../../../../transitive-set.md) is transitive: if $u\in v\in\bigcup_iT_i$, choose $i$ with $v\in T_i$ and infer $u\in T_i$. This proves the limit step and hence **every $V_\alpha$ is transitive**.
