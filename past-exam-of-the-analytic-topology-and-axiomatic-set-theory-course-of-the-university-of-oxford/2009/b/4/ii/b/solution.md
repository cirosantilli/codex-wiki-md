<h1 id="b/4/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $x\in V_\alpha$, transitivity of $V_\alpha$ gives $x\subseteq V_\alpha$. Therefore $x\in\mathcal P(V_\alpha)=V_{\alpha+1}$, proving

$$
\boxed{V_\alpha\subseteq V_{\alpha+1}.}
$$

Together with the [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) definition at limits, [transfinite induction](../../../../../../transfinite-induction.md) now gives $V_\alpha\subseteq V_\beta$ whenever $\alpha\le\beta$. No rank theorem is needed for this monotonicity argument.
