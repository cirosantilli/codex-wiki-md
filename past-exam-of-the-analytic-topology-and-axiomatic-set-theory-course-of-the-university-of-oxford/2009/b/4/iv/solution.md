<h1 id="b/4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

To prove [replacement failure at V omega plus omega](../../../../../replacement-failure-at-v-omega-plus-omega.md), put $M=V_{\omega+\omega}$. The [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $\omega$ belongs to $M$, and for every $n\in\omega$ the [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) $\omega+n$ also belongs to $M$. The relation saying that $y$ is obtained by taking $n$ successive [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) successors of $\omega$ is definable, with parameter $\omega$, and is single valued in $M$.

For clarity this does not assume [Replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) inside $M$. For each finite $n$ its finite computation [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) exists in $M$: its entries have rank below $\omega+n+1$, and the finite coding overhead still has rank below $\omega+\omega$. The [bounded formula](../../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md) verifying this finite successor chain is absolute between $M$ and the universe. The ordinary finite induction identifying its unique last entry uses the actual finite [ordinals](../../../../../ordinal-split.md), which are the same in $M$. This justifies the internal definable [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $n\mapsto\omega+n$ without invoking the axiom being tested.

If [Replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) held there, its range $B=\{\omega+n:n\in\omega\}$ would be an element of $M$. But [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) rank equals the [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md), so

$$
\operatorname{rank}(B)=\sup_{n<\omega}(\omega+n+1)=\omega+\omega.
$$

Every element of $V_{\omega+\omega}$ has strictly smaller rank. This contradiction proves **[Replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) fails in $V_{\omega+\omega}$**. Each individual output is present; the missing object is the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) collecting all outputs.
