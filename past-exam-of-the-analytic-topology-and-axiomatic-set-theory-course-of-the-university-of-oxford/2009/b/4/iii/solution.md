<h1 id="b/4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take a nonzero [limit ordinal](../../../../../limit-ordinal.md) $\lambda$ and $x\in V_\lambda$. Choose $\beta<\lambda$ with $x\in V_\beta$. Transitivity gives $x\subseteq V_\beta$. Hence every actual [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of $x$ belongs to $\mathcal P(V_\beta)=V_{\beta+1}$, and the actual [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $\mathcal P(x)$ belongs to $V_{\beta+2}$. Since $\lambda$ is a limit, $\beta+2<\lambda$, so $\mathcal P(x)\in V_\lambda$. Thus the internal [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) contains every actual [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of $x$, and the [power-set axiom](../../../../../axiom-of-power-set.md) holds in $V_\lambda$.

Here the absoluteness fact used is: every [bounded formula](../../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md) is absolute between [transitive sets](../../../../../transitive-set.md) or classes with the actual membership relation. In particular $z\subseteq x$, expressed by $\forall t\in z\,(t\in x)$, has the same meaning internally and externally. The argument supplies the actual [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) as an internal witness. If zero is included in one's convention for [limit ordinals](../../../../../limit-ordinal.md), its empty stage has no nonempty first-order domain; the assertion is understood for nonzero limits.

If also $\lambda>\omega$, the preceding ordinal-membership result gives $\omega\in V_{\omega+1}\subseteq V_\lambda$. The actual [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $\omega$ contains the [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md) and is closed under $z\mapsto z\cup\{z\}$. These bounded membership and successor conditions are absolute in the transitive stage, so $\omega$ is an internal inductive [set](../../../../../set-split.md). Therefore **[Infinity](../../../../../infinity.md) holds as well**. In contrast $V_\omega$ has only hereditarily finite [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) and cannot use $\omega$ as an element.
