The [cumulative hierarchy](../../../../../cumulative-hierarchy.md) is defined by [transfinite recursion](../../../../../transfinite-recursion.md) on the [ordinals](../../../../../ordinal-split.md):

$$
\boxed{V_0=\varnothing,\qquad V_{\alpha+1}=\mathcal P(V_\alpha),\qquad V_\lambda=\bigcup_{\beta<\lambda}V_\beta\quad(\lambda\text{ a nonzero limit}).}
$$

The recursion theorem of [ZF](../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) supplies each [set](../../../../../set-split.md) stage. The whole universe is $V=\bigcup_{\alpha\in\mathrm{Ord}}V_\alpha$, a proper-class [union](../../../../../set-union.md). Equivalently, $x\in V_\alpha$ exactly when the rank of $x$ is less than $\alpha$, where $\operatorname{rank}(x)=\sup\{\operatorname{rank}(z)+1:z\in x\}$.
