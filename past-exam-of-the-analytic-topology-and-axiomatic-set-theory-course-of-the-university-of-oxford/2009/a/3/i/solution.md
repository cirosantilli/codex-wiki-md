↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [filter base](../../../../../filter-base.md) on a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $X$ is a nonempty family $\mathcal F$ of nonempty [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) such that for every $F,G\in\mathcal F$ there is $H\in\mathcal F$ with $H\subseteq F\cap G$. Repeated application gives a member contained in any specified [finite intersection](../../../../../finite-intersection.md). In particular every [finite intersection](../../../../../finite-intersection.md) is nonempty, so the family has the [finite intersection property](../../../../../finite-intersection-property.md). Its upward closure $\{A\subseteq X:\exists F\in\mathcal F, F\subseteq A\}$ is the corresponding [proper filter](../../../../../filter-set-theory.md).
