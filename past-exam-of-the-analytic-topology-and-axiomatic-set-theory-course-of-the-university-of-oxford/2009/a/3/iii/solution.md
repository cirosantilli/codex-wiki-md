<h1 id="a/3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [Hausdorff compactification](../../../../../hausdorff-compactification.md) of $X$ is an [embedding](../../../../../topological-embedding.md) $h:X\to Y$ into a [compact Hausdorff space](../../../../../compact-hausdorff-space.md) with [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) image. The complement $Y\setminus h[X]$ is the remainder; a one-point or two-point compactification has a remainder of that [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md).

For one point, take the [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) and

$$
h_1(x)=(\cos(2\arctan x),\sin(2\arctan x)).
$$

The angle runs over $(-\pi,\pi)$, so this is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) onto the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) with $(-1,0)$ removed. That image is [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md), and the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) Hausdorff. For two points, take $Y=[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$ and $h_2(x)=\arctan x$. This is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) onto its [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) open interval, leaving exactly the two endpoints. Therefore **both required compactifications exist**; in the first the positive and negative ends meet, whereas in the second they remain distinct.
