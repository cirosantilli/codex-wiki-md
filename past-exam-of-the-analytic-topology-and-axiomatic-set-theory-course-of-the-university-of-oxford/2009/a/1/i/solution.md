<h1 id="a/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\mathscr H$ be the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of [separately continuous](../../../../../separate-continuity.md) real-valued [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) on the plane. Take as a [subbasis](../../../../../subbasis-of-a-topology.md) all [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) $h^{-1}(U)$, where $h\in\mathscr H$ and $U$ is open in the usual [real line](../../../../../real-line.md). Unions of finite intersections of these [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) form a [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md), and every $h$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) for it by construction. Conversely every [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) making every $h$ [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) must contain the [subbasis](../../../../../subbasis-of-a-topology.md), hence this entire generated [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md). Thus **the required coarsest [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) exists and is unique**: it is the [initial topology](../../../../../initial-topology.md) of the family $\mathscr H$.

The family is a [set](../../../../../set-split.md), being a [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of $\mathbb R^{\mathbb R^2}$, so this construction has no proper-class issue. The [coordinate projections](../../../../../projection-map.md) belong to $\mathscr H$; consequently this [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) contains the ordinary [Euclidean topology](../../../../../euclidean-topology.md). This last observation will also make [convergence](../../../../../convergence.md) in the new [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) imply ordinary coordinatewise [convergence](../../../../../convergence.md).
