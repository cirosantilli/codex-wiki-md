<h1 id="a/1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

If the origin had a [countable local basis](../../../../../first-countable-space.md) $U_1,U_2,\ldots$, replace it by the decreasing [local basis](../../../../../first-countable-space.md) $V_n=\bigcap_{j\le n}U_j$. Each $V_n$ meets the [dense set](../../../../../dense-set.md) $Y$, so choose $p_n\in V_n\cap Y$. Given any [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $U$ of the origin, some $U_j$ is contained in $U$; for every $n\ge j$ we then have $p_n\in V_n\subseteq U_j\subseteq U$. Thus $p_n$ converges to the origin, contradicting the preceding part. Consequently **[first countability](../../../../../first-countable-space.md) fails already at the origin**, and $X$ is not [first countable](../../../../../first-countable-space.md).
