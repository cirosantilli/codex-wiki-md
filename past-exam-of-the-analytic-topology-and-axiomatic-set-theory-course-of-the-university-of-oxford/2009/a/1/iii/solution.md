<h1 id="a/1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a fixed nonzero second coordinate $c$, the rational expression defining $h(x,c)$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) on the half-line where $xc>0$. On the opposite half-line it is constant. At $x=0$ the ratio tends to $1$, agreeing with the value on the other side. If $c=0$ the entire restriction is constant. By symmetry the restrictions with the first coordinate fixed are [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) too. Hence $h$ is [separately continuous](../../../../../separate-continuity.md), and so is $h_t(x,y)=h(x-t,y-t)$ for every real $t$.

On the diagonal $D$, $h_t(t,t)=1$, while $h_t(s,s)=0$ for $s\ne t$. Therefore

$$
D\cap h_t^{-1}((1/2,3/2))=\{(t,t)\}.
$$

Each diagonal [singleton](../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md) is open in the induced [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md); arbitrary unions show that **$D$ has the [discrete topology](../../../../../discrete-space.md)**.

If $X$ were [metrizable](../../../../../metrizable-space.md), its [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) [dense subset](../../../../../dense-set.md) from the preceding part would make it [second countable](../../../../../second-countable-space.md). Indeed balls with rational radii about [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) points contain a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) [basis](../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) after the usual smaller-ball argument. Every subspace of a [second-countable space](../../../../../second-countable-space.md) is [second countable](../../../../../second-countable-space.md). A discrete [second-countable space](../../../../../second-countable-space.md) is [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md), since each [singleton](../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md) must itself occur as a [basis](../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) member. But $D$ is [uncountable](../../../../../uncountable-set.md). This contradiction proves **$X$ is not [metrizable](../../../../../metrizable-space.md)**.
