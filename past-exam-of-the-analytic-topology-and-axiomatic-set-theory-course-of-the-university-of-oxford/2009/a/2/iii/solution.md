<h1 id="a/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a [strict-range modification of a continuous extension](../../../../../strict-range-modification-of-a-continuous-extension.md), the [closed sets](../../../../../closed-set.md) $Y$ and $Z=\{x:|f(x)|\ge1\}$ are disjoint. Choose by [Urysohn's lemma](../../../../../urysohn-s-lemma.md) a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) $u:X\to[0,1]$ with $u|_Y=0$ and $u|_Z=1$, and define

$$
\boxed{\widetilde f=(1-u)f.}
$$

This [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) and agrees with $f$ on $Y$. On $Z$ it vanishes. Off $Z$ we have $|f|<1$ and $0\le1-u\le1$, so $|\widetilde f|\le|f|<1$. Hence its range lies strictly inside $(-1,1)$ on all of $X$. The construction also covers empty $Y$ or empty $Z$ by a constant separating [function](../../../../../function-split.md) where appropriate.
