<h1 id="a/2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Let $h:Y\to\mathbb R$ be [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md), without a boundedness assumption. The [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) $\theta(t)=t/(1+|t|)$ maps the [real line](../../../../../real-line.md) onto $(-1,1)$, with inverse $\theta^{-1}(s)=s/(1-|s|)$. The [bounded function](../../../../../bounded-function.md) $g=\theta\circ h$ extends by the preceding bounded construction to a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) real-valued $G$ on $X$. Since $|G|<1$ on $Y$, the strict-range modification gives a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) $\widetilde G$ with range in $(-1,1)$ and the same restriction to $Y$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{H(x)=\frac{\widetilde G(x)}{1-|\widetilde G(x)|}}
$$

is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) everywhere: its denominator is pointwise positive. On $Y$ it equals $\theta^{-1}(\theta(h))=h$. Thus **$Y$ is $C$-embedded**. It is important to modify the extension before applying the inverse: an extension allowed to attain $1$ or $-1$ outside $Y$ would make this formula undefined there.
