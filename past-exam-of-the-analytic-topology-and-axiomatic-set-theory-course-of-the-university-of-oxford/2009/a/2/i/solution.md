[Urysohn's lemma](../../../../../urysohn-s-lemma.md) states that if $X$ is normal and $A,B$ are disjoint [closed subsets](../../../../../closed-set.md), there is a [continuous function](../../../../../continuous-function.md) $u:X\to[0,1]$ with $u|_A=0$ and $u|_B=1$. The values on the rest of $X$ remain in that interval. Interchanging $A,B$ or composing with an affine [function](../../../../../function-split.md) gives any two prescribed real endpoint values. This is **functional separation of [closed sets](../../../../../closed-set.md)**, stronger than merely choosing disjoint open [neighbourhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md).
