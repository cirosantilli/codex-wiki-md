<h1 id="a/2/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Take **the [natural numbers](../../../../../natural-number.md) as the [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) [discrete subspace](../../../../../discrete-space.md) of their [Stone-Čech compactification](../../../../../stone-cech-compactification.md) $\beta\mathbb N$**. The ambient [compact Hausdorff space](../../../../../compact-hausdorff-space.md) is normal, and the [Stone-Čech extension property](../../../../../stone-cech-extension-property.md) makes this subspace $C^b$-embedded. It is not $C$-embedded: the [continuous function](../../../../../continuous-function.md) $n\mapsto n$ is unbounded, whereas every [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) [real-valued function](../../../../../real-valued-function.md) on a [compact space](../../../../../compact-space.md) is bounded. The subspace is not closed, so this does not contradict the preceding closed-subspace theorem.
