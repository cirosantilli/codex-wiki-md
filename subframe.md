# Subframe

↑ **Parent:** [Complete Heyting algebra](complete-heyting-algebra.md)

A subframe is a subset of a [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) closed under arbitrary ambient joins and finite ambient meets, including $0$ and $1$. The inclusion is a [frame homomorphism](frame-homomorphism.md). In contrast, the fixed elements of a [nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md) usually need their own regularized joins and are not a subframe.
