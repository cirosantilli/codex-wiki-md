# Borel postcomposition preserves Lebesgue measurability

↑ **Parent:** [Composition of measurable functions](composition-of-measurable-functions.md)

If $g$ is a [Lebesgue measurable function](lebesgue-measurable-function.md) and $h$ is a [Borel measurable function](borel-measurable-function.md), then $h\circ g$ is a [Lebesgue measurable function](lebesgue-measurable-function.md). For an open [set](set-split.md) $U$, $h^{-1}(U)$ is a [Borel set](borel-set.md). By measurability of $g$, $g^{-1}(h^{-1}(U))$ is a [Lebesgue measurable set](lebesgue-measurable-set.md), and it equals $(h\circ g)^{-1}(U)$. This is [composition of measurable functions](composition-of-measurable-functions.md) with the intermediate [Borel sigma-algebra](borel-sigma-algebra.md) stated explicitly. Merely knowing both functions are Lebesgue measurable would not ensure that the intermediate sigma-algebras match.
