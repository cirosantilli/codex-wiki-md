# Shearing-periodic boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing box](shearing-box.md)

For background velocity $-2Ax\mathbf e_y$, opposite radial faces of a [shearing box](shearing-box.md) are identified after the displayed azimuthal shift, modulo $L_y$. Products and spatial derivatives inherit this condition. Their radial surface integrals cancel because a translation preserves an integral over a periodic azimuthal interval.
