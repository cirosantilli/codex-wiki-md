# Continuously differentiable functions on a compact interval form a Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

With complex scalars and pointwise multiplication, the [product rule](product-rule.md) gives a submultiplicative norm. For a Cauchy sequence, uniform limits $f$ of the functions and $g$ of their derivatives satisfy $f(t)-f(0)=\int_0^tg(s)\,ds$, proving $f'=g$ and completeness. Polynomials are dense in this norm: approximate $f'$ uniformly by polynomials and integrate, fixing the initial value. This also identifies the [character space of C1 on a compact interval](character-space-of-c1-on-a-compact-interval.md).
