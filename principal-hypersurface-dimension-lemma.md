# Principal hypersurface dimension lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-point dimension lemma for affine domains](closed-point-dimension-lemma-for-affine-domains.md)

For an [irreducible variety](irreducible-variety.md) which is an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) of dimension $d$ and a nonzero nonunit [regular function](regular-function.md) $f$, each [irreducible component](irreducible-component.md) of $V(f)$ has dimension $d-1$. Choose a closed point on just the component in question. In its [local ring](local-ring.md) $R$, $\sqrt{(f)}$ is that component's [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md). A [system of parameters](system-of-parameters.md) of $R/(f)$, lifted and supplemented by $f$, generates a maximal-primary ideal; the [Krull height theorem](krull-height-theorem.md) gives $\dim R/(f)\ge d-1$. Extending any chain above $(f)$ by the zero prime of the domain gives the reverse inequality. This proof does not identify dimension with [transcendence degree](transcendence-degree.md).
