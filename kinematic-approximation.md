# Kinematic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal scattering](crystal-scattering.md)

In the [kinematic approximation](kinematic-approximation.md), each incident wave scatters once, so the total [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) is the coherent sum of single-atom amplitudes. Multiple scattering and depletion of the incident beam are neglected. The [structure factor](structure-factor.md) then determines systematic reflection absences. Strong multiple scattering can violate an absence predicted in this approximation.
