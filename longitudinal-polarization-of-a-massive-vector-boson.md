# Longitudinal polarization of a massive vector boson

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization vector](polarization-vector.md)

A physical polarization of a massive spin-one [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) whose spatial polarization points along its spatial momentum. Together with two transverse polarizations it supplies the three physical spin states. In the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md), a broken-direction [Goldstone boson](goldstone-boson.md) supplies this extra state as the [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) acquires mass.
