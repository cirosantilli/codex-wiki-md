# Attribution of recent climate change

↑ **Parent:** [Climate change](climate-change.md)

Attribution of recent climate change assesses the contributions of anthropogenic forcing, natural forcing and internal variability to observed changes. It combines the physics of [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md), measured forcing agents, energy budgets and spatial, vertical and temporal response patterns. A single weather event or a single trend is insufficient by itself to identify all causes.
