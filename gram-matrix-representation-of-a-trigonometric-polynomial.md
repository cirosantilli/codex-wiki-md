# Gram matrix representation of a trigonometric polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md)

Under the displayed diagonal-sum convention, set $w(z)=(1,z^{-1},\ldots,z^{-d})^T$ on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md). A [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) $M$ then gives

$$
p(z)=w(z)^*Mw(z).
$$

Consequently $M\succeq0$ proves nonnegativity. Conversely the [Fejér–Riesz theorem](fejer-riesz-theorem.md) produces the [rank-one spectral-factor Gram matrix](rank-one-spectral-factor-gram-matrix.md). This gives a [semidefinite programming](semidefinite-programming.md) representation of nonnegative trigonometric [polynomials](polynomial-split.md). The reversed convention $j-i=k$ instead uses the unconjugated monomial [vector](vector.md).
