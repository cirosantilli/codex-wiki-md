# Antiphase boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallographic defect](crystallographic-defect.md)

An interface between ordered domains related by a displacement that is a translation of the underlying disordered lattice but not of the ordered chemical structure. Slip by that displacement can leave an [antiphase boundary](antiphase-boundary.md) behind a [dislocation](dislocation.md); a second appropriate displacement can restore the order.
