# Closed-vessel thermal transpiration

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal transpiration](thermal-transpiration.md)

Two equal sealed vessels of fixed [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md), initially at $T_0,p_0$, exchange an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) through a thin effusive opening. Hold one at $T_1$ and the other at $T_0$, with both interiors locally thermalized. Zero net [particle](particle-physics.md) transfer gives $p_h/\sqrt{T_1}=p_c/\sqrt{T_0}$. Writing $q=\sqrt{T_1/T_0}$, conservation of total [particle number](particle-number.md) gives $p_h=2p_0q^2/(1+q)$ and $p_c=2p_0q/(1+q)$. Thus $\Delta p=2p_0q(q-1)/(1+q)$. The alternative $p_c=p_0$, $\Delta p=p_0(q-1)$ requires a cold reservoir whose [pressure](pressure-split.md) is externally held fixed; it is not the closed equal-volume problem.
