## Half-Yearly Report on Pathology and Principles and Practice of Medicine (1865)

↑ **Parent:** [Biology bibliography](biology-bibliography.md)

[https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5180479/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5180479/)

British and Foreign Medico-Chirurgical Review 35(69), January 1865, pp. 248–259. Section XII, pp. 255–256, reports narrowed coronary openings and aortic and cardiac plexus disease in a case of angina; a contemporary report of a case is not proof of a necessary cause.

## The Practice of Medicine by Robley Dunglison (1844)

↑ **Parent:** [Biology bibliography](biology-bibliography.md)

[https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c6/The_practice_of_medicine-_a_treatise_on_special_pathology_and_therapeutics_%28Volume_2%29_%28IA_63350860RX2.nlm.nih.gov%29.pdf](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c6/The_practice_of_medicine-_a_treatise_on_special_pathology_and_therapeutics_%28Volume_2%29_%28IA_63350860RX2.nlm.nih.gov%29.pdf)

Second edition, volume 2, section Pityriasis. Four descriptive varieties and the objection that one usage of nigra lacks scaling. This is a pre-1865 text, not a modern taxonomy.

## Book Reviews in the Edinburgh Medical Journal (1863)

↑ **Parent:** [Biology bibliography](biology-bibliography.md)

[https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5277139/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5277139/)

January 1863 review, pp. 644–650, of the 1862 Researches on the Nature and Treatment of Diabetes. The review records competing theories of hepatic sugar formation; these are evidence about period beliefs, not all modern conclusions.

## On the Treatment of Acute Diseases by Aretaeus of Cappadocia

↑ **Parent:** [Biology bibliography](biology-bibliography.md)

[https://beta.perseus.tufts.edu/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0719.tlg003.perseus-grc2:2/](https://beta.perseus.tufts.edu/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0719.tlg003.perseus-grc2:2/)

Primary Greek and accompanying historical English text. Book II.3, treatment of cardiac affections: impending collapse, the rationale for selective depletion, and the assigned sequence of [venesection](biology.md#venesection), [cupping](biology.md#cupping), [wine](physiology.md#wine), [poultices](biology.md#poultice) and [enemas](biology.md#enema). Distinguish this work from the existing Causes and Signs of Chronic Diseases.

<h2 id="sepulchretum-by-theophile-bonet">Sepulchretum by Théophile Bonet</h2>

↑ **Parent:** [Biology bibliography](biology-bibliography.md)

[https://wellcomecollection.org/works/ebja53uq](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/ebja53uq)

Institutional catalogue for the primary compilation Sepulchretum sive anatomia practica ex cadaveribus morbo denatis. Identifies author, two volumes, Geneva and 1679. The observation numbers in the Oxford extract are those invoked by [Morgagni](physician.md#giovanni-battista-morgagni); no unsupported claim is made to have independently verified every observation or the exact later edition he consulted.

## The Seats and Causes of Diseases Investigated by Anatomy by Giovanni Battista Morgagni

↑ **Parent:** [Biology bibliography](biology-bibliography.md)

[https://wellcomecollection.org/works/z7fb8uxm/items?manifest=2&shouldScrollToCanvas=true](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/z7fb8uxm/items?manifest=2&shouldScrollToCanvas=true)

Primary work in an eighteenth-century English version. Book III, Letter XXX, articles 12–13 locate the preceding anatomical case and the translated extract; volume II, printed p. 53 and continuation. The Oxford exam supplies the assigned Latin wording, including the unclear printed laevi. An independently indexed primary scan was inspected in excerpts; its direct Commons and Internet Archive fulltext endpoints were unavailable during this task. Source identification and locators were retained without claiming a full facsimile review.
