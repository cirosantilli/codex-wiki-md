# Volterra parametrix correction

↑ **Parent:** [Heat parametrix](heat-parametrix.md)

If $R=(\partial_t+\Delta_x)P$ is smooth and bounded up to time zero on a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md), the [Volterra convolution of kernels](volterra-convolution-of-kernels.md) solves $Q+R+R*Q=0$ by $Q=\sum_{k\ge1}(-1)^kR^{*k}$. Bounds by $C^k\operatorname{vol}(M)^{k-1}t^{k-1}/(k-1)!$ prove convergence. Then $H=P+P*Q$ is the exact [Riemannian heat kernel](riemannian-heat-kernel.md) and has the same initial delta limit.
