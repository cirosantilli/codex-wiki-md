# Higgs decay to longitudinal W bosons

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs decay to two W bosons](higgs-decay-to-two-w-bosons.md)

For $x=m_W^2/m_H^2<1/4$, the on-shell longitudinal pair contributes the displayed tree-level partial [decay width](decay-width.md). In the [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) rest frame, its [polarization vectors](polarization-vector.md) have contraction $(m_H^2/2-m_W^2)/m_W^2$. Multiplying by the [Higgs boson coupling to W bosons](higgs-boson-coupling-to-w-bosons.md) gives $|\mathcal M_{LL}|^2=m_H^4(1-2x)^2/v^2$; the [two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space](two-body-lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) then gives the width. The sum over transverse polarizations adds $8x^2$ to the longitudinal polynomial. Below threshold, physical decays through virtual [W bosons](w-boson.md) require a different multiparticle calculation.
