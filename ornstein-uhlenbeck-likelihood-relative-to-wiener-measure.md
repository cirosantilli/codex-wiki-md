# Ornstein-Uhlenbeck likelihood relative to Wiener measure

↑ **Parent:** [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md)

On each finite horizon the displayed [Radon-Nikodym derivative](radon-nikodym-derivative.md) changes [Wiener measure](wiener-measure.md) into the law of $dX=dB-\mu Xdt$. Stop at coordinate magnitude n so the [Novikov condition](novikov-s-condition.md) applies. Under each stopped tilted measure the coordinate solves the drifted equation, and the [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) with the [Doob L2 maximal inequality](doob-l2-maximal-inequality.md) bounds its maximal second moment uniformly in n. The probability of reaching n therefore tends to zero; this proves that the unstopped density has expectation one. No unrestricted large-time Novikov estimate is needed.
