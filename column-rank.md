# Column rank

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix rank](matrix-rank.md)

The [column rank](column-rank.md) of a [matrix](matrix.md) is the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of its [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md), equivalently the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of the [linear span](linear-span.md) of its columns. Multiplication on the left by an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md) preserves all column [linear relations](linear-relation.md), and [elementary column operations](elementary-column-operation.md) preserve their [linear span](linear-span.md). In [row echelon form](row-echelon-form.md) the [pivot columns](pivot-column.md) form a [basis](basis.md) of the [column space](column-space.md). Its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) equals the number of nonzero rows, and hence the [row rank](row-rank.md).
