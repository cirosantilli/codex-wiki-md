# Column rank

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix rank](matrix-rank.md)

The [column rank](column-rank.md) of a [matrix](matrix.md) is the dimension of its image as a [linear map](linear-map.md), equivalently the dimension of the span of its columns. Multiplication on the left by an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md) preserves all column relations, and [elementary column operations](elementary-column-operation.md) preserve their span. In [row echelon form](row-echelon-form.md) the pivot columns form a basis of the [column space](column-space.md). Its dimension equals the number of nonzero rows, and hence the [row rank](row-rank.md).
