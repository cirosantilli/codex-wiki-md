# Delta bonding

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md)

Bonding with two nodal planes containing the internuclear or coordination axis. [metal](metal.md) $d_{xy}$ and $d_{x^2-y^2}$ orbitals can interact with ring combinations of matching delta symmetry; symmetry alone does not imply a strong bond.
