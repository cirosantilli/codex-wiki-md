# Multitype Poisson branching process

↑ **Parent:** [Branching process](branching-process.md)

A particle of type $i$ has independently a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) of type-$j$ offspring with [mean](expected-value.md) $\lambda_{ij}$. Different particles reproduce independently. If $p^{(t)}_i$ is the [probability](probability.md) generation $t$ is nonempty from one type-$i$ ancestor, then $p^{(0)}=\mathbf1$ and $p^{(t+1)}=T(p^{(t)})$, where $T_i(x)=1-\exp[-(\Lambda x)_i]$. This iteration decreases to the [survival probability of a branching process](survival-probability-of-a-branching-process.md), the greatest nonnegative [fixed point](fixed-point.md) of $T$. The expected type-count row [vector](vector.md) in generation $t$ is $e_i^T\Lambda^t$.
