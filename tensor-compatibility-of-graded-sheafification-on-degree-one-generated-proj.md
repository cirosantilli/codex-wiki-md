# Tensor compatibility of graded sheafification on degree-one-generated Proj

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf associated with a graded module](sheaf-associated-with-a-graded-module.md)

Under the [degree-one generation condition for Proj](degree-one-generation-condition-for-proj.md), cover $X=\operatorname{Proj}S$ by degree-one charts. The [degree-one localization of a graded module](degree-one-localization-of-a-graded-module.md) identifies the two local modules in the displayed formula, using [localization commutes with tensor products](localization-commutes-with-tensor-products.md). The maps are natural and agree on overlaps, so they glue to an isomorphism of [sheaves of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md). The analogous assertion can fail for general positively graded rings.
