# Gaussian random matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Random matrix](random-matrix.md)

A standard real Gaussian [random matrix](random-matrix.md) has [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) [standard normal](standard-normal-distribution.md) entries. Each column is an [orthogonally invariant random vector](orthogonally-invariant-random-vector.md): its [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is proportional to $\exp(-\|z\|_2^2/2)$. Consequently multiplying on either side by a fixed [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) preserves the joint distribution. Its [singular values](singular-value.md) are random; the expressions $\sqrt m\pm\sqrt n$ for an $m\times n$ [matrix](matrix.md) describe useful spectral scales, not deterministic endpoints that hold for every realization.
