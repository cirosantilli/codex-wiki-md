# Self-gravitating incompressible slab

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical fluid dynamics](astrophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)

A uniform [mass density](density.md) slab with free surfaces at $z=\pm H$ has $\rho_0=\Sigma/(2H)$, internal gravity $4\pi G\rho_0z$ and hydrostatic [pressure](pressure.md) $2\pi G\rho_0^2(H^2-z^2)$ above ambient [pressure](pressure.md). Its [surface modes of a self-gravitating incompressible slab](surface-modes-of-a-self-gravitating-incompressible-slab.md) separate into thickness and bending parities.
