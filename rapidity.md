# Rapidity

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapidity)

For one spatial dimension and $c=1$, a massive particle has $v=\tanh\theta$, $E=M\cosh\theta$ and $p=M\sinh\theta$. A [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) adds a constant to all rapidities, so a rapidity difference is invariant. Rapidity is useful for [factorized scattering](factorized-scattering.md), where two-body [S-matrices](s-matrix.md) depend only on that difference.
