# Interhemispheric inhibition

↑ **Parent:** [Neural circuit](neural-circuit.md)

Inhibitory influence between systems in opposite [cerebral hemispheres](cerebral-hemisphere.md). It is a candidate mechanism in some recovery accounts, but opposite activation changes or improved behaviour after stimulation do not by themselves establish its direction and causal route.
