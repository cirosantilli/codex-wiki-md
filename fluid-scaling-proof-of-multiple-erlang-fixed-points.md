# Fluid scaling proof of multiple Erlang fixed points

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple Erlang fixed points under alternative routing](multiple-erlang-fixed-points-under-alternative-routing.md)

Let $G_N(B)=E(20N[1+2B(1-B)],21N)-B$. The [proportional scaling limit of the Erlang loss formula](proportional-scaling-limit-of-the-erlang-loss-formula.md) makes its limits at $B=1/100$ and $B=1/8$ respectively $-1/100$ and $7/520$. Since $G_N(0)>0$ and $G_N(1)<0$, sufficiently large $N$ give alternating signs at these four points. The [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) supplies three distinct zeros in the intervening intervals. These are multiple solutions of the [Erlang fixed point approximation](erlang-fixed-point-approximation.md), not multiple stationary laws of an exact finite [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md).
