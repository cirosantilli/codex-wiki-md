# Neutrino mass

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)

The [mass](mass.md) of a [neutrino](neutrino.md) [mass eigenstate](mass-eigenstate.md). A [neutrino flavor](neutrino-flavor.md) need not have one definite [mass](mass.md); [neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md) probes differences of squared [masses](mass.md).
