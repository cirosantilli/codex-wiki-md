# Bessel process

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessel_process)

A Bessel process of dimension $d$ is the radial part of $d$-dimensional [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). Away from zero it solves the [stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md)

$$
dR_t=\frac{d-1}{2R_t}\,dt+dW_t.
$$

This equation also defines noninteger dimensions through its [weak solutions](weak-solution.md).
