# Spherical isochrone model

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

The spherical isochrone model has potential

$$
\Phi(r)=-\frac{GM}{b+\sqrt{b^2+r^2}}.
$$

Its radial period depends only on orbital energy, while its [radial action](radial-action.md) and frequencies are elementary functions of the integrals of motion.
