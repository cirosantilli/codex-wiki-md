# Quasi-circular spiral transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

A weak tangential [force](force-split.md) can move a satellite through a slowly varying sequence of nearly [circular Kepler orbits](circular-kepler-orbit.md). For constant satellite [mass](mass.md), $E=-\mu m/(2r)$ and $v_c=\sqrt{\mu/r}$ give $\dot E=Fv_c$, hence $F/m=\sqrt\mu\,\dot r/(2r^{3/2})$. Integrating the thrust magnitude for a monotone outward transfer proves the displayed formula. The integrated thrust per mass is not the vector difference of the endpoint [velocities](velocity.md); [gravity](gravity.md) acts throughout the transfer. The approximation requires slow orbital evolution and negligible eccentricity.
