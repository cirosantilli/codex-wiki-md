# Protein backbone

↑ **Parent:** [Polypeptide](polypeptide.md)

The [protein backbone](protein-backbone.md) is the repeating chain of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) that connects successive [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md), including their [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md). The attached groups that distinguish [amino acids](amino-acid.md) project from this shared framework. [Protein folding](protein-folding.md) changes the framework's spatial arrangement while ordinarily preserving its sequence connectivity. See [Cooper: polypeptide backbone and attached groups](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9879/).
