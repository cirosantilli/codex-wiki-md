# Stellar nuclear timescale

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

The time for [stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md) to consume the accessible fuel at characteristic [luminosity](luminosity.md) $L$. If a fraction $f$ of a star's [mass](mass.md) $M$ supplies fuel abundance $X$ and released [energy](energy.md) per unit fuel [mass](mass.md) $E_H$, [hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md) gives $t_{\rm nuc}\sim fXME_H/L$. Accessible core fuel, rather than the entire star, sets the lifetime unless mixing is complete. This is longer than a structural [Kelvin-Helmholtz cooling time](kelvin-helmholtz-cooling-time.md) when the nuclear reservoir greatly exceeds the gravitational contraction reservoir.
