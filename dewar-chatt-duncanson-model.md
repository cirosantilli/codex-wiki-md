# Dewar-Chatt-Duncanson model

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

The [Dewar-Chatt-Duncanson model](dewar-chatt-duncanson-model.md) describes an [alkene](alkene.md)–[metal](metal.md) bond through donation from the filled [alkene](alkene.md) $\pi$ orbital to the [metal](metal.md) and [pi backbonding](pi-backbonding.md) from the [metal](metal.md) into [alkene](alkene.md) $\pi^*$. Backbonding weakens and lengthens the [alkene](alkene.md) double bond. A $d^0$ early-[metal](metal.md) ion has little conventional $d$-[electron](electron.md) backbonding.
