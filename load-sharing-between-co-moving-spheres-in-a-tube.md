# Load sharing between co-moving spheres in a tube

↑ **Parent:** [Three drag regimes for a nearly occluding sphere](three-drag-regimes-for-a-nearly-occluding-sphere.md)

Two identical well-separated [spheres](sphere.md) falling at the same speed share one laboratory [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md), rather than producing twice the single-sphere piston flux. In the middle of the [three drag regimes for a nearly occluding sphere](three-drag-regimes-for-a-nearly-occluding-sphere.md), the same overall [Hagen-Poiseuille flow](hagen-poiseuille-equation.md) [pressure](pressure.md) drop is divided equally between them, halving each [sphere](sphere.md)'s drag. In the local-shear and local-gap-pressure regimes each [sphere](sphere.md) retains its own leading resistance, so the drag per [sphere](sphere.md) is unchanged. This conclusion assumes nonoverlapping lubrication regions and sufficient separation from the tube ends.
