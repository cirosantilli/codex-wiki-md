<h1 id="metrizable-separability-and-the-lindelof-property">Metrizable separability and the Lindelöf property</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Metrizable space](metrizable-space.md)

A [metrizable space](metrizable-space.md) is [separable](separable-topological-space.md) exactly when it is [Lindelöf](lindelof-space.md). A [countable](countable-set.md) [dense subset](dense-set.md) gives a [countable](countable-set.md) [topological basis](basis-of-a-topology.md) of balls with rational radii, hence the open-cover property. Conversely, choose [countable](countable-set.md) subcovers of all radius-$1/n$ ball covers; their centers form a [countable](countable-set.md) [dense subset](dense-set.md).
