# Prime-dimensional faithful irreducible module forces simplicity

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)

Let a semisimple complex [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $L=\bigoplus_iL_i$ act faithfully and irreducibly on a space $V$ of prime [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). Complete reducibility for $L_1$ splits $V$ into isotypic components. The commuting other [ideals](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md) preserve these components, so irreducibility leaves just one, of the form $W_1\otimes\operatorname{Hom}_{L_1}(W_1,V)$. Repeating with the other factors gives $V\cong\bigotimes_iW_i$, with each $W_i$ an [irreducible module](irreducible-module.md) for its simple [ideal](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md).

Faithfulness makes every factor nontrivial. A nonabelian [simple Lie algebra](simple-lie-algebra.md) has only the trivial one-dimensional [module](module-mathematics.md), so $\dim W_i\ge2$. Their product is the prime $\dim V$, forcing just one simple [ideal](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Hence $L$ is simple. This is a [representation](group-representation.md)-[dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) obstruction, independent of the classification of [root](root-of-a-root-system.md) systems.
