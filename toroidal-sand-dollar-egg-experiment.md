# Toroidal sand dollar egg experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Cleavage furrow](cleavage-furrow.md)

In 1961, [Raymond Rappaport](raymond-rappaport.md) gently removed membrane and jelly from [sand dollar](sand-dollar.md) [ova](ovum.md) with a constricted pipette. After [fertilization](fertilization.md), he used an inverted [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) and rack-and-pinion manipulator to press a needle-mounted glass sphere through an egg onto the chamber floor, producing a [torus](torus.md)-shaped [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md). Visible [spindle apparatus](spindle-apparatus.md) positions and [cleavage furrow](cleavage-furrow.md) progression were the readout.[Experiments concerning the cleavage stimulus in sand dollar eggs, pp. 81–82, Materials and Methods: egg preparation, inverted microscope and sphere manipulation](experiments-concerning-the-cleavage-stimulus-in-sand-dollar-eggs-by-raymond-rappaport.md)

First [cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md) opened one side into a horseshoe containing two [cell nuclei](cell-nucleus.md). During the next division, daughters separated from its free ends; an additional [cleavage furrow](cleavage-furrow.md) crossed between [mitotic asters](mitotic-aster.md) of different [spindle apparatuses](spindle-apparatus.md). Control eggs supplied the timing comparison. This region lacked intervening [chromosomes](chromosome.md) or a previous nearby [spindle apparatus](spindle-apparatus.md). This supported [mitotic aster](mitotic-aster.md)-associated positioning, without identifying the signal or establishing universal necessity.[pp. 81–82, introductory experiment summary, Results §1 and Figure 1 legend: horseshoe, second division, control comparison and furrowing between separate mitotic figures](experiments-concerning-the-cleavage-stimulus-in-sand-dollar-eggs-by-raymond-rappaport.md)
