# Boundary formula for planar area

↑ **Parent:** [Green theorem](green-theorem.md)

Apply [Green theorem](green-theorem.md) with $P=-y/2$ and $Q=x/2$ to a positively oriented regular planar region. Since $Q_x-P_y=1$, its area is the displayed [line integral](line-integral.md). Clockwise orientation changes the sign; holes have the opposite boundary orientation to the outer curve.
