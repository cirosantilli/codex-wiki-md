# Synaptic conductance

↑ **Parent:** [Synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md)

[Synaptic conductance](synaptic-conductance.md) describes how strongly activated synaptic [ion channels](ion-channel.md) conduct [electric current](electric-current.md) for a given driving force. In the passive approximation, $I_{\mathrm{syn}}=g_{\mathrm{syn}}(E_{\mathrm{syn}}-V)$, with inward-positive [electric current](electric-current.md). It is the [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) contribution of a [synapse](synapse.md); its postsynaptic voltage response also depends on the driving force, the other membrane [electrical conductances](electrical-conductance.md), and the [capacitance](capacitance.md). The [synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md) model and [shunting inhibition](shunting-inhibition.md) example show why a nonzero [synaptic conductance](synaptic-conductance.md) can have zero [electric current](electric-current.md) at rest.
