# Poor-solvent brush height

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer brush](polymer-brush.md)

In a laterally uniform dense [polymer brush](polymer-brush.md) with approximately fixed polymer [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) $\phi$, conservation of occupied [volume](volume.md) gives $\phi h=Nv_0\sigma$, with repeat-unit [volume](volume.md) $v_0$. At fixed solvent quality this gives $h\propto N\sigma$, unlike the [good-solvent brush height](good-solvent-brush-height.md). Isolated collapsed [polymer globules](polymer-globule.md) and laterally segregated grafted layers need not obey this uniform-layer model.
