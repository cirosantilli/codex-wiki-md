# Gymnosperm

↑ **Parent:** [Seed plant](seed-plant.md)

A member of a seed-plant lineage whose [ovules](ovule.md) are not enclosed in angiosperm [carpels](carpel.md). Conifers contribute extensively to forest carbon pools, but the lineage includes different growth forms and reproductive arrangements.
