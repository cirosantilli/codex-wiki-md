# Ionization fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Ionization](ionization.md)

The fraction of nuclei in a specified ionized state. For pure singly ionized [hydrogen](hydrogen.md), $x=n_e/(n_H+n_e)$ by [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md); a different species or [normalization](normalization.md) requires a different definition.
