# Gravitational neutron bound-state transmission

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum bouncer](quantum-bouncer.md)

A horizontal neutron-reflecting mirror supplies the lower boundary of a [quantum bouncer](quantum-bouncer.md), with the Earth's approximately linear gravitational potential above it. An absorber above the mirror removes neutrons with sufficient vertical extent. Scanning the mirror-absorber gap and counting transmitted neutrons probes the lowest allowed gravitational states. The 2002 work led by [Valery V. Nesvizhevsky](valery-v-nesvizhevsky.md) reported this confinement; a subsequent detailed report describes suppression below the lowest-state height.[2002 report, transmission versus absorber height](quantum-states-of-neutrons-in-the-earth-s-gravitational-field-by-valery-v-nesvizhevsky-and-others.md)[2003 abstract: horizontal bottom mirror, top absorber and lowest-state transmission threshold](measurement-of-quantum-states-of-neutrons-in-the-earth-s-gravitational-field-by-valery-v-nesvizhevsky-and-others.md)

This is a count-versus-gap experiment, not a direct image of a [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md). Surface scattering, absorption and the incident vertical-motion distribution enter a quantitative transmission model.
