# sl3 interlacing character formula

↑ **Parent:** [Formal character of a weight module](formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md)

For the [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sl}_3$, the irreducible [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md) with [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) $(a,b)$ has the displayed [formal character](formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md) on diagonal matrices with $x_1x_2x_3=1$. The interlacing rows $(a+b,b,0)$, $(p,q)$ and $(r)$ enumerate a [Gelfand–Tsetlin basis](gelfand-tsetlin-basis.md); each triple contributes the exponent differences $(r-(p+q-r),(p+q-r)-(a+2b-p-q))$ as its weight. This gives exact integer [weight multiplicities](weight-multiplicity.md), including repeated weights, without floating-point character evaluation.
