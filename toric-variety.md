# Toric variety

↑ **Parent:** [Toric geometry](toric-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toric_variety)

A toric variety $X_\Sigma$ is obtained by gluing the affine toric varieties associated with the cones of a fan $\Sigma$. It contains an [algebraic torus](algebraic-torus.md) as a dense open orbit.
