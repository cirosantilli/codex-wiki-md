# Pendulum with quadratic damping

↑ **Parent:** [Physical pendulum](physical-pendulum.md)

In nondimensional time the [quadratic damping](quadratic-damping.md) removes energy from the pendulum at rate $-c|\omega|^3$. Downward resting positions are nonhyperbolic asymptotically stable spirals, even though the linearized equation has a centre. Upward resting positions remain [saddle equilibria](saddle-equilibrium.md). Each fixed-sign branch of $\omega$ has a linear equation for $\omega^2$ as a function of angle.
