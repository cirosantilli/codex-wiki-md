# Gout

↑ **Parent:** [Rheumatology](rheumatology.md)

An inflammatory [arthritis](arthritis.md) associated with deposition of [urate crystals](urate-crystal.md) in [anatomical joints](anatomical-joint.md). Historical descriptions of [podagra](podagra.md) require interpretation rather than automatic assignment of every ancient patient to this modern category.
