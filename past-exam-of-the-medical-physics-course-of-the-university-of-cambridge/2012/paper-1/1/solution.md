For the specified energy-loss model, multiply $dE/dx=-k/E$ by $2E$ and integrate from the entrance:

$$
\frac{d(E^2)}{dx}=-2k,\qquad
\boxed{E(x)^2=E_0^2-2kx.}
$$

The positive [stopping power](../../../../stopping-power.md) is $S=-dE/dx=k/E$. The model reaches zero [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) at

$$
\boxed{R=\frac{E_0^2}{2k}=\frac{200^2}{2(770)}\,\mathrm{cm}=25.97\,\mathrm{cm}.}
$$

At the entrance,

$$
\boxed{S(0)=\frac{770}{200}=3.85\,\mathrm{MeV\,cm^{-1}}.}
$$

Half a centimeter before the formal endpoint, $E^2=2k(0.5\,\mathrm{cm})=770\,\mathrm{MeV^2}$, so

$$
\boxed{E=27.75\,\mathrm{MeV},\qquad S=27.75\,\mathrm{MeV\,cm^{-1}}.}
$$

The signed derivatives are the negatives of the quoted [stopping powers](../../../../stopping-power.md). This [inverse-energy stopping-power model](../../../../inverse-energy-stopping-power-model.md) deposits [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) more strongly toward the endpoint. Its divergent endpoint derivative is integrable, giving finite total deposited [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E_0$; a real stopping curve includes finite [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) spread, range straggling, scattering and a more accurate low-energy stopping law. These numbers are consequences of the supplied ideal equation.
