# Distinct Fermat numbers are coprime

↑ **Parent:** [Fermat number product identity](fermat-number-product-identity.md)

If $m<n$, a common [divisor](divisor.md) of $F_m$ and $F_n$ divides both $F_n-2$, by the [Fermat number product identity](fermat-number-product-identity.md), and $F_n$. It therefore divides two. Both [Fermat numbers](fermat-number.md) are [odd integers](odd-integer.md), so their [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) is one. Each term's [prime factors](prime-factor.md) are consequently different from the [prime factors](prime-factor.md) of every other term.
