# Landweber relaxation parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Landweber iteration](landweber-iteration.md)

The relaxation parameter $\tau$ controls the step in [Landweber iteration](landweber-iteration.md). Convergence from zero to the [minimum-norm least-squares solution](minimum-norm-least-squares-solution.md) requires $0<\tau<2/\|A\|^2$. Thus $\|A\|<2$ allows $\tau=1/2$, while unit steps require $\|A\|<\sqrt2$.
