# Neutral-kaon projection interference asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral kaon mixing](neutral-kaon-mixing.md)

In the [exchange-positive CP convention for neutral kaon mixing](exchange-positive-cp-convention-for-neutral-kaon-mixing.md), a coherent source with [decay amplitudes](decay-amplitude.md) $A$ and $B$ for $K^0$ and $\bar K^0$ has projected amplitudes $(A+B)/\sqrt2$ and $(A-B)/\sqrt2$. For equal [phase space](phase-space.md), their [decay-rate asymmetry](decay-rate-asymmetry.md) is $\mathcal R=2\operatorname{Re}r/(1+|r|^2)$, $r=B/A$. Expanding the squared moduli proves the identity. Knowledge of $|r|$ alone cannot fix the asymmetry: $r=i|r|$ gives zero, whereas $r=|r|$ gives constructive interference in the symmetric channel.
