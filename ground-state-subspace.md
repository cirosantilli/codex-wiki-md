# Ground-state subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Ground state](ground-state.md)

The [ground-state subspace](ground-state-subspace.md) is the eigenspace of a [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) at its lowest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $E_0$. A [computational history state](computational-history-state.md) construction can have many valid [quantum witnesses](quantum-witness.md), hence a degenerate [ground space](ground-state-subspace.md). Perturbation bounds must optimize on this entire subspace, rather than on a chosen basis of ground states.
