# Long-range pressure correlations in turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure Poisson equation for incompressible flow](pressure-poisson-equation-for-incompressible-flow.md)

The quadrupolar kernel of the [pressure Poisson equation for incompressible flow](pressure-poisson-equation-for-incompressible-flow.md) allows mixed pressure-velocity correlations with an $r^{-3}$ tail. Its derivative in the evolution of a longitudinal triple correlation allows an $r^{-4}$ tail, potentially changing the [Loitsyansky integral](loitsyansky-integral.md). The existence of a nonlocal kernel does not fix the tail coefficient or prevent cancellations. An isotropic mean pressure multipole can vanish while a mixed higher-order correlation remains nonzero.
