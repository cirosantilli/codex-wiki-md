# Hypergraph container

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph independent set](hypergraph-independent-set.md)

A container family is a collection of vertex subsets such that every [hypergraph independent set](hypergraph-independent-set.md) lies in at least one member. A useful family has few members and each member is small in [hypergraph degree measure](hypergraph-degree-measure.md) or induces few hyperedges. [Container fingerprints](container-fingerprint.md) encode the members and permit bounds on the number of independent sets.
