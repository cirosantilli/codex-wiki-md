# Stagnation temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) reached by bringing a flow to rest adiabatically while converting its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) to enthalpy. For a calorically [perfect gas](ideal-gas-split.md), $T_{\rm stag}=T+u^2/(2c_p)=T[1+(\gamma-1)M^2/2]$. It is conserved across an adiabatic normal shock even though [entropy](entropy-split.md) increases and [stagnation pressure](stagnation-pressure.md) decreases.
