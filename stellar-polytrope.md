# Stellar polytrope

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

A stellar polytrope obeys $P=K\rho^{1+1/n}$ for constant $K$ and polytropic index $n$. Its dimensionless density profile satisfies the [Lane-Emden equation](lane-emden-equation.md).
