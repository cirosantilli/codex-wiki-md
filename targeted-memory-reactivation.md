# Targeted memory reactivation

↑ **Parent:** [Memory reactivation](memory-reactivation.md)

Re-presenting a cue previously associated with learning, often during sleep, to bias subsequent reactivation. Unpaired-cue, waking-cue and sleep-stage controls help distinguish a memory-specific effect from sensory stimulation or arousal.
