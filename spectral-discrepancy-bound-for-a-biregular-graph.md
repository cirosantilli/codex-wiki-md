# Spectral discrepancy bound for a biregular graph

↑ **Parent:** [Quasirandom bipartite graph](quasirandom-bipartite-graph.md)

Let $H=M-\delta J$ be the centered [biadjacency matrix](biadjacency-matrix.md) of an equal-part [biregular graph](biregular-graph.md), and put $s=\|H\|_{\mathrm{op}}$. For subsets $A,B$ of the two parts, their edge discrepancy is $\mathbf1_A^{\mathsf T}H\mathbf1_B$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) proves the displayed bound. If the part size is $n$, centering the two [indicator vectors](indicator-vector.md) improves it to $s n\sqrt{\alpha(1-\alpha)\beta(1-\beta)}$. The [centered four-cycle identity for a biregular graph](centered-four-cycle-identity-for-a-biregular-graph.md) shows that $t_4(G)\leq\delta^4(1+c^4)$ implies $s\leq c\delta n$ for $c\geq0$.
