# Bottom-quark jet tagging

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor tagging](flavor-tagging.md)

[Bottom-quark](bottom-quark.md) tagging identifies [jets](jet-particle-physics.md) compatible with a [bottom](bottom-quark.md) hadron, often through displaced tracks, a secondary decay vertex or a lepton within the [jet](jet-particle-physics.md). The finite hadron lifetime gives the displacement. Efficiencies and mistaken tags from [charm](charm-quark.md) or light [jets](jet-particle-physics.md) must be [calibrated](calorimeter-calibration.md); a [top quark](top-quark.md) itself decays before [hadronization](hadronization.md).
