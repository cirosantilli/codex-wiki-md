# p-local module

↑ **Parent:** [Modular representation theory](modular-representation-theory.md)

A p-local module here is a finite direct sum of [induced representations](induced-representation.md) from [normalizers](normalizer.md) $N_G(Q)$ of nontrivial [p-subgroups](p-subgroup.md) $Q$. This usage includes induction from $G$ itself when $G$ normalizes such a subgroup. [Green correspondence](green-correspondence.md) and induction on vertex order express every finite module, or lattice over a complete valuation ring, as a difference of p-local modules after adjoining [projective modules](projective-module.md).
