# Sylow subgroup of a subgroup from double cosets

↑ **Parent:** [Double coset](double-coset.md)

If $P$ is a [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) of a finite group $G$ and $H\leq G$, some intersection $H\cap xPx^{-1}$ is a Sylow subgroup of $H$. Otherwise every double-coset size

$$
|HxP|=\frac{|H||P|}{|H\cap xPx^{-1}|}
$$

would be divisible by a larger power of $p$ than $|G|$, as would their sum $|G|$.
