# Measurable space

↑ **Parent:** [Measure theory](measure-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurable_space)

A measurable space is a set equipped with a [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) of measurable subsets. A [measure space](measure-space.md) additionally specifies a [measure](measure-split.md) on that sigma-algebra.
