# Black-hole rigidity theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole uniqueness theorem](black-hole-uniqueness-theorem.md)

For a regular analytic stationary asymptotically flat vacuum black hole with a connected nondegenerate horizon, the stationary [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) either generates the horizon or the spacetime admits an additional commuting axial [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md). In the rotating case the horizon generator is $K+\Omega_H\Phi$, where $\Phi$ has closed rotational orbits. Together with the appropriate staticity and [black-hole uniqueness theorem](black-hole-uniqueness-theorem.md), this identifies an equilibrium uncharged collapse remnant with the [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md). The theorem concerns an equilibrium geometry; it does not itself prove that arbitrary dynamical collapse settles to equilibrium.
