# Carrying capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Population dynamics](population-dynamics.md)

A [carrying capacity](carrying-capacity.md) is an abundance scale at which a specified environment's net population growth vanishes. In the [logistic growth equation](logistic-growth-equation.md), positive unharvested populations approach $K$. It depends on the species, resources and environment rather than being an immutable universal population [limit](limit-of-a-function.md).
