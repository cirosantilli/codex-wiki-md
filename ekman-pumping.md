# Ekman pumping

↑ **Parent:** [Ekman transport](ekman-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekman_pumping)

Spatial convergence or divergence of [Ekman transport](ekman-transport.md) drives vertical motion at the base of the Ekman layer. With upward vertical coordinate,

$$
w_E=\widehat{\mathbf z}\mathbin\cdot
\nabla\times\left(\frac{\boldsymbol\tau}{\rho f}\right).
$$
