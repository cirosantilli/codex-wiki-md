# Entanglement witness

↑ **Parent:** [Separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entanglement_witness)

An entanglement witness is a [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md) $W$ such that $\operatorname{Tr}(W\rho)\geq0$ for every [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md) $\rho$, but $\operatorname{Tr}(W\sigma)<0$ for at least one [entangled state](entangled-state.md) $\sigma$.
