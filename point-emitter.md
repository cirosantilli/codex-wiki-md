# Point emitter

↑ **Parent:** [Radiant intensity](radiant-intensity.md)

An ideal [point emitter](point-emitter.md) emits [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) from one location. It approximates a physical source when its emitting dimensions are small relative to the distances relevant to the calculation. With [isotropy](isotropy.md), its [radiant intensity](radiant-intensity.md) is $I=\Phi/(4\pi)$ for total [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) $\Phi$. An extended source generally requires integrating contributions from different emitting positions.
