# Conditional measures of a factor

↑ **Parent:** [Disintegration theorem for a probability measure](disintegration-theorem-for-a-probability-measure.md)

The fiber-supported [measurable probability kernel](measurable-probability-kernel.md) supplied by the [disintegration theorem for a probability measure](disintegration-theorem-for-a-probability-measure.md) for a [factor map](factor-map-between-measure-preserving-systems.md). For integrable $f$, $\mathbb E[f\mid\varphi^{-1}\mathcal B_Y](x)=\int f\,d\mu_{\varphi(x)}$ [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md). The same construction applies to general measurable maps and countably generated sub-sigma-algebras modulo null sets.
