# Tetrad postulate

↑ **Parent:** [Spin connection](spin-connection.md)

The [tetrad postulate](tetrad-postulate.md) expresses compatibility of the spacetime and Lorentz-frame connections on a [vierbein](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md). For the inverse coframe $e_\nu{}^b$, solving gives $\omega_a{}^\mu{}_{\nu}=e_\nu{}^b(\Gamma^c{}_{ab}e^\mu{}_c-\partial_a e^\mu{}_b)$. [Metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) makes the lowered connection antisymmetric in its Lorentz indices. Lifting this connection through the [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md) defines the [spinor covariant derivative](spinor-covariant-derivative.md).
