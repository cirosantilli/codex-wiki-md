# Radial dilation tangency to homogeneous zero sets

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field](vector-field.md)

Differentiate the displayed homogeneity relation in $\lambda$ at $1$. On every regular point of $H=0$, the radial [vector field](vector-field.md) $x$ is therefore perpendicular to the normal [gradient](gradient.md) and tangent to the level surface. Its flow $x(t)=e^t x(0)$ preserves that zero set. At a singular vertex this flow formulation remains meaningful even if there is no tangent plane.
