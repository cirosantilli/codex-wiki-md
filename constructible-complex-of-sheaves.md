# Constructible complex of sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Derived category of sheaves](derived-category-of-sheaves.md)

A bounded complex is constructible for a given stratification when each of its [cohomology sheaves](cohomology-sheaf.md) restricts to a finite-rank [local system](local-system.md) on every stratum. Constructibility concerns [cohomology sheaves](cohomology-sheaf.md), not an arbitrary choice of resolution.
