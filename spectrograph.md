# Spectrograph

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrometer](spectrometer.md)

A spectrograph records the [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) dispersed by an instrument. Its [spectral resolving power](spectral-resolving-power.md) describes which nearby wavelengths it separates.
