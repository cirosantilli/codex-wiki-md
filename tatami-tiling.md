# Tatami tiling

↑ **Parent:** [Tessellation](tessellation.md)

A [tessellation](tessellation.md) by rectangular tiles in which no point is a corner of four tiles. In the common lattice version, the tiles are $1\times2$ [rectangles](rectangle.md). The local corner restriction makes existence depend on more than the total [area](surface-area.md).
