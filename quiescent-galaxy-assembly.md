# Quiescent galaxy assembly

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy evolution](galaxy-evolution.md)

A [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) with negligible present [star formation](star-formation.md) can still gain [stars](star.md) through mergers. Stellar mass assembly and in-situ [star formation](star-formation.md) therefore describe different histories; quenching the latter does not freeze the former.
