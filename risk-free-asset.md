# Risk-free asset

↑ **Parent:** [Financial asset](financial-asset.md)

For a fixed horizon, a [risk-free asset](risk-free-asset.md) has a deterministic terminal payoff. If the price of a unit terminal payoff is $B>0$, its gross return is $R=1/B$. In a finite state market, the sum of the [Arrow state prices](arrow-debreu-state-price.md) is $B$. Risk-free does not mean that a longer-maturity bond has a deterministic resale price before its maturity.
