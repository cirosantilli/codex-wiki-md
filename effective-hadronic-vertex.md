# Effective hadronic vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A vertex written in terms of composite [hadron](hadron.md) fields, with a coupling or form factor that summarizes unresolved [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md). For a strong [Delta baryon](delta-baryon.md) decay, a $\Delta p\pi$ vertex describes the external channel but does not assert that the decay is perturbative at constituent-quark scales.
