<h1 id="littlewood-richardson-coefficient">Littlewood–Richardson coefficient</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Littlewood–Richardson rule](littlewood-richardson-rule.md)

The nonnegative integer $c_{\alpha\beta}^\nu$ is the multiplicity of $S^\nu$ in $\operatorname{Ind}_{S_{|\alpha|}\times S_{|\beta|}}^{S_{|\nu|}}(S^\alpha\boxtimes S^\beta)$ in characteristic zero. The [Littlewood–Richardson rule](littlewood-richardson-rule.md) counts it by [Semistandard Young tableaux](semistandard-young-tableau.md) of skew shape $\nu/\alpha$, content $\beta$, and [lattice word](lattice-word.md) in right-to-left, top-to-bottom reading order. For example $c_{(3,2,1),(3,2)}^{(5,3,2,1)}=3$: the two new top cells must be ones, and the remaining reading letters can be $122,212,221$. This coefficient also multiplies [Schur functions](schur-polynomial.md) in the [induction ring of symmetric-group characters](induction-ring-of-symmetric-group-characters.md).
