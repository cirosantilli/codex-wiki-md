# Chebyshev estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Second Chebyshev function](second-chebyshev-function.md)

The Chebyshev estimate is

$$
\psi(x)\asymp x.
$$

It gives upper and lower bounds of the correct linear order without asserting the [Prime number theorem](prime-number-theorem.md) asymptotic $\psi(x)\sim x$.
