# Elementary totient-ratio lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md)

List the $k$ distinct prime divisors of $r$ in increasing order. The $j$th is at least $j+1$, and $k\leq\log_2r$. The [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md) product formula gives $\varphi(r)/r\geq\prod_{j=1}^k j/(j+1)=1/(k+1)$. This elementary bound is sufficient to show inverse-polynomial success probability for coprime phase labels in [quantum order finding](quantum-order-finding.md).
