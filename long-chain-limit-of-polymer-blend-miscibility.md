# Long-chain limit of polymer-blend miscibility

↑ **Parent:** [Binary Flory-Huggins polymer blend](binary-flory-huggins-polymer-blend.md)

Connecting segments into chains reduces their independent translational mixing [entropy](entropy-split.md) per segment by factors $1/N_A$ and $1/N_B$. Consequently the tolerable positive contact penalty tends to zero as both chains become long. Equal chain [lengths](length.md) have $\chi_c=2/N$; favorable unlike interactions can still produce a miscible [polymer](polymer-split.md) pair.
