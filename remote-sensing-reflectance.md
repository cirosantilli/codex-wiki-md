# Remote-sensing reflectance

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean colour](ocean-colour.md)

The ratio of water-leaving [radiance](radiance.md) to incident downwelling [irradiance](irradiance.md) just above the surface. It depends on both absorption and backscattering and has units of inverse steradian.
