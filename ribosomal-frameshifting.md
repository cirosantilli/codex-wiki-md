# Ribosomal frameshifting

↑ **Parent:** [Reading frame](reading-frame.md)

A change of reading frame during [biological translation](translation-biology.md). It need not change the underlying [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence, unlike a [frameshift mutation](frameshift-mutation.md). It can be programmed by a recoding signal or occur as a translation error.
