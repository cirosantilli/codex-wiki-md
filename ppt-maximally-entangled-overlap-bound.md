# PPT maximally entangled overlap bound

↑ **Parent:** [Positive partial transpose](positive-partial-transpose.md)

Every [density operator](density-matrix.md) $\sigma$ with [positive partial transpose](positive-partial-transpose.md) has squared [quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) at most $1/d$ with a fixed [maximally entangled state](maximally-entangled-state.md) on $d\otimes d$. The [partial transpose of a maximally entangled projector](partial-transpose-of-a-maximally-entangled-projector.md) and the [trace-adjoint identity for partial transpose](trace-adjoint-identity-for-partial-transpose.md) express that squared overlap as $\operatorname{Tr}(S\sigma^{T_B})/d$. Since $\sigma^{T_B}$ is positive of [trace](matrix-trace.md) one and the [swap operator](swap-operator.md) has [operator norm](operator-norm.md) one, [trace duality](trace-duality.md) gives the bound. A matching [product state](product-state.md) attains equality.
