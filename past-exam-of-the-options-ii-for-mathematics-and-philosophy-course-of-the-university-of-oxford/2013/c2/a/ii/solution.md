The [first supplementary law for quadratic reciprocity](../../../../../first-supplementary-law-for-quadratic-reciprocity.md) is

$$
\boxed{(-1/p)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}=\begin{cases}1,&p\equiv1\pmod4,\\-1,&p\equiv3\pmod4.\end{cases}}
$$

It also follows immediately from [Euler's criterion](../../../../../euler-s-criterion.md) applied to $-1$.
