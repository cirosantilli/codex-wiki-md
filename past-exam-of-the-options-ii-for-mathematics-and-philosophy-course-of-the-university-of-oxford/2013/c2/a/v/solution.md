↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For distinct [odd primes](../../../../../odd-prime.md) $p,q$, the [quadratic reciprocity](../../../../../quadratic-reciprocity.md) law is

$$
\boxed{(p/q)(q/p)=(-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2}\frac{q-1}{2}}.}
$$

Thus the two symbols have opposite signs exactly when both primes are three modulo four; otherwise they agree. Distinctness excludes zero symbols.
