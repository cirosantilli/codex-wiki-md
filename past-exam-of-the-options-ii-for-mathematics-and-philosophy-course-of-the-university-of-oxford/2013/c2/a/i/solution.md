<h1 id="c2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [odd prime](../../../../../odd-prime.md) $p$, the [Legendre symbol](../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) is zero if $p\mid a$, one if $a$ is a nonzero [quadratic residue](../../../../../quadratic-residue-split.md) modulo $p$, and minus one if it is a [quadratic nonresidue](../../../../../quadratic-nonresidue.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{(a/p)\in\{0,1,-1\}.}
$$

It depends only on the congruence class of $a$ modulo $p$.
