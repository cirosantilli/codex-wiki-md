<h1 id="f2/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For an increment $h$, direct expansion and the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) give

$$
\boxed{DF_x(h)=Ah,\qquad Dg_x(h)=2x^Th,\qquad Dk_x(h)=2(Ax)^TAh=2x^TA^TAh.}
$$

For example, $\|x+h\|^2-\|x\|^2=2x^Th+\|h\|^2$, so the quadratic remainder divided by $\|h\|$ tends to zero. The corresponding [gradients](../../../../../gradient.md) are $\nabla g(x)=2x$ and $\nabla k(x)=2A^TAx$; the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of $F$ is the constant [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $A$.
