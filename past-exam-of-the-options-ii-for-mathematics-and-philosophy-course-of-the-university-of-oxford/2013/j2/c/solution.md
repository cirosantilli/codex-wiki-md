<h1 id="j2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Gaussian integral](../../../../gaussian-integral.md) gives $\int_{\mathbb R^3}e^{-\gamma|y|^2}\,d^3y=(\pi/\gamma)^{3/2}$. Thus the normalized [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) is, up to an overall phase,

$$
\boxed{\psi_0(r)=\left(\frac{m\omega}{\pi\hbar}\right)^{3/4}
\exp\left[-\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}\left|r-\frac{q\mathcal E}{m\omega^2}\right|^2\right].}
$$

Its [probability](../../../../probability.md) density is even in each shifted coordinate, so all three expectations $\langle y_i\rangle$ vanish by oddness. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\langle r\rangle=\frac{q\mathcal E}{m\omega^2}.}
$$

The mean position is the minimum of the displaced potential, rather than the original coordinate origin.
