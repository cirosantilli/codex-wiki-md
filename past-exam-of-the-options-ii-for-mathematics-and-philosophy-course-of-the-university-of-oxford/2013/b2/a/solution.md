↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [orbit](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) of $s$ is $O_s=\{gs:g\in G\}$, and its [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is $G_s=\{g\in G:gs=s\}$. The identity fixes $s$; if $g,h$ fix it then $(gh)s=g(hs)=s$; and $gs=s$ implies $g^{-1}s=s$. Thus **$G_s$ is a [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md)**.

A [group action](../../../../group-action-split.md) is [transitive](../../../../transitive-group-action.md) when it has just one [orbit](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md): for every $s,t\in S$ there is $g\in G$ with $gs=t$.
