↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [symmetric group](../../../../../symmetric-group.md) $S_3$ has [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md) of orders $1,2,3,6$. There is one trivial [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md), three conjugate [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md) generated by transpositions, one order-three [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $A_3$, and the whole [group](../../../../../group-split.md). These are all [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md) by [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md). Thus again there is one conjugacy class for each index $6,3,2,1$.

The [coset action](../../../../../coset-action.md) classification in part (c) supplies exactly one transitive [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) type of each of these sizes. The same orbit-multiplicity equation as in part (i) applies, giving

$$
\boxed{8\text{ inequivalent actions}.}
$$

The equality of the two counts comes from their identical lists of transitive [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) types, rather than from the [groups](../../../../../group-split.md) being isomorphic.
