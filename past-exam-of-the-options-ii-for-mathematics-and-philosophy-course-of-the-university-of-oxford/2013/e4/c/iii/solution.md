<h1 id="e4/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $A$ is disconnected, take a separation $A=A_1\cup A_2$. Each piece is also relatively closed, being the complement of the other. They are [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) as closed subsets of the [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) space $A$.

Part (b), using the ambient Hausdorff property, gives disjoint ambient open neighborhoods of $A_1$ and $A_2$. They meet $A$, cover it, and are disjoint everywhere, as required. Conversely, such ambient [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) give a separation on intersecting with $A$, by part (ii). Hence **[compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md) and the Hausdorff property permit fully disjoint ambient neighborhoods**.
