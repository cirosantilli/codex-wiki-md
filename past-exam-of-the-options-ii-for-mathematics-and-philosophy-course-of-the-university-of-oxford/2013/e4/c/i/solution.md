↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md) is **[connected](../../../../../connected-space.md)** if it cannot be written as the union of two disjoint nonempty open subsets. Equivalently, its only subsets that are both open and closed are the empty set and the whole space. Under this definition the empty space is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md).
