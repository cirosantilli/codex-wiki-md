<h1 id="e4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $A$ is disconnected, a separation writes it as $A=A_1\cup A_2$ with $A_1,A_2$ disjoint, nonempty and open in its [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md). By definition there are ambient [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) $U,V$ with $A_1=U\cap A$ and $A_2=V\cap A$. These give exactly the four conditions stated.

Conversely, those conditions make $U\cap A,V\cap A$ two nonempty disjoint relatively [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) whose union is $A$. They are therefore a separation. This proves the equivalence. **The ambient sets may overlap outside $A$**, which is why the condition here is $U\cap V\cap A=\varnothing$.
