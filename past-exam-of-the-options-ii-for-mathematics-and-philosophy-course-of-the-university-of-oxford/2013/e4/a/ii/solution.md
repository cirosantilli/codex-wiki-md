<h1 id="e4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If the intersection were empty, the open complements $X\setminus F_i$ would cover the [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) space. A finite subcover would give an empty finite intersection of the corresponding $F_i$. But the sets are nested, so any finite intersection is the set with largest index, which is nonempty. This contradiction proves

$$
\boxed{\bigcap_iF_i\ne\varnothing.}
$$

This is the nested-closed-set form of the [finite intersection property](../../../../../finite-intersection-property.md) for [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) spaces.
