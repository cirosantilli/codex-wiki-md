<h1 id="d4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [null set](../../../../../null-set.md) $E\subseteq\mathbb R$ has Lebesgue outer measure zero. Equivalently, for every $\epsilon>0$ it can be covered by countably many open intervals whose total length is less than $\epsilon$. This definition also applies before assuming $E$ measurable; every such set is Lebesgue measurable with measure zero.
