For the [Poisson distribution](../../../../poisson-distribution.md), summing the exponential series gives

$$
\boxed{G_X(s)=e^{-\lambda}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\lambda s)^k}{k!}
=e^{\lambda(s-1)}.}
$$

The series is absolutely convergent for every real or complex $s$; its [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) at one is $\lambda$, the expected offspring count.
