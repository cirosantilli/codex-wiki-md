<h1 id="n2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Inside its radius of convergence a power series can be differentiated term by term. Hence

$$
\boxed{p_k=\frac{G_X^{(k)}(0)}{k!}\qquad(k\geq0).}
$$

Every mass is therefore recovered uniquely from the [function](../../../../function-split.md), proving that the generating [function](../../../../function-split.md) determines the entire distribution. No [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md) at the potentially delicate endpoint one are needed.
