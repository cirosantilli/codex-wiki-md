# Binomial likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md)

If $x$ successes occur in $n$ independent Bernoulli trials, the binomial likelihood for the success probability $p$ is proportional to $p^x(1-p)^{n-x}$.
