# Algebraic exceptional divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Blowing up (algebraic geometry)](blowing-up-algebraic-geometry.md)

The divisor contracted by a [blowup of an algebraic variety](blowing-up-algebraic-geometry.md) to its centre. For the blowup of a smooth point on an $n$-dimensional smooth variety it is $\mathbb P^{n-1}$. Being a closed projective subvariety of positive dimension, it can obstruct affineness of a fibre containing it.
