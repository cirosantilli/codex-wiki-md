# Flux tube

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field line](magnetic-field-line.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flux_tube)

A flux tube is a bundle of [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md) bounded by a surface everywhere tangent to the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) makes the [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) through any transverse cross-section constant along the tube. For a narrow tube with nearly uniform field magnitude $B$ and transverse area $A$, this gives $BA=\mathrm{constant}$.
