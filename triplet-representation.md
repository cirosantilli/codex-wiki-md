# Triplet representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A three-dimensional representation. The isospin-one irreducible representation of SU(2) is a [triplet](triplet-representation.md) with third components $-1,0,+1$; the fundamental SU(3) [quark](quark.md) representation is also a [triplet](triplet-representation.md) but belongs to a different group.
