# Closedness of projection from projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Projective morphism](projective-morphism.md)

Projection $X\times\mathbb P^n\to X$ sends closed subsets to closed subsets, with geometric points or schemes understood. On an affine base, choose finitely many homogeneous equations for a closed subset. Its fiber is nonempty exactly when every [graded multiplication map for a projective fiber](graded-multiplication-map-for-a-projective-fiber.md) fails to be surjective, by the [Projective Nullstellensatz](projective-nullstellensatz.md). The image is thus an intersection of closed rank-defect loci. An affine open cover proves the general case. This is the universally closed part of the [proper morphism](proper-morphism.md) property of projective space.
