# Linear Boltzmann equation

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md)

A linear kinetic transport equation with velocity-integral gain $K_t$ and nonnegative loss coefficient $a$. Its characteristic integral formulation combines the [damped free-transport evolution](damped-free-transport-evolution.md) with the [Boltzmann Volterra operator](boltzmann-volterra-operator.md). A uniform square-integrable velocity kernel yields bounded gain on $L^2_{x,v}$ and a globally convergent [Volterra series for the linear Boltzmann equation](volterra-series-for-the-linear-boltzmann-equation.md) on every finite time interval.
