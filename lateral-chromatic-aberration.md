# Lateral chromatic aberration

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic aberration](chromatic-aberration.md)

[Lateral chromatic aberration](lateral-chromatic-aberration.md), also called [transverse chromatic aberration](lateral-chromatic-aberration.md), is colour-dependent image scale: an object away from the [optical axis](optical-axis.md) can have different image heights at different [wavelengths](wavelength.md). Its changing [transverse magnification](transverse-magnification.md) produces displaced coloured edges. The on-axis parallel bundle in the [chromatic aberration](chromatic-aberration.md) diagram illustrates [axial chromatic aberration](axial-chromatic-aberration.md), and does not measure [lateral chromatic aberration](lateral-chromatic-aberration.md). [Nikon, wavelength-dependent focal point and magnification](https://imaging.nikon.com/sport-optics/guide/fieldscopes/choosing/choosing_03/).
