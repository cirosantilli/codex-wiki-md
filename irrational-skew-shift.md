# Irrational skew shift

↑ **Parent:** [Skew product](skew-product.md)

For irrational $\alpha$, the irrational skew shift is the [skew product](skew-product.md) $T_\alpha(x,y)=(x+\alpha,y+x)$ on the [torus](torus.md) $(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)^2$. It preserves normalized [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) and has iterates $T_\alpha^n(x,y)=(x+n\alpha,y+nx+\alpha n(n-1)/2)$ modulo one. Its [Koopman operator](koopman-operator.md) sends the [Fourier basis](fourier-basis.md) character $e_{r,s}=e^{2\pi i(rx+sy)}$ to $e^{2\pi ir\alpha}e_{r+s,s}$. The resulting infinite chains of [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) show that it is an [ergodic transformation](ergodicity.md). Moreover, [uniform equidistribution of an irrational skew shift](uniform-equidistribution-of-an-irrational-skew-shift.md) proves [unique ergodicity](unique-ergodicity.md).
