# Serial dictatorship

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanism design](mechanism-design.md)

An allocation procedure using a fixed priority order: each participant in turn selects one remaining object, which is permanently assigned to them. For participants whose [utilities](utility.md) depend only on their own assigned objects, strict rankings give a unique best available choice and the resulting allocation is [Pareto optimal](pareto-efficiency.md). If a proposed [Pareto improvement](pareto-improvement.md) changes an assignment, its earliest changed participant loses their unique best available object. With ties or [allocation externalities](allocation-externality.md), that argument requires additional assumptions.
