# Scaling invariance of a Bessel process

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel process](bessel-process.md)

If $X$ is a Bessel process of dimension $d$ started at $x$ and $r>0$, then

$$
(rX_{t/r^2})_{t\geq0}
$$

is a Bessel process of dimension $d$ started at $rx$. This follows from [Brownian scaling](brownian-scaling.md) in the stochastic differential equation and [uniqueness in law](uniqueness-in-law.md).
