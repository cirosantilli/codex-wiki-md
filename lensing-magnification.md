# Lensing magnification

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing.md)

The signed magnification is the reciprocal determinant of the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of the [thin gravitational lens equation](thin-gravitational-lens-equation.md). Its absolute value gives the flux and solid-angle amplification; its sign gives image parity. [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing.md) preserves [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) in the geometric-optics approximation.
