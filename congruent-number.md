# Congruent number

↑ **Parent:** [Congruent number elliptic curve](congruent-number-elliptic-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congruent_number)

A positive integer is a congruent number when it is the area of a right triangle with rational side lengths. For square-free $D$, this is equivalent to the [congruent number elliptic curve](congruent-number-elliptic-curve.md) having a [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md) of positive rank.
