# Quadratic Ornstein-Uhlenbeck state-price density

↑ **Parent:** [State-price density](state-price-density.md)

For an [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md) $dX=\sigma dB-\lambda Xdt$ under a reference measure, this is a positive [supermartingale](supermartingale.md) when $\alpha a\geq\sigma^2/2$. Its [short rate](short-rate.md) is $[\alpha a-\sigma^2/2+(\lambda+\alpha/2)X^2]/(a+X^2/2)$. Conditional [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) prices are ratios of the OU second moments of the quadratic factor. The money-market density has diffusion coefficient $\sigma X/(a+X^2/2)$, bounded by $\sigma/\sqrt{2a}$, so the [Novikov condition](novikov-s-condition.md) validates the pricing measure. The reference OU dynamics must not be silently treated as the risk-neutral dynamics.
