# Polar equation of a Kepler orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

For an attractive [inverse-square force](inverse-square-force.md), $r=p/(1+e\cos f)$, where $f$ is [true anomaly](true-anomaly.md), $e$ is [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md), and $p=h^2/\mu$ is the semilatus rectum. The [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) relation $\dot f=h/r^2$ converts this geometric equation into an angular evolution law.
