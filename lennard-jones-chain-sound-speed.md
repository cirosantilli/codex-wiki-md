# Lennard-Jones chain sound speed

↑ **Parent:** [Monatomic-chain dispersion relation](monatomic-chain-dispersion-relation.md)

A nearest-neighbour chain with the [Lennard-Jones potential](lennard-jones-potential.md) $V(x)=4\epsilon[(\sigma/x)^{12}-(\sigma/x)^6]$ has equilibrium spacing $a=2^{1/6}\sigma$ and [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $K=V''(a)=72\epsilon/a^2$. The [monatomic-chain dispersion relation](monatomic-chain-dispersion-relation.md) gives [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md) $v=a\sqrt{K/m}=\sqrt{72\epsilon/m}$ and maximum [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $2v/a$. The cancellation of $\sigma$ from the sound speed holds in this chain model; it does not remove the spacing from the cutoff frequency.
