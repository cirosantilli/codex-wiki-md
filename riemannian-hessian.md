# Riemannian Hessian

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian geometry](riemannian-geometry-split.md)

The symmetric covariant two-tensor defined using the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) by $\operatorname{Hess}_g f(X,Y)=X(Yf)-(\nabla_XY)f$. Its negative [metric trace](metric-trace.md) is the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) applied to $f$. Along a [geodesic](geodesic.md) it gives the second derivative of $f$.
