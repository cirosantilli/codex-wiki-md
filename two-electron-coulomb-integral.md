# Two-electron Coulomb integral

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock method](hartree-fock-method.md)

The electrostatic repulsion integral $J_{pq}=\iint|\phi_p(1)|^2|\phi_q(2)|^2r_{12}^{-1}\,d1\,d2$ in [atomic units](atomic-units.md). For a normalized hydrogenic 1s orbital of exponent $\zeta$, the same-orbital value is $5\zeta/8$. It is a two-electron integral, unlike a diagonal Hückel one-electron parameter.
