# Constancy of line bundle degree in a family

↑ **Parent:** [Semicontinuity theorem for coherent cohomology](semicontinuity-theorem-for-coherent-cohomology.md)

For a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $M$ on $C\times T$, with $C$ a smooth projective curve, this function is locally constant. Twist both $M$ and $M^{-1}$ by a large power of an [ample line bundle](ample-line-bundle.md) on $C$. Near a chosen fiber their first [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) groups vanish. The [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) and upper semicontinuity of their zeroth [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) groups then bound the degree from above and below by the degree on that fiber. Connectedness of $T$ is necessary for a single constant across all fibers.
