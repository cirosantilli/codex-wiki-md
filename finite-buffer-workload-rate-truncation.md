# Finite-buffer workload rate truncation

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-buffer workload map](finite-buffer-workload-map.md)

For the local convex action defining the [linear workload rate for a local convex action](linear-workload-rate-for-a-local-convex-action.md), continuity and the [contraction principle for large deviations](contraction-principle-for-large-deviations.md) give workload [rate functions](rate-function.md). The pointwise bound $\bar q_B\leq q$ makes $\bar J_B(x)\geq\inf_{y\geq x}J(y)=J(x)$ on nonnegative workloads. Constant-rate burst paths produce the reverse bound for x at most B. Values outside the buffer interval are impossible. This statement concerns the rate at the workload value, rather than equality of exact finite-buffer and infinite-buffer stationary distributions.
