# Primitive lattice translation vector

↑ **Parent:** [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md)

A member of a set of [linearly independent vectors](linear-independence.md) whose [integer](integer-split.md) combinations generate every [lattice point](lattice-point.md) of a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md). Such [basis vectors](basis-vector.md) specify translations; an [atomic basis of a crystal](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) specifies the chemical motif repeated under them.
