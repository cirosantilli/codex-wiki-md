# Real conjugacy class

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md)

A [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) is real when it is stable under [inversion of conjugacy classes](inversion-of-conjugacy-classes.md). Equivalently, one, and therefore every, member is conjugate to its inverse. Members need not themselves be [involutions](involution.md).
