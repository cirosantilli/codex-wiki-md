# Cavitation criterion for power-law strain energy

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic cavitation](elastic-cavitation.md)

For [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) $W=\mu[(\lambda_r^2+\lambda_\theta^2+\lambda_\phi^2)^\alpha-3^\alpha]/2$ with $\mu>0$ and $\alpha>0$, the critical loading for [elastic cavitation](elastic-cavitation.md) is finite exactly in the displayed range. To prove this, put $S=\lambda^{-4}+2\lambda^2$ in the reduced [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md). The critical-pressure integrand is $2\mu\alpha S^{\alpha-1}(\lambda^{-2}+\lambda^{-5})$. It is finite at $\lambda=1$ and asymptotic to $\mu\alpha2^\alpha\lambda^{2\alpha-4}$ at infinity. The [comparison test](direct-comparison-test.md) for the resulting [improper integral](improper-integral.md) gives $2\alpha-4<-1$.
