# Bank account

↑ **Parent:** [Interest rate](interest-rate.md)

In a discrete-time market, the bank account reinvests at each successive [spot interest rate](spot-interest-rate.md):

$$
B_0=1,
\qquad B_t=\prod_{s=0}^{t-1}(1+r_s).
$$
