# Explicit cubic SU3 tensor projections

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed SU(3) tensor representation](mixed-su-3-tensor-representation.md)

For $T^{abc}$, split its first pair into symmetric and antisymmetric parts. The fully symmetric part $S^{abc}$ is a [symmetric cubic representation of SU(3)](symmetric-cubic-representation-of-su-3.md). Write $M^{abc}=T^{(ab)c}-S^{abc}$ and $O^a{}_d=\epsilon_{dbc}M^{abc}$. Then $O$ is traceless and $M^{abc}=(\epsilon^{bcd}O^a{}_d+\epsilon^{acd}O^b{}_d)/3$. The antisymmetric part is $T^{[ab]c}=\epsilon^{abd}N^c{}_d$, with $N^c{}_d=\epsilon_{dab}T^{abc}/2$. Splitting $N$ into its traceless part and trace gives the second [adjoint representation of SU(3)](adjoint-representation-of-su-3.md) and the singlet. These maps use [invariant tensors](invariant-tensor.md), so they commute with the group action and explicitly realize the four irreducible summands.
