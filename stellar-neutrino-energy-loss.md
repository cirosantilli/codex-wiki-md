# Stellar neutrino energy loss

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar energy balance equation](stellar-energy-balance-equation.md)

Escaping [neutrinos](neutrino.md) remove energy produced by nuclear reactions or drawn from thermal reservoirs. Pair annihilation, plasma excitations, photoneutrino emission and electron-ion bremsstrahlung provide thermal losses. This reduces photon [luminosity](luminosity.md) or increases the required nuclear and gravothermal supply. At sufficiently extreme collapse densities, [neutrino](neutrino.md) transport must replace a free-escape loss approximation.
