# Scalar wave separation in Kerr spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md)

For the massless [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) on a [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md), the [covariant wave operator](covariant-wave-operator.md) separates into radial and angular [ordinary differential equations](ordinary-differential-equation.md). With $K=(r^2+a^2)\omega-am$ and angular [separation constant](separation-constant.md) $\Lambda$, the radial equation is $(\Delta R')'+[K^2/\Delta-a^2\omega^2+2am\omega-\Lambda]R=0$. The angular equation is $(\sin\theta\,\Theta')'/\sin\theta+[a^2\omega^2\cos^2\theta-m^2/\sin^2\theta+\Lambda]\Theta=0$. No extra radial factor is included in the displayed ansatz.
