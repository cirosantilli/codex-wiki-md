# Brownian radius estimate from endpoint displacements

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes–Einstein relation](stokes-einstein-relation.md)

For $n$ independent three-dimensional [displacements](displacement.md) of identical no-slip spheres over lag $t$, the Gaussian [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) likelihood gives the displayed maximum-likelihood [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) and inverse-drag [radius](radius.md) estimate. The [Stokes–Einstein relation](stokes-einstein-relation.md) assumes an unbounded Newtonian solvent, equilibrium and negligible interparticle coupling. Use squared distances, rather than squaring the [mean](expected-value-split.md) distance. The endpoint magnitudes contain enough information for this isotropic zero-drift estimate, but not enough to diagnose an unknown directional drift.
