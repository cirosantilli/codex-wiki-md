# Local unit of a local field

↑ **Parent:** [Unit group](unit-group.md)

A [local unit](local-unit-of-a-local-field.md) has valuation zero in the local [field](field.md), or equivalently is an invertible element of its valuation ring. A [principal unit](principal-unit.md) additionally reduces to one in the residue [field](field.md). The distinction between [local units](local-unit-of-a-local-field.md) and [principal units](principal-unit.md) retains the prime-to-p residue [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) in a cyclotomic p-adic [field](field.md).
