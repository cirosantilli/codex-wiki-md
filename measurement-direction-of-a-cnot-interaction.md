# Measurement direction of a CNOT interaction

↑ **Parent:** [CNOT control reversal in the Hadamard basis](cnot-control-reversal-in-the-hadamard-basis.md)

A [CNOT gate](controlled-not-gate.md) can correlate either the original control's computational label with a computational pointer, or the original target's sign-basis label with a sign-basis pointer. For the second interpretation prepare the original control in $|+\rangle$ and read it in the [Hadamard basis](hadamard-basis.md). Its two pointer states become $|+\rangle$ and $|-\rangle$, measuring $X$ on the original target. An apparatus role therefore depends on the ready state and readout as well as the [measurement interaction](measurement-interaction.md).
