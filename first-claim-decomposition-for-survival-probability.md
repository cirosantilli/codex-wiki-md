# First-claim decomposition for survival probability

↑ **Parent:** [Survival renewal equation for a classical risk model](survival-renewal-equation-for-a-classical-risk-model.md)

In a [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md), before the first claim at time $t$, available capital is $u+ct$. A claim of size $x\le u+ct$ leaves future survival probability $\varphi(u+ct-x)$ by the [Markov property](markov-property.md). Integrating over the independent first-arrival [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) and claim density gives $\varphi(u)=\int_0^\infty\lambda e^{-\lambda t}\int_0^{u+ct}\varphi(u+ct-x)f(x)\,dx\,dt$.
