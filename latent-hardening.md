# Latent hardening

↑ **Parent:** [Work hardening](work-hardening.md)

Increase in the resistance of one [slip system](slip-system.md) caused by plastic shear on another. It can shift the onset of secondary slip away from a purely geometric equal-Schmid-factor prediction.
