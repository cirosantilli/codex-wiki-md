# Even-degree hyperelliptic model

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelliptic curve](hyperelliptic-curve.md)

If $y^2=f(x)$ with $\deg f=2n$, the coordinates

$$
u=x^{-1},\qquad v=yx^{-n}
$$

give the second chart

$$
v^2=u^{2n}f(u^{-1}).
$$

There are two points $P_+,P_-$ above infinity.
