# Self-intersection of the diagonal of a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Self-intersection number](self-intersection-number.md)

For a smooth projective [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) $C$, the [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) of its diagonal in $C\times C$ is $TC$: on the diagonal the quotient of $TC\oplus TC$ by the diagonal subbundle is identified with $TC$ by taking the difference. The [self-intersection formula](self-intersection-formula.md) therefore gives $\Delta_C^2=\deg TC=2-2g(C)$.
