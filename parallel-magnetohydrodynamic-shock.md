# Parallel magnetohydrodynamic shock

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic shock](magnetohydrodynamic-shock.md)

In a planar [magnetohydrodynamic shock](magnetohydrodynamic-shock.md) with both [velocity](velocity.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) normal to the front, the normal [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) is continuous. Its normal [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md) contribution is consequently the same on both sides, and the [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) vanishes because $\mathbf u\times\mathbf B=0$. The remaining jump conditions are the ordinary [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions for a perfect gas](rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md). This is the purely parallel branch; an oblique or switch-on shock has additional transverse fields and is not covered by this reduction.
