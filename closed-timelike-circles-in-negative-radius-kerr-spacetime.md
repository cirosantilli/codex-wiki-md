# Closed timelike circles in negative-radius Kerr spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Closed timelike curve](closed-timelike-curve.md)

On the equatorial plane of the [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md), the displayed component is negative for sufficiently small negative $r$. A curve of constant $t,r,\theta=\pi/2$, with $\phi$ increasing through $2\pi$, is therefore a regular [closed timelike curve](closed-timelike-curve.md). It avoids the ring at $r=0$. Such circles occur on the negative-radius sheet of the exact analytic extension, not in the exterior region accessible from positive-radius infinity.
