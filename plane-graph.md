# Plane graph

↑ **Parent:** [Planar graph](planar-graph.md)

A [plane graph](plane-graph.md) is a [planar graph](planar-graph.md) together with a specified crossing-free embedding in the plane. Its faces are the connected complementary regions, including the unbounded region. Face counts and facial boundary walks refer to this embedding. For a finite connected [plane graph](plane-graph.md), the [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md) is $v-e+f=2$: deleting a cycle edge lowers both edge and face counts by one until a [tree](tree-graph-theory.md) remains, where $e=v-1$ and $f=1$. The distinction between an abstract [planar graph](planar-graph.md) and its chosen embedding matters when discussing which edges bound a face.
