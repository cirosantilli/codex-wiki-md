# Plane graph

↑ **Parent:** [Planar graph](planar-graph.md)

A [plane graph](plane-graph.md) is a [planar graph](planar-graph.md) with a specified [graph drawing](graph-drawing.md) whose [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) meet only at common endpoints, also called an embedding in the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md). Its [faces of a plane graph](face-of-a-plane-graph.md) and [facial boundary walks](facial-boundary-walk.md) refer to that embedding. This geometric use of embedding differs from a [graph embedding](graph-embedding.md), which maps one abstract [graph](graph-split.md) into another.
