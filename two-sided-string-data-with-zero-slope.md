# Two-sided string data with zero slope

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md)

For the [wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md), prescribing $y(0,t)=f(t)$ and $y_x(0,t)=0$ selects equal incoming and outgoing profiles. Write [D'Alembert's formula](d-alembert-s-formula.md) as $P(t-x/c)+Q(t+x/c)$. The two traces imply $P+Q=f$ and $P'=Q'$, proving the displayed expression. These data do not generally describe a disturbance launched from an everywhere resting string. For nonsmooth $f$, [derivatives](derivative.md) and boundary traces need their piecewise classical or [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md) interpretation; an endpoint cusp may prevent a classical [derivative](derivative.md) at an isolated time.
