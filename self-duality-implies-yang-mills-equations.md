# Self-duality implies Yang-Mills equations

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills instanton](yang-mills-instanton.md)

Both the [self-dual Yang-Mills equations](self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md) and the [Anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations](anti-self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md) imply the second-order [Yang-Mills equations](yang-mills-equations.md) because the [Bianchi identity](bianchi-identity.md) holds for every [principal connection](connection-principal-bundle.md). Finite action is additionally required to call the solution a [Yang-Mills instanton](yang-mills-instanton.md); the implication itself is a local differential identity.
