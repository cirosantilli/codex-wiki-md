# Synchronous density gauge mode

↑ **Parent:** [Residual synchronous-gauge freedom](residual-synchronous-gauge-freedom.md)

A residual synchronous time displacement $\xi^0=C(\mathbf x)/a$ changes a fluid [density contrast](density-contrast.md) by $-\bar\rho'\xi^0/\bar\rho$. For constant equation of state this gives the displayed mode: it scales as $\tau^{-2}$ during [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md) and as $\tau^{-3}$ during [matter domination](matter-domination.md). Such density changes alone do not indicate physical structure growth. Choosing the cold-matter rest frame fixes the residual time displacement, while a spatial relabeling fixes the remaining integration convention for the metric trace.
