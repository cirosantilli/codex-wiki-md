# Kendall rank correlation coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal categorical variable](ordinal-categorical-variable.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendall_rank_correlation_coefficient)

A family of ordinal association measures based on [concordant pairs](concordant-pair.md) and [discordant pairs](discordant-pair.md). Without a tie adjustment, the coefficient is $\tau_a=(C-D)/\binom n2$: the [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md), over unordered pairs, of the product of the two coordinate comparison signs. Reversing one ordering reverses the sign, and strictly increasing relabellings preserve it. [Kendall's tau-b](kendall-s-tau-b.md) makes an explicit adjustment for [tied pairs](tied-pair-ordinal-data.md). The name commemorates [Maurice Kendall](maurice-kendall.md).
