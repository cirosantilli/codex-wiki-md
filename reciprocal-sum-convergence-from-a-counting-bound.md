# Reciprocal-sum convergence from a counting bound

↑ **Parent:** [Abel's summation formula](abel-s-summation-formula.md)

If $A(x)$ counts a set of [positive integers](positive-integer.md) and $\eta>0$, [Abel summation](abel-s-summation-formula.md) gives $\sum_{n\in A,n\le X}1/n=A(X)/X+\int_1^X A(t)t^{-2}\,dt$. A bound $A(t)\ll t/(\log t)^{1+\eta}$ for $t\ge3$ makes the integral convergent, since the substitution $u=\log t$ gives $\int_{\log3}^\infty u^{-1-\eta}\,du<\infty$.
