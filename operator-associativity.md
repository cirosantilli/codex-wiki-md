# Operator associativity

↑ **Parent:** [Operator precedence](operator-precedence.md)

The syntactic grouping convention for successive operators of the same precedence. Left grouping of [subtraction](subtraction.md) makes `a-b-c` mean `(a-b)-c`; it does not make [subtraction](subtraction.md) an [associative](associative-property.md) operation.
