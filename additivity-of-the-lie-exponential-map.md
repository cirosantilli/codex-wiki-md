# Additivity of the Lie exponential map

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential map of a Lie group](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md)

The [Exponential map of a Lie group](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md) is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from the additive [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) exactly when its [identity component of a Lie group](identity-component-of-a-lie-group.md) is abelian. If it is additive, its image is an abelian subgroup containing an identity neighborhood by the [local exponential chart](local-exponential-chart.md); connectedness makes that image the entire identity component. Conversely, in an abelian identity component the product $\exp(tX)\exp(tY)$ is a [one-parameter subgroup](one-parameter-subgroup.md) with derivative $X+Y$, giving the identity by uniqueness. The disconnected [Lie group](lie-group.md) $S_3\times\mathbb R$ shows that the full group need not be abelian.
