# Chloride

↑ **Parent:** [Anion](anion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloride)

[Chloride](chloride.md) is the [anion](anion.md) $\mathrm{Cl^-}$, with net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $-e$. In the [glycine titration](glycine-titration.md) model, it supplies the negative background [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) introduced with [hydrochloric acid](hydrochloric-acid.md).
