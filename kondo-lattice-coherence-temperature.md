# Kondo-lattice coherence temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Kondo lattice](kondo-lattice.md)

A [Kondo lattice](kondo-lattice.md) develops coherent low-energy [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) bands below a material-dependent coherence scale. This lattice scale need not equal the single-impurity Kondo screening scale. Coherence can produce a heavy [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md) with narrow bands, large [electronic heat capacity](electronic-heat-capacity.md) and low-[temperature](temperature-split.md) quadratic resistivity.
