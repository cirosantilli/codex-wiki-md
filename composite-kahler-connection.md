<h1 id="composite-kahler-connection">Composite Kähler connection</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

The scalar fields of chiral matter induce a composite phase connection proportional to $i(K_i\partial_\mu z^i-K_{\bar i}\partial_\mu\bar z^{\bar i})$. For canonical [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md) it is proportional to the scalar phase current. The chiral matter fermions and [gravitino](gravitino.md) have appropriate Kähler weights; expanding their connection terms produces scalar-current–fermion-bilinear couplings. Overall signs and coefficients depend on spinor and Planck-scale conventions.
