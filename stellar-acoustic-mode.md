# Stellar acoustic mode

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar oscillation](stellar-oscillation.md)

A stellar acoustic mode is a pressure-restored [stellar oscillation](stellar-oscillation.md). Its local high-frequency propagation branch lies above the [stellar buoyancy frequency](stellar-buoyancy-frequency.md) and [Lamb frequency](lamb-frequency.md). Inner refraction and near-surface reflection form an acoustic cavity; its standing-wave frequencies probe the [adiabatic sound speed](adiabatic-sound-speed.md) profile.
