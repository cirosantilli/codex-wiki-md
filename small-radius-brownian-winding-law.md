# Small-radius Brownian winding law

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion](conformal-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md)

For planar [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at radius $\varepsilon$ and stopped on first hitting radius one, the net winding count divided by $\log\varepsilon$ converges to a standard [Cauchy random variable](cauchy-random-variable.md) divided by $2\pi$. The complex exponential maps Brownian coordinates in the left half-plane to the punctured unit disk after the [conformal Brownian clock](conformal-brownian-clock.md). The unwrapped argument at exit is the vertical half-plane exit coordinate, with the [Cauchy exit law from a Brownian half-plane](cauchy-exit-law-from-a-brownian-half-plane.md). Counting integer turns introduces only a uniformly bounded error in winding units.
