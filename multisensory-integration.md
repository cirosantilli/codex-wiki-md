# Multisensory integration

↑ **Parent:** [Multisensory perception](multisensory-perception.md)

Interactions or combination of information across [sensory modalities](sensory-modality.md). Operational criteria must be specified: a [neuron](neuron-split.md) can show a changed paired response, a judgment can combine cues, or signals can be attributed to one event. These criteria constrain one another without being interchangeable.
