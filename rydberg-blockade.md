# Rydberg blockade

↑ **Parent:** [Rydberg atom](rydberg-atom.md)

[Rydberg blockade](rydberg-blockade.md) suppresses simultaneous excitation of nearby [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) into strongly interacting [Rydberg atom](rydberg-atom.md) states. If the interaction shifts the doubly excited level by much more than the excitation linewidth and coherent drive scale, a pulse resonant for one isolated [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) becomes off-resonant when a neighbour is already excited. The resulting conditional dynamics can implement an entangling [two-qubit gate](two-qubit-gate.md) for neutral-atom [qubits](qubit.md).
