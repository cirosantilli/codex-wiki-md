# Triose phosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Carbohydrate](carbohydrate.md)

A [triose phosphate](triose-phosphate.md) is a three-[carbon](carbon.md) [sugar](sugar.md) carrying a [phosphate](phosphate.md) group. [Glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate](glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate.md) is one example. The [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) supplies a net [triose phosphate](triose-phosphate.md) for further [carbohydrate](carbohydrate.md) synthesis. [OpenStax Biology 2e, 8.3: the Calvin cycle's carbohydrate product](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/8-3-using-light-energy-to-make-organic-molecules).
