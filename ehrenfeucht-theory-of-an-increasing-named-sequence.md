# Ehrenfeucht theory of an increasing named sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Dense linear order without endpoints](dense-linear-order-without-endpoints.md)

Add constants $c_0<c_1<\cdots$ to the theory of [dense linear orders without endpoints](dense-linear-order-without-endpoints.md). The three countable models have respectively a least upper bound for the named sequence, no upper bound, or upper bounds with no least one. The unbounded model is an [atomic model](atomic-model.md); the bounded model with no least upper bound is an [omega-saturated model](omega-saturated-model.md). The remaining model omits the type $c_i<x<d$ over its least-upper-bound parameter $d$.
