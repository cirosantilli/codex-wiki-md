# Condensate compressibility in an optical lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Optical lattice](optical-lattice.md)

Here $n$ is average [number density](number-density.md) and $\mu$ the ground-state [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md). This commonly used condensate response has units of inverse energy; thermodynamic [isothermal compressibility](isothermal-compressibility.md) is instead $\kappa/n$. In a weakly interacting repulsive condensate, the normalized one-cell ground state $f_s$, with average $|f_s|^2=1$, gives $\mu=E_0(s)+Un I(s)+\cdots$, $I(s)=d^{-1}\int_0^d|f_s|^4dx$. Localization raises $I$ and reduces $\kappa$ at fixed $n,U,d$. In deep harmonic wells $I\propto s^{1/4}$, hence $\kappa\propto s^{-1/4}$. This is a ground-band, repulsive-superfluid statement with the held variables specified, not a theorem for arbitrary signs of the interaction or arbitrary excited branches.
