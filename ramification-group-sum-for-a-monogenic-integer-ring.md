# Ramification-group sum for a monogenic integer ring

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification group](ramification-group.md)

If $L/K$ is a finite [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of local fields, $\mathcal O_L=\mathcal O_K[\alpha]$, and $f$ is the [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) of $\alpha$, then

$$
v_L(f'(\alpha))
=\sum_{1\ne\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)}v_L(\sigma(\alpha)-\alpha)
=\sum_{s\geq0}(|G_s|-1).
$$
