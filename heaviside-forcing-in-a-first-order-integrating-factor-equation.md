# Heaviside forcing in a first-order integrating-factor equation

↑ **Parent:** [Integrating factor](integrating-factor.md)

For $y'+a(x)y=H(x)$ with continuous $a$, the [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) $\mu=\exp(\int a)$ gives the displayed solution. It is continuous at the step and solves the [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) classically on each side; the derivative jumps by one. The value assigned to the [Heaviside step function](heaviside-step-function.md) at the single point zero does not affect the integral. A discontinuity in $y$ would instead produce an unintended delta term in its distributional derivative.
