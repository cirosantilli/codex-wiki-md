# Pointwise closure of the bounded Lipschitz unit ball

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded Lipschitz norm](bounded-lipschitz-norm.md)

For every fixed $x\ne y$ and $z$, the sum $|f_i(x)-f_i(y)|/d(x,y)+|f_i(z)|$ is at most one. [Pointwise convergence](pointwise-convergence.md) preserves this inequality. Taking the independent suprema over the pair $(x,y)$ and the point $z$ proves the assertion. Taking each bound separately would only give the weaker estimate two.
