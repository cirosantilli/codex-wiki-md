# Natural extension of the continued-fraction Gauss map

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss continued-fraction map](gauss-continued-fraction-map.md)

On each branch with digit $a=\lfloor1/x\rfloor$, the displayed extension of the [Gauss continued-fraction map](gauss-continued-fraction-map.md) is invertible almost everywhere: $x=(a+x')^{-1}$ and $y=(y')^{-1}-a$, with $a=\lfloor1/y'\rfloor$. Its invariant joint [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is $[(\log2)(1+xy)^2]^{-1}$. The branch [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $[x^2(a+y)^2]^{-1}$, which cancels the transformation of $1+xy$. Integrating out $y$ gives the [Gauss measure](gauss-measure.md) density.
