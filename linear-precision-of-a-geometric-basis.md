# Linear precision of a geometric basis

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design.md)

A basis with the displayed identities reproduces [affine functions](affine-function.md) from their values at the precision sites $\xi_j$. For two such bases, the tensor-product precision sites are $(\xi_j,\eta_k)$: summation independently recovers $u$ and $v$. Affine images of this site grid therefore produce exact planar parameterizations, independent of the chosen control-grid representation.
