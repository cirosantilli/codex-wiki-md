# Heteroclinic cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Heteroclinic orbit](heteroclinic-orbit.md)

A heteroclinic cycle is a closed chain of invariant states connected by [heteroclinic orbits](heteroclinic-orbit.md). In convection-amplitude models it can describe successive [convection roll](convection-roll.md) switching. Existence and attraction require the global connections and their transverse stability; a single [convection roll](convection-roll.md)'s positive invasion growth rate alone does not establish an attracting cycle.
