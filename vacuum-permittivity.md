# Vacuum permittivity

↑ **Parent:** [Permittivity](permittivity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum_permittivity)

[Vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md) is the electromagnetic constant $\epsilon_0$ in [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md) and the [Vacuum Maxwell equations](vacuum-maxwell-equations.md). In classical [vacuum](vacuum.md), $\mathbf P=0$ and the [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) is $\mathbf D=\epsilon_0\mathbf E$. A material's [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) includes its [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) response; [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md) supplies the common reference, not a separate adjustable material property. Its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is [farad](farad.md) per metre.

For elementary calculations, [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md) is approximately $8.854\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}$. This is a deliberately rounded value, not an exact decimal or a statement of current measurement precision.[2022 CODATA adjustment, vacuum electric permittivity row: value and nonzero standard uncertainty](fundamental-physical-constants-complete-listing-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology-and-others.md) It sets the scale in [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md), $F=|q_1q_2|/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^2)$ for two stationary point [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) with separation [length](length.md) $r$ in [vacuum](vacuum.md), and therefore enters the reference [capacitance](capacitance.md) $C_0=\epsilon_0A/d$ of a [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md).

Since the revised [SI](international-system-of-units.md) took effect on 20 May 2019, the numerical value of [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md) in [SI](international-system-of-units.md) units is determined experimentally, rather than fixed exactly by the unit definitions. The numerical values of the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md) $e$, [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) $h$ and [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) $c$ are fixed exactly. The [fine-structure constant](fine-structure-constant.md) $\alpha$ is measured, and

$$
\alpha=\frac{e^2}{2\epsilon_0hc},\qquad
\epsilon_0=\frac{e^2}{2\alpha hc},\qquad
\epsilon_0\mu_0=\frac1{c^2},
$$

where $\mu_0$ is [vacuum permeability](vacuum-permeability.md). Consequently [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md) and [vacuum permeability](vacuum-permeability.md) have experimental uncertainty, while their product is exact in [SI](international-system-of-units.md) units because $c$ is exact. This is a change in metrological status, not a new material dependence of [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md).[pp. 123–125 (defining constants and alpha relation); section 2.3.1, printed p. 128 (ampere, epsilon0 and mu0); 26th CGPM Resolution 1, printed p. 194 (20 May 2019 effective date)](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md)
