# Deformation mechanism map

↑ **Parent:** [Creep (deformation)](creep-deformation.md)

A [deformation mechanism map](deformation-mechanism-map.md) shows which deformation process dominates in a specified range of [stress](stress.md), [homologous temperature](homologous-temperature.md) and other controlled variables. Boundaries shift with [grain size](grain-size.md), composition, pressure, water content and the constitutive laws; they are not universal lines determined by [homologous temperature](homologous-temperature.md) alone.
