# Centre of percussion

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

For a body constrained to rotate about a fixed pivot, a perpendicular [impulse](impulse.md) $K$ delivered at distance $b$ from the pivot produces $\omega=Kb/I_O$. If the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) is distance $h$ from the pivot, its [momentum](momentum-split.md) is $M\omega h$. The pivot [impulse](impulse.md) is $J_O=M\omega h-K$, and vanishes when $b=I_O/(Mh)$. This impact location is the [centre of percussion](centre-of-percussion.md). The result assumes the stated planar rigid-body geometry and a short [impulse](impulse.md); it is not a claim that all [forces](force-split.md) at the pivot vanish afterward.
