# Thick-barrier transmission prefactor

↑ **Parent:** [Rectangular barrier transmission probability](rectangular-barrier-transmission-probability.md)

For a thick rectangular barrier with equal exterior potentials, constant [mass](mass.md) and $0<E<U$, the exact matched [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md) has the displayed limit when $\kappa a\gg1$. The exponential is fixed by the evanescent [action](action.md); the interface prefactor remains energy-dependent. A leading-exponential estimate can therefore differ by an order-one multiplier from the matched result.
