# Breakdown-limited energy of a conducting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitance of an isolated conducting sphere](capacitance-of-an-isolated-conducting-sphere.md)

For an isolated smooth [conducting sphere](conducting-sphere.md) of radius $a$, [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) gives surface [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $E_s=V/a$. Combining its [capacitance](capacitance.md) $4\pi\epsilon_0a$ with [capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md) $CV^2/2$ gives the displayed bound when the surrounding medium has [dielectric strength](dielectric-strength.md) $E_{\rm bd}$ and approximately vacuum [permittivity](permittivity-split.md). Nearby conductors, surface roughness and discharge geometry can lower the practical limit.
