# Degenerate four-wave mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Four-wave mixing](four-wave-mixing.md)

All four interacting waves have the same [frequency](frequency.md). Counterpropagating equal-frequency pumps can give a source proportional to the conjugate signal field, providing [phase conjugation](phase-conjugation.md). The pump product must have the appropriate uniform spatial phase, and finite [apertures](aperture.md) and [phase mismatch](phase-mismatch.md) limit reconstruction.
