# Papain digestion of IgG

↑ **Parent:** [Fragment antigen-binding](fragment-antigen-binding.md)

Limited [Papain digestion of IgG](papain-digestion-of-igg.md) cuts each [immunoglobulin heavy chain](immunoglobulin-heavy-chain.md) on the [N-terminus](n-terminus.md) side of the hinge crosslinks, releasing two [Fab fragments](fragment-antigen-binding.md) and one [Fc fragment](fragment-crystallizable-region.md).[Immunobiology: The Immune System in Health and Disease, 5th edition by Charles A. Janeway, Jr. and others; §3-3 and Figure 3.3: papain cut positions and two Fab plus one Fc](immunobiology-the-immune-system-in-health-and-disease-5th-edition-by-charles-a-janeway-jr-and-others/the-structure-of-a-typical-antibody-molecule.md)

Small released [peptides](peptide.md), incomplete cleavage of one arm and secondary cleavage are excluded from that model.

A connectivity argument explains the three products. Treat each continuous backbone segment as a [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md), and retained interchain [disulfide bonds](disulfide-bond.md) as [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). After the two stipulated cuts, six backbone segments remain: two complete [immunoglobulin light chains](immunoglobulin-light-chain.md), two [immunoglobulin heavy chain](immunoglobulin-heavy-chain.md) arm segments and two [immunoglobulin heavy chain](immunoglobulin-heavy-chain.md) stem segments. Each light chain remains attached to its own arm segment, producing two separate [Fab fragments](fragment-antigen-binding.md). The two stem segments remain attached to each other, producing one [Fc fragment](fragment-crystallizable-region.md). Thus six backbone segments form three [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md), not six independently released products. Removing crosslinks instead, while keeping each backbone intact, would leave four chain components in this simplified covalent-connectivity model.

For a constructed mixture, start with $5\,\mathrm{pmol}$ of intact [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md) and stipulate that $60\%$ undergoes the complete two-cut event while the rest remains intact. Count [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of each product:

$$
n_{\mathrm{intact}}=2\,\mathrm{pmol},\qquad n_{\mathrm{Fab}}=6\,\mathrm{pmol},\qquad n_{\mathrm{Fc}}=3\,\mathrm{pmol}.
$$

The total amount of separately connected products becomes $11\,\mathrm{pmol}$, although the starting amount was $5\,\mathrm{pmol}$. Each digested [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md) replaces one connected assembly with three. The [antibody binding site](paratope.md) inventory is conserved in this model:

$$
2n_{\mathrm{intact}}+n_{\mathrm{Fab}}=(2\cdot2+6)\,\mathrm{pmol}=10\,\mathrm{pmol}.
$$

The factor two counts sites per intact [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md), not extra [immunoglobulin light chains](immunoglobulin-light-chain.md) per [Fab fragment](fragment-antigen-binding.md). Fragment counts, backbone-segment counts and [antibody binding site](paratope.md) counts must be kept distinct. A real digest's retained binding would additionally depend on preservation of the [paratopes](paratope.md).

The historical functional test was more subtle than asking whether a fragment still formed a visible precipitate. [Rodney Robert Porter](rodney-robert-porter.md)'s earlier rabbit anti-[ovalbumin](ovalbumin.md) preparation was digested with [papain](papain.md) powder. The resulting fragment did not precipitate [ovalbumin](ovalbumin.md) itself, but specifically inhibited [ovalbumin](ovalbumin.md) [precipitation](precipitation-chemistry.md) with undigested antiserum. The intact-antiserum reaction supplied the positive comparison: failure to precipitate alone did not mean failure to recognize the [antigen](antigen.md).[Rodney R. Porter, Separation and Isolation of Fractions of Rabbit Gamma-Globulin containing the Antibody and Antigenic Combining Sites, 1958, opening paragraph: earlier papain-powder digest and inhibition of precipitation](separation-and-isolation-of-fractions-of-rabbit-gamma-globulin-containing-the-antibody-and-antigenic-combining-sites-by-rodney-robert-porter.md)

The inference is that binding and assembly of a precipitating network are different abilities. A one-site [Fab fragment](fragment-antigen-binding.md) can occupy an [epitope](epitope.md) without providing the two-arm bridge available to intact [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md). The inhibition readout supports retained recognition under the tested conditions; it does not locate individual contacts or show that every [papain](papain.md)-generated product binds. This is the conceptual benefit of fragmenting an [antibody](antibody.md): one can ask which functions survive loss of a structural connection.
