# Bochner integral

↑ **Parent:** [Measure theory](measure-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochner_integral)

The Bochner integral integrates a strongly measurable function taking values in a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md). It is obtained as a norm limit of integrals of simple vector-valued functions and satisfies the usual linearity and norm bound.
