# dCas9

↑ **Parent:** [Cas9](cas9.md)

A catalytically inactive form of [Cas9](cas9.md) retaining guide-directed DNA binding. Recruitment of regulatory domains makes it a platform for [CRISPR interference](crispr-interference.md) and [CRISPR activation](crispr-activation.md).
