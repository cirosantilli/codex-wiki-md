# Additive category with truncated primary torsion

↑ **Parent:** [Additive category](additive-category.md)

Fix a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$. Take the full [subcategory](subcategory.md) of [finitely generated abelian groups](finitely-generated-abelian-group.md) with no element of order $p^2$. It is closed under finite [direct sums](direct-sum.md) and [subgroups](subgroup.md). If $t_p(G)$ is the $p$-primary [torsion subgroup](torsion-subgroup.md), the map $G\to R(G)=G/(p\,t_p(G))$ is a [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) into this subcategory: every map to an allowed group kills $p\,t_p(G)$. Therefore ordinary [kernels in a category](kernel-in-a-category.md) and reflected [cokernels in a category](cokernel-in-a-category.md) supply all finite [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) and [colimits](colimit.md). No nonzero finitely generated group has zero reflection, so the [epimorphisms](epimorphism.md) are precisely surjections and are all [conormal epimorphisms](conormal-epimorphism.md). Multiplication by $p^2$ on $\mathbb Z$ is a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) but not a [normal monomorphism](normal-monomorphism.md): its cokernel here is $\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$, whose kernel is $p\mathbb Z$, not $p^2\mathbb Z$. Multiplication by $p$ is normal; composing it with itself gives this nonnormal monomorphism.
