# Neumann Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace operator](laplace-operator.md)

The nonnegative Neumann Laplacian is the [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) realization of $-\operatorname{div}\operatorname{grad}$ with vanishing outward [normal derivative](normal-derivative.md). Its weak quadratic form is the [Dirichlet energy](dirichlet-energy.md) on $H^1$. On a connected compact domain the constants give a one-dimensional zero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) space.
