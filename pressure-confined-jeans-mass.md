# Pressure-confined Jeans mass

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md)

For a cloud supported by an effective [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md) $v$ and confined by a surface [pressure](pressure-split.md) $P$, [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) fixes the [gravitational instability](gravitational-instability.md) mass scale to $v^4/(G^{3/2}P^{1/2})$. The dimensionless coefficient depends on the equilibrium model and the convention used for the instability threshold.
