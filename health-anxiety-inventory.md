# Health Anxiety Inventory

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological assessment](psychological-assessment.md)

A [questionnaire](questionnaire.md) developed to measure [health anxiety](health-anxiety.md) across relevant clinical and medical contexts. A score quantifies aspects of the psychological response to illness-related concerns; it does not determine the presence of physical disease or replace [clinical diagnosis](clinical-diagnosis.md).[2002; original clinical contrasts, sensitivity-to-change studies and medical samples](the-health-anxiety-inventory-development-and-validation-of-scales-for-the-measurement-of-health-anxiety-and-hypochondriasis-by-paul-salkovskis-and-others.md)
