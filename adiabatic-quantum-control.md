# Adiabatic quantum control

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian engineering](hamiltonian-engineering.md)

Design a slowly varying [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) path whose relevant initial eigenstate connects to a desired final eigenstate. Under the gap and slowness hypotheses of the [quantum adiabatic theorem](adiabatic-theorem.md), the state follows that instantaneous eigenstate up to phase. The path, spectral gap and duration all matter; a level crossing or excessive speed can spoil the intended state transfer.
