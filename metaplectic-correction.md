# Metaplectic correction

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization in geometric quantization](polarization-in-geometric-quantization.md)

Tensor the prequantum bundle with a square root of the canonical [determinant](determinant.md) bundle of a polarization, when such a square root exists. The half-form contributes to the state pairing and to the observable action. It improves coordinate invariance and accounts for familiar Maslov and oscillator zero-point shifts. Its existence is additional global data, not automatic from prequantum integrality.
