# Thermal eclipse depth

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet secondary eclipse](exoplanet-secondary-eclipse.md)

For [blackbody](blackbody.md) star and planet, define the planet-star contrast

$$
r_\lambda=\frac{F_{p,\lambda}}{F_{*,\lambda}}=\left(\frac{R_p}{R_*}\right)^2\frac{B_\lambda(T_p)}{B_\lambda(T_*)}.
$$

A complete [exoplanet secondary eclipse](exoplanet-secondary-eclipse.md) removes the planet's light from the combined out-of-eclipse light. Its exact normalized depth is $d_\lambda=r_\lambda/(1+r_\lambda)$. The common approximation $d_\lambda\simeq r_\lambda$ requires $r_\lambda\ll1$. This thermal model omits reflected light and spatial variations.
