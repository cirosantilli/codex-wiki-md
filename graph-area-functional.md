# Graph area functional

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal surface equation for a graph](minimal-surface-equation-for-a-graph.md)

This functional measures the [surface area](surface-area.md) of the [graph of a function](graph-of-a-function.md). On a bounded domain it is finite for $u\in H^1$. Its [directional derivative](directional-derivative.md) is $\partial_vF(u)=\int\nabla u\cdot\nabla v/\sqrt{1+|\nabla u|^2}$, by the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md), since the pointwise difference quotients are bounded by $|\nabla v|$. The resulting dual-valued operator is monotone: the scalar integrand $p\mapsto\sqrt{1+|p|^2}$ is convex and its gradient is $p/\sqrt{1+|p|^2}$. It is not a [nonlinear coercive operator](nonlinear-coercive-operator.md) on the full $H^1$ space, since it vanishes on constant functions whose $H^1$ norms can tend to infinity.
