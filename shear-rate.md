# Shear rate

↑ **Parent:** [Strain-rate tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shear_rate)

The shear rate is the scalar rate at which adjacent material layers acquire shear strain. In simple shear $u_x=u_x(y)$, its signed value is $\dot\gamma=du_x/dy$.
