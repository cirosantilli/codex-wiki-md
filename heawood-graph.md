# Heawood graph

↑ **Parent:** [Cubic graph](cubic-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heawood_graph)

The bipartite [cubic graph](cubic-graph.md) with vertices $A_i,B_i$, $i\in\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z$, and edges $A_iB_i,A_iB_{i+1},A_iB_{i-2}$. The three offsets give a [Tait coloring](tait-coloring.md).
