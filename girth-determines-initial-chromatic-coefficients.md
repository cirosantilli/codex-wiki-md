# Girth determines initial chromatic coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md)

For a finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md) on $n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) with $m$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and no [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) shorter than $\ell$, the coefficient of $x^{n-i}$ in its [chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md) is $(-1)^i\binom mi$ for $0\leq i\leq\min(n,\ell-2)$. In the [chromatic polynomial inclusion-exclusion formula](chromatic-polynomial-inclusion-exclusion-formula.md), an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) [subset](subset.md) containing a [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) of length at least $\ell$ has $n-c(F)\geq\ell-1$. Thus every contribution to $x^{n-i}$ in this range is a [forest](forest.md), with exactly $i$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). Conversely, all $i$-[edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) [subsets](subset.md) are [forests](forest.md), so precisely $\binom mi$ terms contribute, each with sign $(-1)^i$.
