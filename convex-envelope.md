# Convex envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

The greatest [convex function](convex-function.md) lying below a given function, when such an envelope is considered in the specified domain. A [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of phase compositions and their [Gibbs free energies](gibbs-free-energy.md) constructs candidate mixtures; the equilibrium free energy is their lower [convex envelope](convex-envelope.md). For two differentiable coexisting branches, the supporting line is a [common tangent](common-tangent.md).
