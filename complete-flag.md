# Complete flag

↑ **Parent:** [Flag (linear algebra)](flag-linear-algebra.md)

A complete flag in an $n$-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is a nested sequence of [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) with $\dim V_i=i$. An action preserves such a flag exactly when its [matrices](matrix.md) are upper triangular in a [basis](basis.md) adapted to the flag. The [Lie theorem](lie-s-theorem.md) supplies an invariant complete flag for every complex finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) of a [solvable Lie algebra](solvable-lie-algebra.md).
