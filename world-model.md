# World model

↑ **Parent:** [Artificial intelligence](artificial-intelligence-split.md)

An internal model of relationships among states, actions, observations and outcomes, used to predict consequences or support inference. It can be incomplete and task-specific. [Planning](planning.md) through a mistaken model can produce confident errors, so model accuracy and the choice of abstraction are part of intelligent control.
