# Inverse-image direct-image adjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse image sheaf](inverse-image-sheaf.md)

For [sheaves of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md) and a continuous map $f:X\to Y$, there is a natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_X(f^{-1}\mathcal G,\mathcal F)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_Y(\mathcal G,f_*\mathcal F)$. It comes from the [universal property of an inverse image sheaf](universal-property-of-an-inverse-image-sheaf.md). The inverse image of abelian [sheaves](sheaf-mathematics.md) is different from the tensor-adjusted [pullback of a sheaf of modules](pullback-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md) on a [ringed space](ringed-space-split.md).
