# Weight enumerator

↑ **Parent:** [Linear code](linear-code.md)

The homogeneous [weight enumerator](weight-enumerator.md) of a length-$n$ [linear code](linear-code.md) $C$ is the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

$$
W_C(s,t)=\sum_{x\in C}s^{\operatorname{wt}(x)}t^{n-\operatorname{wt}(x)}.
$$

Its coefficients count words of each [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md). In this convention $W_C(1,1)=|C|$; some references interchange $s,t$.
