# Parametric equation of a straight line

↑ **Parent:** [Straight line](straight-line.md)

A [straight line](straight-line.md) through a point $a$ in nonzero direction $t$ is the set of vectors $r=a+st$ for real parameter $s$. If $|t|=1$, changes in the parameter equal signed distances along the line. Two such lines intersect exactly when their two parameter formulas give a common vector for some choice of their parameters.
