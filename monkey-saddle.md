# Monkey saddle

↑ **Parent:** [Saddle point](saddle-point.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monkey_saddle)

This cubic [smooth function](smooth-function.md) has one isolated degenerate [critical point](critical-point.md) at the origin, with three ascending and three descending sectors. Adding $-tx$ with $t>0$ replaces it by two nondegenerate saddle points at $(\pm\sqrt{t/3},0)$. The [Hessians](hessian-matrix.md) there have opposite diagonal entries $6x$ and $-6x$. It illustrates why the number of smooth [critical points](critical-point.md) can be smaller than that required for a [Morse function](morse-function.md).
