# Bifurcation diagram of the 2023 Cambridge quadratic-product system

↑ **Parent:** [Bifurcation diagram](bifurcation-diagram.md)

The equilibria of $\dot x=(a^2-x)(a-y^2)$, $\dot y=x-y$ lie on

$$
x=y=a^2
$$

for every $a$, and on $x=y=\pm\sqrt a$ for $a\geq0$. They meet in a [subcritical pitchfork bifurcation](subcritical-pitchfork-bifurcation.md) at $a=0$; the positive square-root branch meets the $a^2$ branch and exchanges stability in a [transcritical bifurcation](transcritical-bifurcation.md) at $a=1$.
