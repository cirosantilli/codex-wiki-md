# Frazil ice

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frazil_ice)

Frazil ice consists of small suspended ice crystals formed in supercooled turbulent water. A layer of crystals can damp [surface gravity waves](surface-gravity-wave.md) through [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md), even when individual crystals scatter very little wave energy.
