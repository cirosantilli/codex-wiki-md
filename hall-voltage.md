# Hall voltage

↑ **Parent:** [Hall effect](hall-effect.md)

A transverse [Hall voltage](hall-voltage.md) develops when a longitudinal current flows through a sample in a perpendicular [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). With $R_H=E_y/(j_xB_z)$, a uniform slab of thickness $t$ along the magnetic field has a signed transverse voltage magnitude set by $R_HIB_z/t$, with the final sign determined by the chosen voltage leads.
