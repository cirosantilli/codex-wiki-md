# Entropy of a hyperbolic toral automorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Topological entropy](topological-entropy.md)

For a two-dimensional [hyperbolic toral automorphism](hyperbolic-toral-automorphism.md), the stable and unstable [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) obey $|\lambda_s|<1<|\lambda_u|$. In a sufficiently small [Bowen ball](bowen-ball.md), lifting to the stable and unstable coordinates gives widths proportional to $\epsilon$ and $\epsilon|\lambda_u|^{-(n-1)}$. Its normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) is therefore proportional to $\epsilon^2|\lambda_u|^{-(n-1)}$, uniformly in the centre. Maximal [separated sets](separated-subset-of-a-metric-space.md) cover by radius-$\epsilon$ [Bowen balls](bowen-ball.md) and pack disjoint radius-$\epsilon/2$ [Bowen balls](bowen-ball.md), so their exponential growth rate is $\log|\lambda_u|$. Smallness of $\epsilon$ must exclude nonzero lattice jumps between successive lifted iterates; this is essential to the ball-volume argument.
