# Homogeneous calorimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic calorimeter](electromagnetic-calorimeter.md)

A [homogeneous calorimeter](homogeneous-calorimeter.md) uses its showering volume as the active medium, such as a [scintillating crystal](scintillator.md). It avoids absorber-versus-active sampling fluctuations, but still has [signal statistics](poisson-distribution.md), intrinsic [shower](electromagnetic-shower.md) fluctuations, [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) and [calibration](calorimeter-calibration.md) nonuniformity. Its constant and [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) terms need not be smaller than those of a [sampling calorimeter](sampling-calorimeter.md).
