# Curve of growth

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalent width](equivalent-width.md)

A curve of growth describes the [equivalent width](equivalent-width.md) $E(m)=\int(1-e^{-m\kappa_\nu})d\nu$ as a function of absorber [mass path](mass-path.md) or [column density](column-density.md). Its weak-line limit is $E\simeq mS$. Saturation makes growth sublinear: a pure Gaussian core gives $E\sim2\Delta_D\sqrt{\log\tau_0}$, while saturated [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md) wings give $E\sim2\sqrt{mS\Delta_L}$. The spectral coordinate and coefficient convention must be consistent.
