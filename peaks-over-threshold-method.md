# Peaks-over-threshold method

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Pareto distribution](generalized-pareto-distribution.md)

The peaks-over-threshold method fits a [generalized Pareto distribution](generalized-pareto-distribution.md) to exceedances of a high threshold and also estimates their frequency. Together these determine tail probabilities and [return levels](return-level.md). For dependent observations, threshold exceedances may occur in clusters; [declustering of extremes](declustering-of-extremes.md) can separate extreme events before fitting a model for independent events.
