# Gelfand representation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Gelfand representation](gelfand-representation.md)

For a commutative complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), its [Gelfand transform](gelfand-representation.md) is a continuous unital algebra homomorphism into the continuous functions on its compact [character space](character-space-of-an-algebra.md). The values of the transform of $a$ are exactly $\sigma_A(a)$, so $\|\widehat a\|_\infty=r(a)$. Its kernel is the intersection of the kernels of the [algebra characters](character-of-an-algebra.md). For a commutative [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md), [spectral radius norm equality for normal elements](spectral-radius-norm-equality-for-normal-elements.md) and the [Stone-Weierstrass theorem](stone-weierstrass-theorem.md) upgrade this to an isometric [C-star homomorphism](c-star-homomorphism.md) onto $C(\Delta(A))$.
