# Jeffreys prior

↑ **Parent:** [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md)

The Jeffreys prior uses the square root of the determinant of the [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md) as a parameter-density kernel. It is invariant under smooth one-to-one reparameterization, since the information and density Jacobians transform compatibly. It may be an [improper prior](improper-prior.md); invariance does not guarantee [posterior propriety](posterior-propriety.md). With nuisance parameters, a scalar conditional Jeffreys prior and the joint Jeffreys prior need not coincide.
