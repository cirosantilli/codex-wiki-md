# Symmetric underdominant two-cluster equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Globally coupled underdominant metapopulation](globally-coupled-underdominant-metapopulation.md)

With equally many initially pure habitats of each [allele](allele.md), symmetry preserves $y=1-x$. The heterogeneous [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) exists for $\sigma<s/2$. Its full-system [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $-s+3\sigma$ in the mean-frequency direction and $-s+2\sigma$ in the antisymmetric direction, so it attracts general nearby perturbations only for $\sigma<s/3$. Exact symmetry can preserve both [alleles](allele.md) beyond this robust range; at stronger migration it approaches the unstable uniform midpoint.
