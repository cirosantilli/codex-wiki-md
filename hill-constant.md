# Hill constant

↑ **Parent:** [Hill equation](hill-equation.md)

The positive concentration giving half of the maximal response in a [Hill equation](hill-equation.md). This effective threshold need not equal an individual binding site's microscopic [dissociation constant](dissociation-constant.md).
