<h1 id="hilbert-samuel-growth-dimension">Hilbert–Samuel growth dimension</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Samuel polynomial](hilbert-samuel-polynomial.md)

For a [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md) $(R,\mathfrak m)$, let $H_R(t)=\operatorname{length}(R/\mathfrak m^t)$. Its eventual [Hilbert-Samuel polynomial](hilbert-samuel-polynomial.md) has degree $d(R)$, equivalently the pole order at $1$ of the [Hilbert series](hilbert-series.md) of $\operatorname{gr}_{\mathfrak m}R$. This is the local length-growth invariant, not the [embedding dimension](embedding-dimension.md). The [prime-chain lower bound for local length](prime-chain-lower-bound-for-local-length.md) proves $\dim R\leq d(R)$; the full local dimension theorem gives equality.
