# Equal-frequency light between equal-speed inertial observers

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic Doppler effect](relativistic-doppler-effect.md)

For two [inertial frames](inertial-frame.md) with equal [speed](speed.md) in a laboratory, a [photon](photon.md) is measured at equal [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) by their comoving observers exactly when its laboratory propagation [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf n$ obeys the displayed condition. Indeed, measured [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is $-k\cdot U=\gamma\omega_{\mathrm{lab}}(1-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf v/c)$, and equal laboratory [Lorentz factors](lorentz-factor.md) cancel. Thus the permitted directions form a [great circle](great-circle.md) on the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md). In the plane of the two laboratory [velocities](velocity.md), only the internal [angle bisector](angle-bisector.md) line remains. The [velocity](velocity.md) information alone fixes neither direction along that line nor coplanarity of the ray.
