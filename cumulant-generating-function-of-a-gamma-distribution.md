# Cumulant-generating function of a gamma distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md)

For a gamma random variable of shape $a$ and rate $r$,

$$
K_Y(t)=\log\mathbb E[e^{tY}]
=-a\log\left(1-\frac tr\right),
\qquad t<r.
$$
