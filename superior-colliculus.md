# Superior colliculus

↑ **Parent:** [Midbrain](midbrain.md)

A layered [midbrain](midbrain.md) structure involved in orienting. Its deeper layers include [neurons](neuron-split.md) with visual, auditory or somatosensory influences; their spatial and temporal interactions provide an experimental model for multisensory processing. Its role in orienting does not make it a complete explanation of conscious [multisensory perception](multisensory-perception.md).
