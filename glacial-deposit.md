# Glacial deposit

↑ **Parent:** [Sedimentary deposit](sedimentary-deposit.md)

Material deposited directly by ice or by associated meltwater. Its [sedimentary facies](sedimentary-facies.md), transport directions and age constrain a former glacial [paleoenvironment](paleoenvironment.md). Correlating such deposits across continents can test [continental reconstruction](continental-reconstruction.md), but requires distinguishing the original depositional record from later transport and deformation.
