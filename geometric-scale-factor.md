# Geometric scale factor

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)

The ratio between corresponding lengths of two geometrically similar figures. Multiplying every length by $t>0$ multiplies areas by $t^2$ and volumes by $t^3$. For [integer](integer-split.md)-sided [triangles](triangle.md), choosing a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) scale factor preserves [integer](integer-split.md) side lengths.
