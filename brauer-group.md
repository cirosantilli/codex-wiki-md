# Brauer group

↑ **Parent:** [Central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brauer_group)

The Brauer group of a field $k$ consists of [Morita equivalence](morita-equivalence.md) classes of central simple $k$-algebras. Multiplication is induced by tensor product, the identity is $[k]$, and the inverse of $[A]$ is the class of the opposite algebra $A^{\mathrm{op}}$.
