# Endoglucanase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An [endoglucanase](endoglucanase.md) cleaves internal bonds in an accessible glucan chain. On [cellulose](cellulose.md) it can generate new chain ends for complementary processive enzymes.
