# Thermodynamic entropy flux

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy production](entropy-production.md)

Local equilibrium associates the outgoing [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) $q$ with outgoing [entropy](entropy.md) flux $q/\theta$ at positive [temperature](temperature.md) $\theta$. Its [divergence](divergence.md) appears in the [Lagrangian entropy inequality](lagrangian-entropy-inequality.md), with the appropriate nominal flux in reference coordinates.
