# Film drainage

↑ **Parent:** [Thin film](thin-film.md)

Liquid outflow from a narrowing film between nearby surfaces or droplets. [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md), pressure gradients, surface [mobility](order-parameter-mobility.md) and intermolecular [forces](force-split.md) affect its rate and possible rupture.
