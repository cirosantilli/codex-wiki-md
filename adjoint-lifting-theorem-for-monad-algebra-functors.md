# Adjoint lifting theorem for monad algebra functors

↑ **Parent:** [Eilenberg-Moore category](eilenberg-moore-category.md)

For a functor between [Eilenberg-Moore categories](eilenberg-moore-category.md) lying over a right adjoint on the base categories and compatible with the monad structures, an adjoint lifting theorem constructs a left adjoint when the required reflexive coequalizers of algebra presentations exist. Applied through [power-object monadicity](power-object-monadicity.md), this converts a left adjoint of a [logical functor](logical-functor.md) into a right adjoint of that logical functor. The coequalizer hypotheses are part of the theorem; commutation with the forgetful functors alone is insufficient.
