# Doppler spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmospheric observatory](exoplanet-atmospheric-observatory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppler_spectroscopy)

The radial-velocity method detects an [exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md) through the star's orbital [Doppler shift](doppler-effect.md). It measures a projected planetary mass $M_p\sin i$, not the planetary radius. Combining it with [exoplanet transit photometry](exoplanet-transit-photometry.md) determines mass and radius when the stellar properties are known.
