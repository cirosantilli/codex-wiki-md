# Packing fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric packing](geometric-packing.md)

Occupied area or [volume](volume.md) divided by the corresponding total area or [volume](volume.md) of a [geometric packing](geometric-packing.md). For a periodic packing, it is the total object area or [volume](volume.md) within a repeat cell divided by the cell measure. It is distinct from the number fraction of objects of a given size.
