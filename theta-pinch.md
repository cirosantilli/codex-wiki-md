# Theta pinch

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

A [theta pinch](theta-pinch.md) is a cylindrical plasma configuration with axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) and azimuthal [electric current](electric-current.md). For a static equilibrium without gravity, the radial [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) gives $d[p+B^2/(8\pi)]/dr=0$ in [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md). This straight-field configuration has no field-line curvature drive of the usual interchange instability.
