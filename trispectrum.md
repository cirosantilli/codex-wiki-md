# Trispectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Bispectrum](bispectrum.md)

The [trispectrum](trispectrum.md) is the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of a fourth-order [cumulant](cumulant.md), a fourth-order counterpart of the [bispectrum](bispectrum.md). It describes connected four-point correlations; the [primordial trispectrum](primordial-trispectrum.md) applies it to cosmological random fields.
