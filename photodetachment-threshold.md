# Photodetachment threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetachment](photodetachment.md)

In the published [chlorine](chlorine.md) experiment, negative [ions](ion.md) were accelerated and mass-selected, then overlapped with a tunable [laser](laser.md) beam. A downstream [electric field](electric-field.md) deflected charged [ions](ion.md); neutral [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) continued to a plate, whose secondary [Electrons](electron.md) were counted. Scanning the [laser](laser.md) [frequency](frequency.md) produced an onset in this neutral-atom signal. The apparatus therefore inferred an [electron affinity](electron-affinity.md) from a threshold, rather than measuring the [heat](heat.md) of attachment.[§2, printed pp. 2–4, Figure 1: ion preparation, deflectors, neutral-atom secondary-emission detector and threshold fitting](isotope-shift-in-the-electron-affinity-of-chlorine-by-u-berzinsh-and-others.md)

Reversing the [laser](laser.md) direction and averaging the two thresholds corrected the first-order [Doppler shift](doppler-effect.md). A [Fabry–Pérot interferometer](fabry-perot-interferometer.md) and reference spectral lines calibrated the [frequency](frequency.md). Finite [laser](laser.md) bandwidth, unresolved [quantum state](quantum-state.md) splitting and shifts of the calibration lines limited the inference; the authors included these effects in fitting and the error estimate. The level diagram shows only the extracted gap, not a measured signal curve.[§§2.1 and 2.3, printed pp. 3–7, equation (2), Figures 2 and 5: direction reversal, calibration, bandwidth and reference-line systematic shifts](isotope-shift-in-the-electron-affinity-of-chlorine-by-u-berzinsh-and-others.md)
