# Lorentzian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Metric tensor](metric-tensor.md)

A Lorentzian metric is a smooth nondegenerate symmetric [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) with one negative and all other positive directions, or its opposite-sign convention. At each point, a nonzero tangent is timelike, null or spacelike according as $g(v,v)$ is negative, zero or positive in the first convention. Unlike a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), it gives a light cone rather than a positive norm. [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) is its constant flat model, and [proper time](proper-time.md) along a timelike curve is $\int\sqrt{-g(\dot\gamma,\dot\gamma)}\,du$.
