# Resonant series RLC response

↑ **Parent:** [Series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md)

For a [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md) driven by $V(t)=V_M\sin(\omega_0t)$ with $R>0$ and $\omega_0^2=1/(LC)$, substitution shows that $I_0=V_M\sin(\omega_0t)/R$ solves the current equation. The inductive and capacitive terms cancel at this [resonance](resonance.md). Every homogeneous contribution decays, so every solution approaches this periodic response. Its [Q factor](q-factor.md) is $L\omega_0/R$.
