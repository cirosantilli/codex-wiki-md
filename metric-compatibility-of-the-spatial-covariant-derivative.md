# Metric compatibility of the spatial covariant derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial covariant derivative](spatial-covariant-derivative.md)

For a unit timelike normal $n$, the [induced metric](induced-metric.md) is $h_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+n_\mu n_\nu$. The [spatial covariant derivative](spatial-covariant-derivative.md) projects the derivative and all free indices with the [spatial projection tensor](spatial-projection-tensor.md) $P^\mu{}_\nu=\delta^\mu{}_\nu+n^\mu n_\nu$. [Metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) gives $\nabla_\lambda h_{\mu\nu}=(\nabla_\lambda n_\mu)n_\nu+n_\mu\nabla_\lambda n_\nu$; projecting kills the normal factor in each term. Thus the [spatial covariant derivative](spatial-covariant-derivative.md) is compatible with the [induced metric](induced-metric.md).
