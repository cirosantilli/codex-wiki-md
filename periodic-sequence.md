# Periodic sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

A [sequence](sequence.md) indexed from zero with an [integer](integer-split.md) period $p\geq1$ satisfying the displayed identity for all $n\geq0$. Repeating a finite cycle is an example. The [Cesàro mean](cesaro-mean.md) article explains why its cumulative averages approach the one-cycle [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md).
