# Warm ionized medium

↑ **Parent:** [Multiphase interstellar medium](multiphase-interstellar-medium.md)

Diffuse ionized gas near $10^4\ \mathrm K$, maintained by [ionization](ionization.md) and heating balanced by radiative losses. It is physically distinct from warm gas that remains mostly neutral despite a similar kinetic [temperature](temperature-split.md).
