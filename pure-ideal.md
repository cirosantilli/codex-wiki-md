# Pure ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)

An [ideal](ideal.md) $I$ is pure when the quotient is a [flat module](flat-module.md). Equivalently every $a\in I$ satisfies $a=ab$ for some $b\in I$. The latter condition makes $I$ vanish after localizing at primes containing it, while at primes not containing it the quotient is zero. Pure ideals need not be finitely generated. They describe [flat morphisms](flat-morphism.md) that are [closed immersions](closed-immersion.md) on affine charts.
