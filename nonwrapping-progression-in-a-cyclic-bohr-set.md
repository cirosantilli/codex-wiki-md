# Nonwrapping progression in a cyclic Bohr set

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic progression in a cyclic Bohr set](arithmetic-progression-in-a-cyclic-bohr-set.md)

In a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) of arbitrary order $q$, a [Bohr set](bohr-set.md) with $d$ frequencies, defined by $\|rx/q\|\leq1/16$, contains an [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) of distinct residues of length at least $q^{1/(d+1)}/32$. Partition the $d$-dimensional cube into $Q^d$ boxes, with $Q=\lfloor q^{1/(d+1)}\rfloor$. The [pigeonhole principle](pigeonhole-principle.md) supplies $1\leq t\leq Q^d$ with $\|rt/q\|\leq1/Q$ simultaneously. The residues $jt$, $0\leq j\leq\lfloor Q/16\rfloor$, remain in the [Bohr set](bohr-set.md) and do not wrap around the cyclic group. The weaker exponent permits composite moduli without assuming that every nonzero step has large order.
