<h1 id="finite-field-szemeredi-theorem-for-four-term-arithmetic-progressions">Finite-field Szemerédi theorem for four-term arithmetic progressions</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)

For $p\geq5$ and every $\alpha>0$, a sufficiently large subset of $\mathbb F_p^n$ of density at least $\alpha$ contains a nontrivial four-term [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md).
