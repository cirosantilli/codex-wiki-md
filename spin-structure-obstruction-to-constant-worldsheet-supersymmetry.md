# Spin-structure obstruction to constant worldsheet supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid worldsheet supersymmetry boundary term](rigid-worldsheet-supersymmetry-boundary-term.md)

The local rigid [worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md) identity $\delta\mathcal L=\partial_\mu J^\mu$ does not by itself prove preservation of every [spin structure](spin-structure.md). If $X$ is periodic on a spatial circle and $\psi$ is antiperiodic, a constant $\epsilon$ gives antiperiodic $\delta X=i\bar\epsilon\psi$. It does not preserve the bosonic boundary condition. Compatible periodic fields admit the constant transformation; other sectors must be handled with the local superconformal formulation and their global spin data.
