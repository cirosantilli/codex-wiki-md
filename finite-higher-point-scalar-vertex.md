# Finite higher-point scalar vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Effective action](effective-action.md)

A finite higher-point scalar vertex is a term in the [quantum effective action](effective-action.md) with more external scalar fields than the original interaction, whose coefficient remains finite after [renormalization](renormalization.md). It can survive removal of an [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md) even when no corresponding independent [counterterm](counterterm.md) is needed. Its coefficient is then predicted by the renormalized parameters and external momenta.
