# Unit sigma-model lump and size modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Sigma-model lump](sigma-model-lump.md)

For the [O3 nonlinear sigma model](o3-nonlinear-sigma-model.md) with energy $\int|\nabla\mathbf n|^2/2$, the [rational map](rational-map-complex-analysis.md) $w(z)=\rho/(z-z_0)$, $\rho\ne0$, is a degree-one [sigma-model lump](sigma-model-lump.md). Its energy density is $4|\rho|^2/(|z-z_0|^2+|\rho|^2)^2$, whose integral is $4\pi$. The complex center $z_0$ gives its position, while $|\rho|$ gives its size and $\arg\rho$ its internal phase. Varying the size preserves energy. The limit $\rho\to0$ is singular at the center, so conserved degree under smooth deformations does not prevent concentration.
