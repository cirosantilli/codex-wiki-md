# Positivity of a quadratic polynomial across a spectral gap

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral gap](spectral-gap.md)

Let $B$ be a finite-dimensional [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) and $a<b$ real. The [spectral theorem](spectral-theorem.md) gives $v=\sum_jc_jx_j$ in an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md), so

$$
v^\dagger(B-aI)(B-bI)v=\sum_j|c_j|^2(\lambda_j-a)(\lambda_j-b).
$$

If the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) avoids the closed interval, every factor product is positive. Consequently the [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) is positive for $v\ne0$ and zero for $v=0$. More precisely it is bounded below by $\min_j[(\lambda_j-a)(\lambda_j-b)]\|v\|^2$. This is a finite-dimensional instance of testing a [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) with a polynomial in the operator.
