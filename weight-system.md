# Weight system

↑ **Parent:** [Weight-space decomposition](weight-space-decomposition.md)

The weight system of a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) is its collection of [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) with their [weight multiplicities](weight-multiplicity.md). Restriction to a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) projects each [weight](weight-representation-theory.md) to the smaller [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md), retaining and combining [weight multiplicities](weight-multiplicity.md). Grouping the resulting [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) into irreducible [weight systems](weight-system.md) computes the [branching rule](branching-rule.md).
