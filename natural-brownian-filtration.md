# Natural Brownian filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian filtration](brownian-filtration.md)

The natural Brownian filtration is the [natural filtration](natural-filtration.md) generated by a [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). Its usual augmentation adds null events and makes it right-continuous; the [Brownian martingale representation theorem](brownian-martingale-representation-theorem.md) holds for this augmented natural filtration. A larger [Brownian filtration](brownian-filtration.md) can preserve independence of future increments while containing additional randomness, so it need not have martingale representation with respect to the specified [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md).
