# Angular deflection kernel for cosmological lensing

↑ **Parent:** [Weak gravitational lensing](weak-gravitational-lensing.md)

For a source at comoving distance $\chi_s$ in a spatially flat universe, define deflection as apparent minus unlensed source direction. The transverse [Weyl lensing potential](weyl-lensing-potential.md) gradient along the ray receives the distance weight $(\chi_s-\chi)/\chi_s$. Integrating the transverse photon acceleration twice and dividing the source displacement by $\chi_s$ gives the formula. Reversing the definition of the deflection reverses its sign. Its angular lensing potential is $2\int_0^{\chi_s}(\chi_s-\chi)\Psi/(\chi_s\chi)\,d\chi$.
