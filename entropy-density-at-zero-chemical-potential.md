# Entropy density at zero chemical potential

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy density](entropy-density.md)

For an extensive equilibrium system, the [thermodynamic Euler relation](euler-relation-in-thermodynamics.md) gives $E=TS-PV+\mu N$. At zero [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md), divide by $V$ to obtain $s=(\rho+P)/T$. Equivalently, $dP=(\rho+P)dT/T$ makes

$$
d\left(\frac{(\rho+P)V}{T}\right)=\frac{d(\rho V)+P\,dV}{T}.
$$

The [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) identifies this as $dS$; the [thermodynamic Euler relation](euler-relation-in-thermodynamics.md) fixes the entropy normalization. For adiabatic expansion satisfying the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md), the numerator on the right vanishes, proving [cosmological entropy conservation](cosmological-entropy-conservation.md).
