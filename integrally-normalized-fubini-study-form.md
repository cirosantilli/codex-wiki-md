# Integrally normalized Fubini-Study form

↑ **Parent:** [Fubini-Study form](fubini-study-form.md)

The form $\omega_{\rm int}=\frac{i}{2\pi}\partial\bar\partial\log(1+\sum|z_j|^2)$ patches on [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md). The dual tautological metric on $\mathcal O(1)$ identifies it with normalized [Chern curvature](chern-curvature.md), so $[\omega_{\rm int}]=c_1(\mathcal O(1))$. With the unnormalized convention $i\partial\bar\partial\log(1+\sum|z_j|^2)$, divide by $2\pi$ to obtain this integral class.
