# Energy-preserving change of rolling radius

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere rolling in a symmetric groove](sphere-rolling-in-a-symmetric-groove.md)

A [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) rolling with effective [radius](radius.md) $h$ has [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $(m+I/h^2)v^2/2$. If a transition changes the rolling constraint while preserving this total [energy](energy-split.md), the translational [speed](speed.md) changes according to the title formula. Maintaining the same [speed](speed.md) would generally require [energy](energy-split.md) loss or gain. The energy-preserving condition is an assumption about the transition, not a generic property of sudden impacts.
