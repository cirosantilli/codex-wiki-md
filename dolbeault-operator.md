# Dolbeault operator

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

The complexified cotangent bundle decomposes into $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ parts. The Dolbeault operator is the type-$(0,1)$ component of the exterior derivative, $\bar\partial:\Omega^{p,q}\to\Omega^{p,q+1}$, and integrability gives $\bar\partial^2=0$.

Together with its $(1,0)$ counterpart, the [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md) decomposes the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) on complex differential forms.
