# Consecutive-base induction for additive semigroups

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical semigroup](numerical-semigroup.md)

Suppose an additively closed subset $M$ of the [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md) contains a positive [integer](integer-split.md) $a$ and every [integer](integer-split.md) from $N$ to $N+a-1$. Then it contains every [integer](integer-split.md) at least $N$. The first $a$ values are the base cases. For $n\ge N+a$, the smaller number $n-a\ge N$ belongs to $M$ by [strong induction](strong-induction.md), and closure under addition gives $n=(n-a)+a\in M$. Equivalently, write $n=N+qa+r$ by division with remainder, with $0\le r<a$; start at $N+r\in M$ and add $a$ exactly $q$ times.
