# Period lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic function](elliptic-function.md)

Two real-linearly independent [periods](period-of-a-function.md) $\omega_1,\omega_2$ generate the period lattice

$$
\Lambda=\mathbb Z\omega_1+\mathbb Z\omega_2.
$$
