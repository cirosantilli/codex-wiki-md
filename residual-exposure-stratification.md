# Residual exposure stratification

↑ **Parent:** [Mendelian randomization](mendelian-randomization.md)

Residual exposure stratification subtracts the genetically predicted component of an exposure before forming exposure strata. Under a suitable additive first-stage model, this avoids directly conditioning on the component caused by the instrument and can reduce the resulting [collider bias](collider-bias.md).
