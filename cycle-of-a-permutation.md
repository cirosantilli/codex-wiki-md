# Cycle of a permutation

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

For a [permutation](permutation.md) $\sigma$ of a [finite set](finite-set.md), the cycle containing $x$ is its [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) under the iterates of $\sigma$. Its length $k$ is the least positive integer with $\sigma^k x=x$; finiteness and invertibility guarantee such a return. Two cycles that meet are the same, because any iterate of a common point belongs to both. Thus the cycles partition the [set](set-split.md). In [cycle notation](cycle-notation.md), the displayed cycle sends each listed element to the next and the last one back to $x$.
