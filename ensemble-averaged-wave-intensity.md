# Ensemble-averaged wave intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Wave propagation in a random medium](wave-propagation-in-a-random-medium.md)

For a random complex wave amplitude $E$, its mean squared amplitude is $I=\mathbb E[|E|^2]$. Expanding $E=m+(E-m)$, with $m=\mathbb E[E]$, gives

$$
 \mathbb E[|E|^2]=|m|^2+\mathbb E[|E-m|^2].
$$

The mixed terms vanish. Thus the squared [coherent field](coherent-wave-field.md) is only one part of the mean intensity; the other is the diffuse contribution. A fixed acoustic or optical normalization converts this squared-amplitude quantity to physical [energy flux](energy-flux.md). This ensemble mean differs from the angularly averaged radiative-transfer [mean intensity](mean-intensity.md).
