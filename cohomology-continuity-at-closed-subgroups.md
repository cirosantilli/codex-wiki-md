# Cohomology continuity at closed subgroups

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous cohomology of a profinite group](continuous-cohomology-of-a-profinite-group.md)

For a closed subgroup $P$ of a [profinite group](profinite-group.md) $G$ and a discrete $G$-module $M$, restriction gives $H^q(P,M)=\varinjlim_{U\supseteq P,\ U\ \mathrm{open}}H^q(U,M)$. Thus a class restricting to zero on $P$ already restricts to zero on one such open subgroup. The property follows by extending continuous finite-image cochains and their identities to a sufficiently small open neighborhood of $P$.
