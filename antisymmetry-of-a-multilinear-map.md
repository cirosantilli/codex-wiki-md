# Antisymmetry of a multilinear map

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating multilinear map](alternating-multilinear-map.md)

A [multilinear map](multilinear-map.md) is antisymmetric when swapping two arguments negates its value. Over a field of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from $2$, this is equivalent to being an [alternating multilinear map](alternating-multilinear-map.md): repeated arguments give $T=-T$, hence $T=0$; conversely expand $T(\ldots,u+v,\ldots,u+v,\ldots)=0$ to get the sign change. In [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) $2$, the repeated-argument condition is stronger than the sign-change condition alone.
