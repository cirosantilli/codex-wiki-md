# Mutual information

↑ **Parent:** [Information theory](information-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutual_information)

The mutual information between discrete random variables is

$$
I(X;Y)=H(Y)-H(Y\mid X)
=H(X)+H(Y)-H(X,Y).
$$
