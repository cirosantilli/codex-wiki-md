# Decomposing sphere system for a knot

↑ **Parent:** [Splitting sphere of a knot](splitting-sphere-of-a-knot.md)

A finite collection of disjoint [splitting spheres of a knot](splitting-sphere-of-a-knot.md) encodes a [connected sum of knots](connected-sum-of-knots.md). Cut along every [sphere](sphere.md) and fill each spherical boundary with a [three-ball](three-ball.md) containing a straight arc joining its two marked endpoints. The resulting oriented [knots](knot.md) are the factors. The system is complete when its nontrivial factors are [prime knots](prime-knot.md).
