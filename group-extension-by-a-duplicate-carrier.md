# Group extension by a duplicate carrier

↑ **Parent:** [Direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md)

If a [group](group-split.md) has carrier $A$ and a disjoint [set](set-split.md) $B$ is in [bijection](bijection.md) with $A$, identify $A\cup B$ with $A\times C_2$, sending the original carrier to $A\times\{0\}$. Transporting the direct-product multiplication gives a larger [group operation](group-operation.md) that agrees exactly with the old one on $A$. A maximal partial [group](group-split.md) on a fixed carrier universe therefore has complement of [cardinality](cardinality.md) strictly below its own, using cardinal comparability.
