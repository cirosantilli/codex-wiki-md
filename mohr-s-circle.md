<h1 id="mohr-s-circle">Mohr's circle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohr's_circle)

A geometric representation of resolved [normal stress](normal-stress.md) and [shear stress](shear-stress.md). For two [principal stresses](principal-stress.md), its centre is $C=(\sigma_1+\sigma_3)/2$ and radius $R=(\sigma_1-\sigma_3)/2$. A physical plane-normal rotation by $\theta$ corresponds to $2\theta$ on the circle, with the sense depending on the shear-sign convention. Named for [Christian Otto Mohr](christian-otto-mohr.md).
