# Second-derivative estimate at a flat Dirichlet boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md)

For a [classical solution](classical-solution.md) of $\Delta u=f$ on a half-ball with zero data on its flat face, a tangential [Caccioppoli inequality](caccioppoli-inequality.md) controls every second [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md) having at least one tangential index. The equation recovers the remaining second [normal derivative](normal-derivative.md). The resulting [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md) estimate has a dimension-only constant for the fixed radii one and two. Nonzero data are handled by [homogenization of Dirichlet boundary data](homogenization-of-dirichlet-boundary-data.md), adding the $W^{2,2}$ norm of the supplied boundary extension. No condition is imposed on the curved face because the [smooth cutoff function](smooth-cutoff-function.md) vanishes there.
