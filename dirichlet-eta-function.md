# Dirichlet eta function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_eta_function)

For $\Re s>0$, the alternating [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md)

$$
\eta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}n^{-s}
$$

converges locally uniformly and defines a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md). For $\Re s>1$,

$$
\eta(s)=(1-2^{1-s})\zeta(s).
$$
