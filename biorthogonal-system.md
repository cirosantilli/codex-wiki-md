# Biorthogonal system

↑ **Parent:** [Dual pairing](dual-pairing.md)

For a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md) $X$, a family $(x_i,f_i)_{i\in I}$ in $X\times X^*$ is biorthogonal when the displayed Kronecker-delta equations hold. Its [vectors](vector.md) are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md): applying $f_i$ to a finite linear dependence isolates its $i$th coefficient. No inner product is required, and the family need not span a dense [subspace](vector-subspace.md). If its [linear span](linear-span.md) is dense, each prescribed coordinate functional is unique by [continuity](continuous-function.md). For a countable total family, the finite sums $P_nx=\sum_{i\leq n}f_i(x)x_i$ are bounded [projection operators](projection-linear-algebra.md), since biorthogonality gives $P_n^2=P_n$.
