# Spectator multiplicities in gauge beta functions

↑ **Parent:** [Beta function (physics)](beta-function-physics.md)

For a field in a tensor-product [representation](group-representation.md) of a product [gauge group](gauge-group.md), its contribution to the [beta function](beta-function-physics.md) of factor $i$ includes the dimensions of all other factors. Those indices label independent copies for that factor. For an Abelian factor, replace its [Dynkin index](dynkin-index.md) by the squared charge. Thus a quark weak doublet contributes three copies to the weak beta function and two copies to the colour beta function; [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) sums include all six components. [Chirality](chirality-physics.md) conjugation changes a [representation](group-representation.md) and the sign of an Abelian charge, but not its quadratic index.
