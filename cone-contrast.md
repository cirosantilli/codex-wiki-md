# Cone contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Color vision](color-vision.md)

A receptor catch change expressed relative to a positive adapted-background catch. The same absolute increment can represent different contrasts on different backgrounds. These coordinates are useful for isolating signals without asserting that all [neurons](neuron-split.md) explicitly divide in this way.
