# Vacuum-normalized effective action of a free scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Effective action](effective-action.md)

Write $S=\tfrac12\phi A\phi$ and use the vacuum prescription $A\mapsto A+i0$. Completing the [Gaussian path integral](gaussian-path-integral.md) gives $Z[J]/Z[0]=\exp[-iJA^{-1}J/2]$, hence $W=-JA^{-1}J/2$, $\varphi=-A^{-1}J$ and $\Gamma=\varphi A\varphi/2=S[\varphi]$. The source-independent [determinant](determinant.md) cancels in vacuum normalization. Without that normalization, the equality holds up to a field-independent vacuum constant.
