# Residue theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Residue_theorem)

If $f$ is [holomorphic](holomorphic-function.md) on and inside a positively oriented closed contour except for finitely many isolated singularities $a_j$ inside it, then

$$
\oint f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_j\operatorname{Res}(f,a_j).
$$
