# Arithmetic derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)

The arithmetic derivative on [positive integers](positive-integer.md) is defined by $p\prime=1$ for every [prime](prime-number-split.md) $p$ and the [product rule](product-rule.md) $(ab)\prime=a\prime b+ab\prime$. For $n=\prod_i p_i^{e_i}$, these rules give $n\prime=n\sum_i e_i/p_i$. In particular, $(p^e)\prime=e p^{e-1}$.
