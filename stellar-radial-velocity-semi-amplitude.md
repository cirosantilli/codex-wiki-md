# Stellar radial-velocity semi-amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar reflex motion](stellar-reflex-motion.md)

Half the peak-to-peak line-of-sight orbital [velocity](velocity.md) range of a [star](star.md) in a single-companion [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md). It equals $K=2\pi a_\star\sin i/[P\sqrt{1-e^2}]$, where $a_\star$ is the stellar barycentric [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md), $P$ the [orbital period](orbital-period.md), $i$ the sky-plane [orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md), and $e$ the [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md). The [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md) formula follows from [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md) for the relative [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md), $a^3=G(M_\star+M_p)P^2/(4\pi^2)$.
