# Kernels of quasi-coherent sheaf morphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md)

On an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md), a morphism between [quasi-coherent sheaves](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) is determined by the induced map on [global sections](global-section.md), because its maps on [principal open subsets](principal-open-subscheme.md) are their [localizations](localization-of-a-ring.md). [Exactness of localization](exactness-of-localization.md) gives the displayed identity. Thus the [kernel sheaf](kernel-sheaf.md) of a morphism between [quasi-coherent sheaves](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) is quasi-coherent on an arbitrary scheme, by applying the affine argument locally. No finite generation is needed.
