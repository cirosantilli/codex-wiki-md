# Martingale difference sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

A martingale difference sequence is an [adapted](adapted-process.md) sequence of integrable random variables $D_n$, $n\geq1$, with $\mathbb E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=0$. Its partial sums are a [martingale](martingale-split.md), and martingale increments give such sequences. If the differences are square-integrable, they are [uncorrelated random variables](uncorrelated-random-variables.md): for $j<k$, conditioning on $\mathcal F_{k-1}$ gives $\mathbb E D_jD_k=0$.
