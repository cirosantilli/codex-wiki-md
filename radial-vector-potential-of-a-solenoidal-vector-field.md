# Radial vector potential of a solenoidal vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector potential](vector-potential.md)

Let $B$ be a [continuously differentiable](continuously-differentiable-function.md) [solenoidal vector field](solenoidal-vector-field.md) on an open [star-shaped set](star-shaped-set.md) containing the origin. Then

$$
A(x)=\left(\int_0^1tB(tx)\,dt\right)\times x
$$

is a [vector potential](vector-potential.md) for $B$. For $D=\int_0^1tB(tx)\,dt$, differentiation gives $\nabla\cdot D=0$ and $\nabla\times(D\times x)=2D+(x\cdot\nabla)D=\int_0^1\partial_t(t^2B(tx))\,dt=B(x)$. Boundedness near the origin removes the lower endpoint. A field defined only on a punctured domain need not satisfy these hypotheses.
