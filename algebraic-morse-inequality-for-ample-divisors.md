# Algebraic Morse inequality for ample divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Big divisor](big-divisor.md)

For [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) rational Cartier classes $B,C$ on an integral projective $n$-fold with $n\ge1$, $B^n>nB^{n-1}\cdot C$ implies $B-C$ is big. Scale to [very ample](very-ample-line-bundle.md) integral divisors. Choose an effective [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $G\in|C|$. Repeated restriction gives $h^0(m(B-C))\ge h^0(mB)-m h^0(G,mB)$: multiply each negatively twisted restriction by a section avoiding its associated points to inject it into $\mathcal O_G(mB)$. [Asymptotic Riemann–Roch](asymptotic-riemann-roch.md) and [Serre vanishing](serre-vanishing.md) give the positive leading lower bound along sufficiently divisible section indices after undoing the scaling. No complex-analytic Morse theory or characteristic-zero vanishing is used.
