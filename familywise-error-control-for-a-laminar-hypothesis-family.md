# Familywise error control for a laminar hypothesis family

↑ **Parent:** [Familywise error rate](familywise-error-rate.md)

For nonempty index sets forming a [laminar family of sets](laminar-family-of-sets.md), reject an [intersection hypothesis](intersection-hypothesis.md) when the displayed adjusted value is at most $\alpha$. The inclusion-maximal true sets are disjoint and have total size at most $m$. Any false rejection forces some maximal true $J$ to have $p_J\leq\alpha|J|/m$. Validity and the [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) control the [familywise error rate](familywise-error-rate.md) by $\alpha$, without independence or a complete closure.
