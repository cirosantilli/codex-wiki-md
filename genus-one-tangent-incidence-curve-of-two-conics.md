# Genus one tangent incidence curve of two conics

↑ **Parent:** [Dual conic](dual-conic.md)

If two [smooth plane conics](smooth-plane-conic.md) over $\mathbb C$ meet at four distinct points, their tangent incidence curve is a smooth projective [genus one curve](genus-one-curve.md). Projection to $C_2$ is a double cover branched at those four intersections. The tangent equation becomes a quadratic whose discriminant cuts out $C_1\cap C_2$; its four simple zeros make the cover smooth and connected. The [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) then gives genus one.
