# Electrostatic deflection by finite parallel plates

↑ **Parent:** [Electric field](electric-field-split.md)

An [electron](electron.md) accelerated through [voltage](voltage.md) $U_a$ crosses deflecting plates of length $\ell$ and separation $d$. If their [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is uniform and their centre is distance $D$ from a screen, constant transverse [acceleration](acceleration.md) inside the plates followed by free drift gives the displayed screen displacement. It includes the displacement accumulated inside the plates. The plate-exit displacement is $x\ell/(2D)$, which must be smaller than the half-gap for a centrally injected beam to pass.
