# Initial curvature incompatibility at a moment-free beam end

↑ **Parent:** [Simply supported beam normal modes](simply-supported-beam-normal-modes.md)

A prescribed initial beam profile may vanish at a pivot while having nonzero endpoint [curvature](curvature.md). If the post-release pivot is moment-free, its [bending moment](bending-moment.md) condition is $EIy_{xx}=0$. The data then cannot have a classical solution with continuous [curvature](curvature.md) at the initial boundary corners. A finite-energy [weak solution](weak-solution.md) can still represent release from preparing end moments. For example $y_0=ax(x-L)$ has odd [sine](sine.md) coefficients proportional to $n^{-3}$; $\sum n^4|A_n|^2$ converges, giving finite bending [energy](energy-split.md). The [displacement](displacement.md) series takes the prescribed initial shape, but termwise endpoint second [derivatives](derivative.md) cannot be identified with its interior [curvature](curvature.md) limit. This conservative beam evolution does not have the immediate smoothing of a [heat equation](heat-equation.md).
