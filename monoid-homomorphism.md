# Monoid homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid](monoid.md)

A [monoid](monoid.md) homomorphism $h:M\to N$ preserves multiplication and identity: $h(xy)=h(x)h(y)$ and $h(1_M)=1_N$. It therefore preserves every finite ordered product, including the empty product. The [monoids](monoid.md) with these maps form the category $\mathbf{Mon}$.
