# Grafted polymer chain

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

A [polymer](polymer-split.md) chain tethered by an end to a surface. Its surface [number density](number-density.md) $\sigma_g$ sets a typical lateral separation $D\sim\sigma_g^{-1/2}$. Comparing $D$ with the isolated coil size determines whether grafted chains behave almost independently or must stretch cooperatively away from the surface.
