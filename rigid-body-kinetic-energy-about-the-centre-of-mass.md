# Rigid-body kinetic energy about the centre of mass

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

Expanding the squared [velocity](velocity.md) $v_G+\omega\times r$ and integrating over mass removes the mixed term because the centre-of-mass first moment is zero. The remaining rotational [integral](integral-split.md) is half the [scalar product](dot-product.md) of [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) and [angular momentum about the centre of mass](angular-momentum-about-the-centre-of-mass.md). [Principal axes](principal-axis.md) diagonalize that rotational [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md).
