# Stueckelberg mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Massive p-form field](massive-p-form-field.md)

For $p\ge1$, introduce a $(p-1)$-form $C$ and replace the mass term of a [massive p-form field](massive-p-form-field.md) by one built from $A-m^{-1}dC$. The transformations $A\mapsto A+d\Lambda$, $C\mapsto C+m\Lambda$ leave this combination and $dA$ invariant. Gauge fixing $C=0$ recovers the original massive theory. Its massless-component counts add as $\binom{D-2}{p}+\binom{D-2}{p-1}=\binom{D-1}{p}$, explaining the longitudinal degrees of freedom and the corresponding assembly of nonzero [Kaluza-Klein modes](kaluza-klein-mode.md).
