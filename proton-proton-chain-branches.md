# Proton-proton chain branches

↑ **Parent:** [Proton–proton chain](proton-proton-chain.md)

The [proton–proton chain](proton-proton-chain.md) first produces deuterium by a weak reaction and then helium-3 by proton capture. pp I ends with ${}^3\mathrm{He}+{}^3\mathrm{He}\to{}^4\mathrm{He}+2p$. pp II passes through beryllium-7 [electron capture](electron-capture.md) to lithium-7 and then proton capture. pp III passes through boron-8 [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md) and beryllium-8 breakup. Each branch produces one net helium-4 nucleus from four protons, but the [neutrino](neutrino.md) spectrum and deposited heat differ. In pp II the net weak reactions involve one emitted positron and one captured electron.
