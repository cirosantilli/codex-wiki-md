# Burnside matrix-algebra theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Artin–Wedderburn theorem](artin-wedderburn-theorem.md)

For a nonzero finite-dimensional complex [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $V$, a unital [matrix algebra](matrix-algebra.md) acting irreducibly on $V$ is the full endomorphism algebra. Its [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) annihilates $V$: otherwise the radical times $V$ is all of $V$, contradicting nilpotence. Faithfulness kills the radical, and the [Artin–Wedderburn theorem](artin-wedderburn-theorem.md) then leaves one simple matrix factor acting on its standard column [module](module-mathematics.md). This is a matrix-algebra theorem, distinct from the orbit-counting [Burnside lemma](burnside-s-lemma.md) and the finite-group [Burnside's theorem](burnside-s-theorem.md).
