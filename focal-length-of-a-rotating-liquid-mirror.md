# Focal length of a rotating liquid mirror

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A liquid in steady rigid rotation has a free surface $z-z_0=\Omega^2r^2/(2g)$. Comparing with a paraboloid $z-z_0=r^2/(4f)$ gives $f=g/(2\Omega^2)$. The result follows from [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) in the rotating frame and is independent of [density](density.md).
