# Kurepa-family hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Kurepa tree](kurepa-tree.md)

There is $\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal P(\omega_1)$ of size $\aleph_2$ such that $\{X\cap\alpha:X\in\mathcal F\}$ is countable for every $\alpha<\omega_1$. Its initial-segment tree, pruned and leveled to be normal, is a [Kurepa tree](kurepa-tree.md).
