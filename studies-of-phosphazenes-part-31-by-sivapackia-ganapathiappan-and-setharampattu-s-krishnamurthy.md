Full title: _Studies of Phosphazenes. Part 31. Kinetic Studies of the Reactions of Hexachlorocyclotriphosphazene with Aromatic Primary Amines: Evidence for the Intermediacy of a Reactive Three-co-ordinated Phosphorus(V) Species_.

[https://doi.org/10.1039/DT9870000585](https://doi.org/10.1039/DT9870000585)

Journal of the Chemical Society, Dalton Transactions, 585–591. Kinetics with aromatic amines and methanol trapping support a base-promoted elimination/addition route under stated conditions; direct substitution remains important in other regimes.
