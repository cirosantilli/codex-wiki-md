# Kick-direction binary survival criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Supernova kick in a binary star](supernova-kick-in-a-binary-star.md)

For an impulsive [supernova kick in a binary star](supernova-kick-in-a-binary-star.md) initially in a [circular Kepler orbit](circular-kepler-orbit.md), let $\beta$ be the remaining total mass fraction and $\alpha$ the kick speed divided by the original relative speed. The displayed post-kick [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) is negative for a bound pair and positive for escape. Over all directions, the velocity factor ranges from $(1-\alpha)^2$ to $(1+\alpha)^2$. Some direction leaves a bound pair exactly when $2\beta>(1-\alpha)^2$; some direction permits positive-energy escape exactly when $2\beta<(1+\alpha)^2$. Equalities are marginal [parabolic Kepler orbits](parabolic-trajectory.md). The circular initial orbit and impulsive approximation are essential.
