# Disappearance rate

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction rate](reaction-rate.md)

The [disappearance rate](disappearance-rate.md) of a [reactant](reactant.md) A is its positive net loss of [concentration](concentration.md) per unit time, $v_{\mathrm A}=-d[\mathrm A]/dt$. For the fixed-[volume](volume.md), single-[chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) setting of [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md), consuming $a$ units of A per conversion gives $v_{\mathrm A}=av$. The factor belongs to the [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md); stating it is essential when quoting a [rate constant](rate-constant.md). A negative net [disappearance rate](disappearance-rate.md) means that A is accumulating.
