<h1 id="holomorphic-poincare-lemma">Holomorphic Poincaré lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic de Rham complex](holomorphic-de-rham-complex.md)

On a star-shaped holomorphic coordinate neighbourhood, a closed [holomorphic differential form](holomorphic-differential-form.md) of positive degree has a holomorphic primitive. Radial integration gives the homotopy operator. This proves exactness of the [holomorphic de Rham complex](holomorphic-de-rham-complex.md) on stalks.
