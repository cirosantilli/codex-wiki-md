# Metabolic RNA labelling

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

Incorporation of a traceable precursor into newly synthesized [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md). A pulse and chase separates labelled-cohort survival from continuing synthesis, provided incorporation, chase efficiency, toxicity and reutilization are controlled.
