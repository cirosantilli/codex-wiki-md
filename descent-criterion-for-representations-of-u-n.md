<h1 id="descent-criterion-for-representations-of-u-n">Descent criterion for representations of U(n)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

The map $(z,U)\mapsto zU$ identifies the [unitary group](unitary-group.md) with $(U(1)\times SU(n))/K$, where $K=\{(\zeta^k,\zeta^{-k}I_n):\zeta=e^{2\pi i/n}\}$. If an irreducible [SU(n) tensor transformation](su-n-tensor-transformation.md) has $b$ boxes in its [Young diagram](young-diagram.md), the central scalar $\zeta I_n$ acts as $\zeta^b$. Therefore, for integer $q$, $(z,U)\mapsto z^qR(U)$ is trivial on $K$ precisely when $\zeta^{q-b}=1$, proving the criterion. Adding a full $n$-box column changes $b$ by $n$ and leaves the condition unchanged. In particular a lift with trivial first-factor [U(1) charge](u-1-charge.md) descends only if its central action is trivial; this does not prevent other lifts of the same [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) [representation](group-representation.md) from descending.
