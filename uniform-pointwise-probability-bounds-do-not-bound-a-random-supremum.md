# Uniform pointwise probability bounds do not bound a random supremum

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic order](stochastic-order.md)

A bound on $\sup_x\mathbb P(|Z_n(x)|>t)$ does not give the same bound on $\mathbb P(\sup_x|Z_n(x)|>t)$. Even [independent](independent-random-variables.md) standard [normal random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md) each have uniformly bounded tails while their [Gaussian maximum](gaussian-maximum.md) over $N$ indices grows at scale $\sqrt{\log N}$. This distinction matters in [nonparametric statistics](nonparametric-statistics-split.md) when a shrinking bandwidth permits many spatial windows.
