# Tangent-axis inertia of a spherical shell

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md)

For uniform [density](density.md) between concentric radii $R_1,R_2$, subtract the missing solid core's central [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) and then shift the axis by $R_2$. Here $M=4\pi\rho(R_2^3-R_1^3)/3$. The thin-shell limit gives $I=5MR_2^2/3$, while a full solid sphere gives $7MR_2^2/5$.
