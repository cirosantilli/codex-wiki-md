# Locally uniform limit of univalent functions

↑ **Parent:** [Univalent function](univalent-function.md)

A [locally uniform convergence](locally-uniform-convergence.md) limit of [univalent functions](univalent-function.md) on a domain is either constant or univalent. For a nonconstant limit $f$, suppose distinct $x,y$ satisfy $f(x)=f(y)$. Choose a small closed disk around $x$ excluding $y$ with $f-f(y)$ nonzero on its boundary. The [Rouche theorem](rouche-s-theorem.md) then forces $f_j-f_j(y)$ to have a zero in that disk for large $j$, contradicting injectivity of $f_j$. This supplies the injectivity step in an extremal proof of the [Riemann mapping theorem](riemann-mapping-theorem.md) without assuming it as a special lemma.
