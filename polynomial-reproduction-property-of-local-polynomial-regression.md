# Polynomial reproduction property of local polynomial regression

↑ **Parent:** [Local polynomial regression](local-polynomial-regression.md)

Local polynomial weights of degree $p$ reproduce every [multivariate polynomial](multivariate-polynomial.md) of total degree at most $p$: applying the estimator to its design values returns its value at the target point.
