<h1 id="cretaceous-palaeogene-boundary">Cretaceous–Palaeogene boundary</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Geological time scale](geological-time-scale.md)

The geological boundary at approximately 66 million years ago, associated with a major mass extinction. Deep lineage splits, ecological radiation and first [fossil](fossil.md) occurrence are distinct events in assessing mammalian history.
