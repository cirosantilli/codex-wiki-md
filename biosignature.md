# Biosignature

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosignature)

A [biosignature](biosignature.md) is observable chemical, morphological or other evidence attributable to biological activity after assessing plausible abiotic explanations. It can concern present or past life on Earth or elsewhere. An [exoplanet biosignature](exoplanet-biosignature.md) is the remote planetary specialization, requiring atmospheric and environmental context.
