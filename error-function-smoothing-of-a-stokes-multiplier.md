# Error-function smoothing of a Stokes multiplier

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian saddle coalescing with a simple pole](gaussian-saddle-coalescing-with-a-simple-pole.md)

The pole contour in a [Gaussian saddle coalescing with a simple pole](gaussian-saddle-coalescing-with-a-simple-pole.md) changes the multiplier smoothly. For the lower contour, $\int_{\mathcal C_-}e^{-u^2-i\mu u}\,du/u=i\pi\operatorname{erfc}(\mu/2)$. This follows by integrating $-i\sqrt\pi e^{-\mu^2/4}$ and adding the indentation contribution $i\pi$. The transition width is inverse square root of the large parameter; on a [Stokes line](stokes-line.md) the multiplier takes half its switched-on value.
