# Attributional style

↑ **Parent:** [Psychology](psychology-split.md)

A tendency to explain outcomes using particular kinds of causes. The helplessness reformulation distinguishes internal from external, stable from unstable, and global from specific explanations to address self-esteem, persistence and generalization.
