# Equivalence of categories

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor.md)

An equivalence consists of [functors](functor.md) $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ and $G:\mathcal D\to\mathcal C$ with [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) isomorphisms $GF\cong1_{\mathcal C}$ and $FG\cong1_{\mathcal D}$. A [full and faithful functor](full-and-faithful-functor.md) which is essentially surjective, meaning every target object is isomorphic to an image object, is part of an equivalence under the usual choice convention. An isomorphism of categories is stronger: it has a strictly inverse functor.
