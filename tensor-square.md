# Tensor square

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor power](tensor-power.md)

The tensor square is the second [tensor power](tensor-power.md) $V^{\otimes2}=V\otimes V$. Over a field of characteristic different from two it decomposes as the [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of the [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) and [exterior square](exterior-square.md).
