# Fundamental form of a Hermitian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian manifold](hermitian-manifold.md)

For the underlying [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) $g$ and [complex structure](complex-structure.md) $J$, the fundamental form is $\omega(u,v)=g(Ju,v)$. It is a [real (1, 1)-form](real-1-1-form.md). In complex dimension $n$, the compatible orientation has [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md) $\omega^n/n!$, and its [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) satisfies

$$
*\omega=\frac{\omega^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}.
$$
