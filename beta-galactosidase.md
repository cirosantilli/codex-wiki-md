# Beta-galactosidase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An enzyme hydrolysing beta-galactosides. In the [lac operon](lac-operon.md) system, its processing of lactose includes production of [allolactose](allolactose.md), which regulates [LacI](laci.md).
