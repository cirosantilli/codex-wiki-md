# Time-reversal reciprocity of the scattering matrix

↑ **Parent:** [T-symmetry](t-symmetry.md)

If a Hermitian interaction-picture Hamiltonian satisfies $\hat TV(t)\hat T^{-1}=V(-t)$ for an [antiunitary operator](antiunitary-operator.md), its [Dyson series](dyson-series.md) conjugates $(-i)^n$ to $i^n$. The substitution $t_j\mapsto-t_j$ converts ordered integration to anti-ordered integration, giving $\hat TS\hat T^{-1}=S^\dagger$. [Antiunitarity](antiunitary-operator.md) then gives the displayed reciprocity: the time-reversed process has the reversed outgoing state as its incoming state and vice versa. It does not merely reverse the momenta while leaving their incoming/outgoing roles fixed.
