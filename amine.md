# Amine

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance.md)

A simple neutral [amine](amine.md) has [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) bonded to one, two, or three groups attached through [carbon](carbon.md), with [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) filling the remaining positions in the neutral three-bond form. These are the primary, secondary, and tertiary forms respectively. An [amide](amide.md) has different connectivity to a [carbonyl group](carbonyl-group.md). [McMurry: classification of amines](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/24-1-naming-amines)
