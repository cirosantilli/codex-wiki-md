<h1 id="blume-capel-model">Blume–Capel model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blume–Capel_model)

The Blume–Capel model is a [spin model](spin-model.md) with $\sigma_i\in\{-1,0,1\}$ and Hamiltonian

$$
H=-J\sum_{\langle i,j\rangle}\sigma_i\sigma_j+D\sum_i\sigma_i^2-h\sum_i\sigma_i.
$$

Competition between the [Ising interaction](ising-model.md) and the single-ion anisotropy $D$ produces a [tricritical point](tricritical-point.md) in its phase diagram.
