# Barrier option

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent claim](contingent-claim.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrier_option)

A [barrier option](barrier-option.md) activates or expires according to whether the underlying price reaches a prescribed level during its lifetime. Its state must include whether the barrier has already been reached; the current price alone generally does not determine its remaining payoff.
