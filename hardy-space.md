# Hardy space

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic analysis](harmonic-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardy_space)

For $0<p<∞$, an analytic [Hardy space](hardy-space.md) controls the integral means of a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) as it approaches a domain boundary. On the disk, $H^p$ requires $\sup_{0<r<1}\int|F(re^{i\theta})|^p\,d\theta<\infty$; on the upper half-plane it requires $\sup_{y>0}\int_{\mathbb R}|f(x+iy)|^p\,dx<\infty$. For $p=\infty$ use a supremum norm. These analytic spaces differ from the [harmonic Hardy space of the upper half-space](harmonic-hardy-space-of-the-upper-half-space.md); the [Hardy space of the circle](hardy-space-of-the-circle.md) is the boundary Hilbert-space realization of the disk $H^2$ space.
