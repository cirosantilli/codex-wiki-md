# Soliton time delay

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field-theory soliton](classical-field-theory-soliton-split.md)

If a labeled [soliton](soliton.md) approaches $x=vt+a_-$ before a collision and $x=vt+a_+$ afterwards, its spatial shift is $\Delta x=a_+-a_-$. For $v\ne0$, define its time delay at a fixed spatial point by $\Delta t=-\Delta x/v$. Thus a positive forward shift of a right-moving [soliton](soliton.md) is a negative time delay. For a stationary [soliton](soliton.md), the spatial shift is well defined but this time-delay definition is not.
