# Symmetry energy

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetry_energy)

[Nuclear symmetry energy](symmetry-energy.md) is the energy cost associated with proton-neutron imbalance in nuclear matter or finite [nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md). A finite-nucleus [semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md) includes the positive asymmetry term $a_A(N-Z)^2/A$. The [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) and strong-interaction dynamics both contribute.
