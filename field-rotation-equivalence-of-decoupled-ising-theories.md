# Field-rotation equivalence of decoupled Ising theories

↑ **Parent:** [Coupled Ising fixed points near four dimensions](coupled-ising-fixed-points-near-four-dimensions.md)

The [orthogonal transformation](orthogonal-transformation.md) $\psi_\pm=(\phi_1\pm\phi_2)/\sqrt2$ takes $g(\phi_1^4+\phi_2^4+6\phi_1^2\phi_2^2)$ to $2g(\psi_+^4+\psi_-^4)$. These are the same two decoupled [Ising models](ising-model.md) expressed in rotated fields.
