<h1 id="zernike-s-discovery-of-phase-contrast">Zernike's discovery of phase contrast</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-contrast microscopy](phase-contrast-microscopy.md)

[Frits Zernike](frits-zernike.md) traced the idea to unwanted companion lines of a [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md). Around 1930 he found that visible striping disappeared when a telescope focused exactly on the grating. A phase strip placed over the principal spectral line changed its relation to the companion light and restored contrast. He then applied the separation of direct and diffracted light to a microscope and demonstrated it at Zeiss in 1932.[How I discovered phase contrast, pp. 239–243: grating observations, phase strips and microscope demonstration](how-i-discovered-phase-contrast-by-frits-zernike.md)

His later absorbing and annular strips improved contrast and the appearance of halos; commercial Zeiss accessories followed in 1941.[How I discovered phase contrast, pp. 245–246: absorbing strips, annular strips and commercial production](how-i-discovered-phase-contrast-by-frits-zernike.md) The route from a grating defect to biological observation illustrates why apparently unwanted instrumental effects can be productive experimental clues.
