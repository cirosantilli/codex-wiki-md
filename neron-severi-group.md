<h1 id="neron-severi-group">Néron-Severi group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Picard group](picard-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Néron-Severi_group)

The Néron-Severi group is the quotient $\operatorname{Pic}(X)/\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$ of the [Picard group](picard-group.md) by line bundles algebraically equivalent to zero.
