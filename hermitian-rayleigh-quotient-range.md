# Hermitian Rayleigh quotient range

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md)

The [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) of a finite-dimensional [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) ranges over its smallest-to-largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) interval. In an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) it is a weighted average of the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), with nonnegative weights summing to one. Combining the extreme [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) with coefficients $\sqrt{1-t},\sqrt t$ realizes every value of the interval.
