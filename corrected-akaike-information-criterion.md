# Corrected Akaike information criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Akaike information criterion](akaike-information-criterion.md)

The [corrected Akaike information criterion](corrected-akaike-information-criterion.md) adds a finite-sample penalty to the [Akaike information criterion](akaike-information-criterion.md), where $k$ counts fitted parameters and $n>k+1$. The displayed correction is commonly used for small samples; its exact unbiasedness depends on the model family.
