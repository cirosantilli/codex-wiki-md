# Dimension of an irreducible affine variety by transcendence degree

↑ **Parent:** [Krull dimension of an affine variety](krull-dimension-of-an-affine-variety.md)

For an irreducible affine variety $X$ over $k$, its dimension is

$$
\dim X=\operatorname{trdeg}_k k(X),
$$

where $k(X)=\operatorname{Frac}(k[X])$ is its [function field of an algebraic variety](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md). An inclusion $k(Y)\hookrightarrow k(X)$ therefore implies $\dim Y\leq\dim X$.
