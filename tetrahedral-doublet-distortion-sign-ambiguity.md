# Tetrahedral doublet distortion-sign ambiguity

↑ **Parent:** [Jahn-Teller effect](jahn-teller-effect.md)

For a linearly split orbital doublet occupied by one [electron](electron.md) or one [hole](electron-hole.md), the lower adiabatic branch has a cusp $-g|Q|$. A harmonic elastic cost produces equal minima at $Q=\pm g/K$. Orbital degeneracy therefore establishes instability of the regular geometry, but cannot alone distinguish opposite tetragonal distortions. A symmetry-allowed cubic elastic invariant in the two-component distortion can select one sign.
