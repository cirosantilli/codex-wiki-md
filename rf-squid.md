# RF SQUID

↑ **Parent:** [SQUID](squid.md)

A superconducting loop containing one [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md) and coupled inductively to a radio-frequency resonator. Its flux-dependent nonlinear inductive response changes the resonator signal. A [DC SQUID](dc-squid.md) instead uses two junctions and can turn their critical-current [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) into a voltage modulation under bias. Inductance, junction dynamics, noise and feedback determine the practical transfer function.
