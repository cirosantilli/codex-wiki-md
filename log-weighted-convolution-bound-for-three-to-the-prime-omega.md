# Log-weighted convolution bound for three to the prime omega

↑ **Parent:** [Three to the distinct-prime-factor count](three-to-the-distinct-prime-factor-count.md)

For $f(n)=3^{\omega(n)}$,

$$
f(n)\log n\leq3(f*\Lambda)(n).
$$

If $p^a\Vert n$, its contribution to $(f*\Lambda)(n)$ is $f(n)(a-1+1/3)\log p$. Since $3(a-1+1/3)\geq a$, summing over the [prime factors](prime-factor.md) proves the bound. Here $*$ is [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md) and $\Lambda$ is the [Von Mangoldt function](von-mangoldt-function.md).
