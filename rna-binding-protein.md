# RNA-binding protein

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

A [protein](protein-split.md) that binds [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md). Binding specificity can depend on [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md), [RNA secondary structure](rna-secondary-structure.md), chemical modification and other partners; recovery in an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) pull-down alone does not establish direct recognition.
