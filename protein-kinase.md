# Protein kinase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_kinase)

A [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) is an [enzyme](enzyme.md) that catalyzes [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) of a [protein](protein.md). In [receptor tyrosine kinase](receptor-tyrosine-kinase.md) signaling, [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) supplies the transferred [phosphate](phosphate.md) group.
