# Mean-square displacement of an isotropic planar random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Random walk](random-walk.md)

Independent planar unit steps with uniformly distributed direction have zero vector [expected value](expected-value.md). The [squared Euclidean norm](squared-euclidean-norm.md) of their sum has expectation $n$, because each diagonal term is one and all cross terms vanish by [independence](independent-random-variables.md). More generally, zero-mean independent steps with common second moment $s^2$ give mean-square displacement $ns^2$; nonzero drift adds a quadratic contribution.
