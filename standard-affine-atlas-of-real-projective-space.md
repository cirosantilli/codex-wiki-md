# Standard affine atlas of real projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md)

The $n+1$ [manifold charts](manifold-chart.md) $U_i=\{[X]:X_i\ne0\}$ have coordinates $X_k/X_i$, with $k\ne i$. Their inverse inserts $1$ as the $i$th homogeneous coordinate. On an overlap, changing the denominator from $X_i$ to $X_j$ divides each remaining coordinate by the nonzero coordinate $X_j/X_i$. These rational [smooth transition maps](smooth-transition-map.md) give [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) its standard [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) structure.
