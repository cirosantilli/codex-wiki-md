# Support function of a centred ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md)

Let $S$ be a real symmetric positive definite [matrix](matrix.md), $C>0$ and $q\ne0$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) in the $S$-weighted [inner product](inner-product-split.md) gives $q^Tx=(S^{-1}q)^TSx\le\sqrt{q^TS^{-1}q}\sqrt{x^TSx}$. Equality is attained by a positive multiple of $S^{-1}q$. Thus the displayed maximum holds. For a translated [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md), add $q^Ta$ for its centre $a$. This computes a coordinate extremum without constructing a rotation matrix.
