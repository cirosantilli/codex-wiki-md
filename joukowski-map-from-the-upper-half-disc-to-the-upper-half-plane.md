# Joukowski map from the upper half-disc to the upper half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Joukowski map](joukowski-map.md)

The [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $J(w)=-(w+w^{-1})$ maps $D_+=\{w:|w|<1,\ \operatorname{Im}w>0\}$ conformally and bijectively onto the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). Its imaginary part is $\operatorname{Im}w\,(|w|^{-2}-1)>0$, and its [derivative](derivative.md) $w^{-2}-1$ never vanishes there.

Equality of two values implies $(w_1-w_2)(1-1/(w_1w_2))=0$; since $|w_1w_2|<1$, this proves [injectivity](injective-function.md). For any target $\zeta$ in the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), the quadratic equation $w^2+\zeta w+1=0$ has two [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md) with product one and neither on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md). Exactly one therefore has modulus below one; the imaginary-part formula forces that [root](root-of-a-root-system.md) to lie in $D_+$, proving [surjectivity](surjective-function.md).

In particular, $z\mapsto e^{iz}$ maps $\{0<\operatorname{Re}z<\pi,\ \operatorname{Im}z>0\}$ bijectively to $D_+$, and composition gives the [conformal map](conformal-map.md) $z\mapsto-2\cos z$ onto the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). The two sides of the half-strip map to the outer real rays, while its bottom edge maps to the segment $[-2,2]$.
