# Countability of countably generated abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian group](abelian-group.md)

A countably generated [abelian group](abelian-group.md) is a [countable set](countable-set.md). Given generators $g_1,g_2,\ldots$, every element is $\sum_{j=1}^ma_jg_j$ for some $m$ and some $a_j\in\mathbb Z$. For each fixed $m$, this is the image of a finite [cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) of copies of the [countable set](countable-set.md) $\mathbb Z$; taking a [countable union of countable sets](countable-union-of-countable-sets.md) proves the assertion. The same proof works for a [module](module-mathematics.md) generated by a [countable set](countable-set.md) over any countable [ring](ring.md).
