# Submultiplicativity of the operator norm

↑ **Parent:** [Operator norm](operator-norm.md)

For composable bounded [linear operators](linear-operator.md), the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) satisfies $\|AB\|\leq\|A\|\|B\|$. Indeed, $\|ABx\|\leq\|A\|\|Bx\|\leq\|A\|\|B\|\|x\|$, and taking the supremum over unit vectors proves the bound. It implies $\|[A,B]\|\leq2\|A\|\|B\|$ by the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md).
