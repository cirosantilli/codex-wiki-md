# Equivalence closure

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md)

The equivalence closure of a [binary relation](binary-relation.md) $R$ is the least [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) containing it. Add diagonal and reversed pairs, then connect points by finite chains. Constant chains prove reflexivity, reversed chains prove symmetry, and concatenated chains prove transitivity. Any equivalence relation extending $R$ contains every such chain endpoint by induction.
