# Unstable node

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability of a planar equilibrium](linear-stability-of-a-planar-equilibrium.md)

A planar equilibrium is an unstable node when its two [Jacobian](jacobian-matrix.md) eigenvalues are real and positive. Every nearby nonstationary trajectory moves away from it in forward time.
