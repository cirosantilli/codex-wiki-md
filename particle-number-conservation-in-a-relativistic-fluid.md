# Particle number conservation in a relativistic fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md)

The number current is $J^a=nu^a$, where $n$ is the rest-frame [number density](number-density.md) and $u^a$ the unit [four-velocity](four-velocity.md). Its covariant continuity equation is $\nabla_aJ^a=0$, equivalently $u^a\nabla_an+n\nabla_au^a=0$. Integration over a spacetime volume and the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) equate the particle numbers crossing its boundary. This conservation law is separate from [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md).
