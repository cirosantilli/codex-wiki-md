# Repeated amplitude damping limit

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch-vector map of amplitude damping](bloch-vector-map-of-amplitude-damping.md)

Under repeated identical [amplitude damping channels](amplitude-damping-channel.md), all excited population eventually relaxes to the ground state for any fixed $0<p\leq1$. The [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) approaches $(0,0,1)$. For $p=1$ the limit is reached in one action, while $p=0$ is the identity channel and preserves every input. Keeping the zero-damping endpoint separate prevents an incorrect universal relaxation claim.
