# Dirichlet Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace operator](laplace-operator.md)

The nonnegative Dirichlet Laplacian is the [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) realization of $-\operatorname{div}\operatorname{grad}$ with zero boundary values. On a bounded domain, its weak form is the [Dirichlet energy](dirichlet-energy.md) on $H_0^1$. For polygonal domains the weak operator domain is preferable to a requirement of smoothness at corners.
