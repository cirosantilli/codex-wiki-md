# Cyclin B

↑ **Parent:** [Cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclin_B)

[Cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) is a regulatory [protein](protein-split.md) partner of [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md). The [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) catalytic component is [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md); bound [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) helps make that component competent to act. Changes in [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) synthesis and destruction regulate how much assembly can form, while [CDK1 phosphorylation states](cdk1-phosphorylation-state.md) determine whether bound assemblies are inhibited. This separates the supply of regulatory partners from release of an already assembled brake.[Components of the Cell-Cycle Control System: cyclically activated protein kinases, Figures 17-15–17-17, and “Cdk Activity Can Be Suppressed Both by Inhibitory Phosphorylation and by Inhibitory Proteins”](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/components-of-the-cell-cycle-control-system.md)

More [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) therefore need not give a proportional increase in [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) activity. Compare the matched-input [cell-cycle hysteresis experiment](cell-cycle-hysteresis-experiment.md), where different histories produced different activities, and the distinct measurements under [CDK1 activity and protein abundance](cdk1-activity-and-protein-abundance.md). Neither comparison makes [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) itself the catalytic [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md).
