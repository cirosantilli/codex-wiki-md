# Greatest power of omega below an ordinal

↑ **Parent:** [Leading term of an ordinal](leading-term-of-an-ordinal.md)

Every nonzero ordinal $\alpha$ has a greatest exponent $\beta$ such that $\omega^\beta\leq\alpha$. It is the leading exponent in the [Cantor normal form](cantor-normal-form.md) of $\alpha$, and

$$
\alpha=\omega^\beta n+\gamma
$$

for a positive finite integer $n$ and $\gamma<\omega^\beta$.
