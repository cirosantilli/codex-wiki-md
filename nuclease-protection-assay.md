# Nuclease protection assay

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

An assay asking whether nucleic acid remains inaccessible to a [nuclease](nuclease.md) under specified conditions. Protection can reflect [protein](protein-split.md) binding or compartmental enclosure; appropriate disruption controls distinguish them.
