# Borromean handle-pair evaluation

↑ **Parent:** [Two-strand Kirby projector](two-strand-kirby-projector.md)

For a charge-$a$ component threading a pair of zero-framed auxiliary components in the standard [Borromean rings](borromean-rings.md) surgery arrangement, color both auxiliary components by the [normalized Kirby color](normalized-kirby-color.md). Relative to the bare $a$ strand, eliminating this pair contributes $H_a=\mathcal D^2/d_a^2$. The [two-strand Kirby projector](two-strand-kirby-projector.md) applied to the two oppositely oriented portions of the $a$ component supplies a factor $\mathcal D/d_a$ at each of the two eliminations. This local evaluation underlies the surgery calculation of the [handle-cut formula for anyon ground-state degeneracy](handle-cut-formula-for-anyon-ground-state-degeneracy.md).
