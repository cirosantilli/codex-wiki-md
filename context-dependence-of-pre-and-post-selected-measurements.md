<h1 id="context-dependence-of-pre-and-post-selected-measurements">Context dependence of pre- and post-selected measurements</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Aharonov-Bergmann-Lebowitz rule](aharonov-bergmann-lebowitz-rule.md)

In the [ABL rule](aharonov-bergmann-lebowitz-rule.md), probabilities depend on the full intermediate [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) instrument. A binary [Lüders rule](luders-rule.md) [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) preserves coherence within its unresolved complement, whereas a fully resolved [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) removes it. Summing fine-grained probabilities after [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) does not generally reproduce the coarse-grained experiment. Different [postselection](postselection.md) success rates are part of the distinction.
