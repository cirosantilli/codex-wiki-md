# Clifford vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Fermionic Fock space](fermionic-fock-space.md)

A Clifford vacuum is a state annihilated by all the chosen fermionic annihilation operators. In a [massless supermultiplet](massless-supermultiplet.md) it labels the starting [helicity](helicity.md), and applying [fermionic raising and lowering operators](fermionic-raising-and-lowering-operator.md) constructs the remaining states.
