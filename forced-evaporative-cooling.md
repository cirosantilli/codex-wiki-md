# Forced evaporative cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Evaporative cooling (atomic physics)](evaporative-cooling-atomic-physics.md)

Repeated removal of high-energy trapped atoms while lowering the [energy](energy-split.md) cutoff as the sample cools. Elastic rethermalization replenishes the tail. The useful outcome is increased [phase-space density of an atomic gas](phase-space-density-of-an-atomic-gas.md), not [temperature](temperature-split.md) reduction alone.
