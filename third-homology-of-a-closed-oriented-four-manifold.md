# Third homology of a closed oriented four-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md)

For a connected closed oriented four-manifold, [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) identifies $H_3$ with $H^1$. The [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) identifies $H^1$ with $\operatorname{Hom}(H_1,\mathbb Z)$ because $H_0=\mathbb Z$ has zero Ext. Consequently $H_3$ is torsion-free and its rank equals that of $H_1$.
