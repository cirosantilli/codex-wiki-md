# Existence of an Euler witness for every odd composite integer

↑ **Parent:** [Euler witness](euler-witness.md)

Every odd composite integer has an [Euler witness](euler-witness.md). For a nonsquarefree integer, a base congruent to $1+p$ modulo a repeated prime factor $p^2$ fails the Euler congruence by the [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md). For a squarefree integer, use the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) to choose a base that is a [quadratic nonresidue](quadratic-nonresidue.md) modulo one prime factor and $1$ modulo another. Its [Jacobi symbol](jacobi-symbol.md) is $-1$, while its power is $1$ modulo the second factor.
