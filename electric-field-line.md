# Electric field line

↑ **Parent:** [Electric field](electric-field-split.md)

An oriented curve whose tangent points along the local [electric field](electric-field-split.md). Where the field is nonzero, choosing arc length $s$ gives $d\mathbf r/ds=\mathbf E/|\mathbf E|$. These curves visualize the direction of the [force](force-split.md) on a positive test charge; they are not generally particle trajectories, since a particle can have inertia and initial velocity. In [electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md), they are normal to [equipotential surfaces](equipotential-surface.md) and cannot form a closed loop in a region with a single-valued potential that strictly decreases along the line. A field null can separate families of lines.
