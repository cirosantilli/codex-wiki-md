# Transpose of a bounded linear operator

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md)

For a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $T:X\to Y$, its transpose or dual map is $T^*:Y^*\to X^*$ defined by

$$
(T^*y^*)(x)=y^*(Tx).
$$

It satisfies $\lVert T^*\rVert=\lVert T\rVert$.
