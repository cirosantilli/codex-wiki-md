# MS2 coat protein

↑ **Parent:** [RNA-binding protein](rna-binding-protein.md)

A bacteriophage [protein](protein-split.md) recognizing a defined [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) hairpin, used as a fusion partner in [RNA tethering assays](rna-tethering-assay.md) and [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) imaging. Hairpin arrays and bound fusion [proteins](protein-split.md) can themselves perturb [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) behaviour.
