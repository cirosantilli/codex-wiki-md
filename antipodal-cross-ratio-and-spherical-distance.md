# Antipodal cross-ratio and spherical distance

↑ **Parent:** [Antipodal stereographic coordinate relation](antipodal-stereographic-coordinate-relation.md)

Use the north-pole [stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md) and the [cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md) convention $[a,b;c,d]=(a-c)(b-d)/[(a-d)(b-c)]$. Antipodes have coordinates $-1/\bar u$. Rotate the first point to the south pole, whose coordinate is zero: its antipode becomes infinity, while a point at [spherical distance](great-circle-distance.md) $d$ has projected modulus $\tan(d/2)$. [Möbius invariance of the cross-ratio](mobius-invariance-of-the-cross-ratio.md) then gives the displayed identity. Antipodal endpoints require the extended-value interpretation.
