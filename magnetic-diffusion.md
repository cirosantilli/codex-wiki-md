# Magnetic diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

Finite [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) permits resistive smoothing of a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). With uniform coefficients and no motion the field satisfies a diffusion equation; with motion the diffusion term supplements the curl of $\mathbf u\times\mathbf B$. The [magnetic Reynolds number](magnetic-reynolds-number.md) compares these effects.
