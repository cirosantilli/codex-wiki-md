# Global confinement to an immersed leaf

↑ **Parent:** [Integral manifold](integral-manifold.md)

A smooth curve tangent to a regular [integrable distribution](integrable-distribution.md) lies in one maximal [leaf of a regular foliation](leaf-of-a-regular-foliation.md). In a [Frobenius theorem](frobenius-theorem.md) chart its transverse coordinates have zero derivative, so each short segment lies in one plaque. A finite chain of such segments covers any compact parameter interval and joins plaques in the same leaf. A single embedded local plaque only gives confinement while the curve remains in its chart. A [dense immersed cylinder in a three-dimensional torus](dense-immersed-cylinder-in-a-three-dimensional-torus.md) shows why global embedded confinement cannot generally replace the immersed statement.
