# Translation-invariant estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Robust statistics](robust-statistics.md)

A location estimator $T_n$ is translation-invariant when

$$
T_n(x_1+a,\ldots,x_n+a)=T_n(x_1,\ldots,x_n)+a
$$

for every real shift $a$.
