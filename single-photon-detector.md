# Single-photon detector

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

A [photodetector](photodetector.md) capable of registering an individual absorbed [photon](photon.md) as a distinguishable event. The detection record may include a click time and an output-channel label. Such a detector need not resolve the number of [photons](photon.md) in a pulse: an ordinary single-pixel [single-photon avalanche diode](single-photon-avalanche-diode.md) produces essentially the same saturated avalanche pulse when one or several [photons](photon.md) trigger the same detection interval. Detector efficiency, [dark counts](dark-count.md) and [detector dead time](detector-dead-time.md) affect the observed [photon counting](photon-counting.md) statistics.
