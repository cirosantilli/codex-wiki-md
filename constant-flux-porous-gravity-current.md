# Constant-flux porous gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Porous gravity current with background flow](porous-gravity-current-with-background-flow.md)

Constant actual volume input $Q$ into a [porous gravity current with background flow](porous-gravity-current-with-background-flow.md) gives early symmetric scales $h\sim[(Q/\phi)^2t/a]^{1/3}$ and $x\sim[a(Q/\phi)t^2]^{1/3}$. For $U>0$, drift dominates after $t_c=\phi K_bQ/U^3$. The late interior depth is $Q/U$ and the steady upstream edge is $x_-=-K_bQ/U^2$, with upstream linear profile $h=U(x-x_-)/K_b$. Upstream [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) is zero because background drift balances gravity spreading.
