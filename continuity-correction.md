# Continuity correction

↑ **Parent:** [Normal approximation](normal-approximation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_correction)

A continuity correction shifts a discrete probability boundary by half a lattice spacing before using a continuous [normal approximation](normal-approximation.md). For an upper-tail event $T\ge t$ on a unit lattice, the approximation is $1-\Phi((t-\tfrac12-E(T))/\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(T)})$. Lower tails use the boundary $t+\tfrac12$. The correction is used in approximate [Wilcoxon signed-rank tests](wilcoxon-signed-rank-test.md) and [Wilcoxon rank sum tests](mann-whitney-u-test.md); tied ranks also require the appropriate variance adjustment.
