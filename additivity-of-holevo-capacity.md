# Additivity of Holevo capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Holevo capacity](holevo-capacity.md)

A channel's [Holevo capacity](holevo-capacity.md) is additive with another channel when their joint capacity equals the sum of the individual capacities. If this holds with every other channel, the channel's tensor powers have capacity $n\chi^*(\Lambda)$ and regularization does not increase its unassisted [classical capacity of a quantum channel](classical-capacity-of-a-quantum-channel.md). Additivity is a special property, not a general rule for quantum channels.
