# Euclidean subspace of a Cartan subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Nondegeneracy of the Killing form on a Cartan subalgebra](nondegeneracy-of-the-killing-form-on-a-cartan-subalgebra.md)

The real span of the simple [coroots](coroot.md) inside a complex [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) carries a positive-definite restriction of the [Killing form](killing-form.md). Equivalently it consists of the $h$ for which all roots have real values. The formula $\kappa(h,h)=\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi}\alpha(h)^2$ is positive for $h\ne0$, since the roots span the dual. The induced dual [inner product](inner-product.md) makes the [root system](root-system.md) Euclidean and makes [root reflections](root-reflection.md) orthogonal.
