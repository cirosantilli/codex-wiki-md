# Gaussian zero-one law for measurable linear subspaces

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md)

Every linear subspace that is measurable for the [cylinder sigma-algebra](cylinder-sigma-algebra.md) has probability zero or one under a centered Gaussian process.
