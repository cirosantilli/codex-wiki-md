# Breathing Gaussian state in a harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Squeezed vacuum state](squeezed-vacuum-state.md)

A real centered [Gaussian wave packet](gaussian-wave-packet.md) with initial [position](position.md) [variance](variance-split.md) $1/(2\alpha)$ and [momentum](momentum-split.md) [variance](variance-split.md) $\hbar^2\alpha/2$ evolves under a [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) by

$$
M(t)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\omega t&\sin\omega t/(m\omega)\\
-m\omega\sin\omega t&\cos\omega t\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) obeys $\Sigma(t)=M\Sigma(0)M^{\mathsf T}$ and $\det\Sigma=\hbar^2/4$. Thus the [Robertson-Schrödinger uncertainty relation](robertson-schrodinger-uncertainty-relation.md) stays saturated, although the position-momentum [variance](variance-split.md) product can exceed $\hbar^2/4$ because of nonzero [quantum covariance](quantum-covariance.md). The widths are stationary only for $\alpha=m\omega/\hbar$; otherwise they breathe with [frequency](frequency.md) $2\omega$.
