<h1 id="lensmaker-s-equation">Lensmaker's equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thin lens](thin-lens.md)

For a thin [optical lens](lens-optics.md) in air, surface radii $R_a,R_b$ and [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n$ give $1/f=(n-1)(1/R_a-1/R_b)$. The radii carry the usual signed curvature convention.
