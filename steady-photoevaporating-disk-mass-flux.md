# Steady photoevaporating-disk mass flux

↑ **Parent:** [Photoevaporation](photoevaporation.md)

For a sink $W=W_0(r/r_g)^{-5/2}$ outside $r_g$, [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $dF_{\mathrm{acc}}/dr=2\pi rW$ in [steady state](steady-state.md). Remote feeding gives $F_{\mathrm{acc}}=\dot M_d-\dot M_w$ inside $r_g$, and $\dot M_d-\dot M_w\sqrt{r_g/r}$ outside, where $\dot M_w=4\pi W_0r_g^2$. The inward flux decreases towards the star because mass is diverted into the wind. A finite feeding radius or outer truncation supplies an additional boundary condition.
