# Charged-particle action over one relativistic cyclotron orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic cyclotron orbit](relativistic-cyclotron-orbit.md)

For $qB>0$, choose $A_y=Bx$ and parametrize one clockwise orbit by $x=R\cos(\omega_\tau\tau)$ and $y=-R\sin(\omega_\tau\tau)$. Over $\Delta\tau=2\pi/\omega_\tau$, the [relativistic charged-particle action](relativistic-charged-particle-action.md) is

$$
S=-mc^2\Delta\tau+q\oint A\mathbin\cdot dx
=-\frac{\pi mc^2}{\omega_\tau}(1+\gamma^2).
$$

The same formula with $|\omega_\tau|$ applies for either orbit orientation.
