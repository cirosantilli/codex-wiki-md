# Trifluoroacetic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md)

The acid CF3COOH. Its electron-withdrawing substituent makes it useful for acid-labile protecting-group removal; anhydrous tert-butyl [ester](ester.md) cleavage need not follow water-assisted acyl hydrolysis.
