# Cosmic string

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_string)

A [cosmic string](cosmic-string.md) is a hypothetical one-dimensional topological defect formed by symmetry breaking in the early universe. A [cosmic string network](cosmic-string-network.md) consists of many such defects and requires additional assumptions to infer an averaged equation of state.
