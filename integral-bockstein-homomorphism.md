# Integral Bockstein homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Bockstein homomorphism](bockstein-homomorphism.md)

The integral Bockstein is the [connecting homomorphism](connecting-homomorphism.md) of $0\to\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{n}\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/n\to0$. Lift a modulo-$n$ cocycle to an integral cochain $a$; write its coboundary as $\delta a=nb$. Then $\widehat\beta[a]=[b]$. Its image is the subgroup of integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) killed by $n$.
