# Monotonicity of polynomial critical points in their roots

↑ **Parent:** [Interlacing roots of polynomials](interlacing-roots-of-polynomials.md)

For a [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) $p(x)=\prod_i(x-a_i)$ with distinct ordered real [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md), each [critical point](critical-point.md) $\xi$ lies between two consecutive roots by [Rolle's theorem](rolle-theorem.md). Its [logarithmic derivative](logarithmic-derivative.md) gives $\sum_i(\xi-a_i)^{-1}=0$. [Implicit differentiation](implicit-differentiation.md) gives the displayed formula, so moving any root to the right moves every ordered [critical point](critical-point.md) to the right, while the roots remain distinct. Iterating this statement for [derivatives](derivative.md) preserves componentwise ordering of their roots. Weak inequalities extend by [continuity](continuous-function.md) to repeated roots.
