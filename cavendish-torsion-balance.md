# Cavendish torsion balance

↑ **Parent:** [Cavendish experiment](cavendish-experiment.md)

A Cavendish torsion balance measures the small gravitational torque exerted by source masses on test masses mounted on a suspended rod. The fiber's twist reveals the horizontal force.
