<h1 id="rotating-saddle-trap-as-foucault-s-pendulum-by-oleg-n-kirillov-and-mark-levi">Rotating saddle trap as Foucault's pendulum by Oleg N. Kirillov and Mark Levi</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Mark Levi](work-by-mark-levi.md), [Work by Oleg N. Kirillov](work-by-oleg-n-kirillov.md)

American Journal of Physics 84(1), 26–31. Primary mathematical analysis of a rotating potential and its guiding-centre motion.

Author preprint: [https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.03658](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.03658)

The preprint distinguishes a particle in a planar rotating potential from a particle constrained to a rotating surface and from a rolling ball. Its rapid-rotation analysis identifies weak laboratory-frame precession.
