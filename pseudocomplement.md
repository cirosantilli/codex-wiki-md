# Pseudocomplement

↑ **Parent:** [Heyting implication](heyting-implication.md)

The pseudocomplement of $a$ is the greatest element disjoint from $a$. It need not satisfy $a\vee\neg a=1$. In a [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md), double pseudocomplementation defines the [Booleanization of a locale](booleanization-of-a-locale.md); its fixed elements are [regular open elements](regular-element-of-a-heyting-algebra.md).
