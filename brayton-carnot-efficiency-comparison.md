# Brayton-Carnot efficiency comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Brayton cycle](brayton-cycle.md)

For constant-pressure [heat](heat-split.md) addition $A\to B$ and two [reversible ideal-gas adiabats](reversible-ideal-gas-adiabat.md), set $r=(p_h/p_l)^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}$. The [Brayton cycle](brayton-cycle.md) has $\eta_B=1-1/r$, maximum [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T_B$ and minimum $T_D=T_A/r$. A [Carnot cycle](carnot-cycle.md) using those extreme [temperatures](temperature-split.md) has the strictly greater efficiency displayed whenever $T_B>T_A$. Reversibility of the isobaric heating requires continuously matched reservoir [temperatures](temperature-split.md), rather than [heat](heat-split.md) transfer solely at the maximum [temperature](temperature-split.md).
