# Matter-dominated density-perturbation modes

↑ **Parent:** [Linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md)

In a matter-dominated universe, the long-wavelength pressureless growth equation has modes

$$
\delta_+\propto a,
\qquad
\delta_-\propto a^{-3/2}.
$$
