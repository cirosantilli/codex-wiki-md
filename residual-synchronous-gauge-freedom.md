# Residual synchronous-gauge freedom

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronous gauge in cosmology](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md)

Preserving $\Psi=B=0$ under the [scalar gauge transformations with a background lapse](scalar-gauge-transformations-with-a-background-lapse.md) requires $\bar N\xi^0=C(\mathbf x)$ and $\dot\lambda=C(\mathbf x)\bar N/a^2$. Thus arbitrary spatial functions $C,D$ remain after imposing [synchronous gauge in cosmology](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md). This freedom can generate changes in the remaining scalar metric perturbations.
