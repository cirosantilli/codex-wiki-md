# Prime end

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

A prime end describes an approach to the boundary of a [simply connected domain](simply-connected-domain.md) through a nested sequence of [crosscuts](crosscut.md). A [conformal map](conformal-map.md) induces a correspondence between prime ends, even when the Euclidean boundary does not have a continuous one-to-one parametrization. This is the natural boundary interpretation of the endpoints and growing tip of a [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md).
