# Relative attenuation by a linear filter

↑ **Parent:** [Linear filter](linear-filter.md)

For a [linear time-invariant system](linear-time-invariant-system.md), a sinusoidal wanted [signal](signal.md) at $\omega_s$ and an unwanted sinusoidal component at $\omega_n$ acquire their separate [frequency response](frequency-response.md) factors. The ratio of their output [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md), divided by their input amplitude ratio, is $\eta=|H(\omega_n)|/|H(\omega_s)|$. An amplitude attenuation factor of $F=1/\eta$ corresponds to a factor $F^2$ in squared-amplitude [power](power.md) ratios under a common impedance convention. The unwanted component's own absolute attenuation is $|H(\omega_n)|$, which need not equal its attenuation relative to the wanted signal.
