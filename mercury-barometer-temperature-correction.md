# Mercury-barometer temperature correction

↑ **Parent:** [Mercury barometer](mercury-barometer.md)

A measured mercury column represents [pressure](pressure-split.md) through its actual [mass density](density.md) and local [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md). An equivalent reference column therefore obeys $\rho_0g_sh_s=\rho(T)gh$. If the indicated scale length expands as $1+\alpha_s\Delta T$ and mercury volume as $1+\beta\Delta T$, the first-order thermal factor multiplying the scale reading is $1+(\alpha_s-\beta)\Delta T$. These factors describe a pressure-unit reduction, not a physical change in local [gravity](gravity.md).[§§9–10 and equation45](mercury-barometers-and-manometers-by-w-g-brombacher-and-others.md)
