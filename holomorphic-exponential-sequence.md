# Holomorphic exponential sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md)

For the [structure sheaf of a complex manifold](structure-sheaf-of-a-complex-manifold.md), local holomorphic logarithms give the [short exact sequence of sheaves](short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md)

$$
0\to\underline{\mathbb Z}\to\mathcal O_M\xrightarrow{\exp(2\pi i\,\cdot)}\mathcal O_M^*\to1.
$$

The connecting map $H^1(M,\mathcal O_M^*)\to H^2(M,\mathbb Z)$ sends a [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md) to its [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md). In particular, it is onto when $H^2(M,\mathcal O_M)=0$.
