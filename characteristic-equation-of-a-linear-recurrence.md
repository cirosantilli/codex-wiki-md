# Characteristic equation of a linear recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Linear recurrence relation](linear-recurrence-relation.md)

For a constant-coefficient [linear recurrence relation](linear-recurrence-relation.md), substituting a trial sequence $u_n=r^n$ produces a polynomial equation in $r$ called its characteristic equation. Repeated roots contribute factors polynomial in $n$.
