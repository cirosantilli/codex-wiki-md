# Light-emitting diode

↑ **Parent:** [Diode](diode.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light-emitting_diode)

A [light-emitting diode](light-emitting-diode.md) is a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) [diode](diode.md) producing [electroluminescence](electroluminescence.md) under [electric current](electric-current.md). [Forward bias](forward-bias.md) injects [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md); radiative [electron-hole recombination](electron-hole-recombination.md) emits [photons](photon.md). A [double heterostructure](double-heterostructure.md) uses [band offsets](band-offset.md) to retain both [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md) in an active layer. A sufficiently thin layer is a [quantum well](quantum-well.md).[§I, p. 1139, Fig. 1; §IV.A, pp. 1143–1144: injection, active layer and quantum-well distinction](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/pdf/10.1103/RevModPhys.87.1139)

The optical and electrical questions are different: [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) sets a color scale, while [light-emitting diode efficiency](light-emitting-diode-efficiency.md) determines how much supplied [energy](energy-split.md) escapes as [light](light.md). The [blue light-emitting diode](blue-light-emitting-diode.md) made compact [white light-emitting diodes](white-light-emitting-diode.md) and full-color displays practical. A modulated [light-emitting diode](light-emitting-diode.md) can also supply the optical [signal](signal.md) in a [chopped-light lock-in experiment](chopped-light-lock-in-experiment.md).
