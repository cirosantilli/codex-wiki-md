# Quadratic-cost two-player proportional contest

↑ **Parent:** [Proportional allocation contest](proportional-allocation-contest.md)

With values $v_i>0$ and effort costs $b_i^2$, the unique pure [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md) has the efforts displayed above. Interior [first-order conditions](first-order-optimality-condition.md) give $v_i b_j/(b_1+b_2)^2=2b_i$, hence $b_1/b_2=\sqrt{v_1/v_2}$ and $(b_1+b_2)^2=\sqrt{v_1v_2}/2$. Against a positive rival effort, each payoff is a [strictly concave function](strictly-concave-function.md), so these conditions identify global [best responses](best-response.md). On the axes, lowering a positive uncontested effort or adding a sufficiently small effort at the zero tie rules out equilibrium.
