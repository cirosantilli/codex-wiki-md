# Observation of Bose-Einstein condensation in rubidium in 1995

↑ **Parent:** [Dilute-gas Bose-Einstein condensation](dilute-gas-bose-einstein-condensation.md)

The [rubidium](rubidium.md) experiment used [laser cooling](laser-cooling.md), a [magneto-optical trap](magneto-optical-trap.md) and [magnetic trapping of neutral atoms](magnetic-trapping-of-neutral-atoms.md) with a rotating bias field. [Radio frequency](radio-frequency.md) transitions removed energetic atoms; [elastic collisions](elastic-collision.md) restored [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md). Before [absorption imaging](absorption-imaging.md), the trap was decompressed, then nearly released with a gradient supporting against [gravity](gravity.md). Varying the final evaporation cut revealed a narrow anisotropic core within an isotropic thermal cloud. The broad component supplied a simultaneous comparison through the same optics. Resolution and size calibration limited inference; interactions broadened the core relative to an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md).[pp. 198–201, Figs. 1–4](observation-of-bose-einstein-condensation-in-a-dilute-atomic-vapor-by-m-h-anderson-and-others.md)
