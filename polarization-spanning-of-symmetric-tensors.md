# Polarization spanning of symmetric tensors

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric tensor](symmetric-tensor.md)

Over a field of [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md), degree-$n$ [symmetric tensors](symmetric-tensor.md) are spanned by pure powers $a^{\otimes n}$. Inclusion-exclusion expands $\sum_{J\subseteq[n]}(-1)^{n-|J|}(\sum_{j\in J}a_j)^{\otimes n}$ into the sum of all [permutation](permutation.md) orderings of $a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes a_n$. Every surviving term uses each of the $n$ labels once. This converts an invariant-tensor spanning problem into pure [tensor powers](tensor-power.md).
