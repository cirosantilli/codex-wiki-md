# Stalk functor for presheaves of sets

↑ **Parent:** [Stalk of a sheaf](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md)

For presheaves of sets, $F_x=\varinjlim_{U\ni x}F(U)$. This functor preserves finite limits because the neighborhood system is filtered and [filtered colimits commute with finite limits in sets](filtered-colimits-commute-with-finite-limits-in-sets.md). Its right adjoint sends a set $S$ to the presheaf taking the value $S$ on opens containing $x$ and the singleton on other opens.
