# Momentum diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md)

Viscous transfer of tangential [momentum](momentum-split.md) smooths gradients of [velocity](velocity.md). For unidirectional incompressible flow without a [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md), the [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md) reduces to the displayed [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md), whose diffusion coefficient is the [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu=\eta/\rho$.
