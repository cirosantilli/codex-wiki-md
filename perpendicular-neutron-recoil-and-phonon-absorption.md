# Perpendicular neutron recoil and phonon absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron scattering kinematics](neutron-scattering-kinematics.md)

Define transfer gained by the [neutron](neutron-split.md) as $\mathbf Q=\mathbf k_f-\mathbf k_i$. If it is perpendicular to $\mathbf k_i$, expansion of the free-neutron [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) gives $E_f-E_i=\hbar^2Q^2/(2m_n)$. Absorbing one [phonon](phonon.md) requires this positive [energy](energy-split.md) to equal $\hbar\omega$. A periodic [phonon](phonon.md) branch may therefore intersect a nonperiodic recoil parabola in an extended-zone representation. The sign of $\mathbf Q$ is opposite to the common sample-transfer convention and must be declared.
