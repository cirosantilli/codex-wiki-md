# Cohomological cross product

↑ **Parent:** [Cup product](cup-product.md)

The cross product takes classes on two factors to a class on their product. Its multiplication rule has the [graded tensor product](graded-tensor-product.md) sign: $(a\times b)(c\times d)=(-1)^{|b||c|}(ac)\times(bd)$. The [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) makes these products an additive basis when the integral cohomology of the finite cell complexes is torsion-free.
