# Radiative-zero white-dwarf envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Mestel's cooling law](mestel-s-cooling-law.md)

In a negligible-mass [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) envelope, take enclosed mass and [luminosity](luminosity.md) constant, neglect [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md), and use an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) with the [Kramers' opacity law](kramers-opacity-law.md). Dividing [hydrostatic pressure support equation](hydrostatic-pressure-support-equation.md) by [radiative diffusion in a star](radiative-diffusion-in-a-star.md) gives $P\,dP\propto(M/L)T^{15/2}dT$. Neglecting the surface integration constant produces the displayed [pressure](pressure.md) and [mass density](density.md) laws. It is a deep-envelope approximation, not a finite-temperature photospheric boundary condition.
