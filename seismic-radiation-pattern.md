# Seismic radiation pattern

↑ **Parent:** [Focal mechanism](focal-mechanism.md)

The dependence of radiated [P wave](p-wave.md) or [S wave](s-wave.md) amplitude and polarity on direction from an [earthquake](earthquake.md) source. For an ideal double couple it has nodal directions, so amplitude differences between stations do not measure distance or directivity alone.
