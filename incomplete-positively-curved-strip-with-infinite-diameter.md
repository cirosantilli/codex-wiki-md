# Incomplete positively curved strip with infinite diameter

↑ **Parent:** [Myers's theorem](myers-s-theorem.md)

This metric pulls back the round spherical metric by $(u,v)\mapsto(\cos u\cos v,\cos u\sin v,\sin u)$. It has unit [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) and [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md) equal to its metric, yet the meridian reaches a missing boundary in finite time. Every curve between $(0,0)$ and $(0,L)$ has length at least $|L|/\sqrt2$, so its diameter is infinite. It shows that the positive Ricci bound alone does not replace [geodesic completeness](geodesic-completeness.md) in the [Bonnet-Myers theorem](myers-s-theorem.md).
