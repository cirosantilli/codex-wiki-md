# Selberg symmetry formula

↑ **Parent:** [Von Mangoldt function](von-mangoldt-function.md)

The elementary Selberg symmetry formula can be written

$$
\psi(x)+\frac1{\log x}\sum_{n\leq x}\Lambda(n)\psi(x/n)=2x+O\left(\frac{x}{\log x}\right).
$$

Use the weights $\lambda_d=\mu(d)\log(x/d)/\log x$. Their [divisor sum](divisor-sum.md) is $1_{n=1}+\Lambda(n)/\log x$. Apply them to the [integrated Chebyshev sum](integrated-chebyshev-sum.md), then use the [Möbius harmonic logarithmic moments](mobius-harmonic-logarithmic-moments.md). The [Chebyshev estimate](chebyshev-estimate.md) shows this is equivalent, with an $O(x)$ error, to $\sum_{n\leq x}\Lambda(n)\log n+\sum_{mn\leq x}\Lambda(m)\Lambda(n)=2x\log x+O(x)$.
