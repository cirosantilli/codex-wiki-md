# Basic emotion

↑ **Parent:** [Basic emotion theory](basic-emotion-theory.md)

An [emotion](emotion.md) proposed as a biologically primary category in a [basic emotion theory](basic-emotion-theory.md). The status of a category is a theoretical and empirical claim, not something established merely by giving observers a category word in a [forced-choice emotion recognition](forced-choice-emotion-recognition.md) task.
