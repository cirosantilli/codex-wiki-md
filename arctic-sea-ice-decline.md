# Arctic sea ice decline

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_sea_ice_decline)

A decline in Arctic [sea ice](sea-ice.md) can mean reduced extent, concentration, thickness, volume or survival of older ice. These measures differ: extent counts areas above a concentration threshold, whereas area weights actual coverage. Comparisons require consistent season, geographical domain and measurement method.
