# Specific capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Electric battery](electric-battery.md)

Electrical charge delivered per unit mass, commonly expressed in ampere-hours per gram. For an ideal active material transferring $n$ [electrons](electron.md) per formula unit, $q=nF/(3600M)$ when $M$ is in grams per mole.
