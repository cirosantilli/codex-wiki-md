# Density positivity for an axisymmetric logarithmic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric logarithmic gravitational potential](axisymmetric-logarithmic-gravitational-potential.md)

The [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) gives

$$
\rho=\frac{V^2}{4\pi Gq^2}\frac{R^2+(2-q^{-2})z^2}{(R^2+q^{-2}z^2)^2}.
$$

For $V>0$, nonnegative [mass density](density.md) away from the origin is equivalent to $q^2\geq1/2$. Strict positivity requires $q^2>1/2$. This condition does not by itself guarantee a nonnegative [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md).
