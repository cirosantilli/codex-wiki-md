# Lunar illuminated fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Lunar phase](lunar-phase.md)

The [lunar illuminated fraction](lunar-illuminated-fraction.md) is the directly sunlit projected [area](surface-area.md) of the apparent lunar disk divided by its total projected [area](surface-area.md). For a [sphere](sphere.md) with parallel sunlight and a distant observer, outside a [lunar eclipse](lunar-eclipse.md), it is $k=(1+\cos\alpha)/2$, with [phase angle](phase-angle-astronomy.md) $\alpha$ at the [Moon](moon.md). It measures geometry rather than the fraction of full-Moon [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md). [US Naval Observatory: definition of the illuminated disk fraction](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/data/MoonFraction).
