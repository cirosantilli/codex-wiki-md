# Koszul sign rule

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of chain complexes](tensor-product-of-chain-complexes.md)

Interchanging homogeneous objects of degrees $p$ and $q$ introduces $(-1)^{pq}$. In particular, moving a degree-minus-one differential past a degree-$p$ factor introduces $(-1)^p$. This convention produces the differential on the [tensor product of chain complexes](tensor-product-of-chain-complexes.md).
