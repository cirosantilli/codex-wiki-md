# Background-subtracted radiative temperature uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md)

If measured radiative [flux](flux.md) is $S=A-B>0$ and $S=kT^4$ with known $k$, first-order uncertainty propagation gives the displayed result. Independent measurements have zero [covariance](covariance.md); strict error bounds combine differently. Even a small fractional background contribution can matter if its relative uncertainty is large. Inferring the [covariance](covariance.md) or an unknown calibration factor from precision percentages alone is unjustified.
