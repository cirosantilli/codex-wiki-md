# Publication bias

↑ **Parent:** [Meta-analysis](meta-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publication_bias)

Publication bias arises when the availability of studies depends on the results, such as their significance, magnitude or direction. A [meta-analysis](meta-analysis.md) of the available studies can then differ systematically from one including all eligible evidence. [Funnel plot](funnel-plot.md) asymmetry can motivate investigation but does not establish this mechanism; heterogeneity and other [small-study effects](small-study-effect.md) can also cause asymmetry.
