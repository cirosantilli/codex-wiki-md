# Self-adjoint fourth-order scalar differential operator

↑ **Parent:** [Self-adjoint differential operator](self-adjoint-differential-operator.md)

For real coefficient functions, the fourth-order scalar operator

$$
L=D^4+pD^2+qD+r
$$

is formally self-adjoint exactly when $q=p'$. Indeed, $(pD^2)^*=pD^2+2p'D+p''$ and $(qD)^*=-qD-q'$. Under [clamped boundary conditions](clamped-boundary-condition.md), its quadratic form is

$$
\langle y,Ly\rangle=\int\left[(y'')^2-p(y')^2+ry^2\right]dx.
$$
