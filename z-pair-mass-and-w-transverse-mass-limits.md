# Z pair mass and W transverse-mass limits

↑ **Parent:** [W and Z discovery experiments](w-and-z-discovery-experiments.md)

For two visible, approximately massless [leptons](lepton.md), both measured [four-momenta](four-momentum.md) enter

$$
m_{\ell\ell}^2=(p_{\ell^+}+p_{\ell^-})^2
=2p_{T+}p_{T-}\bigl[\cosh(y_+-y_-)-\cos\Delta\phi\bigr].
$$

This is a reconstructed [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md), including longitudinal information, rather than the lower bound obtained from [transverse mass](transverse-mass.md). As an original example, two opposite-azimuth [leptons](lepton.md) with $p_{T+}=p_{T-}=45\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and equal [rapidities](rapidity.md) have $m_{\ell\ell}=90\,\mathrm{GeV}$. Changing their [rapidity](rapidity.md) difference to one raises it to about $101.486\,\mathrm{GeV}$, even though their [transverse momenta](transverse-momentum.md) are unchanged.

The ideal [transverse mass](transverse-mass.md) bound concerns the actual event's parent [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md), not a perfectly sharp universal number. A finite [decay width](decay-width.md) spreads parent [invariant masses](invariant-mass.md); [particle jet](jet-particle-physics.md) recoil changes daughter directions; [invariant-mass resolution](invariant-mass-resolution.md), momentum resolution and imperfect reconstruction can smear an observed endpoint. Recoil alone does not invalidate the ideal inequality when both daughter [transverse momenta](transverse-momentum.md) are correct. Additional invisible [particles](particle-physics.md), radiated [photons](photon.md) and a massless-daughter approximation can invalidate the simple two-body reconstruction. Likewise, a [Z boson](z-boson.md) pair-mass peak requires control of identification, [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md), instrumental response and backgrounds. A peak position and an endpoint are different summaries of selected event populations.
