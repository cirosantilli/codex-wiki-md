# Fold-Hopf bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Codimension-two bifurcation](codimension-two-bifurcation.md)

A fold-Hopf bifurcation combines a simple zero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) and a nonzero imaginary pair at an [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). In a three-dimensional [centre manifold](center-manifold.md), a [normal form of a dynamical system](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md) separates a real fold amplitude from a complex oscillatory amplitude. A rotationally symmetric [normal form](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md) permits reduction to two real amplitude equations, while the oscillation phase continues to evolve. Secondary bifurcations of the amplitude flow can produce invariant [tori](torus.md) in the full [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md).
