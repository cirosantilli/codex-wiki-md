# Nondeterministic planning

↑ **Parent:** [Automated planning](automated-planning.md)

[Automated planning](automated-planning.md) with several possible successors for an action and no specified [probabilities](probability.md) for choosing between them. A plan must say how to act after observed outcomes. A [strong plan](strong-plan.md) guarantees eventual success for every possible outcome history; a favorable outcome on just one branch does not establish that guarantee.
