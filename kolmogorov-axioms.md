# Kolmogorov axioms

↑ **Parent:** [Probability measure](probability-measure.md)

The Kolmogorov axioms require a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) $\mathbb P$ to be nonnegative, normalized by $\mathbb P(\Omega)=1$, and countably additive on pairwise disjoint [events](event.md).
