# Fundamental unit (number theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet's unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_unit_(number_theory))

In a real quadratic [number field](number-field.md), a fundamental unit $\epsilon>1$ generates the infinite cyclic factor of its [unit group](unit-group.md), so every [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) is $\pm\epsilon^n$. Existence follows from the [Dirichlet unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md); choosing the positive generator of the rank-one logarithmic lattice gives this normalization.
