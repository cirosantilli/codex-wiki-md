# Flatness criterion from Lefschetz commutation

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz-Dolbeault curvature commutator](lefschetz-dolbeault-curvature-commutator.md)

The Lefschetz operator commutes with the bundle-valued [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md) on the entire smooth form space exactly when the [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) is flat. The [Lefschetz-Dolbeault curvature commutator](lefschetz-dolbeault-curvature-commutator.md) gives one direction. Conversely test its curvature-wedge operator on degree-zero sections with arbitrary prescribed values at any point to force every [vector-bundle curvature](curvature-form.md) [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) to vanish.
