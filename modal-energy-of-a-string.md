# Modal energy of a string

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md)

For a uniform fixed-end [mechanical string](mechanical-string.md) of length $L$, [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\mu$ and wave [speed](speed.md) $c$, let its $n$th [normal mode](normal-mode.md) be $y_n=[A_n\cos\omega_nt+B_n\sin\omega_nt]\sin(n\pi x/L)$, where $\omega_n=cn\pi/L$. The conserved [energy](energy-split.md) is the sum of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and elastic [potential energy](potential-energy.md),

$$
E_n=\frac\mu2\int_0^L[(y_n)_t^2+c^2(y_n)_x^2]dx.
$$

The sine and cosine squared integrals are $L/2$, so substitution proves the title formula. [Orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) makes energies of distinct [normal modes](normal-mode.md) add. For zero initial displacement and initial modal [velocity](velocity.md) $V_n$, $A_n=0$ and $B_n=V_n/\omega_n$, giving $E_n=\mu LV_n^2/4$.
