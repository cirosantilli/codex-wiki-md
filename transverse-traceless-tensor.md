# Transverse-traceless tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational wave polarization](gravitational-wave-polarization.md)

A transverse-traceless spatial tensor is orthogonal to its [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) wavevector in each index and has zero trace. The [transverse-traceless projector](transverse-traceless-projector.md) extracts this part. Trace terms and symmetrized coordinate-displacement gradients have no such part, explaining the linear gauge invariance of [tensor cosmological perturbations](tensor-cosmological-perturbation.md).
