# Energy balance of coupled RL circuits

↑ **Parent:** [Coupled RL induction model](coupled-rl-induction-model.md)

The [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) of this constant-[inductance](inductance.md) model is

$$
W=\frac12L_1i_1^2+Mi_1i_2+\frac12L_2i_2^2
=\frac12\mathbf i^T\mathbf L\mathbf i.
$$

The [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) condition makes $W\geq0$. Multiplying the two circuit equations by $i_1$ and $i_2$, then adding, gives the displayed [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) statement: input [power](power.md) $Vi_1$ supplies stored [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) and [Joule heating](joule-heating.md). With zero drive, $\dot W\leq0$. After the drive becomes zero, the stored [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) supplies the remaining [Joule heating](joule-heating.md).
