# Optical slit

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument.md)

An [optical slit](optical-slit.md) selects a narrow region of a [focal plane](focal-plane.md). In a slit-limited [spectrograph](spectrograph.md), the width of its image divided by the [linear spectral dispersion](linear-spectral-dispersion.md) sets the spectral width.
