# Intersecting shadow lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Intersecting family](intersecting-family.md)

For an [intersecting family](intersecting-family.md) of $r$-sets with $r\geq1$, its [lower shadow](lower-shadow.md) has at least as many members as the family. Apply [elementary set shifts](elementary-set-shift.md) and then the [ballot reflection injection](ballot-reflection-injection.md) to obtain this bound.
