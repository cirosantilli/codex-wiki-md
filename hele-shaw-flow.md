# Hele-Shaw flow

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hele-Shaw_flow)

Hele–Shaw flow is slow flow between closely spaced parallel plates. Averaging across a gap of thickness $h$ gives the Darcy-type law

$$
\overline{\mathbf u}=-\frac{h^2}{12\mu}\nabla p
$$

relative to stationary plates.
