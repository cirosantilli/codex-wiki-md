# Weight space

↑ **Parent:** [Weight vector](weight-vector.md)

The weight space $V_\mu$ consists of all [weight vectors](weight-vector.md) of weight $\mu$, together with zero.

This is the simultaneous eigenspace belonging to a [Weight](weight-representation-theory.md).
