# Permanganate-iron titration

↑ **Parent:** [Redox titration](redox-titration.md)

A proposed modern teaching measurement puts a [pipette](pipette.md)-measured portion of an [iron](iron.md) $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) in a [conical flask](erlenmeyer-flask.md), acidifies with excess dilute [sulfuric acid](sulfuric-acid.md), and adds standardized [potassium permanganate](potassium-permanganate.md) from a clamped [burette](burette.md) while swirling. The documented endpoint principle is that added purple [permanganate](permanganate.md) is consumed until the first small excess leaves a pale purple colour; no separate colour indicator is required. Insufficient acidity can produce brown [manganese dioxide](manganese-dioxide.md), invalidating the assumed five-[Electron](electron.md) [half-reaction](half-reaction.md).[“Setting the Scene”, final two paragraphs: burette, conical flask, decolourisation, endpoint and excess sulfuric acid](iron-tablet-determination-analytical-competition-royal-society-of-chemistry-resource.md)

Record initial and final [burette](burette.md) readings; their difference is the delivered [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) $V$. The following controls are proposed additions to test the inference. Run a reagent blank with the same [water](water.md), [sulfuric acid](sulfuric-acid.md) and endpoint criterion but no sample, obtaining $V_b$. Use a separately established [potassium permanganate](potassium-permanganate.md) [concentration](concentration.md) $c$, clear the [burette](burette.md) tip of air, and repeat sample portions. A known added [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ should increase consumption by one fifth of that amount. A blank estimates reagent demand and the small endpoint excess; it cannot remove an interfering sample [reactant](reactant.md).

Assuming complete, selective reaction according to the [acidic permanganate-iron balance](acidic-permanganate-iron-balance.md), the corrected inference is

$$
n(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+})=5c(V-V_b).
$$

With $c$ in $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$, use $V$ and $V_b$ in litres. As an original stipulated calculation, not an experimental result, take $c=0.02000\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$, $V=12.60\,\mathrm{mL}$ and $V_b=0.10\,\mathrm{mL}$. Then the [permanganate](permanganate.md) consumption is $0.2500\,\mathrm{mmol}$ and the inferred $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ amount is $1.250\,\mathrm{mmol}$. In a $25.00\,\mathrm{mL}$ sample portion this corresponds to $0.05000\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.

The endpoint detects residual [permanganate](permanganate.md), not the [iron](iron.md) identity directly. Other sample [reactants](reactant.md) that consume [permanganate](permanganate.md) raise the apparent $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ amount; prior [oxidation](oxidation.md) of sample $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ to $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ lowers it. A recovery check probes response to the added [iron](iron.md) but does not prove absence of every interference. The readout determines the reacting $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ inventory under the stated conditions, rather than total [iron](iron.md) in an arbitrary mixture. No measured titres are asserted here.
