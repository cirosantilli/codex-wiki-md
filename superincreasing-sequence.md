# Superincreasing sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

A positive sequence is superincreasing if each term exceeds the sum of all earlier terms. If $w_{k+1}>2w_k$, a [geometric series](geometric-series.md) proves $\sum_{j<k}w_j<w_k$. Distinct finite subsets of its indices have distinct sums: cancel their common terms and compare the largest remaining term with the sum of all smaller terms. This gives uniqueness of [minimum spanning trees](minimum-spanning-tree.md) and of simple [graph paths](path-in-a-graph.md) with fixed endpoints when a finite [weighted graph](weighted-graph.md) has such separated edge weights.
