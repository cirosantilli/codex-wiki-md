# Independent trial safety monitoring

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical trial](clinical-trial.md)

Independent trial safety monitoring reviews accumulating serious outcomes according to a prespecified plan and may recommend stopping or modifying a [clinical trial](clinical-trial.md) when harm or benefit changes the ethical balance. Complete ascertainment of deaths and other severe outcomes is needed even if the trial is sized for a different endpoint. Pooled evidence and prespecified sequential thresholds can make rare-event monitoring useful without presenting a nonsignificant harm comparison as safety.
