# Time evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_evolution)

[Time evolution](time-evolution.md) describes the change of a physical system's state with time. A closed quantum system has [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md); dissipative dynamics need not be unitary.
