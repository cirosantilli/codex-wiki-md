# Alternation of generations

↑ **Parent:** [Sexual reproduction](sexual-reproduction.md)

Alternation between a multicellular [haploid](haploid.md) [gametophyte](gametophyte.md) and a multicellular [diploid](diploid.md) [sporophyte](sporophyte.md). [meiosis](meiosis.md) produces [spores](spore.md), whereas [fertilization](fertilization.md) restores the diploid condition; the generations grow by [mitosis](mitosis.md).
