# Rocket equation with stationary mass accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Dust-collecting rocket](dust-collecting-rocket.md)

For a [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) with exhaust mass rate $\dot m$ and incoming stationary mass rate $\dot D$, mass balance is $\dot M=\dot D-\dot m$. A short-interval [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) calculation gives $M\dot v=u\dot m-v\dot D$. Constant time rates $\dot D=\alpha$, $\dot m=\beta$ give $M\dot v+\alpha v=u\beta$, unlike a constant accretion rate per distance, whose incoming mass rate is proportional to speed. For unequal rates and initial rest, $v=(u\beta/\alpha)[1-(M/M_0)^{\alpha/(\beta-\alpha)}]$.
