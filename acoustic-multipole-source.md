# Acoustic multipole source

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)

Acoustic sources may be organized by the number of spatial derivatives in their effective [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) forcing. A direct scalar source is an [acoustic monopole](acoustic-monopole.md), a divergence of a force density an [acoustic dipole](acoustic-dipole.md), and a double divergence of a stress tensor an [acoustic quadrupole](acoustic-quadrupole.md).
