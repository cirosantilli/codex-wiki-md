# Geographical latitude

↑ **Parent:** [Horizontal coordinate system](horizontal-coordinate-system.md)

For a spherical Earth, geographical latitude is the [angle](angle.md) of the local vertical above the equatorial plane. It equals the [altitude](altitude.md) of the north [celestial pole](celestial-pole.md) in the northern hemisphere. Precision astrometry distinguishes geodetic and astronomical latitude.
