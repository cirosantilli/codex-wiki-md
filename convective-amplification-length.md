# Convective amplification length

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

If a disturbance grows in time as $e^{\sigma_rt}$ while it is transported at positive speed $c$, the distance for one e-fold of growth is $\ell_a=c/\sigma_r$. Traversing distance $d$ gives exponent $\sigma_rd/c=d/\ell_a$, so $d\ll\ell_a$ produces little growth. This estimate compares a temporal [growth rate](growth-rate.md) with transport; it does not by itself determine the full spatial [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) or stability of a finite domain with feedback.
