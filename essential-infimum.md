# Essential infimum

↑ **Parent:** [Essential infimum and essential supremum](essential-infimum-and-essential-supremum.md)

The [essential infimum](essential-infimum.md) of a real [measurable function](measurable-function.md) is $\operatorname{ess\,inf}_\mu f=-\operatorname{ess\,sup}_\mu(-f)$. It is the largest lower bound that holds [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md), with values allowed in the [extended real number line](extended-real-number-line.md). Unlike a pointwise [infimum](infimum.md), it is unchanged by altering a representative on a [null set](null-set.md).

For the [function](function-split.md) $g$ in [essential infimum and essential supremum](essential-infimum-and-essential-supremum.md), the value $-3$ at one input changes the pointwise [infimum](infimum.md), but the [essential infimum](essential-infimum.md) remains zero under [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). If instead a [function](function-split.md) is $-3$ throughout an [interval](interval-mathematics.md) of any positive length and zero elsewhere, its [essential infimum](essential-infimum.md) is $-3$. The distinction concerns the [measure](measure-split.md) of the low-value [set](set-split.md), rather than the magnitude of the low value.
