# Essential infimum

↑ **Parent:** [Essential infimum and essential supremum](essential-infimum-and-essential-supremum.md)

The [essential infimum](essential-infimum.md) of a [measurable function](measurable-function.md) is $\operatorname{ess\,inf}u=-\operatorname{ess\,sup}(-u)$. It is the largest lower bound that holds [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md). Unlike a pointwise [infimum](infimum.md), it is unchanged by altering a representative on a [null set](null-set.md).
