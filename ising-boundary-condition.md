# Ising boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)

A fixed [Ising boundary condition](ising-boundary-condition.md) pins the [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) on a specified boundary, leaving the interior distributed with the original interaction weights. The boundary can be the inner [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) boundary of a finite box, or an exterior neighboring set if that convention is explicitly chosen. Conditioning on pinned [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) preserves the [FKG lattice condition](fkg-lattice-condition.md) of a ferromagnetic [Ising model](ising-model.md). The [FKG inequality](fkg-inequality.md) then makes interior expectations of [increasing observables](increasing-function-on-a-partially-ordered-set.md) nondecreasing in the pinned boundary [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md).
