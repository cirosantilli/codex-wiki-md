# Magnetic pseudomomentum

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic momentum and magnetic pseudomomentum](kinetic-momentum-and-magnetic-pseudomomentum.md)

For a [charged particle](charged-particle.md) in a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), the [symmetric gauge](symmetric-gauge.md) $\mathbf A=\mathbf B\times\mathbf x/2$ makes the displayed magnetic pseudomomentum a conserved quantity when no other position-dependent potential breaks translation symmetry. It differs from the [kinetic momentum](kinetic-momentum.md) $\boldsymbol\pi=\mathbf p-e\mathbf A=m\dot{\mathbf x}$. With $\mathbf B=B\mathbf e_3$, [Hamilton's equations](hamilton-s-equations.md) for $H=|\mathbf p-e\mathbf A|^2/(2m)$ give $\dot p_1=(eB/2)\dot x_2$ and $\dot p_2=-(eB/2)\dot x_1$, proving conservation of $K_1=p_1-eBx_2/2$ and $K_2=p_2+eBx_1/2$. The charge factor is necessary unless it has already been absorbed into the field convention. In [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md) the transverse components need not commute, even though each commutes with this [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md); [magnetic translation algebra](magnetic-translation-algebra.md) records that distinction.
