# Maximum separation of two circles

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

For two nonempty planar [circles](circle.md) with centers $c_i$ and radii $r_i\ge0$, the greatest [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) between their points is the displayed expression. The [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) gives the upper bound. When the centers differ, choose the two boundary points outward along the line of centers to attain it; when they coincide, choose opposite radial directions. For any third nonempty [circle](circle.md) and a point $u$ on it, $|z-w|\le|z-u|+|u-w|$ proves $m(C_1,C_2)\le m(C_1,C_3)+m(C_2,C_3)$. This is not a [metric](metric.md), since $m(C,C)=2r$ for a positive-radius [circle](circle.md).
