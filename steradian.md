# Steradian

↑ **Parent:** [Solid angle](solid-angle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steradian)

A [steradian](steradian.md) is the [solid angle](solid-angle.md) subtended at a [sphere](sphere.md)'s center by a patch of [surface area](surface-area.md) $R^2$. The whole [sphere](sphere.md) subtends $4\pi\,\mathrm{sr}$. In the [SI](international-system-of-units.md), the [steradian](steradian.md) has unit one; retaining its symbol identifies a [solid angle](solid-angle.md), just as [radians](radian.md) identify an [angle](angle.md). A [dimensionless quantity](dimensionless-quantity.md) still needs its physical meaning specified.[Version 4.01, June 2026; English §2.3.3, printed pp. 132–133, and §2.3.4, Table 4, printed pp. 133–134, notes (b)–(c): radian and steradian definitions and unit-one convention](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md)
