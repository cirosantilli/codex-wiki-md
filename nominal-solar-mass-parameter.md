# Nominal solar mass parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Solar mass](solar-mass.md)

The exact conversion factor $1.3271244\times10^{20}\,\mathrm{m^3s^{-2}}$. Dividing a measured [gravitational parameter](standard-gravitational-parameter.md) by this factor expresses a mass in nominal solar units, avoiding a separate conversion through the measured [gravitational constant](gravitational-constant.md).[Resolution B3, PDF pp.2–3, nominal solar mass parameter](resolution-b3-on-recommended-nominal-conversion-constants-for-selected-solar-and-planetary-properties-by-international-astronomical-union.md)
