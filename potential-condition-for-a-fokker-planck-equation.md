<h1 id="potential-condition-for-a-fokker-planck-equation">Potential condition for a Fokker--Planck equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fokker-Planck probability current](fokker-planck-probability-current.md)

For current $J=aP-\kappa\nabla P/2$, a scalar potential exists when the one-form $\kappa^{-1}a$ is exact. Then $D=\kappa/2$, $a=-D\nabla V$, and $J=-PD\nabla(V+\log P)$.
