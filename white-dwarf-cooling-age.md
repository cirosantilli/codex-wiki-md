# White-dwarf cooling age

↑ **Parent:** [White dwarf](white-dwarf.md)

An age inferred from a cooling model for a [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) or a white-dwarf population. The progenitor lifetime must be added to obtain time since stellar formation. Crystallization, composition, envelope properties, population history and observational completeness affect the inference.
