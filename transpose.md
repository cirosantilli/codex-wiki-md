# Transpose

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transpose)

The matrix transpose interchanges rows and columns: $(A^T)_{ij}=A_{ji}$. On a fixed matrix space, $A\mapsto A^T$ is the [transposition map](transpose.md), a [linear map](linear-map.md) that preserves the [trace norm](trace-norm.md) but is not completely positive in dimensions greater than one.
