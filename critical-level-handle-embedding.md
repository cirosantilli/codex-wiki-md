# Critical level handle embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md)

In a critical level [handle](handle.md) embedding, the [handles](handle.md) occur at distinct heights in an ambient product with the real line. Between consecutive heights, the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of the already attached [handles](handle.md) travels through a product [collar neighbourhood](collar-neighbourhood.md). At a [handle](handle.md) height the horizontal slice contains the [handle](handle.md), attached to the incoming [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) along its attaching region. This separates the local topology changes from the intervening product regions.
