<h1 id="eotvos-correction">Eötvös correction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md)

The correction to moving-platform gravity for the change in apparent vertical acceleration caused by motion over the rotating [Earth](earth.md). Eastward motion generally lowers apparent gravity and requires a positive correction; latitude and platform speed enter the full expression.
