# Semisimple linear operator

↑ **Parent:** [Linear operator](linear-operator.md)

A [linear operator](linear-operator.md) is semisimple if it becomes [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md) over an algebraic closure; equivalently, its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) has no repeated root over that closure. If an invertible operator $A$ has finite order $r$ prime to the [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md), its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) divides $X^r-1$. The derivative $rX^{r-1}$ is coprime to this polynomial, so $A$ is semisimple. Its invariant irreducible summands over a [finite field](finite-field.md) become multiplication by scalars in suitable [finite field extensions](finite-field-extension.md).
