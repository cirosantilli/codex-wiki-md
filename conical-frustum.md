# Conical frustum

↑ **Parent:** [Surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md)

The lateral surface obtained by rotating a straight segment in a [plane](plane.md) containing the rotation axis has endpoint [radii](radius.md) $a,c$ and slant length $\ell$. Its [surface area](surface-area.md) is $\pi(a+c)\ell$: the [radius](radius.md) is linear in the segment's [arc length](arc-length.md), so the [surface area of a surface of revolution](surface-area-of-a-surface-of-revolution.md) is $2\pi\int_0^\ell\rho(s)\,ds=2\pi\ell(a+c)/2$. This formula excludes the end disks and assumes that the segment's radial direction is fixed. A straight segment whose endpoint horizontal directions differ generally sweeps a different [surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md).
