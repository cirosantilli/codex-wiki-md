# Quasiconvexity from no missing shells

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiconvex subset](quasiconvex-subset.md)

Let $X$ be a finite combinatorial [2-complex](2-complex.md) satisfying $C'(1/6)$, and let $Y$ be a connected [CW subcomplex](cw-subcomplex.md) of its [universal cover](universal-cover.md) with no missing shells. If the largest cell perimeter is $L$, then $Y^{(1)}$ is $(L/2)$-quasiconvex in the universal cover's unit-edge [1-skeleton](1-skeleton.md). Bound an ambient [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) and an intrinsic [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) of $Y^{(1)}$ by a [reduced disc diagram](reduced-disc-diagram.md). A shell on the ambient side would shorten a [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md). A shell on the intrinsic side belongs to $Y$ and would shorten its intrinsic [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md). Spurs are likewise excluded from side interiors. The [Greendlinger ladder theorem](greendlinger-ladder-theorem.md) forces a ladder between the two marked corners. Each cell gives a path of length at most half its perimeter from either side to the other.
