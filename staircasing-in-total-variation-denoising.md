# Staircasing in total variation denoising

↑ **Parent:** [Total variation denoising](total-variation-denoising.md)

Staircasing is the formation of approximately constant plateaux in reconstructed data, even when the original data vary smoothly. The linear growth of the [total variation seminorm on a domain](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md) favors sparse spatial gradients, so preserving jumps can come at the cost of flattening gradual transitions.
