# Motion of two point vortices

↑ **Parent:** [Line vortex](line-vortex.md)

Take vortex strength to mean circulation and $J(x,y)=(-y,x)$. Each [point vortex](line-vortex.md) moves with the [velocity](velocity.md) induced by the other, so for $\mathbf d=\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_2$ the displayed equation holds. Since $\mathbf d\cdot J\mathbf d=0$, separation is constant; weighting the two velocities also gives $d(\kappa_1\mathbf x_1+\kappa_2\mathbf x_2)/dt=0$. Equal strengths at opposite points of a circle of radius $a$ rotate with angular speed $\kappa/(4\pi a^2)$.
