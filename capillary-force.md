# Capillary force

↑ **Parent:** [Surface tension](surface-tension-split.md)

A [force](force-split.md) caused by an interface through its contact-line [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) and the [capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md) it produces. Both contributions must be counted with consistent geometry. For a narrow, completely [wetting](wetting.md) [liquid](liquid.md) bridge between flat plates of wetted radius $a$ and gap $d\ll a$, the leading attractive [force](force-split.md) is $2\pi a^2\gamma/d$.
