# Rank-one intersection form and the projective-plane cohomology ring

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection form](intersection-form.md)

For a simply connected closed four-manifold with second [Betti number](betti-number.md) one, [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) and the [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) make $H_2$ torsion-free of rank one. The integral [intersection form](intersection-form.md) is unimodular, so its one-by-one matrix is $[1]$ or $[-1]$. Hence the square of a generator $u\in H^2$ generates $H^4$, giving the displayed [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md). This is a ring isomorphism; it need not preserve a preassigned orientation class.
