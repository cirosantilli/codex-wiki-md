# Universal property of sheafification

↑ **Parent:** [Sheafification](sheafification.md)

Every [presheaf morphism](morphism-of-presheaves.md) from $\mathcal F$ to a [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) $\mathcal H$ factors uniquely through the canonical [sheafification](sheafification.md) map. Local representatives give local images in $\mathcal H$, and the [sheaf gluing axiom](sheaf-gluing-axiom.md) makes their union unique.
