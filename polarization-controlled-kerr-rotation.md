# Polarization-controlled Kerr rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-Kerr interaction](cross-kerr-interaction.md)

A one-photon polarization qubit can conditionally rotate another mode's coherent amplitude: the horizontal branch leaves $\alpha$ unchanged and the vertical branch gives $\alpha e^{i\Theta}$. The output is generally entangled. Measuring the polarization in a [superposition](superposition-principle.md) basis can erase the branch label and prepare a coherent [superposition](superposition-principle.md) of the two amplitudes.
