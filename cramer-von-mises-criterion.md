<h1 id="cramer-von-mises-criterion">Cramér–von Mises criterion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Empirical distribution function](empirical-distribution-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cramér–von_Mises_criterion)

This goodness-of-fit criterion integrates the squared difference between the [empirical distribution function](empirical-distribution-function.md) and a specified continuous null distribution. Under a fully specified null, the [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md) makes its law distribution-free; its limiting law is $\int_0^1B^0(u)^2du$ for a [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md). Parameter fitting changes that calibration.
