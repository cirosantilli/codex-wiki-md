# Baryon-number washout

↑ **Parent:** [Baryogenesis](baryogenesis.md)

[Baryon-number washout](baryon-number-washout.md) is erasure of a [baryon asymmetry](baryon-asymmetry.md) by reactions that violate [baryon number](baryon-number.md). Inverse decays and scattering can drive the plasma toward its zero-asymmetry [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md). The relevant comparison is their rate against the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md), not just the [decay width](decay-width.md) that initially produced the asymmetry.
