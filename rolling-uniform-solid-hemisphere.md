# Rolling uniform solid hemisphere

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

Let the flat face make angle $\theta$ with horizontal while the rounded face contacts a horizontal plane. The centroid has height $a-(3a/8)\cos\theta$ and contact distance squared $b^2=a^2+(3a/8)^2-2a(3a/8)\cos\theta$. For [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md), its speed is $b|\dot\theta|$. Combining translation and rotation gives kinetic energy $\tfrac12(I_G+mb^2)\dot\theta^2$. Conservation from rest at $\theta=\pi/2$ gives the displayed result until the flat-face/contact geometry changes.
