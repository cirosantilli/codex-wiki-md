# Recombination hotspot

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recombination_hotspot)

A recombination hotspot is a short region with an elevated local rate of [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md). Repeated ancestral breakpoints there can separate neighboring [haplotype blocks](haplotype-block.md) of strong [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md).
