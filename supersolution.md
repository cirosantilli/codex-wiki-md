# Supersolution

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)

For a [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) $L[u]=0$ with its comparison order fixed, a [supersolution](supersolution.md) satisfies $L[w]\geq0$. For example, if $L=\partial_t-\partial_x^2+Q$ with $Q\geq0$, a sufficiently regular $w$ satisfying $L[w]\geq0$ and dominating the initial and boundary data lies above a solution by the [parabolic comparison principle](parabolic-comparison-principle.md). An unbounded-domain barrier such as $w=\eta e^{3t}(1+x^2)$ can provide the missing boundary control at large distance. The sign convention belongs to the chosen [differential operator](differential-operator.md) and must be stated.
