# Seesaw light-mass estimate needs a Dirac scale

↑ **Parent:** [Seesaw mechanism](seesaw-mechanism.md)

In the type-I regime $M_L\simeq0$, $M_R\gg m_D$, the light [mass](mass.md) is $m_D^2/M_R$. Specifying only the right-handed scale does not fix a numerical light [mass](mass.md). A choice of Dirac [mass](mass.md) is an extra assumption, not a result of [matrix](matrix.md) diagonalization.
