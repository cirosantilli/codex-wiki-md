# Cyclic Bogolyubov lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Bogolyubov lemma](bogolyubov-lemma.md)

For $D\subseteq\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z$ of [subset density](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) $\alpha$, $2D-2D$ contains a [Bohr set](bohr-set.md) of rank at most $8\alpha^{-2}$ and a fixed positive width, including for composite $q$. With normalized [Fourier coefficients on a finite abelian group](fourier-coefficient-on-a-finite-abelian-group.md), retain the frequencies where $|\widehat{1_D}|\geq\alpha^{3/2}/\sqrt8$. [Parseval identity on a finite group](parseval-identity-on-a-finite-group.md) bounds their number and the discarded fourth moment. Their nearly constant phases make $1_D*1_D*1_{-D}*1_{-D}$ positive on the [Bohr set](bohr-set.md).
