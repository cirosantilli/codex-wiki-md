# Borsuk counterexample in dimension 1325

↑ **Parent:** [Kahn-Kalai counterexample to the Borsuk conjecture](kahn-kalai-counterexample-to-the-borsuk-conjecture.md)

Use the [balanced cut-vector distance formula](balanced-cut-vector-distance-formula.md) with $p=13$. There are $\binom{51}{25}=247959266474052$ distinct cuts. A subset of strictly smaller [diameter](diameter.md) contains no pair with side intersection thirteen, so deleting their shared coordinate one and applying the [Frankl-Wilson theorem](frankl-wilson-theorem.md) bounds its size by $\binom{51}{12}=158753389900$. At least 1562 smaller-diameter parts are needed, exceeding $1325+1$. The cut vectors occupy an [affine hyperplane](affine-hyperplane.md) in $\mathbb R^{1326}$, which identifies isometrically with $\mathbb R^{1325}$ and gives the stated explicit counterexample.
