# Two-dimensional hyperbolic metric in polar coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

This metric is the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) with curvature scale $a$. Its nonzero connection coefficients are

$$
\Gamma^r_{\phi\phi}=-\sinh r\cosh r,
\qquad
\Gamma^\phi_{r\phi}=\Gamma^\phi_{\phi r}=\coth r.
$$
