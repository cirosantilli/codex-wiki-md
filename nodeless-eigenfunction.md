# Nodeless eigenfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md)

A nodeless real [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) has no zero in the interior of the domain. For a one-dimensional confining [Schrödinger operator](schrodinger-operator.md), the [nodeless theorem for a one-dimensional ground state](nodeless-theorem-for-a-one-dimensional-ground-state.md) identifies the [ground state](ground-state.md) as the eigenfunction that can be chosen strictly positive. Normalizable excited states have nodes.
