# Lattice translation

↑ **Parent:** [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md)

A [lattice translation](lattice-translation.md) moves every [lattice point](lattice-point.md) of a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) onto another [lattice point](lattice-point.md). Its vector is an [integer](integer-split.md) combination of [primitive lattice translation vectors](primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md). The [Laue equations](laue-equations.md) require the [wave phase](phase-waves.md) factor of every such translation to be one.
