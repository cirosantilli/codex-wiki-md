# Combination (combinatorics)

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)

A choice of $k$ distinct elements from a [set](set-split.md) of $n$ elements, with order ignored. Equivalently it is a $k$-element [subset](subset.md). The [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient-split.md) $\binom nk$ counts such choices; choosing with repetition instead produces a [multiset](multiset.md).
