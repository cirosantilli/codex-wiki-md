# Longitudinal scaling identity for a condensate solitary wave

↑ **Parent:** [Gross–Pitaevskii solitary wave](gross-pitaevskii-solitary-wave.md)

For a finite-energy [Gross–Pitaevskii solitary wave](gross-pitaevskii-solitary-wave.md), write $E=E_z+E_\perp+E_{\rm pot}$ with $E_z=\frac12\int|\psi_z|^2$. The longitudinal dilation $\psi_\lambda(x_\perp,z)=\psi(x_\perp,\lambda z)$ leaves its [renormalized momentum of a condensate](renormalized-momentum-of-a-condensate.md) invariant and changes energy to $\lambda E_z+\lambda^{-1}(E_\perp+E_{\rm pot})$. Stationarity of $E-Up$ at $\lambda=1$ implies $E_z=E_\perp+E_{\rm pot}$ and hence the displayed identity. This is a directional [Pohozaev identity](pohozaev-identity.md), valid when the integrals and variation boundary terms converge.
