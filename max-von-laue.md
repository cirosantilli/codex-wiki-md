The [Laue X-ray diffraction experiment of 1912](laue-x-ray-diffraction-experiment-of-1912.md) connects a periodic [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) to a pattern of diffracted [X-rays](x-ray.md). Its history links a proposed wave test to the measurements made by [Walther Friedrich](walther-friedrich.md) and [Paul Knipping](paul-knipping.md).
