# Gas-rich galaxy merger

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy merger](galaxy-merger.md)

A [gas-rich galaxy merger](gas-rich-galaxy-merger.md) involves a substantial reservoir of gas capable of [radiative cooling](radiative-cooling.md). Dissipation and gravitational torques permit gas inflow and enhanced [star formation](star-formation.md), unlike the nearly collisionless stellar evolution in a [dry galaxy merger](dry-galaxy-merger.md). A surviving or replenished gas reservoir can subsequently form a [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md).
