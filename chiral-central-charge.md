# Chiral central charge

↑ **Parent:** [Modular tensor category](modular-tensor-category.md)

The net chiral [central charge](central-charge.md) of an edge theory. For a bosonic unitary [modular tensor category](modular-tensor-category.md), its residue modulo eight is determined by the [topological spins](topological-spin.md) and [quantum dimensions](quantum-dimension.md) through $e^{2\pi i c/8}=\mathcal D^{-1}\sum_a d_a^2\theta_a$. This phase corrects the [signature](signature-of-a-quadratic-form.md) dependence in the [Witten-Reshetikhin-Turaev invariant](witten-reshetikhin-turaev-invariant.md).
