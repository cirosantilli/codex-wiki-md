# Distance extrema on a central plane section of an ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Distance extrema on a real quadratic surface](distance-extrema-on-a-real-quadratic-surface.md)

Let $A$ be a real [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) and let the columns of $U$ be an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of a two-dimensional [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) in three-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md). Points in that central plane are $x=Uq$, with $|x|^2=|q|^2$. Restricting the [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) $x^TAx=1$ gives $q^T(U^TAU)q=1$. The compressed [matrix](matrix.md) $U^TAU$ is a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md), and its extreme [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) give the distance formulas in the title by the [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md). The two extremal axes are the images under $U$ of the corresponding [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md). This restriction reduces a problem with two [Lagrange multipliers](lagrange-multiplier.md) to a two-dimensional [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md).
