# Electric field energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md)

The local energy per unit volume associated with an [electric field](electric-field-split.md) in vacuum is $u_E=\epsilon_0|E|^2/2$. Its volume [integral](integral-split.md) gives the electric contribution to the [electromagnetic energy](electromagnetic-energy.md). For a static field outside an isolated charged [conducting sphere](conducting-sphere.md), integrating this density from its radius to infinity gives the [electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md) $Q^2/(8\pi\epsilon_0R)$.
