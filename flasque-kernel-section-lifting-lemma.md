# Flasque-kernel section-lifting lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Flasque sheaf](flasque-sheaf.md)

For a [short exact sequence of sheaves](short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md) with [flasque sheaf](flasque-sheaf.md) kernel, every section of the quotient on any open set lifts to the middle sheaf on that set. Use the [Zorn lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) to choose a maximal partial lift, with chains glued by the [sheaf gluing axiom](sheaf-gluing-axiom.md). A new local lift differs from the old one on their overlap by a kernel section. Extend that difference by flasqueness, adjust the new lift, and glue, contradicting maximality unless its domain is the entire open set. Consequently a quotient of two flasque sheaves is flasque. With an [injective resolution of sheaves](injective-resolution-of-sheaves.md), the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) and repeated dimension shifts prove that flasque sheaves have zero positive-degree cohomology.
