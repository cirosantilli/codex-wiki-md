# Vertically averaged alpha closure for a mixed-pressure layer

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha disk](alpha-disk.md)

For the [constant gas-radiation ratio vertical disc structure](constant-gas-radiation-ratio-vertical-disc-structure.md), the integrated alpha prescription $\bar\nu\Sigma=\int\mu\,dz=(\alpha/\Omega)\int p\,dz$ gives the displayed mean [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) and $\lambda/(1+\lambda)=\alpha H\Omega\kappa\Sigma/(8c)$. Uniform [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) and nonuniform [pressure](pressure.md) are compatible because this prescription is an integrated equality. Imposing the corresponding pointwise equality would not preserve the exact vertical solution.
