# Limit point

↑ **Parent:** [Closed set](closed-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_point)

A [limit point](limit-point.md) of a [subset](subset.md) $A$ of a [topological space](topological-space.md) is a point $x$ whose every [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) meets $A\setminus\{x\}$. Thus $1$ is a [limit point](limit-point.md) of $[0,1)$ in the [real line](real-line.md), even though $1\notin[0,1)$. In contrast, an [isolated point](isolated-point.md) of $A$ belongs to its [closure](closure-topology.md) but is not a [limit point](limit-point.md) of $A$. This distinction separates [limit points](limit-point.md) from the more inclusive notion of an [adherent point](adherent-point.md).
