# Sensitivity analysis for unit and item nonresponse

↑ **Parent:** [Unit and item nonresponse](unit-and-item-nonresponse.md)

Vary the unobserved outcome probabilities for item and unit nonresponders, and any unknown auxiliary-variable mixture, over scientifically plausible ranges. Recompute the population estimate and identify values at which substantive conclusions change. This probes untestable transport or [missing at random](missing-at-random.md) assumptions; it is distinct from sampling uncertainty represented by a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md). Letting each missing-outcome probability vary from zero to one gives worst-case identification bounds.
