<h1 id="thevenin-resistance">Thévenin resistance</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thévenin's theorem](thevenin-s-theorem.md)

The negative slope of the [Thévenin terminal response](thevenin-terminal-response.md) with delivered [electric current](electric-current.md) $I$. [Thévenin source suppression](thevenin-source-suppression.md) determines it from the ratio of test voltage to entering test [electric current](electric-current.md). It need not represent a physical positive [resistor](resistor.md) inside the original network.
