# Maps to a sphere above the dimension of a compact smooth manifold are null-homotopic

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy](homotopy.md)

Approximate a continuous sphere-valued map by smooth real coordinate functions, then normalize. A sufficiently close approximation is homotopic to the original map by normalized straight interpolation. [Sard theorem](sard-s-theorem.md) implies the smooth map misses a point when its source dimension is smaller than the sphere dimension. The punctured sphere is contractible, so the map is null-homotopic, even if the source is disconnected.
