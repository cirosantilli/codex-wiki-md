# Subgradient

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

A subgradient of a [convex function](convex-function.md) $f$ at $x$ is a vector $g$ such that $f(y)\geq f(x)+g^T(y-x)$ for every $y$ in the domain.
