# Jacobi determinant derivative formula

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)

For a differentiable invertible [matrix](matrix.md) $A(t)$, $\frac d{dt}\log\det A=\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}\dot A)$ wherever a consistent logarithm is defined. In particular a positive spatial [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) satisfies $h^{ij}\dot h_{ij}=\dot h/h$. This separates its local volume change from shape change.
