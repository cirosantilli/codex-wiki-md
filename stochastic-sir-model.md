# Stochastic SIR model

↑ **Parent:** [SIR model](sir-model.md)

The [stochastic SIR model](stochastic-sir-model.md) is a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) with infection jump $(-1,1,0)$ at rate $\beta SI$ and recovery jump $(0,-1,1)$ at rate $\gamma I$. The state space consists of nonnegative integer compartment counts with conserved total population.
