# Compact self-similar disk with density-linear viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

The [Keplerian viscous diffusion equation](keplerian-viscous-diffusion-equation.md) with the displayed [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) has two such nontrivial finite-support [self-similar solutions](similarity-solution.md): $a=1$, $R=Ct^{2/5}$, and $a=5/4$, $R=Ct^{1/2}$. Both have $\sigma=(15At)^{-1}$ for $A,C,t>0$. Matching powers of radius eliminates the third power only when $(5/2-2a)(2-2a)=0$. The first solution conserves disk [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md); the second conserves disk [mass](mass.md) but requires an inner torque.
