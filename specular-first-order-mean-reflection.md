# Specular first-order mean reflection

↑ **Parent:** [First-order rough-surface scattered field](first-order-rough-surface-scattered-field.md)

For incident amplitude one, $\psi_i=e^{ip_ix-iq_iz}$ with $q_i>0$, Taylor expansion of a small-height [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) gives $\psi_{\rm sc}^{[1]}(x,0)=2iq_if(x)e^{ip_ix}$. If the mean height is constant, its mean transform is supported only at tangential wavenumber $p_i$, giving the displayed specular mean through first order. Small height alone is insufficient: deterministic mean height $a\cos Kx$ produces mean sidebands $p_i\pm K$. Stationary roughness has constant mean and therefore satisfies the first-order requirement.
