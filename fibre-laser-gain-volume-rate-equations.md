# Fibre-laser gain-volume rate equations

↑ **Parent:** [Laser rate equation](laser-rate-equation.md)

If a fraction $f$ of an otherwise uniform [optical cavity](optical-cavity.md) contains gain medium, define upper-state [density](density.md) per gain volume but [photon](photon.md) [density](density.md) per whole cavity volume. Stimulated upper-state depletion is $v_g\sigma Nn$, while cavity [photon](photon.md) production is $fv_g\sigma Nn$. Thus $\dot N=S-v_g\sigma Nn-N/\tau_2$ and $\dot n=(fv_g\sigma N-1/\tau_c)n$. Above threshold $N=N_{\mathrm{th}}$ and $n=f\tau_cN_{\mathrm{th}}(r-1)/\tau_2$, where $r=S\tau_2/N_{\mathrm{th}}$.
