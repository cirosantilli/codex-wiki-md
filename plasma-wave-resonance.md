# Plasma wave resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Cold magnetized plasma](cold-magnetized-plasma.md)

A limiting point of a [plasma](plasma-physics.md) [wave](wave-split.md) branch with divergent [refractive index](refractive-index.md). At finite coefficients of the [magnetized cold-plasma dielectric tensor](magnetized-cold-plasma-dielectric-tensor.md), the generic condition is $S\sin^2\theta+P\cos^2\theta=0$. Poles of that [magnetized cold-plasma dielectric tensor](magnetized-cold-plasma-dielectric-tensor.md) require a separate limiting analysis. [Collisions](collision.md), thermal dispersion or [mode conversion](mode-conversion.md) can regularize a singular cold prediction.
