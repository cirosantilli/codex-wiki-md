# Euler force

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_force)

A [mass](mass.md) $m$ in a [rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md) with varying [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) experiences this apparent [force](force.md). Dividing it by $m$ gives the [Euler acceleration](euler-acceleration.md).
