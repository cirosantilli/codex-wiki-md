# Source-volume length of a turbulent plume

↑ **Parent:** [Top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md)

Source [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) is negligible only when entrained volume greatly exceeds it. In the [jet limit of the top-hat plume model](jet-limit-of-the-top-hat-plume-model.md), this requires $z\gg Q_0/(c\sqrt{M_0})$. In a [pure plume](pure-plume.md) with $Q=C_Qz^{5/3}$, it requires $z\gg(Q_0/C_Q)^{3/5}$, where $C_Q=(3c/5)(9cF_0/20)^{1/3}$ for full-cross-section fluxes and $c=2\alpha_e\sqrt\pi$. These scales differ from the [jet length](jet-length.md) which measures imposed momentum relative to buoyancy. Neglecting source volume does not automatically permit neglecting source momentum.
