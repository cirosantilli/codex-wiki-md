# Poynting flux into lossy coaxial conductors

↑ **Parent:** [Coaxial cable](coaxial-cable.md)

A small longitudinal electric field drives conductor current in a lossy coaxial line. Its [cross product](cross-product.md) with the azimuthal [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) sends [energy](energy-split.md) radially into both conductors, where it becomes [heat](heat-split.md); forward [power](power.md) decreases with [distance](distance.md).
