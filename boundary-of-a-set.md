# Boundary of a set

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

The [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of a subset $A$ of a [topological space](topological-space.md) consists of the points whose every [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) meets both $A$ and its complement. Equivalently, $\partial A=\overline A\setminus A^\circ$, where the bar denotes [closure](closure-topology.md) and the circle denotes [interior](interior-topology.md).
