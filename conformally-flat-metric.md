# Conformally flat metric

↑ **Parent:** [Nordström's theory of gravitation](nordstrom-s-theory-of-gravitation.md)

A metric is conformally flat when it is locally a positive scalar multiple of a flat metric. Multiplication by the conformal factor preserves null cones and unparametrized null geodesics.

A conformally flat metric is locally a positive scalar multiple of a flat metric. It makes its domain a [conformally flat manifold](conformally-flat-manifold.md).
