# Matrix root basis of the symplectic Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md)

Relative to $J=\begin{pmatrix}0&I\\-I&0\end{pmatrix}$, a [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md) matrix has blocks $\begin{pmatrix}P&Q\\R&-P^{\mathsf T}\end{pmatrix}$ with $Q,R$ symmetric. Its diagonal [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) has basis $E_{ii}-E_{l+i,l+i}$. Write $\varepsilon_i(h)=\lambda_i$. A [root-space decomposition](root-space-decomposition.md) is obtained from

$$
\begin{aligned}
e_{\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j}&=E_{ij}-E_{l+j,l+i} &&(i\ne j),\\
e_{\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j}&=E_{i,l+j}+E_{j,l+i} &&(i<j),\\
e_{-\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j}&=E_{l+i,j}+E_{l+j,i} &&(i<j),\\
e_{2\varepsilon_i}&=E_{i,l+i},\qquad e_{-2\varepsilon_i}=E_{l+i,i}.&&
\end{aligned}
$$

The [matrix unit](matrix-unit.md) rule $[h,E_{ab}]=(h_{aa}-h_{bb})E_{ab}$ proves $[h,e_r]=r(h)e_r$. These root vectors and the diagonal basis span all the permitted blocks and are independent. There are $2l^2$ roots and $l$ diagonal basis elements, giving [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $l(2l+1)$.
