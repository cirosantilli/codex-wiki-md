# Stock-inversion reflection for the Black-Scholes equation

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes equation](black-scholes-equation.md)

Differentiating the displayed transformation shows that it preserves the [Black-Scholes equation](black-scholes-equation.md). The first derivative coefficient fixes the exponent $1-2r/\sigma^2$, which also makes the zero-order coefficient correct. Subtracting this reflected image of a [European call option](european-call-option.md) from the call itself enforces a zero boundary at $S=B$. For [strike price](strike-price.md) $K\geq B$, the reflected maturity [contingent claim payoff](contingent-claim-payoff.md) vanishes on $S>B$, giving the no-rebate [down-and-out option](down-and-out-claim.md) formula. Prices using this reflection are conditional on the barrier not having been hit already.
