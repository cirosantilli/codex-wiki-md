# Minimally coupled massless scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Massless scalar field](massless-scalar-field.md)

The displayed [action functional](action.md) contains no [mass](mass.md) term and no $R\phi^2$ curvature coupling. Its [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is $\partial_\mu(\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\nu\phi)=0$. In a flat [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), a [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) obeys $\phi_k''+2(a'/a)\phi_k'+k^2\phi_k=0$ in four dimensions. The expansion can therefore freeze these modes; the curvature term of a [conformally coupled scalar field](conformally-coupled-scalar-field.md) changes that conclusion.
