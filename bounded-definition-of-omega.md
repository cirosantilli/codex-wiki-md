# Bounded definition of omega

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy hierarchy](levy-hierarchy.md)

In [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md), $\omega$ is the least nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md). Ordinality is expressed by a [bounded formula in set theory](bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md) asserting transitivity and strict total membership order, with well-foundedness supplied by the [Axiom of foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md). Put $L(x)=\operatorname{Ord}(x)\land x\ne\varnothing\land\forall y\in x\,\exists z\in x\,(y\in z)$. Then $x=\omega$ is equivalent over [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) to $L(x)\land\forall y\in x\neg L(y)$, again bounded. Therefore nested [transitive models](transitive-model.md) of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) have the same $\omega$.
