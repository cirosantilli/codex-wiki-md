# Jacobian determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md)

The Jacobian determinant is the [determinant](determinant.md) of the square [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md). Its absolute value is the local volume-scaling factor in the [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md).

The [Jacobian matrix and determinant](jacobian-matrix-and-determinant.md) describe respectively the linear derivative and its signed volume factor.
