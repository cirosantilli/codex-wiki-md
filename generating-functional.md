# Generating functional

↑ **Parent:** [Perturbative quantum field theory](perturbative-quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generating_functional)

A generating functional is a [path integral](path-integral.md) with a source $J$ coupled to a field. Its [functional derivatives](functional-derivative.md) with respect to $J$ generate time-ordered correlation functions.
