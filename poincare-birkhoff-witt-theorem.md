<h1 id="poincare-birkhoff-witt-theorem">Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Universal enveloping algebra](universal-enveloping-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt_theorem)

For an ordered basis $(x_i)$ of a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$, the ordered monomials $x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_n^{a_n}$ form a basis of $U(\mathfrak g)$. In particular, a triangular decomposition $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak n^-\oplus\mathfrak t\oplus\mathfrak n^+$ gives $U(\mathfrak g)\cong U(\mathfrak n^-)\otimes U(\mathfrak t)\otimes U(\mathfrak n^+)$ as vector spaces.
