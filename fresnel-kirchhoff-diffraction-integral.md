# Fresnel-Kirchhoff diffraction integral

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md)

A scalar aperture approximation that sums the secondary waves reaching an observation point. For a point source with incident spherical [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $Ae^{iks}/s$, one common forward-normal convention is

$$
U(P)=\frac{A}{i\lambda}\int_{\mathcal A}\frac{K(Q)e^{ik(s+r)}}{sr}\,dS.
$$

Here $s$ and $r$ are source-to-aperture and aperture-to-observer distances and $K$ is an [obliquity factor](obliquity-factor.md). The [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md) approximation retains quadratic path phase; [Fraunhofer diffraction](fraunhofer-diffraction.md) additionally neglects the quadratic phase across the aperture. Kirchhoff boundary assumptions do not give an exact electromagnetic solution at a sharp edge.
