# Diffusion from a gradient energy

↑ **Parent:** [Gradient flow](gradient-flow.md)

The formal $L^2$ [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md) of this energy is $u_t=\operatorname{div}(\Phi'(r)\nabla u/r)$, with a [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) for no flux. The radial Hessian eigenvalues of the gradient integrand are $\Phi''(r)$ and $\Phi'(r)/r$. Their signs distinguish convex forward diffusion from formal backward diffusion. The [heat equation](heat-equation.md) uses $\Phi(r)=r^2/2$, whereas [total variation flow](total-variation-flow.md) uses $\Phi(r)=r$ with a [subgradient](subgradient.md) interpretation at zero.
