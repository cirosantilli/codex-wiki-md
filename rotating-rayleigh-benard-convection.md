<h1 id="rotating-rayleigh-benard-convection">Rotating Rayleigh-Bénard convection</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

A thermally driven fluid layer rotating about the vertical axis experiences a [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) that couples vertical [velocity](velocity.md) to vertical [vorticity](vorticity.md). Its onset depends on the [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md), [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) and [Taylor number](taylor-number.md), as well as thermal and mechanical [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). The force does no mechanical work, but changes the linear mode balance and nonlinear [convection roll](convection-roll.md) competition.
