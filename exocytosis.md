# Exocytosis

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exocytosis)

[Exocytosis](exocytosis.md) releases the contents of a membrane-bounded compartment to the extracellular space by fusion with the [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md). [Synaptic vesicle](synaptic-vesicle.md) fusion is an example. Its contents are released, while its membrane becomes connected to the [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md); see [synaptic vesicle](synaptic-vesicle.md) for recovery and reuse.
