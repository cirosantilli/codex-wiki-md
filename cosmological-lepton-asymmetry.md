# Cosmological lepton asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino degeneracy parameter](neutrino-degeneracy-parameter.md)

A nonzero neutrino chemical potential produces unequal neutrino and antineutrino densities and hence a cosmological lepton asymmetry. It changes the radiation density, and an electron-neutrino asymmetry also changes neutron--proton weak equilibrium during [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md).
