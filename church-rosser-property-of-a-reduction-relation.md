# Church-Rosser property of a reduction relation

↑ **Parent:** [Diamond property of a reduction relation](diamond-property-of-a-reduction-relation.md)

A reduction relation has the Church-Rosser property when its [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) generated by forward and backward steps satisfies $b\leftrightarrow^*c\Rightarrow\exists d\;(b\to^*d\leftarrow^*c)$. It is equivalent to confluence of $\to^*$. The [Church-Rosser theorem](church-rosser-theorem.md) establishes it for [beta reduction](beta-reduction.md).
