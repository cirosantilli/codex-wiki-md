# Quantum steering

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen paradox](einstein-podolsky-rosen-paradox.md)

For a shared [quantum entanglement](entangled-state.md) resource, one party's choice of measurement can prepare different conditional ensembles for the distant party. Discarding the outcome leaves the same distant [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md), so steering does not itself permit signalling. Demonstrating steering against a local hidden-state model is stronger than merely observing a change in a postselected description.
