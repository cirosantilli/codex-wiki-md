# Angular momentum decomposition about the centre of mass

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum about the centre of mass](angular-momentum-about-the-centre-of-mass.md)

Write each position and velocity as its [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) value plus a relative value. Expanding the total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) gives the displayed split into orbital angular momentum of the centre and internal angular momentum about it. The cross terms vanish because $\sum_i m_i\boldsymbol r_i'=\sum_i m_i\boldsymbol v_i'=0$.
