# Enstrophy

↑ **Parent:** [Vorticity](vorticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enstrophy)

[Enstrophy](enstrophy.md) measures squared [vorticity](vorticity.md): a local density is $\omega^2/2$, and its spatial integral or ensemble mean gives a global measure. In homogeneous [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) satisfies $\epsilon=\nu\langle\omega^2\rangle$. [Vortex stretching](vortex-stretching.md) can produce [enstrophy](enstrophy.md) in three dimensions; [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) destroys it.
