# Inelastic neutron scattering of TTF-TCNQ

↑ **Parent:** [Peierls transition](peierls-transition.md)

The experiment follows phonon peaks as both momentum and energy transfer are varied. In [inelastic neutron scattering](inelastic-neutron-scattering.md), $\mathbf q=\mathbf k_i-\mathbf k_f$ locates the lattice modulation and $\hbar\omega=E_i-E_f$ measures the mode energy, so a softened mode can be distinguished from static diffraction alone. Measurements on deuterated [TTF-TCNQ](ttf-tcnq.md) between $60$ and $300\,\mathrm K$ found a growing [Kohn anomaly](kohn-anomaly.md) near $2k_F$ below about $150\,\mathrm K$ as the $54\,\mathrm K$ transition was approached.[Abstract: measurement method, temperature interval and wavevector-dependent anomaly](phonon-dispersion-and-kohn-anomaly-in-tetrathiafulvalene-tetracyanoquinodimethane-ttf-tcnq-by-gen-shirane-and-others.md)
