# Quasi-asymptotic stability

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md)

An [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is quasi-asymptotically stable when some neighborhood of initial conditions has forward solutions converging to it. This attraction condition alone does not include [Lyapunov stability](lyapunov-stability.md). [Asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md) requires both attraction and Lyapunov stability. A positive [Lyapunov function](lyapunov-function.md) with nonpositive derivative and no nonstationary invariant trajectory in its zero-derivative set proves both when its sufficiently small sublevel sets are compact and forward invariant.
