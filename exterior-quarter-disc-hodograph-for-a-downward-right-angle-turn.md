# Exterior quarter-disc hodograph for a downward right-angle turn

↑ **Parent:** [Hodograph plane](hodograph-plane.md)

A unit-speed free layer turning downward around a reentrant wall corner has [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) $q=u-iv$ in the first-quadrant exterior of the [unit disc](unit-disc.md). Upstream $q=1$, downstream $q=i$, and the corner has infinite speed because its fluid angle is $3\pi/2$. Squaring $q$, applying $(s-1)/(s+1)$, and squaring again maps this domain to the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) with upstream, corner and downstream at $0,1,\infty$. The potential strip map $e^{\pi w}$ has the same normalization, proving the formula. The sign of the turn and the fluid corner angle determine whether the relevant hodograph is an exterior or interior quarter disc.
