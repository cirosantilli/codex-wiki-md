# Mechanical momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum](momentum-split.md)

For a charged particle minimally coupled to a magnetic [vector potential](vector-potential.md), the mechanical momentum is $\boldsymbol\pi=\mathbf p-q\mathbf A$, with $\mathbf p$ the [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md). It is the [mass](mass.md) times [velocity](velocity.md) in nonrelativistic mechanics.
