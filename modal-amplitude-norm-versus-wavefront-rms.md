# Modal amplitude norm versus wavefront RMS

↑ **Parent:** [Wavefront error](wavefront-error.md)

For a real [wavefront error](wavefront-error.md) $h=\sum_k a_k s_k$, its squared spatial [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) is $\langle h^2\rangle=\sum_{k,l}a_ka_l\langle s_ks_l\rangle$. Orthogonal unit-peak [sine](sine.md) modes have mean square $1/2$, so $h_{\rm rms}^2=\frac12\sum_k a_k^2$, whereas the coefficient norm is $\sqrt{\sum_k a_k^2}$. On a finite [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) needs checking; independent uniform random [wave phases](phase-waves.md) give the same $1/2$ diagonal after [wave phase](phase-waves.md) averaging. This normalization matters when a [speckle contrast of a sinusoidal wavefront error](speckle-contrast-of-a-sinusoidal-wavefront-error.md) is specified by peak [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md).
