# Embedded two-level quantum rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum pulse area](quantum-pulse-area.md)

For a [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md) satisfying $A^3=A$, its squared operator $P=A^2$ is the projector onto the active eigenvalue-$\pm1$ subspace. All remaining states have [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) zero. Separate even and odd powers in the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) to obtain the displayed rotation on the active subspace and the identity on its spectator subspace. Thus an embedded two-level coupling need not square to the full Hilbert-space identity.
