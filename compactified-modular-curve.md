# Compactified modular curve

↑ **Parent:** [Modular curve](modular-curve.md)

For a finite-index subgroup of the [modular group](modular-group.md), the quotient of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) becomes a compact [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md) after adjoining its cusp classes. At an elliptic fixed point use the quotient coordinate for the finite stabilizer; at a cusp use its exponential local parameter. Weight-zero [modular forms](modular-form.md) descend to [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) on this compact surface, and hence are constant by the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md).
