# Killing derivative contraction identity

↑ **Parent:** [Killing equation](killing-equation.md)

For a [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md), set $F_{ab}=\nabla_aK_b$ and $a^s=K^t\nabla_tK^s$. The [Killing equation](killing-equation.md) makes $F$ antisymmetric, and expansion of normalized three-index antisymmetrization gives the displayed identity. On a [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md), [hypersurface orthogonality](hypersurface-orthogonality.md) removes its first right-hand term and relates $F^{ab}F_{ab}$ to [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md).
