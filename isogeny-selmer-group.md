# Isogeny Selmer group

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer map of an elliptic curve](kummer-map-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For an [isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md) $\phi:E\to E'$ over a [number field](number-field.md) $K$, this finite subgroup of $H^1(K,E[\phi])$ consists of [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md) classes whose restriction at every place comes from $E'(K_v)/\phi E(K_v)$. It fits into

$$
0\to E'(K)/\phi E(K)\to\operatorname{Sel}^{\phi}(E/K)\to\operatorname{Sha}(E/K)[\phi]\to0,
$$

where the last group is the kernel of the induced map on [Tate–Shafarevich groups](tate-shafarevich-group.md). Thus local computations bound the rational-point quotient without necessarily determining it. For multiplication by $n$, this recovers the [Selmer group of an elliptic curve](n-selmer-group.md).
