# Epigraph

↑ **Parent:** [Functional](functional.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epigraph_(mathematics))

The [epigraph](epigraph.md) is $\{(u,t):E(u)\leq t\}$. It is a [convex set](convex-set.md) exactly when $E$ is a [convex function](convex-function.md), and is a [sequentially closed set](sequentially-closed-set.md) in the [product topology](product-topology.md) exactly when $E$ is [sequentially lower semicontinuous](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md).
