# Physical range of a uniaxial nematic order parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Uniaxial nematic order](uniaxial-nematic-order.md)

For $S=\langle3\mathbf s\mathbf s^{\mathsf T}-I\rangle=Q(3\mathbf n\mathbf n^{\mathsf T}-I)$ and unit molecular orientations $\mathbf s$, contraction with $\mathbf n$ gives $2Q=3\langle(\mathbf s\cdot\mathbf n)^2\rangle-1$. Since the squared projection is between zero and one, the displayed bounds follow. The [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) in this normalization are $2Q,-Q,-Q$. A differently normalized [nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md) has correspondingly rescaled bounds; its numerical coefficients cannot be transferred unchanged.
