# Cloud albedo susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Twomey effect](twomey-effect.md)

For $\tau\propto N_d^{1/3}$ at fixed liquid-water content and thickness, and $A=\tau/(\tau+7)$, the derivative is $dA/dN_d=A(1-A)/(3N_d)$. For a fixed fractional number increase, the initial response is greatest at $A=1/2$. The exact finite-change expression is $A'=sA/(1-A+sA)$ with $s=(N_d'/N_d)^{1/3}$. Uncertain activation of additional particles and changes in [water](water.md) content limit direct application to real [clouds](cloud.md).
