# Photographic emulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Photography](photography-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photographic_emulsion)

A [photographic emulsion](photographic-emulsion.md) is a coating that responds to [light](light.md) to record an image in [photography](photography-split.md), for example on a [photographic plate](photographic-plate.md). [Silver halide](silver-halide.md) [crystals](crystal.md), commonly [silver bromide](silver-bromide.md), are dispersed in [gelatin](gelatin.md). Exposure leaves a [latent image](latent-image.md). A developer produces dark [silver](silver.md) grains and fixing removes remaining sensitive material. A [microscope](microscope.md) can reveal a [charged particle](charged-particle.md)'s path through the coating: the basis of [nuclear emulsion](nuclear-emulsion.md) [particle detection](particle-detection-split.md).[Luisa Bonolis, Research Profile: Cecil Powell, “A new visual method: nuclear emulsions”, paragraphs describing composition, coating, development, fixing and microscope observation](research-profile-cecil-powell-by-luisa-bonolis.md)
