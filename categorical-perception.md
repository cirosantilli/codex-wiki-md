# Categorical perception

↑ **Parent:** [Perception](perception.md)

A pattern in which discrimination or similarity depends on category boundaries beyond an appropriate physical or psychophysical baseline. Task demands, labeling and memory can contribute; boundary-related performance does not automatically establish a discontinuous sensory representation.
