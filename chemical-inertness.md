# Chemical inertness

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reactivity](chemical-reactivity.md)

A [chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md) is chemically inert relative to specified conditions when it does not readily undergo the relevant [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md). The conditions and possible reaction partners matter; the term does not assert the impossibility of every [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md).
