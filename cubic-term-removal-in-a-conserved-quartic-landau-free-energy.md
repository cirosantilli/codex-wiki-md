# Cubic-term removal in a conserved quartic Landau free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)

For a scalar [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) $f=a\phi^2/2+b\phi^4/4+c\phi^3/3+d\phi$ with $b>0$, the shift $\phi=\psi-c/(3b)$ removes the cubic term. The quadratic coefficient becomes $a'=a-c^2/(3b)$ and the linear coefficient becomes $d'=d-ac/(3b)+2c^3/(27b^2)$. In a closed [binary fluid mixture](binary-fluid-mixture.md), the linear term is constant at fixed total composition, leaving the symmetric quartic [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) construction in the shifted variable.
