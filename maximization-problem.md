# Maximization problem

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

A maximization problem seeks a feasible point with largest objective value. Its supremum can fail to be attained or be infinite. Negating the objective gives a [minimization problem](minimization-problem.md). A global extremum of an [optimization Lagrangian](optimization-lagrangian.md) can certify a feasible optimum by the [Lagrangian sufficiency theorem](lagrange-sufficiency-theorem.md).
