# Benjamin-Feir instability

↑ **Parent:** [Modulational instability](modulational-instability.md)

For the [complex Ginzburg–Landau equation](complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) $A_t=A+(1+ic_1)A_{xx}-(1+ic_3)|A|^2A$, the uniform oscillation has a neutral [phase](phase-waves.md) mode whose [growth rate](growth-rate.md) is $\lambda=-(1+c_1c_3)k^2+O(k^4)$. Negative [phase](phase-waves.md) diffusion therefore causes a long-wavelength [modulational instability](modulational-instability.md). The [Benjamin-Feir stability condition](benjamin-feir-stability-condition.md) is the complementary positive-diffusion condition. On a finite domain an unstable wavenumber must actually be allowed by the boundary conditions.
