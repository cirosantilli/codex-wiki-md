# Representability from a solution set

↑ **Parent:** [Representable functor](representable-functor.md)

On a [complete category](complete-category.md) that is [locally small](locally-small-category.md), a set-valued [functor](functor.md) is representable exactly when it preserves small [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) and its [category of elements](category-of-elements.md) has a [weakly initial set](weakly-initial-set.md). [Categorical limit](categorical-limit.md) preservation makes that comma [category](category-split.md) complete. The [initial-object lemma for complete categories with a weakly initial set](initial-object-lemma-for-complete-categories-with-a-weakly-initial-set.md) then supplies a [universal element](universal-element-of-a-set-valued-functor.md), hence a [representation of a functor](representation-of-a-functor.md).
