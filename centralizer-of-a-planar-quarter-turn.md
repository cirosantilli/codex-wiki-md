# Centralizer of a planar quarter-turn

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md)

For $J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, the real [matrix](matrix.md) solutions of $AJ=JA$ are precisely

$$
A=uI+vJ=\begin{pmatrix}u&-v\\v&u\end{pmatrix},\qquad u,v\in\mathbb R.
$$

A nonzero such matrix equals $\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\,R_\theta$, where $R_\theta$ is a planar [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) with $\cos\theta=u/\sqrt{u^2+v^2}$ and $\sin\theta=v/\sqrt{u^2+v^2}$. It acts like multiplication by the [complex number](complex-number.md) $u+iv$. The zero matrix is the zero scalar multiple of any rotation.
