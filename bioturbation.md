# Bioturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Ecology](ecology-split.md)

Biological disturbance and mixing of sediment, including burrowing. It modifies habitats and can destroy or change surfaces that otherwise preserve body impressions and [trace fossils](trace-fossil.md).
