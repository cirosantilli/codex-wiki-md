# Triglyceride

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid](lipid.md)

A [triglyceride](triglyceride.md) contains [glycerol](glycerol.md) joined to three [fatty acid](fatty-acid.md)-derived [acyl groups](acyl-group.md) through [ester](ester.md) linkages. Compare the three chains with the two-chain, [phosphate](phosphate.md)-headed [glycerophospholipid](glycerophospholipid.md) illustrated below. See [Biology 2e: fats and oils](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-3-lipids).
