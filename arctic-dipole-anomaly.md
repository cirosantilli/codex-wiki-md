# Arctic dipole anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Climate change](climate-change.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_dipole_anomaly)

The Arctic dipole anomaly describes a pressure pattern with opposed anomalies on the North American and Eurasian sides of the Arctic. It is distinct from the more nearly annular [Arctic Oscillation](arctic-oscillation.md); associated winds can change [sea ice](sea-ice.md) export and warm-air advection.
