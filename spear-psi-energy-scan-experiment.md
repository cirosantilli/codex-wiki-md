# SPEAR psi energy-scan experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Richter-Ting independent J/psi discovery experiments](richter-ting-independent-j-psi-discovery-experiments.md)

At SPEAR, counterrotating [electron](electron.md) and [Positron](positron.md) beams collided inside a magnetic [particle detector](particle-detector.md). Finer steps in stored-beam [energy](energy-split.md) resolved a sharp production peak. A magnetometer and measured orbit positions monitored the energy settings; [Bhabha scattering](bhabha-scattering.md) normalized the [cross sections](cross-section-physics.md). The paper attributed the observed width to beam-energy spread and radiation effects, reporting an upper limit on intrinsic width. Its [muon](muon.md) identification system was not functioning: the non-electron two-body excess was not conclusively a [muon](muon.md) pair.[Discovery of a Narrow Resonance in electron–positron Annihilation, pp. 1406–1408: scan, energy monitoring, normalization, width limit and two-body identification caveat](discovery-of-a-narrow-resonance-in-e-plus-e-annihilation-by-j-e-augustin.md)

A scan must compare normalized production rates, since more selected events can result from more beam encounters or different [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md). A proposed diagnostic is to revisit energies on either side of the peak while monitoring the energy calibration and event selection. Such neighboring settings test the local production background; their event totals alone are not an independent measurement of the intrinsic [decay width](decay-width.md).
