# Blue loop

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_loop)

A blue loop is an excursion to higher [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md), followed by a return redward, during [core helium burning](core-helium-burning.md) in some evolved stars. Its extent depends on core and envelope structure, composition gradients and mixing. Only loops reaching the [Cepheid instability strip](cepheid-instability-strip.md) produce the associated additional [Cepheid variable](cepheid-variable.md) crossings.
