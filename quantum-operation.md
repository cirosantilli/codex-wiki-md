# Quantum operation

↑ **Parent:** [Completely positive map](completely-positive-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_operation)

A [completely positive map](completely-positive-map.md) with $\sum_\alpha K_\alpha^\dagger K_\alpha\leq I$ is a trace-nonincreasing quantum operation, representing an outcome branch with its probability retained in the trace. A deterministic [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) has equality and is trace preserving. Dividing a branch output by its trace gives a conditional state but generally produces a nonlinear update; that normalization is not part of the linear quantum operation.
