# Square-root stock implied volatility

↑ **Parent:** [Square-root stock claim](square-root-stock-claim.md)

For a [square-root stock claim](square-root-stock-claim.md) priced by $C(t,T)=\mathbb E[\sqrt{S_T}\mid\mathcal F_t]$, the constant-volatility [geometric Brownian motion](geometric-brownian-motion.md) price is $\sqrt{S_t}\exp(-\sigma^2(T-t)/8)$. If $t<T$, $S_t>0$ and $0<C(t,T)\leq\sqrt{S_t}$, its unique nonnegative inverse volatility is the displayed $\Sigma(t,T)$. For bounded predictable volatility in $dS=S\sigma\,dW$, bounds $0\leq a\leq\sigma\leq b$ give $a\leq\Sigma\leq b$, without independence from the [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). At zero stock price the claim price is also zero and no volatility is identified by this equation; a separate convention is needed.
