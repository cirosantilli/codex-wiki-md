# Imaginary axis

↑ **Parent:** [Complex plane](complex-plane.md)

The imaginary axis is the set of [complex numbers](complex-number.md) $iy$ with $y\in\mathbb R$. It is the vertical coordinate axis in the [complex plane](complex-plane.md).
