# Hot ionized medium

↑ **Parent:** [Multiphase interstellar medium](multiphase-interstellar-medium.md)

Dilute shock-heated interstellar plasma with characteristic [temperatures](temperature-split.md) around $10^6\ \mathrm K$. [supernova](supernova.md) [energy](energy-split.md) injection and subsequent cooling are important to its evolution; [temperature](temperature-split.md) alone does not determine its volume filling factor.
