# Doublet of quartets

↑ **Parent:** [NMR multiplet](nmr-multiplet.md)

A first-order [NMR multiplet](nmr-multiplet.md) split into a doublet by one spin-one-half nucleus and into quartets by three equivalent spin-one-half nuclei. Ideally it has eight lines; overlap and second-order behavior can change its appearance.
