# Electronic heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)

[Electronic heat capacity](electronic-heat-capacity.md) is the contribution from electronic excitations to [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). A simple normal [metal](metal.md) with a smooth [density of states](density-of-states.md) near its [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) has $C_{\mathrm m}^{\rm el}\simeq\gamma T$ well below its [electron Fermi temperature](electron-fermi-temperature.md). Only a narrow [energy](energy-split.md) interval near the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) changes occupation on heating, explaining the small fraction of active [electrons](electron.md). The [Debye T-cubed test](debye-t-cubed-test.md) separates this contribution from lattice [phonons](phonon.md). Interactions, a [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md) transition or other electronic structure can invalidate a constant $\gamma$ description.
