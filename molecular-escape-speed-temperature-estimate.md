# Molecular escape-speed temperature estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Escape velocity](escape-velocity.md)

Equating the molecular root-mean-square speed to the surface [escape velocity](escape-velocity.md) gives $3k_BT/m=2GM/R$, or $T=2mGM/(3k_BR)$. This compares speed scales; the high-speed tail can escape below that [temperature](temperature-split.md), and molecular dissociation can invalidate the assumed species at high [temperature](temperature-split.md).
