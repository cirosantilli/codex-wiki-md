# Unit of measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Metrology](metrology.md)

A [unit of measurement](unit-of-measurement.md) is a chosen reference for comparing magnitudes of a [physical quantity](physical-quantity.md). A value written as a number times a [measurement unit](unit-of-measurement.md) changes its numerical part when the [measurement unit](unit-of-measurement.md) changes, while representing the same [physical quantity](physical-quantity.md).
