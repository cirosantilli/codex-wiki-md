# Rank-one covariance spike

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md)

For a unit vector $u$ and $\theta>0$, a rank-one covariance spike is the [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $I+\theta uu^\top$. Its [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $1+\theta$ in the direction $u$ and one on the [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md). It arises by adding an independent scalar [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) signal $Yu$ to a standard [Gaussian random vector](gaussian-random-vector.md).
