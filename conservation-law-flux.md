# Conservation law flux

↑ **Parent:** [System of conservation laws](system-of-conservation-laws.md)

A [conservation law flux](conservation-law-flux.md) $F(U)$ transports the conserved density $U$ across a surface. In one dimension $U_t+\partial_xF(U)=0$; a moving discontinuity satisfies $s[U]=[F]$. For a stationary [shock wave](shock-wave.md), each conserved flux has the same value on both sides.
