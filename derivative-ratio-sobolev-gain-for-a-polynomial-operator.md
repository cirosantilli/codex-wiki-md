# Derivative-ratio Sobolev gain for a polynomial operator

↑ **Parent:** [Hypoelliptic operator](hypoelliptic-operator.md)

Suppose a degree-$N$ [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) obeys $|\partial^\alpha Q(\xi)|\leq C_\alpha|\xi|^{-\delta|\alpha|}|Q(\xi)|$ at large real frequency, with $\delta>0$. A nonzero constant [derivative](derivative.md) of total order $N$ gives $|Q|\gtrsim\langle\xi\rangle^{\delta N}$. Reciprocal differentiation then bounds $\partial^\alpha(1/Q)$ by $\langle\xi\rangle^{-\delta N-\delta|\alpha|}$. The [high-frequency reciprocal parametrix kernel](high-frequency-reciprocal-parametrix-kernel.md) therefore gains $\delta N$ Sobolev [derivatives](derivative.md). Localize the [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) by a [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) equal to one near the target; the commutator lies outside that target and the reciprocal kernel makes its contribution smooth. This proves the displayed gain without demanding global regularity of the original [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md).
