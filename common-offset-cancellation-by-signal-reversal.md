# Common-offset cancellation by signal reversal

↑ **Parent:** [Systematic error](systematic-error.md)

If a controlled reversal changes the desired [signal](signal.md)'s sign while leaving an additive offset unchanged, readings $y_+=S+b$ and $y_-=-S+b$ separate them by half-difference and half-sum. Offset drift, gain changes, hysteresis or a background that also reverses violate the simple cancellation. Interleaving polarities and independent blank measurements test those assumptions; [signal](signal.md) reversal does not cancel an arbitrary [systematic error](systematic-error.md).
