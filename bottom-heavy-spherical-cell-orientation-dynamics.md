# Bottom-heavy spherical-cell orientation dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Gyrotaxis](gyrotaxis.md)

For a spherical cell with its [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) displaced by $-\ell_g\mathbf p$ from its geometric centre, gravity produces [torque](torque.md) $mg\ell_g\mathbf p\times\mathbf k$. Rotational resistance $\zeta_r$ gives angular [velocity](velocity.md) $\boldsymbol\omega/2+(mg\ell_g/\zeta_r)\mathbf p\times\mathbf k$. Taking its cross product with $\mathbf p$ gives the displayed orientation equation with $B=\zeta_r/(mg\ell_g)$. Steady orientation therefore requires the two terms on the right to sum to zero; placing the [vorticity](vorticity.md) term alone on the other side changes its sign.
