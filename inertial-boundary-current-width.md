# Inertial boundary-current width

↑ **Parent:** [Western boundary current](western-boundary-current.md)

An inertial closure balances [advection](advection.md) of [relative vorticity](relative-vorticity.md) with [planetary vorticity](planetary-vorticity.md) advection. If a basin of width $L$ and meridional size $B$ has interior speed $V_I$, transport continuity gives $V_b\delta\sim V_IL$. Equating $V_b^2/(B\delta)$ with $\beta V_b$ yields $\delta_I^2\sim V_IL/(\beta B)$, reducing to $V_I/\beta$ when $B$ and $L$ are comparable. This is a scale estimate rather than a full boundary-current profile.
