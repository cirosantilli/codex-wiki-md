# Principal minor

↑ **Parent:** [Minor (linear algebra)](minor-linear-algebra.md)

A principal minor is the [determinant](determinant.md) of a square [submatrix](submatrix.md) selected using the same index set for rows and columns. For a three-by-three [matrix](matrix.md), the coefficient of $\lambda$ in its monic [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) is the sum of its three principal minors of order two.
