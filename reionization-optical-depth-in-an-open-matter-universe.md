# Reionization optical depth in an open matter universe

↑ **Parent:** [Reionization](reionization.md)

For full [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [ionization](ionization.md) after instantaneous [reionization](reionization.md), $n_{e0}=3\Omega_bH_0^2/(8\pi Gm_p)$ and $H(z)=H_0(1+z)\sqrt{1+\Omega_mz}$. Integrating [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) gives

$$
\kappa=\frac{\Omega_b C_H}{\Omega_m^2}\{u^3-1-3(1-\Omega_m)(u-1)\},\quad u=\sqrt{1+\Omega_mz_{\rm re}},\quad C_H=\frac{\sigma_TcH_0}{4\pi Gm_p}.
$$

For $\Omega_m=1$, $\kappa=\Omega_bC_H[(1+z_{\rm re})^{3/2}-1]$. At fixed baryon density and redshift, lowering the matter density reduces the expansion rate and increases the [optical depth](optical-depth.md).
