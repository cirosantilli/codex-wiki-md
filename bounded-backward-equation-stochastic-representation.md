# Bounded backward-equation stochastic representation

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov backward equation](kolmogorov-backward-equation.md)

For a bounded smooth solution of $u_t=Lu$ with initial function $f$, apply the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) to $u(t-s,X_s)$. Its drift vanishes and its boundedness upgrades the localized stochastic integral to a true martingale. Taking endpoint expectations yields $u(t,x)=\mathbb E_xf(X_t)$ without a global bound on $u_x$.
