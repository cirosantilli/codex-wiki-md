# DNA double-strand break

↑ **Parent:** [DNA repair](dna-repair.md)

A break involving both strands of a [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) molecule. Unrepaired breaks can prevent reliable transmission; controlled breaks also initiate meiotic [homologous recombination](homologous-recombination.md).
