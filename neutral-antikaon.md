# Neutral antikaon

↑ **Parent:** [Kaon](kaon.md)

The neutral strange [meson](meson.md) with [strangeness](strangeness.md) $-1$ and valence content $s\bar d$. It is distinct from the neutral kaon flavour state $K^0=d\bar s$, which has strangeness $+1$. These are flavour eigenstates, not the weak-interaction propagation eigenstates of [neutral kaon mixing](neutral-kaon-mixing.md).
