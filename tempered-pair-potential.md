# Tempered pair potential

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md)

A standard sufficient tempering condition is this tail bound beyond a fixed radius, with $\epsilon>0$ in spatial dimension $d$. It makes the distant interaction tail integrable; finite-range [pair potentials](pair-potential.md) satisfy it. Together with a [stable pair potential](stable-pair-potential.md) and suitable growing containers, such assumptions support the conventional [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md). A [Curie–Weiss model](curie-weiss-model.md) with its size-dependent coupling uses a different prescription.
