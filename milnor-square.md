# Milnor square

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic K-theory](algebraic-k-theory.md)

A cartesian square of unital [rings](ring.md) $A=A_1\times_{A_0}A_2$ is a [Milnor square](milnor-square.md) when at least one map $A_i\to A_0$ is surjective. [Finitely generated projective modules](finite-projective-module.md) over $A$ can then be assembled from [projective modules](projective-module.md) over the two upper [rings](ring.md) together with an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) of their reductions over $A_0$.
