# One-to-three oscillator degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Two commensurate quantum harmonic oscillators](two-commensurate-quantum-harmonic-oscillators.md)

For two independent [quantum harmonic oscillators](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) of frequencies $\omega$ and $3\omega$, the product state with occupation numbers $n_+,n_-\geq0$ has energy $(n_++3n_-+2)\hbar\omega$. For a fixed excitation integer $N$, every allowed pair is $n_-=j$, $n_+=N-3j$ with $0\leq j\leq\lfloor N/3\rfloor$. Each contributes one orthogonal product eigenvector, proving the displayed degeneracy. The offset two is the sum of the two zero-point energies in units of $\hbar\omega$.
