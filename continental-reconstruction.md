# Continental reconstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Paleogeography](paleogeography.md)

An inferred arrangement of continental blocks at a specified geological time. Relative rotations must satisfy independent constraints from [paleomagnetism](paleomagnetism.md), [stratigraphy](stratigraphy.md), [sedimentary provenance](sedimentary-provenance.md), [metamorphic belts](metamorphic-belt.md) and dated [suture zones](suture-zone.md). Geometric compatibility alone does not establish uniqueness.
