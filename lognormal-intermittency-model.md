# Lognormal intermittency model

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov refined similarity hypothesis](kolmogorov-refined-similarity-hypothesis.md)

If $\log(\epsilon_r/\epsilon)$ is Gaussian with [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma_r^2$, normalization gives $\langle\epsilon_r^q\rangle/\epsilon^q=\exp[q(q-1)\sigma_r^2/2]$. Taking $\exp(\sigma_r^2)=B(\ell/r)^\mu$ predicts $\zeta_p=p/3-\mu p(p-3)/18$. Its eventual negative high-order exponents show that this extrapolation cannot hold for arbitrarily large orders with finite uniform velocity [moments](moment.md).
