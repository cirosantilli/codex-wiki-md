# Negative colouring error of a forced-set closure

↑ **Parent:** [Finite lattice approximation for monotone clique circuits](finite-lattice-approximation-for-monotone-clique-circuits.md)

Independently colour each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) with one of $m-1$ colours and join differently coloured [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). This [complete multipartite graph](complete-multipartite-graph.md) has no $m$-[clique](clique-graph-theory.md). If a forced set $W$ is rainbow but its $r$ witnesses are not, condition on the colours of $W$. The disjoint petals make those failure events [conditionally independent](conditional-independence.md). For each witness of size at most $\ell$, the probability of a colour collision is at most $\binom{\ell}{2}/(m-1)$. If this is at most $1/2$, one closure step causes an error with probability at most $2^{-r}$. There are at most $n^{\ell+1}$ possible steps; apply the [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md).
