# Charles-Augustin de Coulomb

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles-Augustin_de_Coulomb)

Physicist and engineer whose work includes friction and strength of materials. His name appears in the [Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion](mohr-coulomb-failure-criterion.md).
