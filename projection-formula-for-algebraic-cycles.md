# Projection formula for algebraic cycles

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection product of Cartier divisors](intersection-product-of-cartier-divisors.md)

For a proper [morphism of varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) $f:X\to Y$, a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$ on $Y$, and an [algebraic cycle](algebraic-cycle.md) $\alpha$ on $X$, pullback of $D$, intersection with $\alpha$, and proper pushforward satisfy the displayed identity in the [Chow group](chow-group.md). In particular, for a [generically finite morphism](generically-finite-morphism.md) of surfaces of degree $r$, $(f^*D)^2=rD^2$. For a curve $C$ contracted to a point, $f_*C=0$, so $f^*D\cdot C=0$.
