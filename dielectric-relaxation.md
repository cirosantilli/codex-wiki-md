# Dielectric relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Complex permittivity](complex-permittivity.md)

Delayed adjustment of [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) after a change in [electric field](electric-field.md). A simple illustrative model lets the slowly adjusting part $\mathbf P_s$ obey $\tau\,d\mathbf P_s/dt+\mathbf P_s=\epsilon_0\Delta\epsilon_r\mathbf E$, with a positive response time $\tau$. A harmonic [electric field](electric-field.md) then gives a contribution $\Delta\epsilon_r/(1+i\omega\tau)$ to [complex permittivity](complex-permittivity.md) divided by [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md). This model is an assumption, not a claim that all [dielectrics](dielectric.md) have one response time.
