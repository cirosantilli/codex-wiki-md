# Industrial platinum resistance thermometer

↑ **Parent:** [Platinum resistance thermometer](platinum-resistance-thermometer.md)

An [industrial platinum resistance thermometer](industrial-platinum-resistance-thermometer.md) is a [platinum resistance thermometer](platinum-resistance-thermometer.md) built for practical measurement conditions. The label [Pt100](pt100.md) specifies a nominal reference [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md), not an [SPRT](standard-platinum-resistance-thermometer.md), a calibration certificate, or a universal accuracy. Calibrate the probe together with its readout when the desired result is the performance of the complete indicating system.
