# Circular-binary orbital angular momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Binary star](binary-star.md)

For a [circular Kepler orbit](circular-kepler-orbit.md), neglecting stellar spin, the orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) of a [binary star](binary-star.md) is $J=\mu a^2\Omega=\mu\sqrt{GMa}$, where $M$ is total mass, $\mu$ is the [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md), and $a$ is separation. [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md) also gives $J=G^{2/3}P^{1/3}M_1M_2/[(2\pi)^{1/3}M^{1/3}]$. Distances from the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) are $aM_2/M$ and $aM_1/M$; adding their individual orbital [angular momenta](angular-momentum.md) proves the formula.
