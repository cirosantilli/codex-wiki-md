# Motional carrier transition

↑ **Parent:** [Motional sideband](motional-sideband.md)

A [motional carrier transition](motional-carrier-transition.md) changes an atom's internal state without changing its oscillator [quantum number](quantum-number.md). Its [matrix element](matrix-element.md) from the motional [ground state](ground-state.md) contains the factor $e^{-\eta^2/2}$. A first blue sideband is smaller in amplitude by $\eta$ and changes the motional [energy](energy-split.md) by one trap quantum. Conditional [energy](energy-split.md) changes must be distinguished from averages over all motional outcomes.
