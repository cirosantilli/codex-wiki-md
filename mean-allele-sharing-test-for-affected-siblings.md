# Mean allele-sharing test for affected siblings

↑ **Parent:** [Nonparametric linkage analysis](nonparametric-linkage-analysis.md)

For independent pairs of affected [full siblings](full-sibling.md) with unrelated, noninbred parents and a fully informative [genetic marker](genetic-marker.md), the no-linkage null gives shared [identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md) count $J\sim\operatorname{Bin}(2,1/2)$. Therefore $EJ=1$ and $\operatorname{Var}J=1/2$. An excess-sharing test uses the displayed approximately [standard normal](standard-normal-distribution.md) statistic and an upper-tail critical value. Pairs sharing family members are dependent, so their variance needs separate treatment. This targeted test differs from an omnibus test of all three sharing probabilities.
