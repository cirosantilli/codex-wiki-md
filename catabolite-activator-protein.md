# Catabolite activator protein

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

The [catabolite activator protein](catabolite-activator-protein.md) is a bacterial transcription regulator whose cAMP-dependent conformation supports DNA binding and contacts with RNA polymerase at suitable promoters. Promoter geometry determines which activating contacts are possible.
