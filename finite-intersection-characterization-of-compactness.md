# Finite intersection characterization of compactness

↑ **Parent:** [Compact space](compact-space.md)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is [compact](compact-space.md) exactly when every family of [closed sets](closed-set.md) with the [finite intersection property](finite-intersection-property.md) has nonempty [intersection](set-intersection.md). Take [complements](complement-of-a-set.md) and apply [De Morgan's laws](de-morgan-s-laws.md): an empty total [intersection](set-intersection.md) corresponds to an [open cover](open-cover.md), while an empty finite [intersection](set-intersection.md) corresponds to a [finite subcover](finite-subcover.md). This works without any [Hausdorff condition](hausdorff-space.md). With the convention that an empty-family [intersection](set-intersection.md) is the whole ambient [set](set-split.md), the [finite intersection property](finite-intersection-property.md) includes nonemptiness of that ambient [set](set-split.md), and the criterion correctly treats the empty [compact space](compact-space.md).
