# Single-channel conductance from open probability

↑ **Parent:** [Ion channel](ion-channel.md)

For N independent equivalent [ion channels](ion-channel.md) with open [probability](probability.md) $P_O$ and approximately Ohmic open-state [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) $\gamma$, the mean [electric current](electric-current.md) is $I=NP_O\gamma(V-E)$. A numerical [Electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) cannot be inferred without the relevant [reversal potential](reversal-potential.md) or driving voltage.
