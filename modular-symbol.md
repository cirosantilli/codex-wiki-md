# Modular symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Modular curve](modular-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_symbol)

A modular symbol is the [relative homology class](relative-homology-class.md) of a path between two [modular cusps](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md). Oriented paths satisfy $\{\alpha,\beta\}+\{\beta,\gamma\}=\{\alpha,\gamma\}$. [Continued fractions](continued-fraction.md) subdivide rational-endpoint paths into unimodular edges, giving a finite presentation through [Manin symbols](manin-symbol.md). The [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md) act by finite sums of transformed paths, so their matrices can be computed by rational linear algebra.
