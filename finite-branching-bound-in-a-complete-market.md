# Finite branching bound in a complete market

↑ **Parent:** [Complete market](complete-market.md)

With $n$ assets and a trivial initial sigma-field, [market completeness](complete-market.md) forces at most $n$ positive-probability successors per current atom. Indicators of disjoint successor events are independent payoffs, whereas terminal holdings on a parent atom span at most $n$ asset-price functions. Iterating yields at most $n^t$ atoms at time $t$.
