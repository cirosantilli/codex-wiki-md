# Orthogonality of proper-time deviation vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md)

For a [geodesic variation](geodesic-variation.md) of unit [timelike geodesics](timelike-geodesic.md), [proper time](proper-time.md) normalization holds across the entire variation: $g(T,T)=-1$. [Metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) and $\nabla_TS=\nabla_ST$ then give $d g(S,T)/d\tau=\tfrac12S(g(T,T))=0$. Consequently an initially orthogonal [deviation vector](deviation-vector.md) stays orthogonal. The common normalization is essential: an arbitrary family of affinely parametrized curves with different tangent norms does not imply this conclusion.
