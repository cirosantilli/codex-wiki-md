# Mayer-Vietoris sequence in algebraic K-theory

↑ **Parent:** [Milnor square](milnor-square.md)

The diagonal maps are [extension of scalars](extension-of-scalars.md); the maps to $A_0$ are differences. The connecting map sends $g$ to the difference of its patched [free module](free-module.md) and the corresponding free $A$-module. Elementary lifting and common free stabilization prove exactness at every internal term. The displayed fragment does not assert injectivity at its first term or surjectivity at its last.
