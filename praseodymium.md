# Praseodymium

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical element](chemical-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praseodymium)

A [chemical element](chemical-element.md) of [atomic number](atomic-number.md) 59. Its trivalent [ion](ion.md) has a $4f^2$ open-shell [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md). In [LS coupling](ls-coupling.md), [Hund's rules](hund-s-rules.md) select $L=5$, $S=1$, $J=4$, the ${}^3H_4$ [term symbol](term-symbol.md). The corresponding free-ion [Landé g-factor](lande-g-factor.md) is $4/5$ and its [effective paramagnetic moment](effective-paramagnetic-moment.md) is $(4/5)\sqrt{20}\,\mu_B\simeq3.58\mu_B$.
