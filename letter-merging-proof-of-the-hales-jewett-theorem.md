# Letter-merging proof of the Hales-Jewett theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hales-Jewett theorem](hales-jewett-theorem.md)

Induct on the alphabet size $k$. For an $r$-colouring, choose an $(k-1,k)$-insensitive [combinatorial subspace](combinatorial-subspace.md) whose dimension forces a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) [combinatorial line](combinatorial-line.md) over the first $k-1$ letters. The inductive [Hales-Jewett theorem](hales-jewett-theorem.md) supplies that line. Changing all of its active parameters from $k-1$ to $k$, one at a time, preserves the colour by the [alphabet insensitivity lemma](alphabet-insensitivity-lemma.md). Thus the line extends to all $k$ letters. The one-letter alphabet is the base case.
