# Baryta absorption method

↑ **Parent:** [Analytical chemistry](analytical-chemistry.md)

Determination of [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) by absorption into standardized [barium hydroxide](barium-hydroxide.md) and measurement of the remaining base. [Barium carbonate](barium-carbonate.md) must not be titrated as though it were residual hydroxide.
