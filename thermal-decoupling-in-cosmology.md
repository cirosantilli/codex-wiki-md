# Thermal decoupling in cosmology

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological entropy conservation](cosmological-entropy-conservation.md)

A particle species thermally decouples when its interaction rate falls below the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md). Its collisionless relativistic distribution subsequently redshifts with temperature proportional to $a^{-1}$, while annihilations among still-coupled species can heat the remaining plasma.
