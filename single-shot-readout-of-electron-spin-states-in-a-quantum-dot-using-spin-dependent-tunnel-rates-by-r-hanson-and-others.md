# Single-shot readout of electron spin states in a quantum dot using spin-dependent tunnel rates by R. Hanson and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by R. Hanson](work-by-r-hanson.md)

Physical Review Letters 94, 196802. [https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.94.196802](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.94.196802)

Primary preprint, submitted 31 December 2004: [https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0412768](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0412768)

Pages 1–2 describe energy-selective versus tunnel-rate-selective conversion and the two-electron orbital mechanism. Figures 1–2 show timing/readout; Fig. 4 examines nearly degenerate states. The 2004 preprint date differs from the 2005 journal year.
