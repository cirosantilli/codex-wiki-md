# On the Use of Trigonometric Parallaxes for the Calibration of Luminosity Systems: Theory by T. E. Lutz and D. H. Kelker

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by D. H. Kelker](work-by-d-h-kelker.md), [Work by T. E. Lutz](work-by-t-e-lutz.md)

[https://doi.org/10.1086/129506](https://doi.org/10.1086/129506)

PASP 85, 573–578. Primary classical treatment of parallax-based [luminosity](luminosity.md) [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) under a spatial-population model. Its assumptions do not justify a universal correction to every modern individual-star distance inference. Bibliographic details are also verified in the references of [An eclipsing-binary distance to the Large Magellanic Cloud accurate to two per cent by G. Pietrzyński and others](an-eclipsing-binary-distance-to-the-large-magellanic-cloud-accurate-to-two-per-cent-by-g-pietrzynski-and-others.md).
