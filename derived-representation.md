# Derived representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie-group representation](lie-group-representation.md)

The derived representation of a smooth Lie-group representation is $dD(X)=\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_{0}D(\exp(tX))$. It is a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) because $dD([X,Y])=[dD(X),dD(Y)]$.
