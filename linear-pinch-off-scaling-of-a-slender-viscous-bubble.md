# Linear pinch-off scaling of a slender viscous bubble

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous-capillary pinch-off similarity](viscous-capillary-pinch-off-similarity.md)

For $\tau=t_*-t$, balance $\mu a_t/a\sim\gamma/a$ to obtain radius proportional to $\tau$. Axial lubrication conservation then makes the axial length proportional to $\tau$ as well. The displayed variables reduce the [lubrication equation for a bubble with viscous exterior](lubrication-equation-for-a-bubble-with-viscous-exterior.md) to $\zeta AA'-A^2=\tfrac1{16}\{A^4[(2\zeta A'+1)/A]'\}'$. The physical interface slope is $\sqrt\lambda A'$, compatible with a slender neck when $\lambda\ll1$ and the dimensionless profile varies on order-one scales.
