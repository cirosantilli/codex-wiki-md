# Inverse marginal utility

↑ **Parent:** [Utility function](utility-function-split.md)

For an increasing differentiable strictly [concave](concave-function.md) [utility function](utility-function-split.md) satisfying the [Inada conditions](inada-conditions.md), the inverse marginal utility maps each positive shadow price to the unique positive payoff with that marginal utility. For normalized [CRRA utility](constant-relative-risk-aversion-utility.md), $I(y)=y^{-1/R}$.
