# Batchelor scalar microscale

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous-convective scalar range](viscous-convective-scalar-range.md)

At the Batchelor scalar microscale, molecular diffusion rate $\alpha/r^2$ balances the smooth Kolmogorov strain rate $(\epsilon/\nu)^{1/2}$. For $\alpha\ll\nu$ this scale is much smaller than the [Kolmogorov length scale](kolmogorov-length-scale.md).
