# Reduced scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced_scheme)

A scheme is reduced when every [local ring](local-ring.md) $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a [reduced ring](reduced-ring.md), equivalently when its structure sheaf has no nonzero [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md). The affine scheme $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is reduced exactly when $A$ is reduced.
