# Twofold elastic symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Independent elastic stiffness counts](independent-elastic-stiffness-counts.md)

A rotation through $\pi$ about the third coordinate axis preserves the [strain](strain.md) components $e_{11},e_{22},e_{33},e_{12}$ and reverses $e_{13},e_{23}$. A symmetry-invariant [elastic stiffness tensor](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md) cannot couple the two parity spaces. Its independent symmetric blocks therefore contain $4\cdot5/2+2\cdot3/2=13$ coefficients. A twofold axis alone does not impose invariance under every rotation about that axis.
