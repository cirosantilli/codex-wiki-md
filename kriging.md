# Kriging

↑ **Parent:** [Geostatistics](geostatistics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kriging)

[Kriging](kriging.md) predicts a spatial random field by a linear combination of observations chosen to minimize prediction [variance](variance-split.md) subject to the appropriate mean constraints. Its different forms depend on whether the mean is known, an unknown constant, or an unknown regression drift.
