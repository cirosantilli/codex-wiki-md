# Near point

↑ **Parent:** [Image formation](image-formation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near_point)

The [near point](near-point.md) is the closest position at which an eye can focus comfortably by accommodation. Its distance $D$ is measured from the eye and depends on the observer. The conventional textbook reference $D=250\,\mathrm{mm}$ is an assumption, not a universal anatomical constant. [Angular magnification](angular-magnification.md) of a [magnifying glass](magnifying-glass.md) is usually referred to unaided viewing of the same object at this distance.
