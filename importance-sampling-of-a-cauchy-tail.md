# Importance sampling of a Cauchy tail

↑ **Parent:** [Importance sampling](importance-sampling.md)

For $U$ uniform on $(0,1)$, $Y$ has density $q(y)=2y^{-2}$ on $y\ge2$. The [importance sampling](importance-sampling.md) weight for the standard [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) tail is $W=f(Y)/q(Y)$, and its expectation is the target tail [probability](probability.md). Writing $A=\arctan(1/2)$ gives $\mathbb EW=A/\pi$ and $\mathbb EW^2=(A+2/5)/(4\pi^2)$ by integrating $y^2/(1+y^2)^2$. The weight varies only between $2/(5\pi)$ and $1/(2\pi)$, making its [variance](variance-split.md) much smaller than that of a rare-event indicator. This is a comparison of sampling [variances](variance-split.md), not a deterministic ordering of every pair of empirical [variance](variance-split.md) estimates.
