# Acetonide

↑ **Parent:** [Acetal](acetal.md)

An [acetonide](acetonide.md) is an isopropylidene acetal formed from a diol and [acetone](acetone.md) or an [acetone](acetone.md) equivalent. Formation of its O–CMe2 bonds leaves the original stereogenic [carbon](carbon.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md) bonds intact.
