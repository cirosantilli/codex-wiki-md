# Robert Andrews Millikan

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Andrews_Millikan)

An experimental [physicist](physicist-split.md) known for measurements of [electron](electron.md) charge and the [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md). The [Millikan photoelectric experiment](millikan-photoelectric-experiment.md) describes the apparatus and stopping-voltage measurements behind his 1916 test.[Collection description: full name, 1868–1953 and experimental-physics work](robert-andrews-millikan-collection-by-national-archives-and-records-administration.md)
