<h1 id="odd-betti-numbers-of-a-compact-kahler-manifold-are-even">Odd Betti numbers of a compact Kähler manifold are even</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge decomposition theorem for compact Kähler manifolds](hodge-decomposition-theorem-for-compact-kahler-manifolds.md)

The [Kähler Laplacian identity](kahler-laplacian-identity.md) makes the space of complex [harmonic differential forms](harmonic-differential-form.md) of degree $r$ the direct sum of its $(p,q)$ parts with $p+q=r$. Conjugation identifies the $(p,q)$ and $(q,p)$ harmonic spaces. If $r$ is odd these are distinct and pair off, so the [Betti number](betti-number.md) in degree $r$ is even. This is a topological obstruction to existence of a [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md), exhibited by a [Hopf surface](hopf-surface.md) with first Betti number one.
