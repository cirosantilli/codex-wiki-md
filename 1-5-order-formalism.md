<h1 id="1-5-order-formalism">1.5-order formalism</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-Cartan theory](einstein-cartan-theory.md)

Solve the algebraic connection equation for $\widehat\omega(e,\chi)$, but perform the remaining variations as though the connection were independent. The chain-rule contribution $(\delta S/\delta\omega)\delta\widehat\omega$ vanishes on that solution. The resulting equations are those of the connection-eliminated second-order [action](action.md). This is especially useful for local [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) calculations, where the gravitino-induced contorsion is quadratic in [fermions](fermion.md) and the omitted induced connection variation would otherwise be lengthy.
