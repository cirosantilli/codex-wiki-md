# Cosmological texture

↑ **Parent:** [Topological defect](topological-defect.md)

An extended order-parameter winding associated with a nontrivial third [homotopy group](homotopy-group.md) of a [vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md) in three spatial dimensions. A texture can collapse and unwind when its field leaves the [vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md); it differs from a permanent monopole or string core.
