# Pochhammer contour

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pochhammer_contour)

A commutator contour around two branch points cancels the total [monodromy](monodromy.md) of its integrand. For the contour circling $1,0$ clockwise and then $1,0$ anticlockwise, with initial positive-real branches, the [Beta function](beta-function.md) obeys

$$
\int_Pt^{z-1}(1-t)^{b-1}\,dt=(1-e^{-2\pi iz})(1-e^{-2\pi ib})B(z,b).
$$

The contour integral is [entire](entire-function.md) in $z$, giving a [meromorphic continuation](meromorphic-continuation.md) of the [Beta function](beta-function.md).
