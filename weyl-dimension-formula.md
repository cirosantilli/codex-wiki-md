# Weyl dimension formula

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_dimension_formula)

The dimension obtained by evaluating the [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md) at the identity is

$$
\dim L_\lambda=\prod_{\alpha\in R^+}
\frac{\langle\lambda+\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}
{\langle\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}.
$$
