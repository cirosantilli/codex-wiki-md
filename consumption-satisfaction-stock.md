# Consumption satisfaction stock

↑ **Parent:** [Investment-consumption problem](investment-consumption-problem.md)

A [consumption satisfaction stock](consumption-satisfaction-stock.md) smooths past [consumption](consumption.md) by $\xi_t=e^{-\lambda t}\xi_0+\int_0^t e^{-\lambda(t-s)}c_sds$. Unlike [multiplicative habit utility](multiplicative-habit-utility.md), the running [utility function](utility-function-split.md) may depend on this stock alone. A unit of present [consumption](consumption.md) raises future [consumption satisfaction](consumption-satisfaction-stock.md) while costing one unit of financial [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md).
