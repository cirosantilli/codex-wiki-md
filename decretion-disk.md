# Decretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decretion_disk)

A decretion disk is fed near its inner edge and transports mass outward. Rapidly rotating Be stars can sustain gaseous decretion disks whose outer material is removed by a companion, radiation, or a wind.
