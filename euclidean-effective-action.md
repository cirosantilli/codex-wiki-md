# Euclidean effective action

↑ **Parent:** [Effective action](effective-action.md)

For $Z_E[J]=\int\mathcal D\phi\,e^{-S_E[\phi]+\int J\phi}$, define $W_E=\log Z_E$, $\varphi=\delta W_E/\delta J$ and the [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md) $\Gamma_E[\varphi]=\int J\varphi-W_E[J]$. Its functional derivatives generate Euclidean [one-particle irreducible](one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md) vertices. A bosonic [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) about a background gives the one-loop term $\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}\log S_E^{(2)}[\varphi]$. This is the Euclidean version of the [quantum effective action](effective-action.md), with signs fixed by $e^{-S_E}$ rather than $e^{iS}$.
