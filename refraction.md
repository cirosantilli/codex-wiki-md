# Refraction

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refraction)

Refraction changes the direction of a transmitted [wave](wave.md) when it crosses between media with different [phase velocities](phase-velocity.md). For [light](light.md) at a planar interface, [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) relates the angles measured from the [normal vector](normal-vector.md) through the [refractive indices](refractive-index.md). At normal incidence the direction remains unchanged even though the [wavelength](wavelength.md) and [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) can change. The worked example under [geometrical optics](geometrical-optics.md) distinguishes transmission from [total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md).
