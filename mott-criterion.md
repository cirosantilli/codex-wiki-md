# Mott criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Metal-insulator transition](metal-insulator-transition.md)

The approximate donor metal-insulator threshold is often written $n_c^{1/3}a_B^*\simeq0.25$. It estimates when overlap and screening destroy isolated donor [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md). The numerical coefficient is empirical and not a universal exact result; disorder, compensation and anisotropy can shift the threshold.[Abstract and effective-radius scaling](the-mott-criterion-so-simple-and-yet-so-complex-by-alexander-pergament-and-others.md)
