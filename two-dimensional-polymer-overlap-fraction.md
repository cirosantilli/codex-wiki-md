# Two-dimensional polymer overlap fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer overlap concentration](polymer-overlap-concentration.md)

For polymers restricted to an interface, overlap means roughly one coil per coil area, $n_cR^2\sim1$. A chain contains $N$ monomers with typical occupied area $\ell^2$, so its overlap area fraction is $\phi^*\sim N\ell^2/R^2$. With the planar good-solvent exponent $\nu=3/4$ from the [two-dimensional Flory approximation](two-dimensional-flory-approximation.md), this gives $\phi^*\sim N^{-1/2}$. The same exponent happens to occur for a three-dimensional ideal chain's volume fraction, but its geometric origin is different.
