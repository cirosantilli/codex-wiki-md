# piRNA cluster

↑ **Parent:** [Piwi-interacting RNA](piwi-interacting-rna.md)

A genomic source of [piRNA](piwi-interacting-rna.md) precursor transcripts, often containing fragments of [transposable elements](transposable-element.md). Cluster-derived guides can recognize related active elements elsewhere in the [genome](genome.md).
