# Fragment crystallizable region

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fragment_crystallizable_region)

The [Fc fragment](fragment-crystallizable-region.md) comprises paired [immunoglobulin heavy chain](immunoglobulin-heavy-chain.md) $C_{H2},C_{H3}$ portions, interacts with immune partners and lacks [paratopes](paratope.md). Its name records historical crystallization.[Immunobiology: The Immune System in Health and Disease, 5th edition by Charles A. Janeway, Jr. and others; §3-3: crystallization-based name, paired CH2 and CH3, and effector interactions](immunobiology-the-immune-system-in-health-and-disease-5th-edition-by-charles-a-janeway-jr-and-others/the-structure-of-a-typical-antibody-molecule.md)

For example, [Fc fragments](fragment-crystallizable-region.md) can bind components of the [complement system](complement-system.md), illustrating a function distinct from [epitope](epitope.md) recognition.[Antibody Structure and Molecular Immunology, printed p. 37: Fc binding to complement components](antibody-structure-and-molecular-immunology-by-gerald-m-edelman.md)

An isolated [Fc fragment](fragment-crystallizable-region.md) therefore separates one set of [protein](protein-split.md) interactions from target recognition. In the connectivity example under [Papain digestion of IgG](papain-digestion-of-igg.md), the [Fc fragment](fragment-crystallizable-region.md) contributes one connected product but zero [antibody binding sites](paratope.md). Retaining it preserves different interaction surfaces; it does not restore either severed arm. Conversely, the two [Fab fragments](fragment-antigen-binding.md) preserve the stipulated [epitope](epitope.md)-recognition sites while losing their connection to this common stem.
