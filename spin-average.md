# Spin average

↑ **Parent:** [Spin sum](spin-sum.md)

For an unpolarized initial state, average the squared [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) over its equally populated spin states. With two incoming spin-one-half particles this gives a factor $1/(2\cdot2)=1/4$. Unobserved final spins are summed rather than averaged.
