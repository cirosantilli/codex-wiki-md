# Binary quartic

↑ **Parent:** [Binary form](binary-form.md)

A [binary quartic](binary-quartic.md) is a homogeneous degree-four [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) in two variables. Over the complex numbers it determines four points of the [projective line](projective-line.md), counted with multiplicity. Its [discriminant](discriminant.md) is nonzero exactly when the four points are distinct; this is also its [geometric invariant theory](geometric-invariant-theory.md) stability condition under $SL_2$.
