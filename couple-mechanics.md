# Couple (mechanics)

↑ **Parent:** [Torque](torque.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Couple_(mechanics))

A pair of equal and opposite [forces](force.md) acting along distinct lines has zero resultant [force](force.md) but a nonzero [torque](torque.md). If $\mathbf F$ acts at $\mathbf x_1$ and $-\mathbf F$ at $\mathbf x_2$, the total [torque](torque.md) about any origin is $(\mathbf x_1-\mathbf x_2)\times\mathbf F$. Shifting that origin changes the moment by the [cross product](cross-product.md) of the shift with the resultant [force](force.md), which is zero. A prescribed [couple](couple-mechanics.md) therefore specifies a pure turning moment independently of its reference point. In [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) it can drive a [rotating sphere in Stokes flow](rotating-sphere-in-stokes-flow.md) without a directly imposed translational [force](force.md).
