# Uniformly integrable martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time martingale](continuous-time-martingale.md)

A [martingale](martingale-split.md) whose values over its whole time interval form a [uniformly integrable](uniform-integrability.md) family has an integrable terminal limit and is closed by that limit. Conversely, conditional expectations of an integrable terminal variable form a [uniformly integrable martingale](uniformly-integrable-martingale.md), by [uniform integrability of conditional expectations](uniform-integrability-of-conditional-expectations.md). For [continuous martingales](continuous-martingale.md), one may equivalently use [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md) of the family stopped at finite stopping times.
