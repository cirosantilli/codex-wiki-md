# Affineness is invariant under nilpotent thickening

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent thickening](nilpotent-thickening.md)

Let $X_0\hookrightarrow X$ be a [nilpotent thickening](nilpotent-thickening.md) defined by $\mathcal I^m=0$. If $X$ is an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md), its [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md) $X_0$ is an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md). Conversely, suppose $X_0$ is an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md). The underlying [topological spaces](topological-space.md) are identical, so $X$ is a [quasi-compact scheme](quasi-compact-scheme.md). For any [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$, each subquotient $\mathcal I^j\mathcal F/\mathcal I^{j+1}\mathcal F$ is naturally a [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) on $X_0$. By [sheaf cohomology under a closed inclusion](sheaf-cohomology-under-a-closed-inclusion.md) and [vanishing of quasi-coherent cohomology on an affine scheme](vanishing-of-quasi-coherent-cohomology-on-an-affine-scheme.md), its first [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) on $X$ vanishes. The [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) and descending [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) through this finite filtration prove $H^1(X,\mathcal F)=0$. The [Serre criterion for affineness](cohomological-criterion-for-affineness.md) now makes $X$ an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md).
