# Residual-versus-fitted plot

↑ **Parent:** [Linear regression](linear-regression-split.md)

A [residual-versus-fitted plot](residual-versus-fitted-plot.md) displays residuals vertically against predicted responses horizontally. Curvature suggests an incorrect conditional-mean model; a fan-shaped spread suggests [heteroscedasticity](heteroscedastic.md), while isolated large residuals may indicate unusual observations. It does not alone measure leverage or influence: [regression leverage](regression-leverage.md) records unusual predictor combinations, and [Cook's distance](cook-s-distance.md) combines leverage and residual size.
