# Prime ideals of the integer polynomial ring above zero

↑ **Parent:** [Prime ideal](prime-ideal.md)

The [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md) $P$ of $\mathbb Z[t]$ with $P\cap\mathbb Z=\{0\}$ are the zero [ideal](ideal.md) and the [principal ideals](principal-ideal.md) $(f)$ generated by nonconstant [primitive polynomials](primitive-polynomial.md) that are irreducible in $\mathbb Q[t]$.

To prove the description, invert the nonzero [integers](integer-split.md) to obtain the [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md) $\mathbb Q[t]$. The [extension of an ideal](extension-of-an-ideal.md) $P$ consists of the [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) $f/n$ with $f\in P$ and $n>0$. Its contraction is $P$: if $nh\in P$ and $n\ne0$, the defining condition puts $n\notin P$, so primeness gives $h\in P$. The [prime ideal correspondence for localization](prime-ideal-correspondence-for-localization.md) makes this extension a proper [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) of $\mathbb Q[t]$.

If $P\ne0$, its extension has a nonzero generator because $\mathbb Q[t]$ is a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md). Dividing this generator by its [rational polynomial content](rational-polynomial-content.md) gives an integer-coefficient [primitive polynomial](primitive-polynomial.md) $f$ generating the same extended [ideal](ideal.md). Divisibility descent by [Gauss lemma for polynomials](gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md) then gives $P=(f)$ in $\mathbb Z[t]$. The proper prime [ideal](ideal.md) $(f)$ in $\mathbb Q[t]$ makes $f$ irreducible there. Conversely, any such [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) generates a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) in $\mathbb Q[t]$ whose contraction is $(f)\subset\mathbb Z[t]$ and whose intersection with $\mathbb Z$ is zero.

The intersection condition is essential: $(2,t)$ is a [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) of $\mathbb Z[t]$, since its [quotient ring](quotient-ring.md) is $\mathbb F_2$, but it is not principal. A common divisor of $2$ and $t$ must be a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md), which would generate the whole [ring](ring.md).
