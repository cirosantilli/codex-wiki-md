# Propagation-source ambiguity in an acoustic analogy

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic analogy](acoustic-analogy.md)

The distinction between generation and propagation depends partly on the reference operator chosen in an [acoustic analogy](acoustic-analogy.md). For example, a homogeneous-Laplacian operator with spatially varying reference sound speed omits the first-gradient terms of the [stratified acoustic pressure equation](stratified-acoustic-pressure-equation.md). These terms then reappear as effective forcing, even for a freely propagating small disturbance in a static medium. A right-hand-side term is therefore not automatically an independent physical noise source.
