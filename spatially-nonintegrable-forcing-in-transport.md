# Spatially nonintegrable forcing in transport

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-time Lp bound for Hamiltonian transport](finite-time-lp-bound-for-hamiltonian-transport.md)

Smooth forcing need not be integrable in phase space. For the [hyperbolic characteristic flow for an inverted oscillator](hyperbolic-characteristic-flow-for-an-inverted-oscillator.md), the source $x^2+v^2$ accumulates as $q_t=\tfrac12\sinh(2t)(x^2+v^2)-(\cosh(2t)-1)xv$. Its smallest quadratic-form eigenvalue is $(1-e^{-2t})/2>0$ for $t>0$. Starting with a nonnegative integrable [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md), the solution is therefore unbounded and outside every finite [Lp space](lp-space.md) at positive times. Locally time-integrable $L^p$ forcing prevents this failure by the [Minkowski integral inequality](minkowski-integral-inequality.md).
