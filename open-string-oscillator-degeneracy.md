# Open-string oscillator degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Open string](open-string.md)

For an ordinary critical [open string](open-string.md) with all [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md), there are 24 independent transverse creation oscillators at each positive mode number $n$. Summing their nonnegative occupation numbers gives one factor $(1-w^n)^{-24}$. The coefficient $d_p=[w^p]F(w)$ counts oscillator polarizations at level $p$, excluding continuous center-of-mass momentum and additional endpoint multiplicities. [Cauchy coefficient formula](cauchy-coefficient-formula.md) gives $d_p=(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint F(w)w^{-p-1}dw$.
