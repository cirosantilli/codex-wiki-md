# Simple kriging

↑ **Parent:** [Kriging](kriging.md)

For known zero mean, invertible observation [covariance](covariance.md) $\Sigma$, target [covariance](covariance.md) vector $c$ and target [variance](variance-split.md) $v_0$, simple kriging predicts $c^T\Sigma^{-1}z$ with error [variance](variance-split.md) $v_0-c^T\Sigma^{-1}c$. Completing the [covariance](covariance.md) quadratic form proves optimality. A [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) makes this the exact conditional mean and [variance](variance-split.md).
