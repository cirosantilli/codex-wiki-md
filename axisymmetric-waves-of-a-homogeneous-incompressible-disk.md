# Axisymmetric waves of a homogeneous incompressible disk

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md)

In a thin constant-density disk with [free surfaces](free-surface.md) $z=\pm H$, neglect slow radial gradients and let [perturbations](perturbation.md) vary as $e^{ikr-i\omega t}$. With $h=p'/\rho$, the local inviscid equations are $-i\omega u-2\Omega v=-ikh$, $-i\omega v+\kappa^2u/(2\Omega)=0$, $-i\omega w=-h_z$ and $iku+w_z=0$. Eliminating [velocity](velocity.md) gives $h_{zz}=k^2\omega^2h/(\omega^2-\kappa^2)$. This local [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) assumes $H/r\ll1$ and $|kr|\gg1$ and has both surface and [inertial wave](inertial-wave.md) branches.
