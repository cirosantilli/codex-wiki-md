# Vanishing-penalty ridge limit

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-form ridge regression estimator](closed-form-ridge-regression-estimator.md)

In an eigenbasis of $G=X^TX$, [ridge regression](ridge-regression.md) divides the corresponding component of $X^TY$ by $\gamma+\lambda$. If $\gamma=0$, its [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) $v$ satisfies $Xv=0$, and hence $v^TX^TY=0$ exactly. Only positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) contribute to the limit, giving the [Moore-Penrose inverse](moore-penrose-inverse.md) expression for the [minimum-norm least-squares solution](minimum-norm-least-squares-solution.md). No full-rank or sample-size assumption is needed.
