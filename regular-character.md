# Regular character

↑ **Parent:** [Regular representation](regular-representation.md)

The [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of the [regular representation](regular-representation.md) of a finite [group](group-split.md) is $|G|$ at the identity and zero elsewhere. Left multiplication permutes its group-indexed [basis](basis.md), and a nonidentity element fixes none of those [basis vectors](basis-vector.md). Thus each complex [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) occurs with [representation multiplicity](multiplicity-of-an-irreducible-representation.md) equal to its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md), by the [character inner product](character-inner-product.md).
