# Tensor product of crystals

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal basis](crystal-basis.md)

The tensor product crystal uses the $i$-string data $\varepsilon_i$ and $\varphi_i$ to decide on which tensor factor a [Kashiwara operator](kashiwara-operator.md) acts. Its connected components are highest-weight crystals and encode the decomposition of the tensor product representation.

This tensor-product operation combines one [crystal basis](crystal-basis.md) with another, with factor selection governed by their string parameters.
