# Bounded arithmetic sentence

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic bounded formula](arithmetic-bounded-formula.md)

A [bounded arithmetic sentence](bounded-arithmetic-sentence.md) is an [arithmetic bounded formula](arithmetic-bounded-formula.md) with no [free variables](free-variable.md). Every [quantifier](quantifier.md) is restricted by an [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) [first-order term](first-order-term.md). In the standard [natural numbers](natural-number.md) such a [first-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md) has a definite [truth value](truth-value.md) determined by finite bounded searches.
