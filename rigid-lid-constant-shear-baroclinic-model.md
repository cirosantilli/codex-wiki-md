# Rigid-lid constant-shear baroclinic model

↑ **Parent:** [Baroclinic instability](baroclinic-instability.md)

For a uniform buoyancy frequency, constant Coriolis parameter and zonal shear $U=\Lambda z$ between rigid lids separated by $H$, a zero-interior-PV normal mode has $\Psi''-K^2\Psi=0$. Adiabatic no-normal-flow conditions give $c=\Lambda H/2\pm\Lambda H\sqrt{D(\eta)}/\eta$, $\eta=KH$. Growth requires $D<0$, or a negative squared phase speed relative to the mean flow, rather than a literal $c^2<0$ in an arbitrary moving frame. The cutoff is $\eta_c\simeq2.39936$; for zero meridional wavenumber maximum growth occurs at $kL_D\simeq1.60612$, with rate $0.30982\Lambda f_0/N$.
