# Infrared regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Infrared divergence](infrared-divergence.md)

An infrared regulator suppresses the small-[momentum](momentum-split.md) region responsible for an [infrared divergence](infrared-divergence.md). Examples are a small positive [mass](mass.md) or a finite volume. It must be distinguished from an [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md): setting external [momenta](momentum-split.md) and [masses](mass.md) to zero to extract an [ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md) can introduce a spurious [infrared divergence](infrared-divergence.md). Keep a positive auxiliary [mass](mass.md) until the divergent local coefficients have been separated, then take the desired limit in infrared-safe quantities.
