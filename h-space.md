# H-space

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy category](homotopy-category.md)

A based [topological space](topological-space.md) with a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $m$ whose left and right restrictions at the basepoint are homotopic to the identity. Associativity and inverses up to [homotopy](homotopy.md) are additional conditions, not part of the bare definition.
