# Fixed-target threshold for production of equal-mass particles

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md)

An incident particle of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $m$ and energy $E$ striking a stationary identical target has total squared invariant energy $W^2=2mc^2(E+mc^2)$. Producing $N\geq2$ final particles of the same rest mass requires $W\geq Nmc^2$, giving the threshold. Equality means no final kinetic energy in the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md). From the [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md), the corresponding incident speed is at least $cN\sqrt{N^2-4}/(N^2-2)$.
