# Determinant representation

↑ **Parent:** [One-dimensional representation](one-dimensional-representation.md)

The top [exterior power](exterior-power.md) of an $n$-dimensional [group representation](group-representation.md) is one-dimensional, and $g$ acts on it by $\det R(g)$. It is trivial for the defining [representation](group-representation.md) of $SU(n)$. This is why a full $n$-box column in an $SU(n)$ [Young diagram](young-diagram.md) can be removed without changing the [representation](group-representation.md).
