# Orthonormal sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md)

An orthonormal sequence $(e_n)$ satisfies $\langle e_n,e_m\rangle=0$ for $n\ne m$ and $\lVert e_n\rVert=1$.

This is the sequential form of [orthonormality](orthonormal-set.md).
