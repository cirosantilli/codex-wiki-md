# Band hybridization

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md)

[Band hybridization](band-hybridization.md) mixes two orbital-derived bands through an off-diagonal [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) matrix element. For a real coupling $\alpha$,

$$
H(k)=\begin{pmatrix}E_a(k)&\alpha\\\alpha&E_b(k)\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E_\pm(k)=\frac{E_a+E_b\pm\sqrt{(E_a-E_b)^2+4\alpha^2}}2.
$$

An allowed bare crossing becomes an [avoided crossing](avoided-crossing.md) with direct separation $2|\alpha|$. Orbital character transfers continuously between branches through the crossing. A direct separation alone does not guarantee a global [band gap](band-gap.md); the extrema at different wavevectors must also be compared.
