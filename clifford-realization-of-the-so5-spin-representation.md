# Clifford realization of the so5 spin representation

↑ **Parent:** [Spin representation](spin-representation.md)

Choose five [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md) four-by-four [matrices](matrix.md) obeying the Euclidean [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) relations $\gamma_a\gamma_b+\gamma_b\gamma_a=2\delta_{ab}I$. For $M_{ab}=E_{ab}-E_{ba}$, the displayed [anti-Hermitian matrices](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) satisfy the [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md) [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) relations. A direct proof first establishes $[\rho(M_{ab}),\gamma_c]=\delta_{bc}\gamma_a-\delta_{ac}\gamma_b$, then applies the [commutator product rule](commutator-product-rule.md) to $[\gamma_c,\gamma_d]$. One explicit choice is $\gamma_1=\sigma_1\otimes I$, $\gamma_2=\sigma_2\otimes I$, $\gamma_3=\sigma_3\otimes\sigma_1$, $\gamma_4=\sigma_3\otimes\sigma_2$, $\gamma_5=\sigma_3\otimes\sigma_3$, with the [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md). The [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) operators $-i\rho(M_{12})$ and $-i\rho(M_{34})$ have simultaneous [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $(\pm1/2,\pm1/2)$, identifying the four-dimensional [spinor representation](spin-representation.md). A full $2\pi$ rotation acts as $-I_4$, proving it does not descend to $SO(5)$.
