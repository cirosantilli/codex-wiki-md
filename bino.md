# Bino

↑ **Parent:** [Gaugino](gaugino.md)

The [bino](bino.md) is the spin-one-half [gaugino](gaugino.md) of the [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) $U(1)_Y$ [gauge group](gauge-group.md). Its adjoint representation is the singlet $(\mathbf1,\mathbf1,0)$, so it has no hypercharge of its own. After [electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md), it can mix with the neutral [wino](wino.md) and [higgsinos](higgsino.md) into [neutralinos](neutralino.md).
