<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[cosmic inflation](../../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) is an expanding epoch with $\ddot a>0$, equivalently a shrinking [comoving Hubble radius](../../../../../comoving-hubble-radius.md) $(aH)^{-1}=1/\dot a$. In [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md) it requires $\rho+3P/c^2<0$ for the total effective fluid. A [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) whose [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) dominates its [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) has $P\simeq-\rho c^2$ and can produce approximately exponential expansion. [slow-roll inflation](../../../../../slow-roll-approximation.md) is one realization; rapid expansion alone without acceleration does not have the required effect.

The [horizon problem](../../../../../horizon-problem.md) is that widely separated regions of the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) have nearly the same [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) despite lying outside one another's causal domains in a purely decelerating hot [Big Bang](../../../../../big-bang.md) extrapolation. Sufficient inflation stretches a previously small causally connected region across our observable universe; relevant perturbation [wavelengths](../../../../../wavelength.md) can begin inside the [Hubble radius](../../../../../hubble-radius.md), leave it during inflation and re-enter later. This does not require local signals to outrun [light](../../../../../light.md). Inflation supplies a possible earlier causal history, not a proof that every possible initial state becomes homogeneous.

For the [flatness problem](../../../../../flatness-problem.md), the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $\Omega_{\rm tot}-1=kc^2/(aH)^2$ when $a$ is length-valued. Since $aH$ grows during inflation,

$$
\boxed{|\Omega_{\rm tot}-1|\ \hbox{decreases during inflation}}.
$$

Pre-existing unwanted massive relics such as [magnetic monopoles](../../../../../magnetic-monopole.md) are also diluted: conserved [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) falls as $a^{-3}$. Enough inflation, followed by [reheating](../../../../../reheating.md) that does not recreate the relic problem, can make their abundance negligible. These were central motivations of the original proposal, whose first phase-transition implementation also faced a graceful-exit problem.[Abstract: horizon, flatness, monopole suppression and limitations of the original scenario](../../../../../inflationary-universe-a-possible-solution-to-the-horizon-and-flatness-problems-by-alan-h-guth.md)

[quantum fluctuations](../../../../../quantum-fluctuation.md) of the inflating field and metric supply seeds for [linear cosmological density perturbations](../../../../../linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md). Simple single-field slow-roll models predict a dominant [adiabatic mode](../../../../../adiabatic-mode.md) with nearly Gaussian, nearly scale-invariant [scalar cosmological perturbations](../../../../../scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md), consistent with the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md); [tensor cosmological perturbations](../../../../../tensor-cosmological-perturbation.md) are another possible test. These predictions are model-dependent, and consistency is not unique proof of inflation. [reheating](../../../../../reheating.md) must connect the accelerated phase to the hot expanding universe. **Inflation addresses causal reach, small [cosmological spatial curvature](../../../../../spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) and unwanted relic abundance, while providing a mechanism for primordial structure.**
