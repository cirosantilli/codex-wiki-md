<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a close [binary star](../../../../../binary-star.md), the rotating effective gravitational potential defines two [Roche lobes](../../../../../roche-lobe.md) meeting at the [inner Lagrange point](../../../../../inner-lagrange-point.md). Stellar expansion or orbital shrinkage can bring the donor into [Roche-lobe overflow](../../../../../roche-lobe-overflow.md). Gas then flows through the [inner Lagrange point](../../../../../inner-lagrange-point.md); its [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) can form an [accretion disk](../../../../../accretion-disk.md) around the companion. [wind mass transfer in a binary star](../../../../../wind-mass-transfer-in-a-binary-star.md) is also possible without Roche-lobe filling. For a circular, synchronized binary, the [Eggleton Roche-lobe radius approximation](../../../../../eggleton-roche-lobe-radius-approximation.md) is

$$
\frac{R_L}{a}\simeq\frac{0.49q^{2/3}}{0.6q^{2/3}+\ln(1+q^{1/3})},\qquad q=\frac{M_d}{M_a}.
$$

This describes the lobe geometry, not the donor's dynamical response.[Printed p. 368, equation 2](../../../../../approximations-to-the-radii-of-roche-lobes-by-peter-p-eggleton.md)

[conservative binary mass transfer](../../../../../conservative-binary-mass-transfer.md) has fixed total [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md), neglecting stellar spin. Using $J=M_dM_a\sqrt{Ga/(M_d+M_a)}$ gives

$$
\frac{\dot a}{a}=-2\dot M_d\left(\frac1{M_d}-\frac1{M_a}\right).
$$

Since $\dot M_d<0$, transfer from the more massive donor initially shrinks the orbit; after [binary mass ratio](../../../../../binary-mass-ratio.md) reversal it expands. Stability depends on both stars and their changing lobes. Let $\zeta_*=d\ln R_d/d\ln M_d$ and $\zeta_L=d\ln R_L/d\ln M_d$. A small [mass](../../../../../mass.md) loss reduces the relative overfill if **$\zeta_*>\zeta_L$**. Use the adiabatic donor response for dynamical stability, and its thermal response for slower adjustment; an equilibrium mass-radius law alone cannot decide both.

Stable transfer can proceed on nuclear or thermal timescales, while dynamically unstable overflow may create a [common envelope](../../../../../common-envelope.md), orbital spiral-in and either envelope ejection or a merger. Escaping gas, [gravitational radiation](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) and [magnetic braking](../../../../../magnetic-braking.md) make evolution nonconservative by changing [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md). [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) onto a [compact stellar object](../../../../../compact-stellar-object.md) releases [power](../../../../../power.md) of order $GM_a\dot M/R_a$, producing bright [X-ray binaries](../../../../../x-ray-binary.md); radiation-driven outflows can limit retained [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Eclipses, [radial velocities](../../../../../radial-velocity.md) and disk emission constrain the geometry and [masses](../../../../../mass.md), but inclination, irradiation and assumptions about [mass](../../../../../mass.md) or angular-momentum loss affect the inference. **The essential feedback is the donor's radius relative to its evolving [Roche lobe](../../../../../roche-lobe.md).**
