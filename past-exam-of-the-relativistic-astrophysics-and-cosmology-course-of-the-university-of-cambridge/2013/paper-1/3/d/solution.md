<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) onto a massive [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) releases [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) $L\simeq\eta\dot M c^2$. Even a small coupling of this [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to surrounding gas can matter because a galactic binding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is only of order $M_{\rm gas}\sigma^2$, with stellar [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) dispersion $\sigma\ll c$. [active-galactic-nucleus feedback](../../../../../active-galactic-nucleus-feedback.md) means that the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) or [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) changes the gas supply, cooling or star formation, potentially regulating further [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md). Large [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) availability alone does not establish efficient coupling.

In a radiatively efficient bright nucleus, [radiation pressure](../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) and winds can push on dusty or ionized gas. Outflows can remove fuel or shock-heat it; the outcome depends on cooling, covering factor, clumping and whether the flow escapes. In a mechanically dominated state, [relativistic jets](../../../../../relativistic-jet.md) inflate radio cavities in a hot halo or [galaxy cluster](../../../../../galaxy-cluster.md). Their expansion, shocks, [sound waves](../../../../../acoustic-wave.md) and subsequent motions can distribute [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and suppress a [cooling flow](../../../../../cooling-flow.md). The two descriptions identify useful channels rather than a universal sharp division between all nuclei.

An approximately pressure-balanced cavity containing a relativistic fluid has [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md)

$$
E_{\rm cav}=\frac{\Gamma}{\Gamma-1}pV\simeq4pV\quad(\Gamma=4/3),\qquad
P_{\rm cav}\sim\frac{4pV}{t_{\rm age}}.
$$

[X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) estimates, cavity volume and buoyant or sound-crossing ages permit comparison with radiative cooling losses. Radio images check the jet association; [X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) fronts and ripples help locate transferred [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Deep Chandra observations of Perseus reveal such structures and motivate mechanical heating of the cooling atmosphere.[Abstract and discussion of radio-bubble-associated fronts, ripples and heating](../../../../../a-deep-chandra-observation-of-the-perseus-cluster-shocks-and-ripples-by-a-c-fabian-and-others.md)

Projection, nonrelativistic cavity contents, [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) gradients, uncertain ages and intermittent duty cycles affect the [power](../../../../../power.md) estimate. Injected cavity [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) need not all dissipate where cooling occurs. A successful negative feedback loop requires that falling gas increases [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md), heating then reduces cooling or inflow, and the delay does not destroy regulation. This can explain reduced cooling and the shortage of excessively massive star-forming galaxies; correlations between black-hole [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and bulge properties are supporting context rather than standalone proof of causation. **The key comparison is coupled heating versus local cooling and binding, including where and when [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is deposited.**
