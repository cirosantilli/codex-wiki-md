<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [galaxy cluster](../../../../../galaxy-cluster.md) deflects background [light](../../../../../light.md) through its projected gravitational field. In the [thin-lens approximation for gravitational lensing](../../../../../thin-lens-approximation-for-gravitational-lensing.md), $\boldsymbol\beta=\boldsymbol\theta-\boldsymbol\alpha(\boldsymbol\theta)$ maps image to source angle. Its [surface mass density](../../../../../projected-surface-mass-density.md) enters through

$$
\kappa=\frac{\Sigma}{\Sigma_{\rm crit}},\qquad
\Sigma_{\rm crit}=\frac{c^2D_s}{4\pi GD_lD_{ls}},\qquad
\mu=\frac1{(1-\kappa)^2-|\gamma_s|^2}.
$$

Here $\mu$ is signed [lensing magnification](../../../../../lensing-magnification.md); observed brightness uses $|\mu|$. The $D$'s are [angular diameter distances](../../../../../angular-diameter-distance.md) to lens, source and between them; $\gamma_s$ denotes [lensing shear](../../../../../lensing-shear.md), not a [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md). [strong gravitational lensing](../../../../../strong-gravitational-lensing.md) creates arcs and multiple images near critical curves. For an axisymmetric lens, a tangential [Einstein ring](../../../../../einstein-ring.md) satisfies mean enclosed convergence one, giving

$$
\boxed{M_{\rm proj}(<D_l\theta_E)=\pi(D_l\theta_E)^2\Sigma_{\rm crit}}.
$$

This is projected enclosed [mass](../../../../../mass.md), not a general three-dimensional [mass](../../../../../mass.md) profile inferred from a single arc.

[weak gravitational lensing](../../../../../weak-gravitational-lensing.md) uses coherent distortions of many background galaxies. Their intrinsic orientations average approximately to zero, allowing an estimate of [reduced shear](../../../../../reduced-shear.md) $g=\gamma_s/(1-\kappa)$ and a projected [mass](../../../../../mass.md) map. The method traces all gravitating material without assuming [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), which is valuable in merging clusters. Accurate work requires correction of the [point spread function](../../../../../point-spread-function-split.md), calibrated shape measurement, source [redshifts](../../../../../redshift.md), foreground-member rejection and selection effects. Intrinsic shape noise, unrelated line-of-sight structures and lens-model degeneracies remain. The [mass-sheet degeneracy](../../../../../mass-sheet-degeneracy.md) leaves [reduced shear](../../../../../reduced-shear.md) unchanged under $\kappa\mapsto\lambda\kappa+(1-\lambda)$ and $\gamma_s\mapsto\lambda\gamma_s$, so absolute [mass](../../../../../mass.md) normalization needs additional information.

In the [Bullet Cluster](../../../../../bullet-cluster.md), [weak gravitational lensing](../../../../../weak-gravitational-lensing.md) places the main projected [mass](../../../../../mass.md) peaks near the collisionless galaxies, displaced from the X-ray-emitting gas that contains most observed [baryon](../../../../../baryon.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Comparing the lensing, optical and [X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) maps supports a dominant weakly collisional dark component under the usual gravity model; lensing alone does not identify its particle species. **Cluster lensing measures projected gravitational [mass](../../../../../mass.md) independently of gas [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) or dynamical equilibrium.**[Abstract and spatial comparison of weak-lensing mass, galaxies and X-ray gas](../../../../../a-direct-empirical-proof-of-the-existence-of-dark-matter-by-douglas-clowe-and-others.md)
