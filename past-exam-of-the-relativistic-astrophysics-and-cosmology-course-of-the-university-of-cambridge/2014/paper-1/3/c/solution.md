<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Without feedback, efficiently cooling gas would turn into stars too readily. Young stars return radiation, winds and [supernova](../../../../../supernova.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) to their surroundings. This can heat gas, disrupt dense clouds and drive outflows, with the strongest fractional effect in shallow potential wells. The outflow [mass-loading factor](../../../../../mass-loading-factor.md) $\eta_w=\dot M_{\rm out}/\dot M_*$ is useful, but its dependence on escape speed differs between energy-limited and momentum-limited models. Some expelled gas later recycles; feedback also transports metals and changes subsequent cooling.

[active-galactic-nucleus feedback](../../../../../active-galactic-nucleus-feedback.md) is important in massive galaxies and hot haloes. Radiative or mechanical power from [black-hole accretion](../../../../../black-hole-accretion.md) can suppress the supply of cold gas. Collimated jets inflate radio/X-ray cavities, while broader winds can carry significant [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) and kinetic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). For a relativistic cavity fluid with adiabatic index $4/3$, its [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) is approximately $4pV$, so $4pV/t_{\rm age}$ estimates mechanical power. Comparing it with the gas cooling luminosity tests whether that [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) budget is sufficient, rather than merely showing that a radio source is present.

Deep Chandra observations of Perseus resolved bubbles, a density jump and weak surface-brightness ripples consistent with repeated mechanical disturbance of the intracluster gas. Their interpretation as heating requires a dissipation mechanism and a coupling history, not just a large instantaneous power.[abstract; density front, radio bubbles and the proposed heating/cooling balance](../../../../../a-deep-chandra-observation-of-the-perseus-cluster-shocks-and-ripples-by-a-c-fabian-and-others.md)

Observed outflow geometry, filling factors, cavity ages and cooling corrections create substantial uncertainties. Feedback can also compress gas and locally promote [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md). Simulations must track radiative losses and the scale on which [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) or [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) couples: depositing unresolved thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is not automatically equivalent to driving a resolved wind. A successful model should explain stellar [masses](../../../../../mass.md), gas fractions, [metallicity](../../../../../metallicity.md) and circumgalactic or intracluster thermodynamics together.
