<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [active galactic nucleus](../../../../../active-galactic-nucleus.md) is powered by [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) onto a central [supermassive black hole](../../../../../supermassive-black-hole.md). Binding-energy release gives $L\sim\eta\dot Mc^2$, so a compact source can outshine its host without converting its entire [mass](../../../../../mass.md) into radiation. A disc produces a thermal continuum, a hot corona can Comptonize disc [photons](../../../../../photon.md) into X-rays, and surrounding gas produces broad and narrow [emission lines](../../../../../emission-line.md). Dust can obscure and reradiate the central emission. Orientation explains part of the diversity between unobscured and obscured nuclei, but [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) state and intrinsic variability also matter.

[relativistic jets](../../../../../relativistic-jet.md) channel [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) into collimated magnetized outflows. Disc [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) and, in some models, black-hole [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) supply the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md); magnetic stresses accelerate the plasma and connect the engine to the outflow. [synchrotron radiation](../../../../../synchrotron-radiation.md) [traces](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) relativistic [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), while [inverse Compton scattering](../../../../../inverse-compton-scattering.md) can produce higher-energy emission. Bright knots, terminal hotspots and large radio lobes show that the deposited [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is not confined to the unresolved nucleus.

For bulk speed $\beta c$ at viewing angle $i$, the [Doppler factor](../../../../../doppler-factor.md) is $\mathcal D=[\Gamma(1-\beta\cos i)]^{-1}$, and an approaching feature has

$$
\boxed{\beta_{\rm app}=\frac{\beta\sin i}{1-\beta\cos i}.}
$$

Light-travel delay can therefore give [apparent superluminal motion](../../../../../apparent-superluminal-motion.md) without any material or [signal](../../../../../signal.md) travelling locally faster than light. Beaming also makes approaching jets appear brighter and more rapidly variable; a one-sided image is not proof of a physically one-sided outflow. Spectral, [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) and resolved-motion measurements constrain the geometry and emission mechanisms. Jets can inflate cavities and heat surrounding gas, linking [black-hole accretion](../../../../../black-hole-accretion.md) to galaxy and cluster feedback.
