[core-collapse supernovae](../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) follow loss of pressure support in the evolved core of a massive star. [electron](../../../../../electron.md) capture and nuclear dissociation contribute to collapse; formation of a hot compact object launches a [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md) that can stall as dissociation and [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) losses drain it. [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) heating and multidimensional fluid motions can revive the [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md), but some collapses fail to produce a normal bright explosion. The remnant may be a [neutron star](../../../../../neutron-star.md) or [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md). The [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) burst detected from [SN 1987A](../../../../../sn-1987a.md) provides direct evidence for the core-collapse [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) channel: the water-Cherenkov [signal](../../../../../signal.md) is a particle-interaction measurement, requiring detector-response and accidental-background checks rather than an optical light-curve inference.[pp.1490–1493, burst observation and detector interpretation](../../../../../observation-of-a-neutrino-burst-from-the-supernova-sn1987a-by-k-hirata-and-others.md)

[Type Ia supernovae](../../../../../type-ia-supernova.md) instead involve thermonuclear destruction of a degenerate [carbon-oxygen white dwarf](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-white-dwarf.md). They leave expanding ejecta without an ordinary surviving neutron-star core. The ignition channel need not have a unique total [mass](../../../../../mass.md) or binary history. Radioactive nickel/cobalt decay powers much of the later luminosity, and empirical light-curve corrections make these events useful distance indicators. Spectral type is not identical to explosion mechanism: hydrogen-rich Type II events are core collapse, but stripped-envelope Ib/c events can also be core collapse despite lacking conspicuous [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md).

The ejecta first expand nearly freely while swept-up [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is small. A strong [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md) heats ambient gas, and once swept-up material dominates, an approximately adiabatic remnant approaches the Sedov-Taylor scaling

$$
\boxed{R_s\propto\left(\frac{Et^2}{\rho_0}\right)^{1/5}.}
$$

Subsequent [radiative cooling](../../../../../radiative-cooling.md) makes a dense shell and ends that energy-conserving approximation. Thermal X-ray lines probe shocked gas and nucleosynthetic ejecta; radio and nonthermal X-rays [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) [relativistic particles](../../../../../relativistic-particle.md) and [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md) acceleration makes remnants plausible contributors to Galactic [cosmic rays](../../../../../cosmic-ray.md). Expansion, ambient-density structure, cooling and cosmic-ray [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) losses complicate [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) estimates; a bright synchrotron shell alone is not proof of a specific accelerated-particle composition. Supernovae return metals and mechanical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to the [interstellar medium](../../../../../interstellar-medium.md) and therefore connect stellar death to the feedback cycle.
