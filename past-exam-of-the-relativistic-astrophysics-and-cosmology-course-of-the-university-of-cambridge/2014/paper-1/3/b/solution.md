<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write the [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) [scale factor](../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) as $a=R/R_0$ and the [density contrast](../../../../../density-contrast.md) as $\delta=(\rho-\bar\rho)/\bar\rho$. Gravity amplifies overdensities while expansion and pressure oppose growth. In a sub-Hubble Newtonian single-fluid approximation, a comoving Fourier mode obeys

$$
\ddot\delta_k+2H\dot\delta_k+
\left(\frac{c_s^2k^2}{a^2}-4\pi G\bar\rho\right)\delta_k=0.
$$

For [pressureless matter](../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) in an Einstein-de Sitter background, the growing solution is $\delta\propto t^{2/3}\propto a$ and the decaying solution is $t^{-1}$. Jeans support suppresses growth when the pressure term dominates. The equation is not a substitute for relativistic perturbation theory on horizon-sized scales.

[cold dark matter](../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md) clusters without baryonic pressure support. Sub-horizon growth is much weaker during [radiation domination](../../../../../radiation-domination.md), and [baryons](../../../../../baryon.md) are initially tied to the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) fluid, producing acoustic oscillations rather than unrestricted collapse. Horizon entry, radiation-matter equality, acoustic evolution and [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) therefore give a scale-dependent [transfer function](../../../../../transfer-function.md). The primordial curvature spectrum and the later matter-density spectrum are not the same function: evolution and the gravitational constraints relate them. Late dark-energy domination reduces the growth rate.

When $|\delta|$ approaches unity, linear theory fails. Collapse, [virialization](../../../../../virialization.md), mergers and tidal interactions form a hierarchy of haloes. Gas must cool and lose [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) to make dense stellar systems; feedback then changes its supply and [distribution](../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Simulations combine collisionless gravitational dynamics with increasingly complex gas physics rather than extending the linear growing mode indefinitely. Galaxy clustering measures biased tracers of matter; [gravitational lensing](../../../../../gravitational-lensing-split.md) and CMB constraints provide complementary tests. Approximately adiabatic, nearly scale-invariant initial perturbations explain the coherent acoustic pattern and supply seeds for this later growth.[abstract and standard-model temperature/lensing analysis](../../../../../planck-2013-results-xvi-cosmological-parameters-by-planck-collaboration.md)
