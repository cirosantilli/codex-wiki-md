<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [weak equivalence principle](../../../../../weak-equivalence-principle.md) is universality of [free fall](../../../../../free-fall.md) for sufficiently small test bodies, independent of composition, when non-gravitational forces and self-gravity are negligible. Equality of inertial and passive gravitational [mass](../../../../../mass.md) makes this possible in the Newtonian limit. The [Einstein equivalence principle](../../../../../einstein-equivalence-principle.md) adds [local Lorentz invariance](../../../../../local-lorentz-invariance.md) and [local position invariance](../../../../../local-position-invariance.md) for nongravitational experiments: a small freely falling laboratory has the local physics of [special relativity](../../../../../special-relativity-split.md).

This motivates a universal [spacetime metric](../../../../../spacetime-metric.md). Freely falling unforced test particles follow [geodesics](../../../../../geodesic.md), $u^\nu\nabla_\nu u^\mu=0$. At a point one can choose local inertial coordinates with vanishing connection, but curvature and tidal relative acceleration remain measurable over a finite region. Gravity cannot be transformed away globally. A uniformly accelerating small laboratory and a local gravitational field can have the same nongravitational experimental behaviour without having identical global geometry.

Clock comparisons predict a fractional rate difference of order $g\Delta h/c^2$ in a weak static field. The [Pound-Rebka experiment](../../../../../pound-rebka-experiment.md) tested the associated [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [redshift](../../../../../redshift.md) through resonant gamma-ray emission and absorption at different heights, using controlled Doppler shifts to match the tiny [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) displacement. Such a result tests the metric [redshift](../../../../../redshift.md) prediction, not uniquely every component of the gravitational field equations.[1960 laboratory gravitational-redshift measurement](../../../../../apparent-weight-of-photons-by-robert-vivian-pound-and-glen-rebka.md)

[general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md) adds the dynamical [Einstein field equations](../../../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md),

$$
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu}.
$$

The [equivalence principle](../../../../../equivalence-principle.md) alone does not derive this unique equation; assumptions about covariance, dynamics and the Newtonian limit are also needed. The [strong equivalence principle](../../../../../strong-equivalence-principle.md) extends the universality claim to self-gravitating systems and gravitational experiments. Alternative metric theories can satisfy the weaker local principles while differing in gravitational dynamics, so composition tests, clock/[redshift](../../../../../redshift.md) tests and self-gravity tests probe distinct claims.
