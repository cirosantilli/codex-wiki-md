**[mass](../../../../../mass.md) from motion.** A resolved [Keplerian orbit](../../../../../kepler-orbit.md) gives $M=4\pi^2a^3/(GP_{\rm orb}^2)$ when the orbiting object has negligible [mass](../../../../../mass.md). At the Galactic centre, repeated infrared [astrometry](../../../../../astrometry.md) and [spectroscopy](../../../../../spectroscopy.md) measure sky motion, acceleration and line-of-sight velocity of [stars](../../../../../star.md) around [Sagittarius A\*](../../../../../sagittarius-a.md). Resolving a compact enclosed [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and fitting complete orbits avoids reliance on a single projected velocity. Source confusion, reference-frame stability, distance, inclination and possible extended [mass](../../../../../mass.md) must be included in the fit.[Abstract and orbital-model/systematic-error discussion](../../../../../measuring-distance-and-properties-of-the-milky-way-s-central-supermassive-black-hole-with-stellar-orbits-by-andrea-m-ghez-and-others.md)

For an unresolved stellar-mass binary in a circular orbit, the donor's velocity semiamplitude $K_2$ and [orbital period](../../../../../orbital-period.md) give the [binary mass function](../../../../../binary-mass-function.md)

$$
f(M_{\rm BH})=\frac{P_{\rm orb}K_2^3}{2\pi G}
=\frac{M_{\rm BH}^3\sin^3i}{(M_{\rm BH}+M_2)^2}.
$$

It provides a lower [mass](../../../../../mass.md) bound; donor [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and [orbital inclination](../../../../../orbital-inclination.md) are needed for an actual [mass](../../../../../mass.md) estimate. For an eccentric orbit, multiply the observational combination $P_{\rm orb}K_2^3/(2\pi G)$ by $(1-e^2)^{3/2}$ when evaluating the [binary mass function](../../../../../binary-mass-function.md). Resolved gas or maser [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) and stellar-dynamical models also measure [masses](../../../../../mass.md), provided [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) support, noncircular motion and [line-of-sight projection](../../../../../line-of-sight-projection.md) are modeled. Resolving the [black-hole sphere of influence](../../../../../black-hole-sphere-of-influence.md) improves separation of central [mass](../../../../../mass.md) from the host [galaxy](../../../../../galaxy-split.md).

For active nuclei, [reverberation mapping](../../../../../reverberation-mapping.md) measures a lag $\tau$ between variable continuum and broad-line response. With a line velocity scale $\Delta v$,

$$
M_{\rm BH}=f_{\rm geom}\frac{c\tau\,\Delta v^2}{G}.
$$

The factor $f_{\rm geom}$ accounts for geometry and kinematics and is not known simply from the lag. Cadence, line responsivity, inclination and winds require control. Rapid variability demonstrates compactness but is not by itself a unique [mass](../../../../../mass.md) measurement.

**[black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md) from strong-field emission.** Define $a_*=cJ/(GM^2)$. Large-radius Keplerian motion is principally a [mass](../../../../../mass.md) probe and usually insensitive to [black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md). In the Kerr model, the [radius](../../../../../radius.md) and [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) of the [innermost stable circular orbit](../../../../../innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md) vary with $a_*$ and orbital orientation, making inner-disk [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) useful.

[black-hole continuum fitting](../../../../../black-hole-continuum-fitting.md) infers an inner emitting [radius](../../../../../radius.md) from the disk's thermal spectrum and flux normalization, then identifies it with the [innermost stable circular orbit](../../../../../innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md) in a relativistic thin-disk model. Independent [mass](../../../../../mass.md), distance and inclination, together with atmospheric spectral hardening, [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) and contamination, are needed. Spectra should be selected in a disk-dominated regime where truncation and strong [advection](../../../../../advection.md) are limited. A 2011 Cygnus X-1 analysis combined such spectra with independently constrained binary parameters and reported a near-extreme [black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md) conditional on [black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md)–orbit alignment and its disk model.[Abstract, thermal-continuum fit and dominant mass/distance/inclination/flux-calibration uncertainties](../../../../../the-extreme-spin-of-the-black-hole-in-cygnus-x-1-by-lijun-gou-and-others.md)

[black-hole reflection spectroscopy](../../../../../black-hole-reflection-spectroscopy.md) instead models a relativistically broadened iron-line and reflection continuum: orbital [Doppler shifts](../../../../../doppler-effect.md) and beaming broaden the profile, while [gravitational redshift](../../../../../gravitational-redshift.md) contributes to the extended red wing. Its innermost emitting [radius](../../../../../radius.md) can constrain [black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md) if reflection extends to the stable inner edge. Ionization, abundance, [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md), coronal illumination, disk thickness and possible truncation are competing parameters; a broad line is not a direct [black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md) reading independent of these assumptions. Timing or precession models can add information but introduce their own dynamical identification.

**[mass](../../../../../mass.md) is principally inferred from gravitating dynamics; [black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md) is inferred from strong-field effects with a specified spacetime and [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) model.** Agreement between independent methods is especially valuable because disk inner [radius](../../../../../radius.md), inclination and radiation-transfer assumptions can otherwise mimic a change of [black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md).
