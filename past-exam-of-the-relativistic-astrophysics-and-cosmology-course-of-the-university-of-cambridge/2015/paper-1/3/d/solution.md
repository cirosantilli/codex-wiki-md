<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) has an almost perfect [blackbody spectrum](../../../../../blackbody-spectrum.md) at about $2.725\ \mathrm{K}$. This is strong evidence for an earlier hot, dense phase in which [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) thermalized, followed by expansion that redshifted the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) spectrum while preserving its Planck form. [COBE](../../../../../cosmic-background-explorer.md) [FIRAS](../../../../../far-infrared-absolute-spectrophotometer.md) compared the sky with a precision reference [blackbody](../../../../../blackbody.md); instrument [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), gain, foregrounds and calibrator emissivity were crucial controls. Agreement with a [blackbody](../../../../../blackbody.md) constrains substantial late [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) injection or photon-production distortions.[FIRAS measurement and external-blackbody calibration account](../../../../../nobel-lecture-from-the-big-bang-to-the-nobel-prize-and-beyond-by-john-c-mather.md)

After subtracting the dominant dipole associated mainly with observer motion, anisotropies at the level of roughly $10^{-5}$ reveal primordial structure. [COBE](../../../../../cosmic-background-explorer.md) DMR established large-angular-scale anisotropy; multifrequency measurements and independent maps were needed to distinguish it from Galactic emission and instrumental artifacts.[L1–L5, statistical and systematic checks](../../../../../structure-in-the-cobe-differential-microwave-radiometer-first-year-maps-by-george-f-smoot-and-others.md) Approximate isotropy supports a homogeneous background model under the usual cosmological assumptions, but isotropy at one observing position is not alone a proof of spatial homogeneity everywhere.

The [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) [CMB angular power spectrum](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-power-spectrum.md) has multiple acoustic peaks. Their coherent pattern follows oscillations in the pre-recombination [photon-baryon fluid](../../../../../photon-baryon-fluid.md). Peak positions compare the [sound horizon](../../../../../sound-horizon.md) with [angular diameter distance](../../../../../angular-diameter-distance.md) and hence constrain geometry together with expansion parameters. Relative peak heights test [baryon](../../../../../baryon.md) inertia, matter–[radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) equality and gravitational-potential evolution, motivating both baryonic and nonbaryonic matter. [Cosmic microwave background diffusion damping](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-diffusion-damping.md) suppresses small-scale power. [Cosmic microwave background polarization](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-polarization.md) and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md)–polarization correlations provide further tests of scattering, acoustic phase and [reionization](../../../../../reionization.md); [gravitational lensing](../../../../../gravitational-lensing-split.md) remaps the observed pattern and probes intervening structure.

By the time of this examination, the Planck 2013 [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and lensing analysis supported a spatially flat six-parameter model with [cold dark matter](../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md) and a [cosmological constant](../../../../../cosmological-constant.md). This is a joint fit within an explicit model, not an inference that each peak uniquely measures one parameter.[Abstract and cosmological parameter/extension analysis](../../../../../planck-2013-results-xvi-cosmological-parameters-by-planck-collaboration.md) Foreground separation requires frequency coverage, sky masks, beam and noise characterization, and consistency across instruments. The primary microwave background has geometric and other parameter degeneracies, so acoustic-ruler, supernova and structure observations are valuable complements. **The spectrum establishes a thermal hot-universe history; anisotropy and polarization constrain its geometry, contents and [primordial perturbations](../../../../../primordial-perturbation.md).** None of these observations by itself demonstrates a unique [cosmic inflation](../../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) mechanism.
