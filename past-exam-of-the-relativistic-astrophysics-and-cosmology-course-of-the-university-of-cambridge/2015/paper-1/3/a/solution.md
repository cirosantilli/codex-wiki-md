<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Three tracers give complementary estimates of a cluster's gravitating [mass](../../../../../mass.md). First, [galaxy](../../../../../galaxy-split.md) [redshifts](../../../../../redshift.md) measure a line-of-sight [velocity dispersion](../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md) $\sigma_{\rm los}$. For a roughly stationary bound cluster, the [virial theorem](../../../../../virial-theorem.md) gives a [cluster velocity-dispersion mass estimate](../../../../../cluster-velocity-dispersion-mass-estimate.md)

$$
M(<r)\sim\frac{3\sigma_{\rm los}^2r}{G},
$$

with a coefficient depending on the [density](../../../../../density.md) profile and orbital anisotropy. The [galaxy](../../../../../galaxy-split.md) [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) and plausible stellar [mass-to-light ratio](../../../../../mass-to-light-ratio.md) supply too little [mass](../../../../../mass.md) to bind typical rich clusters. Foreground interlopers, substructure, velocity anisotropy and a recent merger can bias the dynamical estimate; a large dispersion is not by itself proof of equilibrium.

Second, diffuse X-ray emission measures a hot intracluster [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md), not merely unresolved starlight. Spectral continuum and lines constrain its [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), while surface-brightness profiles constrain its [density](../../../../../density.md) after [line-of-sight projection](../../../../../line-of-sight-projection.md) and emissivity modeling. If the gas has constant composition and is approximately spherical and in [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md),

$$
\frac{dP_g}{dr}=-\rho_g\frac{GM(<r)}{r^2}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{M(<r)=-\frac{k_BT(r)r}{G\mu m_p}
\left(\frac{d\log\rho_g}{d\log r}+\frac{d\log T}{d\log r}\right)}.
$$

This [cluster hydrostatic mass estimator](../../../../../cluster-hydrostatic-mass-estimator.md) uses $\mu m_p$ as the mean [mass](../../../../../mass.md) per pressure-supporting particle. The inferred potential requires substantially more [mass](../../../../../mass.md) than the [stars](../../../../../star.md) plus measured gas, although the gas contains much of the visible baryonic [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Turbulence, bulk motion, [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) structure and departures from [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) are important [systematic errors](../../../../../systematic-error.md).

Third, strong-lensing arcs and multiple images, together with coherent weak-lensing shear of background [galaxies](../../../../../galaxy-split.md), measure projected gravitational deflection. In [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md),

$$
\kappa=\frac{\Sigma}{\Sigma_{\rm crit}},\qquad
\Sigma_{\rm crit}=\frac{c^2}{4\pi G}\frac{D_s}{D_lD_{ls}}.
$$

This [cluster gravitational-lensing mass estimate](../../../../../cluster-gravitational-lensing-mass-estimate.md) does not require the cluster [galaxies](../../../../../galaxy-split.md) or gas to be in dynamical equilibrium. It does require source-distance information, a lens model and control of point-spread-function-induced shapes, [line-of-sight projection](../../../../../line-of-sight-projection.md), unrelated line-of-sight structure and the [mass-sheet degeneracy](../../../../../mass-sheet-degeneracy.md).

**The convergence of dynamical, hydrostatic and lensing [masses](../../../../../mass.md), exceeding the baryonic inventory, is the principal evidence.** The Bullet Cluster adds a spatial test: collisional X-ray gas lags behind the [galaxies](../../../../../galaxy-split.md) during the merger, while the inferred lensing concentrations lie nearer the collisionless [galaxy](../../../../../galaxy-split.md) component. An additional weakly collisional gravitating [mass](../../../../../mass.md) accounts naturally for that separation.[L109–L113, optical/X-ray/lensing comparison](../../../../../a-direct-empirical-proof-of-the-existence-of-dark-matter-by-douglas-clowe-and-others.md) The conclusion assumes a gravitational framework and does not identify the microscopic dark-matter particle; the different tracer systematics should be evaluated rather than treating three [mass](../../../../../mass.md) estimates as completely assumption free.
