<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Vacuum density is constant for a true [cosmological constant](../../../../../cosmological-constant.md). Hence $0.3(1+z)^3=0.7$ gives

$$
\boxed{z_{m\Lambda}=\left(\frac{0.7}{0.3}\right)^{1/3}-1\simeq0.32635}.
$$

[matter-vacuum density equality](../../../../../matter-vacuum-density-equality.md) is not the same event as the start of [accelerating expansion](../../../../../accelerating-expansion-of-the-universe.md): neglecting radiation, acceleration begins when $\rho_m=2\rho_\Lambda$.
