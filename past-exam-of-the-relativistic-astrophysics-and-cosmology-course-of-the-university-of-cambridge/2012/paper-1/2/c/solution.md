<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Comparing [photons](../../../../../photon.md) with the constant vacuum density gives $\Omega_{\gamma0}(1+z)^4=0.7$, so

$$
\boxed{z_{\gamma\Lambda}=\left(\frac{0.7}{4.8032\times10^{-5}}\right)^{1/4}-1\simeq9.98735}.
$$

Matter dominates both at this crossing: its density in units of today's [critical density](../../../../../critical-density.md) is $0.3(10.98735)^3\simeq398$, compared with $0.7$ for each of the other components. [radiation-vacuum density equality](../../../../../radiation-vacuum-density-equality.md) therefore does not itself mark a switch in the dominant component.
