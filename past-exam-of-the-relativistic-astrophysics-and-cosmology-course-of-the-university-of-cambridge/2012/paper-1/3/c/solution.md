<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [quasar](../../../../../quasar.md) is a luminous [active galactic nucleus](../../../../../active-galactic-nucleus.md) powered by [gas](../../../../../gas.md) [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) onto a [supermassive black hole](../../../../../supermassive-black-hole.md). The hot disc produces much of the optical/ultraviolet continuum; surrounding [gas](../../../../../gas.md) gives broad and narrow [emission lines](../../../../../emission-line.md), while some systems have [relativistic jets](../../../../../relativistic-jet.md). A small emitting region is inferred from rapid variability, $R\lesssim c\Delta t_{\rm obs}/(1+z)$ when beaming is absent. Sustaining a high [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) from such a compact region favours [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) over ordinary stellar power. For approximately spherical, ionized hydrogen with electron-scattering opacity,

$$
L_E=\frac{4\pi GMm_pc}{\sigma_T}\simeq1.26\times10^{31}\frac{M}{M_\odot}\,\mathrm W.
$$

Comparing [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) with this Eddington scale constrains [mass](../../../../../mass.md) only after assumptions about anisotropy, opacity and [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) state. The emitted power obeys $L=\eta\dot Mc^2$; high binding-energy efficiency makes growth of a massive compact accretor energetically plausible.

Broad-line [reverberation mapping](../../../../../reverberation-mapping.md) estimates a [gas](../../../../../gas.md) radius from a continuum-to-line time lag, $R\sim c\tau$, and combines it with line velocity width to infer $M\sim f_{\rm vir}Rv^2/G$. Geometry, inclination and nongravitational [gas](../../../../../gas.md) motions enter the factor $f_{\rm vir}$. Independent evidence for compact massive nuclei comes from resolved stellar or [gas](../../../../../gas.md) orbits in nearby galaxies. [Andrea M. Ghez](../../../../../andrea-m-ghez.md) and collaborators' Galactic-centre stellar-orbit study fitted a common central potential and [distance](../../../../../distance.md), demonstrating a compact dark [mass](../../../../../mass.md) of order four million [solar masses](../../../../../solar-mass.md); the Galactic centre is a low-luminosity example rather than a [quasar](../../../../../quasar.md).[abstract; joint potential/distance fits and systematic-error analysis](../../../../../measuring-distance-and-properties-of-the-milky-way-s-central-supermassive-black-hole-with-stellar-orbits-by-andrea-m-ghez-and-others.md)

**[quasars](../../../../../quasar.md) are an [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) phase of massive nuclear [black holes](../../../../../black-hole-split.md); [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md), variability and dynamical evidence constrain different aspects of that interpretation.** A finite-resolution dark-mass measurement by itself is not a direct image of an [event horizon](../../../../../event-horizon.md).
