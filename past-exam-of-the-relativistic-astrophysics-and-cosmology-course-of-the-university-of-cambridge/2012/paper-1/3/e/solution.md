<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A [galaxy cluster](../../../../../galaxy-cluster.md) deflects light from background galaxies according to its projected gravitational field. In a thin gravitational lens approximation,

$$
\boldsymbol\beta=\boldsymbol\theta-\boldsymbol\alpha(\boldsymbol\theta),\qquad
\kappa=\frac{\Sigma}{\Sigma_{\rm crit}},\qquad
\Sigma_{\rm crit}=\frac{c^2D_s}{4\pi GD_lD_{ls}}.
$$

The [distances](../../../../../distance.md) are angular-diameter [distances](../../../../../distance.md); only [mass](../../../../../mass.md) in the appropriate foreground/background geometry lenses the chosen source this way. Near critical curves, [strong gravitational lensing](../../../../../strong-gravitational-lensing.md) produces highly magnified arcs and multiple images of the same source. Farther out, [weak gravitational lensing](../../../../../weak-gravitational-lensing.md) produces small coherent shear distortions, inferred statistically because individual galaxies have unknown intrinsic ellipticities. The measured [reduced shear](../../../../../reduced-shear.md) is $g=\gamma/(1-\kappa)$. Transparent lensing obeys [surface-brightness conservation in gravitational lensing](../../../../../surface-brightness-conservation-in-gravitational-lensing.md) at fixed source redshift while changing solid angle and integrated flux.

Strong-lensing image positions and source redshifts constrain the central projected [mass](../../../../../mass.md); weak shear extends the map to larger radii. Multiple source planes help constrain geometry and degeneracies, but the [mass-sheet degeneracy](../../../../../mass-sheet-degeneracy.md) illustrates why image shapes alone do not uniquely fix absolute [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Point-spread-function anisotropy, selection, shape calibration, cluster-member contamination, source redshift errors and unrelated line-of-sight structure require controls. Lensing does not require hydrostatic equilibrium, making it valuable for disturbed clusters, although interpretation still depends on the gravitational model.

An instructive observation is the merging [Bullet Cluster](../../../../../bullet-cluster.md): weak-lensing [mass](../../../../../mass.md) concentrations approximately follow the collisionless galaxies while the dominant visible baryonic [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md), traced by [X-rays](../../../../../x-ray.md), is displaced. Clowe and collaborators combined ground-based and Hubble images for lensing reconstruction and compared them with the [gas](../../../../../gas.md) distribution, providing evidence for [mass](../../../../../mass.md) beyond the observed [baryons](../../../../../baryon.md).[abstract and mass-map/gas comparison](../../../../../a-direct-empirical-proof-of-the-existence-of-dark-matter-by-douglas-clowe-and-others.md)

**Cluster lensing maps total projected gravitating [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and magnifies background sources; it does not by itself identify the microscopic dark-matter particle.**
