<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [weak equivalence principle](../../../../../weak-equivalence-principle.md) says that freely falling test bodies with identical initial conditions follow the same trajectory independently of composition, when nongravitational forces and appreciable self-gravity are negligible. In Newtonian language it implies universal gravitational-to-inertial [mass](../../../../../mass.md) ratio. The [Einstein equivalence principle](../../../../../einstein-equivalence-principle.md) additionally requires [local Lorentz invariance](../../../../../local-lorentz-invariance.md) and [local position invariance](../../../../../local-position-invariance.md) of nongravitational experiments: a sufficiently small freely falling laboratory realizes [special relativity](../../../../../special-relativity-split.md). The [strong equivalence principle](../../../../../strong-equivalence-principle.md) extends universality to gravitational experiments and self-gravitating bodies.

A falling elevator makes the local content clear. Uniform gravitational acceleration can be removed along a freely falling [worldline](../../../../../world-line.md) by a suitable frame, but [curvature](../../../../../curvature.md) cannot generally be removed over an extended region. Nearby [geodesics](../../../../../geodesic.md) have relative acceleration

$$
\frac{D^2\xi^\mu}{D\tau^2}=-R^\mu{}_{\alpha\nu\beta}u^\alpha\xi^\nu u^\beta
$$

for the displayed Riemann-sign convention. [tidal forces](../../../../../tidal-force.md) therefore remain detectable even in free fall. Equivalence motivates a metric description and gravitational clock shifts, but does not uniquely select Einstein's field equations.

Tests compare accelerations of different materials, [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md) of clocks at different gravitational potentials, or the response of self-gravitating bodies. [lunar laser ranging](../../../../../lunar-laser-ranging.md) times pulses reflected by lunar [retroreflectors](../../../../../retroreflector.md) and fits Earth-Moon motion in the Sun's field; a differential free-fall violation would add a characteristic periodic range perturbation. The fit must also model tides, station and reflector geometry and propagation delays, with solar-radiation pressure and reflector thermal effects checked as possible mimics. Williams and collaborators' 2012 analysis is a concrete example of this method, published after this examination.[§§2–4, pulse ranging, orbital fits and solar-radiation/thermal corrections](../../../../../lunar-laser-ranging-tests-of-the-equivalence-principle-by-james-g-williams-and-others.md)

**Equivalence is a local universality statement; tidal [curvature](../../../../../curvature.md) and the detailed gravitational field equations are additional physics.**
