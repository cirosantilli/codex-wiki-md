<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A donor star can feed a compact companion through [Roche-lobe overflow](../../../../../roche-lobe-overflow.md) near the inner [Lagrange point](../../../../../lagrange-point.md), or through capture of a [stellar wind](../../../../../stellar-wind.md). The transferred [gas](../../../../../gas.md) usually carries [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md); when its circularization radius exceeds the accretor's effective inner radius it forms an [accretion disc](../../../../../accretion-disk.md). Outward [angular momentum transport](../../../../../angular-momentum-transport.md) permits inward [mass](../../../../../mass.md) flow, while dissipation converts gravitational [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) into heat and radiation. For a compact object with a surface $L\sim GM\dot M/R$; for a [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) write $L=\eta\dot Mc^2$, with efficiency determined by the flow and inner boundary. Optically thick disc regions emit a sum of thermal [radiation spectrum](../../../../../radiation-spectrum.md) components; a hot optically thin [accretion corona](../../../../../accretion-corona.md) can inverse-Compton scatter softer [photons](../../../../../photon.md) into a harder [X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) component. A [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) has no material surface on which to radiate a stellar boundary layer.

An explicit example is the [wind-fed black-hole binary](../../../../../wind-fed-black-hole-binary.md) [Cygnus X-1](../../../../../cygnus-x-1.md). Its massive stellar companion supplies [gas](../../../../../gas.md), and the [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) flow produces strong [X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) emission.[abstract, H-alpha wind monitoring and X-ray-state comparison](../../../../../wind-accretion-and-state-transitions-in-cygnus-x-1-by-d-r-gies-and-others.md) Compact-object evidence comes from orbital dynamics rather than [X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) brightness alone. The single-lined [binary mass function](../../../../../binary-mass-function.md) constrains the unseen [mass](../../../../../mass.md); inclination, donor properties and [distance](../../../../../distance.md) refine it. [Jerome A. Orosz](../../../../../jerome-a-orosz.md) and collaborators combined optical photometry, [radial velocities](../../../../../radial-velocity.md), rotation and a [distance](../../../../../distance.md) constraint to obtain a black-hole [mass](../../../../../mass.md) of $14.8\pm1.0M_\odot$ in their 2011 analysis. The inference depends on the stellar and orbital model, and that historical value should not be confused with a latest measurement.[abstract; dynamical modelling and observational constraints](../../../../../the-mass-of-the-black-hole-in-cygnus-x-1-by-jerome-a-orosz-and-others.md)

**Binary [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) converts orbital [mass](../../../../../mass.md) transfer into compact gravitational-energy release; a dynamical [mass](../../../../../mass.md) measurement identifies the accretor more securely than [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) alone.**
