<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md), $P=\rho c^2/3$ and $w=1/3$. The continuity equation gives $\rho\propto R^{-4}$, so the flat, zero-$\Lambda$ background expands as $R\propto t^{1/2}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{q=1.}
$$

[Radiation pressure](../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) doubles the [deceleration parameter](../../../../../deceleration-parameter.md) relative to dust at the same density parameter. The printed radiation-filled Einstein-de Sitter wording is interpreted as a flat radiation-only model; the conventional name Einstein-de Sitter normally refers to the dust case.
