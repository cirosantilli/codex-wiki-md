<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For dust, $P=0$ and $w=0$. The continuity equation gives $\rho\propto R^{-3}$, and the flat, zero-$\Lambda$ [Friedmann equation](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) integrates to $R\propto t^{2/3}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{q=\frac12.}
$$

Directly, a power-law [scale factor](../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) $R\propto t^p$ has $q=(1-p)/p$, which gives the same constant. This is the usual pressureless Einstein-de Sitter model.
