**[Astrophysical jets](../../../../../astrophysical-jet.md) are directed [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) outflows whose bulk energy and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) remain concentrated near an axis over distances much larger than the launch region.** They occur around accreting [black holes](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) and [neutron stars](../../../../../neutron-star.md) and in forming stellar systems, with very different characteristic speeds and scales. An accretion reservoir supplies [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md); a rotating [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) can additionally provide spin energy through electromagnetic stresses. An observed [jet](../../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) therefore does not uniquely identify either a [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md) or a particular launch mechanism.

[Magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) couple the rotating engine to the outflow. Gas tied to suitable open field lines can be accelerated centrifugally from a disc; a large-scale poloidal field threading a rotating hole can support outward electromagnetic energy flux. Farther out, magnetic stresses, hoop stresses and external pressure can aid collimation. Pressure-driven acceleration, magnetic-to-kinetic conversion and entrainment depend on loading and environment. A simple stationary axisymmetric picture does not guarantee stability against kinks, mixing or disruption.

The emission commonly contains [synchrotron radiation](../../../../../synchrotron-radiation.md) from relativistic [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) spiraling in [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). Its broad continuum and [linear polarization](../../../../../linear-polarization.md) constrain the particles and ordered field component; [inverse Compton scattering](../../../../../inverse-compton-scattering.md) can extend [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) to higher [photon](../../../../../photon.md) energies. Compact knots can be [shocks](../../../../../shock-wave.md) or moving patterns rather than individual material parcels. Interaction with the surroundings creates [shocks](../../../../../shock-wave.md), hotspots, cocoons and radio lobes, transporting energy far beyond the central emission region.

Relativistic viewing effects are crucial. If a feature moves at speed $\beta c$ at angle $\theta$ toward the observer, its [Doppler factor](../../../../../doppler-factor.md) is

$$
\delta_D=\frac1{\Gamma(1-\beta\cos\theta)},\qquad
\Gamma=(1-\beta^2)^{-1/2}.
$$

A comoving emission frequency is observed at $\nu_{\rm obs}=\delta_D\nu'/(1+z_{\rm cos})$. For a discrete isotropically emitting moving feature with spectral index $F_\nu'\propto\nu'^{-s}$, the flux enhancement scales as $\delta_D^{3+s}$ in the usual unresolved-blob approximation. Continuous-jet conventions give a different exponent, so apparent brightness is not a direct orientation-free jet-power measurement.

Light-travel delays also produce [apparent superluminal motion](../../../../../apparent-superluminal-motion.md). In a source time interval $\Delta t$, the transverse displacement is $\beta c\Delta t\sin\theta$, but the observer receives the two signals separated by $\Delta t(1-\beta\cos\theta)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\beta_{\rm app}=\frac{\beta\sin\theta}{1-\beta\cos\theta}.}
$$

The maximum is $\Gamma\beta$, at $\cos\theta=\beta$. An apparent speed above $c$ violates no local speed limit. The receding [jet](../../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) is deboosted, so an intrinsically two-sided source may look one-sided; absorption and environmental differences can also contribute.

[Jets](../../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) feed back on their host systems by depositing [mechanical energy](../../../../../mechanical-energy.md), inflating cavities, accelerating particles and modifying surrounding gas. Radio [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) measures only part of that budget; unseen ions, [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), radiative losses and work on the environment all matter. Connecting the compact engine to a large lobe therefore requires both relativistic emission physics and the dynamics of its surroundings.
