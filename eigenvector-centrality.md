# Eigenvector centrality

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

[Eigenvector centrality](eigenvector-centrality.md) assigns nonnegative vertex scores proportional to the sum of neighbouring scores: $v_i=\lambda_{\max}^{-1}\sum_jA_{ij}v_j$. It uses a nonnegative [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of the [adjacency matrix](adjacency-matrix.md) for its largest positive [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). State the normalization, such as [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) one. For a three-leaf [star graph](star-graph-theory.md), hub and leaf scores have ratio $\sqrt3$.
