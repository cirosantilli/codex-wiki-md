# Naive pluripotency

↑ **Parent:** [Pluripotency](pluripotency.md)

A pluripotent state associated with the preimplantation mouse [epiblast](epiblast.md) and a characteristic regulatory network. Naive [cells](biological-cell-split.md) differ from the postimplantation [epiblast](epiblast.md) in signalling requirements, [chromatin](chromatin.md) state and responsiveness to differentiation cues.
