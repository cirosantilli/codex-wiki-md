# Hermitian symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md)

If $f$ is a [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) and an [integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md), its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) has [Hermitian symmetry](hermitian-symmetry.md): $F(-u)=\overline{F(u)}$. The value at negative [frequency](frequency.md) is the [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) of the value at positive [frequency](frequency.md).
