# Doubly stochastic realization of summable-sequence majorization

↑ **Parent:** [Majorization of summable sequences](majorization-of-summable-sequences.md)

Decreasing strictly positive [summable sequences](summable-sequence.md) with equal totals and $x\prec y$ admit an infinite [doubly stochastic matrix](doubly-stochastic-matrix.md) realizing the displayed relation. Choose $k$ with $y_k\geq x_1>y_{k+1}$ and average the pair to obtain $x_1$ and $y_k+y_{k+1}-x_1$. The remaining decreasing sequence majorizes the remaining target sequence: short initial sums contain values at least $x_1$, and longer sums are the old sums minus $x_1$. Repeat on the tail, freezing one normalized finite-support row at each stage. Limit column sums are at most one; [Tonelli's theorem](tonelli-theorem.md), equal finite totals and strict positivity of every $y_j$ force every column sum to equal one.
