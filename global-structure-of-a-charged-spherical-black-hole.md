# Global structure of a charged spherical black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md)

For $0<|Q|<M$, a [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md) has an outer [event horizon](event-horizon.md) and an inner [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md). Between them $r$ is timelike; inside the inner horizon $r$ is spacelike and the $r=0$ [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md) is timelike. The exact analytic extension contains repeated exterior blocks beyond the [Cauchy horizons](cauchy-horizon.md). Small perturbations produce severe inner-horizon blueshift and [mass inflation](mass-inflation.md), so that exact continuation is not a robust prediction for collapse. At $|Q|=M$ the horizons merge into a [degenerate Killing horizon](degenerate-killing-horizon.md); for $|Q|>M$ the spherical electrovacuum solution instead has a [naked singularity](naked-singularity.md).
