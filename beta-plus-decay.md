# Beta-plus decay

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)

An energetically allowed weak nuclear transition converts a proton into a neutron, emitting a positron and electron [neutrino](neutrino.md). Positron annihilation deposits additional electromagnetic energy, while the [neutrino](neutrino.md) can escape. These transitions are steps of the [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md) and the pp III branch; an isolated free proton cannot undergo this decay.
