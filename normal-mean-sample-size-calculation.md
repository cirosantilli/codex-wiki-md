# Normal-mean sample size calculation

↑ **Parent:** [Sample size](sample-size.md)

For a one-sided normal-mean test of size $\alpha$ with known standard deviation $\sigma$, a positive alternative $\delta^*$ and target [statistical power](statistical-power.md) $1-\beta>\alpha$, the required [sample size](sample-size.md) is the ceiling of $\sigma^2(z_{1-\alpha}+z_{1-\beta})^2/(\delta^*)^2$. This follows by shifting the [standard normal quantile](standard-normal-quantile.md) in the alternative rejection [probability](probability.md).
