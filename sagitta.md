# Sagitta

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

The maximum distance between a circular arc and its chord of length $L$. For radius $R$ and $L\ll R$, expansion gives $s\simeq L^2/(8R)$. In a uniform transverse [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), a charged track has $R=p/(|q|B)$, so the [sagitta](sagitta.md) measures its [momentum](momentum-split.md).
