# de Broglie-Bohm theory

↑ **Parent:** [Foundations of quantum mechanics](foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Broglie–Bohm_theory)

A deterministic interpretation with a definite particle configuration guided by the [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md). The latter obeys the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md), while the particle follows the [guidance equation](guidance-equation.md). A [Born rule](born-rule.md) distribution of initial positions is preserved by [quantum equilibrium equivariance](quantum-equilibrium-equivariance.md). The guidance law restricts admissible initial velocities, even when trajectories are rewritten as a second-order force equation.
