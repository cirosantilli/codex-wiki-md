# Strict transform

↑ **Parent:** [Blowup of a complex manifold at a point](blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strict_transform)

The strict transform of a subvariety $Y$ under a blowup $\sigma:\widetilde X\to X$ is the closure of $\sigma^{-1}(Y\setminus Z)$, where $Z$ is the center. Its total transform also contains the [exceptional divisor](exceptional-divisor.md) with the multiplicity with which $Y$ passes through $Z$.
