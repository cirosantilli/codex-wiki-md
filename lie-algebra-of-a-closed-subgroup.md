# Lie algebra of a closed subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-subgroup theorem](closed-subgroup-theorem.md)

For a closed [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$ of a [Lie group](lie-group.md), this set is a real [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md). Scalar closure is immediate; the [Lie product formula in a Lie group](lie-product-formula-in-a-lie-group.md) and closedness of $H$ give addition. It is a closed [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md), also directly by continuity of the [Exponential map of a Lie group](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md). Conjugating its [one-parameter subgroups](one-parameter-subgroup.md) by elements of $H$ preserves it. Differentiating the [Adjoint representation of a Lie group](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md) along $\exp(sX)$ gives $[X,Y]\in\mathfrak h$ for $X,Y\in\mathfrak h$. After the [closed-subgroup theorem](closed-subgroup-theorem.md) establishes smoothness, $\mathfrak h$ is exactly the tangent space $T_eH$.
