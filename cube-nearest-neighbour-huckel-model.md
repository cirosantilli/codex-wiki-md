<h1 id="cube-nearest-neighbour-huckel-model">Cube nearest-neighbour Hückel model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hückel method](huckel-method.md)

With one orthonormal site orbital per cube vertex, common site energy $\alpha$, and coupling $\beta$ along every edge, the one-electron [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is $\alpha I+\beta A$, where $A$ is the cube [adjacency matrix](adjacency-matrix.md). Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\alpha+3\beta,\alpha+\beta,\alpha-\beta,\alpha-3\beta$ with [multiplicities](multiplicity-mathematics.md) $1,3,3,1$.

One proof labels each vertex by three signs. The adjacency operator flips one sign at a time, so a product of $k$ coordinate signs is an [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) of $A$ with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $3-2k$. There are $\binom3k$ such products. For $\beta<0$, eight [electrons](electron.md) fill the lowest four spatial [molecular orbitals](molecular-orbital.md), yielding total one-electron energy $8\alpha+12\beta$.
