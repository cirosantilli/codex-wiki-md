# Developmental integration

↑ **Parent:** [Multicellular organism](multicellular-organism.md)

[Developmental integration](developmental-integration.md) here means coordination of cellular growth, organization and function into a developing whole. It describes what the parts do together, whereas [clonal development](clonal-development.md) describes how they descend from founders. Interactions through [signal transduction](signal-transduction.md) can help coordinate [cell differentiation](cellular-differentiation.md); see the Alberts et al. development chapter cited under [multicellular organism](multicellular-organism.md).
