# Hazard ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Hazard function](hazard-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazard_ratio)

A hazard ratio compares two hazard functions at the same time. Under proportional hazards it is constant in time.
