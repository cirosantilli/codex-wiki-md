# Measuring the Joule-Thomson effect with a porous plug

↑ **Parent:** [Joule-Thomson effect](joule-thomson-effect.md)

The 2024 operating instructions for PHYWE's 04361-00 apparatus describe a frit between two gas chambers. A copper-coil [heat exchanger](heat-exchanger.md) first restores inlet [temperature](temperature-split.md) after cylinder-regulator cooling. An upstream manometer reads [gauge pressure](gauge-pressure.md); the outlet stays at [atmospheric pressure](atmospheric-pressure.md). Two NiCr-Ni [thermocouples](thermocouple.md) sit 2–5 mm from the frit. The digital meter's difference mode reports $0.01\,\mathrm K$ precision.[PHYWE, Joule-Thomson apparatus 04361-00, 2024 instructions, pp. 1–2, sections 2–4: heat exchanger, frit, manometer, probes and difference readout](joule-thomson-apparatus-04361-00-operating-instructions-by-phywe.md)

The manual specifies at most $1\,\mathrm{bar}$ [gauge pressure](gauge-pressure.md), one hour of apparatus acclimatization, half an hour of meter warm-up, about 1.5 minutes of flow settling, then 10–15 seconds of averaging. It identifies a stagnant-gas zeroing error that shifts the fitted intercept and notes a maximum [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) signal of about $0.3\,\mathrm K$ in this setup.[PHYWE, 2024 instructions, p. 2, section 4, Hints concerning the experiment: pressure limit, equilibration, drift, averaging and zero compensation](joule-thomson-apparatus-04361-00-operating-instructions-by-phywe.md)

Define $x=p_1-p_2>0$ and $y=T_1-T_2$, so a small-drop fit is $y\simeq\mu_{\rm JT}x+c$. A constant sensor offset $c$ leaves the slope unchanged; pressure-dependent drift or [heat](heat-split.md) leakage can alter it. The observable is the differential [temperature](temperature-split.md) slope across the plug, not cylinder-outlet cooling. Display precision alone does not establish accuracy or prove an [isenthalpic process](isenthalpic-process.md). This low-pressure, room-temperature apparatus does not map a complete [Joule-Thomson inversion curve](joule-thomson-inversion-curve.md).
