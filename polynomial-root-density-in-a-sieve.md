# Polynomial root density in a sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Selberg upper-bound sieve](selberg-upper-bound-sieve.md)

For an integer polynomial $F$ and squarefree $d$, let $\rho_F(d)$ count the roots of $F$ modulo $d$. The [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) makes $\rho_F$ multiplicative, and

$$
\#\{m\leq X:d\mid F(m)\}=\frac{\rho_F(d)}dX+O(\rho_F(d)).
$$

Thus $g(d)=\rho_F(d)/d$ and $r_d=O(\rho_F(d))$ define a [sieve distribution](sieve-distribution.md).
