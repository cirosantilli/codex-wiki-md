# Uniform approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)

Uniform approximation minimizes the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) of the difference between a target [function](function-split.md) and an approximant.

The error is measured uniformly over the domain using the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md). A sequence of approximants whose uniform error tends to zero converges by [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md); the optimization problem and the convergence notion are distinct.
