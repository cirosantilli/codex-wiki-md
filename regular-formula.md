# Regular formula

↑ **Parent:** [Regular logic](regular-logic.md)

A regular formula is built using the constructors of [regular logic](regular-logic.md), with a finite variable context. The image of a definable relation interprets an existential quantifier. Regular formulas remain regular after substitution, conjunction and existential quantification.
