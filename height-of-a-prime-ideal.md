# Height of a prime ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Height of an ideal](height-of-an-ideal.md)

The height of a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) is the supremum of lengths of strict [prime chains](prime-chain.md) ending at it, equivalently the [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) of its [localization at a prime ideal](localization-at-a-prime-ideal.md). The [Krull height theorem](krull-height-theorem.md) bounds the height of a minimal prime over an $n$-generated [ideal](ideal.md) by $n$.
