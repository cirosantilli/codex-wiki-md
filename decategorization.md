# Decategorization

↑ **Parent:** [Social categorization](social-categorization.md)

Reducing the relevance of a group boundary by individuating interaction partners. A central limitation is generalization: liking a particular person need not change [prejudice](prejudice.md) toward other members of that person's [outgroup](social-outgroup.md).
