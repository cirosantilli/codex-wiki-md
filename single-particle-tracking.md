# Single-particle tracking

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement-split.md)

[single-particle tracking](single-particle-tracking.md) estimates successive positions of individual labeled objects. Displacements can constrain [diffusion](diffusion.md), confinement or directed movement, provided localization error, missed detections and label effects are accounted for.
