# Atom-field dressed state

↑ **Parent:** [Dressed state](dressed-state.md)

A [dressed state](dressed-state.md) of an atom and its driving field. Within the [rotating-wave approximation](rotating-wave-approximation.md), the paired configurations $|e,n\rangle$ and $|g,n+1\rangle$ mix. A classical-drive rotating-frame [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H=\hbar(-\Delta\sigma_z+\Omega\sigma_x)/2$ has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\pm\hbar\sqrt{\Delta^2+\Omega^2}/2$. At resonance its [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) are equal-weight combinations of the two bare configurations. Radiative transitions between neighboring manifolds explain the [Mollow triplet](mollow-triplet.md).
