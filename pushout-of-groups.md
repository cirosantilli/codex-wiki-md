# Pushout of groups

↑ **Parent:** [Pushout in a category](pushout-in-a-category.md)

For homomorphisms $i_j:H\to G_j$, the [pushout of groups](pushout-of-groups.md) is $(G_1*G_2)/\langle\!\langle i_1(h)i_2(h)^{-1}:h\in H\rangle\!\rangle$. It has the [universal property](universal-property.md) for homomorphisms out of $G_1,G_2$ agreeing on $H$. When the maps $i_j$ are injective, it is the [amalgamated free product](amalgamated-free-product.md), with the factors and common subgroup embedded.
