# Singular cochain

↑ **Parent:** [Singular cohomology](singular-cohomology.md)

A singular $n$-cochain with coefficients in an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $A$ is a homomorphism from the [singular chain group](singular-chain-group.md) $C_n(X;\mathbb Z)$ to $A$. It is specified by its values on all singular simplices, without a finite-support restriction. Its [coboundary](coboundary.md) evaluates on the alternating sum of the faces. The resulting [cochain complex](cochain-complex.md) computes [singular cohomology](singular-cohomology.md).
