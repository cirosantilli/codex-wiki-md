# Gamow-Teller transition

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay transition](beta-decay-transition.md)

The spin-dependent axial-vector contribution to an [allowed beta decay](allowed-beta-decay.md). Its rotational rank is one, permitting $\Delta J=0,1$ with unchanged [parity](parity.md), but excluding $0\to0$. Here $\boldsymbol\sigma_k$ acts on [nucleon](nucleon.md) [spin](spin.md) and $\tau_k^\pm$ on [isospin](isospin.md). Higher-orbital spin-dependent contributions are conventionally described as Gamow-Teller-type forbidden transitions.
