# Ornstein-Uhlenbeck entropy dissipation identity

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality](gaussian-logarithmic-sobolev-inequality.md)

For bounded smooth positive $h$ bounded away from zero, differentiation and [Gaussian integration by parts](stein-s-lemma-probability.md) give the displayed identity. The [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck gradient commutation identity](ornstein-uhlenbeck-gradient-commutation-identity.md) and weighted [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) bound its right side by $e^{-2t}\int|h'|^2/h\,d\gamma$. Integrating and setting $h=f^2$ proves the [Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality](gaussian-logarithmic-sobolev-inequality.md). Positive clipped approximation extends it to functions of finite [Gaussian Dirichlet energy](gaussian-dirichlet-energy.md).
