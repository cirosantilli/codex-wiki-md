<h1 id="kahler-cone">Kähler cone</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md)

The Kähler cone of a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) is the open cone of real $(1,1)$ cohomology classes represented by positive closed $(1,1)$ forms. If every real degree-two cohomology class has type $(1,1)$, density of rational classes supplies a rational Kähler class. An integral multiple is the [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md) of a positive [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md), and the [Kodaira embedding theorem](kodaira-embedding-theorem.md) implies projectivity.
