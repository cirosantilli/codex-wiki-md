# Thermal pair creation heat capacity for touching parabolic bands

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-density-of-states intrinsic semiconductor](constant-density-of-states-intrinsic-semiconductor.md)

For symmetric touching [parabolic dispersions](parabolic-band-dispersion.md) with constant [density of states](density-of-states.md) $D$ per [energy band](energy-band.md), including [spin degeneracy](spin-degeneracy.md), [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) fixes $\mu=0$. The [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) gives $N_c=Dk_BT\ln2$ and the thermal [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md), including [electrons](electron.md) and [holes](electron-hole.md), is $\Delta U=(\pi^2/6)D(k_BT)^2$. Thus $C=(\pi^2/3)Dk_B^2T$. Its large ratio to $N_c$ is compatible with the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md) because this excitation count grows with [temperature](temperature-split.md) and the [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) includes both species.
