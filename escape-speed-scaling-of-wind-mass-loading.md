# Escape-speed scaling of wind mass loading

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic outflow](galactic-outflow.md)

If [stellar feedback](stellar-feedback.md) supplies energy $e_*$ per unit formed stellar mass, an [energy-driven outflow](energy-driven-outflow.md) requires $\nu v_{\rm esc}^2/2\lesssim e_*$. Fixed energy coupling therefore suggests [mass-loading factor](mass-loading-factor.md) $\nu\propto v_{\rm esc}^{-2}$. A fixed momentum supply $p_*$ instead gives $\nu v_{\rm esc}\lesssim p_*$ and [momentum-driven outflow](momentum-driven-outflow.md) scaling $\nu\propto v_{\rm esc}^{-1}$. These are launch and escape estimates, not universal wind laws; subsequent entrainment, cooling and fallback change the escaping mass.
