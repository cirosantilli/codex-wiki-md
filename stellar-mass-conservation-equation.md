# Stellar mass conservation equation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure equations](stellar-structure-equations.md)

In spherical [stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md), a shell of [radius](radius.md) $r$, thickness $dr$ and [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ has [mass](mass.md) $dm=4\pi r^2\rho\,dr$. Integrating outward gives the [enclosed mass](enclosed-mass.md), $m(r)=4\pi\int_0^r\rho(s)s^2ds$.
