# Reissner-Nordstrom trapped spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md)

In ingoing [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md), orient $-\partial_r$ to the future and normalize the other radial [null vector](null-vector.md) to $\partial_v+(f/2)\partial_r$. The [spherical null expansion in ingoing coordinates](spherical-null-expansion-in-ingoing-coordinates.md) gives $-2/r$ and $f/r$. Hence the strictly future [trapped surfaces](trapped-surface.md) among the round spheres occur exactly between the distinct horizons. At either horizon the outgoing [null expansion](null-expansion.md) vanishes. Inside the inner horizon the same round spheres are not future trapped.
