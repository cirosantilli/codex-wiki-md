# Square part of an integer

↑ **Parent:** [Square number](square-number.md)

The square part of a positive [integer](integer-split.md) $n=\prod_p p^{e_p}$ is its largest square divisor, $\prod_p p^{2\lfloor e_p/2\rfloor}$. Dividing $n$ by its square part leaves the [squarefree part](squarefree-part.md).
