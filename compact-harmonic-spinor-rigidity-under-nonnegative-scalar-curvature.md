# Compact harmonic-spinor rigidity under nonnegative scalar curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Lichnerowicz spinor-square formula](lichnerowicz-spinor-square-formula.md)

On a compact boundaryless Riemannian [spin manifold](spin-manifold.md) with $R\ge0$, integrating the [Lichnerowicz spinor-square formula](lichnerowicz-spinor-square-formula.md) against a harmonic spinor gives $\int(|\nabla\psi|^2+R|\psi|^2/4)=0$. Positivity forces the spinor to be parallel. Its norm is constant on each connected component, so a nontrivial solution is nowhere zero there. Strictly positive [scalar curvature](scalar-curvature.md) at any point on such a component excludes nonzero harmonic spinors.
