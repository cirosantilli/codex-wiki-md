# Anelasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)

Anelasticity is a delayed but recoverable strain response caused by relaxation of internal degrees of freedom. After a small imposed [stress](stress.md) is removed, the strain can return to its original value over a finite time rather than instantaneously. Under oscillatory loading, this delay causes a phase lag and dissipation, measured by a [loss modulus](loss-modulus.md) or [loss tangent](loss-tangent.md). Stress-induced redistribution of interstitial impurities in a metal gives the [Snoek effect](snoek-effect.md). This solid-state response is distinct from an approximation to fluid mass conservation carrying a similar name.
