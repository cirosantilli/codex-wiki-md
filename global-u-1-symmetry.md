<h1 id="global-u-1-symmetry">Global U(1) symmetry</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md)

A global phase symmetry sends a complex field to $e^{i\alpha}\phi$ with the same constant real $\alpha$ everywhere. It is distinct from allowing a spacetime-dependent phase together with a compensating gauge-field transformation. A global phase symmetry can produce a conserved [Noether current](noether-current.md) and, in an appropriate ordered mean-field state, a continuum of degenerate phase choices.
