# Zenith distance

↑ **Parent:** [Zenith](zenith.md)

[Zenith distance](zenith-distance.md) is the [angle](angle.md) from the [zenith](zenith.md) to a viewing direction: $z=\arccos(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf e_U)=90^\circ-\gamma$, where $\gamma$ is [altitude](altitude.md) and $0\leq z\leq180^\circ$. The [astronomical horizon](astronomical-horizon.md) has $z=90^\circ$, while the [zenith](zenith.md) has $z=0$. This unsigned separation supplies no [azimuth](azimuth.md) and is distinct from the signed north/south angular readout used under [declination](declination.md).
