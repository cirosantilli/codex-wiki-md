# Keyhole contour

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)

A contour encircling a branch cut travels on its two sides and joins them by small and large circles. It converts the phase change of a complex power into a real integral through the [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md). The two circular contributions must be bounded before taking their limiting radii.
