# GIM suppression of top-quark neutral-current decays

↑ **Parent:** [GIM mechanism](gim-mechanism.md)

For $q=u,c$ and a neutral [photon](photon.md) or [Z boson](z-boson.md), the [loop](loop-feynman-diagram.md) runs over [down-type quarks](down-quark.md). [CKM unitarity](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) cancels a common mass-independent [loop](loop-feynman-diagram.md) contribution. The large [bottom](bottom-quark.md) [mass](mass.md) relative to down and strange [masses](mass.md) normally makes the surviving [bottom](bottom-quark.md) contribution largest, and $|V_{cb}|>|V_{ub}|$ favours final [charm](charm-quark.md). Adding a resolved [photon](photon.md) to a $qZ$ final state introduces another electromagnetic coupling and three-body [phase space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) without removing the [flavour](flavor-eigenstate.md) cancellation. Some diagrams use the [quartic gauge vertex](quartic-w-photon-z-gauge-coupling.md), so the number of vertices need not increase.
