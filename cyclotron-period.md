# Cyclotron period

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md)

In a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), a nonrelativistic particle of [mass](mass.md) $m$ and nonzero charge $q$ completes one circular transverse orbit in time $T_c=2\pi/\omega_c$, where $\omega_c=|q|B/m$. Parallel [velocity](velocity.md) changes the [helix](helix.md) pitch but not this [period](period-of-a-function.md). In relativistic motion the constant [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) multiplies the [period](period-of-a-function.md): $T_c=2\pi\gamma m/(|q|B)$. The direction of rotation depends on the charge sign; the [period](period-of-a-function.md) is positive.
