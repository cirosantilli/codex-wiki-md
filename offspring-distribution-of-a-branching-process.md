# Offspring distribution of a branching process

↑ **Parent:** [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md)

In a [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md), each individual independently has $j$ children with [probability](probability.md) $p_j$. This [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) determines its offspring [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md), its mean $f'(1)$ when finite, and the [branching extinction probability](extinction-probability-of-a-branching-process.md). A [binomial branching process](binomial-branching-process.md) and a [Poisson branching process](poisson-branching-process.md) have different [offspring distributions](offspring-distribution-of-a-branching-process.md) even when their means agree.
