# Strict descent of powers of a nonzero proper ideal in a Noetherian domain

↑ **Parent:** [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md)

Let $R$ be a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) that is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), and let $I$ be a nonzero proper [ideal](ideal.md). If $I^n=I^{n+1}$ for some $n\geq1$, the nonzero [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) $M=I^n$ satisfies $M=IM$. Choose generators $u_1,\ldots,u_r$ and write $u_i=\sum_j a_{ij}u_j$ with $a_{ij}\in I$. The [adjugate matrix](adjugate-matrix.md) identity implies that $\det(\operatorname{Id}_r-(a_{ij}))$ annihilates $M$. This [determinant](determinant.md) is $1-b$ for some $b\in I$, since every term except the constant term contains an entry from $I$.

The [determinant trick](determinant-trick.md) has thus supplied $(1-b)M=0$. A nonzero element of $M$ and [multiplicative cancellation in an integral domain](multiplicative-cancellation-in-an-integral-domain.md) force $b=1$, contradicting properness of $I$. Therefore all positive [powers of an ideal](powers-of-an-ideal.md) are distinct in this setting. Since they have the same [radical of an ideal](radical-of-an-ideal.md), there are infinitely many [ideals](ideal.md) with radical $\sqrt I$.
