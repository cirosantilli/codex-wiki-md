# Elastic rebound model

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake](earthquake.md)

An [elastic rebound model](elastic-rebound-model.md) describes slow loading of a locked fault, storage of elastic [strain](strain.md) [energy](energy-split.md), and sudden slip with [stress](stress.md) reduction when the fault fails. The surrounding rock partly relaxes while retaining a permanent displacement across the fault. It does not by itself predict the failure time, rupture extent or frictional dynamics, and continuous creep need not follow this ideal cycle.
