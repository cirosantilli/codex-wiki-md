# Grand-canonical chemical potential of a classical ideal gas

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md)

For one internal species, the [grand canonical partition function](grand-canonical-partition-function.md) of a classical [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) satisfies $\ln\Xi=zV/\lambda^3$, with [quantum gas fugacity](fugacity-statistical-mechanics.md) $z=e^{\beta\mu}$ and [thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) $\lambda=\sqrt{2\pi\hbar^2/(mk_BT)}$. Differentiating the [grand potential](grand-potential.md) gives $N=zV/\lambda^3$, hence $\mu=k_BT\ln(n\lambda^3)$. An internal partition factor must be included consistently if internal states are available.
