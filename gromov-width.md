# Gromov width

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gromov_width)

The Gromov width of a symplectic manifold is the supremum of $\pi r^2$ over radii of symplectically embedded balls $B^{2n}(r)$.
