# Nonlocal variable

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language semantics](programming-language-semantics.md)

A variable used within a procedure but declared outside it. Under [lexical scope](lexical-scope.md), its binding is determined by enclosing declarations and the associated runtime environment, often accessed through a [static chain](static-chain.md) or [programming closure](closure-computer-programming.md).
