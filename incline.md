# Incline

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic structure](algebraic-structure.md)

An incline has two associative commutative operations $+$ and multiplication, with multiplication distributing over addition, idempotent addition and absorption $x+xy=x$. Neither operation is required here to have an identity. Its natural order is $x\le y\iff x+y=y$, making addition a [join](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). Multiplication is [order-preserving](order-preserving-function.md), and products lie below both factors. A finitely generated incline is well-quasi-ordered by its reversed natural order, via the [reverse-inclusion well-quasi-ordering of monomial ideals](reverse-inclusion-well-quasi-ordering-of-monomial-ideals.md) applied to exponent supports.
