# Horizontally averaged plume concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Horizontally integrated plume concentration](horizontally-integrated-plume-concentration.md)

For a plume of full width $w(z)$, its horizontally averaged [concentration](concentration.md) is $\overline C(z,t)=c(z,t)/w(z)$, where $c$ is [horizontally integrated plume concentration](horizontally-integrated-plume-concentration.md). In a [line plume](line-plume.md) with $w=\mu z$, division by $z$ generally changes the location of maximum [concentration](concentration.md). A gradient closure for $c$ differs from one for $\overline C$: $w\partial_z\overline C=c_z-c/z$, so integrated diffusive flux $-Kw\partial_z\overline C$ equals $-K(c_z-c/z)$.
