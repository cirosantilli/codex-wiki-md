# Conjugate-base substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand substitution](ligand-substitution.md)

Substitution promoted by prior [deprotonation](deprotonation.md) of a coordinated ligand. In cobalt(III) ammine complexes, an amido donor can assist departure of another ligand; solvent capture and reprotonation complete the substitution.
