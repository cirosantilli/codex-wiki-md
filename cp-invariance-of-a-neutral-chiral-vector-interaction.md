# CP invariance of a neutral chiral vector interaction

↑ **Parent:** [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md)

The [vector current](vector-current.md) is odd and the [axial current](axial-current.md) is even under [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md). Under [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md), the first is a vector and the second a pseudovector. Thus their difference obeys $j_L^\mu(x)\mapsto-P^\mu{}_{\nu}j_L^\nu(x_P)$ under [CP](cp-symmetry.md), with $P=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$. If a neutral vector has $V^\mu(x)\mapsto\xi P^\mu{}_{\nu}V^\nu(x_P)$ under [CP](cp-symmetry.md), the interaction transforms by the factor $-\xi$. It is invariant for $\xi=-1$. Being a real field restricts its intrinsic phases but does not alone fix this sign. The conventional C-odd neutral gauge-vector assignment makes real diagonal [Z boson](z-boson.md) vector and axial couplings CP invariant, despite their separate C and P violations.
