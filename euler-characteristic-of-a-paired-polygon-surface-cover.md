# Euler characteristic of a paired-polygon surface cover

↑ **Parent:** [Paired-polygon construction of finite surface covers](paired-polygon-construction-of-finite-surface-covers.md)

For a paired $2s$-gon with $r$ base [vertex cycles of a paired polygon](vertex-cycle-of-a-paired-polygon.md), there are one face, $s$ edges and $r$ vertices on the base, so $\chi(M_0)=1-s+r$. The full finite-group construction has $|T|$ faces, $s|T|$ edges and $\sum_j|T|/q_j$ vertices, giving the displayed ordinary [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md). If all lifted angles are $2\pi$, the [orbifold Euler characteristic](orbifold-euler-characteristic.md) formula gives equivalently $\chi(M)=|T|\chi_{\rm orb}(M_0)$.
