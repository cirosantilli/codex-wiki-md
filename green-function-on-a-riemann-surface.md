# Green function on a Riemann surface

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function.md)

With the positive logarithmic convention, a Green function with pole $p$ on a noncompact [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md) is positive and harmonic away from $p$, has local form $-\log|\zeta|$ plus a harmonic function in a coordinate $\zeta(p)=0$, and is minimal among positive functions with these properties. It is obtained, when finite, as the increasing limit of zero-boundary Green functions on an exhaustion by compact bordered subdomains. On the [unit disk](unit-disk.md) it is

$$
G_{\mathbb D}(z,p)=\log\left|\frac{1-\overline pz}{z-p}\right|.
$$

Its sign convention is $-\Delta G=2\pi\delta_p$ in a conformal coordinate, with the opposite overall sign from conventions using $\log|\zeta|$ as the singular part.
