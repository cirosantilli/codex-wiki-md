# Lewis acid-base adduct

↑ **Parent:** [Acid-base chemistry](acid-base-chemistry-split.md)

A [Lewis acid-base adduct](lewis-acid-base-adduct.md) forms when an electron-pair donor binds an acceptor through a [coordinate covalent bond](coordinate-covalent-bond.md). A ring [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) can bind $\mathrm{GaCl_3}$ without displacement of a chlorine substituent from phosphorus.
