# Aliasing in a fractional factorial design

↑ **Parent:** [Defining contrast subgroup](defining-contrast-subgroup.md)

Two [factorial contrasts](factorial-contrast.md) are aliased if their columns are proportional on the selected [fractional factorial design](fractional-factorial-design.md). A word $G$ that has fixed sign $s_G$ gives $\chi_{AG}=s_G\chi_A$. Conversely, averaging a character over the fraction vanishes unless its word belongs to the [defining contrast subgroup](defining-contrast-subgroup.md), so every alias class is exactly a subgroup coset. In a half fraction each class has two distinct words; their separate coefficients cannot be identified without assumptions or additional runs.
