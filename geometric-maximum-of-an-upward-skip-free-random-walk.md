# Geometric maximum of an upward skip-free random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Upward skip-free random walk](upward-skip-free-random-walk.md)

Let the increments define an [upward skip-free random walk](upward-skip-free-random-walk.md), and suppose $\phi(\lambda)=\mathbb E e^{\lambda X_1}$ is finite for all $\lambda\geq0$. If $\lambda_0>0$ satisfies $\phi(\lambda_0)=1$ and $\phi'(\lambda_0)>0$, the [exponential tilting](exponential-tilting.md) at $\lambda_0$ gives integrable increments of positive mean. The [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) makes every nonnegative integer level attainable under this tilted law. Upward jumps are at most one, so the value at a first level hit is exactly that level. The [stopped likelihood ratio under exponential tilting](stopped-likelihood-ratio-under-exponential-tilting.md) therefore gives hitting probability $e^{-\lambda_0 k}$ for integer $k\geq0$. Subtracting consecutive tail probabilities yields the displayed [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) on $j=0,1,\ldots$. For real thresholds use $\lceil k\rceil$ in place of $k$.
