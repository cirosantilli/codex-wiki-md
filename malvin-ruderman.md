# Malvin Ruderman

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)

With [Charles Kittel](charles-kittel.md), he calculated [conduction-electron-mediated exchange](conduction-electron-mediated-exchange.md) between nuclear magnetic moments. His later research included [neutron stars](neutron-star.md). Columbia's account of his career also recalls his enthusiasm for order-of-magnitude teaching.[Columbia University, In Memoriam: Professor Mal Ruderman, 25 July 2024](https://fas.columbia.edu/news/memoriam-professor-mal-ruderman)
