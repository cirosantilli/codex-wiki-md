# Tangential component

↑ **Parent:** [Normal and tangential components](normal-and-tangential-components.md)

The tangential component is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) of a [vector](vector.md) onto the [tangent space](tangent-space.md). For a [plane curve](plane-curve.md), it is $(w\cdot T)T$; for a [hypersurface](hypersurface.md), it is $w-(w\cdot n)n$. The scalar tangential component of [acceleration](acceleration.md) is $\dot v$, which can be positive, zero or negative as the [speed](speed.md) increases, remains constant or decreases.
