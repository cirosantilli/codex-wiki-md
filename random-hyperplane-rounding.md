# Random hyperplane rounding

↑ **Parent:** [Vector coloring](vector-coloring.md)

For [unit vectors](unit-vector.md) $u,v$ and a random normal $a$ with independent [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) coordinates,

$$
\mathbb P\{\operatorname{sign}\langle a,u\rangle\ne\operatorname{sign}\langle a,v\rangle\}
=\frac{\arccos\langle u,v\rangle}{\pi}.
$$

The [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md) is invariant under [orthogonal transformations](orthogonal-transformation.md). Projecting onto the [plane](plane.md) spanned by $u,v$ therefore gives a uniformly distributed direction. If the angle between $u,v$ is $\theta$, the sign-disagreement directions form two sectors of total angle $2\theta$ out of $2\pi$. The endpoint cases $u=v$ and $u=-v$ give probabilities zero and one directly.
