# Monocline

↑ **Parent:** [Folding](folding.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monocline)

A step-like bend linking relatively less tilted beds on either side. Distributed deformation above a blind [fault](geological-fault.md) can produce a monocline without a discrete surface break.
