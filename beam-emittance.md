# Beam emittance

↑ **Parent:** [Beam optics](beam-optics.md)

The area occupied by a beam in transverse position-momentum or position-angle [phase space](phase-space.md), with a stated area or rms convention. [Geometric emittance](geometric-emittance.md) uses trajectory angle, while [normalized emittance](normalized-emittance.md) removes the ideal kinematic damping due to acceleration.
