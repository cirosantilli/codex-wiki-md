# Scattering plane

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md)

The plane spanned by incoming and outgoing propagation directions when those directions are not parallel or antiparallel. Polarization perpendicular or parallel to it is useful when expressing a [differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md). The plane is not unique at exact forward or backward scattering.
