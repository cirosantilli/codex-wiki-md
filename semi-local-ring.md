# Semi-local ring

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative algebra](noncommutative-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-local_ring)

A [ring](ring.md) is semilocal when its quotient by the [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) is a [semisimple ring](semisimple-ring.md) that is [Artinian](artinian-ring.md). Equivalently, that quotient is a [right Artinian ring](right-artinian-ring.md): it has zero [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md), and the Artinian structure theorem makes it semisimple. A commutative [ring](ring.md) is semilocal exactly when it has finitely many [maximal ideals](maximal-ideal.md).
