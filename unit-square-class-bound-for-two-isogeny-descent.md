# Unit square-class bound for two-isogeny descent

↑ **Parent:** [Two-isogeny descent](two-isogeny-descent.md)

Suppose the [ring of integers of a number field](ring-of-integers.md) is a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md), $a,b$ are integral, and $b$ is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md). On $y^2=x(x^2+ax+b)$ every nonzero $x$-coordinate has even [valuation](valuation.md) at every finite [prime](prime-number.md): positive valuation gives $2v(y)=v(x)$, and negative valuation gives $2v(y)=3v(x)$. Thus the [two-torsion square-class homomorphism](two-torsion-square-class-homomorphism.md) takes values among unit [square classes](square-class.md). For the [Gaussian integers](gaussian-integer.md), their [unit group](unit-group.md) modulo squares has two elements, represented by $1,i$. This can reduce a [two-isogeny descent](two-isogeny-descent.md) to a very small calculation even though the field contains no ordering.
