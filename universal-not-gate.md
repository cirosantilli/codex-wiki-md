# Universal NOT gate

↑ **Parent:** [Positive linear map](positive-linear-map.md)

The ideal universal NOT map sends every [qubit](qubit.md) [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) $\mathbf s$ to $-\mathbf s$, taking a [pure state](pure-state.md) to its orthogonal [pure state](pure-state.md). It is a trace-preserving [positive map](positive-linear-map.md) but not a [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md): applied to one half of a [Bell state](bell-state-split.md), it produces an operator with a negative [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). A physical approximation can reverse all directions with factor $-1/3$ using the equal mixture of conjugations by the three [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md). The [isotropic qubit map](isotropic-qubit-map.md) complete-positivity bound proves that a larger uniform reversal is impossible among such isotropic maps.
