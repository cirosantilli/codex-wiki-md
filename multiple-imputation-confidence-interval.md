# Multiple-imputation confidence interval

↑ **Parent:** [Rubin's rules](rubin-s-rules.md)

Use the pooled estimate and total [variance](variance-split.md) from [Rubin's rules](rubin-s-rules.md). A basic large-complete-sample scalar interval is $\overline Q\pm t_{\nu,1-\alpha/2}\sqrt T$, with $\nu=(M-1)[1+\overline U/((1+M^{-1})B)]^2$ for $B>0$. Small complete-data samples need an appropriate degrees-of-freedom adjustment; the normal limit applies as the available degrees of freedom become large.
