# Shallow-water quasi-geostrophic inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Potential-vorticity inversion](potential-vorticity-inversion.md)

The [shallow-water quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](shallow-water-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) relation is a [modified Helmholtz equation](modified-helmholtz-equation.md) for the [quasi-geostrophic streamfunction](quasi-geostrophic-streamfunction.md). Its inverse with specified [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) recovers horizontal [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md) and surface elevation $\eta=f_0\psi/g$. The [Rossby deformation radius](rossby-deformation-radius.md) $L_D=\sqrt{gH}/|f_0|$ is the range of the balanced response: for a horizontal Fourier component, $\widehat\psi=-\widehat q/(|\mathbf k_h|^2+L_D^{-2})$, after subtracting background [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md).
