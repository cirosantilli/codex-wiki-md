# Parameter continuity of variational regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Variational regularization](variational-regularization.md)

For objectives $D+\alpha J$ with $J\ge0$ and positive parameter, suppose nearby-parameter [global minimizers](global-minimizer.md) have uniformly bounded penalty, stay in a bounded set with [convergent subsequences](convergent-subsequence.md), and $D$ and $J$ are [sequentially lower semicontinuous](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md) in the chosen [topology](topology-split.md). Their optimality inequalities and lower semicontinuity show that every subsequential limit minimizes the limiting objective. Uniqueness makes the regularized solution [continuous](continuous-function.md) in the parameter. Existence alone does not make an arbitrary selection among multiple [global minimizers](global-minimizer.md) [continuous](continuous-function.md).
