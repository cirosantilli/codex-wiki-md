# Hackenbush

↑ **Parent:** [Partizan game](partizan-game.md)

A [partizan game](partizan-game.md) played on a graph attached to the ground. Left removes blue edges and Right removes red edges; after a removal, all edges disconnected from the ground disappear. The player with no legal move loses under the [normal-play convention](normal-play-convention.md).
