# Absolute monomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Split monomorphism](split-monomorphism.md)

An absolute monomorphism is a morphism sent to a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) by every functor out of its category. The absolute monomorphisms are exactly the [split monomorphisms](split-monomorphism.md).
