# Compound Poisson distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_Poisson_distribution)

The law of a sum of independent identically distributed claims with an independent [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) count of parameter $\lambda$. If the claim [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is $M_X$, the aggregate transform is $\exp(\lambda(M_X(r)-1))$ wherever finite. It has an atom at zero when the claims are positive. This is the fixed-time law of a [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md).
