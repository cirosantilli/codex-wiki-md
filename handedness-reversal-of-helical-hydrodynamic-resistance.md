# Handedness reversal of helical hydrodynamic resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Axial resistance matrix of a slender helix](axial-resistance-matrix-of-a-slender-helix.md)

Mirroring the handedness of a [helix](helix.md) reverses its axial translation-rotation coupling coefficients while preserving its pure translation and rotation resistances. With $\mathbf t=\cos\theta\mathbf e_z-\sin\theta\mathbf e_\varphi$ for a left-handed [helix](helix.md) and the force-on-body convention, $B=C=\ell a(\xi_\perp-\xi_\parallel)\sin\theta\cos\theta$.
