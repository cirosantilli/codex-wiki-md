# Neutrino-antineutrino DIS sea momentum ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md)

For an [isoscalar target](isoscalar-target.md) in the leading massless quark-parton approximation, integrate the two [helicity](helicity.md) weights over [inelasticity](inelasticity.md): $\int_0^1dy=1$ and $\int_0^1(1-y)^2dy=1/3$. Then $R=\sigma_{\bar\nu}/\sigma_\nu=(Q/3+\bar Q)/(Q+\bar Q/3)$, where $Q=\int xq(x)dx$ and $\bar Q=\int x\bar q(x)dx$. Inversion gives the displayed ratio. These are effective weak-flavour [momentum](momentum-split.md) fractions; the data do not determine unweighted [parton](parton.md) number integrals.
