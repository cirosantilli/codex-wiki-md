# Biconjugation as closed convexification

↑ **Parent:** [Fenchel-Moreau theorem](fenchel-moreau-theorem.md)

For a proper extended-real [function](function-split.md) with an [affine minorant](affine-minorant.md), [biconjugate](biconjugate.md) gives its greatest lower-semicontinuous [convex](convex-function.md) minorant. Geometric separation of its [epigraph](epigraph.md) turns containing [closed half-spaces](closed-half-space.md) into affine lower bounds. Vertical half-spaces are recovered by adding multiples of a domain-separating inequality to one fixed affine minorant.
