# Parity transformation of a Dirac bilinear

↑ **Parent:** [Parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md)

For [parity](parity.md) acting as $\psi_P(x)=\gamma^0\psi(x_P)$, the [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md) transforms as $\bar\psi_P(x)=\bar\psi(x_P)\gamma^0$. A bilinear with matrix $B$ therefore transforms by $B\mapsto\gamma^0B\gamma^0$, with its fields evaluated at $x_P$. Since $(\gamma^0)^2=1$ and the [chirality matrix](chirality-matrix.md) anticommutes with $\gamma^0$, the scalar bilinear is even and the [pseudoscalar](pseudoscalar.md) bilinear is odd. The same calculation shows that [parity](parity.md) exchanges the [chiral projectors](chiral-projector.md).
