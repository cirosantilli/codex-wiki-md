# Spin connection

↑ **Parent:** [Connection 1-form](connection-1-form-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_connection)

In an [orthonormal frame](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md), the spin connection is the Lorentz connection acting on spinors. With $\gamma^{AB}=\tfrac12[\gamma^A,\gamma^B]$, its [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) is $\nabla_\mu\psi=\partial_\mu\psi+\tfrac14\omega_{\mu AB}\gamma^{AB}\psi$. The metric connection determines $\omega$ when torsion conditions are specified. A [zweibein](zweibein.md) gives its two-dimensional form; omitting it on a curved worldsheet generally destroys covariance.
