# Cell plate

↑ **Parent:** [Cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_plate)

A [cell plate](cell-plate.md) is an internal partition assembled during [plant](plant.md) [cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md) by fusion of [transport vesicles](transport-vesicle.md). It grows outward to join the parental [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md), providing daughter boundaries and material for a new [plant cell wall](plant-cell-wall.md).[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Chapter 14, The Events of M Phase, Cytokinesis and Figure 14.31: vesicle fusion, outward expansion and joining the parental plasma membrane](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/the-events-of-m-phase.md)
