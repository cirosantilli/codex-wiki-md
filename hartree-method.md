# Hartree method

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree equations](hartree-equations.md)

A variational product of one-electron orbitals produces coupled one-electron equations, each containing the electrostatic density of the other occupied orbitals. Iterating orbitals and potentials gives a [self-consistent field](self-consistent-field.md). The product omits fermionic exchange; an antisymmetric [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md) instead leads to Hartree-Fock theory.
