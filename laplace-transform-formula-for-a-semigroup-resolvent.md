# Laplace-transform formula for a semigroup resolvent

↑ **Parent:** [Hille-Yosida theorem](hille-yosida-theorem.md)

If $A$ generates a [C0-semigroup](c0-semigroup.md) satisfying $\|U(t)\|\leq Me^{\omega t}$, then for $\operatorname{Re}z>\omega$ its [resolvent operator](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) is

$$
(zI-A)^{-1}x=\int_0^\infty e^{-zt}U(t)x\,dt.
$$

Repeated differentiation gives $n!(zI-A)^{-(n+1)}x=\int_0^\infty t^ne^{-zt}U(t)x\,dt$.
