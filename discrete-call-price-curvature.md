# Discrete call-price curvature

↑ **Parent:** [European call option](european-call-option.md)

For an integer-valued underlying with [European call option](european-call-option.md) prices $C(T,K)=\mathbb E(S_T-K)^+$, the second strike difference equals $\mathbb P(S_T=K)$. It is the discrete counterpart of recovering a payoff [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) from strike derivatives.
