# Helium mass fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md)

The helium mass fraction is the fraction of a material's mass carried by [helium](helium.md) nuclei. [Hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md) raises it, whereas [core helium burning](core-helium-burning.md) lowers it while producing [carbon](carbon.md) and [oxygen](oxygen.md).
