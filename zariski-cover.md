# Zariski cover

↑ **Parent:** [Zariski topology](zariski-topology.md)

A Zariski cover of a [scheme](scheme.md) is a family of [open immersions](open-immersion.md) whose images cover its underlying [topological space](topological-space.md). Such families define the [Zariski topology](zariski-topology.md) as a [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md) on the [category](category-split.md) of [schemes](scheme.md); a cover need not be finite.
