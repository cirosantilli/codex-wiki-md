# Exponential covering of an annulus by a vertical strip

↑ **Parent:** [Complex exponential function](complex-exponential-function.md)

The complex [complex exponential function](complex-exponential-function.md) has nonzero derivative and maps the strip onto the [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md), with [fibers](fiber-of-a-function.md) given by translations by $2\pi i$. It is locally conformal, but not injective. No [biholomorphism](biholomorphism.md) from the full strip onto an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) exists because the strip is [simply connected space](simply-connected-space.md) and the [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) has a loop of nonzero [winding number](winding-number.md). The [complex exponential function](complex-exponential-function.md) is a [covering map](covering-space.md) realizing that topological distinction.
