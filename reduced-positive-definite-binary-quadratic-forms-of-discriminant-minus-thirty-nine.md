# Reduced positive definite binary quadratic forms of discriminant minus thirty-nine

↑ **Parent:** [Enumeration of reduced binary quadratic forms](enumeration-of-reduced-binary-quadratic-forms.md)

The [reduced positive definite binary quadratic form](reduced-positive-definite-binary-quadratic-form.md) bounds $|b|\leq a\leq c$ and $b^2-4ac=-39$ imply $1\leq a\leq\sqrt{13}$. Enumerating $a=1,2,3$ and using the nonnegative boundary sign convention gives the four triples $(a,b,c)=(1,1,10),(2,1,5),(2,-1,5),(3,3,4)$. Thus the number of reduced forms is four.
