# Bessel lens displacement method

↑ **Parent:** [Thin lens](thin-lens.md)

For fixed object-screen separation $L>4f$, a converging [thin lens](thin-lens.md) focuses at two positions separated by $d=\sqrt{L^2-4Lf}$. Thus $f=(L^2-d^2)/(4L)$. The method finds [focal length](focal-length.md) without needing to locate a thin lens's optical center precisely.
