# Coleman unit-measure exact sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md)

Let $V_\infty$ be the norm limit of local [principal units](principal-unit.md) in the $p$-power [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md) tower, for odd $p$. The [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md) $f_u$ gives the [Frobenius-corrected logarithm](frobenius-corrected-logarithm.md) $p^{-1}\log(f_u^p/\phi f_u)$, whose inverse [Amice transform](amice-transform.md) is supported on $\mathbb Z_p^\times$. This is the middle map in the displayed [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md); the last map is the first moment $\mu\mapsto\int x\,d\mu$. Its kernel consists of norm-compatible $p$-power [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md). The logarithmic difference equation has one resonant degree, namely degree one in $\log(1+T)$, accounting for the last term. [Complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) acts by $-1$ on both copies of $\mathbb Z_p(1)$. Taking its even part therefore identifies the local principal-unit limit in the real tower with $\mathbb Z_p[[\mathbb Z_p^\times/\{\pm1\}]]$.
