# Interior eccentricity-type mean-motion resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-motion resonance](mean-motion-resonance.md)

For an eccentric inner body and circular outer perturber near the reduced $(m+n):m$ period ratio, the leading [resonant argument](resonant-argument.md) is $\phi=(m+n)\lambda_{\rm out}-m\lambda_{\rm in}-n\varpi_{\rm in}$. Its [disturbing function](disturbing-function.md) amplitude is of order $e_{\rm in}^n$. At a mean [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md), $\phi=n(\Lambda-\varpi_{\rm in})$; at actual alignment the exact expression is $(m+n)x-my$ with $x=f_{\rm in}$ and $y=M_{\rm in}(x)$.
