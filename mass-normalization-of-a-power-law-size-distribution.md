# Mass normalization of a power-law size distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Power-law size distribution](power-law-size-distribution.md)

For spherical [planetesimals](planetesimal.md) with [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ and total-number distribution $n(D)=KD^{-\alpha}$ between $D_{\min}$ and $D_{\max}$,

$$
M_{\rm tot}=\frac{\pi\rho K}{6}\int_{D_{\min}}^{D_{\max}}D^{3-\alpha}\,dD.
$$

Thus $K=6M_{\rm tot}(4-\alpha)/[\pi\rho(D_{\max}^{4-\alpha}-D_{\min}^{4-\alpha})]$ for $\alpha\ne4$. At $\alpha=4$, use $K=6M_{\rm tot}/[\pi\rho\log(D_{\max}/D_{\min})]$. Divide $n(D)$ by belt volume to obtain the number-density distribution used in a [planetesimal collision rate](planetesimal-collision-rate.md).
