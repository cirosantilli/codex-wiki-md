<h1 id="total-variation-hellinger-relative-entropy-inequality">Total variation–Hellinger–relative entropy inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hellinger distance](hellinger-distance.md)

For the [unnormalized Hellinger distance](unnormalized-hellinger-distance.md), $\operatorname{TV}(P,Q)\leq h(P,Q)\leq\sqrt{\operatorname{KL}(P,Q)}$. Factor $|p-q|=|\sqrt p-\sqrt q|(\sqrt p+\sqrt q)$ and use the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) to bound the [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md). For the other bound, $\log t\leq t-1$ gives $p\log(p/q)\geq2(p-\sqrt{pq})$, whose integral is the squared [unnormalized Hellinger distance](unnormalized-hellinger-distance.md). Infinite [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) is allowed.
