# Velocity-switched driven oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Forced harmonic oscillator](forced-harmonic-oscillator.md)

This [damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md) receives a constant force only while moving toward increasing $x$. For $0<k<\omega$, a positive maximum $X$ evolves without forcing to a minimum $-rX$, then with forcing to the next maximum $r^2X+(1+r)a/\omega^2$, where $r=e^{-k\pi/\sqrt{\omega^2-k^2}}$. Its unique periodic maximum is $a/[\omega^2(1-r)]$ and its period is $2\pi/\sqrt{\omega^2-k^2}$. Position and velocity are continuous at switches, but acceleration has jumps. The slope $r^2<1$ of this [turning-point return map](poincare-map.md) shows attraction of nearby turning-point amplitudes.
