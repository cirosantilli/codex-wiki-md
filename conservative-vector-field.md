# Conservative vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector calculus](vector-calculus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_vector_field)

A [vector field](vector-field.md) $X$ is conservative when $X=\nabla\Phi$ for a [potential of a conservative vector field](potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) $\Phi$. On a [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) open subset of $\mathbb R^3$, every continuously differentiable [curl-free vector field](irrotational-vector-field.md) is conservative. Locally this follows from the [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) applied to the [differential one-form](one-form.md) $X_i\,dx^i$; globally, simple connectivity makes its [line integral](line-integral.md) independent of the path. The [fundamental theorem for line integrals](fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) expresses that path independence as the difference of the potential at the endpoints.
