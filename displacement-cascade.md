# Displacement cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement damage](displacement-damage.md)

A sequence of atomic recoils and local relaxation following an energetic impact in a solid. It can leave isolated [Frenkel pairs](frenkel-pair.md), clusters and regions of altered local structure, rather than one permanent defect per initial collision.
