# Rank of a permutation action

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation representation](permutation-representation.md)

The rank is the number of orbits on ordered pairs under the diagonal [group action](group-action.md). Its fixed-pair count at $g$ is $\chi(g)^2$, since the [permutation character](permutation-character.md) counts fixed points. [Burnside's lemma](burnside-s-lemma.md) identifies its average with the orbit count, while real-valuedness makes that average the [character inner product](character-inner-product.md) of $\chi$ with itself. For a transitive action on at least two points, rank two is equivalent to transitivity on distinct ordered pairs.
