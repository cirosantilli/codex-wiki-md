# First-order rate constant

↑ **Parent:** [First-order reaction](first-order-reaction.md)

The coefficient $k$ of a first-order [concentration](concentration.md) term in a [rate law](rate-law.md). Its units are inverse [time](time-in-physics.md); two reversible first-order directions contribute additively to the relaxation rate.
