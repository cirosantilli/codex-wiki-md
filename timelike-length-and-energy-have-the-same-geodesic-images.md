# Timelike length and energy have the same geodesic images

↑ **Parent:** [Unparametrized geodesic equation](unparametrized-geodesic-equation.md)

For a [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md) and timelike $v=dx/du$, put $L=\sqrt{-g(v,v)}>0$ and $\mathscr L=L^2/2$. The [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) of $\mathscr L$ give $\nabla_vv=0$, while those of $L$ give $\nabla_vv=(\dot L/L)v$. Taking $ds/du=L$ removes the latter tangential acceleration and gives a unit timelike tangent. Thus the two actions have the same geodesic images; the quadratic action singles out an [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md). They do not have the same solutions for an arbitrary fixed parameter: $t=u^2$, $\mathbf x=0$, $u>0$, in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) solves the length equation but not the energy equation. Null curves are excluded since $L=0$.
