# Half-line drift boundary kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet boundary-forcing heat-kernel formula](dirichlet-boundary-forcing-heat-kernel-formula.md)

Convolution of this kernel with [Dirichlet boundary data](dirichlet-boundary-data.md) supplies the boundary forcing for $u_t=u_{xx}+\alpha u_x$ on $x>0$. It is $-(2\partial_x+\alpha)H(x+\alpha t,t)$. For $\alpha\ge0$, its total time mass is $e^{-\alpha x}$, tending to one as $x\downarrow0$, and its mass away from zero time tends to zero. Thus the boundary value is recovered through an [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md), not by pointwise substitution $x=0$ in the kernel.
