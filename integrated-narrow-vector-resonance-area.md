# Integrated narrow vector resonance area

↑ **Parent:** [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md)

For a narrow [vector meson](vector-meson.md) formed and observed in $e^+e^-$, neglecting external [masses](mass.md) and slowly varying kinematic factors gives $\sigma_{ee}=3\pi\Gamma_{ee}^2/[M^2((E-M)^2+\Gamma^2/4)]$. Integrating the [Lorentz line shape](lorentz-line-shape.md) gives the displayed result. A normalized instrumental [convolution](convolution.md) preserves this full area, although it changes the observed peak and width. A constant uncorrected acceptance multiplies the area and must be divided out before extracting the [decay width](decay-width.md).
