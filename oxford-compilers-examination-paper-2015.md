# Oxford Compilers examination paper (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The four-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) Finals [compiler](compiler.md) examination dated Thursday 4 June 2015, Trinity Term 2015. The cover permits answers to two of three questions. Public primary-source archive: [Lawrence Attachment.zip](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `2015-compilers.pdf`. The paper concerns ordered case statements with expression-valued labels, nested procedure environments, and [common subexpression elimination](common-subexpression-elimination.md) for array swaps.
