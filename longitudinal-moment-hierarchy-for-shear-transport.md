# Longitudinal moment hierarchy for shear transport

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor dispersion](taylor-dispersion.md)

For a [passive scalar](passive-scalar.md) in a planar [shear flow](shear-flow.md) $u(y,t)$, let $m_j(y,t)=\int_{\mathbb R}x^j\chi(x,y,t)\,dx$. Integrating the [advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md) by parts in $x$ gives the displayed hierarchy for $j\geq0$, with negative-index terms omitted. Sufficient decay and finite moments justify the integrations. Insulating walls give [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) for every moment. Solving the first two equations then determines the long-time growth of the second moment without solving the full scalar field.
