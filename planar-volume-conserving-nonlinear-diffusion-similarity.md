# Planar volume-conserving nonlinear-diffusion similarity

↑ **Parent:** [Barenblatt solution](barenblatt-solution.md)

For $n,A>0$, the one-dimensional [porous medium equation](porous-medium-equation.md) $h_t=A(h^nh_x)_x$ has a volume-preserving [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) $h=[c_n(R^2-x^2)/(At)]_+^{1/n}$, where $c_n=n/[2(n+2)]$ and $R\propto t^{1/(n+2)}$. On a reflecting half-line with conserved area $M$, $R=[M(At/c_n)^{1/n}/I_n]^{n/(n+2)}$, where $I_n=\tfrac12 B(\tfrac12,1+1/n)$. For a whole symmetric release use half its total area as $M$. The [Beta function](beta-function.md) fixes normalization, while finite-front zero [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) fixes the edge condition.
