# Simultaneous triangularization of a Lie algebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie's theorem](lie-s-theorem.md)

A [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) is simultaneously upper triangular precisely when it preserves a [complete flag](complete-flag.md), with $\dim V_j=j$. A [basis](basis.md) adapted to the invariant flag makes every representing matrix upper triangular. For a complex finite-dimensional representation of a [solvable Lie algebra](solvable-lie-algebra.md), the [Lie theorem](lie-s-theorem.md) provides a common [eigenvector](eigenvector.md); iteration on the [quotient representations](quotient-representation.md) constructs the flag.
