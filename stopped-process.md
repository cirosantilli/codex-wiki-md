# Stopped process

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping time](stopping-time.md)

The stopped [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) follows $X$ until the [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $\tau$ and thereafter keeps the value $X_\tau$ when $\tau$ is finite. For a pathwise [right-continuous](right-continuous-function.md) [adapted process](adapted-process.md), the [stopped process](stopped-process.md) is adapted, by [progressive measurability](progressive-measurability.md) and measurable evaluation at $t\wedge\tau$. When $X$ is a [martingale](martingale-split.md), further stopping and integrability conditions determine whether its [stopped process](stopped-process.md) is a [martingale](martingale-split.md).
