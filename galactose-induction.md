# Galactose induction

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

In the GAL1 [gene promoter](promoter-genetics.md) system used in the [yeast cross-species complementation experiment](yeast-cross-species-complementation-experiment.md), the inducing [sugar](sugar.md) galactose promotes [gene expression](gene-expression.md) from the engineered construct, whereas [glucose](glucose.md) provides a noninducing comparison. The comparison concerns an imposed regulatory system, not the donor [species](species.md)’ native regulation.
