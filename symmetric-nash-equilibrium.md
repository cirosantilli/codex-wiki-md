# Symmetric Nash equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md)

In a game invariant under permuting players, a symmetric [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md) gives every player the same strategy, which may be a [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md). Independent randomization does not require realized resource loads to be equal. When resource delays are affine, a player's expected delay depends on their own certain resource use plus the expected use by the other players; replacing all loads by unconditional total expected loads loses that own-player correction.
