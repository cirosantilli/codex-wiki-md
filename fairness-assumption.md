# Fairness assumption

↑ **Parent:** [Formal verification](formal-verification.md)

A condition selecting admissible infinite [paths in a transition system](path-in-a-transition-system.md), often to exclude executions that postpone an enabled activity forever. The notation $TS\models_{\mathrm{fair}}\varphi$ quantifies only over initial [paths in a transition system](path-in-a-transition-system.md) satisfying the chosen condition. For example, $GFp$ requires $p$ infinitely often, while $FGp$ requires $p$ permanently from some point onwards. Neither condition implies the other.
