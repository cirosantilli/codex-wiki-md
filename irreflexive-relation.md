# Irreflexive relation

↑ **Parent:** [Binary relation](binary-relation.md)

A [binary relation](binary-relation.md) is irreflexive when no element is related to itself. An [irreflexive relation](irreflexive-relation.md) that is also a [transitive relation](transitive-relation.md) is a [strict partial order](strict-partial-order.md). Transitivity then excludes every finite directed cycle, since a cycle would imply $R(x,x)$ at its starting element.

A [binary relation](binary-relation.md) is irreflexive exactly when it contains no diagonal pair $(x,x)$. Its negation requires at least one diagonal pair, whereas a [reflexive relation](reflexive-relation.md) requires every diagonal pair.
