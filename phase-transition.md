# Phase transition

↑ **Parent:** [Critical phenomenon](critical-phenomenon-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_transition)

A phase transition is a nonanalytic change in equilibrium behavior as a thermodynamic control parameter varies. A continuous phase transition has a diverging [correlation length](correlation-length.md) and no latent heat.
