# Grignard reagent

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

An organomagnesium reagent commonly written $\mathrm{RMgX}$, with organic group R and halide X. Its carbon group can act as a [nucleophile](nucleophile.md); actual solution structures depend on solvent and aggregation.
