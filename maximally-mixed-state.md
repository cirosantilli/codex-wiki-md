# Maximally mixed state

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix-split.md)

The maximally mixed [density operator](density-matrix-split.md) on an $N$-dimensional [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) assigns equal weights to every vector of any [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). It is invariant under every [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md). Its purity is $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_*^2)=1/N$, the minimum possible because the nonnegative [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) sum to one and obey the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). For $N>1$ it is a [mixed state](mixed-state.md); for $N=1$ it is the sole pure state. A rank-$k$ projector has probability $k/N$ in this state.
