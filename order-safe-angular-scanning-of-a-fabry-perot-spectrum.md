<h1 id="order-safe-angular-scanning-of-a-fabry-perot-spectrum">Order-safe angular scanning of a Fabry–Pérot spectrum</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fabry–Pérot interferometer](fabry-perot-interferometer.md)

Spectral resolution and order identification are separate design constraints. An instrumental width narrower than a line separation does not prevent a different [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) order from placing another [wavelength](wavelength.md) at the same angle. Choose optical thickness so the source range fits a known order interval, sufficient [finesse](finesse.md) to resolve neighbouring lines, and an angular window or order-sorting filter to exclude unwanted orders. Material dispersion and the internal/external angle conversion must be calibrated.
