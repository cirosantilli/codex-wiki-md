# Swing amplification

↑ **Parent:** [Spiral density wave](spiral-density-wave.md)

A self-gravitating disk can transiently amplify a leading disturbance as [differential rotation](differential-rotation.md) shears it into a trailing disturbance. Its strength depends on disk self-gravity, random motions and disturbance wavelength. Amplification does not by itself establish one indefinitely steady spiral.
