# One-dimensional characters factor through the abelianization

↑ **Parent:** [One-dimensional character](one-dimensional-character.md)

Every [one-dimensional character](one-dimensional-character.md) kills the [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) and therefore factors through the [abelianization](abelianization.md). Conversely, every character of the abelianization pulls back to a one-dimensional character of the group.
