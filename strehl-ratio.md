# Strehl ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Point spread function](point-spread-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strehl_ratio)

The Strehl ratio compares the peak of an observed [point spread function](point-spread-function.md) with the ideal [diffraction-limited system](diffraction-limited-system.md) peak for the same pupil and total flux. It measures how strongly [wavefront errors](wavefront-error.md) redistribute light away from the central peak.
