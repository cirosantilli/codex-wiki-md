# Polymer contour length

↑ **Parent:** [End-to-end distance of a polymer chain](end-to-end-distance-of-a-polymer-chain.md)

The path [length](length.md) along a [polymer](polymer-split.md) backbone. For $N$ links of length $a$ it is $Na$, whereas the [end-to-end distance of a polymer chain](end-to-end-distance-of-a-polymer-chain.md) cannot exceed it. A statistical segment count need not equal the chemical [degree of polymerization](degree-of-polymerization.md).
