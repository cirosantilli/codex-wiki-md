# Expander mixing lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral graph theory](spectral-graph-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expander_mixing_lemma)

For a $d$-regular undirected [graph](graph-split.md) whose normalized adjacency [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) other than the constant [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) have absolute value at most $\rho$, center the indicator vectors of $S,T$. Their constant parts give the expected term $d|S||T|/n$, and the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) on the mean-zero subspace bounds the remaining [inner product](inner-product.md). Here $e(S,T)=\mathbf1_S^TA\mathbf1_T$ counts oriented incidences, so an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) within $S\cap T$ is counted twice.
