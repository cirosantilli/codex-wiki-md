# Constant shifts of a divergence-form equation

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence-form elliptic operator](divergence-form-elliptic-operator.md)

If $Lu=\operatorname{div}F+g$ and $L$ contains $\operatorname{div}(bu)+du$, then $L(u-k)=\operatorname{div}(F-kb)+(g-kd)$. Thus shifting a [weak solution](weak-solution.md) to make it nonnegative changes the forcing unless the operator annihilates constants. This bookkeeping is essential in applying the [Weak Harnack inequality](weak-harnack-inequality.md) to upper and lower oscillations.
