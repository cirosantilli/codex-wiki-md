# Quadrupole radiation from a harmonically oscillating point mass

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrupole formula](quadrupole-formula.md)

For a [point mass](point-mass.md) moving as $z=L\cos\omega t$ along one fixed axis, the trace-free [mass quadrupole moment](mass-quadrupole-moment.md) is $mL^2\cos^2\omega t\,\operatorname{diag}(-1/3,-1/3,2/3)$. Its radiating component has twice the source [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). The [quadrupole formula](quadrupole-formula.md) gives $\langle P\rangle=16Gm^2L^4\omega^6/(15c^5)$ for this contribution in the leading slow-motion [weak-field approximation](weak-field-approximation.md). Any physical driver or support has its own quadrupole contribution; this expression is for the prescribed mass alone.
