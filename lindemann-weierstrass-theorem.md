<h1 id="lindemann-weierstrass-theorem">Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Transcendence theory](transcendence-theory.md)

For distinct [algebraic numbers](algebraic-number.md) $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_s$, the numbers $e^{\alpha_1},\ldots,e^{\alpha_s}$ are linearly independent over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$. Equivalently, exponentials of numbers linearly independent over $\mathbb Q$ are algebraically independent over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$. The special case of a nonzero algebraic exponent gives a transcendental exponential. In particular $e^{i\pi}=-1$ forces $\pi$ to be transcendental.
