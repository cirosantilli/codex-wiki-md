# Strictly upper triangular matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Upper triangular matrix](upper-triangular-matrix.md)

A [square matrix](square-matrix.md) is strictly upper triangular if every entry on or below its diagonal is zero. A product of $n$ such $n$-by-$n$ matrices vanishes, so they form a [nilpotent Lie algebra](nilpotent-lie-algebra.md) under the [commutator](commutator.md). Multiplication by an [upper triangular matrix](upper-triangular-matrix.md) preserves strict upper triangularity and zero [trace](matrix-trace.md).
