# Angular interference integral for two point scatterers

↑ **Parent:** [Two-centre scattering-length model](two-centre-scattering-length-model.md)

Taking the polar axis along the separation vector reduces the solid-angle [integral](integral-split.md) to $2\pi\int_{-1}^1e^{ikdu}du$. Thus the total [scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md) contains the [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) term $2\operatorname{Re}(c_1c_2^*)\sin(kd)/(kd)$ as well as the two self terms. This is an outgoing-angle [integral](integral-split.md) for fixed incident direction, rather than an average over the incoming beam.
