# Equibiaxial nominal tension of an incompressible sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint reaction in hyperelastic stress](constraint-reaction-in-hyperelastic-stress.md)

A thin isotropic [hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md) subject to [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md), with equal in-plane [principal stretches](principal-stretch.md) $\lambda$ has transverse stretch $\lambda^{-2}$. With unloaded transverse faces, $N_{33}=W_3+q\lambda^2=0$. Hence $N_{11}=N_{22}=W_1-\lambda^{-3}W_3$, using isotropy to set $W_1=W_2$. The derivative along the constrained path is $W_1+W_2-2\lambda^{-3}W_3$, proving the displayed formula. The two equal nominal tensions do work $2N\,d\lambda$ per reference volume, exactly the change in [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md).
