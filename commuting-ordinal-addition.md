# Commuting ordinal addition

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal addition](ordinal-addition.md)

Nonzero ordinals commute under addition precisely when they are positive finite right multiples of a common ordinal. This follows by comparing their [Cantor normal forms](cantor-normal-form.md) at the first exponent where they differ.
