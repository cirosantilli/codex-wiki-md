# Diabatic state

↑ **Parent:** [Born-Oppenheimer approximation](born-oppenheimer-approximation.md)

A basis state chosen to retain a useful electronic character, such as a localized charge arrangement, as the nuclei move. A [diabatic state](diabatic-state.md) need not diagonalize the electronic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md); off-diagonal coupling between such states produces the [adiabatic states](adiabatic-state.md).
