<h1 id="hund-s-rules">Hund's rules</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Electron configuration](electron-configuration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hund's_rules)

[Friedrich Hund](friedrich-hund.md)'s rules describe the usual ground-term ordering within an atomic [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md) in the [LS coupling](ls-coupling.md) regime. First maximize the total [spin](spin.md) $S$ subject to the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md). Among the terms with that spin, maximize total [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L$. Within the resulting term, the lowest level has $J=|L-S|$ for a less-than-half-filled open subshell and $J=L+S$ for a more-than-half-filled one. These are ordering rules, not universal replacements for diagonalizing an arbitrary atomic [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md).

For two equivalent $p$ electrons, parallel spins give $S=1$. Their spatial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) must be antisymmetric; the highest allowed orbital projection with parallel spins is $1+0=1$, giving $L=1$. A $p^2$ subshell is less than half filled, so its lowest [term symbol](term-symbol.md) is $^3P_0$, followed within that term by $^3P_1$ and $^3P_2$. Closed subshells contribute zero total $L$ and $S$.
