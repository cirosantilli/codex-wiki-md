# Caseous necrosis

↑ **Parent:** [Necrosis](necrosis.md)

A cheese-like pattern of [necrosis](necrosis.md), often associated with [tuberculous granulomas](tuberculous-granuloma.md) but not uniquely diagnostic of [tuberculosis](tuberculosis.md). Architecture, microbiology and the surrounding viable [tissue](tissue-biology.md) help establish the cause.
