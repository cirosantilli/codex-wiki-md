# Global dimension zero and split exact sequences

↑ **Parent:** [Global dimension](global-dimension.md)

If [global dimension](global-dimension.md) is zero, every [module](module-mathematics.md) is a [projective module](projective-module.md). Each [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) splits because its quotient is projective. Conversely, splitting every [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) makes each [module](module-mathematics.md) a direct summand of a [free module](free-module.md) and therefore a [projective module](projective-module.md). Splitting also makes every [module](module-mathematics.md) an [injective module](injective-module.md): for $A\hookrightarrow B$ choose a retraction $B\to A$ and compose it with any prescribed map out of $A$.
