# Radical chain reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Radical (chemistry)](radical-chemistry.md)

A [radical chain reaction](radical-chain-reaction.md) regenerates a [radical](radical-chemistry.md) during propagation. Initiation supplies [free radicals](radical-chemistry.md); propagation consumes one and produces another; termination removes chain-carrying [free radicals](radical-chemistry.md). This explains why one initiating event can lead to many product-forming [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction.md). The [chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md) article develops the corresponding growth of [polymers](polymer.md).
