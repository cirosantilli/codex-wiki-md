<h1 id="piecewise-constant-mumford-shah-problem">Piecewise-constant Mumford–Shah problem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mumford–Shah functional](mumford-shah-functional.md)

The infinite gradient-weight limit restricts to [SBV space](special-bounded-variation-space.md) [image signals](image-signal.md) with $\nabla u=0$ almost everywhere. Their energy is fidelity plus jump length, represented by constants on a [Caccioppoli partition](caccioppoli-partition.md). Internal perimeter is counted once by $\tfrac12\sum_i\operatorname{Per}(E_i;\Omega)$; adjacent equal-valued regions can be merged.
