# Hazard pointer

↑ **Parent:** [Memory reclamation](memory-reclamation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazard_pointer)

A published reader protection record used by a protocol that validates the protected [pointer](pointer-computer-programming.md) and delays [memory reclamation](memory-reclamation.md) while relevant readers may access the object. Merely copying an unprotected pointer does not supply this lifetime guarantee.
