# Weak modular form

↑ **Parent:** [Modular form](modular-form.md)

Here a weak modular form is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) satisfying the weight-$k$ transformation law, with no growth condition at any [cusp of a modular group](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md). This convention differs from weakly holomorphic modular forms, which are required to be meromorphic at the cusps. At level one, translation invariance gives a Laurent expansion on the punctured unit disc, possibly with infinitely many negative powers.
