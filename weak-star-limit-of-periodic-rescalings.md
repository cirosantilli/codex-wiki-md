# Weak-star limit of periodic rescalings

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md)

A [bounded](boundedness.md) measurable one-periodic [function](function-split.md) rescaled to frequency $j$ converges [weak-star](weak-star-topology.md) in $L^\infty(\mathbb R)$ to its period average. Subtract the average and take a [bounded](boundedness.md) periodic primitive $F$. Its [integral](integral-split.md) on $[a,b]$ after rescaling is $[F(jb)-F(ja)]/j$, which tends to zero. Sequential Banach-Alaoglu with separable predual $L^1$ then identifies every cluster point by [interval](interval-mathematics.md) [integrals](integral-split.md); uniqueness of all cluster points gives full [weak-star convergence](weak-star-topology.md). Localizing to [bounded](boundedness.md) [intervals](interval-mathematics.md) avoids falsely assuming that a [bounded](boundedness.md) cluster [function](function-split.md) is globally integrable.
