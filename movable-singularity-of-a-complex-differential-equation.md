# Movable singularity of a complex differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Complex ordinary differential equation](complex-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

A solution has a movable singularity if its location varies with the initial data. For example $w'=w^2$ has solutions $w=-1/(z-c)$, whose [pole](pole.md) moves with $c$. The [Painlevé determinateness theorem](painleve-determinateness-theorem.md) restricts movable singularities of first-order equations rational in the dependent variable to [poles](pole.md) and [algebraic branch points](algebraic-branch-point.md).
