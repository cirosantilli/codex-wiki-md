# Pyrrhotite

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

An iron sulfide with composition often written $\mathrm{Fe}_{1-x}\mathrm S$. Its structure and composition determine whether it carries strong [magnetization](magnetization.md); not all varieties behave identically.
