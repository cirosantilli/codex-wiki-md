# Bending moment

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bending_moment)

A bending moment is an internal [torque](torque.md) transmitted across a cut through a beam or [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md). For a slender linear elastic beam, $M=EI\kappa$ up to sign convention, where $E$ is [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md), $I$ the [second moment of area](second-moment-of-area.md), and $\kappa$ the centreline [curvature](curvature.md). The corresponding bending response of an [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) is described by its [bending stiffness](bending-stiffness.md) and curvature tensor.
