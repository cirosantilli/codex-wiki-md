<h1 id="painleve-iii-equation">Painlevé III equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable systems](integrable-systems-split.md)

The third Painlevé equation is the nonlinear second-order [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

$$
w''=\frac{(w')^2}{w}-\frac{w'}z
+\frac{\alpha w^2+\beta}{z}
+\gamma w^3+\frac\delta w.
$$

The substitution $w=e^{iF}$ in the [scaling reduction of the sine-Gordon equation](scaling-symmetry-of-the-sine-gordon-equation.md) gives the parameter choice $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)=(1/2,-1/2,0,0)$.
