# Matter-logarithm no-scale potential

↑ **Parent:** [No-scale supergravity](no-scale-supergravity.md)

For $s=S+\bar S>0$, $t=T+\bar T-|C|^2>0$, $K=-\log s-3\log t$ and a holomorphic [superpotential](superpotential.md) independent of $T$, the exact [F-term](f-term.md) potential is $V=e^K[s^2|D_SW|^2+(t/3)|W_C|^2]$. Inverting the mixed $T,C$ metric gives $K^{T\bar T}=t(t+|C|^2)/3$, $K^{T\bar C}=t\bar C/3$, $K^{C\bar T}=tC/3$, and $K^{C\bar C}=t/3$. Substitution of $D_TW=-3W/t$ and $D_CW=W_C+3\bar CW/t$ cancels all mixed terms, leaving $3|W|^2+(t/3)|W_C|^2$; the first term cancels the [supergravity](supergravity.md) negative term.
