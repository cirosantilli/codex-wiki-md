# Worldsheet stress-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Polyakov action](polyakov-action.md)

Varying the independent [worldsheet metric](worldsheet-metric.md) gives $T_{ab}=T[\partial_aX\cdot\partial_bX-\tfrac12\gamma_{ab}\gamma^{cd}\partial_cX\cdot\partial_dX]$. Its classical vanishing is the metric equation and produces the [Virasoro constraints](virasoro-constraint.md).
