# Indirect revealed preference

↑ **Parent:** [Revealed preference](revealed-preference.md)

The weak [indirect revealed preference](indirect-revealed-preference.md) is the [reflexive closure](reflexive-closure.md) of the [transitive closure of a relation](transitive-closure-relation.md) of direct revealed comparisons. Equivalently, it consists of finite chains, including chains of length zero. The diagonal convention is essential if the relation is to be an intersection of complete weak [preference relations](preference-relation.md) on all alternatives.
