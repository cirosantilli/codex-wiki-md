# Opposite parity

↑ **Parent:** [Parity (mathematics)](parity-mathematics.md)

Two [integers](integer-split.md) have [opposite parity](opposite-parity.md) when one is an [even number](even-number.md) and the other is an [odd number](odd-number.md). Their [sum](sum.md) is then an [odd number](odd-number.md).
