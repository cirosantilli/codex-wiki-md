# Stationary radiation density

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrostatic equilibrium along a Killing vector](hydrostatic-equilibrium-along-a-killing-vector.md)

The [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) of isotropic [blackbody radiation](black-body-radiation.md) is $p=\rho/3$. The [barotropic hydrostatic first integral](barotropic-hydrostatic-first-integral.md) gives $\rho N^4=C$. The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) $\rho\propto T^4$ then yields the [Tolman–Ehrenfest relation](tolman-ehrenfest-relation.md) $TN=\mathrm{constant}$. Here $N=\sqrt{|K^aK_a|}$ for the stationary timelike [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md); its normalization only changes the constants.
