# Static Poynting flow

↑ **Parent:** [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md)

Time-independent crossed electric and magnetic fields can carry a nonzero energy flux $\mathbf S=\mathbf E\times\mathbf H$. A steady coaxial circuit is an example: radial electric and azimuthal magnetic fields carry energy axially from source to load. Static [energy density](energy-density.md) does not imply zero flux.
