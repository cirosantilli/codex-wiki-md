# Bessel functions of order one half

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel function of the first kind](bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md)

For $x>0$, the [Bessel differential equation](bessel-differential-equation.md) of order $1/2$ has independent solutions $x^{-1/2}\sin x$ and $x^{-1/2}\cos x$. Substituting $y=x^{-1/2}u$ reduces it to $u''+u=0$. The normalization of the [Bessel function of the first kind](bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md) gives the displayed formulas for orders $1/2$ and $-1/2$. The [Frobenius method](frobenius-method.md) obtains the same pair from exponents $1/2$ and $-1/2$: their even-coefficient series reduce to the sine and cosine [power series](power-series.md). In the negative-exponent recurrence, the free coefficient at the next exponent can add the positive-exponent solution, an example of an [undetermined coefficient at Frobenius resonance](undetermined-coefficient-at-frobenius-resonance.md).
