# Solar corona

↑ **Parent:** [Sun](sun.md)

The solar corona is the [Sun](sun.md)'s hot, dilute outer atmosphere. Its optically thin atomic lines diagnose temperature, density and composition through [collisional-radiative models](collisional-radiative-model.md) and ionization balance. Its plasma extends into the [solar wind](solar-wind.md); the [coronal approximation](coronal-approximation.md) describes useful low-density equilibrium limits rather than every coronal condition.
