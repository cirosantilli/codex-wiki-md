# Calibrated geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calibrated_geometry)

Calibrated geometry constructs volume-minimizing submanifolds through closed [differential forms](differential-form-split.md) with a tangent-plane bound. It gives an algebraic sufficient condition for solving the nonlinear [minimal surface](minimal-surface.md) problem.
