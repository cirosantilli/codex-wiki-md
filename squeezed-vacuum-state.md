# Squeezed vacuum state

↑ **Parent:** [Single-mode squeeze operator](single-mode-squeeze-operator.md)

The squeezed vacuum is the zero-displacement [squeezed coherent state](squeezed-coherent-state.md) $|\gamma\rangle=S(\gamma)|0\rangle$. Its position and momentum variances are rescaled in opposite directions,

$$
(\Delta X)^2=\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}e^{-2\gamma},
\qquad
(\Delta P)^2=\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}e^{2\gamma},
$$

so it remains an [minimum-uncertainty state](equality-case-of-the-heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) with $\Delta X\Delta P=\hbar/2$.
