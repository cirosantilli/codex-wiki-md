# Flavor-nonsinglet axial Ward identity with equal quark masses

↑ **Parent:** [Nonsinglet axial charge algebra of quark densities](nonsinglet-axial-charge-algebra-of-quark-densities.md)

For the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) with two equal [quark masses](quark-mass.md) $m$, the equations for $q$ and $\bar q$ give the divergence of the nonsinglet [axial current](axial-current.md) as $im\bar q\gamma_5\tau_aq=mP_a$. The color [chiral anomaly](chiral-anomaly.md) is absent in this nonsinglet identity because the [quark flavor](quark-flavor.md) generator is traceless. Equivalently, the [mass](mass.md) term in the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) is $m\int S\,d^3x$, and the [nonsinglet axial charge algebra of quark densities](nonsinglet-axial-charge-algebra-of-quark-densities.md) gives $\dot Q_{5,a}=m\int P_a\,d^3x$.
