<h1 id="mobius-transformations-from-the-real-axis-to-the-unit-circle">Möbius transformations from the real axis to the unit circle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md)

A nonconstant [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) mapping the [real line](real-line.md) into the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) has the displayed form with $|\lambda|=1$ and $k$ nonreal. Indeed $|ax+b|^2=|cx+d|^2$ for real $x$ yields $|a|=|c|\ne0$, $|b/a|=|d/c|$ and $\operatorname{Re}(b/a)=\operatorname{Re}(d/c)$. Thus $d/c$ is $b/a$ or its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md); the first possibility makes the map constant, so the second is necessary. Conjugate numerator and denominator have equal modulus on the [real line](real-line.md), proving the converse as well.
