# First massive level of a chiral RNS sector

↑ **Parent:** [Type II superstring mass spectrum](type-ii-superstring-mass-spectrum.md)

After the [GSO projection](gso-projection.md), the first positive shifted level is one: $N_{\rm NS}=3/2$ and $N_R=1$. In a closed-string mass convention it corresponds to $M^2=4/\alpha'$. The transverse oscillator basis has 128 NS states and 128 R states. They form the bosonic and fermionic pieces of a massive [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) multiplet.
