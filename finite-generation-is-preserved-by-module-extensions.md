# Finite generation is preserved by module extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md)

If a [submodule](submodule.md) $N\subseteq M$ and the [quotient module](quotient-module.md) $M/N$ are [finitely generated modules](finitely-generated-module.md), then $M$ is a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md). Choose finitely many generators of $N$ and lift finitely many generators of $M/N$. For any $m\in M$, subtract an $R$-linear combination of the lifts to obtain an element of $N$, then express this using the generators of $N$. The union of the two finite lists generates $M$.
