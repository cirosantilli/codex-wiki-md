# Cyclotomic construction of a Steiner 2-design

↑ **Parent:** [Difference family for a Steiner 2-design](difference-family-for-a-steiner-2-design.md)

Put $m=\binom{k}{2}$, take odd $q\equiv1\pmod{2m}$, and let $H$ be the index-$m$ multiplicative [subgroup](subgroup.md). If a $k$-set $A$ has its unordered pair differences in distinct $H$-cosets, choose $S\subset H$ containing exactly one element from each pair $\{h,-h\}$. Then $\{sA:s\in S\}$ is a [difference family for a Steiner 2-design](difference-family-for-a-steiner-2-design.md). Indeed one unordered pair supplies each [coset](coset.md), and its two difference orientations multiplied by $S$ cover that [coset](coset.md) once. Translations yield a $2$-$(q,k,1)$ [Steiner system](steiner-system.md). The [cyclotomic pattern extension by a second-moment bound](cyclotomic-pattern-extension-by-a-second-moment-bound.md) supplies suitable $A$ for all sufficiently large $q$ in this progression.
