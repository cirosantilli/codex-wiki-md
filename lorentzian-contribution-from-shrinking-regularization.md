# Lorentzian contribution from shrinking regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Endpoint transition of a regularized interior pole](endpoint-transition-of-a-regularized-interior-pole.md)

If $h$ has a simple interior zero $u_0$ and $a\to0^+$, the [inner variable](inner-variable.md) $v=h'(u_0)(u-u_0)/a$ turns the local integral into $(1/|h'(u_0)|)\int_{\mathbb R}(1+v^2)^{-1}dv$. Thus a peak with vanishing width can retain finite area. Slowly varying positive regularization factors cancel between peak height and width when the numerator has the matching factor. An ordinary [outer expansion](outer-expansion.md) can miss this contribution even though the peak is exponentially narrow.
