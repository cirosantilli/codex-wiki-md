# Upward absoluteness of countability

↑ **Parent:** [Set-theoretic absoluteness](set-theoretic-absoluteness.md)

If a smaller transitive model has a function witnessing that $x$ is a [countable set](countable-set.md), the same witness exists in every larger transitive model. Downward absoluteness can fail because a larger model may contain a new enumeration of $x$.
