# Composite operator

↑ **Parent:** [Local quantum field operator](local-quantum-field-operator.md)

A composite operator is a renormalized local product of quantum fields and their derivatives. Operators with compatible quantum numbers can undergo [composite operator mixing](composite-operator-mixing.md), and products at coincident insertion points can need [contact terms in a correlation function](contact-term-in-a-correlation-function.md).
