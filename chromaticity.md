# Chromaticity

↑ **Parent:** [Beam optics](beam-optics.md)

The [derivative](derivative.md) of transverse betatron tune $Q$ with respect to relative [momentum](momentum-split.md) error $\delta$. For a thin-lens [FODO cell](fodo-cell.md) with fixed quadrupole fields, natural cell chromaticity is $-\tan(\mu/2)/\pi$ on the stable phase-advance branch. Zero corrected chromaticity requires additional momentum-dependent focusing.
