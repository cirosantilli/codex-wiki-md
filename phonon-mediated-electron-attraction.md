# Phonon-mediated electron attraction

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-phonon interaction](electron-phonon-interaction.md)

For [electron](electron.md) [energy](energy-split.md) transfer $\Delta$ and [phonon](phonon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) $\Omega_q=\hbar\omega_q$, the symmetrized pair amplitude is $V_{\rm eff}=2|M_q|^2\Omega_q/(\Delta^2-\Omega_q^2)$. It is attractive for $|\Delta|<\Omega_q$. This interaction is retarded, and screened Coulomb repulsion must also be considered.
