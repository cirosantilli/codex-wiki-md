# Conormal injectivity for a generically smooth Cartier divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Conormal sheaf](conormal-sheaf.md)

Let $W$ be an integral [effective Cartier divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md) in an integral [variety](algebraic-variety.md) $V$ over a [perfect field](perfect-field.md), and suppose $W$ is not contained in the singular locus of $V$. On a dense open subset both [varieties](algebraic-variety.md) are smooth. The [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md) of $W$ there has codimension one in that of $V$, so the differential of the local defining equation is nonzero. Thus the [conormal sheaf](conormal-sheaf.md) map is injective generically. Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is a subsheaf of a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) on the integral [variety](algebraic-variety.md) $W$ and is therefore a [torsion-free sheaf](torsion-free-sheaf.md); generic vanishing implies zero everywhere. Right exactness is the [Conormal exact sequence for Kähler differentials](conormal-exact-sequence-for-kahler-differentials.md).
