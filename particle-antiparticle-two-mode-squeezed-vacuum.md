# Particle-antiparticle two-mode squeezed vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Multimode squeezed vacuum](multimode-squeezed-vacuum.md)

For a pair of distinct bosonic [particle](particle-physics.md) and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) modes, the normalized state obeying $(a+qb^\dagger)|\psi\rangle=(b+qa^\dagger)|\psi\rangle=0$ is $\sqrt{1-|q|^2}\sum_{n\geq0}(-q)^n|n,n\rangle$, for $|q|<1$. It contains equal occupations and is generated by a pair-creation exponential. Infinite products require a separate implementability or regulator check.
