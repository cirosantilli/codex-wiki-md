# Strontium

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical element](chemical-element.md)

An alkaline-earth element whose divalent [ions](ion.md) can substitute for [calcium](calcium.md) in suitable crystal hosts. Radioactive [strontium](strontium.md) [isotopes](isotope.md) are relevant fission-product constituents.
