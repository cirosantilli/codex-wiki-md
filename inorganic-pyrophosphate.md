# Inorganic pyrophosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[inorganic pyrophosphate](inorganic-pyrophosphate.md) contains two phosphate units joined by a phosphoanhydride linkage. Its hydrolysis can favor net nucleotide-sugar activation when the relevant reactions are coupled.
