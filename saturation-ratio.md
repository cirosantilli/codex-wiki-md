# Saturation ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric supersaturation](atmospheric-supersaturation.md)

The ambient [water](water.md) [vapor](vapor.md) [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md) divided by the equilibrium [saturation pressure](vapor-pressure.md) over a stated reference surface at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md). Relative to flat pure [water](water.md), [atmospheric supersaturation](atmospheric-supersaturation.md) is $s=S-1$. A curved solute-containing [cloud droplet](cloud-droplet.md) generally has a different equilibrium [saturation ratio](saturation-ratio.md) from unity.
