# Straight line

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)

A straight line in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) is the set $L=\{x_0+tv:t\in\mathbb R\}$, for a fixed point $x_0$ and nonzero direction [vector](vector.md) $v$. Two distinct points determine one such line.
