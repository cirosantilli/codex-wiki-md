# C-centered orthorhombic lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Orthorhombic crystal system](orthorhombic-crystal-system.md)

A [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) with three perpendicular conventional axes and [lattice points](lattice-point.md) at $(0,0,0)$ and $(1/2,1/2,0)$. A translation by half a diagonal of an $ab$ face preserves the complete [crystal motif](crystal-motif.md); apparent centering of unlabelled molecular centers alone is insufficient.
