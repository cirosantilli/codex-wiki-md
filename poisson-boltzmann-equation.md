# Poisson-Boltzmann equation

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson–Boltzmann_equation)

A mean-field coupling of the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) to an ideal [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) of mobile [ions](ion.md). In [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md) with uniform dielectric constant $\epsilon$, $\nabla^2\phi=-(4\pi/\epsilon)[\rho_f+\sum_i q_i n_{i,0}e^{-\beta q_i\phi}]$. The potential reference and chemical potentials determine the normalization factors. It neglects discrete-ion correlations and assumes local [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md). A counterion-only model is not the same equation as a symmetric salt reservoir.
