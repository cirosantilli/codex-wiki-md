# Nonuniqueness of spherical galactic distribution functions

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md)

The density and self-consistent potential of a spherical galaxy need not determine its [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md). Different angular-momentum dependence can reproduce the same density with different [velocity-anisotropy parameters](velocity-anisotropy-parameter.md). An isotropic [Plummer model](plummer-model.md) and its positive [anisotropic Plummer distribution with unit anisotropy radius](anisotropic-plummer-distribution-with-unit-anisotropy-radius.md) provide an explicit example. A chosen full [augmented density](augmented-stellar-density.md) is additional information.
