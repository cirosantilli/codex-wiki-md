# Weighted inclusion-exclusion principle

↑ **Parent:** [Inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md)

For finite [subsets](subset.md) $A_i$ of a finite ambient [set](set-split.md) $X$, the [indicator function](indicator-function.md) of their complement is $\prod_i(1-\mathbf1_{A_i})$. Expanding this finite product and summing it against any weight $f:X\to\mathbb R$ proves the weighted formula. The empty intersection is $X$. Negative weights are allowed; finiteness removes every convergence issue.
