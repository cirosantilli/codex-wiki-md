# Upper galaxy mass from gas cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Cooling criterion for galaxy formation](cooling-criterion-for-galaxy-formation.md)

In simple hierarchical models, increasing system mass raises the [virial temperature](virial-temperature.md) and can carry primordial gas beyond its efficient atomic line-cooling interval. At assembly densities, the cooling-time constraint then distinguishes galaxy-sized condensations from larger hot groups or clusters. The resulting characteristic mass is approximate, depends on composition and structure, and does not prohibit subsequent mergers of already formed stellar systems.
