# Kleisli comparison functor

↑ **Parent:** [Kleisli category](kleisli-category.md)

If $L\dashv R$ induces a [monad](monad.md) $T=RL$, the comparison maps $A$ to $LA$ and maps a [Kleisli category](kleisli-category.md) arrow $f:A\to TB$ to $\varepsilon_{LB}L(f)$. It is [full and faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md), by the adjunction bijection $\mathcal D(LA,LB)\cong\mathcal C(A,TB)$. Consequently it is part of an [equivalence of categories](equivalence-of-categories.md) precisely when every object of $\mathcal D$ is isomorphic to some $LA$.
