# Proximity-effect estimator of the ionizing background

↑ **Parent:** [Proximity effect (astrophysics)](proximity-effect-astrophysics.md)

In [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md) at fixed gas density and temperature, the extra [quasar](quasar.md) [photoionization rate](photoionization-rate.md) reduces neutral-hydrogen [column density](column-density.md) by the displayed factor. Its local rate is calculated from the quasar flux, while the isotropic background rate is $\Gamma_{\rm bg}=4\pi\int J_\nu\sigma_{\rm HI}(\nu)/(h\nu)\,d\nu$. Fitting the absorption suppression versus proper distance constrains the [cosmic ionizing background](cosmic-ionizing-background.md). For an unperturbed column-density distribution proportional to $N^{-p}$ with $p>1$, the line abundance above a fixed threshold is reduced by $(1+\omega)^{1-p}$. Density enhancement, quasar age, anisotropy and redshift errors must be distinguished from this ionization effect.
