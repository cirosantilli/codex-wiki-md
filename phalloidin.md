# Phalloidin

↑ **Parent:** [Actin filament](actin-filament.md)

An actin-binding molecule used in fluorescent conjugates to visualize [filamentous actin](actin-filament.md). Weak staining must be distinguished from background, and staining accessibility can affect inference about residual [polymer](polymer-split.md).
