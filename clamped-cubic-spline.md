# Clamped cubic spline

↑ **Parent:** [Cubic spline](cubic-spline.md)

A [clamped cubic spline](clamped-cubic-spline.md) is a [cubic spline](cubic-spline.md) whose endpoint first [derivatives](derivative.md) are prescribed. A [clamped cubic spline](clamped-cubic-spline.md) [interpolant](interpolant.md) additionally matches prescribed values at its [spline knots](spline-knot.md). Distinct [spline knots](spline-knot.md), arbitrary nodal values and arbitrary endpoint slopes determine one such [interpolant](interpolant.md). The [minimum roughness property of a clamped cubic spline](minimum-roughness-property-of-a-clamped-cubic-spline.md) proves uniqueness. Unlike the [natural cubic spline](natural-cubic-spline.md), this construction prescribes endpoint slopes rather than zero endpoint [second derivatives](second-derivative.md).
