# Nonlinear length

↑ **Parent:** [Self-phase modulation](self-phase-modulation.md)

The distance for an order-one [self-phase modulation](self-phase-modulation.md) at peak [power](power.md) $P$ in a lossless Kerr envelope model. With attenuation, the phase accumulates over an effective interaction length instead.
