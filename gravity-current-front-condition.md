# Gravity-current front condition

↑ **Parent:** [Froude number](froude-number.md)

A common high-[Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) closure for a deep-ambient [gravity current](gravity-current.md) is $\dot L=\operatorname{Fr}\sqrt{g'h}$, where $L$ is front position and the order-one [Froude number](froude-number.md) depends on the chosen front model.
