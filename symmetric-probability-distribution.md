# Symmetric probability distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_probability_distribution)

A real probability distribution is symmetric about $m$ when $X-m$ and $m-X$ have the same distribution. A centered [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) is symmetric about zero.
