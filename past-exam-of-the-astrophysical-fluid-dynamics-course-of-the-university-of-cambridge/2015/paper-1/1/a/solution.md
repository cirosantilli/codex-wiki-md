<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a steady, spherically symmetric radial inflow, [conservation of mass](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) makes the [mass accretion rate](../../../../../mass-accretion-rate.md) independent of radius:

$$
\dot M=4\pi r^2\rho |v_r|.
$$

Atomic [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) gives $\rho\simeq m_Hn$. The Newtonian [free-fall speed](../../../../../free-fall-speed.md) from rest at infinity is $|v_r|=\sqrt{2GM/r}$, obtained by setting the specific mechanical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $v_r^2/2-GM/r$ to zero. Hence the [spherical free-fall accretion rate](../../../../../spherical-free-fall-accretion-rate.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dot M=4\pi m_Hn\sqrt{2GM}\,r^{3/2}.}
$$

Using the given values gives $\rho\simeq1.67\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, $|v_r|\simeq2.98\times10^5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, and

$$
\boxed{\dot M\simeq5.65\times10^{18}\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}
\simeq8.96\times10^{-5}\,M_\odot\,\mathrm{yr^{-1}}.}
$$

The area factor assumes a full spherical inflow with negligible angular momentum. With only a local [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) and speed, an arbitrary nonspherical inflow's total rate is not fixed; one instead integrates $-\rho\mathbf v\cdot d\mathbf S$ over the inflow surface. This is a flux estimate, rather than the pressure-selected [Bondi accretion](../../../../../bondi-accretion.md) solution.
