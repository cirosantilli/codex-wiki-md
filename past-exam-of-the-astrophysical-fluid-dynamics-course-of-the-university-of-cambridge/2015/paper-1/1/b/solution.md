<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let all [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) amplitudes refer to the same positive normal direction, from medium 1 into medium 2. For a right-going linear [sound wave](../../../../../acoustic-wave.md), its [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) amplitude is $p'=Zu'$ with [acoustic impedance](../../../../../acoustic-impedance.md) $Z=\rho c_s$; for a left-going wave it is $p'=-Zu'$. Continuity of normal [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) at the interface gives

$$
u_i+u_r=u_t,\qquad Z_1(u_i-u_r)=Z_2u_t.
$$

Eliminating $u_t$ yields the [normal-incidence acoustic velocity reflection coefficient](../../../../../normal-incidence-acoustic-velocity-reflection-coefficient.md)

$$
\frac{u_r}{u_i}=\frac{Z_1-Z_2}{Z_1+Z_2}.
$$

The common [adiabatic index](../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) and mean particle mass $\bar m$ give $c_{s,j}=\sqrt{\gamma k_BT_j/\bar m}$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{u_r}{u_i}=\frac{\rho_1\sqrt{T_1}-\rho_2\sqrt{T_2}}
{\rho_1\sqrt{T_1}+\rho_2\sqrt{T_2}}.}
$$

A negative coefficient reverses the reflected [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) perturbation's phase in this fixed coordinate system. The reflected pressure coefficient has the opposite sign. Pressure balance also gives $\rho_1T_1=\rho_2T_2$, so the impedance ratio can equivalently be written $Z_2/Z_1=\sqrt{\rho_2/\rho_1}$. These are normal-incidence, small-amplitude results without a surface mass or tension.
