<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a steady spherical wind, [conservation of mass](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives

$$
\dot M_w=4\pi r^2\rho(r)v(r)
=4\pi R_e^2m_Hn_e\sqrt{\frac{2GM_*}{R_e}}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\dot M_w=4\pi m_Hn_e\sqrt{2GM_*}\,R_e^{3/2}
\simeq7.99\times10^9\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}
\simeq1.27\times10^{-13}M_\odot\,\mathrm{yr^{-1}}.}
$$

Here $n_e$ denotes the given number density at the escape radius, rather than electron density; the composition is atomic [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md). The reference speed is $v_e\simeq4.22\times10^4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. This boundary flux remains fixed even if pressure changes the subsequent radial velocity profile.
