<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A uniform conducting fluid supports three propagating [magnetohydrodynamic wave](../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-wave.md) branches. [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../../ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md) couples fluid [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) to the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) through the induction equation; the [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) then supplies magnetic tension and magnetic pressure restoring forces. Let $\theta$ be the angle between [wave vector](../../../../../wavevector.md) $\mathbf k$ and background field $\mathbf B_0$, and define [Alfvén speed](../../../../../alfven-speed.md) $v_A=B_0/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho_0}$ and [adiabatic sound speed](../../../../../adiabatic-sound-speed.md) $c_s^2=\gamma p_0/\rho_0$.

The shear [Alfvén wave](../../../../../alfven-wave.md) has

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=k^2v_A^2\cos^2\theta.}
$$

Its [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) perturbation is perpendicular to both $\mathbf k$ and $\mathbf B_0$. It bends field lines without first-order compression, so magnetic tension is the restoring force. Its [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) follows the background field; the displayed phase speed along $\mathbf k$ is not the full group speed.

The [fast magnetosonic wave](../../../../../fast-magnetosonic-wave.md) and [slow magnetosonic wave](../../../../../slow-magnetosonic-wave.md) involve coupled compression and magnetic perturbations, with displacement in the plane of $\mathbf k,\mathbf B_0$:

$$
\boxed{\frac{\omega_{f,s}^2}{k^2}=\frac12\left[c_s^2+v_A^2
\pm\sqrt{(c_s^2+v_A^2)^2-4c_s^2v_A^2\cos^2\theta}\right].}
$$

The plus sign is the fast branch. Parallel propagation gives characteristic speeds $c_s,v_A$; perpendicular propagation gives fast speed $\sqrt{c_s^2+v_A^2}$ while the other two phase frequencies vanish. Degenerate directions require care in assigning polarizations, rather than claiming three distinct nonzero frequencies everywhere.

These [waves](../../../../../wave-split.md) transport [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) through magnetized stellar atmospheres and astrophysical plasmas. Their restoring mechanisms distinguish them from ordinary acoustic waves. Resistivity, viscosity, ion-neutral friction and kinetic effects introduce damping or change the model; an ideal undamped [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) alone does not calculate coronal heating.
