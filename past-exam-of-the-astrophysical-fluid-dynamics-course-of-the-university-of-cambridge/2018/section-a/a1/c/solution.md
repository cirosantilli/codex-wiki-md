<h1 id="section-a/a1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a plane-parallel slab, all equilibrium quantities depend only on height $z$. The vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) equation and the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](../../../../../poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) are

$$
\frac{dp}{dz}=-\rho\frac{d\psi}{dz},\qquad
\frac{d^2\psi}{dz^2}=4\pi G\rho.
$$

With the [polytropic equation of state](../../../../../polytropic-equation-of-state.md) $p=K\rho^2$ and constant $K$,

$$
2K\rho\frac{d\rho}{dz}=-\rho\frac{d\psi}{dz}.
$$

In the gas interior, where $\rho>0$, divide by $\rho$ and differentiate once:

$$
2K\frac{d^2\rho}{dz^2}=-\frac{d^2\psi}{dz^2}=-4\pi G\rho.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\frac{d^2\rho}{dz^2}+\frac{2\pi G}{K}\rho=0.}
$$

The coefficient $2\pi G/K$ has units inverse square metres. The [index-one plane-parallel polytrope](../../../../../index-one-plane-parallel-polytrope.md) illustrates the resulting structure: symmetry about $z=0$ gives $\rho(z)=\rho_c\cos(kz)$, with $k=\sqrt{2\pi G/K}$. A slab bounded by vacuum ends at its first zero, $|z|=\pi/(2k)$; the negative continuation of the cosine is not physical [mass density](../../../../../density.md).
