<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) of fixed composition, the central [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) satisfies $T_c\propto p_c/\rho_c=K\rho_c^{1/n}$. Keeping $T_c$ fixed therefore requires $K\propto\rho_c^{-1/n}$, rather than keeping the [polytropic constant](../../../../../polytropic-constant.md) fixed. Substitution in the [Lane-Emden equation](../../../../../lane-emden-equation.md) scalings gives

$$
M\propto\rho_c^{(3/n-1)/2}\rho_c^{-3/(2n)}=\rho_c^{-1/2},\qquad
R\propto\rho_c^{(1/n-1)/2}\rho_c^{-1/(2n)}=\rho_c^{-1/2}.
$$

Consequently the [homologous polytropes at fixed central temperature](../../../../../homologous-polytropes-at-fixed-central-temperature.md) obey

$$
\boxed{M\propto R.}
$$

The [polytropic index](../../../../../polytropic-index.md) and mean particle [mass](../../../../../mass.md) must be common to this homologous sequence: varying either changes the dimensionless structural coefficient. The fixed-central-temperature assumption is the idealization being used here, rather than a universal property of real [stars](../../../../../star.md).
