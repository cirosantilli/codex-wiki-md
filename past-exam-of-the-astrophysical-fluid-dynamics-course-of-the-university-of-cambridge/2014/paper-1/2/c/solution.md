<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A local thermal imbalance can amplify a gas perturbation even without an initial displacement of an interface. Let $\mathcal L(\rho,T)$ denote net cooling per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md), including heating with a negative sign, and suppose $\mathcal L(\rho_0,T_0)=0$. When the [sound-crossing time](../../../../../sound-crossing-time.md) is short compared with the thermal evolution time, [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) equilibrates and an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) perturbation is approximately isobaric. The linearized thermal equation is

$$
c_p\frac{d\delta T}{dt}
=-\left(\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial T}\right)_p\delta T.
$$

Thus a negative constant-pressure derivative produces [isobaric thermal instability](../../../../../isobaric-thermal-instability.md): a cooler parcel experiences a net loss that cools it further, while a warmer parcel heats further. For example, [isobaric thermal stability of a heated ideal gas](../../../../../isobaric-thermal-stability-of-a-heated-ideal-gas.md) with $\mathcal L=A\rho T^\alpha-H$ gives $\sigma=(1-\alpha)H/(c_pT_0)$ and instability for $\alpha<1$. Different heating laws and fixed-density perturbations give different criteria; [thermal conduction](../../../../../thermal-conduction.md) damps sufficiently short-scale [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) contrasts. Such feedback helps a gas separate into denser cool and rarer warm regions when stable thermal branches exist.

The [Rayleigh-Taylor instability](../../../../../rayleigh-taylor-instability.md) instead releases [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../gravitational-energy.md) when a heavier fluid lies above a lighter fluid in a downward gravitational field. For two inviscid incompressible half-spaces with no [surface tension](../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) or [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), a horizontal perturbation of [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md) $k$ has

$$
\sigma^2=\mathcal A gk,\qquad
\mathcal A=\frac{\rho_h-\rho_l}{\rho_h+\rho_l}>0.
$$

The [Atwood number](../../../../../atwood-number.md) measures the density contrast. A descending heavy-fluid finger and an ascending light-fluid bubble both lower the total [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../gravitational-energy.md), increasing the original displacement. Interchanging the layers gives an oscillatory stable interface. An accelerating or decelerating astrophysical shell has an analogous effective acceleration, allowing mixing at suitable ejecta interfaces in a [supernova remnant](../../../../../supernova-remnant.md). Finite interface width, [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md), [surface tension](../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) and [magnetic tension](../../../../../magnetic-tension.md) modify the short-wavelength behavior; the ideal sharp-interface formula does not select a finite fastest-growing wavelength.
