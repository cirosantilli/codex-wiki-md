<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An [accretion disk](../../../../../accretion-disk.md) forms when inward-moving gas retains enough [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) to orbit a compact object. In a geometrically thin [Keplerian disk](../../../../../keplerian-disk.md), radial [force](../../../../../force-split.md) balance gives $\Omega\simeq\sqrt{GM/r^3}$ and vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives a thickness $H\sim c_s/\Omega\ll r$. Matter cannot fall far inward while keeping its original [specific angular momentum](../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) $r^2\Omega\propto r^{1/2}$: stresses must transport [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) outward.

The [viscous torque in an accretion disk](../../../../../viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) has outward magnitude

$$
\mathcal G=-2\pi r^3\nu\Sigma\frac{d\Omega}{dr}
=3\pi\nu\Sigma\sqrt{GMr},
$$

where $\nu$ is the effective [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) and $\Sigma$ the [surface density](../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md). With constant inward [accretion rate](../../../../../accretion-rate.md) $\dot M>0$ and zero inner torque at $r_{\rm in}$, [conservation of angular momentum](../../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives

$$
\nu\Sigma=\frac{\dot M}{3\pi}\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{r_{\rm in}}r}\right).
$$

The associated [standard thin-disk dissipation flux](../../../../../standard-thin-disk-dissipation-flux.md) from each face is

$$
F(r)=\frac{3GM\dot M}{8\pi r^3}\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{r_{\rm in}}r}\right).
$$

Orbital [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../gravitational-energy.md) becomes [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) and, in a radiatively efficient disk, escaping radiation. Integrating both faces gives the [standard thin-disk luminosity](../../../../../standard-thin-disk-luminosity.md) $L=GM\dot M/(2r_{\rm in})$ under this boundary condition. A central stellar surface or an inner torque can release or redistribute additional [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).

The [alpha disk](../../../../../alpha-disk.md) prescription $\nu\sim\alpha c_sH$ summarizes an effective stress rather than a claim that molecular [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) alone explains observed rates. In an ionized disk, [magnetorotational instability](../../../../../magnetorotational-instability.md) can produce magnetic and fluid stresses. The inward evolution occurs on the [viscous timescale](../../../../../viscous-timescale.md) $t_\nu\sim r^2/\nu$, much longer than an orbit for a thin disk with $\alpha\lesssim1$. Hot inner annuli and cooler outer annuli naturally give a range of emitted [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md); the [blackbody](../../../../../blackbody.md) approximation additionally requires sufficient [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md).
