<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a steady inviscid [barotropic fluid](../../../../../barotropic-fluid.md) in a conservative [gravitational potential](../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md), define $h(\rho)=\int dp/\rho$. Dotting the [Euler equation](../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) with the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) gives

$$
\mathbf u\cdot\nabla\left(\frac{u^2}{2}+h+\phi\right)=0.
$$

The [Bernoulli function](../../../../../bernoulli-function.md) is therefore constant along a [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md). It balances [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) work or [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md), and gravitational [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Different [streamlines](../../../../../streamline.md) can have different constants; for an [irrotational flow](../../../../../irrotational-flow.md), the identity $\nabla H=\mathbf u\times(\nabla\times\mathbf u)$ makes the constant common throughout a connected region.

For an incompressible fluid, $h=p/\rho$ up to a constant, giving $u^2/2+p/\rho+\phi=C$. For a constant-entropy perfect-gas [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md), $h=\gamma p/[(\gamma-1)\rho]$. Together with [mass](../../../../../mass.md) conservation, this explains conversion of [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) into [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) in a nozzle and the need to include [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) in an escaping wind. Viscous losses, heating and [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) production require their corresponding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) balance. Across an adiabatic shock the total [stagnation enthalpy](../../../../../stagnation-enthalpy.md) can still be conserved, but the upstream isentropic pressure-density law cannot simply be continued through the [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) jump.
