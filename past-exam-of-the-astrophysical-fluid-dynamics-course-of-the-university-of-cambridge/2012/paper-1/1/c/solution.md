<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using a center-to-edge [sound-crossing time](../../../../../sound-crossing-time.md) $t_s=R/c_s$ and the pressureless uniform-sphere [free-fall time of a uniform sphere](../../../../../free-fall-time-of-a-uniform-sphere.md) $t_{\rm ff}=\sqrt{3\pi/(32G\rho)}$, equality gives

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm cross}=c_s\sqrt{\frac{3\pi}{32G\rho}}\sim\frac{c_s}{\sqrt{G\rho}}.}
$$

On scales larger than this estimate, [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) information cannot cross the region before its gravitational dynamical evolution. It therefore identifies the order of the [Jeans length](../../../../../jeans-length.md) and separates gravity-dominated from pressure-supported behavior in the simple comparison. It is not an exact normal-mode stability calculation: with the standard Jeans [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda_J=c_s\sqrt{\pi/(G\rho)}$, this radius is $\sqrt{3/32}\lambda_J$. Using the diameter rather than the radius as the crossing distance changes the numerical coefficient. [Boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md), [density](../../../../../density.md) structure, [turbulence](../../../../../turbulence-split.md) and magnetic support also matter for an actual cloud.
