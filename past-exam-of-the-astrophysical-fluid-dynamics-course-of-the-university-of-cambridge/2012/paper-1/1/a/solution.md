<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The equation describes small plane-wave [perturbations](../../../../../perturbation.md) of a uniform, stationary [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) in [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../../ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md), with propagation **parallel to the background [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md)**. The nonzero field defines $\widehat{\mathbf b}=\mathbf v_A/v_A$; $c_s$ is the [sound speed](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) for the assumed thermodynamic closure. Neglect [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md), [electrical resistivity](../../../../../electrical-resistivity.md), [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) and microscopic dispersive effects. A uniform background flow would instead introduce its Doppler-shifted [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md).

Decompose $\mathbf u=\mathbf u_\perp+u_\parallel\widehat{\mathbf b}$. The displayed matrix equation then becomes

$$
(k^2v_A^2-\omega^2)\mathbf u_\perp=0,\qquad
(k^2c_s^2-\omega^2)u_\parallel=0.
$$

Thus there are two independent transverse [Alfvén wave](../../../../../alfven-wave.md) [polarizations](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) with $\omega^2=k^2v_A^2$, restored by [magnetic tension](../../../../../magnetic-tension.md), and a longitudinal acoustic mode with $\omega^2=k^2c_s^2$, restored by [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). The transverse modes have no first-order [density](../../../../../density.md) compression; a strictly longitudinal displacement along the field does not bend it. These are the [parallel-propagating ideal magnetohydrodynamic wave](../../../../../parallel-propagating-ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-wave.md) branches. In the general oblique problem, fast and slow magnetosonic modes mix compression and magnetic response; their parallel limits coincide with the faster and slower of these speeds. If $c_s=v_A$, all three [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) [polarizations](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) share the same [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) magnitude. The [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) equation alone does not enumerate nonpropagating [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) [perturbations](../../../../../perturbation.md).
