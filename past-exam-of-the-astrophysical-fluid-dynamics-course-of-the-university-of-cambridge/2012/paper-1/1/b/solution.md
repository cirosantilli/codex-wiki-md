<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take positive $\dot Q$ to mean net cooling per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md). At fixed [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) for an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) of unchanged composition, $\rho\propto T^{-1}$, so $\dot Q=C T^{\alpha-1}-H$ with $C>0$. At [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md), $CT_0^{\alpha-1}=H$. Because expansion work is included at fixed [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), the perturbed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) obeys

$$
c_p\frac{d\delta T}{dt}=-\left(\frac{\partial\dot Q}{\partial T}\right)_p\delta T
=-\frac{(\alpha-1)H}{T_0}\delta T.
$$

This [isobaric stability criterion for density-dependent power-law cooling](../../../../../isobaric-thermal-stability-of-a-heated-ideal-gas.md) gives stability for $\alpha>1$ and instability for $\alpha<1$. For optically thin [bremsstrahlung](../../../../../bremsstrahlung.md), $\alpha=1/2$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\delta T}{dt}=\frac{H}{2c_pT_0}\delta T,\qquad\text{isobarically unstable}.}
$$

A slightly cooler parcel becomes denser and cools faster; a slightly hotter parcel has reduced cooling and heats further. The conclusion assumes the [sound-crossing time](../../../../../sound-crossing-time.md) is short enough to maintain nearly common [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), with conduction and other stabilizing processes omitted.
