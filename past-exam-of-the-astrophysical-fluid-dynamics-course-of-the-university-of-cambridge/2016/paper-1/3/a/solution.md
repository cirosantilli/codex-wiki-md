<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $e=c_vT$ be the [specific internal energy](../../../../../specific-internal-energy.md) of a calorically [perfect gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) and define its [specific gas constant](../../../../../specific-gas-constant.md) $\mathcal R=R^*/\mu$, so $p=\mathcal R\rho T$. A reversible adiabatic change has no heat input and obeys

$$
de+p\,d(1/\rho)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c_v\,dT-\frac{\mathcal RT}{\rho}\,d\rho=0.
$$

Using $c_p-c_v=\mathcal R$ and $\gamma=c_p/c_v$ gives

$$
\frac{dT}{T}=(\gamma-1)\frac{d\rho}{\rho},\qquad d\ln p=d\ln\rho+d\ln T=\gamma\,d\ln\rho.
$$

Integration yields **$p/\rho^\gamma=K$, or $\boxed{p=K\rho^\gamma}$**, with $K$ fixed on that isentrope. Equivalently $s-s_{\rm ref}=c_v\ln[(p/\rho^\gamma)/K_{\rm ref}]$, so $K$ specifies [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) as well as composition. In a smooth nondissipative flow this means $D(p\rho^{-\gamma})/Dt=0$ along each parcel. A single common $K$ throughout the fluid additionally requires a [homentropic flow](../../../../../homentropic-flow.md). No heat transfer alone does not forbid [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) production: different sides of an adiabatic shock generally have different $K$ values. Constant composition and heat capacities are needed for the fixed exponent used here.
