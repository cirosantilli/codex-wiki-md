<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a Newtonian [circular orbit](../../../../../circular-orbit.md), $v_\phi^2=GM/r$, so the specific orbital [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $e=v_\phi^2/2-GM/r=-GM/(2r)$. Half of the released potential [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) remains in circular orbital motion. The binding-energy estimate between the specified edges is therefore

$$
L_{\rm bind}\simeq\frac{GM\dot m}{2}\left(\frac1{r_{\rm in}}-\frac1{r_{\rm out}}\right)=\frac{GM\dot m}{2R_s}\left(\frac16-\frac1{30}\right)=\frac{\dot m c^2}{30}.
$$

Using $M_\odot=1.98847\times10^{30}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and one Julian year $=3.15576\times10^7\,\mathrm s$ gives $\dot m=6.30\times10^{22}\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$. Thus **the radiative [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) scale is $L\sim1.9\times10^{38}\,\mathrm W$**, approximately $1.9\times10^{45}\,\mathrm{erg\,s^{-1}}$ or $4.9\times10^{11}L_\odot$. The black-hole [mass](../../../../../mass.md) cancels because both edge radii scale with $M$. The specified inner edge is $6R_s$; it must not be silently replaced with a different relativistic orbit radius.

For a finite viscous disc, the precise radiated power also depends on boundary work: [torque transport of energy in a steady accretion disk](../../../../../torque-transport-of-energy-in-a-steady-accretion-disk.md) means that orbital binding-energy release need not all be radiated inside the two stated edges. If the intended model is a radiatively efficient steady [Keplerian accretion disk](../../../../../keplerian-accretion-disk.md) with a [zero-torque inner boundary condition](../../../../../zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md), integrate the one-face flux $F=3GM\dot m[1-\sqrt{r_{\rm in}/r}]/(8\pi r^3)$ over both faces. It gives

$$
L_{\rm diss}=\frac{GM\dot m}{2r_{\rm in}}\left[1-3\frac{r_{\rm in}}{r_{\rm out}}+2\left(\frac{r_{\rm in}}{r_{\rm out}}\right)^{3/2}\right]\simeq\boxed{1.37\times10^{38}\,\mathrm W}.
$$

The difference from $L_{\rm bind}$ is the outward [torque](../../../../../torque.md) power at the outer edge. The question supplies neither boundary [torque](../../../../../torque.md) nor the external material-supply [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) budget, so the two calculations state their assumptions explicitly: about $2\times10^{38}$ W is the binding-energy estimate, while $1.37\times10^{38}$ W is the finite-disc zero-inner-torque dissipation result. Both neglect [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) advection, winds and relativistic corrections as required for this model.[§5.2, printed pp.3–4: steady-disc surface flux and outward torque transport of energy](../../../../../dynamics-of-astrophysical-discs-lecture-5-boundary-conditions-and-steady-accretion-by-gordon-i-ogilvie.md)
