<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $z$ increase upwards and let $g>0$ denote the downward [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md). The environment obeys [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), $dp/dz=-\rho g$. Displace a small parcel by $\zeta$ while it remains in [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) balance with its surroundings and exchanges negligible heat. For fixed composition and [adiabatic index](../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma$, its density change at constant [specific entropy](../../../../../specific-entropy.md) satisfies $d\rho_{\rm par}/dp=\rho/(\gamma p)$. At the displaced position, its density excess over the environment is therefore

$$
\Delta\rho=\left(\frac{\rho}{\gamma p}\frac{dp}{dz}-\frac{d\rho}{dz}\right)\zeta+O(\zeta^2).
$$

The [buoyancy](../../../../../buoyancy.md) acceleration is $\ddot\zeta\simeq-g\Delta\rho/\rho$. Hence

$$
\ddot\zeta=-N^2\zeta,\qquad N^2=\frac g\rho\left(\frac{\rho}{\gamma p}\frac{dp}{dz}-\frac{d\rho}{dz}\right).
$$

**Stability requires $N^2>0$**, equivalently

$$
\boxed{\frac{\rho}{p\gamma}\frac{dp}{dz}>\frac{d\rho}{dz}}.
$$

An upward-displaced parcel is then denser than its surroundings and falls back; a downward-displaced parcel is lighter and rises. Equality gives neutral stability in this parcel model. For a [perfect gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) with constant composition, $ds=c_v\,d\ln(p/\rho^\gamma)$ gives $N^2=(g/c_p)\,ds/dz$, so a rising [specific entropy](../../../../../specific-entropy.md) is stable. This is the parcel form of the [Schwarzschild criterion](../../../../../schwarzschild-criterion.md). Rapid [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) equilibration, negligible heat diffusion during displacement and no composition-driven [buoyancy](../../../../../buoyancy.md) are the assumptions behind this test.
