<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Work in the rest frame of the [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md). The normal [momentum flux](../../../../../momentum-flux.md) is conserved, so $p_1+\rho_1u_1^2=p_2+\rho_2u_2^2$. In the strong-shock limit $p_1\ll\rho_1u_1^2$, while [mass conservation](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives $\rho_2u_2=\rho_1u_1$. The given [Strong-shock Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](../../../../../strong-shock-rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) therefore yield

$$
p_2\simeq\rho_1u_1^2\left(1-\frac{u_2}{u_1}\right)
=\frac{2\rho_1u_1^2}{\gamma+1},\qquad
\rho_2=\rho_1\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}.
$$

Using the downstream [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) $p_2/\rho_2=k_BT_2/m$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{k_BT_2}{m}=\frac{2(\gamma-1)}{(\gamma+1)^2}u_1^2.}
$$

For a monatomic [perfect gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), $\gamma=5/3$ and the coefficient is $3/16$. This is the immediate post-shock [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), before substantial [radiative cooling](../../../../../radiative-cooling.md); ionization, dissociation or unequal [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and ion [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) require additional physical modeling.
