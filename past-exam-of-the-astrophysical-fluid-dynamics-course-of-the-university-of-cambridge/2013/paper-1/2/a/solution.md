<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [Alfvén wave](../../../../../alfven-wave.md) is a transverse disturbance restored by [magnetic tension](../../../../../magnetic-tension.md). In a uniform conducting [fluid](../../../../../fluid.md) with background $\mathbf B_0=B_0\mathbf e_z$, a small transverse displacement $\boldsymbol\xi_\perp(z,t)$ bends the [magnetic field lines](../../../../../magnetic-field-line.md). [Magnetic flux freezing](../../../../../magnetic-flux-freezing.md) gives $\mathbf b_\perp=B_0\partial_z\boldsymbol\xi_\perp$, and the linear transverse [Lorentz force density](../../../../../lorentz-force-density.md) is $(B_0/\mu_0)\partial_z\mathbf b_\perp$. Thus the [Linear Alfvén wave equations](../../../../../linear-alfven-wave-equations.md) reduce to

$$
\rho\,\partial_t^2\boldsymbol\xi_\perp
=\frac{B_0^2}{\mu_0}\partial_z^2\boldsymbol\xi_\perp,\qquad
\boxed{v_A=\frac{B_0}{\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}},\quad\omega^2=k_\parallel^2v_A^2.}
$$

For this shear [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md), $\delta\rho=\delta p=0$ at linear order. The disturbance transports [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) along the background [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md); a single traveling wave has equal perturbation [magnetic energy](../../../../../magnetic-energy.md) and [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), with $\mathbf b_\perp=\mp\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}\,\mathbf u_\perp$ for propagation along $\pm\mathbf B_0$. These properties distinguish it from compressive [magnetosonic waves](../../../../../magnetosonic-wave.md).

The [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../../ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md) approximation requires strong electrical coupling and wavelengths large compared with microscopic scales. Finite [electrical resistivity](../../../../../electrical-resistivity.md) and [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) dissipate the wave's [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). In [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) and other astrophysical [plasmas](../../../../../plasma-physics.md), [Alfvén waves](../../../../../alfven-wave.md) carry disturbances and can contribute to heating when dissipated. The [Lundquist liquid-metal Alfvén wave experiment](../../../../../lundquist-liquid-metal-alfven-wave-experiment.md) provides a laboratory counterpart: a conducting liquid in a strong applied [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) transmits a transverse magnetic disturbance, making the field's mechanical restoring action measurable.
