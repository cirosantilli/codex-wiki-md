<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Interpret “reach” first as remaining an energetic, supersonic [blast wave](../../../../blast-wave.md), rather than as the arrival of any [acoustic wave](../../../../acoustic-wave.md). An [atmospheric blast-wave range](../../../../atmospheric-blast-wave-range.md) estimate must account for the thin atmosphere: the distance $D=8.5\times10^6\ \mathrm m$ is much greater than its height $H=10^4\ \mathrm m$.

For an optimistic estimate, suppose the deposited [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E$ remains in a horizontally expanding atmospheric layer. If its representative [mass density](../../../../density.md) is $\rho_a$, the swept mass is of order $\pi D^2H\rho_a$. [Conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives the [self-similar blast wave](../../../../self-similar-blast-wave.md) scaling

$$
E\sim\pi\rho_aHD^2U^2,\qquad
U(D)\sim\left(\frac E{\pi\rho_aHD^2}\right)^{1/2}.
$$

Using ordinary atmospheric estimates $\rho_a\sim1\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and [speed of sound](../../../../speed-of-sound.md) $c_a\sim300\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, this gives

$$
\pi D^2H\rho_a\simeq2.3\times10^{18}\ \mathrm{kg},\qquad
U(D)\sim66\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},\qquad U/c_a\sim0.22.
$$

These are scaling estimates, not an exact [shock wave](../../../../shock-wave.md) solution. The strong [shock wave](../../../../shock-wave.md) approximation would already have failed at

$$
D_s\sim\sqrt{\frac E{\pi\rho_aHc_a^2}}\simeq1.9\times10^6\ \mathrm m.
$$

Equivalently, keeping the disturbance strongly supersonic out to $D$ would require substantially more than the sonic-transition [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) scale

$$
E_s\sim\pi\rho_aHD^2c_a^2\simeq2.0\times10^{23}\ \mathrm J,
$$

which exceeds $10^{22}\ \mathrm J$ by about a factor of twenty. Reasonable atmospheric averages and order-unity geometrical coefficients do not reverse the comparison. Escape of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) through the top of the atmosphere makes the fully confined estimate optimistic; [Earth](../../../../earth.md)'s curvature changes its area estimate by an order-unity factor.

**The strong atmospheric blast will not survive to Lansing in this model.** A weakened [acoustic wave](../../../../acoustic-wave.md) can nevertheless travel there. The supplied [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and atmospheric height do not determine its detectable amplitude, detailed attenuation or damage: those require an atmospheric propagation model. Thus “no disturbance of any kind reaches Lansing” would be an unjustified stronger conclusion.
