<h1 id="section-b/6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The vanishing total [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) must be imposed at the displaced [free surface](../../../../../free-surface.md), not at fixed $r=R$. The first-order [Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) is

$$
0=p_0(R+\eta)+\pi(R+\eta,z,t)
=p_0(R)+\eta p'_0(R)+\pi(R,z,t)+O(\eta^2).
$$

Since $p_0(R)=0$ and $p'_0(R)=-2\pi G\rho^2R$, the [Lagrangian pressure perturbation](../../../../../lagrangian-pressure-perturbation.md) condition is

$$
\pi(R,z,t)=2\pi G\rho^2R\eta.
$$

The supplied gravitational perturbation and the combined potential, evaluated at $R$, then give

$$
\eta\Pi_0I_0(\kappa)=W(R)
=-4\pi G\rho R K_0(\kappa)I_0(\kappa)\eta+2\pi G\rho R\eta.
$$

Here $K_0$ is the [Modified Bessel function of the second kind](../../../../../modified-bessel-function-of-the-second-kind.md). Equating mode amplitudes and using part (c) yields

$$
\frac{\omega^2I_0(\kappa)}{kI'_0(\kappa)}
=4\pi G\rho R\left[\frac12-K_0(\kappa)I_0(\kappa)\right].
$$

Rearrange to obtain the [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) for the [axisymmetric gravitational instability of an incompressible cylinder](../../../../../axisymmetric-gravitational-instability-of-an-incompressible-cylinder.md):

$$
\boxed{-\frac{\omega^2}{4\pi G\rho}
=\kappa\frac{I'_0(\kappa)}{I_0(\kappa)}
\left[K_0(\kappa)I_0(\kappa)-\frac12\right].}
$$

Every quantity on the right is dimensionless, consistent with $G\rho$ having units $\mathrm{s^{-2}}$. The stabilizing term $1/2$ comes from displacing the background [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) profile; omitting the [Lagrangian pressure perturbation](../../../../../lagrangian-pressure-perturbation.md) would lose it.
