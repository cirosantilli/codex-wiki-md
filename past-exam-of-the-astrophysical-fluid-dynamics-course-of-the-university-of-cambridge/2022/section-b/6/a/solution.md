<h1 id="section-b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Treat the cylinder as infinitely long, with a vacuum exterior and no [surface tension](../../../../../surface-tension-split.md). Let $\Phi_0$ be its [gravitational potential](../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md). Integrating the gravitational [Poisson equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md) over a coaxial cylinder of radius $r<R$ and length $L$, then applying the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md), gives

$$
2\pi rL\frac{d\Phi_0}{dr}=4\pi G(\pi r^2L\rho),\qquad
\frac{d\Phi_0}{dr}=2\pi G\rho r.
$$

Thus the inward [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md) is $-2\pi G\rho r\widehat{\mathbf r}$. [Hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) requires

$$
\frac{dp_0}{dr}=-\rho\frac{d\Phi_0}{dr}=-2\pi G\rho^2r.
$$

Impose zero [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) on the [free surface](../../../../../free-surface.md), $p_0(R)=0$, and integrate inward:

$$
\boxed{p_0(r)=\pi G\rho^2(R^2-r^2),\qquad 0\le r<R.}
$$

This is the [hydrostatic self-gravitating incompressible cylinder](../../../../../hydrostatic-self-gravitating-incompressible-cylinder.md). Its central [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) is $\pi G\rho^2R^2$, and the units of the expression are $\mathrm{Pa}$. A nonzero external [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) would add that constant to $p_0(r)$.
