<h1 id="section-b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The pressure-free equation has the [scalar conservation law](../../../../../scalar-conservation-law.md) form

$$
\partial_tu+\partial_xf(u)=0,\qquad f(u)=\frac12u^2.
$$

A smooth travelling ansatz $u=U(x-Vt)$ obeys $(U-V)U'=0$, so it cannot continuously connect two distinct constant states. The inviscid [shock wave](../../../../../shock-wave.md) must be interpreted as a discontinuous [weak solution](../../../../../weak-solution.md). Its [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) is

$$
V(u_2-u_1)=f(u_2)-f(u_1)=\frac12(u_2^2-u_1^2).
$$

For $u_1\ne u_2$,

$$
\boxed{V=\frac{u_1+u_2}{2}.}
$$

The result is independent of which state is called upstream. For a physical compressive [entropy shock](../../../../../entropy-shock.md) of the [Inviscid Burgers equation](../../../../../inviscid-burgers-equation.md), the left and right states satisfy $u_L>u_R$ and therefore $u_L>V>u_R$: [characteristic curves](../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) run into the [shock wave](../../../../../shock-wave.md) from both sides. The ordering $u_L<u_R$ instead produces a [rarefaction wave](../../../../../rarefaction-wave.md).
