<h1 id="section-b/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $N^2>0$, write $K=\sqrt{k_x^2+k_z^2}$ and choose either signed branch $\omega=sNk_x/K$, with $s=\pm1$. These two branches cover the roots of the squared [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md). The [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) of an [internal gravity wave](../../../../../internal-wave.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf c_g=\nabla_{\mathbf k}\omega
=\frac{sN}{K^3}(k_z^2,\,0,\,-k_xk_z).}
$$

If instead the positive-frequency root is chosen for both signs of $k_x$, set $s=+1$ and multiply the displayed components by $\operatorname{sgn}(k_x)$, away from the cusp $k_x=0$.

Taking the [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with the [wave vector](../../../../../wavevector.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf c_g=0.}
$$

Thus the [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md), which describes the travel of a narrow [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) and its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), is perpendicular to the [wave vector](../../../../../wavevector.md), and hence perpendicular to the [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) $\mathbf c_p=\omega\mathbf k/K^2$. This is the [internal-wave phase and group velocity](../../../../../internal-wave-phase-and-group-velocity.md) geometry. It also follows from [phase-group orthogonality for degree-zero dispersion](../../../../../phase-group-orthogonality-for-degree-zero-dispersion.md): the [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) depends only on the direction of $\mathbf k$, not its magnitude. For $k_z=0$, the [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) is zero, consistent with the stationary extremum $|\omega|=N$.
