<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [buoyancy frequency](../../../../../buoyancy-frequency.md), also called the [Brunt–Väisälä frequency](../../../../../buoyancy-frequency.md), is the [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) of a small vertical displacement restored by [buoyancy](../../../../../buoyancy.md) in a stably stratified fluid. Take $z$ upward, [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md) $-g\widehat{\mathbf z}$, and a background in [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md),

$$
\frac{dp}{dz}=-\rho g.
$$

Displace a small gas element upward by $\xi$, sufficiently slowly for its [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) to match its surroundings but without heat exchange. For this [adiabatic process](../../../../../adiabatic-process.md), $p/\rho^\gamma$ is constant along the element's motion, so its [mass density](../../../../../density.md) changes by $\rho\gamma^{-1}(d\ln p/dz)\xi$. The surrounding [mass density](../../../../../density.md) changes by $(d\rho/dz)\xi$. The element's excess [mass density](../../../../../density.md) is consequently

$$
\Delta\rho=\rho\left(\frac1\gamma\frac{d\ln p}{dz}-\frac{d\ln\rho}{dz}\right)\xi.
$$

Its acceleration is $\ddot\xi=-g\Delta\rho/\rho=-N^2\xi$. Thus

$$
\boxed{N^2=g\left(\frac1\gamma\frac{d\ln p}{dz}-\frac{d\ln\rho}{dz}\right).}
$$

For a gas of fixed composition obeying the [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md), $\rho\propto p/T$. The [adiabatic buoyancy frequency of an ideal gas](../../../../../adiabatic-buoyancy-frequency-of-an-ideal-gas.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{N^2=g\left[\frac1T\frac{dT}{dz}-\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma p}\frac{dp}{dz}\right]
=\frac gT\left(\frac{dT}{dz}+\frac g{c_p}\right),}
$$

where $c_p=\gamma\mathcal R/(\gamma-1)$ is the [specific heat capacity](../../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md) at constant [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), and $\mathcal R=p/(\rho T)$ is the specific gas constant. Here $N^2$ has units $\mathrm{s^{-2}}$; $N$ is an [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md), not a cyclic [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md). Positive $N^2$ gives [simple harmonic motion](../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md), negative $N^2$ gives [convective instability](../../../../../stellar-convective-instability.md), and $N^2=0$ is marginal. The temperature form assumes fixed composition; the preceding density form is more general.
