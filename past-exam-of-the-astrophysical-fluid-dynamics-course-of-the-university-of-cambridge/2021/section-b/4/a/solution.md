<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [shock wave](../../../../../shock-wave.md) forms when a compressive flow steepens or when gas must be decelerated or turned faster than upstream [acoustic waves](../../../../../acoustic-wave.md) can communicate the change. Astrophysical examples include a [supernova remnant](../../../../../supernova-remnant.md) sweeping up surrounding gas, a fast [stellar wind](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) colliding with ambient material, and supersonic accretion onto an obstacle or onto a dense gas layer. A large [Mach number](../../../../../mach-number.md) by itself is not sufficient: a uniform supersonic stream need not contain a [shock wave](../../../../../shock-wave.md).

Choose the [shock frame](../../../../../shock-frame.md), and denote upstream and downstream normal [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) by $u_1,u_2$. Integrating the local [conservation laws](../../../../../conservation-law.md) over a thin control volume spanning a steady [normal shock wave](../../../../../normal-shock-wave.md) gives equal fluxes on both sides. [Mass conservation](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives constant mass flux per unit area,

$$
\boxed{j=\rho_1u_1=\rho_2u_2.}
$$

[Pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) and advected [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) together give the normal [momentum flux](../../../../../momentum-flux.md),

$$
\boxed{p_1+\rho_1u_1^2=p_2+\rho_2u_2^2.}
$$

For a thin non-radiating layer, with negligible gravitational-potential change across it, [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives equal fluxes $j(h+u^2/2)$. A perfect gas has [specific enthalpy](../../../../../specific-enthalpy.md) $h=\gamma p/[(\gamma-1)\rho]$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac\gamma{\gamma-1}\frac{p_1}{\rho_1}+\frac12u_1^2
=\frac\gamma{\gamma-1}\frac{p_2}{\rho_2}+\frac12u_2^2.}
$$

These are the [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions for a perfect gas](../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md). Dissipation inside the thin layer produces an [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) increase and selects the physical compressive branch. Efficient radiative cooling would change the energy relation and may instead lead to an [isothermal shock](../../../../../isothermal-shock.md).
