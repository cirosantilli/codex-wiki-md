<h1 id="section-b/4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For $\theta>\theta_{\max}$, $\gamma\sin\theta>1$, so there is no real solution for an attached straight [oblique shock](../../../../../oblique-shock.md) satisfying the assumed geometry. **The shock detaches and becomes curved**, with a locally strong, nearly normal portion and a subsonic region allowing the flow to adjust to the wall. The precise detached structure depends on the global geometry and downstream conditions; the attached-shock formula cannot be continued to these turns.
