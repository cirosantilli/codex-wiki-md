<h1 id="section-b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take $p_1\ll\rho_1u_1^2$. Let $q=u_2/u_1$. [Mass conservation](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives $\rho_2=\rho_1/q$, and normal [momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives

$$
p_2\simeq\rho_1u_1^2(1-q),\qquad
\frac{p_2}{\rho_2}=q(1-q)u_1^2.
$$

Insert this into the energy condition from part (a):

$$
\frac\gamma{\gamma-1}q(1-q)+\frac12q^2=\frac12.
$$

Rearranging and factoring,

$$
(\gamma+1)q^2-2\gamma q+(\gamma-1)
=(q-1)[(\gamma+1)q-(\gamma-1)]=0.
$$

The root $q=1$ is the unshocked state. The compressive [Strong-shock Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](../../../../../strong-shock-rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) instead give

$$
\boxed{u_2=\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma+1}u_1.}
$$

Correspondingly, $\rho_2/\rho_1=(\gamma+1)/(\gamma-1)$ and $p_2=2\rho_1u_1^2/(\gamma+1)$. These relations require $\gamma>1$ and negligible upstream thermal [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md).
