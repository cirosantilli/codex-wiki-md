<h1 id="section-b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $M(r)$ be the enclosed mass. For [spherical symmetry](../../../../../spherical-symmetry.md), [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) and [mass conservation](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) give

$$
\frac{dp}{dr}=-\frac{GM(r)\rho(r)}{r^2},\qquad
\frac{dM}{dr}=4\pi r^2\rho.
$$

The isothermal [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md) $p=K\rho$ therefore implies

$$
K\frac{d\ln\rho}{dr}=-\frac{GM(r)}{r^2}.
$$

Multiply by $r^2$, differentiate and eliminate $M$:

$$
\boxed{\frac1{r^2}\frac d{dr}\left(r^2\frac{d\ln\rho}{dr}\right)
=-\frac{4\pi G}{K}\rho,}
$$

or equivalently $r^{-2}d[r^2\rho^{-1}\rho']/dr=-4\pi G\rho/K$. This is the density equation for an [isothermal sphere](../../../../../isothermal-sphere.md). Here $K$ has units $\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-2}}$ and equals the squared [isothermal sound speed](../../../../../isothermal-sound-speed.md).

A regular [isothermal sphere](../../../../../isothermal-sphere.md) does not reach zero density at a finite radius. The finite body discussed later must therefore be truncated by a nonzero external [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), as in a [pressure-confined isothermal sphere](../../../../../pressure-confined-isothermal-sphere.md). A finite vacuum boundary would not be compatible with this [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md).
