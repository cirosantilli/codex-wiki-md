<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Take the outward [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md) $k>0$; the absolute value in the source makes the two propagation directions equivalent. The [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) of the [gravity-capillary wave](../../../../gravity-capillary-wave.md) obeys

$$
c_p^2=\frac{\omega^2}{k^2}=\frac gk+\frac\sigma\rho k.
$$

Differentiate with respect to $k$:

$$
\frac{d(c_p^2)}{dk}=-\frac g{k^2}+\frac\sigma\rho=0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad k_*=\sqrt{\frac{\rho g}{\sigma}}.
$$

The second derivative is positive, and $c_p\to\infty$ at both ends of the positive-$k$ range, so this is the global minimum. The [minimum phase speed of a gravity-capillary wave](../../../../minimum-phase-speed-of-a-gravity-capillary-wave.md) is

$$
\boxed{c_{p,\min}=\left(\frac{4g\sigma}{\rho}\right)^{1/4}.}
$$

For $g=9.81\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ and the supplied [surface tension](../../../../surface-tension-split.md) and [mass density](../../../../density.md),

$$
\boxed{c_{p,\min}=0.231\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The corresponding [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md) is $k_*\simeq369\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}$, giving [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda_*=2\pi/k_*\simeq1.70\ \mathrm{cm}$. At this minimum, $c_g=d\omega/dk=c_p+k\,dc_p/dk=c_p$. Thus the [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) and [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) agree for this particular ripple, although they differ for general [gravity-capillary waves](../../../../gravity-capillary-wave.md).
