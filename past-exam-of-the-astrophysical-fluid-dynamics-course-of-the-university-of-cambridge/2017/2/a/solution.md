<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [plasma](../../../../plasma-physics.md) contains mobile charged species whose collective electromagnetic response matters. On scales large compared with the [Debye length](../../../../debye-length.md), positive and negative charge densities nearly cancel: this is [quasineutrality](../../../../quasineutrality.md), not the absence of [electric currents](../../../../electric-current.md) or [electric fields](../../../../electric-field-split.md). For electrons of [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_e$ and number density $n_e$, the characteristic [plasma screening](../../../../plasma-screening.md) and oscillation scales are

$$
\lambda_D=\sqrt{\frac{\epsilon_0k_BT_e}{n_ee^2}},\qquad
\omega_{pe}=\sqrt{\frac{n_ee^2}{\epsilon_0m_e}}.
$$

A collective [plasma](../../../../plasma-physics.md) description requires many particles within a screening volume; long-wavelength, sufficiently slow behavior can often be described by [magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../magnetohydrodynamics-split.md) with an appropriate fluid closure.

An individual particle feels the [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) $q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)$. In a uniform [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), its transverse motion gyrates while its parallel motion remains free, giving a helical path when both components are present. The nonrelativistic [cyclotron frequency](../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md) and [gyroradius](../../../../gyroradius.md) are

$$
\Omega_c=\frac{|q|B}{m},\qquad r_L=\frac{mv_\perp}{|q|B}.
$$

When the system scale is much larger than $r_L$ and the relevant time scale longer than $\Omega_c^{-1}$, the field strongly organizes transport across it, provided [collisions](../../../../collision.md) do not interrupt the gyromotion. A perpendicular [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) also gives the common guiding-centre drift $\mathbf v_E=\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/B^2$.

For a conducting fluid, the macroscopic [Lorentz force density](../../../../lorentz-force-density.md) has the useful decomposition

$$
\mathbf J\times\mathbf B=-\boldsymbol\nabla\left(\frac{B^2}{2\mu_0}\right)
+\frac{(\mathbf B\cdot\boldsymbol\nabla)\mathbf B}{\mu_0}.
$$

These terms are [magnetic pressure](../../../../magnetic-pressure.md) and [magnetic tension](../../../../magnetic-tension.md). Magnetic tension resists bending, while magnetic-pressure gradients resist nonuniform compression. The [plasma beta](../../../../plasma-beta.md) $\beta=2\mu_0p/B^2$ compares thermal and magnetic stresses.

With uniform [magnetic diffusivity](../../../../magnetic-diffusivity.md) $\eta_m$, the [resistive induction equation](../../../../resistive-induction-equation.md) is

$$
\partial_t\mathbf B=\boldsymbol\nabla\times(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)+\eta_m\nabla^2\mathbf B.
$$

If the [magnetic Reynolds number](../../../../magnetic-reynolds-number.md) $R_m=UL/\eta_m\gg1$, [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md) applies approximately and [magnetic flux freezing](../../../../magnetic-flux-freezing.md) links the field geometry to fluid motion. Finite diffusivity permits field slippage and [magnetic reconnection](../../../../magnetic-reconnection.md). Field tension supports [Alfvén waves](../../../../alfven-wave.md) with $v_A=B/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$; coupled magnetic and gas compression also supports [magnetosonic waves](../../../../magnetosonic-wave.md). These effects allow a field to transmit forces and [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) between distant parts of an astrophysical [plasma](../../../../plasma-physics.md).
