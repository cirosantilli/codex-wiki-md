<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Retain the preceding **isothermal** assumption, so the gas [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) remains $T_0$. When two neutral [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) atoms become one [molecular hydrogen](../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md) molecule, the mean particle mass doubles. At fixed [mass density](../../../../density.md) and [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) the [ideal gas law](../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) would halve the [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), but the flowing gas is free to compress and change speed. It cannot in general keep density, [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), and [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) all unchanged.

To describe a thin steady transition, label the atomic and molecular sides by $1$ and $2$ and neglect the integrated external force and radiative recoil across its small thickness. Local [mass conservation](../../../../mass-conservation.md) and [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) require

$$
j=\rho_1u_1=\rho_2u_2,\qquad
p_1+\rho_1u_1^2=p_2+\rho_2u_2^2.
$$

The [isothermal sound speed](../../../../isothermal-sound-speed.md) changes from $a_1^2=k_BT_0/m_H$ to $a_2^2=a_1^2/2$. Dividing the momentum flux by $j$ gives $u_2+a_2^2/u_2=J$, with $J=u_1+a_1^2/u_1$. The subsonic continuation is therefore

$$
u_2=\frac{J-\sqrt{J^2-2a_1^2}}2,\qquad
\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}=\frac{u_1}{u_2},\qquad
p_2-p_1=j(u_1-u_2)>0.
$$

Thus the gas becomes denser and slower. In the well-subsonic limit, where ram stresses are small compared with thermal [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), the useful leading description of [isothermal flow through molecular hydrogen formation](../../../../isothermal-flow-through-molecular-hydrogen-formation.md) is

$$
\boxed{T_2=T_1,\qquad \rho_2\simeq2\rho_1,\qquad
u_2\simeq\frac{u_1}{2},\qquad p_2\simeq p_1.}
$$

The exact momentum condition gives a small [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) increase at low Mach number, rather than an imposed discontinuous factor-of-two [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) drop. The full expressions above apply when the subsonic speed is not asymptotically small.

Radiation of the molecular binding energy prevents that released energy from automatically heating the gas. It does **not**, by itself, determine the [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md): the constant-temperature result uses the inherited isothermal closure. If that assumption is relaxed, an energy equation including cooling and molecular degrees of freedom is needed; the wording about emitted binding energy alone does not uniquely determine all four changes.
