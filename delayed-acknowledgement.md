# Delayed acknowledgement

↑ **Parent:** [Acknowledgement](acknowledgement.md)

A [delayed acknowledgement](delayed-acknowledgement.md) postpones a response briefly so that one [acknowledgement](acknowledgement.md) may cover more data or accompany returning data. Its timer can interact with [Nagle's algorithm](nagle-s-algorithm.md) to delay a short exchange.
