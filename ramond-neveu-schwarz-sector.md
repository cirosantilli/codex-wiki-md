<h1 id="ramond-neveu-schwarz-sector">Ramond–Neveu–Schwarz sector</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Type II string theory](type-ii-string-theory.md)

A [closed string](closed-string.md) sector with left-moving [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md) excitations and right-moving [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md) excitations. Its spacetime states are [fermions](fermion.md). The [closed-string level matching](closed-string-level-matching.md) condition is $N_L=N_R-1/2$. After the supersymmetric [GSO projection](gso-projection.md), the lowest states have $(N_L,N_R)=(0,1/2)$ and give the other massless [gravitino](gravitino.md) and [dilatino](dilatino.md) of [type II string theory](type-ii-string-theory.md).
