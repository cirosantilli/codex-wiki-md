# Electronic heat capacity of a heavy Fermi liquid

↑ **Parent:** [Heavy fermion material](heavy-fermion-material.md)

For [quasiparticles](quasiparticle.md) with [density of states](density-of-states.md) $g(E_F)$ including both spins, the leading [electronic heat capacity](electronic-heat-capacity.md) is $C=\gamma T$ with $\gamma=\pi^2k_B^2g(E_F)/3$. A narrow heavy band makes this coefficient large. The [density of states](density-of-states.md) and heat capacity must use consistent extensive, molar or volumetric normalization.
