# Anti-de Sitter deformation of minimal supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal four-dimensional supergravity](minimal-four-dimensional-supergravity.md)

A real cosmological deformation adds $3m^2e/\kappa^2+2me\bar\psi_\mu\gamma^{\mu\nu}\psi_\nu$ and changes the [gravitino](gravitino.md) variation to $\mathcal D_\mu\epsilon/\kappa$. The modified [commutator](commutator.md) identity is $\gamma^{\mu\nu\rho}\mathcal D_\nu\mathcal D_\rho\epsilon=(G^{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g^{\mu\nu})\gamma_\nu\epsilon/2$. It fixes the negative [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) in terms of the [gravitino](gravitino.md) [mass](mass.md) parameter. A positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) is not obtained by this real unbroken minimal deformation.
