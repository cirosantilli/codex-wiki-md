# Long exact sequence for local cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Local cohomology](local-cohomology.md)

For $U=X\setminus Z$, local and ordinary [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) fit into the natural sequence

$$
\cdots\to H^{i-1}(U,\mathcal F|_U)
\to H_Z^i(X,\mathcal F)
\to H^i(X,\mathcal F)
\to H^i(U,\mathcal F|_U)
\to H_Z^{i+1}(X,\mathcal F)\to\cdots.
$$
