# Electrical circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)

An electrical circuit connects components that carry [electric current](electric-current.md) and sustain [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) differences. Its dynamics may couple [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md), [inductance](inductance.md) and [capacitance](capacitance.md).
