# Subjective confidence

↑ **Parent:** [Metacognition](metacognition.md)

An individual's expressed certainty in a judgment. Its relation to objective correctness is measured separately from the certainty itself. [Metacognitive calibration](metacognitive-calibration.md) concerns numerical agreement with outcomes; [metacognitive sensitivity](metacognitive-sensitivity.md) concerns discrimination between better and worse performance.
