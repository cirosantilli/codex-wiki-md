# Null direction of a linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md)

A [null direction of a linear map](null-direction-of-a-linear-map.md) $T$ is represented by a nonzero [vector](vector.md) $v$ in its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). Every [scalar multiple](scalar-multiple.md) $tv$ is an undetectable displacement: $T(x+tv)=T(x)$. The [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $\operatorname{span}\{v\}$ describes the direction independently of the chosen nonzero representative. More generally, a [basis](basis.md) of the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) supplies all independent [null directions of a linear map](null-direction-of-a-linear-map.md); their number is the [nullity](nullity-of-a-linear-map.md). The [zero vector](zero-vector.md) belongs to the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) but supplies no direction.
