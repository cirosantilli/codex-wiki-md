# Ground-state counting of the frustrated sawtooth Ising chain

↑ **Parent:** [Sawtooth Ising chain](sawtooth-ising-chain.md)

For positive equal exchange constants and $2L$ symmetric [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) on a ring, a minimum-energy triangle has precisely one [spin](spin.md) different from its other two. Given adjacent base [spins](spin.md), there is one permitted apex choice if they agree and two if they disagree. The counting [transfer matrix](transfer-matrix.md) is $C=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $3,-1$, giving $\Omega_L=\operatorname{Tr}C^L=3^L+(-1)^L$. Thus the [residual entropy](residual-entropy.md) per [spin](spin.md) is $(k_B/2)\log3$. If the two apex bonds are strictly stronger than the base bond, only the two globally reversed configurations minimize all triangles and this [residual entropy](residual-entropy.md) vanishes.
