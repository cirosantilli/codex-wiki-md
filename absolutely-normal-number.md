# Absolutely normal number

↑ **Parent:** [Normal number](normal-number.md)

An absolutely normal number is a [normal number](normal-number.md) in every integer base $K\geq2$. The [Birkhoff ergodic theorem](birkhoff-ergodic-theorem.md) and [ergodicity of integer multiplication on the circle](ergodicity-of-integer-multiplication-on-the-circle.md), followed by a countable intersection of full-measure sets, show that almost every real number is absolutely normal.
