# Binary rank-one qubit POVM

↑ **Parent:** [Positive operator-valued measure](positive-operator-valued-measure.md)

Two positive rank-one effects summing to $I$ in dimension two must be complementary [orthogonal projections](orthogonal-projection.md). Write the first as $\alpha|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ with normalized $\psi$. The second has eigenvalues $1-\alpha$ and $1$, so its rank-one condition forces $\alpha=1$. The two unit [Bloch vectors](bloch-vector.md) are antipodal, and this [POVM](positive-operator-valued-measure.md) is a [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md).
