# Signed curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature](curvature.md)

For an oriented plane curve parametrized by [arc length](arc-length.md), let $T$ be its unit tangent and $N$ the tangent rotated anticlockwise by $\pi/2$. Its [signed curvature](signed-curvature.md) is defined by $T'=kN$. For an anticlockwise simple boundary this is the inward normal convention; convex circles have positive signed curvature.
