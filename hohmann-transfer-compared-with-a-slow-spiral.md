# Hohmann transfer compared with a slow spiral

↑ **Parent:** [Hohmann transfer orbit](hohmann-transfer-orbit.md)

For $u=\sqrt{r_1/r_0}>1$, the sum of the two [Hohmann transfer](hohmann-transfer-orbit.md) impulse magnitudes minus the integrated thrust per mass in a [quasi-circular spiral transfer](quasi-circular-spiral-transfer.md) is

$$
v_0\frac{u-1}u\left[\frac{\sqrt2(u+1)}{\sqrt{u^2+1}}-2\right].
$$

It is negative because $(u+1)^2<2(u^2+1)$ is equivalent to $(u-1)^2>0$. Thus the Hohmann transfer uses less ideal total impulse for any strictly outward pair of coplanar circular orbits. This comparison does not optimize over all other transfer families.
