# Social choice theory

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanism design](mechanism-design.md)

Social choice theory studies how individual preferences determine a collective decision. A [social choice function](social-choice-function.md) selects one alternative from a preference profile; [strategyproofness](strategyproofness.md) asks whether agents can benefit by misrepresenting their preferences.
