# Gene amplification

↑ **Parent:** [Mutation](mutation.md)

An increase in genomic copy number of a [gene](gene.md) or region. It can raise expression or activity, but an amplified [gene](gene.md) may instead accompany another selected alteration. It is distinct from laboratory [PCR amplification](polymerase-chain-reaction.md).
