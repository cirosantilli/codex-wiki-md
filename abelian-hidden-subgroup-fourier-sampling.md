# Abelian hidden-subgroup Fourier sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Hidden subgroup problem](hidden-subgroup-problem.md)

Measuring the [coset state](coset-state.md) $|g_0+K\rangle$ in the [group shift operator](group-shift-operator.md) [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md) samples uniformly from the [annihilator of a subgroup of a finite abelian group](annihilator-of-a-subgroup-of-a-finite-abelian-group.md) $K^\perp$. Indeed,

$$
\langle v_\chi|g_0+K\rangle
=\frac{\chi(g_0)}{\sqrt{|G||K|}}\sum_{k\in K}\chi(k),
$$

so the [character-sum cancellation lemma](character-sum-cancellation-lemma.md) gives probability $|K|/|G|$ on $K^\perp$ and zero elsewhere. The [modulus](modulus.md) of $\chi(g_0)$ is one, so the offset has no effect on the distribution.
