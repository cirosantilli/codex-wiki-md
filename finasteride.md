# Finasteride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[Finasteride](finasteride.md) is an unsaturated 4-azasteroid inhibitor of steroid [5α-reductase](5-alpha-reductase.md). Enzyme-generated reduction and [enolate](enolate.md) capture of oxidized cofactor give a covalent cofactor–inhibitor adduct; the protein itself need not be covalently modified.
