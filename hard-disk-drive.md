# Hard disk drive

↑ **Parent:** [Data storage device](data-storage-device.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_disk_drive)

A [data storage device](data-storage-device.md) that records information magnetically on rotating platters. Accessing a sector can require moving a head to its track, waiting for the sector to rotate beneath it, and transferring the data. These contributions are [seek time](seek-time.md), [rotational latency](rotational-latency.md) and [data transfer time](data-transfer-time.md).[Chapter 37, Hard Disk Drives, §37.3, seek, rotation and transfer phases](operating-systems-three-easy-pieces-by-remzi-h-arpaci-dusseau-and-others.md)
