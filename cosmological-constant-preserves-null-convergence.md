# Cosmological constant preserves null convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Null convergence condition](null-convergence-condition.md)

Contract the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) with a [null vector](null-vector.md). Both the trace term and the [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) term multiply $g_{ab}\ell^a\ell^b=0$ and disappear. The [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md) therefore still implies the [null convergence condition](null-convergence-condition.md), independently of the sign of the [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md). A [black-hole area theorem](hawking-s-area-theorem.md) still needs the appropriate global horizon assumptions as well as this local focusing condition.
