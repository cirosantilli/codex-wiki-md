# Quasi-isometrically embedded subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md)

A finitely generated subgroup $H\leq G$ is quasi-isometrically embedded when its inclusion, equipped with word metrics from finite generating sets, is a [quasi-isometric embedding](quasi-isometric-embedding.md). This property is independent of those generating sets.
