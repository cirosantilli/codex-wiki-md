# Gauge protection of a vector mass

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry protection of a small mass](symmetry-protection-of-a-small-mass.md)

An unbroken [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md) forbids an explicit local Proca mass for its gauge vector. A [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md) can generate that mass through a charged [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md), but the stability of the resulting vector scale then depends on the scalar sector that fixes the expectation. Gauge invariance alone does not protect a fundamental [scalar mass](scalar-mass.md).
