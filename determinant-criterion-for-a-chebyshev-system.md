# Determinant criterion for a Chebyshev system

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev system](chebyshev-system.md)

A family of $n+1$ real [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) is a [Chebyshev system](chebyshev-system.md) exactly when every evaluation [matrix](matrix.md) at $n+1$ distinct points is invertible. A singular evaluation matrix supplies a nonzero coefficient vector whose function vanishes at those points; conversely, a violation of the zero bound supplies such a vector in the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md). This also gives the existence and uniqueness of interpolation from its span.
