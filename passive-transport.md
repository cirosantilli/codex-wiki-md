# Passive transport

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passive_transport)

[Passive transport](passive-transport.md) is net movement down the transported substance's [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) without coupling that movement to another source of [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md). [Facilitated diffusion](facilitated-diffusion.md) is [passive transport](passive-transport.md) through an [ion channel](ion-channel.md) or a passive [carrier protein](carrier-protein.md). Providing such a route can speed passage, but does not turn an uphill transfer into a downhill one. For an uncharged [solute](solute.md) under the ideal conditions above, net [passive transport](passive-transport.md) runs from higher to lower [concentration](concentration.md); for an [ion](ion.md), the [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) also matters. See [Cooper, The Cell: transport of small molecules](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9847/).

In the figure, stipulate eight neutral [solute](solute.md) tracers in A and two in B, with equal fixed [volumes](volume.md), identical surroundings, and no imposed [electric potential](electric-potential.md) difference. The [concentration](concentration.md) ratio is $c_{\mathrm A}/c_{\mathrm B}=8/2=4$, so A-to-B transfer has $\Delta\widetilde\mu=-RT\ln4\simeq-1.38629RT$, or $-3.458\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ at the assumed [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T=300\,\mathrm K$. If this [solute](solute.md) can cross, [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) has equal expected [concentrations](concentration.md): five tracers per side on average. Individual crossings occur in both directions; an observed ten-particle snapshot need not contain exactly five on each side. These stipulated counts illustrate a driving force, not a measured flow rate or a prediction of the time to equilibrate.

For a separate illustrative [ion](ion.md) calculation, keep $c_{\mathrm A}/c_{\mathrm B}=4$, but assign $z=+1$ and $V_{\mathrm A}-V_{\mathrm B}=-60\,\mathrm{mV}$ at $300\,\mathrm K$. Using $R\simeq8.31446\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ and $F\simeq96485.33\,\mathrm{C\,mol^{-1}}$, B-to-A transfer gives $RT\ln4-0.060F\simeq3.458-5.789=-2.331\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. Thus an appropriate open [ion channel](ion-channel.md) can permit [passive transport](passive-transport.md) toward the higher [concentration](concentration.md): the electrical contribution outweighs the opposing [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) contribution. This stipulated [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) is not inferred from the tracer counts.
