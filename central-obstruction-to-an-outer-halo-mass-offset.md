# Central obstruction to an outer halo mass offset

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical mass profile for a flat rotation curve](spherical-mass-profile-for-a-flat-rotation-curve.md)

Subtracting a finite stellar mass from the distant spherical mass required for a flat [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md) gives the displayed asymptotic offset. Extending it to $r=0$ would give negative enclosed [mass](mass.md), so it cannot define a physical inner halo. Keeping only its positive leading term instead gives a singular isothermal halo with density proportional to $r^{-2}$ and a nonzero limiting central circular speed. These are different extrapolations, and neither is forced by an outer rotation curve.
