# Bicyclic graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph excess](graph-excess.md)

A connected bicyclic graph has two independent cycles, equivalently excess one. Its [2-core](2-core-of-a-graph.md) has the same excess and every removed vertex lies in a tree attached to the core. The [graph kernel](graph-kernel.md) captures the finite collection of possible branching structures.
