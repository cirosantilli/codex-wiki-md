# Sideband spectrum of a real Ginzburg-Landau plane wave

↑ **Parent:** [Eckhaus instability](eckhaus-instability.md)

For a [real Ginzburg–Landau equation](real-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) plane wave with amplitude squared $b/g>0$, perturbing its amplitude and phase couples Fourier modes on either side of its carrier [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). The displayed [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) follow from the real-linear two-component perturbation equation. Their small-$p$ phase branch is $-\xi(\mu-3\xi q^2)p^2/(\mu-\xi q^2)+O(p^4)$, so the robust infinite-domain stable band is $q^2<\mu/(3\xi)$. On a finite periodic envelope domain only discrete $p$ are allowed; an instability requires an allowed $p$ with $p^2<6q^2-2\mu/\xi$. Uniform amplitude perturbations alone cannot determine this [Eckhaus instability](eckhaus-instability.md).
