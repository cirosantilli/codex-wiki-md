# Receptor quantum catch

↑ **Parent:** [Colour vision model](colour-vision-model.md)

Expected photon absorption by a receptor class. A simple relative model is $Q_i=\int L(\lambda)R(\lambda)S_i(\lambda)\,d\lambda$, with illumination $L$, object [reflectance](reflectance.md) $R$ and effective [spectral sensitivity](spectral-sensitivity.md) $S_i$. Absolute catch also requires geometric and temporal factors.
