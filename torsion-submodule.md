# Torsion submodule

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion module](torsion-module.md)

For a module $M$ over an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $R$, its torsion submodule is

$$
T(M)=\{m\in M:rm=0\text{ for some }0\ne r\in R\}.
$$

The domain condition makes this a [submodule](submodule.md): products of nonzero annihilators remain nonzero and annihilate sums.
