# Substrate-level phosphorylation

↑ **Parent:** [Energy metabolism](energy-metabolism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substrate-level_phosphorylation)

[Substrate-level phosphorylation](substrate-level-phosphorylation.md) forms [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) by [enzyme](enzyme-split.md)-catalyzed transfer of a [phosphate](phosphate.md) group from a phosphorylated [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) to [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md). In canonical [glycolysis](glycolysis.md), two such transfers occur for each three-[carbon](carbon.md) route, giving four gross [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) per [glucose](glucose.md). Unlike [oxidative phosphorylation](oxidative-phosphorylation.md), these transfers do not require an [electron transport chain](electron-transport-chain.md).[§7.2, steps 7 and 10: phosphate transfer to ADP; §7.5, opening comparison of fermentation with respiration](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)

The [pyruvate kinase](pyruvate-kinase.md) example under [Coupled-enzyme readout of glycolytic ATP formation](coupled-enzyme-readout-of-glycolytic-atp-formation.md) separates the material transfer from its detection. A favourable coupled [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) supplies the driving change in [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md); the [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) supplies a catalytic route. Counting [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md)-forming transfers gives a gross yield, while subtracting upstream [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) use gives the net yield.
