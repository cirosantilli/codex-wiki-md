# Product of two independent standard normal random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md)

If $Z_1,Z_2$ are independent $N(0,1)$ random variables, their product has [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) $(1+t^2)^{-1/2}$ and density $K_0(|x|)/\pi$, where $K_0$ is a [modified Bessel function](modified-bessel-function.md). A scale multiple $aZ_1Z_2$ has the corresponding scaled distribution.
