# First-order clause

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

A finite [logical disjunction](logical-disjunction.md) of atomic [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) or their negations, normally understood to have all its [free variables](free-variable.md) universally quantified. An empty [logical disjunction](logical-disjunction.md) is false. A [Horn clause](horn-clause.md) has at most one positive atom.
