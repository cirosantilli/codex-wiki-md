# Airy isostasy

↑ **Parent:** [Isostasy](isostasy.md)

A local compensation model with approximately fixed crustal [density](density.md) and variable crustal thickness. A height $h$ is supported by a crustal root $b=\rho_c h/(\rho_m-\rho_c)$ in a simple constant-density, air-loaded column model.
