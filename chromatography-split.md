# Chromatography

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatography)

[Chromatography](chromatography-split.md) separates components of a [mixture](mixture-split.md) through different migration rates in contact with a [chromatographic stationary phase](chromatographic-stationary-phase.md) while a [chromatographic mobile phase](chromatographic-mobile-phase.md) transports them. [Liquid chromatography](liquid-chromatography.md) describes the physical state of the transporting phase; [size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md) describes a separation mechanism. Classification by phase and classification by mechanism answer different questions. In ideal [size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md), differential access to liquid-filled pores replaces binding as the source of differential migration.

In many other forms of [chromatography](chromatography-split.md), a [solute](solute.md) repeatedly leaves the [chromatographic mobile phase](chromatographic-mobile-phase.md) and returns to it. Reversible [adsorption](adsorption.md) holds it at a surface; partition distributes it between the [chromatographic mobile phase](chromatographic-mobile-phase.md) and an immobilized liquid [chromatographic stationary phase](chromatographic-stationary-phase.md). Spending more time stationary means less progress with the moving fluid. Two initially overlapping components can therefore emerge as separate bands without either being permanently trapped. [Chromatography](chromatography-split.md) is useful both for comparing a sample with reference substances and for collecting separated components; a visible band need not contain just one substance.

Relative retention belongs to the sample together with the chosen [chromatographic stationary phase](chromatographic-stationary-phase.md), [chromatographic mobile phase](chromatographic-mobile-phase.md), [eluent](eluent.md) composition, and [temperature](temperature-split.md). Changing these conditions can change the separation order. [Chemical polarity](chemical-polarity.md) helps explain interactions in some systems but is not a universal ranking rule. In [thin-layer chromatography](thin-layer-chromatography.md), a spot's [TLC retardation factor](tlc-retardation-factor.md) is consequently conditional evidence about identity, rather than a fixed identifier intrinsic to a [molecule](molecule-split.md).
