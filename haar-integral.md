# Haar integral

↑ **Parent:** [Haar measure](haar-measure.md)

A Haar integral integrates a function against [Haar measure](haar-measure.md). On a compact [group](group-split.md), normalizing the measure to total mass one makes it an averaging operation. Translation invariance then implies that the average of a nontrivial continuous unitary character is zero: translating the integral multiplies it by a character value different from one.
