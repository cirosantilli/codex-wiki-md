# Intracluster light

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar halo](stellar-halo.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intracluster_light)

[Intracluster light](intracluster-light.md) comes from [stars](star.md) bound to a cluster rather than an individual [galaxy](galaxy-split.md). [Tidal stripping](tidal-stripping.md) and disruption of member systems can create extended diffuse light and substructure. Distinguishing this component from the outer envelope of the central galaxy depends on the adopted dynamical or photometric definition.
