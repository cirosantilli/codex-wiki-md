# Schauder solution of a small Hammerstein integral equation

↑ **Parent:** [Schauder fixed-point theorem](schauder-fixed-point-theorem.md)

For [continuous](continuous-function.md) $G$ and $f$, restrict to the closed [supremum-norm](supremum-norm.md) ball of radius $R$ in $C([0,1])$. Boundedness on the compact [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) and nonlinearity boxes makes the [integral](integral-split.md) map preserve this ball for sufficiently small positive $\mu$. [Uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md) of $f$ proves [continuity](continuous-function.md) of the map; [uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md) of $G$ gives an [equicontinuous](equicontinuity.md) [bounded](boundedness.md) image. [Arzelà-Ascoli](arzela-ascoli-theorem.md) makes that image relatively compact, and [Schauder](schauder-fixed-point-theorem.md) gives a solution. No global [Lipschitz](lipschitz-continuity.md) hypothesis or uniqueness conclusion is needed.
