# Stabilizer generator

↑ **Parent:** [Stabilizer group](stabilizer-group.md)

A set of stabilizer generators is an independent commuting family of Hermitian [Pauli operators](pauli-operator.md) whose products form a [stabilizer group](stabilizer-group.md).
