# Pressure-volume work

↑ **Parent:** [Work](work.md)

The [work](work.md) done by a uniform [pressure](pressure-split.md) $P$ on an expanding boundary is $P\,dV$. With [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) $U$, the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) for an adiabatically expanding region reads $dU=-P\,dV$. For a homogeneous cosmological [perfect fluid](perfect-fluid.md), $U=\rho V$ and $V\propto a^3$ give the [cosmological continuity equation](cosmological-continuity-equation.md) $\dot\rho+3H(\rho+P)=0$.
