# Infinite cyclic group has no chief series

↑ **Parent:** [Chief series](chief-series.md)

A hypothetical finite [chief series](chief-series.md) in $\mathbb Z$ has a first nonzero term $n\mathbb Z$. The subgroup $2n\mathbb Z$ is normal in $\mathbb Z$ and strictly between zero and that term. This contradicts the defining maximality condition for the first [chief factor](chief-factor.md). Finite [groups](group-split.md) always admit [chief series](chief-series.md), by choosing a maximal chain of their [normal subgroups](normal-subgroup.md); this example shows why finiteness matters.
