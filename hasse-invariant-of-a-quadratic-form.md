# Hasse invariant of a quadratic form

↑ **Parent:** [Hasse-Minkowski theorem](hasse-minkowski-theorem.md)

For a nondegenerate diagonal [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) $q=\langle a_1,\ldots,a_m\rangle$, this product of [quadratic Hilbert symbols](quadratic-hilbert-symbol.md) is independent of diagonalization. Over a [p-adic field](p-adic-field.md), dimension, determinant square class and this invariant classify the form up to isometry. The product of the local invariants of a globally diagonalized form is one by the [Hilbert reciprocity law](hilbert-reciprocity-law.md); real places additionally record signature.
