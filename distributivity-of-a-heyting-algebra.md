# Distributivity of a Heyting algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra.md)

In a [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra.md), meet with a fixed element is left adjoint to Heyting implication, so it preserves joins:

$$
a\wedge(b\vee c)=(a\wedge b)\vee(a\wedge c).
$$

The dual distributive law follows from this one by the lattice absorption laws.
