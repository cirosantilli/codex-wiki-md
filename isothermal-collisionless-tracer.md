# Isothermal collisionless tracer

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical Jeans equation](spherical-jeans-equation.md)

A steady [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) depending only on [relative energy](relative-energy.md) $E=\psi-v^2/2$ is isotropic and has zero mean [velocity](velocity.md). If its one-dimensional [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) is constant, the [Spherical Jeans equation](spherical-jeans-equation.md) reduces to $\sigma_0^2\rho'=\rho\psi'$, yielding the displayed density. This is a tracer relation in a specified [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md), not a requirement that the tracer generate that potential. With no escape-energy cutoff its local [velocity](velocity.md) distribution is [Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-velocity-distribution.md).
