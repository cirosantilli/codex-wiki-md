# Hadamard finite-part reciprocal-power distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Hadamard finite-part integral](hadamard-finite-part-integral.md)

For $m\geq2$, the symmetric [Hadamard finite-part integral](hadamard-finite-part-integral.md) defining $\Lambda_m$ subtracts the [Taylor polynomial](taylor-polynomial.md) through degree $m-2$ from a [test function](test-function.md) before division by $x^m$. It has finite [order of a distribution](order-of-a-distribution.md) and satisfies

$$
\Lambda_m=\frac{(-1)^{m-1}}{(m-1)!}\left(\frac d{dx}\right)^m\log|x|.
$$

The lower-order subtraction terms are integrable at infinity, while the remaining odd $1/x$ singularity cancels under symmetric truncation.
