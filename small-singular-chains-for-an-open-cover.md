# Small singular chains for an open cover

↑ **Parent:** [Singular homology](singular-homology.md)

For an open cover, take the subcomplex generated by singular simplices whose images lie in single cover members. Repeated [barycentric subdivision](barycentric-subdivision.md) makes each finite singular chain small, and the standard subdivision chain homotopy shows that inclusion induces homology isomorphisms. For a two-set cover, the short exact sequence of these small chains gives the [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md); excising the complement of one open set from the pair with the other set gives its equivalent relative-homology formulation.
