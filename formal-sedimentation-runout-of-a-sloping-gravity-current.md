# Formal sedimentation runout of a sloping gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-settling attenuation of a sloping gravity current](weak-settling-attenuation-of-a-sloping-gravity-current.md)

Extrapolating the leading [weak-settling attenuation of a sloping gravity current](weak-settling-attenuation-of-a-sloping-gravity-current.md) to zero flux gives $x_{\max}-x_0=(h_0/E)[\exp(3\beta_*EB_o^{1/3}/v_s)-1]$. Since speed tends to zero there, the weak-settling assumption fails in the terminal region; this is a formal leading-model estimate, not a uniformly accurate full-flow runout.
