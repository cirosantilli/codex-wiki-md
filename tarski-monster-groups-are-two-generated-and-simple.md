# Tarski monster groups are two-generated and simple

↑ **Parent:** [Tarski monster group](tarski-monster-group.md)

For a [Tarski monster group](tarski-monster-group.md), any nonidentity element generates a proper subgroup of order $p$. Adjoining an element outside that subgroup generates the whole group. A proper nontrivial normal subgroup would have order $p$; conjugation into its finite automorphism group forces it to be central. Combining it with another order-$p$ cyclic subgroup would then give a proper subgroup of order $p^2$, a contradiction.
