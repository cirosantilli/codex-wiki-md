# Neutron star merger

↑ **Parent:** [Binary star](binary-star.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutron_star_merger)

Coalescence of a binary containing two [neutron stars](neutron-star.md) can eject neutron-rich material and power a [kilonova](kilonova.md). [Rapid neutron-capture process](r-process.md) reactions in the ejecta produce heavy elements. This connects compact-binary dynamics to chemical enrichment.
