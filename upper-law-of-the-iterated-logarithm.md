# Upper law of the iterated logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Law of the iterated logarithm](law-of-the-iterated-logarithm.md)

For a [simple symmetric random walk](simple-symmetric-random-walk.md), geometric blocking and the [Borel-Cantelli first lemma](borel-cantelli-first-lemma.md) yield the upper estimate $\limsup_n S_n/\sqrt{2n\log\log n}\le1$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). Equality requires a separate lower-bound argument.
