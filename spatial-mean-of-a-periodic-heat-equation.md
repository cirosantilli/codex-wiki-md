# Spatial mean of a periodic heat equation

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

Integrate $u_t=\alpha\Delta u+Q$ over a periodic cell. Opposite boundary contributions to the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) cancel, so the spatial [mean](expected-value-split.md) obeys $\overline u'=\overline Q$. With no source the mean is conserved, although the local [temperature](temperature-split.md) changes. The mean is the [zero Fourier mode](zero-fourier-mode.md) in a normalized [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) convention.
