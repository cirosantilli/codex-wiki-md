# Annular Caccioppoli inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Caccioppoli inequality](caccioppoli-inequality.md)

For a [weak solution](weak-solution.md) of a [uniformly elliptic](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) homogeneous [divergence-form elliptic operator](divergence-form-elliptic-operator.md), testing with $\eta^2(u-c)$ and choosing the [gradient](gradient.md) of the [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) in $B_{2R}\setminus B_R$ gives $\int_{B_R}|\nabla u|^2\leq CR^{-2}\int_{B_{2R}\setminus B_R}|u-c|^2$. The arbitrary constant $c$ can be chosen as an annular average, which makes the estimate suitable for a [hole-filling argument](hole-filling-argument.md).
