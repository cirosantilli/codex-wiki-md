# Shift-refresh chain on a hypercube

↑ **Parent:** [Cutoff for Markov chains](cutoff-for-markov-chains.md)

A shift-refresh chain cyclically moves a pointer through the coordinates of a bit vector and replaces the visited bit by an independent fair bit. Once the pointer walk has visited every residue, all coordinates have been refreshed, giving a [strong stationary time](strong-stationary-time.md).
