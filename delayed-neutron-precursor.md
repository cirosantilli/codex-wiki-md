# Delayed neutron precursor

↑ **Parent:** [Delayed neutron](delayed-neutron.md)

A radioactive [fission product](fission-product.md) whose decay chain can populate a neutron-emitting daughter state. A simplified kinetics model can replace many such species by one effective population and lifetime, losing the distinct decay-time spectrum.
