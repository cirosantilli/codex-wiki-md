# Hydrodynamic mobility matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic resistance matrix](hydrodynamic-resistance-matrix.md)

With a consistent force convention, the mobility matrix maps applied generalized forces to rigid-body velocities; it is the inverse of the [hydrodynamic resistance matrix](hydrodynamic-resistance-matrix.md). For a sphere with force on the fluid $\mathbf F$, its isolated mobility is $1/(6\pi\mu a)$. Coupling between bodies represents [hydrodynamic interactions](hydrodynamic-interaction.md).
