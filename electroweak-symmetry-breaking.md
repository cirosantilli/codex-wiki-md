# Electroweak symmetry breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electroweak_symmetry_breaking)

Electroweak symmetry breaking is the Higgs-field breaking $SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\to U(1)_Q$. It gives masses to the $W$ and $Z$ bosons while leaving the photon massless.

With the [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) convention $Q=T_3+Y$, the lower component of the [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md) has $T_3=-1/2$ and $Y=1/2$. Thus $Q\langle\phi\rangle=0$: the [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) is electrically neutral. This is why one combination of the neutral [gauge fields](gauge-field.md) remains the massless [photon](photon.md), as the [electroweak doublet gauge-boson mass matrix](electroweak-doublet-gauge-boson-mass-matrix.md) shows.

Continue the chosen [Higgs field potential](higgs-field-potential.md) example with $g=3/5$ and $g'=4/5$. The [tree-level electroweak gauge-boson masses](tree-level-electroweak-gauge-boson-masses.md) are $m_W=72\,\mathrm{GeV}$, $m_Z=120\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $m_A=0$. In the $(W^3,B)$ basis,

$$
M_{\rm neutral}^2=\begin{pmatrix}5184&-6912\\-6912&9216\end{pmatrix}\mathrm{GeV}^2.
$$

Its orthonormal [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) $(4/5,3/5)^T$ and $(3/5,-4/5)^T$ have [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) zero and $14400\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$. Thus $\cos\theta_W=3/5=m_W/m_Z$. This is an exact model check, not a fit to measured [electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md) parameters.

A diagonal charged-fermion [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md) expands as

$$
-\frac{y_f}{\sqrt2}(v+H)\bar f f=-m_f\bar f f-\frac{m_f}{v}H\bar f f,\qquad m_f=\frac{y_fv}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md) remains a free parameter: the mechanism relates a [fermion](fermion.md) mass to its scalar interaction but does not predict the numerical mass. This example concerns charged [fermions](fermion.md); the minimal [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) has no right-handed [neutrino](neutrino.md) and does not account for [neutrino oscillations](neutrino-oscillation.md) without an extension.
