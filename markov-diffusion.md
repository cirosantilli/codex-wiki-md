# Markov diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md)

A Markov diffusion is a continuous-path [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) whose local evolution is described by a drift vector and diffusion matrix. For sufficiently regular coefficients, its [infinitesimal generator](infinitesimal-generator-stochastic-processes.md) is a second-order differential operator and its conditional expectations satisfy a [Kolmogorov backward equation](kolmogorov-backward-equation.md).
