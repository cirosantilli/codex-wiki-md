# Dust sublimation

↑ **Parent:** [Sublimation](sublimation.md)

An irradiated grain loses solid material by [sublimation](sublimation.md). Its lifetime depends strongly on temperature and composition. In a planetary [dust tail](dust-tail.md), a short lifetime can truncate the distribution well before a relative orbital wrap.
