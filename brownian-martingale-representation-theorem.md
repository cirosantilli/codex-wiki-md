# Brownian martingale representation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale representation theorem](martingale-representation-theorem.md)

Every square-integrable random variable measurable with respect to a Brownian filtration can be written as its expectation plus an [Itô integral](ito-integral.md) against that Brownian motion. Equivalently, every square-integrable martingale in that filtration has the form

$$
M_t=M_0+\int_0^tH_s\,dW_s
$$

for a predictable square-integrable process $H$.
