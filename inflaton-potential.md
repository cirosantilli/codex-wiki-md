# Inflaton potential

↑ **Parent:** [Inflaton](inflaton.md)

The [inflaton potential](inflaton-potential.md) is the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) density of an [inflaton](inflaton.md) as a function of its field value. Its derivative enters the [inflaton equation of motion](inflaton-equation-of-motion.md), while its slope and curvature enter the [potential slow-roll parameters](potential-slow-roll-parameter.md).
