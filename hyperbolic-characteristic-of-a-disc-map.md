# Hyperbolic characteristic of a disc map

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic derivative density](hyperbolic-derivative-density.md)

This logarithmic area integral measures the accumulated pullback [hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md) area of a [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) disc map. Its nonnegative kernel makes it increasing in $R$. [Schwarz-Pick theorem](schwarz-pick-theorem.md) bounds $H_g(z)$ by $2/(1-|z|^2)$, which controls its characteristic through a critical-point Jensen identity.
