# Force-free inclusion reduces axial mobility of a centred settling sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-force comparison of minimum viscous dissipation](fixed-force-comparison-of-minimum-viscous-dissipation.md)

Take the original [sphere](sphere.md) on the tube axis, with diagonal positive [hydrodynamic resistance matrix](hydrodynamic-resistance-matrix.md) $R_0$ and axial [force](force.md) $W>0$. Add a freely moving neutral [sphere](sphere.md) and extend its rigid [velocity](velocity.md) through its interior. The resulting field is admissible in the original domain, so its dissipation obeys $D>q^TR_0q\geq R_{zz}V_z^2$ for any finite nonoverlapping placement of the extra [sphere](sphere.md). Since only the first [sphere](sphere.md) does external work, $D=WV_z$, giving the displayed bound without assuming zero transverse [velocity](velocity.md) or rotation. Strictness follows because equality would require the original nontrivial analytic flow to have zero strain throughout the added ball.
