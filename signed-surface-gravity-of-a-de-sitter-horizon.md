# Signed surface gravity of a de Sitter horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity](surface-gravity.md)

For the future-directed central-time [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) in a [static patch of de Sitter spacetime](static-patch-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md), the outgoing null chart gives the future branch coefficient $+1/R$ in $\nabla_k k=\kappa k$. The ingoing chart gives $-1/R$ on the past branch. Both follow from $d(k^2)=-2\kappa k^\flat$; the cross-term sign changes the horizon covector. The physical [Hawking temperature](hawking-temperature.md) uses $|\kappa|$, not a signed negative temperature.
