# Radial current dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Current dipole](current-dipole.md)

A [radial current dipole](radial-current-dipole.md) has moment parallel to its displacement from the sphere centre. The [Sarvas formula](sarvas-formula.md) gives identically zero exterior [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) for such a source in a [spherical volume conductor model](spherical-volume-conductor-model.md), although its [electric potential](electric-potential.md) need not vanish. This is a nonuniqueness mechanism for the inverse problem in [magnetoencephalography](magnetoencephalography.md).
