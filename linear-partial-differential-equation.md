# Linear partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)

A linear partial differential equation is a [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) in which the unknown function and its [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) occur linearly, with coefficients independent of the unknown. Its homogeneous solutions obey the [superposition principle](superposition-principle.md).

For example, the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) permits adding independently decaying [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md). For an equation $Lu=f$ with a fixed nonzero [PDE source term](source-term-in-a-partial-differential-equation.md), adding two solutions gives $L(u+v)=2f$, so the sum generally solves a different problem. Likewise, adding solutions adds their [initial conditions](initial-condition.md) and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
