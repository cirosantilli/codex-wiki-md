# Signed normal Wald statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Wald test](wald-test.md)

For an independent normal sample with known standard deviation $\sigma$, the signed [Wald statistic](wald-test.md) for testing mean zero is $W=\sqrt n\bar Y/\sigma$. It has the [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) under the [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md) and distribution $N(\sqrt n\delta/\sigma,1)$ under mean $\delta$. Its square has a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md); retaining the sign permits a one-sided test.
