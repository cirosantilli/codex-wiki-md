# Pseudoinstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Assembly language](assembly-language.md)

An assembler convenience that expands into one or more actual [machine instructions](machine-instruction.md). Its expansion matters when counting instructions, reasoning about temporary registers, or filling a [branch delay slot](branch-delay-slot.md).
