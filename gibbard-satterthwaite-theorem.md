# Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Strategyproofness](strategyproofness.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibbard–Satterthwaite_theorem)

With a finite set of at least three alternatives and unrestricted strict preference orders, every onto deterministic [strategyproof](strategyproofness.md) [social choice function](social-choice-function.md) is a [dictatorship in social choice](dictatorship-in-social-choice.md). Restricting to two alternatives or to a restricted preference domain removes essential hypotheses.
