# Spin orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial orbital](spatial-orbital.md)

When spatial and [spin](spin.md) factors separate, a [spin orbital](spin-orbital.md) combines a [spatial orbital](spatial-orbital.md) $\phi_a$ with a normalized [spin](spin.md) state $\chi_\sigma$. For an [Electron](electron.md), choose [spin one-half along an axis](spin-one-half-along-an-axis.md) with $\sigma=\uparrow,\downarrow$. The two [spin orbitals](spin-orbital.md) $u_{a\uparrow}$ and $u_{a\downarrow}$ are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) even though their [spatial orbital](spatial-orbital.md) is identical, since their [inner product](inner-product.md) contains $\langle\chi_\uparrow|\chi_\downarrow\rangle=0$. When spatial and [spin](spin.md) variables do not separate, the complete [one-particle state](one-particle-state.md) is instead a multicomponent [wavefunction](wave-function.md) $u(\mathbf r,s)$; exclusion still applies to that complete [quantum state](quantum-state.md).
