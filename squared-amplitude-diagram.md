# Squared-amplitude diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A squared-amplitude diagram consists of an amplitude graph and its complex-conjugate graph, with matching external states. Summing an external fermion spin sews the associated spinor indices using a [fermion spin sum](fermion-spin-sum.md). The resulting closed spinor chains become [gamma-matrix traces](gamma-matrix-trace.md). This notation represents probabilities and must be distinguished from adding another physical interaction loop to the amplitude.
