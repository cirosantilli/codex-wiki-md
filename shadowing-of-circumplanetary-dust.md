# Shadowing of circumplanetary dust

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation pressure on circumplanetary dust](radiation-pressure-on-circumplanetary-dust.md)

A [planet](planet.md) can block stellar radiation during part of a [circumplanetary orbit](circumplanetary-orbit.md). Then the full-orbit constant-flux averages for [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) and [Poynting–Robertson drag](poynting-robertson-drag.md) require modification. The detailed shadow duty cycle depends on the [orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md), stellar angular extent and dust radius.
