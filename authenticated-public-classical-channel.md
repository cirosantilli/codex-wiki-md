# Authenticated public classical channel

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum key distribution](quantum-key-distribution.md)

A communication channel whose messages are public but whose origin and integrity are authenticated. In [quantum key distribution](quantum-key-distribution.md), Eve may read basis announcements and reconciliation data, but must not be able to replace them while impersonating a legitimate party. Authentication itself consumes a prior trust resource; the quantum transmission does not authenticate the classical peer automatically.
