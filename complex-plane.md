# Complex plane

↑ **Parent:** [Complex number](complex-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_plane)

The [complex plane](complex-plane.md) identifies the [complex number](complex-number.md) $x+iy$ with the point $(x,y)$ in [Cartesian coordinates](cartesian-coordinate-system.md). The horizontal coordinate is its [real part](real-part.md), the vertical coordinate its [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md). Arrows from zero identify the same points with [vectors](vector.md). Adding $1+i$ moves $3+4i$ to $4+5i$: every point undergoes the same [translation](translation-geometry.md).

[Multiplication](multiplication.md) has a richer geometry. Multiplying by the [imaginary unit](imaginary-unit.md) sends $x+iy$ to $-y+ix$, a counterclockwise quarter-turn [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md). In particular $i(3+4i)=-4+3i$. Multiplying by $1+i$ rotates through $\pi/4$ and scales every [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) by $\sqrt2$. More generally, [multiplication](multiplication.md) by $a+ib$ acts through the real [matrix](matrix.md)

$$
\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}\longmapsto
\begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\b&a\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}.
$$

For $a+ib\ne0$, this preserves oriented [angles](angle.md) between nonzero displacement [vectors](vector.md): every direction turns equally. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is $a^2+b^2>0$, while its common scale is $\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$. This is the elementary geometric reason [complex numbers](complex-number.md) are so useful for [conformal maps](conformal-map.md).

The name Argand plane recalls Jean-Robert Argand's geometric interpretation. In his 1813 article he explicitly reports publishing his Essai in 1806; the original article is available in [Argand, Annales de mathématiques, volume 4, page 133](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Annales_de_math%C3%A9matiques_pures_et_appliqu%C3%A9es,_1813-1814,_Tome_4.djvu/141). A [BnF scan of the 1874 second edition](https://www.hist-math.fr/textes/Argand1874_QuantitesImaginaires.pdf) preserves the essay and its geometric drawings. Reading these alongside a modern [complex plane](complex-plane.md) reveals how directions and lengths give arithmetic a visible meaning.
