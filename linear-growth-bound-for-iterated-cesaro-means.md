<h1 id="linear-growth-bound-for-iterated-cesaro-means">Linear-growth bound for iterated Cesàro means</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cesàro mean](cesaro-mean.md)

Let $Ca_n=n^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^n a_j$ be the [Cesàro mean](cesaro-mean.md) of a [sequence](sequence.md). If $|a_n|\leq Cn$ for every positive [integer](integer-split.md) $n$, the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) gives $|Ca_n|\leq C(n+1)/2\leq Cn$. [Mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) repeats this estimate with the same constant for every iterate. An eventual $O(n)$ bound can be made global by increasing $C$ to absorb the finitely many initial terms. Consequently iterated [Cesàro means](cesaro-mean.md) preserve linear growth in [Big O notation](big-o-notation.md); this bound alone does not assert convergence or summability.
