# One-variable main conjecture for CM elliptic curves

↑ **Parent:** [Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory](main-conjecture-of-iwasawa-theory.md)

At a good split prime, the one-prime CM division tower has a pro-$p$ [Galois group](galois-group.md) isomorphic to $\mathbb Z_p$. Its prime-ramified [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md) is torsion on each finite-character component. The main conjecture identifies its [characteristic ideal](characteristic-ideal.md) with the corresponding elliptic-unit [p-adic L-function](p-adic-l-function.md). The [class-field unit sequence](class-field-unit-sequence.md) and equality of characteristic ideals of global-units modulo elliptic-units and of class groups prove the identification. The cyclotomic Selmer formulation requires the appropriate CM twist and two-variable specialization; the one-prime tower is not the cyclotomic tower.
