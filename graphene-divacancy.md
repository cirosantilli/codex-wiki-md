# Graphene divacancy

↑ **Parent:** [Graphene vacancy defect](graphene-vacancy-defect.md)

A [graphene vacancy defect](graphene-vacancy-defect.md) with two carbon atoms removed. A common adjacent-pair reconstruction has a 5–8–5 ring arrangement; other reconstructed arrangements exist and can be selected by energetic processing.
