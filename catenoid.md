# Catenoid

↑ **Parent:** [Surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catenoid)

A catenoid is the surface of revolution obtained by rotating a [catenary](catenary.md) about its directrix. One conformal parametrization is

$$
(u,v)\mapsto(\cosh u\cos v,\cosh u\sin v,u).
$$
