# Fully developed flow

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)

A fully developed parallel flow has a velocity profile independent of the streamwise coordinate. In a planar channel a representative profile is $\mathbf u=u(z)\hat{\mathbf x}$; wall conditions and translational symmetry remove cross-stream motion. The streamwise [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) may remain nonzero, while the convective acceleration vanishes. Fully developed flow is a modeling branch, not a uniqueness assertion for all flows in that geometry.
