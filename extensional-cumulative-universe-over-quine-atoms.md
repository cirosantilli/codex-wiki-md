# Extensional cumulative universe over Quine atoms

↑ **Parent:** [Quine atom](quine-atom.md)

Start with distinct formal atoms $q\in A$ with membership extensions $\{q\}$. Recursively adjoin a unique representative $S(X)$ of every [subset](subset.md) $X$ of a previous stage, except that $S(\{q\})=q$ uses the atom already present. Define membership by these extensions. Each set-sized collection of objects has bounded construction rank, so it has a representative at a later stage. This gives full [extensionality](axiom-of-extensionality.md), unlike adjoining an ordinary singleton distinct from a [Quine atom](quine-atom.md). Definable [subsets](subset.md) and ranges, [unions](set-union.md) and power [sets](set-split.md) are set-sized collections in the ambient model and hence have representatives. The pure finite hierarchy supplies [infinity](infinity.md). Every permutation of the atoms extends recursively; hereditary [finite support](finite-support-in-a-permutation-action.md) restriction gives the [hereditarily finite-supported Quine-atom model](hereditarily-finite-supported-quine-atom-model.md), whose two-element atom [subsets](subset.md) have no [choice function](choice-function.md).
