# Option pricing

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)

Determining the value of a [European option](european-contingent-claim.md) or [American option](american-option.md) from a specified [financial market](financial-market.md) model and its permitted exercise rules. [Risk-neutral pricing](risk-neutral-pricing.md), [replicating strategies](replicating-strategy.md) and [optimal stopping](optimal-stopping.md) address different parts of this problem.
