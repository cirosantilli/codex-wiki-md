# Polymer overlap volume fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer overlap concentration](polymer-overlap-concentration.md)

The [polymer overlap volume fraction](polymer-overlap-volume-fraction.md) counts the segment [volume](volume.md) of one chain divided by its coil [volume](volume.md). If the segment [volume](volume.md) is $v_0$ and $R\sim bN^\nu$, then $\phi^*\sim(v_0/b^3)N^{1-3\nu}$. The site-volume convention $v_0\sim b^3$ gives $N^{-4/5}$ for the three-dimensional [Flory approximation](flory-approximation.md) and $N^{-1/2}$ for an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md). A geometric coil [volume](volume.md) such as $4\pi R_g^3/3$ changes a prefactor; segment [length](length.md) and segment [volume](volume.md) are not generally interchangeable without a model convention.
