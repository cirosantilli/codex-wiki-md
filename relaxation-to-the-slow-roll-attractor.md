# Relaxation to the slow-roll attractor

↑ **Parent:** [Slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md)

With approximately constant positive $H$ and potential slope $V'$, a homogeneous [scalar field](scalar-field.md) initially at rest solves $\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi=-V'$ with the displayed [velocity](velocity.md). The transient decays on time $1/(3H)$, leaving the [slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md) $3H\dot\phi\simeq-V'$. The fractional potential change over a [Hubble time](hubble-time.md) is of order $M_{\rm Pl}^2(V'/V)^2$. Sustained slow roll normally uses both a small slope parameter and a small absolute curvature parameter $|M_{\rm Pl}^2V''/V|$. Local derivative bounds must hold over the traversed field interval; initial small [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) alone is not a global inflation-duration theorem.
