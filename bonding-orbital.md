# Bonding orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonding_orbital)

Occupying a [bonding molecular orbital](bonding-orbital.md) favours a [chemical bond](chemical-bond.md). For the two equivalent $1s$ [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) of the [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md), the sum $\sigma_g=\sigma_+$ has constructive [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) between the [protons](proton.md). The resulting [Electron](electron.md) density in that region helps stabilize the combined system. Its lower [energy](energy.md) in this model follows from the [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), not from the plus sign alone; see [Field, MIT 5.61, lecture 25, pp. 1–2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-61-physical-chemistry-fall-2017/e9f9413400be87f6a0a512e757d52e3c_MIT5_61F17_lec25.pdf).

For example, $(\sigma_g)^2$ in [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) supplies $N_b=2$, hence [bond order](bond-order.md) one when $N_a=0$. The sum in the [molecular orbital theory](molecular-orbital-theory.md) toy has midpoint [probability density](probability-density.md) $0.303468$, whereas the difference has zero there. The normalization factors must be included in this comparison.
