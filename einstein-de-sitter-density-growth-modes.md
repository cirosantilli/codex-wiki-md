# Einstein-de Sitter density-growth modes

↑ **Parent:** [Linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md)

For pressureless [linear cosmological density perturbations](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) in an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), $H=2/(3t)$ and $4\pi G\bar\rho_m=2/(3t^2)$. The growth equation is an [Euler differential equation](cauchy-euler-equation.md) whose indicial equation is $(p-2/3)(p+1)=0$. Hence the independent modes are $\delta_+\propto t^{2/3}\propto a$ and $\delta_-\propto t^{-1}\propto a^{-3/2}$. The first is the growing [linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md), while the second is a decaying transient.
