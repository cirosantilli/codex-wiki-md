# Geodesics on a punctured circular cone

↑ **Parent:** [Local isometry from a circular cone to the plane](local-isometry-from-a-circular-cone-to-the-plane.md)

For a circular cone with intrinsic metric $d\rho^2+\lambda^2\rho^2d\theta^2$, $0<\lambda\leq1$, local development uses plane angle $\phi=\lambda\theta$. A plane straight segment that avoids the origin lifts to a [geodesic](geodesic.md). Choosing an angular difference at most $\pi$ gives plane difference at most $\pi\lambda$, so for $\lambda<1$ this constructs a joining geodesic between any two distinct points. Different permitted angle lifts can yield distinct joining geodesics; the missing apex cannot be used to join straight segments through the plane origin.
