# Gaussian creation and annihilation operators

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup](ornstein-uhlenbeck-semigroup.md)

On polynomials in $L^2$ of standard [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md), [Gaussian integration by parts](stein-s-lemma-probability.md) makes $D$ and $x-D$ adjoints, with commutator $[a^-,a^+]=I$. The [Probabilists' Hermite polynomials](probabilists-hermite-polynomial.md) are obtained by applying $a^+$ repeatedly to $1$, and $a^-\operatorname{He}_n=n\operatorname{He}_{n-1}$. These give a Gaussian-space realization of [creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md).
