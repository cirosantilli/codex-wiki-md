# Exact Gaussian overflow constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian effective bandwidth](gaussian-effective-bandwidth.md)

For a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) with mean $m$, positive [variance](variance-split.md) $v$, and $\gamma>0$, the target $\mathbb P(X\geq C)\leq e^{-\gamma}$ is equivalent to the displayed constraint, where $\Phi$ is the [standard normal distribution function](standard-normal-distribution-function.md). This follows from $\mathbb P(X\geq C)=1-\Phi((C-m)/\sqrt v)$ and strict monotonicity of $\Phi$. Unlike the sufficient [Chernoff bound](chernoff-bound.md) margin $\sqrt{2\gamma v}$, this constraint is necessary as well as sufficient. The exact quantile can be negative when $e^{-\gamma}>1/2$.
