# 3-10 helix

↑ **Parent:** [Protein secondary structure](protein-secondary-structure.md)

A [3-10 helix](3-10-helix.md) repeats carbonyl-i to NH-(i+3) hydrogen bonds and has about three residues per turn. The “10” counts atoms in its hydrogen-bonded ring.
