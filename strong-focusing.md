# Strong focusing

↑ **Parent:** [Beam optics](beam-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_focusing)

Alternating focusing and defocusing [quadrupole magnets](quadrupole-magnet.md), separated by drifts, can give stable net focusing in both transverse planes. Stability depends on the full transfer matrix of the lattice cell, not on the sign of one magnet alone.
