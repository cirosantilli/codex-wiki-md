# Complex environment overlap in qubit dephasing

↑ **Parent:** [Dephasing channel](dephasing-channel.md)

An environment interaction preserving the computational populations multiplies a qubit's upper off-diagonal density entry by $\overline c$. A real [phase-flip channel](phase-flip-channel.md) representation exists for every input precisely when $c$ is real, with probability $(1-c)/2$. For $c=re^{i\varphi}$, the channel is a [phase-flip channel](phase-flip-channel.md) of probability $(1-r)/2$ followed by the diagonal [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) $\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{i\varphi})$. The coherent phase cannot be discarded just because the populations are unchanged.
