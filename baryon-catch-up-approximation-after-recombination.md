# Baryon catch-up approximation after recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Total and relative density modes of two pressureless matter fluids](total-and-relative-density-modes-of-two-pressureless-matter-fluids.md)

In a dark-matter-dominated growing mode with a constant relative offset and initially negligible baryon [density contrast](density-contrast.md), $\Delta_B/\Delta_D=1-R_r/R=1-(1+z)/(1+z_r)$. Replacing this by $1-z/z_r$ requires high redshift. The initial relative velocity and finite baryon self-gravity alter transients, although the relative difference becomes small as the common growing mode increases.
