# Salience Attribution Test

↑ **Parent:** [Aberrant salience](aberrant-salience.md)

A task using multidimensional cues for which one feature predicts [reinforcement](reinforcement.md) and another does not. Explicit ratings and response times assess adaptive and aberrant cue effects separately. An absolute rating difference also increases with measurement noise.[Psychological Medicine 39, 199–209; Salience Attribution Test, Results and study limitations](do-patients-with-schizophrenia-exhibit-aberrant-salience-by-jonathan-p-roiser-and-others.md)
