# Heat evolution of bounded data need not converge at large times

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

Bounded smooth initial data alone do not guarantee a pointwise large-time limit for the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) on Euclidean space. In four dimensions, $g(x)=(1+|x|^2)^i$ has modulus one. At the origin its [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) average is asymptotic to $4^i\Gamma(2+i)t^i$, which oscillates indefinitely. Initial data vanishing at infinity do have heat averages tending to zero. This distinction matters when using [Duhamel principle](duhamel-s-principle.md) to construct a stationary solution for a time-independent source.
