# Morphogen source-diffusion-degradation model

↑ **Parent:** [Morphogen gradient](morphogen-gradient.md)

An idealized model balancing localized production, [diffusion](diffusion.md) and degradation. In one dimension at [steady state](steady-state.md), away from the source, $Dc^{\prime\prime}-kc=0$ gives $c(x)=c_0e^{-x/\lambda}$ with $\lambda=\sqrt{D/k}$. Tissue geometry and time-dependent source distributions can invalidate a simple point-source interpretation.
