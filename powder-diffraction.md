# Powder diffraction

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallography](crystallography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powder_diffraction)

[Diffraction](diffraction.md) from a specimen containing many crystallite orientations. Equivalent reciprocal-lattice spacings produce powder rings or corresponding peaks in an angular scan. [Powder diffraction multiplicity](powder-diffraction-multiplicity.md) counts the equivalent directed normals contributing to a reflection family.
