# Linear mass density

↑ **Parent:** [Density](density.md)

Linear mass density is [mass](mass.md) per unit [length](length.md), measured in $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}}$. A uniform string of mass $m$ and length $L$ has $\mu=m/L$; with constant [tension](tension.md) $T$, its transverse [wave](wave.md) speed is $\sqrt{T/\mu}$. [Mass density](density.md) instead measures mass per unit [volume](volume.md).
