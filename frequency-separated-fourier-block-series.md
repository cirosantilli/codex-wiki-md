# Frequency-separated Fourier block series

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier partial sum](fourier-partial-sum.md)

If [trigonometric polynomials](trigonometric-polynomial.md) have spectra in increasing disjoint positive-frequency intervals, the displayed summability gives a continuous uniform sum. At any cutoff its [Fourier partial sum](fourier-partial-sum.md) contains complete earlier blocks, at most one active prefix and no later blocks. Differences across an active prefix therefore isolate that block exactly. Fixed-size excursions occurring in arbitrarily late blocks contradict the Cauchy criterion, even when the complete blocks vanish at the point under consideration.
