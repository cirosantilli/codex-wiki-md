# Maxwell shear-wave dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Maxwell fluid](linear-maxwell-fluid.md)

In a homogeneous [Maxwell fluid](linear-maxwell-fluid.md), transverse [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) and shear [stress relaxation](stress-relaxation.md) give $\tau u_{tt}+u_t=\nu u_{yy}$, where $\nu=\mu/\rho$ is the [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md). A [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) $e^{iky+\sigma t}$ has the displayed quadratic [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). For $4\tau\nu k^2>1$ its two exponents oscillate while decaying at rate $1/(2\tau)$; for smaller wavenumbers they decay without oscillating. At fixed $\nu$ and $k$, $\tau\to0$ leaves the [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) branch $\sigma\to-\nu k^2$ and removes the fast [stress relaxation](stress-relaxation.md) branch $\sigma\sim-1/\tau$.
