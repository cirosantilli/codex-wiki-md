# Chromera velia

↑ **Parent:** [Algae](algae.md)

[Chromera velia](chromera-velia.md) is a photosynthetic alveolate closely related to apicomplexan lineages. Comparative evidence from it helps reconstruct photosynthetic ancestry without assuming that a modern parasite itself photosynthesizes.
