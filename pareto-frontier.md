# Pareto frontier

↑ **Parent:** [Pareto efficiency](pareto-efficiency.md)

The Pareto frontier consists of feasible payoff vectors that no other feasible vector weakly improves in every coordinate and strictly improves in at least one. A [Nash bargaining solution](nash-bargaining-solution.md) with positive gains lies on this frontier, because increasing either gain without decreasing the other raises its [Nash product](nash-product.md).
