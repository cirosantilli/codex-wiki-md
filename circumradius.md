# Circumradius

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

The [radius](radius.md) of a [triangle](triangle.md)'s circumscribed [circle](circle.md), whose center is the [circumcenter](circumcenter.md). For side lengths $a,b,c$ and [area](surface-area.md) $A$, it satisfies $R=abc/(4A)$.
