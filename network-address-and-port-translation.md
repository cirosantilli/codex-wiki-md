# Network address and port translation

↑ **Parent:** [Network address translation](network-address-translation.md)

[Network address and port translation](network-address-and-port-translation.md) permits several internal addresses and transport ports to share an external address by maintaining distinct translated bindings. Losing a binding can disrupt an exchange even when the application [endpoints](communication-endpoint.md) retain their own state.
