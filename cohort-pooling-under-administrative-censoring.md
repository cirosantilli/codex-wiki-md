# Cohort pooling under administrative censoring

↑ **Parent:** [Administrative censoring](administrative-censoring.md)

When cohorts share one time-since-entry [survival distribution](survival-distribution.md), their observations can be pooled using the [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md), even when later cohorts have shorter administrative follow-up. A short-follow-up cohort still improves estimation of early survival, which is a factor in survival at later times. Pooling requires comparable survival across cohorts and [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md); calendar-period or cohort effects can invalidate that assumption.
