<h1 id="bell-s-spaceship-paradox">Bell's spaceship paradox</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Born rigidity](born-rigidity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell's_spaceship_paradox)

Two rockets maintaining fixed separation in an inertial laboratory frame do not preserve their comoving separation. A string connecting them is stretched rather than remaining unstrained; sufficiently large strain can break a real string. The contrast with Born rigidity follows from relativity of simultaneity and does not contradict Lorentz contraction.
