# Ring endomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md)

A [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) from a ring to itself. Surjectivity need not imply injectivity in general, but a surjective [ring endomorphism](ring-endomorphism.md) of a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) is a [ring automorphism](ring-automorphism.md).
