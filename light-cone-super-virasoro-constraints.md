# Light-cone super-Virasoro constraints

↑ **Parent:** [Light-cone gauge in string theory](light-cone-gauge-in-string-theory.md)

On a patch with $\alpha_0^+\ne0$, use [worldsheet diffeomorphisms](worldsheet-diffeomorphism.md) and [worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md) to fix $\alpha_{n\ne0}^+=0$ and $\psi_r^+=0$. The [Virasoro constraints](virasoro-constraint.md) and [superconformal current](superconformal-current.md) constraints then determine the longitudinal modes by the displayed transverse bilinears. In the [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md), reduction of the fermionic zero modes includes the zero-mode Dirac constraint; fixing only nonzero modes does not complete the reduction. Eight transverse bosons and eight transverse fermions remain in the critical ten-dimensional [RNS string](spinning-string.md).
