# Dual equation for multiplicative habit investment

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicative habit utility](multiplicative-habit-utility.md)

After scaling wealth by habit and applying the [wealth-variable Legendre dual](wealth-variable-legendre-dual.md), the effective consumption shadow price is proportional to $\mathcal D=z-\lambda RzJ^\prime+\lambda(R-1)J$. Its power in the utility conjugate keeps the dual equation nonlinear. When $\lambda=0$, habit is fixed and the dual equation is a linear [Euler differential equation](cauchy-euler-equation.md) with effective relative risk aversion $R+\alpha$.
