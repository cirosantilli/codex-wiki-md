# D4h molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The sixteen-element molecular [point group](point-group.md) of an ideal square with a horizontal mirror plane. Inversion distinguishes gerade and ungerade [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md).
