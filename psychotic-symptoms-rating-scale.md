# Psychotic Symptoms Rating Scale

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological assessment](psychological-assessment.md)

A structured clinical rating instrument assessing dimensions of psychotic [symptoms](symptom.md). A specified delusion-conviction item and an aggregate delusion-severity score are different endpoints.[Lancet Psychiatry 8, 696–707; 130 patients, same-therapist befriending, six-month continuous conviction and categorical recovery distinction](comparison-of-a-theoretically-driven-cognitive-therapy-the-feeling-safe-programme-with-befriending-for-the-treatment-of-persistent-persecutory-delusions-a-parallel-single-blind-randomised-controlled-trial-by-daniel-freeman-and-others.md)
