# Polar equation of a repulsive inverse-square orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md)

Taking the dot product of the [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md) with $\widehat{\mathbf r}$ gives $A\cos\theta=L^2/r+mk$. Thus

$$
r=\frac{\lambda}{e\cos\theta-1},
\qquad
\lambda=\frac{L^2}{mk},
\qquad
e=\frac{A}{mk}.
$$
