# Intrinsic flatness of a circular cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md)

A [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) of radius $\rho$ has [induced metric](induced-metric.md) locally equal to $du^2+dz^2$ with $u=\rho\varphi$. Its [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) therefore vanishes, despite its nonzero [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md). With outward unit normal and convention $K_{ij}=e_i^ae_j^b\nabla_an_b$, its trace is $1/\rho$ and its [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) are $1/\rho$ and zero. Its averaged [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) is $1/(2\rho)$. The angular identification changes global topology, not local flatness.
