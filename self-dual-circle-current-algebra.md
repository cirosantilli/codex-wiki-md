# Self-dual circle current algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Self-dual circle compactification](self-dual-circle-compactification.md)

At $R=\sqrt{\alpha'}$, a chiral [compact boson](compact-boson.md) has [operator product expansion](operator-product-expansion.md) $Y_L(z)Y_L(w)\sim-\alpha'\log(z-w)/2$. The currents $J^3=i\partial Y_L/\sqrt{\alpha'}$ and $J^\pm=:\!e^{\pm2iY_L/\sqrt{\alpha'}}\!:$ have [conformal weight](conformal-weight.md) one. Contractions give $J^3(z)J^3(w)\sim1/[2(z-w)^2]$, $J^3(z)J^\pm(w)\sim\pm J^\pm(w)/(z-w)$ and $J^+(z)J^-(w)\sim1/(z-w)^2+2J^3(w)/(z-w)$. These are the level-one [SU(2) Lie algebra](su-2-lie-algebra.md) currents. The right-moving copy supplies a second factor. Combining a current with a noncompact coordinate derivative gives a massless [string vertex operator](string-vertex-operator.md) for each of the six vectors; four are additional to the generic two Cartan vectors.
