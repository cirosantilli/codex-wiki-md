# Internal-wave transmission across a stratification step

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary-forced internal gravity wave](boundary-forced-internal-gravity-wave.md)

At a step in [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) without a jump in background [mass density](density.md), vertical [velocity](velocity.md) and [fluid pressure](fluid-pressure.md) are continuous. For a fixed horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) and [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), these are continuity of $W$ and $W'$. They determine reflection and transmission between propagating [internal gravity waves](internal-wave.md) and [evanescent waves](evanescent-wave.md).
