# Zinc blende structure factor

↑ **Parent:** [Zinc blende structure](zinc-blende-structure.md)

The [zinc blende structure](zinc-blende-structure.md) consists of a [face-centered cubic lattice](face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) with two species displaced by a quarter of the body diagonal. Its conventional-cell [structure factor](structure-factor.md) vanishes for mixed-parity [Miller indices](miller-index.md). For equal parity, $S_{\mathrm{FCC}}=4$: odd indices give $|F|^2=16(f_A^2+f_B^2)$ for real [atomic scattering factors](atomic-scattering-factor.md), even index sums divisible by four give $16(f_A+f_B)^2$, and sums congruent to two modulo four give $16(f_A-f_B)^2$. The last reflections diagnose sublattice scattering contrast. They vanish in the equal-factor diamond limit but need not vanish for a binary compound.
