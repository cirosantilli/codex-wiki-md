# Self-inductance

↑ **Parent:** [Inductance](inductance.md)

For a circuit whose flux linkage $\Lambda$ is proportional to its current, the self-inductance is the coefficient $L$ in $\Lambda=LI$. [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives the induced [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) $-L\dot I$ when $L$ is constant. In series with a resistance $R$ and a constant applied electromotive force, the current obeys $L\dot I+RI=\mathcal E_0$, with [time constant](time-constant.md) $L/R$.
