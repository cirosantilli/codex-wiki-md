# Enantiotopicity

↑ **Parent:** [Topicity](topicity.md)

Two sites are enantiotopic if separately replacing one or the other by the same distinguishing group gives [enantiomers](enantiomer.md). Achiral conditions do not distinguish them by [chirality](chirality-physics.md) alone.
