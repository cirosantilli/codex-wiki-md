Here $\mu=\cos\theta$ and $a_{\ell,m}=\sqrt{(\ell^2-m^2)/[(2\ell+1)(2\ell-1)]}$. This follows by combining the [associated Legendre function](associated-legendre-function.md) recurrence $(2\ell+1)\mu P_\ell^m=(\ell-m+1)P_{\ell+1}^m+(\ell+m)P_{\ell-1}^m$ with the normalized [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) constants. It gives the adjacent-multipole couplings in a [photon Boltzmann hierarchy](photon-boltzmann-hierarchy.md).
