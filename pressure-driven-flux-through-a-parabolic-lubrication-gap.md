# Pressure-driven flux through a parabolic lubrication gap

↑ **Parent:** [Parabolic lubrication gap](parabolic-lubrication-gap.md)

Two stationary no-slip surfaces with local gap $H(x)=h+x^2/(2a)$ transmit pressure-driven [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $q=-H^3p_x/(12\mu)$ per unit transverse length. Hence the [pressure](pressure.md) drop is

$$
\Delta p=12\mu q\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{H^3}=\frac{9\pi\mu q\sqrt{2a}}{2h^{5/2}}.
$$

The first relation comes from integrating the parabolic [velocity](velocity.md) $u=-p_x y(H-y)/(2\mu)$. Substitution $x=\sqrt{2ah}\tan\theta$ evaluates the resistance [integral](integral.md) using $\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos^4\theta\,d\theta=3\pi/8$. The formula gives the leading resistance when $h\ll a$.
