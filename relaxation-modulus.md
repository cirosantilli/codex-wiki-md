# Relaxation modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Linear viscoelastic fluid](linear-viscoelastic-fluid.md)

The relaxation modulus is the shear-stress response at lag s to a unit small step of shear strain. By linear superposition it is the causal memory kernel multiplying [shear rate](shear-rate.md). Its decaying part represents stored stress that relaxes after deformation ceases.
