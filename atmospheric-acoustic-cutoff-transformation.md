# Atmospheric acoustic cutoff transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic cutoff frequency](acoustic-cutoff-frequency.md)

In an [isothermal atmosphere](isothermal-atmosphere.md) with constant density [scale height](scale-height.md) $H$, the equation $\delta\rho_{tt}-c^2\delta\rho_{zz}-(c^2/H)\delta\rho_z=0$ loses its first spatial derivative under $\delta\rho=e^{-z/(2H)}\Psi$. Direct differentiation gives $\Psi_{tt}-c^2\Psi_{zz}+c^2\Psi/(4H^2)=0$. Thus a [plane wave](plane-wave.md) has [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega^2=c^2k^2+\omega_c^2$, with real [acoustic cutoff frequency](acoustic-cutoff-frequency.md) $\omega_c=c/(2H)$. The cutoff term is positive; a minus sign would instead predict growing sufficiently long spatial modes. Vertical propagation requires $|\omega|\ge\omega_c$ within this ideal constant-coefficient model.
