# Pushout

↑ **Parent:** [Colimit](colimit.md)

The [colimit](colimit.md) of two arrows $A\to B$ and $A\to C$. A map from the pushout is uniquely a pair of maps from $B$ and $C$ agreeing on $A$. In modules it is $(B\oplus C)/\{(f(a),-g(a)):a\in A\}$.
