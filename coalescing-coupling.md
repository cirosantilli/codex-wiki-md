# Coalescing coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Coupling](coupling.md)

A coalescing [coupling of probability distributions](coupling.md) of two [Markov chains](markov-chain.md) keeps them identical after a meeting time $T$. The [coupling inequality for total variation](coupling-inequality-for-total-variation.md) gives $\|P^t(x,\cdot)-P^t(y,\cdot)\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\leq\mathbb P(T>t)$. A uniform bound on $\mathbb E T$, combined with [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md), bounds the [mixing time of a Markov chain](mixing-time-of-a-markov-chain.md).
