# Trim and fill

↑ **Parent:** [Publication bias](publication-bias.md)

Trim and fill estimates missing studies from [funnel plot](funnel-plot.md) asymmetry by trimming extreme studies to locate a centre and filling in counterparts on the less represented side. The adjusted [meta-analysis](meta-analysis.md) includes these imputed estimates. Its interpretation depends on a symmetry model for missing evidence; it is not a guarantee that genuine [publication bias](publication-bias.md) has been removed.
