# Powerful Lie algebra over p-adic integers

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

A [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) over the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md) whose underlying [module](module-mathematics.md) is free of finite rank and whose [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) satisfies $[L,L]\subseteq p^\epsilon L$, where $\epsilon=1$ for odd $p$ and $\epsilon=2$ for $p=2$. Finite freeness is part of this convention: merely requiring a small [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) on a torsion module would not correspond to [uniform pro-p groups](uniform-pro-p-group.md).
