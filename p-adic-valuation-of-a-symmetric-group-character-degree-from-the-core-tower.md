# P-adic valuation of a symmetric-group character degree from the core tower

↑ **Parent:** [Core tower of a partition](core-tower-of-a-partition.md)

If $n=|\lambda|$ and $d_p(n)$ is its base-$p$ digit sum, then

$$
v_p(\chi^\lambda(1))
=\frac{\sum_{r\geq0}|TC_p(\lambda)_r|-d_p(n)}{p-1}.
$$

This combines the [Hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md), [Legendre formula](legendre-s-formula.md), the [abacus divisible-hook correspondence](hooks-divisible-by-the-abacus-modulus.md), and the recurrence $q_r=c_r+pq_{r+1}$.
