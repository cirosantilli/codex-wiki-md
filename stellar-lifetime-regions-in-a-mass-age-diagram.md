# Stellar lifetime regions in a mass-age diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Evolutionary-state selection by stellar lifetimes](evolutionary-state-selection-by-stellar-lifetimes.md)

A phase bounded by mass-dependent lifetimes $\tau_a(M)$ and $\tau_b(M)$ occupies $\tau_a(M)<t<\tau_b(M)$, clipped to the population age range. Its boundaries can be mapped into uniform [probability](probability.md) coordinates to evaluate evolutionary-state fractions.
