# Joule

↑ **Parent:** [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joule)

The [joule](joule.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [energy](energy.md), [work](work.md), and [heat](heat.md). One [newton](newton-unit.md) of [force](force.md) acting along one metre of [displacement](displacement.md) does one joule of [work](work.md). The relation $1\,\mathrm J=1\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ makes mechanical and electrical transfers comparable in the same energy unit. [The International System of Units (SI), ninth edition, SI Brochure](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md)
