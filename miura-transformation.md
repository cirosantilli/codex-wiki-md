# Miura transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Korteweg-De Vries equation](modified-korteweg-de-vries-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miura_transformation)

Direct differentiation gives

$$
u_t-6uu_x+u_{xxx}=(\partial_x+2v)(v_t-6v^2v_x+v_{xxx}).
$$

Consequently this transformation takes solutions of the [modified Korteweg-De Vries equation](modified-korteweg-de-vries-equation.md) to solutions of the [Korteweg-De Vries equation](korteweg-de-vries-equation.md) in these sign conventions. Both summands in $u$ have the same scaling weight, so the modified-equation scaling induces $(x,t,u)\mapsto(e^sx,e^{3s}t,e^{-2s}u)$.
