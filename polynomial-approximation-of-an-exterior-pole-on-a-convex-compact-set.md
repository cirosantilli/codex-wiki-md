# Polynomial approximation of an exterior pole on a convex compact set

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)

For a compact [convex set](convex-set.md) $K\subset\mathbb C$ and $\zeta\notin K$, strict separation gives a unit vector $u$ with $\operatorname{Re}(\bar u\zeta)>\max_K\operatorname{Re}(\bar uz)$. Taking $c=-Ru$ for large $R$ makes $q=\max_K|z-c|/|\zeta-c|<1$. The polynomial

$$
p_N(z)=-\frac1{\zeta-c}\sum_{j=0}^N\left(\frac{z-c}{\zeta-c}\right)^j
$$

has uniform error at most $q^{N+1}/(|\zeta-c|(1-q))$. This elementary [geometric series](geometric-series.md) argument proves exterior-pole approximation without a general complex approximation theorem.
