# Variant allele fraction

↑ **Parent:** [DNA sequencing](dna-sequencing.md)

The fraction of informative reads carrying a variant. Purity, copy number and allelic configuration affect its relationship to the fraction of [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) carrying that variant.
