# Abelian Chern-Simons five-form field equation

↑ **Parent:** [Chern-Simons 5-form](chern-simons-5-form.md)

For $F=dA$ on a five-dimensional oriented spacetime, the action $S=-\frac12\int F\wedge *F+c\int A\wedge F\wedge F$ has bulk variation $\int\delta A\wedge(-d*F+3cF\wedge F)$. Two integrations by parts make the three variations of the [Chern-Simons 5-form](chern-simons-5-form.md) contribute equally. Under $A\mapsto A+d\lambda$, the extra action is $c\int d(\lambda F\wedge F)$, a boundary term by the [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md). Its bulk field equation is gauge invariant. The Chern-Simons term is metric-independent and has no bulk contribution to the [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) when the differential-form field is held fixed in metric variation.
