# Seasonal source depth for a reversible heat pump

↑ **Parent:** [Heat pump](heat-pump.md)

For a sinusoidal ground-temperature model whose coldest surface day is $t=0$, source [temperature](temperature-split.md) on that day is $T_0-T_Ae^{-x}\cos x$, where $x=z/\delta$. The first and largest thermal maximum occurs at $x=3\pi/4$, not at infinite depth. This maximizes the winter [coefficient of performance](coefficient-of-performance.md) for a reversible pump with a fixed indoor [temperature](temperature-split.md). With a heating load even about that day, the same optimum minimizes annually integrated reversible heating [work](work.md). Excavation cost, finite [heat](heat-split.md) extraction and real heat-pump behaviour can change the optimum.
