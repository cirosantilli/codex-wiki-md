# Intron

↑ **Parent:** [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intron)

A segment represented in a precursor [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) that is removed by [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md); the corresponding segment of a [gene](gene.md) is also called an [intron](intron.md). An [intron](intron.md) can interrupt [ribosomal RNA](ribosomal-rna.md) as well as [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md).
