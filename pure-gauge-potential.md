# Pure gauge potential

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md)

With [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md) $D=d+A$, transforming the zero connection by a smooth group-valued map $g$ gives $A=-dg\,g^{-1}$. This potential has zero [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md). A flat potential is locally of this form; global reconstruction can also depend on topology and holonomy. The sign changes when the definition of the covariant derivative changes.
