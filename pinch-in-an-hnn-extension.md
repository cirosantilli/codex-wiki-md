# Pinch in an HNN extension

↑ **Parent:** [Normal form theorem for an HNN extension](normal-form-theorem-for-an-hnn-extension.md)

In $\langle G,t\mid tat^{-1}=\phi(a),\ a\in A\rangle$, a pinch is a subword $tat^{-1}$ with $a\in A$, or $t^{-1}bt$ with $b\in B=\phi(A)$. It can be replaced by a base-group element, removing two stable letters. [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md) asserts that every identity word with stable letters must have a pinch after base-group multiplication is performed.
