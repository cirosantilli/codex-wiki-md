# Specific volume

↑ **Parent:** [Volume (thermodynamics)](volume-thermodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specific_volume)

The [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) per unit [mass](mass.md); for a uniform substance it is the reciprocal of [mass density](density.md). The [Clapeyron equation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) pairs a difference of [specific volumes](specific-volume.md) with [latent heat](latent-heat.md) per unit [mass](mass.md).
