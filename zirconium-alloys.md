# Zirconium alloys

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fuel cladding](nuclear-fuel-cladding.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zirconium_alloys)

Alloys based on [zirconium](zirconium.md), widely used for water-reactor [nuclear fuel cladding](nuclear-fuel-cladding.md) because of their useful combination of low neutron absorption and material properties. Corrosion, hydrogen uptake, creep and irradiation-induced dimensional changes limit performance.
