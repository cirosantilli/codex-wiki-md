<h1 id="left-su-2-subgroup-of-so-4">Left SU(2) subgroup of SO(4)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SO5 to SO4 branching](so5-to-so4-branching.md)

Identify $\mathbb R^4$ with the [quaternions](quaternion.md). Unit [quaternions](quaternion.md) act by left multiplication, embedding the [SU(2) group](su-2-group.md) in $SO(4)$. Under this subgroup, the complexified vector representation branches as $\mathbf4\to2\mathbf2$, and the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) as $\mathbf6\to\mathbf3\oplus3\mathbf1$. Right multiplication supplies the commuting second factor.
