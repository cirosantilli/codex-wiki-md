# Dirichlet form of a Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Reversible Markov chain](reversible-markov-chain.md)

For a reversible transition matrix $P$ with invariant distribution $\pi$,

$$
\mathcal E(f,g)=\frac12\sum_{x,y}\pi(x)P(x,y)
(f(x)-f(y))(g(x)-g(y)).
$$

This chain energy is a discrete example of a [Dirichlet form](dirichlet-form.md).
