# Truncated explicit formula for the second Chebyshev function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann–von Mangoldt explicit formula](riemann-von-mangoldt-explicit-formula.md)

For $2\le T\le x$, the [truncated Perron formula](truncated-perron-formula.md) and a contour shift give the displayed form of the [Riemann–von Mangoldt explicit formula](riemann-von-mangoldt-explicit-formula.md). The [pole](pole.md) at one contributes $x$ and the [Nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function](nontrivial-zero-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md) contribute the finite sum. One can choose a separated height in $[T,T+1]$ and then restore $T$ using the [local zero count for the Riemann zeta function](local-zero-count-for-the-riemann-zeta-function.md). The error absorbs the half-weight discrepancy at a prime power. Outside this range of $T$, one needs the fuller error with the near-integer terms retained.
