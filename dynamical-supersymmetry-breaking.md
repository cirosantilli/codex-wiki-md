# Dynamical supersymmetry breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry breaking](supersymmetry-breaking.md)

Dynamical supersymmetry breaking occurs when quantum dynamics produces a vacuum that does not preserve [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md). An asymptotically free hidden sector can generate the displayed exponentially small scale through [dimensional transmutation](dimensional-transmutation.md). This supplies a possible origin of a hierarchy, but strong coupling by itself does not prove breaking: the vacuum equations and dynamics must exclude a [supersymmetric vacuum](supersymmetric-vacuum.md).
