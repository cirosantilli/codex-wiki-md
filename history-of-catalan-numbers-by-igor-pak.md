# History of Catalan numbers by Igor Pak

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Igor Pak](work-by-igor-pak.md)

By [Igor Pak](igor-pak.md).

Online text: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.5711](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.5711)

Standalone historical preprint, arXiv:1408.5711. Version 1 was submitted on 25 August 2014; the inspected version 2 is dated 27 August 2014. Its cover says the account will appear as an appendix in a forthcoming book by [Richard P. Stanley](richard-p-stanley.md); the authorship here applies to this preprint, not to the whole book.

Cited passages: §§2–3 and §5, printed pp. 2–4: Euler correspondence, Segner recurrence and Catalan's bracketed products.
