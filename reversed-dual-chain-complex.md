# Reversed dual chain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)

The reversed [dual module](dual-module.md) of a [chain complex](chain-complex.md) becomes a [chain complex](chain-complex.md) with the unsigned precomposition differential above. A functional on $C_{-i}$ is sent to a functional on $C_{1-i}$, lowering the reversed degree by one.
