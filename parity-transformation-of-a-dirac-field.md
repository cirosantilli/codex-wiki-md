# Parity transformation of a Dirac field

↑ **Parent:** [Parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md)

With the mostly-minus [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md), $|\eta_P|=1$ and the usual [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md), this transformation makes $\bar\psi\psi$ even and $\bar\psi\gamma^5\psi$ odd under [parity](parity.md). The latter sign follows from anticommutation of $\gamma^0$ and $\gamma^5$ and does not depend on whether the convention includes an imaginary unit in $\gamma^5$.
