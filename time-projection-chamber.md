# Time projection chamber

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

A detector that reconstructs one coordinate from the time required for [ionization](ionization.md) electrons to drift in an [electric field](electric-field-split.md), with the other coordinates measured by the charge or light pattern. A two-phase [xenon](xenon.md) implementation obtains depth from the delay between prompt [particle scintillation](scintillation-physics.md) and gas-phase charge readout.
