# Correlated Gaussian common-mean estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Linear unbiased estimator](linear-unbiased-estimator.md)

For $X\sim N(\mu\mathbf1,C)$ with a known [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) $C$, the [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) of $\mu$ is $\widehat\mu=(\mathbf1^TC^{-1}X)/(\mathbf1^TC^{-1}\mathbf1)$. It is unbiased with variance $(\mathbf1^TC^{-1}\mathbf1)^{-1}$, attaining the [Cramér-Rao bound](cramer-rao-bound.md). It is also the best [linear unbiased estimator](linear-unbiased-estimator.md). Pairwise admissible [correlation coefficients](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) alone do not ensure that $C$ is a valid [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md); the entire matrix must be symmetric and a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). Inverse-based formulas require positive definiteness.
