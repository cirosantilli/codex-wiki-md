# Rayleigh scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayleigh_scattering)

Rayleigh scattering is elastic scattering by particles much smaller than the wavelength. For a fixed dielectric sphere, its scattering efficiency scales as the fourth power of the size-to-wavelength ratio.
