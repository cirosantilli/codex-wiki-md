# Set-theoretic limit

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set-theoretic_limit)

For a [sequence](sequence.md) of [sets](set-split.md), the [limit superior of sets](limit-superior-of-sets.md) consists of points belonging to infinitely many members, while the lower limit consists of points belonging to every sufficiently late member. If the two [sets](set-split.md) coincide, their common value is the set-theoretic limit.
