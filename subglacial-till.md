# Subglacial till

↑ **Parent:** [Basal sliding](basal-sliding.md)

Subglacial till is a mixture of rock fragments and fine sediment beneath [ice](ice.md). A wet deformable layer can lubricate [basal sliding](basal-sliding.md). Its [constitutive equation](constitutive-equation.md) must be specified; [Newtonian till lubrication](newtonian-till-lubrication.md) is one idealization, rather than a claim that all subglacial till is a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md).
