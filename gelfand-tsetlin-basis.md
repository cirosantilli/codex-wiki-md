<h1 id="gelfand-tsetlin-basis">Gelfand–Tsetlin basis</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra](gelfand-tsetlin-algebra.md)

Successively decompose an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) along a subgroup chain with [multiplicity-free restriction](multiplicity-free-restriction.md). A complete path selects a one-dimensional subspace; choosing one nonzero vector on each path gives a Gelfand–Tsetlin basis. For a [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), paths are [standard Young tableaux](standard-young-tableau.md), and the [Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](jucys-murphy-element.md) act diagonally with their cell contents.
