# Center-of-mass coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Center-of-mass reduction](center-of-mass-reduction.md)

In the [two-body problem](two-body-problem.md), these coordinates separate the common translation from the internal separation. For total mass $M$ and [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) $\mu$, the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $M\dot R^2/2+\mu\dot r^2/2$. The conjugate momenta are $P=M\dot R$ and $p=\mu\dot r$ for the ordinary Newtonian kinetic term.
