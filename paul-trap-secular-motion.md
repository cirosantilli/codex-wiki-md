# Paul trap secular motion

↑ **Parent:** [Paul trap](paul-trap.md)

The slow part of a trapped trajectory in the small-parameter regime of a [Paul trap](paul-trap.md). Its [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is approximately set by the curvature of the [RF-averaged trapping potential](rf-averaged-trapping-potential.md), including any static [electric potential](electric-potential.md) contribution. [Paul trap micromotion](paul-trap-micromotion.md) is superposed on this slow motion.
