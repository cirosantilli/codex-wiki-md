# Lindbladian

↑ **Parent:** [Lindblad equation](lindblad-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindbladian)

The Lindbladian $\mathcal L$ is the linear superoperator on density operators defined by the right-hand side of a [Lindblad equation](lindblad-equation.md), so that $\dot\rho=\mathcal L(\rho)$.
