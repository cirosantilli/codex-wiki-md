# Edge contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Graph minor](graph-minor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_contraction)

Contracting an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) $uv$ identifies its endpoints, then removes loops and duplicate [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) to produce a simple [graph](graph-split.md). It reduces the [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) count by exactly $1+|N(u)\cap N(v)|$: one for $uv$, and one for each [common neighbour](common-neighbour.md).
