# Neutron-proton radiative capture

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md)

Formation of a [deuteron](deuteron.md) from a [neutron](neutron-split.md) and a [proton](proton.md) with [photon](photon.md) emission. For an initial pair at rest, two-body [energy](energy-split.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) conservation gives $E_\gamma=B-B^2/[2(m_p+m_n)]$ in [natural units](natural-units.md), where $B=m_p+m_n-m_d$. The missing [binding energy](binding-energy.md) becomes [nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md).
