# Plasma wakefield acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Particle accelerator](particle-accelerator-split.md)

A driver excites charge-density [oscillations](oscillation.md) in a [plasma](plasma-physics.md) whose longitudinal [electric field](electric-field-split.md) transfers [energy](energy-split.md) to a trailing witness beam. [Density](density.md) fixes a characteristic [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md) and wavelength, while driver shape and loading determine the actual field and transfer efficiency.
