# Vanishing-cutoff limit of the Diesel cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Diesel cycle](diesel-cycle.md)

For an ideal [Diesel cycle](diesel-cycle.md) with compression ratio $r=V_1/V_2>1$, cutoff ratio $\rho=V_3/V_2>1$ and constant heat-capacity ratio $\gamma$, its [thermal efficiency](thermal-efficiency.md) is $\eta=1-(\rho^\gamma-1)/[\gamma r^{\gamma-1}(\rho-1)]$. As $\rho\to1^+$ the quotient tends to $\gamma$, giving $\eta\to1-r^{1-\gamma}$. The maximum-to-minimum [temperature](temperature-split.md) ratio tends to $r^{\gamma-1}$, so the limiting efficiency equals that of a [Carnot cycle](carnot-cycle.md) between those [temperatures](temperature-split.md). Both [heat](heat-split.md) input and net [work](work.md) vanish in this limit: the result is a limit of their ratio, not a finite-work engine at exactly zero cutoff interval.
