# Linear optics

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)

In [linear optics](linear-optics.md), the response is linear in the optical field and propagation or passive mixing maps mode [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md) to linear combinations of mode [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md). [Wave plates](waveplate.md) and beam splitters can rotate or mix photonic modes, but do not by themselves supply a deterministic interaction between independent photon-number [qubits](qubit.md). Conditional measurements and auxiliary [photons](photon.md) change that resource model.
