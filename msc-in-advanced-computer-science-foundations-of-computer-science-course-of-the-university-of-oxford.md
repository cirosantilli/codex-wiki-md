# MSc in advanced computer science (foundations of computer science) course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [MSc in advanced computer science course of the University of Oxford](msc-in-advanced-computer-science-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-advanced-computer-science). Specialist award/stream within Advanced [Computer science](computer-science-split.md); not counted as a separately indexed standalone programme. The specialist menu includes automata, logic and games; categories, proofs and processes; privacy and computation; and quantum processes and computation. [Official specialist module lists](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/teaching/MSCinCS/).
