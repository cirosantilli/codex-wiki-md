# Hagedorn temperature

↑ **Parent:** [String theory](string-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagedorn_temperature)

If a density of states has high-energy growth proportional to a power of energy times $e^{\beta_HE}$, its canonical Boltzmann sum fails for $\beta<\beta_H$. The Hagedorn temperature is the corresponding threshold. The [open bosonic string mass spectrum](open-bosonic-string-mass-spectrum.md) and [large-level degeneracy of an open bosonic string](large-level-degeneracy-of-an-open-bosonic-string.md) give $\beta_H=4\pi\sqrt{\alpha'}$ in units with Boltzmann's constant, the speed of light and Planck's constant set to one. Polynomial prefactors affect behavior at the threshold itself; the growth alone does not distinguish a limiting temperature from a phase transition.
