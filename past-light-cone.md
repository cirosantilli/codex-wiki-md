# Past light cone

↑ **Parent:** [Light cone](light-cone.md)

The [past light cone](past-light-cone.md) of a [spacetime event](event-relativity.md) $O$ is the past-directed part of its [light cone](light-cone.md). In [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), its [spacetime events](event-relativity.md) satisfy $|\Delta\mathbf x|=-c\Delta t$ with $\Delta t<0$. They are the possible emission events of light rays arriving at $O$. The interior also contains events that can send slower signals to $O$. The [retarded potential](retarded-potential.md) of a moving point [electric charge](electric-charge.md), given by the [Liénard–Wiechert potential](lienard-wiechert-potential.md), uses the emission event where the source's [world line](world-line.md) intersects this boundary.
