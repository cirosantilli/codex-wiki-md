# Splitting field of a central simple algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md)

An extension $E/K$ splits a [central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md) $A$ of degree $d$ when $A\otimes_KE\cong M_d(E)$. A finite separable splitting field exists. Splitting is preserved by further scalar extension and is equivalent to the vanishing of the restricted [Brauer group](brauer-group.md) class.
