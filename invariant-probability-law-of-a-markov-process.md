# Invariant probability law of a Markov process

↑ **Parent:** [Markov process](markov-process-split.md)

A [probability measure](probability-measure.md) is invariant for a [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) with transition semigroup $P_t$ when starting with that law gives the same law at every later time. Equivalently, $\int P_tf\,d\nu=\int f\,d\nu$ for every bounded measurable function. Invariant laws may be constructed by [weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) of evolved laws when the [Feller semigroup](feller-semigroup.md) property permits passing the semigroup through the limit, as for the [extremal invariant measures of the contact process](extremal-invariant-measures-of-the-contact-process.md).
