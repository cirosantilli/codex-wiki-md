# SAT solver

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md)

An [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) or software system that decides whether a [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md) has a [satisfying assignment](satisfying-assignment.md), usually returning one when it exists. [Unit propagation](unit-propagation.md) and [clause learning](clause-learning.md) are important mechanisms in modern [SAT solvers](sat-solver.md). A reduction must be correct and its representation costs counted before the performance of the downstream [SAT solver](sat-solver.md) is relevant.
