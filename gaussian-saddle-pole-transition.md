# Gaussian saddle-pole transition

↑ **Parent:** [Saddle-point approximation with a nearby pole](saddle-point-approximation-with-a-nearby-pole.md)

For a simple [saddle point](saddle-point.md), a nearby [pole](pole.md) coalesces with its contributing region when their separation is of order the inverse square root of the large parameter. A local quadratic phase coordinate changes the singular part to a [Gaussian pole integral](gaussian-pole-integral.md). Adding any residue crossed by the [contour deformation](contour-deformation.md) gives a [Faddeeva function](faddeeva-function.md) rather than two separately singular approximations. The regular part of the transformed amplitude remains smaller by the ordinary saddle width.
