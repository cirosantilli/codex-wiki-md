# Knight attack graph

↑ **Parent:** [Nonattacking knights problem](nonattacking-knights-problem.md)

The [knight attack graph](knight-attack-graph.md) has one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) per chessboard square and an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) between squares linked by a knight move. A nonattacking placement is an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md). Colouring squares by the [parity](parity.md) of the coordinate [sum](sum.md) makes this a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md), because every knight move changes that [parity](parity.md). Every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) has [degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most eight, with fewer attacks near the boundary.
