# Slope-driven porous gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Porous gravity current](porous-gravity-current.md)

A buoyant phase beneath a sloping impermeable cap has a slope-driven [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) proportional to density deficit and slope. Far from thickness-adjustment regions, its uniform plateau speed is $K\Delta\rho g\sin\theta/\mu$. With thickness measured normal to the slope, buoyant cap overpressure is $\Delta\rho g h\cos\theta$. A complete depth model also includes the along-current gradient of thickness.
