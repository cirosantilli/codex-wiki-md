# Absorption (electromagnetic radiation)

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometry](radiometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absorption_(electromagnetic_radiation))

The transfer of [energy](energy-split.md) from [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) to matter. [Reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) redirects the incident [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md), and transmission lets it pass through; absorption transfers some of its [energy](energy-split.md) to the material instead.
