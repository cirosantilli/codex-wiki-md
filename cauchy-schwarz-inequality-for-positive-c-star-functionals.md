<h1 id="cauchy-schwarz-inequality-for-positive-c-star-functionals">Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for positive C-star functionals</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Positive functional on a C-star algebra](positive-functional-on-a-c-star-algebra.md)

Positivity of $f((x+ty)^*(x+ty))$ for every complex $t$ proves the displayed [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), including zero diagonal cases. Replacing $x,y$ by their adjoints gives $|f(xy^*)|^2\leq f(xx^*)f(yy^*)$. These two forms cannot be mixed for a general [positive functional on a C-star algebra](positive-functional-on-a-c-star-algebra.md). For the state evaluating the lower-right entry of $2\times2$ matrices and $x=y=E_{12}$, $f(x^*y)=1$ but $f(xx^*)=f(yy^*)=0$, disproving the mixed form.
