# Finite Ramsey theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey's theorem](ramsey-s-theorem.md)

For positive integers $r,k,m$, there is $n$ such that every $k$-coloring of the $r$-element subsets of $[n]$ has a monochromatic $m$-element subset. A diagonal [compactness](compact-space.md) argument deduces this from [Ramsey's theorem](ramsey-s-theorem.md).
