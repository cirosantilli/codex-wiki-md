# Coordinate (linear algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Coordinate vector](coordinate-vector.md)

A coordinate of a [vector](vector.md) in a chosen [basis](basis.md) is one entry of its [coordinate vector](coordinate-vector.md). The corresponding [coordinate functional](coordinate-functional.md) in the [dual basis](dual-basis.md) extracts it. For an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), it can instead be read as an [inner product](inner-product.md) with the basis vector, as in [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). For a [nonorthogonal](nonorthogonal-vectors.md) basis, ordinary [dot products](dot-product.md) need not give the coordinates.
