# Replica symmetry breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Spin glass](spin-glass.md)

[Giorgio Parisi](giorgio-parisi.md)'s 1979 work replaced a single replica [order parameter](order-parameter.md) by an infinite hierarchy in the [mean-field theory](mean-field-theory.md) of [spin glasses](spin-glass.md). The claim concerns a thermodynamic model, rather than inequivalence of the labels attached to two finite laboratory copies.[Abstract, 3 December 1979: an infinite number of order parameters in mean-field replica theory](infinite-number-of-order-parameters-for-spin-glasses-by-giorgio-parisi.md)

For equilibrium states with weights $w_\alpha$ and local means $m_i^\alpha$, define $Q_{\alpha\gamma}=N^{-1}\sum_i m_i^\alpha m_i^\gamma$. The distribution $P_J(q)=\sum_{\alpha,\gamma}w_\alpha w_\gamma\delta(q-Q_{\alpha\gamma})$ records their similarities. This state-based overlap distribution is distinct from the finite instantaneous [spin overlap](spin-overlap.md) used in the triangle example.[§V, equations (12)–(14), overlaps between equilibrium states and their sample-dependent distribution](nobel-lecture-multiple-equilibria-by-giorgio-parisi.md)
