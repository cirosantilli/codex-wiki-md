The [marginal likelihood](../../../../../bayesian-model-evidence.md) integrates the [likelihood function](../../../../../likelihood-function.md) against the [prior density](../../../../../prior-density.md):

$$
\boxed{Z:=p(\mathcal D)=\int_{\mathbb R^d}p(\mathcal D\mid\theta)p(\theta)\,d\theta}.
$$

It is the normalizing constant in [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../bayes-theorem.md). For the subsequent [posterior distribution](../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) and [importance sampling](../../../../../importance-sampling.md) calculations, assume a normalized [prior density](../../../../../prior-density.md) and $0<Z<\infty$.
