↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**[Gaussian process regression](../../../../../gaussian-process-regression.md) supplies a [predictive distribution](../../../../../predictive-distribution.md) and uncertainty estimates.** A [Gaussian process regression posterior](../../../../../gaussian-process-regression-posterior.md) gives both a [posterior mean](../../../../../posterior-mean.md) and a [posterior variance](../../../../../posterior-variance.md), allowing [credible intervals](../../../../../credible-interval.md) for the latent function and a [posterior predictive distribution](../../../../../posterior-predictive-distribution.md) for future noisy observations. The [kernel ridge regression](../../../../../kernel-ridge-regression.md) estimator by itself specifies a point estimate.

With the same [positive-semidefinite kernel](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-kernel-split.md) and matching regularization, the [Gaussian process regression](../../../../../gaussian-process-regression.md) [posterior mean](../../../../../posterior-mean.md) equals the [kernel ridge regression](../../../../../kernel-ridge-regression.md) prediction. Thus the main additional benefit is the explicit [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) of predictions, conditional on the chosen [Gaussian process](../../../../../gaussian-process-split.md) model and its hyperparameters, rather than an automatic improvement in point-prediction accuracy.
