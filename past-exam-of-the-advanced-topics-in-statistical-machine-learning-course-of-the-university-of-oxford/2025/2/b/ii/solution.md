<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose two inputs $0<a<b$. Their proposed [kernel matrix](../../../../../kernel-matrix.md) is

$$
K=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&b\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The coefficient [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $\alpha=(1,-1)^T$ gives

$$
\alpha^TK\alpha=a-b<0.
$$

Thus $K$ is not a [positive semidefinite matrix](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md), and

$$
\boxed{k_2(x,x')=\max(x,x')\text{ is not a positive-semidefinite kernel}}.
$$

Equivalently, its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) $ab-b^2=b(a-b)$ is negative.
