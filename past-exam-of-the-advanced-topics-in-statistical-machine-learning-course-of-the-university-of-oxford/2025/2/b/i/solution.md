<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the real [L2 space](../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) $\mathcal H=L^2((0,\infty),dt)$ and the [feature map](../../../../../feature-map.md)

$$
\varphi(x)(t)=\mathbf1_{(0,x]}(t).
$$

Each [indicator function](../../../../../indicator-function.md) belongs to $\mathcal H$ because its squared [L2 norm](../../../../../l2-norm.md) is $x<\infty$. Their [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) is the length of the overlapping intervals:

$$
\boxed{\langle\varphi(x),\varphi(x')\rangle=\int_0^\infty\mathbf1_{(0,x]}(t)\mathbf1_{(0,x']}(t)\,dt=\min(x,x')=k_1(x,x')}.
$$

This realizes $k_1$ as a [positive-semidefinite kernel](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-kernel-split.md); it is also the [Brownian covariance kernel](../../../../../brownian-covariance-kernel.md).
