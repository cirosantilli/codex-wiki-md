<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Reciprocation reverses the order of positive numbers, so

$$
\boxed{k_3(x,x')=\frac1{\max(x,x')}=\min\left(\frac1x,\frac1{x'}\right)=k_1(1/x,1/x')}.
$$

This is the pullback of the [positive-semidefinite kernel](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-kernel-split.md) from part (i) by $x\mapsto1/x$. Explicitly, the [feature map](../../../../../feature-map.md) into the same [L2 space](../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) is

$$
\psi(x)(t)=\mathbf1_{(0,1/x]}(t),\qquad \langle\psi(x),\psi(x')\rangle=\frac1{\max(x,x')}.
$$

Hence **$k_3$ is a [positive-semidefinite kernel](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-kernel-split.md)**, with $\|\psi(x)\|_2^2=1/x<\infty$ for every allowed input.
