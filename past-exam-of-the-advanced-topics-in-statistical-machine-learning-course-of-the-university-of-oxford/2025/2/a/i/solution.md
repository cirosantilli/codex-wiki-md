↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In this setting a [positive-semidefinite kernel](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-kernel-split.md) is an inner-product similarity: there exist a real [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) $\mathcal H$ and a [feature map](../../../../../feature-map.md) $\varphi:\mathcal X\to\mathcal H$ such that

$$
\boxed{k(x,x')=\langle\varphi(x),\varphi(x')\rangle_{\mathcal H}}.
$$

The [feature map](../../../../../feature-map.md) may be infinite-dimensional. Equivalently, $k$ is symmetric and every finite [kernel matrix](../../../../../kernel-matrix.md) is a [positive semidefinite matrix](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). The equivalence follows by forming the [canonical feature space of a positive-semidefinite kernel](../../../../../canonical-feature-space-of-a-positive-semidefinite-kernel.md) and taking its [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) completion.
