# Selected-area electron diffraction

↑ **Parent:** [Electron diffraction](electron-diffraction.md)

Transmission-electron diffraction from a region selected by an aperture in an image plane. Downstream lenses image the objective [back focal plane](back-focal-plane.md) to the detector, producing reciprocal-space rather than real-space information.
