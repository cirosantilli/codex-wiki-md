# Spectral gap of a shearing-periodic box

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing-periodic boundary condition](shearing-periodic-boundary-condition.md)

For zero-mean fields in a [shearing box](shearing-box.md), [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) have wavevectors $(2\pi n/L_x+2At\,2\pi m/L_y,2\pi m/L_y,2\pi p/L_z)$. A nonzero azimuthal or vertical index supplies at least $2\pi/L$, where $L$ is the largest box side; otherwise the nonzero radial index does. [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md) gives the displayed [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) uniformly in time.
