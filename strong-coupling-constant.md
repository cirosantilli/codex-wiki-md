# Strong coupling constant

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)

The strong coupling constant parametrizes the strength of [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md) interactions. Its dependence on the [renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md) is governed by the [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md). It is dimensionless in four dimensions.
