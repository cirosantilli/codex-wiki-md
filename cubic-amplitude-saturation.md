# Cubic amplitude saturation

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg-Landau plane wave](ginzburg-landau-plane-wave.md)

For $a>0$, the positive amplitude [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) $R'=aR-R^3$ has solution $R(t)=\sqrt a[1+(a/R_0^2-1)e^{-2at}]^{-1/2}$. This follows from the [logistic differential equation](logistic-differential-equation.md) for $R^2$. Every $R_0>0$ approaches $\sqrt a$ as a [monotone function](monotonic-function.md), and the positive distance to this limit decreases; an oscillating complex wave carrying this amplitude has no ordinary monotone ordering.
