# Unitary similarity

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix similarity](matrix-similarity.md)

A [matrix similarity](matrix-similarity.md) implemented by a [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md). It expresses the same [linear operator](linear-operator.md) in two [orthonormal bases](orthonormal-basis.md), preserving [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), [trace](matrix-trace.md) and [singular values](singular-value.md). For a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md), the [spectral theorem](spectral-theorem.md) gives a diagonal representative by unitary similarity.
