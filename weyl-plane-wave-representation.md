# Weyl plane-wave representation

↑ **Parent:** [Outgoing Green function for the three-dimensional Helmholtz equation](outgoing-green-function-for-the-three-dimensional-helmholtz-equation.md)

Here $m=\sqrt{1-p^2-q^2}$ has nonnegative imaginary part. This representation of the outgoing [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md) kernel separates propagating [plane waves](plane-wave.md) from [evanescent waves](evanescent-wave.md). The absolute vertical separation is required for fields on either side of a source; choosing its sign fixes the upward or downward angular spectrum.
