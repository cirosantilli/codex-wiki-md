# Spread-out Bragg peak

↑ **Parent:** [Bragg peak](bragg-peak.md)

An extended high-dose depth interval formed by combining charged-particle beams of different ranges. The component [Bragg peaks](bragg-peak.md) are weighted to cover a finite target thickness.
