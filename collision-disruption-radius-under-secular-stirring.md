# Collision disruption radius under secular stirring

↑ **Parent:** [Catastrophic planetesimal collision](catastrophic-planetesimal-collision.md)

A phase-mixed population secularly forced by a distant eccentric [planet](planet.md) has typical [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) proportional to $e_p a/a_p$. Consequently a relative speed of order [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) times Keplerian speed gives $v_{\rm col}^2\sim e_p^2(a/a_p)GM/a_p$. Comparing [specific impact energy](specific-impact-energy.md) with a [catastrophic disruption threshold](catastrophic-disruption-threshold.md) gives a lower disruption radius $a/a_p\gtrsim Q_D^*a_p/(GM e_p^2)$, up to an order-one coefficient depending on velocity statistics and impact-energy convention. The [planet](planet.md) [mass](mass.md) sets the secular stirring time, but cancels from this energy scaling.
