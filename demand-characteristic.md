# Demand characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments-split.md)

A feature of an experimental setting that suggests how a participant is expected to respond. It can change a [self-report](self-report.md) without the intended change in underlying experience. Concealing a hypothesis helps, but does not prove that all expectancy or reporting effects have been removed.
