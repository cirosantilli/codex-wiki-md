# Polarization sum for a massive vector boson

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization vector](polarization-vector.md)

For $m>0$, the three physical [polarization vectors](polarization-vector.md) are transverse to $k$ and have norm $-1$ in metric $(+---)$. In the rest frame their sum is $\operatorname{diag}(0,1,1,1)$; [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md) then gives the displayed expression for $k^2=m^2$. This includes the longitudinal mode. It cannot be specialized to $m=0$ by setting its denominator to zero.
