# Electron-induced pair-production threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Pair production](pair-production.md)

For a free target [electron](electron.md) at rest, $e^-e^-\to e^-e^-e^-e^+$ has threshold [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) $4m_e$. In [natural units](natural-units.md), $s=2m_e^2+2m_eE_{\rm incident}$ gives incident total [energy](energy-split.md) $7m_e$, or incident [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $6m_e$. Atomic binding and recoil of the rest of an atom are excluded by this free-target model. This threshold differs from photon-induced pair production.
