# Mean square in ANOVA

↑ **Parent:** [Sum of squares in ANOVA](sum-of-squares-in-anova.md)

A [mean square in ANOVA](mean-square-in-anova.md) divides a [sum of squares in ANOVA](sum-of-squares-in-anova.md) by its positive number $d$ of [statistical degrees of freedom](statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md). If the corresponding residual subspace has scalar error [covariance](covariance.md) $\lambda I$, its [expectation](expected-value.md) is $\lambda$. Its scale depends on whether raw observations or group means were projected.
