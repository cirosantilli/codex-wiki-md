# Iwasawa theorem on local cyclotomic units

↑ **Parent:** [Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory](main-conjecture-of-iwasawa-theory.md)

For the real p-power cyclotomic tower, take norm limits of local principal units and closed cyclotomic-unit subgroups. The corrected Coleman logarithm identifies $U_\infty^1$ with the [Iwasawa algebra](iwasawa-algebra.md) of $G=\mathbb Z_p^\times/\{\pm1\}$. On a [symmetric cyclotomic unit](symmetric-cyclotomic-unit.md) it gives $([b]-[a])\zeta_p$. A topological generator generates the cyclotomic-unit limit and the [augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md), yielding the quotient description. This local calculation does not alone identify the characteristic ideal of the global Galois module.
