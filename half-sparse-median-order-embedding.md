# Half-sparse median-order embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Median order of a tournament](median-order-of-a-tournament.md)

A [graph embedding](graph-embedding.md) into an ordered [tournament](tournament-graph-theory.md) is half-sparse when the displayed inequality holds for every proper final interval $I$. Reserving two extra tournament vertices allows extension at any outward-oriented leaf: the [median order of a tournament](median-order-of-a-tournament.md) supplies at least half of the later vertices as outneighbours of the parent's image, while half-sparsity leaves at least one of them unused. Adding one image while extending every old final interval by two preserves the inequality.
