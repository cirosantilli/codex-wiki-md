# Geiger-Nuttall law

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha decay](alpha-decay.md)

The [Geiger-Nuttall law](geiger-nuttall-law.md) expresses the extreme sensitivity of an [alpha decay](alpha-decay.md) [half-life](half-life.md) to its [Q value](q-value.md). In a restricted range of heavy [nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md), a useful local form is $\log_{10}[T_{1/2}/T_*]\simeq aZ_d/\sqrt{Q_\alpha}+b$, where $Z_d$ is the daughter [atomic number](atomic-number.md), and the constants depend on the chosen units and nuclear family. It is named for [Hans Geiger](hans-geiger.md) and [John Mitchell Nuttall](john-mitchell-nuttall.md).

The [Coulomb barrier](coulomb-barrier.md) outside the daughter has $V(r)=2Z_d\alpha/r$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). Its outer turning point is $r_t=2Z_d\alpha/Q_\alpha$. The [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) gives a penetration probability

$$
P\simeq\exp\left[-2\int_R^{r_t}\sqrt{2\mu\,[V(r)-Q_\alpha]}\,dr\right],
$$

where $\mu$ is the daughter-alpha [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md). The leading large-barrier exponent is $2\pi(2Z_d\alpha)\sqrt{\mu/(2Q_\alpha)}$, producing the approximate $Z_d/\sqrt{Q_\alpha}$ dependence. Finite nuclear size modifies the exponent and the slowly varying intercept. The [nuclear decay constant](nuclear-decay-constant.md) also contains an assault frequency and an alpha-cluster formation probability. Extra [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) raises the barrier further, and odd-nucleon structure can introduce substantial hindrance. Thus extrapolation from even-even decays gives an order-of-magnitude reference rather than a precision prediction for an odd-odd [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md).
