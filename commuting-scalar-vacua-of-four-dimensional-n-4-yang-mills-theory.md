<h1 id="commuting-scalar-vacua-of-four-dimensional-n-4-yang-mills-theory">Commuting-scalar vacua of four-dimensional N=4 Yang-Mills theory</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Four-dimensional N=4 super Yang-Mills theory](four-dimensional-n-4-super-yang-mills-theory.md)

The [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) is a positive sum of squared commutators of the six adjoint [scalar fields](scalar-field.md). It vanishes precisely when they commute. For a compact gauge group, simultaneous conjugation puts a commuting tuple in a Cartan subalgebra. Its arbitrary $6r$ real coordinates are classical [flat directions of a scalar potential](flat-direction-of-a-scalar-potential.md), with residual identification by the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md). For $SU(N)$ the simultaneous eigenvalue vectors also obey the tracelessness constraint. Generic values break the gauge group to its maximal torus.
