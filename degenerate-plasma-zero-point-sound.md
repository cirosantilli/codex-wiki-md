# Degenerate-plasma zero-point sound

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate-plasma dielectric response](degenerate-plasma-dielectric-response.md)

The short-[wavelength](wavelength.md) root of the [degenerate-plasma dielectric response](degenerate-plasma-dielectric-response.md), approaching the edge of the occupied parallel [velocity](velocity.md) interval from outside. Writing $s=1+\delta$ makes its logarithmic singularity give the exponentially small positive correction in the title. It is the high-[wavenumber](wavenumber.md) limit of the charged [Langmuir wave](langmuir-wave.md) branch in this approximation; neutral [zero sound](zero-sound.md) has a different interaction and long-[wavelength](wavelength.md) limit.
