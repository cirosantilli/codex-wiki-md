# Absorbing four-ball Ehrenfest chain

↑ **Parent:** [Ehrenfest urn model](ehrenfest-urn-model.md)

Stop the four-ball [Ehrenfest urn model](ehrenfest-urn-model.md) upon reaching count zero or four. From count $r$, the transitions are to $r-1$ with probability $r/4$ and to $r+1$ with probability $(4-r)/4$. [First-step analysis](first-step-analysis.md) with absorbing boundary values gives the displayed vector $h$ of probabilities of absorption at zero and vector $t$ of [expected hitting times](expected-hitting-time.md) of either endpoint. For instance, $t_1=1+3t_2/4$ and $t_2=1+(t_1+t_3)/2$, with $t_1=t_3$, give $t_1=7$. From any interior state, absorption has probability at least $1/4$ within two steps; the resulting geometric tail bound proves both almost-sure absorption and finiteness of the expectations.
