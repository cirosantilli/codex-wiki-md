# Nobel Prize in Physics in 2009

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

One half went to [Charles Kuen Kao](charles-kuen-kao.md) for breakthroughs in transmitting light through [optical fibers](optical-fiber.md) for communication. One quarter each went to [Willard S. Boyle](willard-sterling-boyle.md) and [George E. Smith](george-elwood-smith.md) for inventing the [charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md) imaging sensor.[Individual official 2009 summary: Kao one half for optical-fiber communication; Boyle and Smith one quarter each for the CCD imaging sensor.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2009-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
