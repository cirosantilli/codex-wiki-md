# Polynomial moment bounds for the adjustment coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md)

For positive claims with a finite positive [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) neighbourhood, use $e^u>1+u+u^2/2$ and $e^u>1+u+u^2/2+u^3/6$ for $u>0$ in the defining [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) equation. They imply the displayed strict [upper bounds](upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md), where $r_2$ is the positive quadratic root. Its defining [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is negative at zero and positive at $r_1$, proving $r_2<r_1$. Higher exponential-series truncations similarly yield [upper bounds](upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) from more raw [moments](moment.md).
