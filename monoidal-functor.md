# Monoidal functor

↑ **Parent:** [Monoidal category](monoidal-category.md)

Here the unqualified term allows lax comparison maps $F_2:FX\otimes FY\to F(X\otimes Y)$ and $F_0:I\to FI$, natural and compatible with [associators](associator.md) and [unitors](unitor.md). Invertible comparison maps give a [strong monoidal functor](strong-monoidal-functor.md). The opposite direction gives an [opmonoidal functor](opmonoidal-functor.md).
