# Gauge group

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field](gauge-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_group)

The gauge group is the group of local internal symmetries. Its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) labels the gauge-boson species, while a matter field carries a representation of the group.
