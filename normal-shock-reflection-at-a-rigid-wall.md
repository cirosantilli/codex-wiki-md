# Normal shock reflection at a rigid wall

↑ **Parent:** [Normal shock wave](normal-shock-wave.md)

For a perfect-gas [normal shock wave](normal-shock-wave.md) reflected from a stationary rigid wall, apply the [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions for a perfect gas](rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md) using signed [velocity](velocity.md) in each [shock frame](shock-frame.md). The once-shocked state is the common left state for both applications. Its two relative speeds are roots of $v^2-(\gamma+1)u_sv/2-\gamma p_s/\rho_s=0$. Their product and the [pressure](pressure.md) jump eliminate the [velocity](velocity.md), giving the displayed relation. A strong incident [shock wave](shock-wave.md) gives $p_1/p_s\to(3\gamma-1)/(\gamma-1)$.
