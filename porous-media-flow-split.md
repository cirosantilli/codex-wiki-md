# Porous-media flow

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porous-media_flow)

Porous-media flow is fluid motion through an interconnected pore space. At small pore-scale [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md), its volume-averaged velocity is commonly governed by [Darcy's law](darcy-law.md).
