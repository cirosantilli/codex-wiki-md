# Tensor CMB line-of-sight source

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background line-of-sight solution](cosmic-microwave-background-line-of-sight-solution.md)

Ignoring the last-scattering angular source and later scattering, the tensor-induced temperature anisotropy is $\Theta=-\tfrac12\int_{\eta_*}^{\eta_0}\dot h_{ij}(\eta,\mathbf x_0-(\eta_0-\eta)\mathbf e)e^ie^j d\eta$. An instantaneous [cosmological visibility function](cosmological-visibility-function.md) alone leaves a scattering source at last scattering; leading tensor [tight coupling](tight-coupling-approximation.md) supplies the additional reason to neglect it.
