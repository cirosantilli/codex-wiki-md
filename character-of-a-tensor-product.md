# Character of a tensor product

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md)

For finite-dimensional [group representations](group-representation.md), the [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of their [tensor product](tensor-product.md) is the pointwise product of their [characters](character-of-a-representation.md). In a [tensor-product basis](tensor-product-basis.md), the diagonal entries of $A\otimes B$ are $A_{ii}B_{jj}$, so their sum is $(\operatorname{tr}A)(\operatorname{tr}B)$. This gives a direct route from a [character table](character-table.md) to tensor-product decompositions.
