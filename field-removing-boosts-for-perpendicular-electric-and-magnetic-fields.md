# Field-removing boosts for perpendicular electric and magnetic fields

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic field invariants](electromagnetic-field-invariants.md)

For perpendicular [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $\mathbf E$ and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) $\mathbf B$, the sign of $E^2-c^2B^2$ determines which field can be removed by a [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md). If positive, $\mathbf w=c^2\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/E^2$ gives $\mathbf B'=0$; if negative, $\mathbf w=\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/B^2$ gives $\mathbf E'=0$. Both results follow from $\mathbf E'_\perp=\gamma(\mathbf E+\mathbf w\times\mathbf B)$ and $\mathbf B'_\perp=\gamma(\mathbf B-\mathbf w\times\mathbf E/c^2)$; the relevant strict inequality ensures $|\mathbf w|<c$. A nonzero null crossed field cannot be reduced to a single field by a finite [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md). A particle initially at rest in the laboratory has initial [velocity](velocity.md) $-\mathbf w$ in the chosen [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md), which must be retained when solving its trajectory.
