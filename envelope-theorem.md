# Envelope theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Envelope_theorem)

For a smooth constrained maximum $\phi(b)=f(\bar x(b))$ subject to $g(\bar x(b))=b$, suppose smooth optimizers and multipliers satisfy $\nabla f(\bar x)=Dg(\bar x)^T\lambda$. Differentiating the constraint gives $Dg(\bar x)D_b\bar x=I$, and the chain rule gives $D_b\phi=\lambda^T$. Thus a [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) measures the marginal value of relaxing its corresponding constraint. A global supporting [Lagrangian duality](lagrangian-duality.md) representation implies the needed stationarity, but local stationarity and smoothness alone suffice for this derivative identity.
