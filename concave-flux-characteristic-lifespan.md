# Concave-flux characteristic lifespan

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md)

For smooth compactly supported data in the [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md) $u_t+f(u)_x=0$, put $m=\min_\xi f''(u_0(\xi))u_0'(\xi)$. Its smooth [characteristic solution of a scalar conservation law](characteristic-solution-of-a-scalar-conservation-law.md) exists until $t_*=-1/m$ if $m<0$, and for all times if $m\ge0$. The [characteristic flow map](characteristic-flow-map.md) $X_t(\xi)=\xi+t f'(u_0(\xi))$ has derivative $1+t f''(u_0)u_0'$; its first zero produces gradient blowup. The formula applies to smooth fluxes of either concavity, although it is particularly useful for a [concave function](concave-function.md) flux.
