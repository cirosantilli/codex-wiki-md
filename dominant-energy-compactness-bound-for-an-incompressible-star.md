# Dominant-energy compactness bound for an incompressible star

↑ **Parent:** [Central pressure of a constant-density relativistic star](central-pressure-of-a-constant-density-relativistic-star.md)

The [dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md) for the [Interior Schwarzschild metric](interior-schwarzschild-metric.md) reduces to its largest pressure satisfying $p_c\leq\rho$. Substitution of the exact central pressure gives $\sqrt{1-2M/R}\geq1/2$, hence $M/R\leq3/8$. Equality has finite central pressure and is allowed by the non-strict condition.
