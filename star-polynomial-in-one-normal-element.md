# Star polynomial in one normal element

↑ **Parent:** [Normal element of a C-star algebra](normal-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md)

Since a [normal C-star element](normal-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) commutes with its adjoint, words in those two generators can be rearranged into the displayed finite sum. Including the constant term generates the unital star algebra; omitting it generates the nonunital star algebra. Under [continuous functional calculus](continuous-functional-calculus.md), such a sum is the ordinary function $\sum_{j,k}c_{jk}z^j\overline z^k$ on the spectrum. The [Stone-Weierstrass theorem](stone-weierstrass-theorem.md) makes these functions uniformly dense.
