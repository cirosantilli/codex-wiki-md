# Methanolysis of an anhydro acetal

↑ **Parent:** [Acetal hydrolysis](acetal-hydrolysis.md)

[acid](acid-chemistry.md) activation can cleave an anomeric [acetal](acetal.md) bridge, producing an [oxocarbenium](oxocarbenium-ion.md)-like [electrophile](electrophile.md) and a free [alcohol](alcohol.md) on the former tether. [methanol](methanol.md) capture produces a methyl [acetal](acetal.md); anomeric [stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md) and chair preference must be distinguished.
