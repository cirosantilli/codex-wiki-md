# Positive invariant interval for a cubic biological population map

↑ **Parent:** [Forward-invariant set](forward-invariant-set.md)

For $F(N)=(N-1/2)(N-1)(2-N)+1/2$, every positive initial value with $F(N_0)>0$ has positive subsequent [iterates](discrete-dynamical-system-split.md). Indeed $F(N)<3/2$ for every $N>0$, since $F(N)-3/2=-N(2N^2-7N+7)/2<0$. On $[0,3/2]$, the negative part of the [cubic product](cubic-polynomial.md) has magnitude at most $3/8$, so $F(N)\ge1/8$. Thus the first positive image enters the [forward-invariant interval](forward-invariant-set.md) $(0,3/2)$.
