# Viscously damped U-tube oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [U-tube liquid oscillation](u-tube-liquid-oscillation.md)

If resisting [force](force-split.md) per unit moving length is $\beta v$, the total resistance is $\beta l\dot y$ and $\ddot y+\Gamma\dot y+\omega_0^2y=0$, where $\Gamma=\beta/(\rho A)$. For $\Gamma<2\omega_0$, an initial [velocity](velocity.md) $v_0$ from zero displacement gives $y=(v_0/\omega_d)e^{-\Gamma t/2}\sin\omega_dt$, with $\omega_d^2=\omega_0^2-\Gamma^2/4$. Its quality parameter is $Q=\omega_0/\Gamma$, and the [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) envelope decays at half the energy-decay exponent. The effective [damping](damping.md) model is an assumption, not a derivation of detailed viscous flow near the wall.
