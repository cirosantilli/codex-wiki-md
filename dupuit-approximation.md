# Dupuit approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Unconfined aquifer](unconfined-aquifer.md)

The Dupuit approximation treats flow in a shallow [unconfined aquifer](unconfined-aquifer.md) as predominantly horizontal, with [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) $p=\rho g(h-z)$ and horizontal pressure gradient independent of depth. Integrating [Darcy's law](darcy-law.md) over the saturated depth then gives a [volume flux per unit width](volume-flux-per-unit-width.md) depending only on the groundwater height and its slope.
