# Conjugate symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian inner product](hermitian-inner-product.md)

Exchanging the arguments of a [Hermitian inner product](hermitian-inner-product.md) takes its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md). This property, together with sesquilinearity and positive definiteness, distinguishes a complex [inner product](inner-product-split.md) from a general bilinear pairing.
