# Fenchel theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Total curvature](total-curvature.md)

Every closed regular space curve has [total curvature](total-curvature.md) at least $2\pi$. Equality holds exactly for convex plane curves traversed once. Here curvature is unsigned and integration is with respect to [arc length](arc-length.md).
