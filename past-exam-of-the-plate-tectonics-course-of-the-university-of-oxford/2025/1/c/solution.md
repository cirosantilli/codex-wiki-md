I would prioritize an accessible exposure of **ancient, fine-grained [lacustrine deposits](../../../../lacustrine-deposit.md) associated with a [river delta](../../../../river-delta.md)**, preferably with nearby [hydrothermal deposits](../../../../hydrothermal-deposit.md) or [groundwater](../../../../groundwater.md) alteration that offer a second environmental window. A good target maximizes three separate probabilities: past [habitability](../../../../habitability.md), preservation of a [biosignature](../../../../biosignature.md), and practical access to the preserved material. A spectacular former water channel alone need not score well on the second or third.

**First choice: a stratigraphically resolved ancient lake basin.** Look for a closed or formerly overflowing basin fed by an inlet valley, a [river delta](../../../../river-delta.md) with coherent layering, and adjacent fine [mudstone](../../../../mudstone.md) deposited below strong wave or channel disturbance. [Delta](../../../../river-delta.md) foresets and bottomsets, channel relationships and laterally continuous [lacustrine deposits](../../../../lacustrine-deposit.md) provide a testable [paleoenvironment](../../../../paleoenvironment.md), rather than relying on an isolated fan shape. An inlet plus an outlet and consistent shoreline elevations can constrain lake level and water throughput. Select [Noachian](../../../../noachian.md) or early [Hesperian](../../../../hesperian.md) units where available, while avoiding the assumption that all younger environments were uninhabitable.

Quiet [lacustrine deposits](../../../../lacustrine-deposit.md) can concentrate material from a wider drainage area; [clay minerals](../../../../clay-mineral.md) provide large surfaces for [adsorption](../../../../adsorption.md) of [organic matter](../../../../organic-matter.md). Rapid burial and limited exposure to oxidizing fluids favor preservation. [Carbonate](../../../../carbonate.md) or [silica](../../../../silica.md) cements can trap chemical or microscopic evidence, although later [diagenesis](../../../../diagenesis.md) may erase or redistribute it. A near-neutral, not excessively saline [paleoenvironment](../../../../paleoenvironment.md) is attractive for known [life](../../../../life.md), but should be inferred from mineral assemblages and sedimentary context rather than assumed from the presence of [water](../../../../water.md).

This strategy has direct primary-source precedents. An ancient lake [mudstone](../../../../mudstone.md) investigated by a rover preserved evidence for near-neutral [pH](../../../../ph.md), low [salinity](../../../../salinity.md) and variable [oxidation states](../../../../oxidation-state.md), making a plausible habitat for [chemolithotrophy](../../../../chemolithotrophy.md). This established past [habitability](../../../../habitability.md), not past [life](../../../../life.md).[Science 343, 1242777; NASA repository abstract](../../../../a-habitable-fluvio-lacustrine-environment-at-yellowknife-bay-gale-crater-mars-by-john-p-grotzinger-and-others.md) Independently, rover images of another basin established a [delta](../../../../river-delta.md)-lake succession followed by high-energy floods. That distinction helps choose fine background deposits rather than assuming the coarsest flood layers are the best [biosignature](../../../../biosignature.md) archive.[2021, sedimentary architecture and changing depositional conditions](../../../../perseverance-rover-reveals-an-ancient-delta-lake-system-and-flood-deposits-at-jezero-crater-mars-by-nicolas-mangold-and-others.md)

**Second choice: ancient water-rock reaction systems.** Target exhumed [hydrothermal deposits](../../../../hydrothermal-deposit.md) associated with old volcanic terrain, basin-floor alteration, or impact-generated [hydrothermal circulation](../../../../hydrothermal-circulation.md). Especially useful settings contain [silica](../../../../silica.md), [clay minerals](../../../../clay-mineral.md), [carbonates](../../../../carbonate.md) and reduced [iron](../../../../iron.md) or [sulfur](../../../../sulfur.md) phases in a coherent geological association. Chemical gradients between reacted fluids and surrounding [water](../../../../water.md) can supply [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) for [chemolithotrophy](../../../../chemolithotrophy.md); rapid mineral precipitation may entomb [biosignatures](../../../../biosignature.md). Habitable cooler mixing zones are more promising than assuming the hottest vent was habitable. Mineralogical mapping of thick, ancient basin deposits on [Mars](../../../../mars.md) has motivated a deep-water [hydrothermal alteration](../../../../hydrothermal-alteration.md) interpretation, providing a physically grounded alternative to shallow lakes. The interpretation remains a hypothesis to test in situ, not a detection of [life](../../../../life.md).[2017, proposed deep-water alteration setting and its astrobiological implications](../../../../ancient-hydrothermal-seafloor-deposits-in-eridania-basin-on-mars-by-joseph-r-michalski-and-others.md)

**Additional targets: protected groundwater archives.** Old fractures, mineral veins and [groundwater](../../../../groundwater.md)-fed [sedimentary deposits](../../../../sedimentary-deposit.md) exposed by recent [erosion](../../../../erosion.md) can sample former subsurface habitats protected from surface [ultraviolet radiation](../../../../ultraviolet-radiation.md). [Evaporites](../../../../evaporite.md) can preserve inclusions and record water chemistry, but strongly acidic or hypersaline terminal brines may be poor habitats, and a salt deposit alone does not distinguish a long-lived ecosystem from brief evaporation. Choose a traverse that can test the relation between these deposits and the lake or [hydrothermal alteration](../../../../hydrothermal-alteration.md) succession.

**Preservation and sampling can overturn the apparent ranking.** Favor ancient rocks that were buried for most of their history and only recently exhumed, with little thermal alteration, pervasive oxidation or recrystallization. Drill beneath weathered rinds and sample fresh bedrock; loose modern [regolith](../../../../regolith.md) mixes unrelated sources. Cosmic [ionizing radiation](../../../../ionizing-radiation.md) penetrates deeper than [ultraviolet radiation](../../../../ultraviolet-radiation.md), so shallow abrasion is not complete protection. Laboratory work reported by [National Aeronautics and Space Administration](../../../../national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md) shows that survival depths depend on molecule, mineral matrix and accumulated dose; meter-scale burial or a short exposure history can be important, rather than one universal safe drilling depth.[27 June 2022, irradiation experiment and implications for sampling](../../../../nasa-experiment-suggests-need-to-dig-deep-for-evidence-of-life-on-mars-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)

The landing ellipse needs safe terrain and a feasible traverse to several related exposures. Orbital [spectroscopy](../../../../spectroscopy.md), imaging and [surface topography](../../../../surface-topography.md) should establish the initial target; field observations must then test [stratigraphy](../../../../stratigraphy.md), sedimentary textures and alteration history. Combine [organic chemistry](../../../../organic-chemistry.md), [isotope ratios](../../../../isotope-ratio.md), mineral associations and microscopic structures in their geological context, using contamination controls and samples from neighboring noncandidate material. [Organic molecules](../../../../organic-molecule.md), [carbon isotope fractionation](../../../../carbon-isotope-fractionation.md) or a cell-like shape alone can have abiotic explanations. A convergent suite of contextual observations is a stronger case for ancient [life](../../../../life.md) than any single signal.
