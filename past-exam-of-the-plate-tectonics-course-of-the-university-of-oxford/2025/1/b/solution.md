<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Define the requirement before listing planetary properties.** For known [life](../../../../life.md), a habitable environment must supply a suitable solvent, accessible chemical ingredients, usable [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md), and conditions allowing organized [organic chemistry](../../../../organic-chemistry.md) to persist for sufficient time. [Habitability](../../../../habitability.md) is the capacity to support [life](../../../../life.md), not evidence that [abiogenesis](../../../../abiogenesis.md) or colonization actually occurred. Here the main target is long-lived near-surface [liquid water](../../../../liquid-water.md) on a [rocky planet](../../../../terrestrial-planet.md); a local subsurface habitat can satisfy the biological requirements even if most of the surface is hostile.

**Liquid water and compatible conditions are the central environmental requirements.** The local [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and composition must permit [liquid water](../../../../liquid-water.md) with adequate [water activity](../../../../water-activity.md). The pure-water [triple point](../../../../triple-point.md), approximately $273.16\,\mathrm K$ and $611\,\mathrm{Pa}$, is a useful reference, not a universal habitable threshold: dissolved salts alter the [phase diagram](../../../../phase-diagram.md), and subsurface [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) differs from atmospheric [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md). A brine being liquid does not establish biological usability if its [water activity](../../../../water-activity.md) is too low. There is no reason to require a global mean surface [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) of exactly $288\,\mathrm K$, or to treat $0$ to $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ at one atmosphere as a universal range for all habitats.

**Composition, nutrients and energy must be accessible together.** A planet needs reservoirs of [water](../../../../water.md) and biogenic elements, notably [carbon](../../../../carbon.md), [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md), [nitrogen](../../../../nitrogen.md), [oxygen](../../../../oxygen.md), [phosphorus](../../../../phosphorus.md) and [sulfur](../../../../sulfur.md), in forms available to a habitat. Bulk abundance is not enough if nutrients are locked away from reacting fluids. [pH](../../../../ph.md), [salinity](../../../../salinity.md), toxic solutes and [water-rock interaction](../../../../water-rock-interaction.md) influence accessibility. [Photosynthesis](../../../../photosynthesis.md) can use sunlight, while [chemolithotrophy](../../../../chemolithotrophy.md) can exploit [redox disequilibrium](../../../../redox-disequilibrium.md) generated by [water-rock interaction](../../../../water-rock-interaction.md), for example [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) production during [serpentinization](../../../../serpentinization.md). A warm environment at chemical equilibrium supplies no sustained metabolic [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) merely because it is warm. Conversely, atmospheric [oxygen](../../../../oxygen.md) and an [ozone layer](../../../../ozone-layer.md) are not prerequisites for all [life](../../../../life.md): they favor particular surface environments and metabolisms, whereas anaerobic [life](../../../../life.md) uses other pathways.

**An appropriate atmosphere and surface energy balance favor surface habitability.** The inventory and composition of the [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) control surface [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), the [greenhouse effect](../../../../greenhouse-effect.md), [heat transport](../../../../heat-transport.md), and protection from some harmful [ultraviolet radiation](../../../../ultraviolet-radiation.md). Too little warming can freeze the surface; too much can produce a [runaway greenhouse](../../../../runaway-greenhouse.md) and eventual [water](../../../../water.md) loss. The [albedo](../../../../albedo.md), clouds, [ocean](../../../../ocean.md) coverage and [atmospheric circulation](../../../../atmospheric-circulation.md) matter alongside atmospheric [carbon dioxide](../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and [water vapor](../../../../water-vapor.md). An illustrative globally averaged radiative balance is

$$
\frac{(1-A)L_\star}{16\pi a^2}=\sigma T_{\rm eff}^4,
$$

where $A$ is the [Bond albedo](../../../../bond-albedo.md), $a$ the orbital [semimajor axis](../../../../semi-major-axis.md), $L_\star$ the specified stellar [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md), and $\sigma$ the [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](../../../../stefan-boltzmann-constant.md). This determines an [planetary effective radiating temperature](../../../../planetary-effective-radiating-temperature.md), not the surface [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). The latter requires a model of the [greenhouse effect](../../../../greenhouse-effect.md) and [heat transport](../../../../heat-transport.md). The stellar [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md) here is an imposed boundary condition; the planetary variables under discussion are the [orbit](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), [albedo](../../../../albedo.md) and atmospheric properties, not characteristics of the host system.

**Mass and interior properties govern retention and replenishment.** Planetary [mass](../../../../mass.md), [radius](../../../../radius.md) and [surface gravity](../../../../surface-gravity.md) influence [atmospheric escape](../../../../atmospheric-escape.md), internal [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), and cooling. In a simple thermal-retention diagnostic,

$$
v_{\rm esc}=\sqrt{\frac{2GM_p}{R_p}},\qquad
\lambda=\frac{GM_pm}{k_BT_{\rm exo}R_{\rm exo}},
$$

where $v_{\rm esc}$ is the surface [escape velocity](../../../../escape-velocity.md), $G$ the [gravitational constant](../../../../gravitational-constant.md), $M_p$ and $R_p$ the planetary [mass](../../../../mass.md) and [radius](../../../../radius.md), and $m$, $T_{\rm exo}$ and $R_{\rm exo}$ refer to a gas particle and the [exobase](../../../../exobase.md). A large [Jeans escape parameter](../../../../jeans-escape-parameter.md) $\lambda$ suppresses ordinary thermal escape of that species; it does not exclude [hydrodynamic escape](../../../../hydrodynamic-escape.md) or nonthermal losses. There is no single demonstrated minimum Earth-like [mass](../../../../mass.md) valid for every atmospheric composition, age and habitat. [Radiogenic heating](../../../../radiogenic-heating.md), retained formation heat and possibly [tidal heating](../../../../tidal-heating.md) sustain internal activity, [volcanic outgassing](../../../../volcanic-outgassing.md) and subsurface [liquid water](../../../../liquid-water.md); excessive heating can be destructive. A massive primordial [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) envelope can also make a nominally rocky body unlike a temperate terrestrial surface.

**The orbit must supply a tolerable irradiation history.** With the host irradiation held specified, the [semimajor axis](../../../../semi-major-axis.md) must be compatible with the particular planet's [radiation budget](../../../../radiation-budget.md), not merely with a nominal distance label. The [circumstellar habitable zone](../../../../circumstellar-habitable-zone.md) is a conditional surface-[liquid water](../../../../liquid-water.md) criterion; it neither guarantees [habitability](../../../../habitability.md) nor excludes internally heated subsurface habitats outside it. [Orbital eccentricity](../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md) $e$ produces an apoapsis-to-periapsis flux ratio

$$
\frac{F_{\rm peri}}{F_{\rm apo}}=\left(\frac{1+e}{1-e}\right)^2.
$$

For fixed [semimajor axis](../../../../semi-major-axis.md), the orbit-averaged incident flux is $\langle F\rangle=L_\star/(4\pi a^2\sqrt{1-e^2})$. Both the average and the extremes matter; [thermal inertia](../../../../thermal-inertia.md) may buffer seasonal changes, so a perfectly circular [orbit](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) is favorable in some cases but not necessary. Sustained [orbital stability](../../../../orbital-stability.md) matters more than requiring $e=0$.

**Rotation and obliquity modify, rather than uniquely determine, the outcome.** [Obliquity](../../../../obliquity.md), [rotation period](../../../../rotation-period.md) and their evolution control seasonality, the distribution of sunlight and the efficiency of atmospheric and oceanic [heat transport](../../../../heat-transport.md). Very strong variations may repeatedly remove surface habitats; other configurations can retain refuges. [Tidal locking](../../../../tidal-locking.md) does not automatically make a planet uninhabitable if an adequate [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) or [ocean](../../../../ocean.md) redistributes [heat](../../../../heat-split.md). Neither Earth's particular day length nor its particular [obliquity](../../../../obliquity.md) is a necessary biological condition. [Tidal heating](../../../../tidal-heating.md) should be assessed as a planetary [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) budget, without assuming that more heating is always beneficial.

**Long-lived recycling is favorable; its mechanism need not be unique.** Exchange between interior and surface reservoirs can replace gases lost by [atmospheric escape](../../../../atmospheric-escape.md) or burial, expose nutrients, and moderate [climate](../../../../climate.md) through the [carbonate-silicate cycle](../../../../carbonate-silicate-cycle.md). [Plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) is one effective mechanism, but [stagnant-lid planets](../../../../stagnant-lid-planet.md) can have [volcanism](../../../../volcanism.md), [chemical weathering](../../../../chemical-weathering.md) and crustal recycling sufficient for modeled intervals of surface [habitability](../../../../habitability.md). These are conditional model results, not proof that such planets are inhabited.[2018, abstract: viable carbon cycling without a mobile lid](../../../../carbon-cycling-and-habitability-of-earth-sized-stagnant-lid-planets-by-bradford-j-foley-and-andrew-j-smye.md)

A [planetary magnetic field](../../../../planetary-magnetic-field.md) can alter charged-particle access and atmospheric loss, but it is not a universal shield against all [atmospheric escape](../../../../atmospheric-escape.md) and is not established as a necessary condition for [habitability](../../../../habitability.md). An [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md), [water](../../../../water.md) column or rock overburden can also protect habitats. Likewise, exposed continents, a large moon, modern atmospheric [oxygen](../../../../oxygen.md) and [plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) should not be promoted from plausible advantages to universal necessities. The defensible test is whether this particular body maintains accessible [liquid water](../../../../liquid-water.md), chemical ingredients and usable [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) in sufficiently persistent environments. A planet can pass that test without any demonstration that [life](../../../../life.md) arose there.
