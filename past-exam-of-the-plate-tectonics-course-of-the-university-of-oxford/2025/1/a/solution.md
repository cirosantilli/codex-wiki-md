[Plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) changes the environments in which [evolution](../../../../evolution-split.md) operates: it redistributes habitats, supplies and buries nutrients, and helps regulate [climate](../../../../climate.md). It does not prescribe the direction of [evolution](../../../../evolution-split.md), and its presence neither guarantees [life](../../../../life.md) nor demonstrates that [life](../../../../life.md) could not persist under another tectonic regime.

**Long-term environmental regulation.** [Seafloor spreading](../../../../seafloor-spreading.md) and [subduction](../../../../subduction.md) exchange material between the [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md), [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md), [ocean](../../../../ocean.md) and [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md). [Volcanism](../../../../volcanism.md) and [metamorphism](../../../../metamorphism.md) release [carbon dioxide](../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), while exposure of fresh [silicate minerals](../../../../silicate-mineral.md) by [tectonic uplift](../../../../tectonic-uplift.md) and [erosion](../../../../erosion.md) can promote [chemical weathering](../../../../chemical-weathering.md). Coupled [silicate weathering](../../../../silicate-weathering.md) and [carbonate](../../../../carbonate.md) burial remove [carbon dioxide](../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) from the surface system. In a simplified net reaction,

$$
\mathrm{CaSiO_3+CO_2\longrightarrow CaCO_3+SiO_2}.
$$

The [carbonate-silicate cycle](../../../../carbonate-silicate-cycle.md) can provide a [negative feedback](../../../../negative-feedback.md): warming and greater runoff can accelerate [silicate weathering](../../../../silicate-weathering.md), lowering atmospheric [carbon dioxide](../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and weakening the [greenhouse effect](../../../../greenhouse-effect.md). This requires available fresh rock and [liquid water](../../../../liquid-water.md); a supply-limited [chemical weathering](../../../../chemical-weathering.md) regime need not respond strongly to warming. [Plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) therefore helps sustain some long-lived habitable environments without acting as an infallible thermostat.[1981, abstract and weathering feedback model](../../../../a-negative-feedback-mechanism-for-the-long-term-stabilization-of-earth-s-surface-temperature-by-james-c-g-walker-and-others.md)

**Nutrients and oxygenation.** [Tectonic uplift](../../../../tectonic-uplift.md), [erosion](../../../../erosion.md) and [hydrothermal circulation](../../../../hydrothermal-circulation.md) supply nutrients such as [phosphorus](../../../../phosphorus.md) and [iron](../../../../iron.md) to surface environments. Changes in [continental configuration](../../../../continental-configuration.md), [continental shelf](../../../../continental-shelf.md) area and [ocean circulation](../../../../ocean-circulation.md) alter [primary production](../../../../primary-production.md), nutrient recycling and [ocean anoxia](../../../../ocean-anoxia.md). Burial of [organic carbon](../../../../organic-carbon.md) and reduced [sulfur](../../../../sulfur.md) can leave an oxidizing surface budget, whereas reduced volcanic gases and freshly exposed reduced rock consume oxidants. Thus [plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) can influence atmospheric [oxygen](../../../../oxygen.md) and the ecological opportunities for [aerobic respiration](../../../../aerobic-respiration.md), but the sign depends on competing fluxes rather than simply on the amount of [volcanism](../../../../volcanism.md).

**Geographical isolation and environmental diversity.** [Continental rifting](../../../../continental-rifting.md) separates populations, favoring [allopatric speciation](../../../../allopatric-speciation.md); [continental collision](../../../../continental-collision.md) and the emergence of land bridges can reconnect populations, enabling dispersal and competition. Opening or closing [ocean gateways](../../../../ocean-gateway.md) changes [ocean circulation](../../../../ocean-circulation.md) and [heat transport](../../../../heat-transport.md). [Mountain building](../../../../mountain-building.md) creates elevational habitats and [rain shadows](../../../../rain-shadow.md); changing [continental shelf](../../../../continental-shelf.md) area changes shallow-water habitat. These processes can promote diversification, eliminate habitats or both. A proposed biological response should be tested against independently dated [paleogeography](../../../../paleogeography.md) and the [fossil record](../../../../fossil-record.md), accounting for unequal preservation.

**Abrupt disturbances.** Large episodes of [volcanism](../../../../volcanism.md), including a [large igneous province](../../../../large-igneous-province.md), can rapidly perturb the [carbon cycle](../../../../carbon-cycle.md), [ocean acidification](../../../../ocean-acidification.md) and [ocean anoxia](../../../../ocean-anoxia.md); [sulfur dioxide](../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md) aerosols can instead produce shorter cooling episodes. Such disturbances may cause [mass extinction](../../../../mass-extinction.md), followed by ecological release and diversification of survivors. A [mantle plume](../../../../mantle-plume.md) need not originate at a [plate boundary](../../../../plate-boundary.md), so association with a tectonically active planet is not proof that horizontal [plate motion](../../../../plate-motion.md) directly caused every eruption. The relevant reasoning links a dated geological forcing to its environmental effects and then to biological selectivity, rather than attributing all biological crises to [plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md).

Over billions of years, [plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) can repeatedly create habitable environments and new selective pressures. Nevertheless, models allow [carbon cycling](../../../../carbon-cycling.md) and habitable [climate](../../../../climate.md) on some [stagnant-lid planets](../../../../stagnant-lid-planet.md). This makes [plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) a plausible contributor to sustained environmental diversity, not a demonstrated universal requirement for [evolution](../../../../evolution-split.md).[2018, coupled thermal and carbon-cycle models](../../../../carbon-cycling-and-habitability-of-earth-sized-stagnant-lid-planets-by-bradford-j-foley-and-andrew-j-smye.md)
