**Specify what “onset” means.** I would distinguish the first local, perhaps short-lived [subduction](../../../../subduction.md) event from persistent, self-sustaining recycling in a connected global network of [tectonic plates](../../../../tectonic-plate.md). Modern [plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) combines relatively rigid moving [lithosphere](../../../../lithosphere.md), localized boundary deformation, [seafloor spreading](../../../../seafloor-spreading.md) and sustained convergence. A sinking drip or a single imposed descending sheet is not sufficient evidence of that global regime. Different processes may become established at different times, so a transition interval may be a better hypothesis than an instantaneous worldwide switch.

**Construct competing physical models.** Solve conservation of [mass](../../../../mass.md), [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) for evolving [mantle convection](../../../../mantle-convection.md) and [lithosphere](../../../../lithosphere.md), including [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md)- and stress-dependent [rheology](../../../../rheology-split.md), brittle yielding, [plastic deformation](../../../../plastic-deformation.md), melting, compositional [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md) and relevant phase changes. Use [geodynamic modelling](../../../../geodynamic-modelling.md) to compare a [stagnant lid](../../../../stagnant-lid.md), episodic overturn, drip-dominated recycling and a sustained [mobile lid](../../../../mobile-lid.md). In a simplified incompressible creeping-flow framework,

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0,\qquad
-\nabla p+\nabla\cdot(2\eta\dot{\boldsymbol\epsilon})+\rho\mathbf g=0,
$$



$$
\rho c_p\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial t}+\mathbf u\cdot\nabla T\right)
=\nabla\cdot(k\nabla T)+\rho H+\Phi.
$$

Here $\mathbf u$ is [velocity](../../../../velocity.md), $p$ [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), $\eta$ effective [viscosity](../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md), $\dot{\boldsymbol\epsilon}$ the [strain-rate tensor](../../../../strain-rate-tensor.md), $\rho$ [density](../../../../density.md), $\mathbf g$ [gravitational acceleration](../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md), $c_p$ [specific heat capacity](../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md), $k$ [thermal conductivity](../../../../thermal-conductivity.md), $H$ radiogenic power per unit [mass](../../../../mass.md), and $\Phi$ dissipative heating per unit [volume](../../../../volume.md). These equations are a framework: compressibility, latent heat and chemical transport may require additional terms. A simple [yield stress](../../../../yield-stress.md) law $\tau_y=C+\mu(\rho gz-P_f)$ represents cohesion $C$, friction coefficient $\mu$ and [pore pressure](../../../../pore-pressure.md) $P_f$, illustrating why hydration and interface weakening can change the outcome.

**Vary the early-planet controls rather than assuming the answer.** Explore plausible initial mantle [potential temperature](../../../../potential-temperature.md), [radiogenic heating](../../../../radiogenic-heating.md), [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md) thickness and composition, [water](../../../../water.md) inventory, grain-size evolution, surface [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), and boundary strength. Hotter [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) lowers [viscosity](../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) but can produce thicker buoyant [basaltic crust](../../../../basaltic-crust.md); it does not automatically make sustained [subduction](../../../../subduction.md) easier. A cold strong plate can transmit [slab pull](../../../../slab-pull.md) yet resist bending and initiation. [Water weakening of rocks](../../../../water-weakening-of-rocks.md), melt-induced weakening, inherited faults and mechanically weak [sediments](../../../../sediment.md) can change this balance. Test proposed triggers such as [mantle plume](../../../../mantle-plume.md) impingement or gravitational instability without presupposing an established global plate-force system.

Useful diagnostics include the [Rayleigh number](../../../../rayleigh-number.md),

$$
\mathrm{Ra}=\frac{\rho g\alpha\Delta T D^3}{\eta\kappa},
$$

with [thermal expansion coefficient](../../../../coefficient-of-thermal-expansion.md) $\alpha$, layer thickness $D$, [thermal diffusivity](../../../../thermal-diffusivity.md) $\kappa$ and [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) contrast $\Delta T$, together with surface mobility, the localization of deformation, plate longevity, melt production and material recycling. A large [Rayleigh number](../../../../rayleigh-number.md) establishes vigorous [convection](../../../../convection-split.md) under suitable conditions, not necessarily a [mobile lid](../../../../mobile-lid.md). A convincing model should sustain one-sided [subduction](../../../../subduction.md), connected boundary evolution and [seafloor spreading](../../../../seafloor-spreading.md) after any artificial trigger is removed. Compare initiation thresholds with continuation thresholds: [hysteresis](../../../../hysteresis.md) can allow an established regime to persist under conditions in which it would not spontaneously start.

As an example of the type of experiment, three-dimensional models have tested [mantle plume](../../../../mantle-plume.md)-induced [subduction initiation](../../../../subduction-initiation.md), finding sensitivity to plate [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md), magmatic weakening and hydration of the interface. The scientific use of such results here is to identify a feasible mechanism and its predicted signatures, not to adopt their success as a direct measurement of an ancient onset date.[2015, modeled initiation conditions and alternative dripping behavior](../../../../plate-tectonics-on-the-earth-triggered-by-plume-induced-subduction-initiation-by-taras-v-gerya-and-others.md)

**Connect a model regime to a date through independent evidence.** Couple the experiments to candidate planetary cooling histories, then predict observable [pressure-temperature-time paths](../../../../pressure-temperature-time-path.md), magmatic compositions, deformation patterns and recycling rates for each regime. If independently dated rocks constrain a thermal or compositional state, ask which regimes can reproduce that state and the observed geological associations. Repeat over uncertain initial conditions and constitutive parameters, check numerical resolution, and compare two- and three-dimensional results where geometry matters. Models that prescribe a descending [slab](../../../../slab.md), fix plate [velocity](../../../../velocity.md), or insert a weak boundary may be excellent forward models of a working [subduction zone](../../../../subduction-zone.md) but do not demonstrate spontaneous initiation.

The result is a conditional statement: persistent [plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) is physically feasible under a range of conditions, and dated geological observations may favor that range at a particular time. Uncertain cooling histories, several feasible regimes and uncertain [rheology](../../../../rheology-split.md) prevent numerical feasibility alone from dating onset. The strongest constraint comes when the same parameter range explains multiple independently dated observations and alternative regimes fail those tests.
