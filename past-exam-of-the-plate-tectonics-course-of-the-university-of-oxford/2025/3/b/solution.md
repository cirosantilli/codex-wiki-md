<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Use chemical signatures to reconstruct processes, then test tectonic context.** [Geochemistry](../../../../geochemistry.md) can establish melting conditions, surface alteration and recycling, but [plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) is a kinematic system. I would sample dated suites across a mapped geological succession and compare competing origins, rather than declare an isolated “arc-like” analysis proof of a global regime. [Radiometric dating](../../../../radiometric-dating.md) must identify whether the measured age records crystallization, later [metamorphism](../../../../metamorphism.md), alteration or inheritance.

**Hydrous magmatism and selective element transfer.** Analyze fresh [basalt](../../../../basalt.md), associated intrusive rocks and differentiated volcanic suites for [major elements](../../../../major-element.md), [trace elements](../../../../trace-element.md) and [isotope ratios](../../../../isotope-ratio.md). Relative enrichment of fluid-mobile elements such as [barium](../../../../barium.md), [strontium](../../../../strontium.md) and [lead](../../../../lead.md), together with relative depletion of [niobium](../../../../niobium.md) and [tantalum](../../../../tantalum.md), can support addition of slab-derived components to a depleted [mantle wedge](../../../../mantle-wedge.md). Test coherent variations in [barium](../../../../barium.md)/[niobium](../../../../niobium.md), [thorium](../../../../thorium.md)/[niobium](../../../../niobium.md) and [rare-earth element](../../../../rare-earth-element.md) patterns against modeled fluid addition, sediment melting, source depletion and [fractional crystallization](../../../../fractional-crystallization.md). Do not treat these ratios as fixed universal cutoffs. [Crustal contamination](../../../../crustal-contamination.md), remelting of previously arc-modified [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md), residual [rutile](../../../../rutile.md) or other source effects can create some similar patterns. Altered rocks can also gain mobile elements long after their formation.

The case becomes stronger if the chemical evolution occurs in a plausible succession of oceanic, early forearc and mature [arc magmas](../../../../arc-magma.md), with supporting structure and [geochronology](../../../../geochronology.md). The key evidence would be correlated process changes in an independently reconstructed margin, not resemblance to one modern [basalt](../../../../basalt.md) plot. A [geochemical discrimination diagram](../../../../geochemical-discrimination-diagram.md) is a comparison tool; its modern training associations are not a proof that only those settings could exist on a hotter [Earth](../../../../earth.md).

**High-pressure melting and the fate of mafic crust.** [Tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite](../../../../tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite.md) suites with high [strontium](../../../../strontium.md)/[yttrium](../../../../yttrium.md) or [lanthanum](../../../../lanthanum.md)/[ytterbium](../../../../ytterbium.md) and low heavy [rare-earth element](../../../../rare-earth-element.md) abundances can indicate melting with residual [garnet](../../../../garnet.md) and little [plagioclase](../../../../plagioclase.md). Model the combined [major element](../../../../major-element.md) composition, residual phases and melt fraction to estimate the [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), hydration and source of melting. This could support melting of buried hydrous [oceanic crust](../../../../oceanic-crust.md). However, basal melting of thickened [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md), [delamination](../../../../delamination.md) and nonplate vertical burial can produce related conditions. High [strontium](../../../../strontium.md)/[yttrium](../../../../yttrium.md) alone neither proves a descending [slab](../../../../slab.md) nor dates the first [subduction](../../../../subduction.md). Conversely, hotter ancient [subduction](../../../../subduction.md) might not produce exactly the same magmatic products as modern cold [slabs](../../../../slab.md).[2011, modeled source mineralogies and distinct melting-pressure groups](../../../../the-composite-archaean-grey-gneisses-petrological-significance-and-evidence-for-a-non-unique-tectonic-setting-for-archaean-crustal-growth-by-jean-francois-moyen.md)

**Radiogenic isotopes distinguish new extraction from reworking.** Couple [zircon](../../../../zircon.md) [uranium-lead dating](../../../../uranium-lead-dating.md) with [hafnium isotope ratios](../../../../hafnium-isotope-ratio.md), and whole-rock [samarium-neodymium isotope ratios](../../../../samarium-neodymium-isotope-ratio.md) with [strontium isotope ratios](../../../../strontium-isotope-ratio.md) or [lead isotope ratios](../../../../lead-isotope-ratio.md). These systems can constrain contributions from depleted [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md), older [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md) and altered surface reservoirs. For a closed parent-daughter system,

$$
D^*(t)=P(t)\bigl(e^{\lambda t}-1\bigr),
$$

where $P(t)$ is the present parent abundance, $D^*$ the radiogenic daughter accumulated since closure, and $\lambda$ the [radioactive decay constant](../../../../radioactive-decay-constant.md). Initial daughter composition, closure and mixing must be constrained before interpreting an age. A crustal extraction model age depends on a reference [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) evolution and subsequent history; it is not automatically a rock's crystallization age or the date of a tectonic transition.

Juvenile signatures can indicate addition from the [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md), while evolved signatures can indicate reworking of older [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md); neither is exclusive to a particular tectonic regime. Spatially organized juvenile additions at a reconstructed margin, followed by collision and reworking, are more diagnostic than an undifferentiated global age histogram. Work combining [zircon](../../../../zircon.md) [hafnium isotope ratios](../../../../hafnium-isotope-ratio.md), [oxygen isotope ratios](../../../../oxygen-isotope-ratio.md) and age distributions illustrates how crustal generation, reworking and preservation can be separated conceptually, while remaining dependent on sampling and model assumptions.[2019, interpretation of age distributions and zircon isotope constraints](../../../../rates-of-generation-and-growth-of-the-continental-crust-by-christopher-hawkesworth-and-others.md)

**Stable isotopes can identify a visit to the surface.** Zircon [oxygen isotope ratios](../../../../oxygen-isotope-ratio.md) substantially different from an appropriate mantle reference may record melting of material that previously underwent low-[temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) [water-rock interaction](../../../../water-rock-interaction.md). This would support surface alteration followed by burial or remelting. It would not, by itself, distinguish [subduction](../../../../subduction.md) from burial under a thick volcanic pile or another form of recycling. Alteration, metamorphic exchange, inherited cores and analytical domains need checking. Multiple [stable isotope](../../../../stable-isotope.md) systems, including [boron isotope ratios](../../../../boron-isotope-ratio.md) in appropriate rocks, can strengthen a fluid-transfer interpretation if their fractionation and later resetting are modeled.

**Deep samples provide an independent archive.** If dated [diamond inclusions](../../../../diamond-inclusion.md) or [mantle xenoliths](../../../../mantle-xenolith.md) preserve [eclogite](../../../../eclogite.md) compositions together with a surface-alteration chemical signature, they could demonstrate transfer of crustal material into deep [lithospheric mantle](../../../../lithospheric-mantle.md). Dating the relevant mineral assemblage and distinguishing inclusion growth from later host transport are essential. A primary diamond-inclusion study used coupled chemical and isotopic observations to infer changes in deep recycling; the general lesson is to test a transport history, not to make the first preserved sample an infallible onset clock.[2011, diamond-inclusion data and proposed recycling interpretation](../../../../start-of-the-wilson-cycle-at-3-ga-shown-by-diamonds-from-subcontinental-mantle-by-steven-b-shirey-and-stephen-h-richardson.md) [Delamination](../../../../delamination.md) or other sinking processes remain alternatives unless structure and regional history establish plate convergence.

For each candidate signal, I would seek an older comparison population, quantify alteration and analytical uncertainty, reproduce the pattern in several independent regions, and combine chemistry with the tests in the other parts. A securely dated demonstration of [subduction](../../../../subduction.md) shows that it operated by that time in that region; it does not demonstrate that it began then everywhere. A secular shift toward more frequent, spatially coherent signatures can support expansion of the regime, but only after correcting for the survival of its archive.
