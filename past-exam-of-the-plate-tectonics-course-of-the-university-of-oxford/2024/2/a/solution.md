↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**Cascadia subducts a young, warm plate and lacks the enormous plateau and crustal root of the central [Andes](../../../../andes.md); the central Andean [Nazca Plate](../../../../nazca-plate.md) system also supports much deeper intraslab [earthquakes](../../../../earthquake.md).** These are regional comparisons: neither western margin is uniform along its length.

First, the [Juan de Fuca Plate](../../../../juan-de-fuca-plate.md) entering [Cascadia](../../../../cascadia-subduction-zone.md) is young, typically of order 10 million years old, because its [spreading ridge](../../../../spreading-ridge.md) lies close to the margin. Its [lithosphere](../../../../lithosphere.md) is comparatively thin and warm. Much of the [Nazca Plate](../../../../nazca-plate.md) entering the central [Andes](../../../../andes.md) is older and colder. A cold plate carries low [temperatures](../../../../temperature-split.md) farther into the [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md), sustaining deep intraslab [seismicity](../../../../seismicity.md); the warm Cascadia [slab](../../../../slab.md) loses its brittle seismogenic character much shallower. This concerns [earthquakes](../../../../earthquake.md) within the [slab](../../../../slab.md), not the size of [megathrust earthquakes](../../../../megathrust-earthquake.md): **both margins can generate very large plate-interface events**. The official warm-slab synthesis treats [Cascadia](../../../../cascadia-subduction-zone.md) as an endmember.[USGS, 2002, warm-slab comparison and the 55 km deep Nisqually earthquake](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/cascadia-subduction-zone-and-related-subduction-systems-seismic-structure-intraslab)

Second, the central [Andes](../../../../andes.md) have undergone major sustained [crustal shortening](../../../../crustal-shortening.md) and [tectonic thickening](../../../../tectonic-thickening.md), building a high plateau and locally roughly 60–70 km thick [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md). [Cascadia](../../../../cascadia-subduction-zone.md) has an active [volcanic arc](../../../../volcanic-arc.md) and mountains but no equivalent continuous central-Andean plateau and crustal root. The difference reflects the accumulated history of convergence, upper-plate deformation, [slab dip](../../../../slab-dip.md), trench motion and mechanical coupling, not just the instantaneous existence of [subduction](../../../../subduction.md). The [Andes](../../../../andes.md) include flat-slab segments with gaps in arc volcanism as well as normally dipping segments.[USGS, Seismotectonics of South America, tectonic summary and depth sections](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2015/1031/e/pdf/of2015-1031-E.pdf)[USGS-hosted crustal seismic synthesis, central-Andean crustal thickness](https://escweb.wr.usgs.gov/share/mooney/54seismicvelocitystruct.pdf)

A young warm incoming plate and a faster, colder incoming plate can therefore generate different [thermal structures](../../../../thermal-structure.md), [dehydration](../../../../dehydration.md) patterns and deformation histories. Plate age alone is insufficient to predict the thickness of the overriding [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md).
