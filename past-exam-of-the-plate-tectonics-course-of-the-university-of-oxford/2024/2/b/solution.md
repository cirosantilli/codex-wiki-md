**For a Seattle resident, the main consequences are [earthquake](../../../../earthquake.md) shaking and disruption, strongly modified by local ground conditions, plus location-dependent [tsunami](../../../../tsunami.md), [landslide](../../../../landslide.md) and volcanic hazards.**

Three [earthquake](../../../../earthquake.md) sources matter: the offshore [Cascadia](../../../../cascadia-subduction-zone.md) [megathrust](../../../../megathrust.md), deep events inside the [Juan de Fuca Plate](../../../../juan-de-fuca-plate.md), and shallow upper-plate [faults](../../../../geological-fault.md), including the [Seattle Fault](../../../../seattle-fault.md). Shaking can damage buildings, bridges, ports and utility networks, interrupting transport, electricity and water supply. Soft basin [sediments](../../../../sediment.md) can amplify or prolong shaking; saturated loose fill can undergo [soil liquefaction](../../../../soil-liquefaction.md) and lateral spreading. Steep slopes can fail as [landslides](../../../../landslide.md). The same regional event can therefore have very different consequences on bedrock and on artificial fill.[Pacific Northwest Hazards by United States Geological Survey](../../../../pacific-northwest-hazards-by-united-states-geological-survey.md)

Large [Cascadia](../../../../cascadia-subduction-zone.md) [tsunamis](../../../../tsunami.md) chiefly threaten the outer Pacific coast. Seattle's inland waterways also have local [tsunami](../../../../tsunami.md) hazards from crustal [faults](../../../../geological-fault.md) and underwater [landslides](../../../../landslide.md); one should not equate a Seattle waterfront site with an open-ocean coastal site. Cascade eruptions can affect the region through [volcanic ash](../../../../volcanic-ash.md) and transport disruption. [Lahars](../../../../lahar.md) are strongly channelled by river valleys, so their exposure is location dependent rather than uniform across Seattle. These geological differences influence infrastructure design, land-use decisions and emergency planning.[Washington Geological Survey, earthquake and fault mechanisms](https://www.dnr.wa.gov/programs-and-services/geology/geologic-hazards/earthquakes-and-faults)[USGS, Puget Sound deltas, historic liquefaction damage and the Seattle Fault](https://cmgds.marine.usgs.gov/data/geotech/pugetposter/geologic.html)
