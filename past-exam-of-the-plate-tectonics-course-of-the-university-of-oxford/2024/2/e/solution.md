<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**Support for the left curve comes from high laboratory friction and substantial in situ [differential stress](../../../../differential-stress.md); support for the right curve comes from low inferred fault traction, weak fault materials or high [pore pressure](../../../../pore-pressure.md).** No single observation directly measures the whole plotted envelope.

For the **left, stronger shallow-crust model**:

- Many friction experiments on ordinary [rocks](../../../../rock-geology.md) give a [coefficient of friction](../../../../coefficient-of-friction.md) around 0.6–0.85. Together with approximately [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md), these values predict increasing [frictional strength](../../../../frictional-strength.md) with depth.
- Deep-borehole measurements of [stress](../../../../stress.md) and [pore pressure](../../../../pore-pressure.md) in some continental settings indicate a crust close to frictional failure at appreciable [differential stress](../../../../differential-stress.md). Jointly with appropriate [fault](../../../../geological-fault.md) orientations, these support a strong frictional upper [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md).

For the **right, weak shallow-layer model**:

- On well-studied plate-boundary [faults](../../../../geological-fault.md), low resolved [shear stress](../../../../shear-stress.md) inferred from the orientation of principal [stresses](../../../../stress.md) relative to the active [fault plane](../../../../fault-plane.md) can support low effective traction.
- The absence of the expected localized long-term [frictional heating](../../../../frictional-heating.md) anomaly can support weak sliding. Directly measured high [pore pressure](../../../../pore-pressure.md) or very low-friction [fault gouge](../../../../fault-gouge.md) would give a physical explanation. The heat-flow and stress evidence is particularly developed for the [San Andreas Fault](../../../../san-andreas-fault.md); it is an analogue argument, not an observation that the entire Cascadia upper [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md) follows that curve.[2004, introduction and discussion of strong and weak fault models](../../../../stress-orientations-at-intermediate-angles-to-the-san-andreas-fault-california-by-jeanne-l-hardebeck-and-andrew-j-michael.md)

**To discriminate, measure the strength-controlling quantities through depth and across both faults and their surroundings.** Obtain borehole [stress](../../../../stress.md), [pore pressure](../../../../pore-pressure.md) and [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) profiles where feasible; use [borehole breakouts](../../../../borehole-breakout.md) and hydraulic tests to constrain [effective stress](../../../../effective-stress.md). Recover fault and host [rocks](../../../../rock-geology.md) and test their [friction](../../../../friction.md) and [material creep](../../../../creep-deformation.md) at relevant fluid conditions. The right curve specifically predicts an end to the shallow weakening near its sharp rise: investigate changes in [permeability of a porous medium](../../../../permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md), alteration and [fault gouge](../../../../fault-gouge.md) with depth, rather than testing only a surface sample.

Combine those measurements with [heat flux](../../../../heat-flux-density.md) transects, [magnetotelluric sounding](../../../../magnetotelluric-sounding.md) for conductive fluids, accurate [earthquake](../../../../earthquake.md) depths and [focal mechanisms](../../../../focal-mechanism.md), and [GNSS](../../../../gnss.md) or [InSAR](../../../../interferometric-synthetic-aperture-radar.md) observations of deformation. The latter can help constrain the depth distribution of locking and [postseismic relaxation](../../../../postseismic-relaxation.md), but neither locking nor [seismicity](../../../../seismicity.md) alone gives absolute strength. [Earthquake stress drop](../../../../earthquake-stress-drop.md) is a change in [stress](../../../../stress.md), not the pre-event [stress](../../../../stress.md). Low [heat flux](../../../../heat-flux-density.md) can also reflect heat transport or transient weakening; high electrical conductivity can have causes other than free [water](../../../../water.md). A joint explanation of all these data is needed to choose between the models locally.
