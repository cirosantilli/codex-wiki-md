<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**In the interpretation of [Christopher Kincaid](../../../../christopher-kincaid.md) and colleagues, [slab rollback](../../../../slab-rollback.md) deforms and splits a [mantle plume](../../../../mantle-plume.md)'s thermal anomaly: one branch is swept toward the trench beneath the [High Lava Plains](../../../../high-lava-plains.md), whereas the [Snake River Plain](../../../../snake-river-plain.md) records the overriding plate moving over a deformed plume conduit.** Two opposite age progressions need not represent two stationary hotspots.

In the supplied map, ages decrease toward [Yellowstone](../../../../yellowstone-hotspot.md) in the northeast and toward [Newberry Volcano](../../../../newberry-volcano.md) in the northwest. Southwestward [North American Plate](../../../../north-american-plate.md) motion leaves older [Snake River Plain](../../../../snake-river-plain.md) volcanism to the southwest of the younger source expression. Meanwhile, rollback-driven, three-dimensional [mantle flow](../../../../mantle-flow.md), including [toroidal mantle flow](../../../../toroidal-mantle-flow.md) around the [slab](../../../../slab.md), carries a passive thermal anomaly trenchward beneath the [High Lava Plains](../../../../high-lava-plains.md). The associated migrating heating and melting front produces the opposite younging toward [Newberry Volcano](../../../../newberry-volcano.md).

The original experiment used a viscous syrup [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) analogue, a kinematically moved rigid [slab](../../../../slab.md), and an injected buoyant thermal plume. The result is **advection of deformed plume heat by subduction-driven flow**, rather than simple radial spreading of an intact plume head under a stationary plate. The mapped systematic progression principally concerns [rhyolitic](../../../../rhyolitic-volcanism.md) volcanism; [basaltic](../../../../basaltic-rock.md) eruptions are more diffuse in time. This laboratory result establishes a physically plausible explanation, not a unique observational proof of the deep source geometry.[2013, Figures 2–3, interpretation of the passive thermal anomaly and deformed conduit](../../../../bifurcation-of-the-yellowstone-plume-driven-by-subduction-induced-mantle-flow-by-christopher-kincaid-and-others.md)
