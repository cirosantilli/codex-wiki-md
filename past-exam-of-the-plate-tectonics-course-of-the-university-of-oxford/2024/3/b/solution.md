↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Probably yes in connected fractures or transiently opened [pore space](../../../../pore-space.md), especially where a [transform fault](../../../../transform-fault.md) admits circulating [seawater](../../../../seawater.md); a uniform water-filled porous layer several kilometres thick is not guaranteed.** [Porosity](../../../../porosity.md) and [permeability of a porous medium](../../../../permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) generally decrease as cracks close or seal, while ongoing [faulting](../../../../faulting.md) can reopen pathways. Fluid can occur at high [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), including a supercritical state; it need not resemble cold surface [water](../../../../water.md).

Several complementary tests address presence and origin:

- **Geological:** recover fresh cores or dredged [fault rocks](../../../../fault-rock.md). Hydrous alteration assemblages, veins, [serpentinite](../../../../serpentinite.md) replacing [peridotite](../../../../peridotite.md), and altered [gabbro](../../../../gabbro.md) record [water-rock interaction](../../../../water-rock-interaction.md). Sealed [fluid inclusions](../../../../fluid-inclusion.md) can preserve former fluids. These establish fluid access in the past; they do not by themselves demonstrate currently connected free [pore fluid](../../../../pore-fluid.md).
- **Geochemical:** analyse sampled fluids and [fluid inclusions](../../../../fluid-inclusion.md) for salinity and chlorine, oxygen and hydrogen [isotope ratios](../../../../isotope-ratio.md), and strontium or boron [isotope ratios](../../../../isotope-ratio.md) in alteration products. Compare with [seawater](../../../../seawater.md), host [rocks](../../../../rock-geology.md), and possible magmatic or metamorphic sources. A seawater-like input modified systematically by [water-rock interaction](../../../../water-rock-interaction.md) supports deep circulation from the [ocean](../../../../ocean.md). Exchange, [isotope fractionation](../../../../isotope-fractionation.md), mixing and phase separation mean that altered fluids need not retain the exact surface composition.
- **Geophysical:** combine [seismic tomography](../../../../seismic-tomography.md), [Compressional-to-shear wave speed ratio](../../../../compressional-to-shear-wave-speed-ratio.md) and [seismic attenuation](../../../../seismic-attenuation.md) with [magnetotelluric sounding](../../../../magnetotelluric-sounding.md) or controlled-source electrical measurements. A connected saline fluid network can reduce [electrical resistivity](../../../../electrical-resistivity.md) and change elastic [wave speeds](../../../../wave-speed.md). Sample [hydrothermal vents](../../../../hydrothermal-vent.md), measure [heat flux](../../../../heat-flux-density.md) and, if drilling is possible, make direct [pore pressure](../../../../pore-pressure.md) and permeability measurements.

**No one anomaly uniquely identifies water.** [Seismic velocity](../../../../seismic-velocity.md) can also reflect cracks, composition and [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md); [electrical conductivity](../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) can reflect [melt](../../../../geological-melt.md) or conductive [minerals](../../../../mineral.md). [Serpentinization](../../../../serpentinization.md) demonstrates incorporation of [water](../../../../water.md) into [minerals](../../../../mineral.md), which must be distinguished from fluid currently occupying [pore space](../../../../pore-space.md). At an oceanic [transform fault](../../../../transform-fault.md), an external [seawater](../../../../seawater.md) supply is the natural first hypothesis, but [isotope ratios](../../../../isotope-ratio.md) and chemical budgets can test contributions from deep fluids rather than presuming the source. A research example of coupled deformation and hydrothermal alteration is [Mass transfer and fluid flow during detachment faulting and development of an oceanic core complex, Atlantis Massif by Chiara Boschi and others](../../../../mass-transfer-and-fluid-flow-during-detachment-faulting-and-development-of-an-oceanic-core-complex-atlantis-massif-by-chiara-boschi-and-others.md); a detachment system illustrates the methods without proving identical flow at every [transform fault](../../../../transform-fault.md).
