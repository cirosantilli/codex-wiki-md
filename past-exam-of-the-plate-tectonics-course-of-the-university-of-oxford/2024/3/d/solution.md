<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**Choose a common horizontal [compensation depth](../../../../compensation-depth.md) beneath the deepest cooling-induced [density](../../../../density.md) anomaly, within [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) that can relax pressure differences over the timescale considered.** In a normal oceanic plate model, this means below the old plate's thermal [lithosphere](../../../../lithosphere.md), not at the base of the approximately 7 km thick [oceanic crust](../../../../oceanic-crust.md).

The reason is that cooling changes [density](../../../../density.md) through both [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md) and [lithospheric mantle](../../../../lithospheric-mantle.md). A datum at the [Mohorovičić discontinuity](../../../../mohorovicic-discontinuity.md) would omit most of the changing column [mass](../../../../mass.md). A different datum for each age would also compare unlike columns. Once the common datum is sufficiently deep, extending it farther into laterally uniform [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) adds the same [mass](../../../../mass.md) per unit area to each column and does not change the predicted relative subsidence.

There is no unique number fixed by the question. For an illustrative [oceanic plate cooling model](../../../../oceanic-plate-cooling-model.md) with a thermal thickness about 100–125 km, a common datum beneath that layer, of order 150–200 km below sea level, is a defensible choice provided the model has no substantial lateral [density](../../../../density.md) anomaly there. These are model choices, not measured universal boundaries. In a [half-space cooling model](../../../../half-space-cooling-model.md), use the oldest age $t_{\max}$ to choose a practical cutoff: $z_c\gtrsim4\sqrt{\kappa t_{\max}}$ makes the remaining [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) anomaly less than about 0.5% of its surface contrast. For $\kappa=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$ and $t_{\max}=100$ million years, this is about 225 km. A half-space model has no mathematically sharp finite thermal base.

Finally, equal column [mass](../../../../mass.md) is the local, approximately constant-[gravitational acceleration](../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md) representation of equal [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) at the datum. A weak [asthenosphere](../../../../asthenosphere.md) permits pressure relaxation through slow solid-state [material creep](../../../../creep-deformation.md); it need not be liquid. Short-wavelength loads, [lithospheric flexure](../../../../lithospheric-flexure.md) or [dynamic topography](../../../../dynamic-topography.md) can violate local column-by-column [isostasy](../../../../isostasy.md), requiring a more complete mechanical model.
