<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**I would not assign the [lunar mare](../../../../lunar-mare.md) an automatic terrestrial 100–125 km compensation level. For present-day mare basins, a local equal-column-mass model may have no physically valid [compensation depth](../../../../compensation-depth.md); first test whether [lithospheric flexure](../../../../lithospheric-flexure.md) supports the load.** For ancient thermally driven subsidence that did relax, choose a common datum beneath the cooling layer in deformable [lunar mantle](../../../../lunar-mantle.md) appropriate to that epoch.

The [Moon](../../../../moon.md) is smaller than [Earth](../../../../earth.md) and has lost a larger fraction of its original heat. Its cold, mechanically strong outer shell can be much thicker relative to its radius than the terrestrial oceanic [lithosphere](../../../../lithosphere.md). [Lunar mare](../../../../lunar-mare.md) [basalts](../../../../basalt.md) filled pre-existing impact basins: their loading, basin excavation, mantle uplift, cooling and the evolving strength of the lithosphere all matter. A thick [elastic lithosphere](../../../../elastic-lithosphere.md) can support lateral [stress](../../../../stress.md) differences, so equal column [mass](../../../../mass.md) need not hold even after a long time. The strong [gravity anomalies](../../../../gravity-anomaly.md) of lunar [mascons](../../../../mascon.md), measured by [GRAIL](../../../../gravity-recovery-and-interior-laboratory.md), are evidence that the present mare-basin system is not everywhere locally compensated.[NASA's GRAIL Mission Solves Mystery of Moon's Surface Gravity by National Aeronautics and Space Administration](../../../../nasa-s-grail-mission-solves-mystery-of-moon-s-surface-gravity-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)

For an explicitly assumed ancient relaxed thermal model, estimate its thermal penetration depth from [thermal diffusivity](../../../../thermal-diffusivity.md) and the elapsed cooling time, infer the contemporaneous mechanical lid from [gravity-topography admittance](../../../../gravity-topography-admittance.md) or basin deformation, and place $z_c$ below that layer where lateral [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) anomalies relax. For example, a hypothetical several-hundred-kilometre-thick lid requires a deeper datum than that lid, not the shallow [crust-mantle boundary](../../../../mohorovicic-discontinuity.md). The paper supplies neither an epoch-specific thermal model nor a load wavelength, so a unique kilometre value is not determined. If the chosen datum is a substantial fraction of the lunar radius, spherical geometry and radial variation in [gravity](../../../../gravity.md) must replace the flat, constant-gravity column approximation.

**Does the Moon have an [asthenosphere](../../../../asthenosphere.md)? Not a demonstrated shallow, globally connected Earth-like layer underlying mobile plates; but “no weak or partially molten mantle anywhere, ever” is too strong.** [Apollo lunar seismology](../../../../apollo-lunar-seismology.md) indicates a cold outer interior and more attenuating deep regions. Reanalyses support a deep low-velocity or partially molten region near the [lunar core](../../../../lunar-core.md), while [lunar laser ranging](../../../../lunar-laser-ranging.md) and tidal [Love numbers](../../../../love-number.md) constrain deep deformability and [tidal dissipation](../../../../tidal-dissipation.md). Such a deep weak region is sometimes called a lunar [asthenosphere](../../../../asthenosphere.md); it is not automatically the correct local compensation surface for a mare load.[Seismic detection of the layers of the lunar core by Renee C. Weber and colleagues](../../../../seismic-detection-of-the-layers-of-the-lunar-core-by-renee-c-weber-and-colleagues.md)

The inferred melt fraction and distribution remain model dependent: [Dissipation at tidal and seismic frequencies in a melt-free Moon by Francis Nimmo and others](../../../../dissipation-at-tidal-and-seismic-frequencies-in-a-melt-free-moon-by-francis-nimmo-and-others.md) demonstrates that much of the dissipation can also be explained by hot solid-state [anelasticity](../../../../anelasticity.md), although its model does not reproduce every frequency-dependent constraint. The [Moon](../../../../moon.md) was hotter during mare emplacement and could relax loads differently then. **Choose the datum from the relevant thermal and mechanical history, or use a flexural model when local [isostasy](../../../../isostasy.md) fails.**
