**The expected broad contrast is a less mature, commonly thinner early juvenile [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md), with more [mafic](../../../../mafic.md) material and abundant sodic [tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite](../../../../tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite.md), rather than the more differentiated, potassium-rich granitoid component characteristic of much [Phanerozoic](../../../../phanerozoic.md) [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md).** This is a secular tendency, not a single stratified column applicable throughout the [Archean](../../../../archean.md).

[Archean](../../../../archean.md) terrains commonly expose [tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite](../../../../tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite.md) [gneiss](../../../../gneiss.md) associated with [greenstone belts](../../../../greenstone-belt.md) containing [basalt](../../../../basalt.md), [komatiite](../../../../komatiite.md) and [metasedimentary rocks](../../../../metasedimentary-rock.md). Many later granitoids are richer in [potassium](../../../../potassium.md), including [granite](../../../../granite.md) and [granodiorite](../../../../granodiorite.md). The proportions changed during the [Archean](../../../../archean.md): a reconstruction from [trace element](../../../../trace-element.md) ratios in [sedimentary rocks](../../../../sedimentary-rock.md) infers a markedly [mafic](../../../../mafic.md) upper [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md) before about 3.0 Ga and a more [felsic](../../../../felsic.md) upper [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md) by 2.5 Ga. Thus “all Archean continental crust was mafic” would be wrong. A preserved [tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite](../../../../tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite.md)-dominated nucleus can be strongly [felsic](../../../../felsic.md), including at depth; its composition is not identical to that of its original juvenile precursor.[Archean upper crust transition from mafic to felsic marks the onset of plate tectonics by Ming Tang and others](../../../../archean-upper-crust-transition-from-mafic-to-felsic-marks-the-onset-of-plate-tectonics-by-ming-tang-and-others.md)

**Thickness needs a stated comparison.** An influential seismic compilation found preserved [Archean](../../../../archean.md) [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md) about 27–40 km thick, commonly without the thick high-velocity basal layer of its 40–55 km thick [Proterozoic](../../../../proterozoic.md) comparison provinces. That supports a relatively thin endmember, but is not a measurement of every original [Archean](../../../../archean.md) [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md), nor a demonstration that every [Archean](../../../../archean.md) region was thinner than today's 35–40 km reference column. [Tectonic thickening](../../../../tectonic-thickening.md), [magmatic underplating](../../../../magmatic-underplating.md), [erosion](../../../../erosion.md), [delamination](../../../../delamination.md) and preservation bias all alter what survives. The very thick [lithospheric mantle](../../../../lithospheric-mantle.md) beneath a [craton](../../../../craton.md) must also not be mistaken for thick [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md).[Evolution of the Precambrian lithosphere: Seismological and geochemical constraints by Raymond J. Durrheim and Walter D. Mooney](../../../../evolution-of-the-precambrian-lithosphere-seismological-and-geochemical-constraints-by-raymond-j-durrheim-and-walter-d-mooney.md)

The transferable answer is therefore **more [mafic](../../../../mafic.md) early juvenile precursors, widespread sodic [tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite](../../../../tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite.md) production, and variable thickness evolving through time**; late [Archean](../../../../archean.md) [felsic](../../../../felsic.md) nuclei can already resemble substantial modern [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md).
