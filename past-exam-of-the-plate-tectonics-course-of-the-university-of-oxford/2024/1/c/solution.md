<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Today the usual first melt is [basaltic](../../../../basaltic-rock.md) melt from the [mantle wedge](../../../../mantle-wedge.md); in a hotter [Archean](../../../../archean.md) [subduction zone](../../../../subduction-zone.md), melting of hydrated [basaltic crust](../../../../basaltic-crust.md) itself could generate sodic [felsic](../../../../felsic.md) melt of [tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite](../../../../tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite.md) composition.** Distinguish a primary melt from the evolved melt ultimately added to the upper [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md).

On the modern [Phanerozoic](../../../../phanerozoic.md) [Earth](../../../../earth.md):

- [Hydrothermal alteration](../../../../hydrothermal-alteration.md) introduces [water](../../../../water.md) into the incoming [oceanic crust](../../../../oceanic-crust.md) and locally into [serpentinized mantle](../../../../serpentinized-mantle.md). [Sediments](../../../../sediment.md) also carry [water](../../../../water.md) in [pore space](../../../../pore-space.md) and hydrous [minerals](../../../../mineral.md).
- As the [slab](../../../../slab.md) descends, heating and [metamorphism](../../../../metamorphism.md) break down hydrous [minerals](../../../../mineral.md). Released fluids enter the hotter [peridotite](../../../../peridotite.md) of the [mantle wedge](../../../../mantle-wedge.md) and lower its [solidus](../../../../solidus.md), causing [flux melting](../../../../flux-melting.md). Most ordinary modern [slabs](../../../../slab.md) release fluids before extensive melting of their [basaltic crust](../../../../basaltic-crust.md).
- The primary melt is predominantly hydrous [basaltic](../../../../basaltic-rock.md) [magma](../../../../magma.md). During ascent, storage and [fractional crystallization](../../../../fractional-crystallization.md), [mafic minerals](../../../../mafic-mineral.md) are removed and the remaining liquid evolves toward [andesite](../../../../andesite.md), [dacite](../../../../dacite.md) and [rhyolite](../../../../rhyolite.md) compositions. [Partial melting](../../../../partial-melting.md) of recently emplaced [mafic](../../../../mafic.md) lower [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md), [magma mixing](../../../../magma-mixing.md) and some [crustal contamination](../../../../crustal-contamination.md) can contribute to [felsic](../../../../felsic.md) [magma](../../../../magma.md). Intrusive equivalents include [diorite](../../../../diorite.md), [tonalite](../../../../tonalite.md) and [granodiorite](../../../../granodiorite.md).

This adds juvenile material derived from the [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md); simply remelting old [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md) is reworking, not net juvenile addition. The amount and depth of [slab](../../../../slab.md) [water](../../../../water.md) release depend on its thermal history, as quantified in [Subduction factory: 4. Depth-dependent flux of H2O from subducting slabs worldwide by Peter E. van Keken and others](../../../../subduction-factory-4-depth-dependent-flux-of-h2o-from-subducting-slabs-worldwide-by-peter-e-van-keken-and-others.md).

For the hotter [Archean](../../../../archean.md) endmember posited in the question, a hotter [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) and a warm descending plate make it easier for hydrated [basaltic crust](../../../../basaltic-crust.md) to reach its [solidus](../../../../solidus.md). Its [partial melting](../../../../partial-melting.md) can directly make [silica](../../../../silica.md)-rich, [sodium](../../../../sodium.md)-rich [tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite](../../../../tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite.md) liquids, leaving a more [mafic](../../../../mafic.md) residue. Residual [garnet](../../../../garnet.md) and [amphibole](../../../../amphibole.md), and the presence or absence of residual [plagioclase](../../../../plagioclase.md), help determine the [rare-earth element](../../../../rare-earth-element.md) and strontium signatures. These are not ultramafic liquids merely because the surrounding [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) was hotter.

**The contrast is therefore commonly fluid-fluxed [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) melting followed by differentiation today, versus a greater potential contribution from direct melting of hydrated [mafic crust](../../../../mafic-crust.md) in the hotter case.** [Tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite](../../../../tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite.md) is not unique evidence for [subduction](../../../../subduction.md): melting of thickened [mafic crust](../../../../mafic-crust.md) can produce similar liquids. Nor are all modern arcs devoid of [slab melting](../../../../slab-melting.md). These qualifications are central to [The composite Archaean grey gneisses: Petrological significance, and evidence for a non-unique tectonic setting for Archaean crustal growth by Jean-François Moyen](../../../../the-composite-archaean-grey-gneisses-petrological-significance-and-evidence-for-a-non-unique-tectonic-setting-for-archaean-crustal-growth-by-jean-francois-moyen.md).
