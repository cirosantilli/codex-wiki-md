<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**Hotter [Archean](../../../../archean.md) [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) would generally produce thicker, more magnesium-rich [oceanic crust](../../../../oceanic-crust.md), together with a more strongly depleted residual [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md).** Greater melt production, not merely thermal expansion of an unchanged modern column, is the principal reason for the increased crustal thickness.

At a [spreading ridge](../../../../spreading-ridge.md), upwelling [peridotite](../../../../peridotite.md) undergoes [adiabatic decompression melting](../../../../adiabatic-decompression-melting.md). A higher mantle [potential temperature](../../../../potential-temperature.md) makes the ascending [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) intersect the [solidus](../../../../solidus.md) at greater [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and depth. It therefore melts over a longer decompression interval and generally reaches a larger integrated [melt fraction](../../../../melt-fraction.md). With comparable [melt focusing](../../../../melt-focusing.md), more melt is supplied per unit area of newly formed [oceanic crust](../../../../oceanic-crust.md).

Modern normal [oceanic crust](../../../../oceanic-crust.md) is about 6–7 km thick and contains [basaltic](../../../../basaltic-rock.md) lavas, a [sheeted dike complex](../../../../sheeted-dike-complex.md), and [gabbro](../../../../gabbro.md) with [mafic](../../../../mafic.md) cumulates at depth. An illustrative reconstruction for hot ambient [Archean](../../../../archean.md) [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) gives about 25–35 km of [oceanic crust](../../../../oceanic-crust.md), rather than a universal fixed thickness. Its primary liquids are more magnesium-rich than typical modern primary ridge liquids; their crystallization produces more [olivine](../../../../olivine.md)- and [pyroxene](../../../../pyroxene.md)-rich [cumulates](../../../../cumulate-rock.md). The extracted liquid and its crystal products must be distinguished: the entire final [oceanic crust](../../../../oceanic-crust.md) need not be a homogeneous [komatiite](../../../../komatiite.md) layer.[Thermal history of the Earth and its petrological expression by Claude Herzberg and others](../../../../thermal-history-of-the-earth-and-its-petrological-expression-by-claude-herzberg-and-others.md)

The residual [peridotite](../../../../peridotite.md) loses more melt components, becoming more refractory and commonly [harzburgite](../../../../harzburgite.md)-rich. Thick buoyant [basaltic crust](../../../../basaltic-crust.md), its later [eclogitization](../../../../eclogitization.md), and the depleted underlying [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) can change the mechanics of [subduction](../../../../subduction.md). A hotter [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) does not by itself establish how fast plates moved or whether a globally connected modern [plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) regime operated.

[Komatiites](../../../../komatiite.md), with very magnesium-rich primary liquids and high inferred eruption [temperatures](../../../../temperature-split.md), provide evidence for hot melting, but may sample especially hot [mantle plumes](../../../../mantle-plume.md). Estimates of ambient [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) conditions also use less exceptional non-arc [basalts](../../../../basalt.md). Variable source composition, [water](../../../../water.md) content, cooling, melt extraction and later alteration all limit the precision of a single inferred [Archean](../../../../archean.md) thickness. The robust prediction is **a larger melting column and melt yield, thicker igneous [oceanic crust](../../../../oceanic-crust.md), and a more depleted residue**.
