<h1 id="question-1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**[Slab pull](../../../../slab-pull.md) is generally the largest direct driving contribution for plates attached to substantial cold descending [slabs](../../../../slab.md).** Cooling makes [oceanic lithosphere](../../../../oceanic-lithosphere.md) negatively buoyant; sinking [slabs](../../../../slab.md) transmit a pulling force to the rest of the [tectonic plate](../../../../tectonic-plate.md). The comparison is a force balance, so the following driving and resisting processes both matter.

- [Ridge push](../../../../ridge-push.md) is gravitational sliding away from an elevated [spreading ridge](../../../../spreading-ridge.md). Cooling, contraction and deepening of [oceanic lithosphere](../../../../oceanic-lithosphere.md) establish the lateral difference in integrated [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md); [ridge push](../../../../ridge-push.md) is not simply [magma](../../../../magma.md) squirting sideways into a plate.
- [Basal traction on a tectonic plate](../../../../basal-traction-on-a-tectonic-plate.md) transfers [shear stress](../../../../shear-stress.md) between the [lithosphere](../../../../lithosphere.md) and circulating [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md). It can help or oppose [plate motion](../../../../plate-motion.md), depending on their relative [velocities](../../../../velocity.md). [Slab suction](../../../../slab-suction.md) is the contribution of [slab](../../../../slab.md)-induced [mantle flow](../../../../mantle-flow.md) and its associated [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and [traction](../../../../traction.md) to nearby [plate motion](../../../../plate-motion.md).
- [Frictional sliding](../../../../frictional-sliding.md) along [faults](../../../../geological-fault.md), shearing along the plate interface, bending of the incoming [slab](../../../../slab.md) and [viscous dissipation](../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) in the surrounding [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md) resist [plate motion](../../../../plate-motion.md). [Continental collision](../../../../continental-collision.md) introduces additional resistance through buoyancy and deformation of thick [continental crust](../../../../continental-crust.md).
- [Mantle plumes](../../../../mantle-plume.md) and [continental rifting](../../../../continental-rifting.md) can locally promote uplift, weakening and separation, but do not provide a universal explanation for all [plate motion](../../../../plate-motion.md).

The first two gravitational contributions ultimately belong to the same coupled [mantle convection](../../../../mantle-convection.md) system: the plates are its cold upper [thermal boundary layer](../../../../thermal-boundary-layer.md). They are not passive rafts on a prescribed conveyor belt. A plate with little attached descending [slab](../../../../slab.md) need not have [slab pull](../../../../slab-pull.md) as its largest local driver, and a large gross [slab pull](../../../../slab-pull.md) can be partly consumed by bending and interface resistance. Geological observations and force comparisons nonetheless support the general dominance of [slab pull](../../../../slab-pull.md) for rapidly moving subducting plates.[Section explaining mantle circulation, ridge push and slab pull, including the association between subducting plate edges and faster motion.](../../../../what-causes-earthquakes-by-british-geological-survey.md)
