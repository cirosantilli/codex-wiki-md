<h1 id="question-2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Interpret [habitability](../../../../habitability.md) as the ability to sustain [life](../../../../life.md) with broadly terrestrial [biochemistry](../../../../biochemistry-split.md). **Accessible [liquid water](../../../../liquid-water.md), usable [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md), suitable chemical ingredients and sufficiently persistent conditions are the central requirements.** Earth-like geography, a breathable [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) and actual habitation are not part of that definition.

**A viable local physical environment.** The relevant [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) must permit [liquid water](../../../../liquid-water.md) where the [organisms](../../../../organism-split.md) live. For a surface habitat this normally requires an appropriate [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) and [surface temperature](../../../../surface-temperature.md); the roughly 6.1 millibar triple-point [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) of pure [water](../../../../water.md) is a minimum phase constraint, not a guarantee of a temperate surface. High [salinity](../../../../salinity.md), extreme acidity and low [water activity](../../../../water-activity.md) can make apparently wet environments unsuitable for known [life](../../../../life.md). A subsurface [ocean](../../../../ocean.md) beneath ice can provide liquid conditions even when the surface is frozen, so the entire body need not lie in the conventional surface-water [habitable zone](../../../../circumstellar-habitable-zone.md).

**A suitable orbit and rotation.** For a surface-water [planet](../../../../planet.md), orbital distance must give an absorbed [radiation budget](../../../../radiation-budget.md) compatible with liquid conditions after the body's [Bond albedo](../../../../bond-albedo.md), [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) and [greenhouse effect](../../../../greenhouse-effect.md) are included. The simple [planetary equilibrium temperature](../../../../planetary-equilibrium-temperature.md) estimate

$$
T_{\rm eq}=\left[\frac{(1-A)S}{4\sigma}\right]^{1/4}
$$

uses orbital irradiance $S$, [Bond albedo](../../../../bond-albedo.md) $A$ and the [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](../../../../stefan-boltzmann-constant.md) $\sigma$, assumes unit thermal emissivity and averages over the whole sphere. It is not a prediction of [surface temperature](../../../../surface-temperature.md) beneath a [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) with a [greenhouse effect](../../../../greenhouse-effect.md). Excessive absorbed energy risks a [runaway greenhouse](../../../../runaway-greenhouse.md); inadequate surface heating can freeze surface [water](../../../../water.md), although [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) from the interior or [tidal heating](../../../../tidal-heating.md) can maintain a subsurface habitat.

The orbit must persist without collision or ejection for a biologically useful interval. Large [orbital eccentricity](../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md) can produce extreme variations in heating, but neither exactly circular motion nor Earth's particular [obliquity](../../../../obliquity.md) is essential. [Rotation period](../../../../rotation-period.md) and [obliquity](../../../../obliquity.md) govern illumination and seasons; atmospheric and [ocean heat flux](../../../../ocean-heat-flux.md) determine how severe those variations become. [Tidal locking](../../../../tidal-locking.md) does not automatically make a body uninhabitable, because sufficient heat transport can moderate the day–night contrast. A [natural satellite](../../../../natural-satellite.md) also needs a stable orbit around its primary and a tolerable combination of irradiation and [tidal heating](../../../../tidal-heating.md): enough heating may maintain an [ocean](../../../../ocean.md), whereas excessive heating can make the environment hostile. These are constraints on the body's location and path, without requiring a particular kind of surrounding planetary system.

**An adequate chemical inventory and access to it.** Known [life](../../../../life.md) uses [carbon](../../../../carbon.md), [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md), [nitrogen](../../../../nitrogen.md), [oxygen](../../../../oxygen.md), [phosphorus](../../../../phosphorus.md) and [sulfur](../../../../sulfur.md), together with catalytic trace elements. A [rocky planet](../../../../terrestrial-planet.md) supplies mineral surfaces and reservoirs, but total inventory is not enough: [chemical weathering](../../../../chemical-weathering.md), [water-rock interaction](../../../../water-rock-interaction.md) or other transport must make [nutrients](../../../../nutrient.md) accessible. A water-rich body with an [ocean](../../../../ocean.md) isolated from its rocky interior by a thick high-pressure ice layer could have difficulty recycling some [nutrients](../../../../nutrient.md); this is a possible limitation, not proof of sterility.

**A usable energy supply.** [Photosynthesis](../../../../photosynthesis.md) can exploit light in an illuminated habitat. A dark subsurface habitat instead needs sustained [chemical disequilibrium](../../../../chemical-disequilibrium.md), for example oxidized and reduced species brought together by [water-rock interaction](../../../../water-rock-interaction.md), [hydrothermal circulation](../../../../hydrothermal-circulation.md) or [radiolysis](../../../../radiolysis.md). Warm [water](../../../../water.md) at complete [chemical equilibrium](../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) is not, by itself, an energy supply for sustained [metabolism](../../../../metabolism-split.md).

**Retention, protection and longevity.** The body's [mass](../../../../mass.md), radius, [surface gravity](../../../../surface-gravity.md) and thermal history affect retention of [water](../../../../water.md) and an [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md). [Escape velocity](../../../../escape-velocity.md) must be considered together with gas [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), molecular mass, [atmospheric escape](../../../../atmospheric-escape.md) and replenishment: there is no universal Earth-mass threshold. An [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md), ice or [rock](../../../../rock-geology.md) overburden can protect [life](../../../../life.md) from damaging [ionizing radiation](../../../../ionizing-radiation.md) and short-wavelength light. An intrinsic [planetary magnetic field](../../../../planetary-magnetic-field.md) can modify charged-particle exposure and escape, but it is neither a universal requirement nor a guarantee of retention.

Finally, conditions must last long enough. Stored formation [heat](../../../../heat-split.md), [radiogenic heating](../../../../radiogenic-heating.md) and possibly [tidal heating](../../../../tidal-heating.md) can maintain circulation and chemical exchange. [Plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) can recycle materials and support a [carbonate-silicate cycle](../../../../carbonate-silicate-cycle.md), but it is not established as a necessary condition for every habitable body: conditional long-term [carbon cycling](../../../../carbon-cycling.md) is possible on a [stagnant-lid planet](../../../../stagnant-lid-planet.md) as well.[Abstract and coupled thermal/carbon-cycle model; habitability is conditional on degassing, weathering and interior evolution.](../../../../carbon-cycling-and-habitability-of-earth-sized-stagnant-lid-planets-by-bradford-j-foley-and-andrew-j-smye.md) The necessary end result is persistence of a suitable environment, rather than a checklist requiring every feature of the modern [Earth](../../../../earth.md).
