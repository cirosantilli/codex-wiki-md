<h1 id="question-2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) changes the environments in which [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md), [gene flow](../../../../gene-flow.md) and [extinction](../../../../extinction.md) operate. **It influences both the persistence of habitable conditions and the geographical and chemical opportunities for [evolution](../../../../evolution-split.md).** It does not prescribe a unique sequence of biological innovations.

Separation of continents by [continental rifting](../../../../continental-rifting.md) divides populations and reduces [gene flow](../../../../gene-flow.md), promoting [allopatric speciation](../../../../allopatric-speciation.md). [Continental collision](../../../../continental-collision.md) or newly exposed land connections can reunite populations, permit dispersal and introduce competitors or predators. These mechanisms connect [biogeography](../../../../biogeography.md) to [continental configuration](../../../../continental-configuration.md). Changing the amount and fragmentation of shallow [continental shelf](../../../../continental-shelf.md) habitat can also change the opportunities for diversification of marine [organisms](../../../../organism-split.md).

Over long intervals, [volcanic outgassing](../../../../volcanic-outgassing.md), [silicate weathering](../../../../silicate-weathering.md), carbonate burial and [subduction](../../../../subduction.md) exchange [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) among the interior, [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) and [ocean](../../../../ocean.md). In a suitable regime, warmer, wetter conditions increase [silicate weathering](../../../../silicate-weathering.md), remove atmospheric [carbon dioxide](../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and weaken the [greenhouse effect](../../../../greenhouse-effect.md). This [negative feedback](../../../../negative-feedback.md) can support long-lived [habitability](../../../../habitability.md), though it depends on exposed fresh [rock](../../../../rock-geology.md), available [water](../../../../water.md) and reaction rates.[Weathering–carbon dioxide–temperature coupling in the proposed long-term stabilization mechanism.](../../../../a-negative-feedback-mechanism-for-the-long-term-stabilization-of-earth-s-surface-temperature-by-james-c-g-walker-and-others.md) [Mountain building](../../../../mountain-building.md) changes precipitation, [erosion](../../../../erosion.md) and delivery of [nutrients](../../../../nutrient.md) to the [ocean](../../../../ocean.md), affecting [primary production](../../../../primary-production.md) and organic [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) burial.

The opening and closure of [ocean gateways](../../../../ocean-gateway.md) changes [ocean circulation](../../../../ocean-circulation.md), transport of [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) and the supply of [oxygen](../../../../oxygen.md) to deep [water](../../../../water.md). Together with movement of land through different latitudes and changes in [sea level](../../../../sea-level.md), this reorganizes [climate](../../../../climate.md) and habitat. Enhanced organic [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) burial can change atmospheric [oxygen](../../../../oxygen.md) over long intervals, while a poorly ventilated [ocean](../../../../ocean.md) can develop [ocean anoxia](../../../../ocean-anoxia.md). Such chemical changes alter which forms of [metabolism](../../../../metabolism-split.md) are viable.

[Hydrothermal circulation](../../../../hydrothermal-circulation.md) at [spreading ridges](../../../../spreading-ridge.md) and through fractured [rock](../../../../rock-geology.md) supplies [chemical disequilibrium](../../../../chemical-disequilibrium.md) and mineral surfaces. For example, [serpentinization](../../../../serpentinization.md) can produce [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) usable by [chemolithotrophy](../../../../chemolithotrophy.md) when suitable electron acceptors are available. This supplies a possible setting for [abiogenesis](../../../../abiogenesis.md) and early ecosystems; it does not establish that [life](../../../../life.md) originated there.

There can also be abrupt disruption. Rapid emplacement of a [large igneous province](../../../../large-igneous-province.md) can change [climate](../../../../climate.md) through gas release; sulfur-bearing [aerosols](../../../../aerosol.md) can initially cool, whereas sustained [carbon dioxide](../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) release can warm and contribute to [ocean acidification](../../../../ocean-acidification.md) and [ocean anoxia](../../../../ocean-anoxia.md). Such perturbations can contribute to [mass extinction](../../../../mass-extinction.md), followed by [adaptive radiation](../../../../adaptive-radiation.md) among survivors. The [Deccan Traps](../../../../deccan-traps.md) exemplify the scale of [volcanism](../../../../volcanism.md) involved, but their association with the [Cretaceous–Palaeogene boundary](../../../../cretaceous-palaeogene-boundary.md) does not justify attributing the entire biological crisis to [volcanism](../../../../volcanism.md) alone. Both gradual environmental reorganization and rare rapid perturbations belong in the explanation.
