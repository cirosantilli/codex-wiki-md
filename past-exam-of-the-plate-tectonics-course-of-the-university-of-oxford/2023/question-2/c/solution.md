<h1 id="question-2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Prioritize accessible ancient [lacustrine deposits](../../../../lacustrine-deposit.md) at a preserved [river delta](../../../../river-delta.md), especially fine-grained, [clay mineral](../../../../clay-mineral.md)-bearing [mudstones](../../../../mudstone.md), with nearby evidence of [groundwater](../../../../groundwater.md) or [hydrothermal circulation](../../../../hydrothermal-circulation.md).** The target should combine past [habitability](../../../../habitability.md), good [biosignature preservation](../../../../biosignature-preservation.md) and practical access to minimally altered [rock](../../../../rock-geology.md).

An inlet valley, a [river delta](../../../../river-delta.md) and a stratified basin together can establish a connected fluvial–lake [paleoenvironment](../../../../paleoenvironment.md) on [Mars](../../../../mars.md). A long-lived lake offers [liquid water](../../../../liquid-water.md), chemical transport and a sustained habitat, while a [river delta](../../../../river-delta.md) concentrates material from a larger catchment. Distal [mudstone](../../../../mudstone.md) is particularly attractive: fine particles can adsorb [organic molecules](../../../../organic-molecule.md), burial reduces exposure, and quiet deposition can preserve small-scale structures. Rover observations have independently established ancient lake and [river delta](../../../../river-delta.md) settings on [Mars](../../../../mars.md), so this is a geological search strategy grounded in observed environments.[Abstract: a fluvio-lacustrine sedimentary environment with inferred chemical conditions suitable for microbial life.](../../../../a-habitable-fluvio-lacustrine-environment-at-yellowknife-bay-gale-crater-mars-by-john-p-grotzinger-and-others.md)[Abstract: delta-lake deposition followed by later high-energy flooding.](../../../../perseverance-rover-reveals-an-ancient-delta-lake-system-and-flood-deposits-at-jezero-crater-mars-by-nicolas-mangold-and-others.md)

A second high-value setting is an ancient [hydrothermal deposit](../../../../hydrothermal-deposit.md), particularly [silica](../../../../silica.md)-rich precipitates, spring terraces or mineralized [groundwater](../../../../groundwater.md) fractures near a heat source. Such environments combine [water-rock interaction](../../../../water-rock-interaction.md), [chemical disequilibrium](../../../../chemical-disequilibrium.md) and the possibility of rapid mineral entombment. Distal, moderate-temperature parts of a [hydrothermal circulation](../../../../hydrothermal-circulation.md) system are preferable to strongly reheated zones where [organic molecules](../../../../organic-molecule.md) would be destroyed. Orbital observations identify candidate ancient [hydrothermal deposits](../../../../hydrothermal-deposit.md) on [Mars](../../../../mars.md), although their geological interpretation requires testing on the ground.[Abstract and inferred deep-water hydrothermal setting; evidence for an environment rather than evidence of life.](../../../../ancient-hydrothermal-seafloor-deposits-in-eridania-basin-on-mars-by-joseph-r-michalski-and-others.md) [Carbonate](../../../../carbonate.md)-bearing [sedimentary deposits](../../../../sedimentary-deposit.md) can also preserve textures and chemical records of former [water](../../../../water.md), but precipitation of [carbonate](../../../../carbonate.md) alone is not a [biosignature](../../../../biosignature.md).

Choose ancient deposits with good [stratigraphy](../../../../stratigraphy.md), limited later [metamorphism](../../../../metamorphism.md) and relatively recent exposure by [erosion](../../../../erosion.md), or sample beneath the irradiated surface. Present exposed [organic molecules](../../../../organic-molecule.md) can be destroyed by [cosmic rays](../../../../cosmic-ray.md), [radiolysis](../../../../radiolysis.md) and oxidizing surface chemistry, so a drill, fresh scarp or recently excavated material improves the preservation prospects. Laboratory irradiation experiments on Martian analogue materials demonstrate why shallow ancient surface exposure can erase an organic record.[27 June 2022, experiments on radiation-driven destruction of amino acids and implications for subsurface sampling.](../../../../nasa-experiment-suggests-need-to-dig-deep-for-evidence-of-life-on-mars-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)

At the site, relate every sample to its [sedimentary facies](../../../../sedimentary-facies.md) and mineral context. Seek several independent, spatially associated [biosignatures](../../../../biosignature.md): plausible microscopic structures, distinctive [organic molecules](../../../../organic-molecule.md), and [isotope fractionation](../../../../isotope-fractionation.md) consistent with a biological process. Each has nonbiological alternatives, so test [water-rock interaction](../../../../water-rock-interaction.md), mineral self-organization and contamination before inferring [life](../../../../life.md). Include control samples from adjacent unpromising [rocks](../../../../rock-geology.md) and track terrestrial contamination. A safe landing ellipse with traversable exposures of several related [sedimentary facies](../../../../sedimentary-facies.md) is more useful than a spectacular but inaccessible single outcrop. The desired outcome is contextual evidence that can distinguish former [life](../../../../life.md) from merely former [habitability](../../../../habitability.md).
