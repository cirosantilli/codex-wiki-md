<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The decisive change was from moving continents within a supposedly permanent ocean floor to moving coherent [tectonic plates](../../../../tectonic-plate.md) that include both continental and oceanic [lithosphere](../../../../lithosphere.md). Several independent observations made that change testable.

**Continental connections supplied the initial problem.** In the early twentieth century, [Alfred Wegener](../../../../alfred-wegener.md) assembled matching continental margins, correlatable rock belts and mountain chains, shared [fossil assemblages](../../../../fossil-assemblage.md), and [paleoclimate](../../../../paleoclimate.md) indicators into the case for [continental drift](../../../../continental-drift.md). Reassembling [Gondwana](../../../../gondwana.md), for example, makes geographically separated glacial deposits and striation directions compatible with one ice-sheet system. Fits at the continental shelf edge are physically more meaningful than exact fits to modern coastlines, which have been modified by erosion and sea-level change. The evidence suggested relative movement, but did not justify the proposed forces: continents cannot readily plough independently through strong oceanic rock. A convincing mechanism and independent motion measurements were missing.

**Oceanography changed the boundary conditions.** Echo-sounding surveys revealed a connected [spreading ridge](../../../../spreading-ridge.md) system, axial valleys, [subduction trenches](../../../../subduction-trench.md) and long [fracture zones](../../../../fracture-zone.md). [Seismic refraction surveys](../../../../seismic-refraction-survey.md) established systematic differences between continental and oceanic [crust](../../../../planetary-crust.md). Thin oceanic sediment cover, high ridge [heat flux](../../../../heat-flux-density.md) and the lack of very old exposed oceanic basement made permanent, ancient ocean basins increasingly implausible. [Harry Hammond Hess](../../../../harry-hammond-hess.md) proposed creation of oceanic [lithosphere](../../../../lithosphere.md) at ridges and recycling at trenches: [seafloor spreading](../../../../seafloor-spreading.md) without requiring an expanding [Earth](../../../../earth.md). Slow [mantle convection](../../../../mantle-convection.md) supplied a physically plausible connection between surface transport and internal heat loss. The mechanism need not have been completely quantified before its kinematic predictions could be tested.[Sections Ocean floor mapping, Seafloor spreading and recycling of oceanic crust](../../../../developing-the-theory-in-this-dynamic-earth.md)

**Magnetic records supplied a clock and a speedometer.** Dated continental lavas established a sequence of [geomagnetic reversals](../../../../geomagnetic-reversal.md). Marine [magnetometers](../../../../magnetometer.md) then found elongated, approximately paired [marine magnetic stripes](../../../../marine-magnetic-stripe.md) on either side of ridges. The 1963 interpretation by [F. J. Vine](../../../../f-j-vine.md) and [D. H. Matthews](../../../../d-h-matthews.md) combined repeated reversals with [seafloor spreading](../../../../seafloor-spreading.md): cooling [basalt](../../../../basalt.md) locks in [remanent magnetization](../../../../remanent-magnetization.md), and continuous motion converts a time sequence into a spatial sequence. If a polarity interval lasts $\Delta t$ and occupies width $w$ on one ridge flank, its [half-spreading rate](../../../../half-spreading-rate.md) is $w/\Delta t$. Matching several reversals tests the predicted sequence, rather than merely fitting a single stripe.[Nature 199, pp. 947–949, magnetic striping and the spreading interpretation](../../../../magnetic-anomalies-over-oceanic-ridges-by-f-j-vine-and-d-h-matthews.md) Different [apparent polar wander paths](../../../../apparent-polar-wander-path.md) for different continents also became mutually consistent after continental reconstruction, favoring relative continental motion over independent magnetic-pole histories.

**Earthquakes identified creation, translation and consumption.** Improved global [seismology](../../../../seismology.md) located shallow [earthquakes](../../../../earthquake.md) along ridges and transforms, and dipping [Wadati-Benioff zones](../../../../wadati-benioff-zone.md) beneath trenches. Their progressive focal depths identified cold, coherent [slabs](../../../../slab.md) entering the [mantle](../../../../planetary-mantle.md). [Focal mechanisms](../../../../focal-mechanism.md) showed extension at spreading boundaries, tangential slip at transforms and convergence at trenches. In 1965, [John Tuzo Wilson](../../../../john-tuzo-wilson.md)'s [transform fault](../../../../transform-fault.md) interpretation made a particularly discriminating prediction: active slip occurs between offset ridge segments, with a sense that can be opposite to that inferred by treating the entire lineament as an ordinary offset fault. The outer [fracture zones](../../../../fracture-zone.md) are inherited traces, not continuing active plate boundaries. Agreement of [focal mechanisms](../../../../focal-mechanism.md) with the transform prediction linked magnetic and seismic evidence.

**The late-1960s synthesis made these observations one quantitative theory.** Relative motion of rigid [tectonic plates](../../../../tectonic-plate.md) on a [sphere](../../../../sphere.md) can be described by an [Euler angular velocity vector](../../../../euler-angular-velocity-vector.md), with $\mathbf v=\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r$. Spreading rates, transform azimuths and slip directions must fit the same rotation, and relative angular velocities must close around plate circuits. The resulting framework accounted for global boundary geometry and [earthquake](../../../../earthquake.md) mechanisms without moving continents separately from the seafloor. Deep-ocean drilling beginning in 1968 subsequently confirmed that basement and overlying sediments generally become older away from ridges; this was a powerful confirmation of an already emerging synthesis, not a prerequisite discovered decades earlier.[Sections Magnetic striping and polar reversals, Concentration of earthquakes, and discussion of the 1968 drilling programme](../../../../developing-the-theory-in-this-dynamic-earth.md)

Thus **[plate tectonics](../../../../plate-tectonics.md) succeeded through agreement between geological reconstruction, magnetic chronology, ocean-floor structure, [seismology](../../../../seismology.md) and rigid-plate kinematics**. Modern satellite [geodesy](../../../../geodesy.md) verifies motions directly, but belongs to the later confirmation of the theory, not its original 1960s evidence.
