↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Yes: substantial along-strike segmentation is expected and observed in the [Andes](../../../../andes.md), but its geometry and dominant controls need not match [Cascadia](../../../../cascadia-subduction-zone.md).** The older [Nazca Plate](../../../../nazca-plate.md) has a thicker cooled [lithosphere](../../../../lithosphere.md) and a larger thermal density excess relative to hot mantle than the young Juan de Fuca system. In a [half-space cooling model](../../../../half-space-cooling-model.md), thermal thickness scales as $\sqrt{\kappa t}$, so the age comparison alone gives a thickness ratio $\sqrt{50/5}=\sqrt{10}\simeq3.16$. This changes the balance of [slab buoyancy](../../../../slab-buoyancy.md), bending resistance and [mantle flow](../../../../mantle-flow.md); it does not make the margin laterally uniform.

Changes in incoming ridge and plateau buoyancy, hydration, [fracture zones](../../../../fracture-zone.md), convergence obliquity, upper-plate thickness and motion can all alter [slab dip](../../../../slab-dip.md) and coupling. The Peruvian and Pampean flat-slab regions illustrate this: long nearly horizontal slab sections contrast with steeper subduction beneath active arc sections. Suppression or displacement of a hot [mantle wedge](../../../../mantle-wedge.md) explains why [flat-slab subduction](../../../../flat-slab-subduction.md) can coincide with volcanic gaps and deformation farther inland. The steeply dipping slab beneath much of the central [Andes](../../../../andes.md) should not be conflated with every Andean segment.

The [Juan Fernández Ridge](../../../../juan-fernandez-ridge.md) is associated with the Chilean/Pampean transition, while the [Nazca Ridge](../../../../nazca-ridge.md) intersects the Peruvian system. However, association is not proof that crustal thickness alone supplies all the buoyancy. Wide-angle seismic observations at the Juan Fernández system found only moderate crustal thickening and motivated consideration of hydration as well as ridge emplacement.[Abstract and introduction, seismic profiles, flat-slab association and limits of a crustal-buoyancy explanation](../../../../seismic-investigations-of-the-o-higgins-seamount-group-and-juan-fernandez-ridge-by-heidrun-kopp-and-others.md) The same combination of slab imaging, geodesy, volcanic chronology and incoming-plate characterization is needed to test causes. Thus **the transferable idea is lateral variation in forces and material properties, not an identical three-segment pattern or one age-controlled mechanism**.
