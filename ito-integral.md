<h1 id="ito-integral">Itô integral</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md)

The Itô integral $\int_0^tH_s\,dW_s$ integrates a predictable square-integrable process against [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). It is defined first for predictable step processes and then completed using the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md).
