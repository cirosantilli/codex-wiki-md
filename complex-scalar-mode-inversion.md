# Complex scalar mode inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical quantization of a complex scalar field](canonical-quantization-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md)

With the invariant measure $d\Pi_p=d^3p/[(2\pi)^3 2E_p]$, a free [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) has particle and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) modes. At any fixed time the displayed inverse and $b^\dagger(p)=\int d^3x\,e^{-ip\cdot x}(E_p\phi-i\dot\phi)$ project onto its positive- and negative-frequency components. The equal-time [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) then give $[a(p),a^\dagger(q)]=[b(p),b^\dagger(q)]=(2\pi)^3 2E_p\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$; cross terms vanish because their [coefficient](coefficient.md) contains $E_p-E_q$ on the [momentum](momentum.md) delta function.
