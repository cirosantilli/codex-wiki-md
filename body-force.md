# Body force

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_force)

A [body force](body-force.md) acts throughout a body's or [fluid](fluid.md)'s [volume](volume.md), rather than only through its surface. [Gravity](gravity.md) is the example used in [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md): a [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) $\mathbf g$ gives [force](force-split.md) per unit [volume](volume.md) $\rho\mathbf g$, where $\rho$ is the [mass density](density.md). More generally, a [body force](body-force.md) per unit [mass](mass.md) $\mathbf f$ contributes $\rho\mathbf f$ to [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md). [Forces](force-split.md) from [electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md) are another example. Surface [pressure](pressure-split.md) [forces](force-split.md) produce [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) on an immersed body, while its [body force](body-force.md) from [gravity](gravity.md) acts downward.
