# Observational conditioning

↑ **Parent:** [Classical conditioning](classical-conditioning.md)

Acquisition of a response through observing another individual's reaction to a stimulus. The recipient's later behavior can be tested without the demonstrator present, distinguishing [learning](learning.md) from an immediate reaction to the demonstrator.
