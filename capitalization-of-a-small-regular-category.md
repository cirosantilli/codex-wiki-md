# Capitalization of a small regular category

↑ **Parent:** [Capital regular category](capital-regular-category.md)

Choose one [regular functor](regular-functor.md) witnessing the [regular-logic separation of a proper subobject](regular-logic-separation-of-a-proper-subobject.md) for each proper [subobject](subobject.md) represented by a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) of the small [regular category](regular-category.md). Their product is regular and reflects invertibility of monomorphisms. If an arbitrary [morphism](morphism.md) becomes invertible, its image inclusion becomes invertible and so does the diagonal of its [kernel pair](kernel-pair.md). The original [morphism](morphism.md) is both a [regular epimorphism](regular-epimorphism.md) and a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md), hence invertible. The target is a [power of sets as a capital regular category](power-of-sets-as-a-capital-regular-category.md). This proves an isomorphism-reflecting regular representation without asserting full faithfulness.
