# Striatum

↑ **Parent:** [Brain](brain.md)

A major input structure of the basal ganglia, with cortical inputs and dopaminergic modulation. Distinct circuits contribute to action selection and learning; a functional [actor-critic](actor-critic-architecture.md) analogy does not establish a universal anatomical partition into one actor and one critic.
