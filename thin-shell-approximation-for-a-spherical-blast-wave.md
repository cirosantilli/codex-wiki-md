# Thin-shell approximation for a spherical blast wave

↑ **Parent:** [Self-similar blast wave](self-similar-blast-wave.md)

Let a strong [shock wave](shock-wave.md) of radius $R$ and speed $V$ sweep a uniform [mass density](density.md) $\rho_0$ into a shell of mass $4\pi\rho_0R^3/3$ and speed $2V/(\gamma+1)$. If the cavity [pressure](pressure.md) is $\alpha$ times the immediate post-shock [pressure](pressure.md), shell [momentum](momentum.md) balance gives $R\dot V=3(\alpha-1)V^2$. Constant total [energy](energy.md) gives $R^3V^2=\mathrm{constant}$ and hence $\alpha=1/2$. Including shell internal and kinetic [energy](energy.md), and treating the cavity volume as $4\pi R^3/3$ to thin-shell accuracy, yields $R=\xi(E/\rho_0)^{1/5}t^{2/5}$ with $\xi^5=75(\gamma-1)(\gamma+1)^2/[16\pi(5\gamma-3)]$. This coefficient is approximate; resolving the shell's finite volume changes it.
