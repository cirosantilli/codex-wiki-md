# Complex Watson lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Watson's lemma](watson-s-lemma.md)

Under the corresponding holomorphic and uniform remainder hypotheses, the expansion in [Watson lemma](watson-s-lemma.md) remains valid for a complex large parameter $z$, uniformly in every closed subsector

$$
|\arg z|\leq\frac\pi2-\delta
$$

with $0<\delta<\pi/2$. A rotation of an endpoint contour reduces oscillatory factors to this decaying Laplace form.
