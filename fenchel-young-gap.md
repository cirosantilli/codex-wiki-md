<h1 id="fenchel-young-gap">Fenchel–Young gap</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fenchel–Young inequality](fenchel-young-inequality.md)

For a [proper convex function](proper-convex-function.md) $\Phi$ and its [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md) $\Phi^*$, this gap is nonnegative by the [Fenchel–Young inequality](fenchel-young-inequality.md). It vanishes exactly when $y\in\partial\Phi(x)$. Its integral against a [transport plan](transport-plan.md) is the difference between the half-squared-distance cost and the value of the integrable [Kantorovich potentials](kantorovich-potential.md) $u=|x|^2/2-\Phi$, $v=|y|^2/2-\Phi^*$.
