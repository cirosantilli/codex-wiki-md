# Biological homology

↑ **Parent:** [Evolution](evolution.md)

Correspondence of structures or other characters through shared evolutionary ancestry. Shared function alone does not establish it; comparative position, [biological development](biological-development.md) and [phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md) help distinguish it from [convergent evolution](convergent-evolution.md).
