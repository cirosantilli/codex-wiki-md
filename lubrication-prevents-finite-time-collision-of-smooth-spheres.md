# Lubrication prevents finite-time collision of smooth spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication resistance](lubrication-resistance.md)

For smooth no-slip [spheres](sphere.md) of radius scale $a$ with minimum gap $\delta\ll a$, the lateral gap scale is $(a\delta)^{1/2}$. Squeeze-film [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) gives [pressure](pressure.md) $p\sim\mu a(-\dot\delta)/\delta^2$ and the displayed normal resistance. Bounded forcing therefore gives $-\dot\delta=O(\delta)$, so the gap cannot vanish in finite time. The conclusion uses smooth surfaces, no slip and continuum creeping flow; it is not a contact model for rough particles.
