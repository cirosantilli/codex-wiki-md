# Complete one-loop connected cubic three-point graphs

↑ **Parent:** [Connected Feynman diagram](connected-feynman-diagram.md)

For a real [phi cubed theory](phi-cubed-theory.md) with labelled external points, the connected three-point function at third order in the [coupling constant](coupling-constant-physics.md) has seven labelled [one-loop Feynman diagrams](one-loop-feynman-diagram.md): one triangle, three external-branch bubble insertions, and three tadpole-ended chains. The [symmetry factors](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) are respectively $1,2,2$, with graph weight $1/S$. To prove completeness, use $3V=2I+3$ and $L=I-V+1$: $V=3$ gives $I=3$ and $L=1$. The unique internal cycle is then a triangle, a pair of parallel edges, or a self-loop; the degree-three condition fixes the external attachments. Only the triangle remains [one-particle irreducible](one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md) after [amputation of external propagators](amputation-of-external-propagators.md). A tadpole-free prescription removes the three chains but must be stated separately.
