# Branching rule

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branching_rule)

A branching rule decomposes a representation into irreducible representations after restriction from a Lie group or Lie algebra to a subgroup or subalgebra.

A branching rule gives the [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) decomposition after restricting a [group representation](group-representation.md) to a [subgroup](subgroup.md), or a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) to a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md). For the standard block embedding $SU(5)\subset SU(6)$, $\mathbf6\downarrow=\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf1$ and $\Lambda^2\mathbf6\downarrow=\mathbf{10}\oplus\mathbf5$.
