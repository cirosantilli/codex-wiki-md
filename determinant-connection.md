# Determinant connection

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

A [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) induces a connection on its top [exterior power](exterior-power.md) by differentiating each factor in turn. Its local [connection one-form](connection-one-form.md) is $\operatorname{Tr}A$ and its [curvature form of a connection](curvature-form.md) is $\operatorname{Tr}\Theta_D$.
