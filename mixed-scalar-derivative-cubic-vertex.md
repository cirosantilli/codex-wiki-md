# Mixed scalar derivative cubic vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-field cubic and quartic Feynman vertices](scalar-field-cubic-and-quartic-feynman-vertices.md)

For two [real scalar fields](real-scalar-field.md) with the displayed [field interaction term](field-interaction-term.md), Fourier factors $e^{-ip\cdot x}$ make each derivative contribute $-ip_\mu$. The two identical differentiated field slots cancel $1/2$, giving the [Feynman vertex](interaction-vertex.md) $-ig\,p\cdot q$ when $p,q$ are the incoming [four-momenta](four-momentum.md) of the two $\phi$ legs. With incoming $\Phi$ [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) $k=-p-q$, this is also $-ig(k^2-p^2-q^2)/2$. This identity is valid off shell; substituting squared [masses](mass.md) for internal squared [four-momenta](four-momentum.md) is generally invalid.
