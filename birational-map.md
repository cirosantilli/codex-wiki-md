# Birational map

↑ **Parent:** [Birational variety](birational-variety.md)

A [rational map](rational-map-complex-analysis.md) with a rational inverse restricts to an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) of dense open subsets. It may identify or omit exceptional points outside those subsets.
