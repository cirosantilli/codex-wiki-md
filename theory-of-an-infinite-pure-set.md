# Theory of an infinite pure set

↑ **Parent:** [Complete theory](complete-theory.md)

In the [first-order language](first-order-language.md) of [logical equality](logical-equality.md), assert that there are at least $n$ elements for every positive [integer](integer-split.md) $n$. Eliminating one existential [logical variable](logical-variable.md) either substitutes a [logical equality](logical-equality.md) or avoids finitely many points, so this [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) has [quantifier elimination](quantifier-elimination.md) and is complete. It is categorical in every infinite cardinal. Its complete $n$-types are the [logical equality](logical-equality.md) partitions of $n$ coordinates and are counted by the [Bell numbers](bell-number.md).
