# First-to-zero-sound crossover

↑ **Parent:** [Zero sound](zero-sound.md)

Angular-relaxation collision time $\tau$ distinguishes [first sound](first-sound.md) ($\omega\tau\ll1$) from [zero sound](zero-sound.md) ($\omega\tau\gg1$). Collisions establish local [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) in the former.[Sections 2–3, finite-temperature kinetic equation and absorption](oscillations-in-a-fermi-liquid-by-lev-davidovich-landau.md)

For the figure's stipulated law $\omega\tau=(T/T_\times)^{-2}$, cooling from $T=5T_\times$ to $T=T_\times/5$ changes $\omega\tau$ from $0.04$ to $25$, without changing the driving [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). The crossover temperature is defined by $\omega\tau(T_\times)=1$. Calling [zero sound](zero-sound.md) a high-frequency mode therefore means high relative to the collision rate $1/\tau$, while its excitation [energy](energy-split.md) must still remain within the low-energy domain of the [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md).
