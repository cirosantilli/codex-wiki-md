# Linear charge density

↑ **Parent:** [Charge density](charge-density.md)

Linear charge density is [electric charge](electric-charge.md) divided by length along a charged line, or locally $dQ/d\ell$. Its SI unit is coulombs per metre. For a uniformly charged long cylindrical conductor, it equals the total [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) integrated around the cylinder's circumference; it need not represent a charge literally supported on a one-dimensional line.
