# Gravitational field of an infinite uniform cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

A circular infinite cylinder of radius $b$ and uniform [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ has [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\lambda=\pi b^2\rho$. A coaxial [Gaussian surface](gaussian-surface.md) of length $L$ has inward radial [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) and no end-cap [flux](flux.md). [Gauss's law for gravity](gauss-s-law-for-gravity.md) gives $2\pi rL g_r=-4\pi G\lambda L$ outside, hence the displayed result. Inside, enclosed [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) is $\pi r^2\rho$, so $g_r=-2\pi G\rho r$. An exterior [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) has [speed](speed.md) $\sqrt{2G\lambda}$ independent of radius. The exterior formula presupposes circular axial symmetry; area alone would not specify the near field of a noncircular rod.
