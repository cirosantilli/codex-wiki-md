# Shortest closed geodesic on the three-punctured sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md)

In curvature $-1$, its length is $2\operatorname{arcosh}3$. The level-two [principal congruence subgroup](principal-congruence-subgroup.md) has traces congruent to two modulo four, so the smallest hyperbolic absolute [trace](matrix-trace.md) is six, achieved by $\begin{pmatrix}5&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$. The [hyperbolic translation length](hyperbolic-translation-length.md) formula gives the result; the shortest closed [geodesic](geodesic.md) is nonsimple.
