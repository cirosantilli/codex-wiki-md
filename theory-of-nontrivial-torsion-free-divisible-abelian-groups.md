# Theory of nontrivial torsion-free divisible Abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Model theory](model-theory.md)

This [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) has as models the nonzero [torsion-free divisible Abelian groups](torsion-free-divisible-abelian-group.md). Rational division equips them with the structure of [vector spaces over the rational numbers](vector-space-over-the-rational-numbers.md). A [rational divisible hull](rational-divisible-hull.md) gives an [algebraically prime extension](algebraically-prime-extension.md) of each nonzero torsion-free base, while the zero base uses a one-dimensional rational space.
