# Geometric formula

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric logic](geometric-logic.md)

A [geometric formula](geometric-formula.md) is built from atoms by finite conjunction, arbitrary small disjunction and existential quantification. Empty conjunction and disjunction give truth and falsity. In a [Grothendieck topos](grothendieck-topos.md), these constructions use [finite limits](finite-limit.md), unions and images. Formula contexts have finitely many variables.
