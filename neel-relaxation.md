<h1 id="neel-relaxation">Néel relaxation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Superparamagnetism](superparamagnetism.md)

Thermally activated reorientation of a particle's internal magnetic moment across an anisotropy barrier. It differs from rotating the whole particle in a fluid. Its timescale can determine a measurement-dependent [magnetic blocking temperature](magnetic-blocking-temperature.md).
