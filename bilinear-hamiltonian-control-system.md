# Bilinear Hamiltonian control system

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum controllability](quantum-controllability.md)

A finite-dimensional closed [quantum control](quantum-control.md) system with fixed drift and control [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md), real [control inputs](control-input.md), and dynamics linear in the state at fixed controls and affine in the controls at fixed state. Its [Dynamical Lie algebra](dynamical-lie-algebra.md) is generated over the reals by $iH_0,iH_1,\ldots$. Reachability depends on the admissible controls, allowed duration and phase convention.
