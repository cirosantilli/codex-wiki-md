# Proportional counter

↑ **Parent:** [Scientific instrument](scientific-instrument.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proportional_counter)

A gas-filled [particle detector](particle-detector.md) using [electron avalanche](electron-avalanche.md) multiplication. Within its proportional operating range, the collected [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) tracks the initial [ionization](ionization.md), allowing more than a yes-or-no count.
