# Ground resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Resolution](resolution.md)

The use of [resolution](resolution.md) on [ground clauses](ground-clause.md). A finite refutation ends in the empty [ground clause](ground-clause.md) and establishes unsatisfiability of the chosen ground instances. [Herbrand's theorem](herbrand-s-theorem.md) explains the completeness of searching for such refutations for universal equality-free [first-order clauses](first-order-clause.md).
