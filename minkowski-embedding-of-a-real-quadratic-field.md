# Minkowski embedding of a real quadratic field

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski's theorem](minkowski-s-theorem.md)

For a real quadratic field $K$ with embeddings $\sigma_1,\sigma_2$, the image

$$
\{(\sigma_1(\alpha),\sigma_2(\alpha)):\alpha\in\mathcal O_K\}
$$

is a lattice in $\mathbb R^2$ of covolume $\sqrt{|d_K|}$, and the product of the two coordinates is the field norm.
