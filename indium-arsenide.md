# Indium arsenide

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

Indium arsenide, InAs, is a III-V [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md). An InAs island in a larger-gap host can provide [quantum confinement](quantum-confinement.md) for [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md). The optical transition energy of a strained, confined island is not simply the bulk material's [band gap](band-gap.md).
