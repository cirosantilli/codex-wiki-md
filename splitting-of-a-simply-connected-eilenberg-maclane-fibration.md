<h1 id="splitting-of-a-simply-connected-eilenberg-maclane-fibration">Splitting of a simply connected Eilenberg–MacLane fibration</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md)

For a [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md) with this fiber over a simply connected CW base, the fiber identity class has only one possible nonzero outgoing differential in the [cohomological Serre spectral sequence](cohomological-serre-spectral-sequence.md), landing in $H^{n+1}(B;G)$. If that group vanishes, the class extends to the CW total space. Its representing map, paired with projection to $B$, is an isomorphism on fiber and base [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) and hence a [weak homotopy equivalence](weak-homotopy-equivalence.md) to the product.
