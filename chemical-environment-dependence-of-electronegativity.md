# Chemical environment dependence of electronegativity

↑ **Parent:** [Electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md)

A tabulated [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) is useful because it compresses many comparisons, but one number per [chemical element](chemical-element.md) suppresses chemical context. Changing the bonded partners, [bond length](bond-length.md) or [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md) changes the conditions under which a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) attracts [electron density](electron-density.md). The [bond-dissociation energy](bond-dissociation-energy.md) can change for reasons beyond [bond polarization](bond-polarization.md), so attributing every extra unit of [energy](energy-split.md) solely to an [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) difference is an approximation.[§2.2, opening paragraph: bond distance and atomic valence affect bond energy](energy-electronegativity-and-chemical-bonding-by-stepan-s-batsanov.md)

An [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) labels a formal allocation of [electrons](electron.md); it is not a measured [partial atomic charge](partial-atomic-charge.md). State-sensitive [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) models may give different values for different [oxidation states](oxidation-state.md) or [electron configurations](electron-configuration.md), while a simple [chemical element](chemical-element.md) table intentionally provides a common comparison convention. Likewise, replacing an isolated [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) by a site in an [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md) introduces a surrounding [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and many neighbours. Applying the same tabulated [electronegativity](electronegativity-split.md) without adjustment becomes an additional assumption.[§2.1, Table 1 and valence footnotes; §2.2, Table 2; §2.3, opening paragraph on cooperative effects in solids](energy-electronegativity-and-chemical-bonding-by-stepan-s-batsanov.md)

For the ground-state [Mulliken scale](mulliken-electronegativity.md), the state dependence is visible directly in the formula. Replacing $X$ by $X^+$ replaces $E(X^+)-E(X)$ with $E(X^{2+})-E(X^+)$ as the removal cost, and replaces $E(X)-E(X^-)$ with $E(X^+)-E(X)$ as the attachment release. These are different [ionization energies](ionization-energy.md) and [electron affinities](electron-affinity.md). This isolated-[ion](ion.md) arithmetic does not claim that a bonded [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) with the same formal [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) is an isolated [ion](ion.md). In a [molecule](molecule-split.md), the actual [electron density](electron-density.md) and [dipole evidence for bond polarization](dipole-evidence-for-bond-polarization.md) supply complementary information about that environment.
