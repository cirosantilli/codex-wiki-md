# Baldwin effect

↑ **Parent:** [Evolution](evolution.md)

An evolutionary role for acquired adjustments, including [learning](learning.md), in allowing persistence and changing the [natural selection](natural-selection.md) experienced by inherited variants. It does not require direct inheritance of learned contents.
