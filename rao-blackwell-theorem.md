# Rao-Blackwell theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Probability and statistics](probability-and-statistics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rao-Blackwell_theorem)

If $T$ is a [sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md) and $U$ is an estimator with finite variance, then $U^*=\mathbb E[U\mid T]$ has the same expectation and no greater variance. Indeed, the [tower property of conditional expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md) preserves the mean and the [law of total variance](law-of-total-variance.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Var}(U)
=\operatorname{Var}(U^*)+\mathbb E[\operatorname{Var}(U\mid T)].
$$
