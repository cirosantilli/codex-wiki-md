<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume $\lambda>0$ so that the [Higgs potential](../../../../../higgs-field-potential.md) is bounded below, and work at tree level in four spacetime dimensions with metric $(+---)$. For $\mu^2>0$, the minimum is $\Phi=0$. The quadratic terms describe **three massless gauge [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) and two complex scalars of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $\sqrt{\mu^2}$**, equivalently four real scalar degrees of freedom. These are the perturbative particle masses about this vacuum, without a claim about the nonperturbative asymptotic spectrum of an interacting non-Abelian theory.

Expanding the [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) with $T_j=\tau_j/2$ gives the interaction terms

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm int}=
\frac{ig}{2}W_j^\mu\big[(\partial_\mu\Phi^\dagger)\tau_j\Phi-\Phi^\dagger\tau_j\partial_\mu\Phi\big]
+\frac{g^2}{4}\Phi^\dagger\Phi\,W_j^\mu W_{j\mu}
-\lambda(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)^2.
$$

In the second term, the antisymmetric part of $\tau_j\tau_k$ vanishes against the symmetric gauge-field product. The corresponding [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) vertices have one vector and a scalar–antiscalar pair, two vectors and a scalar–antiscalar pair, or four scalar legs with two particles and two [antiparticles](../../../../../antiparticle.md). The first vertex carries a [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) difference from its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md). The Pauli matrix in it determines which doublet components couple; the two-vector scalar vertex is proportional to $\delta_{jk}$. There is no cubic scalar interaction about the zero-field vacuum.
