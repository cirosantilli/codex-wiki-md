<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [gauge fields](../../../../gauge-field.md) require their own [Yang-Mills Lagrangian](../../../../yang-mills-lagrangian.md), $-W^a_{\mu\nu}W^{a\mu\nu}/4$. The non-Abelian field strength contains a term quadratic in the gauge potentials, so squaring it produces **three-vector and four-vector self-interaction vertices**, with [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) factors in the cubic vertex and SU(2) structure constants in both. They exist in either scalar phase and must be added to the scalar-generated vertices. A gauge-fixed quantum calculation also has gauge-fixing and, where applicable, ghost terms; those are not extra physical [boson](../../../../boson.md) species.

For $\mu^2>0$, the global phase transformation $\Phi\mapsto e^{i\alpha}\Phi$ leaves both the scalar potential and the covariant [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) unchanged while leaving $W_j$ fixed. Its [Noether charge](../../../../noether-charge.md) counts scalar particles minus [antiparticles](../../../../antiparticle.md). Thus $\phi_a\phi_b\to\phi_a\phi_b$ and $\phi_a\phi_a^*\to W_3W_3$ are compatible with this charge, whereas $\phi_a\phi_b\to W_jW_k$ is forbidden: its initial charge is two and final charge zero. Charge conservation is necessary, not sufficient, for a particular amplitude to be nonzero; other [quantum numbers](../../../../quantum-number.md) and kinematics still apply.

After adding the [hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) field, the neutral-vector [mass](../../../../mass.md) term becomes

$$
\frac{v^2}{8}(gW_3-g'B)^2.
$$

Its [mass](../../../../mass.md) matrix has zero determinant. With $G=\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}$, the orthonormal combinations are

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{g'W_3+gB}{G},\quad m_A=0;\qquad
Z=\frac{gW_3-g'B}{G},\quad m_Z=Gv/2.}
$$

The unbroken generator is [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md), $Q=T_3+Y/2$, because the lower doublet component has $T_3=-1/2$ and $Y=1$. The charged combinations $W^\pm=(W_1\mp iW_2)/\sqrt2$ keep [mass](../../../../mass.md) $gv/2$.

The observed charged [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md) couples left-chiral [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) within SU(2) doublets. In the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md), $Q_L=(u_L,d_L)$ and $L_L=(\nu_L,e_L)$ are doublets, while $u_R,d_R,e_R$ are singlets. A vectorlike assignment would also give right-handed charged weak currents, contrary to this observed chiral structure. Once the measured [electric charges](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) are specified, $Y=2(Q-T_3)$ fixes

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
\text{multiplet}&Q_L&u_R&d_R&L_L&e_R&\Phi\\\hline
Y&1/3&4/3&-2/3&-1&-2&1
\end{array}.
$$

An optional [right-handed neutrino](../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md) is a singlet with $Y=0$. Conjugate left-chiral fields have the opposite [hypercharges](../../../../hypercharge.md) to these right-chiral fields. These assignments permit gauge-invariant [Yukawa interactions](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md); they are not obtained by assigning one common [hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) to every [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md).
