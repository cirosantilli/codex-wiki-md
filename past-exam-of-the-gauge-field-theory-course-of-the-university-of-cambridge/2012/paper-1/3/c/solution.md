<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [SU(5) grand unified theory](../../../../../su-5-grand-unified-theory.md) embeds the strong and electroweak groups in a single simple gauge group. Its 24 gauge generators contain the eight [gluons](../../../../../gluon.md), three weak generators and one [hypercharge](../../../../../hypercharge.md) generator, plus twelve additional generators connecting color and weak directions. A common [gauge coupling](../../../../../gauge-coupling.md) applies at the unification scale with the properly normalized [hypercharge](../../../../../hypercharge.md) generator; low-energy couplings differ through their [renormalization group](../../../../../renormalization-group.md) evolution.

Using left-chiral fields throughout, one [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) generation fits into

$$
\overline{\mathbf5}=(d_R^c,L_L),\qquad
\mathbf{10}=(Q_L,u_R^c,e_R^c).
$$

The dimensions are $3+2=5$ and $6+3+1=10$, so no [right-handed neutrino](../../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md) is required in these multiplets. A singlet neutrino can be added. A suitable adjoint scalar vacuum breaks SU(5) to the [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) subgroup; a fundamental scalar contains an electroweak doublet and a colored triplet. The extra heavy vector [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) can mediate transitions between [quarks](../../../../../quark.md) and [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md), hence [proton decay](../../../../../proton-decay.md) in the minimal model. This is a proposed unification, not an experimentally established identification of new particles.

The [hierarchy problem](../../../../../hierarchy-problem.md) concerns keeping an elementary weak-scale scalar light in the presence of much heavier physical scales. A heavy state coupled to it generically induces a threshold correction of order $\delta m_H^2\sim cM^2/(16\pi^2)$, with $c$ a product of couplings. Without protection, a weak-scale result then needs cancellation against a much larger parameter. In SU(5), this is sharpened by [doublet-triplet splitting](../../../../../doublet-triplet-splitting.md): the electroweak doublet must stay light while its colored partner must be sufficiently heavy to avoid rapid [proton decay](../../../../../proton-decay.md). Choosing a small tree-level doublet [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is not by itself a mechanism protecting it from subsequent heavy-particle corrections. [Supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) and other proposals change this protection problem, but the minimal SU(5) embedding alone does not solve it.
