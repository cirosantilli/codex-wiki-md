<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A bare [Dirac mass](../../../../../dirac-mass-term.md) couples left and right [chirality](../../../../../chirality-physics.md), so the distinct electroweak representations prevent writing it as a gauge-invariant term before symmetry breaking. Instead the [Yukawa interactions](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) are

$$
\mathcal L_Y=-\bar Q_LY_dd_R\Phi-\bar Q_LY_uu_R\widetilde\Phi
-\bar L_LY_ee_R\Phi+\text{Hermitian conjugate},\qquad
\widetilde\Phi=i\tau_2\Phi^*.
$$

Weak indices are contracted to singlets. Substituting the vacuum value gives [mass](../../../../../mass.md) matrices $M_f=Y_fv/\sqrt2$ and a physical Higgs coupling $-\bar f_LM_ff_R\sigma/v$ plus its conjugate. Biunitary diagonalization defines [mass](../../../../../mass.md) eigenstates; a mismatch between the up- and down-quark left rotations appears in the [CKM matrix](../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md). Gauge-boson masses arise from the Higgs [kinetic term](../../../../../kinetic-term.md), whereas these [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) masses arise from the Yukawa term.

With only the minimal field content there is no [right-handed neutrino](../../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md), so no renormalizable neutrino Yukawa term. Adding a gauge-singlet $N_R$ permits both a Dirac scale $m_D=y_\nu v/\sqrt2$ and a Majorana scale $M_R$. For one family the symmetric [mass](../../../../../mass.md) matrix in a left-chiral basis is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}0&m_D\\m_D&M_R\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its signed [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $(M_R\pm\sqrt{M_R^2+4m_D^2})/2$. Rephasing gives positive physical masses, and for $|M_R|\gg|m_D|$ the [seesaw mechanism](../../../../../seesaw-mechanism.md) yields

$$
\boxed{m_{\rm light}\simeq |m_D|^2/|M_R|,\qquad m_{\rm heavy}\simeq |M_R|.}
$$

For $m_D\sim100\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $M_R\sim10^{14}\,\mathrm{GeV}$, the light scale is about $0.1\,\mathrm{eV}$. Integrating out the heavy state generates the dimension-five [Weinberg operator](../../../../../weinberg-operator.md), whose Higgs vacuum insertion gives the same suppression. Alternatively a Dirac neutrino can have a very small [Yukawa coupling](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) with [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) number conserved. A tiny observed neutrino [mass](../../../../../mass.md) does not by itself distinguish these mechanisms.
