<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Chirality](../../../../../chirality-physics.md) distinguishes the two inequivalent [Weyl spinor](../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) representations of the proper [Lorentz group](../../../../../lorentz-group.md). For a [Dirac spinor](../../../../../dirac-spinor.md), define $\gamma^5=i\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3$ and the [chiral projectors](../../../../../chiral-projector.md)

$$
P_L=\frac{1-\gamma^5}{2},\qquad P_R=\frac{1+\gamma^5}{2},\qquad\psi=\psi_L+\psi_R.
$$

They satisfy $P_LP_R=0$ and $P_L+P_R=I$. Proper [Lorentz transformations](../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) preserve each chiral representation, whereas [parity](../../../../../parity.md) exchanges them. [Helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) instead measures the projection of [spin angular momentum](../../../../../spin.md) along [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md). For a massive particle it can change under a boost that overtakes the particle, so it must not be identified with [chirality](../../../../../chirality-physics.md). For massless positive-energy particles the left-chiral field annihilates negative-helicity particles and creates positive-helicity antiparticles.

The [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) is a [chiral gauge theory](../../../../../chiral-gauge-theory.md): left- and right-chiral fields have different [gauge group representations](../../../../../gauge-group-representation.md). In the convention $Q=T_3+Y$, one generation contains

$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{field}&SU(3)_c\times SU(2)_L\text{ representation and }Y\\\hline
Q_L=(u_L,d_L)^T&(\mathbf3,\mathbf2)_{1/6}\\
u_R&(\mathbf3,\mathbf1)_{2/3}\\
d_R&(\mathbf3,\mathbf1)_{-1/3}\\
L_L=(\nu_L,e_L)^T&(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{-1/2}\\
e_R&(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_{-1}
\end{array}
$$

The minimal field content has no [right-handed neutrino](../../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md). A sterile right-handed field is a possible extension, not a required member of that minimal list. The table displays [weak isospin](../../../../../weak-isospin.md), [hypercharge](../../../../../hypercharge.md) and [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) assignments, not assignments of massive-particle helicity.

The [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) contains $\bar\nu\gamma^\mu P_Le\,W_\mu^+$ and its [Hermitian conjugate](../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md), with coupling $g/\sqrt2$. Only the left-chiral lepton doublet participates, giving [parity violation](../../../../../parity-violation.md). Both chiralities can couple to the [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md), but their coefficients differ:

$$
\mathcal L_Z=-\frac g{\cos\theta_W}Z_\mu\bar f\gamma^\mu\left[(T_3-Q\sin^2\theta_W)P_L-Q\sin^2\theta_WP_R\right]f.
$$

Here $T_3$ is that of the left-chiral component. For the electron, it is $-1/2$. The [photon](../../../../../photon.md) and [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) couplings of a charged massive [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) are vectorlike; this does not make the full [electroweak interaction](../../../../../electroweak-interaction.md) vectorlike.

A bare [Dirac mass term](../../../../../dirac-mass-term.md) $m\bar\psi\psi=m(\bar\psi_L\psi_R+\bar\psi_R\psi_L)$ joins the two chiral representations. Their different electroweak quantum numbers forbid that term before [electroweak symmetry breaking](../../../../../electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md). A [Yukawa interaction](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md), such as $-y_e\bar L_LHe_R+\mathrm{h.c.}$, is gauge invariant; the [Higgs doublet](../../../../../higgs-field.md) expectation value then gives $m_e=y_ev/\sqrt2$. Up-type quarks use the conjugate doublet. Thus [chirality](../../../../../chirality-physics.md) explains the weak charge assignments, their [parity violation](../../../../../parity-violation.md), and why masses arise through the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md). The consistency of these chiral assignments also requires [Standard Model anomaly cancellation](../../../../../standard-model-anomaly-cancellation.md).
