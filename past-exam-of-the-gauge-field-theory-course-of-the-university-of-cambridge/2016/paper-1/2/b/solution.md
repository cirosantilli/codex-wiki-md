[Gauge boson self-interactions](../../../../../gauge-boson-self-interaction.md) follow from noncommuting generators, rather than a separate assumption that the force carriers should attract one another. With $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+igA_\mu^aT^a$ and $[T^a,T^b]=if^{abc}T^c$, the [Yang-Mills field strength](../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md) is

$$
F_{\mu\nu}^a=\partial_\mu A_\nu^a-\partial_\nu A_\mu^a-gf^{abc}A_\mu^bA_\nu^c.
$$

The last term is required so that $F_{\mu\nu}$ transforms covariantly under the [gauge group](../../../../../gauge-group.md). Let $G_{\mu\nu}^a=\partial_\mu A_\nu^a-\partial_\nu A_\mu^a$. Expanding $\mathcal L=-F_{\mu\nu}^aF^{a\mu\nu}/4$ gives a quadratic kinetic term and the [cubic and quartic Yang-Mills self-interactions](../../../../../cubic-and-quartic-yang-mills-self-interactions.md)

$$
\mathcal L_3=gf^{abc}(\partial_\mu A_\nu^a)A^{b\mu}A^{c\nu},\qquad
\mathcal L_4=-\frac{g^2}{4}f^{abc}f^{ade}A_\mu^bA_\nu^cA^{d\mu}A^{e\nu}.
$$

The [structure constants](../../../../../structure-constant.md) and one [gauge coupling](../../../../../gauge-coupling.md) fix both types of vertex. For an Abelian group the structure constants vanish: the renormalizable pure [electromagnetic field](../../../../../electromagnetic-field.md) action has no elementary three- or four-[photon](../../../../../photon.md) vertex. Photon scattering through charged-particle loops is a different statement, not a contradiction.

In [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md), the non-Abelian $SU(3)_c$ structure produces three- and four-[gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) vertices; the gluons themselves carry [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md). These vertices influence multi-jet production and the [renormalization group](../../../../../renormalization-group.md) running of the strong coupling. In the electroweak sector the $SU(2)_L$ fields mix with the Abelian field after [electroweak symmetry breaking](../../../../../electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md). The resulting charged-vector vertices include $W^+W^-\gamma$ and $W^+W^-Z$, with couplings $e$ and $g\cos\theta_W$, together with the related four-boson vertices. Neutral-only triple vertices such as $ZZ\gamma$ are absent at tree level in the [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md).

The [electroweak triple gauge coupling](../../../../../electroweak-triple-gauge-coupling.md) can be measured using $e^+e^-\to W^+W^-$. The neutrino exchange and neutral-vector exchange diagrams interfere: the gauge-related coefficients cancel the most rapidly growing longitudinal-polarization terms. Their relative strengths and angular dependence therefore test the theory more sharply than merely observing a pair of massive vector bosons. The electron-mass-suppressed Higgs contribution completes the corresponding mass-dependent cancellation. Adding arbitrary independent three- and four-vector couplings would generally destroy these relations.

At the [Large Electron–Positron Collider](../../../../../large-electron-positron-collider.md), the [L3 experiment](../../../../../l3-experiment.md) fitted charged triple-gauge parameters using hadronic and semileptonic W-pair events, complemented by single-W and single-photon channels. These measurements agreed with the predicted self-couplings. Their interpretation uses reconstructed decay products and differential distributions, rather than direct pictures of a three-boson vertex.[2004, abstract: W-pair channels at 189–209 GeV, complementary channels and agreement with Standard Model self-couplings](../../../../../measurement-of-triple-gauge-boson-couplings-of-the-w-boson-at-lep-by-l3-collaboration.md)
