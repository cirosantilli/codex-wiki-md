<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Loop corrections in [perturbative quantum field theory](../../../../../perturbative-quantum-field-theory-split.md) integrate over unbounded internal [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md). For example, after a [Wick rotation](../../../../../wick-rotation.md), a four-dimensional integral with large-momentum behavior

$$
\int^\Lambda\frac{d^4k_E}{(k_E^2+m^2)^2}
\sim\int^\Lambda\frac{dk_E}{k_E}
$$

has a logarithmic [ultraviolet divergence](../../../../../ultraviolet-divergence.md). This is sensitivity to short distances, distinct from a possible low-momentum [infrared divergence](../../../../../infrared-divergence.md).

[Regularization in quantum field theory](../../../../../regularization-in-quantum-field-theory.md) first makes the expression mathematically well defined, for example with a cutoff $\Lambda$ or [dimensional regularization](../../../../../dimensional-regularization.md) in $d=4-2\epsilon$. A regulator is an intermediate prescription rather than a measured physical parameter; a hard [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) cutoff can obscure gauge identities, so their preservation or restoration must be checked. Introduce bare fields and parameters, such as $\phi_0=Z_\phi^{1/2}\phi$, and split the regulated [Lagrangian density](../../../../../lagrangian-density.md) into a renormalized part and local [counterterms](../../../../../counterterm.md). For instance, [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md), [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and coupling counterterms cancel the corresponding divergent parts of Green functions order by order.

[Renormalization conditions](../../../../../renormalization-condition.md) fix finite parameters through specified observables or a subtraction prescription. Once these physical inputs are held fixed, predictions must not depend on the arbitrary regulator. In a [renormalizable quantum field theory](../../../../../renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md), a finite set of operator coefficients suffices to absorb ultraviolet divergences at every perturbative order, with the allowed structures constrained by the theory's [symmetries](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md). Gauge theories impose relations among counterterms through identities such as the [Ward-Takahashi identity](../../../../../ward-identity.md); one cannot choose every diagram's subtraction independently.

The remaining dependence on a chosen [renormalization scale](../../../../../renormalization-scale.md) describes [running couplings](../../../../../running-coupling.md) and is organized by the [renormalization group](../../../../../renormalization-group.md). An [effective field theory](../../../../../effective-field-theory.md) can instead include higher-dimension operators suppressed by a high scale. Infinitely many are allowed in principle, but only finitely many contribute at a fixed accuracy in the low-energy expansion. Renormalization is therefore a controlled relation between parameters and predictions at different scales, rather than an instruction to discard every infinity without fixing physical inputs.
