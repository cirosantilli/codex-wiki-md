<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The left- and right-chiral components of a charged [Standard Model fermion](../../../../../standard-model-fermion.md) transform in different electroweak [group representations](../../../../../group-representation.md). A bare [Dirac mass term](../../../../../dirac-mass-term.md) $-m\bar f_Lf_R+\mathrm{h.c.}$ is therefore not invariant under the full electroweak [gauge group](../../../../../gauge-group.md). The [Higgs doublet](../../../../../higgs-field.md) supplies the missing transformation properties. With $\widetilde H=i\sigma_2H^*$, the gauge-invariant [Yukawa interactions](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) are

$$
\mathcal L_Y=-\bar Q_LY_dHd_R-\bar Q_LY_u\widetilde H u_R
-\bar L_LY_eHe_R+\mathrm{h.c.},
$$

where each $Y_f$ is a flavor-space [Yukawa matrix](../../../../../yukawa-matrix.md). For example, hypercharges in the down-quark term add to $-1/6+1/2-1/3=0$, while those in the up-quark term add to $-1/6-1/2+2/3=0$. Thus the Higgs and conjugate Higgs are required in different terms.

After [electroweak symmetry breaking](../../../../../electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md), choose [unitary gauge](../../../../../unitary-gauge.md) with $H=(0,(v+h)/\sqrt2)^T$. The [fermion mass matrices](../../../../../fermion-mass-matrix.md) become

$$
\boxed{M_d=\frac{v}{\sqrt2}Y_d,\qquad M_u=\frac{v}{\sqrt2}Y_u,\qquad M_e=\frac{v}{\sqrt2}Y_e.}
$$

A [singular value decomposition](../../../../../singular-value-decomposition.md) $U_{fL}^\dagger M_fU_{fR}=\operatorname{diag}(m_{f,1},m_{f,2},m_{f,3})$ defines nonnegative physical tree-level masses. The mismatch $U_{uL}^\dagger U_{dL}$ is the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) in the [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md). The corresponding physical [Higgs boson](../../../../../higgs-boson.md) couples diagonally as $-(m_f/v)h\bar ff$ in the minimal one-doublet theory. This relates its coupling strength to each [mass](../../../../../mass.md), but the measured [Yukawa couplings](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) remain independent input parameters: the minimal theory does not predict their hierarchy.

The minimal renormalizable [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) contains no [right-handed neutrino](../../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md), so it has neither a neutrino Dirac Yukawa term nor a gauge-invariant renormalizable left-handed [Majorana mass term](../../../../../majorana-mass-term.md). Its [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) are massless. Observed [neutrino oscillations](../../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) require going beyond that minimal field content or operator set: adding right-handed singlets permits [Dirac masses](../../../../../dirac-mass-term.md), while the dimension-five [Weinberg operator](../../../../../weinberg-operator.md) gives a [Majorana neutrino](../../../../../majorana-neutrino.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md) of order $v^2/\Lambda$ after symmetry breaking. These alternatives should be distinguished from the charged-fermion Yukawa mechanism.
