<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For eigenvalues $(v,v,-2v)$, the first two entries coincide. Consequently the $12$ generators $t^1,t^2$, as well as $t^3,t^8$, commute with the vacuum. The $13$ and $23$ eigenvalue differences both have magnitude $3|v|$, giving

$$
\boxed{m_{1,2,3,8}=0,\qquad m_{4,5,6,7}=3g|v|.}
$$

There are four massless and four massive [gauge bosons](../../../../../gauge-boson.md). The [centralizer of a Hermitian matrix in SU(n)](../../../../../centralizer-of-a-hermitian-matrix-in-su-n.md) preserves the degenerate two-dimensional [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md), so the unbroken group is

$$
\boxed{H=S(U(2)\times U(1))\cong\frac{SU(2)\times U(1)}{\mathbb Z_2}\cong U(2).}
$$

At the [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) level this is $\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)$, generated by $t^{1,2,3}$ and $t^8$. The finite quotient specifies the actual subgroup inside [SU(3)](../../../../../su-3-group.md); simply writing $SU(2)\times U(1)$ suppresses that global identification. The four real massive fields combine into a complex [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md) doublet and its conjugate. Unbroken [SU(2)](../../../../../su-2-group.md) rotations relate its two components, explaining the equal masses of the $13$ and $23$ sectors in this [SU(3) gauge theory with an adjoint Higgs field](../../../../../su-3-gauge-theory-with-an-adjoint-higgs-field.md).
