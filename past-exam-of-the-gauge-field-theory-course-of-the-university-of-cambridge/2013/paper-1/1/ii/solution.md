<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the stronger limit $m_H\ll m_W$, both intermediate [W bosons](../../../../../w-boson.md) must be virtual. Their heavy propagators suppress the four-fermion [decay amplitude](../../../../../decay-amplitude.md), while the small available [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md) suppresses its rate further. At energies small compared with $m_W$, integrating out the two vectors gives a local interaction proportional to $G_FH J_\mu^+J^{-\mu}/v$. Its dimension-seven coefficient implies a rate of order $G_F^3m_H^7$ when all four final [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) can be treated as massless, with channel-dependent constants. This is a low-energy scaling statement, not the on-shell longitudinal-width formula. Other accessible [Higgs boson](../../../../../higgs-boson.md) decay channels, such as a pair of [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) or loop-induced two-[photon](../../../../../photon.md) decay, must then be compared using their actual masses and couplings.
