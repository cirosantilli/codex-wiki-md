↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Below $m_H=2m_W$, the on-shell two-body [decay channel](../../../../../decay-channel.md) is kinematically closed. An imaginary $p^*$ in the preceding formula is therefore a sign that this formula no longer applies. Slightly below threshold, the physical process can instead contain an on-shell [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) and an off-shell one, $H\to WW^*\to Wf\bar f'$, followed by decay of the remaining [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md). Its rate requires the virtual-vector [propagator](../../../../../propagator.md), its allowed fermionic decay channels and the corresponding multiparticle [Lorentz-invariant phase space](../../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md). The nonzero [decay width](../../../../../decay-width.md) of the [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) smooths the idealized threshold. A polarization-resolved rate for two on-shell longitudinal vectors must not be analytically continued and treated as that multiparticle rate.
