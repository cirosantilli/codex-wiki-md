The [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) uses the local [gauge group](../../../../gauge-group.md) $SU(3)_c\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$. Its [Higgs field](../../../../higgs-field.md) is a complex [Higgs doublet](../../../../higgs-field.md) of [hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) $Y=1/2$. A bare [mass](../../../../mass.md) term for a weak [gauge boson](../../../../gauge-boson.md), or for a charged [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) joining inequivalent left- and right-handed gauge representations, would not respect the unbroken electroweak construction. Instead introduce a gauge-invariant [Higgs potential](../../../../higgs-field-potential.md)

$$
V(\Phi)=-\mu^2\Phi^\dagger\Phi+\lambda(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)^2,\qquad\lambda>0,\quad\mu^2>0.
$$

Its minima satisfy $\Phi^\dagger\Phi=v^2/2$, $v^2=\mu^2/\lambda$. Choosing a representative $\langle\Phi\rangle=(0,v)^T/\sqrt2$ leaves $Q=T_3+Y$ unbroken. In this description [electroweak symmetry breaking](../../../../electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md) is $SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\to U(1)_{\rm em}$; the underlying local [gauge symmetry](../../../../gauge-invariance.md) remains a redundancy of the theory. A vacuum representative is not a physical explicit violation of gauge invariance.

In [unitary gauge](../../../../unitary-gauge.md), write $\Phi=(0,v+H)^T/\sqrt2$. The [gauge-covariant kinetic term](../../../../gauge-covariant-kinetic-term.md) $|D_\mu\Phi|^2$ produces

$$
m_W=\frac{gv}{2},\qquad m_Z=\frac{v}{2}\sqrt{g^2+g'^2},\qquad m_\gamma=0,
$$

with $\tan\theta_W=g'/g$ and $e=g\sin\theta_W$. Three angular scalar directions supply the [longitudinal gauge-boson polarizations](../../../../longitudinal-polarization-of-a-massive-vector-boson.md) of $W^+,W^-,Z$, while one real radial [Higgs boson](../../../../higgs-boson.md) survives with $m_H^2=2\lambda v^2$. Counting physical [degrees of freedom](../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) checks the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../higgs-mechanism.md): the electroweak gauge sector and scalar doublet have $4\times2+4=12$ before the rearrangement and $3\times3+2+1=12$ afterwards.

The [Higgs potential](../../../../higgs-field-potential.md) also fixes the scalar self-interactions:

$$
V=\text{constant}+\frac{m_H^2}{2}H^2+\frac{m_H^2}{2v}H^3+\frac{m_H^2}{8v^2}H^4.
$$

The color gauge group is unbroken, so [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) remain massless. The same electroweak kinetic term fixes

$$
\mathcal L\supset\frac{2m_W^2}{v}HW_\mu^+W^{-\mu}+\frac{m_Z^2}{v}HZ_\mu Z^\mu
+\frac{m_W^2}{v^2}H^2W_\mu^+W^{-\mu}+\frac{m_Z^2}{2v^2}H^2Z_\mu Z^\mu.
$$

Gauge-invariant [Yukawa couplings](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) such as $-y_d\bar Q_L\Phi d_R-y_u\bar Q_L\widetilde\Phi u_R+\mathrm{h.c.}$ give $m_f=y_fv/\sqrt2$ and the physical coupling $-m_fH\bar f f/v$. These relations predict stronger Higgs couplings to heavier massive particles; they do not determine the Yukawa parameters themselves. The minimal [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) does not supply observed neutrino masses. The surviving [Higgs boson](../../../../higgs-boson.md) is neutral and has [spin](../../../../spin.md) zero; its [mass](../../../../mass.md) and self-coupling are tied to the curvature of the [Higgs potential](../../../../higgs-field-potential.md), not fixed by the gauge couplings alone. Higgs exchange also provides the [Higgs cancellation in longitudinal vector scattering](../../../../higgs-cancellation-in-longitudinal-vector-scattering.md), maintaining the required high-energy behavior of weak amplitudes.

At a proton collider, important production mechanisms are [gluon-fusion Higgs production](../../../../gluon-fusion-higgs-production.md) through a heavy-quark loop, [vector-boson fusion](../../../../vector-boson-fusion.md) with two outgoing quark jets, associated $WH$ or $ZH$ production, and production with a [top quark](../../../../top-quark.md) pair. At an [electron](../../../../electron.md)–positron collider, [Higgsstrahlung](../../../../higgsstrahlung.md) $e^+e^-\to ZH$ is a clean example. Tree-level decays include [Higgs decay to a fermion pair](../../../../higgs-decay-to-a-fermion-pair.md) and, when allowed, [Higgs decay to two W bosons](../../../../higgs-decay-to-two-w-bosons.md) or [Higgs decay to two Z bosons](../../../../higgs-decay-to-two-z-bosons.md). Below an on-shell vector-pair threshold, one or both vectors can be virtual. The $gg$ and $\gamma\gamma$ channels arise through loops; neutral colorless $H$ has no corresponding direct tree-level gauge coupling. Rates and [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md) depend on both couplings and kinematics, so “heaviest available particle” is a guide rather than a complete rule.

The [Higgs boson discovery in 2012](../../../../higgs-boson-discovery-in-2012.md) supplied an experimental test. The [ATLAS experiment](../../../../atlas-experiment.md) and [CMS experiment](../../../../compact-muon-solenoid.md) reconstructed two-[photon](../../../../photon.md) and four-lepton [invariant masses](../../../../invariant-mass.md) in [Large Hadron Collider](../../../../large-hadron-collider.md) proton collisions. [Electromagnetic calorimeters](../../../../electromagnetic-calorimeter.md) measure [photon](../../../../photon.md) energies, while tracking and muon systems identify charged leptons; narrow reconstructed peaks can be distinguished from smooth backgrounds with control samples and a likelihood analysis. The ATLAS discovery paper reported a new resonance near $126.5\,\mathrm{GeV}$. An observed [mass](../../../../mass.md) peak supports the existence of a new state; production channels, decay rates and angular distributions test whether it has the particular Higgs couplings and quantum numbers.[abstract; Sections 2, 6–7 and 9](../../../../observation-of-a-new-particle-in-the-search-for-the-standard-model-higgs-boson-with-the-atlas-detector-at-the-lhc-by-atlas-collaboration.md)

Finally, the [hierarchy problem](../../../../hierarchy-problem.md) asks why the electroweak scalar [mass](../../../../mass.md) scale remains much smaller than a putative heavy physical scale without delicate cancellations. A cutoff estimate suggests corrections $\delta m_H^2\sim\Lambda^2/(16\pi^2)$ times couplings, but the quadratic cutoff term itself depends on the regularization convention. The physical concern persists when a heavy state of [mass](../../../../mass.md) $M$ couples to the [Higgs field](../../../../higgs-field.md) and produces threshold corrections of order its coupling times $M^2/(16\pi^2)$. Matching a much smaller Higgs [mass](../../../../mass.md) may then require cancellation between independent parameters. Supersymmetry, compositeness and other protective structures propose different resolutions; [renormalizability](../../../../renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md) of the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) does not by itself resolve this sensitivity or establish that new particles must occur at one particular [mass](../../../../mass.md).
