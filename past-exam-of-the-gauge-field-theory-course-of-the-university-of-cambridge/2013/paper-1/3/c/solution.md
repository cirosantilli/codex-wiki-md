<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The operator $\phi^\dagger\phi\bar\psi\psi$ has [mass dimension](../../../../../mass-dimension.md) $2+3=5$, giving $[g]=-1$. It is **not power-counting renormalizable** as a fundamental interaction treated to arbitrary [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). It is a perfectly usable dimension-five [effective operator](../../../../../effective-operator.md) below its suppression scale. Successive orders in that [effective field theory](../../../../../effective-field-theory.md) require a derivative and operator expansion rather than one finite set of parameters valid at every order.
