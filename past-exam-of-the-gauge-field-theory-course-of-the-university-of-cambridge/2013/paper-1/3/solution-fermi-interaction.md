<h1 id="3/solution-fermi-interaction">Solution (Fermi interaction)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Each Dirac current $\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi$ has [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) three, so their product has dimension six. Its coefficient therefore has dimension $-2$:

$$
\boxed{[G_F]=-2.}
$$

The [Fermi interaction](../../../../fermi-interaction.md) is **not power-counting renormalizable**, but its success at low energies is explained by [effective field theory](../../../../effective-field-theory.md). Exchange of a heavy [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) between the appropriate chiral weak currents has a propagator that expands as $1/(m_W^2-q^2)=m_W^{-2}[1+q^2/m_W^2+\cdots]$. The leading term is a local four-fermion interaction and further terms are suppressed by powers of $q^2/m_W^2$. The complete spontaneously broken electroweak [gauge theory](../../../../gauge-theory.md) resolves the pointlike approximation at higher energies. The source's vector-current product illustrates the operator dimension; the actual weak interaction also has its specific flavors and chiral structure.
