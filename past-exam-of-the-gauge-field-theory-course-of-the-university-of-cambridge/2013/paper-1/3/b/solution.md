<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The operator $(\phi^\dagger+\phi)\bar\psi\psi$ has [mass dimension](../../../../../mass-dimension.md) $1+3=4$, so $[g]=0$. It is a **power-counting renormalizable Yukawa interaction** between a real scalar combination and a [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) bilinear. A scalar [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) contributes to a [Dirac mass term](../../../../../dirac-mass-term.md); its fluctuations couple to the [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) pair. This is a schematic scalar–[fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) theory. In a chiral [gauge theory](../../../../../gauge-theory.md), representations must be combined gauge-invariantly, as in the [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) Higgs–[fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) interactions. Without additional protecting symmetries, all allowed scalar counterterms must be included as well.
