↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The operator $(\phi^\dagger\phi)^2$ has [mass dimension](../../../../../mass-dimension.md) four, so $[\lambda]=0$. It is a **power-counting renormalizable scalar quartic interaction**. It describes scalar self-interactions, including the quartic term in the [Higgs potential](../../../../../higgs-field-potential.md) for the appropriate scalar representation. Its sign in the potential must be chosen for a stable large-field theory; dimension counting alone does not ensure stability.
