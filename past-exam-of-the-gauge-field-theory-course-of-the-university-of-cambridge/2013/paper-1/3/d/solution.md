<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The operator $A^\mu\bar\psi\gamma_\mu\psi$ has [mass dimension](../../../../../mass-dimension.md) $1+3=4$, so $[e]=0$. It is the **renormalizable electromagnetic current coupling** when completed as [quantum electrodynamics](../../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md): a massless Abelian [gauge field](../../../../../gauge-field.md) couples minimally to the conserved Dirac current, with consistent [gauge fixing](../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) and [Ward identities](../../../../../ward-identity.md). Indeed, replacing $\partial_\mu$ by a [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) in the [Dirac equation](../../../../../dirac-equation.md) produces this interaction. The gauge structure, rather than dimension counting alone, ensures the necessary cancellations.[§6.3.1, coupling the electromagnetic field to fermions](../../../../../quantum-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
