# Physiological arousal

↑ **Parent:** [Physiology](physiology-split.md)

Bodily activation measured through specified physiological variables, such as [heart rate](heart-rate.md) or [skin](skin.md) conductance. [Physiological arousal](physiological-arousal.md), subjective distress and catastrophic interpretation are distinct measurements.
