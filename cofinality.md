# Cofinality

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal number](cardinal-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cofinality)

The cofinality of an ordinal $\alpha$ is the least [order type](order-type.md) of an unbounded subset of $\alpha$, equivalently the least ordinal $\beta$ admitting a cofinal function $\beta\to\alpha$.
