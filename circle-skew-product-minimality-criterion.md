# Circle skew-product minimality criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Skew product](skew-product.md)

For a [minimal dynamical system](minimal-dynamical-system.md) on a compact [metric space](metric-space.md), the continuous circle extension $(x,y)\mapsto(Tx,y+\rho(x))$ is minimal precisely when the displayed equation has no continuous circle-valued solution $f$ for any nonzero integer $m$. A [minimal subsystem](minimal-subsystem.md) of the extension has fibers that are cosets of its closed vertical translation stabilizer. If that stabilizer is proper, a nontrivial circle character trivial on it gives the forbidden equation. Conversely a solution makes $e^{2\pi i m y}/f(x)$ a nonconstant continuous invariant function.
