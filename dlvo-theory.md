# DLVO theory

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

[DLVO theory](dlvo-theory.md) combines screened electrostatic repulsion from overlapping [electric double layers](electric-double-layer.md) with attractive [Van der Waals forces](van-der-waals-force.md) to model colloid stability. A repulsive barrier can inhibit aggregation even when a deep short-distance attractive minimum exists. Increasing electrolyte [concentration](concentration.md) reduces the screening length and can lower that barrier. Molecular surface forces and polymer-mediated interactions require additions to this description.
