# Cosmological fate from local expansion data

↑ **Parent:** [Deceleration parameter](deceleration-parameter.md)

For a spatially flat universe consisting only of dust and constant [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), $A_0=\ddot a_0/a_0$ and $H_0$ determine the two model parameters through the [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md). Predicting the future also assumes those matter laws persist. Local derivatives alone do not determine arbitrary future stress evolution: $a_+(t)=1+t+t^2/2$ and $a_-(t)=1+t+t^2/2-t^3$ agree through second derivative at zero, but the first expands forever and the second recollapses.
