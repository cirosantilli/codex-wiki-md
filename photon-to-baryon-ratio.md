# Photon-to-baryon ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md)

The reciprocal of the [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md), not a synonym for it. In a thermal photon gas this number ratio is proportional to, but differs from, the thermodynamic [entropy](entropy-split.md) per baryon. The exact photon entropy per photon is $4u_\gamma/(3k_BTn_\gamma)\simeq3.60$, so the photon-sector entropy per baryon in units of $k_B$ is about $3.60s_\gamma$. The entropy sector must be specified when other relativistic species contribute.
