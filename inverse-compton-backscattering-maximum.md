# Inverse Compton backscattering maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse Compton scattering](inverse-compton-scattering.md)

For a head-on [photon](photon.md) and massive-particle collision, boost into the initial particle rest frame, apply the Compton recoil formula for backward [photon](photon.md) scattering, then boost the outgoing [photon](photon.md) forward. For incident photon energy below the particle momentum times $c$, this backward-scattering endpoint is the maximal outgoing laboratory energy. The result in the title approaches $4\gamma^2\varepsilon$ only when the particle is ultrarelativistic and its rest-frame incident [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) is small compared with $mc^2$. Recoil limits amplification at larger incident [energies](energy-split.md) and preserves the total-energy bound.
