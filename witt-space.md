# Witt space

↑ **Parent:** [Stratified pseudomanifold](stratified-pseudomanifold.md)

A rational [Witt space](witt-space.md) is a [stratified pseudomanifold](stratified-pseudomanifold.md) such that the link $L$ of every stratum of odd codimension $2r+1$ satisfies $IH_r^{\bar m}(L;\mathbb Q)=0$. This removes the only difference between lower and upper middle extensions and gives rational middle-perversity [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) on closed oriented [Witt spaces](witt-space.md).
