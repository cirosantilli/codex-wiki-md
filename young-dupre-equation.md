<h1 id="young-dupre-equation">Young-Dupré equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Young's equation](young-s-equation.md)

Combining reversible work of adhesion per area, $W=\gamma_{SV}+\gamma_{LV}-\gamma_{SL}$, with [Young's equation](young-s-equation.md) gives the displayed relation. It assumes the same smooth equilibrium interfaces; contact-angle hysteresis and dissipation are not included.
