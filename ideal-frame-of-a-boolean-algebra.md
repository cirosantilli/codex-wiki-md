# Ideal frame of a Boolean algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md)

The [lattice ideals](lattice-ideal.md) of a [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md) form a [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) under inclusion. Meets are intersections and joins are ideals generated by unions. A complemented ideal is principal: if $I\vee J=B$ and $I\cap J=\{0\}$, choose $b\in I,c\in J$ with $b\vee c=1$; then $c=\neg b$ and $I=\mathord\downarrow b$. This ideal frame defines an [extremally disconnected locale](extremally-disconnected-locale.md) exactly when $B$ is complete.
