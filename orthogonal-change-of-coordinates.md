# Orthogonal change of coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Change of basis](change-of-basis.md)

An orthogonal change of coordinates relates two [orthonormal bases](orthonormal-basis.md) of a real [inner product space](inner-product-space.md). With the new basis vectors as columns of an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) $Q$, old and new coordinate columns obey $x=Qy$. Then $x^Tx=y^Ty$, so [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md), [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) and [angles](angle.md) are preserved. A [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) $x^TAx$ becomes $y^T(Q^TAQ)y$. For a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md), [orthogonal diagonalization](orthogonal-diagonalization-of-a-real-symmetric-matrix.md) chooses $Q$ so that the transformed quadratic form is a sum of [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) times squared coordinates. This explains the principal-axis descriptions of a [quadric surface](quadric-surface.md).
