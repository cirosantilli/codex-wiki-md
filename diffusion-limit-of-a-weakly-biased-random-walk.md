# Diffusion limit of a weakly biased random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Biased random walk](biased-random-walk.md)

For independent lattice steps of length $a$ at intervals $\tau$, with probabilities $\alpha+\beta=1$, the diffusive scaling $a^2/\tau\to2D$ and $(\alpha-\beta)a/\tau\to V$ requires a bias of order $a$. Expanding the [master equation](master-equation.md) gives the [advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md) $p_t+Vp_x=Dp_{xx}$. The finite-step variance coefficient is $2a^2\alpha\beta/\tau$; it has the same limit because the squared bias vanishes. Fixed nonzero bias does not give finite drift under this diffusive scaling.
