# Dyadic partition

↑ **Parent:** [Partition of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md)

A [dyadic partition](dyadic-partition.md) of $[0,1]$ at level $J$ consists of $2^J$ equal cells of length $2^{-J}$. Each cell splits into two cells at the next level. Use left-closed, right-open cells, adding the endpoint $1$ to the final cell when a pointwise representative is needed. These nested partitions underlie the [Haar scaling functions](haar-scaling-function.md) and [Haar wavelets](haar-wavelet.md).
