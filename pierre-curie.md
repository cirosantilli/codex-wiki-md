# Pierre Curie

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Curie)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md) who investigated [magnetism](magnetism.md), [piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md) and [radioactivity](radioactive-decay.md). He built delicate balances, [electrometers](electrometer.md) and [piezoelectric crystals](piezoelectric-crystal.md); this work on instruments helped make weak electrical effects measurable. With [Marie Curie](marie-curie.md), he investigated radioactive substances under difficult laboratory conditions. The [Curie ionization-current measurement](curie-ionization-current-measurement.md) illustrates how such instrumentation can turn a small signal into a quantitative comparison.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1901–1921; Biographical identity and professional specialization](nobel-lectures-physics-1901-1921/pierre-curie-biographical.md)
