# Homologous chromosome

↑ **Parent:** [Chromosome](chromosome.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homologous_chromosome)

Homologous chromosomes carry corresponding [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md), although their [alleles](allele.md) may differ. In the [diploid](diploid.md) $AB/ab$ example, the two homologous chromosomes carry the parental [haplotypes](haplotype.md) $AB$ and $ab$.
