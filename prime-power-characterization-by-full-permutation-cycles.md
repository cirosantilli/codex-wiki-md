# Prime-power characterization by full permutation cycles

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation cycle](permutation-cycle.md)

For $n>1$, every [permutation](permutation.md) of [order of a permutation](order-of-a-permutation.md) $n$ in $S_n$ is one full cycle if and only if $n$ is a [prime power](prime-power.md). If $n=p^r$, all lengths in its [disjoint cycle decomposition](disjoint-cycle-decomposition.md) divide $p^r$, and their [least common multiple](least-common-multiple.md) is their maximum. One cycle therefore has length $n$ and uses every symbol. If $n$ has more than one [prime divisor](prime-factor.md), write $n=ab$ with $a,b>1$ [coprime](coprime-integers.md) by separating one full prime-power factor from the others. Since $a+b\leq ab=n$, disjoint cycles of lengths $a$ and $b$ fit inside $S_n$. Their product has [order of a permutation](order-of-a-permutation.md) $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=n$, but is not a full cycle.
