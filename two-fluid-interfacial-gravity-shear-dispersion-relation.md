# Two-fluid interfacial gravity-shear dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin-Helmholtz instability](kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md)

For two inviscid incompressible half-spaces, lower density/[velocity](velocity.md) $\rho_1,U_1$, upper density/[velocity](velocity.md) $\rho_2,U_2$, downward [gravity](gravity.md) $g$ and [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) $\sigma$, a mode $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$ with $k>0$ obeys

$$
(\omega-k\overline U)^2=\frac{gk(\rho_1-\rho_2)+\sigma k^3}{\rho_1+\rho_2}-\frac{\rho_1\rho_2}{(\rho_1+\rho_2)^2}k^2(U_1-U_2)^2,\qquad\overline U=\frac{\rho_1U_1+\rho_2U_2}{\rho_1+\rho_2}.
$$

A negative right-hand side produces exponential growth. The gravitational term recovers [Rayleigh-Taylor instability](rayleigh-taylor-instability.md) for heavy-over-light ordering; the shear term can destabilize otherwise stable stratification. Finite layer thickness, [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md), compressibility and [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) lie outside this interface model.
