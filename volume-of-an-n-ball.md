# Volume of an n-ball

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperspherical coordinates](hyperspherical-coordinates.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volume_of_an_n-ball)

The radius-$R$ ball in $\mathbb R^n$ has volume $\pi^{n/2}R^n/\Gamma(n/2+1)$.
