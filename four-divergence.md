# Four-divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Four-vector](four-vector.md)

The four-divergence of a [four-vector](four-vector.md) field is the [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) contraction $\partial_\mu V^\mu$. With $x^\mu=(ct,\boldsymbol x)$, it equals $c^{-1}\partial_t V^0+\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol V$. The [four-current](four-current.md) has zero four-divergence, expressing local charge conservation.
