# Projection in a C-star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md)

A projection in a [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) is a [Hermitian C-star element](hermitian-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) that is also idempotent. Its spectrum is contained in $\{0,1\}$, and a nonzero projection has norm one by the [C-star identity](c-star-identity.md). If $u$ is a [partial isometry](partial-isometry.md), $u^*u$ and $uu^*$ are its initial and final projections. For a projection $p$, the closed subalgebra $pAp$ has unit $p$ and the inherited norm and involution; it is the corresponding corner C-star algebra. In an operator representation, such an element acts as an [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md).
