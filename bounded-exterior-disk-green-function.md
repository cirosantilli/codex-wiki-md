# Bounded exterior disk Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

For the exterior of the unit disc in two dimensions, with the sign convention $\Delta_xG=+\delta(x-y)$, this is the [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md) that vanishes on the circle and is bounded at infinity. The [method of images](method-of-images.md) places an opposite logarithmic source at the inverse point $y/|y|^2$. The constant $-(2\pi)^{-1}\log|y|$ enforces the zero boundary value. The [inversion in a circle](inversion-in-a-circle.md) identity $|x-y|=|y|\,|x-y/|y|^2|$ for $|x|=1$ verifies that condition directly.

The outward normal of the exterior domain is $-x$ on the unit circle. Its [normal derivative](normal-derivative.md) gives the positive exterior [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel.md)

$$
\partial_nG(x,y)=\frac{|y|^2-1}{2\pi|x-y|^2},\qquad |x|=1<|y|.
$$

For bounded [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) this kernel integrates boundary data to the interior value. Boundedness at infinity is essential to uniqueness: without it, a multiple of $\log|y|$ can be added without changing the circle's [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md).
