# Chemical potential equality for particle exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md)

Consider two internally equilibrated reservoirs A and B at the same imposed [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md). They may have different compositions or represent different phases. Transfer a small positive [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) $\delta n$ of the same [chemical species](chemical-species.md) from A to B, holding the other inventories fixed. To first order, their total [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) changes by

$$
\delta G_{\rm total}
=-\mu_{{\rm m},A}\delta n+\mu_{{\rm m},B}\delta n
=(\mu_{{\rm m},B}-\mu_{{\rm m},A})\delta n.
$$

Hence exchange toward lower [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) lowers [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md). If transfer is allowed in both directions and the two reservoirs contain the exchangeable component, [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) requires equal [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) for that component. See [MIT 5.60, Lecture 14, pages 1–2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-60-thermodynamics-kinetics-spring-2008/e763a06aa3b5de09cd6bfea6280f219c_5_60_lecture14.pdf).

An impermeable partition can maintain unequal [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md); equality is a condition on an allowed exchange. Two pure samples of the same [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) already have equal [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md). Different compositions at a common [pressure](pressure-split.md) can instead give different [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) for one component through its [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md).

Equality concerns the same exchangeable component in different places, not all different [chemical species](chemical-species.md). For a permitted [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md), the corresponding interior [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) criterion is $\sum_i\nu_i\mu_{{\rm m},i}=0$, with signed [stoichiometric vector](stoichiometric-vector.md) entries $\nu_i$. The existing [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) and [constrained Gibbs minimization for chemical equilibrium](constrained-gibbs-minimization-for-chemical-equilibrium.md) sections develop those composition constraints.
