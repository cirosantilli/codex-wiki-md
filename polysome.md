# Polysome

↑ **Parent:** [Ribosome](ribosome.md)

A group of [ribosomes](ribosome.md) translating one [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md). Occupancy can increase through faster initiation or slower elongation, so the amount of [polysomes](polysome.md) is not by itself a synthesis-rate measurement.
