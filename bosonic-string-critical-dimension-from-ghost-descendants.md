# Bosonic-string critical dimension from ghost descendants

↑ **Parent:** [Critical dimension of string theory](critical-dimension-of-string-theory.md)

For the [FP ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) vacuum annihilated by $b_{n\ge0}$ and $c_{n>0}$, the [FP ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) level-one and level-two descendant coefficients are $-2$ and $-17$. Matter contributes $\alpha_0^2$ and $2\alpha_0^2+D/2$. [Virasoro closure from BRST nilpotence](virasoro-closure-from-brst-nilpotence.md) at level one fixes the total highest weight to $\alpha_0^2/2-1$; level two then requires $D=26$. Imposing ground-state BRST closure gives $\alpha_0^2=2$ and the open bosonic [tachyon](tachyon.md). Interpret the coefficients with the stated [ghost zero-mode pairing](ghost-zero-mode-pairing.md).
