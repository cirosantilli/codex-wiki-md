<h1 id="su-5">SU(5)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md)

The [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) on $\mathbb C^5$ has dimension $24$. Its complexified [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is $\mathfrak{sl}_5(\mathbb C)$, with the [A4 root system](a4-root-system.md). Its defining [representation](group-representation.md), [exterior square](exterior-square.md) and [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) have dimensions $5$, $10$ and $15$.
