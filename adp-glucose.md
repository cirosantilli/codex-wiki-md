# ADP-glucose

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide sugar](nucleotide-sugar.md)

[ADP-glucose](adp-glucose.md) is an activated donor containing a [glucose](glucose.md) residue linked to [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md). Transfer requires an appropriate [glycosyltransferase](glycosyltransferase.md) and acceptor; access to the correct compartment can be limiting.
