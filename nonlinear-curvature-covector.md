# Nonlinear curvature covector

↑ **Parent:** [Long-wavelength approximation in cosmology](long-wavelength-approximation-in-cosmology.md)

With negligible shear, a [minimally coupled scalar field](minimally-coupled-scalar-field.md) serving as a clock, and $\Pi\ne0$, the [Hamiltonian constraint](hamiltonian-constraint.md) and [momentum constraint](momentum-constraint.md) imply $\dot H=-4\pi GN\Pi^2$ and $\partial_iH=-4\pi G\Pi\partial_i\phi$. Differentiating the local Friedmann relation yields $\dot\Pi=-N(3H\Pi+V_{,\phi})$ and $\partial_i\Pi=-(3H+V_{,\phi}/\Pi)\partial_i\phi$. Inserting these identities into $\dot\zeta_i$ cancels all terms, including gradients of the [lapse function](lapse-function.md). The conserved covector becomes the gradient of a [uniform-density curvature perturbation](uniform-density-curvature-perturbation.md) at linear adiabatic order. Dropping the shear constraint is essential; this is not a proof of conservation in an arbitrary non-attractor phase.
