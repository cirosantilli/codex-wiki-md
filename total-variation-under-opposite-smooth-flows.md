# Total variation under opposite smooth flows

↑ **Parent:** [Total variation seminorm on a domain](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md)

For the [local flow](local-flow.md) of a smooth compactly supported [vector field](vector-field.md) and any scalar [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) function $w$, the [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) and [polar decomposition of a vector measure](polar-decomposition-of-a-vector-measure.md) give

$$
\operatorname{TV}(w\circ\Phi_t)=\int|\operatorname{cof}(D\Phi_{-t})\sigma_w|\,d|Dw|.
$$

The two [cofactor matrices](cofactor-matrix.md) are $I\pm tB+O(t^2)$ uniformly. On $|\sigma_w|=1$, the linear terms in their Euclidean [norm](norm.md) expansions cancel. Integration bounds the remainder by $Ct^2|Dw|(\Omega)$. The identity includes all components of the [decomposition of a BV derivative](decomposition-of-a-bv-derivative.md); it does not require smoothness of $w$ or its jump interfaces.
