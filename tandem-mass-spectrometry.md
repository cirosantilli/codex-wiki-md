# Tandem mass spectrometry

↑ **Parent:** [Mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md)

[tandem mass spectrometry](tandem-mass-spectrometry.md) selects precursor [ions](ion.md), fragments them, and measures the fragments to constrain precursor identity or structure. In [proteomics](proteomics.md), fragmentation of [peptides](peptide.md) provides sequence evidence that can be matched against a database.
