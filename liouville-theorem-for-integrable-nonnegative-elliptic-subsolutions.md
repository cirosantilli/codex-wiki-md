# Liouville theorem for integrable nonnegative elliptic subsolutions

↑ **Parent:** [Moser iteration](moser-iteration.md)

For $n\geq3$, a nonnegative entire [weak subsolution](weak-subsolution-of-a-divergence-form-elliptic-equation.md) of a bounded measurable uniformly elliptic divergence-form equation that belongs to $L^p(\mathbb R^n)$ for some $p>1$ is zero [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md). Rescaling the local [Moser iteration](moser-iteration.md) estimate gives $\|u\|_{L^\infty(B_{R/2})}\leq CR^{-n/p}\|u\|_{L^p(B_R)}$. Let $R\to\infty$.
