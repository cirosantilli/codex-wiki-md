# Character inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md)

For complex [class functions](class-function.md) on a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$, the character inner product is

$$
\langle\chi,\psi\rangle_G
=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}\chi(g)\overline{\psi(g)}.
$$

If $\psi$ is [irreducible](irreducible-character.md), this inner product is its multiplicity in the representation with character $\chi$.
