# Curvature difference of two Chern connections

↑ **Parent:** [Chern connection](chern-connection.md)

Let $D_1$ and $D_2$ be [Chern connections](chern-connection.md) on the same [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md), possibly for different [Hermitian metrics](hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md), and put $a=D_1-D_2$. Then $a$ has type $(1,0)$ and

$$
F_{D_1}-F_{D_2}=\bar\partial_{\operatorname{End}E}a.
$$

Indeed, the [curvature difference formula](curvature-difference-formula.md) has only types $(2,0)$ and $(1,1)$; both Chern curvatures have type $(1,1)$, so the $(2,0)$ part vanishes and the remaining part is $D_2^{0,1}a=\bar\partial_{\operatorname{End}E}a$.
