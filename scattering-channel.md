# Scattering channel

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md)

A specified asymptotic configuration of the particles and their internal [quantum states](quantum-state.md) in [quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md). An incoming channel can scatter into several outgoing channels; [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) elements give their [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md) with a stated [probability current](probability-current.md) normalization. A channel can be energetically inaccessible even when other channels are open.
