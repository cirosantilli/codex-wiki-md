# Surface-reaction-controlled particle growth

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-reservoir diffusion-controlled particle growth](constant-reservoir-diffusion-controlled-particle-growth.md)

When attachment is much slower than exterior [diffusion](diffusion.md), the surface [concentration](concentration.md) stays close to the reservoir value. An attachment coefficient $k_s$ with units of [velocity](velocity.md) gives nearly constant radial growth in the dilute-feed approximation. For a [sphere](sphere.md) the ratio $k_sa/D$ distinguishes attachment control from transport control. A finite attachment rate leads to a [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md) rather than a fixed equilibrium surface concentration.
