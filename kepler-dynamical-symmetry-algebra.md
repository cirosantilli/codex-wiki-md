# Kepler dynamical symmetry algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md)

For $H=p^2/2-\kappa/r$, the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md) obey $\{K_i,K_j\}=-2H\epsilon_{ijk}L_k$. On negative or positive energy regions use $K/\sqrt{|2H|}$ to obtain the compact or Lorentz [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), respectively. At zero energy the algebra on the characteristic orbit quotient is the Euclidean motion algebra. Global [group actions](group-action.md) require complete generators or collision regularization; simply replacing $H$ by a constant inside a Hamiltonian-vector-field derivative is not valid on the unreduced energy hypersurface.
