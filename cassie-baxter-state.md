<h1 id="cassie-baxter-state">Cassie–Baxter state</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Wetting](wetting.md)

A [Cassie–Baxter state](cassie-baxter-state.md) is a composite wetting state in which a drop contacts solid asperities over area fraction $f_s$ and trapped gas over the remainder. An ideal area-weighted contact-angle model gives the displayed apparent angle from the smooth-solid [Young's equation](young-s-equation.md) angle $\theta_Y$. Reducing solid contact can strongly enhance apparent [hydrophobicity](hydrophobicity.md). Such a state can be metastable and can collapse when liquid enters the texture; roughness alone does not guarantee it.
