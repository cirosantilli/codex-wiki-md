# Normalizer condition for finite p-groups

↑ **Parent:** [Finite p-group](finite-p-group.md)

Every proper [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$ of a [finite p-group](finite-p-group.md) is properly contained in its [normalizer](normalizer.md). Let $H$ act on the left [cosets](coset.md) $G/H$ by left multiplication. The fixed cosets are those represented by elements of $N_G(H)$, so there are $[N_G(H):H]$ of them. All other [orbits of a group action](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) have sizes divisible by $p$. Since $[G:H]$ is divisible by $p$ and at least one coset is fixed, the fixed-coset count is a positive multiple of $p$. This also proves $[N_G(H):H]\geq p$.
