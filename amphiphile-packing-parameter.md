# Amphiphile packing parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Micelle](micelle.md)

For an [amphiphile](amphiphile.md), let $v$ be its hydrophobic-tail [volume](volume.md), $a_0$ its effective head-group [area](surface-area.md), and $\ell$ its maximum tail extension. The dimensionless packing parameter $P=v/(a_0\ell)$ estimates compatible aggregate curvature. For a spherical [micelle](micelle.md) of core radius $r$ containing $n$ molecules, $nv=4\pi r^3/3$ and $na_0=4\pi r^2$, giving $v/a_0=r/3$. Since $r\leq\ell$, spherical packing requires $P\leq1/3$. The analogous long-cylinder calculation gives $v/a_0=r/2$ and the limiting value $1/2$; a planar monolayer has $v/a_0$ equal to its thickness and limiting value one. Thus small $P$ favors spherical [micelles](micelle.md), intermediate values favor cylindrical aggregates, and values near one favor [lipid bilayers](lipid-bilayer.md). This is a geometric guide, not a full thermodynamic prediction of every phase boundary.
