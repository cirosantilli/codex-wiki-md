# Tensor-square decomposition of the defining so5 representation

↑ **Parent:** [Traceless symmetric square of the defining so5 representation](traceless-symmetric-square-of-the-defining-so5-representation.md)

For the five-dimensional defining representation of $\mathfrak{so}_5$, the [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) is the sum of its invariant trace and the traceless representation $V(2\omega_2)$, while the [exterior square](exterior-square.md) is the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $V(2\omega_1)$. Hence

$$
V(\omega_2)\otimes V(\omega_2)
\cong V(0)\oplus V(2\omega_1)\oplus V(2\omega_2).
$$
