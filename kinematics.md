# Kinematics

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

The description of motion through [position](position.md), [velocity](velocity.md) and [acceleration](acceleration.md), before specifying the forces responsible. For constant acceleration these are related by integrating time derivatives. [Projectile motion](projectile-motion.md) under an approximately uniform gravitational acceleration is an elementary example.
