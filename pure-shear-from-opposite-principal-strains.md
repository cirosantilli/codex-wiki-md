# Pure shear from opposite principal strains

↑ **Parent:** [Shear strain](shear-strain.md)

The infinitesimal strain tensor $\operatorname{diag}(-e,e)$ compresses one principal direction and stretches the perpendicular one by the same amount. Rotating the coordinate axes through $45^\circ$ turns it into a tensor with zero normal strains and off-diagonal entries of magnitude $e$. The engineering [shear strain](shear-strain.md) is twice the tensor shear component, so its shear angle has magnitude $\gamma=2e$. Likewise principal stresses $(-S,S)$ give zero normal stress and shear stress of magnitude $S$ on those rotated faces. The [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) law $S=G\gamma$ therefore gives $S=2Ge$.
