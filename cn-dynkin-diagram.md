# Cn Dynkin diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md)

For $n\ge2$, the finite [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) $C_n$ is a chain with a terminal double bond. Its arrow points from the terminal long root toward the adjacent short root. It represents the [Cn root system](cn-root-system.md); $C_2$ is isomorphic to $B_2$ after relabelling.
