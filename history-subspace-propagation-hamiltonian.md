# History-subspace propagation Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Unary quantum clock](unary-quantum-clock.md)

In the orthonormal history basis $|e_t\rangle=|\psi_t\rangle|c_t\rangle$, circuit propagation restricts to $E=\frac12\sum_{t=1}^T(|e_t\rangle-|e_{t-1}\rangle)(\langle e_t|-\langle e_{t-1}|)$. This is a [Graph Laplacian](laplacian-matrix.md) on a path. Its uniform zero-energy vector is the [computational history state](computational-history-state.md), while initialization restricts to $D=I-|e_0\rangle\langle e_0|$.
