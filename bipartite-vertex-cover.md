# Bipartite vertex cover

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

König's theorem equates the minimum vertex-cover size of a bipartite graph with its maximum matching size.

A bipartite vertex cover is a [vertex cover](vertex-cover.md) restricted to a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md); [Kőnig's theorem for bipartite matching](konig-s-theorem-graph-theory.md) computes its minimum size.
