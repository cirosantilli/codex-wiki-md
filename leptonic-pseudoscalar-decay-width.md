# Leptonic pseudoscalar decay width

↑ **Parent:** [Leptonic pion decay](leptonic-pion-decay.md)

In the convention $\langle0|J^\mu|P(p)\rangle=-i\sqrt2F_Pp^\mu$, the [Fermi interaction](fermi-interaction.md) gives $\mathcal M=G_FF_Pp_\mu\bar u_\ell\gamma^\mu(1-\gamma_5)v_\nu$. With a massless [antineutrino](antineutrino.md), the external [Dirac equations](dirac-equation.md) reduce this to $G_FF_Pm_\ell\bar u_\ell(1-\gamma_5)v_\nu$. The spin sum is $8G_F^2F_P^2m_\ell^2 k\cdot q=4G_F^2F_P^2m_\ell^2(M_P^2-m_\ell^2)$. The [two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space](two-body-lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) has integrated value $(1-m_\ell^2/M_P^2)/(8\pi)$, and the initial flux factor is $1/(2M_P)$, giving the displayed width. If the decay constant is instead defined without the square root of two, $f_P=\sqrt2F_P$ and the denominator is $8\pi$. A weak mixing coefficient multiplies the amplitude and its modulus squared multiplies the width.
