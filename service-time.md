# Service time

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)

The time a customer occupies a server while receiving service. Its mean is a duration; its reciprocal is a service rate. An [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) is convenient for Markov queue models, but [Little's law](little-s-law.md) can use a general finite mean.
