# Electromagnetic coil

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_coil)

An [electromagnetic coil](electromagnetic-coil.md) is a wound [electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md) whose turns add their [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md) and [magnetic flux linkages](magnetic-flux-linkage.md). For closely spaced equivalent turns carrying the same [electric current](electric-current.md), the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) is the single-turn result multiplied by the number of turns.
