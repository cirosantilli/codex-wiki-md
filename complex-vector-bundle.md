# Complex vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

A complex vector bundle is a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) with complex-linear local trivializations and transition maps. A rank-$r$ complex bundle has an underlying oriented real bundle of rank $2r$, using the orientation of complex bases. Rank-one bundles are [complex line bundles](complex-line-bundle.md), and their higher-rank counterparts have integral [Chern classes](chern-class.md).
