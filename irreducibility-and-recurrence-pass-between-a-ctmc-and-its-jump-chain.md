# Irreducibility and recurrence pass between a CTMC and its jump chain

↑ **Parent:** [Jump chain](jump-chain.md)

For a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) whose jump times tend to infinity, the chain and its [jump chain](jump-chain.md) have the same directed paths of positive-probability jumps. They are therefore [irreducible](irreducible-markov-chain.md) simultaneously, and a state is [recurrent](recurrent-state.md) for one exactly when it is recurrent for the other: the event of returning after departure depends only on the sequence of visited states.
