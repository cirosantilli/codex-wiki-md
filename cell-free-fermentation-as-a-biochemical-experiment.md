# Cell-free fermentation as a biochemical experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Cell-free extract](cell-free-extract.md)

The [cell-free extract](cell-free-extract.md) experiment under [yeast](yeast.md) asks whether [fermentation](fermentation.md) requires intact living [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). In his 1907 lecture, [Eduard Buchner](eduard-buchner.md) described grinding [yeast](yeast.md) with [quartz](quartz.md) sand and [diatomite](diatomaceous-earth.md) in a large mortar using a heavy pestle, wrapping the dough in canvas, then squeezing it in a hydraulic press. Adding concentrated [sugar](sugar.md) syrup to the expressed liquid produced [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) bubbles and foam; separate investigations identified [ethanol](ethanol.md).[Cell-free fermentation, Nobel lecture delivered 11 December 1907, printed pp. 110–112, Figures 2–4 and accompanying preparation/readout text](cell-free-fermentation-by-eduard-buchner.md)

Activity persisted after filtration through cell-removing porcelain candles. [Toluene](toluene.md) inhibited live [yeast](yeast.md) [fermentation](fermentation.md) while the [cell-free extract](cell-free-extract.md) retained activity, addressing a different objection from cell removal. These comparisons support extracted chemical machinery as the cause; bubbles alone would not identify [alcoholic fermentation](ethanol-fermentation.md).[Printed pp. 112–114: residual-cell objection, biscuit-porcelain filtration, toluene comparison and extract activity](cell-free-fermentation-by-eduard-buchner.md)

The inference is activity outside intact living [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md), not that [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) are unnecessary for making or maintaining the machinery. Nor was this a purified single-[enzyme](enzyme-split.md) mechanism: the lecture acknowledges unsuccessful purification and reports that two separated inactive fractions recovered [fermentation](fermentation.md) when reunited. Such a reconstitution asks which components must work together, extending the initial separation of [organism](organism-split.md) from chemical activity.[Printed p. 115: unsuccessful zymase separation; pp. 117–118: gelatine-filter fractions and restored activity on reunion](cell-free-fermentation-by-eduard-buchner.md)
