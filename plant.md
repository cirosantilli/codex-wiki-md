# Plant

↑ **Parent:** [Eukaryote](eukaryote.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant)

[Plants](plant.md) are [eukaryotes](eukaryote.md). A typical photosynthetic leaf cell has a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), [mitochondria](mitochondria.md), [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md), and a [cell wall](cell-wall.md) outside its [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md). A root cell need not have [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md); the [plant](plant.md)'s parts specialize for different functions. [Plants](plant.md) perform [cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md) as well as [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 4.3: plant cell organization](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-3-eukaryotic-cells).
