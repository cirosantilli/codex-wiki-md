# W and Z discovery experiments

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

A [W boson](w-boson.md) decay can leave one visible [charged lepton](charged-lepton.md) and a large [missing transverse momentum](missing-transverse-momentum.md); a [Z boson](z-boson.md) decay can instead supply two visible [leptons](lepton.md) whose [four-momenta](four-momentum.md) determine an [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md). The two signatures answer the same existence question with different missing information. The [electroweak unification history](electroweak-unification-history.md) recognized by the 1979 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) predates these 1983 observations: the [W boson](w-boson.md) and [Z boson](z-boson.md) were predictions to test, rather than particles first proposed in 1983.

The [Super Proton-Antiproton Synchrotron](super-proton-antiproton-synchrotron.md) stored counterrotating [proton](proton.md) and [antiproton](antiproton.md) beams; [stochastic cooling](stochastic-cooling.md) made accumulation of the scarce [antiprotons](antiproton.md) practical. [Carlo Rubbia](carlo-rubbia.md)'s 1984 lecture describes [track reconstruction](track-reconstruction.md) in a large [drift chamber](drift-chamber.md) inside a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), surrounded by segmented [particle calorimeters](calorimeter-particle-physics.md). Their nearly complete angular coverage allowed an imbalance test for unobserved [neutrinos](neutrino.md).[1984 lecture, section 3, p.247, Fig.5; section 4, Figs.7–8](experimental-observation-of-the-intermediate-vector-bosons-w-plus-w-minus-and-z0-by-carlo-rubbia.md)
