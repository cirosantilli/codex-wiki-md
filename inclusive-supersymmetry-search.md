# Inclusive supersymmetry search

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md)

A broad search over event classes such as [jets](jet-particle-physics.md), [leptons](lepton.md) and [missing transverse momentum](missing-transverse-momentum.md), with bins chosen to cover several production or decay possibilities. It still has acceptance and model assumptions; inclusive does not mean sensitive to every supersymmetric spectrum.
