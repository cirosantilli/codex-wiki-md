# Resistive magnetic entry of a falling loop

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic braking](magnetic-braking.md)

A rectangular loop entering a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) while only one horizontal segment is immersed has [motional electromotive force](motional-electromotive-force.md) $Bav$. Neglecting [self-inductance](self-inductance.md), its [electric current](electric-current.md) is $Bav/R$ and its upward [magnetic force](magnetic-force.md) is $(Ba)^2v/R$. The equation $m\dot v=mg-(Ba)^2v/R$ gives exponential relaxation toward $mgR/(Ba)^2$. This entry model ends when both horizontal segments enter the field; then their net magnetic force and the rate of change of external [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) vanish.
