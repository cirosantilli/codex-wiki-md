# Beta barrel

↑ **Parent:** [Protein fold](protein-fold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta_barrel)

A [beta sheet](beta-sheet.md) closed into a barrel-like [protein](protein-split.md) fold by joining its edge strands through backbone [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md). A membrane-spanning barrel can present nonpolar side chains to lipid while lining a pore with polar groups; soluble barrels instead have a different solvent-facing pattern. The topology alone does not imply a membrane location.
