# Congelation ice

↑ **Parent:** [First-year sea ice](first-year-sea-ice.md)

[Congelation ice](congelation-ice.md) grows by freezing onto the underside of an existing sheet. Competitive growth selects grains whose fast basal-plane growth can advance downward, yielding columnar [sea-ice crystal fabric](sea-ice-crystal-fabric.md) with brine-bearing intergranular regions.
