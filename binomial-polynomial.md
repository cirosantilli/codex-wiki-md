# Binomial polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Integer-valued polynomial](integer-valued-polynomial.md)

For $n\geq0$, define $B_0(X)=1$ and $B_n(X)=X(X-1)\cdots(X-n+1)/n!$. The [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md) formula shows $B_n(m)\in\mathbb Z$ for every nonnegative integer $m$. Since these integers are dense in the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md), [continuity](continuous-function.md) of the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) implies $B_n(\mathbb Z_p)\subseteq\mathbb Z_p$, so it is an [integer-valued polynomial](integer-valued-polynomial.md) there and $\|B_n\|_\infty=1$. The [Pascal's identity](pascal-s-rule.md) gives $\Delta B_{n+1}=B_n$ for the [forward difference operator](forward-difference-operator.md).
