<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Denote the three stated pair sums by $r_1,r_2,r_3$. They are pairwise distinct: for example,

$$
r_1-r_2=(\alpha_1-\alpha_4)(\alpha_2-\alpha_3)\ne0,
$$

and the other differences factor similarly. Every [Galois automorphism](../../../../automorphism-of-a-field-extension.md) fixes each $r_i\in F$, and hence fixes each of the three corresponding partitions of four roots into two pairs. The kernel of the permutation action $S_4\to S_3$ on these partitions is

$$
V_4=\{1,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}.
$$

Thus $G$ embeds in the [Klein four-group](../../../../klein-four-group.md). By part (a), $4$ divides $|G|$, and the containing group has order four. Consequently

$$
\boxed{G\cong V_4}.
$$

This [Split resolvent criterion for a Klein four Galois group](../../../../split-resolvent-criterion-for-a-klein-four-galois-group.md) uses the [cubic resolvent of a quartic](../../../../cubic-resolvent-of-a-quartic.md). The pair-sum differences remain nonzero in characteristic two, so this proof does not need the missing characteristic assumption from part (c).
