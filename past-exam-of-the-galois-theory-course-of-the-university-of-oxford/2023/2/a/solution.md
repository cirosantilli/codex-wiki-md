<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Galois group](../../../../galois-group.md) acts [transitively](../../../../transitive-group-action.md) on the $n$ roots because $f$ is [irreducible](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) and [separable](../../../../separable-topological-space.md). The [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{|G|=n\,|\operatorname{Stab}_G(\alpha_1)|},
$$

so $n$ divides $|G|$. Equivalently, the [tower law](../../../../tower-law.md) gives $[K:F]=[K:F(\alpha_1)]n$, while the finite [Galois extension](../../../../finite-galois-extension.md) has group order $[K:F]$.
