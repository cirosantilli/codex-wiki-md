<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta)$, every [Galois automorphism](../../../../automorphism-of-a-field-extension.md) sends the primitive root $\zeta$ to another primitive $m$th [root of unity](../../../../root-of-unity.md), uniquely $\zeta^a$ for $a\in(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times$. The [Cyclotomic character of a finite cyclotomic extension](../../../../cyclotomic-character-of-a-finite-cyclotomic-extension.md) is

$$
\boxed{\chi_m:\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times,\qquad\sigma(\zeta)=\zeta^{\chi_m(\sigma)}}.
$$

If $\chi_m(\sigma)=a$ and $\chi_m(\tau)=b$, then $(\sigma\tau)(\zeta)=\sigma(\zeta^b)=\zeta^{ab}$, proving the [group homomorphism](../../../../group-homomorphism.md) property. Its [kernel](../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is trivial: an automorphism fixing $\zeta$ fixes the field it generates. Thus the character is [injective](../../../../injective-function.md). Here its domain is the finite cyclotomic [Galois group](../../../../galois-group.md); the corresponding map from an [absolute Galois group](../../../../absolute-galois-group.md) need not be injective.
