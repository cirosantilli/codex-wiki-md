<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Irreducibility of the [cyclotomic polynomial](../../../../../cyclotomic-polynomial.md) gives $[K:\mathbb Q]=\varphi(15)=8$. The extension is [Galois](../../../../../finite-galois-extension.md), since $K$ is the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) of $T^{15}-1$ in characteristic zero. The injection in part (a) is consequently an isomorphism, and the [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)\cong(\mathbb Z/15\mathbb Z)^\times\cong C_2\times C_4.
$$

The [Fundamental theorem of Galois theory](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-galois-theory.md) identifies proper intermediate fields strictly above $\mathbb Q$ with proper nontrivial [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md). Such subgroups have order two or four. The two-torsion subgroup is $C_2\times C_2$, with three nonidentity elements and hence three order-two subgroups. There are also three order-four subgroups: they are the kernels of the three nonzero [group homomorphisms](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $C_2\times C_4\to C_2$. Distinct such maps have distinct kernels because the target has only one nonidentity element. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{three quadratic subfields and three quartic subfields, six in total}.}
$$
