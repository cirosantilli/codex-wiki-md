<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The element $\zeta$ satisfies $T^2-\eta T+1=0$, so $[\mathbb Q(\zeta):\mathbb Q(\eta)]\leq2$. Since $\eta=\zeta+\overline\zeta$ is real, $\mathbb Q(\eta)\subseteq\mathbb R$. A primitive root of order $m\geq3$ is nonreal: the only real [roots of unity](../../../../root-of-unity.md) are $\pm1$, of orders at most two. Hence $\zeta\notin\mathbb Q(\eta)$ and

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb Q(\zeta):\mathbb Q(\eta)]=2}.
$$

Equivalently, nontrivial [complex conjugation](../../../../complex-conjugation.md) fixes precisely the [maximal real subfield of a cyclotomic field](../../../../maximal-real-subfield-of-a-cyclotomic-field.md) in this degree-two extension.
