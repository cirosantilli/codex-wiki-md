# Eigenspace decomposition from a square-free annihilating polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Annihilating polynomial](annihilating-polynomial.md)

For distinct [scalars](scalar.md) $\mu_j$, the [Lagrange interpolation polynomials](lagrange-polynomial.md) $\ell_j(t)=\prod_{k\ne j}(t-\mu_k)/(\mu_j-\mu_k)$ sum to one. Their values at $T$ have images in the respective [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md), since $(t-\mu_j)\ell_j$ is a multiple of the [annihilating polynomial](annihilating-polynomial.md). Hence every [vector](vector.md) is the sum of its $\ell_j(T)$ images. On the $i$th [eigenspace](eigenspace.md), $\ell_j(T)$ is multiplication by $\delta_{ij}$, which proves directness. This gives the decomposition without invoking the [primary decomposition theorem](primary-decomposition-theorem-for-linear-maps.md). Under this decomposition a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) commutes with $T$ exactly when it preserves every [eigenspace](eigenspace.md).
