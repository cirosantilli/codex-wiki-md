# Linearized ideal magnetohydrodynamic equations

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

The linearized ideal magnetohydrodynamic equations retain terms first order in perturbations about a chosen ideal-MHD background. Around a uniform state they describe [Alfvén waves](alfven-wave.md) and [magnetosonic waves](magnetosonic-wave.md).
