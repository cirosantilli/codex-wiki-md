# Gravitational radius

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_radius)

For a self-gravitating system of mass $M$ and [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md) $W<0$, its gravitational radius is defined by

$$
R_g=-\frac{GM^2}{W}.
$$

It converts the total binding energy into a characteristic size without assuming a particular density profile.
