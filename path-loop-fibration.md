# Path-loop fibration

↑ **Parent:** [Loop space](loop-space.md)

The evaluation map from the based path space gives a fiber sequence

$$
\Omega X\longrightarrow PX\longrightarrow X.
$$

Since $PX$ is contractible, it relates invariants of $X$ to those of its [loop space](loop-space.md).
