# Deep-water capillary-gravity dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Capillary wave](capillary-wave.md)

A harmonic [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) decaying with depth, together with linearized kinematic and Bernoulli [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) and [capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md), gives $\omega^2=gk+(T/\rho)k^3$. The corresponding [phase speed](phase-speed.md) combines long-wave [gravity](gravity.md) and short-wave capillarity contributions.
