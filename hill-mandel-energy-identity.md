# Hill-Mandel energy identity

↑ **Parent:** [Effective elastic energy](effective-elastic-energy.md)

For symmetric [stress](stress.md) in [static elastic equilibrium](static-elastic-equilibrium.md) without [body force](body-force.md), write $E=\langle\varepsilon\rangle$ and $S=\langle\sigma\rangle$. The difference between microscopic and macroscopic work is $|\Omega|^{-1}\int_{\partial\Omega}(\sigma-S)n\cdot(u-Ex)$. It vanishes for affine boundary [displacement](displacement.md), uniform boundary [traction](traction.md), or matching periodic [displacement](displacement.md) fluctuations and opposite [tractions](traction.md) on paired faces.
