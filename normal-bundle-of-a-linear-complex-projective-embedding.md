# Normal bundle of a linear complex-projective embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Normal bundle](normal-bundle.md)

Near a projective subspace, transverse directions are linear maps from the tautological line to the complementary coordinate vector space. This describes the actual complex [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md). The conjugate line and the dual line are complex-isomorphic using a [Hermitian metric](hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md); neither is the original tautological line as a complex bundle in general.
