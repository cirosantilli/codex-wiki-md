# Isentropic foehn drawdown

↑ **Parent:** [Foehn wind](foehn-wind.md)

Mountain blocking can prevent low-level upstream air from crossing the barrier while air from higher levels passes over and descends. In stable stratification that elevated source air has higher [potential temperature](potential-temperature.md); adiabatic descent can bring it into a lee valley warmer than the blocked low-level air. This source-air change permits a dry foehn without requiring condensation and rainout along the arriving parcel's path.
