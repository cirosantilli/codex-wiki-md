# Consolidated pancake ice

↑ **Parent:** [Pancake ice](pancake-ice.md)

[Consolidated pancake ice](consolidated-pancake-ice.md) forms when [pancake ice](pancake-ice.md) packs, rafts and freezes together with interstitial [frazil ice](frazil-ice.md). Its heterogeneous granular structure can retain stacked layers and acquire later columnar basal growth.
