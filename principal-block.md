# Principal block

↑ **Parent:** [Block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md)

The principal [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) is the unique [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) containing the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md). Its primitive [central idempotent](central-idempotent.md) is the unique one with augmentation $1$. Its [defect groups of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) are the [Sylow subgroups](sylow-subgroup.md) for the residue characteristic.
