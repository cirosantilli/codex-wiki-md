# Odd pair of Gaussian profiles under diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian initial data for the heat equation](gaussian-initial-data-for-the-heat-equation.md)

For $D,\gamma,a>0$, apply the [superposition principle](superposition-principle.md) to two translated solutions for [Gaussian initial data for the heat equation](gaussian-initial-data-for-the-heat-equation.md). The displayed solution of $u_t=Du_{xx}$ has [odd](odd-function.md) initial profile $A(e^{-\gamma(x-a)^2}-e^{-\gamma(x+a)^2})$. Its total spatial [integral](integral-split.md) is zero. For $A>0$, it is positive on $x>0$ and negative on $x<0$ at every $t\geq0$.

Writing it as $2As^{-1/2}e^{-\gamma(x^2+a^2)/s}\sinh(2\gamma ax/s)$ shows how cancellation affects its late-time shape. On the diffusive scale $x=O(\sqrt{s})$, its leading form is $4A\gamma ax s^{-3/2}e^{-\gamma x^2/s}$. Thus the two lobe maxima asymptotically lie at $x=\pm\sqrt{s/(2\gamma)}$, while their absolute heights decay as $s^{-1}$. Their centers are not fixed at the initial Gaussian centers. The first spatial moment remains $\int_{\mathbb R}xu(x,t)dx=2Aa\sqrt{\pi/\gamma}$, as follows directly from the [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) and translation.
