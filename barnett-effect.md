# Barnett effect

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Magnetization](magnetization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnett_effect)

The [Barnett effect](barnett-effect.md) is the change of a body's [magnetization](magnetization.md) caused by mechanical rotation. Turning the whole specimen can bias the internal [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) carrying its [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md), even with zero applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). It is the reciprocal physical question to the [Einstein-de Haas effect](einstein-de-haas-effect.md): impose rotation and measure [magnetization](magnetization.md), rather than change [magnetization](magnetization.md) and observe mechanical motion. The relation between the two becomes especially transparent when the same signed [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md) connects [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) and internal [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).

[Samuel Jackson Barnett](samuel-jackson-barnett.md) published [Magnetization by Rotation](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.6.239) on 1 October 1915 in Physical Review. The [Barnett rotation magnetometry experiment](barnett-rotation-magnetometry-experiment.md) below provides an inspected modern example. The important distinction is between an equivalent term in a [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) and the actual external [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) measured by a [magnetometer](magnetometer.md): the first biases the material; its resulting [magnetization](magnetization.md) produces the second.
