# Taubes nonvanishing theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Seiberg–Witten invariant of a four-manifold](seiberg-witten-invariant-of-a-four-manifold.md)

A closed [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) of real dimension four with $b_2^+>1$ has a nonzero [Seiberg–Witten invariant of a four-manifold](seiberg-witten-invariant-of-a-four-manifold.md) in its canonical structure, with value $\pm1$. The [positive index of the intersection form](positive-index-of-the-intersection-form.md) hypothesis avoids chamber dependence. This provides the [symplectic connected-sum obstruction](symplectic-connected-sum-obstruction.md).
