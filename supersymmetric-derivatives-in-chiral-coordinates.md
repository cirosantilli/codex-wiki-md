# Supersymmetric derivatives in chiral coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric covariant derivative](supersymmetric-covariant-derivative.md)

For [left Grassmann derivatives](left-grassmann-derivative.md) and $D_\alpha=\partial_\alpha+i(\sigma^\mu\bar\theta)_\alpha\partial_\mu$, $\bar D_{\dot\alpha}=-\bar\partial_{\dot\alpha}-i(\theta\sigma^\mu)_{\dot\alpha}\partial_\mu$, the coordinate $y^\mu=x^\mu+i\theta\sigma^\mu\bar\theta$ gives $D_\alpha=\partial_\alpha|_y+2i(\sigma^\mu\bar\theta)_\alpha\partial_{y^\mu}$ and $\bar D_{\dot\alpha}=-\bar\partial_{\dot\alpha}|_y$. The odd chain rule gives $\bar\partial_{\dot\alpha}(\theta\sigma^\mu\bar\theta)=-(\theta\sigma^\mu)_{\dot\alpha}$. A [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md) is consequently independent of $\bar\theta$ at fixed $y$.
