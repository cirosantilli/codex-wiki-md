# Network decentralisation

↑ **Parent:** [Internet architecture](internet-architecture.md)

[Network decentralisation](network-decentralisation.md) concerns the distribution of relevant control, authority or indispensable dependencies across independently operated participants. It is a property relative to a specified decision or service: distributed forwarding can coexist with centralised naming, credentials or application data. Counting physical [servers](network-server.md) is insufficient.
