# Hydrochloric acid

↑ **Parent:** [Strong acid](strong-acid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrochloric_acid)

[Hydrochloric acid](hydrochloric-acid.md) is an aqueous [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) of [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md). The [glycine titration](glycine-titration.md) model treats its dissociation into [hydronium](hydronium.md) and [chloride](chloride.md) as complete, retaining both [ions](ion.md) in the [electric charge](electric-charge.md) balance.
