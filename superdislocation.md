# Superdislocation

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

An order-preserving total [dislocation](dislocation.md) in an ordered crystal, often dissociated into smaller [dislocations](dislocation.md) separated by an [antiphase boundary](antiphase-boundary.md). Two matching $a\langle110\rangle/2$ [dislocations](dislocation.md) can restore order in the [gamma prime phase](gamma-prime-phase.md).
