# Down-and-out put

↑ **Parent:** [Down-and-out claim](down-and-out-claim.md)

A [European put option](european-put-option.md) that becomes worthless when its continuously monitored lower barrier is touched. The alive state has $s>B$ and no earlier hit. A terminal building block for the [Black-Scholes reflection symmetry](black-scholes-reflection-symmetry.md) is $F=C(K)-C(B)+(K-B)C_d(B)$ for $0<B<K$. Its terminal payoff is $(K-S)^+\mathbf1_{S\geq B}$. The alive price is $F(s,t)-(s/B)^{1-2r/\sigma^2}F(B^2/s,t)$ and is set to zero after any hit.
