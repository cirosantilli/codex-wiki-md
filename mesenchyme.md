# Mesenchyme

↑ **Parent:** [Developmental biology](developmental-biology.md)

A tissue organization with cells dispersed through [extracellular matrix](extracellular-matrix.md), rather than arranged as a continuous polarized [epithelium](epithelium.md). Mesenchymal populations differ in origin and developmental potential.
