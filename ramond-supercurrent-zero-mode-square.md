# Ramond supercurrent zero-mode square

↑ **Parent:** [N=1 super-Virasoro algebra](n-1-super-virasoro-algebra.md)

With the standard [string oscillator](string-oscillator.md) algebra and $G_0=\sum_m\alpha_m\cdot\psi_{-m}$, pairing positive and negative modes gives $G_0^2=\alpha_0^2/2+\sum_{m>0}(\alpha_{-m}\cdot\alpha_m+m\psi_{-m}\cdot\psi_m)$. This is the vacuum-normal-ordered [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md) generator $L_0^R$, so $\{G_0,G_0\}=2L_0^R$. Rescaling the [superconformal current](superconformal-current.md) rescales its square quadratically; its normalization cannot be ignored when comparing it with a fixed [Virasoro generator](virasoro-generator.md).
