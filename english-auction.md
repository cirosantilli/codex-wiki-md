# English auction

↑ **Parent:** [Auction](auction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_auction)

In an ideal continuous-price auction with independent private values, bidders remain active up to their valuations. Without a reserve, the last remaining bidder wins at the second-highest valuation. With a [reserve price](reserve-price.md) $r$, there is no sale if every valuation is below $r$; otherwise the winner pays the larger of $r$ and the second-highest valuation. Strict versus weak inequalities at the reserve have no revenue effect for continuous valuation distributions. This payment rule is distinct from a [first-price sealed-bid auction](first-price-sealed-bid-auction.md).
