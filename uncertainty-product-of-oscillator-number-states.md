# Uncertainty product of oscillator number states

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

An [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) has $\langle x\rangle=\langle p\rangle=0$, $\langle x^2\rangle=\hbar(n+1/2)/(m\omega)$ and $\langle p^2\rangle=m\hbar\omega(n+1/2)$. Thus $\Delta x\Delta p=\hbar(n+1/2)$; only the ground state in this sequence minimizes the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md).
