<h1 id="balister-bollobas-entropy-inequality">Balister–Bollobás entropy inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Compression of an entropy sum](compression-of-an-entropy-sum.md)

Let $\mathcal A$ be a finite multiset of subsets and let $r_i$ count its occurrences of coordinate $i$. Its nested compression consists of $L_j=\{i:r_i\geq j\}$. Then $\sum_jH(X_{L_j})\leq\sum_{A\in\mathcal A}H(X_A)$, by repeated [entropy submodularity](entropy-submodularity.md). This is the compression formulation in [Balister and Bollobás, "Projections, Entropy and Sumsets"](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.1151).
