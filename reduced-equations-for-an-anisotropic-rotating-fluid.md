# Reduced equations for an anisotropic rotating fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating fluid](rotating-fluid.md)

Under [critical balance in a rapidly rotating fluid](critical-balance-in-a-rapidly-rotating-fluid.md), define $\mathbf u_\perp^{(0)}=\widehat{\mathbf z}\times\nabla_\perp\Phi$ and $D_\perp=\partial_t+\{\Phi,\cdot\}$. Leading horizontal [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) gives zero divergence, but at the next order $\nabla_\perp\cdot\mathbf u_\perp^{(1)}=-\partial_z w$. Its [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) contribution survives in the vertical [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) equation, producing the displayed coupled system. The vertical momentum equation uses the leading [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p_0=2\Omega\Phi$. A flow that stays purely horizontal is independent of $z$ in this reduced system; the assertion is not an exact theorem for arbitrary fast solutions of the full rotating Euler equations.
