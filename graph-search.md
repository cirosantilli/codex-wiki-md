# Graph search

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

A [graph search](graph-search.md) explores vertices through incident edges while recording visited vertices. On a finite [graph](graph-split.md), depth-first or breadth-first exploration terminates and decides [reachability](reachability-in-a-graph.md); recording predecessors also gives a path. Restricting to vertices reachable from a start and able to reach an accepting vertex identifies the productive part of a [deterministic finite automaton](deterministic-finite-automaton.md).
