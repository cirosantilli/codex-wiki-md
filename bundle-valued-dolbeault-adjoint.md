# Bundle-valued Dolbeault adjoint

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md)

Using the [bundle-valued conjugate-linear Hodge star](bundle-valued-conjugate-linear-hodge-star.md), the [formal adjoint](formal-adjoint.md) of the holomorphic bundle's [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md) is $\bar\partial_E^*=-*_{E^*}\bar\partial_{E^*}*_E$. It lowers antiholomorphic degree. Stokes applied to the coefficient-contracted form $\psi\wedge *_E\eta$ proves the integrated adjoint identity on a compact [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md).
