# Monitor (synchronization)

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronization (computing)](synchronization-computing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monitor_(synchronization))

A construct combining [mutual exclusion](mutual-exclusion.md) for operations on shared state with condition-based waiting. A waiting caller releases the [monitor](monitor-synchronization.md) while asleep and must recheck its predicate after regaining access.
