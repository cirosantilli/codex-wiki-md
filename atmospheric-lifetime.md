# Atmospheric lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

For a well-mixed atmospheric burden $B$ removed by a single first-order sink, $dB/dt=-B/\tau$, the [atmospheric lifetime](atmospheric-lifetime.md) is the [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) time $\tau$. At steady emission rate $E$, this model gives $B=E\tau$. Independent sinks acting on the same burden have additive inverse lifetimes. Local removal times in separate compartments cannot be combined this way without considering exchange and partitioning. A multi-reservoir perturbation, especially atmospheric [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md), generally needs more than one timescale.
