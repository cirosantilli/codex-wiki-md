# Electrical resistance and conductance

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_resistance_and_conductance)

For an ohmic element, electrical resistance is $R=V/I$ and conductance is its reciprocal $G=I/V$. They describe the same voltage-current response using reciprocal units. The relation $V=IR$ is [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md).
