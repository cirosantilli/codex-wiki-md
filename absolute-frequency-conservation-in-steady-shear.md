# Absolute-frequency conservation in steady shear

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute frequency](absolute-frequency.md)

A time-independent ray Hamiltonian conserves the stationary-observer [absolute frequency](absolute-frequency.md). A horizontally uniform background also conserves horizontal wavenumber. The [intrinsic frequency](intrinsic-frequency.md) $\widehat\omega=\omega-kU(z)$ nevertheless changes when the ray moves through shear. These follow from the [Hamiltonian ray-tracing equations](hamiltonian-ray-tracing-equations.md) and are essential to identifying a [critical level of an internal gravity wave](critical-level-of-an-internal-gravity-wave.md).
