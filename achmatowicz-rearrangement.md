# Achmatowicz rearrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Organic chemistry](organic-chemistry.md)

Oxidative conversion of a furfuryl alcohol into a six-membered oxygen heterocycle, often a hydroxypyranone. Oxidation, furan opening and intramolecular [lactol](lactol.md) formation explain the ring expansion.
