# Passive scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar transport](scalar-transport.md)

A [passive scalar](passive-scalar.md) is an advected and diffusing [scalar field](scalar-field.md) whose value has no dynamical effect on the transporting [velocity field](velocity-field.md). Concentration of a dilute nonreacting dye is a common example. Its transport can depend on the flow while the flow equations remain independent of it.
