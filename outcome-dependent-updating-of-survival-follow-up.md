# Outcome-dependent updating of survival follow-up

↑ **Parent:** [Informative censoring](informative-censoring.md)

If a cohort is periodically confirmed event-free but exact event reports update only those who fail, censoring the remaining subjects at their last scheduled confirmation creates [informative censoring](informative-censoring.md). Between confirmations the nominal [risk set](risk-set.md) retains future observed failures and removes nonfailures. An apparent final failure may therefore empty the recorded risk set even though other cohort members remain event-free. Use a common verified cutoff or a complete event register with a common [administrative censoring](administrative-censoring.md) date.
