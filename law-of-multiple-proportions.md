# Law of multiple proportions

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic theory](atomic-theory.md)

For simple [chemical compounds](chemical-compound.md) of two [chemical elements](chemical-element.md), the masses of one combining with a fixed [mass](mass.md) of the other occur in ratios of small integers.
