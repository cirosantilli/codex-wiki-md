# Recurrent fibroid tumour

↑ **Parent:** [Tumour](tumour.md)

A nineteenth-century descriptive category of recurrent fibrous or fibroplastic growths, some showing destructive behaviour or [metastasis](metastasis.md). It is not equivalent to a benign uterine fibroid.
