# Chomp

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorial game theory](combinatorial-game-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chomp)

An alternating grid-removal game in which choosing an occupied cell removes it and every occupied cell above and to its right. The player who removes the designated lower-left poison cell loses. Reachable occupied-cell [sets](set-split.md) are [order ideals](order-ideal.md) for coordinatewise order. A nonempty reachable position always contains the poison cell, and removing that cell empties the board. The [monotone boundary representation of a grid order ideal](monotone-boundary-representation-of-a-grid-order-ideal.md) supports compact board updates.
