# Laplacian at a local maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md)

If a twice differentiable real function has a local maximum at an interior point of a Riemannian manifold, its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is negative semidefinite there and hence its [Laplacian](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) is nonpositive.
