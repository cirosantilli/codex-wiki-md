# Eigenspace invariance for commuting linear maps

↑ **Parent:** [Commutator](commutator.md)

If two [linear maps](linear-map.md) commute and $Av=\lambda v$, then $A(Bv)=B(Av)=\lambda Bv$. Hence $B$ preserves each [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of $A$. On a one-dimensional [eigenspace](eigenspace.md), $Bv$ is a scalar multiple of $v$, so each [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) for a [simple eigenvalue](simple-eigenvalue.md) of $A$ is also an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of $B$. For repeated [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) the invariant [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) can have higher [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md); arbitrary [vectors](vector.md) in it need not individually be [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) of $B$.
