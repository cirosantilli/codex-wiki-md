# Metric compatibility

↑ **Parent:** [Metric connection](metric-connection.md)

A [metric connection](metric-connection.md) satisfies [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md), $\nabla_\alpha g_{\mu\nu}=0$. This allows the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) to commute with [covariant derivatives](covariant-derivative.md) and index raising or lowering.
