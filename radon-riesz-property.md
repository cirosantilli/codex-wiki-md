# Radon-Riesz property

↑ **Parent:** [Weak convergence](weak-convergence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon–Riesz_property)

A [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) has the Radon-Riesz property when [weak convergence](weak-convergence.md) $u_k\rightharpoonup u$ together with $\|u_k\|\to\|u\|$ implies [strong convergence](norm-convergence.md) $u_k\to u$. The Hilbert-space case is the [Radon-Riesz theorem](radon-riesz-theorem.md). The condition prevents norm from remaining in weakly invisible directions.
