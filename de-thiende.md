# De Thiende

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Thiende)

[Simon Stevin](simon-stevin.md)'s [De Thiende](de-thiende.md), published in 1585, presents calculation with [decimal](decimal.md) [fractions](fraction.md) for practical users. Its notation explicitly labels units, tenths, hundredths, and smaller decimal places; it does not use our modern decimal point. This is a historical example of changing representation to make the same [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) operations easier to organize.
