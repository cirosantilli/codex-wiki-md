# Supersymmetric relic abundance requires additional assumptions

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md)

Supersymmetry alone does not guarantee stable neutral dark matter. Stability, mass ordering, production history and annihilation dynamics must be specified; conserved R-parity supplies one common stability mechanism.
