# Relative-entropy barycenter of a quantum ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Donald's identity](donald-s-identity.md)

The ensemble average $\overline\omega$ uniquely minimizes $\sum_jp_jD(\omega_j\|\rho)$ over [density operators](density-matrix.md) $\rho$. [Donald's identity](donald-s-identity.md) separates this objective into a constant plus $D(\overline\omega\|\rho)$, and [nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy](nonnegativity-of-quantum-relative-entropy.md) proves the claim. The minimum is the [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md) $S(\overline\omega)-\sum_jp_jS(\omega_j)$, equivalently the [quantum mutual information](quantum-mutual-information.md) of the associated [classical-quantum state](classical-quantum-state.md).
