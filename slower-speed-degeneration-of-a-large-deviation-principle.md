# Slower-speed degeneration of a large deviation principle

↑ **Parent:** [Large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md)

Suppose a [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) at speed $a_n$ has a [good rate function](good-rate-function.md) with unique zero mu. At any slower speed $b_n\to\infty$ with $a_n/b_n\to\infty$, its rate is zero at mu and infinity elsewhere. On a [closed set](closed-set.md) excluding mu, the positive original rate gives decay faster than every exponential at speed $b_n$. Neighborhoods of mu have probability tending to one, giving the slower-speed open-set lower bound. The new rate is good because its finite [sublevel set](sublevel-set.md) is the singleton mu.
