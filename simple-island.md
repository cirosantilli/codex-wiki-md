# Simple island

↑ **Parent:** [Island of a meromorphic function](island-of-a-meromorphic-function.md)

A simple island is an [island of a meromorphic function](island-of-a-meromorphic-function.md) of mapping degree one. It is mapped [biholomorphically](biholomorphism.md) onto the target [Jordan domain](jordan-domain.md). This is stronger than the source island being simply connected.
