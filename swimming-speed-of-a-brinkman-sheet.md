# Swimming speed of a Brinkman sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Brinkman swimming sheet](brinkman-swimming-sheet.md)

The leading fixed-stroke speed of a transverse [Brinkman swimming sheet](brinkman-swimming-sheet.md) is enhanced over the pure-fluid value by $\sqrt{1+(\alpha/k)^2}$. It follows from the mean displaced-boundary [velocity](velocity.md) and zero mean traction. The expansion is at fixed screening parameter and does not assert an enhancement at fixed available motor power.
