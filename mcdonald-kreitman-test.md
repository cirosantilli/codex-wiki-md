# McDonald-Kreitman test

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)

A comparison of nonsynonymous and synonymous polymorphism and divergence. Under a simple neutral model their ratios match. An excess of nonsynonymous divergence can support adaptive fixation, but mildly deleterious polymorphism, changing demography and site-class assumptions affect interpretation.[Original polymorphism-divergence test](adaptive-protein-evolution-at-the-adh-locus-in-drosophila-by-john-h-mcdonald-and-martin-kreitman.md)
