# Boundary of a domain

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary of a set](boundary-of-a-set.md)

The [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of a planar [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) $D$ is its [boundary of a set](boundary-of-a-set.md). It is distinct from the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of a parametrizing interval: a curve can begin on $\partial D$ while all its positive-time points lie inside $D$.
