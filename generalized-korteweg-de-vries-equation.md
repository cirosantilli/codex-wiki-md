<h1 id="generalized-korteweg-de-vries-equation">Generalized Korteweg–De Vries equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear analysis](nonlinear-analysis-split.md)

The generalized Korteweg–De Vries equation is the [nonlinear partial differential equation](nonlinear-partial-differential-equation.md)

$$
u_t+\partial_x(u_{xx}+u^p)=0.
$$

The cases below the exponent $p=5$ are energy-subcritical in $H^1(\mathbb R)$.
