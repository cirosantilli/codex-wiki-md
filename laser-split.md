# Laser

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser)

A laser generates amplified [light](light.md) through [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md). Its name abbreviates “light amplification by stimulated emission of radiation”. Lasers supply the intense beams used in [optical tweezers](optical-tweezers.md); [optical coherence](optical-coherence.md) also makes them useful for [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) measurements. [OpenStax University Physics 3, §8.6: lasers](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/8-6-lasers).

In the usual material picture, a gain medium stores [energy](energy-split.md) supplied by pumping, for example by incident [light](light.md) or an [electric current](electric-current.md). [Stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) transfers that stored [energy](energy-split.md) to a selected optical [normal mode](normal-mode.md), while competing [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) removes [light](light.md). Producing net [optical gain](optical-gain.md) requires a suitable population distribution, such as [population inversion](population-inversion.md) in the simple model below.

A familiar [laser](laser-split.md) oscillator uses mirrors to return [light](light.md) through the gain medium. This optical feedback favours resonant [normal modes](normal-mode.md); a partly transmitting mirror lets useful [power](power.md) escape. Feedback and mode selection have different implementations, including ring arrangements: every [laser](laser-split.md) need not resemble the same two-mirror device. The [laser threshold](laser-threshold.md) example treats just that device. [Shaoul Ezekiel, MIT, Laser fundamentals I: Simple laser, transcript](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-006-video-demonstrations-in-lasers-and-optics-spring-2008/37b4c2aa5a6e3e0c119688024ce90ce5_Iqp7NxnwaGY.pdf) [Franz Kärtner, MIT 6.974, Chapter 7, §7.1: laser oscillator concept](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-974-fundamentals-of-photonics-quantum-electronics-spring-2006/18f30fad63a62ef4dd894d3752b55a60_chapter7.pdf).

The useful combination is concentrated [power](power.md) and controlled optical [normal modes](normal-mode.md). It gives [optical tweezers](optical-tweezers.md) a localized source of [force](force-split.md) and [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) measurements a source with useful [optical coherence](optical-coherence.md). A [laser](laser-split.md) can nevertheless emit several [frequencies](frequency.md); “laser” alone does not guarantee a single [frequency](frequency.md). [Shaoul Ezekiel, MIT, Laser fundamentals I: Spectrum of laser light, transcript](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-006-video-demonstrations-in-lasers-and-optics-spring-2008/674c0c322cf0d87072e31de3e51f8e27_--Zi_cn4kPE.pdf).

An original experimental landmark is T. H. Maiman's 1960 report of stimulated optical radiation from optically pumped ruby at about $694.3\,\mathrm{nm}$. His paper describes both the pumping path and the observed emission, connecting stored material [energy](energy-split.md) with amplified [light](light.md). [T. H. Maiman, Stimulated Optical Radiation in Ruby, Nature 187, 493–494, 1960, original paper](stimulated-optical-radiation-in-ruby-by-theodore-harold-maiman.md).
