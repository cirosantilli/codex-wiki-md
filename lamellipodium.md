# Lamellipodium

↑ **Parent:** [Cell migration](cell-migration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamellipodium)

A [lamellipodium](lamellipodium.md) is a broad, thin, [actin](actin.md)-rich protrusion at a cell edge. [Actin](actin.md) polymerization pushes the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md), but net advance also depends on mechanical coupling to the environment.
