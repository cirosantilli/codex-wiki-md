# Normalization of an integral scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Normal scheme](normal-scheme.md)

For an [integral scheme](integral-scheme.md), take on each [affine chart](affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) the [integral closure](integral-closure.md) of its coordinate [ring](ring.md) in its [function field](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md). [Integral closure](integral-closure.md) commutes with [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md), so these [rings](ring.md) glue to a [normal scheme](normal-scheme.md) with an integral birational morphism to the original [scheme](scheme.md). This is the normalization. It need not be finite without additional hypotheses; for an algebraic curve of finite type over a [field](field.md) it is finite. It is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) exactly when the original [scheme](scheme.md) is normal.
