# Geodesic coordinate grid

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md)

For a surface metric $E\,du^2+2F\,du\,dv+G\,dv^2$, all coordinate curves are unparametrized [geodesics](geodesic.md) precisely when the displayed identities hold. Parametrize $u=c$ by [arc length](arc-length.md), so $\dot v=G^{-1/2}$, and substitute into the transverse [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) for [energy of a curve](energy-of-a-curve.md). This gives the first identity; $v=d$ gives the second. These identities concern geodesic images and do not assert that the coordinate variable itself is an affine parameter.
