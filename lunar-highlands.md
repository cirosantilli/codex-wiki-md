# Lunar highlands

↑ **Parent:** [Moon](moon.md)

The old, commonly bright and heavily cratered lunar terrains whose crust is rich in feldspathic material, including [anorthosite](anorthosite.md). They occur on both lunar hemispheres, although the [far side of the Moon](far-side-of-the-moon.md) has more extensive highlands and generally thicker crust. Their ancient crustal formation must be distinguished from later impact modification.
