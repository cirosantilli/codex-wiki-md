# Distance modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomical magnitude](astronomical-magnitude.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distance_modulus)

The distance modulus is the difference between [apparent magnitude](apparent-magnitude.md) and [absolute magnitude](absolute-magnitude.md). For distance $d$ in parsecs,

$$
\mu=m-M=5\log_{10}(d/10\,\mathrm{pc}).
$$
