# Quantum harmonic chain

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic chain](harmonic-chain.md)

A [quantum harmonic chain](quantum-harmonic-chain.md) has quadratic couplings between canonical coordinates and momenta. A [normal mode](normal-mode.md) transformation turns it into independent [quantum harmonic oscillators](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md). A continuum elastic [action](action.md) with [time](time-in-physics.md) coefficient $\chi$ and spatial stiffness $\kappa$ has long-wave [frequency](frequency.md) $\omega^2=(\kappa/\chi)q^2$, the structure of a smooth condensate phase [action](action.md).
