<h1 id="absorbing-fabry-perot-mirror-phase-and-transmission">Absorbing Fabry–Pérot mirror phase and transmission</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fabry–Pérot interferometer](fabry-perot-interferometer.md)

For identical mirrors with power reflectivity $\mathcal R$, transmission $\mathcal T$ and absorption $1-\mathcal R-\mathcal T$, summing round trips at [normal incidence](normal-incidence.md) gives

$$
\frac{I_t}{I_0}=\frac{\mathcal T^2}{(1-\mathcal R)^2+4\mathcal R\sin^2(\delta/2)}.
$$

Here $\delta$ includes propagation and both internal reflection phases. [Optical absorption](optical-absorption.md) reduces the resonant peak below unity, while a nonzero reflection phase shifts the resonance condition $\delta=2\pi m$. Frequency-dependent mirror phase and [refractive index](refractive-index.md) can also change the [free spectral range](free-spectral-range.md).
