# Wittig reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Olefination](olefination.md)

Reaction of a [phosphorus ylide](phosphorus-ylide.md) with a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) compound to form an alkene. The [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](carbon.md) and ylidic [carbon](carbon.md) become the two alkene atoms; the [oxygen](oxygen.md) is transferred to phosphorus.
