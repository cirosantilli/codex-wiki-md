# Neuroplasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscience](neuroscience-split.md)

Changes in the functional organization, connectivity or physiological operation of neural systems with experience, altered input or development. Rapid unmasking and slower structural reorganization are different candidate mechanisms; an imaging activation change alone does not distinguish them.
