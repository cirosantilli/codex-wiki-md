# Cohomological equation on a Diophantine torus

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

For a [Diophantine frequency vector](diophantine-frequency-vector.md), the equation $\omega\cdot\partial_x f=g$ on a periodic [torus](torus.md) has an analytic solution on each smaller complex strip exactly when $g$ has zero average. Its nonzero [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) are $f_k=g_k/(i k\cdot\omega)$, and its zero coefficient is arbitrary. Thus the normalized zero-mean solution is unique.
