# Black-hole spin-up by coherent accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole spin](black-hole-spin.md)

Coherent prograde gas accretion increases the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) of a [black hole](black-hole.md). For a zero-torque thin disc, the rest mass $dm_0$ entering from the [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md) changes the hole by $dM=E_{\rm ISCO}dm_0$ and $dJ=\ell_{\rm ISCO}dm_0$. Its dimensionless spin obeys $da_*/d\log M=c\ell_{\rm ISCO}/(GME_{\rm ISCO})-2a_*$. Starting from zero spin and neglecting captured radiation, the formal extremal endpoint has $M_f=\sqrt6M_i$ and requires rest-mass supply approximately $1.8464M_i$. Photon capture leads instead to the [Thorne spin limit](thorne-spin-limit.md).
