# Sigma baryon

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_baryon)

The [baryon octet](baryon-octet.md) contains an [isospin](isospin.md) triplet $\Sigma^+,\Sigma^0,\Sigma^-$. Each has [strangeness](strangeness.md) $-1$; their valence [quark](quark.md) contents are $uus$, $uds$, $dds$.
