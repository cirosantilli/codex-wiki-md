<h1 id="equal-density-ion-neutral-alfven-dispersion-relation">Equal-density ion-neutral Alfvén dispersion relation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ambipolar damping](ambipolar-damping.md)

For equal [ion](ion.md) and neutral mass densities, let $\mu=\mu_{in}=\mu_{ni}$, $a=k_\parallel B_0/\sqrt{4\pi\rho_i}$ and use Fourier time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$. [Solenoidal](solenoidal-vector-field.md) [displacements](displacement.md) obey $\ddot\xi_i=-a^2\xi_i-\mu(\dot\xi_i-\dot\xi_n)$ and $\ddot\xi_n=-\mu(\dot\xi_n-\dot\xi_i)$. For nonzero [frequency](frequency.md), the neutral equation gives $\xi_i=(1-i\omega/\mu)\xi_n$. Substitution in the [ion](ion.md) equation gives

$$
\omega^3+2i\mu\omega^2-a^2\omega-i\mu a^2=0.
$$

Equivalently, the [growth rate](growth-rate.md) $s=-i\omega$ obeys $s^3+2\mu s^2+a^2s+\mu a^2=0$. A time-independent neutral [displacement](displacement.md) with zero [velocity](velocity.md) is a relabeling of a uniform fluid, not an additional [wave](wave.md) root of the [velocity](velocity.md)/magnetic-field system.
