# Plastid-encoded RNA polymerase

↑ **Parent:** [Chloroplast gene expression](chloroplast-gene-expression.md)

A bacterial-type plastid [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md) with core subunits encoded by the plastid [genome](genome.md). Nuclear-encoded [sigma factors](sigma-factor.md) and associated components mean that its name does not imply autonomous regulation.
