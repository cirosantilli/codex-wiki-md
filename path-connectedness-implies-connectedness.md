# Path-connectedness implies connectedness

↑ **Parent:** [Path-connected space](path-connected-space.md)

If $X$ had a separation into two disjoint nonempty open subsets, a [continuous path](continuous-path.md) joining points in the two subsets would pull it back to a separation of $[0,1]$. This contradicts [connectedness of an interval](connectedness-of-an-interval.md). Thus every [path-connected space](path-connected-space.md) is [connected](connected-space.md). The converse fails, for example for the [topologist's sine curve](topologist-s-sine-curve.md) with its limiting vertical segment.
