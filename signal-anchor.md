# Signal anchor

↑ **Parent:** [Protein targeting](protein-targeting.md)

A [signal anchor](signal-anchor.md) combines targeting or initiation of membrane passage with a retained membrane-spanning segment. Its orientation determines which flanking part enters the [ER lumen](er-lumen.md); a single internal signal can yield either orientation.[Shao and Hegde, 2011; “Cotranslational Membrane Protein Topogenesis”, Figure 5](membrane-protein-insertion-at-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-by-sichen-shao-and-ramanujan-s-hegde.md)

In the figure's stipulated internal-anchor example, the [N-terminus](n-terminus.md) remains in the [cytosol](cytosol.md) and the [C-terminus](c-terminus.md) enters the [ER lumen](er-lumen.md). Reversing that orientation would reverse which end is protected from an externally added [protease](protease.md). Merely counting one membrane-spanning segment cannot choose between the two.
