# 4f-Virtual-Bound-State Formation in CeAl3 at Low Temperatures by Klaus Andres and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Klaus Andres](work-by-klaus-andres.md)

The original [CeAl3](ceal3.md) measurements report the low-temperature electronic heat-capacity and quadratic-resistivity coefficients underlying its identification as a [heavy fermion material](heavy-fermion-material.md). The published abstract gives both values and the measurement range below $0.2\,\mathrm K$.

Publisher: [https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.35.1779](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.35.1779)
