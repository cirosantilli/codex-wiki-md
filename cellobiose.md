# Cellobiose

↑ **Parent:** [Disaccharide](disaccharide.md)

[cellobiose](cellobiose.md) consists of two [glucose](glucose.md) units joined by a beta-1,4 linkage. It is a characteristic soluble product in [cellulose](cellulose.md) degradation.
