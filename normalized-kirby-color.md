# Normalized Kirby color

↑ **Parent:** [Kirby color](kirby-color.md)

The [Kirby color](kirby-color.md) divided by the [total quantum dimension](total-quantum-dimension.md). A zero-framed closed loop with this color evaluates to $\mathcal D$ in the [isotopy normalization of anyon diagrams](isotopy-normalization-of-anyon-diagrams.md). An encircling loop acts on a simple charge $x$ by $\mathcal D\delta_{x1}$, the [Kirby killing property](kirby-killing-property.md). Normalization must be tracked explicitly when using a surgery formula.
