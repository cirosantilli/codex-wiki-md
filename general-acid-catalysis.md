# General acid catalysis

↑ **Parent:** [Catalysis](catalysis.md)

[General acid catalysis](general-acid-catalysis.md) uses [proton](proton.md) donation from an acid participating in the rate-influencing reaction pathway. It can activate an electrophile or assist protonation of a departing or newly formed group.
