<h1 id="subordination-of-a-levy-process">Subordination of a Lévy process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy process](levy-process.md)

If $X$ is a [Lévy process](levy-process.md) and $T$ an independent [subordinator](subordinator.md), then $Y_t=X_{T_t}$ is a [Lévy process](levy-process.md). Conditional on the clock, increments of $X$ over disjoint clock intervals are independent; averaging over the independent [stationary increments](stationary-increments.md) of the clock gives the same properties for $Y$. If $\mathbb Ee^{iuX_s}=e^{-s\Psi(u)}$, its [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) is $\mathbb E e^{-T_t\Psi(u)}$. For standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), $\Psi(u)=u^2/2$, so this is the clock's [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) at $u^2/2$.
