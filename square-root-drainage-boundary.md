# Square-root drainage boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Boussinesq equation for an unconfined aquifer](boussinesq-equation-for-an-unconfined-aquifer.md)

A zero-height outlet can carry finite nonzero [volume flux per unit width](volume-flux-per-unit-width.md) in a nonlinear [Darcy flow](darcy-flow.md) model with $q=-Khh_x$. If $hh_x\to J>0$ as $x\downarrow0$, then $(h^2)'\to2J$, and [integration](integral-split.md) gives $h\sim\sqrt{2Jx}$ and $h_x\sim\sqrt{J/(2x)}$. Thus the height vanishes while its slope diverges; the flux must be evaluated as a limit of the product. The divergent slope also identifies a region where the outer [Dupuit approximation](dupuit-approximation.md) eventually needs a local correction.
