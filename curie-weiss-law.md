<h1 id="curie-weiss-law">Curie–Weiss law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curie–Weiss_law)

The [Curie–Weiss law](curie-weiss-law.md) approximates the [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md) of interacting localized moments by $\chi=C/(T-\Theta)$ in a suitable paramagnetic temperature range. A positive Weiss temperature often reflects predominantly ferromagnetic interactions, while a negative value often reflects antiferromagnetic interactions. It is not a universal exact law through an ordering transition, and crystal-field effects or itinerant carriers can require another description.
