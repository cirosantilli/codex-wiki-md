# Invariance from integral duality for a measure-preserving transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Measure-preserving transformation](measure-preserving-transformation.md)

Let $T$ be a [measure-preserving transformation](measure-preserving-transformation.md) of a [probability space](probability-space-split.md) and $X$ an [integrable random variable](integrable-random-variable.md). If the displayed duality holds for every bounded [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $Z$, test it on [indicator functions](indicator-function.md) to identify $\mathbb E[X\mid T^{-1}(\mathcal F)]=X\circ T$. The [measure-preserving transformation](measure-preserving-transformation.md) gives the same [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) to $X$ and $X\circ T$. The principle of [conditional expectation preserving a distribution](conditional-expectation-preserving-a-distribution.md) then forces equality [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). This does not require an inverse for $T$ or require $X$ to be [square-integrable](square-integrable-function.md).
