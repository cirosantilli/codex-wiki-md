# Mass-loading power at fixed velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Variable-mass system](variable-mass-system.md)

A body moving at constant [velocity](velocity.md) $V$ incorporates material at rate $\dot M$ with zero initial [momentum](momentum-split.md) along its motion. The required external [force](force-split.md) is $F=\dot M V$, so the supplied [power](power.md) is $P=\dot M V^2$. Only $\dot M V^2/2$ becomes the added material's translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md); the other half is dissipated during inelastic incorporation. This conclusion concerns the component along the body's motion; an incoming transverse [velocity](velocity.md) can add further dissipation.
