# Pancake ice

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pancake_ice)

Pancake ice consists of approximately disk-shaped young [ice floes](ice-floe.md), often with raised edges formed by collisions. Wave-driven collisions and motion in the surrounding [frazil ice](frazil-ice.md) can dissipate energy.
