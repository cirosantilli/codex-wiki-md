# Almost-everywhere limit under a uniform L1 bound

↑ **Parent:** [Fatou's lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md)

Let $g_n$ be [Lebesgue measurable functions](lebesgue-measurable-function.md) on a [Lebesgue measurable set](lebesgue-measurable-set.md) $E$, converging [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) to $g$, with $\sup_n\int_E|g_n|<\infty$. The limit has a measurable representative: use $\limsup_ng_n$ off the exceptional [null set](null-set.md) and zero on it. The [completeness of Lebesgue measure](completeness-of-lebesgue-measure.md) makes any alternative values on that null set measurable as well. [Fatou's lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) applied to $|g_n|$ gives the displayed bound, so $g$ is finite [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) and [Lebesgue integrable](lebesgue-integrable-function.md). A uniform [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) bound alone does not imply convergence in [L1 space](l1-space.md); the [strict inequality in Fatou lemma](strict-inequality-in-fatou-lemma.md) supplies a shrinking-spike example.
