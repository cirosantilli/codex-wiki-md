# Index notation

↑ **Parent:** [Multilinear algebra](multilinear-algebra-split.md)

Index notation describes [vectors](vector.md), [matrices](matrix.md) and [tensors](tensor.md) by their components relative to a chosen [basis](basis.md). For example, [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md) reads $(AB)_{ij}=\sum_kA_{ik}B_{kj}$. The free indices $i,j$ identify the resulting component, while $k$ is summed. An explicit summation sign avoids depending on an implicit [Einstein summation convention](einstein-notation.md).
