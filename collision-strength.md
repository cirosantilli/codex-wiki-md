# Collision strength

↑ **Parent:** [Collisional excitation](collisional-excitation.md)

A dimensionless atomic collision measure used to parameterize electron-impact transition coefficients. In the Maxwellian approximation used here, $q_{ji}=8.6\times10^{-12}\Omega_{ji}/(g_j\sqrt{T_e})$ in $\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-1}}$, and [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) fixes the excitation coefficient. Its value and [temperature](temperature-split.md) dependence encode atomic scattering physics beyond the elementary [Boltzmann factor](boltzmann-factor.md).
