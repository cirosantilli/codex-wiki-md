# Gamma matrix adjoint and transpose identities

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md)

In the standard [Dirac representation of the gamma matrices](dirac-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md), $(\gamma^0)^\dagger=\gamma^0$ and $(\gamma^i)^\dagger=-\gamma^i$, so $\gamma^{a\dagger}=\gamma^0\gamma^a\gamma^0$. The transpose signs in that representation are positive for $a=0,2$ and negative for $a=1,3$. The [charge-conjugation matrix](charge-conjugation-matrix.md) expresses the basis-independent intertwining relation $C^{-1}\gamma^aC=-(\gamma^a)^T$. Under an arbitrary nonunitary [similarity transformation](similarity-transformation.md), the [Hermitizing matrix](hermitizing-matrix.md) changes by inverse Hermitian congruence; it need not equal the transformed temporal [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md).
