# Trace-determinant stability criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability](linear-stability.md)

For a real $2\times2$ [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) $J$, both [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) have negative [real part](real-part.md) exactly when

$$
\operatorname{tr}J<0
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
\det J>0.
$$

The [linearization stability theorem](linearization-stability-theorem.md) then makes a hyperbolic equilibrium locally [asymptotically stable](asymptotic-stability.md).
