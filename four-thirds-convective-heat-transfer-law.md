# Four-thirds convective heat-transfer law

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

A high-[Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md) closure $\mathrm{Nu}=C\mathrm{Ra}^{1/3}$ gives $q=Ck_T[g\beta/(\nu\kappa)]^{1/3}(\Delta T)^{4/3}$, independent of layer depth. It assumes boundary-layer-controlled [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md), a well-mixed interior, fixed material properties and a fixed [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) regime. It is a model, not a universal asymptotic law for every turbulent convection regime or for an impulsive initial conductive transient.
