# Hamiltonian Liouville equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory.md)

Transport a phase-space density along [Hamilton's equations](hamilton-s-equations.md). The [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) is $b=(\nabla_vH,-\nabla_xH)$ and the equation is $\partial_tf+b\cdot\nabla_{x,v}f=h$. Its zero [divergence](divergence.md) gives volume-preserving transport. For $h=0$, the density is constant along characteristics; a source is integrated by the [Duhamel formula for Hamiltonian transport](duhamel-formula-for-hamiltonian-transport.md). This is distinct from the elliptic Liouville equation.
