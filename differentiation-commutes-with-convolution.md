# Differentiation commutes with convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Convolution](convolution.md)

For a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) and a compactly supported smooth kernel, differentiation with constant coefficients commutes with [convolution](convolution.md) wherever the kernel support stays inside the domain. The identity follows by testing against the translated kernel and tracking the sign of its derivative. A variable coefficient must remain inside the product before [convolution](convolution.md); in general $(bu)_\sigma\ne bu_\sigma$.
