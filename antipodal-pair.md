# Antipodal pair

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

On a [sphere](sphere.md) centred at the origin, an antipodal pair is the unordered pair $\{x,-x\}$. The [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) exchanges its two points.
