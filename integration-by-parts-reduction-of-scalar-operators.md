# Integration-by-parts reduction of scalar operators

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension-six operators of a real scalar field](dimension-six-operators-of-a-real-scalar-field.md)

Two [Lagrangian densities](lagrangian-density.md) differing by a [total derivative](total-derivative.md) yield the same [action](action.md) under suitable [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). For example $\partial_\mu(\phi^3\partial^\mu\phi)=3\phi^2(\partial\phi)^2+\phi^3\Box\phi$. Likewise $\phi\Box^2\phi\simeq-\partial\phi\cdot\partial\Box\phi\simeq(\Box\phi)^2\simeq(\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\phi)^2$. These are algebraic relations modulo boundary terms, not uses of the [equation of motion](equation-of-motion.md).
