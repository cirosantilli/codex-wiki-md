# Wave impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_impedance)

For a [plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) in a homogeneous, linear, lossless, [isotropic](isotropy.md) medium with [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\epsilon$ and [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) $\mu$, the wave impedance relates the field amplitudes by $Z=E/H=\sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$. Its units are ohms. Impedance differences determine the normal-incidence amplitude [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md), $r=(Z_2-Z_1)/(Z_2+Z_1)$ and $t=2Z_2/(Z_2+Z_1)$, with a common tangential electric-field axis.
