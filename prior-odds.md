# Prior odds

↑ **Parent:** [Prior probability](prior-probability.md)

For two exhaustive mutually exclusive models of positive [prior probability](prior-probability.md), prior odds compare their probabilities before observing the data. They must be distinguished from the parameter [prior distributions](prior-probability.md) within those models. A [Bayes factor](bayes-factor.md) multiplies these prior odds to produce [posterior odds](posterior-odds.md).
