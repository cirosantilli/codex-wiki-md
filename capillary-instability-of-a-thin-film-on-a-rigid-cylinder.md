# Capillary instability of a thin film on a rigid cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

A thin axisymmetric coating of physical thickness $d=\epsilon a h$ on a rigid cylinder of radius $a$ has leading curvature $a^{-1}-d/a^2-d_{\bar z\bar z}$. With constant [surface tension](surface-tension.md) $\gamma$, negligible [Newtonian gravity](gravitational-acceleration.md), [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md) at the wall and negligible exterior stress, [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) gives $q=-d^3p_{\bar z}/(3\mu)$. Set $z=\bar z/a$ and $t=\gamma\epsilon^3\bar t/(3\mu a)$ to obtain the displayed [thin-film equation](thin-film-equation.md). A perturbation of a uniform film $h_0$ proportional to $e^{st+ikz}$ has

$$
s=h_0^3(k^2-k^4).
$$

Thus the fastest [Rayleigh–Plateau instability](rayleigh-plateau-instability.md) has $|k|=1/\sqrt2$, or physical wavelength $2\pi\sqrt2a$.
