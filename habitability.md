# Habitability

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary science](planetary-science-split.md)

Capacity of an environment to sustain [life](life.md). Accessible [liquid water](liquid-water.md), chemical ingredients and usable [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) are central criteria for known [life](life.md); actual habitation is a separate question.
