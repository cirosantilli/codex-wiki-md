# Pauli reduction of the pinned-edge mass-action product

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor mass-action law](semiconductor-mass-action-law.md)

For [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) at the conduction edge, divide the exact product by the classical prediction. The ratio is $F_{1/2}(0)e^xF_{1/2}(-x)$, where $x=E_g/(k_BT)$. It is less than one, approaches $F_{1/2}(0)$ as $T\to0$, and approaches its square in the formal high-[temperature](temperature-split.md) parabolic model.
