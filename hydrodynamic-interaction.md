# Hydrodynamic interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

Bodies immersed in one fluid affect each other through the flow that each produces. At large separation, a force-driven sphere generates a [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) that modifies the other sphere's velocity. These interactions produce off-diagonal entries in the [hydrodynamic mobility matrix](hydrodynamic-mobility-matrix.md).
