# Compensator of a counting process

↑ **Parent:** [Counting process](counting-process.md)

The compensator of a [counting process](counting-process.md) $N(t)$ is a predictable increasing process $\Lambda(t)$ such that $N(t)-\Lambda(t)$ is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). For an observed event process with [at-risk process](at-risk-process.md) $Y(t)$ and [hazard function](hazard-function.md) $h(t)$, it is $\Lambda(t)=\int_0^tY(u)h(u)\,du$ under [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md). It is cumulative intensity, rather than an event probability.
