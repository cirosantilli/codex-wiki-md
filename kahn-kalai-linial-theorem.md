# Kahn-Kalai-Linial theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis of Boolean functions](analysis-of-boolean-functions.md)

For a [Boolean function](boolean-function.md) $f:\{0,1\}^n\to\{0,1\}$ under the uniform [probability measure](probability-measure.md), some coordinate has [influence of a variable](influence-of-a-variable.md) at least $c\operatorname{Var}(f)\log(n)/n$, where $c>0$ is an absolute constant and $I_i(f)$ is the probability that flipping coordinate $i$ changes $f$. For [variance](variance-split.md) bounded below, this is order $\log(n)/n$. The [tribes function](tribes-function.md) shows that this order cannot be improved for general balanced [Boolean functions](boolean-function.md).
