# Clinton Davisson

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton_Davisson)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md) whose route into research included a telephone-company job when he could not afford to continue at Chicago. He later worked at Bell Telephone Laboratories, retiring in 1946 after 29 years, and taught [physics](physics-split.md) at Virginia in 1947–1949.[Biography: financial interruption, laboratory employment and visiting professorship](clinton-davisson-biographical.md) The [Davisson–Germer experiment](davisson-germer-experiment.md) connects that industrial-laboratory work to [electron diffraction](electron-diffraction.md) and [matter waves](matter-wave.md).
