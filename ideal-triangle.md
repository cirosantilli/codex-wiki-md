# Ideal triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic triangle](hyperbolic-triangle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideal_triangle)

An ideal hyperbolic triangle has all three vertices on the boundary at infinity of the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md). All its angles are zero and its [hyperbolic area](hyperbolic-area.md) in curvature $-1$ is $\pi$, by [hyperbolic triangle area](hyperbolic-triangle-area.md).
