# Electrostatic pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

The outward normal electric traction on an equipotential conductor adjoining a linear isotropic dielectric, for the ideal normal-field configuration, is $p_e=\epsilon E_n^2/2=\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf D/2$. Integrating this normal traction over a surface gives the electric [force](force-split.md). It is a field-stress contribution; mechanical [pressure](pressure-split.md) and dielectric boundary conditions must be included when determining an actual fluid-interface equilibrium.
