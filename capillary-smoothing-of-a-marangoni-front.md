# Capillary smoothing of a Marangoni front

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-mass Marangoni spreading on a liquid film](finite-mass-marangoni-spreading-on-a-liquid-film.md)

The height jump in [linear similarity profiles for surfactant spreading](linear-similarity-profiles-for-surfactant-spreading.md) is smoothed by [surface tension](surface-tension.md). If $C_x\sim M/x_N^2$ through a region of width $\Delta$, the [Marangoni stress](marangoni-effect.md) flux is $AMh_0^2/(\mu x_N^2)$ and the capillary flux is $\gamma_0h_0^4/(\mu\Delta^3)$. Their balance gives $\Delta\sim(\gamma_0h_0^2x_N^2/(AM))^{1/3}\propto t^{2/9}$. This estimate assumes gravity is negligible and the smoothing region remains narrow compared with the pool.
