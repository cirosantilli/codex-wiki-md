# Unpolarized cross section

↑ **Parent:** [Cross section (physics)](cross-section-physics.md)

For uncorrelated incoming beams with equally populated [spin](spin.md) states, the measured [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) averages over initial [polarizations](polarization-waves.md) and sums over unobserved final [polarizations](polarization-waves.md). The displayed average applies to two massive incoming particles with spins $s_1,s_2$. It is distinct from a prepared-polarization channel and from the final-state [identical-particle factor in a final-state phase-space integral](identical-particle-factor-in-a-final-state-phase-space-integral.md). A polarization channel with an amplitude that grows faster than the others can reach a perturbative bound before the averaged quantity does.
