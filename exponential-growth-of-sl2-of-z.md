# Exponential growth of SL2 of Z

↑ **Parent:** [Growth function of a finitely generated group](growth-function-of-a-finitely-generated-group.md)

The matrices

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix}
$$

generate a [free monoid](free-monoid.md) in $\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)$. Hence the $2^n$ positive words of length $n$ are distinct and $\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)$ has [exponential growth of a group](exponential-growth-of-a-group.md).
