# Solar dynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Solar magnetic field](solar-magnetic-field.md)

The solar dynamo is the induction and regeneration of the [solar magnetic field](solar-magnetic-field.md) by conducting flows powered ultimately by solar convection and rotation. In a [mean-field dynamo](mean-field-dynamo.md), differential rotation supplies the [Omega effect](omega-effect.md) and helical correlations or the [Babcock-Leighton mechanism](babcock-leighton-mechanism.md) regenerate a [poloidal magnetic field](poloidal-magnetic-field.md). The mean induction equation is

$$
\partial_t\overline{\mathbf B}=\nabla\times\left(\overline{\mathbf U}\times\overline{\mathbf B}+\boldsymbol{\mathcal E}-\eta\nabla\times\overline{\mathbf B}\right),
$$

where $\boldsymbol{\mathcal E}=\langle\mathbf u\times\mathbf b\rangle$ is the [mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md). A model must close this correlation and include feedback to predict a saturated magnetic cycle; prescribing a shear alone does not close the regeneration loop.
