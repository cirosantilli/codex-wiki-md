# Negative translation of a classical proof

↑ **Parent:** [Gödel-Gentzen negative translation](godel-gentzen-negative-translation.md)

The [Gödel-Gentzen negative translation](godel-gentzen-negative-translation.md) transforms $\Gamma\vdash_{\mathrm{CL}}A$ into $\Gamma^N\vdash_{\mathrm{IL}}A^N$. Induct on a [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) derivation. The translated [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md), [logical implication](logical-implication.md) and universal rules are ordinary intuitionistic rules. [Logical disjunction](logical-disjunction.md) and existential introduction add double [logical negation](negation.md); their elimination rules first derive the double [logical negation](negation.md) of the stable translated conclusion, then use its stability. Classical [double-negation elimination](double-negation-elimination.md) translates precisely to stability. If [logical equality](logical-equality.md) atoms are double-negated, transport a stable translated conclusion through a [logical equality](logical-equality.md) by contradiction and then remove the resulting double [logical negation](negation.md). Original nonlogical axioms must also be translated; the result does not say arbitrary untranslated classical [first-order theories](first-order-theory.md) are intuitionistically valid.
