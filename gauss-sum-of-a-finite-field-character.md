# Gauss sum of a finite-field character

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicative character of a finite field](multiplicative-character-of-a-finite-field.md)

Here $\psi$ is a nontrivial additive [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of the [finite field](finite-field.md). For nontrivial $\chi$, substituting $x=ty$ in the squared absolute value gives

$$
|G(\chi)|^2=\sum_{t\ne0}\chi(t)\sum_{y\ne0}\psi((t-1)y)=q.
$$

The inner sum is $q-1$ at $t=1$ and $-1$ otherwise; multiplicative [character](character-of-a-representation.md) [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) completes the identity.
