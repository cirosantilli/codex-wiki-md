# Barotropic spherical accretion equation

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md)

Steady spherical [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) gives $r^2\rho u=\text{constant}$. Combining this with [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) and $c_s^2=dp/d\rho$ gives the displayed equation. Smooth transonic passage requires $u^2=c_s^2$ and $\Phi^{\prime}=2c_s^2/r$ simultaneously. The [barotropic enthalpy function](barotropic-enthalpy-function.md) gives the integral $u^2/2+h+\Phi=\text{constant}$.
