# Second collapse of a protostar

↑ **Parent:** [Protostar](protostar.md)

During continued compression of a [first hydrostatic core](first-hydrostatic-core.md), [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) dissociation absorbs [energy](energy-split.md) and softens the effective [equation of state](equation-of-state.md). This can trigger a second rapid contraction before a compact stellar core becomes pressure-supported. It is distinct from the initial nearly isothermal collapse and from later [Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction](kelvin-helmholtz-mechanism.md) at roughly fixed [mass](mass.md).
