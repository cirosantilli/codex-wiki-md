# High-frequency reciprocal parametrix kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Parametrix](parametrix.md)

For a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) with no sufficiently large real zero, a compact low-frequency [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) removes its remaining zeros. A reciprocal satisfying $|\partial^\alpha b|\lesssim\langle\xi\rangle^{-m-\delta|\alpha|}$, $m\geq0$, $0<\delta\leq1$, defines a [Fourier multiplier](fourier-multiplier.md) mapping $H^s$ to $H^{s+m}$. Its inverse-transform kernel is smooth off zero: repeated frequency [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) supplies arbitrary decay even after multiplying by powers of frequency for spatial [derivatives](derivative.md). Consequently a [compactly supported distribution](compactly-supported-distribution.md) supported away from the target contributes only a smooth function there. In a local regularity proof, the [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) commutator is placed in that separated region rather than estimated only by its differential order.
