# Finite chain-growth bookkeeping

↑ **Parent:** [Chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md)

Stipulate a feed of exactly $M_0=40$ [monomer molecules](monomer-molecule.md), four successfully initiated linear chains, one [polymerization-active site](polymerization-active-site.md) per chain, no [chain transfer in chain polymerization](chain-transfer-in-chain-polymerization.md), no loss of incorporated units, and no further [chain-growth initiation](chain-growth-initiation.md). Count only initiated chains in $C$; exclude unreacted [monomers](monomer.md). Assume each consumed [monomer molecule](monomer-molecule.md) contributes exactly one [monomeric unit](monomeric-unit.md). With incorporated feed fraction $q=S/M_0$, the number-average [degree of polymerization](degree-of-polymerization.md) is the counting identity

$$
X_n=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{C}j_i}{C}=\frac{S}{C}=\frac{qM_0}{C}.
$$

This is conditional conservation bookkeeping, not a universal empirical law. Immediately after [chain-growth initiation](chain-growth-initiation.md), the four lengths are $(1,1,1,1)$, so $(C,A,S)=(4,4,4)$ and $q=0.1$. Stipulate twelve subsequent [chain-growth propagation](chain-growth-propagation.md) events, three on each chain: the lengths become $(4,4,4,4)$, giving $(C,A,S)=(4,4,16)$, $q=0.4$ and $X_n=4$. Sixteen units have been incorporated, while twenty-four free [monomer molecules](monomer-molecule.md) remain. The chain-length assignment is deliberately chosen, not a prediction of equal growth in an experiment.
