# Logical functor

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary topos](elementary-topos.md)

A logical functor between elementary toposes preserves [finite limits](finite-limit.md), [exponential objects](exponential-object.md) and the [subobject classifier](subobject-classifier.md), with their canonical comparison maps. It therefore preserves [power objects](power-object.md) and the double-power-object monad. If it has a left adjoint, [power-object monadicity](power-object-monadicity.md) and the [adjoint lifting theorem for monad algebra functors](adjoint-lifting-theorem-for-monad-algebra-functors.md) give it a right adjoint as well.
