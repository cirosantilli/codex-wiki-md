<h1 id="kirchhoff-helmholtz-representation">Kirchhoff–Helmholtz representation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse scattering problem](inverse-scattering-problem.md)

With the normal directed out of an obstacle $D$ into the fluid, a field solving $(\Delta+k^2)\psi=-Q$ outside $D$ has the representation

$$
\psi(\mathbf r)=\int Q(\mathbf r')G_k(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')\,d\mathbf r'+\int_{\partial D}[\psi\partial_{n'}G_k-G_k\partial_{n'}\psi]\,dS'.
$$

Here $G_k$ is the [outgoing Green function for the three-dimensional Helmholtz equation](outgoing-green-function-for-the-three-dimensional-helmholtz-equation.md) and the observation point lies outside the obstacle. The formula follows from [Green second identity](green-second-identity.md); the normal to the exterior fluid on its inner boundary is the negative of the stated obstacle normal. The surface term represents the scattered field.
