# Smooth embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Immersion](immersion.md)

A smooth embedding is an injective immersion that is a homeomorphism onto its image with the subspace topology.

This is the differentiable specialization of an [embedding](embedding.md), with an injective differential in addition to its topological embedding property.
