# Spin-one transverse measurement after axial precession

↑ **Parent:** [Spin coherent state](spin-coherent-state.md)

Prepare the $J_1=+\hbar$ [spin coherent state](spin-coherent-state.md) and evolve with [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H=\omega J_3$ for time $t$. In the descending-$J_3$ basis of the [spin-one angular momentum matrices](spin-one-angular-momentum-matrices.md), the initial vector is $(1,\sqrt2,1)^{\mathsf T}/2$ and the evolved vector is $(e^{-i\theta},\sqrt2,e^{i\theta})^{\mathsf T}/2$, where $\theta=\omega t$. The $J_1$ [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) has vectors $(1,\sqrt2,1)^{\mathsf T}/2$, $(1,0,-1)^{\mathsf T}/\sqrt2$ and $(1,-\sqrt2,1)^{\mathsf T}/2$. Taking their [inner products](inner-product-split.md) with the evolved vector gives [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md) $(1+\cos\theta)/2$, $-i\sin\theta/\sqrt2$ and $(\cos\theta-1)/2$. Their squared moduli give the [Born rule](born-rule.md) [probabilities](probability.md) in the title. At integer half-turns $\theta=\pi k$, the outcome is deterministically $(-1)^k\hbar$.
