# Lunar mare

↑ **Parent:** [Moon](moon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_mare)

A low-albedo lunar plain formed largely by [basaltic](basaltic-rock.md) volcanism, commonly filling an older impact basin. The plural is maria.
