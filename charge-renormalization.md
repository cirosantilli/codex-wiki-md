# Charge renormalization

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

Charge renormalization relates a bare gauge coupling to a coupling fixed by a physical normalization condition. In QED, the [Ward identity](ward-identity.md) equates vertex and fermion wave-function renormalization, leaving photon wave-function renormalization to determine the charge counterterm.
