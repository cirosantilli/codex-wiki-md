# Trans effect

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

The [trans effect](trans-effect.md) is a kinetic influence whereby a ligand accelerates substitution opposite itself, classically in square-planar complexes. It must be distinguished from ground-state bond weakening and from the carbonyl [cis effect](cis-effect.md).
