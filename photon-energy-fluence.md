# Photon energy fluence

↑ **Parent:** [Fluence](fluence.md)

Energy carried by incident [photons](photon.md) per unit area. For monoenergetic [photons](photon.md), $\Psi=E_\gamma\Phi$, where $\Phi$ is [photon fluence](photon-fluence.md). With [charged-particle equilibrium](charged-particle-equilibrium.md), $D=\Psi\mu_{\rm en}/\rho$ relates it to [absorbed dose](absorbed-dose.md).
