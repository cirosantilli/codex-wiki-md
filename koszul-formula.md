# Koszul formula

↑ **Parent:** [Metric connection](metric-connection.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koszul_formula)

The unique torsion-free metric connection satisfies

$$
2g(\nabla_XY,Z)
=Xg(Y,Z)+Yg(Z,X)-Zg(X,Y)
-g(X,[Y,Z])+g(Y,[Z,X])+g(Z,[X,Y]).
$$

This formula both proves uniqueness of the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) and computes it from the metric and [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md).
