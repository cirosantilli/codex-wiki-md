# Nuclear Fermi gas model

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md)

A model with separate filled [proton](proton.md) and [neutron](neutron-split.md) [momentum](momentum-split.md) seas in a [nuclear mean field](nuclear-mean-field.md). Each spin-half species has two [spin](spin.md) states, so counting momentum-space cells gives $p_{F,i}=\hbar(3\pi^2n_i)^{1/3}$. It describes typical [nucleon](nucleon.md) motion and a kinetic contribution to [nuclear symmetry energy](symmetry-energy.md), but binding requires additional interactions.
