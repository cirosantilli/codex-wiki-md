# Power-law distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Power law](power-law.md)

A [power-law distribution](power-law-distribution.md) uses a [power law](power-law.md) for a [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) or a [probability density](probability-density.md) on a specified range. For a continuous model with $p_X(x)=Cx^{-\beta}$ on $x\geq x_{\min}>0$ and zero below it, the total [integral](integral.md) is finite exactly when $\beta>1$. Normalization then requires $C=(\beta-1)x_{\min}^{\beta-1}$, giving a [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md) with shape parameter $\alpha=\beta-1$. Discrete models instead normalize a [sum](sum.md). The support and normalizing coefficient are part of the model, and a [power law](power-law.md) for its [tail probability](tail-probability.md) uses a different exponent from its [probability density](probability-density.md).
