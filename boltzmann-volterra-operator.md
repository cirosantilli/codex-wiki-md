# Boltzmann Volterra operator

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Boltzmann equation](linear-boltzmann-equation.md)

Combine the [damped free-transport evolution](damped-free-transport-evolution.md) and the [linear Boltzmann collision operator](linear-boltzmann-collision-operator.md) in a time-ordered [Bochner integral](bochner-integral.md). Its norm obeys $\|\tau f(t)\|_2\leq C\int_0^t\|f(s)\|_2ds$. This reduces the characteristic integral equation to $(I-\tau)f=U_a(t,0)f_0$.
