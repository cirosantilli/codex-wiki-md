# Relativistic iron-line spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion-disk tests of strong-field gravity](accretion-disk-tests-of-strong-field-gravity.md)

An irradiated [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) can produce iron [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) with a known local [energy](energy-split.md) scale. [relativistic Doppler effect](relativistic-doppler-effect.md), [gravitational redshift](gravitational-redshift.md), beaming and light bending redistribute those [photons](photon.md) into a broadened asymmetric line. The red wing is sensitive to the deepest emitting radii, while inclination and orbital motion influence the profile. Coronal illumination, ionization, [absorption](absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) and the continuum model must be controlled before inferring an inner radius or testing a spacetime geometry.
