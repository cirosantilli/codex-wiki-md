# Quantum register

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md)

A quantum register is a named [tensor product](tensor-product.md) factor of a [quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md)'s [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), usually a collection of [qubits](qubit.md). Registers separate data, control, output and workspace roles; [uncomputation](uncomputation.md) returns workspace to a fixed [quantum state](quantum-state.md) without destroying coherence in the data.
