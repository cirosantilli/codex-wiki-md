# Contact conformal factor along an isotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Gray stability theorem](gray-stability-theorem.md)

If a [time-dependent vector field](time-dependent-vector-field.md) satisfies $\dot\alpha_t+\mathcal L_{Y_t}\alpha_t=h_t\alpha_t$, its [flow maps](flow-map.md) satisfy $\rho_t^*\alpha_t=u_t\alpha_0$, where $u_t(x)=\exp(\int_0^t h_s(\rho_s(x))\,ds)$. Thus the conformal factor is smooth and positive even when $h_t$ changes sign.
