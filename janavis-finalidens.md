# Janavis finalidens

↑ **Parent:** [Bird](bird.md)

A toothed latest [Cretaceous](cretaceous.md) [bird](bird.md) outside the living-bird [crown group](crown-group.md). Its preserved [pterygoid bone](pterygoid-bone.md) supplies evidence for a mobile palatal arrangement before the crown divergence.
