# Fringe shift

↑ **Parent:** [Interference fringe](interference-fringe.md)

A [fringe shift](fringe-shift.md) measures a change of relative [phase](phase-waves.md) between interfering waves in units of a full fringe spacing. A changing optical path difference $\Delta\ell$ gives a shift $\Delta N=\Delta\ell/\lambda$ for monochromatic [light](light.md).
