# Line element on a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Line element](line-element.md)

In [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md) on a [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $R$, the position is $X=R(\sin\theta\cos\phi,\sin\theta\sin\phi,\cos\theta)$. Its coordinate tangent [vectors](vector.md) satisfy $X_\theta\cdot X_\phi=0$, $|X_\theta|^2=R^2$, and $|X_\phi|^2=R^2\sin^2\theta$. Expanding $|dX|^2$ proves the displayed [line element](line-element.md), the [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md) of the sphere. Along a parametrized path it gives the [arc length](arc-length.md) by integrating $R\sqrt{\dot\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi^2}$. The vanishing azimuthal coefficient at a pole is a [coordinate singularity](coordinate-singularity.md), not a singularity of the [sphere](sphere.md).
