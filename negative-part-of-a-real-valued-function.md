# Negative part of a real-valued function

↑ **Parent:** [Real-valued function](real-valued-function.md)

The [negative part](negative-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) of a [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) $f$ is the nonnegative [function](function-split.md) $f_-(x)=\max(0,-f(x))$. It records the magnitude of the negative values, rather than retaining their sign: if $f(x)=-3$, then $f_-(x)=3$. Together with the [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md), it gives $f=f_+-f_-$ and $|f|=f_++f_-$. This decomposition defines the [Lebesgue integral](lebesgue-integral.md) of a signed [function](function-split.md) and makes the [integrability](integrability.md) condition explicit.
