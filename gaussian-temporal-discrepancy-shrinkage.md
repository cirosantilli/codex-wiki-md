# Gaussian temporal-discrepancy shrinkage

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian causal inference in perception](bayesian-causal-inference-in-perception.md)

For a common-event model, let $y\mid\delta\sim N(\delta,s^2)$ and $\delta\sim N(0,\tau^2)$ with positive [variances](variance-split.md). The posterior exponent is $-[(y-\delta)^2/s^2+\delta^2/\tau^2]/2$. Completing the square yields posterior precision $s^{-2}+\tau^{-2}$ and mean $y\tau^2/(s^2+\tau^2)$. The estimate is pulled toward zero, more strongly when the measurement is uncertain. This conditional model does not decide whether a common event should be assumed.
