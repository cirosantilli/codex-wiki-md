# Minimal normal subgroups of finite solvable groups are elementary abelian

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal normal subgroup](minimal-normal-subgroup.md)

A nontrivial [minimal normal subgroup](minimal-normal-subgroup.md) $N$ of a finite [solvable group](solvable-group.md) is abelian: its [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is characteristic, and minimality plus solvability excludes $N'=N$. A nontrivial [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) of this [abelian group](abelian-group.md) is characteristic, so minimality makes $N$ a p-group. The [characteristic subgroup](characteristic-subgroup.md) of pth powers is proper, hence trivial by minimality. Therefore $N$ is an [elementary abelian group](elementary-abelian-group.md).
