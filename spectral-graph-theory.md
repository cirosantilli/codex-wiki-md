# Spectral graph theory

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_graph_theory)

[Spectral graph theory](spectral-graph-theory.md) studies [graphs](graph-split.md) through [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) and [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) of associated [matrices](matrix.md), including the [adjacency matrix](adjacency-matrix.md) and [Laplacian matrix](laplacian-matrix.md). A [graph eigenvalue](graph-eigenvalue.md) usually refers to the adjacency-matrix spectrum unless another matrix is specified.
