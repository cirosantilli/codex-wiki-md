# Alternating link diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Link diagram](link-diagram.md)

A [link](link.md) diagram is alternating if overpassing and underpassing crossings alternate along every component. Its checkerboard smoothing choices are consistent across all crossings of a connected projection. An [alternating knot diagram](alternating-knot-diagram.md) is the one-component case.
