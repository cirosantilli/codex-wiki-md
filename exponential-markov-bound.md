# Exponential Markov bound

↑ **Parent:** [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md)

Applying Markov's inequality to $e^{tX}$ gives

$$
\Pr(X\geq x)\leq e^{-tx}M_X(t),
\qquad t>0.
$$

Optimizing this expression is the basic Chernoff-bound method.
