# Feynman slash notation

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feynman_slash_notation)

Feynman slash notation contracts a [four-vector](four-vector.md) with the [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md). With mostly-minus signature, the [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) gives $\not p^2=p^2I$, so $(\not p-m)^{-1}=(\not p+m)/(p^2-m^2)$ away from the [mass shell](mass-shell.md).
