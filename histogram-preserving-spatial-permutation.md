# Histogram-preserving spatial permutation

↑ **Parent:** [Luminance histogram](luminance-histogram.md)

Permuting pixel locations preserves the multiset of [luminance](luminance.md) values, hence its histogram and every sum $\sum_i f(I_i)$, including raw moments. It need not preserve spatial correlations, edges or relations between highlights and shading. This proves nonuniqueness of an image from its histogram; a particular perceptual consequence requires experiment.
