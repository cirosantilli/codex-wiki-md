# Static link

↑ **Parent:** [Stack frame](stack-frame.md)

A [pointer](pointer-computer-programming.md) from an activation's [stack frame](stack-frame.md) to the activation providing its immediate lexical environment. A [static link](static-link.md) follows lexical enclosure rather than necessarily pointing to the caller. Calls through a [programming closure](closure-computer-programming.md) obtain the environment from the passed closure.
