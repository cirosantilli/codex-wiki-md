# Indicial exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Indicial equation](indicial-equation.md)

An indicial exponent is a root of the [indicial equation](indicial-equation.md) at a [regular singular point](regular-singular-point.md). It gives the leading local power in a [Frobenius method](frobenius-method.md); resonant roots can require logarithmic terms.
