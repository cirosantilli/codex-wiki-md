↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Again $|e^{-ax}\sin(bx)|\leq e^{-ax}$ proves [Lebesgue integrability](../../../../../integrability.md). Taking the [imaginary part](../../../../../imaginary-part.md) of the absolutely convergent complex integral in part [i](../i.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int_0^\infty e^{-ax}\sin(bx)\,dx=\frac b{a^2+b^2}.}
$$
