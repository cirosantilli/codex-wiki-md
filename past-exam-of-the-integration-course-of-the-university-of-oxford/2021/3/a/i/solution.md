<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At stage zero there is one [closed interval](../../../../../closed-real-interval.md). At every later stage each component is replaced by two closed end intervals separated by a positive open gap. Induction therefore gives exactly $2^n$ pairwise disjoint [closed intervals](../../../../../closed-real-interval.md) at stage $n$. Their common length is

$$
\boxed{\ell_n=\frac{q_n}{2^n}\leq2^{-n},}
$$

since the decreasing [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) starts at $q_0=1$.
