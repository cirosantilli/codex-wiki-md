<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The $2^n$ component intervals are pairwise disjoint and each has length $q_n/2^n$. [Finite additivity](../../../../../finite-additivity-of-a-set-function.md) of [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md) gives

$$
\boxed{m(C_n)=2^n\frac{q_n}{2^n}=q_n.}
$$

Their endpoints do not cause overlaps, because the central gaps have strictly positive length.
