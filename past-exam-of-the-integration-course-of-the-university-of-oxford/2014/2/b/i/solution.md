<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fix $t_0\geq0$. On a compact parameter [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) containing all nonnegative $t$ sufficiently close to $t_0$, the corresponding rectangle with $x\in[0,1]$ is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md). The [continuous function](../../../../../continuous-function.md) $f$ is therefore bounded there by some $M<\infty$, by the [extreme value theorem](../../../../../extreme-value-theorem.md).

For any sequence $t_n\geq0$ tending to $t_0$, all sufficiently late $t_n$ lie in this rectangle's parameter range. For each $x\in[0,1]$, [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) gives

$$
f(t_n,x)\longrightarrow f(t_0,x),\qquad |f(t_n,x)|\leq M.
$$

The constant [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $M$ is integrable on $[0,1]$. The [dominated convergence theorem](../../../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{F(t_n)=\int_0^1f(t_n,x)\,dx\longrightarrow\int_0^1f(t_0,x)\,dx=F(t_0).}
$$

The sequential criterion for [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) on a real [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) proves **$F$ is continuous on $[0,\infty)$**. At zero this is right-continuity. This is [continuity of parameter integrals on compact rectangles](../../../../../continuity-of-parameter-integrals-on-compact-rectangles.md); the bound is local in the parameter, so $f$ need not be bounded on the entire unbounded strip.
