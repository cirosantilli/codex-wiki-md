<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [density of the rational numbers](../../../../../density-of-the-rational-numbers.md) gives a countable way to separate two unequal values:

$$
\boxed{\{f<g\}=\bigcup_{q\in\mathbb Q}\bigl(\{f<q\}\cap\{g>q\}\bigr).}
$$

Indeed, $f(x)<g(x)$ holds exactly when some rational number lies strictly between them. Every set inside the union is a [Lebesgue measurable set](../../../../../lebesgue-measurable-set.md), by the definition of a [measurable function](../../../../../measurable-function.md) and the closure properties of a [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md). Since $\mathbb Q$ is countable, the union is measurable.
