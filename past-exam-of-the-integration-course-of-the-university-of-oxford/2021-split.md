# 2021

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the integration course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source: [original released examination PDF, bundle pages 79–81; paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_part_a_examinati/response/2820674/attach/3/FOI%20202410%20944%20Data.pdf#page=79).

Solutions to all three questions of the examination sat on 16 June 2021, including all optional parts. Integrability means [Lebesgue integrability](../integrability.md); a conditionally convergent [improper integral](../improper-integral.md) does not suffice. The Roman items in Question 3(a) are the three required properties of one construction; the construction and its limiting properties are explained first, and each Roman item is checked separately.
