# Pointwise limits in a functor category

↑ **Parent:** [Functor category](functor-category.md)

For small diagrams with complete codomain, choose each objectwise [categorical limit](categorical-limit.md). For $u:d\to d'$, its projections uniquely define the arrow $L(u)$ through $p_j(d')L(u)=H_j(u)p_j(d)$. The [universal property](universal-property.md) proves identity and composition laws. The same projection calculation makes every [categorical cone](cone-over-a-diagram.md) factorization a [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md). Hence these are genuine [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) in the [functor category](functor-category.md), and the objectwise [forgetful functor](forgetful-functor.md) is a [limit-creating functor](limit-creating-functor.md).
