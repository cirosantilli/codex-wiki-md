<h1 id="rothlisberger-channel-drainage">Röthlisberger channel drainage</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Subglacial drainage](subglacial-drainage.md)

Frictional dissipation of [water](water.md) flow melts a channel's [ice](ice.md) walls, while [ice](ice.md) creep under [effective pressure in subglacial drainage](effective-pressure-in-subglacial-drainage.md) closes them. The resulting equilibrium has increasing effective [pressure](pressure-split.md) with discharge in the basic channel theory. A route gaining [water](water.md) can lower its [water pressure](fluid-pressure.md) and attract further discharge, widening at the expense of neighbors. This positive redistribution feedback produces arterial drainage.
