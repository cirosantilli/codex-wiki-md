# Local-energy measurement verification

↑ **Parent:** [Local Hamiltonian problem](local-hamiltonian-problem.md)

Uniformly sample one positive local term and measure the effect $h_j$. The averaged energy-flag effect on a witness is $H/M$, where $M$ is the number of terms. Repetition with a threshold halfway between the promised flag probabilities uses $O(M^2/(b-a)^2)$ witness registers for constant error. A spectral tensor-product analysis, as in [QMA parallel repetition with entangled witnesses](qma-parallel-repetition-with-entangled-witnesses.md), proves soundness against entangled witnesses. Constant-locality [positive operator-valued measures](positive-operator-valued-measure.md) are efficiently implementable using one [ancilla qubit](ancilla-qubit.md).
