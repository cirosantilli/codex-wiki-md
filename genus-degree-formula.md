# Genus-degree formula

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus-degree_formula)

The arithmetic genus of a degree-$d$ [projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md) is

$$
p_a=\frac{(d-1)(d-2)}2.
$$

Equivalently, its structure sheaf has Euler characteristic $\chi(\mathcal O_X)=1-p_a$.
