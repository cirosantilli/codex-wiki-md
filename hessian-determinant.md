# Hessian determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md)

The [determinant](determinant.md) of the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md). For a twice [continuously differentiable function](continuously-differentiable-function.md) of two variables, it is $f_{xx}f_{yy}-f_{xy}^2$. A negative value at a [critical point](critical-point.md) implies a [saddle point](saddle-point.md); a positive value combines with the sign of $f_{xx}$ in the [second-derivative test](second-derivative-test.md). A zero value is inconclusive.
