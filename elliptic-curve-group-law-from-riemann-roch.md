# Elliptic curve group law from Riemann-Roch

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

Fix $O\in E(k)$. On a genus-one smooth projective curve, every degree-one divisor class has a unique effective point representative by the [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md): its section space has dimension one, since the complementary canonical-divisor degree is negative. Thus $P\mapsto[P-O]$ is a bijection onto the [degree-zero Picard group of a curve](degree-zero-picard-group-of-a-curve.md). Transporting divisor-class addition proves the group axioms, with identity $O$, and characterizes $P+Q$ by $P+Q\sim R+O$.
