# Cyclic Moore space in dimension one

↑ **Parent:** [Moore space (algebraic topology)](moore-space-algebraic-topology.md)

Attaching a two-cell to $S^1$ by a map of degree $n$ gives the Moore space $M(\mathbb Z/n,1)$. Its positive-dimensional [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) is

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\ n\ }\mathbb Z\longrightarrow0,
$$

so $H_1\cong\mathbb Z/n$ and all its other reduced integral homology groups vanish.
