# Bracket trace on the three-strand Temperley-Lieb algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Temperley-Lieb diagram algebra](temperley-lieb-diagram-algebra.md)

Close a three-strand planar [tangle](tangle.md) and evaluate its reduced [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md). The basis $1,e_1,e_2,e_1e_2,e_2e_1$ has the displayed trace values, with the last value also valid for $e_2e_1$. Writing braid generators as $A1+A^{-1}e_i$ turns knot-polynomial computations into a five-dimensional algebra calculation.
