# Power-set axiom in the constructible universe

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible universe](constructible-universe.md)

For $a\in L$, ambient [separation](axiom-schema-of-specification.md) forms $S=\mathcal P(a)\cap L$. Ambient [replacement](axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) bounds the first constructible stages of all members of $S$. At a stage containing $a$ and all of $S$, the latter is definable by the bounded property of being a [subset](subset.md) of $a$. Thus $S$ belongs to the next constructible stage and is the internal [power set](power-set.md) in $L$.
