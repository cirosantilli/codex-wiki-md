# Weil-group topology

↑ **Parent:** [Relative Weil group](relative-weil-group.md)

The Weil-group topology makes the [inertia group](inertia-group.md) open with its profinite topology and makes the quotient by inertia discrete. It is generally finer than the topology induced from the ambient Galois group.
