<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[cold dark matter](../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md) is nonrelativistic and effectively collisionless on the scales of interest. Its small [velocity dispersion](../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md) limits [free streaming](../../../../../free-streaming.md), allowing small-scale gravitational wells to survive. [baryons](../../../../../baryon.md) coupled to [photons](../../../../../photon.md) undergo acoustic motion and damping before [recombination](../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md); [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) does not share that [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), and provides potential wells into which [baryons](../../../../../baryon.md) can later fall.

For a subhorizon pressureless perturbation, once a Newtonian treatment is applicable,

$$
\ddot\delta+2H\dot\delta-4\pi G\bar\rho_m\delta=0.
$$

During [radiation domination](../../../../../radiation-domination.md) the matter contribution to the background gravity is small; neglecting it gives $\delta\simeq C_1+C_2\log t$. This slow subhorizon growth is the [Mészáros effect](../../../../../meszaros-effect.md). During [matter domination](../../../../../matter-domination.md),

$$
\boxed{\delta=C_+a+C_-a^{-3/2}}
$$

in an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md). Once $\delta$ approaches unity, linear theory fails and collapse, [virialization](../../../../../virialization.md) and merging become important.

Modes entering the horizon during [radiation domination](../../../../../radiation-domination.md) therefore grow less before equality than longer modes entering later. This scale dependence is encoded in a [transfer function](../../../../../transfer-function.md), with a schematic linear [matter power spectrum](../../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md) $P(k,a)=A k^{n_s}T^2(k)D^2(a)$. [cold dark matter](../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md) supports hierarchical assembly; a sufficiently large warm- or hot-dark-matter free-streaming length suppresses small-scale structure. Superhorizon [density contrasts](../../../../../density-contrast.md) change under a [gauge transformation in cosmological perturbation theory](../../../../../gauge-transformation-in-cosmological-perturbation-theory.md), so the subhorizon growth laws should not be applied indiscriminately outside the horizon. The original cold-dark-matter formation model explicitly connects weak [free streaming](../../../../../free-streaming.md) to the spectrum that seeds galaxies and clusters.[1984, Nature 311, pp. 517–525, abstract: collisionless cold particles, retained fluctuations and the post-recombination spectrum](../../../../../formation-of-galaxies-and-large-scale-structure-with-cold-dark-matter-by-george-r-blumenthal-and-others.md)
