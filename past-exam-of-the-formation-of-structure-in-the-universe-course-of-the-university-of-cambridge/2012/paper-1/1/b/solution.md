<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[inside-out collapse of a singular isothermal sphere](../../../../../inside-out-collapse-of-a-singular-isothermal-sphere.md) starts from an idealized static envelope,

$$
\rho(r)=\frac{a_s^2}{2\pi Gr^2},\qquad
M(r)=\frac{2a_s^2r}{G},
$$

with constant [isothermal sound speed](../../../../../isothermal-sound-speed.md) $a_s$. Collapse begins centrally after formation of a central [mass](../../../../../mass.md). An expansion wave moves outward through the initially static envelope at the [sound speed](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md):

$$
\boxed{r_{\rm ew}=a_st.}
$$

“Expansion” describes the outward-moving information front; the [gas](../../../../../gas.md) behind it moves inward. The exterior remains close to its original $r^{-2}$ equilibrium until the wave reaches it.

In the inner region, the central [mass](../../../../../mass.md) dominates and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) becomes less important:

$$
v_r\simeq-\sqrt{\frac{2GM_*}{r}},\qquad
\rho\simeq\frac{\dot M_*}{4\pi r^2|v_r|}\propto r^{-3/2}.
$$

The self-similar [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) scale is **$\dot M_*=m_0a_s^3/G$**, where the [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) coefficient $m_0$ follows from the dynamical similarity solution, not from counting material swept by the front. Indeed, the initial [mass](../../../../../mass.md) enclosed by the front grows at $2a_s^3/G$, and some of that material remains in the infalling envelope. For fixed [mean particle mass](../../../../../mean-particle-mass.md), $\dot M_*\propto T^{3/2}$. The original similarity calculation establishes the static outer and free-fall inner regimes.[1977, pp. 488–497: expansion-wave collapse after central-core formation and the two radial density asymptotes](../../../../../self-similar-collapse-of-isothermal-spheres-and-star-formation-by-frank-h-shu.md)

The model assumes [spherical symmetry](../../../../../spherical-symmetry.md), isothermality and negligible [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) or magnetic stresses. An asymmetric [optically thick](../../../../../optically-thick-medium.md) [spectral line](../../../../../spectral-line.md) alone does not establish infall: an [optically thin](../../../../../optically-thin-medium.md) systemic-velocity tracer and spatial maps help distinguish inward motion from [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), foreground [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) and outflow.
