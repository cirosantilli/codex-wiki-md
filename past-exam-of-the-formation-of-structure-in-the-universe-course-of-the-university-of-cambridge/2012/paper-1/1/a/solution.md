<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[gas](../../../../../gas.md) heats when compression, [shocks](../../../../../shock-wave.md) or absorbed radiation increase its [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md). Important sources include [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) [shocks](../../../../../shock-wave.md), [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md), [supernovae](../../../../../supernova.md), [photoionization](../../../../../photoionization.md), photoelectric emission from [interstellar dust](../../../../../interstellar-dust.md), [cosmic rays](../../../../../cosmic-ray.md) and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) deposited by [active galactic nuclei](../../../../../active-galactic-nucleus.md). [photoionization](../../../../../photoionization.md) heating uses the photoelectron [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) above the [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) threshold; compression contributes mechanical work rather than [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md).

For specific [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) $u$, an idealized thermal balance is

$$
\rho\frac{Du}{Dt}=-P\nabla\cdot\mathbf v+\mathcal H-\mathcal C,
\qquad
\mathcal C=n_en_H\Lambda(T,Z).
$$

Here $\mathcal H,\mathcal C$ are powers per volume and $Z$ describes abundances. The [electron-hydrogen normalization of the cooling function](../../../../../electron-hydrogen-normalization-of-the-cooling-function.md) must be specified: some conventions instead factor out $n_H^2$. For a monatomic [gas](../../../../../gas.md) with total particle [density](../../../../../density.md) $n$,

$$
\boxed{t_{\rm cool}=\frac{3nk_BT}{2n_en_H\Lambda}}
$$

when heating is negligible. Compare this with the dynamical time: rapid cooling permits loss of [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) support, whereas long cooling times allow a hot atmosphere to persist.

[collisional excitation](../../../../../collisional-excitation.md) followed by [spontaneous emission](../../../../../spontaneous-emission.md) cools partially ionized [gas](../../../../../gas.md); [radiative recombination](../../../../../radiative-recombination.md) also produces continuum cooling. [metal](../../../../../metal.md) lines enhance cooling over substantial intermediate-temperature ranges, while [thermal bremsstrahlung](../../../../../thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) gives a roughly $\Lambda\propto T^{1/2}$ high-temperature tail. Atomic [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) cooling becomes inefficient below roughly $10^4\,\mathrm K$, where [molecular line cooling](../../../../../molecular-line-cooling.md) and [interstellar dust](../../../../../interstellar-dust.md) can be important. [inverse Compton scattering](../../../../../inverse-compton-scattering.md) cools hot [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) against a colder radiation field. A tabulated cooling curve assumes a composition and [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) model; [photoionization](../../../../../photoionization.md) or nonequilibrium [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) can change it.[2008, §§5 and 8: emission mechanisms, cooling normalization, cooling time and limits of collisional-ionization-equilibrium curves](../../../../../thermal-radiation-processes-by-jelle-s-kaastra-and-others.md)

[X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) line ratios and continuum shape constrain [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md), while the [emission measure](../../../../../emission-measure.md) constrains $\int n_en_H\,dV$. Turning that into a [density](../../../../../density.md) and [radiative gas cooling time](../../../../../optically-thin-gas-cooling-time.md) also requires a geometry; unresolved clumping, foreground [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) and multiphase [gas](../../../../../gas.md) are important controls.
