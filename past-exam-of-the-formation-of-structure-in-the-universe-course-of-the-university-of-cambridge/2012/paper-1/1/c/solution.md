↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[galactic feedback](../../../../../galactic-feedback.md) couples [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md) and [black-hole accretion](../../../../../black-hole-accretion.md) back to the [gas](../../../../../gas.md) supply. Stellar radiation, winds, [supernovae](../../../../../supernova.md) and [cosmic rays](../../../../../cosmic-ray.md) inject [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md); [active-galactic-nucleus feedback](../../../../../active-galactic-nucleus-feedback.md) adds radiation, jets and winds powered by [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md). Heating, [turbulence](../../../../../turbulence-split.md) or [gas](../../../../../gas.md) ejection can suppress later [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md), while compression can sometimes promote it. Negative feedback permits self-regulation rather than unrestricted [gas](../../../../../gas.md) consumption.

Define the [mass-loading factor](../../../../../mass-loading-factor.md) $\eta=\dot M_{\rm out}/\dot M_*$. If the launch speed scales with the [escape speed](../../../../../escape-velocity.md) and the injected [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) per stellar [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is fixed, [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) suggests $\eta\propto v_{\rm esc}^{-2}$; fixed injected [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) instead suggests $\eta\propto v_{\rm esc}^{-1}$. Coupling efficiencies, radiative losses and recycling change these ideal scalings. Thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) injected into dense [gas](../../../../../gas.md) can radiate away before driving an outflow, so an [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) budget alone does not demonstrate effective feedback.

Radio-filled [X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) cavities provide a mechanical diagnostic. For a cavity containing a relativistic fluid with $\gamma=4/3$, its [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) is $\gamma PV/(\gamma-1)\simeq4PV$; dividing by an estimated age gives a mechanical-power estimate. [Chandra X-ray Observatory](../../../../../chandra-x-ray-observatory.md) observations of the [Perseus cluster](../../../../../perseus-cluster.md) revealed fronts and ripples associated with radio bubbles, interpreted as weak [shocks](../../../../../shock-wave.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) waves that can transport jet [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) into the cooling atmosphere.[2003, abstract: radio bubbles, weak-shock fronts, ripples and the proposed heating/cooling balance](../../../../../a-deep-chandra-observation-of-the-perseus-cluster-shocks-and-ripples-by-a-c-fabian-and-others.md)

The test requires ambient [density](../../../../../density.md) and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) estimates, radio/[X-ray](../../../../../x-ray.md) spatial comparison, projected cavity volumes and an age model. Brightness edges must be distinguished from [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) and cool rims; wave [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is not automatically thermalized locally. These controls determine whether inferred mechanical heating can actually offset [radiative cooling](../../../../../radiative-cooling.md).
