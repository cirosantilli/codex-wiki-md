↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Galactic [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md) requires [gas](../../../../../gas.md) to collect into cold dense regions that can become self-gravitating. Disk-scale gravity, turbulent motions, shear, [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) and magnetic fields affect this collection; formation then proceeds within [molecular clouds](../../../../../molecular-cloud.md). A useful local parametrization is

$$
\dot\rho_\star=\epsilon_{\rm ff}\frac{\rho}{t_{\rm ff}},\qquad t_{\rm ff}=\sqrt{\frac{3\pi}{32G\rho}},
$$

where the [star-formation efficiency per free-fall time](../../../../../star-formation-efficiency-per-free-fall-time.md) summarizes inefficient conversion rather than requiring every cloud to collapse at once.

Across galactic disks the empirical [Kennicutt–Schmidt law](../../../../../kennicutt-schmidt-law.md) relates [star formation rate](../../../../../star-formation-rate.md) [surface density](../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) to [gas](../../../../../gas.md) [surface density](../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md), $\Sigma_{\rm SFR}\propto\Sigma_g^N$. A slope near $1.4$ is a familiar integrated total-[gas](../../../../../gas.md) fit, while molecular-[gas](../../../../../gas.md) relations in ordinary disks can be closer to linear; the slope and normalization depend on tracer, averaging scale and environment. The [gas-depletion time](../../../../../gas-depletion-time.md) $t_{\rm dep}=M_g/\Psi$ is the corresponding fuel-consumption scale, not a prediction that inflow and recycling cease.

Massive stars supply ionizing radiation, winds and [supernova](../../../../../supernova.md) energy and momentum. This [stellar feedback](../../../../../stellar-feedback.md) can disperse clouds, sustain turbulence and drive outflows, regulating rather than simply extinguishing all future [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md). Fresh accretion and recycled [gas](../../../../../gas.md) replenish the reservoir. UV and recombination-line emission trace young stars, while infrared emission traces absorbed and re-radiated light; extinction, stellar populations and the assumed [initial mass function](../../../../../initial-mass-function.md) affect inferred rates. **A galactic rate reflects supply, cloud formation and feedback together, rather than only the free-fall time of its densest [gas](../../../../../gas.md).**[Sections3–6: diagnostics, [gas](../../../../../gas.md) relations and physical interpretation](../../../../../star-formation-in-the-milky-way-and-nearby-galaxies-by-robert-c-kennicutt-jr-and-neal-j-evans-ii.md)
