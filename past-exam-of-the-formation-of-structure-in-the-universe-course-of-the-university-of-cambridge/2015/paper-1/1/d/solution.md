<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In a matter-dominated [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), the growing linear [density contrast](../../../../../density-contrast.md) is proportional to the scale factor. When $|\delta|\sim1$, linear evolution no longer describes collapse. An overdense region initially expands more slowly than the background, reaches [cosmological turnaround](../../../../../cosmological-turnaround.md), and then contracts under self-gravity.

The [spherical top-hat collapse model](../../../../../spherical-top-hat-collapse-model.md) supplies a useful idealization: pressureless shells obey $\ddot r=-GM/r^2$. At formal collapse, the linearly extrapolated growing mode is

$$
\boxed{\delta_c=\frac35\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)^{2/3}\simeq1.686.}
$$

This is a linear extrapolation, not the true finite nonlinear [density](../../../../../density.md) at that instant. In the ideal energy-conserving spherical model, [virial equilibrium](../../../../../virial-equilibrium.md) gives a virial [radius](../../../../../radius.md) about half the turnaround [radius](../../../../../radius.md); its mean [density](../../../../../density.md) at the collapse time is $18\pi^2\simeq178$ times the cosmic matter [density](../../../../../density.md).

Real perturbations are nonspherical: tides and anisotropic collapse produce sheets, filaments and haloes. Collisionless [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) develops [shell crossing](../../../../../shell-crossing.md) and phase mixing rather than radiatively losing its orbital energy. Baryonic [gas](../../../../../gas.md) can shock, cool and collect within the resulting potential wells. Hierarchical merging then assembles larger systems where small-scale fluctuations have sufficient initial power. [Press-Schechter formalism](../../../../../press-schechter-formalism.md) connects a collapse threshold with the statistics of the smoothed initial field, but halo abundances and internal structure need more than the single spherical model. These ideas apply after perturbations become nonlinear; one must not extend pressureless subhorizon equations through all radiation-era or horizon-scale conditions.[Section3.3: [gravitational collapse](../../../../../gravitational-collapse.md) and structure formation](../../../../../cosmology-by-david-tong.md)
