<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With constant [isothermal sound speed](../../../../../isothermal-sound-speed.md) $a_T$, the equation of state is $p=a_T^2\rho$. Spherical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) and [mass](../../../../../mass.md) continuity require

$$
a_T^2\frac{d\rho}{dr}=-\rho\frac{GM(r)}{r^2},\qquad\frac{dM}{dr}=4\pi r^2\rho.
$$

Try $\rho=A/r^2$. With no central point [mass](../../../../../mass.md), $M(r)=4\pi Ar$, and hydrostatic balance becomes $2a_T^2A=4\pi GA^2$. The positive-[density](../../../../../density.md) branch is therefore

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{a_T^2}{2\pi G},\qquad\rho=\frac{a_T^2}{2\pi Gr^2},\qquad M(r)=\frac{2a_T^2r}{G}.}
$$

The constant external [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) fixes the truncation [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $r_s$ through $p(r_s)=p_0$, giving

$$
\boxed{r_s=\frac{a_T^2}{\sqrt{2\pi Gp_0}},\qquad M_s=\frac{2a_T^2r_s}{G}.}
$$

This is the [pressure-truncated singular isothermal gas sphere](../../../../../pressure-truncated-singular-isothermal-gas-sphere.md), one hydrostatic solution, not a claim that every isothermal cloud has a singular centre. Its central [density](../../../../../density.md) diverges, but its enclosed [mass](../../../../../mass.md) tends to zero as $r\to0$.
