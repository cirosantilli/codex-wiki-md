<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a monatomic [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), write the thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) as $u=3nk_BT/2$. At low [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md), radiative loss uses an [astrophysical cooling function](../../../../../astrophysical-cooling-function.md), often written $\mathcal C=n_en_H\Lambda(T,Z)$, while $\mathcal H$ contains heating. At fixed [density](../../../../../density.md) the net [radiative gas cooling time](../../../../../optically-thin-gas-cooling-time.md) is approximately $u/(\mathcal C-\mathcal H)$ when the denominator is positive; a cooling flow closer to constant [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) must include [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) and compressional work. Comparing this time with the [free-fall time of a uniform sphere](../../../../../free-fall-time-of-a-uniform-sphere.md) determines whether [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) can be lost during gravitational contraction.

Hot ionized gas cools by [bremsstrahlung](../../../../../bremsstrahlung.md) and [recombination](../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md); [collisional excitation](../../../../../collisional-excitation.md) followed by [photon](../../../../../photon.md) escape supplies atomic and metal-line cooling at lower [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md). [molecular line cooling](../../../../../molecular-line-cooling.md) through rotational or vibrational lines and [dust thermal emission](../../../../../dust-thermal-emission.md) become important in cold dense gas. Abundances, [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) state, density-dependent collisional de-excitation, and [photon](../../../../../photon.md) trapping all change the cooling function. Optically thick gas requires [radiative transfer](../../../../../radiative-transfer.md), not a simple local density-squared loss law.

Heating comes from [photoionization](../../../../../photoionization.md), [grain photoelectric heating](../../../../../grain-photoelectric-heating.md) driven by ultraviolet light, [cosmic rays](../../../../../cosmic-ray.md), [shocks](../../../../../shock-wave.md), turbulent dissipation and compression. [photoionization heating](../../../../../photoionization-heating.md) deposits the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) above the [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) threshold as electron [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). A [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md) satisfies $\mathcal H=\mathcal C$; its stability depends on how the net loss changes under the relevant [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) or [density](../../../../../density.md) constraint, not merely on equality. Different stable balances permit a multiphase [interstellar medium](../../../../../interstellar-medium.md). Spectral-line ratios constrain [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md), but interpreting them requires [density](../../../../../density.md) diagnostics, [interstellar extinction](../../../../../interstellar-extinction.md) corrections and a radiation-field model.
