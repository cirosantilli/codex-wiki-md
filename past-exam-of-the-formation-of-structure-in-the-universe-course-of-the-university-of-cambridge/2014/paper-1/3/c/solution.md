<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Stellar [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md), [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) and [supernovae](../../../../../supernova.md) return [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and metals to surrounding gas. Heating, [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md), turbulent support and gas removal can reduce subsequent [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md); swept-up shells can also compress gas locally. Whether thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) drives an outflow depends on its [radiative gas cooling time](../../../../../optically-thin-gas-cooling-time.md) relative to expansion: radiative losses can remove [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) while leaving substantial [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) transfer. Thus deposited [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) alone does not specify the dynamical effect.

The [galactic gas regulator](../../../../../galactic-gas-regulator.md) has [mass](../../../../../mass.md) budget $\dot M_g=\dot M_{\rm in}-(1-R+\eta)\Psi$, where $R$ is the returned fraction and $\eta=\dot M_{\rm out}/\Psi$ is the [mass-loading factor](../../../../../mass-loading-factor.md). In a quasi-steady state, $\Psi\simeq\dot M_{\rm in}/(1-R+\eta)$. This connects feedback to the available gas supply. A wind's kinetic power $\dot M_{\rm out}v_w^2/2$ and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) flux $\dot M_{\rm out}v_w$ must fit the source budget and gravitational binding cost. Gas that fails to escape may form a [galactic fountain](../../../../../galactic-fountain.md) and later return; preferential metal loss changes chemical evolution.

Absorption against a galaxy's stellar continuum provides a concrete outflow test: compare interstellar sodium absorption with the systemic velocity and stellar absorption spectrum. A primary starburst survey found blueshifted interstellar absorption and an inclination dependence consistent with minor-axis outflows. Its saturated lines also illustrate why velocity detection is more direct than a unique column [density](../../../../../density.md) or [mass](../../../../../mass.md) flux.[Abstract: NaD spectra, systemic velocities, viewing angle and optical depth](../../../../../absorption-line-probes-of-gas-and-dust-in-galactic-superwinds-by-timothy-m-heckman-and-others.md) Deprojection, covering fraction, [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md), radius and the unseen hot phase limit total outflow-rate estimates.

An accreting [supermassive black hole](../../../../../supermassive-black-hole.md) adds [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md), winds and [astrophysical jets](../../../../../astrophysical-jet.md). Coupling these to the interstellar or [circumgalactic medium](../../../../../circumgalactic-medium.md) can suppress cooling or expel gas, especially in deep potentials where stellar driving alone may be inadequate. Jet cavities, line-emitting gas and multiphase outflows test different channels; coexisting activity and low [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md) alone do not prove a causal feedback history.
