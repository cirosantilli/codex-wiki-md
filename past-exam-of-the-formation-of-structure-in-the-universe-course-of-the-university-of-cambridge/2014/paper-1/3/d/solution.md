<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Small primordial fluctuations seed later structure. For [adiabatic cosmological perturbations](../../../../../adiabatic-initial-conditions.md) on scales outside the horizon, the [uniform-density curvature perturbation](../../../../../uniform-density-curvature-perturbation.md) is conserved when the [non-adiabatic pressure perturbation](../../../../../non-adiabatic-pressure-perturbation.md) and the decaying mode can be neglected. A [density contrast](../../../../../density-contrast.md) outside the horizon is gauge-dependent, so its apparent power-law growth should not be confused with directly measured local collapse.

For subhorizon, pressureless matter, the linearized [continuity equation](../../../../../continuity-equation.md), [Euler equation](../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) and [Poisson equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md) combine into

$$
\boxed{\ddot\delta+2H\dot\delta-4\pi G\bar\rho_m\delta=0}.
$$

Hubble expansion damps peculiar motion; [self-gravity](../../../../../self-gravity.md) amplifies overdensities. During [radiation domination](../../../../../radiation-domination.md), [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) drives acoustic oscillations and [cold dark matter](../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md) growth after horizon entry is only logarithmic. During [matter domination](../../../../../matter-domination.md) the growing and decaying solutions are $\delta\propto a$ and $\delta\propto a^{-3/2}$. Dark-energy domination later suppresses growth relative to the matter-era law.

For a pressure-supported fluid the Fourier-mode equation also contains $+c_s^2k^2\delta/a^2$, giving a [Jeans length](../../../../../jeans-length.md) separating acoustic support from gravitational growth. Before [recombination](../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md), [baryons](../../../../../baryon.md) are tightly coupled to [photons](../../../../../photon.md) and oscillate rather than freely following [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md). [Silk damping](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-diffusion-damping.md) from photon diffusion damps small-scale photon-baryon fluctuations; after decoupling, [baryons](../../../../../baryon.md) can fall into dark-matter potential wells. [free streaming](../../../../../free-streaming.md) smooths species-dependent scales and distinguishes cold from warm or hot [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md).

Schematically, the linear [matter power spectrum](../../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md) can be written $P_\delta(k,a)=A k^{n_s}T^2(k)D^2(a)$ under the usual primordial-curvature normalization. The [cosmological transfer function](../../../../../cosmological-transfer-function.md) records horizon entry, matter-radiation equality, acoustic physics and damping; the [linear growth factor](../../../../../linear-growth-factor.md) records later amplification. [density contrasts](../../../../../density-contrast.md) approaching unity require nonlinear collapse and halo formation beyond this approximation. [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) anisotropies and [galaxy clustering](../../../../../galaxy-clustering.md) test the same initial perturbations through different transfer histories; [galaxy bias](../../../../../galaxy-bias.md), [redshift-space distortions](../../../../../redshift-space-distortions.md) and observational selection enter the galaxy comparison.
