<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A cold self-gravitating core initially contracts on a dynamical scale $t_{\rm ff}\sim(G\rho)^{-1/2}$ if cooling prevents [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) from halting collapse. Increasing [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) eventually traps [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md) and forms a pressure-supported [first hydrostatic core](../../../../../first-hydrostatic-core.md). Dissociation of [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md) absorbs compressional [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), softens the [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md) and triggers a [second protostellar collapse](../../../../../second-protostellar-collapse.md) to a compact [protostar](../../../../../protostar.md). Rotation produces a disk; angular-momentum transport permits continuing [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md), while outflows remove some [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md).

An accreting object's [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) includes a contribution of order $L_{\rm acc}\sim GM\dot M/R$, with a coefficient set by where [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is radiated or retained. After [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) subsides, gravitational contraction supplies internal [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) over the [Kelvin-Helmholtz cooling time](../../../../../kelvin-helmholtz-cooling-time.md) $t_{\rm KH}\sim GM^2/(RL_{\rm int})$. Hydrostatic contraction heats the interior even while [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is radiated, as follows from the [virial theorem](../../../../../virial-theorem.md) and the [negative heat capacity of a virialized gravitational system](../../../../../negative-heat-capacity-of-a-virialized-gravitational-system.md). [accretion luminosity](../../../../../accretion-luminosity.md) must not be substituted for $L_{\rm int}$ in the contraction time without an energy-budget justification.

Low-mass, nearly fully convective [pre-main-sequence stars](../../../../../pre-main-sequence-star.md) contract down a [Hayashi track](../../../../../hayashi-track.md) at roughly constant [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) and decreasing radius and [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md). A developing [radiative core](../../../../../radiative-core.md) can lead onto a [Henyey track](../../../../../henyey-track.md) toward higher [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md); the route depends on [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and opacity. The [deuterium-burning energy-generation rate](../../../../../deuterium-burning-energy-generation-rate.md) can temporarily moderate contraction, but sustained core [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) marks arrival on the [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md). Massive objects may begin [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) while still accreting, and substellar objects never establish long-lived [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md). An [infrared](../../../../../infrared.md) spectral class reflects envelope and disk emission as well as inclination and [interstellar extinction](../../../../../interstellar-extinction.md), so it is not a unique stellar age or interior evolutionary track.
