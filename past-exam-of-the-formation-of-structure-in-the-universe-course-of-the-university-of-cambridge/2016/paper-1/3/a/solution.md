<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**A [protostar](../../../../../protostar.md) forms when a self-gravitating cloud fragment can contract faster than pressure support and energy loss can arrest it.** A useful local scale is the [Jeans length](../../../../../jeans-length.md), $\lambda_J\sim c_s/\sqrt{G\rho}$, with characteristic [Jeans mass](../../../../../jeans-mass.md) $M_J\sim c_s^3/(G^{3/2}\rho^{1/2})$ and free-fall time

$$
t_{\rm ff}=\sqrt{\frac{3\pi}{32G\rho}}
$$

for an initially uniform sphere. Geometry, [turbulence](../../../../../turbulence-split.md) and magnetic support change the detailed threshold; none of these estimates asserts that every cloud contracts as a uniform sphere.

While cooling is efficient, compression is nearly isothermal. Then $M_J$ decreases as the density rises, allowing fragmentation. Rotation becomes increasingly important during contraction because a parcel conserving [specific angular momentum](../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) $j$ encounters a centrifugal scale $r_c\sim j^2/(GM)$. The resulting [accretion disc](../../../../../accretion-disk.md) must transport [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) before matter can reach the central object. Infall, a dense accreting interior and an outer envelope can therefore coexist; a [protostar](../../../../../protostar.md) is not initially an isolated fixed-mass [star](../../../../../star.md).

Increasing [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) eventually slows [radiative cooling](../../../../../radiative-cooling.md). The central gas then becomes less compressible and can form a pressure-supported first core. [Molecular hydrogen](../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md) dissociation absorbs compressional energy and can trigger a second collapse before a compact stellar core is established. Subsequent [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) supplies a [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) of order

$$
L_{\rm acc}\sim\frac{GM\dot M}{R}.
$$

The retained fraction depends on shock and disc radiation, so this expression is an energy scale rather than a guaranteed [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) all emerging from the stellar surface.

A central accreting core can be nearly hydrostatic on its short sound-crossing time while its mass, [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) and radius evolve on longer times. A low-mass object is often largely convective because of its opacity and steep [radiative temperature gradient](../../../../../radiative-temperature-gradient.md); deuterium burning can temporarily contribute to support and moderate contraction. Neither [convection](../../../../../convection-split.md) nor the timing of deuterium ignition is universal across all masses and [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) histories.

When the main [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) phase ends, gravitational contraction remains an energy source. The [virial theorem](../../../../../virial-theorem.md) explains how contraction both heats the interior and supplies radiation: for an ideal-gas-supported object with negligible surface-pressure correction, $2U+W\simeq0$, so only part of the released [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) appears as increased [thermal energy](../../../../../thermal-energy.md). The corresponding Kelvin-Helmholtz time is

$$
t_{\rm KH}\sim\frac{GM^2}{RL}.
$$

Fully convective pre-main-sequence evolution approximately follows a [Hayashi track](../../../../../hayashi-track.md), with decreasing radius and [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) at comparatively weakly changing [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md). Objects developing radiative interiors can follow [Henyey tracks](../../../../../henyey-track.md). Eventually sustained core [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) balances the [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) and the [star](../../../../../star.md) reaches the [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md). Massive objects can ignite [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) while substantial [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) continues, so “[accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) ends, then [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) ignites” is not a universal ordering.

A useful structural distinction is therefore between an accretion-powered [protostar](../../../../../protostar.md) and a largely fixed-mass pre-main-sequence contraction model. An infrared-bright envelope or disc traces obscured formation and reprocessing of radiation; a schematic evolutionary track should not replace the actual mass- and entropy-dependent stellar-structure problem.
