↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Galactic [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md) is limited by how much gas becomes cold, shielded and self-gravitating, and by how long it remains in that state. A useful local estimate is

$$
\dot\rho_*\simeq\epsilon_{\rm ff}\frac{\rho_{\rm sf}}{t_{\rm ff}},\qquad
t_{\rm ff}=\sqrt{\frac{3\pi}{32G\rho_{\rm sf}}}.
$$

The [star-formation efficiency per free-fall time](../../../../../star-formation-efficiency-per-free-fall-time.md) $\epsilon_{\rm ff}$ absorbs the fact that [turbulence](../../../../../turbulence-split.md), magnetic support and [stellar feedback](../../../../../stellar-feedback.md) prevent all eligible gas from collapsing in one [free-fall time of a uniform sphere](../../../../../free-fall-time-of-a-uniform-sphere.md). Rotation and disk stability influence the supply of dense clouds, while [gravitational collapse](../../../../../gravitational-collapse.md) and [gravitational fragmentation](../../../../../gravitational-fragmentation.md) operate inside them.

For galaxy-averaged measurements, the [Kennicutt–Schmidt law](../../../../../kennicutt-schmidt-law.md) has $\Sigma_{\rm SFR}\propto\Sigma_g^N$, with the classic mixed spiral/starburst sample giving $N\simeq1.4$. This empirical relation was obtained using [H-alpha](../../../../../h-alpha.md) and far-infrared star-formation tracers together with atomic-hydrogen and CO gas measurements.[Abstract: sample, tracer combination and fitted index 1.4 plus or minus 0.15](../../../../../the-global-schmidt-law-in-star-forming-galaxies-by-robert-c-kennicutt-jr.md) Dust attenuation, contamination by older stellar heating, the stellar [initial mass function](../../../../../initial-mass-function.md) and the CO-to-molecular-gas conversion must be controlled. A disk-averaged total-gas exponent is not automatically a small-scale cloud law.

Young stars then photoionize gas, drive winds and eventually inject [supernova](../../../../../supernova.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md). These processes can disrupt clouds, sustain random motions or expel gas; [radiative cooling](../../../../../radiative-cooling.md) and fresh inflow rebuild the cold supply. **Large-scale [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md) is a regulated conversion of gas, rather than unimpeded free fall of the entire disk.** A long [gas-depletion time](../../../../../gas-depletion-time.md) $\Sigma_g/\Sigma_{\rm SFR}$ is compatible with short [galactic dynamical times](../../../../../galactic-dynamical-time.md) in the dense fraction.
