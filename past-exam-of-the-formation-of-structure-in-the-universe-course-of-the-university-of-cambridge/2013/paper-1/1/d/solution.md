<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A dense cloud core first collapses when [self-gravity](../../../../../self-gravity.md) overwhelms its effective support. While radiation escapes readily, compression can remain approximately isothermal and the [Jeans mass](../../../../../jeans-mass.md) decreases as [density](../../../../../density.md) rises, encouraging [gravitational fragmentation](../../../../../gravitational-fragmentation.md). Angular [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) usually leads to a disk rather than unrestricted spherical infall.

As the central [optical depth](../../../../../optical-depth.md) increases, cooling becomes slower than compression and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) produces a [first hydrostatic core](../../../../../first-hydrostatic-core.md). [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md) dissociation near $2000\ \mathrm K$ absorbs compressional [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and lowers the effective adiabatic response, permitting a [second collapse of a protostar](../../../../../second-collapse-of-a-protostar.md). After dissociation the gas stiffens and a much more compact stellar core forms. An [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) shock and surrounding disk then convert infall [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) into radiation. A small central core forms before most of the envelope arrives, as demonstrated in radiation-hydrodynamic collapse calculations.[Abstract: nonhomologous central core formation, subsequent envelope accretion and infrared reprocessing](../../../../../numerical-calculations-of-the-dynamics-of-a-collapsing-proto-star-by-richard-b-larson.md)

The isothermal [inside-out collapse of a singular isothermal sphere](../../../../../inside-out-collapse-of-a-singular-isothermal-sphere.md) model provides one idealized supply law, $\dot M\sim a_T^3/G$, but rotation, [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and an unsteady reservoir modify real [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md). Its [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) scale is $L_{\rm acc}\sim GM\dot M/r_*$. Dust can absorb the emerging short-wavelength radiation and re-emit it in the infrared, so an embedded source need not have a directly visible stellar photosphere.

As envelope [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) declines, gravitational contraction becomes important on the [Kelvin-Helmholtz cooling time](../../../../../kelvin-helmholtz-cooling-time.md) $t_{\rm KH}\sim GM^2/(r_*L)$. [deuterium-burning energy-generation rate](../../../../../deuterium-burning-energy-generation-rate.md) can temporarily influence the radius; sustained core [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) eventually establishes the [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md) if the final [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is sufficient. A low-mass convective object approaches along a Hayashi-like track, while more massive objects need not share that track. **[accretion](../../../../../accretion.md), contraction and nuclear burning are distinct [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) sources**, and a [protostar](../../../../../protostar.md) is not generally a fixed-mass object.
