[heating and cooling balance in the interstellar medium](../../../../../heating-and-cooling-balance-in-the-interstellar-medium.md) depends on [density](../../../../../density.md), [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md), radiation and shielding. Write net cooling per volume as $\mathcal C=n^2\Lambda(T)-n\Gamma$. Ultraviolet [grain photoelectric heating](../../../../../grain-photoelectric-heating.md) is important in diffuse neutral gas; [photoionization](../../../../../photoionization.md) heats ionized regions, [cosmic rays](../../../../../cosmic-ray.md) penetrate shielded gas, and [shock waves](../../../../../shock-wave.md) from [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) and [supernovae](../../../../../supernova.md) supply hot gas. Cooling includes collisionally excited atomic lines, fine-structure metal lines at lower [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md), molecular lines in shielded clouds, and continuum radiation and dust emission where appropriate. The heating efficiency and cooling abundances matter as much as a nominal [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).

A [multiphase interstellar medium](../../../../../multiphase-interstellar-medium.md) can contain a [cold neutral medium](../../../../../cold-neutral-medium.md) near $10^2\ \mathrm K$, a [warm neutral medium](../../../../../warm-neutral-medium.md) and [warm ionized medium](../../../../../warm-ionized-medium.md) near $10^4\ \mathrm K$, a [hot ionized medium](../../../../../hot-ionized-medium.md) near $10^6\ \mathrm K$, and still colder [molecular clouds](../../../../../molecular-cloud.md). Cold and warm atomic equilibrium branches can coexist at similar thermal [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) over a finite [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) interval; this is a calculated heating/cooling balance, not a requirement that all galactic gas have the same [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md).[Abstract: cold and warm neutral equilibrium temperatures and coexistence between minimum and maximum pressures](../../../../../the-neutral-atomic-phases-of-the-ism-in-the-galaxy-by-mark-g-wolfire-and-others.md)

For net cooling per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $\mathcal L=\mathcal C/\rho$, an isobaric perturbation at equilibrium obeys $c_p\partial_t\delta T=-(\partial\mathcal L/\partial T)_p\delta T$. Thus **a negative constant-pressure cooling [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is thermally unstable**: a cool perturbation cools further and condenses. Sound crossing must be fast enough to maintain [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) balance; [thermal conduction](../../../../../thermal-conduction.md) suppresses sufficiently short [wavelengths](../../../../../wavelength.md). Supernova-driven motions, [magnetic pressure](../../../../../magnetic-pressure.md), finite cooling times and interfaces make the actual medium dynamic, so equilibrium branches describe useful phases rather than a static collection of perfectly separated components.
