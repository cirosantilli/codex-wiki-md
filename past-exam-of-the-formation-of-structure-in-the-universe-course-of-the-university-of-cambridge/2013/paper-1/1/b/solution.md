<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[differential rotation](../../../../../differential-rotation.md) winds a material arm because $d\Omega/dr\ne0$. A [spiral density wave](../../../../../spiral-density-wave.md) instead has a [pattern speed](../../../../../pattern-speed.md) different from the material speed: stars and gas can pass through its crests. Collective [self-gravity](../../../../../self-gravity.md), [orbital resonances](../../../../../orbital-resonance.md) and shear can sustain or repeatedly amplify non-axisymmetric patterns; a visible spiral need not be a single permanent rigid material structure.

For a razor-thin gas disk, local tightly wound disturbances satisfy

$$
(\omega-m\Omega)^2=\kappa^2+c_s^2k^2-2\pi G\Sigma|k|,\qquad
\kappa^2=4\Omega^2+r\frac{d\Omega^2}{dr}.
$$

Rotation stabilizes long [wavelengths](../../../../../wavelength.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) stabilizes short ones. Minimizing the axisymmetric right-hand side over $|k|$ gives $|k|_*=\pi G\Sigma/c_s^2$ and

$$
\boxed{Q_g=\frac{c_s\kappa}{\pi G\Sigma}\geq1}
$$

for local axisymmetric stability. A collisionless stellar disk instead has $Q_*=\sigma_R\kappa/(3.36G\Sigma_*)$; the radial random [velocity dispersion](../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md) is not literally a gas [sound speed](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md).[Abstract: collisionless axisymmetric threshold and coefficient 3.36](../../../../../on-the-gravitational-stability-of-a-disk-of-stars-by-alar-toomre.md)

**Local axisymmetric stability does not guarantee global disk stability.** Gas and stars gravitate together, thickness modifies the short-scale force, and a disk with $Q>1$ may still have spiral or bar responses. Dissipation in gas can generate [shock waves](../../../../../shock-wave.md) and concentrate [star formation](../../../../../star-formation.md) near a spiral crest; stellar random motions tend to reduce the collective response.
