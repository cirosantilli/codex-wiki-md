# Quantum propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Time-evolution operator](time-evolution-operator.md)

The position-space kernel of the [time-evolution operator](time-evolution-operator.md) propagates a [wave function](wave-function-split.md) by $\psi(x,t)=\int K_U(x,t;x',t')\psi(x',t')\,dx'$. It tends to $\delta(x-x')$ as $t\to t'$. A retarded convention multiplies it by the [Heaviside step function](heaviside-step-function.md): $K_R=\Theta(t-t')K_U$. This convention obeys $(i\hbar\partial_t-H)K_R=i\hbar\delta(t-t')\delta(x-x')$; dividing by $i\hbar$ gives the [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) with a unit delta source. The time phase for an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) is always $e^{-iE(t-t')/\hbar}$.
