# Slater-Condon rules

↑ **Parent:** [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md)

Rules expressing Hamiltonian matrix elements between [Slater determinants](slater-determinant.md) through one- and two-electron orbital integrals. A two-body Hamiltonian cannot connect determinants differing in more than two spin-orbitals.
