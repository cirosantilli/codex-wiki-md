# Causal angular velocity bounds in Kerr spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr ergoregion](kerr-ergoregion.md)

Outside a subextremal [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md), regular [causal curves](causal-curve.md) obey $(-g_{t\phi}-\sqrt D)/g_{\phi\phi}\le d\phi/dt\le(-g_{t\phi}+\sqrt D)/g_{\phi\phi}$, where $D=g_{t\phi}^2-g_{tt}g_{\phi\phi}>0$. Positive radial and polar metric terms establish this necessary bound. In the open [Kerr ergoregion](kerr-ergoregion.md) both endpoints are positive for $a>0$, forcing co-rotation.
