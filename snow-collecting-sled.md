# Snow-collecting sled

↑ **Parent:** [Variable-mass system](variable-mass-system.md)

A sled intercepting snow with zero horizontal [velocity](velocity.md) in the ground frame is a simple [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) example. At arrival rate $\sigma$, immediate removal with outgoing ground-frame horizontal [velocity](velocity.md) $v_{\mathrm{out}}$ gives $M\dot v=-\sigma v_{\mathrm{out}}$ for constant moving [mass](mass.md) $M$. Removal sideways in the sled frame has $v_{\mathrm{out}}=v$ and exponential deceleration; removal sideways in the ground frame has $v_{\mathrm{out}}=0$ and constant sled [velocity](velocity.md). Retained snow instead gives $(M+\sigma t)v=Mv_0$. The outgoing reference frame must be specified.
