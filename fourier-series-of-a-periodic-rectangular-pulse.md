# Fourier series of a periodic rectangular pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

An even train of [rectangular pulses](rectangular-function.md) of width $w<T$, [period](period-of-a-function.md) $T$ and unit area per pulse has [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) $a_n=(2/T)\operatorname{sinc}(\pi nw/T)$ for $n\geq0$. Here the [sinc function](sinc-function.md) is normalized as $\operatorname{sinc}z=\sin z/z$, and the constant contribution is $a_0/2=1/T$. Integrating the [cosine](cosine.md) over the pulse proves the formula; evenness eliminates the [sine](sine.md) coefficients. At a pulse edge, [pointwise convergence of a piecewise smooth Fourier series](pointwise-convergence-of-a-piecewise-smooth-fourier-series.md) gives the mean of the one-sided values rather than a prescribed endpoint value.
