# Uniformly rotating barotropic star

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical fluid dynamics](astrophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)

In a connected inviscid uniformly rotating [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md), momentum balance makes the displayed sum constant. The [pressure](pressure.md) term is the [barotropic enthalpy function](barotropic-enthalpy-function.md). Perturbations experience [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md), and their rotating-frame frequency depends on the chosen exponential sign convention.
