# Idempotent function

↑ **Parent:** [Self-map](self-map.md)

A [self-map](self-map.md) $f:X\to X$ is idempotent if its [function composition](function-composition.md) with itself leaves it unchanged: $f(f(x))=f(x)$ for every $x$. Equivalently, every element of its [image of a function](image-of-a-function.md) is a [fixed point](fixed-point.md). For example, the [function](function-split.md) on $\{a,b,c\}$ sending $a$ to $b$, $b$ to $b$, and $c$ to $c$ is idempotent. A second application makes no further change.
