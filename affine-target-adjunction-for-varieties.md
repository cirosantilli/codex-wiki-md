# Affine-target adjunction for varieties

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md)

For a classical [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) $X$ and [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) $Y$, a homomorphism on the indicated rings gives a morphism by evaluating the images of affine coordinate generators at each point of $X$. The relations defining $Y$ remain zero. Local fractions pull back to local regular fractions, proving regularity; the [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) generators also prove uniqueness.
