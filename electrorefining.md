# Electrorefining

↑ **Parent:** [Electrolysis](electrolysis.md)

Electrolytic purification by transferring the desired metal from an impure dissolving [anode](anode.md) to a purer [cathode](cathode.md). Impurities can stay dissolved, remain as solids or form precipitated [anode slime](anode-slime.md). Potential control, transport and exclusion of solid contamination are as important as tabulated standard potentials.
