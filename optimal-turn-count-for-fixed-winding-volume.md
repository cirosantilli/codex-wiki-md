# Optimal turn count for fixed winding volume

↑ **Parent:** [Galvanometer](galvanometer.md)

For fixed mean turn length and fixed conductor volume, a coil of $N$ turns has conductor length proportional to $N$ and cross-sectional area proportional to $1/N$. Its [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) is therefore $R_{\rm coil}=aN^2$. With source [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) $\mathcal E$ and [internal resistance](internal-resistance.md) $R_s$, suppose the small-deflection driving [torque](torque.md) is proportional to $NI=N\mathcal E/(R_s+aN^2)$. Its derivative has the sign of $R_s-aN^2$, so the maximum occurs at $R_{\rm coil}=R_s$. Geometry, damping and finite response time can alter the practical optimization; this is a conditional winding model, not a universal rule for all [galvanometers](galvanometer.md).
