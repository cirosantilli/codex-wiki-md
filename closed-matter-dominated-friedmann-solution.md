# Closed matter-dominated Friedmann solution

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

A positively curved [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) universe with zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) has the [cycloid](cycloid.md) parametrization displayed above. In [natural units](natural-units.md), if the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) is $\dot a^2=C/a-k$ with $C,k>0$, then $A=C/(2k)$ and $u=\sqrt{k}\tau$ for [conformal time](conformal-time.md) measured from the [Big Bang](big-bang.md). Substituting in $a'^2=Ca-ka^2$ verifies the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md); integrating $dt=a\,d\tau$ gives [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md). The universe reaches maximum size at $u=\pi$ and recollapses into a [Big Crunch](big-crunch.md) at $u=2\pi$.
