# Convolution of measures on the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Convolution](convolution.md)

For finite [complex measures](complex-measure.md) on the additive [circle group](circle-group.md), [convolution](convolution.md) is the [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md) of their product under addition modulo $2\pi$. Equivalently

$$
\int f\,d(\mu*\nu)=\iint f(x+y)d\mu(x)d\nu(y).
$$

Bounded tests are integrable against the product variation [measure](measure-split.md), so [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) applies. Testing the factorization $e^{-in(x+y)}=e^{-inx}e^{-iny}$ proves the displayed [Fourier coefficient of a measure on the circle](fourier-coefficient-of-a-measure-on-the-circle.md) identity. The [convolution](convolution.md) of two [probability measures](probability-measure.md) is again a [probability measure](probability-measure.md). On these [measures](measure-split.md) [convolution](convolution.md) preserves simultaneous convergence in the [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md): multiply the converging coefficients and use their common unit variation bound and dense trigonometric tests.
