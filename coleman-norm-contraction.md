# Coleman norm contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman norm operator](coleman-norm-operator.md)

For odd $p$, the [Coleman norm operator](coleman-norm-operator.md) satisfies the displayed inclusion for every $k\geq1$. Write $M_h$ for multiplication by $h$ on the [finite free module](finite-free-module.md) $R$ over $\phi(R)$. Its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is $p\phi(S(h))$, where $S$ is the [Coleman trace operator](coleman-trace-operator.md). In $\det(I+p^kM_h)$ the linear term is therefore divisible by $p^{k+1}$, and all higher terms are divisible by $p^{2k}$. [Congruence reflection for Frobenius substitution](congruence-reflection-for-frobenius-substitution.md) gives the assertion. A norm-fixed element of $1+pR$ lies in $1+p^kR$ for every $k$ and hence equals $1$.
