# Quantum state

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum system](quantum-system-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_state)

A [quantum state](quantum-state.md) describes a preparation through its predicted [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) [probabilities](probability.md). In the [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) formulation, a [pure state](pure-state.md) is a [quantum state ray](quantum-state-ray.md): a normalized [vector](vector.md) representative $|\psi\rangle$ satisfies $\langle\psi|\psi\rangle=1$, and representatives differing by a [global phase](global-phase.md) describe the same [quantum state](quantum-state.md). [Preskill, Foundations I: States and Ensembles, §2.1, printed pp. 1–3, equation (2.1) and the definition of a ray](foundations-i-states-and-ensembles-by-john-preskill.md)

Both [pure states](pure-state.md) and [mixed states](mixed-state.md) are represented by a [density operator](density-matrix-split.md) on the chosen complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md):

$$
\rho=\rho^\dagger,\qquad \langle v|\rho|v\rangle\geq0\text{ for every }|v\rangle,\qquad \operatorname{Tr}\rho=1.
$$

Thus $\rho$ is [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md), [positive semidefinite](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) and of unit [trace](matrix-trace.md); in infinite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) it must also be a [trace-class operator](trace-class-operator.md). For a normalized [pure state](pure-state.md), $\rho=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ is a rank-one [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md). A [mixed state](mixed-state.md) has no single such [vector](vector.md) representative. Its [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) is the [matrix](matrix.md) of $\rho$ in a chosen [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). [Watrous, The Theory of Quantum Information, §1.1.2, printed pp. 17–18, equations (1.94)–(1.97), Hermitian, positive semidefinite and density operators; finite-dimensional setting](the-theory-of-quantum-information-by-john-watrous/mathematical-preliminaries.md) For a discrete [POVM](positive-operator-valued-measure.md) with effects $E_a\geq0$ and $\sum_aE_a=I$, the [Born rule](born-rule.md) reads

$$
P(a)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho E_a),\qquad
P(a)=\langle\psi|E_a|\psi\rangle\text{ for a pure state}.
$$

These constraints and [Born rule](born-rule.md) probabilities describe arbitrary finite-dimensional preparations. [Watrous, The Theory of Quantum Information, §2.1.2, printed pp. 62–64, Definition 2.5 and pure states; §2.3, Definition 2.34, measurement probabilities](the-theory-of-quantum-information-by-john-watrous/basic-notions-of-quantum-information.md)

Randomly choosing normalized [pure states](pure-state.md) with [probabilities](probability.md) $w_j$ and forgetting the label gives

$$
\rho=\sum_j w_j|\psi_j\rangle\langle\psi_j|,\qquad w_j\geq0,\quad\sum_jw_j=1.
$$

This [quantum state ensemble](quantum-state-ensemble.md) averages [probabilities](probability.md); a [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md) adds [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md). Several ensembles can give the same [density operator](density-matrix-split.md). [Quantum entanglement](entangled-state.md) can make a [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) mixed while the joint [quantum state](quantum-state.md) is pure. [Preskill, Foundations I: States and Ensembles, §2.3.1, printed pp. 14–18, equations (2.51)–(2.61), partial trace and ensemble interpretation](foundations-i-states-and-ensembles-by-john-preskill.md)

The preparation, the choice of [basis](basis.md), and the measurement are separate ingredients. A [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) $\psi(x)=\langle x|\psi\rangle$ is the position representation of a [pure state](pure-state.md); a general [mixed state](mixed-state.md) requires a [density operator](density-matrix-split.md). For a closed [quantum system](quantum-system-split.md), a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) evolves it as $\rho\mapsto U\rho U^\dagger$, preserving its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) and [purity of a density operator](purity-of-a-density-operator.md). [Preskill, Foundations I: States and Ensembles, §2.3.4, printed pp. 22–23, equation (2.79)](foundations-i-states-and-ensembles-by-john-preskill.md) The [Bloch sphere](bloch-sphere.md) and [Bloch ball](bloch-ball.md) give the [qubit](qubit.md) geometry; [quantum state tomography](quantum-state-tomography.md) connects repeated preparations to an estimated [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md); [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) treats the conditional state after an outcome.
