# Financial asset

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)

A traded claim or instrument carrying a current price and future payments or resale value. In a one-period [financial market](financial-market.md), its terminal payoff is a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) and a [portfolio](investment-portfolio.md) combines payoffs linearly through its asset holdings. A [stock](stock.md), a [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) and a [bank account](bank-account.md) are basic examples. Dividends require treating the full gain process rather than only resale prices.
