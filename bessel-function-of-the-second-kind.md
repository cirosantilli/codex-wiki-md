# Bessel function of the second kind

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel function](bessel-function.md)

For noninteger order, $Y_\nu=(\cos(\pi\nu)J_\nu-J_{-\nu})/\sin(\pi\nu)$; the integer-order function is defined by a limit. It gives a second independent solution of the [Bessel differential equation](bessel-differential-equation.md). At order zero, $Y_0(z)\sim(2/\pi)[\log(z/2)+\gamma]$ near positive zero and $Y_0(z)\sim\sqrt{2/(\pi z)}\sin(z-\pi/4)$ at large positive argument, where $\gamma$ is the [Euler--Mascheroni constant](euler-s-constant.md).
