# Stable homotopy theory

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_homotopy_theory)

[Stable homotopy theory](stable-homotopy-theory.md) studies [topological spectra](spectrum-topology.md), which retain the information that persists under repeated [reduced suspension](reduced-suspension.md). Its [stable homotopy category](stable-homotopy-category.md) admits invertible [suspension of spectra](suspension-of-spectra.md) and exact [cofiber sequences of spectra](cofiber-sequence-of-spectra.md).
