# Frame bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Principal bundle](principal-bundle.md)

The frame bundle of an $n$-dimensional [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) has fiber over $x$ consisting of linear [isomorphisms](isomorphism.md) $u:\mathbb R^n\to T_xM$. Right composition by $GL(n,\mathbb R)$ makes it a [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md). A global [frame of a vector bundle](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) is a global [section of a fiber bundle](section-fiber-bundle.md) and trivializes the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md). A metric restricts the allowed frames to the [orthonormal frame bundle](orthonormal-frame-bundle.md).
