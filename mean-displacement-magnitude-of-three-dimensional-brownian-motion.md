# Mean displacement magnitude of three-dimensional Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Mean squared displacement](mean-squared-displacement.md)

For isotropic [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at zero with independent coordinate variances $2Dt$, the [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md) has density $(4\pi Dt)^{-3/2}e^{-|x|^2/(4Dt)}$. Integrating the radius with spherical volume element $4\pi r^2dr$ gives $\mathbb E|X_t|=4\sqrt{Dt/\pi}$. The [root mean square](root-mean-square.md) displacement is instead $\sqrt{6Dt}$, and the mean vector is zero. None of these is the microscopic path length.
