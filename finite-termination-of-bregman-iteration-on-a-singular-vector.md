# Finite termination of Bregman iteration on a singular vector

↑ **Parent:** [Bregman iteration](bregman-iteration.md)

For a [generalized singular vector](generalized-singular-vector.md), exact data $f=\gamma Ku_\lambda$, and fixed $\alpha>0$, a compatible branch of [Bregman iteration](bregman-iteration.md) has $p^k=\min\{k\gamma/\alpha,\lambda\}K^*Ku_\lambda$ and the displayed $u^k$. Verify these formulas with the [subgradient](subgradient.md) characterization of an [absolutely one-homogeneous functional](absolutely-one-homogeneous-functional.md); before activation the dual coefficient remains at most $\lambda$, and after activation it is $\lambda$. The branch reaches $\gamma u_\lambda$ at $k_*=\lceil\alpha\lambda/\gamma\rceil+1$. Every minimizer branch has the same predicted data, since the quadratic data fidelity is [strictly convex](strictly-convex-function.md) in $Ku$. Exact recovery of this particular vector for arbitrary branch choices needs uniqueness, for example injectivity of $K$. For $K(x,y)=x$ and $J(x,y)=|x|$, the second coordinate of every minimizer is arbitrary.
