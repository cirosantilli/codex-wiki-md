# Burnside basis theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Frattini quotient](frattini-quotient.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnside_basis_theorem)

A subset generates a finite p-group precisely when its images span the [Frattini quotient](frattini-quotient.md). Indeed $\langle S\rangle\Phi(G)=G$ implies $\langle S\rangle=G$, since otherwise a maximal subgroup contains both factors. Minimal generating sets correspond to vector-space bases in the quotient.
