# Long-run variance of a stationary process

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md)

With absolutely summable [autocovariance](autocovariance.md), the asymptotic variance of the sample mean satisfies

$$
T\operatorname{Var}(\overline X_T)\longrightarrow\sum_{h\in\mathbb Z}\gamma(h)=2\pi f(0).
$$

The sum is signed, rather than a sum of absolute values. It can be zero: the first difference of [strong white noise](strong-white-noise.md) has telescoping partial sums. A [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md) needs further dependence assumptions.
