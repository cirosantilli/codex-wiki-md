# Rank-to-index limit for subgroups of a free group

↑ **Parent:** [Nielsen–Schreier formula](nielsen-schreier-formula.md)

For finite-index subgroups of a rank-$n$ [free group](free-group.md), the [Nielsen–Schreier formula](nielsen-schreier-formula.md) gives the displayed quotient. Along any sequence whose finite indices tend to infinity, the quotient tends to $n-1$. Subtracting one from the rank before dividing gives the constant rank gradient $n-1$.
