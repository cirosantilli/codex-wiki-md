# Galois representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A Galois representation is a continuous [group representation](group-representation.md) of an [absolute Galois group](absolute-galois-group.md), usually on a vector space or lattice over a local field. Examples include the [cyclotomic character](cyclotomic-character.md) and the representations attached to [modular forms](modular-form.md). The coefficient topology is part of the definition.
