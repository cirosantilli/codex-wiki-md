# Base-p digit sum

↑ **Parent:** [P-adic valuation](p-adic-valuation.md)

If

$$
n=\sum_{r\geq0}\alpha_rp^r,
\qquad 0\leq\alpha_r<p,
$$

then the base-$p$ digit sum is $d_p(n)=\sum_r\alpha_r$. The [Legendre formula](legendre-s-formula.md) can equivalently be written

$$
v_p(n!)=\frac{n-d_p(n)}{p-1}.
$$
