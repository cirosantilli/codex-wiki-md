# Point accumulation for imaging in nanoscale topography

↑ **Parent:** [Super-resolution microscopy](super-resolution-microscopy.md)

Transiently binding fluorescent probes provide spatially separated single-emitter observations whose fitted positions accumulate into a structural map. Probe exchange can replenish bleached emitters. Precision, specificity, event density, optical background and drift all affect the usable resolution; accumulating events does not itself remove systematic labelling error.
