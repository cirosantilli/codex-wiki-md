# Magnetic arcade

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field line](magnetic-field-line.md)

A magnetic arcade is a family of arch-shaped [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md) joining two regions of a boundary. The boundary intersections are its footpoints. In a simple line-tied family, paired footpoints can be labelled by [Euler potentials](euler-potentials.md). A [decaying linear force-free magnetic arcade](decaying-linear-force-free-magnetic-arcade.md) is one explicitly solvable planar example; a general arcade need not be linear or force-free.
