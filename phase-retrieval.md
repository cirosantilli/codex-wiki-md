# Phase retrieval

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)

Recovering the phase information needed to reconstruct a signal from intensity or transform-magnitude measurements. Constraints on the signal or additional measurements are needed because magnitudes alone generally admit multiple solutions. [Frequency-resolved optical gating](frequency-resolved-optical-gating.md) supplies a nonlinear family of delayed spectra rather than only a single Fourier magnitude.
