# Chronological future

↑ **Parent:** [Causal hierarchy](causal-hierarchy.md)

The chronological future $I^+(S)$ of a subset $S$ of a [spacetime](spacetime.md) is the set of points reachable from $S$ by future-directed [timelike curves](timelike-curve.md). The chronological future is an [open set](open-set.md).
