# Preliminary Reference Earth Model

↑ **Parent:** [Radial density inversion of the Earth](radial-density-inversion-of-the-earth.md)

A one-dimensional reference model combining [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md) [speeds](speed.md), [density](density.md) and attenuation, developed by [Adam M. Dziewonski](adam-m-dziewonski.md) and [Don L. Anderson](don-l-anderson.md). It incorporates joint seismic and global integral constraints rather than treating [density](density.md) as a direct travel-time measurement.
