# Statistical segment length

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal chain](ideal-chain.md)

An effective step length describing a specified chain-count convention: an ideal [polymer](polymer-split.md) with $N$ counted units has mean-squared [end-to-end distance of a polymer chain](end-to-end-distance-of-a-polymer-chain.md) $\langle R^2\rangle=Nb^2$. If $N$ counts freely jointed coarse-grained units, $b$ is their [Kuhn length](kuhn-length.md). If $N$ counts chemical [repeat units](repeat-unit.md), the effective $b$ incorporates local conformational correlations and is not automatically a physical monomer diameter. A repeat-unit volume $v_0$ is independent information; setting $v_0\sim b^3$ is a scaling approximation.
