# Rectangular electromagnetic waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave.md)

A rectangular electromagnetic waveguide has a vacuum interior bounded by four [perfect conductor](perfect-conductor.md) walls parallel to its axis. The harmonic fields obey zero tangential [electric field](electric-field.md) and zero normal [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) at the walls. [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations.md) determine transverse fields from longitudinal components; a purely longitudinal oscillating [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) by itself cannot propagate, since its divergence would be nonzero.
