# Characteristic polynomials of AB and BA

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md)

For square matrices $A,B$ over a field,

$$
\det(tI-AB)=\det(tI-BA).
$$

If $A$ is invertible, the products are [similar matrices](matrix-similarity.md). In general, apply that case to $(A-sI)B$ and $B(A-sI)$ for all but finitely many $s$, then use polynomial identity in $s$.
