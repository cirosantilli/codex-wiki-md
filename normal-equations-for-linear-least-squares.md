# Normal equations for linear least squares

↑ **Parent:** [Linear regression](linear-regression-split.md)

Minimizing $\|y-X\theta\|^2$ gives the displayed equations by differentiation. If the design matrix has full column rank, the minimizer is unique and the fitted vector is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) of $y$ onto its column space. For an intercept and one explanatory variable, these equations give $\widehat\beta=S_{xy}/S_{xx}$ and $\widehat\alpha=\bar y-\widehat\beta\bar x$, provided $S_{xx}>0$.
