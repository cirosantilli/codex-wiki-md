# Universal three-point space for product embeddings

↑ **Parent:** [Topological embedding](topological-embedding.md)

Every [topological space](topological-space.md), including one without the [T0 space](kolmogorov-space.md) property, embeds into a power of this three-point space. For each [open set](open-set.md) $U$, use a coordinate equal to $2$ on $U$ and $0$ elsewhere. Its [inverse image](preimage.md) of the only proper nonempty [open set](open-set.md) is $U$. For each point $a$, add a coordinate equal to $1$ at $a$ and $0$ elsewhere. These coordinates are continuous because the subspace $\{0,1\}$ has the [indiscrete topology](indiscrete-topology.md), and they separate all points. The combined diagonal map is [injective](injective-function.md) and induces precisely the original [topology](topology-split.md), hence is a [topological embedding](topological-embedding.md).
