# Detailed balance without rapid convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Detailed balance](detailed-balance.md)

[Detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) does not imply that a [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md) is an [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md): the identity [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) is reversible for every [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md). Even for an [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md) with [aperiodicity](aperiodic-markov-chain.md), the two-state matrix with diagonal $1-\varepsilon$ and off-diagonal $\varepsilon$, $0<\varepsilon<1/2$, has uniform [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) and nontrivial [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $1-2\varepsilon$. From either fixed state its [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md) after $n$ steps is $\tfrac12(1-2\varepsilon)^n$, so convergence can be arbitrarily slow. Invariance, reachability and [mixing time](mixing-time-of-a-markov-chain.md) are distinct questions.
