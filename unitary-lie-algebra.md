# Unitary Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra of a matrix Lie group](lie-algebra-of-a-matrix-lie-group.md)

The real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of the [unitary group](unitary-group.md) is $\mathfrak u(N)=\{X:X^\dagger=-X\}$, of real dimension $N^2$. Differentiating unitarity gives necessity; exponentiating any [skew-Hermitian matrix](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) gives a curve in the group, proving sufficiency.
