# Weierstrass uniformization by half-period values

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

The [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) and its derivative give an isomorphism $z\mapsto(\wp(z),\wp'(z))$ from a one-dimensional [complex torus](complex-torus.md) to a [smooth plane cubic](nonsingular-plane-cubic.md), with $0$ sent to its identity at infinity. The three nonzero [two-torsion points of a complex torus](two-torsion-point-of-a-complex-torus.md) are zeros of $\wp'$. Its triple pole implies that these three zeros are simple. If two [half-period values of the Weierstrass elliptic function](half-period-values-of-the-weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) coincided, $\wp-e$ would have at least four zeros counted with [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md), contradicting its double pole. The three cubic roots are therefore distinct, proving [smoothness of an algebraic variety](smoothness-of-an-algebraic-variety.md). The degree-two map $\wp$ has fibers $\{z,-z\}$, distinguished by $\wp'$ away from the half-periods, proving bijectivity. A line pulls back to an [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md) with a triple pole, and the [zero-pole sum of an elliptic function](zero-pole-sum-of-an-elliptic-function.md) says that its three intersections sum to zero on the torus. This gives exactly the [chord-and-tangent group law](chord-and-tangent-group-law.md), including repeated intersections interpreted by [intersection multiplicity](intersection-multiplicity.md).
