# Survey window function

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy survey](galaxy-survey.md)

A survey window function describes the finite spatial coverage and weights of a [galaxy survey](galaxy-survey.md). Multiplication by this window in position space mixes [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) by convolution in [Fourier space](fourier-space.md). A dimension of extent $L$ cannot robustly measure wavelengths much larger than $L$; the fundamental [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) of a periodic box of that size is $2\pi/L$.
