# Connected chain of overlapping subsets

↑ **Parent:** [Connected space](connected-space.md)

A sequence of [connected subsets](connected-subset.md) with each consecutive pair intersecting has connected union. If the union admitted a separation, each [connected subset](connected-subset.md) would lie entirely on one side. Consecutive intersections force consecutive subsets onto the same side, hence all subsets onto one side, contradicting that both sides of the separation are nonempty.
