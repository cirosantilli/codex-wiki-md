<h1 id="b/6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let the two oriented surfaces carry positive area forms $\alpha$ and $\beta$, obtainable from oriented Riemannian metrics. A top-degree form on a surface is automatically closed. On their product set

$$
\omega=\pi_1^*\alpha+\pi_2^*\beta.
$$

It is closed, and

$$
\omega^2=2\pi_1^*\alpha\wedge\pi_2^*\beta
$$

is a nowhere-vanishing four-form; the two squared terms vanish because each comes from a two-dimensional factor. Thus **the product is symplectic**, with no [compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md) assumption needed.
