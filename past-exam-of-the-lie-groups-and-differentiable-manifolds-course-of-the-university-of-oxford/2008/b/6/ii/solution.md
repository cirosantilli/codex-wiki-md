<h1 id="b/6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the standard convention that the [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [manifold](../../../../../topological-manifold-split.md) is nonempty and has no boundary. Orient it by $\omega^m$. Since $d\omega=0$, every power $\omega^k$ is closed. If $\omega^k=d\eta$ for some $1\le k\le m$, then

$$
\omega^m=d(\eta\wedge\omega^{m-k}),
$$

again by Leibniz and closedness. [Stokes theorem](../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) would give $\int_M\omega^m=0$. But this integral is strictly positive in the symplectic [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md), a contradiction. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[\omega^k]\ne0\in H^{2k}_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\quad(1\le k\le m)}.
$$

The boundaryless hypothesis matters: a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) disk has a symplectic area form but no nonzero ordinary second cohomology class.
