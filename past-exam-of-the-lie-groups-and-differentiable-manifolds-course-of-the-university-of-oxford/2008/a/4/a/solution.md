<h1 id="a/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

As usual here, representations are finite-dimensional and [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md). Start with any positive definite [Hermitian form](../../../../../hermitian-form.md) $h_0$ and normalized [Haar measure](../../../../../haar-measure.md) $\mu$ on the [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [group](../../../../../group-split.md). Define

$$
h(v,w)=\int_Gh_0(gv,gw)\,d\mu(g).
$$

This is Hermitian, sesquilinear, and positive definite: for $v\ne0$ its [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) integrand $h_0(gv,gv)$ is strictly positive. Bi-invariance of [Haar measure](../../../../../haar-measure.md) shows $h(av,aw)=h(v,w)$ for every $a\in G$.

If $U\subset V$ is invariant, then so is its [orthogonal complement](../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md): for $v\perp U$ and $u\in U$, $h(av,u)=h(v,a^{-1}u)=0$. Hence $V=U\oplus U^\perp$ as a representation. Induct on dimension, splitting whenever a nontrivial proper [invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md) exists. This proves **every such representation is a direct sum of irreducible representations**.
