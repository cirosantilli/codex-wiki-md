↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Schur [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) for irreducible complex characters of a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [group](../../../../../group-split.md), with normalized [Haar measure](../../../../../haar-measure.md), is

$$
\langle\chi_\alpha,\chi_\beta\rangle:=\int_G\chi_\alpha(g)\overline{\chi_\beta(g)}\,d\mu(g)=\delta_{\alpha\beta}.
$$

Equivalent representations clearly have equal characters, since trace is invariant under conjugation. Conversely complete reducibility gives $\chi_V=\sum_\alpha m_\alpha\chi_\alpha$, where the nonnegative [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) multiplicity is recovered by $m_\alpha=\langle\chi_V,\chi_\alpha\rangle$. Equal characters thus give equal multiplicities of every irreducible constituent, hence **equivalent representations**.
