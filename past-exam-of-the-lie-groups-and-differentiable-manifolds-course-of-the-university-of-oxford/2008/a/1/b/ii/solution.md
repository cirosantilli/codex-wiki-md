<h1 id="a/1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiating the affine block form gives

$$
\boxed{L(\operatorname{Aff}_n(\mathbb C))=
\left\{\begin{pmatrix}B&u\\0&0\end{pmatrix}:B\in M_n(\mathbb C),\ u\in\mathbb C^n\right\}}.
$$

Conversely the exponential of such a block matrix has bottom row $(0,1)$ and invertible upper-left block $e^{tB}$, so it remains in the [affine group](../../../../../../affine-group.md). Its [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) corresponds to

$$
[(B,u),(C,v)]=([B,C],Bv-Cu).
$$

The space is closed under multiplication by $i$ and its bracket is complex bilinear. Thus it is a **complex [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) of dimension $n^2+n$**, and its real dimension is $2n^2+2n$.
