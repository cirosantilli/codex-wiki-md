<h1 id="a/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}=L(G_{\mathbb C})$ and $\mathfrak h=L(H)$. A root is a nonzero weight $\alpha\in\mathfrak h^*$ of the adjoint action. Its [root space](../../../../../root-space.md) is

$$
\mathfrak g_\alpha=\{Y\in\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}:[Z,Y]=\alpha(Z)Y\text{ for all }Z\in\mathfrak h\}.
$$

At the [group](../../../../../group-split.md) level these weights come from nontrivial characters of the Cartan [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) acting by conjugation. The Cartan [root-space decomposition](../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md) is

$$
\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}=\mathfrak h\oplus\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Phi}\mathfrak g_\alpha.
$$

The zero-[weight space](../../../../../weight-space.md) is $\mathfrak h$; for the complexification of a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [group](../../../../../group-split.md) the algebra is reductive, with any centre contained in this zero-[weight space](../../../../../weight-space.md).

For the product in question,

$$
G_{\mathbb C}=SL_2(\mathbb C)\times SL_2(\mathbb C),\qquad
\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}=\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C).
$$

Take the diagonal [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) generated by $H_1=(\operatorname{diag}(1,-1),0)$ and $H_2=(0,\operatorname{diag}(1,-1))$. On $Z=xH_1+yH_2$, define $\alpha_1(Z)=2x$ and $\alpha_2(Z)=2y$. The roots are exactly

$$
\boxed{\Phi=\{\alpha_1,-\alpha_1,\alpha_2,-\alpha_2\}}.
$$

If $E=E_{12}$ and $F=E_{21}$, their four root spaces are respectively $\mathbb C(E,0)$, $\mathbb C(F,0)$, $\mathbb C(0,E)$, and $\mathbb C(0,F)$. These four lines and the two-dimensional diagonal subalgebra form the full six-dimensional decomposition. There are no mixed roots: the two direct summands commute. This is the [root system](../../../../../root-system.md) $A_1\times A_1$.
