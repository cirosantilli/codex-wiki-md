<h1 id="a/2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [matrix exponential](../../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) series differentiates term by term on bounded intervals and gives $(e^{tX})'=Xe^{tX}$ and $e^{0X}=I$, proving existence. If $a$ is another differentiable solution, then

$$
\frac d{dt}(e^{-tX}a(t))=-Xe^{-tX}a(t)+e^{-tX}Xa(t)=0.
$$

Its initial value is $I$, so $e^{-tX}a(t)=I$, giving **$a(t)=e^{tX}$ uniquely**.
