↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [maximal torus](../../../../../../maximal-torus.md) is $T=SO_2$, which is normal of index two in $O_2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{W=O_2/SO_2\cong C_2}.
$$

A reflection represents its nontrivial element and conjugates a rotation of angle $\theta$ to the rotation of angle $-\theta$.
