<h1 id="a/3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the given [torus](../../../../../../torus.md) of independent rotations in $n$ mutually [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) two-planes and a fixed final axis. A [normalizer](../../../../../../normalizer.md) element permutes the two-plane rotation characters, and on each plane either preserves or reverses its [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md). Its induced action therefore permutes the $n$ angle coordinates and changes their signs independently.

Every such signed permutation is realized. Permutations of two-dimensional blocks have [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) one. A reflection in any chosen plane reverses that angle; reflecting the remaining one-dimensional axis as necessary restores total [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) one. The kernel consists precisely of independent plane rotations: commuting with a generic [torus](../../../../../../torus.md) element forces preservation of its distinct rotation planes, and the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) condition then fixes the final axis. Thus the kernel is $T$ and

$$
\boxed{W\cong(C_2)^n\rtimes S_n,\qquad |W|=2^nn!}.
$$

This is the signed permutation [group](../../../../../../group-split.md), the [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) of type $B_n$.
