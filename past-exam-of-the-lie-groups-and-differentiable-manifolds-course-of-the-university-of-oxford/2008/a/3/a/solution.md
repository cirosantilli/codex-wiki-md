<h1 id="a/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With the convention applicable also to disconnected [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) groups, the [Weyl group](../../../../../weyl-group.md) is $W=N_G(T)/T$, where the [normalizer](../../../../../normalizer.md) consists of elements taking $T$ to itself by conjugation. Put $\mathfrak t=L(T)$ and $\Lambda=\ker(\exp:\mathfrak t\to T)$. This is a full [lattice](../../../../../lattice.md) with $T\cong\mathfrak t/\Lambda$.

If $\psi$ is a [torus](../../../../../torus.md) automorphism, exponential compatibility gives $\psi(\exp X)=\exp(D\psi_I X)$. Therefore $D\psi_I$ sends $\Lambda$ into itself; applying the inverse proves it sends the [lattice](../../../../../lattice.md) onto itself. In a [lattice](../../../../../lattice.md) [basis](../../../../../basis.md) this derivative is an element of $GL_r(\mathbb Z)$.

Conjugation by a [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $Q\subset N_G(T)$ induces a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) family of these [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) matrices. Since $GL_r(\mathbb Z)$ is discrete, this family is constant and equals its value at the identity. Its action on $\mathfrak t$ is therefore trivial, and surjectivity of the [torus](../../../../../torus.md) exponential shows that **$Q$ centralizes $T$**.

Apply this to the [identity component](../../../../../identity-component.md) $N_G(T)^0$. It lies in $G^0$ and centralizes $T$. The allowed maximal-abelian property of $T$ in $G^0$ gives $N_G(T)^0\subseteq T$, while the [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $T$ lies in that [identity component](../../../../../identity-component.md). Thus $N_G(T)^0=T$. The [normalizer](../../../../../normalizer.md) is a closed [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) Lie [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md), so its component quotient is discrete and [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md), hence finite. Consequently **$W$ is finite and discrete**. For disconnected $G$, its action on the [lattice](../../../../../lattice.md) need not be faithful; the component argument is essential.

The distinction is recorded in [Weyl group of a disconnected compact Lie group](../../../../../weyl-group-of-a-disconnected-compact-lie-group.md).
