# Superconducting critical surface

↑ **Parent:** [Superconducting critical current density](superconducting-critical-current-density.md)

The boundary of a stated superconducting transport criterion in [temperature](temperature-split.md), [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and current-density space. A value measured under one combination does not specify a winding's usable current at another field or [temperature](temperature-split.md). Strain and sample geometry can require further parameters.
