# Harish-Chandra isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Universal enveloping algebra](universal-enveloping-algebra.md)

For a finite-dimensional complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) with [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak h$, the shifted Harish-Chandra map identifies the centre of the [universal enveloping algebra](universal-enveloping-algebra.md) with the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md)-invariant polynomial functions on $\mathfrak h^*$. To normalize the shift, let $M^\chi$ have [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\chi-\rho$, where $\rho$ is the [Weyl vector](half-sum-of-positive-roots.md). A central element $z$ acts by a scalar $j_z(\chi)$, and $z\mapsto j_z$ is the displayed algebra isomorphism. The [Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem](poincare-birkhoff-witt-theorem.md) gives polynomial dependence on $\chi$ by projecting $z$ onto its pure Cartan component and evaluating at $\chi-\rho$. The associated [central characters](central-character-of-an-associative-algebra-module.md) agree exactly when their shifted parameters lie in the same Weyl orbit. Without incorporating the shift into the parameter, the invariance is expressed using the dot action $w\cdot\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$.
