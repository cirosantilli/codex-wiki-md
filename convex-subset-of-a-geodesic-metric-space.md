# Convex subset of a geodesic metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic metric space](hyperbolic-metric-space.md)

A subset $Y$ of a [geodesic metric space](geodesic-metric-space.md) is convex when every [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) whose endpoints lie in $Y$ is contained in $Y$.
