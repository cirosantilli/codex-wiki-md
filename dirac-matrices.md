# Dirac matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md)

Matrices that factor the relativistic energy-momentum relation. In [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) form, $\alpha_i^2=\beta^2=I$, $\{\alpha_i,\alpha_j\}=2\delta_{ij}I$ and $\{\alpha_i,\beta\}=0$. A physical [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) uses [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md); in three spatial dimensions the smallest complex representation has dimension four.
