# Clamped second-variation inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Higher-order Euler-Lagrange equation](higher-order-euler-lagrange-equation.md)

A perturbation preserving both values and slopes has $\eta=\eta\prime=0$ at the endpoints. Apply the [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) to $\eta$ and then to $\eta\prime$. The resulting inequality makes a quadratic [second variation](second-variation.md) proportional to $\int(\eta^2-(\eta\prime\prime)^2)$ nonpositive. If a functional is quadratic, its exact expansion about a stationary function then proves a global maximum.
