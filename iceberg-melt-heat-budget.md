# Iceberg melt heat budget

↑ **Parent:** [Iceberg](iceberg.md)

For an initially cold [iceberg](iceberg.md), melting to liquid at temperature $T_m$ requires both [latent heat of fusion](latent-heat-of-fusion.md) $L_f$ and sensible warming determined by the ice [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md) $c_i$. Spatial [mass density](density.md) and temperature profiles determine their mass-weighted integrals. Additional warming of the meltwater requires a separate heat contribution.
