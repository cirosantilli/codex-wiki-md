# Hyperelastic energy well

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)

A [hyperelastic energy well](hyperelastic-energy-well.md) is a set of [deformation gradients](deformation-gradient.md) minimizing the stored-energy function at a fixed temperature. [Material frame indifference](material-frame-indifference.md) makes each well invariant under left multiplication by [proper rotations](proper-rotation.md). A phase-changing [crystal](crystal.md) can have a single austenitic rotational well at high temperature and several martensitic wells below a transition. Continuous deformations cannot freely join arbitrary wells: planar interfaces require a [rank-one connection](rank-one-connection.md) between the selected gradients.
