# Nerve

↑ **Parent:** [Nervous system](nervous-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerve)

A bundle of [axons](axon.md) in the [peripheral nervous system](peripheral-nervous-system.md), enclosed by supporting connective tissue. A [nerve](nerve.md) can carry sensory fibres, motor fibres, or both; it is distinct from an individual [neuron](neuron-split.md).[§12.2, Nervous Tissue: peripheral axon bundles and connective tissue](anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/12-2-nervous-tissue.md)
