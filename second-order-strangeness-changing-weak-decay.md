# Second-order strangeness-changing weak decay

↑ **Parent:** [Weak interaction](weak-interaction.md)

A [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md) changing [strangeness](strangeness.md) by two units requires at least two insertions of the low-energy [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) in the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md). Each charged-current insertion can remove only one net strange [quark](quark.md) in such a process. Both two-[W boson](w-boson.md) tree topologies and loop contributions can occur; a loop is not required for every exclusive final state. At hadronic energies, the extra power of the [Fermi constant](fermi-constant.md), with the needed powers of an energy scale to balance units, strongly suppresses a second-order amplitude relative to a first-order one. This is distinct from [Cabibbo suppression](cabibbo-suppression.md) within a fixed weak order.
