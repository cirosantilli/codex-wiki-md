# Cutting and stacking

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodic theory](ergodic-theory.md)

Cutting and stacking constructs [measure-preserving transformations](measure-preserving-transformation.md) from towers of equal-measure levels. Cut a tower into equal-width subcolumns, add new spacer levels, and stack the resulting columns. Map each level to the one above by a measure-preserving identification; subsequent stages extend the partial map. When uncovered and top-level measures tend to zero, the limiting map is defined modulo null sets.
