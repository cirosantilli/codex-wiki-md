# Capillary tensile force of a hollow slender thread

↑ **Parent:** [Capillary tensile force of a slender thread](capillary-tensile-force-of-a-slender-thread.md)

For a hollow [slender viscous thread](slender-viscous-thread.md), $A=R_e^2-R_i^2$ and both surfaces contribute axial [surface tension](surface-tension.md). The liquid cut [force](force.md) relative to exterior [pressure](pressure.md), including these surface terms, is $\pi[3\mu Aw_z+(p_i-p_e)R_i^2+\gamma(R_e+R_i)]$. The axial traction on the sloping inner gas surface contributes $-\pi(p_i-p_e)(R_i^2)_z$ to [force](force.md) balance. For constant gas [pressures](pressure.md) the extra derivative cancels, giving $3\mu(Aw_z)_z+\rho gA+\gamma(R_e+R_i)_z=0$. The displayed $T$ is this effective tension after accounting for gas-pressure work, not the unadjusted liquid cut [force](force.md).
