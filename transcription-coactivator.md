# Transcription coactivator

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

A regulatory factor that helps a [transcription activator](transcription-activator.md) increase target [gene](gene.md) expression, often through interactions with [chromatin](chromatin.md) or the [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md) machinery.
