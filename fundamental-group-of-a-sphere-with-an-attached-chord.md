# Fundamental group of a sphere with an attached chord

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md)

Attach a [closed interval](closed-real-interval.md) to a [sphere](sphere.md) $S^2$ at two distinct endpoints, with its interior disjoint from the [sphere](sphere.md). The [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is [infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md). Remove a point of the [sphere](sphere.md) away from the endpoints: the remaining plane has a [deformation retraction](deformation-retraction.md) onto an [arc](arc-topology.md) between the endpoints, keeping the added [closed interval](closed-real-interval.md) fixed. This gives a [circle](circle.md). An open cap about the removed point is [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md), and its punctured overlap has trivial map into the first [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) because it lies in the plane. The [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) therefore adds no further [relator](relator.md).
