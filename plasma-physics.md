# Plasma (physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_(physics))

A [plasma](plasma-physics.md) is a gas of mobile charged particles whose collective electromagnetic response is important to its dynamics. It commonly contains [ions](ion.md) and [Electrons](electron.md), and may also contain a neutral component: complete [ionization](ionization.md) is not required. A multi-species fluid description has [current density](current-density.md) $\mathbf J=\sum_s q_s n_s\mathbf u_s$. The relative motion of the species can matter even when a single-fluid [magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md) approximation describes the large-scale dynamics.
