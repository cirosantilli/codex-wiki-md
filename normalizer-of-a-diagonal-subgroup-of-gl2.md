# Normalizer of a diagonal subgroup of GL2

↑ **Parent:** [Normalizer](normalizer.md)

Over the reals or a [finite field](finite-field.md) with more than two elements, the [normalizer](normalizer.md) of all invertible diagonal [matrices](matrix.md) in $GL_2$ consists of [monomial matrices](monomial-matrix.md) and has quotient $C_2$ over that diagonal [subgroup](subgroup.md). Over $\mathbb F_p$ its size is $2(p-1)^2$ for $p>2$. For $p=2$ the diagonal [subgroup](subgroup.md) is trivial, so its normalizer is all of $GL_2(\mathbb F_2)$, of order six.
