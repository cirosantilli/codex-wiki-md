# Doppler ultrasound

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrasound](ultrasound.md)

Acoustic measurement of a moving-scatterer [velocity](velocity.md) projection using [frequency](frequency.md) or [phase](phase-waves.md) changes. A [speed](speed.md) estimate needs the scattering geometry; finite pulsed sampling can alias the measured shift.
