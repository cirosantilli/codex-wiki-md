# Clean Lagrangian intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian immersion](lagrangian-immersion.md)

A clean Lagrangian intersection is a [clean intersection](clean-intersection.md) between two local Lagrangian sheets. A small exact perturbation of one sheet near a compact clean component replaces the intersection by the critical points of a [Morse function](morse-function.md) on that component.
