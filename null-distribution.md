# Null distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Test statistic](test-statistic.md)

A [null distribution](null-distribution.md) is the [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) of a [test statistic](test-statistic.md) under a specified [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md). It calibrates a [hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test-split.md)'s [test size](size-of-a-statistical-test.md) and [p-value](p-value.md). For a [simple hypothesis](simple-hypothesis.md) it is one distribution; for a composite [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md) it may depend on unknown [nuisance parameters](nuisance-parameter.md).
