# Sea level

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean](ocean.md)

The elevation of the [ocean](ocean.md) surface relative to a specified reference. Global mean, local relative and instantaneous levels answer different questions. Changes of basin capacity, [water](water.md) amount and [surface topography](surface-topography.md) can alter flooded [continental shelf](continental-shelf.md) area and the distribution of marine environments.
