# Effective back-and-forth construction

↑ **Parent:** [Back-and-forth method](back-and-forth-method.md)

An effective back-and-forth construction alternately extends a finite [partial isomorphism of structures](partial-embedding.md) to include the least unused domain and range elements, searching computably for a compatible extension. If extension always exists and its finite compatibility conditions are decidable, the union is a [computable isomorphism](computable-isomorphism.md). Decidable presentations of a [dense linear order without endpoints](dense-linear-order-without-endpoints.md) provide a basic example.
