# Clapeyron pressure relation for equal-density phases

↑ **Parent:** [Thermomolecular pressure](thermomolecular-pressure.md)

For equal solid and liquid [mass densities](density.md), $d\mu=-s\,dT+dp/\rho$ and $s_l-s_s=L/T_m$ imply the displayed relation from equality of [chemical potentials](chemical-potential.md). $T_m$ must be absolute [temperature](temperature.md). The phase pressures differ; this should not be confused with differentiating a coexistence curve along a common pressure.
