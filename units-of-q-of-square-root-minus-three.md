# Units of Q of square root minus three

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet's unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md)

For $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$ and $\omega=(-1+\sqrt{-3})/2$,

$$
\mathcal O_K^\times
=\{\mathord\pm1,\mathord\pm\omega,\mathord\pm\omega^2\}
\cong C_6.
$$
