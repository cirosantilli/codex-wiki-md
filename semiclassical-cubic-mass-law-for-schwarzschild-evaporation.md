# Semiclassical cubic mass law for Schwarzschild evaporation

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole evaporation](black-hole-evaporation.md)

For a large isolated [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) with a fixed effective set of radiated species, [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md) has $T\propto M^{-1}$ and luminosity $P=\alpha/M^2$ in natural units. Integrating $dM/dt=-\alpha/M^2$ gives the displayed law. A horizon-area [blackbody radiation](black-body-radiation.md) estimate gives $\alpha=1/(15360\pi)$; [greybody factors](greybody-factor.md) and species change it. With mass-dependent $\alpha$, integrate $t=\int_{M(t)}^{M_0}m^2\,dm/\alpha(m)$ instead. The [semiclassical gravity](semiclassical-gravity.md) approximation fails near [Planck mass](planck-mass.md), so a formal zero of the cubic law does not settle the final state.
