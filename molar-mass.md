# Molar mass

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molar_mass)

[Molar mass](molar-mass.md) turns a weighed sample into a count of specified particles. For a pure [chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md) with specified [chemical formula](chemical-formula.md) and [isotopic abundances](isotopic-abundance.md), its [molar mass](molar-mass.md) is

$$
M=\frac{m}{n},\qquad n=\frac{m}{M},
$$

where $m$ is the sample [mass](mass.md) and $n$ its [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md). The [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is $\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}$; the common laboratory unit is $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$, with $1\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}$. Equal values of [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) correspond to equal counts of specified entities, but heavier entities give greater [masses](mass.md).

Combining this definition with [molecular count and amount of substance](molecular-count-and-amount-of-substance.md) gives

$$
N=\frac{mN_{\mathrm A}}{M},\qquad \bar m_{\rm particle}=\frac{m}{N}=\frac{M}{N_{\mathrm A}},\qquad N_{\mathrm A}=6.02214076\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}.
$$

The [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) is exact under the modern definition of the [mole](mole-unit.md). The [mass](mass.md) $M/N_{\mathrm A}$ is the mean [mass](mass.md) per specified entity; if [isotopes](isotope.md) differ within the sample, individual [molecules](molecule-split.md) need not all have that mean [mass](mass.md).[§2.3.1, The mole, printed p. 131: exact Avogadro constant, specified entities, and M(X) = N\_A m(X)](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md)

Specify what $n$ counts: [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md), [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), and [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) give different values of [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) for the same sample. For a [mixture](mixture-split.md), dividing total [mass](mass.md) by total [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of its chosen entities gives a composition-dependent mean. A [polymer](polymer-split.md) sample can contain many chain [molar masses](molar-mass.md); its [number-average molar mass](number-average-molar-mass.md) and [weight-average molar mass](weight-average-molar-mass.md) answer different averaging questions. Relating either to [degree of polymerization](degree-of-polymerization.md) also requires a unit-mass convention and end-group accounting.
