# Molar mass

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)

[Molar mass](molar-mass.md) is [mass](mass.md) per [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md), commonly expressed in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. For chain counts $n_i$ and chain [molar masses](molar-mass.md) $M_i$, the number average is $M_n=\sum_i n_iM_i/\sum_i n_i$. Relating it to a [degree of polymerization](degree-of-polymerization.md) requires a defined unit-mass convention and end-group accounting.
