# Phase covariance in the first Rytov approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Rytov approximation](rytov-approximation.md)

For a centered real [scattering potential](scattering-potential.md) fluctuation $W$ and the imaginary part $b$ of the incident-field-weighted [Green function](green-s-function.md), $\varphi(\mathbf r)=-\int b(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')W(\mathbf r')d\mathbf r'$. Its mean vanishes, and its [covariance function](covariance-function.md) is the double integral of the two deterministic kernels against the [covariance function](covariance-function.md) of $W$. The correlation of the [refractive index](refractive-index.md) itself is insufficient when the [scattering potential](scattering-potential.md) depends quadratically on that index.
