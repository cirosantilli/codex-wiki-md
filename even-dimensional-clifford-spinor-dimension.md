# Even-dimensional Clifford spinor dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

After complexification, pair the $D=2n$ [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) generators into $n$ fermionic creation and annihilation operators. Their occupation basis has $2^n$ states, giving the irreducible complex Dirac module. The chirality operator separates its even and odd occupation subspaces, each with $2^{n-1}$ states. Majorana reality conditions concern real dimensions and depend on spacetime signature; they must not be imposed in every even dimension indiscriminately.
