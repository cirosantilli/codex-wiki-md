# Sheaf of meromorphic functions on a complex manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor on a complex manifold](divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md)

The sheaf $\mathcal K_X$ assigns to an open subset of a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) its [meromorphic functions](meromorphic-function.md). Its subsheaf $\mathcal K_X^*$ consists of the nonzero meromorphic functions, while $\mathcal O_X^*$ consists of the nowhere-zero [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md).
