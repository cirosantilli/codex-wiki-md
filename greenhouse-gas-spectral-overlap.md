# Greenhouse-gas spectral overlap

↑ **Parent:** [Greenhouse gas radiative efficiency](greenhouse-gas-radiative-efficiency.md)

Overlap of a gas [absorption spectrum](absorption-spectrum.md) with existing atmospheric absorption changes its incremental radiative effect. A strong band in an already opaque spectral interval can contribute less additional outgoing-flux reduction than a weaker band in a window. [Pressure broadening](pressure-broadening.md), altitude, [temperature](temperature-split.md) and the source spectrum also matter.
