# Fundamental diffusion mode

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

For a [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) with fixed homogeneous [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), a fundamental diffusion mode is an [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) of its nonnegative decay operator belonging to the smallest decay [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\nu_0$, when such an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) exists. It is the slowest decaying separated [normal mode](normal-mode.md). With a discrete [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) and an isolated lowest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md), initial data having a nonzero projection onto its [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) approach this decay law at late times. Strongly vanishing [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md) can destroy existence of a lowest mode, as illustrated by the [loss of a spectral gap under strongly degenerate radial diffusion](loss-of-a-spectral-gap-under-strongly-degenerate-radial-diffusion.md).
