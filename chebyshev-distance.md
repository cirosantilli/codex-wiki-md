# Chebyshev distance

↑ **Parent:** [Metric](metric.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebyshev_distance)

The [metric](metric.md) induced by the [maximum norm](supremum-norm.md) measures the largest difference in [Cartesian coordinates](cartesian-coordinate-system.md). Simultaneous changes of [Cartesian coordinates](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) cost only as much as the slowest coordinate requires.
