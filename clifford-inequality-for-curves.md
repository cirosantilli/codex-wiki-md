# Clifford inequality for curves

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md)

If a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) has both $h^0(\mathcal L)>0$ and $h^0(\omega_C\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})>0$, the displayed inequality holds. Write these dimensions as $r,s$. The [product dimension bound for sections on a curve](product-dimension-bound-for-sections-on-a-curve.md) gives $r+s-1\leq g$, and the [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives $r-s=\deg\mathcal L+1-g$. Adding gives $2r\leq\deg\mathcal L+2$.
