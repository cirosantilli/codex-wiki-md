# Coherent elastic scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md)

In [coherent elastic scattering](coherent-elastic-scattering.md), amplitudes from the scattering constituents combine while their internal state is unchanged. For [X-rays](x-ray.md), recoil taken by a whole [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) can produce an effectively unshifted component alongside the [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md) component.
