# Differentiability in quadratic mean

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical path](statistical-path.md)

A dominated [statistical path](statistical-path.md) is differentiable in quadratic mean at $P$ if its square-root [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) has an [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) [derivative](derivative.md) $(1/2)g\sqrt p$, with $g\in L^2(P)$. The [score function](informant-function.md) is $g$. This formulation controls mass near zeros of $p$ as well as the [derivative](derivative.md) on the support of $P$; a pointwise log-density [derivative](derivative.md) is insufficient by itself.
