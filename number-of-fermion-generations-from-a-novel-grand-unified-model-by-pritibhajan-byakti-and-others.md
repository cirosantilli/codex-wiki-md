# Number of fermion generations from a novel grand unified model by Pritibhajan Byakti and others

Official publication: [https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-014-2730-9](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-014-2730-9)

Section 3 gives the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) [branching rules](branching-rule.md) of the $SU(6)$ [representations](group-representation.md) $\overline{\mathbf6}$ and $\mathbf{15}$. Their cubic [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md) coefficients are $-1$ and $2$, respectively; one $\mathbf{15}$ with two $\overline{\mathbf6}$ therefore cancels this [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md). Additional charged [fermions](fermion.md) can form a [vectorlike gauge spectrum](vectorlike-gauge-spectrum.md) with invariant mass terms.
