<h1 id="p-q-string">(p,q) string</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Type IIB superstring theory](type-iib-superstring-theory.md)

A [(p,q) string](p-q-string.md) carries $p$ units of [fundamental string](fundamental-string.md) charge and $q$ units of [D1-brane](d1-brane.md) charge. Coprime integers label an elementary primitive charge vector; nonprimitive vectors describe multiple units. The displayed tension uses the string metric and vanishing axion. In the [M-theory torus duality](m-theory-torus-duality.md), it comes from an [M2-brane](m2-brane.md) wrapped on the corresponding primitive one-cycle.
