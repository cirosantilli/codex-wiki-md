# Nash-Williams inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Effective resistance](effective-resistance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nash-Williams_inequality)

If $\Pi_1,\ldots,\Pi_m$ are pairwise edge-disjoint cutsets separating $a$ from $z$ in an [electrical network](electrical-network.md), then

$$
R_{\mathrm{eff}}(a,z)\geq\sum_{k=1}^m\left(\sum_{e\in\Pi_k}c(e)\right)^{-1}.
$$
