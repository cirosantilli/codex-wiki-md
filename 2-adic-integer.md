# 2-adic integer

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic integer](p-adic-integer.md)

A 2-adic integer is an element of the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md) for $p=2$, equivalently a compatible [sequence](sequence.md) of residues modulo $2^r$. Its binary [p-adic digits](p-adic-digit.md) begin with its [parity](parity.md). The highlighted residue-tree path for $13$ begins $1,0,1,1$, while [modular inversion converges to a p-adic inverse](modular-inversion-converges-to-a-p-adic-inverse.md) computes the infinite expansion of $1/3$.
