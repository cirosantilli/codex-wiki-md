# 2-adic integer

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic integer](p-adic-integer.md)

A 2-adic integer is an element of the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md) for $p=2$, equivalently a compatible sequence of residues modulo $2^r$. Its reduction modulo two determines its [parity](parity.md).
