# Half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Half-space](half-space.md)

A half-plane is a [half-space](half-space.md) in a two-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), consisting of points on one side of a [line](straight-line.md). For distinct [centroids](centroid.md), each of the two [Voronoi cells](voronoi-cell.md) defined by their [Euclidean distances](euclidean-distance.md) is a closed half-plane bounded by their [perpendicular bisector](perpendicular-bisector.md), with an explicit convention needed to assign boundary ties.
