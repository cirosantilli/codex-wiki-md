# Indel

↑ **Parent:** [Mutation](mutation.md)

An [indel](indel.md) is an insertion or deletion of [nucleotides](nucleotide.md) relative to a specified reference [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md). Its length and location matter: within a coding region, a length not divisible by three changes the downstream [reading frame](reading-frame.md), producing a [frameshift mutation](frameshift-mutation.md). A [gene deletion](gene-deletion.md) is a larger removal defined by the [gene](gene.md) it eliminates.
