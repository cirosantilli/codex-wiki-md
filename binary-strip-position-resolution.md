# Binary strip position resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Silicon microstrip detector](silicon-microstrip-detector.md)

If a single strip of pitch $w$ reports only its centre and the impact position has a [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md), the position error is uniform on $[-w/2,w/2]$. Its [variance](variance-split.md) is $w^{-1}\int_{-w/2}^{w/2}x^2dx=w^2/12$. [Charge sharing](charge-sharing-in-a-strip-detector.md) and analogue readout can improve this resolution.
