<h1 id="mobius-translation-scaling-inversion-factorization">Möbius translation-scaling-inversion factorization</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md)

Let $T_a(z)=z+a$, $S_k(z)=kz$ with $k\ne0$, and $I(z)=1/z$ on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md). For $F(z)=(az+b)/(cz+d)$ and $\Delta=ad-bc\ne0$, $c=0$ gives $F=T_{b/d}\circ S_{a/d}$. If $c\ne0$, division gives $F=T_{a/c}\circ S_{-\Delta/c^2}\circ I\circ T_{d/c}$. The expressions agree at poles and at infinity by the sphere conventions. These elementary maps therefore generate the [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) group.
