# Factor pair

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor](divisor.md)

A pair of [positive integers](positive-integer.md) $(a,b)$ with $ab=n$ for a specified [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$. Pairing each [divisor](divisor.md) $a$ with $n/a$ gives $\tau(n)/2$ unordered [factor pairs](factor-pair.md) for nonsquare $n$, and $(\tau(n)+1)/2$ for a [perfect square](square-number.md), where $\tau(n)$ is the [divisor function](divisor-function.md).
