# Comoving particle horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Particle horizon](particle-horizon.md)

The comoving particle horizon is the [particle horizon](particle-horizon.md) expressed in [comoving coordinates](comoving-coordinate.md), assuming a finite initial cosmic-time boundary at zero. In a spatially flat [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), it is the distance a photon could have travelled since that boundary. The corresponding [proper distance in cosmology](proper-distance-in-cosmology.md) at time $t$ is $a(t)r_p(t)$. It differs from the future-directed [cosmological event horizon](cosmological-event-horizon.md) and from the instantaneous [comoving Hubble radius](comoving-hubble-radius.md).
