# Exact amplitude-phase equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ermakov-Pinney equation](ermakov-pinney-equation.md)

Substitution of $y=Ae^{i\epsilon^{-1}\int\sigma dX}$ into $\epsilon^2y''+k^2y=0$ gives the displayed real amplitude equation and conserved imaginary-part flux. Conversely these equations produce an exact complex solution wherever $A\ne0$. The [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) is obtained by expanding this exact system where $|k|$ stays positive.
