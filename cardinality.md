# Cardinality

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal number](cardinal-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardinality)

The cardinality $|X|$ of a [set](set-split.md) $X$ is the [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) shared by all sets in [bijection](bijection.md) with $X$. For a [finite set](finite-set.md), it is the number of elements.
