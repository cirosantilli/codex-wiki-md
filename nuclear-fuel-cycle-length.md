# Nuclear fuel cycle length

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fuel cycle](nuclear-fuel-cycle.md)

The operating interval between specified refuelling events. It must be distinguished from the full material [nuclear fuel cycle](nuclear-fuel-cycle.md) and from an individual fuel batch's multi-cycle residence time. Reactivity history, thermal limits and fuel management constrain the achievable interval.
