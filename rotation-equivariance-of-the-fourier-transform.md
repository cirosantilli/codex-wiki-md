# Rotation equivariance of the Fourier transform

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform of a tempered distribution](fourier-transform-of-a-tempered-distribution.md)

For an orthogonal matrix $R$, a unit-Jacobian change of variables gives $\widehat{\varphi\circ R^t}=\widehat\varphi\circ R^t$. The dual definition of the [Fourier transform of a tempered distribution](fourier-transform-of-a-tempered-distribution.md) consequently gives

$$
\widehat{T\circ R}=\widehat T\circ R.
$$

Since the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) is invertible on the [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md) and its dual, a [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md) is invariant under a rotation group exactly when its transform is invariant. This includes [radial tempered distributions](radial-tempered-distribution.md).
