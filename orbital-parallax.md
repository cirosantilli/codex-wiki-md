# Orbital parallax

↑ **Parent:** [Astrometry](astrometry.md)

Inferring distance by combining angular orbital motion with a physical orbital scale, for example from [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md). The [mass-distance degeneracy of an angular stellar orbit](mass-distance-degeneracy-of-an-angular-stellar-orbit.md) gives a worked example.
