# Synchronous fellow-traveller property

↑ **Parent:** [Combing of a group](combing-of-a-group.md)

Two [paths](continuous-path.md) satisfy the synchronous fellow-traveller property with constant $K$ if $d(p(t),q(t))\le K$ for all equal times, with completed [paths](continuous-path.md) held at their endpoints. Checking integer times suffices up to increasing $K$ by at most two for unit-speed edge [paths](continuous-path.md). A synchronous [combing of a group](combing-of-a-group.md) requires a uniform constant for all adjacent endpoints.
