# Customer sojourn time

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)

The total time between admission to a selected queueing system and departure. It includes waiting and service if both are inside the chosen system, and only [service time](service-time.md) if the selected system consists of the server alone. [Little's law](little-s-law.md) relates its mean to mean population and admitted [queue throughput](queue-throughput.md).
