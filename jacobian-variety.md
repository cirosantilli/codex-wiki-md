# Jacobian variety

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobian_variety)

The [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md) of a smooth projective [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) parametrizes degree-zero [line bundles](line-bundle.md). Its dimension is the genus of the [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md). An effective degree-$d$ [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) defines a point in a degree-$d$ torsor through the [Abel map of an algebraic curve](abel-map-of-an-algebraic-curve.md). The [theta divisor](theta-divisor.md) induces an isomorphism with the [dual abelian variety](dual-abelian-variety.md).
