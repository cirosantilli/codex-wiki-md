# Similarity reduction for a cubic absorption equation with dilating drift

↑ **Parent:** [Similarity solution](similarity-solution.md)

The transformation $\tau=\log t$, $\xi=x/\sqrt t$, $v=\sqrt t\,u$ gives $v_\tau=v_{\xi\xi}+(v-v^3)/2$. Thus the stationary half-line profile $v=\tanh(\xi/2)$ yields $u=t^{-1/2}\tanh[x/(2\sqrt t)]$, with zero value at the origin and far-field value $t^{-1/2}$. For two bounded [classical solutions](classical-solution.md), their difference has reaction coefficient $[1-(v_1^2+v_1v_2+v_2^2)]/2\leq1/2$. Multiplying it by $e^{-\tau/2}$ therefore produces a linear parabolic equation with nonnegative absorption. A positive growing quadratic spatial barrier and the [maximum principle](maximum-principle.md) give uniqueness on the unbounded half-line. This proof identifies the finite-time boundedness or growth hypothesis needed at [spatial infinity](spatial-infinity.md) rather than assuming a supremum is automatically attained there.
