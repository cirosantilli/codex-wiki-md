# Skin effect

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skin_effect)

An alternating [electric current](electric-current.md) and its [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md) concentrate near a conductor's surface. For a local isotropic good conductor with real [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) $\sigma$, magnetic permeability $\mu$, and $\sigma\gg\omega\epsilon$, the field penetrates over the [skin depth](skin-depth.md) $\delta=\sqrt{2/(\mu\sigma\omega)}$.
