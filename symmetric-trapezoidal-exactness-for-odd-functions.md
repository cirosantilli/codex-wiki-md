# Symmetric trapezoidal exactness for odd functions

↑ **Parent:** [Composite trapezoidal rule](composite-trapezoidal-rule.md)

On a [partition of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md) symmetric about zero, the [composite trapezoidal rule](composite-trapezoidal-rule.md) gives opposite [quadrature nodes](quadrature-node.md) equal [quadrature weights](quadrature-weight.md). Samples of an [odd function](odd-function.md) cancel pairwise, and a zero [quadrature node](quadrature-node.md) contributes zero. For a continuous [odd function](odd-function.md), its exact [integral](integral-split.md) over $[-L,L]$ is also zero. Adding any [affine function](affine-function.md) preserves exactness by [linearity of the integral](linearity-of-integration.md) and of the [quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md). For example, $1+\sin x$ is nonpolynomial but both the exact [integral](integral-split.md) and every uniform-grid [composite trapezoidal rule](composite-trapezoidal-rule.md) value on $[-1,1]$ are two.
