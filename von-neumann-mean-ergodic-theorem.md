# Von Neumann mean ergodic theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary operator](unitary-operator.md)

For a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) $U$ on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), the [Cesaro averages](cesaro-mean.md)

$$
A_n=\frac1n\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}U^j
$$

converge strongly to the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto the fixed-point subspace $\ker(U-I)$.
