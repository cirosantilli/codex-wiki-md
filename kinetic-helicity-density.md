# Kinetic helicity density

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamical helicity](hydrodynamical-helicity.md)

The local density of [kinetic helicity](hydrodynamical-helicity.md) is $q=\mathbf u\cdot\boldsymbol\omega$. Conservation of its volume integral does not imply that $q$ is a materially advected scalar.
