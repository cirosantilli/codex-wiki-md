# Fermi transition

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay transition](beta-decay-transition.md)

The spin-independent vector contribution to an [allowed beta decay](allowed-beta-decay.md). The [isospin](isospin.md) operator $\tau_k^\pm$ changes a [neutron](neutron-split.md) into a [proton](proton.md) or the reverse; the sign convention depends on the assigned nucleon isospin projection. Its rotational rank is zero, so $J_f=J_i$ and the [parity](parity.md) is unchanged. The phrase Fermi-type forbidden contribution extends the $S=0$ classification to higher [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md).
