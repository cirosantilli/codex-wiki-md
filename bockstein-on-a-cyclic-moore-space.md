# Bockstein on a cyclic Moore space

↑ **Parent:** [Moore space (algebraic topology)](moore-space-algebraic-topology.md)

Attach an $(i+1)$-cell to $S^i$ by a degree-$n$ map, with $i\geq1$ and $n\geq2$. The integral cellular differential is multiplication by $n$, while its modulo-$n$ reduction is zero. Lifting the degree-$i$ generator modulo $n^2$ and applying the differential gives $n$, so the [Bockstein homomorphism](bockstein-homomorphism.md) sends that generator to the degree-$(i+1)$ generator. This supplies a nonzero example in every positive degree.
