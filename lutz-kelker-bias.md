# Lutz-Kelker bias

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar parallax](stellar-parallax.md)

The classical parallax-calibration problem combines measurement errors with the greater volume available at larger distances. A locally uniform spatial population has $p(r)\propto r^2$, which transforms under $r=1/\varpi$ to $p(\varpi)\propto\varpi^{-4}$ on the valid distance interval. Parallax cuts and the nonlinear conversion to [luminosity](luminosity.md) can bias [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) samples. A correction is not universal: the population model, selection information and inferential target must be specified. Distinguish the repeated-sampling bias of a selected sample from inference for a particular [star](star.md) using a justified [prior](prior-probability.md).[PASP 85, 573–578; classical calibration model](on-the-use-of-trigonometric-parallaxes-for-the-calibration-of-luminosity-systems-theory-by-t-e-lutz-and-d-h-kelker.md)
