# Decay heat

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md)

Heat deposited by the [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) of radioactive constituents. It persists after a reactor stops sustaining a [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md) chain reaction and affects spent-fuel and waste management.
