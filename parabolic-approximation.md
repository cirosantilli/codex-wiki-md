# Parabolic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md)

A parabolic approximation retains terms through second order in a local [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md). Near the closest point of a circle of radius $a$ and its tangent line, the separation is $x^2/(2a)+O(x^4/a^3)$.
