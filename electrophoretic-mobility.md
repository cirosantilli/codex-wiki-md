# Electrophoretic mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Electrophoresis](electrophoresis.md)

The [electrophoretic mobility](electrophoretic-mobility.md) $\mu$ is the proportionality coefficient between drift [velocity](velocity.md) and a weak applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md). Its sign specifies motion relative to the field. When only travel distances are used, the mobility magnitude is often intended. For long linear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) in a weak-field [reptation](reptation.md) regime, $|\mu|$ can scale inversely with chain length, whereas free-solution mobility is approximately length-independent. Neither law supplies a universal [gel electrophoresis size calibration](gel-electrophoresis-size-calibration.md) for arbitrary gel concentration, field strength or molecular topology.
