# Electrophoretic transit time in a conical pore

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic resistance of a conical pore](ionic-resistance-of-a-conical-pore.md)

For a point-like object with constant [electrophoretic mobility](electrophoretic-mobility.md), deterministic traversal is the integral of inverse local drift speed. In a conical frustum of [length](length.md) $L$ at pore voltage $V$, the one-dimensional field is $E(z)=Vab/[Lr(z)^2]$. Integrating gives $t=L^2(b/a+1+a/b)/(3\mu V)$ for positive $\mu V$. For two symmetric frusta of total [length](length.md) $2l$ and the same total pore voltage, replace $L$ by $2l$. The inequality $b/a+a/b\geq2$ shows that this nonuniform field cannot produce a shorter deterministic point-particle transit than a uniform field at fixed total [length](length.md) and voltage. Reservoir [forces](force-split.md), [diffusion](diffusion.md), adsorption and finite-object conformation are additional physics.
