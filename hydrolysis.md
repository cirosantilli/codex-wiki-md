# Hydrolysis

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrolysis)

[Hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) is a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) involving [water](water.md) that breaks a [chemical bond](chemical-bond.md). The [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) released by suitably coupled [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) can drive [active transport](active-transport.md); the available [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) depends on the actual composition and conditions.
