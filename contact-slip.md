# Contact slip

↑ **Parent:** [Friction](friction.md)

[Contact slip](contact-slip.md) is the tangential [relative velocity](relative-velocity.md) between the two material points instantaneously in contact. For a nonrotating box on a belt it is $w=v-U$; for a [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md), use the [velocity](velocity.md) of its contact point, including the contribution from [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md). Zero [contact slip](contact-slip.md) defines [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) at a rolling contact, even when the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) moves.
