# Beta of an asset

↑ **Parent:** [Market portfolio](market-portfolio.md)

For a nondegenerate market return $R_M$, $\beta_i=\operatorname{Cov}(R_i,R_M)/\operatorname{Var}(R_M)$. It measures the asset's market-related exposure and enters the [capital asset pricing model](capital-asset-pricing-model.md).
