# Commuted wave energy

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field method for wave equations](vector-field-method-for-wave-equations.md)

A commuted wave energy controls derivatives of a solution after applying the [commutation vector fields for the wave equation](commutation-vector-field-for-the-wave-equation.md). A convenient equivalent norm is $A_m(t)=\sum_{|I|\leq m}\|\partial Z^Iu(t)\|_2$. The [wave energy estimate](wave-energy-estimate.md) controls its growth through commuted sources, while the [Klainerman-Sobolev inequality](klainerman-sobolev-inequality.md) gives pointwise decay for low-order derivatives.
