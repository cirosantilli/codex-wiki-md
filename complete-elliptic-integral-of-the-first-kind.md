# Complete elliptic integral of the first kind

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic integral of the first kind](elliptic-integral-of-the-first-kind.md)

For $0<k<1$, the complete elliptic integral of the first kind is

$$
K(k)=\int_0^1\frac{dt}{\sqrt{(1-t^2)(1-k^2t^2)}}.
$$

It is the complete first-kind member of the [elliptic integral](elliptic-integral.md) family.
