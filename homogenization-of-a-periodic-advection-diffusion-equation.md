# Homogenization of a periodic advection-diffusion equation

↑ **Parent:** [Advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md)

At long wavelengths and on a [diffusive time scale](diffusive-time-scale.md), a rapidly varying periodic [advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md) can have a constant effective [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md). A [multiple-scale expansion](method-of-multiple-scales.md) solves periodic cell equations for the fast variables. The [solvability condition](solvability-condition.md) at the next order gives the macroscopic equation. For a periodic sinusoidal shear this is the [effective diffusivity of a periodic sinusoidal shear](effective-diffusivity-of-a-periodic-sinusoidal-shear.md); its transverse zero-mean cell modes decay, leaving the slow conserved concentration. This use of homogenization concerns differential equations, rather than polynomial [homogenization](homogenization-algebra.md).
