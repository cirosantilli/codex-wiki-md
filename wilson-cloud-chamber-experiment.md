# Wilson cloud-chamber experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Expansion cloud chamber](expansion-cloud-chamber.md)

[Charles Thomson Rees Wilson](charles-thomson-rees-wilson.md)'s 1927 lecture recalls [electron](electron.md) cloud trails produced with [X-rays](x-ray.md) in 1911, then [alpha particle](alpha-particle.md) trails from a radium-tipped source. The improved 1912 chamber had a wide, shallow space with a suddenly falling floor to limit stirring. A roof–floor clearing [electric field](electric-field-split.md) removed unwanted [ions](ion.md). Sharp [photography](photography-split.md) required expansion, then the ionizing [particle](particle-physics.md), then illumination.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; Wilson, 1927 Nobel lecture, pp.199–201, Fig.3: apparatus and operating sequence](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/on-the-cloud-method-of-making-visible-ions-and-the-tracks-of-ionizing-particles-by-charles-thomson-rees-wilson.md)

The [piston](piston.md) mechanism connects the space beneath the moving floor to an evacuated vessel: opening the valve lets the floor drop. [Water](water.md) in a trough supplies the [vapor](vapor.md). The Smithsonian's surviving demonstration object is explicitly a 1961 replica of the 1912 apparatus.[Smithsonian Institution, Wilson's cloud chamber, replica of Cavendish Lab apparatus, object nmah\_700205, “History and basic principle” and “Date made”](wilson%E2%80%99s-cloud-chamber-replica-of-cavendish-lab-apparatus-by-smithsonian-institution.md)

Original functional schematic, with an invented illustrative trail. Positions, sizes and delays are schematic; this is not a historical photograph or a mechanical reconstruction.

After dust removal, [X-rays](x-ray.md) increased droplet formation above a lower expansion threshold; larger expansions also produced dense background cloud.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; Wilson, 1927 Nobel lecture, pp.194–197: cloud limits and clearing controls](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/on-the-cloud-method-of-making-visible-ions-and-the-tracks-of-ionizing-particles-by-charles-thomson-rees-wilson.md)

The control logic separates a localized [ionization](ionization.md) trail from fog. Delays permit [ion](ion.md) displacement and drop growth; stirring deforms the trail. A photograph alone supplies neither an absolute event time nor a calibrated [particle](particle-physics.md) [energy](energy-split.md).
