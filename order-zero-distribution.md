# Order-zero distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Order of a distribution](order-of-a-distribution.md)

An order-zero distribution is locally bounded by the sup norm of its test function. Such distributions are precisely the distributions represented locally by signed or complex [Radon measures](radon-measure.md).
