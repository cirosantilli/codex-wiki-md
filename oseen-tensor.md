# Oseen tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md)

The [Oseen tensor](oseen-tensor.md) is the unbounded three-dimensional incompressible [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) Green tensor relating a point [force](force-split.md) to the [velocity](velocity.md) it produces: $u_i=\mathsf G_{ij}F_j$. At separation $r$, its longitudinal mobility is twice its transverse mobility. It generates the leading far-field [hydrodynamic interaction](hydrodynamic-interaction.md) between externally forced particles. The singular value at $r=0$ is not a particle's self mobility; a no-slip sphere instead has self mobility $1/(6\pi\eta a)$. Walls and near-contact lubrication require different or corrected tensors.
