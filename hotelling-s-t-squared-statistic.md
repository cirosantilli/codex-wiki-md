<h1 id="hotelling-s-t-squared-statistic">Hotelling's T-squared statistic</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test.md)

For independent [multivariate normal](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) observations with [positive-definite](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md) covariance, this statistic is the maximum squared studentized mean contrast. With unbiased [sample covariance matrix](sample-covariance-matrix.md) $S$ and $n>p$, its null distribution obeys $(n-p)T^2/(p(n-1))\sim F_{p,n-p}$. This follows from [independence](independent-random-variables.md) of the Gaussian [sample mean](sample-mean.md) and the residual [Wishart distribution](wishart-distribution.md).
