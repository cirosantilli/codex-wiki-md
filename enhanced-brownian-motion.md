# Enhanced Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Rough path](rough-path.md)

The Stratonovich enhancement of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) is a geometric $p$-rough path for every $p>2$. Its second level consists of [Lévy area](levy-area.md) and the prescribed symmetric tensor. Its increments satisfy the [Chen identity](chen-identity.md) and have finite Hölder rough-path norms at every exponent below $1/2$.
