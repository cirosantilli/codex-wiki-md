# Magnetic-field cancellation of an arc and tangent leads

↑ **Parent:** [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md)

For a circular arc of radius $R$ carrying clockwise [electric current](electric-current.md) $I$ and subtending angle $\theta$, with two tangent semi-infinite leads extending on the crossing side of the arc, the axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) at height $h$ above the centre is

$$
B_z(h)=\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi R}\left[\frac2{1+(h/R)^2}-\frac{\theta}{[1+(h/R)^2]^{3/2}}\right].
$$

The crossing is insulated. The positive term is from the two leads and the negative term from the arc, with $+z$ out of the clockwise-current plane. The [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md) yields the lead integral $\int_0^\infty R(R^2+h^2+s^2)^{-3/2}\,ds=R/(R^2+h^2)$. Cancellation of the axial component gives the title formula, expanding to $\theta=2+h^2/R^2+O(h^4/R^4)$ in [radians](radian.md). This is cancellation of one component away from the plane, not necessarily the entire [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).
