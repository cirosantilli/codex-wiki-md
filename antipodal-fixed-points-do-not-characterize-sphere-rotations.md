# Antipodal fixed points do not characterize sphere rotations

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md)

The [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $M(z)=(3z-1)/(3-z)$ fixes the [antipodal pair](antipodal-pair.md) $1,-1$ under [stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md). But $M'(1)=2$, so it multiplies infinitesimal spherical lengths at that fixed point by two, as follows from the round line element $2|dz|/(1+|z|^2)$. It is not an [isometry](isometry.md) and hence not a sphere [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md). Fixing an [antipodal pair](antipodal-pair.md) is therefore necessary for a nontrivial rotational [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md), but is not sufficient.
