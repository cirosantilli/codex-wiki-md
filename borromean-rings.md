# Borromean rings

↑ **Parent:** [Link](link.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borromean_rings)

The Borromean rings are a three-component [link](link.md) whose components are unknotted, which is nontrivial although removing any one component leaves an unlink. Its complement has a complete finite-volume [hyperbolic three-manifold](hyperbolic-three-manifold.md) structure obtained from two regular ideal octahedra.
