# High-minimum-degree multipartite stability subgraph

↑ **Parent:** [Erdős-Stone theorem](erdos-stone-theorem.md)

For fixed positive integers $r,t$, a [graph](graph-split.md) $G$ on $n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) with $e(G)=(1-1/r+o(1))n^2/2$ and no [balanced complete multipartite blow-up](balanced-complete-multipartite-blow-up.md) $K_{r+1}(t)$ contains an $r$-partite [subgraph](subgraph.md) $H$ with $n-o(n)$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), balanced classes of size $n/r+o(n)$, and [minimum degree of a graph](minimum-degree-of-a-graph.md) $\delta(H)=(1-1/r+o(1))n$.

One proof first removes a vanishing fraction of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) of low [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md), finds a slowly growing $K_r(L)$ by the [Erdős-Stone theorem](erdos-stone-theorem.md), and assigns almost every remaining [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) to a root class in which it has fewer than $t$ [neighbours of a vertex](neighbour-of-a-vertex.md). After discarding a further vanishing fraction, a $K_{t,t}$ inside an assigned class would extend to $K_{r+1}(t)$ using the root classes. Thus internal [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and missing cross-class [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) both number $o(n^2)$. Removing the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) with unusually many missing cross-class [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) gives the stated [minimum degree of a graph](minimum-degree-of-a-graph.md).
