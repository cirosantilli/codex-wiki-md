# Extendible spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Spacetime extension](spacetime-extension.md)

An [extendible spacetime](extendible-spacetime.md) admits a [spacetime extension](spacetime-extension.md) in the specified regularity class. A coordinate horizon need not obstruct an extension: the exterior Schwarzschild chart extends through its horizon in regular null coordinates. Maximality refers to extensions of the given spacetime, not to the mere absence of a singularity in one coordinate chart.
