# Entropy balance of a thermostatted resistor

↑ **Parent:** [Joule heating](joule-heating.md)

A [resistor](resistor.md) at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) and unchanged [thermodynamic state](thermodynamic-state.md) has no net [entropy](entropy-split.md) accumulation. Electrical work becomes [heat](heat-split.md) at rate $I^2R$, which is exported to the thermostat. In the ideal work-source model the surroundings gain [entropy](entropy-split.md) at rate $I^2R/T$, equal to the total [entropy production](entropy-production.md). Additional losses inside a real [power](power.md) source add to this idealized budget.
