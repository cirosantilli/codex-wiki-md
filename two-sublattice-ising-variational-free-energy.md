# Two-sublattice Ising variational free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Two-sublattice Ising mean-field approximation](two-sublattice-ising-mean-field-approximation.md)

Here $s(m)=\frac{1+m}{2}\ln\frac{1+m}{2}+\frac{1-m}{2}\ln\frac{1-m}{2}$. This [free-energy density](free-energy-density.md) is the [energy](energy.md) minus temperature times [entropy](entropy.md) of independent sublattice [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md). Since $s'(m)=\operatorname{atanh}m$, stationarity yields the [two-sublattice Ising mean-field approximation](two-sublattice-ising-mean-field-approximation.md). Writing $M_\pm=(M_B\pm M_W)/2$, the quadratic coefficients are $(k_BT+qJ)/2$ for $M_+^2$ and $(k_BT-qJ)/2$ for $M_-^2$. The positive quartic coefficient $k_BT/12$ in the staggered direction stabilizes the continuous transition. Unlike a log-cosh auxiliary potential, this variational expression can be minimized away from self-consistency.
