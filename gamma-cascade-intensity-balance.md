# Gamma-cascade intensity balance

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma decay](gamma-decay.md)

For a prompt nuclear level fed in a steady decay cascade, incoming and outgoing transition populations balance. Quoted [photon](photon.md) [intensities](intensity-physics.md) must represent efficiency-corrected emission counts; [internal conversion](internal-conversion.md), alternative [particle decays](particle-decay.md) and unobserved feeds must either be included or explicitly excluded. Equal [photon](photon.md) counts suggest a serial cascade, while a feed [intensity](intensity-physics.md) equal to the sum of two branches suggests splitting. [intensities](intensity-physics.md) alone do not determine the chronological order of equal-count lines.
