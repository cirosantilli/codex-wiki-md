# Terrestrial planet

↑ **Parent:** [Planet](planet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrestrial_planet)

A planet dominated by rocky and metallic material rather than a massive H/He envelope. [Earth](earth.md) and [Mars](mars.md) are examples. A [super-Earth](super-earth.md) is a mass category and need not be a terrestrial planet.
