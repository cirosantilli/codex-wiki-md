# Simple Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_Lie_group)

A connected Lie group is simple when its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is nonabelian and simple. Some conventions additionally quotient out or exclude a discrete center; the infinitesimal gauge fields depend only on the Lie algebra.
