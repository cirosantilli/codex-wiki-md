# Magnetic-radiation detectability at a marginal far-field distance

↑ **Parent:** [Near and far field](near-and-far-field.md)

For a harmonic [magnetic dipole](magnetic-dipole.md) observed with kr of order unity, its quasistatic, [induction](mathematical-induction.md) and radiation fields scale as $r^{-3}$, $kr^{-2}$ and $k^2r^{-1}$ and need not be cleanly separated experimentally. The radiated-power law remains an asymptotic [flux](flux.md) statement, but $P/(4\pi r^2)$ alone does not specify the local detected field. Detectability further depends on directional pattern, [plasma](plasma-physics.md) propagation, antenna coupling, [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md), integration and [noise](noise-signal-processing.md). A tiny nonzero vacuum radiation term does not by itself establish either a practical detection or an absolute impossibility.
