# Bus transaction

↑ **Parent:** [Computer bus](computer-bus.md)

One requested operation and its completion, potentially including several payload transfers. A [bus transaction](bus-transaction.md) identifies a target, specifies an operation and exchanges data or status. A [CPU](central-processing-unit.md) or [DMA](direct-memory-access.md) engine can initiate one; a target responds. The terms “master” and “slave” in older hardware documents refer to these initiator and target roles.[AMBA Specification, revision 2.0, sections 1.4–1.5: transfer, initiator and responding target](amba-specification-rev-2-0-by-arm-limited.md)
