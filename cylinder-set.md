# Cylinder set

↑ **Parent:** [Product topology](product-topology.md)

A cylinder set in a product of spaces restricts a finite collection of coordinates $F$ and leaves all other coordinates free. It has the form $\pi_F^{-1}(A)$, where $\pi_F$ is the coordinate projection onto the finite subproduct. Taking $A$ to be a product of [open sets](open-set.md) gives the basic open cylinders for the [product topology](product-topology.md). In a finite-alphabet [full shift](full-shift.md), specifying exact letters at finitely many coordinates gives a [clopen set](clopen-set.md).
