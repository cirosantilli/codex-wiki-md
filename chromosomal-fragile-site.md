# Chromosomal fragile site

↑ **Parent:** [Chromosome](chromosome.md)

A locus prone to abnormal gaps, undercondensation or breakage under specified cellular conditions. A cytological gap is not by itself proof of a [DNA double-strand break](dna-double-strand-break.md).
