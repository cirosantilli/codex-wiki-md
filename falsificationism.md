# Falsificationism

↑ **Parent:** [Falsifiability](falsifiability.md)

A methodology emphasizing conjectures exposed to demanding attempts at refutation. [Falsifiability](falsifiability.md) is a property of claims under testing assumptions; falsificationism is a view about scientific practice.
