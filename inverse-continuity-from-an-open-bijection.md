# Inverse continuity from an open bijection

↑ **Parent:** [Homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md)

A continuous bijection that is an [open map](open-map.md) is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md). If $g=f^{-1}$ and $U$ is open in the original domain, then $g^{-1}(U)=f(U)$ is open by the open-map property. This proves continuity of the inverse directly.
