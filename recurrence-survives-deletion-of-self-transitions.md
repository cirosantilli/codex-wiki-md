# Recurrence survives deletion of self-transitions

↑ **Parent:** [Jump chain of a discrete-time Markov chain](jump-chain-of-a-discrete-time-markov-chain.md)

An irreducible recurrent discrete-time [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) with at least two states has $P_{ii}<1$ everywhere. Removing repeated consecutive states from a positive-[probability](probability.md) path proves irreducibility of its [discrete-time jump chain](jump-chain-of-a-discrete-time-markov-chain.md). Each sojourn in a state is almost surely finite; infinitely many visits to a [recurrent state](recurrent-state.md) therefore correspond to infinitely many distinct returning sojourns. The jump chain remains recurrent, although deleting [holding times](holding-time.md) may change periodicity.
