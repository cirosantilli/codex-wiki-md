# Subadditivity of matrix rank

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix rank](matrix-rank.md)

The image of the [linear map](linear-map.md) $A+B$ is contained in the sum of the images of $A$ and $B$. Since the [dimension of a vector space](dimension-vector-space.md) of this sum is at most the sum of their dimensions, [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) is subadditive. Iteration shows that a sum of $r$ [matrices](matrix.md) of [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) at most one has [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) at most $r$, over any [field](field.md).
