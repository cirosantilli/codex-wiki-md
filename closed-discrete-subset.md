# Closed discrete subset

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete subset](discrete-subset.md)

A closed discrete subset $D$ of a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$ is a [closed set](closed-set.md) whose [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md) is the [discrete topology](discrete-space.md). Every point of $D$ therefore has a [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) meeting $D$ at that point alone. Closedness excludes all accumulation points outside $D$ as well.

In a plane [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md), a closed [discrete subset](discrete-subset.md) is exactly a set meeting every [compact subset](compact-space.md) in finitely many points. This local finiteness is necessary for a nonzero [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) to have the subset as its entire zero set. A merely isolated-point subset can have an accumulation point outside the subset but inside the domain, as $\{1/n:n\geq1\}\subset\mathbb C$ does.
