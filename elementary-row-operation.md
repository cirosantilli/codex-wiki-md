# Elementary row operation

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian elimination](gaussian-elimination.md)

An [elementary row operation](elementary-row-operation.md) interchanges two rows of a [matrix](matrix.md), multiplies one row by a nonzero scalar, or adds a scalar multiple of another row to it. These invertible operations are used in [Gaussian elimination](gaussian-elimination.md). For a square [matrix](matrix.md), interchange changes the sign of its [determinant](determinant.md), multiplication scales its [determinant](determinant.md) by the same scalar, and adding another row leaves its [determinant](determinant.md) unchanged, by multilinearity and alternation.
