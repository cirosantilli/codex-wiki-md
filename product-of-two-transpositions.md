# Product of two transpositions

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

A product of two transpositions is the identity when they coincide, a [three-cycle](three-cycle.md) when their supports meet in one letter, and a product of two [disjoint permutation cycles](disjoint-permutation-cycles.md) when their supports are disjoint.
