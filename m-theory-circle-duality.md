# M-theory circle duality

↑ **Parent:** [M-theory](m-theory.md)

Compactifying [M-theory](m-theory.md) on a circle gives [type IIA superstring theory](type-iia-superstring-theory.md). The circle radius sets its coupling. Multiplying [M2-brane](m2-brane.md) and [M5-brane](m5-brane.md) tensions by the circle circumference gives the [fundamental string](fundamental-string.md) and [D4-brane](d4-brane.md) tensions, while unwrapped objects give [D2-branes](d2-brane.md) and [NS5-branes](ns5-brane.md). Momentum and a [Kaluza-Klein monopole](kaluza-klein-monopole.md) supply [D0-brane](d0-brane.md) and [D6-brane](d6-brane.md) charges. The circle dictionary and worldvolume reductions are developed in [Four Lectures on M-theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9612121).
