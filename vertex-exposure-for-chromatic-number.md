# Vertex exposure for chromatic number

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial random graph](binomial-random-graph.md)

Group random [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) by their larger endpoint. These independent coordinates each change [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) incident to only one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). Removing that [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) leaves the same [graph](graph-split.md) under any two outcomes, so their [chromatic numbers](chromatic-number.md) differ by at most one. The [McDiarmid inequality](mcdiarmid-s-inequality.md) with $n$ coordinate ranges of length one gives the displayed concentration bound.
