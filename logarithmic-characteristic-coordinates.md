# Logarithmic characteristic coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md)

For the Euler-type operator $-x^2\partial_x^2-x\partial_x+\partial_y^2$, set $z=\ln|x|$ on either half-plane $x\ne0$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $x\partial_x=\partial_z$ and $x^2\partial_x^2+x\partial_x=\partial_z^2$. The operator becomes $\partial_y^2-\partial_z^2$. Its [characteristic coordinates](characteristic-coordinate.md) $\xi=y+z$, $\eta=y-z$ reduce it further to $4\partial_\xi\partial_\eta$. Thus the constant-forcing equation $-x^2u_{xx}-xu_x+u_{yy}=c$ has solutions $u=(c/4)\xi\eta+g(\xi)+h(\eta)$, with arbitrary sufficiently differentiable [functions](function-split.md) $g,h$. The logarithmic [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) cannot by itself describe smooth passage across $x=0$.
