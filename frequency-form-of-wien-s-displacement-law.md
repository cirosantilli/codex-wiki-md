<h1 id="frequency-form-of-wien-s-displacement-law">Frequency form of Wien's displacement law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Wien's displacement law](wien-s-displacement-law.md)

For the frequency version of [Planck law](planck-s-law.md), differentiating $x^3/(e^x-1)$ gives $3(1-e^{-x})=x$ at its nonzero maximum. Here $x=h\nu/(k_BT)$, so the peak frequency is proportional to [temperature](temperature.md). This peak differs from converting the peak of the wavelength version by $\nu=c/\lambda$, because spectral densities transform with a Jacobian.
