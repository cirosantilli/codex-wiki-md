# Collinear quantum point contacts

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum point contact](quantum-point-contact.md)

An injector and collector aligned across an open [two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md). A direction-sensitive signal between them probes [ballistic electron transport](ballistic-electron-transport.md). Contact resistance and diffuse current backgrounds must be separated from the direct beam contribution.
