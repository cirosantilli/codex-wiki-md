# Conjugate-state steering from a maximally entangled pair

↑ **Parent:** [Maximally entangled state](maximally-entangled-state.md)

For $|\Phi_d\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_i|ii\rangle$ and normalized $|u\rangle=\sum_i u_i|i\rangle$, contraction of the first factor gives $d^{-1/2}\sum_i\overline{u_i}|i\rangle$. Hence a rank-one [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) outcome $u$ occurs with [probability](probability.md) $1/d$ and leaves the other subsystem in the coordinatewise [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) state $|\overline u\rangle$. The [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) refers to the paired [bases](basis.md) defining $|\Phi_d\rangle$; it must not be omitted for a complex measurement [vector](vector.md). Conditioning is essential: the unconditional [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) remains $I/d$.
