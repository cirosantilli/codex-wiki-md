# Kummer map of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md)

The map is a connecting homomorphism from the multiplication exact sequence. Its image is constrained by the local conditions defining the [Selmer group of an elliptic curve](n-selmer-group.md).

The multiplication-by-$n$ sequence gives an injective connecting map

$$
E(K)/nE(K)\hookrightarrow H^1(K,E[n]).
$$

Local conditions restrict its image to the finite $n$-Selmer group.
