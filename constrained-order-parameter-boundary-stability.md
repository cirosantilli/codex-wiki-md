# Constrained order-parameter boundary stability

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)

A [stationary point](stationary-point.md) on the domain $\phi\geq0$ must be tested using allowed one-sided variations. For $F=A\phi^2/2-B\phi^3+\lambda\phi^4/4$ with $B>0$, the boundary is a [local minimum](local-minimum.md) when $A>0$ and a [local maximum](local-maximum.md) when $A<0$. At $A=0$, the leading negative cubic term makes it a one-sided [local maximum](local-maximum.md) even though $F''(0)=0$; on an unrestricted real line it is instead a stationary inflection point.
