# Superleak

↑ **Parent:** [Thermomechanical effect](thermomechanical-effect.md)

A [superleak](superleak.md) is a narrow or porous connection that strongly suppresses the viscous [normal component of a superfluid](normal-component-of-a-superfluid.md) while allowing appreciable [superfluid](superfluid.md) flow. Treating $\mathbf v_n\simeq0$ is a regime assumption, not a perfect material filter for particular atoms. The [thermomechanical effect](thermomechanical-effect.md) uses that distinction to maintain a [temperature](temperature-split.md) difference during chemical equilibration. The idealization fails when normal leakage or dissipative [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md) motion becomes appreciable.
