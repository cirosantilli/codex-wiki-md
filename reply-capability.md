# Reply capability

↑ **Parent:** [Capability (computer security)](capability-computer-security.md)

A [security capability](capability-computer-security.md) authorizing a reply to a particular waiting caller. In the [seL4](sel4.md) tutorial's [IPC](inter-process-communication.md) model, a call establishes one-time reply authority, allowing a [user-space service](user-space-service.md) to return to the correct client. The exact representation depends on the [operating-system kernel](kernel-operating-system.md) configuration. [IPC tutorial, Background: System calls, seL4\_Call and reply capabilities](ipc-tutorial-by-sel4-project.md)
