# Vector subbundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_subbundle)

A vector subbundle $F\subseteq E$ is a subset whose fibers are [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) $F_x\subseteq E_x$ of constant dimension and which admits local frames extending to local frames of $E$.
