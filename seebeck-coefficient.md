# Seebeck coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Seebeck effect](seebeck-effect.md)

The [Seebeck coefficient](seebeck-coefficient.md) is thermoelectric voltage per temperature difference in the small-gradient limit, with its sign depending on the stated voltage and orientation convention. In a two-material loop the net electromotive force is an oriented integral $\int_{T_1}^{T_2}(S_1-S_2)\,dT$. It is a transport coefficient, so equilibrium density-of-states data alone do not determine it.
