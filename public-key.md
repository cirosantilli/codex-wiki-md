# Public key

↑ **Parent:** [Public-key cryptography](public-key-cryptography.md)

The published member of a public-key cryptographic key pair. Its association with a particular identity must itself be authenticated; mere knowledge of a purported [public key](public-key.md) does not establish that identity.
