# Squeeze resistance of an eccentric journal bearing

↑ **Parent:** [Journal bearing](journal-bearing.md)

Let the inner-centre displacement from a fixed outer centre have magnitude $r=\Delta e$, with $0\leq e<1$. The leading [full-film journal bearing](full-film-journal-bearing.md) resistance to translation in the displacement direction and perpendicular to it is

$$
R_r=\frac{12\pi\mu a^3}{\Delta^3(1-e^2)^{3/2}},\qquad
R_t=\frac{12\pi\mu a^3}{\Delta^3\sqrt{1-e^2}(1+e^2/2)}.
$$

For $h=\Delta(1-e\cos\theta)$, the [Reynolds lubrication equation](reynolds-equation.md) gives radial squeezing flux $av_r\sin\theta$ and tangential squeezing flux $av_t[-\cos\theta+3e/(2+e^2)]$. The additive constant enforces periodic [pressure](pressure.md). Integrating the [pressure](pressure.md) traction gives these coefficients; at $e=0$ they coincide, as required by [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md).
