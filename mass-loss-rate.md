# Mass loss rate

↑ **Parent:** [Mass flux](mass-flux.md)

The mass loss rate is the positive rate at which a body loses mass. For a steady spherical [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md), the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) gives $\dot M=4\pi r^2\rho u_r$, constant across spheres enclosing the source. It is positive for outward radial flow; an accretion convention often uses a separate positive inward rate.
