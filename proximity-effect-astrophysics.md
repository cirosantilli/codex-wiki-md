# Proximity effect (astrophysics)

↑ **Parent:** [Lyman-alpha forest](lyman-alpha-forest.md)

The quasar proximity effect is a reduction of nearby [Lyman-alpha forest](lyman-alpha-forest.md) absorption caused by extra [photoionization](photoionization.md) from the [quasar](quasar.md). At fixed density and temperature in [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md), an added rate $\Gamma_Q$ changes the neutral fraction by the factor $\Gamma_{\rm bg}/(\Gamma_{\rm bg}+\Gamma_Q)$. A host environment with positive [density contrast](density-contrast.md) can mask this reduction, and a variable or anisotropic source complicates its interpretation.
