# Heat equation energy identity

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

On a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) with a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), a smooth solution of $\partial_tu+\Delta u=0$ for the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) satisfies the displayed identity by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). Zero initial data force the solution to vanish, proving uniqueness and the [semigroup property](semigroup-property.md) for heat evolution.
