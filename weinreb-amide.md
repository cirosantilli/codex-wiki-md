# Weinreb amide

↑ **Parent:** [Amide](amide.md)

An N-methoxy-N-methyl [amide](amide.md) that can form a chelated tetrahedral intermediate on organometallic addition. Controlled [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) gives a [ketone](ketone.md) rather than immediate repeated addition.
