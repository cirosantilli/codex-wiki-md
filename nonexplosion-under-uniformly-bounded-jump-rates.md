# Nonexplosion under uniformly bounded jump rates

↑ **Parent:** [Explosion of a continuous-time Markov chain](explosion-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

If every holding rate of a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is at most $C<\infty$, its holding times can be coupled as $E_n/q_{Y_n}\geq E_n/C$, where the $E_n$ are independent rate-one [exponential random variables](exponential-distribution.md). The [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) gives $\sum_nE_n=\infty$ almost surely, so the jump times tend to infinity and the chain cannot [explode](explosion-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md).
