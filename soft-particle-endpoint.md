# Soft-particle endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic three-body decay](relativistic-three-body-decay.md)

A boundary of [phase space](phase-space.md) approached as a massless outgoing particle's [energy](energy-split.md) tends to zero. A zero four-vector is not a physical positive-energy one-particle state. An energy bound requiring such a particle is a [supremum](supremum.md) or [infimum](infimum.md), even when the closed kinematic interval is conventionally described using minimum and maximum. This distinction can matter when applying three-body formulas at mixed massive-massless recoil thresholds.
