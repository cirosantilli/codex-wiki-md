# Zeta transform

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)

The zeta transform on a finite [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) sends $f$ to $F(x)=\sum_{y\le x}f(y)$. The [subset zeta transform](subset-zeta-transform.md) is the case where the order is inclusion of subsets.
