# Homogeneous divergence-form elliptic equation

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence-form elliptic operator](divergence-form-elliptic-operator.md)

For a bounded measurable coefficient [matrix](matrix.md) $A$, an $H^1$ [weak solution](weak-solution.md) of $\operatorname{div}(A\nabla u)=0$ satisfies $\int A\nabla u\cdot\nabla\varphi=0$ for every compactly supported smooth [test function](test-function.md). By density this identity extends to compactly supported $H^1$ tests. If $A$ is symmetric and $\lambda I\le A\le\Lambda I$, [Caccioppoli inequalities](caccioppoli-inequality.md) follow without differentiating $A$.
