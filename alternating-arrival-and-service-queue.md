# Alternating arrival and service queue

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)

A [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) may alternate between an arrival phase $C$, with upward transitions at rate $\lambda$, and a service phase $W$, with downward transitions at rate $\mu$ when the level is positive. Switching rates $C\to W$ and $W\to C$ are $\alpha,\beta$. Its [stationary balance equations](global-balance-for-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md) give a geometric invariant tail with ratio $\theta=\lambda(\mu+\beta)/[\mu(\lambda+\alpha)]$. A stationary probability exists exactly when $\mu\alpha>\lambda\beta$, consistent with the stationary average service rate $\mu\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)$ exceeding the arrival rate $\lambda\beta/(\alpha+\beta)$.
