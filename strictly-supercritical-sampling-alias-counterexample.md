# Strictly supercritical sampling alias counterexample

↑ **Parent:** [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md)

For every $\epsilon>0$, choose a nonzero smooth spectral bump supported in $(-\epsilon_0,\epsilon_0)$ with $\epsilon_0<\min(\epsilon,\pi)$, and let $h$ be its inverse Fourier transform. Then $g$ is a nonzero [Schwartz function](schwartz-function.md), vanishes on all integer samples, and its transform is the difference of bumps shifted to $\pi$ and $-\pi$. Its support lies strictly within $[-\pi-\epsilon,\pi+\epsilon]$. Thus even a small bandwidth increase destroys uniqueness from unit-spaced samples.
