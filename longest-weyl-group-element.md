# Longest Weyl-group element

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl group](weyl-group.md)

The longest element of a finite [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) is the element sending all positive [roots of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md) to negative roots. It is the [longest element of a finite Coxeter group](longest-element-of-a-coxeter-group.md). For the [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md) it is $-I$: its twelve roots form two hexagons, and the dihedral [reflection group of a root system](reflection-group-of-a-root-system.md) contains the rotation through $\pi$. It determines the [highest weight of a dual representation](highest-weight-of-a-dual-representation.md).
