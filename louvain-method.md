# Louvain method

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

The [Louvain method](louvain-method.md) is a greedy multilevel [community detection](community-detection.md) algorithm maximizing [network modularity](network-modularity.md). It alternates positive-gain single-vertex moves with contraction of communities into weighted [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). Different vertex orders can produce different local optima; a result is not a certificate of globally maximum modularity.[§2, Method](fast-unfolding-of-communities-in-large-networks-by-vincent-d-blondel-and-others.md)
