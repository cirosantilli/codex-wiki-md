# Long-memory time series

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

A [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) can have very slowly decaying [autocovariance](autocovariance.md), for example $\gamma(h)\sim Ch^{-\beta}$ with $C>0$ and $0<\beta<1$. Its sample mean can require a normalization different from $\sqrt T$. Long memory is dependence over long lags, rather than a changing marginal distribution.
