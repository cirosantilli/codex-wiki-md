# Projection with a compact factor is closed

↑ **Parent:** [Closed map](closed-map.md)

For [metric spaces](metric-space.md) $X,Y$ with $X$ [compact](compact-space.md), the [projection with a compact factor is closed](projection-with-a-compact-factor-is-closed.md): if $F\subset X\times Y$ is a [closed set](closed-set.md) and $y_n\in\pi_Y(F)$ converges to $y$, choose $x_n$ with $(x_n,y_n)\in F$. [Sequential compactness](sequentially-compact-space.md) supplies $x_{n_k}\to x$, so $(x_{n_k},y_{n_k})\to(x,y)\in F$, proving $y\in\pi_Y(F)$. The [projection map](projection-map.md) is also [continuous](continuous-function.md) for the sum [metric](metric.md). Without [compactness](compact-space.md), the [closed set](closed-set.md) $\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:xy=1\}$ projects to the nonclosed set $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$.
