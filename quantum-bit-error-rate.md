# Quantum bit error rate

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum key distribution](quantum-key-distribution.md)

The disagreement probability of Alice's and Bob's sifted bits under their agreed outcome-to-bit maps. A finite test sample estimates this probability; the estimate and its uncertainty enter parameter estimation before [information reconciliation](information-reconciliation.md) and [privacy amplification](privacy-amplification.md).
