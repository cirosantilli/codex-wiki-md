# Cisternal maturation

↑ **Parent:** [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md)

In [cisternal maturation](cisternal-maturation.md), a cargo-containing [Golgi cisterna](golgi-cisterna.md) changes from early to later identity as its resident [proteins](protein-split.md) change; retrieval of resident [enzymes](enzyme-split.md) toward earlier compartments helps maintain [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md) polarity. In the contrasting stable-compartment model, cargo moves between persistent [Golgi cisternae](golgi-cisterna.md) in [transport vesicles](transport-vesicle.md). These mechanisms need not be mutually exclusive.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; Transport Through the Golgi Apparatus May Occur by Vesicular Transport or Cisternal Maturation, Figure 13-30 and concluding comparison](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/transport-from-the-er-through-the-golgi-apparatus.md)

The distinction is between following cargo and following its container. A cargo label encountered first in an early processing environment and later in a late one is compatible with either changing containers or a container changing identity. Finding [transport vesicles](transport-vesicle.md) nearby does not by itself establish which cargo they carry or their direction of travel. Testing [cisternal maturation](cisternal-maturation.md) therefore requires evidence about compartment identity and continuity as well as cargo position; a single mechanism should not be assumed for every cargo and [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md).
