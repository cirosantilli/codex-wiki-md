# Composition of affine transformations

↑ **Parent:** [Affine map](affine-map.md)

If $f(x)=Ax+b$ and $g(x)=Cx+d$, their [function composition](function-composition.md) is $g(f(x))=CAx+Cb+d$, again an [affine map](affine-map.md). With column-vector [homogeneous coordinates](homogeneous-coordinate.md), its matrix is the product of the matrix for $g$ with that for $f$, so the rightmost factor acts first.
