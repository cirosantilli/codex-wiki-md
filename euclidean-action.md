# Euclidean action

↑ **Parent:** [Wick rotation](wick-rotation.md)

A [Wick rotation](wick-rotation.md) from real time to imaginary time replaces the oscillatory weight $e^{iS}$ by $e^{-S_E}$. For a real scalar with positive kinetic normalization, $S_E=\int[\tfrac12(\partial\phi)^2+V(\phi)]\,d^dx$. Positivity of its kinetic term does not ensure convergence if the potential is unbounded below. Fermionic integrals instead use independent [Grassmann fields](grassmann-field.md) and need not define a positive measure.
