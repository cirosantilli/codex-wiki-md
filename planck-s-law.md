<h1 id="planck-s-law">Planck's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planck's_law)

[Planck's law](planck-s-law.md) gives the equilibrium spectral radiance of a [blackbody](blackbody.md) at [temperature](temperature.md) $T$. The [Planck function](planck-function.md) per unit [frequency](frequency.md) and per unit [wavelength](wavelength.md) is

$$
B_\nu(T)=\frac{2h\nu^3}{c^2}\frac1{e^{h\nu/(k_BT)}-1},
\qquad
B_\lambda(T)=\frac{2hc^2}{\lambda^5}\frac1{e^{hc/(\lambda k_BT)}-1}.
$$

The relation $B_\nu\,d\nu=-B_\lambda\,d\lambda$ accounts for the change from [frequency](frequency.md) to [wavelength](wavelength.md). Dividing the isotropic spectral energy density $4\pi B_\nu/c$ by the photon energy $h\nu$ gives the [Planck photon distribution](planck-photon-distribution.md).
