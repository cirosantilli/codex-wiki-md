# Conformational entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Protein folding](protein-folding.md)

Conformational entropy measures the multiplicity of molecular conformations compatible with specified macroscopic constraints. For equally weighted accessible conformations it is $k_B\ln\Omega$; for probabilities $p_i$, it is $-k_B\sum_i p_i\ln p_i$. A folded [protein](protein-split.md) generally has less chain conformational entropy than its unfolded ensemble, but folding can still lower total [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) when solvent and interaction contributions are included.
