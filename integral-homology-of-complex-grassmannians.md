# Integral homology of complex Grassmannians

↑ **Parent:** [Diagonal trace Morse function on a complex Grassmannian](diagonal-trace-morse-function-on-a-complex-grassmannian.md)

Here $N_r$ counts $k$-subsets $i_1<\cdots<i_k$ with $\sum_a(i_a-a)=r$, equivalently [partitions of an integer](partition-of-an-integer.md) $r$ contained in a $k$ by $(n-k)$ rectangle. The [diagonal trace Morse function on a complex Grassmannian](diagonal-trace-morse-function-on-a-complex-grassmannian.md) has only even [Morse indices](morse-index.md). Its [Morse chain complex](morse-chain-complex.md) therefore has zero differentials, proving the displayed torsion-free [integral homology](integral-homology.md). For $\operatorname{Gr}(2,4)$ the even-degree ranks are $(1,1,2,1,1)$ in degrees $0,2,4,6,8$.
