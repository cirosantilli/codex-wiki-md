# Gaussian white noise model

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian white noise](gaussian-white-noise.md)

Observe $Y(t)=\int_0^tf(s)\,ds+\sigma W(t)$ on $[0,1]$, where $f\in L^2[0,1]$ is an unknown deterministic drift and $W$ is standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). The observation against a deterministic test [function](function-split.md) is $Y(h)=\langle f,h\rangle+\sigma W(h)$, with [covariance](covariance.md) $\sigma^2\langle h,g\rangle$. [Gaussian white noise](gaussian-white-noise.md) is represented by an [isonormal Gaussian process](isonormal-gaussian-process.md) on test [functions](function-split.md) rather than a pointwise noise [function](function-split.md). Taking $\sigma=n^{-1/2}$ is the usual statistical scaling.
