# Worldsheet gravitino

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md)

The worldsheet gravitino is the fermionic vector-spinor gauge field for local [worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md), paired with the worldsheet metric in two-dimensional [supergravity](supergravity.md). In a locally supersymmetric string action it couples to the worldsheet supercurrent. Fixing its gauge and the bosonic [conformal gauge](conformal-gauge.md) leaves the usual flat rigid transformations on embeddings and [worldsheet Majorana fermions](worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md); it should not be silently omitted when claiming an arbitrary curved-worldsheet locally supersymmetric action.
