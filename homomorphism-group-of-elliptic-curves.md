# Homomorphism group of elliptic curves

↑ **Parent:** [Isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md)

The group $\operatorname{Hom}_K(E_1,E_2)$ consists of morphisms of [elliptic curves](elliptic-curve.md) defined over $K$ that preserve the [group operation](group-operation.md), with pointwise addition. Its zero element is the constant map to the identity. Every nonzero element is an [isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md): its image is a closed connected subgroup of the target, so nonconstancy makes it the whole curve, and a nonconstant morphism of smooth projective curves is finite. The [degree of an isogeny](degree-of-an-isogeny.md) extends to this group by assigning degree zero to the zero homomorphism.
