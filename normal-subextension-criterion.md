# Normal subextension criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Galois correspondence](galois-correspondence.md)

For a finite Galois extension $L/K$ with group $G$, an intermediate extension $E/K$ is normal exactly when its corresponding subgroup $\operatorname{Gal}(L/E)$ is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of $G$.
