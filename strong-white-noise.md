# Strong white noise

↑ **Parent:** [White noise](white-noise.md)

A sequence of [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with zero mean and finite variance is strong [white noise](white-noise.md). It is [weak white noise](weak-white-noise.md), but need not have a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md).
