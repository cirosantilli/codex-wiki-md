# Nucleation

↑ **Parent:** [Metastability](metastability.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleation)

Nucleation forms a sufficiently large region of a new phase within an old [metastable](metastability.md) phase. In [classical nucleation theory](classical-nucleation-theory.md), positive surface energy competes with bulk free-energy gain, producing a [critical nucleus](critical-nucleus.md) and a barrier to conversion. The [Arrhenius nucleation time](arrhenius-nucleation-time.md) depends exponentially on this barrier; boundaries and impurities can alter it. This explains how [metastability](metastability.md) and [hysteresis](hysteresis.md) can persist despite a different globally stable phase.
