# Hermitian hyperbolic plane over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian form over a quadratic finite field](hermitian-form-over-a-quadratic-finite-field.md)

In a two-dimensional nondegenerate [Hermitian form over a quadratic finite field](hermitian-form-over-a-quadratic-finite-field.md), choose an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $v,w$ and $a$ with $a\bar a=-1$. Then $e=v+aw$ is an [isotropic vector](isotropic-vector.md). Choose $g$ with $B(e,g)=1$, and choose $c$ with $c+\bar c=B(g,g)$. The vector $f=g-ce$ satisfies $B(e,f)=1$ and $B(f,f)=0$. Thus $e,f$ give the displayed matrix, including in characteristic two. Iterating on nondegenerate [orthogonal complement for a sesquilinear form](orthogonal-complement-for-a-sesquilinear-form.md) leaves only a unit-norm line in odd dimension.
