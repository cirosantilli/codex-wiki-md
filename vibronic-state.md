# Vibronic state

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)

A joint electronic and vibrational [quantum state](quantum-state.md) of a [molecule](molecule-split.md). In a product approximation its [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) is the [tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) of the electronic and vibrational factors.
