# A good rate function is separated from zero away from its minimizer

↑ **Parent:** [Good rate function](good-rate-function.md)

If a [good rate function](good-rate-function.md) has unique zero mu, every [closed set](closed-set.md) missing mu has strictly positive [infimum](infimum.md) rate, possibly infinity. Otherwise a sequence of rates tending to zero lies eventually in the compact unit [sublevel set](sublevel-set.md); a subsequence converges to a point of the [closed set](closed-set.md), and [lower semicontinuity](lower-semicontinuity.md) makes that point another zero. Compactness also ensures existence of a zero whenever the [infimum](infimum.md) is zero.
