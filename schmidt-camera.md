# Schmidt camera

↑ **Parent:** [Optical telescope](optical-telescope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt_camera)

A Schmidt camera combines a spherical primary mirror with a thin aspheric corrector at the mirror’s centre of curvature. The corrector compensates [spherical aberration](spherical-aberration.md); the symmetric stop location gives a wide useful field. The native focal surface is curved and lies inside the instrument.
