# Fundamental temporal bright soliton

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal optical soliton](temporal-optical-soliton.md)

For positive Kerr nonlinearity and anomalous [group velocity dispersion](group-velocity-dispersion.md), the displayed sech envelope solves the ideal cubic fibre equation. Substitution uses $(\operatorname{sech}u)''=\operatorname{sech}u-2\operatorname{sech}^3u$. Dispersion and [nonlinear lengths](nonlinear-length.md) coincide. Higher-order corrections, attenuation and a delayed response can change actual propagation.
