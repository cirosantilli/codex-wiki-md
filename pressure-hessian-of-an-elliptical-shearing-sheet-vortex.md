# Pressure Hessian of an elliptical shearing-sheet vortex

↑ **Parent:** [Kida vortex](kida-vortex.md)

For the incompressible velocity $u_x=\alpha y$, $u_y=-\beta x$ in a [shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md), advection produces $-\alpha\beta(x,y)$ and the [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) produces $(2\Omega\beta x,2\Omega\alpha y)$. Including the radial tide $2\Omega Sx$ gives $p/\rho=(Ax^2+By^2)/2+C$. A material ellipse has $\beta=r^2\alpha$. A vortex patch is immersed in surrounding fluid, so this [pressure](pressure.md) need not vanish on its boundary; imposing that condition would describe a different free-boundary problem.
