# Diode minority-carrier diffusion current

↑ **Parent:** [Shockley diode equation](shockley-diode-equation.md)

For a long, nondegenerate, ideal [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md) under low injection, the [diode saturation current](diode-saturation-current.md) is set by [minority carriers](minority-carrier.md) supplied from the neutral regions. Their equilibrium densities are $n_{p0}=n_i^2/N_A$ and $p_{n0}=n_i^2/N_D$. Solving the steady diffusion and recombination equations gives $I_S=e\mathcal A(D_nn_{p0}/L_n+D_pp_{n0}/L_p)$, where $\mathcal A$ is junction area, $D_n,D_p$ are diffusion coefficients, and $L_n,L_p$ are diffusion lengths. Reverse bias suppresses opposite majority-carrier injection exponentially while collecting this minority supply. Thus $I_S$ is approximately bias independent before breakdown, but depends strongly on [temperature](temperature-split.md) through $n_i^2$ and on transport, doping, and geometry.
