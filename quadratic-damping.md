# Quadratic damping

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md)

A resistive velocity term proportional to the speed squared removes energy at rate proportional to $|\omega|^3$. Its derivative at zero velocity vanishes, so it does not appear in the [linearization of a dynamical system](linearization-of-a-dynamical-system.md) at rest. Nonlinear energy arguments can still prove [asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md).
