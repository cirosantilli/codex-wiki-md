<h1 id="teichmuller-s-uniqueness-theorem">Teichmüller's uniqueness theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmüller map](teichmuller-map.md)

On a closed [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-at-least-two [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md), a [Teichmüller map](teichmuller-map.md) uniquely minimizes [maximal dilatation](maximal-dilatation.md) in its [homotopy class](homotopy-class.md) among maps to the same target. The [Reich–Strebel inequality](reich-strebel-inequality.md) forces equality of [Beltrami coefficients](beltrami-coefficient.md) for an extremal competitor; their conformal difference is [homotopic](homotopy.md) to the identity and hence is the identity. In [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) one, translations must be factored out.
