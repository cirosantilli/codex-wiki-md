# Shear strain

↑ **Parent:** [Strain](strain.md)

Engineering [shear strain](shear-strain.md) is the first-order change of the right [angle](angle.md) between material lines initially directed along two perpendicular axes. For a displacement $\mathbf u$, $\gamma_{xy}=\partial_yu_x+\partial_xu_y=2\varepsilon_{xy}$, where $\varepsilon_{xy}$ is the tensor [strain](strain.md) component. Slender [Euler-Bernoulli beam equation](euler-bernoulli-beam-equation.md) models neglect the transverse [shear strain](shear-strain.md) contribution to deflection.
