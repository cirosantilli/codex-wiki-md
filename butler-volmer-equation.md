# Butler-Volmer equation

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemical polarization](electrochemical-polarization.md)

A kinetic model for the net [electrode](electrode.md) current near and away from equilibrium. For a single effective reaction with consistent activities and $\alpha_a+\alpha_c=1$, $j=j_0[\exp(\alpha_anF\eta/RT)-\exp(-\alpha_cnF\eta/RT)]$. Near equilibrium it gives $j\simeq j_0nF\eta/(RT)$; at sufficiently large [overpotential](overpotential.md) one exponential gives the [Tafel equation](tafel-equation.md). Multistep reactions can require a different kinetic interpretation.
