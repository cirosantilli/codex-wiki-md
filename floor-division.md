# Floor division

↑ **Parent:** [Floor function](floor-function.md)

The operation returning $\lfloor a/b\rfloor$ for $b\ne0$. It rounds the exact quotient toward negative infinity; for nonnegative [integers](integer-split.md) and positive $b$, it is the quotient in [Euclidean division](euclidean-division.md).
