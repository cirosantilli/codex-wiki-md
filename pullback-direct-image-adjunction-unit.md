# Pullback-direct-image adjunction unit

↑ **Parent:** [Pullback of a sheaf of modules](pullback-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md)

For a [morphism of ringed spaces](morphism-of-ringed-spaces.md), the [pullback of a sheaf of modules](pullback-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md) is left adjoint to the [direct image sheaf](direct-image-sheaf.md) functor. The unit sends a local section $h$ to $1\otimes h$ after inverse image. On the [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) it becomes the given [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $\mathcal O_Y\to f_*\mathcal O_X$.
