# Oscillating point-mass defect

↑ **Parent:** [Weak convergence of distributions](weak-convergence-of-distributions.md)

A sequence can converge as [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) off one point while retaining a nonconvergent multiple of a [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) at that point. If $u_j=v+c_j\delta_p+o_{\mathcal D'}(1)$ with $c_j$ nonconvergent, restriction to the punctured domain tends to $v$, but a test nonzero at $p$ detects the obstruction to global convergence.
