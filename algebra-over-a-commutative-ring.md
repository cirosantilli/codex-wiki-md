# Algebra over a commutative ring

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative algebra](commutative-algebra-split.md)

An algebra over a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$ is a [ring](ring.md) $A$ with a specified unital [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $R\to A$ whose image commutes with every element of $A$. Scalar multiplication is $r\cdot a=\varphi(r)a$. For commutative $A$, the commutation requirement is automatic.
