# Diels-Alder reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Cycloaddition](cycloaddition.md)

A concerted cycloaddition of a conjugated [diene](diene.md) and a [dienophile](dienophile.md) that makes a six-membered ring with two new [sigma bonds](sigma-bond.md) and one retained alkene. The internal [diene](diene.md) atoms become the residual alkene atoms.
