# Two-port network

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-port_network)

A [two-port network](two-port-network.md) relates voltage and current, or incoming and outgoing travelling-wave amplitudes, at two pairs of electrical terminals. Power-normalized scattering parameters obey $b_i=\sum_jS_{ij}a_j$. In a reciprocal network with equal reference impedances, $S_{12}=S_{21}$. A load reflection at port 2 can return through both transmission coefficients to port 1.
