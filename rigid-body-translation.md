# Rigid-body translation

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

A [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) undergoes translation when every point has the same [displacement](displacement.md) at each time, so its orientation stays fixed. For a freely moving system of particles coupled only by internal springs, the common [displacement](displacement.md) is a zero-frequency [normal mode](normal-mode.md): internal spring lengths and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) are unchanged. Its mode coordinate is $A+Bt$, representing constant [centre-of-mass velocity](center-of-mass-velocity.md), rather than an oscillation at nonzero [frequency](frequency.md).
