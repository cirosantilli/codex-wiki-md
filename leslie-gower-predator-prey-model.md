# Leslie-Gower predator-prey model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)

The system $\dot u=u(1-u)-auv/(u+d)$, $\dot v=bv(1-v/u)$ combines logistic prey growth, saturating predation and a predator carrying level proportional to prey density. Its unique positive equilibrium is $u=v=s$, where $(1-s)(s+d)=as$. Its [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has [determinant](determinant.md) $bs\sqrt{(1-a-d)^2+4d}/(s+d)>0$ and trace $s(1-d-2s)/(s+d)-b$. The [positive trapping construction for the Leslie-Gower model](positive-trapping-construction-for-the-leslie-gower-model.md) gives a compact positive region and a [limit cycle](limit-cycle.md) whenever this equilibrium is a repeller.
