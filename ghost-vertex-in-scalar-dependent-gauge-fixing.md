# Ghost vertex in scalar-dependent gauge fixing

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-dependent gauge fixing](scalar-dependent-gauge-fixing.md)

The [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) couples to both the [gauge field](gauge-field.md) and the [adjoint scalar field](adjoint-scalar-field.md). In a convention with $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+[A_\mu,\cdot]$, the ghost action after [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) contains $f^{abc}(\partial_\mu\bar c^a)A_\mu^b c^c$ and $f^{abc}(n\cdot\partial\bar c^a)\phi^b c^c/\sqrt2$.
