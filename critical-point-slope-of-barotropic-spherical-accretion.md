# Critical-point slope of barotropic spherical accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic spherical accretion equation](barotropic-spherical-accretion-equation.md)

At a regular [sonic point](sonic-point.md), differentiating the vanishing coefficient and numerator yields $(2+C/c_s^2)(u^{\prime})^2+4Cu^{\prime}/(ru)+(4C+2c_s^2)/r^2+\Phi^{\prime\prime}=0$. A real slope compatible with the desired branch is needed in addition to the sonic equalities. For constant [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) this reduces to $(u^{\prime})^2=-c_s^2/r^2-\Phi^{\prime\prime}/2$.
