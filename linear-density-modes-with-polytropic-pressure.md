# Linear density modes with polytropic pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological density perturbation](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md)

In a flat matter-dominated universe with $a\propto t^{2/3}$ and $P\propto\rho^{4/3}$, the squared [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) scales as $t^{-2/3}$. Each comoving Fourier mode then satisfies an [Euler-Cauchy equation](euler-cauchy-equation.md), with gravitational growth at long wavelengths and logarithmic-time acoustic oscillations at short wavelengths.
