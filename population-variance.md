# Population variance

↑ **Parent:** [Variance](variance-split.md)

For a finite population $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ with [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md) $\bar x$, its variance is $\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n(x_i-\bar x)^2$. It averages squared deviations over the whole population.
