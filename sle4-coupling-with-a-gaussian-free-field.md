# SLE4 coupling with a Gaussian free field

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-boundary Gaussian free field](zero-boundary-gaussian-free-field.md)

For a [Gaussian free field](gaussian-free-field.md) with boundary heights $-\lambda$ and $+\lambda$ on the two sides of the starting point, its distinguished zero-height interface has chordal $\operatorname{SLE}_4$ law. Conditional on an initial curve segment, the field is a zero-boundary field in the slit domain plus $m_t(z)=\lambda-(2\lambda/\pi)\arg(g_t(z)-W_t)$. Its mean bracket equals minus the variation of its conditional Green [covariance](covariance.md). Subtracting the initial [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) gives the corresponding coupling to a zero-boundary field.
