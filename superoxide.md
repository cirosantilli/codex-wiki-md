# Superoxide

↑ **Parent:** [Reactive oxygen species](reactive-oxygen-species.md)

The radical anion $\mathrm{O}_2^{\bullet-}$. Its production, reactivity and localization differ from those of [hydrogen peroxide](hydrogen-peroxide.md); the two should not be treated as an interchangeable oxidant measurement.
