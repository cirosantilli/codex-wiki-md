# Skeleton and collider characterization of Markov equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Markov equivalence of directed acyclic graphs](markov-equivalence-of-directed-acyclic-graphs.md)

Two [Directed acyclic graphs](directed-acyclic-graph.md) are [Markov equivalent directed acyclic graphs](markov-equivalence-of-directed-acyclic-graphs.md) if and only if they have the same [skeleton of a directed graph](skeleton-of-a-directed-graph.md) and the same [unshielded colliders](unshielded-collider.md). Thus these two structures determine the observational equivalence class. This structural theorem is what converts the skeleton and collider phases of the [PC algorithm](pc-algorithm.md) into identification of an equivalence class.
