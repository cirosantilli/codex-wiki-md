# Spacetime isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Isometry](isometry.md)

A transformation preserving a spacetime [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). For [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), the affine transformations with this property form the [Poincaré group](poincare-group.md). A spacetime isometry is a symmetry of particular dynamical equations only if their other fixed structures and couplings are preserved as well.
