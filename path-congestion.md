# Path congestion

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical paths Poincare bound](canonical-paths-poincare-bound.md)

For a family of transition [graph paths](path-in-a-graph.md), path congestion is the largest, over transitions, ratio of routed pair weight times [path](continuous-path.md) length to stationary transition capacity. The [canonical paths Poincare bound](canonical-paths-poincare-bound.md) makes this an upper bound on the [relaxation time](relaxation-time.md).
