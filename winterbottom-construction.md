# Winterbottom construction

↑ **Parent:** [Wulff construction](wulff-construction.md)

An equilibrium construction for a supported particle that modifies the [Wulff construction](wulff-construction.md) using particle-support and support-medium interfacial energies. The support truncates the corresponding free-particle shape.
