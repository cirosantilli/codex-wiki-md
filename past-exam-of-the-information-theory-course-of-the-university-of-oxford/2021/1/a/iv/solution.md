<h1 id="1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Here the asserted bound is actually **an identity, so equality always holds** whenever the displayed quantities are defined. Expand the same [mutual information](../../../../../mutual-information.md) in two orders:

$$
\begin{aligned}
I(X,Y;Z)&=I(Y;Z)+I(X;Z\mid Y),\\
I(X,Y;Z)&=I(X;Z)+I(Y;Z\mid X).
\end{aligned}
$$

Equating these expressions and using [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of [mutual information](../../../../../mutual-information.md) and [conditional mutual information](../../../../../conditional-mutual-information.md) gives

$$
\boxed{I(X;Z\mid Y)=I(Z;Y\mid X)-I(Z;Y)+I(X;Z).}
$$

There is no further [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) condition. Treating this part as a strict inequality would obscure its cancellation.
