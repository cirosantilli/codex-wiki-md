<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Exchange the two complete [codewords](../../../../../codeword.md) while leaving the [code tree](../../../../../prefix-tree-of-a-code.md) unchanged. The [prefix code](../../../../../prefix-code.md) property is preserved because the set of [codewords](../../../../../codeword.md) is unchanged. If $p_i>p_j$ but $l_i>l_j$, the change in [expected codeword length](../../../../../expected-codeword-length.md) is

$$
(p_il_j+p_jl_i)-(p_il_i+p_jl_j)=(p_i-p_j)(l_j-l_i)<0,
$$

contradicting optimality. Hence **$p_i>p_j$ implies $l_i\leq l_j$**. This [probability monotonicity of optimal prefix-code lengths](../../../../../probability-monotonicity-of-optimal-prefix-code-lengths.md) is valid even if some [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) are zero; equal [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) impose no order.[p. 1099, Derived Coding Requirements: probability ordering and permissible exchange of equal-probability messages](../../../../../a-method-for-the-construction-of-minimum-redundancy-codes-by-david-a-huffman.md)
