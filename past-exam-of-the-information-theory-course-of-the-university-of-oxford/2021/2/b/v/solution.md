<h1 id="2/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Let $L$ again denote the largest [codeword length](../../../../../codeword-length.md), and assume a [full-support finite distribution](../../../../../full-support-probability-distribution-on-a-finite-alphabet.md). The missing-branch exchange from part iii forces every internal [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) at depth at most $L-2$ to have three [tree children](../../../../../child-in-a-rooted-tree.md). At depth $L-1$, all [tree children](../../../../../child-in-a-rooted-tree.md) are [tree leaves](../../../../../leaf-of-a-tree.md). Part ii rules out one [tree child](../../../../../child-in-a-rooted-tree.md) there; the hypothesis that there is no pair of two deepest [sibling codewords](../../../../../sibling-leaves-in-a-code-tree.md) rules out two [tree children](../../../../../child-in-a-rooted-tree.md). Thus each such [tree parent](../../../../../parent-in-a-rooted-tree.md) also has three [tree children](../../../../../child-in-a-rooted-tree.md). **Every internal [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) has exactly three [tree children](../../../../../child-in-a-rooted-tree.md)**, which is a [full ternary tree](../../../../../full-ternary-tree.md), called semi-complete in this paper. The same last-level argument covers $L=1$.

Without the assumption of a [full-support finite distribution](../../../../../full-support-probability-distribution-on-a-finite-alphabet.md) the implication is false: for $p=(1,0,0)$, the optimal [prefix code](../../../../../prefix-code.md) $(0,10,110)$ has no deepest pair of two [sibling codewords](../../../../../sibling-leaves-in-a-code-tree.md), but its [code tree](../../../../../prefix-tree-of-a-code.md) contains [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) with a single [tree child](../../../../../child-in-a-rooted-tree.md). Empty unused [tree children](../../../../../child-in-a-rooted-tree.md) are not [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) of the [code tree](../../../../../prefix-tree-of-a-code.md).
