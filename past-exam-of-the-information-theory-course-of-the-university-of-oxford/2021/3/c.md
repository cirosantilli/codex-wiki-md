↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Interpret additive noise in the standard channel sense: $Z$ is [independent](../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the chosen input $X$ and its law is fixed as the input law is varied. A uniform marginal law for $Z$ alone, without this [independence](../../../independent-random-variables.md) assumption, would not specify the [transition matrix](../../../stochastic-matrix.md). Only reachable output symbols are counted.
