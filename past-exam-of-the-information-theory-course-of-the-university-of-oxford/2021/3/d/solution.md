<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For every input [probability mass function](../../../../probability-mass-function.md), [conditional entropy](../../../../conditional-entropy.md) is nonnegative, so

$$
I(X;Y)=H(X)-H(X\mid Y)\leq H(X)\leq\log_2|\mathcal X|=1.
$$

Taking the maximum over input laws proves **$C\leq1$ [bit](../../../../bit.md) per channel use**. This bound comes from the binary input [alphabet](../../../../alphabet.md) itself; no specific [transition probabilities](../../../../transition-probability.md) or [decoder](../../../../channel-decoder.md) are needed.
