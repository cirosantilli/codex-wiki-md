# Unstable density stratification

↑ **Parent:** [Stable density stratification](stable-density-stratification.md)

A fluid has unstable density stratification when a vertical displacement amplifies itself. Denser fluid above lighter fluid can overturn by [Rayleigh-Taylor instability](rayleigh-taylor-instability.md) or develop [thermal convection](thermal-convection.md) when the density difference is thermal.
