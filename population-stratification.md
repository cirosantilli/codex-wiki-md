# Population stratification

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_stratification)

Population stratification is systematic variation in allele frequencies between subpopulations. If those subpopulations also differ in outcome causes, [genetic variant](genetic-variant.md) associations can exhibit [confounding](confounding.md), violating [instrumental-variable independence](instrumental-variable-independence.md) in [Mendelian randomization](mendelian-randomization.md).
