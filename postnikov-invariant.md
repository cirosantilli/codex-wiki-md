# Postnikov invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Principal Eilenberg–MacLane fibration](principal-eilenberg-maclane-fibration.md)

The $n$th [Postnikov invariant](postnikov-invariant.md) is the [cohomology class](cohomology-class.md) $k_n\in H^{n+1}(P_{n-1}X;\pi_nX)$ whose representing map has [homotopy fibre](homotopy-fiber.md) $P_nX$. It specifies how the new [homotopy group](homotopy-group.md) is attached to the previous stage. A zero invariant gives the product $P_nX\simeq P_{n-1}X\times K(\pi_nX,n)$. Merely tensoring [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) with $\mathbb Q$ does not describe [rationalization of a topological space](rationalization-of-a-topological-space.md) unless these classes are also transported.
