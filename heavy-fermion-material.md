# Heavy fermion material

↑ **Parent:** [Electron correlation](electron-correlation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_fermion_material)

A strongly correlated material whose low-energy electronic [quasiparticles](quasiparticle.md) have a very large thermodynamic [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md). An enhanced [electronic heat capacity coefficient](electronic-heat-capacity-coefficient.md), spin susceptibility and quantum-oscillation [cyclotron effective mass](cyclotron-effective-mass.md) are complementary signatures; they need not all be related by a single isotropic band mass. A [Kondo lattice](kondo-lattice.md) is an important setting for such behaviour. In [CeAl3](ceal3.md), low-temperature specific heat and quadratic resistivity provided an early quantitative example.[Abstract: low-temperature electronic heat capacity and electrical resistivity](4f-virtual-bound-state-formation-in-ceal3-at-low-temperatures-by-klaus-andres-and-others.md)
