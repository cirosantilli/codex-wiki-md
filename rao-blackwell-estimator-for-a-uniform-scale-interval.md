# Rao-Blackwell estimator for a uniform scale interval

↑ **Parent:** [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md)

For independent [uniform random variables](uniform-random-variable.md) on $(\theta,3\theta)$ with $\theta>0$, the sample minimum and maximum form a [sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md). Conditional on these endpoints, [exchangeability](exchangeable-random-variables.md) and the uniform law of the remaining observations give $E[X_1\mid X_{(1)},X_{(n)}]=(X_{(1)}+X_{(n)})/2$. Applying the [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md) to $X_1/2$ gives the displayed [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md). Its [variance](variance-split.md) is $\theta^2/[2(n+1)(n+2)]$, compared with $\theta^2/12$ for $X_1/2$.
