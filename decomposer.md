# Decomposer

↑ **Parent:** [Consumer (food chain)](consumer-food-chain.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decomposer)

A [decomposer](decomposer.md) breaks down dead organic material and absorbs products of that breakdown; many [bacteria](bacteria.md) and [fungi](fungus.md) perform this role. [Decomposers](decomposer.md) connect [detritus](detritus.md) from different [trophic levels](trophic-level.md) to material recycling, so placing all [decomposers](decomposer.md) at one final step of every [food chain](food-chain.md) is misleading. See [Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e, 46.1: grazing and detrital food webs](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/46-1-ecology-of-ecosystems).
