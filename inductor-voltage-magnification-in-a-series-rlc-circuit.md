# Inductor voltage magnification in a series RLC circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md)

For series resistance $R$, [inductance](inductance.md) $L$ and [capacitance](capacitance.md) $C$, with output measured across the [inductor](inductor.md), the $e^{i\omega t}$ transfer function is $H_L=i\omega L/[R+i(\omega L-1/(\omega C))]$. At current [resonance](resonance.md) $\omega_0=1/\sqrt{LC}$ its modulus is $\omega_0L/R=Q$. Large and oppositely phased [inductor](inductor.md) and [capacitor](capacitor.md) [voltages](voltage.md) nearly cancel in the loop sum. This is [voltage](voltage.md) magnification in a passive circuit, not [power gain](power-gain.md). The maximum of $|H_L|$ need not occur exactly at the current-resonance [frequency](frequency.md).
