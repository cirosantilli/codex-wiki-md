# Diagonal compactness for bounded Lipschitz functions

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded Lipschitz norm](bounded-lipschitz-norm.md)

On a separable [metric space](metric-space.md), a sequence uniformly bounded in [bounded Lipschitz norm](bounded-lipschitz-norm.md) has a pointwise convergent subsequence. Enumerate a countable dense subset and use the [Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem](bolzano-weierstrass-theorem.md) and [diagonal subsequence argument](diagonal-subsequence-argument.md) to obtain convergence there. Uniform control of the [Lipschitz constant](lipschitz-constant.md) then makes the subsequence Cauchy at every point. The [pointwise closure of the bounded Lipschitz unit ball](pointwise-closure-of-the-bounded-lipschitz-unit-ball.md) preserves the original norm bound after rescaling.
