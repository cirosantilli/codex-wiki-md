# Equatorial Kerr null geodesic radial equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md)

For an equatorial [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) of the [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md), $E=-p_t$ and $L=p_\phi$ are [geodesic conserved quantities from Killing vectors](geodesic-conserved-quantity-from-a-killing-vector.md). In units $G=c=1$, $\Delta=r^2-2Mr+a^2$, and an [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) gives the displayed equation. It follows by solving the stationary and axial momentum equations for $\dot t,\dot\phi$ and substituting them into the [null condition](null-condition.md). A zero of its radial polynomial is only a turning point; an exterior [circular null geodesic](circular-null-geodesic.md) also requires the derivative of that polynomial to vanish with $E,L$ held fixed.
