# Affine bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_bundle)

A [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md) whose fibers are [affine spaces](affine-space.md) and whose transition functions are affine. Differences of points in one fiber lie in a corresponding model [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md), but there need not be a preferred zero in each fiber.
