# Spherical elastic ray invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic ray](elastic-ray.md)

For an [isotropic](isotropy.md) radial [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $\alpha(r)$, the [elastic ray](elastic-ray.md) equations imply conservation of $\mathbf x\times\mathbf y$, where $\mathbf y$ is the [slowness](slowness.md) vector. Indeed, its derivative is $\alpha\mathbf y\times\mathbf y+\mathbf x\times\nabla(1/\alpha)=0$. Thus each nonradial [elastic ray](elastic-ray.md) lies in a fixed plane through the centre and $p=|\mathbf x\times\mathbf y|$ is constant. The angle $i$ is measured from the radial direction; tangential [slowness](slowness.md) continuity preserves $p$ across spherical interfaces.
