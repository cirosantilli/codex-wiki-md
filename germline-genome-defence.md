# Germline genome defence

↑ **Parent:** [Germline](germline.md)

Mechanisms protecting the inherited [genome](genome.md) from [transposable elements](transposable-element.md) and other threats. Suppression of transposon [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md), [chromatin](chromatin.md) repression and removal of damaged [cells](biological-cell-split.md) are different protective strategies.
