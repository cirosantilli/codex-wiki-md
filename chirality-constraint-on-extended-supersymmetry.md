# Chirality constraint on extended supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Extended supersymmetry](extended-supersymmetry.md)

In a four-dimensional theory with unbroken $\mathcal N\ge2$ [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md), a full charged [hypermultiplet](hypermultiplet.md) contains $\mathcal N=1$ [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md) in $R$ and $\overline R$. Their net complex [chiral gauge spectrum](chiral-gauge-spectrum.md) vanishes. The [vector multiplet](supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) is in the real adjoint [gauge group representation](gauge-group-representation.md). Half-[hypermultiplets](hypermultiplet.md) in [pseudoreal representations](pseudoreal-representation.md) do not supply genuinely complex gauge chirality. Thus the chiral matter of the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) can have at most unbroken $\mathcal N=1$ [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) in four dimensions.
