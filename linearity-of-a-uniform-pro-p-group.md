# Linearity of a uniform pro-p group

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform pro-p group](uniform-pro-p-group.md)

Every [uniform pro-p group](uniform-pro-p-group.md) embeds continuously in $\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb Z_p)$ for some finite $n$. Apply the [Ado theorem](ado-s-theorem.md) to $\mathbb Q_p\otimes L_G$ and choose a deep power lattice whose representing [matrices](matrix.md) lie in $p\operatorname{End}(\Lambda)$, or $4\operatorname{End}(\Lambda)$ at $p=2$. The [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) and [logarithm](logarithm.md) give a [faithful representation](faithful-representation.md) of the corresponding open normal power [subgroup](subgroup.md). Induce that representation to the whole [group](group-split.md) using its finitely many [cosets](coset.md). An element outside the [open subgroup](open-subgroup.md) permutes the [coset](coset.md) blocks nontrivially; an element inside it is detected by the original representation. Thus the [induced representation](induced-representation.md) is faithful. All block [matrices](matrix.md) preserve a finite free $\mathbb Z_p$-module, giving the integral embedding.
