# Cover (topology)

↑ **Parent:** [Set](set-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cover_(topology))

A cover of a [set](set-split.md) $E$ is a family of [sets](set-split.md) whose [union](set-union.md) contains $E$. The members may overlap and may include points outside $E$; intersecting each member with $E$ gives a cover by its [subsets](subset.md). An [open cover](open-cover.md) requires its members to be [open sets](open-set.md). In [Hausdorff measure](hausdorff-measure.md), [countable](countable-set.md) covers with a bound on each member's [diameter](diameter.md) determine geometric size. Constructing one inexpensive [cover](cover-topology.md) supplies an upper bound, while a lower bound must control all allowed [covers](cover-topology.md).
