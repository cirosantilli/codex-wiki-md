# Atomic finite-surjection site

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic topology](atomic-topology.md)

Use a small skeleton of nonempty finite sets and surjections, with every nonempty sieve covering. Fiber-product projections are surjections, so the atomic coverage exists. Surjections are effective quotients of their kernel pairs, making the site [subcanonical](subcanonical-topology.md). Its sheaves admit the [primitive decomposition of an atomic finite-surjection sheaf](primitive-decomposition-of-an-atomic-finite-surjection-sheaf.md).
