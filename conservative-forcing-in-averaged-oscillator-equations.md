# Conservative forcing in averaged oscillator equations

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude-phase equations for a weakly perturbed oscillator](amplitude-phase-equations-for-a-weakly-perturbed-oscillator.md)

For a weak force depending only on [position](position.md), the amplitude average is a periodic total [derivative](derivative.md) and vanishes. The leading harmonic amplitude is constant, while the phase may drift. The [method of multiple scales](method-of-multiple-scales.md) agrees with the exact [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) for bounded orbits in the effective potential. A cubic [position](position.md) force on the right side of the oscillator equation lowers the [frequency](frequency.md) at first order when its coefficient is positive.
