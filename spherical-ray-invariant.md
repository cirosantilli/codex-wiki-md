# Spherical ray invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics-split.md)

For a [light ray](light-ray.md) in a spherically stratified [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n(r)$, $p=n(r)r\sin\psi$ is constant, where $\psi$ is the angle to the radius. Approximate the medium by concentric homogeneous shells. Straight propagation within each shell conserves $r\sin\psi$, the line's distance from the centre; [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) at each shell boundary conserves $nr\sin\psi$. Passing to the smooth limit proves the result. In an exterior region with $n=1$, $p$ is the distance of the asymptotic straight [light ray](light-ray.md) from the centre. Consequently a surface-tangent [light ray](light-ray.md) at radius $R$ and [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n_s$ has vacuum asymptote at altitude $(n_s-1)R$, provided the [light ray](light-ray.md) reaches the exterior without trapping.
