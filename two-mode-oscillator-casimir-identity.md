# Two-mode oscillator Casimir identity

↑ **Parent:** [Schwinger boson representation](schwinger-boson-representation.md)

For the [two-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator](two-dimensional-isotropic-harmonic-oscillator.md), $H=\hbar\omega(N+1)$ and the [Schwinger boson representation](schwinger-boson-representation.md) gives $J^2=\hbar^2N(N+2)/4$. Substitution yields the displayed relation, including its quantum constant term. The fixed-total-occupation sector is a spin-$j=N/2$ representation, so [energy](energy-split.md) is $\hbar\omega(2j+1)$ with degeneracy $2j+1$.
