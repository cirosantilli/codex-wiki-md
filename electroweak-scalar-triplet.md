# Electroweak scalar triplet

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs field](higgs-field.md)

An electroweak scalar triplet transforms in the three-dimensional adjoint representation of $SU(2)_L$. A complex triplet of [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) one has components of electric charge two, one and zero, and its neutral component can couple to two left-handed lepton doublets.
