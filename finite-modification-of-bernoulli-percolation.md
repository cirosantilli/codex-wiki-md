# Finite modification of Bernoulli percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-energy property of Bernoulli percolation](finite-energy-property-of-bernoulli-percolation.md)

For $0<p<1$, every prescribed state pattern on a finite [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) [set](set-split.md) has positive conditional [probability](probability.md) given all exterior states: [independence](independent-random-variables.md) makes it $p^a(1-p)^b$ for $a$ prescribed open and $b$ prescribed closed [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). Thus a positive-probability exterior event remains possible after any finite local surgery. This is the [finite-energy property of Bernoulli percolation](finite-energy-property-of-bernoulli-percolation.md), and applies equally to [dual bond percolation](dual-bond-percolation.md).
