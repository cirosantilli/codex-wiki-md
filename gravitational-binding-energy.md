# Gravitational binding energy

↑ **Parent:** [Binding energy](binding-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_binding_energy)

In [Newtonian gravity](gravitational-acceleration.md), assembling a spherical mass distribution from shells gives the gravitational [potential energy](potential-energy.md) in the title formula, with zero at infinite separation. Its magnitude measures the gravitational contribution to binding. The full energy accounting of a hot self-gravitating body also includes its [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md); the [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) relates the two in equilibrium. A uniform-density [sphere](sphere.md) has $\Omega=-3GM^2/(5R)$.
