# Pro-p ring

↑ **Parent:** [Topological ring](topological-ring.md)

A pro-$p$ ring is a topological ring with continuous ring operations whose underlying additive [group](group-split.md) is a [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md). Standard local analytic examples are complete Noetherian local rings with finite [residue field](residue-field.md) of characteristic $p$, such as $\mathbb Z_p$ and $\mathbb F_p[[t]]$. The [topology](topology-split.md) is essential to the convergence of [power series](power-series.md) used for analytic [group](group-split.md) operations.
