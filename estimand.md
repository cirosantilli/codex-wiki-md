# Estimand

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estimand)

An estimand is the population quantity an analysis seeks to learn, as distinct from an [estimator](estimator.md) calculated from a sample. In a [randomized controlled trial](randomized-controlled-trial.md), the [causal effect](causal-effect.md) of treatment assignment and the effect of actual treatment receipt are different estimands when adherence is imperfect.
