# Pattern matching

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language](programming-language-split.md)

Selecting a branch and binding components according to a value's constructor structure. In [Haskell](haskell.md), matching a constructor can demand the outer constructor without demanding every field; a failed or undefined match has distinct consequences from an [irrefutable pattern](irrefutable-pattern.md).
