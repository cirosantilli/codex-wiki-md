# Adjoint-orbit criterion for maximal-torus conjugacy

↑ **Parent:** [Maximal torus](maximal-torus.md)

Give the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of a [compact Lie group](compact-lie-group.md) an [inner product](inner-product.md) invariant under the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). For a [maximal torus](maximal-torus.md) $T$ with Lie algebra $\mathfrak t$, its Lie-algebra centralizer is $\mathfrak t$: any additional commuting vector would generate, together with $T$, a larger compact connected abelian subgroup. The torus weight decomposition therefore supplies $H\in\mathfrak t$ whose centralizer is exactly $\mathfrak t$, by avoiding finitely many nonzero-weight hyperplanes. For any $X$, maximize $\langle\operatorname{Ad}_gX,H\rangle$ on the group. Its directional derivative gives $\langle Z,[\operatorname{Ad}_gX,H]\rangle=0$ for every $Z$, so $\operatorname{Ad}_gX\in\mathfrak t$. For a connected compact group, the [bi-invariant Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-riemannian-metric.md) and [Hopf-Rinow theorem](hopf-rinow-theorem.md) make the exponential onto; exponentiating this conjugacy puts every element in a conjugate of $T$.
