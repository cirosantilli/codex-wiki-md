# Linear thermal expansion coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md)

The linear thermal expansion coefficient is the fractional change in a specified [length](length.md) per change in [temperature](temperature.md), at fixed external [pressure](pressure.md) for a sample free to expand. For small changes, $\Delta L/L\simeq\alpha_L\Delta T$. An isotropic sample with the same response in all directions has volumetric [coefficient of thermal expansion](coefficient-of-thermal-expansion.md) $\alpha=3\alpha_L$, since differentiating $V\propto L^3$ gives $dV/V=3\,dL/L$. Direction-dependent materials require a specified measurement direction. [OpenStax University Physics 2, §1.3: linear and volumetric expansion](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/1-3-thermal-expansion).
