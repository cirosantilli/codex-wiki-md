# Instantaneous interface kinetics

↑ **Parent:** [Solidification attachment mobility](solidification-attachment-mobility.md)

Instantaneous interface kinetics is the limit of negligible kinetic undercooling at a moving [solidification](freezing.md) interface. Its [temperature](temperature.md) is set by local [liquidus](liquidus.md) equilibrium and [curvature-induced melting-temperature depression](curvature-induced-melting-temperature-depression.md), without a further velocity-dependent attachment term. It does not imply that [diffusion](diffusion.md) or heat transport is instantaneous.
