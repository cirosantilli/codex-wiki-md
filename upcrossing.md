# Upcrossing

↑ **Parent:** [Doob upcrossing inequality](doob-upcrossing-inequality.md)

An [upcrossing](upcrossing.md) of an interval $[a,b]$ consists of an observation at or below $a$ followed by a later observation at or above $b$. Successive completed [upcrossings](upcrossing.md) use disjoint buy-sell pairs.
