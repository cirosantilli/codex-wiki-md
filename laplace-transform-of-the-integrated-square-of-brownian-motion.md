# Laplace transform of the integrated square of Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Integrated square of Brownian motion](integrated-square-of-brownian-motion.md)

The independent squared-normal series for the [integrated square of Brownian motion](integrated-square-of-brownian-motion.md) gives the product $\prod_n(1+2\lambda/((n-1/2)^2\pi^2))^{-1/2}$. The half-integer cosine product reduces this to $(\cosh\sqrt{2\lambda})^{-1/2}$. Expanding at zero gives mean $1/2$ and variance $1/3$.
