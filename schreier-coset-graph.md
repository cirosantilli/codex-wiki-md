# Schreier coset graph

↑ **Parent:** [Covering graph](covering-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schreier_coset_graph)

For $H\leq G$ and a generating set $S$, the Schreier coset graph has vertices $Hg$ and a directed $s$-edge from $Hg$ to $Hgs$ for every $s\in S$. For a free group, this is the covering graph associated with $H$.
