<h1 id="knott-smith-optimality-criterion">Knott–Smith optimality criterion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal transport](optimal-transport.md)

For [probability measures](probability-measure.md) on $\mathbb R^d$ with finite second [moments](moment.md), a [transport plan](transport-plan.md) minimizes the quadratic cost exactly when it is concentrated on the graph of the [subdifferential](subdifferential.md) of a [sequentially lower semicontinuous](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md) [proper convex function](proper-convex-function.md).
