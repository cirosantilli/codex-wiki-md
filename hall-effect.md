# Hall effect

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hall_effect)

The [Hall effect](hall-effect.md) is a transverse [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) produced when an [electric current](electric-current.md) flows through a material in a transverse [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). The contact orientation fixes its measured sign. The [Drude conductivity and Hall experiment](drude-conductivity-and-hall-experiment.md) gives an explicit convention and the limitations of inferring a [number density](number-density.md) from it.
