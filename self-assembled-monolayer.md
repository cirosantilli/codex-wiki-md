# Self-assembled monolayer

↑ **Parent:** [Self-assembly](self-assembly.md)

A molecular layer formed through [adsorption](adsorption.md) and lateral organization at a substrate. Anchoring groups, chain packing and exposed terminal chemistry jointly determine its properties. Hydroxyl-terminated alkanethiol films on [gold](gold.md) provide a nonbiological example with controllable [water](water.md) wetting.
