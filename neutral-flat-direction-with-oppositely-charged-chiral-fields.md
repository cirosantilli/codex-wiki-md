# Neutral flat direction with oppositely charged chiral fields

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum moduli space of a supersymmetric gauge theory](vacuum-moduli-space-of-a-supersymmetric-gauge-theory.md)

For canonical [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md), nonzero $\lambda$, a gauged $U(1)$ with charges $(0,+1,-1)$, and no [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](fayet-iliopoulos-term.md), [F-flatness](f-flatness.md) requires the charged product to vanish and [D-flatness](d-flatness.md) requires equal charged magnitudes. Together they force both charged scalar values to zero, leaving the neutral scalar arbitrary. The [vacuum moduli space of a supersymmetric gauge theory](vacuum-moduli-space-of-a-supersymmetric-gauge-theory.md) has zero energy, unbroken gauge symmetry and unbroken [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md). At $\lambda=0$, equal nonzero charged magnitudes instead permit a gauge-Higgsed supersymmetric branch. A neutral modulus alone does not imply internal gauge breaking.
