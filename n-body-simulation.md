# N-body simulation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

An [N-body simulation](n-body-simulation.md) approximates a dynamical system by evolving $N$ interacting particles. In a collisionless [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) calculation these particles sample a smooth [mass](mass.md) distribution rather than represent individual physical [stars](star.md). Their larger individual [masses](mass.md) enhance artificial [two-body relaxation](two-body-relaxation.md); softening, [force](force.md) accuracy and convergence with particle number must be checked over the intended duration.
