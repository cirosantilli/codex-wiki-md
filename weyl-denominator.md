# Weyl denominator

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl denominator formula](weyl-denominator-formula.md)

The Weyl denominator is the alternating expression in the [Weyl denominator formula](weyl-denominator-formula.md). Its product form uses the [Weyl vector](half-sum-of-positive-roots.md) $\rho$, half the positive-root sum. On a compact [maximal torus](maximal-torus.md), the factor $e^\rho$ has unit modulus; if it is only defined on a covering torus, the squared modulus still descends. Thus $|\Delta(t)|^2=\prod_{\alpha>0}|1-\alpha(t)|^2$ is a globally defined density in the [Weyl integration formula](weyl-integration-formula.md). For the [unitary group](unitary-group.md) an integer central shift gives the [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md) $\det(z_i^{n-j})$, whose modulus is the same.
