<h1 id="sersic-profile">Sérsic profile</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Surface brightness](surface-brightness.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sérsic_profile)

A [Sérsic profile](sersic-profile.md) models projected galactic light, with index $n$ controlling curvature and $b_n$ chosen so that $R_e$ encloses half the [luminosity](luminosity.md). Its logarithmic slope is $d\ln I/d\ln R=-(b_n/n)(R/R_e)^{1/n}$. The $n=1$ case is exponential; $n=4$ gives the traditional de Vaucouleurs form. Unlike a fixed power law, its slope steepens outward.
