# Bias expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory of large-scale structure](effective-field-theory-of-large-scale-structure.md)

A bias expansion expresses a tracer density, such as a galaxy or halo density, as a renormalized sum of local and nonlocal operators built from matter density, tidal fields, derivatives, and stochastic fields. Its coefficients encode unresolved tracer formation physics.
