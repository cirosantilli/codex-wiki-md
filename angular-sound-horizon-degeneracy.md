# Angular sound-horizon degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Physical cosmology](physical-cosmology.md)

The angular [sound horizon](sound-horizon.md) compares two lengths using the same distance convention. If $D_A$ is the [angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md) to last scattering at [redshift](redshift.md) $z_*$, define the transverse [comoving distance](comoving-radial-distance.md) $D_M=(1+z_*)D_A$. A comoving [sound horizon](sound-horizon.md) $r_s$ has proper length $r_s/(1+z_*)$ at emission, so its angular size is $\theta_*=r_s/D_M$. Mixing a comoving ruler with $D_A$ would introduce an erroneous factor $1+z_*$. In a curved [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), $D_M$ is generally different from the radial [comoving distance](comoving-radial-distance.md).

For a stipulated example, $r_s=144\,\mathrm{Mpc}$ and $D_M=14000\,\mathrm{Mpc}$ give $\theta_*=0.010286$ radians, or $0.589^\circ$, and an acoustic scale $\ell_A=305.4$. Multiplying both lengths by $1.1$ gives $158.4\,\mathrm{Mpc}$ and $15400\,\mathrm{Mpc}$ with exactly the same $\theta_*$ and $\ell_A$. This example establishes a ratio degeneracy, without claiming that any arbitrary rescaling corresponds to an allowed cosmological model. Equivalently,

$$
\delta\ln\theta_*=\delta\ln r_s-\delta\ln D_M.
$$

Other observables are needed to separate changes that preserve the ratio.

The approximate [adiabatic acoustic-peak spacing](adiabatic-acoustic-peak-spacing.md) is $\ell_A$. The first observed temperature peak is shifted by gravitational driving and [angular projection of density and Doppler acoustic sources](angular-projection-of-density-and-doppler-acoustic-sources.md); it need not occur at $\ell_A$. In particular, neither the illustrative $305.4$ nor the [BOOMERANG acoustic-peak measurement](boomerang-acoustic-peak-measurement.md) of $197\pm6$ should be substituted for the other.

The [cosmological baryon density](cosmological-baryon-density.md) enters through the [baryon loading parameter](baryon-loading-parameter.md) and [cosmological recombination](recombination-cosmology.md), changing relative acoustic amplitudes as well as the [sound horizon](sound-horizon.md). The [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) counts net [baryon number](baryon-number.md) per [photon](photon.md), whereas $\theta_*$ is an angle. Constraining [cosmological baryon density](cosmological-baryon-density.md) requires the pattern of relative peak heights within a model, with other parameters fitted jointly. [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) provides a separate abundance test. [Baryon-density consistency between nucleosynthesis and the microwave background](baryon-density-consistency-between-nucleosynthesis-and-the-microwave-background.md) is consequently a test of one common cosmic history, rather than two direct measurements of the same angle. A first peak by itself cannot determine every component of the [cosmological density parameter](cosmological-density-parameter.md) or uniquely fix [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md).
