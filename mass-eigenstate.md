# Mass eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Mass matrix](mass-matrix.md)

A [mass eigenstate](mass-eigenstate.md) diagonalizes the Hermitian [mass](mass.md) operator or [mass matrix](mass-matrix.md) and has a definite [mass](mass.md). In a flavor basis with off-diagonal [mass](mass.md) terms, it is a linear combination of flavor states. For unstable particles, decay propagation instead involves an effective complex matrix; its [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) need not inherit the orthogonality of a Hermitian [mass](mass.md) problem.
