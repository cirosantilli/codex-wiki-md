# Orbital-frame rotation from inclination and node

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md)

In a right-handed reference frame, the [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) from an [orbital plane](orbital-plane.md) frame whose first axis points along the [ascending node](ascending-node.md) is $R_z(\Omega)R_x(I)$. Its columns are the orbital frame's [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) expressed in reference coordinates. The [unit vector](unit-vector.md) normal is $(\sin\Omega\sin I,-\cos\Omega\sin I,\cos I)$; its [dot product](dot-product.md) with another such normal gives the cosine of their [mutual inclination](mutual-inclination.md).
