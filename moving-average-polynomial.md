# Moving-average polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-average model](moving-average-model.md)

The [moving-average polynomial](moving-average-polynomial.md) records the coefficients of the finite noise filter. Evaluating it at the [backshift operator](backshift-operator.md) gives $\Theta(B)w_t=w_t+\sum_{j=1}^q b_jw_{t-j}$. In a reduced [ARMA](autoregressive-moving-average-model.md) representation, invertibility is equivalent to having no roots in the closed unit disc.
