# Electric dipole field

↑ **Parent:** [Electric dipole](electric-dipole.md)

At distances large compared with the separation of a neutral charge pair, its [electric field](electric-field-split.md) has the displayed leading form in terms of the [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) $\mathbf p$. Measure the polar angle from $\mathbf p$. The radial and angular components are $E_r=2p\cos\theta/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^3)$ and $E_\theta=p\sin\theta/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^3)$, giving $|\mathbf E|=p\sqrt{1+3\cos^2\theta}/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^3)$. At fixed radius the polar field magnitude is twice the equatorial one. This leading neutral-pair field falls as $r^{-3}$, faster than the $r^{-2}$ [electric field](electric-field-split.md) of an isolated [point charge](point-charge.md).
