# Pressure anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md)

Directional dependence of particle [pressure](pressure-split.md). In a gyrotropic [plasma](plasma-physics.md), only [parallel plasma pressure](parallel-plasma-pressure.md) and [perpendicular plasma pressure](perpendicular-plasma-pressure.md) are independent. Their difference changes the effective [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) and can drive [firehose instability](firehose-instability.md) or [mirror instability](mirror-instability.md).
