# Fission barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md)

The increase in a nucleus's collective deformation [potential energy](potential-energy.md) between a local minimum and a saddle on a path toward separated fragments. A positive final-state [Q value](q-value.md) does not remove this intermediate barrier. A nucleus can cross it after sufficient [excitation energy](excitation-energy.md), or undergo [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md). The relevant barrier for neutron-induced fission belongs to the compound nucleus formed after capture, not automatically to the target before capture.
