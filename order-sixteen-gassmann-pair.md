# Order-sixteen Gassmann pair

↑ **Parent:** [Gassmann equivalence](gassmann-equivalence.md)

Embed $C_4\times C_4$ and $Q_8\times C_2$ in $S_{16}$ by their [regular representations](regular-representation.md). Both have one element of order one, three of order two, and twelve of order four. An order-$r$ element in a regular action has cycle type $r^{16/r}$. Their intersections with every [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) of $S_{16}$ consequently have the same size, so they are [Gassmann equivalent](gassmann-equivalence.md). The first group is abelian and the second is not, so their underlying groups are not isomorphic. Finite-cover constructions from the [Sunada theorem](sunada-theorem.md) therefore give [isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md) with different [fundamental groups](fundamental-group.md).
