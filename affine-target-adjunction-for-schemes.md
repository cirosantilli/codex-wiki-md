# Affine-target adjunction for schemes

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md)

A [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) into an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md) is uniquely determined by its homomorphism on [global sections](global-section.md). Conversely, a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $A\to\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$ restricts on every [affine open subscheme](affine-open-subscheme.md) of $X$ to a map into $\operatorname{Spec}A$; equality on affine covers of overlaps glues these maps. No quasi-compactness or affineness assumption on $X$ is needed.
