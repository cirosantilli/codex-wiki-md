**Right-hemisphere activity after left-hemisphere damage can be compensatory, inefficiently helpful, maladaptive or incidental. Its role depends on the region, task, residual left network and recovery stage; a group activation map cannot decide among those possibilities.** [Functional compensation](../../../functional-compensation.md) means that the recruitment supports performance in the damaged system. It need not restore normal performance or reproduce the computation formerly performed on the left.

Longitudinal evidence immediately challenges a uniform interpretation. [Dorothee Saur](../../../dorothee-saur.md) and colleagues repeatedly imaged auditory comprehension in 14 patients after left middle-cerebral-artery [stroke](../../../stroke.md). Initially weak activation was followed by increased bilateral activity with a prominent right inferior-frontal homologue response, and later by a shift towards left [language](../../../language.md) areas alongside further recovery. The trajectory makes time after [stroke](../../../stroke.md) central to interpretation. [Correlation](../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) between activation and improvement is compatible with a contribution to recovery, but can also reflect restored capacity to engage with the task.[Brain 129, 1371–1384; 14 patients, auditory comprehension and fMRI at three recovery stages](../../../dynamics-of-language-reorganization-after-stroke-by-dorothee-saur-and-others.md)

A decrease in left dominance is particularly ambiguous. The [laterality index](../../../laterality-index.md) $(L-R)/(L+R)$ can fall because left activity falls, without right activity increasing. Acute [ischemia](../../../ischemia.md), [diaschisis](../../../diaschisis.md) and altered [neurovascular coupling](../../../neurovascular-coupling.md) also change the [fMRI](../../../functional-magnetic-resonance-imaging.md) signal. Matching the task instruction is insufficient if one patient comprehends the sentences and another only attempts them. Accuracy, response time and an independent physiological assessment should therefore accompany the activation contrast.

A maladaptive account proposes that increased activity in a right homologue interferes with useful remaining left function or promotes an inefficient strategy. [Interhemispheric inhibition](../../../interhemispheric-inhibition.md) is one candidate mechanism, but cannot be assumed from the fact that the two [cerebral hemispheres](../../../cerebral-hemisphere.md) change differently. Greater right activation among more severely impaired patients could mean that right activity causes impairment; equally, larger left lesions may both worsen performance and make right support more necessary. Statistical adjustment must account for lesion location and disconnection, not simply lesion volume.

Perturbation supplies a more direct test. [Lutz Winhuisen](../../../lutz-winhuisen.md) and colleagues used [PET](../../../photoinduced-electron-transfer.md) to select inferior-frontal targets and then disrupted their activity during a semantic task in 11 patients examined within two weeks of [stroke](../../../stroke.md). Right-sided stimulation impaired performance in a subset of patients with right IFG activation. This supports a necessary contribution to residual performance in those individuals, even though their [language](../../../language.md) remained less effective than in patients relying more on recovered left function. The result is not evidence that every activated right region is beneficial.[Stroke 36, 1759–1763; 11 patients within two weeks, PET-guided left/right IFG perturbation](../../../role-of-the-contralateral-inferior-frontal-gyrus-in-recovery-of-language-function-in-poststroke-aphasia-by-lutz-winhuisen-and-others.md)

Evidence sometimes presented for maladaptation also needs qualification. [Margaret A. Naeser](../../../margaret-a-naeser.md)'s open study applied repeated right inferior-frontal stimulation to four people with chronic [aphasia](../../../aphasia.md) and reported later picture-naming improvement. Suppression-associated improvement is compatible with a counterproductive component of right activity. Without a randomised sham comparator, however, practice, expectancy and nonspecific intervention effects remain possible; the result is preliminary, not a general treatment rule. An anterior target in chronic [aphasia](../../../aphasia.md) also need not have the same function as a different inferior-frontal site early after [stroke](../../../stroke.md).[Brain and Language 93, 95–105; four patients 5–11 years poststroke, open protocol and naming follow-up](../../../improved-picture-naming-in-chronic-aphasia-after-tms-to-part-of-right-broca-s-area-by-margaret-a-naeser-and-others.md)

A proposed mechanistic study would recruit a sufficiently characterised [stroke](../../../stroke.md) cohort across prespecified stages, quantify left cortical and tract damage, and measure baseline naming, comprehension and connected [speech](../../../speech.md). Use individual structural imaging and independent functional localisation to identify a right language-related target and a control target. Because an activated voxel can contain mixed functions, site selection should use a relevant contrast, not just the maximum response to any difficult task.

Within each participant, counterbalance a transient perturbation condition with credible sham and an [active control](../../../active-control.md), use masked assessment, parallel stimulus sets and a prespecified primary [language](../../../language.md) outcome. Include nonlinguistic reaction and motor tasks to detect general distraction or response slowing. Session spacing should address carryover, and the analysis should model repeated measurements and interactions with stage and residual left-network integrity. Safety screening and consent are integral to a clinical stimulation study; a [sample size](../../../sample-size.md) should be justified from variability and the smallest informative effect, not invented from the exam prompt.

If perturbing the right target worsens [language](../../../language.md) selectively relative to both controls, that supports a currently compensatory role. If it improves [language](../../../language.md) selectively and reproducibly, that supports a maladaptive contribution under those conditions. No behavioural change is inconclusive unless the perturbation was effective and the outcome sensitive. The intervention could spread through the network, so neither sign of effect automatically identifies a local code or proves inhibition of the left hemisphere.

Finally, a treatment trial asks a different question from transient necessity. Randomise a longer intervention against sham with matched speech-language therapy, assess untreated items and everyday communication, and follow durability. Repeated imaging can test whether behavioural improvement accompanies a predicted network change, while avoiding circular selection of patients or outcomes on the same data. **The evidence favours conditional, stage- and site-specific right-hemisphere contributions; perturbation with appropriate controls, followed by a separate clinical trial, can distinguish useful [functional compensation](../../../functional-compensation.md) from harmful recruitment more convincingly than activation alone.**
