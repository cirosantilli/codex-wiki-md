**[Basal ganglia](../../../basal-ganglia.md) abnormalities plausibly contribute to some developmental [speech](../../../speech.md) and [language](../../../language.md) disorders, especially through motor sequencing and [learning](../../../learning.md), but the evidence does not establish a single basal-ganglia cause across diagnoses.** A causal account must specify the disorder, the affected computation and how an alteration in a circuit produces that impairment. [Childhood apraxia of speech](../../../childhood-apraxia-of-speech.md), [developmental language disorder](../../../developmental-language-disorder.md) and [stuttering](../../../stuttering.md) are different [phenotypes](../../../phenotype.md); difficulty with [speech](../../../speech.md) output does not by itself identify a grammatical deficit.

The [basal ganglia](../../../basal-ganglia.md) participate in loops with [cerebral cortex](../../../cerebral-cortex.md) and [thalamus](../../../thalamus.md). Such circuits could support selecting, initiating and [learning](../../../learning.md) sequences, including the rapid articulatory transitions needed for [speech](../../../speech.md). A plausible mechanism is impaired acquisition or reliable initiation of a learned motor sequence, rather than storage of every word or syntactic rule in the [striatum](../../../striatum.md). This gives testable predictions about skill [learning](../../../learning.md), [nonword repetition](../../../nonword-repetition.md) and sensitivity to external timing cues. General plausibility, however, does not establish which circuit is responsible in an individual child.

The KE family supplied a particularly informative genetic case. [Cecilia S. L. Lai](../../../cecilia-s-l-lai.md) and colleagues identified an [FOXP2](../../../foxp2.md) alteration segregating with severe [speech](../../../speech.md) and [language](../../../language.md) impairment, together with an independent case in which a chromosome translocation disrupted the same [gene](../../../gene.md). The [phenotype](../../../phenotype.md) included major problems in coordinated orofacial sequencing alongside broader [language](../../../language.md) difficulties. This strengthens a causal connection between the [gene](../../../gene.md) and development of the [phenotype](../../../phenotype.md). It does not isolate the anatomical route: a [transcription factor](../../../transcription-factor.md) can alter several developing systems, and the family is not a representative sample of every [developmental language disorder](../../../developmental-language-disorder.md).[Nature 413, 519–523; KE-family cosegregation and independent chromosome-translocation case](../../../a-forkhead-domain-gene-is-mutated-in-a-severe-speech-and-language-disorder-by-cecilia-s-l-lai-and-others.md)

[Kate Watkins](../../../kate-watkins.md)'s [MRI](../../../magnetorotational-instability.md) study compared affected family members, unaffected relatives and controls. Voxel-based morphometry identified abnormalities in multiple motor- and speech-related regions; targeted volumetry confirmed reduced [caudate](../../../caudate-nucleus.md) volume bilaterally. Relationships with [oral praxis](../../../oral-praxis.md) and [nonword repetition](../../../nonword-repetition.md) made the [caudate](../../../caudate-nucleus.md) result functionally relevant. The unaffected relatives provide a valuable comparison, but an anatomical [correlation](../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) cannot establish that [caudate](../../../caudate-nucleus.md) reduction alone mediates the [gene](../../../gene.md)'s effects. Multiple abnormalities and correlated behavioural measures make that inference underdetermined.[Brain 125, 465–478; affected/unaffected KE-family members, matched controls, VBM and caudate volumetry](../../../mri-analysis-of-an-inherited-speech-and-language-disorder-structural-brain-abnormalities-by-kate-watkins-and-others.md)

Animal experiments move beyond an association between a human scan and a symptom. [Matthias Groszer](../../../matthias-groszer.md) and colleagues studied mice with a corresponding alteration of the [FOXP2](../../../foxp2.md) orthologue. Heterozygotes had relatively preserved baseline motor abilities but impaired motor-skill [learning](../../../learning.md), with abnormal plasticity in striatal and cerebellar circuits. The distinction between baseline performance and improvement with practice supports a learning-related mechanism. Yet a mouse motor task is not human [syntax](../../../syntax.md) or learned [speech](../../../speech.md), and the cerebellar findings themselves argue against an exclusively basal-ganglia interpretation.[Current Biology 18, 354–362; heterozygote motor-skill learning and striatal/cerebellar plasticity](../../../impaired-synaptic-plasticity-and-motor-learning-in-mice-with-a-point-mutation-implicated-in-human-speech-deficits-by-matthias-groszer-and-others.md)

[Stuttering](../../../stuttering.md) offers a partially independent line of evidence. [Soo-Eun Chang](../../../soo-eun-chang.md)'s study combined resting-state [fMRI](../../../functional-magnetic-resonance-imaging.md) and probabilistic [tractography](../../../tractography.md) in children aged 3–9. Differences involved basal-ganglia–thalamocortical and auditory–motor networks, including weaker connectivity between [putamen](../../../putamen.md) and supplementary motor regions. Studying young children reduces the extent to which decades of compensatory speaking strategies can explain the result. Nevertheless, the measurements were taken after symptoms began. Correlated activity at rest is not direct synaptic coupling, and [tractography](../../../tractography.md) does not demonstrate the direction or necessity of a physiological connection.[Brain 136, 3709–3726; children aged 3–9, resting-state fMRI and probabilistic tractography](../../../neural-network-connectivity-differences-in-children-who-stutter-by-soo-eun-chang-and-others.md)

Improved fluency with an external pace would be compatible with compensation for impaired internal timing, but it would not uniquely identify a basal-ganglia abnormality. External cues also change [attention](../../../attention.md), anticipation, auditory feedback and task structure. Similarly, a group difference in a procedural-learning task can result from poor motor execution, [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) or comprehension of instructions. The critical comparison separates [learning](../../../learning.md) rate from initial accuracy and uses nonverbal tasks, matched feedback and retained skill after a delay.

Several causal alternatives remain. A shared developmental influence may alter both [caudate](../../../caudate-nucleus.md) structure and cortical [language](../../../language.md) function. Reduced [language](../../../language.md) practice may itself change network measurements. A cortical or white-matter abnormality may produce downstream striatal differences. An experimentally supported gene-to-phenotype relationship does not automatically identify every intermediate step in a proposed gene-to-basal-ganglia-to-language chain.

A stronger programme would combine prospective measurements before or close to onset, repeated follow-up of persistence and recovery, detailed [speech](../../../speech.md) versus [language](../../../language.md) phenotyping, and independent motor-learning measures. Analyses should control age, [hearing](../../../hearing.md), motion, general motor ability and broader development, and assess multiple network regions. Selective perturbations in appropriate models can test mechanisms, while their behavioural scope must remain explicit. **The strongest claim is that distributed corticostriatal [learning](../../../learning.md) circuits are one important contributor in some [phenotypes](../../../phenotype.md); neither a universal basal-ganglia lesion model nor a language-specific function of [FOXP2](../../../foxp2.md) follows from the available evidence.**
