# Atmospheric biosignature gas

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet biosignature](exoplanet-biosignature.md)

An atmospheric gas or gas combination whose abundance and environmental context can support a biological interpretation. Detectability, a plausible production flux and exclusion of significant abiotic alternatives matter together. Modern-Earth [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) and [nitrous oxide](nitrous-oxide.md) have predominantly biological sources, but an [oxygen biosignature false positive](oxygen-biosignature-false-positive.md) illustrates why extrapolation to another planet requires context.
