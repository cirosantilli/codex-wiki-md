# Born-Oppenheimer approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born–Oppenheimer_approximation)

The [Born-Oppenheimer approximation](born-oppenheimer-approximation.md) separates electronic and nuclear motion. At leading order, fixed nuclear [positions](position.md) and [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) specify an electronic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) curve, on which the [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) move with their own [masses](mass.md).
