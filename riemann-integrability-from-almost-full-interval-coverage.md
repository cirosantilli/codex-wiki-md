# Riemann integrability from almost full interval coverage

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann integrability criterion](riemann-integrability-criterion.md)

Let $|f|\le M$ on $[0,1]$. Suppose finitely many nonoverlapping closed intervals, of total length at least $1-\delta$, can be chosen for each $\delta>0$, with $f$ [Riemann integrable](riemann-integrable-function.md) on each. Refine the intervals to make their total [Darboux sum](darboux-sum.md) gap less than $\varepsilon/2$. The remaining length contributes at most $2M\delta$. Taking $\delta<\varepsilon/(4M)$ proves global [Riemann integrability](riemann-integrable-function.md) by the [Riemann integrability criterion](riemann-integrability-criterion.md).
