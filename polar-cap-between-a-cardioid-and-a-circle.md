# Polar cap between a cardioid and a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Cardioid](cardioid.md)

For $a>0$ and $0<c<2a$, consider the region $c\leq r\leq a(1+\cos\phi)$ in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md). It exists precisely for $|\phi|\leq\beta$, where $\beta=\arccos(c/a-1)\in(0,\pi)$. The [polar area element](polar-area-element.md) $r\,dr\,d\phi$ gives

$$
\mathcal A=\frac12\int_{-\beta}^{\beta}\big[a^2(1+\cos\phi)^2-c^2\big]d\phi.
$$

Expanding the square and integrating its even terms gives the title formula. This cap lies inside the [cardioid](cardioid.md) and outside the concentric [circle](circle.md), so reversing the radial bounds would select the wrong region or a signed area.
