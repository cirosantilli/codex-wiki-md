# Translation-covariant phase expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Phase modulation](phase-modulation.md)

Let $p=\phi_Y$, $r=\phi_{YY}$ and $w_2=C_1(\xi)r+C_2(\xi)p^2$. Two applications of the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) give $\mathcal D_Y^2w_2=C_1\phi_{YYYY}+2(C_1'+C_2)p\phi_{YYY}+(C_1'+2C_2)r^2+(C_1''+5C_2')p^2r+C_2''p^4$. In a [Fredholm solvability condition](fredholm-solvability-condition.md), the fourth term generally contributes to the coefficient of $\phi_Y^2\phi_{YY}$. It cannot be omitted merely because the fast-coordinate correction functions have been denoted by $C_j(x)$.
