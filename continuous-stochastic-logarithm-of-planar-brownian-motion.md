# Continuous stochastic logarithm of planar Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithmic radius of planar Brownian motion](logarithmic-radius-of-planar-brownian-motion.md)

[Planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) started away from zero almost surely avoids zero. On every finite time interval its continuous path therefore has positive minimum modulus, making the logarithmic [stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md) well-defined. Since complex [quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md) satisfies $d[Z,Z]=0$, analytic Itô calculus gives the displayed exponential identity without a drift correction. Its [real part](real-part.md) is the change in logarithmic radius and its [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) is the continuous accumulated argument, including every winding. This is a pathwise logarithmic lift, not a single-valued [logarithm](logarithm.md) on the entire punctured plane.
