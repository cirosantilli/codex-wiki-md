A [teleconnection](../../../../../teleconnection.md) is correlated atmospheric variability between separated regions. A principal dynamical pathway is a [Rossby-wave teleconnection](../../../../../rossby-wave-teleconnection.md): a displaced air column conserves [potential vorticity](../../../../../potential-vorticity.md), so changing planetary vorticity must be balanced by changing relative [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md). The background potential-vorticity gradient supplies the restoring mechanism.

In a uniform zonal current $U$ on a [beta plane](../../../../../beta-plane.md), a simple [Barotropic Rossby wave](../../../../../barotropic-rossby-wave.md) has

$$
\boxed{\omega=Uk-\frac{\beta k}{k^2+l^2},\qquad\beta=\frac{df}{dy}.}
$$

The intrinsic phase propagates westward for $\beta>0$; advection can leave a nearly stationary pattern in the terrestrial frame. Energy and information travel with the [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md), not necessarily with individual crests or the same air parcels. A [jet stream](../../../../../jet-stream.md) can guide wave packets and link distant ridges and troughs.

For example, persistent anomalous tropical heating can excite wave trains that alter remote pressure, temperature and storm tracks. Tropical Pacific ocean-temperature anomalies can modify that heating, yielding reproducible extratropical patterns. Their phase and strength depend on the background winds, potential-vorticity gradients, damping, season and eddy feedbacks. **A correlation pattern is not by itself proof of a unique causal pathway**, and not every teleconnection is a single stationary linear wave.[9March2015: rotating-sphere response to localized equatorial heating and extratropical ENSO patterns](../../../../../teleconnections-and-stationary-rossby-waves-by-isaac-held.md)
