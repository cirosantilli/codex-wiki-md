↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [isentropic potential vorticity](../../../../../isentropic-potential-vorticity.md) is [Ertel potential vorticity](../../../../../ertel-potential-vorticity.md) expressed on constant [potential temperature](../../../../../potential-temperature.md) surfaces:

$$
\boxed{q=\frac{\boldsymbol\omega_a\cdot\nabla\theta}{\rho}=\frac{\zeta_\theta+f}{\sigma_\theta},\qquad\sigma_\theta=-\frac1g\frac{\partial p}{\partial\theta}>0.}
$$

Here $\zeta_\theta$ is the vertical curl of horizontal velocity differentiated on an isentropic surface, and $\sigma_\theta$ is mass per unit area per unit potential-temperature interval. For smooth inviscid adiabatic motion, **$Dq/Dt=0$**. Conserved anomalies are advected and deformed, explaining much balanced large-scale weather variability. Diagnosing velocity from PV additionally needs balance, stratification and boundary information. Strong latent/radiative heating, friction and irreversible mixing generate or modify PV, limiting a purely conservative explanation.[Potential-vorticity conservation, balance and Rossby-wave dynamics](../../../../../fundamentals-of-atmosphere-ocean-dynamics-by-michael-edgeworth-mcintyre.md)
