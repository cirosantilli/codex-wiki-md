# Cold minor-merger size growth

↑ **Parent:** [Minor galaxy merger](minor-galaxy-merger.md)

For repeated [minor galaxy mergers](minor-galaxy-merger.md) with negligible internal satellite binding energy and zero orbital energy at infinity, [parabolic dry-merger energy accounting](parabolic-dry-merger-energy-accounting.md) leaves total energy nearly constant. The [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) then gives [gravitational radius](gravitational-radius.md) $R_g\propto M^2$ and mean-square speed proportional to $M^{-1}$. Small satellite mass does not by itself imply negligible internal binding energy.
