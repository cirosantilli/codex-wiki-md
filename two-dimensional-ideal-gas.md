# Two-dimensional ideal gas

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md)

An [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) whose particles move in an [area](surface-area.md) $A$ with two independent translational momentum components. In its dilute classical limit the one-particle [translational partition function](translational-partition-function.md) is $q=A/\Lambda^2$, where $\Lambda$ is the [thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md), and $Z_N=q^N/N!$. The translational [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) is $Nk_{\rm B}T$, and the surface pressure conjugate to [area](surface-area.md) is $Nk_{\rm B}T/A$. A mobile dilute adsorbed gas is an example; a fully occupied array of fixed [adsorption sites](adsorption-site.md) has different state counting.
