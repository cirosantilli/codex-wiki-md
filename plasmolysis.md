# Plasmolysis

↑ **Parent:** [Cell wall](cell-wall.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasmolysis)

[Plasmolysis](plasmolysis.md) is withdrawal of the membrane-bounded contents from parts of a [cell wall](cell-wall.md) after [water](water.md) loss. A sufficiently concentrated external [hypertonic solution](hypertonic-solution.md), with [solutes](solute.md) effectively excluded by the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) over the observation time, can cause this. The [cell wall](cell-wall.md) remains; the enclosed material contracts. [Plasmolysis](plasmolysis.md) is therefore distinct from [osmotic lysis](cytolysis.md), which breaks the cellular boundary during excessive swelling. A return to a less concentrated surrounding [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) can reverse [plasmolysis](plasmolysis.md) if the [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) remains functional.[Biology 2e, §5.2, Tonicity in Living Systems and Figure 5.13: membrane withdrawal under hypertonic conditions](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/5-2-passive-transport.md)
