# Acid-base titration

↑ **Parent:** [Weak acid dissociation measurement](weak-acid-dissociation-measurement.md)

An [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md) adds measured quantities of a standardized [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md) or [chemical base](base-chemistry.md) to determine an initially unknown acid or base inventory through [neutralization](neutralization.md). Monitoring [pH](ph.md) also constrains [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) parameters such as the [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md).
