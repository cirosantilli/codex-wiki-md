# Acid-base titration

↑ **Parent:** [Weak acid dissociation measurement](weak-acid-dissociation-measurement.md)

An [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md) adds measured quantities of a standardized [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md) or [chemical base](base-chemistry.md) to determine an initially unknown acid or base inventory through [neutralization](neutralization.md). Monitoring [pH](ph.md) also constrains [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) parameters such as the [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md).

The added reagent is the [titrant](titrant.md); a [pipette](pipette.md) selects an [aliquot](aliquot.md) of the sample and a [burette](burette.md) measures the added [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). Known [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) converts this [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) into [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md). This separates two questions that a single [pH](ph.md) reading cannot answer: how much [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md) is present, and how readily it donates a [proton](proton.md). A large inventory of [weak acid](weak-acid.md) can consume more [titrant](titrant.md) than a small inventory of [strong acid](strong-acid.md) despite having a higher initial [pH](ph.md).
