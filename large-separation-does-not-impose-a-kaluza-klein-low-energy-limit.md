# Large separation does not impose a Kaluza-Klein low-energy limit

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein compactification scale](kaluza-klein-compactification-scale.md)

A pointlike pulse can contain frequencies above the compactification gap even when its travel time and distance greatly exceed the circle size. Its [retarded circle-compactified massless propagator](retarded-circle-compactified-massless-propagator.md) can resolve winding images and massive-mode oscillations. Suppressing those modes requires low-frequency or long-wavelength sources/measurements, or suitable smearing; mere coordinate separation is not that restriction. The [static Green function across a circle compactification](static-green-function-across-a-circle-compactification.md) gives a precise long-distance zero-mode limit without this ambiguity.
