# Bioaccumulation

↑ **Parent:** [Ecology](ecology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioaccumulation)

Increase of a substance within an organism when uptake from its surroundings and food exceeds elimination. This is distinct from [biomagnification](biomagnification.md) across a [food chain](food-chain.md).
