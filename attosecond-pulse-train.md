# Attosecond pulse train

↑ **Parent:** [Attosecond physics](attosecond-physics.md)

An [attosecond pulse train](attosecond-pulse-train.md) contains repeated short [optical pulses](optical-pulse.md). For a group of odd multiples of a common driving [frequency](frequency.md), the separation is half the driving period when the relative [spectral phases](spectral-phase.md) recur. The construction resembles [mode locking](mode-locking.md), but the participating components come from [high harmonic generation](high-harmonic-generation.md), rather than separate resonant modes of a [laser](laser-split.md) oscillator.
