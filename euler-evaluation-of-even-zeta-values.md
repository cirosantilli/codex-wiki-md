# Euler evaluation of even zeta values

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli number](bernoulli-number.md)

Expand the [cotangent partial-fraction expansion](cotangent-partial-fraction-expansion.md) at zero to obtain $\pi\cot(\pi z)=z^{-1}-2\sum_{r\geq1}\zeta(2r)z^{2r-1}$. Substituting $t=2\pi iz$ in the generating function of the [Bernoulli numbers](bernoulli-number.md) gives the same [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) as $z^{-1}+\sum_{r\geq1}B_{2r}(2\pi i)^{2r}z^{2r-1}/(2r)!$. Equality of [Laurent coefficients](laurent-coefficient.md) proves the formula.
