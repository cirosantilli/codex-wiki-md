# Logarithmic scoring rule

↑ **Parent:** [Strictly proper scoring rule](strictly-proper-scoring-rule.md)

Under true density $q$, the advantage of truthful reporting is the [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) $D_{\rm KL}(q\Vert p)$. Its nonnegativity and equality condition prove strict propriety when the expected log scores are well defined.
