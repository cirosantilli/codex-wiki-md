# Sudbury Neutrino Observatory

↑ **Parent:** [Kajita-McDonald neutrino flavor-conversion experiments](kajita-mcdonald-neutrino-flavor-conversion-experiments.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudbury_Neutrino_Observatory)

The SNO Collaboration used $1000$ tonnes of [heavy water](heavy-water.md) in a $12\,\mathrm m$ acrylic sphere, surrounded by ordinary [water](water.md) shielding and $9456$ [photomultipliers](photomultiplier.md). [Weak charged current](charged-current.md) (CC) breakup of a [deuteron](deuteron.md) selects [electron neutrinos](electron-neutrino.md); [weak neutral current](neutral-current.md) (NC) breakup measures all active [neutrino flavors](neutrino-flavor.md); [electron](electron.md) [elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md) (ES) has reduced non-electron sensitivity.[PDF p. 2: reactions and apparatus](measurement-of-the-rate-of-nu-e-plus-d-%E2%86%92-p-plus-p-plus-e-minus-interactions-produced-by-8b-solar-neutrinos-at-the-sudbury-neutrino-observatory-by-q-r-ahmad-and-others.md)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nu_e+d&\longrightarrow p+p+e^-&&\mathrm{CC},\\
\nu_\alpha+d&\longrightarrow p+n+\nu_\alpha&&\mathrm{NC},\\
\nu_\alpha+e^-&\longrightarrow\nu_\alpha+e^-&&\mathrm{ES},\qquad\alpha=e,\mu,\tau.
\end{aligned}
$$
