# Lowest-degree alternant proof of the Weyl dimension formula

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md)

For a [root system](root-system.md) with $N$ positive [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md), put $A_\nu(H)=\sum_{w\in W}\det(w)e^{(w\nu,H)}$. Its degree-$m$ Taylor coefficient is alternating separately in $\nu$ and $H$, and hence divisible in each set of variables by the product of positive-root linear forms. Thus it vanishes for $m<N$ and its degree-$N$ term is $C\prod_{\alpha>0}(\nu,\alpha)\prod_{\alpha>0}(H,\alpha)$. The [Weyl denominator formula](weyl-denominator-formula.md) at $\nu=\rho$ determines the nonzero constant $C$. Taking the ratio $A_{\lambda+\rho}(tH)/A_\rho(tH)$ for regular $H$ and letting $t\to0$ gives the [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) $\dim L(\lambda)=\prod_{\alpha>0}(\lambda+\rho,\alpha)/(\rho,\alpha)$. Factors converting roots to [coroots](coroot.md) cancel.
