# Ruminant

↑ **Parent:** [Artiodactyla](artiodactyla.md)

A member of [Ruminantia](ruminantia.md), characterized by a specialized [foregut fermentation](foregut-fermentation.md) system and [rumination](rumination.md). It is not a general name for every foregut-fermenting herbivore.
