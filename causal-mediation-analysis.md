# Causal mediation analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Causal inference](causal-inference-split.md)

Causal mediation analysis decomposes a [total causal effect](total-causal-effect.md) into effects transmitted through a designated mediator and effects operating through other pathways. Identification generally requires assumptions about exposure-outcome, exposure-mediator, and mediator-outcome confounding.
