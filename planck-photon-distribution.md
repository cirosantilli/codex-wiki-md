# Planck photon distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md)

For [photons](photon.md) in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) at [temperature](temperature.md) $T$, [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md) gives the photon [number density](number-density.md) per frequency interval. In terms of the [Planck function](planck-function.md), it is $dn/d\nu=4\pi B_\nu(T)/(ch\nu)$, hence

$$
\frac{dn}{d\nu}=\frac{8\pi\nu^2}{c^3}\frac1{e^{h\nu/(k_BT)}-1}.
$$
