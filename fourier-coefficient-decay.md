# Fourier coefficient decay

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md)

The rate at which [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) approach zero as their index increases. The differentiability of the [periodic extension](periodic-extension.md), including matching values and derivatives at its joins, controls this rate through [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). An absolutely bounded tail $O(n^{-p})$ with $p>1$ gives a uniform truncation error $O(N^{1-p})$.
