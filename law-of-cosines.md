# Law of cosines

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_cosines)

For a [triangle](triangle.md) with side lengths $a,b,c$ and angle $C$ opposite $c$, the law of cosines states

$$
c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos C.
$$
