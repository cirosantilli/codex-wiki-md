# Spectral reflectance

↑ **Parent:** [Reflectance](reflectance.md)

The ratio of reflected to incident [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) as a function of [wavelength](wavelength.md), under specified geometrical and illumination conditions. Unlike a perceived colour, [spectral reflectance](spectral-reflectance.md) describes a physical response; perceived colour also depends on the incident spectrum and the observer.
