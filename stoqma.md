# StoqMA

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum complexity theory](quantum-complexity-theory.md)

[StoqMA](stoqma.md) is a restricted quantum-verifier class using [stoquastic circuits](stoquastic-circuit.md) and polynomial-size [quantum witnesses](quantum-witness.md). A YES instance has a [quantum witness](quantum-witness.md) accepted with probability at least $a$; on a NO instance every [quantum witness](quantum-witness.md) has acceptance at most $b$, with an inverse-polynomial gap $a-b$. An optimal [quantum witness](quantum-witness.md) can be chosen with nonnegative amplitudes, since the [witness acceptance operator](witness-acceptance-operator.md) is entrywise nonnegative. The [quantum witness](quantum-witness.md) is generally a superposition, rather than a [computational basis](computational-basis.md) vector. The [stoquastic acceptance floor](stoquastic-acceptance-floor.md) explains the lower limit $b\geq1/2$ for a nontrivial soundness promise.
