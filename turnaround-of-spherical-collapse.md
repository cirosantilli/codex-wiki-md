# Turnaround of spherical collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical-collapse model](spherical-collapse-model.md)

A pressureless bound spherical shell first expands, reaches $\dot R=0$ at its maximum radius, and recollapses. In the cycloidal [spherical-collapse model](spherical-collapse-model.md), turnaround occurs at $\theta=\pi$, with $R_{\max}=2A$ and $t_{\max}=\pi B$. Its mean density is $9\pi^2/16$ times the background density in an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md).
