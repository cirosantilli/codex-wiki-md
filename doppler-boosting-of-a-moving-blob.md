# Doppler boosting of a moving blob

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic beaming](relativistic-beaming.md)

For an unresolved, isotropically emitting rest-frame blob with [power-law spectrum](power-law-spectrum.md) $F'_\nu\propto\nu^{-\alpha}$, the observed [spectral flux density](spectral-flux-density.md) at a fixed [frequency](frequency.md) is enhanced by $D^{3+\alpha}$. The factors follow from [Lorentz-invariant specific intensity](lorentz-invariant-specific-intensity.md) and the [frequency](frequency.md) shift. The bolometric or corresponding-logarithmic-bin [luminosity](luminosity.md) transforms with $D^4$. A spatially steady continuous jet has a different equal-arrival-time sampling and can have exponent $2+\alpha$ instead.
