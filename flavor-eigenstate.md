# Flavor eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state](quantum-state.md)

A [flavor eigenstate](flavor-eigenstate.md) is a state with a definite flavor label in the specified interaction or flavor-charge basis. For neutral [kaons](kaon.md), $K^0=d\bar s$ and $\bar K^0=s\bar d$ have definite opposite [strangeness](strangeness.md). [Weak interactions](weak-interaction.md) mix them, so these flavor states are not the [mass eigenstates](mass-eigenstate.md). The precise flavor basis must be specified when discussing mixing.
