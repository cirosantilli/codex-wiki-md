# Chemical cross-linking mass spectrometry

↑ **Parent:** [Mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md)

[chemical cross-linking mass spectrometry](chemical-cross-linking-mass-spectrometry.md) identifies residue pairs joined by a chemical reagent and uses them as spatial constraints on [protein structures](protein-structure.md) or assemblies. A distance limit depends on linker chemistry, reactive residue positions and accessible conformations.
