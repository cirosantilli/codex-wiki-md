# Trivial Lie algebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

The trivial representation sends every element of the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) to the zero linear map. For a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), the irreducible representation of [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) zero is the one-dimensional trivial representation.
