# Unshielded collider

↑ **Parent:** [Collider](collider.md)

An unshielded [collider](collider.md) in a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md) is a triple $a\to b\leftarrow c$ with $a$ and $c$ nonadjacent. The absence of an edge between its outer vertices makes this orientation detectable from [conditional independence](conditional-independence.md) information under [faithfulness of a directed acyclic graph](faithfulness-of-a-directed-acyclic-graph.md).
