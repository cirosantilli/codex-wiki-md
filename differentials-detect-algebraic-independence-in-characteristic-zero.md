# Differentials detect algebraic independence in characteristic zero

↑ **Parent:** [Separating transcendence basis](separating-transcendence-basis.md)

For a finitely generated [field extension](field-extension.md) $L/k$ in characteristic zero, a tuple $t_i$ is algebraically independent exactly when its differentials $dt_i$ are linearly independent in the [Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md). A relation of minimum total degree gives a nontrivial differential relation; an independent tuple extends to a [transcendence basis](transcendence-basis.md). In positive characteristic, derivatives of nonconstant relations can vanish identically.
