# Charmed baryon

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)

A [baryon](baryon.md) containing a [charm quark](charm-quark.md). For one charm and two up/down [quarks](quark.md) in the spatial ground state, [light-diquark spin-isospin correlation](light-diquark-spin-isospin-correlation.md) gives the isosinglet [Lambda c baryon](lambda-c-baryon.md) and the isotriplet [Sigma c baryon](sigma-c-baryon.md) families.
