# Lithium aluminium hydride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

$\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$, a strong [hydride](hydride.md) donor capable of reducing [ketones](ketone.md) to [alcohols](alcohol.md) and [amides](amide.md) to [amines](amine.md), with oxygen removal in the latter transformation.
