# Amino acid starvation

↑ **Parent:** [Translation (biology)](translation-biology.md)

Insufficient availability of one or more [amino acids](amino-acid.md) relative to cellular requirements. Its translational consequences include substrate limitation and regulated changes in initiation and stress adaptation.
