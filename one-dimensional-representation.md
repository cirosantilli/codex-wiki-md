# One-dimensional representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A one-dimensional complex [group representation](group-representation.md) acts on a one-dimensional complex [vector space](vector-space-split.md), and is equivalently a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) into the multiplicative [group](group-split.md) $\mathbb C^\times$. It is automatically an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md). Its [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) is its scalar value, a [one-dimensional character](one-dimensional-character.md). A change of [basis](basis.md) cannot change those scalar values, so two such [group representations](group-representation.md) are equivalent exactly when their homomorphisms agree. For a [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_n$ with $n\geq2$, all [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md) are conjugate, square to the identity and generate the group. Their common scalar image is therefore $1$ or $-1$, giving exactly the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) and the [sign representation](sign-representation.md).
