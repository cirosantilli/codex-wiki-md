# Schulze-Hardy rule

↑ **Parent:** [Critical coagulation concentration](critical-coagulation-concentration.md)

Strong sensitivity of a lyophobic [colloid](colloid.md)'s [critical coagulation concentration](critical-coagulation-concentration.md) to counterion valence. A high-surface-potential DLVO approximation gives $c_{\rm ccc}\propto z^{-6}$; specific adsorption and charge regulation can invalidate this simple scaling.
