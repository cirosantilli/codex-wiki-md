# Emotional arousal

↑ **Parent:** [Emotion](emotion.md)

The degree of activation associated with an emotional state. [Emotional arousal](emotional-arousal.md) is distinct from [emotional valence](emotional-valence.md): both positive and negative states can be highly activating.
