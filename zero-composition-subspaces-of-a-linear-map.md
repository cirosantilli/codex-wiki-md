# Zero-composition subspaces of a linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Linear map](linear-map.md)

For $\alpha:U\to V$, maps in $M^l(\alpha)$ factor uniquely through $V/\operatorname{im}\alpha$; maps in $M^r(\alpha)$ have [image](image-of-a-function.md) in $\ker\alpha$. Thus these [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) are naturally $L(V/\operatorname{im}\alpha,U)$ and $L(V,\ker\alpha)$, of dimensions $(\dim V-\operatorname{rank}\alpha)\dim U$ and $(\dim U-\operatorname{rank}\alpha)\dim V$. Passing to a [dual map](transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) reverses composition and interchanges these two spaces. This gives a basis-free route to equality of the ranks of a map and its dual.
