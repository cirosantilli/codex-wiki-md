# Symmetric gradient and curl decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian second-rank tensor](cartesian-second-rank-tensor.md)

For a differentiable three-dimensional [vector field](vector-field.md) $F$, split its gradient [tensor](tensor.md) into symmetric and antisymmetric parts. The symmetric part is $S_{ij}=(\partial_iF_j+\partial_jF_i)/2$. Contracting the antisymmetric part with the [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md) gives the [axial vector](pseudovector.md) $u_k=\epsilon_{kij}\partial_iF_j/2=(\nabla\times F)_k/2$, proving the decomposition. If $\nabla\cdot F=0$, the divergence of the symmetric part is $\partial_jS_{ij}=\Delta F_i/2$. This lets a vector [Laplacian](laplacian.md) be converted into a boundary integral of a symmetric [tensor](tensor.md) by the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md).
