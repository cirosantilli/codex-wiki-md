# Radial energy flow after switching a line current

↑ **Parent:** [Switched infinite-wire retarded potential](switched-infinite-wire-retarded-potential.md)

The induced [electric field](electric-field-split.md) opposes the increasing axial current, while the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) circles it. Their [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) points radially outward and transports energy into the growing magnetic-field region.
