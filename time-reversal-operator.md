# Time reversal operator

↑ **Parent:** [Time reversal acoustics](time-reversal-acoustics.md)

For a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $H$ mapping transmitted signals to recorded signals, the [positive operator](positive-operator.md) $H^*H$ describes adjoint back-propagation after recording. In a reciprocal array, physical [time reversal acoustics](time-reversal-acoustics.md) implements this operation up to a final [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) and the chosen signal conventions.
