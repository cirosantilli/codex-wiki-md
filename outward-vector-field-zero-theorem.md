# Outward vector field zero theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md)

A continuous [vector field](vector-field.md) on a closed Euclidean ball whose boundary values have nonnegative outward component has a zero in the ball. Otherwise $T(x)=-Rg(x)/|g(x)|$ is a continuous self-map of the ball. Its [fixed point](fixed-point.md), supplied by the [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md), lies on the boundary and satisfies $g(x)\cdot x=-R|g(x)|<0$, a contradiction. The argument turns a zero-finding problem into a [fixed point](fixed-point.md) problem without any differentiability assumption.
