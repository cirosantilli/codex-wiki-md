# Characteristic-three sign quotient of the Specht module of shape three two

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

For the symmetric group on five letters, $S^{(4,1)}$ is the four-dimensional augmentation representation. In characteristic three it is simple, since its only possible proper nonzero submodule would be the constant line, which is excluded by $5\ne0$ in the field. Thus it is $D^{(4,1)}$. If this module occurs as a submodule of $S^{(3,2)}$, the quotient has dimension one, since the standard polytabloid basis gives $\dim S^{(3,2)}=5$.

A transposition has trace one on $S^{(3,2)}$, by the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md), and trace two on $S^{(4,1)}$, by subtracting the trivial line from the five-point permutation representation. These integral traces reduce to the same elements in characteristic three. The quotient trace is therefore minus one, so its one-dimensional action is the [sign representation](sign-representation.md). In particular an arbitrary permutation acts on it by its sign. This identifies the quotient action without asserting that the original short exact sequence splits.
