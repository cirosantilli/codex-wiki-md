# Conformal map of a circular lens

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

Two intersecting [circles](circle.md) bound a lens. A [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) sending their intersection points to zero and infinity sends the two boundary arcs to rays, and the lens to a sector. A suitable power straightens the sector to a half-plane. The chosen sector and its branch must be identified by an interior test point; merely squaring an arbitrary boundary-straightening map need not be injective on the intended domain. A [Cayley transform](cayley-transform-complex-analysis.md) then gives a conformal bijection to a disc.
