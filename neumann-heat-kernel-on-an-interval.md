# Neumann heat kernel on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

For $L>0$ and $t>0$, the [Sturm-Liouville eigenfunction expansion](sturm-liouville-eigenfunction-expansion.md) for $u_t=u_{xx}$ on $(0,L)$ with homogeneous [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) gives

$$
K_N(x,y,t)=\frac1L+\frac2L\sum_{n=1}^\infty e^{-(n\pi/L)^2t}\cos\frac{n\pi x}{L}\cos\frac{n\pi y}{L}.
$$

The constant [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) mode preserves the spatial [integral](integral.md). This is the $\beta=0$ limit of the [weighted Neumann heat kernel with constant drift](weighted-neumann-heat-kernel-with-constant-drift.md), and its boundary inputs have the signs in the [Neumann boundary-forcing formula](neumann-boundary-forcing-formula.md).
