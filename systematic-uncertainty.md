# Systematic uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Systematic error](systematic-error.md)

Uncertainty associated with imperfect knowledge of [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md), efficiency, backgrounds or other persistent model inputs. Repeating the same measurement does not necessarily average it away. Correlated [nuisance parameters](nuisance-parameter.md) can propagate it through a [likelihood](likelihood-function.md); estimating such an uncertainty is distinct from asserting that the systematic error has a known sign and magnitude.
