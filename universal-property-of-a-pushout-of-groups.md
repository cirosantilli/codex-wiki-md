# Universal property of a pushout of groups

↑ **Parent:** [Pushout of groups](pushout-of-groups.md)

For [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) $\phi_j:G_0\to G_j$, a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from their [pushout of groups](pushout-of-groups.md) to $H$ is uniquely a pair $u_j:G_j\to H$ with $u_1\phi_1=u_2\phi_2$. Indeed extend the pair to the [free product](free-product.md) and factor through its [quotient group](quotient-group.md): the agreement kills the [normal closure](normal-closure.md) of all $\phi_1(g)\phi_2(g)^{-1}$. Uniqueness follows because the images of the two factors generate the [pushout of groups](pushout-of-groups.md).
