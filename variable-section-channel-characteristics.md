# Variable-section channel characteristics

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

For cross-sectional area $S(x,h)$ and cross-sectionally uniform velocity, [volume conservation](volume-conservation.md) gives $S_h(h_t+uh_x)+Su_x+uS_x=0$, while hydrostatic inviscid momentum gives $u_t+uu_x+gh_x=0$. Their [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) are $u\pm c$. With $D_\pm=\partial_t+(u\pm c)\partial_x$, the robust compatibility equation is $(g/c)D_\pm h\pm D_\pm u=-ucS_x/S$. Replacing $D_\pm h$ by $D_\pm c/c_h$ requires $c_x=0$ at fixed height; otherwise an explicit geometry term remains.
