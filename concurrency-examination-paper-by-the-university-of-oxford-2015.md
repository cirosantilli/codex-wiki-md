# Concurrency examination paper by the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The five-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) Finals examination dated 1 June 2015, 2:30 pm–4:00 pm, Trinity Term 2015. The cover permits answers to two of three questions. Public released archive: [Lawrence Attachment.zip](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `2015-concur.pdf`. Printed p. 2 covers [recursion](recursion-computer-science.md), a reversal network and semantic indistinguishability; p. 3 covers a [FIFO buffer](fifo-buffer.md) and a modified [CSP interrupt](csp-interrupt.md); pp. 4–5 concern [termination detection](termination-detection.md) on a [ring network](ring-network.md).
