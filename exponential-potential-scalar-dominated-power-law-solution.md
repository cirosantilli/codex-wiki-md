# Exponential-potential scalar-dominated power-law solution

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-field cosmology](scalar-field-cosmology.md)

For $V=V_0e^{-\lambda\varphi/M_{\rm Pl}}$, $\lambda\ne0$, the displayed ansatz satisfies the flat [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) and [scalar field equation of motion](scalar-field-equation-of-motion.md) when $V_0t_0^2=2M_{\rm Pl}^2(6-\lambda^2)/\lambda^4$. Its [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) is $w=-1+\lambda^2/3$ and its [deceleration parameter](deceleration-parameter.md) is $q=\lambda^2/2-1$. It gives [exponential-potential power-law inflation](exponential-potential-power-law-inflation.md) only for $0<\lambda^2<2$; the same algebraic family includes decelerating expansion outside that range when its potential normalization is real.
