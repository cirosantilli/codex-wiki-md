# Inversion mapping torus of a torus

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping torus](mapping-torus.md)

This [mapping torus](mapping-torus.md) fibers over the circle with fiber $T^{n-1}$ and inversion as monodromy. The [Wang sequence](wang-sequence.md) computes its additive [cohomology](cohomology-split.md). Coordinate projections to the [Klein bottle](klein-bottle.md) expose its mod-two [cup products](cup-product.md).
