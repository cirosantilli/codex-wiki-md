# Majorana-Weyl spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Majorana spinor](majorana-spinor.md)

A Majorana-Weyl spinor satisfies both a [Majorana spinor](majorana-spinor.md) reality condition and a [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) chirality condition when dimension and signature permit them. In ten-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime it has sixteen real components off shell and eight physical massless spin-one-half polarizations.
