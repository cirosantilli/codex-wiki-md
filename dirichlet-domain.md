# Dirichlet domain

↑ **Parent:** [Properly discontinuous group action](properly-discontinuous-group-action.md)

For a [group](group-split.md) of [isometries](isometry.md) acting properly discontinuously on the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md), and a point $p$ with trivial [point stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md), the closed [Dirichlet domain](dirichlet-domain.md) is

$$
D_G(p)=\{x:\rho(x,p)\le\rho(x,gp)\text{ for every }g\in G\}.
$$

It is the intersection of the closed half-planes bounded by [perpendicular bisectors](perpendicular-bisector.md) between $p$ and its other [group orbit](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) points. The [group orbit](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) is locally finite and the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) is a [proper metric space](proper-metric-space.md), so every point has a nearest [group orbit](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) point. This shows that the translates of $D_G(p)$ cover the plane. Distinct interiors are disjoint, since being strictly closer to two different [group orbit](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) points is impossible. Boundary ties are allowed in the closed domain. Thus it is a geometric fundamental region.
