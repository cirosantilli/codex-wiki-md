# Dirichlet-to-Neumann map for a Helmholtz half-space

↑ **Parent:** [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md)

For the upward derivative at a flat boundary, the [Dirichlet-to-Neumann map for a Helmholtz half-space](dirichlet-to-neumann-map-for-a-helmholtz-half-space.md) is the [Fourier multiplier](fourier-multiplier.md) $i\beta(q)$. The outward normal of the upper half-space points downward and has the opposite sign. Its evanescent multiplier grows like $|q|$, explaining why fine roughness matters even when the height is small.
