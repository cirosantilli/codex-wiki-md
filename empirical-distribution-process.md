# Empirical distribution process

↑ **Parent:** [Empirical distribution function](empirical-distribution-function.md)

The empirical distribution process is the centred, scaled [empirical distribution function](empirical-distribution-function.md) of an independent sample with [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $G$. Its mean is zero and its [covariance](covariance.md) is $G(\min(x,y))-G(x)G(y)$. This follows by expanding the two indicator averages: different sample indices have zero covariance, while the same index contributes the covariance of the two nested events. The scaling by $\sqrt n$ keeps these covariances independent of $n$.
