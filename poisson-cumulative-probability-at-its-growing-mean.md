# Poisson cumulative probability at its growing mean

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson central limit theorem](poisson-central-limit-theorem.md)

If $X_\lambda$ has a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md), then $\mathbb P(X_\lambda\leq\lfloor\lambda\rfloor)\to1/2$ as $\lambda\to\infty$. The [Poisson central limit theorem](poisson-central-limit-theorem.md) standardizes by mean $\lambda$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $\lambda$; the cutoff $(\lfloor\lambda\rfloor-\lambda)/\sqrt\lambda$ tends to zero. Squeezing between fixed nearby cutoffs and using continuity of the standard [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) function gives the limit.
