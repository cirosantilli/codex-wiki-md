# Radial tempered distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md)

For $n>1$, a [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md) $T$ on $\mathbb R^n$ is radial when $T\circ R=T$ for every $R\in SO(n)$, where $\langle T\circ R,\varphi\rangle=\langle T,\varphi\circ R^t\rangle$. This agrees with the usual [radial function](radial-function.md) definition for regular smooth distributions, because the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md) acts transitively on spheres.
