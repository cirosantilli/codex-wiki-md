# Character module

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

For a [module](module-mathematics.md) $M$ over a commutative [ring](ring.md) $A$, its character module is the additive-group dual into $D=\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$, with action $(a\phi)(m)=\phi(am)$. The group $D$ is an [injective cogenerator of abelian groups](injective-cogenerator-of-abelian-groups.md). The natural adjunction $\operatorname{Hom}_A(N,M^*)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(N\otimes_AM,D)$ sends $h$ to the balanced pairing $(n,m)\mapsto h(n)(m)$.
