# Charged-current W branching-fraction counting

↑ **Parent:** [W boson](w-boson.md)

Neglect final-state masses and QCD corrections. Each of the three leptonic [W boson](w-boson.md) decay channels contributes one common unit of width. Each open quark channel contributes $3|V_{ij}|^2$, with the factor three counting final colours. The [top quark](top-quark.md) is kinematically inaccessible in an on-shell W decay because $m_t>m_W$. Thus $B(W\to\ell\nu)=1/D\simeq1/9$ and $B(W^-\to b\bar u,b\bar c)=3(|V_{ub}|^2+|V_{cb}|^2)/D$. The large $V_{tb}$ does not open a forbidden top-quark channel.
