# Halo density estimate of formation redshift

↑ **Parent:** [Virial radius of a dark-matter halo](virial-radius-of-a-dark-matter-halo.md)

If a halo retains the mean density acquired at collapse, $3M/(4\pi R^3)=\Delta_{\rm vir}\bar\rho_{m,0}(1+z_f)^3$ estimates its formation redshift. In the non-dissipative [spherical-collapse model](spherical-collapse-model.md) during matter domination, $\Delta_{\rm vir}=18\pi^2$. Subsequent mass growth, profile evolution and the reference-density convention limit this inference.
