# Neumann solution of the Stefan problem

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

The Neumann solution places a planar phase boundary at $h(t)=2\lambda\sqrt{\kappa t}$. Similarity reduction of the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) gives temperature profiles in terms of the [error function](error-function.md), while the [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) determines $\lambda$ implicitly.
