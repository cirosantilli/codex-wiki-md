# Credit spread option

↑ **Parent:** [Credit spread](credit-spread.md)

An option with payoff linked to widening or narrowing of a specified [credit spread](credit-spread.md). A simple spread-widening payoff has the displayed form, where $A_T$ is an annuity or settlement scale. This transfers spread-mark-to-market risk and need not require an actual [default](credit-default.md) event. Pricing depends on spread volatility, discounting, [default](credit-default.md) treatment and the settlement convention.
