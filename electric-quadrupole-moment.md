# Electric quadrupole moment

↑ **Parent:** [Electric multipole expansion](electric-multipole-expansion.md)

The trace-free [electric quadrupole moment](electric-quadrupole-moment.md) is a symmetric tensor measuring the second-order term in an [electric multipole expansion](electric-multipole-expansion.md). For a localized charge distribution its contribution to the far-field [electrostatic potential](electric-potential.md) is $Q_{ij}\widehat r_i\widehat r_j/(8\pi\epsilon_0r^3)$, with summation over indices. The moment may be nonzero even when the total [electric charge](electric-charge.md) or [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) is nonzero; it should not be confused with an [electric quadrupole](electric-quadrupole.md) whose lower moments vanish. Changing the origin generally changes the tensor when lower moments are nonzero.
