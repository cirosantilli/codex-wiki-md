# Right-continuous adapted processes are progressively measurable

↑ **Parent:** [Progressive measurability](progressive-measurability.md)

For an [adapted process](adapted-process.md) with pathwise [right continuity](right-continuous-function.md), fix $T$ and approximate its time argument by the next endpoint of a deterministic partition of $[0,T]$. Every approximation is jointly measurable for the terminal [product sigma-algebra](product-sigma-algebra.md) because all sampled values are $\mathcal F_T$-measurable. [Right continuity](right-continuous-function.md) makes them converge pointwise, proving [progressive measurability](progressive-measurability.md). Sampling from the right need not preserve adaptedness at intermediate times, but that is not required in this proof.
