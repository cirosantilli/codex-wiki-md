# Coordinate tensor Lie derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md)

For every contravariant slot of a [tensor field](tensor-field.md), the [tensor Lie derivative](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md) contributes a negative derivative of its generating [vector field](vector-field.md); every covariant slot contributes a positive one. The remaining term differentiates the tensor's components along the generator. This follows from the [flow definition of the Lie derivative of a tensor field](flow-definition-of-the-lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md) and its product and contraction rules, without choosing a [affine connection](affine-connection.md).
