# Support of a mixed strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md)

The [support of a mixed strategy](support-of-a-mixed-strategy.md) consists of the pure strategies used with positive probability. At a [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md), every supported pure strategy is a [best response](best-response.md), while unused strategies may give no higher payoff.
