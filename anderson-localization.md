# Anderson localization

↑ **Parent:** [Wave propagation in a random medium](wave-propagation-in-a-random-medium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anderson_localization)

[Anderson localization](anderson-localization.md) is the confinement of [electron](electron.md) [wave functions](wave-function-split.md), or other [wave](wave-split.md) modes, by [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) between repeated [wave scattering](wave-scattering.md) paths in static disorder. A [localized eigenstate](localized-eigenstate.md) occupies a limited region even in a much larger sample. Disorder can therefore suppress [diffusion](diffusion.md) without absorbing the [wave](wave-split.md). In 1958, [Philip Warren Anderson](philip-warren-anderson.md) studied random site [energies](energy-split.md) and intersite coupling, finding a regime without transport. His paper develops a theoretical model; it does not report an Anderson laboratory measurement.[Anderson, 1958, pp.1492–1494, Sections I–II](absence-of-diffusion-in-certain-random-lattices-by-philip-warren-anderson.md)

The distinction from ordinary [multiple scattering](multiple-scattering.md) is the retention of [wave phases](phase-waves.md): alternative paths contribute [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md) before their squared [modulus](modulus.md) is taken. Adding path probabilities alone loses the cross terms responsible for [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md). This makes [Anderson localization](anderson-localization.md) useful as a warning about transport models: repeated deflections need not produce unlimited [diffusion](diffusion.md). It also connects [electron](electron.md) transport with [wave propagation in a random medium](wave-propagation-in-a-random-medium.md), although the detailed equations and experimental diagnostics differ between [electrons](electron.md), [light](light.md) and [acoustic waves](acoustic-wave.md).

[Philip Warren Anderson](philip-warren-anderson.md)'s 1977 lecture distinguishes localized magnetic moments from disorder-localized states. It credits [Nevill Francis Mott](nevill-francis-mott.md) with recognizing the significance of the [mobility edge](mobility-edge.md). A [Mott insulator](mott-insulator.md) instead requires [electron](electron.md) interactions and can exist without disorder.[Local Moments and Localized States, pp.376–377,395–396; inspected institutional mirror [https://www.nevis.columbia.edu/~haas/documents/physics_nobel_lectures/physics71-80-2.pdf](https://www.nevis.columbia.edu/~haas/documents/physics_nobel_lectures/physics71-80-2.pdf)](local-moments-and-localized-states-by-philip-warren-anderson.md)

[John Hasbrouck Van Vleck](john-hasbrouck-van-vleck.md) developed [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md) explanations of [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md), including its [temperature](temperature-split.md) dependence; this is a distinct contribution from [Anderson localization](anderson-localization.md).[Quantum Mechanics: The Key to Understanding Magnetism, pp.355–356; inspected institutional mirror [https://www.nevis.columbia.edu/~haas/documents/physics_nobel_lectures/physics71-80-2.pdf](https://www.nevis.columbia.edu/~haas/documents/physics_nobel_lectures/physics71-80-2.pdf)](quantum-mechanics-the-key-to-understanding-magnetism-by-john-hasbrouck-van-vleck.md)
