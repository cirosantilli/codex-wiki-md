# Levi decomposition of a quiver automorphism group

↑ **Parent:** [Automorphism group of a quiver representation](automorphism-group-of-a-quiver-representation.md)

Fixing a [Krull-Schmidt decomposition](krull-schmidt-decomposition.md) embeds $L=\prod_a\operatorname{GL}_{m_a}(k)$ by its actions on multiplicity spaces. This splits reduction modulo the [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md). Hence $\operatorname{Aut}_Q(X)=(1+J(\operatorname{End}_Q(X)))\rtimes L$, with a closed normal unipotent first factor. The explicit section works in every characteristic.
