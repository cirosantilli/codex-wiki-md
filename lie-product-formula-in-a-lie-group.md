# Lie product formula in a Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential map of a Lie group](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md)

The [Lie product formula](lie-product-formula.md) extends from [matrices](matrix.md) to any finite-dimensional [Lie group](lie-group.md). In a [local exponential chart](local-exponential-chart.md), smooth multiplication and its differential give $\log(\exp(sX)\exp(sY))=s(X+Y)+O(s^2)$. Taking $s=t/m$ and using the [one-parameter subgroup](one-parameter-subgroup.md) identity shows that the $m$th power is $\exp(t(X+Y)+O(m^{-1}))$, proving the displayed [limit](limit-of-a-function.md). This proof needs only the first-order expansion, rather than convergence of the full [Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula](baker-campbell-hausdorff-formula.md).
