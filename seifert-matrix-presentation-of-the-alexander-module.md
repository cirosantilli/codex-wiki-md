# Seifert-matrix presentation of the Alexander module

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert matrix](seifert-matrix.md)

Cut a [knot exterior](knot-exterior.md) along a [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md) and stack copies to form its [infinite cyclic cover](infinite-cyclic-cover-of-a-knot-exterior.md). Linking-dual generators for the [homology](homology-split.md) of the cut-open piece give the positive and negative inclusion [matrices](matrix.md) $V$ and $V^T$ in row convention. The gluing relations therefore have [matrix](matrix.md) $tV-V^T$. The H0 gluing map is multiplication by $t-1$ and is injective, so these relations present the entire [Alexander module of a knot](alexander-module-of-a-knot.md). Its order is the determinant, up to a Laurent unit.
