# Novikov additivity

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection form](intersection-form.md)

When compact oriented four-manifolds are glued along an entire closed boundary three-manifold, the [signature](signature-of-a-quadratic-form.md) of the result is the sum of their [intersection form](intersection-form.md) signatures. It applies to $X_0\cup_{\partial X_0}(-X_1)$ with the difference of the two signatures. Gluing along only part of a boundary can require a correction; that is a different situation.
