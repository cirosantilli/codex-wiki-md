# Future horismos

↑ **Parent:** [Causal future](causal-future.md)

The [future horismos](future-horismos.md) is the part of the [causal future](causal-future.md) outside the [chronological future](chronological-future.md). For a [compact](compact-space.md) set in a [globally hyperbolic spacetime](globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md), it equals the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of the [chronological future](chronological-future.md). Its normal [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) generators cannot have passed a [conjugate point to a spacelike surface](conjugate-point-to-a-spacelike-surface.md) when the initial set is that surface.
