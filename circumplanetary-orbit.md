# Circumplanetary orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

An [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) about a [planet](planet.md). Stellar tides, [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) and other perturbations can limit its stability; a sufficiently small [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) compared with the [Hill sphere](hill-sphere.md) permits a local approximately planet-centred [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md).
