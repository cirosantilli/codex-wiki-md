# Top-edge exit probability of a Brownian half-strip

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion](conformal-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md)

For [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) started at $p+iq$, $p>0$ and $0<q<a$, in $\{x+iy:x>0,0<y<a\}$, the displayed value is its probability of exiting through $y=a$. The [half-strip conformal map by hyperbolic cosine](half-strip-conformal-map-by-hyperbolic-cosine.md) sends the top edge to $(-\infty,-1)$ and the starting point to $A+iB$, where $A=\cosh(\pi p/a)\cos(\pi q/a)$ and $B=\sinh(\pi p/a)\sin(\pi q/a)$. Integrating the [Cauchy exit law from a Brownian half-plane](cauchy-exit-law-from-a-brownian-half-plane.md) over that ray proves the formula. As $p\to\infty$ it tends to $q/a$.
