# Euclidean unit ball

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md)

The [Euclidean unit ball](euclidean-unit-ball.md) is the closed [unit ball](unit-ball.md) of the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md), centered at zero. Its radial function is identically $1$, making it a natural reference body for [John ellipsoids](john-ellipsoid.md) and [volume ratios](volume-ratio.md).
