# Hydration shell

↑ **Parent:** [Solvation shell](solvation-shell.md)

A [hydration shell](hydration-shell.md) is a [solvation shell](solvation-shell.md) in [water](water.md). The existing [hydration of ions](hydration-of-ions.md) discussion describes preferential orientations around [cations](cation.md) and [anions](anion.md).

![](hydration-shell-charge-orientation.png)

**[Figure 3](#image-hydration-shell-charge-orientation). Qualitative hydration shells: opposite orientations of polar water molecules around a cation and an anion, with each ion's net electric charge retained**. Original schematic, not measured molecular positions. Atom spacings and the dashed shell boundary are illustrative. Five selected [water](water.md) glyphs in each panel do not assert a [coordination number](coordination-number.md). Each [water](water.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md) is neutral despite its [partial atomic charges](partial-atomic-charge.md).
