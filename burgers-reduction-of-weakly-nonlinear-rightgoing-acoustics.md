# Burgers reduction of weakly nonlinear rightgoing acoustics

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md)

For a pure rightgoing [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md), retarded time $\theta=t-x$ and slow distance $X=Mx$ separate fast oscillations from nonlinear steepening. Expanding the [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) gives $p=\rho+M(\gamma-1)\rho^2/2+O(M^2)$. The leading perturbations satisfy $u_0=\rho_0=p_0$. Adding the first-order [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) and [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) equations cancels the unknown first corrections and yields the displayed [viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md). Inviscid characteristics satisfy $\theta=s-(\gamma+1)F(s)X/2$, $p=F(s)$; loss of invertibility marks [shock](shock-wave.md) formation and the end of the smooth inviscid approximation.
