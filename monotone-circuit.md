# Monotone circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone circuit complexity](monotone-circuit-complexity.md)

A monotone [Boolean circuit](boolean-circuit.md) uses only AND and OR gates, variables, and optional constants. It computes a [monotone Boolean function](monotone-boolean-function.md); negation gates are excluded.
