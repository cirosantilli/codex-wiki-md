# Indefinite Hermitian form

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md)

An indefinite [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md) takes both positive and negative values on $\langle v,v\rangle$ for nonzero [vectors](vector.md). For example, $-|v_0|^2+|v_1|^2+|v_2|^2+|v_3|^2$ is indefinite. It is not a positive [inner product](inner-product.md) defining a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), and a nonzero [vector](vector.md) can have zero norm without being orthogonal to all [vectors](vector.md). A positive semidefinite restricted form becomes a positive [inner product](inner-product.md) only after quotienting its [radical of a Hermitian form](radical-of-a-hermitian-form.md).
