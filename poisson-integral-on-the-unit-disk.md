# Poisson integral on the unit disk

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)

The disk [Poisson integral](poisson-integral.md) is the [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) with boundary data $u$, given by

$$
P_r[u](t)=\int_{\mathbb T}\frac{1-r^2}{|e^{2\pi is}-re^{2\pi it}|^2}u(s)\,ds.
$$

It is the real part of the [Schwarz integral on the unit disk](schwarz-integral-on-the-unit-disk.md) for real data. The nonnegative kernel has integral one; smooth boundary data extend smoothly to the circle. This disk formula should be distinguished from the half-plane formula.
