# Real inverse-hyperbolic antiderivative branches

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse hyperbolic cosine](inverse-hyperbolic-cosine.md)

For $a>0$ and $|x|>a$, the displayed real [antiderivative](antiderivative.md) equals $\operatorname{arcosh}(x/a)$ on the right component and $-\operatorname{arcosh}(-x/a)$ on the left, up to constants. Differentiating the absolute logarithm gives $1/\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$ on both components. The constants are independent because the domain is disconnected. The integrals from either finite singular boundary are [improper integrals](improper-integral.md) that converge, since their integrands diverge only like the inverse square root of distance to that boundary.
