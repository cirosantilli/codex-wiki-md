# Integral basis of a cubefree pure cubic field

↑ **Parent:** [Integral basis](integral-basis.md)

Let $m_1,m_2$ be coprime positive [square-free integers](square-free-integer.md), with $m_1\not\equiv\pm m_2\pmod9$. The displayed [integral basis](integral-basis.md) has [discriminant](discriminant.md) $-27m_1^2m_2^2$. Both nonconstant generators are integral, and their products are integral linear combinations of the three basis elements. At a prime dividing $m_1$, the cubic for $\alpha$ is [Eisenstein](eisenstein-criterion.md); at a prime dividing $m_2$, the cubic for $\alpha^2/m_2$ is [Eisenstein](eisenstein-criterion.md). If $3\nmid m_1m_2$, then $m_1m_2^2\not\equiv\pm1\pmod9$, and a shift by $1$ or $-1$ gives an [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md) at three. These local [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) generators show that the order is maximal at all primes dividing its [discriminant](discriminant.md); elsewhere the [discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice](discriminant-index-formula-for-an-integral-lattice.md) already gives maximality.
