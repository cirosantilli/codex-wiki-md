# Multiple slip

↑ **Parent:** [Slip system](slip-system.md)

Plastic flow involving more than one active [slip system](slip-system.md). Interactions between [dislocations](dislocation.md) on intersecting systems can give substantially faster [work hardening](work-hardening.md) than easy single slip.
