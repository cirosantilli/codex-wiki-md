# Single-error test from two binary BCH syndromes

↑ **Parent:** [Root-evaluation syndrome for a cyclic code](root-evaluation-syndrome-for-a-cyclic-code.md)

For a binary [cyclic code](cyclic-code.md) with [defining zeros of a cyclic code](zero-of-a-cyclic-code.md) $\alpha$ and $\alpha^3$, a single error in coordinate $j$ has [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $X^j$ and [syndromes](syndrome.md) $S_1=\alpha^j$, $S_3=\alpha^{3j}$. If $S_1\ne0$ and $S_3=S_1^3$, recover $j$ from the exponent of $S_1$, flip that coordinate, and verify that the resulting [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) vanishes at both roots. When the [linear code](linear-code.md) has [minimum Hamming distance](minimum-distance-of-a-code.md) at least five, this verified correction is the unique [codeword](codeword.md) within two errors. The [syndrome](syndrome.md) relation alone should not replace checking the corrected word.
