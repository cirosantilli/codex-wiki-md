# Unrestricted consistency self-strengthening is inconsistent

↑ **Parent:** [Iterated consistency progression](iterated-consistency-progression.md)

Consider extending $S+\operatorname{Con}_S$ by a rule sending every $\operatorname{Con}_A$ to $\operatorname{Con}_{A+\operatorname{Con}_A}$. Put $A=S+\neg\operatorname{Con}_S$. The initial [consistency](consistent-first-order-theory.md) [axiom](axiom.md) proves $\operatorname{Con}_A$ by the negated-consistency extension theorem, so the rule proves $\operatorname{Con}_{A+\operatorname{Con}_A}$. But $\operatorname{Con}_A\to\operatorname{Con}_S$ is provable by inclusion of proofs, making $A+\operatorname{Con}_A$ [inconsistent](consistent-first-order-theory.md). Arithmetic verifies its finite [contradiction](contradiction.md) proof. Thus the unrestricted rule proves both that [theory](first-order-theory.md)'s [consistency](consistent-first-order-theory.md) and inconsistency. This differs from an externally justified progression along specified sound [theories](first-order-theory.md).
