# Hamiltonian Lie algebra homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md)

On a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md), use $\iota_{X_f}\omega=-df$ and $\{f,g\}=\omega(X_f,X_g)=X_f(g)$ for the [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md). [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md) gives $\mathcal L_{X_f}\omega=0$. Taking the [interior product of a differential form](interior-product.md) with $[X_f,X_g]$ then yields $-d\{f,g\}$, proving the homomorphism. Its kernel consists of locally constant functions, since $X_f=0$ precisely when $df=0$. On a connected manifold these are the constants. The quotient of smooth functions by this kernel is therefore the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of [Hamiltonian vector fields](hamiltonian-vector-field.md). A different contraction sign requires a compatible bracket sign.
