↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

**The liver uses glucose sensing chiefly to regulate fuel storage and production, while pancreatic beta cells convert glucose availability into an endocrine signal.** The comparison therefore includes transport, metabolism and allosteric control; it should not identify one transporter as the complete sensor in either tissue.

[glucokinase](../../../../../glucokinase.md) phosphorylates [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) to [glucose-6-phosphate](../../../../../glucose-6-phosphate.md) in both [hepatocytes](../../../../../hepatocyte.md) and pancreatic [beta cells](../../../../../beta-cell.md). Unlike many other [hexokinases](../../../../../hexokinase.md), it is not strongly inhibited by its product and operates over a higher glucose range. Its monomeric enzyme nevertheless shows a sigmoidal steady-state response to [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md), arising from its kinetic conformational behaviour rather than cooperative binding between several subunits. A glucose half-saturation concentration in the physiological millimolar range makes its flux responsive to changing [blood glucose](../../../../../blood-sugar.md), whereas a low-affinity constant cannot alone establish the in vivo sensing step without considering transport and downstream metabolism.

In [beta cells](../../../../../beta-cell.md), glucose metabolism through [glycolysis](../../../../../glycolysis.md) and mitochondrial oxidation increases the cellular ATP-to-ADP state. [ATP-sensitive potassium channels](../../../../../atp-sensitive-potassium-channel.md) then close: ATP inhibits the Kir6.2 pore, while Mg-nucleotide effects mediated through SUR1 additionally regulate opening. Reduced potassium conductance produces [depolarization](../../../../../depolarization.md), opening [voltage-gated calcium channels](../../../../../voltage-gated-calcium-channel.md). Calcium entry triggers [exocytosis](../../../../../exocytosis.md) of insulin-containing secretory granules. Thus the channel senses nucleotides downstream of glucose metabolism; it does not bind extracellular [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) as its initiating ligand. Metabolic amplification and other signals also regulate the amount secreted, so this triggering pathway is necessary context rather than a complete description of every phase of insulin secretion.

[glucokinase](../../../../../glucokinase.md) helps set the glucose threshold for that response. An activating glucokinase mutation associated with familial hyperinsulinism provides evidence that shifting the enzyme's glucose responsiveness can shift whole-body insulin control.[Results and pp. 226–230, familial mutation and altered glucokinase function](../../../../../familial-hyperinsulinism-caused-by-an-activating-glucokinase-mutation-by-benjamin-glaser-and-others.md) To separate this interpretation from altered expression, the variant's kinetics should be compared with the normal enzyme and its effect on secretion assessed at matched expression. Inhibiting the potassium channel downstream can bypass part of the sensing chain, whereas preventing calcium entry tests the final electrical-to-secretory coupling.

Glucose transport must be discussed with species in mind. [GLUT2](../../../../../glut2.md) is the prominent transporter in the classical rodent beta-cell model, but human [beta cells](../../../../../beta-cell.md) use predominantly [GLUT1](../../../../../glut1.md) rather than sharing the same abundance of GLUT2. Direct comparison of human and rat preparations found much lower GLUT2 abundance in human cells while glucokinase retained a comparable influence on glucose utilization.[Abstract and pp. 2489–2495, human/rat transporter expression and glucose utilization](../../../../../human-and-rat-beta-cells-differ-in-glucose-transporter-but-not-in-glucokinase-gene-expression-by-a-de-vos-and-others.md) A transport step with capacity greater than phosphorylation can allow intracellular glucose availability to track the external supply, leaving [glucokinase](../../../../../glucokinase.md) as a major metabolic control point. These observations argue against presenting rodent GLUT2 expression as a universal human sensing mechanism.

In the [liver](../../../../../liver.md), [GLUT2](../../../../../glut2.md) supports rapid bidirectional facilitated transport. Hepatic [glucokinase](../../../../../glucokinase.md) traps abundant glucose as [glucose-6-phosphate](../../../../../glucose-6-phosphate.md), supplying [glycogenesis](../../../../../glycogenesis.md) and other metabolic pathways after feeding. [glucokinase regulatory protein](../../../../../glucokinase-regulatory-protein.md) adds tissue-specific control: at low glucose it binds and sequesters glucokinase in the nucleus; increased glucose promotes release and cytoplasmic use. [fructose-6-phosphate](../../../../../fructose-6-phosphate.md) favours the inhibitory interaction, while [fructose-1-phosphate](../../../../../fructose-1-phosphate.md) antagonizes it, coordinating glucose trapping with nutrient context. Hormonal regulation of glucokinase expression adds a slower level of control. Unlike the beta-cell response, the immediate output is a change in hepatic metabolic flux, not release of insulin.

The liver also has an important **direct glucose sensor in glycogen phosphorylase**. Binding of [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) to liver [glycogen phosphorylase](../../../../../glycogen-phosphorylase.md) a favours an inactive conformation and its dephosphorylation by [protein phosphatase 1](../../../../../protein-phosphatase-1.md). Loss of phosphorylase-a activity switches off [glycogenolysis](../../../../../glycogenolysis.md) and relieves its inhibitory influence on glycogen-synthase phosphatase activity, helping activate [glycogen synthase](../../../../../glycogen-synthase.md). Glucose administration experiments observed phosphorylase inactivation preceding synthase activation, supporting an ordered switch rather than simultaneous independent responses.[Glucose-administration time courses, sequential phosphorylase and synthase responses](../../../../../the-sequential-inactivation-of-glycogen-phosphorylase-and-activation-of-glycogen-synthetase-in-liver-after-the-administration-of-glucose-to-mice-and-rats-by-willy-stalmans-and-others.md) This direct allosteric route is distinct from glucokinase-mediated metabolic sensing and complements insulin signalling. Together they suit an organ that must stop exporting and begin storing glucose when supply rises, while beta-cell sensing coordinates that transition across multiple tissues.
