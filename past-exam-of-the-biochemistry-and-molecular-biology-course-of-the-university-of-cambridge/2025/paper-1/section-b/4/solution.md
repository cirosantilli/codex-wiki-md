↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**RNA structure controls which ribosome-binding and decoding events are kinetically accessible.** A bacterial [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) can base-pair with itself or other [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) molecules, forming alternative structures whose populations depend on sequence, ligand concentration, [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and translating [ribosomes](../../../../../ribosome.md). Its sequence consequently encodes both a polypeptide and part of the mechanism controlling production of that polypeptide.

At initiation, a stem can sequester the [Shine-Dalgarno sequence](../../../../../shine-dalgarno-sequence.md) or start region, reducing pairing with the anti-Shine-Dalgarno region of the small-subunit [ribosomal RNA](../../../../../ribosomal-rna.md) and preventing productive initiation. Opening that structure makes the same coding sequence more accessible. Start-site accessibility also matters in bacteria whose transcripts do not use a canonical [Shine-Dalgarno sequence](../../../../../shine-dalgarno-sequence.md); not all bacterial initiation can be reduced to this one motif. The regulatory variable is often competition between folding, unfolding and ribosome binding rather than a permanently closed structure.

A [riboswitch](../../../../../riboswitch.md) couples a ligand-binding [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) domain to an expression platform that can alternatively expose or hide a translation-initiation region. Binding of a metabolite can stabilize one structure without requiring a separate regulatory [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). Direct binding of thiamine derivatives to bacterial [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) established an RNA-based route to metabolic regulation.[Abstract and pp. 952–956, direct metabolite binding by regulatory RNA](../../../../../thiamine-derivatives-bind-messenger-rnas-directly-to-regulate-bacterial-gene-expression-by-wade-winkler-and-others.md) In an [RNA thermometer](../../../../../rna-thermometer.md), heating destabilizes a stem and exposes the initiation site. The FourU thermometer provides a concrete example in which uridines help pair with the ribosome-binding region of a heat-responsive transcript.[Abstract and results on the agsA 5′ untranslated-region thermometer](../../../../../fouru-a-novel-type-of-rna-thermometer-in-salmonella-by-torsten-waldminghaus-and-others.md) Sequence specificity and a biologically relevant transition range distinguish such a sensor from the nonspecific fact that all RNA eventually denatures on heating.

Trans-acting [small regulatory RNAs](../../../../../small-regulatory-rna.md) can remodel target [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) structure by base pairing. Some block initiation directly, often aided by the RNA-binding chaperone [Hfq](../../../../../hfq.md); others disrupt an inhibitory stem and activate translation. Pairing can also expose an [ribonuclease](../../../../../ribonuclease.md) cleavage site or recruit degradation machinery. Protein-binding regulators provide another route: a ribosomal protein can recognize a structured element in its own polycistronic transcript and repress excess ribosomal-protein production. Occupancy of a structured site thus links translation to stoichiometric demand.

In a [polycistronic messenger RNA](../../../../../polycistronic-messenger-rna.md), translation of an upstream coding region can unwind a structure masking a downstream initiation site, producing [translational coupling](../../../../../translational-coupling.md). During elongation, the [ribosome](../../../../../ribosome.md) can unwind many structures, but a sufficiently resistant downstream [RNA pseudoknot](../../../../../rna-pseudoknot.md) or stem, combined with an appropriate slippery sequence, can promote [programmed ribosomal frameshifting](../../../../../programmed-ribosomal-frameshifting.md). This changes the reading frame and hence the ratio of distinct protein products. Structure can also influence recoding or pause positions; a predicted stable hairpin alone does not establish that a physiologically meaningful pause occurs.

RNA structure further couples translation to transcription. In the tryptophan [attenuation in bacterial gene regulation](../../../../../attenuation-in-bacterial-gene-regulation.md) mechanism, rapid or stalled translation of a leader peptide changes which alternative RNA stems form. This decides formation of a transcription terminator and therefore how much downstream transcript is available. The immediate switch is termination of [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md), even though ribosome movement and structured RNA generate the decision. Likewise, intrinsic terminator formation, transcript degradation and ribosome protection change protein output without necessarily changing translation efficiency per intact transcript.

A convincing experiment measures both transcript abundance and protein output. Fuse the regulatory region to a reporter while retaining a control promoter; compare ligand or temperature conditions with a construct lacking the proposed structural element. A mutation disrupting one side of a stem, followed by a compensatory mutation restoring pairing with a changed sequence, distinguishes pairing from a single nucleotide-specific contact. [RNA structure probing](../../../../../rna-structure-probing.md) can test accessibility directly, and [toeprinting](../../../../../toeprinting.md) can report initiation-complex occupancy. Include controls for reporter folding, growth, RNA half-life and effects of the perturbation on the ribosome itself. These tests connect a structural transition to the appropriate mechanistic step rather than assuming every change in protein abundance is a change in initiation.
