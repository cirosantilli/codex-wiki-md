<h1 id="section-b/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**Directed evolution searches experimentally for useful sequences; rational design proposes changes from a mechanistic model. Their strengths are complementary.** Both require a clear target phenotype and a reliable assay. “Improved” must mean improvement in a relevant property, such as catalytic efficiency in the intended solvent, rather than simply more expression or a larger reporter signal.

In [directed evolution](../../../../../directed-evolution.md), a parent [gene](../../../../../gene.md) is diversified by [error-prone PCR](../../../../../error-prone-pcr.md), recombination or targeted mutagenesis. Each variant must remain linked to the activity it encodes, for example in a separate bacterial clone or an appropriate display or compartmentalization system. A [screening assay](../../../../../screening-assay.md) measures individual variants, whereas a [selection assay](../../../../../selection-assay.md) allows successful variants preferential survival or recovery. Improved variants seed another cycle. Maintaining this [genotype-phenotype linkage](../../../../../genotype-phenotype-linkage.md) is essential: a diffusible product shared between cells could otherwise reward inactive neighbouring variants.

A classic example is evolution of [subtilisin E](../../../../../subtilisin-e.md) for activity in [dimethylformamide](../../../../../dimethylformamide.md). Sequential random mutagenesis and screening found combinations of substitutions that improved performance in an environment outside the native enzyme's usual conditions. The result illustrates that useful mutations need not be obvious from the catalytic-site structure: they can alter stability, solvent interactions or the conformational ensemble.[pp. 5618–5622, sequential mutagenesis, screening and characterization in organic solvent](../../../../../tuning-the-activity-of-an-enzyme-for-unusual-environments-sequential-random-mutagenesis-of-subtilisin-e-for-catalysis-in-dimethylformamide-by-keqin-chen-and-others.md) To identify the reason for an improved clone, I would compare purified variants at equal active-enzyme concentration, measure activity and stability separately, and reconstruct individual and combined substitutions. Otherwise increased secretion, expression or resistance to the assay conditions could masquerade as altered intrinsic catalysis.

The advantages of [directed evolution](../../../../../directed-evolution.md) are that it can exploit effects too complicated to predict and optimize several coupled properties in the actual assay environment. Its limitations are experimental throughput, the availability of a starting activity and a faithful assay. The accessible sequence space is tiny relative to all possible proteins. [Epistasis](../../../../../epistasis.md) means that combining individually beneficial substitutions may fail, and a series of local improvements may miss a route requiring a temporarily deleterious intermediate. Selection on an easy surrogate substrate can improve the surrogate while failing on the intended substrate. A large library does not rescue an assay that measures the wrong property.

[rational protein design](../../../../../rational-protein-design.md) instead uses sequence comparisons, structural information and chemical reasoning to propose changes. Examples include repositioning a catalytic group, reshaping a binding pocket, introducing a stabilizing interaction or removing a reactive surface residue. A focused set of [site-directed mutagenesis](../../../../../site-directed-mutagenesis.md) is easier to interpret and cheaper to test than a vast random library when the relevant mechanism is understood. It can also create an initial active site for chemistry that the parent does not catalyse detectably. However, the accuracy of the proposal depends on treatment of folding, water, electrostatics, dynamics and the difference between substrate binding and transition-state stabilization.

Computational [Kemp eliminase](../../../../../kemp-eliminase.md) design provides a useful comparison with the subtilisin case. Designed active sites placed a base in a scaffold to promote proton abstraction in [Kemp elimination](../../../../../kemp-elimination.md), creating experimentally measurable catalytic activity. Subsequent [directed evolution](../../../../../directed-evolution.md) improved the designs, demonstrating that the initial geometric model captured useful chemistry but did not fully optimize the physical enzyme.[pp. 190–195, designed catalytic sites and experimental improvement](../../../../../kemp-elimination-catalysts-by-computational-enzyme-design-by-daniela-rothlisberger-and-others.md) For a designed catalyst, controls should include the unmodified scaffold, removal of the proposed catalytic residue, no-enzyme reactions and direct product identification. A bound substrate, a plausible predicted structure or increased fluorescence from a reporter is not by itself evidence for the intended reaction mechanism.

A productive combined strategy uses rational knowledge to choose scaffolds and library positions, then lets [directed evolution](../../../../../directed-evolution.md) explore variants under the desired conditions. Sequencing and biophysical characterization of the successful variants can then improve the design model. **Prediction focuses the search; controlled experiments decide whether the resulting protein actually has the desired function.** Neither approach eliminates the need to assess trade-offs among activity, stability, specificity and manufacturability.
