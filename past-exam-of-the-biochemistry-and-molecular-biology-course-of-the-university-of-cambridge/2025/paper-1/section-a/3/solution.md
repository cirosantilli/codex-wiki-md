↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Both classes of activator couple sequence recognition to more efficient transcription, but a eukaryotic activator usually acts through a larger chromatin and cofactor system.** A typical [eukaryotic transcriptional activator](../../../../../eukaryotic-transcriptional-activator.md) has a [DNA-binding domain](../../../../../dna-binding-domain.md) and an [activation domain](../../../../../activation-domain.md). The former positions it at a regulatory sequence; the latter recruits or regulates other components. These activities can be separated experimentally by tethering an activation region to an unrelated [DNA-binding domain](../../../../../dna-binding-domain.md) and asking whether a reporter with the corresponding binding site is activated. Binding alone is insufficient: a DNA-bound derivative lacking an effective activation region need not stimulate [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md).

In [eukaryotes](../../../../../eukaryote.md), the target [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is packaged in [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md). An activator at an [enhancer](../../../../../enhancer.md) or promoter-proximal site can recruit [histone acetyltransferases](../../../../../histone-acetyltransferase.md) and [ATP-dependent chromatin remodelling complexes](../../../../../atp-dependent-chromatin-remodelling-complex.md). Histone modification and nucleosome repositioning can increase access to regulatory and promoter sequences; acetylated residues can also recruit reader proteins. These are context-dependent routes, not a universal requirement that every activated gene first undergo the same histone change. Activators can cooperate with pioneer or partner [transcription factors](../../../../../transcription-factor.md), and their activity can be regulated by ligand binding, phosphorylation, localization or degradation.

Activator contacts with the [Mediator complex](../../../../../mediator-complex.md) and the general transcription machinery help assemble or stabilize an [RNA polymerase II](../../../../../rna-polymerase-ii.md) [preinitiation complex](../../../../../preinitiation-complex.md). [TFIID](../../../../../tfiid.md), including [TATA-binding protein](../../../../../tata-binding-protein.md), recognizes appropriate promoter features; other general factors recruit the polymerase, promote opening and enable promoter escape. Regulatory contacts may additionally influence release of paused polymerase and productive elongation. [DNA looping](../../../../../dna-looping.md) allows an enhancer-bound activator to affect a distant promoter, but proximity does not mean every enhancer contact is productive or that all promoters are interchangeable. Biochemical reconstitution showed that a mediator fraction was required for activated transcription in vitro, providing evidence that activator–polymerase communication can require an intermediate rather than a direct DNA-to-polymerase contact.[Abstract and pp. 436–438, biochemical requirement for mediator activity](../../../../../a-mediator-required-for-activation-of-rna-polymerase-ii-transcription-in-vitro-by-peter-m-flanagan-and-others.md)

The bacterial [catabolite activator protein](../../../../../catabolite-activator-protein.md), also called [cAMP receptor protein](../../../../../catabolite-activator-protein.md), is a simpler example of regulated recruitment. In the usual [Escherichia coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md) model, increased [cAMP](../../../../../cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) under carbon conditions favouring its accumulation promotes a CAP conformation competent for specific binding. A CAP dimer uses its [helix-turn-helix](../../../../../helix-turn-helix.md) regions to recognize a DNA site and bends the duplex. At a class I CAP-dependent promoter such as the canonical lac arrangement, a site centred near $-61.5$ positions an activating surface to contact the C-terminal domain of the [RNA polymerase](../../../../../rna-polymerase.md) alpha subunit. This stabilizes polymerase recruitment; the CAP–alpha-domain–DNA structure supplies a direct molecular basis for that contact.[pp. 1562–1566, CAP–alpha-domain contact and transcription-activation complex](../../../../../structural-basis-of-transcription-activation-the-cap-alpha-ctd-dna-complex-by-brian-benoff-and-others.md)

CAP is not limited to that single geometry. At class II promoters, a site near $-41.5$ overlaps the promoter region and permits additional contacts with polymerase, affecting more than initial recruitment. Promoter position, orientation and other repressors therefore matter. [cAMP](../../../../../cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) does not encode a complete “glucose absent, transcription on” truth table: the lac system additionally depends on substrate-responsive repression and other physiological inputs. An activating protein can improve a limiting step without determining the entire expression pattern.

The common principles are modular sequence targeting, regulated [protein-protein interactions](../../../../../protein-protein-interaction.md) and cooperative assembly of a transcriptionally productive complex. The differences are the canonical bacterial polymerase with its [sigma factor](../../../../../sigma-factor.md) versus [RNA polymerase II](../../../../../rna-polymerase-ii.md) and general factors, local promoter arrangements versus enhancer action over distance, and the extensive contribution of [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) in [eukaryotes](../../../../../eukaryote.md). A strong test combines binding and function: footprinting or an [electrophoretic mobility shift assay](../../../../../electrophoretic-mobility-shift-assay.md) measures DNA occupancy, whereas a transcript assay measures output. An activation-surface mutant that retains DNA binding but loses transcriptional stimulation separates recruitment from recognition. In a reconstituted system, removing and restoring a required cofactor tests causality; in cells, changes in chromatin or reporter activity alone do not identify the direct protein contact.
