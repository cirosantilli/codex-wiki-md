<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**The useful product is a correctly identified, active and reproducible protein preparation, not merely a large expression band.** I would first examine the [coding sequence](../../../../../coding-sequence.md), [protein sequence](../../../../../protein-sequence.md) and available biological information to identify the likely mature chain, [protein domains](../../../../../protein-domain.md), [signal peptides](../../../../../signal-peptide.md), transmembrane segments, [disulfide bonds](../../../../../disulfide-bond.md) and required [post-translational modifications](../../../../../post-translational-modification.md). A predicted [protein structure](../../../../../protein-structure.md) can guide construct boundaries, but cannot establish that the expressed product will fold or function. A [eukaryotic](../../../../../eukaryote.md) genomic sequence containing [introns](../../../../../intron.md) generally needs replacement by an intron-free [Complementary DNA](../../../../../complementary-dna.md) or synthetic coding region for expression in [Escherichia coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md). I would verify the chosen isoform and full sequence, since a database annotation can be wrong.

The expression host follows from the biochemical requirements. [Escherichia coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md) is inexpensive and convenient for many soluble [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md); yeast, insect or mammalian cells may be preferable for secreted [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) or essential [glycosylation](../../../../../glycosylation.md). A [membrane protein](../../../../../membrane-protein.md) also requires a suitable membrane environment during expression and an appropriate detergent or membrane mimic after extraction. I would preserve biologically necessary domains and modifications. Removing a flexible region to improve yield creates a different construct whose suitability must be justified by the intended analysis.

For a bacterial preparation, I would clone the coding region into an [expression vector](../../../../../expression-vector.md) with a controllable [promoter](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md), a suitable [ribosome-binding site](../../../../../ribosome-binding-site.md), a selectable marker and a removable [affinity tag](../../../../../affinity-tag.md). A terminal polyhistidine tag is a practical initial choice, provided the terminus is compatible with activity. Correct reading frame, both junctions and the complete insert must be checked by [DNA sequencing](../../../../../dna-sequencing.md). The [T7 expression system](../../../../../t7-expression-system.md) offers strong inducible expression through a phage [RNA polymerase](../../../../../rna-polymerase.md); the original work established selective expression of cloned genes, but strong transcription alone does not guarantee soluble, active product.[Abstract and pp. 113–130, inducible selective expression](../../../../../use-of-bacteriophage-t7-rna-polymerase-to-direct-selective-high-level-expression-of-cloned-genes-by-f-william-studier-and-others.md)

I would screen small cultures before scale-up, varying induction strength, induction duration and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), and comparing total lysate with soluble and insoluble fractions by [SDS-PAGE](../../../../../sds-page.md). Empty-vector and uninduced controls identify host bands and background expression; an [immunoblot](../../../../../western-blot.md) against the tag can help identify the product. Expression at lower [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) or a weaker rate can improve [protein folding](../../../../../protein-folding.md); persistent insolubility may require changing the construct, host or folding environment. An [inclusion body](../../../../../inclusion-body.md) is evidence of abundant insoluble material, not of correctly folded [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). Refolding may be possible, but its yield and recovered activity need independent validation.

Once a small-scale condition produces active soluble material, I would expand the culture or fermentation while monitoring growth and retaining the validated induction conditions. After harvesting, controlled lysis at low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), an appropriate [buffer solution](../../../../../buffer-solution.md) and protection against [proteolysis](../../../../../proteolysis.md) preserve the product. [Centrifugation](../../../../../centrifugation.md) removes debris; a membrane-associated target instead requires isolation and solubilization of the relevant fraction. I would use [immobilized metal affinity chromatography](../../../../../immobilized-metal-affinity-chromatography.md) for a polyhistidine-tagged construct, wash away nonspecific binders, and elute under conditions compatible with activity. Tag cleavage, a second affinity step to remove tag and tagged protease, and an orthogonal separation such as [ion-exchange chromatography](../../../../../ion-exchange-chromatography.md) can improve purity. A final [size-exclusion chromatography](../../../../../size-exclusion-chromatography.md) step removes aggregates and separates different assemblies. No single chromatographic peak proves identity or homogeneity.

At each stage I would record [protein concentration](../../../../../protein-concentration.md), total recovered amount and a suitable activity or binding readout, rather than optimizing yield alone. Final checks would include [SDS-PAGE](../../../../../sds-page.md), intact or peptide [mass spectrometry](../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md), a native-size measurement and the expected biological activity. Buffer-only and host-purification controls test whether apparent activity comes from contaminants; comparing tagged and cleaved material tests tag interference. Small frozen aliquots should be used only after checking stability and freeze–thaw effects. **Scale-up is justified by recovery of the desired molecular species with retained function**, with any differences from the native [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) documented.
