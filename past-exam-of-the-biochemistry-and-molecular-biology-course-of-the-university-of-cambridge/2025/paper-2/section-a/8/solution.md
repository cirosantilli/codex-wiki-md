<h1 id="section-a/8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Two distinct possibilities are **a second [mutation](../../../../../mutation.md) affecting drug sensitivity of the targeted [receptor tyrosine kinase](../../../../../receptor-tyrosine-kinase.md)**, so that it remains active at the achieved inhibitor concentration, and **activation of a bypass signaling route**, such as another [receptor tyrosine kinase](../../../../../receptor-tyrosine-kinase.md) or a downstream [Ras](../../../../../ras.md) or [protein kinase](../../../../../protein-kinase.md) component, restoring proliferation despite inhibition of the original target. The resistant population may arise by selection of rare pre-existing [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) or by later acquisition of a change; recurrence alone cannot distinguish those histories. Compare target engagement, target [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) and downstream signaling in the original and recurrent samples, with [DNA sequencing](../../../../../dna-sequencing.md) and functional rescue or perturbation of the candidate resistance mechanism.
