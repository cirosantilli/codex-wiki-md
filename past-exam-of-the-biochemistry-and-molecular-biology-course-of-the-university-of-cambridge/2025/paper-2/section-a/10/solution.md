↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

The [hydrogenosomes](../../../../../hydrogenosome.md) and [mitosomes](../../../../../mitosome.md) are specialized mitochondrion-related [organelles](../../../../../organelle.md) found in various anaerobic or oxygen-limited [eukaryotes](../../../../../eukaryote.md). **Typical [hydrogenosomes](../../../../../hydrogenosome.md) support anaerobic [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) production**, converting substrates such as [pyruvate](../../../../../pyruvate.md) into products including [acetate](../../../../../acetate.md), [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and molecular [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md). [Substrate-level phosphorylation](../../../../../substrate-level-phosphorylation.md) supplies [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md), while [hydrogenase](../../../../../hydrogenase.md) disposes of reducing equivalents as [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md); oxygen is not the terminal acceptor of a conventional respiratory chain in this pathway.[Abstract and biochemical characterization of hydrogenosomal pyruvate metabolism](../../../../../hydrogenosome-a-cytoplasmic-organelle-of-the-anaerobic-flagellate-tritrichomonas-foetus-and-its-role-in-pyruvate-metabolism-by-donald-g-lindmark-and-miklos-muller.md)

**The [mitosomes](../../../../../mitosome.md) retain selected essential metabolic functions but do not generate [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) by [oxidative phosphorylation](../../../../../oxidative-phosphorylation.md).** In [Giardia](../../../../../giardia.md), their role includes [iron-sulfur cluster](../../../../../iron-sulfur-cluster.md) assembly and [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) maturation, demonstrated using organelle markers and biochemical reconstitution.[Abstract: localization and iron-sulfur protein maturation](../../../../../mitochondrial-remnant-organelles-of-giardia-function-in-iron-sulphur-protein-maturation-by-jorge-tovar-and-others.md) Their functions are lineage-dependent: [Entamoeba histolytica](../../../../../entamoeba-histolytica.md) [mitosomes](../../../../../mitosome.md) compartmentalize a [sulfate activation](../../../../../sulfate-activation.md) pathway.[Abstract and Results: mitosome fractionation, protein localization and activated sulfate products](../../../../../mitosomes-in-entamoeba-histolytica-contain-a-sulfate-activation-pathway-by-fumika-mi-ichi-and-others.md) Thus neither organelle class should be described simply as a nonfunctional [mitochondrion](../../../../../mitochondria.md).
