<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Incubate a labelled precursor [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) containing an authentic $3'$-processing signal with a [HeLa](../../../../../hela.md) nuclear extract, then resolve the products by [denaturing polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis](../../../../../denaturing-polyacrylamide-gel-electrophoresis.md). With [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md), the upstream cleavage product acquires a heterogeneous [poly(A) tail](../../../../../poly-a-tail.md). Replace [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) with an appropriate non-incorporable analogue, such as alpha,beta-methylene-ATP: a discrete product ending at the normal cleavage site still appears, but the extended tail does not. Thus **cleavage can occur when [polyadenylation](../../../../../polyadenylation.md) is blocked**; it is not cleavage brought about by synthesis of the tail.[Abstract: cleavage without polyadenylation in the presence of alpha,beta-methylene-ATP](../../../../../accurate-cleavage-and-polyadenylation-of-exogenous-rna-substrate-by-claire-l-moore-and-phillip-a-sharp.md)

Conversely, supply an [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) substrate already ending at the normal cleavage site: addition of [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) and the appropriate [poly(A) polymerase](../../../../../poly-a-polymerase.md) machinery can extend its free $3'$ hydroxyl without any new endonucleolytic cut.[Abstract: efficient polyadenylation of pre-cleaved RNA substrates in nuclear extract](../../../../../the-aauaaa-sequence-is-required-both-for-cleavage-and-for-polyadenylation-of-simian-virus-40-pre-mrna-in-vitro-by-david-zarkower-and-others.md) Compare precursor, pre-cleaved substrate, no-extract and no-[ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) controls, and map the product ends to distinguish correct processing from non-specific degradation. **These are separable reactions, normally coordinated in one processing complex.** Their separability does not imply that they use completely independent recognition factors.
