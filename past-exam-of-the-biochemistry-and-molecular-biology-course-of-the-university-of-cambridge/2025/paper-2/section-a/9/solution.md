<h1 id="section-a/9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

An [antibody-drug conjugate](../../../../../antibody-drug-conjugate.md) couples an [antibody](../../../../../antibody.md) against a tumor-associated surface [antigen](../../../../../antigen.md) to a potent cytotoxic payload through a suitable chemical linker. Binding enriches delivery to [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) displaying the target. Many designs undergo receptor-mediated [endocytosis](../../../../../endocytosis.md), trafficking through [endosomes](../../../../../endosome.md) to a [lysosome](../../../../../lysosome.md), and release of an active drug through linker cleavage or degradation of the [antibody](../../../../../antibody.md). The released payload can disrupt [microtubules](../../../../../microtubule.md) or damage [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), killing the target [biological cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). **Specificity comes from targeted delivery combined with controlled payload release.** It is relative rather than absolute: normal [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) may share the [antigen](../../../../../antigen.md), and a membrane-permeable released payload can also affect neighboring [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Successful action therefore depends on antigen abundance, accessibility, internalization, linker stability and payload sensitivity.
