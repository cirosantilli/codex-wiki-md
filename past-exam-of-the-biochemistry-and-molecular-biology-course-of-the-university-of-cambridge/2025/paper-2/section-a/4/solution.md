<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The enzyme [adenylate kinase](../../../../../adenylate-kinase.md) rapidly interconverts the adenine [nucleotides](../../../../../nucleotide.md):

$$
2\mathrm{ADP}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{ATP}+\mathrm{AMP},\qquad K=\frac{[\mathrm{ATP}][\mathrm{AMP}]}{[\mathrm{ADP}]^2}.
$$

For approximately fixed conditions and near-equilibrium activity ratios,

$$
\frac{[\mathrm{AMP}]}{[\mathrm{ATP}]}=K\left(\frac{[\mathrm{ADP}]}{[\mathrm{ATP}]}\right)^2.
$$

**The AMP:ATP ratio responds quadratically to ADP:ATP.** Doubling ADP:ATP therefore quadruples AMP:ATP. In working [skeletal muscle](../../../../../skeletal-muscle.md), [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) is also buffered by [creatine kinase](../../../../../creatine-kinase.md) and continuing [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) production, so its fractional change is relatively small. The initially smaller [AMP](../../../../../adenosine-monophosphate.md) pool can undergo a much larger fractional increase, providing a sensitive signal of energetic stress to regulators such as [AMP-activated protein kinase](../../../../../amp-activated-protein-kinase.md). This concerns sensitivity of relative changes; it does not mean that the absolute [AMP](../../../../../adenosine-monophosphate.md) concentration exceeds [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md).
