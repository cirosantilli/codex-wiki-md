↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The [mitochondrial fission-fusion cycle](../../../../../mitochondrial-fission-fusion-cycle.md) is continuous remodeling of a mitochondrial population by division and merger. In mammalian [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), [mitofusins](../../../../../mitofusin.md) promote fusion of outer mitochondrial membranes, while [OPA1](../../../../../opa1.md) supports fusion of inner mitochondrial membranes. Fusion mixes matrix contents and permits functional complementation between [mitochondria](../../../../../mitochondria.md). Genetic disruption and rescue of [mitofusins](../../../../../mitofusin.md) demonstrated their requirement for normal mitochondrial fusion.[Abstract and Results: mitofusin loss, fragmented mitochondria and rescue](../../../../../mitofusins-mfn1-and-mfn2-coordinately-regulate-mitochondrial-fusion-and-are-essential-for-embryonic-development-by-hsiuchen-chen-and-others.md)

For fission, recruited [Drp1](../../../../../drp1.md) assembles at constrictions and uses [GTP](../../../../../guanosine-triphosphate.md)-dependent remodeling to help divide the [organelle](../../../../../organelle.md).[Abstract and Results: Drp1 recruitment and division defects](../../../../../dynamin-related-protein-drp1-is-required-for-mitochondrial-division-in-mammalian-cells-by-elena-smirnova-and-others.md) **The balance controls network shape, distribution and quality control.** Division can help distribute [mitochondria](../../../../../mitochondria.md) to daughter [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) and segregate damaged portions for [mitophagy](../../../../../mitophagy.md); fusion can restore mixed contents. These are regulated competing events in a network, not an obligatory synchronized sequence that every [mitochondrion](../../../../../mitochondria.md) completes on a fixed timetable.
