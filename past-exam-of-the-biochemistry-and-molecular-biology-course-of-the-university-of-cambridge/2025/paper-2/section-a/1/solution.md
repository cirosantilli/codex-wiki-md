For a primary [sigma factor](../../../../../sigma-factor.md) of the $\sigma^{70}$ family, distinguish the following regions. Region 1.1 restrains inappropriate [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) binding by free [sigma factor](../../../../../sigma-factor.md) and must move out of the downstream-[DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) channel during formation of the open [RNA polymerase](../../../../../rna-polymerase.md) complex; region 1.2 contributes to recognition around the discriminator downstream of the $-10$ element. Domain 2 binds the [RNA polymerase](../../../../../rna-polymerase.md) core, recognizes the $-10$ [gene promoter](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md) element and helps melt the duplex and stabilize the exposed non-template strand. Domain 3 recognizes an extended $-10$ element; its region 3.2 projects toward the active-centre channel, helps position template and initiating substrates, and must be displaced as the nascent [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) grows. Domain 4 recognizes the $-35$ element through a [helix-turn-helix](../../../../../helix-turn-helix.md) motif and contacts the [RNA polymerase](../../../../../rna-polymerase.md) beta flap and some [transcription activators](../../../../../transcription-activator.md). **Domains 2 and 4 supply the principal $-10$ and $-35$ sequence recognition, with the other regions controlling opening and productive initiation.** This domain map concerns the $\sigma^{70}$ family, rather than every structurally distinct bacterial [sigma factor](../../../../../sigma-factor.md).[Abstract and structural analysis of sigma domains 2, 3 and 4](../../../../../structure-of-the-bacterial-rna-polymerase-promoter-specificity-sigma-subunit-by-elizabeth-a-campbell-and-others.md)
