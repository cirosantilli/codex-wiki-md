<h1 id="section-a/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

A [steroid hormone](../../../../../steroid-hormone.md) crosses the [lipid bilayer](../../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) and binds an intracellular [nuclear receptor](../../../../../nuclear-receptor.md). For a cytoplasmic [glucocorticoid receptor](../../../../../glucocorticoid-receptor.md), ligand binding alters association with [molecular chaperones](../../../../../chaperone-protein.md), exposes nuclear-targeting functions and promotes accumulation in the [cell nucleus](../../../../../cell-nucleus.md). Other [steroid hormone](../../../../../steroid-hormone.md) receptors already have substantial nuclear localization. The activated [receptor](../../../../../receptor-biochemistry.md) binds specific [hormone response elements](../../../../../hormone-response-element.md) in [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), often as a dimer, and changes recruitment of [transcription coactivators](../../../../../transcription-coactivator.md) or [transcription corepressors](../../../../../transcription-corepressor.md). These factors alter local [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) and recruitment or activity of the [RNA polymerase II](../../../../../rna-polymerase-ii.md) machinery. **The result is selective activation or repression of target [genes](../../../../../gene.md)**, followed by altered [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) and [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) abundance; the [hormone](../../../../../hormone.md) itself does not act as an [RNA polymerase](../../../../../rna-polymerase.md) or directly rewrite the [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence.
