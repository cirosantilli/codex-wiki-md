↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

[Fucci](../../../../../fucci.md) uses two fluorescent [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) fusions carrying cell-cycle-regulated degradation signals: a red/orange [Cdt1](../../../../../cdt1.md) fragment accumulates mainly in [G1 phase](../../../../../g1-phase.md), whereas a green [geminin](../../../../../geminin.md) fragment accumulates through [S phase](../../../../../s-phase.md), [G2 phase](../../../../../g2-phase.md) and much of [M phase](../../../../../m-phase.md). Opposing activities of [SCF ubiquitin ligase](../../../../../scf-ubiquitin-ligase.md) and [anaphase-promoting complex](../../../../../anaphase-promoting-complex.md) regulate reporter degradation through the [ubiquitin-proteasome system](../../../../../ubiquitin-proteasome-system.md); the fluorophores report those regulated stabilities rather than simply measuring total endogenous [Cdt1](../../../../../cdt1.md) and [geminin](../../../../../geminin.md).[Abstract and reporter-design experiments](../../../../../visualizing-spatiotemporal-dynamics-of-multicellular-cell-cycle-progression-by-asako-sakaue-sawano-and-others.md)

**Live imaging follows individual [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) through red, overlap and green states**, allowing measurements of phase timing, spatial patterns and responses to perturbation without repeatedly fixing the sample. [Flow cytometry](../../../../../flow-cytometry.md) can separate populations by reporter color. The basic two-color version does not independently resolve every phase, and a color transition is an indirect readout of [ubiquitin ligase](../../../../../ubiquitin-ligase.md) activity. Validate timing with [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) content or a [DNA replication](../../../../../dna-replication.md) marker and control for altered reporter expression, fluorophore maturation and drug effects on degradation.
