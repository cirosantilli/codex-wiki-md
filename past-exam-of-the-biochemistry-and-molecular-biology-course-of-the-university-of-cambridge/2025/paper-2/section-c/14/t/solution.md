<h1 id="section-c/14/t/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [T](../t.md)

**An increased colorectal-cancer risk is mechanistically plausible in the supplied model, but not demonstrated by these data.** Reduced normal TCF-long production and a shift toward TCF-short could weaken a differentiated-cell expression program and favor proliferation. This resembles one functional consequence of truncating long products, but the splice-site allele and repeat [frameshift mutations](../../../../../../frameshift-mutation.md) are distinct changes, and the allele need not itself impair [DNA mismatch repair](../../../../../../dna-mismatch-repair.md). The pancreatic [HNF1β](../../../../../../hnf1-beta.md) reporter result cannot establish the mechanism in colon [epithelium](../../../../../../epithelium.md), where the relevant cofactors and targets differ.

Determine actual allele-specific isoforms and total [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) in colon tissue, test proliferation and differentiation in matched edited cells or [organoids](../../../../../../organoid.md), and compare allele frequencies in well-controlled case and population samples. **The table of repeat mutations supplies no direct association test for this SNP.** The designation as a diabetes-risk allele alone does not establish either the direction or size of a colorectal-cancer association.
