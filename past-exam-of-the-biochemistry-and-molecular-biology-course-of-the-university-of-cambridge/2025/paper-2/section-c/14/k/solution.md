↑ **Parent:** [K](../k.md)

**It is the free labelled [DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) probe**, not bound in the slower-moving [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md)–[DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) complexes. Its smaller effective hydrodynamic size allows faster migration. Excess unbound probe commonly gives a strong lower band; a probe-only lane would verify this assignment and distinguish it from a labelled degradation product.
