<h1 id="section-c/14/j/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [J](../j.md)

Prepare nuclear extract from the specified pancreatic [cell line](../../../../../../cell-line.md), retaining [DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)-binding activity and protecting against degradation. Make and label a double-stranded [DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) probe containing the two candidate binding sites and their flanks. Incubate equal probe amounts with extract under suitable binding conditions, including non-specific competitor [DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Prepare the extract-only binding reaction and separate reactions containing anti-[TCF transcription factor](../../../../../../tcf-transcription-factor.md) or anti-[HNF1β](../../../../../../hnf1-beta.md) [antibody](../../../../../../antibody.md); keep other conditions and input amounts matched. Resolve the reactions by non-denaturing [polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis](../../../../../../polyacrylamide-gel-electrophoresis.md) and detect the labelled probe. **Binding changes probe mobility; addition of an appropriate [antibody](../../../../../../antibody.md) can produce a supershift.** Include probe integrity and a probe-only reference, and avoid denaturing reagents that destroy the complexes being measured.
