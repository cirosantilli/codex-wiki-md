<h1 id="section-c/14/o/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [O](../o.md)

Co-transfect a reference [reporter gene](../../../../../../reporter-gene.md) expressed from a suitable constitutive promoter, such as a [Renilla luciferase](../../../../../../renilla-luciferase.md) construct, alongside the experimental [firefly luciferase](../../../../../../firefly-luciferase.md) reporter. **Normalize experimental luminescence to the reference signal** to reduce variation in transfection, cell number and recovery. The reference promoter must be insensitive to the tested [Wnt signalling](../../../../../../wnt-signalling.md) and [HNF1β](../../../../../../hnf1-beta.md) manipulations; otherwise the denominator introduces an apparent effect. Keep total transfected [DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) comparable and confirm that the assay signals are within their measurable range.
