<h1 id="section-c/14/q/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Q](../q.md)

**There is no large, consistent stimulation by added [Wnt protein](../../../../../../wnt-protein.md) ligand in the displayed experiment.** The paired bars are close in the empty-vector and TCF-long conditions; TCF-short shows a modest upward difference, while some other pairs decrease slightly. The error bars and absence of raw replicate information do not permit a firm significance claim. This result concerns the particular reporter and transfection conditions, not every aspect of endogenous [Wnt signalling](../../../../../../wnt-signalling.md). A known ligand-responsive positive control, dose/time testing and validation of receptor/pathway competence would distinguish a genuinely weak response from failed stimulation or saturation caused by transgene expression.
