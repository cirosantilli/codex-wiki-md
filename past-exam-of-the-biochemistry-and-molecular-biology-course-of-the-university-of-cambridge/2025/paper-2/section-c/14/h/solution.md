<h1 id="section-c/14/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

The ascending-colon tumors carry the exon-5 [frameshift mutation](../../../../../../frameshift-mutation.md) in $3/4$ sampled patients, transverse tumors in $1/2$, and descending and sigmoid tumors in $0/2$ each. Grouping these gives **$4/6$ proximal tumors versus $0/4$ distal tumors with the repeat alteration**, suggesting proximal enrichment of this molecular subtype. The matched samples show a one-allele alteration in the adjacent tissue of patients 1 and 7, whereas patients 5 and 9 have tumor-associated alterations without a detected alteration in their matched normal sample.

This is a small case series, and all ten patients already have tumors. **It cannot determine whether a region has a higher absolute risk of transformation**, nor establish defective [DNA mismatch repair](../../../../../../dna-mismatch-repair.md) from one altered repeat alone. Larger location-matched series, population denominators, testing of additional [microsatellites](../../../../../../microsatellite.md) and direct assessment of [DNA mismatch repair](../../../../../../dna-mismatch-repair.md) components are needed to support those stronger claims.
