<h1 id="section-c/14/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The repetitive tract can cause [DNA polymerase slippage](../../../../../../dna-polymerase-slippage.md) during [PCR amplification](../../../../../../polymerase-chain-reaction.md) or the sequencing extension itself, generating stutter products. A heterozygous length variant creates overlapping [Sanger sequencing](../../../../../../sanger-sequencing.md) traces downstream of the [indel](../../../../../../indel.md) because the two templates lose register; this does not by itself indicate random sequencing failure. Tumor heterogeneity or contamination by normal tissue can add further mixed templates. **Avoid creating or obscuring the mutation during amplification and avoid interpreting a mixed trace as one clean allele.** Use a suitable high-fidelity [DNA polymerase](../../../../../../dna-polymerase.md), independent amplifications and bidirectional reads, and resolve individual alleles by molecular cloning or an appropriate single-molecule/amplicon sequencing method if necessary. Check for non-specific products and allelic dropout caused by variation in a primer-binding site.
