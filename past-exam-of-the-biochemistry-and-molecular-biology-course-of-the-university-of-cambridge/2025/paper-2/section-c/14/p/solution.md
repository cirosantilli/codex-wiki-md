<h1 id="section-c/14/p/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [P](../p.md)

The empty expression [plasmid](../../../../../../plasmid.md) controls for introducing the vector and transfection reagents without adding either [TCF transcription factor](../../../../../../tcf-transcription-factor.md) isoform. It establishes reporter activity supported by endogenous factors and, when used to balance DNA amounts, controls total plasmid load between conditions. **Changes relative to this baseline can then be attributed more specifically to the expressed transgene.** It is not a substitute for an independent normalization reporter or for checking that the two transgenes produce comparable amounts of functional [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md).
