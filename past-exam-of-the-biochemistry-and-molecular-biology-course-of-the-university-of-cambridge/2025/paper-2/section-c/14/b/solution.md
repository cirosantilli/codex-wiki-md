<h1 id="section-c/14/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Two desirable properties are **similar [primer melting temperatures](../../../../../../primer-melting-temperature.md)**, allowing one annealing condition to support both primers efficiently, and **specific binding with little self-complementarity or mutual complementarity**, especially at the $3'$ ends, to reduce off-target products and [primer dimers](../../../../../../primer-dimer.md). Suitable length and a balanced [GC content](../../../../../../gc-content.md) help achieve these goals. A long homopolymer, strong hairpin or repeated genomic binding site would compromise a candidate even if its calculated melting temperature looked acceptable.
