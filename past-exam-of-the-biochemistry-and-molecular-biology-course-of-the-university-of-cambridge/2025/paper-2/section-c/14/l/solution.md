<h1 id="section-c/14/l/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [L](../l.md)

A strong specificity control is competition with a large excess of unlabelled cognate promoter [DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). It should reduce the shifted labelled complexes, whereas an unrelated or binding-site-mutant competitor should compete much less effectively. **This tests sequence-dependent association rather than non-specific probe retention.** A probe-only lane identifies the free probe, and an irrelevant isotype-matched [antibody](../../../../../../antibody.md) controls whether any added [antibody](../../../../../../antibody.md) produces aggregation or altered mobility. These controls address different uncertainties and should not be treated as interchangeable.
