<h1 id="section-c/14/s/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [S](../s.md)

Replacing the acceptor's A with C changes the canonical $3'$ [splice site](../../../../../../splice-site.md) from AG to CG. **The predicted primary effect is impaired recognition of the exon-5 acceptor**, reducing normal exon-5 inclusion. If the processing machinery instead selects exon 6, TCF-long would decrease and TCF-short would increase, removing the long-specific corepressor-interaction sequence from more of the translated products. Other outcomes are possible, including a cryptic acceptor, [intron retention](../../../../../../intron-retention.md), unstable transcripts or altered total expression; the nucleotide change does not uniquely determine one mature product. Test allele-specific [RNA splicing](../../../../../../rna-splicing.md) by junction assays and sequencing, with matched alleles or an edited endogenous locus. Unlike a repeat [indel](../../../../../../indel.md), this is initially a splice-site perturbation rather than necessarily a coding-region [frameshift mutation](../../../../../../frameshift-mutation.md).
