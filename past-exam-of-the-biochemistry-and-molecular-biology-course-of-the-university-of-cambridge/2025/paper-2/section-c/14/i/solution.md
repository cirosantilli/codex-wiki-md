<h1 id="section-c/14/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The lower region can contain both the genuine TCF-short product and a truncated product derived from the exon-5-containing TCF-long transcript. A repeat [frameshift mutation](../../../../../../frameshift-mutation.md) creates an early [stop codon](../../../../../../stop-codon.md), producing a long-derived product close in size to TCF-short. The N-terminal [antibody](../../../../../../antibody.md) recognizes their shared retained region. **The two nearby bands need not represent two copies of the normal short splice product.** In patient 1's healthy sample, one mutated allele permits a truncated product alongside intact long and short products; in the tumor, mutation of both alleles explains loss of the intact long band while short-sized products remain. Test the assignment with an exon-5/C-terminal-specific reagent or peptide identification; phosphorylation or other modifications could also create doublets, so mobility alone is not definitive.
