<h1 id="section-c/14/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A practical design uses one common exon-4 forward [PCR primer](../../../../../../pcr-primer.md) with an exon-specific reverse [PCR primer](../../../../../../pcr-primer.md) for each [transcript isoform](../../../../../../transcript-isoform.md). Candidate sequences, all written $5'$ to $3'$, are

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_4&:\quad\mathrm{GCGCTTTGGCCTTGATCAAC},\\
R_5&:\quad\mathrm{AGCTTCCATCTGAAGAGGGT},\\
R_6&:\quad\mathrm{CTTGTATGTAGCGAACTCCG}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Use **$F_4/R_5$ for TCF-long and $F_4/R_6$ for TCF-short**: $R_5$ recognizes exon 5, while $R_6$ recognizes exon 6. The displayed mature reference sequences predict approximately $130$- and $67$-base-pair products, respectively. Check actual splice products because a transcript with unexpected exon combinations could defeat the assumed specificity. The simple melting-temperature estimates are approximately $62$, $60$ and $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

Prepare matched [RNA](../../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), remove contaminating [DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), carry out [reverse transcription](../../../../../../reverse-transcription.md) and validate each [RT-qPCR](../../../../../../rt-qpcr.md) assay with known isoform standards, a single-product check, a no-template control and a no-reverse-transcriptase control. The primers span an [intron](../../../../../../intron.md) in the genomic arrangement but do not themselves cross a [splice junction](../../../../../../splice-junction.md), so the DNA control remains important. **Calibrate amplification efficiency and fluorescence response before comparing molecule numbers between the two assays.** A redesigned junction-specific assay is another option once additional suitable flanking sequence is available.
