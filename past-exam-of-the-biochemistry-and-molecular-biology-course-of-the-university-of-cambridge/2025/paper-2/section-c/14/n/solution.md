<h1 id="section-c/14/n/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [N](../n.md)

Include both specific [antibodies](../../../../../../antibody.md) in the same [electrophoretic mobility shift assay](../../../../../../electrophoretic-mobility-shift-assay.md) and compare with each single-antibody reaction and isotype controls. **An additional double supershift of one defined complex would support simultaneous occupancy**, especially when reproduced with purified [TCF transcription factor](../../../../../../tcf-transcription-factor.md), [HNF1β](../../../../../../hnf1-beta.md) and one probe containing the paired sites. Mutating each site separately tests the relevant binding requirement. Failure to obtain a double supershift is not decisive because antibody binding may be sterically incompatible or disruptive. For the native promoter, sequential [chromatin immunoprecipitation](../../../../../../chromatin-immunoprecipitation.md) with the two factors, with reciprocal order and background controls, can test co-association with the same promoter-containing chromatin fragment; it does not alone prove direct contact or base-specific binding.
