<h1 id="section-c/14/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

**A housekeeping Ct is unnecessary for the within-sample short:long ratio**, because a common reference cancels. With matched doubling efficiencies,

$$
2^{-[(C_{t,S}-C_{t,\mathrm{TBP}})-(C_{t,L}-C_{t,\mathrm{TBP}})]}=2^{C_{t,L}-C_{t,S}}.
$$

However, a validated reference such as [TATA-binding protein](../../../../../../tata-binding-protein.md) transcript is needed when comparing the abundance of either isoform separately between samples while correcting for input, [RNA](../../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) recovery and [reverse transcription](../../../../../../reverse-transcription.md). Reference stability must be tested under the differentiation conditions. Neither reference normalization nor cancellation removes the need to validate the two isoform assays' efficiencies and response calibration.
