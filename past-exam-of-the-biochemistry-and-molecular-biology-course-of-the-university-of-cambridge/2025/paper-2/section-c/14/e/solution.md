<h1 id="section-c/14/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Use [RNA sequencing](../../../../../../rna-sequencing.md) and quantify reads spanning the exon-4/exon-5 and exon-4/exon-6 [splice junctions](../../../../../../splice-junction.md), with appropriate normalization for informative read opportunities. These distinguish production of the long- and short-associated [transcript isoforms](../../../../../../transcript-isoform.md) rather than merely measuring total [TCF transcription factor](../../../../../../tcf-transcription-factor.md) transcripts. Adequate junction coverage and biological replication are required; [long-read RNA sequencing](../../../../../../long-read-rna-sequencing.md) can independently check whether the assumed complete isoforms are present. This avoids relying on a comparison of raw Ct values from two different [PCR primer](../../../../../../pcr-primer.md) pairs.
