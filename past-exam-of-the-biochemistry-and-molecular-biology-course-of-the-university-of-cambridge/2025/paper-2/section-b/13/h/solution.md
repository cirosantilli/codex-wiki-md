<h1 id="section-b/13/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

[PDGFR](../../../../../../pdgfr.md) is a [receptor tyrosine kinase](../../../../../../receptor-tyrosine-kinase.md); its key ligand-induced autophosphorylation is on [tyrosine residues](../../../../../../tyrosine-residue.md). Ras-PST25 recognizes phosphorylated [serine residues](../../../../../../serine-residue.md) and [threonine residues](../../../../../../threonine-residue.md), so **it is not the appropriate reagent to detect the characteristic activated [PDGFR](../../../../../../pdgfr.md) phosphotyrosines**. Use a phosphotyrosine or validated phospho-[PDGFR](../../../../../../pdgfr.md) [antibody](../../../../../../antibody.md), ideally alongside total [PDGFR](../../../../../../pdgfr.md). The larger [receptor](../../../../../../receptor-biochemistry.md) may also require a different gel and transfer range, but changing the size range alone would not correct the epitope mismatch.
