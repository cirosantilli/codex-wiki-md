↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The healthy sample establishes the basal and normal stimulated patterns for the same [cell type](../../../../../../cell-type.md) and handling conditions. Sample A contains bands around $96$ and $30\,\mathrm{kDa}$ in both conditions; [PDGF](../../../../../../pdgf.md) additionally induces bands near $73$, $62$ and $44$–$45\,\mathrm{kDa}$. These are compatible, respectively, with stimulated [Raf](../../../../../../raf.md), [Akt](../../../../../../protein-kinase-b.md) and unresolved [MEK](../../../../../../mek.md)/[ERK](../../../../../../erk.md) signals, given the supplied masses. **Ligand-dependent appearance of those bands is the normal comparison for suspected constitutive signaling.** Their identities remain provisional until confirmed with target-specific reagents. The unchanged bands are useful internal observations but cannot be assumed to be validated loading controls merely because their intensities look similar.
