<h1 id="section-b/13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Culture each patient and control sample under matched conditions, including equivalent growth-factor withdrawal where appropriate, then prepare paired unstimulated and [PDGF](../../../../../../pdgf.md)-stimulated samples with the same stimulation time. Rapidly lyse on ice with suitable detergents and [protease](../../../../../../protease.md) and [phosphatase](../../../../../../phosphatase.md) inhibitors. Measure [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) concentration and load equal amounts after [SDS-PAGE](../../../../../../sds-page.md) sample preparation. Separate the [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md) with molecular-mass markers, transfer them to a suitable membrane, verify transfer and block non-specific binding. Incubate with the Ras-PST25 [primary antibody](../../../../../../primary-antibody.md), wash, add a detectable [secondary antibody](../../../../../../secondary-antibody.md), wash again and record the signal within its linear range. **Preserve the original [phosphorylation](../../../../../../phosphorylation.md) state and compare equal, verifiably transferred inputs.** Include a total-[protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) loading measure, appropriate [antibody](../../../../../../antibody.md) controls and independent biological replicates; use matched exposures rather than independently rescaling lanes.
