<h1 id="section-b/13/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use a total-[protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) stain or validated constitutive loading control, and measure total amounts of the candidate signaling [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md) alongside their site-specific phosphorylated forms. This distinguishes increased phosphorylation fraction from increased protein abundance. Include a no-primary or secondary-only control and a phosphatase-treated aliquot to test phospho-dependent detection. **Confirm candidate identities using validated target-specific phospho-[antibodies](../../../../../../antibody.md)**, suitable positive controls and, where feasible, depletion or inhibition of the relevant target. Repeat the paired time course with biological replicates and non-saturated imaging. Matching culture state, stimulus dose and sampling time is essential because an ordinary difference in response kinetics could otherwise mimic constitutive activation.
