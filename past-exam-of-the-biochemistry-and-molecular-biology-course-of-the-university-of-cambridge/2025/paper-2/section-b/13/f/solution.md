<h1 id="section-b/13/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

[mTOR](../../../../../../mechanistic-target-of-rapamycin.md) has the supplied molecular mass of about $259\,\mathrm{kDa}$, so optimize the separation and transfer for a much larger [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md). Use a suitably low-percentage or gradient [SDS-PAGE](../../../../../../sds-page.md) gel and high-mass markers, and verify efficient transfer with a protocol appropriate for large [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md), such as optimized wet transfer. **Probe with a validated phospho-[mTOR](../../../../../../mechanistic-target-of-rapamycin.md) reagent and measure total [mTOR](../../../../../../mechanistic-target-of-rapamycin.md) in parallel.** The broad Ras-PST25 reagent cannot be assumed to recognize a useful site on [mTOR](../../../../../../mechanistic-target-of-rapamycin.md) merely because it recognizes other pathway [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md). Include a positive pathway control and a suitable inhibition or phosphatase control; a missing band on the existing blot could reflect poor separation or transfer rather than absent [mTOR](../../../../../../mechanistic-target-of-rapamycin.md) phosphorylation.
