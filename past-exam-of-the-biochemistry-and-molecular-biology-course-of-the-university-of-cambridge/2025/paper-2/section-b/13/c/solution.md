<h1 id="section-b/13/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The most informative difference is phosphorylation without [PDGF](../../../../../../pdgf.md), relative to sample A. The following are candidate interpretations of the supplied [Western blot](../../../../../../western-blot.md), not definitive clinical diagnoses or protein identifications:

- **B: evidence consistent with constitutive signaling in the [phosphoinositide 3-kinase](../../../../../../phosphoinositide-3-kinase.md)–[Akt](../../../../../../protein-kinase-b.md) branch.** A basal band near $62\,\mathrm{kDa}$ is compatible with [Akt](../../../../../../protein-kinase-b.md), while the apparent [Raf](../../../../../../raf.md) and [MEK](../../../../../../mek.md)/[ERK](../../../../../../erk.md) bands still require stimulation.
- **C: evidence consistent with constitutive [Raf](../../../../../../raf.md)–[MEK](../../../../../../mek.md)–[ERK](../../../../../../erk.md) pathway output.** The basal $44$–$45\,\mathrm{kDa}$ band is compatible with [MEK](../../../../../../mek.md) or [ERK](../../../../../../erk.md); basal [Raf](../../../../../../raf.md) phosphorylation is not evident, so the lesion need not be in [Raf](../../../../../../raf.md) itself.
- **D: evidence consistent with excess signaling in both branches.** Basal bands compatible with [Raf](../../../../../../raf.md), [Akt](../../../../../../protein-kinase-b.md) and [MEK](../../../../../../mek.md)/[ERK](../../../../../../erk.md) resemble the stimulated healthy pattern.
- **E: an abnormal pattern requiring further identification.** The additional bands near $85$ and $35\,\mathrm{kDa}$ are constitutive. The larger could be the [PI3K regulatory subunit p85](../../../../../../pi3k-regulatory-subunit-p85.md), making altered [phosphoinositide 3-kinase](../../../../../../phosphoinositide-3-kinase.md) signaling a candidate, but basal [Akt](../../../../../../protein-kinase-b.md) and [MEK](../../../../../../mek.md)/[ERK](../../../../../../erk.md) outputs are not clearly increased. Neither mass matching nor phosphorylation of a regulatory subunit alone proves pathway activation or a [Rasopathy](../../../../../../rasopathy.md).
- **F: no excess signaling is demonstrated under these conditions.** Its basal and induced patterns resemble A; this single assay cannot exclude every possible [Rasopathy](../../../../../../rasopathy.md) mechanism.

A single broad phospho-[antibody](../../../../../../antibody.md) cannot reliably distinguish the closely sized [MEK](../../../../../../mek.md) and [ERK](../../../../../../erk.md) bands or determine whether any phosphorylation site is activating. Clinical assessment and genetic and functional confirmation remain necessary.
