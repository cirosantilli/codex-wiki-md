<h1 id="section-b/13/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Analyze the corresponding region from a parallel preparative [SDS-PAGE](../../../../../../sds-page.md) gel, digest the recovered [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md) and identify peptides by [liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry](../../../../../../liquid-chromatography-mass-spectrometry.md). The lane and gel region should match the $35\,\mathrm{kDa}$ signal, with an adjacent-background or control-sample region processed in parallel. Because several [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md) can comigrate, **validate the candidate against the actual phospho-band**, for example by immunoprecipitating the candidate and testing Ras-PST25 reactivity, using a target-specific [Western blot](../../../../../../western-blot.md), or showing disappearance of the band after specific depletion. Phosphopeptide identification provides additional evidence; mass alone or detecting a common contaminant in the excised region is insufficient.
