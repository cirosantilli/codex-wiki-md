<h1 id="section-b/12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Hexokinase](../../../../../../hexokinase.md) consumes exported [ATP](../../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) in the reaction

$$
\mathrm{glucose+ATP\longrightarrow glucose\ 6\!\! -\!phosphate+ADP}.
$$

The [adenine nucleotide translocator](../../../../../../adenine-nucleotide-translocator.md) imports regenerated [ADP](../../../../../../adenosine-diphosphate.md), permitting [ATP synthase](../../../../../../atp-synthase.md) to use the [proton-motive force](../../../../../../proton-motive-force.md). Increased proton return reduces the opposing gradient and allows faster activity of the [electron transport chain](../../../../../../electron-transport-chain.md) and oxygen consumption: this is [respiratory control](../../../../../../respiratory-control.md). The first [hexokinase](../../../../../../hexokinase.md) additions therefore increase demand and respiration. **By about the fourth addition, demand no longer limits the measured flux**: the respiratory and transport system has reached its available capacity, so more [hexokinase](../../../../../../hexokinase.md) cannot increase it. This does not imply that [hexokinase](../../../../../../hexokinase.md) ceases functioning, and its zero incremental control at maximal demand does not make it dispensable for maintaining that demand.
