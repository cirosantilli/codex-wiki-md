<h1 id="section-b/12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [ADP](../../../../../../adenosine-diphosphate.md) addition contains

$$
n_{\mathrm{ADP}}=(10\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm L)(20\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}})=0.200\,\mathrm{\mu mol}.
$$

During the initial stimulated interval, the plotted [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) concentration falls from about $240$ to $207\,\mathrm{\mu M}$. The chamber volume after this addition is $1.900+0.050+0.050+0.010=2.010\,\mathrm{mL}$, giving

$$
\Delta n_{\mathrm{O_2}}\simeq33\,\mathrm{\mu mol\,L^{-1}}\times0.002010\,\mathrm L=0.0663\,\mathrm{\mu mol}.
$$

The conventional [phosphorylation-to-oxidation ratio](../../../../../../phosphorylation-to-oxidation-ratio.md) counts [ATP](../../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) formed per oxygen atom reduced, so its denominator is twice the number of oxygen molecules:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{P/O}\simeq\frac{0.200}{2(0.0663)}\simeq1.5.}
$$

This assumes the added [ADP](../../../../../../adenosine-diphosphate.md) is phosphorylated and uses the displayed total oxygen loss over the pulse; it is an apparent experimental ratio. A separate correction for background respiration or [proton leak](../../../../../../proton-leak.md) would change the denominator and must be specified explicitly. Dividing by oxygen molecules without the factor of two would instead give an ATP:$\mathrm O_2$ ratio near $3$.
