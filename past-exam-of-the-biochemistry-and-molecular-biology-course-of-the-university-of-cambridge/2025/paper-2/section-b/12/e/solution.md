↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [mitochondrial dicarboxylate carrier](../../../../../../mitochondrial-dicarboxylate-carrier.md) is an inner-membrane transport [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) that exchanges suitable dicarboxylates, including [succinate](../../../../../../succinate.md) and [malate](../../../../../../malate.md), with counter-substrates such as [phosphate](../../../../../../phosphate.md). In this experiment, it supplies external [succinate](../../../../../../succinate.md) to the matrix side of [respiratory complex II](../../../../../../succinate-dehydrogenase.md); the [inner mitochondrial membrane](../../../../../../inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md) does not simply allow unrestricted diffusion of the charged [substrate](../../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md). **Without adequate uptake, substrate delivery would restrict respiration even if the respiratory enzymes remained intact.** Its essentiality is substrate- and preparation-dependent: it is not the obligatory entry route for every possible respiratory fuel.
