<h1 id="section-b/12/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

Titrate the omitted activities using small perturbations around the same steady-state condition, measuring oxygen consumption and calculating local slopes rather than assigning control from complete inhibition. For example, suitable [ATP synthase](../../../../../../atp-synthase.md), [succinate dehydrogenase](../../../../../../succinate-dehydrogenase.md) and carrier perturbations could test their contributions, with independent checks of inhibitor selectivity and activity loss. **Measure [ATP](../../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) production alongside oxygen consumption**, as well as [mitochondrial membrane potential](../../../../../../mitochondrial-membrane-potential.md), to separate a change in phosphorylation efficiency from a change in electron flux. Check chamber oxygen availability, mitochondrial integrity, substrate abundance and assay reproducibility.

Repeat the control measurements across [ADP](../../../../../../adenosine-diphosphate.md) demands and with defined alternative substrates. Carefully titrated [FCCP](../../../../../../fccp.md) can test respiratory capacity when proton-gradient backpressure is relieved, but the uncoupled state is a different operating condition and does not itself reveal the coupled-state coefficients. Fit the measured local sensitivities with uncertainty and test whether the expanded set satisfies the [flux summation theorem](../../../../../../flux-summation-theorem.md). This distinguishes omitted control from technical inconsistency and shows whether the distribution changes with demand.
