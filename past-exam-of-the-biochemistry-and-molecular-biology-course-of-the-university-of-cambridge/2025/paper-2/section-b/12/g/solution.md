Important unlisted candidates include [ATP synthase](../../../../../../atp-synthase.md), the [mitochondrial phosphate carrier](../../../../../../mitochondrial-phosphate-carrier.md), and [succinate dehydrogenase](../../../../../../succinate-dehydrogenase.md) itself, which supplies electrons from [succinate](../../../../../../succinate.md) to [ubiquinone](../../../../../../ubiquinone.md). Availability or transfer of [ubiquinone](../../../../../../ubiquinone.md) and [cytochrome c](../../../../../../cytochrome-c.md), and associated transport or metabolite constraints, may also need explicit treatment depending on how the pathway is grouped. **ATP synthesis, phosphate supply and substrate oxidation have not all been individually represented.** [Respiratory complex I](../../../../../../respiratory-complex-i.md) is absent from the table but is not required for the direct forward route from [succinate](../../../../../../succinate.md) into the [ubiquinone](../../../../../../ubiquinone.md) pool; its omission alone does not account for missing control of that route.
