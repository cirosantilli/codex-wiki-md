<h1 id="section-b/12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**[Succinate](../../../../../../succinate.md) is the most plausible substrate.** Its oxidation by [succinate dehydrogenase](../../../../../../succinate-dehydrogenase.md), or [respiratory complex II](../../../../../../succinate-dehydrogenase.md), supplies electrons to the [ubiquinone](../../../../../../ubiquinone.md) pool, bypassing [respiratory complex I](../../../../../../respiratory-complex-i.md). Electron passage through [respiratory complex III](../../../../../../respiratory-complex-iii.md) and [cytochrome c oxidase](../../../../../../cytochrome-c-oxidase.md) supports a lower phosphorylation yield than entry through [NADH](../../../../../../nadh.md) at [respiratory complex I](../../../../../../respiratory-complex-i.md), consistent with an apparent [phosphorylation-to-oxidation ratio](../../../../../../phosphorylation-to-oxidation-ratio.md) around $1.5$. The later involvement of a [mitochondrial dicarboxylate carrier](../../../../../../mitochondrial-dicarboxylate-carrier.md) is additional evidence because [succinate](../../../../../../succinate.md) is one of its transported substrates. The ratio alone is not a chemically unique identification: other electron-entry routes and altered coupling can also produce a similar yield. Confirm by comparing defined substrates and sensitivity to a [succinate dehydrogenase](../../../../../../succinate-dehydrogenase.md) inhibitor.
