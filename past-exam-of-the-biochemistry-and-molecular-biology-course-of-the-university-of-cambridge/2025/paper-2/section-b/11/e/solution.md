<h1 id="section-b/11/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The dilution in the [Bradford assay](../../../../../../bradford-assay.md) is $1000/5=200$. Read the fitted line rather than assuming it passes through the origin: the two absorbances correspond approximately to $4.3$ and $15\,\mathrm{\mu g\,mL^{-1}}$ in the assay mixture. Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
c_{\mathrm{TEM1}}&\simeq200(4.3)=860\,\mathrm{\mu g\,mL^{-1}}=0.86\,\mathrm{mg\,mL^{-1}},\\
c_{\mathrm{TEM52}}&\simeq200(15)=3000\,\mathrm{\mu g\,mL^{-1}}=3.0\,\mathrm{mg\,mL^{-1}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Multiplying by the $0.500\,\mathrm{mL}$ eluate volume gives **about $0.43\,\mathrm{mg}$ of [TEM-1](../../../../../../tem-1.md) and $1.5\,\mathrm{mg}$ of [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md)**. Only [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md) meets the requirement.

Assuming the same recovery per culture volume, the required volumes are

$$
V_{\mathrm{culture,1}}\simeq50\frac{1}{0.43}\simeq116\,\mathrm{mL},\qquad V_{\mathrm{culture,52}}\simeq50\frac{1}{1.5}\simeq33\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$

**Use approximately $120\,\mathrm{mL}$ or more for [TEM-1](../../../../../../tem-1.md); the existing $50\,\mathrm{mL}$ is sufficient for [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md).** Rounding the former up to $125\,\mathrm{mL}$ is sensible given graphical precision. Reading the lower standard as $4$–$4.5\,\mathrm{\mu g\,mL^{-1}}$ gives a corresponding requirement of roughly $110$–$125\,\mathrm{mL}$, not an exact integer volume. These estimates assume comparable dye response to [bovine serum albumin](../../../../../../bovine-serum-albumin.md), suitable buffer blanks and that recovered [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) is the purified target; contaminants would inflate the inferred target yield.
