↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For the [Hanes-Woolf plot](../../../../../../hanes-woolf-plot.md), the slope is $1/V_{\max}$, the vertical intercept is $K_m/V_{\max}$, and the horizontal intercept is $-K_m$. The lines have approximately equal slopes, $0.50\,\mathrm{min\,\mu M^{-1}}$. The dashed [TEM-1](../../../../../../tem-1.md) line intercepts the vertical axis at about $13\,\mathrm{min}$, while the dotted [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md) line intercepts it at about $2.5\,\mathrm{min}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{V_{\max,1}\simeq V_{\max,52}\simeq2.0\,\mathrm{\mu M\,min^{-1}},\quad K_{m,1}\simeq26\,\mathrm{\mu M},\quad K_{m,52}\simeq5\,\mathrm{\mu M}.}
$$

The negative-axis intercepts independently give approximately $-26$ and $-5\,\mathrm{\mu M}$. Allow normal graph-reading uncertainty, especially at the smaller intercept. **The limiting rates are similar; the [Michaelis constant](../../../../../../michaelis-constant.md) is substantially lower for [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md).** A [Michaelis constant](../../../../../../michaelis-constant.md) is not generally a binding [dissociation constant](../../../../../../dissociation-constant.md), and calculating a [turnover number](../../../../../../turnover-number.md) additionally requires the concentration of active [enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md).
