<h1 id="section-b/11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The catalytic [serine residue](../../../../../../serine-residue.md) supplies a [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md): its side-chain oxygen attacks the carbonyl carbon of the [beta-lactam](../../../../../../beta-lactam.md) ring. This generates a tetrahedral intermediate, followed by opening of the ring and formation of a covalent [acyl-enzyme intermediate](../../../../../../acyl-enzyme-intermediate.md). Subsequent attack by activated [water](../../../../../../water.md) hydrolyzes that intermediate and regenerates the [enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md). **The [serine residue](../../../../../../serine-residue.md) performs covalent catalysis and is regenerated, rather than being consumed as a reagent.**
