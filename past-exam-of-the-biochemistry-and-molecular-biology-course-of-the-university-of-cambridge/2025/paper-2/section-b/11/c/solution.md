<h1 id="section-b/11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Bond c**, the carbonyl-carbon–nitrogen bond within the four-membered [beta-lactam](../../../../../../beta-lactam.md) ring, is cleaved. [Hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md) opens the cyclic [amide](../../../../../../amide.md); it does not cut the side-chain amide or a carbon–carbon bond. The resulting open-ring product no longer has the intact [beta-lactam](../../../../../../beta-lactam.md) pharmacophore required for its usual antibacterial action.
