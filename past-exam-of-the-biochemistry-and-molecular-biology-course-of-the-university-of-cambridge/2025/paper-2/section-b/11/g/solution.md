<h1 id="section-b/11/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

The displayed separation between the two entrance landmarks increases from approximately $6.2$ to $7.7\,\text{\AA}$ in [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md), indicating a wider opening. The Gly238-to-Ser substitution can alter backbone packing and loop geometry; its effect is not simply the steric size of the new side chain. Increased accessibility or altered productive positioning could increase the effective association rate for [penicillin G](../../../../../../penicillin-g.md), producing a lower apparent [Michaelis constant](../../../../../../michaelis-constant.md) without changing the limiting catalytic step. In a simple [Michaelis-Menten kinetics](../../../../../../michaelis-menten-kinetics.md) scheme, $K_m=(k_{-1}+k_{\mathrm{cat}})/k_1$, so increasing $k_1$ can lower $K_m$.

**The structural change is consistent with improved access and the lower measured $K_m$, while similar $V_{\max}$ suggests similar limiting turnover under these conditions.** It does not uniquely establish the mechanism or prove tighter equilibrium binding. [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md) contains additional substitutions, so a matched single-substitution series would be needed to isolate the contribution of residue 238. Structural work on [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md) independently supports the importance of remodeling access to its [active site](../../../../../../active-site.md).[Abstract and TEM-52 structural analysis: access remodeling and the E104K/M182T/G238S combination](../../../../../../predicting-the-emergence-of-antibiotic-resistance-by-directed-evolution-and-structural-analysis-by-m-c-orencia-and-others.md)
