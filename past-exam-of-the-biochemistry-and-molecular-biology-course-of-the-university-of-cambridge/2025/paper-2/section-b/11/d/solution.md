<h1 id="section-b/11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The compound [imidazole](../../../../../../imidazole.md) resembles the metal-coordinating part of the [histidine](../../../../../../histidine.md) side chain and competes for binding to the [nickel](../../../../../../nickel.md) sites in [immobilized metal affinity chromatography](../../../../../../immobilized-metal-affinity-chromatography.md). At the lower concentration in the wash, it helps displace weakly or non-specifically bound [proteins](../../../../../../protein-split.md) while the multiple contacts of the [histidine tag](../../../../../../histidine-tag.md) retain the desired [enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md). At the higher elution concentration, competition is strong enough to displace the tagged [enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md). **Low [imidazole](../../../../../../imidazole.md) improves washing; high [imidazole](../../../../../../imidazole.md) elutes the target.** Remove or match the elution-buffer components before interpreting downstream assays, and verify purity independently of total [protein](../../../../../../protein-split.md) concentration.
