↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An appropriately positioned [general base](../../../../../../general-base.md) helps remove the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) from the [serine residue](../../../../../../serine-residue.md) hydroxyl, increasing its nucleophilicity. A [lysine residue](../../../../../../lysine-residue.md), such as Lys73 in the class A [beta-lactamase](../../../../../../beta-lactamase.md) catalytic network, is a plausible participant; its protonation state depends on its active-site environment and interactions with other residues. A [glutamate residue](../../../../../../glutamate-residue.md), notably Glu166, also participates in proton transfer and activation of the [water](../../../../../../water.md) needed for deacylation. **Efficient catalysis requires proton-transfer chemistry as well as positioning the attacking oxygen.** The precise division of roles during acylation depends on the [enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md) state, so the drawing alone cannot assign a complete mechanism to one residue.[Abstract and active-site protonation analysis](../../../../../../probing-the-role-of-the-conserved-residue-glu166-in-a-class-a-beta-lactamase-using-neutron-and-x-ray-protein-crystallography-by-patricia-s-langan-and-others.md)
