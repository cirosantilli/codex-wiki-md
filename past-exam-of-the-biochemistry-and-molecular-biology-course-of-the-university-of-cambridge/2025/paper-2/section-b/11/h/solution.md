<h1 id="section-b/11/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

The bulky substituents of [ceftazidime](../../../../../../ceftazidime.md) can hinder productive entry into the more restricted [TEM-1](../../../../../../tem-1.md) [active site](../../../../../../active-site.md). A wider entrance in [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md) could accommodate this [substrate](../../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) and allow its [beta-lactam](../../../../../../beta-lactam.md) ring to be hydrolyzed. **The prediction is reduced susceptibility of [TEM-52](../../../../../../tem-52.md)-expressing [bacteria](../../../../../../bacteria.md) compared with matched [TEM-1](../../../../../../tem-1.md)-expressing [bacteria](../../../../../../bacteria.md).** This is a mechanistic prediction, not a quantitative conclusion from the [penicillin G](../../../../../../penicillin-g.md) data: entry alone does not guarantee efficient catalytic chemistry for a different [substrate](../../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md). Test [ceftazidime](../../../../../../ceftazidime.md) [hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md) directly and measure its [minimum inhibitory concentration](../../../../../../minimum-inhibitory-concentration.md) in an otherwise matched bacterial background, controlling [enzyme](../../../../../../enzyme-split.md) expression, growth, drug entry and other resistance mechanisms.
