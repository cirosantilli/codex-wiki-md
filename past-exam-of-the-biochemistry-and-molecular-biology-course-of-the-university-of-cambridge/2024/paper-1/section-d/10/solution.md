↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

**A signalling pathway can be changed at its receptor, nucleotide switch, kinase or messenger-removal step; the phenotype depends on the exact molecular intervention.** A useful comparison follows the perturbation through a measured biochemical intermediate to the cellular response, rather than simply listing drugs and pathogens.

[imatinib](../../../../../imatinib.md) inhibits the deregulated [BCR-ABL](../../../../../bcr-abl.md) tyrosine kinase responsible for a major proliferative signal in [chronic myeloid leukaemia](../../../../../chronic-myeloid-leukaemia.md). Occupancy of its kinase ATP-binding region favours an inactive configuration and reduces substrate [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) and downstream survival signalling. Early cellular experiments compared BCR-ABL-expressing cells with cells lacking that driver and measured growth and [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md), showing the value of a molecularly defined target.[Druker and colleagues, 1996, abstract and BCR-ABL-positive versus negative cellular experiments](../../../../../effects-of-a-selective-inhibitor-of-the-abl-tyrosine-kinase-on-the-growth-of-bcr-abl-positive-cells-by-brian-j-druker-and-others.md) Inhibition need not mean complete specificity: related kinases can also be affected, and target-site mutations or alternative survival pathways can confer resistance. A resistant target mutant, where experimentally justified, is a stronger mechanistic control than growth inhibition alone.

At a cell-surface receptor, a beta-adrenoceptor antagonist reduces catecholamine activation of a [G protein-coupled receptor](../../../../../g-protein-coupled-receptor.md) and hence its downstream response. In contrast, inhibiting a [phosphodiesterase](../../../../../phosphodiesterase.md) can prolong a cyclic-nucleotide signal after it has been generated. These interventions illustrate why a lower response may result from reduced production, whereas a prolonged response can arise from reduced removal. Binding measurements, second-messenger time courses and downstream [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) distinguish the two.

[cholera toxin](../../../../../cholera-toxin.md) hijacks a nucleotide switch rather than competing with extracellular ligand. Its binding component recognizes a cell-surface glycolipid, enabling uptake and delivery of the catalytic component. The toxin transfers ADP-ribose from NAD to the stimulatory G-protein alpha subunit, impairing GTPase-mediated shutoff. Persistent stimulation of [adenylyl cyclase](../../../../../adenylyl-cyclase.md) increases [cyclic AMP](../../../../../cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) and [protein kinase A](../../../../../protein-kinase-a.md) activity. In intestinal epithelium, increased [CFTR](../../../../../cftr.md)-dependent chloride secretion promotes salt and water loss. Early experiments linked toxin-dependent modification of a membrane GTP-binding [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) to increased adenylate-cyclase activity; the later intestinal pathway supplies the physiological interpretation.[Gill and Meren, 1978, abstract and pp. 3050–3054, protein modification correlated with adenylate-cyclase activity](../../../../../adp-ribosylation-of-membrane-proteins-catalyzed-by-cholera-toxin-basis-of-the-activation-of-adenylate-cyclase-by-d-m-gill-and-others.md)

[pertussis toxin](../../../../../pertussis-toxin.md) also catalyses ADP-ribosylation, but acts on inhibitory Gi/o-family alpha subunits and prevents normal receptor coupling. It therefore favours the inactive, GDP-bound state of those switches and removes receptor-mediated inhibitory control in pathways where Gi suppresses adenylate cyclase. It must not be described as simply locking every G [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) in its active state: the two toxins can increase [cyclic AMP](../../../../../cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) through different molecular routes. Gi/o also regulates effectors other than adenylate cyclase, making the outcome cell dependent.

Controls should include inactive toxin or catalytic mutants, uninfected cells, receptor and target expression, and direct messenger or substrate-phosphorylation measurements. Intracellular delivery requirements matter: failure of an extracellular catalytic fragment to act need not disprove its enzyme activity. Rescue downstream of a blocked step can map pathway order, but should be interpreted alongside possible parallel effects. **Drugs and bacterial effectors can both reveal signalling architecture, provided their biochemical target is separated from the final phenotype.**
