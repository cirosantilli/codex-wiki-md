<h1 id="section-d/11/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [11](../11.md)

**Selective destruction makes key transitions depend on rebuilding [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), rather than merely reversing their [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) state.** [ubiquitin-proteasome system](../../../../../ubiquitin-proteasome-system.md) supplies this directionality while [cyclin-dependent kinases](../../../../../cyclin-dependent-kinase.md) and phosphatases provide more rapidly reversible regulation. An [ubiquitin-activating enzyme](../../../../../ubiquitin-activating-enzyme.md) uses [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) to activate [ubiquitin](../../../../../ubiquitin.md); transfer through an [ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme](../../../../../ubiquitin-conjugating-enzyme.md) and recognition by an [ubiquitin ligase](../../../../../ubiquitin-ligase.md) directs substrate modification. Suitable polyubiquitin chains target [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) to the [proteasome](../../../../../proteasome.md) for unfolding and destruction. Substrate recognition, rather than a general increase in [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) breakdown, gives temporal specificity.

At the G1/S transition, an [SCF ubiquitin ligase](../../../../../scf-ubiquitin-ligase.md) can recognize a phosphorylated inhibitor of a cyclin-dependent kinase. In budding yeast, multisite [phosphorylation](../../../../../phosphorylation.md) of [Sic1](../../../../../sic1.md) promotes its destruction, releasing S-phase kinase activity. This links a threshold of previous kinase activity to removal of an inhibitor. Different SCF substrate receptors recognize different [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), and phosphorylation-dependent recognition is important for many, but not every, SCF target. Destruction reinforces entry into the new state while the kinase network supplies additional feedback.

The [anaphase-promoting complex](../../../../../anaphase-promoting-complex.md) controls a particularly decisive transition. Its [Cdc20](../../../../../cdc20.md)-associated form is restrained by the [spindle checkpoint](../../../../../spindle-checkpoint.md) until chromosome attachment conditions permit progression. Ubiquitination and destruction of [securin](../../../../../securin.md) release [separase](../../../../../separase.md), which cleaves the [cohesin](../../../../../cohesin.md) component that holds sister chromatids together. Chromatid separation cannot be undone by putting the phosphate groups back onto an intact [securin](../../../../../securin.md) molecule, because that molecule has been degraded and the cohesion linkage has been cleaved. The combination of selective proteolysis and substrate cleavage explains why a brief regulatory transition commits the cell to anaphase.

Destruction of [cyclin B](../../../../../cyclin-b.md) lowers [CDK1](../../../../../cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) activity and enables mitotic exit. Nondegradable cyclin blocked inactivation of maturation-promoting activity and exit from [mitosis](../../../../../mitosis.md) in frog experimental systems, directly testing necessity of cyclin destruction rather than merely observing a correlation.[Murray, Solomon and Kirschner, 1989, abstract and nondegradable-cyclin interventions](../../../../../the-role-of-cyclin-synthesis-and-degradation-in-the-control-of-maturation-promoting-factor-activity-by-andrew-w-murray-and-others.md) The [Cdh1](../../../../../cdh1.md)-associated anaphase-promoting complex helps keep mitotic cyclins low through late [mitosis](../../../../../mitosis.md) and G1, creating an interval suitable for replication licensing. In animal cells, destruction of [geminin](../../../../../geminin.md) also permits [Cdt1](../../../../../cdt1.md) to support licensing. Subsequent kinase activation and licensing-factor regulation help prevent rereplication before the next cycle.

The mechanism is irreversible on the timescale of the transition, not an assertion that the cell can never make cyclin or [securin](../../../../../securin.md) again. ATP-dependent [ubiquitin](../../../../../ubiquitin.md) activation and proteasomal processing occur in an open, energy-consuming system; fresh [gene expression](../../../../../gene-expression.md) later restores substrates under a different regulatory state. Conversely, ubiquitination alone is not synonymous with degradation: chain type and context can give other signals. Tests should compare normal and degron-mutant substrates at similar expression levels, monitor actual [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) loss and kinase activity, and distinguish checkpoint arrest from failure of destruction itself. **Proteolysis commits the cell by removing selected regulators, while checkpoint and feedback circuits determine when that commitment occurs.**
