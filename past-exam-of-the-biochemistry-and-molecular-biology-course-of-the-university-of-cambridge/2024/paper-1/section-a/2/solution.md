**PCR converts a selected [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence into a detectable and manipulable quantity; different downstream measurements give it different uses.** A pair of [PCR primers](../../../../../pcr-primer.md) defines the interval amplified by [DNA polymerase](../../../../../dna-polymerase.md). Repeated denaturation, annealing and extension produce approximately $N_n=N_0(1+E)^n$ copies while [PCR efficiency](../../../../../pcr-efficiency.md) $E$ remains constant. At ideal efficiency, twenty cycles give about a millionfold amplification; depletion and accumulating products eventually cause a [PCR plateau](../../../../../pcr-plateau.md). Specificity derives from both primer-binding sites and their orientation, and should be checked by product size, melting behaviour or sequencing.

One application is [genotyping](../../../../../genotyping.md). A disease-associated substitution can be detected by amplifying the surrounding region and reading the sequence, probing with an allele-specific oligonucleotide, or digesting a restriction site changed by the variant. An early demonstration amplified the human beta-globin region for restriction-based diagnosis of [sickle cell disease](../../../../../sickle-cell-disease.md). Amplification increased sensitivity; the restriction pattern supplied the allele information.[Saiki and colleagues, 1985, abstract: amplification followed by restriction-site analysis](../../../../../enzymatic-amplification-of-beta-globin-genomic-sequences-and-restriction-site-analysis-for-diagnosis-of-sickle-cell-anemia-by-r-k-saiki-and-others.md) A negative disease-specific signal is interpretable only if a control locus amplifies, because poor extraction or an inhibitor can mimic absence of an allele. In [forensic DNA profiling](../../../../../forensic-dna-profiling.md), several [short tandem repeat](../../../../../short-tandem-repeat.md) loci are amplified and fragment lengths compared. A multilocus match supports identity statistically; it does not eliminate contamination, mixtures, allelic dropout or the need for appropriate population-frequency estimates.

For infectious agents, primers can detect a pathogen-specific [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence. An [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) virus first requires [reverse transcription](../../../../../reverse-transcription.md), followed by [polymerase chain reaction](../../../../../polymerase-chain-reaction.md). In [RT-qPCR](../../../../../rt-qpcr.md), fluorescence is monitored during amplification, and a threshold cycle is related to starting [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) using calibrated standards and efficiency measurements. This also measures changes in cellular [gene expression](../../../../../gene-expression.md): compare a target transcript between conditions after suitable normalization. Include extraction controls, a no-template reaction and, for [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) measurements, a no-reverse-transcriptase control to detect genomic [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) contamination. A positive pathogen signal establishes the presence of target nucleic acid, **not necessarily a viable organism or infectious virus**. Likewise, [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) abundance is not a direct measurement of [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) abundance or transcription rate, since [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) degradation also matters.

PCR is also a preparative method. Amplified coding sequences can be assembled into expression vectors; primer tails can add restriction sites, assembly overlaps, sequencing adaptors or an affinity tag. High-fidelity polymerase reduces unwanted substitutions, but the final clone should still be sequenced. [Site-directed mutagenesis](../../../../../site-directed-mutagenesis.md) introduces a specified substitution using mutagenic primers, whereas [error-prone PCR](../../../../../error-prone-pcr.md) creates a library for [directed evolution](../../../../../directed-evolution.md). Sequencing after selection distinguishes the intended change from other mutations. In [chromatin immunoprecipitation](../../../../../chromatin-immunoprecipitation.md), quantitative amplification of recovered loci can test enrichment of a DNA-associated [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md), with input [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and immunoprecipitation controls needed to interpret it.

These examples separate presence, identity, quantity and construction. Endpoint band intensity is a poor substitute for quantitative amplification measured before the [PCR plateau](../../../../../pcr-plateau.md); multiplex assays can also suffer unequal amplification. The appropriate answer therefore identifies **what is amplified, what is measured, and which alternative explanation each control excludes**.
