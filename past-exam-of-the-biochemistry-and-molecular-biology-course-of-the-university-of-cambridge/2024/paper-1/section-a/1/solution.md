<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**A guide specifies the target; the attached [Cas9](../../../../../cas9.md) machinery determines whether expression changes through [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) repair or direct transcriptional regulation.** In [CRISPR genome editing](../../../../../crispr-genome-editing.md), a [guide RNA](../../../../../guide-rna.md) supplies sequence complementarity to a genomic target adjacent to a suitable [protospacer adjacent motif](../../../../../protospacer-adjacent-motif.md). The commonly used [Cas9](../../../../../cas9.md) from [Streptococcus pyogenes](../../../../../streptococcus-pyogenes.md) recognizes an NGG motif, where N is any base. Recognition involves both that motif and pairing between the guide and target [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md); an arbitrary complementary sequence is therefore insufficient. In mammalian experiments, a nuclear-localized [Cas9](../../../../../cas9.md) and guide can be delivered by expression constructs or as a preassembled [ribonucleoprotein](../../../../../ribonucleoprotein.md). Multiplexing means supplying several guides, rather than engineering a new DNA-binding [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) for every target. The original mammalian demonstration combined programmable targeting with endogenous repair to change genomic sequences.[Cong and colleagues, 2013, abstract and results on endogenous mammalian targeting](../../../../../multiplex-genome-engineering-using-crispr-cas-systems-by-le-cong-and-others.md)

For a loss-of-function experiment, a cut in an early constitutive [exon](../../../../../exon.md) is repaired by [non-homologous end joining](../../../../../non-homologous-end-joining.md) and related end-joining pathways. Insertions or deletions can change the [reading frame](../../../../../reading-frame.md), introduce a premature stop, and trigger [nonsense-mediated decay](../../../../../nonsense-mediated-decay.md). Neither cleavage nor an insertion/deletion automatically proves a [gene knockout](../../../../../gene-knockout.md): repair can preserve the reading frame, alternative splicing can bypass the exon, and a mammalian cell may retain an intact allele. Targeting a [promoter](../../../../../promoter-genetics.md) or [enhancer](../../../../../enhancer.md) can instead alter the amount or cell specificity of expression. Two guides can delete a defined regulatory segment, but the resulting junction and possible larger rearrangements need verification. [homology-directed repair](../../../../../homology-directed-repair.md) with a donor can introduce an expression tag, repair a mutation, or replace a regulatory sequence; its efficiency depends on cell state and competing repair pathways. Changing the genomic coding sequence can change [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) activity without changing transcript abundance, so these outcomes should be distinguished.

Expression can also be controlled without cleavage. Catalytically inactive [dCas9](../../../../../dcas9.md) retains guide-directed binding. Positioning it at an appropriate regulatory region can obstruct transcription; recruiting a repressive domain such as [KRAB](../../../../../krab.md) strengthens [CRISPR interference](../../../../../crispr-interference.md) in mammalian cells. Fusing an activation domain, or recruiting several activators, gives [CRISPR activation](../../../../../crispr-activation.md) at an endogenous promoter. These approaches preserve the coding sequence and permit graded or inducible perturbation, although [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) context and guide position strongly affect performance. Mammalian experiments comparing regulatory fusions established that the same targeting platform can repress or activate transcription.[Gilbert and colleagues, 2013, results on dCas9-based repression and activation in eukaryotic cells](../../../../../crispr-mediated-modular-rna-guided-regulation-of-transcription-in-eukaryotes-by-luke-a-gilbert-and-others.md)

The readout should match the proposed mechanism: sequence the target for editing, measure [messenger RNA](../../../../../messenger-rna.md) by [RT-qPCR](../../../../../rt-qpcr.md), and measure the [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) and its biological activity. Use multiple independent guides, a nontargeting guide, delivery-matched controls, and several independently derived clones or a controlled pooled experiment. Rescue with a guide-resistant functional gene supports causality; overexpression rescue alone can have dosage artefacts. Check relevant predicted [off-target effects](../../../../../off-target-effect.md), assess cell viability, and distinguish direct expression changes from selection of surviving cell states. A nuclease perturbation can itself activate a DNA-damage response. Thus **the strongest conclusion connects a verified molecular perturbation to [RNA](../../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) and phenotype**, rather than treating a guide-dependent phenotype as sufficient evidence.
