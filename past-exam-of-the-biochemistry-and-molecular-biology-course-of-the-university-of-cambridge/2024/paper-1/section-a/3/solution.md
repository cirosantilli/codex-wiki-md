<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Strong lac expression requires relief of lactose-responsive repression together with a favourable glucose-responsive activation state.** In [Escherichia coli](../../../../../escherichia-coli.md), the [lac operon](../../../../../lac-operon.md) encodes [beta-galactosidase](../../../../../beta-galactosidase.md), [lactose permease](../../../../../lactose-permease.md) and a transacetylase. [LacI](../../../../../laci.md) binds operator [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and represses transcription; binding of the inducer [allolactose](../../../../../allolactose.md) shifts its conformation and reduces operator occupancy. Basal expression provides enough permease and beta-galactosidase to import and process some [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md), including formation of [allolactose](../../../../../allolactose.md). This explains induction without requiring a completely silent system somehow to manufacture its own detector.

[Glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) availability affects positive regulation. When [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) is scarce, the physiological state of the [phosphotransferase system](../../../../../phosphotransferase-system.md) favours activation of adenylate cyclase and increased [cyclic AMP](../../../../../cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md). Binding of [cyclic AMP](../../../../../cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) activates [catabolite activator protein](../../../../../catabolite-activator-protein.md), also called CRP, which binds near the lac promoter and helps recruit [RNA polymerase](../../../../../rna-polymerase.md). In glucose-rich conditions this activation is reduced. Operator repression and activator-dependent recruitment act at distinct sites, so relief of repression does not guarantee maximum expression. The promoter behaves approximately as a requirement for [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md) availability AND [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) scarcity, but its quantitative output is graded rather than a perfect digital gate.

[Glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) also regulates inducer entry. During [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) uptake through the [phosphotransferase system](../../../../../phosphotransferase-system.md), the glucose-specific [EIIA glucose](../../../../../eiia-glucose.md) [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) tends to be dephosphorylated. That form can bind and inhibit [lactose permease](../../../../../lactose-permease.md), reducing [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md) entry and hence [allolactose](../../../../../allolactose.md) formation. This [inducer exclusion](../../../../../inducer-exclusion.md) reinforces repression while a preferred substrate is being used. Mutations in the central cytoplasmic loop of the permease that abolish regulation and binding provide a way of separating transport inhibition from promoter regulation.[Hoischen and colleagues, 1996, abstract and permease-loop mutagenesis results](../../../../../involvement-of-the-central-loop-of-the-lactose-permease-of-escherichia-coli-in-its-allosteric-regulation-by-the-glucose-specific-enzyme-iia-of-the-phosphoenolpyruvate-dependent-phosphotransferase-system-by-c-hoischen-and-others.md) It is therefore incomplete to attribute every [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) effect to [cyclic AMP](../../../../../cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) alone.

The four limiting conditions make the integration clear. Without [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md), [LacI](../../../../../laci.md) represses whether [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) is high or low; an active CRP cannot simply substitute for inducer. With [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md) and abundant [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md), induction is limited by weak activation and often by inducer exclusion. With [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md) and little [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md), repression is relieved and CRP-dependent recruitment is favoured, giving the highest expression. With both sugars initially available, [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) consumption followed by adaptation to [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md) can produce [diauxic growth](../../../../../diauxic-growth.md); the timing depends on concentrations, transport and culture conditions.

Genetic and physiological controls distinguish the mechanisms. A loss-of-function [LacI](../../../../../laci.md) mutation should remove inducer dependence and can be complemented by a functional repressor supplied in trans. An operator mutation acts primarily in cis, on the linked operon. A superrepressor that cannot respond appropriately to inducer can remain repressive despite [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md). Compare promoter reporters and lactose-uptake measurements separately, because an uptake defect and a transcription defect can give similar growth phenotypes. Adding [cyclic AMP](../../../../../cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) tests one activation arm, but cannot necessarily overcome a permease blocked by inducer exclusion. **The cell combines a local substrate signal with a global carbon-use signal, acting through both transcription and transport.**
