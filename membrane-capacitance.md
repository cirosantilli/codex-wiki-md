# Membrane capacitance

↑ **Parent:** [Whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md)

[Membrane capacitance](membrane-capacitance.md) is the [capacitance](capacitance.md) associated with separated [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) across [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md). A local ideal [capacitor](capacitor.md) has charging [electric current](electric-current.md) $I_C=C_m\,dV/dt$. If a known actual membrane voltage change is $\Delta V$, integrating the charging transient after subtracting sustained ionic and leak components gives $C_m=\Delta V^{-1}\int I_C\,dt$. Pipette [capacitance](capacitance.md), finite [patch-clamp series resistance](patch-clamp-series-resistance.md) and imperfect transient subtraction can bias the estimate. A capacitive transient is not by itself an [ion channel](ion-channel.md) opening.
