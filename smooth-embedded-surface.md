# Smooth embedded surface

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md)

A smooth embedded surface in $\mathbb R^3$ is a two-dimensional [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) locally represented by an injective regular [parametrized surface](parametrized-surface.md) $\sigma:U\subset\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^3$, with $\sigma_u\times\sigma_v\ne0$. The [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md) measures lengths and angles, and a choice of [unit normal](unit-normal.md) determines the [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md), which measures bending. A surface with boundary additionally uses local half-plane charts along its boundary.
