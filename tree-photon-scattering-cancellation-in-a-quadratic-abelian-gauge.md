# Tree photon-scattering cancellation in a quadratic Abelian gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic Abelian gauge fixing](quadratic-abelian-gauge-fixing.md)

The nonlinear [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) $\partial\cdot A+\lambda A^2$ produces cubic and quartic [photon](photon.md) [Feynman vertices](interaction-vertex.md). For physical [photon polarization vectors](photon-polarization-vector.md), the two-external-leg cubic vertex reduces to a longitudinal internal momentum times the scalar product of the external polarizations. Its exchanged [photon propagator](photon-propagator.md) cancels that momentum squared. Each of the three exchange graphs contributes $+4i\lambda^2(\varepsilon_i\cdot\varepsilon_j)(\varepsilon_k\cdot\varepsilon_l)$, cancelling the corresponding term of the quartic vertex. This vanishing physical [tree-level Feynman diagram](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) sum does not assert that the [off shell](off-shell.md) four-point function vanishes.
