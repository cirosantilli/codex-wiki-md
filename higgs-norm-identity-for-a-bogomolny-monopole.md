# Higgs norm identity for a Bogomolny monopole

↑ **Parent:** [Bogomolny-Prasad-Sommerfield monopole](bogomolny-prasad-sommerfield-monopole.md)

For the trace [inner product](inner-product.md) and [adjoint covariant derivative](adjoint-covariant-derivative.md), compatibility gives $\Delta|\Phi|^2=2|D\Phi|^2+2\langle\Phi,D_iD_i\Phi\rangle$. The [gauge-theory Bianchi identity](gauge-theory-bianchi-identity.md) and $B_i=\pm D_i\Phi$ imply $D_iD_i\Phi=0$. Therefore $\Delta|\Phi|^2=2|D\Phi|^2$, equal to the energy density $|B|^2+|D\Phi|^2$ on a [Bogomolny-Prasad-Sommerfield monopole](bogomolny-prasad-sommerfield-monopole.md). If the energy density is instead defined with an overall factor $1/2$, this Laplacian is twice that density.
