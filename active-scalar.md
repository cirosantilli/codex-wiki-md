# Active scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar transport](scalar-transport.md)

An [active scalar](active-scalar.md) is a transported [scalar field](scalar-field.md) that feeds back on the [velocity field](velocity-field.md), for example a density anomaly producing [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). In an [adjoint equations for Boussinesq scalar mixing](adjoint-equations-for-boussinesq-scalar-mixing.md) calculation its dynamical coupling must also be transposed; a passive-scalar sensitivity equation generally omits that feedback.
