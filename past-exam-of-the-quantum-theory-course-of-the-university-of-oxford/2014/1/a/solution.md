<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md), with the [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) for a particle of [mass](../../../../mass.md) $m$ in a real [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) $V$, is

$$
i\hbar\frac{\partial\Psi}{\partial t}=H\Psi,\qquad H=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}+V(x).
$$

A [stationary state](../../../../stationary-state.md) is an [energy eigenstate](../../../../energy-eigenstate.md): its [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) evolves by a single [global phase](../../../../global-phase.md),

$$
\Psi(x,t)=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar},\qquad \boxed{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\psi''+V\psi=E\psi.}
$$

Its [probability density](../../../../probability-density.md) is independent of time. For a normalized [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md), the [probability density](../../../../probability-density.md) and [probability current](../../../../probability-current.md) are

$$
\boxed{\rho=\Psi^*\Psi,\qquad j=\frac{\hbar}{2mi}(\Psi^*\partial_x\Psi-\Psi\partial_x\Psi^*)=\frac{\hbar}{m}\operatorname{Im}(\Psi^*\partial_x\Psi).}
$$

Subtracting the [complex conjugate](../../../../complex-conjugate.md) of the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md), multiplied by $\Psi$, from the original equation multiplied by $\Psi^*$ cancels the real [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) terms and gives the [continuity equation](../../../../continuity-equation.md) $\partial_t\rho+\partial_xj=0$. Thus $j$ measures signed probability flow in the positive $x$ direction.
