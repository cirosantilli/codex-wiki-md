<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a normalized [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md), the [expectation value](../../../../expectation-value.md) of a function of [position](../../../../position.md) is

$$
\mathbb E_\psi(g(x))=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}g(x)|\psi(x)|^2\,dx,
$$

provided the integral exists. For an unnormalized state, divide this expression by $\int|\psi|^2dx$. Put $b=m\omega/\hbar>0$. The [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) has [probability density](../../../../probability-density.md) $\sqrt{b/\pi}\,e^{-bx^2}$, which is [even](../../../../even-function.md). Thus

$$
\mathbb E_{\psi_0}(|x|)=2\sqrt{\frac b\pi}\int_0^\infty xe^{-bx^2}\,dx=2\sqrt{\frac b\pi}\left[-\frac{e^{-bx^2}}{2b}\right]_0^\infty=\boxed{\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{\pi m\omega}}}.
$$

The zero mean [position](../../../../position.md) $\mathbb E_{\psi_0}(x)=0$ expresses symmetry, whereas this positive [expectation value](../../../../expectation-value.md) measures the mean distance from the centre.
