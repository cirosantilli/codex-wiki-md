<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substitute the [power series](../../../../power-series.md) and collect each power of $\xi$:

$$
\sum_{k\geq0}\left[(k+2)(k+1)a_{k+2}+(\epsilon-1-2k)a_k\right]\xi^k=0.
$$

Matching the [power series](../../../../power-series.md) coefficients gives the [recurrence relation](../../../../recurrence-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{a_{k+2}=\frac{2k+1-\epsilon}{(k+2)(k+1)}a_k,\qquad k=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$

The [even](../../../../even-function.md) and [odd](../../../../odd-function.md) coefficients form separate sequences, determined by $a_0$ and $a_1$, respectively. For example, $a_2=(1-\epsilon)a_0/2$ and $a_3=(3-\epsilon)a_1/6$. These are the two independent [power series solutions of the Hermite differential equation](../../../../power-series-solutions-of-the-hermite-differential-equation.md).
