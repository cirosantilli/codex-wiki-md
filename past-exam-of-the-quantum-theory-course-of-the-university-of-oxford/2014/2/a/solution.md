<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The dimensionless coordinate for the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) gives $d/dx=\sqrt{m\omega/\hbar}\,d/d\xi$. The [time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) therefore becomes

$$
-\psi_{\xi\xi}+\xi^2\psi=\frac{2E}{\hbar\omega}\psi.
$$

For $\psi=e^{-\xi^2/2}f$, direct [differentiation](../../../../differentiation.md) yields

$$
\psi_{\xi\xi}=e^{-\xi^2/2}\left[f''-2\xi f'+(\xi^2-1)f\right].
$$

Cancelling the nonzero exponential reduces the equation to the [Hermite differential equation](../../../../hermite-differential-equation.md)

$$
f''-2\xi f'+(\epsilon-1)f=0,\qquad \boxed{\epsilon=\frac{2E}{\hbar\omega}.}
$$
