<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Define the reflected function $\phi(x)=\psi(-x)$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\phi'(x)=-\psi'(-x)$ and $\phi''(x)=\psi''(-x)$. Evaluating the [time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) for $\psi$ at $-x$ and using the [even function](../../../../../even-function.md) identity $V(-x)=V(x)$ gives

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\phi''(x)+V(x)\phi(x)=E\phi(x).
$$

Thus reflection preserves the [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md). The same substitution $y=-x$ in the [norm](../../../../../norm.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\phi(x)|^2\,dx=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\psi(-x)|^2\,dx=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\psi(y)|^2\,dy=1.}
$$

This is the action of the [parity operator](../../../../../parity-operator.md) $R\psi(x)=\psi(-x)$, which is a [unitary operator](../../../../../unitary-operator.md). For a reflection-symmetric physical problem it preserves the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md)'s [operator domain](../../../../../operator-domain.md) as well as its differential expression.
