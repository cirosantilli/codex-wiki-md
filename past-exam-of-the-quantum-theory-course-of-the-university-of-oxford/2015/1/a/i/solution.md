↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) is the [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) equation for the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md):

$$
\boxed{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2}+V(x)\psi(x)=E\psi(x).}
$$

Here $\hbar$ is the reduced [Planck constant](../../../../../planck-constant.md). A corresponding stationary [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) evolves as $\Psi(x,t)=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$, which follows by substitution in the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md). Its position [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) is time independent because the time factor has unit modulus. Physical [energy eigenstates](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) must also satisfy the system's [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md); for a [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md), [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) requires $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\psi(x)|^2\,dx=1$.
