<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At a finite jump in [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md), the [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) and its first [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) must be continuous. A jump in the derivative would create a [Dirac delta function](../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) in $\psi''$, for which the finite potential has no balancing term. For the even solution, matching at $x=a$ gives

$$
B\cos\beta=De^{-\alpha},\qquad -B\kappa\sin\beta=-kDe^{-\alpha},\qquad \alpha=ka,\quad\beta=\kappa a.
$$

A nonzero solution cannot have $B=0$. It also cannot have $\cos\beta=0$: the first condition would give $D=0$, while the second would force $B\kappa\sin\beta=0$, a contradiction. Division is therefore valid and yields

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\beta\tan\beta.}
$$

[Parity](../../../../../parity.md) makes the conditions at $x=-a$ identical to those already imposed. Finally the definitions of the decay and oscillation constants give

$$
\boxed{\alpha^2+\beta^2=a^2\left(-\frac{2mE}{\hbar^2}+\frac{2m(E+V_0)}{\hbar^2}\right)=\frac{2mV_0a^2}{\hbar^2}.}
$$

The circle relation fixes the well strength; the tangent relation selects its even [bound states](../../../../../bound-state.md).
