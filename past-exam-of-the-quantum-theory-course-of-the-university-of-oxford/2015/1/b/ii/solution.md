<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an [even function](../../../../../even-function.md), the interior sine term must vanish, because [sine](../../../../../sine.md) is odd and [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) is even. Comparing reflected exterior tails at any $x>a$ gives $De^{-kx}=Ae^{-kx}$. Hence the [parity](../../../../../parity.md) condition alone is

$$
\boxed{C=0,\qquad A=D.}
$$

It does not yet impose the matching condition on $B$. Continuity at $x=a$, when imposed in the next part, additionally gives $A=D=Be^{ka}\cos(\kappa a)$.
