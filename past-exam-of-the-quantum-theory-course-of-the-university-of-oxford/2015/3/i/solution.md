<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Introduce the reduced [radial wavefunction](../../../../radial-wavefunction.md) $u(r)=r\psi(r)$. Multiplying the [radial Schrödinger equation for a central potential](../../../../radial-schrodinger-equation-for-a-central-potential.md) by $-2mr/\hbar^2$ gives

$$
u''-\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{r^2}u+\frac{2mKZ}{\hbar^2r}u+\frac{2mE}{\hbar^2}u=0.
$$

For the decaying [bound state](../../../../bound-state.md), take $\alpha>0$ and put $u=f e^{-\alpha r}$. Differentiation yields

$$
u''=e^{-\alpha r}\left(f''-2\alpha f'+\alpha^2f\right).
$$

Since $2mE/\hbar^2=-\alpha^2$, the two constant multiples of $f$ cancel. Dividing by the nonzero exponential gives

$$
\boxed{f''-2\alpha f'-\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{r^2}f+\frac{2mKZ}{\hbar^2r}f=0.}
$$

This separates the large-radius exponential decay from the remaining [Coulomb bound state](../../../../coulomb-bound-state.md) equation.
