<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Twice applying the [creation operator](../../../../../creation-operator.md) or twice applying the [annihilation operator](../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) changes the [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) by $2\hbar\omega$, unless they give zero. The same [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) argument as in part (i) therefore gives

$$
\mathbb E_\psi(a_+^2)=0=\mathbb E_\psi(a_-^2).
$$

Also the factorization in part (a) gives

$$
\mathbb E_\psi(a_+a_-)=2m(E-\hbar\omega/2),\qquad \mathbb E_\psi(a_-a_+)=2m(E+\hbar\omega/2).
$$

Now $P=(a_++a_-)/2$ and $X=(a_+-a_-)/(2im\omega)$. Squaring in the correct operator order yields

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_\psi(P^2)&=\frac14\mathbb E_\psi(a_+^2+a_+a_-+a_-a_++a_-^2)=mE,\\
\mathbb E_\psi(X^2)&=-\frac1{4m^2\omega^2}\mathbb E_\psi(a_+^2-a_+a_--a_-a_++a_-^2)=\frac{E}{m\omega^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

By part (i), the means are zero, so these are the [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md). Taking positive square roots gives the requested [standard deviations](../../../../../standard-deviation.md):

$$
\boxed{\Delta_\psi(X)=\sqrt{\frac{E}{m\omega^2}},\qquad \Delta_\psi(P)=\sqrt{mE}.}
$$

These [harmonic-oscillator number-state second moments](../../../../../harmonic-oscillator-number-state-second-moments.md) also show equal mean [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md), each $E/2$. Their uncertainty product is $E/\omega=(n+\tfrac12)\hbar$, so the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) saturates the [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md).
