<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $a_-\psi\ne0$, it has energy $E-\hbar\omega$, distinct from the energy of $\psi$. [Eigenstates](../../../../../eigenstate.md) of a [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) with distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md). Explicitly,

$$
E\langle\psi,a_-\psi\rangle=\langle H\psi,a_-\psi\rangle=\langle\psi,H(a_-\psi)\rangle=(E-\hbar\omega)\langle\psi,a_-\psi\rangle,
$$

so $\langle\psi,a_-\psi\rangle=0$. If $a_-\psi=0$, the same conclusion is immediate.

Dividing by $\|\psi\|^2$ and using the definition of $a_-$ gives

$$
\mathbb E_\psi(P)-im\omega\mathbb E_\psi(X)=0.
$$

Both [expectation values](../../../../../expectation-value.md) are real because $P$ and $X$ are [self-adjoint operators](../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md). Its real and imaginary parts therefore vanish separately:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E_\psi(X)=0,\qquad \mathbb E_\psi(P)=0.}
$$

An oscillator [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) is centered in both position and momentum, despite having nonzero [quantum uncertainties](../../../../../quantum-uncertainty.md).
