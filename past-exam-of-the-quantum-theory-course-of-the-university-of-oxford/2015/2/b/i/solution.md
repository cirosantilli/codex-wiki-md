<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

From $H=a_+a_-/(2m)+\hbar\omega/2$ and the [commutator product rule](../../../../../commutator-product-rule.md),

$$
[H,a_+]=\frac1{2m}a_+[a_-,a_+]=\hbar\omega a_+,\qquad [H,a_-]=\frac1{2m}[a_+,a_-]a_-=-\hbar\omega a_-.
$$

Consequently, if $H\psi=E\psi$,

$$
\boxed{H(a_\pm\psi)=(E\pm\hbar\omega)(a_\pm\psi).}
$$

Thus the [creation operator](../../../../../creation-operator.md) raises the [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) by $\hbar\omega$, and the [annihilation operator](../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) lowers it by $\hbar\omega$, **provided the resulting vector is nonzero**. The zero vector is not an [eigenstate](../../../../../eigenstate.md); in particular the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) will be annihilated by $a_-$. The displayed operator identity holds even in that case.
