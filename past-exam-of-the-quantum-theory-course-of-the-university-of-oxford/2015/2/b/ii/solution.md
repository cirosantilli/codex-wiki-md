<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) is obtained by moving the [adjoint operator](../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) to the other side of the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md):

$$
\|a_-\psi\|^2=\langle\psi,a_+a_-\psi\rangle
=2m\left\langle\psi,\left(H-\frac12\hbar\omega\right)\psi\right\rangle.
$$

For an [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md), this is

$$
\boxed{\|a_-\psi\|^2=2m\left(E-\frac12\hbar\omega\right)\|\psi\|^2.}
$$

Since a squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) is nonnegative, every nonzero [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) has $E\geq\hbar\omega/2$. Moreover $a_-\psi=0$ exactly when $E=\hbar\omega/2$.
