↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The spatial [quantum phase](../../../../../quantum-phase.md) has unit modulus. Thus the [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) and total [probability](../../../../../probability.md) are

$$
|\Psi(x,0)|^2=\frac2a\sin^2\frac{\pi x}{a},\qquad \boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\Psi(x,0)|^2\,dx=\frac2a\int_0^a\sin^2\frac{\pi x}{a}\,dx=1.}
$$

The exterior contributes zero. This establishes [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md), although the spatial [quantum phase](../../../../../quantum-phase.md) means the state is not the ground [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md).
