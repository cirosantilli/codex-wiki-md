<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H=\omega J_3$ is already diagonal in the [angular momentum eigenstate](../../../../../angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) basis of part (b). Its three distinct [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) and [stationary states](../../../../../stationary-state.md) are

$$
\boxed{E_\mu=\hbar\omega\mu,\qquad
|\Psi_\mu(t)\rangle=e^{-i\omega\mu t}|1,\mu\rangle,\qquad \mu=1,0,-1.}
$$

Thus the three column [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) are $(1,0,0)^{\mathsf T}$, $(0,1,0)^{\mathsf T}$ and $(0,0,1)^{\mathsf T}$, with [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) $+\hbar\omega$, zero and $-\hbar\omega$, respectively. Each is nondegenerate since $\omega>0$. Any nonzero scalar multiple gives the same [stationary state](../../../../../stationary-state.md) after normalization; a [quantum superposition](../../../../../quantum-superposition.md) of different [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) is generally not stationary.
