<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose the ordered [angular momentum eigenstates](../../../../../angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) $(|1,1\rangle,|1,0\rangle,|1,-1\rangle)$, so $J_3=\hbar\operatorname{diag}(1,0,-1)$. Since $[J_3,J_\pm]=\pm\hbar J_\pm$, a nonzero [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) of $J_+$ must increase the [magnetic quantum number](../../../../../magnetic-quantum-number.md) by one, and one of $J_-$ must decrease it by one. Thus only entries $(1,2),(2,3)$ of $J_+$ and $(2,1),(3,2)$ of $J_-$ can be nonzero. The [adjoint operator](../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) identity $J_-=J_+^\dagger$ gives

$$
\boxed{J_+=\begin{pmatrix}0&A&0\\0&0&B\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
J_-=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\\overline A&0&0\\0&\overline B&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

For the $j=1$ [irreducible spin representation](../../../../../irreducible-spin-representation.md), both adjacent transitions are present; the following calculation determines their sizes.
