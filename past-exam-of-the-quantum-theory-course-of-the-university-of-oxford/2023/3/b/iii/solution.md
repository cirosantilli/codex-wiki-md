<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Recover the [angular momentum operators](../../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md) from $J_1=(J_++J_-)/2$ and $J_2=(J_+-J_-)/(2i)$. The [spin-one angular momentum matrices](../../../../../spin-one-angular-momentum-matrices.md) in the chosen descending-$J_3$ [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) are

$$
\boxed{J_1=\frac\hbar{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
J_2=\frac\hbar{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0&-i&0\\i&0&-i\\0&i&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
J_3=\hbar\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

They are [self-adjoint operators](../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md), satisfy $[J_i,J_j]=i\hbar\varepsilon_{ijk}J_k$, and give $J^2=2\hbar^2I$, as required for $j=1$.
