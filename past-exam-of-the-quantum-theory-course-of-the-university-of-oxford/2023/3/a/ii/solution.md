<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the ordered [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $(|\tfrac12,\tfrac12\rangle,|\tfrac12,-\tfrac12\rangle)$, the [spin one-half](../../../../../spin-one-half.md) [angular momentum operators](../../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md) are $J_i=(\hbar/2)\sigma_i$, with [Pauli matrices](../../../../../pauli-matrices.md)

$$
\boxed{J_1=\frac\hbar2\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
J_2=\frac\hbar2\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
J_3=\frac\hbar2\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The [ladder operators](../../../../../ladder-operator.md) are

$$
\boxed{J_+=\hbar\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
J_-=\hbar\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Explicit multiplication gives $J_+J_-=\hbar^2\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ and $J_-J_+=\hbar^2\operatorname{diag}(0,1)$. Their [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{[J_+,J_-]=\hbar^2\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}=2\hbar J_3.}
$$
