<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [angular momentum commutation relations](../../../../../angular-momentum-commutation-relations.md) are

$$
\boxed{[J_i,J_j]=i\hbar\sum_{k=1}^3\varepsilon_{ijk}J_k},\qquad
J^2=J_1^2+J_2^2+J_3^2,\qquad [J^2,J_i]=0,
$$

where $\varepsilon_{ijk}$ is the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md). The [angular momentum eigenstates](../../../../../angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) have

$$
\boxed{J^2|j,\mu\rangle=\hbar^2j(j+1)|j,\mu\rangle,\qquad
J_3|j,\mu\rangle=\hbar\mu|j,\mu\rangle,}
$$

with [angular momentum quantum number](../../../../../angular-momentum-quantum-number.md) $j=0,\tfrac12,1,\tfrac32,\ldots$ and [magnetic quantum number](../../../../../magnetic-quantum-number.md) $\mu=-j,-j+1,\ldots,j$. There are $2j+1$ such states in one [irreducible spin representation](../../../../../irreducible-spin-representation.md), so the $J^2$ [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) has [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $2j+1$ within that multiplet. The use of $\mu$ avoids confusing the [magnetic quantum number](../../../../../magnetic-quantum-number.md) with the particle [mass](../../../../../mass.md) in the earlier questions.

For completeness, the [ladder operators](../../../../../ladder-operator.md) $J_\pm=J_1\pm iJ_2$ satisfy $[J_3,J_\pm]=\pm\hbar J_\pm$ and

$$
\|J_\pm|j,\mu\rangle\|^2=\hbar^2\bigl[j(j+1)-\mu(\mu\pm1)\bigr].
$$

The upper and lower ends are $\mu=\pm j$; integer steps between them require $2j$ to be a nonnegative integer. This explains both the spectrum and the multiplet [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md). Pure [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) permits only integer $j$, whereas general [spin](../../../../../spin.md) allows half-integers. If a larger [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) contains several copies of the same [irreducible spin representation](../../../../../irreducible-spin-representation.md), the full $J^2$ [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) includes all those copies: its [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is $(2j+1)$ times their multiplicity, and can be infinite. The paper's dimension question is understood per irreducible multiplet.
