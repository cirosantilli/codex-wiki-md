<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L_3=X_1P_2-X_2P_1$ generates rotations in the plane. The [canonical commutation relations](../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) give

$$
[L_3,X_1]=i\hbar X_2,\qquad [L_3,X_2]=-i\hbar X_1,\qquad
[L_3,P_1]=i\hbar P_2,\qquad [L_3,P_2]=-i\hbar P_1.
$$

Use the [commutator product rule](../../../../commutator-product-rule.md) $[A,B^2]=[A,B]B+B[A,B]$. Since different coordinates commute,

$$
\begin{aligned}
[L_3,P_1^2+P_2^2]&=2i\hbar P_1P_2-2i\hbar P_2P_1=0,\\
[L_3,\omega_1^2X_1^2+\omega_2^2X_2^2]
&=2i\hbar(\omega_1^2-\omega_2^2)X_1X_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus, for arbitrary positive [angular frequencies](../../../../angular-frequency.md),

$$
[L_3,H]=i\hbar m(\omega_1^2-\omega_2^2)X_1X_2.
$$

At $\omega_1=\omega_2$, this becomes $\boxed{[L_3,H]=0}$. The [rotational symmetry](../../../../rotational-symmetry.md) of the [two-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator](../../../../two-dimensional-isotropic-harmonic-oscillator.md) allows a basis of simultaneous [energy eigenstates](../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) and [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) [eigenstates](../../../../eigenstate.md).
