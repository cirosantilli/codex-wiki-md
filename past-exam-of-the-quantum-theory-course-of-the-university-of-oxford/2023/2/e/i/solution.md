<h1 id="2/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $\omega_1=\omega_2=\omega$. Inverting the [creation and annihilation operators](../../../../../creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) gives

$$
X_j=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}(a_{j,+}+a_{j,-}),\qquad
P_j=i\sqrt{\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}}(a_{j,+}-a_{j,-}).
$$

Substitute these expressions in $L_3=X_1P_2-X_2P_1$. Terms with two [creation operators](../../../../../creation-operator.md) or two [annihilation operators](../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) cancel because different modes commute. The remaining [orbital angular momentum in oscillator ladder operators](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum-in-oscillator-ladder-operators.md) is

$$
\boxed{L_3=i\hbar\left(a_{2,+}a_{1,-}-a_{1,+}a_{2,-}\right).}
$$

Equivalently it is $-i\hbar(a_{1,+}a_{2,-}-a_{2,+}a_{1,-})$, consistent with the [circular ladder operators of a two-dimensional oscillator](../../../../../circular-ladder-operators-of-a-two-dimensional-oscillator.md).
