<h1 id="2/e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $\alpha=m\omega/\hbar$. The first [Hermite polynomials](../../../../../hermite-polynomial.md) give $\psi_{0,0}\propto e^{-\alpha(x_1^2+x_2^2)/2}$ and $\psi_{1,0},\psi_{0,1}$ proportional, with the same constant, to $x_1e^{-\alpha(x_1^2+x_2^2)/2}$ and $x_2e^{-\alpha(x_1^2+x_2^2)/2}$. Thus in [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) the new [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\chi_0\propto e^{-\alpha(x_1^2+x_2^2)/2},\qquad
\chi_\pm\propto(x_1\pm ix_2)e^{-\alpha(x_1^2+x_2^2)/2}.}
$$

Using [polar coordinates](../../../../../polar-coordinates.md) $x_1=r\cos\varphi$, $x_2=r\sin\varphi$ gives

$$
\boxed{\chi_0\propto e^{-\alpha r^2/2},\qquad
\chi_\pm\propto r e^{\pm i\varphi}e^{-\alpha r^2/2}.}
$$

The [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) is $L_3=-i\hbar\partial_\varphi$, which immediately verifies its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $0,\pm\hbar$. Multiplying these spatial [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) by $e^{-i\omega t}$ for $\chi_0$ and $e^{-2i\omega t}$ for $\chi_\pm$ gives the corresponding full [stationary states](../../../../../stationary-state.md).
