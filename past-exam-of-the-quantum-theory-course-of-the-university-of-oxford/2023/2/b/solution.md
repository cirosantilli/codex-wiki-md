<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $X$ and $P$ are [self-adjoint operators](../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md), the [adjoint operator](../../../../adjoint-operator.md) relation requires $c_+=c_-=c$ and $d_+=-d_-= -d$. Thus $a_-=cX+idP$ and $a_+=cX-idP$. The [canonical commutation relation](../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) $[X,P]=i\hbar I$ gives

$$
[a_-,a_+]=2\hbar cd\,I,\qquad
a_+a_-=c^2X^2+d^2P^2-\hbar cd\,I.
$$

Matching the $X^2$, $P^2$ and [identity operator](../../../../identity-operator.md) coefficients in $H=\hbar\omega(a_+a_-+\lambda I)$ yields

$$
c^2=\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar},\qquad
d^2=\frac1{2m\hbar\omega},\qquad cd=\frac1{2\hbar},\qquad\lambda=\frac12.
$$

Consequently all real solutions are

$$
\boxed{c_+=c_-=\varepsilon\sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}},\qquad
d_-=-d_+=\frac{\varepsilon}{\sqrt{2m\hbar\omega}},\qquad
\lambda=\frac12,\qquad \varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}.}
$$

The conventional [creation and annihilation operators](../../../../creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) use $\varepsilon=1$. The other choice multiplies both by minus one and satisfies all the given equations. This harmless freedom is the [oscillator ladder-operator sign convention](../../../../oscillator-ladder-operator-sign-convention.md); the subsequent formulas use the positive choice in both coordinates.
