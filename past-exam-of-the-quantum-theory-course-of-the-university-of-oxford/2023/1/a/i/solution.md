<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [free particle](../../../../../free-particle.md) has [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H=P^2/(2m)$, with [momentum operator](../../../../../momentum-operator.md) $P=-i\hbar\partial_x$. Its time-dependent [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{i\hbar\frac{\partial\Psi}{\partial t}=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2\Psi}{\partial x^2}},\qquad
\Psi(t,x+2\pi R)=\Psi(t,x).
$$

Here $x$ measures arc length for the [particle on a circle](../../../../../particle-in-a-ring.md), so the [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) uses $dx$, rather than $d(x/R)$.
