<h1 id="1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

By the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md), the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) for a measured [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) is the squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) of the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto its whole [energy eigenspace](../../../../../energy-eigenspace.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\Pr(E=0)=\frac23,\qquad
\Pr(E=E_\star)=\frac16+\frac16=\frac13.}
$$

Every other [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) has [probability](../../../../../probability.md) zero. These [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) are independent of $T$: [unitary time evolution](../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md) under the unchanged [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) changes the [dynamical phases](../../../../../dynamical-phase.md) but not the squared expansion coefficients. The two equal-energy terms describe one measurement outcome.
