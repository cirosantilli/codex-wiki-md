<h1 id="1/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Let $\Pi_\star=|\phi_2\rangle\langle\phi_2|+|\phi_{-2}\rangle\langle\phi_{-2}|$ be the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) for $E_\star$. The [Lüders rule](../../../../../luders-rule.md) gives the normalized [post-measurement state](../../../../../post-measurement-state.md)

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Psi(T^+,x)&=\frac{\Pi_\star\Psi(T^-,x)}{\sqrt{1/3}}\\
&=-\frac{e^{-iE_\star T/\hbar}}{\sqrt2}\bigl(\phi_2(x)+\phi_{-2}(x)\bigr)
=-\frac{e^{-iE_\star T/\hbar}}{\sqrt{\pi R}}\cos\!\left(\frac{2x}{R}\right).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The factor $-e^{-iE_\star T/\hbar}$ is a [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md), so $\cos(2x/R)/\sqrt{\pi R}$ is an equivalent representative. The [projective measurement](../../../../../projective-measurement.md) preserves the [quantum coherence](../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) between the two [momentum eigenstates](../../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md); it does not select one of them. This matters for the subsequent position [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md).
