↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using $\sin^2u=\tfrac12-\tfrac14e^{2iu}-\tfrac14e^{-2iu}$, the [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) decomposition is

$$
\boxed{\Psi(0,x)=\mathcal N\sqrt{2\pi R}\left(\frac12\phi_0(x)-\frac14\phi_2(x)-\frac14\phi_{-2}(x)\right).}
$$

Only the [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) zero and $E_\star=2\hbar^2/(mR^2)$ occur. The last two terms are distinct [momentum eigenstates](../../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md) in the same [energy eigenspace](../../../../../energy-eigenspace.md).
