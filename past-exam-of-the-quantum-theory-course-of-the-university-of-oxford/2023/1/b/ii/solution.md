<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) makes the squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) equal to the sum of squared expansion coefficients:

$$
1=|\mathcal N|^2(2\pi R)\left(\frac14+\frac1{16}+\frac1{16}\right)
=|\mathcal N|^2\frac{3\pi R}{4}.
$$

Choose a positive real [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) constant, fixing an irrelevant [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathcal N=\frac2{\sqrt{3\pi R}},\qquad
\Psi(0)=\sqrt{\frac23}\,\phi_0-\frac1{\sqrt6}(\phi_2+\phi_{-2}).}
$$

As a direct check, $\int_0^{2\pi R}\sin^4(x/R)\,dx=3\pi R/4$.
