<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Under [unitary time evolution](../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md), each [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) acquires its own [dynamical phase](../../../../../dynamical-phase.md). Since $E_2=E_{-2}=E_\star$, the [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Psi(t,x)&=\sqrt{\frac23}\,\phi_0(x)-\frac{e^{-iE_\star t/\hbar}}{\sqrt6}\bigl(\phi_2(x)+\phi_{-2}(x)\bigr)\\
&=\frac{1-e^{-i\Omega t}\cos(2x/R)}{\sqrt{3\pi R}},\qquad
\Omega=\frac{E_\star}{\hbar}=\frac{2\hbar}{mR^2}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

At $t=0$, the identity $1-\cos(2x/R)=2\sin^2(x/R)$ recovers the normalized initial [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md).
