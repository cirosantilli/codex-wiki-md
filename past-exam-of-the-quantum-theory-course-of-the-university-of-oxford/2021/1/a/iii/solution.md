<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $P\psi_n=n\hbar\psi_n$, applying the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) gives

$$
H\psi_n=\frac{P^2}{2m}\psi_n=\frac{n^2\hbar^2}{2m}\psi_n.
$$

Thus the [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) and corresponding full [stationary states](../../../../../stationary-state.md) are

$$
\boxed{E_n=\frac{n^2\hbar^2}{2m},\qquad \Psi_n(x,t)=e^{-in^2\hbar t/(2m)}\psi_n(x).}
$$

The [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) is $\psi_0$ with [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) zero and [quantum degeneracy](../../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md) one. For each $n=1,2,\ldots$, the positive [energy level](../../../../../energy-level.md) $n^2\hbar^2/(2m)$ has [quantum degeneracy](../../../../../degenerate-energy-levels.md) two: its [energy eigenspace](../../../../../energy-eigenspace.md) is $\operatorname{span}\{\psi_n,\psi_{-n}\}$. This is the [free particle spectrum on a circle](../../../../../free-particle-spectrum-on-a-circle.md).
