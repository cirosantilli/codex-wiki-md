<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [momentum eigenstate](../../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $p$, the equation $-i\hbar\psi'=p\psi$ has solution $\psi(x)=Ce^{ipx/\hbar}$. The [periodic boundary condition](../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) imposes $e^{2\pi ip/\hbar}=1$, so $p/\hbar\in\mathbb Z$. The [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) gives $1=2\pi|C|^2$. Choosing a convenient [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\psi_n(x)=\frac{e^{inx}}{\sqrt{2\pi}},\qquad p_n=n\hbar,\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

These [momentum eigenstates](../../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md) form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), the [Fourier basis](../../../../../fourier-basis.md) for the [particle on a circle](../../../../../particle-in-a-ring.md):

$$
\langle\psi_n,\psi_k\rangle=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}e^{i(k-n)x}\,dx=\delta_{nk}.
$$

Here $\delta_{nk}$ is the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md).
