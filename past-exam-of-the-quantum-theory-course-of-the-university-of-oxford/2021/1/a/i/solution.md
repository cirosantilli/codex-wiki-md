<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [free particle](../../../../../free-particle.md), the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is $H=P^2/(2m)$, where $P=-i\hbar\,d/dx$ is the [momentum operator](../../../../../momentum-operator.md). The [time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2}=E\psi,\qquad \psi(x+2\pi)=\psi(x).}
$$

The full [stationary state](../../../../../stationary-state.md) is $\Psi(x,t)=e^{-iEt/\hbar}\psi(x)$. The [periodic boundary condition](../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) identifies $x$ and $x+2\pi$, giving a [particle on a circle](../../../../../particle-in-a-ring.md) with [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) over one period.
