<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a [position measurement](../../../../../position-measurement.md), the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) integrates the [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) over the chosen interval. The three terms from part (b) have integrals

$$
\int_0^{\pi/4}1\,dx=\frac\pi4,\qquad
\int_0^{\pi/4}\cos^2(2x)\,dx=\left[\frac x2+\frac{\sin4x}{8}\right]_0^{\pi/4}=\frac\pi8,
$$

and

$$
\int_0^{\pi/4}2\cos(2x)\cos(\Omega t)\,dx=\left[\sin(2x)\cos(\Omega t)\right]_0^{\pi/4}=\cos(\Omega t).
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\Pr\left(0\leq x\leq\frac\pi4\right)=\frac18+\frac1{3\pi}\cos\left(\frac{2\hbar t}{m}\right).}
$$

The oscillating term comes from [quantum interference](../../../../../quantum-interference.md) between the zero and positive [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) components. The [probability](../../../../../probability.md) lies between $1/8-1/(3\pi)$ and $1/8+1/(3\pi)$, both within $[0,1]$; integrating the same [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) over the whole circle gives one.
