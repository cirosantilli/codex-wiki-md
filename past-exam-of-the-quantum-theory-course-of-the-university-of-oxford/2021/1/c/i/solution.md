<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) is the single [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) $\psi_0$. Its coefficient in the normalized [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is $\sqrt{2/3}$, so the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Pr(E=0)=\left|\sqrt{\frac23}\right|^2=\frac23.}
$$

This [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is independent of $t$ because [unitary time evolution](../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md) changes only the [dynamical phases](../../../../../dynamical-phase.md) of the [energy eigenstates](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md).
