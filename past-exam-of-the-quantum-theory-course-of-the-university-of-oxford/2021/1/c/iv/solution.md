<h1 id="1/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [parity operator](../../../../../parity-operator.md) exchanges $\psi_n$ and $\psi_{-n}$. Their coefficients in the [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) are equal, so

$$
(R\Psi)(x,t)=\Psi(-x,t)=\frac{1+e^{-i\Omega t}\cos(-2x)}{\sqrt{3\pi}}=\Psi(x,t).
$$

Thus the [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is a [parity operator](../../../../../parity-operator.md) [eigenstate](../../../../../eigenstate.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $+1$ at every time. The [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto the $-1$ [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) is $(I-R)/2$, which annihilates it. By the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md),

$$
\boxed{\Pr(R=-1)=0.}
$$
