<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The coefficient of the [momentum eigenstate](../../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md) $\psi_2$ is $e^{-i\Omega t}/\sqrt6$. The [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) therefore gives $\Pr(P=2\hbar)=1/6$. Conditional on this outcome, the [projective measurement](../../../../../projective-measurement.md) prepares the [post-measurement state](../../../../../post-measurement-state.md) $e^{-i\Omega t}\psi_2$, equivalent to $\psi_2$ up to [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md).

Since $H\psi_2=(4\hbar^2/(2m))\psi_2$, the subsequent [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) measurement has [conditional probability](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) one of giving the specified [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md). The [probability](../../../../../probability.md) for the entire sequence is consequently

$$
\boxed{\Pr\left(P=2\hbar\ \text{then}\ E=\frac{4\hbar^2}{2m}\right)=\frac16\times1=\frac16.}
$$

Any intervening [unitary time evolution](../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md) under the same [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) preserves this [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md), up to [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md).
