<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the ordered [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $(\psi_1,\psi_2)$, the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) has [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
H=\hbar\omega\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}=\hbar\omega\sigma_1,
$$

where $\sigma_1$ is a [Pauli matrix](../../../../../pauli-matrices.md). Its symmetric and antisymmetric [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) give

$$
\boxed{E_+=+\hbar\omega,\qquad \chi_+=\frac{\psi_1+\psi_2}{\sqrt2};\qquad
E_-=-\hbar\omega,\qquad \chi_-=\frac{\psi_1-\psi_2}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

The [energy eigenstates](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) $\chi_\pm$ are normalized and [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md); each [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) is nondegenerate. Their full [stationary states](../../../../../stationary-state.md) are $e^{-i\omega t}\chi_+$ and $e^{i\omega t}\chi_-$. Since they form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), the general solution to the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Psi(t)=c_+e^{-i\omega t}\chi_++c_-e^{i\omega t}\chi_-,\qquad c_+,c_-\in\mathbb C.}
$$

For a normalized [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md), $|c_+|^2+|c_-|^2=1$; the constants are determined by its initial [inner products](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) with $\chi_\pm$.
