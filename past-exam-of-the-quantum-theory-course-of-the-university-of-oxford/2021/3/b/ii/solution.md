<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The initial [quantum state](../../../../../quantum-state.md) is $\psi_1$, up to [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md), and $\psi_1=(\chi_++\chi_-)/\sqrt2$. Applying [unitary time evolution](../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Psi(t)&=\frac1{\sqrt2}\left(e^{-i\omega t}\chi_++e^{i\omega t}\chi_-\right)\\
&=\cos(\omega t)\psi_1-i\sin(\omega t)\psi_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $J_3\psi_1=(\hbar/2)\psi_1$ and $J_3\psi_2=-(\hbar/2)\psi_2$, the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Pr\left(J_3(t)=+\frac\hbar2\right)=|\langle\psi_1,\Psi(t)\rangle|^2=\cos^2(\omega t).}
$$

The other outcome has [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $\sin^2(\omega t)$, verifying that the two [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) sum to one. This is a [two-level quantum return probability](../../../../../two-level-quantum-return-probability.md).
