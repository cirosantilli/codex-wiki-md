<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Inverting the definitions of the [creation and annihilation operators](../../../../../creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) gives

$$
X_j=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}(a_j+a_j^*),\qquad
P_j=-i\sqrt{\frac{m\omega\hbar}{2}}(a_j-a_j^*).
$$

Substituting in the [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L$ gives

$$
L=-\frac{i\hbar}{2}\left[(a_1+a_1^*)(a_2-a_2^*)-(a_2+a_2^*)(a_1-a_1^*)\right].
$$

Operators belonging to distinct modes commute. Therefore the terms with two [annihilation operators](../../../../../annihilation-operator.md) and those with two [creation operators](../../../../../creation-operator.md) cancel, and $a_1^*a_2=a_2a_1^*$, $a_2^*a_1=a_1a_2^*$. The surviving terms give the [orbital angular momentum in oscillator ladder operators](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum-in-oscillator-ladder-operators.md):

$$
\boxed{L=i\hbar(a_1a_2^*-a_2a_1^*).}
$$
