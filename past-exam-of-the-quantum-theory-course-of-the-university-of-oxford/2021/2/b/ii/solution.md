<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

From part (a), $[N,a_j]=-a_j$ and $[N,a_j^*]=a_j^*$. The [commutator product rule](../../../../../commutator-product-rule.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
[N,a_1a_2^*]&=[N,a_1]a_2^*+a_1[N,a_2^*]=-a_1a_2^*+a_1a_2^*=0,\\
[N,a_2a_1^*]&=[N,a_2]a_1^*+a_2[N,a_1^*]=-a_2a_1^*+a_2a_1^*=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{[N,L]=0.}
$$

Each term in $L$ transfers one excitation between the two modes, preserving the total [number operator](../../../../../number-operator.md) [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). This is an instance of the [oscillator bilinear commutator](../../../../../oscillator-bilinear-commutator.md); every [number operator](../../../../../number-operator.md) [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) is an [invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md) of $L$.
