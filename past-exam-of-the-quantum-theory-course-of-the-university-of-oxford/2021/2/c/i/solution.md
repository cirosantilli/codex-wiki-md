<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The common [Fock vacuum](../../../../../fock-vacuum.md) obeys $N\psi=\sum_j a_j^*(a_j\psi)=0$. Put $B=(a_1^*)^{n_1}(a_2^*)^{n_2}$. By the [commutator product rule](../../../../../commutator-product-rule.md) and part (a),

$$
[N,B]=n_1B+n_2B=(n_1+n_2)B.
$$

Moving $N$ through $B$ therefore gives

$$
NB\psi=BN\psi+[N,B]\psi=(n_1+n_2)B\psi.
$$

Dividing by $\sqrt{n_1!n_2!}$ proves

$$
\boxed{N\psi_{n_1,n_2}=(n_1+n_2)\psi_{n_1,n_2}.}
$$

The constructed [number state](../../../../../number-state.md) is nonzero: the relation $a_j(a_j^*)^r\psi=r(a_j^*)^{r-1}\psi$ follows from $[a_j,a_j^*]=I$ and $a_j\psi=0$. Commuting different modes and successively moving each [creation operator](../../../../../creation-operator.md) across the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) yields

$$
\|B\psi\|^2=n_1!n_2!\,\|\psi\|^2=n_1!n_2!.
$$

Thus the given factorial denominator also gives unit [norm](../../../../../norm.md), as expected for a [number state](../../../../../number-state.md).
