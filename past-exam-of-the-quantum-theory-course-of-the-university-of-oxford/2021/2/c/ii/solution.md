<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $u=\psi_{1,0}=a_1^*\psi$ and $v=\psi_{0,1}=a_2^*\psi$. The [canonical commutation relations](../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) for the [creation and annihilation operators](../../../../../creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) give

$$
a_1u=\psi,\qquad a_2u=0,\qquad a_1v=0,\qquad a_2v=\psi.
$$

For example, $a_1a_1^*\psi=(I+a_1^*a_1)\psi=\psi$. The expression for $L$ from part (b), with distinct modes reordered, then yields

$$
Lu=i\hbar v,\qquad Lv=-i\hbar u.
$$

Thus $\operatorname{span}\{u,v\}$ is an [invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md), and the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) of $L$ in the ordered [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $(u,v)$ is

$$
[L]_{(u,v)}=\hbar\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\ell^2-\hbar^2$, giving the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and normalized [eigenstates](../../../../../eigenstate.md)

$$
\boxed{\ell_+=+\hbar,\qquad \chi_+=\frac{\psi_{1,0}+i\psi_{0,1}}{\sqrt2};\qquad
\ell_-=-\hbar,\qquad \chi_-=\frac{\psi_{1,0}-i\psi_{0,1}}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

Indeed, $L(u\pm iv)=\pm\hbar(u\pm iv)$ and $\|u\pm iv\|^2=2$ by the assumed [orthonormality](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md). Both [eigenstates](../../../../../eigenstate.md) remain in the $N=1$ [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md).
