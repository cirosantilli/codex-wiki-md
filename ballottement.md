# Ballottement

↑ **Parent:** [Pregnancy](pregnancy.md)

Rebound felt after displacement of a mobile object in fluid. In a suitable obstetric setting it is a probable sign of [pregnancy](pregnancy.md), not by itself proof of a living [fetus](fetus.md).
