# Ekman spin-down in a shallow-water layer

↑ **Parent:** [Ekman pumping](ekman-pumping.md)

In a thin [Bottom Ekman layer](bottom-ekman-layer.md), slowly evolving [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md) drives upward [Ekman pumping](ekman-pumping.md) into a uniform-depth exterior layer. With $f>0$, $\alpha=\sqrt{f\nu/2}$ and $R_D=\sqrt{gH}/f$, its balanced height satisfies $(\nabla_h^2-R_D^{-2})\eta_t=-(\alpha/H)\nabla_h^2\eta$. Small-scale [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) decay on $H/\alpha$; large-scale modes obey a [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) with diffusivity $g\alpha/f^2$. The balanced approximation requires $|f|\tau\gg1$.
