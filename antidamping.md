# Antidamping

↑ **Parent:** [Damping](damping.md)

[antidamping](antidamping.md) is a velocity-proportional term that supplies rather than removes mechanical [energy](energy-split.md). For the displayed [damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md) with negative damping coefficient, $d[(\dot x^2+\omega_0^2x^2)/2]/dt=-2\lambda\dot x^2\geq0$. [causality](causality.md) of a driven solution and its long-time stability are different properties.
