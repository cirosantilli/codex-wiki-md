# Next-event estimation

↑ **Parent:** [Path tracing](path-tracing.md)

Explicit light-source sampling at a surface interaction: connect to a sampled source and use a [shadow ray](shadow-ray.md) to test visibility. It avoids relying solely on a randomly continued path to hit an emitter; this is essential for ordinary continuous direction sampling with ideal point lights.
