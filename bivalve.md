# Bivalve

↑ **Parent:** [Animal](animal.md)

A member of the molluscan group with a shell comprising two articulated valves. Marine [bivalves](bivalve.md) provide abundant [fossils](fossil.md) for studying [extinction selectivity](extinction-selectivity.md), geographic distributions and changes in [species](species.md) assemblages.
