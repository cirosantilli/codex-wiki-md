# Antifreeze glycoprotein

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

A sugar-bearing [protein](protein-split.md) that inhibits ice growth in relevant organisms. Comparative analysis traced the Antarctic notothenioid form to recruitment of ancestral [trypsinogen](trypsinogen.md) gene segments and amplification of a short coding element.[PNAS 94, pp. 3811–3816](evolution-of-antifreeze-glycoprotein-gene-from-a-trypsinogen-gene-in-antarctic-notothenioid-fish-by-liangbiao-chen-and-others.md)
