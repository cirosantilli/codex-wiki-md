# Sediment-bed linear instability

↑ **Parent:** [Bedform](bedform.md)

Combining the [Exner equation](exner-equation.md), [sediment transport saturation](sediment-transport-saturation.md), [bed shear response](bed-shear-response.md), and [inclined-bed sediment threshold](inclined-bed-sediment-threshold.md) gives a competition between upstream forcing and downstream relaxation. With $a=\tau_0A$, $b=\tau_0B-\tau_{\mathrm{th},0}/\mu_s$, $C=\chi\gamma(\tau_0-\tau_{\mathrm{th},0})^{\gamma-1}$, and positive [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$, the [growth rate](growth-rate.md) is $\sigma=Ck^2(b-akL_{\mathrm{sat}})/(1+k^2L_{\mathrm{sat}}^2)$. Thus $b>0$ permits long-wave growth when $a>0$, while a finite [saturation length](saturation-length.md) stabilizes shorter waves.
