# Factor theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factor_theorem)

For a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $f$ over a [field](field.md), $f(a)=0$ exactly when $X-a$ divides $f(X)$. [Polynomial division](polynomial-division.md) by the monic linear factor gives

$$
f(X)=(X-a)q(X)+f(a).
$$

The constant [polynomial remainder](remainder-of-polynomial-division.md) is thus the value obtained by evaluation. For $p(X)=X^3-3X^2+4$, the [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) $2$ gives factor $X-2$ and [polynomial quotient](quotient-of-polynomial-division.md) $X^2-X-2$. The latter also vanishes at $2$, explaining the repeated factor. This provides a practical way to move between evaluation and factorization.
