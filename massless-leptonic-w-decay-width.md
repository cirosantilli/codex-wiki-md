# Massless leptonic W decay width

↑ **Parent:** [W boson](w-boson.md)

The chiral [weak charged current](charged-current.md) vertex $-ig\gamma^\mu(1-\gamma_5)/(2\sqrt2)$ gives the spin-averaged squared [matrix element](matrix-element.md) $g^2M_W^2/3$ for an on-shell [W boson](w-boson.md) decaying to a massless charged [lepton](lepton.md) and [antineutrino](antineutrino.md). Integrating [two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space](two-body-lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) gives $\Gamma_\ell=g^2M_W/(48\pi)$. The [massive-vector spin average](massive-vector-spin-average.md) divides by three; an extra neutrino-helicity factor would incorrectly double count the [chiral projector](chiral-projector.md).
