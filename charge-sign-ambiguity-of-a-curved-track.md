# Charge-sign ambiguity of a curved track

↑ **Parent:** [Anderson positron track experiment](anderson-positron-track-experiment.md)

A photograph records a path but supplies no arrow of [time](time-in-physics.md). The [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) depends on $q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$: reversing both [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and direction of travel leaves the spatial bending compatible with the same path. Thus a particular arc can represent a [Positron](positron.md) travelling one way or an [electron](electron.md) travelling the other. Knowing the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md)'s polarity is necessary but insufficient. [Gravity](gravity.md) does not justify assuming that every [cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md) track travels downward.

The [lead](lead.md) plate adds a physical ordering. For a single particle crossing a passive absorber without an accelerating interaction, the outgoing [momentum](momentum-split.md) is smaller, so the outgoing [gyroradius](gyroradius.md) is smaller in the same [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The less curved segment comes first. Once this direction is assigned, the [cross product](cross-product.md) in the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) determines the sign of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). For the convention below, $\mathbf B=B\widehat{\mathbf z}$ points out of the page; at upward [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf v=v\widehat{\mathbf y}$, $\mathbf v\times\mathbf B=vB\widehat{\mathbf x}$. Bending to the right therefore means positive [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md).

[Magnetic forces do no work](magnetic-forces-do-no-work.md): the change of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) occurs through interaction with the material, not through the bending [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The ideal construction suppresses [multiple scattering](multiple-scattering.md), [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) fluctuations, and loss of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) in the gas. In an actual analysis one compares arcs away from the plate and allows a change of tangent from [multiple scattering](multiple-scattering.md); one cannot interpret every kink as a change of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). Short visible arcs, uncertain projection and small bending also limit a determination of [magnetic rigidity](magnetic-rigidity.md).
