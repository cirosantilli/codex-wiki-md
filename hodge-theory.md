# Hodge theory

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodge_theory)

Hodge theory connects [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) with [harmonic differential forms](harmonic-differential-form.md) through the [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md) on a compact [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md). The [Hodge decomposition theorem](hodge-decomposition-theorem.md) supplies a unique harmonic representative of each cohomology class. On a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), the decomposition additionally respects the complex bidegrees of [differential forms](differential-form-split.md).
