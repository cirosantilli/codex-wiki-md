# Compact first-cohomology obstruction to a Lagrangian fibration

↑ **Parent:** [Nearby exact Lagrangian intersection lemma](nearby-exact-lagrangian-intersection-lemma.md)

A compact [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) $L$ with vanishing first [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) cannot be a fibre of a smooth positive-dimensional [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md) whose nearby fibres are also [Lagrangian submanifolds](lagrangian-submanifold.md). In a [Weinstein neighborhood](weinstein-neighborhood-theorem.md), nearby fibres are [one-form graphs](graph-of-a-differential-one-form.md) of closed forms. They are exact by the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) assumption, so meet the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) at a critical point of their potential, contradicting disjointness of distinct fibres.
