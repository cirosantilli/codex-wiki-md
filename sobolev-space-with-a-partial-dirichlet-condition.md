# Sobolev space with a partial Dirichlet condition

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

For a boundary portion $\Gamma_D\subseteq\partial U$, the space

$$
V=\{v\in H^1(U):\operatorname{Tr}v|_{\Gamma_D}=0\}
$$

is the kernel of the restricted [trace map](sobolev-trace-theorem.md), hence a [closed vector subspace](closed-vector-subspace.md) of $H^1(U)$.
