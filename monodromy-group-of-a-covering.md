# Monodromy group of a covering

↑ **Parent:** [Monodromy theorem](monodromy-theorem.md)

For a [covering map](covering-space.md) $p:X\to Y$ and base point $y$, the [path lifting theorem](path-lifting-theorem.md) lifts each [based loop](based-loop.md) from each point of the [fibre](fiber-of-a-function.md) $p^{-1}(y)$. Its endpoint defines a [permutation](permutation.md) of that [fibre](fiber-of-a-function.md); [path reversal](path-reversal.md) gives the inverse. The [homotopy lifting theorem for a covering map](homotopy-lifting-theorem-for-a-covering-map.md) makes the permutation depend only on the [based homotopy](based-homotopy.md) class. These permutations form the monodromy group, the image of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) action

$$
\pi_1(Y,y)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sym}(p^{-1}(y)).
$$

The image is independent of the convention for composing routes; one may reverse the loop classes to obtain a left [group action](group-action-split.md) when route multiplication means following the first route and then the second.

For $p(w)=w^2$ on the [punctured plane](punctured-complex-plane.md), the [fibre](fiber-of-a-function.md) over $1$ is $\{1,-1\}$, and a once-around [based loop](based-loop.md) exchanges its two points. The monodromy group has two elements: only the parity of the [winding number](winding-number.md) matters. For the [covering map](covering-space.md) $p(w)=e^w$ from the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) to the [punctured plane](punctured-complex-plane.md), the [fibre](fiber-of-a-function.md) over $1$ is $2\pi i\mathbb Z$, and a positive turn acts by $2\pi ik\mapsto2\pi i(k+1)$. Its monodromy group is an infinite [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md). These are the geometric versions of [logarithm and square-root continuation along different paths](logarithm-and-square-root-continuation-along-different-paths.md): the base point returns, while its lifted endpoint can move to another sheet.
