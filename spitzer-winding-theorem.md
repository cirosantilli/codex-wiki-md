# Spitzer winding theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md)

For [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) started at $1$, let $\Theta_t$ be its continuous unwrapped angle, initialized at zero. Then $2\Theta_t/\log t$ converges in distribution to the standard symmetric [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md), with density $1/[\pi(1+x^2)]$. The angle records all completed turns rather than a bounded principal argument. The assertion is a [convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md) at deterministic times; it does not assert convergence of sample paths. A precise statement appears as Theorem 7.31 in [Mörters and Peres, Brownian Motion](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/205B/bmbook.pdf).
