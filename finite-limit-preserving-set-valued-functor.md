# Finite-limit-preserving set-valued functor

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor.md)

A [functor](functor.md) $F:\mathcal C\to\mathbf{Set}$ is finite-limit-preserving if it carries each [finite limit](finite-limit.md) cone to a limiting cone. When $\mathcal C$ is a [small category](small-category.md) with [finite limits](finite-limit.md), this is equivalent to $F$ being a [filtered colimit in a category](filtered-colimit-in-a-category.md) of covariant [representable functors](representable-functor.md), and to the opposite of its [category of elements](category-of-elements.md) being a [filtered category](filtered-category.md). It is also equivalent to every [comma category](comma-category.md) $(S\downarrow F)$ having [finite limits](finite-limit.md), for all sets $S$.
