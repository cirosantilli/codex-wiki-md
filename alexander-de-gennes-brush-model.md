# Alexander-de Gennes brush model

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer brush](polymer-brush.md)

A scaling model of overlapping end-grafted [polymers](polymer-split.md) assigns each chain a column of lateral width $D\sim s^{-1/2}$ at [grafting density](grafting-density.md) $s$. Each [concentration blob](concentration-blob.md) contains $g\sim(D/a)^{1/\nu}$ statistical segments, and stacking $N/g$ blobs gives [polymer brush](polymer-brush.md) height $H\sim ND/g$. For assumed segment [volume](volume.md) $a^3$, the mean [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) is $\phi\sim Na^3s/H$. It assumes a long many-segment-blob brush and supplies scaling powers rather than a precise chain-end distribution.
