# Radiation fluid has no regular free surface

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary radiation density](stationary-radiation-density.md)

A connected nonvacuum [perfect fluid](perfect-fluid.md) with [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) $p=\rho/3$ obeys the [stationary radiation density](stationary-radiation-density.md) law $\rho=C/N^4$ with $C>0$. On a compact region of a regular [spacetime](spacetime.md), a smooth [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) has bounded norm. Consequently $\rho$ cannot tend to zero at a finite regular [free surface](free-surface.md). A vanishing timelike norm instead makes the density diverge. A radiation-filled cavity can have a material wall with nonzero boundary [pressure](pressure.md); this is a different boundary condition.
