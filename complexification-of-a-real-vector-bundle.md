# Complexification of a real vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

Complexification replaces each fiber of a real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) by its [tensor product](tensor-product.md) with $\mathbb C$ over $\mathbb R$. The [complexification of a real vector bundle](complexification-of-a-real-vector-bundle.md) of rank one is a [complex line bundle](complex-line-bundle.md). For the [tautological bundle](tautological-bundle.md) over [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md), this [complex line bundle](complex-line-bundle.md) squares to the [trivial vector bundle](trivial-vector-bundle.md).
