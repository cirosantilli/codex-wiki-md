# Digital systems, linear algebra, introduction to formal proof examination paper by the University of Oxford (2013)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

Primary source: [Released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/past_exam_papers_for_1st_year_of/response/1398416/attach/2/Fakour%20Zip%20Final%201.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `4224-1.pdf`. The original nine-page scan identifies the [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) Preliminary Examination in [computer science](computer-science-split.md) on 14 June 2013. Questions 1–3 cover [digital electronics](digital-electronics.md), the [datapath](datapath.md) and [file systems](file-system-split.md); Questions 4–6 cover [linear algebra](linear-algebra-split.md); Questions 7–8 use the supplied [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) rules on printed p. 9. The original p. 8 repeats Question 8(a)(i) as Question 8(a)(iii), and has a redundant closing parenthesis in Question 7(d)(i).
