# Ridout theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt subspace theorem](schmidt-subspace-theorem.md)

Let $\alpha$ be an algebraic irrational number, let $S$ be a finite set of [primes](prime-number.md), and let $\varepsilon>0$. There are only finitely many reduced fractions $p/q$ whose denominator is [S-smooth number](s-smooth-number.md) and which satisfy

$$
\left|\alpha-\frac pq\right|<q^{-1-\varepsilon}.
$$

This is the denominator-restricted consequence of the finite-place [Schmidt subspace theorem](schmidt-subspace-theorem.md).
