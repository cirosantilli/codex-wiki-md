# CncC

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A Drosophila counterpart of mammalian [NRF2](nrf2.md), regulating oxidative-stress-response genes. Its activation can be protective yet also alter neighbour-dependent competitive behaviour.
