# Gaussian maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) standard [normal random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md) $Z_1,\ldots,Z_N$, the maximum of $|Z_j|$ has scale $\sqrt{\log N}$. A [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) and the [Gaussian tail bound](gaussian-tail-bound.md) give the upper scale. Integrating the [normal density](normal-density.md) over $[r,r+1/r]$ gives a lower tail bound $c r^{-1}e^{-r^2/2}$, implying $\mathbb P(\max_j|Z_j|\leq\sqrt{\log N})\to0$.
