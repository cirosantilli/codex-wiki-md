# Centre manifold theorem for a discrete dynamical system

↑ **Parent:** [Centre manifold theorem](centre-manifold-theorem.md)

For a sufficiently [smooth](smooth-function.md) local map fixing the origin, a local invariant [centre manifold](center-manifold.md) is tangent to the [generalized eigenspaces](generalized-eigenspace.md) with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of [modulus](modulus.md) one. Writing the map as $(u,v)\mapsto(F(u,v),G(u,v))$ and the manifold as $v=h(u)$ gives the invariance equation $h(F(u,h(u)))=G(u,h(u))$. Finite [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) coefficients can be found from this equation. If all transverse [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) have [modulus](modulus.md) less than one, local asymptotic stability reduces to that of the map on the [centre manifold](center-manifold.md).
