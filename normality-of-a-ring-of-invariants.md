# Normality of a ring of invariants

↑ **Parent:** [Ring of invariants](ring-of-invariants.md)

A fraction of invariant elements is fixed by every automorphism. If that fraction is integral over $R^G$, its monic equation also makes it integral over $R$. A [normal domain](integrally-closed-domain.md) therefore places it in $R$, and fixedness places it in $R^G$. This proves integral closedness of the [invariant subring](ring-of-invariants.md) in its own [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md). The proof works for any group and in every characteristic; no averaging or finiteness of the group is needed for normality.
