# Addition law for square-root branching diffusions

↑ **Parent:** [Square-root branching diffusion](square-root-branching-diffusion.md)

For solutions with independent Brownian drivers in a common filtration, combine the drivers with weights $\sqrt{Z/(Z+Z')}$ and $\sqrt{Z'/(Z+Z')}$ where the sum is positive. At zero use one original driver. The resulting continuous [local martingale](local-martingale.md) has bracket $t$ and is [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) by the [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md). Multiplying its differential by $\sqrt{Z+Z'}$ gives exactly the sum of the original stochastic differentials.
