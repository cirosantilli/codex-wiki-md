# Conventional superconductivity at 203 kelvin at high pressures in the sulfur hydride system by A. P. Drozdov and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

Primary article: [https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14964](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14964)

Author manuscript: [https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.08190](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.08190)

The authors distinguish metallization near $90\,\mathrm{GPa}$ from the high-temperature superconducting phase at pressures around $155\,\mathrm{GPa}$. Transport, magnetic and isotope observations support conventional electron–phonon-mediated superconductivity; pressure, phase and thermal preparation must accompany the quoted transition temperature.
