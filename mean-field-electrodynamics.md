# Mean-field electrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

[Mean-field electrodynamics](mean-field-electrodynamics.md) applies averaging and a response closure to magnetic induction in a fluctuating conducting fluid. The mean equation contains the correlated [mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md) in the title. Scale separation permits a local expansion into alpha transport, [turbulent diffusion](eddy-diffusion.md) and higher gradients; general anisotropic or finite-memory statistics require tensorial and nonlocal responses. A [mean-field dynamo](mean-field-dynamo.md) is a self-exciting application of this framework, not a consequence of averaging alone.
