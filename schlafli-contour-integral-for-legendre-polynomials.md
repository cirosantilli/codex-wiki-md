<h1 id="schlafli-contour-integral-for-legendre-polynomials">Schläfli contour integral for Legendre polynomials</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre polynomial](legendre-polynomial.md)

For a contour enclosing $t$,

$$
P_n(t)=\frac1{2^{n+1}\pi i}
\oint\frac{(z^2-1)^n}{(z-t)^{n+1}}\,dz.
$$
