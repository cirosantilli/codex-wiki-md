# At-rest force for a symmetric phi-four pair

↑ **Parent:** [Kink–antikink attraction from the stress tensor](kink-antikink-attraction-from-the-stress-tensor.md)

For the initial field $\phi(x)=\tanh(x+c)-\tanh(x-c)-1$ and zero initial time derivative, evaluate the [scalar-field momentum flux](scalar-field-momentum-flux.md) at the midpoint: $\phi_x(0)=0$ and $\phi(0)=2\tanh c-1$. The force on the left [kink](scalar-field-kink.md) is the displayed positive value, attractive toward the right [antikink](antikink.md). This exact initial expression has the large-separation asymptotic $32e^{-2d}$ for $d=2c$. If the initial velocity at the cut is $\psi(0)$, subtract $\psi(0)^2/2$.
