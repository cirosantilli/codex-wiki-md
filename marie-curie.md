# Marie Curie

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Curie)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md) and [chemist](chemist.md) whose investigation of [radioactivity](radioactive-decay.md) joined sensitive measurement to chemical separation. She moved from Warsaw to Paris to study, later becoming a professor of [physics](physics-split.md). The [Curie ionization-current measurement](curie-ionization-current-measurement.md) shows the experimental craft behind the work: balancing a tiny [electric current](electric-current.md) against a calibrated [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). She shared the 1903 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) and received the 1911 prize in [chemistry](chemistry-split.md).[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1901–1921; Biographical identity and professional specialization](nobel-lectures-physics-1901-1921/marie-curie-biographical.md)
