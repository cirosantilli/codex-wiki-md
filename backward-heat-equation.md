# Backward heat equation

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

A [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) has multiplier $e^{Dk^2t}$, amplifying arbitrarily small high-frequency data. For a fixed positive time $t_0$, initial modes $e^{-Dn^2t_0/2}\sin(nx)$ tend to zero in $L^2$ while their values at $t_0$ grow without bound. This violates [continuous dependence on initial data](continuous-dependence-on-initial-data.md) and gives an [ill-posed problem](ill-posed-problem.md) in ordinary unweighted spaces. An analytic-data restriction or physical short-scale regularization changes that conclusion.
