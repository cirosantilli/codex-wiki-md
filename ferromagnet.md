# Ferromagnet

↑ **Parent:** [Ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md)

A [ferromagnet](ferromagnet.md) is a material that exhibits [ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md). Its [magnetic domains](magnetic-domain.md) can cancel the net [magnetization](magnetization.md) even while each domain remains ordered.
