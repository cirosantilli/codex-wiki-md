# Black-hole uniqueness theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)

In four-dimensional asymptotically flat [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md), a regular stationary vacuum black hole is a [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md) under the standard connectedness and analyticity hypotheses. The electrovacuum extension is the Kerr--Newman family.
