# Split link

↑ **Parent:** [Link](link.md)

A split [link](link.md) admits an embedded [sphere](sphere.md) in its complement with nonempty portions of the [link](link.md) on both sides. A split diagram admits a simple closed curve in the projection [sphere](sphere.md) separating nonempty portions of the diagram. A split diagram always represents a split [link](link.md); the converse need not hold for an arbitrary diagram.
