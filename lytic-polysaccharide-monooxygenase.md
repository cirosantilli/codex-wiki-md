# Lytic polysaccharide monooxygenase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A [lytic polysaccharide monooxygenase](lytic-polysaccharide-monooxygenase.md) is a copper-dependent [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that cleaves polysaccharides oxidatively. Its performance depends on substrate access, reduction and oxidant supply; uncontrolled peroxide can damage the catalyst.
