# Indicial root

↑ **Parent:** [Indicial equation](indicial-equation.md)

An [indicial root](indicial-root.md) is a root of the [indicial equation](indicial-equation.md) obtained from a [power-law ansatz](power-law-ansatz.md). For a [Cauchy-Euler differential equation](cauchy-euler-equation.md), such roots give the exponents of its power solutions. Their sign determines which modes remain bounded near a singular endpoint.
