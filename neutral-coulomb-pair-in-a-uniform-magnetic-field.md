# Neutral Coulomb pair in a uniform magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic momentum and magnetic pseudomomentum](kinetic-momentum-and-magnetic-pseudomomentum.md)

For charges $+e,-e$ and relative coordinate $\mathbf r=\mathbf r_2-\mathbf r_1$, total magnetic pseudomomentum is conserved in a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). In an appropriate symmetric-gauge [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) differing by a [total derivative](total-derivative.md), it is the canonical centre-of-mass [momentum](momentum-split.md). Mechanical centre-of-mass [momentum](momentum-split.md) instead equals $\mathbf K+e\mathbf B\times\mathbf r$ and need not be constant. The sector $\mathbf K=0$ is not generally a stationary-centre-of-mass frame.
