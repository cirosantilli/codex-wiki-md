# Bright-field microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Optical microscope](optical-microscope.md)

Transmitted-light imaging with an [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) in which the directly transmitted illumination contributes the bright background. An absorbing specimen can reduce that background locally. The ideal lossless [phase object](phase-object.md) calculation in [coherent zero-mode model of phase contrast](coherent-zero-mode-model-of-phase-contrast.md) shows why ordinary brightness detection need not reveal every optical variation.
