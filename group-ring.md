# Group ring

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_ring)

The [group ring](group-ring.md) $R[G]$ consists of finite formal sums $\sum_{g\in G}r_gg$ with coefficients in a [ring](ring.md) $R$. Addition is coefficientwise and multiplication extends the [group operation](group-operation.md) and multiplication in $R$ distributively. A [group algebra](group-algebra.md) is the case in which $R$ is a [field](field.md).
