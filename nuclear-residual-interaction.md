# Nuclear residual interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md)

The interaction remaining after a chosen [nuclear mean field](nuclear-mean-field.md) is subtracted from the nuclear [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). It can split configurations having the same occupied one-particle levels but different coupled [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). Thus the ordering of nuclear orbitals alone does not determine every [odd-odd nucleus](odd-odd-nucleus.md) ground-state [spin](spin.md).
