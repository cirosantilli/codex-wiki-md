# Symplectic group over a field

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic group](symplectic-group.md)

For a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) over any [field](field.md) with a [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md) $B$, the [symplectic group over a field](symplectic-group-over-a-field.md) consists of [invertible linear maps](invertible-linear-map.md) preserving $B$. In a [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md) its matrices satisfy $g^{\mathsf T}Jg=J$. Matching two [symplectic bases](symplectic-basis.md) gives a unique such [linear map](linear-map.md), and a [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md) exists by successively splitting off paired vectors. This includes arbitrary-characteristic and infinite-field versions, beyond the real [Lie group](lie-group.md) and the [symplectic group over a finite field](symplectic-group-over-a-finite-field.md).
