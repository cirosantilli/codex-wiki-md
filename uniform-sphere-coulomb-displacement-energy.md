# Uniform-sphere Coulomb displacement energy

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear Coulomb energy](nuclear-coulomb-energy.md)

The electrostatic self-energy of a uniformly charged sphere of sharp radius $R$ is $3Z^2\alpha\hbar c/(5R)$. For mirror nuclei, subtract the changed neutron/proton rest-mass contribution from their nuclear mass difference before identifying the residual with a [nuclear Coulomb energy](nuclear-coulomb-energy.md) difference. The inferred $R$ is a sharp-sphere radius; the corresponding [nuclear charge radius](nuclear-charge-radius.md) defined as an rms radius is $\sqrt{3/5}R$. Replacing $Z^2$ by $Z(Z-1)$ removes a proton self-interaction approximation and shifts the estimate.
