<h1 id="su-n-fundamental-completeness-relation">SU(N) fundamental completeness relation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental representation](fundamental-representation.md)

For traceless [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) representing the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of the [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) $SU(N)$, normalized by $\operatorname{tr}(T^aT^b)=\delta^{ab}/2$, completeness of $\{I/\sqrt{2N},T^a\}$ in the space of [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) gives the displayed identity. In particular $\sum_a T^aXT^a=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(X)I-X/(2N)$. For traceless $X=T^b$, this becomes $\sum_a T^aT^bT^a=-T^b/(2N)$. These identities reduce [QCD](quantum-chromodynamics.md) [color charge](color-charge.md) sums without choosing explicit matrices.
