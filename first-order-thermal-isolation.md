# First-order thermal isolation

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)

A lumped sample of [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) $C$ coupled by [thermal conductance](thermal-conductance.md) $G$ to a bath has the displayed linearized [energy](energy-split.md) balance. For constant internal power, the bath-to-sample [temperature](temperature-split.md) response is $1/(1+i\omega C/G)$. Insulation and [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) suppress rapid bath fluctuations but have unity static bath-to-sample gain. A steady power change shifts the sample by $\delta P/G$, so improved isolation increases self-heating sensitivity and equilibration time. The lumped approximation requires internal equilibration faster than the variations being modeled.
