# Generalized Lane-Emden equation

↑ **Parent:** [Lane-Emden equation](lane-emden-equation.md)

A [Generalized Lane-Emden equation](generalized-lane-emden-equation.md) is a [semilinear partial differential equation](semilinear-partial-differential-equation.md) whose highest-order term is the [Laplacian](laplacian.md):

$$
\Delta u+u^p=0.
$$

For a [radial function](radial-function.md) in $d$ dimensions it becomes $u''+(d-1)u'/r+u^p=0$. The case $d=3$ gives the classical [Lane-Emden equation](lane-emden-equation.md).
