# Pressure-dependent Darcy drainage

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy law](darcy-law.md)

Suppose a porous layer has thickness $h(p)$ and [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) $k(p)$, and the pressure drop $p$ acts across its thickness. [Darcy's law](darcy-law.md) gives the drainage [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md)

$$
w=\frac{k(p)}{\mu h(p)}p.
$$

If $k/h=Kp^{-\beta}$, then $w=(K/\mu)p^{1-\beta}$.
