# Nucleosome

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatin](chromatin.md)

A repeating [chromatin](chromatin.md) unit in which [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) wraps around a [histone](histone.md) core. Position, turnover and modification affect accessibility of the associated [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md).
