# Hyperplane section

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperplane divisor](hyperplane-divisor.md)

A hyperplane section of a projective variety $X\subset\mathbb P^n$ is the scheme-theoretic intersection $X\cap H$ with a [projective hyperplane](projective-hyperplane.md) $H$. Its associated [line bundle](line-bundle.md) is $\mathcal O_X(1)$.
