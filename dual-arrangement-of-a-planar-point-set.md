# Dual arrangement of a planar point set

↑ **Parent:** [Point-line duality](point-line-duality.md)

Dualize a noncollinear finite point set in the [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md) to projective lines. Their intersection vertices and consecutive line segments form an embedded graph. A vertex incident to $k$ lines has graph degree $2k$ and corresponds to a primal line containing $k$ points. [Ordinary lines](ordinary-line.md) correspond to degree-four vertices. The faces are polygons with at least three sides.
