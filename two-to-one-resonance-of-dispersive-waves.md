# Two-to-one resonance of dispersive waves

↑ **Parent:** [Phase matching for a quadratic wave interaction](phase-matching-for-a-quadratic-wave-interaction.md)

A fundamental wave and its second harmonic satisfy [phase matching for a quadratic wave interaction](phase-matching-for-a-quadratic-wave-interaction.md) when $\omega(2k)=2\omega(k)$. The square of the fundamental drives the second harmonic, while its product with the conjugate second harmonic drives the fundamental. Projecting these terms onto the resonant modes gives coupled [amplitude equations](amplitude-equation.md) rather than a uniformly valid correction at fixed amplitude.
