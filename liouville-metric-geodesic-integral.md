# Liouville metric geodesic integral

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)

For a surface metric $(U(u)+V(v))(du^2+dv^2)$ with $U+V>0$, an affinely parametrized [geodesic](geodesic.md) has constant energy $E=(U+V)(\dot u^2+\dot v^2)/2$. Put $p=(U+V)\dot u$. The [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) give $\dot p=EU'/(U+V)$. Thus $K=p^2-2EU$ has derivative zero, proving the displayed additional quadratic integral. This separation structure is a geometric example of [Liouville integrability](integrable-hamiltonian-system.md).
