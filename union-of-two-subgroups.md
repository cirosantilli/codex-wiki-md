# Union of two subgroups

↑ **Parent:** [Subgroup](subgroup.md)

The union of two [subgroups](subgroup.md) is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) exactly when one contains the other. Otherwise choose $h\in H\setminus K$ and $k\in K\setminus H$. Their product cannot lie in $H$, since $h^{-1}(hk)=k$ would then lie in $H$, and cannot lie in $K$, since $(hk)k^{-1}=h$ would then lie in $K$. The union therefore fails closure. Unlike the union, the [subgroup](subgroup.md) generated by both [subgroups](subgroup.md) always exists.
