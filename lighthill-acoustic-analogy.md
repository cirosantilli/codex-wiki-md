# Lighthill acoustic analogy

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)

The Lighthill acoustic analogy rewrites fluid mass and momentum conservation as a constant-speed [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) driven by a nonlinear [Lighthill stress tensor](lighthill-stress-tensor.md). It is an exact rearrangement before making source approximations. The outgoing field can be constructed with a [retarded acoustic Green function](retarded-acoustic-green-function.md).
