# Symmetric-group character co-degree vanishing criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory of the symmetric group](representation-theory-of-the-symmetric-group-split.md)

For $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(S_n)$, if the order of $g\in S_n$ does not divide $|S_n|/\chi(1)$, then $\chi(g)=0$. The [Hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md) turns the co-degree into a product of hook lengths, while the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) detects a cycle whose required prime-power hook cannot be removed.
