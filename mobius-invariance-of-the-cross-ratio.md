<h1 id="mobius-invariance-of-the-cross-ratio">Möbius invariance of the cross-ratio</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md)

Every [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) preserves the [cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md). The identity $M(u)-M(v)=(ad-bc)(u-v)/[(cu+d)(cv+d)]$ makes all extra factors cancel in the ratio; limiting conventions handle points at infinity.
