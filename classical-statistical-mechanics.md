# Classical statistical mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical mechanics](statistical-mechanics.md)

[classical statistical mechanics](classical-statistical-mechanics.md) assigns [probability](probability.md) [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) to classical states. For a [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) particle system the canonical weight is $e^{-\beta H}$ on [phase space](phase-space.md). The weak-exchange, high-temperature limit of a quantum gas supplies the measure $d^dx\,d^dp/(2\pi\hbar)^d$ per particle and the indistinguishable-particle factor $1/N!$.
