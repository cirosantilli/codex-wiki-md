# Good edge of a dual line arrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Dual arrangement of a planar point set](dual-arrangement-of-a-planar-point-set.md)

A [good dual-arrangement edge](good-edge-of-a-dual-line-arrangement.md) has degree-six endpoints and two triangular adjacent faces. Other edges are bad. The [Euler defect identity for a projective line arrangement](euler-defect-identity-for-a-projective-line-arrangement.md) bounds the number of bad edges by $16t_2$: charge them to degree-four or higher-degree vertices and to nontriangular faces. This turns a count of [ordinary lines](ordinary-line.md) into quantitative control of local triangular geometry.
