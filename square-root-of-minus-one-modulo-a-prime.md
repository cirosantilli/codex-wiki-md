# Square root of minus one modulo a prime

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic residue](quadratic-residue.md)

The congruence is soluble exactly for $p=2$ or $p\equiv1\pmod4$. For odd $p$, [Fermat's little theorem](fermat-little-theorem.md) applied to a prospective root [forces](force.md) $(p-1)/2$ even. Conversely, when $p\equiv1\pmod4$, pair opposite factors in $(p-1)!$ and use the [Wilson theorem](wilson-s-theorem.md) to obtain $(((p-1)/2)!)^2\equiv-1\pmod p$.
