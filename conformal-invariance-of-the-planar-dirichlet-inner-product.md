# Conformal invariance of the planar Dirichlet inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet inner product](dirichlet-inner-product.md)

For a [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md) $f:D\to\widetilde D$ and [smooth](smooth-function.md) $u,v$ of [compact support](compact-support.md) on $\widetilde D$, $\boxed{(u\circ f,v\circ f)_{\nabla,D}=(u,v)_{\nabla,\widetilde D}}$. The [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of $f$ is a rotation times $|f'|$. Consequently the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) introduces $|f'|^2$ into the [gradient](gradient.md) pairing, while the [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) introduces precisely the same factor into area. They cancel. The identity extends by completion to the [Dirichlet energy spaces](dirichlet-energy-space.md); it does not preserve the $L^2$ term of an inhomogeneous [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md).
