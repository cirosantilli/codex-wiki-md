# Explanatory asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Scientific explanation](scientific-explanation.md)

Some physical relations permit [deduction](deductive-reasoning.md) in both directions while supporting different explanatory roles. Pole height and solar angle can explain a shadow length even though the height can also be inferred from that length. An evidential inference need not have the same direction as the [causal explanation](causal-explanation.md).
