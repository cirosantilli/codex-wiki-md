# Proximity and orientation catalysis

↑ **Parent:** [Catalysis](catalysis.md)

[Proximity and orientation catalysis](proximity-and-orientation-catalysis.md) favors reaction by bringing partners together in a productive geometry. A binding interaction is catalytic only insofar as it improves access to the reactive configuration relative to the starting state.
