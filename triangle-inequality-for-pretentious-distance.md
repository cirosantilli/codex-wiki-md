# Triangle inequality for pretentious distance

↑ **Parent:** [Pretentious distance](pretentious-distance.md)

The pretentious distance obeys

$$
\mathbb D(f_1f_2,g_1g_2;x)
\leq\mathbb D(f_1,g_1;x)+\mathbb D(f_2,g_2;x).
$$
