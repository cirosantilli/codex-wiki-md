# Plastoquinone

↑ **Parent:** [Electron transport chain](electron-transport-chain.md)

[Plastoquinone](plastoquinone.md) is a mobile lipid-soluble electron carrier in photosynthetic membranes. Its reduction state helps communicate the balance of electron supply and demand between photosystems.
