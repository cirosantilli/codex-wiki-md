# Profinite completion

↑ **Parent:** [Profinite group](profinite-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Profinite_completion)

The profinite completion of a group $G$ is the [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) of its finite quotient groups:

$$
\widehat G=\varprojlim_{N\triangleleft_fG}G/N.
$$

The canonical map $G\to\widehat G$ has dense image and is injective exactly when $G$ is [residually finite](residually-finite-group.md).
