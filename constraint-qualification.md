# Constraint qualification

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

A [constraint qualification](constraint-qualification.md) is a regularity hypothesis on [optimization constraints](optimization-constraint.md) that guarantees a specified optimality or duality conclusion. For the necessity of [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md), an appropriate [constraint qualification](constraint-qualification.md) ensures that a [local minimum](local-minimum.md) admits suitable [Lagrange multipliers](lagrange-multiplier.md); [linear independence](linear-independence.md) of the [gradients](gradient.md) of equality [optimization constraints](optimization-constraint.md) and [active constraints](active-constraint.md) is one sufficient hypothesis in [KKT regularity at a local minimum](kkt-regularity-at-a-local-minimum.md).[Lecture 7, §L7.1.3, Theorem L7.3, printed p. 4: independent active gradients](nonlinear-optimization-mit-6-7220-and-15-084-spring-2025/lecture-7-lagrange-multipliers-and-kkt-conditions.md) [Slater's condition](slater-s-condition.md) provides a different [constraint qualification](constraint-qualification.md) for [convex optimization](convex-optimization-split.md), yielding [strong duality](strong-duality.md) and dual attainment.[§5.2.3, printed pp. 226–227: Slater's condition, strong duality and dual attainment](convex-optimization-by-stephen-boyd-and-lieven-vandenberghe.md)
