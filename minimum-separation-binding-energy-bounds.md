# Minimum-separation binding-energy bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md)

For positive [masses](mass.md), let $r$ be the actual minimum pairwise separation and use positive binding [energy](energy.md) $U=\sum_{j<k}Gm_jm_k/r_{jk}$. If $m_{(1)},m_{(2)}$ are the smallest [masses](mass.md), then $Gm_{(1)}m_{(2)}\leq rU\leq G\sum_{j<k}m_jm_k$. Thus $U^{-1}$ is comparable to $r$ with constants depending only on the masses.
