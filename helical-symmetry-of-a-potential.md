# Helical symmetry of a potential

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics.md)

A potential $V(r,\phi,z)=f(r,\phi-kz)$ is invariant under the screw transformation $(\phi,z)\mapsto(\phi+ks,z+s)$. The [Noether conserved quantity for a mechanical point symmetry](noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) is

$$
p_z+kp_\phi,
$$

a linear combination of axial [linear momentum](momentum.md) and axial [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
