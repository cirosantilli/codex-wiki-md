# Linear filter of a stationary time series

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md)

An absolutely summable sequence of coefficients defines $Y_t=\sum_s a_sX_{t-s}$ in mean square. It remains weakly stationary and has [covariance](covariance.md) $\gamma_k^Y=\sum_{s,u}a_sa_u\gamma_{k-s+u}$. Its frequency response is $\alpha(\omega)=\sum_sa_se^{is\omega}$.
