# Finitely presented group

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md)

A finitely presented group is a [group](group-split.md) admitting a [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md). Finite presentability concerns the existence of such a presentation, not the decidability of its [word problem for a group](word-problem-for-groups.md).
