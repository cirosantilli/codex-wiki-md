# Solute potential

↑ **Parent:** [Water potential](water-potential.md)

[Solute potential](solute-potential.md) is the contribution to [water potential](water-potential.md) from dissolved material, with pure [water](water.md) assigned zero. In the nearly incompressible description, $\Psi_s=(RT/\bar V_w)\ln a_w$. In a dilute ideal [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), $\Psi_s\simeq-RTc_{\mathrm{osm}}$, where $c_{\mathrm{osm}}$ is the [osmotic concentration](osmotic-concentration.md) of dissolved particles. Thus [solute potential](solute-potential.md) is negative while the corresponding [osmotic pressure](osmotic-pressure.md) is positive: $\Psi_s\simeq-\Pi$. This sign difference distinguishes a contribution to [water potential](water-potential.md) from the compensating mechanical [pressure](pressure-split.md).[Biology 2e, §30.5, Solute Potential: negative concentration contribution](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/30-5-transport-of-water-and-solutes-in-plants.md)
