# Bounded convergence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Measure theory](measure-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bounded_convergence_theorem)

On a [finite measure](finite-measure.md) space, if measurable functions are uniformly bounded and converge pointwise, then their integrals converge. This is the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) with a constant dominating function.
