# Weak temperature gradient approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_temperature_gradient_approximation)

In the tropical free troposphere, small [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md) and rapid [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) adjustment keep horizontal [temperature gradients](temperature-gradient.md) weak. An idealized model therefore suppresses horizontal temperature perturbations at leading order and balances diabatic heating by vertical [advection](advection.md) of the reference stratification. This is not a claim that surface temperature or moisture is spatially uniform, nor is it ordinary midlatitude [geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md).
