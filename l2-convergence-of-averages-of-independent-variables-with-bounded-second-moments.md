# L2 convergence of averages of independent variables with bounded second moments

↑ **Parent:** [Variance additivity for independent random variables](variance-additivity-for-independent-random-variables.md)

For independent random variables with $\sup_n\mathbb E|X_n|^2<\infty$, write

$$
Y_N=\frac1N\sum_{k=1}^NX_k,
\qquad
m_N=\frac1N\sum_{k=1}^N\mathbb EX_k.
$$

Then $\lVert Y_N-m_N\rVert_2^2=N^{-2}\sum_{k\leq N}\operatorname{Var}(X_k)=O(N^{-1})$. Hence $Y_N$ converges in $L^2$ exactly when the scalar [Cesaro means](cesaro-mean.md) $m_N$ converge.
