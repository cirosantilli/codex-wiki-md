# Toric scheme over a base ring

↑ **Parent:** [Toric geometry](toric-geometry-split.md)

Given a dual pair of free lattices $N,M$ and a rational fan, glue $\operatorname{Spec}A[\sigma^\vee\cap M]$ over common-face charts. The monoid [rings](ring.md) are [semigroup algebras](semigroup-algebra.md). A lattice map respecting cones gives a [toric morphism](toric-morphism.md). This construction works over a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $A$, including $\mathbb Z$, and also for an [infinite fan in toric geometry](infinite-fan-in-toric-geometry.md).
