# Separating finite Laurent support by an integer weight

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md)

Given a finite set $F\subset\mathbb Z^r$ of exponent vectors, there is $w\in\mathbb Z^r$ for which the integers $w\cdot m$, $m\in F$, are pairwise distinct. For $r=2$, take $w=(1,N)$ with $N$ larger than every first-coordinate difference in $F$. In higher dimensions take $w=(1,N,\ldots,N^{r-1})$ with $N$ sufficiently large. This proves that a nonzero [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md) can be detected by a substitution $X_i=T^{w_i}$ without cancellation of its distinct terms.
