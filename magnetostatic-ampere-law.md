<h1 id="magnetostatic-ampere-law">Magnetostatic Ampère law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ampère's circuital law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md)

In vacuum [magnetostatics](magnetostatics.md), the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) reduces to $\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J$. Applying [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) gives $\oint_C\mathbf B\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=\mu_0I_{\mathrm{enc}}$, where $I_{\mathrm{enc}}$ is the [electric current](electric-current.md) crossing an oriented spanning surface. Time-dependent [electric fields](electric-field-split.md) require the additional [displacement current](displacement-current.md) term in the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md).
