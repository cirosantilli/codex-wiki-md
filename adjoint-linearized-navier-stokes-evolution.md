# Adjoint linearized Navier-Stokes evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

Relative to the [kinetic-energy inner product](kinetic-energy-inner-product.md), integration by parts reverses incompressible base transport, transposes the velocity-gradient coupling, and preserves viscous diffusion. With reverse time $\tau=-t$, the adjoint base state is evaluated at $-\tau$. The [Leray-Helmholtz projection](leray-helmholtz-projection.md) or adjoint pressure enforces divergence-free adjoint velocity. The [curl of a cross product](curl-of-a-cross-product.md) and [vorticity cross-product identity](vorticity-cross-product-identity.md) give the equivalent form $\partial_\tau v=\Omega\times v-\nabla\times(U\times v)-\nabla\pi+\mathrm{Re}^{-1}\Delta v$.
