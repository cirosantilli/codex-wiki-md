# Five-point Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference method](finite-difference-method.md)

On a square mesh, the dimensionless five-point Laplacian is $\Gamma_5u=u_{i+1,j}+u_{i-1,j}+u_{i,j+1}+u_{i,j-1}-4u_{i,j}$. The scaled operator $h^{-2}\Gamma_5$ approximates the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) with error $O(h^2)$ for sufficiently smooth functions.
