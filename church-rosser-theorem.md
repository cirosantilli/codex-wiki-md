# Church-Rosser theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Lambda calculus](lambda-calculus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church-Rosser_theorem)

If a lambda term beta-reduces to both $M$ and $N$, then $M$ and $N$ beta-reduce to a common term. Consequently two [beta-equivalent](beta-equivalence.md) beta-normal forms are [alpha-equivalent](alpha-equivalence.md).
