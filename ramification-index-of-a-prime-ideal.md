# Ramification index of a prime ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Prime ideal factorization](prime-ideal-factorization.md)

If $\mathfrak p$ is a prime ideal of $\mathcal O_K$ above a rational prime $p$, its ramification index is the exponent $e_{\mathfrak p}$ of $\mathfrak p$ in the factorization of $(p)$. Equivalently, for the normalized ideal valuation, $e_{\mathfrak p}=v_{\mathfrak p}(p)$.
