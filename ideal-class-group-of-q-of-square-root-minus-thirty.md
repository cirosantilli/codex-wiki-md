# Ideal class group of Q of square root minus thirty

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md)

Put $\theta=\sqrt{-30}$. The [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) is $\mathbb Z[\theta]$ and its [discriminant](discriminant.md) is $-120$. The [Minkowski bound for ideal classes](minkowski-s-bound.md) $4\sqrt{30}/\pi<7$ shows that the [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md) is generated by the classes of the ramified [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md) $P_2=(2,\theta)$, $P_3=(3,\theta)$ and $P_5=(5,\theta)$. There is no additional [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) of norm four because two ramifies with residue degree one. The relations $P_i^2=(i)$ and $(\theta)=P_2P_3P_5$ show that these classes have order dividing two and their product is one. None is a [principal ideal](principal-ideal.md): the element [field norm](field-norm.md) $a^2+30b^2$ cannot equal two, three or five. Thus there are exactly four classes, with the displayed group structure. This also supplies the class-number input for the [Hilbert class field of Q of square root minus thirty](hilbert-class-field-of-q-of-square-root-minus-thirty.md).
