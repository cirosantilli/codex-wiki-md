# Mass dependence of fixed-barrier tunnelling

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md)

At fixed incident [energy](energy-split.md) and fixed potential-energy profile, the forbidden-region WKB action is $\int\sqrt{2m(V-E)}\,dx$. Its leading transmission exponent grows as the square root of [mass](mass.md). Equal electrostatic voltages need not define equal potential-energy barriers for particles with different charges. The leading exponential comparison assumes a sufficiently opaque barrier; prefactors and the WKB turning-point conditions remain relevant.
