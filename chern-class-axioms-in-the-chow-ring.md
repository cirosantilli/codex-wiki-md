# Chern class axioms in the Chow ring

↑ **Parent:** [Chern class](chern-class.md)

For a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E$ of rank $r$ on a smooth [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md), algebraic [Chern classes](chern-class.md) have $c_0(E)=1$, vanish for $i>r$, commute with pullback, and obey the [Whitney sum formula for Chern classes](whitney-sum-formula-for-chern-classes.md) for every short exact sequence of vector bundles. A [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $\mathcal O(D)$ has total class $1+[D]$. The projective-bundle splitting construction makes pullback injective and supplies a filtration by line bundles, so these axioms determine all classes as elementary symmetric functions of their first classes. In particular, $c_i(E^*)=(-1)^ic_i(E)$ and $c_1(L\otimes M)=c_1(L)+c_1(M)$ in the ordinary [Chow ring](chow-ring.md).
