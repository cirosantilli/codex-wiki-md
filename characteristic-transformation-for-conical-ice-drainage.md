# Characteristic transformation for conical ice drainage

↑ **Parent:** [Volume-conserving conical ice-current similarity solution](volume-conserving-conical-ice-current-similarity-solution.md)

Putting $y=x^{5/3}$ and $w=x^{1/3}h$ transforms $h_t+x^{-1}(xDh^3)_x=0$ into the [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md)

$$
w_t+\frac{5D}{3}\partial_y(w^3)=0.
$$

Along smooth [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md), $w$ is constant and $y$ grows at speed $5Dw^2$. Starting from $h_0(s)$ gives

$$
h(x,t)x^{1/3}=h_0(s)s^{1/3},\qquad x^{5/3}=s^{5/3}+5D[h_0(s)s^{1/3}]^2t.
$$

After [characteristic crossing](characteristic-crossing.md), the [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) and [entropy solution](entropy-solution.md) select the physical front.
