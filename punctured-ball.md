# Punctured ball

↑ **Parent:** [Open ball](open-ball.md)

A punctured ball is an [open ball](open-ball.md) with its centre removed. In the [real line](real-line.md) it has two [connected components](connected-component.md). In [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) of dimension $n\geq2$ it is [path-connected](path-connected-space.md): for any two points, choose a third point inside the ball outside the two [lines](straight-line.md) through the centre and those points. Such a point exists since two [lines](straight-line.md) do not cover an [open ball](open-ball.md) in dimension at least two. The two [line segments](line-segment.md) joining through that point stay inside the ball, which is a [convex set](convex-set-split.md), and avoid its centre. They form a [continuous path](continuous-path.md).
