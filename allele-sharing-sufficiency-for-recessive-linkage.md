# Allele-sharing sufficiency for recessive linkage

↑ **Parent:** [Recessive affected-sib-pair likelihood](recessive-affected-sib-pair-likelihood.md)

For independent affected sib pairs with IBD counts $n_0,n_1,n_2$, the [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) of the [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md) is proportional to $s^{2n_0+n_1}(1-s)^{2n_2+n_1}$, with $s=2\theta(1-\theta)$. At fixed sample size, the total shared-copy count $2n_2+n_1$ is a [sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md) by the [Fisher-Neyman factorization theorem](fisher-neyman-factorization-theorem.md). Thus a one-sided [likelihood-ratio test](likelihood-ratio-test.md) has the same ordering as a test for excess mean IBD sharing. This equivalence depends on the stated fully penetrant recessive model and does not extend to arbitrary disease models.
