# Nematic bend

↑ **Parent:** [Distortion free energy density](distortion-free-energy-density.md)

[Nematic bend](nematic-bend.md) curves the integral lines of the [nematic director](nematic-director.md). For a unit field, $(\mathbf n\cdot\nabla)\mathbf n=-\mathbf n\times(\nabla\times\mathbf n)$, so the bend energy measures curvature along those lines.
