# Linear matroid

↑ **Parent:** [Matroid](matroid.md)

A [linear matroid](linear-matroid.md) has a finite [ground set](ground-set.md) indexing a family of [vectors](vector.md) in a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) over a specified [field](field.md). A [subset](subset.md) of labels is a [matroid independent set](independent-set-of-a-matroid.md) precisely when its [vectors](vector.md) are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). Labels distinguish repeated [vectors](vector.md); the [ground set](ground-set.md) is not required to consist of distinct numerical [vectors](vector.md). The [matroid bases](basis-of-a-matroid.md) correspond to [bases](basis.md) for the [linear span](linear-span.md) of all the supplied [vectors](vector.md).

**Exchange outline.** The [linear span](linear-span.md) of $|I|$ [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) [vectors](vector.md) has [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $|I|$. If $|J|>|I|$, not all the [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) [vectors](vector.md) indexed by $J$ can lie in that [linear span](linear-span.md). A [vector](vector.md) outside it can be added to $I$. Heredity follows because a [linear dependence](linear-dependence.md) in a [subset](subset.md) would also be a [linear dependence](linear-dependence.md) in the full selection.
