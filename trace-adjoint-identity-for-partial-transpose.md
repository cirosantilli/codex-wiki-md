# Trace-adjoint identity for partial transpose

↑ **Parent:** [Partial transpose](partial-transpose.md)

For bipartite operators $X,Y$ in a fixed product basis,

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(XY^{T_B})=\operatorname{Tr}(X^{T_B}Y).
$$

Expand both operators in products of matrix units. On the transposed factor, the identity is $\operatorname{Tr}(AT)=\operatorname{Tr}(A^TT^T)$; bilinearity gives the bipartite result. It allows a negative [partial transpose](partial-transpose.md) eigenvector to define an [entanglement witness](entanglement-witness.md).
