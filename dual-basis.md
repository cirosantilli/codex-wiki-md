# Dual basis

↑ **Parent:** [Dual space](dual-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_basis)

For a basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ of $V$, its dual basis $e_1^*,\ldots,e_n^*$ is characterized by $e_i^*(e_j)=\delta_{ij}$.
