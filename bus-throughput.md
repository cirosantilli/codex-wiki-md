# Bus throughput

↑ **Parent:** [Computer bus](computer-bus.md)

Useful payload completed per unit elapsed time, with the observation interval stated. A raw [data bus](data-bus.md) rate counts available transfer capacity; achieved [bus throughput](bus-throughput.md) also depends on setup, [bus wait states](bus-wait-state.md), idle intervals and sharing. [Bus contention](bus-contention.md) can increase a requester's delay even when the aggregate [bus throughput](bus-throughput.md) remains unchanged, as the [shared-bus timing example](shared-bus-timing-example.md) shows.
