<h1 id="tolman-oppenheimer-volkoff-equation">Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff_equation)

The Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equation governs a static, spherically symmetric [perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md). In geometrized units, if $m(r)$ is the mass inside areal radius $r$, then

$$
\frac{dm}{dr}=4\pi r^2\rho,
\qquad
\frac{dp}{dr}=-\frac{(\rho+p)(m+4\pi r^3p)}{r(r-2m)}.
$$
