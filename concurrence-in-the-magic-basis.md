# Concurrence in the magic basis

↑ **Parent:** [Concurrence](concurrence.md)

For a normalized pure two-qubit state written in a [magic basis](magic-basis.md), [concurrence](concurrence.md) is $\mathcal C=|\sum_jb_j^2|$, while $\det\rho_A=\mathcal C^2/4$. This bilinear sum uses no [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) inside its square; replacing it by $\sum_j|b_j|^2$ would incorrectly give maximal [concurrence](concurrence.md) for every state.
