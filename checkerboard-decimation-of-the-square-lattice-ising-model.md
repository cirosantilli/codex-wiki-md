# Checkerboard decimation of the square-lattice Ising model

↑ **Parent:** [Spin decimation](spin-decimation.md)

Checkerboard [spin decimation](spin-decimation.md) retains one parity sublattice of a square-lattice [Ising model](ising-model.md) and sums over the other. The retained primitive vectors are $a(1,1)$ and $a(1,-1)$, so the coarse lattice is again square with spacing $\sqrt2a$. A rotation and length rescaling restore the original geometry. Every original nearest-neighbour bond joins the two parity classes, whereas a diagonal next-nearest-neighbour bond stays within one class.
