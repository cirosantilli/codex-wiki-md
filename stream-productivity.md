# Stream productivity

↑ **Parent:** [Lazy list](lazy-list.md)

A stream computation is productive when every demanded finite prefix of its output can be produced in finite work. Filtering an infinite [list](list-abstract-data-type.md) need not be productive: asking for odd elements of `[0,2..]` never produces even an empty-list constructor. [Lazy evaluation](lazy-evaluation.md) alone does not prove productivity.
