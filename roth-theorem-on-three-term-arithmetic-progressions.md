# Roth theorem on three-term arithmetic progressions

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)

For every $\delta>0$, every sufficiently long integer interval has a nonconstant three-term [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) in each subset of [subset density](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) at least $\delta$. This is a theorem about [additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md), distinct from the [Roth theorem](roth-s-theorem.md) on approximation of algebraic irrational numbers. The [Roth density-increment step](roth-density-increment-step.md) proves it by repeatedly increasing [subset density](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) on a shorter [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md).
