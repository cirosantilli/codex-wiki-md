# Eddy-current damping

↑ **Parent:** [Damping](damping.md)

[Eddy-current damping](eddy-current-damping.md) is [electromagnetic damping](electromagnetic-damping.md) in which motion of an [electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md) through a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) induces circulating [electric currents](electric-current.md), producing an opposing [force](force.md) and [Joule heating](joule-heating.md). In the cart experiment it approximates [viscous damping](viscous-damping.md) over the fitted range; spatial variation of the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) or changes in [velocity](velocity.md) can limit that approximation.
