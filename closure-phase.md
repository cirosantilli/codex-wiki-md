# Closure phase

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomical interferometer](astronomical-interferometer.md)

For three collectors whose measured pair phases are $\phi_{ij}=\phi_{ij}^{\rm source}+\epsilon_i-\epsilon_j$, the oriented sum around the triangle cancels the collector-based errors. [closure phase](closure-phase.md) retains source phase information, including asymmetry, but does not cancel arbitrary baseline-dependent errors or fill missing spatial-frequency coverage.
