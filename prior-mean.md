# Prior mean

↑ **Parent:** [Prior probability](prior-probability.md)

The [prior mean](prior-mean.md) is the [expected value](expected-value-split.md) of a [statistical parameter](statistical-parameter.md) under its [prior distribution](prior-probability.md), when this [expected value](expected-value-split.md) exists. The [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) instead uses the [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) after conditioning on observations.
