# Formal inverse

↑ **Parent:** [Formal group law](formal-group-law.md)

Every one-dimensional [formal group law](formal-group-law.md) has a unique formal inverse $i_F(X)=-X+O(X^2)$ satisfying $F(X,i_F(X))=0$. Comparing coefficients recursively determines it degree by degree.
