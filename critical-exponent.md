# Critical exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Critical phenomenon](critical-phenomenon-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_exponent)

A critical exponent describes a power-law singularity near a continuous [phase transition](phase-transition.md), such as $\xi\sim|t|^{-\nu}$ or $\chi\sim|t|^{-\gamma}$.
