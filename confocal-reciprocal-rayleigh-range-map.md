# Confocal reciprocal-Rayleigh-range map

↑ **Parent:** [Confocal optical resonator](confocal-optical-resonator.md)

For two equal [mirrors](mirror.md) of radius $L$ separated by $L$, a Gaussian [waist](beam-waist.md) at the centre is reflected to another [waist](beam-waist.md) at the same plane. Putting $a=L/2$, the [mirror](mirror.md) maps $q=a+iz_R$ to $-a+ia^2/z_R$, so the reflected [Rayleigh range](rayleigh-length.md) is $a^2/z_R$. Both beams have equal spots on the [mirrors](mirror.md) although their [waist](beam-waist.md) radii differ. Equal [waist](beam-waist.md) sizes select $z_R=L/2$.
