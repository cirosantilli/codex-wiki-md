# Open-cover proof of the Lusternik-Schnirelmann-Borsuk theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Lusternik–Schnirelmann theorem](lusternik-schnirelmann-theorem.md)

For a finite [open cover](open-cover.md) $U_0,\ldots,U_d$ of $S^d$, take continuous nonnegative functions $\phi_i$ summing to one and positive only in $U_i$. Such a [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) comes from normalizing the [distance to a set](distance-to-a-set.md) functions $d(x,S^d\setminus U_i)$. The [Borsuk-Ulam theorem](borsuk-ulam-theorem.md) applied to $(\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_d)$ gives equal values at an [antipodal pair](antipodal-pair.md). The sum condition makes $\phi_0$ equal too, and some common positive value places both points in one $U_i$.
