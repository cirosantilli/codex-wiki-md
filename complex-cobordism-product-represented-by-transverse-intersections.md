# Complex cobordism product represented by transverse intersections

↑ **Parent:** [Complex cobordism](complex-cobordism.md)

Pulling the external product of two normally complex [submanifolds](submanifold.md) back along the diagonal gives their [transverse intersection](transverse-intersection.md). Its normal [complex structure](complex-structure.md) is the direct sum of the restricted normal [complex structures](complex-structure.md). Multiplication of [Thom classes](thom-class.md) for a direct sum identifies this geometric representative with the [cup product](cup-product.md) in [complex cobordism](complex-cobordism.md). Empty intersection represents zero.
