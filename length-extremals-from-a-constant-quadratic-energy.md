# Length extremals from a constant quadratic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus of variations](calculus-of-variations-split.md)

For an autonomous smooth [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) homogeneous of degree two in velocities, the [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md) gives $\dot q\cdot L_{\dot q}-L=L$ constant on its [Euler-Lagrange](euler-lagrange-equation.md) extremals. On a regular segment with this constant positive, the Euler-Lagrange expression for $\sqrt L$ equals that for $L$ divided by $2\sqrt L$, since the extra term is proportional to $\dot L$. Hence an energy extremal has the same path as a length extremal. Length is invariant under regular reparametrization: in two coordinates, on a graph $v=v(u)$, its density is $\sqrt{E+2Fv_u+Gv_u^2}\,|du|$. If a nonnegative semidefinite energy vanishes along a curve, the curve instead minimizes length at zero; division by $\sqrt L$ is not permitted there. For a positive definite metric, regular nonconstant curves have positive energy.
