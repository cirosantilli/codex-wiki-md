# Unbuttressed Newtonian grounding-line stress

↑ **Parent:** [Grounding line](grounding-line.md)

For a two-dimensional [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) [ice](ice.md) sheet attached to an unbuttressed [ice shelf](ice-shelf.md), [ice-sheet flotation](ice-sheet-flotation.md) gives $\rho H=\rho_wb$. The difference between the depth-integrated [ice](ice.md) and ocean [hydrostatic pressures](hydrostatic-pressure.md) is $\rho gH^2/2-\rho_wgb^2/2=\rho g'H^2/2$, where $g'=g(1-\rho/\rho_w)$. Shelf force balance integrates $4\mu Hu_x-\rho g'H^2/2$ to a constant; absence of a buttressing force makes that constant zero. The [shallow-shelf approximation](shallow-shelf-approximation.md) therefore gives the displayed boundary condition, or $u_x=g'H/(8\nu)$ with [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu=\mu/\rho$.
