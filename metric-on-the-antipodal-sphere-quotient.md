# Metric on the antipodal sphere quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md)

The displayed metric on antipodal classes of the unit sphere induces its [quotient topology](quotient-topology.md). [Triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) follows by choosing the signs that realize each minimum and applying the Euclidean [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md). Distinct classes have disjoint saturated unions of small balls, proving the quotient is Hausdorff; compactness follows from the continuous quotient map.
