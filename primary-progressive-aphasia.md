# Primary progressive aphasia

↑ **Parent:** [Aphasia](aphasia.md)

A clinical syndrome with progressive [language](language.md) impairment as the principal initial problem. Detailed profiles distinguish loss of word meaning, motor [speech](speech.md) difficulty, [agrammatism](agrammatism.md) and phonological-memory demands; a syndrome label is not a unique anatomical lesion or molecular pathology.
