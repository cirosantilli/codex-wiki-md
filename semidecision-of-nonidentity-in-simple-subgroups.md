# Semidecision of nonidentity in simple subgroups

↑ **Parent:** [Boone-Higman theorem](boone-higman-theorem.md)

Let $S$ be a nontrivial [simple group](simple-group.md) inside a [finitely presented group](finitely-presented-group.md) $P$, and fix $a\ne1$ in $S$. For an input word $w$ in $S$, enumerate products of conjugates in $P$ of $w^{\pm1}$ and proofs in $P$ that such a product equals $a$. This search terminates exactly when $w\ne1$: the [normal closure](normal-closure.md) of a nontrivial element of $S$ is all of $S$, and its conjugators are also elements of $P$, while a null word has only trivial conjugates. Run this search alongside the enumerable proofs that $w=1$. Exactly one search terminates, solving the [word problem for a group](word-problem-for-groups.md) on any specified finitely generated subgroup of $S$. No effective generating set for the possibly infinitely generated $S$ is needed.
