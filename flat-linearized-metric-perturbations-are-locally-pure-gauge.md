# Flat linearized metric perturbations are locally pure gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge invariance of the linearized Riemann tensor](gauge-invariance-of-the-linearized-riemann-tensor.md)

On a [contractible](contractible-space.md) Cartesian patch of a flat background, zero [linearized Riemann curvature operator](linearized-riemann-curvature-operator.md) says that the [connection 1-forms](connection-1-form-split.md) $\Gamma^{(1)a}{}_{bc}\,dx^c$ are closed. The [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) writes them as [derivatives](derivative.md) of a matrix-valued potential. Symmetry of the two lower [affine connection](affine-connection.md) indices then supplies a second potential, giving $\Gamma^{(1)a}{}_{bc}=\partial_b\partial_c\xi^a$. Linearized [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) implies that $h_{ab}-2\partial_{(a}\xi_{b)}$ is constant. Its symmetric constant part is absorbed by an affine [vector field](vector-field.md). Thus the [metric perturbation](linearized-gravity.md) is locally a coordinate change. Global [topology](topology-split.md) and imposed [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) can obstruct the corresponding global gauge choice.
