# Elliptic divisibility sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Divisibility sequence](divisibility-sequence.md)

A [divisibility sequence](divisibility-sequence.md) associated with multiples of a point on an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md), using suitably normalized [division polynomials](division-polynomials.md). With $W_0=0$ and $W_1=1$, its terms satisfy the Ward identity $W_{m+n}W_{m-n}=W_{m+1}W_{m-1}W_n^2-W_{n+1}W_{n-1}W_m^2$. Setting nearby indices gives doubling identities, enabling logarithmic-depth evaluation.
