# Track reconstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

Inferring a [particle](particle-physics.md) [trajectory](trajectory.md) from recorded detector positions or a continuous trail. The inferred [trajectory](trajectory.md) depends on [spatial resolution](spatial-resolution.md), detector geometry and the assumed particle-motion model; it is not a direct picture of the [particle](particle-physics.md) itself.
