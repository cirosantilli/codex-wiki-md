# Tensioned-sheet acoustic impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wave on a tensioned massive membrane](acoustic-wave-on-a-tensioned-massive-membrane.md)

A sheet of [mass](mass.md) per area $m$ and [elastic-sheet tension](elastic-sheet-tension.md) $T$ backed by an identical outgoing fluid half-space presents the stated [surface acoustic impedance](surface-acoustic-impedance.md) to the other half-space, using upward sheet [velocity](velocity.md) $i\omega\eta=-V$. The fluid term is its [normal acoustic impedance](normal-acoustic-impedance.md); the remaining term is structural. Infinite [elastic-sheet tension](elastic-sheet-tension.md) suppresses every fixed nonzero spatial wavenumber, but not $k=0$, whereas infinite [mass](mass.md) suppresses even spatially uniform motion. A massless untensioned sheet between identical fluids is transparent.
