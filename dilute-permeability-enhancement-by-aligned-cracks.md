# Dilute permeability enhancement by aligned cracks

↑ **Parent:** [Effective permeability](effective-permeability.md)

At small areal number density $\phi$, identical aligned cracks contribute independently the [fixed-throughflow dissipation reduction of a conductive crack](fixed-throughflow-dissipation-reduction-of-a-conductive-crack.md). Equating the mean dissipation density to $\mu U^2/k_{xx}^*$ gives the displayed first-order enhancement. Writing $k/[1-\pi\phi a^2\alpha/(1+\alpha)]$ is an algebraic noninteraction estimate, not a claim of accuracy at higher density. The enhancement saturates as crack conductance becomes large compared with transport through the surrounding porous matrix.
