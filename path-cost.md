# Path cost

↑ **Parent:** [Search problem](search-problem.md)

The sum of step costs along the action history represented by a [search node](search-node.md). Positive costs give lower bounds on [search depth](search-depth.md), and the same physical state can have different [path costs](path-cost.md) when reached along different histories. [Uniform-cost search](uniform-cost-search.md) orders by accumulated cost; [A-star search](a-star-search.md) adds an estimate of remaining cost.
