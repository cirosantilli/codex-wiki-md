# Early two-stage electron microscope experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscope](electron-microscope.md)

[Ernst Ruska](ernst-ruska.md) and [Max Knoll](max-knoll.md) developed [electron optics](electron-optics.md) while improving the writing spot of a [cathode-ray oscilloscope](cathode-ray-oscilloscope.md).[Ruska, Nobel lecture, §C; RMP reprint pp. 628–629](the-development-of-the-electron-microscope-and-of-electron-microscopy-by-ernst-ruska.md) A structured object tests [image formation](image-formation.md) more strongly than a single bright spot: each feature must retain its own image position.

On 7 April 1931, a [cold cathode](cold-cathode.md) source and two short magnetic coils imaged aperture grids at $50\,\mathrm{kV}$. A [platinum](platinum.md) grid preceded the first coil; a bronze grid preceded the second. The [fluorescent screen](fluorescent-screen.md) showed one-stage grid images and the first grid reimaged through both stages. Comparing these patterns tested successive [image formation](image-formation.md), not merely beam concentration. Heating could destroy even metal grids. This prototype did not establish resolution beyond an [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md).[Ruska, §E, Figs. 2–3; RMP reprint p. 630: dated one-stage and two-stage comparison](the-development-of-the-electron-microscope-and-of-electron-microscopy-by-ernst-ruska.md)

In 1933, Ruska measured $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ [focal lengths](focal-length.md) at $75\,\mathrm{kV}$ with a pole-piece lens, then built an instrument giving $12000$-fold [magnification](magnification.md).[Ruska, §E, Figs. 4–5; RMP reprint p. 631](the-development-of-the-electron-microscope-and-of-electron-microscopy-by-ernst-ruska.md) TU Berlin dates images surpassing conventional [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) resolution to 1933.[TU Berlin, indexed historical biography: 1933 prototype surpassing light-microscope resolution](ernst-ruska-by-technische-universitat-berlin.md)

A [fluorescent screen](fluorescent-screen.md) converts the spatial [electron](electron.md) distribution into visible [light](light.md).[JEOL, The difference between Electron Beam and Light: fluorescent image readout](transmission-electron-microscope-tem-by-jeol.md) A collector reporting only total [electric current](electric-current.md) would discard that spatial pattern. In a two-stage image relay, the first lens makes an intermediate [real image](real-image.md); the second treats that image as its object.
