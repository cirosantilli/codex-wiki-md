# Euclidean bounce

↑ **Parent:** [Instanton](instanton.md)

A [Euclidean bounce](euclidean-bounce.md) is a finite-action Euclidean saddle associated with decay of a [false vacuum](false-vacuum.md). It returns to the same metastable vacuum at the two ends of Euclidean time, instead of joining two degenerate stable vacua. Its characteristic negative fluctuation direction produces the imaginary part associated with a decay rate; the leading semiclassical suppression is $e^{-S_B/\hbar}$. Translation zero modes are treated as collective coordinates. It should not be confused with the stable double-well tunnelling instanton whose repeated crossings give a real level splitting.
