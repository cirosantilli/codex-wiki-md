# Reciprocal Hertzian-dipole echo

↑ **Parent:** [Friis transmission equation](friis-transmission-equation.md)

Two aligned lossless [Hertzian dipoles](hertzian-dipole.md) in one another's broadside [radiation zone](radiation-zone.md) have gain $G=3/2$ and one-way received-power fraction $\eta=G^2[\lambda/(4\pi r)]^2$. If the second remains conjugately matched, its [matched-load scattering of a Hertzian dipole](matched-load-scattering-of-a-hertzian-dipole.md) reradiates the same [power](power.md) as its load absorbs. Applying the reciprocal far-field link on the return gives $P_{\rm echo}=P_t\eta^2$, proportional to $r^{-4}$. The statement concerns physical dipole reradiation; a pure [antenna load-mismatch echo](antenna-load-mismatch-echo.md) model omits that scattering and is not interchangeable with it.
