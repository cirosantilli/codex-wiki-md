# Toroidal magnetic-field winding equation

↑ **Parent:** [Toroidal magnetic field](toroidal-magnetic-field.md)

In axisymmetric [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md), $\Omega=u_\phi/R$ is the material angular velocity. Cylindrical basis-vector derivatives and [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) give the displayed winding equation. Thus $B_\phi/(\rho R)$ is an advected scalar only when $\mathbf B_p\cdot\nabla\Omega=0$. Differential rotation along a field line otherwise produces toroidal field. This does not violate [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md), which concerns flux through a material surface rather than a cylindrical vector component.
