# Quasi-one-dimensional quantum transport

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum transport](quantum-transport.md)

A narrow conductor has discrete transverse modes and nearly continuous longitudinal motion. Its conductance depends on the transmissions of occupied [quantum wire](quantum-wire.md) subbands, spin counting and contacts. The [Landauer formula](landauer-formula.md) connects these transmissions to two-terminal conductance. Disorder, interference, dephasing and interactions distinguish ballistic, diffusive, localized and interacting regimes.
