# Maximum total angular momentum of equivalent fermions

↑ **Parent:** [Addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md)

For $N$ identical [fermions](fermion.md) restricted to one angular-momentum multiplet $j$, with all other one-particle labels fixed, the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) permits distinct [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) projections $j,j-1,\ldots,j-N+1$. Their sum is $Nj-N(N-1)/2$, provided $N\leq2j+1$. The resulting [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md) is killed by the total raising [angular momentum ladder operator](angular-momentum-ladder-operator.md) because raising any occupied state either exceeds $j$ or duplicates an occupied state. It is therefore a [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md) of precisely this total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). The formula is invariant under replacing particles by holes in the full shell.
