# Random transposition shuffle

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain.md)

Choose two positions independently and uniformly from $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and swap them. This [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) on the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) holds with probability $1/n$ and assigns probability $2/n^2$ to each unordered [transposition](transposition-permutation.md). Its [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) is uniform, and its ordinary [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) is $2/n$ for $n\geq2$. The latter follows from the [Aldous spectral gap theorem](aldous-spectral-gap-theorem.md): the single-label [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) has rate $2/n^2$ between every pair of positions, whose mean-zero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are all $-2/n$.
