# SU6 tensor branchings to SU5

↑ **Parent:** [Branching of a representation](branching-of-a-representation.md)

For the embedding $U\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(1,U)$, the defining [SU(6)](su-6.md) [representation](group-representation.md) restricts to the defining [SU(5)](su-5.md) [representation](group-representation.md) plus a [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md). Taking [dual representations](dual-representation.md), [symmetric squares](symmetric-square.md), [exterior squares](exterior-square.md) and traceless [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) gives $\overline{\mathbf6}\downarrow=\overline{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf1$, $\mathbf{21}\downarrow=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf1$, $\mathbf{15}\downarrow=\mathbf{10}\oplus\mathbf5$, and $\mathbf{35}\downarrow=\mathbf{24}\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\overline{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf1$. The fixed diagonal direction in the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is $\operatorname{diag}(-5,1,1,1,1,1)$. Dimensions label [representations](group-representation.md) of different [groups](group-split.md) on the two sides: the fifteen-dimensional [SU(6)](su-6.md) [representation](group-representation.md) is alternating, while the fifteen-dimensional [SU(5)](su-5.md) [representation](group-representation.md) is symmetric.
