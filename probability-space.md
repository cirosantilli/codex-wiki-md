# Probability space

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_space)

A probability space is a [measure space](measure-space.md) whose total measure is one. Its measurable subsets are [events](event.md), and its measure $\mathbb P$ is their probability.
