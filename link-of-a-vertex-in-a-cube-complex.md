# Link of a vertex in a cube complex

↑ **Parent:** [Cubical complex](cubical-complex.md)

The link of a vertex $v$ records the local directions from $v$: its vertices are incident edge germs, and a collection spans a simplex exactly when the corresponding edge germs lie in a common cube.
