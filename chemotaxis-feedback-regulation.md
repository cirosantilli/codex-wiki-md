# Chemotaxis feedback regulation

↑ **Parent:** [Negative feedback](negative-feedback.md)

In [Escherichia coli](escherichia-coli.md), [chemotaxis feedback regulation](chemotaxis-feedback-regulation.md) couples [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) activity to modification of the [receptors](receptor-biochemistry.md) by [CheR](cher.md) and [CheB](cheb.md). It is a molecular example of [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md) supporting [signaling adaptation](signaling-adaptation.md), with precision and kinetics assessed by the [chemotaxis FRET assay](chemotaxis-fret-assay.md) above.
