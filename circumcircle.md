# Circumcircle

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

The [circumcircle](circumcircle.md) of a nondegenerate [triangle](triangle.md) is the unique [circle](circle.md) through its three vertices. Its center is the [circumcenter](circumcenter.md), found by intersecting two [perpendicular bisectors](perpendicular-bisector.md); equality of distances to all three vertices proves existence, and subtracting the squared-distance equations proves uniqueness. For a [right triangle](right-triangle.md), the midpoint of its hypotenuse is equidistant from all vertices, so its circumradius is half the hypotenuse length.
