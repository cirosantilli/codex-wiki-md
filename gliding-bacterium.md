# Gliding bacterium

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell.md)

A [gliding bacterium](gliding-bacterium.md) moves along a surface without swimming through the bulk fluid. In a thin-fluid-film model, its relative motion dissipates energy through [Couette flow](couette-flow.md), although the actuation mechanism can involve additional dissipation.
