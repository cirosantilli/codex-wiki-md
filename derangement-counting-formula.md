# Derangement counting formula

↑ **Parent:** [Derangement in a transitive group action](derangement-in-a-transitive-group-action.md)

[Inclusion-exclusion](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) applied to the events that a [permutation](permutation.md) fixes a specified point gives the displayed formula. There are $\binom nk$ choices of $k$ fixed points and $(n-k)!$ permutations fixing them. The probability that a uniformly chosen permutation is a [derangement](derangement-of-a-permutation.md) tends to $e^{-1}$.
