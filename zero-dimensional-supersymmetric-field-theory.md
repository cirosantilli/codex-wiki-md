# Zero-dimensional supersymmetric field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md)

A zero-dimensional model is a finite-dimensional integral with bosonic and [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md), rather than a field depending on spacetime. Odd [graded derivations](graded-derivation.md) preserve its action and integration measure. Such models expose [supersymmetric Ward identities](supersymmetric-ward-identity.md) and [supersymmetric localization](supersymmetric-localization.md) without spacetime dynamics.
