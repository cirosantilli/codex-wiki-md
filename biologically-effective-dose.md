# Biologically effective dose

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation therapy](radiation-therapy.md)

An effect-equivalent dose derived from the [linear-quadratic radiobiology model](linear-quadratic-radiobiology-model.md), with a subscript often indicating the tissue's assumed $\alpha/\beta$ in gray. It is distinct from the physical total [absorbed dose](absorbed-dose.md) $Nd$. Increasing fraction size at fixed tumour BED can increase the normal-tissue BED when the latter has smaller $\alpha/\beta$.
