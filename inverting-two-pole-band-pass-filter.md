# Inverting two-pole band-pass filter

↑ **Parent:** [Inverting amplifier](inverting-amplifier.md)

An ideal inverting [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md) with series $R_1,C_1$ input and parallel $R_2,C_2$ feedback has the displayed [transfer function](transfer-function.md), where $K=R_2/R_1$ and $\tau_i=R_iC_i$. Its peak is at $\omega_0=1/\sqrt{\tau_1\tau_2}$ with voltage gain $K\tau_1/(\tau_1+\tau_2)$. A wide input-high-pass/feedback-low-pass separation produces a broad plateau, not a narrow [resonance](resonance.md). Validity requires stable [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md), sufficient loop gain and linear output operation.
