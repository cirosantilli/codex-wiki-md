# Eisenstein series of weight two

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md)

The series

$$
E_2(\tau)=1-24\sum_{n\geq1}\sigma_1(n)q^n
$$

is quasimodular: $E_2(-1/\tau)=\tau^2E_2(\tau)+6\tau/(\pi i)$.
