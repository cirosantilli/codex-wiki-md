# Parity of monic orthogonal polynomials for an even weight

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md)

Assume a positive even weight on a symmetric domain has finite [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) moments. Subtracting the [orthogonal projection onto a finite-dimensional subspace](orthogonal-projection-onto-a-finite-dimensional-subspace.md) of $x^n$ onto the lower-degree [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) gives the unique monic [orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md). Reflection produces another such [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md), $(-1)^n\phi_n(-x)$, so uniqueness gives the parity identity. Every coefficient with the opposite parity to its degree therefore vanishes.
