# Magnetospheric truncation radius

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetically channelled accretion](magnetically-channelled-accretion.md)

The inner radius at which a stellar [magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md) disrupts a surrounding [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md). In a [free fall](free-fall.md) [pressure](pressure-split.md)-balance estimate with fixed aspect ratio $\epsilon=H/r$, $r_m\sim(\epsilon^2\mu^4/(GM\dot M^2))^{1/7}$. Geometry and inflow structure change its numerical coefficient.
