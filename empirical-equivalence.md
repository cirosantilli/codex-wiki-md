# Empirical equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of science](philosophy-of-science.md)

Two scientific accounts are empirically equivalent relative to a stated domain when they give the same observable consequences there. Agreement on the observations so far is weaker than agreement on every possible observation.
