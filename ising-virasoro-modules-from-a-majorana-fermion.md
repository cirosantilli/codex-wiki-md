# Ising Virasoro modules from a Majorana fermion

↑ **Parent:** [Free chiral Majorana fermion conformal field theory](free-chiral-majorana-fermion-conformal-field-theory.md)

A chiral [Majorana fermion](majorana-spinor.md) with modes $\{b_r,b_s\}=\delta_{r+s,0}$ and $b_r^\dagger=b_{-r}$ has $T=-:\psi\partial\psi:/2$ and [central charge](central-charge.md) $1/2$. The even and odd fermion-number parts of its [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md) give unitary modules with lowest weights $0$ and $1/2$. In its [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md), a choice of ground-state zero-mode eigenvalue gives the module of weight $1/16$; the other choice gives an isomorphic [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md) module. Their cyclic highest-weight submodules are irreducible: a positive-energy invariant proper submodule would have a lowest vector orthogonal to every descendant of the original highest vector, which is impossible in a positive inner-product cyclic module.
