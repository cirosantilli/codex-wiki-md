# Algebraic quotient by a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md)

For an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) with [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) $A$ acted on by a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$, the affine quotient is $\operatorname{Spec}A^G$. Elements of $A$ satisfy monic orbit polynomials over $A^G$, so the quotient map is a [finite morphism](finite-morphism.md). Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), invariant functions separate finite orbits; in characteristic not dividing $|G|$, interpolate and average a function separating two orbits. The quotient fibres are consequently the orbits.
