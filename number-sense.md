# Number sense

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical cognition](numerical-cognition.md)

A broad term for intuitions and skills concerning [numbers](number.md) and quantities. Studies use the term for different tasks and representations, so a [number](number.md)-sense score or training effect should not be assumed to identify one process or general mathematical ability.
