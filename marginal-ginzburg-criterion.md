# Marginal Ginzburg criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg criterion](ginzburg-criterion.md)

At an [upper critical dimension](upper-critical-dimension.md), the leading [Ginzburg criterion](ginzburg-criterion.md) ratio has zero thermal power rather than a negative one. It does not vanish asymptotically, but its magnitude can depend on the coupling. The criterion alone does not establish a power-law divergence or new critical power exponents. Running marginal interactions generally produce logarithmic corrections; the ordinary quartic and tricritical sextic scalar theories are examples.
