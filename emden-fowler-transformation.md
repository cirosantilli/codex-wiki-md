# Emden-Fowler transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear analysis](nonlinear-analysis-split.md)

The [Emden-Fowler transformation](emden-fowler-transformation.md) is a change of variables for scaling-invariant radial equations, including the [Lane-Emden equation](lane-emden-equation.md). The Emden-Fowler transformation replaces a radial variable $r$ by logarithmic time $s=\log r$ and rescales the dependent variable by a power of $r$. It converts many scale-invariant radial equations into autonomous ordinary differential equations.
