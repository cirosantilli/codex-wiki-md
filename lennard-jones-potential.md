# Lennard-Jones potential

↑ **Parent:** [Potential energy](potential-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lennard-Jones_potential)

Named after [John Lennard-Jones](john-lennard-jones.md), this is a pair [potential energy](potential-energy.md) combining steep short-range repulsion with nonretarded [London dispersion force](london-dispersion-force.md) attraction. Its minimum is $U=-\epsilon$ at $r=2^{1/6}\sigma$, found by differentiation. The power twelve is a convenient model for repulsion, rather than an exact consequence of the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md). The power six in the attractive energy follows from second-order coupling of atomic [electric dipoles](electric-dipole.md), before retardation matters.
