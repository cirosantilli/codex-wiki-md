# Approximate weakly null lifting through a quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient Banach space](quotient-banach-space.md)

If a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $X$ has separable dual and $q:X\to X/Y$ is a quotient by a closed subspace, every [weakly null sequence](weakly-null-sequence.md) in the quotient's closed unit ball has a subsequence with approximate weakly null lifts of norm at most $3$. Choose representatives of norm below $3/2$, pass to a common bidual weak-star limit, and subtract [convex blocks](convex-block.md) whose quotient images tend to zero in norm. [Convex-block cancellation of a weak-star limit](convex-block-cancellation-of-a-weak-star-limit.md) gives weak nullity and the triangle inequality gives the bound $3$.
