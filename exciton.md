# Exciton

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exciton)

An [exciton](exciton.md) is a [bound state](bound-state.md) of an electron and an [electron hole](electron-hole.md). Coupling this material excitation strongly to a [photon](photon.md) can form a [polariton](polariton.md).
