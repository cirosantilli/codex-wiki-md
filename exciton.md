# Exciton

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exciton)

An [exciton](exciton.md) is a [bound state](bound-state.md) of an [Electron](electron.md) and an [electron hole](electron-hole.md). Their opposite [electric charges](electric-charge.md) attract through the [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md), producing a neutral [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) with internal relative motion and motion of its [center of mass](center-of-mass.md). In a [semiconductor](semiconductor.md), promoting an [Electron](electron.md) from the [valence band](valence-band.md) into the [conduction band](conduction-band.md) leaves an [electron hole](electron-hole.md); the pair can occupy either an [exciton](exciton.md) [bound state](bound-state.md) or an unbound [scattering state](scattering-state.md). Merely creating the two [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md) does not establish binding. A neutral [exciton](exciton.md) can transport excitation [energy](energy.md) without transporting net [electric charge](electric-charge.md). Coupling this material excitation strongly to a [photon](photon.md) can form a [polariton](polariton.md).

The striking optical consequence is that an [exciton optical transition](exciton-optical-transition.md) can absorb a [photon](photon.md) with less [energy](energy.md) than the threshold for creating unbound [Electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md). The difference is the [exciton binding energy](exciton-binding-energy.md), not an extra one-electron [energy band](energy-band.md) inside the [band gap](band-gap.md). The [Wannier-Mott exciton](wannier-mott-exciton.md) supplies a useful large-radius model; the [Frenkel exciton](frenkel-exciton.md) describes the opposite, tightly localized limit.[§3, printed pp. 4–6: bound pairs, effective masses and localized contrast](excitons-in-semiconductor-quantum-dots-by-matjaz-humar.md)
