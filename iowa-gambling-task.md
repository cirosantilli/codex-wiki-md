# Iowa gambling task

↑ **Parent:** [Decision theory](decision-theory.md)

A choice task in which options differ in immediate outcomes and their accumulated future consequences. Performance combines learning, valuation, [human memory](human-memory.md) and uncertainty; disadvantageous choices do not uniquely diagnose one emotional mechanism.
