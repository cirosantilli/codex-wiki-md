# G protein

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G_protein)

A [G protein](g-protein.md) is a [protein](protein.md) switch whose activity depends on the bound [guanine](guanine.md) [nucleotide](nucleotide.md). The three-subunit [G proteins](g-protein.md) coupled to [G protein-coupled receptors](g-protein-coupled-receptor.md) relay activation to other [proteins](protein.md); they are distinct from the [receptors](receptor-biochemistry.md) themselves. See the Alberts reference under [G protein-coupled receptor](g-protein-coupled-receptor.md).
