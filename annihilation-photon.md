# Annihilation photon

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-positron annihilation](electron-positron-annihilation.md)

For two-photon [positron annihilation](electron-positron-annihilation.md) with negligible initial total momentum, each [photon](photon.md) has energy $m_ec^2$ and the two propagate oppositely. Their common energy differs from the positron's continuous emission-energy spectrum before slowing in matter.
