# Infinite-arity partition relation

↑ **Parent:** [Partition relation](partition-relation.md)

A [partition relation](partition-relation.md) whose colored subsets are infinite. For countably infinite arity, choosing representatives modulo finite [symmetric difference](symmetric-difference.md) and coloring its parity shows $\kappa\nrightarrow(\omega)^\omega_2$ for every infinite cardinal $\kappa$.
