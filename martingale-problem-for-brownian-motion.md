# Martingale problem for Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale problem](martingale-problem.md)

A continuous adapted process started at a fixed point is [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) exactly when $f(X_t)-f(X_0)-\frac12\int_0^t\Delta f(X_s)ds$ is a [martingale](martingale-split.md) for every $f\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^d)$. Cutoff coordinate and coordinate-product tests give continuous [local martingales](local-martingale.md) with [quadratic covariations](quadratic-covariation.md) $[X^i,X^j]_t=\delta_{ij}t$. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) makes $\exp(iu\cdot(X_t-X_0)+|u|^2t/2)$ a local martingale, whose deterministic modulus bound on bounded horizons makes it a true martingale. Its conditional expectation identifies every increment as centered Gaussian with covariance $(t-s)I$, independent of the past. This proves the converse via the [Lévy characterization of multidimensional Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-multidimensional-brownian-motion.md). The forward implication is the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md).
