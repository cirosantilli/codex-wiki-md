# Poincare inequality on an annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md)

For $n\geq2$, a round [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) $A_R=B_{2R}\setminus B_R$ is [connected](connected-space.md) and obeys $\int_{A_R}|v-v_{A_R}|^2\leq C(n)R^2\int_{A_R}|\nabla v|^2$. Scaling reduces the claim to a fixed [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md). In dimension one the [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) has two components, so the inequality fails with a single average; one needs a separate average on each component.
