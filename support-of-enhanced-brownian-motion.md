# Support of enhanced Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Enhanced Brownian motion](enhanced-brownian-motion.md)

For $2<p<3$, the [support of a measure](support-of-a-measure.md) of [enhanced Brownian motion](enhanced-brownian-motion.md) is the rough-path closure of [path signatures](signature-of-a-bounded-variation-path.md) of paths in the [Cameron-Martin space of Wiener measure](cameron-martin-space-of-wiener-measure.md). Polygonal convergence gives one inclusion. For the reverse inclusion, decompose [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) into its polygonal interpolation and independent pinned bridges. The bridge enhancement becomes small in probability as the mesh decreases, while every finite-dimensional neighborhood of the polygonal vertices has positive Gaussian probability. [Rough path translation](rough-path-translation.md) then gives positive probability in every ball around a prescribed control [path signature](signature-of-a-bounded-variation-path.md).
