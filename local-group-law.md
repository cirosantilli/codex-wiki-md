# Local group law

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

A local group law is the coordinate expression $z=F(y,x)$ of multiplication near the identity of a [Lie group](lie-group.md). The identity, inverse, and associativity equations for $F$ encode the group structure locally.
