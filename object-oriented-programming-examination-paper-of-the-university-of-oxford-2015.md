# Object Oriented Programming examination paper of the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The seven-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) [computer science](computer-science-split.md) Finals paper dated Thursday 11 June 2015, 2:30 pm–4:00 pm. The released [public examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains it as `2015-oop.pdf`. Questions 1–3 occupy printed pp. 2–7; Question 3 is explicitly marked corrected. The calendar example on p. 3 interprets its starting offset in weekday cells. The axes upper-corner expression on p. 5 has an extra closing parenthesis; its coordinatewise bounds remain readable. Figure 3 on p. 7 shows the supporting baseplate.
