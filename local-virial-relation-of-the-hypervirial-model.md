# Local virial relation of the hypervirial model

↑ **Parent:** [Hypervirial model](hypervirial-model.md)

For the [hypervirial model](hypervirial-model.md), $\langle v^2\rangle=\Psi/2=-\Phi/2$. The local kinetic- and potential-energy densities

$$
K=\frac12\rho\langle v^2\rangle,
\qquad
W=\frac12\rho\Phi
$$

therefore obey $2K+W=0$ at every radius, a stronger statement than the integrated [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md).
