# Alpheus

↑ **Parent:** [Crustacean](crustacean.md)

A genus of snapping shrimp. Comparisons of related [Alpheus](alpheus.md) lineages separated by the Isthmus of Panama have been used to study geographical isolation and [allopatric speciation](allopatric-speciation.md); inferred separation times vary among pairs.
