# Statistical estimation

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

Statistical estimation uses observed data to infer numerical properties of a population or model. A point [estimator](estimator.md) gives a value, while a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) describes uncertainty with a specified coverage property. [Maximum likelihood estimation](maximum-likelihood-estimation.md) is one approach; bias, variance, identifiability and the sampling or selection mechanism all affect an estimate's interpretation.
