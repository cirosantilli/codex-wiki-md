# Positive-expansion convention for extrinsic curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface](extrinsic-curvature-of-a-spatial-hypersurface.md)

With a future unit timelike normal in signature $(-,+,+,+)$, this convention makes the [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md) trace equal to the normal congruence's expansion, $K=\nabla_\mu n^\mu$. An expanding flat [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) has $K_{ij}=a'\delta_{ij}$ and $K=3a'/a^2$. This is the opposite sign to the negative-expansion convention used in many [3+1 decomposition of spacetime](3-plus-1-decomposition-of-spacetime.md) formulas. Projection identities and momentum constraints must use the same convention throughout.
