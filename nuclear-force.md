# Nuclear force

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_force)

The residual [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md) between [nucleons](nucleon.md). Its long-distance part involves [pion](pion.md) exchange and depends on [spin](spin.md) and [isospin](isospin.md). It is distinct from directly exchanging gluons between color-singlet nucleons.
