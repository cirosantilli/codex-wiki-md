# Rod cell

↑ **Parent:** [Photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md)

A [photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md) class important for low-light vision. Greater sensitivity and downstream pooling support detection under different conditions from cone-dominated vision. When only one spectral receptor class supplies usable information, the principle of univariance prevents independent [wavelength](wavelength.md)–[intensity](intensity-physics.md) identification.
