# Logical depth of a measurement pattern

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement pattern](measurement-pattern.md)

Logical measurement depth is the number of sequential measurement layers required by outcome-dependent basis choices. Depth one means all bases are fixed in advance, so the [measurement in quantum measurements](quantum-measurement-split.md) on distinct [qubits](qubit.md) can be simultaneous. Classical output corrections may still depend on all outcomes. This does not assert depth-one graph-state preparation or constant-depth classical processing.
