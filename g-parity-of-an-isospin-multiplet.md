# G-parity of an isospin multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [G parity](g-parity.md)

For integer [isospin](isospin.md) $I$ and neutral-state [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) value $\eta$, the entire [isospin multiplet](isospin-multiplet.md) has $G=\eta(-1)^I$. Both charge conjugation and the second-axis half-turn reverse the first and third generators, so their product commutes with the full isospin algebra. Evaluate it on the $M=0$ state and use ladder connectivity to obtain the value for every $M$.
