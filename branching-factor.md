# Branching factor

↑ **Parent:** [Search tree](search-tree.md)

The number of children of a node in a [search tree](search-tree.md), or an upper bound on that number over the tree. With branching factor at most $b$ and depth at most $d$, a complete traversal visits at most $1+b+\cdots+b^d$ nodes.
