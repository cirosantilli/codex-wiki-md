# Weierstrass sigma function

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

For a [period lattice](period-lattice.md), the displayed canonical product converges normally because its logarithmic tails are bounded by a constant times $|\omega|^{-3}$. It defines an odd entire function with simple zeros exactly at the lattice and derivative one at zero. Its logarithmic derivative is the [Weierstrass zeta function](weierstrass-zeta-function.md), and the derivative of that logarithmic derivative is minus the [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md). Sigma is quasi-periodic rather than elliptic; balanced products of its translates construct meromorphic functions with prescribed [divisors](divisor.md) on a complex torus.
