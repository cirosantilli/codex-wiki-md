# Arrow category

↑ **Parent:** [Functor category](functor-category.md)

The arrow category of $\mathcal C$ has morphisms $A_0\to A_1$ of $\mathcal C$ as objects and commutative squares as morphisms. It is the [functor category](functor-category.md) $[\mathbf 2,\mathcal C]$, where $\mathbf 2$ is the category with one nonidentity arrow.

It is also the [comma category](comma-category.md) $(\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal C}\downarrow\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal C})$.
