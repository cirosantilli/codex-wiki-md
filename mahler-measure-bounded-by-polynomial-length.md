# Mahler measure bounded by polynomial length

↑ **Parent:** [Mahler measure](mahler-measure.md)

For every complex polynomial $P$,

$$
M(P)\leq\mathcal L(P).
$$

This follows from Jensen's formula, or directly by bounding $|P(z)|$ by its [polynomial length](polynomial-length.md) on the unit circle and using the integral formula for the [Mahler measure](mahler-measure.md).
