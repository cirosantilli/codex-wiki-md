# Travel-time coordinate for a one-dimensional variable-speed wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md)

For a one-dimensional [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) whose [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) is $\xi_t^2-c(y)^2\xi_y^2$, a travel-time coordinate is

$$
s(y)=\int^y\frac{dr}{c(r)}.
$$

Its two families of [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) are $s(y)-t=\text{constant}$ and $s(y)+t=\text{constant}$ wherever the [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $c$ is positive.
