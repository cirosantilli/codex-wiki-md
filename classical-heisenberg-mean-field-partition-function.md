# Classical Heisenberg mean-field partition function

↑ **Parent:** [Classical Heisenberg model](classical-heisenberg-model.md)

For unit three-component classical [spins](spin.md) and energy $-J\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}\mathbf S_i\cdot\mathbf S_j$ on a lattice of coordination $z_c$, mean-field decoupling gives $E_{\rm MF}=-z_cJS\sum_iS_{iz}+Nz_cJS^2/2$. Integrating each [spin](spin.md) over [solid angle](solid-angle.md) yields

$$
Z_{\rm MF}(S)=e^{-\beta Nz_cJS^2/2}
[4\pi\sinh(\beta z_cJS)/(\beta z_cJS)]^N.
$$

The constant term corrects double counting. A normalized angular measure removes $(4\pi)^N$ but does not change the [self-consistency equation](self-consistency-equation.md). The lattice dimension and spin-space dimension are different data.
