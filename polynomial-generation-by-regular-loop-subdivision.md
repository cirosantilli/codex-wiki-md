# Polynomial generation by regular Loop subdivision

↑ **Parent:** [Loop subdivision surface](loop-subdivision-surface.md)

For the three unit-grid edge vectors $v_1,v_2,v_3$ and a [multivariate polynomial](multivariate-polynomial.md) $f$ of total degree at most three, sampled regular-grid [Loop subdivision](loop-subdivision-surface.md) generates the graph of $f+\frac1{12}\sum_{r=1}^3(v_r\cdot\nabla)^2f$. Thus affine polynomials are reproduced exactly and all polynomials through degree three retain their degree. Fourth-degree data constant along one edge direction reduce to a [Cardinal cubic B-spline](cardinal-cubic-b-spline.md), whose limit need not be a polynomial.
