# Effector saturation

↑ **Parent:** [Physiological effector](physiological-effector.md)

[Effector saturation](effector-saturation.md) is a capacity limit beyond which a larger command cannot produce a larger corrective action. In the [homeostatic temperature model](homeostatic-temperature-model.md), take $u=\min(4x,1/2)$ during the sustained unit disturbance. Before reaching the cap, $x=(1-e^{-5t})/5$. The cap is reached at $t_c=\log(8/3)/5$, when $x=1/8$. Thereafter

$$
x(t)=\frac12-\frac38e^{-(t-t_c)},\qquad u(t)=\frac12.
$$

The lasting displacement is now $1/2$, compared with $1/5$ without the cap. The [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md) command still asks for more cooling, but the action cannot supply it. This illustrates a capacity limit without predicting a human [temperature](temperature-split.md) limit. Increasing [feedback gain](feedback-gain.md) cannot remove a binding cap; [integral action](integral-action.md) cannot overcome inadequate physical capacity either.
