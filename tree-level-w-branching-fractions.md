# Tree-level W branching fractions

↑ **Parent:** [W boson](w-boson.md)

With three massless [lepton](lepton.md) families, two accessible up-type [quarks](quark.md), three colors, and no [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md) radiative correction, a [W boson](w-boson.md) has total [decay width](decay-width.md) $9\Gamma_\ell$. With an identity [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md), each leptonic channel has [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md) $1/9$ and each of the two allowed quark-flavour channels has $1/3$. With mixing, each accessible quark channel is weighted by $|V_{ij}|^2$, while row [unitarity](unitary-operator.md) preserves the total at this order if all down-type channels are accessible.
