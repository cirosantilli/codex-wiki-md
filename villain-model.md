# Villain model

↑ **Parent:** [XY model](xy-model.md)

A Villain model replaces the cosine [Boltzmann factor](boltzmann-factor.md) of the [XY model](xy-model.md) by a periodic [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) weight. The [Poisson summation formula](poisson-summation-formula.md) makes its bond weight equal to $\sum_{p\in\mathbb Z}\exp[-\beta(\psi-2\pi p)^2/2]$. The periodic Gaussian retains integer winding defects and permits an explicit separation into [spin waves](spin-wave.md) and [vortices](phase-vortex.md) in two dimensions. Its replacement of the cosine model is an approximation; subsequent transformations within the Villain model can be exact.
