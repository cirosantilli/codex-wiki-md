# Lie subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_subgroup)

A Lie subgroup is a subgroup carrying a [Lie group](lie-group.md) structure such that its inclusion is an injective [immersion](immersion.md) and a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md). Its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) includes in that of the ambient [Lie group](lie-group.md).
