# Eisenstein polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Local field](local-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisenstein_polynomial)

For a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) with [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\pi$, an Eisenstein polynomial is a monic polynomial whose nonleading coefficients are divisible by $\pi$ and whose constant coefficient is not divisible by $\pi^2$. It is [irreducible](irreducible-polynomial.md), and adjoining one of its roots gives a totally ramified extension.
