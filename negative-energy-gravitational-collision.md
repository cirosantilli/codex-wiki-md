# Negative-energy gravitational collision

↑ **Parent:** [Two-body problem](two-body-problem.md)

Two point [masses](mass.md) released from rest at separation $a$ have negative [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) $-Gm_1m_2/a$ but collide in time $\pi\sqrt{a^3/[8G(m_1+m_2)]}$. Their separation approaches zero along a regular trajectory before collision. The [energy](energy.md) sign alone therefore does not supply a positive lower separation bound.
