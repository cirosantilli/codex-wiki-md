# Planar domain

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md)

A connected open subset of the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md). For a bounded polygonal example, the [Dirichlet Laplacian](dirichlet-laplacian.md) is the self-adjoint operator associated with the [Dirichlet energy](dirichlet-energy.md) on $H_0^1(\Omega)$; its [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) are smooth in the interior but need not be smooth at reentrant boundary corners.
