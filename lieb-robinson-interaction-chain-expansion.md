# Lieb-Robinson interaction-chain expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Lieb-Robinson bound](lieb-robinson-bound.md)

Iterating the integral [commutator](commutator.md) inequality gives ordered interaction chains with factors $(2|t|)^n/n!$. A per-site interaction weight at most $se^{-\mu}$ bounds each successive chain extension by $ks e^{-\mu}$. No chain shorter than the [interaction distance](interaction-distance.md) connects separated supports. Reversing chains supplies the smaller support prefactor, producing the [Lieb-Robinson bound](lieb-robinson-bound.md). Overlapping supports require retaining the equal-time [commutator](commutator.md) term.
