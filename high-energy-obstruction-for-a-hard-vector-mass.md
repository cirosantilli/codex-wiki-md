# High-energy obstruction for a hard vector mass

↑ **Parent:** [Proca propagator](proca-propagator.md)

A free [Proca field](proca-field.md) is consistent and has three physical [particle polarizations](particle-polarization.md). The difficulty for generic interactions is the ultraviolet behavior of its [Proca propagator](proca-propagator.md): the longitudinal numerator makes part of it approach order $1/m^2$, rather than decay as $1/k^2$. Longitudinal external [polarization vectors](polarization-vector.md) also behave as $\varepsilon_L^\mu=k^\mu/m+O(m/E)$. Without suitable identities and cancellations, amplitudes grow with energy and perturbative counterterms of increasing dimension are needed. Conserved-current Abelian models and the [Stueckelberg mechanism](stueckelberg-mechanism.md) are important exceptions; a massive interacting vector is not intrinsically inconsistent as an [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md).
