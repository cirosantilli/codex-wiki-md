# KDPG

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[KDPG](kdpg.md) is 2-keto-3-deoxy-6-phosphogluconate, a sugar-phosphate intermediate that can serve as a ligand for [RccR](rccr.md). Binding can change transcriptional regulation without changing the regulator concentration.
