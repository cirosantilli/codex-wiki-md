# Donor-wind period invariant with fixed retention fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Donor-wind angular-momentum loss](donor-wind-angular-momentum-loss.md)

In a circular [binary star](binary-star.md) let the [donor star](donor-star.md) lose [mass](mass.md) $\dot M_1<0$, with constant retained fraction $f$ and $\dot M_2=-f\dot M_1$. A [donor star](donor-star.md) [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md) escaping with the [donor star](donor-star.md)'s orbital specific [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) has $\dot J/J=(1-f)\dot M_1M_2/[M_1(M_1+M_2)]$. Differentiating $J=G^{2/3}(P_{\rm orb}/2\pi)^{1/3}M_1M_2(M_1+M_2)^{-1/3}$ and using this loss law gives

$$
\frac{\dot P_{\rm orb}}{P_{\rm orb}}=-3f\frac{\dot M_1}{M_1}-3\frac{\dot M_2}{M_2}-2\frac{\dot M_1+\dot M_2}{M_1+M_2}.
$$

For constant $f$ this integrates to $P_{\rm orb}M_1^{3f}M_2^3(M_1+M_2)^2=\mathrm{constant}$. If $f$ varies, the differential relation remains valid but the first factor must be replaced by $\exp(3\int f\,d\log M_1)$; treating a time-dependent exponent as a fixed power introduces an extra $\dot f\log M_1$ term.
