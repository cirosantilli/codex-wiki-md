# Stair-rod dislocation

↑ **Parent:** [Partial dislocation](partial-dislocation.md)

A sessile partial junction associated with intersecting [stacking faults](stacking-fault.md). A typical FCC resultant has vector $a\langle110\rangle/6$. Its [Burgers vector](burgers-vector.md) and junction line can define a plane outside the ordinary $\{111\}$ glide family.
