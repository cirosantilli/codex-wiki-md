# Skyrmion model of a nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md)

A charge-$A$ multi-[Skyrmion](skyrmion.md) provides an intrinsic field configuration for an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus.md) with mass number $A$. Its spatial and [isospin](isospin.md) collective coordinates must be quantized, with [Finkelstein-Rubinstein constraints](finkelstein-rubinstein-constraints.md), before assigning physical nuclear states. A classical intrinsic shape is distinct from rotationally invariant observables in a spin-zero state.
