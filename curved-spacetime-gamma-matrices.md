# Curved spacetime gamma matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal coframe in spacetime](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md)

An orthonormal coframe converts fixed flat [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) to coordinate-index Clifford multiplication. Its metric identity gives the curved anticommutation relation. The [spin connection](spin-connection.md) is chosen so that these matrices are covariantly constant under the combined spin and tensor connection, which is the tetrad postulate in Clifford form.
