# Hubble parameter for matter and dynamical dark energy

↑ **Parent:** [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md)

For pressureless matter and dark energy with equation of state $w(z)$,

$$
H(z)=H_0\left[\Omega_{m,0}(1+z)^3
+\Omega_{{\rm DE},0}(1+z)^3
\exp\left(3\int_0^z\frac{w(z')}{1+z'}\,dz'\right)
\right]^{1/2}.
$$
