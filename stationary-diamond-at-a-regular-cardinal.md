# Stationary diamond at a regular cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary diamond principle](stationary-diamond-principle.md)

A sequence $D_\alpha\subseteq\alpha$, indexed by a stationary [subset](subset.md) $S$ of a regular uncountable [cardinal](cardinal-number.md) $\lambda$, guesses every $X\subseteq\lambda$ stationarily often: $\{\alpha\in S:D_\alpha=X\cap\alpha\}$ is stationary. This extends the usual [stationary diamond principle](stationary-diamond-principle.md) at $\omega_1$.
