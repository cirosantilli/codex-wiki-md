# Superstable periodic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md)

A [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) of a differentiable [interval map](interval-map.md) is superstable when its [periodic-orbit multiplier](multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) vanishes. A cycle passing through a [critical point](critical-point.md) has zero multiplier because the [derivative](derivative.md) product includes $f'(c)=0$. For the nonconstant quadratic family $f_\mu(x)=1-\mu x^2$, a superstable $2^n$ cycle satisfies $f_\mu^{2^n}(0)=0$ and does not have a smaller critical period.
