# Mitochondrial genome

↑ **Parent:** [Genome](genome.md)

A [mitochondrial genome](mitochondrial-genome.md) is the genetic material carried in a [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md). In comparing sampled [mitochondrial genomes](mitochondrial-genome.md), the age of their ancestral split is the relevant branch-time calibration; it need not equal the time of separation of the species carrying them.
