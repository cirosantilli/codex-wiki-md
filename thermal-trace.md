# Thermal trace

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md)

The [trace](matrix-trace.md) of imaginary-time evolution is the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) of a quantum system. In a coordinate basis it is the integral of the diagonal [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md) kernel. Inserting a symmetry operator instead gives a weighted thermal [trace](matrix-trace.md).
