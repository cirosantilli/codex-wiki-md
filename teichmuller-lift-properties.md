# Teichmuller lift properties

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmuller representative](teichmuller-representative.md)

For residue field $\mathbb F_q$, these two properties characterize the [Teichmuller lift](teichmuller-representative.md) on every residue class, including zero. The [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) applies to $T^q-T$, whose derivative reduces to $-1$. Uniqueness implies $[ab]=[a][b]$, $[0]=0$, $[1]=1$, and $[a^p]=[a]^p$. In mixed characteristic this multiplicative section need not be additive.
