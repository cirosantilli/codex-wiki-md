# Surjectivity of reduced norms over C2 fields

↑ **Parent:** [C2 field](c2-field.md)

For a [central division algebra](central-division-algebra.md) $D$ of degree $d$ over a [C2 field](c2-field.md), the form $\operatorname{Nrd}(X)-aT^d$ has degree $d$ in $d^2+1$ variables. Its nontrivial zero must have $T\ne0$, because the [reduced norm](reduced-norm.md) of a [division algebra](division-algebra.md) is anisotropic. Dividing $X$ by $T$ gives norm $a$. Zero is the norm of zero.
