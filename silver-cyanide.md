# Silver cyanide

↑ **Parent:** [Cyanide](cyanide.md)

A white silver–cyanide solid used historically in [gravimetric analysis](gravimetric-analysis.md) of [hydrogen cyanide](hydrogen-cyanide.md). Excess [cyanide](cyanide.md) can dissolve it as [dicyanoargentate](dicyanoargentate.md).
