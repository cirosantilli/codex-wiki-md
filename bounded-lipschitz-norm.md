# Bounded Lipschitz norm

↑ **Parent:** [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md)

The bounded Lipschitz norm combines the [Lipschitz constant](lipschitz-constant.md) and [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) of a bounded real-valued [Lipschitz function](lipschitz-continuity.md) on a [metric space](metric-space.md). Both summands satisfy the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md); the supremum term makes the sum definite even on constant functions. Its unit ball requires the sum of the two bounds to be at most one, a stronger requirement than bounding each separately by one.
