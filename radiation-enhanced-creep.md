# Radiation-enhanced creep

↑ **Parent:** [Irradiation creep](irradiation-creep.md)

Enhancement of a stress-driven [creep](creep-deformation.md) mechanism by irradiation-maintained point-defect populations, for example accelerated [diffusion](diffusion.md) or [dislocation climb](dislocation-climb.md). It need not have the [creep](creep-deformation.md) rate of an unirradiated specimen.
