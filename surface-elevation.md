# Surface elevation

↑ **Parent:** [Free surface](free-surface.md)

The [surface elevation](surface-elevation.md) is the signed height of a [free surface](free-surface.md) relative to a chosen reference level, commonly its undisturbed position. With upward coordinate $z$, the surface is the graph $z=\eta(x,y,t)$. Its evolution obeys the [kinematic boundary condition for a free-surface graph](kinematic-boundary-condition-for-a-free-surface-graph.md).
