# Strict monotonicity without coercivity on H1

↑ **Parent:** [Strictly monotone operator](strictly-monotone-operator.md)

The log-cosh [derivative](derivative.md) operator is strictly monotone on $H^1$ but does not have $\langle A(v),v\rangle/\|v\|_{H^1}\to\infty$. [Constant functions](constant-function.md) $v=t$ make the ratio approach $|\Omega|^{1/2}$. Restricting to $H_0^1$ removes the uncontrolled constants: [Poincaré](poincare-inequality.md) gives $\langle A(v),v\rangle\ge\|v\|_{H^1}^2/(1+C_P^2)$, which proves coercivity. [Strict monotonicity](strictly-monotone-operator.md) and large-norm coercivity are distinct properties.
