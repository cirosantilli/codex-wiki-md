# Abstract family of languages

↑ **Parent:** [Formal language theory](formal-language-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_family_of_languages)

An [abstract family of languages](abstract-family-of-languages.md) is a family closed under specified operations, including union, concatenation, iteration, appropriate homomorphisms, inverse homomorphisms and intersection with [regular languages](regular-language.md). These axioms organize [closure properties of formal languages](closure-properties-of-formal-languages.md).
