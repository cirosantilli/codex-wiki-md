# Maximum domain of attraction

↑ **Parent:** [Extreme value theory](extreme-value-theory.md)

A [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F$ lies in the [maximum domain of attraction](maximum-domain-of-attraction.md) of $G$ if $F(a_nx+b_n)^n\to G(x)$ at every continuity point of $G$ for some $a_n>0$ and real $b_n$. This expresses [convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md) of normalized [sample maxima](sample-maximum.md).
