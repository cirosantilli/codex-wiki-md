# Landauer formula

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum transport](quantum-transport.md)

A transport expression relating current to occupied incident modes and their transmission probabilities. For spin-degenerate independent [electrons](electron.md), $I=(2e/h)\sum_i\int T_i(E)[f_L(E)-f_R(E)]\,dE$, with the direction convention chosen consistently. In [linear response](linear-response-theory.md) a perfectly transmitted mode contributes $2e^2/h$.
