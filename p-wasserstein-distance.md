# p-Wasserstein distance

↑ **Parent:** [Wasserstein distance](wasserstein-distance.md)

For $1\leq p<\infty$ and [probability measures](probability-measure.md) with finite $p$th [absolute moments](absolute-moment.md),

$$
W_p(\mu,\nu)=\left(\inf_{\pi\in\Pi(\mu,\nu)}\int\rho(x,y)^p\,d\pi(x,y)\right)^{1/p},
$$

where $\rho$ is the ground metric and $\pi$ ranges over [transport plans](transport-plan.md). For $p=2$ this is the [second Wasserstein distance](second-wasserstein-distance.md).
