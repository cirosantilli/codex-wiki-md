# Rectangular function

↑ **Parent:** [Signal](signal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectangular_function)

The rectangular function has value one on $|t|<1/2$ and zero on $|t|>1/2$. Endpoint values are convention-dependent and do not affect its ordinary [integrals](integral-split.md) or [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md). Shifts, amplitude changes and time rescaling produce a [rectangular pulse](rectangular-function.md) of arbitrary height, center and duration. With $\widehat f(k)=\int f(t)e^{-ikt}dt$, for $a>0$ the width-$2a$ pulse $f(t)=\mathbf1_{|t|<a}$ has [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) $2\sin(ak)/k$, with value $2a$ at zero by a [limit](limit-of-a-function.md). Its [convolution](convolution.md) with itself is a rescaled [triangular function](triangular-function.md), $(2a-|t|)$ on $|t|<2a$ and zero outside, because the [convolution](convolution.md) measures the overlap length of two intervals.
