# Projective twistor space

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor space](twistor-space.md)

The [projective space](projective-space-split.md) of one-dimensional complex subspaces of [twistor space](twistor-space.md). A point corresponds to an [alpha-plane](alpha-plane.md) in [complexified compactified Minkowski space](complexified-compactified-minkowski-space.md); a projective line corresponds to a spacetime point.
