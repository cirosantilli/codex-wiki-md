# Normal cone

↑ **Parent:** [Variational analysis](variational-analysis.md)

Normal cones describe generalized supporting directions to sets. For a convex set, the normal condition is $\langle w,z^\prime-z\rangle\leq0$ for every $z^\prime$ in the set. For nonconvex sets, the [Fréchet normal cone](frechet-normal-cone.md) and the [limiting normal cone](limiting-normal-cone.md) distinguish local regular support from limits of such supports.
