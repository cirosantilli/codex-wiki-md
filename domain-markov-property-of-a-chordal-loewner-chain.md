# Domain Markov property of a chordal Loewner chain

↑ **Parent:** [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md)

Conditionally on the entire past up to $t$, the future domains mapped and centered by $g_t-U_t$ have the original chain law and are independent of that past. For [Schramm–Loewner evolution](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) this follows from independent stationary [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) increments and the [composition rule for chordal Loewner driving functions](composition-rule-for-chordal-loewner-driving-functions.md). The [Strong Markov property](strong-markov-property.md) gives the version at almost surely finite stopping times.
