# Continuity of the fixed-point assignment

↑ **Parent:** [Contraction mapping theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md)

For [contraction mappings](contraction-mapping.md) $f,g$, compare their [fixed points](fixed-point.md) using $f$ on both arguments, then bound the change from $f$ to $g$ by the [uniform metric](uniform-metric.md). The resulting inequality gives the displayed estimate. Continuity at $f$ only needs its own contraction constant $q_f<1$, even if nearby maps have constants approaching one.
