# Time integrability of an entropy-dissipation product

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy dissipation identity for Ornstein-Uhlenbeck flow](entropy-dissipation-identity-for-ornstein-uhlenbeck-flow.md)

If finite nonnegative entropy $H(t)$ decreases, its limit $H_\infty$ exists. The [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) applied to $H^2$ proves the displayed absolute-integrability identity. This product conclusion does not require a zero limiting entropy.
