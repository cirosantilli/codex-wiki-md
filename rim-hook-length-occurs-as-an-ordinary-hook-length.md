# Rim-hook length occurs as an ordinary hook length

↑ **Parent:** [Rim hook](rim-hook.md)

Let a removable [rim hook](rim-hook.md) occupy rows $i$ through $j$. Connectedness and absence of a two-by-two square imply that its pieces in consecutive rows overlap in exactly one column. Therefore $\mu_l=\lambda_{l+1}-1$ for $i\leq l<j$, and summing the row lengths gives $|\lambda/\mu|=\lambda_i-\mu_j+j-i$. The column $c=\mu_j+1$ has height $j$, since lower rows are unchanged and have length at most $\mu_j$. The [hook length](hook-length.md) at $(i,c)$ is exactly that sum. Thus a diagram with only odd [hook lengths](hook-length.md) has no removable even [rim hook](rim-hook.md). Applying the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) shows that its ordinary [character](character-of-a-representation.md) vanishes on every cycle type containing an even part.
