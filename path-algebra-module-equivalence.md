# Path-algebra module equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Path algebra](path-algebra.md)

For a [quiver](quiver.md) with finitely many vertices, send a [representation of a quiver](representation-of-a-quiver.md) $(X_i,f_\rho)$ to $\bigoplus_iX_i$. Vertex [idempotents](idempotent.md) act as projections, and paths act as composites of the arrow maps. Conversely, a unital [module](module-mathematics.md) $M$ gives vertex spaces $e_iM$ and arrow maps $x\mapsto\rho x$. Compatible vertex maps are exactly [module homomorphisms](module-homomorphism.md). These constructions give inverse equivalences of [categories](category-split.md).
