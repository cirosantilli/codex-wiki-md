# Off-diagonal all-ones quadratic form

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic form](quadratic-form.md)

Its symmetric matrix is $\mathbf1\mathbf1^T-I$. The line spanned by $\mathbf1$ has eigenvalue $n-1$ and its orthogonal complement has eigenvalue $-1$. Thus for $n>1$ it is nondegenerate with inertia $(1,n-1,0)$; for $n=1$ it vanishes. This gives a useful explicit example for [Sylvester's law of inertia](sylvester-s-law-of-inertia.md).
