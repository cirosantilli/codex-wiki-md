# Affine hyperplane

↑ **Parent:** [Affine subspace](affine-subspace.md)

In a finite-dimensional real vector space, a level set of a nonzero [linear functional](linear-functional.md) is an affine hyperplane. Choosing $v_0$ in that level set gives $H=v_0+\ker\ell$, so its dimension is one less than that of the ambient space. With a Euclidean inner product, translation followed by an orthonormal basis of $\ker\ell$ identifies it isometrically with the lower-dimensional Euclidean space. The coordinate-sum constraint on balanced cut vectors is an example.
