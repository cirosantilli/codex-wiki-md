# Future-pointing timelike four-vector

↑ **Parent:** [Four-vector](four-vector.md)

In [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(+---)$, a future-pointing timelike [four-vector](four-vector.md) has positive time component larger than its spatial magnitude. Two such vectors have strictly positive [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) scalar product, since $P\cdot Q=P^0Q^0-\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf Q\geq P^0Q^0-|\mathbf P||\mathbf Q|>0$. In the opposite signature the scalar-product sign reverses, while the causal condition is the same. The future timelike cone is closed under addition, which rules out a null [photon](photon.md) momentum equalling a sum of two massive future [four-momenta](four-momentum.md).
