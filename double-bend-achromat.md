# Double-bend achromat

↑ **Parent:** [Beam optics](beam-optics.md)

A lattice cell with two bending magnets whose dispersion and dispersion slope vanish at its boundaries. In an ideal identical-uniform-bend model with horizontal damping partition one, its minimum [geometric emittance](geometric-emittance.md) is $C_q\gamma^2\theta^3/(4\sqrt{15})$, larger than the unconstrained theoretical minimum because of the achromatic [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
