# Cyclic algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Crossed-product algebra of a Galois extension](crossed-product-algebra-of-a-galois-extension.md)

For a cyclic extension $E/K$ of degree $m$ with generator $\sigma$ and $a\in K^{\times}$, the [cyclic algebra](cyclic-algebra.md) is generated by $E$ and $u$ with $ub=\sigma(b)u$, $u^m=a$. It is a [central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md) of degree $m$. Its Brauer class can also be written $(\chi,a)$, where $\chi(\sigma)=1/m$.
