# Entropic Ruzsa sum-difference inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Entropic Ruzsa distance](entropic-ruzsa-distance.md)

For finitely supported random variables,

$$
d_R(X,-Y)\leq3d_R(X,Y).
$$

Equivalently, independent $X,Y$ satisfy

$$
H(X+Y)+H(X)+H(Y)\leq3H(X-Y).
$$
