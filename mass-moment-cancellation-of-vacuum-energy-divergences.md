# Mass-moment cancellation of vacuum energy divergences

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md)

For finitely many free physical degrees of freedom with nonnegative [masses](mass.md), assign positive weights to [bosons](boson.md) and negative weights to [fermions](fermion.md). With a common spherical [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md), the unsubtracted [vacuum energy density](vacuum-energy-density.md) is finite precisely when the three displayed [supertraces](supertrace.md) vanish. Expanding $\sqrt{p^2+m^2}=p+m^2/(2p)-m^4/(8p^3)+O(p^{-5})$ proves that they cancel the quartic, quadratic and logarithmic [ultraviolet divergences](ultraviolet-divergence.md). The remaining radial integral converges as $\int^\infty p^{-3}dp$. Equal-mass [boson](boson.md)–[fermion](fermion.md) pairing is sufficient but stronger than the three conditions; a renormalized [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md) with a free [counterterm](counterterm.md) need not satisfy them.
