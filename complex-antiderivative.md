# Complex antiderivative

↑ **Parent:** [Antiderivative](antiderivative.md)

A [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $F$ on a domain with complex derivative $F'=f$. Its [contour integral](contour-integral.md) satisfies $\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz=F(\gamma(b))-F(\gamma(a))$ by the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) along each smooth contour segment. In particular every closed contour has integral zero. Having local complex antiderivatives need not provide a single one on a domain with holes.
