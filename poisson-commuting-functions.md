# Poisson-commuting functions

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md)

Functions $F_i$ for which $\{F_i,F_j\}=0$ for all pairs. Their [Hamiltonian vector fields](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) commute, and each is constant along the flows of the others. When they are [integrals of motion](integral-of-motion.md) of a [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), they are [first integrals in involution](first-integrals-in-involution.md).
