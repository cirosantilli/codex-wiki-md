# Pendulum separatrix approach takes infinite time

↑ **Parent:** [Separatrix](separatrix.md)

For the nondimensional [simple pendulum](simple-pendulum.md) $\ddot x=-\sin x$, the [separatrix](separatrix.md) has energy $\dot x^2/2+1-\cos x=2$. Starting at $x=0$ with velocity $2$, its time to angle $a<\pi$ is $\log(\sec(a/2)+\tan(a/2))$. The saddle at angle $\pi$ is approached only as time tends to infinity.
