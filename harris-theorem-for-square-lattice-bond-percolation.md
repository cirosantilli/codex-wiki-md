# Harris theorem for square-lattice bond percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Harris-Kesten theorem](harris-kesten-theorem.md)

Uniform fixed-aspect [rectangle crossing in bond percolation](rectangle-crossing-in-bond-percolation.md) bounds at the self-dual parameter yield a positive lower bound for closed dual circuits in geometrically spaced disjoint annuli. The [independent annular barriers for percolation](independent-annular-barriers-for-percolation.md) imply that an origin-to-infinity open [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) has probability zero. Parameter monotonicity gives zero [percolation probability](percolation-probability.md) for every $p\leq1/2$.
