# Relative energy fluctuation of a monatomic ideal gas

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical energy fluctuation](canonical-energy-fluctuation.md)

At fixed [particle number](particle-number.md) and [volume](volume.md), a classical monatomic [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) has $U=3Nk_BT/2$ and $C_V=3Nk_B/2$. The [canonical energy fluctuation](canonical-energy-fluctuation.md) identity $\operatorname{Var}(E)=k_BT^2C_V$ therefore gives $\sigma_U/U=\sqrt{2/(3N)}$. Absolute fluctuations grow as $\sqrt N$, while relative fluctuations decrease as $N^{-1/2}$. A particle reservoir changes the fluctuation problem because [energy](energy-split.md) and particle-number fluctuations are then correlated.
