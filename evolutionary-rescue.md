# Evolutionary rescue

↑ **Parent:** [Evolution](evolution.md)

Recovery of a declining [biological population](population-biology.md) through inherited adaptive change. Immediate survival enabled by [phenotypic plasticity](phenotypic-plasticity.md) can provide time for rescue, but is not itself evidence that inherited rescue occurred.
