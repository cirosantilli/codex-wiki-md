# White noise

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_noise)

For the [AR(1)](autoregressive-process-of-order-one.md) and [MA(1)](moving-average-process-of-order-one.md) formulas here, [white noise](white-noise.md) means a real sequence indexed by all integer times with

$$
\mathbb E[\varepsilon_t]=0,\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon_s,\varepsilon_t)=\sigma^2\mathbf1_{\{s=t\}},\qquad 0<\sigma^2<\infty.
$$

These are the [weak white noise](weak-white-noise.md) assumptions: zero [mean](expected-value-split.md), a common finite positive [variance](variance-split.md), and [uncorrelated random variables](uncorrelated-random-variables.md) at distinct times. Independence and a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) are additional assumptions. [Strong white noise](strong-white-noise.md) requires [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md); [discrete Gaussian white noise](discrete-gaussian-white-noise.md) is the jointly [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) special case. Some definitions allow a constant nonzero [mean](expected-value-split.md); subtract it before using the centered formulas here.

For a concrete dependent example, let $A$ equal $1$ or $2$ with equal probabilities, independently of a sequence of [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [Rademacher random variables](rademacher-distribution.md) $Z_t$, and put $\varepsilon_t=AZ_t$. Its [mean](expected-value-split.md) is zero, its [variance](variance-split.md) is $\mathbb E[A^2]=5/2$, and its nonzero-lag [autocovariances](autocovariance.md) vanish. Nevertheless every $|\varepsilon_t|$ equals the same $A$: seeing one magnitude determines all the others. This is [white noise](white-noise.md) with dependence invisible to its [ACF](autocorrelation.md), and is also an example of the issue discussed in [ergodic stationary process](ergodic-stationary-process.md).
