# Approximate eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (functional analysis)](spectrum-functional-analysis.md)

An approximate eigenvalue of a bounded operator $T$ is a scalar $\lambda$ admitting unit vectors $x_n$ with $\|(T-\lambda I)x_n\|\to0$. Every approximate eigenvalue belongs to the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md); for a [normal operator](normal-operator.md), all spectral points are approximate eigenvalues.
