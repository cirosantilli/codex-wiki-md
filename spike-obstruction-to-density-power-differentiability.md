# Spike obstruction to density-power differentiability

↑ **Parent:** [Density fourth-power functional](density-fourth-power-functional.md)

A perturbation can have negligible [Hellinger distance](hellinger-distance.md) but a large [integral](integral.md) of its density's fourth power. At the uniform density on $[0,1]$, let $b(s)=6s(1-s)$ on $[0,1]$ and zero elsewhere, and let $f_t(u)=1-t^4+t^{-2}b(u/t^6)$. Then $\int f_t=1$ and $\int(\sqrt{f_t}-1)^2\leq2t^4=o(t^2)$, so the [score function](informant-function.md) is zero. However $\int f_t^4\geq72/(35t^2)$. Thus the [density fourth-power functional](density-fourth-power-functional.md) is not continuous along this [differentiable-in-quadratic-mean path](differentiability-in-quadratic-mean.md), despite a bounded baseline and individually bounded nearby densities.
