# Antilinear charge-conjugation intertwiner

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation of a Dirac field](charge-conjugation-of-a-dirac-field.md)

In the complex-conjugate convention for [charge conjugation of a Dirac field](charge-conjugation-of-a-dirac-field.md), a unitary matrix $B$ obeys the displayed [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) identity. For $M^{\rho\sigma}=\frac14[\gamma^\rho,\gamma^\sigma]$, two minus signs cancel to give $M^{\rho\sigma}B=BM^{\rho\sigma*}$. Real [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) parameters therefore give $S B=B S^*$, so $B\psi^*$ has the same [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md) transformation law as $\psi$. Complex conjugating the free [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) and multiplying by $B$ proves that the conjugate also solves it for real mass. This $B$ is the matrix multiplying $\psi^*$; the convention multiplying $\bar\psi^T$ instead uses $B(\gamma^0)^T$.
