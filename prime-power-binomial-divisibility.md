# Prime-power binomial divisibility

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md)

For a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$ and integer $r\ge1$, every interior [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md) in row $p^r$ is divisible by $p$. The polynomial identity $(1+x)^p\equiv1+x^p\pmod p$ iterates under the [Frobenius endomorphism](frobenius-endomorphism.md) to $(1+x)^{p^r}\equiv1+x^{p^r}\pmod p$. This concerns divisibility by $p$, not necessarily by $p^r$.
