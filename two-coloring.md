# Two-coloring

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring-split.md)

A [graph coloring](graph-coloring-split.md) with two available colors in which adjacent vertices receive different colors. A graph admits a two-coloring exactly when it is a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md). Every nonempty connected bipartite graph has two such colorings when the two color names are distinguished.
