# Euler factor of a coefficientwise product of Hecke eigenforms

↑ **Parent:** [Rankin–Selberg convolution](rankin-selberg-convolution.md)

For normalized level-one [Hecke eigenforms](hecke-eigenform.md), let $\alpha_p+\beta_p=a_p$, $\alpha_p\beta_p=p^{k-1}$, and similarly $\gamma_p+\delta_p=b_p$, $\gamma_p\delta_p=p^{k-1}$. The [Hecke multiplication relations](hecke-multiplication-relations.md) give $a_{p^r}=(\alpha_p^{r+1}-\beta_p^{r+1})/(\alpha_p-\beta_p)$, with the repeated-root case obtained by continuity. Multiplying the two expressions and summing four geometric series proves the displayed local factor. Multiplicativity gives an [Euler product](euler-product.md); multiplying by $\zeta(2s-2k+2)$ cancels its local numerator and produces the degree-four convolution factors.
