# Translocon

↑ **Parent:** [Cotranslational protein translocation](cotranslational-protein-translocation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translocon)

A [translocon](translocon.md) is membrane machinery through which a [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) crosses or enters a membrane. In [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) [protein targeting](protein-targeting.md), the [Sec61 complex](sec61-complex.md) supplies the central channel.[Shao and Hegde, 2011; “The Sec61 Translocon”, Figure 4](membrane-protein-insertion-at-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-by-sichen-shao-and-ramanujan-s-hegde.md)
