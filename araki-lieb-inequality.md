<h1 id="araki-lieb-inequality">Araki–Lieb inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](subadditivity-of-von-neumann-entropy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araki–Lieb_inequality)

For a bipartite [density operator](density-matrix.md) $\rho_{AB}$,

$$
|S(\rho_A)-S(\rho_B)|\leq S(\rho_{AB}).
$$
