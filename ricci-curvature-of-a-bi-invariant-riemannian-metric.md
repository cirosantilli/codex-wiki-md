# Ricci curvature of a bi-invariant Riemannian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Bi-invariant Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-riemannian-metric.md)

For a [bi-invariant Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-riemannian-metric.md) on a [Lie group](lie-group.md), the induced inner product on its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) makes every adjoint operator skew-adjoint. The [Levi-Civita connection of a bi-invariant metric](levi-civita-connection-of-a-bi-invariant-metric.md) gives $R(X,Y)Z=-\tfrac14[[X,Y],Z]$. Thus $K(X,Y)=\tfrac14\|[X,Y]\|^2$ for orthonormal $X,Y$, and

$$
\operatorname{Ric}(X,X)=\frac14\sum_i\|[X,e_i]\|^2.
$$

The nullspace of this quadratic form is exactly the [center of a Lie algebra](center-of-a-lie-algebra.md). A zero center therefore gives positive [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md), uniformly bounded below by a positive constant times the metric through left invariance and compactness of the unit sphere in the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md).
