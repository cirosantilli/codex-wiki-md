# Local inertial frame

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial frame](inertial-frame.md)

A local inertial frame is a coordinate frame in which, at one chosen spacetime event, the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) is Minkowskian and the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) vanishes. Freely falling experiments confined to a sufficiently small neighbourhood therefore obey the laws of [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) up to tidal corrections.
