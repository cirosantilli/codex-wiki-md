# Electromagnetic waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md)

An [electromagnetic waveguide](electromagnetic-waveguide.md) confines an [electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) across its axis while allowing it to travel along the axis. Conducting walls or changes of [refractive index](refractive-index.md) select transverse [normal modes](normal-mode.md): reproducible cross-sectional field patterns with their own longitudinal [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md). A hollow metal pipe and an [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md) are different realizations of this principle. Their usefulness is to deliver [electromagnetic energy](electromagnetic-energy.md) along a chosen route instead of letting it spread freely.

For a uniform [vacuum](vacuum.md)-filled conducting guide, write a [normal mode](normal-mode.md)'s axial and time dependence as $e^{i(\beta z-\omega t)}$. Here $\omega>0$ is [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), $\beta$ is the longitudinal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md), and $k_\perp^2$ is the transverse [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) selected by the wall [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). The [electromagnetic wave equation](electromagnetic-wave-equation.md) separates into

$$
\beta^2=\frac{\omega^2}{c^2}-k_\perp^2.
$$

The geometry consumes part of the available squared [wavenumber](wavenumber.md): when $\omega/c<k_\perp$, this branch is an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md) rather than a propagating [normal mode](normal-mode.md). The [waveguide cutoff frequency](waveguide-cutoff-frequency.md) is therefore a property of a specified branch and geometry. A [transverse electromagnetic mode](transverse-electromagnetic-mode.md) in a suitable two-conductor geometry can have $k_\perp=0$ and no positive geometric cutoff. An [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md) needs its own [dielectric](dielectric.md) [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), rather than the metal-wall formulas below.

The [rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md) makes these ideas explicit with a single [sine](sine.md) profile. The [scalar-potential conducting waveguide modes](scalar-potential-conducting-waveguide-modes.md) give a broader construction. At an open [aperture](aperture.md), a confined field can radiate into the [electromagnetic aperture angular spectrum](electromagnetic-aperture-angular-spectrum.md); it need not remain one guided [normal mode](normal-mode.md).
