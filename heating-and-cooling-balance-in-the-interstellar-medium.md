# Heating and cooling balance in the interstellar medium

↑ **Parent:** [Multiphase interstellar medium](multiphase-interstellar-medium.md)

A useful optically thin model subtracts volumetric heating from [radiative cooling](radiative-cooling.md). At equilibrium its net cooling vanishes. Pressure-confined [temperature](temperature-split.md) perturbations are governed by the [derivative](derivative.md) of net cooling per unit [mass](mass.md) at fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md), so a negative [derivative](derivative.md) is unstable. The heating law, species abundances and radiation field must be specified before predicting an equilibrium curve. [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) and finite sound-crossing times limit the isobaric approximation.
