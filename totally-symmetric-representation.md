# Totally symmetric representation

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md)

The one-dimensional [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md), in which every symmetry operation acts as $+1$. An integral over a symmetry-invariant domain can be nonzero only when its integrand contains such a component. Molecular [character tables](character-table.md) often label it $A_1$ or $A_g$.
