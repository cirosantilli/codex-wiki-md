# Orbital parity

↑ **Parent:** [Parity](parity.md)

An [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) eigenstate with quantum number $\ell$ changes by the factor $(-1)^\ell$ under [spatial reflection](spatial-reflection.md). Equivalently,

$$
Y_\ell^m(-\widehat{\mathbf r})=(-1)^\ell Y_\ell^m(\widehat{\mathbf r}).
$$
