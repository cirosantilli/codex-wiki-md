# Differentiability from continuous partial derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Differentiability](differentiability.md)

If all [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) exist near a point and are continuous there, successive coordinate increments and the one-variable [mean value theorem](mean-value-theorem.md) express $f(a+h)-f(a)$ as $\sum_j h_j\partial_jf(a+\eta_j(h))$ with $\eta_j(h)\to0$. Subtracting the displayed [linear map](linear-map.md) leaves $o(\|h\|)$, proving [Fréchet differentiability](frechet-differentiability.md). In two dimensions the absolute remainder is at most $\epsilon(|h_1|+|h_2|)\le\sqrt2\epsilon\|h\|$ for small enough increments.
