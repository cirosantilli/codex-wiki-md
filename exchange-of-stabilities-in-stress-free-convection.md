# Exchange of stabilities in stress-free convection

↑ **Parent:** [Stress-free convection growth-rate polynomial](stress-free-convection-growth-rate-polynomial.md)

In nonrotating [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md) with fixed-temperature, [stress-free boundary conditions](stress-free-boundary-condition.md), the [stress-free convection growth-rate polynomial](stress-free-convection-growth-rate-polynomial.md) has real roots on the destabilizing side. Consequently the first instability is stationary, rather than oscillatory. Minimizing the [free-slip convection neutral curve](free-slip-convection-neutral-curve.md) selects $\lambda_c=\pi/\sqrt2$ and $\mathrm{Ra}_c=27\pi^4/4$.
