# Compactly generated group

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compactly_generated_group)

A topological group is compactly generated in the algebraic sense when a compact subset generates its underlying group. Adjoining its inverse and the identity gives a compact symmetric generating set $K$, and $G=\bigcup_{n\geq1}K^n$ is a [sigma-compact space](sigma-compact-space.md). Some conventions restrict the term to locally compact groups or require a compact generating neighbourhood; those stronger conventions supply local compactness. Compact generation by an arbitrary compact subset alone does not.
