# Polymer overlap concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer solution](polymer-solution.md)

The overlap concentration is the concentration at which distinct dilute [polymer](polymer-split.md) coils first substantially interpenetrate. It corresponds to roughly one chain per coil volume. If a chain contains $N$ [repeat units](repeat-unit.md), has [molar mass](molar-mass.md) $M$ and occupies a coil volume $V_{\mathrm{coil}}$, then its chain [number density](number-density.md) is approximately $1/V_{\mathrm{coil}}$, its repeat-unit [number density](number-density.md) is $N/V_{\mathrm{coil}}$, and its mass concentration is $M/(N_AV_{\mathrm{coil}})$. A scaling convention uses $V_{\mathrm{coil}}\sim R^3$; a commonly chosen geometric convention uses $V_{\mathrm{coil}}=4\pi R_g^3/3$. These give different order-one prefactors because a coil has no sharp surface. For an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md), $R\sim bN^{1/2}$ and a site-volume estimate $v_0\sim b^3$ gives overlap [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) $\phi^*\sim N^{-1/2}$. A [good solvent](good-solvent.md) chain with the [Flory approximation](flory-approximation.md) $R\sim bN^{3/5}$ instead gives $\phi^*\sim N^{-4/5}$.
