# Momentum-cutoff two-point function in six-dimensional cubic scalar theory

↑ **Parent:** [Six-dimensional cubic scalar field theory](six-dimensional-cubic-scalar-field-theory.md)

The [self-energy](self-energy.md) [bubble diagram](bubble-diagram.md) at [loop order](loop-order.md) one in [six-dimensional cubic scalar field theory](six-dimensional-cubic-scalar-field-theory.md) has [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) two. With positive Euclidean [scalar propagators](scalar-propagator.md), its [amputated Green's function](amputated-connected-correlation-function.md) is the displayed integral. Its [superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) is two. Expanding at large internal momentum and taking the angular average shows that its divergent part is a constant plus a multiple of $p^2$, precisely the [mass counterterm](mass-counterterm.md) and [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) structures.

For a spherical [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md) $|k|\leq\Lambda$ and $m^2>0$, its value at $p=0$ is

$$
B_\Lambda=\frac{g^2}{256\pi^3}\left[\Lambda^2-2m^2\log\left(1+\frac{\Lambda^2}{m^2}\right)+\frac{m^2\Lambda^2}{\Lambda^2+m^2}\right].
$$

The divergent $p^2$ coefficient is $-g^2\log(\Lambda^2/m^2)/(768\pi^3)$. Finite terms depend on the [regularization](regularization.md) convention. [Tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md) insertions are excluded from the [one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagram](one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md), and are removed from the connected [two-point function](two-point-correlation-function.md) by imposing a vanishing [one-point function](one-point-correlation-function.md).
