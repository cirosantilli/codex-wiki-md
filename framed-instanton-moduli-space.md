# Framed instanton moduli space

↑ **Parent:** [Instanton moduli space](instanton-moduli-space.md)

Fix a trivialization at infinity and divide smooth finite-action [instantons](instanton.md) only by [gauge transformations](gauge-transformation.md) tending to the identity there. Global gauge orientations remain genuine parameters. For regular $SU(2)$ [instantons](instanton.md) of charge magnitude $k$, the real dimension is $8k$ by the [ADHM construction](adhm-construction.md). Forgetting the framing divides by the three-dimensional global [group action](group-action.md) of $SU(2)$, giving $8k-3$ on the irreducible locus. For one [instanton](instanton.md) the framed parameters are four center coordinates, one positive size, and three orientation coordinates.
