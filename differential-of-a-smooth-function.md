# Differential of a smooth function

↑ **Parent:** [Differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md)

For a real-valued [smooth function](smooth-function.md) on a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), its [differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) is a [differential one-form](one-form.md): $df_p$ maps a [tangent vector](tangent-vector.md) to the directional derivative of the function. In local coordinates, $df=\sum_i(\partial_i f)dx^i$. The [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) extends this operation from functions to higher-degree [differential forms](differential-form-split.md).
