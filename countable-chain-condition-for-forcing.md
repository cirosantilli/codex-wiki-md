# Countable chain condition for forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Chain condition for forcing](chain-condition-for-forcing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countable_chain_condition_for_forcing)

A forcing order has the countable chain condition when every [antichain in a forcing order](antichain-in-a-forcing-order.md) is [countable](countable-set.md). Such forcing preserves cardinals and cofinalities at least $\aleph_1$.
