# Generator of a multiplication semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplication semigroup](multiplication-semigroup.md)

The [infinitesimal generator of a semigroup](infinitesimal-generator-of-a-semigroup.md) for $P_tf=e^{-tk}f$ is multiplication by $-k$ on its maximal domain. The bound $|(1-e^{-tk})/t|\leq k$ proves the [norm](norm.md) [derivative](derivative.md) when $kf$ is integrable; in $C_0$ use a compact-small-tail argument on $kf$. Conversely [norm](norm.md) convergence identifies the pointwise [derivative](derivative.md) as $-kf$. Limits of graph pairs satisfy the same multiplication identity, proving it is a [closed operator](closed-linear-operator.md).
