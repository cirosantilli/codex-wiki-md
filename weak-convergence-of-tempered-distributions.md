# Weak convergence of tempered distributions

↑ **Parent:** [Tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md)

A sequence $u_j\in\mathcal S'$ converges weakly to $u$ if $\langle u_j,\varphi\rangle\to\langle u,\varphi\rangle$ for every [Schwartz function](schwartz-function.md) $\varphi$. This is the weak-star topology of the continuous dual of the [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md), and differs from choosing a strong dual topology defined by uniform convergence on bounded sets of test functions.
