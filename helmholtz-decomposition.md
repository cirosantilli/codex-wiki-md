# Helmholtz decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Vector calculus](vector-calculus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helmholtz_decomposition)

The [Helmholtz decomposition](helmholtz-decomposition.md) separates a [vector field](vector-field.md) into a [gradient](gradient.md) field and a field with zero [divergence](divergence.md). The precise function spaces, [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) and decay hypotheses determine existence and uniqueness. In the square-integrable bounded-domain formulation, the solenoidal component is selected by the [Leray-Helmholtz projection](leray-helmholtz-projection.md).
