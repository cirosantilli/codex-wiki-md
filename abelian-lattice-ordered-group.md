# Abelian lattice-ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md)

A commutative [lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md), usually written additively. Examples include real-valued function groups with pointwise [addition](addition.md), [maximum](maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) and [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md). Such a group need not have a [total order](total-order.md).
