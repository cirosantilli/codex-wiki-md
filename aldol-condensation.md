# Aldol condensation

↑ **Parent:** [Aldol reaction](aldol-reaction.md)

An aldol addition followed by water elimination, commonly producing a conjugated [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) compound. Identifying which [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) supplies the [enolate](enolate.md) distinguishes constitutional products.
