# Fixed-phase sinusoidal least squares

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted least squares](weighted-least-squares.md)

When [frequency](frequency.md) and phase are known, sinusoidal [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and offset fitting is a two-parameter [linear regression](linear-regression-split.md). For equal known errors $\sigma$, profiling out the offset gives $K=\sum(s_i-\bar s)(y_i-\bar y)/\sum(s_i-\bar s)^2$ and $\operatorname{Var}(K)=\sigma^2/\sum(s_i-\bar s)^2$. Identical phases cannot separate [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) from offset. Unknown phase instead requires independent sine and cosine coefficients.
