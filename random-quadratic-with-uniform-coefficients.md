# Random quadratic with uniform coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md)

For independent $U,V$ uniform on $[0,1]$, real roots occur on $U\leq V^2$, a region of [probability](probability.md) $1/3$. On that region both roots are nonpositive. Both lie in $[-1,0]$ exactly when $\max(0,2V-1)\leq U\leq V^2$. Its area is $1/12$, so the conditional [probability](probability.md) of both absolute values being at most one is $1/4$. [Joint probability density](joint-probability-density.md) integration turns algebraic [discriminant](discriminant.md) and root constraints into simple planar areas.
