# Linear approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Chain rule](chain-rule.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_approximation)

A [linear approximation](linear-approximation.md) replaces a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) near a reference input by its value plus the first-order change supplied by its [derivative](derivative.md). In one dimension, this is the [tangent line](tangent-line.md) $f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) combines such local approximations through [function composition](function-composition.md); it does not assert their equality to the original [functions](function-split.md) over a finite [interval](interval-mathematics.md).
