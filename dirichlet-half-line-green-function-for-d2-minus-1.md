# Dirichlet half-line Green function for d2 minus 1

↑ **Parent:** [One-dimensional modified Helmholtz Green function](one-dimensional-modified-helmholtz-green-function.md)

For $x,\xi>0$, the [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) satisfying

$$
(\partial_x^2-1)G(x;\xi)=\delta(x-\xi),\qquad
G(0;\xi)=0,\qquad G(x;\xi)\to0
$$

as $x\to\infty$ is

$$
G(x;\xi)=-\sinh(\min\{x,\xi\})e^{-\max\{x,\xi\}}.
$$
