# Wick-rotated cutoff tadpole mass shift

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop massive phi-fourth counterterms](one-loop-massive-phi-fourth-counterterms.md)

With interaction $-\lambda_0\phi^4/4!$, the [tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md) has [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) one half. After [Wick rotation](wick-rotation.md) and a rotationally invariant Euclidean cutoff, its [self-energy](self-energy.md) is $(\lambda_0/2)\int_{|k|<\Lambda}d^4k\,(2\pi)^{-4}(k^2+m_0^2)^{-1}$. The sphere area $2\pi^2$ and radial integral $\int_0^\Lambda k^3dk/(k^2+m_0^2)=\tfrac12[\Lambda^2-m_0^2\log(1+\Lambda^2/m_0^2)]$ give the displayed shift. It is independent of external momentum, so no one-loop momentum-dependent [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) arises from this graph. Different regulator schemes can treat power divergences differently while defining the same chosen [pole mass](pole-mass.md).
