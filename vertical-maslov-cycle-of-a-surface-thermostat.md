# Vertical Maslov cycle of a surface thermostat

↑ **Parent:** [Maslov cycle](maslov-cycle.md)

For a [surface thermostat](generalized-thermostat-on-a-riemannian-surface.md), the planes containing $F$ form the [projective bundle](projective-bundle.md) of $T(SM)/\mathbb RF$. The vertical Maslov cycle is the section selecting the image of the [vertical vector field of a surface unit tangent bundle](vertical-vector-field-of-a-surface-unit-tangent-bundle.md). It is a smooth hypersurface. In the local projective chart $\mathbb RF+\mathbb R(V+rH)$, it is $r=0$; the lifted flow satisfies $\dot r=1$ on the cycle, hence crosses it transversely.
