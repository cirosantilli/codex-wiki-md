<h1 id="primitive-1-1-forms-on-a-kahler-surface-are-anti-self-dual">Primitive (1,1)-forms on a Kähler surface are anti-self-dual</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Primitive differential form on a Kähler manifold](primitive-differential-form-on-a-kahler-manifold.md)

On a complex two-dimensional [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), a primitive $(1,1)$ form satisfies $*\alpha=-\alpha$, making it an [anti-self-dual differential form](anti-self-dual-differential-form.md). For a nonzero real primitive harmonic form on a compact surface, $\int\alpha\wedge\alpha=-\|\alpha\|_{L^2}^2<0$.
