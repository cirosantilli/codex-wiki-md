# Projective morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Proper morphism](proper-morphism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_morphism)

A morphism $X\to S$ is projective when it factors as a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md) $X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P_S^n$ followed by the projection to $S$. Every projective morphism is proper.
