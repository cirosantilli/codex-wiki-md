# Vanishing top Ext without finite generation

↑ **Parent:** [Top Ext detects finite projective dimension](top-ext-detects-finite-projective-dimension.md)

Put $A=k[[t]]$ and $K=A[1/t]$. A [free resolution](free-resolution.md) has countably many generators $e_i$ and relations $e_i-te_{i+1}$. Applying the [Hom functor](hom-functor.md) with coefficients in $A$ gives $(b_i)\mapsto(b_i-tb_{i+1})$ on sequence products. This is surjective: for prescribed $(c_i)$ use $b_i=\sum_{j\ge0}t^jc_{i+j}$, a well-defined [formal power series](formal-power-series.md). Therefore the degree-one [Ext functor](ext-functor.md) vanishes. But $\operatorname{Hom}_A(K,A)=0$, since every image is infinitely divisible by $t$. A nonzero projective module has a nonzero coordinate map into $A$, so $K$ is not projective. Its displayed resolution proves that its [projective dimension](projective-dimension.md) is exactly one. The finite-generation hypothesis in [top Ext detects finite projective dimension](top-ext-detects-finite-projective-dimension.md) is essential.
