# Largest component of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Component (graph theory)](component-graph-theory.md)

The largest [graph component](component-graph-theory.md) is one with the most [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). Ties may be broken arbitrarily; $L_1$ records the order, which is unaffected by the tie. For a sequence of [random graphs](random-graph.md), the size of its [giant component](giant-component.md) is a central observable.
