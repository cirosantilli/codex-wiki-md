# Bol identity for modular forms

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative transformation of a weak modular form](derivative-transformation-of-a-weak-modular-form.md)

For integer $k<0$, the operator $D=(2\pi i)^{-1}d/dz=q\,d/dq$ sends a weight-$k$ level-one [weak modular form](weak-modular-form.md) to a weight-$2-k$ one after $1-k$ iterations. In the [derivative transformation of a weak modular form](derivative-transformation-of-a-weak-modular-form.md), every lower derivative coefficient then contains a zero factor. This also holds on the subspace $M_k^!$ of forms meromorphic at the cusp.
