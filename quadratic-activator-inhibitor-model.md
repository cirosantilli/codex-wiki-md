# Quadratic activator-inhibitor model

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md)

For positive concentrations and positive parameters, this [reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md) has homogeneous equilibrium $(u_*,v_*)=(1/b,1/b^2)$ and reaction [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}b&-b^2\\2/b&-1\end{pmatrix}$. Its trace is $b-1$ and determinant is $b$, making the equilibrium reaction-stable exactly when $0<b<1$. Stable complex eigenvalues permit damped oscillations, which must be distinguished from sustained periodic oscillations.
