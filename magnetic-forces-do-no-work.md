# Magnetic forces do no work

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

The magnetic part of the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) is perpendicular to the particle [velocity](velocity.md). Its power is therefore zero, so with no other forces the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) remains constant. This remains true relativistically because $dE/dt=\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v$; a magnetic force can change momentum direction without changing total energy.
