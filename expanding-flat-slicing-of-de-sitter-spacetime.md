# Expanding flat slicing of de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md)

The expanding flat slicing has metric

$$
ds^2=-dt^2+e^{2Ht}d\mathbf x^2,
\qquad H>0.
$$

It covers one half of global [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md) and has a past coordinate boundary at $t\to-\infty$.
