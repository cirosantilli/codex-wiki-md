# Module retraction

↑ **Parent:** [Module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md)

A [module retraction](module-retraction.md) onto a [submodule](submodule.md) $U\xrightarrow iX$ is a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) $r:X\to U$ with $ri=I_U$. It gives $X=i(U)\oplus\ker r$: write $x=i(r(x))+(x-i(r(x)))$. Thus a nonzero proper submodule admitting a retraction contradicts indecomposability. Extending a projection from a larger submodule can produce such a retraction through the [long exact sequence of Ext groups](long-exact-sequence-of-ext-groups.md).
