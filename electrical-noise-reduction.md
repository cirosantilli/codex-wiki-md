# Electrical noise reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Noise (signal processing)](noise-signal-processing.md)

Improve a circuit's [signal-to-noise ratio](signal-to-noise-ratio.md) by reducing unwanted coupling and physical noise sources while retaining the signal bandwidth. Important methods include lowering appropriate resistances or temperature to reduce [thermal electrical noise](thermal-electrical-noise.md), controlling leakage and bias currents relevant to [shot noise](shot-noise.md), minimizing pickup with shielding and small loop area, and selecting an [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md) for the source [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md). Filtering limits integrated [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) only if it preserves the information-bearing spectrum; a [lock-in amplifier](lock-in-amplifier.md) can move detection above a [flicker noise](flicker-noise.md) region and select a narrow band. Uncorrelated noise [variances](variance-split.md) add, whereas coherent pickup requires its phases and correlations to be considered. Repeated averaging reduces independent random error, but does not remove an arbitrary [systematic error](systematic-error.md) or slow drift.
