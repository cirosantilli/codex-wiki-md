# Weak topology of probability measures

↑ **Parent:** [Weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md)

This is the coarsest topology making all displayed bounded-continuous test integrals continuous. On a separable [metric](metric.md) space it is metrized by the [bounded-Lipschitz metric](bounded-lipschitz-metric.md). This probability-[measure](measure.md) topology should be distinguished from the weak topology of a Banach space.
