# Clinical equipoise

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical trial](clinical-trial.md)

Clinical equipoise is genuine professional uncertainty about the relative overall benefit and harm of the treatments being compared in a [randomized controlled trial](randomized-controlled-trial.md). A favorable intermediate endpoint does not by itself resolve uncertainty about [mortality](mortality.md). Ethical design requires [informed consent](informed-consent.md), meaningful benefit-risk assessment and monitoring of serious harms; an underpowered safety comparison is not evidence that harm is absent.
