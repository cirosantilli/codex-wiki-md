# Dual Lie algebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

The dual of a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) on $V$ acts on its [dual space](dual-space.md) by $(x\phi)(v)=-\phi(xv)$. The minus sign makes this a [Lie algebra homomorphism](lie-algebra-homomorphism.md): taking transposes reverses the order in a [commutator](commutator.md). It is the infinitesimal version of the [dual representation](dual-representation.md) of a [group](group-split.md).
