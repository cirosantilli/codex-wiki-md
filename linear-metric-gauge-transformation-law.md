# Linear metric gauge-transformation law

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge transformation in cosmological perturbation theory](gauge-transformation-in-cosmological-perturbation-theory.md)

For the passive convention $\widetilde x=x+\xi$, expanding the tensor coordinate-transformation law around a fixed background gives this [Lie derivative](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) formula. The background time shift and Jacobian terms must both be retained. On an [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), the scalar lapse, shift and spatial potentials transform under two scalar coordinate functions.
