# Dioxolene ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

An ortho-dioxygen aromatic framework that can bind through both [oxygen](oxygen.md) atoms in several redox states. The semiquinonate monoanion has a ligand-centred unpaired [electron](electron.md), whereas the catecholate dianion has a filled-shell configuration. [non-innocent ligand](non-innocent-ligand.md) behaviour means [metal](metal.md) [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) cannot be inferred from an overall complex charge without specifying the ligand redox state.
