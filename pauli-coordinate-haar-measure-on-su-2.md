<h1 id="pauli-coordinate-haar-measure-on-su-2">Pauli-coordinate Haar measure on SU(2)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Haar measure](haar-measure.md)

The hemisphere graph $u_0=\pm\sqrt{1-|\mathbf u|^2}$ of [SU(2) as the three-sphere](su-2-as-the-three-sphere.md) has induced metric $g_{ij}=\delta_{ij}+u_iu_j/u_0^2$, whose [determinant](determinant.md) is $1/u_0^2$. Its volume density is therefore $d^3u/|u_0|$. Fixed left or right multiplication preserves the Euclidean norm on the four Pauli coefficients, so the round volume is [Haar measure](haar-measure.md). Both hemispheres have volume $\pi^2$, giving total volume $2\pi^2$ and normalized density $d^3u/(2\pi^2|u_0|)$ on each hemisphere. The apparent equatorial divergence is an integrable coordinate singularity.
