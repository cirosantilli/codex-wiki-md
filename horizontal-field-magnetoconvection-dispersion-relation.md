# Horizontal-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md)

For a unit-depth [Boussinesq](boussinesq-approximation.md) layer with [stress-free boundary conditions](stress-free-boundary-condition.md), fixed boundary temperatures, imposed field along $y$, and horizontal magnetic perturbations at the boundaries, put $a_h^2=k^2+m^2$ and $\Lambda=a_h^2+\pi^2$. Poloidal vertical velocity, temperature and vertical field proportional to $e^{st+ikx+imy}\sin\pi z$ have amplitudes satisfying

$$
\Lambda(s/\sigma+\Lambda)W=Ra_h^2\Theta+\zeta Qim\Lambda C,\qquad
(s+\Lambda)\Theta=W,\qquad(s+\zeta\Lambda)C=imW.
$$

The [determinant](determinant.md) gives the stated [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). Here $\sigma$ is the [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md), $\zeta$ the [magnetic-to-thermal diffusivity ratio](magnetic-to-thermal-diffusivity-ratio.md), $Q$ the [Chandrasekhar number](chandrasekhar-number.md) and $R$ the [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md). The steady neutral curve is $R=\Lambda^3/a_h^2+Qm^2\Lambda/a_h^2$.
