# Rotation about the z-axis

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation gate](rotation-gate.md)

The one-qubit [rotation gate](rotation-gate.md) is $R_z(\theta)=\operatorname{diag}(e^{-i\theta/2},e^{i\theta/2})$. It implements the two relative phases needed in [simulation of a computable diagonal Hamiltonian](simulation-of-a-computable-diagonal-hamiltonian.md).
