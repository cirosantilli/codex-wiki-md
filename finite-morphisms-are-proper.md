# Finite morphisms are proper

↑ **Parent:** [Finite morphism](finite-morphism.md)

A finite morphism is of finite type and separated: on affine charts the multiplication map $B\otimes_AB\to B$ is surjective and represents its closed diagonal. Each element of a finite algebra satisfies a monic equation by the determinant trick. In a valuative lifting problem, its image in the fraction field is therefore integral over the valuation ring and belongs to that ring, since valuation rings are integrally closed. This gives a lift, unique because the valuation ring embeds in its fraction field. The [valuative criterion for properness](valuative-criterion-for-properness.md) proves properness.
