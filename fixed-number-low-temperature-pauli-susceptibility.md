# Fixed-number low-temperature Pauli susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli paramagnetism](pauli-paramagnetism.md)

For a smooth single-spin [density of states](density-of-states.md) $g$ near a positive [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) and fixed total [particle number](particle-number.md), the [Sommerfeld expansion](sommerfeld-expansion.md) gives $\mu=\epsilon_F-(\pi^2/6)(g_F'/g_F)(k_BT)^2+O(T^4)$. The [spin](spin.md) contribution to the total-moment [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md), defined by differentiating with respect to $B$, is $2\mu_B^2\int g(E)[-n'(E)]dE$. Expanding about $\mu$ and including the shift of [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) gives the displayed formula. The $g_F''$ term is thermal broadening; the $-g_F'^2/g_F$ term enforces fixed [particle number](particle-number.md). A band edge or singular [density of states](density-of-states.md) within $k_BT$ of $\epsilon_F$ requires a separate analysis. Orbital magnetic response is excluded.
