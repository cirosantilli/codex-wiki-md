# Rotational line spacing and diatomic bond length

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md)

For a rigid heteronuclear diatomic rotor, $E_J=\hbar^2J(J+1)/(2I)$ and electric-dipole absorption has $\Delta J=+1$. Therefore the $J'\to J'-1$ line [frequency](frequency.md) is $hJ'/(4\pi^2I)$ and adjacent absorption lines are spaced by $h/(4\pi^2I)$. With [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) $\mu$, the bond-length estimate is $r=\sqrt{I/\mu}$. The conversion assumes [frequency](frequency.md) rather than [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md); [centrifugal distortion](centrifugal-distortion.md) makes real high-J spacings imperfectly uniform.
