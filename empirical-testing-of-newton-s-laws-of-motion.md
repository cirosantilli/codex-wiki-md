<h1 id="empirical-testing-of-newton-s-laws-of-motion">Empirical testing of Newton's laws of motion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's laws of motion](newton-s-laws-of-motion.md)

Testing requires independently constrained [force laws](force-law.md), [mass](mass.md) assignments, [initial conditions](initial-condition.md) and a common family of [inertial frames](inertial-frame.md). Defining the net [force](force-split.md) separately for every observation by $\mathbf F=m\mathbf a$ cannot test [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md). With interaction parameters fixed, the same equation predicts new [trajectories](trajectory.md). Likewise, zero net [force](force-split.md) implies uniform [motion](motion.md) within the second-law framework, but the existence and empirical identification of its [inertial frames](inertial-frame.md) are extra content. Pairwise [Newton's third law](newton-s-third-law.md) adds a constraint not supplied by the individual second-law equations.
