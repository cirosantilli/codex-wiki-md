# Positive-frequency part of a quantum field

↑ **Parent:** [Mode expansion of a free field](mode-expansion-of-a-free-field.md)

For a free field, its positive-frequency part consists of the annihilation terms multiplying $e^{-i\omega t}$ with $\omega>0$; its negative-frequency part consists of the creation terms. The sign refers to positive [energy](energy.md), even though the exponential has a minus sign. Imposing the [Lorenz gauge](lorenz-gauge-condition.md) only through $(\partial\cdot A)^{(+)}|\mathrm{phys}\rangle=0$ is the subsidiary condition in [Gupta-Bleuler quantization](gupta-bleuler-formalism.md), not an operator identity for the whole divergence.
