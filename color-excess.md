# Color excess

↑ **Parent:** [Interstellar extinction](interstellar-extinction.md)

The observed colour minus the inferred intrinsic colour in specified [photometric passbands](photometric-passband.md); for B and V it is $E(B-V)=(B-V)_{\rm obs}-(B-V)_{\rm intrinsic}$.
