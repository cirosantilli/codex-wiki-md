# Complexified transport Cauchy kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral reconstruction of an attenuated Radon transform](spectral-reconstruction-of-an-attenuated-radon-transform.md)

For $w=x_1+ix_2$ and $0<|k|\ne1$, the operator $D_k=(k+k^{-1})\partial_{x_1}/2+(k-k^{-1})\partial_{x_2}/(2i)$ has the displayed decaying [fundamental solution](fundamental-solution-of-a-linear-differential-operator.md). A rotated real-linear coordinate turns it into a multiple of a [Wirtinger derivative](wirtinger-derivatives.md), reducing the kernel identity to $\partial_{\bar z}(1/(\pi z))=\delta$. The kernel is analytic in $k$ separately inside and outside the unit circle, is $O(k)$ at zero and $O(k^{-1})$ at infinity, and develops a jump at the real directional-transport limit.
