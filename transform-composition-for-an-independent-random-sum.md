# Transform composition for an independent random sum

↑ **Parent:** [Random sum](random-sum.md)

For independent identically distributed summands, independent also of the count $N$, conditioning on $N=n$ gives $\mathbb E[e^{tZ}\mid N=n]=M_Y(t)^n$. The [law of total expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md) gives the composition of the count's [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) with the summand's [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md). This identity is valid as an extended nonnegative expectation; finiteness near zero requires additional exponential-integrability assumptions.
