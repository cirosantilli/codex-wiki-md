# Photon rocket

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic rocket equation](relativistic-rocket-equation.md)

A [photon rocket](photon-rocket.md) converts stored mass-energy into a collimated beam of [photons](photon.md). In its instantaneous [rest frame](rest-frame.md), [energy](energy-split.md) conservation gives $c^2dM=-dE_\gamma$, and [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) gives $Mc\,d\eta=dE_\gamma/c$. Therefore a monotone change in [rapidity](rapidity.md) costs $M_f/M_i=e^{-|\Delta\eta|}$. The rocket's [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) includes its fuel and stored [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md); inefficiency or an angular spread of the exhaust increases the fuel requirement.
