<h1 id="bombieri-vinogradov-theorem">Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic number theory](analytic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombieri–Vinogradov_theorem)

For every $A>0$ there is $B>0$ such that, for $Q\leq x^{1/2}/\log^Bx$,

$$
\sum_{q\leq Q}\max_{(a,q)=1}\max_{2\leq y\leq x}\left|\psi(y;q,a)-\frac y{\phi(q)}\right|\ll_A\frac{x}{\log^Ax}.
$$

Here $\psi(y;q,a)$ is the [Chebyshev function in an arithmetic progression](chebyshev-function-in-an-arithmetic-progression.md) and $\phi$ is the [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md). [Partial summation](abel-s-summation-formula.md) gives the corresponding result for the [prime-counting function](prime-counting-function.md) and the offset [logarithmic integral function](logarithmic-integral-function.md). The theorem controls the total error over many moduli; it need not give the same bound for each individual modulus.
