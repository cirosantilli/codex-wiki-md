# Impulse-driven slipping-to-rolling transition of a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

For a uniform sphere struck horizontally with [impulse](impulse.md) $J$ at height $d$ above its centre, $u_0=J/M$ and clockwise spin $\Omega_0=5Jd/(2Ma^2)$. [friction](friction.md) brings the contact slip $u-a\Omega$ to zero. The final rolling [speed](speed.md) is $u_f=5J(1+d/a)/(7M)$, independent of a positive constant [friction](friction.md) coefficient; [friction](friction.md) can accelerate an initially overspinning [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md).
