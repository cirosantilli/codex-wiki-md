# Density-wave dispersion relation in a non-self-gravitating disk

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial-acoustic wave](inertial-acoustic-wave.md)

Let $c_s^2=(dP/d\Sigma)_0$ be the [barotropic closure of a razor-thin disk](barotropic-closure-of-a-razor-thin-disk.md) sound-speed squared and $\kappa_r$ the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md), with $\kappa_r^2=2(2-q_r)\Omega^2$ for [orbital shear parameter](orbital-shear-parameter.md) $q_r$. For a radial axisymmetric [normal mode](normal-mode.md) in a uniform barotropic [shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md), let $U_x,U_y$ be velocity amplitudes and $\sigma$ the surface-density amplitude. Linear momentum gives $-i\omega U_x=2\Omega U_y-ikc_s^2\sigma/\Sigma_0$ and $-i\omega U_y=-\kappa_r^2U_x/(2\Omega)$; mass conservation gives $\omega\sigma=k\Sigma_0U_x$. Eliminating the amplitudes for $\omega\ne0$ proves the displayed dispersion relation. The rotational term is epicyclic restoration; the [pressure](pressure.md) term is acoustic restoration. Without a perturbed gravitational potential, no self-gravity term belongs in the formula.
