# Electron microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscope](electron-microscope.md)

[Electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md) is the practice of preparing specimens, producing images with an [electron microscope](electron-microscope.md), and interpreting their contrast. A dark region can reflect [electron](electron.md) deflection out of the admitted beam rather than complete absorption. A useful image therefore needs a stated preparation, detection signal and length calibration; brightness by itself does not identify a material. [Transmission electron microscopy](transmission-electron-microscopy.md) probes thin specimens, whereas a [scanning electron microscope](scanning-electron-microscope.md) can examine the exposed surface of a bulk specimen.[JEOL, Observation of the micro world using electrons and Various Function of SEM: transmitted versus surface-generated signals](scanning-electron-microscopes-sem-by-jeol.md)
