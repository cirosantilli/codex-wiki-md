<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the stipulated [Strong-shock Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](../../../../strong-shock-rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md), $\rho_d=4\rho_u$ and $v_d=v_u/4$. The [ideal gas law](../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) is $P_d=\rho_d k_BT_d/\mu$, with $\mu$ a dimensional [mean particle mass](../../../../mean-particle-mass.md). The [specific enthalpy](../../../../specific-enthalpy.md) of a nonrelativistic [monatomic gas](../../../../monatomic-gas.md) is $5k_BT/(2\mu)$. Dividing the [energy flux](../../../../energy-flux.md) equation in [d](../d.md) by the common [mass flux](../../../../mass-flux.md) gives

$$
\frac{v_u^2}{2}=\frac{v_u^2}{32}+\frac{5k_BT_d}{2\mu}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\frac{5k_BT_d}{2\mu}=\frac{15v_u^2}{32},\qquad
\boxed{k_BT_d=\frac{3}{16}\mu v_u^2.}
$$

As an independent check, [conservation of momentum](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives $P_d=\rho_uv_u^2-4\rho_u(v_u/4)^2=3\rho_uv_u^2/4$, and division by $\rho_d=4\rho_u$ gives exactly the same [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). The numerical coefficient relies on $\gamma=5/3$ and the absence of dynamically important radiative or magnetic stresses.
