<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In a sufficiently tight [binary star](../../../../binary-star.md), the [main sequence](../../../../main-sequence.md) companion can fill its [Roche lobe](../../../../roche-lobe.md). [Roche-lobe overflow](../../../../roche-lobe-overflow.md) then transfers gas through the [inner Lagrange point](../../../../inner-lagrange-point.md) towards the [neutron star](../../../../neutron-star.md). The gas carries [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md), so it generally forms an [accretion disk](../../../../accretion-disk.md) rather than falling radially onto the surface. Internal stresses carry [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) outwards and permit [accretion](../../../../accretion.md) inwards. Capture of a stellar wind is another possible supply mechanism if the companion does not fill its [Roche lobe](../../../../roche-lobe.md).

Near the [neutron star](../../../../neutron-star.md), the rapidly increasing [magnetic dipole field](../../../../magnetic-dipole-field.md), with $B\propto r^{-3}$, becomes dynamically important when its [magnetic pressure](../../../../magnetic-pressure.md) $B^2/(2\mu_0)$ is comparable to the inflow stresses. The [accretion disk](../../../../accretion-disk.md) is truncated around a magnetospheric radius. Ionized material attaches to [magnetic field lines](../../../../magnetic-field-line.md) and streams along them in [magnetically channelled accretion](../../../../magnetically-channelled-accretion.md), forming an [accretion column](../../../../accretion-column.md) above one or both magnetic poles. The [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) impedes motion across the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), but does not forbid motion parallel to it. Gas can therefore reach the polar surface, with its gravitational [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) released in a hot [shock wave](../../../../shock-wave.md) and radiation.

This description presumes the [neutron star](../../../../neutron-star.md) rotates slowly enough for material to reach it: sufficiently rapid rotation can centrifugally expel the inflow instead. A strong [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) by itself does not guarantee surface [accretion](../../../../accretion.md).
