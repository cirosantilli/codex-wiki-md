<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Interpret the terminal infall [speed](../../../../speed.md) as the free-fall [speed](../../../../speed.md) from a radius much larger than the [neutron star](../../../../neutron-star.md) radius $R_*=12\,\mathrm{km}$. In the Newtonian estimate,

$$
v_u\simeq\sqrt{\frac{2GM_*}{R_*}}
=2.00\times10^8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\simeq0.666c,
\qquad M_*=1.8M_\odot.
$$

For fully ionized [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) with [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and [protons](../../../../proton.md) at a common [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), there are two particles per atom and $\mu=(m_p+m_e)/2\simeq m_p/2$. The result of [e](../e.md) then yields

$$
\boxed{k_BT_d=\frac{3GM_*m_p}{16R_*}\simeq39\,\mathrm{MeV},\qquad
T_d\simeq4.5\times10^{11}\,\mathrm K.}
$$

This is the thermal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) scale $k_BT_d$ per particle. In the same classical [monatomic gas](../../../../monatomic-gas.md) approximation, the mean random [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) per particle is $3k_BT_d/2\simeq59\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Before the [shock wave](../../../../shock-wave.md), the bulk [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) per [proton](../../../../proton.md) is $m_pv_u^2/2=GM_*m_p/R_*\simeq208\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Distinguishing these quantities avoids an ambiguity in “particle energy”. If only the ions support the downstream [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) because [electrons](../../../../electron.md) have not equilibrated, $\mu\simeq m_p$ instead gives $k_BT_i\simeq78\,\mathrm{MeV}$; that is a different, two-temperature assumption.

These are estimates within the question's Newtonian, $\gamma=5/3$ model. Its self-consistency is poor here: $2GM_*/(R_*c^2)\simeq0.443$, and $39\,\mathrm{MeV}$ is much larger than the [electron](../../../../electron.md) rest [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Relativistic [electrons](../../../../electron.md), electron–positron [pair production](../../../../pair-production.md), radiation, unequal species [temperatures](../../../../temperature-split.md), and [general relativity](../../../../general-relativity-split.md) can alter the [equation of state](../../../../equation-of-state.md) and [shock wave](../../../../shock-wave.md) structure. In particular, the numerical $39\,\mathrm{MeV}$ must not be presented as a precision surface [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) prediction.
