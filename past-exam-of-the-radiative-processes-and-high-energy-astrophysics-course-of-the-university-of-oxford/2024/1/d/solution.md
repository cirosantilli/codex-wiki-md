<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Work in the locally planar [shock frame](../../../../shock-frame.md), taking $v_u,v_d>0$ towards the surface. For a stationary [normal shock wave](../../../../normal-shock-wave.md) with negligible radiative loss inside the discontinuity, the [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions for a perfect gas](../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md) express [conservation of mass](../../../../mass-conservation.md), [conservation of momentum](../../../../momentum-conservation.md), and [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) per unit area as

$$
\boxed{\rho_uv_u=\rho_dv_d\equiv J,}
$$



$$
\boxed{P_u+\rho_uv_u^2=P_d+\rho_dv_d^2,}
$$



$$
\boxed{J\left(\frac{v_u^2}{2}+h_u\right)
=J\left(\frac{v_d^2}{2}+h_d\right),\qquad
h=\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}\frac P\rho.}
$$

Here $h$ is [specific enthalpy](../../../../specific-enthalpy.md), including the [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) work; using only [internal energy](../../../../internal-energy-split.md) would omit that work. The [gravitational potential](../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) is continuous across an infinitesimally thin front. For cold upstream gas, $P_u$ and $h_u$ are negligible, while a [monatomic gas](../../../../monatomic-gas.md) has $\gamma=5/3$ and $h=5P/(2\rho)$.

The hydrodynamic equations apply to flow parallel to the guiding [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) when its stress contributions cancel across the front. An oblique [magnetohydrodynamic shock](../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-shock.md) requires magnetic stresses and [Poynting flux](../../../../poynting-flux.md); a radiation-mediated front or appreciable escaping radiation requires the corresponding radiative [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and [energy flux](../../../../energy-flux.md) terms. These are additional physics, not terms hidden in $h$ here.
