<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In the [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md), hot intracluster [electrons](../../../../electron.md) transfer [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) to [cosmic microwave background](../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) [photons](../../../../photon.md) by [inverse Compton scattering](../../../../inverse-compton-scattering.md). Their thermal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is much larger than the [CMB](../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); in the nonrelativistic limit the mean fractional [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) gain per scattering is about $4k_BT/(m_ec^2)$. Multiplication by the incremental [Thomson scattering](../../../../thomson-scattering.md) [optical depth](../../../../optical-depth.md) $d\tau=\sigma_Tn_e\,dx$ explains why $y=\int[k_BT/(m_ec^2)]\,d\tau$ measures the electron [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) integrated along the line of sight. The result is a spectral distortion, with a low-[frequency](../../../../frequency.md) decrement and a high-[frequency](../../../../frequency.md) increment, rather than a positive additional emission component at every [frequency](../../../../frequency.md).

For a uniform sphere, a line of sight at impact parameter $b$ has path length $2\sqrt{R^2-b^2}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(b)=\frac{2\sigma_T}{m_ec^2}n_ek_BT\sqrt{R^2-b^2},\qquad 0\le b\le R.}
$$

On the central line of sight this reduces to the requested expression

$$
\boxed{y(0)=\frac{2\sigma_T}{m_ec^2}Rn_ek_BT.}
$$

The central-ray qualification matters: the average over the projected disk is $\bar y=(2/3)y(0)$, while the tangent ray $b=R$ has $y(R)=0$. Thus the printed formula does not hold for an arbitrary sightline. Part [f](../f.md) uses the specified $y$ as this central value, as required by the preceding formula.
