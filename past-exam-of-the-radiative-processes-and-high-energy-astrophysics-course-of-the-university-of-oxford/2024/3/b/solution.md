<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $h\nu\ll k_BT$, expand the exponential in the [thermal free-free emissivity](../../../../thermal-free-free-emissivity.md):

$$
e^{-h\nu/(k_BT)}=1-\frac{h\nu}{k_BT}+O\!\left(\frac{h^2\nu^2}{k_B^2T^2}\right).
$$

The [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) emitted per unit [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) is therefore approximately flat, with the weak [Gaunt factor](../../../../gaunt-factor.md) dependence superposed. At these low [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energies](../../../../energy-split.md), most of the thermal [electrons](../../../../electron.md) can participate in emission; there is no exponentially small population threshold.

For $h\nu>k_BT$, the spectrum turns down approximately as $e^{-h\nu/(k_BT)}$, apart from the [Gaunt factor](../../../../gaunt-factor.md). [Conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) requires an emitting [electron](../../../../electron.md) to supply at least $h\nu$. The high-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) tail of its [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md) is exponentially rare, which produces this cutoff.

“Flat” refers to $j_\nu$, an [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) spectrum per unit [frequency](../../../../frequency.md). The emitted [photon](../../../../photon.md) number spectrum instead contains $1/(h\nu)$, while [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) per logarithmic [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) interval is $\nu j_\nu$. At sufficiently low [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) the [plasma](../../../../plasma-physics.md) can also become [optically thick](../../../../optically-thick-medium.md) to [free-free absorption](../../../../free-free-absorption.md); the given optically thin emissivity is then not the escaping spectrum.
