<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Charge neutrality in fully ionized [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) gives $n_e=n_p$ and $\rho=(m_p+m_e)n_e\simeq m_pn_e$. With only [Thomson scattering](../../../../thomson-scattering.md), the [photon](../../../../photon.md) [mean free path](../../../../mean-free-path.md) is $\ell=(n_e\sigma_T)^{-1}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\ell(r)=L\frac{ar}{\sin ar},\qquad
L=\frac{m_p}{\sigma_T\rho_c}=\frac{4m_pR^3}{\pi\sigma_TM},\qquad
\frac LR=\frac{4m_pR^2}{\pi\sigma_TM}.}
$$

Here and below the tiny [electron mass](../../../../electron-mass.md) correction can be retained by replacing $m_p$ by $m_p+m_e$. $L$ is the central [mean free path](../../../../mean-free-path.md), not a [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md). The divergence at $r=R$ reflects the vanishing [electron](../../../../electron.md) [number density](../../../../number-density.md).

Using $M_\odot=1.9885\times10^{30}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $R_\odot=6.957\times10^8\,\mathrm m$, $m_p=1.6726\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$, and $\sigma_T=6.6525\times10^{-29}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ gives

$$
\rho_c\simeq4.64\times10^3\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}},\qquad
\boxed{L_\odot\simeq5.42\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm m,\qquad L_\odot/R_\odot\simeq7.79\times10^{-12}.}
$$

These are this profile's values, rather than the [Sun](../../../../sun.md)'s actual central [mass density](../../../../density.md) or opacity profile.
