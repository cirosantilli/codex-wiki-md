# Q branch

↑ **Parent:** [Rovibrational spectroscopy](rovibrational-spectroscopy.md)

The [rovibrational spectroscopy](rovibrational-spectroscopy.md) branch with unchanged rotational [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) quantum number. Differences between upper and lower [rotational constants](rotational-constant.md) can resolve its transitions into closely spaced lines. Allowed lower-state values depend on the angular momenta of both states.
