# Affine connection

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_connection)

An affine connection on a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) is a [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) for its [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md). It satisfies $\nabla_{fX}Y=f\nabla_XY$ and $\nabla_X(fY)=X(f)Y+f\nabla_XY$. Its curvature is

$$
R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z.
$$

The [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) makes curvature tensorial in all three slots. An affine connection is also called a linear connection on the manifold; a [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) is the distinguished torsion-free metric-compatible example.
