# Self-reproducing incompressible annulus cut

↑ **Parent:** [Three-manifold hierarchy](three-manifold-hierarchy.md)

Let $P$ be a once-punctured [torus](torus.md) and let $c$ be a nonseparating essential curve. The [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) $c\times[0,1]$ is an [incompressible surface](incompressible-surface.md) in $P\times[0,1]$. Cutting gives a [pair of pants](pair-of-pants-mathematics.md) times an interval. Both products are genus-two [handlebodies](handlebody.md), since a [compact](compact-space.md) [connected](connected-space.md) [surface](topological-surface.md) with [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) times an interval is a handlebody of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $1-\chi(P)$.
