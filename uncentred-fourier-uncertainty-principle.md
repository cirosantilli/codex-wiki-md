# Uncentred Fourier uncertainty principle

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md)

For nonzero $f$ with $f,xf,f'\in L^2(\mathbb R)$, integration by parts gives $\|f\|_2^2=-2\operatorname{Re}\langle f',xf\rangle$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md) yield the moment product bound $1/4$ with the angular-frequency Fourier convention. Equality occurs exactly for $f(x)=C e^{-ax^2/2}$ with $a>0$ real and $C\ne0$. Translation, modulation or a quadratic phase increase one of these uncentred moments; the equality family is narrower than that for centred variances.
