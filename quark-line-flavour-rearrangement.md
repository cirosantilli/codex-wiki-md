# Quark-line flavour rearrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Strong interaction](strong-interaction.md)

A quark-line diagram can show how existing [valence quarks](valence-quark.md) are regrouped into outgoing [hadrons](hadron.md) while [gluons](gluon.md) exchange [momentum](momentum-split.md) and colour. Alternatively, a quark-antiquark pair can annihilate and create a different flavour pair with zero net flavour. Such drawings enforce global charge, [baryon number](baryon-number.md) and flavour constraints; they are not controlled perturbative calculations of an exclusive low-energy hadronic [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) because binding is nonperturbative.
