# Cosmic Background Explorer

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_Background_Explorer)

The [Cosmic Background Explorer](cosmic-background-explorer.md) connected two questions: does the [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) follow a [Planck spectrum](blackbody-spectrum.md), and how does its [temperature](temperature-split.md) vary across the sky? [John C. Mather](john-c-mather.md) and [George F. Smoot](george-f-smoot.md) shared the 2006 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) for these discoveries.[2006 award and discovery summary](george-f-smoot-facts-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md) The [FIRAS](far-infrared-absolute-spectrophotometer.md) and [COBE DMR](differential-microwave-radiometer.md) comparisons below explain why measuring spectral shape and measuring angular structure require different information.
