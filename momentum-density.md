# Momentum density

↑ **Parent:** [Stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md)

[Momentum density](momentum-density.md) is [momentum](momentum.md) per unit physical volume. In a local [orthonormal tetrad](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) of the measuring observer it is $q^{\hat i}=T^{\hat0\hat i}$. A [perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) moving at a small velocity relative to that observer has $q^{\hat i}=(\rho+P)v^{\hat i}$ to first order. For isotropic background [photons](photon.md), $P=\rho/3$, so the factor is $4\rho/3$ rather than $\rho$. Integrating the [kinetic stress-energy tensor](kinetic-stress-energy-tensor.md) gives $q^{\hat i}=\int f p^{\hat i}\,d^3p$.
