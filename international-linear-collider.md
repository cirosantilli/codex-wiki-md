# International Linear Collider

↑ **Parent:** [Linear collider](linear-collider.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Linear_Collider)

A proposed electron–positron [linear collider](linear-collider.md) based on superconducting RF acceleration. The 2007 reference design relevant to an exam-era comparison targeted $500$ GeV centre-of-mass energy and peak [collider luminosity](collider-luminosity.md) $2\times10^{34}\,\mathrm{cm^{-2}s^{-1}}$, with approximately $31$ km total footprint. These are historical design parameters, not achieved machine performance. [ILC Reference Design Report, Volume 3—Accelerator, abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.2361)
