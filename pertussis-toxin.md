# Pertussis toxin

↑ **Parent:** [Signal transduction](signal-transduction-split.md)

A bacterial ADP-ribosylating toxin that prevents normal receptor coupling of Gi/o proteins. Its action differs from the persistent active-Gs signalling caused by [cholera toxin](cholera-toxin.md).
