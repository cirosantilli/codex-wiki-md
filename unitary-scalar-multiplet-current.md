# Unitary scalar multiplet current

↑ **Parent:** [Noether current](noether-current.md)

For a [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) multiplet with canonical kinetic term and [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) invariant under $\Phi\mapsto e^{i\varepsilon T}\Phi$, a constant [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) $T$ gives the displayed [Noether current](noether-current.md). Vary the field and its conjugate independently in the [Noether current for a first-derivative scalar field](noether-current-for-a-first-derivative-scalar-field.md). A common alternative convention reverses both the parameter and current sign. Its divergence vanishes [on shell](on-shell.md).
