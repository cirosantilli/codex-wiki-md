# Background subtraction

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

Background subtraction estimates a source contribution by removing a sky or detector-background measurement. An independent background estimate adds [photon shot noise](photon-shot-noise.md); a mismatched background adds [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md).
