# Separated polar solutions of the biharmonic equation

↑ **Parent:** [Biharmonic equation](biharmonic-equation.md)

For angular [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) of integer order $n\geq2$, the [biharmonic equation](biharmonic-equation.md) in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) has radial basis $r^n,r^{-n},r^{n+2},r^{2-n}$. Apply the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) twice to obtain the displayed indicial equation. For $n=0$, repeated roots give the radial basis $1,\log r,r^2,r^2\log r$; for $n=1$, the basis is $r^{-1},r,r\log r,r^3$. These are [Cauchy-Euler equations](cauchy-euler-equation.md). Regularity at the origin and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) select the admissible powers. For $n=2$, a constant radial factor still multiplies $\cos2\theta$ or $\sin2\theta$, so it is bounded but fails continuity at the origin.
