# Conformational isomerism

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)

[Conformational isomerism](conformational-isomerism.md) concerns spatial arrangements interconverted by internal motion, such as turning about a single [chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md). Moving the entire [molecule](molecule-split.md) or applying a [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) changes its position or viewing direction, not its internal arrangement.
