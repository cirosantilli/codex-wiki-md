↑ **Parent:** [Total influence](total-influence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margulis–Russo_formula)

For the indicator $f$ of a monotone family under a [p-biased product measure](p-biased-product-measure.md), the Margulis–Russo formula identifies the derivative of $\mathbb E_{\mu_p}f$ with the suitably normalized [total influence](total-influence.md) of $f$.
