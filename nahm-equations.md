# Nahm equations

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations in temporal gauge](anti-self-dual-yang-mills-equations-in-temporal-gauge.md)

For three Lie-algebra-valued functions of one variable, the [Nahm equations](nahm-equations.md) are

$$
\dot A_a=\frac12\epsilon_{abc}[A_b,A_c].
$$

They arise by taking a solution of the [ASDYM equations](anti-self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md) with orientation $dx^1\wedge dx^2\wedge dx^3\wedge dt$, imposing independence of $x^1,x^2,x^3$, and setting $A_t=0$ by a local [Yang-Mills gauge transformation](yang-mills-gauge-transformation.md). Then $F_{ab}=[A_a,A_b]$, $F_{a t}=-\dot A_a$, and [anti-self-duality of gauge curvature](anti-self-duality-of-gauge-curvature.md) gives the displayed equations. The opposite orientation reverses their sign.
