# Renewal measure

↑ **Parent:** [Renewal process](renewal-process.md)

For a positive interarrival law $K$, the renewal measure $U=\sum_{j\geq0}K^{*j}$ counts the expected number of renewal epochs in a set, including the epoch at zero through $K^{*0}=\delta_0$. Its cumulative function is one plus the usual [renewal function](renewal-function.md). Convolution against $U$ solves a [renewal equation](renewal-equation.md).
