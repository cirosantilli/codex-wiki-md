# Infrared phase fluctuations of a homogeneous Bose gas

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-gas phase-only action](bose-gas-phase-only-action.md)

The [Bose-gas phase-only action](bose-gas-phase-only-action.md) gives a thermal infrared integral $\int d^dq/q^2$ and a zero-temperature equal-time integral $\int d^dq/|q|$. Thus an infinite uniform short-range interacting gas has no true finite-temperature condensate in $d\le2$, and the one-dimensional [ground state](ground-state.md) also lacks true condensate [long-range order](long-range-order.md). This does not exclude local density coherence, a finite-size condensate description or two-dimensional algebraic order and [superfluidity](superfluidity.md).[abstract; nonzero-temperature theorem](existence-of-long-range-order-in-one-and-two-dimensions-by-pierre-hohenberg.md)
