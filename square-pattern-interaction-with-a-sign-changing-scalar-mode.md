# Square-pattern interaction with a sign-changing scalar mode

↑ **Parent:** [Square-symmetric cubic steady-state normal form](square-symmetric-cubic-steady-state-normal-form.md)

A scalar mode that changes sign under a quarter-turn and is fixed by diagonal reflections couples quadratically through $AC,BC,A^2-B^2$. In the saturated three-mode [equivariant dynamical system](equivariant-dynamical-system.md), a diagonal state $A=B=s$, $C=0$ has $s^2=\mu_1/(1+\lambda)$ and $p=2(\lambda-1)s^2$. The symmetric perturbation decays at rate $2\mu_1$; the remaining [stability matrix](stability-matrix.md) is the displayed block. With $K=2\alpha_1\gamma_1/(\lambda-1)>0$, its [linear stability](linear-stability.md) interval is $-K<\mu_2<-p$, provided $p<K$. The endpoints are a symmetry-breaking [pitchfork bifurcation](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md) and a [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md), respectively, away from degeneracies.
