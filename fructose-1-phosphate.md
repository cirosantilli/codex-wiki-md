# Fructose-1-phosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Fructose-1-phosphate](fructose-1-phosphate.md) is a product of fructose phosphorylation. It can antagonize the inhibitory glucokinase–regulatory-protein interaction, linking hepatic glucose handling to fructose metabolism.
