# Maxwell equations

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_equations)

The [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations-split.md) are the field equations of classical [electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md). In [SI](international-system-of-units.md) units, in vacuum with prescribed [charge density](charge-density.md) $\rho$ and [current density](current-density.md) $\mathbf J$, they are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla\cdot\mathbf E&=\rho/\epsilon_0,
&\nabla\cdot\mathbf B&=0,\\
\nabla\times\mathbf E&=-\frac{\partial\mathbf B}{\partial t},
&\nabla\times\mathbf B&=\mu_0\mathbf J+\mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{\partial\mathbf E}{\partial t}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $\mathbf E$ is the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) in volts per metre, $\mathbf B$ the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) in teslas, $\rho$ in coulombs per cubic metre, and $\mathbf J$ in amperes per square metre. The constants are [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md) $\epsilon_0$ and the vacuum value $\mu_0$ of [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md). “Vacuum” specifies the field laws without a material response; “source-free” additionally means $\rho=0$ and $\mathbf J=0$. Matter described by averaged fields instead requires [Maxwell equations in matter](maxwell-equations-in-matter.md) and [constitutive relations](constitutive-relations.md).

The first equation, [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md), makes [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) a source of [electric flux](electric-flux.md). [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) sets the net [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) through a closed surface to zero. The two [curl](curl.md) equations, [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) and the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md), couple changes of the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). Appropriate [initial conditions](initial-condition.md) and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) select a physical solution; the two [divergence](divergence.md) equations constrain the initial fields. [MIT 8.02 notes, §13.3: Maxwell's equations](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-02-physics-ii-electricity-and-magnetism-spring-2007/2d2f227e6fd26ee25f366759e02f03dc_chapte13em_waves.pdf).
