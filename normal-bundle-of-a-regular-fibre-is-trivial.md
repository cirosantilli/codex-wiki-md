# Normal bundle of a regular fibre is trivial

↑ **Parent:** [Regular level set theorem](regular-level-set-theorem.md)

For a [regular value](regular-value.md) $q$ of a [smooth map](smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) $f:M\to N$, let $F=f^{-1}(q)$. The [differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) identifies the quotient [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $TM|_F/TF$ with the fixed target [tangent space](tangent-space.md) $T_qN$ over every point of $F$. After choosing a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), that quotient identifies with the [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md). Hence the normal bundle is trivial even when the target manifold has no global orientation.
