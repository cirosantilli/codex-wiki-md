# Minimum-weight perfect matching

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect matching](perfect-matching.md)

A minimum-weight [perfect matching](perfect-matching.md) pairs every vertex exactly once while minimizing total edge weight. General weighted [perfect matching](perfect-matching.md) has polynomial-time algorithms. In the [Christofides algorithm](christofides-algorithm.md), it corrects precisely the odd-degree vertices of a [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md). Alternating edges of a cyclic order on an even number of vertices give two [perfect matchings](perfect-matching.md); the cheaper one costs at most half that cycle's cost.
