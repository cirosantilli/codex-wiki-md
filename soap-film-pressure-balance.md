# Soap film pressure balance

↑ **Parent:** [Mean curvature](mean-curvature.md)

For an ideal soap film with equal constant [surface tension](surface-tension.md) $\gamma$ on both faces, the effective tension is $\tau=2\gamma$. Choose a [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $N$ from the minus side to the plus side, and $\Delta p=p_- -p_+$. Translating the [Young–Laplace equation](young-laplace-equation.md) to $S=-dN$ gives

$$
\Delta p=-\tau(k_1+k_2)=-2\tau H.
$$

A single interface uses $\tau=\gamma$. An outward-oriented spherical soap bubble has $H=-1/R$, giving $\Delta p=4\gamma/R$. The positive-convex curvature sum in the [Young–Laplace equation](young-laplace-equation.md) article is $-2H$. [18.357 Interfacial Phenomena, §2.6, pp. 9–10: drop pressure and the two-interface soap-bubble factor](18-357-interfacial-phenomena-lecture-notes-fall-2010.md)

An open film with the same resting [gas](gas.md) on both sides has zero [pressure](pressure.md) jump. Neglecting film weight, [soap film pressure balance](soap-film-pressure-balance.md) gives $H=0$: a [minimal surface](minimal-surface.md). A pressurized bubble instead has nonzero [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md). These statements assume [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), constant [surface tension](surface-tension.md), and negligible normal [viscous stress](viscous-stress-tensor.md). [Surfactant](surfactant.md) gradients or motion require the fuller [normal stress](normal-stress.md) balance. [§2.4, p. 7, equations (2.3)–(2.4): normal and tangential interfacial stress balances](18-357-interfacial-phenomena-lecture-notes-fall-2010.md)

The variational outline is short. With fixed boundary, a normal displacement $fN$ gives $\delta A=-2\int_M Hf\,dA$ by the [first variation of area formula](first-variation-of-area-formula.md). For a closed interface oriented outward, $\delta V=\int_M f\,dA$, so stationarity of $\tau A-\Delta p\,V$ gives $-2\tau H-\Delta p=0$. This is a [first variation](first-variation.md) calculation; testing for a [stable minimal surface](stable-minimal-surface.md) needs the [second variation](second-variation.md), and deciding a [global minimum](global-minimum.md) needs a comparison of competitors.
