# Fermi contact interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperfine interaction](hyperfine-interaction.md)

The isotropic magnetic interaction of a nuclear [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) with [electron spin](electron-spin.md) density at the [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md). A nonrelativistic penetrating $s$ [orbital](single-particle-orbital.md) can have $|\psi_s(0)|^2\ne0$ and a substantial contact contribution to [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md). A filled paired shell cancels its net contact [spin](spin.md) density in the independent-particle approximation; unpaired occupation and core polarization can restore it. Relativistic and finite nuclear-size effects modify the point-contact description.
