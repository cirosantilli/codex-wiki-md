# Euclidean relation

↑ **Parent:** [Binary relation](binary-relation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_relation)

A [binary relation](binary-relation.md) is right Euclidean when any two successors of the same element are related in the indicated order. A [reflexive relation](reflexive-relation.md) with this property is an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md): reflexivity gives symmetry, and symmetry followed by the Euclidean property gives transitivity. Without reflexivity, even the empty [binary relation](binary-relation.md) is Euclidean.
