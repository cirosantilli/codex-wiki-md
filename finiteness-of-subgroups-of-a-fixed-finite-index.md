# Finiteness of subgroups of a fixed finite index

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)

A [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) has only finitely many [subgroups](subgroup.md) of any prescribed [index of a subgroup](index-of-a-subgroup.md) $n$. Every such subgroup is a point stabilizer for a transitive action on $n$ points, while a homomorphism from a finitely generated group to the finite [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_n$ has only finitely many possible images of the generators.
