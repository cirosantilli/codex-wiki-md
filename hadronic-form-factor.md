# Hadronic form factor

↑ **Parent:** [Hadron](hadron.md)

A [hadronic form factor](hadronic-form-factor.md) parametrizes a current [matrix element](matrix-element.md) between [hadron](hadron.md) states, separating allowed [Lorentz covariant](lorentz-covariance.md) [tensor](tensor.md) structures from [scalar-valued functions](scalar-valued-function.md) of [Lorentz invariant](lorentz-invariance.md) combinations of [momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md). These functions encode composite structure and [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md) dynamics. Equal kinematic [phase space](phase-space.md) does not imply equal form factors for different [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) transitions.
