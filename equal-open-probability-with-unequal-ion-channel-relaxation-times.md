# Equal open probability with unequal ion-channel relaxation times

↑ **Parent:** [Two-state ion-channel gating](two-state-ion-channel-gating.md)

Multiplying opening and closing rates by the same factor preserves equilibrium open [probability](probability.md) but rescales the relaxation and dwell times. At equal opening and closing rates, $p_\infty=1/2$ and relaxation is twice as fast as the reciprocal mean individual-state dwell. A two-state constant-voltage model cannot alone produce activation followed by inactivation during a maintained step.
