# Massive particle

↑ **Parent:** [Particle (physics)](particle-physics.md)

A [particle](particle-physics.md) with strictly positive [rest mass](invariant-mass.md). Its relativistic [energy](energy-split.md) obeys $E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4$, and its speed in an inertial frame is below the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md).
