# Sturm-Liouville operator

↑ **Parent:** [Sturm-Liouville form](sturm-liouville-form.md)

The weighted operator $L=W^{-1}[-(P\xi\prime)\prime+Q\xi]$, with real coefficients and $P,W>0$ in the interior, defines a [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md). Its realization as a [self-adjoint differential operator](self-adjoint-differential-operator.md) requires a domain whose endpoint conditions annihilate the boundary form $[P(\eta\prime^*\xi-\eta^*\xi\prime)]$. Its [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) uses the weight $W$ in the denominator.
