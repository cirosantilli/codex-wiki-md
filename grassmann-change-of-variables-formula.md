# Grassmann change-of-variables formula

↑ **Parent:** [Berezin integral](berezin-integral.md)

For independent [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md) and an invertible ordinary matrix $S$, the linear change $\eta=S\xi$ transforms the [Berezin integral](berezin-integral.md) measure by the inverse [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md). Indeed, the highest-degree monomial transforms by $\det S$, and coefficient extraction must undo that factor. For paired variables $\eta=S\xi$ and $\bar\eta=\bar\xi S^{-1}$ the two factors cancel. The order of odd variables and differentials fixes the overall sign and must be kept consistent.
