# Banach space of bounded linear operators

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md)

If $X$ is a normed vector space and $Y$ is a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), then the bounded linear maps $\mathcal B(X,Y)$ form a Banach space under the [operator norm](operator-norm.md). Completeness of the domain is unnecessary.
