# Telegraph relay

↑ **Parent:** [Wireless telegraphy](wireless-telegraphy.md)

A telegraph relay uses a small control [electric current](electric-current.md) to switch a separate local [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md). The recording mechanism's [energy](energy-split.md) can therefore come from its own [battery](electric-battery.md), rather than from the weak received [signal](signal.md). A sensitive trigger and a powerful readout serve different functions.
