# Mendelian segregation

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)

In an ordinary diploid [meiosis](meiosis.md), the two parental [allele](allele.md) copies segregate so that either copy is transmitted with [probability](probability.md) $1/2$. Transmissions in different [meioses](meiosis.md) are independent conditional on parental [genotypes](genotype.md). Multilocus transmissions also depend on chromosome phase and [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md).
