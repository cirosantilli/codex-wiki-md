# Adjoint of the Hodge star on middle-degree forms

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md)

On $m$-forms in an oriented Riemannian manifold of dimension $2m$, the [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) is orthogonal and satisfies $*^2=(-1)^{m^2}$. Hence

$$
*^\dagger=*^{-1}=(-1)^{m^2}*.
$$

It is self-adjoint when $m$ is even and skew-adjoint when $m$ is odd.
