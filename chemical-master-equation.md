# Chemical master equation

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic chemical kinetics](stochastic-chemical-kinetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_master_equation)

For reactions with [stoichiometric vectors](stoichiometric-vector.md) $\nu_r$ and [reaction propensity functions](reaction-propensity-function.md) $a_r$, the probability mass function $p(x,t)$ obeys

$$
\partial_t p(x,t)=\sum_r\left[a_r(x-\nu_r)p(x-\nu_r,t)-a_r(x)p(x,t)\right].
$$

It is the [forward operator of a Markov jump process](forward-operator-of-a-markov-jump-process.md) equation for the copy-number [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md).
