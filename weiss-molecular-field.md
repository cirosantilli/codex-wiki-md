# Weiss molecular field

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field theory](mean-field-theory.md)

The [Weiss molecular field](weiss-molecular-field.md) replaces exchange coupling to neighbouring moments by an effective local field proportional to the average [magnetization](magnetization.md). For two-state moments and $B_E=\mu_0\lambda M$, self-consistency gives $m=\tanh(m/t)$ at zero applied field, where $m=|M|/(n\mu)$ and $t=T/T_C$, with $k_BT_C=\mu_0\lambda n\mu^2$. This field represents a quantum exchange energy, not generally the actual magnetostatic field generated by the moments.
