# Derrick virial identity

↑ **Parent:** [Derrick's theorem](derrick-s-theorem.md)

Stationarity of a static solution under [Derrick scaling](derrick-scaling.md) requires $dE(\lambda)/d\lambda|_{\lambda=1}=0$. This necessary relation among the separately scaling energy terms is the Derrick virial identity.
