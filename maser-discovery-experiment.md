# Maser discovery experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Ammonia maser](ammonia-maser.md)

Ammonia gas entered a vacuum chamber through fine channels; the selected beam entered a cavity with input and output waveguides.[pp. 1264–1265, Fig. 1, source and vacuum](the-maser-new-type-of-microwave-amplifier-frequency-standard-and-spectrometer-by-james-power-gordon-and-others.md) With external [microwaves](microwave.md) swept across a molecular [resonance](resonance.md), the 1954 experiment read an increase in transmitted [power](power.md); with sufficient beam emission, it sustained [oscillation](oscillation.md).[p. 283, operation as spectrometer, amplifier and oscillator](molecular-microwave-oscillator-and-new-hyperfine-structure-in-the-microwave-spectrum-of-nh3-by-james-power-gordon-and-others.md)

The 1955 readout mixed two maser signals with a local [oscillator](oscillator.md) in a crystal detector, amplified them at an intermediate [frequency](frequency.md), and combined them in a diode detector. An [oscilloscope](oscilloscope.md) showed their [beat](beat.md) [frequency](frequency.md). Changing cavity tuning, source pressure or focuser field tested sensitivity; excessive detuning stopped [oscillation](oscillation.md).[pp. 1266–1267, Experimental Results, heterodyne and controls](the-maser-new-type-of-microwave-amplifier-frequency-standard-and-spectrometer-by-james-power-gordon-and-others.md) The [beat](beat.md) tests relative [frequency](frequency.md) stability; it does not by itself establish an absolute [frequency](frequency.md) or an intrinsic linewidth independent of detector noise. Detuning also changes the field–molecule interaction, so the threshold model's constant $G$ cannot describe arbitrary tuning scans.
