# Brownian paths are not uniformly continuous on the half-line

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

For every fixed mesh $1/k$, [independent increments](independent-increments.md) with the [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) imply increments of absolute value greater than one occur infinitely often [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), by the second [Borel-Cantelli lemma](borel-cantelli-lemmas.md). A countable intersection over $k$ contradicts [uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md) on $[0,\infty)$.
