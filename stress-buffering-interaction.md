# Stress-buffering interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Protective factor](protective-factor.md)

A pattern in which a resource modifies the relationship between [adversity](adversity.md) and an adverse outcome. In the model $\mathbb E[D\mid S,P]=\beta_0+\beta_1S+\beta_2P+\beta_3SP$, a negative $\beta_3$ attenuates the [adversity](adversity.md) slope as the resource increases. Its interpretation is scale dependent, and an observational interaction is not automatically a causal buffering effect.
