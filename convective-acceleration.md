# Convective acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Material derivative](material-derivative.md)

Convective acceleration is the spatial part $(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u$ of the [material derivative](material-derivative.md) of fluid velocity. It can remain nonzero in [steady flow](steady-flow.md), because a moving particle passes through positions with different velocities. The identity $(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=\nabla(|\mathbf u|^2/2)-\mathbf u\times(\nabla\times\mathbf u)$ relates it to [vorticity](vorticity.md).
