# Alternating trilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating multilinear map](alternating-multilinear-map.md)

An alternating trilinear form is a scalar-valued map, linear in three arguments, which vanishes whenever two arguments agree. In characteristic zero, interchanging two arguments negates its value, while cyclic permutation preserves it. For a [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) $B$ on a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), the tensor $B([x,y],z)$ is alternating exactly when $B$ is an [invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra](invariant-bilinear-form-on-a-lie-algebra.md).
