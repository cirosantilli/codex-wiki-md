# Aryl halide

↑ **Parent:** [Organohalide](organohalide.md)

An [organohalide](organohalide.md) with the halogen attached directly to an aromatic ring [carbon](carbon.md). Its substitution chemistry differs from an alkyl halide because the C–halogen bond is attached to an sp2 [carbon](carbon.md).
