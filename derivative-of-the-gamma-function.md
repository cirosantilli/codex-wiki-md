# Derivative of the gamma function

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma function](gamma-function.md)

For positive real $a$, differentiating the [gamma function](gamma-function.md) integral under the integral sign gives the displayed formula; successive [derivatives](derivative.md) insert higher powers of $\log t$. Dominated differentiation on compact positive-$a$ intervals is justified by exponential decay at infinity and integrability of $t^{a-1}|\log t|^n$ at zero. At $a=1$, these derivatives give moments used in logarithmic endpoint [asymptotic expansions](asymptotic-expansion.md).
