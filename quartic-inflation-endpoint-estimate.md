# Quartic-inflation endpoint estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic-potential slow-roll solution](quartic-potential-slow-roll-solution.md)

For $V=\lambda\phi^4/4$ and unreduced [Planck mass](planck-mass.md) $M_P$, the [potential slow-roll parameter](potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) is $\epsilon_V=M_P^2/(\pi\phi^2)$. Setting it to one gives the displayed conventional endpoint. The exact end of scalar-dominated [accelerating expansion](accelerating-expansion-of-the-universe.md) is $\epsilon_H=1$, equivalently $\dot\phi^2=V$. Since [slow-roll](slow-roll-approximation.md) is failing near that point, a precise endpoint requires evolving the full [inflaton equation of motion](inflaton-equation-of-motion.md); potential and kinetic criteria evaluated with a failing approximation need not give the same order-one coefficient.
