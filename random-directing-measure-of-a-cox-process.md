# Random directing measure of a Cox process

↑ **Parent:** [Cox process](cox-process.md)

A [Cox process](cox-process.md) can be directed by a random locally [finite measure](finite-measure.md), which need not have a density. Conditional counts in disjoint sets are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) Poisson variables with that measure's masses. Uniform circular offspring displacements give a directing measure supported on random circles; its unconditional mean can still be a multiple of planar [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). A random intensity function is the special case in which the directing measure is absolutely continuous.
