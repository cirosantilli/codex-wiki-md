# Albert Abraham Michelson

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md).[Independent biographical identity and professional specialization](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/michelson-a-a.pdf)

The [Michelson interferometer](michelson-interferometer.md) turns a change in an optical path into a shift of [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md). Controlling the returning beams and counting the shifts can connect very small distances to a [wavelength](wavelength.md).
