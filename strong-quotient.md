# Strong quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Strong epimorphism](strong-epimorphism.md)

A strong quotient of an object $A$ is the codomain of a [strong epimorphism](strong-epimorphism.md) out of $A$. A class of objects is closed under strong quotients when every such codomain remains in the class up to isomorphism.
