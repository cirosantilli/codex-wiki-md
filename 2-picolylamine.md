# 2-Picolylamine

↑ **Parent:** [Amine](amine.md)

[2-Picolylamine](2-picolylamine.md) is pyridin-2-ylmethanamine, with a [pyridine](pyridine.md) [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) and an aminomethyl [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) adjacent on the ring. It binds as an unsymmetrical neutral bidentate ligand, forming a five-membered [chelate ring](chelate-ring.md).
