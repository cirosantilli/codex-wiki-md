# Equal-tailed credible interval

↑ **Parent:** [Credible interval](credible-interval.md)

An [equal-tailed credible interval](equal-tailed-credible-interval.md) uses [quantiles](quantile-function.md) of a continuous [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) to leave [posterior probability](posterior-probability.md) $\alpha/2$ in each tail. It is also called a [central credible interval](equal-tailed-credible-interval.md); central refers to equal tail probabilities, not equal distances from the [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md). For the [beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) $\operatorname{Beta}(10,4)$ in [credible interval](credible-interval.md), the 95% endpoints are approximately $0.461868461$ and $0.909079605$, whose midpoint $0.685474033$ differs from the [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) $5/7$.
