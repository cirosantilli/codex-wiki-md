# Projective triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane](projective-plane.md)

A projective triangle is an ordered triple of noncollinear [projective points](projective-point.md) in a [projective plane](projective-plane.md), together with their three joining [projective lines](projective-line.md). In the [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md) this means that three chosen representative vectors are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). Pairing the vertices of two such triangles determines corresponding sides and the three corresponding-vertex joins.
