<h1 id="shannon-s-block-entropy-approximations">Shannon's block-entropy approximations</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy rate](entropy-rate.md)

In [A Mathematical Theory of Communication by Claude Shannon](a-mathematical-theory-of-communication-by-claude-shannon.md), [Claude Shannon](claude-shannon.md) compares block uncertainty per symbol, denoted $G_N$, with conditional uncertainty given the preceding symbols, denoted $F_N$. Theorems 5 and 6 connect both decreasing approximations to the source's limiting uncertainty. He uses symbol statistics at increasing block lengths to expose structure missed by single-letter counts.[§7, Theorems 5–6 and discussion, printed p. 14; Appendix 3, printed p. 30: block and conditional entropy approximations](a-mathematical-theory-of-communication-by-claude-shannon.md) The fair marginal [bits](bit.md) of the [binary Markov source](binary-markov-source.md) provide a small exact example of that distinction.
