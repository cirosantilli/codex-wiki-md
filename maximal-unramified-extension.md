# Maximal unramified extension

↑ **Parent:** [Unramified extension](unramified-extension.md)

The maximal unramified extension $K^{\mathrm{nr}}$ is the union of all finite unramified extensions of a non-Archimedean local field $K$. Its Galois group is canonically $\widehat{\mathbb Z}$, generated topologically by [Frobenius](frobenius-automorphism.md).
