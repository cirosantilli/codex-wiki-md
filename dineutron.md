# Dineutron

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md)

A two-[neutron](neutron-split.md) system. At low [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) its identical-fermion exchange symmetry forces the spin-singlet, isospin-one channel; there is no bound [dineutron](dineutron.md) in vacuum at physical parameters. This does not forbid correlated [neutron](neutron-split.md) pairs inside larger nuclei.
