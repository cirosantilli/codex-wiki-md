# K-theory of the unit tangent sphere bundle of complex projective space

↑ **Parent:** [K-theory Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](k-theory-gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md)

The [K-theory Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](k-theory-gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) and $e^K(T\mathbb{CP}^n)=(n+1)t^n$ give

$$
K^0(S(T\mathbb{CP}^n))
\cong\mathbb Z[t]/(t^{n+1},(n+1)t^n)
\cong\mathbb Z^n\oplus\mathbb Z/(n+1),
$$

and

$$
K^{-1}(S(T\mathbb{CP}^n))
\cong\ker((n+1)t^n\cdot-)
=(t)\cong\mathbb Z^n.
$$
