# Prussian blue

↑ **Parent:** [Inorganic chemistry](inorganic-chemistry.md)

An iron hexacyanoferrate material, often hydrated and nonstoichiometric; $\mathrm{Fe_4[Fe(CN)_6]_3}$ is an idealized composition used to explain a historical [cyanide](cyanide.md) recognition test.
