# Error evaluator polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Error locator polynomial](error-locator-polynomial.md)

For errors $E_i$ at distinct nonzero locations $X_i$, the evaluator associated with syndromes $S_\ell=\sum_i E_iX_i^{b+\ell}$ is $\Omega(z)=\sum_i E_iX_i^b\prod_{h\ne i}(1-X_hz)$. Its degree is smaller than the number of errors. Together with the [error locator polynomial](error-locator-polynomial.md) it gives the [Reed-Solomon key equation](reed-solomon-key-equation.md) and the [Forney algorithm](forney-algorithm.md) for error magnitudes.
