# Surface Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md)

The surface Laplacian is the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) of a surface with its induced [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md). It is $\Delta_sf=\nabla_s\cdot\nabla_sf$, using the [surface gradient](surface-gradient.md) and [surface divergence](surface-divergence.md). On a sphere of radius $a$, a degree-$l$ [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) has [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $-l(l+1)/a^2$.
