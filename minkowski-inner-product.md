# Minkowski inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperboloid model](hyperboloid-model.md)

This indefinite bilinear form supplies the metric of [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md). On $\mathbb R^{n+1}$ the Minkowski inner product with mostly-positive convention is

$$
\langle x,y\rangle_M=-x_0y_0+x_1y_1+\cdots+x_ny_n.
$$
