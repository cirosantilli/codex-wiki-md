# Linearly degenerate characteristic field

↑ **Parent:** [Simple wave](simple-wave.md)

A characteristic field is linearly degenerate if the directional derivative of its speed along its right [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) vanishes. Its simple waves have constant propagation speed along the state curve, so they do not steepen by amplitude-dependent transport.
