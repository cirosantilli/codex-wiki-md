# Deflection and spin in a distant sphere encounter

↑ **Parent:** [Mobility correction from a fixed distant sphere](mobility-correction-from-a-fixed-distant-sphere.md)

In a planar force-driven encounter with a fixed equal [sphere](sphere.md) and positive impact parameter $b\gg a$, the [mobility correction from a fixed distant sphere](mobility-correction-from-a-fixed-distant-sphere.md) gives $Y-b=27a^2b/[32(X^2+b^2)]+O(a^4/b^3)$. The moving [sphere](sphere.md) reaches maximum deflection $27a^2/(32b)$ and rotates clockwise through $9\pi a^2/(32b^2)$ to leading order. The spin is obtained by integrating half the reflected [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) [vorticity](vorticity.md), as prescribed by [Faxén rotation law](faxen-s-rotational-law.md). These numerical formulas require a distant encounter.
