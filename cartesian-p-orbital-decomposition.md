# Cartesian p-orbital decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md)

The angular parts $x/r$, $y/r$ and $z/r$ span the $\ell=1$ [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) basis. A radial function times $x+y+z$ therefore has definite $L^2=2\hbar^2$ and equal probabilities for the three $L_z$ outcomes.
