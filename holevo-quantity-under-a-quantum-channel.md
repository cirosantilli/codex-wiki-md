# Holevo quantity under a quantum channel

↑ **Parent:** [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md)

Attach an orthogonal classical label register to an ensemble. Its [quantum mutual information](quantum-mutual-information.md) with the quantum system equals the ensemble [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md). A local [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) changes the ensemble states but preserves their labels and probabilities. [Data processing for quantum mutual information](data-processing-for-quantum-mutual-information.md) then proves that the output [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md) cannot exceed the input value.
