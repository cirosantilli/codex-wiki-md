# Median filter

↑ **Parent:** [Image smoothing](image-smoothing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Median_filter)

A [median filter](median-filter.md) replaces an [image signal](image-signal.md) value by the [median](median.md) of nearby values. Unlike a linear average, it can reject isolated extreme observations without averaging both sides of an [image edge](image-edge.md). The small-radius response depends on neighbourhood geometry and measure; a uniform disk-area [median](median.md) and a circle-boundary [median](median.md) have different time scales.
