# Kurepa tree from an inaccessible binary tree

↑ **Parent:** [Kurepa tree](kurepa-tree.md)

Let $\kappa$ be [strongly inaccessible cardinal](strongly-inaccessible-cardinal.md) in the ground model and perform the [finite Lévy collapse to omega-one](finite-levy-collapse-to-omega-one.md). The ground full binary [set-theoretic tree](set-theoretic-tree.md) of height $\kappa$ has all levels of size less than $\kappa$, so these levels become countable. Its at least $(\kappa^+)^M$ ground branches remain distinct. The [chain in a partial order](chain-in-a-partial-order.md) condition preserves $(\kappa^+)^M$, which is the extension $\aleph_2$. The unchanged ground [set-theoretic tree](set-theoretic-tree.md) therefore witnesses the [Kurepa hypothesis](kurepa-hypothesis.md).
