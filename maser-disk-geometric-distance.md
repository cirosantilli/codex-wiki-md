# Maser-disk geometric distance

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic distance ladder](cosmic-distance-ladder.md)

An orbit model for a resolved [maser](maser-split.md) disk combines angular positions, Doppler velocities, accelerations and sometimes [proper motions](proper-motion.md) to infer physical orbital dimensions and distance. For an ideal matched circular radius, $R=v^2/a_{\rm orb}$ and $d=R/\theta$; velocities and accelerations of unrelated [maser](maser-split.md) spots cannot simply be inserted into that expression. Warps, inclination, eccentricity and feature identification enter real disk fits.[1999 abstract; VLBI proper motions and accelerations](a-4-percent-geometric-distance-to-the-galaxy-ngc4258-from-orbital-motions-in-a-nuclear-gas-disk-by-j-r-herrnstein-and-others.md)
