# Debye screening of colloid repulsion

↑ **Parent:** [DLVO theory](dlvo-theory.md)

For a symmetric monovalent reservoir with [number density](number-density.md) $n$ of each species, linearizing Poisson-Boltzmann theory gives the displayed inverse screening length. A planar potential decays exponentially; a spherical far field also has a geometrical inverse-distance factor. Increasing salt reduces the range of repulsion. Fixed surface potential, fixed charge and charge regulation need not give identical interaction amplitudes.
