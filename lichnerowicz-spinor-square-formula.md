# Lichnerowicz spinor-square formula

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md)

With $\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}$ and [spin](spin.md) [curvature](curvature.md) $[\nabla_\mu,\nabla_\nu]=R_{\mu\nu ab}\gamma^{ab}/4$, the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) operator squares to the [rough Laplacian](rough-laplacian.md) minus $R/4$. In the [curvature](curvature.md) contraction the four-gamma term vanishes by the [first Bianchi identity](first-bianchi-identity.md), the two-gamma terms vanish by Ricci symmetry, and the scalar term is $-2R$. In Riemannian signature $i\not\nabla$ is the [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) convention and its square is $-\nabla^2+R/4$.
