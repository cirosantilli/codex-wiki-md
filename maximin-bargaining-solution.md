# Maximin bargaining solution

↑ **Parent:** [Nash bargaining solution](nash-bargaining-solution.md)

For a finite two-person game, let $d_i$ be player $i$'s [security level payoff](security-level-payoff.md), obtained by maximizing its minimum expected [payoff](payoff.md) against the other player's actions. Apply the [Nash bargaining solution](nash-bargaining-solution.md) to the convexified feasible [payoff](payoff.md) region with this [disagreement point](disagreement-point.md). This is the [maximin bargaining solution](maximin-bargaining-solution.md). “Maximin” specifies the disagreement guarantees; the arbitration [objective function](objective-function.md) remains the [Nash product](nash-product.md), not the minimum of two numerically incomparable utilities.
