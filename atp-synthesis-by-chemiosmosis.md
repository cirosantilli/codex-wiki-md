# ATP synthesis by chemiosmosis

↑ **Parent:** [Chemiosmosis](chemiosmosis.md)

Downhill ion transport through [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) can drive [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) formation from its substrates. The electrochemical free-energy drop per transported ion combines the [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) and concentration term. Reverse coupling can use [ATP hydrolysis](atp-hydrolysis.md) to maintain the ion gradient.
