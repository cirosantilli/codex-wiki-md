# Polynomial polyvector field

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md)

For a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) $A$, a degree-$p$ polynomial polyvector field is an element of $\bigwedge_A^p\operatorname{Der}_k(A)$. Its product is the [exterior product](exterior-product.md). Together with the [Schouten-Nijenhuis bracket](schouten-nijenhuis-bracket.md) these spaces form a [Gerstenhaber algebra](gerstenhaber-algebra.md).
