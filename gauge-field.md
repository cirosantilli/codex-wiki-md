# Gauge field

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic quantum field](relativistic-quantum-field-split.md)

A [gauge field](gauge-field.md) is a connection associated with a local symmetry in a [gauge theory](gauge-theory.md). Its [gauge potential](gauge-field.md) defines the internal-symmetry contribution to a [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md). For an Abelian [gauge field](gauge-field.md), the [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) is $F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu$.
