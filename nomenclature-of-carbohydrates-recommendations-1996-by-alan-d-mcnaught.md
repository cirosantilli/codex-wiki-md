# Nomenclature of Carbohydrates (Recommendations 1996) by Alan D. McNaught

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry bibliography](chemistry-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Alan D. McNaught](work-by-alan-d-mcnaught.md)

Document prepared by [Alan D. McNaught](alan-d-mcnaught.md) for the IUPAC-IUBMB Joint Commission on Biochemical Nomenclature. The inspected landing page distinguishes the printed document’s preparation from the World Wide Web version prepared by [G. P. Moss](g-p-moss.md). Queen Mary University of London hosts the web version. Pure and Applied Chemistry 68, 1919–2008, is the 1996 publication; the listed 1997 reprints are distinct.

Official web version and credits: [https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/2carb/](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/2carb/)
