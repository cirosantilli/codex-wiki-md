# Reduction certificate for a nonintegral elliptic point

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel of reduction of an elliptic curve](kernel-of-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

On an integral [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) with [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) at a [prime](prime-number.md) $p$, a nonidentity [rational point](rational-point.md) reducing to the identity cannot have both coordinates integral at $p$. Indeed, an affine integral pair reduces to an affine point, distinct from the point at infinity. This can prove nonintegrality of a large [linear combination](linear-combination.md) without computing its coordinates. The nonidentity condition is essential and can often be checked using the known [torsion subgroup](torsion-subgroup.md).
