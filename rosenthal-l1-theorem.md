# Rosenthal l1 theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Basic sequence](basic-sequence.md)

Every bounded sequence in a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) has either a [weakly Cauchy sequence](weakly-cauchy-sequence.md) as a subsequence or a subsequence equivalent to the unit vector basis of $\ell^1$. The real-scalar proof represents vectors as continuous functions on a compact dual ball and uses infinite Ramsey theory to select independent lower and upper level sets when pointwise convergence cannot be obtained.
