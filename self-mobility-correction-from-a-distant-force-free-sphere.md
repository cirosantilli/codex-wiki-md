# Self-mobility correction from a distant force-free sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Method of reflections for Stokes flow](method-of-reflections-for-stokes-flow.md)

For two equal spheres of radius $a$ separated by $\mathbf R$, let the first have applied force $\mathbf F$ and let the second be [force-free](force-free.md) and [torque-free](torque-free.md). With $\mathbf U_0=\mathbf F/(6\pi\mu a)$, the leading reflected [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md) changes the first velocity by

$$
\delta\mathbf U=-\frac{15a^4}{4R^6}(\mathbf U_0\cdot\mathbf R)\mathbf R.
$$

The distant sphere's freely translating and rotating rigid motions remove its monopole force and torque fields; its incident [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md) produces the first self-mobility correction.
