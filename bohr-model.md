# Bohr model

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohr_model)

The Bohr model assumes circular electron orbits with quantized [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $N\hbar$. It reproduces the hydrogen energy levels, but [wavefunctions](wave-function.md) in quantum mechanics describe spatial probability distributions rather than definite trajectories. In particular, spherically symmetric [Coulomb bound states](coulomb-bound-state.md) have zero [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md).
