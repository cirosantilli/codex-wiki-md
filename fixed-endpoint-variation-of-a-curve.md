# Fixed-endpoint variation of a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Energy of a curve](energy-of-a-curve.md)

A fixed-endpoint variation of a [curve](curve.md) $\gamma:[a,b]\to S$ is a smooth family $\Gamma(s,t)$ with $\Gamma(0,t)=\gamma(t)$, $\Gamma(s,a)=\gamma(a)$ and $\Gamma(s,b)=\gamma(b)$. Its [variation vector field](variation-vector-field.md) $V(t)=\partial_s\Gamma(0,t)$ vanishes at the endpoints. Consequently integration by parts in the first variation of [energy of a curve](energy-of-a-curve.md) has no boundary contribution, and stationarity is characterized by the [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md).
