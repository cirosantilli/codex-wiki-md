# Conjugacy-class sum

↑ **Parent:** [Group algebra](group-algebra.md)

For a [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) $C$ of a finite [group](group-split.md), $z_C=\sum_{g\in C}g$ is central in its [group algebra](group-algebra.md). In a complex [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) of degree $d$ and [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) $\chi$, it acts by $|C|\chi(g)/d$; its trace gives this formula and [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) gives scalarity.
