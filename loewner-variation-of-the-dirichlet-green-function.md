# Loewner variation of the Dirichlet Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Chordal Loewner equation](chordal-loewner-equation.md)

With [half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md) $2t$, $f_t=g_t-W_t$ and $G_{\mathbb H}(z,w)=\log|(z-\overline w)/(z-w)|$, differentiating $G_{D_t}(z,w)=G_{\mathbb H}(f_t(z),f_t(w))$ gives the displayed identity. The translation by the driver cancels in the Green function. This rank-one [covariance](covariance.md) loss balances the quadratic covariation of the harmonic mean in the [SLE4 coupling with a Gaussian free field](sle4-coupling-with-a-gaussian-free-field.md).
