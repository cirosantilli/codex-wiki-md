# Skin friction coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Wall shear stress](wall-shear-stress.md)

The local [skin friction coefficient](skin-friction-coefficient.md) normalizes [wall shear stress](wall-shear-stress.md) by the outer [dynamic pressure](dynamic-pressure.md):

$$
c_f(x)=\frac{\tau_w(x)}{\tfrac12\rho U^2}\simeq\frac{0.664115}{\sqrt{\mathrm{Re}_x}}.
$$

For the ideal [Blasius boundary layer](blasius-boundary-layer.md) on one face of length $L$, averaging the local [skin friction coefficient](skin-friction-coefficient.md) gives $\overline c_f\simeq1.328230/\sqrt{\mathrm{Re}_L}$. The factor of two follows from integrating $x^{-1/2}$, not from counting both plate faces. [Drag](drag-physics.md) on two equally exposed faces doubles when the reference area remains that of one face.
