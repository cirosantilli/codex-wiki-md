# Big real divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Big divisor](big-divisor.md)

A big real divisor is an actual positive real combination of [big Cartier divisors](big-divisor.md). Equivalently it is real linearly equivalent, or numerically equivalent, to an [ample real divisor](ample-real-divisor.md) plus an effective real divisor. The [rational approximation of an ample-plus-effective real divisor](rational-approximation-of-an-ample-plus-effective-real-divisor.md) and [Kodaira's lemma](kodaira-s-lemma.md) connect these formulations. [Fujino's notes on big real divisors](https://www.math.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~fujino/big-r-divisor5.pdf) give the definition also for nonnormal varieties; [bigness under finite normalization](bigness-under-finite-normalization.md) relates it to section growth.
