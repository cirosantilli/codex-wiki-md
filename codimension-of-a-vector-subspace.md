# Codimension of a vector subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient vector space](quotient-vector-space.md)

For a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $W$ of $X$, its codimension is the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of the [quotient vector space](quotient-vector-space.md) $X/W$. This definition also applies when $X$ is infinite-dimensional. If $X=W\oplus E$ is a [direct sum](direct-sum.md), the map $e\mapsto e+W$ is a [linear isomorphism](linear-isomorphism.md) from $E$ to $X/W$, so $\operatorname{codim}_XW=\dim E$. In particular, the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of a nonzero [linear functional](linear-functional.md) has codimension one: normalize $Te=1$ and write $x=(x-(Tx)e)+(Tx)e$.
