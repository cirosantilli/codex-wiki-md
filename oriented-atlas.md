# Oriented atlas

↑ **Parent:** [Orientable smooth manifold](orientable-smooth-manifold.md)

An oriented atlas is a [smooth atlas](smooth-atlas.md) whose [smooth transition maps](smooth-transition-map.md) have positive [Jacobian determinants](jacobian-determinant.md) on every overlap. The coordinate [bases](basis.md) then give compatible choices of [orientation of a vector space](orientation-of-a-vector-space.md) on all [tangent spaces](tangent-space.md). A nowhere-vanishing top-degree [differential form](differential-form-split.md) gives such an atlas: restrict each chart until its coefficient has constant sign, reverse one coordinate if necessary to make that sign positive, and compare the positive coefficients on overlaps. Conversely, a [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) glues positive local coordinate top forms to a global [volume form](volume-form.md).
