# Uniform-sum crossing time below one

↑ **Parent:** [Renewal process](renewal-process.md)

For independent [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) increments on $(0,1)$ and $0\leq x\leq1$, let $N(x)$ be the first index at which their partial sum exceeds $x$. [First-step analysis](first-step-analysis.md) gives $M(x)=\mathbb E N(x)=1+\int_0^xM(s)ds$, hence $M=e^x$. Equivalently, $\mathbb P(N(x)>n)=x^n/n!$ is a simplex-volume probability and the [tail-sum formula](tail-sum-formula.md) sums to $e^x$. The strict inequality gives $N(0)=1$ almost surely.
