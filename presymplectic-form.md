# Presymplectic form

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic geometry](symplectic-geometry-split.md)

A [presymplectic form](presymplectic-form.md) is a closed [two-form](2-form.md), commonly assumed to have constant rank, that need not be nondegenerate. A [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) is its nondegenerate special case. Its kernel distribution is integrable: for kernel fields $X,Y$, the identity $d\omega=0$ gives $\iota_{[X,Y]}\omega=0$. If the characteristic leaf quotient is smooth and the form descends, that quotient has a [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md). A regular [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) energy hypersurface carries such a form with kernel spanned by its [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md).
