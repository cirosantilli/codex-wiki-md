# Brauer induction

↑ **Parent:** [Character theory](character-theory.md)

Every complex [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) of a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$ is an integer linear combination of [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) of [induced representations](induced-representation.md) from one-dimensional [group representations](group-representation.md) of subgroups $H_j\leq G$. Negative coefficients are allowed. Unlike [Artin induction](artin-induction.md), the subgroups need not be [cyclic groups](cyclic-group.md). Applied to an [Artin L-function](artin-l-function.md), compatibility with [induced representations](induced-representation.md) turns this character identity into a product of one-dimensional [Artin L-functions](artin-l-function.md) with integer exponents, reducing the behavior at $s=1$ to abelian extensions.
