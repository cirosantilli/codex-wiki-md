<h1 id="hund-s-coupling">Hund's coupling</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hund's rules](hund-s-rules.md)

An effective local exchange coupling can favor parallel [electron spins](electron-spin.md) in different orbitals on the same atom. The displayed sign convention represents that preference. It underlies the high-spin tendency in [Hund's rules](hund-s-rules.md) and the alignment of itinerant and core spins used in [double exchange](double-exchange-mechanism.md). The coupling is an effective description of intra-atomic Coulomb and exchange energies, rather than a separate classical magnetic force.
