# Rolling-cylinder loop contact threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

A solid cylinder rolling inside a circular loop has centre-path [radius](radius.md) $\ell=R-a$, [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $3mv^2/4$, and [speed](speed.md) $v^2=4g[h-\ell(1-\cos\theta)]/3$ at angle $\theta$ from the bottom. The normal contact [force](force-split.md) is smallest at the top. Requiring $v_t^2\ge g\ell$ gives the displayed necessary contact threshold under imposed no-slip rolling. The centre's maximum [acceleration](acceleration.md) on the circle is at the bottom, and at the threshold it is $11g/3$ upward. This is a limiting contact condition, not a guarantee for arbitrary finite [static friction](static-friction.md) or an unspecified approach curvature.
