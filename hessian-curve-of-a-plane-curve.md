# Hessian curve of a plane curve

↑ **Parent:** [Hessian criterion for a flex](hessian-criterion-for-a-flex.md)

For a [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) $F$ of degree $d\geq2$, the determinant of its three-by-three [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is homogeneous of degree $3(d-2)$ if nonzero. Its zero locus is the Hessian curve; it can be reducible or degenerate, and a nonzero constant for $d=2$ has empty zero locus. Over $\mathbb C$, its intersections with the nonsingular locus of $V(F)$ are precisely the [flexes](inflection-point-of-an-algebraic-plane-curve.md), by the [Hessian criterion for a flex](hessian-criterion-for-a-flex.md), as long as the tangent is not a component.
