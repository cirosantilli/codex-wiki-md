# Positive partial transpose

↑ **Parent:** [Partial transpose](partial-transpose.md)

A bipartite [density operator](density-matrix.md) has positive partial transpose when $\rho^{T_B}\geq0$. Every [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md) has this property, since transposing one factor of a positive [product state](product-state.md) preserves positivity and the separable sum preserves it as well. The property does not depend on which local basis defines the transpose.
