# Alkene ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

An [alkene ligand](alkene-ligand.md) commonly binds through both double-bond carbons, $\eta^2$. The balance of donation and backbonding is described by the [Dewar-Chatt-Duncanson model](dewar-chatt-duncanson-model.md).
