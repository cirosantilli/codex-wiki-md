# Kruskal extension of the five-dimensional Schwarzschild-Tangherlini metric

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild-Tangherlini metric](schwarzschild-tangherlini-metric.md)

The [tortoise coordinate](tortoise-coordinate.md) is $r_*=r+(r_h/2)\log|(r-r_h)/(r+r_h)|$. Set $u=t-r_*$ and $v=t+r_*$, then $U=-e^{-u/r_h}$ and $V=e^{v/r_h}$ in the right exterior. The extended radial metric is $-r_h^2(r+r_h)^2e^{-2r/r_h}dU\,dV/r^2$, regular at $r=r_h$. The horizons are $UV=0$, while $r=0$ gives $UV=1$ and is a spacelike curvature singularity. Compactification by $\arctan U,\arctan V$ displays two exteriors, a future [black hole](black-hole.md) and a past [white hole](white-hole.md).
