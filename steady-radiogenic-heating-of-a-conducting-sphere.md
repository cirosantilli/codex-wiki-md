# Steady radiogenic heating of a conducting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

For constant volumetric [heat generation rate](heat-generation-rate.md) $q_v$ and [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) $\kappa$, spherical steady [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) obeys $\kappa r^{-2}(r^2T')'+q_v=0$. Regularity at the centre and a specified surface [temperature](temperature-split.md) give the displayed parabolic profile. For a black surface radiating into negligible background radiation, global [energy conservation](conservation-of-energy.md) gives $\sigma_{\rm SB}T_S^4=q_vR/3$. The central excess scales as $R^2$, while the surface [temperature](temperature-split.md) scales only as $R^{1/4}$.
