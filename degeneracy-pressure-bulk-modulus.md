# Degeneracy-pressure bulk modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md)

For a nonrelativistic zero-temperature [free electron model](free-electron-model.md) with fixed particle number, [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) obeys $P\propto(N/V)^{5/3}$. Consequently its [bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md) is $K=-V(\partial P/\partial V)_N=5P/3=2nE_F/3$. At [temperatures](temperature-split.md) small compared with the [electron Fermi temperature](electron-fermi-temperature.md), the leading [isothermal compressibility](isothermal-compressibility.md) is $1/K$. This electronic contribution alone omits ionic binding and interaction corrections in a real [metal](metal.md).
