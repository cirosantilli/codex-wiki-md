# Carrier densities with chemical potential pinned to a band edge

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized complete Fermi-Dirac integral](normalized-complete-fermi-dirac-integral.md)

For equal-mass conduction and valence edges at $0,-E_g$ and fixed $\mu=0$, $n=N(T)F_{1/2}(0)$ and $p=N(T)F_{1/2}[-E_g/(k_BT)]$, where $N(T)=2[mk_BT/(2\pi\hbar^2)]^{3/2}$. Other states pinning the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) can compensate the unequal carrier charges; imposing intrinsic [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) in addition would change the problem.
