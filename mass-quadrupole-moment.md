# Mass quadrupole moment

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational wave](gravitational-wave.md)

The trace-free mass quadrupole moment of a mass distribution is

$$
Q_{ij}=\int \rho(\mathbf x)
\left(x_ix_j-\frac13\delta_{ij}|\mathbf x|^2\right)d^3x.
$$

It is the leading time-dependent mass multipole that can radiate in [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md): mass conservation removes monopole radiation, while momentum conservation removes mass-dipole radiation.
