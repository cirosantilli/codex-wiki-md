# Phosphoenolpyruvate

↑ **Parent:** [Phosphoenolpyruvic acid](phosphoenolpyruvic-acid.md)

[Phosphoenolpyruvate](phosphoenolpyruvate.md) supplies a [phosphate](phosphate.md) group to [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) in the final [glycolysis](glycolysis.md) step catalyzed by [pyruvate kinase](pyruvate-kinase.md). The free ionic form used in the worked balance has charge $-3$.
