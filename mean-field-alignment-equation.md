# Mean-field alignment equation

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field closure for velocity alignment](mean-field-closure-for-velocity-alignment.md)

The continuum [velocity alignment](velocity-alignment.md) equation with $\mathbf L[f](\mathbf x,\mathbf v)=\int K(\mathbf x,\mathbf x_\star)(\mathbf v_\star-\mathbf v)f_\star\,d^3x_\star\,d^3v_\star$. For symmetric nonnegative $K$, it conserves total [mass](mass.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) while dissipating [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). Its force is affine in [velocity](velocity.md), with a nonzero [velocity-space divergence](velocity-space-divergence.md).
