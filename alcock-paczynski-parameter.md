<h1 id="alcock-paczynski-parameter">Alcock-Paczyński parameter</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alcock-Paczyński test](alcock-paczynski-test.md)

The Alcock-Paczyński parameter compares radial and transverse distance conversion at one redshift. An intrinsically isotropic comoving feature obeys

$$
\frac{\Delta z}{\Delta\theta}=H(z)\chi(z),
$$

so its unknown absolute size cancels.
