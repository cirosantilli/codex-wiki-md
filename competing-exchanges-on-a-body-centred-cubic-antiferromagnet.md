# Competing exchanges on a body-centred cubic antiferromagnet

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md)

With $H=\sum_{ij\ \mathrm{bonds}}J_{ij}\mathbf S_i\cdot\mathbf S_j$ counting bonds once, type-I and type-II classical collinear energies per [spin](spin.md) are $S^2(-4J_1+3J_2)$ and $-3J_2S^2$. Their boundary is $J_2/J_1=2/3$. The corresponding ordering reflections have integer indices with odd sum, and three half-integer indices, respectively, in units of $2\pi/a$. This compares classical order, not an exact quantum product-state [ground state](ground-state.md).
