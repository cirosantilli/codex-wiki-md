# Closed uniformly tight compactness criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Prokhorov's theorem](prokhorov-s-theorem.md)

A weakly closed [uniformly tight](uniform-tightness.md) family of Borel [probability measures](probability-measure.md) on a [Polish space](polish-space.md) is weakly compact. Embed the underlying space into a compact [metric](metric.md) closure, take subsequential pushforward limits, and use the closed-set [Portmanteau theorem](portmanteau-theorem.md) on the common tightness compact sets. The limit retains full mass in the original space; inverse continuity of the [probability pushforward embedding theorem](probability-pushforward-embedding-theorem.md) then returns the weak limit.
