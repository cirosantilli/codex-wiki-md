# Tensor cube of the defining unitary representation

↑ **Parent:** [Schur–Weyl duality](schur-weyl-duality.md)

For the [unitary group](unitary-group.md) $U(n)$ with $n\geq3$, the third [tensor power](tensor-power.md) of its [defining representation](defining-representation-of-a-matrix-lie-algebra.md) decomposes as displayed. The first two summands have [highest weights](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $(3,0,\ldots)$ and $(1,1,1,0,\ldots)$ and [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md) $\binom{n+2}3$ and $\binom n3$. The mixed summand has [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $(2,1,0,\ldots)$ and [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $n(n^2-1)/3$. In the three-dimensional [weight space](weight-space.md) spanned by [permutations](permutation.md) of $e_1\otimes e_1\otimes e_2$, the vectors killed by all positive [root vectors](root-vector.md) have coefficient sum zero, giving multiplicity two. For $n=2$, the exterior cube vanishes and $V_{(2,1)}=\det\otimes\mathbb C^2$.
