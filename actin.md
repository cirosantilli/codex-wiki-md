# Actin

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein.md)

[Actin](actin.md) is a [protein](protein.md) whose monomers assemble into filaments. An idealized actin filament has a bending modulus and persistence length; inferring its effective elastic modulus from those measurements additionally requires a cross-sectional model.
