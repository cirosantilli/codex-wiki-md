# Feynman-gauge adjoint propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-boson propagator](gauge-boson-propagator.md)

With a mostly-plus [metric signature](metric-signature.md), $e^{iS}$, and gauge-fixing term $-(\partial\cdot A)^2/2$, the free adjoint gauge-field kernel is $-p^2\eta_{\mu\nu}\delta_{ab}$. Inverting it gives the displayed [Feynman propagator](feynman-propagator.md). This convention uses the same denominator and sign as a massless real [scalar propagator](scalar-propagator.md).
