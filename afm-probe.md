# AFM probe

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

A sharp tip carried by a calibrated [AFM cantilever](afm-cantilever.md) and brought near a surface to sense [forces](force-split.md). Tip geometry and contamination influence both lateral resolution and mechanical or electrical measurements.
