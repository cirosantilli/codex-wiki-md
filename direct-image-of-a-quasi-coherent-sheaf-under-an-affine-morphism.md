# Direct image of a quasi-coherent sheaf under an affine morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Direct image sheaf](direct-image-sheaf.md)

For an [affine morphism](affine-morphism.md) $f:X\to Y$, the direct image of every [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) on $X$ is quasi-coherent. On affine charts this is the fact that restriction of scalars turns the module sheaf $\widetilde M$ into the module sheaf associated with the same underlying module over the target ring.
