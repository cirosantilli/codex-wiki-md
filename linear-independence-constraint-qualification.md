# Linear independence constraint qualification

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint qualification](constraint-qualification.md)

For smooth equality constraints $h_j=0$ and inequality constraints $g_i\geq0$, this condition requires linear independence of all equality gradients and active inequality gradients at the feasible point. It ensures existence of [Lagrange multipliers](lagrange-multiplier.md) at a local optimum and uniqueness of their values for a fixed objective gradient. It implies the [Mangasarian–Fromovitz constraint qualification](mangasarian-fromovitz-constraint-qualification.md) by solving the independent gradient equations with zero equality derivatives and positive active inequality derivatives.
