# Rogue planet

↑ **Parent:** [Planet](planet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogue_planet)

A rogue planet has no bound host star. Without stellar irradiation, its luminosity can still be supplied by internal cooling and [Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction](kelvin-helmholtz-mechanism.md); its emitting [temperature](temperature.md) need not equal the negligible irradiation-based [planetary equilibrium temperature](planetary-equilibrium-temperature.md).
