# Joint spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Simultaneous diagonalization](simultaneous-diagonalization.md)

For commuting diagonalizable operators $A_1,\ldots,A_r$ on a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), the joint spectrum is the set of tuples $(a_1,\ldots,a_r)$ for which there is a common nonzero [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) $v$ with $A_iv=a_iv$. Commuting self-adjoint operators on a finite-dimensional [inner product space](inner-product-space.md) admit a simultaneous [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md).
