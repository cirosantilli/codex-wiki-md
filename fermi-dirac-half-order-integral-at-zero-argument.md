# Fermi-Dirac half-order integral at zero argument

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized complete Fermi-Dirac integral](normalized-complete-fermi-dirac-integral.md)

Expanding the Fermi denominator as an alternating exponential series gives $F_{1/2}(0)=(1-2^{-1/2})\zeta(3/2)\simeq0.765147$. Thus a band with [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) pinned exactly at its lower edge has $n=F_{1/2}(0)N_c$, rather than the classical result $N_c$.
