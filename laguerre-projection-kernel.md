# Laguerre projection kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal polynomial projection kernel](orthogonal-polynomial-projection-kernel.md)

For $w(x)=x^ae^{-x}$ on $[0,\infty)$, $a>-1$, the monic [Generalized Laguerre polynomials](generalized-laguerre-polynomial.md) have squared norms $h_j=j!\Gamma(j+a+1)$. The corresponding [orthogonal polynomial projection kernel](orthogonal-polynomial-projection-kernel.md) is $(xy)^{a/2}e^{-(x+y)/2}\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}j!L_j^{(a)}(x)L_j^{(a)}(y)/\Gamma(j+a+1)$ for nonnegative $x,y$, and zero otherwise.
