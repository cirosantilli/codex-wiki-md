# Hall coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Hall effect](hall-effect.md)

The [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md) relates a transverse [electric field](electric-field-split.md) to longitudinal [current density](current-density.md) and transverse [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), with oriented axes as in the displayed formula. Its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is $\mathrm{m^3/C}$. For one isotropic [Drude model](drude-model.md) species it is $1/(nq)$; multiple [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md) need not follow that formula.
