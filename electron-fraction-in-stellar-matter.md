# Electron fraction in stellar matter

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear statistical equilibrium](nuclear-statistical-equilibrium.md)

For electrically neutral fully ionized matter, $Y_e$ is the number of electrons, or total [protons](proton.md), per baryon. With isotope [mass](mass.md) fractions $X_i$, charge numbers $Z_i$ and [mass](mass.md) numbers $A_i$, the displayed sum neglects small nuclear mass-defect corrections. Strong and electromagnetic reactions preserve it, while [electron capture](electron-capture.md) and [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md) reduce it. The proton-to-neutron number ratio is $Y_e/(1-Y_e)$.
