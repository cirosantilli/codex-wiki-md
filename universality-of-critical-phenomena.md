# Universality of critical phenomena

↑ **Parent:** [Critical phenomenon](critical-phenomenon-split.md)

[Universality](universality-of-critical-phenomena.md) of critical phenomena is the agreement of critical exponents or normalized scaling forms between microscopically different systems. Dimension, symmetry and interaction range help determine the universality class. A [renormalization-group flow](renormalization-group-flow.md) can explain this agreement by showing that different microscopic models approach the same fixed-point description, while irrelevant differences fade at large scales.
