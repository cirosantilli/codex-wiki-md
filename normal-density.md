# Normal density

↑ **Parent:** [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md)

The standard [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) has [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $\phi(z)=(2\pi)^{-1/2}e^{-z^2/2}$. Its rapid decay explains both concentration at one location and the logarithmic growth of a [Gaussian maximum](gaussian-maximum.md) over many locations.
