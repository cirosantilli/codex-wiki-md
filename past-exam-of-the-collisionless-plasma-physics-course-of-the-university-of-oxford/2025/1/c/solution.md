<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [plasma wave resonance](../../../../plasma-wave-resonance.md) has $n^2\to\infty$. At finite coefficients of the [magnetized cold-plasma dielectric tensor](../../../../magnetized-cold-plasma-dielectric-tensor.md), its generic condition is the vanishing of the coefficient of $n^4$:

$$
\boxed{S\sin^2\theta+P\cos^2\theta=0,\qquad \tan^2\theta=-P/S.}
$$

A [plasma wave cutoff](../../../../plasma-wave-cutoff.md) has $n^2=0$, so the constant term of the [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) vanishes:

$$
\boxed{PRL=0,\quad\text{that is }P=0,\ R=0,\ \text{or }L=0.}
$$

Both statements are branch conditions: check the undivided [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) at special angles and simultaneous zero factors. In particular, the parallel [plasma oscillation](../../../../plasma-oscillation.md) at $P=0$ is a longitudinal branch with arbitrary $k$ in the cold limit, while the transverse parallel branches have [plasma wave cutoffs](../../../../plasma-wave-cutoff.md) at $R=0$ or $L=0$. Poles of the [magnetized cold-plasma dielectric tensor](../../../../magnetized-cold-plasma-dielectric-tensor.md) can give additional limiting [plasma wave resonances](../../../../plasma-wave-resonance.md), such as the parallel [cold-plasma right-handed wave](../../../../cold-plasma-right-handed-wave.md) at $\omega=\Omega_e$; these are not obtained by assuming finite $S,P,g$.
