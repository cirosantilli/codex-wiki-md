<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For parallel propagation, use the undivided [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md):

$$
P(n^2-R)(n^2-L)=0.
$$

The five requested cases are:

- **[Plasma oscillations](../../../../plasma-oscillation.md):** $P=0$ and $\delta\mathbf E\parallel\mathbf B_0\parallel\mathbf k$. With negligible [ion](../../../../ion.md) response, $\omega^2=\omega_{pe}^2$, independently of $k$ in this cold approximation.
- **[Cold-plasma right-handed waves](../../../../cold-plasma-right-handed-wave.md):** $n^2=R$, $E_y=iE_x$, $E_z=0$, for propagation along $+\mathbf B_0$. At a fixed point, the real [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) rotates from $+\hat{\mathbf x}$ towards $+\hat{\mathbf y}$, in the sense of [electron](../../../../electron.md) gyration.
- **[Cold-plasma left-handed waves](../../../../cold-plasma-left-handed-wave.md):** $n^2=L$, $E_y=-iE_x$, $E_z=0$. The real [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) rotates in the opposite sense.
- **[Ordinary plasma waves](../../../../ordinary-plasma-wave.md):** for perpendicular propagation, $\theta=\pi/2$, $n^2=P$, and $\delta\mathbf E\parallel\mathbf B_0$. Their [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) is $\omega^2=\omega_{pe}^2+c^2k^2$.
- **[Extraordinary plasma waves](../../../../extraordinary-plasma-wave.md):** for perpendicular propagation, $n^2=RL/S$, $E_z=0$, and $E_y=igE_x/(S-n^2)$. In general the [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) has both longitudinal and transverse components.

For the perpendicular cases the undivided [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) factors as $(n^2-P)(Sn^2-RL)=0$, up to an overall nonzero factor. This also identifies the [extraordinary plasma wave](../../../../extraordinary-plasma-wave.md) [plasma wave resonance](../../../../plasma-wave-resonance.md) at $S=0$.
