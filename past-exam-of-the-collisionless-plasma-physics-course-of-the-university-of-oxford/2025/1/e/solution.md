<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

An [electrostatic plasma wave](../../../../electrostatic-plasma-wave.md) has $\delta\mathbf E=-i\mathbf k\phi$. In the absence of externally imposed [charge density](../../../../charge-density.md), the [Gauss law](../../../../gauss-s-law.md) for the [magnetized cold-plasma dielectric tensor](../../../../magnetized-cold-plasma-dielectric-tensor.md) is $\mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\epsilon\cdot\delta\mathbf E=0$. Consequently,

$$
\boxed{k_\perp^2S+k_\parallel^2P=0.}
$$

The antisymmetric terms of the [magnetized cold-plasma dielectric tensor](../../../../magnetized-cold-plasma-dielectric-tensor.md) cancel in this scalar contraction. For oblique propagation this is the leading longitudinal condition in the large-[refractive index](../../../../refractive-index.md) approximation; a small transverse [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is generally required by the full [Maxwell equations](../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) at finite $n$. For parallel propagation an exactly longitudinal solution exists: $P=0$, hence $\omega^2\simeq\omega_{pe}^2$. It coincides with the $\theta\to0$ limit of the finite-coefficient [plasma wave resonance](../../../../plasma-wave-resonance.md) condition in [c](../c.md). At exactly $\theta=0$, $P$ factors out of the transverse [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md), leaving the independent [plasma oscillation](../../../../plasma-oscillation.md) rather than an isolated divergence of the transverse branches.
