<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For the [ordinary plasma wave](../../../../ordinary-plasma-wave.md), choose $\mathbf k=k\hat{\mathbf x}$ and $\delta\mathbf E=E_z\hat{\mathbf z}$. The equations in [a](../a.md) give $u_{xs}=u_{ys}=0$ and

$$
u_{zs}=\frac{iq_s}{m_s\omega}E_z.
$$

The driven particle motion is entirely parallel to $\mathbf B_0$: the parallel [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) has no magnetic contribution. Nevertheless, **the [ordinary plasma wave](../../../../ordinary-plasma-wave.md) is electromagnetic**, since $\delta\mathbf E\perp\mathbf k$ and the [Faraday law](../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\delta\mathbf B=-\frac{ck}{\omega}E_z\hat{\mathbf y}\ne0.}
$$

An [electrostatic plasma wave](../../../../electrostatic-plasma-wave.md) instead has $\delta\mathbf E\parallel\mathbf k$ and zero perturbed [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). Alignment with the equilibrium [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) does not make a wave electrostatic. At the exact [plasma wave cutoff](../../../../plasma-wave-cutoff.md) $k=0$, this propagating-wave distinction becomes degenerate.
