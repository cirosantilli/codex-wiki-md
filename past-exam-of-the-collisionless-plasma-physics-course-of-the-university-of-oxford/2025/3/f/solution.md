<h1 id="3/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Both mechanisms can extract [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) from an equilibrium [pressure gradient](../../../../pressure-gradient.md) in unfavorable [magnetic field-line curvature](../../../../magnetic-field-line-curvature.md), but the dynamics retained here is the macroscopic electromagnetic displacement of a [flux tube](../../../../flux-tube.md). It depends on the total [pressure gradient](../../../../pressure-gradient.md), including [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [ion](../../../../ion.md) contributions, and it does not require an [ion temperature gradient](../../../../ion-temperature-gradient.md) specifically. Its stable branch has $\omega\sim k_\parallel v_A$; with low [plasma beta](../../../../plasma-beta.md), this is much faster than [ion](../../../../ion.md) parallel streaming at the same $k_\parallel$.

The familiar [ion-temperature-gradient instability](../../../../ion-temperature-gradient-instability.md) is a slower drift-scale instability. In its usual electrostatic ordering the [Boltzmann electron response](../../../../boltzmann-electron-response.md) is established by rapid [electron](../../../../electron.md) parallel motion, while [ion](../../../../ion.md) magnetic drifts, [ion](../../../../ion.md) temperature evolution and [polarization drift](../../../../polarization-drift.md) provide the instability dynamics. A representative ordering is

$$
k_\parallel v_{\mathrm{th},i}\lesssim|\omega|\ll k_\parallel v_{\mathrm{th},e},\qquad
|\omega|\ll k_\parallel v_A,\qquad
\omega_{*T_i}\sim\frac{k_yv_{\mathrm{th},i}^2}{\Omega_iL_{T_i}},\quad
\omega_{di}\sim\frac{k_yv_{\mathrm{th},i}^2}{\Omega_iR}.
$$

The last two expressions specify scales rather than exact geometric factors; $L_{T_i}^{-1}=-d\ln T_{0i}/dx$. The present leading [kinetic magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../kinetic-magnetohydrodynamics.md) system drops these relative species drifts and their finite-[gyroradius](../../../../gyroradius.md) corrections. It also retains the perturbed field direction, which enforces the advected-distribution cancellation in [c](../c.md); a rapid [electron](../../../../electron.md) streaming rate alone cannot turn that cancellation into a [Boltzmann electron response](../../../../boltzmann-electron-response.md) on an unperturbed field.

To recover [ion-temperature-gradient instability](../../../../ion-temperature-gradient-instability.md) physics, take the slow, nearly electrostatic limit $|\omega|/(k_\parallel v_A)\ll1$, retain [magnetic curvature drift](../../../../magnetic-curvature-drift.md), [gradient-B drift](../../../../gradient-b-drift.md) and [polarization drift](../../../../polarization-drift.md) in low-flow [drift kinetics](../../../../drift-kinetics.md) or [gyrokinetics](../../../../gyrokinetics.md), and allow a sufficiently strong destabilizing [ion temperature gradient](../../../../ion-temperature-gradient.md). At long perpendicular [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md) one may still have $k_\perp\rho_i\ll1$, provided the leading nonzero [ion](../../../../ion.md) polarization is kept; at $k_\perp\rho_i=O(1)$ full [finite Larmor radius effects](../../../../finite-larmor-radius-effect.md) are needed. There is no contradiction between a drift-scale [ion-temperature-gradient instability](../../../../ion-temperature-gradient-instability.md) and the macroscopic [interchange instability](../../../../interchange-instability.md) derived here: they use different asymptotic balances.
