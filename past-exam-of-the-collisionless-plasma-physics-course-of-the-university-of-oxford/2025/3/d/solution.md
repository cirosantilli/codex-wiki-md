<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The result is a [fluid Eulerian perturbation](../../../../eulerian-perturbation-of-a-fluid-variable.md) generated by translating the entire equilibrium [phase-space distribution function](../../../../phase-space-distribution-function.md) through $\xi_x$:

$$
f_\alpha(x,\mu,v_\parallel,t)=f_{0\alpha}(x-\xi_x,\mu,v_\parallel)+O(\xi_x^2),\qquad
\boxed{\Delta f_\alpha=\delta f_\alpha+\xi_x\partial_xf_{0\alpha}=0.}
$$

Here $\Delta$ follows the displaced [flux tube](../../../../flux-tube.md). [Magnetic flux freezing](../../../../magnetic-flux-freezing.md) carries the tube and its particles across the equilibrium [pressure gradient](../../../../pressure-gradient.md). To leading order the tube changes neither its field strength nor its length by enough to alter the particle [phase-space distribution function](../../../../phase-space-distribution-function.md): these changes are smaller by [plasma beta](../../../../plasma-beta.md) or $L_p/R$. Parallel streaming remains within the same displaced tube, on which the original equilibrium [phase-space distribution function](../../../../phase-space-distribution-function.md) was uniform. It therefore cannot erase the displacement-generated contrast between adjacent tubes. This is why the term $v_\parallel\delta b_x\partial_xf_{0\alpha}$ in [c](../c.md) exactly cancels the apparent parallel relaxation of $-\xi_x\partial_xf_{0\alpha}$. No collisional restoration of a [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md) is needed.
