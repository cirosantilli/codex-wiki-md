<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At $a=-x_T$, define

$$
\alpha=-\left.\frac{dk^2}{dx}\right|_a=\frac{2\omega_{pe0}^2x_T}{c^2L^2}>0,\qquad
\ell=\alpha^{-1/3},\qquad z=\frac{x-a}{\ell}.
$$

The first-order [Taylor expansion](../../../../taylor-expansion.md) is $k^2(x)=-\alpha(x-a)+O((x-a)^2)$, so the local [wave equation](../../../../wave-equation-split.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{\frac{d^2 E}{dz^2}-zE=0,\qquad E=C_A\operatorname{Ai}(z)+C_B\operatorname{Bi}(z).}
$$

This is the [Airy ordinary differential equation](../../../../airy-ordinary-differential-equation.md). Its $z<0$ side is oscillatory and its $z>0$ side is evanescent. A matching overlap requires $\ell\ll|x-a|\ll x_T$, hence well-separated [wave turning points](../../../../wave-turning-point.md) with $\ell\ll x_T$. At $b=x_T$, the coordinate $z_R=(b-x)/\ell$ gives the same [Airy ordinary differential equation](../../../../airy-ordinary-differential-equation.md), with its evanescent side again at positive argument.
