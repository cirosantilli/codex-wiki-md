<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [wave turning point](../../../../wave-turning-point.md) satisfies $k^2(x)=0$, equivalently $\omega_{pe}^2(x)=\omega^2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{x=\pm x_T,\qquad x_T=L\sqrt{1-\frac{\omega^2}{\omega_{pe0}^2}}.}
$$

For $0<\omega<\omega_{pe0}$ the two [wave turning points](../../../../wave-turning-point.md) lie inside the column and are simple. Defining $a=-x_T$ and $b=x_T$, the local [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) becomes

$$
k^2(x)=\frac{\omega_{pe0}^2}{c^2L^2}(x^2-x_T^2).
$$
