# Inertia tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inertia_tensor)

The inertia tensor about a point is

$$
I_{ij}=\int\left(r^2\delta_{ij}-r_ir_j\right)\,dm.
$$

It maps [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) to [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
