# Plastic flow rule

↑ **Parent:** [Plastic strain](plastic-strain.md)

A [plastic flow rule](plastic-flow-rule.md) prescribes the direction and rate of plastic straining once a yield condition is active. In a plastic-potential model its direction is the [stress](stress.md) [gradient](gradient.md) of $g$, multiplied by a nonnegative plastic multiplier. An [associated flow rule](associated-flow-rule.md) takes $g$ equal to the yield function. The [stress](stress.md)-[strain](strain.md) split, loading conditions and hardening evolution are needed along with this equation to close an elastoplastic model.
