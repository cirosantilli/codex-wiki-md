# Paratope

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paratope)

A [paratope](paratope.md) is the recognizing surface on an [antibody](antibody.md); the [epitope](epitope.md) is the recognized feature on its [antigen](antigen.md). Contacts depend on spatial arrangement and chemistry, including [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md) and [Van der Waals forces](van-der-waals-force.md). Thus a [paratope](paratope.md) is not simply a slot for a [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md) string. A folded [protein](protein.md) can bring distant [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) into one [epitope](epitope.md).
