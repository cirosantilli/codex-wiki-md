# Separated solutions of the Black-Scholes equation

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes equation](black-scholes-equation.md)

In the [Black-Scholes equation with continuous stock dividends](black-scholes-equation-with-continuous-stock-dividends.md), [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) gives $g'=(r-\lambda)g$ and $\sigma^2S^2f''/2+(r-q)Sf'-\lambda f=0$. Substituting $f=S^\alpha$ gives the displayed eigenvalue. A stationary solution uses the two roots of $\sigma^2\alpha(\alpha-1)/2+(r-q)\alpha-r=0$. For positive volatility and $r>0$, their product is negative, so they are distinct real roots.
