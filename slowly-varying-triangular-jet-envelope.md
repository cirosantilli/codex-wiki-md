# Slowly varying triangular-jet envelope

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md)

For $A_t=-a(\epsilon x)A_x-ia(\epsilon x)^2A_{xx}$, a downstream [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) at fixed frequency $\Omega<1/4$ has local [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k=q/a$. The next-order [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) equation is $(a-2a^2k)B_X=a^2k_XB$, giving the stated envelope. For small $\Omega$, it reduces to $B\propto a^{-\Omega}$ to first order in frequency. The approximation requires positive slowly varying width and separation from the double root at $\Omega=1/4$.
