# Theta series of a Euclidean lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian theta sum](gaussian-theta-sum.md)

The [lattice theta series](theta-series-of-a-euclidean-lattice.md) of a full-rank [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) records the multiset of squared lengths of lattice vectors. The Gaussian form shown here converges for $t>0$. For a [flat torus](flat-torus.md) $\mathbb R^d/\Lambda$, its [heat trace](heat-trace.md) is $\Theta_{\Lambda^*}(4\pi t)$, by the [spectrum of a flat torus](spectrum-of-a-flat-torus.md). Equal theta series do not in general imply orthogonal equivalence of lattices.
