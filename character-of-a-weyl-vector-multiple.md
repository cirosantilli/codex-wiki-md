# Character of a Weyl-vector multiple

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md)

For a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) and an [integer](integer.md) $k\ge0$, the [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md) and [Weyl denominator formula](weyl-denominator-formula.md) give

$$
\operatorname{ch}L(k\rho)=e^{k\rho}\prod_{\alpha\in R^+}(1+e^{-\alpha}+\cdots+e^{-k\alpha}),\qquad
\dim L(k\rho)=(k+1)^{|R^+|}.
$$

Apply the dilation $e^\mu\mapsto e^{(k+1)\mu}$ to the denominator identity to obtain the numerator. Cancellation leaves a finite [geometric series](geometric-series.md) for each positive [root of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md). Evaluating the resulting polynomial at all formal exponentials equal to one gives the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md), without taking an undefined quotient at the identity.
