# Lp almost period

↑ **Parent:** [Almost period of a function](almost-period-of-a-function.md)

For $f:G\to\mathbb C$ and $X\geq0$, an $L^p$ almost period of $f$ with error $X$ is an element $t\in G$ such that

$$
\|\tau_tf-f\|_p\leq X,
\qquad (\tau_tf)(x)=f(x+t).
$$
