# Lottery over joint action profiles

↑ **Parent:** [Bimatrix game](bimatrix-game.md)

A probability distribution over joint action profiles implements a [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of their two-player payoff vectors. The attainable expected payoffs form the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of the pure joint payoffs. The distribution need not factor into independent [mixed strategies](mixed-strategy.md), and a freely enforceable lottery need not satisfy the incentive constraints of a [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md).
