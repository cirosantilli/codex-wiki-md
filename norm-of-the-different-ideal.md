# Norm of the different ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Relative discriminant](relative-discriminant.md)

The [relative discriminant](relative-discriminant.md) of a finite extension of [number fields](number-field.md) is the relative [norm of a fractional ideal](norm-of-a-fractional-ideal.md) applied to its [different ideal](different-ideal.md). Consequently, at $\mathfrak p$,

$$
v_{\mathfrak p}(\mathfrak d_{E/K})=\sum_{\mathfrak P\mid\mathfrak p}f_{\mathfrak P/\mathfrak p}d_{\mathfrak P},
$$

where $f_{\mathfrak P/\mathfrak p}$ is the [residue-field degree](residue-field-degree.md) and $d_{\mathfrak P}$ is the [different exponent](different-exponent.md). Over $\mathbb Q$, $N(\mathfrak D_{E/\mathbb Q})=|D_E|$.
