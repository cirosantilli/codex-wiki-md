# Hadamard basis

↑ **Parent:** [Hadamard transform](hadamard-transform.md)

The qubit Hadamard basis consists of $|+\rangle=(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and $|-\rangle=(|0\rangle-|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$.
