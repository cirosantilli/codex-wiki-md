# Vacuum prescription for inflationary in-in integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Keldysh formalism](keldysh-formalism.md)

The Gaussian [Bunch-Davies vacuum](bunch-davies-vacuum.md) is selected by tilting the early endpoint to $-\infty(1-i\varepsilon)$ for integrands $e^{iK\tau}$ with $K>0$. They then decay at the early boundary. The complex-conjugate integrand requires the opposite tilt. Ordered contractions and their contour must be conjugated together.
