# Rigid-boundary buoyancy condition for quasi-geostrophic waves

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-geostrophic potential-vorticity equation](quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity-equation.md)

With basic zonal velocity $U(z)$, basic horizontal [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) gradient $b_y=-f_0U_z$, and $b'=f_0\psi'_z$, adiabatic buoyancy evolution at a flat impermeable wall gives

$$
(\partial_t+U\partial_x)\psi'_z-U_z\psi'_x=0.
$$

A [normal mode](normal-mode.md) thus obeys $(U-c)\widehat\psi'-U_z\widehat\psi=0$ at the wall. This dynamical condition, rather than vanishing streamfunction, sets the frequency of an [Eady edge wave](eady-edge-wave.md).
