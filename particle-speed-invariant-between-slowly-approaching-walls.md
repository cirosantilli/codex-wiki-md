# Particle-speed invariant between slowly approaching walls

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic invariant](adiabatic-invariant.md)

An [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md) with a wall moving toward the particle at speed $V$ changes the particle speed by approximately $2V$ when recoil is negligible. The collision rate is approximately $v/\ell$ for $v\gg V$, and $\dot\ell=-2V$, yielding $\dot v/v=-\dot\ell/\ell$. For a freely moving finite wall of mass $M$, the neglected recoil changes the increment by order $(m/M)v$, so relative accuracy also requires $(m/M)(v/V)\ll1$.
