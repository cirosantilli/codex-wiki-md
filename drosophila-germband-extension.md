# Drosophila germband extension

↑ **Parent:** [Morphogenesis](morphogenesis.md)

Elongation of the early fly embryonic tissue through processes including polarized junction remodelling and [cell intercalation](cell-intercalation.md). Neighbour changes and individual cell-shape changes make separable contributions to tissue deformation.
