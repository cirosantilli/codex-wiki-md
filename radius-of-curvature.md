# Radius of curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature of a plane curve](curvature-of-a-plane-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radius_of_curvature)

Where a [regular curve](regular-curve.md) has positive [curvature](curvature.md) $\kappa$, its radius of curvature is the [radius](radius.md) $\rho=1/\kappa$ of its [osculating circle](osculating-circle.md). A sharper bend has a smaller radius. At zero [curvature](curvature.md) there is no finite radius; writing $\rho=\infty$ is a limiting convention rather than an ordinary real reciprocal of zero.
