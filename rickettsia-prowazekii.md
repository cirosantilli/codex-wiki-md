# Rickettsia prowazekii

↑ **Parent:** [Bacteria](bacteria.md)

[Rickettsia prowazekii](rickettsia-prowazekii.md) is an intracellular bacterium with extensive biosynthetic losses but retained respiratory and citric-acid-cycle functions. Its cytosolic niche and metabolic inventory differ from those of inclusion-resident [Chlamydia](chlamydia.md).
