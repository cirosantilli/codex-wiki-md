# Sea-ice draft

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)

Sea-ice draft is the depth of the ice underside below the local water surface. Total thickness also includes the part above the water. A [sea-ice pressure ridge](sea-ice-pressure-ridge.md) has a spatially variable draft, and its peak draft differs from its mean along a track.
