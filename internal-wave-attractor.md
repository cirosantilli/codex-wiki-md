# Internal-wave attractor

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave ray tracing](internal-wave-ray-tracing.md)

A periodic spatial [internal-wave ray](internal-wave-ray-tracing.md) toward which neighbouring ray paths converge after reflection in a confined stratified basin. A fixed point of an [internal-wave ray return map](internal-wave-ray-return-map.md) is attracting when its derivative has magnitude below one. [Viscous attenuation of an internal-wave beam](viscous-attenuation-of-an-internal-wave-beam.md) and [internal-wave breaking](internal-wave-breaking.md) regularize the ideal [concentration](concentration.md) of energy. A spatial ray construction does not remove turning-level limitations of the [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md).
