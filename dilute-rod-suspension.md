# Dilute rod suspension

↑ **Parent:** [Suspension rheology](suspension-rheology.md)

In the non-interacting dilute limit, the particle contribution to bulk stress is number density times the orientation average of the [particle stresslet tensor](particle-stresslet-tensor.md). Alignment, tumbling, rotational diffusion and interactions determine the orientational distribution and hence the excess [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md).
