# One-sided interval averaging operator

↑ **Parent:** [Uncentered maximal function of a finite measure](uncentered-maximal-function-of-a-finite-measure.md)

For $r>0$, the forward average is $r^{-1}\int_x^{x+r}f(t)\,dt$ and the backward average is $r^{-1}\int_{x-r}^xf(t)\,dt$, both with positive interval length in the denominator. They are [linear operators](linear-operator.md). Their absolute values are bounded by the [uncentered maximal function](uncentered-maximal-function-of-a-finite-measure.md): enlarge the interval to put $x$ in its interior and pass to the limit. The same boundary-enlargement argument works regardless of the value of the chosen representative at $x$.
