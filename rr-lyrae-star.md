# RR Lyrae star

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)

An [RR Lyrae star](rr-lyrae-star.md) is a short-period pulsating evolved [star](star.md) used as a distance tracer in old populations. Its calibrated [luminosity](luminosity.md) depends on composition and photometric band; infrared period-luminosity-metallicity relations are especially useful. [extinction](extinction.md), crowding, mode identification and [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) remain important.
