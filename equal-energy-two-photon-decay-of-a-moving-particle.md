# Equal-energy two-photon decay of a moving particle

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)

For a parent moving at $\beta c$ and decaying into two [photons](photon.md) of equal laboratory energies, [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) gives [photon](photon.md) directions symmetric about the parent direction with $\cos\theta=\beta$. The opening [angle](angle.md) is $2\arccos\beta$. In the parent [rest frame](rest-frame.md) the [photons](photon.md) are opposite and perpendicular to the boost direction.
