# Nonnegative truncation of a semilinear source

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded semilinear Dirichlet existence theorem](bounded-semilinear-dirichlet-existence-theorem.md)

If $f$ is continuous, bounded and nonnegative on $[0,\infty)$, extend it to a bounded continuous function on the real line by the displayed formula. The [bounded semilinear Dirichlet existence theorem](bounded-semilinear-dirichlet-existence-theorem.md) gives $-\Delta u=F(u)$ with $u\in H_0^1$. Test with the negative part $u_-=\max\{-u,0\}$ to obtain $-\|\nabla u_-\|_2^2=\int F(u)u_-\geq0$. The [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) gives $u_-=0$, so $u\geq0$ and $F(u)=f(u)$. Thus singularities or uncontrolled growth of the original formula at negative arguments need not prevent a nonnegative [weak solution](weak-solution.md).
