# Magnetic buoyancy

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy](buoyancy.md)

[Magnetic buoyancy](magnetic-buoyancy.md) is the tendency of magnetized fluid to rise when [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) support reduces its density relative to its surroundings. For a thin flux concentration in lateral balance, $p_{\rm in}+B^2/(2\mu_0)=p_{\rm out}$. At equal [temperature](temperature.md) and composition an ideal-gas equation of state then gives $\rho_{\rm in}<\rho_{\rm out}$, producing an upward buoyant force. Thermal exchange, [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) and stable stratification modify this simple argument. In a [solar dynamo](solar-dynamo.md), buoyant toroidal flux can emerge as tilted bipolar active regions.
