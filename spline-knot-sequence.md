# Spline knot sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Spline knot](spline-knot.md)

A spline knot sequence is a nondecreasing list of [spline knots](spline-knot.md), including their multiplicities, that specifies the junctions and smoothness constraints of a spline space. An order-$k$ [B-spline](b-spline.md) uses $k+1$ consecutive knot entries. Its support must have positive width, and repeated knots are interpreted by confluent [divided differences](divided-difference.md) or their limiting convention.
