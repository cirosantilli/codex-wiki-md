# Weak and norm Borel sigma-algebras in a separable Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Weak topology](weak-topology-split.md)

The [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md) and [norm topology](norm-topology.md) of a [separable Banach space](separable-banach-space.md) generate the same [Borel sigma-algebra](borel-sigma-algebra.md). A [countable norming family](countable-norming-family.md) makes every norm ball weakly Borel measurable, and separability makes every norm-open set a countable union of such balls. The reverse inclusion follows because the [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md) is coarser.
