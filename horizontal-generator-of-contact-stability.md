# Horizontal generator of contact stability

↑ **Parent:** [Gray stability theorem](gray-stability-theorem.md)

For a family of [contact forms](contact-form.md), require $Y_t\in\ker\alpha_t$ and solve $(\iota_{Y_t}d\alpha_t)|_{\ker\alpha_t}=-\dot\alpha_t|_{\ker\alpha_t}$. The [contact distribution symplectic form](contact-distribution-symplectic-form.md) gives a unique smooth solution. Then $\dot\alpha_t+\mathcal L_{Y_t}\alpha_t=h_t\alpha_t$, with $h_t=\dot\alpha_t(R_t)$ and $R_t$ the [Reeb vector field](reeb-vector-field.md).
