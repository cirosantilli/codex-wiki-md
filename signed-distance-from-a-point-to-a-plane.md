# Signed distance from a point to a plane

↑ **Parent:** [Distance from a point to a plane](distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md)

Orient the [plane](plane.md) $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf r=d$ with a [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $\mathbf n$. The signed distance of a point $\mathbf p$ from the plane is

$$
s=\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf p-d.
$$

Its absolute value is the ordinary [distance from a point to a plane](distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md). The closest point in the plane is $\mathbf p-s\mathbf n$, so the displacement from the point to its projection is $-s\mathbf n$. Reversing the normal and $d$ reverses $s$ without changing the geometric plane or the unsigned distance. A sphere of radius $q$ centred at $\mathbf p$ has [sphere-plane intersection](sphere-plane-intersection.md) radius $\sqrt{q^2-s^2}$ when $|s|<q$.
