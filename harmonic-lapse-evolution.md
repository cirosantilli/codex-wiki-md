# Harmonic lapse evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic slicing](harmonic-slicing.md)

For a positive [lapse function](lapse-function.md) $\alpha$, [shift vector](shift-vector.md) $\beta^i$, and the convention $K_{ij}=-(\partial_t\gamma_{ij}-\mathcal L_\beta\gamma_{ij})/(2\alpha)$ for [extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface](extrinsic-curvature-of-a-spatial-hypersurface.md), the time-coordinate [harmonic slicing](harmonic-slicing.md) condition yields the displayed advective lapse equation. Combining $\partial_t(\sqrt\gamma/\alpha)=\partial_i(\sqrt\gamma\beta^i/\alpha)$ with the [spatial volume evolution identity](spatial-volume-evolution-identity.md) proves it without setting the [shift vector](shift-vector.md) to zero.
