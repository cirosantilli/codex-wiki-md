# Electric drift in crossed uniform fields

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

In uniform perpendicular electric and [magnetic fields](magnetic-field.md), the guiding centre of a charged particle drifts with $\mathbf v_D=\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/|\mathbf B|^2$. This [velocity](velocity.md) is independent of the charge and mass; cyclotron motion is superposed on it.
