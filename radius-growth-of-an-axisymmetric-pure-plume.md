# Radius growth of an axisymmetric pure plume

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric pure plume](axisymmetric-pure-plume.md)

For pi-normalized top-hat fluxes, $b=Q/\sqrt M$. The pure [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) therefore has $b=(6\alpha/5)(z+z_0)$, so the radius grows linearly with slope $6\alpha/5$. The corresponding [plume virtual origin](plume-virtual-origin.md) is $-z_0$ and a finite source has $z_0=5b_s/(6\alpha)$. This law translates geometric plume-contact criteria into heights.
