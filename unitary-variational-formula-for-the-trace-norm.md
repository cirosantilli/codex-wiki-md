# Unitary variational formula for the trace norm

↑ **Parent:** [Trace norm](trace-norm.md)

For a finite square matrix $X$, maximize over [unitary operators](unitary-operator.md). Write its [singular value decomposition](singular-value-decomposition.md) as $X=PDQ^\dagger$. Then $|\operatorname{Tr}(XU)|=|\operatorname{Tr}(DQ^\dagger UP)|\leq\sum_jD_{jj}$, since every diagonal entry of a unitary has modulus at most one. Choosing $U=QP^\dagger$ attains equality, proving the formula for the [trace norm](trace-norm.md).
