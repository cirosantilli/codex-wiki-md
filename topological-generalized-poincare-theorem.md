# Topological generalized Poincare theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy sphere](homotopy-sphere.md)

Every closed [topological manifold](topological-manifold.md) with the [homotopy type](homotopy-type.md) of $S^d$ is homeomorphic to $S^d$. This is a classification theorem about [homeomorphisms](homeomorphism.md), and does not assert a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) between prescribed smooth structures. It is the precise final step needed after proving that a cone link is a [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [integral homology sphere](homology-sphere.md) in the [manifold criterion for a suspension](manifold-criterion-for-a-suspension.md).
