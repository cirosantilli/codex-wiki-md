# MSc in psychological research course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

1 year full time. Historical taught programme. The department explicitly states that it will not accept applications for entry after 2024. This is distinct from the MSc by Research in Experimental Psychology, which is excluded.

Official course page: [https://www.psy.ox.ac.uk/study/graduate/msc-in-psychological-research](https://www.psy.ox.ac.uk/study/graduate/msc-in-psychological-research)
