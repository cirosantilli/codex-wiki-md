# Hyperbolic translation length

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md)

For a [hyperbolic Möbius transformation](hyperbolic-element-of-psl2-r.md) represented by $A\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$, the infimum of $d(z,Az)$ is attained on its axis and equals $2\operatorname{arcosh}(|\operatorname{tr}A|/2)$. It is the length of the corresponding closed [geodesic](geodesic.md) in a quotient.
