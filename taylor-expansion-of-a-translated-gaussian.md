# Taylor expansion of a translated Gaussian

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md)

Multiply the two [exponential series](exponential-series.md) in $e^{-(x-a)^2}=e^{-a^2}e^{2ax}e^{-x^2}$ to obtain the displayed [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md). Its first three nonzero terms depend on the real parameter $a$. For $a\ne0,\pm1/\sqrt2$, they end at degree two. At $a=0$, odd coefficients vanish and the first three terms are $1-x^2+x^4/2$. At $a=\pm1/\sqrt2$, the quadratic coefficient vanishes, leaving $e^{-1/2}(1+2ax-4ax^3/3)$ as the first three nonzero terms. Parameter-dependent vanishing coefficients must be checked when a question counts nonzero terms rather than prescribing an expansion order.
