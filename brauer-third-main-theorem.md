# Brauer third main theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer correspondence](brauer-correspondence.md)

If a [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) $b$ of $kH$ has [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) $D$ and $DC_G(D)\le H\le G$, then its [block induction](block-induction.md) $b^G$ is the [principal block](principal-block.md) if and only if $b$ is the [principal block](principal-block.md). Equivalently, the [Brauer morphism](brauer-morphism.md) of the principal block idempotent at any [p-subgroup](p-subgroup.md) $Q$ is the principal block idempotent of $kC_G(Q)$.
