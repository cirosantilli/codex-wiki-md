# Vinylmagnesium halide

↑ **Parent:** [Grignard reagent](grignard-reagent.md)

A [grignard reagent](grignard-reagent.md) in which [magnesium](magnesium.md) is attached directly to an alkenyl [carbon](carbon.md), $\mathrm{RCH{=}CHMgX}$. Addition to a [carbonyl compound](carbonyl-compound.md) can form an allylic [alcohol](alcohol.md) while retaining the geometry of the pre-existing [alkene](alkene.md).
