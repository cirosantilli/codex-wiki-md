# Fay shock-layer asymptotics

↑ **Parent:** [Fay solution](fay-solution.md)

For $|\theta|\ll1$ and $s=\delta Z\ll1$, the [Fay solution](fay-solution.md) becomes $q\sim4\delta\theta\sum_{j\ge0}[((2j+1)s)^2+\theta^2]^{-1}$. The partial-fraction identity for the [hyperbolic tangent](hyperbolic-tangent.md) sums this to the displayed smooth shock layer. It resolves the odd transition from $-\pi/Z$ to $+\pi/Z$ on angular thickness $O(\delta Z)$; at $\theta=0$ both sides vanish and the comparison is read through their derivatives.
