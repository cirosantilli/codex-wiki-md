# Guanosine

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanosine)

A [nucleoside](nucleoside.md) comprising [guanine](guanine.md) attached to [ribose](ribose.md). Its free $3'$ [hydroxyl group](hydroxy-group.md) initiates [group I intron](group-i-catalytic-intron.md) [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md); [guanosine](guanosine.md) is incorporated into the excised [intron](intron.md), rather than acting as a regenerated [catalyst](catalyst.md).
