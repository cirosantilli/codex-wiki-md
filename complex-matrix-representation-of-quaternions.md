# Complex matrix representation of quaternions

↑ **Parent:** [Quaternion](quaternion.md)

The displayed complex [matrices](matrix.md), with real coefficients, form a four-dimensional real [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md). Their three imaginary [basis](basis.md) [matrices](matrix.md) square to minus the identity and satisfy $JK=L$, $KL=J$, $LJ=K$, with the reversed products negative. For $\alpha=aI+bJ+cK+dL$, quaternionic conjugation gives $\alpha\overline\alpha=(a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2)I$. Every nonzero [matrix](matrix.md) therefore has inverse $\overline\alpha/(a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2)$ inside this algebra. This realizes the real [division algebra](division-algebra.md) of [quaternions](quaternion.md) within $M_2(\mathbb C)$; it is not a complex [vector](vector.md) subspace.
