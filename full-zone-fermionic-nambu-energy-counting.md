# Full-zone fermionic Nambu energy counting

↑ **Parent:** [Ising-chain Nambu spinor](ising-chain-nambu-spinor.md)

If the traceless matrix $h_k$ has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\pm d_k$ and the [Nambu spinor](nambu-spinor.md) includes an opposite-momentum [creation operator](creation-operator.md), a full-zone sum contains both partners. Its physical [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) energy is $2Jd_k$, not $Jd_k$. The negative term becomes a vacuum constant only after [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md). In the [Ising-chain Nambu spinor](ising-chain-nambu-spinor.md), $d_k=\sqrt{1+g^2-2g\cos k}$. A half-zone calculation instead groups one complete two-mode block; changing the momentum range without changing the normalization is inconsistent.
