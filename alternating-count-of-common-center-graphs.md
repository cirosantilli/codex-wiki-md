# Alternating count of common-center graphs

↑ **Parent:** [Common-center graph property](common-center-graph-property.md)

For $n\geq3$, count the empty [graph](graph-split.md) once, all one-edge [graphs](graph-split.md) once, and each larger accepted [graph](graph-split.md) under its unique center. The latter weighted contribution is $n(n-2)$, giving $1-\binom n2+n(n-2)$. Its nonzero value proves evasiveness by the [alternating-sum criterion for decision-tree evasiveness](alternating-sum-criterion-for-decision-tree-evasiveness.md).
