# Strong Hill autorepression response

↑ **Parent:** [Negative autoregulation](negative-autoregulation.md)

Neglecting the one in a strongly repressed [Hill equation](hill-equation.md) gives $\dot Y=\beta K^nY^{-n}-\alpha Y$. The substitution $z=Y^{n+1}$ makes this a linear [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md). It yields $Y_{\rm st}=(\beta K^n/\alpha)^{1/(n+1)}$ and half-response time $-\log(1-2^{-(n+1)})/[(n+1)\alpha]$. The singular initial slope is an artifact of the approximation, whose range is not uniform at arbitrarily large $n$ and fixed production/degradation ratio.
