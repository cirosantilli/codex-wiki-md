# Skew-adjoint difference criterion for metric connections

↑ **Parent:** [Metric connection](metric-connection.md)

Let $\nabla$ be a [metric connection](metric-connection.md) for a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) $g$, and let $\nabla'$ be another [affine connection](affine-connection.md) on the same [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md). The [difference of affine connections is a tensor](difference-of-affine-connections-is-a-tensor.md) $A=\nabla-\nabla'$. Expanding the derivative of the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) gives

$$
(\nabla'_Xg)(Y,Z)=g(A(X,Y),Z)+g(Y,A(X,Z)).
$$

Thus $\nabla'$ is a [metric connection](metric-connection.md) precisely when each endomorphism $A(X,\cdot)$ is skew-adjoint. This imposes no antisymmetry in $X,Y$; that separate condition characterizes agreement of parametrized [geodesics](geodesic.md). If both conditions hold, $g(A(X,Y),Z)$ is alternating in all three arguments.
