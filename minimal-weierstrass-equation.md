# Minimal Weierstrass equation

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Minimality here concerns the discriminant valuation of an integral [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) over a discretely valued field.

Over a discretely valued field, a minimal Weierstrass equation has integral coefficients and has least possible discriminant valuation among integral Weierstrass equations related by admissible changes of variables.
