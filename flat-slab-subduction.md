# Flat-slab subduction

↑ **Parent:** [Slab dip](slab-dip.md)

A regime in which an initially descending [slab](slab.md) becomes nearly horizontal beneath the [overriding plate](overriding-plate.md) for a substantial inland distance before descending again. It can narrow or eliminate a hot [mantle wedge](mantle-wedge.md), shift deformation inland and coincide with a volcanic gap. Incoming buoyant crust, hydration, upper-plate motion and mantle forces can all contribute; association with an oceanic ridge does not isolate one cause.
