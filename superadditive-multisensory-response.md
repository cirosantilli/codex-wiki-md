# Superadditive multisensory response

↑ **Parent:** [Multisensory response enhancement](multisensory-response-enhancement.md)

A combined response exceeding the sum of its separately measured component responses under an explicit baseline convention. Consistent subtraction of spontaneous activity prevents counting background twice. Enhancement above the strongest component need not be superadditive.
