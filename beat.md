# Beat

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)

The slow change in the resultant [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) when two nearby [frequencies](frequency.md) interfere. Two steady sinusoidal components at $f_1$ and $f_2$ produce successive amplitude maxima separated by $1/|f_1-f_2|$.
