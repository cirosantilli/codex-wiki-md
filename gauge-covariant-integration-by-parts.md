# Gauge-covariant integration by parts

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint covariant derivative](adjoint-covariant-derivative.md)

For Lie-algebra-valued fields and a constant [invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra](invariant-bilinear-form-on-a-lie-algebra.md), the connection terms in the displayed identity cancel. Indeed, invariance gives $\kappa([A,Y],Z)+\kappa(Y,[A,Z])=0$. Ordinary [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) then yields $\int\kappa(D_\mu Y,Z)=-\int\kappa(Y,D_\mu Z)$ when boundary terms vanish, for example for compactly supported variations. This preserves covariance in variational calculations of [Yang-Mills equations](yang-mills-equations.md).
