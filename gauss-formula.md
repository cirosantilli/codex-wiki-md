# Gauss formula

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss–Codazzi equations](gauss-codazzi-equations.md)

For a submanifold with induced [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) $\nabla$ and [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) $A$, the ambient derivative decomposes as

$$
\overline\nabla_XY=\nabla_XY+A(X,Y).
$$

For a parametrized surface this reads $X_{ij}=\Gamma^k_{ij}X_k+h_{ij}N$.
