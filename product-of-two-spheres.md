# Product of two spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

The [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) $S^p\times S^q$ has one cell in each of dimensions $0,p,q,p+q$, counting the two middle cells separately when $p=q$. For positive $p,q$, its integral [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md) has generators $a,b$ of degrees $p,q$, with $a^2=b^2=0$ and $ba=(-1)^{pq}ab$. The class $ab$ is a product orientation class. This follows from the [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md).
