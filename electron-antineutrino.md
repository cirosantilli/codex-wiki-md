# Electron antineutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Antineutrino](antineutrino.md)

The [antineutrino](antineutrino.md) associated with the [electron](electron.md) flavour of the [weak charged current](charged-current.md). It has zero [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and [lepton number](lepton-number.md) $-1$ in the usual production-and-detection description.
