# Gravitational force

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity](gravity.md)

The [force](force-split.md) exerted through [gravity](gravity.md). In a Newtonian [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) $\Phi$ per unit [mass](mass.md), a [point mass](point-mass.md) $m$ experiences the displayed [force](force-split.md), while a continuous [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ experiences force density $-\rho\nabla\Phi$.
