# Unit fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Fraction](fraction.md)

A [fraction](fraction.md) of the form $1/n$ for a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$. Its [numerator](numerator.md) is 1 and its [denominator](denominator.md) determines it uniquely.
