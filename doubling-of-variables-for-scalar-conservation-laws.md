# Doubling of variables for scalar conservation laws

↑ **Parent:** [Kruzhkov entropy flux](kruzhkov-entropy-flux.md)

Apply the [entropy solution](entropy-solution.md) inequality for $u(t,x)$ with level $v(s,y)$ and for $v(s,y)$ with level $u(t,x)$, integrate both in the other pair of variables, and add them. Symmetry of the [Kruzhkov entropy flux](kruzhkov-entropy-flux.md) produces [derivatives](derivative.md) $\partial_t+\partial_s$ and $\partial_x+\partial_y$. A nonnegative mollifier concentrated near the diagonal then yields the [Kato inequality for scalar conservation laws](kato-inequality-for-scalar-conservation-laws.md). Boundary-time terms require an initial trace argument, not merely interior translation continuity.
