# Softplus

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Softplus)

The softplus function $s(x)=\log(1+e^x)$ is convex because

$$
s''(x)=\frac{e^x}{(1+e^x)^2}>0.
$$
