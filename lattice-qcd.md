# Lattice QCD

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lattice_QCD)

Lattice QCD is a [lattice gauge theory](lattice-gauge-theory.md) formulation of [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md). A [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md) on a discrete spacetime lattice provides a route to computing [hadron](hadron.md) observables numerically, particularly when weak-coupling expansions are inadequate. Physical predictions require control of lattice spacing, finite spatial volume and quark-mass effects.
