# Polynomial substitution in a size-two Jordan block

↑ **Parent:** [Jordan block](jordan-block.md)

For $T=\lambda I+N$ with $N^2=0\ne N$ and a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $p$, the binomial formula gives $p(T)=p(\lambda)I+p'(\lambda)N$. Hence its [module](module-mathematics.md) on the two-dimensional space is indecomposable exactly when $p'(\lambda)\ne0$: in that case the nonzero nilpotent part is one [Jordan block](jordan-block.md); otherwise the operator is scalar and every line is a [submodule](submodule.md). For $p(X)=X^2$ over a [field](field.md) of characteristic different from two, the criterion is $\lambda\ne0$.
