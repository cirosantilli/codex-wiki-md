# Pair of pants (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Surface with boundary](surface-with-boundary.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pair_of_pants_(mathematics))

The pair of pants is the compact genus-zero [surface with boundary](surface-with-boundary.md) having three boundary components. It is obtained from the sphere by deleting the interiors of three disjoint discs and is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of two circles.
