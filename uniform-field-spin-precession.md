# Uniform-field spin precession

↑ **Parent:** [Spin operator](spin-operator.md)

For a spin-half particle with $H=\alpha\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf S$ and $\mathbf S=\hbar\boldsymbol\sigma/2$, exponentiating the [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) gives a rotation about the field axis. A field along $y$ evolves initial $z$-up [spin](spin.md) to $(\cos(\alpha Bt/2),\sin(\alpha Bt/2))^T$. The sign of $\alpha$ sets the precession direction. [spin](spin.md) parallel to the field is conserved even while transverse components vary.
