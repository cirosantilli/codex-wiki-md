# Acrolein

↑ **Parent:** [Aldehyde](aldehyde.md)

The conjugated [aldehyde](aldehyde.md) $\mathrm{CH_2{=}CHCHO}$. A [carbon](carbon.md) [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) can add at its [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](carbon.md) or, in a [conjugate addition](conjugate-addition.md), at the terminal beta [carbon](carbon.md). Atom mapping distinguishes the two [product](product-chemistry.md)s.
