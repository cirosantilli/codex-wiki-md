# Thermal eigenvalues of the spherical nonlinear sigma model

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop stiffness of a spherical nonlinear sigma model](one-loop-stiffness-of-a-spherical-nonlinear-sigma-model.md)

With $A_D=S_D\Lambda^\epsilon/(2\pi)^D$, the one-loop [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md) is $\beta_g=-\epsilon g+(n-2)A_Dg^2$. For $n>2$ and $\epsilon>0$, its two nonnegative [renormalization-group fixed points](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md) are zero and $g_*=\epsilon/[(n-2)A_D]$. Differentiating the beta function gives the displayed thermal [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), so the nonzero point has [correlation-length critical exponent](correlation-length-critical-exponent.md) $\nu=1/\epsilon$ to leading order. At $\epsilon=0$ the zero eigenvalue requires nonlinear analysis: $g$ is marginally relevant for $n>2$, but the $n=2$ perturbative spin-wave theory has a line of fixed points and omits vortices.
