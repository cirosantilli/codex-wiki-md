# Ramanujan sum

↑ **Parent:** [Root of unity](root-of-unity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan_sum)

The Ramanujan sum is $c_n(k)=\sum_{1\leq j\leq n,\,(j,n)=1}e^{2\pi i jk/n}$. It is an integer: insert $\mathbf1_{(j,n)=1}=\sum_{d\mid(j,n)}\mu(d)$, sum the geometric series, and obtain $c_n(k)=\sum_{r\mid(n,k)}r\mu(n/r)$. Here $\mu$ is the [Möbius function](mobius-function.md). Such sums connect [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) with integer [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) values.
