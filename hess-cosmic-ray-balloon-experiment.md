# Hess cosmic-ray balloon experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md)

[Victor Franz Hess](victor-franz-hess.md)'s 1912 series used two sealed, 3-mm-walled [ionization chambers](ionization-chamber.md) with [Theodor Wulf](theodor-wulf.md)'s fiber [electrometers](electrometer.md). [Karl Bergwitz](karl-bergwitz.md) suggested internal plating; Günther & Tegetmeyer improved focusing. Basket-mounted ground comparisons, insulation-loss measurements, simultaneous detectors and barograph-derived heights supplied checks. The highest flight reached 5350 m; its comparisons came from Vienna and after landing, without a launch-site baseline.[1912, pp.1084-1085 apparatus and preparations; pp.1087-1088 seventh flight; English primary-text translation [https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.02927](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.02927) pp.1-2, 5-6](uber-beobachtungen-der-durchdringenden-strahlung-bei-sieben-freiballonfahrten-by-victor-franz-hess.md)

A third, thin-walled chamber admitted ambient air. Hess multiplied its readings by $750/b$, with [pressure](pressure-split.md) $b$ in millimeters of mercury, warning that vessel-wall contributions might violate this scaling. He recorded outside [temperature](temperature-split.md) but applied no absolute-temperature correction. Night flights, an eclipse and weather observations constrained alternatives to radiation from above; the eclipse argument addressed directly propagating solar radiation.[1912, p.1085 pressure reduction; p.1086 eclipse; p.1088 temperature footnote; pp.1090-1091 inference; English translation pp.2-3, 6-8](uber-beobachtungen-der-durchdringenden-strahlung-bei-sieben-freiballonfahrten-by-victor-franz-hess.md)

The [electrometers](electrometer.md) read gradual [electric potential](electric-potential.md) loss caused by continuing air [ionization](ionization.md). The low-altitude dip and strong high-altitude rise favored an extraterrestrial contribution, without measuring [proton](proton.md) composition.[1912, pp.1089-1091 altitude averages and interpretation; English translation pp.7-8](uber-beobachtungen-der-durchdringenden-strahlung-bei-sieben-freiballonfahrten-by-victor-franz-hess.md) An [ionization chamber](ionization-chamber.md) integrates [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md): one energetic particle may create many [ion pairs](ion-pair.md), several particles may contribute to one reading, and a [photon](photon.md) can create [charged particles](charged-particle.md) in the gas or walls. A voltage-loss reading is therefore neither a count of [primary cosmic rays](primary-cosmic-ray.md) nor a chemical analysis of them.

[Domenico Pacini](domenico-pacini.md) supplied independent evidence: in June 1911 he compared a copper-box-enclosed [electroscope](electroscope.md) at the sea surface and 3 m underwater, more than 300 m offshore above an 8-m-deep seabed. Reduced underwater [ionization](ionization.md), stable electrical loss between runs, and a lake comparison supported an external contribution independent of soil [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md). Water provided shielding; this established a different constraint from Hess's altitude comparison.[Pacini's 1912 report, English translation pp.3-5, especially surface/underwater comparison and residual-ionization discussion; original Italian facsimile pp.9-14](la-radiazione-penetrante-alla-superficie-ed-in-seno-alle-acque-by-domenico-pacini.md)
