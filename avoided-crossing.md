# Avoided crossing

↑ **Parent:** [Band gap](band-gap.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avoided_crossing)

An avoided crossing occurs when two parameter-dependent [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) approach one another but an interaction prevents equality. Near a two-level crossing, a nonzero off-diagonal [matrix element](matrix-element.md) opens a positive minimum gap.
