# Polytropic constant

↑ **Parent:** [Polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md)

The coefficient $K$ in a [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md) $p=K\rho^\Gamma$. It is spatially constant for one simple [polytrope](polytrope.md), but need not stay constant between different equilibrium models. For a fixed-composition [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) with $\Gamma$ equal to its [adiabatic index](heat-capacity-ratio.md), $K$ parametrizes [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md): $s=c_v\ln K+\text{constant}$ with a suitable dimensional reference inside the logarithm.
