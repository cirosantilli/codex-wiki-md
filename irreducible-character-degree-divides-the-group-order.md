# Irreducible character degree divides the group order

↑ **Parent:** [Central character value of a conjugacy-class sum](central-character-value-of-a-conjugacy-class-sum.md)

For every irreducible complex character $\chi$ of a finite group $G$,

$$
\chi(1)\mid|G|.
$$

Indeed, row orthogonality expresses $|G|/\chi(1)$ as a sum of products of [algebraic integers](algebraic-integer.md):

$$
\frac{|G|}{\chi(1)}
=\sum_C\frac{|C|\chi(c)}{\chi(1)}
\overline{\chi(c)}.
$$

It is a rational algebraic integer and therefore an integer.
