# Scalar-pair production by fermion annihilation

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar electrodynamics](scalar-electrodynamics.md)

For unpolarized ultrarelativistic charged spin-half particles annihilating through a photon into minimally coupled unit-charge scalars of mass $m$, set $\beta=\sqrt{1-4m^2/s}$. The scalar vertex contains the difference $k_--k_+$ of outgoing four-momenta. Contraction with the spin-averaged initial fermion current gives $\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=e^4\beta^2\sin^2\theta/2$. The two-body final-state flux factor contributes another $\beta$, giving

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}=\frac{\alpha^2\beta^3}{8s}\sin^2\theta,\qquad
\sigma=\frac{\pi\alpha^2\beta^3}{3s}.
$$

The $\sin^2\theta$ angular dependence and threshold suppression contrast with [electron-positron annihilation into a muon pair](electron-positron-annihilation-into-a-muon-pair.md). These predictions assume the specified scalar electrodynamic coupling, rather than an arbitrary spin-zero interaction.
