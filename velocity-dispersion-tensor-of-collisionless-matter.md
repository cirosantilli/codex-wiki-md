# Velocity-dispersion tensor of collisionless matter

↑ **Parent:** [Collisionless dark-matter Vlasov equation](collisionless-dark-matter-vlasov-equation.md)

The velocity-dispersion tensor is the second central moment of a phase-space distribution,

$$
\sigma^{ij}=\langle u^iu^j\rangle-v^iv^j.
$$

It contributes the stress-divergence term $\rho^{-1}\partial_j(\rho\sigma^{ij})$ to the Euler equation and vanishes in the ideal single-stream limit.
