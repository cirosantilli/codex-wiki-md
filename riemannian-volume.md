# Riemannian volume

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md)

The total Riemannian volume is the integral of the [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md), or its density on a nonorientable manifold. In local coordinates the density is $\sqrt{\det g}\,dx^1\cdots dx^d$. It is preserved by [Riemannian isometries](riemannian-isometry.md). On a closed manifold it is the leading coefficient among the integrated [heat invariants](heat-invariants.md), and on a surface it is the [surface area](surface-area.md).
