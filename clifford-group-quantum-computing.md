# Clifford group (quantum computing)

↑ **Parent:** [Clifford gate](clifford-gate.md)

The [group](group-split.md) of unitary [Clifford gates](clifford-gate.md), defined by normalization of the [Pauli group](pauli-group.md). For a single qubit, $H$ and $S$ generate its usual finite gate group, and modulo [global phase](global-phase.md) its action permutes the six [Pauli eigenstates](pauli-eigenstate.md). On multiple qubits, one adds an entangling [controlled-NOT gate](controlled-not-gate.md). This quantum-computing use of the name is distinct from groups attached to a general [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md).
