<h1 id="gateaux-derivative">Gâteaux derivative</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Directional derivative](directional-derivative.md)

A [functional](functional.md) on a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) is [Gâteaux differentiable](gateaux-derivative.md) at $v$ when the directional [limits](limit-of-a-function.md) exist and form a [bounded linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) of $h$. Fréchet [differentiability](differentiability.md) is stronger, requiring a remainder uniformly small compared with the [norm](norm.md) of the increment. A quadratic [norm](norm.md) bound on the [Taylor remainder](taylor-remainder.md) gives the stronger property directly. Mere existence of unrelated directional [limits](limit-of-a-function.md) would not establish linear [differentiability](differentiability.md).
