# Pressure-coordinate thermal wind

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal wind](thermal-wind.md)

Combining [geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md), $fu=-\Phi_y$, $fv=\Phi_x$, with [hydrostatic balance](hydrostatic-balance.md), $\Phi_p=-RT/p$, and commuting the horizontal and vertical derivatives proves the displayed equations. Horizontal derivatives hold [pressure](pressure-split.md) fixed. This relates [vertical shear](vertical-shear.md) to horizontal [temperature gradients](temperature-gradient.md) away from the equator; the derivation requires nonzero [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md) and the validity of [geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md).
