# Semiclassical charged-kink spectrum with a theta angle

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating charged kink in a spherical sigma model](rotating-charged-kink-in-a-spherical-sigma-model.md)

In units $\hbar=1$, periodic-angle quantization gives canonical charge $Q_\theta=\ell\in\mathbb Z$. The mechanical charge is $Q_0=\ell+\theta T/(2\pi)$ and the rotating-kink [mass](mass.md) gives the displayed leading semiclassical spectrum. The $T=1$ and $T=-1$ families are related by reversing the [kink](scalar-field-kink.md). Theta periodicity relabels the integer; fluctuation corrections are not excluded by this leading calculation.
