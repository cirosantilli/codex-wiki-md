# Viscous termination of a stretched string

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic impedance of a stretched string](characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md)

A semi-infinite [wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md) with [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\rho$, [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $c$, and [tension](tension.md) $T=\rho c^2$ has positive characteristic [mechanical impedance](mechanical-impedance.md) $Z=\rho c$. At an end attached to a massless viscous damper of coefficient $\alpha>0$, displacement and transverse-force balance give the displacement [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md)

$$
r=\frac{Z-\alpha}{Z+\alpha},\qquad
\mathcal R=|r|^2,\qquad \mathcal A=\frac{4\alpha Z}{(\alpha+Z)^2}.
$$

Here $\mathcal A$ is the fraction of mean incident [power](power.md) dissipated. A prescribed $0<\mathcal R<1$ gives two positive damper coefficients, $\alpha/Z=(1\pm\sqrt{\mathcal R})/(1\mp\sqrt{\mathcal R})$, with opposite reflected [phases](phase-waves.md). Matching $\alpha=Z$ removes reflection.
