# Access to mental health care

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical psychology](clinical-psychology.md)

The practical possibility of recognising need, seeking and receiving appropriate support, and continuing to effective treatment. Referral counts, treatment starts, treatment completion and clinical benefit are different stages. Equity assessment must include eligible people who never enter or discontinue care.[European Child and Adolescent Psychiatry 27, 1023–1031; interviews with parents of sixteen school-identified children aged 7–11](barriers-and-facilitators-to-parents-seeking-and-accessing-professional-support-for-anxiety-disorders-in-children-qualitative-interview-study-by-tessa-reardon-and-others.md)
