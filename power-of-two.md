# Power of two

↑ **Parent:** [Positive integer](positive-integer.md)

In this [positive integer](positive-integer.md) context, a [power of two](power-of-two.md) is $2^k$ for a [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) $k$, giving $1,2,4,8,\ldots$. Allowing negative or real [exponents](exponent.md) gives a broader meaning of [exponentiation](exponentiation.md), which is not intended in the integer divisibility criteria here.
