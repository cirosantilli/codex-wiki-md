<h1 id="a/2/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The nonzero cubes modulo thirteen are $\{1,5,8,12\}$, which excludes three. Thus this cubic has no root in its base [field](../../../../../../field.md) and is irreducible. For a root $\alpha$, the extension $\mathbb F_{13}(\alpha)$ has degree three. The base [field](../../../../../../field.md) already contains the primitive cube [root of unity](../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) $3$, since $3^3=1$ and $3\ne1$. Hence the other two roots are $3\alpha$ and $9\alpha$, already in this extension, making it the full [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md).

The [Frobenius endomorphism](../../../../../../frobenius-endomorphism.md) satisfies $\alpha^{13}=\alpha(\alpha^3)^4=3\alpha$, so it cycles all three roots. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[L:\mathbb F_{13}]=3,\qquad\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb F_{13})\cong C_3.}
$$
