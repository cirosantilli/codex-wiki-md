<h1 id="a/2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Factor $f_1=(X^2-3)(X^2+2)$. Its [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $L=\mathbb Q(\sqrt3,\sqrt{-2})$. The first [quadratic field](../../../../../../quadratic-field.md) is real, so it cannot contain the nonreal $\sqrt{-2}$. Thus adjoining the second square root doubles the degree again. Independent sign changes of the two square roots give all four [automorphisms](../../../../../../automorphism.md), so

$$
\boxed{[L:\mathbb Q]=4,\qquad\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q)\cong C_2\times C_2.}
$$
