<h1 id="a/2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Eisenstein's criterion](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at two makes $X^5-4X+2$ irreducible: all nonleading coefficients are divisible by two and the constant is not divisible by four. Its [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) is therefore transitive on five roots, and its order is divisible by five, so [Cauchy's theorem for finite groups](../../../../../../cauchy-theorem-for-groups.md) supplies a five-cycle.

The [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is $5X^4-4$, with real critical points $\pm c$, $c=(4/5)^{1/4}$. The values are $f(-c)=2+16c/5>0$ and $f(c)=2-16c/5<0$. The [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is positive outside these two points and negative between them, so there are exactly three real roots and one nonreal conjugate pair. [Complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) therefore acts as a single [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md).

A five-cycle together with any [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) generates $S_5$: conjugating that [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) by powers of the cycle gives edges joining labels with a fixed nonzero difference modulo five. These edges form a connected graph, and [transpositions on a connected graph generate the symmetric group](../../../../../../transpositions-on-a-connected-graph-generate-the-symmetric-group.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q)=S_5,\qquad[L:\mathbb Q]=120.}
$$

The equality of degree and [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) order uses separability in [characteristic](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) zero.
