↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [finite group](../../../../../finite-group.md) is solvable if its [derived series](../../../../../derived-series.md), defined by $G^{(0)}=G$ and $G^{(j+1)}=[G^{(j)},G^{(j)}]$, reaches the identity after finitely many steps. Equivalently it has a [subnormal series](../../../../../subnormal-series.md) with abelian factor [groups](../../../../../group-split.md). The factors need not all have prime order for this definition.
