<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $V_4=\{1,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}$. This is an [abelian subgroup](../../../../../abelian-subgroup.md), and conjugation preserves its nonidentity cycle type, so it is normal in $S_4$ and hence in $A_4$. The series

$$
1\trianglelefteq V_4\trianglelefteq A_4\trianglelefteq S_4
$$

has factors $V_4\cong C_2\times C_2$, $A_4/V_4\cong C_3$, and $S_4/A_4\cong C_2$, all abelian. Thus $\boxed{S_4\text{ is solvable}}$.
