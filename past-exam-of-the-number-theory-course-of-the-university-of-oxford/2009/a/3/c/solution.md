<h1 id="a/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The rotations form a [cyclic subgroup](../../../../../cyclic-subgroup.md) $R\cong C_n$ of index two, so it is normal. The quotient $D_{2n}/R$ has order two and is cyclic. Hence $1\trianglelefteq R\trianglelefteq D_{2n}$ has abelian factors, proving $\boxed{D_{2n}\text{ is solvable}}$. Its [commutator subgroup](../../../../../commutator-subgroup.md) is contained in the abelian rotation [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md), so its second derived [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) is already trivial.
