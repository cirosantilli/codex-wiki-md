↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Set $Y=X+1$. The [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) becomes $Y^4+Y+1$, and translating all roots leaves their pairwise differences unchanged. Part (b) gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta_f=256-27=229.}
$$

It is nonzero and lies strictly between $15^2=225$ and $16^2=256$. An [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) which is a rational square is an [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) square, so this is not a rational square. The roots are distinct because the [discriminant](../../../../../discriminant.md) is nonzero. By (c), $\boxed{G_f\not\subseteq A_4}$.
