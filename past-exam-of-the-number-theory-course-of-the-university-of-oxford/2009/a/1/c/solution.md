<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) with $n$ distinct roots, the criterion is

$$
\boxed{G_f\subseteq A_n\iff\Delta_f\text{ is a nonzero square in }\mathbb Q.}
$$

Write $\delta=\prod_{i<j}(\alpha_i-\alpha_j)\ne0$. Part (a) gives $\sigma\delta=\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\delta$. If every Galois [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md) is even, $\delta$ is fixed by the entire [Galois group](../../../../../galois-group.md) and therefore belongs to its [fixed field](../../../../../fixed-field.md) $\mathbb Q$. Thus $\Delta_f=\delta^2$ is a rational square. Conversely, if $\Delta_f=q^2$ with $q\in\mathbb Q^\times$, then $\delta^2=q^2$ in a [field](../../../../../field.md) implies $\delta=\pm q$. Every [automorphism](../../../../../automorphism.md) fixes this nonzero element, so its [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md) sign must be one.

The distinct-root hypothesis is necessary in the statement's intended degree-$n$ [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md) action. The source says “arbitrary degree” without stating that hypothesis. If repeated roots were allowed, zero [discriminant](../../../../../discriminant.md) would carry no sign information: $(X-1)^2(X^2-2)$ has [discriminant](../../../../../discriminant.md) zero, while its nontrivial [automorphism](../../../../../automorphism.md) swaps $\sqrt2,-\sqrt2$ and fixes the two rational-root positions, an odd [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md). The criterion above applies to the [squarefree polynomial](../../../../../square-free-polynomial.md), or to the squarefree part with its degree and root action adjusted accordingly.
