The [Galois group](../../../../../galois-group.md) $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ is the [group](../../../../../group-split.md) of all [field automorphisms](../../../../../field-automorphism.md) of $L$ fixing every element of $K$, with composition as its operation. A finite extension is a [Galois extension](../../../../../finite-galois-extension.md) if it is both normal and separable; equivalently, it is a splitting extension of separable polynomials, or its [group](../../../../../group-split.md) has order $[L:K]$. Normality means that every [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) over $K$ with a root in $L$ splits there.
