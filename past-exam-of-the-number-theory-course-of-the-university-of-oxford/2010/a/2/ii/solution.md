The [Fundamental theorem of Galois theory](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-galois-theory.md) gives an inclusion-reversing bijection for a finite Galois extension $L/K$ with [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $G$:

$$
H\longmapsto L^H,\qquad
M\longmapsto\operatorname{Gal}(L/M).
$$

It satisfies $[L:L^H]=|H|$ and $[L^H:K]=[G:H]$. An intermediate extension $M/K$ is Galois precisely when its corresponding [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ is normal, in which case restriction induces $\operatorname{Gal}(M/K)\cong G/H$.
