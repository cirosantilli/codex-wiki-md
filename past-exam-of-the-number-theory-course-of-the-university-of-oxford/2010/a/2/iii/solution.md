<h1 id="a/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [cyclotomic polynomial](../../../../../cyclotomic-polynomial.md) is

$$
\Phi_n(X)=\prod_{\substack{1\le a\le n\\\gcd(a,n)=1}}(X-\xi^a).
$$

By the allowed irreducibility, this is the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) of $\xi$ and its degree is $\varphi(n)$. The [field](../../../../../field.md) $\mathbb Q(\xi)$ contains every $n$th [root of unity](../../../../../root-of-unity.md) and is the [splitting field](../../../../../splitting-field.md) of $X^n-1$. This [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has no repeated roots in characteristic zero, so the extension is Galois.

An [automorphism](../../../../../automorphism.md) sends $\xi$ to another primitive root $\xi^a$, where $a$ is a unit modulo $n$. Conversely each such conjugate defines an embedding, and it is an [automorphism](../../../../../automorphism.md) of $\mathbb Q(\xi)$ because $a$ has an inverse modulo $n$. Composition corresponds to multiplication of exponents. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\xi)/\mathbb Q)\cong(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times.}
$$
