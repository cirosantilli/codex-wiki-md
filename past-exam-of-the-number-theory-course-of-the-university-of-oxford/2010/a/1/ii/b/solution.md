<h1 id="a/1/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An algebraic [field extension](../../../../../../field-extension.md) $E/K$ is separable if every $\alpha\in E$ has a separable [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) over $K$.

For an inseparable example, take $K=\mathbb F_p(t)$ and $f(X)=X^p-t$. [Eisenstein's criterion](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at the prime $t$ in $\mathbb F_p[t]$, followed by [Gauss lemma for polynomials](../../../../../../gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md), proves irreducibility over its fraction [field](../../../../../../field.md). Its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is zero, and in a [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) it is $(X-t^{1/p})^p$, so it is inseparable.

Now let $M/K$ be a separable splitting extension of a nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $f$. Every irreducible factor $g$ of $f$ has a root $\alpha\in M$, and $g$ is a scalar multiple of the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) of $\alpha$. Separability of $M/K$ makes this [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) separable. Therefore every irreducible factor of $f$ is separable, which proves the requested conclusion in the factorwise convention. The squarefree qualification under the alternative convention is explained in part (a).
