<h1 id="a/3/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take $K=\mathbb Q$. For every positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $n$, the real number $\sqrt[n]2$ lies in $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ and has degree $n$, by [Eisenstein's criterion](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at $2$. If $\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q$ had finite degree, all its subextension degrees would be bounded by that degree, contradicting this unbounded family. Therefore **$[\overline{\mathbb Q}:\mathbb Q]$ is infinite**.
