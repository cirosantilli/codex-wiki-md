<h1 id="a/3/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $K=\mathbb R$. Every complex number is algebraic over $\mathbb R$, since $\mathbb C=\mathbb R(i)$, so its algebraic closure inside $\mathbb C$ is $\mathbb C$. This is a proper containment and

$$
\boxed{[\overline{\mathbb R}:\mathbb R]=2.}
$$
