<h1 id="a/3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let the monic [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) of $\alpha$ over $L$ have coefficients $a_0,\ldots,a_{r-1}$, and put $F=K(a_0,\ldots,a_{r-1})$. Each coefficient is algebraic over $K$, so adjoining these finitely many coefficients successively gives $[F:K]<\infty$. The same [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) makes $\alpha$ algebraic over $F$, giving $[F(\alpha):F]\le r$. The [tower law for field extensions](../../../../../tower-law-for-field-extensions.md) yields $[F(\alpha):K]<\infty$. Its subfield $K(\alpha)$ therefore also has finite degree over $K$. This holds for every $\alpha\in M$, proving **$M/K$ is algebraic**.
