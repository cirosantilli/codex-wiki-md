<h1 id="b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For degree $3$ there are either three real embeddings or one real embedding and one complex pair. The respective [Minkowski bound for ideal classes](../../../../../minkowski-s-bound.md) are

$$
\frac{2}{9}\sqrt{|\Delta^2(L)|},\qquad
\frac{8}{9\pi}\sqrt{|\Delta^2(L)|}.
$$

Under the given [discriminant](../../../../../discriminant.md) bound they are at most $\frac29\sqrt{45}<2$ and $\frac{8}{9\pi}\sqrt{45}<2$. Hence every [ideal class](../../../../../ideal-class.md) contains an [integral ideal](../../../../../integral-ideal.md) with positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) [norm](../../../../../norm.md) strictly below $2$, so with [norm](../../../../../norm.md) $1$. The only such [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) is $\mathcal O_L$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{h_L=1.}
$$
