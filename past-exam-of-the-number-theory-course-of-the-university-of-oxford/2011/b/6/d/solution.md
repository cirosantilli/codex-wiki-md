<h1 id="b/6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Part (a), applied to any [ideal class](../../../../../ideal-class.md), produces an [integral ideal](../../../../../integral-ideal.md) of positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) [norm](../../../../../norm.md) at most $c_K$, so $c_K\ge1$. Writing $n=[K:\mathbb Q]$ and using $s\le n/2$ gives

$$
\sqrt{|\Delta^2(K)|}\ge\left(\frac\pi4\right)^s\frac{n^n}{n!}
\ge\left(\frac\pi4\right)^{n/2}2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}.
$$

For the last inequality, write $n^n/n!=\prod_{j=1}^n(n/j)$; the first $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ factors are at least $2$, and the remaining factors are at least $1$. Since $\lfloor n/2\rfloor\ge(n-1)/2$, squaring yields

$$
|\Delta^2(K)|\ge\frac12\left(\frac\pi2\right)^n.
$$

As $\pi/2>1$, the assumed upper bound $D$ forces

$$
\boxed{n\le\frac{\log(2D)}{\log(\pi/2)}.}
$$

For $D\ge1$, the ceiling of the right-hand side is a valid [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $M$. If $D=0$, no number [field](../../../../../field.md) satisfies the premise, so any positive $M$ works.
