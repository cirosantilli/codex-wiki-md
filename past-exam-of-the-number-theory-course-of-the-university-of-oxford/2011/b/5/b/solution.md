<h1 id="b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\omega=(1+\sqrt{-3})/2$, whose [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is $x^2-x+1$. For $p_2\equiv1\pmod3$, the [cyclic group](../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb F_{p_2}^{\times}$ has a nontrivial cube [root of unity](../../../../../root-of-unity.md) $t$. Then $-t$ and $-t^2$ are distinct roots of $x^2-x+1$. By the [Kummer-Dedekind theorem](../../../../../kummer-dedekind-theorem.md),

$$
(p_2)=QQ',\qquad \boxed{N(Q)=N(Q')=p_2.}
$$

Since the given [ring of integers](../../../../../ring-of-integers.md) is a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md), write $Q=(a+b\omega)$. Its [norm](../../../../../norm.md) is

$$
p_2=N(a+b\omega)=a^2+ab+b^2.
$$

Multiplication by $4$ completes the square:

$$
\boxed{4p_2=(2a+b)^2+3b^2.}
$$

Thus $x=2a+b$ and $y=b$ supply the required integers.
