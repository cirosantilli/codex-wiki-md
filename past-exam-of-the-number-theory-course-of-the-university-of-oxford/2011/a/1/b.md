For a [splitting field](../../../../splitting-field.md) $L$ of $f$ over $K$, define $\operatorname{Gal}(f)=\operatorname{Aut}_K(L)$. Each [automorphism](../../../../automorphism.md) permutes the distinct roots, since it fixes the coefficients. An [automorphism](../../../../automorphism.md) fixing every root fixes the [field](../../../../field.md) they generate, so this action is faithful. If there are $r\le n$ distinct roots, it gives an embedding into $S_r$, and hence into $S_n$ by fixing $n-r$ additional symbols.
