↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Over the [finite field](../../../../../../finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_{13}$,

$$
x^3-x^2+x+1=(x+2)(x^2-3x-6).
$$

The [discriminant](../../../../../../discriminant.md) of the quadratic is $9+24\equiv7\pmod{13}$. The nonzero squares are $1,3,4,9,10,12$, so the quadratic is irreducible. Its two roots lie in $\mathbb F_{13^2}$, with the [Frobenius automorphism](../../../../../../frobenius-automorphism.md) interchanging them. Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f)\cong C_2.}
$$
