<h1 id="a/1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Factorization over the [rational numbers](../../../../../../rational-number.md) gives

$$
f(x)=(x-1)(x+1)(x^3-7).
$$

The rational roots add nothing to the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md). By [Eisenstein's criterion](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at $7$, $x^3-7$ is irreducible. Put $a=\sqrt[3]7$ and $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/3}$. Its [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $L=\mathbb Q(a,\zeta)$. The real [field](../../../../../../field.md) $\mathbb Q(a)$ has degree $3$; the nonreal element $\zeta$ has degree $2$ over it. Thus $[L:\mathbb Q]=6$, and its faithful action on the three nonrational roots identifies

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f)\cong S_3.}
$$
