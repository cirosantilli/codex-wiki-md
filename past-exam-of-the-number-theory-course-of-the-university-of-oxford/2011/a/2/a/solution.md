<h1 id="a/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [fixed field](../../../../../fixed-field.md) is

$$
K^G=\{a\in K:\sigma(a)=a\text{ for every }\sigma\in G\}.
$$

It is a subfield because every [field automorphism](../../../../../field-automorphism.md) preserves sums, products and inverses.

Set $F=K^G$. If $[K:F]$ is infinite the desired finite lower bound is immediate. Otherwise let $b_1,\ldots,b_d$ be an $F$-[basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $K$. If $|G|>d$, the homogeneous system over $K$

$$
\sum_{\sigma\in G}c_\sigma\sigma(b_j)=0\qquad(1\le j\le d)
$$

has a nonzero solution. For $a=\sum_j a_jb_j$, with $a_j\in F$, every $\sigma$ fixes $a_j$, so the same coefficients give $\sum_\sigma c_\sigma\sigma(a)=0$. This contradicts [linear independence of distinct field embeddings](../../../../../linear-independence-of-distinct-field-embeddings.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{[K:K^G]\ge |G|.}
$$
