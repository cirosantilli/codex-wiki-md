<h1 id="a/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Reciprocal roots require $c_3\ne0$, equivalently every original root is nonzero. Their [monic polynomial](../../../../../monic-polynomial.md) is the normalized reciprocal [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md)

$$
\frac{X^3f(1/X)}{c_3}
=\boxed{X^3+\frac{c_2}{c_3}X^2+\frac{c_1}{c_3}X+\frac1{c_3}}.
$$

If $c_3=0$, at least one reciprocal in the source is undefined, so that part has no [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) interpretation without the nonzero-root qualification.
