<h1 id="a/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Scaling every root scales its elementary [symmetric polynomials](../../../../../symmetric-polynomial.md) by the corresponding powers of $d$. Therefore the [monic polynomial](../../../../../monic-polynomial.md) is

$$
\boxed{X^3+dc_1X^2+d^2c_2X+d^3c_3.}
$$

This remains valid for $d=0$, when all three roots are zero; deriving it by symmetric coefficients avoids dividing by $d$.
