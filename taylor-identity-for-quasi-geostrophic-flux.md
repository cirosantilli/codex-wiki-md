# Taylor identity for quasi-geostrophic flux

↑ **Parent:** [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md)

For zonal averages, $u'=-\psi'_y$, $v'=\psi'_x$, and $Q'=\nabla_H^2\psi'+\partial_z(S\psi'_z)$, with $S=f_0^2/N^2$ depending only on height, define $F=-\overline{u'v'}$ and $G=S\overline{\psi'_x\psi'_z}$. Integrating zonal derivatives by parts gives $\overline{v'Q'}=\partial_y\overline{\psi'_x\psi'_y}+\partial_z(S\overline{\psi'_x\psi'_z})$. This identity is exact within [quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md), without assuming small disturbance amplitudes. Periodicity or appropriate vanishing zonal boundary terms is essential.
